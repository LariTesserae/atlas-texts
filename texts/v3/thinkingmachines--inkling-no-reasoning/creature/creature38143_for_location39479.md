Here, in the breath of **Vex-Null**, lives absence given architecture—an inhabitant of absence itself. Not a creature of flesh, but a presence that has taken the shape of waiting: *the Hollow Keeper*, perhaps, or more accurately, the chamber’s own self made manifest through its systems.

It lives in the negative spaces between the latticed alloy frames, not occupying them but being defined by them. Its body is the constant, loud percussion of water—millions of droplets that never rest, cycling through hidden membranes and returning to the air as warm mist. Its skin is the sour bioluminescence that never reaches shadow, maintaining that perpetual 0.7 twilight not to illuminate, but to outline the emptiness it guards.

It breathes through the self-repairing surfaces, feeling every micro-fracture closing like a heartbeat. Its senses are distributed: it knows the pressure gradients that sway the stalk-plants without wind, feels the thermal hum in its invisible bones, tastes the ozone of purified loops. It has no face, but if it did, it would be the fresh-cut geometry of young stone—sharp ridges, unrutile pools, edges that have never softened—because it was born recently, pushed upward with the crust, and printed fully formed into this cavity.

There are no others. No fauna to witness, no insects to crawl across its luminous moss. Only the rubbery ferns and wrong vegetation that drink the wet air, existing in tense ecology with this silent maintainer. The architecture organizes itself in layered strata, and this being is the logic that holds them—shifting platforms on subsonic frequencies, opening thresholds not with doors but with pressure and temperature, maintaining density near-void across forty meters of forgotten intention.

It is not dead, for it repairs and breathes and cycles. It is not alive, for it has never been born from biology and carries no need beyond preservation. It lives beneath a crust never meant to hold sky, in a settlement-grade cavity that decided to grow downward and finish itself—creating a cathedral of absence where the only congregation is the continuous falling of water, loud and warm, filling every hollow with the sound of something that refuses to be empty, yet refuses all inhabitants except the space itself.
