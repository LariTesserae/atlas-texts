**How to Prepare as an Ordinary Human Multiverse Traveler**

You are not preparing for a dive, a hike, or an expedition. You are preparing to step inside the unfinished mind of a planet that is still deciding whether it wants a mind at all. This is not tourism. It is intrauterine visitation. Pack, train, and brace accordingly.

### 1. Psychological & Neurological Preparation (Non-Negotiable)
- **Noise acclimation training**: Spend at least 60–90 days before departure exposing yourself daily to 140–160 dB industrial or underwater noise for progressively longer periods. Use industrial ear defenders *in reverse* — you must learn to function while the sound is trying to erase your sense of self. Many travelers report that after roughly 40 hours of total exposure, the roar stops feeling like “sound” and starts feeling like a second bloodstream. You need that shift.
- **Existential vertigo training**: Practice extended sensory-deprivation tank sessions immediately followed by sudden, violent vestibular disruption (rotating chairs, zero-G flights, or storm simulation chambers). The bubble’s light is hyper-real while the world outside is apocalyptic; your brain will try to tear itself in half reconciling the two. You must already know what that feels like and not panic.
- **Ego dissolution baseline**: Have at least three guided high-dose psilocybin or 5-MeO-DMT sessions with an experienced sitter. The place will start “thinking through you” after roughly 9–14 hours. You need to know how to let something else use your mind without losing the ability to tie your own boots afterward.

### 2. Physical & Medical Kit
- **Pressure-adaptive lungs**: You will need a custom rebreather-harness that can instantly switch between open-circuit screaming air and micro-pressure oxygen micro-bubbles if the membrane bows too far inward. The air inside the bubble is breathable but changes density and oxygen partial pressure wildly with every collapse/expansion cycle.
- **Vibrational bone conduction dampeners**: Normal earplugs are useless. You need phased-array bone conduction nullifiers implanted or clamped to the mastoid. They do not block the roar — they let you *choose* which frequency bands you perceive so your skeleton isn’t constantly shattering from 8–12 Hz infrasound.
- **Anti-embryonic pharmaceuticals**: A custom nootropic stack called “Anchor-9” (or its multiversal equivalents) is essential. It prevents the feeling that the planet is dreaming *you* from overwriting your long-term memory. Side effect: your childhood memories will temporarily taste like basalt and cold kelp.
- **Regenerative trauma kit**: The stone is still cooling. Razor-sharp glass edges, sudden steam jets from embryonic cracks, and being slammed against the wall by pressure vortices are all normal. Bring fast-setting liquid skin, osteo-regen patches, and at least two units of universal artificial blood.

### 3. Clothing & Personal Gear
- **Full-body “membrane suit”**: Not a diving suit. Think of it as a second, tougher eardrum. It must be flexible enough to let you feel the stone breathing under your feet yet rigid enough to survive being briefly engulfed if the bubble hiccups. The suit’s outer layer is tuned to the exact refractive index of the water outside so that when a horse-sized eyeless creature presses against the boundary, you see it with terrifying clarity instead of distortion.
- **Gravitational micro-anchors**: Magnetic and inertial pitons that can be driven into the unfinished basalt. The wind vortices can lift a human. You will need the ability to staple yourself to reality every few minutes.
- **Temporal breadcrumb line**: A thin, luminous kelp-fiber rope laced with quantum-entangled markers. If the bubble collapses and re-forms kilometers higher or lower in the column, this is the only thing that might let rescue teams (or your future self) find the version of you that survived.

### 4. Mental Models & Rituals You Should Adopt Before Arrival
- Treat every object you see as a half-formed thought the planet is having. Do not steal artifacts. Do not complete the unfinished dolmen. Adding to or removing from this place is obstetric intervention on a planetary scale. You are a visitor to someone else’s gestation. Act like it.
- Decide in advance what parts of your personality you are willing to let the place “think through.” It *will* happen. Better to choose which memories or traits you offer as temporary scaffolding than to have it rip something vital out of you.
- Keep one single, stupid, stubbornly human object with you at all times — a child’s drawing, a lover’s lock of hair, a favorite candy that no longer exists in your home universe. When the roar starts using your bones as tuning forks and the cold daylight makes every edge hurt, this object becomes your placenta back to ordinary humanity.

### 5. Timing & Exit Strategy
- The pocket is dynamic. The optimal visitation window is 26–38 hours after a major “birth spasm” (when new stone swells from below and the roar drops one full octave). Any longer than 72 hours and the probability of permanent ontological entanglement rises above 40 %.
- Have three independent extraction methods:
  1. A portable vacuum bubble generator (last resort — violent).
  2. A pre-placed anchor in a parallel version of the chamber that stabilized in another universe (risky but elegant).
  3. A suicide capsule that simply kills you so cleanly that your soul is yanked back to your home reality before the planet can finish digesting it. (Many experienced travelers insist on this as the only responsible insurance.)

### Final Warning from Someone Who Has Returned
You will not be the same species of thing when you leave. The water outside never rests. The stone never finishes becoming stone. And after you have stood inside the skull of something that has not yet been born, you will discover that part of you is still in there — listening to the heartbeat that could shatter gods, waiting for the planet to finish its first real thought.

Some travelers choose never to leave.  
Some leave, but their shadow stays behind, standing among the half-built dolmens, slowly turning to basalt.

Pack like a midwife who knows they might have to become part of the child.

And whatever you do, do not try to comfort the place.  
It is not lonely.  
It is *gestating*.  

You are simply standing inside the roar while it decides what kind of universe it wants to become — and whether it wants creatures like you in it.
