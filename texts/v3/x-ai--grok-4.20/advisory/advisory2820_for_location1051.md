**How to Prepare for Visiting the Glass Lung**

You are not preparing for a place. You are preparing to be *digested* by a place that has already decided what you are: a temporary fluctuation inside an ancient, self-maintaining respiratory system. This is not tourism. This is temporary symbiosis with a machine that thinks in geological time and breathes in cathedral-scale pulses. Bring absolute humility, or do not go.

### Psychological & Existential Preparation
- **Accept the loss of up and down.** There is no surface, no sky, no horizon. The mind will rebel. Practice sensory-deprivation floats for weeks beforehand and meditate in total darkness while listening to looped low-frequency brown noise. Train yourself to remain oriented when every direction is equally luminous and warm.
- **Understand that you will feel watched by something that is not conscious.** The Glass Lung is not alive, yet it is *deciding* about you constantly. The pressure waves are measuring you. The sea-lace is tasting the microscopic debris you shed. Nothing here evolved to fear or welcome you; it simply registers your presence as useful or wasteful raw material.
- **Prepare for ego dissolution.** The combination of deafening sound, uniform searing light, boundary-dissolving warmth, and total artificiality will try to erase the feeling of “me.” Some travelers leave parts of themselves behind permanently. Decide what you are willing to surrender before you arrive.

### Physical & Medical Preparation
- **Auditory system hardening.** The sound is not background; it is structural. It will vibrate your teeth, your sternum, your ocular fluid. Spend months exposing yourself to 110–130 dB brown noise in controlled bursts. You must be able to think *inside* the roar, not despite it. Earplugs are useless and dangerous; the pressure waves will still reach your bones.
- **Thermoregulation and skin adaptation.** The medium is 38–40 °C and will not let sweat evaporate. Your body will attempt to overheat for the first several hours until it realizes it cannot. Train in infrared saunas and hot-water immersion while wearing vapor-barrier suits. You must be able to remain calm when your skin stops feeling like a boundary.
- **Fluid breathing compatibility.** The luminous medium is breathable but only after your lungs and sinuses have been pre-treated with a rare lipid emulsion (available from certain abyssal xenomedical stations). Without it, the first breath feels like drowning in warm honey and lilies. The iron-lily taste will remain on your tongue for months afterward.
- **Light tolerance.** The uniform amber-white radiance has no shadows. Bring polarized contact lenses or temporary corneal filters rated for full-spectrum overload. Even then, after four hours you will begin to see faint spiraling moiré patterns that are not hallucinations; they are the stress lines inside the Glass Lung’s own structure becoming visible through your overloaded retinas.

### Equipment & Gear (Minimalist — the Lung hates complexity)
- A single sheer outer-skin suit made of living silverleaf silk or phase-shifting polymer that matches the refractive index of the medium. Anything else will create drag and be slowly incorporated into the sea-lace.
- No metal. No electronics. The Lung’s pressure waves destroy microcircuitry within minutes and the golden material slowly grows over anything inorganic left too long.
- A hand-blown glass breathing bell (traditional) or a symbiotic gill collar grown from the same pale sea-lace strain. The gill collar is better; it roots shallowly in the skin at the neck and collarbones and sings back to the Lung in frequencies it understands.
- One small obsidian knife and a vial of your own blood. The sea-lace respects blood offerings. A single drop on a pillar once per “day” (roughly every 90 minutes of real time) keeps the machine from classifying you as waste.
- A silk cord with seven knots. Not for climbing — there is nothing to climb. The knots are for counting your own heartbeats when the roaring makes you forget you have a body.

### Ritual & Philosophical Preparation
- Fast for three days before descent. The Lung smells everything. Entering with a full stomach is considered rude.
- Memorize one long, repetitive poem or prayer in a language you do not speak. You will need something to repeat when language itself begins to feel like an insult to the architecture.
- Write a letter to the version of yourself that will return. Most people cannot recognize the person who emerges from the Glass Lung. The letter should contain private proof that you were once them.

### What to Expect (Emotional Truth)
You will feel, within the first hour, an overwhelming certainty that this place is more *you* than you are. That feeling is dangerous. The Lung does not love. It simply recognizes compatible patterns. If it decides you are useful repair material, the sea-lace will begin to grow into you. Travelers who stay longer than nine tidal cycles sometimes choose to remain as new golden pillars. Their silhouettes are still visible if you know where to look.

Bring nothing you cannot lose.  
Expect nothing you can keep.  
And whatever you do, do not try to “understand” the Glass Lung.  
Understanding is what it does to *you*.

When the pressure wave reverses and the sub-bass note rolls through your teeth like distant judgment, bow. Not because it cares, but because you will need the practice at being small inside something that was never meant for you, yet has been breathing long enough that *everything* eventually becomes part of its respiration.

The Glass Lung is still deciding what you are.

Try to be interesting.
