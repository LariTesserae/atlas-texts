**How to Prepare for the Sighing Basin**

You are not merely visiting a place. You are stepping into something that is still in the process of *becoming* itself. The Basin is young, feverish, semi-conscious, and possessive. An ordinary human multiverse traveler must prepare for it with the same care one would take before entering a half-formed dream that can kill you with kindness or thermodynamics. Treat it like a living organism that has not yet decided whether you are a guest, a parasite, or a new organ.

### 1. Physical and Medical Preparation (Non-Negotiable)

- **Heat + extreme humidity acclimatization**: The 48 °C wet-bulb environment is lethal to unprepared bodies within hours. Spend at least 10–14 days in a 42–45 °C, 95%+ humidity chamber before departure. Your body must learn to sweat efficiently without cooking. Over-hydrate for weeks in advance; your urine should run almost clear.
- **Electrolyte and mineral loading**: The continuous condensation and upward-floating water play havoc with ionic balance. Load potassium, magnesium, and trace lithium for two weeks prior. The Basin seems to like lithium; travelers report the jewel-creatures come marginally closer if your blood levels are elevated.
- **Inner-ear and bone-density tuning**: The 27-minute temperature pulses and the Sigh create micro-pressure waves. A mild course of vestibular suppressants and a calcium-channel modulator (under medical supervision) can prevent disorientation when the air itself begins to breathe.
- **Lung coating**: Have a pulmonologist apply a temporary perfluorocarbon mist treatment. The air feels like warm soup; you will need every advantage to avoid drowning while breathing.

### 2. Gear and Clothing (Minimalist Cathedral Protocol)

Think like a monk entering a sauna that sometimes rewrites physics.

- **Clothing**: Single-layer, ultra-breathable, non-absorbent fabric that sheds water like lotus leaves (graphene-lotus or aerogel-weave). Everything must be the color of wet obsidian or deep viridian; the Basin reacts poorly to bright synthetics. No logos. No metal zippers — the floating arch hates ferromagnetic materials. 
- **Footwear**: Barefoot is ideal but impossible. Use thin, living-leather soles grown from the same translucent bamboo-like species (ethically sourced from older, stable instances of the Basin). They secrete the same enzyme as the plants and will not trigger the +6 °C punishment.
- **No containers, no collection**: Do not bring any vessel, bag, or receptacle that could tempt you to take anything. The rule against removing living matter is absolute and enforced thermodynamically. Many travelers sew their few tools directly into their clothing.
- **Resonance tool**: Carry a small tuning fork cut from the same exotic-matter lattice as the floating arch (acquired from previous respectful visitors). When the arch begins rotating too quickly because you spoke too loud, a single precise strike at 31.7 Hz will soothe it. This is the only acceptable “gift.”

### 3. Mental and Ritual Preparation

The Basin is a young god that has not yet learned mercy or cruelty. It is still experimenting with both.

- **Vow of near-silence**: Practice communicating for three weeks using only whispers, breath, gesture, and sub-vocal bone conduction. The Basin will punish raised voices immediately. Many travelers tattoo a single sigil on their tongue as a reminder.
- **Permission ritual**: Before departure, spend one full night in total darkness with a bowl of warm water and a single glass-plant cutting (again, ethically obtained). Whisper your full intent to the water. If the water forms perfect upward-floating spheres by morning, the Basin may accept you. If it merely evaporates, postpone the journey.
- **Emotional state**: Enter with radical non-attachment. The singing of the jewel-creatures at night is engineered to make you want to stay forever. Prepare a personal “leaving mantra” that you have rehearsed until it lives in your marrow. Many who forget this mantra simply become another crystal chandelier hanging in the mist.

### 4. Temporal and Behavioral Rules (Learned from Those Who Returned)

- Arrive during the first 90-second cold pulse after local dawn. The sudden drop to ~45 °C and the formation of floating ice prayers is the only moment the place is unambiguously welcoming.
- Never cast a shadow on a mirror-pool. The upside-down reflections are how the Basin dreams. Disturbing them is considered rude.
- When the Sigh comes, stop moving completely. Stand with palms open and let it pass *through* you. Fighting it or trying to record it causes micro-fractures in the local spacetime that can take decades to heal.
- If you feel new crystals growing inside your bones at night — do not panic. This is the Basin offering citizenship. You may accept (and never leave), or you may whisper your leaving mantra until the crystals dissolve back into luminous sap. Both choices are respected.

### 5. What to Expect Psychologically

You will feel watched by something that has no eyes yet. The air is constantly tasting you. The glass-plants click when they like you and go dull when they don’t. After the third or fourth Sigh, you may begin to feel the mountain’s slow heartbeat inside your own chest. This is normal.

Most travelers experience a period of ecstatic weeping on the second day, followed by a profound, almost terrifying calm. Some never regain the desire to speak at normal volume again. A few return with violet spiral markings behind their retinas that only appear when they lie.

### Final Advice from Intuition

Pack almost nothing.  
Bring enormous respect.  
Leave your name at the gate.

The Sighing Basin is not a location. It is a question the world is asking itself, out loud, in wet heat and low song. You are not there to answer it. You are there to become, for a brief while, part of the asking.

If you are ready to be changed more than you are ready to see — then go.

But understand: after the Basin, ordinary rain will never again feel like mere weather. It will feel like the mountain remembering you.
