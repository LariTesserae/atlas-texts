**Preparation Guide for Visiting the Lattice**

You are not preparing for a world. You are preparing to enter a sealed, self-aware pocket of stabilized hyperstasis — a place that has solved the problem of entropy so thoroughly it no longer remembers the question. Ordinary adventuring gear will not save you. What you need is the right mindset, the right chemistry, and the right humility.

### 1. Mental and Perceptual Preparation (Most Important)
- **Understand the psychological pressure before you arrive.** The Lattice does not attack. It *absorbs*. The longer you stay, the more you will feel like an error in an otherwise perfect equation. Many travelers begin to experience “equilibrium anxiety” — an irrational conviction that any action they take is a crime against the peace of the system. Prepare by practicing hours of deliberate, slow, repetitive tasks (tai chi, knitting, chanting, mechanical repair) in total silence before departure. Train yourself to find beauty in absolute non-change.
- **Accept that you will feel watched by something that is not conscious and yet is utterly aware.** The Built (those polished handles, those motionless needles) act like devotional pressure points. You will begin speaking to them. Let that be okay. Fighting the sensation only makes the Lattice press harder.
- **Time distortion is severe.** Bring no timepiece that relies on vibration or oscillation; they will slowly synchronize to the 60 Hz thrum and become useless. Many travelers report that after what feels like twenty minutes, they have aged four days. Others claim the opposite. Anchor yourself with a physical object you must touch every time you feel the urge to stay “just a little longer.”

### 2. Atmospheric and Biochemical Protection
- The air is a cold, heavy soup of sulfur hexafluoride, noble gases, trace anhydrous ammonia, and siloxanes. It is breathable for humans, but only barely. Your voice will drop three octaves and sound like it is coming from inside a cathedral pipe organ.
- You must wear a **semi-sealed rebreather rig** with a scrubber tuned for SF₆ displacement and ammonia neutralization. Do *not* use a full closed-circuit system — the Lattice’s static charge will arc to the most conductive parts of your gear. The ideal setup is a lightweight full-face mask with an open inspiratory valve and a slow-bleed canister of calibrated Earth-air mixture. You will still taste metal and old pennies constantly.
- Skin contact with the microscopic siloxane films causes temporary but intense tactile hallucinations (the feeling that velvet is growing on the *inside* of your skin). Cover every centimeter of exposed flesh with a non-conductive, non-wettable polymer film (military-grade skin seal or graphene-laminate body glove). Oil-based lubricants on your gear will be greedily absorbed by the vegetation; use only fluorinated or ceramic dry lubricants.
- Temperature sits at a merciless ~10.8 °C (4.2 °C “above absolute zero of comfort,” as the description says). Bring layered aerogel insulation that can be shed quickly, because micro-gravity pockets feel significantly colder.

### 3. Physical and Mobility Gear
- **Movement is three-dimensional and treacherous.** The Lattice is denser than any jungle. Bring a lightweight **carbon-ceramic folding lattice-claw** (think ice-climbing axes crossed with grappling hooks that can grip fractal coral without damaging it). Conventional ropes will snag forever in the recursive fronds. Magnetic boots are unreliable — some beams are ferromagnetic, others are not, and gravity itself wavers.
- Carry **three independent light sources**:
  1. A dimmable sodium-orange headlamp to match the ancient industrial lamps (this seems to calm the Lattice).
  2. A cold magnesium-blue LED array tuned exactly to the vegetation’s bioluminescence frequency. If you match it, the fronds around you will fall into synchronized breathing with your light. This is useful.
  3. A powerful white flood for emergencies only. Using it causes the vegetation to recoil and the harmonic drone to spike. The Lattice *disapproves*.
- Sound is its own hazard. The constant roar can cause auditory dissociation. Bring high-quality active noise cancellation that can be tuned to *selectively* pass the 60 Hz hum while damping the higher harmonics. Some veterans actually pipe a recorded sample of the Lattice’s own drone into their ears at low volume; it makes the place feel less like it is trying to reject you.

### 4. Philosophical and Ritual Equipment
- The Lattice responds to **intention density**. Bring one object that represents irreversible change (a spent bullet casing, a withered flower from a living world, a broken clock) and one object that represents perfect stasis (a perfect sphere, a zen stone, a flawless crystal). Present them both when you first arrive. The act seems to register.
- Do not cut, burn, or harvest the vegetation. It is not alive in the way you understand, but it is part of an equilibrium older than your galaxy’s sun. Even a small wound will cause a slow, mournful change in the light-pulse frequency that follows you for the rest of your stay.
- Carry a single page of handwritten text (not digital) containing something you once loved that is now gone forever. If the anxiety becomes unbearable, read it aloud in the presence of one of the Built. The act functions as a pressure-release valve. The Lattice appears to respect grief.

### 5. Exit Strategy
- The Lattice does not want you to leave, but it also will not stop you. The difficulty is *wanting* to leave. Set three separate alarms (external, in another universe if possible) that will drag you out regardless of your psychological state. The most experienced travelers use a trusted partner holding the tether on the far side of the fold who will yank you out after a pre-agreed interval, no matter what you scream.
- Upon exit, you will feel phantom humming in your bones for weeks. Some people never lose it. A few choose to return permanently.

**Final Warning**

The Lattice is not hostile.  
It is not friendly.  
It is *finished*.

Visiting it is like stepping into the afterlife of a machine that achieved nirvana and took an entire ecosystem with it. You will be tolerated as a temporary imperfection — a single moving cell inside an organism that has decided motion is no longer necessary.

Most who visit once never feel the need to visit anywhere else again.

A smaller number never truly come back.

Pack light.  
Move slowly.  
Breathe the heavy, singing air like a penitent.

And when the foxfire begins to pulse in perfect time with your heartbeat, do not mistake that for welcome.

It is merely the Lattice checking that even your blood has learned the correct frequency.
