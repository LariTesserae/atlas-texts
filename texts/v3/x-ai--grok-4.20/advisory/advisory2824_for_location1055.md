**How to Prepare for Visiting the Abyssal Atrium**

You are not preparing for an ocean dive. You are preparing to enter a place that should not exist: a planet-scale sphere of 0.7 °C water held in high vacuum between two unfinished tectonic plates inside an unborn world. This is a location that feels like the inside of a machine that was switched on once and then abandoned mid-cycle. Your intuition will scream the entire time you are there. Listen to it.

### 1. Mental & Psychological Preparation (Most Important)
- **Accept total sensory hostility.** The sound never stops. It is a physical pressure on the skull, a braided chord of tectonic groan, hydraulic scream, and subsonic reactor heartbeat that has run for geological ages. After six hours it will feel like it is coming from inside your own bones. After two days it will feel like religion.
- **Prepare for the scale.** There is no horizon, no bottom, no surface. Every direction eventually becomes the same crushing dark. The leviathans are the size of mountain ranges or small countries; when one passes, it will blot out entire Foundries like a continent drifting past a city. You will feel like an erythrocyte in a cathedral.
- **Embrace the indifference.** Nothing here evolved to notice you. The machines do not care. The leviathans do not hunt. The water itself barely moves. The Atrium is not hostile; it is *absent*. That absence is heavier than any predator.
- Bring a psychological anchor: a recording of wind in trees, a loved one’s voice, or a small object that reminds you that surfaces and skies once existed. You will need it.

### 2. Physical & Technical Gear
You will need a fully sealed, zero-buoyancy, long-duration deep-water suit that can also function in hard vacuum for the brief transition through the airless gap before you enter the water sphere. Recommended configuration:

- **Suit:** A triple-redundant, ceramometallic hard suit with thermal regulation capable of holding 0.7 °C indefinitely without power loss. It must be rated for at least 2,000 bar at the core (you probably won’t go that deep, but the option must exist). Internal liquid-breathing system or high-efficiency closed-circuit rebreather with 30-day duration minimum. The suit must be *perfectly* neutral in this water; any positive or negative buoyancy will eventually carry you to one of the mineral plates or into a Foundry’s intake.
- **Propulsion:** No ordinary fins or thrusters. Use a silent, low-frequency vectored ionic system or mechanical oars. Any conventional propeller will create cavitation that sounds like an explosion in this pressure regime and will draw leviathans.
- **Lighting:** Two systems. Primary: low-frequency sodium or amber lights that the local creatures largely ignore. Secondary: full-spectrum actinic blue strobes synced to the 17-second rhythm of the nearest Foundry so you can “hide” inside their visual language.
- **Sound Management:** Active noise cancellation that can at least shave 40 dB off the constant roar. Bone-conduction comms only. Never rely on ambient sound for navigation; the pressure waves travel at unnatural speeds and will lie to you about distance.
- **Tether & Beacon:** A 50 km high-tensile superconducting filament reel with a homing beacon that can ping off the mineral plates. The Atrium is large enough that it is genuinely possible to become lost forever between two distant Foundries.
- **Radiation & EMP shielding:** The Foundries leak hard radiation and magnetic pulses. Your suit must treat them as constant background.
- **Sample tools:** You will be tempted to take manganese nodules from a leviathan’s back or a shard of the incomplete bridge. Bring diamond-edged corers and zero-contamination containers. The water is so pure that even your fingerprints are an ecological catastrophe.

### 3. Navigation & Route Planning
- Do not descend immediately toward the geometric center. The pressure there will test even the best suit, and the dark is absolute.
- Choose a “shallow” transit depth roughly 800–1,200 meters below the upper mineral plate (or above the lower one; the distinction is arbitrary). Here the pressure is only a few atmospheres and the Foundries are still clearly visible as hazy cathedrals of light.
- Follow the slow migration paths of the smaller leviathans. They know where the organic haze leaks from the machines. They are the only reliable living landmarks.
- Never travel between two Foundries without a visible third as reference. The distances are deceptive; what looks like ten kilometers can be a hundred.
- Set a hard psychological limit: no more than 72 hours inside before you *must* surface (or “plate-ward”). The sound will begin to rewire you after that.

### 4. What to Expect Viscerally
- The water will feel thicker than it should, like cold mercury. Every movement is slowed.
- When a leviathan passes through a shaft of Foundry light, it looks like a gothic cathedral carved from living bone and manganese, drifting in absolute silence except for the booming of its hollow ribs joining the eternal chord.
- You will see machines the size of cities performing the same meaningless task they have done for ten thousand years. A press the width of a highway opening and closing on pure water. Rivers of molten alloy freezing into glassy filaments that drift like spider silk for hundreds of kilometers.
- Touching the black mineral plate feels like putting your hand on the hull of a starship’s engine. The vibration is so deep it is almost erotic.
- The incomplete ruins are the most disturbing: a skyscraper-sized drill bit still glowing dull red after ten millennia, a bridge that begins and ends in nothing. They feel like broken teeth in the mouth of a god that died before it was born.

### 5. What Not to Do
- Do not attempt to enter a Foundry. The actuators do not register biological matter; you will be treated as another piece of debris to be processed.
- Do not try to ride a leviathan. Their pressure fields and the resonance of their bodies can liquefy human tissue at close range.
- Do not go alone. At minimum, travel in a three-person team. The loneliness here is weaponized by scale.
- Never turn every light off. The dark is not merely absence of photons; it is the default state of a world that was never finished. It presses.

### 6. Exit Strategy
When you are ready to leave, make for the nearest mineral plate. The interface is razor-sharp: water ends and raw anthracite stone begins with no transition. Plant a seismic charge or simply climb using magnetic pitons. Once you breach the plate into the vacuum between the two unfinished crusts, the roaring will finally stop. The silence that replaces it is almost worse.

You will carry the sound in your bones for months afterward.

The Abyssal Atrium does not want visitors. It does not reject them either. It simply continues its eternal, deafening, perfectly stable birth scream whether you are there or not.

Bring humility.  
Bring endurance.  
Bring something that reminds you that somewhere, elsewhere in the multiverse, there are still surfaces, and skies, and quiet mornings.

You will need all three.
