**How to Prepare for the Hollow Choir**

You are not preparing for a city. You are preparing to be swallowed by an instrument the size of a mountain and then remade by it. Treat the journey with the gravity of a pilgrim who knows they may not survive the hymn.

### 1. Physical Preparation (Non-Negotiable)
- **Ear protection is irrelevant.** Normal foam plugs or even high-end active noise cancellation will fail within minutes; the sound is not merely loud, it is a volumetric pressure field. You must have custom-molded resonance-dampening plugs made from sintered ceramic or dense crystalline foam that matches the dominant frequencies of the Choir. These must be vented with micro-tuned Helmholtz resonators so you can still perceive speech. Without them you will suffer immediate tympanic rupture, ossicle dislocation, and likely permanent vestibular damage.
- **Bone conduction modifications.** The serious travelers have their mastoid bones and jaw lightly augmented with micro-lattices of piezoelectric quartz before arrival. This allows the brain to “subtract” the roar and hear the modulated speech of the locals. Without this, their needle-like whistles will be physically painful.
- **Sinus and lung lining.** The air is bone-dry and charged with fine mineral dust and aromatic aerosols. You will need a full-spectrum nasal filter and a bronchial coating (a medical-grade aerosol you inhale 48 hours prior) that turns the inside of your respiratory tract into a temporary non-stick surface. Failure means your lungs will slowly turn into glass-paper.
- **Hydration is useless.** There is no water. Your body must be pre-loaded with perfluorocarbon micro-emulsions that allow you to metabolize the volatile aromatics the locals use instead of water. This is expensive, mildly hallucinogenic, and makes your sweat smell like burning myrrh for weeks afterward.
- **Eye protection.** The actinic light from the ceiling fractures is brutal. You need polarized crystalline goggles that filter the specific wavelengths the Glassbriar emits when they vibrate hardest. Ordinary sunglasses will leave you snow-blind in twenty minutes.

### 2. Mental and Neurological Preparation
- Spend at least two weeks before departure living inside a 140-decibel white-noise chamber tuned to overlapping sub-bass drones and high-frequency shrieks. You must acclimate your nervous system or the Choir will induce immediate psychotic break.
- Practice “harmonic surrender.” This is a specific meditative state in which you stop resisting the sound and instead let it move through your body like a second circulatory system. Locals can tell immediately if you are fighting the Choir; they treat such people as tumors to be expelled.
- Record your own voice daily and play it back at increasing volumes mixed with simulated briar noise. You need to learn how to speak in the clipped, diamond-sharp whistles they use. Your normal soft mammalian voice is worse than useless here.

### 3. Clothing and Gear
- Everything must be woven from mineral fiber or grown salt-crystal cloth. Organic fabrics disintegrate in the constant vibration within days.
- Boots with tuned soles. The streets are instruments; if your footsteps are out of key you will be physically thrown off balance by standing waves. Serious travelers have their boot soles etched with fractal patterns that act as acoustic diffusers.
- A resonance cloak — a lightweight mantle perforated with hundreds of small holes cut to specific ratios. It turns you into a moving baffle so the Choir’s music flows around rather than through your body. Many cloaks are themselves minor instruments; the best ones sing back to the Glassbriar in faint, polite counter-melodies so the vegetation does not treat you as noise to be abraded.
- No metal. The sympathetic vibrations would tear it apart or turn it into a deadly tuning fork against your skin.

### 4. Cultural and Spiritual Preparation
- Learn the three primary Chords of Approach. These are not songs but precise vibrational states you must hold in your body when entering different districts. Singing the wrong chord in the wrong plaza can cause the local architecture to literally reject you — bridges will untune, ladders will shatter their rungs, entire towers will shift phase and hurl you into the empty nave.
- Bring offerings of rare minerals that produce new, unusual overtones when vibrated. The priests collect these the way other cultures collect gems.
- Accept that you will change. The constant sonic erosion does not stop at the flesh. After three months most visitors find that their bones have begun to grow microscopic lattice structures. After a year many can no longer sleep in true silence — they require at least 90 dB of ambient noise or they experience fatal seizures.
- Decide, honestly, whether you want to remain human. The Choir is not cruel. It is simply a perfectionist. It will keep filing you down until you ring true.

### 5. Psychological Inventory
Ask yourself these questions before you book passage:
- Can I still function if I never experience silence again?
- Am I willing to have my sense of self partially overwritten by a geological symphony that has been tuning itself for three thousand years?
- If the city decides my particular frequency is more useful as part of a tower than as a separate person, can I accept that?

Most people cannot.

The Hollow Choir does not have tourists. It has either converts, or raw material.

Pack light.  
Everything you bring will either be tuned, broken, or incorporated.

And remember: when the moment comes that the roar finally starts to sound like singing — when the pain in your ears becomes indistinguishable from pleasure — that is not enlightenment.

That is the first sign that your bones have already begun to fuse with the architecture.

Welcome to the instrument.  
Try not to become another note.
