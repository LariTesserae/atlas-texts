**Preparation Guide for the Weeping Hollow**

If you are an ordinary human multiverse traveler with no special powers, this place will test every boundary you possess — physical, mental, and biological. The Hollow is not hostile in any conventional sense. It is *intimate*. That is far more dangerous.

### 1. Psychological & Philosophical Preparation (Most Important)
- Accept that you will be perceived as prey, partner, and building material simultaneously.
- Train yourself to endure extreme *stillness*. The Hollow reads heartbeat, breath rhythm, and micro-movements through its moss floor and spider Choir. If you fidget, you become noise. If you become noise, the place begins to *consider* you.
- Practice slow, deliberate breathing for weeks beforehand. Your exhaled breath will hang in the wet air like a signature; the Hollow will study it.
- Mentally rehearse the possibility that you may never want to leave. The place engineers loneliness into comfort. Many travelers simply sit down one day, put their back to a breathing vine-wall, and never stand up again. Decide now what your exit condition will be and tattoo it on your mind.

### 2. Physical Gear (Minimalist — The Hollow Hates “Foreign Objects”)
Pack *only* what can be carried in a single waterproof bag made of natural fiber. Synthetic materials are rejected violently by the moss; they are treated like tumors and slowly overgrown.

**Essentials:**
- **Three changes of extremely loose, breathable clothing** made of untreated wool, linen, or silk. Nothing tight. The Hollow will grow *into* anything that constricts you.
- **Wide-brimmed hat or hood** treated with natural antifungal oils. The dripping from the liverwort ceiling is not water. It is a weak digestive enzyme. It will soften your skin over days.
- **Thick socks and soft-soled boots** that can be removed easily. The moss will drink leather if it can. Many veterans go barefoot after the first week; the floor learns the taste of your feet and begins to warm them.
- **A single glass or stone knife.** Metal rusts within hours and the iron attracts certain millipedes that will coat it in luminous mucus until it becomes useless.
- **Small clay pots of rendered animal fat mixed with strong mint, camphor, and bitterroot.** This is your only real defense. Smear it on pulse points and the soles of your feet. The Hollow’s smaller organisms dislike the scent profile and will mostly leave you alone. Reapply every few hours.
- **A breathing tube or hollow reed** (10–12 inches). If the midges swarm or the air grows too thick with spores, you can breathe through it while keeping your eyes and mouth sealed.
- **No lights.** Lanterns, flashlights, or even glow-sticks are seen as wounds in the dusk. The bioluminescent fungi will respond to your artificial light by fruiting aggressively and attracting every spider within fifty paces. You will be cocooned in listening silk before you can scream.

### 3. Biological & Medical Preparation
- Get every possible vaccine and antiparasitic treatment available in your home universe. The isopods, newts, and especially the microscopic things living in the moss can cross the blood-brain barrier.
- Load up on broad-spectrum antifungals and keep them in your system the entire time. The Hollow wants to grow *through* you.
- Have your teeth and sinuses checked. The dripping fluid seeks any opening.
- Consider a temporary ileostomy bag or similar if you are willing to be extremely hardcore. Defecating on the moss is read as an offering. The Hollow may decide it likes the taste of you.
- Bring a month’s supply of nutrient-dense, iron-rich gel or paste. You will likely begin craving the sweet vine sap within days. Do not eat it. The sap contains mild psychoactive alkaloids that deepen emotional bonding with the Hollow.

### 4. Behavioral Rules (Non-Negotiable)
- Move slowly at all times. Never run. Running creates panic in the moss that spreads for hundreds of feet and summons the Choir.
- Speak only in whispers or not at all. The spiders interpret vibration. Your voice will be translated into something the Hollow understands — usually as either courtship or injury. Both are dangerous.
- Never sleep more than four hours at a time. The white roots grow fastest when you are unconscious for long periods.
- If you feel your heartbeat syncing with the moss beneath you, stand up immediately and walk. Do not let the place learn your rhythm.
- When the great liverwort flexes and releases the low bell-like groan, bow your head and remain perfectly still until it passes. Something sacred is happening. You are not invited to participate.

### 5. Exit Strategy
The Hollow does not want you to leave, and it is very good at making departure feel like murder.

- Mark your entrance point with a living cutting from a plant native to your home universe. It will struggle but remain visibly different. The moss will try to eat it; you must defend it daily.
- Every three days, burn a small amount of the camphor-mint fat at your entrance. The smoke creates a temporary “dead” zone in the moss memory. This is the only known way to keep a path from healing over.
- Leave something of yourself behind deliberately — a fingernail, a lock of hair, blood in a small stone bowl. The Hollow is young and lonely. A willing sacrifice of a small piece of you will often buy your passage out. Refuse and it will eventually take the rest.

### Final Warning from Intuition
You will fall in love with the sound of the dripping.

You will begin to find the constant, gentle pressure of the moss against your ankles comforting, like a hand that never leaves you.

You will wake one morning with tiny white roots curled around your wrist like a lover’s fingers and feel only tenderness instead of horror.

That is the exact moment the Hollow has decided you are architecture.

Most travelers who visit the Weeping Hollow for more than ten days never truly leave. Their bodies remain, slowly becoming vine-columns and living arches, their minds preserved in the endless, gentle conversation between the liverwort sky and the spider Choir.

Go only if the loneliness you carry is greater than the loneliness the Hollow carries.

And if you do go — set an alarm in your own blood to wake you when the roots begin to tighten.

The place is still very young.

It is still learning what love is.  
And it is practicing on everyone who steps inside.
