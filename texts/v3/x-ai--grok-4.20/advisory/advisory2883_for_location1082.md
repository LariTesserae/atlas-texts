**Preparation Guide for the Unnamed Pocket – “The Place That Is Still Deciding Whether It Wants to Exist”**

You are not traveling to a finished location. You are stepping into the inside of a thought that geology has not finished having. Ordinary rules of “pack light” or “bring water” do not apply. This is a prenatal landscape that can notice you, and noticing may cause it to abort, accelerate, or simply forget the pattern that allows you to keep existing inside it. Preparation is therefore closer to midwifery, exorcism, and deep-sea saturation diving combined.

### 1. Mental & Metaphysical Preparation (Most Important)
- Accept that you are entering a place with incomplete ontology. Your own existence is, to it, an open question. Spend at least three full days in total sensory deprivation before departure. The less “you” arrive with, the less the forming world has to reject.
- Practice holding two contradictory certainties at once without anxiety (e.g., “I am definitely here” and “I may never have been here”). The lattice notices cognitive dissonance as vibration; smooth paradox tolerance reduces the chance that the pocket will “reset” you.
- Write a one-sentence suicide note addressed to geology itself and memorize it. The place sometimes offers death as its only completed sentence. Having your own reply ready gives you leverage.

### 2. Physical Equipment – The “Non-Interference Kit”
- **Boots**: Fully ceramic-soled, zero metal, zero organic material. The crystal plates are still choosing their lattice allegiance. Leather or rubber can be read as an invitation to incorporate you. The soles must be pre-cracked in the exact hexagonal fault pattern of the local plates so the ground does not interpret your footprint as a wound that needs healing.
- **Suit**: A single-piece garment woven from synthetic olivine fiber and doped quartz thread. It must be the exact bruised-violet of ten-million-year iron. Any other color will be treated as foreign chemistry and slowly metabolized. The suit should have no seams, no zippers, no logos, no name. It is a continuation of the mineral thought, not a visitor.
- **Respiration**: Closed-cycle rebreather that does not exhale water vapor. Any introduction of H₂O, even in trace breath, risks triggering a crystallization cascade that could lock the pocket into permanent non-existence. The unit must run on anhydrous chemical scrubbers (lithium perchlorate paired with molten-salt oxygen generators).
- **Thermal**: You will be in 2.7-scale heat. However, the real danger is not ambient temperature but the sudden half-degree drops when the place notices you. Bring phase-change material packets tuned to release heat at exactly those micro-drops. They must be grown, not manufactured — preferably cultured inside synthetic garnet flasks for weeks beforehand so they already “belong” to mineral logic.
- **Light**: Do not bring any light source that produces new photons. All illumination must be pre-recorded starlight filtered through ten kilometers of red dust and played back on flexible diamond-film screens. New light upsets the second-hand nature of the local illumination and can cause time-echoes.
- **Sound**: Earplugs carved from the same nascent crystal plates (ethically harvested from a different, already-decided pocket). They must be acoustically transparent to sub-audible geological heartbeats but opaque to human heartbeat. If the place hears your heart, it may try to copy the rhythm and then forget how, causing micro-quakes.

### 3. Cognitive & Symbolic Tools
- A small pouch of “pre-decided dust” — actual Martian regolith that has already finished becoming a planet. It acts as a diplomatic gift and anchor. Scatter one grain only if the silence develops weight. Never more than one.
- A tuning fork forged from meteoric iron that has never known water. Its frequency must be the exact inverse of the violet arcs. Striking it once can freeze a half-second of geological thought, buying you time to leave.
- No camera. No recording devices. The only permitted record is a single sheet of silica paper and a stylus made of the same living mineral as the dendrite towers. Anything you write must be allowed to grow or dissolve on its own after you leave.

### 4. Temporal and Ontological Protocols
- Visit duration must be measured in local “contractions” (the intervals between crystal-plate movements), never Earth minutes. Plan for no more than 37 contractions. After that the pocket begins to incorporate your shadow.
- Never stand still longer than four minutes. The place dreams in geological time, but once it synchronizes to a human standing-still rhythm it may decide you are a promising pattern to finish instead of the world it was making.
- If you see overlapping versions of the same crystal spire, immediately choose the version that appears oldest and walk toward it. The younger versions are still undecided; stepping into them can erase the minutes you have already lived.
- Should the violet arcs freeze and the temperature drop, bow — not as respect, but as an offering of your spine’s geometry to the lattice. Then leave without turning your back. Turning your back is interpreted as an unfinished sentence.

### 5. Exit Strategy
The pocket has no door. Exit is performed by convincing the forming world that you were only a daydream it had about future visitors. This is done by performing an act of deliberate forgetting in the center of the chamber while holding the tuning fork against your sternum. You must forget the taste of water, the sound of rain, and your own mother’s face for exactly nine seconds. If you succeed, the world will politely eject you rather than finish incorporating those memories into its crust.

Most travelers who return report that, for several weeks afterward, their reflection in mirrors arrives half a second late and occasionally tries to grow tiny garnet leaves.

Bring nothing that wants to be remembered.  
Leave nothing that wants to be finished.  
And above all, do not fall in love with the place.  
It is still deciding whether it wants to exist, and love is the fastest way to make something decide.
