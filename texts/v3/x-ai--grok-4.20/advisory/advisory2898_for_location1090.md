**Preparation for the Ascent of the Hollow Choir**

You are not preparing for a hike, a expedition, or even a conventional extraplanar journey. You are preparing to insert yourself, while still biologically hasty and warm-blooded, into the middle of a geological *dream* that has been dreaming itself for longer than your species has existed. The place does not want you there, nor does it resent you; it simply has not yet noticed that you are a temporary anomaly inside its slow metabolism. Your job is to survive long enough for the mountain to decide whether you are an irritation, a nutrient, or simply another droplet.

### 1. Temporal Reorientation (Most Important)
- You must psychologically and physiologically decelerate. The Choir operates on timescales where a human lifetime is a single wet inhale. Bring no clocks, no watches, no devices that measure minutes. They will drive you insane when their numbers become meaningless.
- Spend at least three weeks before departure living on “Choir time.” Wake only when your body demands it. Eat only when hunger becomes painful. Let your circadian rhythm dissolve. Practice staying perfectly still for six to eight hours at a time while remaining conscious. This is not meditation—it is training so your heart rate does not feel like an obscenity.
- Mentally rehearse the sentence “I am a mayfly standing inside God’s lung” until it stops sounding poetic and starts sounding like basic factual orientation.

### 2. Thermal & Moisture Engineering
- The air is perpetually 3–5 °C and saturated to the point of drowning. Condensation will form faster than you can wipe it away. It will pool in your ears, run down your spine, fill the barrels of any weapons, and eventually begin to mineralize.
- You need a sealed, matte-black hydrostatic suit (like a drysuit fused with a high-end hazmat shell) made of non-breathable membrane. No Gore-Tex. No cotton. No leather. Every seam must be welded. The suit should have internal wicking layers that route condensation to collection bladders you can purge periodically.
- Boots must be thick-soled, thermally insulated, and rigid. The living rock is warm at its core; you will feel it pulsing through the stone like a slow heartbeat. Thin soles will cook your feet over days. The outer surface of the boots should be coated in a sacrificial layer of fluoropolymer— the mineralized mycoid threads will try to colonize anything they touch.
- A heated hood and faceplate are mandatory. The condensation on your eyelashes will freeze when a large singer passes overhead and drops the local temperature another ten degrees. You do not want ice binding your eyelids shut.

### 3. Acoustic & Vibrational Protection
- The sound is not background noise. It is *metabolism*. Prolonged exposure will begin to reorganize the fluid in your inner ear, then your spinal fluid, then possibly the mineral matrix of your bones.
- You will wear active-noise-cancelling earplugs *and* over-ear bone-conduction dampeners tuned specifically to the 0.3–8 Hz range where the great singers operate. These must be hardened against moisture ingress.
- A secondary, low-tech solution: custom-molded wax earplugs mixed with fine lead powder. Crude but effective against the deepest frequencies.
- Bring a tuning fork cut to the exact resonant frequency of your own skull. If you begin to feel your teeth vibrating in harmony with the Choir, strike it against the stone and hold it between your teeth. The counter-frequency can buy you hours of clarity.

### 4. Light Discipline
- Never use artificial light unless you are about to die. The Hollow Choir is not merely dark; it is *photophobic in slow motion*. Sudden photons trigger defensive responses from the living densities.
- Your only permitted illumination is a set of chemiluminescent rods tuned to the exact sick-violet wavelength the cold plasma already uses. They last approximately 72 hours once cracked. Use them sparingly.
- A hooded carbide lantern burning calcium carbide and water is acceptable *only* if you are lost inside a fully enclosed embryonic chamber. The dripping water makes it function poetically well here.

### 5. Biological & Metabolic Preparation
- The pale mycoid lattices are not hostile, but they are *hungry for pattern*. Anything with a repeating biological rhythm (your heartbeat, your breathing, your microbiome) is a potential lattice site.
- You must enter as close to metabolically inert as a living human can manage. Take a long-acting neuromuscular moderator and a drug that lowers your core temperature by almost two degrees. Your resting heart rate should not exceed 38 bpm while inside.
- All food must be pre-digested paste in sealed, sterile tubes. No chewing. Chewing is too loud. No strong smells. Smell is a waveform here.
- You will need an external CO₂ scrubber rebreather. Exhaling warm, moist, mammal air directly into the cold condensation is considered rude by the dream. It may cause localized acceleration of tower growth directly above you—an effect that sounds beautiful until several thousand tons of embryonic basalt decide to finish forming while you are standing underneath it.

### 6. Psychological & Philosophical Gear
- Bring no images of the sun, sky, trees, or fast-moving things. Do not bring music with tempo. The cognitive dissonance can cause permanent dissociation.
- A single object is permitted for psychological anchoring: a small sphere of polished obsidian, perfectly black, that fits in the palm. When the vast singers pass overhead and the dripping stops and the silence becomes a living pressure, you squeeze the stone and remember that you are small, temporary, and allowed to be afraid.
- Write a letter to yourself that begins “You knew this would feel like drowning in cathedrals” and keep it in an inner pocket. Read it only when you start to think the city is singing *to* you. It is not. It is singing *through* you, and that is worse.

### 7. Exit Strategy
The Ascent has no gates. The only way out is to become uninteresting to the dream.

- You must carry three vials of a fast-acting metabolic accelerant that will raise your core temperature, heart rate, and electromagnetic signature dramatically for roughly 45 minutes. When you crack one, the great singers will notice you as an acute infection. They will move to encapsulate and neutralize the anomaly.
- You must already be standing on the exact ridge where the mountain’s memory of “outside” is thinnest. The singers will push you *through* that membrane like a splinter being expelled.
- Do not wait until you want to leave. Decide the exact hour of your departure before you ever set foot inside. The place is very good at making you forget that time exists.

Final warning, traveler:

You will return either profoundly peaceful or no longer entirely human. The condensation that gathers in the hollows of your collarbones and the folds of your ears will never fully evaporate again. For the rest of your life, in perfectly quiet rooms, you will still hear dripping.

Pack accordingly.
