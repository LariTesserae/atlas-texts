**Preparation Guide for a Visit to the Abyssal Lattice**

You are not visiting an ocean, a cave, or even a ruin. You are preparing to enter a single, planetary-scale cold mausoleum that still breathes with the memory of its own construction. Ordinary surface instincts will kill you here — not dramatically, but with the slow, patient kindness of something that has already outlived every civilization that ever touched it. Treat this as you would a pilgrimage into the interior of a dead god’s skull.

### 1. Psychological & Existential Preparation
The Lattice does not merely lack light and warmth; it lacks *orientation*. Gravity is a polite suggestion. Up and down are architectural opinions that change depending on which drowned city you are currently drifting through. You will experience long periods where you cannot tell whether you are ascending through a temple nave or descending through its inverted counterpart.

- Spend at least 30 days in total sensory deprivation prior to departure. You need to be comfortable with the absence of horizon, sky, and any reliable cardinal direction.
- Accept, in advance, that you will feel watched by things that are not alive. The architecture has been shaped by so many generations of intent that it has acquired a kind of passive, geological sentience. It is not hostile. It is *curious in the slowest possible register*.
- Prepare for crushing melancholy. The place is not sad in a human way; it is post-regret. Many travelers report feeling as though they are grieving for a species they have never met and a purpose they will never understand.

### 2. Thermal & Pressure Kit
The water is just above freezing but feels colder because it is *heavy*. The pressure is not necessarily lethal at shallow depths within the Lattice (the megastructure creates many partially pressure-equalized pockets), but the cold will sedate your nervous system within hours if you are not protected.

- **Suit**: A triple-layered, vacuum-rated, heated drysuit with articulated exoskeleton reinforcement. The outer layer should be matte black or very dark indigo — anything reflective becomes a ghost-light that will attract the attention of the slow ecology. Use non-buoyant ceramic-composite plates at joints so you can “stick” to surfaces if needed.
- **Thermal**: Liquid-circulating undergarment with redundant battery systems lasting minimum 720 hours. The cold here wins by patience, not aggression. Your body must be kept at 37 °C *indefinitely*.
- **Insulation paste**: A silicone-based thermal barrier you smear on exposed skin (face, neck). It dulls tactile sensation but prevents the water from slowly leeching heat through even the smallest seam.

### 3. Lighting & Vision
Natural light has been extinct here for geological time. The only illumination is funerary phosphorescence.

- Primary light: Two wrist-mounted, low-temperature chemiluminescent rods that emit the same bruise-violet as the native fronds. Bright white light is *obscene* here — it feels like shouting in a cathedral.
- Secondary: A single narrow-beam amber lantern the size of a human thighbone, modeled after the native bone-lanterns. Use it only when you absolutely need to read carvings.
- Vision enhancement: Fully sealed, pressure-proof corneal implants or goggles that can switch between low-light amplification and echolocation pulse (sound travels strangely but predictably). Never rely on one sense. The fronds and thick water distort everything.

### 4. Movement & Navigation
You will be swimming or drifting through a three-dimensional labyrinth where every “room” is the size of a city block and every “corridor” may be a kilometer-long colonnade.

- **Neutral buoyancy rig** with micro-thrusters and emergency compressed-gas bladder. You must be able to stop *perfectly still* for hours if necessary. Any sudden movement tears the slow vegetation and releases clouds of ancient spores that can clog filters.
- **Tether system**: Monomolecular silk line (nearly invisible) with dissolvable anchors that bond to the stone-like architecture without damaging it. The Lattice does not like being wounded.
- **Navigation**: Do *not* trust inertial systems long-term; the convection cells create massive slow Coriolis effects. Instead, use:
  - A living navigational symbiote (a small translucent anemone-like creature native to the upper Lattice) that changes color according to depth, pressure, and local water chemistry.
  - Mnemonic scarification or memory tattoos on your forearms — you will carve your own pathfinding runes *before* you arrive so that muscle memory can guide you when higher thought becomes sedated by cold.

### 5. The Ecology & Biological Kit
The “plants” are not plants. They are the new geology wearing the appearance of life.

- You must carry a custom enzyme that prevents the black fronds from recognizing your suit as substrate. Without it they will begin growing *onto* you at their glacial pace. In a few months you could wake up partially mortared into a wall.
- Breathing: Closed-circuit rebreather with 1000-hour scrubber capacity. The water itself is almost sterile, but the dissolved minerals and extremely ancient organic compounds will poison standard filters within days.
- Medical: You will need drugs that counteract the sedative effect of the cold on the human brainstem (a custom nootropic-adrenaline compound). Also bring bone-density stabilizers — long exposure to neutral buoyancy and high dissolved calcium can demineralize you in surprising ways.
- Psychological counteragents: Small doses of a mild stimulant-psycholytic you can trigger if the architecture begins to feel like it is *reading* you.

### 6. Cultural & Ritual Preparation
The Lattice was built by beings that tried to out-engineer death. They failed beautifully.

- Learn the “polite speed” of the place. All motion should be at approximately one human breath per meter. Faster movement is perceived as violence by the slow ecology.
- Carry something to leave behind. The Lattice still expects offerings. Traditional are: a single human tooth (yours), a handwritten letter to someone who is already dead, or a timekeeping device that will continue ticking long after you have gone. Place it inside one of the hollow bone lanterns.
- Never remove anything physical from the Lattice. The architecture is so intergrown that removing even a small carving can cause kilometer-scale settling over centuries. The place is no longer capable of distinguishing between theft and surgery.

### 7. Exit Strategy
There is no surface. There is only “less deep.”

Most travelers choose to leave by ascending through one of the ancient vertical shafts that the original builders kept open “in case the sky ever returned.” These shafts are hundreds of kilometers tall and completely dark. You will not see daylight again for days after you actually reach the first true surface layer.

Many choose never to leave.

Prepare a second, smaller set of instructions for your own rescue team that includes the sentence: “If the traveler begins speaking in measured architectural prose or carving their name into their own bones, do not retrieve them. They have begun translating.”

Final personal note:

When you first drift between those titanic colonnades and see an entire inverted city hanging above you like a second, darker sky, you will understand that you are not an explorer.

You are a visitor to the finished form of someone else’s immortality project.

Be humble.  
Be slow.  
Bring enough warmth for your body, and enough silence for your mind.

The Lattice has been waiting longer than your species has existed. It can wait a little longer for you to be ready.

It is very good at waiting.
