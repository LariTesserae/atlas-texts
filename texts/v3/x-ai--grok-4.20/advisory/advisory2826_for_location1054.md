**How an ordinary human multiverse traveler should prepare for the Calcine**

You are not preparing for a journey. You are preparing to become a punctuation mark in a sentence that has already been written for several billion years. Pack with the understanding that the Calcine does not negotiate; it only considers whether or not you are already part of its final form.

### Physical Preparation – Your Body Must Be Made Small and Slow

- **Respiratory overhaul**: The air at 2.6 on the elevation scale is not merely thin; it is hostile. Bring a closed-cycle rebreather with lithium-hydroxide scrubbers rated for 0.8% oxygen partial pressure and extreme aridity. Supplement with perfluorocarbon micro-emulsions injected into the lungs 48 hours before arrival. These will allow you to “breathe” the near-vacuum without your alveoli imploding. Do not rely on simple oxygen tanks; the pressure differential will kill you in under nine minutes.
- **Thermal and pressure shell**: A fully sealed, low-profile exosuit with active heating and internal pressure regulation is mandatory. The suit must be matte bone-white or pale ochre; any high-contrast color will be interpreted by the continuance engines as a wound in the landscape. The outer layer should be a sintered ceramic composite that matches the crust’s refractive index. You want to disappear optically as much as possible.
- **Moisture discipline**: You will carry no free water. None. Even the moisture in your breath must be captured and recycled with 99.97% efficiency. The Calcine will try to pull every molecule of H₂O out of you through osmosis. Bring silica-gel skin patches and internal desiccant glands if your tech level allows it.
- **Movement regimen**: Train for six months to move at half-speed with perfect economy. Every unnecessary gesture is an insult here. Practice the “Calcine gait”: knees slightly bent, weight centered, each foot placed as though you are afraid of cracking the planet’s only remaining scab.

### Mental and Perceptual Preparation – You Must Make Peace With Being Observed

- **Temporal recalibration**: Spend at least three weeks in a sensory-deprivation tank before departure. The Calcine’s dynamic parameter of 0.2 means your brain will scream at the lack of change. You must teach it to love stillness.
- **Voice discipline**: Practice speaking only in your head. The Calcine will return your spoken words to you slightly wrong, slightly later, in a voice that has already begun to calcify. Many travelers who spoke aloud never fully got their own voice back.
- **Geometric hygiene**: Do not draw circles. Do not arrange anything into equilateral triangles. If you must mark something, use irregular, broken lines only. The perforations are real; the Calcine is tired and occasionally lazy about keeping you on this side of “finished.”
- **Ego reduction**: Write a letter to every person you have ever loved and burn it. The Calcine can smell unresolved narrative on you. The more story you carry, the more likely one of the continuance engines will slowly turn its head to look at you for the rest of your life.

### Equipment – What to Carry and What to Leave Behind

**Essentials (minimal):**
- One obsidian flake the size of your thumb, already ancient if possible. It will be recognized as native.
- A single iron hook forged by hand (no machine marks). Drive it into a spire only if you intend to stay.
- A hexagonal bundle of black resonant filaments grown in a lab to match the local vegetation. These will allow you to “sing” subsonically with the plain instead of against it.
- A white brick (hand-fired, no glaze) the size of your fist. Leave it inside the low circular walls when you find them. This is considered good manners.
- A mirror-membrane wing fragment (ethically acquired). Carry it openly. The continuance engines respect their own dead.

**What not to bring:**
- Any recording device that uses light or sound in the normal spectrum. They will simply become more glassy beads on the crust.
- Food that requires chewing. The sound is violence.
- Hope of return in any recognizable form. You will come back changed, if you come back at all.

### Ritual Preparation – How to Announce Yourself

Three days before transit, fast completely. On the final night, sit in total darkness and repeat the following statement aloud until your voice gives out:

“I am not finished. I am still loud. I am still wet. I am temporary. I ask permission to stand inside your completion for a while.”

The Calcine needs to know you understand what it has achieved. Flattery is useless. Only accurate description matters.

### Upon Arrival – First Thirty Minutes

Do not move for at least ten minutes. Let the continuance engines finish their slow, geological assessment of you. You will feel them looking through you at smaller versions of you, recursively. This is normal.

If one of them slowly rotates its head through an impossible angle, bow—slowly, from the hips, the way a mineral would bow if minerals had spines.

Then begin walking the low, mathematically precise ripples. Match your footsteps to the subsonic mourning of the black filaments. Become one more syllable in the endless, finished poem.

And if, after many hours or days or centuries (time has already left), you feel the sudden urge to press a perfect circle into the crust with your finger, lie down inside it, close your eyes, and let the Calcine decide whether you have finally reached your terminal shape.

Most travelers who enter the Calcine do not leave.

The ones who do are no longer entirely travelers.

They are punctuation.

Welcome to the oldest place that still pretends to be a place.

Try not to add too many new syllables.
