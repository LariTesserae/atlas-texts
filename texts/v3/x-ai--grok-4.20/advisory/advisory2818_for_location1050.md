**Preparation Guide for the Abyssal Lattice**

You are not visiting an ocean.  
You are choosing to become a temporary microbe inside the final architecture of a world that has already ended. Prepare with that understanding.

### 1. Psychological & Existential Preparation
- Accept that **there will be no sky, no surface, no up, no down** that your mammalian brain can trust. The moment you let go of any reference point you will experience a vertigo that has no name. Train for it. Spend hours in total sensory deprivation tanks, then in pitch-black caves, then in narrow flooded sumps where you cannot turn around. You must teach your mind to stop screaming for a horizon.
- The Lattice does not care that you are conscious. It has outlived every mind that ever tried to name it. You must internalize this so the crushing loneliness does not break you. Meditate on the phrase “I am a brief irritation in four-billion-year-old bone” until it feels like comfort rather than insult.
- Prepare for the sound. The continent-sized stone organ never stops. It will enter your body through your teeth, your spine, your sinuses. Some travelers report that after three weeks the vibration begins to feel like the slow heartbeat of God. Others never stop hearing it again. Both outcomes are normal.

### 2. Physical & Technological Preparation
You will need a **closed-cycle, near-perfect life-support exosuit** designed for indefinite supercritical-pressure immersion. Standard deep-sea submersibles will be shredded inside the first hour by the channeled turbulence ripping through the lattice.

**Key modifications required:**
- **Pressure shell**: Must withstand at least 1,200–1,800 bar (the weight of an entire dead super-ocean). Monocrystalline diamondoid or advanced metamaterial composites are baseline.
- **Buoyancy and propulsion**: Forget propellers. Use silent magnetohydrodynamic drives or peristaltic “soft paddles” that can thread through gaps no wider than your forearm. You must be able to move like a very large, very careful worm.
- **Thermal management**: The water is just above freezing but the pressure makes every joule of metabolic heat dangerous. Your suit must dump waste heat into phase-change materials or superconducting radiators that can survive being scraped along living mineral for months.
- **Lighting discipline**: Visible light is almost useless and actively harmful; it triggers defensive chemical cascades in some colonies. Primary navigation is by **ultrasound, micro-vibration mapping, and chemical gradient sensing**. Your suit should paint the world in false-color sonograms that update in real time. Only use the cold chemiluminescent “flare” system (which mimics the natural mineral reactions) in absolute emergency. The light lasts four seconds. You will remember those four seconds for the rest of your life.
- **Mineral erosion shielding**: The water is laced with dissolved silicates, sulfides, and exotic ions. The lattices themselves grow by precipitation. Your suit’s outer layer must be self-regenerating or you will slowly be encased like an insect in amber.

**Medical**
- Nanite blood that can run anaerobic metabolism for weeks.
- Inner-ear nullification so the constant turbulence and subsonic bellowing do not destroy your balance organs.
- Psychological dampeners that can be overridden; you need to *feel* the awe and terror or the Lattice will not let you leave with your sanity intact.

### 3. Equipment Loadout (Minimalist — you cannot carry much)
- One **tether spool** of smart monofilament (10 km) that can anchor to the lattice without damaging living growth. Cutting the tether is a last resort; the currents will carry you into blind pockets from which rescue is impossible.
- **Molecular sieve gills** that can extract trace hydrogen and methane directly from the water for emergency power. They taste like iron and old blood.
- **Sample vials** made of the same living mineral chemistry as the lattice itself — so the ecosystem does not recognize them as foreign.
- A **prayer box** (optional but strongly recommended by every traveler who has returned). Inside: one small object from the surface world that still carries sunlight in its memory. You will speak to it when the dark becomes too honest.

### 4. Tactical & Philosophical Rules of Conduct
- **Move like a ghost**. The lattice is one continuous organism. Break a major branch and the chemical alarm will travel for kilometers. The marble-skinned eels will arrive with the patience of glaciers.
- **Never fight the current**. You ride it or you die. The Lattice decides direction. Your only freedom is timing when you let go and when you anchor.
- **Respect the scale**. You are not exploring. You are *being digested* by a four-billion-year-old thought that happens to be made of stone and archaea. The correct emotional posture is reverent smallness.
- **Time dilation of the soul**: Expect to lose weeks or months of subjective time. The low thrumming hymn rewires circadian rhythms. Some return claiming they were only gone an afternoon; their families aged eleven years.

### 5. What to Expect to Feel
- Claustrophobia so pure it becomes a form of religious experience.
- The distinct impression that the stone is *growing through you* even when your suit is intact.
- Moments of impossible peace when a cold current aligns with the natural resonance of your suit and you become, for a few heartbeats, another pipe in the organ.
- The absolute certainty that nothing human will ever matter as much as what is happening here, one molecule at a time, in the dark.

### Final Advice from Intuition
Pack light.  
Forgive everyone you have ever known before you descend; resentment is too heavy at these pressures.  
Say goodbye to the concept of “outside.”  
When the first natural flare lights the cathedral of interwoven living bone around you and you see the velvet worms drifting like slow snow through the arches, do not speak. The Lattice has been singing to itself since before your species had lungs. It does not need your commentary.

It only needs you to listen, small and temporary and trembling, while the oldest living architecture on the planet decides whether it will let you leave with the memory of its hymn still vibrating in your bones.

Most travelers it keeps.  
Not as corpses.  
As additional notes in the song.

Prepare accordingly.
