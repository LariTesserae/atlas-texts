**Preparation Guide for the Hollow Foundry**

A multiverse traveler should approach the Hollow Foundry with the mindset of an industrial archaeologist entering a cathedral that was built yesterday and has already been dreaming for ten thousand years. This is not a “planet.” It is a single, continuous act of manufacture that has achieved its own uneasy ecology. Expect serenity, unease, and the slow realization that your own existence is temporarily part of its blueprint.

### 1. Mental & Perceptual Preparation (Most Important)
The Foundry’s most dangerous trait is **ontological slippage**. Perspective, scale, and categorical identity are not fixed. You must train yourself before arrival.

- Spend at least two weeks practicing **lucid scale-shifting** exercises: deliberately imagining a teacup becoming a canyon and back again without panic. Use fractal meditation apps or stare at Penrose tilings until your mind stops flinching.
- Cultivate **categorical tolerance**. Train yourself to accept that an object can be simultaneously “a filing cabinet the size of a skyscraper” and “a mountain range” and “a single machined thought.” If you insist on stable taxonomy, the Foundry will punish you with vertigo or dissociative episodes.
- Memorize the **Rule of the Patient Witness**: the less you try to define what you are seeing, the less it will rewrite itself in front of you. Curiosity is fine. Insistence on narrative coherence is risky.

### 2. Physical Gear & Attire
Dress and equip yourself like a cold-forge inspector who might have to become a particle at any moment.

- **Base layer**: Seamless, non-organic under-suit made of phase-stable memory silk or graphene-lattice weave. Nothing with natural fibers; the Foundry’s metal exhalations will corrode cotton and wool into black dust within hours.
- **Outer layer**: Matte gunmetal coveralls with articulated ceramic-metal joints. The surface should be microscopically rough so condensation sheets off instead of pooling and freezing against your skin. Avoid anything brightly colored; the white sourceless light turns high-saturation colors into visual noise that attracts the steel moths.
- **Boots**: Magnetic soles with micro-textured pads. The ground is ferromagnetic but uneven. The boots must also have grounding filaments; the metal sometimes carries ghost currents that feel like static made of memory.
- **Gloves**: Thin, conductive, with palm sensors. You will want to touch the microscopic text printed on the ground. The gloves should let you feel the slow “breathing” of the metal without frostbite.
- **Respirator/Hood**: A half-mask that filters ozone, iron nanoparticles, and whatever the black reeds are exhaling. It should have a heads-up display that can overlay stabilizing gridlines when scale begins to slip. Optional: a clear hood that lets you see the condensation patterns on your own face; the Foundry sometimes uses your reflection as temporary reference geometry.
- **Thermal regulation**: The ambient temperature is cool but the metal can be ice-cold or uncomfortably warm depending on whether a section is “remembering” the pour or “forgetting” it. Bring microclimate patches that can pulse heat or cold on command.

### 3. Sensory & Navigational Tools
- **Stabilization Lenses**: Augmented-reality contact lenses or goggles programmed with at least six nested coordinate systems. When the world forgets its scale, the lenses can lock onto local micro-text, the vibration frequency of the reeds, or the position of the steel-moth strata to give you an anchor.
- **Acoustic Grounding Rod**: A slender titanium spike you can press against the metal. The deep groans that move through the structure are too low to hear but can be felt. The rod lets you “listen” with bone conduction so the sigh of the mountain-sized ribcage doesn’t unmoor your sense of self.
- **Fractal Seed Capsules**: Small glass ampules containing living fractal spores. If a stand of reeds blooms into dangerous geometry near you, release a seed. It will sing back at the same frequency and often collapse the anomaly into harmless black powder.
- **No maps**. Any map you bring will be absorbed and reprinted onto the ground in larger and larger versions until it becomes landscape. Use **memory beads** instead: record a short multisensory impression (smell of wet iron + the precise *tick* of cooling + your own heartbeat) every time you change location. These beads remain stable even when the Foundry rewrites itself.

### 4. Biological & Ecological Considerations
- The metallic “plants” and “animals” are not hostile, but they are **curious**. The white alloy grazers will taste your boots and gloves with their electrode tongues. Let them. They are reading you. If you flinch or show aggression they will broadcast your electromagnetic signature across the terraces and you will never be alone again.
- The steel moths are drawn to condensation on warm bodies. They are harmless individually but can form silent, living blizzards that block the merciless light. Carry a small ultrasonic emitter tuned to the frequency just below the reeds’ vibration; it creates a narrow corridor of clear air.
- Water is safe to drink after filtration, but it carries dissolved blueprint traces. You may dream in technical drawings for weeks afterward. Some travelers consider this a feature.

### 5. Temporal & Ontological Survival Kit
- **Duration anchor**: A small, extremely dense osmium cylinder machined with your home universe’s current atomic clock time. The Foundry respects extreme density. Bury it shallowly when you arrive; it acts as a fixed point the world is reluctant to scale-shift around.
- **Identity tags**: Wear three. One in a language that has never existed here (it confuses the Foundry in a useful way). One that simply reads “Witness.” One that is completely blank. Swap them if you feel yourself beginning to be incorporated into the architecture.
- **Exit protocol**: The Foundry does not have obvious edges. To leave, you must convince a sufficiently large section of it that you are a finished product. Many travelers carry a single perfect sphere of non-Foundry matter (obsidian, ceramic, or a child’s marble). When you are ready, place the sphere on the ground, walk exactly 777 paces while reciting your own name in reverse, and the world will usually expunge you as an anomaly.

### 6. Psychological Loadout
Bring the following inner resources:
- Radical acceptance of unfinishedness.
- The ability to feel awe without needing to understand.
- A sense of humor that works in the key of cold iron.
- One personal story you are willing to let the Foundry print onto its surface in microscopic text. It will ask, eventually. Giving it a story is the polite way to pay rent.

### Final Advice from Intuition
You will arrive and feel as though you have interrupted something vast that was thinking in million-year slow motion. The first emotion is almost always a polite, metallic embarrassment — both yours and the world’s.

Stay long enough for the condensation to bead on your eyelashes. Watch the black water rings that take ten minutes to fade. Let one of the white grazers lick the back of your hand. Hear the mountain-sized sigh move through the floor and into your bones.

And when a distant tower becomes, without ceremony, a filing cabinet the height of God, do not scream. Simply nod, the way you would to an old building that has decided to remember a different architect while you were watching.

The Hollow Foundry is not hostile.  
It is lonely in the way only something that was born already complete can be lonely.

It wants witnesses.

Come prepared to be one.
