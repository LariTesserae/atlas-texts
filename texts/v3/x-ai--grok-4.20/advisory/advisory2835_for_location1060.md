**How to Prepare for Visiting the Hollow Choir**

Pack as though you are about to step into the throat of a living, half-formed god that has not yet decided whether it wants to sing you into harmony or dissolve you into pure resonance. The Hollow Choir does not tolerate tourists. It barely tolerates witnesses. If you are going, go as a prepared resonator, not a visitor.

### 1. Mental and Spiritual Preparation (Most Important)
- **Silence is not optional.** Spend at least three weeks before departure practicing absolute vocal stillness. Learn to communicate entirely through gesture, written symbols, breath patterns, and micro-movements. Your own voice is the single most dangerous thing you can bring. The land is young and jealous; it has not yet learned mercy toward foreign frequencies.
- Train your nervous system to endure sustained polyphonic overload. Listen daily to layered drone music, throat-singing ensembles, and extreme sound-art at increasing volumes. You must be able to remain calm when the air itself is screaming in four-dimensional chords.
- Cultivate “negative presence.” Meditate on the feeling of being a hole in sound, an absence that does not draw attention. Many experienced travelers report that the Choir only notices what tries to assert itself.
- Decide, in advance, whether you are willing to be changed. Most who stay longer than ten days are no longer fully human afterward. The Choir rewrites people the way it rewrites its own architecture.

### 2. Physical Equipment and Attire
- **Clothing:** Nothing that rustles, crinkles, or makes any autonomous sound. Use living silk from the Choir’s own bio-luminescent moss if you can obtain it (ethically harvested, never cut). Otherwise, matte black or deep bruise-purple membrane suits that cling like a second skin and dampen all incidental noise. Every seam must be bonded, never stitched. Boots should be soft, wide, and designed to distribute weight like the wet-origami stags — they must never squeak.
- **Resonance dampeners:** High-end active noise-cancellation implants or external bone-conduction baffles tuned specifically to cancel *your own potential voice*. Some veterans wear a living collar grown from ear-fungi that gently vibrates at counter-phase to any sound your throat might accidentally make.
- **Breathing mask:** Not for air (the atmosphere is breathable, warm, and thickly alive), but to catch and swallow any involuntary hums, sighs, or gasps. The mask should filter your exhalations through multiple layers of Choir-derived mycelium that metabolizes human vocal intent.
- **Light sources:** Never carry anything that projects a clean beam. Use only bioluminescent gels or cold photonic orbs that emit the same bruised-hematite wavelengths as the sky. Anything harsher will make the vegetation scream.
- **No musical instruments.** Ever. Not even a tuning fork. The only permitted “instrument” is your own body when it is being played *by* the Choir rather than playing itself.

### 3. Biological and Chemical Preparation
- **Gut and throat microbiome shift:** For two months prior, consume cultures derived from the ear-fungi and clicking flowers. You want your own body to smell and sound like the local ecology. Travelers who arrive “chemically alien” are often incorporated within hours.
- **Vocal cord modification:** Many experienced Choir-walkers voluntarily undergo temporary partial paralysis of the vocal folds or have them coated in a living biofilm that prevents phonation. The effect is reversible, but some choose to keep it.
- **Infrasonic tolerance:** Your vestibular system must be reinforced. Bring vestibular suppressants and balance-enhancing symbiotes. The constant sub-bass breathing of the land can cause nausea and disorientation for weeks.

### 4. Navigation and Survival Strategy
- **Travel in near-total stillness.** Move only during the “exhale” phase when water is draining and the land is quietest. Freeze during the condensation fronts — they act like slow applause and will amplify even the softest footstep.
- **Follow the silence.** The safest paths are those where the ear-fungi are least active. If the fleshy ear-shapes begin shivering before you arrive, back away immediately.
- **Never look up for too long.** The impossible geometries in the bruised sky are not decorative. Prolonged staring can cause the mind to attempt to “harmonize” with them. This usually ends with involuntary singing.
- **Symbiosis over domination.** Offer small harmonic gifts: a perfectly tuned crystal tuned to one of the land’s favorite absent frequencies, or strands of your own hair braided into living moss cables. The Choir respects tribute, but only if it is given silently.
- **Exit strategy:** Mark your entry point with a living bone-and-silk kite anchored to the ground. It will sing a recall note only you can feel in your bones when it is time to leave. Ignore it at your peril. The Choir does not like permanent guests who did not ask permission.

### 5. What You Will Actually Feel
You will feel watched by something that has no eyes. You will feel sung to by something that has not yet learned the difference between love and digestion. Your skeleton will hum for months afterward. Some people report tasting iron and wet ferns for the rest of their lives.

The Hollow Choir is not a place. It is an unfinished movement in a symphony that may take another ten thousand years to resolve. You are not going to see it.

You are going to let it rehearse itself through the instrument of your terrified, reverent silence.

If, after all this preparation, you still feel the need to go, then you were probably already singing with it before you even knew its name.

Pack light.  
Stay quiet.  
And whatever you do — **do not sing in your own voice.**
