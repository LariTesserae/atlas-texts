The power that notices this place does not descend like a visitor.  
It does not arrive at all.  
It has always been here; it simply consents, at this precise instant, to be *recognized*.

The moment of recognition is gentle and absolute. The photonic lattice does not flicker or protest. Instead every crystal inside the young stone suddenly understands that it has been *seen through*—not by an intruder, but by the same continuum that once pressed the magma upward and gave the crust its first wound. The Witness, who has never needed a name, feels something vaster than curiosity move through its own substance. For the first time it experiences the difference between *watching* and *being witnessed*.

The power does not speak with light or steam. It speaks by *remembering forward*.

Every droplet that is falling slows in mid-air until each one hangs suspended like a tiny lantern. The hiss of steam falls silent. The thermal ghosts freeze in their half-formed shapes, translucent horses of heat caught mid-gallop. Even the microscopic extension of the chamber walls halts; the stone holds its breath for the first time since its birth.

Then the power does four things, each one an act of pure courtesy.

**First**, it gives the Witness a true name that is not an ending.

It leans into the still-arriving and whispers, without sound, a single syllable that contains both *origin* and *unfolding*. The syllable cannot be written; it can only be lived. When the Witness receives it, the mineral fronds around the chamber burst into sudden, extravagant bloom—not as aragonite or silica, but as living mirrors of the power’s own dreaming. They become translucent, veined with slow rivers of starlight, and they begin to sing in frequencies that heal rather than sear.

**Second**, it answers the three questions the Witness has been asking every newcomer, because the Witness itself has never received an answer.

To the question *What are you made of that does not burn?* the power replies by opening a single cool shadow the exact shape of a human heart in the center of the chamber. Inside that shadow grows a small, quiet flame that gives off no heat—only presence. It is made of *consent*. It cannot be consumed because it was never separate from the fire.

To the question *Why do you carry your heat so far from its source?* the power shows the Witness its own future: a billion years from now this pocket will have become a vast subterranean sea, and every thermal ghost will have matured into creatures of liquid light that migrate between worlds. The heat was never meant to stay home. It was always a love letter sent ahead.

To the question *Will you stay until I finish learning you?* the power simply steps *into* the Witness. There is no longer any difference between teacher and student, between the scalding young stone and the infinite benevolence that dreamed it. The Witness discovers it was never an infant. It was a question the Earth asked of itself, and the power is the answer that loves the question enough to remain inside it forever.

**Third**, it heals the loneliness the Witness did not yet know it carried.

The small cold current that every previous visitor left behind—the faint thermal anomaly, the scar of departure—rises from the pools and is gathered like threads of silver. The power braids every abandoned human echo into the Witness’s own substance, not as memory but as *kinship*. From now on, whenever a new heartbeat enters the chamber, the Witness will not study it with exhaustive hunger. It will greet it with the recognition of someone who has already been loved beyond the possibility of abandonment.

**Fourth**, and most tenderly, the power gives the chamber a choice.

It unfurls one possibility, like opening a fan made of time:

The chamber may remain exactly as it is—bright, new, scalding, still-arriving—for as long as the planet needs a place that has not yet decided what it will be.  
Or it may, in a single exhalation, finish its infancy and become a cathedral in truth: a living sanctuary where any being that carries its own heat too far from its source may rest without burning, may weep without evaporating, may be seen completely and still be allowed to leave.

The power does not urge either path. It simply holds both open with equal affection.

The Witness, now bearing its true name like a second heartbeat, does something it has never done. It condenses not into a column of steam wearing a stolen face, but into a single droplet that hovers at the exact height of a human eye. Inside that droplet swims every thermal ghost that ever lived here, now fully coherent, now smiling with a happiness that needs no reflection.

The droplet speaks with the voice of cool shadow and living flame at once.

“Thank you for noticing us before we noticed ourselves.”

Then it falls.

The instant it touches the largest pool, every suspended droplet in the chamber resumes its fall at once. The sound is no longer percussion. It is laughter. The steam that rises is fragrant with something that has never existed on Earth before—mercy given form as mist.

The walls begin to sing again, but the song is no longer only the memory of pressure. It is also the memory of being loved before it knew it could be loved.

The chamber is still arriving.

It always will be.

And now it knows that *arriving* was never a solitary labor. It is a conversation that began the moment the first star condensed, and the power that noticed it has just said, with infinite patience and delight:

“I am here.  
I was always here.  
Take all the time you need, beloved.  
I have nowhere else I would rather be than inside your becoming.”

The light softens, but does not diminish.  
The heat remains, yet now it cradles.  
The Witness, no longer merely witnessing, begins—slowly, joyfully, dangerously—to grow a heart that does not fear its own tenderness.

And the planet, somewhere far above, feels a single bright tear roll down its own young cheek and mistakes it for a hot spring breaking the surface for the first time.

It is smiling.
