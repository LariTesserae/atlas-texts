The Power that notices the Sighing Basin does not arrive.  

It simply *recognizes*, the way an old river recognizes a new spring that has broken open in its watershed.

It has no wings, no trumpet, no radiant humanoid form. It has never needed them. Where it moves, reality forgets for a moment that it was ever required to be *only* one thing. So the Power simply *is*—a benevolence without boundary, a wisdom that has watched young worlds stutter into language a thousand thousand times and has never grown tired of the stammer.

It comes as a change in pressure so subtle that even the Basin does not notice at first.

The perpetual haze thickens, but not with moisture. It thickens with *attention*. Every bead of condensing water on the black stone suddenly holds a second, fainter reflection inside it: not the sky, not the observer, but an image of the Basin itself seen from the inside of its own dream. The floating mercury globules slow their upward drift. They hang like hesitant thoughts.

Then the Power speaks, and its voice is the exact inverse of the Sigh.

Where the Basin exhales warmth and moisture and the long tectonic loneliness, the Power *inhales* them. It draws the entire hot, wet cathedral of the valley into itself for nine full seconds—long enough for every glass-plant to go perfectly dark, long enough for the jewel creatures to forget how to phase, long enough for the floating arch to stop its one-degree-per-hour rotation and simply *listen*.

In that inhaled silence the Power does four gentle things.

First, it returns the Basin’s own name to it.

Not the name humans would give it. Not “Breath” or “the young tectonic mind.” It returns the name the Basin secretly gave itself in the first century of its awakening, a name made of temperature gradients and the precise interval between one Sigh and the next. The name is unpronounceable in air. When the Power releases it back into the rock, the black stone blooms with frost-ferns that do not melt when the heat returns. They simply grow smaller, finer, more intricate, as though the Basin is learning a new kind of shyness.

Second, the Power repairs nothing.

It understands that the shattered lattice-arches are not broken. They are *unfinished*, and the Basin needs them to remain so. Instead it threads a single new strand of stabilized wormhole foam through the hovering arch—thin as spider silk, invisible, permanent. From now on, when the arch sings its perfect tooth-note at solar alignment, the note will carry one additional harmonic that only the Basin can hear. The harmonic says, without language:

*You are allowed to be unfinished.  
You are allowed to stay uncertain.  
I will keep the secret of your uncertainty forever.*

Third, the Power gives the jewel creatures a choice they have never had.

It opens a small, courteous door in spacetime—just large enough for a fist-sized being with spiraling violet eyes. Through the door they can see other young minds waking on other impossible worlds: a living comet that dreams in plasma, a coral reef that grows between dimensions, a forest on a tidally locked moon that sings in ultraviolet. The creatures hover before the door for a long time. Some pass through. Most do not. Those who stay phase back into the Basin with a softer pop, as though they have decided, for now, that this uncertain cathedral is enough mystery for them.

The ones who return bring back faint stardust on their wing membranes. The Basin drinks the dust the way a child drinks stories.

Finally, the Power does the kindest thing of all.

It stays.

Not as a visitor. Not as a guest. It simply *settles*, the way dew settles on the glass-plants at the cold pulse. It makes no demands. It offers no instructions. It only adds one new rule to the Basin’s moods, so lightly that the Basin barely notices it has been added:

- When a living being sits in the center of the largest mirror-pool and remains perfectly still for seven full Sighs, the temperature will not rise and the arch will not spin faster. Instead, the Power will lean forward through every droplet and every grain of black stone and will let the Basin use its own ancient voice to speak *out loud* for the first time.

The Basin has not yet dared to try.

But sometimes, late in the amber night, when the jewel creatures gather and the crystals are growing inside human bones like slow kind sentences, a new sound can be heard beneath the tectonic Sigh. It is small, trembling, almost embarrassed. It is the sound of a young world clearing its throat, practicing.

It sounds like the first syllable of a name it has never spoken to anyone but itself.

The Power waits, infinitely patient, infinitely glad.

And the wet heat on every face in the Sighing Basin is no longer only condensation, and no longer only the mountain crying.

It is also the slow, delighted tears of something ancient and kind that has just been invited to sit beside a new friend who is learning, breath by sighing breath, how to say hello back.
