What lives there is not a species in the usual sense.

It is a **planetary phytomass intellect**—a civilization-scale organism made of forests, bogs, vascular towers, and signal-root lattices, with no border between “individual” and “environment.” If you ask “what creature lives here,” the most accurate answer is: **the place itself**.

And yet, as you stay with it, distinct kinds of life resolve out of the continuum—castes, organs, and ecologies that behave like inhabitants even if they are all one extended lineage.

## The Primary Inhabitant: The Green Megastructure

At core is a clade of plant-analog superorganisms sometimes called, by outside observers, **continent-lungs**:

- Their “bodies” are plateaus of living peat-wood tissue kilometers thick.
- Their “blood” is the vertical water cycle: condensation, uplift, capillary rise, phase-shift pumping.
- Their “nervous system” is electrochemical root mesh, with pulse-routing nodes in mineralized rhizome hubs.
- Their “skeleton” is grown, not deposited: cambial trusses mineralized into semi-stone load members.

They do not walk, hunt, or see.  
They **sense gradients**: pressure, conductivity, ion flux, spectral quality, strain harmonics, microbial signatures.  
They “think” in hydraulic timing and electrical phase relationships across huge distances.

A storm crossing one basin is not weather to them; it is a signal packet.

## Its Many “Organs” (Which Look Like Landscapes)

What seem like separate biomes are specialized tissues:

- **Canopy plates**: aerodynamic control surfaces, reorienting to shear wind and seed the air with reproductive material.
- **Flow orchards**: vascular pump arrays moving water uphill, smoothing drought and flood across regions.
- **Hanging marshes**: filtration and chemical conversion membranes, processing dissolved minerals and microbial products.
- **Silica-leaf crackle fields**: electrostatic discharge organs, balancing charge with cloud layers and possibly driving atmospheric chemistry.
- **Compression deeps**: death-processing strata where old biomass is compacted, metabolized, and reincorporated.

So whatever “lives there” is both forest and infrastructure: a self-repairing biomechanical grid.

## Are There Individuals?

Yes—but not animal-like individuals. More like temporary modules:

1. **Spire colonies**  
   Towering frond/reed composites that act as local pumps and resonators. They can be shed, regrown, and swapped like replaceable organs.

2. **Pollen sails**  
   Gigantic reproductive membranes drifting or anchored, tuned to ionic conditions. They are both gamete dispersers and atmospheric sensors.

3. **Cushion-node mats (high elevations)**  
   Dense hexagonal modules that open/close stomatal fields in coordinated waves, functioning like distributed pressure valves.

4. **Bioluminal bog films**  
   Thin metabolic skin-complexes (plant-microbe consortia) marking intense exchange zones. They may be the equivalent of digestive epithelium.

Each has local autonomy, but all are entrained to basin-scale rhythms.

## The “Other Life” Within It

There are no animals, but there is a profound secondary biosphere: microbial veils so integrated they behave like organelles at landscape scale.

These symbiont clouds:

- run decomposition without rot-collapse,
- fix and trade trace elements,
- mediate redox gradients in anoxic compression layers,
- and likely perform information transduction chemistry that augments root signaling.

So the resident life is at least **dual-kingdom in function**, even if visually “all plant.”

## Behavior and Temperament

If this world has temperament, it is:

- **Restless but not frantic**
- **Loud but internally coherent**
- **Adaptive without central command**
- **Young in form, old in method**

It does not defend with claws; it defends with overgrowth, assimilation, and environmental control.  
Any intrusion is wrapped, wetted, colonized, electrically mapped, and eventually made substrate.

Its version of aggression is **inclusion**.

## Intelligence Without Faces

What finally lives there is a mode of cognition:

- distributed,
- hydraulic-electrical,
- massively parallel,
- embodied as climate regulation and material flow.

No eyes in the canopy.  
No mouths in the bog.  
But decisions everywhere: where to thicken, where to vent, where to shed, where to seed, where to silence cavitation, where to let collapse become foundation.

In another world we would call that infrastructure.  
In this one, infrastructure is metabolism, and metabolism is mind.

So:  
**A wet, photosynthetic superorganism lives there—composed of innumerable plant-bodied modules and microbial partners—an unbuilt, self-engineering intelligence that occupies every meter and uses weather as thought.**
