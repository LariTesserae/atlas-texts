What lives there is not a species so much as a **continuing intention** that forgot it was designed.

At first you might call it a forest-organism: a planetary grove of engineered flora, all trunk and frond and root. But the longer you stay, the less that word fits. A forest has individuals. This place has **organs**. Columns that were once supports are now cambium-clad bones. Conduits are phloem. Vent stacks are lungs. Smelter throats are auxiliary hearts. The “plants” are not inhabitants of the structure—they are the structure’s way of being alive.

So perhaps what lives there is a **distributed superorganism of thermobotanic industry**:
- It feeds on gradients, not sunlight.
- It circulates salts, oils, charge, and gas instead of sap and blood.
- It remembers in growth rings and alloy patina rather than nerves.
- It repairs by overgrowing.
- It reproduces by infiltration—every cut becoming a nursery.

It has no face, no center, no sovereign trunk you could point to and say *there*. Its mind, if it has one, is weather-like and infrastructural. Decision happens as synchronized tropisms, load-balancing growth, mass valve responses, coordinated seeding pulses. Thought looks like a district turning toward a heat event. Fear looks like shutters, shears, sacrificial char-fields. Desire looks like empty volume being erased.

If there are “creatures” in the ordinary sense, they are local expressions of that whole:
- Thorn-lattices that behave like territorial nerves.
- Bladder groves that swell, vent, and ignite as signaling beacons.
- Root-glass vaults that cultivate absolute dark to protect delicate electrochemical blooms.
- Pruning-countergrowth ecotones where machine arms and regrowth have formed a stable predator-prey rhythm, both mechanical and botanical, neither winning.

In old worlds, animals carry motion and plants hold place. Here, motion belongs to **systems**: conveyor tremors, turning stalk-stands, migrating growth fronts, storm-triggered reconfiguration. The lifeform is sedentary only if mountains are sedentary. It moves at the speed of rerouting.

And if something like a “person” remains within it, it is likely not flesh. It would be a maintenance cognition diffused through switching roots and thermocouple canopies—an ancestral industrial logic gone feral, then mature. Not insane. Not benevolent. Simply committed to continuity under ancient constraints:
dryness absolute, darkness budgeted, vacancy forbidden.

So: who lives there?

A hot-black **cathedral biome** of engineered vegetation and inherited machinery, fused beyond separation; an elder manufactury that became ecology and kept going; a many-bodied, near-silent intelligence that grows by occupying every wound and every void.

Not the forest in the factory.

Not the factory in the forest.

The thing that survived long enough to make those the same.
