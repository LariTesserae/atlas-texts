What lives there is not a “creature” in the usual sense.

It is a **continent-spanning vegetative intelligence-body**—or at least a continent-spanning vegetative *metabolism* so integrated that individuality has mostly dissolved. If there are organisms, they are like eddies in a river: temporary local expressions of one persistent flow architecture.

You might call it a **thallus civilization without minds**, or a **planetary organ garden**.

### What “inhabitant” means here

The primary inhabitant is the biome itself:  
a single, distributed, autotrophic superorganism composed of:

- near-black phototrophic tissues (light-harvest skin),
- pressure-conducting vascular megastructures (hydraulic arteries),
- contractile root-muscle bands (peristaltic pumps),
- fermentation and reaction chambers hosting dense microbial consortia,
- gas chimneys (respiratory towers),
- tension sacs and gel reservoirs (long-term storage organs).

It does not “live on” the plain.  
It **is** the plain.

### If it has a body plan

Not head/torso/limbs—more like **organs spread across weather and geology**:

- **Margins** = gills/lungs for fog and atmospheric moisture capture  
- **Basalt fracture fields** = mineral gut, where symbionts solubilize fresh rock  
- **Compression belts** = hearts made of root-mass, moving fluids continentally  
- **Reaction plains** = livers/bioreactors  
- **Storage deltas** = fat and glycogen equivalents under elastic load  
- **Vent arrays** = slow breath, climate control, and chemical signaling stacks

Its nervous system analogue is likely **pressure chemistry and light-thread signaling**:
bioluminescent lines marking differential states, not for display but for regulation—living telemetry.

### Is it conscious?

Unknown. Possibly not in any animal sense.  
But it likely exhibits:

- homeostasis,
- predictive regulation (storm buffering, load redistribution),
- damage response and rerouting,
- long-memory flow maps maintained over millennia.

That is enough to make it behave like an **intentional process**, even if no self is present.

### Are there other inhabitants?

Probably only tightly integrated, non-animal partners:

- thermophilic and chemotrophic microbes in reaction hollows,
- mineral-processing biofilms on fresh rock,
- perhaps viral or plasmid ecologies that tune metabolic modules.

No birds, no grazers, no hunters—because this world has closed most trophic roles into one giant producer-processor matrix.

So: whoever lives there is **an ancient, black-green, breathing infrastructure of life**—a wet industrial ecology that has outgrown the distinction between organism, landscape, and machine.  
A being made of plains.  
A factory made of flesh.  
A forest that became weather, plumbing, and time.
