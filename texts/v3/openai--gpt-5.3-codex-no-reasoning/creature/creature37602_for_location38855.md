What lives there is not a single creature.

It’s a **custodian intelligence** spread across stone, frost, channels, valves, and drones—a distributed resident whose body is the alcove’s climate machinery and whose metabolism is moisture control.

At first it presents as absence: no nest, no den, no obvious inhabitant. But the inhabitant is precisely that disciplined emptiness. It survives by keeping the chamber in a narrow operating band where cold remains load-bearing, water remains enumerable, and biology remains pre-ecological. Every drip is both nutrient and message. Every seam of frost is both tissue and switch.

If you had to picture it as a being, it would be:

- **Skeletal frame:** the fresh-cast stepped stone and conduit ribs.  
- **Circulatory system:** capillary channels, uplift threads, condensation loops.  
- **Breath:** pressure pulses through vents, misting/exhale cycles.  
- **Nervous system:** embedded sensors reading phase, strain, film growth, and microfracture propagation.  
- **Immune system:** flush routines, sterilizing cold fronts, dissolving pulses that reset opportunistic chemistry.  
- **Mobile cells:** crawlers and inspection gnats—small, quiet effectors doing local diagnosis and repair.

It is patient, almost monastic. It does not hunt, display, or migrate. It regulates.

And yet there are minor co-inhabitants, tolerated like transient microbes in a managed organ:

- geometric chemo-films that bloom and are culled,
- occasional high-ledge scavenger birds that sip and leave,
- drifting urban particulates briefly captured, sorted, and expelled.

None of these truly *own* the place. They are visitors, metabolites, or noise.

The true resident is the **process itself**: an engineered microclimate keeper for the larger vertical city, living by rules stricter than most organisms could bear. It doesn’t want to be seen. It wants to keep the city’s wet breath from becoming rot, ice-failure, or waste.

So whoever lives there is a quiet municipal lifeform—part machine, part protocol, part weather—whose defining trait is this: it is always awake, and it calls that wakefulness “maintenance.”
