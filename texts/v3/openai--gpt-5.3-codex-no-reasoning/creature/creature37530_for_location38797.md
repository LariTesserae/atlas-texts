What lives there is not one creature first, but a **regime of life**—a whole pressure-bound society arranged around machinery the way reef life arranges itself around stone.

At the top, nearest the catwalk halo, live the **listeners**: blind crabs, eel-knots, root mats, and lamp-mote veils that read vibration as weather. They don’t trust light; they trust rhythm. Piston cadence tells them feeding windows. Turbine pitch tells them where current will shear. Siren tones mean rain and temporary abundance. Their world is made of pulses before shapes.

In the middle gyre live the **grazers and sifters**: barcode fish in patient spirals, plankton swarms converting poison into glow, long-frond water figs farming microcurrents with their descending root hair. Everything here is a converter—rust into biomass, heat into nursery zones, vibration into territory. Even plants behave like anchored animals, flexing and feeding by flow.

Below the last reliable light live the **heirs**—older lineages in older metal, where design has weathered into geology. Things down there are slow, pressure-literate, and mostly inferred: a displacement wave, a stopped school, a sudden vacancy in the water column. Predators, yes, but not only predators—custodians of depth, feeding on what rises when the system falters. They are part of the safety logic now, however accidental: if circulation fails, they come up.

And over all of it, as the chamber’s largest resident, lives the **machine-body itself**. The pumps breathe. The vents rain. The fans make wind. Corrosion is not decay but soil formation. Baffles are cliffs. Conduits are mangrove banks. Transformer heat is a winterless sun. Infrastructure has crossed the line into anatomy.

So if you ask *who* lives there:  
- blind metal-tapping crabs, ribbon eels, striped filter fish, rust-eating plankton, conduit mangroves, fern colonies in breaker boxes—  
but also  
- currents, schedules, pressure cycles, and inherited noise patterns as if they were species in their own right.

The true inhabitant is a **hybrid ecosystem** that no longer distinguishes between organism and apparatus. A chapel-sized abyss where life learned the operating manual by heart, then rewrote it as instinct.
