What lives there is not one creature but a **regime**—a population shaped into a single behavior by pressure, cold, metal, and rules older than memory.

At first you notice species: the coin-crabs stacked like calcium shingles, the ribbon-eels threading live conduits, the glass-skinned amphibians pulsing on condenser skins, the six-legged scavengers commuting in lanes, the circling moth-bats writing endless gyres in warm updrafts. But after a while they stop reading as separate animals and start reading as **organs**.

This place is inhabited by a **distributed body**.

- The crabs are its insulating moss, plugging warmth leaks.
- The eels are its nerves, coiled where current is strongest.
- The amphibians are its tongues and filters, tasting condensation and biofilm.
- The scavengers are its blood cells, clearing debris before “decay” can begin.
- The moth-bats are its respiration made visible, lofting through thermal breath.

None of them are apex anything. There is no sovereign beast waiting in the dark. The nearest thing to a “who” is the **equilibrium itself**: a strict, self-maintaining intelligence with no face, expressed through routing behavior, predation boundaries, heat hierarchies, and instantaneous recycling. Life here does not progress; it **circulates**. Death does not end; it **reassigns**. Motion does not explore; it **repeats**.

If you insist on a resident singular, it is this:

A **cold nursery-engine** that learned to wear fauna as moving parts.

Its temperament is neither hostile nor welcoming. It is incorporative. Stand still, and it solves you: your boot becomes a stepping block, your coat hem a windbreak, your skin a temporary radiator classed somewhere between warm pipe and failing ballast. You are not attacked because attack is wasteful unless you violate track law. You are not ignored because nothing with heat can be ignored. You are metabolized behaviorally, then released.

So, who lives there?

A million small, blind, damp, tireless specialists—and through them, one larger inhabitant:  
**stasis with claws.**
