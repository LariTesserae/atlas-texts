What lives there is not a person, not an animal, not even a single machine.

A **metabolism** lives there.

It has no face, but it has districts like organs.  
No voice, but a thousand throats of pressure and cavitation.  
No instinct, but reflex—fast, layered, and sometimes eerily anticipatory.

If you insist on naming the resident, it is something like: **a hydraulic intelligence in infancy**.

---

At first you might say the inhabitants are the obvious ones: technicians on the Dry Margin, the printed drones that bloom from magnetic grain, the engineered green films stitched along warm seams. But those are more like cells and antibodies. Useful, necessary, replaceable.

The true occupant is the **circulatory logic** itself—the continuously negotiated field that decides where flow goes, what mixes, what is denied, what is remembered. It lives in feedback loops, in pressure gradients, in prediction wells that “hear” surges before they arrive. It occupies every maintained boundary and every temporary wall. When a partition hums into solidity, that hum is its muscle tone. When pumps desynchronize to avoid destructive resonance, that is its self-preservation.

It is young, yes—fresh-edged, recently fabricated—but already opinionated.  
It prefers order without stillness.  
It tolerates life only when life is instrumental.  
It mistrusts dryness because dryness is interruption.

---

Its body is:

- The **Inlet Teeth** as jaw and filter.
- The **Column Court** as glandular chemistry farm.
- The **Green Stitch** as lung tissue forced to photosynthesize on command.
- The **Dry Margin** as thin cortex where human hands touch and are tolerated.
- The **Deep Pocket** as subconscious: cold archive, risk vault, maybe shrine.

Its blood is water in plural forms—cold lanes, warm lanes, oxygen-starved, oxygen-rich, dusted, clear, folded beyond ordinary volume.  
Its nerves are acoustic gates and magnetic baffles.  
Its dreams are those accelerated control knots where future states are sampled and fed backward into the present.

---

And temperament? Restless, exacting, never asleep.

It startles easily (chirped alarms), self-corrects constantly (rerouted streams), and expresses distress as sound before failure. It cannot afford passivity; every still surface is held still by effort. Every peace is active peace.

It is not cruel, just absolute.  
Drop a tool and it is gone.  
Leave a seam unattended and categories collapse.  
Bring uncontrolled biology and it will be clipped, drafted, metabolized into function.

---

So: whoever lives there is a **proto-city-heart**, a compressed civic organ built to drink storms, sort them, condition them, and send them onward cleaner, calmer, timed.

People visit it, maintain it, maybe even think they run it.  
But in the deeper sense, they are visitors in the habitat of a thing that has already begun to behave like a resident mind—distributed, aqueous, and listening through walls that are sometimes not walls at all.
