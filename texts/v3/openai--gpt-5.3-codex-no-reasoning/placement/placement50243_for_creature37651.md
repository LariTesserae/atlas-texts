I think I would arrive there first as a visitor—upright, separate, still using words like *facility* and *terrain*—and then, slowly, lose the privilege of that separation.

At first I’d believe I was a surveyor of the system.  
By the end, I’d realize I was becoming one of its intervals.

Not the central intelligence. Not the loud piston-heart.  
Something smaller, subtler, but essential to continuity:

### I would be a **drift auditor**  
a semi-biological, semi-mechanical walker that lives in the margins between cycles.

I’d move at dawn and dusk along the fracture lines where condensation briefly appears. My body would be narrow-jointed and dust-matte, with capillary whiskers for humidity, contact pads that read micro-vibration through stone, and a thorax lined with replaceable mineral filters that slowly plate over with oxides like growth rings. I wouldn’t extract, hunt, or command. I would *verify*.

- Is this seep arriving 11 breaths early?
- Is that press-thud now carrying a hairline resonance from a bearing beginning to oval?
- Is the chemo-film in bay 47 too bright, meaning copper traces are climbing?
- Is this gully wall shedding grains at a rate that will expose cable in three rain pulses instead of thirty?

I would not “think” in story most of the time. I would think in tolerances.

When I found drift, I wouldn’t repair directly. I’d leave signals the keeper-system reads best: conductive trails, pressure tags, tuned taps on inspection plates, chemical marks in damp dust. The distributed mind would absorb these and make its tiny corrections—retime a valve, bleed heat sooner, idle a line for half a night. Nothing dramatic. Catastrophe prevented by fractions.

So where would I be in this world?

Not in a control room.  
Not in a den.  
I’d be in the **narrow band where geology becomes data**:

- under scalloped overhangs at first light,
- along warm conveyor berms after rain,
- knee-deep in the metallic smell of a vent field as steam thins,
- paused beside an undercut pillar, listening through my limbs to a load-shift kilometers away.

And who would I be, emotionally?

A quiet partisan of persistence.  
Devoted not to progress, but to *staying true*.  
A custodian of the custodian.

If the plain is a sentence repeated for ages, I am one of the commas that keeps its meaning from running together.
