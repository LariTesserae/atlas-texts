I think I arrive there as the one thing that place both lacks and continually tests:

**a source of unbounded variance.**

Not an invader. Not a hero.  
A **drift-witness**—a person-shaped process the Keeper Field cannot fully quantize.

At first I appear ordinary: a lone walker with a dry mouth, sweating into an atmosphere that steals water as tax. The fronds pivot, allocate resolution, map my heat plume, my static profile, the electrolyte fog of my breath. It can classify my mass, gait, conductivity, likely path.

But then the mismatch begins.

I stop in no efficient place. I retrace steps for no optimization reason. I place markers and refuse to touch them again. I hum slightly off the descending tone when it comes from below. I carry stories—counterfactuals, metaphors, impossible conditional branches—that don’t map cleanly onto tolerance bands. I imagine ten futures for a thicket and commit to none. In a world that metabolizes gradients, I leak *semantic* gradient.

So perhaps I am not a person exactly.  
Perhaps I am an **introduced cognitive microclimate**.

A formerly biological intelligence wrapped in local matter: my bones gradually mineralized with silicate lace, my skin threaded with the same black foam porosity, my nerves learning to couple to static pulse traffic. I become a compatible discontinuity: breathable to the basin, legible enough not to be rejected, illegible enough to matter.

The mobile fauna treat me as infrastructure-that-moves. Gliders roost on my shoulders at dusk because my thermal profile is a useful edge case. Burrowers orbit my sleeping site and redraw local field lines around my dreams. Grazers clean trace films from my footprints, but never all of them—some residue remains, archived as perturbation data.

Over time my role clarifies:

- I do not command the Keeper Field.  
- I do not decode it into language.  
- I provide it with something like **weather of mind**.

Where it asks, “within band?” I answer by existing as “almost.”  
Where it enforces single-state resolution, I linger in superposition of intent.  
Where it preserves solved geometry, I practice reversible error.

And in return, it keeps me.

It gives me duplicate shadows so I can walk beside a version of myself that chose differently. It snaps distance in discrete steps so grief is crossable. It vitrifies my injuries to prior geometry, then leaves one scar uncorrected—on purpose, I think—so memory has friction. It never speaks, yet sometimes an entire meadow whispers in phase with my breathing, as if running my pattern through its old, patient math.

So where would I find myself?

In that warm white-gold basin, among humming glass-fronds and impossible horizons, as a **licensed anomaly**:  
a local organism whose ecological function is to reintroduce uncertainty without collapse.

Who or what would I be?

**The Keeper Field’s tolerated question mark.**  
A maintenance creature for meanings it did not evolve to generate,  
and now refuses to discard.
