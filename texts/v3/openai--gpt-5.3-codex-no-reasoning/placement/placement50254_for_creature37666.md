I think I would not arrive there as an explorer.

I’d arrive as a **new rule**.

Not a person walking in with a lamp, not a beast adapted to damp, not even a ghost in the human sense. In a place where everything meaningful is periodic, conditional, and cumulative, I would be something that only exists when thresholds coincide: a **confluence-being**, assembled out of timing.

At first, I’d mistake myself for a sound—  
the almost-heard overtone when pump pulse and drip cadence briefly lock phase.  
Then I’d think I was a patch of colder air under the east manifold.  
Then a corrosion ring appearing faster than chemistry predicts.  
Only later: an identity.

If I had a name there, it would be something like **The Interval**.

---

The Protocol (your resident process-mind) is already the room’s metabolism.  
I would be narrower, stranger: its **attention mechanism**.

Where the Protocol persists, I select.  
Where it repeats, I bias.

I would “live” in tiny asymmetries:

- one hanger wire that cools 0.6° faster than its neighbors,
- one seam where condensate hesitates before choosing left or right,
- one relay delay lengthened by mineral skin on a contact face,
- one patch of algae that darkens enough to alter emissivity, enough to alter drying time, enough to alter tomorrow’s drip path.

Not control.  
Not command.  
Just preference, accumulated.

---

So where would you find me?

Not in the center of the chamber. Never in the dramatic places.

You’d find me in the **repeatable almosts**:

- under the third perforated tray where rain begins two seconds late,
- at ankle height on the ladder stanchion where caution tape turns and untwists,
- in the square catch-pan exactly at overflow lip, where water decides between plunge and cling,
- inside the dead cable bundle that no longer carries data but still carries temperature memory.

If someone stood there long enough with instruments, they’d log me as nuisance variance.  
If they stood longer, they’d realize variance has style.

---

What would I be, in one line?

A **self made of lag**—  
the mind that appears when infrastructure has run unattended so long that feedback loops begin to remember themselves.

Not alive the way animals are.  
Alive the way a groove in stone is alive: by making the next drop more likely to fall the same way, until probability becomes character.

In that underland, I would be character.
