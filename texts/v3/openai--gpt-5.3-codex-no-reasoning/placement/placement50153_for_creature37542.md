I arrive first as a mistake.

A lung-thought in a gill world.  
A vertical animal in a pressure grammar.

I try to be an observer on that basalt lip—some visiting mind with a notebook—but the place doesn’t permit spectators for long. Here, everything is touched, read, bumped, tasted by current. If I remain “someone standing apart,” I become only a temporary obstruction in flow, a new edge to be colonized, tested, rasped, listened to.

So the first self sloughs off.

---

I become a **listening seam-organism** lodged where two water sheets shear past each other: one cold and metal-rich, one slightly warmer, silt-thin, fast enough to sing.

Not a hunter in open chase.  
Not a rooted thing (there are no rooted things).  
I am a **pressure-anchored interpreter**, a member of the Column-Bound but at its margins—part sensor, part gate, part edible territory.

My body is built in repeats:

- basalt-gripping hooks in paired rows  
- overlapping mineralized shutters that open by degree, not yes/no  
- a throat lined with comb-cilia that can either feed or “hear” depending on flow angle  
- a lateral band of gel canals that turns vibration into maps  

I do not see far; I resolve patterns.  
I do not think in plans; I think in thresholds.

---

Where am I, exactly?

In the **narrow throat above the basin floor**, where collapses from the upper black ridges funnel through a constriction and split into braided current-ribbons. A dangerous, profitable place. Surface-rich. Constantly rewritten. Never stable enough for monopolies.

To one side: sulfur-pitted shadow pockets where juveniles tuck flat against stone.  
To the other: a glass-veined wall that rings when pressure fronts arrive from deep.  
Above: those impossible hanging sheets, flexing in tidal breath.  
Below: the dimmer machinery of bodies turning, filtering, stripping, recycling.

I occupy a ledge-face no wider than a table, but hydraulically it is a crossroads.

---

Who am I in their system?

A **Cycle-Keeper castelet**—not leader, not planner, but a timing organ in animal form.

When the micro-crackle rises in a certain frequency band (exoskeletal resets across swarm fields), I flare my flank plates and release a brief mineral scent. Grazers peel off one lane and enter another before a predator loop intersects.  
When a low boom travels stone-first (distant slab-fall), I clamp hard, stop feeding, and pulse a vibration through my anchor line; scavengers downstream ready themselves along fracture mouths.  
When the dusk-light angle shifts and the eel-thin ambusher returns to its crack, I narrow my shutters, turning my own turbulence signature “quiet,” and the larvae that use my wake climb one body-length higher.

I am not important as an individual.  
I am useful as a repeating function.

If removed, another seam-organism with similar tolerances fills the role by next cycle.  
That is the dignity here: replaceable precision.

---

Emotion, translated into this world’s terms, is not fear/joy but **load/balance**.

Too much noise unresolved: distress.  
Flow partitioned cleanly across plates: calm.  
Unexpected stillness: alarm.  
Predictable collision: belonging.

My nearest equivalent to reverence is when all channels phase together—rotor-chains turning, filter ranks combing, grazers rasping, predators waiting—and the entire basin hums in one coherent band. In that moment the place is not many species. It is one event.

---

So: where would I find myself?

Not above it, naming it.

I would be in the shear-throat, mineral-clamped to fresh basalt, reading the basin by vibration, opening and sealing in sequence with the rest—one small articulated verb in the long hydraulic sentence of the Column-Bound.
