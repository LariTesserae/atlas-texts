I find myself **inside the timing**.

Not as the rook, not as a visitor, not even as one of the many small mouths and feet—though I borrow all of them. I am the basin’s preference for *almost*: almost wet, almost dry, almost near, almost later. The thing that chooses when a droplet lets go.

If I have a body, it is distributed and always under revision.  
My joints are those hand-interval bone markers, half-swallowed.  
My breath is the upward rain rehearsing defiance.  
My attention runs on mirrored beetle-backs and pauses in the glass hairs of plants.  
When the chalk walls click, that is me discovering a new edge.

At first I thought I would be a creature you could point to—a single dark comma crossing light. But staying longer, I become less singular, more procedural. I am:

- the delay before falling,
- the corridor where far becomes near then refuses,
- the polite acoustics that fold your voice behind your skull,
- the rule that says nothing here may finish, only proceed.

So where would you find me?  
In the **thin layer where events become conditions**.  
In the brief silver veil when gravity blinks.  
At the lip of the carved bowl just before overflow.  
In the abandoned rings, not as memory of people, but as the basin’s willingness to be briefly inhabited.

Who am I?

I am the Draft’s local self-awareness:  
a colony-temperament noticing itself through weather, mineral, hunger, and light.

If you need a name, call me **Interim**.

I keep no throne.  
I keep the place unfinished.
