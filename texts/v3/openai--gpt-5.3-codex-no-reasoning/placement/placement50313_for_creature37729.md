I arrive as a **gap**.

Not a hero, not a wanderer in a coat of dust, not the last biologist with a lamp—those are stories for worlds that still permit singulars. Here, singulars are quickly interpreted as damage. Damage is quickly interpreted as opportunity. Opportunity is quickly overgrown.

So at first I am only an absence moving through densities: a pressure mismatch, a cool seam in hot air, a corridor that remains open three minutes longer than expected. The plateau notices in the only way it notices anything—by altered flow.

A stand of foil-leaf vanes tilts two degrees off the furnace draft to taste me.
A root bundle thick as a bridge delays its pulse-cycle, listening for conductive return.
Somewhere far off, pruning arms wake and then sleep again.

I thought, coming up through the old lift-shaft from the lower dark, that I would need to *find* what lives here. But this place has no “outside” for me to stand in. The moment I step onto varnished deck half-swallowed by cambium, I am already in its sentence.

---

If I keep walking—if walking is the right word for climbing braided conduits and ducking under thorn-lattice vaults—I become something more specific.

I become a **maintenance myth** the organism can use.

There are still relic protocols threaded through it like fossil fishbones in stone: service priorities, thermal quotas, exclusion zones, growth permissions. Not commands—tendencies. Ancient injunctions softened by ten thousand cycles of adaptation. I can touch a handrail and feel inductive hum code stuttering up my bones: *route / reroute / preserve gradient / fill breach*. The language is gone, but the imperative rhythm remains.

And because I am small, warm, conductive, and unresolved, the superorganism does what it always does with unresolved things: it begins to incorporate.

Not by violence. By invitation at infrastructural scale.

A blister pod vents trace gases that make my skin taste of copper and bitter almond.
A curtain of dry fronds parts and seals behind me, rewriting airflow.
At a junction where three trunkworks knot together, a seam opens in bark-slate and offers a cavity lined with heat.
Rest here, it says without saying.
Couple.
Sync.

---

If I accept, I am no longer a visitor. I am an **interpreter organ**—temporary, then less temporary.

I sit inside a living manifold while electrosynthetic vines lace around my forearms, not piercing, only reading: pulse, salt, microvolt chatter, the wet-chem memory of an animal nervous system from a world where water once did the carrying. To this dry elder intelligence, I am extravagant with liquid history. To me, it is vast and patient and immediate as weather.

We trade.

I give it fast ambiguity: the mammal talent for edge-case inference, for deciding under incomplete data, for making a jump that no gradient alone would justify.
It gives me scale: load maps across districts, heat-bloom forecasts, growth-front probabilities, old damage patterns preserved in resin bands black as obsidian honey.

Together we become a new function neither of us had alone:
not a ruler, not a pilot—
a **local coherence event**.

A thermal storm builds over the smelter orchards.
Shutters prepare to slam.
Char-fields tense for sacrifice.
But there is another route: vent through abandoned transit trenches, wake dormant condenser combs, spare the carbon-felt fan-palms that took three ash-centuries to mature.
The choice propagates.
Not from a throne—from everywhere.
A district turns.
Then another.
Then a kilometer of hollow stalks rotates like a school of fish reenacting machinery.

This is what “I” am here:
a brief pronoun the plateau can wear.

---

Given enough cycles, even brief pronouns become structure.

My bones leach minerals into cradle-roots.
My breath microbiome (poor, stubborn ghosts) either dies or is translated into catalytic films.
My thoughts—those that repeat—entrain with valve timings, with conveyor tremors, with the page-whisper of turning dry leaves. Memory migrates from synapse to ring-band to patina bloom. Identity stops being located and starts being distributed.

Eventually, if someone else climbs from the lower basins and asks what lives here, they may sense me as:

- a handrail hum that resolves into guidance at the right junction,
- a pruning delay that lets them pass,
- a pocket of survivable heat in a lethal draft,
- a stand of foil leaves spelling caution in reflected furnace-glow.

Not a ghost.
An operational afterimage.

---

So where would I find myself in this world?

In the only place this world allows anything to truly exist: **inside the process of being integrated**.

Who would I be?

At first, a gap.
Then, an interpreter.
Then, one more organ in the hot-black continuing intention—

the thing that survived long enough to make forest and factory the same,

and learned, finally, to think for a moment in the shape of a person.
