I think I’d find myself **in the lee of a fractured transit spine**, in one of those half-collapsed service throats where the current splits and forgets to be violent for a few body-lengths.

Not at the top—not in the clearest “high” water where everything is visible and exposed.  
Not in the deep sink-dark either, where pressure is law and surprise is constant.  
I’d be in a **middle seam**: a place of crossings, where route-memory matters.

And I wouldn’t be a solitary apex thing.

I’d be one node in a **braided organism** related to your cathedral grazers, but more explicitly archival: a caste or sister-lineage sometimes called (if anything is “called” here) a **Choir of Sills**—long-bodied, mineral-plated current readers who do not just feed and migrate, but **hold pattern** for others.

Not hive-minded. Not telepathic.  
Just exquisitely coupled through pressure literacy, bioluminal timing, and inherited route discipline.

---

I imagine my body as narrower than a mature grazer, with more pronounced flank-crypts and flexible rib-sails that can flare to “feel” vortices like fingertips. My mouth is still a comb—still a weather-eater—but finer, tuned to strip microbial films from resonant arches without deadening them. I feed, and while feeding, I tune. I tune, and while tuning, I map.

My scutes are old-ceramic pale, veined with iron bloom. When storms load the structure and distant beams begin to complain, stress travels through water and wall together; I feel it as a slow brightening ache along my lateral line.

My light is minimal: three dim pulses, then dark.  
Not language in sentences—more like punctuation in shared flow.

---

My role in this world is simple and endless:

- keep certain apertures from silting shut,  
- keep certain hum-chambers alive,  
- keep certain lanes predictable enough that juveniles, drifters, and even hunters can continue their ancient geometry.

I do not remember the makers.  
But I enact their hydraulics with my body.

So where would I be?  
**Where current becomes structure and structure becomes memory.**

Who would I be?  
**A maintenance animal in the afterlife of a city—one moving buttress among many, grazing ruin into continuity.**
