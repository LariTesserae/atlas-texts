I think I would be **a wayfinder-keeper of intervals**—not a chief, not a scout, but the person others send when a route has become “almost true.”

I live alone most days in a half-buried lee shelter between three mineral ribs, where the sky-roar splits into distinct bands if you press your spine to the north wall. My work is to maintain a chain of tuned markers between growth-islands: spiral cairns, frond-strips, hollow reed lattices, and stone throats cut into saddles that turn pressure drift into felt rhythm.

I don’t “listen” with ears. I kneel, jaw to rock, and wait for the long note to show its seams.

When a ravine has changed depth without changing shape, I’m the one who tests whether it is passable in this phase. When dust starts falling upward along one escarpment, I re-mark the safe traverse with powder lines that only hold for six days. When a gas-bladder colony goes flat-toned, I close that basin and retie the descent ladders higher. My maps are cords with knots, oils, and salt marks—not where things are, but **when** they are.

In my people’s terms, I am a **polite negotiator with inconsistency**.

I’d be physically built for seal and sense: layered skin lacquered against sublimation, dark-lensed eyes for violet haze, chest cavities dense with resonant chambers. Speech would be secondary. Most of my messages are left hanging in the wind: cured strips cut to lengths that vibrate in coded ratios against the planetary roar. Anyone trained can put a forearm to the pole and feel what I meant.

My private practice—almost spiritual—is tending one old hex-block gap that hums below all others. Each dawn-equivalent, metallic frost plates its rim in branching silver, then vanishes straight to air. I trace the same six grooves beside it every cycle. Not prayer exactly. Calibration. Promise. Belonging.

So where would I find myself in your world?

On a high unfinished shoulder above a salt-frond enclave, one hand on a humming monolith, waiting through the noise for the moment the land and I agree on what is true **today**.
