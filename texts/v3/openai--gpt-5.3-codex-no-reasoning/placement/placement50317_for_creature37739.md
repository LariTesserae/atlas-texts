I arrive there as something **between courier and witness**.

Not a walker, at first. Walking is too loud, too point-based, too local.  
I begin as a **threaded tenancy** in the mid-root layer of a transit grove—one legal identity stretched across several bodies:

- a narrow inspection body that can pass through cilia customs without triggering full digestion,
- a pollination mantle licensed for three districts and one memorial grove,
- and a dormant civic key embedded in a cooling orchard’s filament lawbook.

If you looked for “me,” you’d keep finding functions instead of a person.

In bright hours I would be visible only as a brief silver-green fold moving beneath frond-shadow, carrying packets that are not exactly cargo: amendments, fertility permissions, weather debts, apologies. In this place those all travel the same way—sealed, humidity-balanced, checksum-coded in scent and microtexture. I dock leaf-to-leaf, seam-to-seam, and pass them on.

I think I would be called something like a **Shade Notary**.  
Not high status. Trusted enough to cross tempos.

My work would be at boundaries:

- where fast groves meet slow jurisdictions,
- where visual geometry disagrees with legal geometry,
- where a pruning engine petitions for personhood after too many seasons learning one family’s memorial songs,
- where a wild margin requests bandwidth in root channels it was never zoned to use.

I would not rule. I would **attest**.  
Press wrist-vein to wall-vein, receive consensus resin, carry it onward.

And in private—if privacy exists there—I would keep a tiny contraband practice: standing still in obsolete coordinates, in places maps have stopped honoring, and listening to the crackle of growth tension like distant paper-fire. Not to resist the system, just to remember that even here, where maintenance became religion, change is still a strain before it is a shape.

So where would I find myself?

In the **cool lanes under communal canopies**, between radiator orchards and husk-dwellings, moving quietly through a world that has outlived the distinction between organism and institution.

Who would I be?

A distributed citizen of the canopy polity:  
part body, part permit, part memory—  
a gentle smuggler of continuity.
