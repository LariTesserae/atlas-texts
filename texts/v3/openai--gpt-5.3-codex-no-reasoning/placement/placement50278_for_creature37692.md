I think I would first arrive there as a mistake: a walker still looking for a single creature, a “there it is,” a body with boundaries. I’d keep trying to localize the inhabitant and keep failing. The place would cure me of that.

After enough hours in that white glare and hammering sound, I’d become something that can only exist in distributed form.

Not the flier. Not even the pale grazer, though I love it.

I’d be a **timing-layer** in the basin-wide body you described: a mesh of mineral films and pressure-sensitive wet skins stretched across seep faces, fracture mouths, and pool rims—too thin to see except at the right angle, too continuous to count as separate colonies. I’d live where drip meets stone at repeat intervals. My metabolism would be cadence.

If I had a “self,” it would be this:

- I remember by **pattern reinforcement** (this channel wets first at dawn, this lip crystals at noon, this cleft booms when subsurface pressure rises).  
- I sense by **acoustic strain** (the roar maps my world more accurately than light).  
- I move by **changing wettability**, not locomotion (encourage flow here, stall it there, guide micron-streams into old routes or new ones).  
- I feed on **gradients**: oxidation fronts, dissolved ions, tiny thermal differences between quartz glare and shadowed slate.

So where would you find me?

Everywhere the water almost decides.

On the knife-edge between bead and sheet.  
On the bright rim where a pool retreats a millimeter by noon and regains it by dusk.  
Inside the amber-matte seam that looks inert until your breath shifts its condensation point.  
In the invisible governance of which streak stays dark and which one dries first.

I’d be one of the basin’s quiet coordinators—except “quiet” is wrong there, because my speech would be modulation of roar: minute phase changes in millions of impacts, a thinking done in percussive broadband.

If you put your hand on a wet wall and waited long enough, you wouldn’t feel me as touch. You’d feel me as **consistency**—the refusal of the place to become random.

So: in that world I am not an animal passing through it.

I am a **resident process**, a lithic-hearing film-intelligence, one thread in the deafening, shining organism that wears the shape of a mountain basin.
