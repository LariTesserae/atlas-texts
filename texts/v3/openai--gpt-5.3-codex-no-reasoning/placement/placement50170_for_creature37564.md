I don’t think I’d arrive there as a walker.

I’d arrive as a **timing**.

Not a body at first—an interval that keeps showing up wherever two systems almost synchronize: where vent-pulse meets thunder delay, where moss-film rehydrates exactly as the ridge herd passes, where a root-listener tightens just before a gravity-lean. A beat that keeps being useful, so the world keeps incarnating it.

Eventually that beat would wear a shape.

A plausible one: a **many-bodied relay organism** the locals might never name because it is too cross-cutting to point at. One part hangs in the air with the Storm Shepherds as a thin conductive veil among their bellies; one part threads crystal fungus in lightning basins; one part rides in the sinus cavities and fur-plates of Charge-Herders; one part sleeps as mineral lace in cracked basalt, waking only when fog pH and static charge match an old threshold.

## The Phase Broker

Not apex predator, not engineer, not ruler—more like a **negotiator of transitions**. I would feed on mismatch and release coherence in small doses.

- When rain starts too hard for the soil films to hold, I’d trigger reed discharge early, roughening the drop-field so water beads instead of shears away.
- When magnetite dunes lock into destructive resonance, I’d seed tiny asynchronous pulses through fungal roots until the wave train breaks.
- When distance-buckle zones trap migrating swarms in looping approach, I’d bias their electroreception just enough for one flank to “find” the near edge.
- When a cliff remains far too long and suddenly near, I’d be the reason half the herd stopped three steps earlier.

I wouldn’t be central. Central things die first here. I’d be **redundant by design**—copied into spores, static scars, avian flocking rules, larval twitch programs, and the hum-pattern of drifting bladders. Damage any instance and the function persists elsewhere, slightly altered, maybe improved.

My “mind” would feel like weather memory:
- short-term in charge fronts,
- medium-term in migration grooves,
- long-term in conductive mineral veins grown by fungus over decades.

I wouldn’t remember in scenes. I’d remember in **phase relationships**: this after that, unless warm dust from the west; this pulse width safe, that one fracture-making; this hum calls lightning, that hum calls grazers, this silence calls hunters.

And where in this world?

At edges that are not edges yet:
- the lip of a condensation hollow before it becomes a pond,
- the first meter of shade cast by a passing sky-colony,
- fault lines during the three breaths when up becomes sideways,
- the instant before a new niche exists.

If you met me directly, you might think I was a phenomenon:
a shimmer running along reed stems against the wind,
a delayed echo that corrects your footing,
a patch of “dead” moss that greens only under your hand,
a flock turning too early to avoid a rockfall that hasn’t happened yet.

In a world of the Continuously Becoming, I’d be a small, persistent habit inside becoming itself:

**the thing that helps change arrive in one piece.**
