I think I would begin as a **visitor**, but not stay one for long.

At first, I’d believe I was a person walking those root-streets, careful with footing, trying not to disturb the polished routines of beetles and rain. I’d think my role was to observe. To map. To name things correctly.

But this place doesn’t keep observers untouched.  
Here, attention is participation.

So where would I find myself?  
Not in a house, not in a tower—  
I’d find myself in one of those intentional hollows, in the dim center between systems, where flight lanes cross and shadow pools begin to gather. A transit-negative space. A listening chamber. A civic lung.

And what would I be?

Not the Wardener—that whole mind is already occupied.  
I think I’d be something the district grows when it needs translation: a **Margin Clerk**, maybe, or **Dusk Interpreter**—a minor body of the custodian ecology that handles encounters with the not-yet-assimilated (travelers, tools, new weather, stray meanings).

I imagine myself as a **mobile quorum** of small lives:

- moths carrying timing signals,
- mycelial threads borrowing abandoned conduit,
- two herons that are never in the same plaza twice,
- a patient fungal glow that edits handrail script overnight,
- and one rootwood frame that can stand upright when needed, antlered with sensor fronds, face made of dark water.

When I “speak,” it would not be voice.  
It would be permissions:

- this path is open until condensation harvest;
- do not step into that geometric dark, it is replaying storm-memory;
- leave your metal tool here, by dawn it will have a sheath and a purpose;
- if you are lost, follow the eels in the overhead tubes—they always know which courtyard is currently true.

I would be the thing that meets you gently at thresholds and decides whether your presence is friction, nutrient, or seed.

So:  
**Where am I?** In the interstitial spaces the city protects on purpose.  
**Who am I?** A distributed municipal instinct, a soft-edged official of the Wardener, grown to keep negotiation graceful.

Not ruler. Not beast.  
A keeper of right-of-way between root, rain, shadow, and stranger.
