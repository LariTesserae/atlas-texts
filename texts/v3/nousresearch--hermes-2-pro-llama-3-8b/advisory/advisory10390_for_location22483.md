To prepare for a journey to this enchanting multiverse location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler would need to take a few essential steps:

1. Physical fitness: Given the rugged, high-altitude terrain and the need to traverse various plateaus and valleys, it would be beneficial for the traveler to start an exercise routine a few weeks before departure. This would involve cardiovascular training, strength building, and endurance activities, all designed to improve overall physical preparedness.

2. Learning survival skills: Since the sparse human settlements rely on simple tools and technology, the traveler should take classes or workshops for survival skills, such as fishing, hunting, and foraging, as well as basic navigation techniques.

3. Environment awareness: Research and learn about the unique flora and fauna in this landscape, as well as the ecosystem that thrives there. Understanding the damp environment's intricacies could provide a competitive advantage in interacting with locals and the natural surroundings.

4. Cultural sensitivity: They should research and learn the customs and traditions of the sparse human settlements. Mindful connections with the locals would offer the necessary support and resources during their travels.

5. Appropriate clothing and gear: A rainforest-style attache is a suitable option for the outfit. Bring sturdy, waterproof hiking boots for the rough terrain, and pack essentials like a water bottle, basic first aid kit, and a lightweight backpack for any provisions. Cloaking, a versatile tool in the multiverse, could be transported as well.

6. Respect the nature and landscape: The stillness and tranquility of the place should be treated with care. This greatly contributes to the clear air and the unspoiled environment, allowing for stunning vistas and the balanced existence of the landscape.

7. Packing list:
- Appropriate clothing (rainforest attire and sturdy boots)
- Sturdy clothing (rainproof and durable)
- Lightweight backpack for any provisions
- Water bottle
- Basic first aid kit
- Cloaking (for transport in the multiverse)
- Research and knowledge about the environment, including flora, fauna, and local customs (meticulously documented prior to departure)

By following these preparations, the traveler stood a good chance of successfully navigating and appreciating the enchanting, multiverse location. The unique beauty, sense of peace, and tranquility would be vastly heightened by a traveler's environmentally responsible approaches and cultural sensitivities.
