For an ordinary human multiverse traveler visiting Tectum Valley, the following should be considered for preparation:

1. Physical strength and endurance: Tectum Valley is a vast landscape with extensive treks, scaling ancient structures, and navigating through rugged terrain. Travelers should be in good physical condition and have the necessary stamina for such a journey.

2. Appropriate Clothing: The cool temperature of Tectum Valley dictates the clothing choice. Thick garments suitable for the spring season and protective gear for navigating rough terrains are necessary. 

3. Emotional readiness: Understanding the unique environment and accepting the unchanging landscape will be crucial for a pleasant experience. Locating oneself within the vestiges of history and simultaneous ecosystem might evoke strong emotional responses, requiring adjustment. 

4. Personal safety gadgets: In spite of its fertile ecosystem, Tectum Valley is home to peculiar creatures. Some potentially dangerous ones have evolved over time, and therefore, an ordinary traveler should consider having protective gadgets such as flashlights, personal alarms, first-aid kits, and communicative devices for emergencies.

5. Exploration framework: A sound exploration framework will allow an organized and efficient exploration of the valley's structures. Having a thorough understanding of the valley's historical, geographical, and ecological features would be beneficial. 

6. Respect for the environment: The valley has maintained a balance of existence surviving on the simple technologies used by the ancient civilization. Travelers should avoid damaging this equilibrium and respect the environment and its resources.

7. Mental preparation: As the landscapes epitomize ancient records, the valley aims to challenge the minds of travelers. Each structure and terrain has its story, some intertwining with human history, others painting a unique painting of the sun, moon, stars, and Earth. Keeping an open mind about theories, myths, or stories could enhance the experience in this multiverse landscape. 

Remember to drink plenty of water and carry a hydrated pouch. Over-exertion in high altitudes can lead to dehydration so staying adequately hydrated is necessary for the traveler's well-being. And finally, respect and honor the history and culture of the Tectum Valley as their ancient ways of life are still alive in the hills and dales of their rich valleys. 

Happy exploration!
