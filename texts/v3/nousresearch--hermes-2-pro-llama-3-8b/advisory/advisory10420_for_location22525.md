To prepare for a visit to Aridnium, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Pack for the extreme conditions: The planet is dominated by cold temperatures, sparse breathable air, and an ammonia-rich atmosphere. Thus, it's essential to bring specialized clothing, breathing apparatus, and protective gear, including insulated suits, gloves, and boots. It would be wise to bring multiple layers of clothing since the planet's fluctuating temperatures can cause sudden drops, posing risks of hypothermia.

2. Familiarize yourself with the terrain: Aridnium’s terrain is often compared to a subterranean eden, characterized by porous rocks and microbial mats - be sure to study up on geology to be prepared for an alien landscape.

3. Research the flora and fauna: While the flora is mostly adapted to tiny pockets of fecundity, the fauna is primarily microbial, with strange creatures emerging from the ink-black mineral veins. Familiarize yourself with extremophiles and adaptability to acidic, pressure-filled environments.

4. Learn about the local history: The industrial remnants of an unknown civilization offer a fascinating look into the planet's history. Study up on the methods and tools of a bygone era to better appreciate the ingenuity required to wrangle life from a barren landscape.

5. Familiarize yourself with the circadian rhythm: With scarcity as the guiding principle of survival, the flora and fauna operate on a slow, patient cycle that defies the norm - the traveler should prepare to adapt to sudden changes and the stark contrasts of day and night.

6. Expect the unexpected: As you traverse the landscape, be prepared for startling discoveries, and the slow unraveling of a world that blurs the lines between the natural and unnatural. Deeply consider how you will react to the breakdown of conventional reality.

Ultimately, the preparation for a visit to Aridnium requires a mental and physical readiness to accept and navigate an entirely new way of life. While it may be challenging, the traveler's journey through this hidden, otherworldly ecosystem promises to yield transformative insights into the durability and possibility of life.
