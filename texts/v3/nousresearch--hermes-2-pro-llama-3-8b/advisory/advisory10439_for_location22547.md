For an ordinary human multiverse traveler, visiting the Asphalt Plains can be both exhilarating and terrifying. To prepare for this unique destination, they should consider the following steps:

1. Research the Environment: It's essential to understand the landscape before venturing into the isolated territory. Familiarize yourself with the harsh, desolate beauty of the Plains, the deep, almost midnight blue sky, the lack of water and plant life, the strange fauna, etc. Since the landscape defies many familiar aspects of the earth, some research can be crucial to adjusting expectations and ensuring understanding.

2. Pack Appropriately: Due to the lack of water and plant life, the traveler should pack enough food and water to last throughout their stay. Due to the absence of nearby towns or shops, they should also pack all the necessary supplies, including shelter, clothing appropriate for the harsh weather, and nighttime gear.

3. Mental Preparation: The traveler should prepare mentally for the otherworldly experience. The feeling of stepping into another realm, a world that may feel alien and unnerving, might be disorienting. Recognizing that this landscape is a unique ecosystem and not the well-developed places of Earth could be essential for appreciating its beauty.

4. Physical Conditioning: Conditions on the Asphalt Plains can be quite tough. The rocky terrain and lack of water can tire even an experienced traveler quickly. A traveler should be in good physical condition to maneuver through the challenging environment.

5. Carry a map and compass: The deep canyons and tall peaks might easily confuse a traveler. Carrying a map and compass can help re-orient in case of getting lost.

6. Respect the local wildlife: The fauna of the Plains are strange, active, and possibly unknown for humans. Respect and avoid confrontation. It’s wise to keep a distance and observe silently.

7. Learn Basic Survival Skills: Learning how to start a fire without matches in an arid environment, reading weather signs, identifying edible plants are skills that could come handy to increase adaptability to the Plains environment

8. Respect the history: Recognize the remnants of the possible past civilizations are scattered. Being sensitive to the potential history and investigating these sites can lead to a deeper understanding of the Plains.

The traveler should remember that visiting the Asphalt Plains requires adjusting to a drastically different landscape than the usual. Preparing physically, mentally, and logistically can ensure an enjoyable and enlightening experience in this otherworldly destination.
