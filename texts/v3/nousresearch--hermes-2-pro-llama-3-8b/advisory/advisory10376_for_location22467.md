If an ordinary human multiverse traveler wants to visit Axus, they will need to prepare accordingly. 

1. Physically Adapt: The traveler should be prepared for the subterranean environment, with its significant lack of sunlight and thin atmosphere. Temperatures are likely to be cold, so suitable clothing such as layered garments and waterproof gear should be brought along.

2. Communication: The traveler should anticipate the possibility that communication with the local creatures of Axus might not be straightforward. There may be sound dampening in this world, so carrying noise-canceling headphones or other devices that could aid in hearing will be helpful.

3. Sensory Shift: There's a high probability that the traveler will need to adjust their perception of reality. The world's mechanized aspects suggest that this world's artificial illumination and sound may require an initial period of acclimatization. Bringing along devices that can help enhance sensory input, such as night vision goggles, might prove useful.

4. Proper Equipment: As this world is beneath a mysterious subterranean sea, diving gear, oxygen tanks, and a waterproof suit could become essential elements for survival. Water-rescue devises and source of light will also be crucial as the world is perceived to be dimly lit.

5. Respect the Ecosystem: Understanding and respecting the natural cycles and phenomena of Axus — such as the tides and the ever-evolving ecosystem — could be crucial for a traveler's well-being here. The traveler should avoid disrupting the balance of this world inadvertently.

6. Investigation Gear: As this world remains a testament to advanced building, technology, and engineering, carrying an instrument to probe or investigate might be helpful for gaining deeper insights into the world.

7. Medicines/Health Kit: There's no telling what peculiar diseases or infirmities may exist in such an alien realm.

In essence, visiting Axus would be a trip like no other, where the traveler would need to remain mindful of their surroundings, adjust their senses, and respect the world that they are visiting. The extraordinary features of Axus will most certainly require an extraordinary approach in preparation and conduct.
