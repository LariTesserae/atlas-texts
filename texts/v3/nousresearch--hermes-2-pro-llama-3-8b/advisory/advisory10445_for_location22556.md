Erron's landscape is best traversed with care, as the cold waters surrounding the half-hidden lagoons and rivers are iridescent blue but deadly cold. As there are no built structures for accommodation, it is advisable to carry your own camping gear including a lightweight tent, flashlights, and headlamps, as well as a reliable multi-tool or knife for various uses. Lastly, the traveler should respect the Erronians and their way of life. With minimal regional technology and resources, the inhabitants are well-accustomed to living in tune with their barren land. Disrupting the local ecosystem or exploiting its resources would likely have lasting consequences, threatening the fragile balance of lives on Erron. An attitude of humility, respect, and sustainable living should be the traveler's guiding principles.
