For an ordinary human multiverse traveler, preparing for a visit to this hidden realm beneath the earth involves several key steps:

1. Prepare for lack of plant life: As there is no plant life in this underground world, stock up on nutrient-dense, synthetic or fortified foods to sustain your body during the visit. Consider supplements like Vitamin D, as the dim environment may be lacking in sunlight.

2. Protect yourself from damp conditions: With moisture being a constant in the environment, bring waterproof gear such as a good set of boots, a quality waterproof jacket, and pants. Also, pack a moisture-wicking base layer to stay dry and warm.

3. Adapt to the unstable infrastructure: Be prepared for primitive infrastructure built into the clay and stone, and be mindful when exploring to avoid accidental damage. Safety gear like a hardhat and sturdy gloves are recommended.

4. Pack a source of light: With dim, cool, and faintly lit conditions, pack a powerful flashlight or headlamp to navigate through the fog-filled landscape.

5. Bring natural bird calls or a sound machine: To counterbalance the unusual sounds of the realm, pack resources to mimic the sounds of nature, such as birdsong or a sound machine playing a forest ambience. This can help create a sense of normalcy and calm.

6. Embrace the rhythm of time: Acknowledge that in this realm, the slow and steady seep of water and the quiet working of mineral formations will create a different experience of time. Allow yourself to be present and adapt to this rhythm in order to better appreciate the changes and evolutions of the landscape.

7. Connect with the energy patterns: As the underground realm is established in its unique state with energies dancing between crystals and volcanic glass, acknowledge the interconnectedness of the land and its life forms. Seek to see, hear, and feel these patterns, as connecting with the energy is an essential part of the experience.

8. Wear comfortable clothing: Dress for the dim, cool conditions and the added moisture. Layers are key, as well as comfortable and supportive footwear for navigating uneven terrain.

9. Pack appropriate tools: Depending on the purpose of your visit and your level of exploration, packing tools such as a geological hammer and chisel, a small collection of basic survival tools, or other equipment specific to your goals (e.g., documenting the environment, studying the fauna) can be helpful.

10. Be adaptable and open-minded: Finally, be prepared for the unusual and ready to experience wonder around every corner, as the hidden landscape will undoubtedly defy normal expectations. Embrace the unique and let your curiosity guide you.
