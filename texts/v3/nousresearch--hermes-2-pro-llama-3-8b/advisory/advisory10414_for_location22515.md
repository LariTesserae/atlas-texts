For an ordinary human multiverse traveler visiting this location, preparation is key. Here are some steps to consider:

1. Physical fitness and acclimatization: Due to the high altitude and unpredictable weather, the traveler should be in good physical shape and have time to acclimatize to the changes in atmospheric pressure. This will reduce the likelihood of altitude sickness.

2. Appropriate attire: The region is cold and damp, and since there are no settlements, clothing should be waterproof, warm, and wind resistant. Mittens, hats, and scarves are essential. Layers of lightweight, breathable clothing is recommended to adapt to sudden temperature fluctuations.

3. Footwear: The treacherous terrain includes tricky slopes, so sturdy and grippy boots that can withstand rough surfaces are a must.

4. Food and water: In the absence of human settlements, it's crucial to carry sufficient food and water. High-energy, non-perishable snacks, such as energy bars or dried fruit, are ideal for maintaining strength during the hike. Water purification tablets or a water filter should also be considered, given the presence of glacial streams.

5. Navigation tools: A map and compass could come in handy, as the constantly shifting landscape might make it difficult to stay on track. A GPS device could also be helpful. However, these should be used in conjunction with each other to avoid relying solely on technology.

6. Protection: Mosquitoes, insects, and possibly other wildlife could pose a problem, so carrying insect repellent, protective clothing, and maybe even a small net for tents or places to rest could be considered.

7. Lighting and survival gear: As the skies are often murky, a flashlight or headlamp is required for navigation at night. A whistle, first-aid kit, and a fire starter kit should also be included in case of emergency situations.

8. Mental preparedness: Given the surreal, dreamlike nature of the landscape, the traveler might experience a sense of unease or disorientation. They should be mentally prepared for this experience and remind themselves that the world, while perhaps disconcerting, is not dangerous.

9. Patience and a sense of wonder: With unpredictable weather, erosion, and the vast machinery, the traveler should come prepared with patience. There will undoubtedly be times where activity is impossible due to the elements. A sense of wonder and curiosity for the landscape is also vital, as it's a unique and rare place few humans visit.

10. Investigation and documentation: As this location is a testament to the constant struggle between the built and the natural, between the created and the pristine, the traveler should explore and document this landscape to understand its complexities. Photo or video documentation can be an excellent way to capture and reflect upon these experiences.
