For an ordinary human multiverse traveler planning a visit to this mysterious underground world, careful preparation is essential.

Firstly, one would need to acquire suitable diving or underwater gear for the journey ahead, as the world is predominantly submerged in a perpetual wall of atmospheric water. A waterproof suit, flippers, a helmet with an integrated oxygen supply and a handheld light source should suffice. As this world is perpetually bright, a high-powered flashlight or underwater lamp will help to navigate the darkness.

Secondly, since the landscape is unknown, it would be beneficial to bring tools for self-defense and exploration, such as a geological hammer, picks, and a knife. Resistance to the unknown, and adapting to the terrain, flora, and fauna is a key to survival in this strange world. Depending on the traveler's preferences, one may also consider bringing cameras, food, drinks, and other necessary supplies for the journey. 

One should also study up on the basics about this world. Knowing a bit about the unique ecology, rock formations, built environments, and flora will help you better understand the world and what you are experiencing.

It goes without saying that navigation should also be a critical consideration when planning a visit to this place. Due to the lack of animal life and the absence of traditional landmarks or compass references, a well-studied map and compass might be the only guide.

Lastly, the traveler should also ensure a form of safety. Perhaps this can include a communication device, such as a walkie-talkie, to communicate with others, in case of an emergency. 

But perhaps, the most vital preparation is a sense of wonder and curiosity. This world offers unique and enchanting experiences that are unlike any other, and stepping into the unknown with an open mind is the best way to truly appreciate and enjoy this alien landscape.
