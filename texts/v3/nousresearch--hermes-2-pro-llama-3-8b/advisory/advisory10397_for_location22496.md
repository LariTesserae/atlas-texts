For the ordinary human multiverse traveler, preparing for a visit to the Caelorum Desert would require a significant shift in perspective and adaptation to its unique environment. Here are some practical steps to consider:

1. Minimalist approach: Given the barren landscape and the lack of resources, it's crucial to pack only the essentials. A lightweight and sturdy camping tent or portable shelter, a water filtration system (as water is scarce), and comfortable yet robust walking shoes are items to consider including in your kit.

2. Sun protection: Due to the perpetual warmth from the sun, sunscreen with high SPF and a wide-brimmed hat are essential.

3. Navigation aids: As there are no landmarks, a compass and a GPS device would be vital to prevent getting lost in the dunes.

4. Clothing: Pack loose and breathable clothing that can also be layered to protect against the harsh elements like wind and sandstorms.

5. Medications and first aid supplies: Pack a basic first aid kit and any necessary medications, as medical help may be miles away.

6. Communication devices: A satellite phone or a two-way radio would be instrumental for communication with the outside world or in cases of emergency.

7. Mental preparedness: The solitude and isolation of the desert might be challenging for some people. In such a place, it's crucial to have a positive mindset, embrace the slow pace of life, and engage in mindfulness and meditation techniques to cope with stress and the feeling of being cut off from the rest of the world.

8. Familiarize yourself with desert survival skills: Learn how to find water and build a fire without natural resources, as they would be scarce in this environment. Research also the local flora and fauna and the basic survival skills needed when they are scarce.

9. Finally, respect the local environment: Pack in biodegradable waste only, practice conscious consumption, and respect the local ecosystem to minimize the impact on this delicate balance.

Overall, a visit to the Caelorum Desert is not for the faint-hearted. It's a place where the traveler needs to be prepared to face the glaring sun, the harsh environment, and the solitude of a world untouched by time. Embrace this unique experience as an opportunity to immerse oneself in a completely different otherworldly atmosphere.
