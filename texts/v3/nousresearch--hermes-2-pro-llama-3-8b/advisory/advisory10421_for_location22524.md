Preparing for a visit to the Eldritch Wasteland would require a unique set of considerations. As this location is devoid of water and has intense weather systems, the first step would be to ensure that you have a reliable, long-lasting, and self-sustaining water supply. This could include water filters, desalination tablets or devices, or a method of distilling snow or ice if it were available.

Next, the traveler would need to equip themselves with suitable protective clothing and gear to withstand the harsh winds and abrasive environment. This includes layers of warm clothing, wind-resistant jackets, durable pants or coveralls, sturdy footwear, and possibly a face mask or head gear to shield the face and head from blowing sands and debris.

Since technology in the Eldritch Wasteland would have outpaced our modern understanding, it is wise to prepare with technology or tools that could function beyond our current technology or be adapted to function in the environment. This could involve portable power banks, multifunctional tools, and equipment that can withstand the harsh conditions.

Since the fauna in the wasteland may be rare and elusive, a knowledge of their behaviors and habitats could be valuable, or it might be advisable to have live-in guidance or a local guide with the area. This could be obtained through communication with the locals in the multiverse.

One might also want to come prepared with a sunshade or solar umbrella, as the sun's rays reflecting off the ebon dunescape may increase the heat and cause dehydration. Lessons in how to orient oneself in the desolate landscape could be useful, as the lack of landmarks can be disorientating.

Lastly, a comprehensive understanding or familiarity with the long-gone civilizations and history of the Eldritch Wasteland could enriches the experience's historical and cultural significance for the traveler. Some research or guided tour by a historical expert could be a good way to prepare for this.

In conclusion, to visit the Eldritch Wasteland, the traveler would need to be well-prepared with clothing, gear, tools, local knowledge or a guide, a reliable water supply, and an appreciation for this ancient and primordial landscape, as a misstep or mistake could easily lead to significant hardship or danger.
