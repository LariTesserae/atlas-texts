Perched atop the zenith of the ancient weathered continent Arepos, the jagged peaks of Montes Iridescere tower thousands of meters into the sky, silent and ancient, radiating with a mystic aura. These peaks pierce the heavens, inadvertently splitting the sky in twain - and yet, these mountains seemed to defy the parameters ascribed to them.

Aerial rivers wander down the slopes, dabbling with the jagged stony crag. As erosion has taken its toll, the peaks have subtly but unmistakably begun to amalgamate, striving for equilibrium with the peddle of time. Occasional rain pours, discourse with the expanse of the weary rock, fostering the dampness preferred by the agile apex-realm of the druidic flora. Silently, though, the slopes have grown a touch more barren and sere: so much so that the distinct traces of the deep peculiar starkness in the sparse vicinity of the forlorn alpine flora are becoming increasingly heavyline.

The sky is often dark at dawn, though not unwelcoming: in the profundity of the murkiness, it is said that powerful aurorae spontaneously erupt, designed to bereave the mountain of the verisimilitude to a lifeless rock. Then, the phantasmal light dances, for a moment, with the meekly protruding flora amen to the biting cold of the criminally high peaks. On the other end, nights over Montes Iridescere are less somber, embellished by a display of unconventional constellations & islands of light. Industrial nature isn't alien here -luminous technology outside the ken of standard human civilization mystically pervades the mountaintops, an anomaly disregarded by the alien flora & fauna.

Despite the stringent conditions atop Montes Iridescere, the slopes are teeming with bizarre life. A strident abundance of fauna revel against the alpine environment, their thoraxes uniquely designed to endure the rigorous conditions of the brittle vastness. The populace here ranges from quasi-invisible to the naked eye denizens to armoured variants sinisterly aglow with the unnatural emanations of the contraband tech.

There is a peculiar humming sound in the air, a murmur which resonates like some kind of primordial music. Nonetheless it is quiet enough, little sound to be heard above the white noise, ere the unyielding mountain bellows aloud, an aura of unseen whispers echoing all around, sometimes each vocalization uttering quietly in hushed accomplice with the regime of wind.

The ecosystem, reaching an organic crescendo, overtakes the paradoxical in-balance of the otherwise sterile mountain slopes. Predators predate their prey with a logic devoid of the calculative sounds of larger creature populations elsewhere. The amalgamated ecology, in its entirely strange active process, narrates a non-existent tale of the peaks, of existence, of life's unbending determination to flower in the odd clusters scattered about the cropped worthwhile strategic tourist stop here on Montes Iridescere. In doing so, the world maintains its own schism of silence & activity, darkness & light, sparse & full, artificial & genuine, as it creaks with the passage of time.
