The elevation may be extreme, yet nature, in her circular logic, has still managed to insinuate herself upon this pinnacle.

The bright light that illuminates this landscape produces a surreal, otherworldly atmosphere. The absence of animal life does little to dampen the unusual tendencies of this landscape – the wintry scene, with its frost-encrusted vegetation and its history of early human habitation, bends the rules of the world as we know it.
