In the hidden pocket of this landscape, the air is heavy with presence – the air millows and condenses around you, revealing what was once ancient stone is now an ever-fresh terrain. You find yourself knee-deep in water that weeps from the surface, tracing the veins of ageless hills that still stand as proud monuments to creation's energy. Every so often, you'll catch a glint of construction and industry – remnants of the town that once filled this valley – but they appear worn and devoured by an exclusively plant-oriented urban fabric. 

The flutters of green – the vegetation total – are aimed high into the sky, defying or rather, comprehending this cold, sparse, and dark world. The vibrant green is the plant-substrate designed to emerge victorious while claiming architecture all around. Here, absence of animal life isn't a mystery – its a rule of this world. Despite the silent air of the realm, nature blooms proud and tall like a pure column of life. Every plant discovered on your journey is filled with an array of texts written on their bark—existing proof of a primitive industry of the ancients.

Yet, the strangest part of this landscape is its dynamism. Young and hidden it may be, but there is life teeming here. The water near surface crawls over the rocks of this terrain as erosion continues to have its way while the condensing atmosphere performs its ritual. Predations occur – those with sharp features and the ones with young, fresh lines feast. 
Here, you stand in a silent room, where power surge unpredictably. You can feel it – an active civilization with nature doing its own thing – a room full of weather cycles, organic and inorganic fauna, and a micro-ecosystem. 

This is a place of scale lesser talked about – a victim of human progress – yet so much life persists. The reflections in your mind of what once was mixed with what currently exists create a truly enthralling puzzle of a memory – a place of weirdness before our time, waiting to be tiptoed around with booted understanding.
