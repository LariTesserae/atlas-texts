The landscape is a high-altitude, heavily wooded valley deeply entrenched by a meandering river, which adds to the damp environment and fosters a thriving plant ecosystem. The valley is nestled within a mountainous region, providing a sense of isolation and uniqueness from other landscapes. The unusual elements of this place lie in the midst of its constant turmoil and change.

Settled in balance for millennia, this valley has exhibited a long and stable existence, with terrain weathered and shaped by the passage of time. The valley's dense vegetation, both flora and fungi, sprawls throughout this high-altitude sanctuary. The outcome is an atmosphere that is both chilly and saturated with life's vital presence.

Industry in this valley is mostly fueled by the river's power and the mechanical gains derived from hidden factories nestled among the valley's dense woodland. Despite its industrial activity, the valley retains a significant sense of untouched and natural wilderness, with human-made structures few and far between. Much of the valley's engineering is focused on harnessing the river's flow to fuel production, rather than on erecting dwellings or other structures. The region has a subdued, muted lighting during most of its daylight hours.

The fauna of this valley are rarely seen and remain largely a mystery. The prevalence of plant life rarely attracts much attention from passersby, and the most common sight in the valley are the occasional deer or fox, foraging for sustenance. The silence is often broken by the gentle sound of the river's flow cutting through the land.

Over time, the valley has become a turbulent zone of creation and destruction, as nature's eternal cycles push and pull at the world. The region is like an organism in and of itself. Random pockets of heat and cold, light and darkness, make the valley's identity a fascinating and fluid enigma. The entire valley boasts a unique wildness that emerges from the interplay between the river, climate, distant mountain peaks, and industrial activity — each of them shifting and affecting the other, fostering a sense of constant lively-ness and excitement within the valley.
