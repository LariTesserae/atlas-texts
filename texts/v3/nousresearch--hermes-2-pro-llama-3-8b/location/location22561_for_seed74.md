In the heart of a forgotten valley lies Ehaar, a world unto itself, forged through the marriage of nature, industry, and the curious workings of time itself. Enveloped by the delicate beauty of a forged, virgin rainforest that reaches towards the heavens, the settlement sprawls out in irregular, naturalistic patterns that find harmony within their wilderness environs. One would know the scale of this primeval grove if they sought to traverse its tapestry of flora; a task that would prove arduous, as the lush canopy defies even the most daring of expeditions.

Ehaar's populace embraces the prevailing damp; they have come to revere and harness the capricious whims of the skies. For here, water manifests as a serendipitous muse, providing nooks of sanctuary to the thirsty and a foundation upon which a monument to industry can rise. An intricate network of rivers, springs, and waterfalls carefully winds its way through this wet, fecund continent - only to be tamed by the deft hands of its pioneering dwellers.

Though nestled within the girth of the verdant arboreal expanse, a full bloom of human ingenuity ensures one cannot overlook signs of life outside of the flora. For on certain days, the interlaced hum of industry peels through the tranquility, a melodic ephemera heralding the presence of town and home. Factory chimneys belch forth smoke and exhaust, transforming the natural dimness of the woods with a pall of electricity that illuminates the inky air. Looming cathedrals of steel and stone bear witness to ambition unknown to most regions, stretching skyward like the reverent spires of ancient faith.

Yet for all the earth-bound marvels, Ehaar poses as a stern reminder of the power of the heavens. Anderson, an elderly mechanic - her only child a vanished prodigy - views the skies with a mix of faith, trepidation, and a thirst for knowledge. Drawn to the constellations that speck the indigo firmament, she nonetheless fears the stars that govern their destinies; a duality that resonates with the essence of the foreign found within.

Here, amid the whispers of industry and the hush of the santos woods, the rules chafe under the aegis of the once-hidden rivers. Little do those who wander know the ebb and flow of the buried memories which shape this land; a body of lore that parochial scholars have not yet disturbed. This domain harbors remnants of ancient tomes penned upon stone or bark, whispered among adepts in hushed tones. And it is within these tales that secrets of the burgeoning world hidden in plain sight first emerge.

The sound that usually pervades Ehaar is the quiet, lilting flow of water throughout the verdant forest. With variations in pitch and volume, listeners here learn to decipher the silent messages imparted by their surroundings; a skill useful in the realm amid the mechanical cacophony of industry. But on occasion, the cries of distant creatures echo through the woods, a strange song of survival that pierces the serenity.

Amid the verdant glory of the forest giants, the ecosystem thrives according to its own chaotic design. Fauna here utilises every space, inhabiting each layer of the forest's hierarchy. Yet, it remains rare; elusive and full of surprises. Predators and prey alike are driven in their ceaseless dance of life and death, subtly changing the landscape in each season.

Paired with growth, Ehaar's soil keeps a rich, established patina of antiquity. The erosive effects of the stormy heavens and the advancement of the ever-creeping roots have fashioned this land, finding an intricate balance. What has been yielded by time is seized by fellow denizens and embraced as part of the patchwork of history in which Ehaar enshrouds itself.

Boundaries in this realm are as fluid as the rivers that bend through the Wraithwood valley. Areas of human influence blend with the wilds, and spiritual realms coexists with the physical. In the end, Ehaar remains startlingly, undeniably alive, and history continually unfolds through lens of the valley's eyes.
