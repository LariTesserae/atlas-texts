In the mountains where the sky touches the Earth in jagged peaks and cliffs casts watery shadows, lies the hidden corner of a world peculiar and ever-changing. This pocket-scale landscape is a secret haven nestled within the boulders and rocks, an unusual and unexplored place where time seems to flow in its own peculiar rhythm.

As you descend into this strange micro-environment, you're immediately struck by how wet everything is; a testament to the ever-present humidity condensing upon the air and the lingering traces of rain. Water courses through the cracks,-filtering downwards in thin liquid threads that feed the thriving, lush growth. The continuous moistness supports a population unlike any other, with strange kettle mosses, delicate lichens clinging to the raw rock surfaces, and color-lush fungi painting the golden hour in iridescent hues. 

In this rather hot, packed environment, nature's law works to ensure all the more dense a flurry of life; earth-action is full-glide and happening with a particular active force, as if the elements themselves are doing battle with time. Life persists at every turn, no space left between presences. Creatures that lurk in shallow depths come alive in the bejeweling dim light, cobbling together vibrant life from simple tools and raw materials; fauna is not common, but adaptive, resisting colonization by aiding other sprites sharing in the territory. 

Awe-inspiring are the brilliant makeshift constructs of industry in the primordial landscape. Stone- and wood-made by the predominant folks who reside in this place of wild artistry, and the strange sense of soft-shaping infrastructures. Accorded and organic, these constructions bow to the ancient vein of rocky ground whose charm is unabated in the rebellious wonder levity of light and life.

Through this surreal landscape run subtle, odd earthquakes still emerging—They're part of a world on a cusp, a landscape winding down from the favor of eerie life, simultaneous to the next world's movement toward some odd resurrection.

This is a place of great light and its striking analogs: colors that overflow; they dance harmonically with organic, ephemeral routines and roar of life. The music of this landscape is loud, bafflingly lovely, making no sense to translate in any language but a cerberal swaying, unwords. Yet, intuitive forces wordlessly merge and overlap, fusing language with the panoply of landscape to create a truly enchanting world that defies easy description. Young and lately scorning of such constructs as language and order, this dynamic realm wrestles with its oddest and least civil predictions amidst a roaring tumble, becoming profound precisely because of its eclectic and ortdomic chaos. 

All the while, the landscape teems with all facets, with every source of life, and bizarre unconnectable weirdness overwhelmingly pours from the groves and crevices. Everywhere, the agony and elation of what we call living struggle to identify themselves. The place becomes itself, a completely unrelated setting beyond which nothing can exist other than its own mind-scape, perpetually evolving as every single instant seems so timely in comprehending everything. 

This, then, is the lyrical landslip, that closes its eyes slowly to the masculine anger of time, nudging into innocence, remembering its good ol' salty sea roots, echoing forever fresh skies. 

Without the templates of reality, without the rigged structures of east and west, of high and low, this place cries with its atoms and affirms that, in the hidden landscape, we are all emerging. For, in this landscape, there's no hard limit to the sprawling, heavenly turf, only spectacular steps diving healthily into the wet-roped poetry of life.
