In this strange, waterlogged world, vegetation is the dominant force. It is a place of lush plant life, but unlike any forest or jungle you might imagine. Here, flora has chosen to exist submerged under water, thriving in the cold, dark depths where sunlight barely penetrates. The plants grow in great, winding clumps, their tops touching just below the surface where the sunlight struggles to break through the relentless rain that drizzles from above.

The exotic landscape is built on ancient, high landforms that have been eroded and weathered over countless centuries. These formations create a multitude of niches and grottoes, providing ample shelter and protection from the ceaseless downpour for the flora. The scale of the terrain is intimate and tight, with limited movement between areas due to the dense vegetation and labyrinthine rock formations.

Despite this, there are pockets of sparse human settlements clinging to the natural resilience of this world. These small, urban communities live in harmony with the environment, with primitive technology and a deep understanding of their surroundings. They rely on hand tools and manual craftsmanship, conserving their resources and adapting to life beneath the ever-present water.

The fauna is abundant, and the dense mats of vegetation provide a rich ecosystem for countless creatures. From strange breeds of fish to mysterious amphibians and elusive crustaceans, a lively world thrives beneath the surface, feeding off roots and gliding through the murky depths.

An unusual phenomenon graces this landscape: a deep, faint luminescence that seems to emanate from the plants themselves. There's something almost surreal about the way the water reflects this eerie, blue-green light, throwing everything into an ashen, ghostly visage. The sound of water dripping and the occasional splash of fish breaking the silence draw out the whispers of weirdness that float in the atmosphere.

Here, even time seems to move slowly, with the cycle of day and night extended to a surreal, dreamscape of never-ending rain and perpetual twilight. The inhabitants have learned to respect the pace of nature and to be content with the gradual, gentle changes that happen across long, tranquil durations in this submerged world.

It is a place of contrasts – lush vegetation fighting for survival under constant rain, the lost, fractured remnants of land towering above, a strange, weak light struggling to reach down to life, and communities clinging to the minimal resources at their disposal. Yet all of this contributes to a place that holds its own delicate balance.

Welcome to the weird, watery, and wonderful world of Submergence.
