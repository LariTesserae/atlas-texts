I imagine a place called "The Whispering Hollows," a region with an ethereal and surreal atmosphere. The land is marked by a complex network of low-lying valleys and winding rivers, mostly choked by the deep, astringent dampness that permeates everything. Water marks as such, crafting the natural ballet that is rainfall and evaporation, creating a mystical tableau. It is neither a desert nor a swamp, but something infinitely more curious -- a realm of ceaseless dampness, humidity that clings to every facet, nurturing its vibrant, riotous growth.

Vegetation here exceeds a lushness that one might otherwise partisan -- ferns and fungi, vines, and mosses; everything proliferating, each moment breathing new life upon the earth, slick and grasping vines forming a canopy above. This fecundity gives the subtle impression that the landscape itself drips with a wilting, decaying beauty, nourishing the tropes of what is preserved and lost. Painters might call it enigmatic.

Under Heavenly Objects, this valley enjoys fairly warm weather when compared to its neighbors. The stars, universe outlawed from other places' intermediate climates, bathe it in a warmth that never quite congeals yet inspires a sense of wistful longing.

Erosion is as young, fresh, and new as one might fathom, with newly-formed hills offering sharp features, jagged edges, and cantilevered mountains cloaked in a jarring beauty. These young earth carvings are simultaneously ancient and ephemeral, a delicate equilibrium disrupted by the environment's activity.

On an open scale, the Whispering Hollows could speak for thousands of miles around. It stands tall as a perpetual canvas but also fails to quell the heated dissidents between settlements– farming estates fragmenting amidst the valley's towering ramparts. The sacred strangeness element, at a striking 2.2, is magnificently encapsulated by the Dichloridium Whispers – a species unknown to the greater world, that blooms in these Hollows only. Its eerie bio-luminescence skirts the edge of danger, a standard for "Weirdness" that amplifies the Hollows' exoticism.

Though timelessly empty, the Whispering Hollows are pressure-release valves for a lost cosmos. The sound the environment makes -- deafening -- is akin to the quietest rush of things unknown, amplified infinitely. Numerous elements assault the senses with cavalcades of alien fauna; the pace teems with active, otherworldly specters, never ceasing to deliver the melodic, discordant noise of life. Paraphrased leads to confusing.

Finally, every yin has its yang, with the Whispering Hollows embracing those who reside there. Directly contradicting its empty status, the Hollows serve as a vibrant biotope laboring with The Striders -- a species, theoretically mad and adamantly inventive, known to manipulate nanoscale materials to survive.

As the sun sets, a purplish dim light takes over, ceding to the Whispers' own eccentric hue. Attraction to the supernatural takes place with a deeply quotidian pace. As nightlife settles, replacing needs with desires and forgotten histories try to rise to the surface, The Whispering Hollows glitter and growl, welcoming their nocturnal patrons in. A perennial space of constant exchange, its perpetual ambience continues to breathe new life. Its strangeness perfectly accommodates itself, speaking a poorly understood language of existence.(2/2)
