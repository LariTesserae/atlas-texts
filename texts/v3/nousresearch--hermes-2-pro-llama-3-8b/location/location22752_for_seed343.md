Nestled within the rugged cradle of an ancient geological wonder, a world of perpetual verdant bounty unfurls. Billions of years of erosion, lightning, and rain have softened the jagged edges of the sprawling primordial landscape, chiseling towering columns, and buttresses from the silica-streaked stone. This otherworldly cathedral of plant life, though, has merely grown around and through these ossified formations with the effortless grace of something as intimately connected to one's surroundings as the roots of the trees that adorn our parks and gardens.

The verdure of this place staggers the senses, with ferns and other near-preternaturally colossal flora joining hands high above the soil to paint an ever-shifting canvas of dappled light and ink-wash darkness. The plants actively knit the landscape together, performing the roles of architecture, landscapist, and habitat all at once. As far as one can see, from verdant and serrated valleys to the skyline of detrusive crags and peaks, only the iridescent pulse of inferred life remains.

As the omnipresent green is leavened with brief, simple, bouts of warmth, the great up-down rush of this landscape jointly stimulates and sedates the viewer, inducing a pleasantly soporific yet dna-charged trance. Upon accepting this strange equilibrium, one can begin to pick out minuscule variations in the great dominant mass around them. It becomes evident on closer inspection that the omnipresent plant life itself must be in perpetual motion. Within these gusts of botanical breeze, fantastical wing-shaped leaves perform the whims of slumbering giants and their myriad offspring. The dancers sway and shuffle, glide, and riot in the fierce daily duet with wind and sun, while, in countless filigreed profiles candle flames, their supposedly inconsequential concord with the atmosphere spells oligarchy.

Occasional water sources strew themselves as isolated treasures across the otherwise empty expanse. These brackish tributaries, disrupted by weather's changing caprice, grant the parched ground and hydrophilic biota therein a brief respite from the leased amenity of dew. This quixotic watercourse here ebbs and flows, and all that it does reel such riches into the stewardship of these consummate landscape artists.

Set within the bosom of this wild epic, a worn and weathered scattering of homesteads limn themselves, improvising architecture from humble slate and stick. Walling life into small holdfasts, they seem at once a part and apart from the neighboring vegetation. Yet they are not so much a commentary as they are a quiet, unassuming element in the constant cycling of resources, which the ecosystem cannot truly resist, but will neither abandon. They are as silent and evocative as a museum’s relic, yet as imposing as a bullet.

The hum of the idling tech mediated this changed land is simple in its doings - an animal hound upon the scarce resources and areas of the ancient land. Prehistoric signs speak as much of mastery over this world as of its mangling by the feckless and clumsy hand of man.

The disorienting days here are lit normally normal, with neither too much nor too little light doing any one thing too out of place. Neither too dim nor too blinding, the light allows a leveled plane of enriched restfulness and the dazzling bio-playful colors of the day enhance the appreciation of fauna everywhere.

Sounds here mess around at a deafening quantity — the trailing purrs of sleepy gusts, the whispers of plant going about their communal business, the tooting of game birds, water bubbling, and so much more - a fabulous symphony yet almost muted - a delayed reverb, so to say. There's something about the way the air waves trumpet out sound here that lends it a sluggish, slow-motion quality, so it never quite reaches scream level but hovers above the cacophonous, lingering like the soupy air before a summer downpour.

The dynamic is intense yet not irksome - with trade winds on a catwalk, the garden world orbiting around, the lush display's kept going by weather's shifting traits - each in its stead postponing any hint of dilution or decadence. Life pulsates all around in delicate and robust balance, with firelit banks emitting a faint beat while fat-bodied predators contentedly rummage the thickets. It's like the perpetual ebb, flow, and crash of a turbulent sea, and time seems to alternately highlight and smoother its natural narratives. In this place, there's rarely a dull moment.
