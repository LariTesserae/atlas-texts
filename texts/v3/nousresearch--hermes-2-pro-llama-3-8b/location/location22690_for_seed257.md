Its ceaseless flow, its relentless rape of the land, of every adherent of this mournful and desolated landscape, tolls the hours in a damp, cold, and desolated serenade. An unfamiliar liquid, yet familiar in its insistence and nagging familiarity, the water creates nightmarish echoes in this realm.
