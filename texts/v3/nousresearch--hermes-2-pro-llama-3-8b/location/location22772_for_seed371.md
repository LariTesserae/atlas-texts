Perched atop the highest peak of this small, delicate island lies a verdant and dense forest. One can almost taste the dampness, the air rich with the scent of decay and the promise of life. The land beneath the thickly layered canopy of foliage is young, still sharp-edged from the unpredictable eruptions that created it. It's a realm of Christmas tree boughs, scratching and swaying in the crisp, cool air. Violent squalls and gusts fill this sky, whipping a film of moisture across the landscape and lending a life to the land.

The atmosphere here is often dark, a heavy curtain of grey clouds obscuring the zenith, casting everything beneath in muted shadows. Twilight endures much of the time, a soft and lingering half-light that obscures the edges of things—idle mirrors that blend leaf and rock into a natural chiaroscuro.

In this isolated and inhospitable place there are no human constructs, no sign of mechanized technology; only the irregular scar-tongued cliffs, weathered stone, and geothermal springs speak of the planet's wayward creation. The ground beneath the feet is a terrestrial quilt of mud and gravel, stitched together by fungal seams and grassy tufts.

Despite its isolation the most remarkable feature of this mostly forgotten landscape is its proliferating life. It brims with life, from the simplest lichen to the most massive tree, bursting with unnatural speed. The flora here defies all expectations, birthing shoot and blade from soil and rock with an almost manic energy; plants grasp in the perpetual chill at the brink of twilight, shadows cast long and hollow.

This place lives at the very definition of turbulent and loud. It's an amalgamation of contending ecosystems in perpetual struggle for raw Earth upon raw Earth; possessed with a wild, fantastical power that is both human creation and stranger. The fauna mostly keeps its distance, safe within its ever-sought solace; influenced by the chaos of this world but untouched, like the couple in the bar at closing time reaching for the stars in hazy reveries. The natural order of this land is bespoke, every asset pushed to the absolute edge of its unfathomable potential, unfettered. It is relentless, and it is beautiful.
