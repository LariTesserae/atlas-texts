In the world of Sylvania, water abounds on its low-lying terrain, fed by an unending supply of condensing atmosphere. Marshes and shallow water bodies cover the land, with steam rising from the soggy ground as the heat of a distant sun beats down on these wetlands. The air quivers with a quiet hum, a distant murmuring that suggests the near absence of vegetative life that might crawl, creep, or flutter about. The temperature stays consistently hot, with beads of moisture forming at the horizon where the land meets the air.

Sylvania emerged from the raw geology of a lesser-formed planet, with no pre-existing life or natural substrate to interrupt its solidifying. This world formed from the molten mesas and chasms that once decorated its favored geography. Yet, it bears a wild and turbulent energy, as if it actively creates and destroys on a massive scale, influenced by untamed forces.

A sparse density cascades through the landscape, leaving a wavering balance of empty space and constructed presence. Immense structures and creations fill this world, towering above the marshlands, their scale defying their own physics. The result is an immense gap between human-like constructs and the embryonic nature of this land.

Made by hands and minds with simple tools and rudimentary technologies, the inhabitants of Sylvania adhere to a primitive culture. The lustrous ores of this virgin world allowed the emergence of producing a fully-concocted environment, with the tiniest of cogwheels, circuits, and gears helping to build this reality.
