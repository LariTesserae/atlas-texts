The landscape is called Erywanthia, a small, densely packed hamlet nestled in a low-lying valley between two mountainous regions. This town thrives on the unique combination of warm temperatures and lush vegetation, creating an atmosphere that is both wet and teeming with plant life, evocative of a marshy landscape that has seen numerous existential changes. Erywanthia is built up with urban and industrial activity, mostly centered around factories and mechanical processes, with infrastructure and construction layered throughout the town, indicating a long-established presence.

The landscape appears to be dimly lit, as if the sun only partially pierces through the thick layers of clouds that crown the sky. This dimness allows for an eerie, strange quality to permeate the air, contributing to the mysterious and somewhat unsettling mood of the place.

Surprisingly, given the lush vegetation, little to no animal life exists in Erywanthia. The peculiar and erratic fauna that initially occupied the valley were gradually forced out by the relentless expansion of the town over the years. Consequently, the main sounds come from the mechanical operations of the industrial complexes rather than any natural residual noise.

Despite its vividly portrayed character, the landscape of Erywanthia progresses slowly. Its cycles are gradual, with change across long durations, emphasizing patient processes and weak gradients. Industrial production continues at a steady pace, maintaining the structured and technologically advanced civilization that the people currently know and are content with, living in the near-touching layers of existence in this small and fascinating place.
