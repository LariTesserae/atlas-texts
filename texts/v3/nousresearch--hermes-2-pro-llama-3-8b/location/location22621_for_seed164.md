The landscape is a vast, high-altitude plain, perched atop a burgeoning plateau carved from a young and porous earth. Protected by the curvature of the planet and an unyielding density, the place retains a plush and airless atmosphere, one that softly luminesces under the normal light it encaptivates.

On the craggy, wet limestone expanse, a serene and ambiguous urban development is scattered about, its infrastructural chorus singing in an eerie, amplified harmony. Terrifically engineered, the factories, engines, and other sources of industry forge resilient roots intertwined with the landscape's geological elements. These machines unfailingly churn and huff, marvels of ingenuity transmuting raw resources into unassuming outputs.

However, the auditory symphony of the urban complex seems to be an anomaly within this strange, empty domain. It cannot dilute the pervasive silence that filaments through the architecture of the land and engulfs its haunted air. Any noise, even the rhythmic hum of industry, hollows out in the zephyrs and winds now condensing overhead. The din moves closer to a mere hum, which can only whisper in this cold and dark landscape.

This place, for as lucid as it may seem, defies the subtle cogs of a world regulated. Time, once a limping beast, now tread disdainfully upon the uneven, youth-infested terrain. Unused to heavy footsteps, it leaves some areas no less than fresh; it captures on exposed rocks and forming cliffs, making the land itself nearly ethereal. But fear not - patterns graze upon the mottled landscape with definitive regularity.

For all its peculiarity, there is one peculiar absence about the place: animal life. Indeed, it lacks fauna, a fluke in the routines that this stretch of otherwise land-marred surface takes. Yet, the distinctive construction, the same structures executing prodigious mechanical processes, suggests that even in this forsaken void, vestiges of greater earth remain. Even now, a cold, slow rain permeates with life, sullying the rotted surface, droplets glistening on waiting limestone. Amid this neonome affair, workaround the exhausted remnants of manufacture, a whiff of an ideal template still haunts as a faint whisper, repudiating the ever-lasting silence in waiting.
