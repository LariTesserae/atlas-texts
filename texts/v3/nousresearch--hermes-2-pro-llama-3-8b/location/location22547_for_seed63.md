The landscape that emerges is a strange, isolated territory tucked away in a remote corner of the universe. Known as the Asphalt Plains, it is characterized by its harsh, desolate beauty. Dusty and rocky, the Plains are devoid of water and plant life. The sky above is a deep, almost midnight blue, blanketed by a thick layer of wispy clouds that never seem to move. Shadows are difficult to come by, as the scant light that filters through the clouds is barely enough to navigate the landscape.

The Plains stretch out in all directions, but on a scale that feels intimate and close-knit. Towering peaks on the horizon mark the borders of this unique territory, and the land itself is sliced by deep, winding canyons that seem to reach down into the core of the planet. Despite the absence of flora, the landscape is frighteningly alive with fauna. Creatures--skittering, undulating, pulsating--that defy the biological, emerging from the mineral soil or climbing up the canyon walls. Despite the overwhelmingly active fauna, the Plains keep a strange quiet. A hushed silence that occasionally breaks with the trumpet sound of a creature calling to its kin, or the soft patter of tiny feet seeking to flee from a predator.

Built structures are scattered and fleeting on this arid plain, remnants of a civilization that may or may not have once thrived in this strange place. The scattered structures tell little stories of stone and rust, of slow decay in a place that seems to have forgotten the meaning of time.

The extreme conditions, the wild and otherworldly fauna, the isolated nature of the Plains--all contribute to a landscape that is both beautiful and terrifying in equal measure. It's a place that sucks you in with its strange allure, but leaves you feeling uneasy, feeling as if you stepped into another realm, a world that has forgotten everything we take for granted. This is the world of the Asphalt Plains.
