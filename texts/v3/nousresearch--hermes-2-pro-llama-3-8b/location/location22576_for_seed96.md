This landscape is unlike anything you've ever witnessed or imagined. It lies high upon a jagged, recently emerged plateau, its peaks still shimmering with moisture from the infrequent rains that fall upon it. It is a mineral world, its terrain dominated by a vast array of strange, young rock formations that rise starkly from the earth. Here, there is no plant life as you know it, no soft grass or verdant trees. Instead, the landscape is overshadowed by sharp, grey cliffs of volcanic glass, whose surfaces refract the bright, encompassing light in a thousand eerie, rainbowed hues.

The air is thin and frequently tinged with the scent of seared stone, as if the very rocks themselves are smoldering with the heat of the distant, long-dead volcanoes that gifted them their fiery, glassy nature. Despite the region's generally cool temperature, heat still rises from the ground here, a warm sigh from the depths that speaks of every geological secret hidden just beneath the surface. The stones themselves breathe and shiver away to the slow, patient cycles that govern all life here.

Yet, it is not only rock that dwells upon this plateau, but the faintest echoes of life itself in the poorly-lit, water-sodden corners that persist despite the Landscape's otherwise barren, bleak nature. Here, lines of misery, one might say; there, a desperate clinging to hope. It is as if the plateau is in a constant process of tentative birth, with the barest stages of energy life-cycle cramped into niches of dark, lonely weakness.

The sounds here are mostly quiet but for the strange whispers borne upon wayward breezes (for they rarely blow strong on this packed, saturated plateau). They flit between the cliffs in pursuit of some unspeakable, other-personae, as if seeking a benediction from the place's alien claims to life. Through these voices, or perhaps a lingering trace of that calling, a single note holds sway across this multi-feature plateau: wet.

Herein rests the key to life on this jagged landscape. The water, when it falls from the sky or seeps slowly from the rocks, creates a world unlike any other. For on this high, open expanse with all its clues to alien life forms, this relic of a frozen world—the delicate play of wet upon the jagged volcanic glass can unlock a spectacle of shimmering beauty and, in places, flashes of crystal-like structures that seem to hold traces of strangeness; whispers of life struggling to emerge from the embrace of ancient stones.

Nevertheless, this peculiar world seems to owe its form to a mysterious balance. The plateau's deck is packed full, yet mostly it is built with a primitive technology, with simple construction carried out by hand. Here, the anomalous trappings of true life contend with an oddity of the innocuous and the unusual; all working together in silent concert against the tide of time. Thus, on this high, jagged, mineral plateau shared among elements of emerging life and the energy patterns of things long past, an ever-unraveling, silent dance continues in the bright, thin light of the distant sky above.
