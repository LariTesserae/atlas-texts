The landscape is a densely packed, high-elevation grove of towering plants, each one more ancient and impressive than the last. It's a living mosaic, with each plant adopting the most stunning complexity in their architecture and the ways they interweave and inquire about one another. In some spots, a plant falls, and another quickly takes its place, while in other areas, plants might share tendrils and take each other's burdens.

At the courtyard scale of a cathedral, the grove houses a small yet developed town built at the roots and canopies of these colossal trees. Paths meander through the grove, uniting different branches of the town. Cobblestone streets are immaculately adjoined with groaning produce markets, bakeries, quilted-wall dormitories, polished-tool forges, and murmuring bars.

This urban symphony thrives in its balance between the technologically primitive and the masterful plant-as-architecture. In the town's heart, every local kid possesses the ability to shape with their hands. Here, with cobblestone anvil and welfaring hammer, stainless steel artisanship appears primitive yet high in its field.

Throughout the grove, dynamism comes with chaos in weather. Check for a sudden, terrible quiet, and around the same time, running footsteps down the streets. At street level, a frightful-aunt fog, knitted from earthly breaths, often enshrouds the town. Above the fog, a towering archway woven of weeping, water-infused tree barks marks the boundary of this living realm. The fog is no ordinary dampness; it condenses loose air into crystalline droplets, watering the roots of trees while bright sparks of light mix through every dangling droplet. These are the paths of insects flying into the proverbial light, entomological points of brightness piercing into the foggy mists.

Overall, the auditory sphere of the landscape is loud with fish in waters in the grove and the tumultuous sweeping fabric that the fog weaves in the Urban heights. The water, found near the surface, nourishes life in all its noise and chaotic change. In barrel-vaulted and rock-hewn abodes, low-pitched voices chant songs and solaces inherited from countless generations. In this aurally vibrant world, animals persist in overwhelming numbers. Residing amidst these natural wonders, humans busy themselves with combining smoked vegetable pigments, fermenting condiment preserves, and thumping seeded textiles.

In this grove town, the surprising part is just how spanning and stable it all is. Perhaps we are reading these signals correctly, or perhaps there's a better exploration waiting at the intersection of so many big numbers on these tiny grids.
