The landscape is a perplexing, enchanting realm on the edge of an enigmatic plateau, high up in the mountains. The environment betrays a strange, abrupt biogenesis - a spontaneous emergence of life from an otherworldly pool of primordial goop. The power of self-creation and the secret pulse of the world surge in every muddy ditch, every creeping thing, and weep from even the moss-leaden rocks. Its every form muted, but a polychromatic gloss seems to be layered within nature's veins. It shows in the dulling grays of rock and the seeping greens of fragile moss, and even in the deep purples of petrified flowers embedded in dull black slate. 

Climbing from the ground, the factual cold whispers in the whispering, lucid winds. Even the silence of the place, thins out, leaving a chilly draft that seems to carry the sign of deeper, more rigid colds held within the workings of the higher altitude. The wet then greedily starts to drink that cold, producing a strange vibratory soundscape, a Cinqucento of dripping water playing but an inches off from your ears.

As you overcome the steep assault of the raw play of elevation, frail and paltry as if even the plants understand, micro and sparse as spiderweb threads, fluttering with watery breaths. Here, you find yourself in a grove-or-room scale of economicstadt, a feat of architecture not *of* metal but *with* metal: a fusion forged by a practical alliance of primitive tribes and yet-to-be-reclaimed industrial sites. Rays of steely sharps slice through the sibilant deluge of water cascading from the higher peaks, pooling into pits, making a livelihood for the all-but-invisible flora.

You notice all of this with great effort, for the vivid flickering of living light, vibrant against the uniformity and the dense layering of truth, appears like a staggered ladder's rungs on every flora, every flitting creature, both up against and intertwining with the gearing teeth of metal-city workings.

This world of puce and steel, yielding and constructed with only thin veils of difference, offers the strain of fluctuating weirdness — the innate, unnatural attributes of the environment wriggling against one another, eliciting both a sense of strangeness and the heartening knowledge of the world's restlessness and unpredictable dynamism.

Experience this place so: a grove clung to, huddled about in the predatory clutches of a city structure, quivering with thermals from the saline breaths heavy with the iron hardened from mountain mines - a cathedral of gray-flowers entwining the rust-ravaged metallic bones. Thus, the fragile organic mingling and interconnecting, forever thriving in the cold, dense, turbulent industry that spills and weeps its rainbowed gory upon the land. A tantalizing combination of industrial marvels with the natural generation of this plateau's bounty. Witness the wail of the wind, in the ethereal chill of elevation and staggering humidity from riven mountainside, as it weeps into the puddle at your quiet, solitary, transient presence.
