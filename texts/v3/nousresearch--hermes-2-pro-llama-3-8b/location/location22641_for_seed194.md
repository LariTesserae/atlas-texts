In a remote corner of the ancient world, hidden within the folds of time, there lies an enigmatic realm shrouded in darkness and mystery. This secluded landscape is a testament to the primordial forces that shaped it, bearing the weight of countless eons and the imprint of every passing cycle.

The terrain is a low-lying expanse, where the ground beneath your feet is a mosaic of dense, intertwined vegetation that covers every inch of the landscape. Though it is difficult to see through the mass of flora, one can discern a plethora of plants adapted to the dimly-lit environment. These plants serve as the very architecture of this world, creating a tapestry of life in a realm bereft of water. Unlike other landscapes teeming with vegetation, this one is void of any dew or rain, as if it has somehow managed to escape the wetness of life. Instead, it is a desert of plant substance, with every living being relying on non-aqueous chemistry for sustenance.

Although the temperature is cool, a stark contrast with the infernal fires of old, the lack of water fends off the possibility of frost. This coolness serves as a gentle buffer against the overwhelming presence of the ancient landscape. Erosion has left its mark here, shaping the terrain through slow, patient processes. The vegetation and the land have settled into their final forms, as if frozen in time since the dawn of life. Hills and valleys, each a testament to the persistence of the plant as substrate, are nearly flattened, and the deep age of this place is felt in every stride.

The scale of this landscape defies easy description, as if the very fabric of space-time conspires to keep it hidden from prying eyes. The world is embedded within itself, with a pocket-scale that holds in its grasp a micro-environment where intimate-room like spaces exist for the lonely flora. These pockets of solitude are interconnected by a network of shadows, silent witnesses to the secrets of the earliest life forms.

Though the landscape is empty and hollow, devoid of animal life, its emptiness is not devoid of character. Minimal-flux and weak gradients hint at the strange dynamics hidden within it. The constant hum of the strange sounds that fill the air, generated by the plants themselves, lend to the feeling of eavesdropping on ancient, desperate whispers of life.

At the heart of this world is an urban settlement, a testament to the tenacity of living beings in the face of adversity. Despite the lack of water, the plants refuse to give up their hold on the landscape. Layered construction and infrastructure-density are emblematic of the plants' determination to survive, a struggle that spans for eons.

This urban landscape, though primitive, shows early signs of technology in the form of simple metal tools and fabrics. Hand-tools and manual-craft are the norm as the flora work tirelessly to fortify their dominion, creating a world within the void.

Despite the darkness that pervades this ancient place, the plants have found ways to adapt, to survive, and to flourish in the face of all odds. This landscape, caught in the struggle between life and death, poetically captures the essence of what it means to be alive in a seemingly inhospitable world.
