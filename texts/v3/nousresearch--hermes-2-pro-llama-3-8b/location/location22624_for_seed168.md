Hidden away in the vast expanse of a barely explored sector of the galaxy, there exists a world of unusual beauty and mystery. Its barren, mostly featureless landscape stretches for kilometers in every direction, such that it would take many years of travel even to get a sense of its true scale. The lack of water on this planet is a remarkable aspect of its geology, leaving the terrain awash in hazy, shimmering mirages of liquid mirroring the desperation of a world without rain. The air is moving, sometimes, thin and dry as a desert gust, sometimes thick with fine particles escaping from the porous surface of the silent, ancient rock.

Temperature-wise, the world is pleasantly balmy, comparable to a Mediterranean summer's day. A hint of gentle warmth lingers year-round, a constant perk of being shielded from the universe's cold and harsh elements by the atmospheric veil of an enigmatic gas giant looming not too distantly.

At a staggering height, standing prominently amidst the otherwise flat terrain are the claimstays of a myriad of forgotten machines, metallic giants frozen into place as if in reverence to their own inner workings. Though rust does not exist in this environment, the metallic menagerie still reflects the inevitable march of even the most indestructible away from their prime. Around these hulks of long-forgotten industry, debris and fallen stars gather, silently orbiting the core of a civilization that sought to harness the energy hidden in the planet's heart.

The planet creaks under the weight of an ancient ecosystem, now nearly inert but alive in ways not yet fully imagined. Lush vegetation pokes through the cracks in the pavement and the bases of ancient building shells, a testament to life's perseverance even in the harshest of conditions. Despite the lack of animal life, the microscopic fauna within the soil work tirelessly to create their own patch of vegetative utopia.

The skies are filled with an unusual ambient hum, a result of the planetary-scale industry of old. It is as if the planet itself is whispering an extension of its heartbeat, a faint, metallic resonance of rumors of toil now silenced by the inexorable passing of epochs.

A profound stillness pervades the air on this planet, as though time itself has paused, hesitating before some unseen precipice. The Old World clings to a fragile equilibrium amidst the relentless march of cosmic time. Despite (or because of) its emptiness, this landscape engenders an atmospheric alienness that is difficult to shake off, carrying an allure that might just pull one further into the strange, under-explored depths of its mysteries, despite the lack of fauna, changing dynamics, or an inkling of real water.
