The landscape that emerges is a strange and dense grove, nestled within the shadows of a mountain range. The terrain, though small in scale, is comprised of a twisted, young and fresh landscape. Amidst the jagged rocks, sharp cliffs, and soaring peaks, lives a grove of sparse but lasting vegetation. These twisted vegetation, compelled by the persistent dampness that seeps from above and below, provide an eerie, haunting atmosphere. 

Water is abundant but unpredictable. Rivulets of water, captured in depressions or crevices of the rock, trickle down to join with what seems to be a river, snaking up from the lower reaches of the mountain. The river passes through the grove, shimmering amongst the twisted vegetation and reflecting the looming heights above. The atmosphere is cool, with an enveloping darkness that clings to the shadows of the landscape.

Fauna, overwhelming though they may be, find shelter within the protecting grove. Creatures that could thrive in the sparse vegetation, large enough to avoid the lurking threats of this strange land, move with a force of their own. Amidst the echoing whispers of night, they stir a sound loud enough to test the patience of the slow, patient processes that persist across this elongated period.

The landscape is linear, with an upriver motion along the river. Here, young rock formations mimic the groove of the river, with an almost strange interaction. The landscape, dense with micro-environments, holds a weak gradient. One could walk in the path of the river and experience many different habitats in a small distance. The dynamics play out at a gradual pace, as the river patiently carves through the yielding rock and the vegetation clings to the cliffs longing for light.

This world, marked by the overwhelming sense of strangeness, holds an intriguing charm within the valleys of the mountains. Despite the absence of technology and constructed structures, a primitive manual-craft has shaped this grove, through the persistent force of the water and the will of the fauna. The darkness is a cover for a more profound mystery, waiting to unveil itself by those who dare to explore.
