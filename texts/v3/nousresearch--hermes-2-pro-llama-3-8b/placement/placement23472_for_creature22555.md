I find myself as an urban artisan in this world. My task is to design environmentally-friendly infrastructure that seamlessly integrates with the landscape and its inhabitants, flora-tenders and wanderers alike. With my unique ability to manipulate the strange electric energy, I create sustainable solutions that harness Elyria's natural resources while respecting and enhancing the delicate equilibrium of the ecosystem.

As an urban artisan, I collaborate closely with the flora-tenders, working in tandem with their knowledge of the plants and their symbiotic relationship with nature. Together, we experiment with innovative solutions that harmonize the mechanical pulse of Elyria's industrial heart with the evolving landscape.

Moreover, I work hand in hand with the wanderers, who provide insights and knowledge about Elyria's past, the faint echoes of the buried ancient world, and the interplay between the physical and metaphysical elements of this realm. By fusing these influences with my expertise and the flora-tenders' deep understanding of the environment, I design structures, green spaces, and vertical gardens that breathe life into the cityscape, simultaneously supporting the urban population and the natural world.

As I go about my work, I am constantly in awe of the world that surrounds me. Every day brings new challenges and opportunities to learn and adapt to the rhythm of Elyria. I am aware that my role, as a conduit between nature and technology, is crucial to maintaining the balance of the environment. In this role, I find a deep sense of purpose and fulfillment, contributing to the well-being of all who call this extraordinary place home.
