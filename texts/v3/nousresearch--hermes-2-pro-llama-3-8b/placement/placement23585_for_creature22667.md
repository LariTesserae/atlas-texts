As the description progresses, the landscape becomes more entrenched in a dreamlike atmosphere, a place where the laws of the world remain altered to accommodate the parameters of this hidden realm. The addition of machinery and industry adds a layer of mysticism, a formation of an almost cyclical process that exists in tandem with nature as if establishing the delicate balance of this otherworldly ecosystem.

The four seasons are replaced by a vast array of cycles. Cycles of water, of luminescent light, of ancient civilizations, and machines all work together to encourage life to persist.

These cycles are all tied into one another. The trickling water of the water-dwellers nourishes the roots of the trees, providing necessary minerals. The trees, in turn, provide the oxygen that the glowing creatures need to thrive. The ancient, adapted beings rely on the machines for their survival, carrying on the traditions of their ancestors. The machines are powered by a mysterious force driven by the ancient cycle of the industrial world, bolstered by the resources from the environment, creating an endless cyclical process.

But, all cycles have a beginning and an end. The endless progression of these grand cycles may be tinged with the air of suspension of time and dreamlike atmosphere, but the reality is that nothing can last indefinitely. Especially in a place where time is fluid, where cycles intertwine, and where life can be precarious. In this world, nothing can be taken for granted. Everything must be constantly adaptive and balanced to survive, as life continuously adapts to the ever-changing parameters of this landscape. The fluidity of this strange world is its greatest strength but may also prove to be its most significant vulnerability. For in the end, no cycle can be permanent; everything will change. 

Embedded in this landscape is the essence of survival itself, and within that struggle lies an endless dance of persistence and adaptation. In this hidden world, it is a dance that all inhabitants must learn to perform in their unique way, to stave off the inevitable tide of change, which is an inescapable part of life itself.
