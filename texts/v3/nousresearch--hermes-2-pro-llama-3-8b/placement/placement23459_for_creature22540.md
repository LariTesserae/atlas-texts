As a player of this world, I find myself as an apprentice druid within the covenant. My connection to the elements is limited, but as I wield the Gift of Time, I have an advantage in understanding the rhythmic cycles of life and death within the Hollows. Through my connection to the druidic assembly and the environment that surrounds me, I am learning their ways of living in harmony with nature and preserving the delicate balance that keeps our realm thriving.

As I progress through my training, I find solace in the presence of the few moments that linger in this eternal landscape and the beauty within the everyday struggles of our people. I become aware of the intricate web of life that exists in the Hollows, from the tiniest of insects to the majestic creatures that roam the rocky terrain. Each living entity contributes to the ever-evolving canvas of life and plays a vital role in keeping the delicate balance within our realm.

I learn to communicate with other druids, our affinity for the elements aligning us with a bond that transcends bloodlines. We work in unison, and as I progress through my training, I begin to understand the true power of our collective magic. Through our deep connection with nature, we can shape and guide the environment with a great sense of responsibility.

As a druid apprentice, my role in the Hollows is significant, for every action has potential consequences for the balance of life. I strive to learn more about the intricate tapestry of life within the realm and work diligently to maintain the harmony that sustains our home. In doing so, I come to realize the profound beauty within the landscape and the miraculous resilience that courses through every plant, creature, and druid that inhabits the Hollows.
