In this world, I would find myself in the rugged landscape of Dürö, nestled amongst the ancient boulders and sparse vegetation. As a resident of this plateau, I would most likely be a Jōdar, embodying the earth itself and sharing in the bioluminescence that lights up the twilight sky. My body would contain the untapped minerals of the land, and I would bask in the reprieve of the day's heat, lolling about the valleys and rims of this weary expanse.

Living in harmony with the other races of Dürö, I would share in the collective wisdom and knowledge of the land, preserving it as the landscape continues to beat with the rhythm of time. As a Jōdar, I would be a guardian of the land's stories, and my existence would be a testament to the cyclical processes that keep this plateau alive and thriving.

Despite the strange anomalies and disturbances that occasionally disrupt the quietude of Dürö, I would find solace and peace in the slow, gradual changes that shape this land. The enchanting world of Dürö, with its unique races and ancient roots, offers a life-filled experience that is both ancestrally dreamlike and celestially whispering. Here, in this pre-tool world brimming with unadulterated biological wonder, I would find a deeper connection with the earth and my fellow denizens, amidst a landscape that is simply, intriguingly, wholly and unashamedly itself.
