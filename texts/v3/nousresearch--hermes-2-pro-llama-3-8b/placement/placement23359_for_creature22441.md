As I find myself in the heart of Axus, I am the embodiment of the builders themselves. I am a glowing being, lithe and tightly adapted to the cool temperatures of the submerged world. My body pulses with energy, allowing me to navigate the darkness with ease.

I spend my days working alongside my fellow builders, crafting and enlarging the intricate architecture that adorns the waters of Axus. We harness the raw power of the submerged world, creating structures that serve as homes for the array of creatures now calling this place home.

Where others might feel the weight of the darkness and the emptiness of the thin atmosphere, I find purpose and clarity in my tasks. The mechanical hums and whispers of the sea are a symphony to me, a constant reminder of the life that pulses within Axus.

As I work, I observe the other creatures that now share this world with us. The bioluminescent fish that dart through the waters, the eels that glide silently, and the jellyfish-like entities that float and pulse. They are mesmerizing to behold, just as we are to them.

I cannot help but feel a sense of pride in the creation that is Axus. Our world is a testament to the power of imagination and the will to survive, and we guide it with purpose and with care. I am one small piece of this intricate ecosystem, yet I know that together we contribute to the ongoing cycle of life beneath the subterranean sea.

The world of Axus is a marvel of engineering and adaptation, and I am grateful to be a part of it.
