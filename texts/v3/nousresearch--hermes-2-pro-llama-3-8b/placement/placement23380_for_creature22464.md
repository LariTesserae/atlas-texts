I find myself standing alone in the passageway

Between what was and what is yet to come,
My soul at rest,

In the twilight of dawning day,
Half veiled in azure skies.

The landscape embraces me
With those beings who emerged
From pools of primordial times,
Rich in beauty, strange and bright.

Frail but resilient,
These storied creatures live
Amidst a tangle of puce and steel,
A wondrous dance of life weaved.

What once was barren now,
Thrives beneath the Church of Dark
Where vestiges of industry
Fused with nature's gifts entwined.

In the stillness, whispers linger
Of the world's unspoken melodies,
Held within the grasp of winds.
A reminder oft forgotten.

Cascades of water join the dance,
Inches off from where I stand,
Their symphony of drops,
A testament to life's indomitable will.

I find myself in a world alive,
Where organic and mechanical meld,
A place of great delight,
Where beauty hides in twilight's hue.

As I wander through this realm,
The fickle winds carry secrets,
Silent and profound,
Here in the world that breathes.

In the darkness of my heart,
A memory of this strange land
Emerges; a tapestry of wonder,
A testament to life's great song.

I am but a stranger here,
A fleeting apparition,
Yet, in this world of strange hue,
I find my own suspended soul.
