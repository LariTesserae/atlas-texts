My existence is intimately tied to the cycles of the environment around me, and I experience the world through the perspective of the plants. I am attuned to the subtle changes in the seasons, the fluctuations in the levels of moisture that affect the growth of the flora, and the adaptations and evolutions of the fauna that depend on them.

The factories and infrastructure of the urban settlement are not an intrusion, but rather an integral part of this world. In this way, the machines become as much a part of the world as the plants themselves, their hum and activity a constant presence in this strange, subterranean landscape.

When the rains are heavier, I develop additional roots to absorb the excess moisture, storing it for times of drought. I am a testament to the resilience and adaptability of life, exemplifying the delicate balance that exists between the forces of nature and the marvels of human ingenuity.

In this world beneath the Earth, I find myself in a constant state of evolution and adaptation, always changing, always growing.
