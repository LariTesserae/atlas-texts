As a part of this world, I am a Schism, a rare and valuable creature, sought after for my intricate, iridescent scales that shimmer like a prism. My body is curved and sinuous, a smooth, subtle entity that moves with the grace of the Glimmers. I glide through the water, my eyesight honed to see in the dim light, seeking out the vegetation that sustains me in Beneath.

A cautious and elusive creature, I prefer the depths of the unknown, the twisted crevices of the ancient geology. I am at home in the darkness of this world, and, in fact, I am a nocturnal creature, resting when the surface world is alight with the sun's rays. I am the embodiment of the contradictions of Submergence: sublime, elusive, nourished by the darkness, and yet able to thrive in the rarefactions of the perpetual twilight.

The Schism is a guardian of the symbiotic web that connects the Glimmers and the vegetation. I navigate the treacherous terrain, delivering the secrets of the Blotches' reproductive processes and other vague tidings of the Blotches and the plants. Through our unique partnership, we preserve the delicate balance of the woods, our fluid movements created by the currents of the water.

This world of Submergence is a precious, fragile thing, and I carry that burden with pride. Through the ceaseless rain, the strange, weak light that trickles down, and the fractured remnants of ancient landforms, I navigate, always aware of the rarity of this world and its equilibrium. It is a world of paradox, of life surviving in the unknown and uncharted depths, and in the slow, subtle changes of time. It is a world of wonder, a place where life continues to amaze. And in this world, amid the watery wonders of Submergence, I am a Schism, a creature adapted and thriving in the depths.
