As a falsely-spawned dream, I find myself in this world, a misplaced mystic in a cavernous landscape. The dim light and moisture of this subterranean world confound me, but I begin to acclimate to the strange beauty of it all. My path guides me toward a modest dwelling that blends into this grand environment, and as I walk from the misty veil I use my frail body to support the ancient wisdom of this place.

The dwellers of this world, with their primitive ways, seem trusting of me, almost attuned to my presence. As if by fate, my humble existence parallels their chaste knowledge and makes me a guardian of this Earth-shattering balance.

One day, a large creature comes upon me, the likes of which I have never seen before, both terrifying and majestic. I am awestruck by its enormity and ponder its place in the ecosystem. Through time, perhaps even measured in the eternal cycles of this dim and shifting world, I learn its exquisite role in this landscape, a creature of peace in amidst the strife. It has tusks like a tusked mammoth, a sharp antlers, and limbs that can withstand the harshest of terrains.

Initiated and called forth by its massive presence, my ephemeral mortal form makes way for a lasting bond between brute and human. In this act of faith and trust, I become the link between the creatures that populate the damp cracks of this world and the larger animals dominant in the valleys.

A cosmic dance of living in harmony ensues, dedicated to the preservation of the cooperative balance of life. Coexistence and adaptation drive the narrative of this world, where humans learn to live without destroying the very essence of the land.

As a guide, I change the course of this world, inspiring the dwellers to understand and cherish the rhythms of this land. With my guidance, they learn to read the subtle shifts and changes in the lay of the land, determining the health of the environment through the fine lines and contours.

I live till I grow old, surrounded by the cool dampness of the environment and the perpetual dim light that stains my eyes. My roots are in this world now, and my tomb is the soil that will house the next mystic to come. 

My spirit guides will remain with these gardens and lives that I helped cultivate, in this silent tribute to the beauty of nature and its unending wonders that I conferred upon this cavernous home.
