In this world, I find myself to be a member of the secretive hamlet, nestled within the lush vegetation and towering cliffs. As a craftsman, I am responsible for adorning our buildings with intricate designs and symbols, interweaving stories and history into the very fabric of our homes. I am constantly juxtaposed between the vibrancy of the natural world and the stillness of the people around me, seeking to create a harmonious balance within my work and my life.

I am awed by the land I inhabit, observing the slow dance of the elements and the gradual cycles of growth and decay that define our surroundings. As the seasons change and time adapts to the geology of this place, I feel an ingrained connection to the land and its inhabitants, both concealed and revealed. The world around me is imbued with a sense of mystique, and I do my best to capture this essence in my artwork, honoring the heritage of my ancestors and preserving the beauty of this extraordinary realm. In turn, I am enriched by the quiet strength and wisdom of the people, the industrious animals, and the looming cliffs and streams that shape our lives. This landscape and its inhabitants have become a part of me, and through my creativity and connection with the land, I find my own life taking on the slow, patient rhythm of the world around me.
