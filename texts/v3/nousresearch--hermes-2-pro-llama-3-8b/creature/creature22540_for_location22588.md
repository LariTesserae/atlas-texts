In the nether regions of the Hollows, a druidic assembly thrives. They are the architects and artists of this realm, their hands carved from stone, hardened by the elements. Their skin, darkened from years of toil under the dim sun, is a map of the land—a testament to countless laborious steps.

Bound by bonds beyond blood, these drifters move in harmony with the land, the water, and every living creature that shares the Hollows with them. Druids can communicate with nature, connect with it in such an intricate dance. They find solace in the budding plant life and even more in the blooming of new life.

But it's more than that; the druidic covenant bears the sacred duty to preserve and arbitrate the Hollows' intricate tapestry of life. Every druid is born into ancient roles they must take upon. Some are so attuned to water that they can shape it to their will; others yield the power over rock and soil, shaping, shifting, and guiding the very land itself.

Each druid has a unique affinity, a work of art of the elements—a distinct brand of magic that they wield. Still, they all know they are bound by a ceaseless struggle; to survive, they must forge cooperation between humans and nature—a harmony in harmony with the incessant cycles of death and life.

Time in the Hollows has an ebb and flow, an irregular beat; secrets Holden in the blackness of every life, every stone, every creak of the land. Some claim it endowed with The Gift of Time, as generations can be born and pass within a fleeting moment. But in the endless pursuit of delivering harmony, they find peace in their insignificance—the sheer scale of existence beyond anything they can conceive.

For druids are but vessels—a conduit for the cotton-wool moments that silently unravel in the Hollows. They bear witness to the silent whispers of former denizens that once called this realm home. Every echo of the Hollows is a melody of lives lived and lost; a symphony interwoven with oblivion.

Life in the Hollows is a dance—a tango of whispers and echoes; every heartbeat struggles in the gaps between the rocks of chaos. True to form, the Hollows finds beauty in broken and aged, love within hatred, and a flickering light that breaks through the encroaching haze of darkness.
