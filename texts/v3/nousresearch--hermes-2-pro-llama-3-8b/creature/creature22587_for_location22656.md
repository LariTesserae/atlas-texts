In this primordial land, the ichthyosans are the dominant species. These mermaid-like creatures have evolved to thrive in this world of perpetual twilight, their bodies adapted to withstand the biting winds and cold temperatures. Their flowing, translucent garments protect them from the chill, as well as camouflage them into their surroundings. 

The ichthyosans have formed a complex, interdependent society, with each individual playing a crucial role in the survival and prosperity of their people. Social hierarchy is based largely on their proficiency in accessing the limited resources of the land, with the most skilled hunters, gatherers, and fishers at the top of the social order. 

Their communication is a melodic, haunting language that echoes hauntingly across the landscape, with unique dialects and inflections based on regional variations in the environment. As a result, a single sentence could carry the weight of months of communicated knowledge, experience, and emotions. 

Despite their reliance on the land, the ichthyosans have mercilessly hunted the unique wildlife that inhabits the marshes to the brink of extinction. In some cases, they have crossed into the realm of the paranormal, summoning ancient spirits to aid them in their struggle to survive.

But as the different communities gather in the monolithic temples of their gods, it is clear that these creatures worship ancient gods. The rituals continue to be passed down from generations, and their beliefs hold fast to their origins, despite the constant fight for survival in a harsh environment. 

Against all odds, these mermaid-like creatures have turned their land into a living temple of ancient times, a place where the unknown Paranormal thrives. In this place, change and contrast are juxtaposed, and it is up to those who dwell here to harness and understand the secrets of this enigmatic realm.
