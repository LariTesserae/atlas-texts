The residents of this landscape are equally as hardy as the environment they inhabit. They are somewhat nomadic, following the sparse resources and the animal migrations, as their ancestral tribe has done for generations.

Their bodies are toned from the physically demanding life they lead, which involves climbing vertical rock faces and traversing treacherous cliff edges in their quest for survival. Their feet are toughened by the rough terrain, and their hands are skilled at using tools made from simple materials, such as stone and bone.

Despite their rugged exterior, these people are emotionally sensitive to their surroundings. They communicate through gestures and nuances, their bodies language reflecting the landscape's diverse elements. Their voices, when they do speak, are deep and resonant, echoing as if carried on the wind to reach distant listeners in the valleys below.

These people have a vast knowledge of the plant and animal life close to them. They have intimately studied the patterns of the seasons, the behaviors of their prey, and the effects of the elements on the landscape. Their existence is intertwined within the cycles of the natural world, surviving off the fruits of the land and the kills of the hunt.

Sacred rituals play a vital role in their communal life. They believe in offering respect and gratitude to their spirits, which are deeply connected to the forces of the land, the animals, and the ancestors who have gone before them. Their ceremonies include singing, dancing, and telling stories that reflect and reinforce the values of their tribe.

One would think that such a life would be solitary and lonely, but the social bonds among these people are strong. In times of need, they depend on each other as much as they do the land. They gather in communal living spaces that are carefully designed to integrate harmoniously with the environment.

Furthermore, there is a strong sense of pride and belonging taking place in this tribe. Those who leave to explore the world outside are often ostracized and criticized for breaking from the ancient traditions that define their identity. For them, their existence is not representable in any other place but this, leaving them grounded and deeply tethered to the landscape that sustains them, and to whose rhythms they are inherently connected.
