The residents of Elyria, or Elyrians, are a versatile collection of beings, each with its own unique characteristics. The most prominent among them are the flora-tenders, a group of individuals who have forged a bond with the plantlife, cultivating and nurturing the environment as their home. With a strong affinity for the natural forces, they possess a heightened awareness of the weather cycles, the growth patterns of plants, and the subtle nuances that make Elyria's ecosystem thrive.

In their endeavor to manage and sustain the landscape, the flora-tenders have developed a symbiosis with the plants, proudly referring to themselves as "walkers and talkers of the green." They blend seamlessly into the vegetation, their bodies adapting to the rhythms of the land. Their appearance varies from one individual to another, some taking on features of the plants they care for, and others echoing the colors and textures of the surroundings.

Despite their close relationship with nature, the flora-tenders are not immune to the technological advancements of Elyria's urban centers. They are well-versed in the workings of the factories and the machines that harness the energy of the environment. Some even develop a talent for modifying these devices, adapting them to the needs of both the landscape and the inhabitants.

In addition to the flora-tenders, there are urban artisans who have found a way to live in harmony with the wilderness. They often collaborate with the flora-tenders to develop sustainable ways of living, blending technology with nature to create vertical gardens, self-sustaining green spaces, and environmentally friendly infrastructure. The urban artisans are as varied as the buildings they construct, and many of them possess a unique ability to manipulate the strange, electric energy that courses through Elyria.

Lastly, there are the wanderers, elusive figures who move through the landscape with a shroud of mystery. They are connected to the strange, metaphysical elements of Elyria, able to commune with the fading echoes of the old world and seeking to uncover the secrets that the land holds. Some wanderers believe that the thinness of the veil between reality and the strange is tied to the ancient ruins buried beneath the green, and they often delve into the archives of knowledge to uncover the history of Elyria and the truths it contains.

In the grand tapestry of Elyria, the inhabitants – flora-tenders, urban artisans, and wanderers alike – weave together the threads of nature, technology, and mysticism to create something extraordinary. As they learn, adapt, and collaborate, they ensure the delicate balance of the environment persists, bringing life to the realm and enriching the landscape with their shared experiences.
