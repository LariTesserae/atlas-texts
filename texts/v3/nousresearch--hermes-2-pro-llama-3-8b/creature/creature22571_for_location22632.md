A select few are explorers, tasked with learning more about the still-mysterious depths of the valley, while still others are craftsmen, using the abundant raw materials available to create beautiful and functional objects for their fellow citizens. And some are protectors, guardians of the delicate balance that keeps this flourishing, if unconventional, ecosystem thriving.

These individuals come together in a society that values collaboration and innovation, working together to overcome the challenges of living in a world that is both isolated and interconnected, natural and technologically driven. The people of this hidden valley are respected for their ingenuity and resilience, their ability to fashion a life from the rock and the river, the machine and the plant.

Despite the solitude that comes with living in such a secluded environment, these individuals have formed a tight-knit community, bound together by their shared efforts to carve out an existence in the heart of the earth.
