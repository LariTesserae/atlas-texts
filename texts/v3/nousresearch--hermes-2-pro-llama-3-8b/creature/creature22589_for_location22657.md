In the drenched, ever-changing landscape of this world, a variety of dwellers find their home. The flora, with its diverse range of ferns, fungi, and flowers, serve as the primary inhabitants, unfettered by the tumultuous storms that define their environment. These plants grow with unyielding zeal, coiling and intertwining with the soil and stone, both plant-as-substrate and plant-as-architecture vying for dominance.

Despite their resilience, animal life is sparse within this realm. The air is devoid of the sounds and sights of creatures that would typically call such a place home. Instead, the plant life pulses with energy and reaches out with tendrils, seeking connection with other forms of life and forging tenuous alliances when storms provide brief moments of respite from the chaos.

The infrastructure that exists within this landscape is limited, with towns and valleys folding into the curves of the terrain, layer upon layer of organized construction reflecting the vibrant growth of the plant life. This built environment melds with the natural landscape, creating an intricate interplay between the two that is both odd and beautiful.

Humans dwell in this place as well, their voices rising above the symphony of nature to create a chorus of a million accents, speaking a language that oscillates with a lilting, unknown tone. They cling to life among the plants on which they depend, wary of the storm's tempestuous moods.

With each storm, alliances between the dwellers of this realm are tested and broken, only for new, temporary connections to form for the sake of survival. It is a world that thrives on chaos, where life is both fragile and persistent, and where order holds little sway. The ground beneath their feet prickles with the energy of life, a reminder of the dream that sparked the birth of this realm. Such is the place where the constant damp brings forth a kaleidoscope of life, and where the heart of the storm beats strong and wild.
