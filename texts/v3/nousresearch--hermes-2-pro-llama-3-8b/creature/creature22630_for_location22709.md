The native inhabitants of Synchweb, known as the Trivii, are a curious race of beings evolved specifically to a life beneath the perpetual veil. They have developed an extraordinary symbiotic relationship with the environment which in turn has allowed them to adapt, flourish, and survive in this seemingly inhospitable world. 

Trivii are caprine in shape, resembling small to medium-sized golems with four limps; two arms and two hooves. They possess a unique, xenomorphic physiology, with a tough outer epidermis built to handle the rigours of Synchweb. Despite their alien appearance, Trivii are gentle and biped-friendly, performing tasks such as tending to the ethereal plants, maintaining the miasma that provides the area's energy source and trading with the rare travelers of this curious world.

Materializing mysteriously from a schism in the icy rock, the Trivii have an almost spiritual connection with the land. They are miners and caretakers, administering to the needs of Synchweb as well as extracting the minerals necessary for their society's sustenance. Their communal way of life cultivates an openness, with the Trivii collectively helming the well-being of the land and its people.

The unique relationship that exists between the Trivii and Synchweb is part of what preserves the environment's fragile balance. The ethereal miasma is a colossal source of energy for both the flora and fauna of Synchweb, as well as providing the Trivii with the environmental resources needed for their own survival. In turn, the Trivii provide the sustainment and maintenance of the miasma, mate-producing synchweb trees and assisting the Selfxy organism - a strange yet pivotal part of Synchweb’s endemic environment.

These Selfxy organisms are the wireless energy that courses through Synchweb: they are both a byproduct and crucial element in the synthesis of the miasma. They are organisms that possess a spontaneity and existence independent of external sources, working in synergy with the Trivii and the ethereal miasma to sustain life on Synchweb. 

And then there’s the ruined metropolis, a relic that the Trivii treat with circumspection and reverence. These ancient structures are an echo of a time when Synchweb was apparently more vibrant and alive than it is now. A civilization based on industrial progression once thrived here; the sleeping machines a constant reminder of what was lost. Yet, the Trivii neither fear nor ignore them, understanding them as an essential piece of their existence.

In their empathic activities, the Trivii bring life to Synchweb, and bring it into a synergistic connection with the Selfxy. The Trivii understand that the communication between these pieces is integral to maintaining balance. Understanding this, they plug and unplug the energy of the meta-nocturnal miasma and Selfxy organisms, safeguarding the continuation of life as it is.

In their symbiotic relationship with Synchweb, the Trivii embody its essence. As much a part of the environment as the miasma and Selfxy, the Trivii take the form of a delicate dance with the ecosystem, embodying a living piece of the land’s puzzle. Their story exists in the cracks between Synchweb’s complex tapestry of existence, blending the dark mystery of its ruined metropolis with the simple beauty of its Miasma. 

The Trivii of Synchweb represent not just a people but a living flourish of its creation story. Their symbiosis with the strange world around them seems to whisper that all things, however different they may be, can share the same vibrant life pulse. In this place of intense, alien beauty, the Trivii might be the most extraordinary of Synchweb's vibrant secrets.
