The inhabitants of the Asphalt Plains are a diverse and enigmatic collection of creatures that have adapted to the harsh, barren environment. They defy our understanding of biology, hailing from a place where animal, vegetable, and mineral blur into unrecognizable forms.

At the bottom of the food chain are the Skitters, small, insect-like creatures that move in an unsettling manner, with too many legs and erratic movements that make them difficult to track. Their chameleon-like colors help them blend in with the rocky surroundings, which makes them even more elusive and adds to the feeling of unease on the Plains.

Moving up the ladder, we have the Wind Crawlers, snake-like entities that glide across the ground with an eerie, undulating motion. Their physical appearance is unsettling, with multiple tentacle-like limbs that end in a series of warts or bumps, which may serve as sensory organs or even tools to manipulate surroundings. Their eyes are the only constant, sharp and piercing, always watching. Wind Crawlers are carnivorous, feeding on Skitters and smaller creatures, and they have the ability to create an eerie, rhythmic call that echoes through the canyons, communicating with others of their kind.

Living in the canyon walls are the Slicks, a curious organism that likely uses rock to its advantage. Slicks have a slimy exterior that allows them to attach to the rocky walls or even the underside of ledges to remain hidden from potential predators. Their eyes are buried deep within the mucus, a protective measure that might also help their camouflage. Little is known about their diet, but research has suggested they may feed on Dew Dusters.

The rarest and possibly the most dangerous inhabitants of the Plains are the Fading Ones. These Things drift between moments of substance and apparition, often causing confusion among other creatures. They never seem to be in one place for too long, fading in and out of sight like a chameleon's color changes, blending into their surroundings. It's believed that they feed on the wave-like patterns of light and dark, filtering the limited illumination the Plains receive.

There are traces of possible advanced civilizations on the polished remnants found throughout the Plains. However, these ruins remain mysterious, without any clear indication of their origin or purpose. The Asphalt Plains are a self-contained, secretive world, hosted by a seeming multitude of bizarre, ethereal, and unsettling lifeforms. The atmosphere of perpetual twilight, the strange fauna and flora, and the ruins of unknown civilizations create a hauntingly beautiful but terrifying ambiance. It's a world that feels alien and inhospitable, but also fascinating, as one cannot help but wonder about the countless unknowns that are hidden beneath the rocky, dusty surface.
