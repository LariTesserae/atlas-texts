The inhabitants of this landscape are truly unique and fascinating. They are the engineered beings, remnants of the once-thriving civilization that built and operated the factories and engines that still exist today. These beings have adapted to the harsh conditions of their surroundings, evolving to withstand the cool temperatures and dim light that bathe the landscape.

Operating the machines left behind by their creators, these beings are the heart of the landscape's ecology. Their industrious nature and adaptability have allowed them to survive in a place where water and life as we know it have struggled to take hold. As a result, they now form a symbiotic relationship with the twisted plant life, relying on one another to survive in this isolated world.

The engineered beings have evolved to be a vital part of this extraordinary ecosystem. Their machinery and the resources they extract from the landscape provide the energy and materials needed to sustain both themselves and the strange plants that surround them. Though they may not have the same physical form as the plants or exhibit the same biological processes, they still play an essential role in the balance of this realm.

As they operate the machinery and tend to the environment, the engineered beings have come to understand the rhythms and cycles of their world. They have learned to respect the balance of nature and the importance of maintaining equilibrium. It is through this understanding that they have become one with the landscape, embracing their role in the unique ecosystem that exists in this hidden world.
