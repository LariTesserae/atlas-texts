The denizens of Dürö conceive of themselves as the offspring of the earth itself; they embody isomorphic relationships with the abiotic elements that surround them. Their bodies synchronize with the shifting landscapes, evolving in concert with the weather patterns that course their regions.

The Jōdars, large and round, are the embodiment of the earth itself, projecting bioluminescence to emerge in the twilight. Following the ebb and flow of the day, the Jōdars bask in the reprieve of the day's heat, lolling amongst the valleys and rims of Dürö's expanse. Their bodies contain a dream of minerals yet untapped, the magmatic remnants of a bygone era, and their energy peaks with the setting sun is a spectacle worth witnessing, illuminating the land with a faint, otherworldly glow.

The Munits, thin waifs of a race, embody the ancient water systems that zigzag throughout Dürö; they stand as a synecdoche of the tumultuous existence of Dürö's waterways. Like the water they represent, the Munits' energy ebbs and flows with the day, and they are known for their intelligence and wisdom. Their ghostly hovers, thin and ethereal, are sighted near water-bearing regions, an embodiment of the fragile yet resilient essence of water itself.

The Udars, twisted and gnarled, are akin to the ancient roots of the sparse landscape. Blemishing the area with a vitality that clashes against the rocky surface, their bodies are enigmatic, contorted shapes, reminding one of the wiry, twisted roots that cling to the jagged terrain. Their arcana are said to embody wisdom kept close to the chest, but a wronged Udar might unleash ancient, abusive replications of the very earth which bore it; some Udars are said to carry a shard of lightning within them. They are seen as powerful guardians of the land and its stories, and their knowledge is coveted by all who live in these weary heights.

Together, these three races originate from the time-cycled lives witnessed within the ancient bedrock of Dürö. Their existence is preserved by the continued beating of the landscape's heart, while whispers of hope spring eternal for a future where such wonder persists, a dream that lives not only in post-human eras but in the ever-present pause of this time-locked land.
