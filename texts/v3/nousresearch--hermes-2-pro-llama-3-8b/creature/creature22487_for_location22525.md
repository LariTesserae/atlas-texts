The inhabitants of this place are as diverse and unusual as the environment they've adapted to. The flora in the enclaves, having clung so tightly to their resources, have evolved into something akin to symbiotic network, with each species lightly touching and borrowing from the others. They have learned to exist in this state of stasis, these plants have become adaptable to the harshness of their circumstances. Time moves differently within this small but rich ecosystem, so what may have once seemed swift and monstrous now appears languid and careful.

The shadows that rarely fall, and the darkness that permeates much of the terrain, have given rise to life unlike any seen elsewhere. The inhabitants of these deep, dark recesses can truly be considered native to Aridnium. Their adaptations, like the break from conventional reality that brought them to life, are so profound that they seem as otherworldly as the planet itself. Despite the inhospitable conditions of their world, they've found ways to thrive and build new ecologies in the unlikeliest of places.

Among these organisms are those that owe their existence to the metal-rich veins that crisscross the landscape. Now, stillness can also seem like a potent form of motion -- the patient, slow changes wrought by these organisms reflect the broken but resilient world they've built. They have found alliances with the plants lingering above ground, making use of even the cast-offs from their tiny ecosystem. Sometimes their darkness seeps out into the barren expanse surrounding this hidden haven, like a sinister infection that will transform everything that it touches.

The industrial remnants, the old machinery and rusting metal of forgotten factories, now have their own unique ecosystem as well. They are home to a variety of creatures adapted to the cold, the feel of rough metal against their skin, and the smells of oil and decay. These creatures dwell in the ruins, sometimes with the flora above, sometimes, when conditions are right, with those below. They are spokepersons for all that has been lost and left behind, and for everything that has emerged in its place.

As time continues to move in its strange, broken way on Aridnium, these creatures and plants, the few of them that are lucky enough to survive and thrive, exist in a precarious balance. The world itself is kept alive by their adaptations and by the echoes of its industrial past. Together, they form a new and strange ecosystem, built from the broken pieces of a dead civilization and the sheer force of natural selection. The inhabitants of this place are the ultimate survivors, which is to say, they are anything but ordinary. Maybe they represent a future that, against all odds, just might be possible.
