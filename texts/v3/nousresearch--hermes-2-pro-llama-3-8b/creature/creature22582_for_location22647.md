The inhabitants of this wondrous and often damp landscape are those who have chosen to live in harmony with its natural surroundings. They are a community of people who value the delicate balance of this ancient ecosystem, and have adapted their lives around the rhythms and cycles of this environment.

These individuals exhibit a resilience that permeates every aspect of their lives. They are as much a part of this landscape as the trees and the rivers, their bodies built to withstand the cold temperatures and the wet conditions. Their homes are a testament to this relationship; small, simple huts built in accessible spots, with no desire for grandeur or unnecessary adornment.

The locals have long since learned to respect the technology of the past, repudiating the noise and chaos of modern life. Instead they rely on manual craft and rudimentary techniques, taking pride in the honest simplicity of their creations. Metal tools have all but vanished, replaced with stone and seasoned skill, the latter being passed down through generations. The implements of a simpler time still dot the landscapes, their use a fading memory upon the tongues of the people.

In this world, the extraordinary is simply ordinary, and the locals have learned to keep a watchful eye on the subtle shifts of their surroundings. Those who have lived here long enough understand the ever-changing nature of the landscape, learning from the animals and insects, observing the dance between predator and prey. The unique illumination cycles, where some flora lures nocturnal life and others grow in indirect light, further engage their routine and way of life.

This world is alive, humming with the vibrant sounds of fauna and the rich scent of exotic blooms mixed with earthy undertones. Even in the silent core, the wind whispers among the leaves, and a river murmurs nearby. The peace of the place is profound, the constant dampness neither melancholic nor oppressive but rather a natural, visceral connection to the world. In the silence, there is a symphony of life.

Life in this land is not without its challenges - the weather, the terrain, and the rigorous work needed to carve out even a modest living. However, these people have found a resilient and fulfilling existence in this place of perpetual motion. This environment that captivates with its soft light and lush foliage also provides the backdrop for the harmony of its inhabitants: their balance amidst conflict, their simplicity amidst complexity, and their peace amidst light and dark.
