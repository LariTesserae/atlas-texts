In this colossal city, a diverse array of beings coexist, each uniquely adapted to the harsh and ever-changing environment. The inhabitants embody the essence of postmodernism, reflecting the city's constant state of flux and reinvention.

At the core of the city, the Muddlers thrive. Resilient and resourceful, they navigate the maritime labyrinth of the swampy outskirts, their body structures infused with the essence of the flora that lines their edges. Their eyes, keen to the aquatic environment, bear a deep understanding of the native eels and crocodile men, their mouths formed to mimic the song of the swamp that binds them to their watery home.

Down in the teetering wretched basements of the city, the Unearthers toil. Anchored by the instability on which they rely, they have developed chameleon-like skin, capable of camouflaging them in the ever-shifting hues of their surroundings. Air, water, and soil are their media, via networks of pipes and cables that cling precariously to the cliffs; from there, they pull apart the throat of living exoskeletons and other thriving dimensions, their fingers prying open the porous tracheary networks of the earth.

In the factories and mines that bleed red with the raw materials of this world, the Strikers arrive. Equipped with the latest cutting-edge technology, they have mastered the art of blasting apart the rock face with burr and spangle light. In the city's veins, where the veins themselves pulse with an almost vital energy, they are legion; their machines bore through previously uncharted territories, tapping into the geothermal heart of the planet.

Moreover, the Highflyers soar above the plateaus, their wings carbonated after the image of dragon's and moth's feats. They commune with the atomic air of this alien geography, their wholly-outlined planes printing viewings and golemate-chants against the twilight where the metropolophony reaches its most virile attenuation.

And lastly, the Planetarys dwell in the spherical hives coiled above the valleys, where the fractal tendons and ossefilaments of this Cybernetic Wild extend. Their bodies are frivable and wrinkle-soft, carrying the seeds of harvest that are clouds and wind and none else; hollow and full of voids, their need for gravity is near-zero. The outside world in high-orbit and precarious-spot, they plumb the NanoCOSMOS through scattered dirt and nutrient and spore, set free from the ground, and sing with the elements as they speak the spanglish of their own volatile metalsong.

United by a rough-hewn patchwork of bridging languages, these diverse and dynamic beings live in a perpetual state of adaptation and transformation. In the ever-shifting landscape of this megacity, they embody the resilience and resourcefulness of their environment, constantly evolving to survive in the midst of the darkness that reigns supreme.
