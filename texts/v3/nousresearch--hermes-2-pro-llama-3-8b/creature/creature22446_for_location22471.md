In such a landscape, there exist organisms both mighty and minuscule, all intertwined intricately in the fabric of this ecosystem. The hills and stones themselves serve as lifeforms, with flora springing from every crevice, painting the barren granite with tufts of chlorophyll. Silhouettes of various forms, dictated either by happenstance or ardent planning, discernibly manifest on these structures - teeming with mosses and lichens, neither restricting nor nurturing the charred teeth of ancient hives, pedestals to now extinct fungal giants.

Inverting on walls, craggy outcrops, and refuse of ancient infrastructures, fungi predominate amongst the flora. Their adaptation, a byproduct of the harsh environment they live in, has given them the spike to thrive anywhere and everywhere, their structure as a stomatal system providing a home for few. The ecologies in this zone categorically evolve around these dank infestations. The plant-substrate, when true, are species that specifically feed on these fungi, stripping them of valuable nutrients, and shading them out, alternating in a peculiar DD-EC (Defense-Deception Ecosystem) interaction. 

Beneath stones and under logs, elusive fauna navigate their lives. They exist ground-up in the briny solution that steams from this mineral terrain, crumbling freckled mantles with relative ease. They move in darkness - primordially equal to the bronze age plants that flourished hereabouts and left their cyanobacterial blue-range behind in the Permian rocks. These creatures are not of the ordinary pint-sized amphipods that thrive in streams; instead, these beings possess archaic chelicerae, serrating the grime for sustenance.

These lifeforms partner with the plant-substrate was an ancient trick of terra-forming the barren terrain. Collaborating as a microbial mat, they sequestered sulfur and nitrogen, converting carbon dioxide into usable oxygen, drinking up the water's brine, and offering instead nutrients that encouraged communities of bacterial mosses to colonize. 

In the muted kinfolk of nature and loneliness, this bare-witted landscape creates an audible silence; where the only jabberwocky that echoes is the haunting hum of quiet tides lapped up by hushed waterfalls, a cacophony of flora-bound biomechanisms; where lightning strikes coalesce a misfortune of stone loons and humping ammonites. It's a place where the fauna knows not the victim of human progress - but beckons of their own kind - existing freely restrained by geological solidarity. 

That being said, one can watch the natural world thriving and retreating, unfazed by our absence. This magical world of ours, once altered by human touch, can still be a wellspring of biological miracles. These organisms eke out a living on a treacherous land, surviving against all odds. As you stand among these living relics, you touch what's pristine and untouched - the chronicling of life on this Earth.
