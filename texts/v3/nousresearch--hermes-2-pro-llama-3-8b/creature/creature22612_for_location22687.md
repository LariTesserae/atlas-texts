In this land of jagged, green mountains, there exists a unique congregation of sentient beings, cohabituating alongside the flora in a harmonious and intricate dance. There are those with unimaginable power, able to bend and warp the very landscape to fit their grand designs, like the ancient Titans or Dragonborn from distant tales. They carve defiles into the mountain ranges, creating passageways for their kin, providing shelter under their colossal bodies as they take respite from their monumental tasks. Their mere presence can cause the ground to tremble or the air to crackle with energy as they perform some arcane task within their deific powers.

In addition to these powerful titans, there are those who have chosen a more subtle path, taking residence within the living structures forged by the plants themselves. Rather than reshaping the land, they observe and learn from the wisdom of the ages-old flora and fungi, incorporating their essences into themselves. Their forms are stalk-like, intertwined and entwined with the very essence of the land, devoting their existence to comprehending and commingling with nature. They go unseen and unheard by most, only making themselves known in times of need, utterly ancient and mysterious in their demeanor.

Lastly, there are those who wander the landscape freely, fleet-footed and quick-witted, often with the aid of a special race of fey horses that are endemic to this untamed land. These wanderers are relentless in their determination to unravel the mysteries of existence in this eternal now.

It is said that these beings all coexist, each fulfilling a purpose in the grand tapestry of this land. Together, they embody the unending struggle and awe-inspiring majesty that defines this land of perpetual change. So, in the jagged, green mountains, the panorama of this world unfolds not as static and lifeless, but teeming with life, wonder, and infinite possibilities, where even time submits to the power of the land.
