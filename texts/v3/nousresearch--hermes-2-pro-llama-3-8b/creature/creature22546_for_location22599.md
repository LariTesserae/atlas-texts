In the heart of Veridese, a mutable gallery of life resides. The flickering, gas-lit chiaroscuro within the town brings forth a motley assortment of tenants.

On the edges of the settlement, denizens of the wild find solace under the thin canopy of native flora. The quiet guardians of the unassuming landscape, they weather the changing environment with a quiet, stoic grace. These creatures have adapted to the grey OS and the wild, wet world beyond, their line blurred between survival and the survival of the village. The wetland's fauna—fleeting and somewhat fanciful—are as tied to the industry of Veridese as they are to the perilous wilderness that encircles the village. Many will never know the lush rainforests that once graced the lands; they will be forever entwined with the village, blooming amidst the cacophony.

Within the industrial heartland of Veridese, a different caliber of life makes its dwelling. Mankind's tireless endeavor has given life to beings whose existence relies solely on the ceaseless production that suffuses Veridese's ailing skies. Machines of all sorts fill the cracks and crevices between the factories, humming and whirring with insatiable purpose, maintaining a cold drive that allows the village to progress.

And, as one might expect, woven within these societal cracks emerge a host of beings that, though not born of the chaotic fusion of wetland and human endeavor, have come to call Veridese their home. These migrants bring new faces and accents to the already diverse landscape, creating intricate webs of personal histories and pursuits. For many in Veridese, they simply live amongst the constant hum of industry, the background noise of human ambition enveloping them since birth. 

Veridese thrives not despite its wild and urban dichotomy, but because of it. The ceaseless drizzle of rain, the vibrant soil, and the cacophony of progress all come together to form an ever-evolving ecosystem, spurred on by the hands of humanity. This world, borne of a warring mingling of primordial wilderness and the inexorable march of human ambition, ensures that Veridese's inhabitants remain suspended in a perpetual state of tension, with the potential for glorious ascension and swift collapse equally ever-present.
