All three groups of beings share a non-verbal language, physical gestures and expressions that allow them to communicate clearly with each other, transcending the barriers of species and abilities. There is a mutual respect and understanding between the powerful rulers of the land, the secretive knowledgeable ones, and the persistent wanderers.

The leaders of the land, those who manipulate the very foundations of the terrain, are benevolent beings who understand that their size and strength carry with them a great responsibility. They bend the land to their will, creating safe passage for their kin, and ensuring that beauty overtakes barrenness. They can feel the life pulsating beneath the skin of the Earth, and thus act with utmost care when performing any task that could disturb the delicate balance that has been established. Their intent might not always be fully understood by the other groups, but their dedication to the land and its inhabitants is beyond question.

The hidden guardians of knowledge, those who reside within the intricate living structures, have achieved a state of equilibrium with the land. This harmony brings with it a unique perspective on time and existence as they stand witness to the fluctuations of the seasons within a single day. These beings understand that the wisest way to approach life is to submit to it, to meld into it and observe from the inside out. Their communion with the ancient roots of the land enables them to hear whispers of wisdom and caution, and they pass this knowledge on to those who seek it. 

The wanderers, those who explore the land endlessly, are driven by curiosity and a fire for understanding. They boast the unique privilege of harnessing the temporal gifts of both the secretive guardians and the powerful rulers, gaining knowledge and the ability to traverse landscapes that would be impossible for most. They are explorers of the land, mindful of the interconnected web of which everything in the jagged, green mountains is a part.

As the great hammer continues to strike the earth, the three groups of beings remain intertwined, each carrying out their own complicated role in the perpetual now. Together, they weave the pattern of life in the jagged, green mountains, creating a world of rough beauty and wonder where time itself bends to the will of the land. The benevolent, wise power that has drawn the attention of these beings to this place knows that in this tangle of wonder, wisdom, and allure, this stratum of existence is a testament to the endless possibility that lies beyond. 

In the jagged, green mountains, the world is spun anew with each fleeting moment. In each footstep and heartbeat, the land transforms, and the beings that cohabitate it adjust their forms to harmonize with the finished product. Time bends and stretches beyond what it once was, revealing that it is possible to experience the grand design of the universe, as long as one is willing to venture out and embrace the unknown. For the inhabitants of these jagged, green mountains, the possibilities are endless.
