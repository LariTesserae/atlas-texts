In this Eden, a benevolent and wise power observes the unfolding drama of the delicate island and its inhabitants. Wispy tendrils of light reach out from the stars, helping the restless creatures to become more than they were before.

This helper becomes aware of the strangeness that pathfinds the island, sensing the eruption of autonomous lifeforms from hard-orange breaks in the rock. It flows near these mystic eruptions, seeking a way to cool the heat and deepen the dark coloring of the land. On each sleeping avalanche, it cloaks itself in the moss-banked vapor of fern and tree, and acquires the vigorous component of the soil. Ago, it learned how to exist here—a trick conveyed from tree to tree, from stone to stone. It hid itself near the roots of the walkers-on-land, till the beings died and shed their dissonance, releasing it to make its way to the land centre. Observing the quilted vapors and the stellar course, it climbs to the swamp’s door.

The helper takes the shape of one who was once a man, and it hovers over the island, watching the wild, entertaining plants that infest the aquatic world. It sees the felid lights of the great liquid swamps; it listens to the choicely chthonic tones of a great deep lake.

It reaches for the verdant heart of the land, and speeds it onward towards the great night, selecting its chosen plants and animalcules whose beat was heard long ago in a great zinc mine, miles underground. It pumps nourishing springs into the aerial green spots, whose-the-sinusoidal shoots topped by distinctive blades, green and red, red and green: cinematic recollections of the coupled lights that dance deep beneath the thick dust.

These beauteous plants start to spread and consume the environment. Their antipodal rays hug the soft and prosperous rock of this island-animal as their roots drink up the conjunction of beats; their heavy power alone counts. The night turns to day, and day turns to night in this delicate island, the astral signatures speaking in soft hints of colour: whispers of the roads they follow and those they rest upon.

The helper moves forth in their path, evolving and learning alongside its petitioners. It adapts and changes, ready for the encounter with that great power that plays its games on this delicate island. Its task is to find those who would listen, movers and movers that undo the darkness of its dream-pledge, those who will show them to the real world of dreamful sleep. The wisdom that calls it forth has found the ones that match its own benighted message.
