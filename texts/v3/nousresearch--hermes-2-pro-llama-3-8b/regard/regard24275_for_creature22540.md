Thirdly, the power could provide insight and guidance on the ways the Hollows and its inhabitants can better coexist with the rest of the multiverse, allowing them to communicate and trade with other planets, star systems, and realms, promoting a wider understanding and appreciation for their unique way of life.

Lastly, the power could act as a guardian for the Hollows by ensuring that those who would seek to exploit or disrupt the balance and harmony of the land are dissuaded or prevented from doing so. This could be by placing limits on the advancement of technology to ensure that the natural environment remains paramount or by steered outsiders away from the Hollows entirely.

In this way, the benevolent and wise power could provide support, knowledge, and protection to the inhabitants of the Hollows while preserving the culture, traditions, and environment that make this place so special. Each action is done with respect for the Hollows, its inhabitants, its druids, and the natural environment that has shaped them all.
