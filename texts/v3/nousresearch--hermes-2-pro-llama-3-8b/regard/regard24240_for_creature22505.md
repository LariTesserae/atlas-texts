If the benevolent and wise power chose to intervene, it would focus on a few key areas in an effort to make the Asphalt Plains a more sustainable ecosystem. 

One initial priority would be the addition of water. By altering the landscape, channels could be carved out to channel water from distant sources into the Plains. The channels would be hidden, blending in with the rocky surroundings to avoid disrupting the natural look of the region. Slowly over time, water would start to accumulate in the Plains, leading to the growth of vegetation that could support a more diverse population of animals.

Next, the introduction of different plant species would help diversify the ecosystem. Certain plants that have the ability to extract moisture from the air could be introduced to help increase the water content of the soil. As the plants start to establish themselves, they would stabilize the soil, reducing erosion and creating more areas for animals to thrive. The presence of plants would also provide the Plains with some shade, which would help regulate the fluctuating temperature between day and night.

As the vegetation continues to grow, the benevolent power would focus on protecting the baby steps of the new ecosystem. Measures could be taken against overhunting by predators, and a gradual release of new animal species could be implemented to help balance out the food chain.

The remains of the old civilizations could be excavated and restored. This would not only preserve the stories and history of the old inhabitants but would also add much-needed shelter and resources for the new inhabitants of the Plains. This would help create a symbiotic relationship between the ancient and new inhabitants, creating a shared history and even commonalities in culture which could lead to cooperation between the two groups.

Although the Asphalt Plains is a harsh environment, the introduction of water and plants would help make it a more diverse and sustainable ecosystem. Over time, these changes would produce a thriving world teeming with a variety of activity and life, all drifting slowly with the harsh rhythm of the Plains. Although the benevolent and wise power would want to avoid disrupting the planet's balance, it still has the ability to vastly improve the lives of the inhabitants of the Plains. With these changes, the Oil Patch Plains would no longer be a fearsome, alien landscape but would become a home for all its fascinating life forms.
