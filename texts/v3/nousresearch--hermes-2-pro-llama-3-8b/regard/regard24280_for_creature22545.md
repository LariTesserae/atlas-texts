In response to the benevolent and wise power's attention, the Subaqueous Heights would undergo a gentle transformation. The landscape's primary focus on dual elements of water and stone would remain intact, but the inhabitants would evolve.

Firstly, the flora would begin to flourish with new plant species adapted to the unique conditions found here. These new plants would color the greenscape, bringing a more vibrant feel to the landscape. Visitors would marvel at the variety of life forms that have made their home in the Subaqueous Heights.

Secondly, the igloo-like structures would be redesigned to allow for the integration of sunlight. This would bring a new connection to the natural world and a balance to the usually dark environment. Visitors would now experience a dramatic contrast between the darkness of the caves and the brilliant light of the exterior world.

Thirdly, the industrial hum from below would be harnessed more efficiently, converting the potential energy into useful forms. This transformation would be achieved through the development of sustainable and renewable energy sources, which would eventually render the entire subterranean industry obsolete. 

Lastly, new amenities would be created to cater to the many visitors drawn to the Subaqueous Heights. A restaurant, serving delicacies prepared from the sustainable bounty of the area, would open its doors. Eco-friendly accommodations would also be provided, ensuring that the experience would be accessible to all.

While the Subaqueous Heights would continue to maintain its sense of uniqueness, its core essence would continue to evolve with time. It would be a place where visitors would learn about the delicate balance between nature and technology, and perhaps leave with a greater understanding of how harmonious coexistence can be achieved.
