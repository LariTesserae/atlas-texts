The humans living in the oasis settlement would undergo guided evolution, where their biological, physical, and cultural adaptations would be supported. This could occur in various ways, such as introducing new plants that help the people to understand their environment better or provide sustenance, or helping the people's existing plants to propagate and flourish.

The benevolent and wise power would aim to preserve the tranquil atmosphere of this landscape, further enhancing it by subtly increasing the strange and mesmerizing occurrences without destabilizing the environment.

To support the growth of the plants beneath the soil, the power would ensure that the complex lifeforms there have access to more nutrients and moisture without disrupting their delicate balance. This might mean introducing more moisture sources or conditions favorable to nutrient preservation.

As this place begins evolving towards a more supportable state for all organisms, including humans, the power could introduce a form of technology that enhances human existence in this landscape without disrupting its already fine-tuned balance. This technology might be something akin to biotechnology, helping the inhabitants to interact and understand the environment better.

Ultimately, the benevolent and wise power would ensure this landscape is preserved and evolves gradually on its own, working with the people, plants, and creatures that inhabit it in harmony. The landscape's time-bending nature would be allowed to exist, and further, subtle changes would be encouraged to occur with evolutions that maintain the delicate balance of this odd yet harmonious landscape.
