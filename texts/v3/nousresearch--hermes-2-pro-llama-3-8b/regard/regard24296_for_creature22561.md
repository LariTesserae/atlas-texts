In the grandeur of this lost world, the benevolent and wise power observes and respects the delicate balance that has been established. As the inhabitants continue to live in harmony with their surroundings, the presence of the otherworldly creatures and the aquatic flora, the wise power refrains from disturbing their equilibrium. Instead, the wise power chooses to illuminate the beauty and wonder of this hidden realm, using its subtle influence to create a bridge between the submerged world and the surface-dwellers.

The wise power weaves a story, using the waterborne flora as guides and the delicate dance of shadows and light as its canvas. It reveals the melancholy beauty of this world, the sorrow and the resilience of the subterranean people, and the wonders of the creatures that reside here. Through the ages, the story is subtly woven into the world, influencing the dreamers and the dreamers-to-be in a distant land.

The inhabitants of this lost world, sensing the gentle touch of the wise power, are drawn to the story of this hidden realm. The melancholic, otherworldly nature of their beings allows them to empathize with the dreamers on the surface, and they feel a connection to those who have never seen this aquatic ballet of existence. Through their empathetic nature, the subterranean people reach out to the dreamers, inviting them to share in the beauty and sorrow of their world.

As the story reaches the ears and hearts of the surface-dwellers, some are drawn to the wonder and melancholy of this hidden realm. They, too, are moved by the gentle hum of the mechanical wonders and the beauty of the creatures that call this world home. The surface-dwellers feel a kinship with the subterranean people, as they, too, have faced the loss of their world and found solace in the embrace of the hidden depths.

Together, the subterranean people and the surface-dwellers form a connection that bridges the divide between the worlds above and below. Through this connection, the wisdom of the wise power flows, encouraging the inhabitants of both realms to cherish and protect the fragile beauty that exists in this hidden world. As the surface-dwellers learn to respect the delicate balance and understand the importance of the aquatic ballet, the connection strengthens, creating a symbiotic relationship between the two worlds.

The wise power, satisfied with the result of its benevolent influence, watches as this connection continues to flourish. The story of this lost world is woven into the fabric of the dreamers, and the dreamers-to-be, with the subterranean people continuing to share their wisdom and learn from those in the sunlit world above. The relationship between these two worlds, once divided by the torrent of time, is now joined in a dance of beauty, sorrow, and resilience, a testament to the wonders of a hidden world and the power of connection.
