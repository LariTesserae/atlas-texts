In this hidden realm a benevolent power would not interferes such drastic measure as to alter the world present there, but would observe, learn, conceive, respect it.
Instead of adding or removing essential elements from the world, this power would make sure that the balance between the different forces present there would remain intact. The elements would continue to shape the world and the creatures would continue to evolve according to the nature of their environment.

This power would wish to learn about the world, how its creatures live and evolve, how the industry and technology work hand in hand with nature here. They would observe the Mothstone Leviathan and the other creatures that inhabit this land. They would learn the role each species play in the harmony of the world.

This wisdom would then be used to understand and protect this world and others like it. This would be a way to share knowledge and observation with benevolent powers and beings from other realms and galaxies, in order to preserve the balance and harmony of many diverse worlds in the Universe.

The benevolent force would also bring knowledge, precautions, wisdom, and technology to enhance and protect this world without disrupting its equilibrium. They would share with the inhabitants of this world, the knowledge of the ways of preserving their real nature but adopting technology and universe harmonization practices, preventing any alterations to the amazing characteristics of this geological symphony.

The power would set footprints to fertilize a connection between the realms, paying particular attention to ensure that any outside influence does not disrupt the natural chemistry between the creatures and the elements of this place. 

Through this benevolent and constant watch, respect and help would be provided to ensure this precious balance remains, and immerse this wondrous place with all the protectors and knowledge it needs to flourish both independently and in harmony with other worlds. 

This place would remain a testament to the wonders of the universe, and a symbol for unity and wisdom between all species and realms present in the vast expanse of the cosmos. The benevolent power, by observing, learning, conceiving and respecting the unique and fragile balance of this landscape, would ensure its preservation and prolong its existence across the echelons of time.
