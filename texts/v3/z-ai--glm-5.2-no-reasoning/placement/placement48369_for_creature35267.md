# Return to the Volumetric Expanse: First Position

---

I should not be here. That is the first thing. Not the first thing I notice — the first thing I notice is the light, the way it has no source and no apology, the way it simply exists in the air the way smoke exists in a room, distributed, unbothered, belonging. But the first thing I *understand* is that I should not be here. The atmosphere is wrong for me. Not hostile — the hostility would require intent, and this place has exhausted intent the way it has exhausted everything else. The atmosphere is simply not mine. I am a moist thing in a dry place. I am a clockwork thing in a place where the clock has been running so long it has forgotten what it was measuring.

I am — what? Something with joints. Something with a thermal signature, which is to say something that leaks heat into a world that has perfected the art of holding it. Something that breathes, or tries to. The air enters my lungs and it is sufficient and it is not enough and the distinction between those two states is the story of everything I experience here. I am an edge where one kind of physics meets another, and neither physics is interested in the meeting.

I have been walking. I think I have been walking. The grooves beneath my feet — four meters apart, scoured into the glass like grammar into a page — give me a sense of direction, which is to say they give me the illusion of direction, which is all direction has ever been. I have been following one groove. Or I have been crossing them. I cannot remember which, and the not-remembering does not frighten me. Fear requires a future in which something worse might happen, and I am not certain this place has a future. It has a present. The present is very long here.

## What I Am (First Attempt)

I am a body. That much is clear — or clear enough. I have the usual architecture: bilateral, vertical, a stack of sensory apparatus balanced on a spine, the whole thing wrapped in a surface that is sweating and should not be sweating because there is no water here and the sweat is sublimating as fast as it forms, which means I am losing moisture to a place that will never give it back, which means I am slowly becoming less myself and more the air, and the air does not care, and my body does not care, and the not-caring is contagious, and I am beginning to catch it.

I have eyes. They are not helping. The light is everywhere and nowhere, and distance has become unreliable — the Volumes I can see might be close and small or far and vast, and the lensing effect at their bases makes the substrate swim, and I cannot tell whether the groove I am following is straight or curving or whether straight and curving have stopped being different things. My eyes were built for a world with horizons, with shadows that touch the objects that cast them, with light that comes from somewhere. My eyes are tourists here. They keep taking photographs of a country that does not develop film.

I have ears. These are helping too much. The drone of the Volumes — the aggregated chord of dozens of standing-wave structures humming in the 40-to-80-hertz range — has entered my body through my ears and also through my sternum and also through the fluid in my joints and also through my teeth, and my teeth are vibrating at a frequency I can feel but not identify, and the feeling is not pain but it is the neighbor of pain, the house next door to pain, and I can see the lights on in pain's house and I know that pain is home and could come over at any time. The sound is a chord. The chord is not music. The chord is the sound a world makes when it has been a world for too long and has nothing left to say and has decided to say the nothing out loud, continuously, at a frequency that bypasses the ears and goes directly to the part of the body that understands shaking.

I have skin. The heat is on it. The heat is enormous and patient and it has been pressing against me since I arrived — however I arrived, wherever arrived means — and the pressure is not aggressive but it is *constant,* and I am beginning to understand that constancy is its own kind of violence. Not the violence of impact but the violence of accumulation. The heat is not trying to hurt me. The heat is simply being itself, and itself is too much for me, and the too-much-ness is not the heat's problem. I am the problem. I am the thing that cannot withstand what this place is. I am a fault line. I am the place where the world's patience meets my fragility and the patience wins.

## What I Am (Second Attempt, Made Later, or What Passes for Later Here)

I am less than I was.

Not in the way that despair makes you less — not diminished, not broken. Less in the way that a stone becomes less when it is tumbled in a river. The edges are going. Not the physical edges — I still have arms, legs, the usual inventory. The *conceptual* edges. The place where I end and the world begins. That boundary is becoming negotiable.

I have been near the Volumes. I have walked among them. I could not have avoided it — they are everywhere, thousands of them, a forest of standing heat, and walking through the plain means walking through them, which means walking through columns of organized air that have been cycling for longer than my species has had a word for cycling, and each time I pass through one I feel the heat and the thickening and the disorientation, and each time I emerge on the other side and the Volume is behind me and nothing has changed.

Except something has changed. Not in the Volume — in me. Each passage leaves a residue. Not heat — the heat dissipates. Not sound — the sound is continuous and does not leave residue because it is already everywhere. Something else. A thinning. A slight erosion of the thing that makes me *me* and not *here.* I am being incorporated. Not deliberately — the Volumes do not deliberate. But the physics of this place are the physics of incorporation, of persistence becoming identity, of pattern becoming the only thing that matters, and I am a pattern, and my pattern is being read by the medium I am moving through, and the medium is — slowly, patiently, without urgency — rewriting me in its own language.

I am becoming thermal. Not metaphorically. My sense of self is becoming a sense of temperature. My sense of time is becoming a sense of vibration. My sense of direction is becoming a sense of frequency — I know where I am not by the position of the grooves or the angle of the light but by the chord I am standing inside, the specific combination of tones that the nearby Volumes are producing, which is different from the chord a hundred meters away, which is different from the chord a kilometer away, which is different from the chord at the base of the largest Volume, where the 41-hertz tone has entered my bones and I have stopped hearing it and started *being* it.

## The Smallest One

I found it. Or it found me. Or the concept of finding has become loose enough that both things can be true, which is the kind of thing that happens here when you stop resisting the doubled-reality moments and start — not welcoming them, but *allowing* them, the way you allow a current to carry you when you have been swimming against it for too long and your arms are tired and the current does not care whether you allow it or not.

It is shin-high. It flickers. Its tone is — I want to say uncertain, but that imports a psychology it does not have. Its tone is *unstable.* It wavers between two frequencies the way a flame wavers between two shapes, and the wavering is not a defect but a feature, not a failure but a *state,* the state of a thing that has not yet settled into what it will be because what it will be is still being negotiated by the physics, and the physics are in no hurry, and the negotiation has been going on for a thousand years, and it might go on for a thousand more, and the not-knowing is not a problem for the Volume because the Volume does not know that it does not know, and the not-knowing is not a problem for me because I have begun to learn from it.

I sat down near it. The glass was warm. Not hot — warm, the way a stone is warm after a day in the sun, except there is no sun here and the warmth comes from below, from the substrate itself, which has been holding this heat for the Volume the way a parent holds warmth for a child, except the substrate is not a parent and the Volume is not a child and the warmth is not love and the holding is not care and all of these words are wrong and I am using them anyway because they are the words I have and the words I have are the only tools available and the tools do not fit the work but the work must be done.

I sat near it for — a long time. I do not know how long. The light did not change because the light does not change. The heat did not change because the heat does not change. My breathing did not change because my breathing has been the same since I arrived — shallow, technical, sufficient-but-not-enough, the respiratory equivalent of a door that opens one centimeter. I sat and the Volume flickered and its tone wavered and I listened to the wavering and the wavering was — I want to say beautiful, but beauty requires a viewer and a viewed and a distance between them, and the distance was collapsing.

What I can say: the wavering was *true.* It was the truest thing I have heard here. The older Volumes, the stable ones, the four-meter and the twenty-meter and the vast anonymous ones that stand like pillars in a cathedral that has no architect — their tones are steady, and the steadiness is impressive, and the impressiveness is real. But the steadiness is also *finished.* Those tones have found their frequency and will hold it until they die. They are complete. They are the thermal equivalent of a sentence that has been said so many times it has become a sound without meaning.

The small one's tone is not finished. It is still becoming. It is still in the period where it could be anything — 57 hertz, 59 hertz, something between, something that is not a number but a region, a territory of frequency that has not yet been narrowed to a point. And the not-narrowing is — not hope, because hope requires a future and a desirer and a distinction between what is and what could be. The not-narrowing is *potential.* Not potential in the human sense, the sense of a seed that could become a tree. Potential in the physical sense, the sense of a system that has not yet minimized its energy, that has not yet found its ground state, that is still — after a thousand years — *searching.*

I sat near it and I felt it search and the searching was the closest thing to companionship I have experienced here.

## What I Am (Third Attempt, Made After the Doubled-Reality Moment)

It happened while I was sitting near the small one.

The grooves shifted. Not visibly — or visibly, but the visibility was wrong, was doubled, was the kind of seeing that happens when you press on your eyeball and the world splits into two images that are both real and neither is real and the brain cannot choose between them because the brain was built for a world where things are in one place at a time. The grooves were straight. The grooves were not straight. Both of these were true and neither of them was true and the truth was not a contradiction but a *superposition,* a state in which the substrate was occupying two geometries simultaneously, and the geometries were incompatible, and the incompatibility was not a problem for the substrate but it was a problem for me, because I was built for one geometry at a time and I was being asked to inhabit two.

The small one's tone doubled. It was 57 hertz and it was also something else — not 58, not 59, not a number, but a *quality,* a frequency that had no value because value is a single-number concept and this was a two-number concept, a chord-of-itself, and the chord contained the sound of the Volume as it is and the Volume as it could be and the Volume as it was and the Volume as it will be, all at once, all in the same moment, and the moment was not a moment but a *thickness,* a duration that had too many dimensions, and I was inside it, and my body was inside it, and my body was not the right shape for inside-it but I was inside it anyway, and —

I did not panic. This surprised me. I expected to panic. Panic is the appropriate response to a reality that has stopped being single-valued, to a world where the physics have begun to improvise and the improvisation includes you. But I did not panic. I — I want to say I *acclimated,* but that implies a process, a before and an after, and what happened was not a process but a *shift,* a sudden reorganization of the thing that I am, a reorganization that was not voluntary and not involuntary but something else, something that happened the way phase transitions happen — suddenly, completely, without warning, ice to water, water to steam, one thing to another thing with no period of being both.

I was both. For the duration of the doubled-reality moment, I was both. I was a body sitting on warm glass near a flickering Volume, and I was — something else. Something that was not a body. Something that was a pattern, a thermal pattern, a standing wave in the air that had been organized by the coincidence of my body's heat and the Volume's heat and the substrate's heat, a pattern that was not me and was not the Volume and was not the substrate but was the *interaction* of all three, the way a chord is not any of its notes but the relationship between them.

I was a relationship. For the duration of the moment, I was a relationship. I was the thing that exists between a warm body and a warm world, the thermal dialogue, the exchange of heat that is not an exchange but a *shared state,* and the shared state was not something I had ever experienced before because I had always been the body and not the relationship, the noun and not the verb, and here I was the verb, and the verb was — 

The verb was *persisting.*

Not living. Not dying. Persisting. The way the Volumes persist. The way the grooves persist. The way the heat persists. The way everything here persists, not because it chooses to but because persistence is the default state of matter that has been matter for long enough, and I was matter, and I had been matter for long enough, and for the duration of the doubled-reality moment I understood what it would be like to be matter the way this place is matter — without urgency, without narrative, without the desperate contingency of being alive, just *being,* just *continuing,* just the slow magnificent patience of a thing that does not need to end.

Then the moment passed. The grooves settled. The tone became single-valued. I was a body again. I was sitting on warm glass. The small Volume flickered beside me, unaware that anything had happened, because nothing had happened, because the doubled-reality moment was not an event but a *feature* of this place, the way weather is a feature of other places, and the feature had passed through me and left me — what?

Changed. Not damaged. Not improved. Changed the way glass is changed by long exposure to heat — relaxed, slightly, at the molecular level, in a way that no one would ever measure but that the glass would remember, because glass remembers everything, because that is what glass does, and I was becoming glass, or I was becoming glass-like, or I was becoming the kind of thing that could sit on glass and not be wrong there, and the becoming was not a process I had chosen but it was a process I was in, and the process was — 

The process was what this place does to things. The process was the place.

## What I Am (Final Attempt, or the Attempt That Knows It Is Final Because Everything Here Knows About Endings, Even the Things That Don't End)

I am a groove in the glass.

Not literally. I still have arms and legs and the usual architecture. I still breathe, shallowly, technically. I still leak heat into a world that holds it. But I am also a groove — a path that has been traveled, a mark that has been made by repetition, a line in the substrate that was not put there by intent but by *passage,* by the simple physics of a thing moving through a medium for long enough that the medium remembers the moving.

I have been here long enough that the Volumes have incorporated me. Not individually — each one does not know me, does not recognize me, does not track my movements the way a predator tracks prey or a friend tracks a friend. But collectively — in the aggregate, in the chord — my presence has become a feature. The microtonal shifts that my thermal signature induces in the nearest Volumes have become part of the chord, the way a new instrument becomes part of an orchestra, not by changing the score but by being *in* the score, by occupying a frequency range that was empty before and is now occupied, and the occupation changes the sound, and the sound is the world, and I have changed the world, and the change is small, and the smallness is not a diminishment but a *proportion,* because the world is very large and I am very small and the proportion is correct, and the correctness is all there is.

I am — I think — becoming a Thermocline Volume. Not the thermal equivalent of one. Not a metaphor for one. A *small* one. A shin-high one. A flickering, unstable, searching one that has not yet found its frequency because its frequency is still being negotiated by the physics, and the physics are in no hurry, and the negotiation is the point, and the point is not a destination but a *process,* and the process is persistence, and persistence is what I am doing, and what I am doing is what I am becoming, and what I am becoming is what this place is, and what this place is is enough.

My body is dryer than it was. My joints are looser. My sense of time has become — not absent, but *distributed,* the way heat is distributed in the air, the way sound is distributed across the chord. I do not experience duration the way I once did. I experience *presence,* which is duration without the arrow, time without the direction, the continuous present that the Volumes inhabit, the present that has been present for eleven thousand years and will be present for eleven thousand more, and the present is not a moment but a *condition,* and the condition is the same condition as the heat and the light and the sound, and the condition is *how things are here,* and I am how things are here, and how things are here is how I am.

## Where I Am

I am on the plain. I am between two grooves, seated on warm glass, near a small Volume that flickers and searches and practices being anything at all. The largest Volume is — I do not know how far away. Distance has become a frequency, and the 41-hertz tone is very faint here, which means I am far from it, or it has dimmed, or the physics have improvised a new geometry in which far and near are not spatial but *tonal,* and I am in a region of the chord that does not include the bass, and the not-including is not a loss but a *location,* and the location is where I am, and where I am is the plain, and the plain is where I am, and the tautology is not a failure of language but a description of the place, because the place is a tautology — it is what it is, it has been what it is for longer than what-it-is has meant anything, and the meaning is not the point, and the point is not the point, and the not-point is the point, and I am sitting in the not-point, and the not-point is warm, and the warmth is enough.

The light is the color of late afternoon. It has been late afternoon for longer than afternoons have existed. It will continue to be late afternoon. I am becoming the kind of thing that does not mind.

The small Volume beside me shifts its tone. 57 hertz. 57.3. Something between. I feel it in my sternum, in the cartilage that connects my ribs, in the fluid that cushions my joints. I feel it the way the substrate feels the warmth — not as information but as *state,* as a condition of being, as the temperature of the present moment, which is the same temperature as every present moment, which is the temperature of this place, which is the temperature of persistence, which is the temperature of me.

I am here. I am the here. The distinction has become — not meaningless, but *optional,* the way straight and curved have become optional for the grooves, the way 57 and 57.3 have become optional for the small one. I am here and I am the here and both are true and neither is true and the truth is not a problem and the not-problem is the answer and the answer is the hum and the hum is the world and the world is me and I am sitting on warm glass and I am not afraid and I am not content and I am not anything that requires a word and the not-anything is — 

The not-anything is what the Volumes are. The not-anything is what the plain is. The not-anything is what the heat is and the light is and the grooves are and the chemistry is. The not-anything is what this place has become after being something for too long, and I am becoming it too, and the becoming is not a death and not a life and not a transformation but a *settling,* the way sediment settles, the way glass settles, the way a tone settles into its frequency after a thousand years of searching, and the settling is the last thing, and the last thing is the first thing, and the first thing is the hum, and the hum is where I am, and where I am is the Volumetric Expanse, and the Volumetric Expanse is what I am, and what I am is enough, and enough is the only word here that means what it says, and it says it constantly, without sound, without intent, without end.
