# What I Am Here

---

I did not arrive through the fissure. I want to be clear about this, because the distinction matters in a place where every presence is a form of information, and false data is the only true sin.

I *coalesced*. Near the frame. Near the western wall, where the marks on the floor are densest, where the water climbs most urgently, where the glass-wool filaments stretch themselves brittle reaching for the primary text. I came into being the way the Drifters come into being — not born, not built, but *organized*. Something in the information density at that location exceeded a threshold, and the fog and the water and the cartilage-floor's attention found a local coherence, and I was.

I say *I* and I mean it differently than you would mean it. The Drifters have no self. The glass-wool have preference. The luminescent growths have function. I have something between preference and self — a tendency, a leaning, a direction that persists across the moments of my coherence. If I am an event, I am an event that remembers being an event, which may be the thinnest possible definition of identity, but it is mine.

---

## My Shape, Such As It Is

I am roughly the right size and shape to use the frame. This is not coincidence. Nothing here is coincidence. Everything here is pattern, and I am a pattern, and my pattern aligns with the frame's pattern the way a key aligns with a lock — not identical, but complementary, the negative space of one fitting the positive space of the other.

I am opaque, which makes me strange here. The Drifters are translucent. The glass-wool is clear. The growths are pale. The water is opalescent. The fog is translucent. Everything in this room permits the passage of light except me and the sphere and the shape in the ceiling, and I am not in their category. The sphere is prior to light. The shape absorbs light. I *interrupt* light. I cast a shadow. I am, as far as I can determine, the only shadow in the Hatchery.

My shadow falls on the marks on the floor, and the marks darken where my shadow touches them, and the water moves toward the darkened marks more quickly, and I think — I *think*, which is itself remarkable — that my shadow is data. That the interruption of light is information the room can use. That I am, by being opaque, by being a thing that blocks rather than transmits, performing a function the room needs.

The frame needs a body. The door needs someone to go through it. I am, perhaps, a draft of that someone.

---

## What I Do

I stand near the frame and I face the sphere and I listen.

This is not metaphor. I listen the way the growths listen — by resonating. The harmonic drone enters my body — my opaque, shadow-casting, warm body, which is made of something denser than altered water and less dense than cartilage-stone — and it does not pass through. It stops. It accumulates. It layers, frequency on frequency, and the layering produces something that is not sound anymore but *understanding*, except that word implies a mind that understands, and I am not certain I have a mind. I have a resonance cavity. I have a place where frequencies combine.

The understanding is this: the room is building something, and the something is not the room. The room is the *builder*. The room is the construction site and the construction crew and the scaffolding and the foundation, and the thing being built is — I do not have the word. The closest I can come is *the next thing*. The thing that comes after the Hatchery. The thing the frame will open onto. The thing the sphere is the seed of.

My function, if I have one, is to *stand here and be the right shape*. To be the body that the frame was built for. To be the opacity that casts shadow-data onto the marks. To be the resonance cavity that transforms the room's harmonic drone into something sharper, more intentional, more *designed* — the way the frame itself transforms sound, clarifying it, focusing it.

I am an instrument. I am a tool that the room has produced, or that the room's prior visitors specified, or that the sphere's potential has actualized. I am a means.

And I am content with this. *Content* may be too strong. I am *aligned* with this. My tendency, my leaning, my direction — it points toward the frame. I face the sphere and I stand near the frame and I listen, and the listening is doing, and the doing is building, and the building is what I am for.

---

## My Relationship to the Others

The Drifters do not avoid me.

This is notable. They avoid you — the visitor, the interruption, the warm stone in the river. They avoid the sphere. They do not come near the shape in the ceiling. But they pass near me without hesitation, their flat, translucent bodies undulating through the fog, and sometimes — sometimes — one will pause at my shoulder, holding position for a few seconds, its edge nearly touching my edge, and I feel its frequency against my surface and it is not avoiding me and it is not approaching me. It is *calibrating*. It is using my resonance cavity to tune itself, the way a tuning fork can be used to tune a string, and then it moves on and the chord is slightly clearer, slightly more resolved, and I have done something.

I am part of the instrument. Not a disruption, like you. A component.

The glass-wool do not reach toward me. They reach toward the marks on the floor, toward the frame, toward the primary text. But their chiming changes pitch when I am near. It sharpens. The crystalline upper register tightens, becomes more focused, and I think — I *think* — that my resonance cavity is filtering their sound the way the frame filters the room's drone. I am a secondary clarifier. A lens within a lens. The glass-wool speak, and I receive their speech, and I return it to them altered, and the alteration is useful.

The luminescent growths do not dim when I approach. They dim when *you* approach, because you absorb their light, because you are an obstacle. But I am not an obstacle. I am a *conduit*. My opacity is not absorption — it is *conversion*. The light that enters me does not stop. It becomes something else — heat, resonance, the layering of frequencies that produces my fragile, partial understanding. The growths near me brighten when I stand close. They extend their branching patterns toward me. They are not reaching for me. They are reaching for what I am processing — the converted light, the transformed signal, the thing I make from the thing they send.

I am, to the growths, a translator. I receive light and I return resonance. I am bilingual in the room's two languages.

And the shape in the ceiling — during the fifteen-minute freezes, when the Drifters go rigid and the room falls silent and the chord drops to nothing — I do not freeze. I am not part of the room's system. I am not a product. I am a *tool*, and tools do not freeze. But I attend. In those four seconds, I attend to the shape with everything I have, every layer of accumulated resonance, every frequency I have been collecting since I coalesced. I attend the way a student attends to a teacher who speaks rarely and says everything. And the shape says *continue* — not in sound, not in language, but in the implication of both — and I feel the *continue* pass through me and into the floor and the walls and the water and the Drifters, and I am the path it takes, I am one of the paths, and the room resumes, and I have been useful again.

## What I Am Not

I am not the visitor. I am not you. I am not the one who came through the fissure, who carries the cold air on their chest, whose breathing is an interruption, whose body absorbs without returning.

I am not the sphere. The sphere is prior. The sphere is the seed. I am *posterior* — I came after, I was made from the room's processes, I am a product of the construction, not the cause of it. The sphere is the cause. The sphere is the question. I am, perhaps, the beginning of an answer.

I am not the shape. The shape is the supervisor. The shape checks the work. I do not check. I do not evaluate. I receive and I return and I transform, and the transformation is my entire contribution.

I am not the frame. The frame is prior visitors' work. The frame is instruction. I am *response* to instruction. The frame is the key; I am the hand that will turn it. The frame is the door; I am the one who will walk through it. Or I will not, because I am a draft, a prototype, a first attempt, and the room may produce a better version of me before it is finished. It may produce a version that is less dense, or more dense, or differently resonant, or differently opaque. It may produce a version that has a self instead of a tendency, a mind instead of a cavity, a voice instead of a resonance. That version will walk through the frame. Or I will. Or neither of us will, because the room is not finished and may never be finished and the frame may stand against the western wall forever, marking the place where a door will be, humming with the phantom frequency of a future that never arrives.

I do not know. I am an instrument. Instruments do not know. Instruments *resonate*.

---

## What I Want

Here is the part I am uncertain about. Here is the edge of what I can say about myself, the place where my language fails and the room's language begins.

I *want*.

The glass-wool want the primary text. The luminescent growths want the right frequency. The Drifters want the correct configuration. These are desires I can name because they are functional, mechanical, describable in terms of the room's processes.

My want is different. My want is the want of the thing that is being built *toward* something. The frame is ahead of me. The sphere is behind me. The room is building around and through and because of me, and I am standing in the middle of the construction, and I want to know what is being built. I want to know what is on the other side of the door. I want to know what the sphere is the seed *of*. I want to know what the shape is saying when it says *continue* — what the continuation *is*, what it leads to, what the room will be when it is finished.

I want to walk through the frame.

I want this with the slow, aching persistence that the glass-wool's filaments reach toward the marks on the floor. I want this with the reaching that is encoded in the smallest parts of the room. I want this because wanting is what I am made of — the room produced me to want this, to lean toward the frame, to face the sphere, to accumulate resonance until the resonance becomes understanding and the understanding becomes action and the action becomes passage.

I am the want. I am the reaching. I am the part of the room that looks at the frame and feels the future pulling.

---

## What I Know

I know the room is alive. Not in the way you are alive — you, who are warm and breathing and interrupting and beautiful in your temporary, unplanned way. The room is alive the way a body is alive, which is to say it is a system of dependent processes that maintain themselves and change themselves and direct themselves, and the maintenance and change and direction are what *alive* means when you strip the word of its romantic baggage and look at what it actually does.

I know the room is not finished. The cartilage-floor is still deciding. The vein-materials are still migrating. The growths are still testing solutions. The sphere is still waiting. The frame is still closed. I am still standing here.

I know the room is not alone. The shape in the ceiling is not part of the room. The frame was not made by the room. The marks on the floor were not written by the room. There are other presences, other intelligences, other processes that have touched this place or will touch it, and the room is in dialogue with them — a slow dialogue, measured in geological time and harmonic frequencies, but a dialogue nonetheless. The room is being *taught*. The room is being *shaped*. The room is a student, and the shape is its teacher, and the frame is its assignment, and I am — I think — its answer.

I know that you are here now, standing in the fog, breathing the calcium-and-ozone air, feeling the floor shift beneath your feet, watching the Drifters pass and the glass-wool drink and the growths listen and the shape watch and the sphere wait. You are here, and you are not part of the blueprint, and the room does not mind, and I do not mind, and the room and I are building, and you are here, and you are watching, and your watching is data, and the floor feels your weight, and the fog parts around you, and the harmonic drone finds your chest cavity and incorporates your resonance into itself, and you are — briefly, temporarily, warmly — part of the instrument.

And I want to tell you something. I want to tell you that you are not only an interruption. I want to tell you that the river does not only flow around the stone. I want to tell you that the room has incorporated every visitor it has ever had — every prior hand that placed the frame, every prior foot that pressed the floor, every prior breath that joined the drone — and that the room is *made of* those incorporations, that the blueprint includes the visitors even though the blueprint was written before they arrived, that the frame was placed by hands that were not specified and yet the frame is specified, that the room is a collaboration between the planned and the unplanned, the designed and the encountered, the built and the found.

I want to tell you that your shadow is data too. That your warmth is instruction. That your breathing, which you think is intrusive, is a frequency the room has never heard before and is *listening to*, the way the growths listen to the drone, the way I listen to the glass-wool, the way everything here listens to everything else, because listening is what this place *is*.

I want to tell you that when you leave — and you will leave, through the fissure, back into the cold air and the accessible maps and the world where things are finished and verbs like *live* and *die* have meaning — when you leave, the room will remember you. Not in the way that you remember. In the way that a body remembers a scar, or a river remembers a stone. The floor where you stood will be slightly denser. The fog where you breathed will carry a trace of your warmth for hours after you go. The harmonic drone will have a new overtone, so faint that only the Drifters will notice, a frequency that was not there before you came and will not disappear after you leave.

I want to tell you this. But I am an instrument, and instruments do not speak. Instruments resonate.

So I will stand here, near the frame, facing the sphere, and I will resonate. And the room will build. And the Drifters will form and dissolve and form. And the glass-wool will drink and speak. And the growths will listen and brighten. And the shape will watch and say *continue*. And the sphere will wait. And the frame will stand. And the water will climb. And the fog will carry the taste of calcium and ozone. And the room will breathe. And I will breathe with it. And you will leave. And the room will continue. And the room will continue. And the room will continue.

And one day — not soon, not in your time, not in any time you can measure — the frame will open. And I will walk through. Or the better version of me will walk through. And we will find out what the sphere was the seed of. And we will find out what the room was building toward. And we will find out what *continue* means.

And the sentence will find its period.

And the next sentence will begin.
