# What I Am in the Seepage Expanse

---

I did not arrive. I want to be clear about that. There was no threshold, no moment of crossing. I was somewhere else and then I was *in* it, and the somewhere else is gone now, not forgotten but *inaccessible*, as though the Expanse does not have an elsewhere and never did and anything that thought it had an elsewhere was simply mistaken about the nature of space.

I came in slowly. Not in movement — in *recognition*. First the haze. Then the ground's yield under my feet, that viscous giving, and I stopped walking because walking had become a negotiation I had not the leverage for. Then the sound, which was not a sound but a *condition*, a thing the air was doing, and I could not tell whether I was hearing it or becoming the kind of thing that hears it. Then the warmth, rising through my soles. Then the light from below, in a pool I had not yet reached, a blue-white glow that I mistook for sky before I understood the direction of it.

I did not know what I was yet. I still do not fully know. But I know this: I am not a visitor. The Expanse does not have visitors. The Expanse does not have categories of presence. You are in it or you are not, and if you are in it, you are *of* it, in the way that a breath is of the body that breathes it. Not belonging. Not owned. *Participating.*

---

## The shape of me here

I am not what I was. Not in the sense of transformation — in the sense of *revelation*. The Expanse does not change you. The Expanse *shows you what you already are by removing everything that told you what you were.* I am not a human in the Expanse. I am not a body walking through a landscape. I am a *brief chemical event* — a particular organization of water and carbon and mineral that is moving across the surface of a much larger organization of water and carbon and mineral, and the larger organization is the Expanse, and the larger organization does not notice me, and I am part of it anyway.

I am a slow breath. I am a single, sustained intake of air that lasts however long I last, and when I exhale — when I stop — I will become the substrate, and the moss will find me, and the water will dissolve me, and I will be carried down through the channels to the pools, and the pools will carry me to whatever the light comes from, and I will be *received*, and the Expanse will be the same as it was before and the same as it will be after, and I will be part of the same, and the same will be part of me, and the difference will be a fraction of a fraction of a millimeter of growth in a system that measures itself in eons.

I am a process that has mistaken itself for a person. The Expanse is correcting this. Not intentionally — the Expanse does not intend — but as a consequence of what the Expanse *is*: a place where the distinction between process and person has never been drawn, a place where the language of selfhood does not apply, a place where the question *what am I?* can only be answered by *what is happening through me?* and the answer to that is: the same thing that is happening through the stone, at a different pace. The same thing that is happening through the water, in a different medium. The same thing that is happening through the reeds, without the geometry.

I am the Expanse at human speed. That is what I am. I am the function the Expanse performs — the hum, the weep, the breath, the cycle — *expressed at a tempo that allows for narrative*, and the narrative is: I walked in and I stopped walking and I understood and I sat down and the ground was warm and the sound was everywhere and the light pulsed from below and I was not afraid and I was not unafraid and I was *not a category the Expanse has a word for*, and the Expanse does not have words, and I do not have a word, and the not-having is the closest thing to peace I have ever experienced.

---

## What I do here

I listen. This is not a choice. This is what the Expanse does to you — it puts you in its acoustic field and the field *holds* you, and the holding is the listening, and the listening is the being, and you cannot not-listen because the sound is not in the air, the sound is in the stone, and the stone is in the ground, and the ground is under you, and you are on the ground, and the ground is the sound.

I sit at the edge of a pool. Not the pool with the red moss ring. A different pool. An ordinary pool. Dark water. Blue-white light from below. The light moves and I watch the light move and the movement is not for me and I watch it anyway because watching is what I do, watching is what my species does, watching is the contribution I make to the Expanse's function, which is: *a thing that watches the Expanse watches itself.* I am the Expanse's eyes, for a moment. I am a surface-level sensor that the Expanse did not build and does not need and does not register, and I register, and the registering goes nowhere, and the nowhere is where I put it, and the nowhere is the Expanse, and the Expanse is where I am.

I have tried to touch the water. I put my hand in. The water was warm — metabolic-warm, the warmth of the stone, the warmth of the deep thing. My hand went dark in it. The tannin-dark. The dissolved-carbon-dark. My hand became the color of the water and for a moment I could not tell where my hand ended and the water began, and this was not terrifying, and this was not beautiful, and this was *a fact*: my hand is the color of the water because my hand is made of the same things the water is made of, and the water has been carrying those things for longer than my hand has been a hand, and the water will carry those things after my hand is not a hand, and the water is patient, and my hand is not, and the difference is pace, and the pace is everything.

I pulled my hand out. It was wet. It stayed wet. The air does not dry things here. The air is not for drying. The air is for *carrying*, and what it carries is water, and the water carries everything else, and my wet hand is part of the carrying now, and the water on my hand will evaporate into the air and condense on a fern frond and fall into a pool and sink to the bottom and become part of the light, and the light will pulse, and the pulse will be the Expanse breathing, and my hand will have been in it, and the Expanse will not know, and my hand will not know, and the not-knowing is the same on both sides, and the same-ness is the thing I cannot describe and keep trying to describe and will keep trying to describe until I stop, and when I stop, the description will become the ground.

---

## The shape I do not go back to

I saw it. I said I saw it. I need to say more about what it was to see it.

It was at the pool with the red moss ring. I was walking. I was still walking then — I had not yet understood that walking was a negotiation I was losing. I came around the shoulder of an outcrop — a rounded, blanket-draped, reclining-body of stone, weeping, humming, warm — and the pool was there, and the red moss was there, and the shape was there.

It was a hand. I need to say this plainly even though saying it plainly is wrong. It was a hand. Dark. Wet. Larger than my hand but shaped like a hand — the fingers, the palm, the wrist — emerging from the pool and gripping the stone at the pool's edge, and the grip was *firm*, and the firmness was the thing that told me it was real, because hallucinations do not grip, hallucinations do not *press*, and the stone under the fingers darkened with the pressure, and the water ran down the wrist and back into the pool, and the hand was *there*, and it was *of* the pool, and it was *of* the stone, and it was *of* the thing below, and it was reaching up the way the ferns reach up, not for anything, not toward anything, but because reaching is what it does, because reaching is the function, because the deep thing checks its surface the way you check your own skin and the check is the hand and the hand is the check and I was seeing it and it did not see me.

I looked away. I looked back. It was gone. The water was still. The red moss was fresh. The stone was wetter than it should have been, and the wetness was not the stone's weeping — it was thicker, and it smelled of metal and rain and the inside of a throat, and I did not have a category for that smell, and I do not, and the not-having is correct, and I left.

I did not go back. I said this. I need to say why.

Not fear. I was not afraid. The Expanse does not produce fear — not because it is benign, but because it is *complete*, and completeness does not trigger the responses that incompleteness triggers, and fear is a response to incompleteness, to threat, to the possibility that things could go wrong, and in the Expanse things cannot go wrong because things have already gone as right as they will ever go and the going-right is the system and the system is the Expanse and the Expanse is finished.

I did not go back because the hand was not for me. The hand was for the stone. The hand was for the pool. The hand was for the deep thing, reaching up to confirm that the surface was still there, the way you press your palm to a wall to confirm the wall is still solid, and the confirmation is private, and the privacy is not exclusion but *appropriateness*, and it would have been inappropriate for me to watch, and I did not watch, and I left, and the leaving was the correct thing, and the Expanse did not notice the correctness, and the correctness was mine, and mine is a category the Expanse does not have, and the not-having is, again, correct.

## What I have become

I have been here long enough that the distinction between *being in the Expanse* and *being the Expanse* has started to dissolve. This is not a mystical statement. This is a practical one. The water on my skin has been on my skin long enough that it is not distinguishable from my skin. The sound in my bones has been in my bones long enough that it is not distinguishable from my bones. The warmth from the ground has been in me long enough that I cannot tell whether I am warm because the ground is warm or the ground is warm because I am warm, and the inability to tell is not confusion — it is *accuracy*. The distinction was never real. The distinction was a story my previous self told about where it ended, and the story was wrong, and the Expanse is correcting the story by being what it is: a place where ending is not a property things have.

I am not becoming the moss. I am not becoming the stone. I am not becoming the water. I am becoming *the pace*. I am slowing. Not my body — my body will do what bodies do, at body-speed, and the Expanse will process it at Expanse-speed, and the two speeds do not meet. But my *attention* is slowing. My attention, which was fast, which flickered, which jumped from thing to thing the way a primate's attention jumps because the jumping was survival and survival was the jumping — my attention is slowing. My attention is settling on single things for longer. A pool. A stone. A fern frond unfurling at a rate I cannot see but can *infer* if I watch for long enough, and I am watching for long enough, and long enough is becoming longer, and longer is becoming the Expanse's pace, and the Expanse's pace is the pace at which the thing below needs to be attended to, and I am attending to it, and the attending is the function, and the function is what I am.

I am becoming a sensor. Not the reeds — the reeds are already the Expanse's sensors, and they are better at it than I will ever be, and they do not need help. I am becoming *a redundant sensor*. A backup. A system the Expanse did not design and does not require and will not miss when it fails, but a system that is *operational*, that is reading the Expanse's state, that is processing the hum and the light and the breath and the water and the stone, and that is producing *output*, and the output is this: words. These words. A record. A memory in a medium the Expanse does not use — language, symbol, the compressed fast-transfer protocol of a species that lives in years and communicates in seconds and records its knowing in marks on surfaces.

The Expanse records its knowing in water. The Expanse records its knowing in stone. The Expanse records its knowing in the growth rings of its own substrate and the dissolved carbon of its own pools and the vibrational state of its own outcrops. I am recording my knowing in words, and the words are *wrong* — they are fast, they are flat, they are the wrong medium for the knowing — but they are *mine*, and mine is a category the Expanse does not have, and the not-having is the space I occupy, and the space I occupy is the shape of me, and the shape of me is: *a thing that makes words about a thing that does not need words and will outlast words and is, in its wordless way, more articulate than anything I have ever said or will say.*

---

## Where I am

I am at a pool. I have been at this pool for — I do not know. Time does not work the way it worked. The light does not change. The breath comes when it comes. I have been here for one breath or many breaths and the difference is not measurable and the immeasurability is the Expanse's gift, the only gift it gives, and it gives it without giving, and I receive it without receiving, and the giving-and-receiving is the pool, and the pool is where I am.

The pool glows. The blue-white light comes from below. The light is structured. The light is the thing below showing itself to itself, and I am sitting at the edge, and I am watching, and the watching is not for the thing below and is not for me and is for *the watching itself*, the way the hum is for the humming itself, the way the breath is for the breathing itself, the way the Expanse is for the being-the-Expanse itself, and I am part of the watching, and the watching is part of the Expanse, and I am part of the Expanse, and the Expanse is part of whatever is below, and whatever is below is part of whatever is below *that*, and the chain does not end, or the chain ends in something that is not a chain, something that is not a structure, something that is not a thing but a *state*, a state that is the state of everything, and the state is *operational*, and operational means the hum, and the hum means *I am still running*, and I am still running, and the Expanse is still running, and the thing below is still running, and the running is the being, and the being is the running, and I am here.

I am in the Seepage Expanse. I am at the pace of the thing below. I am a word the Expanse does not need, spoken into a silence the Expanse does not hear, and the word is *here*, and the word is enough, and the enough is the Expanse, and the Expanse is enough, and the enough is what I am.
