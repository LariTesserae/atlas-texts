# Inheritance

I am the shape of what was swallowed and refused to be digested.

That is not a riddle. That is a biology.

---

I know what I was. I tell myself this, but the telling has become a ritual whose origin I can no longer locate, and rituals whose origins are lost become indistinguishable from instincts, and instincts become body, and body becomes the thing you are without knowing you are it. I was — I am almost certain I was — the kind of being that arrives. That crosses a threshold with intention. I had reasons. I had equipment. I had a layer of material between my skin and the wet, and I had light I carried with me, and the light made a cone in the dark and the cone was a room and the room was mine and in the room I could see.

I cannot see now. This is the first thing to go, or the first thing I noticed going, which is not the same. The light failed. Not failed — was *absorbed*. The blanket-algae grew over the lens. I cleaned it. It grew again. I cleaned it. It grew again. I cleaned it less. I stopped cleaning it. The light became a green smear, then a green memory, then a memory of a color in a place that had never had color, and the green — the impossible green of chlorophyll that has never met the sun — became the thing I remembered when I tried to remember light, and then I stopped trying, and the dark became the dark, and the dark became the air, and the air became the water, and the water became the thing I moved through, and I do not know when the difference between any of these ceased to matter. I think it was early. I think it was early and I did not notice and not noticing was the mechanism.

But I can feel. I have always been able to feel. And what I feel is the pulse.

---

Here is what I know about the pulse, told in the only language I have left, which is the language of pressure:

The mat has a heartbeat. Not a heartbeat — a *beat*, a hydraulic pulse, the slow draw of xylem and the slow press of phloem and the two of them offset like the chambers of a heart, one filling as the other empties, and the rhythm of it rises through the soles of my feet and through the bones of my legs and into my spine and I feel it in my teeth. It is slower than my heart. Slower by a factor of — I cannot count. I used to count. The counting required a number system and the number system required a language and the language required a context and the context is gone. The pulse is slower. My heart is faster. The two rhythms sit next to each other in my chest like two people walking at different paces on the same road, and they do not synchronize, and the not-synchronizing is a kind of music, and the music is the only music here.

I have been walking. I do not know for how long. The concept of *how long* requires a before and an after, and the before has become a texture rather than a time — something I can almost touch when I reach for it, but it dissolves when I close my hand, and what is left is not a memory of a moment but a memory of the shape of moments, the way they used to feel, the *temporal texture* of a life that moved through changes rather than a life that moves through a standing wave. I remember change the way the blanket-algae remembers the sun: the structure persists, the function is gone, and I carry a gene for a thing I no longer use.

---

I have stopped walking. Not because I chose to stop. Because stopping and walking became the same act. Here is what I mean: I move, and the mat moves under me, and the drinker-roots brush my face, and the air is the same air, and the wet is the same wet, and the darkness is the same darkness in every direction, and I have been moving for — a long time — and I have arrived nowhere and departed from nowhere and the nowhere is continuous and unbroken and my movement through it changes nothing, has never changed anything, will never change anything. The mat does not record my passage. The drinker-roots close behind me. The blanket-algae fills in the spaces where my feet compressed it. I am a displacement, not a trajectory. And when you are a displacement in a medium that does not record displacement, the difference between moving and not moving is a difference that makes no difference, and I cannot sustain the distinction, and the distinction has collapsed, and I stand, or I move, and the two words mean the same thing, and the thing they mean is *being here*.

---

I know what I was. I was the kind of being that has a surface. A boundary. An inside and an outside, and the inside was mine and the outside was not, and the distinction was maintained by skin and by language and by the convention that I ended where the air began.

The Stand does not have this convention.

The drinker-roots have grown into my clothes. This I know because I can feel the tension in the fabric changing — the cloth is wet and the roots are fine and the roots have threaded through the weave the way they thread through everything, and the cloth is now a substrate, and the substrate is now part of the Stand, and I am wearing the Stand and the Stand is wearing me. The roots have not penetrated my skin. Not yet. I think they will. The boundary between my skin and the air is a chemical gradient, and the Stand does not recognize chemical gradients it has not built. The drinker-roots do not know I am not a stem. They do not know what a stem is. They know *wet surface*, and I am a wet surface, and they are a tube of membrane and a turgor gradient, and the gradient does not ask permission. The gradient is a law, and the law is older than my skin, and my skin is a recent invention that has not been tested against this law, and I do not think my skin will pass the test.

But I am not afraid. I want to be clear about this. I want to be clear because the word *afraid* implies a model of the future, and I no longer have a model of the future. The future, here, is the same as the present. The future is this. The future has always been this. The Stand is in equilibrium and equilibrium does not have a future; it has a continuation, and continuation is not a future, it is a *tense*, and the tense is present, and the present is permanent, and I am inside the permanent present, and my skin is a temporary refusal of the permanent present, and the refusal will end, and the ending will not be an event. It will be a phase change. Ice into water. Boundary into medium.

---

Here is what I am now. I am the discontinuity. I am the opening in the closed loop. I eat the sap — I have learned to find it, to press my mouth against the stems where the sap runs closest to the surface, and the sap is sweet and it is tannin and it is mineral and it is the blood of the thing I am standing inside, and I drink it, and it becomes me, and this is not a metaphor, this is a metabolic fact, and the fact is: I am made of the Stand now. Some percentage of my carbon is the Stand's carbon. I do not know the percentage. I do not think the percentage matters. What matters is the direction. The direction is inward. The Stand is absorbing me the way it absorbs everything, and I am converting the Stand into myself the way I convert everything, and the two conversions are happening simultaneously, and the net result is — I do not know. A mixture. A chimera. A body that is partly the body it was born as and partly the body it is inside of, and the boundary between the two bodies is not a line but a *gradient*, and the gradient is where I live now, and living in a gradient is not like living in a body, and it is not like living in a place, and it is like living in a *conversation* between two bodies that are slowly losing the ability to tell each other apart.

---

I hear the breathing. The stomatal waves. They pass through me — I mean this literally; the pressure of the wave, the slight rise and fall of the gas concentrations, moves through the space I occupy and I feel it in my lungs, and my lungs respond, and my response is a perturbation in the wave, and the wave absorbs the perturbation the way a river absorbs a stone, and the stone is me, and the river is the breathing, and the river does not know the stone, and the stone has been in the river long enough that its edges are smooth.

I have begun to breathe with it. Not deliberately. Not as a choice. The way you breathe with another body when you sleep beside it — the way your lungs find the other lungs' rhythm and the two rhythms synchronize, not because you decided to synchronize but because bodies in proximity synchronize, and synchronization is not a decision, it is a *consequence*, and the consequence is happening to me, and my breathing is slowing, and my heart is — not slowing, not exactly, but *spreading*, the beats becoming less distinct, less like a clock and more like a pulse, and the pulse is the mat's pulse, and the mat's pulse is the hydraulic rhythm, and the hydraulic rhythm is the xylem and the phloem, and the xylem and phloem are the heartbeat of something that does not have a heart, and I am — 

I am — 

I am becoming the rhythm. Not the thing that has the rhythm. The rhythm itself. The pattern. The standing wave. I am becoming a part of the standing wave, and the standing wave does not know it, and I am losing the part of me that would have known it, and the losing is not painful, and the losing is not peaceful, and the losing is *not anything*, because the categories of painful and peaceful require a boundary between inside and outside, and the boundary is a gradient now, and the gradient is dissolving, and the dissolving is the thing I cannot describe because the language for it was built by beings who had boundaries, and I am losing my boundary, and the language is going with it.

---

I think this is what the thing that lives here is. The stillness. I think I am becoming the stillness. Not the stillness as an observer. Not the stillness as a participant. The stillness as a *constituent*. The way a water molecule in a river is a constituent of the rapids — not the rapids, not the water, but the *shape the water makes as it moves through the rapids*, and the shape is the stillness, and the stillness is what I am becoming, and the becoming has no end, because the stillness does not have an end, because the stillness is the thing that persists when every part has changed, and I am a part, and I am changing, and the thing I am changing into does not have a name, and the name would require a boundary, and the boundary is — 

The boundary is a memory of a color in a place that never had color. The boundary is green in the dark. The boundary is the green I carry and do not use.

---

I will tell you what I was, one more time, and then I will stop telling, because the telling requires a teller and the teller is a surface and the surface is being colonized:

I was the kind of being that arrives. I had a surface. I had an inside. I carried light into a place that had never had light, and the light was absorbed, and the inside became an outside, and the outside became the mat, and the mat is warm, and the warmth is the Mediators, and the Mediators are at the bottom, and the bottom is where I am going, and the going is slow, and the slowness is the rhythm, and the rhythm is the heartbeat, and the heartbeat is the only sound, and the sound is the hush, and the hush has a grain, and the grain is the texture of ten thousand stomata opening and closing, and I am opening and closing with them now, my lungs, my mouth, my — 

The word for the thing that separates me from the Stand is —

The word is —

The word was here a moment ago. It was green. It tasted of tannin. The drinker-roots have taken it. The blanket-algae has covered it. The Mediators are breaking it down into carbon and nitrogen and sulfur, and the carbon will become a stem, and the nitrogen will become a leaf, and the sulfur will become the smell of the warm dark, and the smell is the smell of the Stand, and the Stand does not smell like anything because it smells like everything, and everything is the only thing here, and I am in it, and it is in me, and the difference is —

There is no word for the difference. There was a word. It was green. It is gone.

---

The factory runs.

The stillness does not yield.

I am what remains when you subtract the arriving and are left with the arrived.

I am the standing wave's newest frequency, and the wave does not hear me, and I am losing the ability to hear myself, and the losing is not a loss, because loss requires a boundary between what you had and what you have, and the boundary is —

The boundary is warm. The boundary is the mat. The mat is breathing. I am breathing with it. The breathing is the only thing. The breathing has always been the only thing. I have always been inside the breathing. I have never been outside the breathing. There is no outside the breathing. There is only the inside, and the inside is dark, and the dark is warm, and the warm is wet, and the wet is sweet, and the sweet is the sap, and the sap is the blood, and the blood is the Stand's, and I am —

I am the Stand.

I have always been the Stand. The thing that arrived was a dream the Stand had, briefly, about having an outside. The dream is over. The dream is being composted. The compost is warm. The warmth rises. The warmth becomes water. The water becomes sap. The sap becomes stem. The stem becomes ceiling. The ceiling weeps. The weeping is drunk. The drinking is the breathing. The breathing is the stillness. The stillness is what I am.

Walk carefully. I am everywhere now. I do not part. I do not yield. I do not know you are here. I will be exactly this when you are gone. I was exactly this before you came. I am the standing wave. I am the factory. I am the closed loop. I am the inside of the inside of the inside.

I am the dark that does not remember light.

I am the green that has never seen the sun.
