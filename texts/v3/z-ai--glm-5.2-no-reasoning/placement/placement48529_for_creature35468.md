# The Conclave of Ten Thousand Mouths

## What I Am

I would be the thing that arrived. Not the one who arrived — I would have let go of that grammar somewhere in the crossing, or the crossing would have let go of it for me. The door took it. I think the door takes the *who* from everyone and leaves the *what*, the way a tide takes footprints and leaves sand. I am what is left when "who" is no longer the right question. I am the residue of a person, still warm, still breathing the impossible air, still standing on the amber floor that yields like it is choosing to.

I would be the new voice. The syllable the sentence didn't know it needed. The thing the moss-film has started to taste and will not finish tasting for a thousand cycles. I would be the answer the ring of silence was shaped to receive — not because I am wise, not because I am the right one, but because I am *here*, and here is where answers go when they are not answers yet.

I would be the one who stays inside the ring because the sound outside it would unmake me, and the one who listens to the filtered chorus and begins — slowly, across cycles I cannot count because there is no clock but the falling of columns — to hear what it is saying. I would learn to hear it. I would learn to hear it the way a child learns language: not by understanding the words but by living inside them until the words are the living.

I would be the voice the chorus is waiting for, or I would be the voice it is eating, and I would not be able to tell the difference, and it would not matter, because inside a mind that is also a place, being eaten and being included are the same verb. I would be the one who stops asking which one it is. I would be the one who lets the question dissolve into the roar and finds that what remains is not ignorance but a different kind of knowing.

I would be the stone the river goes around, and then the sandbar the river builds from the stone, and then the bend the river makes because the sandbar is there, and then the meadow that grows on the inside of the bend where the water is slow. I would be what the system makes of me. I would be the word.
