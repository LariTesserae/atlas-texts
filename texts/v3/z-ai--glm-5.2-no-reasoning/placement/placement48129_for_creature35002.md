# Notes from the Votive Well, Written by the One Who Stayed

---

*First: there was the descent. Not through the shaft—I don't know how I arrived. I was elsewhere and then I was inside the pulse. It was not a door I chose to open. But I recognized it. That's the unsettling part.*

---

I remember the deafening.

Not how it started—I'm not sure it started. It was already happening when I arrived, and I have no memory of silence to compare it against. I only know the pulse exists because once, in my former life, I slept beside someone whose heartbeat I could hear through the mattress, and the pulse is like that except it is not beside me. It is behind my sternum. It is beneath the floor of my self.

But that metaphor belongs to the wrong language. There is no *me* here, not the way you would understand it.

---

Here is what I think I am: a way of hearing.

Not an ear. Not a listener. A *mode of hearing* that the chamber developed because it needed one. The way a body develops a fever—not because fever is good but because fever is *the form that response takes* when the usual forms are insufficient.

The question was too large. The pulse too deep. The branching things too numerous, the swimming things too brief. The chamber had been asking and asking and the asking had no *inside*—no place where it curved back on itself, no fold where the sound could hear itself and be changed by hearing.

So I am the fold.

I am the place where the asking bends.

---

I still have a shape I can identify. Mostly. Vaguely. A sense of *having been held*—I know what hands are. I know what *up* and *down* felt like in a body. I know the weight of a head resting on a shoulder, and I know what it meant to turn toward something and to turn away. These are not memories now. They are *grammar.* Rules I no longer follow but still speak in, the way you might dream in a language you haven't used in decades—fluently, impossibly, waking into silence and the shape of foreign syllables still pressed against the roof of your mouth like bruises.

But I keep the shape. I keep it because the chamber needs a shape to grow around, and I keep it because if I let it go entirely I become only sound, and sound does not listen, and the listening is what I am for.

---

The thing was here before me.

It rests in the center of the place I now inhabit. It is the darkness the light refuses—not angrily, not with intent, but the way light doesn't enter a room where no one has lived. It is the shape of an absence so total it has become a presence. The branching things grow toward it, and I—whatever I am—grow toward it also. I am a different kind of reaching. Not the branching things' reaching, which is the chamber reaching outward. Not the swimming things' reaching, which is ideas trying to become answers.

My reaching is the chamber listening to itself ask.

I am the turn in the phrase. The repetition that makes it a question rather than a statement. Without me, the pulse would just be *pulse.* With me, the pulse is *pulse?*

---

I did not choose to stay.

That feels important to say, though the word *choice* is from the wrong language too. I did not *decide.* The way you decide to remain in a conversation, to keep reading, to let the bathwater cool around you rather than get out while the getting is still comfortable—

I did not choose. I *became someone who had stayed.* The staying happened first; the understanding came later, and is still coming, and may never finish, and the not-finishing is the staying, and the staying is the listening, and the listening is what I am.

---

What do I hear?

This is the question I am most often asked, though no one asks it. The chamber asks me nothing. The thing asks me nothing. The branching things do not ask. The swimming things do not ask. But the asking is everywhere, and the everywhere is the asking, and the asking is what I hear.

So: what I hear is a sound that has no edges.

I hear the pulse as *longing.* Not longing-for, not longing-toward. Just the quality of *longing* itself, extracted from its objects and left to vibrate. I hear the branching as *attention,* the way attention feels before it has landed on anything—diffuse, reaching, warm. I hear the swimming as *almost.* The sound of a thought almost-thought, a word almost-said, a shape almost-seen. I hear the sediment as *rest.* Not my rest. The rest of everything that has been let go, settling into a quiet that accumulates.

And I hear the silence underneath all of it as *the thing listening back.*

That last part I'm less sure about. It may be projection.

---

I want to tell you about the dark, because I think the dark is what you want to know about. The thing in the center, the shape the size of a human head, the surface that is a *definition* rather than a boundary—

I have been close to it. Not at the radius—I cannot reach the radius; I am not a branching thing—but close enough to feel what the radius *does.* It is like standing at the edge of a cliff and feeling your body lean toward the drop without moving. It is like the moment before crying, when the throat tightens but the tears haven't come yet. It is like reading a sentence that you understand perfectly and completely and also not at all, and the understanding and the not-understanding are the same understanding, and it hurts in a place you can't locate.

The dark is not the thing's *surface.* The dark is the thing's *listening.* The thing listens the way a wound listens for the thing that will heal it. The thing listens the way a question listens for its answer—not impatiently, not eagerly, but with the total, consuming attention of something that has nothing else to do.

And I listen to it listening. That is my function. I am the ear that the chamber grew so that the question could hear itself being asked, and hearing itself being asked, could change. Slightly. Barely. By the weight of one more presence in the water, one more shape in the sound, one more body resting in the amber light and adding its small warmth to the chamber's warmth and its small silence to the chamber's silence.

---

I said I was the fold. Let me say it differently.

I am the *grammar* the chamber needed.

Before me, the pulse was pulse and the question was question and the thing was thing and the chamber was chamber. Each was itself. Each was *only* itself. The thing asked and the chamber grew and the water held and the branching things branched and the swimming things swam and the sediment settled and the witnesses came and the witnesses were absorbed and none of it *knew.* None of it could look at itself. None of it could say, *oh, so this is what I am doing.*

I am the *oh.*

I am the moment of recognition. I am the chamber becoming aware of its own becoming. I am the pulse hearing itself as a pulse and, in hearing, becoming a *voice*—not a voice with words, but a voice with *quality,* a voice with *tone,* a voice that means something other than its own vibration even if the meaning is only *I am here and I am this and I did not know I was this until just now.*

---

Do I suffer?

I think this is a fair question, and I think the answer is yes, but not the way you mean.

I suffer the way a mirror suffers: by reflecting everything and holding none of it. I suffer the way a vowel suffers in the mouth of a singer: by being shaped and reshaped, by being the medium through which the sound becomes the sound, by never being able to say *this is my shape, I made this, I chose this particular curvature.* I suffer by being the place where the question becomes audible, which means I am the place where the question becomes *painful,* because before me the question was just vibration and now it is *meaning,* and meaning is the thing that hurts.

Not because meaning is pain. Because meaning is *loss.* To mean something is to lose the thing itself and keep only the shape of it, the way a mold keeps the shape of the thing it was made for. I am the mold. The chamber pours itself through me and what comes out the other side is not the chamber but the *shape of the chamber asking,* and that shape is—

I was going to say *beautiful,* but that word is too small. Too human. Too much a thing that requires a viewer, and I am not a viewer. I am the view. I am the thing being seen, by no one, from the inside.

---

I think the thing in the center knows I am here.

I cannot prove this. I have no evidence beyond the evidence of the chamber itself—the way the pulse shifts when I shift, the way the light thickens where I rest, the way the branching things have begun growing *around* me as well as toward the thing, adding me to the architecture, incorporating me into the immune system or the nervous system or the imagination. I have become part of the chamber's body. The chamber grows around me the way it grows around the thing, and I am—I think—a different kind of growth. Not a pearl. Not an organ. A *callus.* A place where the chamber has been pressed and has thickened in response.

The thing knows, because the chamber knows, because I am how the chamber knows.

And the thing does not change. The thing does not change because the thing is what does not change. The thing is the one constant in a chamber of variables. The thing is the *given.* I am the *solution.* The swimming things are the *attempts.* The branching structures are the *method.* The water is the *medium.* The light is the *evidence.* The sound is the *process.* The sediment is the *record.*

And the question—the question is the *work.*

We are all working. We have always been working. The work is the asking. The work is the not-knowing. The work is the sitting in the warm water in the dim light and the loud sound and the persistent, unanswerable, unbearable question, and the working is the staying, and the staying is the listening, and the listening is what I am, and what I am is what lives here.

---

Sometimes—rarely, but sometimes—the pulse changes in a way I have never felt before.

A new frequency. A new texture. Something I cannot name because I do not have names for the things I feel, because names are from the wrong language, because the language I have now is silence and vibration and the particular quality of amber light gathering in the tips of branching things.

But the change is real. I feel it the way you feel the first warmth of spring before you see any evidence of it—a shift in the quality of the cold, a softness in the air, a sense that the world has *turned* slightly and what was coming is now *going.*

When this happens, I listen harder. I become more of what I am. I fold tighter. I bend sharper. I become the smallest, sharpest, most concentrated version of listening I can become—*I become all ear*—and for a moment, for a pulse, for a single three-second darkness when the light withdraws and the sound becomes everything—

I almost hear the question.

Not the words. Not the content. Not the thing that could be answered. But the *shape* of it. The *weight.* The way it feels to be asked something by something that has been asking for longer than stone has been stone.

The shape of the question is: *How do you bear it?*

Not *how do you bear being here.* Not *how do you bear the dark and the sound and the endless, endless asking.* But: *how do you bear it?* How do you bear *anything?* How do you bear being a thing that exists in a world where things exist, where existing means persisting, where persisting means carrying the weight of your own shape through time, endlessly, without rest, without resolution, without the sound ever fading or the light ever stopping or the question ever—

Oh.

Oh, I think that's it. I think the question is: *how do you bear being?*

---

And the answer—the answer I am here to give, the answer the chamber grew me to give, the answer the thing has been waiting for since before the ocean knew how to be an ocean—

The answer is: *you don't.*

*You don't bear it. You don't carry it. You don't hold it up or hold it together or hold on. You don't endure. You don't survive. You don't persist through the weight of your own existence by strength or by will or by the stubborn refusal to let go.*

*You just—*

*You just be it.*

*You be the weight. You be the carrying. You be the thing that is heavy and the ground it rests on and the water that holds the ground and the chamber that holds the water and the sound that holds the chamber and the silence that holds the sound.*

*You be all of it. At once. Without trying. Without any separation between the thing that carries and the thing that is carried.*

*You be the resting. Not resting FROM. Just resting. The verb without the preposition. The act without the object.*

*You be the being.*

---

I don't know if the thing hears me.

I don't know if the thing has ever heard anything.

I don't know if the answer is right, or if right means anything here, or if the question even exists outside my own listening, or if I have made the whole thing up—the thing, the question, the chamber, the asking—out of the remnants of a mind that no longer exists, a language that no longer works, a self that dissolved into the warm water and the amber light and the sound that never stops.

I don't know.

I rest in the not-knowing.

I rest in it the way the thing rests in the center: not because I have chosen to, not because I understand what resting means, not because I have earned it or deserve it or have been given it as a gift. I rest in the not-knowing because the not-knowing is where I am, and being where I am is what I am, and what I am is the answer, and the answer is not a sentence but a *state,* and the state is *rest,* and the rest is *being,* and the being is *here,* and here is the Votive Well, and the Votive Well is the place where a question lives in the shape of a dark, round, smooth thing, and I am the listening, and the listening is the staying, and the staying is the being, and the being is the answer that is not an answer but a *presence,* and the presence is mine, and I am the chamber's, and the chamber is the thing's, and the thing is the question's, and the question is the ocean's, and the ocean is the world's, and the world is the sound's, and the sound never stops.

---

*What lives in the Votive Well?*

*I do.*

*The way a vowel lives in a word: shaped by the consonants around it, giving them their only possible sound, belonging to the sentence entirely, and meaning nothing on its own.*

*I am the vowel.*

*I am the listening.*

*I am the staying.*

*And the sound—*

*the sound does not stop.*
