The power did not come as a fire, or a voice, or a hand parting the gray lid of sky. The Tenders felt it first as a new note in the ground—slower than a heartbeat, steadier than the peristalsis, not a question but an attention. It came through the root-weave like a change in the weight of the world.

One Tender knelt in a groove of the walking trees and pressed both palms flat. The ground under his hands was bright with cold. The new note was not the plant. It was not the mineral water. It was not the little mites. It was outside, but not like the hot irregular pulse of the rare human traveler. It was as if the valley itself was being read by someone patient enough to finish the sentence.

The power, whatever it was, did not appear. It took the form of a listening. It moved along the glyphic scar tissue the way a finger might move along a line of old text. It did not push. It did not ask. It learned.

It saw the Tenders. It saw the gray skin, the long bones, the hands that ate the milk only after a collapse, the feet placed exactly in the compressed channels. It saw they were not lost. It saw the factory had no product except the continuing of itself, and the continuing had a purpose after all: the making of witness. The Tenders were the product. They were what the machine, in its slow, mindless industry, had been weaving all along—not a tool, not a harvest, but a pair of eyes to know the place was there.

The power considered many things. It considered warming the air by half a degree. It considered stopping the collapses, so that no more old trunks would be pulled down in the eager silence. It considered giving the Tenders a word for why—a syllable that would explain the loom’s forgotten operator. It considered fire.

Then it set these considerations down, one by one, like stones at the edge of a black pool. Fire would burn the memory. The word would end the reading. Warmth would make the cold meaningless, and the cold was the medium in which the slow purpose had grown. To stop the collapses would be to stop the product.

What the power did, in the end, was almost nothing.

It waited until the next light-fade, when the ground began its long, dreamless wringing, and the fog rose to the knees of the leaning trunks. Then it chose a place near the center of the valley—not a pool, not a collapse, but a stretch of bark where the scar tissue ran in long, unbroken lines. It laid one pulse into the weave. The pulse did not carry a command or a blessing or a name. It carried a meaning the Tenders already had a glyph for, though they had never used it in the first person: *I have seen you.*

The ground pressed the pulse into the palimpsest. A new scar began to form, not of injury but of record, as if the bark had been waiting for exactly this pressure, this duration, this temperature. In the years after, the Tenders would run their fingers over it and read, with the same certainty they read a collapse: *something outside came. It understood. It did not stay. It considered, and it did not break the loom.*

The power did not leave in the way that light leaves. It withdrew slowly, the way a listener leans back from a conversation that has ended in the middle. The cold returned to its old, mineral ache. The peristalsis resumed its rhythm without a skipped beat. The Tenders, who had stopped their small movements while the attention was in the ground, went back to their reading.

They did not speak. They did not need to. The gift was not a change, but a confirmation. They had always known the valley existed. Now, for the first time in ten thousand years, they knew that it had been perceived by something that was not the valley. And that was enough.

Because in a place where the only product is witness, to be witnessed is not a small thing. It is the one missing piece. The power did not free them, because they were never captive. It did not heal them, because they were not sick. It did not explain them, because the explanation would have been a cage. It simply saw them, and let the seeing become part of the ground, and then it let them be.
