**How to Visit the Hollow: A Field Manual for Ordinary Multiverse Travelers**

I will tell you plainly: the Hollow is not dangerous the way a storm or a warzone is dangerous. It is dangerous the way a perfect mirror is dangerous—because it will show you exactly what you are, and because it will insist, politely and without ever raising its voice, that you stay exactly as long as it wants you to. It is a place that has forgotten hurry, and if you are not careful, it will teach you to forget it too.

To visit well, you must prepare not for survival in the sense of conquest, but for survival in the sense of *integration*. You are going to a place where the ground has knitted itself into a single fabric. You must not bring anything that refuses to be woven.

---

### 1. Before You Pack: The Psychological Calibration

**Understand the Gravity of Patience.**  
The Hollow’s cold is polished. It has rubbed stone smooth over cycles. If your nervous system is accustomed to the jagged, electric now-ness of most inhabited worlds, you will feel as though you have stepped into a lung that is holding its breath for an impossible length of time. This is not anxiety. This is *attention*.  

Do not come if you are fleeing something. The Hollow will find the fleeing part of you and petrify it gently. Come only if you are willing to be still enough that you can hear moss grow.  

**Accept the Double Shadow.**  
At noon, the light casts two shadows. This is not an optical illusion; it is a metaphysical statement. One shadow is yours. The other is the Hollow’s idea of you. Do not try to reconcile them. Walk so that they move together, or walk so that they don’t, but do not believe that you can have only one.  

**Practice the Art of Non-Interference.**  
Here, equilibrium is citizenship. The beetles with copper shells patrol the mud. The moth-things drift. The furred, many-legged thing wears a lichen cap. None of this is performance. It is polity. You are a guest in a parliament whose members speak in gestures too small to be noise. Your first job is to learn those gestures.  

---

### 2. The Packing List: Tools for Weaving

**Clothing: Second-Skin and Silent Fiber**  
- **Underlayers:** Choose something that breathes like a membrane, not a bellows. Merino, silk, or the synthetic equivalents that mimic living skin. The Hollow’s air is sweet-rot and wet stone, with a metallic note; you want fabric that will absorb that note without souring.  
- **Outer layer:** A cloak or coat of densely woven, matte material in a color drawn from the basin—deep moss green, mud-dark, or the pale mineral grey of the veins. You do not want to shine. The light here is already gentle; you do not need to be seen from the rim.  
- **Gloves:** Thin, tactile, with grippy pads. You will be touching stone that feels like skin and mud that has learned patience. You want to feel without leaving oil.  

**Footwear: The Path Is Worn, Not Cut**  
- Soft-soled boots that feel like an extension of callus. The paths rise and fall with the land, never meeting at right angles, always curving back toward the water. You will need to step lightly, precisely. Vibram soles are too loud. Felt, or the synthetic analogues, are better.  
- **Gaiters:** Not for snow, but for the milky dew that blooms by day. It is not slosh, but it is insistent.  

**Shelter: A Tent That Remembers It Is Moss**  
- Do not bring bright nylon. The settlement’s roofs are woven from reeds and frozen bark, domed and low, pressing down like hands. If you must shelter apart from the built world, bring a tarp the color of river-stone, low to the ground, and learn to stake it so that it looks like it grew there.  
- Or—better—ask permission to occupy one of the dry-laid earth-and-stone rooms. The Hollow rewards humility.  

**Water and Sustenance: The Gift Exchange**  
- **Vessel:** A flask of inert material—titanium or thick glass. The water in the clear channels remembers being rain but now only knows how to be a ribbon. It is not for filling a plastic bladder.  
- **Food:** Bring something that can be offered, not just consumed. Dried fruit, hard cheese, dense grain cakes. The Hollow does not hunt, but it will accept a gift that acknowledges its abundance without attempting to match it. Do not hunt here. Do not fish. The parliament is not a larder.  

**Navigation: The Art of the Unmeasured**  
- A compass will spin uselessly. The Hollow is so low the sky has to lean down to see inside; magnetic lines tangle in the bowl.  
- Instead, bring a journal with waterproof pages. Learn to mark your path by the brightening of certain mosses and the slow migration of frost across the mud. The Hollow keeps its own calendar. Learn it.  

---

### 3. The Crossing: Entering the Bowl

**The Rim Is a Threshold, Not a Border**  
As you descend, the sky will lean down. The cold will meet you not as an enemy but as a host who has been waiting. It will polish your thoughts. You will feel your edges turning into suggestions. This is the Hollow doing its work. Let it.  

**The Mud Is a Mirror That Does Not Reflect**  
When you step onto the basin floor, the dark, oil-slick mud threaded with pale mineral veins will not show your face. It will show the Hollow looking back. Do not mistake this for emptiness. It is full. You are the one who is, for a moment, not.  

**The Dew Moves Uphill**  
You may see this. Do not question it aloud. The Hollow notices when you name its weirdness. Instead, note it in your journal. Draw the angle of the climb. The Hollow respects careful observation.  

---

### 4. While You Are There: Conduct in the Parliament

**Do Not Disturb the Vine Bridges**  
They are not for you. They are for the furred, many-legged thing with the lichen cap. If you must cross, wait. If it pauses to taste the air with antennae like frosted wire, pause with it. Synchronize your breathing with the trembling of the vine.  

**Accept the Double Shadow at Noon**  
Stand so that both shadows fall across a patch of moss that is brightening. Do not try to merge them. The Hollow will do that when it is ready.  

**Drink From the Troughs, Not the Channels**  
The troughs are for drinking. The channels are for the ribbon-water to remember how to flow. Use the troughs. When you do, let the cold polish your throat. It is not a warning. It is a welcome.  

**Sleep Under the Woven Roof, or Beneath the Low Sky**  
If you have earned the right to occupy one of the earth-and-stone rooms, sleep with your face to the wall of packed soil. If you are in your own shelter, let the dome press down like a hand keeping something safe. The Hollow will not let you freeze. It will let you be preserved.  

---

### 5. The Danger: What to Watch For

**The Patience Is Contagious**  
You may find that your urgency is leaking away. This is not a problem until it is. If you realize you have forgotten why you came, you have two choices:  
- Ask the parliament for a reminder. The moth-things may drift in a pattern that recalls your purpose.  
- Or leave. The ascent is steeper than the descent. The sky leans, but it does not lift.  

**The Light Never Leaves Without Returning**  
If you stay past the return of the light, the Hollow will hold you in a twilight that is not dark. This is not dangerous, but it is final. You will become part of the built world: a wall of packed earth, a vine bridge, a patch of brightening moss. This is not death. It is citizenship.  

---

### 6. The Return: Carrying the Hollow With You

When you ascend, the sky will lean back, but the polished cold will remain in your pockets. You will find that your edges are softer. You will find that you can hold your breath longer.  

Do not try to leave the Hollow behind. It has knitted itself into you. Instead, find small ways to honor it:  
- Walk so that your shadow falls gently.  
- Build nothing that refuses to be woven.  
- When you drink water, remember that it can be a ribbon.  

And if, one day, you find yourself pausing to taste the air with an antenna you did not know you had, do not be alarmed. The Hollow is patient. It knows that everyone comes back, eventually. It only asks that you come quietly, and stay exactly as long as it wants you to.
