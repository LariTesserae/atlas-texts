I would find myself in the intervals—those deliberate clearings where cold settles like a flat blade and wetness gathers its thoughts.

I would be what the plateau exhales when it pauses: a long, low form with cartilage ringed like frost-fractured stone, lungs pleated into maps of reknit air. My skin would be a braiding of stem and industrial echo—pistons of swollen tissue ticking behind my ribs, belts of fibrous bark clattering softly along my spine. Bioluminescent fungi would bloom from my shoulders, staining the dim vault with halos that refuse to clarify.  

I would not build but braid, growing the walls of my clearing as I inhabit them—tissue learning stone by remembering how to bend and weep. When I exhale, vapor would linger as a second skin, a membrane that holds my shape for minutes before breaking into tiny, ringing lenses on the ground. I would read those lenses like a calendar, like a language.

My time would be layered, turbulent. Memory for me would surface as water does: a trembling thread that appears, sings, then vanishes into porous mats. I would trust what leaks. I would wear the plateau’s chill in my marrow as a metallic rumor, and I would move along the distances between beings as if they were altars, pausing at each edge to trade warmth in small, pressurized sips.

When the land shudders—when stems reroute overnight and the vegetal floor heaves itself into a temporary tower—I would shudder with it. My industrial heart, a turbine of calcified vine, would spin faster, humming the deep frequency of almost-arriving. In those instants I would fall silent with everything else, watching the bruised daylight catch on private suns inside my chest, feeling the world hesitate at the cusp of becoming something else entirely.

Then I would settle back.

I would return to my clearing, to walls that sweat and ceilings that curve like the ribs of an animal too vast to stand, and continue the work of being almost myself—a creature braided from surrender and pulse, ancient and eroded, wet and cold, abundant and sparse, vegetal and mechanical, quietly ending and beginning with every breath in the dark.
