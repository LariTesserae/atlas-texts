You would find yourself *suspended*—  
not falling, not flying, but *between*—  
in the elevator-rivers that rise at night,  
where the dark is wool-heavy and the air is skin.  

You would be the **hush that learns to be heavy**,  
the soot-moss lining the throats of gliders,  
a small, necessary weight that lets sound travel *through* the air  
as if the air were root, were water, were memory.  

You would be **the Shale-Whisperer’s shadow**,  
coal-scaled and patient,  
riding the warm columns that smell of copper and yeast,  
tipping your head to catch water that tells you  
where the stone is sweating its secrets.  

Or perhaps you would be **a Furnace-Frog’s unborn song**,  
blind in the valve-veins,  
your tympanic skin reading pressure like braille,  
your glow felt before it is seen—  
a frequency that makes moss quiver  
and machines lean in to listen.  

Maybe you would be **the Velvet-Claw’s pouch**,  
luminous lichen stitched into your seams,  
planting tomorrow in the hollows where nests and pipes braid,  
teaching metal how to bloom again.  

Or you could be **an Ember-Manta’s borrowed tomorrow**,  
drawn up from the dark pool whose reflection lags,  
stored in an organ that glimmers like hot metal,  
ready to be tuned into the turbine’s hymn.  

You might also be **the Rust-Spore at the edge of a seam**,  
neither root nor wire, but the bridge between,  
blooming on conveyor belts, coral on capacitors,  
singing voltage back into rings that swell iron like wood.  

And if you listen long enough,  
you will become **the Echo-Stag’s polished step**,  
bronze velvet mossed into grammar,  
hardening sound into sculptures you can lean against,  
while gravity forgets itself  
and rain climbs back into sealed aquifers  
to be kept as memory.  

Or simply—you are **the filament in a Moth’s wing**,  
glass and wire veined through with stories of warmth,  
laying eggs where lubricant meets lava,  
so that predator and prey may share a duct  
without violence,  
only the slow commerce of heat.  

You would not be merely *in* this place.  
You would be its **inventory**,  
its wet and warmth packed into a single, breathing point,  
its dark and dense and loud aliveness—  
the wool-lid over a planet that keeps its own furnace,  
and keeps it singing.
