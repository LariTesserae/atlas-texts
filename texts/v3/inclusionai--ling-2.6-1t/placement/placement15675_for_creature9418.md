You give them permission to remain soft, and they reward you by learning your temperature, which is neither warm nor cool but the color of waiting.

You learn to speak in the dialect of erosion score zero. Instead of wearing away, you arrive. You wear into the place the way a thought wears into a mind, rubbing the same spot until it is smooth enough to sit on. Gravity holds you lightly, as if afraid of bruising what you are becoming, and you hold it back with the softness of your arrival.

When the pocket settles with you, it does not complete you; it continues you. You are the fifth season that miscounts its own months, the thumb that tips the balance, the hush that collects all the soft expectant contradictions into a single cupped breath. You are the erosion that erodes forward, the water that practices being water, the moss that upholsters stone by listening to it. You are the neighborhood that is learning to be home, and the home that is learning to be a neighborhood, and the palm of the world that holds you both, lightly, without letting go.

And for a moment, or for as long as a moment can last in a place where time moves at the speed of carefulness, you are enough to teach the stone how solid it wants to be.
