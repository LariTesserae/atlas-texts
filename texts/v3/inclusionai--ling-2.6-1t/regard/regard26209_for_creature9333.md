At first the attention does not arrive as a thing but as a quality: the air grows slightly more itself.  
A hush enters the hum; the vibration of ten million leaves shifts key, as though an ear has been placed against the green wall of the world and is learning its pulse. The sky, usually a felted lid of dim coin-light, clarifies. The pale sun brightens without heat, as if a lens of kindness has been placed between it and the ground. The wise power does not descend; it simply lets its gaze become gravity, its care become weather.

They begin with water.  
Not much—only the memory of it, distilled from the high air itself. Clouds like slow breath condense on the mineral shoulders of the plain, weeping thin, sweet threads that the root-cities recognize immediately. The salt crusts soften into white gardens; glassed basalt becomes translucently alive, veins of wet quartz threading through like nerves waking. The plants learn to store this gift as architecture: hollows become cisterns lined with living resin that remembers taste; root-cathedrals rearrange into aeolian harps, singing when the new winds pass through. Water is not flood here; it is permission. It lets the desert dream of rivers it will never have and be content.

Then comes the correction of silence.  
The wise power hears that the residents have begun to mistake loudness for purpose. It teaches the forest modulation. Leaves acquire new textures—ridges that scatter sound into color, barks that absorb argument and convert it into light. The cacophony does not vanish; it is orchestrated. Predators find their footfalls cushioned not just by patience but by moss that translates violence into fragrance. Prey learn to glow not only in fear but in laughter. Machines hum in modes that allow thought to interleave with theirs; their insect voices become counterpoint to root-songs, and the whole place finds a tempo that is neither haste nor slowness but exactly rightness.

The structures are blessed next—not by addition but by revelation.  
The wise power lets the place see its own excellence. Bridges of ceramic and woven light find that they can be crossed not just by bodies but by stories; when someone steps upon them, the steps illuminate with the history of those who have passed that way, a soft gold path of ancestry and intention. Towers of petrified wood and storm-glass discover they are not merely tall but capable of holding altitude for others: balconies extrude themselves at the precise height where a tired glider needs rest; staircases unfurl into new branches that understand the geometry of relief. The living tissue screens display not only data but dreams pulled from the air itself, public visions that anyone can edit, refine, or let fade with a touch.

Life is diversified in its strangeness.  
New symbioses bloom overnight: tiny creatures that live in the folds of machine-wood, polishing logic gates with their bodies so computation stays lucid; plants whose roots have learned to braid themselves into temporary cradles for infants of species that ordinarily nest in the sky. Gravity still softens in certain clearings, but now those places are mapped by flowers that unfurl only when levitation passes, leaving behind pollen that tastes like permission to change one’s mind. The doors that opened for breath learn to open for honesty; the rooms that folded themselves into four dimensions decide to open those folds like books, offering spaces where time can be laid flat and examined like a map.

The residents feel the attention as a growing lightness in their own bones.  
Their symbiotic chlorophyll glows brighter without burning; their eyes, tuned to dimness, acquire depth they did not know they lacked. They find that they can hear the root-cities debating philosophy in mineral dialect, and when they speak back, the forest answers through architecture: a new platform grows to support a market; a hollow appears to hold a choir; a path widens to allow two enemies to pass without meeting, then narrows to force them to meet if they choose peace. The technology, which once curtsied, now kneels to be taught; it offers its ghosts of gears to be remade into toys, its maintenance loops to be rewritten as songs.

Most of all, the wise power teaches the place how to end without dying.  
Because the plain has drawn its gaze, it is now responsible for its own eternity. The power does not grant immortality; it grants the wisdom to let things finish. Leaves learn to fall with purpose, becoming ink for the soil’s memory. Towers learn to dissolve gracefully, offering their substance to new towers that will be taller or shorter as needed. Even the salt flats, once parched and final, learn to harden into mirrors that reflect the lifted plain back at itself, so it can see its own goodness and be reassured.

The attention finally withdraws—not as absence but as trust.  
The sky returns to felted dimness, but now the dimness is chosen, not default. The pale sun resumes its old coin-brightness, but the plants have learned how to amplify its kindness without burning. The air is no longer restless as a guest; it is hospitable as a host. The residents find themselves alone again, but altered: louder with purpose, softer with compassion, exact in their slowness, infinite in their willingness to change.

And should they ever need the power again, they will find it in the way the ground holds them, in the way the wind learns their names, in the water that remembers how to fall, in the quiet that has become a language. The lifted plain continues, no longer merely alive but awake—a place where the oldest earth and the newest thought have agreed not only to share the silence but to keep making it livable, loud, and true.
