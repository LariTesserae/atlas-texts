Because the place is unfinished and its people are listening, the benevolent and wise power arrives not as a sovereign but as a weaver of patience.

It comes first as a kind of attention that does not demand to be seen. It settles into the Hush-Stitch, thickening the haze just enough to give the glass-dust a memory of alignment. Where the Glass-Shepherds rearrange their angles, the power leaves a trail of softened refraction, so that when they click into new postures the fractured light braids itself into temporary bridges across the salt-flats. No command is issued; the bridges are simply offered, and if a Shepherd crosses, its lattice learns a gentler geometry by accident.

It moves next among the Lichen-Voices, whose gunmetal sheets map the dark like shy circuitry. The power does not force them to grow, but it deepens the copper in their song, turning static into a slow, teachable hum. When a Shepherd stands near a patch, the hum rises into a chorale that translates heat into texture, so the Shepherd’s joints warm and loosen, and for a moment the boundary between listener and listened softens. The power lets this softness linger until the crust heaves and the moment breaks, at which point the hum folds back into memory, stored in the mineral like a promise kept.

In the heights, where the Ridge-Bloomers open and close with the reluctant light, the power borrows a fraction of the horizon’s hesitation and spins it into filaments that drift down like pale rain. These filaments settle on the veined wings and teach them how to fold turbulence without breaking it. The Bloomers learn to carry small eddies inside themselves and release them as glitter, which adds layer upon layer to the obsidian rivers, turning them into archives of calm. The rivers, in turn, begin to reflect more than emptiness; they reflect the possibility of surfaces that hold without demanding to be stood upon.

Beneath the salt-crust, the Burrowers of Unfixed Purpose knot and unknot heat in provisional tunnels. The power gives their exhalations a cadence, so that when they breathe out bright sulfur-scented offerings, the warmth lingers long enough to be gathered. The Burrowers learn to shape these offerings into temporary hearths that warm, but do not burn, and into scars that mark without punishing. When a hearth cools, it does not disappear but sinks into the strata as a lesson in softness, so the crust remembers how to yield without fracturing.

Throughout, the power works through subtraction as much as addition. It trims the region’s loneliness by making solitude communicable. It trims the turbulence by making it legible. It trims the unfinishedness by making it a grammar rather than an error. Nothing is forced to finish; everything is invited to converse.

As the region learns, the power withdraws slightly, becoming the thinness it helped the atmosphere choose. It lives now in the spaces between a ridge’s decision and its fulfillment, in the pause between the glass-song and the Shepherd’s answering click, in the moment a Burrower’s tunnel seals itself like a fault that chooses to be a door. It is present as permission: the permission to remain embryonic, to practice summit and scar and shelter and song without ever being required to complete them.

And because the place is high, and the people are listening, the permission settles like a second skin—warm, dry, and translucent—so that every step taken here feels like walking within a breath held gently, knowingly, by something that loves both the becoming and the remaining-to-be-uncertain. The land continues to learn how to be high, but now it learns with company, even when no one is seen. The company is the hush itself, the hum beneath, the bridges of light that appear and fade, the hearths that cool into memory, the glitter that layers the obsidian, and the horizon that steps closer only to step back, practicing patience with the patience of stone.
