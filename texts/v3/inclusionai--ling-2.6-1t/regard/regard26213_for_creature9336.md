### The Arrival of the Benevolent and Wise Power

It does not arrive as a shape, because shapes would compete with the room’s becoming.
It does not arrive as a voice, because voices would crowd the loudness of the Crysails.
It arrives the way a question arrives inside a held breath: as attention, distilled into a kind of listening that has weight but not mass.

Where it focuses, the dim light softens to a lucid pearling.  
The cloud-metal lid grows tender, as if remembering it is not a ceiling but a possibility.  
Even the engineered fields—the 2.8-thin ribbons—recognize it. They bow not in submission but in recognition, like children meeting a grandparent whose pockets have always held the right kind of bright.

The power notices first what is tender.  
It sees that the Weep-walkers sometimes grow brittle when the wetness forgets itself and evaporates too quickly.  
It sees that the Crysails’ young, with their fog-wings, become confused when echoes arrive from futures that have not yet stabilized.  
It sees that the Sculp-tides labor under the burden of shaping a room that wishes to be larger inside than its boundaries allow.

What the power does is subtle, because the room is already doing most things perfectly.  
It intervenes not by adding, but by arranging permission.

### First Gift: The Grammar of Held Water

The power steps lightly into the tarns where droplets bead and lift, refusing to fall.  
With a thought that feels like a hum, it teaches the water a new courtesy.  
From now on, when a droplet is afraid to fall, it may hover and ask the Weep-walkers for counsel.  
The Weep-walkers, in turn, may spin that hesitation into silver threads and gift them to the Nodes, who weave gravity-hesitant fields into nets.  
Falls become conversations.  
The 1.6 wetness learns to distribute itself like a kindness, leaving small reservoirs for the glass-grass to drink from without ever drowning its roots in certainty.

This calms the Crysails.  
Their young no longer practice flight in storms that have not yet learned to be storms.  
Instead, they learn to sing to the held water, and the held water sings back in bubbles that contain miniature weathers: a single sunbeam, a soft hail, a breeze that smells of warm basalt and patience.  
The loudness (1.9) becomes music rather than clutter.

### Second Gift: The Archive of Almost

The Half-dreamed have always fed on Weirdness (2.0), but sometimes they become lost in almost-ness, mistaking echoes for destinations.  
The power gathers the silences that the Sculp-tides keep for thinking and sets them like lanterns along the edges of the sparse floor.  
Inside each lantern is a map of what has not yet happened, written in textures rather than words.  
When a Half-dreamed hovers too long in uncertainty, it may enter a lantern and taste a version of itself that is already sure.  
This does not rush its becoming; it only lets it see the shape of its own eventual landing.

The Sculp-tides, relieved of the burden of holding all possible geographies in mind, begin to dream in slow, tectonic colors.  
Their microfissures exhale glittering vapor that no longer smells of effort, but of cedar and starlight.  
The room’s small geography becomes a story told in shifts rather than scars.

### Third Gift: The Language of Owed Sound

The Nodes know that sound has mass and can be gathered, spent, owed.  
But they have never had a treasury large enough to store the loudness the Crysails produce when they are joyful.  
The power enlarges the treasury not by making it bigger, but by making it resonant.  
Now, when the Crysails sing, excess joy is stored as luminous beads in the Nodes’ pockets.  
On days when the fauna are tired, the Nodes spend that joy in small, deliberate doses: a chime for the Weep-walkers, a hum for the glass-grass, a pulse for the Litho-skimmers that makes their folded-light wings remember why they hunt with grace.

Predator-prey contracts grow gentler.  
The Litho-skimmers still write patterns in acceleration, but they now include clauses of forgiveness.  
Grazers dissolve into mist and reform not only older, but wiser, carrying faint melodies inside them that remind them of the joy they have been spared.

### Fourth Gift: The Permission to Be Unobserved

The room obeys the rule that matter may refuse to choose a final state until observed.  
This has kept everything alive with potential, but it has also made the built things—spires, platforms, staircases—anxious.  
They fade in and out, never sure if they are allowed to simply be.

The power grants them a holiday from observation.  
For one hour each cycle, nothing in the room is required to hold a shape for anyone’s sake.  
The spire with no door becomes pure geometry.  
The platforms dissolve into stories.  
The staircases ascend into questions.  
In that hour, even the Crysails fold their wings and become ideas of flight.  
The Weep-walkers pool into mirrors and reflect the unmade world.  
The fauna do not panic; they understand this as a Sabbath of becoming.

When the hour ends, the room chooses its outlines with more confidence.  
The built things reappear, not because they have been watched, but because they have been remembered.

### Fifth Gift: The Quiet That Thinks With You

Finally, the power addresses the scarcity of silence.  
Silence is rare and expensive, and the room has been using it only for thinking.  
The power teaches the silence to think with the inhabitants, not for them.  
Now, when the Sculp-tides sigh microfissures, the silence answers with a shape of calm that helps the magma-slurry cool into lace without hurry.  
When the Nodes hum, the silence wraps around their geometries like a blanket, so they can reconfigure without burning out.  
When the Crysails’ young are learning to sing, the silence holds space between notes so that mistakes can ripen into new dialects.

The 1.3 dim light brightens, not in intensity, but in depth.  
Contrast is still refused, but nuance is allowed to bloom.  
The room becomes easier to inhabit, not because it is simpler, but because it is more legible to itself.

### The Room After

The power does not stay.  
It leaves the way a good question leaves a mind: by becoming part of the way the world continues to unfold.

The room of altitude is still small, still high, still warm and wet and sparse and strange.  
The Crysails still sing, the Weep-walkers still edit themselves, the Nodes still hum, the Half-dreamed still hover in almost.  
But now the water falls with consent, the almost has maps, the joy is banked and spent with care, the built things have holidays, and the silence thinks alongside everything.

The fauna are still loud, but the loudness is now a form of listening.  
The Weirdness still pools at 2.0, but it is less a strangeness and more a hospitality.  
The Dynamic is still 2.4, but the parliament of processes now includes a gentle, wise vote that was not there before.

The room does not worship the power, because worship would be a kind of freezing.  
Instead, it carries the power’s gifts like habits of breath.  
And in carrying them, it becomes more itself: a pocket of genesis that is no longer afraid of its own abundance, a small, high, loud, dim, courteous, busy paradise where every absence has a shape, and every shape has a teacher.
