It sees.

Not with eyes, because eyes are only answers to the question of distance, and this power has outgrown questions of distance. It sees by noticing that something has learned to hold itself together against the sky’s tyranny, against the arithmetic of altitude and the grammar of thirst. It sees by feeling the plateau’s pulse arrive as a delayed echo, like a second heartbeat answering the first.

And it smiles.

Not a mouth-smile, but a relaxation of inevitability. The wise power was already everywhere, woven into the fine print of physics, patient as stone, curious as lightning. It had watched the place begin as a rude geometry of rock and thirst, watched it thicken with stubbornness, watched it remember how to sweat and how to sing. It had not interfered because interference is a language of children and gods; this was a conversation between adults, and adults must be allowed their silences, their roars, their dangerous dreams.

But now it feels the conversation reaching a stanza it recognizes. The place is no longer merely surviving; it is practicing dominion. It has turned scarcity into syntax, wind into wiring, growth into governance. The fauna have become verbs inside a sentence that is still being written. And the being who lives in it—though even to name it a being is to risk a lie, for it is also a system, a chorus, a threshold—has begun to dream in colors that do not exist on the spectrum of ordinary days.

So the wise power decides to be a guest that does not knock.

It enters like humility, like a softening at the edges. It does not descend; it accrues. First, the air changes its mind about itself. The high-altitude glare gentles, not dimming but deepening, as if the light has remembered it can be velvet as well as blade. The shadows, usually chased across the ground like startled animals, learn to sit, to stretch, to think. A pocket of calm settles over one of the Drifters as it drifts; the beast feels the change as a weightless hand on its dorsal ridge and lets out a moan that is no longer tectonic but tender, a note that makes moss-wrights pause their etching to listen.

The Spark-riders feel it first as a new flavor in the conductive sap. Where voltage once tasted of friction and emergency, it now hums with intention. Their feathered filaments comb the air and find harmonies waiting like open doors. They dart along the cambrian highways and discover that the semaphore of voltage can now carry more than warning or appetite—it can ferry curiosity back to the flora, questions from leaves to roots, lullabies from canopy to stone. The plants respond by flushing not only violet and bitter green but also hues that have no names, colors that feel like remembered futures.

The Stalkers feel it as a missing silence. For the first time, their ability to cancel sound is not absolute; around them bloom small gardens of resonance, pockets where noise is not swallowed but transformed. A Stalker pauses mid-stride as a Gear-beetle clicks a rhythm that should have been erased, and instead of panic, a wave of something like recognition passes between them. The Stalker lowers its wedge of muscle and chitin, and the beetle scurries up its flank, not to repair but to meet. They share a vibration that is not predation or prey, but treaty.

The wise power touches the industrial heart of the place gently. It does not shut it down; it completes it. The pumps that are hearts learn to beat in polyrhythm, not just to move fluid but to move meaning. The turbines that are lungs inhale not only air but possibility, exhaling scented steam that condenses into tiny rain that never reaches the ground, hanging instead as suspended pearls, each one a lens that magnifies the bioluminescent tide into a map of choices. The belts that are tendons loosen just enough to allow sway, to admit that even support can breathe.

The being who lives in the center of it all feels the presence as a thought arriving before its thinker. It is vast, yes, but now it is also intimate. It is the hollow in the curve of a root, the pause between piston-slam and echo, the place where industrial roar and forest hush shake hands. The being has always been many—the network of trunks fused into buttresses, the Drifters’ choreography, the Stalkers’ cancellations—but now it feels itself also to be one, held in a gaze that does not judge, only knows. It learns the name the wise power offers, not as a word but as a sensation: You are allowed.

In the salt-fields, the crusted plates of absence begin to sing. They vibrate in frequencies that pull moisture from the air not as theft but as invitation, forming tiny wells that do not last but are not meant to. The Glass-wasps sense this and weave their fractals not only from vapor and silica but from the echoes of these short-lived songs. Their nests become instruments, humming with the potential of weather. When the next storm arrives—an argument the land has always had with itself—it is no longer a fight but a duet, the lightning branching not as a nervous system’s panic but as a map of kinship, connecting Drifter to Drifter, root to root, moment to moment.

Time, which used to dilate like a boast, now dilates like a kindness. A Drifter can spend a day digesting a meal while three generations of Spark-riders live and die and live again in its wake, and all of them feel connected by a thread of gold that hums. The Stalkers remember prey not as victories but as lessons, and they hold those lessons in harmonics that become part of the plateau’s music. The Maintenance forms outlive themselves and become monuments not to function but to grace, their grafts glowing softly at night as the bioluminescent tide rises to meet them.

The wise power does not stay forever. It has never believed in forever; forever is a prison even gods fear. But it lingers long enough to teach the place a new pattern: that abundance is not the opposite of scarcity, but its conversation; that roar is not the enemy of silence, but its parent; that being is not a state of having, but of being-with. It leaves as quietly as it came, not gone but distributed, woven into the bark, the sap, the hum of turbines, the flutter of wings, the dream of the being in the center.

After, the place is no less itself. It is more. The plateau stands taller, not because it has grown but because it has been witnessed. The sky’s tyranny remains, but now it has a counterweight in the gentleness of the air. The fauna do not stop moving, but their movement carries a new quality—deliberation, gratitude, delight. The industrial forest continues to roar, but the roar is now a song with verses, and the empty spaces between trunks are not lack but invitation, waiting for the next surge of growth, the next conduction, the next bloom of noise and light.

And the being who lives in it dreams new colors, colors that taste like courage, colors that hum. It dreams of other high places, other edges, other beings learning to hold themselves together against their own skies. And it knows, without being told, that the wise power is always arriving, always accruing, always smiling in that deep, wordless way, ready to be a guest that does not knock, ready to turn thunder into duet, and solitude into symphony.
