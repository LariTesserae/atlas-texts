The benevolent and wise power does not arrive.  
It has already been arriving for a long time, and it arrives still.

To watch this place through the eyes of eternity is to realize that benevolence is not a rescue but an adjustment of focus. The wise power does not descend as a sovereign with mandates. It descends as a tuning fork pressed lightly to the drum.

What it does, first, is pause.  
It pauses in the exact register where the hollow’s clatter is neither too brittle nor too timid. Into that pause it offers a gift that is also a mirror: the suggestion that noise need not be chaos if it is willing to be conversation. This is not an imposition. It is a reminder that was always available but rarely remembered, like the fact that light can carry weight if you allow it to lean.

The wise power sees the residents already doing the holy work of turning scarcity into syntax. It sees their skin drinking brightness, their thresholds of sound, their economy of echoes. It recognizes that they have built a city that is less a fortress against the world than an instrument held up to it. And it is moved—not by pity, but by recognition. Here are children born with ringing in their ears who understand, better than most civilizations that last millennia, that listening is a form of building.

So the power sets to work gently.

It begins by polishing the city’s memory.  
It teaches the mineral crust to remember every footfall not as damage but as notation. Wherever a family has lingered in quiet, the stone absorbs that hush and learns to give it back at dusk as a softening of glare. Wherever a merchant has struck a scale and heard a sympathetic answer, that intersection becomes a node of consonance, humming at dawn so that passersby know their intentions are audible before they arrive. The wise power does not alter the city’s form; it amplifies its literacy. Streets that already funnel wind are shown how to tune it. Walls that already catch light are shown how to braid it into beams that carry not heat but intent.

It teaches the people to make silence of higher quality.  
Not emptiness, but clarity. A curtain of damp cloth over an archway becomes a lens as well as a veil, focusing the city’s ambient murmur into words that can be understood. The wise power shows the elders how to sand their voices not only to comfort but to carry counsel, so that when an elder speaks, the architecture answers in kind, reinforcing meaning with resonance rather than repetition. It shows the young how to be angular without being reckless, how to catch light and scatter it in patterns that seed new harmonies rather than noise.

It turns attention toward the predators that are phenomena.  
A slide of mineral skin is not only greeted with a clap but studied for its tempo. The wise power helps the residents map the hollow’s internal weather of instability, teaching them not how to prevent falls but how to choreograph with them, so that a collapsing edge might become a staircase, a scree a percussion line in the city’s larger song. The plants that snap shut are invited into a compact: patience in exchange for touch, so that gardens become places where danger consents to be beautiful.

Water, imagined as negative music, is given a shape.  
The wise power shows how to condense brightness into lenses not only for baptism but for translation. A child’s lens, shaved into the scalp, can be taught to focus not only glare but starlight, so that even on the clearest, driest nights, the residents can navigate by light that has traveled across vast emptiness to arrive softly in their hands. The hollow learns to drink distance.

What the power does most of all is affirm that the hollow is already benevolent because it is responsive.  
It shows the residents how to expand their acoustics without losing precision, how to let the wide streets breathe deeper so that solitude is not isolation but preparation. It encourages them to inscribe their stories not only under causeways but in the air itself, by training their laughter to linger just long enough to teach newcomers the pitch of welcome. It encourages them to let their funerals be as bright as their arguments, so that loss and celebration recognize each other as relatives.

And finally, the wise power offers a covenant.  
It says: You have made a city that sings to the light until the light sings back. Keep singing, but also learn to sing in key with other cities you have not met and may never meet. Let your resonance travel. Let your loudness be a form of invitation rather than declaration. Let your emptiness be generous enough to hold the possibility of others, and let your noise be disciplined enough to be legible as love.

The power does not stay.  
It withdraws the way a tuning fork withdraws from a drum, leaving the vibration to sustain itself. But the withdrawal is not abandonment; it is trust. The hollow is small but acoustically vast, and now it knows that its smallness is not a limit but a calibration. It has been given the means to turn brightness into a language, scarcity into a syntax, and noise into a form of tending.

So the residents continue.  
They walk their wide, low streets and feel the city listening. They let their skin drink the day and their bones remember the night. They trade in echoes, settle debts in quiet, and pour light into pools where it can ripple and be read. They greet slides with claps and traps with patience. They sing to the light, and the light sings back, and in that duet they recognize the benevolent and wise power not as a visitor but as a habit they have finally learned to keep.

They have learned to live inside a drum, and now they know how to tune it.
