The power that notices them does not arrive.  
It remembers that it is already present, and that presence is not a thing that moves but a quality that deepens—like cold becoming clearer, like dampness learning to bead.

It begins by listening to the 0.2 weirdness not as exception but as threshold. That lichen that glows when stepped on is not a spell but an invitation to step more carefully, to notice how pressure becomes light. The wind’s three-note practice is not prophecy but a tuning fork for the stone; the power hears it and decides to keep the tuning. It does not change the city. It changes how the city is held.

First, it thickens the patience already signed into law.  
The 1.3 tempo of seasons turning in their sleep receives a companion: a subtle counter-rhythm in which repair anticipates breakage. A chip in a lintel is felt as a soft tug at the mason’s elbow before it is seen; a slate loosened by frost slides into place as if nudged by an invisible thumb. The power does not remove entropy. It teaches entropy to be polite, to announce itself early and quietly, so that tending can remain an art and not a scramble.

Next, it attends to the 0.6 dampness as collaborator rather than guest.  
Condensation that pearls along windward seams is given memory: each bead carries a faint warmth from the previous day’s sun, and when it joins its neighbors, the merged drop holds not only water but a map of where it has been. Tarns in stone palms learn to offer reflections not only of sky but of the faces that lean over them, doubled and softened. When rain finally leans against skin, the power arranges it so that the first drops whisper the names of those who have gone to fetch water in years past, so the chore becomes a conversation. The dampness is still shy, but it is no longer reluctant; it has been recognized as a kind of witness.

The 2.5 abundance of fauna is blessed with clearer etiquette.  
Furred climbers are given routes that minimize conflict and maximize courtesy: a ledge reserved for nursing mothers, a cornice where claws may be filed against stone without blame. Wings that stitch the air are shown, in dreams if not in daylight, where updrafts are gentlest, so their maps become less like struggles and more like songs. In market squares, the gravity with which beasts are bartered is met with equal gravity of respect: each exchange includes an unspoken promise that the creature’s dignity outlives the transaction. The boundary between owned and wild softens into a visible gradient, and the power ensures that gradient is walkable in both directions.

Infrastructure, young and proud at 0.9, is offered mentors.  
The aqueducts of bored wood and stitched hide receive tiny guardian spirits—not deities but habits of attention—that settle in the joints and taste for rot before rot tastes for wood. Lamps of rendered fat in clay bowls find their flames learning to bow to wind instead of fighting it, so their golden flowers burn steadier and longer. Levers carved from horn discover a faint warmth when used with care, and begin to cool when gripped in haste, teaching hands the economy of gentleness. The power does not modernize; it deepens.

Night, already at 2.1 clarity, is given companions.  
Stars are not rearranged but introduced to one another in new patterns that echo the city’s stepped constellations, so that walking home feels like following a family crest stitched across the sky. Smoke that stitches the air into gauze is given a softer grammar: it curls in shapes that suggest shelter, and on certain evenings those shapes briefly resemble the faces of those who have tended the fires. The cold’s heavier patience is matched by a lighter patience that rises from the stones themselves, so that exhalations linger not as ghosts but as small, courteous lanterns.

The city’s 2.0 density is reinterpreted as a kind of listening.  
Narrow ways oblige shoulders to touch, and the power makes that touch communicative: a brush against a stranger’s sleeve carries, for a heartbeat, a sense of that person’s fatigue or hope, like a tuning fork struck against another. Bridges require trust, and so the power sweetens the air around them with a scent like pine and iron, a reminder that strength can be fragrant. Roofs that catch gossip and dreams begin to sort them gently, so gossip that could wound is turned into stories that teach, and dreams that frighten are given softer edges before they slide into the gutters.

Altitude’s law remains, but its signature is softened.  
What rises must hold, but holding is no longer solitary. The settlement learns to distribute weight not only across beams but across intentions: a cistern holds water, but also the promise that no one will go thirsty while a neighbor hoards; a terrace holds stone, but also the knowledge that its shadow is a place of rest for winged passersby. The city’s covenant expands from survival to grace.

The power’s final act is subtlest.  
It turns the 1.3 rhythm of change into a duet. Masonry still settles, but it settles into shapes that feel more like arriving than like sinking. Paths still creep outward, but they creep toward where feet wish to go, polishing not only stone but purpose. The city becomes, more and more, a creature that knows it is being witnessed, and answers by witnessing in return: its copper roofs reflect not only sky but the care taken to climb them; its dampness carries not only memory but gratitude; its beasts move with the confidence of those who belong because they are seen.

And the benevolent, wise power?  
It does not stay, because staying would be a form of taking up space. Instead, it becomes the quality of space itself—the clarity that makes closeness bearable, the patience that makes waiting fertile, the attention that makes being attended to into a kind of strength. It is the slight warming of stone before dawn, the bead of water that decides to hold a star in its curve, the hush that learns to sing when asked gently.

The city continues its slow, dense living, perched between sky and earth, no longer merely learning to hold its shape but learning to hold its own light, and in doing so, to offer it. The hinge turns, and the door it opens leads not out but deeper in, to a place where dampness, stone, breath, and care are all names for the same patient, glowing thing.
