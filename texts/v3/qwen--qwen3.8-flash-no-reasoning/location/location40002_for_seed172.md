**The Materiality of the Void**

It does not come from a sun. It comes from the ceiling, a vast dome of translucent aerogel panels that diffuse the output of fusion reactors burning miles away. The light is flat, even, and unchanging. It casts no shadows because there are no angles left for darkness to hide in. Every corner is rounded, every edge beveled, every transition engineered for zero friction.

This is **young erosion**, but not in the way of wind or water carving canyons. Here, "erosion" means *freshness*. The surfaces look as if they were machined seconds ago. There are no scratches, no scuffs, no dust. In this sealed environment, entropy has been paused. The metal is pristine because nothing touches it without permission. If a bolt loosens, it is tightened before it falls. If a wire frays, it is replaced before it sparks. The world is maintained at a state of eternal newness, a paradox of agelessness in a place built by hands now long dead or digitized.

It feels cool against the skin, not because it is freezing, but because it is *still*.
