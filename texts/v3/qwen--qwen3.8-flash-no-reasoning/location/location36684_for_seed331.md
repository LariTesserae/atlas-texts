You are standing in the **Basin of Last Stillness**, though "basin" implies a depression in the earth, and this place has long since forgotten what it means to be depressed or elevated. The silence returns, heavier than before, filled with the soft rustle of wings and the distant, muffled drip of condensation. This is a place that has reached the end of its evolution. It is settled.
