But the silence (sound 0.9) is broken by the machinery.

This is the paradox: **Built: 0.3** suggests no structures, yet **Tech: 2.3** implies industry. Here, the industry is not made *on* the land; it is grown *from* it. The "factories" are biological. They hunt the moths by listening to the slight change in the echo-field caused by a wingbeat.

The scale (2.7) is overwhelming. If you were to walk from one Resin-Engine to the next, it would take days. The horizon is never visible; it is swallowed by the glowing mist. The landscape feels infinite, a labyrinth of dark stone, towering glass plants, and throbbing organic factories. It is a place where the line between nature and machine has dissolved completely. There are no ruins, no roads, no fences. There is only the cycle: the rain falls, the sponge-rock drinks, the heat rises, the engines wake, the moths dance, the stalkers listen, and the darkness holds it all together.

It is a quiet apocalypse, ongoing and sustainable.
