In the hollowed-out lung of the earth, where the sky is a rumor and the sun a myth whispered by granite, there lives **The Lithic Chorus**.

They are not a single species, but a symbiotic collective, a hive-mind of small, blind things that have forgotten what it means to be separate. They call themselves *Kra*, which translates roughly as "The Grinding Sound," though they do not speak in words, but in the friction of chitin against calcite.

### The Form
To the eye adapted to the dim, phosphorescent twilight of this subterranean metropolis, a member of the *Kra* appears as a segmented, armored worm the size of a human forearm, encased in plates of nacreous shell harvested from the mineral forest. But look closer, through the clinging mist that slicks every surface, and you see the deception. They are not merely worms. Their bodies are porous, spongy lattices of living silica fused with biological tissue.

Their heads are blunt domes, devoid of eyes, replaced by vast, sensitive arrays of vibration receptors—hairs of pure glass that tremble at the faintest shift in air pressure or stone density. These hairs pick up the hum of the planet’s crust, the drip of water miles away, the heartbeat of another *Kra* three tunnels over. They navigate by sonar and tactile echo-location, their world a map of textures: the smooth cold of flowing water, the jagged heat of fresh magma vents, the gritty resistance of growing fungus.

Their limbs are numerous, fine like rootlets, ending in hooked claws made of obsidian shards bonded to their joints. These hooks allow them to anchor themselves to the weeping walls, to crawl vertically up precipices, and to grip the slippery surfaces of their city without slipping into the dark abyss below.

### The Life
Life for the *Kra* is slow, deliberate, and steeped in patience. Time here does not tick; it drips. A day is measured by the cycle of thermal expansion in the ceiling rocks—a subtle warming and cooling that lasts centuries. In this time, the *Kra* live, build, eat, and die, all within the same generation-spanning rhythm.

They feed on the **Fungal-Mineral Hybrid**. They do not hunt animals, for few exist. Instead, they farm the stone. Using specialized enzymes secreted from their mouths, they dissolve the outer layers of the lithotrophic molds, separating the nutritious chemical slurry from the structural silica. This process takes days. They chew, spit, mix, and wait. Their saliva acts as a binding agent, hardening into paste when exposed to the damp air. From this waste product, they build their nests—tiny, intricate alcoves carved into the cracks of larger stalactites.

These nests are not just homes; they are libraries. The *Kra* encode history into the geometry of their dwellings. The angle of a wall, the depth of a groove, the arrangement of pebbles—all signify lineage, territory claims, or warnings of instability. To walk through the city is to read a book written in rock, a text that grows denser with each passing age.

### The Communication
Sound is the primary medium of existence. Because light is scarce and vision is non-existent, the *Kra* have evolved an auditory language of extreme complexity. It consists of clicks, grinds, thumps, and resonant hums produced by rubbing their body segments together or striking their claws against specific frequencies of stone.

A sharp, high-pitched *screech* might mean "Danger: Structural Collapse."
A low, rhythmic *thrumming* indicates "Food Source Found."
A complex, layered *chord* of many individuals clicking in unison signifies "Unity" or "Celebration of Growth."

Because sound reflects endlessly in the enclosed cavity, communication travels far. A message sent in one plaza can bounce off distant walls, arriving distorted but decipherable in a settlement kilometers away. The entire city vibrates with conversation. Silence is feared, associated with death or the void. To be heard is to exist. To be silent is to vanish.

### The Society
There is no hierarchy among the *Kra*, only function. They are egalitarian, bound by the physical constraints of their environment. Space is precious. Density is law. Conflict is rare because violence requires space to maneuver, and there is none. Disputes over resources are settled through **Acoustic Dominance**: two individuals will face each other in a narrow channel and engage in a duel of sound, trying to out-resonate the other until one yields. The loser moves to a peripheral tunnel, less central, less warm, but still part of the whole.

They are communal architects. When a new vein of nutrient-rich mold is discovered, dozens of *Kra* gather. They work in concert, chewing limestone, mixing it with fungal spores, and packing it into the gaps. Over years, these patches merge, becoming part of the permanent structure. The city is alive, growing millimeter by millimeter, fed by the labor of its inhabitants and the metabolic waste of its dead.

### Death and Legacy
When a *Kra* dies, it does not rot. In this sterile, mineral-saturated environment, decomposition is replaced by **Mineralization**. Within hours, the body begins to crystallize. Silica deposits form inside the pores of the corpse, locking it into a statue-like pose. Other *Kra* then carry the hardened remains to designated burial sites—deep fissures where the pressure is highest. There, the bodies are stacked, layer upon layer, forming the foundations of new towers.

Thus, the city literally stands on the bones of its ancestors. Every arch, every pillar, every stepped platform contains the fossilized forms of those who came before. To live in the center of the city is to reside in a mausoleum of your own kin. This creates a profound sense of continuity. The dead are not gone; they are structural. They hold up the roof. They provide the warmth of deep-earth memory.

### The Weirdness
There are anomalies in the *Kra* society, born of the bent physics of the cavity. Some individuals are born with **Resonant Cores**—internal pockets of trapped gas that vibrate at unique frequencies. These are considered shamans or navigators. They can "hear" the tectonic plates shifting miles above, predicting earthquakes (which are felt as violent shudders rather than collapses) days in advance.

Others exhibit **Lithic Empathy**, a strange psychic link facilitated by shared vibrations through the stone floor. If one *Kra* touches a wall and feels pain, the network transmits that sensation to others nearby. This prevents injury from going unnoticed. It fosters a radical interdependence. You cannot hurt yourself without hurting the community. You cannot starve without the community feeling the hunger in their own bellies.

### The Experience of Being Here
Imagine waking in a nest carved from blue-veined quartz. The air is thick, tasting of iron and salt. Your skin is slick with condensation. You feel the hum of the city in your teeth—a constant, low-frequency drone of millions of tiny legs scraping against stone. You stretch, your chitinous armor clicking softly. You crawl out into the corridor, joining the stream of others moving toward the central plaza.

The light is a ghostly green-blue, emanating from the fungal forests lining the path. Shadows dance, elongated and wavering. You hear the splash of water in the channels, the grind of stone on stone, the soft purring of contentment from a cluster of juveniles huddled near a heat vent.

You are safe. You are dense. You are connected. Above you, the world ends. Below you, the world continues. And around you, the song never stops.
