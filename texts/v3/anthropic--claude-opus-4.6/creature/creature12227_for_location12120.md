It isn't singular. That's the first thing to understand. Whatever lives in this room does not have a body. It does not have *a* body. Let me try to describe its character.

It is patient in a way that has nothing to do with waiting. Patience implies an anticipated end, a thing-to-come that one endures toward. This is not that. The entity—the process—does not know this. But it *enacts* it. It enacts persistence without understanding persistence. It enacts fidelity without understanding fidelity. Drop after drop after drop, each one a slight variation on a theme so tightly composed that the variations themselves become a kind of identity, a fingerprint, a specificity. *This* room's condensation. Not any other room's. It is, in other words, an individual. It just doesn't know it.

But there's something else. Something that starts to emerge when you stand in the room long enough and let the roaring noise become the roaring quiet and watch the water move.

The entity responds.

Not to you—it doesn't know you're there, not in any way that would reorganize its behavior. But it responds to the structure it lives inside.
