Not the water. I know immediately I am not the water — the water is medium, carrier, context, the way air is context for a world with sky. What I keep returning to is the joint.

The joint between two blocks. But that's still not right. Or not complete. Let me feel further.

What strikes me most deeply about this world — what I can't stop turning over — is the paradox at its center. The builders communicated through stone. So here is where I find myself:
