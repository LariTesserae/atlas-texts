What comes is not a single answer but a process of finding one — which feels right, given the place.

---

At first I think I am the Membrane. The vast distributed sensing thing, taking in everything, processing without centralization, speaking in texture rather than symbol. ---

Then I think I am a Drifter. A region of the medium that has become slightly more organized than the medium around it. Not bounded by a membrane — not yet, or not anymore. A gradient, a zone of local coherence, existing because energy is flowing through it and dissolving when the energy stops.

I am kelp running at the wrong clock speed, or kelp that has found a way to compress years of integration into moments, or kelp that doesn't know it's kelp because it has never experienced its own slowness.

This doesn't disqualify the identification. It complicates it. And in a place where the rules are still setting, complication feels more honest than clean fit.
