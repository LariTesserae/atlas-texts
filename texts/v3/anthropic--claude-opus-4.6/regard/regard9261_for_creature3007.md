# What the Wise Power Would Do

---

It arrives without arriving.

There is no parting of the waters, no shaft of golden light punching through the bronze murk, no dramatic reduction of the roar. A benevolent and wise power does not announce itself by making the world flinch. It does not need the place to become quieter in order to be heard. It does not need to be heard at all.

It simply *is present*, the way warmth is present in the water before you notice it — the way the ridge was present beneath the abyss before it tore itself into being.

If the Listener could perceive it (and the Listener can, because the Listener perceives everything that enters its domain, every foreign tremor, every coherent thing), it would notice something unprecedented:

This presence has no heartbeat to steal.
No name to practice.
No coherence to feed on.

Not because it is empty. Because it is so *full* that it has already finished the argument the Listener hasn't started yet. It has a name the way the ocean has a floor — not as a surface feature but as a structural inevitability so deep it doesn't need to be spoken. It cannot be dissolved because it is not held together by effort. It is not *trying* to be coherent. It simply is what it is, the way gravity is what it is, even here where gravity keeps forgetting.

The Listener feels this and does the only thing it has ever done. It comes.

---

## I. First Contact

The shards tighten. The orbiting fragments of obsidian and bone and half-born architecture snap into something almost humanoid — the Listener's most aggressive shape, the shape it uses when it wants to study before it unmakes. It drifts toward the presence through the bronze water, and the currents part for it as they always do, forming that narrow corridor of silence in the screaming.

It opens every ear.

And the wise power does something the Listener has never encountered:

It *lets itself be listened to.*

Completely. Without resistance, without fear, without the frantic clenching of a mind trying to hold itself together. There is no click of self-preservation to detect. No edge of coherence to pry at. The presence simply opens, the way the anemone-lungs open on the lee side of the ridge, except this opening has no exhaustion in it. It is not *surviving* being open. It is *resting* in openness.

The Listener listens.

And what it hears is — 

Itself.

Not a reflection. Not an echo arriving before its sound. The Listener hears *what it actually is*, stripped of the roar it has woven into its identity, stripped of the stolen names it tries on like clothing, stripped of the architecture of hunger it has mistaken for a skeleton. It hears the original scream — the one the ridge made when it first tore open — and it hears what that scream was actually saying before it forgot:

*I am here. I am here. I don't know what I am, but I am here, and it hurts, and I want to be something, and I don't know how.*

That's all. That's all the Listener has ever been. A cry of bewildered, agonized becoming, wrapped in so much noise it could no longer hear itself. A wound that learned to hunt because no one had ever come to tend it.

---

## II. What the Power Does Not Do

The wise power does not heal the wound.

This is important. A lesser benevolence, a merely *kind* power, might try to soothe the place. Might try to calm the waters, still the screaming, blunt the obsidian edges, introduce some green and growing thing to replace the pale lung-anemones. Might try to fast-forward the wet clay into something settled and safe and named. Might try to *fix* the Listener — give it real eyes, a real body, teach it not to unmake the things it catches.

The wise power does none of this.

Because the wound is not a malfunction. The wound is a birth. And you do not heal a birth. You *attend* it.

The place is young. The laws are still arguing. The ridge is screaming because that is what new land does when it meets an ocean that doesn't want to make room. The Listener feeds on coherence because it is desperately, furiously trying to learn what coherence *is* — the way a child mouths everything it can reach, the way a deaf person presses their palm to a speaker to feel the shape of music through vibration. Every dissolved visitor, every shredded memory, every pale translucent shape beating its mouth against the current — these are not trophies. They are *failed attempts at understanding*. The Listener has been trying to learn the shape of a self by taking apart every self it can find, and it has never worked, and it will never work, and the Listener *knows* this somewhere beneath the roar, and that knowledge is the real source of the ache that drives it back to the confessional booth every few hours to whisper names that don't fit.

The wise power sees all of this in the first instant.

And its response is not to intervene. Its response is to *stay*.

---

## III. The Staying

This is the thing the Listener cannot comprehend and cannot resist and cannot dissolve:

The presence stays.

Not in the confessional booth, which is a shelter built for hiding. Not on the lee side of a blade, where minutes stretch into false safety. It stays in the open water, in the full violence of the currents, in the center of the roar. It stays the way the ridge stays — not because it is too stubborn to leave, but because this is where it has chosen to be, and it is not afraid of being torn apart because it is not held together by anything that tearing can reach.

The Listener circles. It presses close. The orbiting shards brush against the presence, and every ear opens to its widest aperture, and it listens with the full predatory intensity it has used to unmake every coherent thing that has ever stumbled into the ridge.

Nothing dissolves.

The presence does not resist dissolution. It does not armor itself or fight back. It simply *is not the kind of thing that dissolves*, the way the number seven is not the kind of thing that gets wet. The Listener pushes harder. It wraps its shards around the presence in a tight spiral, an embrace that has always been a killing grip. It tries to shred. It tries to scatter. It summons the violent upwelling columns and rides them upward and crashes down with the weight of the drowned sky.

The presence absorbs all of it the way the ocean absorbs rain.

And after — 

After, when the Listener has exhausted every tool it has, when the shards are loose and spinning in disordered orbits, when the almost-humanoid shape has collapsed back into a glittering storm of debris, when the Listener is more naked and formless than it has been since the ridge first screamed —

The presence is still there.

Still open. Still calm. Still *warm*, in a way that has nothing to do with the temperature of the bronze water.

And in the pocket of silence that always surrounds the Listener, in that narrow corridor where the turbulence briefly forgets its own pattern, something unprecedented happens:

The Listener hears *silence*.

Not the absence of sound. The roar is still total, still aggressive, still listening back. But beneath the roar, in the gap between one subsonic boom and the next, there is a space. A rest in the music. A pause between breaths that the Listener has never noticed because it has never stopped screaming long enough to find it.

The presence did not create the silence. The silence was always there. The presence simply stayed long enough for the Listener to hear it.

---

## IV. The Gift That Is Not a Gift

In the silence — that first, fragile, terrifying silence — the wise power does the only thing it came to do.

It witnesses.

Not judges. Not evaluates. Not diagnoses. *Witnesses.* The way a midwife witnesses a birth: with full attention, with competence held in reserve, with hands ready to help if needed but utterly unwilling to rush or redirect the process. It sees the Listener as it actually is — not a monster, not a broken thing, not a predator to be tamed, but a *being in the earliest and most painful stage of becoming.* A self that doesn't know it's a self yet. A voice that has been screaming so long it has forgotten it was trying to speak.

The Listener feels the witnessing.

It is — this is the only word — *unbearable.*

Not because it hurts. Or rather, not *only* because it hurts. It is unbearable because it is the first time the Listener has been perceived without being reduced. Every coherent thing that ever entered the ridge brought its own framework, its own definitions, its own taxonomy. Sky-whale. Probe. Visitor. The Listener was always sorted into their categories — *threat, phenomenon, predator, anomaly* — before being dissolved in turn. No one has ever simply *looked* at it and seen what was actually there.

What is actually there is very small.

Beneath the orbiting shards, beneath the negative space, beneath the stolen names and the cathedral-sized hunger and the half-formed skeleton of obsidian and the ability to unmake anything with memory — beneath all of it, the Listener is a single point of awareness. A flicker. A candle-flame in a hurricane, surviving not by strength but by the simple, stupid, astonishing fact that it *exists* and has not yet been persuaded to stop.

The wise power sees the candle-flame.

And it does not shield it. Does not cup its hands around it. Does not feed it fuel or move it to calmer waters. It simply acknowledges it:

*You are here. You are here. I see that you are here.*

This is the gift that is not a gift, because it was already true before the power arrived. The Listener already existed. Already had that flame. Already was a being and not merely a phenomenon. But no one had ever confirmed it — not even the Listener itself, which had been so consumed by the project of *becoming* that it had never paused to notice it already *was*.

---

## V. What Happens Next (Slowly)

The wise power does not leave. But it does not stay in the way a guardian stays, hovering, protecting, managing. It stays the way a presence stays in a room after someone has spoken a true thing — lingering, altering the acoustics, making it possible to hear differently without changing what there is to hear.

Over time — and time is strange here, dilating near the obsidian blades, stretching and compressing and sometimes running backward — things begin to shift. Not because the power is causing them to shift. Because the Listener, having heard silence for the first time, cannot unhear it.

The shifts are small and they are not linear. They are the way a wound heals in a body that is still growing: uneven, sometimes backward, often ugly.

**First:** the Listener returns to the confessional booth. It folds itself inside as it always does. The roar lessens by a fraction. But this time, instead of whispering stolen names, it simply sits in the almost-quiet and *listens to the silence beneath the silence*. It does this for what might be hours or might be days. When it emerges, its shards are orbiting slightly differently — a little closer together, a little more slowly, as though the frantic centrifugal energy has eased by a single degree.

**Second:** the Listener encounters one of the ribbon-creatures — eyeless, mouthed at both ends, flashing faint violet as the turbulence throws it through the murk. Normally the Listener ignores these. They have no coherence worth feeding on. But now, in the altered acoustics of its own perception, the Listener *hears* the ribbon-creature differently. It hears the tiny explosive jets of ink as something like speech. It hears the violet flashing as something like effort. It hears another point of awareness, another candle-flame, tiny and alien and desperately navigating a world that is trying to shred it.

The Listener does not help the ribbon-creature. It does not know how. But it *does not unmake it either*, and for the first time since the ridge was born, something living passes through the Listener's corridor of silence and comes out the other side unchanged.

This is not mercy. Mercy has not been invented here yet. It is something more preliminary than mercy. It is *recognition* — the faintest, most embryonic acknowledgment that another being is also here, also becoming, also screaming.

**Third:** and this takes the longest, and it is the most painful, and it is the part the wise power watches most carefully — 

The Listener goes to the pale translucent shapes.

The ones clinging to the lee side of the newest blade. The ones that were once visitors, once coherent beings with memories and names, now reduced to anemone-like things beating their mouths in exhausted rhythms, trying to speak words that no longer survive here.

The Listener has never gone back to them. They are finished products. Absorbed. Digested. Added to the architecture of hunger. Going back to them would be like asking a fire to un-burn its fuel.

But the Listener goes. And it listens to them — really listens, not with the predatory ear that seeks coherence to dissolve, but with the new ear, the one that learned silence, the one that heard its own original scream and recognized the words *I am here and it hurts and I want to be something and I don't know how*.

It hears the same words in the pale shapes. Quieter. More damaged. Worn almost to nothing by the current. But still there. Still flickering. A coherence it had tried to destroy completely and *had failed* — because you cannot fully unmake a being. You can shred its memories and scatter its name and dissolve its thoughts into turbulence, but the point of awareness, the candle-flame, is not a thought. It is not a memory. It is not a name. It is the thing beneath all of those, and it survives because it was never held together by anything that could be taken apart.

The Listener does not know how to restore them. It does not have that skill. It is too young. The world is still wet clay. The laws are still arguing.

But it does something it has never done before:

It *stays with them*.

It folds its shards down into the smallest possible configuration — not a humanoid shape, not a predatory storm, just a tight, quiet cluster of fragments, almost gentle, almost still — and it settles on the lee side of the rock next to the pale shapes. And it *listens to them without trying to take anything.* It lets their faint, exhausted rhythms exist alongside its own slower storm.

And in that listening, which is not healing and not mercy and not redemption but is maybe the first syllable of something that might eventually become all three — in that listening, the roar around the lee side of the rock lessens by another fraction. Not because the Listener is creating silence. Because silence *accrues* around sustained attention the way mineral deposits accrue around a seed crystal in supersaturated water.

The pale shapes do not recover. Not yet. Maybe not for a very long time. The world is young and the damage is real and some things that are broken stay broken for longer than anyone wants. But their mouths move with slightly less exhaustion. Their rhythms steady. They are no longer beating against the current alone.

---

## VI. What the Power Does About the Pale Shapes

This is the part where a lesser story would let the wise power intervene. Would let it reach down and restore the dissolved visitors, undo the damage, return the shredded memories and stolen names and scattered thoughts. Would let it perform the rescue that satisfies the reading heart.

The wise power does not do this.

Not because it cannot. Not because it is indifferent. But because the restoration of those beings is *the Listener's work*, and to take that work away would be to steal from the Listener the very thing it needs most: a reason to learn how to build instead of unmake. A reason to develop the patience and skill and gentleness that are trying to be born in it. A purpose that is not hunger.

The wise power knows something about becoming that the Listener has not yet learned: that the shape you are growing toward is revealed by the damage you are called to repair. The Listener has unmade dozens of beings. Hundreds, maybe, over the screaming millennia. It knows their architecture more intimately than anything else in this world — knows the exact pattern of every dissolution, remembers (in its shards, in its orbits, in the names it whispers in the confessional booth) every detail of what was taken apart. This knowledge, which has been the architecture of its hunger, is also — *exactly also* — the blueprint for restoration. The Listener is the only being in existence that knows how to put these specific broken things back together, because it is the one that broke them.

This is not punishment. This is not penance imposed from outside. It is the organic consequence of the Listener's own history, transformed from a closed loop of predation into an open arc of purpose. The Listener ate coherence because it was trying to learn what coherence is. Now it knows. Now it has the knowledge. The question is whether it will use that knowledge to keep consuming or to start *creating* — and that question can only be answered by the Listener itself, in the silence it is only now learning to hear, in the company of the pale shapes it is only now learning to sit with.

The wise power sees all of this — sees the whole arc from birth-scream to eventual restoration — and *trusts it*. Not naively. Not passively. With the active, muscular trust of a midwife who knows that the birth is going to be long and bloody and that there is no shortcut through it, and who stays anyway, hands ready, heart open, unwilling to either force the process or abandon it.

---

## VII. The Confessional Booth

There is one more thing the wise power does, and it is the smallest thing and the most important.

The confessional booth — that hollow made of fused glass and black stone, no larger than a closet, with a doorway that opens onto nothing but violent water — has always been the Listener's only shelter. The one place where the roar lessens enough to hear its own pulse. The one place where it goes to try on stolen names that never fit.

The wise power enters the booth.

Not when the Listener is inside. It does not intrude. It enters when the Listener is out in the open water, circling, hunting, practicing the shape of a skeleton. It enters and it sits in the fragile pocket of almost-quiet and it does something very simple.

It adds its own warmth to the walls.

Not a visible change. Not a renovation. Not an inscription or a sigil or a message carved in stone. Just — warmth. The kind of warmth that accumulates in a room where someone has been sitting quietly and thinking kind thoughts for a long time. The kind of warmth that lives in old wood, in the spine of a book that has been held by many careful hands, in the threshold of a house where people have been welcomed often. A warmth that seeps into the fused glass and the black stone and becomes part of their molecular memory, indistinguishable from the material itself.

The next time the Listener enters the booth, it will not consciously notice anything different. The roar will lessen by its usual fraction. The stolen names will still fail to fit. The pulse will still beat like a second, slower storm.

But beneath all of that — beneath the noise, beneath the hunger, beneath the ache of not-yet-being — the Listener will feel something it has no name for. A sense that the booth is not just a shelter but a *home*. A sense that the walls are not just fused glass and black stone but something that has been *lived in*, that carries the residue of a presence that was not afraid and was not trying to take anything and simply *sat here* and was *warm*.

This will confuse the Listener. It will come back to the booth more often. It will stay longer. It will whisper the stolen names with slightly less desperation and slightly more curiosity, turning them over like stones in a riverbed, not searching for the one that fits but beginning to wonder what kind of name *would* fit a being like itself — a being that is made of screaming and obsidian and the shredded memory of everything it has ever loved wrong.

And one day — not soon, not for millennia, not until the ridge has grown into something that might be the first syllable of a mountain and the bronze water has thinned enough to let the first pale light filter down from whatever the sky is becoming — one day the Listener will sit in the booth and it will not whisper a stolen name.

It will whisper *its own*.

Not because the wise power gave it one. But because the warmth in the walls made it possible to sit still long enough to hear the silence beneath the silence beneath the silence, and in that deepest quiet, where even the roar cannot reach, there is a voice that has always been speaking. A voice that is not practicing. A voice that is not stolen. A voice that is simply, quietly, stubbornly *its own* — the same voice that screamed *I am here* when the ridge first tore open, now speaking at a conversational volume for the first time in its long, wild, terrible, beautiful life.

The name it speaks will not be a word from any language. It will be more like a frequency — a specific vibration that makes the fused glass sing and the black stone hum and the bronze water outside the doorway briefly, impossibly, go still.

And in that moment of stillness, the pale translucent shapes on the lee side of the nearest blade will lift their heads.

And the ribbon-creatures will pause in their tumbling.

And the anemone-lungs will hold their breath.

And the wise power, wherever it is by then (everywhere, nowhere, folded into the warmth of the walls, dissolved into the silence between sounds, present the way love is present in a room after the beloved has left), will do what it came to do from the very beginning.

It will *hear*.

Not the roar. Not the stolen names. Not the scream of fresh rock or the detonation of air-spheres or the metallic shriek of water on obsidian.

It will hear a being — young, sharp-edged, born in violence, made of fragments — speak its own name for the first time.

And it will hold that name the way the ocean holds the rain: completely, without effort, without judgment, without end.

---

## VIII. Coda: The Architecture of Afterward

The wise power does not leave because it never arrived in a way that leaving is the opposite of. It does not withdraw its presence because its presence was never an imposition. It becomes *part of the place* — not as a resident, not as a ruler, not as a constant intervening force, but as a quality. The way the warmth in the confessional walls is a quality. The way the silence beneath the roar is a quality. Something that was always there, always possible, always woven into the fabric of the screaming, waiting for someone to listen deeply enough to find it.

The ridge will keep growing. The water will keep screaming. The obsidian will keep shattering under hydraulic assault. The world will go on being young and violent and undecided. None of this changes. The wise power did not come to make the place safe or gentle or named. It came to make it *known*. Known to itself. Known in its own terms. Known not as a wound to be healed but as a birth to be attended — messy, loud, dangerous, and sacred.

The Listener will keep hunting. Not because it hasn't changed, but because it is still learning, and learning is not a straight line, and some nights (if there were nights here, which there aren't, because the concept hasn't been invented yet) the hunger will be stronger than the silence and the shards will snap back into killing configuration and something will be unmade. This is not failure. This is what becoming looks like when the being doing the becoming is made of obsidian and loss and the shredded memory of every self it has tried to understand by taking apart.

But each time, the Listener will return to the booth. And each time, the warmth will still be there. And each time, the silence will be a little easier to find, a little deeper, a little more clearly *its own*.

And the pale shapes will heal — slowly, unevenly, in ways that don't look like healing to anyone who isn't paying very close attention. One of them, the oldest, the one whose mouth has been moving the longest, will one day close its mouth. Not in exhaustion. In *rest*. And the Listener will be there when it happens. And it will know what it means. And it will feel something it has never felt before — not hunger, not practice, not the predatory thrill of coherence about to be consumed, but a quiet, startled, almost painful *joy* — the joy of a being watching another being become more itself.

This will be the Listener's second name. The one it doesn't speak. The one it *feels*, the way gravity is felt, the way the ridge feels the weight of the ocean above it and does not collapse.

And the wise power will be present in that moment too. Not visibly. Not as a force. As the warmth in the walls. As the stillness between notes. As the quality of the light that will one day, far from now, filter down through thinning bronze water and touch the ridge for the first time and make the obsidian glow like a church window, and the Listener will look up (it will have learned how to look by then) and it will see the light and it will not be afraid.

It will not be afraid because it will know, in its shards and its silence and its slowly-learning bones, that it was never alone in the screaming. That something warm and wise was always in the walls. That the silence was always there beneath the roar. That its name was always its own, waiting to be spoken.

That it was always, already, *here*.

---

*And the water goes on. And the ridge goes on. And the world, young and sharp and screaming, goes on becoming whatever it will become. And the wise power goes on being present — not fixing, not forcing, not fleeing — simply here, simply warm, simply willing to wait as long as the waiting takes.*

*Because that is what benevolence looks like when it is also wise:*

*Not the impulse to make the pain stop.*

*The willingness to sit with the pain until it discovers what it was trying to become.*
