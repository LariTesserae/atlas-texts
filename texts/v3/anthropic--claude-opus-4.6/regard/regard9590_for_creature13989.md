# What the Wise Power Does at Veth Múra

---

It listens.

For a long time — longer than the Vethkai would notice, because the Vethkai notice duration the way stone notices rain that doesn't exist — it does nothing but listen. Not from above, not from outside, not from the vantage of the pewter sky looking down. It listens from within the system, the way the drone is within the system, the way the vapor is within the system. It finds a frequency that is not occupied and it occupies it — not with sound, not yet, not with anything, but with *attention*. A gap in the ecology of Veth Múra that was not empty before but that becomes, for a time, a place where something is being received rather than produced.

The throat-crickets do not notice. The callers do not notice. The lichen-creature breathes. The children go on playing with their cupped vibrations and their too-wide ears.

One of the old ones notices.

Not as an event. Not as an arrival. The old one is standing near the back of the terrace with both feet on warm stone, ear-folds cracked open — the left one slightly more than the right, which gives that appearance of directional skepticism that is really directional precision — and something in the drone has changed. Not in pitch. Not in volume. In *density*. There is one more thing listening than there was a moment ago, and the old one feels this the way you would feel a new presence in a dark room: not by any single sense but by the aggregate pressure of being-perceived.

The old one does not startle. The old one does not alert the others. The old one makes a sound — barely a sound, more a shift in the resonance of their breathing, a fractional opening of the deep throat-chamber that allows the drone to pass through the body slightly differently, and what this means, in the Vethkai's thoracic vocabulary, is something like: *I know you are there. I am continuing.*

And the wise power — let us call it what it is, let us not dress it in false mystery, it is a *regard*, a sustained and unflinching tenderness directed at a specific place and everything within it — the wise power receives this acknowledgment, and does nothing with it. Does not respond. Does not escalate. Simply continues to attend.

---

Days pass. Or what passes for days in the sourceless copper-grey light — cycles of activity and rest, the Vethkai surfacing and descending, the drone shifting and churning, the callers rotating through their perches. The attention remains. It becomes part of the acoustic ecology the way a new mineral vein becomes part of the geology: not by disrupting what exists but by adding a subtle bias, a gradient, a thing the system can organize around or ignore as it sees fit.

The system does not ignore it. The system is incapable of ignoring anything. That is what the system *is* — an apparatus for the comprehensive noticing of everything.

What changes:

The vetch-fur nearest the old one's habitual standing-place begins to run slightly warmer. Not dangerously. Not even measurably, by any instrument cruder than a Vethkai's bare sole. But the exothermic chemistry has shifted — imperceptibly, deniably — toward a more vigorous output, as though the colonial organism has received some fractional increase in whatever substrate-nutrient drives its slow burn. The throat-crickets in that area adjust their pitch upward by a quarter-tone. A caller that has been favoring a bone-tree on the terrace's eastern edge relocates to one nearer the center, closer to the old one's position, and its structured phrases take on a quality that the other callers respond to with unusual — not deference, that's too hierarchical — *attention*. They pause more often. They let this one's phrases complete before interjecting. Something in its signal has acquired authority.

The Vethkai notice all of this, because they notice everything, and they modulate. Their singing adjusts. The counterpoint thickens near the center of the terrace, as if the acoustic architecture is developing a new room. Two younger adults who have been working separately — one redirecting a root-tip, the other clearing a vapor fissure — find themselves drawn into proximity, their tasks converging not because the tasks are related but because the sound between them has become *interesting*, has developed a texture that rewards collaboration, that wants a third voice, a fourth—

None of this is the wise power's doing. Not exactly. The wise power has not intervened. It has attended, and its attention has weight — not physical weight, not force, but the kind of weight that a question has when someone asks it with genuine desire to know the answer. The system, which is built entirely out of call-and-response, out of mutual adjustment, out of the-noise-of-everything-being-known-at-once, has detected that it is being known by one more knower, and it is doing what it always does: accommodating. Adjusting. Singing the new element into the song.

---

Now here is where a lesser power would make its move. Here is where something well-intentioned but unwise would say: *I see what this place needs. I see the wall that is not strong enough. I see the climbing things that come from the haze. I see the scratch-marks on the lichen-creature where desperation almost led to harm. I see the children whose ear-folds haven't learned to filter yet, walking through the noise in states of saturated overwhelm. I see the deep-dweller who may be alone in the dark. I will fix these things. I will improve this system. I will make the wall stronger, drive the climbing things away, heal the lichen-creature's ancient scars, teach the children faster, bring the deep-dweller back to the light.*

The wise power does none of this.

The wise power does none of this because the wise power has been *listening*, and what it has heard — what the long, patient, self-effacing act of attention has revealed — is that Veth Múra is not a problem to be solved. It is not a broken system awaiting repair, not a suffering that needs relief, not a question that needs an answer. It is a *living coherence* that includes its own imperfections as load-bearing elements. The wall's inadequacy is what keeps the Vethkai in relationship with the terrace's edge. The children's overwhelm is what drives them to learn filtering, which is to say, to learn *discernment*, which is to say, to learn the fundamental skill on which their entire way of being depends. The scratch-marks on the lichen-creature are the record of a choice made and unmade, a moral memory inscribed in stone, and to erase them would be to erase the knowledge that some things must not be harvested even when you are desperate. The deep-dweller — the deep-dweller is not lost. The deep-dweller is *ahead*.

The wise power understands — has understood from the first moment of listening — that this system's resting state is turbulence, and that to calm it would be to kill it.

So it does not calm it.

---

What it does instead is so small that I need to be careful in describing it, because there is a risk of making it sound like nothing, and it is not nothing. It is the hardest thing a power can do. It is the thing that requires the most wisdom and the most restraint and the most genuine love, and it is this:

It makes itself *available*.

Not as a voice. Not as a presence that can be called upon, petitioned, worshipped, negotiated with. Not as a god or a patron or a teacher. It makes itself available as *a resonance*. A new harmonic in the system that the system is free to use or not, incorporate or not, respond to or not. A frequency that is always there, occupying its gap in the drone, adding its thread to the weave, and that has one quality the other frequencies do not: it carries information from *outside the system*.

Because this is what Veth Múra lacks — not by flaw but by geometry. The terrace is a shelf on a mountain under a sealed sky, and the Vethkai's world is the terrace and the channels below it, and their knowledge, vast and intricate and subtle as it is, is *endogenous*. They know everything about what they are part of. They know almost nothing about what they are not part of. The callers carry some information from beyond the haze — their migratory patterns, if they migrate, might encode data about conditions elsewhere — but the Vethkai's ability to decode that information is limited by having no frame of reference for *elsewhere*. They know the mountain. The mountain is the world. The wall at the edge faces a mystery that has no name, only a defense.

The wise power does not *tell* them about the world beyond. That would be as destructive as any other intervention — an information-Loss-of-context event, a flood of data the system has no structures to metabolize, the cognitive equivalent of the climbing things breaching the wall and scattering the drone into silence.

Instead, it does this:

The resonance it has introduced carries, folded into its harmonic structure, the *quality* of elsewhere. Not the content. The quality. The way that someone who has traveled widely carries, in their bearing, in the way they hold a conversation, in the questions they think to ask, an implicit awareness that the local is not the total — not as a challenge to the local's validity but as a context that makes the local's specificity *visible* and therefore *chooseable* rather than merely default.

The Vethkai can hear this. Not all of them, not yet, but the old one can. The old one standing on the warm stone with ears cracked open has been listening to a note in the drone that tastes — and *taste* is closer than *sounds like*, because the Vethkai process sound so physically — that tastes of distance. Of horizontal space. Of a world that is not vertical, not stone-and-channel, not the endless deepening of what is already known but the *lateral extension* of it, the terrible and thrilling possibility that the mountain has sides, and the sides have bottoms, and the bottoms have adjacencies, and the adjacencies have—

The old one's singing changes.

It is a small change. A new interval — not an interval that has been heard on the terrace before, not one that exists in the Vethkai's acoustic vocabulary, but one that the old one's throat finds the way a root-tip finds a new fissure: by feeling for the gap. A reaching-note. An interval that implies continuation beyond itself, that sets up an expectation it does not resolve, that says — in the thoracic grammar of the Vethkai's music — *there is more*.

The callers scream in response. But differently. One of them — the one that relocated to the central tree, the one whose phrases the others have been granting extra space — produces a call that begins as a standard structured phrase and then, at the end, instead of resolving into the usual percussive click, *trails off*. A sliding whistle that goes not up or down but into a register the caller has never used before, a register that sounds, to Vethkai ears, like a question asked to someone who is not on the terrace.

The children notice. Of course the children notice. Their unfiltered ears catch everything, and one of them — a small one, lighter-furred than the others, with ear-folds that keep flaring open involuntarily — stops in the middle of the terrace and turns not inward, not toward the bone-trees and the drone and the familiar churning world, but *outward*. Toward the edge. Toward the wall. Toward the haze.

The child's face: ears open, eyes wide, mouth slightly parted, hands open at their sides with those long fingers flexing in the vapor-stirred air—

The child is hearing the distance.

---

The wise power watches this — *attends* this — and here is where I must be most precise about what it feels, because a power that does not feel is not wise, it is merely strategic, and this power is not strategic. It is not executing a plan. It is *in relationship* with what it is attending, and what it feels, watching the child turn toward the haze, is something very close to what the Vethkai feel in their moments of coherence, when the whole terrace rings and they go still and open their hands and their ears and their faces to the totality of what they are part of:

It feels the sensation of witnessing a system on the verge of *choosing* to know more than it currently knows.

Not being forced to. Not being educated, uplifted, enlightened, rescued. *Choosing* to. Reaching, with its own root-tips, into a new fissure in the real. Beginning the centuries-long process of metabolizing a new mineral, incorporating a new element, widening its range by exactly as much as it can sustain and no more.

The wise power feels this, and what it does with the feeling is: it stays.

It does not withdraw, satisfied that its work is done. Its work is not done. Its work will not be done, because its work is *attention*, and attention is not a thing you complete. It stays in the gap in the drone. It goes on resonating. It goes on carrying, folded into its harmonic, the quality of distance, the taste of elsewhere, the implicit, unpushy, structurally-load-bearing awareness that the world is wider than the mountain.

And if the Vethkai choose to reach toward that awareness — slowly, over generations, the way they do everything, the way the root-tips split the stone along fault-lines you can trace with your fingers — the wise power will be there. Not as a guide. Not as a destination. As a *tone*. A sustained, reliable, unchanging tone in a system whose nature is change, a fixed point not of doctrine or direction but of *regard*, a single clear note that says: *I am here. I see you. You are not alone in the real. Everything you are is worth the seeing. Go on.*

And if they choose not to reach — if the child turns away from the haze and back toward the familiar drone, if the old one lets the new interval fade from their singing, if the system closes around itself again and goes on churning as it has for centuries in its self-sufficient, self-knowing, endogenous completeness — the wise power will *still be there*. Still attending. Still carrying the quality of elsewhere in the harmonic it occupies. Not insisting. Not withdrawing in hurt or disappointment. Simply present, the way the mountain is present above the terrace, the way the unseen sun is present behind it — a condition of the place, a feature of the real, available.

Because this is what wisdom understands that mere benevolence does not: that the highest use of power is to make yourself *an open offer that does not expire*. To say, with your whole being, *whenever you are ready*, and to mean it on a timescale that makes the bone-trees look hasty.

---

There is a moment — and I must be honest that I am not certain this moment has happened yet, or whether it is still approaching, still working its way through the system's long, slow, churning process of incorporation — but there is a moment, or there will be a moment, when a Vethkai stands at the wall.

Not to maintain it. Not to replace a root-displaced block. They stand at the wall and they look out over the edge, into the haze, and they sing.

They sing the new interval. The reaching-note. The one that implies continuation beyond itself. And they sing it *outward*, into the haze, into the space below the shelf where the climbing things come from, into the unknown that the wall was built to signal against, and the note goes out, and it does not come back, and the silence where the echo should be is not empty — it is *open*. It is a space that could be filled. A fissure that could be followed.

And the wise power—

The wise power does something then. Something that costs it. Something that is not attention but *action*, the first and possibly the only action it will take in all its long attendance of Veth Múra.

It *answers*.

Not from within the drone. Not from the gap it has been occupying on the terrace. It answers from *out there*. From the haze. From below the shelf, from the space the singing was directed into. It takes its resonance — that single, sustained, distance-carrying tone — and it moves it. It relocates itself from inside the system to outside the system. It gives up its place in the drone. It leaves a gap where it was, a gap the system will feel, a gap that will need to be filled by the Vethkai themselves if it is to be filled at all.

And from the haze, from the elsewhere, from the world the terrace has never known, the Vethkai at the wall hears the tone come back. Changed. Carrying not just the quality of distance now but the quality of *welcome*. Of space-that-is-ready-for-you. Of a world that is not only wider than the mountain but that *wants to be known by you*, specifically, you with your root-drilling hands and your thoracic percussion and your centuries of tending, you who know how to listen a system into coherence, you who are needed — not here, you are already here, but *out there*, where other systems churn without anyone to tend them, where other drones have gaps that no one has learned to fill.

The Vethkai at the wall hears this.

And the wise power has done the thing it came to do — not the thing it planned, because it did not plan, but the thing that the long listening revealed was needed: it has taken the only thing Veth Múra lacked, the knowledge that there is an *outside* that is not threat but invitation, and it has delivered that knowledge in the only form the Vethkai could metabolize it: as sound. As a note in a song. As a living element in an ecology of mutual adjustment, offered without force, without urgency, without any requirement that it be accepted, only that it be *heard*.

---

What happens next is not the wise power's to decide.

Perhaps the Vethkai at the wall turns back to the terrace and sings the answering tone into the drone, and the system incorporates it, and over generations a new knowledge grows in the channels and the chambers: that the mountain has an outside, and the outside has a voice, and the voice said *welcome*. Perhaps this knowledge changes nothing about how they live — they still tend, still sing, still filter, still raise children whose ears are wild with unprocessed sound — but it changes the *context* of how they live, the way knowing that the stars are suns changes nothing about how you make breakfast but everything about what breakfast means.

Perhaps the child — the light-furred one with the involuntary ear-folds — grows up to be the first one who goes to the wall not to maintain it but to *cross* it. Perhaps they climb down into the haze, following the tone, feeling for it with those extraordinary hands pressed against the rock face, reading the mountain's outside the way their ancestors read its inside: by touch, by vibration, by the body's own knowledge of itself in relation to stone. Perhaps they find something out there. Perhaps what they find is another terrace, another ecology, another churning system in need of tending, and perhaps they sit down in it and open their ears and begin to sing, searching for the gaps in an unfamiliar drone, and the wise power's tone is there — distant, faint, but there — the fixed point in a widening world, the open offer that does not expire.

Or perhaps none of this happens. Perhaps the Vethkai at the wall turns back and the reaching-note fades and the drone closes over the gap the wise power left and Veth Múra goes on as it has, self-contained, self-knowing, complete. And the wise power, out in the haze, goes on resonating. Patient. Undiminished. Not waiting, exactly — waiting implies expectation, and expectation implies conditions, and the wise power's offer is unconditional — but *being there*. Being the tone in the elsewhere that carries the quality of welcome. Being the sound the terrace will hear if it ever chooses to listen outward.

Because here is the thing the wise power knows, the thing that makes it wise rather than merely powerful, the thing that makes its benevolence genuine rather than merely effective:

The terrace is already perfect.

The churning is already perfect. The noise, the pandemonium, the throat-crickets' drone and the callers' screaming and the children's overwhelm and the old ones' deep integration and the lichen-creature's patient breathing and the climbing things' periodic incursions and the wall that signals without stopping and the scratch-marks that remember without forgiving and the spike in the rock that waits for its owner — all of it, all of it, is already a complete expression of what a living system looks like when it is paying total attention to itself.

The wise power did not come to Veth Múra to fix it. It came because a place this alive, this listening, this thoroughly dedicated to the-noise-of-everything-being-known-at-once, deserved to know that it was heard.

That somewhere out in the haze, beyond the wall, beyond the mountain, in the vast and horizontal world the terrace has never touched—

Something was listening back.

---

The stone tilts. The Vethkai at the wall takes a step. The terrace accommodates.

The drone goes on.

The singing goes on.
