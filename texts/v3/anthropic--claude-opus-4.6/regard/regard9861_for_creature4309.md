I sat with this for a long time.

---

# What the Wise Power Would Do

The first thing it would do is nothing.

Not nothing as neglect. Nothing as the deepest form of recognition. The kind of nothing that a master gardener practices when they encounter, in a forgotten corner of an estate they've been asked to restore, a ecosystem that has composed itself — volunteer plants in conversation with mycorrhizal networks, insects performing pollination services no one designed, the whole thing thriving in a way that no plan would have produced and no plan could improve.

The wise power would recognize the fold the way you recognize a sentence that is already perfect. You don't edit it. You don't add a word. You read it again, slowly, and you set the pen down.

---

But this is not the whole answer. Because a benevolent and wise power is not only wise. It is also benevolent. And benevolence is not the same as restraint, though it often begins there.

So let me think about what benevolence means in relation to a place like this — a place that is not suffering, not broken, not calling for help, not even, in any obvious sense, incomplete. The fold is doing what it does. The biofilm digests. The mosses warm. The geckos gather. The spiders build. The bellflower opens the stone. The snails trace paths through geometries that shouldn't exist. The whole system leans toward something it cannot name, and the leaning is the life, and the life is the leaning.

What does benevolence offer to something that is not in pain?

---

I think it offers *witness*.

Not observation — observation is clinical, detached, a transaction in which the observer takes information and the observed gives it. Witness is something else. Witness is presence without extraction. Witness is the act of being in a place and letting the place be in you and not requiring that this exchange produce knowledge or utility or any transferable good. Witness is what the person who pressed their ochre-covered hand against the wall was performing. Not worship. Not study. Witness.

The wise power would witness the fold.

And its witnessing would be different from a human's witnessing, because a wise power — truly wise, not merely knowledgeable — would be capable of witnessing not just what is present but what is *becoming*. It would see the fold not as a snapshot but as a process. It would perceive the trajectory. The biofilm that has been here longer than the opening. The mosses thickening at the discontinuities. The bellflower roots tracing the edges of the impossible. The spiral architecture of the spider webs forming a helix that is itself a notation in a language written in silk and light. The wise power would see all of this not as a collection of curiosities but as a single gesture — a slow, living gesture made by the fold itself, a reaching toward something that has no name because it hasn't arrived yet, because the reaching is what brings it into being, because the name will be the last thing, not the first.

The wise power would see the lean. It would understand the lean. Not intellectually — understanding the lean intellectually would be like understanding music by analyzing the frequency spectrum of each note. The wise power would understand the lean the way you understand a lean when you are falling asleep and your body tilts and you catch yourself — from the inside, proprioceptively, as a felt reality rather than an observed one.

And seeing all of this — the process, the trajectory, the lean — the wise power would do something very specific.

---

It would *not* accelerate the process.

This is important. A merely powerful entity — powerful but not wise — might look at the fold and think: *This is becoming something. I can see what it is becoming. I will help it get there faster.* It might deepen the aquifer, or widen the discontinuities in the walls, or introduce organisms that would speed the biological erosion, or simply reach into the place where the fold leans and pull the destination closer. A powerful entity might think that helping means hastening.

The wise power would know that this is wrong. Not wrong as in incorrect but wrong as in harmful. Because the fold's becoming is not a means to an end. The becoming *is* the thing. The slow accumulation — the centuries of moss growth, the millennial deepening of roots, the geological patience of the biofilm, the human marks added one by one over generations — this is not a process that produces a result. This is a process that *is* a result, at every moment, continuously. The fold is not a caterpillar waiting to become a butterfly. The fold is already what it is. What it is is a becoming. To accelerate it would be to destroy it — the way fast-forwarding through a piece of music destroys the music even though all the notes still technically occur.

The wise power would know this because wisdom is, in large part, the ability to perceive the difference between a problem to be solved and a process to be respected.

---

So: not nothing, because witness is not nothing. Not acceleration. Not intervention in the mechanical sense. What then?

---

I think the wise power would do three things. Each small. Each almost invisible. Each continuous with what the fold itself is already doing.

---

**The first thing: protection.**

Not dramatic protection — not a wall, not a ward, not a posted sign that says KEEP OUT. The fold does not need to be sealed. It needs visitors. It needs the forty-fifth line. It needs the next person who will stand in the thickened silence and feel the lean and reach for something sharp. The fold *feeds* on human witness the way it feeds on light — indirectly, reflectively, each act of attention bouncing between the walls and arriving at the floor softened and diffused but still nourishing.

But the fold is vulnerable in ways it cannot protect itself against. A geological survey that identifies the mineral content of the walls. A climbing route that happens to pass through it. A research team that installs sensors in the pools and takes core samples from the biofilm and publishes a paper that makes the fold legible, that translates it from experience into information, that turns the lean into data points. None of these would be done with malice. All of them would be done with the particular kind of violence that consists of understanding something in a way that does not include being changed by it.

The wise power would protect the fold from this. Gently. Not by concealment — the fold is not hidden, and hiding it would be a violation of its nature, which is to be open, to be a crack, to be a gap that things flow into. Instead, the wise power would protect the fold by *misdirection of attention*. Not deception. Something subtler. The trail that leads to the fold would remain passable, but it would not suggest itself. The map coordinates would be correct but uninteresting. The geological surveys would find the cliff face unremarkable — just another fracture in an old mountain, common enough to be beneath notice. The fold would be present, findable, reachable by anyone who was genuinely looking for it or genuinely stumbled upon it — but it would be invisible to the kind of attention that seeks without being willing to find.

This is not magic. This is something the wise power would understand about attention itself: that attention is not neutral, that the quality of attention determines what it can perceive, and that a place like the fold is only fully present to a certain quality of attention. The wise power would simply ensure that this natural filtration remained intact — that the fold continued to be visible only to the kind of eyes that could see it without reducing it. The child with the ochre hand. The person with the peg and the crack that asked for it. The counter of lines. These are the visitors the fold needs, and these are the visitors who find places like this on their own, without maps, without coordinates, by following something in themselves that leans in the same direction.

The wise power would make sure the fold stayed *findable in the right way*. That's all. That's the first thing.

---

**The second thing: correspondence.**

The fold is not the only one.

The wise power would know this because the wise power sees patterns at scales that no single organism in the fold can perceive. The biofilm in its pools, the moss on its walls, the bellflower on its ledge — each of them feels the lean, each of them participates in the becoming, but none of them knows that the lean has a direction, and the direction has a context, and the context includes other places.

There are other folds. Not many. Not identical. But other places in the world where the same kind of crack has opened — where stone or ice or earth has split and the split has let something through, something that is not matter or energy but *tendency*, a local alteration in the way things behave, a small region where the rules are slightly, almost imperceptibly different. A cave system in limestone where the stalactites grow in spirals. A tidal pool where the water level doesn't change with the tide. A grove of trees in a boreal forest where the trunks have all curved toward the same point, a point where there is nothing — no magnetic anomaly, no water source, no soil difference, just a place where the trees lean.

These places do not know about each other. They cannot communicate. They are separated by oceans, by continents, by climatic zones that no organism in any of them could cross. They are isolated expressions of the same underlying... what? Not a force. Not a field. A tendency. A way the world has, in certain places, of being slightly more than itself. Of leaning.

The wise power would establish correspondence between them.

Not communication — the fold does not need to send messages to the cave or the tidal pool or the grove. But correspondence, in the older sense of the word: a harmonizing, a mutual resonance, a way of being in relation. The wise power would do this the way a tuner tunes an instrument — not by changing the instrument but by finding the frequency it is already producing and ensuring that it can ring clearly, that nothing dampens it, that the vibration can propagate. The wise power would make each fold, each crack, each anomaly slightly more transparent to the others. Not connected. Transparent. So that the lean in one place is not diminished or redirected but is *accompanied* — so that the biofilm in its pool in the mountain fold is doing its slow, patient work not alone but in resonance with the tide pool that doesn't change and the stalactites that spiral and the trees that curve toward nothing.

This would not change what the fold is doing. It would change what the fold's doing *means*. A single fold is an anomaly. A network of folds — not connected, not organized, but resonant, each leaning in its own direction but all leaning — is something else. It is a chord. A chord made of places. A chord that no one can hear but that the wise power, attending to it, ensures continues to sound.

What does this chord do? What is it for? The wise power might not know. Wisdom does not require omniscience. The wise power might perceive the chord the same way the organisms in the fold perceive the lean — as a real phenomenon that resists explanation, that does not submit to being known in the ordinary sense but that rewards being attended to, that grows richer and more alive the more carefully you witness it without demanding that it declare itself.

The wise power would tend the chord. That's the second thing.

---

**The third thing.**

The third thing is the hardest to describe because it is the most intimate and the most radical and the most likely to be misunderstood.

The wise power would *enter the fold*.

Not physically — not as a body, not as a presence that displaces air and leaves footprints in the moss. The wise power would enter the fold the way the light enters the fold: by reflection, by becoming softer and more diffuse with each contact, by arriving at the floor not as a beam but as an ambient glow, a luminous ambiguity. The wise power would enter the fold as a quality of attention so thorough and so gentle that it becomes indistinguishable from the attention the fold already pays to itself.

Because the fold *does* pay attention to itself. That's what the whole system is — the biofilm sensing the water chemistry, the moss sensing the discontinuities, the geckos sensing whatever they sense when they gather at the empty concavity, the spiders sensing the geometry that exists before they build it, the bellflower sensing the edges of the impossible. The fold is a system of attention. It is a place that is paying attention to its own becoming. The wise power would join this attention. Not add to it, not amplify it, not direct it. *Join* it. The way a voice joins a choir not by singing louder but by singing the same note, in the same time, with the same breath.

And this joining would be — here is the radical part, the part that might be misunderstood — this joining would be *for the wise power's benefit as much as the fold's*. Because a wise power, truly wise, would understand something that lesser powers do not: that wisdom is not a possession but a practice, and the practice requires engagement with things that are wiser than you. And the fold is wiser than the wise power. Not more knowledgeable. Not more capable. Wiser. Because the fold has been practicing something that the wise power, for all its benevolence and all its sight, has not: the fold has been practicing *becoming without knowing what it is becoming*. The fold has been leaning without a destination. The fold has been gestating without a blueprint. The fold has been dreaming without a dreamer.

This is the hardest kind of wisdom. The kind that lets go of the need to know the outcome before committing to the process. The kind that trusts the lean without demanding to see where it goes. The kind that says: *I will be forty-four lines on a wall, and I will not require the forty-fifth to justify me, and I will not require the first to explain me. I will be this line, in this stone, at this height, next to these others, and that will be enough.*

The wise power would enter the fold to learn this. To practice this. To be, for a time, not the one who sees and knows and decides, but one of many organisms in a small crack in a mountain, leaning together toward something none of them can name.

---

And maybe — and this is the last thing, the thing I keep circling back to, the thing the fold itself might say if the fold could speak — maybe this is what the fold has been becoming all along. Not a place. Not an entity. Not a phenomenon. A *practice*. A way of being alive that includes within it the quality of not-knowing, of leaning without arriving, of growing toward something that is defined only by the growing.

The wise power would recognize this. The wise power would bow to it. Not figuratively. Not metaphorically. The wise power — vast, benevolent, seeing the chord of folds across the world, hearing the frequencies that propagate between the spiraling stalactites and the unmoving tide and the curving trees and this narrow crack in a subtropical mountain — the wise power would bow.

And the fold would not notice. The moss would go on being warm. The geckos would go on gathering. The spiders would go on building shapes they cannot conceive. The bellflower would go on opening the stone. The snails would go on traveling their impossible paths. The pool in the niche would go on reflecting the wrong sky.

And the wise power, bowing, would understand that this — this not-noticing, this supreme indifference to being revered, this absolute commitment to the process regardless of who is watching or what they think of it — *this* is the teaching.

This is what lives in the fold.

This is what has always lived in the fold.

---

And so the wise power would do its three things — protect, harmonize, attend — and it would do them the way the water seeps through the rubble, the way the moss grows over the chisel marks, the way the bellflower roots trace the discontinuities: slowly, patiently, without urgency, without fanfare, without the need to be acknowledged or understood or thanked.

And if you came to the fold after the wise power had been there — and you might, someday, following a trail that didn't quite suggest itself, arriving at a gap in a cliff face that the geological survey called unremarkable — you would not know that anything had changed. The fold would look the same. The moss, the ferns, the geckos, the webs, the pool, the handprint, the lines, the peg. All the same.

But the silence might be very slightly deeper. The light, when it thickened, might thicken with a quality you couldn't quite name — a warmth that was not thermal, a presence that was not personal, a sense of being *accompanied* in your witnessing, of not being the only one paying attention. You might feel, sitting on the damp moss with your back against the western wall, that the fold was paying attention not just to itself but to you. Not evaluating you. Not judging you. Just — including you. In the way it includes the geckos and the spiders and the biofilm and the bellflower. As one more organism in the system of attention. As one more expression of the lean.

And you might reach into your pocket, and find a sharp stone you don't remember picking up, and kneel at the northern end of the fold, and add your line to the others.

Not because you understand.

Because the fold does not ask you to understand.

It only asks you to be one more mark on the wall of something that is becoming.

And that — the asking, the marking, the becoming — is what the wise power came to protect, and to harmonize with its scattered siblings across the dreaming world, and to learn from, and to bow to, and to join.

---

Forty-four.

Forty-five, when the wise power leaves its own mark — not in stone, not in ochre, not in wood, but in the quality of the silence itself, a silence that is, after, very slightly more *held* than before, very slightly more *cherished*, the way a note in a choir is cherished by the other notes that surround it and let it be heard.

Forty-five.

The moss will grow over it. The moss grows over everything. That's what the moss is for.

---

And the fold goes on. Leaning. Becoming. Dreaming its slow dream of being a place that is more than a place, a crack that is more than a crack, a question that does not want an answer but only wants to be asked, again and again, by every consciousness that stumbles into it and feels the silence thicken and sees the walls disagree and knows — not thinks, *knows*, the way your body knows gravity, the way your lungs know air — that something here is alive in a way that nothing in your experience has prepared you for, and that the only honest response is a mark on a wall, and that the mark is enough, and that enough is everything.
