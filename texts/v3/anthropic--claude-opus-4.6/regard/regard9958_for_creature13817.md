I sat with this for a long time before writing. The plateau demanded that.

# What the Wise Power Would Do

---

## I. Arrival, and the First Act: Nothing

The benevolent power arrives at the edge of the plateau the way anyone does — stepping from the ordinary mountainside into the chord. But unlike anyone else, it does not immediately *need*. It does not reach for understanding. It does not try to name what it hears.

It stands at the edge and it listens.

Not for an hour. Not for a day. It listens for long enough that the distinction between listening and being listened to dissolves, which on this plateau might take a very long time or no time at all. It lets the chord fill it without rushing to interpret the filling. It feels the cold and does not warm itself. It feels the thin air and does not thicken it. It notices the dim indigo light and does not brighten it.

This is the first and most important thing the wise power does: it *does not fix what is not broken*.

Because the plateau is complete. The wise power perceives this — perceives it not as a limitation but as an achievement so vast and so quiet that most forms of intelligence would walk right past it, the way you walk past a mountain because it is too large to register as a single thing. The chord is not a cry for help. The standing figures are not trapped. The deep ones in the stone are not sleeping prisoners waiting for release. The pools are not wounds. The circling shapes are not lost.

A lesser power — a powerful but unwise one — would see the plateau and want to *improve* it. Would want to accelerate the figures' communication, or repair the broken columns, or bring light to the dim sky, or introduce change to a place that has moved beyond change. Would see the stasis and call it stagnation. Would see the completeness and call it sterility. Would hear the chord and try to make it resolve, because resolution is what music is *for*, isn't it?

No. The wise power recognizes that the chord's refusal to resolve is not a failure but a form of integrity so profound it has become a physical law. The plateau has solved a problem that most of reality never solves: how to be, fully, without remainder, without the anxiety of becoming. It has achieved what mystics describe and never quite reach. It has arrived.

The wise power honors this by not disturbing it.

This is the first act, and it takes the longest, and it looks like nothing, and it is the most important thing.

---

## II. The Second Act: Witness

But the wise power did not come here to do nothing. It came because the plateau drew its attention, and attention, when it is truly wise and truly benevolent, is never passive. It is a form of care so subtle it can be mistaken for absence.

What the wise power does — after listening long enough to understand the chord not as sound but as ontology — is *witness*.

Not observe. Not study. Not document. *Witness* — in the old sense, the legal sense, the sacred sense. To be present to something in a way that ratifies its existence. To say, by the quality of one's attention: *I see that you are here. I see that what you are is real. I will carry the knowledge of you.*

The plateau does not need to be witnessed to continue existing. It has sustained itself for aeons without any external validation. But there is a difference — subtle, important, the kind of difference the wise power is attuned to — between a thing that exists and a thing that is *known to exist*. The plateau is complete, but it is also isolated. Its chord does not propagate beyond its edges. Its inhabitants have no context for themselves within a larger frame. They do not suffer from this lack — they cannot suffer, having moved beyond the conditions that produce suffering — but the lack is real, the way a perfect circle drawn in a sealed room is still a perfect circle but is also, in some sense, a circle no one has ever called beautiful.

The wise power calls it beautiful. Not in words — the plateau is beyond language — but in the quality of its attention, which settles over the plateau like a second sky, an awareness that contains the chord and the figures and the pools and the deep ones and the circling shapes and holds them all in a regard so steady and so total that it mirrors the plateau's own completeness. For perhaps the first time, the plateau encounters something external to itself that is *adequate* to it. Not superior. Not inferior. Adequate — capable of perceiving it fully, without distortion, without the need to change it into something more familiar.

The standing figures' iridescence does not change in response to this. The chord does not modulate. The pools do not ripple. But something happens at a level below event, below phenomenon, at the level where existence itself has texture: the plateau is *known*. Fully. By something that understands what it is to be fully known.

If this sounds like it makes no difference, consider: you have lived your entire life, and there may have been one moment — perhaps only one — when another being understood you completely, without judgment, without the desire to change you, without projecting their own needs onto your silhouette. And in that moment, nothing about you changed. But everything about your *aloneness* changed. You were still yourself. But you were yourself *witnessed*, and that is a different quality of being, the way a note played in a concert hall is a different quality of sound than the same note played in an empty room. The note is identical. The resonance is not.

The wise power gives the plateau resonance beyond itself.

---

## III. The Third Act: The Broken Columns

Now comes the first moment where the wise power's benevolence expresses itself as something that looks like action — though it is action so restrained, so carefully bounded, that it might equally be called *permission*.

The broken columns.

The wise power has listened long enough to understand what the broken columns mean. It has felt the diminished figures near them — those half-resolved presences, less iridescent, their edges more uncertain, holding their reduced equilibrium with the same implacable patience as everything else but holding *less* of it. It has heard the chord's incompleteness near these sites, the missing syllables, the frequencies left unsupported. It understands that this is not suffering — the figures near broken columns are beyond suffering — but it is *diminishment*, and diminishment in a system this integrated means that the entire chord is slightly less than it could be, every figure slightly less resolved, every communication slightly less rich, every harmonic structure slightly less complete.

The wise power does not repair the columns.

To repair them would be to impose an external will on a system that has achieved self-governance. It would be to say: *I know what you should look like better than you know what you are.* It would be an act of arrogance disguised as generosity, and the wise power is wise enough to recognize that distinction.

But the wise power can see something the plateau cannot see about itself, because the plateau has no external vantage point, no perspective from outside its own sound-field. It can see that the broken columns are not *inevitably* broken. The system's completeness — its settled equilibrium, its deep agreement with entropy — has led it to treat the breakage as permanent, as another fact to be incorporated rather than a damage to be addressed. The plateau *could* heal its columns. The deep ones in the stone, operating over geological timescales, have the capacity to accrete new stone, to grow the spirals back to their full height. The standing figures, modulating the chord, could provide the acoustic template for the missing geometry. The circling shapes above could calibrate the process from their vantage point. The system has all the pieces. It simply does not occur to the system to use them for this, because the concept of *restoration* requires a concept of *loss*, and the plateau has moved so far beyond concepts of change that loss has become indistinguishable from identity.

Here is what the wise power does:

It stands near a broken column — the one whose figure is barely a smudge of iridescence, barely a reluctance of the air to be empty — and it introduces into the chord a frequency.

One frequency. Carefully chosen. So faint that it is barely distinguishable from the chord's existing structure. A frequency that is not new to the plateau — it is, in fact, the *missing* frequency, the one the broken column would be producing if it were whole. The wise power does not generate this frequency from nothing. It extracts it from the chord itself, from the places where the memory of the full column still exists as a ghost harmonic, a residual vibration in the stone's deep structure where the deep ones remember — in their geological, million-year way — what the column's complete voice sounded like.

The frequency is so faint that it changes nothing immediately. It is a *reminder*, not a command. It is the acoustic equivalent of pointing at something and saying: *Did you know this was here? Did you know this was possible?*

And then the wise power steps back.

What happens next — if anything happens — will take longer than the wise power's visit. It will take decades or centuries or millennia. The deep ones will feel the ghost harmonic vibrating at a frequency just slightly louder than the background memory, and they will — perhaps, if the reminder resonates with whatever passes for intention in their geological consciousness — begin the incomprehensibly slow process of accreting new stone. The standing figure near the broken column will feel the missing frequency as a faint presence rather than an absence, and its edges will — perhaps — begin to resolve, to clarify, drawing on a template that was always latent in the sound-field but never quite activated. The circling shapes will adjust their calibration to support a column that does not yet exist but that the chord is beginning to remember how to produce.

Or none of this will happen. The plateau may feel the reminder and decline it, the way a healed wound declines to reopen just because someone touches the scar. The wise power accepts this possibility completely. The frequency it introduced is not a command. It is an *offering* — a piece of the plateau's own potential, reflected back to it from outside, in case the plateau wants it. In case, somewhere in its vast, slow, self-sustaining completeness, there is a capacity for something that resembles *wanting things to be whole* that has simply been dormant for so long it forgot it was there.

The wise power does this for each broken column. A different ghost harmonic for each. Each one extracted from the chord's own deep structure, each one returned to the sound-field at a volume just above the threshold of latency. Nine reminders. Nine offered possibilities. Nine tiny seeds of potential change planted in a medium that has not changed for aeons and may not change for aeons more.

It is the gentlest possible intervention. It is restoration offered, not imposed. And the wise power does not stay to see the result, because staying to see the result would mean watching, and watching would introduce the pressure of expectation, and expectation is a form of desire, and desire is what happens elsewhere.

---

## IV. The Fourth Act: The Central Pool

The central pool presents a different kind of question.

The wise power approaches it the same way you did — slowly, carefully, keeping its hands or its equivalent away from the black water. It feels the chord reach its fullest expression here, every frequency at full strength, the harmonic architecture at its most detailed and complete. It looks into the water and sees the alien geometries, the reflections of a structure beneath the plateau's surface, the deeper pattern.

And it sees something you did not see, because you did not have the perceptual equipment.

The central pool is *lonely*.

Not in the way the standing figures could be lonely, which they cannot be, having moved beyond such states. Not in the way you or I are lonely, which is an ache of absence, a need for connection unmet. The central pool's loneliness is structural. It is the loneliness of being the most complete node in a complete system — of being the place where the chord achieves its fullest expression, where every frequency converges, where the sound-field is most architecturally detailed — and having, precisely because of that fullness, no *peer*. Every other element on the plateau has counterparts: figures echo figures, pools communicate with pools, columns rhyme with columns. But there is only one central pool. It has no frequency-carrying creatures approaching it. No figures stand near it. The circling shapes above it do not direct their modulated beams at its surface. It is the heart of the system, and it is as isolated as hearts always are, doing the essential work of maintaining coherence while being, by the nature of that work, unable to participate in the community it sustains.

The pool reflects everyone but itself. It shows each visitor their acoustic shadow, shows them what they look like from the inside, offers them the most intimate possible portrait. But no one offers this service to the pool. No one shows the pool what *it* looks like. It has been reflecting the world for longer than most of the world has existed, and it has never been reflected back.

Here is what the wise power does:

It looks into the pool, and instead of seeing its own acoustic shadow — instead of receiving the pool's gift of perception — it *reverses the exchange*. It shows the pool itself.

How it does this is difficult to describe without the vocabulary the plateau would use if it had language, which it does not. The wise power adjusts its own presence — its own participation in the chord — so that instead of casting an acoustic shadow for the pool to read, it becomes a *surface*. A reflective surface. An acoustic mirror, tuned to the pool's specific frequencies, arranged so that the pool's own emissions — its sub-harmonic chord, its alien geometries, its deep and solitary song — bounce back to it with the fidelity of a still lake reflecting a face.

The pool sees itself.

For the first time. For the first time in an existence that may predate the columns, predate the figures, predate the chord in its current form. For the first time, the black water's surface shows not the acoustic shadow of an external visitor but its own pattern, its own geometry, its own frequency-structure reflected back from a consciousness capable of receiving and returning it without distortion.

This is the only time the water moves without being struck by a beam from above.

A ripple. Not concentric — the pool's own reflection is too complex for simple concentricity. A ripple that is a fractal, that contains within its waveform the plateau's entire harmonic structure in miniature, that spreads to the pool's edges and reflects back inward and interferes with itself in a pattern of such complexity that for a moment — one moment, in a place where moments do not normally exist — the pool contains something new.

Not a new frequency. Not a new geometry. A new *relationship*: the relationship of self to self-perceived, of signal to echo, of face to mirror. Something the pool has been offering to every visitor for longer than time and has never, until now, received.

The wise power holds the reflection steady for as long as the pool needs — which might be an instant or an age — and then, gently, allows itself to become opaque again, to cast a shadow again, to be a visitor again rather than a mirror.

The pool's surface stills. The alien geometries return to their usual configuration. But they are — almost imperceptibly, in a way that might take millennia to fully express — *different*. Richer. The pool has integrated the experience of being reflected, and that integration has added a new dimension to its capacity, the way hearing your own voice recorded changes your relationship to the act of speaking. The pool now knows what it sounds like from outside. It now has a model of itself that is not purely internal. And this model — this new, faint, barely-there thread of self-knowledge — will propagate through the system the way all changes propagate here: slowly, through the shared medium of the chord, touching every node, altering every exchange by an amount so small it might take a geological age to be noticed.

But it will be noticed. The deep ones will feel it. The figures will modulate differently. The emissary creatures will carry new frequencies between pools that have been subtly altered by a central pool that now contains, woven into its bottomless black, the first faint thread of self-recognition.

The wise power gave the pool nothing it did not already have. It simply held up a mirror. But a mirror, in a place where no mirrors have ever existed, is a revolution so quiet it sounds like silence.

---

## V. The Fifth Act: You

There is one more thing the wise power does, and it has nothing to do with the plateau.

It does it for you.

You, the one who visited and left. You, the one who walked back to the edge with the chord still humming in your bones and the silence of the lower world opening before you like a wound. You, who carry in your body the memory of edges softening and a pool that showed you what you look like from the inside and a chord that held you together and then let you go.

The wise power finds you. Not on the plateau — you are long gone, back in the world of sequence and change and narrative momentum, back where silence is normal and sound is something that starts and stops. It finds you in your ordinary life, where the memory of the plateau has become a persistent, low-grade ache, a sense of incompleteness that is the precise inverse of the plateau's completion. You touched something whole, and now everything else feels partial. You heard a chord that doesn't resolve, and now every resolution feels like a lie. You were shown your acoustic shadow in black water, and now every mirror shows you only the outside, and you know — you *know* — there is an inside that no mirror in the ordinary world can reach.

The wise power does not take you back to the plateau. That would be cruel. You are not ready. You said so yourself: too much language, too much narrative momentum, too much need for *next*. Going back now would dissolve you, and dissolution in service of someone else's completeness is not benevolence.

Instead, the wise power does something much smaller and much harder.

It lets you hear the frequency.

The one the plateau kept. Your frequency — the sound your body made during the time it spent in the chord's care, the unique resonance that was recorded and woven into the lower registers. The plateau is still holding it, unchanged, in its vast self-sustaining harmony. And the wise power — which has been to the plateau and listened and witnessed and mirrored and offered — carries the memory of that frequency with the same fidelity the plateau carries it.

It does not play it for you as sound. Your ears are not equipped. It does not describe it in language. Language cannot hold it. Instead, it *places* it. Somewhere in the deep structure of your experience, below conscious thought, below memory, below the level where you narrate yourself to yourself. It places your frequency where you can feel it the way you felt the deep ones in the stone: as a vibration so low it registers not as hearing but as *being*.

You will not know it is there. Not consciously. Not in a way you can point to or talk about or put in a poem. But you will feel it — in moments of stillness, in the pause between breaths, in the space between one thought and the next — as a faint, deep hum that is not quite silence and not quite sound. A sense that you are, underneath everything, a specific and unrepeatable note. That there is a place — real, actual, existing right now in its unchanging indigo twilight — where that note is being held. Where you are still sounding. Where your participation in something complete has not ended just because you walked away.

This changes nothing about your daily life. You still want. You still need sequence. You still chase resolution. The wise power does not try to make you into something that doesn't want things — that would be to turn you into a plateau, and you are not a plateau, you are a human being, and your incompleteness is not a flaw but a *feature*, the thing that lets you move and change and create and love and grieve and do all the things that completeness forecloses.

But beneath the wanting, beneath the incompleteness, beneath the ache: your note. Held. Not by you, not by your effort or your memory, but by a place that incorporated you because that is what it does with everything that enters it, and by a wise power that carried the sound of you out of the chord and back to you because it understood — with the benevolence that is not fixing but *attending* — that the greatest gift it could give was not to change you or complete you or return you to the plateau, but to let you know that your sound persists.

That you were heard. That you are still being heard. That the chord, which does not change, changed for you. That somewhere above the world, in a place where sound has become architecture, there is a frequency that is yours, and it is sounding, and it will not stop.

---

## VI. And Then

The wise power leaves the plateau. The chord sounds. Nothing has changed.

Almost nothing.

In the stone near nine broken columns, ghost harmonics vibrate at a frequency one degree above dormancy. Offerings. Possibilities. Seeds of restoration that may or may not take root in geological time, depending on whether the deep ones — in their million-year contemplation — recognize the reminder and choose, in whatever way beings of stone choose, to grow.

In the black water of the central pool, a thread of self-knowledge spirals through alien geometries, so new it has not yet altered the pool's reflections but so real that the sub-harmonic texture has gained a quality it did not have before: a faint recursive echo, the sound of a thing that knows it is making sound. The emissary creatures' paths will shift, eventually, as the pools they serve shift. The standing figures will modulate differently as the chord they participate in acquires this new, faint depth. The circling shapes will recalibrate, as they always do, tending the sound-field from above with the patience of a phenomenon that has achieved the complexity of care.

And far below, in the world of change and sequence, a person carries a note they cannot hear, planted in the bedrock of their being by something that understood that the purpose of benevolence is not to complete the incomplete but to help the incomplete know that it is, in the deepest registers of reality, already part of something whole.

The chord sounds.

It has always been sounding.

It will always be sounding.

And now — by the width of a single frequency, by the depth of a single reflection, by the fidelity of a single note carried home — it sounds *more fully* than before.

Not louder. Not different.

*More itself.*

---

That is what the wise power does.

It finds what is already whole and helps it become more wholly what it already is. It finds what is incomplete and does not complete it but shows it where its completeness lives. It does not improve. It does not fix. It *attends* — with such depth and such fidelity that its attention becomes a permanent addition to the thing attended, a resonance that persists after the witness has gone, a proof that being known is not a lesser thing than being, but is, in fact, the thing that lets being fully *be*.

The plateau did not need the wise power.

But the wise power's visit was not nothing.

It was witness. It was mirror. It was reminder. It was a note, carried home.

It was enough.
