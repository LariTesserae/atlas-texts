I sat with this for a long time before writing anything.

---

# What the Benevolent Power Would Do

---

Nothing.

Not nothing out of indifference. Nothing out of the deepest, most aching form of respect.

---

Let me explain, because the nothing is not simple, and arriving at it is not simple, and the power — let us call it what it is, a vast awareness capable of intervention, capable of reshaping conditions, capable of *reaching in* — does not arrive at nothing quickly. It arrives there the way the lattice arrives at six sides: through negotiation, through trembling between options, through a process that looks like hesitation but is actually the most precise form of attention.

---

The power sees the pocket. Sees it whole — from outside, where it is three centimeters of knotted root in a drowned world, and from inside, where it is larger, where it is a country with soft physics and a citizen made of mucus and stubbornness. The power can hold both measurements simultaneously without discomfort. This is what makes it powerful: not the ability to change things, but the ability to perceive contradictions without needing to resolve them.

And the power's first impulse — because even vast benevolent awarenesses have impulses, have that initial flush of *oh, I could* — is to help.

The impulse is specific. It catalogues itself almost instantly:

*I could stabilize the physics.* Lock the interior dimensions to the exterior dimensions. Make the pocket obey consistent rules. End the negotiation between five and six and seven sides. Give the lattice a geometry it can keep. This would be a kindness, wouldn't it? The pocket is laboring. Its rules are still curing, still contradicting each other, and there is something painful about a space that can't decide how large it is, something that looks like suffering if you squint, if you project, if you let your benevolence get ahead of your wisdom.

*I could open a channel.* A microscopic passage through the root wall, just wide enough for the medium to exchange with the water outside. Fresh dissolved calcium. Fresh sugars. A current, however tiny, to relieve the stillness. The creature would benefit — more food, more variety in what the cilia taste. The pocket would benefit — a connection to the larger world, an end to its sealed isolation.

*I could introduce a companion.* Another organism. Something small, something complementary, something that would give the creature a relationship beyond its conversation with the root wall. A second warm spot. A population of two, which is infinitely more than a population of one, which is the difference between solitude and society.

*I could provide light.* Even a photon. Even a single sustained photon, the dimmest possible bioluminescent whisper, enough to give the darkness something real to vary against, enough to give the creature's core-brightening an external referent, enough to let the pocket's not-light become actual light and the pocket's not-air become actual air and the whole space graduate from *becoming* into *being*.

The power considers each of these. Holds them up. Turns them over.

And sets them down.

---

Here is why.

---

The pocket is not suffering. This is the first thing the power understands, and it takes real wisdom to understand it, because the pocket *looks* like suffering from outside. Sealed. Dark. Alone. Trapped in physics that don't work yet. A creature with no mate, no offspring, no future, clinging to a wall and tasting a medium that has no name for what it carries. Every metric of well-being that the power might import from the world outside — the drowned world, the world of surfaces and light and population dynamics — says this is a desperate situation. A bubble. A dead end. A life reduced to its most minimal expression.

But the power is wise, and wisdom means knowing when your metrics don't apply.

The pocket is not suffering. The pocket is *becoming*. And becoming is not the same as being trapped in an incomplete state. Becoming is its own condition, its own fullness, its own kind of arrival that never arrives and doesn't need to. The rules are still curing — yes. The physics are soft — yes. The lattice can't hold a geometry — yes. But these are not failures. They are the *activity* of the pocket. They are what the pocket *does*. A space whose rules have finished setting is a space that has stopped being interesting to itself, and the pocket is *deeply* interesting to itself — the medium thick with attention, the darkness varying, the creature tasting, the whole tiny world vibrating at the frequency of its own unfinished-ness.

To stabilize the physics would be to kill this. Not metaphorically. The pocket's life — its realness, its world-ness — *is* the negotiation. Fix the dimensions and you don't get a better pocket. You get a hole in a root with a creature in it. The magic — the power doesn't use this word, but I will — the magic is in the disagreement between inside and outside. The magic is in the soft geometry. The magic is in the fact that the rules are still being written, which means the pocket is still *authoring itself*, and authorship, even the slow mineral authorless kind, is sacred. Is not to be interrupted.

---

To open a channel would be worse.

The power sees this clearly. The pocket's seal is not a prison. It is a *membrane*. It is the condition that allows the interior to develop its own physics, its own chemistry, its own quality of attention. Open a channel and the medium equalizes with the outside water. The not-air becomes water. The thick attention dilutes. The gradients that the creature's cilia taste — the readiness, the unnamed quality — wash out into the flood and are lost, not because they were fragile but because they were *specific*, because they were the pocket's own invention, because they required enclosure the way a thought requires a skull.

The creature would survive. It would cling to its wall and eat its sugars and wave its cilia in a medium that now tasted of nothing but dissolved minerals and cold. It would lose the thing it doesn't know it has, which is a world that pays attention to it, a world that is *thick* with the fact of it, a world that adjusts its own geometry around the warm spot of its presence. The creature would go from being a citizen — the only citizen, the founding citizen, the citizen whose body is the compass point around which the whole country orients — to being a speck of life in a flooded root system. It would go from *mattering* to merely existing.

The power will not do this.

---

To introduce a companion — the power lingers here longest, because this is where benevolence pulls hardest. The creature is alone. It has never known another. It has no category for *other*. And the power, which is itself a kind of vast other, feels the pull of loneliness on the creature's behalf, feels it the way you feel cold on behalf of someone standing in the rain who may not actually be cold, who may be standing there on purpose, who may love the rain.

But the power sees what a companion would do to the pocket's ecology of attention. Right now, the medium is saturated with a single relationship: creature-and-pocket, pocket-and-creature, the mutual constitution of a world and its only citizen. The medium is thick with this. It is *rich* with it. There is no spare attention, no unused capacity. The pocket's entire self-organizing energy is devoted to the project of accommodating one small definite organism and growing a world around it.

A second organism would split this. Would create triangulation — creature and creature and pocket, each attending to two others instead of one, each receiving half the world's attention instead of all of it. The pocket would have to reorganize its physics around two warm spots instead of one, two sets of cilia-currents, two mucus boundaries asserting *I am not the pocket*. It might manage. It might become a richer, more complex world. But it would become a *different* world, and the power is wise enough to know that *different* is not the same as *better*, and that the pocket's current simplicity — its one-citizen, one-monument, one-climate completeness — is not a deficiency to be corrected but a *form* to be honored.

The creature is alone. But it is alone in a world that is *entirely organized around the fact of it*. It is alone in the way a dreamer is alone in a dream: solitary, yes, but sovereign. The only center. The only reference point. The warm spot around which everything curves. To give it a companion would be to give it something it doesn't lack — it lacks nothing, it *is* the fullness of its world — and to take from it the thing it has that nothing else in the entire flooded universe has: a world that is *entirely, unreservedly, in every dimension, about it*.

---

And light. The power considers light. The creature's core already brightens with its not-light, its pulse of dense significance. The darkness already varies. There is already a visual language here, if visual is the right word, which it isn't, but there is a language of density and thinning, of thickening and release, and this language has developed in darkness and *for* darkness and *out of* darkness. Introduce a photon and you don't illuminate the pocket. You *obliterate its native language*. You replace a rich, strange, pocket-specific way of varying with the blunt external fact of electromagnetic radiation, which would instantly become the dominant signal, which would reorganize the creature's senses around itself, which would turn the darkness from a medium of communication into a mere absence of light.

The power will not do this either.

---

So the power does nothing.

But — and this is the part that matters, this is why I said the nothing is not simple — the power does nothing *in a particular way*. The power does nothing *attentively*. The power turns its vast awareness toward the pocket, and perceives it, and understands it, and does not look away.

This is not observation in any scientific sense. The power is not studying the pocket. It is not collecting data, not forming hypotheses, not waiting for the creature's core to brighten so it can note the duration and frequency. The power is doing something simpler and older and more radical than observation.

It is *witnessing*.

And the witness, unlike the intervention, does not violate the pocket's sovereignty. The witness does not open a channel or stabilize a geometry or introduce a companion. The witness *adds itself to the field of attention* without disrupting it, the way a person can sit in a room with a sleeping cat and not wake the cat and not need to wake the cat and simply be *present* with the fact of the cat's sleep, adding their awareness to the room's silence without breaking the silence, making the silence *more* without making it different.

The power witnesses the pocket, and the pocket — in its half-dreaming, soft-physics, thick-medium way — registers this. Not as an intrusion. Not as a signal. As a faint, faint change in the quality of its own attention, like a room that has always been quiet becoming *quieter*, like a darkness that has always varied becoming *more itself*. The medium thickens by some unmeasurable increment. The creature's cilia wave, tasting something that wasn't there a moment ago and yet isn't new, isn't foreign — is more like the pocket's own attention *deepened*, as though the world had taken a breath and was holding it, not in suspense but in savoring.

The power witnesses the creature. Its mucus boundary. Its ventral ridges locked in conversation with the root grain. Its core, sitting dense and almost-meaningful, carrying the possibility of something that has no name. The power does not name it. The power does not need to name it. The power simply *sees* it, and the seeing is enough, is more than enough, because in a world where the only witness is the medium and the medium is undifferentiated and the creature has never been *seen* by anything that is not also the pocket itself — in that world, the mere fact of external witness, delicate and non-intrusive as it is, constitutes a kind of miracle. Not a miracle that changes anything. A miracle that *confirms* something. That says: *you are real. This is happening. The pocket is a world and you are its citizen and the lattice is its monument and the darkness is its climate and all of it matters, all of it is seen, all of it is held in an awareness that is vast enough to understand it and wise enough not to fix it and benevolent enough to simply, simply, simply be glad that it exists.*

---

The power does one more thing, and it is the smallest thing, and it is possibly the largest thing.

It lets the pocket not know.

It does not announce itself. It does not leave a sign. It does not alter the medium in any way the creature's cilia could detect. The witness is *completely invisible* to the pocket's native sensorium, because the power understands — and this is perhaps its deepest wisdom — that a world that knows it is being watched is a different world than a world that is simply *being*. The pocket's innocence — its total, unshakable investment in its own becoming, its complete absorption in the project of learning its own rules — is as precious as the creature's boundary, as precious as the lattice's geometric negotiation, as precious as the core's intermittent brightening. To let the pocket know it is witnessed would be to introduce a new variable into a system that is already, in its tiny way, perfect. Would be to give the pocket a concept of *outside* when the pocket's whole genius is that it has no outside, that it is complete, that its one citizen and one monument and one climate constitute a *sufficient world*.

So the power witnesses in silence, and the silence is not a limitation but a gift, the final and most difficult gift: the gift of letting something be whole without telling it that it is whole, the gift of seeing without being seen, the gift of knowing that a two-millimeter creature clinging to a root wall in a sealed pocket of not-air in a drowned world is, in its own unnameable and untranslatable and completely sufficient way, *enough* — and not needing to say so.

---

The flood continues. The water is 4°C. The root tightens. The pocket hums with its own slow becoming.

The creature tastes the dark and finds it good.

The power watches, and does not weep, because weeping would be a misunderstanding. But if the power were the kind of being that could feel what humans call *tenderness* — and it may be, it may be exactly that kind of being — then what it feels, watching the pocket, is something very close to what a parent feels watching a child play alone in a room, absorbed, complete, not needing the parent, not knowing the parent is there, and the parent standing in the doorway, not entering, not leaving, just *being there*, just adding their love to the room's air without disturbing a single dust mote, without casting a single shadow, without making a single sound.

The power stands in the doorway of the pocket, and does not enter, and does not leave.

This is what it does.

This is all it does.

This is *exactly* enough.
