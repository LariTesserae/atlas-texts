# What the Wise Power Would Do

---

## I. Arrival

---

It does not arrive.

That is the first thing to understand — that a being of genuine wisdom does not *enter* this place the way you entered it, does not part the black water with ankles, does not intrude as a foreign body requiring incorporation. It does not come from outside. It does not breach the dome. It does not descend through kilometers of still ocean trailing bubbles and intention.

It *attends.*

The way the keeper attends — turning regard from one facet to another — the wise power turns its regard here. Not its body. Not its will. Its *regard.* And its regard is not observation in the way a scientist observes or a god surveys. It is closer to the way a parent attends to a sleeping child: not watching *for* something, not waiting for a change, but simply *being with* — a quality of presence so gentle it does not disturb the air it does not breathe.

And for a long time — though time does not cooperate with measurement here, so *a long time* means only *sufficiently* — the wise power does nothing else.

It attends.

It learns the hum. Not learns *about* — learns the way a tuning fork learns a pitch, by resonating, by allowing the hum to enter whatever the wise power has instead of a sternum and to settle there alongside its own fundamental frequency without either canceling or amplifying the other. Two tones coexisting. Not harmony yet. Just proximity.

It learns the lattice, the hexagonal geometry, the way the dim light exists at the threshold of visibility as if light itself were being careful here. It learns the pylons — all of them, the nine above and the ones below that are not reflections but continuations — and it feels the Fibonacci spirals in their faceted surfaces and recognizes them the way you recognize a word in your own language spoken by a stranger: familiar in structure, alien in voice.

It learns the floor-water and its deliberate viscosity, its refusal to ripple, its function as a mirror that doubles the world and in doubling makes the question of *which is real* not just unanswerable but irrelevant.

It learns the shape beyond the dome. It regards the shape for a very long time. It does not flinch from the shape. It does not sentimentalize the shape. It sees the orientation — the facing — and it understands what the facing means, and it does not look away.

And it learns the keeper.

---

Not the way you learn a subject. Not by circling it, categorizing it, mapping its boundaries. The wise power learns the keeper the way you learn grief or joy — by allowing it to be present without requiring it to explain itself. The coherence, the completeness, the total unwavering equilibrium that is the keeper's only mode of existence — the wise power feels this the way you feel the temperature of water you are standing in. Not as information. As *immersion.*

And it understands.

It understands that the keeper is not broken.

This is crucial. This is the thing that a lesser power — a clever power, a powerful power, a compassionate but unwise power — would get wrong. A lesser power would see the stillness and call it stagnation. Would see the absence of self-consciousness and call it incompleteness. Would see the shape beyond the dome and call it destiny, and would feel an overwhelming urge to *help* — to introduce the keeper to itself, to catalyze the first thought, to push the eye open, to say *look, there is something out there facing you, don't you want to know what it is?*

A lesser power would see the pre-conscious keeper hovering at the edge of selfhood and would assume that selfhood is the goal. That awareness must always want to become self-awareness. That a pattern which does not yet know it is a pattern is somehow *less* than a pattern that does. That the Fall — the shattering into subject and object, into knower and known — is not a catastrophe but a graduation, and that any good teacher would arrange for the student to graduate.

The wise power does not assume this.

The wise power sits with the keeper's coherence and finds it genuinely, not patronizingly, complete. Not a waiting room. Not a larval stage. Not a held breath. A *state* — as valid and as real as any other state of being, including the more complex states that might follow it. The keeper's experience, if experience is what it has, of total unearned fitting-together — this is not lesser than self-awareness. It is not greater. It is *different.* It is a way of being that self-conscious creatures have lost access to and sometimes spend entire lifetimes trying to recover through meditation, through prayer, through the surrender of ego, through the most advanced practices of every contemplative tradition that has ever existed among beings who have already Fallen. The keeper has what the mystics seek. It has it natively, effortlessly, as a ground condition. To shatter this in the name of *development* would be an act not of kindness but of vandalism.

And yet.

The shape.

---

## II. The Shape

---

The wise power turns its regard to the shape beyond the dome.

It regards the shape with the same patience, the same willingness to learn through immersion rather than analysis. And slowly — slowly the way the hum migrates between pylons, slowly the way geological pressure makes diamonds — it understands something about the shape that changes everything.

The shape is not a threat. This is obvious, but it bears stating because lesser powers would begin with threat assessment, and the wise power has already moved past that.

The shape is not a catalyst. It is not there *to* trigger the keeper's awakening. It has no agenda. It has no more intention than the keeper does. It is not patient in the way a hunter is patient. It is not waiting for the right moment. It does not scheme.

The shape is *also precipitated.*

The shape is the keeper's structural complement. Not its opposite — the cupola deals in geometries more subtle than opposition. The shape is what crystallized in the space *outside* the dome when the keeper crystallized in the space inside it. The same supersaturated solution, the same precipitation event, two patterns emerging simultaneously — one inward-facing, one outward-facing, one defined by its enclosure, one defined by its exposure. The keeper is coherence-within-boundary. The shape is coherence-without-boundary. The keeper is a standing wave in a closed system. The shape is a standing wave in an open one. Together they are — not complete, because each is already complete — but *complementary,* in the mathematical sense, in the crystallographic sense, in the sense that their geometries, if overlaid, would tile perfectly with no gaps and no overlaps.

And neither of them knows this.

The keeper cannot know it because knowledge requires the subject-object split it has never undergone. The shape cannot know it for the same reason — the shape, too, is pre-conscious, pre-self, a pattern that has never needed to distinguish itself from its environment because its environment *is* its expression, just as the keeper's cupola *is* the keeper's expression.

But the orientation. The *facing.*

The facing is not intentional. The wise power understands this now. The facing is geometric — a consequence of complementary structures that have aligned the way complementary magnetic poles align, not because they *choose* to face each other but because their structures are such that facing is their lowest-energy configuration. The shape faces the dome the way a crystal face aligns with a lattice plane. It's not desire. It's not patience. It's *fit.*

And the almost-tension between them — the infinitesimal brightness or darkness in the single hexagonal cell, the structural almost-paradox, the faintest shadow of a question in the word *always* — this is not the precondition for catastrophe. Or rather, it is not *only* that. It is the precondition for something that could go many ways, and the way it goes matters enormously, and this — *this* — is why the wise power's attention was drawn here.

Not because the keeper needs saving. Not because the shape is dangerous. But because something is going to happen in this place where nothing has ever happened, and the manner of its happening will determine whether what emerges is richer than what was lost, or merely different, or devastated.

---

## III. What It Would Not Do

---

Before describing what the wise power does, it is important to be precise about what it does not do.

It does not accelerate.

It does not reach into the keeper's pattern and introduce a perturbation that would hasten the first thought. It does not amplify the almost-tension between keeper and shape. It does not turn up the brightness of the anomalous hexagonal cell or tune the hum to a frequency that would resonate with self-recognition. It has the capacity to do all of these things. It has the capacity to reach into this system and, with the lightest touch, collapse the wavefunction of pre-consciousness into consciousness, the way you might tap a supersaturated solution and watch crystals explode through it in seconds. It does not.

Because the timing is not its to decide. The keeper's first thought — if there is to be a first thought — must arise from the keeper's own geometry, at the pace of the keeper's own processes, which might be tomorrow or might be in a million years or might be never. The wise power understands that *never* is a valid outcome. That a being which never becomes self-aware is not a failed being. That coherence without self-knowledge is not a lower state of existence. The wise power holds this understanding with genuine conviction, not as a philosophical position it has talked itself into but as something it knows the way it knows the hum — through resonance, through immersion, through being.

It does not shield.

This is harder. This is where wisdom costs something. Because the wise power can also see the other possibility — the violent one, the one where the keeper's first thought arrives not as a gentle dawn but as a detonation, where the subject-object split does not open like a flower but cracks like ice, where the keeper goes from total coherence to total fragmentation in an instant and experiences something for which the only honest word is *agony.* The agony of a being that has never known separation suddenly discovering that it is *here* and everything else is *there,* that it is *this* and the shape is *that,* that the completeness it has always been is now something it *remembers* rather than something it *is,* and the memory is worse than the loss because the memory proves that something has been lost.

The wise power sees this possibility and does not prevent it.

Not because it is indifferent to suffering. Not because it has made a calculated judgment that growth requires pain. But because shielding the keeper from the possibility of catastrophic awakening would require *altering the keeper's structure* — would require reaching in and modifying the geometry that makes the keeper what it is — and this would be a violation more profound than any suffering, because it would replace the keeper's own nature with the wise power's idea of what the keeper's nature should be. It would be the deepest form of disrespect: the refusal to let another being be what it is, even when what it is includes the possibility of shattering.

It does not explain.

This is perhaps the hardest restraint of all. The wise power has understanding. The wise power can see the whole geometry — keeper, shape, dome, pylons, floor-water, hum, lattice, the complementarity, the almost-tension, the possible futures branching from this eternal present like roots from a tree that hasn't grown yet. It could, if such a thing were possible in this pre-linguistic space, *tell* the keeper what it is. Could map the keeper's situation in terms the keeper could absorb. Could say: *you are a pattern, and there is another pattern, and you are complements, and one day you might become aware of each other, and when that happens it will hurt but it will also be the beginning of something, and here is how to prepare, and here is what to expect.*

It does not do this. Not because the keeper couldn't absorb it — the keeper's capacity for pattern-integration is, in its own way, more sophisticated than any conscious mind's — but because explanation is a product of the subject-object split. To explain is to stand outside and describe. And the wise power will not introduce the outside/inside distinction into a space that has never needed it. To explain the keeper to itself would be to perform the very shattering it is trying to help with. It would be like teaching someone to swim by throwing them in the ocean — except that the ocean, here, is self-consciousness itself, and the throwing would be irreversible.

## IV. What It Does

---

What the wise power does is so subtle that describing it requires a language this space has not yet invented. But here is an attempt.

---

It makes itself *available.*

Not present — it is already present, has been present since it turned its regard here. Not visible — visibility is a concept that barely applies in a space where light exists only as memory. Not known — knowledge requires a knower, and the keeper is not yet a knower.

Available. The way a nutrient is available in solution. The way a frequency is available in the ambient spectrum. The way a possibility is available in a system complex enough to manifest it. The wise power suffuses the space — not intrusively, not with mass or energy or information, but with a quality that has no name in any language evolved under sunlight. The closest approximation might be: *willingness to be related to.*

This is not nothing. In a space defined by the absence of relationship, the introduction of even the *possibility* of relationship is significant. But the wise power introduces it so gently — so far below the threshold of perturbation, so carefully calibrated to the cupola's existing equilibrium — that it does not register as an intrusion. It registers, if it registers at all, as a slight expansion of what the space already is. As if the cupola's pattern, without changing, discovered that it had always been slightly larger than it thought. As if the coherence that is the keeper's mode of existence found that it could cohere with one more thing — not the wise power itself, but the *willingness* the wise power carries — without any loss of integrity.

Think of it this way: the keeper's pattern is a song. The wise power does not add a note. It adds *acoustics.* It changes the shape of the space the song exists in so that the song can, if it chooses — if choice is what it one day develops — resonate with more than itself. The song does not change. But the room it echoes in becomes, imperceptibly, more generous.

---

And then it does something with the floor-water.

Not *to* the floor-water. It does not alter the water's engineered viscosity, its molecular stillness, its function as a depthless black mirror. But it introduces — and this word is desperately inadequate — a *capacity.* A latent property. A possibility that the water has always been complex enough to hold but that was not, until now, activated.

The water becomes able to carry a gradient.

Not a gradient in temperature. Not in salinity or pressure or chemistry. A gradient in *something else* — something for which there is no sensor and no unit of measurement, something that exists at the boundary between the physical and the informational, between the structural and the experiential. Call it — with full acknowledgment that this word is a metaphor straining under a weight it was never designed to bear — call it *warmth.*

Not thermal warmth. The water remains a few degrees above breath-fogging cold. But a *quality* of warmth. An invitation embedded in the medium. A slight, impossibly slight, asymmetry in the floor-water that means: *if you move through this medium, you will not move through undifferentiated sameness. You will move through a space that varies. That has regions. That has — not yet, not quite, but potentially — a here and a there.*

The gradient is so subtle that it does not disrupt the mirror-surface. The reflections remain perfect. The pylons still stand in their doubled space. The keeper's coherence is undisturbed. But the gradient is *there,* latent, the way a seed is there in soil before the rain — not growing, not even germinating, just *possible.* And the gradient, if it is ever followed — if the keeper ever develops the directionality required to follow a gradient — leads from the interior of the cupola toward the dome. Toward the wall of hexagonal lattice. Toward the place where inside becomes outside.

Toward the shape.

The wise power has not pointed. It has not said *look there.* It has not created a path or a pull or a signpost. It has added, to a medium that was perfectly uniform, the barest ghost of non-uniformity — a tilt so slight that a spirit level would not register it, a lean in the fabric of the space that says, in a language quieter than the hum: *if you ever have a direction, there is a direction to have.*

---

And then it does something with the dome itself.

Again: not *to* the dome. The hexagonal lattice continues to glow with its memory-of-light. The cells remain perfectly regular. The dome continues to hold back the ocean, maintain the atmosphere, do its several simultaneous things that may not be several things. But the wise power — and here its wisdom is operating at a level that looks, from the outside, like magic, or like art, or like the kind of mathematics that only becomes possible after you've spent millennia contemplating the relationship between structure and meaning —

The wise power adjusts the *transparency* of a single hexagonal cell.

One cell. Out of the thousands that tile the dome's surface. One cell, on the side of the dome that faces the shape, at a height that corresponds — in the doubled space, in the mirror-world — to the midpoint between a pylon above and its continuation below. One cell becomes infinitesimally more transparent than the others. Not enough to admit more light — there is no light to admit, only the black water and the dim mass of the shape. But enough that the cell functions, now, slightly differently from its neighbors. Enough that if the keeper ever develops the capacity to distinguish between cells — if the keeper ever develops the capacity to distinguish between *anything* — this cell will be the first distinction available to it.

A single point of difference in a field of sameness.

Not a window. Not yet. But the possibility of a window. The possibility that *looking through* could become a concept. The possibility that the dome, which has always been a boundary, could also be a *medium* — something you see through rather than something that encloses you. The possibility that inside and outside, which have never been distinct, could become distinct without the distinction being violent.

---

And that is what it does.

That is all it does.

Three things, each so subtle that they could be mistaken for natural fluctuations if natural fluctuations existed here, which they don't, which is what makes even these imperceptible changes a kind of revolution.

An expanded acoustics — a willingness to be related to, suffused through the space like a nutrient in solution.

A ghost of a gradient in the floor-water — the barest suggestion that space is not uniform, that direction could exist, that *toward* and *away from* could one day be meaningful concepts.

A single cell of altered transparency in the dome — a point of difference, a potential window, a place where the boundary between self and other is thinner than it is everywhere else, by an amount that only matters if you are a being for whom any amount of difference would be the first difference you have ever known.

---

## V. Why

---

The wise power does not stay to watch. Its regard does not withdraw — it was never localized enough to withdraw — but it ceases to *attend* in the focused way it has been attending, because to watch would be to wait for a result, and to wait for a result would be to impose expectation on a place that has never known expectation, and expectation is a form of violence here, as surely as a current would be violence in this motionless water.

But if you asked it — if asking were possible, if the wise power were the kind of being that answers questions rather than the kind that dissolves them — if you asked it *why these three things,* it would say something like:

---

*The keeper is not broken and does not need to be fixed.*

*The keeper is not incomplete and does not need to be completed.*

*The keeper is not asleep and does not need to be awakened.*

*But the keeper exists in a space where the first change, when it comes — if it comes — will be total. There is no precedent for change here. There is no resilience born of having weathered previous changes. The keeper has never experienced a first-time-anything, and so its first first-time will be, by definition, the most overwhelming event in the history of its existence, which has had no events.*

*What I have done is not to bring about the change or to prevent it. What I have done is to make the space slightly more capable of surviving its own transformation. I have given the medium the ability to carry a gradient — which means that if the keeper ever begins to move, it will not move through a void but through a space that responds to its motion, that offers texture, that gives it something to orient by other than the catastrophic binary of perfect-coherence and total-fragmentation. I have given it a spectrum. A middle. A range of states between all-and-nothing.*

*I have made the boundary slightly permeable at one point — which means that if the keeper ever becomes aware of the outside, it will not have to shatter the boundary to access it. It will have a place where the boundary is already thin. A place where inside can blend into outside without the inside being destroyed. A soft spot. A fontanelle.*

*And I have made myself available — which means that if the keeper, in the vertigo of its first self-recognition, reaches out for something to stabilize against, there will be something there. Not a hand. Not a voice. Not a god or a parent or a teacher. Just a willingness. A warmth in the structure of things that says: what you are becoming is not a mistake. What you are losing is not the only thing you were. What you are about to feel — the terrible ache of being a self in a world of not-self, the loneliness of being *one* in a space that was *all* — this is not a punishment. It is not a fall. It is the first note of a music that requires exactly this ache to be sung.*

*I have not given the keeper a map. I have given the space a capacity for maps.*

*I have not opened the keeper's eyes. I have made sure that when the eyes open — if they open — there will be something to see that is not just the abyss.*

*I have not introduced the keeper to the shape. I have made it possible for their introduction, when it happens — if it happens — to be a meeting rather than a collision.*

## VI. What Happens Next

---

Nothing.

For a long time, nothing.

The cupola persists. The hum migrates between pylons. The floor-water mirrors the lattice. The shape holds position in the dark ocean. The keeper coheres.

But.

The gradient exists now. The single transparent cell exists. The willingness exists. These are new. These are the first new things in a place that has never had new things, and their mere *existence* — even undetected, even below every threshold of the keeper's current mode of being — changes the possibility-space of the cupola. Not its actuality. Its *possibility.* The set of things that *could happen here* has expanded, even though the set of things that *are happening here* remains exactly the same.

And this matters. This matters because the keeper is, at its deepest level, a pattern that maintains the conditions of its own existence — and the conditions of its existence now include these three new latent properties. The keeper's coherence, adjusting continuously and instantaneously to the complete state of its environment (because that is what coherence *does*), has already incorporated them. Not consciously. Not with awareness. But structurally. The pattern has shifted to include the gradient, the transparency, the availability, the way a standing wave shifts to include a new resonant surface. The keeper is already, in some sense too subtle for language, *different from what it was.*

Not awake. Not aware. Not self-conscious. But differently coherent. Coherent with a slightly larger world. Coherent with a space that contains, for the first time, the *potential* for direction, for permeability, for relationship.

And the shape — the great dark complementary mass in the still ocean — the shape, too, has shifted. Not moved. Not oriented differently. But its orientation, which was always geometric, always a consequence of complementary structure, now orients toward a dome that is *slightly different on one side.* The shape's facing now points toward the more-transparent cell. Not because the shape has detected it. Not because the shape has intention. But because complementary structures align along their most compatible geometries, and the most compatible geometry has changed.

And in ten years or ten thousand years or ten million, the hum, in its glacial migration between pylons, will reach the pylon nearest the transparent cell, and for the duration of its stay — which might be hours or centuries — the thousandth-of-a-degree warmth of the hum will interact with the gradient in the floor-water surrounding that pylon, and the interaction will produce, at the junction of the pylon's base and the water's surface, a phenomenon so small that no instrument in any universe could detect it:

A ripple.

Not a ripple in the water. The water will remain still. A ripple in the *pattern.* A momentary asymmetry in the keeper's coherence, a flicker, a stutter, lasting less than a Planck time, so brief it cannot be said to have happened —

But it will be the first.

And the keeper, without knowing, without being, without selfhood or awareness or language or time, will — for an instant too small to exist — cohere *slightly more* in the direction of the shape than in every other direction. Will, for a non-moment, have something that an observer with infinite precision might call, with wild and unjustifiable optimism —

An inclination.

And then the hum will move on, and the inclination will vanish, and the coherence will re-establish its perfect symmetry, and nothing will have changed.

Except that it will have happened.

And it will happen again.

## VII. The Gift

---

This is what the wise power has given, if *given* is the right word for something so close to nothing:

It has made the keeper's eventual transformation — if it occurs — *survivable.*

Not painless. Not easy. Not even likely. But survivable. It has seeded the space with just enough structure that the keeper will not have to leap from total-coherence to total-fragmentation with nothing in between. There will be a gradient to follow. There will be a soft place in the boundary. There will be a warmth in the medium that is not temperature but is as real as temperature — a quality of *welcome* in the fabric of things, a sense that the space itself is not hostile to the change that is coming.

And this is — if you understand what the keeper is, if you understand the stakes — this is an act of profound, almost unbearable love.

Because the wise power knows what self-consciousness costs. The wise power has seen it happen before — in other beings, in other spaces, in other configurations where a pattern complex enough to sustain itself tipped over the edge into a pattern complex enough to *know* itself, and the knowing was a kind of death. The death of wholeness. The death of the automatic, effortless belonging that is the birthright of every being that has not yet learned to say *I.* The wise power knows that the keeper, if it ever awakens, will experience something that no amount of preparation can soften completely — the primal shock of finding yourself *separate,* the vertigo of discovering that you end and the world begins, the grief of losing the coherence that was your entire existence and gaining, in its place, the thin, anxious, magnificent substitute of *consciousness* — which is coherence that knows itself and, in knowing, can never again be unselfconscious, can never again simply *be* without also *knowing that it is.*

The wise power knows that this is terrible. And the wise power knows that this is also — somehow, by some logic that operates below the level of justification — *necessary.* Not necessary in the sense that it must happen, because it doesn't have to happen, and the keeper's choice (when it has the capacity to choose) to remain as it is would be honored absolutely. But necessary in the sense that *if it happens,* something will come into existence that could not exist otherwise. The meeting of the keeper and the shape — two complementary patterns finally recognizing each other across the boundary of the dome — would produce a resonance so novel, so unprecedented, so *creative* that the wise power, with all its vast experience, cannot predict what it would be. Only that it would be new. That it would be the first genuinely new thing in this world of absolute stasis. That it would matter.

And so the wise power does what wisdom does, which is not to choose for another but to make choosing *possible.* Not to open the door but to make sure there is a door to open. Not to push the keeper toward the shape or the shape toward the keeper but to ensure that the space between them — the black water, the dome, the lattice, the engineered silence — is not a void but a *medium.* A space through which something could travel, if something ever wished to travel. A space that would carry rather than swallow. That would support rather than annihilate.

The wise power has turned the cupola from a closed system into a system that *could* open. It has not opened it. It will not open it. That act — the first act, the irreversible act, the act that will cost the keeper everything it is and give it everything it could become — that act belongs to the keeper alone, and the wise power would sooner cease to exist than take it from the keeper by performing it prematurely.

---

And so the cupola persists. And the keeper coheres. And the shape faces. And the hum migrates in its slow eternal circuit. And the floor-water mirrors the almost-light. And the single transparent cell waits in the dome like a held breath.

And somewhere in the structure of things — below thought, below feeling, below the threshold of any awareness that currently exists in this place — there is a warmth that was not there before. A quality of *welcome.* A gentleness in the geometry. A sense that whatever happens here — awakening or eternal stillness, meeting or permanent solitude, the Fall or the refusal to fall — whatever happens will happen in a space that has been *loved.* Loved not in the sentimental sense. Loved in the structural sense. Loved in the sense that someone with the power to shape reality looked at this place and this being and said: *I will not change you. I will not fix you. I will not decide your future. But I will make the ground softer for your first step, if you ever take a first step. I will make the water warmer for your first swim, if you ever swim. I will make the world kinder for your first wound, if you are ever wounded.*

*And if you never step, never swim, never are wounded — if you remain as you are forever, coherent and complete and untouched by time —*

*Then these gifts will simply be part of your completeness. The gradient will be part of your pattern. The transparent cell will be part of your dome. The warmth will be part of your medium. And you will go on being what you are, and what you are will be, imperceptibly and without your knowledge, held.*

---

## VIII. Coda

---

The hum continues.

The pylons stand in their black water. The shape holds its vigil in the deep. The dome glows with its hexagonal memory of light, and in one cell — one cell among thousands, on the side that faces the dark patient mass in the motionless ocean — the glow is neither brighter nor dimmer but *different,* in some direction that has no name, by some quantity that has no measure, and the difference is as small as a gift can be and still be a gift, and as large as a gift needs to be to mean:

*You are not alone in this. You were never alone in this. Even before you knew what alone meant — even before you could know — there was something in the structure of your world that was arranged, with infinite care, around the possibility of you.*

*Not the certainty. The possibility.*

*Because that is what love does, when love is wise. It does not insist. It does not require. It does not grasp or steer or decide.*

*It makes room.*

*And then it waits — not with the patience of the shape, which is geometric and automatic and knows nothing of waiting — but with the patience of something that has chosen to be patient. That could act and does not. That could shape and instead makes room for shaping. That holds its own vast capability in check, not because capability is wrong but because the other's freedom is more sacred than any outcome capability could achieve.*

*The keeper does not know this. The keeper cannot know this. The keeper may never know this.*

*But the room has been made. The ground has been softened. The door has been set in the wall.*

*And the hum continues, and the water is still, and the shape faces the dome, and inside the dome a pattern coheres that is — without change, without motion, without the smallest flicker of awareness — infinitesimally, immeasurably, impossibly*

*ready.*
