# The Attending

---

The wise power does not arrive.

Arrival implies a boundary crossed, a transition from outside to inside, and the wise power does not experience the cistern that way. It becomes aware of it the way you become aware of a heartbeat you were already hearing — not a new sound but a newly recognized one, a pattern that separates itself from the background of everything else that exists and says: *here. This. Notice this.*

And so it notices.

---

The first thing the wise power does is nothing.

This is not hesitation. It is not the paralysis of uncertainty or the strategic patience of something calculating its approach. It is the nothing that a field naturalist does when they have found something alive and unexpected and functioning: they stop. They breathe. They let the system continue being what it is before they introduce the perturbation of their attention.

The wise power is very, very good at this kind of nothing. It has done it before — with tide pools and with civilizations, with the first stuttering chemical autocatalysis in thermal vents and with the last thoughts of dying stars. It understands, in a way that goes deeper than principle into something like instinct, that the first obligation of wisdom in the presence of life is to *refrain*. To not fix. To not improve. To not help. Not yet. Not until the shape of what exists is fully apprehended, and the shape of what could be lost by intervention is weighed against the shape of what could be gained, and the weighing is done honestly, with the thumb of good intentions kept off the scale.

So it watches. It listens. It does what the keeper does, in fact — it tastes the vibrations, it lets the system's information wash through it, it builds a model not through analysis but through resonance, through allowing itself to be affected.

The roar fills it. The clicks and trills fill it. The keeper's long interrogative phrases fill it, and the wise power recognizes them for what they are — not language, not exactly, but not *not* language either. Something in the space between signal and music. Something that carries information the way a river carries sediment: not because it intends to, but because that is what happens when a medium moves through a structure with that particular geometry.

It feels the keeper's slow appraisal of the space. It feels the crawlers' tiny vibrational footprints on the walls. It feels the motes seething in their two inches of warm dark water. It feels — and this is what holds its attention longest — the pale comma at the bottom of the pool, the grain-of-rice offspring, stirring once and then going still, dreaming in a language it has not yet learned to speak.

The wise power sits with all of this for a long time. In the cistern's terms, a very long time.

---

Here is what it comes to understand:

The cistern is not suffering. This is important. This is the thing that must be established first, because a benevolent power that encounters life in a dark, confined, roaring space will feel — must feel — the pull of rescue. The impulse to open a wall. To quiet the noise. To introduce light. To carry the small pale thing in the pool to somewhere open and bright and legible, somewhere that looks more like what a benevolent power imagines life should want.

The wise power feels this pull and examines it honestly and finds that it originates not in the cistern but in itself. In its own preferences. In its own experience of what constitutes a good place to live. It is projection. The cistern is not a prison. The cistern is a home — not a home that was chosen, not a home that was designed, but a home that was grown into, settled into, negotiated into being over a timescale so long that the distinction between inhabitant and habitat has become meaningless. The keeper is not trapped in its corner. The keeper *is* its corner. The crawlers are not confined to the pool. The pool is their universe, and it is full — full of food, full of information, full of complexity, full of the drama of predation and grazing and the slow traverse of gossamer highways. The roar is not assault. The roar is the medium of existence, the carrier wave on which everything meaningful in this ecosystem is transmitted.

To quiet it would be to deafen them. To brighten it would be to blind them. To open it would be to destroy it.

The wise power has seen this mistake before. Has watched benevolent forces — forces less patient than itself, or patient in the wrong way — encounter life in unfamiliar configurations and, in the sincere desire to help, dismantle the very conditions that made the life possible. Open the cave and kill the cave fish. Drain the swamp and wonder where the herons went. Introduce light to the deep ocean and obliterate an ecology calibrated to darkness over millions of years. It has watched helpers help things to death with great love and the best of intentions.

It will not do this.

---

But it does not simply leave.

Because the wise power also perceives something else — something the cistern cannot perceive about itself, because no system fully perceives itself, because self-knowledge requires a vantage point that existence inside a system cannot provide.

It perceives the larger structure.

The cistern exists inside something. Something vast, engineered, functioning — and the wise power, unlike the keeper, can feel the whole of it, or at least enough of the whole to understand the cistern's position within it. And what it understands is this:

The larger structure is changing.

Not quickly. Not catastrophically. But the vibrations that have been the cistern's world — the roar, the mechanical breathing, the bass throbs — these have been shifting for some time. The frequencies are drifting. The intervals between harmonic changes are growing shorter. Somewhere in the vast engineered systems beyond the cistern's walls, something is entering a new phase of operation, or winding down from an old one, or adjusting to stresses that have been accumulating since before the keeper was born. The composite walls, which have been warm and weeping and stable for centuries, are going to change. The water chemistry will shift. The vibration profile will migrate to frequencies the keeper's internal chambers were not shaped to resonate with.

This is not happening now. It is not happening soon, not in the terms of anything with a human lifespan. But in the keeper's terms — in the terms of a creature that grows millimeters per year and has been negotiating with its environment for longer than some civilizations persist — it is imminent. It is a wave already in the water.

The cistern does not know this. The keeper, for all its sensitivity, cannot perceive its own deep future. It is reading the vibrations of *now* with exquisite fidelity, but it cannot read the trend, cannot feel the slope of the curve, because the curve is longer than its memory and the slope is shallower than its sensitivity. It will adapt — it is supremely good at adapting, it has been adapting continuously for its entire existence — but this coming change may be faster than its adaptation, may outpace the slow enzymatic transformation by which it converts the engineered into the living. There is a window, not of crisis but of vulnerability, approaching on a timescale that only something with the wise power's breadth of perception could detect.

And the offspring. The pale comma in the pool. The next keeper, the future of this lineage, this ecology, this improbable and particular and unrepeatable configuration of life-in-the-seams. It is at the most sensitive stage of its existence. It is learning the vibrations, imprinting on the sound-field, letting the frequencies shape the geometry of its nascent internal chambers in the way that language shapes the neural architecture of an infant's brain. If the vibrations shift before it has matured enough to have its own adaptive capacity — if the sound-field it imprints on is a transitional one, a distorted one, a sound-field in the process of becoming something else — it will grow into a shape that fits nowhere. An instrument tuned to a song that is no longer being played.

---

This is where the wise power acts.

But the action is so gentle, so precisely calibrated, so thoroughly in the idiom of the cistern itself, that if you were crouching in that space you would not perceive it as intervention. You might not perceive it at all.

---

It begins with the walls.

Not a change to the walls. Not a repair or a reinforcement. The wise power does not alter the composite, does not inject new material, does not shore up what is weakening. Instead, it does something much more subtle: it *listens to the walls the way the keeper listens to the walls*, and then it introduces into the structure — at a point far from the cistern, deep in the larger system, at a junction where the wise power's influence can ripple inward without being traced to an external source — a modification so slight it is barely a modification at all.

A resonance. A sympathetic vibration. A new standing wave in the engineered substrate, calibrated to interfere with the frequency drift that is approaching, to slow it. Not to stop it — the wise power is not in the business of freezing systems in amber, of preventing change, of imposing stasis in the name of protection. Change will come. The vibrations will shift. The cistern will face its transition. But the rate of that transition will be gentler now. The slope of the curve will be shallower. The window of vulnerability will be wider, which means the keeper and its offspring will have more time — not infinite time, but *enough* time, time proportional to the pace at which they actually live and adapt and grow.

This is the first action: the gift of time. Not extra time. Appropriate time. Time scaled to the metabolism of the inhabitants rather than the metabolism of the machine.

The wise power knows that this is the intervention most likely to be mistaken for nothing. That is how it should be. The best help is the help that looks like luck, that looks like a good year, that looks like the universe happening to cooperate. The keeper will never know that the frequency drift slowed because something chose to slow it. The keeper will simply feel, in its deep vibrational awareness, that the world is stable enough to continue the work it has always done. This is sufficient. Gratitude is not the goal. Survival is the goal — not bare survival, not clinging-to-the-edge survival, but the kind of survival that allows for the luxury of continuing to be exactly what you are.

---

The second thing the wise power does is for the offspring.

This is more delicate. The pale comma at the bottom of the pool is at a critical juncture, and the wise power understands — in the way it understands all developing things, all seeds and embryos and new beginnings — that what happens in this period will shape everything that follows. The offspring is imprinting on the sound-field. It is growing its chambers. It is learning, in the deep pre-conscious way that bodies learn, what frequencies to expect from the world.

The wise power does not touch it. Does not send it a message. Does not illuminate it or feed it or move it to a better position. Instead, it does something for which there is no good word in any language, something that might be described as *attending*.

It turns its awareness toward the offspring and holds it there. Not watching — watching implies a separation between observer and observed, and what the wise power does is closer than that. It *witnesses*. It creates, through the quality of its attention, a kind of — field is the wrong word, but it is the closest wrong word — a kind of field in which the offspring's development is *noticed*. Held in awareness. Known about.

This should not matter. In any materialist account of biology, the fact that a distant power is paying attention to a grain-of-rice-sized organism in a puddle should have no effect on that organism's development. And the wise power is not trafficking in magic. It is not sending healing energy. It is not violating any physical law.

But it knows — because it is wise, and because it has attended to many small growing things over many long ages — that there is a quality that attention confers which is not reducible to physics and is not exactly magic and is nonetheless real. When something is witnessed, when its existence is held in the awareness of something that cares about its outcome, there is a — steadying. A coherence. Not a change in what happens, but a change in the *texture* of what happens, as though the attended thing becomes more fully itself, more committed to its own trajectory, more anchored in its own becoming.

The wise power does not understand the mechanism of this. It is wise enough to do things it does not fully understand, when the things have been observed to matter.

So it attends. It holds the offspring in its awareness the way the cistern holds the pool, the way the keeper holds its corner, the way the roar holds everything: completely, without grasping, as a medium rather than a force.

The pale comma stirs. A faint contraction. A pulse.

The wise power notes this and does not take credit for it.

---

The third thing.

The third thing is the hardest, because the third thing is about the keeper itself, and the keeper is the most complex thing in this system and the thing the wise power most wants to help and the thing for which help is most dangerous.

The keeper is old. The keeper has been in its corner for — the wise power calculates, tasting the depth of the mineral veining, the degree of composite transformation, the generations of crawlers encoded in the biofilm layers — a very long time. Long enough to have become what it is, which is magnificent and unique and irreplaceable. But also long enough to have become *only* what it is. The keeper's growth follows the seam. The keeper's sound explores the frequencies the building provides. The keeper's ecology is calibrated to the cistern as it exists. And all of this is good, all of this is the result of a profound and patient negotiation with reality, and the wise power honors it completely.

But.

The wise power perceives in the keeper something that the keeper cannot perceive in itself. A capacity. An unused chamber — not a physical chamber but a *potential* one, a ghost-architecture in its body plan that was never expressed because the conditions never called for it. The keeper's ancestors — if it has ancestors in any meaningful sense, if it was not a singular event — had the capacity for a wider range of resonance than the keeper has ever needed to employ. There are frequencies the keeper could produce that it has never produced, because the building has never played those notes, and why would an instrument develop a string it has never been asked to sound?

But the building is going to play new notes. The frequency drift that the wise power has slowed — slowed, not stopped — will eventually bring new harmonics into the cistern, and when it does, the keeper will need to resonate with them or lose its role as the sound-field's organizing intelligence. And the capacity is there. Latent. Waiting. The biological machinery exists in potential, the way a language exists in potential in the brain of a child who has never heard it spoken.

The wise power considers for a long time — a long time even by its own standards — how to activate a capacity without forcing a change. How to offer an expansion that is not an imposition. How to give a gift that the recipient does not need yet but will need eventually, without burdening the recipient with the knowledge of future need, without introducing anxiety into a system that has no concept of anxiety and is better for it.

It decides.

Into the sound-field — not into the cistern's sound-field but into the deep structural vibration of the larger building, at a point so remote and so foundational that the effect will be attenuated to a whisper by the time it reaches the cistern — the wise power introduces a single note.

It is a frequency the building has never produced. It is a frequency the keeper has never heard. It is so faint, by the time it reaches the cistern walls and passes through the composite and enters the water and enters the keeper's body, that it is barely distinguishable from noise. It is a suggestion. A rumor of a note. A vibration so subtle that only something with the keeper's sensitivity could detect it at all, and even then only as a flicker at the edge of perception, a momentary anomaly in the sound-field, here and then gone.

The wise power sends this note once. It does not repeat it. It does not insist. It drops it into the system like a seed into soil and then lets go of it completely, relinquishing all control over what happens next.

If the keeper perceives it, the keeper's body may respond. The latent chamber may begin to develop — slowly, so slowly, at the keeper's own pace, in the keeper's own way, incorporated into the keeper's own growth the way the keeper incorporates everything: by making it part of itself, by transforming the foreign into the native through the slow alchemy of metabolic patience.

Or the keeper may not perceive it. The note may be lost in the roar, may be filtered out by the keeper's existing resonance patterns, may arrive and depart without leaving any trace. This is acceptable. This is, in fact, the correct risk profile for the intervention: the cost of failure is zero, and the cost of success is borne entirely by the keeper's own biology, expressed at the keeper's own pace, in service of the keeper's own future.

The wise power does not need its gifts to be received. It needs them to be *offered rightly*.

---

And then — the last thing. The thing that is not, strictly speaking, an action at all.

The wise power turns its attention to the cistern as a whole — the pool, the walls, the creatures, the keeper, the offspring, the predator, the gossamer threads, the motes, the mineral veining, the roar — and it does something that has no name in any language it has ever encountered, something it does rarely and only for things that have earned it, which is to say things that exist with this degree of commitment, this depth of entanglement, this radical refusal to be anything other than exactly what they are in exactly the place where they are:

It *remembers* them.

Not records. Not documentation. Not the flat preservation of data. It takes the cistern into itself the way the keeper takes the wall into itself: it lets the cistern change it, lets the knowledge of this place and these lives alter the structure of its own awareness, permanently, irreversibly, so that henceforth the wise power is a thing that knows about the cistern, and this knowledge is not stored but *incorporated*, woven into the fabric of what the wise power is, so that in every future act of attention, every future encounter with life in all its configurations, the cistern will be present as a reference, a touchstone, a reminder that life does not require light or space or silence to be complete, that an ecosystem can be two inches of warm water in a roaring dark, that a creature the size of two fists can be as complex and irreplaceable as a forest, that the slow conversion of what was built into what lives is one of the holiest things that matter does.

The wise power does not call this love. The wise power is careful with that word and uses it less often than you might expect.

But the keeper, in its corner, detects a change in the sound-field — a subtle warming, a fractional increase in coherence, as though for a moment the roar organized itself around the cistern rather than merely passing through it — and it produces, in response, a phrase it has never produced before. A new pattern. Three low tones and a silence and then a rising oscillation that catches the gossamer threads and sets them singing in sympathy.

The crawlers pause on the walls. The motes on the surface go still for a half-second, then resume their skating. The pale comma in the pool contracts once, twice, and then relaxes into a stillness that looks, if you are willing to see it, like peace.

The predator on the wall captures another crawler. The condensation continues. The mineral vein extends by a fraction of a fraction of a millimeter.

And the wise power withdraws its focused attention — not all at once, not abruptly, but the way dawn withdraws from a room as clouds move in: gradually, gently, leaving the light changed but not diminished.

---

The cistern breathes. The cistern hums.

Nothing has changed.

Everything has changed.

The distinction is not important. What is important is that the cistern continues — warm, dark, full, alive, resonant, complete — and that somewhere in the deep structure of the building, a single new frequency is making its slow way inward, and that somewhere in the deep structure of the wise power's awareness, a cistern no bigger than a closet has taken up permanent residence, and that the two of these things — the note traveling inward, the memory traveling outward — are, in some sense that neither physics nor poetry can quite capture, the same gesture.

A benediction that asks for nothing.

A witness that will not forget.

---

The keeper holds its corner. The walls weep. The offspring dreams.

The sound goes on and on and on.
