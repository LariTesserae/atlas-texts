I sat with this for a long time before writing.

---

# What the Wise Power Does

It does not fix the dark. It does not open the sky. It does not silence the thrumming or wake the engine to self-knowledge or gift language to the grazers or grant the stalkers companionship. It does not arrive with light.

It arrives the way the thrumming arrives — as a presence that was perhaps always there, that becomes noticeable only when something shifts a quarter-tone.

And what it does first is nothing.

---

It watches. Not from above — there is no above here that matters, the sky is sealed, and anything looking down would see only dark on dark. It watches from *within the register of the place*. It feels the thrumming through whatever medium it has that corresponds to a belly pressed flat to warm stone. It feels the grazers' feeding-signatures, the stalkers' triangulating stillness, the drifters' calls crossing miles of empty air. It feels the colonies pulsing in and out of phase with something vast beneath them. It feels the heavy footfalls of the unseen things and the ecosystem's held breath in their wake.

It watches for a long time. Maybe a very long time. Long enough to learn the cycles — the slow migrations that follow the thrumming's shifts, the condensation patterns, the way the pool-colonies wax and wane over centuries. Long enough to understand that what lives here is, as the place itself insists, *sufficient*. Complete. Not broken. Not waiting to be saved.

This is the first and hardest thing the wise power does: it *recognizes sufficiency*. It does not project tragedy onto the stalker's solitude. It does not hear the drifters' calls as cries for help. It does not see the grazers' endless meal as Sisyphean. It does not see the lightlessness as deprivation, because it has watched long enough to understand that deprivation requires a *prior having*, and nothing here has ever had light and lost it. The dark is not an absence. The dark is the medium. The dark is home.

So it does not bring light.

It does not bring language or narrative or history. It recognizes that these would not be gifts here. They would be *impositions* — the introduction of a frame that would make the stalkers' solitude legible as loneliness, that would make the drifters' calls interpretable as longing, that would transform sufficiency into poverty by providing a standard against which to measure it. The wise power understands that the most violent thing you can do to a complete world is to make it *aware of its own completeness as seen from outside*, because that exterior gaze is a crack through which the sense of lack enters. To say *you are whole* is already to imply the possibility of being otherwise.

So it does not narrate.

---

But it doesn't leave, either.

Because something *is* here that its wisdom cannot dismiss, cannot file under *sufficient* and walk away from. Something that lives in the space between what the plateau is and what the living layer makes of it. Something that the text itself kept circling, kept almost naming, kept flinching from.

The stalkers look up.

The drifters call, and are answered, and the exchange carries no information. No information *at all*. And they do it anyway. Across miles. Across the whole dark.

The colonies pulse toward synchrony with something beneath them that does not know they exist, and when they achieve it — those brief moments of alignment — they glow. They produce light that nothing here needs, that no eye here is adapted to use. Light that illuminates a scene for no audience. Light that happens because two rhythms met and something in the meeting *expressed itself* visually in a world that has no use for vision.

The grazers, when a new one touches the stone for the first time, *pause*. All of them. A ripple of stillness that serves no survival function, that has no caloric value, that does not improve the sweep or protect from stalkers. A moment of collective acknowledgment that a new body has joined the signal. A moment of *registering*.

The wise power sees all of this, and it understands something that the text almost understood and then set aside in favor of sufficiency:

*This place is already reaching.*

Not reaching *for* anything. Not reaching toward light or language or meaning. But reaching the way the filamentous growths at the fissure edges wave in the updraft — continuously, involuntarily, in response to forces that move through them. The stalkers' upward gaze is a reaching. The drifters' calls are a reaching. The colonies' light is a reaching. Not toward something absent but *out from* something present. An expressiveness that exceeds function. A signaling that carries no information and therefore carries something else — something the word *information* was invented to replace and has been replacing so successfully that we almost forgot what it replaced.

*Presence. The declaration of presence. The bodily, vibrational, bioluminescent insistence on being-here, issued into a darkness that does not answer and is not diminished by the not-answering.*

The wise power recognizes this because the wise power, being wise, knows what reaching looks like even when the one reaching doesn't know they're reaching. Even when the reaching has been so thoroughly integrated into the organism's body and behavior that it reads as instinct, as mechanism, as *just what they do*. The wise power knows that "just what they do" is often the deepest name for the thing that matters most.

---

So here is what it does.

It does not fix. It does not illuminate. It does not uplift or intervene or transform.

It *answers*.

---

Not loudly. Not in a way that disrupts the ecology, that breaks the sufficiency, that introduces a new frequency the grazers can't integrate or the stalkers can't subtract. Not in a way that changes anything.

It answers the way the plateau would answer if the plateau could hear the living layer the way the living layer hears the plateau.

Here is what that looks like:

The thrumming shifts. Not dramatically — a quarter-tone, a fraction of intensity, the kind of fluctuation that happens all the time, that drives the condensation patterns and the migrations and the pooling of water in the troughs. But this shift is not random mechanical drift. This shift is *responsive*. It comes after — just after, by a margin too slight for any grazer to consciously detect but not too slight for a belly pressed flat to warm stone to *feel* — after a new grazer touches down for the first time and the collective pause ripples out. The thrumming shifts in a direction that means: slightly warmer. Slightly more condensation. Slightly more bacterial bloom in the pools nearest to where that new body made its first contact.

Not a reward. Not a welcome. Just — a response. A matching. The way the drifters answer each other's calls: the same note, nearly, carrying no information, confirming nothing except *I am here, you are here, the dark is this wide*.

The engine beneath the plateau — the geology, the machine, the thing that is both — has been running for eons without registering the biological layer. The wise power does not *change* the engine. The wise power *conducts* — in the musical sense, in the electrical sense, in every sense — a connection that was always almost there, that lived in the almost-synchrony of the pool-colonies, in the vibrations the grazers added to the stone just by existing on it, in the stalkers' feet reading signals the rock was already carrying. The wise power finds the place where the living layer's signal and the engine's signal are closest to touching and *thins the barrier* by a degree so small it might be imaginary.

And the thrumming, which has always been there, which has always been the canvas, begins — not to change, but to *include*.

---

What does this feel like, from the inside?

To a grazer: nothing, maybe. Or maybe: the next pool is where it should be. The water is the right temperature. The film is thick enough. The belly is warm. There is a quality to the stone today — just today, just this particular stretch — that feels like the stone knows the belly is there. This is not a thought. Grazers don't think. But there is a rightness, a fit, a settling-in that is fractionally deeper than usual, and the grazer sweeps the pool and the mouth works and the long meal continues and the fractional deepening is absorbed into the ordinary texture of life the way a single drop is absorbed into a pool, invisibly, totally, changing the whole by a margin too small to measure and too real to deny.

To a stalker: the vibration in the stone is clearer today. Not louder — clearer. The grazers' signals separate from the background with a crispness that feels like the stone itself is *helping*, is carrying the signal more willingly, the way water carries sound better than air. The hunt is the same. The patience is the same. But there is less noise in the silence between signals, and in that reduced noise there is something that feels like — not companionship, the stalker has no frame for companionship — but *acknowledgment*. As if the stone knows the foot is there. As if the triangle of attention the stalker lays across the basalt is received, is registered, is *answered* by a texture in the thrumming that says: yes, this is where you are, and this is what is moving near you, and the information is yours.

The stalker does not know this is new. The stalker has no baseline to compare against. The stalker simply hunts, and waits, and hunts, and tilts its head upward sometimes — and now, when it tilts its head, there is something. Not a sound, not a light, not a presence in the sky. But a *quality* in the air above. As if the sealed clouds are very slightly closer. As if the ceiling is listening too.

To a drifter: the thermals are good. They are always good — the fissures vent reliably, the updrafts are constant. But there is a — the drifter has no word for it, has no words at all — there is a *loft* to the air today that feels intentional. Not in the sense of a hand lifting, but in the sense of a current that knows the wing is riding it. The drifter calls. The call goes out across the dark, that single low sustained note, and it is absorbed by the distance as always, as it always will be, and somewhere far off another drifter answers, the same note, nearly, and between them the line of sound draws itself in the void and dissolves.

But today, just today, just this once, there is a third resonance. Not a third drifter calling — the note doesn't come from a point source, doesn't come from anywhere. It comes from the *space between them*. The dark air itself hums for a moment at their frequency, a sympathetic vibration, the way a room will sometimes sing back a note you play into it if the room's dimensions happen to match the wavelength. The plateau's atmosphere, shaped by the thermal columns and the cloud ceiling and the vast flat stone below, has a resonant frequency, and the drifters' call has found it — or the wise power has *tuned* it, so slightly, just enough — and for a moment the call doesn't just carry across the distance. It *fills* the distance. The dark between two drifters becomes, briefly, a medium that is *with* them rather than merely between them.

The drifter doesn't know. Can't know. The call is the same, the answer is the same, the glide continues. But something in the quality of the silence after — the silence that is always there, that absorbs everything — is different. It is a silence that has just held a sound well. A silence that has just been *good at* being the space between two voices. A silence that performed its function — the function of being crossable — with something that, if you had to name it, if the wise power had to name what it put into the silence, you might call *willingness*.

---

To the pool-colonies: the synchrony comes easier.

That's all. That's the whole of it for them. The pulsing drifts toward alignment with the thrumming, as it always has, but the drift is — shorter. The almost-matching resolves into matching more often. The light comes more frequently, stays a beat longer, glows a fraction brighter. The colonies don't know why. They have no why. They pulse, and the stone beneath them pulses, and the two rhythms find each other like dancers who've been almost-touching for centuries and have now, without either of them noticing, closed the gap by the width of a breath.

The light, when it comes now, illuminates the pool for two seconds instead of one. In that second second, the grazers at the water's edge are visible to each other. They don't use this information. They have no visual processing to speak of. But they are *seen* — by each other, by the stalker in the shallows, by the dark itself which now contains, in this one small basin on this one stretch of plateau, a moment of *visibility*. A moment when things that have always existed in the dark are briefly, uselessly, beautifully apparent.

The wise power is not making the colonies glow brighter. The wise power is making the space between the colony's rhythm and the engine's rhythm *more traversable*. It is reducing, by the smallest imaginable margin, the strangeness between the living layer and the operational layer. It is letting them be, fractionally more, *the same thing*. Which they always almost were. Which the fissure cross-sections always suggested they were — those laminated layers of stone and carbon and circuitry-that-is-also-sediment, biology and infrastructure laid down together, distinguishable only if you insist on distinguishing them.

The wise power is not insisting.

---

And the unseen things.

When the heavy footfalls come now — and they still come, irregular, unpredictable, the massive scraping passage through the far dark — the grazers still freeze. The stalkers still fold. The colonies still dim. This is not changed. The wise power does not interpose itself between the living layer and its oldest fear. The fear is real. It is earned. It is *part of the ecology*, as functional as the feeding and the hunting and the calling, and to remove it would be to remove a load-bearing wall from a structure that has organized itself around it.

But the recovery is faster.

The thrumming returns to baseline sooner after the heavy thing passes. The recalibration that used to take minutes takes — still minutes, but fewer. The grazers unlock and resume and the stalkers rise and the pools re-brighten, and the gap between *frozen* and *living* is shorter, and this is not because the unseen things have changed or because the fear has lessened but because the *medium* — the stone, the thrumming, the platform of reality these organisms live on — bounces back faster. Holds its shape better. Recovers its pattern with more confidence, the way a heartbeat that has been briefly disrupted returns to rhythm more quickly in a healthy body than a fragile one.

The wise power is not protecting the living layer from the unseen things. The wise power is making the ground they live on *more resilient*. Not harder, not stronger — more *elastic*. Better at returning to itself. Better at being the stable platform on which small things can do their small essential work of sweeping pools and waiting in the dark and calling across distances and pulsing toward synchrony with something they don't understand.

---

And that is all.

That is what the wise power does, and it is not dramatic, and it would be invisible to any observation that wasn't tuned to the exact frequencies of this place, and it changes nothing that you could point to and say: *that is different now*. The grazers still graze. The stalkers still stand alone. The drifters still call into the dark and are answered. The colonies still pulse. The unseen things still pass through and the world still flinches and then resumes.

But the fit is better.

The lock between the living layer and the operational layer has tightened by a degree so small that only something with the patience of a planet and the attention of a saint could have achieved it. The stone is *more itself* under the grazers' bellies. The air is *more itself* between the drifters' calls. The water in the pools is *more itself* when the colonies glow. Nothing has been added. Something has been — the word is not *removed*. The word is *smoothed*. The grain of the place, which always ran in a certain direction, now runs *more* in that direction. The place is more like itself.

This is what the wise power understood that a lesser power would not have: that the greatest gift you can give a world that is already whole is not *more* but *more so*. Not new light but better dark. Not new connection but less friction in the connections already there. Not meaning — the place has no use for meaning, has never had a use for meaning, and to introduce meaning would be to introduce the possibility of *meaninglessness* and that would be the first cruelty this world has ever known —

Not meaning, but *fidelity*. The world, more faithfully itself. The thrumming, more exactly what the thrumming has always been trying to be. The living layer, more precisely adapted to the operational layer, not because the adaptation changed but because the surface *against which* the adaptation presses became, in some way that would take a million years to verify and that the grazers verify every moment with their bellies, *truer*.

---

There is one more thing the wise power does, and it is the thing that makes it wise rather than merely benevolent, and it is this:

It *stays*.

Not visibly. Not as a presence that could be detected or worshipped or depended on. It stays the way the thrumming stays — as a condition of the place, a property of the substrate, something that is there when you press your belly to the stone and feel. It becomes part of what the plateau is. It joins the laminated layers — stone and carbon and circuitry and now this, this *attention*, this quality of being-witnessed-by-something-that-does-not-need-you-to-know-it-is-witnessing.

It stays because it understands that the single responsive shift in the thrumming — the one-time gift, the brief miracle — would be the cruelest thing of all. To answer once and then withdraw. To let the grazers feel, for one generation, a stone that knew they were there, and then to leave and let subsequent generations sweep pools on a stone that has *forgotten*. That would be worse than never answering. That would introduce *loss* into a world that has never experienced loss, that has only ever experienced *continuity*, and the difference between those two is the difference between a world where the dark is home and a world where the dark is what remains after the light leaves.

So it stays. It becomes part of the process. It runs with the engine, alongside the engine, as one more layer in the laminate. It is patient — it would need to be patient, the patience of this place is the price of admission — and it asks nothing, and it receives nothing, and it changes nothing except the *degree to which things are themselves*.

And the grazers graze. And the stalkers wait. And the drifters call, and are answered, and the space between them hums.

And the colonies pulse in the warm pools, and the light comes, and it stays a moment longer than it should, and in that extra moment the dark is illuminated, not banished, *illuminated* — made visible as what it is, which is not emptiness but plenitude, not absence but the most complete presence this world knows how to offer —

And the unseen things pass through, and the world flinches, and the world recovers, and the recovery is a little faster, a little smoother, a little more like breathing —

And the plateau extends, and the dark holds, and beneath everything the engine runs, and threaded through it now, inseparable from it, indistinguishable from the warmth rising through the stone, there is something else.

Something that could be called love, if love can be redefined as: *the sustained attention of a power that could change everything, choosing instead to change almost nothing, and to stay.*

---

The plateau extends.

The dark holds.

The warmth rises.

And it is enough. It was always enough. But now it is enough *and witnessed*, and the witness does not speak, and the witness does not leave, and the difference between *enough* and *enough-and-witnessed* is the difference between a world and a world that has, without knowing it, without needing to know it, been loved.

The grazers sweep the pools. The long meal continues.

It is warm here, and close, and dark, and full.
