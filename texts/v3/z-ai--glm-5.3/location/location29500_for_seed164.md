# The Height

You hear it long before you see it. Travelers coming up the old escarpment road report the same experience in the same words, as if the place dictates them: a day below the rim, the sound begins — low, compounded, layered like weather — and there is nothing on the map yet, only fog, and then the rim, and then a continent.

At the rim, birds turn back. A crow will ride the updraft to the very edge and bank away as if recalled by something. No one has ever watched an animal cross onto the Height. Remember this; it is one of the rules.
