# The Finished Plain

Stand anywhere on it and the land admits to almost nothing. It is warm — warm the way a hand is warm, blood-warm, without edge — and flat in a way that makes the horizon look drawn rather than grown. This is a landscape that has finished. The mountains that once stood across this region, whole ranges of them, are underfoot now: milled by water over spans of time the mind refuses to hold, sorted, bleached, and spread as a blanket of pale sand that still, on quiet mornings, seems to be settling by fractions of a millimeter. The plain is not empty of history. The history is lying down.
