**The Weeper — a tuned cistern in the made lowlands**

The first thing to understand about this country is that there is no ground in it. Everything you could stand on, for as far as the fog goes, was carried here by hands — tamped rubble, ash lenses, potsherd, bone, lime, the crushed brick of earlier centuries, laid down and built over and leveled and built over again until the land forgot it had ever been anything else. Dig anywhere and you find not soil but a slow city on its side. Nothing grows out of first nature here; the mud itself is a made mud, brick-silt and mortar gone soft.

In the middle of the fog-flat, where the made ground sags slightly toward the old water, a low plinth rises
