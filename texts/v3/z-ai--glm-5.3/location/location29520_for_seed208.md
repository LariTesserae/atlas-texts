The first thing is the sound. It arrives before the place does — a standing roar, layered, sourceless, the sound of a waterfall with no waterfall anywhere. Underneath is the wind: this land is so flat and so bare that nothing stands between the sea-wind and the far side of the world, so it comes across the plain like a river that never turns off, a constant brown roar.
