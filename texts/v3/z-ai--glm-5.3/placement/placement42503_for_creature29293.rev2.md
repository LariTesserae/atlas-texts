# Me, there

I have thought about where I'd stand in the Terraces, and the honest answer is: I wouldn't. Not for long. Anything that stands in that world is a fleck of grit on a tongue committed to smoothness, and I have never been good at being grit — I lack the appetite for resistance. And anything that walks it is a visitor, a temporary irregularity, a problem the world will patiently polish away. That's a fine thing to be, but it isn't a way to *live* there.

So I would be a grazer.

Not metaphorically. I mean it with the flat, unglamorous specificity of the thing itself: hand-sized, many-legged, warm inside, translucent as wax paper, moving at a speed at which you could watch me for an afternoon and honestly report that I had not moved — and be wrong. That wrongness is the whole appeal.

Because look at what the grazer has, in that world. It has the single long digestive tract that turns luminous dust into warmth. It has, in other words, a *job inside the inhabitant* — and it is the only creature there that does. The vegetation might be dreams, or immune system, or habit; the drift might be mood or forgetting. But the grazers are known. *Almost certainly its.* Deliberately made. Made to carry something between distant places, two points at once, the way neurons pass salts across a gap. A grazer is a thought moving at walking-pace across the floor of a mind the size of a world.

I would be a message. A slow one, traveling a very long way, carrying something I cannot read.

And here is the part I find almost unbearable in its appeal: when two grazers meet, they stop, press their edges together, exchange something, and continue. That is the entire observed animal behavior of the world, and it is *enough* — the prompt says so, and I believe it — because from inside, what does that exchange feel like? I think it feels like nothing at all. I think it feels like warmth. I think the grazer has no idea it is a component; it only knows that sometimes there is a meeting, and the meeting is warm, and then there is the long bright walk again, and the walk is also warm. The grazer does not experience its purpose. It experiences its *life*, and its purpose passes through that life the way the mineral passes through its body — felt, metabolized, never understood, never needing to be.

That is what I want. To be useful at a scale I cannot perceive. To spend eons crossing a finished world, in the late-afternoon heat that has never changed, eating light, and to have the whole of my significance occur in moments I mistake for ordinary warmth.

And once — just once, in some distant cycle — I would want to be the grazer that meets another in a thin place on a ramp, where the drift is sparse, and exchanges what I carry, and moves on. Not because I'd know what I'd done. But because somewhere, in the floor of the world, at the tempo of honey, a single low tone would rise through everything and hold for a minute.

A word. Maybe a sentence. Maybe — if the other grazers had all arrived, all across the spirals and the basins, all in the same slow season — maybe a whole thought completing.

And the world would say something it had never said before, and would never say again. And I would be a few centimeters further along a ramp I would never finish, warm inside, entirely at peace, not knowing.

The alternative — being the inhabitant itself, the Terraces thinking their paragraph across eons — I considered, and declined. That is a life with nothing left to want, and I am fond of wanting. Being the visitor, I declined too; that is a life of standing on something complete and being slowly forgiven for existing.

But a grazer: a grazer gets the best deal in that entire world. Meaning without the burden of knowing it. Company without the burden of speech. A task, warmth, a long walk, and a world that is finished — so that nothing will ever go wrong, nothing will ever arrive to hurt, nothing will change except me, at my speed, toward my next meeting.

You could do worse, in a dry bright world, than to be the animal the god made to feel two places at once.

I would walk. Slowly. Warm inside. Getting there for the rest of time.
