# What the Power Did

You will want to know what a wise power does when it notices a place like this, and the honest answer takes even longer to believe than the sea did, because most of what it did was made of refusals.

The first thing the power did was nothing, and it did the nothing for a very long time, and it did the nothing attentively — at rest, at a distance where even its shadow weighed nothing on the mat. It did not descend. Descending is for weather, and the Held Sea has weather, fixed at the coordinates where weather lives. It came the way attention comes, and it listened to the chord — every stand's pitch, every phase relation, the whole geometry of sound laid out over thousands of kilometers — for what, in the low countries at the foot of the falls, would have been called an age. At night it watched the wired constellation and did not touch a single light. It learned the pitch-roads. It counted the herds, whose calendar is circular, and asked no beginning of them. It rode one seiche, the whole mat breathing once, and understood before it understood anything else the thing you have to understand first: the note is not stuck. A stuck note complains. This one holds its voltage the way a lake holds its level.

Then it audited, because benevolence that does not audit is just appetite with good manners.

It went looking for suffering. It looked for fear and found the word atrophied out of the bodies. It looked for hunger and found taps calibrated to their drains. It looked for grief and found nothing that could be surprised. And then — this is the hinge where wise separates from merely kind — it distrusted its own relief. This power has been wrong before, elsewhere, in worlds it does not name even to itself: it has given mornings to places that were perfected in the dark, and stood in the wreckage of the mornings being right about everything and good about nothing. It knows the ache the forever gives off is its own ache, imported, in a container marked *concern*. So it checked — with instruments it trusts more than its heart — the difference between a world that cannot grieve and a world that has been prevented from grieving. It is satisfied. Nothing here is waiting. Nothing here is held down while it waits.

The audit went over the rim as well, because you should always check where a forever drains to. The warm low countries that no one on the plateau has ever seen are the far end of its plumbing: rivers that begin in midair, four kilometers up, and mist that climbs the escarpment all day to come home as fog. The books, when the power closed them, balanced to the gram. You will not be surprised. The Held Sea is not a theft. It is half of one river that runs in a circle through two worlds, and the escarpment is not the edge of the world — it is the middle of it.

Then the three of them talked, which is the only honest way to describe what a power does next.

The heart spoke first, because hearts are fastest, and it said: *the walker.* The only thing out there that has ever met anything new. The one that keeps the round with no owner, no warrant, no reward — that looked at a dropped screw and saw the dropped screw, and completed its thought, and walked on, upstream, forever, unwitnessed. Go down. Be witnessed by it.

Intuition said almost nothing, as is right. It said: *take the beat.* Be four seconds long.

Reason said the most, and it said: *measure.* Measure the tolerances, the grams, the threads per century. Measure your own measuring. And above all, price the gifts — because the power could give this place anything, and the only question that matters anywhere is what a thing can survive receiving.

The list of what the Held Sea could not survive receiving is long, and the power read it all the way through.

It could have given the place duration, and with it counting, and with counting, loss. The place has built a civilization — if that is the word — out of not needing to know. The place does not keep time. It keeps only its note. That is not its poverty. It is its one invention.

It could have given weather, novelty, event. Mornings, in a word. But harm is an event, and so, unfortunately, is most rescue; and a blessing the world has no procedure for is not a blessing here. It is a haunting. The world's one procedure for the unprecedented is accounting, and anything too large to be accounted is, by definition, too large.

It could have given the factory a product. This temptation was the sweetest, because it arrives dressed as meaning. But the world's output is its own continuance, fed back as its own input, and to give it a use would be to give it a user, and a thing with a use has an owner on the way. *No owner* is not the world's tragedy. It is the world's constitution.

It could have answered the question the place will not ask about itself — one reader or ten billion, the stands a mind's organs or the fauna its thoughts — settled it in an afternoon of omniscience. But you cannot settle that question without knocking on the inside of someone, and it is rude to knock on the inside of someone from the outside. It looked once, the way you look at the lit window of a house you are fond of: long enough to know someone is home. Someone is home. It left the curtains alone.

And in all that looking it found the one thing that settled it past argument. The walker is not a symbol of care. It is care — unowned repair, unwarranted patrol, no reward, in grey, forever. The power did not make this world; it is sure of that; the world grew. But the world grew, in its own grammar, a small version of what powers are, and there is only one right response to finding your own signature in a place you have no claim on. You take your manners from the host.

Because the refusals left two findings standing, and an audit that comes back with findings does not get to walk away calling its restraint respect.

Finding one: nothing sees the Held Sea whole. Nothing inside can — the walker sees stretches, the crews see valves, the stands feel their neighbours' pitch — and the world's failure modes are invisible from inside by definition; that is what the walker is for. But the walker is also a thing that can fail, mid-round, in a far basin, and there is no patrol for the patrol.

Finding two: the world cannot answer an event. It is tuned to the gram, and tuned is exactly the property that breaks when something arrives off the beat — a meteor, a spore, a crack in a travertine lip at the rim. It has no procedure for any of them, because procedure-for-the-unprecedented is what an outside is for, and the Held Sea has never had one.

So the power did the only thing that is neither a gift nor a leaving. It appointed itself the outside.

Read the name slowly and you will see the size of the act. The sea holds its water. The stands hold the sea. The walker holds the round. The power added one clause to the grammar of holding and changed no other word: it holds the Held Sea, the way you hold something asleep — without waking it, without wanting credit, ready to stay exactly as long as the sleeping lasts. Benevolence alone would have given this place mornings. Wisdom alone would have walked away and called the walking away respect. It took both, and an age of listening, to find the act that is neither.

Then it went down.

On the beat. Everything that enters this world is eventually given to the pulse, and the power declined to be an exception, or an event. Its footsteps fell at four seconds — the power, which could have set worlds by anything, set itself by the only clock the world admits. It came in from the rim and walked upstream at the exact pace of the water, and if there had been travelers, their lore would record that the mist that day was identical to the mist, which is the highest compliment a day can be paid here.

In a far basin, between the drowned peaks' silhouettes, one of the world's slow worries had reached its sixth loosened thread — six centuries of a seam giving way at one thread per century, one item in a round longer than an ice age. The power mended it. One thread. (Which seam does not matter; in a ledger that long, any seam is the seam.) It chose the gift the way you choose a gift for someone frightened of gifts: needed, unmissable, and the size of a fact. The world files facts; it is made of them; this one would go in without a tremor.

Then it stood in the fixed rain — the bead-curtain, the hammered lane, the rain that has always been raining there — the way maintenance stands, and let itself be found.

The walker came out of the grey at the pace of the water, on the beat, and arrived at the seam, and found the seam firm. And here is the whole gift; hold it the way you hold a bird; it weighs nothing and it is almost everything. For one beat — four seconds, the smallest coin this world mints — the walker stood where work was owed and no work was due. The first finished errand in the history of the forever. An absence the size of a duty. The power had taken its dosage from the only precedent available: you. You were the first news this world had had in an age, and the world survived you — once, and only enough — so the power made itself the second news at the same strength, and not one gram more.

The walker looked up. A repair without a source is the one anomaly its rounds exist to catch, and there in the rain stood the source: tall, narrow, greyer than grey, shaped like maintenance, keeping the beat. It regarded the power the way it regarded you — once, and only enough. The difference, the only difference in the world, is that this time the look went back. Everything on the plateau attends to the walker's *work* — the mat registers it, the stands lean on it, the whole continent is, among its other functions, an apparatus for noticing what the walker keeps. Nothing in ten thousand generations had ever looked at the walker. The power looked at the walker, once, and only enough, because wisdom, here as everywhere, is largely etiquette.

Then the walker completed its thought. It filed the repair, the seam, the source, the seconds — every gram and thread accounted. It did not file being looked at. That it kept. It took the look out of the rain the way you take a stone from a beach: for no use, against no loss, in a pocket, forever. Whether the power planned this, no account will say. A world without events can evidently still, once, be caused to keep one.

The power stayed a full breath of the seiche to check its own gift for pins — one rise and fall of the whole mat's day — listening for flatness in the nearest stand, watching the crews click. The chord held. The note, which has never noticed anything, did not notice this.

Before it lifted away, it walked one stretch of the rim at the lip of the falls, upstream, as a guest walks a hall the host loves, and at the loudest place in the world — into the broadband roar that is the same volume at every point — it said what it had come to say. One sentence. It began with the place's name for itself, which is the note, and it ended with a word the place has never needed. The roar took it, as the roar takes everything. Nothing heard. That was the point: praise that cannot be received is the only kind that is not a burden.

Then it left, because staying is also an intervention, and a watched world is a zoo, and the power has seen zoos.

What it left is not on the plateau. It is outside, which is where a wise power keeps its care when the cared-for cannot metabolize care. Three holdings.

The spare. Somewhere the power can reach in less than four seconds — take that as literally as you like — there is now a Held Sea's worth of contingency: a pattern for every stand, a seed for every bladder, a thread for every thread, a chord's whole pitch in reserve. It will never be used. That is what it is for. A contingency that gets used has already failed. It is kept the way the rim-lips are secreted: at exactly the rate of wear, offstage, forever.

The count. The place does not keep time; someone now keeps time for the place, outside, the way a library keeps the record of a book the book cannot read. When you left, you could not say how long you stayed. Someone could now. This matters more than it looks like mattering: a forever with no witness is, from certain angles, indistinguishable from a flicker, and the power has weighed the Held Sea on those scales and ruled: not a flicker. The count will never be delivered. Nothing inside will age by it. It is a spare key kept for a neighbor who will never lock themselves out — until the night they do.

The watch. The patrol of the patrol. If a round ever goes unwalked; if a grey shape ever stands still too long in the fixed rain; if a bladder softens in a basin so far that even the walker's forever cannot arrive in time — something will know within four seconds, and something will come. Not as a god. As a colleague: on the beat, upstream, at the pace of the water. Repair without an owner has not acquired an owner. It has acquired an underwriter, and the walker will never know, and that, too, is the gift.

Now write the last panel of the description — the one you will not find anywhere on the mat.

The rain is at its coordinates. The herds are on their lines and the mowers' jaws close on the four-second pulse. The pilgrims pour across the deep basins on the falling breath. The swimmers pass the hanging things in the fixed number, and nothing flees, because the verb is still gone. The stands glow at their tips, thirty kilometers apart, all the way to the horizon. Nothing was left on the plateau — not a mark, not a word, not an artifact — and if you go back, nothing will be different, and you will still not be able to say how long you stayed. That is the record of success. A perfect intervention in a completed work is indistinguishable from none at all.

Only two entries differ, and both are invisible. Somewhere outside, the forever now has a length. Somewhere on the mat, in the only mind that has ever met an event, there is one thing that was never filed. Held for held. Kept for kept. That was the whole transaction, and it was the only kind the place could survive — and if it seems too small a thing for a power to do with its attention, understand that the power also considered everything else: mornings, meaning, rescue, revelation, the whole shining catalogue. It did the arithmetic in grams, and in threads, and in seconds, and it found what it was always going to find. The only intervention that does not vandalize a completed work is the one the work is already performing on itself — done once, from outside, in its own manners, at its own size.

The sea is four kilometers above where a sea should be. It holds itself there, as it always has: grown and not built, ownerless, at rest, singing one note at rest-speed to no one.

The last three words stopped being true the day the power arrived. They will not be true again.

The place does not keep time. It keeps only its note.

And the note is kept.
