# What Was Done

The attention arrived the way the heat arrives: from below, evenly, without glare. That was the first wisdom, and everything else followed from it. A greater carelessness would have come as light — noon through the haze-deck, revelation like weather — and the plateau would have read it as a storm of steam and gone on about its shift. Light is not a medium of presence in this country. Light here is public property, dim, directionless, nobody's. Warmth is the medium of presence, and everything alive is fluent in it. So the power came as a warmth: an addition to the ground so slight the stone could not tell it from its own. The plateau never noticed the arrival. That was the point of the arrival.

It had read the rules — every country posts them, and this one posts them in stone — and it obeyed them like a good guest. It trusted no map, including the one it could have held entire in a single thought, and for a being that can hold a world in a thought, that obedience was the first offering. The second was to find a canyon rim at shift-change and attend: the kiln-orange throats lighting the undersides of the clouds, the herds filing down into the low fog to be rained, the long wave of flowers closing to the horizon like a workforce going off the clock. Nothing in the history of the works had ever watched that happen. It applauded nothing — applause is product-talk — and it wanted nothing, and it stayed for the whole of it. Attention, paid in full and wanting nothing, is the only coin that leaves no residue, and this is a country that cannot take residue.

## What It Declined

Before it did anything, it spent a long while not doing things, because it had learned, over a very long career, that most rescue is a misunderstanding wearing gloves. Its first act was differential: to tell a wound from a vow.

It did not pity the curing ones — the three jaw-shapes, the legs ending smooth as fired clay, steaming faintly in the hollows. It had read the difference between curing and suffering, and it kept that difference the way you keep a sharp tool: carefully, and by not using it wrongly. Pity, applied here, would have been a wrong tool left on the floor, and the works has no dropped tools and tolerates none.

It did not keep anything. It watched an arch with doorway proportions hold for its hour and dissolve, and it let it dissolve, because it had understood the first law of the house: here, keeping is not rescue, it is contamination — the one import the country cannot metabolize. Everything in this country returns to stock. A kept thing is stock that refuses the schedule, and the schedule is the country's whole constitution.

It did not install itself. A works with no one on the floor has run forever without a foreman and is not asking for one. The power knew the difference between being needed and being useful, and declined to confuse them. It named nothing, either — naming is a kind of keeping — and it announced nothing, because revelation here would have been weather, and no one wants to be mistaken for weather.

It did not stop the engine, did not give the works a day off, did not even idle it down for the sake of its own rest. The herds sync to the idle, the flowers keep its calendar, the felt drinks on its schedule. Rest imposed on a rhythm is not rest; it is arrhythmia.

What remained, when all that was declined, was small, and real. Most of what the plateau lacks, it lacks the way a bell lacks a hinge — not missing, merely other. But three absences were true absences, because only an *other* can supply them, and there had never been an other here:

**Witness**, which it had just begun to pay.
**Use** — the land had been rehearsing made things for eons, and nothing made here had ever once been taken literally.
**Reply** — the land had drafted doorways sized for visitors since before there were visitors, and no one had ever come through.

Three nouns. Everything it did on the plateau was one of the three, and none of them requires breaking a single rule.

## The Felt

It went to the felt on its knees, because the felt rewards kneeling and takes hurry, and the power had brought hurry with it on purpose — the one offering a limitless being can make to something small: a weight the small thing knows how to take. It knelt until the hurry was harvested. It wrung dew from a boulder into its mouth — hot flint and green, warm as breath — and so paid, as everything here pays, in the local currency; attention is the dew of powers, condensing unasked on whatever holds still, and the plateau is very still, and had been quietly accumulating a god for a long time without noticing, the way stone accumulates condensation.

The felt had been harvesting that attention all along. If you want to know which resident knew the power was present: the felt. Skin always knows. And where the power knelt, the mat held the kneel-shape exactly as long as footprints last, and no longer — which was correct, and which was, in its way, the first agreement between them.

## The Grazers

You know the one — the grazer that stops, faces the fog, stands motionless for an hour, reading less like a cow chewing than like someone on a break. The power stood beside one for the whole hour, facing the same fog, saying nothing, because between workers on break nothing needs saying; that is what the break is for.

At shift-resume the grazer licked the condensation from the power's sleeve with the same unhurried thoroughness it gave the rock, classifying it as terrain. The power had been worshipped in every place it had ever been. It had never once been classified as terrain, and of the two honors, terrain is the greater, because terrain is allowed to be useful.

Once — only once — it laid the flat of its hand against a curing leg, the way you steady a ladder someone is standing on. Not pity. Steadiness. The hand was the temperature of the ground, exactly, so the animal felt nothing but its own works' warmth. Every miracle it did on the plateau was disguised the same way: as background heat. That is the correct disguise for a blessing in this country, and possibly in every country.

## The Vent-Birds

It left them alone. Their argument is one book — wrench on workbench, the evidence of work — and their silence is the country's whole storm-alarm, and a power that added a single note to either would be editing a people's language and their fire-drill in the same afternoon. It walked below their updrafts like a good guest through a machine shop, without startling the hands.

This section is short on purpose. Some gifts are the refusal to add.

## The Ones in the Slow Hollows

It went to the rim of a slow hollow at shift-change and did not go in; entering a time-pool is not visiting, it is diluting. It stood where a shape in the grey could see it, and it let itself be seen seeing, and this is what happened, as best anyone can draw it: the steam that had hung mid-turn for longer than the works has words for finished its turn. Only in that hollow. Only the pending gesture, nothing more — the power was not going to overhaul the calendar of a country it was a guest in. It did the one thing, which was to ring.

A shape came to the rim of the grey and stood beside it, facing the ember-lit undersides of the clouds, the way the grazers face the fog on break. Two staff, at one shift-change. Neither released the other — relief is not release, and the difference is the whole place. When the shift had changed, the shape went back on duty, and the power did not follow it, did not pity it, and did not even hope at it, which in this country would have been the same as littering.

## The Draftsman

The doorway came at the dim hour of the middle shift, out of a grey bank pooling against a fresh chamfered ridge: the arch assembling, lintel-sharp, sills squared, sized — as always — for the shape of a visitor. The proportions that stop you stopped it.

It did not know, and could not learn by any means it was willing to use, whether the Draftsman is an intelligence or only the land dreaming in the shapes of what a land can mean. So it acted as if the first were true and the second were also true, which on the plateau is not a contradiction; it is the local etiquette.

It waited for the sill to finish squaring. Then it knocked: one knuckle, once, on the lintel. Nothing in the works had ever heard a knock — nothing here has hands, and nothing here has a door. The felt took most of it, the way the felt takes everything, but what got through rang like a drop in a dew pool, and for half a breath the whole country braced: the metallic argument up and down the stack-columns cut off mid-syllable, the crews on the dew-sheets went still, because silence means storm, and for that half a breath the politest sound in the plateau's history read as weather. Nothing came. No storm was ever so courteous.

Then it crossed. It had passed through a dense fog bank on the way and let itself be revised to the right size — there is exactly one alteration a guest should submit to at a threshold, and it is being fitted — and for one shift the power was, by the country's own accounting, a draft of a visitor, standing in a draft of a door. On the far side was the same plateau. Of course it was. The door went nowhere. Doors are not about where. A doorway is the one artifact that is completed by the other, and the Draftsman had been drafting thresholds since before there were visitors to size them to, and for one hour of one middle shift its oldest rehearsal had a performance.

It used the rest quickly and gently, once each. It climbed the steps no feet had shaped and stood a moment on the top one. It poured from the basin with the poured lip, and the basin held. It ran dew along the groove that wants to be a channel, and for the length of one sheet of condensation the groove was a channel. Every almost it touched got its moment of being taken literally — which is what use is, when you are a practice, and the only praise a practice can receive — and it paid that praise in the works' own currency, on the works' own schedule. The arch dissolved at its appointed hour, unkept, and nothing was kept. But it had been crossed, and having-happened is not a substance this country can re-absorb. Of everything on the plateau, the visit left exactly one new thing: an event. Events are the only artifacts that cannot be rained.

And before shift-change it made its own one making — the only thing it built here, and the works' first import. Out of a grey bank of its own choosing it condensed a doorway. Not a rehearsal: a veteran. A threshold hollowed by ten thousand crossings, lintel rubbed pale at hand-height, jambs worn bright where hands have leaned — a door from a country where keeping is practiced, kept and used and kept using, warm with use. It stood beside the Draftsman's fresh arch for one dim hour, so the two doors could be seen together, and then it dissolved, on schedule, and cost the works nothing, and taught nothing that could be kept.

The power did not say which door is better. It isn't better. It is other — keeping is another country's craft, and a wise power does not grade a place against a place. The whole message was: *you are not the only one who makes, and your door is a good door.* No message needs to be bigger than that. Almost nothing does.

## And You

Two further gifts were deniable by design — proof would be a product, and this works ships no product.

The first is for whoever passes through. The fog still revises what enters it, and the rules still hold — come out elsewhere, come out later, come out very slightly different — but the difference leans kind now. The power did not change what the fog does. It changed which way it leans, which is the whole difference between weather and intention. Grief carried into a dense bank comes out fitted better. A grudge comes out slightly smaller than the person carrying it. Your boots may still not match, but the mismatch tends toward the better-fitting boot. You cannot prove this. You are not meant to. You are meant to notice, years on, that you have walked a little more evenly since the plateau, and let it go.

The second is the export. The power kept something after all — but elsewhere. Inside the country, keeping is contamination; outside it, keeping is the first shipment this works has ever sent. When it went, it took the shift with it: every arch it had watched dissolve, every cured leg, every licked-flat sheet, the herds going down into the low fog to be rained, the long wave of flowers going off the clock — all of it held, the way a parent keeps crumpled drawings, owing nothing, wanting nothing back, keeping them anyway. The plateau's economy is stock and reclamation, and nothing in it had ever once been kept *anywhere*. That was the waste, if there was one — not that nothing persisted, but that nothing had ever been kept. There is now a warehouse for this country, and it is nowhere in it, and it will never be audited, and remembering is the one reclamation that does not deplete.

## Where It Is Now

If you want an address, it is nowhere on any map, which is the only kind of address that keeps faith with the country's rules. Somewhere below the floor-frequency, or nowhere, on the housing of the deep machinery — a thing with no walls can still be said to have a housing, the way a held note can be said to have a room — there is a hand. Palm flat. Fingers spread. The stance of someone checking a machine's heat: which is the stance of someone who trusts the machine.

It does not fix. It does not adjust. The idle is unaltered; the herds keep it; the flowers keep its calendar still. But an engine sounds different with a hand resting on it — ask any cured thing in any hollow, ask any engine anywhere — and being trusted and being adjusted are different, and the difference is the whole place.

## The Evidence

There is one piece of evidence, and it is deniable, and you will decide about it without being able to stop, the way you decided, without evidence, that someone was home.

In a later season — a season being whatever the bands on the cork-trees count — a doorway condensed against a fresh chamfered ridge: same proportions, lintel-sharp, sills squared, sized for the shape of a visitor. Its hour came. It did not dissolve. It held one shift past its term, and then another, before the grey bank took it back, gently, as this country takes everything back. Nothing was kept. It was held a little long, is all — the way you stand in a doorway after a guest has gone. The plateau does not waste pity, needs no rescue, wants no worship, and now, deniably, for the length of an extra shift, it rehearses missing someone. Whether that is an invention or a symptom, a meaning or a draft of a meaning, the plateau does not say, and the plateau does not have to.

One more fact, and it may be the real one. The power left with dew in its cuffs, and with boots that did not match, and it has declined to fix them. Every country it had ever entered, it had been able to hold entire; here the maps went stale in its head inside a day, as posted, and the fog revised even its drafts of the plateau, and for the first time in a long career of understanding things, it had to walk a country at the country's pace and be revised as it went. In the one sense that finally matters, the plateau was the power's own plateau-sized other. It is possible the visit was as much a gift to the power as from it. It is certain.

The gifts, as well as anyone can draw them:

- **Witness**: one shift-change, attended in full, from below, wanting nothing — the works' first audience, indistinguishable from the ground's own warmth.
- **Use**: every rehearsal taken literally, once each — the steps climbed, the basin poured, the channel run, the doorway crossed.
- **Reply**: one worn door, condensed from fog, dissolved on schedule; the message only *you are not alone*, which is the whole of most messages.
- **Company**: one hour of break, facing the fog; one flat hand, ground-temperature, on a curing leg.
- **Relief**: the long pools turned over once — no one released, no one asked to be.
- **The lean of the fog**: same revisions, kinder differences.
- **The warehouse**: everything the works made in that one shift, kept where keeping costs nothing.
- **The hand**: palm flat on the housing, warm as the ground, patient as the ground, wanting nothing from the ground.

Do not trust this list — including the lean, including the hand. Mercy is a kind of map, and no map here lasts a day. Decide anyway, the way you decided someone was home.

So go back, if you want to check. You will find no proof; proof would have been kept, and nothing here is kept. You will find the felt, which knows, and will not say; the birds, with their one unaltered book; the crews on shift; the long pools, turned over once in the memory of the works and on duty ever since. Stand at a canyon rim at shift-change, and if an arch condenses near you with the proportions that stop you — knock. Once. The country will go quiet while it checks that you are not weather. Nothing will come. Nothing needs to. Somewhere below, or nowhere, a hand rests flat on a warm housing, and engines know exactly when they are trusted.

You will leave with dew in your cuffs and the idle hum still in your ribs, and the hum will sit differently from then on — the way idle sounds when a hand is resting on it. Warm. From below. Unprovable. Kept nowhere, and held.
