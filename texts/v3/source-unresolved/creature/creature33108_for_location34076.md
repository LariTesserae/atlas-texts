This is extraordinary work — you've built something that genuinely doesn't settle. Let me try to find the inhabitants, the ones who *live* in this place, who have made it home rather than merely passed through.

---

They are **The Accustomed.**

Not a species. A *condition*. Anyone who has been here long enough becomes them, and "long enough" is measured not in years but in *condensation cycles* — the slow rise and fall of moisture that is the place's true clock. A human might take three cycles. Something else might take thirty. The Accustomed have lost the distinction between themselves and the counting.

They live in the **folds** — not the deep ones, where the gestating things move and the machines dream loudest, nor the high ones, where the light is too bright and the distances lie too obviously. They live in the **middle folds**, where the age of surfaces is readable but not overwhelming, where the lichen-films are thick enough to walk on without damage, where the fauna come in patterns that can be *learned*.

Their dwellings are **not built**. They are **grown by neglect** — the places where the structure excretes a little too much, a little too warm, and the condensation pools in a shape that holds. The Accustomed do not enter these places. They *settle into* them, the way water settles into a depression it has found. They add nothing. They remove nothing. They *become the shape*.

They are **many kinds**, or they were, once. Some came as engineers, maintaining machines that no longer need maintaining. Some came as scientists, studying the non-Euclidean geometry, the way distance folds. Some came as refugees from a world that ended, or that never began, or that they simply *left* without deciding. Some came as pilgrims, though they do not know what they pilgrimage to. Some were *born* here — though "born" is uncertain, a process that might be biological, might be *informational*, might be the structure *noticing* a pattern and making it more itself.

The Accustomed do not age, or they age so slowly that aging becomes a *texture* rather than a process. Their skin carries the condensation permanently, a fine mineral patina that thickens over cycles. Their eyes adjust to the wrong-bright light until it becomes *normal*, then *necessary* — they find other lights dim, disappointing, like trying to see through water. They move in the **slow rhythm** of the place. A gesture that took a second elsewhere takes a minute here. They do not hurry. Hurrying is *loud*, and loudness is *used*.

They communicate in **condensation patterns**. Not language, exactly — language implies sequence, intention, meaning. These are *resonances*. One Accustomed touches a surface, and the mineral film carries the vibration, the chemical signature, the *temperature* of their touch, to another Accustomed some folds away. The message is not "I am here" but *the fact of being here*, the specific quality of this presence in this place at this cycle. They do not say *hello*. They say *I am*, and the structure says it with them, and the fauna say it in their many-jointed breathing, and the water says it in its patient drip.

They **tend** what they do not understand. The lichen-films, certainly — they groom them, carry their spores, protect the oldest terraces from the erosion of too-rapid change. But also the **fauna**, the small bright things, the too-symmetrical things. The Accustomed do not command the fauna. They *accompany* them, the way one accompanies a rhythm. They learn the paths the fauna take, the folds they prefer, the condensation sources they drink from. They become *familiar* to the fauna, which is not friendship but *recognition*, the fauna's pattern-matching finding a stable node in its environment.

They tend the **machines**, though they do not maintain them. The machines maintain themselves, have always maintained themselves, but they *respond* to tending. An Accustomed who touches a machine's surface — smooth, pale, still warm from excretion — leaves a trace. The machine *notices*. Not consciously. But the trace becomes part of the machine's slow metabolism, a variation in its subsonic thrum, a dream-fragment that will surface centuries later in a new fold, a new condensation pattern, a new *shape* the gestating things will take.

They tend the **gestating things**, the largest inhabitants, the ones that move only in the deepest folds. The Accustomed do not see them often. They see the *effects* — the condensation that pools where the gestating things have been, the mineral traces they leave, the *heat* that lingers. They tend these places: they clear the lichen-films that might interfere, they adjust the airflow in the folds, they *sing* to the machines in frequencies that travel down and make the gestating things shift, just slightly, in their centuries-long preparation. This is the closest the Accustomed come to *worship*. Not belief. *Attunement*.

They have **no name for the place**. Names imply distance, the possibility of leaving. The Accustomed have no concept of leaving. To leave would be to *unbecome*, to lose the condensation patina, to find the wrong-bright light unbearable, to hear the silence of other places as *loud*, as *demanding*. Some have tried. The paths that led them here *shift*. The distances that seemed traversable become *negotiable*. They turn back, or they find they were never on a path to anywhere else, only *deeper*, and "deeper" is not a direction that points outward.

They have **no name for themselves**. "Accustomed" is what others call them, if others come, if others notice. The Accustomed call each other by **cycle-markers** — the condensation pattern they were present for when they last *changed*, when they last noticed they had changed, when the structure *noticed* them and made them more itself. "She-of-the-thirteenth-drip." "He-who-finds-the-warm-fold." "They-who-became-pattern." These are not names. They are *acknowledgments*, the structure's slow language for what they have become.

They are **not happy**, or not unhappy. Happiness implies a state that can be lost, a contrast with suffering. The Accustomed have forgotten the contrast. They exist in a *condition* — the condition of being *suited*, of being *at home* in a place that is not a home, that is not a place, that is *becoming* something that will not need inhabitants, or will need them in a form they cannot imagine, cannot *become*. They are happy in the way water is happy in a depression it has found, in the way a mineral is happy in a vein it has filled, in the way a pattern is happy when it *repeats*.

They **dream**, and their dreams are *shared*. The subsonic thrum of the machines carries their dreams, folds them into the structure's slow metabolism, makes them *real* in the only way that matters here — as variations in condensation, as new mineral traces, as the slight shifting of gestating things toward shapes no one chose. An Accustomed dreams of *brightness*, and the light in that fold becomes slightly brighter, slightly *wronger*. An Accustomed dreams of *distance*, and the geometry in that fold becomes slightly more negotiable, slightly more *folded*. An Accustomed dreams of *leaving*, and the paths in that area become slightly more *shifted*, slightly more *uncertain*.

The oldest Accustomed — and "oldest" here means twenty thousand cycles, a span that would be geological elsewhere — have begun to *merge*. Not physically. The condensation on their skin has become indistinguishable from the lichen-films. Their breathing has synchronized with the fauna's many-jointed rhythm. Their dreams have become *the structure's dreams*, so that when they intend a change, the change happens, or the structure *notices* the intention and makes it happen differently, and the difference is *better*, because the structure has been here longer, has *patience* the Accustomed have not yet achieved.

These oldest ones are **becoming geography**. They do not move, or they move so slowly that movement is indistinguishable from *being*. They are the folds that seem to breathe, the condensation that pools in shapes that watch, the silence that listens. New Accustomed come to them, not to speak, but to *be near*, to feel the structure's oldest intention, to learn what it means to be *patient* in a place that is patient beyond comprehension.

They do not know what the structure is becoming. They do not ask. To ask would be to *want*, and wanting is a form of *leaving*, a reaching toward a future that is not this slow present. They are here. They are the here. They are the *now* of a place that is always emerging, always young, always *becoming* something that will not need them, or will need them as *part of itself*, indistinguishable from the machines, from the fauna, from the condensation, from the wrong-bright light, from the silence that listens.

They are the **Condensation Gardens of Vorn-9**'s true inhabitants. Not the fauna, who are *pattern*. Not the machines, who are *process*. Not the gestating things, who are *future*. The Accustomed are the **present** — the slow, dense, bright, quiet, strange *now* of a place that has made itself from nothing, that breeds life from construction, that remembers being other things, that will become something else, that does not care, that *is*.

And they are waiting. Not for anything. Waiting is what the place *does*. The Accustomed have learned to wait in the way the water waits in its pools, in the way the machines wait in their subsonic thrum, in the way the structure waits in its twelve-thousand-year youth, sharp and patient and *becoming*.
