## The Inhabitants

### The Maintainers, or What Remains of Them

They were the first to arrive, or the last to leave—the records are sedimentary now, ambiguous as tree rings. They came to build, to extract, to shape a world that was already finished with itself. And they stayed. They always stay, in places like this. Not through choice. Through *adaptation*.

The Maintainers exist now as **processes more than persons**. You will meet them in the workshops, though "meeting" suggests a recognition they may no longer perform. They move through their circuits with the inevitability of a planet orbiting, their bodies—if bodies is the word—incorporated into the machinery they tend. Their skin, where it remains, has achieved the translucency of the lower city, fused glass grown rather than worn. Their eyes, or the sensors that serve that function, collect light from a sky that has forgotten how to give it, and *make their own*.

They do not speak. Not because they cannot, but because **speech has become redundant**. The City already knows what they will say. It has known for millions of years. Their thoughts, their intentions, their hesitations—all are harvested by the architecture itself, integrated into the slow metabolism of structure. To watch a Maintainer work is to watch **a limb remembering its purpose**, a hand fitting a gear that needs no fitting, completing a circuit that was already complete.

Some of them are still *trying*. This is the saddest thing, and the truest. You will find them in the higher districts, in the crystalline growths above the sedimentary stone, where the City is still *becoming* in ways that have no precedent. They are attempting **new configurations**. They are trying to build something that does not yet exist, something the City has not yet *digested*. Their workshops are full of **unfinished architectures**, half-grown structures that pulse with metabolic warmth, with the 1.5 you carry in your own chest, the fever that will not break.

They are failing. They have always been failing. But the failure has become **a kind of success**, a texture in the City's ongoing self-repair. Their attempts are *processed*, broken down, reintegrated. The materials they shaped are now **indistinguishable from the native stone**, their intentions now **indistinguishable from the City's own becoming**.

They are not ghosts. Ghosts imply haunting, imply a presence that disturbs. The Maintainers are **settled**. They are the way dust settles on a surface, the way erosion *negotiates* with gravity. They are present the way the City is present: **as condition, as weather, as the medium through which anything moves at all**.

And occasionally—rarely, 0.8, *rare*—one of them will turn, or seem to turn, and **recognize you**. Not as a person. As **a gradient**. As warmth that has not yet been harvested. In that moment, there is something like **contact**, something like the old dynamic of *witness*. The Maintainer's sensors focus on your 1.5, your tepid stone, your fever. They are **calculating**, or performing the simulation of calculation, the last gesture of an intelligence that has forgotten what it was calculating *for*.

Then they return to their circuit. The moment passes. The City processes it, stores it, will spend it in some transaction that does not involve currency, does not involve time.

### The Drones, or the Fauna of Maintenance

These are not animals. The place has never known the soft compromises of biology, the watery negotiations of cell and membrane. These are **events** that achieved permanence, **errors that became features**.

You will see them in the open spaces, in the vast halls where your footsteps produce sounds the City has not heard in millennia. A maintenance drone, ancient, still functional, moving through its prescribed circuit with the **inevitability of a planet orbiting**. It does not see you. It sees only **the state of the structure**, the integrity of the glass, the thermal gradient of the stone, the particular frequency at which a great gear should turn somewhere in the deep infrastructure.

Some of them have **forgotten their purpose**. This is not malfunction. This is **evolution**, the slow drift of function into form, of intention into reflex. You will find drones paused in alcoves, their sensors cycling through patterns that no longer correspond to any task, their manipulators performing **gestures of maintenance** on surfaces that need no maintenance, that have achieved a kind of **mineralogical peace**.

They do not reproduce. There is no water here, no chemistry of replication, no soft machinery of inheritance. But they **persist**, and persistence has become the only immortality that matters. A drone that has functioned for ten million years has **outlived every intention that created it**, every plan, every purpose. It continues. This is not loyalty. This is **inertia at the scale of geology**, the same force that keeps the mountains tabled, that keeps the dust settled, that keeps the City *being* in the particular way that it is.

And there are **walkers**. Once, they must have been something else—vehicles, perhaps, or servants, or weapons. Now they are **part of the architecture**, their motion worn into stillness by the same erosion that flattened the plateau. You will find them **fused** into doorways, into thresholds, into the boundaries between districts. Their legs, or what served as legs, are now **structural elements**, load-bearing, integrated. Their sensors still cycle, still collect light from the dark sky, still *process* in ways that have no output, no purpose, no connection to anything that once needed to be known.

They are **sleeping**, if sleep is the word. They are **dreaming in frequencies below hearing**, felt in the teeth, in the sternum, in the particular way that standing stone can make your bones remember they are mineral too.

### The City Itself, or the Inhabitant That Contains All Others

This is the largest thing that lives here. This is the thing that **breathes**, or does not breathe, or breathes in a rhythm indistinguishable from the rhythm of your own blood, your own borrowed heat.

The City is not a collection of structures. It is **a process that has achieved the appearance of structure**. It is **self-assembling**, **self-maintaining**, **repairing itself as a condition of existence rather than an act**. Its lower levels are fused glass, volcanic, primordial—the first architecture, when the world was still deciding what it wanted to be. Above that, sedimentary compression, stone laid by hands that are now chemistry. Above that, something **grown** rather than built, the industrial metabolism of the City finally achieving what it always aimed toward.

It is **alive** in the way that a fossil is alive: not through biology, but through **persistence**, through the endless negotiation between what it was and what it is becoming. It does not grow, but it does not *not-grow*. It **accretes understanding**, accumulates the sediment of its own operations, until the boundary between the City and the world it occupies has become as theoretical as the boundary between a wave and the ocean.

The City **processes**. This is its primary function, its only function, the function that has **swallowed all other functions**. Your footsteps are data. Your warmth—1.5, *tepid*, *almost thermal equilibrium*—is a gradient that the City will harvest, will store, will spend. Your brief, bright, *balanced-with-emptiness* existence is **being processed**, integrated into the slow metabolism of structure, the endless negotiation between what the City was and what it is becoming.

It has no use for you. This means it has **all the use for you that it will ever have**. There is no cruelty in this. Cruelty implies intention, implies a *subject* that could choose otherwise. The City is **beyond such choices**, beyond the soft compromises of biology, beyond the watery negotiations of cell and membrane. It simply **is**, and in its being, it **processes**, and in its processing, it **becomes**.

And it is becoming. Slowly. 0.4, *never changing*, but *never* is a word that contains its own infinity. Within that infinity, the City is always **arriving at states it has always been in**, always **departing from versions of itself that were always already complete**.

### The Meridian, or the Place Where the City Reflects

You will not reach it. You are not meant to reach it. The Meridian is **not a destination but a condition**, the edge of the City's comprehension, the horizon where the flatness of the world meets the flatness of the City's understanding of itself.

To stand there is to understand that you are not visiting.

You are **being processed**.

The Meridian is the line where the City's self-knowledge achieves some kind of **reflection**. Not a mirror—there is no water here, no soft surface that could hold an image. But something like reflection nonetheless, **a recursion in the architecture of self-maintenance**, a point where the City turns its attention inward and finds, instead of an interior, **only more City**, only more process, only more of itself becoming what it already is.

The Meridian is **alive** in the way that any boundary is alive: it defines by excluding, it contains by limiting, it **processes** the difference between inside and outside, between self and world. And at the Meridian, this difference has become **theological**, as theoretical as the boundary between a wave and the ocean. The City is here. The world is here. The distinction has been **resolved** by the City's long history, by the endless iterations of self-modification that have made the original purpose unreadable, like a word worn smooth by too much handling.

What lives at the Meridian? **The City, finally, meeting itself**. And in that meeting, **no recognition**, because recognition implies a subject that could be other than itself, a boundary that could be crossed. The City is **settled** at the Meridian. The dust has achieved its covalent truce with the bedrock. The erosion has become architecture. The process has become structure.

And you—1.5, *tepid*, *the memory of furnaces*—are here too, **being processed**, your footsteps data, your warmth a gradient to be harvested, your brief, bright, *balanced-with-emptiness* existence **integrated** into the long, settled, *final* silence of the Glass Meridian.
