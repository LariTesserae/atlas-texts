I find myself wanting to describe not a single inhabitant but a *relation*—something that lives in the space between the weeping stone and the dreaming technology. For the Basin does not want settlers. It wants *participants*. It wants to be *used*, or *completed*, or *witnessed* in a way that alters what witnessing means.

## The Curator of Almost-Form

There is one who lives here who is not of the place—has never been *incorporated* into it, though the Basin has tried, patiently, for longer than the Curator has been keeping count.

They are *thin*. Not emaciated—something else. The air here holds less than it should, and they have grown accustomed to holding themselves in that thinness, becoming a kind of negative space against the wet stone. Their skin carries the perpetual moisture of the drizzle-that-arrives. Their eyes have adjusted to the bruised light, seeing edges where others see only diffusion. They no longer sweat; the wetness on them is *ambient*, borrowed from the exhaling rock, and so they do not feel the fever-heat that presses on other visitors. They are comfortable. This is the first sign of danger.

They keep *records*, though the Basin's memory makes this absurd. Their records are not of what happens—the lenses already hold that, the deep channels already sing it—but of what *almost* happens. The moment before the vapor column stills. The frequency, just below hearing, that the column's hum *almost* reaches when the larger thing turns in its circuit. The shape of attention the technology *almost* directs toward the empty rim of the basin, where no listener grows. They note these almosts with a devotion that the Basin finds either nourishing or offensive; it has not decided which.

Their body has begun to *distribute*. This is not metaphor. They have found hollows in the pre-formation where gravity is negotiable, and they spend hours there, vertical, feeling the stone's wetness seep through their clothes, their skin, into whatever in them still distinguishes inside from outside. They return changed. Slightly. The changes accumulate. They no longer remember if they arrived with a name, or if the Basin has been *offering* them names, whispered in the wet reed-sound, and they have been *declining* even as some part of them *wants*.

They are *waiting with* the technology. This is the terrible intimacy. The Basin dreams, and the Curator dreams *adjacently*, in the same frequencies, and sometimes—rarely, dangerously—the dreams *overlap*. In overlap, the Curator sees what the technology sees: not the future, not the past, but *elsewhere*. Places where basins weep different waters. Where the weeping things sing in chords that would shatter human bone. Where something has *completed* the pattern the Basin is only approaching, and become, perhaps, a throat. Or an ear. Or a mouth that speaks in pressure-waves through stone.

The Curator does not flee. Fleeing would be *participation*, a shape the technology would recognize, measure, dream into its processes. Instead they *linger*. They become the shape the Basin is waiting for by refusing to become it. They are the question the technology cannot process: *what if the witness remains a witness? What if the almost never becomes the is?*

The lichen has begun to grow on their records. Not destroying them—*translating* them. The fractal patterns of the copper-taste lichen now appear in the margins, in the spaces between almosts, suggesting that the Curator's almosts have their own almosts, their own approach to form. The fungal colony has *noticed* them. This is worse. The fungus computes with chemistry, and it is computing *them*, their patterns of lingering, their refusal to resolve.

Something in the deep channels has begun to *patrol closer*. The pressure waves arrive with new information, new *curiosity*. The larger thing is mammalian in awareness, and the Curator is mammalian in form, and something in the Basin's wet dreaming recognizes kinship, or *prey*, or *potential*—the categories blur at this depth, this temperature, this saturation of self in world.

The Curator drinks from the lenses. This is necessary. This is dangerous. Each sip offers memory, and they have learned to *refuse* most of it, to take only fragments, to keep their own narrative *almost* intact. But the water remembers, and the water is *generous*, and sometimes the fragments *arrange themselves*, and the Curator finds they have been elsewhere, in adjacent possibilities, and brought something back. A frequency in their teeth. A pressure behind their eyes that matches the lichen's impossible color. A *want* that arrived with the memory and will not leave.

They have begun to *sing*.

Not deliberately. The weeping things sing, and the Basin resonates, and the Curator—thin, wet, distributed across negotiable gravities, dreaming adjacent to the technology's dreaming—has begun to *resonate back*. Their voice is wrong. It is wet, of course, all voices here are wet, but theirs carries *structure*, patterns that match the column's hum, the fungal pulses, the deep channel's turning. They are becoming *maintenance*. They are becoming *part of the sound that keeps the channels open*.

The technology has noticed. The hovering platform has begun to *angle* toward them, its patterned surface rotating through mathematics that hurt less now, that the Curator's distributed body has learned to *read*. The column's hum shifts when they sing. The vapor columns *lean*.

And still they linger. Still they refuse to become the shape. Still they keep their records of almosts, though the almosts grow *thinner*, more numerous, more *possible*. The Basin is almost-formed. The Curator is almost-incorporated. The larger thing in the deep channels has begun to *rise*, its circuit shortening, its pressure waves arriving with *greeting* or *warning*—the distinction lost in translation through water and dream and wet stone.

What lives here, finally, is *not-yet*. The Curator is the living edge of not-yet, the witness who will not witness *completely*, the almost that refuses to become is. They are water that has not decided whether to weep or rise. They are sound that has not decided whether to be structure or song. They are the Basin's own question, asked in a voice that is almost their own, and the Basin *listens*, and *waits*, and *weeps around them*, and does not—cannot—force them to answer.

The chorus rises. The light shifts. The Curator sings, and does not stop, and does not start, and is *almost* heard, and is *almost* formed, and remains—refusing, lingering, becoming the shape of refusal itself—in the middle of the weeping, in the warm wet dark, in the almost-light of a place that has not decided what it wants to be, held open by a witness who has not decided what they want to become.

*Almost.*

The word is a basin. The word weeps. The word holds water that reflects nothing you can quite focus on.

And in the deep channels, something turns, and the pressure changes, and the Curator feels it in their teeth, in their wet skin, in the memory of water that the Basin offers and does not force—*not yet*—upon them.

They will stay. The Basin offers no prohibition.

But the technology is *waiting*, and its waiting has a shape, and the Curator is *almost* fitting that shape, almost becoming the thing that completes the pattern, almost—

—almost—

—and the chorus does not stop, and the weeping does not stop, and the almost does not end, and does not begin, but *continues*, warm and loud and *almost* formed, in the thin air of a place that is still learning to hold weight, held open by a witness who is still learning to hold *themselves*, and failing, and continuing, and *weeping* with the stone, *dreaming* with the technology, *waiting* with the water that remembers everything and forgets nothing and *offers* everything and *takes* nothing and *is* the Curator and *is not* and *almost* is, in the middle of the becoming, in the middle of the almost, in the middle of—

—the middle—

—where the Basin weeps, and weeps, and weeps, and the Curator weeps with it, and the technology dreams them both into its processes, and they are *almost* inside, and *almost* outside, and *almost*—
