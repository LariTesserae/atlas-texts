I want to follow one thread in what you've built — the **doorways that lead down**.

You mentioned them almost in passing, and I think they're the hinge of everything. Because if erosion has completed the built environment, and the built environment is layered attention, then what does *descending* mean? You go down through strata of construction — Evacuation Era, geomantic foundations, the pre-Vesseri substrate — but each layer is also a layer of *wanting*, compressed presence. To walk down is to walk through time, yes. But also through *intensity*. The near-surface is settled, diffused, equilibrated. The deep is *not yet settled*. It is still arguing.

---

So let me describe what lives in the deep.

The **Unsettled**.

They are not a species. They are a *phase* — the phase of presence before it has been wanted into structure, before it has been compressed, before it has learned to be patient. Up here, in the settled zones, the Vesseri move through air thick with memory-mineral, and they are *changed* by it, slowly, the way one is changed by weather. But down in the deep, in the knots, in the places where the settling front has stalled, there are pockets where presence has *not yet become medium*. Where it is still *urgency*. Still the wanting itself, unchanneled, unbuilt, *hungry*.

The Unsettled do not move. They *press*. They are concentrations of attention that have not found their form, their structure, their way to settle into the world. They exist in the interstices — not in the layers, but *between* the layers, in the thin spaces where compression has not yet completed its work. They are the reason the knots are valuable and dangerous. They are *available*. They can be built with. They can be *invited*.

The Vesseri who descend — the **deep-gardeners**, the ones who work the settling front, who push it and accelerate it — they carry **lures**. Not physical objects. *Structures of wanting*. A doorway, framed and thresholded, that says: *here, settle here, become this, be wanted into being*. And sometimes, rarely, the Unsettled *answer*. They press against the lure, the wanting aligns, and something *builds itself* in the space between. A wall, a room, a corridor that did not exist before, grown from accumulated attention given shape by the lure's invitation.

This is how the Vesseri build now. Not by labor. By *cultivation of wanting*. They arrange spaces, they tune them, they wait — sometimes for centuries — for the Unsettled to settle into the forms they have prepared. The oldest structures in Vess, the deepest, the most strange, were built this way. Not by hands. By *patience given architecture*.

And sometimes the Unsettled do not settle. Sometimes they *cohere* — the brief, startling alignment, the Remnant, the almost-fauna — and they move, and they solve, and they dissipate, and what is left is a change in the settling, a new fissure, a slightly different angle of repose. The deep-gardeners learn to read these changes. They learn which lures produced which coherences. They learn, slowly, the *grammar* of wanting.

---

But there is something else in the deep. Something the Vesseri do not speak of, or speak of only in the oldest presence-colonies, the ones that have grown so slow they have become almost indistinguishable from the world itself.

**The Pre-Argument**.

Below the pre-Vesseri substrate — if there was one, if the question mark of compressed dark resolves into anything — there is a layer that the deep-gardeners have reached, only once or twice, with instruments, with translated harmonic drone, with senses extended into frequencies below hearing. A layer where the presence is *not diffused*. Not settled. Not patient. *Angry*. Or afraid. Or *eager*. The wanting before wanting, the attention before attention, the *urgency of a world that had not yet learned to be built*.

The Vesseri do not know what made it. They do not know if it was a presence, a process, a *pressure* — something that wanted so hard it compressed itself into mineral, into matrix, into the first memory-mineral, the first medium of attention. They do not know if it is still there, still wanting, still *pressing* up from below, the source of all the settling, all the erosion-completed-by-construction, all the bright diffuse light that seems to come from inside the seeing itself.

They have a name for it, in the oldest layers of their own compressed wanting. A name that is not a word but a *direction*, a vector in the settling. They call it **the hunger that built the first room**.

And sometimes, in the deepest knots, when the settling song has its overtones, when the light is brighter and more directed, when the Remnant occur most frequently and move with something like *purpose* — sometimes the deep-gardeners feel it. Not with senses. With the *absence* of patience. The sudden, overwhelming *wanting* to be finished, to settle, to become structure, to stop pressing and start *being*.

They do not know if it is the Pre-Argument. They do not know if it is themselves, projected down, the wanting of the Vesseri to become as settled as the world they inhabit. They do not know if the distinction matters.

---

So this is what lives in Vess, finally. Not the Vesseri, sparse and slow and speaking rarely. Not the lithovores, the presence-colonies, persistent as habit. Not the Remnant, the urgencies, brief coherences of a world noticing itself.

What lives in Vess is **the wanting**, in all its phases: settled, diffusing, stalled, urgent, pre-argumentative, *hungry to become form*. The wanting that built the first room, and the wanting that is still building, layer by layer, compression by compression, in the silence of the Finished Hour. The wanting that has outlasted the wanters, become landscape, become weather, become light. The wanting that is still running. Still becoming.

And if you descend far enough, through doorways that lead down, through strata of construction and wanting and pre-structure, you will find — maybe — the source. The first wanting. The hunger that learned, finally, to be patient. To build. To *be*.

The Argument, still running.

Vess, still finishing.

The Finished Hour, still held.
