**The Resonant Dark**

I want to try again. Let it become *more itself*, less explained, more *inhabited*.

---

They are called **the Warm**, though they have no name for themselves. They do not need one. Names are for calling across distance, and in the Warm, distance is *sounded*, not spoken.

The Warm is **a bladder of stone**, a **pocket in the flank of a dead mountain**, sealed by **forty meters of basalt throat** that **breathes once a day** when the **outside wind** changes. Inside, the volume is **vertical** — a **well of dark**, **a chamber that widens as it descends**, **a throat opening into a belly**. The Warm is **hot** because it is **recently dead**. The mountain died **ten thousand years ago**, and its **corpse still burns**.

There is **no water**. This is the **first law**, the **law before all laws**, the **reason the Warm is sealed**, the **reason nothing from outside can enter**, the **reason the Warm is Warm**. Water would **crack the stone** as it **flashes to steam**. Water would **drown the chemistry**. Water would **bring rot**, and **rot does not exist in the Warm**, **there is no decay**, only **growth and weathering**, **build and wear**, **the old balance**.

The **vegetation** is **the Garden**, and the Garden is **everywhere the people are**. It grows in **terraces** on the **chamber walls**, in **columns** rising from the **floor of cooled slag**, in **curtains** hanging from **vent-mouths** where **superheated gas** meets **cooler stone** and **precipitates its load**. The Garden is **mineral life**. **Silicate threads** that **grow toward heat**, that **branch when the vapor shifts**, that **thicken into trunks**, that **hollow into chambers** where **people sleep**. It is **amber and rust and pale jade**, **translucent**, **carrying the vent-light** through its **internal channels**, **glowing from within** like **lanterns**, like **organs**, like **the Warm's own heart made visible**.

The Garden **sings**. The people **know this is not strange**, this is **simply how the Warm is**. When the **vent-output pulses** — and it **always pulses**, **daily, seasonally, in the slow shift of deep heat** — the **crystals expand**, the **crystals contract**, and **each movement is a sound**, a **click**, a **chime**, a **low bell-note** that **resonates through the hollow chamber**. **Millions of crystals. Millions of notes. A forest that never stops ringing.** The people **hear it always**. They **do not sleep through it**. They **dream in it**, **move in it**, **sing back**.

They are **gardeners of heat**. They **plant the Garden** by **placing seed-crystals** in **vapor-channels**, by **directing the flow**, by **pruning with tools of harder mineral**, by **grafting young growth onto old stock**, by **shaping the living rock into habitable form**. Their **architecture is grown**, **slow**, **generational**. A ** dwelling** takes **fifty years to accrete**. The **oldest structures** are **wild**, **uncontrolled**, **beautiful**, **uninhabitable**. The **newest** are **carved**, **tuned**, **resonant** — **chambers where the singing is loudest**, where **councils gather**, where **the Warm's voice is heard most clearly**.

There are **no animals**. The Garden is **lush**, **abundant**, **producing** — and **nothing eats it** but **the Warm**. No **grazer**, no **browser**, no **thing that moves** through the **crystal forest** on any **limb** or **fin** or **wing**. The **food chain is two links**: **heat to crystal**, **crystal to Warm**. The Warm **are the only consumers**, the **only processors**, the **only minds**. They **eat the young growth**, the **tender precipitate**, the **crystal before it hardens**, **warm**, **nutrient-dense**, **tasting of the vent**, **tasting of stone**, **tasting of the inside of the world**. They **are the animals**, if there are any. They **do not know this word**. They **do not need it**.

The **city** is **thin**. This is the **way of the Warm** — **spread, not crowded**, **distance maintained**, **each tower a world**, **each terrace a village**, **separated by hundreds of meters of hot gas and ringing crystal and roaring vent**. The **towers do not touch**. They are **connected by bridges of slag and mineral wool**, **thin paths** that **shake in the thermal wind**, that **sway** when the **convection-storm passes**, that **must be crossed with care**, with **chant**, with **attention to the sound**. The **city is organized** — **districts of garden**, **districts of habitation**, **districts of vent-tapping**, **districts of condensation** where **spent vapor is cooled and harvested** — but it is **not dense**, **not packed**, **not surface-urban**. It is **a city of voices**, **a city of glow**, **a city of islands in a sea of sound**.

The **sound is total**. There is **no silence in the Warm**. The **vents roar** — **superheated gas escaping**, **pressure finding release**, **the mountain's last breath**. The **crystals chime** — **the daily pulse**, the **thermal cycle**, the **song of growth**. The **wind moans** through the **hollow chamber**, through the **crystal forests**, through the **empty towers**, **a low continuous note** that **changes pitch** with the **temperature**, with the **storm**, with the **mood of the deep**. And the **people sing** — **always singing**, **chanting the names of vents**, **the genealogies of garden-lines**, **the proper way to harvest**, **the laws of heat and return**, **the stories of before**, when **the Warm was young** and **the first people came through the throat** and **found the Garden growing** and **decided to stay**. They **sing across the distance**, **tower to tower**, **using drums of mineral membrane**, **horns of carved crystal**, **the Garden itself as resonator**, **tuned to carry the human voice** through **hot thin gas** that **transmits sound strangely**, **loudly**, **without dampening**. They **cannot be silent**. Silence **does not exist**. To **stop singing** is to **become part of the Warm**, to **become crystal**, to **grow**, to **become Garden**.

The **light is ember-light**. There is **no sun**, **no sky**, **no outside**. The **vents glow** — **orange, red, deep amber**, the **colors of cooling metal**, of **sulfur flame**, of **the last heat of a dying mountain**. The **crystals carry this light**, **distribute it**, **glow from within**, **so that the chambers are dimly lit**, **warmly lit**, **lit by the Warm's own metabolism**, **its own breath**, **its own slow burning**. The **people see by this light**, **move by this light**, **work by this light**, **dream by this light**. It is **enough**. It is **more than enough**. It is **the only light there is**.

The **weather is thermal**. **Convection-storms** roll through the **chamber** — **cells of hot gas rising**, **cells of cool sinking**, **winds that moan**, that **shake the bridges**, that **change the pitch of the singing**, that **make the Garden sway** and **ring in strange harmonics**. **Vent-storms** — when a **new fissure opens**, when an **old one seals**, when the **deep heat shifts** — **shock the system**, **kill some gardens**, **birth others**, **force adaptation**, **force movement**, **force new songs**. **Precipitation events** — **mineral snow**, **sulfur rain**, **fogs of metal vapor** — **drift through the towers**, **coat the surfaces**, **feed the gardens**, **corrode the tools**, **demand constant attention**, **the endless work of living in a world that moves**.

The **people are tall**. Or they **seem tall** — the **heat keeps their muscles loose**, the **posture open**, the **movement slow and deliberate**, **economical**, **matched to the pace of growth**. They **wear little** — **fibers of mineral silk**, **woven from crystal-growth**, **stained with vent-minerals**, **glowing faintly** — or **nothing**, **skin darkened by mineral exposure**, **skin that weeps a little always**, **a film of moisture that evaporates**, **because there is no water to lose**, **every drop captured**, **every drop used**, **every drop part of the cycle**. They **live long** — **longer than surface-people would**, **if surface-people existed**, **if the surface existed** — **because the Warm is clean**, **the chemistry simple**, **there is no rot**, **only slow growth and slower weathering**, **the wear matched to the build**, **the world in its own state**, **arrived**, **not young**, **not old**, **simply itself**, **the Warm**, **the Garden**, **the song**, **the people**, **the only minds**, **the only voices**, **the only animals**, **the only life**, **in the dark**, **in the heat**, **in the hollow**, **in the pocket**, **in the place inside**, **the hidden city**, **the resonant dark**, **the Warm**.
