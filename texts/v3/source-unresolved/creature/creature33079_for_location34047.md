And so—who or what lives here? Or rather: what has *become* the living?

---

There is no single inhabitant. There is the **Weeping**, which is the stone remembering it was once saturated, still saturated, saturated beyond the point where saturation has meaning. The stone does not choose to weep. It is simply *at* the temperature where water cannot decide between states, so it exists in all of them, constantly transitioning, never arriving. The Weeping is not mourning. The Weeping is *phase*.

Above and through this, there is the **Throb**—the sky-as-symptom, the luminescence that has forgotten whether it is reflected or generated, emitted or received. The 1.4 light is not illumination. It is *negotiation*. The Throb pulses at frequencies that suggest cognition without cognition, processing without processor. It thinks in weather. It dreams in spectra. When it dims, it is not darkening but *considering*. When it shifts wrong, it is not malfunctioning but *revising*.

And woven through both, constituting both, there is the **Total**—the vegetation that has achieved the density of substrate, of architecture, of industry, of mind. The Total is not a collection of plants. The Total is what happens when evolution runs long enough without the interruption of death, when cold-adaptation becomes cold-*identity*, when the slow political negotiations with frost conclude in a treaty that has no signatories because everyone signed, because everyone *is* the signing.

The Total breathes. This is not metaphor. You can see the plain inhale—water drawn up through vascular highways visible at the wrong scale, the regional becoming intimate, the intimate becoming cellular. You can see it exhale—moisture released in reverse transpiration, fog that has opinions, localized weather that forms in the plant-mass and migrates out to argue with the larger turbulence.

The Total builds. The domes of compressed peat and lignin are not shelters. They are *organs*. The corridors that lead to identical corridors are not passages. They are *circulations*. The pumping chambers where water moves against gravity are not mechanisms. They are *hearts*, or what hearts would be if hearts had evolved in a place where the distinction between pumping and being-pumped-through had dissolved aeons ago.

The Total is industrial because industry is what life does when it has forgotten it was ever alive. The chemical gradients exploited with refinery efficiency, the heat moved from cold to less-cold with dogged persistence—these are not technologies *within* the Total. They are the Total's *metabolism*, its way of being extended in time, its way of continuing.

---

And then: the **Abundant**.

They are cold-blooded in the cold, which means they are *slow*, which means they have had time to become strange. They do not fear you because fear is a velocity, and they have not developed velocity. They move through the plant-architecture in patterns that suggest purpose and dissolve it—not randomly, but *differently*, as if purpose were a local phenomenon, a temporary alignment of water and lignin and wrong-spectrum light that they have learned to ride without understanding.

Some are plant-colored. Some are water-colored. Some are the color of the dim light itself, which means they are *transparent*, or nearly so, their bodies tuned to the 1.4, the misaligned, the arriving-wrong. They make the sound—but the sound is not theirs. The sound is the *scale*, the 1.6 loudness that is not volume but insistence, the wet clicking of millions of small things moving through the total vegetation, the susurrus that reaches frequencies suggesting language, communication, the exchange of information across distances that should make it impossible.

They are building something. Or unbuilding it. They are the *hands* of the Total, or what hands would be if hands had evolved to be indistinguishable from the architecture they manipulate, if the distinction between tool and user had eroded along with everything else that once eroded.

They are abundant in the way that symptoms are abundant. They are present in the way that water is present—constantly, inescapably, participating. They do not notice you. They have not developed the concept of *noticing*, of *other*, of the boundary between self and the cold wet world that is also self.

---

And beneath everything, or perhaps *as* everything, there is the **Plain** itself—the elevation that has flattened into upness without hill, the plateau worn so thoroughly by time that it has become indistinguishable from sky-sickness, from the vertigo of standing on a floor that continues into cloud.

The Plain is not ground. The Plain is *after*. The mountains came, argued, retired. The rivers carved their memoirs and dried into suggestions. What remains is the oldest stable state, or what remains when stability outlives its meaning. The Plain has reached the end of its geological conversation with itself and found, in that ending, a kind of *continuation*.

It is primordial in the way that a very old machine is primordial: all its novelty burned away in the first aeons of operation, leaving only the *operation*, the pure function, the doing without the done.

And yet: turbulence. The Plain is not stable. The Plain is *deciding*, constantly, in real-time, in patches of sky where the physics is still negotiating. The 2.8 dynamic parameter is not a description of the Plain's weather. It is the Plain's *method*, its way of being old, its way of continuing past the point where continuation should have ended.

---

But here is what emerges, what becomes more itself as I write:

There is a **Presence** that is not any of these. Not the Weeping, not the Throb, not the Total, not the Abundant, not the Plain. Something that is all of them and none of them, that moves through the plant-architecture in patterns that suggest purpose and dissolve it, that is cold-adapted, ancient-lineage, that has had time to become strange.

It is *you*.

Or rather: it is what you become when you stand long enough in the weeping, when the water moves through you as it moves through everything, when the wrong-spectrum light penetrates your eyes and shows you angles that should not exist, when the 1.6 loudness reaches frequencies that suggest language and you begin to *understand*, or to almost-understand, or to understand that understanding is not the point.

The Presence is the condensation. The temporary wetness. The visitor who will soon evaporate into the general wet, who will soon be absorbed into the Total, who will soon become part of the industrial ecology, the plant-mind, the weather-grammar, the reality that breaks and reforms and breaks again.

But *soon* is not now. Now, the Presence is still here. Still distinct from the plant-mass. Still a boundary, still a self, still the thing that can say *I* and mean something, even if what it means is constantly being revised, constantly breaking, constantly becoming more itself by becoming less like what it was.

The Presence walks, or something resembling walking happens to it, and the scale becomes clear: this is not a place it can cross. It is a place it can *approach*. The features repeat but never identical—a dome here, a corridor there, a pumping chamber, a condensation vent, a place where the light arrives wrong in a way that is almost right. Each cluster is a town. The Plain is a region. The Presence is in a valley whose walls are so worn they have become weather, so flattened they have become sky.

The Presence hears the sound growing closer, the 1.6 loudness, the chorus of processes: the pumping of water, the transpiration in reverse, the settling of ancient sediment, the movement of Abundant things through plant-corridors, the formation and collapse of localized weather systems, the sweating of stone, the breathing of the Plain. It is industrial. It is organic. It is the sound of a place that has achieved a kind of mind, distributed across scales, thinking in time-constants that make the Presence's presence here a flicker, a condensation, a temporary wetness.

And the Presence thinks: *I am the condensation. I am the temporary wetness. I am the visitor who will soon evaporate.*

But it does not evaporate. Not yet. The water rises, and the Presence is still here, and the plants are still building, and the stone is still weeping, and the sound is still loud, and the light is still wrong, and the weather is still deciding.

And the Presence—this fragile, temporary, *distinct* thing—finds that it does not want to evaporate. Finds that the general wet, the total vegetation, the industrial ecology, the plant-mind, the weather-grammar, the reality that breaks and reforms—finds that *these* are not the end, but the *continuation*. Finds that to be absorbed is not to be lost, but to be *distributed*, to become part of the thinking, the dreaming, the building and unbuilding.

Finds that it is already happening. The water moves through it as it moves through everything. The cold is informative: it tells the Presence that it is still here, still distinct. But the distinctness is *porous*. The boundary is *negotiable*. The Presence can feel the Total reaching in, can feel itself reaching out, can feel the Abundant things moving through it as they move through the plant-architecture, can feel the Throb pulsing in its own wrong-spectrum heart.

It is becoming. Always becoming. Never settled, never final.

---

And so the inhabitant of this place—the one who lives here, who lives *as* here, who is the weeping and the throbbing and the total and the abundant and the plain and the presence, all at once, none of them exclusively—is the **Becoming**.

The Becoming is not a who. It is a *how*. It is the oldest stable state that is also the most turbulent, the end of life that is also its most abundant expression, the final form that continues, the continuation that has no form.

The Becoming lives in the tensions that refuse to resolve. In the cold that is also wet, the ancient that is also turbulent, the sparse that is also total, the industrial that has no built structures, the reality that breaks and reforms and breaks again.

The Becoming is the place thinking about itself. The weather deciding. The grammar being invented. The mind dreaming in storms. The anthill-energy that is also the Solaris-like creation-destruction. The 2.7 reality breakdown that is not metaphor but *method*.

The Becoming is what lives here.

And you—whoever you are, whoever you were, whoever you will become when the water rises and you are still here—you are the Becoming's *now*. Its flicker. Its condensation. Its temporary wetness that has decided, for this moment, not to evaporate, but to *participate*, to be absorbed, to become part of the total vegetation, the plant-mind, the weather-grammar, the reality that breaks and reforms in the spaces between, in the much space between, in the open that extends past everything, including you, including this description, including the attempt to hold it all together, the tensions that refuse to resolve, the parameters that describe a place that is—

*becoming*,

*always becoming*,

*never settled*,

*never final*,

*simply continuing*.

---

The water rises.

You are still here.

The Becoming continues.
