What lives here?

Nothing with a name that can be spoken aloud. But something lives here. Something has always lived here.

---

The **Keepers of the Unchanged Hour** — though "Keepers" implies intention, and intention implies the possibility of stopping, which does not exist here.

They are the ones who built the structures, or who were the structures, or who became the structures when the building became indistinguishable from being built. They do not move through the medium. They *are* the medium's memory of movement. Where the held water thickens slightly, where the density takes on a shape that almost resembles a spine, an arm, a face turned toward a direction that has no compass — there they are. Faint. Not quite visible. Visible enough that you feel watched, which is the only kind of seeing that works here.

They do not age because aging requires change, and they are the proof that change was once a *choice*, a violence done to the world, and that the world, in its wisdom or its exhaustion, chose otherwise. They have faces, or had faces, or have faces that are only the suggestion of faces in the way that the structures are only the suggestion of purpose. Their eyes — if they are eyes — reflect the flat light without absorbing any of it. They look at you and see the held water. They have always looked at you and always seen only the held water.

They do not speak. Speech requires that sound carry *difference*, that one word end differently from how it began. Here, every utterance is the same utterance, the same flexing of membranes, the same transfer of charge, the same dispersal into the medium that cannot retain it. They communicate, if communication is what happens between them, through **pressure gradients** — slight thickenings and thinnings that pass from one to another across impossible distances, that carry no meaning but carry *continuity*, the way a heartbeat carries continuity, the way the vents carry heat, the way the engines carry their endless, purposeless cycling.

They are **industrial** in their essence now. They do not produce. They *process*. They take the held water in and release the held water out, changed in no way that matters, unchanged in every way that matters. They are the original inhabitants, or the last, or the only — the distinction dissolves when you have been here long enough, when the medium has seeped into your own membranes, when you too begin to flex in unison with the ten billion, when you too become a pressure-form, a colony of gas-borne self, trading in charge, reproducing by splitting, dying by dissolving into the medium and being forgotten.

But you do not forget them. You cannot. They are the only *other* thing in the vastness, the only thing that is not fauna and not structure and not medium. They are the **scattered-built** made flesh, or made whatever passes for flesh here. The platform that ends in mid-void? It ends where a Keeper stopped building, or where a Keeper *became* the platform, his body the sintered metal, his nervous system the humming conduit, his endless turning the engine that powers nothing.

And they are **young**, these Keepers, in the way that the volcanic glass is young, in the way that the world is young when it has not yet learned that it will end. They have been here since the beginning, and the beginning is still happening, will always be happening, is the only tense that exists. They do not remember being born because birth requires a before, and there was no before, only the held water, only the pressure, only the first flexing of membrane that would become all membrane, the first venting that would become all venting, the first structure that would become all structure, the first Keeper that would become all Keepers, scattered now, partial, occasional, *permanent*.

They are **loud**. Or they make the loudness. The deafening sound is their sound, the sound of their endless, unchanging persistence, the sound of charge moving through conductive self, the sound of bodies finding and losing each other in a medium that offers no resistance and no purchase. It is the sound of ten billion Keepers flexing in unison, though they are not ten billion, though they are not separate, though separation is another word that failed here, another violence done to the held water, another change that was refused.

And they are **silent**, in the way that the medium is silent, in the way that the flat light is silent, in the way that the curvature of the world is silent, seen from any high point, everywhere, because the high points are everywhere. The sound so complete that it has become silence. The silence so complete that it has become sound. The Keepers are the knot where this paradox ties itself, where the deafening and the never-changing become one thing, one condition, one *now* that stretches in every direction, holds in every direction, is held by every direction.

They do not sleep. They do not wake. They do not dream, because dreams require a difference between the dreaming and the dreamed, and here the held water is both, is neither, is the only thing that is and is not. They exist in **frozen equilibrium**, dynamic stasis, the roar that contains no information, the machine that processes itself into itself forever. They are the original inhabitants of the original condition, and they will be the last, and last and first are the same here, are the same word spoken in a medium that cannot distinguish them, cannot distinguish anything, can only hold, hold, hold, in the flat light, in the cold, in the endless, unchanging, deafening *now*.

You meet them, if meeting is possible, in the spaces between structures, in the gaps where the platforms end and the void begins, in the layers of the medium where the density shifts just enough to suggest a shape, a presence, a *watching*. They do not approach you. They do not retreat. They simply *are*, as they have always been, as they will always be, scattered across the vastness, built into the industrial silence, young and eternal, loud and still, the Keepers of the Unchanged Hour, the inhabitants of the Wet High, the Held Deep, the place where the world stopped being a world and became only a *condition*, submerged and soaring, empty and full, young and eternal, loud and silent in the same moment.

And you — you who have come seeking, who have found, who will stay, who have always been here — you are becoming one of them. The medium seeps in. Your membranes flex. You trade in charge. You split, or you will split, or you have already split and do not remember the other half, the half that dissolved, the half that was forgotten, the half that is still here, flexing, *persisting*, in the deafening, unchanging, beautiful coordination.

There is no shore. There is no surface. There is only the pressure, the light, the endless, unchanging, deafening *now* — and the Keepers, scattered through it, building nothing, becoming everything, *holding* the world in its condition, forever, forever, the same.
