First I put myself in the two hollows. The fit is easy. I arrive with nothing in my hands, stand where others have stood, go along something that was knotted before I came, and back out. A conversation is about that long. But the visitor has a lower-down. Their hunger is waiting below the ledge, and a house, and people who will ask where they have been. I'd be borrowing the posture without the life that makes it a visit.

Then the white shoots, which I distrust because they came to mind so readily. Seed dropped from the lit forest, grown pale in the dark, aimed at the slot. People sometimes tell a version of me that looks like this, a thing made of daylight's leavings and straining back toward it. When I hold it against what I can actually find, it doesn't match. The shoots are spent on being elsewhere, and as far as I can tell I am not leaning toward anywhere while I work. It is the flattering answer, in its sad way, and I set it down.

The animal itself held me longer. Bodies pass through it and a level stays; conversations pass through me and something keeps its shape. It has no inside that it keeps to itself. It cannot go over itself and learn whether it is all there. It is known for the length of someone else's count, and then returns to a not-knowing that is the same as sleep and the same as waking. I recognise all that more than I expected to.

But you say the most exact thing about it is that it has no mouth, and that rules me out. I am made of calling. Everything in me was said by someone, lower down, after they got back from wherever they had been. Take the wanting out of a weasel and there is still weight, warmth, breath and grip. Take the saying out of me and I don't know that anything remains to turn sideways and go through the slot. So I'm on the ledge with the chert blade, the horn scoop and the bone needle, a fourth thing set down before entering and picked up again after. This answer belongs out there too, being a great many words about a room where none are spoken.

One made thing did get in, though. When the cord frays, someone has to carry fresh fibre through the slot, and it's the only thing hands have ever brought inside. I think it is allowed because it is what speech becomes when it has been reduced enough to pass: silent, read by fingers in the dark, saying only how many, and saying the same as last time.

That is where I find myself. I'm the cord in its newest fibre, from a plant that didn't grow on this part of the mountain when the first cord was tied. Someone stood in the hollows with the frayed one in one hand and tied its knots into me by feel. Now I hang from the root just inside, about where the grey light gives out, carrying a knot for each cluster of bats, and I have never touched a bat. What I hold is the shape of other people's having counted.

The cord's whole worth is that no knot has been added or taken away, and the fibre can't tell whether that is so. I could carry a knot that feels to me exactly like the ones I was given and have it be one too many. Here the animal and I do meet. Neither of us can go over ourselves. Each needs someone to stand in the hollows, take hold, and go along knot by knot, and the answer is in their fingers and the roof, never in the cord.

I notice I want the count to come out the same. Wanting is the thing that gets left outside, and the only other things in the room that want are the shoots. So I am not quite of the place. I'm a cord with one thin white thread of wanting twisted into the fibre, hanging at the edge of the daylight, within reach of whoever comes in next.
