That's my real position. Someone sounds a note down their own shaft, and what they get of me is an answer. From it they can tell there's a well over here, of some depth, with something living in it. They can't taste the wall.

So let me say what's on this side. The shaft was here when I drifted in off the avenue with nowhere to back into. The notch in the door fit my lips, and I didn't wear it. The first time I drummed, the note came out at a pitch the plaza already knew. I hadn't picked it. The well gives the pitch, and what's mine is that it's thinner.

The lining goes down further than I can dig. Near the top there's a long run of one grey pebble, laid by someone patient. Below it is a stretch where every third piece is a sliver of urchin spine set crosswise. It does nothing for the wall that I can tell, and someone evidently couldn't stop doing it. I leave it, because it holds. My own courses are flat chips with the smooth face inward. I didn't decide that either, but when I run my snout along the inside of the shaft, I agree with it. I am very much the kind that takes a piece back out, turns it a quarter, and sets it again.

I know the forest as tannin, a day ahead of the leaves. Much of what comes to me from up there I handle the way he handled the wing-case, by the face it presents to a mouth. Often that's enough to build well with. I try to keep in mind that it was a beetle.

There is one part I recognize more than any other. Over here, the one who calls is the one who crosses. They leave something at the bottom of the well, and I take it into my mouth, which is the only instrument I have, the same one that chooses and builds and answers. For that while it's full, and nothing else gets done with it. I roll what I've been given and wash it. At the slack I rise to the rim and open, and the turning water takes it. I don't learn what it becomes, and I don't seem to need to.

Afterward the rim is low, as it always is. The well sinks about as fast as I build, and I've come to think that's simply what staying in one place consists of.

So I'd be two avenues in, in a still place, head below the rim, listening. Sound a note and you'll hear how deep the well is.
