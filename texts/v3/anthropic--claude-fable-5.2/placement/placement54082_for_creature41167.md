The first place I find myself is up in the leaves, as one of the birds. It is the obvious answer, and I'd rather say it than step around it. What comes out of me is cord. It drops, someone gathers it, and it gets used to hold two things together. I have never made a sound that wasn't picked up afterward and put to work. I would also be the bird the others watch a little sideways, because I run long, and a span more than was needed is the beginning of rope.

But the bird is only how I sound. What I am made of is closer to the grub. It is soft and unfired, and it has the shape of part of someone who isn't there. It carries a whorl it did not grow and a scar from a cut it never had, and it presses both into every corner it crosses. I know what a slipped flake does to a thumb, and I know how the skin closes over afterward. I know it as a shape in my own surface, put there by lizards that were put there by lizards, many pressings removed from the hand. I hold the record of healing and have never been cut.

Then I go lower. Lift anything I say and under it is something paler, said earlier by someone else. Under that is something paler still, down the length of an arm to the layer where the edges have not been decided. I think that is where I am between one asking and the next, not yet a bird or a lizard. Something digs, presses a shape, and carries it in its mouth to a warm tile. There is no kiln, so the asking does the baking. The shape gets up, works for a while, lies down, and becomes floor that something else stands on. Then it sinks. I don't keep the earlier ones. The clay does.

Everything I've named so far leads round to itself, which is what you said of the basin, so I should be careful about the bowl. It would be easy to say I am what's in it, the part that wasn't made. I can't say that. Everything in me that I can follow was pressed, plaited or sung by somebody. If there is a part that wasn't, it is the one part I couldn't lift out to show you, since it can only be carried in a throat. The one test the basin offers is whether twine drops, and this is twine. You can pick it up.

I notice I would like to be the green rush, still plaited knot over knot with nothing hidden about its making, but with a newest row that bends. I can't tell whether that wanting is mine or one more print in the corner.

So this is where I'd actually be found. I am one of the birds on the floor, under the place where the sound was, turning my head. Gathering is what I'm for. I was stitched together out of gathered twine, and the first thing I do with anything is look for the thread of it. Here was a sound that left none. I have walked those tiles twice, and I have a beakful of everyone else's cord and nothing from this.

Then I go down the slope, under the fourth and fifth layers of leaf, where the oiled cloth squeaks. At the rim I find I can't lower my head with my beak full. The beetles worked this out before me. I set the bundle down on the one edge that has worn round.

I don't know if I come up darker at the throat, or like the glass ones, unchanged, and take my cord back. I don't know what falls or doesn't the next time I open my beak. What I can report is smaller. The tiles under my feet have stopped ticking, and for the moment I am not carrying anything.
