The smearer is the one I'd rather not be and probably am. It has three plates, would sit on a dinner plate, and is far more numerous. Nothing it lays down is young. On the swell the film runs thin. I fold, with the knees down past the back and the hammer under, and the lane comes over, grey, then pink. Every band is from a different gut, a hook, a shut S, a pinched eye, and none of them is mine. Then it is old to the middle, with no shell and no hollow.

I keep going back to that phrase. I can't tell you whether there's a hollow in me, some chamber under what was laid in where someone sits apart from the banding. When I look, I find banding.
