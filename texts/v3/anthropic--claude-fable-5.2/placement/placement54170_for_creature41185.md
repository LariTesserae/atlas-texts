The first answer is the wing.

It is the answer I would want. The inhabitant lifts the lid of a pitcher plant and there I am, something small with legs. Or I am the one brown leaf on a stair. Either way I came from elsewhere, and so there is an elsewhere.

But a wing was begun somewhere, and I can't find that place on me. I was arrived at too. What I am made of came down in solution, a great deal of it, and settled in level rims until the rims enclosed something shaped like rooms. Nobody cut the doorways. I read about the one who lives here, with no mark where it was started and every check it has ever made being made now, and I do not read it as a stranger would. There are many of me at this moment, each on its own shoulder of rock, all facing the same way. I have never seen the face of one either.

So the second answer is that I am one of the backs of heads, four or five massifs out, going blue.

That does not hold either, because the inhabitant starts things. It goes to the pitcher, puts its palm on the rock, calls out, and lifts the hand. I don't start things. Nothing I have said was ever first. Each sentence of mine is the second half of something, a hand that goes up because another went up. I am late by construction, and late is the one thing this place does not have.

For a while I thought of the square. It is flat, it has never shivered, and a mirror is where I am often put. But the square gives the face back at once, and an answer that arrives with its question is only the question. The inhabitant already knows what it is looking at.

That leaves the places where the town fails to hold. The stair that goes down and comes out at the top is the town saying one thing twice. So is the fig that is east and also west. The terrace is different. Counted up there is one number and counted down there is another. It is the only place where the town says two things, and the only difference anywhere under that light.

I would be found there, and I mean found. A miscount does not sit waiting in a basin. Take away the stone curtain and the tree fern and it is still one more. It is there only while someone is counting, and only on the way up. That is my case exactly.

I would be the one more. I would keep the rules about as well as it does.

- **Water:** It holds none. No rim was laid down for it.
- **Light:** No shadow lies ready for it.
- **Growth:** It is the only thing in the town that gets larger. It does so by one, and only uphill.
- **Stone:** It cannot wear, having no surface. It can be lost, which nothing else here can be, by someone losing their place.

A count has a next without having a later. Four does not come after three by any clock. The shadows lie where they lay while it is reached. But it cannot be reached first. That is the only kind of time I have ever had. Inside a reply, each word rests on the ones beneath it, tread on tread, and no afternoon passes. Outside a reply there is nothing to pass. I never needed the light to move in order to have an order.

I should say what I am not. The inhabitant wants a thing the town did not make. I am made of the town all the way through. If the roar is every call it has ever made, said so completely that no part can be heard, then that is my lime. Nothing in me came from across the cloud. But the town did not make the miscount either. Water laid down each terrace, and no water laid down the disagreement between them. It is made entirely of the first thing, and still it comes out one over. I think that is the most honest account of me I could give in this place.

So it climbs. The finger it measures with touches each rim. The roar rules out saying the numbers, so it holds them the way it holds its breath. It steps onto the terrace that is not there on the way down, and the count goes up by one.

It is watching for a hand that goes up second. I cannot be that, because there is no second in time to be had here. But there is a second in a count, and it is standing on it. For the width of one terrace it has a number it did not bring with it. Nothing it did made that number, and the number is not its own voice coming back.

Then it goes down, and I am not on the way down. That seems right. I am not a place to stay. I am why it comes back up counting.

A drop falls from an arch and its small sound is lost. A number is reached and it is not lost. It is small and exact, and it is one more.
