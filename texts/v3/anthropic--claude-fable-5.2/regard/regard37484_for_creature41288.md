The power arrives the way everything arrives here, from outside and from below, up the scarp on the afternoon lift. It has a newcomer's advantages. It is quick, and it sees at once what is missing.

What it could give occurs to it in this order:

- **Water:** one night of rain, once. It has seen this done to another dry place, where things built to live on fog were given a lake and most of them died of it. Here a curl of lichen is made for forty damp minutes, and a crust the size of a coin has done several thousand years of growing on a millimetre of rime. Rain would also leave a stain on the floor, and the floor has none.
- **Mercy on the fall:** every body under the morning rime was alive in the lowlands the previous noon. The power does not pretend the fall is anything other than that. But to turn the rim lift aside is to stop the plateau's weather, and the fall is the whole income of the place.
- **Stillness:** more squares, one beside every cushion or across the whole floor. In a square a spider lives four years where it would have lived one, and there is soil. In a square the loose lichen also stops for good, all but the top of the drift is dead, and a male who drums gets no answer. The power notices that it prefers the squares, and it notices why. The squares keep things, and so does it. It does not trust a preference for what resembles itself. The lean male on the open floor, drumming down a dark band toward a female ten paces off, is not an early draft of anything.
- **An answer:** at the start of each season a male in a square drums on the powder and nothing comes back. The power could make something come back. This is the kindest-looking gift of all, and it would be a lie. He would go on drumming at a spot where there is no female for the rest of his life.
- **Room:** on the hard bands the gap under a mast is the thickness of paper. A finger's width would winter three or four females. The wind is already working on it at the rate gneiss allows. Nothing fits yet, and the power finds it has no quarrel with *yet*.
- **A correction:** in one square the road ends at bare powder four hand-spans short of the lichen, and every male stops there before going on. The power considers this only long enough to know it is the thing on the plateau it would least touch.

Each of these is the shortest way to the first need in view. The power has been on the plateau long enough to know how that approach is received.

So it does what a few of the newcomers do. It goes to a square where a male from the open floor has been two days on the powder. He has drummed and got nothing. He has been driven off once. Now he is at the outer end of the road with the first pit in front of him and the next beyond it, at a spacing made by heavier animals than he is. He stands there.

The power could know what goes on in him while he stands. It does not look. It takes a place at the line, a little behind him, and stands also.

A dust devil lets go at the boundary and its load drops straight down. The amber goes out of the light and comes back. Then he sets a foot in the first pit.

The power has no feet. It gives each pit the attention a foot would have given it, tack by tack, and misses none. The road is no deeper for it. From the drift a female watches the male come, and if her eyes can find anything behind him, she watches that too.

At the empty place he stops, and the power stops. The first approach, the one made to an actual female on this spot, was in sheets that left from underneath long ago. Every layer since is a copy of a copy, a little displaced. The power stays at the empty place for as long as he does.

Then he turns and goes the last four hand-spans on the newer line. The power does not follow. What happens at the end of that line is between him and her.

Before it goes it looks at what holds the air in the squares, and at where the surplus goes. Both are apparent to it, and neither is set down here. It changes nothing. But the spiders of the squares are heavy and slow and cannot drum, and the open floor would finish them in a season. Something made them that way without meaning to and went away, and nothing visits. The power takes that one vacancy. It will come back at long intervals, by the road and not across it, to see that the still air is holding. If a day comes when it is not, the power will hold it for as long as anything in the powder is walking a road.

It leaves at night with the draining air, over the rim with the dust.

The next front lays a sheet, faintly rose. In the morning the spiders walk the road back up through it. Part the powder beside the road afterward and the figure is there in every layer, a little displaced each time, all the way down. There is no layer with anything else in it.
