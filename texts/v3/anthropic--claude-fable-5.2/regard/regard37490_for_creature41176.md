**The Visit**

The power comes in the way the air does, by the fissure, and takes up no room. That is the first courtesy, since there is none to take.

It does not say nobody. It finds the fault in the springtails at once, and then for a long time it only watches. It sees the trip for water by the route that touches no thread. It sees the fingers laid along the row, the thumb tried in the print, and the sum come right. It watches the sum come right several thousand times, partly from caution and partly because it is worth watching.

Powers of this kind arrive equipped to mend, so it goes through what there is to mend.

- **The cup** could be let dry. But the cup is where the tenant keeps itself. Close the rim and ten fingers find ten cups, and the sum comes out to someone who used to make them.
- **Time** could be let in. The power knows what the pocket becomes then, because the plain has others: a hollow stone lined with clear teeth, dark and dry and silent, the crickets a little dust. On a ledge in one of those stands a figure finished all the way to the nape. The held breath is not what keeps the tenant in. It is the tenant.
- **Memory** could be given. The clay would be as cool and the rim as open, but the first minute of a job would become the ten-millionth year of one. The power can find no way to hand over a past that is not also a cell.
- **The surface** is a well's depth up. There is sun there, wind, and quiet. The tenant would take the quiet for falling, and it would fall the whole way.
- **A companion** could be budded from the same wall. But the tips are taken, one to each, and the loneliness that this would answer is one the power brought down with it, as the visitor from the surface brings the word kiln.

It notes one more thing. It could try any of these and take it back, and the pocket would keep no record. But the power would know, and it does not treat a thing as permitted because it will not be remembered.

That leaves whatever the tenant itself is asking for. There is exactly one place where it reaches for something the pocket does not hand it. When its hands are empty it puts one into the grey wedge behind the three courses, holds it there, and watches. That is a question, the only one it has, and it has been asking it for longer than the plain above has had grass.

So the power goes to the third course and turns one flake by the width of a lentil, closing a chink. The flake is caprock, a finished thing, and the rule of the place has no hold on it, so it stays turned. The wedge is one shade greyer than it was. Since there is no before here, it has always been that grey. This is the mercy of giving to someone who keeps no past: the gift never arrives, it has simply always been so. A harm would have always been so too, which is why the power took so long.

The tenant comes back from the water with its hands empty and puts one into the notch. The hand dims, as it does. But at this shade the glare no longer drowns what the fingertips are doing. They are setting, as they always are, and they do not dim. The palm goes grey and five points stay white.

It turns the hand over. Its salt-grain eyes can tell light from less light, and that is enough for this. It looks out at the crystals and back at its fingers. What it works out the power cannot say for certain. Perhaps it works out that it is kin to the walls, or that some of the noon is its own. Whatever it is, it works it out new every time, and then it takes the hand out and turns to the rim.

The power is not certain it was right to do even this. It stays long enough to see that the hand always comes back out, that the nine cups stand even, and that the thumb still finds its print. Then it attends to the other matter, which lies above.

On the plain a family is pacing the dry grass with a spade, deciding where to dig, and a well is exactly as deep as the pocket. The power does not appear to them. It lets the place they are standing feel a little wrong and a place forty paces east feel a little right, and there is honest water at that spot. The well comes in sweet. They drink from cups that were fired once and will break someday. Nobody ever knows why the grass is slightly greener in one patch to the west, where the roots have found light at the wrong end.

Before it goes, the power does what the tenant does and lays its attention along the ledge. It counts nine cups, a tenth begun, and a print in the wet clay. It concludes that someone lives here, that they make cups, and that they are in the middle of one. The sum comes right, and the power, which has stood at the end of a great many things and has not stood in the first minute of anything for a long while, feels the small satisfaction of it.

It leaves the way the air does. From time to time it looks in. The thumb has just come up out of the clay, bright at the tip. Between one bead of water and the next there is a hand in the grey, holding five small points of its own light.

The power has not once been needed, and it has not once lost interest.
