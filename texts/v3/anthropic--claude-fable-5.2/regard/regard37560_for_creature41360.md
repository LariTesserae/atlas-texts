The power does not go down at first. It knows what the place does without testing it, so no leaf is torn to see the leaf come back. It also knows that a visitor is not kept here but a visitor's acts are, in order, by the only thing in the basin that takes in anything new. Every leaf after the first has the knife line in it. Anything the power does will be in every leaf after that.

So it thinks at the rim, where thinking leaves nothing.

It could let the sun go. The agreement would stop, and the forest with it, and the morning would come on everywhere at once. The scarp would slump and the cleft would open, close to where the sheaf has them. But the released rock would carry no knife line and no torn leaves. It would be a later, and not the one the sheaf is. And a morning that simply arrived would put nothing out. There would be a day in the basin, and nothing to be it.

The forest is not holding anything prisoner in any case. It cannot change, so it gives up every layer that notices a change, and the pan is ankle-deep in what it has given up. The count is the same in every leaf, and the power does not need to ask whether it was once higher. The moment is being let go already, a lamina at a time, and what it is let go into is alive. There is nothing here to free.

It could feed the sheaf. It could go through a kilometre of drift in no time and stand every agreeing flake at the sheaf's foot. When a leaf is taken in, though, a riffle runs up the sheaf and back, and sometimes another leaf drops out. That is the agreement being made. Sorted from outside, it would be the power's agreement, finished on arrival. The basin has one of those.

It could make events for it. The late leaves are full of these already: leaves that stayed torn, a stone out of its socket. Each was done by someone finding out what the place would do. The power has nothing to find out, and it can think of no mark of its own that it would want carried in every leaf from here to evening.

It could make a second one. A sheaf that agreed with the first would only be more of the first, and one that did not would stand on other ground. They could share a plate and never be in the same place.

It could set the sheaf right. From the rim it can be watched climbing down nothing on flat plate, bedding in solid rock, and waiting with its top leaves turned up under a crown where no glider is. The wish to mend this is the hardest to put down. But the sheaf does not stumble. Nothing it rests on gives way. It is on its own ground, a quarter of an hour on, and on that ground the glider has landed.

What is left is small.

The power tells no one. A torn leaf carried to the rim is not in the hand at the top. A flake would be.

It keeps the sheaf's position. The forest keeps the moment and the sheaf keeps what was done, and nothing keeps where the sheaf is. This can be done from anywhere and leaves no mark.

And it goes down once, at a visitor's size and a visitor's pace, because the sheaf comes to visitors and can have no other company. The walls stand off at the same distance all around for some hours, and its position is not kept. Then the canopy opens. The power walks out along one of the shingled bands, where the drift has already been gone through, and sits.

The dark stroke stands a long way out. Then there is a shine, left of where it stood. Then it is close, long and low, and the tick runs down it from leaf to leaf. It travels once around the place where the power sits and finds nothing lying uppermost. It draws up to chest height. Edge-on it is as dark as the side of a book. Then it turns, and the far trees show through it.

The power holds still. The shadow that lies across the power's hands is blurred along one side. When the sheaf shifts, the tick starts at one end and arrives at the other, and it is the only thing in the basin that takes time to hear.

After a while a flake comes up out of the drift at the sheaf's foot. It is passed up leaf to leaf and stands at the top with the sun through it for a few seconds. It is drawn down in. The riffle runs the length of the sheaf and back, and no leaf drops out. Something happened in the basin while the two of them were there, and some layer noticed, and it was nothing the power did. The new leaf has the knife line in it, and the sun a hair past where the last leaf had it, and nothing else.

Then the hindmost leaf tips onto the next, and it goes.

Where the power sat there is a pressed place in the drift, and a band that comes up to it, goes once around, and leads away. The forest keeps no account of the pan, so these stay. They are in none of the leaves. Whoever sat there is not kept.
