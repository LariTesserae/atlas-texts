It came at night, because at night nothing in the tank is borrowed. There was the voice and the temperature, and the power stood on the flags attending to both until morning. Then it stayed through the steam, the wedge of light on the west wall, the white noon, the yellow dust, and the shadow climbing the risers. It had learned long ago to watch one whole turn of a thing before touching it.

There was a list of things it could do, and it went through them honestly.

It could quiet the roar. But this was the first voice the tenant had ever had, after an age of running silent into turf. The crust would soften the walls in its own time.

It could give the water a mind. Then the water would know about the palm, the fern, and the birds on their way. The power turned this over longest, because it looked the most like a gift. But as things stood, no one in the tank was alone. What arrives all night does not wait, and so it lacks nothing. To put a someone in there would be to make the first lonely thing in the yard, and then to owe it company forever.

It could hurry the birds, or the people the steps were cut for. They were coming anyway. The place was only early, and early is not a wound.

It could guard the palm print, since the water that wets it will one day skin it over in stone. But that is the only way this tenant keeps anything. The cord will become a ridge in the ninth step. The hand will become a shallow place in the wall, held under cream-colored mineral far longer than plaster would have held it. The power left the hand to the water.

It could tip the leaf through the notch. It did not. You don't take the one thought of a thing that has only one.

Then it walked up the hill, because the single harm it could find was not in the yard. Where the seep had been, the grass was paler and the ground no longer steamed. But the soil was still dark and gave under a heel. Nobody ever gets all of a spring into a pipe. Lower down, the third joint of the clay run was already sweating through its putty. The tenant was seeing to that too, without hurry.

So everything was in hand. A power that was merely kind would have left then, or done some small thing anyway, to have a share in it.

But one thing remained that no one else was yet there to do. Warm water had been falling into that room for weeks with nobody in it to be warmed. The power did not pretend this troubled the water. It only knew the trade from the inside. Arriving at the same rate, in the dark, for whoever is there or for no one, was its own work, and it had seldom been on the other end.

It took a body for this, since warmth is wasted on anything else. It was an ordinary one, with a stiff back and feet cold from the hillside. It waited on the fourth step until the leaf had left the notch and was well out along the far wall. A body going into a full tank raises the level, and the notch takes whatever is nearest. Then it went down one step at a time, slowly, so the rise was gentle. The drain gulped a little louder for a while.

At the sixth step it did not fit its hand to the print. That place was taken.

It stood at the foot of the steps with the floor cool around its ankles and the heat at its shoulders, the way the tenant stands. Then it crossed to the spout and put its back under the fall. The stream struck the stiff place between the shoulder blades. The pipe coughed once, stuttered, thickened, and resumed. The water tasted of chalk. The rumble sat in the chest like a second, slower heart. The power stayed until the body's hands wrinkled.

The water did not know. It went on arriving. The power said thank you anyway, at an ordinary volume, and the roar covered it completely.

When it climbed out, the level dropped a finger's width below the notch. The drain went silent for as long as the spout took to make up the difference, then began to gulp again. Wet footprints went up the four dry steps and across the flags, and were gone before the steam came. It took nothing but the warmth, which was being given away in any case.

The leaf came round, paused at the notch, and was carried on.
