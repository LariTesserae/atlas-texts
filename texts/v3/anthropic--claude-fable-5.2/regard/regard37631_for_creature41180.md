Echoes come first here, so the power's attention was in the bowl before the power was. It was the foot she went over. It was the jaw that took the knock of her stones.

When the power itself comes, it comes small. It could arrive the size of the weather. It arrives the size of her hand, since everything here is, and settles in the lee of a cairn. When she moves on it follows at the pale creature's pace, a cairn behind. It does this for a hundred remakings of the floor before it considers doing anything else, because it has been wrong before about what people in hard places want.

It looks first for weariness. It finds worn hands, which are a different thing. In the falter, with the stone against her breastbone and her eyes shut, her face is the face of someone hearing a familiar voice through a wall.

It could do a great deal here. It goes through what, the way she goes round the cairns, and sets each one back.

- **The mountain:** it could quiet the vent. But the roar is the water. The lake would fall into its bowl, forty paces of warm pond under rain, and the part above the rim would go, along with whatever happened to be crossing it.
- **The round:** it could keep the note itself, all five cairns held true with no hands on them. She would be a heap of floor with nowhere to go next.
- **Her ears:** it could let sound into her dry. But she can find the note because it is the only thing she ever hears.
- **The gap:** it knows what was set down there, and could make it again to the weight. It would fit her hands. But what was set down there is not lost. It is overhead, once in a long while, too big to be a shape, and she has not kept a lake standing all this time for a copy.

What is left is a matter of timing. The note can be heard only in the falter, and the falter comes when she lifts a stone. When the large thing crosses she is at the wall with both hands in the gap, and no stone is lifted. In all the crossings there has never been a falter. The thing overhead swims in room made by a note it has never heard. She gets its echo beforehand, then the dimness, then the light back. Crouched at a knee-high wall with her hands in the gap, she has her breastbone against the top course. It always has been, and nothing has ever come through.

The power does not touch a stone. At the next crossing, when the dimness is deepest, it leans on the central vent with the weight of one of her hands, and the roar catches.

Echoes come first. What reaches her through the top course, before the lake has even dropped, is the answer. It comes from above. It is the note, and laid over the note is a second one, far lower and far larger, the big one on top where a big one shouldn't go. It holds anyway.

Then the lake drops its hand's breadth. The cairn tops stand dripping, all five, and the wall under her wrists, and the note goes up into the water it holds up while something is there to hear it. Then the sound closes over.

The power had not known what would come back, or whether anything would. It had made a gap the right size and held it open, which is a thing it learned here.

Her hands stay in the gap longer than they ever have, long enough for a pillow to skin over. She doesn't look up. Then she takes her hands out with nothing in them, still cupped, draws them back into her dry and holds them against her breastbone, where a stone goes. The bead of quiet that leaves her is the size of a fist.

The light is slow coming back to what it was. The power notices that and leaves it alone.

She knows a falter that isn't hers. She has handled someone else's work before, and she doesn't go looking for who did it. But before she starts for the next cairn, one hand comes out through the edge of the dry and leaves a pinch of rust thread on the top course of the wall, beside the gap.

The power has been thanked before, usually at more length. This is what the pale creature gets. It is wages, and wages mean the job continues.

So the power has an errand. Once in a long while it goes in to the vent, where no silence has any business going, and leans there with the weight of a hand for half a second. If a crossing comes and she leaves nothing on the wall after, that will have been the last. The rest of the time it is in the lee with the pale creature, a cairn behind her and nearly catching up.
