**The approach**

It comes up the valley on foot. It could have arrived in other ways, but most of them involve light, and it has listened from far off long enough to know what light means here. The rest involve arriving in silence, and it knows what that means too. So it walks, in a body sized to fit under a hand, and lets the roar take the sound of its boots.

The hand closes on its forearm where the hand closes on everyone's. It goes through the pockets and removes everything that could make a light. In this case that takes a while, and the power stands still for all of it. Then its palm is set on a shoulder, and the shoulder starts to walk.

It cannot hum yet. In the first street it goes silent without meaning to and gets walked into, and it is its own fault.

**The count**

A knapper makes it a glass, pitched to the day it came. It is older than the peaks, and it is led by the hand, fed, and passed along. It corrects no one. After a while it sees that the count is accurate: about this place, it is newborn.

It stays until the glass begins to swell against the junction. That is the only length of time the city recognises, and nobody here thinks it long. It knots nets, learns to grumble at the lickers in their own tone, and eats the bread.

At each sleep it lies in the household heap with its back to the Tap's warmth and listens. It listens down the shaft to where the rock is liquid, along the footings, and through the cisterns. It had been told there was a place here and beings living in it. By the time the glass is breathing, it can no longer find the line between them. The dead in the lowest course lift the living, the film eats what falls, and the fliers call in the key of grandmothers. It begins to think of the city as one being, humming and growing taller very slowly.

**What it brought**

It came with the usual gifts: sight, a sky, a calendar, a remedy for the eggers' knuckles, the explanation of the Tap, the news that there are other cities. It goes through them the way the hand went through its pockets.

Here the word for light is the word for a crush.

For the sky, it finds the residents who were once carried down into the sun. Others had come before it, kindly, with lamps, and it learns from them what it would have learned by repeating them. What these residents could not bear was the quiet. The sky is no gift to them yet.

It tells a tender how the Tap works, mouth to temple, accurately. The tender listens as you would to an account of how you made your teeth, then goes back to clearing the growing edge. The explanation was true and of no use.

It also finds, checking, that most of what it would have done is done. The footings follow the rise, the walls carry the droppings down to the film, and the populations don't swing. Whoever grew the first course asked the power's questions long ago, answered them well, and left no instructions. It takes that as a precedent.

**What it was asked for**

It asks people what they would have. Most answers are small: a wider rack room, a netter's place nearer the wall. An old woman in a shimmering district says she wants her street to sing as high as it used to.

It could do that. It works out what it would cost. Every glass on every living chest in the district would be untuned in a moment, leaving a street of people with no age. The hung glasses would stop being the only place where the old note lives.

She strikes her glass for it, unasked, to prove the street has sunk. The beat is a flutter. It tells her she is right, and it turns out that this was what she wanted.

**The pause**

It is on an upper span when the hole opens in the noise and comes across the tower face. Everyone around it hums louder. The power does what no one does and goes quiet. Going quiet beside another is an insult, an intimacy, or a warning. It means the second and hopes to be understood.

The hole stops over it. Something is beside it, and it is dry.

They stay like that. Then the pause goes by.

The power now knows what the thing is, how long it has been here, and that the colonies hold steady because of it. It decides the city's name for it is more exact than any it could offer. When it starts humming again, a stranger's palm settles on its back and stays there for a junction or two, as with a child who has had a fright.

**The slip**

It finds one thing. The footings answer the rise, but the two peaks are not rising evenly. Far under the saddle the rock is holding a strain that it will let go all at once, some generations on. Nothing has ever happened to these people quickly, and the city has no organ for it. The builders missed this, or the mountain has invented it since.

So it goes down, the way the heat comes up, and teaches the rock to let go a little at a time. Above, the city gets a few more rifle cracks per sleep than it used to, and no one's hum wavers. The gift was made in the shape of something they already ignore.

It tells a tender anyway, because it decided early to do nothing here that it would not say. It says the mountain was holding its breath and is now letting it out slowly. The tender says the rock has always cracked. The power lets that stand.

**The lip**

Before it leaves, it goes to the low end of the saddle and stands where the overflow goes over. It listens down the warm few hundred metres and then down the icefall, to the groove.

It can hear where this is going. In time the groove will be a valley, the warm water will reach farther, and the shells will be the first soil this mountain has had. That will be a way down that the city makes for itself, at its own pace, and it will be loud for its whole length. The power could hurry it, and it does not.

**The leaving**

A glass is hung when its wearer dies. The power is not dead, so the glass stays on its cord.

At the head of the valley the hand gives back everything that could make a light. This takes a while. The power carries it all down unlit.

The roar thins to a stadium, then to a waterfall, then to nothing. The far mountains stand in plain view, enormous, making no sound. It finds it does not like them. It hums until it is well out of the mountains, and for some time after.

Away from its junction, the glass is only a note. To learn how old it is, the power will have to come back and strike it in the street. It intends to.
