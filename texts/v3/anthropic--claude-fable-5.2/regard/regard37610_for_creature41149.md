It comes from downwind, since it has the choice and would rather know at once.

It has no body. Here that puts it in established company, alongside the firebox rule, the gulls' grid and the chord, and it takes its place among them without shifting a stone. It arrives as the light begins to thin and stays for one turn of the place: the rope of bats, the petrels like burnt paper, the fox, the roar, the dew, the ticking of the plates.

It had been told of a place and a being, and it looks for the being. The fox is the obvious candidate, since there is only one of it. The power looks into the fox, which it can do, and finds a route, some scabs, and a high opinion of eggs. That nobody relieves it is the counter's remark, and the fox has never made it. It looks into a tick, and the fifteen years are not there. There is a foot, and then there is a foot. It looks into the hen in the ninth tube and finds no patience, which would need something to push against. It finds warmth underneath, the old dead keeping the draught off her tail, and the note all around.

Pity, it finds, has the same fault as the count. The power sets it down and turns to what it brought.

It has mercies with it, carried a long way, and it goes through them one at a time, as the place would.

Water is the first. A seep at the low point of the dish would be enough. But with water, things rot. The husk behind the hen would go soft, and the terraces would go to paste. In the second year something green would come up, the first thing ever made here from nothing, and both rules would be finished. What stood in the hollow afterward would be an oasis. An oasis is a good thing and a different one, and it would belong to whatever came to drink.

It could send the fox a relief, but the grid can afford one fox.

It could move the falcons on. But the petrels come in when the light has thinned to the point at which a falcon begins to miss, and without a falcon nothing would tell them when that is.

It could draw the plugs from the shortened tubes and give the chord back its single note. But the plugs are someone's shelter. And the sharpening is the only mark the tenants have made on the sound that called them. It is their answer, and the power does not correct it.

That leaves the windward plate. The power finds the place where the iron dimples under a thumb, and rests a thumb there. It knows which winter. It looks past that winter to see what is on the other side. The wind comes through the silver flank and the note breaks, or drops, or stops. The purr goes on. It is still pitched a little off a note that is no longer sounding, and it is kept there by birds who learned the interval in the egg from birds who learned it in the egg. Young ones crossing the plain in the dark come down to it, because by then the rumour has been true for a century and does not need the iron to vouch for it. The reason is gone and the rule is in perfect health. The place already knows how to do this. It is doing it at the manhole.

The power, as it happens, knows what was wrong with the manhole. It was nothing much, and it was over long ago. What the rule does now is send the bats out at one end while the petrels come in at the other, so the two crowds never meet in the air. Nobody arranged that.

It takes the thumb away.

It does one thing. Some weeks from now a man will drive out along the old rail bed with an auger and a box of sample bags, because somewhere the price of nitrate has been noticed again. His line would bring him to the hollow from the downwind side. The works gave this place everything it has by leaving. He is the works coming back, and behind his notebook is a road. The power will not touch him. For one hour the wind will back a quarter. He will pass thirty paces off, on what is for that hour the upwind side, see stones to the horizon, and write the plain down as empty.

For that hour the wind will cross the tube mouths at the wrong angle and the chord will fail. The purr will go on without it. No bird will lose its place.

The power sees that the case against the man is also the case against itself. He comes with a purpose, and so did it. The best thing that ever happened to this plain was a door coming off, and nobody meant it. A power that means everything it does is poorly placed to improve on that.

So it takes instruction from the petrels. A voice dead on the note would vanish into it. The power sets its attention a little off the place, close enough to hear the feet through the shell and far enough that nothing in the hollow will ever have to answer it. It means to hold it there.

Before it goes, it stops at the fourth row, ninth tube. The chick has its bill in the air pocket and is listening. Two sounds reach it, and it is learning the distance between them, which is the most useful thing it will ever know. A third sound would be easy to add, a word of some kind. The power is as quiet there as it has ever been anywhere.

It leaves with the dew, in the first hour of light. The manhole is nearer, and stands open. It goes out by the firebox door.
