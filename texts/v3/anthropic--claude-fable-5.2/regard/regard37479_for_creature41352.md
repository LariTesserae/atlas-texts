It comes before the sun, because it has watched who else does.

It brings no light. The hollow has a north window's worth, and anything that glowed in there would be a second sun, and the sun settles what it finds. So the power looks the way the steam carries daylight, evenly and from no direction, and nothing it looks at is asked to be one thing or the other.

First it counts. The lice give a number and then a larger number. The water gives five liters, near enough. The grey ones give ten, and counted again they give ten.

Then it goes through what it could do. The list is long, since it could do anything.

It could widen the band. A hand's width between scalding and frost is not much to live in. But the band is not where the grey ones are confined; it is what they are the size of. In a wider band the heat is different, and a thumb's worth no longer holds together. Something would live in the wider place, and it would not be these ten.

It could quiet the cold at the lip. Then nothing would whiten there, and no figures would stand on the rim at dusk with their legs raised. There would also be no flakes on the floor in the morning, since the same cold cuts them. And the crack under the lip would stay empty, and the count would stay ten until it was nine.

It could learn which figures will walk, and see that only those are brought in. It looks, and finds what the grey ones found without looking: there is nothing to learn. To know beforehand, the power would have to choose beforehand, and then it would be the sun, arriving early.

It could give the tenth a good edge. It watches the tenth draw a flake down the rock, get no tick, put it down, and pick up a worse one. The nine go around it. The power goes around it.

It could soften the knob on the dry wall. Steam would do it in a day. It considers this longest. But what ran down would be yellow water, and not the one who thickened. And the knob is the only thing in the hollow that says a grey one can be lost, which is the same as saying a grey one is someone. It leaves the glass standing edge outward.

It could look into the crack. There may be a figure in it now, going the color of old snow beside a road. It does not look. The tenth was made in the one place that neither care nor the sun got into, and the power takes this as something it is being shown about itself: a reach ought to have a crack in it somewhere.

That leaves the slit.

The flow is three or four winters old and cooling. The power can count this too, and the count comes out the same twice: some winters, not many. One night the vent will slow, the long animal will gather in the basin, the ten will range along the rim facing out, and before dawn nothing will come apart along the old seams.

It could relight the mountain. It does not. Help in that quantity condenses on whatever it touches and waters it down, until what is left is the helper. Withholding is the frost side, and the knob on the dry wall shows where that goes. Between the two there is a band about a hand wide. The power means to live in it, and the right amount turns out to be small.

So on a night when the slit cannot manage its breath, the power pays one in. It is no better a breath than the slit's own. The long animal leans toward the opening and settles back. The ten, along the rim, see what they see every night. Before dawn the seams open from the end nearest the heat.

It will have to be done again. The power could arrange things so that it need not, and it does not arrange that. A thing fixed once is settled, and it has come to distrust settling. This way each bad night asks, and it answers that night, and the next night is asked separately. It is upkeep, bitter and then sweet, in small corrections. It learned upkeep here.

About the last night, when a breath will not be enough, it has decided nothing. It thinks it will be on the lip before the sun. It thinks it will handle whatever it finds there all alike.

It does not leave the way it came. It arrived as clean as steam, any amount, anywhere, none the worse. Now there is a hollow the size of a cupboard that it can lose. That is an impurity, and it gives the power something it did not have before, which is a size. A thumb's worth of it stays turned toward one mountain. Counted, there is one of these, and counted again there is one.

In the morning there are a few dozen clicks on the lip. One still lands flat, though a little less flat than yesterday. The power does not take this for its own doing, and it is right.
