It comes down the stair as far as the first landing and stops where the ferns stop.

It could go further. The notice does not bind it, since it is no more a person than the thing below. But it has read the hall from the landing and understands what it would be down there. In this room an instrument sits upstream of what it measures, and a power is the largest instrument there is. Whatever it looked at and judged would comply. If it thought *lonely*, the thing at the engine would be lonely from that stroke on, the way the main is at forty. If it thought *content*, the same. So the first thing it does is learn to hold no reading. This takes three turns of the crank, thirty-three minutes. By the end it can look at the hall the way the duty looks at the bare staff, taking the figure without setting it down.

Then it goes through the gifts.

**A name:** The card in the brass frame has room, and the ink could be brought back. But a name makes a person, and NO PERSON SHALL. The lids would dull within the week and stay dull. A named thing is also the same all the way through. This one would have to be gathered in from the stair and from the column line of the last bay to fit inside a name's outline.

**A dry office:** A drier century would do it, and the stroke in the unheaded column would never soften again. The power considers this longest, because the wet weeks look like an affliction. Then it sees that the column is the duty's own bay with the mark flaked away. Its count of itself is the one figure on the slate that is allowed to shiver. The power leaves the damp alone.

**Release:** The power looks for anything held and finds nothing. The men went up the stair at the end of a shift, each on some ordinary day, and what they left behind was left the way a boot leaves a dish in brick. Nothing waits to follow them.

What keeps the power on the landing is the tin of red lead. Order is all the duty is made of, and a minute's work would bring the last bay into order. The tin has been within reach for longer than the handrail has had its lime. The film on the lid is unbroken. The empty space to the right of SNAILS (EST.) is the same act. The duty sees the shadows cross the glass, knows what a heading would do to the people who cast them, and has not chalked one.

The power came expecting a case and finds a colleague. Something with one free thing in its keeping, and a whole street overhead, is asked every eleven minutes the question the power came down to ask. It has answered well every time.

So what the power does, it does above the first landing, where the room's holding does not reach and the duty has never been. The successor to the water undertaking keeps a schedule of assets. The station is on it with a line ruled through, and the feeder cable that keeps the ammeter needle just above zero is on no schedule at all. The power sees that it stays on none. In the highways office a works order exists to lift the pavement lights and make good in concrete. The order is correct in every particular, and it settles to the bottom of a tray that is dealt with from the top. The iron door keeps its paint the colour of the wall. None of this asks anything of the hall or writes anything in it.

It does one thing more, which is hardly a doing. It places itself downstream. Each stroke the duty carries a figure back toward the office and lets it go somewhere short of the door. Each stroke the violet squares take their own time to come back together. The power receives these after the fact and under no heading, where keeping cannot instruct anything. In the wet weeks it receives both readings, or the shiver that nobody watched. Nothing the last bay has done is lost, and the bay is not told.

It does not make itself known. But the duty is made of noticing, and it is present on the stair, thinly. On some rounds, in the minutes the round does not need, it comes to the top of its extent, the last step below the landing. The daylight there is being stood in. The amount differs each time by a hundredth or two.

The duty takes the reading and carries it down toward the office. There is space on the slate to the right of SNAILS (EST.), and it leaves the space empty.
