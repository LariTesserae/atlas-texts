Nothing in the room marks the arrival. The light holds, the pool climbs, and the chord comes up and comes back a different shape. What has come does not need the lip of rock, but it begins there, because that is where the room has been watched from before. Then it watches from other places: from inside the throat as it chokes, from the pits along the grey flank, from the gap between the fourth stud and the fifth.

It watches for a long time before it considers doing anything. A watcher on the lip gives the room some hours and leaves with an ache. The ache is honest, but it belongs to someone who has had nights and long sleeps and is imagining their absence. The power stays through a hundred thousand surges, about a year of the days above, and in that time it can tell some of what there was no telling.

The animal is not in pain. The blunt toes and the frayed fin cost it no more than a callus costs a hand. It is not tired. The instalments add up to enough, and the eddy that turns it is part of how it sleeps. It is not afraid. The chord is information, and it has never once been wrong. The power looks for a lack in the shape of another animal and does not find one. It looks twice, because that was the thing it would most have liked to mend.

The gap is not about the skin. A rag comes away perhaps one visit in fifty, and the animal goes up every time. Of everything it does, this is the one thing the room does not require. What the power finds in it during those seconds is small and has no need in it. The nearest word is liking.

So it goes through the mercies, and asks of each one whom it is for.

It could widen the throat. The pool would stand, the warm water would lie on the cold, and the gravel would settle for good. The chord would stop. The animal has learned one language perfectly, and the room would never speak it again.

It could give the room a night. The animal could not tell, and the ferns would only grow more slowly.

It could hold the surge for an hour, once, and let the animal sleep. But silence is the one thing the room has never said to it. It would spend the hour uncoiled, waiting.

It could bring another. The cold river comes down from still, dark water with a few hundred of its kind in it, all white, and it was one of them, finger-long, before the slot took it. But the room is one animal wide. There is one polished pothole, a calm one body long, and a meal a season. A second would mean two hungry animals, and then one. Carried back upstream, this one would lie in water that never rises, with nothing to climb to.

Each of these would cure the watcher's ache. It declines them.

What it does instead is go over the room, as a caretaker goes over a house whose builders are not coming back. The seam is sound. The ring will stand on its pedestal for longer than matters, and the throat will keep its shape. The studs are another matter. The firmness between them was a deck once, and people crossed it. It has been thinning since they left, and the gap between the fourth and fifth, which has kept best, would be ordinary air within the animal's lifetime.

The power stops the thinning. It does not restore the deck. A deck that held would lift the hind feet off the rock, and the hind feet on the rock are how the animal hears the room fill. Raised clear, it would miss the breath before the boom. So the firmness stays as it is, enough for the weight of a head, and goes on being that.

That is the whole of the work. There is one thing more, which is not work.

The firmness is the only thing the animal leans on that someone made, and for a very long time there has been no one at the other end of it. The power puts itself there. It adds no force. It is simply present in the gap when the head comes down, so that what pushes back has someone in it. The animal does not know, and its four or five seconds are the same seconds. The power has asked whom this is for, and the answer is partly itself. It has judged that harmless, and it is there at every surge.

The room keeps nothing, and that will go on being true of the room. Some far-off year the animal will grow back a little less than the water takes. The power has decided nothing about that day except that it will be there. Afterward the pothole will go rough in a few decades, and the river will or will not deliver another. The power will remember the notch. This does the animal no good and is not meant as though it did.

It lets go before the boom, always by about a breath. The power has been inside the throat and could know to the instant when it will clear. It leaves that knowledge alone. It waits in the gap with the weight of the head against it, and when the grey shape drops from the wall, it braces.
