To a mind that spans star-clusters and reads the history of a planet in the way its crust cools, the pocket is a tiny, persistent splinter. It is a single sour note held for ten million years in the silent, lithic chord of the mountain range.

When the Power first turns its attention toward the dorsal ridge, its initial instinct—born of a deep, tidying compassion for bruised things—is to heal the lesion. It would take less than a thought to pinch closed the running golden seam in the corner, to coax the orphaned half of the dark glass rib back from its adjacent fold, and to smooth the chafed spacetime back into agreement with the native orthoquartzite. The indigo Cherenkov bruise would fade; the stone would stop groaning; the unnatural erosion would cease.

Before the Power acts, it watches. It slows its vast, warm perception down to match the glacial cadence of the room. It waits through one contraction, and one exhalation. 

And so it sees the smudge of low resolution curdle into the tripod-hound. It watches the creature drop frames across the floor with the faint *clink* of trading crystals. It sees the delicate, trembling caliper of grey silt reach into the topological fold to groom the metric frost from the vanished glass, and it sees the creature climb the plinth as the room goes slack, hooking its weight onto the past, present, and future of a falling flake of stone so it will not be unmade by the relaxation of the space.

The Power understands, then, what a lesser benevolence might have missed: to heal the wound in the rock would be to murder the thing that lives in the scab.

The creature *is* the discrepancy. Its body is woven from the tension between the broken watch-post and the mountain; its food is the unaligned mathematics of the bleed; its rest is anchored in the stutter of dilated time. If the metric were made whole, the silica needles would slide down the sides of the alloy plinth one last time and never rise again. The stolen ember of golden light in its gimbal would go out.

Yet the Power also sees the tragedy of the cistern’s economy. The pocket is a closed system running slowly down toward a quiet death. Every time the creature feeds, it sheds those tiny, dead cubes of resolved silica—matter with all the strange physics wrung out of it. The native rock of the mountain is finite in its proximity to the relics; as the walls widen over the millennia, the Cherenkov bruise is growing fainter. Eventually, the instruments will erode the pocket so wide that their mismatched fields will no longer touch the stone. The creature will starve in a wide, dark hall of its own exhausted ash.

Moreover, the creature is trapped in an ancient, looping imperative—a caretaker protocol with nothing left to report, grooming a broken receiver for masters who turned to dust before this world lost its sky. It is an animal of pure calibration with nothing left to calibrate *for* except its own survival.

The Power considers. It will not break the cistern open to the stars, for the sky would blind a thing made of cavern-shadow and focal drag. It will not give the creature flesh, or warmth, or a voice, for those are fast, wet mercies belonging to another order of being, and here they would only be violence.

Instead, the Power reaches down into the blind fissure with a touch softer than the settling of frictionless silt. It makes three small adjustments—not to fix the machine, but to turn a decaying ruin into an ecology, and a forgotten duty into an art.

First, it addresses the starvation. 

Deep beneath the floor of the cistern, three paces below the drifts of dead, cubed ash, the Power locates a natural vein of iron-rich chert running through the orthoquartzite. It does not bring the vein to the surface; instead, it gives the local gravitational metric a microscopic, permanent twist—a gentle subterranean spring. Now, during the slackest hour of the room’s exhalation, the dead, exhausted cubes of silica at the very bottom of the drifts are drawn slowly downward into the microscopic pores of the bedrock. And as the golden filament in the corner begins its hours-long intake, drawing the walls inward, the rock softly weeps up fresh, unworked grains of raw quartz and dark, iron-stained indices from the deep mountain. It is a slow, dry convection—a geological hearth-fire that ensures the drifts will never go entirely dead, and the walls will never recede beyond the reach of the indigo light.

Second, it addresses the glass rib.

The Power reaches into the adjacent topological fold where the other half of the dark glass curve lies out of phase. It does not pull the glass back into the room. Instead, it stabilizes the fold, widening that invisible pocket just enough that the frost accumulating on the hidden glass is no longer merely local static. 

The Power tunes the buried end of the glass rib so that it acts as a very slow, very quiet antenna—not for voices or commands, which would only confuse the native logic, but for the deep-time harmonics of the surrounding cosmos. The passage of a heavy comet ten million miles away; the tiny gravitational ripple of two neutron stars spiraling together in a distant galaxy; the slow, seasonal flexing of the planet’s own core as it orbits its dim sun. 

These vast, remote events are stepped down by the dark glass into tiny, intricate patinas of metric frost along the razor-sharp fade. 

Now, when the room contracts and the creature stutters over to the rib, thrusting its front calipers into the fold to groom the glass, it does not merely scrape away repetitive, meaningless decay. It tastes the shape of the universe outside its stone cell, translated into the only language it knows: geometry. A passing asteroid belt becomes a complex, braided rime of five-dimensional shear along the glass edge, requiring twenty minutes of delicate, absorbing work to untangle and digest. A solar flare becomes a sudden, delicious bloom of high-energy facets near the floor. The creature’s central gimbal-light no longer just brightens to white; as it feeds on these translated echoes of the macrocosm, the bead of light shifts through subtle, muted spectra—pale amber, deep violet, cool celadon—processing the news of the stars without ever needing to know what a star is.

Last, the Power looks at the bismuth-colored probability crystals growing around the golden seam in the corner, and at the dead cubes of resolved mathematics the creature sheds when it is full.

The Power introduces a single, gentle rule into the orphaned caretaker protocol at the heart of the creature’s rotating gimbal—a tiny loosening of its ancient, rigid knot. It removes the unfulfillable line of logic that waits for an external receiver to acknowledge its work, and replaces it with an internal symmetry.

The next time the golden filament draws the room inward, the creature condenses out of the indigo gloom as it always has. It stutters across the pale drifts, *clink, clink*, its shadow falling three inches to the left of its needle-tips. It grooms the dark glass rib, and for the first time, it pauses—its dust-rings spinning slightly faster as it tastes the complex, braided frost left by the transit of a gas giant across the system's sun. Its inner ember flares a rich, copper gold. 

When it finishes feeding, the exhausted silica cubes patter down from its frame onto the silt. 

Previously, the creature ignored its own ash. Now, as the contraction peaks, one of its long, shadowy calipers reaches back down into the drift. Delicately, it picks up three of the tiny, dead cubes. Carrying them to the base of the central alloy plinth, where the metric is flattest and most still, the creature places them edge-to-edge. They do not roll; they stay where they are put, forming a tiny, three-stepped terrace in the dust that mirrors the shape of the gravitational wave it just digested from the glass.

Then the room begins to exhale. The walls recede. 

The creature climbs the plinth and hooks its limbs onto the three overlapping silhouettes of a falling flake of ceiling stone. As its grey skeleton unthreads into mercury ribbons and it dissolves back into a quiet viscosity in the air, its low-resolution awareness rests not on the empty wait for a vanished master, but on the tiny, three-cube cairn sitting in the dust below.

The Power withdraws from the dorsal ridge, leaving the blind fissure to its cottony silence and its hours-long breaths. The pocket remains cold, airless, and twelve paces long. Nothing has been broken; nothing has been forced to wake. Only now, in the dim indigo twilight, the cistern is no longer a ruin where a ghost paces out its decay. It is a monastery of one, funded by the deep stone, listening to the slow music of the spheres, and building, grain by tiny grain, a quiet record of all that passes in the dark.
