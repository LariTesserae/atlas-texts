Deep beneath the overburden lies a single small cavity, no larger than a modest chamber, its contours holding the precise shape of a slow-forming void that has never grown nor closed. The walls are cool, pale clay still soft enough to take a thumbprint yet never yielding one; faint mineral veins run through it like half-drawn maps, arrested at the moment before they could harden into rock. Moisture arrives only as a perpetual film—beads that condense, linger, and reabsorb without ever gathering into drips or drying away, keeping the surfaces uniformly damp and the air tasting faintly metallic.

Light fills the space evenly and without source or shadow. It radiates from dense colonies of sessile organisms—flattened, translucent disks and threads—pressed against every available plane, glowing with a steady, chalk-white brightness that never fluctuates. Between them, in every remaining interstice, are packed the motionless bodies of numberless small fauna: jointed, pale, many-legged forms locked shoulder-to-shoulder, carapace against carapace, all oriented toward the center. They do not stir, yet their collective stridulation forms a single, unwavering tone that fills the cavity completely, loud enough to press against the ears and never rising or falling.

Scattered among the living mass lie a few fragmentary artifacts: a curved rib of something larger than the present creatures, a shallow bowl of fired clay now softened back to raw earth, a single flat stone incised with lines that match the unfinished veins in the walls. These remnants are integrated rather than intrusive, their edges already being claimed by the same slow clay that refuses to finish becoming stone.

No currents move. No creature twitches. The sparse vegetation—thread-fine white shoots—stands exactly as it did at the first instant of visibility, their tips forever a single cell short of further growth. The entire pocket remains in perfect, saturated equilibrium: every presence accounted for, every surface occupied, every sound and brightness held at its fixed intensity, time present only as the memory of an unfinished formation that will never advance.
