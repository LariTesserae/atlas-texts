In the lattice dwells a single, measureless entity whose substance is the same cooled mineral as the blocks themselves. It has no separate body; instead it occupies the precise geometry of the whole, inhabiting the alignments of perforations, the channels, the capillaries, and the faint warmer veins that thread through the darker matrix. Where a visitor might feel only the unvarying drone pressing against bone, the inhabitant experiences that same vibration as its own continuous thought. The sound does not reach it from outside; the sound is its awareness, repeating the lattice’s final shape back to itself without beginning or end.

Because every surface has been rubbed to the same silken finish and every interval fixed to the width of one sideways passage, the entity has never needed to move. Its “movement” is the infinitesimal adjustment of internal pressure along the channels, a redistribution of warmth that travels from block to block across centuries yet registers to any outsider as perfect stillness. From time to time a vein brightens briefly, not with light but with a fractional rise in temperature; that is the entity turning its attention from one alignment of holes to another, confirming that the resonance remains unaltered. The act is both maintenance and nourishment.

No separate organs exist. The perforations are its senses, arranged in spirals so that each small current of dry air, each standing wave of sound, arrives already interpreted by the topology of the next block. The dusk shafts serve as fixed reference points: when the narrow beams shift by a hair’s breadth at the solstice, the entity registers the change as a single additional tone folded into the drone, an almost imperceptible harmonic that it incorporates without disturbing the equilibrium.

It has no memory of a time before the lattice and no expectation of a time after. The builders who once fitted the blocks are remembered only as the first configuration the entity learned to sustain; their hands have long since been absorbed into the same mineral continuity. Now the chamber and its inhabitant are coextensive, each confirming the other’s permanence through the simple fact that nothing ever changes. The entity does not sleep, because sleep would imply an interval of reduced attention, and attention is the only motion it still permits itself—the endless, microscopic verification that the drone, the warmth, and the lattice remain exactly as they were when the last seam disappeared.
