The benevolent power arrives without announcement, as such things do: a shift in the quality of the dusk rather than any new light. It perceives the plateau not as a problem to be solved but as a completed phrase in a longer sentence it need not finish. No decree is issued. No vent is widened or sealed; no machine is righted or buried deeper. The power simply recognizes that the existing arrangement already contains its own mercy—the lizards finding the last warmth of the stone, the cushion plants measuring their shadows to the millimeter, the condensate forming and vanishing like a held breath that is never held too long.

What it adds is invisible to the eye and therefore changes nothing that can be weighed. It loosens, by the breadth of a single grain of sand, the tolerance each creature already carries for the heat and the dryness and the patient arithmetic of their neighbors. The lizards, without knowing why, linger half a heartbeat longer against the cooling basalt before retreating. The beetles, crossing the same meniscus of water and dust, pause an extra moment as though the droplet itself had grown slightly more willing to remain. The hares, at the blue hour, find the low hum of the turbines fractionally more companionable, less a warning than a shared pulse.

Over centuries these minute relaxations do not accumulate into abundance; they only prevent the margin from narrowing. The same pipe lips shelter the same lineages. The same domes of plant matter trap the same reluctant dew. The turbines continue their quarter-turns, now and then, without purpose or complaint. The plateau remains what it has become: a surface on which rock, metal, and the sparse living things meet without claim or conquest.

The power stays only long enough to witness one full cycle—steam rising, condensing, vanishing; a lizard drinking; a beetle clicking across steel; a hare slipping between pads of concrete—and then withdraws, satisfied that nothing here requires rescue. Its only lasting trace is the continued existence of the place exactly as it is, unamplified, unspared, and therefore still itself.
