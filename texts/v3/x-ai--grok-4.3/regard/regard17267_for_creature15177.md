The power arrives not as a rush of wind or a voice, but as a change in pressure so slight that only the lichen registers it. For one cycle of the spray ring the water arrives half a degree warmer. The difference is nothing a thermocouple would notice, yet the photosynthetic partner, long starved for anything but the sodium spectrum, expands its thalli by two cell-widths before the next reclamation. The consortium feels the shift as a brief abundance and answers by metabolizing the lubricant mist a fraction more completely, leaving the bearings fractionally cleaner. The turbine’s shriek loses a single overtone.

That is all the power does at first.

Over the following weeks it learns the rhythm of the place the way a reader learns a difficult poem: by lingering on the same lines. It watches the nematodes rafting in their condensation drops and notices how the spider-silk thread, already colonized at one end by lichen, has become a migration corridor. The power does not add creatures. Instead it permits a single spore that had been drifting for years inside the bypass manifold to settle on the thread rather than being swept onward. The spore is a tardigrade in its tun state. When the next spray arrives it rehydrates, uncurls, and begins to graze on the bacterial film. Its metabolism is so slow that the closed loop does not register the extra consumption; the only evidence is a new, almost imperceptible clearing in the biofilm that reduces bearing noise by another half-decibel.

The power’s second intervention is smaller still. It slightly alters the expansion coefficient of three rivets on the lee side of the chamber so that when the metal cools after each spray cycle they contract a micron less. This creates a hairline gap that never existed before. Over months, moisture collects there long enough for a second species of yeast to establish itself—one that had been present only as dormant cells in the original fabrication water. The new yeast produces a faint surfactant. The surfactant changes how the next film of condensation beads form, which in turn changes how the nematodes ride between perforations. Their population distribution shifts by a few dozen individuals. Nothing larger notices.

After that the power mostly watches.

It watches the lichen overgrow the spider silk until the filament is no longer foreign but part of the seam. It watches the tardigrades enter and exit tun in precise synchrony with the valve clicks, using the ultrasonic vibration as a clock. It sees that the chamber’s self-contained metabolism now includes a second, slower metabolism nested inside it, and that both still fit inside the original design tolerances. The larger structure growing around the ridge continues to ignore the pocket; the power ensures it keeps doing so by making one distant pump choose a slightly different duty cycle, routing an extra length of pipe elsewhere. The node remains sealed, high, wet, and unvisited, yet it is no longer suspended in its first hour. Time has begun to move inside it in the only direction life tolerates—toward greater internal distinction.

The power does not name what it has done. It simply continues to pay attention, which for a power of this kind is the same as continuing to exist in the vicinity of the chamber. The lichen cannot tell the difference between attention and blessing. Neither, in the end, can the water.
