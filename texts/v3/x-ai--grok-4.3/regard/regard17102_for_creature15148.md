The power arrives not as wind or rain or any alteration of light, but as a deeper stillness laid over the one already present. It does not descend; it recognizes. In that recognition the basin is seen entire—every scale-insect, every pellet of bound silt, every mineral ghost left by the encysted films. Nothing is added and nothing is taken away except the faint pressure of being overlooked.

What the power does, then, is infinitesimal and permanent. It draws the high cloud-layer a single degree closer to the temperature at which its own droplets will linger one heartbeat longer before the warmth reclaims them. The change is smaller than the difference between one shadow and the next. Only the mycelium feels it, as a slightly slower retreat of its hyphae; the pellets it forms afterward are, for a few cycles, fractionally darker. The scale-insects drink one more time before retreating, their translucent abdomens holding the extra measure until the next true rain. The disk-colonies separate and reform with one additional exchange of dissolved minerals. The linear marks in the silt, already half-erased, receive another layer of the quiet elevation that texture alone can record.

Beyond that single adjustment the power does nothing further. It does not multiply the cushions, nor widen the pans, nor quicken any tempo. To do so would replace the equilibrium with abundance, and abundance here would be another name for noise. Instead the power folds itself into the held note, becoming part of the interval between one condensation and the next. From then on the basin continues exactly as before, yet the continuation itself is now under gentle guardianship: no desiccating gust from beyond the rim will ever reach it with quite the same force, and the rare larger rain that does arrive will be met by ground that has, over centuries, retained a fraction more of itself.

The place therefore remains what it has always been—an equilibrium expressed at the scale of droplets and years—while also becoming something slightly larger: a single, tended note within a wider composition that the power keeps hearing. The insects, the fungi, the films, and the stones continue without ever knowing they have been noticed. Only the silence, already perfect, now carries inside it the knowledge that it will not be required to end.
