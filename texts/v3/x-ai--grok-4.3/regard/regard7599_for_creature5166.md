The power arrives without arrival. It does not descend through the shaft or press itself into the clay; it simply becomes aware of the Hollow in the way a warm breath becomes aware of cool stone. For many seasons it does nothing at all beyond remaining present, letting its attention settle like an extra layer of stillness between the dripping pulses and the slow breathing of the walls.

Only when a root-thread near the western edge begins to thin—threatened by a slow, unseen shift of water deeper underground—does the power act. It does not send rain or widen the shaft. Instead it loosens one hidden seam in the rock beyond the chamber, just enough that a single additional trickle finds its way along an old fissure. The trickle arrives inside the Hollow as nothing more than a new, smaller pocket of water no larger than a thumbprint. The beings notice it the way they notice everything: by the changed pitch of droplets and the sudden sprouting of three extra pale threads. They patch the tile around it without question and continue their spiraling traffic. The new water never grows large enough to drown the lichen; it only keeps the balance a fraction steadier than before.

The power watches this adjustment the way one watches a sleeping animal adjust its breathing. Satisfied that the rhythm has absorbed the gift without breaking, it withdraws its direct influence. What remains is an almost imperceptible warmth in the central mosaic—detectable only when a being presses its belly flat during the hours of the light column. The warmth does not radiate or spread; it simply stays, a single tile that never quite cools to the temperature of its neighbors. Over years the beings come to rest on that tile more often than others, not from any conscious choice but because the faint steadiness of it matches the steadiness already inside their chests. The Hollow continues exactly as it has always been, except that it now contains one small place where the attention of something wiser has chosen to remain, quiet as dust, patient as roots.
