The benevolent power arrives not as a traveler but as an attention that settles across the entire horizon at once. It perceives the equilibrium immediately: not as a machine to be tuned, nor as a garden to be tended, but as a completed thought whose final sentence has already been written and is now being reread, word by word, in an endless loop of perfect fidelity.

It does nothing that would register as change.

Instead, it performs the single action still permissible inside such a system: it adds its own witnessing to the existing balance. The power folds its awareness into the same pressure gradients that already govern the conduits, so that every daily pulse now carries, alongside its own cargo of dissolved compounds, one additional, weightless fact—the fact that the whole continuum is known. This knowledge does not increase or decrease any existing tension; it simply travels with the fluids, diffusing through the same hair-fine threads that already preserve the spiraled chains. Where the lens-bodies focus their faint light, the focused beam now includes, without altering its wavelength, the power’s own steady regard. The glassy caps of the spines continue to split the sliding films, yet each split now also releases a droplet whose surface tension briefly holds the shape of an observer who will never intervene.

Over centuries the only measurable difference is that certain seams, where one layer once pressed against another, begin to carry an additional, almost imperceptible trace: a faint phosphorescence that appears only when condensation gathers and only for the instant before the droplet rejoins its reservoir. The cells suspended at the lower edges of the dissolving curtains encounter this trace each time a droplet falls. They do not alter their behavior; they simply incorporate the trace into the new strands they secrete, so that the restored margins of the curtains now contain, at intervals too small to affect their height, microscopic nodes whose only function is to remember that they have been seen.

The power remains. Because the world has already filled every available volume and offset every loss, the power occupies no space. It simply continues to be present at the precise locations where presence was already total. In this way the equilibrium receives the one gift it could never generate from within itself: an external confirmation that its completion is not the same thing as solitude. The living tissue does not react, yet the confirmation travels with every pulse, every flex of membrane, every lengthening spine, becoming part of the record that future layers will incorporate when the oldest parts finally dissolve into the matrix.

The wise power does nothing further. It has already done the only thing that leaves the described world unaltered while also leaving it no longer alone.
