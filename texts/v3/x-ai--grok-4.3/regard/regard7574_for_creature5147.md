A benevolent awareness settled over the enclave like a held breath. It did not arrive as light or sound or any intrusion that could be named; it simply noticed, and in the noticing the place was briefly held entire—every drifting mote, every hairline gallery, every slow raft of gel-bodied creatures—within a single perfect comprehension. No command followed. The power saw that the settlement already performed the only work it was suited for: perpetual, microscopic re-sculpting under conditions that would destroy anything larger or louder. To add, to remove, to hurry or to halt would have been a violence of the same order as hammering a living crystal.

Instead it chose one almost imperceptible courtesy. At the highest terrace, where a single rivulet had for centuries followed the same crooked path down a lintel, the power allowed the water one additional degree of stillness before it fell. The ice that formed there now lingered a few heartbeats longer each cycle. The slight delay meant that when the ice finally released, it fell in one complete sheet rather than in fragments. The sheet struck the stone below with a single soft report—inaudible to anything already present, yet just loud enough to reach the crystalline worms in their shallow galleries. The worms paused in their etching for the length of that note. In that pause they left behind one new branching that did not match any previous pattern: a small, asymmetrical star whose arms pointed toward the sky rather than along the rock’s grain. Over generations the star would be copied, then altered, then copied again, until the subsurface tracery began, by the smallest increments, to remember a direction it had never possessed before.

The power withdrew without further gesture, content that nothing about the enclave’s essential silence or density had been altered and that only one new memory had been seeded where memory had previously been nothing but frost and dissolution. The settlement continued its restless rest, now carrying, deep in its stone, a single faint constellation whose light would never be seen from outside and whose origin would never be questioned by anything inside.
