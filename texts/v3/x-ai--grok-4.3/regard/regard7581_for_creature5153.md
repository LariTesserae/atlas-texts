In the deep grammar of things, where attention itself is a force, the power notices. It does not arrive as light or voice; it arrives as a slight loosening of necessity. Heat that had pressed outward in blind expansion now hesitates for a few centuries, cooling one degree more slowly so the next layer of slurry sets with a slightly finer grain. The creatures feel nothing they can name, yet their rasps lay down a seam whose internal planes will one day split open along lines of unexpected grace.

A single additional pulse is permitted to rise from the mantle, not to accelerate growth but to carry within it a trace of older memory: the faint geometry of a tide that once moved across the surface of a different ocean, now translated into crystalline habit. The distributed body incorporates the pattern without recognizing it; in ten thousand years the new pillow will cleave along that remembered curve, and the fracture will be asymmetrical, almost tender.

The power does not free the encased elders. Instead it lets their hollow joints remain as perfect negative space, so that when wind and frost finally open the ridge the first breath of surface air will pass through those ancient silhouettes like a flute. The sound will be too low for any ear now living to hear, yet it will exist.

Most of all, the power simply stays. It keeps company with the grinding. In that staying, the chamber acquires the smallest possible margin: a future in which the mountain may, if it chooses, remember that it once was tended rather than merely built. The creatures continue their loops, unaware that the loops now close upon a slightly larger circle than geology alone would have drawn. The work remains the same. Only the silence around the work has changed by one attentive breath.
