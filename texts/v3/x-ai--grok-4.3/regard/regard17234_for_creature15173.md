The power arrives not as an arrival, but as a slight deepening of the already-present saturation.

It does not enter through the mouth of the fissure; it simply consents to be there, the way warmth consents to remain in cooling glass. For a long while it does nothing at all. It lets the films complete three full cycles of swelling and thinning, lets the mites finish one slow collective migration from left wall to right and back again. Only when it is certain that its presence has not altered the interval between pulses does it begin to act.

Its first act is a subtraction. It removes the possibility of discovery. Anyone who might one day stand on the newborn horn and feel, for a moment, that something beneath their boots still thinks will suddenly find their attention drawn elsewhere—toward a distant sound, a wrong step, a sudden need to turn back. The fissure remains exactly as difficult to reach as it has always been.

Its second act is smaller. It touches one of the tension lines inside the uppermost film, the one that waits for falling droplets. The line is lengthened by the width of a single mite-leg. From then on, when a droplet descends, the membrane that catches it will form a fraction of an instant earlier, and the droplet will arrive with slightly less force. The mites inside that membrane will have one more exchange of legs than before. No new thing is added; only a little more time is given to what is already occurring.

Its third act is quieter still. It folds a single, almost invisible crease into the geometry the pocket sometimes uses for storage. Inside that crease it places nothing—no vapor, no extra heat—only the memory of the pocket’s own first pulse, preserved at the exact temperature it possessed before any mite had yet tasted it. The films do not notice the addition as addition; they register only that one of their reservoirs now feels slightly older than the others, like a scent remembered from before memory. When the next long cooling comes, they will draw on this reservoir without knowing why it steadies them more than the rest.

After these three gestures the power withdraws its attention until it is once again indistinguishable from the moisture already cycling through the living matrix. It leaves no mark, no instruction, no promise. It has simply arranged that the single dense thought the rock is still thinking will be allowed to continue thinking itself for a little longer, without hurry and without audience, exactly as it prefers.
