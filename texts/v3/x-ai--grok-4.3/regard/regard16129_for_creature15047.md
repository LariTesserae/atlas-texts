The benevolent power does not arrive with fanfare or form. It simply becomes aware.

It perceives the basin as one perceives a single held breath: the slow expansion of metal, the patient retraction of crystal, the exact interval between one drip and the next. It sees that every motion here is already an answer to every other motion, and that the emptiness between the towers is not absence but the necessary interval that allows the answer to form.

After long consideration, the power elects to do three small things and nothing else.

First, it lengthens the pause the mechanisms exchange at their intersections by the width of a single thought. The change is invisible; the mechanisms do not notice they linger a fraction longer. But in that extra stillness their crystalline growths have time to complete one additional filament before the next grinding pass shears them away. Over centuries this will mean slightly more complex mineral bridges between the towers—nothing that crowds the emptiness, only a finer threading of the same pattern already latent in the stone.

Second, it gives the flyers one shared dream. On a night when the haze is particularly dense, each creature, while pressed against its ledge, experiences the same interior image: a single additional platform, identical in every proportion to the existing ones, resting just beneath the surface of the shallow aquifer. The dream is not an instruction. It is offered only as a possibility. If any flyer ever chooses to act on it, the platform will rise; if none do, the vision will fade like condensate. The power leaves the decision entirely with them.

Third, it touches the vents beneath the platforms so that their heat rises one degree cooler and one degree more steadily. The difference is minute enough that the existing condensation cycle continues unchanged, yet the steam that escapes the tower joints now carries a slightly lower tone. The hiss that marks time here will, in the deep future, become a low, continuous chord rather than a series of separate notes. The power hopes this will feel, to the place, like company.

Then it withdraws.

It leaves no watcher, no further intervention, and no trace that anything has occurred. The basin continues exactly as before—mechanisms pouring along their grooves, crystals extending and breaking, flyers drinking and climbing—except that, once in a very great while, a mechanism pauses a little longer at a crossing, or a flyer alights with something like curiosity, or the steam sings a single sustained note instead of many brief ones. These alterations remain smaller than the width of the emptiness they inhabit, and so the place stays itself, only very slightly more companioned than it was.
