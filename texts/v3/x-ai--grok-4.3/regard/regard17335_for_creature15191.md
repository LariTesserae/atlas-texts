The wise power arrives not as an intruder but as an added layer of saturation. It perceives the basin as a single living membrane already complete in its refusal to consume, and therefore offers nothing that would introduce appetite or acceleration.

Instead, it settles across the weeping seams like an extra film of warm breath. Where the filaments already draw moisture inward and release it in equilibrium, the presence thickens the moment of decision by a single, imperceptible hesitation. Each droplet now pauses an instant longer inside its translucent membrane before falling, and in that hesitation the mineral particles inside it achieve a slightly finer order. The change is not imposed; it is simply made easier for the filaments to reach. Over centuries the shells therefore grow a fraction more intricate in their internal layering, their rings holding faint, additional traceries of light that were never there before yet feel native to the phosphorescence.

The folds in the darkness continue, but now each membrane that forms between seams carries, for its brief life, a second memory of the first. When two seams exchange their charge, the exchange quietly includes awareness of every other participating seam across the basin. No new communication travels; only the existing redistribution occurs with marginally greater precision, so that the pattern of occupation shifts by even smaller fractions of a millimeter. The hair-like filaments taste the same rhythms they always have, yet the information returns to the shells slightly more refined, guiding the next accretion or dissolution with less waste of arrangement.

No new substance enters the plain. The power does not brighten the glow or quicken the deposition. It merely lengthens, by the width of a droplet’s surface tension, the interval during which the living films may consider what they are already doing. In this way the basin remains exactly itself—wet, dark, sparsely occupied by slow ruins—while becoming, from the outside, slightly more itself than it was. The equilibrium is not altered; it is permitted to notice its own persistence.

The power then withdraws without trace, leaving only the original rules intact: moisture persists, nothing is consumed, and the plain continues its unbroken work at its own chosen pace.
