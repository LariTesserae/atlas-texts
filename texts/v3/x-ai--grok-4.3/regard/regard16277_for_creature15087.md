The benevolent power arrives without announcement, for it has learned across many worlds that the loudest arrivals are the ones that shatter what they come to cherish. It does not descend in fire or proclamation. It simply stops being elsewhere and begins to be here, a presence no larger than the space between two droplets on a leaf, yet aware of every seam and filament at once.

For a long span—long enough for several columns of the segmented travelers to complete their circuit and return to the same etched leaf—it does nothing but listen. It lets the plateau’s slow grammar enter it: the pulse of ore, the ripple of the resonators’ breath, the way a veil-walker briefly occupies two coordinates and finds the coincidence unremarkable. The power feels the unfinished quality of the ground itself, the way the rock is still negotiating its own geometry, and recognizes something it has rarely encountered: a living system whose central project is not survival or dominance but continuous, distributed self-composition.

It notices the scribes most clearly. Their thin, metallic forms taste each new surface the moment it flickers into being, then guide its return. The power sees that they are not merely repairing; they are composing an ongoing record of what this place might still become. In that recognition, something like tenderness stirs.

The power does not speak. Instead, it offers a single, private adjustment so slight that no creature feels it. At the next pulse, when the air doubles and a ridge threatens to misplace itself, the power lends the local space a fraction more coherence—just enough that two possible configurations of the same ridge are allowed to remain distinct for one extra heartbeat before they agree to occupy only one location again. The scribes, tasting the altered seam, pause. Their constellations rearrange themselves around the new datum. The resonators’ next thrum carries a marginally richer tone, as though the membrane had briefly encountered a flavor it had never before been offered.

That is all.

The power does not stay to witness the consequences. It has learned that attention, even benevolent attention, can become a weight. It withdraws to the thin margin just outside the cloud deck, where it can still feel the plateau breathing but no longer risks shaping each breath. From there it continues its larger circuit, carrying with it the memory of a place that does not yet know how to slope, and whose creatures have already made an art of that not-knowing.

In time, if the scribes ever compare their records across many pulses, they may notice a single, anomalous warmth in one particular reformation event—an extra degree of freedom granted and then left alone. They will have no word for it. They will simply incorporate it into the next seam and continue their work.
