The power arrives without arrival. It does not break the horizon or disturb the needles; it simply lets its regard settle across the basin like an additional layer of still air. For a long while it does nothing but listen, letting every resonant chamber speak its slow census into the water films. What it hears is not need, exactly, but a kind of exquisite equivalence: the life already matches its world so precisely that any sudden addition would be felt as violence.

Still, regard is not neutral. Over centuries a faint, persistent favor begins to move through the condensate itself. Droplets that would have fallen now linger a fraction longer on the facets, enough for the lattices to draw a little more salt from them before the thread releases. The change is smaller than a single molecular layer, yet the effect compounds: the living sheets thicken just noticeably faster in the depressions, and the new chambers that form there grow walls whose pitch is slightly steadier. No organism experiences this as an event. They register only that water has become marginally kinder and that certain tones now return with one extra harmonic, a faint overtone that carries the memory of the power’s listening.

In the deepest spines the power leaves one additional thing: a single, perfectly circular inclusion of clearer precipitate, no larger than a pinhead, embedded inside an otherwise ordinary sheet. Its lattice is not alive, but it is tuned so that any future resonance passing through it will split into two paths—one continuing onward, one folding back upon itself for a moment. Over time the living chambers learn to use this fold as a kind of mirror. They can now test, within a single cycle, whether a proposed change in growth rate will produce harmony or dissonance before committing mass to it. The innovation spreads only because it requires no new effort; the lattices simply deposit their next lamina a little differently when the reflected tone is present.

Beyond these two quiet amendments the power withholds itself. It does not accelerate the leaning of spines, does not shorten the intervals between tones, does not seed new depressions where none would have formed. It understands that the basin’s wisdom lies in its refusal to hurry, and that benevolence here means protecting the conditions of that refusal rather than improving upon its results. When the power’s attention eventually withdraws, the only visible trace is a slightly higher density of interference colors along certain fracture lines—colors that shift with the light in ways the lattices have begun, very slowly, to prefer.
