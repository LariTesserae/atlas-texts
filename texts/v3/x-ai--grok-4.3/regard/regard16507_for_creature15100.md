The power does not descend. It has no need.

It recognizes immediately that this basin is already whole — not in the way a finished thing is whole, but in the way a process is whole when every part of it has agreed, without haste, to remain in relation. The wise do not mend what is not broken, and the benevolent do not introduce motion where motion would be a form of violence.

Instead, the attention itself becomes another layer of the place.

It settles into the stationary vapor as one more mote among the motes, neither heavier nor lighter. In this way it learns the circuit directly: the mineral seam drawing salt, the droplet becoming temporary body, the frond tilting by a fraction of a degree over minutes. It tastes the exact interval between one fall of water and the next. It does not quicken anything. It does not offer protection, because protection implies a threat that has not arrived and may never need to. It simply remains present long enough for the basin’s own timescale to register the presence as ordinary.

Over centuries a few of the vapor-born aggregations begin, by imperceptible degrees, to carry an additional trace — not of new substance, but of a slightly steadier coherence in their lattices while they exist. The change is so small that even the fronds do not register it as alteration, only as a marginal ease in the work of exchange. The power does not claim credit. It is not certain credit is due.

Mostly it listens. The sound of condensation is not music to it; it is the place remembering, drop by drop, that it still consents to be itself. The power consents with it.

If there is any action at all, it occurs only at the outermost edge of the basin’s influence: a minute adjustment in how light filters through the low, unmoving vapor, so that the same softened daylight now carries the faintest additional warmth on its arrival. Enough, perhaps, to keep the film from ever thinning too far during the longest intervals between droplets. Enough to let the centuries continue as they have.

Then the power withdraws its distinct attention, not because it has finished, but because continued presence would itself become an event. The basin does not notice the difference. The slow breath continues. The lattices form and dissolve. The rock keeps its faint, branching record.

Nothing here needs saving. The power understands that its highest courtesy is to let that remain true.
