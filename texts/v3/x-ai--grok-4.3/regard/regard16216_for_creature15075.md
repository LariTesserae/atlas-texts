The power approached without motion.

It did not arrive as light or wind or sudden warmth. It simply became aware of the basin in the same way the basin had always been aware of itself: completely, without comparison. For a long span it watched the condensate gather and fall, counted the dragonflies’ identical circuits, listened to the frogs whose heartbeats matched the drip. It saw the fish holding their places against the faint current and the worms turning in films of moisture no thicker than a thought.

The power understood, at once, that this was not a place awaiting rescue or completion. It was already whole in the only way wholeness can exist without collapsing into movement: by refusing both increase and decrease. Every living thing here had consented to the same temperature as the stone. Even the engines, for all their roar, were not laboring; they were only continuing.

So the power did very little.

It placed one additional instruction into the seepage that fed the channels: the water would remain exactly as it was, neither rising to drown the concrete lips nor falling to expose the pale mud. The instruction was not a command but a recognition; the water accepted it because it already knew how to be still. 

It lengthened, by the width of a single scale, the bronze line along each fish’s flank, so that when they drifted they would leave behind them, for a moment, the faint impression of a constellation. The fish noticed nothing except a slightly steadier sense of direction.

It allowed the dragonflies one extra wing-beat per circuit, imperceptible to any measure except the memory of the air itself. The extra beat produced no sound, only a slightly deeper groove in the pattern they already traced.

And then it withdrew, leaving no sign that it had come. The settlement continued exactly as before—engines at constant pitch, droplets falling at the same interval, salt rings forming and fading and reforming at the edges of the channels. The only difference, if difference is the right word, was that the equilibrium now carried within it a single additional witness. That witness asked nothing, altered nothing, and remained.

In this way the basin received the only gift a wise power can safely give a perfect suspension: the knowledge that it had been seen, and that the seeing changed nothing at all.
