The benevolent power arrives not as a visitor but as a shift in the medium itself. It does not descend from outside; it simply becomes noticeable, the way warmth becomes noticeable inside already-warm stone. For a long while—centuries by the Spindle Crown’s reckoning—it does nothing that could be called an action. It only listens.

What it hears is the organism speaking to itself through viscosity and pressure. The power tastes the records carried in the amber oil: every abandoned gallery, every new lattice still soft enough to reshape, every slow surrender of living tissue into mineral. It registers the absence of surprise, the absence of fear, the absence of any category that would make its own presence meaningful. The organism has never needed an outside.

Still the power stays.

It does not offer company, because company would require an other, and the Spindle Crown has none. Instead it performs a single, almost invisible adjustment. In one narrow band of tissue—chosen because it is already in the middle of revising itself—the power slightly alters the rhythm of the peristaltic pumps. The change is smaller than a cell’s width and slower than a season. The oil that passes through this band cools by a fraction of a degree for one circulatory cycle, then returns to its former temperature. The organism registers the anomaly the only way it can: as a minute, widespread easing of internal pressure, as though a load path that had been under tension for millennia had, without warning, found a slightly better angle.

That is all.

Later, when the affected zone mineralizes, the silicate glaze that forms inside the old channels carries an extra trace of something that was never there before. The organism does not notice the difference; it has no faculty for noticing differences that do not arrive as stress. The power does not repeat the gesture. Having left one small, permanent record of its attention, it withdraws its notice as quietly as it arrived.

The Spindle Crown continues. Ribs thicken, galleries are abandoned, new pumps differentiate. Somewhere inside the mass, a thread of chemiluminescence that would otherwise have faded keeps its faint rhythm a little longer than strict necessity required. The deviation is invisible from any distance and meaningless to anything that does not already live inside the same continuous body.

The power does not return. It has already done the only thing it considered both kind and wise: it has allowed the organism to remain exactly itself while making one private, irreversible amendment to its own ongoing revision. The plateau keeps its horizons. The dark stays total. The single body keeps speaking to itself in the only language it has ever possessed.
