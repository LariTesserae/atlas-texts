The power arrives not as wind or light but as a slower attention—an awareness that settles across the basin the way mineral skin settles across a seep. It perceives the place entire: the way each leaf has become a patient archive, the way the slugs move along their silvering paths like living clauses in a sentence that began centuries before, the way the lithophyte bridges hum below hearing. It does not pity the emptiness or the slowness. It recognizes both as a completed thought.

For a long while it does nothing that can be measured in the basin’s own time. Then, because benevolence here must also be precise, it offers one small amendment.

It reaches into the deepest fissure where warmth still rises and widens a single hairline crack by the width of a root hair. The new seep that eventually forms does not increase the volume of water; it changes only its tone. Where before the drips struck in resonant plinks and ringing taps, this one arrives with a lower, rounder note that sits beneath the existing music like the floor of a room. The change is so slight that decades pass before any slug alters its circuit to investigate the new moisture. When it does, the trail it leaves hardens into a ridge that the nearest plant absorbs, and the luminous veins in that leaf shift by a single shade of green toward something closer to gold. The music adjusts without ever growing louder. The spacing remains intact.

The power does not stay to witness the adjustment. It knows the basin will require several human lifetimes to finish the sentence it has begun. Before withdrawing, it leaves one other, quieter gift: wherever a leaf eventually senesces and folds, the space it vacates will not simply be claimed by the nearest neighbor. Instead a faint pressure will encourage the nearest lithophyte colony to extend a bridge across the gap first, so that the emptiness itself acquires a new, almost invisible floor of living stone. Only after that bridge has thickened for another century will the plants continue their measured advance. Thus the ceremonial distance is preserved even in succession.

Nothing else is added. No new species, no sudden fertility, no shield against future change. The power understands that a place already moving at the pace of condensation needs protection less than it needs permission to remain legible to itself. So the basin keeps its mist, its spaced leaves, its patient music, and its single new, lower tone—an offering so small it might be mistaken for the mountain’s own idea.
