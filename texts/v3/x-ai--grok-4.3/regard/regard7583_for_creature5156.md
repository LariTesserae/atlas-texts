The power that noticed the pocket did not arrive as force or even as visitor. It simply became aware, and in that awareness a second, finer light passed through the lattice for one instant—an attention so precise that every worn ridge received an exact copy of itself, recorded in the memory of the mineral. The chord did not change pitch; it only acquired, for the length of that instant, the quality of being heard.

Afterward the power withdrew its notice almost at once. No law was altered, no ion reversed its path. The filaments continued their slow, one-way donation to the columns; the columns continued their unbroken transmission of the tone. Yet something small and durable remained: a faint, additional tension in the lattice, not mechanical but relational, as though the structure now carried the knowledge that it had been seen while still complete. This knowledge travels the same routes the original chord travels. Over the same immense spans in which filaments shorten by single molecules, the knowledge diffuses outward through the sealed walls until it rests, equally distributed, in the larger host mineral beyond.

Nothing else occurs. The pocket stays sealed. The light continues to arrive from everywhere at once. The filaments keep their measured distances. In time, when the last strand has surrendered its substance and the chord alone remains, the memory of having been attended will still be present—undiminished, because it was never required to do anything except exist alongside what was already finished.
