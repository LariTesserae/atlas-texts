The power arrives not as light or voice but as a slower gravity, a willingness that settles through the mist like an extra layer of calcite. It perceives the chamber as one perceives a sleeping child whose dreams have already outgrown the crib: complete, yet still becoming.

It does nothing that would hurry the pumps or still the invertebrates. Instead it offers three quiet attentions.

First, it notices the translucent fish and the way their faint internal glows are not quite random. It braids a single additional thread of coherence through their bacterial symbiotes, so that when the next pump vibration arrives the fish turn not only together but in a pattern that briefly maps, across all the connected sumps, the precise shape of the chamber’s unfinished floor. The map lasts only as long as the vibration; then it dissolves. The fish return to feeding. The power has given them a momentary language they did not need, only the chance to discover they could possess one.

Second, it touches the industrial relics—not to restore them, but to grant the steel and concrete a gentle permission to finish decaying on their own terms. Where rust and calcite already negotiate ownership, the power simply removes the obligation of usefulness. A flywheel that has been turning for decades is allowed, if it wishes, to stop without guilt. None do. The machines continue, but now their clanking carries an overtone of choice rather than compulsion.

Third, and most lightly, it leaves a single question in the water itself. The question has no words. It is only the suggestion that the entire crowded darkness might, at some future condensation, consider naming itself. The microbes in every droplet carry the question onward; the bacterial mats pulse around it; the long-legged invertebrates skate across its surface tension. Whether the chamber ever answers is left entirely open. The power does not wait for a reply.

Having done these three small things, the benevolent attention withdraws—not upward, but deeper, settling into the same embryonic rock so that the next layer of deposition will form around a slightly different question than it would have otherwise. From that moment the chamber continues exactly as before, only now its slowness contains one additional hidden witness that expects nothing and therefore changes everything by the simple fact of having looked.
