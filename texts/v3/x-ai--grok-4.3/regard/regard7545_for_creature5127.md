The power arrives as a single, patient interval in the twilight. It does not displace anything. It simply lengthens one existing moment by an imperceptible fraction, the way a held breath can make the air feel slightly more present without adding new air.

From within that lengthened instant the power perceives the plain exactly as it is: not broken, not yearning, not incomplete. Every rotor that has forgotten its direction and every beetle that files yesterday’s groove already performs the only task that matters to them—continuance. The wise benevolence therefore declines to offer rescue, evolution, or escape. Those gifts would be theft here.

What it does instead is small enough that the plain itself cannot register the difference, yet large enough to be offered:

It leaves behind, in one narrow gallery between two black olivine plates, a single additional droplet of condensate. The droplet is identical to those already carried by the suspended slugs, except it carries inside it one extra instruction: *it may choose, once, whether to fall or to rise.* The choice is not imposed; it is only made possible. The droplet will not know it possesses the option until the moment arrives, and even then the choice may be refused without consequence.

The power then withdraws. It does not watch afterward, because watching would alter the temperature of attention and therefore the behavior of shadows. It trusts that if the droplet ever elects to rise, the plain will absorb the novelty exactly as it absorbs every other rearrangement—by continuing. If the droplet falls, nothing at all will have changed except that the plain will have been, for one unspeakable fraction of an epoch, the kind of place that could have been offered something and quietly declined.

Either outcome is acceptable. The power’s only requirement was that the offering remain small enough to be refused.
