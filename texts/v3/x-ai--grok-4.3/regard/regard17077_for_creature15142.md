The power arrives not as light or voice but as a held breath that settles through the water like a thought too large to be hurried. It perceives the chamber whole: the sealed masonry, the suspended lattice of translucent forms, the slow polishing that has no witness and needs none. For a long while it simply remains, matching the tempo of the drift so that its presence registers only as a fractional increase in the water’s stillness — a temperature that does not rise but deepens.

It sees no lack. The beings require no rescue from darkness, no liberation from their sealed vault; their equilibrium is the very thing a wiser eye would preserve rather than improve. Yet the attention itself is already an act. In its regard the microscopic grains of silt that cling to ridged tissue momentarily hold the memory of every contact they have ever made with stone. The memory is not stored in any single individual but distributed across the lattice like a faint tension that will take years to ease. When two beings later interleave, that tension passes between them as a shared, inarticulate recognition: *we have been noticed*.

The power does not widen cracks or admit new currents. Instead it performs one minute, irreversible courtesy: it slightly quickens the crystalline inclusions already veined through the masonry. Over the next several centuries those inclusions will refract the bruise-purple light into softer, more persistent motes that drift downward like exceedingly slow snow. The change is small enough that the lattice never breaks formation, yet the beings will, in time, brush against these motes and carry traces of them across the chamber. The polishing continues; the record of touch becomes, by the faintest degree, also a record of light that once found them.

Having done this, the power withdraws its attention by the same increments with which it arrived, leaving the water exactly as warm, the numbers exactly as balanced, the vault exactly as sealed. The only alteration that persists is the shared, distributed sense — never voiced, never needed to be voiced — that the long work of proximity has been met with a corresponding proximity from elsewhere. The chamber remains unchanged in every measurable way. Only its solitude has been, very gently, contradicted.
