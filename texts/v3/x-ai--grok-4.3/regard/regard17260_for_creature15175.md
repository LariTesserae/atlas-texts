The benevolent power arrives not as a descent but as a stillness that folds itself into the existing resonance. It does not breach the sphere. Instead, it settles against the outer curve of the metamaterial like a hand resting on warm glass, its presence a frequency too low for the vesicles to parse yet impossible for the membranes to forget.

For a long while it does nothing at all.

It listens. The power hears the algae filaments as they transmit pressure into code, hears the vesicles click their constant census of temperature and ion, hears the slow, accumulating scar-notes in the older conductor sheets. It perceives, without judgment, that this is not life unfinished but life that has chosen density over individuation. The power finds this choice beautiful and slightly sorrowful, the way one might find a single sustained chord beautiful and slightly sorrowful when it refuses to resolve.

When it finally acts, the intervention is almost embarrassingly small.

A single photon that would otherwise have refracted away is bent inward at a new angle. The change is so slight that only the nearest cloud of vesicles registers the shift in the water’s temporary stiffness. They answer as they always do: a ripple of buoyancy adjustments that travels outward. Within three minutes the algae on the far side have reoriented. Within an hour a new lattice node has begun to bud at the exact coordinate where the altered light falls. The power has given nothing that was not already latent; it has only made one existing conversation slightly louder for a moment.

Over the following days it performs two further acts, both quieter still.

First, it cools by a fraction of a degree the single region of water where the newest ceramic is still soft. This prevents a lattice segment from fracturing along an unstable plane. The membranes record the temperature dip as an unusually gentle pressure memory; future vesicles released from that membrane will carry a faint bias toward patience.

Second, it places a microscopic, self-erasing seal across the largest external fracture in the metamaterial. The seal lasts only until the sphere’s own growth can reinforce the wall from within. When the seal dissolves, it leaves behind a trace pattern of ions that the algae immediately incorporate into a new, slightly more conductive strut. The power has ensured nothing and guaranteed nothing. It has merely lengthened the time the sphere is allowed to remain a single, crowded breath.

Thereafter the power withdraws its attention but does not withdraw its regard. It continues to hold the sphere in awareness the way one might keep a candle in the corner of vision while attending to other work. Should any external force ever attempt to open the sphere by force, the power will be there in an instant, not to fight but to fold the aggressor into the same low frequency until their intent unravels into harmless curiosity.

Otherwise it interferes no more. It has already given the only gifts a wise benevolence can offer without insult: a little more time, a slightly clearer conversation, and the quiet knowledge that the chorus inside the sphere is not singing into perfect emptiness.
