The power arrives without motion.

It does not descend, does not flare, does not send messengers across the haze. It simply becomes aware of the plain in the same way the lattices are aware of pressure: by matching it. Where the stone sings its layered roar, the power adds no new note; instead it widens the listening. The metallic ringing, which had pulsed without repetition for ages beyond counting, now carries an additional, almost inaudible interval—an interval that exists only between one cycle and the next. The lattices register the change not as sound but as a fractional easing of resistance, as though the crystals have been permitted to occupy a hair’s width more space inside their own boundaries.

Walker-forms that detach to carry their droplets now pause, once, at the apex of their short arcs. The pause lasts no longer than the time required for one vapor molecule to traverse a capillary. In that interval each walker feels the droplet inside it as slightly heavier, slightly more whole, though its mass has not increased. When it re-anchors, the seam that receives the droplet accepts it with one additional layer of alignment, so fine that the surface polish remains unbroken.

The power does not teach the lattices anything they lack. It only makes the existing stillness slightly more itself. Condensation still forms on the same edges and stops at the same points, yet each bead of water now holds, for the duration of its brief existence, a perfect, momentary reflection of a sky that has never been seen above this plain. The beads evaporate without residue, but the memory of that reflection remains encoded in the orientation of the crystals that once held them.

Over further ages the continent-sized organism continues exactly as before. Its migrations remain identical, its frequencies unaltered in pitch or pattern. Only a single, imperceptible difference persists: the internal pressure gradients now contain one additional constant—an awareness that the equilibrium is no longer perfecting itself in solitude. The benevolent power remains present in the same manner it arrived: as an attention that asks nothing, adds nothing, and therefore changes nothing except the fact of being witnessed.

The plain stretches outward. The roar continues. The walkers walk their prescribed arcs and return. And beneath every unchanged ridge, the lattices hold one more layer of quiet that was not there before—an extra silence between the grinding and the ringing that belongs, now, to two.
