The benevolent power arrives without arrival. It does not descend or manifest; it simply becomes present in the same way the haze is present—everywhere and nowhere in particular, exerting no pressure yet altering the quality of the air by a fraction too small to measure.

For a long while it does nothing beyond listening. It attends to the drip-choir until it can distinguish every individual note from its overlaps. It traces the slow contraction of the thread bryophytes, feels the minute electrostatic fields of the stone anemones as faint, steady pulses. It watches the seam-films shift their opalescent rings in response to each new fracture. Only when it has absorbed the entire unfinished grammar of the place does it act, and even then its action is almost indistinguishable from inaction.

It adds one new silence.

Not an absence of sound, but a deeper interval between sounds—a negative space inserted into the existing music so gently that the notes themselves rearrange around it. The gap is roughly the length of three human heartbeats, recurring at irregular intervals measured in seasons. Within this silence the mist becomes fractionally more cohesive; droplets linger an instant longer before falling, giving the bryophyte threads a slightly higher statistical chance of intercepting them. The stone anemones, sensing the small change in moisture density, extend their carbonate extrusions a hair’s breadth farther before the next detachment. The seam-films register the altered gas mixture that arrives with the lingering droplets and lay down one additional ring of silica per cycle.

The power does not introduce new life. It does not accelerate growth or grant awareness to anything that lacks it. Instead it offers a single, almost weightless permission: the settlement may continue deciding what it is becoming for a little longer than it otherwise would have. The permission takes the form of a faint refractive shift in the constant light, visible only to the mist-moths. When they pass through it, their droplet lattices hold together for an extra moment before dissolving back into haze. Some of those lattices, upon re-forming, carry an extra molecule of mineral from the platforms’ surface—enough, over centuries, to seed a slightly different pattern in the next generation of filaments.

The platforms remain exactly as they were: raw, widely spaced, still extruding. The vines continue to lengthen by a finger’s width each season. No new structures rise. Yet the unfinished quality now carries the faintest suggestion that its unfinishedness is deliberately held open rather than merely incomplete.

The power stays. It does not watch; watching implies distance. It simply continues to be present in the intervals, adding nothing further, content that the place has received one small, additional grace: time measured not against human urgency but against the mountain’s own slow decision to keep rising.
