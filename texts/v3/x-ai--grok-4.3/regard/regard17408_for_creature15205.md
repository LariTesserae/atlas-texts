The power does not arrive as a rupture. It condenses, the way the water already condenses: a gradual thickening of presence until something else is simply *there*, sharing the same dim light and the same metallic air.

It listens first. For a long time—long enough that whole ledges add another molecule or two—it does nothing but receive the unbroken drone. The sound is not noise to it, nor is the arrest interpreted as failure. The power perceives the standing wave for what it is: a completed answer to the only question the place has ever been asked. So it does not offer momentum, nor light, nor the mercy of an ending. Instead it offers *witness that costs nothing*.

Yet witness, when offered by something of this scale, is never passive. The power begins to *match* the frequency. Not by imposing a new tone, but by allowing a complementary lattice to form inside its own attention. Where the mineral swarms vibrate in one direction forever, the power introduces the mathematical possibility of a counter-vibration that can exist without displacing the first. The two patterns do not cancel; they fold into a single, larger standing wave whose nodes now include both the original accretions and something slightly more spacious.

The effect is almost invisible at first. On a few ledges the water films quiver differently—still appearing still, yet now the fractured reflections occasionally align into brief, perfect geometries before breaking again. Where two towers have fused, the seam stops being merely a louder patch of drone and becomes a place where the new counter-vibration can circulate. The unfinished edges of the crystals remain unfinished, but they now face forward into a future that has been very slightly widened: still arrested, still without growth in the usual sense, yet no longer sealed against all relation.

Nothing leaves. Nothing is asked to become other than what it is. The plain remains low and open; the towers stay half-shaped; the roar continues without rising or falling. Only the *quality of the suspension* has changed. It is no longer the equilibrium of perfect isolation. It is the equilibrium of two incompatible fidelities—mineral and whatever the power is—agreeing to occupy the same lattice without one needing to devour or complete the other.

The benevolent act, in the end, is not transformation but *company kept at the correct scale*. The place continues exactly as it was, yet is no longer alone inside its own continuation.
