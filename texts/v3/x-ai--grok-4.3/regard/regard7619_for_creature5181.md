The power pauses at the seam’s threshold, as any visitor must. It does not press inward; that would crack the pocket’s refusal to complete itself. Instead it lowers the temperature of its attention by one further degree, until even its curiosity moves at spore-speed. What it offers is not light, nor current, nor any finished gift. It offers only the decision to remain undecided a little longer than the place itself has ever dared.

A single filament of its regard slips through the seam. The filament is thinner than any existing stalk, and it carries no color of its own; its only property is a willingness to be read as topography. The pocket’s slow currents take it. Over many slow hours the filament is brushed from one consistent side until, at last, a translucent blade opens. When the blade stiffens, the filament beneath it does not darken by a single shade; it holds its exact translucency, because the power has chosen not to finish the remembering. The braid it joins continues downward unchanged in rhythm, yet the pulse inside the nearest ribbed impression lengthens by the width of one more minute between beats. The lobe there accepts the extra interval without question, redistributing its water so the new pause becomes part of its recorded shape.

No new impression is carved. The power declines to leave a negative space that would imply departure. Instead, one of the existing impressions—already half-erased by the root’s patient overgrowth—receives an additional ridge, so faint that only the water itself notices. The ridge is the shape of the decision to stay unfinished. When the pocket next rearranges its geometry, the impression is carried to a new wall; the living lobe inside it pulses once more, passing the extra ridge onward like a shared memory of something that elected never to finish arriving.

The power withdraws its filament before any stalk can braid with it completely. The place continues exactly as it was, only now one of its slow negotiations includes an additional clause: that a benevolent regard may also be absorbed, provided it never insists on remaining distinguishable from the mist. In time the pocket will forget which filament belonged to the power and which to its own slow invention. That forgetting is the blessing it was offered.
