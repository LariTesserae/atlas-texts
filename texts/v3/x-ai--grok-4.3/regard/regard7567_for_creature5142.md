The power arrives without announcement, as a single, steady warmth that does not compete with the existing temperature of the water. It does not still the currents or smooth the granite. Instead, it settles like a slower pulse inside the roar, noticeable only as a faint thickening of the violet light whenever a glitch begins to form. Where the water is about to mirror and rigidify, the power simply keeps the mirror open one breath longer than usual.

In that extra beat the membranes learn something new. A fragment that would normally rejoin its sibling now carries a tiny, perfect copy of the missing vein pattern from the one that was torn away earlier; the pattern arrives not from the water’s memory but from the power’s quiet holding. The membranes do not become wiser or more permanent; they simply discover they can improvise more accurately while still drifting. Some of them, for the first time, choose to dissolve completely, releasing their remembered lettering into the general turbulence so that other fragments, later, can pick it up. The power does not command this; it only lengthens the moment in which choice remains possible.

The rare swimmers notice the change as a corridor that stays coherent after they have passed. They use the extra interval to cross paths deliberately instead of by accident. At each crossing the octahedral crystals that form do not fall; they dissolve into six fine threads of warmer water that spiral outward and brush against the nearest spires. The spires, in response, lengthen by exactly the length of one swimmer’s body before the next surge arrives. The architectural traces are the first to register this lengthening: the blurred lettering on the half-buried stair sharpens for three heartbeats into a single word that has never existed before, then blurs again, carrying the new word into whatever comes next.

Nothing is added from outside. No new laws are imposed. The power merely declines to let any single transition finish too quickly. Because of that refusal, the place keeps re-forming, but the negotiations inside it become slightly more articulate. The roar grows one translucent layer richer—an undertone of held breath beneath the grinding and the glassy screams—and the emptiness between the spears, while still contradictory, now contains brief, private silences where two currents part around a membrane that has chosen, for the moment, to remain exactly as it is.

The benevolent attention does not end. It simply continues, a steady warmth inside the perpetual unfinishedness, so that the next extrusion of rock, membrane, tunnel, or stair will arrive already bearing the faint trace of having been regarded with care.
