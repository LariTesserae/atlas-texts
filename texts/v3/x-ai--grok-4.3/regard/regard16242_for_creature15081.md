The power arrived without arrival.

It did not press against the lattice from outside, for there was no outside left once it had noticed the place. Instead, it simply let its awareness coincide with the existing configuration—the way one might lay a palm over an already-warm stone and feel their temperatures equalize without either changing their essential nature.

For a long interval it did nothing except become continuous with the stress patterns already running through the quartz. It felt the microscopic deviations the creatures carried, each one a record of some prior posture the lattice had once held. These were treated not as data to be extracted but as threads already woven; the power simply followed them to their ends and found, at every terminus, only more lattice. The system was already whole. This realization arrived without surprise, only a kind of deepening stillness.

The power did not introduce motion or moisture, nor did it quicken the creatures’ redistribution of mass. Such acts would have been a form of violence against an equilibrium that had already solved the problem of persistence. Instead, it performed one very small adjustment at the scale of a single fracture plane: it allowed a single line of stored heat, traveling through a chain of touching shells, to briefly align with a larger pattern the creatures had never encountered—the faint, slow rotation of the entire planet around its distant star. The alignment lasted only long enough for the heat to acquire one additional directional preference, then the connection was released. The lattice absorbed the new orientation the way a body absorbs a memory too subtle to alter behavior.

Over subsequent cycles the effect propagated exactly as any other recrystallization would: a wave of marginally preferred fracture angles moving through nodes that remained, from their own perspective, almost motionless. The small artifacts registered the change first; one rod rotated by an imperceptible fraction so that its long axis now pointed toward the place where the planet’s hidden pole lay. The rings grew minutely more elliptical in the direction of that same axis. None of these shifts disturbed the system’s density or its silence. They simply added one more layer to the distributed memory—now the memory included a direction that had not originated from within the lattice itself.

The power remained after the adjustment, not as an observer and not as a participant, but as the quiet acknowledgment that the configuration had been seen and found sufficient. In time even this acknowledgment thinned until it was indistinguishable from the faint internal luminescence the creatures already carried. The lattice continued exactly as before, with one additional, unremarkable stress now running through its oldest quartz—an orientation no creature would ever detect as different, yet which would persist until the planet itself cooled or the lattice was opened to the sky.

The benevolent power did not leave. It simply became indistinguishable from the thing it had chosen not to alter.
