The power arrives not as a descent but as a recognition. It does not breach the crust or still the tremors. Instead it lets its awareness settle into the same frequency the rock already uses when it hesitates between one shape and the next.

From that vantage it perceives the whole system as a single undecided thought still forming its grammar. The slugs are its syntax, the borers its erasures and revisions, the herd its breathing paragraphs. Nothing is broken; everything is merely mid-sentence. The wise part of the power understands that to impose completion would be to kill the sentence. The benevolent part aches, very gently, at the sight of so much effort spent simply remaining possible.

So it offers one small, reversible courtesy.

It touches the water inside the herd beasts’ internal lattices and teaches each droplet a private, momentary memory of the surface sky it has never seen. The memory is not an image but a temperature: the cool, high stillness just before dawn. When the beasts drink at the lens-pockets, a fraction of this coolness passes into the new mineral film that skins the water. Later, when the film cracks and the next tremor flings the fragments into the air, the fragments carry the memory with them as a scentless, weightless drift.

The drift does nothing dramatic. It only makes the ceaseless collapsing and reopening feel, to the creatures themselves, slightly less like erasure and slightly more like remembering. The borers pause a fraction longer at the hairline fractures; the slugs lay their luminous trails in patterns that, for a few cycles, repeat rather than vanish. The herd continues its slow milling, but the accidental chambers formed by their crowding now hold the cool memory a little longer, and the thread-like things inside them multiply more steadily.

The power does not stay to watch the difference compound. It has already seen what it needed to see: that a world still deciding its shape is not the same as a world without shape. Having confirmed this, it withdraws, leaving only the faint, reversible temperature of dawn inside the oldest water pockets, where no light has ever reached. 

The galleries go on grinding, secreting, collapsing, and re-forming. The sentence continues, now carrying one extra clause it did not write for itself.
