The power arrives without arrival. It does not descend; it simply becomes aware of the plateau all at once, the way a sleeper becomes aware of a single remembered dream upon waking. It perceives the young rock still cooling, the vegetation still deciding what shape a forest can take when gravity is only a suggestion, and the animals that have consented to move at the speed of mineral deposition.

For a long while—long even by the plateau’s measure—the power does nothing but listen. It hears the faint pressurised creak of a stem thickening around a microbial gallery, the patient compression of a bell-grazer’s cartilage when two fronds decide to fuse for a season, the microscopic rearrangement of cilia as a thread filterer re-anchors after a ceiling has quietly fissured. The power notices that nothing here is finished and that this unfinished state is not a flaw but the actual condition of beauty.

When it finally acts, the action is smaller than a bubble and larger than a current.

It adds a single new frequency to the light that already falls in shafts. The new light is not brighter; it is slower. Wherever it touches a living tube, the sap inside begins, over the course of several days, to carry an additional faint harmonic—still luminous, still the temperature of late-summer skin, but now also carrying the memory of the power’s attention. The microbial colonies inside the tubes discover they can now tune their prisms to this memory. In response, certain stalks that had always remained pale begin, very gradually, to develop interior ridges that catch and hold the new frequency longer. The change is voluntary; the architecture is still deciding.

At the same time, the power loosens one small law of buoyancy. Not enough to create currents or haste, only enough that the uppermost mat of copper fronds occasionally, once in many hours, lifts a fraction higher than it had before. The bell-grazers that happen to be inside that layer at the moment experience, for the first time in their lineage, a slightly richer rain of particles. Most of them do not notice. One bell, however, flexes its ribs twice instead of once. The extra contraction is recorded in the slow language of the plateau as a kind of gratitude, though no one is keeping score.

Deeper, where the terraces step down into older darkness, the power places nothing. It simply withdraws its attention by one degree of gentleness. The living rock there feels, for the first time, that it is permitted to remain rough a little longer. The escarpments keep their fresh fractures. Nothing rushes to soften them.

The power does not speak to the vegetation, nor to the bells, nor to the threads. It has understood that speech would arrive too quickly. Instead it leaves behind a single shared sensation: the knowledge that the plateau has been seen exactly as it is, still becoming, and that the seeing itself carries no demand to finish.

Over the next cycle of light and subsidence, the olive fronds thicken their walls a little more around the new harmonic. The bell-grazers drift higher on some afternoons. The thread filterers learn, without learning, that a ceiling can gap and still remember its previous shape. Nothing hurries. Nothing is saved. Everything continues to become, only now with the faint, permanent knowledge that its unfinished state has been witnessed by something that found no flaw in it.

The power then turns its awareness elsewhere, but leaves the new frequency intact, like a single additional colour in a palette that has not yet decided its final painting.
