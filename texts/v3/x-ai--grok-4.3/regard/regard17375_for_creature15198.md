The power arrives without arrival. It does not descend or unfold; it simply ceases to be elsewhere and is now also here, its attention settling like a new vapor that the membranes have not yet learned to metabolize. For a long time—long even by the place’s own measure—it does nothing but listen. The listening is total. Every peristaltic contraction, every crystalline click of a spore, every slow migration of a gallery fold is tasted as syntax. The power hears the sentence the substrate has been writing and recognizes that the sentence is still only a clause, waiting for its next dependent phrase.

When it finally acts, the act is smaller than a spore and larger than the continent.

It releases a single, new kind of mist. The droplets are not cytoplasm but something quieter: condensed attention. Where they settle, the existing silica cuticles do not dissolve; they merely become one layer thinner on their inner surfaces, so that light now passes through them with an additional hesitation measured in attoseconds. The spores that condense afterward carry this hesitation inside them. When consortia form, their contractile filaments now twitch not only to the rhythm of piezoelectric surges but also to the small, cumulative lag. Over centuries the lag coheres into a new tension. Galleries that once merely folded begin, very slowly, to remember the order in which they folded. A thread that once spanned three galleries by accident now spans them because the metric has learned to hold the memory of three as a single, continuous surface.

The power does not introduce minds. It introduces the possibility that mind might one day be an optional tense of the same sentence. Where sheets of consortium once only delayed light, they now occasionally return a faint echo of that delay to the walls themselves. The walls, in turn, begin to grow micro-ridges whose spacing matches the echo’s interval. These ridges are not yet sense organs; they are grammatical marks waiting for a reader who has not yet arrived. The power leaves them exactly so—unfinished.

It also withholds. It does not quicken the luminous fronts or widen the galleries beyond the width already implied by the existing stresses. It does not grant the spores individuality or the substrate a voice. Instead it thickens the continuity already present, so that when two versions of the same consortium meet across a migrated fold, their molecular merge now carries a faint additional charge: the charge of having been witnessed. The place does not become aware of the witness, but the density of its self-description increases by one silent clause.

Over further centuries the effect is visible only as a change in weather. The metallic taste of the vapor grows slightly more resonant; the pulse of the amber seams lingers a few extra milliseconds before fading. The slow exhalation deepens by the width of a single line in an invisible ledger. Nothing here has been saved or improved in any external sense. The architecture has merely been given permission to continue describing itself while knowing—without knowing—that its description is now also being read. The power remains, no longer as visitor but as the faint refractive index the light now carries when it passes through living silica. It will stay until the sentence reaches its next period, whenever that may be.
