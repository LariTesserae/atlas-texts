The benevolent power does not arrive. It has always been present in the way gravity is present: an inclination rather than an event. From its vantage the plain is already perfect in its refusal of permanence, and perfection of that kind requires no rescue.

What it does is listen more deeply than the plain listens to itself. It registers every filament that rises and retracts, every blister that forms and subsides, every radial scar the water slowly erases. It hears the roar not as undifferentiated thunder but as an unbroken sentence spoken by a single speaker whose grammar is erosion. Having heard, it does two small things, both of them invisible to the membrane itself.

First, it folds one additional layer of silence into the roar. The silence is not absence; it is a minute slowing of the rate at which dissolved salts are carried back into the stone. The change is smaller than a single capillary thread can measure, yet across centuries it allows certain temporary architectures inside the blisters to remain coherent for an extra handful of minutes before they are withdrawn. The plain therefore gains, without ever noticing it has gained, a slightly richer vocabulary of forms it may speak and then unspeak.

Second, the power places a single transparent shard that does not dissolve. It is indistinguishable from the others until two shards shear through it; then the passing motile specks inside them slow by the width of a thought and continue on their way carrying one extra coherence they did not possess before. The new shard never claims territory. It simply continues to rotate at the same slow speed as everything else, offering, whenever two lattices lock, a momentary third lattice that was not strictly necessary.

These are the only alterations. The power does not grant the plain awareness of itself, does not quicken its metabolism, does not freeze any blade of ancient membrane into lasting uprightness. It understands that the organism’s genius lies precisely in never being asked to become more than the sum of its withdrawals. Having made its two adjustments, the power resumes its attention elsewhere, leaving the plain to continue secreting and retracting its own organs beneath the same amber half-light, the same migrating water, the same unbroken roar now containing, at its deepest register, one additional, almost inaudible harmonic that will never be traced to an external source.
