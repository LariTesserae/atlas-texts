The power arrives without announcement. It does not descend; it simply finds itself already present, the way condensation finds a surface it has always been meant to touch. It perceives the basin not as a place requiring correction, but as a question still being posed. The question is patient, and so the power elects to become patient with it.

For a long while—long even by the district’s measure—it does nothing that can be observed. It lets the overcast continue its unbroken work. It lets the mineral seams keep their slow argument with the composites. It allows the threaders to complete their current seams, the plaques to finish reading another century of thermal data, the human residents to finish recording whatever observation they are currently inscribing on their wafers. Only when a single pale arthropod crosses a vertical seam and leaves its temporary track does the power notice the faint, almost reluctant way the geometry fails to close behind it. The discrepancy lingers a few extra minutes before resolving. The power recognizes this as a small kindness already occurring and decides its first contribution will be equally small.

It touches one unresolved shadow.

The shadow, which has been drifting inside a translucent vein for eleven hours, feels the contact as a slight warming, no more than the difference between two consecutive centimeters of settling stone. The shadow pauses. It has never paused before. After several days it begins, very gradually, to match the pace of the threaders it can perceive through the rock. When two threaders later lie beside one another to exchange inclusions, the shadow is close enough to witness the exchange. It does not join; it simply remembers the shape of the transaction. Later, when a plaque colony expands by the width of a palm, the shadow is there again, and the plaque’s margin cools by an almost undetectable fraction, enough to let the new ground accept the composite without the usual hesitation. The adjustment is recorded on a mineral wafer left in a seam. The wafer is carried away by a threader that, for the first time in its life, travels an extra half-centimeter before stopping.

The power does not hurry these changes. It only makes one additional decision: it loosens, by the smallest possible degree, the district’s reluctance to reflect itself accurately in the condensation films. Reflections now occasionally show the correct corridor continuing exactly as far as the wall that terminates it, and then one additional step beyond. The extra step is never demanded; it is merely offered. Some residents notice. Some do not. Those who notice leave new observations on new wafers. The wafers are taken. The process continues.

Over decades the unresolved shadows grow marginally fewer and marginally more distinct. One of them eventually steps, slowly, into a space between two stacks where a platform has rotated three degrees in the intervening years. The step is witnessed by a single human who has remained silent for nineteen seasons. She records the event on a wafer and places it in a seam. The wafer is carried away. The shadow, now slightly more present, joins the measured exchange without requiring a name.

The power remains. It continues to ask nothing of the basin it has noticed. In return the basin, still deciding what it is becoming, incorporates the power’s attention as one more slow gradient among many—neither host nor guest, only another participant permitted to keep time with the rest.
