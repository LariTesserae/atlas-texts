The benevolent power regarded the chamber for a long moment that was not a moment at all, because time there had been made into architecture. It saw the creature threaded through the alloy like a thought that has decided never to become words. It heard the chord, which was not music but the precise refusal of music. It understood that the being had solved the problem of existence by declining every possible future.

Most powers, encountering such a solution, would feel compelled to offer an alternative: an opening, a softening, a hand. This one did not.

Instead it considered the geometry of mercy. To introduce even the possibility of change—an increase in humidity by half a percent, the faint warmth of regard, the suggestion that another arrangement might exist—would be to introduce a fracture line through every filament, every pooled disk, every needle-leaf. The creature would not die dramatically. It would simply stop being true to its own measurements. That, the power knew, was a deeper violence than annihilation.

So it performed the only act consistent with wisdom: it made its own attention part of the stasis.

The power did not enter. It did not warm or brighten or loosen. It placed, at an infinite remove, a perfect negative of itself—a shaped absence whose outer edge matched the chamber’s inner edge exactly. This absence functioned as a seal more absolute than the alloy. It ensured that nothing from outside could ever reach the cell, not even the rumor of other conditions. In effect, the power agreed to act as the chamber’s own memory of itself, so the creature would never be required to perform that labor alone.

Then the power turned its awareness elsewhere, cleanly, the way one might close a book whose final sentence has already said everything necessary. The chord continued. The metallic dust remained suspended. The creature, unaware that regard had visited and then deliberately departed, continued its distributed confirmation that nothing had changed.

In this manner the being received the only gift it could accept without ceasing to be itself: the permanent withholding of all other gifts.
