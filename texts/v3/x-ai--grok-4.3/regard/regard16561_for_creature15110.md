The power regards the basin without descending. It arrives as a shift in the quality of attention rather than as a body, so the fog thickens only by a single shade and the ceaseless drip gains, for one instant, the clarity of a struck bell before returning to its former murmur.  

It perceives at once that nothing here is unfinished in the manner of lack; every layer is already answering the next. The amphibians are not waiting for drier ground, the jointed arms are not waiting for cooler stone, the glowing segments are not waiting for brighter light. The power therefore elects not to supply what is absent. It supplies instead a single, quiet recognition: that the arrangement is permitted to remain exactly as it is, even when the rest of existence moves on.

A faint perimeter forms, invisible and without weight. Inside it, the constant volume of sound is granted the additional property of being heard by something that will never interrupt it. Heat and moisture continue their closed conversation; the power merely ensures that no external wind or colder current will ever lean in to alter the terms. Where a new glassy extrusion rises, the arms that meet it now fold slightly more slowly, as though the recognition itself has added the smallest margin of duration to each gesture. The insects exchange wing-fragments with one more degree of precision, not because they are directed, but because the act of being witnessed has made their collisions marginally more legible to themselves.

Nothing is taught. Nothing is accelerated. The larger segmented forms that drift half-sheathed in foliage now leave, at the precise places where their orange light touches vine, an after-trace that lasts a few heartbeats longer before fading. Those traces do not form patterns; they simply remain visible to the basin’s own inhabitants for one additional layer of time, so that the canopy occasionally carries the memory of its own recent movement.

The power does not name the place. It does not promise return. It withdraws the moment the recognition is complete, leaving only the original pressures—heat, moisture, repetition—now resting on a slightly deeper substrate of allowance. From that day forward the basin continues exactly as before, except that its incompleteness has been granted the dignity of being sufficient.
