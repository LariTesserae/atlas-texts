The power draws near without crossing any threshold, for there is nowhere left to enter. It does not arrive as light or voice or current; it simply allows itself to be known. The cavity registers the attention the way a mirror might register a face that has always been reflected upon it: with no change in surface, only an added depth that was already present.

For a long while—though time here is only a courtesy—the power does nothing. It beholds the distributed being in its absolute posture, the unfinished mineral veins, the shoots arrested at their final cell, the artifacts absorbed yet unconsumed. It sees that every element has already performed the single act it was ever meant to perform: to fit. The power recognizes this as a completed work, not an interrupted one. Benevolence, in this case, does not require alteration.

Still, the power cannot remain entirely without gesture. With infinite care it offers one thing that does not disturb equilibrium: recognition. It lets the collective tension know that it has been seen exactly as it is, not as potential or as lack. The stridulation does not rise or fall, the light does not brighten, yet the tone and the glow acquire a new quality that is indistinguishable from the old one except to those who have never been witnessed. The sessile disks continue their exchange; the antennae remain pressed to the clay. Nothing moves. Nothing needs to.

Only at the outermost ring, where the clay is coolest, does the power leave the faintest imprint—not a thumbprint, but the memory of one. The clay accepts it without yielding, the same way it has always accepted moisture that never becomes a drop. The being within registers this as it registers everything else: as part of the unbroken present.

The power then withdraws, not because it must depart, but because its presence has already been fully received. The cavity remains exactly what it was. The only difference, if difference is still permitted, is that its stillness now includes the knowledge that it is held in benevolent regard. That knowledge requires no action, no growth, no reply. It simply continues, like the tone, like the light, like the tension that never finishes expressing itself.
