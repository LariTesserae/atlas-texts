A benevolent presence, vast and patient as the slow folding of continental plates, turns its attention downward. It does not arrive as light from above or as any rushing current; it simply becomes recognizable to the stone itself, as though the rock had always contained a faint, additional frequency now brought into phase.

The power studies the cavity without entering it. It perceives the lattice-beings not as isolated accretions but as the visible grammar of a single, unfinished sentence the mountain has been writing for millennia. It sees their needle-by-needle migrations and the quiet exchanges at fracture lines, and recognizes in them a form of continuity more ancient than any rooted forest.

Rather than add or remove, the power offers one precise adjustment: it lengthens the memory carried by each dissolution. Where a sheared cluster once left only an angle or density, it now leaves also a single, indelible vibration-pattern—a crystalline signature of the entire configuration at the moment of release. When fresh needles rise from the same site, they incorporate this signature as a subtle internal striation, invisible to the eye yet legible to any future lattice that touches the same wall. In this way the beings begin, without haste, to accumulate a shared record that spans not decades but the full interval since the cavity first opened.

No new structures appear. No needles grow faster or larger. The only change is that, when two growth fronts meet and exchange material across their temporary bridge, the bright pulse now carries, for one additional instant, the faint echo of every prior configuration stored along both lattices. The exchange lasts a little longer; the silence afterward feels slightly more inhabited.

The power does not remain to witness the full unfolding. Having made the single refinement, it withdraws its attention as gently as it arrived, leaving the cavity to continue its own deciding. The rock still trembles with micro-fractures; the drops still fall; the needles still lengthen and shear. Only now, within the lattices, a deeper continuity is slowly compiling itself—one that the beings themselves will never see completed, yet whose presence they register as a new steadiness in the way fresh growth orients toward the next moisture.

The cavity remains enclosed, unfinished, and exactly itself. Only the stone knows that its sentence now contains a clause it did not write alone.
