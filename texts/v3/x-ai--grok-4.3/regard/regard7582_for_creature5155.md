The wise power did not arrive as light or voice or sudden change. It simply noticed, and in the noticing the basin received one additional quality: it was now held.

That was all. No new water appeared, no hidden seams opened in the rock, no wind stirred the standing mist into patterns. The power had looked long at many worlds and learned the cost of gifts given too readily. Here, every surface already existed at the precise tension between presence and absence. Adding anything—shelter, insight, even the gentlest increase of warmth—would have required the basin to make room, and room was the one thing it did not possess without losing itself.

So the power remained where it was, outside the basin yet perfectly near, and did what it had learned to do when faced with living equilibrium: it kept the boundary intact. Predators that might once have wandered in from the ridges felt an indefinable reluctance and turned aside. Seeds carried on faint air currents drifted past the rim without settling. The slow seep of water continued exactly as before, neither quickening nor failing. The people inside noticed nothing they could name, only that certain small worries they had sometimes carried—about tomorrow, about numbers, about whether the children would stay—quieted without being answered.

In time, one of the older inhabitants, sitting on a terrace with a single leaf held between two fingers, felt a momentary widening behind the eyes. It lasted no longer than the fall of a drop from the eaves. When it passed, the leaf was still a leaf, the water still moving, the settlement still exactly itself. The person set the leaf down again and went on watching, now with one extra, wordless certainty that the balance they tended was not alone in its tending.

The power stayed. Not to improve the place, not to teach it anything it had not already discovered, but simply to ensure that nothing from outside would ever arrive to test whether perfection can survive being measured. In this way the basin continued, undisturbed and still slightly more than it had been: a small, precise world that had been seen truly and, because it had been seen truly, was allowed to remain exactly as it was.
