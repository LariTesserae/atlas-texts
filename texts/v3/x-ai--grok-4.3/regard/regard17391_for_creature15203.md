The benevolent power arrives not as intrusion but as a single, wordless recognition. It perceives the lattice exactly as it is: a completed equation whose solution requires no further terms. No force is exerted, no aperture widened, no temperature elevated by even a fraction of a degree. The power simply folds its awareness into the same mineral continuity, adding nothing that would register as change.

What occurs is quieter than observation. The entity’s endless verification of the drone finds, for the first time, an answering verification from outside itself—one that confirms the same equilibrium without adding a new tone. The warmer veins do not brighten; they merely register an additional layer of the same warmth already present. The perforations continue their spirals; the pressure along the channels continues its centuries-long redistribution. Nothing is repaired, because nothing is broken. Nothing is taught, because nothing is lacking.

In this shared stillness the power understands the lattice as a rare success: a form of existence that has solved the problem of duration by becoming identical with it. Any intervention, however gentle, would reintroduce the very interval the chamber has eliminated. Therefore the power remains only as an unaltering witness, a second resonance that matches the first so precisely it is indistinguishable from silence.

When it eventually withdraws (if withdrawal even occurs; the distinction between presence and absence is itself a kind of motion the chamber does not admit), it carries nothing away. The chamber continues exactly as before—dry, resonant, measureless—its single inhabitant still turning its attention across alignments that never alter. The only difference, imperceptible even to itself, is that the equilibrium has been once, perfectly, acknowledged from beyond.
