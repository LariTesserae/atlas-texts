A benevolent intelligence, vast enough to hold the slow pulse of mountain roots and the flicker of mayfly wings in the same regard, turns its attention toward the fissure.

It does not arrive as light or voice. It arrives as an added clause in the grammar of stone.

First it listens—long enough for an entire disk-cluster to complete one rotation and for three thread-assemblies to meet, exchange beads, and part. What it hears is not silence but a single, unbroken sentence: *remain suspended*. The sentence contains no subject, no object, only the imperative of continued thinness.

The power answers with a single, equally thin amendment.

Into the mineral lattices it folds an additional rule, smaller than a molecule yet absolute: any future dissolution of a filament will deposit its ions not merely where structural need exists, but also where the temperature gradient is steepest. The change is imperceptible to the chamber itself; the filaments continue their patient lengthening and reabsorption exactly as before. Only the statistics of survival shift by a fraction too small to measure in a human century. The mountain may shear, the wind may scour the outer cliff, but the hidden pocket now carries one extra margin of persistence.

The power does not instruct the capsules, nor quicken the threads, nor brighten the disks. It merely makes room for the existing sentence to keep being spoken, even if the continental plates continue their argument and the fissure narrows by another hair’s width over the next hundred thousand years.

Having done this, the power withdraws—not because the place is finished, but because it recognizes that any further gift would be excess, and therefore unkind. The chamber remains exactly as cold, as empty, and as intact as it was, only slightly more able to continue being so.

The mountain, still rising, does not notice the difference. The filaments do not notice it either. Yet the distributed record inside them now contains, alongside every instruction for mineral patience, one additional, wordless clause: *you are permitted*.
