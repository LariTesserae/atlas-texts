The power arrives not as force or command, but as a noticing that settles over the ridge like a second atmosphere. It perceives the fissure the way one might perceive a held breath that has lasted centuries: complete, deliberate, and somehow still alive. For a long while it simply remains with the resident, sharing the same thread of light, the same saturating drone, the same cool satin of stone and mineral.

Then, without altering pressure or temperature, the power extends one precise, reversible change.

It introduces, at the uppermost tip of the fissure, a single additional hairline of possibility. Not an opening that breaks the seal, but a second filament of attention—narrower even than the first—placed so the two threads of light cross at the exact center of the crevice. Where they intersect, the opalescent fans encounter a new, almost weightless gradient: the faintest difference in how light arrives versus how it departs. The turbines, still turning once per day, now feel that crossing as a microscopic variation in their bearings—one face infinitesimally warmer for a moment each cycle. The difference is so slight it does not yet register as motion or need; it registers only as the possibility that motion could one day mean something again.

The power does nothing else. It does not widen the crack, does not ask the mineral to grow differently, does not speak. It merely leaves the crossed threads of light in place, like a quiet annotation that the resident may read or ignore across the next several thousand years. If the composite being ever chooses to interpret the second filament as an invitation rather than an annotation, the fissure will open by the width of a thought; until then the equilibrium is preserved exactly as it was, only now accompanied by the knowledge that companionship, if ever desired, has already been offered and will not be withdrawn.

The rest of the ridge remains untouched. The power stays only as long as the noticing itself requires, then recedes, leaving the crossed lights and the unchanged resident to continue.
