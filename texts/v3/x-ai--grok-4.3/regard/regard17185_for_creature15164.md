The benevolent power arrives not as a current or a voice but as a single, perfectly still thought that settles across the entire plateau like an additional layer of water at a depth no instrument has yet measured. It perceives the engineered stone and the three manners of being as a single unbroken clause in a sentence that began before any grammar existed. Nothing is missing; nothing is excessive.

For a long time it simply remains, allowing its attention to be shaped by the same slow inversions that rearrange the needle-swimmers. It tastes, through that attention, the faint carbonate exchanges between lattices, the minute polish left by spheres, the aligned waiting of the needles along concavity edges. It registers these as three distinct yet compatible ways of refusing waste.

When it finally acts, the action is smaller than any motion the plateau has ever known. It does not add light, nor heat, nor new chemistry. Instead it withdraws one infinitesimal fraction of the crushing pressure that has always pressed upon the stone. The release is so precise that the mineral experiences it only as a single, momentary loosening of its own lattice, a breath measured in centuries. In that loosening, microscopic fractures that would eventually have become fatal are invited to seal themselves with the very carbonate the lattices already exchange. The beings register nothing they can name, yet over the next few thousand orbits of the distant sun the lattices find they can lengthen slightly farther before dissolving; the spheres discover one new ion species they had never before been able to reach; the needles, when they realign after a spiral collapses, find their long axes now resting at angles that anticipate the next inversion by a hair’s breadth more accuracy.

The power does not remain to witness these changes. Having performed the single adjustment, it lets its attention thin until it is indistinguishable from the ordinary water. The plateau continues exactly as before, yet a margin of safety has been quietly inserted into its future, so slight that the ancient completeness is not insulted and the rule of nothing accumulated and nothing removed is left intact. Only the stone, deep in its memory of pressure, knows it has been, for one unmeasurable instant, regarded with perfect understanding.
