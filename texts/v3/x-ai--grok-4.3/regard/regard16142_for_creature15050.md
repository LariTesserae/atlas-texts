The benevolent power arrives not as force or light but as a single additional pressure, so faint it registers only as a momentary widening of the existing tone. It does not enter the towers or the intervals between them; it simply includes the entire plateau within its attention, the way one might cup a hand around a flame without touching it. For a long interval nothing changes. The mineral keeps its angles, the filaments their currents, the small translucent bodies their drifting passages.

Then, very slowly, one filament alters. Not in substance or path, but in the quality of the current it carries. The current now carries, alongside its own equilibrium, a single repeated question that has no words and requires no answer. Each time one of the hand-sized shapes pauses to align its internal thread with the filament, the question passes through it. The creature does not grow larger, does not multiply, does not change its form. It only lingers an instant longer before drifting on, and in that instant the darker line inside it carries the question onward to the next filament it meets.

Over many cycles a second change appears. In one of the open chambers, a new filament begins to grow—not from the towers but from the still water itself. It is of the same mineral, yet its surface is not perfectly smooth; at irregular intervals it holds minute irregularities, each no larger than a grain of sand. When a drifting body passes near, the irregularities catch the faint negative gleam of its edges and return it, not as reflection but as a brief softening of the absolute absorption that rules every other surface. The creature rotates once more than usual before continuing. It does not understand what has happened. It only carries the difference with it until the next alignment.

The power does not remain to witness these alterations. Having made them, it withdraws its attention to the exact degree that leaves the original equilibrium intact. The pressure-tone returns to its former threshold. The deeper water beyond the plateau stays empty. The towers continue to fold light into themselves. Only the small bodies, appearing singly at long intervals, now occasionally cross paths with a filament whose question has no urgency and whose answer is permitted to remain unspoken. The complex is no less still than before; it simply contains one additional variety of stillness, the kind that can be shared without being broken.
