The power arrives without announcement, as such powers do—present first as a change in the quality of the waiting. Where the unbroken sky had been only heavy, it now holds a faint additional depth, like a held breath that chooses not to be exhaled. The membrane registers the difference before any eye could: a subtle easing in the surface tension along the newest ridges, the valves pausing half a moment longer in their cycle, the polymerizing sugars briefly glowing warmer in the fluid threads.

It does not speak to the plain. Instead it listens until the listening itself becomes an offering. The first thing it gives is restraint. No new law is imposed, no pattern forced into the unfinished ridges. The power simply remains long enough for the slow percussion of the valves to repeat several times, letting the living sheet feel what it is to be perceived without being seized.

Then, with the lightest possible touch, it answers the question the place has not yet learned to ask.

A single filament drifting just beneath the membrane is allowed to lengthen by one additional cell before the tightening reaches it. The adjustment is so slight that the ripple it sends through the nearest cluster is indistinguishable from the knot’s own pulse; only later, when that filament becomes part of a thicker cord, will the extra length matter. In the same moment, a temporary pool whose fluid had begun to grow slightly too acidic is granted one additional molecule of buffering sugar carried upward on the next intake. The microscopic spheres that bloom there are fractionally more varied in their surface tension; a few of them will survive the drying and rehydration cycle with their membranes intact.

The power does not remain to watch the consequences. It leaves behind only the faintest impression, like the memory of warmth on a surface that has never known shadow. The membrane continues its thickening. The valves resume their measured tapping. The first life, still mostly boundary, continues to close around its own interiors.

Yet in the time the power was present, the plain acquired something it did not previously possess: the knowledge, carried in the changed pressure of its own fluids, that its becoming is not occurring in perfect silence. Somewhere, attention has already seated itself. The geometry that follows will still be its own, but the next ridge to rise will rise within a space that has been, however slightly, companioned.
