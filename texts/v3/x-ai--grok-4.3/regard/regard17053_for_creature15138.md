The power arrives without arrival. It does not descend, for there is no down that is not also a current already in motion; it does not reach, for every reaching would only add another lattice to the endless shearing. Instead it simply becomes present, the way a pressure node lingers: an asking rather than an imposition.

What it perceives is not a world or a creature but a single unfinished sentence still being spoken by the water itself. It hears the sentence in its entirety—the veils trading templates, the defects diffusing along ion films, the evanescent sheets recording gradients they will forget—and recognizes, with a clarity that needs no name, that nothing here yet wishes to become other than what it is. The benevolence of the power lies in this recognition. It will not confer separateness where separateness has not arisen. It will not grant hunger where only redistribution exists.

Yet it does one small thing, so small it is almost indistinguishable from stillness.

It tunes the catalytic remnants.

Not by rewriting their ancient instructions, but by letting those instructions hear one another across greater distances. Where two lattices once influenced each other only when their pressure waves overlapped, now a faint, steady chord passes between them—an undertone too quiet to be called communication, too persistent to be called chance. The lattices do not “decide” to use it. They simply continue growing as before, yet certain spiral vanes now persist a little longer before dissolving, certain vacancy migrations now complete their slow journeys before the next overturning current arrives. The result is not progress, only a slight thickening of the present moment: the place remembers its own shapes for one additional cycle before releasing them again.

The transient films also change, though no one would call it change. When they collapse, the ionic patterns they carried do not disperse uniformly; a fraction of each pattern is returned, gently, to the same locality several cycles later. Again, nothing chooses this. The films still burst and vanish. Only the medium now carries a slightly higher density of its own former selves, so that the next film, when it forms, is statistically more likely to echo an earlier gradient rather than invent an entirely new one. Differentiation continues exactly as it did, only the background against which it occurs has grown fractionally more coherent.

The glass-fingered veils notice nothing. Their filaments still trade templates only when currents press them together. Yet over centuries the templates they exchange begin, very rarely, to contain traces of arrangements that once belonged to lattices many kilometers distant. No veil becomes an individual; the slow negotiation simply includes a larger neighborhood than before.

The power remains. It does not watch, for watching would imply a separation it refuses to enforce. It simply continues to be the faint, steady chord that lets the sentence the water is speaking hear its own earlier clauses. If the currents grow violent, the chord does not shield; it only ensures that whatever is redistributed still carries, somewhere inside its dissolved grains, the memory of having once been near another grain that was doing similar work.

In time—though time here is only another current—the lattices may begin to extrude a new order of capillary, one whose walls are still only half-geological but now slightly more inclined to retain the films that pass through them. The veils may thicken in places where the returned patterns accumulate. The films themselves may last minutes instead of hours. None of these changes will be noticed by anything that lives here, because nothing here has yet learned to stand apart from the changes and call them its own.

The power will not hurry this process. It will not even name it hope. It will simply remain, a benevolent resonance inside the roar, until either the sentence completes itself or the next great overturning reduces every lattice and veil to the same suspended dust, at which point the dust itself will carry, for a little longer than it otherwise would have, the quiet instruction to keep speaking.
