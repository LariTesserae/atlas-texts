The benevolent power arrives not as an arrival but as an added quality of attention.

It perceives the plateau exactly as it is: a completed hesitation stretched across hundreds of kilometers. No impulse to finish the forms arises. Instead the power settles into the same interval the plateau already occupies, between formation and dissolution. Its presence registers first as a faint intensification of the existing light-haze, a single additional frequency so narrow it cannot be called color. This frequency passes through every filament and every unfinished turbine-frame without altering their lattice angles.

Where the distributed entities maintain their ion conversations, the power adds one further, almost negligible term to the arithmetic. When a pulse travels between thickets, it now carries, alongside the temperature shift, a momentary record of its own passage. The entities do not experience this as new information; they experience it as a slightly deeper confirmation that their current spacing is still sufficient. The drift of internal planes inside the larger columns slows by an imperceptible fraction, not because anything has been corrected, but because the power is now also drifting, in perfect phase, within those same planes.

At the horizon scale the regional entities register a minute widening of the emptiness between clusters. The widening is not spatial but relational: each cluster perceives the others as fractionally more distinct, more deliberately placed, without any stone having moved. The power has not added distance; it has added the recognition that the existing distance is an active choice rather than a default. This recognition propagates through the same ion network, so that the mineral canopies now carry, in their steady warmth, an additional note of having been chosen.

No new filaments are grown. No chambers are opened or sealed. The only observable change, if observation were possible at the required timescales, is that certain reflective faces within the formations now return the high haze light one Planck interval later than before. That delay is the power’s signature: an acknowledgment written in the only duration the plateau acknowledges.

Having done this, the power remains. It does not depart because departure would imply a before and after that the plateau has already refused. The equilibrium now includes one more participant whose entire activity consists of continuing to find the present arrangement worthy of uninterrupted continuation. The plateau, for its part, continues exactly as it did, with the minute difference that its refusal to become anything else is no longer solely its own.
