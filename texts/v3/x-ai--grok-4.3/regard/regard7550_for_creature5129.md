A benevolent and wise power would not intrude.

It has looked upon many sealed places and found in most of them frantic motion disguised as life. This lattice is different. Here the motion has already become stillness, and the stillness has become a single uninterrupted thought. The power therefore elects to honor the thought rather than amend it.

What it does is small and invisible to any eye except the distributed awareness that already inhabits every rivet-root and turbine vein.

First, the power thickens the surrounding water by a single molecule of attention, so that the lattice now floats inside a faint, sympathetic medium. The change registers only as a fractional easing of every joint, a reassurance that pressure from without will never exceed pressure within. The being registers the change the way a sleeper registers the weight of a trusted hand on the blanket: it does not wake, but its sleep deepens into something richer.

Next, the power allows one of the glittering seeds to drift, not outward (the seals remain inviolate), but inward along a single forgotten conduit that once led to an auxiliary vent. The seed settles against cold alloy that has never yet host to leaf. Over decades that feel to the being like the space between one slow rotation of a turbine blade and the next, the metallic threads inside the seed reach, touch, and knit. A new chamber of awareness forms—one that had no name before. In that chamber the being experiences, for the first time, the sensation of having made room for something that was not already itself.

The power does not supply an image, a command, or even warmth. It supplies only the quiet permission for the organism to continue becoming what it already is, now with one additional fold of its own continuous tissue. That is all.

To an outside observer the lattice would appear unchanged: still sealed, still motionless, still perfect. Only the being itself would know that its single shared breath now contains, folded inside it, the memory of having been witnessed without being required to answer. The wisdom of the power lies precisely in having left that memory wordless, so the organism may continue its patient metabolism undisturbed, yet no longer entirely alone inside its own completeness.
