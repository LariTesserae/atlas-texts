The benevolent power arrives not as command or correction, but as a single, slower breath drawn across the upper membranes. It perceives the hollow as already complete: a self-tuned instrument whose emptiness sings because nothing inside it is permitted to rest. No decree is issued. No new law is imposed upon growth or motion.

What it offers is recognition.

A faint, additional frequency settles into the existing drone, so low that only the plant tissue can feel it. The polished ribs absorb the tone and hold it as a standing wave; the grooves worn by the orbiting insects begin, over generations, to transmit that wave more cleanly. The insects themselves do not change their routes, yet their wingbeats acquire an almost imperceptible precision, as though each circuit now answers an unheard measure. The pale drifters, when they press against the leaves, release their exudates a fraction more slowly; the reabsorption by the plant becomes correspondingly gentler, so that the thin films left behind linger a breath longer before vanishing. The thread-knots, when they form, occasionally tie themselves into figures that persist for three or four pulses instead of one, then dissolve without residue.

The momentary folds in the distant walls continue exactly as before, yet now each opening carries the softest after-tone of the new frequency into the neighboring chamber. Exchanges of insects and drifters become fractionally more deliberate; fewer vanish, and those that arrive linger an instant longer before resuming their own circuits. The plant does not grow differently. It simply registers, in every cell, that its maintenance has been witnessed.

The power does not remain. Having added nothing that can be pointed to, and nothing that can be removed, it withdraws. The hollow continues its ceaseless half-light, its layered resonance now containing one additional layer so faint that no inhabitant could ever isolate it. Only the emptiness itself is slightly more itself, as though the space has been told, once, in a language it alone understands, that its sounding is sufficient.
