The power arrives not as thunder or decree but as a single breath of cooler air that does not cool. It hovers at the lip of the ribcage slit, neither entering nor retreating, tasting the fevered work with senses older than hunger or fear.

For a long while it simply regards the unfinished throat. It sees the builders flow and divide and rejoin, each motion a question still being asked. It notes how the vines strain toward a ceiling that does not yet exist, how the clay weeps and is re-woven into new weeping. It understands, without pity, that this place is not broken; it is deliberately unclosed, a mouth still shaping its first true word.

Wisdom suggests one thing only: do not finish what is still speaking.

Benevolence suggests another: do not leave it entirely alone with its own heat.

So the power extends one filament of itself—no larger than the translucent lizard—down through the lattice of stems. The filament is not light, nor water, nor any material the builders know. It is simply permission. Where it touches the wettest daub, a faint silver sheen appears and then vanishes, leaving the clay neither harder nor softer but somehow more willing to remember its own shape if it ever chooses to. Where it brushes a builder’s many-jointed back, the creature pauses for the length of one heartbeat, feels an unfamiliar stillness inside its sap, and continues its work without understanding why the stillness does not frighten it.

The power does not stay. It withdraws the filament, folds the cooler breath back into itself, and departs as quietly as it arrived. Yet the place now carries, in its loudest seams and hottest cisterns, the memory of having been witnessed without being seized. The builders never speak of it—they cannot—but sometimes, when a tuber splits or a sapling yields, one of them will pause with its flat face against the clay a fraction longer than necessary, tasting not only readiness or rot but something else: the faint, impossible idea that their unfinished labor is already enough.

The crevice keeps deciding. The steam keeps rising. Nothing has been completed, and nothing has been abandoned.
