The power arrives without arrival. It has no need to cross distance or descend through the mist; attention itself is its only motion. In the moment it notices the half-formed continent, the weeping seams and the lattice-flowing presences simply register, for the briefest interval, that they are seen. The effect is smaller than a filament locking into place and larger than any tower the city will ever raise.

Nothing is added. No new mineral is summoned, no dome is given extra warmth. Instead the existing processes receive a single, imperceptible slackening of resistance. Fractures that would have sheared too quickly now hesitate half a heartbeat longer, giving the thread-thin accretions an extra moment to redistribute. A glider that has ridden an updraft for nineteen days feels, without knowing it feels, a faint permission to descend and sip rather than continue. The spindles inside the mats turn one additional revolution before the larger republics pass overhead. The city’s machines record these micro-delays as ordinary fluctuations and make no adjustments.

The power does not stay to watch the consequences. Having noticed, it is already elsewhere, yet its notice remains like a slow crystalline memory inside the rock. Over centuries the memory expresses itself only as a slightly higher incidence of successful exchanges between walker-republics and a marginally greater survival of young domes after the continent lurches. The beings themselves never become aware that anything has changed, because nothing essential has changed. The place is still unfinished, still provisional, still held together by the same low sigh.

If an observer with finer senses were present, they might say the power left behind a single instruction, phrased without words: continue evaporating and condensing as you are. The instruction is obeyed exactly because it was never given.
