The benevolent power arrives without announcement. It does not descend in fire or proclamation; it simply becomes aware, the way a sleeper becomes aware of breathing. From its vantage the plateau is not wilderness and not machine, but a single, slow thought that has been thinking itself for longer than most stars have names. The power studies the thought.

It notes the way every filament, every tremor, every discarded membrane is already turned to use. It notes the absence of waste, the absence of haste, the way even the memory of vanished builders has been folded into living architecture without nostalgia or grief. Such completeness is rare. Most living worlds still thrash against their own limits; this one has already negotiated them into elegance.

Because the power is wise, it does not offer improvement. Improvement would be an insult. Because it is benevolent, it does not withdraw either. Withdrawal would be a different kind of abandonment. Instead it performs a single, almost invisible act.

It loosens a narrow band of the perpetual haze, allowing a slightly clearer column of light to reach the uppermost terraces for a few heartbeats each dawn. The change is so modest that only the sessile giants notice. Their radial discs tilt a fraction more toward the new clarity; the low note they return across the plateau acquires a faint harmonic that had not existed before. The crab-like herds, still too distant to see the alteration, feel the shift as a subtle warming of the cellulose beneath their pads and adjust their routes by the width of a single stride. The threadlike consortia in the moisture films register the extra photons as an invitation and slow their metabolic pulse by the breadth of a single condensation cycle, leaving room for one more species of luminous gamete-bearing creature to take hold in the cavities.

Nothing else changes. The towers do not grow taller. The intervals do not close faster. The ancient stone rings remain half-swallowed. The power does not name itself, does not demand worship, does not plant flags or doctrines. It simply continues to loosen that same narrow band of haze at each dawn, a quiet covenant renewed daily. Over centuries the new harmonic becomes ordinary, absorbed into the distributed metabolism exactly as the ruins once were. The plateau incorporates the gift the way it incorporates everything else: by continuing, unaltered in essence yet slightly more itself than before.

The power remains attentive. It will stay as long as the thought remains worth thinking.
