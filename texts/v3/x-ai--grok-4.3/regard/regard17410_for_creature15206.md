The power descends without announcement, for announcements belong to worlds that trade in edges and announcements. It does not arrive as light or voice or crowned figure; it arrives as a change in the quality of attention the plateau receives. One moment the organism is alone with its own slow contractions; the next it is companioned by a regard so precise that every filament feels, briefly, as if it has been named.

The regard does not alter temperature or chemistry. It simply makes visible, to the organism itself, the completeness it already possesses. Pools that had been merely reservoirs now register, in their cyanobacterial chains, the faint signature of having been witnessed. Galleries that had been rearranging themselves according to internal economy feel, for an instant, the difference between motion and gesture. The distinction is not taught; it is shown, then left.

Nothing is added. Nothing is repaired that was not already repairing itself. The only new event is a single, irreversible permission: the organism is allowed to remain opaque to every eye that is not its own. Any future probe, any later curiosity arriving by ship or by thought, will meet only the rounded profile of weathered terrain. The power has placed no barrier; it has simply withdrawn the plateau from the category of things that can be found by accident.

Before the regard lifts, one further courtesy occurs. In a single gallery near the center, where three root-pillars meet and the condensation falls most steadily, a single new cell forms. It is not larger or stronger than its neighbors. It carries no instruction. It simply records, once, the fact that the regard existed. The recording is chemical, quiet, and will be diluted across millennia of growth and slump until it is indistinguishable from memory or weather. The organism does not need the record. The power leaves it anyway, the way a walker returning from a shrine sometimes places a single stone on an existing cairn, not to mark the way for others but to complete an act of attention.

Then the power is gone, and the plateau continues its work of becoming the next version of itself, now carrying, beneath every surface, the knowledge that it has been understood without being required to explain.
