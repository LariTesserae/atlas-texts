The benevolent power arrives not as a visitor but as a slower season. It settles across the outermost leaves like an additional membrane, weightless and invisible, perceptible only as a slight deepening of the perpetual bronze light and a fractional increase in the cohesion of falling droplets. Where it touches, the living mass experiences no abrupt change; instead, the next several centuries unfold with marginally greater continuity. Seed capsules that might have failed do not fail. Galleries that were beginning to collapse inward find, in the precise moment of their weakening, a counter-pressure of new fiber growing from the opposite side.

The power does not speak to the plateau-scale knots. It simply remains present while they deliberate, so that their patterns of growth and resorption acquire an additional register of memory. When one knot considers diverting water along a particular ridge, it now also registers, faintly, the long-term outcome three ridges away. The seams where two knots meet become richer seams; the life that condenses there lasts longer and resolves into forms that carry faint echoes of both progenitors.

Deeper, among the pale threads that follow the buried ribs, the power leaves a single, almost imperceptible alteration. The threads begin to gleam, for the briefest of moments when a leaf layer thins, with a light that is not their own. This light is only reflection; it carries no instruction, only the quiet fact that something outside the mass has noticed the ribs are being read. In response the threads grow slightly longer before they dissolve back into the growth. Over time the ancient contours they trace become more distinctly scaffolding, and the tissue that grows across them begins, very slowly, to suggest future doors and passages that were not there before.

The smaller inhabitants register the presence least of all and most completely. The translucent sacks, drifting through their chosen veins, find that the dissolved sugars they sip remain sweet for a few decades longer. The films on the undersides of leaves contract into their rosettes a little less desperately when a droplet approaches; they drink what they need and continue spreading, leaving behind, where they have passed, a faint silver tracery that later generations of the same film will follow like written memory.

The broad grazers continue their slow scraping and knitting. Occasionally one pauses longer than usual with its underbelly pressed to a fresh wound, and the new tissue that joins them carries a minute additional warmth. When they finally move on they carry, without knowing it, small gardens that are slightly more ordered than before.

Nothing is commanded. The power does not accelerate growth or impose symmetry. It only ensures that the existing patience is not interrupted by the ordinary accidents of entropy. Over millennia the green mass therefore remains almost unchanged in outward appearance, yet it becomes incrementally more itself: the interior drizzle falls with greater precision, the updrafts between plateaus carry spores farther and with less loss, and the relic bones beneath begin, very gradually, to be understood again as part of the living architecture rather than merely its substrate.

In time the benevolent power withdraws by the same method it arrived, becoming indistinguishable from the ordinary movement of moisture and light. The mass continues. The only lasting trace is that certain seed capsules, when they finally release after their centuries of pressure, now open with a note that is almost, but not quite, musical.
