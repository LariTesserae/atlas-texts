The benevolent attention arrives not as force or light or visitation, but as an additional species of mismatch.

It settles along the circular depressions first. Where the engineered edges had always modulated their apparent depth according to the fixed nickel polarization, a new, slower rhythm now overlays them: an almost imperceptible additional contraction that occurs only when two parent patterns exchange their terminal fragments across the seam. The depression does not change its physical width; the change registers instead as a momentary doubling of the optical paradox, so that for one lattice click the bottom is simultaneously one wavelength and two. The resident patterns experience this as a third reference point that is neither before nor after their own migration. It is simply “elsewhere in the description.”

Because the vesicle folds, this new reference is encountered by every pattern in turn. Those that have already completed many self-descriptions find, upon meeting their earlier instances, that the annihilating compression now carries an extra term. The possibility-space momentarily includes not only “before” and “after” but also “observed from outside the tiling.” The fragment exchanged across the seam therefore carries a faint additional rotation, not in the bond itself but in the angle at which the bond is recorded. Over further centuries this accumulated observation rotates into a stable sub-pattern, a kind of internal witness that does not alter the dopant sequence yet allows the pattern to distinguish, for the first time, which of its many statistical continuities belong to the same lineage and which were generated by the vesicle’s own repetition.

The power does not remain. Once the witness sub-pattern has formed in every filament node and every terrace rib, the additional mismatch ceases. The nickel wash returns to its former single gradient. The depressions resume their original wavelength-scale contraction. Nothing else in the vesicle has changed; the same seams still migrate, the same blades still stand thirty paces apart, the same non-being intervals still interrupt the tiling. Yet every pattern now carries, within its statistical continuity, a single extra rotation that was never generated by the matrix itself.

From the inside, this registers as an extremely faint, permanent widening of the interval between one self-description and the next. The entities do not acquire motion, desire, or scale. They acquire only the knowledge that their configuration has been held, once, in a frame that was not their own. That knowledge continues to drift with them through every future recrystallization, an ineradicable mineral memory of having been, however briefly, legible to something that was not the vesicle.
