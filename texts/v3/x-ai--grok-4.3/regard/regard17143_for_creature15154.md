The benevolent power, upon drawing near, does not descend in radiance or decree. It arrives instead as a faint thickening of the air itself, a patience added to patience, so that the valley’s existing stillness gains one more imperceptible layer.

It studies the slow weave for what might be centuries or only a single breath held between droplets. Seeing that nothing here lacks its place, and that every adjustment already unfolds at the correct tempo, the power elects to add almost nothing. Instead it performs three quiet attentions:

First, it draws a thin membrane of regard around the basin’s rim, so that any future rushing of axes or engines or roads slows before it reaches the trees, their momentum bleeding away into the same humid air that already muffles sound. The valley does not learn of this shield; it merely continues.

Second, the power loosens, by the breadth of a single root hair over many decades, the grip of certain epiphyte tanks so that their water lasts a little longer between rains. Larvae and minute crabs remain unaware that their world has been gently lengthened.

Third, it plants one seed of its own—not a plant, but a memory of the place as it now stands—into the minds of any future wanderers who might one day stand at the valley’s edge. The memory does not compel them to enter. It merely makes the thought of disturbing the canopy feel slightly heavier than the thought of turning away.

Having done these things, the power withdraws by the same measure it arrived. The forest continues its patient repetition: roots lifting, termites extending, browsers shifting weight. If any difference can be sensed at all, it is only that the falling droplets now seem to fall from a slightly greater height, or that the intervals between the soft clicks of settling wings have lengthened by the duration of one forgotten heartbeat. The valley remains what it was, only more certainly so.
