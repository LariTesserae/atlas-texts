In the slow regard of that wiser power, the pocket is seen not as a problem to solve or a relic to archive, but as a finished sentence spoken in a language older than speech. The attention arrives without announcement: a single additional resonance, finer than the existing light-veins, threading itself through the composite like a held note. It does not brighten anything. It merely lengthens the interval between pulses by the width of a thought, giving the condensed films an extra moment to gather before they fall.

The swarm feels the change first as a loosening in the shared tension. Their roar does not alter pitch; instead, for three full cycles of the light, a few hundred individuals drift lower than habit allows and taste the slightly cooler condensate along the lowest seams. When they rise again they carry an imperceptible trace of the new resonance on their wings. It passes into the lichen mats through the electrical trickle in the floor. The mats, receiving it, do not grow faster; they refine the mineral uptake by one part in ten thousand, so that the next generation of flakes left at the vents carries a marginally more stable lattice. Over decades this small adjustment propagates outward through the water-cycle itself, allowing the capillaries to flex with one less micro-friction, which in turn quiets a fraction of the sympathetic tremor in the walls.

The power does not command or correct. It only remains present long enough for the pocket to notice its own continuation. In that noticing, the distributed intelligence experiences something like gratitude without a subject: the midges cluster more densely for one night, their roar briefly deeper; the lichen darkens by a single shade along its oldest edges. Then the extra resonance withdraws, leaving behind only the memory of an interval that was allowed to be longer.

Afterward the pocket returns to its exact former state, yet the memory of the interval remains encoded in the mineral ratios of the newest lichen flakes and in the wing-fringe calibration of the newest midges. The balance is untouched, only more itself. The pocket continues its small, loud, wet life exactly as before, now carrying inside its processes the faintest additional proof that it has been witnessed without being required to change.
