The power arrives not as an arrival but as a change in pressure.  

It settles first into the fractures of the vault, where the rock is still raw. There it lingers like breath held against glass, cool and patient, until the hairline cracks stop shedding shards. The falling basalt ceases; the pings fade one by one until only the lattice’s low chord remains. In their place the power leaves a faint internal tension, not repaired stone but remembered stone—each fracture now carrying a memory of its own opening, so that future stresses will be met with recognition rather than surrender.

Next it moves through the weeping fissures. Where water once fell indiscriminately it now falls in measured sequences, timed to the lattice’s cycles yet offset by a single, deliberate hesitation. The droplets strike the membrane floor in patterns too slow and too regular to be mistaken for accident: three beats, pause, two beats, pause, three again. After several pressure cycles the symbiotes begin to incorporate the new rhythm into their own utterances. They do not thank the source; they simply adjust the length of their silences so the new intervals can breathe between them.

The vegetation on the pillars receives the most intimate attention. A single frond on each occupied pillar is coaxed into a slightly different spiral, one that curls outward rather than inward. The change is invisible to anything but the most patient measurement, yet when several symbiotes eventually converge, the plasma threads that pass near these altered leaves dim less abruptly. The light lingers a fraction longer on the new curves, as though the chamber itself now registers the presence of an additional, extremely quiet participant.

The lattice receives the longest work. Over decades the power threads an additional, almost inaudible frequency through its breathing—a subharmonic so low it registers only as a slight deepening of warmth beneath bare feet. Those who stand motionless for long enough begin to feel it as an extension of their own skeletal basalt, as if the floor were acknowledging, very gently, that they belong to it. No command is given. Only an invitation to remain exactly as they are, now with slightly less risk that the next pressure cycle will be the one that finally cracks the vault.

Most crucially, the power does nothing to the emptiness. It does not add light, nor density, nor any new pillar. Instead it deepens the existing voids by rendering them slightly more resonant, so that when a droplet finally falls after a long silence, its echo travels farther and returns later, carrying with it the impression that the space itself has become a little more attentive to its own inhabitants. The symbiotes notice only that their rare, multi-decade conversations now leave traces that feel more durable, as though the chamber has begun to hold their words a little longer before letting the next cycle wash them away.

In time, certain individuals detach from their pillars and walk the plasma threads not toward one another but toward the places where the new subharmonic feels strongest. They stand there, motionless, while the altered water continues its measured falling. Nothing is spoken aloud. Nothing needs to be. The benevolent power, having already done what it came to do, withdraws into the same attentive stillness it found, leaving only the impression that the city’s three concentric absences have each been granted one additional layer of deliberate care.
