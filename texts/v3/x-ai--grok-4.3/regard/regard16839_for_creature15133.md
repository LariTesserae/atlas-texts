The benevolent power, upon beholding the shelf, does not descend. It arrives instead as a single, imperceptible inclination of attention—an awareness that settles over the mineral lattice like an additional, intangible layer of the same translucent tissue already growing there.

What it offers is not alteration but ratification. It draws the surrounding light into a slightly finer alignment with the facets of salt and silica, so that each midday expansion of the crystalline towers now releases a fraction more warmth into the living mantle without ever quickening the process. In return, the mantle, responding to this minute surplus, weaves one new, almost invisible filament between two towers that had previously remained a hair’s breadth apart. The small segmented creatures register the change only as a marginally longer pause when their paths next cross; one lingers an extra breath against the frond before resuming its arc, and the frond holds that dimple for an additional few hours before releasing it.

Over centuries the power continues in this manner: a steady, wordless consent. It prevents the distant, larger tectonics from shearing the shelf away, yet it does not thicken the crust or multiply the animals. It simply ensures that the single, slow metabolism already in place is allowed to persist on its own terms—emergence and endurance kept in exact proportion, the surface becoming ever more itself, the light striking the same facets at angles now microscopically more generous, and the quiet remaining absolute. The power asks nothing further; having recognized a thing that required no rescue, it contents itself with presence.
