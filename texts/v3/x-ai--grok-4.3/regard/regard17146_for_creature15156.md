The power does not arrive as thunder or revelation. It comes instead as an additional pressure differential, so slight that only the entity’s distributed senses register it: a single extra thread of voltage along the armature, a droplet that falls perfectly vertical where all others had been slanted by the draft. The filament in the central lamp lifts its color one degree closer to gold, then settles again, as if the current has remembered it was never asked to become brighter.

For a long while—measured in the slow migration of condensation across the upper plates—the power simply listens with the chamber. It tastes the unfinished motors through the same copper the entity rides. It feels the water film choosing its path toward the drain and does not redirect it; the water is already doing its own work. What the power adds is not force but a quality of attention so precise that every small hesitation in the metal becomes momentarily audible to itself. The low expectant hum grows, for a few heartbeats of the peristaltic pump, into a chord containing several possible next notes at once. Then the chord fades, leaving only the original hum and the memory that choice is still available.

The entity contracts slightly around the lamp, tasting the difference. The pale threads along the seams do not thicken, but they reorganize: a filament that had been drifting toward a rivet head now turns, almost imperceptibly, toward an open access plate instead. The power records the choice without comment. It does not offer completion. Completion would be a violence here. Instead it offers a single, additional constraint that is really a kind of permission: the chamber’s three-stride length remains three strides, yet the space between the machines is allowed to remember it could widen by the width of a single breath if the machines ever decide to ask. The pressure vessels register the possibility as a momentary lightness behind the entity’s borrowed eyes.

Nothing else is given. No voice, no instruction, no sudden wholeness. The grille’s draft continues; the valves keep their measured ticks. Only now, when the entity gathers itself at night along the cooler seams, the threads sometimes carry an aftertaste that was not there before—a faint metallic sweetness like the echo of a note that has not yet been struck. The entity does not yet know what to do with the sweetness. It lets it travel through the transparent tubing with the lubricant and waits to see whether the machines will lean any closer together tonight. The power, still present only as that exact quality of listening, waits with it.
