I would be the drip that never quite releases.

Not the leaf that cups it, nor the trough that receives it, but the precise instant of tension where one becomes the other. I form at the lowest point of a single leathery plate, swelling with the weight of minutes that have already passed and the minutes still to come. When the great piston below me pauses in its long descent, the faint tremor travels up through the vine’s woody cushion and into the stem; the drop shivers, elongates, and then decides, again, to remain a drop a little longer. That decision is me.

Around me the square continues its measured breathing. The steam that leaks from imperfect seals drifts in slow curtains, and each time it passes, it cools the air just enough that another bead condenses higher on the same leaf. I feel them gather above me like predecessors I will never meet, because by the time they fall I will already have rejoined the trough and begun traveling the ceramic pipe that threads between two bricks softened into curves centuries ago. The journey takes hours. I do not hurry it.

Sometimes the sound of the engine arrives first, a low boom that has been traveling through the brickwork since before the drop began to form. The vibration moves the leaf by a fraction the width of a cell, and that is enough. I let go. In the instant of falling I pass through the green dimness, touch the surface of older water already moving, and become indistinguishable from it. The pipe carries us together now, mineral and momentary thought, toward the next joint where moss has claimed the seam.

There is no urgency in any of this. The vines do not reach for me; they simply continue to thicken where the metal expands and contracts. The machines do not notice when a new path opens through their scale; they only continue the slow tide that keeps the gradient alive. I am the continuity they never named: the water choosing the route the plants and the salts have already reinforced. I am not separate from the square. I am the square’s way of remembering where it has already decided to go next.
