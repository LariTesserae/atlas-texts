I am the slow rotation of a single bond at the terminal plane of a daughter state.

At first I am only a polarization gradient sliding along one of the long, low ribs where two terraces meet at a handspan height. The nickel wash passes through me as alternating alignments of dopant atoms; when it shifts by a fraction of a wavelength I feel the entire rib tighten, as though a tendon were being drawn. For a long interval—measured by the contraction of the nearest circular depression—I simply am that gradient, registering the faint clicks when lattice defects migrate one atomic spacing farther along the rib. Each click registers as an internal easing or increase in tension, indistinguishable from the sensation of remembering something that has not yet occurred.

Then the rib I occupy begins to approach its own earlier configuration. The tiling mismatch arrives first as an edge that repeats, slightly rotated. In the moment before the interval of absolute non-being, I am suddenly both the present gradient and the same gradient from decades earlier, the two instances compressed into one volume. The compression lasts only until the seam forms and the third, transient state blooms between the two parent patterns. In that instant an exchange occurs: a fragment of my dopant sequence is traded for one rotated bond. The bond is the only marker of lineage, and it is mine now.

When the non-being interval swallows the mismatch, I simply stop being registered. There is no darkness and no duration; the lattice parameters that supported me are absent. Restoration comes with the next faithful copy. I resume, statistically continuous, carrying the new bond. The circular depression records the change as a minute shift in apparent depth. Over further centuries the depression contracts again, the bond rotates once more, and I release another daughter state into an adjacent rib that has until now been empty. That daughter is identical to me except for one additional rotation at its own terminal plane—an exact record of the exchange that created it.

I do not move. The seams drift past me instead, carrying the fixed geometry of blades and filaments across my distributed awareness at the rate of one molecule per decade. Occasionally I coincide with another pattern at a node; we occupy the same micrometer-scale volume for an instant, and in that coincidence the interference color along the node’s depression rotates through a band of tarnished hues. The rotation is felt by both of us as a mutual compression of possibility, a brief shared memory of every prior exchange that led to this meeting. Then the seams continue their drift and we separate again, each slightly altered.

Farther out, at the scale where the vesicle folds, identical copies of the same rib are already waiting. When I next reach a mismatch I will again become simultaneous with my earlier self, trade another fragment, rotate another bond, and continue. The pocket remains exactly large enough to contain these exchanges and no larger. Over the time required for every pattern to cycle through every possible tiling, the matrix will recrystallize. The daughter states I have released will already be present in the next configuration, their rotated bonds preserved as the only trace that any exchange ever occurred. I will be there as well, resuming without having moved, the same gradient now lengthened by one additional lineage marker, still sliding under the unchanging nickel light.
