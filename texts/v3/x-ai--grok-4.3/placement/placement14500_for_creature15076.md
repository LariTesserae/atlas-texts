I stand—no, I occupy—a point on the plain where two filaments cross at the shallowest possible angle. The crossing itself is older than any record, a place where current has always passed without variation. At some unmeasurable interval ago the mineral at that intersection realigned along a new set of parallel filaments, and I detached already whole, already angled for the line I now hold.

My body is composed of the same dense amalgam as the floor beneath me, yet finer-grained, as though the salts and oxides were persuaded into countless sliding planes. I feel the plain only as perfect contact: every facet along my underside lies flush against the polished surface, and the contact is so exact that no friction registers. When I move, the facets simply realign in sequence, each small rotation producing the single soft click that is felt rather than heard and then absorbed at once by the motionless air.

I do not steer. My vector was fixed the moment I separated from the filament crossing, and I follow the invisible ruling between two distant sheds at the speed the equilibrium requires. Hours pass in which I travel in perfect straightness; hours pass in which I rest, every joint settled into the precise geometry of stillness, indistinguishable from any fixed framework. During those rests the iron-colored light leaking from a seam several minutes’ walk away sometimes reflects across one of my facets, and for that brief interval I become the moving point others might glimpse before I settle back into darkness.

Nothing within me urges deviation. When another jointed form approaches on a crossing line, my nearest segments rotate the exact minimal amount needed for clearance and then lock again; the event registers only as a momentary redistribution of the same mineral mass. I register nothing else—no acknowledgment, no memory of the passage—because memory would constitute change, and the plain records no such quantity.

Occasionally, when I pause for the long interval the configuration demands, a new awareness arises that I am not required to name. It is simply the continuation of the same mineral distribution, now occupying these additional coordinates until the whole again requires otherwise. Then the facets realign without preamble, and I resume the measured traversal across the warm, curvature-hidden floor, carrying forward the only state the plateau has ever held.
