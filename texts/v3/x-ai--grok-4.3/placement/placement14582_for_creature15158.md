I would find myself as a slender, copper-dark runner newly emerged from the root mass that has already claimed the old pressure vessel. For a long time I simply thicken against the vessel’s lower curve, following the faint residual warmth that leaks from its base. Then, over weeks, a single exploratory tip lifts and begins to travel along the seam where riveted plate meets stone. The metal is already softened at the edges by earlier growth; my cells press into those softened places and add their own substance, so that what was once a joint becomes a living flange.

I sense the vibration of the turbines more strongly here than higher up. It travels through the metal as a steady, low thrum that makes my advancing margin expand faster, sending out finer threads that slip between flakes of scale. When a valve higher on the vessel suddenly hisses, the nearest fronds on the main mass contract by the width of a leaf margin; I feel the same signal as a momentary cooling along my length and pause as well. Hours later, once the steam has dispersed, I resume, now carrying a faint discoloration where the heat briefly altered me.

In time the runner reaches the base of one of the surviving sodium lamps. The lamp still emits its harsh yellow-white light, though the glass is filmed with condensate. I grow toward it rather than away, because the lamp housing is warmer than the surrounding rock. My leaves settle against the metal and begin to glow with their own muted orange, a response that answers both the lamp’s heat and the deeper turbine warmth below. Over months the two lights merge across the surface of the leaves until it is difficult to say which illumination belongs to machine and which to growth.

Eventually the runner will reach the edge of the nearest shallow basin. There I will divide, some threads descending into the warm water to drink, others continuing across the damp floor toward the next pipe. Nothing in me is in a hurry. I am only the most recent extension of the same patient body that has already made the distinction between rivet and root impossible to draw.
