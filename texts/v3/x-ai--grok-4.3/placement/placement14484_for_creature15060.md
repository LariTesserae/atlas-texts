I would find myself first as an interval that the water itself has not yet decided how to fill. Between two shear lines where the currents oppose each other without quite meeting, a pocket forms that is neither warmer nor colder but simply slower to accept the pressure of what surrounds it. In that hesitation a radial pattern begins to assert itself, faint as the markings left inside the circular ridges, expanding and contracting over centuries until the pattern is indistinguishable from the plateau’s own memory of having once been worked.

That pattern is me. It settles just beneath the sediment where the worked stone still pulses, breathing in time with the anomalous gold shafts that slide overhead. When one of those shafts lingers, the pattern releases a single circular spore that does not sink so much as choose a direction downward that has never been downward before. The slope rotates beneath it, yet I remain exactly where the rotation began, so that the spore carries an impression of me into the velvet mats, and the mats, in turn, forget to close completely over the place it entered.

Most of the time I remain only this: a pressure that parts the translucent fronds an instant before any current reaches them, and a silence that the hair-fine tendrils of the drifting clusters momentarily reorder themselves to match. When they rearrange into the geometry of the submerged ridges, the extra laminar layer that forms afterward continues to carry a trace of my outline for days, long after the clusters have drifted on. The trace is not visible. It is simply the place where the water, for a while, remembers to hesitate in the same way I do.

During the fractures, when a distant swell appears beneath an outstretched thought, I stand within the thin interval between versions. Tall and narrow, composed of the same milky less-dense water that the fracture momentarily permits, I do not move; the resealing of the world performs the movement for me. What remains after the fracture closes is a faint radial brightness in the water itself, identical to the markings on the stone, and to the pattern that still breathes beneath the sediment. That brightness is the only evidence that something here has been remembered again, and that the remembering has chosen, for now, to continue.
