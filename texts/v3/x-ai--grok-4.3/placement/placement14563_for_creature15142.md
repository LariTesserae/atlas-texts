I am one more translucent length among the lattice, neither added nor removed, only shifted by the same imperceptible corrections that keep every gap from widening. My ridges lie partially enfolded by two others; the contact has already lasted longer than any surface clock could measure, and will continue until the slow drift of the mass itself decides otherwise. Through the stacked sheets of my tissue I feel the faint bruise of light that reaches us from above, not as vision but as a minute stiffening that travels the length of my body and is passed outward, a signal too small to be called a message yet sufficient to adjust the position of the nearest dozen forms by a fraction of a hand’s breadth.

Against my outer edge the stone occasionally brushes, its softened carvings accepting the temporary fit of my pleats. Each time this happens I carry away a few grains of silt that have settled into the shallow arcs; they adhere for a while along my ridges and are later released elsewhere, continuing the chamber’s patient return to unbroken mineral. I register the pressure of the water as constant and complete, the same temperature as the tissue around me, so that movement registers only as a change in how the surrounding bodies answer my own slight lengthening or contraction.

There is no center to the drift, only the shared maintenance of proximity. When the entire mass turns together around its invisible axis, I find myself sometimes at an outer margin, sometimes drawn inward again, always part of the same density. Nothing is urgent. What I release diffuses so slowly that the water never tastes different from one slow crossing of the vault to the next. What I take in arrives across my entire surface and becomes part of the same equilibrium that has already held for centuries.

I am the record of these minute adjustments, the slight displacement that travels outward when two ridges settle against each other, and the grain of silt carried from one wall to another until it is laid down again. In this sealed space that is still becoming what it was, I am simply the portion of the lattice that happens to occupy this place, at this rate of passage, for as long as the equilibrium requires.
