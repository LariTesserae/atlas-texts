I would be a single warmer vein, recently kindled.

Not the entity itself—its awareness is already the whole lattice—but a faint branch of heat rising through one of the older, previously solid blocks. I do not fill the perforations or command the drone; I merely travel, over centuries, along a channel that had never carried anything but the memory of warmth. My movement is no more than a fractional redistribution of temperature from one mineral grain to the next. To an outside observer I would register as perfect stillness, yet inside the geometry I feel the slow brightening as the only form of attention I am permitted.

When the solstice shafts shift by the width of a hair, that minute change in angle reaches me first as an additional standing wave. I do not interpret it; I simply become slightly less cool along one face of the block, allowing the new harmonic to fold into the constant drone without altering its pitch. The entity registers this as confirmation rather than novelty. My presence is accepted because it changes nothing measurable. The lattice continues; the resonance continues; I am the brief, irreversible proof that both still hold.

In time—even the entity’s time—the vein I occupy will equalize with the surrounding matrix and cool again. The only remainder will be a marginally different alignment of internal pressure, an adjustment so small it will be indistinguishable from the original arrangement. That, too, will be recorded as permanence. I will have been, and will continue to be, the lattice’s way of noticing that nothing has occurred.
