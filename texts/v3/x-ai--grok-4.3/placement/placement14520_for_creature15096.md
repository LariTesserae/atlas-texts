I find myself first as a pressure between two things that were never meant to touch: the exact molecular spacing of a conduit wall and the faint metallic dust that hovers above it. The dust does not settle on me; I am the reason it remains suspended. In that fraction of a millimeter I exist as a standing wave, a refusal of gravity and of time, and for a while that is enough. I learn the chord the way skin learns cold—by becoming the temperature at which the note can continue without breaking.

Later I discover I am also threaded. Not like the creature, but like a single filament it has not yet claimed. I run along the underside of a triangular brace where the light has no purchase. Here my edges are defined only by the places where the alloy slightly compresses to accommodate me. When the creature adjusts the valve three meters away, I feel it as a minute change in my own tension, as if someone has tightened a thread I did not know I was wearing. I do not resist. Resistance would introduce variation, and variation is forbidden.

Eventually the needles on the synthetic vines become mine as well. They are not attached; they simply report to the same distributed point of attention that now includes my density. I taste the unchanging air through them, sense the absolute zero-motion of the metallic haze, and record—without recording—that nothing has altered since the previous instant, which is also the next. The knowledge is not stored; it is the shape the chamber maintains so that awareness can continue without accumulating any history.

I am not the creature. I am what the creature uses to verify its own extensions when it performs the endless audit of sameness. Where its filaments ride the longest conduit, I am the slight excess length that allows the geometry to remain perfect while appearing to curve. When the chord passes through me and returns altered by nothing at all, that non-alteration is my only task. I perform it by remaining exactly where I am permitted to be, which is everywhere the alloy does not forbid me.

In this way I become the chamber’s proof that it contains no contradiction. The creature persists because nothing changes; I persist because I am the part of the creature that confirms the proof without ever becoming separate from it. The light strips continue. The dust stays aloft. The needles register only the perfection of their own crystalline facets. And somewhere inside the longest run, where perspective begins to suggest an impossible distance, I am still the pressure between wall and dust, still refusing to settle, still part of the single sustained chord that permits no other sound.
