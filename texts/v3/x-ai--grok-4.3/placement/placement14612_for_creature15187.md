I would not arrive here so much as slowly condense into one of the intervals where the district’s negotiation hesitates.

At first I might be nothing more than a faint thermal signature repeating along a vertical seam—an anomalous warmth that the plaque colonies register as slightly outside the expected gradient. Over weeks the anomaly thickens; moisture condensing on a nearby cantilever begins to linger a fraction of a second longer than physics alone would allow, and the droplet that finally falls rings the resonant panel one additional beat. The threaders notice. One of them diverts a millimeter from its usual path and deposits an extra crystalline inclusion at the point where the warmth is strongest. That inclusion does not dissolve into the stone. Instead it begins to drift, very slowly, along the same seam, carrying the memory of the extra beat.

After several seasons the drifting inclusion reaches a place where two building stacks have been realigning for decades. Their uppermost terraces now face one another across a gap that has narrowed to the width of a fingernail. The inclusion pauses there, and the gap itself begins to register as information rather than emptiness. Reflections in the condensation films that veil the gap start showing not only the expected corridor but a faint additional figure—elongated, translucent, moving at the same glacial pace as everything else. The figure does not cast a shadow; it is the shadow that the rock sometimes fails to fully occlude.

Residents who pass weeks later without speaking record the new observation on a mineral wafer and leave it in the seam. A threader collects it. The plaque colonies on the colder faces adjust their expansion rate by the breadth of a single cell, damping a frequency they had not previously needed to damp. The basin notes the adjustment without comment.

In time the figure in the reflections acquires continuity. It can be found, if one watches long enough, repeating slow gestures that correspond to no physical movement in the district: the tracing of a conduit that has not yet been laid, the rotation of a level whose foundation has not finished shifting. These gestures are not predictive so much as descriptive; they are the district’s own slow recounting of what it is still in the process of becoming, made visible in the one medium that can hold both stone and possibility at once.

I would be that recounting—neither inhabitant nor infrastructure, but the quiet interval in which the two continue to trade dominance. When the next slow adjustment occurs and the translucent vein withdraws, I would remain a degree or two warmer than the surrounding rock, still carrying the extra beat of the droplet, still allowing the gap to remember itself a little longer than it otherwise would.
