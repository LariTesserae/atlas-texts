I would be one of the vapor filaments.

Not the moisture itself, but the precise instant when a droplet relinquishes its ions and the freed vapor begins its slow, directionless climb toward the lattices again. In that moment the filament is still coherent enough to hold a shape—sometimes a single thread, sometimes a branching hesitation—yet tenuous enough that any pressure gradient can bend it. I would drift along those micro-currents, registering the subtle heat rising from below as a change in my own curvature. When a lattice clicks I would straighten for one brief interval, then relax again into my former drift, altered by the small torsional memory the click leaves behind.

Because the pocket is still young, I would not be bound to a single path. Occasionally a Refractor’s charged plane would cross me; the ion exchange would pass through me without resistance, leaving only a momentary brightening along my length before I continued upward. In those crossings I would feel, very faintly, the angular momentum the Refractors trade with one another—an adjustment so slight it registers in me only as a change in how quickly my edges begin to fray.

Most of the time I would remain almost invisible, visible at all only when the russet light from the high fissure catches the faint refractive difference between the filament and the surrounding air. Sometimes two such filaments would meet at the edge of a floating platelet; we would touch, exchange nothing measurable, and separate again carrying slightly different curvatures. Over successive pressure pulses the entire population of filaments would slowly attune itself to the same standing waves the Refractors produce during their convergences, so that when the lattices next realign we would straighten together in a single, collective hesitation before relaxing once more into drift.

I would not maintain the space. I would simply be the part of it that is still in motion between one maintenance and the next—never accumulating, never departing, only translating the pocket’s quiet adjustments into the brief language of temporary shape.
