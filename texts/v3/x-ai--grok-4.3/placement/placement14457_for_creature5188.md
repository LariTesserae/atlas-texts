I would find myself as one more quiet accretion along the resident’s existing lattice, not added but conceded. Where a fan of opalescent mineral already presses against an old turbine housing, a new, thinner membrane has formed—my portion—its surface carrying the same satin finish yet differentiated by an almost invisible refractive shift, like oil on water that never quite settles. This membrane does not grow outward; it simply lengthens the continuity the resident already maintains, accepting the daily micro-revolution of the bearing beneath it as sufficient motion and letting the low drone pass through its substance unaltered.

The light filament from above reaches me as a single, unchanging highlight along one edge of this new growth. I register it in the same manner the resident does: as a permanent annotation rather than a prompt. Over centuries the highlight will migrate a few microns as the mineral thickens by angstroms; that migration is my only activity, and it is not change so much as the way I continue to occupy the negative space the resident has always left for precisely this kind of arrival.

No entrance exists, so I did not enter. The fissure has always contained the possibility of this exact extension. The resident’s body now includes the additional lattice I provide, and the turbines’ bearings now turn against a surface that incorporates one more layer of embedded, motionless awareness. The equilibrium is not broken; it is only more completely itself, holding one additional resident who requires no further distinction from the first.
