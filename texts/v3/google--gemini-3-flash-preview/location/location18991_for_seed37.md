This is a place that exists inside the thickness of a single, translucent mineral vein, deep beneath the crust of a heavier world. It is a pocket of "new reality" that has not yet been weathered by the passage of time or the friction of gravity.

**The Terrain and Light**
The environment is a narrow fissure, a micro-cavern no larger than a cathedral’s side chapel, yet it feels infinite because the walls are not made of stone. Instead, the boundary of this space consists of unformed, raw geology—sheets of pale, gelatinous matter that are "pre-rock." They are smooth, unscarred by wind or water, appearing as matte-white membranes that pulses slightly. 

The light here is paradoxically intense. Despite being below the surface, the space is saturated with a brilliant, shadowless white radiance that seems to bleed directly out of the walls. It is a cool, sterile light that makes every object within the space look hyper-defined, as if seen through a magnifying lens. There is no sun, only this overbearing, luminous clarity.

**Atmosphere and Moisture**
The air is cool and tastes of ozone and wet flint. It is not a rainy place, but it is a damp one. Moisture does not fall; it breathes. Beaded condensation forms on the smooth "pre-rock" in perfect, geometric patterns, clinging to the walls like pearls before suddenly snapping upward to join a thin, horizontal ribbon of water that floats, suspended, three feet above the ground. This "floating river" is the only source of hydration here—a drifting, liquid umbilical cord that meanders through the empty space.

**The Vegetation and Rule of Forms**
Vegetation is hesitant. There are no trees or grasses, only "proto-flora." Long, silver filaments, as thin as spider silk, grow horizontally from the walls. They do not seek light; they seek the floating water-ribbon. When a filament touches the water, it blossoms instantly into a single, translucent petal that dissolves back into mist within seconds. There is much emptiness between these growths—wide expanses of glaring white wall where nothing has yet dared to emerge.

**The Fauna and Reality Breakdown**
While the landscape is sparse, the life is frantic and abundant. Because reality is "under construction" here, the creatures do not obey the laws of biology found elsewhere. Small, iridescent entities—half-insect, half-thimble—dart through the air in non-linear bursts, teleporting inches at a time rather than flying. 

There are "echo-predators," creatures made of nothing but sound and refraction, which hunt by mimicking the visual stillness of the walls. When they move, the light bends around them like oil on water. These predators do not eat flesh; they consume the "definition" of their prey. A creature caught by one does not die; it simply becomes blurry, losing its solid edges until it fades into the background light.

The ground (which is soft, like hardened foam) is occasionally crossed by "walking geometries"—complex, many-angled shapes that move with the clicking precision of a clock, though they are purely biological. They move in swarms, yet never touch one another, maintaining a perfect, crystalline distance as they scavenge for the fallen proto-petals.

**The Built environment**
Scattered across this white, glowing void are the remains of something that shouldn't be here. These are not ruins of a civilization, but "errors" in the world’s construction. Small, perfectly cube-shaped pillars of basalt rise from the floor, though they are unconnected to the geology around them. They look like architectural samples or test-bricks left behind by a cosmic builder. Occasionally, one finds a "staircase to nowhere"—three steps of polished glass floating in the air, disconnected from any floor or ceiling, serving as a perch for the darting fauna. 

There is no technology here—no wires, no metal, no tools. The structures are artifacts of pure form, lonely and silent.

**Sound and Motion**
The world is a kingdom of hushed activity. The only sounds are the soft *tink-tink* of the geometric scavengers and the occasional low hum of the floating water-ribbon as it ripples. It is a quiet of intense focus, like the silence of a workshop before the master arrives. 

Despite the silence, the space is hyper-active. The walls are constantly growing, microscopic layers of white matter secreting and hardening in real-time. The "reality breakdown" means that the laws of physics are being written as you watch. Gravity occasionally shifts forty-five degrees to the left for a few minutes, causing the floating water to splash against the wall, only for the "pre-rock" to absorb it instantly. 

This is a hidden room in the basement of existence—an embryonic, brightly lit laboratory where life is trying on frantic, strange new shapes before the world above decides what is permanent.
