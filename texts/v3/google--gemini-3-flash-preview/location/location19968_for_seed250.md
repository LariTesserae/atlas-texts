It is an ocean that begins miles above its own floor, suspended in the upper reaches of a thin, cold atmosphere. This is a world of **Aqueous Suspension**. Here, the "elevation" is not a measurement of land above sea level, but of the sea itself, held aloft by graviton-lattices that hum with the frequency of a thousand grinding tectonic plates.

### The Material and the Void
The density of this world is unnervingly low. Imagine a sphere the size of a gas giant, but instead of a solid core or a thick mantle, there is only a vast, hollow interior of dark air. The "land" consists of massive, floating globules of water—some the size of mountain ranges, others the volume of continents—orbiting a central point of light that no one built. Because the density is so low (0.2), there is almost nothing between these floating oceans but a cold, howling vacuum-wind. You can look across the void and see another sea, five thousand miles away, shimmering like a blue marble in the midday sun.

### The Emerging Terrain
There is no "old" rock here. The erosion is nearly zero (0.4) because the geology is currently being born. From the center of these floating water-masses, mineral "spines" are crystallizing in real-time. This is **embryonic geology**: jagged, translucent pillars of obsidian and raw silicon are extruding from the water, cooling instantly in the thin air to form sharp, untouched crags. They haven't had time to be worn down by wind or tide. They are raw, geometric, and terrifyingly sharp.

### The Atmosphere and Sound
The world is deafening (2.2). Because the scale is so vast (2.7) and the masses are in constant, turbulent motion (2.6), the sound is a permanent, low-frequency roar—the sound of ten thousand waterfalls crashing into an abyss that has no bottom. 

The light (2.2) is clear and clinical. It reflects off the curved surfaces of the drifting oceans, creating multiple horizons and blinding "sun-dogs" that dance across the void. It is a world of high-contrast shadows; the water is a deep, bruised indigo where it is thick, and a pale, foamy turquoise where it thrashes against the emerging stone.

### The Tech and the Rules
There are no cities, no ruins, and no animals. Yet, the world is saturated with "Technology" (2.7). This is not tech in the form of wires or metal; it is **encoded physics**. The graviton-lattices that keep the water from collapsing into a single, dead core are intentional. The way the minerals crystallize into specific, non-random lattices suggests an engineering of the atomic level. It is a planet-sized laboratory that has been switched on and left to run. The "rules" of this world allow for water to behave like a solid when struck at high speeds, but to flow like mist when the graviton-currents shift. 

### Ecology and Sparse Life
Vegetation (0.9) is nearly non-existent. There are no forests or grasslands. Instead, clinging to the wet, newborn obsidian crags are thin sheets of bioluminescent moss—hard, glass-like membranes that "eat" the high-altitude radiation. They don't sway; they are as brittle as the stone they grow on. They are the only green in a world of blue, black, and silver.

There is no fauna. Not even a microbe has been allowed to evolve yet, or perhaps the environment is too violent for biology to take root. The only "movement" comes from the water itself.

### The Dynamics of the Storm
The world is in a permanent state of **Hyper-Flux**. Because the water-continents are suspended in a low-density void, they are constantly colliding and merging, then tearing apart again. When two seas collide, the impact creates a "shatter-storm" of crystalline droplets that can strip the moss off a mountain in seconds. 

You are standing on a ledge of newborn silica, looking up. Above you is not a sky, but the "underbelly" of another ocean, miles thick, drifting over you like a slow, sapphire cloud. The roar of its internal currents vibrates in your bones. The air is cool—not freezing, but the kind of cold that feels like a wet sheet against the skin. 

Everything is under construction. Everything is loud. Everything is vast. It is a world awaiting its first soul, a cathedral of water and high-physics mid-prayer, empty and roaring in the light of a silent sun.
