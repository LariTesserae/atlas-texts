This is the Green Machine, an abyssal hothouse of continental proportions where the biological has achieved the precision of the mechanical.

The sky is not a sky, but a ceiling of interlocking, translucent leaves the size of city blocks, vibrating at a frequency so low it is felt rather than heard. Because the light parameter is near absolute zero, these leaves do not photosynthesize using a sun; instead, they are bioluminescent organs of heavy glass-like cellulose, glowing with a faint, sourceless infrared that provides heat without illumination. The world is a vast, warm, pitch-black humid vault.

### The Terrain of Flesh and Gear
The "ground" does not exist. The elevation is low because the entire ecosystem has collapsed into a single, dense horizon of growth. You stand upon a substrate of compressed moss that has the structural integrity of vulcanized rubber. Beneath this surface, the "geology" of the world is revealed: thick, pressurized vines as rigid as steel girders weave into complex lattices. 

There is an industrial logic to the growth here. The trees do not grow haphazardly; they grow in repeating, interlocking geometries. Trunks rise like pistons, perfectly cylindrical, polished and oily. They act as massive hydraulic pumps, moving thousands of gallons of nutrient-rich sludge upward into the canopy. The sound of this is a rhythmic, muffled thrum—the heartbeat of a factory made of wood.

### The Wetness and the Weight
The air is saturated at a constant 100% humidity. It does not rain in drops; the atmosphere simply transitions from gas to liquid at certain pressure points, creating hanging curtains of warm, gelatinous mist. Water seeps from everything. The rocks—which are not mineral, but calcified knots of ancient root-matter—weep a constant, clear sap. 

Because the erosion is "young," the features of this world are sharp, jagged, and aggressive. The thorns are like obsidian needles; the edges of the leaves are serrated and hard enough to cut glass. Nothing has been smoothed by time. It is a fresh, terrifyingly efficient engine of growth that has just reached its peak output.

### The Industrial Ecology
There are no animals. There is no birdsong, no scurrying in the undergrowth. The ecology has bypassed the need for mobile consumers. Instead, the plants have developed "industrial" organs. 

*   **Refineries:** Massive, bulbous gourds the size of hangars hang from the ceiling-vines. Deep inside them, chemical reactions take place, cracking complex sugars into volatile gasses to power the hydraulic pressure of the forest.
*   **Conveyor Belts:** Long, flat ferns move in a perpetual, slow crawl, powered by the expansion and contraction of their cellular walls, transporting fallen detritus back to the "central pits" to be re-processed.
*   **The Grid:** Networks of copper-rich fungal filaments stretch between the trees like high-voltage lines, pulsing with bio-electricity. In the dark, these lines occasionally spark—a brief, silent flash of blue that illuminates miles of terrifyingly organized green machinery before the darkness swallows it again.

### The Rules of the World
This is a realm of **Frozen Equilibrium**. Despite the industrial churning and the hydraulic pumping, nothing ever changes. The growth is perfectly balanced by the decay. A leaf falls, and another unfurls in its exact geometric position at the same microsecond. The temperature never fluctuates by a fraction of a degree. The shadows never move because the infrared glow is static, built into the very veins of the ceiling.

It is a silent, warm, and wet perpetual motion machine. It is a factory with no foreman and no product other than its own continued existence. The scale is so massive that the horizon is lost to the curve of the planet, yet every inch is packed with the same dense, oily, mechanized vegetation. 

You are in a world that is breathing, pumping, and processing, yet it is as still and silent as a tomb. There is no "nature" here in the sense of wildness; there is only a biological industry that has achieved total dominance, a green machine that has filled the universe and then stopped, satisfied with its own perfect, dark, and humid efficiency.
