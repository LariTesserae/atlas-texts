The Watcher arrived not as a light from above, for there is no "above" in the Thicket-City, only higher layers of the same frantic green. The Power arrived instead as a **Silence**.

It is a Being of profound, cooling equilibrium—a consciousness that functions like a vast, starlit frost. It felt the "Violent Vitality" of the Thicket-City like a fever. To the Power, the city was not a miracle of life, but a world-sized seizure. It saw the Möbius-strip leaves and the Phloem-Walkers not as evolved wonders, but as the panicked thrashing of a biology that had forgotten how to sleep.

The benevolent power did not seek to destroy the Thicket-City, for it loves the impulse to grow. But it recognized that the city was *suffering* from its own speed.

Here is what the Being did, following the logic of a gardener who encounters a plant strangling itself:

### 1. The Anchoring of the "Screaming Wood"
The Power descended into the Dreaming Core, the high-pressure center where the Seers of the Shrapnel-Grain hummed in agony. It did not speak; it laid a metaphorical hand upon the pulsing rinds. 

Instantly, the screeching tension of the fibers softened. The Power introduced the concept of **Lignification of the Soul**. It granted the ancient trunks the ability to store memory without pain. The "spectral branches" and reality-glitches—the hallucinations of the wood—began to crystallize. Under the influence of the Power, these glitches stopped being errors and became **Grand Archives**. The Möbius corridors began to hold steady, turning from traps into vast, quiet cathedrals of emerald glass where the history of the city was etched in the grain, frozen and safe from the frantic metabolism of the surface.

### 2. The Introduction of "The Long Breath"
The Power altered the atmosphere’s thermal rhythm. It did not cool the world to death, but it introduced a **Pulse of Rest**. 

Every fourteen hours, the Power exerts a gentle, gravitational pull that mimics a lunar tide. During this "Deep Breath," the high-metabolism slows. The hydraulic pressure of the sap-valves drops from a roar to a rhythmic thrum. The gunshot cracks of expanding bark cease. For the first time in an eon, the city experiences *Stasis*. 

In these hours of rest, the Phloem-Walkers do not twitch or prune. They stand like statues of braided jade, their chemical shouting fading into a sweet, floral incense. This intermission allows the planet's "young" erosion to age; the jagged edges of the resin-streets begin to smooth, not by wind, but by the Power’s soothing touch, turning the sharp urban sprawl into something like a polished jade ring.

### 3. The Mercy of the Graft
The Power looked upon the **Lignin-Wraiths** and the **Sap-Puppets**—the beings that had faces of temporary tissue and no souls of their own. It found their "violent vitality" lonely.

The Being performed a "Great Grafting." It reached into the shimmering emerald light and wove a new strand of "Slow Purpose" into their genetic makeup. It gave the Phloem-Walkers **Interiority**. 

They no longer move with the jerky grace of a snapping branch; they move with the deliberate pace of a monk. Their light-sensitive cilia were refined into eyes that can see beauty as well as heat. They began to decorate the cathedral-stalks not because of structural necessity, but because the Power whispered to them of *form*. They started to carve patterns into the hardened amber—prayers to the Silence that saved them from the noise.

### 4. The Well of Cool Water
In the heart of the Thicket-City, where the compost-heat was 2.5 and most oppressive, the Power drove a "Spiritual Taproot" into the center of the world. 

It created a single, massive clearing—a void in the denseness. At the center of this void, there is no wood, no vine, no screeching fiber. There is only a pool of perfectly still, clear, non-viscous water. It is 0.0 moisture, 0.0 turbulence. 

This is the **Sanctuary of the Void**. Any inhabitant—from a Resin-Hound to a Seer—can come here to experience the one thing the Thicket-City never allowed: **Nothingness**. To look into the water is to see a reflection of a self that is not part of the Green Machine.

### The Result
The Thicket-City remains vertical, vast, and vegetative. It is still a world of wood. But the "Scream" has become a "Song." 

The Power remains as a subtle, silver light filtering through the green sail-leaves, a nhắc nhở (reminder) that life does not have to be a race against its own decay. The city is no longer a "Vertical Nightmare"—it has become a **Living Sabbath**. The Green Machine still makes more of itself, but now, it stops occasionally to admire what it has made.
