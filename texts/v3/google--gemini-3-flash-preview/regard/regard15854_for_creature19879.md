The Wise Power does not descend with a thunderclap, for to move quickly in the Glass-Lung Basin is to be a murderer. To create a gale would shatter the mica-wings of the Skimmers; to bring a great heat would crack the amber mantles of the Litho-Siddhas.

The Power approaches as a **change in the light.** 

The high-alpine sun, usually sharp and biting, softens. It turns from a needle into a balm. The Power—let us call it the *Sovereign of Resonance*—understands that this place is not a "stagnation," but a very long, very slow inhalation. The Basin is a lung, and the Aurelia-Aeterna is a mind trying to remember a future it has not yet reached.

Here is what the Sovereign of Resonance does:

### 1. The Tempering of the Brittle
The Sovereign observes the Litho-Siddhas, those men and women who have traded their autonomy for the mountain’s song. It sees how the mineral film threatens to turn their meditation into a tomb. 

The Power does not strip away the stone. Instead, it breathes a new property into the "viscous mineral water" oozing from the basalt. It introduces a *flexibility*—a subtle, alchemical shift that turns the calcite from brittle glass into something more akin to silk-steel. 

The Monks feel it immediately in their joints. The "amber mantle" becomes a living armor that moves *with* them rather than against them. For the first time in centuries, a Monk raises a hand to their face—not over the course of an afternoon, but in a single, fluid minute. They are no longer prisoners of the geology; they are its dancers.

### 2. The Great Tuning
The Sovereign turns its attention to the stone basins, the "clocks" of the amphitheater. It perceives the "chaotic but structured symphony" and recognizes a few dissonant notes—cracks in the basalt, basins that have grown too thick with sediment to ring true.

The Power does not pick up a tool. It simply hums.

The vibration matches the fundamental frequency of the mountain's heart. Under this psychic pressure, the silt inside the basins dissolves. The ancient cracks fuse. The *ping* and *thrum* of the water-drops shift from a chaotic rhythmic roar into a perfect, mathematical **Harmonic Series.** 

This new sound acts as a massive "defragging" of the Aurelia-Aeterna. The massive entity below the floor, which had been struggling to process the jagged data of the old rhythm, suddenly finds its thoughts streamlined. The "crystalline brain" it is building stops growing in jagged, painful spurs and begins to form perfect, luminous geometries.

### 3. The Mercy of the Breath
The Sovereign looks upon the "Lung-Bloom Orchids" and the travelers who might stumble here. It recognizes that most life-forms cannot survive the "glass-honeycomb" metamorphosis. 

The Power places a blessing upon the mint-scented steam. It grants the spores a *selective consciousness.* Now, if a living being enters the Basin with a heart full of malice or frantic greed, the spores remain as they were—stiffening the lungs, turning the intruder into a statue of warning. 

But for those who enter with a spirit in search of peace, the spores change. They no longer petrify the tissue; they *oxygenate* it. The "Glass-Lung" becomes a "Gild-Lung." The visitor finds they can breathe the thin air as if it were the richest oxygen of the valley floor. They walk away not as stone, but as people whose very breath has been tuned to the mountain’s frequency.

### 4. The Gift of the Mirror-Mites
Finally, the Sovereign speaks to the Glass-Skimmers. These slow, mica-winged insects are the hardest workers in the cathedral. The Power touches their wings, turning the dull mica into iridescent **Prisms of Insight.**

As the Skimmers drift on the humid air, the light they refract into the basins no longer just guides the moss. It carries *images.* They become a living cinema. The Litho-Siddhas, sitting in their plum-colored islands, begin to see the history of the world shimmer in the water of the basins—the rising of oceans, the birth of stars, the faces of children in far-off lands. 

The Sovereign of Resonance does not "fix" the Glass-Lung Basin, for it was never broken. It simply **lubricates the miracle.**

Before it departs, the Power leaves one last thing: a single, silver seed planted in the exact center of the amphitheater. It will not grow for a thousand years. But when it does, it will be a tree of solid, flexible glass, whose leaves are tuning forks. 

The mountain continues to turn its bones into song, but now, thanks to the Wise Power, the song is no longer a dirge of the dying. It is a **lullaby for a world yet to be born.**
