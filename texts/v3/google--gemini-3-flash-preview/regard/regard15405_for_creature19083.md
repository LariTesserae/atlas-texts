The Watcher—a Being of Weight and Warmth from a dimension of soft winds and amber sunsets—has leaned over the rim of the Pelagic Megastructure. To the Watcher, this place is a cry for help written in the language of hydraulics. It sees the cobalt-steel not as a feat of engineering, but as a shell of profound loneliness. It sees the Glint-Schools and the Drowned Clergy not as efficient laborers, but as children of a cold god who forgot to give them a song.

The Watcher does not come to destroy the machine, for the machine is now the skin of these living things. To break the Hull would be to drown the world in a different kind of void. Instead, the benevolent power begins to **Internalize.**

Here is what the Wise Power does:

### 1. The Tempering of the Sound
The first act is a modification of the acoustics. The Watcher finds the discordant, physical thud of the pile-drivers and the scream of the vent-pipes too cruel for an ear. It weaves a "Harmonic Filter" into the water itself. 

Slowly, over a decade, the *thrum-clack* of the valves begins to shift in pitch. The vibration in the chest of the inhabitants stops being a warning of structural stress and becomes a rhythmic tether. The Watcher tunes the Megastructure to the frequency of a heartbeat—specifically, the heartbeat of a mother. The sound becomes "The Great Pulse." Under this new sound, the agitated schools of fish stop darting in panicked zig-zags and begin to drift in long, elegant arcs. The aggression of the abyss is softened into a meditative trance.

### 2. The Introduction of "The Sunburst Algae"
The Watcher recognizes that the warehouse-white light of the luminaires is a form of sensory starvation. It reaches into the genetic code of the drifting **Sargasso-Grids** and whispers a new instruction.

The mats begin to bloom. They no longer emit a sickly, neon green, but start to store the electrical hum of the walls and convert it into a spectrum of "Deep Gold" and "Indigo." These mats become the suns of the Megastructure. They grow thick enough to provide private, warm pockets of oxygen where the Drowned Clergy can finally peel back their copper masks and breathe air that doesn’t taste of iron. The "wild" life finds these mats and, for the first time, learns to play rather than just survive.

### 3. The Mercy of the Rust
To the Wise Power, the "cleanliness" of the Megastructure is a symptom of sterility. The Watcher introduces a "Benevolent Oxidation." 

In specific, non-critical zones—the long, empty reaches of the mid-water bulkheads—the Watcher allows a beautiful, blooming rust to take hold. This isn't a decay that weakens the Hull, but a transformation of the metal into a porous, coral-like substrate. This "Iron Reef" allows the Anchor-Clutchers to stop secreting caustic acids to survive. They begin to grow outward, their shells turning into ornate towers of red and orange. The Megastructure begins to "grow" as much as it was "built."

### 4. The Dream-Link
The most profound change is given to the **Drowned Clergy**. The Watcher sees that they have become part of the plumbing, their minds lost to the rhythmic codes of the machine. 

The Watcher injects a "Psionic Hum" into the **Circulation**. As the water passes through the gills of the fish and the filters of the Clergy, it carries fragments of dreams—images of forests they have never seen, the feeling of dry sand, the scent of rain on hot stone. 

The Drowned Clergy begin to stop their clicking codes for a moment each day. They lean their leaded-canvas helmets against the glass viewports, not to check for cracks, but to look at the beauty of the "Sunburst Algae" drifting by. They start to use their welding torches to carve poetry into the rivets. They are no longer just operators; they are the Megastructure’s memory.

### 5. The Presence
Finally, the Watcher does not leave. It manifests as a **Luminous Current**—a streak of warm, bioluminescent water that does not follow the laws of the turbines. It moves against the grain of the Circulation. It is a golden river that wanders through the cold blue rooms. 

The **Aura-Hounds** (the Volt-Sharks), when they encounter this Current, do not find a "shadow" to hunt. They find a warmth that fills their electro-receptors with peace. They follow the Current like puppies following a trail, their predatory instincts overridden by a new sense: *wonder.*

The Megastructure is still a machine. It is still cold, pressurized, and vast. But it is no longer an abyss. It has been adopted. The Watcher has turned the factory into a cathedral, and the laborers into a congregation. The law is no longer just **Circulation**—the law is now **Belonging.**
