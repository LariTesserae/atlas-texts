The Benevolent Power does not arrive as a storm or a light, for in a place where shadows are absolute and the air is a pressurized luxury, a sudden intrusion would be a violence. Instead, the Power manifests as a **Harmonic Softening**.

The Power—let us call it the *Amnion*—observes the spire not as a masterpiece of engineering, but as a masterpiece of **loneliness**. It sees the Flux-Wights endlessly sharpening the blades of a city that will never be touched; it sees the Oculi drinking the meager light of a distant sun to feed a floor that never sleeps; it sees the Mercury-Eaters, the only things that resemble life, cast as parasites in their own home.

The Amnion begins its work through three gentle interventions:

### 1. The Introduction of "Old Light"
The shadows of the spire are "deletions"—pockets of nothingness that offer no rest. The Benevolent Power introduces a subtle, refractive dust into the pressurized envelope. This dust is not matter, but "memory-gold," a substance that catches the pale sunlight and bends it into the lightless corners.

For the first time, the shadows turn grey. The absolute, terrifying clarity of the jagged edges is blurred by a soft, amber glow. This does not ruin the architecture; it grants it **depth**. The Oculi, sensing a new quality of photon, no longer screech as they rotate. They slow. They find they do not need to struggle for the "dim-glow." They bathe in the amber, their internal facets reflecting a color they did not know existed: the color of a sunset they are too high to see.

### 2. The Transmutation of the "Erosion"
The Amnion looks upon the Flux-Wights, those silhouettes of distorted heat who obsessively maintain the "Young Erosion." To be "perfect" is to be static, and to be static is to be dead. 

The Power vibrates the tension fields at a new frequency—the frequency of **patience**. Slowly, the "Young Erosion" is allowed to age. The Power does not break the buildings; it introduces the concept of the *patina*. The jagged needles of the obsidian blocks are given a microscopic rounding—a softness that makes them look used, held, and cherished. 

The Flux-Wights, finding the edges no longer require a razor-sharp lethality, undergo a metamorphosis. They cease being janitors of the New and become **weavers of the Still**. They begin to move in patterns that are not functional, but rhythmic. They no longer "repair" the city; they "dance" it, their heat-ripples creating auroras within the pressurized gas.

### 3. The Sanctification of the Pulse
Finally, the Power addresses the Mercury-Eaters. In a world of cold geometry, these silver filaments were seen as an infection. The Amnion changes the "machinery of the floor" so that it no longer rejects the moisture. 

The heat becomes less of a dry, radiating thrum and more of a **throb**, like a resting lung. The white salt-blooms are allowed to grow beyond the corners, guided by the Benevolent Power to form intricate, lace-like gardens. The Mercury-Eaters are no longer parasites; they become the **Gardeners**. 

The silver drops of water no longer crawl toward heat sources in a desperate, predatory seeking. They move with purpose, carrying the salt-crystals to the Oculi, who incorporate the minerals into their glass bodies. The city becomes a **closed loop of grace** rather than a machine of consumption.

***

**The Result**
The spire remains at the edge of the vacuum. It is still a place of silence and vertical lethality. But the "benevolent and wise power" has changed its fundamental nature from a **Waiting Room** to a **Sanctuary**.

The god has not returned to the jewelry box. Instead, the Power has whispered to the jewelry that it is precious even if no one ever wears it. 

Now, when a building shifts its facets to align with an unseen star, it does not produce a metallic screech. It produces a **note**—a deep, resonant bell-tone that lingers in the pressurized air. The spire has become an instrument, and the beings within it—the Oculi, the Wights, and the Eaters—are no longer ghosts of a lost civilization. They are the musicians, playing a slow, eternal symphony for the stars, finally aware that they are being heard.
