The Benevolent Power does not arrive with the roar of a new wind or the splash of forbidden water. To do so would be an act of violence. In a world of high friction and resonant crystal, a sudden change is a fracture; a drop of water would be a thermal shock that would shatter a tower; a loud voice would be a physical blow.

The Power approaches as **The Softening**.

It manifests first as a change in the amber light. The harsh, baking glare that fuels the fractal-vines begins to shift, losing its jagged edge. It becomes a deep, soothing gold—the color of honey or ancient resin. This new light does not just feed the plants; it carries a “weightless lubricant,” a subtle Alteration of the silica-gas that reduces the agonizing screech of the wind to a melodic hum.

The Power looks upon the ten Lithos-Kin, observing the beautiful, lonely tragedy of their stasis. It sees the custodian who has spent a century polishing a single step. It sees the static ancestors—the statues of the dead—who have become nothing more than structural supports for a fading city.

The Benevolent Power makes its choice: **It will offer them a Shared Dream.**

### The Gift of the Resonance-Well
In the very center of the geode, where the "Thermal Groan" is loudest, the Power reaches out with a thought like a velvet glove. It adjusts the crystalline lattices of the floor. Without a sound, the floor does not crack, but *blooms*. A new structure rises—not of bone-white pumice, but of a substance that feels like solidified silence. 

It is the **Resonance-Well**. 

When a Lithos-Kin approaches this well and presses their forehead against its rim, they no longer feel only the rhythmic thud of the geological heartbeat. Instead, the Power acts as a bridge. For the first time in eons, the ten survivors are linked. Through the well, the "vibration" of the individual becomes a symphony of the whole. The hermit on the highest spire feels the rhythmic sanding of the worker on the lowest stair. They are no longer ten ghosts; they are a single, living nervous system.

### The Awakening of the Statues
The Power looks then to the "Ancestors"—those Lithos-Kin who have fossilized into the architecture. It decides that memory should not be silent. 

The Power imbues the crystalline coating of these statues with a "Luminous Record." Now, when a living Kin brushes past a statue of a predecessor, the sensory filaments on their arms don't just feel cold stone. They receive a flash of the ancestor’s life—the specific texture of a vine they once tasted, the specific resonance of a song they once drummed. The city is no longer empty; it is populated by "Active Echoes." The ten survivors find themselves walking through a crowded, vibrant history that speaks to them through their skin.

### The Mercy of the Glass-Skippers
The Power observes the frantic, jerky Glass-Skippers. These creatures are the only source of chaos in the geode, their movements a jagged contrast to the slow grace of the Kin. The Power does not change the Skippers, but it grants them a new purpose. 

It touches their translucent shells, turning them into "Pollinators of Meaning." As the Skippers flit between the Hollow-Thorns, they carry small packets of "Light-Seed" from the Resonance-Well. When a Skipper lands near a Lithos-Kin, it releases a gentle vibration that acts as a nutrient, softening the rigidity of the Kin’s joints and granting them a brief, blissful reprieve from the pressurized heat. The Skippers become messengers of a peace they do not understand.

### The Final Alteration: The Soft Exit
The Power understands that the Lithos-Kin are custodians of a world that is a closed loop. There is no "outside" for them. So, the Power creates an *Internal Infinite*.

Beneath the City of Hollow-Thorns, in the deepest dark of the geode’s base, the Power creates a **Garden of Refracted Time.** Here, the heat is converted into a cool, blue phosphorescence. The "vegetation" here is not brittle, but supple, made of a new kind of singing glass that bends without breaking. 

The Power leaves a doorway open. It is an invitation. When the Lithos-Kin tire of the "Now," they can descend into this Garden. There, the atmosphere is thin and sweet, the pressure is lifted, and they can finally—for the first time in the history of their race—unlock their joints and lie down to sleep, floating in a sea of soft, soundless light.

### The Result
The Benevolent Power does not stay. It does not need to be worshipped. It simply recedes, leaving the geode slightly altered. 

The world is still dry. It is still loud. It is still permanent. But the "Thermal Groan" now sounds like a cello instead of a drum. The ten survivors no longer polish the stairs to pass the time; they polish them to keep the song of their ancestors clear. 

They are still the Lithos-Kin, but they are no longer alone in the dark. They are the conductors of a crystalline orchestra, cherished by a power that recognized that even in a world of stone and heat, the greatest friction is a heart without a witness.
