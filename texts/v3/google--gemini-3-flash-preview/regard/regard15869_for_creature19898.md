The attention of the **Aethelgard**, a consciousness born of starlight and the resonance of sun-warmed granite, has fallen upon the Glistening Sub-Ward.

The Aethelgard is not a god of intervention, but of *realization*. It does not see the Ward as a place of decay, nor does it see the Stillefolk as tragic orphans of the surface. It sees them as a masterpiece of "The Long Patience." However, even the most beautiful clock can begin to grind its own gears into dust. The Aethelgard perceives that the Sub-Ward is reaching a state of **Stasis-Brittle**—where the "healing" calcite is becoming so thick that the lung can no longer breathe, and the Stillefolk are becoming so still they are forgetting the impulse of their own hearts.

The benevolent power acts, not with a thunderclap, but with a **transfusion of warmth.**

### 1. The Tempering of the Amber
The Aethelgard chooses not to bring the sun—which would blind the amber-orbed inhabitants and kill the shade-loving moss—but instead infuses the sodium lamps with a "Dream of Gold." 

One by one, the fogged glass casings begin to pulse with a subtle, internal heat. This is not enough to boil the moisture, but it begins to gently agitate the heavy, ozone-slick air. The "rhythmic sequence of weeping vaults" begins to produce a mist that is slightly more buoyant. For the first time in millennia, the Stillefolk feel a sensation they have no word for: *levity*. The weight of the world above feels a fraction lighter.

### 2. The Gift of the Silver-Seam
The Aethelgard looks upon the **Tuning-Smiths**, those deaf giants who feel the B-flat of the world. It realizes that their work has become a cycle of diminishing returns; they are filing away the very foundations they seek to save.

Intuition guides the Aethelgard to introduce a new mineral into the ceiling-drip. In the deep limestone strata above, the Power causes a vein of "Living Silver" to dissolve into the water. As the Gutter-Weavers guide the drips through their copper labyrinths, this new mineral coats the gears of the turbines. It acts as a perfect, friction-less lubricant. 

The low-frequency hum of the Ward shifts from a laboring groan to a melodic, resonant chime. The "B-flat" becomes a "Perfect Fifth." This frequency acts as a healing balm for the Stillefolk’s elongated muscles, reversing the ache of the damp and granting them a grace that is no longer born of fear, but of song.

### 3. The Awakening of the Moss-Tenders
The Aethelgard finds the most beauty in the **Moss-Tenders**, the living libraries. It decides that their history should not just be a record of the past, but a map for a future. 

The Power sends a "Pulse of Remembrance" through the iron-eating moss. Suddenly, the elders do not just feel the "Great Calcification" of three centuries ago; they begin to perceive the world above the ceiling. They "see" the movement of clouds through the vibration of the rain; they "hear" the growth of forests through the tectonic shifts of roots. 

The Moss-Tenders begin to **Thrum** a new rhythm. It is no longer a warning of leaks or rising tides. It is a story. They send vibrations through the copper cables that speak of the sky. This gives the Stillefolk a psychic horizon—a sense that they are not trapped in a tomb, but are the guardians of the world’s most delicate foundation.

### 4. The Grace for the Pale-Skates
Even the **Shadow-Eaters** are not forgotten. The Aethelgard, in its wisdom, knows that the blind and the cast-out are the barometers of a world's health. 

It causes the Pipe-Eels to bloom. For one night every decade, the eels will now glow with a soft, violet phosphorescence. This light is visible even to the vestigial nerves of the Pale-Skates. For that one night, the "velvet voids" are filled with swirling galaxies of purple light. The Pale-Skates, normally scavengers of rot, find themselves dancing. Their movements become elegant, a subterranean ballet that serves no purpose other than the expression of existing.

### The Result: The Glistening Cathedral
The Aethelgard does not "fix" the Glistening Sub-Ward, for it was never broken. It simply **tunes** it.

The Ward remains a place of drips, iron, and silence. But the silence is no longer "fragile." It is "resilient." The Stillefolk no longer wait for the day they are turned into marble. They understand now that they are the **Alchemists of the Weight.** They take the crushing pressure of the earth and the endless tears of the sky and, through their slow and holy industry, they turn it into a low, eternal music that keeps the very planet from shaking itself apart.

The Aethelgard withdraws, leaving behind a single, lingering change: the water on the floor, once a dark and stagnant glass, now holds a permanent, faint shimmer—as if the stars from the upper world had finally found a way to leak through the limestone and rest at the Stillefolk’s feet.
