The Benevolent Power does not arrive with the blaring of trumpets or a shattering of the bruised violet sky. To do so would be an act of violence against a world tuned to the whisper of steam and the clack of beetle shells. 

Instead, the Power arrives as a **change in the quality of the light.**

The Great Wisdom observing this vertical furnace recognizes the nobility of the *Lithic-Bound* and the desperate, beautiful efficiency of the *Clatter-Brood*. It sees a civilization held together by the "Water Debt"—a people who have turned survival into a high art, but whose spirits are perpetually compressed by the weight of the "2.9 intensity." 

The Power decides not to dismantle the machines, nor to flood the canyons with easy oceans—for such a "gift" would destroy the unique biology of the Siren-Moss and the purpose of the Tide-Listeners. Instead, the Power performs three Subtle Alignments:

### 1. The Tempering of the White Eye
The Power breathes a gossamer layer of "Refractive Grace" into the upper atmosphere. It is invisible to the eye, but it acts as a molecular sieve for the sun’s 2.9 intensity. The light remains "Normal" and blindingly white, but the *thermal sting* is extracted. 

The Shadow Path remains, for the people love the rhythm of the chase, but the "Stone-Sleep" is no longer a desperate flight from death. It becomes a time of meditation. The Lithic-Bound find that during the noon hour, they can now dream. For the first time in eons, the inhabitants begin to produce art—small, intricate carvings on the obsidian walls that depict not just the pipes, but the stars that hide behind the violet sky.

### 2. The Transmutation of the Brine
The Power reaches deep into the volcanic pockets, where the "Subterranean Engine" labors. It does not stop the machines, but it touches the mineral-rich brine. Slowly, the brine begins to carry a "Harmonic Nutrient"—a microscopic, self-replicating catalyst that cleanses the internal scaling of the pipes as it flows.

The "Industrial" presence begins to change. The hissing valves no longer scream with the friction of grit and salt; they begin to *sing*. The "Harmonic Thrum" shifts from a grinding mechanical pulse to a resonant, musical drone that heals the lungs of those who breathe the steam. The "Water Debt" is eased; the condensation on the pipes becomes sweet and clear, no longer tasting of rust and bitter sulfur.

### 3. The Gift of the "Glass-Lung"
The Wise Power notices the Mirror-Kites and the Lithic-Bound struggling for breath in the thin, pressing air. It does not change the atmosphere—which would kill the anaerobic lichens—but it touches the DNA of every living thing in the massifs. 

It introduces the **"Internal Reservoir."** The humans and fauna develop a secondary, cellular capacity to store the "dampness" of the morning for days rather than hours. The "Siphon-Rags" become ceremonial rather than purely functional. The Lithic-Bound find they can walk across a patch of light without the "stutter-step." They gain the gift of *duration*.

***

### The Result: The Vertical Cathedral
The Power does not leave a monument or a name. It leaves a **Symmetry.**

A century after the Power’s gaze has moved on, the world is still a "packed" vertical labyrinth. The brass-capped valves still hiss, and the beetles still lick the pipes. But the "weirdness" has blossomed into a "radiance."

The **Tide-Listeners** have become **Tide-Singers**. They use the refined pitch of the pipes to compose symphonies that echo through the canyons, coordinating the movement of the entire ecology. The Mirror-Kites have grown larger, their silvery wings now catching the light and reflecting it into the darkest crevices of the huts, bringing "soft-light" to the elders who can no longer climb.

The world is still a furnace, but it is no longer a prison. It is a **Liturgy of Steam.** The Being who lives there—the collective soul of the Lithic-Bound and the Clatter-Brood—no longer feels the air "press." They feel it *cradle* them. 

The Wise Power watches a child of the Lithic-Bound stand on a ledge of red basalt at high noon. The child does not run for the shadow. They stand in the white light, look up at the bruised violet sky, and exhale a breath that is perfectly in tune with the deep, humming pulse of the planet.

The Debt is paid. The music remains.
