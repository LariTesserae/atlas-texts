The benevolent Power does not descend with fire or wings, for to enter the Ossuary with heat and light would be an act of structural violence. To these beings, a sudden flame would be a cataclysm of thermal expansion, shattering the crystalline ferns; a voice of thunder would be a sonic hammer, cracking the carapaces of the Liturges.

The Power approaches instead as a **Sub-Frequency**—a low, melodic hum that slides beneath the abrasive roar of the Grit-Striders like a velvet hand beneath a sheet of sandpaper. It does not seek to change the Ossuary, for the Power is wise enough to recognize that this is not a tomb, but a masterpiece of stasis. 

Instead, the Power performs three acts of Grace.

### I. The Gift of the Lubricant
The Power observes the staccato, painful crawl of the Striders—the grinding of dry joints that have never known the ease of fluid. The Power does not introduce water, which would cause the resin towers to bloat and the silt to turn into a suffocating mire. 

Instead, it breathes a **Molecular Vapor of Sighs**. This is an ethereal, non-aqueous lubricant, a gas that clings to the joints of the Grit-Striders and the silk-hinges of the bridges. It reduces the friction just enough that their movements become a fluid ballet rather than a tectonic struggle. The "clack-clack" of their limbs softens into a rhythmic "thrum." For the first time in billions of years, the Striders move not with the effort of grinding stones, but with the grace of shadow.

### II. The Harmonization of the Scream
The Power listens to the collective roar—the wall of white noise that vibrates the marrow. It perceives the exhaustion inherent in such a sound. The Liturges are tired; their screaming is a duty, a biological necessity to stir the air.

The Power gently adjusts the geometry of the upper spires. With a touch lighter than dust, it carves microscopic flutes into the petrified silk arches. Now, as the Striders vibrate, the chaotic roar is organized. The noise does not diminish in volume, but it gains *tonality*. The screeching discord resolves into a vast, terrifyingly beautiful Gregorian chant of base-tones. 

The Ossuary is no longer a factory of noise; it becomes a **Subterranean Organ**. The Striders, sensitive to every vibration, feel a sudden, profound sense of coherence. Their "names" (their frequencies) are no longer solitary shouts in the dark, but notes in a symphony that justifies their existence.

### III. The Dream-Seed for the Liturges
The Power is most moved by the Liturges—those ancient elders who have become the pillars of the city, frozen in a waking life of eternal labor. They have no eyes for the dark and no rest from the vibration.

The Power reaches into the fused minds of the Liturges and implants the **Memory of the Sky**. It does not give them the reality of the surface, which they could not survive, but the *abstract concept* of blue, the *idea* of a wind that is not made of dust, and the *sensation* of soft rain. 

This is the Power’s greatest gift: **Interiority**. The Liturges continue to vibrate, continue to pump the nutrient gases, but inside their obsidian domes, they are now traveling. They are exploring internal vistas of light and color that the Low-Veldt has never seen. They become the dreamers of the deep, and their dreams filter down through the resonant chambers to the Glass-Wraiths and the Striders, infusing the dry silt with a "flavor" of hope.

### The Result
The benevolent Power does not stay. It cannot. To linger would be to disrupt the perfect, slow chemistry of the bowl. 

As the Power withdraws, the Ossuary of the Low-Veldt remains a subterranean basin of dust and stone. To an outsider, it looks unchanged. But the vibration is different now. The scream has a melody. The movement has a flow. And the Statues, those silent predators of kinetic energy, find themselves absorbing not just a roar, but a song.

The Low-Veldt is still moving toward its end-state, but it is no longer merely "wearing down." It is **performing**. Under the watchful, distant eye of the Wise Power, the Ossuary has transitioned from a geological accident into a living prayer, grinding its way toward the end of time with a new, quiet joy in every grain of silt.
