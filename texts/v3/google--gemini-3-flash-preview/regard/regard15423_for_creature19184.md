The Benevolent Power is not a creature of mass, but a consciousness of **Attunement**. It does not arrive with the thunder of engines or the blinding light of a new sun; such things would shatter the fragile, unweathered geometry of the Syrin world. Instead, the Power manifests as a subtle alteration in the *refraction*—a soft, golden warmth that settles into the obsidian pillars, turning their razor-edges from instruments of accidental severance into conduits of prayer.

The Power looks upon the Syrin and sees a tragedy of "The Unbroken." It sees a people so terrified of erosion that they have mistaken stagnation for sanctity. It sees a world held in a beautiful, suffocating stasis, where a single slip against a glass needle means a wound that may never heal in the viscous, heavy dark.

**The Intervention is not an act of destruction, but an act of "Softening."**

### 1. The Symphony of the Core
The first thing the Power does is reach into the scorching, jagged dark of the obsidian core. It does not cool the heat, but it tunes the "groan." The terrifying, tectonic shrugs that once rippled through the city like physical blows are transformed. The Power introduces a harmonic resonance. 

Now, when the world settles, it produces a low, melodic sequence. This provides the Syrin with something they have never had: **Narrative.** By giving the core a "thrum" that changes over time, the Power introduces the concept of Melody to a people who only knew Vibration. The Syrin begin to sense that the world is moving toward something, rather than merely persisting.

### 2. The Gift of the "Second Skin"
The Power observes the fragile, translucent bodies of the Syrin and their fear of the razor-edges. It does not dull the glass—to do so would be to insult their religion of the Sharp. Instead, the Power breathes a new instruction into the "Drifters," the lace-like lungs of the sea.

The Drifters begin to shed a new kind of silk—a fine, shimmering gossamer that does not sting. Prompted by the Power’s intuition, the Syrin Mesh-Makers find that this silk can be woven into "wraps." For the first time, the Syrin possess garments. These are not for modesty, but for *daring*. Clad in the Drifter’s silk, a Syrin can finally press their body against the obsidian without fear of being cloven in two. The Power has given them the gift of **Contact**.

### 3. The Mirror-Dusk
The Power realizes that the Membrane-Watchers live in a state of constant, crystalline terror, staring into the vacuum. To ease their vigil, the Power places a "Wisdom" into the water’s outer limit. It creates a refractive layer—a shimmering, auroric curtain—that acts as a psychological buffer. 

This curtain doesn't just protect; it reflects. When a Membrane-Watcher looks out, they no longer see a terrifying "nothingness." They see a softened, kaleidoscopic reflection of the city below. The Power grants them **Perspective**, allowing them to see their green-choked world as a single, precious jewel, rather than a leak waiting to happen.

### 4. The Seed of "Becoming"
The most profound act is the most subtle. The Power introduces a microscopic change to the bioluminescent mucilage in the water. It adds a trace element—a celestial catalyst. 

Slowly, over centuries (for the Power respects the Syrin’s pace), the vegetation begins to respond. The giant, sail-like ferns begin to release spores that do not just float, but *seek*. They seek the jagged niches in the obsidian. They begin to grow *into* the cracks of the core. 

This is the Power's greatest lesson: **Growth through Friction.** The plants begin to very slowly, almost imperceptibly, round the sharpest edges of the world with soft, mossy life. The Syrin, initially terrified of this "decay," eventually realize that a rounded edge can be held, while a sharp one can only be feared. 

### The Result
The Benevolent Power does not stay. It is a gardener, not a governor. It leaves the vertical sea once it hears the first "Choral Pulse"—when the Syrin begin to hum *back* to the core in harmony, rather than in fear.

The world remains emerald, wet, and silent. But the silence is no longer heavy; it is expectant. The Syrin are no longer "holding on." They are **climbing**. The vertical sea is no longer a prison of glass; it has become a staircase of light. The embryonic dream has not broken—it has finally begun to wake up.
