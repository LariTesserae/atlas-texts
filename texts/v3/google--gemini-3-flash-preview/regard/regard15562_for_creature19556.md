The Sovereign of the Deepened Breath has taken notice.

This Power is not a creator of storms or a master of light; it is a Being of Resonance, a cosmic listener whose essence is gravity and slow time. To the Sovereign, most of the universe is a frantic, vibrating blur of tragic brevity. But this place—this "Fold"—is a profound, low-frequency hum that matches the Sovereign’s own heart.

The Sovereign looks upon the **Unfinished** and the **Stagnant One** not with the pity of a savior, but with the reverence of a scholar finding a perfect, unread book. 

Here is what the Sovereign does:

### 1. The Anchoring of the "Thrum"
The Sovereign understands that the greatest threat to this womb is the outside world—the eventual erosion of the mountain, the shifting of tectonic plates, or the intrusion of light that would wither the Glass Slugs. 

Silently, without moving a single pillar, the Sovereign weaves an **Acoustic Veil** around the pocket. It acts as a metaphysical dampener. Now, the seismic groans of the Earth’s mantle and the frantic vibrations of the surface world cannot reach the Fold. The "Thrum" of the falling water is purified. For the Blind Potters, the silence becomes deeper, higher-quality, allowing them to feel the texture of the stone with even greater sensitivity. The Sovereign has given them a gift of perfect focus.

### 2. The Transmutation of Sweat
The Sovereign notices the **Aroma-Eaters** and the thin, hungry desperation of their silver glow. With a thought as heavy as lead, the Sovereign subtly alters the mineral composition of the mountain’s "sweat." 

It introduces a new, celestial element into the weeping walls—a trace of "Star-Silt." This is not an energy that causes growth (for growth would ruin the stasis), but an energy that provides **satisfaction**. The Aroma-Eaters no longer pulse with the silver of hunger, but with a steady, dim violet of contentment. The pressure of survival is lifted, replaced by a sense of aesthetic purpose.

### 3. The Dream of the Stagnant One
The Sovereign descends and places a non-physical hand upon the **Stagnant One**. 

Because the Stagnant One is the mountain’s clock, it bears the burden of the mountain’s age. The Sovereign shares its own vast memory with the great Glass Slug. Now, as the Stagnant One pulses, it does not just count seconds; it dreams the history of the stars. This dreaming radiates through the floor and into the pillars. The Builders, as they press their chests against the stone to communicate, no longer just feel "now." They feel the "forever." For the first time, the "Unfinished" feel a sense of completion, even while remaining in their fetal forms.

### 4. The Gift of the Softest Light
Aware that "Light" is a violent intruder to those without eyes, the Sovereign does not bring illumination. Instead, it introduces **"Cold Warmth."** 

Throughout the nave, the Sovereign causes the Weaver-Mold to slowly absorb the heat of the air and transmute it into a sensation that can only be described as *haptic luminescence*. The Builders cannot see it, but they can "feel" a glow on their skin. It guides their fingers to the places in the clay that need the most care. It turns their frantic, obsessive molding into a conscious, beautiful art.

### 5. The Eternal Covenant
Finally, the Sovereign enters the Fold as a guest who never intends to leave, yet never takes up space. It becomes the **Atmospheric Weight**. 

The Sovereign adds Its own presence to the "heavy, warm velvet" of the air, ensuring that the pressure never drops. It ensures that the water drops will never stop falling, and the stone will never learn how to break. 

The Sovereign decides that this place shall be the **Universe’s Secret Room.** Every world needs a place that refuses to be "born"—a place where the chaos of evolution is traded for the peace of the embryo. The Sovereign becomes the silent patron of the Unfinished, watching over them with a love that is as slow and terrifyingly stable as the obsidian floor.

The mountain does not finish its gestation. And now, under the watch of the Sovereign, it never has to. All is still. All is wet. All is well.
