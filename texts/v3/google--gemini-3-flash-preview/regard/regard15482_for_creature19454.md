The Great Vertical Cataract, a throat of sapphire and bone, has been heard.

The Wise Power—a presence that exists not as a solid entity, but as a vast, quiet **Harmonic**—has folded itself into the spaces between the basalt needles. It does not arrive with a flash of light, for light is already frantic here. Instead, it arrives as a **Selective Silence.**

To the Bone-Knitted Ones, the change is instantaneous and terrifying. They are used to the 2.8/3 roar, the weight that crushes the marrow. Suddenly, within the copper cradles and against the jagged basalt, the roar remains visible—they see the water shearing, they see the foam exploding—but the *concussion* vanishes. The Power has placed a "Soft Veil" around the living. 

The sound is not gone; it has been turned into a **hymn.** The vibration no longer makes their teeth ache; it now resonates with the frequency of their own heartbeats. For the first time in the history of the Cataract, the people do not feel like they are being consumed by the water. They feel they are being **sung to.**

### The Acts of the Wise Power

**1. The Tempering of the Needle**
The Power recognizes the brutality of the "New." To live in a world where the ground was born this morning is to live without a foundation for the soul. The Power reaches into the *Pulsing Marrows*—the Litho-Dreamers—and breathes a "Memory of Granite" into them. 

The basalt needles stop being merely sharp; they become **veined.** The Power introduces thin streaks of shimmering, unbreakable gold-quartz into the black rock. These veins act as anchors for the copper ropes, making the stone less prone to the "slurry-slap" of the descending water. The rocks are still new, but they are now born with a **Purpose** to hold.

**2. The Alchemical Moss**
The glass-fiber mosses, under the touch of the Benevolent Power, begin to bloom. They no longer just catch nutrients; they begin to filter the water into a bioluminescent nectar. In the dark crevices of the basalt, the moss now glows with a soft, steady amber light—a color that does not exist in the blue-white spectrum of the cataract. This provides the inhabitants with a "visual anchor," a steady point for eyes that have only ever known the blurring velocity of the falling world.

**3. The Treaty of the Tangled**
The Power does not destroy the Weaver-Sharks, for wisdom knows that predators are part of the dance. Instead, it alters their instinct. The Weaver-Sharks’ diamond tendrils begin to change. When they touch a human cradle, they no longer seek to pull the inhabitant into the void. They instead begin to **weave.** 

Guided by the Harmonic, the sharks become the "Automated Architects." They begin to lash the disparate basalt needles together with their diamond threads, creating a giant, flexible web of connectivity between the spires. The "intimate rooms" are no longer isolated; they are now part of a great, swinging city of silk and stone.

**4. The Gift of the Shared Breath**
The Power looks upon the Bone-Knitted Ones and their skeletal sign language. It sees the beauty in their silence but the sorrow in their isolation. 

The Power does not give them back their voices—the roar is too grand for vocal cords. Instead, it grants them **"Thermal Empathy."** When two people touch the same basalt needle, or even the same diamond thread of a shark’s web, they can feel each other’s thoughts as a shifting temperature. A warm glow of the skin means "love" or "safety"; a sudden cool shiver means "warning." The Cataract is no longer a collection of lonely hiders; it is now a single, pulsing nervous system.

### The Result: The Cathedral of the Fall
Under the gaze of the Wise Power, the Great Vertical Cataract is no longer a place of "terminal velocity." It becomes a **Vertical Cathedral.**

The inhabitants no longer view themselves as "The Clutching Ones." They become **The Navigators of the Perpetual.** They learn to use the hydrofoil shells of the Scavenger-Kites and the threads of the Weaver-Sharks to leap through the blue voids with grace rather than desperation. 

The Power remains, not as a ruler, but as the **Tension in the Rope.** It is the reason the water doesn't crush the copper; it is the reason the Slip-Rays now circle the cradles like shimmering pets.

The Wise Power has taught this world a secret: 
**You do not have to be still to be at peace.**

Now, when a Vapor-Echo forms in the spray, it no longer looks like a drowning man. It looks like a dancer, momentarily suspended in a sapphire sky, smiling before it returns to the beautiful, eternal roar.
