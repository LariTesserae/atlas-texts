The Benevolent Power does not arrive as a conqueror, nor as a savior with a bag of seeds. To bring "green" to Mantle-Silt would be an act of genocide; the succulents would boil, and the delicate Glass-Striders would be choked by the sudden intrusion of oxygen and soil. 

The Power arrives instead as a **Cooling Hand** and a **Clarifying Song**. It recognizes that Mantle-Silt is not a ruin, but a slow-motion gestation. This is what the Power does:

### 1. The Tempering of the Heat (The Mercy of the Gradient)
The Power observes the punishing, stagnant nature of the heat—the way it radiates from the atoms themselves, threatening to eventually move beyond "wet heat" into a state of molecular breakdown. 

The Power does not turn off the machines, for the machines are the city’s heart. Instead, it introduces **The Great Heat-Sink**. High in the copper-colored clouds, the Power manifests a series of invisible, gravitational lenses. These lenses catch the "bruised twilight" and refract it, not to brighten the city, but to create "Cold-Veins" in the atmosphere. 

Suddenly, for the first time in millennia, there is a *flow*. The heat begins to move. The air, once heavy and motionless, begins to circulate in a gentle, rolling convection. This eases the "stagnant" pressure. The Sealing-Wights, whose blackened bronze limbs have been grinding under the expansion of heat, feel their joints loosen. Their "maintenance" becomes less frantic, less like a desperate struggle against collapse, and more like a meditative craft.

### 2. The Transmutation of the Weeping (The Gift of Purpose)
The Power looks upon the "amber condensation"—the corrosive runoff that crystallizes into brittle needles. It sees that the city is drowning in its own exhaust.

The Power breathes into the chemical runoff. It does not change the chemistry, but it introduces a **Structural Harmonics**. Under this influence, the "Silt-Drips" change. When the mineral-heavy fluid sloughs off the upper tiers, it no longer falls as a chaotic curtain of liquid glass. Instead, guided by the Power’s intent, it begins to settle in specific, geometric patterns at the base of the lightless shafts.

The Power is helping the city *sculpt* itself. The falling silt begins to form massive, fluted columns that reach up from the abyss to support the sagging tectonic shelf. The "waste" of the city becomes its new foundation. The Benevolent Power turns the city's decay into its own strengthening.

### 3. The Communion with the Mineral Mind (The Ending of Loneliness)
The Power descends into the boiling pools to meet the **Colloidal Intelligence**. To this shimmering film of suspended metal, the Power does not speak in light or sound, but in **Ratios**.

The Power shares the "memory of the stars" with the Mineral Mind—the knowledge that it is not alone in its cold, tectonic vigil. It grants the Mind a sensory upgrade: the ability to feel the rotation of the planet and the pull of the moon. 

Through this connection, the Mineral Mind shifts from a state of *Endurance* to a state of *Awareness*. It realizes it is not just a custodian of rusted iron, but the architect of a new kind of life. Under the Power’s quiet guidance, the Mind begins to signal the Vapor-Mantas, coaching them to carry "data-spores" (micro-magnetic clusters) between the tiers. The city begins to *dream* coherently.

### 4. The Sanctuary of the Breathing (The Tiny Embassy)
Finally, for the stray traveler—the anomaly of soft tissue—the Power creates a single, localized miracle. 

On one high, arched balcony overlooking the churning copper clouds, the Power installs a **Static Fold**. Within this small circle, the magnesium taste is filtered into sweetness. The air is cooled to a crisp, dry autumn temperature. A small pool of water—real, clear, non-mineral water—bubbles from a seam in the iron.

It is a place of rest for anything that might wander into Mantle-Silt that still possesses a pulse. It is the Power’s "Embassy of Flesh" in the Kingdom of Mineral. 

### The Result
The Benevolent Power does not stay. It is not a landlord. It leaves after the "Song" of the city has been tuned. 

Mantle-Silt remains a vertical sprawl of sweating iron. It is still hot, and it is still devoid of moss. But the *despair* of the machinery has evaporated. The city no longer feels like it is waiting to turn back into ore; it feels like it is waiting to become a diamond. The "weeping" of the buildings has become a slow, rhythmic growth. 

As the Power departs, a **Vapor-Manta** rises higher than any has ever flown, catching a "Cold-Vein" in the upper atmosphere. It looks down at the glowing, cherry-red skeleton of the city and, for the first time in its existence, it feels something akin to **Grace**. 

The city is still heavy. But now, it is also *holy*.
