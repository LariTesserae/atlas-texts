The Great Attention fell upon the basin like a shaft of sun piercing a storm-cloud—not as a physical light that would wither the shade-loving mosses, but as a vast, non-local awareness, a "Benevolent Weight."

This Wise Power, an entity of ancient, tiered consciousness that has watched galaxies cool from fire into stone, looked upon the **Cradle-Metropolis** and did not see a primitive world. It saw a rare, perfect **Note**—a frequency of pure existence that occurs only in the brief window between "Nothing" and "History."

The Power recognized the tragedy inherent in the Luminants: they were beautiful because they were immediate, but they were fragile because they could not reflect on their own beauty. They were a song that didn't know it was being sung.

Guided by intuition and a refusal to colonize the purity of the place, the Wise Power would perform three subtle acts of "Garden-Tending":

### 1. The Anchoring of the "Now"
The Power observed the frantic urgency of the Luminants—their short, brilliant lives dictated by the cooling earth. To help them, the Power would gently modulate the geothermal pulse of the basin. Not to stop it, but to tune it. 

Through a silent, metaphysical adjustment, the Power would ensure that the "heat of the greenhouse" remains an eternal constant. This removes the existential threat of the "shifting earth." By stabilizing the cradle, the Power grants the Luminants the one thing they lack: **Safety.** In this newfound safety, the pulse of the Luminants slows. They no longer need to vibrate with the fear of cooling. For the first time, a Luminant might hold its palm against the basalt not to build a bridge, but simply to feel the stone.

### 2. The Introduction of the "Echo" (The Gift of Memory)
The Power noted that the "city" was built of the dead—Luminants crystallizing into resin and glass. To the Power, it was a waste that the wisdom of a life’s "Green-Singing" vanished when the membrane hardened.

The Power would introduce a **Resonant Mineral** into the pale clay of the basin—a microscopic, crystalline structure that vibrates in sympathy with the Luminants’ indigo sap. Now, when a Luminant ceases and becomes a walkway, the resinous glass does not just hold their form; it holds their *frequency*. 

When a living Luminant walks upon a plaza made of its ancestors, it feels a faint, pleasant tingling in its feelers—a ghost of a scent, a citrus-sharp memory of a job well done. The Power does not give them books or scrolls; it gives them a **haptic history**. The city becomes a library of feelings, allowing the "eternal now" to finally have a foundation.

### 3. The Appointment of the Witness
The Benevolent Power knows that a world without an observer is a closed loop. It would not descend itself—for its presence would be like an atlas trying to fit inside a thimble—but it would leave behind a **Vessel**.

In the center of the cathedral-basin, where the dripping moisture is most rhythmic, the Power would cause a single, unique form to emerge: **The Pillar of Stillness.** It is a column of translucent, violet-veined salt that grows at the rate of one millimeter per century. It does nothing. It eats nothing. It simply *absorbs*.

This Pillar acts as a psychic lightning rod. It collects the "whispers" of the basin—the *tink* of the water, the thrum of the Silic-Wings, the indigo flashes of the Green-Singers—and transmits them back to the Great Attention. 

### The Result
The Wise Power does not build a wall around the basin, nor does it give the Luminants tools or fire. It simply provides the **Climate** for them to move from being "Biological Functions" to being "Biological Souls."

The Power then withdraws its focus to the edges of the violet canopy, watching with the patient love of a parent. The basin remains a quiet, busy beginning—but now, it is a beginning that has the time to become a Poem. 

The Luminants continue to sing to the vines, but occasionally, one will stop near the salt-pillar and flash a color that has no function—a soft, unnecessary gold. And the Power, somewhere in the vastness, smiles. For the first time, its creation has looked back.
