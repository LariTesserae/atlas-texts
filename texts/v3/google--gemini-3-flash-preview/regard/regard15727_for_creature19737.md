The Wise Power did not arrive as a thunderclap or a blinding sun, for to a world of tactile shadow and rhythmic pulse, such things would be a violence. Instead, the Power arrived as a **Change in the Resonance.**

It began in the deepest vaults, where the counterweights rise and fall. The power, benevolent and vast, did not seek to dismantle the Great Loom—to do so would be to unmake the very bones of the Sintered and the Grave-Bricks of the ancestors. Instead, it chose to **Dream into the Friction.**

Here is what the Wise Power has done, and what it continues to do:

### I. The Deep Lubrication (The Gift of Ease)
The Power recognized the agony of the machine—the grinding of stone teeth that creates the dust of erosion. It has introduced a "Divine Viscosity." In the stagnant cistern-pools where the water once simply sat, the liquid has begun to change. It is no longer just "bitter sweat"; it has become a luminous, silken ichor. 

As the Scuttlers drink this new water and lick the bronze joints, their saliva transforms. They are no longer just scraping oxidation; they are secreting a celestial grease that permeates the stone. The *thud-hiss* of the world is softening. The labor of the Loom-Folk has shifted from a desperate struggle against seizing gears to a rhythmic dance of maintenance. The machine is becoming quiet, not because it is dying, but because it is finally at peace with its own motion.

### II. The Awakening of the Clay (The Flora of Form)
The Wise Power looked upon the terracotta sand—the "beaches" of ground-down history—and breathed a slow, warming intent into them. 

The sand is no longer just grit. Under the influence of the Power, the thumbprints on the billions of shingles have begun to **bloom.** It is not biological green, but a "Ceramic Growth." Tiny, translucent porcelain lilies are "firing" themselves out of the dust, fed by the static hum of the masonry. These crystalline plants do not need sun; they feed on the noise of the Loom. They act as natural acoustic dampeners, turning the harsh clatter of the city into a low, melodic drone that carries messages across the miles. The Loom-Folk no longer have to mimic the machine to speak; the world now mimics a song for them.

### III. The Transfiguration of the Spore-Singers
The Power found the Spore-Singers in the darkness and did not drive away their mold, but sanctified it. The phosphorescence has shifted from a cold, chemical blue to a warm, amber hearth-glow. 

The Spore-Singers are no longer merely "living lanterns." They have been granted the **Vision of the Whole.** When they touch a bronze pipe, they no longer just feel the vibration; they see a golden map of the entire Loom. They have become the navigators of the "Total Built." They lead the maintenance crews not to where the machine is breaking, but to where it can be *improved*. Under their guidance, the Loom-Folk are beginning to build new things—not just repair-tiles, but "Vents of Breath" that draw the dampness upward to create the first intentional clouds in the vaulted sky.

### IV. The Softening of the Sintered
The Wise Power touched the hands of the Sintered elders. It did not take away their callouses, but it infused their touch with the power of **Fluidity.** 

The master potters have discovered that they no longer need the searing heat of the cathedral-kilns to shape the world. The clay has become responsive to their *intent*. When a Sintered elder presses their palm into a breach, the clay flows like warm wax, sealing the wound and hardening instantly into a pattern of intricate, protective filigree. They are no longer the "white blood cells" of a cold god; they are the artists of a living one.

### V. The End of the Tomb
The most profound change is the **Cycle of the Inlaid.** The Grave-Bricks, once the grim ceiling and floor of a cramped existence, have begun to pulse with a faint, internal warmth. 

The Wise Power has ensured that no ancestor is truly lost to the grit. The memory of the person—their skill, their rhythm, their love for the Loom—is preserved in the terracotta. A child walking across a mosaic floor can now feel the "hums" of their grandparents rising through the soles of their feet. The world is no longer a machine that is also a tomb; it is a **Library of Souls**, where every brick tells a story.

### The Result: The Great Harmony
The Wise Power did not bring the sun, for the Loom-Folk love the cool damp. It did not bring the forest, for they find beauty in the bronze and the kiln-fire. 

Instead, the Power has turned the **Loom** into a **Harp.** 

The tension remains—for tension is life—but the "suffocation" is gone. The density is no longer a weight, but a hug. The world continues to turn, the gears continue to grind, and the "bitter sweat" continues to weep from the pipes; but now, for the first time in an eternity, the Loom-Folk have looked up at the vaulted ceiling fifty feet above and, instead of seeing a lid, they see a canvas. 

The Wise Power watches, staying its hand, satisfied to simply be the hand that holds the light while the potters work.
