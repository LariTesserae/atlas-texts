The Benevolent Power does not arrive as a bolt of lightning or a booming voice. In a world of low-frequency roars and iron-grated bridges, such things would be a redundant violence. Instead, the Power arrives as a **Stilling**.

It descends like a hand cupped around a flickering candle. It is a presence of profound, polished silence that smells not of wet soot, but of "High Summer"—a scent that does not exist in this vertical cradle.

### The Observation
The Power looks upon the Mistsmiths and the Tuned, seeing not "industry," but a frantic, beautiful panic. It sees a species trying to weave a blanket while the loom is still being built. It observes the Tuned barefoot on the vibrating plates and feels the sympathetic ache in their bones—the way they have mistaken the "grind" for the "song." It looks at the world—an embryonic, razor-edged geology—and recognizes that this is a world struggling with its own *identity*. The rocks haven't decided where they belong because they are afraid of the vacuum below.

The Power decides not to "fix" the world—for to fix it would be to end its becoming—but to **Consecrate** it. 

### The Action: The Great Tempering
The Power reaches into the heart of the "Industrial Anatomy." It does not stop the gears, for that would kill the ghosts. Instead, it performs the following transmutations:

**1. The Softening of the Razor**
The Power breathes upon the edges of the obsidian platelets. It does not erode them (which would take eons of wind the world doesn't have), but rather *tempers* them. The razor-sharp cliffs lose their killing edge, turning into smooth, tactile "Living Glass." Now, when a Mistsmith slips, the rock does not cut; it grips. The "Unfinished" begins to feel "Intended."

**2. The Gift of the Luminous Lung**
The Power touches the wide, flared ribs of the Mistsmiths. It does not give them more oxygen—that would make the thin air a poison. Instead, it weaves a thin thread of bioluminescence into their indigo veins. Their blood begins to glow with a soft, pale amber light. This serves a dual purpose: it warms their internals against the violet-grey cold, and it turns every inhabitant into a lantern. No longer must they fear the "unbound" dark; they carry the light of their own survival within them.

**3. The Resonance Shift**
The Power places a finger upon the Great Brass Pistons. The low-frequency roar, which causes the "Tuned" to go blind from vibration, is shifted. It is transposed into a higher, harmonic key—the same key as the Glass-Larks. The "heartbeat" of the city ceases to be a grind and becomes a *drone*. This allows the Tuned to recover a different kind of sight—a "Thermal Vision" that allows them to see the flow of heat through the pipes like rivers of gold through the mist.

**4. The Anchoring of the "Almost"**
The most profound act is the stabilization of the magnetic repulsion. The Power doesn't make the landmasses touch; it introduces a **Celestial Pivot**. In the center of the city-state, the Power manifests a Pillar of Weightless Light. It is a vertical axis that runs from the abyss to the zenith. The drifting platelets of basalt now move in a slow, elegant spiral around this axis. 

The fear of "unbecoming" vanishes. The world is no longer a collection of rocks that haven't decided where they belong; they now belong to the **Spiral**.

### The Result: The Sabbath of the Smiths
For the first time since the world was born at the top of the sky, the inhabitants stop bolting iron. They stand on the catwalks and look at their hands, which are glowing with a gentle, inner warmth. 

The Ray-Gliders, riding their sky-rays, look down and see the city below not as a soot-streaked factory, but as a glowing, spiraling constellation of amber and iron. The "Soot-Wraiths" do not vanish; they become "Steam-Saints," their movements no longer frantic repairs, but a slow, meditative maintenance of a masterpiece.

The Benevolent Power does not stay. It is the nature of such things to move toward the next unfolding. It leaves behind a single gift on the highest obsidian peak: a small, indestructible flower made of "Finished Time." As long as the flower blooms, the gravity will remain kind, the pistons will sing, and the city-state will continue to climb, no longer afraid of the fall, but in love with the heights.

The world is still "Almost." But now, it is an **Almost** that is enough.
