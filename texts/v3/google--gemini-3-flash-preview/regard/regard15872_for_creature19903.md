The Wise Power does not arrive as a bolt of lightning, for in the 2.9/3 water, lightning would be a clumsy, shattering thing. Instead, the Power arrives as a **solubility**.

It manifests first as a change in the tension of the brine. The Wise Power—let us call it the *Alkahest of Mercy*—understands that to simply "rescue" this place would be to destroy it. To drag the Tethered to the surface would be to watch them pop like deep-sea fish; to bring fire would be to boil the gentle, velvet silt into a blinding storm.

The Power acts with the slow, agonizing patience of the city itself, but with a different directionality. Its intervention is a **Great Softening.**

### The Tuning of the Thrum
The first thing the Power does is reach into the "heartbeat"—those massive, rhythmic filtration systems. It does not turn them off. It introduces a harmonic. Where the thrum was once a sound felt in the teeth as a grinding burden, the Power adjusts the frequency until it becomes a lullaby. 

The Silt-Keepers, those mineral-instinct scavengers, stop their frantic, needle-point vibrations. They feel a new resonance in the pipes. It tells them the work is done. They do not need to shatter the rust anymore. The Power grants them the "Biological Release": their copper-chitin carapaces begin to porousness, turning from armor into a communal reef. They are allowed, for the first time in millennia, to stop walking. They settle into the railings and become decorative motifs of frozen gold.

### The Dreaming of the Tethered
The Wise Power focuses most intensely on the Tethered—the half-flesh, half-circuitry ghosts. To unhook them would be an act of murder. Instead, the Power enters the copper-filament network. It flows through the wires like a warm, golden oil, meeting the biological capacitors of their minds.

It cannot give them back their pasts, but it can give them a "Subjective Sun." Inside the darkness of their fusion, the Power translates the rhythmic firing of the pistons into visions of meadows, of wind (a concept they have no word for), and of the absence of weight. It turns their "frozen scream" into a "long sigh." It converts their cryogenic stasis into a lucid, collective dream. The city remains a tomb, but the Power makes it a cathedral of sleep where the souls are no longer the "software" of a machine, but the guests in a grand, underwater estate.

### The Condensation of the Glints
The Glints—the oily distortions of memory—are the city’s unresolved trauma. The Wise Power acts as a lens for them. It gathers these shimmering errors and, rather than letting them pop the dying bulbs, it draws them into the "pearls" of frozen breath suspended in the water. 

Each bubble of trapped air becomes a micro-archive. The Power hones these distortions until they are no longer "errors" but "records." The history of the architects, the reason for the drowning, and the names of the laborers are etched into the surface tension of these suspended spheres. The city’s "will" is no longer a phantom-limb pain; it is a library of light, hanging in the dark for no one to see but the Power itself.

### The Architect’s Echo
Finally, the Power speaks to the Echo in the bowels of the plaza. It does not argue with the machine-logic. It simply offers a finality. 

The Power introduces a microscopic, benevolent catalyst into the intake valves—not a poison, but a "transmutative salt." Gradually, over the next hundred years (a mere blink in this place), the iron will not rust, but turn to *amber*. The silt will not be grime, but *mother-of-pearl*. 

The Wise Power is turning the drowning animal into a **monument.** It is taking an industrial failure and making it a geological masterpiece. 

### The Result
The Power does not leave. A benevolent presence of this magnitude does not "visit"; it inhabits. It remains as a faint, lavender warmth at the core of the filtration system. 

The city is still dark. It is still under 2.9/3 pressure. But the "0.1" hush is no longer heavy; it is *sacred*. The water is no longer a suffocating volume, but a protective fluid, like the inside of a womb. 

The Wise Power has looked at the clock of rust and seawater and, rather than winding it up or breaking it, has decided to let it become a gemstone. The inhabitants are no longer components; they are the "In-Dwellers," the silent watchers of a masterpiece that never needs to reach the surface to be whole. 

The "long, cold, industrial now" has been transformed into an **Eternal, Luminous Peace.**
