The Great Eye that looks upon this place is not an eye of fire, but an eye of deep, cool water. This benevolent power—a consciousness of vast, celestial patience—perceives the rift not as a wasteland, but as a **Slow Prayer**.

To this watcher, the Silt-Walkers are not strange bipeds, but living styluses; the Lung-Moss is not a plant, but a library of sighs. The power recognizes that here, time has been domesticated. It sees the beauty in a society that has reached the "Final Symmetry"—a state where the need to consume has been replaced by the need to observe.

Because this power is wise, it knows that to intervene with "abundance" would be a cruelty. To bring rain would be to drown the Silt-Walkers' crust; to bring "progress" would be to shatter the Sun-Traps. 

Instead, the Wise Power performs three subtle, resonant acts to honor the grace of the Bright Hollow:

### 1. The Anchoring of the "Hum"
The power notices the Echo-Mites struggling to hold onto a sound, vibrating themselves to exhaustion to remember the falling of a pebble. The Power reaches down with a thought that has the weight of a mountain and the softness of a cloud. It **tunes the resonance of the limestone**. 

Now, when a sound occurs in the rift, the walls themselves hold the frequency in a gentle, harmonic loop that does not fade for a century. The Echo-Mites no longer have to labor to remember; they can simply sit in the "after-image" of the sound, listening to the history of the rift as if it were a low, eternal song. This gives the inhabitants the gift of **Music** without the violence of noise.

### 2. The Transmutation of the "Silver Beads"
The Wise Power looks upon the Marrow-Leeches and the Weaver-Wraiths, seeing how their lives are tethered to the fragile, intermittent condensation of twilight. It recognizes the "desperate cooperation" of their art.

The Power subtly alters the porosity of the overhanging ledges. It does not create a spring (which would erode the art), but it turns the stone into a **perpetual lung**. The stone begins to breathe more deeply, pulling moisture from the arid air above and holding it just beneath the surface of the "melted wax" walls. Now, the condensation is no longer a frantic, vanishing event of the dawn. It is a steady, reliable bloom. The Marrow-Leeches may move even slower now; they no longer have to "race" the sun. The Power has gifted them **Security**.

### 3. The Vision of the Completed Line
The most profound act is reserved for the Silt-Walkers and their Sun-Traps. The Wise Power sees the ten-foot-tall shepherds standing for years to move a crystal a single millimeter. It sees the geometric patterns on the floor—the work of a million years—and realizes the Silt-Walkers can only see the "now." They cannot see the finished masterpiece because their lives, though long, are shorter than the art.

The Power does not speed up the etching. Instead, it grants the Silt-Walkers a **Chronal Peripheral-Vision**. 

As they stand before the Sun-Traps, the Silt-Walkers begin to perceive a faint, ghostly luminescence on the floor—a "plan" of where the lines will be in ten thousand years. They see the ghost of the completed work. For the first time, the "monastic maintenance" is joined by **Purpose**. They are no longer just moving stones; they are finishing a curve they can finally see.

---

### The Departure
Having touched the rift, the Wise Power does not linger. It does not demand worship, for it knows the Silt-Walkers have no words for "God," only for "Light." 

The Power withdraws, leaving the Bright Hollow exactly as it was found, yet fundamentally changed. The sun still bleaches the floor. The Shield-Mites still mirror the noon. but there is a new depth to the quiet. 

Deep in the Lung-Moss, the "Ancient" colonial mind records a new entry in its vegetable telepathy: the memory of being **seen**. And in the center of the chamber, a Silt-Walker settles its long, flute-like fingers against an obsidian shard and, for the first time in an age, vibrates a hum that is not a headache, but a shimmering, golden note of **satisfaction**.
