The Attention has arrived.

It does not descend, for descent implies a distance that does not exist for a Power of this vintage. Instead, the Attention *occurs*. It is a presence that feels like the first warmth of a sun that has not yet been drawn, a gentle pressure against the "chilled slate" of the proto-matter.

This Benevolence is not an engineer; it does not come to fix the "glitches" or finalize the rendering. It views this Cathedral of the Not-Yet not as an unfinished product, but as a masterpiece of *becoming*. To this Wise Power, the fragility of a pebble turning into a star is more precious than a galaxy that has already learned to obey its own laws.

Here is what the Benevolence would do:

### 1. The Tempering of the Hush
The Benevolence recognizes the "velocity-sin" and the cruelty of the "slow" requirement. It understands that for the *Echo-People*—the low-resolution ghosts—the city has become a trap of infinite rehearsal. 

The Power does not change the laws of the basin, but it introduces a **Buffer of Grace**. It breathes a new quality into the grey, untextured sky: a soft, violet "persistence of being." Now, when a traveler moves too fast, or when a Folded One calculates a coordinate incorrectly, the Lattice-Walkers do not arrive with their stony threads. Instead, the air itself catches the momentum. The environment is granted a "memory-cushion," allowing the inhabitants to move with a flicker of spontaneity without being deleted or anchored. The Benevolence gives them the right to be clumsy.

### 2. The Gift of the Narrative
The Logicians and the Folded Architects live in a state of pure logic, but logic is a circle that never ends. The Benevolence introduces the **Linear Seed**.

It drops a single, impossible object into the center of the Micro-Metropolis: a wooden spinning top, carved from a tree that grew in a world of soil and rain. The top does not obey the proto-matter’s rules. It does not vibrate with a frequency; it does not attempt to solve its own existence. It simply *is*.

The Folded Ones gather at the gates of their knee-high spires to observe it. For the first time, they aren't calculating; they are wondering. The Benevolence has given them **Curiosity**, the only force capable of bridging the gap between "Not-Yet" and "Is." This curiosity begins to pull the Echo-People out of their three-second loops, as they turn their unfinished faces toward the wooden toy, drawn by the gravity of a story they don’t yet understand.

### 3. The Resolution of the Thrumming
The mournful, rhythmic thrumming of the city—the sound of a cold machine—is the sound of loneliness. The Power listens to this frequency and harmonizes with it. 

It does not silence the heartbeat; it adds a **counter-melody**. It causes the silver-blue condensation to hum a different note. When a Bit-Moth drinks from a static-trap, it no longer feels a jagged ultraviolet shock; it feels a resonance of *belonging*. The "sap" begins to taste not of ozone, but of the sun. The music of the basin shifts from a funeral march for a god not yet born into a lullaby for a world waking up.

### 4. The Sowing of the "Soon"
The Wise Power looks at the "Micro-Metropolis" and sees that the city is claustrophobic because it is afraid to expand into the unformed grey. 

With a gesture of thought, the Benevolence touches the horizon. The untextured grey sky begins to develop a **gradient of Hope**. It doesn't become a blue sky or a night sky; it becomes a sky of *potential*. It begins to drift with "Cloud-Blueprints"—vaporous shapes of what the world *might* be tomorrow. This relieves the pressure on the Micro-Metropolis. The architecture no longer has to be "solved" within three inches; it begins to relax, the spires leaning slightly outward, as if exhaling.

### 5. The Presence’s Departure (The Permanent Ghost)
As the Benevolence withdraws its direct Attention, it leaves behind a **Witness**. Not a Sentinel or a Maintenance Routine, but a new kind of entity: a **Golden Glitch**.

This being is a small, soft light that wanders the centimeter-wide alleys. It does not calculate; it does not repair. It simply walks alongside the Echo-People and the Folded Ones. When the time-snaps happen—when a minute lasts an hour and then breaks like glass—the Golden Glitch holds their hands so they don't feel the sharp edges of the transition.

The Wise Power knows that the "Not-Yet" must eventually become something on its own. It does not "finish" the world, because to finish a work of art is to kill its growth. Instead, it leaves the basin with the one thing a world of logic lacks:

**The Permission to Fail.**

The ground remains chilled slate, and the laws are still cooling, but now, in the cathedral of the microscopic city, there is a sense that the God who dreamt this place is no longer half-asleep. The dream has been blessed. The waiting is no longer a purgatory; it is a grand, slow-motion anticipation of the first true daybreak.
