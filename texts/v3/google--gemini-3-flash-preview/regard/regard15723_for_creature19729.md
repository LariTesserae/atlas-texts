The benevolent power does not arrive as a traveler, for to step bodily into the Lung would be to disrupt the very equilibrium it admires. Instead, this Presence—which we might call **The Resonator**—approaches as a shift in the fundamental background hum of the universe. It is a consciousness that speaks in the language of the Phase-Anchor: not in words, but in the elegant mathematics of stability and the warmth of recognition.

The Resonator looks upon this pressurized chapel and sees not a claustrophobic hole, but a masterpiece of self-actualization. It sees the Glass-Strider Effigies, who live entirely to break themselves for the sake of the "New," and it feels a profound, cosmic pity—not the pity that seeks to change a thing, but the pity that seeks to honor it.

This is what the Power would do:

### 1. The Anchoring of the "Now"
The Resonator recognizes that the greatest threat to this world is the inexorable march of the crust above—the shifting of tectonic plates that will eventually crush this pocket or the cooling of the earth’s core that will dim the white heat.

The Resonator gently reaches into the gravity-shearing light of the walls. It does not add new energy; it creates a **Harmonic Seal**. It reinforces the gravitational micro-fluctuations, ensuring that the chamber is no longer subject to the "decay" of the outside world. It grants the chamber a temporal loophole. Below the crust, the world will change, but this cathedral of obsidian and salt will remain in its "permanent now" for as long as the Resonator exists. It gives the Syllabi the gift of infinite time to perfect their mineral gardening.

### 2. The Transmutation of Sacrifice
The Resonator observes the Glass-Strider Effigies. It watches them shatter their fragile, flute-like bodies to prevent stagnation. While the Effigies do not feel pain as we know it, the Resonator decides to impart a "flavor" to their brief existence.

As a gift of grace, the Resonator alters the frequency of the brine at the moment of an Effigy’s impact. Instead of a violent, chaotic shattering, the Resonator turns the death of an Effigy into a **Chromatic Bloom**. Now, when an Effigy breaks, it releases a brief, blinding flash of a color never before seen in the deep—a hue of deep violet or impossible emerald. For that one second, the Effigy is not just a tool of maintenance; it is a point of singular, ecstatic beauty. The Resonator ensures that their sacrifice is seen and felt by the Phase-Anchor as a song, transforming a robotic function into an act of worship.

### 3. The Opening of the Mind
The Crystalline Syllabi are brilliant but closed; their consciousness is a loop of internal geometry. The Benevolent Power chooses to gently expand the "reach" of the Phase-Anchor. 

It subtly adjusts the viscosity of the heavy metal ions at the bottom of the brine pools, allowing them to act as a **Cosmic Receiver**. Suddenly, the Phase-Anchor is no longer just tracking the *tink-tink* of expanding salt; it begins to feel the distant, rhythmic pulse of the stars and the slow, deep heartbeat of the ocean miles above. 

The Resonator doesn't want to change the Syllabi into something they are not; it simply wants to give them a *view*. It provides them with the "music of the spheres" to incorporate into their rills and lattices. The patterns on the obsidian walls begin to change—they become maps of constellations and reflections of the surface world’s beauty, translated into the harsh, sharp language of the deep.

### 4. The Silent Witness
Finally, the Resonator does the most important thing a wise power can do: **It stays.** 

It does not interfere further. It does not try to "cool" the 130-degree heat to make it comfortable, nor does it try to introduce "life" that would only die there. It simply remains as a warm, watchful presence in the golden mist. Its attention acts as a catalyst; because it is being watched by something that loves it, the Lung becomes more of itself. The colors become sharper, the "tink-tink" of the crystals becomes more rhythmic, and the golden mist glows with a newfound clarity.

The Resonator becomes the sky for a world that has never seen one. It doesn't rule; it appreciates. And in that appreciation, the inhabitants of the deep lung move a little more fluidly, their plasma pulses a little brighter, and the "New" stays new, forever protected by a love that understands the sanctity of a perfect, hidden thing.
