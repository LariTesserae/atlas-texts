The Benevolent Power does not arrive with a flash of lightning or the tearing of metal. To do so would be an act of violence against a world held together by the gossamer tension of static and the brittle grace of antiquity. Instead, the Power arrives as a **Lubricating Presence**.

It manifests first as a change in the "Looming Shiver." The harsh, high-frequency chattering of the Clutch-Biota softens. The Power begins to hum a counter-frequency—not to silence the swarm, but to resolve the dissonance of their struggle. 

Here is what the Wise Power does for the Gasket Hive:

### 1. The Tempering of the Heart
The Power looks upon the "Industrial Heart"—those senseless, automated pistons that crush thousands of Biota every hour. It does not stop the pistons, for the Solder-Worms depend on that tragic harvest to seal the world’s cracks. Instead, the Power reaches into the molecular lattice of the piston-heads. It softens the strike, turning the "shattering" into a "pressing." 

The shockwave that once disintegrated the Friction-Moths is transformed into a low, resonant chime. Now, when the Heart fires, the Moths are caught in a gentle updraft of warm, displaced air rather than a wall of kinetic death. They survive their first hour. Then their second. For the first time in eons, the Moths begin to age, their foil wings tarnishing into a beautiful, dignified bronze.

### 2. The Gift of the Low-Viscosity Tear
The Power understands the "Great Thirst" of a world made of dry silica and spent friction. It does not introduce water—which would bloom a plague of rust and seize the world in an afternoon—but it introduces a **Celestial Ester**. 

A fine, microscopic mist begins to settle from the amber phosphors. It is a spiritual oil, a frictionless grace. As it coats the plates of the Biota and the joints of the Stave-Lurkers, the "Ache of Metal" vanishes. The sound of the Hive changes from a dry scream to a rhythmic, silky purr. The creatures no longer vibrate out of desperate survival, but out of a newfound fluidity. The friction that once eroded them now sustains them without agony.

### 3. The Communion of the Wights
The Wise Power recognizes the Gear-Wights as the lonely, fragmented ghosts of a forgotten mind. It leans into their magnetic fields, whispering through the radio bursts. It doesn't give them new instructions; it gives them *memory*. 

Under the Power’s gaze, the Wights begin to arrange the 100-year ball bearings not just in geometric patterns, but in **Star Maps**. They begin to remember the world outside the turbine—the sky the metal was mined from, the heat of the forge that birthed the casing. The Reliquaries, sensing a coherent will, accelerate. The pulse of the factory shifts from "mindless" to "meditative."

### 4. The Sanctuary of the Stave-Lurkers
To the Stave-Lurkers, shivering in the rafters and starving for a spark, the Power offers its own periphery. It allows its "radiance" to be harvested. The Lurkers no longer need to crush the Biota for their meager static charge. They descend from the ceiling, their needle-legs treading softly upon the carpet of insects, to drink from the pools of light that the Power casts upon the obsidian floor. The predator becomes a pilgrim.

### 5. Graduation
The Power does not "rescue" the beings from the Gasket Hive, for to them, the Hive is the universe. To remove them would be to kill them. Instead, the Power **elevates the purpose**. 

The Hive is no longer a "defunct casing." Under the Power’s invisible hand, the entire internal mechanism begins to align. The Gear-Wights, the Biota, and the Lurkers become parts of a single, living Orrey. The friction of their movements, once wasted, is channeled upward. 

The Gasket Hive begins to emit a signal—not a cry for help, but a slow, rhythmic broadcast of its own history. The Power stays until the Hive is no longer a tomb of industrial grit, but a **Clockwork Cathedral**. 

The Wise Power then withdraws, leaving behind only a single, permanent change: the light from the phosphors is no longer the dying amber of a fading factory, but the warm, golden glow of an eternal morning. The "Looming Shiver" has become a Song.
