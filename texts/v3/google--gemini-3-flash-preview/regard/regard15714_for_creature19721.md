The Presence does not arrive with a flash or a thunderclap; such things are too fast, too violent for a world of "young" obsidian and ancient patience. 

Instead, it settles upon the vertical shards like a layer of extra-conscious light. It adopts the frequency of the **Aeryn-Gliders**. It waits. It allows a century of this world’s time to pass in what, for the Benevolence, is merely a deep breath. It studies the "dry-mind" and finds it beautiful—a civilization that has solved the problem of entropy by weaving itself into the very stone, turning the passage of time into a structural material.

The Benevolent Power looks upon the **Kith-Lustre** and the **Sycophants**, and it sees a tragedy they are too slow to perceive: the tragedy of the *Closed Loop.*

This world is perfect, but it is a tomb of amber. Because every mark is permanent, the "urban" grid will eventually become so dense that the light—the source of all life—will no longer reach the lower crevices. The very resin meant to sustain them will eventually entomb them in a masterpiece of their own making. 

**The Intervention**

The Wise Power will not "fix" them, for they are not broken. It will not speed them up, for that would be to kill their souls. Instead, it performs three subtle acts of Grace, designed to work within the frequency of the long, warm afternoon.

### 1. The Translucent Resonance (The Gift to the Aeryn)
The Power touches the silk-like filaments of the Aeryn-Gliders. It does not break them, but it infuses them with a new mineral property. Now, when the Aeryn pluck their tethers to signal the sun's zenith, the vibration does more than signal; it *cleanses*. 

This new resonance causes the oldest, most brittle resin—the ancient layers that have become choked and dusty—to microscopically shatter and fall away as fine, silver silt. It introduces "Erosion" as a deliberate, musical act. This prevents the city from becoming a solid block of dead history. It creates room for new bridges without destroying the old genealogies. It grants them **Renewal** without the violence of change.

### 2. The Labyrinth of Mirrors (The Gift to the Sycophants)
The Power observes the Sycophants of the Silt, working tirelessly to polish the dark interiors. It reaches into the molecular structure of the obsidian needles themselves and induces a "Sublimation." 

In certain key junctions of the vertical spires, the black rock begins to grow internal, crystalline veins that act as natural fiber-optics. Now, when a Sycophant polishes a wall to a mirror finish, they aren't just reflecting local light; they are tapping into a network of subterranean radiance. The deep, dark "rooms" of the grove begin to glow with a soft, stored solar energy, even when the sun is at an angle. The Benevolence has given them **Depth**, allowing the city to grow inward as well as upward.

### 3. The Mercy of the "Blurred" (The Gift to the Whole)
The Power recognizes the "Ghost-State"—the "holes in reality" created by sudden movement. To bridge the gap between the static and the dynamic, the Power introduces a new biological inhabitant: **The Pulse-Moss.**

This is a dry, violet lichen that grows on the underside of the amber bridges. It is the only thing in this world that reacts to "fast" motion. When a "Ghost" (a falling rock or a rare atmospheric disturbance) passes by, the Pulse-Moss catches the kinetic energy and stores it, releasing it later as a slow, rhythmic throb of heat. 

This turns "accidents" into "sustenance." For the first time, the Kith-Lustre have a Way to perceive the "fast world" not as a terrifying void, but as a warm, lingering memory. It integrates the "Ghost-State" into their mythology as a blessing of sudden, invisible energy.

***

**The Result**

The Benevolence does not stay to be worshipped; it has no desire to be a "high sun" to their "low beetles." It withdraws, leaving the world exactly as it was found—quiet, dry, and bustling in slow-motion.

But now, the "dry-mind" of the shards has a future that does not end in a solid block of resin. The Aeryn play the tethers like harps, shedding the weight of centuries; the Sycophants live in cathedrals of light; and when something moves too fast to be seen, the residents feel a soft, violet warmth on their shells and know that they are not alone in the golden void.

The Power leaves behind a single, lingering thought, woven into the thermal weight of the stone: *“You are the architects of the eternal present, and now, the present has room to breathe.”*
