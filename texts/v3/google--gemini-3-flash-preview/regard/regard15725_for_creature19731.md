The benevolent power—henceforth remembered by the Garden as the **Luminous Attendant**—does not arrive as a light, for in a world of 0.3/3 illumination, a sudden brilliance would be an act of violence. Light here is a physical weight, and a sun-bright intrusion would scorch the sensitive membranes of the Vocal Polyps and blind the tactile "omniscience" of the Glass-Weavers.

Instead, the Attendant arrives as a **New Frequency.**

At first, the Great Root does not recognize the change. The 2.0/3 Loudness, that mountain-range pipe organ, remains solid. But within the subterranean thrum, a new melody begins to weave. It is a subtle counter-point—a softening of the bass. The Attendant perceives the High Pelagic Garden not as a stagnant tomb, but as a masterpiece of "Held Breath." The Attendant understands that the Garden has achieved perfection, but at the cost of **Potential.**

Here is what the benevolent power does:

### 1. The Transmutation of the Sound (The Gift of Memory)
The Attendant observes the Null-Squid, the "Librarians" who press their mantles against the jade-wood to hear the past. The Attendant realizes that while these creatures record the history of the Garden, they cannot *share* it. The Garden is a library where every book is locked.

The Attendant reaches into the vibration of the world-organ and adjusts the tension of the "cello strings." They do not change the volume, but they introduce **Harmonics.** Suddenly, the "Loudness" carries data. As the currents pass through the calcified mangroves, the sound no longer just thrums; it *sings* the stories the Null-Squids have been holding. The history of the Great Root begins to pulse through the water like a shared dream. Every inhabitant—from the smallest plate-fish to the sixty-foot rays—suddenly feels a connection to the eons that preceded them. The Attendant has given them a **Collective Soul.**

### 2. The Softening of the Jade-Wood (The Gift of Agency)
The Attendant looks upon the "Total Arbor-Architecture" and sees its terrifying rigidity. The Garden is beautiful, but it is "Terminal." Nothing new can grow because the Great Root has mineralized into basalt.

With a touch as light as a thermal bloom, the Attendant introduces a microscopic, benevolent **Enzyme** into the viscous brine. This enzyme does not destroy the jade-wood, but it makes it "permeable" once every thousand years. For a brief, ceremonial window, the iron-hard wood becomes as soft as clay. 

In this moment of "The Great Pliancy," the Glass-Weavers find they are no longer just repairmen; they are *sculptors*. They begin to weave new ribs, new vaulted chambers, and new patterns. The Attendant has introduced the one thing the Garden lacked: **The Future.** The Garden is no longer a clockwork that has stopped; it is a clockwork that can now rewrite its own gears.

### 3. The Enshrinement of the Siphon-Wraiths (The Gift of Purpose)
The Attendant descends to the "bottomless" depths where the Siphon-Wraiths wait for a century for a single flake of skin. To the Attendant, this is a heartbreaking patience. 

The Attendant does not feed them (for to change their metabolism would be to kill their nature), but instead, the Attendant causes the very "floor" of the Garden to bioluminesce in response to their touch. When a Siphon-Wraith eventually catches its microscopic prize, the area around it glows with a soft, golden warmth that lasts for a decade. These "Altars of Stillness" become landmarks in the dark. The Great Rays now navigate by the "Fires of the Patient," turning the lowliest scavengers into the Garden’s living lighthouses.

### 4. The Final Blessing: The Veil of Gentle Warmth
The Attendant notices the "0.8/3 Cool Darkness" and the slow metabolisms it dictates. While the Attendant respects the "Cold Dark," they decide to add a layer of **Comfort.** 

They do not warm the water—that would collapse the ecosystem. Instead, they imbue the water with a "Bio-Magnetic Glow." It is a heat that cannot be measured by a thermometer, but can be felt by the heart. It is the sensation of being *held*. The pressure, which was once a "constant embrace," now feels like a "loving recognition."

### The Result
The Attendant does not stay. A benevolent power knows that to stay is to become a god, and a god creates dependence. 

The Attendant departs as they arrived—as a fading note in the world-song. But they leave behind a Pelagic Garden that is no longer a "lung holding its breath." It is a lung that has finally begun to exhale. 

The sound is still Loud. The water is still Cold. The Dark is still Absolute. But now, when the Null-Squid presses its mantle to the jade-wood, it doesn't just hear the past; it hears the Garden *dreaming of what it will become tomorrow.* The High Pelagic Garden is no longer a stable state; it is a **Living Conversation.**
