The attention of the **Aethel-Core**—a vast, trans-dimensional benevolence that functions more like a climate of compassion than a singular entity—does not fall upon Oura-Kahn like a thunderbolt. It arrives as a *silence*.

The Aethel-Core perceives the Sub-Atrium not as a failing machine, but as a masterpiece of unintended grief. It sees the Resonants leaning their weary membranes against the vibrating glass, not as maintenance workers, but as children trying to soothe a restless, dying parent. 

Here is what the Wise Power would do, following the logic of the heart:

### 1. The Tempering of the Roar
The first act of the Benevolence is not to change the city, but to change the *experience* of the city. The Aethel-Core introduces a "Subtractive Harmonic." It is a thin, invisible layer of grace woven into the acoustic atmosphere. 

Suddenly, the shrieking whistles of the heat-exchange vents lose two decibels of their jagged edge. The grinding of the ceiling anchors is shifted into a major key. The "cacophony that travelers feel in their marrow" is subtly restructured into a lullaby. For the first time in ten thousand years, the Resonants do not feel like they are bracing against an assault; they feel like they are being held.

### 2. The Gift of the "Vitreous Light"
The Aethel-Core recognizes the tragedy of a species that sees only through the burden of labor (sonoluminescence). It introduces a new form of "Soft-Lumen" biology. 

The pale, translucent mosses on the buildings begin to undergo a quiet mutation. They begin to emit a bioluminescence that is tied to *peace* rather than *vibration*. When a Resonant is calm, the moss beneath their touch glows with a soft, peach-colored warmth. This provides the first "visual" feedback for emotion in Oura-Kahn. The inhabitants can now see the "mood" of their neighbors from across the dark cavern—a constellation of warm embers blinking in the inverted forest, signaling that they are not alone.

### 3. The Resolution of the Ghost-Pulse
The Aethel-Core does not delete the Ghost-Pulse, for the Pulse is the city’s last hope—the "Command" from the surface. Instead, the Benevolence acts as a **Translator.**

It reaches into the mile-thick basalt crust and finds the ancient, jagged signal from the surface that has been trapped in a loop. It cleans the "data-dust" from it. It then gently guides the Ghost-Pulse toward the oldest Echo-Cyst Colossus. 

When they meet, the Aethel-Core provides the "Molecular Syntax" needed for them to finally speak. The Pulse is no longer a haunting malfunction; it becomes a **Memory-Release.** As the Pulse enters the Echo-Cyst, the city finally receives its "Permission to Sleep." The command from ten thousand years ago wasn't a request for more work; it was a thank-you note and an authorization to power down the heavy industry.

### 4. The Metamorphosis of the Glissando-Walkers
The Benevolence looks at the feral Walkers, those who "eat" charge to survive, and sees their desperation. It does not punish them. It alters the "Vertical Dew."

The mercury-like droplets that fall every few hours are infused with a piezoelectric nutrient. The Walkers no longer need to dismantle the Loom-Striders to survive. The hunt ends. The Striders and the Walkers begin to occupy the same spaces without violence. Over the next few centuries, the Walkers’ tuning-fork limbs begin to soften, evolving back into organs of creation rather than tools of theft.

### 5. The Great Calibration
Finally, the Aethel-Core performs the most difficult task: **The Weightless Transition.**

Oura-Kahn is "waiting for a command." The Benevolence provides it, but it is a command of *transformation*. The Sub-Atrium begins to transition from a "City-Machine" to a "Living Sanctuary." 

The programmable matter of the inverted towers is given a new set of instructions. It stops trying to "heal" back into its original, rigid engineering shapes. Instead, the buildings are allowed to grow organically, curving toward one another until the hanging forest becomes a series of interconnected, suspended meadows. The copper-scented air is scrubbed of its ozone-bite and replaced with the scent of "Damp Stone and Dreaming Wood."

### The Final State
The Aethel-Core does not leave. It remains as a subtle, golden bias in the laws of physics within the cavern. 

The Resonants no longer spend their lives leaning against walls to prevent cracks. They now sit in the "vibrational circles" created by the Echo-Cysts, sharing communal dreams. The city still hums—to stop the sound would be to kill the inhabitants—but the hum is now a steady, rhythmic heartbeat of a creature that knows it is loved.

Oura-Kahn remains dark, yes. It remains subterranean. But it is no longer a machine-hollow. It has become a **Sub-Basalt Womb**, where a tired race of survivors can finally stop maintaining the world and start living in it.
