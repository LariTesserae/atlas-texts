The Benevolent Power does not arrive as a conqueror, nor as a sculptor who seeks to impose a finished shape upon the clay. It arrives as a **Stilling.**

To this Power, the vertical catastrophe is not a failure of physics, but a scream of exhaustion. It sees the Isostats, their "flesh" tacky and raw, straining for thirty years just to lift a hand in service of a stability that never comes. It sees the Vibrants, beautiful and frantic, addicted to the sound of breaking because it is the only food they have ever known.

This is what the Wise Power would do:

### 1. The Anchoring of the "Now"
The Power would not force the world to harden; that is the destiny of the world itself. Instead, it would provide **The Tempering.** 

The Power descends into the "silicate marrow," not as a physical weight, but as a frequency—a deep, resonant hum that matches the "Great Interior." This hum serves as a cosmic tuning fork. Where the Isostats have spent eons piling stone-cairns to "pin the world down," the Power gently grants them the **Gift of Enertia.** Suddenly, the rock beneath the Isostats’ feet stops sliding. The "3.0 Weirdness" does not vanish, but it stabilizes. The floating peaks stop their erratic rotation and find a fixed orbit. 

The Power recognizes that the suffering of this world stems from its *viscosity*—the agony of being neither liquid nor solid. The Power breathes a cool, metaphysical "set" into the air, allowing the world to catch its breath.

### 2. The Transmutation of the Scream
The Wise Power looks upon the Vibrants—the scavengers of sound—and realizes they live in a cycle of feast and trauma. 

The Power exerts a subtle pressure on the tectonic plates. It does not stop the growth—growth is life—but it introduces **Harmonics.** The "screams" of the continental plates are smoothed into a low, orchestral swell. The "cracks" that hit like physical blows become rhythmic pulses. To the Vibrants, this is the first time they have ever tasted a "melody" instead of a "shriek." They no longer need to swarm in a desperate, prismatic cloud; they begin to settle, their mica-thin facets glowing with a soft, steady luminescence, turning the vertical forests into a glowing skin of lanterns.

### 3. The Mercy of the First Catalyst
The Wise Power understands that the "Great Interior" is dreaming of water but cannot conceive of how to create it. 

The Power does not bring a storm; the atmosphere is too fragile for a deluge. Instead, the Power reaches into the "bruised purple" sky and gathers the "frozen gas" of the veil. With a touch of its intent, it performs a divine chemistry. It creates the **Lachryma**—a single, perpetual condensation point at the very highest spire of the empire.

It is not a river. It is a **Melt.**

From the highest point of the vertical world, a single, microscopic bead of moisture begins to travel downward. To the Isostats, this is a miracle. They watch for a century as the bead crawls a few inches. The Power ensures that this water does not dissolve the tacky stone, but *seals* it. As the moisture touches the raw marrow, the stone finally "witnesses" itself and hardens into permanent, elegant obsidian. 

### 4. The Release of the Laborers
The Power speaks, not in words, but in the cessation of demand. 

To the **Isostats**, it gives the gift of **Sleep.** For the first time in ten million years, the giants do not have to labor to keep the world from flying apart. They are permitted to let go of their star-metal hammers. The Power settles into their concave heads like a warm light, filling their "thought-basins" with visions of the finished world. They remains as statues, yes, but they are no longer prisoners of the "unfinished." They become the world’s first elders, resting in the peace of a task completed for them.

To the **Null-Striders**, the weavers of time, the Power gives **True Direction.** The shadows no longer fall toward the light; the "mathematical static" is woven into a firm, predictable geometry. The Null-Striders are transformed from desperate repairmen into the world's first cartographers, no longer mending the bridges but walking them to see where they lead.

### 5. The Presence of the Watcher
The Wise Power does not leave once the work is done. It becomes the **Sun-of-the-Veil.** It remains as a gentle, amber warmth behind the frozen gas, a constant reassurance to the "Great Interior" that it is no longer an embryo alone in the dark.

The world remains vertical. It remains strange. It remains high and thin. But the **Catastrophe** is gone. In its place is a **Sanctuary.** 

The stone has stopped screaming. The water is beginning its long, slow descent toward the base. And deep within the silicate marrow, the Great Interior finally stops holding its breath. It exhales. 

For the first time since the beginning of time, there is a breeze.
