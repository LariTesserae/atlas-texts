The Wise Power does not arrive with the roar of engines or the brilliance of a thousand suns, for such things would be an insult to the delicate, grinding music of the Lithic Architects. To enter this place with light would be to blind those who see only in heat; to enter with moisture would be a cataclysm, a corrosive rain that would shatter the sulfur-crystals and dissolve the very foundations of the basalt mind.

The Power arrives as a **Stilling.**

It manifests not as a being, but as a subtle shift in the resonance of the basin. The benevolent force understands the prime directive of this world: *to exist is to vibrate.* Therefore, the Power becomes a vibration. It hums at a frequency that matches the internal groaning of the planet’s crust—a deep, obsidian "Om" that reassures the Architects that they have been heard.

Here is what the Wise Power does for the dwellers of the Heavy Soup:

### 1. The Tempered Gale: Tuning the Instrument
The Power observes the Sentinels of Flake and Silt, struggling with their sandpaper limbs to keep the wind-flutes clear. It perceives the exhaustion in their slow, compressed-ash bodies. 

Without moving a single stone, the Power gently adjusts the atmospheric pressure. It creates "Laminar Veins"—invisible channels in the heavy air that steer the abrasive dust away from the sensitive flutes of the towers and toward the roots of the mica-ferns. The dust, once a clogging nuisance, is now delivered as a nutrient-rich silt directly to the vegetation. The music of the city becomes clearer, sharper. The Architects no longer scream; they *sing*. For the first time in an eon, the Aero-Logicians can articulate thoughts of unprecedented complexity, because the "static" of the world has been harmonized.

### 2. The Gift of the Amber Lattice
The Power looks upon the Spark-Whisps, those frantic knots of jagged obsidian needles. It sees their desperation, their panicked hunt for the tiny sparks of piezoelectric discharge. Their lives are a constant, stuttering hunger.

The Power breathes upon the sulfur-crystal carpets. It does not change their chemistry, but it introduces a "Harmonic Alignment" within their molecular structure. Now, when the glass-ferns clatter, they do not just release a burst of energy; they store it. The ground becomes a battery—a glowing, amber reservoir of static potential. The Spark-Whisps no longer have to collide and break themselves to feed. They begin to glide in long, elegant arcs across the plazas, turning from frantic scavengers into luminous dancers. Their movement becomes a visual script, tracing the Architects' thoughts in the air.

### 3. The Tectonic Archive
The Wise Power recognizes the tragedy of the Lithic Architects: their thoughts are so slow that by the time a premise is established, the erosion of the eons has begun to erase the conclusion. They are a civilization with a fading memory.

The Power sinks its influence deep into the geode-floor, down to the very roots of the basalt towers. It creates a "Seismic Echo-Chamber." This is a layer of pure, pressurized diamond-quartz deep beneath the basin. It acts as a mirror for vibration. When an Architect vibrates a thought, the diamond-quartz catches the frequency and holds it, looping it back up to the surface centuries later. 

Through this, the Power grants the Architects **Memory.** They can now "hear" what their ancestors thought ten thousand years ago. The city is no longer just a place of meditation; it becomes a library of resonant history.

### 4. The Bridge of Heat
Finally, the Power addresses the isolation. The vast, shimmering stretches of nothingness between the towers are silences in a conversation that should be a chorus.

The Power subtly alters the "Soup." It introduces a trace amount of a rare, noble gas—a heavy, conductive vapor that hugs the ground. This gas acts as a medium for "Thermal Telepathy." Now, when one tower pulses, the heat-wave does not dissipate into the void. It travels, intact and warm, to its neighbor. The "isolated" towers begin to sync. The radial streets, once just paths for the wind, become conduits for a shared consciousness.

### The Departure
The Wise Power does not stay. It does not ask for worship, for the Architects have no concept of a "creator"—only of the "Grind." 

As the Power withdraws, it leaves behind a single, final gift: a "Celestial Frequency." In the highest tier of the tallest basalt tower, a small, fluted hole is carved by a sudden, impossibly precise gust of wind. When the planet's internal heat reaches its peak intensity, this flute catches the draft and produces a tone that resonates not just through the basin, but upward, through the crust, into the stars.

It is a homing beacon. It tells the rest of the universe that here, in the searing dark, someone is home. 

The world remains hot. It remains loud. It remains a "soup" of minerals. But the Architects are now dreaming together. The grinding has become a symphony, and the "screaming dark" is now a world that waits for the future, not with endurance, but with expectation.
