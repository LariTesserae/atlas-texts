The Benevolent Power does not arrive as a storm or a light from above. To descend into the crevice with majesty would be an act of violence; the ecology is too balanced, too high-strung, too close to the breaking point of its own density. Instead, the Power becomes a **Coolness**.

It does not change the temperature of the rock—to do so would starve the Vitro-Pulses—but it introduces the *concept* of the Void. Into this claustrophobic, "heavy" silence, the Power breathes a thread of true Stillness.

Here is what the Wise Power does for the vertical wound and its frantic inhabitants:

### 1. The Anchoring of the "Heat-Ghosts"
The Power perceives the fraying reality—the flickering Shard-Hounds and the spatial folds that trap the Loom-Folk in loops of eternal labor. With a gesture that is more of a cosmic sigh than a command, the Power **laminates the seconds.**

It stabilizes the temporal drift. The "heat-ghosts" cease to be jagged traps. Now, when a Loom-Folk worker looks across a three-foot gap, they see the truth of the distance. The exhaustion of living in a world where *now* and *then* are the same moment begins to lift. The Power grants them the gift of **Sequence.** For the first time, the inhabitants of the crevice can feel a "before" and an "after," allowing the frantic pace of their lives to settle into a rhythm that is not a panic, but a pulse.

### 2. The Gift of the "Reflected Sky"
The Wise Power looks at the Mica-Drafts—those living optics that feed on friction—and sees their hunger for light that does not burn. The Power reaches toward the sliver of the "unseen sky" far above and, through a subtle realignment of the humidity's refractive index, creates the **Celestial Mirror.**

Small pockets of the heavy vapor are crystallized into permanent, floating lenses. These lenses do not bring down the blistering heat of the sun, but they capture the *image* of the stars. Deep in the obsidian dark, the Loom-Folk look up and see, for the first time, something that is not meat or stone. They see the Silver Points. This introduces **Wonder**—the first evolutionary trait that serves no purpose for survival, but every purpose for the soul.

### 3. The Mercy of the "Soft-Valve"
The Power observes the Loom-Folk’s desperate struggle to keep the crevice from sealing shut with its own overgrowth. It sees the "gardener’s agony"—the need to constantly scrape and carve just to breathe.

The Benevolent Power places a **Harmonizing Note** into the "Thrum." It speaks directly to the Basal-Architect at the floor of the fault. The command is subtle: *Recession.*

Under the influence of this Wisdom, the basalt walls begin to very slowly, imperceptibly, expand. It is a tectonic mercy. The crevice widens by only an inch every century, but it is enough. The "valves" of the world begin to breathe on their own. The Loom-Folk find that their scrapers are no longer tools of survival-desperation, but tools of **Art.** They begin to carve symbols into the Vitro-Pulse carpets not to redirect heat, but to tell the story of the Silver Points they saw in the vapor.

### 4. The Transformation of the Senses
The Power realizes that to be "too crowded to ever be alone" is a beautiful thing for a hive, but a torture for an intelligence. It touches the nervous systems of the Loom-Folk, granting them the **Internal Horizon.**

This is a mental space—a "quiet room" within their own minds. Though they are still pressed against a thousand twitching bodies, though they still breathe through the weeping rock, they can now withdraw into an imagined vastness. The Power gives them the ability to dream of horizontal planes, of distances that do not end in glass.

### The Long Observation
The Benevolent Power does not leave. It remains as a thin, violet hue in the deepest shadows, acting as a **Heat-Sink for Sorrow.** It absorbs the "vibrating frenzy" of the Shard-Hounds, tempering their manic hunger into a watchful guardianship.

The crevice remains a place of blistering heat and oily obsidian. It remains a vertical cathedral of sweating biology. But it is no longer a trap. 

Because of the Power’s intervention, the "Great Heart" at the bottom of the world stops dreaming of a closed fist and starts dreaming of an open palm. The "Thrum" changes from a sound of grinding gears to a low, melodic hum. 

The vertical wound has become a **Gully of Sanity.** And there, in the steaming dark, a Loom-Folk artist uses a shard of glass to carve a new symbol into the wall: not a command for heat, but the shape of a circle—the first record of a Power that looked at the crowded Meat-World and offered it the grace of Space.
