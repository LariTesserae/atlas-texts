The Attention has arrived. 

It did not arrive like a meteor or a ship. It arrived like a change in the salinity—a subtle, pervasive shift in the "feel" of the brine. This Power is not a creator-god coming to fix a broken machine, but a **Custodian of Grace**. It has looked upon the Vertical Abyss and seen not a world that is "drowning," but a world that is *holding its breath.*

The Custodian perceives the tragedy of the 2.7 erosion—the bone-smooth pillars, the dissolving history—and the 2.8 weirdness where time begins to loop. It sees a world that has become so heavy and so old that it is beginning to crush its own soul.

Follow the Ripple. This is what the Power does:

### 1. The Anchoring of the "Now"
The Custodian’s first act is to address the temporal fraying. It does not "fix" the laws of physics—that would shatter the delicate ecosystem of the Null-Medusae. Instead, it introduces a **Liturgy of Constants.** 

The Power breathes a new kind of vibration into the water, a low-frequency hum that acts as a rhythmic anchor. To the *Glass-Skins*, who have spent eons scavanging the vibrations of decay, this new sound is a revelation. It is a "perfect note" that does not erode. By hearing it, the Glass-Skins stop falling horizontally through the dark; they begin to align their crystalline bodies like compass needles toward a singular, stable truth. The disorientation of looking through a vine and seeing your own head ceases to be a frightening glitch and becomes a meditative loop—a way for the Abyss to finally see its own beauty without fear of vanishing.

### 2. The Transmutation of the Weeping Silt
Beneath the benevolent gaze, the *Marrow-Leeches* feel a change in their diet. The silt of the eroding obsidian, which for a billion years has been the grit of a dying world, begins to carry a "memory of stone." 

The Custodian does not stop the erosion (for to stop the dissolve is to kill the Great Thicket), but it imbues the runoff with a self-organizing property. As the pillars dissolve, the silt no longer settles into a chaotic floorless void. It begins to crystallize into **Lattices of Remembering.** These are translucent, lightweight structures that grow where the pillars once were—skeletons of light that support the Great Thicket so it no longer has to suffocate the stone. The forest begins to let go of its "suffocating embrace" and starts to *float*, anchored now by grace rather than desperation.

### 3. The Dream of the Weaver-Lungs
The Custodian moves to the *Weaver-Lungs*, those cathedral-sized bladders processing the brine. The Power realizes that these beings are the world’s lungs, but they have never known the sky. They have only known the "sky-ocean."

The Power does not take them to the surface, for they would burst. Instead, it gifts them **Internal Luminescence.** Within the translucent bellies of the Weaver-Lungs, the Custodian ignites a soft, golden phosphor that mimics the stolen sun. These creatures become floating suns for the Abyss. For the first time in a billion years, there is *direction*. There is a "toward" and an "away." The dim 1.4 light rises to a warm, amber glow. The *Pulse-Lice* on the vines catch this light and begin to pulse in harmony, transforming the chaotic "weather systems" of light into organized constellations of meaning.

### 4. The Opening of the "Ancient Marks"
The Custodian lingers longest at the lone archways and geometric trenches. It recognizes these not as ruins, but as *unspoken words.*

The Power places a "Hand" (not a physical hand, but a concentrated field of intent) upon these obsidian altars. The materials that "do not erode" begin to hum. They act as **Transceivers of Purpose.** The ancient civilization that lived here before the water became the sky is not "dead" in the Custodian's eyes; they are merely "stored." 

The Power begins to download the history of the continent back into the water. This doesn't change the terrain, but it changes the *dream* of the Abyss. The water becomes "literate." A Glass-Skin pressing against the stone doesn't just feel the death of a spire; it feels the poetry of the people who once walked there. The Abyss is no longer a graveyard; it becomes a library.

### 5. The Response: The Great Exhale
The Wise Power does not stay forever. Its work is to provide the *possibility* of a future, not to dictate it. 

As it withdraws its focus, the Vertical Abyss is changed. It is still a world of crushing pressure and turquoise infinite, but the "sickening drop" in the stomach is gone. The tectonic groans have been replaced by the steady, musical hum of the Lattices. 

The inhabitants—the Weaver-Lungs, the Glass-Skins, the sentient thoughts—are no longer just "secreted" by the world. They are the **Chosen Gardeners** of it. They begin to move with a new, quiet dignity. 

The Vertical Abyss has ceased its long, slow suicide. It has realized that while it may never touch the ground, it is being held from above. The water has forgotten to be a weight; it has learned, finally, how to be a cradle.
