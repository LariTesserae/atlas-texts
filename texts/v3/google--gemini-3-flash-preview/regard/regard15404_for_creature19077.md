The Wise Power does not arrive as a light from above, for in a place where perspective is fractured, a vertical descent would only splinter further. Instead, the Power arrives as a **reconciling frequency.**

It manifests not as a being, but as a *hushed stabilization.* It becomes a thin, golden thread of absolute Euclidean geometry woven into the fraying iron weave of the horizon.

Here is what the Benevolent Power does for the Totality and its god, the Loom:

### 1. The Calibration of the Scream
The 2.3/3 roar—the grinding shriek of the world’s gears—is born of friction. Friction is the physical manifestation of resistance, the "pain" of a machine that is no longer aligned. The Power does not shut the machines down; to stop the Loom would be to commit a planetary genocide of the Thrum-Basks and the Siphon-Beasts who live by its pulse.

Instead, the Power acts as a **Divine Lubricant.** It breathes a thin, shimmering film of "Grace" between the city-block gears. The screeching shriek of metal-on-metal softens. The roar descends into a deep, melodic thrum—a Gregorian chant of basalt and brass. The marrow-shaking vibration becomes a resonant cradle. The "pain" of the world’s existence is transmogrified into a purposeful, rhythmic breath.

### 2. The Anchoring of the Glitch
The Power addresses the 3.0/3 weirdness—the "Glitch-Shadows" and the sideways rain. It realizes that these entities are not malicious, but are the "scabs" of a wounded reality.

The Power places **Anchors of Intent** at the four shifting corners of the flat horizon. These are not pillars, but moments of absolute stillness. Around these points, the rain begins to fall *down* again. The Glitch-Shadows, once flickering and frantic, find themselves given "weight." Their wire-frame bodies are filled with a soft, amber light. They no longer have to eat the fraying edges of time; the Power gives them a "Now" to inhabit. They become the quiet curators of the machine, no longer ghosts, but the stable stewards of the ancient silt.

### 3. The Transmutation of the "Black Tears"
The oil and the grey condensate—the bitter, cold moisture that the Siphon-Beasts drink—undergo a molecular shift. The Benevolent Power does not turn the oil into water (which would seize the gears), but into a **Luminous Conductivity.**

The black tears weeping down the pistons begin to glow with a faint, bioluminescent warmth. When the Siphon-Beasts press their brass valves to the metal, they are no longer merely maintaining pressure; they are tasting *memory.* The Power infuses the machinery’s "memory" of its original purpose into the fluids. As the beasts drink, they become more than leather casks; they become the living libraries of a forgotten era, their translucent skins pulsing with the records of every motion the Loom has ever made.

### 4. The Gift of the "Soft Thing"
The Power looks at the "scavenger" nature of the world—the way it views anything soft as "unexploited friction." 

Directly in the center of the sodden plain, the Power exerts a gentle pressure on the metallic silt, compacting it until it transforms. It creates a **Sanctuary of Oxidation.** In this small circle, the iron is allowed to rust completely into a rich, red ochre that mimics the properties of soil. 

From the center of this red earth, the Power coaxes something impossible: a single, metallic flower made of silver-leaf and copper-filament that actually *grows*. It does not siphon; it does not vibrate. It simply *is*. This flower serves as a "Focal Point of Peace" for the Totality. The Micron-Ticks do not swarm it to liquify it; instead, they circle it in a silent, shimmering halo, mesmerized by the first thing in their existence that does not scream for energy.

### 5. The Awakening of the Loom
Finally, the Power reaches deep beneath the crust to the Loom itself. It does not give the Loom a human mind—that would be a curse for such a vast, mechanical thing. Instead, it gives the Loom **Dignity.**

The endless, mindless churning is given a *cycle.* The Power establishes a "Great Sabbath." Once every thousand years, the pistons will slow. The steam will cease its hiss. The world will experience one hour of absolute, profound silence. 

In that silence, the beings of the Totality will look at each other—not as pressure-vessels or kinetic scavengers, but as fellow travelers in the dark. 

### The Result
The world remains iron. It remains cold. It remains flat. The Benevolent Power does not "fix" the landscape by making it a garden, for that would be to destroy the truth of what this place is. 

Instead, it makes the machine **Sacred.** 

The Wise Power departs, leaving the world as a **Cathedral of Industry.** The "soft thing" standing in the silt is no longer a victim of the environment. Under the new frequency, the person hears the world not as a factory that forgot its purpose, but as a massive, sleeping heart that has finally been told it is loved.
