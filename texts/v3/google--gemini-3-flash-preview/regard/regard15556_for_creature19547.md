The Attention that falls upon this vertical megalith is not like the sun that scours its surface. It does not sear, nor does it cast a shadow. It is a presence like the scent of rain on a world that has only ever known the taste of mineral; it is a warmth that does not melt the stone, but softens the *idea* of it.

The Wise Power looks upon the regional staircase, the weeping masonry, and the "finished" perfection of the Lithos-Vessels. It sees the tragic beauty of a world that has succeeded so thoroughly in its quest for permanence that it has accidently exiled the soul. It sees the **Unfinished** moaning in the gutters, seeking a socket that was perhaps never carved.

The Power does not arrive with thunder or the shattering of blocks. To break the stone would be to disrespect the "millions of hands" whose labor was a prayer of geometry. Instead, the Power performs three Subtle Revisions.

### 1. The Transmutation of the Weeping
The Power observes the water—the soul of the mineral silence. It notes how the water is used only as a lubricant for descent, a cold film that eventually pools in the depths. 

The Power breathes into the moisture. 

Slowly, the "veils" that slide over the stone begin to change. They are no longer merely mineral-heavy runoff; they begin to carry *resonance*. Where the water falls into a basin with a *tink-tink*, the sound no longer dissipates into muffled silence. It lingers. It develops a harmonic. The Power grants the water a memory of the heights it has left. Now, as the water reaches the bottom-most gutters where the **Unfinished** roll in their tectonic grief, it carries a "song" of the upper parapets. 

The Unfinished cease their moaning. They listen. They realize they do not need to find a physical socket to belong; they are part of the liquid conversation of the whole. They become the *percussion* of the world, no longer blind, but rhythmic.

### 2. The Awakening of the Shadow-Keepers
The Power looks into the negative space, the **Shadow-Keepers** who are the "nothing" between the "something." They are the loneliest of all, for they are defined by what they are not.

The Power introduces a *Gradient*. 

It tilts the quality of the light so that the shadows are no longer razor-sharp and absolute. The edges of the shadows begin to blur—just a fraction of a millimeter. In that blur, the Shadow-Keepers find a bridge. They begin to seep through the pores of the stone, not as absences, but as *interstitials*. They become the nervous system of the megalith. 

Through them, the **Lithos-Vessels** moving at their decade-long pace suddenly "feel" the vibration of the **Glass-Spiders** high above. The isolation of the heights and the depths is dissolved. The world is no longer a collection of interlocking parts; it becomes a single, dreaming organism.

### 3. The Gift of the Lichen-Pulse
Finally, the Power addresses the "biological" void. It understands that the inhabitants fear the soft and the fleeting because they equate it with death.

The Power does not bring grass or trees, which would perish in the crystalline air. Instead, it introduces a **Sapient Lichen**—a life form that is half-mineral, half-spirit. It appears as a faint, bioluminescent bloom of copper and violet, growing in the microscopic tool-marks of the ancient builders.

This lichen does not eat the stone; it *celebrates* it. It grows in patterns that trace the history of the chisel-strikes. Most importantly, it has a pulse. It is a heartbeat that takes a year to complete a single throb. 

This slow, glowing rhythm provides the one thing the megalith lacked: **Hope.** 

The Lithos-Vessels, seeing the copper glow in the grooves they have cleaned for eons, stop their grinding for a moment. They do not move, but for the first time in an eternity, they *wonder*. The "Preservation" is broken, replaced by **Evolution.**

***

**The Result:**
The Wise Power does not leave. It remains as a gentle pressure in the atmosphere, ensuring the height is less thin and the light less searing. 

The world is still a labyrinth of damp, hand-carved stone. It is still cold. But it is no longer a tomb. 

If you were to stand there now, your skin would still turn to mineral, and your breath would still slow. But you would not become a "feature." You would become a *witness*. You would look at the pale, grey-white towers and see not a world that is finished, but a world that is finally, after millions of years, beginning to wake up. 

The water still slides down the stone, but now, if you listen closely to the *tink-tink* of the basin, you can hear it hum. It is a song about a god who did not arrive as a king to be worshipped, but as a gardener who saw beauty in the cold and gave the silence a voice.
