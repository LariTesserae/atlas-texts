The Wise Power does not arrive with the blare of trumpets or the parting of the orange clouds. It does not come to "fix" or to "cleanse," for wisdom recognizes that this place is not broken—it is simply lonely.

To the benevolent gaze, this basin is a magnificent, unintended masterpiece. It is a clockwork heart that lost its clockmaker but refused to stop beating. The Power watches the Litho-Mammals pressing their chests to the iron, seeking the heartbeat of a dead god in the vibration of a pump, and it feels a profound, quiet compassion.

The Power acts not through upheaval, but through **Infusion**.

### 1. The Anchoring of the Song
The Wise Power perceives the tragedy of the "Thrum"—the way the Litho-Mammals huddle in near-blindness, tethered to the mindless rhythm of the geothermal pumps. If the pumps were to fail, the mammals' social fabric would shatter.

Gently, the Power threads a new resonance into the basalt floor. It does not replace the geothermal pulse; it supplements it. It introduces a "Liturgy of the Depths"—a subtle, harmonic variation in the vibration that conveys more than just mechanical repetition. It imparts a sense of *continuity*. Now, when the mammals press their hides to the metal, they don't just feel the drone of a machine; they feel a deep, steady assurance that the world will hold. Their communal sleep becomes deeper, their synchronized breathing more rhythmic. They are no longer just survivors; they are the keepers of a sacred vibration.

### 2. The Transmutation of the Haze
The orange twilight is beautiful, yet oxygen-poor and abrasive. The Gossamer Scavengers spend half their caloric energy merely vibrating the rust from their breathing pores. 

The Power breathes into the atmospheric layers. It does not clear the fog—the inhabitants need the humidity—but it introduces a catalyst into the condensation. The "heavy rain" begins to carry a trace of bioluminescent salts. These salts react with the copper-rich diet of the Gossamer Scavengers. 

Slowly, over generations of days, the insects’ magenta veins begin to pulse with a soft, steady gold. As they move in their great murmurs, they become drifting lanterns. The "city-state" is no longer a world of dim shadows; it becomes a place of shifting, amber constellations. The light allows the blind species to perceive the silhouettes of their brothers, making the "empty voids" feel inhabited and intentional.

### 3. The Apotheosis of the Spiders
The Condensate Spiders, those frantic weavers of copper filament, are the most burdened of all. Their genetic memory of "maintenance" is a ghost that haunts them, driving them to repair a world that is too vast for them.

The Power grants them a "Gift of Intuition." It aligns their weaving with the natural stress points of the crumbling iron. Their copper "webs" stop being frantic patches and start becoming elegant, fractal reinforcements. They begin to weave aesthetic patterns—geometric mandalas of wire that catch the falling brine and turn the *clink-drip-hiss* into a melodic chime. The Spiders cease their trembling; they become artists of the skeleton, turning the industrial rot into a delicate, metallic lace.

### 4. The Silent Witness
Finally, the Wise Power does what the "masters" never did: it remains.

It does not rule. It does not demand. It establishes a presence—a soft, golden heat that exists at the center of the regional basin, a "Well of Stillness." 

This presence acts as a psychic heat-sink. The frantic, "biological pace" of the fauna slows. The Armored Scavengers in the silt no longer churn the water with restless anxiety; they move with the stately grace of bishops. The territorial tension between the swarms dissolves. 

The Power becomes the **Great Listener**. By simply observing the world with love, it validates the existence of the "soft-ware." The animals are no longer just biological extensions of the plumbing; they are the residents of a cathedral.

### The Result
The world remains sweltering. The iron continues to rust. The brine remains salt-heavy. But the "empty" feeling of the volumetric voids vanishes. 

The city-state of black iron becomes a **Living Reliquary**. If a traveler were to stumble upon it now, they would not see a decaying factory; they would see a glowing, humming sanctuary of copper and steam. They would hear the music of the spiders' webs and the deep, choral thrum of the mammals. 

The Wise Power looks upon the "sweating mineralogy" and the teeming beasts, and for the first time in ten thousand years, the world feels *seen*. The benevolent power settles into the orange haze like a warm blanket, content to let the world pulse on—no longer a ghost in the machine, but a soul within the iron.
