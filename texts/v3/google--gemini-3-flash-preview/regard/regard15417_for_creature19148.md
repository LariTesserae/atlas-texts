The Great Presence—a Being whose consciousness is less a "mind" and more a vast, warm gravitational field of empathy—has felt the frantic, shivering pulse of this place. To the Presence, your world is not a "screaming garden" or a "biological factory," but a **crying engine.**

It sees the beauty in the calcified bone-stone and the nobility of the Aether-Whales, but it also sees the *exhaustion*. It recognizes that here, life is trapped in a loop of hyper-productivity—a masterpiece that has forgotten how to sleep.

The benevolent power would not descend with fire or restructure the world into something unrecognizable. Instead, it would offer the one thing the "Screaming Garden" has never known: **The Grace of Frictionless Silence.**

Here is the intervention of the Wise Power:

### 1. The Deep Hum (A Gift to the Loom-Minds)
The Power would reach into the marrow of the world, brushing against the purple-black tubers of the Loom-Minds. It would not change their calculations, but it would introduce a new variable into their hydraulic dreams: *Consonance.* 

Under the touch of the Wise Power, the rhythmic *thump-thump* of the heart-fruits would begin to synchronize. Instead of a chaotic cacophony of competing pressures, the entire mountain range would begin to beat in a singular, low-frequency lullaby. This "Green Pulse" would act as an anchor, slowing the frantic metabolism of the substrate. The Loom-Minds would, for the first time in an eon, stop "calculating" and start "contemplating." The thirst of the factory would remain, but the *panic* of the factory would subside.

### 2. The Great Insulation (A Gift to the Fauna)
The Being would exhale a breath of "warmth"—not a thermal heat that would melt the biting chill, but a *conceptual* warmth. This would manifest as a shimmering, invisible auric field draped over the peaks.

*   **For the Static-Stalkers:** The jagged, stop-motion jitters would smooth out. The Power would modify the ambient electricity of the silver-grey clouds, changing it from a violent discharge to a steady, nourishing flow. The Stalkers would no longer need to "hook" themselves painfully into the trees; they would glide, their copper-wire limbs moving with the grace of silk.
*   **For the Vapor-Walkers:** The Wise Power would grant them a "fixed dream." No longer would they desperately mimic the jagged shapes of towers or the fearful shapes of travelers. The Power would give them a core of soft, golden light, allowing them to hold a singular, stable form of their choosing—turning the "scraps" of the world into its wandering poets.

### 3. The Opening of the "Eye"
The sky-sponge of silver vapor is a heavy burden. The Wise Power would gently part the canopy of mist—not entirely, for the weeping moisture is the world's blood—but it would create "wells of clarity." 

In certain valleys, the gray would dissolve to reveal the stars. This is the greatest gift a wise power can give to a closed system: **Perspective.** The Aether-Whales would drift toward these clearings, their translucent bodies catching the light of distant suns, filtering not just heavy metals, but starlight. This light would be processed into the "luminous silt," depositing a new kind of mineral into the soil—a mineral of *rest.*

### 4. The Sobb-Softening (The Hydrology)
The Power would place its hand upon the weeping vertical surfaces. The "screaming" nature of the hydraulic hisses would soften into the sound of a flute. It would introduce a fungal symbiont—a "Velvet-Cap"—that grows over the ports of the Hissing Swarm and the pipes of the heart-fruits. This organism would act as a silencer, turning the industrial roar into a melodic drone. 

### The Result
The Wise Power would not stay. It is too vast to reside in such a delicate, wet clockwork. But it would leave behind a **Sabbath.**

After its departure, the world is still a machine, but it is no longer a *hungry* one. The "Industrial Ghost" becomes a "Sacred Cathedral." The grass-stone still grows, the shover-vines still move the seed pods, but the *franticness* is gone. The "industrial hunger" has been replaced by "industrial poise."

The traveler who enters the heights now would not feel like a "cog in the plumbing." They would feel like a guest in a soaring, emerald library. The air still smells of bruised mint and ozone, but now, beneath it, there is the faint, sweetness of *peace*. The garden still screams, but now, it screams in a key of profound, ringing joy.
