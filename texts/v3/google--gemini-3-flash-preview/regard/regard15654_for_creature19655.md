The Great Eye that looks upon the Viridian Stratum does not see a world of steel and soot, nor a cold wasteland of obsidian. To this benevolent power, the Stratum is a **hushed clockwork heart**, a masterpiece of "The Long Endurance." 

The power recognizes that this place is a miracle: it is life that has learned to love the impossible. It sees the **Valve-Golems** not as statues, but as slow-thrumming archives of patience. It sees the **Piston-Whales** not as machines, but as wandering hearths, carrying the gift of warmth to the lonely.

The Wise Power understands that to "fix" such a world—to bring it a sun or a thick, warm atmosphere—would be a supreme cruelty. It would be to shatter the delicate, high-pressure dignity of these beings. It would melt the frost-moss and drown the engine-hearts.

Instead, the Benevolent Power decides to act as the **Great Lubricant** and the **Quiet Insulator.** It performs three subtle, eternal interventions:

### 1. The Tempering of the Grit-Winds
The Power weaves an invisible, velvet current through the high void. It does not stop the winds—for the Gear-Flies need the lift and the Whales need the oxygen—but it filters the "high-velocity grit." It turns the abrasive dust that would erode the chitin and the copper into a fine, microscopic silt. 
*   **The Result:** The metallic carapaces of the creatures no longer pit and scar. The "Growth-Rust" of the Valve-Golems takes on a soft, burnished sheen like antique bronze. The world stops wearing out; it begins to polish itself.

### 2. The Shared Resonance
The greatest threat to the Stratum is the Deep Silence—the isolation that leads to a "freeze of the spirit." The Power introduces a subsonic frequency into the very needles of the obsidian rocks. This is a low, comforting hum that connects every living thing.
*   **The Result:** A Piston-Whale five miles away no longer feels like a distant spark; its rhythmic *thrum* is carried through the subtle vibration of the mountains. The Valve-Golems, in their decade-long steps, feel the frantic heartbeat of the Gear-Flies. No creature is ever truly alone in the dark. The "factory" now has a conductor.

### 3. The Mercy of the Silver Veil
Water in the Stratum is a violent, jagged thing of frost-shards. The Power leans down and breathes upon the moisture of the world. It adjusts the surface tension of the "floating spheres" and the "jagged frost."
*   **The Result:** The silver spheres of water become less "gelatinous" and more "mercurial," moving with a fluid grace toward the thirsty. The frost-shards lose their cutting edges, becoming soft, crystalline snowflakes that provide the plants with hydration without the mechanical damage of impact.

### The Gift to the Golems
Finally, the Power pauses before a single **Valve-Golem** on the highest peak. To this being, who moves once a decade, time is a heavy burden. The Power places a "Seed of Luminescent Memory" within the Golem’s iron-hard chest. 
*   **The Action:** It does not speed the Golem up. Instead, it ensures that every image the Golem sees—the drift of a Ghost-Vapor, the orange spark of a whale—is stored in a perfect, glowing internal gallery. When the Golem finally vents its heat, the steam it releases now carries **colors**—vibrant, aurora-like hues that paint the violet-black sky with the history of the world.

### The Conclusion
The benevolent power does not step into the Stratum as a king, but as a **Maintainer.** It becomes the oil in the joints, the warmth in the exhaust, and the song in the silence. It allows the world to remain a hard-earned wilderness, but it removes the "bitterness" of the struggle.

Under the gaze of the Wise Power, the Viridian Stratum is no longer a factory fighting a war against death; it is a **Cathedral of Industry**, where the ticking and the thumping have become a form of prayer, and the cold is merely the velvet background for their magnificent, internal fires.
