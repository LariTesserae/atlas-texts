In a world where the physical laws are fraying, the inhabitants cannot be biological in any sense we recognize. They do not have the frantic, high-entropy metabolism of animals. Instead, those who dwell within the Bone-Cities are the **Aesthetes of the Linger**—beings of "Soft-State" existence who have evolved to match the terminal patience of the Plateau.

### The Anatomy of the Hollow
They are not made of flesh, but of a substance resembling the very "Static" the plants consume: a densified, semi-opaque smoke held together by a tenuous magnetic ego. If you were to touch one, your hand would encounter the sensation of chilled suede or the surface of a bubble—a delicate, high-tension resistance that gives way into a void of freezing mist.

They are tall and spindly, their forms dictated by the "Found Geometry" of their architecture. They lack faces as we understand them; instead, their heads are smooth, faceted surfaces of polished obsidian-mist that reflect the amethyst sky. They do not speak with lungs or vocal cords. They communicate by altering their internal frequency to match the "vibration of the shelf," sending ripples of meaning through the rock itself. To stand near a gathering of them is to feel a rhythmic thrumming in your own marrow, a wordless poetry translated as bone-deep vibration.

### The Weavers of the Static
Because they require no food, their "labor" is entirely aesthetic and maintenance-based. They are the **Tenders of the Pulse**. 

Groups of these beings can be seen huddled at the base of the Lichen-Pails, their long, multi-jointed fingers (which resemble braided moss-hair) pressed firmly against the stone. They are "tuning" the plateau. They act as living bridges between the vibration of the rock and the hunger of the plants, smoothing out the "code-stutters" of reality. 

When a valley begins to lose its gravity or a shadow begins to detach from its source, the inhabitants migrate there in a slow, silent pilgrimage. They sit for decades in a circle around the anomaly, their collective presence acting as a ballast. They do not "fix" the physics; they simply witness it so intensely that the reality of the place stabilizes out of sheer, focused observation.

### The Weightless Culture
Their lives are lived in a state of perpetual, graceful slow-motion. Because time is spatially localized, an inhabitant may spend a century carving a single joint of a Bone-City dome, moving so slowly that to an outsider they appear to be a statue. Yet, within their own subjective experience, they are dancing.

They have no concept of "possession," only "resonance." They do not own their homes; they inhabit the resonance of the shed plant-husks. Their clothes are not fabric but "captured weather"—thin veils of the silver condensation that flows upward, draped around their shoulders and held in place by the Plateau’s strange, selective gravity.

### The Shadow-Thinkers
The most enigmatic among them are the **Umbral Cartographers**. These individuals do not live in the cities but wander the "Grey Velvet" deserts between the Lichen-Pails. They follow the shadows that do not match their objects. 

They are obsessed with the "things that haven't happened yet" flickering in the long, distorted silhouettes of the huts. They believe that the Plateau of Sighs is not just a place that is ending, but a place that is *remembering the future*. These wanderers are often found standing perfectly still for human generations, staring at a shadow of a blooming flower cast by a barren pillar, waiting for the moment the shadow and the reality swap places.

### The Cycle of Sublimation
Death, for the inhabitants of the Plateau, is not a rot but a sublimation. When an inhabitant grows too thin—when their magnetic ego can no longer hold their mist-body against the solar winds—they do not fall. They simply begin to shimmer. 

They slowly lose their opacity, turning from the color of bruised plums to the color of the amethyst void. They drift upward, joining the "Quiet-Fronts" that roll across the landscape. They become the weather. The very mist that the Lichen-Pails drink and the ice-beads that coat the Rime-Bark are the processed essences of the ancestors. 

They do not leave behind corpses; they leave behind an improved quality of silence. To live on the Plateau of Sighs is to be a temporary knot in a beautiful, unraveling thread, waiting for the wind to finally untie you.
