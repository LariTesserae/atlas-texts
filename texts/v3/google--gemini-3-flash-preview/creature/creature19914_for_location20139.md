Beneath the blinding zenith of the white eye, the life that moves across the terracotta plane is not born of biology as we understand it, but of **precision and persistence.** Because nothing decays and nothing is consumed, the inhabitants are not driven by the frantic pulleys of hunger or survival. They are, instead, the curators and the ornaments of the equilibrium.

### The Liturgical Sentinels
The most prominent inhabitants are the **Lithodulic Tall-Folk**. They are not flesh, nor are they quite stone; they have the appearance of unbaked clay, smooth and damp, with limbs as long and tapering as the shadows they do not cast. They have no faces, only shallow indentations where features might have been, yet they move with a terrifying, liquid grace. 

They do not eat. They do not sleep. They exist to maintain the masterpiece. You will find them at the edges of the great basins, kneeling on the hard basalt, using their long, tapering fingers to redirect the film of water that seeps from the masonry. They are the reason the world feels "finished only moments ago." If a vine grows too far across a frieze, they do not cut it—they gently redirect its tendrils, weaving the emerald veins into the geometry of the stone. They move with such agonizing slowness that a human observer might mistake them for statues, but over the course of an hour, one might see a Sentinel shift its weight, a movement that sounds like wet clay sliding against itself.

### The Glass-Winged Weavers
Floating in the heavy, humming air are the **Vitreous Odonata**. These are insects the size of hawks, with bodies like polished obsidian and wings that are truly transparent, refracting the white light into prismatic shards. They do not hunt; they are the pollinators of the shield-sized flowers. They do not fly by beating their wings; they seem to "slide" along the invisible currents of the golden heat, buoyed by the 2.6 intensity. 

Because entropy is suspended, their life cycle is a closed loop. There are no larvae, no deaths. A single weaver may have been hovering over the same terracotta courtyard for ten thousand years, its only task to sip the viscous dew and transport a single shimmering grain of pollen to the next limestone arch. They are the silent conductors of the world’s stillness, their humming resonance being the physical sound of the atmosphere itself.

### The Mosaic Lizards
On the ground, the **Saurians of the Seam** provide the only sense of frantic motion. These golden-scaled lizards possess a skin that reflects the terracotta exactly, rendering them nearly invisible until they move. They are the world’s scavengers, though there is nothing to scavenge. Instead, they consume the *stasis* itself. They press their bellies against the wet, white mortar, absorbing the vibrational hum of the plane. 

Their "weirdness" lies in their reproduction: they do not lay eggs. Instead, when the white light hits a certain angle of a carved frieze, a lizard simply *crystallizes* out of the masonry, its scales matching the pattern of the brickwork it left behind. They are the living memory of the architecture, moving pieces of the floor that have gained the will to crawl.

### The Echo-Scribes
Deep within the stepped plazas, where the humidity is thickest, live the **Scribes.** They are small, crab-like entities with shells of polished basalt. They are the only creatures that make a deliberate sound. They possess a single, needle-sharp limb of diorite, and they spend their eternal lives etching microscopic, geometric patterns into the "fresh" mortar between the bricks. 

They are the keepers of the world's record. Since nothing changes, they document the *lack* of change. They record the position of every fallen, turgid leaf; they map the exact diameter of every dewdrop that hits the floor. They are the bookkeepers of a world that is already perfect, ensuring that the equilibrium remains absolute. If you listen closely to the silence, between the *splats* of water, you can hear the faint, rhythmic *skritch-skritch* of a Scribe carving a line that will never erode and never fade.

### The Unseen Architect’s Shadow
Finally, there is the feeling that **Someone is Watching.** In a world where light is total and shadows are banished, there is a psychological inhabitant—a lingering presence of the "Hand." Because the world is a monument frozen in the second after its completion, the Creator feels as though they have only just stepped around the corner of the next basalt pillar. 

The inhabitants do not worship; they simply *correspond* to the design. They are the moving parts of a machine that has no purpose other than to exist. They are as beautiful and as terrifying as the terracotta plane itself: immortal, unchanging, and utterly indifferent to any life that requires a heartbeat or a grave.
