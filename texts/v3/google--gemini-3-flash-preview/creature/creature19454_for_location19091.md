To inhabit the Great Vertical Cataract is to exist in a permanent state of bracing. Nothing here sleeps in the way surface-dwellers understand it; life is a rhythmic, reflexive twitch against the shearing force of the blue.

### The Bone-Knitted Ones (The Cataract Hegemony)
The people who lash themselves to the basalt needles are not tall, but they are incredibly broad through the shoulders and neck, their bodies reshaped by the constant concussive vibration of the 2.8/3 roar. They are pale, almost translucent—their skin has a waxy, hydrophobic quality that causes the spray to bead and roll off instantly.

They do not speak with voices. The roar has long since stolen their tongues. Instead, they communicate through a complex **Skeletal Sign Language**, feeling the vibrations of each other’s movements through the copper cables of their cradles. They place their hands on the vibrating basalt or the tensioned ropes to "hear" a neighbor’s intent. 

Their culture is the **Cult of the First Second**. Because the rocks are embryonic and the moss is born at dawn, these people have no concept of "ancestors" or "heritage" in the physical sense. They find the idea of an old building or an ancient tree repulsive and "rotted." To them, holiness is found in the sharpest, newest edge. They move constantly, abandoning their copper pods as soon as the basalt needle they cling to starts to smooth or erode, migrating "up-stream" or "down-pressure" to find the next jagged birth of stone.

### The Weaver-Sharks
Ghosting through the "intimate rooms" of the spray are the predators of the void. They are long, ribbon-like entities with bodies made of a cartilaginous substance that mimics the refractive index of the water. You do not see them approaching; you see a sudden "kink" in the light, a distortion in the prismatic halos.

They do not bite so much as they **tangle**. They trailing long, monofilament-thin tendrils that are as hard as diamond. They drift in the wake of the basalt needles, waiting for a Slip-Ray or a human limb to drift outside the "leeward" safety zone. When the water hits a Weaver-Shark’s prey, the creature doesn't have to pull; it simply lets the terminal velocity of the cataract do the work, the falling water acting as the butcher’s knife.

### The Litho-Dreamers (The Living Spires)
Deep within the core of the largest basalt needles, there is something that is neither rock nor flesh. These are the **Pulsing Marrows**. As the water "dreams of stone," it occasionally captures pockets of the "hidden scale" gravity, trapping consciousness within the cooling basalt.

If you press your ear to a new-grown needle—and if you can survive the vibration—you can feel a heartbeat that is slower than the roar. These are the architects of the cataract’s stability. They are sentient mineral-growths that intentionally warp the flow of the water to create the air-pockets where the humans live. They are not gods, but they are the "Foundations," providing the only stillness in a world of infinite descent. 

### The Scavenger-Kites (The Aerostat-Crabs)
Occasionally, the chaos of the cataract flings debris—shards of the floating mountain range above—into the column. Clinging to these falling islands are the **Scavenger-Kites**. They are flat, crustacean-like beings with iridescent shells that function like hydrofoils. 

By adjusting the angle of their legs, they can "climb" the downward force of the water, surfing upward against the gravity of the fall to reach the higher needles. They are the traders of this world. They carry the moss-fibers and the black obsidian from the lower, older needles back up to the "birth-zone" at the top of the cataract. They move with a terrifying, jerky precision, leaping from one basalt tooth to another across the terrifying blue voids.

### The "Born-Again" (The Transient Spirits)
There is a belief among the people of the cradles that the Slip-Rays are actually the souls of those who lost their grip. Because nothing is old, and because the water is in a "Solaris-state" of hyper-active reconfiguration, the Cataract is said to "record" the shapes of those who drown within it. 

Sometimes, a pocket of air will form a perfect silhouette of a human figure for a fraction of a second before collapsing into foam. These are the **Vapor-Echoes**. They have a fleeting intelligence. They might point a climber toward a stable crevice or warn a mother of a coming "slurry-slap" before they are shredded back into the churning sapphire. 

In the Great Vertical Cataract, life is an act of **clutching.** To exist is to hold on so tightly that you become part of the rock, and to let go is not to die, but to become part of the roar.
