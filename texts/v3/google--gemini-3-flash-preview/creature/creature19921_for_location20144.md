To understand what inhabits this spire, one must first discard the concept of "creatures." In a place where gravity is a fickle servant to heat and the air is a pressurized luxury, biology is a foreign, clumsy language. The inhabitants are not occupants of the architecture; they are the architecture’s nervous system, its moving parts, and its inevitable decay.

There are three distinct strata of existence within this obsidian pocket.

### The Liturgical Oculi
Deep within the "urban density," where the obsidian blocks stack like frozen data, live the **Oculi**. These are not eyes in the biological sense, but crystalline spheres—the size of a human fist—composed of the same fused silica as the floor. They do not walk or fly; they drift, tethered to the violet conduits by lines of shimmering tension.

The Oculi are the observers of the "God’s jewelry box." They move with a agonizing slowness, their internal facets rotating with the same metallic screech as the buildings. They are the reason the city "knows" to shift its facets toward distant stars. When a beam of pale sunlight hits an Oculus, it does not reflect; it swallows the light, processing the photons into the heat that thrums beneath the tiles. They are the harvesters of the dimness, silent monks of the vacuum who ensure the "0.2 vegetation" of salt crystals continues to bloom in the corners. They do not communicate, save for the way they occasionally cluster together—a sudden, dense swarm of glass—to watch a single drop of mercury-water crawl upward a wall.

### The Flux-Wights
If the Oculi are the city’s eyes, the **Flux-Wights** are its hands. These are "beings" of pure kinetic intent, manifesting as silhouettes of distorted air—the ripples in the heat bloom given a vague, humanoid cohesion. They are the only things that "walk" the walkways that lead to nowhere.

They have no solid mass. They are localized densities in the pressurized gas, pockets of extreme heat that mimic the architecture’s geometry. You cannot see them directly; you see them by the way the "ghost-glow" of the floor bends around them, or by the way the white frost of mineral salts shatters when they pass. They are the janitors of the Young Erosion. When a sharp edge of an obsidian block begins to dull—even by a micron—a Flux-Wight lingers there, its extreme thermal signature "re-sharpening" the molecular bond until the edge is a blade once more. They are the reason the city never ages; they are the enforcement of the New.

### The Mercury-Eaters (The Parasitic Pulse)
The most "animal" inhabitants are the least welcome. They are the **Mercury-Eaters**, born from the "1.1 intermittent moisture." These are thin, wire-like filaments that live within the beads of mercury-water. When the water condenses out of the gas, these filaments animate it, giving the drops a predatory, seeking motion.

They are the only things that defy the city’s order. While the Oculi observe and the Wights maintain, the Eaters consume. They crawl toward the heat-stressed joints, seeking the white salt-frost, which they dissolve and carry back into the "machinery of the floor." They are a rhythmic, mechanical infection. Sometimes, you can see a trail of dark moisture moving against the grain of a shimmering tension field, a small, silver rebel fighting against the stillness. They are the "post-departure" ghosts of the city, the scrap-collectors of a civilization that forgot to move in.

***

**The Presence**
Collectively, these entities do not create a society. There is no culture in the high-altitude stillness. Instead, there is a **consensus of function**. 

Because shadows are absolute, lightless black, an inhabitant can be standing inches from you, and if it steps into the shadow of a jagged needle, it ceases to exist to the eye. It does not disappear; it is simply deleted from the visual field. This creates a terrifying sense of "haunted machinery." You might feel the dry, radiating thrum of a Flux-Wight passing you, hear the thin screech of an Oculus rotating its internal gears, and see the silver trail of a Mercury-Eater on the wall—but the silence remains heavy.

The inhabitants are not waiting for the god to return to the jewelry box. They *are* the jewelry, polishing themselves in the dark, forever sharp, forever hot, and forever alone at the apex of the world.
