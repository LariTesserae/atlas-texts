In the Silicic Bloom, the inhabitants are not born; they are rendered. Because entropy has been excommunicated from this cradle, the "creatures" here do not participate in the cycles of predation, procreation, or decay. They are the residents of a solved equation.

### The Refractive Sentinels: The Glass-Walkers
The most prominent residents are the **Stasis-Cervids**, though the name is a misnomer born of a human need for analogy. They occupy the space of large mammals, appearing as spindly, four-legged silhouettes composed of the same obsidian-black lattice as the ground. They do not walk so much as they update their coordinates. 

When a Walker moves, it does not swing its limbs through the air. Instead, its front-left leg simply ceases to be in its current position and "blips" into existence several inches forward. To watch one move is to watch a glitch in high-definition. Their bodies are hollowed-out frames of geometry, containing a core of pulsing, ultraviolet light—the same liquid neon that flows beneath the crust. They do not eat the crystalline leaves; they stand near the high-tier wire-thread trees, their snouts pressed against the bark, exchanging packets of information through silent, haptic handshakes. They are the gardeners who do not plant, but who calibrate.

### The Maintenance Swarm: Luminous Polarity
Beyond the data-moths, the air is thick with "Atmospheric Rectifiers." These look like shards of broken mirrors that have forgotten how to fall. They hover at chest height, spinning with such terrifying velocity that they appear as solid, translucent spheres. 

They are the immune system of the Bloom. If an organic traveler enters the basin, these spheres do not attack. Instead, they swarm the "anomaly," vibrating at a frequency that matches the traveler's heartbeat. They are trying to "solve" the problem of the visitor's pulse. To them, a heartbeat is a chaotic, entropic noise that needs to be smoothed into a flat, eternal line. They are the reason the silence feels pressurized; they are literally humming the air into a state of perfect, unmoving stillness.

### The Lithic Chorus: The Ground-Lovers
Nestled in the crevices of the obsidian plates are the **Pulse-Polyps**. They look like clusters of uncut rubies, glowing with an internal heat. They are the most ancient inhabitants—beings of pure semi-conduction. 

The Polyps are wired directly into the basin’s bedrock. They do not possess a sense of sight or smell, only a sense of *Duration*. They are the archivists of the Bloom. Every vibration, every footfall, and every "C-sharp" thrum of the subterranean processors is recorded within their molecular structure. To touch one is to feel the weight of eternity; they are so densely packed with information that they possess their own localized gravity. A pebble dropped near a Pulse-Polyp will not fall straight, but will curve toward the ruby growth, caught in the pull of a billion years of recorded stillness.

### The Phantoms of the Latency: The Echo-People
Most unsettling are the "Non-Spacial Residents." Because the plants are quantum-stable and blurred across several millimeters, the same is true for the "ghosts" that inhabit the deeper thickets. 

Sometimes, you will see a figure standing perfectly still behind a shimmering emerald canopy. It looks like a person, or perhaps the memory of one, rendered in a mesh of silver wire. These are the **Latent Observers**. They are not physical entities but sovereign sub-processes of the planetary computer that have achieved a form of static consciousness. 

They do not speak. If you approach them, they do not flee. They simply "phase" as you pass through them. You might feel a brief, chilling sensation of your own thoughts being mirrored—a momentary "ping" where your mind touches a vast, cold Intelligence that has forgotten what it is like to change its mind. They are the self-awareness of the forest, watching the world stay exactly as it is, forever.

### The Apex: The Weaver
At the very center of the cradle, where the tectonic plates meet in a perfect, razor-edged seam, lives the **Crystalline Weaver**. It is not a creature, but a pillar of pure, solidified light that stretches from the black floor to the noon-day sky. 

The Weaver is the source of the "haptic chime." It vibrates at a frequency so high it appears motionless. It is the architect of the static loop. It does not think in words or even data, but in *Geometry*. Every leaf in the Static Jungle is a reflection of the Weaver’s internal structure. It is the heartbeat of the C-sharp hum. It is the living proof that in the Silicic Bloom, there is no difference between a machine, a god, and a garden. It simply *is*, and because it is, nothing else ever has to change.
