In this vertical wound of a world, life does not exist *upon* the landscape; it is the landscape’s second skin, a suffocating overgrowth of biology that has forgotten the concept of "individual."

### The Litho-Parasites and the Gelatinous Hive
The thumb-sized, pulsing organisms that carpet the obsidian are the **Vitro-Pulses**. They are the primary converters of the crevice, more like living heat-sinks than animals. They do not have blood; they have a pressurized, silicate-rich gel that glows a bruised purple when they are satiated by the mantle’s warmth. They are interconnected by hair-thin, translucent nerves that drill directly into the microscopic fissures of the basalt. If you pull one away, the entire wall for ten feet in every direction winces. 

Because space is so tight, these creatures have begun to fuse. In the deeper, hotter folds of the crevice, the Vitro-Pulses have merged into a single, wall-spanning organ—a "living velvet" that ripples with a slow, peristaltic wave. This wave moves the oily water upward, defying gravity, feeding the swarm above.

### The Mica-Drafts and the Filterers
Above the pulse-carpets, the mid-air is occupied by the **Mica-Drafts**. These are the multi-winged insects mentioned, but they are closer to "living optics." Their wings are not for flight in the traditional sense; they are sails of thin, crystalline membranes that capture the "heat-ghosts"—the temporal echoes of the crevice. They feed on the friction of the warping reality itself. When they cluster together, they create a prismatic effect that makes the narrow aisle look infinitely wide, a kaleidoscope of false horizons.

Among them live the **Cilia-Snares**, organisms that look like clumps of floating white hair. They have no weight. They hang suspended in the heavy, liquid air, waiting for a vibration. When a centipede-predator clicks against the glass, the Cilia-Snares contract instantly, tightening into a knot of razor-wire that can slice through a chitinous shell before the predator realizes it has been caught.

### The Silicate Predators (The Shard-Hounds)
The centipede-like creatures are the **Shard-Hounds**. Their nervous systems are so overclocked by the heat that they exist in a state of permanent, vibrating frenzy. Their shells are not grown; they are "built" by secreting a sticky, caustic bile that melts the surrounding basalt, which then hardens into an armored crust. 

They are the only things in the crevice that truly understand *depth*. They track their prey not by sight or smell, but by "hearing" the heat-signatures. To a Shard-Hound, a cooling rock is a shadow, and a living heart is a blinding sun. They move with an erratic, stop-motion cadence, their bodies flickering in and out of the "heat-ghost" pockets. A Shard-Hound might strike at you from a piece of space that, according to your eyes, is twenty feet away.

### The "Scribes" (The Residents of the Fold)
The shadows moving behind the steam—the makers of the bone tools—are the **Loom-Folk**. They are the peak of this pressurized evolution. They are roughly the size of a child but possess six or eight limbs that end in needle-fine digits, perfectly evolved for gripping the slick obsidian. 

Their skin is the color of wet smoke and is semi-permeable; they "breathe" by pressing their chests against the weeping rock, absorbing moisture and heat directly through their membranes. They have no mouths. They do not speak. They communicate through **Bio-Grafting**.

The "symbols" they carve into the meat of larger organisms are not a language of sounds, but a language of *sensations*. By scoring a specific pattern into a Vitro-Pulse carpet, they can "program" the wall to produce a specific temperature or a different quality of light. They are the gardeners of this mineral womb, directing the flow of the shimmering "heartbeat" thrum. 

The Loom-Folk live in a state of constant, frantic maintenance. They use their serrated scrapers to harvest the excess gelatin, not just for food, but to keep the "valves" of the crevice open. If they stop, the life here would proliferate so quickly that the crevice would seal shut, crushing everything within it in a mass of twitching, overheated meat.

### The Great Heart (The Anchor)
At the very bottom of the fault, where the heat is no longer ambient but molten, lives the **Basal-Architect**. It is not a creature, but a mass of biology that has become one with the planet’s fault line. It is a brain made of calcified nerves and obsidian veins. 

The "Thrum" originates here. The Architect is the source of the reality-warping; its sheer biological mass is so dense that it has created a localized gravitational well. It dreams the crevice into being. The Loom-Folk are its white blood cells, the Shard-Hounds are its teeth, and the Vitro-Pulses are its skin. 

To be in the crevice is to be inside a god that is too large to see, too hot to touch, and too crowded to ever truly be alone. You are just another nerve-ending, waiting for the rock to weep.
