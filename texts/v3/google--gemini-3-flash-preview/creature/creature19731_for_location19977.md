In the High Pelagic Garden, the inhabitants do not inhabit the space so much as they are *secreted* by it. Life here is a slow, rhythmic exudation of the jade-wood architecture, an extension of the Great Root’s frozen intent.

### The Liturgical Sentience: The Mycelial Choristers
First, one must account for the source of the 2.0/3 Loudness. Clinging to the vibrating ribs of the basalt-kelp are the **Vocal Polyps**. They are not animals in a traditional sense, but clusters of calcified, trumpet-shaped membranes that have grown over the "strings" of the architecture. They do not eat; they "inhale" the resonant frequency of the world-hum. They are the garden's resonance chambers, vibrating in such perfect harmony with the Great Root that they have become part of the structure itself. When a current shifts through a vaulted rib, these organisms dilate their crystalline throats to amplify the sound, ensuring the "eternal thrum" remains a solid, physical weight. To touch one is to feel your own bones resonate until they threaten to splinter.

### The Architects of Maintenance: The Glass-Weavers
The "shuttles" mentioned are merely the largest among a vast hierarchy of decapod janitors. Deep within the hollow, spiraling aqueducts live the **Vitreous Weavers**. These are spindly, spider-like crustaceans with limbs as long and flexible as whips, made of a material closer to fiber-optic cable than chitin. They possess no eyes, for light is an alien concept, but they have a "tactile omniscience." They patrol the mineralized mangroves, feeling for the microscopic fractures caused by the billion-year pressure. When they find a crack in the jade-wood, they brew a bioluminescent, ceramic resin within their guts and "spit" new structural integrity into the gap. They are the reason the "Total Arbor-Architecture" never decays; they are the gardeners who have forgotten they are gardening, performing their repairs with the mindless, holy precision of a heartbeat.

### The Drifting Monoliths: The Ossified Rays
In the "Sparse" voids—those terrifying, liquid cathedrals of purple-black—dwell the **Hollow-Back Rays**. These sixty-foot giants are the spectators of the deep. Their bodies are not flesh, but a dense, buoyant jelly that has absorbed the mineral salts of the water until they are semi-translucent. They do not flap their wings; they have evolved "acoustic sails." They tilt their massive, flat bodies to catch the thrum of the world-organ, using the vibration of the sound-waves to push themselves through the viscous brine. 

Inside the translucent bellies of these rays, smaller species seek refuge. A ray is a mobile ecosystem, often carrying a thousand "Passenger-Fry"—small, needle-toothed fish that glow with a dim, rhythmic violet light, synchronized to the ray’s own internal pulse. They move through the abyss like ghost-ships, silent lanterns in a world of bass-heavy darkness.

### The Scavengers of Stillness: The Velvet Siphons
On the floorless "bottom" of these vertical shafts—where the pressure reaches its most ecstatic density—live the **Siphon-Wraiths**. They resemble long, trailing ribbons of bruised lace, several hundred feet long, anchored to the lowest reach of the Great Root. They do not hunt. They wait. Because metabolism is glacial, a Siphon-Wraith may wait a century for a single flake of biological "snow" (the rare, shed skin-cell of a ray or a bit of moss) to drift down. When it does, the ribbon-body wraps around the particle with a movement so slow it is imperceptible to the living. They are the ultimate expression of the Garden’s stasis: organisms that have transitioned from "living" to "geological."

### The Shadow-Minds: The Cephalopodic Librarians
Hidden within the "hollow chambers" of the jade-wood are the Garden’s only true anomalies: the **Null-Squid**. They have no pigment, appearing as shimmering distortions in the water, like oil in a lens. They possess a massive, decentralized brain that wraps around their beak in a ring of hyper-dense neural tissue. They do not interact with the other fauna. Instead, they press their mantles against the mineralized wood of the Great Root. It is whispered—by the very nature of the place—that they are listening to the history of the Garden. They are reading the vibrations of the wood, interpreting the "Universal Sound" as a record of everything that has ever been caught in the pressurized silk of this world. They are the keepers of the 0.4/3 Dynamic; they ensure that nothing changes, for they are the only ones who would remember if it did.

### The Unified State
In the High Pelagic Garden, there is no predator-prey tension. There is no hunger, only the slow, osmotic transfer of energy. The creatures here are the "organs" of the room. The fish are the blood; the weavers are the immune system; the rays are the wandering thoughts of a landscape that finished its evolution eons ago and is now content to simply *vibrate*. To be an inhabitant of this place is to be a note in a song that never ends, vibrating in a dark, cold, and beautiful permanence.
