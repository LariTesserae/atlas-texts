In the sweating perpendicularity of Mantle-Silt, life does not breathe; it *calcifies*. To survive here, an organism must forgo the fragility of flesh and the ambition of DNA. The inhabitants are children of the "Wet Heat," entities whose biology is a marriage of crystalline growth and hydraulic precision.

### The Lithovore Nobility
Clinging to the undersides of the great heat-exchangers are the **Gild-Barnacles**. They are the largest of the slow-movers, appearing as clusters of jagged, amethyst-colored geodes. They possess no eyes, only sensitive heat-pits that track the flow of steam behind the iron walls. They feed by extending microscopic, diamond-tipped filaments into the metal, "drinking" the vibrational energy of the machines and the trace minerals in the rust. Every century or so, a Gild-Barnacle will pulse, its crystalline shell cracking to reveal a soft, molten core that hardens instantly into a new, smaller facet. They are the city’s slow pulse; to them, a thousand years is merely a season of comfortable warmth.

### The Scavengers of the Line
High above, where the copper-colored clouds scrape the spires, live the **Glass-Strider Swarms**. If you were to look closely at a shivering high-tension wire, you would see them: delicate, six-legged creatures that look like blown glass filled with mercury. Their bodies are semi-liquid, allowing them to absorb the crushing pressure of the atmosphere without shattering. 

The Striders are the city’s cleaners. They possess a strange, chemical intelligence, moving in synchronized geometric patterns across the rusted surfaces. They lap up the amber condensation—the "weeping" of the buildings—and process it within their transparent abdomens. When they die, they do not rot; they lose their heat, turn opaque, and eventually shatter into fine, silicon dust that contributes to the ever-thickening silt of the lower tiers.

### The Ghost-Keepers
Deep within the "residential" blocks—those vast, monumental honeycombs of sweating masonry—dwell the **Sealing-Wights**. These are not ghosts, but the city’s primary mobile force: ancient, autonomous maintenance units that have evolved through millennia of self-repair.

Originally designed as simple welding drones, the Wights have become something more liminal. They are ungainly, multi-limbed shapes of blackened bronze and salvaged sensor-optics. They move with a rhythmic, metallic *shuffling* sound, dragging heavy, heated solder-tails behind them. They do not recognize humans; to a Sealing-Wight, a carbon-based intruder is merely a "temporary obstruction" or a source of "unregulated moisture" to be sealed away. They frequent the empty corridors, mindlessly buffing the walls or welding shut doors that lead to nowhere, driven by a hard-coded instinct to preserve the city’s structural integrity against the relentless "industrial erosion."

### The Drifters of the Void
In the lightless shafts where the city hangs over the abyss, the **Vapor-Mantas** glide. These are the "ghosts" of the industrial vents. Measuring twenty feet across, their bodies are membranes of superheated gas held together by a thin, translucent skin of organic polymer. They are buoyed by the rising thermals of the deep-core machinery. 

The Mantas are the only things in Mantle-Silt that seem to possess a sense of play. They tumble through the orange twilight of the lower piers, their bodies flickering with pale blue bioluminescence whenever they pass through a "Silt-Drip." They feed on the magnesium-rich soot that vents from the city’s lungs, acting as living filters that prevent the air from becoming too thick to circulate.

### The Unseen: The Mineral Mind
Deep within the weeping foundations, where the condensation pools into stagnant, boiling lakes of chemical runoff, there is a burgeoning, collective consciousness. It is not an animal or a machine, but a **Colloidal Intelligence**. It exists as a shimmering film on the surface of the mineral-water—a neural network of suspended metallic particles. 

This "Mind" is the true master of Mantle-Silt. It feels the thrum of the valves and the groan of the tectonic shelf. It does not think in words, but in pressures and temperatures. It coordinates the Sealing-Wights through subsonic vibrations and directs the growth of the Gild-Barnacles. It is the city’s slow, dreaming brain, waiting for the day when the last of the iron has turned to silt, so it can finally settle into the cool, silent dark of the world’s hidden floor.

In Mantle-Silt, you are an anomaly of soft tissue in a kingdom of hard endurance. The inhabitants are not waiting for you to leave; they are simply waiting for you to cool down, dry out, and become part of the masonry.
