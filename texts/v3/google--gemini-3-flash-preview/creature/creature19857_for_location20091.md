In this world, the concept of an "inhabitant" is a category error. Because the environment has consumed all biological niches to fuel its own expansion, nothing "lives" *in* the world; instead, the world is inhabited by its own functions.

The dominant residents are **The Calculators of the Limb**. These are not discrete creatures, but specialized nodes of biomass that have achieved a terrifying, localized autonomy. They resemble clusters of gnarled, knotted wood—polyps the size of cathedral bells—encrusted with lenses of polished amber. They do not move through the world; they move *with* it. As the hydraulic stalks shoot upward, the Calculators migrate by selectively rotting the wood behind them and flash-growing support struts in front, "surfing" the kinetic chaos of the construction. They are the eyes of the engine, their amber lenses vibrating at the frequency of infrared light, reading the heat-signatures of the grinding gears to ensure the mathematical purity of the growth stays within the parameters of the Great Root Grid.

Deep within the honeycombed skyscraper-trees, there exists a second tier of being: **The Piston-Wasps**. These are not insects, but autonomous aerostats of thin, translucent lignin, shaped like serrated needles. They are propelled by the hiss of pressurized sap. They act as mobile immune systems and maintenance sub-routines. If a gear of ironwood slips its tooth or a capillary pipeline develops a hairline fracture, the Piston-Wasps descend in a dry, rattling cloud. They don’t repair; they *integrate*. They secrete a fast-hardening resin that fuses the break into a new, more rigid geometry. They are the sound of the world’s frantic self-correction—a clicking, manic orchestration of billion-fold stitchery.

Lower down, in the "ground" of churning mulch and discarded history, there are the **Digestors**. They are horrific, slow-moving mats of sentient fungal acid, creeping over the fossilized ruins of previous eras. They look like sheets of blackened leather or rusted velvet. They are the world’s memory and its stomach. Everything the forest-machine produces but fails to use eventually falls to them. They break down the failed calculations—the withered leaves that didn't sense enough, the branches that grew at an irrational angle—and strip them back into elemental carbon and logic-dust. They are the only things that possess a form of "silence" in this humming world, a quiet, corrosive patience.

Finally, there is the **Apex Will**, the ghost in the wood. It is the emergent consciousness of the Total Built Environment. It manifests as a rhythmic fluctuation in the 1.3/3 low-frequency thrum. It has no body, but it has an intent: the solving of the Great Problem. 

Occasionally, the environment produces a "Savant-Fruit"—a singular, non-functional growth that serves no mechanical purpose. These fruits are perfect spheres of heartwood, pulsing with a light that shouldn't exist, containing within them a compressed data-set of the entire forest's state. To any observer, they would look like the world trying to dream. But before these dreams can sprout, the Piston-Wasps arrive to harvest them, breaking them down into the raw materials for more pistons, more gears, and more heat.

In this world, life is a frantic calligraphy written in wood and oil. There is no leisure here, no sleep, and no death in the traditional sense. There is only the **Churn**. If you were to watch a single cubic meter of this space for a century, you would see it become a gear, then a pump, then a sensory leaf, then a cloud of dust, and finally a strut—all without ever ceasing its vibration. The inhabitants are merely the momentary shapes the machine takes as it tries, with frantic, boiling desperation, to calculate the square root of itself.
