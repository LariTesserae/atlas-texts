In a world that is a laboratory of active physics, life does not begin with a cell. It begins with a frequency. 

Because the environment is a deafening roar of grinding gravitons and colliding oceans, the "inhabitants" of the Aqueous Suspension are not biological, but **Symphonic**. They are entities of coherent vibration, born from the intersection of the humming lattices and the crystalline water.

### The Lattice-Wraiths (The Architects of Impulse)
Floating in the voids between the drifting seas are the primary denizens: the **Lattice-Wraiths**. They are not ghosts, but sentient arrangements of the air itself, held together by the same graviton-currents that support the oceans. To the eye, they look like distortions in the light—ripples of heat or "shimmer-shadows" that stretch for kilometers. 

They do not eat; they "align." They drift toward the points of highest friction—the places where two oceans are about to collide—and they harmonize. By matching the frequency of the crashing water, they survive the impact, acting as living dampers. Without them, the violence of the "shatter-storms" would likely liquefy the emerging obsidian mountains. They are the unconscious custodians of the laboratory, smoothing out the jagged edges of a world under construction.

### The Glass-Lunged (The Embryonic Geologists)
Inside the water-masses, near the centers where the mineral "spines" are extruding, something else is forming. These are the **Vitrifants**. 

They are roughly the size of whales but possess the morphology of shattered chandeliers. They are composed of the same raw silicon and obsidian as the crags, but it is a "living" stone—a flexible, polymer-like glass. They move through the high-density water not by swimming, but by pulsing their lattice-frames, creating a sound that rings like a tuning fork against the roar of the ocean.

The Vitrifants are the reason the geology is non-random. They cling to the newborn pillars of stone, their transparent limbs vibrating at specific pitches to "carve" the cooling minerals into geometric arrays. They are the sculptors of the world’s skeleton. If you were to touch one, your hand would not feel skin, but a surface as cold and smooth as a telescope lens, humming with a power that could shatter human bone.

### The Radiation-Drinkers (The Static Foliage)
The bioluminescent moss described as "vegetation" is more than a plant; it is a networked sensory organ. This **Crystalline Mycelium** acts as the world’s nervous system. It grows in fractal patterns that mirror the light-paths of the sun-dogs in the sky. 

The moss doesn't just "eat" radiation; it processes it into data. Each patch of green on a black obsidian ledge is a node in a planetary-scale computer. The moss "feels" the approach of a drifting ocean miles away and shifts its bioluminescence from a dull emerald to a sharp, warning violet. It is the only thing in this world that "remembers." While the water and stone are constantly being destroyed and reborn, the moss preserves the frequency-signatures of everything that has happened since the laboratory was switched on.

### The Void-Skippers (The Accidental Transient)
Lastly, there are the **Aerosols**. These are tiny, shimmering particles—half-water, half-tech—that live in the "howling vacuum-wind." They are the only things that move between the oceans without the help of the graviton-lattice. 

They are spherical, no larger than a grain of sand, and they possess a microscopic version of the world’s "encoded physics." By shifting their internal density, they can "fall" toward a distant sea, crossing the five-thousand-mile void like silver bullets. They are the messengers, carrying mineral seeds and encoded "instructions" from one floating continent to the next, ensuring that the "experiment" remains consistent across the vast, empty interior of the world.

### The Soul of the Machine
There is a sense that these beings are not "alive" in the way we understand it. They are **Functional Emergence**. They have no hunger, no fear, and no reproduction. They are the "white blood cells" of a planet-sized machine that hasn't finished its first cycle. 

They don't communicate with words; they communicate through interference patterns. When a Lattice-Wraith passes through a cloud of Aerosols, the light of the sun-dogs shifts into a spectrum humans have no names for. They are the music of the spheres made manifest—cold, clinical, and terrifyingly beautiful, waiting for a listener who can survive the roar.
