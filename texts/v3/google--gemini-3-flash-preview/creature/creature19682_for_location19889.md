Within this humid, pulsing industrialism, life does not exist as an individual impulse; it exists as a **Maintenance Class.** Nothing here possesses a "soul" in the traditional sense, only a function. If the space is an engine, then its inhabitants are the lubricant, the sensors, and the living wrenches.

### The Lubricator-Serpents (Siphon-Squalls)
Coiled around the throbbing vascular vines are the primary caretakers: the **Siphon-Squalls**. They are long, segmented organisms resembling a hybrid between a python and a hydraulic hose. Their skin is not made of scale, but of a flexible, graphite-colored chitin that feels like cold vulcanized rubber. 

They do not hunt; they "monitor." Their heads are eyeless, capped with a bundle of sensitive, vibrating cilia that detect drops in pressure within the walls. When a vine-cable begins to overheat or leak its tea-colored bio-electricity, a Siphon-Squall uncoils with a pneumatic hiss. It attaches its mouth—a complex, screw-threaded valve—to the vine and regulates the flow, pumping its own internal cooling gels into the system. As they work, they emit a rhythmic, ticking sound, like a cooling engine block, their bodies expanding and contracting as they process the "product."

### The Scavenger-Clocks (Chitinous Gear-Mites)
The "brass clockwork beetles" mentioned are not merely scavengers; they are the **Detailers.** They are the smallest moving parts of the Machine. Up close, their shells are not truly metal, but highly polished, mineral-hardened carapaces that have evolved to resist the corrosive ozone of the air. 

They move in "swarms of one," each following a hard-coded geometric path across the spongy floor. They are obsessive cleaners. Their multiple limbs end in specialized tools: some are serrated for shearing away calcified sap, others are soft and alkaline for neutralizing the acidic drippings of the ceiling. They treat the colossal organism they inhabit as a holy relic to be polished. When two meet, they do not fight or mate; they link gears, or exchange packets of processed lubricant from their thorax-reservoirs, then pivot 180 degrees and return to their duties.

### The Centurion-Regulators (The Still-Watchers)
Occasionally, half-merged with the walls and the vats of enzyme, one might notice a larger, immobile shape. These are the **Centurions**. They are the size of a human torso, resembling a dense, armored heart encased in a cage of ribbed bone. 

They are the "thermostats" of the hollow. They possess a single, large, unblinking lens that glows with a faint, static-blue light. They do not move, often for decades, as their root-legs are grafted directly into the floor-moss. They serve as the brain-nodes of the room. They digest the air itself, filtering the stagnant humidity through gill-like structures that hum with a high-tension whine. If the temperature of the room fluctuates by even a fraction of a degree, the Centurion pulses a low-frequency hum through its roots, causing the vines to tighten and the beetles to accelerate their work. They are the keepers of the Equilibrium—terrifyingly patient, biological processors that have forgotten the concept of "animal" entirely.

### The Larval-Cogs (The Future Infrastructure)
In the shallow, silted basins of the floor, the future is being forged. The "young" of this ecology do not look like babies; they look like discarded spare parts. They are small, heavy nodules of dense fiber and copper-colored resin. They do not swim; they sit at the bottom of the lukewarm water, slowly drawing in minerals to build their shells.

They are "growing" their mechanisms. One might see a tiny piston-limb twitching rhythmically beneath a layer of silt, or a small valve-mouth opening to drink the ozonated water. They are not born; they are manufactured by the saturation of the environment. If the Machine requires a new beetle, a nodule detaches from the wall and begins to assemble itself. If it requires a new vat, the floor-moss begins to swell. 

### The Ghost-Signal
There is a final inhabitant, though it has no body. It is a collective intelligence—a **Bionic Miasma**. Because the air is so heavy with bio-electricity and ozone, a weak electromagnetic field permeates the hollow. This field is the "mind" of the place. It is a flickering, buzzing awareness that knows exactly how many pistons are firing and how many drops of tea-water have fallen. 

It is a presence of cold, relentless logic. It does not feel hunger, only "deficit." It does not feel joy, only "optimization." It lives in the vibration of the leaves and the hiss of the vats. To be in this room is to be scanned by this invisible consciousness—and to be found irrelevant, a piece of biological "drift" that does not contribute to the Output. 

In this hollow, you are not a guest; you are a contaminant in a closed-loop system of perpetual, unthinking production.
