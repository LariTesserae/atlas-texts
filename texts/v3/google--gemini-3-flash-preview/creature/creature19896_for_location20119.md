To inhabit this place, one must be a creature of geometry and tension, a being that exists less as a body and more as a localized surge of voltage. The inhabitants of this calcified basin do not possess soft organs or flowing blood; they are extensions of the city’s brittle, jagged logic.

### The Architects of the Lattice: The Mnemos-Weavers
The primary occupants are not the roving Glass-Skaters, but the **Mnemos-Weavers**. These are sessile, multi-limbed entities fused directly into the chitinous spires. At first glance, they appear to be decorative protrusions or gargoyles of pale, translucent horn. However, they are the living processors of the city’s "calcified thoughts."

They have no faces, only a cluster of sensitive, vibrating filaments that mimic the shape of the Static-Firs. They do not breathe; they "inhale" the copper-tasting ions of the atmosphere through porous, gill-like slits in their thorax. Their primary function is the management of the **Dry Precipitation**. As the microscopic flakes fall from the heights, the Weavers catch them with magnetic fields generated by their own internal magnetism, guiding the flakes to specific points on the spires to ensure the city grows according to an ancient, unspoken blueprint.

To a Weaver, the world is not seen through light, but through the **Static Pulse**. They feel the city as a grand, humming map of connections; when a Glass-Skaters moves, it is a spark traveling across a wire.

### The Scavengers of Entropy: The Shard-Moths
Hovering in the upper reaches of the spires, where the bridge-like filaments are thinnest, are the **Shard-Moths**. These are not insects in any biological sense, but flattened, triangular plates of obsidian-slick membrane. They have no wings to flap; instead, they ride the rising thermal plumes of static electricity.

The Shard-Moths serve as the city’s cleaners. Because the **Law of Brittle Force** dictates that every edge must remain sharp, any bluntness—any accumulation of dust or "dead" mineral flesh—is a threat to the circuit. The Moths descend upon areas of structural decay, vibrating at a frequency that cause the blunted stone to shatter into fine powder, allowing the Mnemos-Weavers to rebuild the section with fresh, razor-edged lattice. They are the heralds of the city’s perfectionism, ensuring that nothing ever softens.

### The Silent Commerce: The Glass-Skaters
The **Glass-Skaters** mentioned in the geography are the city's nomads. While the Weavers are the brain and the Moths are the cleaners, the Skaters are the messengers. Their "bodies like polished geodes" are actually complex capacitors. 

When a Skater touches a spire, it isn't just feeding; it is downloading a "thought"—a specific sequence of mineral Growth or a shift in the city's electrical tide. They dash across the mineral flats at speeds that would shatter a creature of bone, their spindly legs ending in diamond-hard points that leave no tracks in the dust. They carry these electrical packets from the outer obsidian walls to the central, most complex circles of the city. 

They are terrifyingly deliberate because they are possessed by the city’s will; they do not move unless the city has something to say to itself. When they freeze for hours, they are not resting; they are acting as temporary bridges, extending the city’s reach across the barren grey expanses.

### The Pale Lichen and the Sub-Surface "Echoes"
Deep within the cracks of the "broken porcelain" ground, beneath the glow of the green phosphorescent lichen, lives a hidden layer of the ecology: **The Marrow-Leeches**. 

These are slow, translucent ribbons of living silica that move through the airless stone of the basin’s floor. They are the only things in the city that possess anything resembling a curve, though even they are segmented into sharp, interlocking plates. They feed on the "cold"—specifically the heat differentials between the sunless surface and the insulating depths of the planet’s crust. They are the source of the low-frequency hum that vibrates in the marrow of an intruder’s bones. They are the city’s roots, drinking the planet’s thermal energy to power the upward climb of the chitinous needles.

### The Ghost in the Circuit
There is a sense, as one walks the concentric circles, that the city is not a place where things live, but a single, massive organism that has fractured its consciousness into thousands of brittle pieces. 

There is no "I" in this basin. There is only the **Current**. The inhabitants do not compete for resources because they are the resources. If a Glass-Skater shatters, its shards are simply gathered by the Shard-Moths and repositioned by the Weavers into the wall of a spire. Everything here is recycled thought, recycled energy, and recycled stone. 

It is a society of absolute efficiency and zero empathy. The inhabitants do not communicate through sound or gesture, but through the shared experience of the **Static Pulse**. To live here is to be a note in a song that has been playing since the atmosphere first cooled—a song that is becoming more complex, sharper, and more silent with every passing eon.
