To survive in this echoing nursery, a creature must be forged of the same materials as the world: stone, vibration, and patience. The life here does not "thrive" in the traditional sense; it persists as a physical extension of the geography.

### The Grinding Choir
While the Percussives are the most visible of the residents, they are not the most numerous. Deep in the crevices where the obsidian sheets meet in razor-edged joints, there exist the **Laminates**. They are translucent, paper-thin organisms that resemble sheets of mica or stacked scales of fish. They do not have limbs; they exist as living wedges between the shifting tectonic plates. 

The Laminates possess a terrifying metabolic relationship with the "sub-sonic groaning" of the world. As the massive stone slabs settle and grind against one another, the Laminates harvest the friction. They are literal pressure-feeders, converting the immense, bone-crushing weight of the tilting cathedrals into slow, chemical heat. When the world groans, they glow with a faint, internal friction-light—a dull, smoldering orange that outlines the cracks in the dark world like cooling magma. They are the record-keepers of the world’s weight.

### The Veiled Hydras
Hovering in the thick, broth-like atmosphere are the **Occuluded**. If you were to look up into the crowded geometry of the stone overhangs, you might see what looks like a particularly dense patch of mist, tethered to an obsidian ceiling. These are gelatinous, atmospheric drifters that have more in common with heavy water than with flesh.

They are hunters of the "Accumulations." Lacking the mineral strength to endure the crushing stone, they hang like inverted ghosts, trailing miles of crystalline, hair-thin filaments. These filaments are almost invisible, swaying in the rhythmic pulses of the condensation. When a filament brushes against a glass plume or a growing salt-web, the Occuluded secretes a solvent that "un-grows" the crystal, reclaiming the mineral salts for its own translucent mass. They are the only force of erosion in this world, the quiet liquid thieves of the stone’s slow growth.

### The Echo-Mimics
Between the Sentinels and the deep basins, there flits something truly unsettling: the **Phonons**. They are not physical in any way that a soft-bodied creature would recognize; they are sentient knots of sound-waves, trapped in the perfect acoustics of the obsidian. 

Because the stone reflects sound with zero loss of fidelity, certain frequencies have become "coherent." They move through the canyons as distortions in the mist—ripples in the air that look like heat-shimmer. The Phonons "feed" on the *tink-tink-tink* of the water by intercepting the sound waves and refracting them. To stand near one is to hear the world’s roar suddenly go silent, replaced by an agonizing, high-pitched ringing that mimics the heartbeat of whatever living thing is nearby. They are predators of information; they steal the vibrations cast off by the Percussives and the Sentinels, growing larger and more complex until they can mimic the sound of a stone plate breaking—a sound so loud it can shatter the very glass plumes they pass through.

### The Pulse-Attendants
The most enigmatic inhabitants are those that appear only during the "Slow-Pulse." As the light shifts to milky grey and the water is drawn back into the stone, the **Glass-Walkers** emerge from the pitch-black "alleyways" between the slabs. 

They are spindly, multifaceted beings that look like a fusion of a spider and a prism. Their bodies are made of the same dark material as the Sentinels. They do not walk so much as they *click*, their needle-point legs finding the microscopic imperfections in the polished obsidian. They congregate around the Sentinels, placing their limbs into the interlocking geometric rings. 

During this brief period of quiet, the Glass-Walkers act as a living valve. They vibrate in sympathy with the pillars, helping to draw the moisture out of the air and back into the mineral pores of the mountain. They are the janitors of the atmosphere, preventing the "thick broth" from becoming a drowning ocean. When the violet light returns and the roar of the water begins anew, they retract into the seams of the world, becoming indistinguishable from the jagged debris of the floor.

### The Experience of Life
To live here is to be an instrument played by the terrain. There is no sleep, only different states of resonance. The inhabitants do not communicate via sight or scent; they communicate through **Sustained Chords**. A Percussive might signal its presence to a mate by changing its density, thereby altering the pitch of the water-drop hitting its shell. 

This is a society of the cold and the hard. There is no nursing of the young, no warmth of the hive. There is only the slow, relentless integration of the self into the mineral cycle. To be alive in this nursery is to be a note in a symphony that has been playing since the first stone moved—and which intends to play, unchanged, until the glass finally turns to dust.
