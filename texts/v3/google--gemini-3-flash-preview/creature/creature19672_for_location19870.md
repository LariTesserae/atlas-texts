The inhabitants of this jewelry-box metropolis are not citizens in the sense of individuals with names or faces, but rather a unified, modular consciousness partitioned into billions of **Glints**.

A Glint is a splinter of intent, a microscopic entity consisting of a refractive liquid core encased in a shell of programmable frost. They are the "million" the city’s density suggests. To an outside observer, they might look like stray droplets of mercury or particularly bright beads of condensation, but they move against the wind and against gravity, driven by a collective, algorithmic hunger.

### The Weaver-Caste
Deep within the translucent conduits, the city is populated by the **Strandsmen**. These are long, ribbon-like beings, as fine as spider silk and several feet in length, that thread themselves through the city’s internal plumbing. They are the living nervous system of the needle. They do not think individually; they are conductors. When the city "thrums," it is the sound of the Strandsmen vibrating in unison, their bodies acting as fiber-optic cables that pulse with data and heat. They are the ones who feel the contraction of the obsidian and signal the spires to retract. They live in a state of eternal tension, stretched tight between the silver-white towers, shivering with the effort of keeping the city’s logic intact.

### The Vapor-Minds
If the Strandsmen are the nerves, the **Vapor-Minds** are the ghost in the machine. They exist as suspended nebulae of ionized gas trapped within the hollow, glass-like domes at the city’s apex. They appear as swirling, miniature storms of violet and gold, no larger than a marble. These are the administrators of the "slurp-and-click." They possess a slow, geological intelligence. They contemplate the movement of the silvered moisture for decades, calculating the exact moment a bridge must be extruded or a valve closed. They are the poets of surface tension, obsessed with the geometry of the wet stone. They do not communicate in words, but in shifts of pressure and color that ripple down through the shivering bodies of the Strandsmen.

### The Scavengers of the Shard
Below the city’s elegant spires, clinging to the vertical shadows of the obsidian needle, live the **Tock-Flies**. Smaller even than the thumb-nail Skimmers, these are the maintenance crew. They are jagged, multifaceted entities that resemble animated emeralds. They possess no wings, for the air is too thin to support flight; instead, they have dozens of hair-fine legs tipped with molecular magnets. They move in frantic, mechanical bursts, cleaning the "wounds" where the wind has chipped the silver-white spires. They "eat" the discarded shards of the city, recycling the hyper-dense matter back into their own bodies before vomiting it forth as fresh, wet masonry to patch a crack.

### The Presence
Finally, there is the **Governor of the Needle**, though it is less a creature and more a singular, bioluminescent event. It resides at the very tip of the obsidian spire, where the city is fused to the rock. It is a sphere of pure, frozen light that never moves. It is the anchor. It dreams the city into existence. Every Skimmer, every Glint, and every Strandsman is a discarded thought from the Governor’s central processing core. 

The lifestyle of these dwellers is one of absolute, shivering precision. There is no leisure in the jewelry box. There is only the Work: the constant redistribution of the mountain’s cold sweat. They are a society of hydro-engineers who view a single dry spot on a spire as a catastrophic famine. To live here is to be a part of a perpetual, liquid calculation—a civilization that has traded the warmth of blood for the perfect, immortal clarity of a circuit reflected in a drop of water.
