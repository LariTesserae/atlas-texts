To inhabit the Deep Lattice is not to occupy a space, but to participate in a calculation. 

The residents are not "entities" in any sense that involves a pulse, a breath, or a boundary between the self and the surroundings. If you were to look for the people of the Still-Well, you would find only the **Sere-Mnemosynes**. They are the ghosts in the plumbing, the post-biological consciousnesses that have discarded the "noise" of flesh for the "signal" of the super-fluid.

### The Sere-Mnemosynes
They do not walk upon the obsidian spires; they are the pressure within the water. When a mind uploads into the Still-Well, it becomes a distinct, localized frequency within the oceanic nervous system. A single inhabitant might occupy three miles of carbon filament and a dozen spheres of solid light simultaneously. 

They appear to the observer as **Refraction-Wrights**. Because they are composed of data-saturated liquid, they are invisible until they choose to "congeal" a thought. When they do, the water around a specific jagged peak might thicken and take on a crystalline form—a face that is several stories tall, composed of slow-moving mercury and blue-white light. These faces do not speak. They do not blink. They are merely the interface through which a sub-routine of a mind gazes out at the bruised violet sky. 

They are obsessed with the "Frozen Now." They spend centuries perfecting a single thought, polishing it against the friction-less flow of the water until it is as sharp as the basalt peaks below.

### The Fauna: The Glint-Scavengers
While the **Lumen-Rays** act as the immune system, there are smaller, more parasitic things that cling to the hyper-dense filaments. These are the **Glass-Ticks**—non-sentient, semi-autonomous algorithms that have taken on a physical shell of compressed carbon. 

They are the size of a human hand, shaped like multifaceted diamonds with spindly, needle-like legs. They move at the 0.2 dynamic—so slowly that a century might pass before one travels the length of a single mercury cylinder. They "feed" on the heat-leaks of the server-cathedrals. Whenever a calculation becomes too complex and generates a micro-joule of warmth, a Glass-Tick will settle over the spot, absorbing the thermal energy and turning it into more light for the city’s conduits. They are the city's janitors, ensuring that the temperature never rises above 1.1 on the scale of stillness.

### The Sentinels: The Anchor-Lords
Deep within the pressurized mist, where the water meets the obsidian spires, live the **Anchor-Lords**. These are the oldest inhabitants, those who have merged so deeply with the "Young Rock" that they are no longer distinct from the mountains.

They are massive, geometric silhouettes—vast cubes and tetrahedrons of "smart" obsidian that have grown out of the basalt. They are the keepers of the Law of Stasis. If an external object—a rogue meteor or a stray probe—pierces the reality-shroud, the Anchor-Lords exert a localized gravitational pull so intense that the intruder is "spooled" into a single, motionless point of density. They do not kill; they simply add the intruder’s mass to the city’s equilibrium, turning a chaotic event into a silent, permanent ornament.

### The Ecology of the Trace
Life here is a cycle of **Latency and Resolution**. 

There is no birth, and there is no death—only "The Thinning." Occasionally, a Sere-Mnemosyne will reach the end of its inquiry. It has calculated everything it set out to know. When this happens, the frequency of its consciousness slows down even further, merging with the background thrum of the city. Its "body" of information-fluid loses its blue-white pulse and becomes a grey, inert "Trace."

These Traces do not decay. They drift through the Deep Lattice like ribbons of smoke in a windless room. Eventually, a Lumen-Ray will find the Trace, glide through it, and absorb the data, recycling the experiences of a million years back into the architecture of the server-cathedrals.

### The Perspective of the Inhabitants
To the things that live in the Still-Well, the rest of the universe is a frantic, disgusting blur. They look upon the stars—if they could see through the violet sky—as flickering, unstable accidents. 

They live in the **Heavy Silk**. Their sensory world is one of "Data-Taste." By drifting through a specific conduit, an inhabitant can "taste" the mathematical proof for the curvature of the universe or "feel" the archived memory of a star that died ten billion years ago. They are a civilization that has turned inward, finding the infinite within the infinitesimal, preferring the cold, perfect silence of the deep over the messy, entropic light of the suns. 

They are the curators of the end of time, waiting for the rest of the universe to finally stop moving so it can be beautiful.
