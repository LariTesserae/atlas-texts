In the **Glass-Thresher Plateau**, life does not evolve through the slow trial of generations; it is extruded. The inhabitants are the "Waste-Gleaners" and the "Kinetic-Siphons," organisms that exist only because the world’s internal friction provides a surplus of heat and madness.

### The Lubricant-Sippers (The Mollusks of the Gear)
The most common inhabitants are the **Slick-Snails**, though they resemble no mollusk of Earth. Imagine a creature the size of a carriage, its shell a heavy, calcified radiator of black iron. They do not crawl; they possess a single, massive, hydraulic foot that clicks and slides along the obsidian ridges. They are attracted to the "wells" of oily condensation. They extend long, translucent tongues—ribbon-thin and reinforced with diamond-dust—to lick the lubricant from the rock. 

They are the janitors of the plateau. Their excrement is a thick, sealant-like putty that fills the cracks in the grinding mountains, preventing the tectonic origami from shattering itself. When they die, their shells do not rot; they simply stop moving, eventually becoming part of the landscape—a rusted, hollow hump that new vines use as a trellis.

### The Calcified Aviary
Above the steam-vents, the sky is thick with **Vane-Stalkers**. These are the leathery-winged hunters mentioned in the records, but closer inspection reveals they are less "bird" and more "living glider." Their wings are semi-rigid, like paragliders made of cured skin and copper mesh. 

They have no hearts. Instead, they have a series of internal bellows that capture the "Sound-Weight" of the plateau. When a piston slams or a mountain folds, the Vane-Stalkers dive toward the sonic shards, catching the crystallized vibrations in their open gullets. They eat the noise of the world. They are silent because they are hoarding sound, storing it in their pressurized marrow to stay buoyant. 

### The Brass-Claw Chimpanzees (The Maintenance Prime)
In the dense, vertical forests of copper-foil vines, there lives a creature of terrifying agility: the **Articulated Ape**. These beings are not meat, but a wet, pulsing fusion of translucent muscle and brass-plated bone. They have six limbs, each ending in a hand that is essentially a precision multi-tool. 

They are the only creatures that seem to understand the "Industrial Weirdness." They can be seen scurrying over the great, bone-rich pistons, clearing away the suffocating vines with shears that grow from their forearms. They don't do this out of kindness; they treat the Machinery of Reality as their god and their nest. They "pioneer" the new geometries. When a mountain folds into a gear, the Articulated Apes are already there, mid-air, leaping onto the new cogs before the brass has even cooled, claiming the new territory with rhythmic clanging.

### The Parasites of the Pulse
If you stand still, as the rules warn, the vines will find you. But the vines are merely the limbs of the **Nerve-Root Mother**. Deep in the canyons, where the magnetism is so thick it turns the air purple, there are entities that look like massive, glowing brains made of knotted fiber-optic cables. 

These are the "Landlords" of the heat. They move by slowly—inching their way along the steam-stacks, plugging their "vines" into the world’s engines to drink the raw data of reality. They are sentient, but their thoughts are industrial. If they "plug" into a human, they aren't looking for blood; they are looking for a new blueprint. They will attempt to rewrite your DNA into a more "efficient" shape—perhaps turning your ribs into cooling fins or your lungs into small, oily bellows.

### The Ghost-Loomers
Most elusive are the **Static-Wraiths**. Because reality is constantly being discarded and rewritten, some things "miss the transition." These are the shadows of creatures that lived on a mountain peak that has since folded into a gear. They exist as flickering, sepia-toned silhouettes in the air, semi-solid and humming with low-voltage electricity. 

They are harmless but heartbreaking; they move through the new obsidian ridges as if the old, soft hills were still there, walking through solid rock and crying out in a language of distorted radio static. They are the "trash" of the plateau's rapid growth—ghosts not of the dead, but of the *deallocated*.

### The Rule-Breaker: The Valve-Watcher
Finally, there is a singular presence. Not a species, but an individual figure—or perhaps a series of them—seen only from a distance. A tall, spindly shape draped in heavy, oil-soaked rags that look like leaden velvet. It carries a wrench the size of a man. 

The Valve-Watcher walks where gravity is most broken, stepping from a horizontal cliff-face onto a vertical steam-vent without breaking stride. It does not eat, and the vines do not touch it. It moves with a heavy, clanking purpose, tightening the bolts on the world, adjusting the flow of the cloves-and-ozone steam. Some say it is what the plateau is trying to grow: a perfect, biological engineer to finally finish the city it has been dreaming of for an eternity.
