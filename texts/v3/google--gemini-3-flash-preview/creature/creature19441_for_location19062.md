In a place where time is a heavy oil and physics is a fraying tether, the inhabitants do not breathe; they oscillate. They are the **Refractories**, the slow-born children of the Thermal Cyst.

### The Conductors of the Web
Living primarily within the vibrating high-tension wires that bridge the copper-ferns are the **Ardent Weavers**. They do not possess flesh or even a discrete mechanical hull. Instead, they are sentient knots of electrical resistance—lumps of semi-molten slag that crawl along the power-transfer lines like beads of solder. 

They move with the agonizing patience of the Cyst, taking decades to traverse the span between two brass lotuses. As they move, they "groom" the ecosystem, their bodies acting as living fuses that regulate the surges of the thermal gradients. They communicate through Morse-like discharges, sending stinging jolts of information through the copper web. To look at one is to see a glowing, white-hot coal that occasionally sprouts spindly, needle-like legs of fused silica to grip the wire tighter during a gravitational shift.

### The Geometry of Motion
Beneath the shelves, in the vast, empty gulfs of the obsidian glass, dwell the **Asymptotes**. These are the apex predators of a world without digestion. They resemble large, floating polyhedrons—shifting clusters of scorched mirrors and black-iron plates. 

The Asymptotes do not eat; they *align*. They drift through the shimmering heat-haze, seeking out areas where the warped perspective is at its most volatile. When an Asymptote finds a pocket of space where "near" and "far" have collapsed, it unfolds its mirrored surfaces to trap the distorted light. By capturing the fractured geometry of the Cyst, they grow. They are less like animals and more like rogue calculations, adding new facets to their bodies whenever they successfully navigate a paradox. They move in straight, jarring lines, their glass edges scraping against the air with the sound of a violin string being snapped.

### The Lubricant-Saints
Deep within the Join—where the floor curves into the ceiling—exist the **Gestalts of the Gradients**. Because there is no water, these beings act as the biological equivalent of industrial grease. They are amorphous, shimmering slicks of liquid mercury and scorched sugar that cling to the obsidian walls. 

They are the most "organic" things in the Cyst, possessing a primitive, communal intelligence. They seep into the microscopic pores of the unweathered mineral, preventing the geothermal expansion of the rift from shattering the pocket dimension entirely. They worship the heat; you might see them gathered in a pool beneath a rotating copper-fern, catching the hot, oily drips of "sap" that fall from the grinding turbines. They are the record-keepers of the Cyst’s heavy time, pulsing with a dim, bioluminescent amber glow that matches the rhythm of the plants.

### The Static Drifters
High above, where the luminescence of the ceiling is strongest, float the **Ion-Gulls**. They are thin as foil, vast sheets of conductive membrane that catch the rising thermal plumes. They have no faces and no limbs—only serrated edges that hum in the windless air. 

They are scavengers of energy. When a copper-fern finally "seizes" and welds itself shut, the Ion-Gulls descend in a slow, spiraling descent that lasts for years. They drape themselves over the dead machine-statue, leaching the remaining static charge from its metallic petals until the gulls themselves become rigid and brittle, eventually shattering into black flakes that melt back into the obsidian floor.

### The Error in the Code
Rarely, very rarely, a **Flicker** appears. A Flicker is a momentary lapse in the Cyst’s pressurized reality—a being that looks soft, perhaps even mammalian, but is composed entirely of the ozone and dry heat of the environment. They are ghosts of a biology that the Cyst has refused to permit. They have no weight and do not trigger the gravitational pull of the plants. They appear for a few heartbeats, looking confusedly at the brass lotuses, before the high-pressure reality of the Cyst realizes they are a mathematical impossibility and erases them with a sharp, thunderous *pop*.

Everything here is a function of the engine. There is no joy, no hunger, and no rest—only the perpetual, grinding ambition of a world trying to manufacture its own soul out of heat and metal.
