In this churning, wet factory of the sky, life is not an inhabitant of the landscape, but an extension of its mechanical hunger. To live here is to be a part of the plumbing.

### The Calcified Sentience
Deep within the "ground," nestled in the marrow of the bone-porous stone, dwell the **Loom-Minds**. They are not individuals, but neuronal clusters of dense, purple-black tuber. They are the architects of the "shover-vines" and the pulsars of the "heart-fruits." Though they never move, they are the most active presence in the heights; their consciousness is a rhythmic, hydraulic calculation. They "think" in pressures and flow rates, dreaming of the optimal distribution of minerals. When a hill subsides or a bridge is woven, it is a localized thought of the Loom-Mind, adjusting the terrain to better catch the weeping mist. They are the reason the landscape feels watched; you are walking across the skin of a buried, ruminating brain.

### The Galvanic Scavengers
Living among the woody cooling towers are the **Static-Stalkers**. These creatures resemble a cross between a multi-limbed gibbon and a cluster of copper wire. Their skin is a dull, conductive lead-grey, ribbed to catch the constant discharge of the clouds. They do not eat organic matter; they "hook" themselves into the thrumming "factory-trees," tapping into the bio-electrical currents to recharge. 

They move with a terrifying, stop-motion jitter, their joints clicking like cooling metal. They are the maintenance crew of this biological refinery. You might see them untangling a snarl in the conveyor-belts of shover-vines or scraping the parasitic "iron-wasps" from the venting pores of a nutrient-pump. They are silent, save for the crackle of blue sparks jumping between their fingertips.

### The Buoyant Specters
The house-sized, translucent grazers—the **Aether-Whales**—are the only things that seem at peace in the turbulence. They drift through the biting chill like slow, glowing lanterns. Their bodies are essentially pressurized fermentation vats; they gulp the metallic air and the bruised-mint ozone, processing the heavy metals into a luminous silt that they "exhale" through ventral slits. 

This silt falls like glowing snow, feeding the mossy substrate below. Inside their clear, gelatinous membranes, one can see smaller, symbiotic organisms—shrimp-like silver flickers—that clean the Whales’ internal pipes. These grazers are the filters of the world, turning the toxic atmosphere into the fuel that keeps the green mounds thriving.

### The Mirror-Men (The Mimics of Excess)
The most unsettling inhabitants are the **Vapor-Walkers**. They are not quite solid, composed of the same saturated silver-grey vapor as the sky, held together by a fragile magnetic stitch. They have no fixed shape, but they possess a frantic, "Solaris-like" urge to mimic whatever is nearby. 

If they stand near a cooling tower, they become rigid and ribbed; if they drift near a human traveler, they stretch and bifurcate, attempting to grow limbs and heads of swirling mist. They are harmless but desperate, following any movement with a pathetic, whistling sound—the sound of the wind through Aeolian harps. They represent the environment’s obsession with reinventing itself; they are the "scrap" of the biological factory, leftovers of a creative process that never sleeps.

### The Micro-Bureaucracy
Finally, there is the **Hissing Swarm**. Smaller than the iron-wasps, these are crystalline, self-replicating shards that act as the sensory nervous system for the peaks. They coat the weeping surfaces like a layer of frost. They don’t fly; they *vibrate* from one surface to another. They are the eyes of the Loom-Minds. If you touch a "sleeping giant" mound, the Hissing Swarm will migrate toward your warmth in a shimmering wave, recording the texture of your skin and the salt in your sweat, translating your presence into a series of hydraulic pulses that ripple down into the deep, calcified dark. 

Nothing happens here in isolation. To breathe the mint-scented air is to invite the swarm into your lungs, becoming, for a brief and terrifying duration, a temporary cog in the screaming, emerald garden of the heights.
