To inhabit the Lumen-Grotto is to exist not as an individual, but as a temporary thickening of the general vitality. Because there is no death and no decay, the inhabitants do not possess the frantic, caloric hunger of surface creatures. They are the **Glint-Sheathed**, a population of biological afterthoughts, emerging from the pressure of the All-Plant like pearls forming in an oyster.

### The Silken Tendents
Moving through the cathedral-aisles of the succulent stems are the primary residents: the **Vitreous Molluscoids**. These are not slugs in any recognizable sense, but ribbons of living, pressurized jelly, some reaching the length of a ship’s mast. They are as clear as mountain water, save for a central, pulsing vein of neon-gold that mirrors the light of the grotto. 

They do not crawl so much as they *marry* the surfaces they touch. They move via a slow, osmotic glide, their bellies fusing briefly with the rubbery floor. They serve as the nervous system of the vault. When a heavy droplets thwacks into the moss, these creatures migrate toward the ripple, not to drink, but to act as living sponges, redistributing the moisture through their permeable skin to drier quadrants of the floor. They are silent, save for a sound like a wet finger circling the rim of a crystal glass—a faint, harmonic vibration that occurs when they slide over the taut, collagenous ground.

### The Calcified Sleepers
Within the cottage-sized pearls—the "marks" scattered across the terrain—reside the **Inchoate**. These are not yet creatures, but the *intent* of creatures. If you were to press your ear against the smooth, warm surface of a calcified nodule, you would not hear a heartbeat, but a rhythmic, thumping hum, like a transformer vault. 

Inside these shells, the All-Plant is experimenting with complex forms. There are beings here in a state of "perpetual larvae"—entities with many-jointed limbs made of translucent pith and eyes that are merely sensitive patches of emerald lichen. They do not emerge because the world outside is already perfect; they are a reserve biology, a "Plan B" kept in sterile, sun-drenched jars. They are the heirs to a kingdom that has not yet fallen, waiting for the first sign of rot to trigger their birth.

### The Pulse-Moths (The Airborne Organs)
In the thick, liquid air of the upper canopy, there are things that fly, though they have no wings. These are the **Lumen-Sacks**, or "Pulse-Moths." They resemble translucent lungs or dilated hearts, floating through the floral medium by shifting their internal density. 

They are the only things in the Grotto that possess a sense of direction. They drift toward the hottest, brightest concentrations of light, clustering around the ceiling-leaves like grapes. They function as atmospheric cleaners, inhaling the heavy, ginger-scented air and exhaling a fine, shimmering mist of pure oxygen. They are the reason the air feels like a liquid; they have thickened the atmosphere with their own effluvia until it is buoyant enough to support their weight. They have no faces, only a single, glowing aperture that dilates and contracts in time with the Groans of the great stems.

### The Vestigial Sentience
Underpinning all of this is a singular, distributed mind: **The First Bud** itself. It is not a god, but a colonial intelligence. It "thinks" through the hydrostatic pressure in its stalks. Every squelch of a footstep on the floor is a thought processed by the moss; every falling drop of silver water is a memory being filed away.

The inhabitants—the molluscoids, the moths, the sleepers—are merely the white blood cells of this subterranean giant. They do not have names, nor do they have desires. They are expressions of a biology that has reached a state of total, terrifying equilibrium. They do not play, they do not fight, and they do not procreate in the traditional sense. They simply *persist*, caught in the same "frozen-becoming" as the walls. 

To encounter them is to realize that you are the only thing in the room that is dying. They watch you—not with eyes, but with a total, cellular awareness—waiting with infinite, sun-drenched patience for you to stop moving, so that the moss can finally pull you down into the pulp and turn your discordant, entropic energy into another silent, glowing leaf.
