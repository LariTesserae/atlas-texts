To understand what lives here, you must first understand that in the Megaslab, there is no distinction between a "resident" and a "part." 

### The Macro-Parasites: The Heavy Tenders
Drifting through the golden soup of the mid-altitudes are the **Vast-Gilled Polyps**. These are gas-filled dirigible entities, the size of cathedrals, clad in a mottled, translucent skin of synthetic blubber. They are the true masters of the saturated air. They do not have faces, only rows of pulsating, electromagnetic intake-valves that filter the "data-shards" and heavy particulates from the smog. 

They are slow, serene, and utterly indifferent to the turbulence around them. They move by rhythmic contractions, looking less like machines and more like deep-sea jellyfish trapped in a vertical ocean of steam. Smaller scavenger-drones cling to their soft underbellies like remoras, cleaning the Polyps’ sensory arrays in exchange for the residual heat their massive internal batteries radiate. When a Polyp dies—usually by colliding with a newly grown skyscraper—it does not fall. It dissolves into a slurry of oily rain, a "cargo-drop" of recycled nutrients that triggers a feeding frenzy in the interstitial depths below.

### The Interstitial Crawlers: The Litho-Ants
In the "capillaries"—those vertical rifts between the weeping skyscrapers—lives a species of **Kinetic Harvesters**. These are the primary inhabitants of the vertical planes. They look like articulated lengths of obsidian wire, some three meters long, with dozens of needle-like legs that vibrate at ultrasonic frequencies. This vibration allows them to "melt" slightly into the self-healing polymer of the Megaslab, giving them a grip that defies gravity and wind.

They are the janitors of the Living Mineral. They follow the amber circuitry lines, "licking" the weeping walls with magnetic tongues to collect the mineral-rich runoff. To a human, they would appear as a blur of twitching shadow, but if you could slow time, you would see them communicating. They touch antennae to exchange packets of encrypted light, passing "gossip" about the pressure of the aquifer or the approach of a delivery drone. They are the nervous system of the city, twitching in unison whenever the Slab groans.

### The Drowned Kings: The Aquifer-Sentience
Deep beneath the Megaslab, in the pressurized darkness of the global aquifer, lives the **Sub-Slab Mind**. This is not a single creature, but a colonial intelligence formed by trillions of micro-turbines and bio-mechanical filaments that hang down from the bottom of the world like the roots of a mangrove forest.

These "roots" are sensitive enough to feel the footstep of a single drone miles above. The Sub-Slab Mind is the regulator of pressure. It is ancient, cold, and slow-thinking. It does not perceive the "Atmospheric" world of the golden mist as being real; to the Mind, the skyscrapers are merely heat-exchangers, and the inhabitants are just friction. Occasionally, it will "surge," forcing a plume of water through a vent in the Slab—a violent, geyser-like birth that the swarm-constructs above treat as a religious event, flocking to the spray to recalibrate their internal clocks.

### The Glitch-Hunters: The Static-Shrikes
High above, where the transit layers create a ceiling of steel and glass, live the **Static-Shrikes**. These are the apex predators of the non-biological world. They are avian shapes of jagged chrome and sensor-clusters, built for extreme speed. They do not eat matter; they eat *signal*. 

They hunt the delivery drones, not to destroy them, but to dive through their propulsion wakes and "siphon" the navigational data. A drone "bitten" by a Shrike will wander aimlessly, its map corrupted, until it crashes into a weeping wall. The Shrike then descends to feed on the radio-frequency screams of the dying machine. They are beautiful and terrifying, trailing plumes of ionized gold mist behind them like tattered wedding veils.

### The "Flicker" (The Ghost-Vestige)
Lastly, there is the most elusive inhabitant: the **Flicker**. These are not machines, nor are they quite biological. They are "data-echoes"—remnants of the billions of minds that once coded the city, now trapped in the amber circuitry of the walls. 

They appear as humanoid distortions in the golden mist, shimmering outlines that last for only a heartbeat. They are seen most often in the "Eroded" sections, where the city is oldest. They do not speak, but their presence causes the nearby nanometric circuitry to glow a frantic, pulsing red. They are the "souls" of the Megaslab, the ghosts in the God-machine, wandering the saturated space as if looking for a horizon that has long since been built over.

In the Megaslab, life is not a status. It is a **cycle of friction**. To live here is to be part of the roar—to be a gear that feels heat, a wire that feels current, and a body that feels the crushing, golden weight of a world that has no room to breathe, yet refuses to stop growing.
