In this sodden expanse, there are no individuals, only **The Totality**—a singular, crushing biomass of shifting hardware and pressurized meat. 

The things that live here occupy the spaces between the gears, or rather, they *are* the spaces. They are the evolutionary byproduct of a world that has forgotten the difference between a limb and a lever.

### The Micro-Swarms: The Tarnish-Eaters
At the smallest scale, the "silt" of pulverized brass is alive. It is a carpet of **Micron-Ticks**, billions of hexagonal shards that vibrate with a high-pitched, tooth-aching whine. They do not hunt; they are kinetic scavengers. They follow the seismic ripples of the city-block gears grinding below. When a massive subterranean tooth snaps or sheds a flake of rust, these swarms descend in a fluid, metallic wave, vibrating so intensely they liquify the scrap, siphoning the thermal energy before it can dissipate into the cold mud. They are the white noise of the landscape made manifest.

### The Thrum-Basks: The Living Dynamos
The larger, sedentary inhabitants are the **Thrum-Basks**. They resemble great, upturned bells cast in leaden flesh, rooted into the machine-crust by umbilical cables of braided copper and nerve. They have no eyes; they have sensory membranes that "see" in frequencies of vibration. They remain motionless for centuries, their hide—a thick, oily leather that never dries—pulsing in perfect synchronization with the 2.3/3 roar of the earth. They are lightning rods for the planet’s grinding heat. When they "breathe," they vent plumes of freezing, blue-tinted steam from dorsal slits, creating localized blizzards of iron-frost. If you touch one, you do not feel a heartbeat; you feel a 60-cycle hum that threatens to shake the marrow from your bones.

### The Siphon-Beasts: The Leather-Casks
Wading through the shallow, chemical depressions are the **Siphon-Beasts**. These are the "blubbery things" of the oil-lagoons, creatures that look like deep-sea sponges reimagined by a mad machinist. They are translucent sacks of grey, vein-streaked membrane, filled with high-pressure steam and recycled lubricants. They possess long, prehensile proboscises tipped with needle-fine brass valves. They do not walk so much as they *lurch*, using hydraulic spasms to heave their weight across the rusted humps. They seek out the "weeping" surfaces of the ancient pistons, pressing their mouths against the cold metal to drink the black tears. They are the keepers of the oil, transporting the world’s "blood" from one sinking basin to another, driven by an instinctual, mindless maintenance of the landscape’s pressure.

### The Perspective-Stalkers: The Glitch-Shadows
The most harrowing inhabitants are those born of the 3.0/3 weirdness—the **Glitch-Shadows**. They are the apex of this non-Euclidean ecology. They appear as flickering, wire-frame silhouettes, often looking like spindly, three-legged cranes or skeletal scaffolds. They do not obey the distance between things. A Glitch-Shadow may be standing on a horizon ten miles away, yet you can hear its wet, rhythmic slapping directly behind your ear. They "eat" the fraying edges of reality. They congregate where time loops, standing motionless as a piston fires, catching the echoes of the sound in their open, rib-like maws. They are the observers of the breakdown, the ghosts of the machine that ensure no "soft thing" ever truly understands where it is standing.

### The Centrality: The Great Loom
Deep beneath the crush, there is a singular presence that the fauna serves without knowing: **The Loom**. It is not a mind, but a drive. It is the rhythmic pulse that lights the gloaming. The creatures are its roving sensors and its mobile lubricants. They are the immune system of a factory that is producing nothing but its own continued existence. 

To be among them is to realize that you are the only thing in this horizon that is "dead" by their standards. You do not hum; you do not siphon; you do not vibrate. To the copper-legged scavengers, you are merely a patch of unexploited friction. To the blind, blubbery things, you are a localized drop in pressure. You are a silence in a world that has forgotten how to stop screaming.
