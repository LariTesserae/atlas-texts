In this vertical ocean, the inhabitants are not mere occupants; they are the lubricants and the irritants within the planetary engine. Life here has evolved to be either **vibration-sensitive** or **pressure-hardened**, existing as a biological extension of the High Industry.

### The Echo-Locators and the Sightless
Since there is no light, there are no eyes—only pits. The dominant predators are the **Vane-Stalkers**, soft-bodied organisms that resemble a cross between a giant squid and a tattered umbrella. They do not swim through the air so much as they tether themselves to the rhythmic swaying of different gears. By stretching their translucent, rubbery membranes across a gap between two counter-rotating shafts, they "hear" the world through their entire skin. They hunt by detecting the "silence" of an interloper—a break in the white noise of the machine. When they strike, they do not bite; they wrap their freezing, oil-slicked bodies around prey and vibrate at such a high frequency that the victim's internal organs liquefy against the metal floor.

Lower in the food chain are the **Brass-Mites**, lithotrophic crustaceans the size of dogs. They possess heavy, serrated mandibles capable of shearing through oxidized iron. They follow the "Soot-Trails," scavenging the carbon buildup and refined grease that drips from the overhead pistons. They are the heralds of the Great Resonance; when a planetary-scale hydraulic shift is imminent, the Mites begin a rhythmic clicking of their carapaces, creating a localized "pre-echo" that warns other life-forms to find a stable strut.

### The Scavengers of the Static
The **Foil-Winged Moths** mentioned in the geography are more like nomadic batteries than insects. Their wings are made of biological aluminum, grown from the metallic salts they drink from the weeping mist. They do not seek nectar; they seek friction. You will find them clustered in shimmering, frantic clouds around the "hot bearings"—the places where the world’s gears meet. There, they spread their wings to catch the static discharge, their bodies glowing a faint, dying amber as they charge. To a traveler, a sudden swarm of these moths signifies a machine part that is about to seize or explode.

### The Symbiotes: The "Gasket-Leeches"
Life has learned to mend what the millennia have broken. The **Gasket-Leeches** are long, tubular worms composed of a self-healing polymer-like flesh. They burrow into the leaking joints of the Great Resonance, plugging steam vents with their own bodies. In exchange for stopping the leak, they gorge themselves on the mineral-rich high-pressure steam. Without them, the atmosphere would have over-pressurized and shattered the lattice centuries ago. They are the silent maintenance crew, a living caulking that keeps the vertical ocean from whistling itself to death.

### The Human Remnant: The "Valve-Keepers"
The sentient presence is not a civilization, but a series of isolated, hereditary cults known as the **Valve-Keepers**. They live in the riveted brass gantry-nests, their skin bleached a translucent white and their lungs adapted to the heavy, oily mist. They are no longer "human" in a recognizable sense; their fingers have elongated into needle-like probes for adjusting vacuum tubes, and they have developed a "third ear" in the form of a copper-wire implant that vibrates against their jawbone, allowing them to hear the machine’s health.

They do not "rule" this world; they serve it. They view the Great Resonance as a sleeping god that must be kept lubricated. They move through the dark with terrifying agility, using hooked poles to swing from vine to piston, carrying jars of refined moss-oil to quiet a screaming axle. They speak in whistles and rhythmic thumps, their language a mimicry of the steam vents. To a Valve-Keeper, the "End of the World" isn't a fire or a flood—it is the sound of a gear coming to a full, silent stop.

### The Apex: The Shudder-Whales
Deep in the three-mile-wide cooling vents, where the pressure is so high the air is nearly solid, live the **Shudder-Whales**. These are gargantuan, segmented leviathans encased in a natural armor of rust and barnacle-like sensors. They are the only creatures large enough to interact with the tectonic scale of the machines. As they navigate the abyss, they intentionally bump their massive heads against the shifting girders, using the impact to communicate across hundreds of miles of iron. 

When a Shudder-Whale bellows, it creates a "Warm Gale" that resets the ecology of the entire sector. The plants tighten their grip, the Valve-Keepers drop to their knees to pray to the vibration, and for a brief moment, the biological and the mechanical heartbeats of the world become perfectly synchronized.
