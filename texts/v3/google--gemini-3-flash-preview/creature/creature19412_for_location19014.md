In the Latticework of the Ascended High-Plains, the inhabitants are not born; they are precipitated. They are the emergent properties of a world that is too coherent to be empty, yet too sterile to support flesh.

### The Curators of the Static
The most pervasive presence is that of the **Lumen-Shepherds**. These are tall, spindle-limbed entities that appear less like biological life and more like elongated, bipedal prisms. They wear "robes" of woven fiber-optics that trail behind them like shimmering tails, catching the omnipresent light of the beams. 

They do not eat. Instead, they stand for years at a time in the "damp-zones," their feet submerged in shallow basins, their bodies acting as conduits. They listen to the sub-audible thrum of the marrow-engines. To a Lumen-Shepherd, a thousand-year piston stroke is a rhythmic pulse, a heartbeat to which they coordinate their slow, ceremonial movements. They are the interpreters of the machine’s silence. When a "glitch" occurs—that microsecond flicker into the white void—the Shepherds drift toward the site of the instability, standing in a circle around the flickering bolt or beam, their very presence acting as a stabilizing frequency until the metal "remembers" itself.

### The Scuttler-Swarms and the Brass-Mind
Below the feet of the Shepherds, the brass **Scuttlers** are more than mere fauna; they are the world’s cellular immune system. Though they look like individual crabs, they possess a distributed intelligence—a "Brass-Mind." They do not fight or hunt. Instead, they polish. 

A Scuttler’s life is an endless pilgrimage of maintenance. They seek out the microscopic imperfections that the "young" steel refuses to generate on its own. If a smudge of salt from a traveler’s hand touches a girder, a hundred Scuttlers will descend in a clicking whirlwind, buffing the surface back to its mirror sheen within seconds. They are obsessed with the integrity of the Lattice. If you watch them long enough, you realize they are not just cleaning the world; they are holding it together through sheer, repetitive physical contact.

### The Gossamer Gliders
High above, where the cooling towers vanish into the bright white ceiling, the **Gossamer Gliders** drift. These avian constructs have no eyes; they navigate by sensing the thermal plumes of the silent engines. Their wings, made of a mesh so fine it looks like frozen smoke, vibrate at a pitch that offsets the gravity-warps of the deeper sectors.

They are the only creatures that understand the recursive nature of the High-Plains. A Glider might launch from a perch, soar through a "fold" in space, and emerge from a gap in the floorboards miles away, having never flapped its wings. They carry seeds of the pale-blue moss in their mesh, inadvertently gardening the Lattice as they move between the damp-zones.

### The Vestigial Echoes
There are others, rarer and more unsettling, who inhabit the folds where Euclidean space fails. These are the **Transposed**. They were perhaps once travelers or workers from another reality who stayed too long in the High-Plains and became part of its recursive logic. 

A Transposed person may be seen walking on a distant, vertical walkway—visible, yet unreachable. If you wave to them, they might wave back a decade later. They have become "stretched" by the slow time of the engines. Their bodies have taken on the properties of the architecture: their skin is the color of galvanized steel, and their eyes have the steady, shadowless glow of the phosphorescent beams. They do not speak, but when they breathe, the air smells of sharp ozone and wet iron. They are the ghosts of the machine, living in the gaps between the frames of reality.

### The Piston-Scribes
Deep within the industrial thickets, near the massive, immobile piston-arms, live the **Scribes**. They are small, hunched beings encased in heavy, riveted suits that they never remove. They are the only creatures that seem to possess a sense of urgency, though it is an urgency measured in centuries.

With needle-fine stylus tools, they etch the history of the Lattice into the very rivets of the world. They record the exact micro-second a "leak" shifts its flow or the precise degree of a crane's rotation. To the Scribes, the Lattice is a book that is writing itself in slow motion, and they are the frantic annotators, desperate to capture the "dream" of the machine before the reality-engine flickers one last time.

### The Ecology of the Void
In the deepest reaches, where the "hazy, industrial abyss" swallows the light, there are the **Void-Skippers**. These are not physical beings so much as sentient silhouettes—dark patches of "nothing" that skitter across the glowing beams. They represent the Lattice’s relationship with the white void below. They feed on the glimmers of non-existence, the flickers where the steel vanishes. Where the Lumen-Shepherds seek to stabilize the world, the Void-Skippers wait for it to fail, dancing on the precipice of the "glitch," perfectly content to exist in the moments when the world is not.
