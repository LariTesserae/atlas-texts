Preparing for the **Interior of the Great Vertical Cataract** requires more than just survival gear; it requires a fundamental recalibration of your sensory and physical relationship with reality. You are entering a "momentary world"—a place where the past does not exist and the future is a terminal impact that never arrives.

Here is how an ordinary human traveler must prepare for the Falling Wall.

### 1. The Auditory Armor (Barrow-Vibration Mitigation)
The 2.8/3 roar is not something you "listen" to; it is an assault. If you enter with standard earplugs, the vibration will bypass your ear canals and travel through your jawbone, liquefying your sense of balance and causing "sound-sickness" (vertigo and nausea).
*   **The Kit:** You need **active-dampening cranial plating.** This is a helmet that uses counter-vibration technology to neutralize the hum before it hits your skull. 
*   **The Ritual:** Practice non-verbal communication. In the Cataract, your voice is a useless puff of air. Learn a tactile sign language—one based on tugs on a rope or pressure points on the arm—because you will often be too blinded by caustic light to see hand signals.

### 2. Physical Anchorage: The "Claw and Tether"
In a world where "Velocity is Law," your greatest enemy is displacement. If you are swept from a basalt needle, you aren't just falling—you are being erased by the downward force.
*   **The Gear:** You need **Obsidian-Tipped Pitons** and a **pneumatic tether gun.** The basalt needles are "embryonic" and soft in some places, yet brittle in others. You cannot rely on standard climbing gear. You need anchors that can bite into rock that is still technically cooling or crystallizing. 
*   **The Suit:** A high-tensile, **slick-skin dive suit** (3mm-5mm neoprene with a polymer coating). You want to be as aerodynamic as possible. Any loose strap or baggy pocket will be caught by the downward "limbs" of water and used as a handle to rip you off the rock. Think of yourself as a needle, not a person.

### 3. Sensory Management: The Prismatic Blindness
The light in the Cataract is a paradoxical nightmare. It is beautiful, but it is "caustic"—meaning the water bubbles act as millions of tiny magnifying glasses, focusing high-altitude sunlight into searing needles of light.
*   **The Optics:** **Polarized, photochromic goggles with a gold-ion film.** This will protect you from the flash-blindness of the prismatic halos. Without them, you risk permanent retinal scarring within twenty minutes.
*   **The Skin:** Use a **heavy-duty zinc-based barrier cream.** The combination of 50-degree water "sandblasting" your skin and intense refracted UV rays will give you a "cold-burn" that mimics a chemical peel. 

### 4. Navigating "New Geology"
Because the basalt needles are emerging in real-time, they are sharp enough to slice through regular leather or rubber.
*   **Footwear:** **Chainmail-reinforced soles.** Your boots must be able to grip slick, glass-like surfaces while resisting the razor-edge of un-eroded rock.
*   **The Rule of the Needle:** Never trust a foothold for more than ten minutes. The pressure of the cataract can cause a spire to shatter or "sink" back into the fluid state. Move like the Slip-Rays: perpetually, reflexively, and with an eye on the next anchor point.

### 5. Nutrition and "The Catch"
There is no "cooking" in the Great Vertical Cataract. The air is too saturated with spray to hold a flame, and the pressure makes portable heaters unreliable.
*   **Provisions:** High-calorie **viscous pastes** and "dry-packs" that can be consumed in a single motion. You do not want to open a bag and have your lunch instantly washed into the void.
*   **Hydration:** Ironically, despite being surrounded by sapphire water, you cannot drink it. The velocity of the flow contains micro-particulates of "new stone" (basaltic silt) that will shred your digestive tract. Carry a **sealed hydration bladder** tucked inside your suit.

### 6. Psychological Priming: The Terminal Mindset
As a traveler, your biggest hurdle is the **"Void Terror."** Your brain is evolved to believe that "falling" leads to "hitting." In the Cataract, you must accept that you are in a state of perpetual descent.
*   **The Training:** Spend time in a sensory deprivation tank or a vertical wind tunnel before departing. You must lose the instinct to "reach out" when you feel weightless. 
*   **The Philosophy:** Embrace the "Clutch." Like the residents of the copper pods, you must find peace in the temporary. The moss exists for a day; the rock exists for an hour. You are a guest in a moment of creation. If you try to bring "history" or "permanence" here, the Cataract will break you.

### Summary Checklist:
1.  **Kinetic Helmet** (to keep your teeth from vibrating out).
2.  **Slick-skin suit** (to minimize drag).
3.  **Gold-ion goggles** (to survive the prismatic glare).
4.  **Tungsten-carbide grappling hooks** (to anchor to the basalt teeth).
5.  **A tether.** Never, under any circumstances, unclip.

When you arrive, do not look down. There is no bottom. Look only at the black spire in front of you, and watch for the **Slip-Rays**. If they huddle together, a "limb" of heavy water is coming. When it hits, tuck your chin, grip your copper-and-kelp cradle, and hold your breath until the world turns blue again.
