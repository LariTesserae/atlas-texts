Preparing for a visit to the **Oven of Green Frost** is not like preparing for a hike or a dive; it is a preparation for an industrial sensory assault and a biological contradiction. You are entering a space where "nature" has mimicked a machine, and a machine has achieved the immortality of a desert.

As a human multiverse traveler, your primary enemies are not predators or toxins, but **friction, deafness, and structural claustrophobia.**

Here is how you must prepare:

### 1. The Suit: "The Slick-Skin"
Do not wear standard Gore-Tex or outdoor gear. The velvet-ferns and blue-black mosses are bone-dry and abrasive, like fine-grit sandpaper. To move through them is to be constantly "brushed" by millions of dry, snapping edges.
*   **Material:** You need a seamless, high-density polyethylene or "slick-skin" hardshell suit. The goal is zero friction. If your clothes have a "tooth" or a weave, the dry-moss will hook into it and tear your garments to shreds as you push through. 
*   **No Pockets/External Straps:** Everything must be internal. Any snag-point—a buckle, a strap, a lace—will be caught by the unyielding, parchment-like fronds. You want to be a smooth pill sliding through a container of dry cereal.

### 2. Respiratory Defense: The Desiccant Filter
The Oven of Green Frost is hyper-oxygenated, which will make you feel a manic surge of energy, but the air is "thirsty" beyond comprehension. 
*   **The Dust:** Every movement you make will snap leaves and release clouds of aromatic, bone-dry spores. This isn't pollen; it’s more like powdered glass or extremely fine sawdust. Without a high-grade respirator, your lungs will be coated in a fine layer of carbon dust within minutes, leading to immediate respiratory distress.
*   **The Humidity Trap:** You must use a "closed-circuit" breathing system or a mask with a moisture-recycler (an HME - Heat and Moisture Exchanger). Because the Oven has zero water, every breath you exhale will be sucked dry by the environment. If you breathe the ambient air directly, your throat and nasal passages will crack and bleed within a half-hour.

### 3. Sensory Protection: The Bone-Conduction Dampener
The sound in the Oven is not just heard; it is felt. The *thrum-clack-hiss* of the brass cogs vibrates the air so violently that the plants act as physical amplifiers.
*   **Active Noise Cancellation is insufficient.** You need heavy-duty industrial plugs combined with a fluid-damped helmet. 
*   **The Teeth-Guard:** You must wear a custom-molded bite guard. Because the machinery vibrates at a frequency that resonates with mineral structures (like the spire itself), the drone will cause your teeth to chatter and potentially micro-fracture if they are held together. The vibrating "marrow" described in the location is literal—the sound will make your skeleton hum.

### 4. Navigation: The Vertical "Swim"
Gravity is a "suggestion" because the Oven is packed solid. You are not walking; you are burrowing.
*   **Tactile Navigation:** Traditional GPS or compasses will fail near the massive, spinning copper vents and ancient porcelain insulators. The electromagnetic field is chaotic.
*   **The Tether:** You must use a high-tensile, smooth-coated tether anchored at the entrance. Because the vista is a "saturated closet" of identical green and indigo fronds, it is impossible to maintain a sense of direction. Once you are five feet in, the entrance will have "snapped" shut behind you as the dry-moss springs back into place. Without a physical line, you will be buried alive in a tomb of velvet-ferns.

### 5. Interaction Protocol: The "Cold Touch"
Do not attempt to oil the gears. Do not attempt to "water" anything.
*   **Thermal Sensitivity:** Your body heat is an anomaly here. The Oven is at a constant, biting chill. If you touch the brass gears with a bare hand, the heat transfer will be so rapid that you risk flash-freezing your skin to the metal, despite the "oven" name. 
*   **Static Hazard:** Because it is so dry and the air is filled with kinetic energy and friction, you will become a walking capacitor. You must wear a grounding wire that trails behind you to bleed off static electricity into the mineral spire. Failure to do so could result in a massive arc-flash between you and one of the porcelain insulators, which still hold an ancient, unspent current.

### 6. The Psychological "Weight"
Prepare for **Sensory Paradox.** Your eyes will tell you that you are in a lush, verdant garden. Your skin will tell you that you are in a freezer. Your nose will smell an intense, spicy, industrial perfume. Your ears will tell you that you are standing inside the engine of a steamship.

The lack of life—the absence of anything that breathes or moves of its own volition—creates a profound sense of loneliness. You are the only thing in the Oven that is not in "frozen equilibrium." You are the only thing that is decaying. 

**Traveler’s Note:** Do not stay longer than four hours. The hyper-oxygenation will eventually lead to oxygen toxicity, and the unceasing roar of the machinery has been known to cause a specific type of "mechanical vertigo" where the traveler forgets which way is out and simply begins to burrow deeper into the green, convinced they are a part of the gearwork.
