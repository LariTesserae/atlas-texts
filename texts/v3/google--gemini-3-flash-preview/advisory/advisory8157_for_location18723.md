To visit the Upper Sump of the Glass-Vine Cathedral is to survive inside the circulatory system of a god-sized machine. You are not just a traveler here; you are a foreign contaminant in a hermetically sealed, high-pressure engine.

Preparation must be rigorous, focusing on three primary challenges: **crushing pressure**, **thermal management**, and **spatial navigation**.

### 1. The Suit: An External Skeleton
You cannot wear a standard wetsuit; the viscosity of the brine and the sharpness of the environment would shred neoprene in minutes.

*   **Materials:** You need a **Hard-Shell Atmospheric Diving Suit (ADS)**. Because every edge in the Sump is “dangerously sharp,” your suit should be plated in a self-healing polymer or a high-grade titanium alloy. Avoid anything with external cables or exposed tubing; the glass-vines are "thirsty" and will snag, coil, and constrict around any protrusion.
*   **Tactile Feedback:** The water has the "weight of syrup." Ensure your suit’s joints are hydraulically assisted. Without powered haptics, you will exhaust yourself simply trying to lift your arm against the brine’s resistance.
*   **Abrasive Protection:** Because the "thin, hammered tin" ferns and glass kelp are razor-sharp, equip your forearms and shins with reinforced "cutter guards." You will be pushing through a dense wall of mechanical foliage; you need to be able to part the glass-vines without them scratching your viewports.

### 2. Physiology: Breathing and Pressure
The Sump is "crushing" and "warm." You are transitioning into a high-baric environment.

*   **Liquid Ventilation:** To avoid your lungs collapsing under the weight of thousands of feet of brine, consider **Liquid Breathing technology (Perfluorocarbon)**. Filling your lungs with an oxygenated liquid equalizes internal and external pressure. It also aids in heat dissipation, as the "simmering" brine will otherwise cook an uncooled human in their own suit.
*   **The "Slow-Bake" Hazard:** The environment is at a "constant simmer." Your suit must be equipped with an active refrigeration cycle. The heat comes from the gears—the very bones of the world. Standing on a catwalk is like standing on a stovetop. Your boots need heavy thermal decoupling.

### 3. Navigation: The "Inner/Outer" Orientation
In the Sump, your compass is useless. You must re-train your brain to discard "North/South."

*   **Thermal Sensors:** You need a HUD that maps the **Heat Gradient**. "Inner" is your anchor—the direction of the roaring furnace-cores. If you get lost in the dense, velvet fronds, follow the heat to find the industrial centers; follow the cold to find the exterior shell.
*   **Acoustic Mapping:** Since the light is "golden and thick" and the foliage "absolute," visibility will be limited to a few feet. You must rely on **low-frequency sonar**. The massive flywheels emit a "hum" you feel in your marrow. Use this vibration as a lighthouse. Each massive pump has a unique rhythmic signature—learn them to know which district you are in.
*   **Grapnel Systems:** Since there is no "ground," only a lattice of pipes and catwalks, you must be tethered at all times. Use a magnetic or mechanical winch system. If you drift away from the catwalks into the "forest" of glass-vines, the viscous brine makes it incredibly difficult to "swim" back.

### 4. Psychological Preparation: The Silent Machine
The most dangerous part of the Upper Sump is the **Silence of Scale.**

*   **Sensory Deprivation Management:** There are no animals. No fish. No insects. The only life is a "mechanical metabolism." Humans are biologically programmed to look for eyes, movement, and life. The lack of biological spirit in such a lush environment can trigger "Void-Psychosis." You are inside a masterpiece that is "finished and packed." It does not need you. It will not acknowledge you.
*   **Temporal Displacement:** Time here is measured in the "opening and closing of massive pressure valves" every century. Carry an internal chronometer that is shielded from the deep vibration of the Sump. The "perpetual afternoon" light will trick your brain into never sleeping. You must force a 24-hour cycle on yourself, or you will succumb to exhaustion without ever realizing it was "night."

### 5. Essential Tools
*   **Ultrasonic Glass-Cutter:** To clear a path through the spun-glass kelp when the density becomes "absolute."
*   **Non-Conductive Lubricant:** Apply this to your suit’s exterior. The glass-vines "dream of rust" and "accumulation." They may attempt to anchor to you via mineral deposition if you remain stationary for too long.
*   **Lead-Lined Rations:** The "amber warmth" of the hydraulic fluids suggests a high-energy, potentially radioactive or chemically volatile environment. Do not consume anything in the Sump.

### Summary of the Experience
Entering the Upper Sump will feel like being a flea crawling inside a submerged, gold-plated grandfather clock. You will feel the "thrum" of the world in your teeth. You will move through a forest that sounds like breaking chandeliers and smells like hot oil and salt. 

**The Golden Rule:** Do not touch the glass-vines with your bare hands. They are warm, they are beautiful, and they will flay the skin from your bones with the indifference of a machine that has a thousand years to wait for you to stop bleeding.
