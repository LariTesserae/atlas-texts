To visit a **Thermal Cyst** is to step inside a pressure cooker of nascent reality. You are not entering a landscape; you are entering a *process*. For an ordinary human traveler, the challenge is not just the physical heat—it is the psychological and physiological demand of existing in a place where "nature" is synonymous with "factory."

Here is how you must prepare for a journey into the Unproven.

---

### 1. Thermal Regulation: The "Cold-Core" Exosuit
The 2.7 thermal rating is not merely "hot." It is a dry, penetrating radiation that will cook human protein in minutes. Standard heat-resistant fabrics will fail because the heat here is conductive, radiating from the obsidian floor and the oscillating copper webs.

*   **The Liquid Nitrogen Circulator:** You will need a pressurized hardsuit equipped with an internal liquid nitrogen cooling loop. The suit’s exterior must be mirrored or plated in high-albedo ceramics to reflect the ambient luminescence of the rift walls.
*   **The Ozone Filter:** The air smells of scorched sugar—this is the smell of your lungs caramelizing. Do not breathe the atmosphere. The ozone levels will shred your respiratory tissue. Your suit must be a closed-loop system; do not attempt to "filter" the local air. There is no oxygen here, only industrial exhaust.

### 2. Locomotion: Magnetic "Gecko" Soles
The floor of the Cyst is a singular slab of frictionless obsidian-glass. It is perfectly smooth and slanted into razor-sharp geometric shelves. A standard rubber sole will melt; a spiked boot will find no purchase and likely snap on the unyielding mineral.

*   **Electromagnetic Cleats:** Your boots should utilize switchable electromagnets. While the floor is obsidian (volcanic glass), the Thermal Cyst is laced with heavy metallic deposits and the root-systems of the Xylo-Industrial flora. Targeted magnetism will allow you to "lock" yourself to the shelves.
*   **Edge-Awareness Training:** Because the terrain is "wireframe," depth perception will fail you. You must move with the deliberate, slow pace of the plants themselves. If you slip, the razor-edges of the shelves will slice through a reinforced suit like a hot wire through wax.

### 3. Navigational Hyper-Awareness: Anti-Parallax Visors
The "Rules of the Breakdown" make sight a secondary, untrustworthy sense. In the Thermal Cyst, distance is a suggestion.

*   **LIDAR Integration:** Your visor must project a constant LIDAR grid over your field of vision. When your eyes tell you a brass lotus is a mile away, the LIDAR will pulse red to show it is actually three inches from your nose. Trust the math, not the light.
*   **Gravity Anchors:** Pack a "gravity tether"—a weighted cable that you can spike into any small crevice. Because gravity is inconsistent and may pull you toward a high-tension copper wire or a nearby rotating fern, you must always be anchored to the obsidian floor.

### 4. Acoustic and Electronic Shielding
The silence of the Cyst is deceptive. The "thrum-thrum" of the copper web is a low-frequency vibration that can shatter human teeth and cause internal organ resonance.

*   **Faraday Lining:** Your suit *must* be a Faraday cage. The electrical potential between the copper-grown plants and the mineral walls is immense. If you touch a plant without being grounded and shielded, you will become a fuse in the ecosystem's nervous system, vaporizing instantly to facilitate a power transfer.
*   **Active Noise Cancelling:** Use internal dampened headphones. The "cracks" of the expanding mineral shelves are loud enough to burst eardrums.

### 5. Biological "Deadening"
The Xylo-Industrial flora does not recognize carbon-based life. To these machine-plants, you are an impurity—a wet, soft anomaly in a world of hard angles.

*   **Scent Masking:** The "scorched sugar" smell is an indicator of the plants' chemical signaling. To avoid being perceived as a "resource" to be dismantled or a "contaminant" to be welded over, apply a synthetic resin coating to your suit that mimics the scent of cooling slag or inert silicon.
*   **Mental Pacing:** Time in the Cyst is viscous. If you move too quickly, you may experience "Temporal Whiplash," where your body moves faster than the reality around it can render. Move with the heavy, grinding rhythm of the turbines. If you stay for what feels like an hour, expect to return to your home dimension and find that decades have passed—or only seconds.

### 6. The "Join" Protocol
If you reach the Join—the place where the floor becomes the ceiling—**do not touch it.** The Join is the edge of the pocket dimension's "render distance." It is the most pressurized part of the Cyst. Touching the Join can cause a localized "pop," a decompression event that would collapse the entire Thermal Cyst onto your singular point of contact.

### Summary of the Experience
You will feel like a ghost in a machine. You will hear the heartbeat of a world that hasn't discovered blood yet. You will stand on a black mirror, looking at brass flowers that grow without sun, and you will feel an overwhelming sense of *patience*. The Thermal Cyst is not in a hurry to be finished. 

Bring a camera with a physical shutter—digital sensors will likely fry in the electrical hum. And for your own sanity, bring a single small stone from a weathered, watery world. Hold it in your glove. It will be the only thing in that entire hyper-pressurized reality that knows what it means to be old.
