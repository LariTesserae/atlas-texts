To step into this world is to enter a biological forge where the anvil is glass and the hammer is hunger. This is not a place for "exploration" in the traditional sense; it is a place for tactical navigation through a high-pressure metabolism.

As an ordinary human traveler, your preparation must focus on three primary threats: **Laceration**, **Sensory Overload**, and **Biotic Infiltration**.

### 1. Armor: The "Scabbard" Suit
Because the terrain consists of unweathered obsidian and shale, standard hiking gear will be shredded within minutes. You require a **cut-resistant exoskeleton or "Scabbard" suit**.
*   **The Sole:** You need articulated plates of ceramic or titanium-mesh soles. Standard rubber will be sliced to ribbons by the "razor" ridges.
*   **The Skin:** Use a layered ballistic nylon or a high-molecular-weight polyethylene (like Dyneema). It must be slick. If you snag on a lashing vine or a tectonic edge, you want to slide, not tear.
*   **Tactile Dampening:** The ground vibrates with the grinding of plates and the thrum of wings. Your boots should have internal dampening gels to prevent the constant subsonic rumble from shattering the small bones in your feet over long durations.

### 2. Sensory Management: The "Muffle-Mask"
The sound level (2.6/3) is nearing the threshold of permanent hearing damage and physical nausea.
*   **Active Noise Cancellation:** You need industrial-grade internal ear protection that filters out the "roar of frictions" but allows specific frequencies—like the warning scream of the primitive wind-chimes—to pass through.
*   **Vision:** The world is "bruised purple-black." Your eyes will strain to find depth in the glass reflections. Use **active-contrast goggles**. Do not use high-beam white lights; they will attract the prismatic insect swarms, which move like "turbulent weather." Use low-intensity red or amber frequencies that do not mimic the bioluminescence of the local fauna.
*   **Olfactory Filter:** The smell of "crushed mint and rotting musk" indicates high VOCs (volatile organic compounds) and fungal spores. A recirculating respirator is mandatory. Without it, the "translucent fungal veils" will take root in your wet lung tissue within hours.

### 3. Locomotion: The "Cane and Cleat"
Do not trust the moss. The phosphorescent moss "swallows the sound of footsteps" because it is a thick, deceptive carpet over cracks in the glass.
*   **Probing Pole:** Use a collapsible carbon-fiber staff to test the depth of the moss before every step. What looks like a solid floor may be a "tectonic crack" draped in greenery.
*   **Mucus Resistance:** The dog-sized mollusks leave trails of thick, acidic mucus. This slime is non-porous and will make the obsidian as slippery as ice. Carry a chemical "de-greaser" or "grit-spray" to create temporary traction on slime-coated inclines.

### 4. Tactical Timing: The "Exhale" Cycle
Your survival depends on the **Humidity Rhythm**.
*   **The Steam Window:** When the temperature spikes and the vegetation "breathes" out the hot fog, move with extreme caution. The opacity will be 100%. However, this is also your only period of relative safety from the fauna. The "deafening turbulence" of the insects and predators stops during the fog. 
*   **The Sound-Gate:** Use the fog-silent period to rest, but ensure you are anchored to a ridge. When the "ticking of a billion clocks" (the dripping water) begins to accelerate, it means the fog is clearing and the "evolutionary war" is about to resume. You must be in a defensible niche before the sound returns.

### 5. Diplomacy of Interference
The "primitive intellect" (1.2/3) here is likely paranoid and highly reactive. 
*   **The Wind-Chime Protocol:** If you encounter the lashed-together obsidian shards, **do not touch them.** They are tuned to "warning frequencies" that likely trigger a biological response from the fauna or alert the survivors. 
*   **Offerings:** If you wish to signal non-aggression to the locals, do not offer food (your proteins will be alien and potentially toxic) or technology. Offer **hollowed containers** or **binding material**. Based on their use of "cured gut-string" and "hollowed gourds," they lack high-tensile cords and durable vessels. A length of paracord or a collapsible water bladder would be seen as a god-tier relic.

### 6. Biological Decontamination
Everything in this world is "frantic" and "saturated." When you leave, you will be covered in the bioluminescent slime, prismatic insect eggs, and fungal spores.
*   **Flash-Burn or Chemical Bath:** Do not return to your transport or home-base without a multi-stage chemical scrub. The "slow-motion riot of creation and consumption" in this world is so aggressive that a single stray mite or "lashing vine" seed could consume a suburban garden or a spacecraft's hydroponics bay in forty-eight hours.

**Final Intuition:** This world feels like the inside of a giant, heaving lung that has been filled with broken glass. You are a foreign body; the world will try to either scab you over with moss or expel you with a tectonic twitch. Wear your armor, keep your lights low, and move only when the greenery breathes.
