To visit the Glass-Thicket is to enter a state of total, static permanence. Because the world is "finished," any traveler is, by definition, an intruder, a source of chaotic entropy, and a biological anomaly. You are a wet, soft thing entering a dry, hard machine. 

To survive and maintain your sanity within this infinite, screaming jewelry box, an ordinary human must prepare for a sensory and physiological siege.

### 1. Physiological Fortification: The Hydration Crisis
The most immediate threat is the **non-aqueous atmosphere**. Humans are roughly 60% water; the Glass-Thicket is 0%. The bone-dry, cold air will act like a desiccant, pulling moisture from your eyes, throat, and skin with every breath.

*   **Internal Hydration:** You must wear a closed-circuit rebreather system. Do not pipe the Thicket’s air directly into your lungs; the fine silicate dust will cause immediate silicosis, scarring your lung tissue within hours. Your suit must recycle every drop of perspiration and breath-moisture.
*   **Ocular Protection:** Your eyes will be the first things to fail. The air will drink your tears. You need airtight, moisture-sealed goggles. Without them, your corneas will dry and crack like the mica leaves around you.
*   **Topical Barriers:** Coat your entire body in a thick, medical-grade silicone grease or petroleum jelly before donning your suit. This creates a secondary "skin" to prevent the dry cold from leaching moisture through your pores.

### 2. Sensory Management: Blinding Gold and Piercing White
The light in the Thicket is shadowless and "flat." Human depth perception relies on shadows to understand distance. Without them, in a world of translucent geometry, you will suffer from **"Glass Blindness."** You won't know if a wall is three feet away or right against your nose.

*   **Polarized High-Contrast Visors:** You need lenses that can artificially introduce depth by filtering the high-frequency bioluminescence. Deep amber or rose-tinted lenses will help differentiate the "Wire-Palms" from the structural ribs.
*   **Active Noise-Canceling (Industrial Grade):** The piezoelectric hum is not just a sound; it is a physical force. It will vibrate the fluid in your inner ear, causing permanent vertigo. You require haptic-dampening earmuffs that phase-cancel the "billion wine glasses" drone. If you don't, the resonance will eventually shatter your teeth and cause a mental breakdown within ninety minutes.

### 3. Locomotion: Navigating the 3D Hive
In the Glass-Thicket, "ground" is an illusion. You are moving through a solid-state honeycomb.

*   **Surface Preparation:** The glass surfaces have smoothed over eons. They are not sharp, but they are incredibly slick, like polished marble coated in a microscopic layer of dust. You need **suction-grip footwear** or soft, high-friction rubber soles. 
*   **The "Slow-Touch" Protocol:** Do not move quickly. The Glass-Thicket is brittle. While the ribs are structural, the "vegetation" is delicate. Striking a Wire-Palm will cause a chain reaction of shattering mica that could puncture your suit or cause a structural shift in the immediate cell. Move like a diver in a coral reef.
*   **Spatial Anchoring:** Because every cell looks identical and there is no horizon, you will get lost within fifty feet. GPS is useless in a subterranean vault of vibrating silica. You must use **physical anchors**—inert, high-visibility neon markers that you suction-cup to the glass ribs as you move. Do not use chalk or spray; it won't stick to the vitrified resin.

### 4. Psychological Conditioning: The Terror of the Finished
The Glass-Thicket is not a ruin; it is a perfection. For a human, this is deeply unnatural. We are creatures of "becoming"—we breathe, we age, we change. The Thicket is "been."

*   **The Silence of Action:** You will be the only thing moving in your field of vision. This creates a profound sense of isolation and the feeling that you are a "glitch" in the world's code. You must bring a **rhythmic chronometer**—a watch with a physical tick. The sound and motion of the second hand will be your only proof that time is still passing, as the Thicket offers no temporal cues.
*   **Claustrophobia Management:** If you are prone to "the squeeze," do not enter. The ten-foot visibility limit combined with the infinite scale creates a paradox of feeling crushed while being in an endless space. Practice "box breathing" to regulate your heart rate against the claustrophobic density.

### 5. Essential Kit: The "Intruder's" Bag
*   **Ultrasonic Rangefinder:** To compensate for the lack of depth perception. It will tell you where the glass starts and the air ends.
*   **Emergency Sealant:** If you brush against a mica leaf and it shatters, the shards will be needle-sharp. Keep "instant-patch" adhesive for your suit.
*   **Vibration Dampeners:** Small rubber pads to place under your feet if you need to stand still. This decouples your skeleton from the world's high-pitched vibration, giving your nervous system a moment of rest.

**The Golden Rule of the Thicket:** Do not attempt to take a souvenir. To break a piece of the Glass-Thicket is to introduce the first "new" event in a million years. The resonance of the world might change in response to that void—and you do not want to be inside the jewelry box when the frequency shifts.
