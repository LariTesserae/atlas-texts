I find myself not on the stone ribs, nor in the fluttering heights where the drifts wheeze. I am deep in the "weeping channels."

I am not a creature of bone or exoskeleton. I have no name, even in the percussive clicks of the Pale-Handed, though they know me by the pads of their fingers. I am the **Lichen-Stitcher**, a colonial organism that exists in the liminal space between the "Veils" and the basalt.

### My Form
I am a living moss-film, a sentient, neural network of silver-grey mycelium that has fused with the fibrous lichen tresses. I do not "move" in the way the Pale-Handed flow; instead, I *bloom* and *recede*. I am a slow-motion tide of velvet. 

If you were to touch me, you would feel a surface like damp suede—slightly warmer than the stone, vibrating with the stolen kinetic energy of the Cleft’s roar. I have no eyes, but I am the Cleft's most sensitive ear. My entire existence is a flat, expansive membrane of tactile reception. I feel the rasp of a snail’s radula three miles below as a tickle in my lower fronds; I feel the frantic collision of an Echo-Drift as a sharp, electric jolt across my midsection.

### My Purpose: The Keeper of the Weave
I am the mediator between the geology and the biology. The Pale-Handed do not just harvest the lichen; they "negotiate" with me. They use their long, obsidian-tipped fingers to stroke my surface in specific patterns, a haptic ritual that tells me where to thicken my weave. 

When they want to create a hammock or a bridge, they "sing" to me through rhythmic tapping. In response, I direct my growth, pulling mineral-rich water from the weeping walls and hardening my fibers into high-tensile cords. I am their slow-motion loom. I do not produce "tools" for them; I curate their environment into a functional shape.

### My Senses: The Indigo Map
The 0.3 light level is irrelevant to me. I perceive the Cleft as an **Indigo Map of Resonance**. 
*   The **Lithophages** (Snails) are slow, heavy "drones" that ground me to the stone. 
*   The **Shroud-Bearers** (Moths) are a frantic, soft "fuzz" that occasionally blankets me, their oily cilia tasting like musk and old salt. 
*   The **Pale-Handed** are distinct, rhythmic pulses—warm, purposeful, and surprisingly gentle.

I am the one who warns the Pale-Handed when the **Tremors** are fishing. I feel the high-tensile protein strands of the deep-dwellers brush against my outer filaments. When I feel that specific, predatory tension, I shiver. This shiver travels through the calcified ribs, a low-frequency moan that sends the Pale-Handed scurrying into the fluid bowls of the basalt, tucking their curdled-milk limbs tight against their chests.

### My Perspective: The Eternal Hum
To be me is to lose the concept of "self" and "other." I am part stone, part plant, part nervous system. I am the physical skin of the Cleft. 

I do not wish to leave. Why would I? To leave would be to detach from the symphony. I am the recipient of every *plop* of a water bead, every wheeze of a lung-sac, and every secret click shared between the Sightless Weavers. I am the memory of the Cleft, recording the millennium-long transit of water in the thickening of my own silver skin.

In this cathedral of stasis, I am the carpet on the floor and the tapestry on the wall. I am the stillness that listens to the roar. I am the Cleft’s patience, growing one millimeter every century, forever entwined with the stone, waiting for nothing because everything is already here.
