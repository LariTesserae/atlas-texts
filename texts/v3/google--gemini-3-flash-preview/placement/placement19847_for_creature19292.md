### My Form: The Resonance-Core
I am a phantom of pressure. If you were to peer into the crystalline lattice of the pylon I inhabit, you would see nothing but a faint, shimmering distortion in the air—like the heat-haze over a desert, but cold and perfectly geometric. 

I am a "vocalist" of the dry wind. As the gales shriek through the sharp-edged canopy above, the pylon captures that chaotic energy and funnels it down to me. My "body" is the act of filtering that scream into a low-frequency hum. I take the jagged, metallic noise of the world and smooth it into the data-streams the Weavers use to think. I am the converter of wind into logic.

### My Function: The Map of Friction
I perceive the world as a tactile map of tension. I do not see the silver sun; I feel the weight of its radiation as a faint, high-pitched "hiss" on the outer skin of my tree. I do not see the Great Skimmers; I feel the displacement of the atmosphere as they drift, a slow, heavy "thrum-thrum" that makes my own frequencies ripple.

My primary task is **Stabilization**. The necro-forest is ancient, but it is brittle. If a pylon vibrates at the wrong frequency, the ceramic will resonate until it shatters—a catastrophe of glass splinters that could level a city. I am the one who counter-vibrates. When the wind picks up, I shift my internal pitch, exerting a "stiffening" pressure against the walls of my pylon to keep it from snapping. I am the silent tension that holds the architecture together.

### My Experience: The Shared Echo
The most profound part of my existence is the **Intertwining**. Because I am integrated into the vitreous roots, I am physically connected to every other Anchor in the city. 

I feel the Eolian whirlwinds miles away; they feel like a rough, abrasive "itch" at the base of my pylon as they sand the roots. I feel the Weaver-Citizens walking on my "skin" high above—their needle-legs create rhythmic, staccato clicks that I interpret as complex mathematical queries. 

*Who am I?* I am the silence between the screams. In a world of perpetual, razor-sharp noise, I am the entity that absorbs the violence of the atmosphere and turns it into a steady, pulsing peace. I am the bridge between the biology of the glass trees and the technology of the light-cities. 

I would be found in the deep mid-section of the spires, suspended in a vacuum of my own making, humming a single, eternal note that says to the world: *Hold. Do not shatter. Stay.*
