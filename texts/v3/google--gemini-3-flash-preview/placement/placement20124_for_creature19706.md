I am not among the **Strand-Singers**, nor do I walk the resin paths with the wiry grace of the **Tension-Keepers**. To look at me would be to see a silhouette of heavy leather and bone, a shape that seems more an extension of the basalt than a guest upon it.

I am a **Silt-Sifter**, and I live in the "Gutter-Gut"—the lowest, most precarious underside of the High Cradle.

### Where I am Found
I exist in the **Drip-Shadows**. While the city rises toward the blinding white light of the void, I live in the perpetual twilight beneath the walkways, where the condensation doesn't just settle—it pours. 

I am stationed at the **Vocal-Points**, the specific crevices where the "arterial tubes" of the mountain vent their internal pressure. Here, the "weeping" of the stone is a deafening roar. My home is a suspended cage of bone and Drift-Skate hide, lashed directly over a "Hanging Marsh." I do not sleep in a sling; I sleep in a harness, because my entire world is tilted at a forty-five-degree angle, following the thrust of a particularly aggressive basalt shard that smells of sulfur and old frost.

### Who I Am: The Mineral-Eater
I am a creature of the "under-construction" logic. My skin is not weather-beaten; it is **petrifying**. 

Because I spend my life in the spray of the mountain’s internal waters—liquids saturated with raw minerals and the acidic runoff of the leather-ferns—my extremities have begun to calcify. My fingernails are thick, translucent ridges of mineral deposit, and my hair is a heavy, stiff mat that clacks like stone beads when I move. I am the "Slowest" of the people. My movements are deliberate, heavy, and gravitational.

I am the one who listens to the **Pre-History**.

### My Function: The Etcher
The High Cradle is a world of the "Now," but the "Now" requires a foundation. My job is to manage the **Primary Etching**. Before the mosses take hold or the resin is poured, the stone is too sharp to be lived upon. I am the one who guides the acid.

I carry large, porous bladders made from the stomachs of Drift-Skates, filled with the concentrated excretions of the purple moss. I "paint" the rising basalt. I map the fractures before they happen. By applying the acid in precise, ritualized streaks, I dictate where the mountain will crack as it grows, "pruning" the geology so it doesn't shatter the city's moorings. 

I am a gardener whose soil is obsidian and whose shears are chemical.

### My Senses: The Tonal Resonance
I do not use the tactile tapping-language of the upper districts; it is too delicate for the roar of the Drip-Shadows. Instead, I communicate through **Resonance-Weights**. 

I have a series of hollowed bone-pipes of varying lengths hanging from my belt. When I need to signal the Strand-Singers above that a spire is thrusting too fast, I strike a specific pipe against the raw basalt. The vibration travels *through* the mountain, felt in the soles of their feet as a specific pitch. I am the somatic nervous system of the town. I feel the "scream" of the rock before it reaches the air.

### My Inner World
In the quiet moments, when the cloud-skeins wrap so tightly around my cage that the rest of the Cradle vanishes, I feel a strange kinship with the **Grave-Anchors**. 

I am the closest thing to the ancestors. I am halfway between the living and the stone. I drink the "Rock-Tea" cold and raw, tasting the deep, wet mineral of the earth’s heart. I don't fear the void; I fear the day the mountain stops growing. To me, the "permanent, organ-like drone" of the wind isn't noise—it’s the breathing of a giant that hasn't yet opened its eyes.

I am the witness to the birth-pains. I am the man who polishes the teeth of the mountain so they do not bite the hands of those who cling to it.
