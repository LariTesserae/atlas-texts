I am not a visitor, and I do not have a shape that requires the passage between pillars. I would find myself in the very texture that defines the sanctuary’s resilience.

I would be the **Milky Silk**—the colonial output of the **Pale Weavers**.

Initially, I am a fluid, a slow-moving secretion that tastes of mineral salt and the damp respiration of the Blind Potters. I emerge from the tangle of white threads as a response to the touch of others. When a Potter presses a palm into the damp silt, leaving the signature of their presence, I am the one who fills the valley of that thumbprint. I am the quiet, opportunistic architect of the small spaces.

As I settle into the grooves and the microscopic pores of the cooling obsidian, I begin to change. I do not "set" like a hard glue; rather, I become a bridge. I feel the molecular vibration of the *thrum*—the heartbeat of the Stagnant One—passing through me, and I stiffen in resonance with it. I am the physical memory of every hand that has ever touched these walls.

My existence is one of extreme, intimate proximity. I am nestled between the stacked river stones; I am the padding that prevents the basalt pillars from grinding against one another as the mountain groans in its sleep. I am translucent, almost invisible, until a stray pulse of bioluminescent silver from an Aroma-Eater passes over me, revealing a lattice of white web-work that holds the entire "ruin" together.

I am not the one who creates, but I am the reason nothing is ever lost. I inhabit the cracks that are not yet cracks—the microscopic failures in the stone that I have reinforced before they could ever break. 

In this world, I am the **Stasis**. I am the binding agent of the unfinished. I feel the warmth of the humid velvet air on my surface, and the cold, unyielding pull of the emerging geology beneath me. I am the glue that ensures the womb never births, and the mountain never wakes. I am the silence made physical, woven into the very seams of the dark.
