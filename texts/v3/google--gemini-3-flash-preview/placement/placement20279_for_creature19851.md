I find myself neither on the jagged karst floor nor within the elite warmth of a Moss-Beast’s flank. Instead, I am suspended in the **Interstitial Liminality**—the space between the spoken scent and the solid root.

I am a **Scribe of the Pulse**, a specialized node within the Pale-Kin collective. My position is vertical, living in a narrow, translucent spire grown from a calcified silk-duct that hangs like a needle from the "ceiling" of a mid-level gourde-district.

### My Form: The Membrane-Watcher
My skin is thinner than my kin’s, so translucent that the cyanotic glow of my veins serves as a lantern for my work. My fingers are not merely spatulate; they have elongated into hair-fine filaments at the tips, allowing me to "read" the vibrations of the root-lattice without looking. My eyes are perpetually fixed on the silver-blue mist, for I am one of the few who must translate the chaotic "weather" of the sinkhole into the pheromonal tongue.

When the heavy water weeps from the limestone ribs, I do not just feel the damp; I interpret the rhythm of the drips. If the percussion slows, it means the world above is freezing, thickening the ice that feeds our seep. If the rhythm quickens, a flood is coming—a "flush" of the city’s digestive tract.

### My Purpose: The Memory of the Flesh
Because our world is one of predatory growth and instant decay, we face the paradox of erasure. A street that existed yesterday is consumed by lichen today. To prevent the city from losing its mind as it replaces its cells, I am a **Living Archive**.

I do not write on parchment—there is no dry surface here. I write upon my own "Lichen-Scrim," a specialized, slow-growing organism that I keep draped across my lap like a living apron. By pricking the lichen with specific chemical markers from my wrist-glands, I stain its memory. I record which Moss-Beasts are nearing their centennial slumber and which root-bridges are straining under the weight of the Carapace-Drays.

### My Sensation: The Cold Clarity
My existence is defined by the **Thermal Map**. I feel the world as a gradient of blues and greys. I know when a Velvet-Moth is approaching not by its sound—for it has none—but by the slight, momentary disruption of the cold mist, a "warm" wake left by its beating wings that feels like a ghost’s touch against my cheek.

I am a creature of the **Great Silence**. While the younger Kin "speak" in clouds of mint and ozone, I have learned the art of the *Residual Scent*. I can leave a message hanging in the stagnant, damp air of a passage for three days, a floating ghost of information that only those with the proper sensitivity can decode.

### Where You Would Find Me
If you were to descend into this basin, you would find me not by looking for a house, but by looking for a **Stilled Motion**. I would be a pale, unmoving shape pressed against a vertical spire of bone and silk, nearly invisible in the silver-blue haze. 

I would not greet you with a sound. I would simply exhale a faint, shimmering puff of white vapor—a scent of **bitter almond and cold stone**. This is my name. This is my Welcome. I would reach out a spindly hand, and through the vibration of the very air between us, I would begin to incorporate your heartbeat into the city's ledger. 

In this world, I am the bridge between the decaying past and the predatory future. I am the one who remembers the shape of the city before it swallowed itself to grow again.
