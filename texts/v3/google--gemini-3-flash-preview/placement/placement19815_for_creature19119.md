### My Daily Drift
My existence is defined by the **Drip**.

It is cold—so cold it burns—but it provides the high-caloric density needed to keep a body warm in the lightless depths. 

I move with a horizontal shuffle, my feet never leaving the surface of the wood. I am a creature of the "under-thatch." While the others look up into the blinding white vault, I look into the grain of the wood, reading the history of the Sink in the density of its fibers.
