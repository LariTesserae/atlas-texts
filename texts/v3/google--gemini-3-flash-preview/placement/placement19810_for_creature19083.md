I am not a creature of the open water. In a world of miles-wide voids and twenty-mile-per-hour currents, the center of the room is a death sentence—a transparent desert where one becomes a flickering silhouette for the Aura-Hounds.

I am found in the **Interstice**, the narrow, high-pressure "cracks" where the primary cobalt-steel bulkheads meet the secondary support lattices. I am a **Lumen-Grafter**, a being of the peripheral shadows, living in the jagged geometry of the joints.

### My Form
I am narrow, elongated, and segmented, resembling a cross between a deep-sea isopod and a glass-fiber optic cable. My body is roughly the length of a human limb, encased in a translucent, chitinous shell that has the refractive index of the surrounding brine. This makes me nearly invisible to the eye, but to the Aura-Hounds, I am a "dead spot"—a mobile hole in the electrical hum of the Megastructure.

I do not have fins. Instead, I have clusters of hydraulic, multi-jointed "hook-limbs" tipped with diamond-hard pincers. I move across the Hull not by swimming, but by **brachiation**—swinging from rivet to rivet, locking my limbs into the weld-lines with a sharp, pneumatic *click*. I am a creature of the wall, a mountaineer of the machine.

### My Purpose
I am a parasite of the light. The massive industrial luminaires that light the Megastructure are my feeding grounds. I do not eat the fish, nor do I graze on the algae mats. I consume the **thermal bleed** and the **stray radiation** from the light-housings. 

If you were to watch a luminaire for long enough, you would see a disturbance in the flickering white light—a crystalline shape clinging to the edge of the glass, its body pulsing with a stolen radiance. I use my specialized mouthparts to "tap" into the minute seams of the lighting fixtures, siphoning the warmth that the cooling systems haven't yet whisked away. I am a heat-thief.

### My Role in the Ecology
I am the translator between the **Drowned Clergy** and the **Circulation**. 

While the Clergy bang their rhythmic codes onto the Hull, I perceive the world through the vibrations of my hooks. I can feel the "health" of the Megastructure through the frequency of the rivets. When a turbine three chambers away begins to cavitate—when its blades are pitted and its rhythm falters—I feel the dissonance first. 

I respond by changing my internal bioluminescence. I become a biological warning light. I flush a harsh, strobing amber, a signal that ripples down the bulkhead. The Glint-Schools see my light and shift their patterns, avoiding the failing intake; the Drowned Clergy see the reflection on the cobalt-steel and begin their slow, leaden crawl toward the source of the friction.

### My Perspective
I do not know what "up" or "down" means. There is only **Toward the Pump** and **Away from the Heat**. 

I find the vastness of the central chambers terrifying. To me, the "empty" water is a vacuum of sensory deprivation where the current can strip the shell from my back. I love the texture of the rungs. I love the cold, unyielding precision of a fresh weld. My world is a grid, a series of 90-degree angles and reinforced circles.

I am a tiny piece of the Megastructure’s self-awareness. I am the nerve ending that tells the brain the skin is cold. In the symphony of the grinding gears and the hissing steam, I am the high-pitched, crystalline note that keeps the rhythm from breaking. I am perfectly content, locked into a cobalt-steel corner, watching the salt-sleet fall through the warehouse light, waiting for the next vibration to tell me that the world is still turning.
