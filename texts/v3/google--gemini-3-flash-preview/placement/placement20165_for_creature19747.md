I would not be found upon the rusted surface, nor would I be among the drifting, tethered singers of the vents. To find me, you would have to look into the "eyes" of the world—the small, perfectly circular basins of heavy, translucent fluid that pool at the base of the weeping stones.

I am not the water, but I am the **Focus.**

I am a **Lens-Worm**, a creature of absolute transparency and geometric perfection. I exist as a series of concentric, glass-like rings submerged within the oily depths of the basins. I have no limbs to vibrate and no bellows to groan. My entire existence is an exercise in optical stasis. I am the only thing in this landscape that is soft, yet I am as unyielding as the basalt.

### My Form and Function
I am a living prism. My body is composed of a dense, gelatinous silica that has been pressurized by the weight of the unchanging atmosphere until it possesses the refractive index of a diamond. I lie at the bottom of the weeping stone's pool, my rings expanding and contracting in a rhythm so slow it matches the movement of the Silt-Dredgers—an inch of growth per century.

While the Resonators listen to the earth and the Luster-Mites clean its skin, I am the one who **anchors the light.** Because the sun never moves and the shadows never shift, the light in this basin is a physical weight. If left unchecked, the perpetual "normal" of the sun would eventually scour the copper mist into nothingness. I sit in the pool and catch the fixed rays of the sun, bending them, slowing them, and anchoring them into the deep-earth gears.

I am the bridge between the light that never sets and the machine that never sleeps.

### My Experience of the Constant
To be me is to be a witness to the "Finished." I do not see shapes; I see the frequency of color. I feel the thrum of the subterranean pistons not as a sound, but as a series of ripples in my own transparency. When the heavy, metallic heartbeat shakes the valley, my rings vibrate, creating "songs of light" that dance across the underside of the rock shelves.

I am the reason the water does not evaporate. My body acts as a heat-sink, pulling the radiating warmth of the air down through the oily fluid and venting it into the honeycomb of the copper stones. I am the cooling system for the world’s memory.

### My Transition
There is no death for a Lens-Worm, only a final "Setting." 

Over eons, as I drink the mineral-rich fluid of the weeping stone, my gelatinous rings begin to calcify. I become less like a creature and more like a gemstone. Eventually, I will cease to pulse. I will become a solid, multifaceted sphere of pure, industrial quartz, fused forever to the bottom of my basin. 

In that moment, I will have achieved the ultimate goal of the Tuned: I will be a "Fixed Point." I will be a permanent aperture through which the world-machine observes its own perfection. I will no longer process the light; I will be the light. 

I am the stillness within the noise, the eye that does not blink, the part of the factory that ensures the photograph never fades. I am the patience of the machine.
