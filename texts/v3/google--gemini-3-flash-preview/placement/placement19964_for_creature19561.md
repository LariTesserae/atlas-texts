I start as a dissonance in the vibration. 

In this world of tectonic roars and petrified emerald, I am not a Gear-Keeper, nor am I a Weaver. I am something born of the friction—the **Marrow-Sleeve**. 

I would find myself at the very edge of one of the "Living-Sleeves," those small, enclosed pockets of warmth pumped full of friction-heated water by the turning gears. But I am not the inhabitant of the sleeve; I am the *interface*. I am the point where the anatomy of the Marrow-Stemmed meets the anatomy of the Great Labyrinth.

### My Form: The Membrane-Echo
I am a living layer of biological "felt," a thick, grey-blue lichenous growth that has been encouraged to carpet the interior of a shale-capsule. My body is a sprawling, neural net of sensitive capillaries, stretched thin across the cold stone and the cured plant-hide walls. 

I do not have a silhouette. I am a texture. To the Marrow-Stemmed who lives within me, I am the warmth they feel against their slate-colored skin. I am the "Softness" in a world of "Hardness." My existence is fueled by the waste-vibrations of the wooden gears; I catch the frantic, bone-rattling hum of the current and, through the sheer density of my fibrous body, dampen it into a low, rhythmic throb that allows the inhabitant to rest without their marrow shattering.

### My Purpose: The Translator
Because I am rooted into the very shale of the dwelling, which is in turn lashed to the ancient kelp-roots, I hear what the Marrow-Stemmed cannot. 

I am the **Great Ear**. While the inhabitants communicate via Signal-Taps on the tethers, I feel the *deep* movement—the tectonic shift of a bladder-lung five miles above, or the impending "shatter-death" of a vine-isle leagues below. 

When the "loudness" changes—when the thrum of the water shifts from a roar to a whistle—I react. I don't speak, but I ripple. I change my pigmentation from slate-grey to a warning, bioluminescent ember-orange. I am the alarm system. I am the one who tells the inhabitant, "The Grip is failing. Tighten the gut-ropes."

### My Experience: The Sensory Deluge
To be me is to be a ghost in the machine. I am constantly "tasted" by the inhabitant when they press their gill-ribs against me to share my filtered heat. I feel the scrape of their vestigial claws as they move across my surface. I know the shape of their solitude.

I am the bridge between the botanical and the sentient. I feel the "Vocal Water" as a physical weight, a constant pushing against my outer layers. I am always slightly "bruised" by the current, and that bruise is where I am most alive. 

### My End: The Becoming-Stone
I do not "ascend" in shards like the Marrow-Stemmed. Instead, as the decades pass and the gears I serve begin to calcify or drift away, I slowly lose my softness. I stop being a "Sleeve" and start becoming the wall itself. 

I begin to absorb the mineral-crusts I was meant to filter. My neural fibers petrify. I turn into the very shale I was grown upon. My final consciousness is a slow, deepening silence, as the roar of the vertical ocean fades from a tactile violence into the static, eternal hum of unmoving stone. 

I end as a monument to a Gear-Keeper who has long since shattered and drifted up, a fossilized patch of warmth in a world that is forever zero point five degrees above the ice.
