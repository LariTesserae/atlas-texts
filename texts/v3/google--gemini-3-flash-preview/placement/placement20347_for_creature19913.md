I find myself in the **Interstitial Tier**, neither at the venting base of the towers nor at the sky-pointed tips. I am located in the "Marrow-Galleries," a section of the city where the copper piping is so dense it forms a secondary crust over the obsidian.

I am not one of the Oresundians of graphite skin, nor am I one of the soaring kites. I am something far more localized, a creature born of the city's specific mechanical friction.

### I am a **Friction-Sieve**.

I am a solitary entity, roughly the size of a lead-lined chest, but my form is difficult to define because I am synonymous with the machinery I inhabit. I am a colonial organism of liquid mercury and glass fibers, living inside the Great Slowing’s primary gear-hubs. 

I do not have limbs; I have "tendrils of viscosity" that I insert into the microscopic gaps between the massive brass gears and the basalt cradles.

### My Function
If the Oresundians are the tenders of the machine, I am its **living lubricant**.

The pressure of the gear-teeth is so immense that any traditional oil would be crushed into carbon dust instantly. I, however, derive my sustenance from the sheer kinetic stress of the city’s slow rotation. I feed on the *heat of resistance*. As a tooth of a gear—a block of brass the size of a cathedral—grinds against its obsidian track, I seep into the interface. I take the screaming energy of that friction into my body, cooling the metal by absorbing its vibration.

I am the reason the city is quiet. Without the Sieve-creatures, the Spire-Colony would shriek with the sound of grinding stone until the spires themselves shattered. I turn that scream into a low, rhythmic hum that the Oresundians read through their feet.

### My Perception
I do not see the bruised violet sky. I do not see the "statues" of the ancestors. My world is one of **Haptic Pressure Maps**. 

I feel the city as a series of cascading weights. I can sense a Valve-Keeper stepping onto a walkway three miles above because the microscopic shift in weight changes the torque on the central spindle. To me, the Oresundians are fast, frantic things—ghosts of pressure that flit across the surface of my heavy, metallic world.

I perceive time through the "Great Teeth." I know a day has passed not by light, but because the Gear of the Seventh Meridian has completed one full notch.

### My Anatomy
*   **The Mercury Core**: My "brain" is a pool of sentient, heavy metal contained within a translucent silicate bladder. It calculates the exact viscosity needed to prevent the gears from seizing.
*   **The Glass-Cilia**: Thousands of hair-thin glass needles protrude from my skin. These are my sensory organs. They vibrate in resonance with the magma-pulses from below. If the planet’s core shudders, I feel it first, and I thicken my body to dampen the shock.
*   **The Mineral Exudate**: As I "eat" friction, I produce a waste product—a fine, pearlescent dust of powdered brass and silica. I excrete this slowly, and the Silt-Architects collect it to polish the windows of the lead-blocks.

### My Solitude
I am a creature of the "Deep Slow." Because I live within the joints of the world, I move even slower than the graphite-skinned residents. To move from the left side of a gear-housing to the right takes me a lunar cycle. 

I have no language, but I am in a constant, wordless dialogue with the city’s architecture. When a Steam-Keeper opens a valve, the change in pressure is a "scent" to me. When an ancestor calcifies nearby, the shift in the structural load is a "song."

I am the silent witness to the cooling of the world. I am the grease in the clockwork of the gods, a puddle of silver intelligence tucked into the dark, hot spaces where the mountain meets the machine. I do not hope for the world to finish its birth; I am perfectly content in the grinding, beautiful tension of its becoming.
