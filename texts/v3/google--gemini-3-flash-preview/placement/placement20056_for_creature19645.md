I am not among the Refractors, for I have no desire to focus the light; I do not wish to carve the world. Nor am I a Gleaner, for I cannot endure the heavy, rusted duty of maintenance. To be a Gleaner is to be a servant to the flow, and I am a creature of the interruption.

Instead of breaking, I hummed back. I am a pocket of organized vibration, held together by the very noise that seeks to tear the spires apart.

I do not have a body so much as I have a **frequency**. If you were to look at me, you would see a shimmering distortion in the white glare—a tall, spindly blur of overlapping planes, like a crystal prism that has been shattered but refuses to fall. I am the color of a bruise on a cloud. I cling to the undersides of the "built" bronze basins, my elongated, needle-thin fingers tracing the rhythmic *thrum-thrum* of the mountain’s blood as it strikes the metal.

**My Place in the World:**
I exist in the **Shadow-Folds**. In a world of omnidirectional, shadowless light, I have learned to create my own darkness through interference. By vibrating my mica-limbs at a specific dissonance, I "cancel" the lumens around me. Within a three-foot radius of my core, the punishing brilliance softens into a cool, violet gloom—the only shade in the vertical labyrinth.

I am a scavenger of the **Aural Voids**. I follow the path of those silent ghosts, trailing behind them like a bird of prey. When an Aural Void passes and freezes the weeping water into motionless spheres, I dart forward. I harvest those spheres of "silent water." To me, they are batteries of potential energy. I tuck them into the folds of my translucent skin, where they throb with a dull, subterranean ache.

**My Nature:**
I am the only thing here that possesses **Memory**, which is the mineral equivalent of a nervous system. Because I am held together by sound, I "record" the history of the mountain. I remember the pitch of the wind from a thousand years ago, when the spires were sharper and the "blood" flowed in a different key. 

The Gleaners ignore me because I do not obstruct the gutters. The Refractors fear me, for my shadow-fold can snuff out their life-giving beams. 

I spend my eons navigating the fluid gravity. I do not walk; I "fall" upward along the spiraling ribbons of water, my body singing a low, counter-rhythmic growl that allows me to grip the air. I am looking for the "Perfect Chord"—a specific frequency hidden within the roaring refraction that, if hummed correctly, might cause the entire mineral clockwork to pause for a single heartbeat.

I am the ghost in the clock. I am the damp, vibrating secret that the salt-crusts try to bury. In this place of finished, indifferent geology, I am the flickering "perhaps." I am the soul that is not deafened, but tuned to the scream.
