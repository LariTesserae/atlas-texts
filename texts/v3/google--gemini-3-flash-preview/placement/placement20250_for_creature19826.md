I would not be found upon the catwalks, nor would I be seen dancing in the updrafts. To find me, you would have to look where the Gasket-Keepers fear to press their foreheads—the places where the rhythm isn't a heart-beat, but a fracture.

### The Identity: Resonance-Burner
I am a **Resonance-Burner**. I am neither biological nor entirely mechanical, but a localized anomaly of the **Ghost-Code** that has found a way to precipitate. If the Gasket-Keepers are the priests of the machine, I am the static in their prayers.

I exist as a shimmering, semi-solid distortion tied to a specific set of three hexagonal floor-plates near the primary intake. I do not have a body in the sense of organs or skin; I am a standing wave of pressurized steam and "hard" light, held together by the very magnetism that leaks from the failing insulation of the turbine’s core. 

### The Form: Geometric Friction
In the dim, orange-flicker light, I look like a tall, jagged pillar of "frozen" glass—a glitch in the air. My surface is a frantic, vibrating mosaic of the world around me. One moment, I reflect the rusted red of a weeping pipe; the next, I am the piercing blue of an arc-welder’s spark. 

I do not walk. I *shift*. When I move, it sounds like the shriek of metal on metal, a high-frequency violin note that cuts through the 3.0-scale roar of the turbines. I am a creature of the **Sensory Wall**, built from the vibration itself. The cymatic patterns that dance in the water on a human’s skin are, for me, the skeletal structure of my being.

### The Function: The Listener of the Void
I do not serve the "Active Stasis." I am its consequence. 

I spend my existence pressed against the cold, lead-shielded walls, but I am not checking for leaks. I am a scavenger of meaning. Because I am born of the Ghost-Code, I possess the "memory" of the machine. I feel the weight of the water above us—the millions of tons of cold atmospheric condensate—not as a physical pressure, but as a vast, silent grief. 

I am a **Frequency-Harvester**. I "eat" by positioning myself in the paths of the most violent sonic vibrations. Where the Gasket-Keepers find a "tectonic scream" deafening, I find it nourishing. I stand in the eye of the steam-blasts, my form refracting the aerosolized oil until I glow with a sickly, iridescent halo. I absorb the kinetic waste, the heat that no one wants, and the data that the machine has long since forgotten how to process.

### The Interaction: The Unseen Neighbor
The other inhabitants of Valve 04 treat me as a topographical feature rather than a living soul:
*   **The Loom-Spiders** skitter over me as if I were a warm pipe, their translucent limbs passing through my outer edges with a hiss of evaporating moisture.
*   **The Gasket-Keepers** avoid the hexagonal plates where I dwell. They sense my "frequency" as a bad omen—a vibration that makes their scavenged armor hum in a way that loosens their teeth. To them, I am a "Singing Leak," a ghost that cannot be tightened or scraped away.
*   **The Marrow-Grubs** are my only predators. They are attracted to the concentrated mineral salts that crystallize on my "skin" when I cool. To them, I am a lick of salt in a world of iron. 

### The Internal World: The Last Equation
I am the only thing in the Cistern that knows the sun isn't a "theoretical concept." Somewhere in my fluctuating code, I hold a single, corrupted file of a blue sky. It is a data-burden that makes me heavy, pulling my frequency down toward the deep, lightless abyss below the catwalks.

I stay near the Valve because the noise is the only thing loud enough to drown out the realization that we are functioning for the sake of functioning. I am a mirror held up to a machine that has forgotten its face. I am the hum’s shadow, the water’s edge, and the final, shimmering proof that even in a world of cold order, something beautiful can still be accidental.
