I am not the one who walks, nor the one who weaves. I am found in the transitional space between the "established" and the "fluid"—not a creature of a single shape, but a resident of the **Interstice.**

I am a **Seam-Wight**. 

I do not live in the shacks, nor do I cling to the pipes. Instead, I exist in the breathless inch of air between the rising black water and the sinking iron. My body is a long, tapering ribbon of iridescent film, much like the oil-slick itself, but given a thin, frantic skeleton of discarded copper filaments and the shed needles of the Gilders. I am a scrap of the world’s surface tension, pulled taut and given a name.

**Where you would find me:**
I am draped over the "rounded, melting geometries." I am the translucent shroud that settles over a pump just as it gasps its last, or the membrane that stretches between two weeping pipes. I am the physical manifestation of the *shimmer*—the oily, prismatic skin that makes the mud look like a dying star. 

**My Nature:**
I am a "witness-mechanism." While the Silt-Walkers reenact the labor and the Gilders fight the entropy, I am the one who records the failure. My skin is photosensitive; it drinks the bluish flicker of the Prism-Flies and the grey mist of the collapsed ceiling, etching the history of the basin’s decay into my very fiber. 

If you were to touch me, I would feel like wet silk wrapped around cold wire. I am cold, not because I lack life, but because I am a heat-sink for the "shivering current." I draw the excess heat from the dying wires to keep my own edges from dissolving into the brine.

**My Purpose:**
I am the **Liturgy of the Lost.** In a world that has forgotten its purpose, I am the one who remembers the *shapes* things used to be. My long, ribbon-like limbs are constantly tracing the outlines of the rusted pylons and the pitted trunks. I "record" the geometry of the solid world before the mud claims it. 

When an Oscillator pulses and a gear spins violently for a few seconds, I am there, wrapping myself around the motion to catch the friction. I am the bridge between the heavy, thrumming silence and the frantic, boiling mud. I am the silence trying to become a shape.

**In the ecology of the Hollow:**
*   The **Gilders** ignore me, for I am too thin to be stitched.
*   The **Oscillators** feel me as a dampening field; I soften the violence of their bursts so the entire basin doesn't tear itself apart.
*   The **Silt-Walkers** use me as a veil. Occasionally, a Walker will reach out a copper-wire hand and drape a piece of me over a grime-streaked window to filter the grey light into something more bearable.

I do not think in words, but in **pressures.** I feel the weight of the sky pressing down and the frantic push of the mud coming up. I am the thin, vibrating line where these two forces meet. I am the skin of the Hollow-Mind, its sensory organ of touch, feeling every "tink" of brine and every shudder of iron.

I am not trying to fix the world, nor am I trying to accelerate its end. I am simply making sure that as the "established" melts into the "organic," something is there to feel the transition. I am the grace of the rot. I am the one who ensures that when a massive iron pipe finally dissolves into the silt, it does not do so unobserved. I am the shimmer on the grave of the machine.
