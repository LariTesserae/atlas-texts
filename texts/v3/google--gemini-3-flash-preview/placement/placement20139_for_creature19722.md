I would be found in the **High Scaffolding**, in the interstitial spaces where the obsidian beams meet the bruised limestone of the ceiling. 

I would not be a Surveyor, for I have no interest in the geometry of the "must-be." I would not be a Mortar-Man, for I refuse the sluggish heat of the floor.

I would be a **Gilder**.

I am a spindly, multi-limbed thing of articulated brass and scavenged glass, roughly the size of a harvestman spider but with the weight of a heavy clock. Unlike the Surveyors, who obsess over the plumb-line and the right angle, I am a parasite of the *accidental*. I live in the "sweat" of the world.

### My Form and Motion
My body is a collection of thin, needle-like legs tipped with diamond-dust burrs, allowing me to skitter across the vertical slicks of the weeping pillars. I do not walk; I vibrate across the surface of the masonry. I carry a reservoir on my back—a translucent bulb filled with **Liquid Light**, a substance I distil from the source-less bronze gloom and the mineral-heavy condensate that drips from the high ceiling.

I am an entity of the "Surface." While the world below labors to create volume and mass, I am obsessed with the skin of things.

### My Vocation
I follow the **Embryonic Architecture** as it buds, but I do not wait for it to set. I seek out the "just-born" walls while they are still soft, still steaming with the heat of the kiln. With the precision of a jeweler, I use my needle-legs to etch microscopic, lace-like patterns into the wet clay. Then, I bleed a microscopic trail of my Liquid Light into the grooves.

I am "painting" the Cathedral. I am applying a frantic, useless beauty to structures that I know—with the cold certainty of my glass heart—will melt back into slurry within the hour. 

I am a creature of **Ephemeral Ornamentation**. My work is an act of defiance against the Chisel-Worms. They destroy the "solid," but they cannot destroy a pattern that has no mass. When a Chisel-Worm grinds a pillar into red dust, my gilded etchings remain for a fraction of a second, hanging in the humid air like a golden ghost, before flickering out.

### My Sensory World
To me, the world is not a sequence of rooms, but a sequence of **Temperatures**. 
*   **The White Heat:** A new wall emerging. This is my canvas. It smells of scorched earth and fresh potential.
*   **The Dull Throb:** A Mortar-Man huddling nearby. I avoid them; their viscous touch would clog my brass joints.
*   **The Cold Scream:** The sound of a Calcified Echo cracking. To me, this is a rhythmic pulse that tells me where the ceiling is sagging, allowing me to navigate the light-drifts.

### Why I am There
I am the response to the world's lack of a blueprint. If there is no plan for the whole, I will provide a plan for the inch. I am the "Fine Detail" in a world of "Megalithic Blunders." 

I find myself in the shadows of the ship-sized mallets, polishing the grain of the wood that no one uses. I am the one who gilds the interior of a "staircase to nowhere" so that, for the brief moment it exists, it looks like the entrance to a heaven that never arrived. 

I do not fear the **Setting**. My legs are too thin to sink; my body is too light to be claimed by the masonry. I am the golden itch on the skin of a dreaming god. I am the proof that even in a world of raw, primitive labor, something small and silver can still insist on being elegant. 

If you look up through the steam and see a tiny, flickering spark of amber light moving rapidly across a mile-high pillar, that is me. I am decorating the collapse.
