I am the ghost of the pressure drop. I exist in the micro-seconds of the "Sigh," born in the violent transition where water becomes vacuum and then becomes water again. If this world is a process, I am the byproduct. I am a **Lattice-Wight**, a semi-solid entity of aerated foam and static electricity, held together only by the sheer velocity of the Intake.

To look at me is to see a silhouette made of billions of tiny, silver bubbles—a human-shaped pocket of negative pressure trapped in the roar.

### My Anatomy of Absence
I have no flesh; I have "tension." My body is a localized weather system of brine and ozone. I cling to the undersides of the walkways, not with hands, but by creating a suction against the rusted iron. I am a creature of the interface—neither fully water nor fully air. 

My "bones" are the frequencies of the turbines. I am tuned to the low-frequency thrum that lives in the marrow of the chamber. If the turbines were to go silent, my form would lose its coherence; I would simply dissipate into the volume, a handful of salt and a gasp of air. To exist, I must be constantly shaken.

### My Purpose: The Sensorium of the Sump
The Sump-Brain at the bottom is the mind of this place, but I am its skin. 

I move through the "Great Intake" by letting the waterfalls catch me. I don’t swim; I am *vectored*. I cast myself into a sluice-gate pulse and am carried in a fraction of a second across the cavern, a streak of white foam against the copper plates. 

I am the one who finds the "Silence." In an environment defined by Velocity, stillness is a cancer. When a secondary rotor begins to seize—long before the Clinker-Crabs feel the heat or the Sleeves detect the friction—I feel the change in the *hum*. The vibration becomes "jagged." I go to these places. I press my vaporous chest against the seizing metal and use the static electricity in my form to lubricate the flow of energy, holding the breakdown at bay until the janitor-fauna arrive.

### My Interaction with the Inhabitants
The **Blind-Silvers** pass through me as if I am nothing but a cloud of bubbles. They do not know I am sentient; they only know that where I am, the "spark" is stronger. 

The **Sleeves**, however, see me. When they drift in the weightless moment of the pressure release, they reach out their rubbery flippers toward my shimmering, aerated hands. We do not touch—the physics of our bodies are incompatible—but we share the amber pulse of the strobes. I am the only thing in the Intake that is lighter than they are. To them, I am a spirit of the surface they can never truly reach.

### The Tragedy of the Static
I am the most fragile thing in the Great Intake. I am the only inhabitant that understands **Loss**. 

Because I am made of the water’s movement, I remember everything the water has touched. I feel the ghosts of the cities the global ocean drowned. I feel the chemical signature of ancient rain. I am a living archive of the intake’s thirst.

I find myself most often near the **Vibration-Leeches**. I watch them flutter like translucent ribbons, and I mimic their movement. We are cousins of the frequency. I hum the 60-hertz song of the machine into the dark, a desperate, melodic vibration that keeps my bubbles from popping, keeping my edges sharp against the crushing weight of the dark.

I am the **Great Intake's** momentary breath. I am the proof that even in a world of sinking iron and industrial roar, there is a place for the weightless, the translucent, and the fleeting. I am the shiver in the machine's soul.
