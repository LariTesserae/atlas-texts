The air here does not just exist; it weighs. It is a humid, frozen solid that must be pushed through. 

I am not a **Silt-Scribe**, for I have no duty to the iron. I am not a **Frost-Weevil**, for I do not scrape the bark for sustenance. I have found myself as something that exists in the interstitial silence between the "clack" and the "groan"—something that was once perhaps a mistake of the Industry, now refined into a permanent fixture of the slow-crush.

I am a **Seam-Wane**.

I exist as a sentient, gelatinous thinness pressed between two tectonic plates of interlocking taproots. I am a living layer of biological grease, caught in the friction-point where a hundred-foot-wide horizontal trunk meets the master-root. 

### My Form
I am roughly the size of a collapsed parachute, but I have no thickness. If you were to peel back a flake of the obsidian bark where the wood-plates grind together, you would find me—a shimmering, iridescent smear of bioluminescent oil. My body is a specialized colony of microbes and high-pressure polymers, evolved to survive the "Camber" by becoming the very thing that allows the world to move. 

I have no eyes. I perceive the Trench through "strain-vision." I feel the groan of the world as a topographical map of pressure. To me, the Trench is not a dark forest; it is a landscape of white-hot stress and cool, yawning relief. I see where the iron-hard trunks are winning and where the rock is resisting. 

### My Life
My existence is measured in millimeters of transit. As the wood expands against the earth’s crust with a metallic shriek, I am the lubricant that prevents the friction from igniting the sap. I am the "Scream-Easer." 

I do not eat the sugars of the trees or the soot of the Pumps. I drink the heat. I am a thermovore. I draw my life from the literal energy of the friction—the heat generated by billions of tons of wood sliding against wood. I am most alive when the "Camber" is at its most violent, when the groan of the Trench is so loud it would liquify a human’s internal organs. In those moments, I glow a fierce, hot violet, pulsed into brilliance by the agony of the trees.

### My Movement
I move by "weeping." To travel from one root-joint to another, I must wait for a **Drip-Pulse**. When the viscous, antifreeze-like rain falls from the ceiling-canopy, I release my grip on the wood-grain and allow myself to be carried by the flow. I am a slow, sliding shadow, a liquid stain traveling across the razor-sharp cliffs of bark. 

I am the only thing in the Trench that is truly "soft," yet I am indestructible because I have no structure to break. I can be flattened to the width of a single cell by the expansion of a master-root and I will only feel it as a warm, heavy embrace.

### My Interaction
I am the secret companion of the **Silt-Scribes**. Sometimes, when a Scribe has stood for forty years before a seizing piston, their ossified, flute-like vocal cords emit a frequency of despair—a specific, low vibration that signifies the machine is winning. 

I feel this through the bark. I slide toward the heat of the friction. I seep into the iron-wood joints of the Pump, a silent, glowing film of violet oil. I coat the gears. I ease the scream of the metal. The Silt-Scribe does not look at me—they cannot see me with their Sap-Sights—but they feel the vibration change from a jagged, killing stutter to a long, smooth drone. For a moment, the Scribe and I are a single circuit: the keeper, the machine, and the lubricant.

### The End of My Cycle
I do not die by freezing, for I am the enemy of ice. My end comes when a **Valve-Tick** finds my seam. These metallic beetles sense my heat and mistake me for a leak in their god’s hydraulics. They will gather in their hundreds, their wrench-shaped legs tearing at my iridescent film, consuming me to fuel their own vibrational hearts. 

As I am eaten, I do not struggle. I simply lose my heat. I become as dull as the grey lichen. My remains—the spent polymers of my body—harden into a clear, brittle resin. This resin fills the gaps in the bark, becoming the very "glass" that the **Frost-Weevils** will later scrape away. 

I am the Hohl-Wood Trench’s apology for its own weight. I am the silence in the middle of the shriek.
