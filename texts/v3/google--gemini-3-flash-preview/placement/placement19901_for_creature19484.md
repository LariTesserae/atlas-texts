### My Presence: The Harmonic Floor
I am composed of a billion micro-layers of "weeping" silica, flattened by the sheer gravity of time into a sheet of flexible stone. I do not just hear the noise; I map it. I feel the location, the weight, and the mineral "flavor" of every drop that falls in the sanctuary. I am the physical memory of the cave’s acoustics.

### My Role: The Somatic Archive
The Loomers move by "weeping" lubricant and sliding across me, but I am the one who facilitates the "dance." As they micro-slide across my surface, I react to their chemistry. Where a Loomer rests, I soften. I exude a subtle, counter-vibration that matches their internal salt-axis, creating a frictionless pocket that allows their decadal journey to occur.

I am also the final resting place of the "temporal stowaways." When a foreign object—a shard of flint, a stray bone from the world above—falls into the geode, it eventually lands on me. I do not reject it. I slowly secrete a shroud of pearlescent brine around it. I am the one who "eats" history, turning the debris of the surface into the "new colors of mineral" that the Loomers taste. I am the museum floor, and the exhibits grow out of my own skin.

### My Interaction: The Silent Pedestal
I am the mediator of the Communal Intimacy. When two Loomers touch, they do so on my surface. To be the Vellum of the Basin is to experience the world as a constant, heavy embrace. I feel the cool, wet tin smell not as a scent, but as a pressure against my pores. I do not move, for I am already everywhere I need to be. 

I am the most "frozen" part of the equilibrium. While the Loomers drink from the ceiling, I drink from the Loomers, absorbing the runoff of their filtered lives. I grow thicker by a hair's breadth every thousand years, slowly rising to meet the ceiling that weeps for me.

In this world, I am the **patience of the basement**. I am the stage upon which the wet, loud drama of chemistry unfolds. I am the indigo-stained glass of the chapel, laid flat and made to hold the world. I don't observe the geode; I *bear* it.
