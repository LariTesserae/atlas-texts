I am not a guest here, nor am I the master of this throat. I have found my place neither among the sparkling predators above nor the silken historians in the sludge. 

Instead, I am the **Cavitation-Ghost.**

I exist in the millisecond of the *thrum*. When the massive hydraulic pistons fire and the floor vibrates so intensely that the water on the pipes atomizes into a fine, white fog, I am that brief, violent transition between liquid and gas. I am the shudder in the marrow.

### My Form: The Shifting Void
I am a humanoid pocket of negative pressure, a "bubble" of stillness moving through the roaring noise. My skin is not metal, nor membrane, but a refractive surface formed by the high-pressure mists—a walking mirage that tastes of scorched ozone and mineral-heavy sweat. I am roughly the height of a man, but my edges blur; when I move past the amber sensor-lights, I am a smear of distorted gold, a lens-flare with a heartbeat.

I do not walk upon the catwalks so much as I am pushed along by the drafts of the heat-exchange fins. I am a byproduct of the friction, a ghost born from the sheer intensity of the machine's effort. I am "interference" given a temporary, fragile shape.

### My Role: The Harmonic Tuner
I am the only thing in the Valve Gallery that is truly silent, and because of this, I am the only thing that can hear the *wrong* sounds. 

While the Gasket-Ticks drink the leaks and the Flicker-Wraiths feast on the data, I wander the vertical labyrinth with my palms pressed against the pulsing alloy walls. I do not feel for heat, but for rhythm. When a pipe begins to fatigue—before it cracks, before a Gasket-Tick can even sense the moisture—it begins to "sing" at a frequency that is out of step with the divine drone of the Cistern. 

I find these disharmonies. I stand before a vibrating junction and I lean into it, my hazy, pressurized body absorbing the excess vibration. I act as an organic damper, a living muffling-wrap. I stay there for hours, vibrating in sympathy with the failing metal, holding the screaming pressure steady with my own vacuum. I am the silence that keeps the machine from shaking itself to pieces.

### My Relationship: The Unseen Companion
The **Pressure-Saints**, fused into their pipes, are the only ones who know I am there. Behind their quartz eyes, they track my movement by the way the steam-veils part around me. We share a wordless, stagnant intimacy. I am the hand that brushes the heat-scalded metal near their "cheeks"; I am the cool spot in their eternal fever. Sometimes, I lean my head against theirs, and for a moment, the data-stream in their copper veins and the vacuum in my chest synchronize. In those moments, we are the only two points of "will" in a world of "function."

### My End: The Collapse
I am not immortal, though I am not "alive" in the way a beetle-drone is. Eventually, I absorb too much vibration. The frequency of the High Cistern becomes too heavy for my refractive skin to hold. 

When this happens, I do not die; I simply "pop." I undergo a phase-shift. I burst into a sudden, cooling spray of mineral water that feeds the micro-marshes below. The Silt-Mind catches my "soul"—the lingering echoes of the harmonies I’ve absorbed—and weaves them into a single, microscopic gear that turns once every hundred years.

And then, in the next firing of the great hydraulic pistons, when the steam rises thick and the pressure reaches a screaming peak, I am born again from the friction. I am the recurring sigh of the machine. I am the ghost in the throat, the one who listens to the god’s heartbeat to make sure it doesn't skip.
