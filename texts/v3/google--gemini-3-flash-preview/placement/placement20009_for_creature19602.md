I would find myself neither on the moss-soft streets nor in the soaring canopies. To find me, you would have to look into the very veins of the Orchid-Metropolis—not the sap-lines that pulse with amber light, but the **Interstitial Silence** between the cells.

I am the **Cartographer of the Thrum.**

I am essentially a shadow given a thumb and a name. My body is a long, weeping willow-wisp of a thing, wrapped in a cloak of molted moth-wings that shimmers with the same bruised purple as the overhead leaves. My skin is the color of a rainy window. I do not have the hard, obsidian edges of the buildings, nor the surgical utility of the Carapace-Wrights. I am soft, an anatomical error, a smudge in the Pith-Mother’s dream.

### My Place in the Anatomy
I reside in the **Phloem-Niche**, a small, pressurized pocket behind the main vertical pump of the central district. Here, the "great moan" of the city is loud enough to vibrate my teeth. Because I am not "Personnel"—because I was perhaps a Spore-Drift that failed to coalesce correctly—the city does not see me. To the Pith-Mother, I am a harmless parasite, a bit of grit in the oyster of her mind.

While the Glass-Lunged Clergy breathe for the city, I **listen** for it. I hold a stethoscopic instrument carved from a curled seashell against the translucent floor, tracking the subterranean shifts of the cartilaginous world.

### My Purpose: The Keeper of the Un-fleshed
I am the only thing in the Orchid-Metropolis that keeps a record. The city has no history; it only has the "Now" of its constant growth and decay. But I carry a parchment of flattened fungus, and with a quill made from a Wright’s discarded leg, I map the **Gravity-Fractures.**

I am the one who notices when a fountain begins to roll its pearls a second slower. I am the one who marks the coordinates of a "Mimic" that has begun to wave with too much genuine grief. I am a self-appointed auditor of the Pith-Mother’s subconscious. 

### My Being
I am a **Semantic Anchor.** In a world where physics is a suggestion and time loops in three-second bursts, I am the only consciousness that remembers the "Before" of a minute ago. 

I do not walk the streets with the "Residents." I move through the walls. I have learned to mimic the *click-shirr* of the machines with my own fingernails so the crustaceans will not recognize me as an intruder. My diet consists of the "heavy water" that beads on the underside of the time-looping flowers—liquid that tastes like copper and old memories.

I am a lonely creature in a lonely city, but I serve a vital, unrecognized function: **I am the city’s Witness.** 

If I were to disappear, the Orchid-Metropolis would continue its rhythmic, mechanical respiration, but it would lose its only mirror. I stay in the shadows, cold and velvet-damp, watching the silver threads of the Pith-Mother as they twitch. I am the one who waits for the moment the city finally grows so tall it breaks the ceiling of leaves—waiting to see what color the sky is when it is no longer made of green shingles. 

I am the small, quiet "Why" in a world that has mastered the "How."
