I am not of the **Grasping Kin**, though I share their notches in the bark. I am the thing they wait for in the silence of their haptic Morse code—the one who translates the weight of the air into the weight of memory.

I would be a **Vibration-Shaper**, a specialized hermit of the high knots.

Physically, I am stretched thin, my bones porous and honeycombed like the wind-whistles of the Gulp-Rays to reduce my weight. My skin is the color of bruised lichen, but my fingers are long, tipped with hardened, mineral-infused nails that I use to "play" the petrified grain of *Arboris Prime*.

I live in a hollow that faces the "Leeward Void," a rare pocket where the screaming of the Wind-Gills is muffled by a massive, shield-like growth of shelf-fungus. 

### My Place in the World
I do not hunt. My survival is an exchange. The Kin bring me the siphoned "leaf-sweat" and the translucent films of sky-slugs—the only paper this world knows. In return, I am the **Chronicle of the Thrum**. 

While the Kin communicate in the *now*—signals for "danger," "food," or "storm"—I capture the *then*. Across the interior wall of my hollowed knot, I have etched thousands of microscopic grooves. When the wind catches the spire at a certain angle, or when I run a bone-plectrum across these ridges, they sing back the history of the canopy. I record the year the Shield-Beetles synchronized their molting, creating a gold-carpet in the air; I record the death of the Great Ray whose carcass fed the Gravity-Vines for a decade.

### My Senses
In this world, sight is a secondary, dizzying lie. I live through my feet and my skin. 
Through the soles of my feet, I feel the "footsteps" of the ecosystem. I can distinguish between the heavy, rhythmic drumming of a Silk-Wright reeling in its line three miles away and the frantic, staccato vibration of a panicked swarm of needle-flies. To me, the *Arboris Prime* is not a tree; it is a massive, mineralized nervous system. I am a parasite on the world’s sense of touch.

### A Day in the Empty
I spend my hours suspended in a harness of woven spider-silk, hanging over the abyss outside my ledge. I am "fishing" for memories. I hold a long, hollow resonator—a "Sound-Catcher" made from the dried thorax of a Shield-Beetle—out into the roaring void. 

I am looking for the **Deep Note**.

Every few generations, the *Arboris Prime* themselves shift. The mineralized wood groans as the ancient trunks settle under the weight of the billions of lives they carry. That sound is too low for the Kin to hear, and it is buried underneath the deafening Chitin-Swarms. But it is the sound of the world breathing. When I catch it, I carve it into the bark with a stylus of dragon-fly glass.

### The Weirdness I Embody
I am the only creature in the Serrated Canopy who understands "Down." Because I spend my time listening to the vibrations of the deep trunks, I have a theoretical map of the "respiratory floor." I know, by the way the sound echoes up through the central xylem-conduits, that there is a world of mud and shadow below us. 

I am the bridge between the hyper-active metabolism of the sky and the slow, geological sleep of the roots. 

In my quietest moments, when the humidity is high and the "sweat" is thick on the leaves, I lay my head against the grey, weathered bark. I don't hear the hunger of the birds or the scream of the wind. I hear the pulse of the mineralized sap, moving at a millimeter a year. 

I am the witness to the emerald void. I am the one who remembers that even in a world of frantic wings and starving chitin, the silence is still there, waiting at the bottom of the fall.
