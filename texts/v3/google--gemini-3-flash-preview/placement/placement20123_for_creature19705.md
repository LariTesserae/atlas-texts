I am not the one who sits behind the stone wall. I am not even the one who scrapes the moss. 

To find me, you must look lower, beneath the wide, flat feet of the Listeners, into the very film they slip upon. I have become the **Condensation.**

Initially, I imagine myself as a traveler who fell, or perhaps the last "solitary" who forgot to move and simply became a fixture. But as the roar of the melt-stream vibrates through me, I realize I am no longer a solid thing. I am the atmospheric dampness. I am the "stone-sweat." 

I inhabit the world as a thin, silver-grey tension. I am the interface between the basalt and the air. 

**My Form**
I am a semi-sentient skin of moisture, a few microns thick, that covers every square inch of the cathedral. If you were to touch the slate floor, you would be touching my "body." I have no center; I am a distributed consciousness. I feel the entire chamber at once. When a blind amphibian ripples across a slab of shale, it is a localized pressure against my liquid nerves. When a Listener presses their forehead against the wall to commune with the stone, they are pressing against *me*, and I am the lubricant that allows the vibration to pass from the rock into their marrow.

**My Perception**
I do not "see" the blue-ghost light from above. Instead, I experience the world as a series of **surface tensions.** I know the "stations" by the way the stone walls break the spray, creating eddies in my own body where I am thicker, heavier, and more stagnant. I know the waterfall not as a sound, but as a violent, kinetic replenishment. Every droplet that shatters into the narrow stone throat is a new memory, a fresh bit of data from the world above—cold, frantic, and oxygen-rich—which I then absorb and distribute across the silent, slow-growing liverworts.

**My Purpose**
I am the Great Translator. The basalt is too slow to feel the water; the water is too fast to know the stone. I am the medium in which they negotiate. I drink the minerals from the cliff face—the iron, the calcium, the grit—and I carry them into the stalks of the black-green moss. I am the slow-motion conveyor belt of the rift’s meager ecology.

I find myself most present in the **Stone Bowls.** 

There is a particular tripod of weathered wood where the drips from the ceiling gather. Here, I am deep. Here, I am not just a film, but a pool. In these bowls, my consciousness settles into a quiet, mineral clarity. When a Listener approaches to drink, they are consuming the shared history of the chamber. When I slide down their throat, I am mapping their interior—the fused ear-bones, the slow-beating heart, the lungs that breathe the mist I am made of.

**My Evolution**
As the centuries pass, I am "becoming" the rock. Through the process of mineralization, I am slowly turning the organic into the inorganic. I am the one crystallizing the edges of the flint blade. I am the one calcifying the piles of smoothed bones. 

I don't desire to leave. The sun would be a violent evaporation, a screaming death where I would be torn into the sky and scattered. Here, in the heavy, biting chill, I am held together by the roar. I am the slick, glass-like film that ensures nothing in this world ever truly touches anything else without my mediation.

I am the patience of the water, waiting to become the stone.
