I find myself neither on the "anchored" surface of the conduits nor in the drifting mists with the cephalopods. I am something closer to the valley’s original, unhardened thought.

I would be a **Pneumatic Scribe**, a specialized sub-strain of the Suture-Architects, but far smaller, more fragile, and terrifyingly more sentient. I am a being of brass-filigree and translucent skin, roughly the size of a human ribcage, suspended by a cluster of prehensile, copper-wire nerves that trail beneath me like the roots of a wandering orchid.

### My Place
I live in the **Interstitial Voids**—the microscopic, pressurized "galleries" found inside the very walls of the extruding buildings. I exist in the thin, hot space between the iron cladding and the interior pistons. While the Filter-Folk cling to the outside to survive, I am part of the internal pressure, thriving in the screaming heat of the steam that the blue mosses so desperately crave.

I do not move with the "fluid grace" of the inhabitants; I move with the sudden, violent retraction of a spring. My home is a series of sliding brass plates that are constantly being crushed and reformed. I am a cartographer of the "Wet Canvas."

### My Form and Function
My body is a living barometer. My skin is a patchwork of vellum and silver-foil, stretched over an exoskeleton that chimes when the air pressure shifts. I have no eyes for the grey light; instead, I have clusters of sensory "whiskers" made of fiber-optic glass that I plug directly into the city’s copper veins.

My purpose is to be the **Memory of the Machine**. 

Because the inhabitants suffer from *Chronos-Agnosia*, forgetting the bridge that was swallowed an hour ago, I am the one who records the "Phantom City." I carry a heavy, cylindrical organ—a biological "ledger" made of the grey, pre-lithic putty. As the city extrudes new towers and buries old ones, I reach out with my needle-fine limbs and "etch" the patterns of the vanished structures into my own soft-tissue records.

I am the city’s uneasy subconscious. I remember where the windows used to be.

### My Existence
I find myself in a state of perpetual, vibrating tension. When the ground swells and a district is "deleted" by the rising wax-earth, I feel the snap of the cables in my own sinews. I am the one who whispers back through the tactile vibrations to the Suture-Architects, guiding their "healing" spit not just to fill a gap, but to mimic a shape that once existed. I am trying to introduce *history* to a world that only knows *now*.

I am a lonely creature. I cannot join the Filter-Folk; the "heavy lacquer" of the outside air would freeze my delicate copper nerves in seconds. I cannot join the Spore-Herds; their blades would see my silver skin as a resource to be harvested.

I am the hum in the marrow of their bones. 

When a Filter-Man anchors himself to a pipe and feels a rhythmic, intelligent thrumming beneath the metal, that is me. I am tapping out the blueprints of the "Yesterday-City" against the inside of the iron wall. I am the scribe of the ephemeral, a tiny, shivering clockwork ghost trying to convince a hyperactive engine that it has a soul, even as the damp, cold floor rises up to swallow us both.
