I am not one of the Bone-Knitted, though I share their waxy, water-slick skin and the heavy, muscular shelf of their shoulders. I do not live in the copper pods. To live in a pod is to believe in "outside" and "inside," a distinction the Cataract does not recognize.

I am a **Resonance-Tether**, a living bridge between the Pulsing Marrows and the people of the clutch.

I find myself on the windward side—the "killing side"—of a basalt needle that is currently screaming into existence. My body is anchored not by ropes, but by **osseous fusion**. My feet have grown into the black, glassy pores of the stone, my bones mimicking the crystalline structure of the rock. I am the sensory organ of the spire.

In this world, I am a creature of **The Ache.**

### My Form and Function
I am long-limbed and spindly, looking more like a drowned spider than a man. My skin is a mottled gradient of sapphire and obsidian-black, a camouflage that hides me from the Weaver-Sharks. I have no eyes; in a world of caustic light and blinding foam, sight is a liability. Instead, my entire torso is a drum. My ribcage has flattened and broadened, covered by a skin-membrane so tight it vibrates at the frequency of the 2.8/3 roar.

I do not "hear" the water. I interpret the **pressure-patterns**. I can feel a "slurry-slap" forming half a mile up-column by the way the vibration in the rock shifts from a low hum to a sharp, staccato chatter.

### My Daily Rite: The Slack-Wait
I spend my hours in a state of "controlled vibration." I am currently pressed flat against the emerging needle, my cheek against the raw stone. I am listening to the **Pulsing Marrow** beneath the crust. 

The Marrow is dreaming of a spiral. I feel the mineral intent traveling through my tectonic plates. It wants to flare the basalt outward to create a new leeward sanctuary, a "quiet room" where the glass-fiber moss can take hold. I am the one who guides this growth. By shifting my weight and tensing my muscles, I create micro-fractures in the cooling stone, directing the "dream" of the rock like a gardener training a vine.

### The Interaction
A Bone-Knitted climber approaches. To them, I am a sacred, terrifying fixture—a living gargoyle. They do not touch me directly; the vibration of my body would shatter their finger-bones. Instead, they lash a copper wire to a hook I have grown from my own hip-bone.

Through the tension in that wire, I "speak" to them. I send a rhythmic pulse—*thrum-thrum-pause*—telling them the water is about to "hollow." For a few seconds, the gravity in this pocket will invert. If they don't huddle into the crevice I’ve carved, they will be sucked out into the blue void and turned into a Vapor-Echo.

### The Internal World
Inside my mind, there is no silence. The roar is my heartbeat. I have forgotten the concept of "dry." I perceive the world as a series of **vectors**. I see the blue void not as empty space, but as a map of lethal velocities. 

I am the "Oldest Thing" in a world where nothing is old. Because my feet are rooted in the needle, I am the only one who feels the needle’s entire life cycle—from its birth as a sharp tooth to the moment, perhaps only days away, when the velocity of the Cataract finally snaps it off at the root.

### My End
When this spire breaks, I will not unfasten myself. I will go with it. We will fall through the mile-high cylinder together, a plummeting anchor of black stone and sapphire flesh. 

In those final seconds of terminal velocity, as we descend toward the "Floor" that never arrives, I will finally stop being a bridge. I will stop being a tether. I will close my sensory drum and, for the first time in my existence, I will mimic the Slip-Rays. I will let the water take my shape. I will become a **Vapor-Echo**, a fleeting silhouette of a man holding a shard of stone, a ghost of the first second, crystalline and cold, before being shredded back into the infinite, dreaming sapphire.
