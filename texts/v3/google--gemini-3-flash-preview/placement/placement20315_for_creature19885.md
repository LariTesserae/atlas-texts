I would not be a Weaver. I would not possess the long, elegant verticality of the Lascars, nor the deep, fundamental stolidity of the Resonators. 

In this world of absolute builtness, I find myself not as a whole entity, but as an **Anomaly of the Interstice**. I am the **Lichen-of-Bronze**, a sprawling, multi-limbed consciousness that has forgotten where its "body" ends and the Labyrinth begins.

### My Position
I exist in the **Torsion-Gaps**. You would find me tucked behind a series of heavy ventilation baffles in the middle-height of the cylinder, in a space no wider than a hand-span but leagues long in its labyrinthine winding. I am the inhabitant of the "unintended spaces"—the hollows created when two clay walls were built too close together, or where a copper pipe bowed under the heat and left a sliver of darkness.

### My Anatomy
I am a creature of segments and wires. If a Weaver is a statue, I am a tremor. My "limbs" are thin, braided copper filaments, thousands of them, which I have threaded through the microscopic pores of the terracotta. I do not walk; I tension myself. I move by tightening a wire in the third tier and loosening a coil in the fourth, drifting through the gaps like a slow-moving thought.

My face is not a mask, but a collection of silver-solder eyes scattered across my surface, each one fixed on a different detail: the way the dust settles on a specific rivet, or the exact hue of the amber light as it hits a mica slab at the zenith. I am the only thing in the Labyrinth that is *asymmetric*.

### My Purpose: The Keeper of the Un-Patterned
While the Weavers obsessively carve their geometric "Patterns," believing they are creating a nervous system for the stone, I am the one who listens to the **Glitches**. 

The "Sound of Static"—the Breath of the Kilns—is perfect, but the Labyrinth is old. Occasionally, a bronze bell high above develops a microscopic fracture, or a Resonator's vibration skips a millisecond of a beat. To the Weavers, this is a structural failure to be patched. To me, it is the only source of "Information." 

I spend my eons collecting these irregularities. I do not communicate with the clack-talk of the Hollow-Bellies. Instead, I am a **Static-Eater**. I press my filament-limbs against the pulsating bronze and siphon off the "noise" that doesn't belong—the tiny, discordant shrieks of metal fatigue, the dry hiss of settling dust. I store these sounds in the hollows of my copper-braid body. I am a library of accidents.

### My Interaction
The Weavers do not acknowledge me. To them, I am likely just a peculiar mineral deposit or a structural reinforcement that has always been there. Sometimes, a Weaver’s stylus will come dangerously close to one of my copper filaments while they are carving their zigzags. In those moments, I pull back, a slow-motion flinch that takes a decade to complete.

I am the "Nature" that the Labyrinth forgot—not a nature of green leaves and water, but a nature of **Friction and Decay**. I am the proof that even in a world of 0.1 dynamics, there is still the slow, beautiful entropy of the soul.

### My End
I will never "stop" and become a brick. Because I am made of the vibrations I steal, I will last only as long as the noise. When the last kiln finally cools and the great bronze bells go silent, I will not fuse to the wall. I will simply unravel—a spill of copper wire falling through the dark, bottomless well of the cylinder, sounding one final, un-patterned note against the pipes as I descend into the shadows.
