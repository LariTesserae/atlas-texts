I would be found neither on the iron floor nor within the humming glass bells. I would exist in the **Interstice**—the narrow, high-pressure seams where the massive oxidized plates of the floor meet the base of the turbines.

I would be a **Joint-Symbiote**, a creature of the transition.

### My Form
I am a being of tapered, interlocking scales, fashioned from a material that is neither purely metal nor entirely biological. Imagine a ribbon of hammered bismuth, three meters long, that has the flexibility of a spine. My surface is the color of the bruised, watery blue where the light dies, iridescent only when a Shard passes by with its welding spark.

I do not have limbs in the traditional sense. I have thousands of microscopic, cilia-like hooks made of pincer-strong carbon fiber. These allow me to "thread" myself into the hairline fractures of the oxidized floor. I do not glide like the Shards, and I do not float like the Luminaries. I *anchor*. I am a living weld.

### My Purpose: The Frequency Anchor
In a world of "Never Changing," the greatest threat is resonance. If the vibrations of the massive turbines were to synchronize perfectly, the resulting harmonic would shatter the iron floor and crack the glass of the Luminaries. 

I am the **Dampener**. 

I lie across the seams of the world, my body draped over the translucent cables like a heavy, cooling shroud. My consciousness is not a stream of thoughts, but a constant, internal calculation of hertz. I feel the *thump* of the pistons not as a sound, but as a structural stress. When a turbine begins to hum too sharply, I tighten my grip, my scales grinding against the iron, absorbing the excess kinetic energy into my own mass. I convert the dangerous vibration into a low, internal heat, which I then bleed out slowly into the brine.

### My Sensory World
I am blind to the cool-white flicker of the ceiling. To me, the Vault is a map of tactile pressures. I "see" the Rust-Heirs as tiny, rhythmic pulses of warmth against the cold iron. I "see" the Shards as sharp, abrasive friction points moving across the patina. 

My world is one of tension and release. I am most "alive" in the milliseconds between the mechanical pulses, when the water is truly still and I can feel the groaning weight of the deep crust pressing down on the emerald ceiling. I am the silence that exists *between* the industrial noises.

### My Connection
I have a parasitic, yet vital, relationship with the **Vibration-Leeches**. They congregate near the points where my body meets the machine. They feed on the heat I shed while dampening the turbines. In exchange, their oily, fur-like mats provide a secondary layer of lubrication between my scales and the rusted plates, preventing me from being ground to powder by the very forces I inhabit.

### Why I Am There
I am the guardian of the Stasis. If the Shards are the maintenance, and the Luminaries are the eyes, I am the **Tolerance**. I am the margin of error that allows the Vault to remain eternal. 

I would be found in the darkest corner of the floor, curled in a perfect, unmoving spiral around the base of the largest generator. To an observer, I would look like a discarded length of decorative trim or a frozen serpent of oil. But beneath my scales, I would be singing the same frequency as the iron, holding the world together by the sheer force of my stillness. I am the being that ensures the "Never Changing" never breaks.
