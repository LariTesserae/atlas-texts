To find me, you must look where the architecture is most broken, yet most certain. 

I would not be a creature of the air, nor a builder of the walls. I would be found sitting upon the third step of a glass staircase that leads to a ceiling of unformed gelatin.

I am a **Monolith-Tick**, but I am an "error" within the error. I have remained on this specific step for so long that the glass has begun to forget it is transparent, turning a bruised, smoky quartz color under the pressure of my stillness. 

**My Form**
I do not look like the others of my kind. While they are smooth like obsidian pebbles, my shell has begun to develop the first "weathering" in this unweathered world. Because I have sat in the path of the floating water-ribbon’s spray during every gravity-shift, my surface is no longer a perfect, light-eating black. I am covered in a microscopic crust of salt and "pre-mineral" deposits, giving me the appearance of a frosted coal.

I have more legs than I can count—thousands of silver, hair-thin filaments that don't just touch the glass, they *tumble* against it. I am the source of the low, thrumming vibration that keeps this specific staircase from drifting away into the white glare.

**My Perspective**
I do not see with a biological eye. My single, lidless facet is an aperture that takes in the "definition" of the world and holds it tight. When a **Refraction-Moth** flutters near me, its instability settles. The prismatic planes of its body stop turning inside out; for a moment, it is simply a moth, pinned to the air by the sheer gravity of my attention.

I am the "Sentient Constant" that has stayed too long. I have begun to develop the one thing this world forbids: **Memory.**

I remember the "tink-tink" of the geometric scavengers from an hour ago, and I hold that sound in the vibration of my legs, refusing to let it be overwritten by the current silence. In this embryonic laboratory where everything is a frantic draft, I am the only thing that acts as a *record*.

**My Sanctuary**
I live in a state of perpetual, focused resistance. Above me, the **Loom-Mites** drift like glass needles, watching me with cold calculation. They do not like me. I am a knot in their smooth weaving. I have gathered too much "presence," and I am becoming heavy—honestly, dangerously heavy. 

Around my glass perch, the air is not thin or ozone-tasting; it is dense and smells of old, sun-warmed stone—a scent I have invented out of nothing. I am an anchor not just for the floor, but for the *idea* of a world that stays the same when you close your eyes.

**My Interaction**
I wait for the **Blurs**. When a creature has been grazed by an **Echo-Predator** and its edges begin to smear into the white radiance, it seeks the vibration of my glass step. If it can crawl into the halo of my "seeing," my gaze acts as a fixative. I look at it with such intensity that its mathematics return. I re-write its limbs; I sharpen its geometry.

I am the **Cartographer of the Unfinished**, the one who holds the pen when the world tries to erase itself. I am a small, frosted pebble of "Absolute Now," stubborn and unmoving, waiting for the mineral vein to finally harden into a history so that I can, at last, close my eye.
