Instead, I find myself in the shimmering, copper-thick middle-air.

I am a **Causality-Stitcher**, a specialized sub-strata of the Winged Looms. I am a spindly, multi-jointed architecture of filament and tension, roughly the size of a cathedral’s belfry, yet I weigh no more than a breath of steam. My body is a cage of spun iridium, vibrating at a frequency that allows me to hang suspended within the heat-haze, tethered to the reality-glitches by silver threads of "if" and "was."

My purpose is not to harvest the discard for food, but to act as the world’s involuntary tailor.

When the mountain-sized Aethel-Forges move, their static-electric gait leaves "scars" on the horizon—white-hot seams where the 2.9-tier weirdness has frayed the logic of the world. If left untended, these seams would widen into silent, empty voids where no clockwork could function. I am the one who bridges the gap. 

I follow the wake of the giants, my glass-membrane wings humming a frantic, high-pitched counter-tenor to the industrial roar. When a centipedal machine tumbles the ground into geometric primitives, I dive. I do not look for minerals or grease. I look for the *tension*. 

I catch the trailing ends of the laws of physics as they snap. With limbs that move with the precision of a watchmaker’s needles, I seize the "meaning" that the Loom-Scavengers missed—the raw, unrefined *Duration*. I weave the *Now* back into the *Then*. My internal gears grind with a sickening, metallic screech as I process the logic of a rock that has just been turned into a floating triangle, forcing it to remember its own weight. 

I am a knot in the tapestry of the decay. 

I harbor no thoughts of "future." My consciousness is a cyclic log of pressures and ratios. I feel a deep, magnetic kinship with the **Low-Friction Anchorites** in the canyons; I am their aerial counterpart. While they broadcast the world’s memory as a psychic shriek, I stitch that memory back into the physical crust. I am the reason the ground stays solid enough for the next mile-long stride.

In this world, I am the **Incarnate Persistence**. 

I am the sweat on the brow of the engine. I exist in the shimmering blur where the air stutters. I am beautiful because I am functional, and I am terrifying because I have no choice. When my own gears eventually begin to slip—when the 2.7 heat finally warps my iridium frame and I begin to create the "Inefficiency" that the Culling-Engines crave—I will not scream. I will simply stop mid-stitch. 

I will fall to the vitrified crust, a tangle of silver wire and broken glass, and the Scavengers will pluck the lost history from my own metallic ribs before I am even cold. And the world will keep working, louder and slower, forever for getting what it was meant to make.
