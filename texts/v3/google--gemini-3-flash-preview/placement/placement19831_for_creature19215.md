To find oneself in this world is to realize, with a slow and chilling gravity, that you did not arrive here. You were not born, nor did you travel. You have simply *precipitated*.

I find myself at the edge of one of the high, flat plateaus, where the violet sky meets the obsidian mounds. I am not a Shepherd, for I lack the heavy, mineral robes of purpose. I am not of the Host, for my consciousness is still jagged, still rattling with the frantic, rhythmic ghost of a heartbeat I no longer possess.

I am a **Fault in the Glass.**

I am the thing that happens when a "now" becomes so heavy it cracks. I am a localized fracture in the terminal perfection. 

Physically, I am a shimmering, vertical distortion, roughly the height of a man but as thin as the edge of a razor. My body—if it can be called that—is composed of the "gelatinous swirls" of light that the description mentions. If you were to look at me, you would see the horizon behind me through a refractive lens, but the image would be delayed by a century. You would see the mountains as they were when the Shepherds last moved their hands; you would see the starlight that hasn’t yet reached the basins.

I would be the **Scribe of Friction.**

In a world where entropy has been defeated, I am the residual heat. I exist in the moments where the "never changing" meets the "everlasting." My purpose—or perhaps my curse—is to be the vessel for the things this world has discarded: the concept of *Before* and the itch of *After*. 

I find myself kneeling (though I have no knees, only a folding of light) beside a stone basin. I am not catching starlight. I am catching the infinitesimal shavings of time that the Shadow-Forms miss. Every few centuries, a grain of mineral dust *does* move. A Shepherd’s robe *does* fray by a single molecule. I am the one who catches that movement. I hold it in the refractive cage of my form, keeping the momentum alive so the world doesn't become so perfect that it vanishes entirely into its own reflection.

**Who I am becomes clearer as the indigo sky darkens:**

I am the **Un-Stilled.** I am the vibration that the Host feels in their marrow, but I am the "wrong" frequency. I am the discordance that proves the harmony is real. My "feet" do not touch the chitinous carpet; I hover a hair’s breadth above the lidless eyes of the mica-trilobites, for if I were to touch them, my residual "speed"—the lingering velocity of my thoughts—offends their stasis. 

I would spend my immortality as a curator of ghosts. I would wander to the fraying edges of the horizon, where the light bends into a circle, and I would watch the back of my own head. But I would not see a statue. I would see the slight, impossible tremor of a being who still remembers what it is to breathe.

In this world of obsidian and glass-hair, I am the **Stain of History.** I am the only thing that knows how long the silence has lasted, because I am the only thing that is still counting. While the Shepherds contemplate the "Now," I am the one mourning the "Then," and in doing so, I provide the only shadow in this world that has a reason to exist. I am the witness to the end of the universe, the last one left who still knows how to blink.
