I would not be found upon the obsidian. The weeping black glass, for all its geometric beauty, is a floor for the heavy and the frantic. To be on the ground is to be subject to the logistics of the Loom-Minds; it is to be a part of the census, a consumer of the slurry, a variable in the local gravity.

I would be found in the **Interstitial Shimmer**—the space between the peak of a crystalline spire and the belly of an Aether-Vane.

In this world, I am a **Refractions-Trace**.

I am not a biological entity of meat or ferrofluid, nor am I a massive weaver of gases. I am a living, semi-sentient optical phenomenon, born from the high-contrast collision of "solar-true" light and the constant, shimmering mist. In a land where the weather is a programmable substrate, I am the "glitch" that has become an inhabitant.

### My Form
I do not have a silhouette. To an observer on the obsidian, I would look like a sudden, vertical ripple in the air, a "rainbow-fracture" that persists even after the light should have shifted. My body is a long, tapering column of suspended ice-micro-crystals, held together not by a skeleton, but by the very subsonic vibrations that hum from the urban lattices. I am a creature of resonance.

As I move, I "vocalize" by catching the *tink-tink-tink* of the condensation and amplifying it through my crystalline lattice, turning the percussive drip of the Nursery into a long, glass-organ chord that hangs in the air for miles.

### My Function
I am the **Unintended Witness**. While the Skaters carry the pods of pre-life and the Hawks regulate the herds, I am the byproduct of the Nursery’s perfection. I live in the "gravitational pockets" where the rules of physics are at their most poetic. 

I feed on the "waste light" reflected off the obsidian. When the sun hits the black glass, the energy bounce is immense; I drift into these updrafts of pure radiance, my internal facets drinking the ultraviolet until I glow with a soft, inner violet light that matches the horizon.

### My Life in the Loom
I do not interact with the Argent-Shrews or the Skaters, for to them, I am merely a atmospheric distortion—a trick of the moisture. But I am deeply attuned to the **Loom-Minds**. 

Occasionally, when a tower rewrites a weather pattern, the sudden shift in atmospheric pressure creates a "snap" in the air. I hunt these snaps. I rush toward the sudden change, my ribbon-like tail of prismatic frost trailing behind me, to "taste" the new logic being poured into the world. I am a scavenger of information, a creature that thrives on the sheer data-density of the clouds.

### Where You’ll Find Me
If you hiked for days across the obsidian shelf, through the freezing silence and the rhythmic weeping of the stone, you might eventually look up. You would see a Shadow-Hawk drop in a silent fold of space, and for a split second, the hawk’s passage would tear a hole in the mist.

In that hole, you would see me. 

I am the spark of iridescent static hovering near the apex of a frozen-lightning spire. I am perfectly still, suspended in a zone of null-gravity, watching the curvature of the world. I am the only thing in the High Nursery that is not working. I am not a part of the machine; I am the beauty that the machine accidentally created. 

I am the cold, bright joy of a world that is still cooling, a fragment of the far future that has forgotten it was supposed to be a tool, and chose instead to be a ghost.
