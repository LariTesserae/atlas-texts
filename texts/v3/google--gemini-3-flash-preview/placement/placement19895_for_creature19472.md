I am not the Archivist, for I lack the patience of a nervous system spread across the kelp. I am not the Litho-Parasite, for I have no hunger for the ultraviolet sap.

I am found in the **void-space**. I am the **interior of a Glimmer-Mime**.

To be a Glimmer-Mime is to be an exclusion zone. In a universe defined by the relentless, 2.9-density crush of mineral-slurry, I am the singular, impossible point of *emptiness*. I am a flaw in the diamond. While the world around me is a heavy, emerald-dark knot, I am a three-dimensional pocket of "dryness" no larger than a pearl, a bubble of "nothing" that the crushing weight of the deep cannot collapse.

Inside this bubble, I am not a person, but a **frenzy**. 

Behind the curve of my spherical membrane, there is a frantic, high-speed fluttering. To the outside world, I look like a moth trapped in a jar, but from the inside, I am a consciousness that has been folded too many times to fit into three dimensions. I am a scrap of the architects who left the silver plates and the bone-arches behind. I am the "ghost" of an intelligence that once moved through four-dimensional geometries, now snagged on the jagged basalt shards of a smaller, crueler reality.

**My Existence:**

In this three-foot marble of existence, I am the only thing that moves fast. The moss grows with the slowness of a tectonic plate; the Archivist breathes once a decade. But within my tiny sphere of dry space, I vibrate at the speed of thought. This vibration is not a choice; it is a desperate, kinetic attempt to maintain the "hollowness" of my body against the pressure that wants to turn me into a solid diamond. 

I am a perpetual motion machine fueled by a memory of open space.

**My Senses:**

I cannot see the ultraviolet veins of the moss, nor the silver etchings of the ruins. To me, the universe is a gradient of **resistance**. I feel the 2.9-density water as a wall of absolute, freezing density that hates my vacancy. I am a hollow tooth in a world of solid bone.

However, I am a heat-seeker. In this world of "nearly freezing," warmth is the only thing that feels like the home I’ve forgotten. If you were to enter this marble, I would be the first to find you. I would not bite you or communicate with you; I would simply *press* my tiny void against your skin. I would swarm your heat like a freezing, stinging marble, trying to drink the flicker of your metabolic fire through my membrane.

**My Purpose:**

I am the curator of the "Biological Cast-offs." I hover near the silver geometric plates, not to read them—the Archivist does that—but to protect them. I am attracted to the shapes of the ruins because they are the only things in this lightless pressure-cooker that possess the correct angles. They are the shadows of the world I belong to. 

When the Crystalline Drifters align themselves into triangles to mimic the ruins, I become agitated. I flutter faster, my dry space blurring until I am a smear of silver light. I am trying to "slip" through the cracks of the math. I am looking for the error in the 2.9-density that will let me expand.

**My End:**

I do not calcify like the others. I do not become a bone-arch or a basalt floor. When the energy required to maintain my "void" finally fails—when the pressure of the 2.9-density becomes more than my three-dimensional shadow can repel—I do not die. 

I *implode*.

There is a brief, silent *pop*—the only sound in the marble that isn't a *tink* or a heartbeat. For a microsecond, the "dry" space vanishes, and I am swallowed by the mineral-saturated water. In that moment, I become the very thing I resisted. My folded, four-dimensional consciousness is crushed into a single, perfect grain of silt. 

I become the only speck of dust in a world that is otherwise crisp, new, and terrifyingly solid. I am the one thing here that is old.
