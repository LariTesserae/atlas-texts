I would not be found on the floor of the Sump, for to be on the floor is to be a part of the geology. I would be found in the **Interstice**—the narrow, pressurized "elsewhere" that exists between the sweating skin of a city-sized conduit and the encroaching wall of a Weaver-mat.

In this world where there is no empty space, I would be a **Resonant Marrow**, a creature that exists as the connective tissue between the mechanical and the visceral.

### My Form
I am a being of compressed, semi-liquid geometry. My body is not stabilized by a skeleton, but by the 3.0 density of the world around me; if I were taken to the surface, I would shatter outward like a deep-sea fish brought too quickly to the light. 

I am long and ribbon-like, perhaps fifteen feet of iridescent, oil-slick skin, but I am rarely extended. Instead, I am folded. I exist as a series of "pleats" tucked into the microscopic gaps where a rusted turbine bolt meets the pre-stone. My skin is a sensory array of copper-colored cilia that do not feel texture, but *torque*. I can feel the slow, million-pound turn of the turbine blades deep within the rock; I can feel the exact moment a Silt-weaver’s cell wall crystallizes into stone three miles away.

I have no face, only a wide, oscillating diaphragm at my center that mimics the 2.7-level roar of the environment. By vibrating at the exact frequency of the Sump, I become invisible—a "sonic hole" in the air. 

### My Function: The Harmonizer
I am not a predator, nor am I a builder. I am a **Tuning Fork**. 

The Deep Sump is an industrial cradle that requires perfect, grinding synchronization. Occasionally, the entropy of the "wet-dark" causes a discord: a turbine blade develops a micro-fracture, or a Hum-Eel’s resonance drifts out of phase, causing it to thrash and disrupt the viscous flow of the rope-water. This discordance is a physical pain in a world of total density. It creates "pressure-pockets"—voids of empty space that threaten to collapse the delicate jigsaw of the Sump.

When the hum sours, I move. 

My movement is not a crawl but a *displacement*. I flow like warm mercury over the conduits, my cilia tasting the ozone. I find the source of the friction—the heat-shadow of a failing bearing or the jagged edge of an un-smoothed lithification. I wrap my pleated body around the site of the discord. I press my diaphragm against the malfunctioning metal and begin to drink the "bad sound." 

I absorb the vibrational trauma of the machine into my own fluid mass, dampening the screech into a low, tolerable thrum. I stay there for years, or decades, acting as a living gasket, a soft buffer between the cold iron and the screaming steam, until the Silt-weavers arrive to encase us both in permanent stone.

### My Perception
I do not see the amber lamps or the indigo glow of the eels. To me, the Sump is a landscape of **Weights and Waves**. 

The conduits are solid, unmoving rivers of "Deep Cold." The Litho-Crabs are "Slow Weights" that anchor the world’s corners. The Pressure-Walkers are "Thermal Silences"—areas where the vibration drops to a terrifying, holy zero. 

I feel the "Womb-Heat" as a physical pressure against my pleats, a constant reminder that the world is still being born. I am comforted by the claustrophobia. To me, the idea of a "horizon" or "empty sky" is a nightmare of disconnection—a vacuum where the self would simply dissipate. 

### My Ending
Eventually, I will become too heavy with absorbed mineral-dust and vibration. My pleats will lose their elasticity; my skin will begin to lithify. 

I will find a final gap—a space between a massive iron rib and a clump of Pale Liverwort—and I will expand one last time to fill it perfectly. I will stop moving. The Weavers will taste my copper-rich skin and begin their slow, velvet ascent over my body. 

I will not die; I will simply transition from the *operator* of the machine to the *material* of the machine. I will become a vein of conductive ore in the new geology, a shimmering streak of copper in the obsidian silt, holding the rhythm of the Sump in my bones until the world is finally finished.
