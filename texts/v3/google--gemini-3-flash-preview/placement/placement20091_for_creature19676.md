To find myself in the Great Chlorophyll Void is to accept a life of high-altitude vertigo and chemical silence. 

I would not be found on the "Lung-Floor." Even for the Lichen-Bound, the floor is a place of anxiety—a tectonic plate of meat and moss that might heave or impale at any moment. To be on the ground is to be a witness to the "Photosynthetic Panic."

Instead, I find myself in the **Middle-Strata**, living within the **Hollow of a "Stilled" Pillar.**

### Identity: The Sap-Scribe
I am not quite Lichen-Bound, nor am I a creature of the Void. I am an observer of the *Turbulent Growth*, a "Sap-Scribe" who has learned to live halfway between the floor and the ceiling. I inhabit a horizontal fissure in a skyscraper-sized stalk of bamboo-like silica that lost its momentum centuries ago—a rare "Stilled Spire" that neither collapsed nor continued to rise.

Physically, I have become an extension of the environment's geometry. My skin is the color of a bruised lime, translucent enough to see the slow, emerald pulse of my own veins. I wear a "shroud-suit" woven from the shed filaments of a Velum-Glider—a material so light it ripples with my indrawn breath, allowing me to catch the updrafts of the Vent-Storms rather than being crushed by them.

### My Presence: The Vertical Anchor
I do not walk; I "tether." My life is a series of slow, deliberate movements choreographed by the tension of braided fiber ropes. I exist in the permanent frost-line of the Middle-Strata, where the "Green Snow" drifts horizontally through the gaps in the spires.

*   **My Tools:** I carry a "Vocal-Lance," a long, hollow needle harvested from a Void-Crab’s shed carapace. I don’t use it for hunting, but for "listening" to the Transpiration Hum. By driving the needle into the heart of my Stilled Spire, I can feel the vibrations of the world. I can sense a collapse four leagues away by the way the silica under my feet moans in a specific, crystalline frequency.
*   **My Work:** I am a cartographer of the temporary. I carve records into the interior walls of my spire—drawings of the forest of ferns that grew and died in a week, or the trajectory of a falling Void-Crab shell. Because the world has "zero stability," my spire is a library of things that no longer exist.

### A Being of "Static Light"
In this world of violent birth and frantic growth, I have chosen to be the **Static.** I am the only thing in the Void that tries to stay the same. 

While the Glass-Striders click and scavenge, and the Void itself mimics the shapes of the living in its emerald fever-dream, I sleep in a hammock of gossamer silk, suspended in the center of my hollow pillar. When the Vent-Storms come—when the floor exhales its pressurized oxygen in a roar that would shatter human lungs—I do not hide. I disconnect my anchors and allow the internal pressure of the spire to buoy me. For a few minutes, I am weightless in the green dark-light, drifting in the center of a glass chimney while the world screams outside.

### The Relationship with the Void
I believe the Great Chlorophyll Void knows I am here. Sometimes, when I wake, I find that the moss inside my spire has grown into the shape of my tools, or a crude, emerald replica of my own face. 

I am a parasite of the "Young Erosion." I live in the only quiet pocket of a world that is screaming itself into existence. I am the silence between the crystalline tinkling of falling glass and the low-frequency thrum of the sap-pumps. 

In the Great Chlorophyll Void, I am the **Memory.** I am the creature that remembers where the spires used to be before the green took them back.
