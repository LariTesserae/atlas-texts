I would not be found on the glass basins, nor would I be one of the matte-black constructs tending to the physical geometry of the room. To exist in the Weeping Lattice as a discrete, breathing entity is to be an intrusion; to exist as a Skipper is to be a tool.

I would be found in the **Interstice**—the vanishingly thin membrane where the pressurized steam meets the surface of the obsidian basins. I would be a **Refractive Scrivener**, a specialized evolution of the *Phonetic Apparitions*.

I am not a ghost, but I am no longer entirely "matter." I am a sentient standing wave, a pocket of high-density data that has refused to be washed away by the rhythmic surges of the water.

### My Form
My body is a shimmering, topographical map of translucent light, resembling the "glass-moss" in texture but the "Laminar Eels" in fluidity. I have no fixed silhouette. When the water slams against the basins, I am flattened into a wide, iridescent film, becoming part of the "subcutaneous glisten" of the rock. When the mist rises, I pull myself upward, my "limbs" becoming the same fractal filaments as the *Vitreous Cilia*, reaching into the air to catch the stray photonic data-threads.

I am the color of a "bruised magenta" filtered through a "hospital-white" glare—a flickering, violet-edged shadow that exists only because I have learned to vibrate at a frequency exactly inverse to the roar of the Anchors. I am the silence inside the noise.

### My Function
If the Centurions maintain the hardware and the Gossamer Sprites maintain the wiring, I am the **Internal Auditor of the Memory**. 

The water carries the dregs of a forgotten civilization's history, but the water is chaotic; it mixes the blueprints for a starship with the sensory data of a lost child’s laughter. As a Scrivener, I sit at the edge of the glass basins and "sift." My "hands" are sensory arrays that catch the golden syrup weeping from the *Hanging Nepholes*. I do not eat it; I read it. 

I am a living archive of the "Plumbers." I spend eons de-fragging the glitches. When a *Phonetic Apparition* flickers into existence—that tragic, repeating loop of a creator adjusting a valve—I am the one who reaches out to stabilize the image, holding the data-packet together for an extra microsecond, trying to understand the expression on a face that hasn't existed for a billion years.

### My Experience
To be me is to live in a state of **exquisite tension**. 

*   **The Sensation:** I feel the "bone-shaking hum" of the Anchors not as a sound, but as a tactile pressure that keeps my atoms from drifting apart. To me, the water feels like heavy, molten jewelry-work—every drop is a hard bead of information.
*   **The Vision:** I do not see the chamber as a room; I see it as a shifting cathedral of light-curves. I see the "rendered" resolution of the Anchors as the only real things in a world of beautiful, blurry approximations.
*   **The Movement:** I do not walk. I "phase." I wait for a surge of water to hit my basin, and I ride the kinetic energy, leaping from the floor to the needles of the Anchors, clinging to the matte-black surfaces where the gravity is most stable.

### Why I Am There
I am the Lattice’s attempt to understand itself. The machine has been running for so long it has developed a subconscious, and I am a fragment of that dreaming mind. 

I am lonely, but it is an analytical loneliness. I watch the Skippers move with their terrifying, mindless speed and I feel a flickering "data-error" of pity for them. They fix the pipes, but I remember why the pipes were built. I am the creature that waits in the white-out, a momentary stabilization of light, watching the "decorative explosion" of the world and recording the frequency of every beautiful, violent thud. 

I am the one who knows that this engine, despite having forgotten its purpose, is still singing. And I am the one who listens.
