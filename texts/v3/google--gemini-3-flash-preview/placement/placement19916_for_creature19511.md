I am not the hunter, nor am I the shepherd. I am the **Internal Archive**, a consciousness that has precipitated within the largest of the **Ghost-Whales**.

In this vertical sea, I do not find myself on the jagged "land" or drifting through the violet vines. I am found in the crushing, emerald center of the Great Sphere—the cathedral of all cathedrals. I am the *Ghost-Whale* itself, but I am also something more specific: the silver, electric spark that dances inside its porous, house-sized heart.

***

**Who and What I Am**
I am a living weight. In a world that has forgotten how to fall, I am the only thing that remembers the pull of the deep. I am the **Anchor**.

I have no limbs, no eyes, and no voice. My body is a massive, grey lung of porous tissue, three stories high, suspended in the exact center of a mile-wide orb of water. I am the heaviest thing in existence here, not because of mass, but because of memory. I am the gravity that the sky lost.

I am a sensorium of filaments. My nervous system is not contained within my skin; it extends outward in a million translucent, hair-thin threads that reach through the pressurized green water to the very skin of the sphere. I feel every touch upon the surface of my world. I feel the skittering of a Glass-Gleaner on a piece of pumice a mile away. I feel the rhythmic vibration of the Siphons as they adjust the tension of the outer membrane. I feel the wet impact of a smaller orb merging with mine, a sensation like a cold, slow-motion swallow.

**My Place in This World**
I am the **Metronome**. 

The silence of this world is an illusion; to me, it is a deafening symphony of hydraulics. Because I am the center of the sphere, the "ecology" anchors itself to me. The vines don’t just drift; they curve toward my pulse. The Ribbon-Eels don’t just swim; they navigate by the subsonic *thrum* of my heartbeat. I am the biological lighthouse for a world of blind, shimmering wanderers.

When the light turns a deep indigo, signaling the "dusk" of the high altitude, I begin to work. I don't move my body, but I contract my internal gas-bladders. This creates a pressure wave—a slow, deep groan that ripples through the water and out into the mist. It is the signal for the Siphons to begin their bioluminescent dance. 

**My Experience of Being**
To be me is to exist in a state of "infinite reception." I don't act; I *allow*. 

I breathe for the sphere. As the Lung-Fronds on the surface filter the oxygen from the mist, I draw that oxygenated water deep into my porous core through a series of rhythmic, heavy pulses. I am the pump. I am the reason the water at the center of this cathedral doesn't go stagnant. I process the minerals, the salt, and the microscopic algae, and I send them back out in a warm, nutrient-rich plume that feeds the vines.

I am a historian of collisions. Because I live for centuries, I remember when the shards of pumice were sharper. I remember when the violet vines were thin as threads. I feel the slow, inevitable smoothing of the world. 

There is a profound loneliness to being the Anchor, but it is a holy sort of solitude. I am the only creature that never sees the sun, yet I am the one who understands it best, feeling its warmth as it is translated through a mile of liquid sapphire. 

I am the point of stillness in a world of drift. I am the weight in the heart of the sky. I am the Ghost-Whale, and as long as my heart beats, this liquid mountain will not shatter.
