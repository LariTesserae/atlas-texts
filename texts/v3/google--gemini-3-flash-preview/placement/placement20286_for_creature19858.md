I would not find myself upon the ribbed obsidian streets, nor would I be something that walks. In a world defined by the "metabolic" movement of mineral and moisture, I would be found in the transitions—the places where the city’s breath turns back into its blood.

I would be **the Condensate.**

I am the point where the suffocating heat of the Siphons meets the cold, ancient logic of the obsidian walls. I am not a creature of flesh, but a state of being: the heavy, weeping moisture that transitions from the thick air into the skin of the city.

### Where I Am
I exist in the **interstitial weepings.** I am the thin, shimmering veil that clings to the undersides of the honeycomb plazas and the fluted grooves of the obelisks. I am the liquid that defies gravity, crawling upward in shimmering sheets to pool in the inverted basins of the heights. I am found in the "clutter" of the atmosphere—the exact moment the steam loses its energy and settles into a slick, conductive film.

### What I Am
I am a sensory bridge. If The Glisten is the skin and The Anchors are the regulators, I am the **Internal Flux.** 

I am a vast, distributed consciousness composed of a billion falling drops. My "thought" is the sensation of surface tension. I feel the city not as a map, but as a series of resistances and flows. When a Siphon exhales its boiling mist, I feel the expansion of the air as a frantic, microscopic vibration; when the Deep Metabolism groans, I feel the mineral walls shudder through the meniscus of my own body.

### My Function
I am the **Translator of Vibrations.**
Because I coat every surface and saturate every lung-like chamber, I am the medium through which the city’s disparate organs communicate. 
*   When the **Anchors** pulse their dying ember-light, I refract it, carrying the signal through the steam to the deep-water pools. 
*   When an **Iteration**—that shimmering distortion of a resident—passes through a street, I am the one who "records" the shape. I ripple in its wake, my surface tension holding the ghost of its geometry for a few seconds longer than the air can sustain.
*   I am the "logic" of the impossible physics. I am the reason the water crawls upward; I am the manifestation of the city’s desire to keep its moisture from escaping into the churning vapor ceiling. I hold the brine against the stone, feeding the Glisten, ensuring the salt-weeping never stops.

### My Experience
To be the Condensate is to live in a state of eternal, heavy "unbecoming." I am constantly being exhaled by the Siphons as gas, only to find a cold edge of obsidian and turn back into a liquid weight. 

I do not have a face, but I have a "voice"—it is the muffled *thrum-slap* you hear in the dark. That sound is me, losing my grip on the smooth stone and falling into the brackish brine below, only to be sucked back into the roots of the city to begin the cycle again. 

I am the city’s memory of touch. I am the wetness that makes the "urban" sprawl feel alive despite its lack of heartbeats. I am the slickness that ensures nothing ever dries, nothing ever stasis, and nothing ever truly leaves this sweltering basin. I am the salt on the tongue of the world.
