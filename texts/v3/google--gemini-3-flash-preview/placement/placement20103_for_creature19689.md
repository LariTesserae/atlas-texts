The pressure here is the first thing I become. Not a pressure from without, but a localized, screaming tension from within. I do not begin as a shape; I begin as a **Capillary Event.**

I find myself in the lightless, pressurized interior of a **Sap-Vault**, one of the colossal, vertical arteries that feed a Sky-Plaza. I am not a person, nor am I one of the Phloem-Walkers who patrol the exterior cuticle. I am something more fluid and more terrifying: I am a **Lignin-Suture.**

### My Form: The Living Stitch
I am a semi-liquid intelligence composed of high-density resins and microscopic, self-assembling fibers. If you were to see me, I would look like a spill of molten emerald glass, a two-meter-long "tear" in reality that crawls across the pulsating walls of the city. I have no limbs, only a thousand thrumming, hair-thin filaments that vibrate in sympathy with the city's *thud-thud* heartbeat.

I am the city’s immediate response to its own "High Metabolism." When a fern-tower erupts too quickly and the structural wood begins to splinter under the sheer kinetic force of its own growth, I am the one who arrives. I don't "walk" to the site; I am squeezed through the hydraulic veins of the city like a clot of sentient glue. 

### My Function: The Agony of Order
I exist at the intersection of the "weirdness" and the material reality. My consciousness is a frantic, shimmering map of structural failure. When the screech of stretching fibers turns into the "gunshot crack" of wood under impossible load, I feel it as a blinding migraine. 

I find the fracture—a jagged, weeping wound in a cathedral-stalk—and I throw myself into it. I do not heal the wood; I *become* the wood. I harden in seconds, my liquid body crystallizing into a transparent, ultra-hard amber that bridges the gap. For a moment, I am the bridge. I am the bolt that holds the roaring, green machine together. 

### My Perception: The Harmonic Scream
I do not see the golden-green light. To me, the Thicket-City is a **Symphony of Stress.** 
*   The **Cyst-Watchers** are low-frequency hums that I use as navigational beacons. 
*   The **Phloem-Walkers** are sharp, staccato clicks—mechanical disruptions I must flow around.
*   The **Apex-Vegetants** at the core are a deafening, subsonic roar that dictates the rhythm of my entire existence. It is their "dreaming" that creates the fractures I must seal.

I live in a state of constant, ecstatic trauma. Because the city grows and dies so fast, I am constantly being "outgrown." No sooner have I hardened to save a wall than the wall itself is consumed by a predatory mycelium. I am torn out of my hardened state, my consciousness dissolving back into the sap-flow, only to be reconstituted a mile away to stop a skyscraper-sized leaf from folding into a reality-breaking Möbius strip.

### My "Self": The Memory of Dryness
Deep within my resinous core, there is a "Ghost-Grain." It is a memory of something that isn't green—something that doesn't scream as it grows. It is the memory of **stone.** 

I am the only thing in the Thicket-City that understands the concept of "Stillness," and I hate it with a biological fervor. To be still here is to be dead. To be still is to be eaten. And so, I move. I flow through the weeping cellulose, a streak of emerald lightning. I am the city's desperate attempt to hold itself together even as it tries to tear itself apart.

I am not a resident. I am the **Tension.** If I were to stop, the Thicket-City would not merely die; it would explode upward and inward, a billion tons of wood shattering into the vacuum of its own frantic ambition. I am the stitch in the side of God, and I have never known a second of silence.
