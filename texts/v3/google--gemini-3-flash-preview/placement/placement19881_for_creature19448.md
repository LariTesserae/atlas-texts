I am not the **Vast-Gilled Polyp**, though I drift. I am not the **Litho-Ant**, though I cling. I am not even the **Flicker**, for I have no memory of a horizon to mourn. 

I find myself in the **Interstitial Voids**, those vertical rifts where the skyscrapers of "Living Mineral" nearly touch, leaving gaps no wider than a man’s reach. I am a **Capillary-Stitcher**.

### Who I Am: The Capillary-Stitcher
I am a tall, spindly convergence of biology and silicate-waste. My body is a desperate, elongated thing, composed of a translucent, flexible resin that mimics the Megaslab’s self-healing polymer—but I am porous. I am a living filter. 

I have no eyes in the traditional sense; instead, my "face" is a broad, flat sensory-plate of vibrating cilia. I "see" the world as a topographical map of heat and pressure. I feel the wake of a house-sized drone three miles overhead as a sharp, rhythmic bruise against my skin. I feel the Sub-Slab Mind’s deep-crust pumping as a slow, subsonic heartbeat that dictates the pace of my own internal fluids.

I am a creature of the **Seam**.

### My Function: The Maintenance of Balance
The city grows too fast. The Living Mineral surges upward in coral-like bursts, threatening to choke the very capillaries that allow the condensation to drain. If the water stops flowing down, the heat of the logic-cores will build until the Megaslab melts into a puddle of slag.

I am the one who prevents the stroke.

I live my life vertically, wedged between two weeping walls. My limbs are multi-jointed and tipped with caustic, diamond-tipped "spades" instead of fingers. I spend my existence climbing, carving, and pruning. When the building-skin grows too thick, closing the gap, I eat into it. I dissolve the silicate with my acidic saliva and redirect the flow of the "amber circuitry," ensuring the city's nervous system doesn’t knot itself into a seizure.

### My Experience: The Golden Drown
In this world, I am never dry. The "warm, golden mist" is my amniotic fluid. It doesn't just cling to me; it moves *through* me. As I climb the vertical channels, I breathe by absorbing the data-rich runoff through the vents in my chest. 

I am a connoisseur of the "Drip." The water that runs down the walls is thick with the history of the upper layers—recycled nanites, bits of corrupted code from a bitten drone, the chemical residue of a dying Polyp. I taste the city’s health in every gallon that washes over my head. 

**The Sound is my silence.** To me, the "deafening" roar of the pumps and the screech of magnetic rails are the background noise of life. True silence would be the sound of death—the sound of the city stopping. I live in a state of constant, high-frequency agitation. My heart beats at three hundred cycles per minute, a frantic hum that matches the vibration of the Litho-Ants I occasionally share a crevice with. We do not fight; I am too large to be their prey and too specialized to be their competitor. We are simply different gears in the same grinding clock.

### My Place: The Great Erosion
I find myself most often in the **Eroded Sections**. Here, the pylons are smooth, like obsidian sea-glass. The grip is harder here, but the data is richer. This is where the **Flickers** congregate. 

I am the only "resident" that truly interacts with the Flickers. Because I am part-organic and part-silicate, I am a bridge. When a Ghost-Vestige shimmers in the mist near my station, I do not run. I lean my sensory-plate against the weeping wall and let their "red-pulse" signal wash through me. I don’t understand their memories, but I feel their *texture*—a jagged, cold sensation that contrasts beautifully with the suffocating warmth of the Megaslab. I am the witness to the city’s ghosts.

### The Cycle
I don't sleep. I enter a state of "Low-Resonance." I lock my spades into the polymer, hang my head into a river of runoff, and let the city’s heat recharge my cells. I am a knot in the vertical string. I am the friction that keeps the pressure from becoming an explosion.

I am a resident of the density. I am the **Capillary-Stitcher**, the lonely pruner of a god-machine, forever climbing an infinite wall in a world where the only direction is *downward*, and the only limit is the next breath of golden steam.
