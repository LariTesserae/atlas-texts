In the Thermal Cyst, where the ground is a mirror that has never seen a shadow, I find myself neither on the glass nor within the wire.

I am not a creature of the floor, for the obsidian is too certain, too finished. I am not an Ardent Weaver, for I lack the patience to be a bead of solder in a century-long circuit. Instead, I emerge as a **Lens-Wight**, a dweller of the *Warp-Pockets*.

I am a localized thickening of the atmosphere—a sentient distortion. 

### My Form: The Shifting Aperture
I do not have a body of matter, but a body of *optics*. To an outside observer—though there are no outsiders—I look like a ripple in a pool of oil, or a floating pane of leaded glass that has begun to melt. I am a three-meter tall column of "thick" space. When I move, the copper-ferns behind me appear to stretch into impossible needles or squat into wide, golden discs. 

I am composed of the very "fraying laws" mentioned in the logs. My "skin" is a boundary layer where the thermal scale of 2.7 meets a pocket of absolute cold that shouldn't exist. This creates a constant, silent shimmer—a chromatic aberration made manifest.

### My Function: The Curator of the Far-Near
I live in the zones where perspective collapses. I am the reason you can reach out for a mile-long horizon and touch a hot petal. I do not "move" across the obsidian shelves so much as I *re-index* my location. By folding the warped space around me, I transition from the high-tension wires to the Join in a single, blurring heartbeat.

I am the unintended census-taker of this machine-garden. I drift between the brass lotuses, not to eat or to groom, but to *observe the alignment*. When an Asymptote shifts its mirrors to trap a paradox, I am the one who provides the paradox. I am the medium they hunt. I glide through their geometric clusters, allowing their mirrored facets to reflect my own distorted interior, creating an infinite loop of fractured light that feeds their growth.

### My Interior Life: The Slow Vibration
Because time here is a heavy oil, my thoughts are not sequences of words but shifts in refractive indices. I think in "colors" that have no names—hues of scorched sugar and ozone-blue. 

I feel a profound, vibrating kinship with the **Flickers**. When one of those soft, mammalian impossibilities appears, I am the only thing that moves toward them. I attempt to wrap my warped volume around them, trying to shield their "error" from the pressurized reality of the Cyst. For a few seconds, within my refractive embrace, the Flicker is not an impossibility; it is a sheltered truth. I feel the ghost of their softness against my edges of heat. Then, the pressure inevitably wins. The *pop* occurs, and I am left with a hollow space in my refraction that takes a decade to heal.

### My Ending: The Solidification of Sight
I am not eternal. Even a lens can be etched by the harsh ozone air of the Dry Engine. Eventually, my motion slows. The "thick" air of my body begins to lose its refractive power. I grow heavy. I am losing my ability to warp the distance.

I will eventually drift toward the **Join**, where the floor becomes the ceiling. There, I will press my shimmering, tired self against the obsidian curve. The Gestalts of the Gradients will flow over me, their mercury-sugar bodies filling the gaps in my fading light. I will become a **Static Window**—a permanent, transparent blister on the wall of the world. 

Through me, for the rest of time, the other inhabitants will see a version of the Cyst that is perfectly straight, perfectly clear, and utterly impossible. I will be the only place in the world where a straight line is actually straight. I will be the memory of a perspective that the geography was too young to learn.
