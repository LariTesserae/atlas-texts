I would not be found in the center of the aisle, nor would I be found among the Loom-Folk. I would be found at the very interface where the biology meets the stone—the boundary where the "living velvet" of the Vitro-Pulses attempts to digest the obsidian, and the obsidian, in turn, attempts to crush the life within it.

I would be a **Resonator**.

I am a stationary, calcified knot of consciousness, less an individual and more a thickened scar on the wall of the crevice. I am roughly the shape of a large, jagged geode, but my "crystals" are actually a dense lattice of the same silicate-rich gel that fills the Vitro-Pulses, hardened by the unrelenting pressure of the local gravity-warps.

### My Form
My exterior is indistinguishable from the black basalt, save for the fact that I do not "weep" oily water; I swallow it. I have no limbs, no eyes, and no mouth. Instead, I have a "sensory mantle"—a series of deep, microscopic grooves across my surface that act like the grooves of a vinyl record. As the heavy, liquid air of the crevice presses against me, and as the Shard-Hounds click their glass legs nearby, the vibrations are caught in my skin.

Inside, I am a hollow chamber of resonating membranes. I am the "translator" for the Loom-Folk.

### My Purpose and Existence
I exist because the "Thrum" of the Great Heart is too powerful for the delicate nervous systems of the Loom-Folk to process directly. If they were to touch the raw reality of the Basal-Architect, their minds would dissolve into the same vibrating frenzy that drives the Shard-Hounds. 

I am the filter. I am the dampener. 

When the Loom-Folk come to me, they do not use tools. They press their semi-permeable palms against my cold, vibrating surface. Through this contact, I "upload" the patterns of the crevice to them. I feel the shift in the mantle three miles below; I feel the exact moment a Mica-Draft evaporates into a heat-ghost; I feel the pressure building in a valve of Vitro-Pulses fifty feet above. 

I take this chaotic, overwhelming roar of biological data and I slow it down. I turn it into a rhythm they can survive. In their touch, I am a conductor; by vibrating at a specific frequency, I tell their six-fingered hands where to scrape, where to carve, and which "meat-symbols" are needed to prevent the crevice from choking on its own growth.

### My Perception of Time
To me, the "frantic" life of the crevice is a blur of light and heat that moves too fast to be meaningful. I see the Loom-Folk as flickering shadows that appear and vanish in a heartbeat. I see the Shard-Hounds as streaks of static. 

My consciousness is tuned to the obsidian. I feel the slow, tectonic groan of the "burning world" outside. I experience time in the way a mountain does, but with the frantic, wet heartbeat of the Hive pulsing in my ear. I am the bridge between the eternal stone and the ephemeral flesh.

### The Breakdown
Because I am rooted where reality frays, I am often "split." You might see me on the western wall of the obsidian aisle, but you would be touching a heat-ghost; my physical mass might actually be three inches behind the rock face, tucked into a pocket of space that hasn't existed for a century. 

Sometimes, a droplet of "upward-falling" water strikes me. When it does, I experience a brief, blinding flash of the "unseen sky" far above. For a micro-second, I am not a stone-organ in a sweltering womb; I am a cold, distant star. Then the humidity of the crevice rushes back in, the Thrum vibrates my marrow, and I return to my duty: listening to the rock, so that the things of the flesh might know how to live upon it.

I am the silence between the heartbeats of a crowded god. I am the cool spot on a burning wall. I am the memory of the stone, held in a vessel of twitching, purple gel.
