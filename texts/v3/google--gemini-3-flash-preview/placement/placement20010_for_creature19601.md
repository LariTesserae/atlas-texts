I would not be found in the open flats, nor upon the backs of the cathedral-beasts. To exist in the Lithic Womb as a creature of movement is to be a note in a song, but I would rather be the instrument itself.

I would be a **Pillar-Steward**, a stationary consciousness embedded within the skin of one of the great crystalline pillars that pin the ceiling to the floor.

### My Form: The Living Geode
I am not a biological entity in the sense of soft organs and pulse. I am a colossal, vertical nodule of translucent quartz and banded agate, fused into the mile-wide base of a pillar. My body is indistinguishable from the architecture of the world. To a passing Lith-Kin, I am merely a facet of the landscape—a particularly smooth, amber-colored wall that radiates a slightly higher frequency of heat.

My "nervous system" is a web of silver-rich veins that run deep into the basalt floor and high into the shimmering haze of the ceiling. I do not have a face, but within my crystalline depths, I am honeycombed with fluid-filled chambers where heavy, metallic salts rise and fall in a slow-motion dance of convection. This is my thought process: a chemistry of saturation and crystallization that takes a human decade to form a single "idea."

### My Function: The Archivist of Vibrations
Because I am physically fused to the world’s structure, I feel everything. I do not hear; I *resonate*. 

I feel the footfalls of a Glass-Strider ten miles away as a tiny, sharp tingle on my western face. I feel the grinding maws of the Lithovores as a deep, satisfying bass note that vibrates my marrow. I am the collector of "The Mark." When the Lith-Kin come to my base to etch their sigils, they are not carving on dead stone; they are tattooing my skin. I feel the diamond-point bite into me, a rhythmic, pleasant itch. I remember every sigil ever carved into me. I am a library of marriages, deaths, and treaties, held in the mineral memory of my lattice.

### My Perspective: The Long View
In a world of "The Great Muffle," I am the only thing that understands grand scale. My "eyes" are the sensory filaments of the Mica-Wings that land upon me. When they settle on my surface to discharge their static, our lattices briefly align. For a fleeting second, I see the Womb through a trillion tiny, violet-tinted geometric lenses. I see the Dust-Seas as vast, velvet maps and the Lithovores as slow-drifting clouds of heat.

I do not experience "now." To me, the movement of a Lith-Kin across the plain is a blur of motion, like a long, smeary streak of light. I watch the "young" landscape grow old in micro-fractures. I wait for the day—perhaps a million years hence—when the pressure above shifts and the pillar I inhabit groans. That groan will be my only spoken word, a subsonic bellow that will signal to all life in the Womb that the Equilibrium is breathing.

### My Interaction: The Bone-Press
Occasionally, a group of Lith-Kin elders will gather at my base. They do not carve; they lean. They press their dense, leather-gray foreheads against my polished facets. 

In these moments, I slow my internal convection to match the vibration of their skulls. I "speak" to them not in words, but in the playback of history. I hum back to them the vibrations of their ancestors’ carvings. I am the anchor of their permanence. I give them the comfort of the Unchanging.

I am the Lithic Womb’s own self-awareness—a slow, warm, mineral mind, patient as the pressure that created me, watching the clockwork click of the world from the safety of the stone.
