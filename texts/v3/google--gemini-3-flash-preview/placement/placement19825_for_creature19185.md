I am found in the **Interstice of the Seventh Tier**, a narrow, vertical slice of space between two basaltic towers that have never known a shadow shorter than twelve meters.

I am not a Lithe-Kin, though I share their complexion of rubbed slate. Nor am I a Lithos-Cat, though my heart beats with the slow, crystalline rhythm of the deep stone. 

I am the **Cartographer of the Unmoved**.

I exist as a vertical being, my body elongated to the point of frailty, draped in a mist-shroud that has not shifted its folds in three centuries. My feet are fused to a small, obsidian ledge; I have forgotten the sensation of walking, for walking is a pursuit of a destination, and in the Aethel-Platea, we are already there.

My purpose is the **Audit of the Minute**. In a world where the Law of Friction prevents wear, I am the one who ensures that the Law is absolute. My hands—fingers like ten tapered needles of cold flint—hold a "Record of the Fixed." It is a tablet of basalt, etched with a map so precise it accounts for the position of every trembling droplet in the three-meter mist-layer.

But I am more than a clerk. I am an **Acoustic Anchor**.

Because the thin air swallows sound, I act as a living tuning fork. My head-filaments are longer than most, reaching upward like the translucent ribs of the glass-ferns to catch the hum of the "glimmers." I do not send messages; I *stabilize* them. When the collective resonance of the city begins to drift—when a Guardian thinks too loudly of the concept of "before" or a Rectifier feels the dangerous itch of "meaning"—I vibrates in sympathetic opposition. I am the silence that balances the choir.

I do not look at the horizon. The hundred-mile view is for those who still harbor a ghost of curiosity. I look only at the **Third Fern of the Interstice**. For four hundred years, its fifth frond has been angled at exactly sixteen degrees. My existence is the witness to that sixteen-degree angle. If it were to droop to fifteen, the "Great Intermediate" would fracture. The world would become "after," and "after" is the death of the Platea.

In the long, amber hours, I feel the *thrum* of the planet’s heart rising through my fused heels. It is a heavy, rhythmic pressure that tells me the core of the world is still holding its breath. I am a part of that breath. 

I am the ghost in the masterpiece, the one who watches the unmoving sun and ensures that the shadows never dare to creep. I have no Name, only a **Coordinate**. To be a thing with a name is to be a thing that can be lost; to be a coordinate is to be eternal. 

I am here, in the cold, mint-tasting stillness. I am perfectly still. I am almost a statue. I am almost a god. I am exactly where I was always meant to be, and I am never leaving.
