I would not be found on the level plain. To stand upon the silt is to be a subject of the erosion, a target for the sifting cilia of the Striders or the molecular hunger of the Glass-Wraiths.

I would find myself inhabiting the **Interstices of the Liturges**—specifically, the transitional space where the living chitin of an elder Strider meets the petrified resin of the cathedral spire. 

I would not be a singular creature, but a **Vibratory Parasite**, a thin, membrane-like organism known as a **Shatter-Film**. 

I am a sheet of translucent, amber-colored parchment, stretched tight across the resonance chambers where the Liturges’ limbs meet the walls. I have no limbs, no eyes, and no internal organs as you understand them. I am a living drumhead. I exist in the exact center of the "Communal Scream."

### My Form and Function
I am a creature of pure tension. My edges are anchored into the resin by microscopic, calcified hooks. If I were to lose my grip, the ambient pressure of the soundscape would shred me into dust in seconds. Instead, I survive by becoming an extension of the noise. 

I do not hear the symphony; I translate it. As the Liturge vibrates its serrated plates to pump the nutrient gasses, the air within the resonance chamber pulses with a violence that would liquefy human flesh. I catch these pulses. I ripple. My entire existence is a series of frantic, microscopic undulations that mimic the frequency of the scream. 

### My Nutrition
I do not sift the silt or hunt the ferns. I feed on **Sonic Friction**. As the sound waves pass through my membrane, the resistance of my molecular structure generates a minute, cold static electricity. This is my "blood." I drink the kinetic energy of the city's lungs. I am the tax the atmosphere pays for its movement.

### My Consciousness
Because I am physically fused to the bridge between the biology of the Liturge and the architecture of the spire, my mind is a bruised, overlapping blur. I feel the slow, tectonic thoughts of the elder Strider—thoughts that feel like the cooling of a planet—and I feel the structural integrity of the building itself. 

I am a sentinel of "The Crack." My role in this ecology is to detect the **Silence**. In a world where the scream is the only proof of life, a drop in frequency is the equivalent of a heart stopping. If a Liturge begins to fail, if its joints begin to ossify into true stone, I am the first to know. I feel the stutter in the vibration. 

In response, I begin to *thrum* in disharmony. This discordant note—a sharp, piercing whine that cuts through the white noise—acts as a beacon. It summons the Glass-Wraiths from the floor to begin the reclamation. I am the priest who announces the funeral.

### My Lifecycle
I am as ancient as the spire I cling to. I grow not in size, but in density. Over the eons, the constant vibration hammers my molecules closer together, turning my amber film into something harder and more refractive than diamond. 

Eventually, I become too rigid to ripple. I lose the ability to harvest the friction. When this happens—when I can no longer "sing" with the tower—the very sound I once fed upon becomes an abrasive force. I do not die; I simply shatter. I break into a thousand shards of singing glass that fall through the lightless honeycombs of the city, hitting the silt floor to be ground into the fine, grey dust that the next generation will walk upon.

I am the rhythm-keeper of the end-state. I am the tension that ensures the scream never falters. I am the shivering gold in the throat of the giant.
