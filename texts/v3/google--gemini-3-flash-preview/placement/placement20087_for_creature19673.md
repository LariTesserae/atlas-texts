I am not the Suture-Walker, for my bones do not carry the longing for the valley, nor do I possess the plum-bruised skin of those who must eventually descend. I am not the Rime-Stalker, for I have no patience for the static ice; I find the slow accumulation of mineral shell to be a kind of living death.

Instead, I find myself in the frantic, shimmering vibration of the **Glass-Gilder**, but I am something of an anomaly within the colony—a mutation of the "in-between."

I am a **Sentinel-Gilder**, a creature of high-frequency heat and ultraviolet sight. I live within the velvet folds of the sky-moss, but my purpose is not merely the harvest of seeds. I am the bridge between the biology of the moss and the geometry of the stone.

**Who I am:**
I am small, barely the size of a Suture-Walker’s palm, wrapped in a coat of hollow, glass-like filaments that do not just trap air, but refract the "roaring" sun into a private Aurora around my body. To an observer, I look like a localized distortion in the air—a blur of white light and rapid movement. 

I do not vibrate simply to stay warm; I vibrate to *hear* the mountain. My chitinous feet are sensitive to the *tink-tink-tink* of the granite's thermal expansion. I feel the microscopic fissures opening seconds before they crack, and I move the colony’s nurseries away from the splintering obsidian.

**Where I am found:**
You will find me at the very edge of the "parchment" flowers, where the succulents meet the abyss. While the others scramble for fatty seeds, I am drawn to the bone-chimes of the Suture-Walkers. I like the clatter of the shale. I sit upon the wooden pylons, my nictitating membranes flickering, watching the Aether-Whales drift by like ghosts of the upper atmosphere.

I am the one who tastes the ozone before the "roaring" sun arrives. I feel the change in the pressure of the thin air, the subtle shift in the wind that signals the arrival of a cloud-snag. When the world is about to flash-freeze, I am the first to pulse my bioluminescence—a sharp, sapphire strobe that tells the Suture-Walkers to pull their furs tight and the swifts to tuck their four wings.

**My Experience of this World:**
To me, the "hazy, violet void" is not empty. Because I see in the ultraviolet and feel the kinetic hum of the earth, the space around the pinnacle is crowded with texture. I see the magnetic leylines of the planet as glowing, violet curtains that sway in the solar wind. I see the thermal ribbons of the Suture-Walkers’ breath as golden ghosts that linger in the air long after they have moved on.

I am a tiny, frantic heartbeat on a needle of glass. I am the "dynamic" of the peak made flesh. I do not fear the verticality because, to me, there is no "down." There is only the *Center*—the warmth of the moss—and the *Out*, the great, shimmering potential of the light.

When the Aether-Whales descend with their trailing filaments to graze upon our home, I do not hide. I climb the highest stalk of the parchment-flowers and vibrate at a frequency that mimics the humming of the Great Bladders themselves. For a brief moment, as the moss is lifted into the sky, I am part of the "near-void," a spark of white fur suspended between the jagged tooth of the world and the silent, roaring fire of the sun. 

I am the witness to the brief. I am the heat in the heart of the frost.
