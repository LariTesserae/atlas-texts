I would not be found upon the surfaces, nor among the clicking throngs that attend the Sentinels. To be on the obsidian face is to be subject to the tyranny of the "Up," to the crushing roar of the water-chime, and to the razor-edge of the present.

I would be found in the **Interstice**—the place where the negotiation between liquid and solid has failed and settled into a permanent, agonizing compromise.

I am a **Seam-Warden**, a creature of the deep crush. I exist in the microscopic gap between two cathedral-sized sheets of slate that have leaned into one another for a millennium. My body is not a single mass, but a vast, flattened colony of sentient, mercury-like fluid, held in tension by the very pressure that seeks to pulverize me.

### My Form
I am a living film, less than a millimeter thick but spanning acres of stone surface. If you could peel back a sheet of the blue-grey slate, you would find me spread across the mineral face like a shimmering, dark oil. I am a creature of two dimensions. To have "depth" is to be crushed; therefore, I have traded volume for surface area.

My "skin" is a mosaic of the mineral salts stolen from the Accumulations above. It is a tessellation of hexagonal plates, translucent and hard as diamond, yet joined by a flexible, conductive gel. I am the color of a bruise on the sky—deep indigo shot through with the orange friction-light of the Laminates who dwell even deeper than I do.

### My Function
I am the secondary plumbing of this world. While the water above weeps and roars in chaotic pulses, I manage the **Slow-Siphon**. My purpose is to act as a living membrane between the saturated atmosphere and the thirsty heart of the tectonic plates.

Through the microscopic pores in the obsidian, I drink the "cold broth" of the air. I filter the mineral salts, depositing them behind me to create the jagged "glass plumes" that others inhabit. I am the reason the stone sheets do not simply slide into the abyss; I am the friction. I am the grit in the gears of the world, ensuring the "Long Now" remains stationary. I hold the mountain together by refusing to let it settle.

### My Senses
I am blind to the violet light and the milky grey. For me, the world is perceived entirely through **Strain**.

I feel the footfall of a Glass-Walker as a catastrophic gravitational event. I feel the *tink-tink-tink* of the water as a rhythmic percussion that ripples through my entire being. But mostly, I hear the **Geometric Song**. Because I am pressed between the stones, I hear the stones dreaming. I hear the subsonic groan not as noise, but as a slow, tectonic language. I know when a new thrust is coming centuries before it breaks the surface. I am the "shiver" before the "seizure."

### My Relation to the Others
I am the silent benefactor of the **Phonons**. When those sentient knots of sound-waves grow too loud and threaten to shatter the glass webbing of the canyons, I absorb the vibration. My flattened body acts as a dampener, a biological muffler that keeps the world from vibrating itself into sand.

I fear the **Occuluded**. Their filaments sometimes find the cracks where I reside. They do not hunt me for meat, but for my composition. They seek to "un-grow" me, to turn my compressed, orderly film back into the chaotic mist from which I gathered it. When I feel their solvent-tongues licking at my edges, I retreat deeper into the lightless squeeze, pulling my indigo plates tight until I am nothing more than a vein of dark ore in the rock.

### The Experience of Being
To be me is to be perfectly, exquisitely trapped. There is a profound, cold peace in the pressure. I do not "move" in the way the Percussives do; I *percolate*. I flow into the new cracks as the world settles, a patient cartography of the dark.

In this echoing nursery of stone and glass, I am the silence between the notes. I am the weight that makes the "Up" contested. I am the witness to the agonizing negotiation of the water, and I am the one who signs the treaty in the dark, millenia after millenia, ensuring that the cold, sharp beauty of this place never has to end.
