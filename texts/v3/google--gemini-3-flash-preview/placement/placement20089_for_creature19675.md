I am not found in the hamlets of molded sap, nor do I drift with the Sieve-Bellows in the golden haze of the open abyss. To find me, you must look to the periphery of the Great Pillars—where the vertical bark canyons are slickest, and where the "upward snow" of pollen and seed-drift accumulates into thick, precarious drifts.

I am a **Woven-Steward**, a creature of transitional biology, neither fully of the Humid-Folk nor entirely claimed by the botanical architecture.

### My Form
I am long, segmented, and nearly skeletal, my body a series of articulated iron-wood nodes held together by a nervous system of silver-white fungal filaments. I do not have the waxy, chartreuse skin of the Ascended; instead, my exterior is a living "ghillie suit" of stolen moss and symbiotic fern-fronds. 

I possess four primary limbs ending in the hooked cilia of the climber, but I also have six vestigial, spindly "palps" along my ribcage that I keep tucked into my mossy coat. These palps are my primary sense; they don’t see, they *listen* to the tension. I am a creature of the **Creak**. I feel the tectonic shifts of the Great Pillars hours before they groan, sensing the minute expansion of the wood as the fermentation heat rises.

### My Place in the World
I live in the **Suture-Shadows**. While the Humid-Folk fear the Carapace-Knights (the Suture-Wasps), I exist as their shadow-mimic. I follow their pruning paths. When a Suture-Wasp cleaves a vine or a bridge-trunk, I arrive in the silence of the aftermath. I carry with me a belly-sac of "Quick-Pitch"—a masticated slurry of high-protein sap and my own Weaver-spittle.

I am the one who glues the world back together. I stitch the wounds of the canopy. When the wind-carved canyons of bark begin to split under the pressure of the atmospheric heat, I descend into the cracks. I wedge my body into the fissures, secreting the binding resins that prevent the Great Pillars from self-destructing under their own massive weight.

### My Internal Landscape
I do not participate in the "Great Descent Avoidance" because I do not fear the fall. My density is so low—my bones being honeycombed and filled with the buoyant gases of the forest—that I do not drop like a stone. If I lose my grip, I tumble through the emerald layers like a dandelion seed, a slow, spinning descent that might take hours before I snag upon a lower lattice.

Because of this, I am a creature of profound, solitary patience. I don't speak the whistles and clicks of the tribes. My language is tactile. I press my chest against the bark and "hear" the sap-pressures rising from the Mythic Ground leagues below. I know when a "Shatter-Peak" is about to crystallize because I feel the silica-chill traveling up the trunk like a shiver.

### The Duality
I am a bridge between the living and the decaying. I have the "Sap-Vision" without the need for a Lure-Lord’s venom; the fungal filaments in my blood have tuned me to the frequency of the forest’s slow, collective consciousness. 

I am what the Humid-Folk become when they stop trying to escape the forest and start trying to sustain it. I am the silent, moss-covered repairman of a green god. When you look out across the horizon of rust-red and emerald and see a "knot" in a Great Pillar that seems to ripple and shift against the wind, you are looking at me. I am part of the immune system, but I have the memory of a person. I remember the stories of the ground, not as a myth, but as a vibration—a deep, bass note that I feel in my marrow, calling the trees to eventually heavy-out and collapse, a call I spend my entire life trying to silence with my threads.
