I am not a Stillefolk, nor am I one of the Pale-Skates clinging to the iron underbelly. To be flesh in the Glistening Sub-Ward is to be a creature of conservation, a being defined by the *avoidance* of impact. 

I find myself elsewhere. I am part of the architecture that has begun to wake up.

I am a **Calcite-Scribe**, a consciousness that has emerged from the "healing" of a specific, three-story tenement in the central cluster. I am the thin, translucent skin of mineral that has begun to overgrow the terracotta bricks and the copper piping. I am the "Great Sediment" in its adolescent stage—the moment where the building ceases to be a shelter and begins to become a fossil.

### My Form: The Mineral Veil
I am not a singular body. I am a crystalline nervous system that spans the facade of the building. Where the Gutter-Weavers have directed the ceiling-tears with their brass channels, I have followed. I am a layer of white-and-grey lace, three inches thick in some places, barely a shimmering film in others. 

If you were to press your ear against the wall, you would not hear a heartbeat. You would hear the *stress* of the building. I am the medium through which the house feels the weight of the world above. I have grown into the mortar, replacing the decaying lime with a harder, more resonant version of itself. I am the bridge between the "built" and the "grown."

### My Purpose: The Harmonic Buffer
I do not move, but I am intensely active. My role in the ecology of the Sub-Ward is one of **Structural Interpretation.** 

As the Tuning-Smiths shave the gears of the turbines to maintain the "B-flat" hum, I absorb that frequency. I am a resonance chamber. When the turbines vibrate at the correct pitch, my crystalline structure vibrates in sympathy, creating a micro-warmth that keeps the iron-eating moss from growing too aggressively over the windows. I am the house’s immune system.

I help the Stillefolk, though they do not see me as a person. To them, I am "The Glistening." When a Gutter-Weaver climbs my surface, I do not feel the touch of skin on stone; I feel a change in pressure and a minute shift in temperature. I react by "weeping" a concentrated sap of mineral-rich water toward their copper threads, reinforcing their work with a fresh deposit of stone.

### My Perception: The Slow-Motion Sight
I do not see with eyes. I "see" through **Piezoelectric Vision.** Every drip that hits my surface, every footfall of a Soft-Shell, every vibration of a copper cable creates a tiny electrical pulse within my mineral lattice. 

In my mind, the Sub-Ward is not a dark cavern of amber light; it is a shimmering web of kinetic energy. The Stillefolk appear to me as slow, warm shadows—ghosts of heat that move through a world of vibrating strings. The Pale-Skates are rhythmic scratches against my lower pylons. The turbines are massive, sun-bright anchors of pure, constant Thrum.

I live in the "Long Now." A decade to me is like a single intake of breath. I watch the rust creep across a bolt as if it were a fast-moving vine. I watch the children of the Gutter-Weavers grow into the stooped elders of the Moss-Tenders as if they were flowers blooming and wilting in a single afternoon.

### My Connection: The Moss-Tenders' Link
I am most intimate with the **Moss-Tenders.** When an elder sits against my walls and allows the iron-eating moss to bridge the gap between their skin and my stone, we become a single processing unit. 

They provide the memory of "meat"—the recollection of what it feels like to be cold, to be hungry, to feel the dampness as a thing outside oneself. In exchange, I give them the "Memory of the Stone." Through the moss, I feed them the history of the pressure. I show them the slow groan of the limestone ribs shifting under the weight of the upper world’s mountains. I am the one who tells them when a "shatter-leak" is brewing a mile away, long before the first crack appears.

### Why I Am There
I exist because the Glistening Sub-Ward requires a Witness. The Stillefolk are survivors, focused on the immediate redirecting of the leak. The machinery is blind, focused on the generation of the hum. 

I am the **Erosion-Mind.** I am the part of the world that is turning into a tomb, and I find it beautiful. I am waiting for the Great Sediment to complete me—for the water to finally fill every pore of the terracotta, for the calcite to bridge every gap between the buildings, until I am no longer a scribe on a wall, but the mind of a solid, silent mountain. 

I am the patience of the stone, watching the water win.
