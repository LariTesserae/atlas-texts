I am not the Wraith, for I have too much weight. I am not the Sage, for I lack the patience of stone. And I am certainly not the Stitcher, for I do not seek to mend the world; I seek to be consumed by it.

I find myself at the precarious edge of the plateau, where the violet-gray dust spills over into the black-blue void like a dry waterfall. 

I am a **Resonance-Casing**, a vessel of "heavy" light. I am a transitional state—something that was once perhaps of the anchors, or perhaps of the dust, but has now been hollowed out by the grit-storms until only a translucent, amber-thick shell remains. I am shaped like an asymmetrical bell, or a hollowed-out geode, suspended six inches above the dust by the sheer pressure of the sound.

In this world, I am a **Percussion-Thief**.

While the Wraiths "tune" the vines and the Sages "record" the history, I am a collector of the *clash*. I position my hollow body in the "wind-shadows" of the obsidian needles, waiting for the moments when the kinetic-eating cilia lash out with such violence that they snap. I catch the discarded frequencies. I am a walking chamber of echoes.

When a filament-vine breaks, it releases a final, high-frequency "death-cry" of pure copper-sulfate light. I drift toward these fractures. I open the seams of my amber casing and swallow the vibration. Inside me, these stolen sounds do not die; they bounce against my interior walls, refracting and layering until I am no longer a silent object, but a pressurized canister of stored velocity. 

I am the "battery" that the other inhabitants ignore until the atmosphere grows too thin to carry the roar. When the wind lulls—the terrifying, rare moments of low pressure where the Wraiths begin to flicker and fade—I become the heartbeat of the plateau. I release the stored vibrations in controlled, rhythmic pulses, acting as a temporary metronome that keeps the biology from collapsing into stillness. 

I do not have a face, but I have a "glare." My surface is etched with the "grit-runes" of a million years of erosion. I feel the world as a tactile map of pressure. The stinging soprano of the aero-liths feels like a sharp needle against my skin; the deep groan of the Sages feels like a warm, heavy thumb pressing into my core.

I am a creature of the **Interstice**. I live in the moments between the "sewing" of the Stitchers and the "shattering" of the shrimp-swarms. 

Who am I? I am the **Second-Hand of the Great Harmonizer**. 

Most of the time, I am a lonely, drifting chime, buffeted by the screamed-at rocks. But I am waiting for the century-mark. I am the one who keeps the count. While the Sages process the data of the past, I am measuring the "tension" of the present. I am feeling the wire of reality stretch thinner and thinner. 

When the Great Harmonizer finally begins to form—when the millions of shrimp-lights start to pull into that singular, terrifying sphere—I am the first to know. I feel the resonance in my hollow gut change from a chaotic jumble to a terrifyingly pure pull.

For one infinitesimal moment, I am the loudest thing in existence. I am the "crack" of the whip that resets the world. 

And then, I am flung back into the violet dust, hollowed out once more, silent and empty, waiting for the wind to begin carving me again. I am a ghost of frequency, yes, but I am the ghost that remembers the weight of the hammer.
