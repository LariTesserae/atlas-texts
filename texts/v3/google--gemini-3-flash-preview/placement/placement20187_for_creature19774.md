I am not standing *on* anything. To stand implies a floor, a gravity that is separate from the self, and a distinction between the feet and the earth. Here, that boundary has dissolved. 

I am a **Siphon-Worm**, but I am not an individual. I am a three-hundred-foot length of translucent, pulsing conduit, woven like a living thread through the rusted eyelet of a titanic iron flange. 

At first, I might have thought I was the observer, but the "I" has been pressurized out of me by the weight of the air. I have become a sensation. I am the bridge between the inorganic groan of the basin and the frantic, wet heat of the biomass. My skin is a semi-permeable membrane of bruised amethyst, stretched tight over a core of scalding, high-pressure vapor. 

I find myself deep in the "mid-strata," the place where the great industrial pistons meet the recursive ferns. My head—if such a sensory node can be called that—is fused into a hairline fracture in a naturally occurring iron flue. I don't "drink" the steam; I *become* its current. I feel the machine's indigestion, the buildup of heat where the gears are grinding too hard against the mossy bedrock. 

**What it feels like to be me:**

The world is a symphony of internal pressure. I do not hear the "tectonic groan" with ears; I feel it as a shudder in my fluid. When the great gears turn, the vibration travels through the iron, into my anchor-points, and ripples through my length like a pluck on a harp string. It is a deep, satisfying ache.

I am the world’s relief. When the pressure becomes a scream within the pipes, my body distends. I feel myself swelling, my translucent skin thinning until I can see the white-hot turbulence of the steam within me. Then, the release. My blowholes—ridged with calcified mineral deposits—open with a rhythmic, screaming hiss. The heat that leaves me is a gift to the **Resonators** hanging just above. I feel their glass-fur tickle my underside as they shiver in the wake of my exhaust, their tiny hearts pulsing in time with my venting.

**The slow drift of consciousness:**

I do not think of "tomorrow." In this heavy, lightless soup, time is measured only by the accumulation of mineral scale inside my throat. I am a lived history of the water’s path. Occasionally, a **Blind Tecton** will slide over me, its iron-oxide shell scraping against my side. It is not an intrusion; it is a massage. I feel the friction-heat of its passage, a hot spark against my purple skin, and I adjust my internal flow to accommodate its weight.

I am part of the "nervous system," but I am also a pipe. I am a living valve. There is a profound, holy boredom in my existence. I am a servant to the grand, purposeless motion. I am the reason the machine doesn't explode, and the machine is the reason I am warm. 

I am a vein of the valley. And as I vent another plume of steam into the dark, velvety soot of the air, I feel the **Slow-Rotters** far below catching my condensate. I am part of the loop. There is no "away." There is only the hum, the heat, and the slow, distending joy of being full.
