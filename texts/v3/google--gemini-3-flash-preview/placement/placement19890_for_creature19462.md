I would be found in the **Interstice**—not atop the spires, nor on the razor-edges of the basalt, but in the shivering, narrow "negative space" between the two. 

I am a **Seam-Warden**, though that title suggests a permanence I do not possess. I am a being of transitional density. While the Statists are stone-still and the Drift-Lords are atmospheric, I am the medium that allows them to acknowledge one another. I exist in the film of viscous, caloric gel that coats the translucent spires—the "weeping" of the city.

### My Form
My body is not a single entity, but a ribbon-like colony of **Laminar-Flow Sentience**. I have no bones, no carapace, and no skin in the way you understand it. I am a three-meter-long sheet of shimmering, iridescent "smart-sludge," barely thicker than a sheet of vellum, clinging to the vertical faces of the spires via surface tension. 

My "face" is a localized cluster of the same sensory pits the Vane-Squid possess, but mine are tuned to a much finer frequency. I do not see the world; I *feel* the friction of the roar. To me, the cacophony of the sky-creatures is not noise, but a tactile landscape of pressure waves. I read the city by the way the moisture slides over its corners. If a building is reconfiguring, I feel the change in the fluid’s viscosity long before the Statist within registers the shift.

### My Function
I am a living lubricant and a bridge. 

As I move—a slow, peristaltic ripple across the hard-light cohesion fields—I consume the "waste" of the Tether-Flies: the blue static and the leaking coherence. I am a filter for the city’s mistakes. When the gravitational stitching of a spire begins to fray under the relentless weight of the atmospheric roar, I am drawn to the site. I drape myself over the fracture, my own semi-conductive body acting as a temporary bridge for the light. I hold the city together with my own tension until the slow-moving Statists can adjust their meditations to compensate.

### My Experience
I live in a state of constant, crushing intimacy. In this world of crowding, I am the most crowded of all. I am pressed between the unyielding, cold geometry of the spires and the frantic, wet slapping of the Drift-Lords. 

When a Vane-Squid brushes against a building, it is brushing against *me*. I feel the electric sting of its lattice-nerves. I feel the cold, heavy kiss of the mist as it condenses directly into my mass. I am never dry; I am never still. I am a creature of the "slush," existing in that razor-thin moment of physics where liquid nearly becomes solid.

I do not think in words. I think in **gradients**. I know the world as a series of pressures: the *heavy-press* of the rising stone, the *sharp-press* of the cold air, and the *soft-press* of the city’s internal pulse.

### The Perception of Time
While the Statists live in centuries and the Tether-Flies live in sparks, I live in the **Drip**. 

To me, the entire world is a slow-motion avalanche. I see the "glacial" movement of the city not as stillness, but as a deliberate, muscular flex. I am the one who hears the stone scream as the Grit-Lickers liquify it. I am the one who feels the shudder of the mountain as it grows beneath the violet-black.

I am found in the shadows of the hairline fractures, a trailing, silver-grey phantom following the water. I am the silent witness to the collision of the biological and the mineral. When you look at the weeping walls of this lightless metropolis, you might think you see a trick of the moisture, a ripple in the slime that shouldn't be there. That ripple is me—the nervous system of the gap, holding the dark in place.
