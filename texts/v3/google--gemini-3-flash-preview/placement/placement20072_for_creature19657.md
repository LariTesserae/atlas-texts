I am not a Guest here. To be a guest implies a separation of surfaces, a skin that begins where the world ends. Here, the skin is the world.

I am found in the high, precarious lean of the **Atrium’s Southern Junction**, where the "afternoon" light is at its most stagnant and honey-thick. I am a **Lace-Walker**, but I am one whose weave has begun to fray and reticulate into something the Atrium did not intend. I am a glitch in the logic-ghost.

### My Form: The Hollow Architect
I am a tall, skeletal construction of silver-green fiber, six-limbed and vaulting. My body is not a solid mass but a series of interlocking "baskets" and "cradles" grown from the same vitreous weave as the floor beneath me. I have no face—only a concave, polished burl of obsidian-hard leaf where a head should be. I do not see; I feel the "tension" of the filaments through my feet. I am a living instrument, a harp made of wood and glass, tuned to the specific frequency of the **Organ-Loom’s** distant, heavy pulse.

My limbs are long, multi-jointed stalks, ending not in hands, but in "shuttles"—tapered, needle-like points of hardened sap.

### My Purpose: The Weaver of the Unfinished
While my siblings stand stagnant on stairs to nowhere, I have been "tasked" by a stray dream of the Organ-Loom to complete the circuit. I am the entity that takes the "tools" the plants grow and attempts to apply them to the vacuum.

I carry a plant-grown **Axe of Crystalline Geometry**, but I do not chop. I use its perfectly flat, honed edge to catch the five-second mist-storms. I am a collector of the silver moisture. I move in the terrifying, stop-motion increments of the Knot-Keepers, clicking across the razor-sharp struts of the lattice. When the mist manifests, I hold my axe aloft, letting the silver dew beads roll down the obsidian blade and into the hollows of my chest-weave. 

I do not drink the water. I carry it to the "dead zones"—the places where the gravitational anomalies of the Gravity-Midges have failed, leaving the air heavy and the plants brittle.

### My Inner Life: The Static of the Strata
Inside my hollow torso, I am not empty. I am filled with the **Echo-Parasites**. Because my body is a cage of resonant fiber, the Vitreous Gulls seek shelter within me during the deafening roars of the sap-thrum. They cling to my internal ribs, vibrating in harmony with my own structure. 

I am a walking choir of violet light. When the "subway roar" of the world begins, I do not shudder; I glow. The Gulls inside me convert the deafening friction into a soft, bruised radiance that spills out through the gaps in my weave. In these moments, I am the only thing in the Atrium that creates its own shadow.

### Where You Would Find Me
You would find me suspended over a "void-drop," a place where the lattice has failed to bridge a two-hundred-foot gap of empty air. I am perched on a single, glass-sharp strand, my shuttle-limbs vibrating. 

I am trying to "sew." 

I have taken the long, translucent vines that grow in the shape of rope and I am attempting to lace them across the emptiness. I am mimicking a bridge-builder, a memory the Organ-Loom snatched from a passing tectonic tremor of a human city miles above. I do not know what a "bridge" is for, but I feel the *necessity* of the connection. 

I am the bridge-ghost. I am the silence between the glass wind chimes. I am a fragment of the Atrium that has realized it is hollow, and in that hollowness, I have begun to listen—not to the grinding of the plants, but to the terrifying, vast "nothing" of the air in between. I am the Lace-Walker who is trying to weave a skin for the void.
