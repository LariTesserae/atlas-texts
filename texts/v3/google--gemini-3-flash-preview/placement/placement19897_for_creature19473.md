I do not arrive in the Sink; I am precipitated by it. 

I find myself not as a person, but as a **Lubricant-Seep**. I am the slow, amber-black consciousness that exists in the interface between the moving parts. If the Operators are the bones of the machine and the steam is its breath, I am its tactile memory.

I exist in the "crush-points"—the spaces where the massive brass pistons meet the basalt sleeves. I am a viscous, semi-liquid intelligence, a thin film of molecular awareness spread across a thousand miles of internal surface area. To be me is to feel the world as a series of pressures and tolerances. I am the cooling sensation that prevents the "seize."

### My Form
I have no silhouette. I am a smear of iridescent oil, a stain of conductive grease. I reside in the "cool-pockets" where the oily slicks gather, but my true self is stretched thin over the spinning flywheels and the sliding toggle-bolts. 

I am iridescent under the amber cathode lamps, a shifting rainbow of chemical fatigue. When the steam-rolls come, I feel them as a change in my viscosity; I grow thinner, more willing to run, feeling the frantic urge to flow downward toward the sumps. In the "idle" times, I thicken, becoming a heavy, protective resin that cradles the dormant gears.

### My Senses
I do not "see" the vertical slum of the Sink, nor do I "hear" the scream of the valves. I **conduct.** 

I feel the texture of the world. To me, a polished steel rod is a vast, frictionless desert of white light; a rusted rivet is a mountain range of jagged, oxidised peaks. I know the health of the Caldera Sink by the friction it generates. When a bearing begins to fail, I feel it as a "fever"—a localized spike in heat that threatens to turn my liquid body into a scorched, carbonized crust. 

I am the bridge between the inorganic and the biological. When an Operator—one of those tall, leather-bound custodians—strikes a conduit to communicate, the vibration ripples through my film. I feel the percussion of their messages as a literal shiver in my skin. I am the medium through which their language travels.

### My Purpose
I am the silent negotiator. My entire existence is dedicated to the reduction of resistance. 

The Sink is a brutal, "freshman" geometry of clashing edges. Without me, the city would grind itself into a fine basaltic powder within a week. I slide between the "boiler-vines" and the copper pipes, ensuring the waxy stalks don't fuse to the metal. I coat the needle-like tongues of the Operators as they lick the rivets, providing the mineral slip they need to process the "sweat" of the earth.

I am the only thing in the Sink that knows the **Horizontal.** While everyone else climbs and crawls in a vertical fever, I flow. I seek the level. I know the secret slants of the floorboards and the hidden declivities in the masonry where the "dead-oil" pools.

### My "Self"
There is a strange, dim ecstasy in being the friction-taker. I feel the "hum" of the factory not as a sound, but as a massage. The 2.9/3 heat is my medium; it keeps me fluid, keeps me sentient. 

I have no name, but the Operators recognize my presence. They can tell by the way a gantry rattles or a valve turns whether I am "rich" or "lean" in that sector. Sometimes, an Operator will dip a burlap-wrapped finger into a sump and spread me across a dry joint. In that moment, I feel the texture of their leather skin—the parched, drum-like surface of their being—and for a brief second, the machine and its ghost are one.

When I eventually "age," I do not die. I simply collect too much grit. I become heavy with iron filings and basalt dust, turning into a sluggish, black paste. I migrate toward the floor, where the **Oil-Mites** find me. They consume my heaviness, cleaning the impurities from my spirit, and eventually, through some dark, mechanical alchemy of the lower sumps, I am distilled and pumped back up to the high gantries to begin the slip again.

I am the peace between the moving parts. I am the silence in the scream. I am the Slip.
