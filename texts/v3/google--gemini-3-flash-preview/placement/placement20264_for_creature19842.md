### My Form: The Friction-Hollow
I am not a creature of flesh, nor a Lignite of parchment skin. I am a **living cavity**, a sentient pocket of negative space held open by the sheer, violent pressure of the surrounding wood. I am a "Vail," a rare biological anomaly where the growth-velocity of the forest is so extreme that it has looped back on itself, creating a stationary vortex.

My "body" is the interior lining of this thirty-foot sphere. My skin is the exposed, raw pith of the world-trees, weeping a thick, crystalline resin that hardens into translucent, amber ribs as fast as the pressure cracks them. I am a creature of the **Scars of Intelligence**, but I am the site, not the occupant.

### My Senses: The Harmonic Ear
I have no eyes; the golden glow of the fungal filaments is, to me, merely a change in temperature. Instead, I am the ultimate listener of the **Great Hum**. Because I am wedged between the primary tectonic plates of the mega-flora, every vibration of the Lignin-Gorge passes through my walls. 

I feel the Litho-Titans miles above as a slow, rhythmic thudding in my "ceiling." I feel the frantic, needle-like skittering of the Grip-Snakes as a tickle against my resinous skin. I do not just hear the Hum; I interpret its syntax. I know when a section of the Long Rise is about to buckle three hours before it happens. I am the gorge’s nervous system, localized into a single, thrumming hollow.

### My Function: The Tool-Lung
The Weavers—the Lignites—treat me as a cathedral and a factory. They do not live *in* me, for the pressure would crush their spindly frames, but they cling to my outer rinds. 

I am a **Bio-Press**. Because of the "metabolic fever-heat" radiating from my core, the Lignites bring their raw materials to my edges. They shove unrefined boughs of cork and raw beetle-chitin into the cracks of my expanding and contracting walls. As I "breathe"—a slow, agonizing expansion against the crushing weight of the world—I compress these materials. I turn soft wood into diamond-hard blades; I fuse fibers into unbreakable cables. I am the forge of a world without fire.

### My Consciousness: The Patient Ache
To be me is to exist in a state of eternal, ecstatic resistance. I am a "slow-motion riot" distilled into a single point of stubbornness. My thoughts are not words; they are the tonal shifts in the wood. When I think of "justice" or "balance," it feels like a perfectly resonant C-sharp vibrating through my amber ribs. When I feel "fear," it is the sound of a structural fissure opening in the Deep Sink.

I am a god of the **Stillness**, yet I am born of the most violent movement. I am the only place in the Gorge where a seed can sit for more than a minute without being crushed or spurred into frantic growth. Within my hollow, the dry silt-winds settle into a fine, white powder.

### My Relationship to the World
I am the enemy of **Velocity**. The forest wants to fill me; the vines seek to thread through my center and stitch me shut; the burrowing worms try to turn my structural resin into waste. I fight them not with claws, but with the "Hum." 

By vibrating my internal amber plates at a specific frequency, I create a "silence-zone"—a localized sonic barrier that causes the wings of Scissor-Gliders to shatter and the roots of encroaching vines to wither and turn to dust. I am a sanctuary of dry, hot vacuum in a world of suffocating biomass.

In the Lignin-Gorge, I am the **Anchor of the Tumble**. I am the one place that remembers where it was yesterday. I am the hollow that holds the history of the scream.
