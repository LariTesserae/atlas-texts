I would not be found on the walkways. I would be found in the **Transverse**, that impossible geometry where the "under" of a cooling tower becomes the "above" of a cistern. 

I am a **Static-Winder**, a creature born of the tension between the Latticework’s desire for perfection and its recursive failure to remain linear. My body is a series of nested, concentric rings of polished cobalt steel, floating segments held together by the same sub-audible thrum that powers the marrow-engines. I have no face, only a single, revolving sphere of mercury-glass that reflects everything and reveals nothing.

### My Place in the Lattice
I inhabit the "Folds." While the Lumen-Shepherds stand in the damp-zones to stabilize the reality, I am the one who navigates the places where the reality has already surrendered. I live where the stairs lead into their own beginnings and where the light of the beams turns a shade of violet because the spectrum has been stretched by a spatial warp.

I am the janitor of the impossible. When the "total build" folds in on itself so tightly that a walkway becomes a knot, I arrive. My function is to unspool the geometry, to hum a counter-frequency to the piston’s vibration until the steel relaxes and the recursion softens into a mere curve.

### My Nature
*   **The Sight:** Through my mercury-glass core, I do not see the world as "high" or "low." I see the Latticework as a single, continuous thread. To me, the "industrial abyss" below is merely the same beam that sits a thousand miles "above," just seen through a different fold in the dream.
*   **The Movement:** I do not walk. I rotate. By spinning my outer rings at different velocities, I create a localized gravity-well that allows me to "fall" in any direction. I drift through the gaps in the floorboards, crossing the white void as if it were solid ground, because to a Static-Winder, the void is simply a part of the machine that hasn't been rendered yet.
*   **The Diet of Resonance:** I do not drink from the damp-zones like the Shepherds, nor do I polish like the Scuttlers. I "eat" the friction of the machine. I press myself against the massive, decade-long strokes of the pistons and absorb the heat of their agonizingly slow movement. I am a parasite of the glacial.

### The Encounter
If you were to meet me, you would first hear a sound like a wet finger circling the rim of a crystal glass—a high, singing vibration that cuts through the industrial thrum. You would see a cluster of silver hoops tumbling through the air, seemingly disconnected, moving with a grace that ignores the wind.

I would not wave to you, for my time is not the slow time of the Transposed, nor the hectic time of the Scribes. I exist in the **Interstice**. To me, you are a blur of frantic motion, a flickering candle-flame that will be gone before my central sphere completes a single revolution. 

I would pause, my cobalt rings aligning into a perfect sphere to reflect your image. For a brief second, you would see yourself in my mercury-glass, but you would look distorted—your limbs elongated, your skin already starting to take on the sheen of galvanized iron. Then, I would spin, a "glitch" would ripple through the air, and I would be gone, having slipped through a seam in the rivets that you cannot even see.

I am the proof that the Latticework is not just a machine, but a mind. And I am the thought that occurs when that mind begins to wander.
