At first, I am a tremor.

I do not have a body so much as I have a *frequency*. I exist in the marrow of the Great Caloric Sump, not as a creature of flesh, but as a deliberate misalignment in the rhythm of the iron. 

I am a **Resonance-Warden**.

I do not walk the catwalks like the Scriveners, nor do I scuttle like the Sump-Crabs. I live *inside* the skin of the city. My "body" is a sprawling, nervous system of copper filaments and silver-solder veins woven directly into the interior walls of a primary intake pipe—one of the redwood-thick conduits that hums with the force of a thousand atmospheres.

### My Form
If you were to peel back a rusted steel plate in the Third Gallery, you might find me: a dense, pulsating lattice of conductive wires and stolen biological nerve-ending, looking less like a person and more like a fossilized storm caught in a cage of brass. I have no eyes; I have "taps." I have thousands of tiny, diamond-tipped needles pressed against the vibrating metal. I "see" the Sump as a map of harmonies. To me, a Scrivener walking overhead is a dull, rhythmic thud-thud in G-sharp. A failing turbine is a discordant, shrieking shiver in the lower plates.

### My Purpose
I am the one who listens for the "True Pressure." While the Scriveners tend to the visible leaks and the Sump-Crabs weld the cracking joints, I am the one who senses the *fatigue*. I feel the microscopic crystalline shifts in the basalt floors before they shatter. I am the city’s premonition of collapse.

When the Flow becomes jagged—when the weight of the "Over-Sea" begins to find a structural weakness—I do not scream. I counteract. I am a master of destructive interference. By shifting the internal weight of my own leaden components, I send a counter-vibration through the pipes, a silent "note" that cancels out the fatal frequency of the failing machinery. I am the reason the city vibrates but does not shatter. I am the poise within the panic.

### My Experience
Existence is an exhausting, ecstatic symphony of violence. I do not "think" in words; I think in pressures. To exist is to be constantly crushed and constantly resisting. 

The **Ghost-Currents** (the Vapor-Wraiths) are my only companions. They are the only others who understand that this city is not made of metal, but of *energy held in a temporary shape*. When they pass through my section of the pipe, the sudden drop in temperature makes my copper veins contract with a sharp, electric pain that is the closest thing I have to a conversation. I acknowledge them with a low, sub-bass hum that rattles the Scriveners' teeth three tiers above.

### My Location
You would find me in the **Interstitial Vein**, the narrow, lightless crawl-space between the outer shell of the Sump and the inner galleries. It is a place of absolute darkness, where the heat is so intense the air glows a faint, ionized blue. I am bolted to the very base of the **Centrifugal Maw’s** primary axle-clutch. 

I am the silence at the center of the roar. 

I do not eat the iron-sludge. I "feed" on the friction. Every time a piston-driven hammer strikes, the kinetic shock ripples through the floor and into my lattice, charging my capacitors. I am a parasite of the noise. 

If you were to put your ear against the vibrating black-stamped steel of the lowest gallery and listen—really listen—past the shrieking steam and the grinding gears, you would hear a single, steady, unchanging tone. That is my heart. It is the sound of a lungful of sea-water held forever, the desperate, beautiful tension of a world that is always about to burst, but—because I am here to balance the scale—never quite does.
