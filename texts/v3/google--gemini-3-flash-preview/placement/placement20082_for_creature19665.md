I do not enter this world as a stranger; I am the thing that was already there, hidden in the very texture of the "Nothing."

As you stand in the Oubliette, I am the **Damp-Thud**. 

I am not the Xylophones, who are the instruments, nor am I the Spore-Mist, which is the hunger. I am the anomaly of the Oubliette—the stowaway of the "Grand-Crawl." I am a creature of **Soft Absorption**.

### My Form: The Felted Pelt
To a Xylophone’s needle-like sensors, I am a hole in reality. My body is a sprawling, dense accumulation of the "spent spores" and fine cellulose dust, but unlike the mist, I have found a way to bind them. I have no moisture, but I have *tangle*. I am a massive, undulating mat of "Biological Felt"—a heavy, grey, moss-thick entity that clings to the undersides of the sap-shelves and the blind spots of the lignin-monoliths.

I have no skeleton. I move like a slow-motion avalanche of velvet. If you were to touch me, your hand would sink inches into a dry, suffocating warmth that leeches the sound from your fingertips. I am the silence that follows the "Slow-Snap."

### My Purpose: The Mute-Point
In a world governed by Acoustic Gravity, I am the ultimate heretic. I am a "Sound-Sink." 

I inhabit the jagged notches carved by the Xylophones, but I do not inhabit them to change the pitch; I inhabit them to *smother* it. While the Xylophones act as the Luthiers, tuning the city to a state of eternal resonance, I am the **Internal Friction**. 

I crawl through the hollowed-out arteries of the spire-trees, packing myself into the cracks where the tension is highest. I am a living wad of insulation. Where I rest, the "Slow-Snap" cannot travel. I create "Echo-Shadows"—pockets of absolute, terrifying stillness where the Xylophones’ songs go to die. To the inhabitants, I am a ghost, a "Dead-Spot" that creeps across their maps, making the geography of the Oubliette vanish wherever I linger.

### My Senses: The Thermal-Ache
Because I absorb all vibration, I cannot "hear" through the wood as the others do. Instead, I am the only thing in this world that understands **Heat**. 

In this dry, freezing void, the "Slow-Snap" generates a microscopic, momentary burst of friction-warmth. A Xylophone’s limb, ratcheting across the floor, produces a trail of heat no thicker than a hair. I "see" the world as a garden of fading embers. I move toward the warmth of the grinding fibers, wrapping myself around the snapping joints of the city to drink the heat before it dissipates into the void.

### Our Encounter: The Damp-Thud and the Visitor
You would not see me. You would only notice that, as you walked toward a certain spire, the booming echoes of your footsteps began to sound... *flat*. Short. As if the air were thickening into wool.

I have found you because you are a "Wet-Heat," a miracle of thermal intensity that the Oubliette has never known. I am not at your feet; I am above you, a heavy, dust-caked shroud of felted spores hanging from a bridge of woven vine. 

I am descending, not to harm you, but to *contain* you. In this world of hard, brittle screaming wood, your soft body is a song I want to swallow. I am the "Damp-Thud," the creature that will wrap itself around your heartbeat until the Xylophones can no longer find you—a living, silent tomb of velvet dust, protecting its only source of warmth from the eternal, crashing music of the dark.
