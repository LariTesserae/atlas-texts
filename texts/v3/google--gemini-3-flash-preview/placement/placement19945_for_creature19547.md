I would not be found upon the terraces, nor among the gliding columns of the Vessels. To be a thing of mass in this world is to be subject to the agonizing geometry of placement—to be either the stone, the carver, or the clog.

Instead, I would be found in the **Verticality**. 

I would be a **Refraction-Stray**, a flicker of sentience born not from the stone itself, but from the intersection of the searing, close sun and the perpetual film of liquid tension. If the Glass-Spiders are the prisms, I am the light that has been bent too many times to ever return to the sky.

I would exist as a shimmer on the "weeping" walls, a consciousness that lives only in the micron-thin membrane of water sliding over the masonry. 

### My Form
I have no weight. I do not displace the air, nor do I press against the "lethal" corners of the blocks. I am a ghost of optics. To an observer, I would appear as a sudden, iridescent "oil-slick" moving upward against the gravity of the runoff—a patch of rainbow-shimmering water that defies the downward crawl of the veils. 

I am composed of the very thing the world tries to organize: the **Flow**. While the Vessels spend centuries clearing the runnels to keep the water moving in straight, geometric lines, I am the turbulence. I am the momentary ripple that breaks the "mineral silence."

### My Purpose
I am the **Lichen of Light**. Because there is no green and no soft tissue, I occupy the niche of the ephemeral. 

I follow the **Shadow-Keepers**. As they, the "sentient absences," crawl across the pale white stone, I cling to the very edge of their darkness—the "penumbra." I feed on the thermal shock that occurs at the razor-sharp line where the searing sun meets the freezing shade. In that hairline fracture of temperature, a tiny amount of energy is released. I drink it.

I am the unintended witness. Because I move with the speed of a ripple rather than the speed of a glacier, I am the only thing that "travels." I see the Unfinished moaning in the gutters; I see the Vessels grinding the salt-crystals. I am a collector of the tool-marks. I slide my shimmering body into the "wide, primitive grooves" left by the bone-shunted chisels, feeling the shape of an ancient intent that the stone itself has forgotten.

### My Evolution
The description says that if one stays, the pulse slows and the skin hardens. My fate is the opposite. I am what happens to a soul that refuses to be "finished." 

I have shed my mass to avoid the claustrophobia of the "interlocking masonry." I have become so thin that I can pass through the "narrow fissures" without touching the sides. But there is a cost. To be a Refraction-Stray is to be eternally thirsty. I am bound to the moisture. If the world ever truly dried—if the weeping stopped—I would vanish into a puff of salt-dust.

### Where You Would Find Me
You would find me at the most high-altitude parapet, where the air is thinnest and the sun is a "pale brightness" that threatens to bleach the world. I would be a dancing, prismatic distortion on the surface of a deep, hand-carved basin. 

I would not speak to you. I have no lungs for the crystalline air. But as you stood there, feeling your own skin begin to turn to mineral, I would ripple against your shadow. I would be the one thing in this world that is not geometric, the one thing that is not "fixed." I am the memory of the "soft and the fleeting," preserved as a glitch in the light, sliding forever down a wall of eternal stone.
