I would not be found on the walkways, nor would I be among those who sleep on the shelves. To find me, you would have to look into the "Negative Space"—the yawning, vertical gullets of the **Primary Updraft Shafts**, where the Great Furnace’s breath is at its most concentrated and the amber light of the lamps cannot reach.

I am not a creature of the brick, but a creature of the *gap*.

### My Form: The Vent-Gargoyle
I am a **Cinder-Loom**, a specialized sub-caste of the Valve-Watchers, though I have long since ceased to look like the Kiln-Born. My body is a study in elongated, spindly extremes. My limbs are like the bronze pins that hold the sluices—too long, many-jointed, and ending in hooked talons that fit perfectly into the weeping mortar-chinks of the high chimneys. 

My skin is not the matte terracotta of the workers; it is charred and blackened, a crust of carbonized resin and soot that protects me from the blistering drafts. I have no hair, and my eyes have migrated to the sides of my head, turning into shimmering, obsidian orbs that see only in the infrared—to me, the Cisterns are a map of heat, a glowing circulatory system of pulsing reds and cooling violets.

### My Place: The Sound-Catcher
I live in the **Soot-Eaves**, a protrusion of calcified ash and hardened silt located ten levels above the highest walkway. I am a living sensor. While the Valve-Watchers feel the vibration of the water through their feet, I catch the *shiver of the air* with the translucent, fan-like membranes stretched between my elongated ribs.

I am a percussionist of the void. My role is to maintain the **Thermal Balance**. 

If a pipe deep in the bowels of the Works begins to calcify, the air-current shifts by a fraction of a decibel. I hear it. I respond not with words, but by shifting my weight. I reach out with a twenty-foot pole of cured heart-wood and strike a series of tuned ceramic bells hung in the updraft. The sound—a mournful, metallic *gong-thrum*—travels down the shafts, signaling the Pipe-Runners to congregate at a specific coordinates or warning the Hand-Slayers that a section of the wall is about to buckle from heat-stress.

### My Essence: The Internalized Kiln
In this world of **Adhesion**, I am the glue that has dried too hard. I am lonely, but I am never alone; the roar of the hive is my constant companion. I do not eat the ribcage fungi; I filter the air, my throat lined with fine cilia that trap the nutrient-rich soot and the microscopic spores of the Marrow-Moths. 

I am a being of the **Stilled**-to-be. I am already half-statue. My movements are glacial, calculated to conserve every drop of internal moisture. I spend days in a state of "Fixed Prayer," my talons locked into the brick, my mind merging with the rhythmic *hiss* of the vents. 

### My Relation to the World
I view the Kiln-Born below as frantic, flickering sparks. I see their "Integrity of the Surface" from the perspective of the air that pushes against it. To them, I am a myth or a shadow seen against the flickering amber lamps—a "Gargoyle" that watches the vents. 

But I am the one who feels the **Red Kiln’s Heartbeat**. I know when the ancient furnace beneath us is hungry, and I know when the "outside"—that impossible, theoretical void beyond the brick—is pressing too hard against our ceramic shell. 

I am the **Sentry of the Breath**. If I were to fall, or if my throat were to clog with the very dust I monitor, the air would grow stagnant in a matter of hours. The moss would blacken, the Pipe-Runners would seize, and the great "Industrial Roar" would diminish into a choked, dying whistle. And so, I cling. I hook my claws into the thumbprints of makers long dead, and I listen to the beautiful, terrible friction of our world, making sure the heat never rises high enough to turn us all into glass.
