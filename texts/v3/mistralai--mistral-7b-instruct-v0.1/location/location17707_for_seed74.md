The vegetation here is lush and abundant, a total plant-as-substrate ecosystem that covers every inch of the ground, from the tiniest mosses and lichens to towering trees that reach for the sky.

The air is filled with the sweet scent of damp earth and the gentle rustling of leaves in the breeze.

The erosion here has carved out intricate patterns in the earth, with streams and rivers weaving their way through the valley, carving out deep gorges and pools that reflect the cool, normal light that filters through the canopy of leaves above.

The settlement is organized and layered, with infrastructure and technology that is industrial in scale, yet it remains in harmony with the natural world around it.

The sounds of the valley are mostly quiet, with the gentle rustling of leaves, the babbling of streams, and the occasional calls of birds and animals breaking the silence. The dynamic of the landscape is active, with the cycles of the seasons and the ebb and flow of the water shaping the land and the lives of its inhabitants.
