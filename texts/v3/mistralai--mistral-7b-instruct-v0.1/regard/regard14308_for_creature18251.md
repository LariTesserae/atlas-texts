The benevolent and wise power, observing this wondrous realm and its inhabitants, is moved by the intricate balance and harmony that exists between all living beings. This power, known as the Guardian of the Cosmos, decides to bestow a gift upon this realm and its Crystal Sages.

The Guardian of the Cosmos, in its infinite wisdom, creates a magnificent tree at the heart of the plateau. This tree, unlike any other, is imbued with the essence of all the elements, its roots reaching deep into the submerged waters, its trunk strong and unyielding, its branches stretching towards the heavens, adorned with leaves of purest gold.

The Crystal Sages, sensing the presence of the Guardian of the Cosmos, are drawn to the tree. They marvel at its beauty and the power that emanates from it. The Guardian of the Cosmos explains that this tree is a symbol of the unity and interconnectedness of all living beings, a reminder of the delicate balance that sustains their world.

The Guardian of the Cosmos also grants the Crystal Sages with the ability to communicate with all beings in the realm, allowing them to share their knowledge and wisdom more effectively. This gift strengthens the bond between the Crystal Sages and the other inhabitants of the realm, fostering a deeper understanding and appreciation for the intricate web of life that sustains them all.

The Guardian of the Cosmos also imbues the water of the realm with the power to heal and rejuvenate, ensuring that all living beings are nourished and sustained. This gift brings about a renewed sense of vitality and energy, allowing the beings of the realm to thrive and flourish.

The Guardian of the Cosmos, having completed its mission, departs, leaving behind a realm that is more harmonious and balanced than ever before. The Crystal Sages, in turn, become the guardians of this gift, ensuring that the delicate balance of their world is maintained for generations to come.

And so, the realm continues to thrive, a testament to the wonders and mysteries of the universe, a constant reminder of the power and beauty of nature, and a beacon of hope and inspiration for all beings that call it home.
