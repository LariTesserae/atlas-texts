This gift is given as a soft, melodic hum that resonates through the water, connecting all beings and allowing them to share their thoughts, ideas, and emotions.

The Guardian also grants them the ability to manipulate the water around them, allowing them to create currents, shape the water into structures, and even control the weather. This gift is given as a gentle touch of energy that flows through the water, imbuing them with the power to shape their environment and harness the raw power of the water.

Finally, the Guardian blesses the realm with a protective shield, ensuring that it remains a place of magic and wonder, free from harm and destruction. This shield is a subtle, invisible barrier that surrounds the realm, deflecting any negative energy or intentions that may threaten its inhabitants and their home.

With these gifts, the inhabitants of this realm are able to continue to explore and thrive in their unique and otherworldly home, their connection to the water and each other deepening and growing stronger with each passing day.
