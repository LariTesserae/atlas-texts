Some, like the mountain goats that cling to the sides of the cliffs, have developed strong limbs and sure-footedness to navigate the rocky terrain. Others, like the birds of prey that circle above the mountains, have developed keen eyesight and powerful talons to hunt their prey from the air.

The vast, sprawling expanse of life and wonder that is this land is home to a diverse array of creatures, each adapted to the unique conditions of their environment.
