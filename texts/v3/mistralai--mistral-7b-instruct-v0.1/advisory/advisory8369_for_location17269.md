Preparing for a visit to this desolate and ancient expanse requires careful planning and adaptation to the unique challenges of this otherworldly location. Here are some suggestions for an ordinary human multiverse traveler:

1. Hydration: Given the lack of water in this environment, it is essential to carry an ample supply of water or other hydrating fluids. A camelback or a large, insulated water bottle would be ideal.
2. Protective Clothing: The extreme temperatures and harsh terrain call for protective clothing. Wear layers to adapt to the temperature fluctuations, and cover exposed skin to protect against the sun and wind. A wide-brimmed hat and sunglasses are also recommended.
3. Sturdy Footwear: The rugged terrain calls for sturdy footwear with good traction. Hiking boots or sandals with strong soles and good grip would be suitable.
4. Navigation: Given the vastness of the landscape, it is essential to carry a reliable navigation system. A map and compass or a GPS device would be helpful.
5. Shelter: Given the extreme temperatures and harsh conditions, carrying a lightweight and portable shelter would be a good idea. A tent or a tarp would provide protection from the elements.
6. Food: Carry high-energy and nutritious food to sustain yourself during your journey. Dried fruits, nuts, and energy bars would be suitable options.
7. Tools: Carry a basic toolkit, including a multi-tool, a knife, and a hammer or a mallet. These tools may come in handy for repairing equipment or constructing shelter.
8. Communication: Carry a communication device, such as a satellite phone or a radio, to stay in touch with others and to call for help in case of an emergency.
9. First Aid Kit: Carry a well-stocked first aid kit to treat injuries and illnesses. Include bandages, gauze, antiseptic, pain relievers, and any necessary prescription medications.
10. Adaptability: Be prepared for the unexpected. The terrain may be more challenging than anticipated, and the weather may change rapidly. Be flexible and adaptable, and be prepared to adjust your plans accordingly.
11. Respect for the Environment: Be respectful of the fragile ecosystem and the ancient ruins. Do not disturb the plants or animals, and do not leave any trash behind.
12. Cultural Sensitivity: Be respectful of the history and culture of the people who once lived in this place. Learn about their customs and traditions, and be mindful of any artifacts or structures that you encounter.
13. Safety: Travel with a companion or in a group, and let someone know your itinerary and expected return date. Be aware of any potential hazards, such as extreme temperatures, unstable terrain, or dangerous wildlife.
14. Preparation: Spend time researching the location and the challenges you may face. Familiarize yourself with the terrain, the climate, and the potential hazards. The more prepared you are, the more enjoyable your journey will be.
