# PREPARATION PROTOCOL: VISITOR TO THE LITTORAL ENGINE

**Acknowledgement:** You are an ordinary human. This environment is not built for you, nor does it acknowledge your existence. Your presence is a potential anomaly. Proceed with extreme caution and respect. This guide is for **survival**, not comfort.

## I. PHYSICAL PREPARATION: SUIT UP OR DIE

This is not diving. This is **emplacement**. You will be inserting yourself into the bloodstream of a colossal machine.

*   **Pressure Suit (Absolute Necessity):** You require a full-rigid, pressure-retaining atmospheric diving suit. This is not a flexible wetsuit or soft-shellsuit. Think a humanoid submarine: hard composite or titanium shell, spherical joints, and a non-compressible fluid layer (likely a specialized perfluorocarbon) inside to equalize pressure across your body. The suit must maintain 1 atmosphere internally against an external pressure that increases by ~1 atmosphere every 10 meters. At mid-tiers, you could be under hundreds of atmospheres. Your bones would implode without this shell.
*   **Atmosphere:** Closed-circuit rebreather with **triple-redundant CO2 scrubbing**. The "Working Brine" is not breathable; it's a conductive, nutrient soup. Inhaling it would be fatal—your lungs would become clogged filters. The suit's atmosphere must be carefully managed; the only breach allowed is one that seals with hyper-compressed ice, and you do not want to be inside that ice.
*   **Thermal Regulation:** 12°C is hypothermia territory for prolonged exposure. Your suit needs an internal heating system, likely a bio-ethermal layer that recycles your body heat, supplemented by chemical packs. The cool temperature is stable, but your survival depends on fighting it.
*   **Mobility & Propulsion:** This is a **3D environment** with vertical chasms, horizontal voids, and complex infrastructure. You need:
    *   **Magnetic Boots:** For walking on ferromagnetic surfaces (most will be alloy). Essential for stable footing.
    *   **Vector-Control Backpack:** A compact, vectored-thrust system (like a miniaturized submarine's ROV thrusters) for maneuvering in the voids and water columns. **Not a jetpack.** The water is dense; you need to "swim" with controlled bursts.
    *   **Tether Points:** Your suit must have hardpoints for attaching safety tethers to structural members. Getting lost in the drift could mean being swept into a turbine intake or an unforgiving current.

## II. TECHNICAL & NAVIGATIONAL PREPARATION

Your senses are useless here. You must augment them or become lost in seconds.

*   **Sensory Suite (The Most Critical Gear):**
    *   **Active Sonar/ Lidar Array:** Your primary "eyes." Must have a multi-kilometer range and be able to filter out the Engine's constant industrial noise. It will build a 3D map of your surroundings, highlighting mega-structures, voids, fauna, and dense swarms of maintenance-mites. **You will navigate by wireframe model, not sight.**
    *   **Electromagnetic Field (EMF) Scanner:** To detect power conduits, plasma arcs, and reactor piles. The Engine's energy flows are its capillaries; straying into a high-power zone could fry your systems or cook you.
    *   **Chemical Sniffer:** To detect gradients in the brine—nutrient blooms (indicating "seasons"), waste products from machinery, or the molecular information traces that serve as communication.
*   **Communication:** Radio is useless in deep, conductive water. You need:
    *   **Hardline Tether Spool:** A fiber-optic cable that can unspool for kilometers, allowing a direct data link to your entry point or a relay. **This is your umbilical cord.**
    *   **Low-Frequency Acoustic Modem:** For short-range, encoded pulses of sound. Be aware: your transmissions may interfere with the Engine's own sonar systems or attract curious fauna. Use sparingly. Speak in coded data bursts, not voice.
*   **Lighting:**
    *   **Multi-Spectrum Helmet Array:** White light for detail work (short range, drains power), red light to preserve any innate night vision (minimal benefit), and **ultraviolet/blue** light to trigger fluorescence in the synthetic patina, litho-lichen, and certain fauna, revealing hidden patterns or structures.
    *   **Disposable Chem-Light Strobes:** Drop these to mark your path or create reference points in the vastness. The currents will carry them away, so use them for temporary orientation only.

## III. KNOWLEDGE & MENTAL PREPARATION

Your greatest threat after pressure is your own mind. The scale, sound, and emptiness are psychologically crushing.

*   **Study the Cycles:** Understand the "tidal" rhythms of the Engine. If you arrive during a "lean" period, the water will be clearer, the fauna less active, but resources (if you need to harvest any) will be scarce. A "flood" period is biologically intense and can be dangerous, but may also mask your presence in the biological noise.
*   **Respect the Scale:** You will be in places where the nearest solid object is a kilometer away, and the ceiling is a shimmering, unreachable membrane of light. **Vertigo will be constant.** Train yourself to rely on your sonar map, not your eyes. Trust the instruments.
*   **Interpret the Soundscape:** Learn to parse the roar. The deep thrum is the Engine's heart—the closer you are to it, the more you will feel it in your chest cavity, even in the suit. The shriek of turbines indicates active processing zones. The groan of metal is stress; the ping of cooling is normal. **A sudden, total silence is the most dangerous sound of all.** It means a catastrophic failure or a predator has silenced its clicks to hunt.
*   **Accept Your Role:** You are **parasite, not symbiont.** You are not part of the system. The maintenance drones may ignore you, but they may also see you as a fouling agent to be scraped away or a stress fracture to be patched. Do not interfere with operations. Do not block flow channels. Be a ghost. Observe, record, leave no trace.
*   **Emergency Protocol - "The Ice":** Understand that in a breach, the world will seal itself around you. Your suit's outer layer may fuse with the hyper-compressed ice-VI. You might survive, encased in a clear, pressurized tomb, listening to the Engine's roar muffled. Hope for a repair drone to mistake you for a structural flaw and patch you, rather than scrap you.

1.  **Suit Integrity:** Pressure-tested to 120% of expected depth.
2.  **Power Cells:** Triple redundant. Cold drastically reduces battery life.
3.  **Sonar Calibration:** Tuned to filter Engine-specific noise signatures.
4.  **Tether Secured:** Anchor point is unshakeable. Verify.
5.  **Acoustic Profile:** Minimized. All external moving parts are dampened.
6.  **Mental State:** Calm, focused, ready to be utterly insignificant.

You are not visiting a place. You are submitting to it. The Littoral Engine does not care about you. Your goal is to ensure your own survival long enough to witness its terrible, beautiful, and unfathomable purpose.
