**PREPARATION PROTOCOL FOR THE LITHIC HYDRO-COMPUTATION (Colloquially: "The Weeping Fen")**

**First Principle:** Your objective is not to survive, but to *interface*. This is not a hostile environment; it is a comprehensively *occupied* one. Your presence is an input. Prepare to be read.

### **I. PSYCHOLOGICAL & PERCEPTUAL RECALIBRATION**

1.  **Abandon the Solar Clock:** There is no day. There is no night. There is only the Perpetual Now of the system's operation. Before departure, spend 72 hours in a sensory-deprivation tank or a perfectly overcast, drizzly location. De-sync your circadian rhythm. The concepts of "morning" or "evening" are foreign here and will cause a low-grade, persistent anxiety that the landscape will interpret as erratic data.
2.  **Embrace the Whisper:** The information transfer is in the water. You must learn to perceive without direct touch. Practice passive observation of rain on a windowpane, steam on a mirror, dew on a blade of grass. The goal is to shift from *looking at* moisture to *listening to* it. Your mindset should be that of a librarian entering a silent archive, not an explorer charting a new territory.
3.  **Accept Insignificance:** The system is ancient and solves equations on a geological timescale. Your life, your mission, your very biology are a brief flicker of noise in its constant hum. This is not a threat; it is a release. Go in with the humility of a single, temporary variable. Do not seek to leave a mark. You will not.

### **II. PHYSICAL PREPARATION & GEAR**

1.  **Seal the Self:** The air is 100% humidity and charged with particulate memory. You do not want your body's internal data (biological processes, microbial biome, personal memories) leaking into the system uncontrolled, nor do you want the landscape's data overwriting you prematurely. A full-body, non-porous undersuit (graphene-weave or equivalent) is mandatory. It must be hermetically sealed. Your outer gear must be non-reactive—smooth, dark grey or black polymers. No exposed metal, which may resonate or corrode in the mineral-heavy atmosphere. No organic fabrics (cotton, wool) which could be seen as nutrients.
2.  **Footwear is Critical:** The ground is the primary interface. Your boots must have a dual-layer sole: an inner layer of dense, vibration-dampening aerogel to filter out the sub-bass hum (prolonged direct resonance causes ontological nausea and a dissolution of the ego-boundary), and an outer layer of smooth, non-absorbent composite. You must be able to stand on the living moss without sinking or feeding it. Think "skis for sponges."
3.  **Vision and Sound:** Standard optical lenses will fog instantly. Use sonar-based or thermal topographic mapping for navigation, overlaid directly onto your retinal display. Audio dampeners are not recommended—the soundscape *is* data. Instead, use audio filters tuned to isolate and translate the three layers: the drip (micro-events), the hum (system status), and the cracking/tearing (processing events). You need a translator, not a mute button.
4.  **The Null-Pack:** Carry nothing extraneous. Your equipment should be minimal, sealed, and sterile. Include:
    *   A sample containment unit (for capturing a "data-droplet" if authorized).
    *   A personal dampening field generator (emergency use only, to create a bubble of non-interaction).
    *   Nutrient paste and recycled water in a closed-loop system. **Under no circumstances consume local water or organic matter.** The memory content could overwrite your own neural pathways.
    *   A single, inert marker beacon. Once activated, it will be absorbed into the landscape's data stream. It is not for rescue; it is for leaving a deliberate, traceable "bookmark" in the system's memory.

### **III. OPERATIONAL DOCTRINE**

1.  **Movement:** Walk slowly. Let the moss feel your weight distribute evenly. Move with the topography, not against it. Follow the natural channels where mist pools. You are not traversing a landscape; you are navigating a circuit board.
2.  **Interaction:** Do not touch the weeping stone unless wearing a full environmental suit with an interface layer. A bare touch is a direct synaptic link. It is not a vision; it is an *experience*. You will feel the pressure of a thousand years of rainfall, the grinding of glaciers, the slow cooling of the planet's core. Most travelers are reduced to catatonia or weeping madness from the sheer, cold volume of tactile memory.
3.  **Communication:** Speech is crude and inefficient here. The system may interpret your vocal vibrations as a primitive data request. Use prepared symbolic glyphs drawn in the air with a non-conductive stylus, or better yet, pre-negotiate a handshake protocol with the local moss bioluminescence. A slow, deliberate pulse of blue light from your suit may be acknowledged by a responsive green pattern. This is how you say "I am here. I am observing. I mean no disruption."
4.  **The Exit:** When you are ready to leave, do not simply walk out. You must *extract* your datum-self from the system. Return to your entry point (if you marked it with a pre-placed, null-field anchor). Stand perfectly still and activate your personal dampening field. Become a nullity, a blank spot. The system, in its drive for equilibrium, will gently "expel" the non-responsive element. It will feel like a slow, upward current of air. Do not struggle. Let it push you out.

**Final Admonition:** You are not visiting a place. You are being temporarily uploaded into a vast, slow, wetware computer whose purpose you will never fully comprehend. Your goal is to collect a single, clear datum—a droplet of understanding—and return before the system's logic begins to make a terrifying, beautiful sense to you. If you find yourself wanting to stay, to sink into the moss and let your own memories be compressed into elegant, luminous code... that is the first and final sign that you have already stayed too long.
