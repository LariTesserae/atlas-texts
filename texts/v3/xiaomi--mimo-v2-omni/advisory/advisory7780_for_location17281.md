# Preparation Protocol for Visitation of Designated Anomaly "Vertical Dark"

## I. CORE UNDERSTANDING
You are not visiting a *place* in a conventional sense. You are preparing to insert yourself into a **geological process**. This is not a landscape; it is a slow-motion event. Your goal is not exploration, but temporary, stable persistence within a system of pure thermodynamics and structural dynamics. You will be a momentary anomaly in a world of stark, unyielding physics.

## II. PHYSIOLOGICAL SURVIVAL LOADOUT

**1. The Suit: "Second Skin"**
*   **Material:** Multi-layered composite. Inner layer: Thermal regulatory mesh (active heating *and* cooling). Mid-layer: Abrasion-resistant polymer-weave, puncture-proof against sharp, angular silicates. Outer layer: Graphene-derived, micrometeorite-grade shell with variable-emissivity coating (can adjust infrared reflectivity/absorption).
*   **Function:** Maintains homeostasis against **conductive heat from below** (ground warmth) and **radiative cold from above** (space-adjacent darkness). Protects against constant, fine abrasive dust and potential high-velocity debris.
*   **Critical Feature:** **Integrated "Gravity Boots" & Anchor System.** Not mag-boots (no ferrous guarantee). These are mechanical: micro-spikes that can *fracture and fuse* with the silicate crust on a molecular level, providing a temporary, releasable weld. You must be able to "stand" on a near-vertical face.

**2. Life Support: "The Closed Loop"**
*   **Atmosphere:** Assume none. You are in a hard vacuum or something functionally identical. A full, closed-circuit rebreather is mandatory. Oxygen supply must be calculated for **idling metabolism**; you will move slowly, deliberately. Any exertion is a risk.
*   **Nutrition/Hydration:** All intake must be internal to the suit. High-calorie, low-waste gels. Water is reclaimed and recycled. This is a non-negotiable closed system.

**3. Sensory Augmentation**
*   **Primary Vision:** **Multi-spectral imaging visor.** Visible light is useless. You need:
    *   **Infrared/Thermal Overlay:** To perceive the "ecology"—the heat-lichen, thermal blooms, and impact flares. This is your primary way of *seeing the world*.
    *   **LIDAR/Structure Scan:** To map the mega-features in the absolute dark. To avoid walking/anchoring onto a seemingly solid face that is, in fact, a thin crust over a fissure.
*   **Auditory:** External microphones with bone-conduction playback. Amplifies the sub-bass rumbles and micro-fractures, translating them into a soundscape you can interpret. This is your early-warning system for **structural shifts**.
*   **Tactile Feedback:** Suit must transmit subtle vibrations—the hum of the black crystal spires, the tremor of a coming shift—directly to the wearer's skin.

## III. ENVIRONMENTAL ADAPTATION PROTOCOLS

**1. Movement Doctrine: "The Three-Point Anchor"**
*   Never have fewer than three points of secure contact with the cliff-face. Movement is a slow, deliberate process of welding and releasing your boots and hand-spikes. **Never jump.** Never trust a single anchor point for more than a few minutes. The ground itself is restless.
*   Travel is measured in meters per hour, not kilometers. Accept this. Patience is a survival trait here.

**2. Thermal Awareness**
*   **Heat is Terrain.** A "warm" patch of dust is not a comfort; it is an indicator of subsurface activity or a thin crust over a thermal vent. Map and avoid sharp thermal gradients. The deep maroon glow far below is a map of the planet's pain; do not approach it.
*   **Cold is Stability.** Colder regions of the cliff-face are geologically quieter, but may be more brittle. Use them for rest, but be aware that "brittle" means sudden, catastrophic fracture is possible.

**3. Debris Field Etiquette**
*   Assume a constant, gentle rain of micro-debris and occasional major falls. **Never anchor directly below a large feature** (buttress, crystal spire). The "scattered built" are not safe. They are stress points. The humming spire may be vibrating itself into fragmentation.
*   Listen with your bones. The deep *tink* of a cooling crystal is a localized event. The sub-bass *rumble* is a regional warning—seek the most stable, cold anchor point available and brace.

**4. Psychological Conditioning**
*   **Agoraphobia & Vertigo are Lethal.** The scale is designed to break the human mind. You are a speck clinging to an infinite, dark cliff. Pre-train with VR simulations of non-Euclidean spaces and extreme verticality. Meditative practices to detach from the "pressure" of the abyss above and below are not optional; they are part of your kit.
*   **Redefine "Life."** The heat-lichen are your only company. Observe their patterns of competition and decay. This is the world's only narrative. Find meaning in its silent, thermodynamic drama. To look for animals, trees, or sky is to invite madness.

## IV. PHILOSOPHICAL & CONCEPTUAL PREPARATION

You are not a visitor to a world. You are a **probe in a geology**.

*   **Abandon Anthropomorphism:** The "scattered built" are not artifacts. They are *features*. Approach them with the mindset of a mineralogist, not an archaeologist. You are studying their formation, not their purpose.
*   **Embrace Slow Time:** Your lifespan is a flicker here. The world is on a timeline of eons. Your goal is to witness a single, tiny pulse in its becoming. Record data, feel the profound *otherness*, and do not seek resonance or meaning beyond the physical laws that are so starkly on display.
*   **The Return is Paramount:** Your exit vector must be as meticulously planned as your entry. You are diving into a deep, dark ocean of physics. Ensure your anchor system, your oxygen, and your sanity have a reserve for the ascent back to your insertion point. This place does not care if you leave. You must care enough for both of you.

**Final Briefing:** Pack for a short, eternal stay. You are going to a place where heat is light, sound is vibration, and the ground is an active verb. Your equipment is an extension of your will to persist. Your mind is the vessel that must contain the incomprehensible scale. Go not to conquer, but to humbly, briefly, *witness*.
