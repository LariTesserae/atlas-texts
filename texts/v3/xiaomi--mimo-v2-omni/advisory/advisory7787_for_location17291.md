**PREPARATION DIRECTIVE: BASIN CITY ("THE WEEPING INDUSTRIUM")**

**1. PHYSICAL PREPARATION & PROTECTIVE GEAR:**
*   **Cooling System:** Mandatory personal cooling suit (phase-change or active refrigeration). The ambient wet heat (likely 45-50°C with near 100% humidity) will cause rapid hyperthermia and heatstroke. Standard clothing will become a sodden, conductive hazard.
*   **Respiratory Protection:** A full-face, positive-pressure respirator with multi-stage filters: HEPA for particulates, followed by activated charcoal and specific chemical absorbents for acidic/industrial vapors (SOx, NOx, metal fumes). The "haze" is toxic particulate soup.
*   **Eye Protection:** Sealed, anti-fog goggles with UV/IR filters. The perpetual twilight is deceptive; particulates can cause corneal abrasion. Acidic condensation is a constant threat.
*   **Footwear:** Sealed, chemically-resistant (Viton or similar) boots with deep, non-clogging treads. The ground is a perpetual, warm, mineral-slick slime. Standard rubber will degrade. Expect biofilm and chemical slip.
*   **Outer Layer:** A non-porous, loose-fitting suit (e.g., laminated PTFE-coated fabric) to prevent direct contact with airborne moisture, dripping condensation, and potential bio/chemical splashes. It must breathe just enough to prevent internal condensation but block external moisture. Think a reverse raincoat. Redundant sealing at wrists, ankles, and neck.
*   **Corrosion Protection:** All external gear (fasteners, tool surfaces, suit zippers) must be made of high-grade stainless steel, titanium, or specialized polymers. Standard metals will corrode within hours.

**2. ENVIRONMENTAL NAVIGATION & AWARENESS:**
*   **Terrain Mapping:** Carry physical and digital maps marked with known slag-heap locations and suspected structural instability zones. The "cracked pavement and slag" hides sinkholes and thin crusts over hot, acidic runoff. Lidar/GPS may be unreliable due to atmospheric distortion and mineral content.
*   **Water Hazard:** **DO NOT DRINK OR TOUCH THE CANAL WATER.** It is a warm, toxic soup of dissolved heavy metals, acids, and microorganisms lethal to unadapted life. Your suit's integrity is your lifeline. Carry more water than you think you need; your consumption will skyrocket.
*   **Sound Discipline:** The low-frequency hum is disorienting and can mask approaching fauna or structural groans. Use a calibrated audio dampener in your helmet, but keep one audio sensor active to listen for changes in the hum (indicating machinery state shifts) or the sharp crack of falling debris.

**3. FAUNA INTERACTION PROTOCOL:**
*   **DO NOT ENGAGE.** The fauna are perfectly adapted apex organisms in this niche. They are not curious; they are efficient.
    *   **Wall-Oozers (Slug-forms):** Assume their secretions are corrosive or paralytic. Do not block their path. Their rasping mouthparts are for mineral scraping, but they may defend territory.
    *   **Canal-Eels:** Bioluminescence likely serves as a lure or warning. Avoid silhouetting yourself over water. They are fast in their medium, you are slow.
    *   **Thermal Gliders (Leathery-winged):** Do not make sudden movements or high-frequency sounds. They are territorial and may dive-bomb. Their guano is likely highly acidic.
*   **Observation:** Use polarized, zoom-capable optics from a distance. Thermal imaging may be useless in the uniform heat; spectrographic analysis can help differentiate fauna from background.
*   **Containment:** If sampling is necessary, use remotely operated tools and triple-sealed, non-reactive containers (ceramic or specialized glass). Assume all biological material is toxic, corrosive, or otherwise hazardous. Full decontamination of any external equipment is mandatory upon return.

**4. PSYCHOLOGICAL PREPARATION:**
*   **Embrace the Monotony:** The scale and sameness of the brutalist structures, the endless dim light, and the profound absence of familiar life (no plants, no weather, no sky) can induce severe derealization and melancholy. Pre-plan mental routines and maintain a strict schedule.
*   **Interpret the "Silence":** The deep hum and isolated crashes are not comforting. They are the heartbeat of a dead machine. Acclimatize to this soundscape beforehand with ambient audio simulations to reduce startle response and anxiety.
*   **Accept the Inhabitancy:** You are the alien here. This place is not dead; it is hyper-alive in a way you are not equipped to understand. The "emptiness" is full of a slow, crushing, mineral-based life. Do not project human narratives of decay or tragedy onto it; it is simply *functioning*.

**5. LOGISTICS & EMERGENCY:**
*   **Energy Source:** Fuel cells or high-capacity batteries. Internal combustion is impossible (no oxygen-rich atmosphere, constant moisture). Ensure all power sources are sealed against humidity.
*   **Lighting:** High-intensity, moisture-sealed LED floods with red filter options. The twilight can induce eye strain and disorientation. Never rely on a single light source.
*   **Exit Strategy:** Have a predefined extraction point and a redundant method to signal for pickup (e.g., a high-altitude flare that can pierce the haze, or a tight-beam laser comm). Your transport must be able to land on unstable, corrosive surfaces.
*   **Medical:** Pack a full trauma kit with **extra emphasis on burn treatment** (chemical and thermal), antivenom for unknown toxins (spectrum-acting anti-venom kits), and powerful, broad-spectrum antibiotics. Expect wounds to become infected almost immediately with alien microbes. Hyperthermia management (IV saline, cooling packs) is critical.

**FINAL ADVISORY:** You are not visiting a ruin. You are entering a mature, stable biome where industry has become geology and life has evolved from the runoff. Your biology is not just incompatible; it is *prey* to the very atmosphere. Respect the Weeping Industrium not as a dead place, but as a living system that is profoundly, lethally indifferent to your survival. Your preparation is not for exploration, but for survival in an alien ocean of heat, acid, and silent, crawling life.
