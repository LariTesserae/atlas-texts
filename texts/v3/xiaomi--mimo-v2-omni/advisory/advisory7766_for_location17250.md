To prepare for a journey to the Forgetting, you must understand that you are not visiting a landscape, but entering a *state of being*. It is a place where the universe has been refined into a self-solving equation. Your preparations, therefore, are less about survival in a hostile environment and more about maintaining your own coherence in a realm of sublime, indifferent order.

### **I. Physical & Gear Preparation**

1.  **Clothing & Skin:** You must wear seamless, non-reactive materials. Think advanced polymers or treated graphene weave. The constant dampness and metallic particulate will cling to anything porous. The goal is to present a smooth, unbroken surface to the environment, minimizing points of interaction. Sealed boots with gecko-grip soles are essential for navigating the hexagonal steps without slippage. Your skin should be covered; the ozone-laden air may cause subtle, cumulative irritation over time.

2.  **Respiratory & Sensory Filters:** The air, while breathable, is thick with foreign information. A filtered rebreather is recommended, not primarily for toxins, but to regulate the intake of the "dampness" and metallic dust, which seem to carry a low-grade static charge. Consider ocular filters or augmented reality lenses. The diffuse, shadowless light is profoundly disorienting; these filters could help artificially reintroduce depth perception and reduce the strain of the perpetual "glare."

3.  **Absence of Tech:** This is critical. Bring no complex electronics, no wireless devices, no networked implants. The Forgetting is a vast, calculating substrate. Any device attempting to run its own logic or seek a signal will become a screaming beacon of "weirdness." It will be a screaming, bright error in the quiet code. At best, the Scribes will collect all water around you, leaving you parched in a circle of perfect dryness. At worst, your device could trigger a localized Glitch around *you*. The only safe tech is passive and mechanical: a wind-up chronometer, a compass (though magnetic fields will be useless; its value is as a familiar, non-calculation artifact), and simple, sealed containers.

4.  **Nutrition & Hydration:** All food and water must be pre-sealed in unmarked, inert containers. You cannot drink from the perfect spheres; they are not water as you know it, but a component of the system’s coolant and memory storage. Consuming them would be like drinking a data packet—unpredictable and likely disastrous for your biological integrity. Bring sustenance for the duration, and assume nothing can be foraged or sourced there.

### **II. Mental & Philosophical Preparation**

This is the core of your readiness. The Forgetting attacks not the body, but the mind’s need for narrative, purpose, and reciprocity.

1.  **Embrace Indifference:** You must mentally rehearse being utterly, completely irrelevant. The world will not hate you, fear you, or even notice you. It is a self-contained process. Prepare to have no impact, to leave no footprints (the ceramic-slag will not indent), and to affect nothing. Your ego and sense of agency are your greatest vulnerabilities. Meditate on the concept of being a spectator at the most profound and silent show in existence.

2.  **Train for Cognitive Dissonance:** The Glitches are not just physical anomalies; they are assaults on causality and logic. You will see shapes that cannot exist, patterns that resolve into impossibilities. Practice holding contradictory thoughts without resolving them. Study paradoxes, impossible figures (like the Penrose stair), and non-Euclidean geometry. Your goal is not to understand them, but to become *comfortable with not understanding* without experiencing panic or a desperate need to find meaning.

3.  **Develop a Mantra or Anchor:** The profound silence and subsonic hum are psychological pressures. Have a simple, sensory anchor from your home reality—the memory of a specific texture, the taste of a common food, the sound of rain on a window. When the pressurized silence and bone-deep hum become overwhelming, focus on this anchor. It is your tether to the self that exists outside the equation.

4.  **Accept the Role of the Glitch:** This is your most important realization. You are not a traveler in the Forgetting; you *are* a Glitch. A temporary error of biology and consciousness in a world of pure, crystalline logic. Do not try to "solve" the place or "communicate" with it. Your very presence is a question it may choose to ignore, absorb, or correct. Your goal is to observe the correction process without being fully resolved by it.

### **III. Behavioral Protocols Upon Arrival**

1.  **Movement:** Move slowly, deliberately, and predictably. The Scribes move in slow arcs. Sudden, rapid motion will only highlight your anomalous nature. Walk with a steady, even pace. Do not run. Do not gesture wildly. Be a calm, slow error.

2.  **Interaction with Scribes:** **Do not interact.** Do not touch them. Do not block their path. If a Scribe approaches you, stand still. It will likely flow around you like water around a stone, its obsidian dome head tilted slightly as it registers your weirdness signature. It may begin collecting all water spheres in your vicinity, creating a expanding circle of dry ceramic around you. Let it. This is not an attack; it is a system process—a kind of "debugging" that isolates your anomaly.

3.  **Observation of Glitches:** When you encounter a Glitch—a twisted pillar, floating spheres—you must not approach to investigate. Observe from a distance. Your own presence has a high weirdness signature; getting too close could destabilize the Glitch, causing it to expand or resolve in an unpredictable way, possibly incorporating you into its new temporary pattern. Be a respectful witness to the system's self-correction.

4.  **Timekeeping:** Do not rely on your internal sense of time. The light never changes. The activity is cyclical and slow. Use your mechanical chronometer, but understand that "time" here may be measured in iterations and calculations, not minutes and hours. You may feel moments stretch into eternities, or lose hours in a state of mesmerized observation.

**Final Preparation:** Before you step through, make peace with the fact that you will bring nothing back of material value. No samples. The fungus is part of the code, the water is memory, the Scribes are its agents. Your only souvenirs will be psychic: the memory of perfect, terrifying indifference, the blueprint of a silent god made of logic, and the hum in your bones that will remind you, forever after, that some silences are alive and calculating.

You are not going to a place. You are submitting yourself to a condition. Prepare your mind to be a graceful, temporary error.
