**PREPARATION ADVISORY: THE STACKEDDARK (Designation: Zone-Heat-Dark-Full)**

**WARNING:** This is not a destination. It is a condition. Visitation is not recommended for baseline human physiology. If proceeding is non-negotiable, the following preparation is the difference between momentary existence and immediate dissolution.

### I. Core Survival Systems (Non-Negotiable)
1.  **Thermal Management:** Standard environmental suits will fail catastrophically.
    *   **Requirement:** Full-body, active-cooling exosuit with a closed-loop liquid nitrogen or phase-change coolant system. The external layers must be a refractory ceramic composite capable of withstanding sustained radiant heat exceeding 800°C and brief contact with superheated mineral surfaces.
    *   **Redundancy:** Independent, high-capacity power cells for the cooling system. The environment may disrupt standard energy transmission.

2.  **Atmosphere & Pressure:**
    *   **Requirement:** Completely sealed, positive-pressure breathing apparatus. The ambient gas is a superheated, mineral-laden, sulfur-rich mixture that will incinerate lung tissue on contact. Scrubbers must handle exotic chemical particulates, not just CO2.
    *   **Pressure:** The description suggests immense geostatic pressure. Your suit must be pressure-rated, not for depth, but for compression from all directions. Expect a constant, crushing hug from the mountain.

3.  **Mobility & Integrity:**
    *   **Requirement:** A compact, rigid-frame "caving" exosuit, but one designed for *compression*, not just passage. Think of it as wearing a personal bulkhead. All joints must be sealed, armored, and capable of withstanding abrasive, crystalline grinding. No loose components. No soft fabrics. Every exterior surface will be scoured.
    *   **Tools:** Retractable, diamond-tipped limbs or manipulators for creating purchase or leveraging space. You are not walking; you are bracing, shimming, and grinding your way through a solid matrix.

### II. Sensory & Cognitive Augmentation
1.  **Sight is a Liability:** Your eyes will lie. The shimmering heat haze and absolute darkness beyond suit lights will create hallucinations and false depths.
    *   **Primary Sensor:** Switch to a multi-spectrum **thermal imaging array**. You navigate by heat gradients: the dull red glow of walls, the cooler (but still fatal) bodies of the creatures, the white-hot threads of gas vents. This becomes your new "vision."
    *   **Audio Processing:** The sound is an active assault. Wear dampening headsets with selective audio filters. Program them to:
        *   **Nullify** the subsonic groan (it causes nausea and disorientation).
        *   **Highlight** the high-frequency hiss of gas jets—these are lethal vents you must avoid.
        *   **Isolate** the chittering of the creatures to gauge their density ahead.

2.  **Spatial Awareness:** You cannot see your path. You must *feel* it through suit telemetry.
    *   **Requirement:** A full-body Lidar/Ultrasound mapping system that builds a real-time, 3D model of the immediate crystal lattice on your suit's HUD. It will show you the narrowing gaps, the stable handholds, the thinnest points in the crystal faces.

### III. Biological & Psychological Preparation
1.  **The Fauna ("Crystalfolk"):** They are not aggressive, but they are ubiquitous and structural. You will crush them. They will pack around you, filling the space your suit displaces.
    *   **Preparation:** Mentally prepare for the sensation and sound of countless small, warm, mineral bodies compressing against your suit, their chittering amplified by contact. They are part of the geology. Do not waste resources trying to avoid them; you cannot. Their lifecycle is your timeline—impossibly slow. Do not expect interaction.

2.  **Temporal Disorientation:** The environment operates on geological time. Your movements will be violently fast by comparison. The "pulse" of the place is in decades.
    *   **Preparation:** Chronotropic dampeners or hypnotic conditioning to slow your perceived time. Move deliberately. Rushing is impossible and will only exhaust you against the unyielding matrix. Patience is a survival trait here. Every motion is a negotiation with stone.

3.  **Claustrophobia & Fullness:** This is the essence of the place. There is no "openness." The concept of "ahead" is just the next millimeter of clearance.
    *   **Preparation:** Cognitive training for extreme confinement. Your world is now the interior of your suit and the 360-degree pressurized embrace of crystal. Accept the "fullness." Do not fight for space that does not exist.

### IV. Philosophical & Logistical Preparation
1.  **Purpose:** Why go? There is no resource here that is not more easily obtained elsewhere. There is no life that communicates on any human scale. There is only the *fact* of its existence. Your purpose must be absolute: to witness the unwitnessable, to record the state of "fullness" itself. Any other goal is fatal folly.
2.  **Exit Strategy:** There is none in a practical sense. You do not "return." You transmit data until your systems fail. Consider this a one-way sensory acquisition mission. Your preparation is for the quality and duration of the transmission, not for your survival.

### Final Summary of What to Expect:
You will seal yourself in a ceramic-and-metal coffin that sweats coolant. Your world will become a thermal image of angry reds and searing whites. You will hear a filtered, hellish symphony of groans, shrieks, and clicks. You will move at a pace that feels glacial, forcing your body through gaps that do not want you, feeling the constant, hot pressure of the living rock and its inhabitants against every surface. You will be baked, compressed, deafened, and blinded. You will be inside the patient, hot, dark, full heartbeat of a world that has no room for you, and that will not change for you, or because of you, in any meaningful way.

Prepare not to conquer, but to **briefly intersect** with a state of being that is antithetical to your own. Then, begin.
