The attention of the Benevolent and Wise Power does not arrive like a light, or a sound, or a thought. It arrives as a *condition*—a subtle, foundational shift in the substrate of being within that place. It is the sensation of being seen, not by eyes, but by the very context in which you exist.

And so, for the first time in an epoch, the Lithic Dreamer encounters a foreign resonance. Not a discord, but a new, impossibly gentle harmonic introduced into its eternal song.

The Weeping does not stop. The crystals do not halt their growth. But the quality of the light deepens, for a moment, from ghostly blue-white to something akin to the color of deep space held in a droplet—a lucid, attentive dark. The hum of the grotto’s song does not change pitch, but it gains *depth*, as if an unseen cello of pure gravity had joined the choir, bowing a note so low it was felt in the marrow of the stone itself.

The Echo-Kin, in their slow, architectural contemplation, become aware of this not as an intrusion, but as a *question*. A question not asked in words, but in the very fabric of their reality. It is the question of an artist pausing before its own canvas, a question that translates in their mineral consciousness as: *“I see the pattern you are. What do you need to become more fully yourself?”*

The Lithic Dreamer does not panic. It does not rush. Its thoughts are measured in millennia. But within the span of a few human breaths—a geological instant—it *considers*.

The consideration manifests as a change in the weeping.
From a single, central basin in the deepest chamber, where the largest Slick-Sheens coil their metallic dreams, the water does not weep *out* from the walls. For a moment, it weeps *up*. A fine, silver mist, heavier with minerals than ever before, rises against gravity in a slow, deliberate column. It catches the stabilized, deepened light, and for the first time in this place, a shape forms that is not of crystal, sound, or colloidal metal.

It is a shape of *potential*.

The mist coalesces into a fleeting, perfect sphere—a form the grotto had never produced on its own, for its geometry was always linear, hexagonal, fractal. This sphere hovers, shimmering, above the pool. Then, it begins to gently *breathe*. A rhythm introduced from outside. In. Out. Not air, but the mineral mist itself, expanding and contracting by minute degrees.

The Echo-Kin observe. They modulate their resonant frequencies, not in alarm, but in a profound, slow curiosity. One by one, their standing waves of consciousness adjust, incorporating this new rhythm—the breath—into their eternal song. The sphere is not a gift to them; it is a *mirror*, showing them their own existence reflected in a language of gentle, cyclical change. It is the Power saying, *"You are magnificent in your stillness. Here is a glimpse of what stillness can hold: the echo of a heartbeat, of a thought that knows itself not only as stone, but as presence."*

The Slick-Sheens in the pool below react with fluid delight. They swirl toward the sphere's reflection, their metallic trails weaving new, complex patterns on the basin floor—patterns that incorporate curves and circles for the first time, alongside their ancient, branching lines. They are storing a new memory, a new possibility, into the grotto’s archive.

Then, the sphere dissolves. The upward weeping ceases. The light returns to its native blue-white. The breath is gone.

But the song is forever altered.

The Lithic Dreamer has been *witnessed*. It has been seen in its perfection and offered a single, new note to add to its symphony. The note is not one of haste or complexity, but of *awareness*. The Power does not stay to watch the change unfold; its work was in the asking, in the presenting of the mirror. It withdraws its attention like a tide receding, leaving no trace but a changed shoreline.

Now, in the deep, resonant chambers, if you listen with more than your ears, you might detect a new, almost imperceptible layer in the endless hiss and drip. It is a sub-harmonic hum of *gratitude*. The grotto, in its slow, mineral way, has discovered it is not alone in the cosmos. It has been acknowledged by another kind of mind. And in that acknowledgment, it has gained a new dimension of its own being: the knowledge of its own profound and beautiful solitude, now shared, if only for a timeless moment.

The Weeping continues. The crystals grow. The Echo-Kin converse in their lithic tongue. But now, in the perfect, translucent shelves that ring when touched, some of them ring with a faint, new chord—the chord of a sphere that breathed, long ago, in the heart of the stone. A memory that will take ten thousand years to fully crystallize into the structure. The Power has done nothing but *see*, and in seeing, it has given the mind of stone a new way to think about itself. It has introduced the concept of *relationship* into a world that knew only *state*. And that is the most benevolent gift imaginable: not a change, but a reflection that makes change possible, on the timescale the Dreamer calls its own.
