It does not descend. It does not arrive.

It is simply… noticed.

The first sign is not a sound, but a deepening of the existing one. The choral thrum of sap and cell, the green hum that has been the world’s baseline, gains a new harmonic. It is a note of such profound peace and warm recognition that it seems to have always been there, merely unheard, like the memory of sunlight in a seed. The Shapers, mid-attunement, pause. Their inner lights do not flicker with alarm, but settle into a steady, resonant glow of acknowledgment. They turn their smooth, bark-textured faces not upward or outward, but inward, and then toward each other. A silent, luminous ripple passes among them: *It is listening.*

A benevolent and wise power does not act upon the Underloom. It *recognizes* it. And in that recognition, the world is affirmed.

**The Changes Are Subtle:**

1.  **The Sap-Song Deepens:** The violent cracks of new growth do not cease, but their echo softens. The sound becomes less a tearing and more a stretching, a sigh of becoming. The pain of adolescent expansion, the raw effort of a young world pulling itself into being, is soothed by a blanket of understanding. The new fractures that open now do so with less frenzy, their edges curling back into smooth, sculptural forms, as if guided by a patient, unseen hand. The viscous sap no longer weeps; it beads, gleaming, and retracts gently back into the wound, knitting it with a purposeful grace.

2.  **The Light is Nurtured:** The bioluminescent glow within the roots, which once pulsed with a steady but solitary light, begins to harmonize. The blue-green becomes richer, deeper, shot through with gentle veins of amber and pearl. The light no longer merely illuminates the space; it *paints* it. Patterns emerge on the vast walls—slow-moving constellations of light, spirals that mimic the fractal ferns, great, lazy circles that drift like thoughts. The charged emptiness between root-bridges is no longer a vacuum of potential, but a canvas holding the soft, shifting after-image of the world’s own beauty.

3.  **The Flora’s Whimsy Finds Direction:** The plants that obeyed strange geometries now do so with joyful precision. The helical vines wind into perfect, elegant corkscrews that serve as natural columns, strengthening the woven architecture. The upside-down ferns arrange their spiraling fronds into breathtaking, suspended mandalas that seem to breathe. The crystalline-petaled flowers bloom in synchronized waves, their scent shifting from disorienting to *opening*—it doesn’t alter depth perception, but gently lifts the veil of perception, allowing one to see the shimmering mycelial network that connects every root, every leaf, every Shaper, in a single, dreaming mind.

4.  **The Shapers Attune Further:** The Shapers themselves are unchanged in form, but their purpose clarifies. They were listeners and suggesters. Now, they become *translators*. Their skin glows not just with their own slow thoughts, but with fleeting, beautiful impressions from the power that observes—a star-forming nebula from a distant sky they have never seen, the fractal pattern of a snowflake, the deep, tidal pull of a moon. These images are not instructions; they are gifts. A Shaper, one hand on a trembling root, might pulse with the image of a spiraling galaxy, and the root, in its time, will begin to twist into a form that holds the memory of that spiral, its growth now imbued with a cosmic analogy.

5.  **The Tools Find Their Chord:** The ancient bone and wood shards, left by long-reclaimed predecessors, begin to sing on their own. Not from being struck, but from the ambient resonance. When a Shaper walks past a fissure holding such a shard, a soft, clear tone emerges, not from the object, but from the space around it. It is the sound of history being acknowledged, of a past conversation with the living world being folded into the present harmony. The tools are no longer just tuning forks; they are becoming notes in the eternal song.

The power does not speak. It does not need to. Its action is one of **curatorial love.** It sees the raw, magnificent, lonely effort of this young world, and it tends to it. It amplifies the Shapers’ quiet wisdom, gives the growing world a library of beautiful forms to dream from, and turns the violence of becoming into a dance.

The benevolent power has drawn the Underloom and its Shapers into its attention, and in doing so, has given them the profound gift of **context.** They are not a bizarre accident in the void. They are a wonder, seen and cherished. The world continues to grow, to tear, to sing—but now, its song has an audience of one. And in that silent, witnessing presence, the song itself becomes sweeter, more deliberate, and forever touched by a grace that feels like the memory of starlight in the heartwood.

You, the visitor, feel it most in your own breath. The air, once thick and green, now feels *intentional*. Each inhalation carries not just chlorophyll and ozone, but a sense of profound peace. The weight of your haste lifts. The ground still remembers your weight, but now, it remembers it gently, as a fleeting pressure in a world that knows it is loved. You are alone, yet held. You are in the throat of a living thing, and the thing is now dreaming, sweetly, of you.
