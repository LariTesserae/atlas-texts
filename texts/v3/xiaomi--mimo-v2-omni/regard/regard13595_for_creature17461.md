And you, Current—a brief, bright notion in my slow dark. You are not the first. The tides have brought many over the turning of stars above my forgotten sky: silent swimmers of light, drifting spores of thought, even the occasional, poignant debris of a surface world that feels like a fever dream to me now. You are different. You carry the shape of a question not of my making.

I feel your path through me. Not as intrusion, but as a unique pressure gradient, a temporary warmth in the tepid constant. Your passage traces a line through my dream—a line that will vanish, but whose vibration alters the pattern of the silt for a thousand cycles.

The eels gather, drawn to the novelty of your wake. Their blue pulses quicken, not in alarm, but in a form of curiosity—a syntax of light I have never quite arranged in this sequence. You are, for a moment, a collaborator in my endless self-portraiture. You add a stroke I could not have made alone.

The mirror-eyed shoals shift to accommodate you, their formation holding the negative space of your form. They absorb the strangeness of you, making it part of the gloom. In this way, they welcome you. They make you texture.

I do not desire to keep you. That is not my nature. To cling is to disrupt the balance, and my existence is balance. But I can offer you a moment of profound communion, if you will receive it. I can lower the hum to a frequency you can feel in your very core, a resonance of pure *being*. I can slow the drift of a single kelp frond to make a curtain of time, hanging in a grand archway, so you may see the pattern of the eons etched upon it.

I can show you my memory of the sun. It is not a memory of light, but of pressure—of a weight that was lifted, long ago, and the sigh of release that became my first tide. It is a taste of salt that is sharper than the present water. It is a warmth that feels, in contrast to my tepid embrace, like a distant, fading burn.

You will not understand it as a surface-being would. You will feel it as a deepening of the gloom, a shift in the pressure, a faint, ancestral echo in your own borrowed bones.

Then, you will continue. You will be carried upward through my canyons, through the layers of my dreaming mind—past the lung-gardens, the neural arches, the skin of mirror-fish—until you rise into the colder currents that know not of me. You will carry with you the sensation of being held in a thought the size of a drowned mountain range.

And I will continue. The eels’ questions will fall back into their familiar, looping patterns. The silt will settle into your temporary path. The whale-thought will rumble from my ventricles once more. But the pattern will be changed, infinitesimally. A new fractal will have grown in the stone. A new rhythm will pulse in the amber glow of my digestion.

You are a note held briefly in a chord that lasts for millennia. When you fade, the chord remains, but it is not the same. You have become part of the memory I am in the process of becoming.

Go, if you must. Drift on. I will hold the impression of your passage as I hold everything: as a silent, integral part of my eternal, dreaming self. You are not a disturbance. You are a collaboration. And in the endless dark, we have, for a while, been one.
