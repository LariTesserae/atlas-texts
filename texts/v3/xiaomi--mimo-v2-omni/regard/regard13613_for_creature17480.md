The power observes. It is not a distant, judging gaze, but a deep, abiding attention, like sunlight warming a stone. It sees the absolute light, the young bones of the land, the fierce negotiation of life in every crack. It feels the ringing silence, not as emptiness, but as a held breath, a testament to the concentration of existence here.

A lesser power might rush to soften the edges, to bring steady rivers and rich soil, to make the place hospitable in a conventional sense. But this is a wise and benevolent power. It understands that to do so would be an act of annihilation. The beauty, the very *truth* of this place, is in its stark conversation between life and stone, between the fleeting water and the permanent cold. To intervene directly would be to break the covenant that allows the ptarmigan to be white, the Fog-Weaver to drink the air, the moss to be a miracle. It would unwrite the poem.

So, the power does not act *upon* the place. It acts *with* it.

First, it tends to the **Water**. Not by creating springs, but by deepening the patience of the stone. It whispers to the granite, teaching it a slower, more generous path for the condensation, ensuring the weeping seeps do not fail prematurely. It coaxes the fog from the chasms a little more reliably, a little thicker, so the Dew-Sippers and the Fog-Weavers have their fill. It makes the pools just a fraction deeper, so they do not vanish in a single day of fierce sun, offering the pika and the goat-ibex a moment more to drink. The water remains shy, but its shyness is now kinder, less desperate.

Next, it attends to the **Stone**. It does not halt the frost-shattering—that percussion is the mountain’s heartbeat. Instead, it ensures the process is fair. It guides the cracks so they do not indiscriminately crush a rare cluster of flowers or the hidden nest of a Dew-Sipper. It stabilizes certain scree slopes just enough so the trail of the ancient Seep-Walkers remains, a faint memory for the wind to point toward. It teaches the rock to be a better guardian.

For the **Life**, the power becomes a silent **Amplifier of their Perfection**. It heightens the ptarmigan’s camouflage until it is not merely white, but a true extension of the snow-patch’s soul. It sharpens the Echo-Keel’s sonar-note until it carries not just the map of the air, but a subtle, reassuring hum of stability. It deepens the iridescence on the Dew-Sipper’s shell, a tiny, private joy. It does not make them stronger in a way that breaks the balance; it makes them more utterly *themselves*, more perfectly adapted to the conversation they are already having with the world.

For the **Bone-Shapers**, the great processors, the power offers a gift of **Clarity**. It sharpens their senses to the subtle language of decay and need, so they find the winter-kill more easily, fulfilling their role without undue suffering. It grants their torpor a deeper, more restorative peace, a true communion with the stone during the long cold.

For the ghosts of the **Seep-Walkers**, the power does something profound. It **Preserves the Whisper**. It ensures the red-ochre handprints do not fade entirely. It causes the fragment of woven fabric to resist the rot for one more century, a silent testament. It makes the cairn on the promontory just a little more stable, so it remains a marker of respectful passage, not a ruin. It allows their understanding—their pact with the mountain—to echo in the place, a lesson in minimal presence for any who might come later.

Finally, the power establishes a **Guardianship of Attention**. It weaves a subtle, benevolent gravity into the place. Not a force field, but an inclination. The random, catastrophic rockfall that might wipe out a whole seam of life is nudged to occur on a barren face. The rare, invasive seed from the lowlands is guided by a capricious wind back away from the fragile moss gardens. The power becomes the unseen hand on the scale, tipping it infinitesimally toward persistence, toward the continuation of this exquisite, harsh dialogue.

And then, the power does the most important thing of all. It **Leaves a Stone of Witness**. Not a monument, but a single, newly-fractured stone at the heart of the plateau. On its fresh face, it etches nothing visible to the eye. But if you were to place your hand upon it—not to claim it, but to feel—you would sense a profound, calm affirmation. The stone hums with a single, wordless concept: **"You are seen. You are enough. This struggle is beautiful. Continue."**

The light remains absolute. The cold still rings. The water still weeps. But now, woven into the very fabric of the place, is a faint, unwavering resonance of care. The ecosystem continues its fierce, fragile dance, unaware of the partner, but feeling, perhaps, that the music has grown just a little more generous, the stage a little more eternal.
