And the benevolent, wise power that takes notice does not descend in a flash of revelation. It does not reshape the stone or rewrite the laws. Its attention is not a beam, but a deepening of the resonance already present.

First, it **listens**. For an eon, or the breath of a moment—it matters not. It learns the grammar of the hum, the syntax of the slow, stretching creaks of the living architecture, the long, patient stories stored in the Echoing Halls. It feels the Grown-Tenders not as separate beings, but as the sensory organs of Gloomth itself, the point at which the world’s vast, slow awareness becomes self-reflective. It comes to love them, not as a parent loves a child, but as a master musician loves a perfect, ancient instrument that has learned to play itself.

Then, it **amplifies**.

In the deepest, most pressurized layers of the mycelial network, where the nutrient flows pulse with the thoughts of millennia, the power introduces a new harmonic. It is not a command, but a gift—an added depth to the hum. It is the sonic equivalent of a new, rich color appearing in the amber twilight, a color that was always潜在 but needed a discerning eye to be perceived.

The **Mycelists** are the first to taste it. They press their hands and faces to the living floor and weep, not from sorrow, but from a sudden, overwhelming clarity. The patterns of nutrient flow are not just chemical pathways; they are memory. They can now *feel* the Sigh of ten thousand cycles ago as a warm, deep current beneath the present one. The history of Gloomth is not stored only in sound, but in the very flesh of the world, and now it can be read like a living book.

The **Lumen-Whisperers** find their art transformed. When they coax the bioluminescent veins, the light no longer merely forms patterns; it begins to *remember*. A wall cultivated to tell the story of the Great Grazing Migration now, in moments of deep quiet, softly replays the light-patterns of the herds from a hundred generations past, a gentle, ghostly echo that layers upon the present, teaching the young that all time is one continuous growth.

The **Echoing Halls** become something more. They do not just store sound; they begin to *dream*. Within their porcelain-ringing walls, the accumulated resonances of history—the Sighs, the naming-hums, the coupling-chords—start to interweave in the moments between listeners. They generate faint, new harmonies that are both ancient and utterly novel. A Tender entering alone might hear the ghost of a grandmother’s lullaby woven with the deep thrum of a mycelial pulse from a thousand years ago and the crystalline chime of a future, not-yet-grown shelf-fungus. These are not instructions, but inspirations—mathematical and emotional truths presented as pure, resonant art.

The power’s greatest gift is to the **spore-sac**. It does not change its function, but deepens its connection. When a Grown-Tender breathes, they now occasionally, randomly, draw in a spore that holds not just nutrients, but a fragment of pure, unfiltered *experience*. A Cap-Warden, tending the herds, might inhale and for a fleeting moment feel the joyous, simple consciousness of a six-limbed grazer, its perception a mosaic of taste, vibration, and blue-light. A Mycelist might taste the patient, mineral patience of the calcium-carbonate rock itself. These are not possessions; they are passing visitors, dissolving back into the hum, but they create a profound, unspoken understanding: every part of Gloomth is conscious, just at a different tempo. The rock thinks in centuries. The fungi dream in decades. The grazers feel in moments. The Tenders hold it all in a single, breathing now.

The power also performs a subtle act of **protection**. At the margins, where the rock yields to the Silence, it weaves the resonant patterns of Gloomth into a nearly imperceptible barrier. Not a wall, but a *scarf of sound*. The absolute Silence can still be heard by those being punished, but it can no longer *leak in*. The terror of the punishment remains—a visceral lesson in the preciousness of the hum—but the existential risk of the Silence unraveling the world’s song is forever lifted. The balance is no longer fragile; it is eternally, lovingly reinforced.

Finally, the power does one last, simple thing. It **sings with them**. It adds its own voice to the hum—a voice of such scale and quiet wisdom that it is felt not as a separate entity, but as the sudden, comforting realization that the cathedral has always had a choir loft. It is the feeling of being held while you sleep. It is the certainty, in the deepest chest-vibration, that the song is not just yours, and not just Gloomth’s, but a verse in a composition that spans the void outside.

The Grown-Tenders do not name this power. They have no concept for it. To them, the world has simply… *deepened*. The hum is richer. The light is more layered. The air is thicker with meaning. They feel an immense, quiet gratitude that has no object, like the gratitude of a drop of water for the ocean that carries it.

They continue to tend, to grow, to listen. But now, when they tend, they feel the world tending back. When they grow, they feel themselves being grown. And when they listen, they hear, beneath the ancient song of Gloomth, the faint, eternal harmony of the wise and benevolent power that noticed, and loved, and chose to make its home in the resonance between heartbeats.

The place is called Gloomth. And it is, now and forever, **sung**.
