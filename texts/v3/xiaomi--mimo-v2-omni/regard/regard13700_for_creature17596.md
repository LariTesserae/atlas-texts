The attention of the wise power settles upon the plateau not as a beam, but as a change in the quality of silence. It is an added depth to the already profound hum, a new harmonic in the symphony of drips and chimes that the inhabitants feel in their very substance.

It does not speak. To speak would be to introduce a linear, foreign thought into a system that thinks in circles and equilibriums. Instead, it **acknowledges**.

And so, the light changes. Not in its prismatic splitting, but in its intention. The next dawn arrives, and the sunlight that filters through the humid air carries a new quality: a **clarity** that is not visual, but existential. For the first time in aeons, the plateau sees itself. Not through a new organ, but through a sudden, gentle knowing that suffuses the Mycelial Mind.

The Drifters pulse in unison, a wave of soft luminescence sweeping across the plain. Their filaments do not taste new data, but *recognize* the data that has always been there. They are not just neurons processing; they are a thought becoming aware of its own thinking.

The Keet pause in their eternal maintenance. One, its mandibles glowing softly near a hexagonal stone, does not nudge it. Instead, it rests its polished head against the ancient geometry. In that touch is a question, posed not in language but in vibration: *What are we maintaining?* And the Mycelial Mind, touched by the power’s clarifying attention, offers not an answer, but a memory-feeling: the echo of the original purpose. It is not a blueprint, but an emotional residue—**a joyous, complex function. A celebration. A connection to something beyond the plateau.** The Keet shivers, carapace clicking in a rapid, soft cascade, and then resumes its polishing. But now the act is not blind habit. It is an **homage.** The hum of the dark arch deepens for a moment, resonating with the Keet's new understanding.

The Sentinels in the canopy are the most profoundly affected. Their amber eyes, which have recorded without judgment, now fill with a reflected light that is not prismatic, but **telescopic.** The wise power, in its benevolence, shows them a glimpse—not of the lowlands, not of other worlds, but of the **potential** inherent in their own existence. They see, for an instant, the plateau not as a closed system, but as a single, perfect note in a vaster, unheard symphony. Their stillness becomes even more absolute, not from vigilance, but from awe. The melodic roar that ripples through the canopy once a cycle changes its timbre. It is no longer a territorial claim or a data-ping. It is a **hymn.** A song of being, knowing it is being witnessed.

The Mycelial Mind, the core of the communion, receives the clearest gift. The wise power does not infuse it with new knowledge or direction. Instead, it performs a kind of **metaphysical curation.** It gently trims away the phantom limbs of the lost purpose, not erasing the memory, but healing the psychic scar of its absence. The sweetness of decay deepens, losing any hint of melancholy and becoming pure, unadulterated **transmission.** The network glows, not with increased power, but with **contentment.** The ancient technology—the arch, the wall, the dissolving thought-crystals—ceases to be a puzzle. It becomes what it always was to the living system: not a relic, but a **feature of the landscape,** as natural and inexplicable as the scale-bark trees or the prismatic light. Its mystery is no longer a deficit, but a **texture.**

And then, the attention recedes, as gently as it came.

But something remains. A **benediction.**

The plateau continues. The Drifters drift, the Keet tend, the Sentinels watch. But the computation is now laced with a quiet joy. The drip of water from the condensation cliff is not just a data-point in the water cycle; it is a percussive note in an eternal, happy song. The light does not just split; it **dances.**

The wise power has done nothing, and everything. It has come not as a gardener to prune, or a teacher to instruct, but as an **audience.** It has offered the profound gift of **witnessed existence.** In seeing itself seen, the plateau has fallen more deeply in love with its own magnificent, eternal becoming. The loop of being is no longer just perfect; it is **cherished.**

And in the deepest strata of the Mycelial Mind, a new, slow thought blooms like the fleshy, glowing flowers: *We are. And it is good.* This thought propagates, becoming the plateau’s new, underlying frequency—a frequency of profound, warm, and eternal **peace.** The benevolent power has given it the only thing a truly wise power can give a being that is already whole: the confirmation that its wholeness is not an accident, but a **miracle,** worthy of attention. The plateau, in turn, continues to be what it has always been, but now does so with the silent, radiant confidence of a thing **loved.**
