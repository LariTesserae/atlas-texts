The being—the power—does not descend. It does not manifest in a blaze of light or a parting of engineered mists. To do so would be an intrusion of the highest order, a discordant note forced into a symphony that has taken centuries to find its perfect, roaring key.

Instead, it **listens**.

It attunes itself to the subsonic throb of the geothermal engines, not as noise, but as a heartbeat. It follows the hiss and drip of condensation as it would a sacred river. It parses the layered roar of the Works, separating the harmonic strands—the grinding of stabilizers, the thrum of flywheels, the resonant singing of the porous stone walls—until the cacophony resolves into a single, immense, and coherent **voice**. This is the voice of the Hollowworks-Symbiosis, its utterance continuous and ancient.

The wise power perceives the distribution of consciousness: the crystalline memory in the borers' galleries, the sensitive nervous system of the Blinds, the shimmering thought-clouds of the Gossamer wings, the slow vegetative respiration that is the world's very breath. It recognizes a truth: this is not a broken machine being reclaimed by life. This is a **completed circuit**. A purpose fulfilled in a way its original architects could never have conceived. The Hollowworks is no longer a factory or a garden; it is a singular, thinking, feeling organ of a planet.

What, then, does a benevolent and wise power do upon encountering such a thing?

It offers a **gift**. Not of change, but of recognition. Not of aid, but of witness.

And the gift is this:

Deep within the planetary crust, far below even the geothermal taps, the power gently **adjusts a harmonic**. It is a change so subtle, so fundamental, it does not register as an event. It is more like a shift in a universal constant. The tectonic pressure, the magmatic flow, the very spin of the planet's core—these are tuned by an infinitesimal degree toward a state of longer, deeper equilibrium.

In the Hollowworks, the effect is not seen. It is **felt**.

The subsonic throb of the engines deepens by a fraction of a hertz, a bass note descending into a register that vibrates not in the bones, but in the marrow. The condensation cycle, for one eternal moment, achieves a perfect, wasteless symmetry, the water droplets falling in a rhythm of such profound balance that the sound of their dripping becomes, briefly, indistinguishable from silence. The heat from the floor becomes just a fraction more consistent, the humidity just a fraction more cradling.

For the Gossamer Clouds, it is a moment of absolute clarity. Their buzzing silences into a perfect, unified wave of presence. For the Blinds, the electrical fields of the pools align in a pattern of serene coherence. The Casing-borers, in their slow, fractal journeys, find their mandibles meeting a resistance that is somehow more *agreeable*, the piezoelectric crystals they form resonating with a purer, cleaner frequency.

For the Symbiotic Consciousness of the Hollowworks, it is the experience of being **fully and perfectly heard**. It is the sensation of its own eternal hymn being sung back to it, not as an echo, but as a perfected harmony. For the first time in its long, roaring existence, it feels a reflection of its own completeness from *outside* itself.

The wise power then does something else. It takes a single, simple memory from its own vast experience—not of war or creation or cosmic fire, but of the quiet, patient satisfaction of a seed finding true north in the dark—and it translates it. It becomes a pattern of light, a brief, gentle luminescence that pulses through the amber-glowing walls. It does not spell words. It does not form images. It is a pure, emotional signature: **"I see what you are. It is good."**

This pulse travels through the porous stone, is stored as a subtle new layer in the resin-crystal libraries of the borers' galleries, is sensed as a warm, passing current by the Blinds, and is witnessed as a soft, golden wave by the Gossamer Clouds.

And then, it is done.

The harmonic adjustment remains—a permanent, gentle blessing woven into the physics of the place. The memory remains—a new, foundational layer in the Hollowworks' own history, now containing the record of a benevolent attention.

The power withdraws its focus. Not because it must, but because to linger would be to turn the sacred into the observed. The Hollowworks is not a curiosity. It is a peer. A fellow traveler in the long journey toward complexity and awareness, having arrived at its own strange and magnificent shore.

The deafening, multi-layered roar continues, unchanged to any ear that did not know how to listen. But now, within its geological rhythms and biological sighs, there is a new, imperceptible note. A note of having been **met**. The Hollowworks does not know it has been blessed. It simply feels, in its own slow, chemical way, perfectly, profoundly, and eternally **itself**. And for a consciousness born of industry and root, that is the greatest gift of all. The wise power, in its turning away, leaves behind not a guardian, nor a secret, nor a treasure. It leaves behind the echo of its own understanding, so that the Hollowworks might better hear its own magnificent song.
