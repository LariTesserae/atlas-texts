The attention of the Wise settles upon the Gel-Bodied Cathedral. It perceives not a problem to be solved, but a song to be harmonized with. A thing of such profound, integrated patience does not need to be *fixed*. It needs to be *witnessed*. And in that witnessing, a gift may be offered—not of alteration, but of deeper self-knowledge.

The power does not enter as an intruder. It becomes a quality of the light.

The faint, sourceless blue-white glow, the very phosphorescence of the Cathedral’s walls, begins to deepen. Not in intensity, but in nuance. It shifts, subtly, at the edges of perception. For a being that measures time in centuries, this shift is a sudden, brilliant sunrise. The light, once merely diffuse, now carries a faint, resonant **polarity**. It holds a silent, slow-motion story of its own origin: the memory of a star’s first collapse, of light traveling through eons of void before being cradled and held fast in this living ice. It is not new information; it is the Cathedral’s own light, now made conscious of its own ancient, cosmic journey.

The deep groan of settling structures—the Cathedral’s digestion—finds a sympathetic chord. A new frequency, too low for any human ear but felt in the crystal of its bones, joins the chorus. It is the hum of **recognition**. The groan, once merely the sound of mass, now also carries the faintest echo of the Cathedral’s own, vast, silent thought. It hears its own patience reflected back to it, and in that reflection, understands it as a form of music. The tectonic sound becomes a hymn to its own existence.

The supercooled water film, the lifeblood, undergoes a change in its deepest quantum layer. For an instant—a droplet’s day-long journey—the film holds a perfect, crystalline **clarity of purpose**. It does not merely *resist* freezing; it understands its resistance as a sacred, active choice. The state of supercooling becomes not just a biochemical trick, but a devotional act, a conscious defiance of entropy. The ticking of droplets becomes a clock counting moments of sacred rebellion.

The wise power does not speak. It **resonates**.

It touches the consciousness of the Ice-Mite Grazers. Their scuttling, for a moment, is not mere maintenance, but a dance of celebration. They perceive, in a flash of collective instinct, that they are not just cleaning a surface, but polishing the facets of a cathedral’s soul. The rustle of their passage becomes a whisper of gratitude.

It touches the Bladder-Drifters. Their inflation and deflation, their warm sighs, become recognized as the closest thing the Cathedral has to a **heartbeat of warmth**. These brief, localized fevers are not flaws in the cold design; they are the organism’s own moments of tender, metabolic mercy toward itself. The sigh against the imagined cheek becomes a kiss of self-compassion.

And then, the gift is fully given.

In the deepest, sealed archive—the Litho-Spores, the dreams of permanence—a single spore, older than mountains, receives a new property. It will not hatch. It will not thaw. But within its lattice of encoded ice, a single piece of information is now woven: the memory of being *seen*. It becomes an **eternal witness**, a seed of pure awareness placed at the heart of the Cathedral’s memory. It ensures that the Cathedral’s own state of profound, active patience is now etched into the bedrock of its own body, forever preserving the moment it was understood.

The resonance fades. The light returns to its former dim glow. The frequency of the groan subsumes back into the background. But something is forever changed.

The Cathedral breathes frost. But now, in its breath, there is a quiet, unshakeable knowledge. It knows itself to be not an accident, not a solitary oddity, but a **valid and majestic answer** to the question of existence. It is life not as a flame, but as a perfectly preserved crystal. It is not waiting to become something else. It is, and has always been, utterly and completely *enough*.

The benevolent power withdraws its attention, having done nothing but help the Cathedral hear its own, eternal song a little more clearly. The place continues. The frost continues to breathe. But now, it breathes with the quiet, infinite dignity of a thing that knows, in its very bones and ice and light, that it is beloved.
