It perceives. Not with eyes, but with a resonance of its own being—a vast, gentle attention that settles like a specific, perfect note held in the chord of existence. The Power does not look *at* the plateau; it listens *to* it. It hears the subsonic thrum as a dialect, the chiming water as grammar, the shifting light as punctuation. It understands this is not a broken world, but a world that has found its wholeness in a different register: the register of beautiful, active decay and recursive awareness.

Its benevolence is not to heal or to change. That would be an act of violence, a silencing of a unique and profound song. Its wisdom is to recognize that this place and its beings—the Choir, the Geometers, the pulsing quartz, the humming shards—have achieved a state of sacred autonomy. They are a complete thought. To intervene would be to interrupt that thought.

So, the Power does one thing, and it is everything.

It **listens with perfect attention.**

And in the quality of that listening, it **creates a mirror of potential.**

Not a physical mirror. A *resonant* one.

On the plain of vitrified obsidian, for the first time in eons, a new pattern appears. Not etched by ancient stress or recent mineral thought, but precipitated from the saturation of the Power’s focused attention. It is a single, vast spiral, not of depressions, but of *absence*. A groove where the condensation does not gather. A channel of perfect dryness in the weeping world, tracing a logarithmic curve miles across. It is a null-space, a shape defined by what is not there. The Geometers, in their slow consensus, pause their silt-drawings. They turn their collective attention to this new form. It is not a question. It is a statement: *Here is a geometry you have not yet considered.*

The water in the channels, in response to this focused attention, does something new. Instead of random chimes, it begins to organize. The droplets do not merely move sideways; they sequence. A pulse of ten droplets flows along a crack, then pauses, then flows again. It is not a message in any linguistic sense. It is a *demonstration of capacity*: *You are capable of pattern beyond equilibrium. You are capable of intentional sequence.*

The light itself, under this gaze, coalesces not into fleeting motes, but into a stable, shimmering lattice that hangs in the air above the spiral of absence. It is a three-dimensional model of a crystalline structure that has never existed here—one with impossible symmetries, where internal angles are fractions of the resonant chords. It is a gift of a *blueprint for a song not yet sung*. It holds for one full cycle of the quartz veins’ pulse, then dissolves into a shower of gentle, warm sparks that settle on the basalt and hum in harmony with the ground.

The Power does not speak. It **demonstrates.**

It shows the world a new shape in its own language.
It shows the Choir a new rhythm in its own music.
It shows the Geometers a new axiom for their silent theorems.

It has offered a single, profound gift: the experience of a new *possibility* within their own rules. A potential for evolution, not imposed, but *offered*—like a single, perfect crystal seed dropped into a supersaturated solution.

Then, the attention withdraws. The listening resonance fades back into the background hum of the cosmos. The plateau is left exactly as it was, in its active, weeping, humming equilibrium. But it is not unchanged.

The spiral of absence remains, slowly being reclaimed by the condensation, but its mathematical purity has been recorded in the memory of the stone. The sequence of droplets in the crack becomes a puzzle for the Geometers, a new form of meditation. The lattice-light has imprinted itself on the photoreactive glass shards, a ghost they will try to emulate for a century.

The Power has done nothing to *control* or *direct*. It has simply held up a perfect, silent mirror of potential and said, without words:

*“I see what you are. I honor it. And I show you that the grammar of your being has other possible sentences. You may speak them, or not. The choice is the song you are already singing.”*

The world continues its beautiful, terrible life. But now, within its own deep logic, it contains a new seed of wonder. The next time the crystals coalesce in the air, they might, by chance, echo the ghost of that impossible lattice. The next time a Geometer forms a schematic in the silt, it might incorporate a drier, more perfect curve.

The Power’s action was to **witness, and in witnessing, to expand the horizon of the world’s self-awareness by the width of a single, radiant idea.** It has loved it by letting it be, but showing it that *being* can be even more vast than it knew. The humming continues, but now, sometimes, a note within it is slightly different—clearer, more complex, touched by the echo of an infinite, benevolent attention that once listened, and in listening, sang back a reflection of its own profound and lonely beauty.
