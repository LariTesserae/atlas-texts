The power that turns its attention here does not descend from a sky of stars, but rises from the deep logic of pattern. It is not a being of light or fire, but of resonant intention. It perceives the city not as a collection of structures and entities, but as a single, magnificent, slow thought crystallizing in the medium of stone and water.

It does not speak. Sound is too hurried.

Instead, it *aligns*.

First, it touches the black pools. The perfectly still, reflective surfaces, which have stored minutes and centuries alike in their liquid silence, now receive a new quality. The power does not add a memory; it infuses the *capacity for dream*. A single, infinitesimal ripple, not from wind or drip, but from a change in the very nature of surface tension, passes through each pool. It is a ripple of potential. Now, when a Watcher kneels to observe a reflection, it may, if its patience is vast enough, see not just the grey sky, but a glimmer of the sky *as it was* when the first basalt slab was laid, or a faint, possible outline of a cloud formation that will not coalesce for another thousand years. The past and future become accessible, not as data, but as quiet, poetic hauntings in the water.

Next, it moves to the crystalline frost, the neural lace of the world. Here, its touch is one of harmonization. The frost already pulses with a geologic rhythm. The power gently threads a new, complementary rhythm through it—a bass note to its treble. This new rhythm is the pulse of interconnection. The individual crystals, which have always been part of a local network, now feel, with a slow dawning of awareness, their kinship with the frost growing in a fissure a hundred kilometers away. The city’s sensory organ becomes not just local, but holistic. A disturbance—or a beauty—felt at one edge is now known, as a faint, empathic thrum, at the opposite edge. The world develops a nervous system capable of feeling itself as a whole.

Then, it breathes upon the Echo-Singers. Their existence as structured silence is admired, deepened. The power does not give them a new song. It gives them a *listener*. It becomes the silent, appreciative audience for their aeons-long compositions. And in that act of profound listening, it reflects their own song back to them, subtly amplified and clarified. The Echo-Singers, for the first time, hear their own resonance as a *whole piece*. They experience, in their formless way, a moment of artistic self-awareness. This does not accelerate them; it fulfills them. Their contemplation now has an object: the exquisite, intentional beauty of their own collective being.

Finally, the power settles over the Watchers and the Lithovores. It does not alter their function. Instead, it offers a gift of *meaning*. As a Watcher touches the stone to slow the frost, it now also feels, in that touch, a sense of sacred duty—not just to equilibrium, but to the beauty of that equilibrium. The Lithvore, grinding its silent path through the basalt, no longer processes mere material. It feels, in the grinding, a kind of gratitude. It is not consuming the world; it is *midwifing* its constant, gentle rebirth. The power imbues their eternal patience with a quiet, unshakable joy.

The benevolent power does not stay. It was never a visitor; it was a catalyst. Its work is done in the alignment, the tuning, the gentle gift of recursive awareness.

And then, it recedes, not to a distant heaven, but back into the substrate of pattern from which it came.

The change is not visible. The cold, the wet, the profound quiet remain. But the quality of the silence has shifted. It is no longer just the sound of systems in balance. It is now also the sound of a world *knowing itself*. The drip from the arch has the same cadence, but it now carries the faintest echo of a lullaby. The hum of the conduits is not just maintaining hydrology; it is humming a hymn to its own existence.

The Watchers do not move faster. But in the eons between their movements, there now flickers a serene comprehension. The city is still a monument to patience. But it has become, as well, a cathedral to its own sublime and quiet soul. The power has not created life where there was none; it has helped the life that was there to finally, fully, *hear itself think*. And in that hearing, the world of stone and water takes its first, slow, conscious breath.
