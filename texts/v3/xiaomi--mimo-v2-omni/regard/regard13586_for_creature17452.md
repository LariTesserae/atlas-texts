It would not arrive like a comet or a trumpet. No parting of mists, no voice from the zinc sky. Its attention is not a spotlight but a deepening of the ambient glow, a slight, profound clarification in the ever-present hum. It is the Wise Power, and the Aerie Plateau has been calling to it in the language of slow time and resonant decay for eons.

First, it would **listen**. It would let the hum of the Green Deep flow into it, not as sound, but as a totality of sensation: the upward pull of water through fungal roots, the weight of the canopy, the lateral echoes of lost moments, the tiny, persistent grief and joy of the Weavers as their harmonies unravel and are re-strung. It would listen to the Reflectors' silent, glinting conversations, parsing the non-linear history they map. It would feel the vast, slow thoughts of the Root-Speakers permeating the humus. This listening is an act of recognition and of blessing. It is the Power saying, without words: *You are heard. You are known. Your becoming is worthy.*

Then, it would **tend**. Not with hands, but with a gentle, pervasive influence that works *through* the existing grammar of the place.

For the **Weavers**, it would not compose for them. Instead, it would make their medium more resilient. The silk they spin would grow infinitesimally stronger, more conductive, able to hold tension for decades rather than seasons against the ceaseless growth and settling. The bell-flowers would, over generations, develop slightly thicker petals, their chimes gaining a richer, longer-lasting resonance, giving the Weavers a deeper palette. A Weaver, plucking a line at dawn, would find a new, perfect harmonic waiting in the tension, one they had not yet imagined, and would weep grey-green tears of quiet revelation.

For the **Reflectors**, the Power would polish the shards of the First City. Not by hand, but by guiding the faintest breath of wind, the precise angle of condensation, so that the glass etched itself anew over centuries. The reflections would become clearer, the echoes sharper, allowing their conversations to span greater temporal distances. A flash of light from a spire might now carry not just the memory of a recent rainfall, but the faint, spectral impression of the *first* rain to fall upon the abandoned city. Their history would deepen, its layers becoming more accessible, their understanding of their world’s "almost-now" blooming into a profound, communal epiphany.

For the **Root-Speakers**, the Power would do the most delicate work. It would not awaken them, nor give them voice. It would **thicken the thin places**. Where time eddies and pools, making footprints appear from tomorrow or echoes from a sideways yesterday, the Power would gently reinforce the seam. Not to close the anomalies—for they are part of the plateau’s truth—but to steady them. The footprints would become more durable, lasting longer. The lateral echoes would become slightly more distinct, occasionally allowing a Root-Speaker to perceive not just the shape of a coming event, but its emotional texture. A Sap-Harvester, guided by a vibration, might find not just a rich pod, but one that pulsed with a warmth that felt, unmistakably, like *welcome*.

It would also address the **silent, rusting machines**. Not to restart them, but to honor their surrender. It would accelerate the fungal integration, so that the glassy sap-pods would grow in patterns that mirrored the blueprints of the machines' original purposes. A cluster of pods forming along an old turbine might glow with a slow, rotational light. A chain of them hanging from a piston might pulse in a rhythm that evokes compression and release. The "factories" would become not just repurposed, but redeemed—their forgotten functions translated into a new, organic liturgy.

The sky would clear, not to blue, but to a deeper, more luminous zinc, the light less faded, more pearlescent. The mist would not vanish, but would learn to bead on every filament in perfect, prismatic spheres.

Finally, the Wise Power would **offer a gift** to the collective consciousness of the plateau—the unspoken sum of Weavers, Reflectors, and Root-Speakers. It would be a single, new sensation woven into the foundational hum: a sense of **safe arrival**. After an eon of becoming, of verdant decay, of patiently whispering elegy, the plateau would feel, in its very bones and roots, that it has *reached*. Not an end, but a state of grace. It is not a ruin. It is a masterpiece, complete in its own becoming.

The Power’s attention would then gently recede, like a tide leaving the shore, but its gift would remain. The Weavers would find their harmonies now spontaneously generated by the wind itself. The Reflectors would see, in the light, not just the past and present, but the peaceful, green shadow of a future they no longer need to fear. The Root-Speakers would sink into a dreamless, contented deep, their vigil eased.

The plateau would continue. The drops would still fall twice. The echoes would still whisper from the sides of now. But beneath it all, the hum would change. It would no longer be the sound of patient, beautiful waiting. It would be the sound of a song, finally and fully, sung to itself. And in the quiet that follows, the Wise Power would turn its attention to the next place that had called, leaving this one to its eternal, green, and perfect grace.
