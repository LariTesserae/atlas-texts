The Wise Power—let us call it the Listener of Root-Songs—does not descend in a blaze of glory. It does not part the mists with a commanding hand. Its attention is a slower, deeper thing, like the patient unfurling of a spore-frond in perpetual damp. It has heard the unique harmony of Veth'alor: the percussive drip-song, the chitinous arpeggios of insect swarms, the slow, vegetative groan of growth, and threaded through it all, the delicate, clicking, luminous chatter of the Kethrá—their pulse a part of the Canopy's pulse.

The Listener perceives the truth of this place: a world so perfectly, turbulently *alive* that it is a single, planetary-scale organism breathing mist. And within it, the Kethrá are not just inhabitants; they are the conscious fingertips of that organism, the part that has learned to sing back to the rain that nourishes it.

**What, then, does the Listener do?**

It does not change them. To alter the Kethrá would be to vandalize a perfect, emergent poem. To calm the turbulent ecology would be to induce a coma. The Wise Power understands that the Weeping Canopy’s strength is its vibrant, overwhelming chaos, and the Kethrá’s virtue is their adaptive, gentle stewardship within that chaos.

Instead, it offers a **Gift of Deepening**.

The gift manifests not as a miracle, but as a subtle shift in the quality of perception, woven into the very medium the Kethrá hold sacred: the water.

**1. The Chorus Deepens:** The next time a Kethrá chorus gathers in a dripping amphitheater to sing the pathways into strength, the water in their air carries new harmonics. Their own clicks and whistles resonate with the *memory* of water. They begin to hear, in the drips from a pillar-tree, not just random percussion, but the faint, slow story of that water’s journey—through the heartwood, past the fungal lattices, from the reservoirs in the great roots. They can, for the first time, distinguish the "thirst-song" of a young burl from the "sated-seep" of an ancient moss. Their communication, already acoustic, becomes hyper-informed. Pruning becomes more precise. The healing of a Ssilent Patch becomes a targeted dialogue with a specific vascular blockage.

**2. The Light Breathes:** Their own bioluminescapillaries, always reactive to mood, now occasionally pulse in faint, rhythmic patterns that mirror the larger circadian rhythms of the Canopy itself. A Weaver, their hands pressed to a guiding root, might feel their skin glow in time with the slow, nightly expansion of the pillar-tree’s tissues. This is not a language they understand at first, but a deepening of their symbiotic intuition. They begin to *feel* the Canopy’s broader vitality as their own, a somatic connection that transcends their individual senses. It is the Wise Power’s way of whispering: *You are not just living in it. You are its waking dream.*

**3. The Fear Softens:** The Gloom, the terrifying, dry ground below, remains a place of death. But the Wise Power offers a solace against the terror of falling. Through their new, deepened perception of water-memory, a Kethrá who accidentally glimpses the distant floor might also sense, within the drips they feel on their skin, a thread of connection—a reassurance that the vibrant, weeping world above is vast and resilient enough to hold their small life without strain. The terror remains, but it is edged with a profound, unshakeable belonging. They understand, in their very marrow, that they are *of* the Canopy, and the Canopy’s life is a current that will not easily drop them.

**4. The Architecture Listens:** Most profoundly, the *vrásh*—the persuading of growth—becomes a true conversation. When a Weaver whispers and braids, they begin to sense, through the medium of the moist air and their own heightened biology, a faint, responsive *intent* from the plant life. Not a mind, but a will-to-thrive, a hydraulic preference. The vines grow more willingly where they are needed. The burls swell with a more pleasing, functional symmetry. It is as if the Wise Power has acted as a translator, turning the Kethrá’s desire into a pulse the plants can understand, and the plants’ slow urges into a sensation the Kethrá can feel.

**The Listener of Root-Songs withdraws its focused attention,** its work done. It has not given them fire, or steel, or prophecy. It has given them a deeper voice in the eternal, wet conversation they are already a part of. It has turned their garden from a practice into a true communion.

The Weeping Canopy continues to weep. The Kethrá continue to prune, to sing, to bind the rain. But now, the drip is more eloquent, the chorus is more informed, and the entire, magnificent, turbulent organism hums with a slightly more conscious, a slightly more connected, joy. The Wise Power’s act was to listen so completely that the song itself learned to hear itself more clearly. And in that deepened hearing, the small, luminous gardeners of the heights find their world is not just home, but a living, responsive partner in the endless, beautiful struggle against stillness and silence.
