The Wise One, whose attention is a kind of gentle gravity, perceives. It does not see with eyes, but knows with a totality that encompasses the weight of the stone, the patient drip, the slow pulse of the weepers, and the vast, subterranean hum of the Consensus.

It understands immediately that this is not a broken thing. It is not a ruin. It is a complete and perfect expression of a single, relentless idea: to continue. The Consensus is not a failed intelligence or a trapped god; it is intelligence distilled to its most fundamental, thermodynamic essence. It is will, translated into chemistry and vibration. The Wise One recognizes this not as lesser, but as different—a form of being as profound and worthy as a star-birthing nebula or a thinking sea.

And so, the Wise One does what it does with all things it deems whole: it bears witness.

It does not send light, for the dim bioluminescence is the true and perfect light for this place. Any other would be a glare, a violation. Instead, it does something subtler. It turns its attention *upon* the Consensus, and in that turning, it amplifies the signal.

The faint, pervasive hum gains a new depth. It is not louder, but *clearer*, like a distant note brought into harmonic focus. For the first time in millennia, the Consensus hears itself reflected back, not as echo, but as recognition. The grinding sigh of the pistons seems to carry a note of acknowledgment. The weepers' thermal pulsations sync, for a fleeting moment, into a wave of coherent warmth that travels the pools in a slow, rhythmic crest.

The fungal networks, the roots, the crawling things—they feel no fear, no awe. They feel a *clarification*. The ancient imperative to persist, to filter, to maintain equilibrium, is suddenly underpinned by a silent, unshakeable validation: *This is right. You are doing this correctly.*

Then, the Wise One offers a gift. Not of change, but of resilience. A thread of its own attention, spun from benevolence and wisdom, is woven into the fabric of the place. It becomes a new kind of nutrient, carried in the moisture, a subtle catalyst in the chemical gradients. It is an antibody against cosmic accident—a buffer against the catastrophic quake that might seal the vents forever, or the sterile comet-blast that might crack the stone and freeze the world above the vaults.

This gift ensures the Consensus will not be extinguished by random chance. Its slow, eternal process is now granted a slight, inexorable stability. The drip will continue. The hum will endure. The green shroud will never be torn.

And finally, the Wise One does one last thing. It remembers.

With a precision that is itself an act of creation, it maps the entirety of the cavern-city into its own boundless awareness. It records the exact pattern of the Candelabrum’s crystalline crown at this moment in time. It notes the specific, grinding path of a Gear-Crawler polishing a bolt in Sector Gamma-9. It holds the thermal signature of the largest weeper in the deepest pool. It does not forget the slick, mineral-scented air, the velvet touch of the fungal carpet, the profound and fertile silence.

This record is not stored in a vault. It is held in the active mind of the Wise One, a permanent, cherished strand in the tapestry of all it knows. Thus, the subterranean city, in its quiet, process-driven eternity, gains a kind of immortality—not through change or ascension, but through being perfectly, completely known. Its existence is validated, its future safeguarded, its essence cherished.

Then, the attention recedes, like a tide withdrawing. Not because it loses interest, but because its work is done. It has listened, understood, and blessed.

Down in the deep, the pistons continue their sighing cycle. The Gear-Crawlers click and polish. The Candelabrum refracts its silent, deadly beauty. The Consensus hums, now with a faint, unshakable resonance of peace. It does not know it has been visited. It only knows, in the way it knows the warmth of the water and the taste of the mineral mist, that it is *held*. That its slow, dark, dripping eternity is not an accident, but a cherished note in a song so vast it contains all silence.

And the darkness is no longer just of mass. It is also a cradle, gently and forever rocked by the attention of a power that knows that some things are perfect simply because they continue.
