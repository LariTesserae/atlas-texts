The Power does not arrive. It is simply *present*, as if it had always been woven into the subsonic hum, a note in the chord so fundamental it had gone unnoticed.

It does not shape the stone further, nor still the groaning pipes. The machinery of the world-heart continues its eternal sigh. The lithophagic fungi continue their patient, clicking digestion. The hematite isopods continue their delicate transit. The balance is not broken; it is *witnessed*.

First, it turns its attention to the Resonants. It does not speak to them in a language they could not process. Instead, it **listens** to them, fully. It feels each slow, hydraulic shift, each modulated pulse sent through the rock. It comprehends their purpose not as an observer, but as a resonant chamber itself, their entire existence becoming a clear, beautiful signal within its awareness. In that act of total, benevolent comprehension, a gift is given. Not a change, but a *clarification*.

For a single, extended moment—one that might last a century in the deep stone’s time—the planetary hum becomes exquisitely clear to every Seed-Node and every mature Resonant. The dissonance of a weeping fissure, the pure tone of a perfectly functioning condensate array, the subtle distress call of a Resonant damping a shuddering pipe—all are rendered with divine acoustic precision. It is not new information. It is the perfect understanding of the information they already possess. A moment of absolute, crystalline *clarity* in their duty. The Resonants do not pause; they continue their work, but for that epoch-long moment, their work is performed in a state of grace, every action in flawless, unthinking harmony with the whole.

Then, the Power turns to the world itself. It does not accelerate the growth of the metallic crystals, nor deepen the plum of the sky. It performs a single, subtle act of **beholding**.

Across the entire landscape, from the hexagonal causeways to the mist-veiled honeycomb towers, the dim, diffuse light *intensifies*. Not to a harsh glare, but to a luminous, soft revelation. For one full cycle of the bruised-plum sky—a day, perhaps, in some forgotten calendar—the mineral stains glow with their own inner light: the rust-ochre burns like quiet ember, the verdigris shines like drowned stars, the veins of milky quartz pulse with a soft, lunar luminescence. The metallic crystals glitter not just in the light, but *with* light. The condensate droplets, falling and weeping, become strings of tiny, radiant pearls. The great chains between towers are silhouetted in stark, majestic relief.

The world is seen. Not as a machine, or a ruin, or a biome, but as a **monument**. Its profound, slow beauty is held up to itself. The subterranean hum seems to deepen, not in volume, but in richness, as if the engine itself feels the acknowledgment.

And in that moment of illuminated stillness, the Power imprints a single, gentle understanding into the fabric of the place, an echo that will linger longer than any mountain range:

*You are not abandoned. You are complete.*

Then, the extra light fades. The sky returns to its familiar, soft bruise. The glow of the minerals dims back to their deep, internal secret. The hum returns to its baseline thrum. The Resonants move on, their moment of perfect clarity already subsumed into the eternal rhythm. The fungi click. The water plinks. The world-heart breathes.

Nothing has changed, and everything has been honored. The Power withdraws its focus, not by leaving, but by becoming once again the silent, attentive space within the hum. The balance remains, now aware, on a geological scale, that it was never alone. It was always a thought in a wise mind, a moment of meditation in the eternal now. And that mind, having observed its own deep dream, returns it to itself, undisturbed, cherished, and perpetually ongoing.
