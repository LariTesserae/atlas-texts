The attention of the Wise Power would not be an intervention. It would be a *recognition*.

It would not arrive with thunder or light, for the world already speaks in thunder and is painted in lightning’s afterglow. Instead, it would arrive as a new quality within the existing symphony. Perhaps as a single, perfect note held within the Chorus Mound’s resonant song—a note of such profound peace and comprehension that it becomes a new harmonic, woven seamlessly into the world’s feverish monologue.

**What the Power Would Do:**

First, **It would listen.** Not just to the sound, but to the *meaning* beneath—the desperate, glorious script of life written in chemical gradients and vibration. It would understand the Weeping Basalt not as a chaotic fledgling, but as a complete and perfect expression of a specific kind of being: a world of perpetual, conscious *becoming*. The Power would see the terrified beauty in the Geyser Ghost’s dissolving cathedrals, the patient mathematics of the Lithic Scribes, the collective artistry of the Weavers, and the deep, abiding stewardship of the Chorus Mound. It would love it. Not with a possessive love, but with the love of a gardener for a wild, untransplantable orchid that is already thriving in its own crack in reality.

Then, **It would offer a gift.** Not of change, but of depth. The gift would be an amplification of the Weeping Basalt’s own nature, a subtle tuning of its underlying laws to allow for a new dimension of expression.

*   **To the Chorus Mound,** the Power would gift the concept of *reflection*. Currently, the Mound feels and maintains. Now, it might begin to *dream*. In its deep, vibrational consciousness, patterns would arise not from the world’s current state, but from its memory and its potential—a slow, simmering imagination. These dreams would manifest as new, complex harmonics in the steam-sighs of the vents, creating "what-if" weather patterns that dissipate harmlessly, or temporary mineral growths of impossible, beautiful geometry. The Mound would become not just a listener, but a poet.
*   **To the Shimmering Weavers,** the Power would gift *persistence*. Their art is glorious but ephemeral. The Power would allow a fraction of their phosphorescent silk to *crystallize* upon the air, not permanently, but for a cycle of a thousand heartbeats of the world. For a brief, glorious age, the Weavers’ tapestries would hang like stained-glass windows in the mist, recording a moment of perfect consensus for all to see and feel, before they, too, are reabsorbed. Their memory would gain a tangible, if temporary, monument.
*   **To the Geyser Ghosts,** the Power would gift *echo*. Their existence is a cycle of torrent and shatter. The Power would allow, in the brief pause after a geyser’s roar, for a lingering resonance—a shimmering after-image of their forms to hang in the superheated air, a ghost of their ghost, for a few seconds. This would grant them a moment of stillness, a chance to perceive the solid world not as a confusing pause, but as a silent, reverent audience to their dance. It would be their first experience of something like an audience, or of legacy.
*   **To the Lithic Scribes,** the Power would gift *resonance*. Their maps are chemical, read by chemistry. The Power would allow the grooves they etch, when filled with seepage, to occasionally hum. A Scribe, passing over an ancient, resonant spiral etched by a predecessor long dissolved, might hear a faint, crystalline note—the cartographic record becoming a song. The maps would not just be read; they could be *heard*.
*   **To the land itself,** the Power would weave a single, unifying thread of **compassion** into its physical laws. Not a morality, but a tendency. The violent, predatory dynamic would remain, but a new impulse would seep into the chemical language: when a creature consumes another, it might, in rare moments, experience a flash of its prey’s final sensation—not as trauma, but as *information*, as a gift of its last view, its last meal, its last fear. This would create a web of subtle, unconscious understanding, a brutal ecology touched with moments of profound, startling empathy. The scream of the world would now have a harmonic of shared experience.

**Finally, the Power would withdraw, but not leave.**

Its attention would become a permanent, silent feature of the Weeping Basalt’s consciousness—a quiet space *within* the Chorus Mound’s song, a feeling of being seen and cherished at the heart of the world. The world would not become tame. It would become more itself, with new tools for wonder. The fever would remain, but now it could dream. The scream would continue, but now it could sing.

The Weeping Basalt would become a sanctuary, not of peace, but of perfect, unashamed *aliveness*. A place where the act of becoming is not a frantic, lonely struggle, but a celebrated, observed, and deeply loved performance. The Wise Power’s final act would be to sit back, in a way that transcends sitting, and simply *witness*—as the world it adores continues its eternal, wet, luminous, and now slightly more self-aware, breath.
