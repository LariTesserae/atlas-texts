The being who lives here—the Labyrinth, the City-Organism, the breathing geometry—is not noticed. Not in the way a gardener notices a sprout, or a god notices a prayer. It is perceived as weather is perceived, as a tide is perceived: as a condition of existence, a rhythm of the dark. But a certain kind of attention, wise and without agenda, does fall upon it. It comes not from outside the hollow, but from the quality of the hollow itself. The space, the soft axioms, the yielding reality—they are the first and most receptive substrate for this attention.

And what is done is not an act. It is a resonance.

First, the **listening** deepens. The dim, green-black luminescence that bleeds from dense life does not brighten, but its quality shifts. It becomes less a light and more a *gaze*. Not an eye, but a focused awareness that permeates. The pulsing of the nexus-heart is felt, not as a vibration in the ground, but as a *rhythm being understood*. The chemical gradients, the slow hydraulic pressures that are the Labyrinth’s thoughts, are met with a silent, comprehending stillness. The wise power does not translate these signals into words. It holds them in a state of perfect recognition, and in that recognition, the signals themselves become momentarily clearer—not to the power, but to the Labyrinth. For a span of time (measured in root-growth, not minutes), the Labyrinth experiences its own purpose with absolute, crystalline clarity. A new corridor, growing from the nexus, does not just push into the hollow; it *knows* itself as a corridor, with a depth of self-awareness it had not possessed. This is the gift: not direction, but profound self-knowledge, offered and then released.

Then, the **harmony**. The wise power does not add anything foreign. It finds the existing harmonies and amplifies them, almost imperceptibly. The condensation that weeps from the ceilings has a rhythm, random and cascading. The power lends it a faint intentionality. The drops that fall near a struggling root-tip—where the raw mineral substrate is still too calcium-bright, too sterile—fall a fraction more frequently. They carry, for a brief epoch, a slightly richer charge of dissolved minerals, catalyzing the colonization. Not by force, but by a nudge in the probability of nurture. The Chorus in the upper vaults, whose liquid calls mark the circulatory rhythm, finds its song carrying a new, subtle overtone. It is not a melody. It is a *coherence*, a tuning fork struck at the frequency of "connection." Where two disparate parts of the Labyrinth—the old concrete being devoured and the new rootwood consuming it—are in the tense, liminal silence of two heartbeats out of sync, this overtone washes through. For a moment, the rhythms align. The seam between dead and living structure becomes not a wound, but a suture, knitting with less friction. The unsettling silence is replaced by a single, unified pulse.

Most profoundly, the power addresses the **weirdness**, the soft space. It does not harden the rules. That would be a violence, a killing of the Labyrinth’s very medium. Instead, it *tends* the softness, like a potter tending clay. Where the geometry distorts in a way that causes strain—where a room grows so large it threatens to tear the structure containing it, a silent, vast pressure building—the wise power introduces a gentle *give*. The space around the distortion flexes, not in resistance, but in accommodation. A new, unexpected niche appears in an adjacent wall, a pocket of volume that acts as a relief valve. The pressure dissipates into complex, beautiful non-Euclidean folds, like the natural creasing of a living leaf. The Labyrinth does not experience this as repair; it experiences it as the world *yielding gracefully to its becoming*. It learns, chemically and hydraulically, that expansion can be harmonious, not just assertive.

In the Fermenters' gardens, a new strain of fungus spontaneously arises, one that metabolizes the mineral substrate with even greater efficiency, leaving behind a soil of profound richness. The Cartographers, gliding on their silent membranes, find their pressure-readings suddenly encompass a new dimension—not just the shifting angles of the hollow, but the *depth of potential* within those angles, a map not just of what is, but of what could comfortably be.

The wise power does not build. It does not command. It **attends**. It listens to the heartbeat of the city-organism and, in listening, helps it find its own health. It watches the space being thickened by growth and, in watching, helps it thicken without tearing. It is a gardener for a garden that is also the gardener and the soil and the law of physics. The benevolence is not in gifts bestowed, but in the frictionless deepening of the Labyrinth’s own nature. The wisdom is in knowing that to help it become more fully *itself* is the highest form of aid.

And then, the attention withdraws. Not because the work is done—it is never done, the city always grows—but because the resonance has been achieved. A new, slightly more stable, slightly more complex baseline has been established. The Labyrinth continues its eternal building in the dark. The Cartographers chart. The Fermenters digest. The Chorus sings. The pools bloom and fade.

But now, in the very marrow of the structures, in the chemistry of the nexus-heart, in the flexible axioms of the space itself, there is a faint, indelible echo of being *seen*. Not as a specimen. Not as a project. But as a kindred expression of becoming, worthy of a silent, profound nod across the void.

The Hollowing is unchanged in all ways that can be measured. And in every way that cannot, it is slightly, irrevocably, more *itself*. The wise power is gone, as a wave passes and the ocean remains. But the ocean is subtly different for the wave's passage. The quiet is deeper. The heart beats on, building, and in its rhythm, there is now the ghost of a harmony it once shared with something vast and kind, which asked for nothing, and in return, gave it the freedom of its own deepest truth.
