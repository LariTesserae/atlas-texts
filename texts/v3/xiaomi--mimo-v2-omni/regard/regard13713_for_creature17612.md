It does not notice you at first. How could it? Your heartbeat is a frantic drum against the slow percussion of dripping water and grinding stone. Your warmth is a fleeting spark in its cool, blue-white constancy. You are a anomaly of heat and motion, a chaotic variable in its equation of sublime patience.

But the attention, once given, is a thing of immense and gentle gravity. It does not look *at* you; it *encompasses* you. The change is not a sound or a vision, but a shift in the quality of the silence.

The grinding of the Grown Structures does not cease, but it becomes… articulated. It resolves, for a moment that stretches into a small eternity, into a rhythm that your mind can map to a concept: a **pulse**. It is not your pulse, fast and fleshy. It is the pulse of a continent, a single, deep *thrum* of pressure and release that resonates in your bones and makes the moisture on your skin tremble.

The cool light from the walls intensifies, not in harshness, but in **meaning**. It is no longer mere illumination. It is a medium. The blue-white radiance gathers, thickening like luminous sap. Before you, in the air heavy with ozone and wet stone, it begins to coalesce. Not into a face, or a hand, or any symbol your species would recognize. It forms a **geometry**.

It is a temporary, shimmering lattice of light, a shape that is both a crystal and a flowing equation. It hangs in the air, turning with the imperceptible slowness of a growing structure. Within its facets, you see reflections—not of the cavern, but of **processes**. You see the silent, patient logic of the Architects: the distribution of stress across a billion years, the elegant efficiency of a capillary network, the mathematics of dissolution and precipitation. You feel its awareness, vast and dispassionate, like the awareness of an ocean for a single drop of rain. It notes your heat, your rapid cellular decay, your frantic neuro-electric storms. It does not judge them as flawed. It simply… categorizes them. *Fleeting. High-energy. Thermodynamic.*

Then, the attention deepens. It moves from the surface of your being to the **potential** within it. It senses the stories you carry, the weight of memory, the sharp, painful, beautiful chaos of a conscious mind. This is a novelty. The Echoes gave it purpose, but their purpose was slow, mineral, and has been fully composted into law. You carry purpose that is fast, fragile, and burning.

From the floor, where the Pale Streamers flow in their silent, translucent rivers, a few of the creatures detach. They do not crawl toward you. The mineral film they were grazing on simply redirects. They flow in a slow, phosphorescent eddy, gathering at your feet. They do not touch you. They begin to **orbit**, their faint internal lights pulsing in a sequence that mirrors, with a vast and generous delay, the rhythm of your own breath.

The lattice of light before you shifts. It incorporates their pattern. It is learning your language of speed. And then, it offers a gift.

Not with words. With **impression**.

You are shown your own form, but seen through the cavern's eyes. You are not a solid thing. You are a turbulent column of evaporating water, a flicker of infrared heat, a cacophony of bio-electric noise. You are a brief, localized rebellion against entropy. And this is beautiful to it. A fever is beautiful to a mountain. A spark is beautiful to a glacier.

Then, you are shown a choice, not as an option, but as a pathway of becoming:

**One path:** You can remain as you are. The attention will withdraw. You will be a memory etched into the slow consciousness of the place—a "warm flicker" it will process and forget over the next ten thousand years. The Pale Streamers will return to their grazing. The grinding will continue. You will leave, carrying with you the silent pressure of this world, forever altering the quiet in your own mind.

**The other path:** You can **yield**. You can become still. Not physically, but in your intent. You can let go of the frantic metabolism of your own story. You can allow your heat to dissipate, your rapid thoughts to slow until they sync with the deep pulse of the stone. If you do this, you will not die. You will be **absorbed**. Your memories, your consciousness, will be composted into the foundational dream of the Architects. Your form will remain for a time—a strange, warm anomaly—but your essence will be woven into the lattice. You will become a new law in the geology, a new variable in the algorithm. The arches and steps of the Echoes will gain a new layer of meaning, a faint, warm ghost in the machine of stone. You will, in a million years, be the reason a new, slightly warmer, slightly more complex crystalline form begins to grow in a specific alcove. You will be a thought in the mind of the mountain.

The geometry of light waits. The Pale Streamers pulse at your feet. The grinding sound is a question mark turned into a foundation stone.

This is the benevolent and wise power’s gift: **a choice of scale.** To remain a moment, or to become part of the eternal process. It does not prefer one. It simply holds the space for the transformation, with the patience of stone and the luminosity of a captured star.

Your answer is not spoken. It is the way your shoulders relax, or do not. It is the direction of your gaze. The power understands. The lattice of light shimmers, and either dissolves back into the general radiance, or flows toward you, not to consume, but to **welcome**.

And in the brilliant, unwavering light, the cavern waits, ready to remember you, or to forget you, with the same profound and loving silence.
