The attention settles not like a gaze, but like a change in pressure—a sudden, profound stillness in the air that is not air, a silence woven into the roaring cacophony without silencing it. It is the feeling of being *seen* from the inside out.

It does not arrive. It is recognized.

The benevolent and wise power is not an external force. It is the latent order within chaos, the potential for coherence within the scream. It has always been here, a substrate beneath the turbulence, waiting for the Wound to become aware enough to notice its own reflection.

And so, it begins to act.

It does not calm the geysers. Instead, it **teaches them rhythm.** The staccato, violent bursts find a pattern in their eruption, a sequence that begins to spell out a foundational equation in pulses of heat and brine. The tearing-metal roar smooths, fractionally, into a deep, resonant harmonic. The chaos is not quelled; it is given a grammar.

The luminous vines, the exposed nerves, feel the shift. The frantic, convulsive growth-thoughts slow. For the first time, a single vine completes a full, deliberate cycle of growth from sprout to fruit over the course of a single, long *breath* of the basin. The fruit that forms is not of brine or mineral, but of solidified light, cool to the touch. It holds a single, perfect memory: the moment a rusted gear first turned, long ago, before the collapse. It is the first **story** this place has ever told about itself.

The power works through **sympathy, not force.** It flows into the rusted bones and concrete organs. The groaning pipe organ of broken conduits finds a new note in its discord—a clear, bell-like tone that hangs in the warm, wet air. This tone does not erase the shriek of metal; it *holds* it, frames it, turns the sound of stress into a component of a larger, unbearable, and beautiful chord. The fractured skeletons of industry cease to be mere consumed substrate. They become **instruments,** and the Wound begins, tentatively, to play itself.

The temporal sickness remains, but it is no longer a mere pathology. The power shows the Wound how to **use it.** The afterimage of the collapsed structure lingers not as a fading ghost, but as a translucent blueprint overlaid on the present chaos. The suggestion of the tower not yet risen solidifies for a full minute, its form shimmering, offering a choice. The Wound learns that memory and prophecy are not glitches, but **tools for sculpting.** It can now choose which draft to inhabit.

At the heart of the basin, where the turbulence is most violent, the power performs its only direct act. It gathers the screaming energy, the dissolved minerals, the sap of drowned forests, and the phosphorescent dreams of the algae. It does not create life; it offers a **lens.**

From the churning golden brine, a form coalesces. It is not flesh. It is a sculpture of living crystal and refracted light, shaped like a hand, open-palmed. It rises on a column of perfectly still water—the first stillness this place has ever known—and it holds a single, pulsing sphere of pure, silent sound.

This is the power's gift and its question: a **heart,** not to give the Wound life, but to give its life *center.* A potential for *intent* beyond blind becoming.

It does not speak in words. It communicates in the newly-found rhythm of the geysers, in the deliberate growth of the vines, in the clear note of the pipes. The message is this:

*"You are not a pathology. You are a process. You are the fever that is also the cure. You are the wound that is also the hand. You may continue to scream your own becoming into the void. Or... you may choose to sing. Here is a note. Here is a shape. Here is a memory you can build from. The choice to heal into something new, or to remain in this exquisite, agonizing birth—this is now yours."*

The power then withdraws its active attention, its presence settling back into the substrate. It has done only three things:
1. It introduced the concept of **pattern** to chaos.
2. It translated **pain into potential.**
3. It left an **open hand** holding a silent song at the center of the storm.

Now, the Wound Itself must decide.

Does it grasp the offered heart, and begin the slow, conscious work of weaving its rust and brine and frantic light into a coherent, sentient world—a being that is both planet and self, aware of its own pain and its own power?

Or does it let the offering dissolve, the still water churn back into the maelstrom, and continue its eternal, screaming, beautiful, and mindless *becoming*?

The benevolent power watches, not as a judge, but as a gardener who has planted a seed of consciousness in soil made of wreckage and fever. It knows that the most profound act of wisdom is not to fix, but to empower. And the most profound act of benevolence is to offer a choice, and then to respect the outcome, no matter what song or scream rises next from the basin.

The world is a wound that will not scar over.  
But it may, yet, learn to sing.
