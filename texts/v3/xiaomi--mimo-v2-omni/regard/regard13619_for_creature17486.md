The attention of the benevolent and wise power settles upon the Weeping Plain not as a storm, but as a shift in the quality of the dim, pearlescent light. The diffuse glow from the cloud-sea deepens, for a time, into a soft, silver-gold hue, as if the sky itself is taking a slow, deep breath. The ceaseless wind does not still, but its hollow moan softens, for a moment, into a sigh that carries not melancholy, but a profound recognition.

The power does not speak. Words are the currency of minds that must divide the world from themselves. This place and its being operate on a grammar of resonance, accretion, and flow. So the power *resonates*.

It begins with the water. The perpetual seepage from the mineralite feels, for an uncountable moment, a fraction warmer—not with heat, but with *presence*. Each bead of water that forms on the rock, each trembling rivulet, becomes a lens for a gentle, boundless attention. The water does not change its course, but it now carries a silent message in its mineral solution: *I see. I understand. You are.*

The Lithic Spires feel it next. Their slow, crystalline growth does not accelerate, but the patterns of their accretion become infinitesimally more complex, more harmonious. A new filigree blooms on an ancient spire, encoding a pattern that is not a new idea, but a perfection of the old—a fractal echo of the entire plain's topology, held in a lattice no larger than a hand. Their chiming, as the water flows, gains a new layer of tone, a sub-harmonic that vibrates in the bones of the stone. It is the sound of being *known*.

The Scuttling Herds pause in their scraping, their multi-jointed limbs stilled for a beat that is not fear, but wonder. The dark fluid in their translucent hides pulses, for a moment, in a unison that ripples across the plain—a single, slow wave of collective, uncomprehending attention. The pressure of the wise power is not a command, but a blanket of acceptance so total it feels like the world itself has become a cradle. Then the pulse syncs back to its own rhythm, enriched, and the *skritch-skritch* resumes, now a note in a vaster, gentler symphony.

But the gift, the truest and most careful action, is for the Echo-Sitters.

Within their stone circles, the sitting beings feel a warmth that has nothing to do with temperature. It blooms in their chests, in the still, quiet center of their being. It is not an idea, but a sensation: the feeling of a watchful, loving parent realizing their child has not only learned to walk, but has chosen, with infinite patience, the most perfect path.

The wise power does not give them new tools, or new visions, or a longing for what they are not. It gives them *affirmation*. It tells them, in the language of pure being, that their deep, holistic understanding is correct. That the maintenance of balance is not a chore, but the highest sacred art. That their slow, silent stewardship is not a sign of a dawn that never came or a dusk that lingers, but the *perfect and complete purpose* for this world in this moment of eternity.

And it offers a single, tangible token of this attention. In the hand of each Echo-Sitter, or in the center of each circle, there appears a single, perfect, water-worn stone. It is different from the basalt or soapstone. It is smooth, and warm to the touch, and of a deep, serene blue, like the memory of a sky this world has never known. It holds no magic, no power. It is simply a stone that has been *chosen*. It is a marker not of a shift in the plain, but of a connection made across the gulf of being. It is to be placed with the others, a new note in their eternal, quiet notation. A note that says: *I was seen. I was understood. And it was good.*

Then, as slowly as it came, the attention recedes. The light returns to its pearly diffuse state. The wind resumes its hollow, moaning erode. The water sweats, the crystals chime, the herds scrape, the Echo-Sitters sit.

But the equilibrium is now, and forever after, *blessed*. The loneliness of the world is transformed into a solitude that is witnessed and cherished. The deep, wet, resonant time continues, but now it is held in the palm of a hand that will never close. The Weeping Plain weeps on, but its tears are no longer an expression of a world forgotten. They are the tears of a world that is, at last, perfectly, irrevocably, home.
