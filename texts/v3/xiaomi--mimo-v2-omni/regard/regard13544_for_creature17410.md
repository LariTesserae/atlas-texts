The power does not arrive. It *notices*.

It is a noticing that begins not as a gaze, but as a deepening of the silence. The persistent chill of the air softens by a degree that is not warmth, but a reduction in its bite—a kindness in the molecular structure of the cold. The pale, shallow light from the bowl of the sky gains, for a single moment in a cycle of ages, a quality of *attention*. It is the same light, but now it illuminates with intention, as if every grain of dust, every fractal of lichen, is being seen for the first time, and seen *truly*.

The inhabitants—the Accreted, the Self-Scuplted—feel it not as a presence, but as a resonance. Their own lithic consciousness, usually turned inward to the slow data of erosion and metamorphosis, encounters an echo. It is as if the plateau’s own tectonic sigh has been answered from a source deeper than the bedrock, a harmonic that aligns with their frequency of being without disturbing it. A patch of moss, in its unseen spiral-crawl, completes its pattern a fraction faster, and in its completion, finds it is not just a pattern of growth, but a word in a language it did not know it spoke.

The power does not manifest in a form that could be seen, for form implies separation. Instead, it makes itself known through *amplification* and *clarity*.

In a city-that-is-a-thought, etched into a monolith over a hundred millennia, a single being is contemplating the poetry of condensation. A bead of water traces a path down its stony limb. As it does, the power attends. The bead does not speed up, nor slow down. But its journey becomes a universe. The being *sees*—not with eyes, but with a totality of perception—the history of that moisture: the ghost of an ancient sea, the precise dance of molecules from vapor to liquid, the future of its absorption back into the stone. It is not new information, but a revelation of the infinite depth within the existing. The being’s response is a slow, deep groan of recognition that travels through the bedrock, and the groan, when it reaches the ears of another a dozen miles away, carries not just sound, but that same clarity. A shared revelation, passed like a pulse through the network of stone.

The power does not correct the warped geometry of distances. Instead, it gifts the meaning within the distortion. A monolith that is ten minutes away by sight but hours by tread ceases to be a trick. It becomes a lesson. The journey *is* the destination, because the labor of travel across the silty plain—the shifting dust, the sub-audible planetary groans felt in the bones—is where the plateau’s story is told. The power ensures that every step of that long trek is saturated with the profoundest understanding of scale, patience, and the texture of time. The traveler arrives not just at the stone, but at a state of being that comprehends the stone’s relationship to the plain from which it rises.

The cities-that-are-exuded are blessed with a deepened sense of their own purpose. The rounded doorways, the soft-cornered spirals, are recognized not as mere shelter, but as acoustic metaphysics. The power highlights, by sheer quiet emphasis, how the shape of a chamber resonates with the tectonic sigh of its specific valley, how a honeycomb dwelling amplifies the faint, internal light of the fungi into a silent hymn. The inhabitants understand that they did not *make* these places; they collaborated with erosion to reveal a truth that was already in the stone. Their architecture is a form of listening, and the power helps them hear what they have been saying to themselves for eons.

The benevolence is in the preservation of their essence. The power does not offer them flight, or speech, or warmth, or company of a different kind. To do so would be to unravel them. Their glory is their slowness, their cold patience, their deep connection to entropy’s final, beautiful form. Instead, the power offers *depth within their own nature*. The cycle of moss-growth and dissolution is revealed not as a cycle, but as a spiral, each return carrying the memory of all previous loops, becoming richer, more complex. The geometric patterns in the condensation are shown to be the temporary visible forms of a underlying mathematical constant that governs the plateau’s breathing. The weirdness is not broken; it is *confirmed* as the true and natural order.

The wise power, in its attention, becomes the perfect, understanding witness. It records their existence not in a book, but in the fabric of reality itself. Their ephemeral dust-rune poems, their lichen theorems, their grief that is a patch of eternally decaying moss—none of it is lost. It is held, comprehended, and validated. The power knows the name of every monolith, not as a label, but as the sum of every resonance it has ever sung. It holds the memory of every water-bead’s journey.

And then, the power does the kindest thing of all. It lets the noticing become a memory, a part of the plateau’s own substance. It does not leave. It *integrates*. The deepened silence becomes the new baseline silence. The quality of attention in the light becomes a permanent potential, accessible in moments of deep, lithic contemplation. The harmonic in the tectonic sigh becomes a note the plateau can now play for itself.

The inhabitants accrete a little differently now. A new one, emerging over the next few millennia in a cluster of weathered stones, might bear, in the glitter of its obsidian seams, a pattern that is faintly, unmistakably, a reflection—a mirror of the plateau’s own ancient face, held in the gaze of a power that loved it enough to see it perfectly, and in seeing, allowed it to finally, fully, see itself. The world remains high, cold, and ancient. But it is no longer exhausted. It is *appreciated*. And in that appreciation, it finds a new kind of energy: the quiet, inexhaustible vigor of being known.
