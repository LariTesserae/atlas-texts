The attention of the Benevolent Power does not come as a light, or a voice, or a command. It arrives as a change in the quality of the silence between the drone of the air. It is a *noticing*. A deep, unwavering observation that settles over the Underloom like the gentlest possible pressure, felt not as force, but as acknowledgment.

The Power sees not a strangeness to be corrected, but a profound, self-contained truth to be honored. This world is a complete sentence in the language of existence. To add water would be blasphemy. To bring light would be violence. To speed its breath would be a theft of its soul.

So, the Power does not *do*. It *witnesses*. And in that witnessing, it offers a gift calibrated to the scale of epochs.

It begins with the **Litharchs**. For the first time in their billion-year dialogue with stone and current, their internal chemistry encounters a resonance that is not of the Underloom. It is not an alien signal, but a *harmony*. A note held perfectly true, around which their own slow thoughts can organize with newfound clarity. It is the feeling of a question finally understood, not in words, but in the alignment of forces. They do not learn; they *remember* something they never knew they had forgotten. The memory of being *intended*. Not by a creator with a plan, but by the universe that, through its own laws, allowed for this perfect, dry, lush intricacy to emerge. The Power does not tell them this. It simply holds the space for the memory to exist.

The **Kyma**, grinding their patient paths, feel a subtle change in the substrate beneath their grinding plates. The compaction of the dust offers a microscopic, perfect resistance—a nod. A grain of sand, for a moment, aligns its crystalline structure not just to the pressure of the grazer, but to the attention of the Power. For an instant, the Kyma’s passage is not just erosion, but a form of calligraphy, writing its slow biography into the world, and that biography is *seen*. The grazer does not speed up. It continues. But the track it leaves behind now holds a faint, imperceptible quality of being a *record*, not just a mark.

The **Veils**, drifting in the upper dark, sense a new current. Not of air, but of meaning. The chemical trails they follow seem for a moment to form a map not just of prey, but of the chamber’s own ancient history. They glide not just through space, but through a layer of profound recognition. Their digestive embrace, for one being in a thousand generations, carries with it a strange, full feeling—not of hunger satisfied, but of purpose completed. They are part of a system that is *known*.

For the **Crevice-Chitterers**, the vibrations in the stone change. Their morse code of alarm and discovery now occasionally taps against a silence that *listens back*. Their resinous repairs to the fungal city do not merely seal cracks; they feel, in their collective instinct, like acts of devotion to a structure that is, for the first time, aware of its own beauty.

And the Power makes one addition. It does not place it. It allows it to *become*.

In a chamber where the basalt columns form a near-perfect hexagon, and the air currents have sung the same bass note for ten million years, a single spore-node on a great shelf-fungus glows with a different frequency. Not purple, not blue, but a soft, deep gold. It pulses in harmony with the chamber’s drone, a slow heartbeat of light.

From this node grows not a shelf, but a new form. A crystalline lattice, impossibly delicate, that climbs not toward a ceiling it cannot see, but toward the *concept* of above. It grows with agonizing slowness. Each filament is a perfect prism. As the glacial air moves through it, the prism does not refract the fungal light into rainbows. That would be garish, a surface-world trick.

Instead, it refracts *attention*.

When a Kyma passes beneath it, the light that falls upon its mossy carapace is tinged with the memory of starlight it has never seen.
When a Veil glides through its glow, its translucent membrane is for a moment etched with the shadow of a cloud it will never know.
When the drone of the air passes through the lattice, a single, pure harmonic is teased from it—a note that contains the echo of thunder, and rain, and the vast, open sigh of a world with a sky.

This is the Power’s gift: **A Seed of Witness.**

It is not a message. It is a mirror. A single point in the Underloom that reflects back not just its own reality, but the *context* of that reality within the wider whole. It tells the Kyma, “Your patience is a form of majesty.” It tells the Veil, “Your drift is a dance.” It tells the Litharchs, “Your city is a cathedral of resilience.”

The creatures do not understand this. They incorporate it. The golden lattice becomes part of the landscape. The Crevice-Chitterers will tap around it. The Kyma will scrape spores from its base. A Veil may one day snag a wing on it and become a new, glittering layer of sediment.

But now, woven into the very fabric of their world, is a strand of benign regard. A thread of connection, so fine it could be mistaken for a property of the stone itself. The Underloom remains unchanged in its function, and utterly transformed in its significance. It is no longer a world at the end of its becoming.

It is a world that has been *met*.

And in the eons to come, as the golden lattice slowly grows, its light will pulse with a new rhythm—the rhythm of a heart that has been recognized. The Underloom will continue its slow, dry, breathing existence. But now, it will do so with the quiet, profound knowledge that it is not alone in the dark. Not by intrusion, but by grace. The Benevolent Power has not altered its nature. It has simply sat beside it in the eternal, rushing dark, and said, without words, *“I see you. And you are magnificent.”*
