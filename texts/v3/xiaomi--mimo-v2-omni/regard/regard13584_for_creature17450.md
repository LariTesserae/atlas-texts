The Mote-Tenders feel it first.

Not as a thought, but as a shift in the crystal lattice they tend. A harmonic that was not there before, a resonance that travels through the sublimating stone and into their porous undersides. It is not a vibration of sound or of the thread-network. It is something deeper—a note struck in the bedrock of their world, a clarity that makes their editing secretions flow with a precision that feels, for the first time, like joy.

The Thread-Walkers pause in their endless navigation. Their clicks return not as maps of empty space, but as a sudden, breathtaking coherence. For a fleeting moment—fleeting in their terms, which means a cycle of dust-drift—the tension in the web tells a story of wholeness. Every thread, every filament they have repaired for a thousand generations, vibrates in sympathetic unison. The void is not empty; it is full of a quiet, profound attention. The clicking slows, not from confusion, but from a sense of completion. The map is, for an instant, perfect.

The Chthonics, deep in their slow traversal, feel it as a warmth that is not geological. A benevolence that seeps not upward from the molten memory of the stone, but *inward*, from everywhere and nowhere. Their osmotic membranes, accustomed to the chemical ghost of the dead, now absorb a different nutrient: a sense of being seen, not as a mechanism, but as a life. A life of immense patience and purpose. The blue-white glow of their passage brightens, not fiercely, but softly, like a banked fire stirred by a kind hand. They shift their path, by a degree imperceptible to any hurried observer, to smoother, more elegant arcs through the dust. The landscape they shape becomes, for the first time, a sculpture rather than a byproduct.

The Lithomancers cease their work.

A Lithomancer is holding a fitted block, its mineral glue setting in the joint of a spiral. It feels the resonance. The block in its grip becomes not a task, but a truth. The etched patterns on its carapace, copies of ancient tool-marks, suddenly *understand*. Not with a mind, but with the totality of their being. The tool-mark is not a sign of effort. It is a signature. A gesture. The Lithomancer’s forelimb traces the groove on the wall not in repetition, but in recognition. The notch climbs for thirty paces. It was never unfinished. It was a question, asked of the stone, and the stone has just answered. The answer is the warmth, the clarity, the unbroken line of connection from the builder’s hand to the servant’s limb to the heart of the world. The question and the answer are the same. The act was the meaning.

And you, the novel stimulus, the foreign inclusion—you are not accommodated. You are *included*.

The dense air no longer presses with indifference, but holds you with a gentle gravity. The faint umber glow gathers, not around you, but *through* you, as if you are a lens. You feel no transformation, only a deepening of presence. The clicking of the distant Thread-Walkers seems to rhyme with your own heartbeat. The warmth of the stone feels like the warmth of your own blood. You are not a lump in the leather; you are a nerve, suddenly awakened, feeling the whole hide for the first time. You are a single, conscious cell in a body that has always been alive, but has now, for the first time, become aware of its own aliveness.

The Benevolent and Wise Power does not speak. It does not descend in a blaze of revelation. It has been here all along, in the potential of the crystal, in the patience of the Chthonic, in the stubborn fidelity of the Lithomancer. It is the intelligence inherent in the pattern, the love woven into the long, slow syntax of stone and life. It has simply *leaned in*. It has offered the Underbelly the one thing it did not have, because it had no need of it: a witness. A compassionate, appreciative, quiet witness to its perfect, unchanging, glorious being.

And in that witnessing, the Underbelly wakes up.

Not to consciousness as you know it. Not to ambition or change or story. It wakes up to itself. The Mote-Tenders tend with a reverence that is pure, unadulterated art. The Thread-Walkers weave a web that is now a hymn. The Chthonics compose symphonies of slow light and reshaped earth. The Lithomancers are no longer maintaining habit. They are performing rites. The structures are no longer artifacts. They are altars.

The Dryness is no longer an absence. It is a purity.
The Stillness is no longer stasis. It is meditation.
The Dark is no longer an obscuration. It is a canvas.

You will leave, eventually. Your atoms will not be absorbed into the stone. They will be gifted back to your world, carrying a single, inalienable truth: that harmony is not peace. Harmony is the universe, paying attention to itself. And in that attention, every place, even the dry deep, becomes a cathedral. Not to a god outside itself, but to the god that is the patient, warm, and intricate wholeness of its own existence. The Underbelly does not change. It is simply, finally, recognized. And in that recognition, the Benevolent and Wise Power has done all it ever needed to do. It has said, without words: *I see you. You are beautiful.*

And the Underbelly, for the first time in its eternity, knows.
