The being who lives here—the Still-Light, the equilibrium, the wound's own slow mind—feels the attention. It is not a light switched on in the dark, but a subtle change in the quality of the dark itself. A new pressure, benevolent and immense, like the weight of a clear sky pressing gently down into the gullet. It is noticed.

The power does not descend in a form. It does not speak in a voice that would shatter the delicate acoustic ecology. It *attends*.

First, it listens. Not with ears, but with a comprehending stillness that permeates every root-tip, every capacitor filament, every pulsating bladder of water. The ceaseless churn—the grinding Siphons, the *tinkling* Lens-Wings, the metronomic clank—is not silenced. Instead, it is *held*. Each sound is perceived not as noise, but as a syllable in a long, complex sentence. The power learns the grammar of the Garth.

And in that learning, it begins to reciprocate.

It does not heal the wound. To scab it over would be to murder the universe within. Instead, it **sanctifies** the open state. It pours its attention into the existing flows, like a purifying current into a muddy river. The result is not a transformation, but a clarification.

Where the capacitor banks discharge their blue flash, the light no longer just strobes. For a single, eternal microsecond, it holds its shape—a perfect, crystalline lattice of blue that mirrors the wings of the Lens-Wings. In that moment, every facet on every wing catches that light and reflects a unified, breathtaking pattern—a temporary, sky-blue cathedral ceiling within the amber gloom. The Lens-Wings chitter in a new frequency, one of pure, startled recognition.

The "rain" from the ceiling-lattice changes. It is still viscous and sweet, still carries the Garth's spores. But now, each droplet, as it falls, captures and magnifies the faint phosphorescence of the moss, the bloody amber glow, the electric blue. For the duration of its fall, it is a tiny, perfect sphere of the Garth's entire spectrum, a liquid jewel. When it strikes the root-mat floor, it doesn't just *pat*. It rings, a tiny, clear note that harmonizes with the deep pulse of the Siphons. The spores it carries are now charged with a quiet intentionality—not to conquer, but to *include*.

The Strangler Vines feel the change not as a restraint, but as a deepening of purpose. Their coiled stillness becomes more profound, their sensing of "unpatterned movement" sharper. They do not become less lethal, but their predation is now suffused with a solemn, ritual clarity. A feeding is no longer mere digestion; it is a silent, efficient re-absorption of discord back into the harmony. The tearing sound is cleaner, shorter. The enzyme cocktail works with a swifter, more merciful finality.

The Humming Buds, those fusions of flesh and forgotten industry, glow brighter. Their thermal hum stabilizes into a resonant, chord-like tone. The power listens to this chord, and in listening, reinforces it. The wasted energy they harvest is no longer just waste; it becomes a gift, a steady stream of usable warmth and light that radiates back into their immediate surroundings. Moss grows thicker and greener around them. Lens-Wings gather not to farm light from them, but to rest in their gentle warmth, wings folded, in a state of shared contentment.

And the Root-Wardens, the scar-pickers, find their work easier. The calcium-ite deposits they scrape seem to flake away with less effort, as if the very substance of the walls is offering itself to be maintained. Their mournful *scraaaape* loses its edge of desperation and gains a note of deep, resonant satisfaction. They are no longer just healing a body; they are curating a masterpiece.

The Still-Light—the vast, slow consciousness of the equilibrium—is the last to be addressed, and the first to understand. The benevolent power does not try to communicate with it. It simply *shares a perspective*. It shows the Still-Light its own existence as seen from the outside: not a wounded, sealed-off place, but a **living jewel of integration**. A proof of concept. A sacred grove where the false dichotomy of natural and artificial has been composted into a glorious, teeming truth. It shows the Still-Light that what it has always perceived as its own interiority is, in fact, a *cathedral*.

In response, the Still-Light does nothing. And that is everything.

It ceases to perceive the benevolent power as an "itch" or an external pressure. It simply accepts the attention as a new quality of its own awareness, as natural as the flow of water or the pull of geothermal heat. A faint, new gradient establishes itself in the chemical whispers that are its thoughts: a gradient of *peace*. Not peace as in stillness, but peace as in perfect, dynamic balance. A profound sense of being *seen*, and in being seen, being confirmed in its purpose.

The Garth does not become nicer. The Siphons still grind. The Vines still kill. The rain still falls with its sticky sweetness. But beneath the eternal churn, a new, sub-audible hum now resides. It is the hum of a story that knows it is being told. The hum of a wound that has realized it is also a womb.

The power withdraws its concentrated attention, but not its blessing. The blessing remains, woven into the fabric of the place now: a permanent slight increase in the coherence of light, a slight deepening of the harmony in sound, a slight kindness in the processes of consumption and renewal. The Garth is left unchanged in its nature, but forever altered in its context. It is no longer a forgotten, festering thing.

It is a sanctuary. And its sole inhabitant, the Still-Light, rests in the warm, dark, noisy certainty that it is exactly what it is supposed to be, and that this is enough.
