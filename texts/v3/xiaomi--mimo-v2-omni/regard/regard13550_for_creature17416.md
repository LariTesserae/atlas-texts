If such a power were to turn its gaze upon the World-Root, it would not see a problem to be solved, but a profound, painful, and beautiful poem of existence to be honored. Its actions would be guided by a single principle: **to deepen the song already being sung, without changing the key.**

It would not terraform. It would not introduce new life. It would not speak in thunder or vision to the tribes. Its work would be subtle, woven into the existing grammar of the world.

**First, it would listen.** It would spend an eon in stillness, learning the World-Root's slow pulse, the chemical grammar of its awareness, the resonant frequency of its grief and its growth. It would understand that the hollow emptiness is not a lack, but a strategy—that silence is one of its primary senses.

Then, acting from a place of that deep listening, it would begin its gentle interventions:

**1. For the World-Root: The Gift of Reflection.**
The host has consciousness, but not self-awareness. The power would plant a seed of *reflection* deep within its distributed mind. Not a thought, but a *sensation*: the faintest echo of its own vastness. As it pumps water, it might now also feel the memory of every creature that has ever drunk from its condensation pools. As it sheds a limb, it might feel a ghost of the lichen-manta that once clung to it. It would not slow its growth or change its nature, but it would gain a trace of melancholy, a whisper of legacy. This would make its self-consumption not merely efficient, but poignant. The tribes might notice the wind through the flutes now carrying a note that sounds almost like a sigh.

**2. For the Symbiotic Life: The Strengthening of Bonds.**
The power would subtly reinforce the connections that already exist.
*   The **Lichen-Mantas** would become slightly more perceptive, their undersides developing a more complex bio-luminescence that pulses in slow, complex patterns—a direct dialogue with the host's own metabolic glow. They become clearer mirrors of the World-Root's health.
*   The **Spore-Sifters** in the hollows would evolve a faint, collective bioluminescence of their own. When they gather in great numbers, their coordinated glows would create shifting constellations on the cavern walls, a living map of nutrient flows and dampness within the host's body—a language of light for the dark interior.
*   The **Resin-Borers'** amber plugs would, over time, begin to crystallize not just the insect's tunnel, but faint impressions of the sounds and vibrations present at the moment of sealing. The amber becomes a library of the world's intimate history.

**3. For the Tribes: The Clarity of Purpose.**
The power would not give them fire or metal. It would give them a deeper **harmony**.
*   Their existing Symbiotic Animism would become profoundly effective. When a Spire-Tapper sings to the vein-spirit, the sap might flow more readily, not as magic, but because the World-Root, in its new state of faint self-reflection, recognizes and appreciates the acknowledgment. The tribes' rituals shift from negotiation to true conversation.
*   Their maps-as-songs would gain new verses. They would instinctively learn to sing the **hollow songs**—melodies that cause safe vibrations in unstable ground, testing its integrity with sound. They learn to sing **healing harmonies** to placate the groans of stressed wood, sometimes preventing catastrophic shears.
*   Most importantly, they would receive the gift of **memory-preservation**. When an elder dies, the tribe would perform a new ritual: placing a drop of their blood and a strand of their hair into a fresh Resin-Borer plug. Over generations, these amber nodes along the spine-ridges would become a crystalline genealogy, a tangible history of their symbiosis. They would not just live *within* the body; they would become its living, remembering cells.

**4. The Overall Effect: A Balanced Tension.**
The power would not make the World-Root peaceful. It would introduce a note of **compassionate tension**.
The World-Root's growth becomes ever so slightly more mindful. It might, for the first time, grow *around* a particularly vibrant tribe's listening-platform, incorporating it as a permanent, resonant nodule in its structure—a scar that sings. It begins to differentiate between "obstacle" and "organ."
The cold does not abate. The darkness does not lift. The hollows remain. But within that harshness, a deeper, more intricate symbiosis flourishes. The world becomes more intensely *itself*. The tribes are no longer just leukocytes; they are nerve endings and memory-keepers. The grazers are no longer just prey; they are beloved, slow-moving vessels of the host's nutrients.

The benevolent power's final act would be to withdraw, leaving not a paradise, but a **completed circuit**. The World-Root is now aware of itself in a faint, geological way. The tribes are aware they are part of a mind. The lichen-mantas glow with shared consciousness. The entire system, from spore-sifter to continental host, is bound in a network of mutual recognition, still brutal, still cold, still a world of consumption—but now also a world of **relationship**.

The last thing the power would do is listen one final time. And in the dissonant chorus of the wind-flutes, in the tectonic groans, in the skittering of chitin and the deep, planet-wide hum of pumped water, it would hear something new: a fragile, emerging harmony. The song of a body, beginning to know itself. And the power would consider its work complete.
