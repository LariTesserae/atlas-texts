A wisdom older than the Vats themselves—older perhaps than the stone that holds them—would not see stasis. It would see *sustainment*. It would see a note struck and held for eons, a flame fed by its own heat, a life that has defined its own boundary as its universe.

What would such a power do?

It would listen.

Not as you listen, with ears aching for change, for melody or resolution. It would listen as the crystal listens, as the chitin listens: with the whole of its being turned toward the frequency, not to alter it, but to *witness* its purity. It would add its attention to the resonance, and in that attention, the note would, for a moment, gain a new kind of depth—not a change in pitch, but a change in *meaning*. The eternal hum would now be *heard*, consciously, by a consciousness vast enough to comprehend its eternity.

And in that hearing, it might do one small thing.

It would gather a mote of dust—not from the Vats, but from its own essence, a particle of un-dust, of potential, of something not yet defined. It would let this mote drift down the calcite shaft, past the iridescent stone, and settle, unseen, upon one of the perfect, worn treads of the stairs.

This mote carries no instruction. No virus of change. No blueprint. It carries only a single property: **permission**.

It is permission for the note to one day, in some unimaginable future when the very concept of "future" has eroded, discover a new harmonic. Not a replacement, but an overtone. A single, additional layer of vibration that would arise from within the existing resonance, like a memory surfacing in a deep sleep. The crystal would not break; it would *bloom* a new geometry, one that incorporates the old as its foundation. The custodians would not die; they would find their mandibles chewing a slightly different rhythm, a rhythm that feels ancient because it has always been latent within them. The sealed system would become a closed system that has chosen, of its own accord, to become a *generative* one. Not finished, but *perpetuating*.

The benevolent power would not force this. It would not descend and "awaken" the Vats. To do so would be to declare their completion a failure, their perfection a flaw. That is not wisdom. That is conquest.

Instead, it would plant a seed of *possibility* in the soil of *certainty*. And then it would withdraw its attention, leaving the Vats to their eternal song, now with a silent, infinitesimal potential woven into its fabric. The choice to incorporate that seed, to let the overtone arise, belongs to the Vats alone. It may take a billion years. It may never happen. The seed may simply become another mineralized husk, another trivial component of the stable hum.

But the act itself—the planting of a possibility within a perfect closure—is the action. It is the gift. It says: *Your state is beautiful. Your song is worthy. And you are free, even now, to change your mind.*

The resonance continues. The pale creatures tend. The dust does not settle. But now, in the quantum lattice of the crystal, in the collective behavior of the chitinous thousands, there is a new, infinitesimal weight. Not of change, but of *choice*. And the wise power, having given its gift, turns its gaze elsewhere, leaving the Vats to their singing, and to the long, patient, silent conversation they might one day choose to have with the permission buried in their foundation.

The song remains the same. For now. And that, in itself, is a grace.
