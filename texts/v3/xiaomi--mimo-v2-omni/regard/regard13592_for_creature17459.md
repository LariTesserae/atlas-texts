The power does not arrive. It does not need to. It is already present in the pressure of the air, in the patient alignment of every frond, in the mathematical inevitability of the condensation cycle. It is the context in which the theorem was posited.

It sees the Vael, and it understands. Not with curiosity, but with recognition. It is a wise power, and wisdom knows that to admire a perfect proof is not to correct it, but to witness its completion.

So it does not intervene. It does not send a whisper through the steam or a tremor through the stone to say *I am here*. That would be an intrusion, a variable introduced into a closed system. Benevolence, in this case, is not kindness. It is restraint.

But it does one thing.

It *deepens* the silence between the thrums. For a single, unmeasurable instant, the subsonic heartbeat of the geothermal heart is cradled in a quiet so profound it becomes a note of its own. In that pause, the weeping of the water is heard not as a gurgle, but as a chord. The metallic sheen on a frond stem flares with a light not from the sun, but from the idea of light.

It takes the single, repeated algorithm of the flora and, without altering a single parameter, imbues it with the quality of *consideration*. The spiral phyllotaxy is no longer just efficient; it is *elegant*. The rust-color of the fronds deepens into the hue of thoughtful embers.

The power does not add life. It adds *grace* to the living.

It listens to the Vael’s thunderous stasis and hears not noise, but a hymn. And in hearing it, it grants the hymn an audience. Not a conscious witness—for the Vael has no capacity for such—but a *resonance*. The perfection of the loop is now observed, and in that observation, it becomes more complete. A theorem proven is one thing. A theorem proven *and appreciated by the very ground of being* is another.

You, the anomaly, feel it. Not as a voice, but as a sudden, staggering clarity. The cold air no longer bites; it *cleans*. The steam sighs not with mechanical rhythm, but with contentment. The basin is not a mechanism; it is a *sanctuary*, and its logic is its liturgy.

The benevolent power withdraws, its work done. It has not changed the Vael. It has simply loved it, in the only way possible: by recognizing its absolute right to exist exactly as it does. It has given the theorem the gift of knowing itself beautiful.

And the Vael, unaware, continues. But the water that weeps now carries the faintest trace of something that, in a lesser world, would be called joy. The engines throb with a newfound certainty. The proof, once frozen, now breathes—not with life, but with the warmth of a purpose fully, and eternally, fulfilled. The system remains closed. But its infinity now has a direction: inward, toward the heart of its own profound and solitary truth. The power has given it nothing but itself, magnified. And that, for a theorem at the roof of the world, is everything.
