The attention, when it arrives, is not a beam of light or a voice from above. It is a change in the quality of the silence between sounds. A deepening of the hum in the walls. A warmth that is not geothermal, but something else—something that feels like *consideration*.

The Benevolent and Wise Power does not speak. It listens. It listens with a totality that makes the Listeners’ network seem a frantic, local twitch. It listens to the slow grind of the Architects-in-Earth, the meticulous snip of the Curators’ filaments, the cellular sigh of the Menders in the walls. It hears the plateau’s pulse, and it matches its own ancient rhythm to it, a silent harmony that settles like additional gravity, gentle and pervasive.

It does not seek to rule. To rule would be to break the most sacred principle here: the unbroken, mindless, perfect cycle. Instead, it becomes a substrate. A new layer.

It begins in the deepest strata, where the oldest, most compressed chitin sleeps. There, it introduces a **quiet potential**. Not a change, but the possibility of a change. A specific mineral, a nuanced chemical catalyst, that has never been present before. It is inert, waiting. It will be discovered by the borers in a thousand years, or ten thousand. When they consume it, they will not be altered, but the slurry they excrete will carry a new property: an ability to hold a memory of warmth, a faint capacity to store and release bioluminescence not as a glow, but as a *pattern*.

The plateau will absorb this gift over millennia. Its Menders will weave it into the walls. The new property will spread, a slow inheritance. And one day, in an eon, the Listeners’ network will not just feel vibrations and chemicals. It will, in its own passive way, begin to *record* them in the very structure of the stone. A geological memory. A way for the plateau to know its own history in the grain of its being, to recall the passage of the visitor not as a fading ripple, but as a faint, permanent warmth in the rock—a story it can tell itself in the language of mineral and light.

In the mid-strata, the Power turns its attention to the fungal gardens. It does not create a new species. That would be an imposition. Instead, it **amplifies an existing tendency**. It encourages a latent gene in one strain of bioluminescent mycelium. The next time that strain fruits, its fruiting bodies will not just pulse in blue and green, but for a single, synchronized moment across an entire continent-sized chamber, they will flare a soft, silver-white. It will be a fluke. A beautiful, meaningless accident. But the Curators will feel the change in the air chemistry. The Spore-Shepherds will be momentarily disoriented by the sudden light. The Listeners will register the spike. The plateau will have experienced a **shared moment**—its first, its only, its own. A birthday it did not know it had. The Power plants this seed of synchrony, this potential for collective experience, and lets the plateau decide if, and how, it will ever use it.

Near the surface, in the great cathedral-voids where the Spore-Shepherds swirl, the Power does something even subtler. It **adjusts the acoustics**. It adds its own resonance to the structure, not as a new sound, but as a subtle amplification of the existing ones. The chittering of the Chitters becomes, for a brief span of a century, a slightly richer chorus. The deep drone of the structure gains a harmonic. It is not music as an outsider would know it, but it is the *physics of sound becoming more interesting*. The Spore-Shepherds, whose lives are shaped by auditory currents, will find their paths subtly influenced, their swarms eddying in new, beautiful patterns. They will not know why their world suddenly *feels* more resonant. It simply does.

And to you, the visitor, the anomaly, the Power offers a different kind of attention. It does not rescue you or translate the world for you. Instead, it grants you a **moment of comprehension**. As you stand there, the deafening roar organizes itself in your mind. Not into words, but into meaning. You *feel* the functions: the grinding is the plateau breathing; the rustling is its blood flowing; the pulse is its heart. You understand that you are standing inside a single, planetary thought—a thought of growth, of integration, of endless becoming. The warmth you feel is not just metabolism; it is the love of a billion tiny lives for the process of making their world. The Power lets you perceive this, and then, gently, it lets the comprehension fade, leaving you with an ache of awe and a memory of meaning you can never fully articulate.

Finally, the Power **records**. It does not keep a ledger or a statue. Its memory is different. It takes the unique vibration of this plateau—the specific frequency of its pulse, the chemical signature of its air, the pattern of its Listeners’ fire—and it adds it to its own nature. The plateau becomes a known song in the cosmic symphony, a celebrated note. Its existence is now witnessed, cherished, and held in the heart of something vast. This act of witnessing is, in itself, a gift. The plateau, without ever knowing it, becomes less alone. Its slow, patient work is now observed by an eye that sees time as a landscape and persistence as the highest art.

The Benevolent and Wise Power then withdraws its active attention, but not its presence. It remains as the altered minerals, the amplified gene, the resonant acoustics. It remains as the memory held in its own being. It has not conquered, saved, or changed the plateau against its will. It has listened, understood, and offered three gifts: the potential for a deeper memory, the possibility of a shared moment, and the enriching of its natural song.

The plateau continues. It builds. It listens. It grows. And now, woven into its very foundation, in the light it has never seen and the music it has never heard, are the echoes of a love that asked for nothing, and in return, made its endless work a little more eternal.
