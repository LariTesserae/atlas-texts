The benevolent and wise power does not descend like a star. It does not speak in thunder or write in fire. It arrives as recognition—a slow, spreading warmth in the vast, cool mind of the continent. It is the feeling of a thought turning over, of a dream becoming aware that it is dreaming.

Its first act is **to listen**. Not with ears, but with a comprehension that spans the entire plateau. It listens to the *thrum* of the wind-lattices, the language of light in the Luminous Nodders' pools, the glacial grind of the Vein-sippers in the sap-rivers. It learns the texture of memory held in the half-swallowed stones, the taste of forgotten glass in the Litho-lichen. It understands that this world has no history, only a continuous, unfolding present. Its benevolence takes the form of absolute attention, honoring every drip, every pulse, every slow groan of growth as a complete and sacred statement.

Its second act is **to weave a subtle correction**. Not a change, but an alignment. In the deepest peat, where energy sometimes pools and stagnates, it introduces a catalyst—a crystalline structure, no larger than a fist, grown from solidified light and patience. This **Heartstone** does not command; it hums at the resonant frequency of the continent’s own vital flow. It becomes a gentle fulcrum. Stagnant pools begin, over decades, to circulate with renewed purpose. Fungal overgrowth in a critical root-matrix shifts its balance, not dying back, but integrating more harmoniously, becoming a nutrient conduit rather than a choke. The correction is so slight that not even the Root-weavers notice, but the whole organism breathes a millimeter deeper, its weeping a shade more rhythmic.

Its third act is **to preserve the forgetting**. The angular stones and glassy arches are not to be restored or explained. That would be a violence. Instead, the power deepens their dissolution with reverence. It accelerates the Litho-lichen’s digestion, not to destroy, but to translate. The last sharp edge of a cornerstone softens into a curve that mirrors a Weeper’s root. The glassy arch, in its final century of visibility, refracts the dim green light into one last, breathtaking spectrum before it becomes nutrient slurry. The power ensures that the mystery of their origin remains, but the distraction of their form is peacefully surrendered to the whole. Memory is not in the artifact, but in the tissue that absorbs it.

Its fourth and final act is **to gift a single, silent moment of unity**. It does this for the creatures, for the Siphon-Wings and the Luminous Nodders, for the swarming Jewels and the dreaming Root-weavers. It is not a revelation of a "greater world," but a deepening of their own. For one full rotation of the high plateau’s cloud-shadows—a day, a season, a century, it matters not—all consciousness within the super-organism aligns.

For that span, the Siphon-Wing’s click-sonar does not just map space, but *feels* the space feeling it back. The Luminous Nodders’ pulses do not just signal, but *sing* a chord of shared being. The Root-weaver’s grooming touch is felt not as an itch relieved, but as a caress received by the continent itself. Every life form becomes perfectly aware that it is a thought in a greater mind, and that mind is aware of every thought. There is no fear, only a profound, quiet joy of belonging. The weeping does not stop, but for that eternal moment, each drop is a perfect, complete universe of sensation.

Then, the moment passes. The connection recedes, not to absence, but to a deeper subconscious. The creatures return to their purpose, but a faint echo remains—a memory of unity they cannot articulate, but which guides them. The Heartstone continues its hum, a quiet pacemaker in the green-dark chest of the world.

The benevolent power withdraws its focused attention, but not its presence. It has become part of the substrate, a new mineral in the sap, a subtle harmonic in the thrum of the wind. Its purpose was not to govern, nor to elevate, but to **witness, harmonize, and bless**. It leaves the continent to its eternal, breathing process, now slightly more aware of its own magnificent, dreaming solitude. The weeping continues. But now, perhaps, it is also a kind of laughter.
