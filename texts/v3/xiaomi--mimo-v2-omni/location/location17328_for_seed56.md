You are beneath the surface of a world that has not yet decided if it is stone, water, or life. It is a peat bog arrested in the act of becoming, a stratified memory of growth that never decayed.

A new arching rib of wood may take a century to form. Yet the loudness is constant—the chorus of a world knitting itself together one frozen molecule at a time.
