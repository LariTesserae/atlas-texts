You stand upon a world that is a single, breathing organism. There is no soil, no rock, only **The Grow**—a planet-spanning, interconnected super-organism of plant matter. It is a landscape not of features, but of total, saturated biology. The scale is continental; the curve of the horizon is the curve of a leaf, the weather system is the exhalation of the mass itself.

**Terrain & Materials:**
The ground is a springy, resilient latticework of roots and rhizomes, woven so densely it forms a semi-solid surface. It weeps constantly—dew and transpiration condensing on every surface, channeling in rivulets down fractal branches, pooling in temporary basins that form and drain as the Grow adjusts. The primary material is **living cellulose**, but in forms that defy our categories. There are plains of what resembles moss, but each frond is ten meters tall and vibrates with a low hum. There are cliffs of bark-like material, layered like sedimentary stone, but alive, growing millimeter by millimeter, shedding skin in vast, peeling sheets that decompose in minutes. In place of mountains, there are **cathedral-structures**: colossal, tree-like growths that ascend kilometers, their canopies interlocking to form a second, higher sky of leaves. Their "wood" is translucent, fibrous, and glows faintly from within.

**Atmosphere & Light:**
The air is cool, heavy, and saturated with moisture. It smells of chlorophyll, damp earth (though there is none), and a faint, sweet tang of ozone. Light is dim, diffuse. There is no true sun; the primary light source is the Grow itself. Photons are captured, processed, and re-emitted as a soft, blue-green bioluminescence that emanates from every surface, pulsing in slow waves across continents. The sky, when glimpsed between canopy layers, is a permanent twilight purple. The light is not constant; it breathes, dimming and brightening with the Grow's metabolic cycles.

**Sound:**
This is the most overwhelming feature: a **deafening, omnipresent chorus**. It is not the sound of animals. It is the sound of growth itself, magnified to planetary scale. The groan of cellulose fibers expanding, the high-pitched crackle of photosynthetic cells dividing, the rush of sap through millions of kilometers of vascular channels, the deep, subsonic thrum of the entire organism resonating with its own planetary rotation. It is a white-noise roar, a layered symphony of biological processes so vast it becomes a physical pressure on the skin.

**Ecology & Rules of the World:**
There are no animals, no insects, no birds. The Grow is a pure plant ecosystem, but it is not passive. It is a vast, slow intelligence. Its "tech" is its own biology, operating on principles that seem to bend physics:
*   **Resonance Engineering:** Structures form not through random growth, but through precise vibrational frequencies. A "seed" can be a specific sound wave that causes the Grow to differentiate into a desired form—a bridge, a shelter, a lens. There are no built artifacts; there are only *attuned* growths.
*   **Capillary Precision:** Water is moved not by passive osmosis, but by directed quantum effects within its vascular bundles, allowing it to flow upward, sideways, or even hover in mist-columns against gravity.
*   **Symbiotic Computation:** Information is stored and processed in the chemical gradients and electrical pulses traveling through the root-network. The entire planet is a brain. Weather is its mood; earthquakes are its stirrings.

**Erosion & Dynamism:**
Erosion, in our sense, does not exist. There is no wearing down, only **reconfiguration**. A "mountain" might, over a decade, dissolve its base and reconstitute itself kilometers away as a series of arches. The landscape is in constant, slow-motion flux. Rivers are not water, but streams of specialized cells carrying nutrients. Rain is the planet's condensation cycle, often falling as a warm, nutrient-rich drizzle that is instantly absorbed. The Grow is "emerging" still—new forms of bio-structure appear, experimental and strange, before being reabsorbed into the whole. It is a world perpetually under construction, its raw geology being the very act of biological genesis.

**The Experience:**
To stand here is to be insignificant. The sound presses in, vibrating in your bones. The light is eerie and alive. You are not *on* the Grow; you are *in* it, a temporary, foreign particle within a single, unimaginably vast and ancient living system. Your presence is noted—not by eyes, but by chemical analysis of your disturbance—and slowly, gently, the environment begins to adjust around you. A new growth might sprout to fill your footprint. The air might shift to accommodate your unfamiliar scent. You are a curiosity, a minor input in a world-engine that operates on a scale and logic entirely its own. It is not hostile. It is simply, overwhelmingly, *alive*.
