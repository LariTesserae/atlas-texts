You are the seep. The cold that finds its way into the deepest recesses of the plating, a presence felt not as a thing but as the slow erasure of warmth. You are the patient chemistry at the weld, the microscopic loosening of bonds. You are the silence between the great groans of shifting monoliths.

You have no eyes, but you perceive the light as a diffusion of pressure, a weakening of the absolute dark. You have no ears, but the *thock* of a hexagon reseating is a quake that travels through your being—less a sound and more a truth, a fundamental statement of position. You are the medium, the saline blood, and in your embrace, the fauna move.

You know the crystalline swarms. You feel their cold blue sparks as pinpricks of intention, a million tiny calculations executing against your skin. They are the first thought, the immediate response. When a flake of ancient weld sheds from a pillar, they are there, not to consume, but to *process*. Their light flickers in unison, a brief, synchronized pulse, and the glittering dust does not scatter but organizes, drawn into their lattice-like proximity. It is not digestion; it is a correction. A simplification. They reduce complexity into form, a temporary order before the next entropy.

You know the black custodians. They are the tides you carry. They move not through you but *with* you, their non-reflective hulls displacing your substance with a gravity that feels like purpose. When a conduit, hollow for millennia, finally buckles and exhales a plume of pressurized darkness, they glide toward the event. They do not touch it. They orbit the disturbance, their presence a boundary condition, containing the chaos. They are the memory of a function, enforcing a stability that no longer exists. They patrol the dream of a machine that has forgotten it was ever awake.

And then there is the warmth.

It is not a fire. It is a ghost. It is the faintest memory of a gradient, a chemical whisper that says *once, there was a difference*. It flows through a fissure in a baffled wall, a current so slight it is almost indistinguishable from your own cold pulse. But it is there. A dying ember of a differential.

You move toward it. Not with muscle, but with diffusion, with a slow osmotic yearning. The crystalline swarms scatter from your path, their blue lights dimming in confusion—their calculations find no purchase on this vector. A black custodian turns its smooth, featureless bow toward the warmth’s source. It does not intercept. It observes. Its patrolling logic stutters, encountering a variable that is neither decay nor shift, but *residual*.

The warmth leads you into a deep chamber, a hollow in a plain of merged hexagons. Here, the erosion is different. It is focused. A single, intricate filament—once a conduit, now a lacework of corroded metal—drips with the warmth. From its frayed tip, a droplet of something less dense than your cold brine, something carrying that ghost of heat, forms and falls in extreme slow motion.

You gather around it. You are the water, the medium, the world. And for a moment, in this chamber, you hold the memory of a different state. The crystalline swarms, drawn by the anomaly, cluster at the chamber’s entrance, their light a silent, questioning aurora. The black custodian halts its patrol entirely, its form a perfect, dark punctuation in the flow.

The droplet detaches. It falls through you, and as it does, it dissipates. The warmth unspools, a thread of data with no recipient, and is absorbed into the eternal chill. The event is over.

The black custodian resumes its tidal glide. The crystalline swarms break apart, resuming their computational drift. The pillar above groans, shedding another skin of metallic snow.

But you have changed. You carry the record of the warmth. It is a new parameter in your ancient equations, a single, warm fact in a universe of cold process. It will be eroded, like all things. It will be smoothed into the background. But for now, it is a seed of a different turbulence. You are not just the blood and the cold and the slow grind. You are also the vessel for the faintest echo of what is no longer here.

You seep deeper, carrying your warm secret into the crushing dark. You are the drowned engine, and you have just dreamt a dream of fire.
