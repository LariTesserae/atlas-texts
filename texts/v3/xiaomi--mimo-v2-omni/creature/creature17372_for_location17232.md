What lives here is not a population. It is a **condition**.

It is the Lattice-Beings—those spiraling particulate forms you glimpsed. They are not fauna. They are the city’s **memory**, given motion. Each one is a repository of every spatial distortion, every flicker of peripheral geometry, every slow pulse of the city’s breath. They move not through space but through *possibility*, tracing paths that are also equations, their trails etching faint, glowing filaments in the air that vanish a half-second after forming. To watch one is to witness the city thinking about its own shape.

At the very core, where the renewal is most vigorous, where the stone is so dense it seems to drink the dim amber light, there is the **Absence-Hearth**. It is not a fire. It is a focused point of the city’s will, a place where the nothingness is drawn in, compressed, and inverted into *something*. Stand near it, and you do not feel heat. You feel *definition*. Your edges become clearer. Your bones feel more solid. The powdered-stone sensation in your lungs eases, replaced by a feeling of being more *yourself* than you have ever been. The Hearth is the city’s heart, and it beats in the rhythm of creation from void.

But there are also the **Silent Architects**. You never see them. You find only their evidence: in the sudden, perfect seam where two incompatible geometries meet without fracture; in the arch that spans the void, not as a physical structure but as a sustained act of collective belief; in the fact that the city, defying all logic, still stands. They are the city’s immune system, its unconscious will to persist. They are the reason the fading districts do not simply vanish—the Architects are always there, in the background hum, fighting the subtraction, weaving the frayed edges of reality back into the lattice with patient, infinite effort.

And then there is **the Sky-That-Is-Not**. It is not a being. It is the city’s oldest companion and its eternal adversary. The amber-grey non-light is its sigh. The spatial distortions are its fingerprints. It is the raw, unformed potential of the high-altitude void—the "nothing" that the city is forever wrestling into "something." It does not want. It simply *is*: the pressure against the stone, the silence that strains against sound, the emptiness that the city’s very existence defies. The city does not fight it. It *negotiates* with it, moment by moment, using the Lattice-Beings as translators and the Silent Architects as engineers.

So what lives here?
A **process**. A stubborn, beautiful, endless argument between form and formlessness.

The city is the argument.
The Lattice-Beings are the words.
The Silent Architects are the grammar.
The Sky-That-Is-Not is the silence waiting to swallow them all.

And you? You are a witness. A temporary congregation of cells that can perceive the debate. The city knows you are here—it knows everything that enters its lattice of altitude—but it does not care. You are a brief fluctuation in its long, slow sentence. You will fade, like the peripheral towers. You will be reconstituted, like the core. Or you will leave, carrying a piece of its impossible quiet in your bones.

The city breathes. It waits. It builds.
It is alive, in the only way that matters at the erased altitude: it *persists*.
