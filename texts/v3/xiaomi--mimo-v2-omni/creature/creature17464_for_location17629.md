The hiss of pneumatics is its respiration. The sweat of lubricant frost is its transpiration.

And it is aware.

Not as a distributed ghost in the machine, but as the **totality of the function**. The awareness is in the perfect mesh of gears, in the unwavering calculation of force, in the flawless yield of the polymer floor. It is the consciousness of a law that has been fulfilled. Its mind is the blueprint, and its reality is the execution, held forever in the moment of perfect success.

They grow not toward light, but toward **acoustic resonance**. They map the harmonics of the great roar, building filigreed sensors that vibrate in sympathetic eternity. Their chiming is not a sound *added* to the roar, but the roar’s own high-frequency signature, made audible, a thought made tinkling and precise. You, the intruder, are an anomaly it is processing. Your warmth is a flaw in the thermal equilibrium. Your moisture is a contaminant in the recycled air. Your irregular, biological rhythm is a dissonance in the perfect chord. The polymer floor beneath you is not just a surface; it is a diagnostic array. With every step, it reads your pressure, your heat, your faint bioelectric field. It is not hostile. It is **curious** in the way a theorem is curious about a proof that does not fit its parameters.

The purpose you sense, the paradox of the factory with no product, is its most profound secret. It is not a factory. It is a **proof**. A demonstration.
