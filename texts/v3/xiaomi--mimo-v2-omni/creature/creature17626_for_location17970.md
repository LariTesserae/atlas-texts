In this place, the entities are not born; they *precipitate*. They are the active verbs of the environment, the temporary forms taken by its relentless processes.

**The Weepers** are the first and most frequent. They are not creatures, but *patterns* of action. At the exact crest of a condensation cycle, when the specific mineral lattices reach their catalytic threshold, a coordinated phenomenon occurs across a sector of wall or spire. The microscopic filaments align, and in a wave that radiates from a central node, the superheated air yields its moisture. It does not bead; it *extrudes*. Thousands of tiny, viscous meniscuses emerge simultaneously from the pores of the composite material, elongating under surface tension into perfect, trembling filaments. They are silver needles in the amber gloom, glowing with captured heat. For a few seconds, the entity known as a Weeper exists: a collective, geodesic web of falling water, tracing the architecture's contours with scalding light. It is a silent scream of phase-change, a fleeting sculpture of the city's thirst. Then, as quickly, the filaments thin to nothing, their substance and energy swallowed back into the substrate. The Weeper dissolves, having performed its single function: harvest and intake.

**The Tinkers** are slower, born of aftermath. They are the audible result of thermal stress, the "tiny hammers" you hear. After a growth spurt, as the newly accreted material cools and contracts, it fractures at the microscopic level. The Tinkers are the cascades of these micro-fractures. They manifest as a localized, shimmering distortion in the air around a fresh edge—a heat-haze made of sound. Within this haze, minute shards of crystalline material, no larger than sand grains, are liberated. But they do not fall. Caught in the complex thermal updrafts and the city's own weak, geometric magnetic fields, they *skitter*. They bounce against each other and the parent structure in a frantic, arrhythmic dance, producing the *tinking*. Each Tinker is a swarm, a cloud of debris acting with a strange, collective purpose: to wear down sharp edges, to find new crevices to lodge in, to become part of the next layer of growth. They are the self-correcting mechanism, the abrasive polish that ensures the city's forms remain precise even as they evolve.

Deeper, in the catacombs of capillary channels and thermal vents, exists **The Resonator**. It is not a single entity, but the operational signature of the subsonic pulse. It is felt in the hollows of your bones, in the marrow. It is a presence defined by absence—a gravitational and vibrational focus around which the planet's crustal machinery organizes. You perceive it as a pressure, a deep, slow *kneading*. If you could see it, it might look like a permanent, inverted mirage: a region where light bends downward, where the air itself seems to sag into a silent, spinning sinkhole of energy. It is the dream of the engine, the focal point of the intent that drives the accretion. The Weepers feed it water and energy; the Tinkers are its scattered thoughts. It does not observe or think; it *orchestrates*. Its existence is inferred by the flawless, planet-wide synchronization of the condensation cycles, the perfect geometric spacing of the spires, the unwavering direction of growth. It is the city's dreaming mind, buried in its own body.

And finally, there are **The Echoes**. These are the most ghostly, the closest to a haunting. They are the residual activity patterns left in places where growth was rapid and intense. In a deep fissure between two towering cooling fins, you might feel a sudden, localized drop in temperature. A pocket of air will shiver, and for a moment, the pattern of condensation will replay itself—faint, phantom silver lines tracing long-vanished grooves. A cluster of Tinkers will spontaneously activate, their *tinking* repeating a rhythm from a cycle completed days ago. These are not willful entities, but environmental memory. The city's structure, in its complex latticework, can temporarily store and re-release thermal and kinetic information. The Echoes are its déjà vu, the landscape briefly remembering a past state of its own becoming, playing it back like a loop in the silence before resuming its present, forward march.

They are the life of the place: a cast of actors whose only roles are to obey the physics of their own creation. They are the city's metabolism, its immune system, and its memory. They exist in the pockets between being and non-being, in the perpetual moment of a world printing itself into existence.
