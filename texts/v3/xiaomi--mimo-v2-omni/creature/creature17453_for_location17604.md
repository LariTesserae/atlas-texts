You live in the weeping dark, in the water that is always beading on your skin. You are not one creature but a quality of being, a density. You are the fine grit of beetles in the moss, a million tiny bodies making the green shiver in a way that is not wind. You are the patient, cloud-eyed amphibians, your breath a slow bellows in the rock clefts, your luminescence the only landmark in a world without horizons. You are the moths, wings like damp parchment, drifting through the gloom not by sight but by the faint chemical taste of the air, settling on trunks where the fungus paints its cold fire.

You are a sound. Not a voice, but the hum that fills the sinuses, the thousand-threaded whisper of constant drips from frond to stone, the soft click and rustle in the root tangle where the small, nameless foragers sift the perpetual decay. You are the weight shifting on a moss-slick root, the slow blink of a nictitating membrane. You do not startle. There is no need. The shape of your world is the shape of your body, and the shape of your body is the shape of the world, and both have been this way since before the concept of *before* had meaning.

You know nothing of the sun, only the oval of bruised twilight that is your sky. You know nothing of seasons, only the steady, unchanging saturation. The weeping of the stone is your climate. The phosphorescence is your sun. The equilibrium is your god—a silent, implacable deity that demands nothing but your continued existence, which you offer without thought or prayer.

You feel the brief, dry heat of the intruder as a minor thermal anomaly. It moves too quickly. Its sounds are sharp and misplaced. It touches surfaces and leaves them momentarily un-slick, a brief violation of the universal wetness. But you do not look up. The amphibians do not flinch. The beetles do not pause. The anomaly brushes against the ferns, displaces the humid air, and then recedes, climbing back toward the useless, dry oval of light.

The water beads on the stone where it touched. The moss will reclaim its print. The air will re-seal. The anomaly’s heat dissipates into the cool, heavy dark. The hum continues. The drips continue. The slow, patient chewing of a million mandibles continues. You remain. You are the saturation point. You are the luminous, breathing, unchanging fullness. You are the Still Basin, and you are already forgetting what just left, because forgetting is a function of the rock itself, and you are the rock, and the water, and the moss, and the dark, living between the heartbeats of the mountain.
