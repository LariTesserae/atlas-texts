Anchored in the damp, silvery moss, the threads draw up trace minerals and moisture. The nodules are clusters of piezoelectric crystal, shaped by biological processes. As the hollow imperceptibly expands, micro-stresses in the quartzite generate minute electrical charges. The Veils absorb this energy, causing their gentle, bioluminescent pulse. It does not photosynthesize; it *piezo-synthesizes*. The constant, faint vibration of the hollow’s expansion provides the catalytic energy for its metabolism. In return, its dense, velvety mats soften the erosion of the crystal dust hills, creating stable valleys where moisture pools. It is the ground-cover and the cushion, the gentle stasis against which all other movement is measured. Its color is not an absence of green, but a perfect adaptation to the sourceless light—a reflective silver that minimizes energy waste.
