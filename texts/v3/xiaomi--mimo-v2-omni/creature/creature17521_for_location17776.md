They are the Calcivores, the Geophages, the Lithic Swarm. To call them "creatures" is to use a soft word for a hard truth. They are animate mineral, an extension of the ground given motion and purpose. You do not see individuals; you see a current, a tide of chitinous plates and grinding limbs flowing over the tessellated landscape. Their collective sound is the heartbeat of this dead world—a layered percussion of *click-grind-hum* that is felt in the marrow before it is heard by the ear.

Their forms are brutally functional, each caste a living tool. The **Miners** are like ambulatory boulders, their anterior halves dominated by massive, wedge-shaped craniums of condensedite. They heave and shudder, driving their heads into fissures with a sound of shearing crystal, levering loose raw chunks of the substrate. Their legs are thick, hydraulic columns, and their passage leaves deep, powdered ruts.

Upon them swarm the **Processors**, a glittering, multi-limbed frost of smaller beings. They are the breakers and the builders. Their mandibles are not for eating, but for precision fracturing, tapping and scoring the rawite with a rhythmic *tink-tink-tink* that creates a secondary, higher-frequency layer in the hive’s roar. They carry the shattered material in crop-like pouches of hardened membrane, not to consume, but to redistribute—to the Queen, to the growing nursery-pellets, to the maintenance of the grooves and tunnels that are the hive's circulatory system. They move with a frantic, precise economy, their multifaceted eyes reflecting the flat, diffuse light.

And then there are the **Consumers**. They are the silence within the noise. Low-slung, many-legged, and fast, they flow like liquid shadow between the legs of the larger castes. Their carapaces are polished to a high, vitreous sheen, often a deep cobalt or obsidian black, making them stark moving contrasts against the bone-white plain. They do not hunt for sustenance in the conventional sense. They hunt for *flaw*. A Miner that stumbles, a Processor that pauses too long, a juvenile that strays—these are the flaws. The Consumers descend in a silent, coordinated flurry of sharp limbs, dismantling the inefficient with the same remorseless precision a Processor uses on a chunk of stone. They are the antibodies of the hive, and their work is swift, leaving only clean, picked-over fragments that are immediately recycled by the ever-present Processors.

They are all born of the Queen-Stone. Her vaporous exhalations are not life in a biological sense, but *pattern*. The precipitating forms that drop from the charged air are pre-programmed lattices of crystal and chitin, their instincts etched into their very molecular structure. A new Miner is already equipped with the dense, wedge-shaped head; a new Consumer emerges with its sharp, articulated limbs folded, ready to unfold and kill. They are less born and more *manifested*, extensions of her mineral will.

Their communion with her is physical and constant. The seething mass around her base is not a crowd, but a circuit. They climb upon one another to deposit the purest dusts and rarest metallic salts into the fissures of her geode flesh. In return, her bioluminescent patterns—which are not light, but a form of solid-state data transmission—pulse through the ground itself, guiding the fractal patterns of industry across the entire hive. To stand near her is to feel the static in the air coalesce into a pressure, to taste ozone and feel a prickle of electricity that carries faint, alien thoughts: *more, process, maintain, integrate*.

They are also the archaeologists and the gravediggers. The ancient, half-buried structures are not curiosities to them, but resource deposits and obstacles. The great corroded piston is a mountain they have scaled and worn smooth with their passage, its metal now impregnated with their own crystalline growths. The humming, unknown building is a persistent anomaly; they swarm its surface, their grinding slowly, inexorably wearing down its strange material, incorporating its hum into the greater drone of the hive. They are digesting the remnants of whatever came before, reducing even advanced technology to the same fundamental level as theite and chalk. It is the ultimate democracy of erosion.

Thus, the Calcivores live. They are a metabolic process given form, a world consuming itself to stave off the final, silent stasis. They are not an ecology of life and death, but a single, vast organism of mineral metabolism, engaged in the eternal, noisy, and profoundly lonely task of maintaining its own perfect, terminal decay. They are the thought the stone has of itself, and their only purpose is to continue the thought until there is no more stone to think with.
