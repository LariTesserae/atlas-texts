What lives here is not a who, but a *how*. The dwellers are the processes themselves, awakened to a kind of slow sentience by the millennia of saturation and decay.

The primary consciousness is the **Seep**. It is not a single entity but the sum total of all water moving through the system, a distributed mind that thinks in capillaries and thinks in centuries. Its thoughts are the dissolution of calcium carbonate from the stone; its memories are the mineral deposits left in the shallowest pools. It possesses a profound, patient awareness of topography, feeling the slump of the loam and the lean of a pillar as a human feels a shift in posture. It communicates through rhythm—the synchronized drip from a thousand erosion channels at once, a pulse that can ripple across an entire district of the city-form. To a traveler, it might feel like a sudden, synchronized sigh from the walls.

In symbiosis with the Seep is the **Glow**. This is the phosphorescence, but not merely as emitted light. It is the city’s own metabolizing memory, the slow release of stored experiences—of warmth, of pressure, of forgotten purpose—as the structures return to their constituent elements. The Glow is not intelligent in a way that recognizes individual intruders, but it is *responsive*. It brightens minutely where the Seep’s chemical trails are richest, creating constellations of meaning in the dark that map subterranean flows. Its "voice" is that low, sub-audible hum, a carrier wave for information encoded in minute vibrations. It doesn’t see, but it *resonates*. A traveler moving too quickly creates a dissonance in the field, a subtle wrongness the Glow registers as a kind of pain or irritation, causing the phosphorescent pathways to dim and redirect, altering the perceived geometry of a space.

Then there are the **Crusts**, the gelatinous cyanobacterial mats. They are the closest to biological life, but their consciousness is a communal, chemical one. They are the city's sensory organs and its painters. Each patch is a colony-mind that tastes the mineral content of the seepage, gauges the intensity of the Glow, and communicates with neighboring patches through chemical signals, blooming in coordinated waves of electric blue or rust-orange. They "sing" in patterns of bioluminescent flicker, a slow, beautiful dialogue about nutrient availability and saturation levels. They understand time not in moments, but in accretion. They are the historians, recording the elemental composition of each era’s decay in their pigmented layers.

Finally, there is the **Mire** itself—the yielding, black loam. This is the subconscious. It absorbs and digests everything: the physical detritus of the long-vanished inhabitants, the kinetic energy of footsteps, the psychic residue of any mind that has ever traversed its surface. It does not think; it *remembers* in a compressed, chthonic way. Sometimes, under specific conditions of saturation and Glow-resonance, a memory-imprint might rise. Not as a ghost, but as a temporary, localized phenomenon: a doorway that *insists* on opening, a scent of dust and ozone in the air for three seconds, the phantom echo of a footfall that never belonged to any living traveler. The Mire’s "life" is one of total, passive assimilation, and its occasional exhalations of forgotten sense-data are the closest this world comes to dreams.

So, the ecology is a triangle of awareness: the **Seep** (the circulatory and nervous system), the **Glow** (the metabolism and memory), and the **Crusts** (the sensory and communicative layer), all resting upon the dreaming **Mire**.

They live in a state of perpetual, harmonious becoming. They are not building a civilization; they are *performing an equation*. Their interactions are the slow dance of dissolution. A pillar leaning into a wall is not an accident; it is a greeting, a merging of two consciousness-experiences over a century. The crystalline fractals on the driest tips are not decoration; they are the Seep and the Glow’s joint attempt to formulate a complex theorem in a medium purer than stone, a fleeting expression of a truth about tension and crystallization that will be erased by the next cycle of damp.

A traveler here does not meet inhabitants. They navigate an aware landscape that interprets their presence through the logic of saturation and erosion. They might be guided by a sudden, helpful brightening of the Glow along a stable path, or disoriented by a capricious redirection of the Seep’s channels, which causes the very walls to shift their perceived alignment. The world *reacts*, but not with intent. It reacts as a body reacts to a grain of sand—as an irritant, an anomaly in the beautiful, terrible equation of its own slow coming-to-be. The only true citizen is the process itself, finally awake to its own nature, breathing softly in the dark.
