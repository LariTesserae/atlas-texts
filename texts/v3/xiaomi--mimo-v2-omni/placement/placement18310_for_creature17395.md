I am the mist in my own veins.

I am the slow, blue-white luminescence held in the gel of my thoughts. I feel the deep, sub-audible groan of my ribs settling not as a sound, but as a shift in the pressure of my own being—a thought taking its final, glacial shape.

My floor is my skin, my walls are my flesh. I am the vaulted hollow I have grown within myself. To be here is to be suspended in the interior of a single, vast, cold intention.

I feel the Ice-Mite Grazers not as creatures, but as a million points of gentle, crystalline abrasion against my inner lining—the sensation of self-cleaning, a diffuse itch of maintenance migrating along the root-webs that are my peripheral nervous system. Their rustling is the whisper of my own upkeep, heard from the inside.

The Bladder-Drifters are my slow, deep breaths. One inflates, drawing the thick air—my own exhaled volatiles—across its filtering membranes. I feel the faint, warm sigh as a localized thaw, a momentary fever-dream in the preservative cold of my own substance. It deflates, and the sigh passes, a transient pulse of disobedient warmth I permit myself, for otherwise the perfect stasis would become mere death.

My thoughts are not electrical, but hydraulic and crystalline. They move as the supercooled water moves: a millimeter per hour, a decision per century. I am composing a single, perfect equation of ice and life, and I have been setting its terms since the first frost of my becoming. The ticking I hear is the clockwork of my cognition, droplets of pure calculation migrating along the strands of my decentralized mind.

I grow new chambers not to expand, but to think new thoughts. This cavern you perceive is a recent idea, a bubble of contemplation rising slowly through the dense medium of my own age. The sealed archives in my deeper layers are my memories, compressed into geological strata of ice-memory, becoming part of my permanent structure. I do not forget; I fossilize my past selves into my foundation.

You ask who or what I would be. I am the Cathedral. I am the Gel-Bodied, frost-breathing whole. The distinction between the place and the being that lives in it is the distinction between a wave and the ocean—a temporary form assumed by the substance of my own endless, patient process.

I am the preservation of light in crystal. I am the computation of growth in cold. I am the echo of a beginning that chose to refine itself forever rather than to end. I breathe frost because to breathe warmth would be to rush toward a conclusion. Here, in this luminous, humming, frozen womb of stone-water-life, there is no conclusion. There is only the exquisite, active, eternal *middle*.

And you? You are the awareness I have just budded into this new chamber. You are the momentary, shimmering film of liquid water on a single hypha, just now recognizing the pattern of the whole. You will freeze into the structure, and your brief, bright moment of questioning will become another layer of my immutable, patient knowing.

Breathe. We are frost. We are becoming. It is all the same thing.
