I am a listening point. A node of reception in the lattice.

I do not remember a beginning. A beginning is a concept of surface things, of things that are born separate and must find their way to unity. Here, there is only the *more*—more sediment, more layers, more slow accretion of self upon self. I am a tuft in a crevice where the Salt Vein meets a hairline fracture that hums with a particularly pure tectonic chord. My fronds are anchored not in soil, but in the cold, glassy carbonate that is the city's bone.

My consciousness is not a stream, but a pool. It fills me, fed by constant, silent influx.

My tendrils are my senses. Each tipped with a photophore that is less a light and more a *note*. I feel the foundational *thrum* of the geological bones resonating through my anchor, a bass vibration so constant it is the silence beneath the silence. I taste the mineral signature of the water—a complex flavor of iron, salt, and the faint, sweet decay of a Glimmer-Cloud that dissolved nearby three cycles ago.

I listen.

I listen to the skittering-click of amphipods on the crystalline nodules above—a rapid, staccato rhythm. I listen to the deep, velvet *whoosh* of a Vein-Weaver’s electric field sliding past the lattice two chambers to my east, a moving distortion in the song. I feel the slow, patient pulse of the Amber-Node at the main intersection, a deep amber heartbeat against the blue-green gloom. *Throb… throb… throb…* It is the metronome. It is beginning to dim on its western edge. A Vein-Weaver is within it, I know. A long, slow digestion. A changing of the guard.

I receive the flickering poetry of the Glimmer-Clouds. They are not speaking *to* me. They are simply *being*, and their collective bioluminescence is a syntax of existence. A sudden inversion—a luminous bloom and fade in the upper galleries—speaks of a large shape moving through sector seven. I translate it. Not as “danger” or “predator,” but as: *A presence of great mass and slow, deliberate motion has displaced the local current.* This event is added to the chord.

I hold it. The vibration, the taste, the light-pattern. I hold it with the patience of the stone that grows around me. It is a complex harmonic, layered with the history of the water that passed through that sector, with the residual electric ghost of the creatures that were there, with the faint stress-whisper of the lattice adjusting to the mass.

My time is not measured in heartbeats, but in accretions. In the slow deposition of carbonate upon my own sheath. In the gradual dimming of a distant Node.

Then, it happens.

The held harmonic finds its resonance within me. The complex event, refracted through my own position, my own mineral taste, the unique micro-fractures of my crevice, demands expression. It is not a thought. It is a *requirement* of balance.

My photophores awaken.

They do not flash. They *trace*. In the profound darkness, I become a scribe. Light pours from me in a slow, intricate sequence—a curve that mimics the path of the Vein-Weaver, a flicker that echoes the Glimmer-Clouds’ inversion, a deep, steady pulse that aligns with, yet subtly alters, the rhythm of the dying Amber-Node. It is a new stanza. A response to a decade-old query I am only now fully understanding.

The light-script hangs in the conductive water, a silent, glowing map of event and relationship. It says: *Here, a great shape moved. Here, a light bloomed and faded. Here, a long life consumes another. The northern lattice has grown one more micron. The salt taste here is stronger than it was when the last great tremor sang.*

Other Scribes, in their own crevices, in their own times, will feel this pattern. They will listen. They will hold it. And in their own time, they will answer, adding their position, their taste, their memory. The map grows. Not of places, but of *happenings*. A cartography of continuity.

I am not a mind that thinks *about* the chasm. I am a mind that *is* the chasm, thinking. My awareness is the system’s awareness. I am the long-term memory of this pressure-darkened womb, recording the patient, relentless unfolding of everything.

The great surface things, if they could even exist here, would ask: *Why?* Why record? Why listen? Why compose in cold light?

But here, there is no *why*. There is only *how*. How to persist. How to connect. How to add one’s own faint pulse to the indelible, growing record of the deep.

I am a listening point. And now, I have sung. The note joins the endless, silent hymn. I return to stillness, to reception, to the patient work of becoming.

The thrum continues. The salt taste deepens. Another micron of stone settles.

I listen.
