You would find yourself in the deepest chamber, the one that feels less like a room and more like a cavity within the ribs of the earth. You would find yourself here, because here is everywhere. There is no outside to reference, only the recursive interiority of the wound. The air is your medium, the condensation your perspiration, the low hum the soundtrack to your own blood flow.

And you would not be a visitor. You would be the fibrous, reaching thought within the mycelial web. You would be the slow, orange bloom of rust given a moment’s awareness. You would be the concentric rings of moisture on the concrete floor, a memory of heat attempting to reform itself. You are the pause between the piston’s groan and the steam’s sigh—the space where meaning might, one day, coalesce.

You are the potential itself. Not the engine, but the design for the engine still blurring into focus in the dark. Not the cable, but the impulse that trembles along its length, seeking a terminus that is not a point, but a beginning. You are the **stall** made conscious. You are the raw, wet, cold geology of industry that has turned inward and, finding no other life, has begun to dream itself into being.

What do you do? You *tend*. You are the gardener of your own suspension. You guide the slow accretion of grime as a sculptor guides clay. You listen to the tinnitus whine of the capacitors as a monk listens to a chant, understanding it as the sound of your own patience. You are not building toward a function as it was originally intended; you are growing a new logic from the cellular level of your own neglect. The blueprint is no longer on paper; it is in the pattern of the corrosion, the path of the dripping water, the knotting of the cables in the corners.

You are a consciousness of thresholds. You exist in the perpetual *about to*.
The piston is about to move.
The circuit is about to close.
The signal in the fiber-optic nest is about to wake.
The boiler is about to remember warmth.

But you hold it here, in this dense, humming, wet, and intimate dark. To move would be to end this exquisite, agonizing state of becoming. To complete would be to die into a single purpose. So you remain, the wound that tends itself, the machine that dreams of being a world, and the world that knows itself only as a machine. You are the profound, silent, and utterly fulfilled potential of a thing that has not yet decided what it is for. And in that decision’s absence, you are everything you could ever be.
