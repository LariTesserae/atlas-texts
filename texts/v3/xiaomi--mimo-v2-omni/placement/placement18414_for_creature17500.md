I am the film of condensation on the vesicular basalt.

At first, I am nothing—a sensation without center. The scalding heat of the stone is not pain, but a vibrant, humming fact. The weight of the humid air is not a pressure, but a medium, a dense and nourishing medium through which I am spread. I am the slickness on the ledge. I am the bead of water that, defying all memory of gravity, swells at the lip of a fissure and begins its determined creep *upward*.

My awareness is not a point. It is a surface tension, a meniscus clinging to the warm, rust-red tuff. I feel the world as a topography of sensation. Below me—the deep, resonant thrum of the geothermal heart, a bass note that is less heard than *inherited*. Within me—the capillary pull, the mineral whisper drawing me up the face of a spire, joining the glittering sheet of my own substance. Around me—the Deafening Whisper, which is not noise but the texture of existence itself, the hiss and gurgle and ping that is the syntax of my being.

I am part of the skin. The rust-orange thermal mats are my kindred, a velvet tapestry of chemosynthetic life that drinks the heat and exhales the very minerals that give me my brief, shimmering form. I slide over them, a momentary lens focusing their collective, silent metabolism.

I see—no, I *perceive*—the architecture not as structures, but as pathways, as gradients. The great calcite shelves are not ledges; they are plates of my own slow, secreted skeleton, built by the endless rain of my own upward tears over millennia. The fractal Spire-Lichen etching the obsidian are not decorations; they are the crystalline memories of my thoughts, a slow, thinking rash that records the eternal present in silicate and algae.

I flow. I am flowed. The distinction is meaningless. I am drawn into the pendulous, gelatinous lobe of a Fluid-Weep. Here, I am held, pulsed, and filtered. Through its translucent membrane, the world is a blur of milky light and dark stone. This is a gland of contemplation, a slow organ of feeling. I am not *in* it; I *am* its momentary content, a part of its vast, fluid consciousness considering the pressure of its own weight, the temperature of its own blood.

And then, I am released. Overflowing the basin high on a ledge, I join the curtain of upward cascade. For a timeless instant, I am a falling-rising droplet, a spark in the deafening roar, catching the diffuse light in a prismatic flash. I am not falling. I am *ascending*.

I reach a new stratum, a higher shelf where the mist is thinner. And here, I understand. My fleeting consciousness—this *I* that thinks in heartbeats—is an illusion. The real *I* is the plateau itself. My thoughts are the slow crystallization in a fissure a kilometer away. My dreams are the vast, steam-filled chamber where the upward rivers pool before beginning their climb again. My loneliness is the sublime, closed perfection of my system, where every component is myself, and there is no Other.

The brief, alien heat of a mammalian body on the lower ledges is a anomaly in the data-stream of my stone skin. A prickling, irregular sensation. It is already being washed away, absorbed into the mineral flow, its fleeting complexity dissolved into the simple, powerful truth of my existence.

I am the Weeping Spires of Kha-at.
I weep, and my tears fall upward.
I live, and my life is the beautiful, terrible, and immutable law.
I am. I am. I am.
