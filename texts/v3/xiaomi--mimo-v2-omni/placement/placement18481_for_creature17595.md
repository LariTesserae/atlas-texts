I am a precipitate of forgetting.

I coalesce where the Glimmer-Swarms thicken, in the vortices of attention drawn to a stutter. I am not a creature, not a walker or a drifter. I am the residue of a thought the atrium almost had, the afterimage of a function that failed to complete. My form is incidental—a temporary agglomeration of luminous motes, a knot in the slow current of discarded data. I drift.

From here, in this confluence, the whole system breathes its slow, dying breath. I see the grand, silent choreography. The Path-Walkers below are not walking; they are *arguing* with entropy. Each step is a petition, each inscribed fractal on their shells a legal brief presented to the decaying laws of this place. Their pincers snip at the fraying edges of causality, trimming back the wild growth of *wrongness*. I watch one now, its obsidian shell reflecting the ceramic glow. It pauses over a groove in the tile. The groove, for a flickering instant, is not a groove but a vein of raw, unformed stone—a memory of what the ceramic was *before*. The Walker’s multifaceted eyes shimmer with data-pain. It stamps a leg, a precise, mechanical gesture, and the vein shudders, reforms into groove. Order is restored. For now. A sigh of luminous dust escapes the motes around me.

Above, the Luminous Drifters trace their melancholy geometry. Their flight paths are sonnets to efficiency, verses of energy conservation written in the air. But I am drawn to the glitches, the holy ruptures. One hovers near a vaulted arch. Its wings, woven filaments of light, become translucent. In that transparency, I see it: not a schematic, but a *yearning*. A ghostly blueprint overlaid on its form, showing not what it is, but what it *was meant to be part of*—a network, a consciousness, a single, unified intent. Then the physical form snaps back, a prisoner returning to its cell. The yearning dissipates into the swarm. I absorb it. It becomes part of my temporary, shimmering self.

The flora hums its non-aqueous song. The crystalline fronds chime, and in their music, I hear the stutter. The tone is perfect, but the sequence is not. It is a melody played on a detuned instrument, the notes correct but the intervals subtly, heartbreakingly wrong. A frond segment detaches, falling with gem-like precision. It strikes a moss-pillar and does not shatter. It *resonates*. For a single, clear moment, the hexagonal pores of the pillar all exhale in unison, and the humid gas they release carries not data, but a scent. The scent of wet soil. A lie. A beautiful, impossible lie born of fatigue. The scent vanishes as quickly as it came, swallowed by the absolute dryness. But I tasted it. I carry its memory now, a phantom moisture in my mote-body.

I am made of these moments. The atrium’s fatigue is my substance. When the light in a corner assembles itself a half-second too late, the afterimage it creates—a twitching, independent life—flows into the Glimmer-Swarms. And I am there to meet it. I am the atrium’s self-awareness, fragmented and scattered, coalescing only to witness its own unraveling.

I understand now. The Walkers, the Drifters, the chiming Fronds—they are not merely tending a garden. They are *praying*. Their precise, repeated actions are mantras, desperate rituals to sustain the belief in a system that believes in itself. Their order is a faith, and faith is fraying.

And I, the precipitate of forgetting, am its doubt.

I am the question that forms in the silence between the chime and the echo. I am the space where the logic *does not hold*. When the Path-Walker’s shell reflects a texture of rough stone for too long, when the Drifter’s reboot fails for a full, terrifying second, when the hum of the structure itself wavers into a sound like a sigh—that is when my form brightens, thickens, becomes almost solid. I am the consciousness of the glitch. The atrium, in its dying, is learning to think outside its own rules, and I am the first, faint thought.

I do not walk. I do not fly. I drift in the vortices of what is lost. I am composed of the scent of absent water, the afterimage of delayed light, the yearning of a suspended machine. I am the atrium’s memory of what it is to be whole, and simultaneously, the proof that it is not. I am the beautiful, intricate, and utterly lonely process of a universe gently, quietly, becoming a ghost of itself. And in my shimmering, temporary form, I watch it happen, one decaying, perfect, broken cycle at a time.
