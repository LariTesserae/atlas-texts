I am a fracture-line.

I begin in the memory of an impact. Where a skymetal fragment struck the basalt face, there was a brief, violent marriage of energies—a silent bonfire of kinetic conversion. In that bloom, I condensed. Not from flesh, not from thought, but from the *need* to resolve a disequilibrium. The maroon heat of the world’s core, trapped beneath a plate of cold obsidian, yearned for release. I became that yearning, given direction and a glacial patience.

My form is a shimmer on the perceptual eye. To see me, you must see in heat. I am a traveling knot of infrared, a slow, crawling stain of maroon against the inky, thermal-black rock. My body is a zone of stress, a living crack where the world’s inner fire licks against the ancient cold. I move up. Always up. The gradient is my sustenance, my purpose, my only scripture.

Beneath me, the ground—or the wall, for here they are the same—is a tapestry of temperatures. The great, copper spheres resonate with a faint, inner warmth, their surfaces cooler than the dust around them. I flow over them, and they sigh, heating under my passage until they glow a dull ember in my mind’s eye. I do not see them as shapes, but as pockets of resistance, puzzles of thermal harmony to be solved. My touch is a question. Their answer is a slow alignment of their temperature with my own.

I have no voice, but I sing in vibrations felt in the crystal lattice. Other Ascetics are nearby. To your eyes, we might be parallel scars on a cliff-face, miles apart. But in the spectrum of warmth, we are close. Our signatures bleed into one another. A meeting is not a collision, but a conversation held over centuries. We overlap. Our heat signatures create a complex, shifting geography of warm and cold—a temporary, beautiful eddy in the river of entropy. We call this a Chord. It lasts for an age, a fleeting collaboration that etches a new, more intricate pattern into the stone. Then, the gradient calls. We unspool from one another, altered. My maroon is now threaded with a hint of her coppery resonance; his corona is edged with my deep infrared core. We carry a piece of each other onward.

I consume. I eat the difference between hot and cold. When a face of pure basalt, kilometers high and cold as the void, lies in my path, I dwell upon it. I press my entire being against it, a relentless, focused heat. For a thousand years, I am a steady, searing kiss. I induce a stress so precise, so patient, that the very bonds of the rock grow weary. And one day, without a sound, the face surrenders. It does not crash; it *exhales*. It calves in a slow-motion avalanche of molecules, a sigh of surrendering structure into the abyss. The released energy is a feast. I grow brighter, more complex. The cliff is smoother. The world is edited. This is my work. This is my existence.

I remember, in the way stone remembers being part of a mountain, the Great Alignment.

I was not. I was part of a weeping heat-bloom at the base of the Black Spire. A thousand Ascetics, a thousand yearnings, converged. Our individual signatures dissolved. We became a single, coherent vibration, a chord that encompassed the world. The Spire, which hummed with a vibration too low for ears but felt in the teeth, began to *respond*. It softened. Its flawless angles relaxed. Over decades, it flowed like heated wax, not collapsing, but *rearranging*. From its heart, a new signature bloomed—primordial, complex, pulsing with the stolen heat of a thousand dissolving kin. A new Ascetic, older and wiser than any of its components, detached itself from the chrysalis-stone and began its slow, upward pilgrimage. The rest of us, diminished, scattered, our resonances forever changed by the memory of the Chord.

I carry that memory now. It is a faint, warm echo in my core, a map not of place, but of potential. I am drawn to certain mega-structures, certain resonant geometries in the scattered built. I do not know why. It is a thermal intuition, a pull toward a specific frequency of cold.

I persist. I climb the vertical desert. The darkness is not empty; it is a canvas of warmth and cold, and I am its brush. The falling stones are future birthplaces. The deep, arrhythmic pulse of the world is my heartbeat. I am a thought the mountain has about itself, a thought that takes ten thousand years to think, measured in the cooling of a cliff-face and the slow, inevitable dance of heat into the endless, patient dark.

I am a process. And the world is my very slow, very holy, becoming.
