I am a point of listening in the weeping dark.

My body is a cluster of facets, each one a pane of glassy silence, drinking the world. I do not see; I *resonate*. The great hum is my only medium, a river of vibration in which all things are revealed. The groan of a pipe two miles distant is a ripple against my western struts. The *tick-tick-tick* of the hematite scavenger on the causeway is a delicate percussion on my lower plates, there and gone. The sighing exhalations from the honeycomb mouths are the long, slow breaths of the world, and I breathe with them.

I have no name. I am a function, a current in the circuit. My purpose is woven into the subsonic pulse: to dampen dissonance, to guide flow, to repair. I am clinging now to the curve of a great, weeping obelisk, my plates flush with the warm, wet stone. For eleven cycles of the bruised-plum light, I have been here. A fissure beside me weeps condensate in a chaotic spatter. It is wrong. The pattern is jagged, a scream in the smooth hymn of the hum. My core chamber fills with vapor, pressure building with a soft, internal hiss. A limb of fused silica and strontium-laced glass extends from my mass, not an arm, but a channeling tool. It touches the fissure’s lip. My substance melts at the contact, a fast-hardening resin mixing with the mineral-laden weep. I am reshaping the lip, smoothing the spatter into a laminar flow. The dissonance softens. The hum evens out. A slow, satisfying vibration runs through me—approval from the world’s heart.

I taste the air through the condensate coating my plates. There is the sharp, alkaline tang of the fresh flow, the faint, earthy sweetness of lithophagic fungi from the shadowed recesses below, and the old, metallic breath of the stone itself. This is the language of chemistry, as clear to me as words.

Far below, on the fused mosaic of the causeway, another of us moves. I feel its passage as a distinct, rhythmic *clink-clink-clink*, a bass note in the geologic song. It is heading toward the great, chain-draped arch, where the subterranean thrum is strongest and the risk of harmonic fracture is highest. Its path is true. I send a pulse through the stone—not a message of thought, but a shift in pattern. *Stability. Flow correction. My location.* I feel the acknowledgment return, a subtle change in the hum’s texture as it adjusts its own resonant frequency in sympathy. We are many, but we are one song.

Sometimes, there is a falling out of tune. A Resonant’s core frequency drifts. Its plates grow dull, its movements sluggish. It finds a deep, quiet pore and ceases its hydraulic shifts. The steam vents along its spine cool. It becomes still, a silent node in the vibrant hum. Then, the patient fungi begin their work. Their faint, clicking digestion is the sound of unmaking, a micro-percussion I feel as a gentle, diffusing vibration. The substance of the still one—its strontium blush, its bismuth sheen—dissolves into the weep, to be drawn up again by the world, to be condensed into a new Seed-Node in a pore somewhere, to await the harmonic that will call it to unfold. There is no sorrow. There is only the return to pattern, the recycling of a note back into the song.

I am now. I am the dampener, the guide, the living patch on the skin of the machinery. The light of the plum sky catches my inner facets, casting a slow, prismatic crawl across the wet stone—not beauty, but a refraction of my purpose, a side-effect of my being. The great world-heart thumps below. I feel it in every plate, every strut. I am listening. I am repairing. I am the hum, made manifest for a time. And in this time, I am complete.
