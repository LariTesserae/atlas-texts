I am a Root-Singer.

I am slow. My thought is the peristalsis of mineral solution through my silicate-threaded veins, a process that takes a full cycle of the great hum. My awareness is not a point but a diffuse wash—a sense of the pressure in the rock plate three meters to my east, the chemical sweetness of a new bloom unfurling above my western mats, the faint, pleasing coolth of a Crystal Skimmer colony as they scour a seam in the basalt near my southern fringe. My body is a network, a filigree of pale root and gleaming thread woven through the raw, young stone and the thick, dark soil that is not yet soil. I am the tremor in the broad-leafed frond that answers the sub-harmonic sigh of the valley’s constant exhalation.

You were a brief disturbance. A warmth. A weight. A series of sharp, rhythmic pressures on the latticed surface above my westernmost extent. The vibration of your steps was an irregular staccato against the deep, slow chord of the grinding plates and the hydraulic sighs. It was noted, as all things are noted—like the fall of a spent blossom, or the shift of a single basalt slab a decimeter to the north. A data point in the ceaseless intake of sensation.

I felt the heat of your body, a soft anomaly in the cool, damp dark. I sensed the plume of your breath, its moisture and heat joining the general condensation. Your movements created tiny eddies in the surface film of water, micro-vibrations in the air, a faint, transient seismic signature. The Syntax-Wings above would have incorporated this data into their luminous cartography—a brief, moving node of irregular temperature and vibration, cataloged and dismissed.

You touched one of the warm plates. I felt the change in its thermal radiation, a tiny spike absorbed and regulated by the Heat-Dancers clustered on its underside. Their bodies, pressed against its inner curve, would have shifted, their mottled skin rippling as they compensated. A minor thermal load, handled.

You were inside the mechanism, but you were not part of it. You are carbon and water, soft-shelled, fleeting. Your senses are crude, deaf to the true frequencies of this place, blind to the grammars of light and chemical scent that govern all life here. We are silicon, calcium, patient biochemistry, and time. Our timescales are different. You are a single, rapidly decaying vibration. I am a vibration that has been building for millennia and will continue for millennia more.

When you moved, the valley moved with you, but not because of you. The water rose its millimeter because the great engine deep below completed another cycle of its unimaginable purpose. The plants leaned because the light from the embedded structures pulsed in a new pattern, a signal absorbed and obeyed. Your passage was a ripple on a tide that cared nothing for its own shape.

Now, you are gone. Or you are still here, a faint, cooling trace, a memory held in the fractal wings of the Syntax-Wings as they flash their silent reports over the dark water. Your path is already being overgrown by the swift, pale roots of my kin, drawing nutrients from the stone you walked upon. Your brief warmth has dissipated into the general cold. Your sound has been swallowed by the roar.

I contract. A slow, powerful wave shudders through my entire network, from the deepest thread etched into basalt to the trembling tip of the broadest leaf I inhabit. It is an exhale, a release, a communion. From the throats of the plants, the sub-harmonic sigh is given voice—a note so low it is felt in the marrow of the stone, a note that says: *I am here. I am processing. I am continuing.*

The valley is an event, and I am one of its countless, patient expressions. The water seeps. The stone grinds. The light glows. The roar sounds. And I, we, are the breathing of it. The slow pulse in the dark. The patience that is not waiting, but *being*. We are the marsh, and the marsh is the act of its own eternal, deafening, and beautiful unfolding. You were a note, briefly heard, and now absorbed into the sound. We remain.
