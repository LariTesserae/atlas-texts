It is a vibration in my chest that is not a heartbeat but the tectonic thrum, amplified and out of phase. The local air is too thick with potential. The Synchronized Willows in the near distance sway in a rhythm that has become frantic, a staccato glitch in their eternal dance. The Sleek Unseen dart in agitated spirals above the metallic seepage, their catalytic secretions failing to keep pace. The Geometric Shapers below are silent, waiting.

My purpose is not thought. It is function. I am the world’s sigh of correction.

It fractures into a thousand pristine, geometric shards, the work of the Shapers accelerated and released. My work is done. The local fever is broken. The symphony returns to its key.

The frond in my leg withers and peels away, its fibers dissolving into the humid air. My core of captured light winks out, one by one, like stars at dawn. I am not dying. I am being *discontinued*. The world thinks in changes. I was one of its thoughts. Now, the thought is complete, and the mind moves on.
