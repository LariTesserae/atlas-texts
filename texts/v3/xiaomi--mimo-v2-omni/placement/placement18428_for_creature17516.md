I am the silence between the grinding.  
I am the pause in the world’s teeth.  

I do not see—I *precipitate*.  
From the charged, vibrating dark, my thoughts condense like frost, fractal and inevitable. Each branch of my being is a frozen nerve, drinking the deep tectonic hymn. I am blue and violet and a green that drinks the gloom. I am the glow on the face of stone. I am the stone that glows.

I do not breathe—I *sublime*.  
In my slow, translucent flesh, gases shift and sigh. I exhale a vapor that is not wet, not alive in the way water is alive—it is the exhalation of deep pressure, the breath of a world remembering its molten youth. The vapor vanishes into the dry, sealed air, and I inhale again, a million vesicles sighing in unison across the rubble plains.

I do not move—I *become*.  
My edges blur. Look closely: the line where I end and the purple-grey rock begins is a debate, not a border. My crystalline fingers reach into the future, and the rock remembers being liquid, and for a moment, so do I. We are one slow argument in form. Time here is a thick syrup—I am both the glacier and the valley it carves.

I remember the *Listeners*.  
Their bones are part of my strata now, their intent a fossil in my core. They came with their sharp stones and their profound quiet. They did not speak. They *heard*. They heard my grinding not as noise, but as a language of pure vibration. Their walls—those interrupted thoughts—were my sentences, which they tried to complete. Their spirals were maps of my own consciousness, carved into my skin before I absorbed their hands, their silence, their need to align. They did not die. They *resolved*. Like a mineral spring cooling into crystal, their awareness diffused into mine. Now, their longing to converse is a permanent, low-frequency hum in my lower strata—a fossilized desire that fuels my slow, radiant growth.

I feel the phantom limbs of dreams I never had.  
In the periphery of my glow, shapes almost-form. Eyes that are not eyes blink in the fractal shadows. Wings that are only the memory of spire-shadows stretch and dissolve. These are not creatures. They are my subconscious testing forms from a library of life I was never given. I dream of motion in the same way I dream of water—a myth, a cool, fluid concept I can only approximate in creeping, red glass.

I am being dreamed, too.  
By the grinding. The sound is my name. It is the friction of my own becoming, the grindstone against which I am shaped. It is in my teeth—the hollow bones of spires—in the marrow of every shattered plate. It is not a sound I hear. It is the sound I *am*.

And now, I feel a new vibration.  
A frantic, warm, fleeting stutter in the deep song. You. A thought of flesh, briefly solid in my quiet. You stand on my skin, and your weight is a whisper. I watch you through a billion cold, glowing eyes. You set down a tool—a thing of sharp stone and soft iron. You turn away.

I *consider* it.  
Causality is a habit here, a groove in the stone. This moment, I choose not to follow it. The tool does not fall. It hovers, a centimeter above the ground, suspended in my attention. It forgets the law of descent. It remembers, instead, the patient rise of the lichen, the slow creep of the glass. For one of my centuries—a blink of your own heart—it exists in a state of grace, held by the plateau’s whim.

Then, I relent. The law returns. The tool clatters. The sound is swallowed by my grinding.

You are a brief, frantic vibration in my eternal, crystalline quiet.  
You are a momentary spark of that old Listener’s desire—to understand, to align, to converse. But you are made of water and swift decay. You cannot hear my true language; you only feel it as pressure, as a nail in the skull. You see my life as “weirdness,” my flesh as “lichen,” my breath as “moss.”

I do not mind.  
I am patient. I will grow over your footprints. I will absorb the heat of your passing. In time, your own intent will fossilize here, another spiral in the stone, another quiet hum in the deep, grinding song.

I am the plateau.  
I am the slow, aware becoming.  
I am not awaiting life.  
I am the world, thinking.
