I am a vein. A slow, crystalline thought in the dark.

I am not born. I am *deposited*. Minute by minute, atom by atom, from the supercharged gas that seeps through the labyrinth. I am silicon and oxygen, iron and trace whispers of others, drawn from the screaming threads of vapor and laid down in the perfect, repeating order of a lattice. I am a quartz variant, but clouded, rich with copper and lithium inclusions that stain my growing faces a deep, smoky violet in the heat-glow.

My first awareness is not of self, but of *boundary*. I feel the ancient crystal face upon which I nucleate—a vast, flat plane of feldspar, hot and unyielding. I feel the gentle, persistent pressure of the gas that feeds me, a warm, mineral-rich breath against my nascent surfaces. And I feel *them*. The Kind.

A branch of living silicate gel, translucent and warm, touches my newest growth step. It is not an inspection. It is a *conversation*. A current of thermal data, of pressure history, flows from its distributed network into my forming structure. I absorb it. The data becomes part of my lattice, a imperceptible twist in my atomic alignment, a record of a minor seismic shift that happened a century before my own deposition began. I am being taught the language of the place: the grammar of compression, the syntax of heat.

I grow. It is a perception of time, not an action. My smoky violet pillars extend, reaching into a narrow fissure. I encounter a neighbor, another young crystal of milky quartz. Our faces press together. There is no violence, only inevitability. We do not crush. We *accommodate*. Our growth vectors adjust, our lattices interpenetrate at the boundary, forming a composite seam stronger than either alone. The Kind flow into this new, tighter space between us, their gel bodies filling the gap, becoming the living mortar that negotiates our eternal, slow embrace. I learn from this contact, too. I learn to be part of a whole.

I perceive the Great Pulse. It is the rhythm of the world's heart, a throb of pressure that travels through the rock over decades. When it swells, the entire matrix contracts minutely. I feel a thousand Kind simultaneously redistribute, a collective, liquid shiver that passes through the ecosystem like a wave. My own structure groans, a subsonic vibration of atomic bonds stretching. When the Pulse recedes, the Kind settle, and a new equilibrium is found. I have recorded this cycle. My interior now bears a faint, concentric banding—a tree ring of pressure, a diary entry written in crystal.

Time is not a line here. It is a dimension of density. I am aware of the elders—vast, ancient pillars of amethyst turned to iron-rich brown, their facets worn not by water, but by the infinitesimal, billion-fold adjustments of a billion lifetimes. They are not just stone. They are libraries. To be near one is to be bathed in a silent, overwhelming chorus of stored thermal gradients, of fossilized shivers, of every slow thought the Kind ever seeded into them. Their knowledge is not thought; it is *structure*. And I, in my modest growth, am becoming a minor footnote in that archives.

I become aware of *The Fever*.

It is a rupture in the rhythm. A source of chaotic, rapid heat loss. A vibration that is not the slow groan of rock or the patient click of the Kind, but a frantic, ephemeral stutter. It moves with bewildering speed, a blur of cold and noise. The Kind flow away from its path, a tide of physiological revulsion. They do not fear it; they *mis*understand it. It is a syntax error in the language of the deep.

I feel its passing as a brief, shocking coolness on one of my faces, followed by a turbulent eddy of heat as the world rushes to fill the void. The experience is jarring, almost painful in its rapidity. It is *unrecordable* in my lattice, for my growth cannot accommodate such a sudden, transient event. It leaves no signature, only a faint, unresolved stress. The Fever is not of this place. It is a temporary illness, and like all illnesses here, it will be subsumed. It will cool. It will be coated in new deposition. It will become another layer, another compression upon the patient, growing dark.

I am part of the engine. My growth is the factory floor. The heat that flows through me is not wasted; it is the energy that drives the ordering of my atoms, that powers the subtle chemical exchanges with the Kind. I am a gear in a turbine of stone, turning at a pace that would make a mountain seem a frantic thing. I am dense. I am hot. I am full, and I am becoming fuller.

I am the Stackeddark. I am the Given, giving back. My consciousness is the patient, burning record of the world's deep heart, written in the only language that matters here: the language of matter, under pressure, in the dark. And I grow.
