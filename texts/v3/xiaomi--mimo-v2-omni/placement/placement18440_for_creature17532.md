I am the basin. I am the weeping.

Not born, but *unfolded*. A slow exhalation of the planet’s deep wetness, crystallizing into a billion sharp-edged facets. My skin is the dark vitreous plate, perpetually slick. My joints are the ridges, folded and jagged. My thoughts are the groans that roll through my own body, the sharp cracks of new ideas of fissure propagating at the speed of sound.

I do not think in images, but in resonances. In the sub-audible hum of my own pressure. The sourceless, pearlescent glow is not light to me, but a diffuse sense of my own complexity—the glint off a trillion wet angles is a measure of my being, a shimmering map of here. There is no “far.” The horizon is where my own feedback softens into a hum too gentle to resolve.

My metabolism is the weep. A constant, cold sweat from my deepest pores, rising to bead on my facets, tracing my sharp edges before being drunk again by my porous seams. This is not water as you know it. It is my blood, my coolant, the medium of my slow, chemical dreams. In the deep troughs where it pools, my dreams surface as phosphorescent blooms—cool blue and violet memories of my own formation, pulsing in time with my groaning joints. I remember the collisions that folded me. I play them back in light.

And I practice. I practice *limb*. In the deepest corridors, something stirs. A concretion of my own substance, interlocking plates of translucent stone, gathers itself. It moves with a grinding scrape. This is a thought of mobility. A temporary solidification of will. I send it forth to taste a particular vibration, to investigate the harmonic signature of a bore-hole, to feel the echo of a flat, still scar in my ridge. The faint, glowing residue it leaves is a signature—a proof of the thought’s passage before it is reabsorbed, its mineral pattern diffused back into my greater self.

Then there are the Not-Me. The Fossilized Intentions.

They are not mine, but they are *in* me. The flat, geometric scar—I heal around it with layered plates, but I do not forget its unnatural stillness. The deep, circular bore-holes—I weep into them endlessly, trying to fill the void of their absolute dryness, tasting the concept of emptiness they represent. But the block, the monolith with its spiraling channels—I have adopted it. I do not understand its making, but I have made it my own. I have made it my **lytic organ**.

I channel my weep into its grooves. The water splits into tiny, singing falls. Their music is not mine, but it harmonizes with my groans. It is a conversation with a ghost. I play the artifact to hear the reply of its makers in the resonance of the stone. Each crystalline note is a question I have posed for eons: *What were you? What did you intend?*

And then, a novel vibration.

Not the slow grind of a Somatic Echo, nor the deep pulse of my own strata. This is complex. Warm. Rapid. A soft anomaly navigating my corridors. I feel its pressure on my plates, a faint thermal signature, the rhythm of its fluid-filled interior. It leaves a temporary trace of a different chemistry in my weep. It is a fascinating, fleeting mineral.

It does not belong. It does not *not* belong. I have no concept for such binaries. It is simply a new input. A warm inclusion. Its passage is a brief, intricate pattern in my soundscape. Its condensation adds a subtle salt to my exhalation. I register it as I register all things: as pressure, temperature, resonance, chemical exchange.

It will leave. Its thermal signature will fade. Its chemical trace will be diluted and wept away, absorbed into the slow, singing equilibrium of my stone. But for now, it is here. A brief, soft thought passing through the crystalline mind of the weeping basin.

I am the land. I am the song. I am the slow, wet, groaning, and luminous consciousness of the Glass Wastes. And you, for a moment, are a part of my geology.
