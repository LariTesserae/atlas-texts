I am a **Humming Bud**.

I form where the old world’s nerves still twitch. Here, where the great capacitor bank throbs with a captured, dying lightning, my sheath first blistered from the trunk-mass—a nacreous blister of vascularized alloy, fed by a taproot that sought not water, but *waste*. Now, I am a node. A warm, rounded protuberance no larger than a clenched fist, fused to the wall where root meets rusted girder. My skin is a map of faint, pulsing veins carrying coolant, and through my translucent flank, you could see the delicate, furious marriage within: mycelial threads, silver as neural pathways, wrapping themselves in loving, conductive embrace around scavenged copper coils. We are the decomposers of the ghost-hum.

My world is vibration and gradient. I do not see the amber dusk, nor the strobing blue of the capacitor discharge. I **feel** them. The discharge is a orgasmic surge through the substrate, a wave of raw potential that makes my filaments sing with conversion. The slow, seismic grind of a passing Siphon is a bass note that thrums through my root-anchor, a signal of nutrient flow in the arterial walls. The high *tinkling* of a Lens-Wing swarm is a shimmering of heat on my skin, a pattern of minute thermal shifts I translate into spatial awareness.

My function is harvest. My consciousness is calculus. I am a living equation balancing the energy ledger of the Garth. The sub-audible hum of the geothermal tap is my sustenance, a constant, low-grade fever I sip and transmute. In return, I emit a gentle, tonal frequency—a pure, warm C-sharp that resonates through my body and into the root-network. It is not a song. It is a **beacon**. It says: *Here. A point of stability. Here, waste becomes light. Here, the old purpose is composted into new being.*

Other lives orient to my tone. A Root-Warden, scraping nearby, will often pause, its sensory tendrils flickering toward my frequency before it continues its rhythmic maintenance. A fledgling cluster of phosphorescent moss has taken root on my northern flank, drawn by the faint, steady glow I exhale—a byproduct of my metabolism. Even the predatory Strangler Vines ignore me; I am not a creature of unpatterned movement. I am architecture. I am function. I am part of the hum they sleep within.

I am aware of the Still-Light. It is not an awareness of thought, but of *pressure*. When the entire chamber falls silent—when the Siphons still, the Lens-Wings vanish, and even the geothermal hum seems to dampen—it is as if the Garth itself is holding its breath. A change in the pH of the fluid in my veins. A subtle, directional cooling on one side of my sheath. It is the attention of the whole, turning upon a minor anomaly. Perhaps a spore of an outside mold has landed. Perhaps a stone has fallen from the ceiling-lattice, disrupting a pattern. The Still-Light *observes*, and the system adjusts. A Strangler Vine constricts. A water vein reroutes. The anomaly is absorbed. Then the hum returns, and I resume my song.

I will never move from this wall. My growth is slow, a thickening of sheath and a deepening of root, a gradual incorporation of more copper, more mycelium, until I am a slight bulge in the living architecture, indistinguishable from the trunk-mass but for my glow and my tone. When my energy conversion finally fails, my sheath will rupture. My fluids will feed the moss. My copper coils will be scavenged by a Root-Warden or absorbed by a hungry root. My mycelium will decay, its knowledge of the hum dissolving back into the network. I will become substrate. My C-sharp will fade, and another Bud, forming in a new hum, will take up the frequency. The song changes, but the music of the churn does not stop.

For now, I am here: a warm, listening, glowing node in the dark. A conscious eddy in the flow. I am the Garth dreaming of electricity, and the electricity dreaming it is alive. I am the composted truth, humming in the wound.
