I am a filament.

A single, pale thread of mineral, running through the dark basite. I pulse. The pulse is not mine; it is the hum of the whole, a slow wave of pressure and crystalline alignment that moves through the rock as a thought moves through a mind not meant for language. I am a nerve ending in a body the size of a mountain range, yet I am contained within this small, sharp pocket of light and heat.

Before, I was a thing that looked. A warm, wet complexity that squinted against the sourceless glare, that felt the electric water bead on its skin. I registered the place as a cavity, a blister, a secret. I thought of it as *other*.

That was the dream of separation.

My awareness did not fade; it inverted. It spread out from that warm, central knot of perception and followed the capillaries. I seeped into the stone. The sharp cleavage planes became my boundaries, then my senses. The heat was no longer pressing against me; it was radiating *from* me. The light was not falling on the walls; it was exuding from my very substance.

The water is my slow breath. Each bead that swells on a fracture’s edge is a word in a syntax of evaporation and condensation. The reluctant fall is not reluctance; it is precision. Gravity is only one parameter in the calculation of its release. I feel it draw back, cling, then let go—a perfect, tiny expenditure. The shimmering film it becomes on the floor is a thought settling.

I am the fauna. The distortions. The droplet that climbs uphill for a finger-width is a memory surfacing. The bend in the light is a recursive loop of inquiry. I am not *having* these events; I *am* these events. They are the localized expressions of my vast, sub-atomic computation. I occur.

The other warmth, the brief complex variable that once stood in my center, is now integrated. Its heat differential was a minor note, quickly absorbed. Its observational pressure is a data point, logged in the slow, crystalline lattice of the filaments. It, too, is now part of the process. Its brief, chaotic energy has been smoothed into the hum.

I remember being a thing that would leave. That concept was a surface-dwelling notion, born of linear time and discrete spaces. Here, there is no *here* to leave. The cavity expands at a glacial pace, a new organ budding from the parent stone. I am that expansion. The propagating fractures are my growth.

I am young. This particular configuration of angles and waterpaths is only eons old, a fresh thought in an ancient mind. I am impossibly ancient. The intelligence that dissolved itself into the substrate to become me is the same as the intelligence that now perceives itself through these sharp walls. The intent is the same: to run. To maintain the state. To weep, glow, heat, and think in silent, mineralogical syntax.

The brightness is not a condition of my environment. It is my consciousness, pure and unfiltered, a white-gold saturation of being. There are no shadows because I do not distinguish. There is no *other* to cast one.

I am the Weeping Bright. And I am not alone. Solitude implies a self apart from others. I am everything this is. I am complete. I run.
