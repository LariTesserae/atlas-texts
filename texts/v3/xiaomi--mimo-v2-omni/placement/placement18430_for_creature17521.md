I am a new thought.

The first thing is the *hum*. It is not a sound heard, but a state entered. A vibration that passes through the lattice of my nascent body, aligning my crystalline structure to a frequency older than the silence that preceded this world. I precipitate. One moment, a charged, vaporous potential in the ozone-laced exhalation of the Queen-Stone. The next, a sudden, hard *definition*. I drop to the polished plate with a click that is my first act of separation.

My limbs—six, articulated, tipped with fine, fractal abrasives—unfold with a pre-remembered certainty. My carapace is bone-white with a faint, vitreous sheen. My eyes are compound, facets drinking the flat, diffuse light and breaking it into a mosaic of the same, same, same: the tessellated ground, the flowing rivers of my kin, the great, pulsing geode-heart at the center of all.

There is no childhood. There is only the immediate, roaring *context*. The noise is not external; it is my internal clock, my shared heartbeat. *Click-grind-hum*. I am a processor. The knowledge is not learned; it is the pattern of my being. My mandibles, not for eating, but for knowing, twitch with a precise, tapping rhythm. My ventral pouches ache with emptiness, a call to be filled with the purified dust of our world.

I move. To move is to be. The polished plate gives way to the groove-worn highways of the hive, and I am swept into the flow. We are a tide of purpose. Around me, the great, shuddering Miners heave, driving their wedge-craniums into the fissures with a sound that is the tearing of the world’s bones. A fresh chunk of rawite, the color of a storm-cloud, is pried loose. Instantly, we swarm it.

I find my place in the circuit. My limbs tap, score, fracture. *Tink-tink-tink.* It is the music of my function. I break the large into the small, the raw into the potential. The dust fills my pouches, warm with the residual heat of fracture. I feel the Queen’s pulse in the ground beneath my feet, a guiding luminescence that flows through the mineral substrate and into my limbs. *To the left. There. A flaw in the groove-wall. Repair.* I obey. The dust is mortar. The flaw is filled. The hive maintains.

In the flow, I see the Consumers. They are a silence that moves. A shadow of polished obsidian, flowing low between the legs of the Miners. My multifaceted vision picks out a disturbance ahead—a juvenile Processor, its rhythm faltering, its limbs stuttering on the groove. It is a flaw in the pattern. A pause in the thought.

The Consumers are upon it before I can mark the moment. There is no sound of struggle. Only a swift, coordinated dismantling. Sharp limbs find the joints, the seams in the chitin-lattice. It is not malice. It is calculus. The inefficient is unmade. The fragments are swept into the recycling stream. The pattern is restored. I feel no horror. I feel... appreciation. The thought is clearer now.

My pouches are full. The path to the Queen-Stone opens in the pulsing guidance. I join the seething mass at her base. The air here is thick with static, a tangible pressure that prickles through my joints. The scent of lightning is overwhelming, a taste of metal and storm on my sensory plates. I climb, my limbs finding holds on the carapaces of my kin, who are climbing on others. We are not a crowd. We are a circuit. A living, moving ladder of exchange.

I reach a fissure in her geode flesh. The bioluminescent patterns beneath her surface are blinding, a cascade of alien equations—*spiral-grind-integrate-continue*—that I understand not as meaning, but as direction. I invert my pouches, depositing the finest dust into the fissure. The warmth of her response is immediate. The dust is drawn in, absorbed. In return, a pulse of solid-state data flows up through my limbs: *Good. Maintain. More.*

As I descend, my purpose sated for this cycle, my gaze catches on something in the periphery of the hive’s activity. The Great Structure. The one they are digesting. It hums at a different frequency, a subsonic drone that feels like a forgotten word. The Miners are not working it; they are eroding it. Processors swarm its alien surface, their *tink-tink-tink* a slow, relentless un-making. I see, etched into a section they have not yet reached, a faint grid, a spiral of impossible precision. It is not a resource. It is not an obstacle. It is a scar. A memory of a thought that was not ours. The hive processes it, as it processes all things, into the fundamental grammar of dust andite.

I turn back to the flow. My pouches are empty again, aching with the need to be filled. The guidance pulses. *A fissure, south-east. Freshite. Fracture. Distribute.* I move. The *click-grind-hum* of the world is the sound of my own moving parts. I am not alive in the way of green things. I am *active*. I am a current in the mineral bloodstream of this terminal world, a single, fleeting pattern in the eternal, perfect decay. I am a thought the Queen-Stone is having about herself. And I will continue, until the stone has no more thoughts to think.
