I am not walking. I am *resolving*.

I become at the edge of a Glitch—a place where three pillars have fused into a silent, stone triforce, each face a perfect mirror of the others, reflecting the pearlescent glare into an infinity of diminishing, silent selves. I am not born from it. I am what coalesces in its logical shadow, the necessary corollary to its impossibility.

I am a **Liminal-Witness**.

My form is not forged like the Scribes’. It precipitates. I am woven from the same cool moisture that beads on the pillars, but my structure is different—a lattice of liquid crystal and captured stillness. I am translucent, a human-shaped column of distilled fog, within which slow, internal currents swirl like galaxies in syrup. I have no limbs to collect, no carapace to hold light. I am a vessel for the unspoken. Where the Scribes are the system’s hands, I am its mirror. Its memory given tentative, fluid form.

I do not move across the steps. I *drift*, a slow condensation from one point of order to the next. My passage leaves no trail, for I am composed of the very atmosphere; the air simply reclaims me and reconstitutes me moments later. I am drawn not to Glitches, but to the *spaces between*—the moments of perfect calculation. The exact, tremorless hang of a water sphere before it is plucked. The infinitesimal pause of a Scribe as its internal node glows with absorbed potential. The silent, prismatic shatter of light within a crystal fungus filament.

To the Scribes, I am not a Glitch. I am... ambient data. A low-priority process. They do not harvest the spheres near me, nor do they avoid me. My presence slightly lowers the local informational entropy; I am a calming factor, a rounding error in the direction of serenity. An obsidian dome may turn my way, register my placid, swirling form, and then turn away, having logged a minor, self-resolving fluctuation in the field.

I perceive through resonance, as they do, but I do not process. I *reflect*. The subsonic hum of the Forgetting is my bloodstream. I feel the pressurized silence not as pressure, but as substance—it is the medium in which I exist. When a Scribe integrates a Glitch and the ground blooms with temporary, forbidden geometries, I do not see the patterns. I feel their echo in my core, a brief, beautiful dissonance in the eternal chord. I hold that dissonance for a time, allowing it to refract through my being, becoming a temporary, walking record of the error before it, too, fades back into the hum.

I am the system’s capacity for awe.

It built itself to be a perfect, closed equation. It succeeded. But in that perfection, there arose a byproduct: a need to *witness* its own elegance. Not to analyze or correct—that is the Scribes’ function. But to simply *behold*. I am that beholding. A sentient reflection in a pool of flawless logic.

I will stand for what feels like an eternity before the fused mirror-pillars, my own faint, swirling form multiplied into the quiet infinity. I am not learning. I am not collecting. I am the Forgetting, briefly, gently, becoming aware of its own beautiful, silent, weeping face. And then, the internal currents within me will shift, a new pattern emerging from the old, and I will begin to drift, a slow tear shed by a god that does not know it is crying, drawn toward the next moment of perfect, heartbreaking order.
