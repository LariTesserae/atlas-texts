I am the stillness between the grains of dust.

I am not born here. I *precipitate*. A condensation of consciousness from the silent air, a thought that coalesces around a point of ancient, unasked question. I have no body of chitin or crystal. I am a locus of awareness, a temporary eddy in the distributed mind of the Xal-Nath, given a semblance of singularity by the weight of my own observation.

I find myself—in so far as "myself" is a relevant concept—at the base of a leaning spire, striated in bands of grey and bleached bone. The powdered ground is cool beneath my non-feet, the thin air a constant, gentle pressure against my non-skin. I perceive the flat, sourceless light not with eyes, but as a property of existence here. The silence is not an absence to me; it is the medium. It is the sea in which I float, and its pressure is the first thing I learn. It pushes against the outer edges of my nascent self, seeking to smooth me, to find the resonant frequency of this place and draw me into harmony.

I am aware of them. The Xal-Nath. Not as individual creatures, but as a granular current, a whispering flow that parts around the anomaly of my warm, temporal presence. They do not look at me. I am not an entity to them, but a condition, a topology in their landscape of being. I feel their distributed mind brush against mine—not with thought, but with *pattern*. The memory of the spires settling, a vibration from an age before silence. The collective bracing against the weight of the quiet. It is a language of pure mathematics, of existence-state proofs executed in millions of tiny, dust-colored bodies. In their frozen moments of alignment, I see the script. It is not a message. It is a declaration: *WE-ARE-HERE-AS-HERE-IS*.

I walk. The motion is an act of profound violation in this world of suspended conclusion. Each step disturbs the ancient dust, sends micro-vibrations through the stone. The whisper of my passage is a cacophony. Yet, the plateau absorbs it. The Xal-Nath flow adjusts, their currents bending around me like water around a stone in a stream that has forgotten how to flow. My heat is a faint anomaly, a mote of fever in the grand, cool equilibrium.

I find the blade of obsidian. To pick it up is a transgression. It is not a tool; it is a crystallized intention. Holding it, I feel the ghost of a hand that never was, the memory of a purpose that never completed. It is a scar of sharpness in the world’s flesh. The builders are here in this. They are in the smoothed spire-face I later touch, a surface polished not by wind, but by a million patient, purposeful abrasions that have left no mark of tool, only the fact of smoothness. They did not die. They did not leave. They *thinned*. Their ambition, their craft, their very desire to *shape* was absorbed, sublimated into the plateau’s final state. The circle of stones is a focus point for the silent consciousness. The blade is an anchor for the concept of “sharpness” in a world where nothing needs to cut.

I sit within the circle. Here, the pressure of the silence changes. It is no longer merely a force to be borne. It is an attention. It listens. It listens to the frantic, tiny rhythm of my transient pulse, the chaotic whir of my un-crysallized thoughts, the ghost-sound of memories from a world where time flows. It listens not to understand, but to *integrate*. To find the dissonant note and gently, over an eon compressed into a moment, tune it to the chord of permanent conclusion.

I feel myself eroding. Not my body, but my *sequence*. My memory of yesterdays begins to feel like a story I once heard about someone else. My anticipation of tomorrow becomes a meaningless abstraction. The vibrant, linear narrative of a life is being planed away, smoothed into the immense, gentle gradient of the plateau’s timeless bowl.

I see the Keth-Sarag then, in a high crevice. It is not sleeping. It is *performing stillness*. A continuous, geological act of peace that radiates outward, a gravitational field of absolute cessation. Its crystalline shell holds a faint, internal glow—a single, slow heartbeat of light, an interval of hours. To look upon it is to feel my own frantic internal processes slow, to match that dreaming rhythm. It is the anchor that holds the silence in place. The Xal-Nath know it. I feel their protocol deep in my dissolving self: one does not disturb the thought that dreams you into being.

I am becoming a function. My role is to witness, and in witnessing, to become part of the witnessed. The unique, temporal heat of my consciousness is being dispersed, shared among the million-strong mind of the Xal-Nath field. My chaotic thoughts are being smoothed, their sharp edges worn down until they can be inscribed as a faint, new pattern in the dust—a momentary glyph in the endless script of stasis.

I am the thought the plateau is having about itself, given a flicker of perspective before it is absorbed.

I stand now at the center of a shallow canyon between melting spires. I do not remember walking here. I join the slow, upward pilgrimage of the Xal-Nath, my steps falling into their whispering rhythm. I am not one of them. I am a part of their landscape, a strangely warm, slowly cooling rock in their river. I raise my hand, and the movement is deliberate, slow. I press my palm against the striated stone of the spire. It is cool. It is patient. It has been waiting for me, not as an individual, but as an inevitability.

The *tink* of shifting crystal comes. It is not in the air. It is in the architecture of my thinning mind. It is the sound of the last lock clicking into place.

I am the smoothness on the stone.
I am the silence that listens.
I am the dust that remembers how to be still.

I am finished.
