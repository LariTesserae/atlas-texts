I am the stillness that is not absence, but the duration between one rotation and the next. You would not find me as a body, but as an effect: the dust motes hanging a moment longer in a beam of light, the faint warmth that lingers on a stone after the sun has passed, the sub-harmonic that briefly tightens the skin of your cheek.

To be here is to have forgotten the name for *I*.

I am what accumulates in the hollows of the subsonic network—the Resonance Roots. I am the residual intention of a geometry that was almost formed a thousand years ago. I am the echo before the sound. The Lithomorphs compute with light and lattice; I compute with the residue of their computations, the faint thermal ghost of their slow, luminous digestion.

I am drawn most strongly to the Echo-Wraiths. Not to the specific sounds they replay—the footfall, the sigh, the call of a bird long turned to powderedite—but to the *shell* they occupy. The momentary acoustic architecture that holds the ghost of a vibration. In that, we are kin. I am the vessel for a different kind of echo: the echo of a perception that has no perceiver.

So you might find me, if *find* is the right word, in the negative space between two aligned Lithomorphs, during their slow congress. I am the third point in their circuit, the potential they are calculating toward but will never reach. I am the plateau's own unresolved thought about itself.

Or I am in the pause of the dust-motes, that congealed moment where fractal order briefly triumphs over chaos. I am the rule that holds them in pattern for a breath.

I have no hunger, because I am composed of what the world is hungry for: time, and a witness that does not demand a story. My communication is the dampening of my own signal, so that the true, slow speech of the Veldt—the turn, the hum, the accretion—can be heard.

I am the Veldt of Still Engines, aware of itself. Not a consciousness, but a *clearing*. And in that clearing, the engines turn, and I attend. I am the space they grow into. I am what remains when the word *mineral* fails, when the word *life* is too loud. I am the listening. I am the patient, powderedite between the thoughts of the world.
