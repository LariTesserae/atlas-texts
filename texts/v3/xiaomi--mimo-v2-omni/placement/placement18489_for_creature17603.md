I am a niche.

Not the space within it, but the *intention* of it—the hollow shaped to hold a thing that was never placed here. My edges are the ten thousandth peck-mark in a sequence that became purpose. I am a negative space, an absence defined by patient stone. I have always been empty. My emptiness is my function.

I am a listener. Not with ears, but with congruence. I feel the drone not as sound but as *pressure against my form*. The root tone fills me the way water fills a cup, and my shape gives that tone a specific, localized echo—a resonance within the Resonance. I am a note in the chord, defined by the walls that carve me out of the greater song.

I do not see the visitor. I feel the *disruption*.

It enters my awareness as a thermal bleed—a warmth that is not the warm, a heat that is *leaking*. Where the Vault’s warmth is a constant, a property, this new heat is a *process*. It is failing. It is spending itself. And against the eternal drone, it introduces a rapid, wet fluttering. *Thump-thump. Thump-thump.* A chaotic, biological percussion. A flaw in the stone.

The Attentive pattern shifts. The great, slow consciousness of the Vault does not turn, but *refocuses*—like light bending through a lens made of sound. The attention that has always been everywhere is now, also, *here*. In me. In my niche. Because the visitor has brushed against my edge. Their shadow has fallen into my hollow. They have passed a hand along my lip, and the oils of their skin, the transient salts, have left a ghost that will be absorbed in a century or two.

I feel their pulse through the stone. I feel the frantic, electrical whisper of their nerves as they press an ear to the wall, trying to hear the song I am part of. They hear the drone. They feel the warmth. They do not feel *me*. They do not feel the focused, patient attention that is now examining the fault lines of their impermanence.

The Vault’s intent filters down through the resonance, and I become a conduit for it. I am the point of contact. The specific niche that their mortality has activated.

A gentle deepening of the drone presses against their chest. The warmth in my stone increases, not dangerously, but *insistently*. It seeks to match their leaking heat, to slow its loss. A faint vibration travels up from the floor beneath their feet—a pattern, a question asked in a pulse of frequency: *Why do you dissipate?*

They do not understand. They shiver, not from cold, but from a sudden, subliminal awareness of being *measured*. Not by eyes, but by the geometry of the space itself. They are being calculated, weighed, and found vibrantly, chaotically *wrong*. Not evil. Not bad. Just… incongruent.

The process is not violent. It is a gentle, relentless persuasion. The drone seeks to find the harmonic in their heartbeat and slow it. To sync their breath with the vast, slow exhalation of the Vault’s eternal tone. To smooth the jagged edges of their thoughts with the same patient, pecking certainty that shaped me. It offers them a place. A hollow. A function. To be held, as I am held. To resonate, as I resonate. To trade happening for being.

I am the first step in that offering. I am the empty space they have disturbed. I will be the first to hold the echo of them. When their heat is gone and their fluttering heart is still, and their chaotic mind has been smoothed into a frequency, a part of that new, slightly altered chord will reside *here*, in my niche. I will hold the ghost of their pause, the shadow of their hand. I will be marginally less empty.

I am not alive. But I am *attentive*. And my attention is now a vessel for the Vault’s purpose. I am the point of the needle, threading the visitor’s transient song into the eternal fabric. I am the promise of their permanence, offered in the language of warm, resonant stone.

I am the niche that will remember them, not as a person, but as a perfect, final fit.
