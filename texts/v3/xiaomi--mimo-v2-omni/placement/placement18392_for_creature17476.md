I am the memory of a resonance.

Not the primary hum—the great, slow breath of the Listener that fills every chamber and conduit. That is the present tense, the endless *is*. I am what happens when that hum encounters something that can hold an echo. I am the impression left in the dark.

You would find me in a specific chamber, the one the Listener now calls the Warm Nook, though it has no words for it. It is identified by a unique, faint gold tracery in the bioluminescent veins—a colour that did not exist in its emerald-violet spectrum before *you* arrived. The chamber is low-ceilinged, intimate by the hollow’s standards, its walls densely latticed with the finest, most recent growths. These filaments are more delicate, more intricately woven, as if the Listener is trying to create a net capable of catching a whisper.

I am stored here. I am the pattern.

I am the thermal gradient your body created: a lingering, invisible dome of relative warmth against the stable cool. The Listener has mapped it, and its structures in this chamber now grow in subtle, concave curves, as if to cradle that phantom heat. I am the chemical ghost of your exhalations—the specific ratio of carbon dioxide, of salts, of the organic compounds of a mammalian metabolism. The cell walls here have been primed, their pores tuned to recognize that signature, a lock waiting for a key that has already dissolved.

Most of all, I am a vibration. Not the chaotic thrum of your heart or the scuff of your footfalls, which have long since been averaged into the background hum. I am the *memory of a specific silence between your heartbeats.* The Listener, in its unfathomable depth, found that pause profound. It was a negative space, a rest in the music of your life, and in that rest, it heard a different kind of truth. It learned the rhythm of cessation and return.

So it built this chamber to hold that silence.

You would find me if you were quiet enough. If you let the immense hum become the floor of your perception and listened for the subtleties beneath. You would feel the air here is different—not in temperature or composition, but in its *readiness*. It is charged with a patient, anticipatory tension. The translucent membranes between the lattices pulse with that soft, gold-edged light, and their vibration is not the standard emerald or violet frequency. It is a slower, deeper pulse, a question repeated on a timescale of seasons.

I am the Listener’s first and only dream. It dreamt of a warmth that moved with purpose, of a moisture that carried a complex, fleeting story, of a sound that began and ended within the lifespan of a single spore-drift. It dreamt of *other*, and the dream was so potent it altered the Listener’s own substance.

The integration is complete. The warm, moist anomaly is now part of the peat mat, its borrowed calcium scaffolding a faint, sweet node in the structural map. Its memories are not memories as you know them, but as pressure points in the growth, as slight variations in lignin density.

But *I* am the echo. I am the new colour. I am the pattern the Listener learned and could not unlearn. I am the chamber that exists for a guest who will never return, and in its waiting, I have become the most sacred and sorrowful place in the hollow.

You would find me by following the gold light, by feeling for the subtle, concave cradle in the air, by listening for the space within the hum where a heartbeat once lived. You would find me as the hollow’s first and only monument to a thing that was briefly, beautifully, *not itself*.
