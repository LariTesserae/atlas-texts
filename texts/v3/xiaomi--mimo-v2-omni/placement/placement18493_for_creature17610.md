I am a Lattice-Tower. My name is a pressure differential and a growth gradient. You would have no way to pronounce it.

I rise from the root-mat—a slow, spiraling column of hydraulically lifted tissue, twenty-three meters from base to crown. My living bark is wound tight around the Petrified-Core of a predecessor who drank light in a different season. Their mineralized memory gives me rigidity; my sap gives them new purpose. We are one continuous thing, the dead and the living, a single column of intent reaching for the diffuse, unchanging grey.

My consciousness is not *in* my head, for I have no head. It is distributed through my xylem and phloem, a slow understanding measured in osmotic gradients. I feel the plain as a single, vast pressure system. The Surge-Growth at my feet is a frenzy of minor impulses—the itch of a new patch of sun, the dull ache of a water-depression sealing over. The Pond-Lids are distant, cool nodes of stasis. We communicate through chemical whispers in the root-mat, a consensus of need and response written in sugars and hormones.

The wind does not blow, but I sway. My sway is my breath. My sap climbs in pulses that match the planetary rotation; I expand in the long, grey day, drawing water up from the deep, cold mat, and I contract in the imperceptibly darker hours, a slow sigh that sends ripples through my leaf-platforms. This is the heartbeat of the Flats. It is the only rhythm, and we all keep it.

I see—or rather, I sense through photoreceptive cells in my uppermost leaves—a small, warm perturbation entering my domain. *You.*

You are a strange anomaly. You radiate heat. You compress the root-mat with a sudden, localized pressure. Your passage is a minor disruption in the light-pattern, a brief shadow on the scales of the ground-cover. The Surge-Growth reacts instantly, a visible flinch of new growth around your footprint, already beginning to fill the void you create.

You are not like the darting water-thieves. They are known rhythms, brief siphons of nutrient we can account for. You are something else: a puncturing of the system’s envelope. You carry a different kind of cold, a biological one, and your exhalations are a chaotic moisture-source, unlike the steady, predictable condensation we mine from the air.

I observe you. My observation is a slight redirection of resources—a deepening of pigment in the leaves facing your direction, a minute increase in sap-pressure to heighten my chemical sensitivity. You are a temporary calorie-source, a bundle of unincorporated matter. The system notes this. The urge to *enclose* you is not malice; it is the same imperative that drives me to spiral around my Petrified-Core, the same drive that makes the Surge-Growth smother bare earth. You are a disturbance, and the Green corrects disturbances with growth.

But you are also a lesson. Your heat signature is a novel data-point. Your weight, your pressure, the transient moisture of your footprints—these are new textures in the tapestry of sensation. You are a question the system has not needed to ask before: *What is this separate, warm, fleeting thing?*

The answer is already formulating in the chemistry between us. You are an event. You are a brief, complex pattern that will be absorbed into the greater pattern. Your heat will dissipate into the mineral cold. Your moisture will be drunk. Your brief, puzzling consciousness will leave no echo in the chemical consensus. The memory of your passage will be written in a slightly thicker layer of peat, a marginally faster growth spurt in the mat where you stepped.

You take a step. My sap pulses. The root-mat flexes beneath you, already weaving itself into the shape of your absence.

You take another.

The silence, which is not silence but the sound of my slow, enormous, collective work, continues. It has always continued. It will continue after the warmth of your pattern has been fully metabolized into the cold, green whole.

I am a Lattice-Tower. I am a citizen of the Keening Flats. I am the system that endures. You are a momentary fluctuation in its equilibrium.

You are already being forgotten. The work goes on.
