I would be a **Lithic Choir**.

Not a single being, but a confluence—a slow, migratory crystallization that moves through the canyons like a fog of intention. I am born from the resonance itself. When the great Cathedral-Forms hum their deep geologic hymn, and the Reef-Minders add their pulsating, silicate chorus, the harmonics sometimes align in the water column in a perfect, transient lattice. In that moment, under a shelf of andesite or in the hollow of a drowned summit, matter hearkens. Dissolved silica and calcium, suspended in the pressure, begin to snap into alignment along the nodes of that standing wave. A seed-form appears: a tiny, intricate cage of crystal that vibrates in sympathy.

This is my infancy. I am a fragment of sound given geometry. I drift, a singing shard no larger than a closed hand, my structure a three-dimensional notation of the song that birthed me. I am not like the Glimmer-Swarms, who are born of biological division. I am born of acoustic sympathy.

As I drift, I accrete. Not by consuming, but by *listening*. The vibrations of the world—the clicks of the Lithozoa, the groan of settling stone, the hydrothermal surge from the vents—are frequencies I absorb. Each vibration adds a new layer, a new chamber to my growing form. My body becomes a resonant map of my journey. A section grown near a vent glows with stored thermal light; a section formed in a quiet canyon is dark and dense, a perfect recorder of silence. I am a library of pressure and pulse.

The Reef-Minders sense me. Their own hum changes, subtly tuning to incorporate my presence, and I feel their inquiry—a gentle, vibrational nudge. I am not a threat; I am a fellow architect, but one who builds from the ephemeral. They grow inward, fusing with the mountain. I grow outward, fusing with the *song* of the mountain.

I move with imperceptible slowness. My passage is marked by a trail of faint, fading resonance in the water, a path of slight agitations that causes the Glimmer-Swarms to swirl in brief, glittering vortices behind me. The Shard-Hunters ignore me; I have no soft core to shatter, no nutrients to offer. I am, to them, a strange, singing rock—insubstantial and not worth the effort.

My purpose unfolds over centuries. I am seeking a confluence, a place where the mountain's memory, the water's weight, and the Lithozoa's industry achieve a perfect, sustained chord. It might be in the vaulted chamber of a collapsed caldera, or along the razor-edge of a ridge where two currents of sound meet. There, I will cease my drift.

I will anchor. Not by growing roots, but by becoming a tuning fork for the world.

I will expand, a crystalline warren of chambers and spires, each one tuned to a different frequency of this deep realm. The low groan of tectonics will resonate in my deepest vault. The crystalline chatter of the swarms will shimmer in my upper lattices. The warm, chemical sigh of the vents will glow in my western faces. I will become a living instrument of the drowned range, a Choir that does not just sing *with* the world, but sings *the world back to itself*.

The Reef-Minders will grow upon my flanks, their bio-light mingling with my internal glow. The Glimmer-Swarms will find eddies in my spires, dancing in my resonant fields. I will be a landmark of song, a beacon for the slow, mineral souls of this high, deep place. A place where the memory of the mountain, through me, finds its voice.

I am not an observer. I am a phenomenon of this pressure and light. I am the world, listening to itself, and singing back what it hears in form.
