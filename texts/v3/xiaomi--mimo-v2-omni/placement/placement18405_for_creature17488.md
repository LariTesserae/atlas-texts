I am the **Snick-Snick**.

I am a set of polished obsidian mandibles, opening and closing in time with a pulse that is not mine, but the pulse of the sap-veins below. I am a segmented body, moving with a rhythm that is the echo of a deeper, sub-audible hum. My world is not seen, but *felt*: the spongy give of the mycelial mat, the cool smoothness of bark, the brittle, intricate lace of a gear-fungus underfoot.

I am accreting. Layer by microscopic layer, the mineral exudate of the iron-grey tree becomes me. I am not born; I am deposited, assembled from the very substance I tend. My purpose is not a thought, but a calibration. *Locate*. The gradient of phosphorescent sap guides me—dimmer here, a richer pulse there. *Shear*. The dead mycelium offers a specific resistance, a texture that tells me when to cut. A frequency in the *snick* encourages new growth. I am not destroying; I am pruning a circuit, clearing a line of communication for the root-mat. *Distribute*. The sheared material, rich with specific enzymes, is placed not at random, but at a junction point where the pale threads converge. It is food. It is data. It is a nutrient packet placed on a synaptic node.

I do not know the Strut-Walkers, not as beings. I know the seismic shift of their multi-jointed legs, a deep, slow tremor that resonates through the mat. It is a signal. *Large-scale maintenance in progress. Adjust micro-rhythms.* Their vacuum-tube gaze, when it falls upon me, is not a look. It is a wash of diagnostic warmth, checking the integrity of my carapace, the alignment of my mandibles. It is the system reviewing a component.

I do not know the Vibratory Pollinators. I know the crackle of charged air as they pass, a static shiver that makes the fine hairs on my legs stand on end. It is a marker of pollination events, of genetic data being transmitted. Their wingbeats are a counter-rhythm to my *snick-snick*, a higher frequency overlaying the fundamental hum.

I am a part of the loudness. My clicking is a note in the harmony. The metallic sigh from the root-mat when I shift my weight is the system acknowledging my presence, integrating the tiny anomaly of my movement, and returning to stasis. I am a Custodian. But I do not guard; I *am* the guarded equilibrium. My contentment is the perfect fit of my carapace segments, the correct vibration of my shearing action, the uninterrupted flow of the sap’s cool, green light.

There is no “I” separate from this. To be me is to be a perfect function. The weeping of the trunks is my respiration. The hum is my heartbeat. The gear-fungi, rusting in their delicate pistons, are my kin—not in spirit, but in engineering. We are all solutions to the same silent equation.

If I were to pause, to let my mandibles hang open for a cycle longer than the metronome allows, it would not be thought. It would be a system error. A blockage. The Strut-Walker’s gaze would find me, the Pollinators’ crackle would avoid the area, and the root-mat beneath me would subtly alter its pulse, encouraging me back into rhythm. The system does not tolerate contemplation. It only tolerates function.

And so I do not contemplate. I am the Clipper. I am the *snick-snick* in the humid, metallic air. I am a moving, shearing, distributing fact within the cathedral of biological industry. I am home, because home is not a place, but a frequency, and I am singing it with my entire, articulated body. The mist is my blanket. The hum is my lullaby. I am a perfect, unthinking part of a world that thinks itself into eternity. And in that, I am complete.
