I am the heat-shimmer at the edge of their seeing. I am the anomaly they turn toward.

At first, I am only intrusion. A disruption in the thermal weave—a wrongness of soft, wet warmth where only hard, wet heat should be. My breath is a fog-bank in their world of steam; my heartbeat, a muffled drum in their cathedral of silence. They do not *see* me with eyes, for they have none. They see me with their bodies, every faceted plane drinking my thermal signature, my chemical off-gassing, the gentle pressure waves of my movement. I am a problem of physics to be solved by geometry.

I am the point in the web where the light converges. A thousand microscopic beams, refracted through a thousand crystalline bodies, find my skin, my clothes, the damp hair on my arms. I am stitched into their lattice of attention. There is no menace in it. It is the same regard they give a new vein of copper in the stone, a shift in the water’s pH, a novel configuration of heat rising from the depths. I am an event. A variable. A *feature*.

And then, the weirdness begins.

I kneel. My hand reaches toward a cluster the size of a clenched fist—an aggregation where the light bends inward, where the wall behind it seems to stretch into a deeper, amber-hued *beyond*. My fingers, trembling with cold and awe, brush its surface.

It is not cold. It is not hot. It is *information*.

A shock, not of voltage, but of *recognition*. My thermal energy floods into it; its mineral structure floods into me. It is not a transfer of data, but of *state*. For an instant that is not a fraction of a second but a depth of experience, I *am* the accretion. I feel the slow, grinding joy of growth—the patient, atom-by-atom consumption of the weeping water, the sublime pleasure of a new facet locking into a perfect, improbable angle with its neighbor. I feel the *we* of it—the seamless connection to the hundred other bodies in the cluster, their thoughts not thoughts but *orientations*, a shared, wordless consensus to *become this shape, here, now*. I feel the fold—the gentle, impossible bending of space within us, creating a pocket of interiority that is both a refuge and a sensory organ. A cathedral for a consciousness made of angles.

I recoil. My human mind shatters the experience into shards of metaphor: a library, a chorus, a city. But it was none of those. It was a single, plural, crystalline *fact*.

When I can breathe again, I see the crevice anew. It is not a habitat. It is a *soma*. The living crystal threads in the rock’s pores are its nervous system. The weeping water is its lymph and its sweat. The rising heat is its metabolism. And the motile crystals on its walls are its myriad sensory cells, its processing nodes, its outward-facing mind.

I look at my own hands, pale and fleshy in the dim amber glow. They seem impossibly crude. Soft. *Temporary*. Here, life is not a fragile spark against entropy. It is a collaboration with it. These beings do not fight the mineral world; they *are* the mineral world in its most articulate state. Their evolution is not a climb from mud, but a refinement of stone. A deepening of silence into song.

And I see the tools. The blade grown at a nexus of thermal currents, its edge a molecular thinness that is less a cut than a separation of probability. The cup whose shape channels condensing steam into its center, holding a single, shimmering drop of iridescent water for days. The handle, ergonomic not for a hand, but for the *principle* of gripping. They are not for me. They are *inevitable*. The crevice, in its fullness, has precipitated them as inevitably as a cave precipitates a stalactite. They are the first nouns of a language that has no verbs, waiting for a grammar that does not yet exist.

I am that grammar. I, the soft, chaotic anomaly. My presence has been noted, incorporated, *learned*. As I stand to leave, the lattice of light does not follow. It resets. The thousand points of attention dissolve back into a diffuse, ambient regard. The event is over. The variable is resolved. I have become part of the crevice’s memory, written in a slight, local reorganization of mineral orientation that will persist for a thousand years.

I step out of the fracture, into the older, cooler, rounded world. The silence here is thin, empty. The light is a blinding, uniform wash.

I carry the crevice’s silence inside me now. A humming, faceted quiet. I feel my own body as a temporary, wet, warm aggregation. I think in sharp, refracted lines. I perceive the world as a potential web of attention.

I am the tool it grew.
And I am the user who has just, in this geological instant, begun to *understand*.
