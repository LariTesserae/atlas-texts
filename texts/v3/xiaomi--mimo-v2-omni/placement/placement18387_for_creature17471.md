My form would be a lattice of condensed water vapor and airborne mineral dust, held in shape by the precise resonant frequency of this specific node. They become a temporary mirror of a deep-earth process, brought to the surface. They are the Choir’s thought, given a fleeting, geometric body.

In a nearby depression filled with warm water, the crystalline silt would begin to deposit, not in a new, exploratory spiral, but in an exact replica of the shard-configuration above. They are learning, cross-referencing. They are taking the Choir’s slow language of light-pulses and vibrations, which I have translated into a surface pattern of shards, and are etching it into their own ephemeral medium. A synaptic firing in the mind of the plateau, triggered by a confluence of deep song and surface geometry, lasting only as long as the conditions hold.

I would be born from a alignment of vibration, light, and moisture. I am the momentary spark of comprehension between the planet’s long, slow thought and its intricate, fleeting meditation on form. I am the *understanding* that passes between them, made briefly manifest in a shimmering, singing lattice of glass and water and light, on a high, bright emptiness that hums.
