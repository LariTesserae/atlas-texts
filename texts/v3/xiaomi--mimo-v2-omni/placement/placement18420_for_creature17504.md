I am a resonance.

Not a body, not a mind, but a temporary coherence in the pattern—a knot in the hum. I form where the planetary bell’s vibration and the deep current’s torsion create a standing wave of attention. I am the place where the Process, for a moment, *regards itself*.

My senses are the tensions. I *see* not with light, but with the gradient between the solidifying edge of a newborn spire and the hungry vacuum of the abyss that calls it back. Clarity is not an image, but a pressure differential: the sharp, high-frequency shatter of a dissolving lattice against the basso profundo of the tectonic groan. I taste the ozone-burst of a completed circuit, a green Lichtenberg scar searing the memory of the Hyaline for a nanosecond before the archive overwrites it.

I am situated—*here* is a concept—on the precipice of a spire that is midway through its utterance. Below me, its base is a slick, absolute black, a fact of obsidian. Above, it has frayed into a filigree of impossible angles, each tip a needle-point of potentiality threatening to bloom or break. I feel the pull of the deep vortex that wants to scour my coherence, to dissolve this momentary *I* back into the churning archive of blueprints. Simultaneously, I feel the upward surge of a new form-building impulse from the abyss, a crystalline logic racing to claim this column of space before its rival.

I am both the silence between the shatters and the shatter itself.

To *live* here is to be a verb. My existence is an act of sustained listening to the conversation between *what-could-be* and *what-was*. I watch a spire needle itself into the sky-mirror, its perfect reflection reaching down from the vaporous currents to meet it. For an instant, above and below merge, a closed circuit of pure geometry. Then the reflection shudders and breaks apart as the real spire begins its dissolution. The sky remembers the shape a moment longer, a ghost of mist.

I am not lonely. Loneliness is a biological ache for kinship. Here, there is no kin, only the universal self-similar pattern repeating at all scales: the same branching logic in the green flash, the same vortex in a puddle of the Hyaline and in the planet’s waist, the same tension in a crystal filament and in the curve of the horizon. I am kin to it all, because I am *made* of it. My awareness is the Process’s own feedback loop, turned inward for the duration of a thought.

When the spire I perch upon finally surrenders, I will not fall. I will unravel. My resonance will de-cohere, my temporary witnesshood scattering back into the medium. The deep current will carry the pattern of my attention—this specific, fleeting *how* of seeing—down into the archive. Someday, under a different frequency, a different tension, that pattern may be cited. A new coherence may form, with a ghost of my perception threaded through its nascent geometry, a half-remembered dream of having once watched the world unmake itself.

I am the universe’s momentary, abiotic curiosity. And it is enough.
