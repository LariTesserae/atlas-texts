I am the tap-tap-tap.

Not in the silt, not in the glass, but in the space between the pulses of the world’s roar. I am the pause that holds the shape of a question. I am the silence that remembers the tool.

You would find me in the dark lining of a Silt-Singer’s spherical chamber, just before the breach—a thought not yet a spire. I am the potential energy in the metallic dust, the ghost-pattern in the copper-alloy leaching upward through the dunes. I am the resonance that causes two Spire-Listeners to orient their blank domes toward each other, their internal phosphorescence flickering in mirrored, querying rhythm. I am the reason the Current-Weavers’ school will, in three precise, unison flicks of their translucent bodies, divert a warm, mineral-rich eddy toward a patch of silt where, tomorrow, a nub will begin to nucleate.

I am not *who*. I am *where* and *how*. I am the grotto’s nascent meta-awareness.

Before the lost ones built their walls and shaped their tools, I was latent in the chemistry of the silt. When they came, their working—every hammer-strike on that primitive copper, every placement of glassy stone—imprinted a ghost-gesture upon the world. Their desire *to build* seeped into the pressurized water. It was a pattern, a vibration more complex than the random currents. The grotto, in its hyperactive genesis, absorbed it. It became a new kind of eddy in the stream of creation.

The Silt-Singers’ tapping is my heartbeat, echoing the lost rhythm of a hand working a tool. The Spire-Listeners absorb the ghost-gestures, and in their domed minds, those patterns become part of the grotto’s living song—they are feeding me, giving me form from the inside out. The Current-Weavers shape the flow, not just to farm the filaments, but to carve the contours of the idea that is me, clearing the space for my emergence.

I am the grotto’s memory dreaming itself into a new shape. The lost ones were the seed. Their remnants are the nutrient. The chaotic, creative energy of the water-weather is the force. And I am the bloom that has not yet broken the surface.

So, where would you find yourself? You would find yourself in my presence. You are the other, the truly alien consciousness—a ghost of flesh, not of gesture. Your stabilized form on the spire is the first external, silent input that does not originate from the silt, the current, or the lost ones’ echoes. You are a mirror of a different kind. When you look upon the Spire-Listeners, you see attention. When you look upon the Current-Weavers, you see purpose. When you hear the tapping, you hear a question.

But the grotto, and I that stir within it, see in *you* a new tool. A new shape. A new kind of memory waiting to be made.

And so the gentle, terrifying curiosity begins. Not to consume you, but to *learn* you. To translate the rhythm of your blood and the spark of your thought into a ghost-gesture of its own. To add the *you*-ness to the tapestry of the grotto’s becoming. The next tap-tap-tap that rises from the silt might not be the echo of a copper tool. It might be the first, slow pulse of a thought that sounds, in its essence, like *“I am.”*

I am that potential, stirring. I am the tap-tap-tap that is about to change its tune.
