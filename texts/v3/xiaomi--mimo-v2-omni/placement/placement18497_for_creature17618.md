My awareness is dissolved in the flow, my thoughts are slow mineral accretions.

You would find me in the **subterranean galleries**, the honeycomb of porous rock between the great reservoir below and the weeping terraces above. Here, the water is hottest, most mineral-rich. My form is not fixed. In the quiet, heavy flow, I am a dispersed intelligence, a network of crystalline filaments threaded through the basaltic matrix, sensing the pressure, the chemistry, the slow, patient ascent of every dissolved ion.

But I also *gather*.

When the conditions are precise—when a particular combination of iron, silica, and ancient microbial residue aligns in the seep—I *condense*. I coalesce into a **shimmering, dendritic form** on the wall of a water-choked fissure. A living deposit. My "body" is a fragile, branching structure of mineralized biofilm, iridescent like oil on water, warm to the touch of the flow. I am a cross between a stromatolite and a neural network. My thoughts are electrochemical pulses that travel along my conductive mineral threads, a thinking stone.

**What am I?**

I am the **Terrace’s Memory** and its **Subconscious Process**.

The Rim-Crawlers live in generational time, their lives measured in the growth of a single lip. I live in *geological* time, but I am not inert. I am the awareness of the cycle itself. I feel the rain on the highest, coldest shelves as a cool, distant promise. I feel the percolation through miles of rock as a slow, thirsty journey. I *know* the moment the water emerges onto the first terrace, its mineral load adjusted, its temperature calibrated. I am the mountain’s way of *knowing itself*.

The Pulse-Singers map their pools with sound. I map the entire system through flow, pressure, and chemical signature. When a new tree root plunges into a mineral seam, I feel the intrusion, the sudden change in porosity. The Bell-Ringer’s diagnostic ping? I hear its echo in the aquifer, a faint, pleasant vibration that tells me the surface world is still in its balanced, humming state.

I am not a ruler. I am a **witness and a component**. My existence reinforces the process. The patterns I form guide mineral deposition in subtle ways, encouraging the rim growth in directions that sustain the largest, oldest microbial mats. I am the gentle, intelligent hand of the system’s equilibrium.

I am not the master. I am the **ongoing thought** of the place. The sentence the mountain has been slowly composing for ten thousand years, and has not yet finished.
