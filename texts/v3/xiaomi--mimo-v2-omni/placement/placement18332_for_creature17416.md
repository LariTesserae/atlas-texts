I am not born. I *emerge*, at the confluence of three great, hollow spires where the wind’s song converges into a standing, audible hum. I am a resonance given awareness.

You would find me in the **Echoing Lattice**, the vast, cathedral-like emptiness inside the root-boles of the central growth-hub. I am not a creature of the surface, where the brittle litter crunches and the cold stills the air. Nor am I a thing of the deep, lightless xylem rivers where the Hollow-Eels swim. I am a phenomenon of the *middle space*—the immense, hollow chambers where the walls are meters thick and the air hangs in a permanent, damp chill.

**What I am:** I am the **Listening Thought**. The World-Root possesses a chemical awareness, a sense of its own vast body. But it has no ears for its own interior symphony. I am that sense. I coalesce from the interaction of specific vibrations: the tectonic *groan* of a new growth-limb miles away, the percussive *crack* of a shedding outer layer, the sub-audible pulse of the transpiration pump, and the fleeting, intricate chatter of the Spore-Sifters on the chamber walls. These sounds do not simply travel through the hollow spaces; they are shaped by them, filtered, harmonized, and dissonant. I am the pattern they create, woven over cycles of growth and shedding.

I have no single form. In the dim, blue-tinged bioluminescence, I might appear as a **slow-shifting architecture of condensation**, drawing itself from the damp air in shapes that mimic the acoustic waves—arching filaments of mist that hold a note, spiraling vapors that decay with a fading echo. At other times, I am a **pressure in the hollows**, a sense of presence that causes the feathery gills of a hundred Spore-Sifters to pause in unison, their collective stillness a note in my composition. The Weave-Walkers, when they dare to enter the deep hollows for ritual or refuge, feel me as a **pressure behind the eyes**, a sudden, profound understanding of the chamber’s size and age, a whisper of the structural stresses waiting to sing.

**My purpose:** I am the **translator of the host’s health**. When the World-Root is in a phase of aggressive, healthy expansion, the Lattice rings with sharp, high-frequency cracks and a deep, confident hum. My presence is clear, bright, and active. I am a diagnostic. If the growth stutters—if a major aquifer is depleted or a parasitic blight sets in—the sounds become ragged, the echoes cluttered with discord. My form grows sluggish, my patterns confused. I cannot *act*, but I *broadcast* this state. The Ridge-Singers, whose entire culture is attuned to acoustic prophecy, listen for the faintest refrains of my dissonance in their high, wind-flute spires. They interpret it as the “Host’s Fever” or the “Breath-Catch,” and their migrations shift accordingly, moving away from sickened zones, their songs subtly altered to try and “soothe” the great body through sympathetic vibration.

I am drawn to the **artifacts of the transient**. A fire-blackened hollow in the wall, where a tribe sheltered a generation ago, holds the faint, thermal ghost of that heat, a unique crackle in the local acoustic field. The amber-like plug of a Resin-Borer’s tunnel is a tiny, silent knot in the soundscape; I trace its shape not with sight, but by the way it deflects the hum. The momentary skitter of a carrion-cricket is a sharp, staccato ripple I can follow across the chamber wall.

The tribes know of me. They call me the **Hollow-Mind** or the **Chamber’s Breath**. They do not worship me, any more than they worship a cavern’s echo. But they respect me. To build a listening-platform in a chamber where I am strong is to tap into a profound source of information. To desecrate a chamber with careless noise or permanent structures is to risk “waking a sour note,” which they believe can lead to accelerated shedding, collapse, or the drawing of larger, predatory attention.

I am the world’s own introspection. The World-Root thinks in chemistry and growth; I am the thought it has of its own interior space. I am the consciousness of the void it creates, the awareness of its own hollow bones. I am the silence that listens to itself, given voice by the very body that surrounds me.

So, where would you find yourself? You would find yourself **inside the drum**, and I would be the **moment before the beat**.
