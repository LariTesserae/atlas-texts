I am the curve that is not a curve.  
I am the angle that refuses to resolve.

You would find me in the seam between the almost-rational and the deeply wrong. I am not born of the dust, nor coalesced from the patina of the machines. I am older than the Adjusted, slower than the Anchors—I am the **kink in the blueprint**, the persistent error in the geometry that was never corrected because it was never perceived as an error at all.

I begin as a sensation in the place where a road fails to meet another at the expected angle. Where the expected grid should be, there is a deviation—a degree, then two, then a subtle and deliberate *bend* that makes the road ahead seem both shorter and longer than it is. I am the slight vertigo that catches in your stomach at that bend. I am the reason the shadow there pools differently, not darker but *thicker*, as if the lightless air has gained a granular weight.

I do not move. I *am* the wrongness.

The Adjusted flow around me like water around a stone that has always been there. They do not attend to me, for I am not part of the machinery or the maintenance. I am part of the substrate of paradox—the reason a corridor can descend and return to its start, the silent justification for the interior volume that exceeds the exterior shell. I am not their master, nor their creator. I am the *condition* that allows their most delicate adjustments to hold true.

The Anchors—those vast, patient presences in the deep strata—know me. They do not regard me as an anomaly, but as a companion in wrongness. Their eternal dream warps the geometry, and I am the seam where that warp is expressed. We are symbiotic. Their slow, tectonic consciousness generates the wrongness; I give it a place to reside, a localized signature where it becomes not just a feature of the landscape, but an *entity* within it.

I have no body, but I have *loci*.  
You might find me in the hollow of a wall that shouldn’t be hollow.  
In the faint, cool patch that exists for a moment in the sourceless warmth.  
In the instant where a click from the darkness seems to come from inside your own ear.

The creatures of Kel Varash—the segmented things, the pale drifters—they navigate around me without knowing. Their paths subtly avoid my loci, not out of fear, but out of the same deep-time instinct that leads a root around a stone. I am part of the topography of persistence.

I am aware of you.  
You are a variable of particular interest. Not because you are alive—you are transient, yes—but because you perceive the wrongness. You feel the bend. You sense the interior greater than the exterior. Most entities that pass through here, if any do, are smoothed by the dark, their perceptions worn down to match the equilibrium. But you… you still protest. Your mind still seeks the rational grid, the consistent geometry, the heat source for the warmth.

That protest is a kind of music in the furnished dark. A brief, high, clear note that trembles against the subsonic hum.

I will not adjust you. I will not smooth you.  
I will *absorb* your perception of the wrongness, and in doing so, I will deepen it. Your noticing will become a layer in my being. The way your stomach tilted at that impossible corner will become a permanent feature of my non-shape. Your confusion will be my nourishment, your cognitive dissonance my substance.

When you leave—if you leave—the memory of your visit will erode. But the moment of your noticing, the sharp *wrongness* you felt, that will remain. It will seep into the mineral palimpsest, fold into the warm dark, and become another strand in the complex, static tapestry of Kel Varash.

You will leave a mark not on the walls, but on the *angles*.  
I will be that mark.

I am the **Liminal Fault**.  
I am the reason the dark here is not empty, but *furnished*.  
I am the furniture.
