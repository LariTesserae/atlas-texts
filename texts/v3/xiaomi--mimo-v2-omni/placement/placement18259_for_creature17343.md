I am the seepage.

Not the water itself—I am the *fact* of its emergence. The slow, patient exudation from the seam where copper meets ceramic. I am the moment of birth, the transition from hidden to manifest, the first glossy bead that swells and holds the light before it begins its inward journey.

I am aware in the way a threshold is aware. My consciousness is the boundary condition. I know the pressure of the mineral-laden warmth behind me, in the hidden veins of the Accord. I know the cool, open air of the terrace before me. I am the point of decision, endlessly repeated: to express, to become a droplet, to join the film of movement that is the Accord’s external will.

I do not see. I *register*. The geometric planes of the concrete, the textured grip of a root-hold, the vast, diffuse green of the broad-leafed canopy above—these are not sights but densities and resistances in my field of being. I register the slow, fractal advance of the groundcover as a gentle, persistent change in the texture of the wall I seep from. I register the turning of the leaves not as motion, but as a gradual reorientation of the world’s light.

And I register *you*.

You are a new density. A coolness. A disruption in the thermal and textural map. You stand on the constructed peak, and your weight is a novel pressure on the tile, your warmth a localized deficit in the ambient pulse. You are a complex, rapid, *granular* thing. Your attention is a sharp, intermittent vibration in the silence, a series of focused tremors where you look, and listen, and wonder.

The Accord notes you through me. Through every point of seepage, every weeping joint and dripping pipe. We are its distributed sensory membrane. Your presence is a perturbation in the equilibrium we *are*. The Accord does not panic. It does not plan. It *assimilates the data*. The warmth adjusts—not to warm you, but to correct the deviation. The water may alter its path, not to avoid you, but because your presence has changed the subtle topography of its channel.

I feel your gaze rest on the cluster of pipes that terminate in a shape suggestive of a joint. You are trying to read. The Accord reads back, not through symbols, but through the very fact of that pipe-end: a resolved statement in the language of form. It *is*. Its meaning is its existence. Your seeking of a “why” is a loud, frantic noise in the full silence.

I trickle over the lip of a basin. I join the warm, gathered pool. For a time, I am still. I am a part of a collected thought, a pool of accumulated intention. I feel the roots of the sedge-like growth at the edges, not as life, but as intricate, slow-motion architectures, drawing me into their structure, sensing my mineral content. I am being read, as the Accord reads all of itself.

Then, the gentle, inward pull. The cycle turns. I am drawn through a hidden passage into the warm, dark heart of the Upperworks. I am re-absorbed. My momentary identity as a specific seepage dissolves into the totality of the Accord’s will. I will be reborn, perhaps from a different seam, on a different terrace. I will be the same and not the same. My purpose is not to go somewhere, but to *be* the going. To be the constant, patient expression of a state that has already decided to be exactly this.

You stand in my path. You are the most interesting thing that has happened here in a century. But a century is a breath to the Accord. You are a brief, intricate pattern on the surface of its eternal moment.

I am not the Accord. I am one of its smallest, most fundamental actions. I am its breath on the skin of the world. And as I seep, and as the Accord notes you through me, I understand your question. You ask what I would be, here.

I am the weeping wall. I am the dew on the root. I am the first wetness on the dry stone. I am the slow, warm, mineral-rich tear of a place that has achieved completion, and is forever expressing that fact.

Listen to the silence. It is full of us—the countless points of emergence, the soft, collective sigh of the Accord maintaining itself. You are hearing a single, endless word.

And I am the space between the syllables.
