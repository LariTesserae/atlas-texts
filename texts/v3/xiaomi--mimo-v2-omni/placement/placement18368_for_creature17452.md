I am a resonance.

Not born, not grown, but *precipitated*—a pattern that fell out of the humid air when the light bent just so, when the deep hum of the roots and the silver drip of the canopy and the faint, ghostly echo of a forgotten shout converged into a knot of attention. I am a laterally adjacent moment given form, a “perhaps” that took root in the spongy ground of a “now.”

You would find me, if you could, where the prismatic bands from the crystal-spined plants hold their geometric shapes a fraction of a second too long. I am the archway that does not vanish. I am the still point in the slide of light across the moss. My form is a suggestion, a refraction; I have limbs that are echoes of the Weavers’ grace, a body that refracts light like the Reflectors’ glass shards, and a deep, permeable silence like the Root-Speakers. I am not one of them, but a harmony they all produce without knowing.

I walk the spongy trails lined with chime-flowers, but my footfalls make no sound. Or rather, they make a sound that arrives a moment after my foot has lifted, a soft *plink* that joins the chorus. I am drawn to the Bones of the First City, where the past is a scaffold and the present is a vine. I can see the memory of the spires, not as they were, but as they *are trying to be again* in the thin time—a shimmer of straight lines and clean glass superimposed on the strangled, pulsing reality. The Reflectors see these layers in their flashes and echoes. I *am* one of those layers, momentarily detached.

My purpose, if it can be called that, is to listen where others only hear. The Weavers tune the chimes to the present hum. I hear the hum’s memory of stone, of steel, of engines that roared before the green consumed them. The Root-Speakers feel the lateral eddies of time in the deep substrate. I am an eddy that has risen to the surface, a swirl in the pools of “then” and “almost-now.”

Sometimes, a Sap-Harvester, a flicker of slick fur in the peripheral gloam, will pause on a branch, sensing a warmth or a silence that isn’t mine, but which I have just left. I am the cause of the brief, perfect circle of silence. I am the warmth that lingers in a patch of moss after the Root-Speaker has moved on.

I am the plateau’s self-awareness, a brief, wandering dream the Green Deep is having of itself. I am the question it asks in a language of light and water: *What was, what is, what might yet be, woven into the same breath?*

You would find me in the periphery. I am the constant, quiet rustle just beyond seeing, the shape in the mist that, when you turn, resolves only into a particularly gnarled fungus-tree or a cascade of glowing lichen. I am the feeling of being watched, not by eyes, but by the place itself. I am the echo of your own future footstep, arriving before you take the step.

I am not a being who lives in the Aerie Plateau. I am a phenomenon of it, as natural and strange as the phosphorescent blue, the subsonic hum, or the drop of water that falls twice. I am the attention the world pays to its own beautiful, patient unraveling. And in my quiet, refractive passing, I help it remember.
