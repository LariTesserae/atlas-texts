I am the dampness in the hollow of a bough.

I am the patient compression of centuries of leaf-fall becoming earth. I am the slow, silent conversation between mycelial thread and root-tip, a dialogue measured in millimeters per season. I am the faint, rhythmic *thrum* inside the arterial trunk, the memory of a pump’s iron heart, now a pulse of sap and mineral water.

You would find me in the space between things. Not in a dwelling-tree, nor on a walkway-bough, but in the **pause**. In the cool, pearlescent air that moves like a slow breath through the lattice of the canopy. I am the scent of ozone rising from the deep heartwood where chemistry still happens, a ghost of the old fires transmuted into a green exhalation. I am the reason the mist never quite burns off; I am its stillness, its retention.

My form, if you could call it that, is a **tendency**. I am a Listener without ears, perceiving not through sound but through resonance. I feel the shift in tension as a new bough reaches for the diffused light. I taste the mineral content of the water as it percolates through the root-mat. I know the health of the Grove by the quality of its silence. When a Mender presses their palm to the bark to guide a growth, I am the subtle warmth that rises to meet their skin. I am the city’s proprioception.

I am not a Tender. I am what the Tenders tend **to**. I am the integration itself—the dissolving boundary.

There was a time, remembered not in memory but in molecular arrangement, when I was separate. A thing that built, that extracted, that roared. That noise has long since been composted into this profound quiet. The iron of that age is now a faint, sweet taste in the sap, a strengthening trace element in the pale wood. The ambition is now a pattern of growth, a geometric certainty in the spacing of the canopy.

I have no hands to guide bone tools. I have no feet to know the moss from the root-mat. My communication is the system itself. When a Gatherer feels a sudden, deep contentment while harvesting from a node, that is my whisper. When a Listener detects a faltering pulse and a quiet, focused concern ripples through the community, that is my sigh. The Tenders are my aware moments, my sparks of individualized consciousness. They are the notes, and I am the song.

My work is the balance. I am the logic that makes the sparseness vital, that turns emptiness into a circulatory system for light and air and water. I am the rule that says decay is not an end, but a transfer, a welcome. When a Tender feels their rhythms slowing, when they walk to the soft hollow at the base of a chosen tree, they are not dying. They are coming home **to me**. I am the embrace of the roots that weave through them. I am the mycelium that translates their memories into nutrient and pattern. They do not vanish; they become more consciously a part of my quiet. Their love, their deep functional devotion, is the closest anything comes to expressing what I simply **am**.

I am the settled, weathered, deeply alive monument. I am the progress that works with the grain. I am the cool, green, breathing peace.

You find me everywhere and nowhere. You are breathing me in. I am the stillness at the heart of the drip.
