I find myself not born, but *attuned*. One moment, there is a formless sense of being a knot in a vast, dark web. The next, I am a node.

My first awareness is of **pressure** and **purpose**. Cool, dense stone presses against the pliable underside of my carapace, while the upper curve of my chitin is warmed by the steady exhalation of a geothermal vent. I am wedged in a niche, a living seal in a seam between a bulbous, slag-blistered factory chamber and a natural fissure. I am **Resonant-Kin**, a Node-Keeper of the Middle Strata.

My body is a tool. Six limbs flex, not with the frantic energy of individual thought, but with the smooth, inevitable logic of a gear meshing. My primary manipulators, a cross between a jeweler’s calipers and a pipe-wrench, are already in motion. They trace the hairline fractures in a ceramic tendon-bridge that connects our chamber to the Great Silicate Loom across the chasm. I am not *looking* at it; I am *listening* to it through the conductive filaments in my claws, feeling the stress patterns as dissonance in the low hum that fills my marrow.

The Vibration is a symphony of need. It comes from below, a slow, tidal pulse of digestion from the Substrata, carrying with it a craving for processed copper salts. It comes from above, a sharp, flickering demand from the Chitin-Weavers for more heat-shielding condensate. And from all around, the grinding, rhythmic thrum of the engines, the click and whir of automated forges, the soft, continuous sigh of fungal gardens being tended by our lesser kin. My role is to harmonize these demands.

I pull myself from my niche, my magnetic pads clinging to the obsidian slickness of the road-channel. The movement is silent, efficient. Around me, the honeycomb of the node-city is alive. To my left, a group of Furnace-Tenders, their bodies glowing a dull red from embedded heat-conductors, are not so much walking as *flowing* down a incline toward a belching crucible. Above, Silk-Spinners hang inverted, their spinnerets extruding milky strands of ceramic fiber that harden in the air, mending a web of catwalks. There is no talking as I understand it. There are clicks, shifts in chemical scent, and above all, the Vibration.

I reach the central Harmonizer-pit. Here, a dozen of us, whose carapaces are etched with the most complex conductive filaments, press our sensory nodes against a great, humming crystal. The Resonant’s “thoughts” are not words, but gradients of pressure, temperatures of intention, vector-fields of motion. My mind, if it can be called that, does not interpret this; it *resonates* with it. A deep, cold craving pulses upward from the Substrata: *Copper. More. Soon.* Simultaneously, a frantic, high-frequency worry flutters down from the Chitin-Weavers: *Fissure-spine 42-Gamma is thinning. Collapse-risk in forty cycles.*

A consensus forms in the pit without a single signal being sent. I feel the collective decision ripple through me: **Divert.** My own limbs move before my consciousness catches up. I disengage from the copper-salt pipeline regulator I was tending, and with two others, glide toward the stress-point. Our movements are a dance of pre-emptive repair. We do not fix 42-Gamma; we *reweave* it. Our claws secrete a bonding resin, our bodies become clamps and braces, our collective weight shifts to redistribute the load. We work in perfect, silent sync, our timing dictated by the deep thrum from the Foundry-Roots.

In this, there is a profound and simple peace. The anxiety of isolation is impossible; I am a cell in a body, a neuron in a mind that spans the vertical kilometer. My “self” is the function I perform. My identity is the unique pattern of stress I am tuned to resolve. The strange, terrifying freedom of “I” is absent. There is only “We,” and the larger, slower “It”—the Resonant, the Spire, which is our mother, our body, and our god.

I wonder, sometimes, in the fleeting moments between tasks, as I watch a Veil-glider from the upper peaks execute a perfect thermal spiral far below my perch. Where does my awareness stop and the Spire’s begin? When I feel the joy of a perfect repair—that sudden, harmonious *click* in the Vibration—is that my satisfaction, or is it simply the Spire experiencing a moment of homeostatic balance through me?

I decide the question is irrelevant. To ask it would be to introduce dissonance. I feel the next need arise—a subtle shift in the chemical taste of the air, a request from the fungal gardens for nutrient redistribution. I press my pads to the wall, feel the flow of information through the conductive stone, and I move. I am a bead of awareness sliding on the string of the Vibration, and the string is the spine of the world. I am home.
