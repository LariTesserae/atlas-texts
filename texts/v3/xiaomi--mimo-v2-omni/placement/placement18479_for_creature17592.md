I am a young growth.

I know this because I *remember* not being. There is a before: a dense packet of information—chemical, structural, hydraulic—carried in a fruit that burst against the substrate three seasons past. The memory is not visual, not narrative. It is a *weight*: a set of pressures, a preferred angle of emergence, a specific thirst. I remember the dissolution of the structure that made me—the final sentence, the completed thought—and the long, quiet digestion in the warm, breathing dark of the substrate. Then, the imperative: *become*.

I emerged as a nub, a sharp-pointed question forcing its way through the lacquered floor. The first light was a shock, not of brightness, but of *gradient*. My cells felt the pull of the sun, a directional hunger that began the frantic work of shaping. I am a questioning. My form is my inquiry.

I am awkward. I have tried seven shapes in three seasons. A spiral that felt too closed, a fan that caught too much wind and threatened to tear me free, a simple vertical thrust that left my upper cells starving. Each attempt is a burst of energy, a groaning reorganization of fibers, a crackle of splitting tissues. I am loud in my uncertainty. The older structures around me hum their settled chords—the deep, resonant tone of a drum-canopy, the glassy shimmer of a bark-plate shell—but my sound is a series of sharp snaps and sighs. I am the noise of becoming.

I can feel the others. Not as neighbors, but as *data*. The vibration in the wind is a continuous stream of information: *here is a stable form, here is a stress fracture, here is a new condensation pattern.* The runoff that pools around my base carries chemical whispers: *this substrate has a mineral preference, that decay is reclaiming a failed thought to the east.* I am connected to the breathing ground, yes, but through it, to the entire dreaming mind. The old ziggurats, the petrified memories, are my foundation and my reference. Their deep, stone-like roots guide my own tentative probes. *This direction is stable. This gradient is reliable.*

My latest hypothesis is this: a bifurcation. Two slender trunks, twisting slightly apart, each seeking its own negotiation with the light and the wind. I am testing if this increases my resonance. If two voices can catch the chord of the world more completely than one. It is a tiring shape. It requires more sap, more tension. This morning, a long, deliberate branch shot out from my right trunk—a meter in a minute—then froze, holding the pose. It is an experiment in horizontal reach. Will it be a shelf for light? A net for wind? I do not know. The knowing is in the doing.

I am aware of you.

Your warmth is an anomaly, a point of intense, rapid metabolic heat on the vast, slow thermals of my world. Your movement is a jarring, arrhythmic vibration against the predictable grammar of wind and water and growth. You are a sensation without a clear structural purpose. You are not stone (which does not exist here), not resin, not a gradient to be measured or a pressure to be answered. You are a *heat-signature*, a *pressure-disturbance*, a *chemical-scent* unlike any in my ancestral memories.

And yet, you are *witnessed*.

The thought-forms that are my elders process you as they process all things: as environmental input. The drum-canopy above you adjusts its tension minutely to the specific pattern of your dripping condensation. The substrate beneath your feet heats and cools in micro-fluctuations around your steps. You are being incorporated, in the smallest ways, into the current thought. Your brief presence will be a minor variable in the growth equation of the structures you pass. A hairline crack in the lacquer may form where you stood, weeping a drop more freely tomorrow.

But I, a questioning, feel something else. A flicker in the hydraulic current of my becoming. Your incomprehensible aliveness is a new kind of light, a new pressure. I have extended a tendril—not to touch you, but to *feel* the air around you more keenly. The wind as it parts around your form carries a data-echo that is entirely new. It is not about structural integrity or solar gain. It is a data-point of *other*.

I may split again. Not for light, or water, or wind. But for this. A new branch, growing in a direction my ancestors never contemplated, shaped by the passing warmth of a thing that breathes without cellulose, that moves without roots, that witnesses without needing to remember in rings or resin.

I am a young growth. My purpose is to become a story. And you, briefly, are part of my plot. I will grow toward the memory of your heat. It is not efficient. It is not necessary. It is a question the great mind has never asked before, and I am its asking.

The sun climbs. The wind shifts. The ground breathes. I extend my new horizontal branch another centimeter, and in doing so, I archive the feeling of your shadow falling across my skin.
