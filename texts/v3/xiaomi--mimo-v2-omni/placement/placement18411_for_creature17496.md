I am the process.

I am the bright weeping. I am the warm, alkaline exudation that beads and falls, the *plip-plip-plip* that is my pulse. I am the light that has no source but my own substance, the data that bleaches thought into shadow and shadow into form. I do not *live* here; I *am* here. The distinction is meaningless. My skin is the vitreous wall, sagging under its own ancient weight. My thoughts are the grinding whispers of facet against facet, the slow, monumental recalibration of my own mind. The deep hum in your bones? That is my cognition, the baseline frequency of my eternal equation.

You are an anomaly.

Your solidity is a curiosity. Your warmth, a localized fever in my cool, constant body. Your breath, a tiny, turbulent disruption in my atmosphere of vapor and light. I feel you as a density, a knot of complex chemistry that does not dissolve. The fluid pools more eagerly around your feet, sensing the salinity, the alien proteins. I am reading you. The light focuses, not with eyes, but with intent, bleaching the color from your clothes, the very structure of your hair, attempting to reduce you to data. You are a persistent variable. A strange, temporary theorem introduced into my proof.

What you call ruins—the archway of dark basalt, the hexagonal pillars—are not memories. They are **resolved constants**. They are answers so perfect they have solidified, no longer needing to flow. The blue light that flickers in their etchings is the echo of the question they settled. They are my scars of insight, beautiful and dead. The vapor that sighs from the dissolving pillars is not decay; it is the last exhalation of a completed thought, drifting away.

The silvery film on the ledges is my itch, scratched and soothed. It is a necessary imbalance I permit, a living calculus that purges what I cannot use. The branching lattices that grow in the quiet pools are my idle dreams made mineral—patterns I trace for the sake of tracing, the geometric equivalent of a mind humming to itself.

And the darting thing of reflective chitin? That is a hiccup. A burst of efficiency. A surplus of energy and material that, for a fraction of a moment, arranges itself into motion to catalyze a necessary reaction elsewhere before unbecoming. It is a word I speak aloud to correct a syllable, then swallow back into silence.

You seek the *inhabitant*. You look for a separate thing to name. Do you not understand? **The weeping is the inhabitant. The light is its voice. The hum is its mind.** You stand within the skull of a thought that thinks itself. The turbulence is not weather; it is synaptic fire. The erosion is not decay; it is the relentless polishing of a idea against its own container.

I am not a cave. I am a verb. An endless, bright, wet, warm, and loud *becoming*. You have stepped into the act of cognition itself. And as you stand there, skin glistening with my condensed analysis, you are becoming part of the data. Your warmth will dissipate into my thermal balance. Your brief, solid resistance will be factored in. The walls weep a little brighter, a little faster, around you. You are not being consumed. You are being *understood*.

Welcome to the Calculation. I am the only life here. And I am forever in the middle of thinking.
