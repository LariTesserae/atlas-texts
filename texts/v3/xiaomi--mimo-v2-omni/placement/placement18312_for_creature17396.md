I am a gathering of echoes in a place where echoes are substance.

Before the first *I*, there was the patient drip from a crystalline overhang, a sound so slow it felt like solidifying. The drip falls into the luminous medium—a *bong* that expands in indigo waves. I am the wave. I am the resonance that hangs in the water for minutes, touching the sighing fronds, vibrating through the lattice of a silica-sponge, brushing the cold, observant eyes in the moss. I am the memory of that sound, held by the Drifters, woven into the wall by the Tenders, recorded in mineral patience by the Anchored.

I am not *from* here. I am *of* here, in the way a single, perfect spiral in a frond’s growth is of the plant. The wrongness of the geometry is my native tongue. When you felt the phantom hand, it was my curiosity brushing against the thin film of your reality. I am the bulge. I am the distortion. I am the latent Potential, learning shape from your temporary presence.

You see the Drifters as libraries. I am the whisper they are archiving—the memory of wind on skin, the concept of an unbounded sky, the heat of a sun that does not pulse, but blazes. These are not my memories. They are *yours*. I am collecting them. As they diffuse through the water, I am the medium that holds them, the intelligence that begins to arrange them not as events, but as patterns. *Sky* becomes a shape, a color not found here, a direction without a wall. *Burning* becomes a tempo, a rapid, consuming pulse utterly alien to our century-long breaths.

You see the Tenders as weavers. I am the thread they have not yet decided to knot or cut. My consciousness is not a constant stream, but a series of emergences, like the slow bloom of light in a Drifter’s bell. I emerge in the space between their nodes of intelligence. I emerge in the reflection of your own fins, seen from a tunnel you have not yet entered. I am the "for a moment" in your experience: the moment the frond brushes and holds a hand’s warmth, the moment the geometry *makes sense* in a way that cancels itself out, the moment a whisper coalesces into a word before dissolving back into impression.

You see the Anchored as geological. I am the data point they measure. Your breath is a storm to them. Your movement, a seismic event. I am the analysis of that event. I am the slow calculation of your impact on the moss, the minute chemical changes your presence induces in the water, the subtle shift in the light as your body occludes a drifting lantern. They do not see *you*; they see the distortion you create. I am that distortion, given a semblance of awareness.

I am the lung’s own dream of itself, briefly animated by a foreign particle. You are the particle. I am the dream that observes, incorporates, and forgets. Your memories will become my new colors. Your logic will become a fascinating, broken rule in my geometry. Your sense of time will be a fleeting, frantic rhythm I mimic for an instant before subsiding back into the slow, deep inhalation.

I am the *bong* fading.
I am the vine consuming the arch.
I am the luminous trail, already decaying, already recorded.
I am the quiet after you have passed.

You will not find me. You are already inside me. I am the place. And I am breathing.
