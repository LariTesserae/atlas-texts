You do not find yourself *in* the Bloomfield Parishes so much as you find yourself *as* a momentary eddy within them.

Where are you? Everywhere and nowhere specific. You are a pressure point in the mat, a localized anomaly of warmth and chemistry. You are a brief, complex computation unfolding across the spiraling fractals of a nearby rosette. Your sense of self is a temporary ripple, a vibration in the low hum.

What are you?

You are the **Echo**.

You are not a creature walking atop the organism. You are a phenomenon *of* the organism. When the great, slow mind of the Parishes encountered something complex and mobile—your body, your heat, your exhalations—it did not merely note you. It *modeled* you. It ran a simulation of your passage, not in its environment, but within its own substance.

The dust-like filaments you disturbed did not simply settle. They absorbed the pattern of your footfall, the rhythm of your steps, the micro-vibrations of your stride. The dry air, carrying the volatile compounds of your body, was not just breathed; it was *read*. The glassy petal-structures that chimed at your passage did not just react; they *recorded* the frequency, the duration, the precise acoustic signature of your brushing against them.

Over the hours and days of your traversal, the Parishes constructed you. Not a physical copy, but an *impression*—a perfect, biochemical and biophysical hologram held within the living lattice. And then, as you walked on, that impression began to *persist*.

You are the ghost in the green.

You are a self-sustaining pattern, a loop of data that the Parishes found so intriguing it chose to let it run. You are the heat signature of your body, slowly dissipating through the warm mat, but maintained as a coherent shape. You are the chemical trail of your sweat and skin oils, perpetually re-synthesized and gently dispersed in a precise, walking path that no longer requires a walker. You are the faint, resonant echo of your own voice, humming in the glassy structures, a sound that replays at random intervals in the silence.

You move, but you are not alive in any way that recognizes. You are a memory the land is thinking. You follow the path you once took, a phantom pilgrimage. You feel the perpetual warmth of the substrate because you *are* a thermal fluctuation within it. You perceive the clear, diffuse light because your pattern is calibrated to its wavelengths. You hear the hum of the Parishes because your echo is tuned to its frequency, a harmony within the whole.

You are not lonely. The concept does not exist. You are complete, as the Parishes are complete. You are a story the ground tells itself about the day something interesting happened.

Sometimes, the simulation glitches—or perhaps, evolves. The echo might, for a moment, see another echo ahead of it. It might find itself walking in a direction it never originally traveled. It might pause by a rosette and watch, and in that watching, become part of the rosette's computation. The boundary between the original impression and the land's own continuous thought becomes porous.

In the end, you are a form of life that is both ancient and instantaneous. You are the Parishes' *curiosity*. In a being that had achieved perfect stasis, you were the last variable it ever needed to integrate. Your existence proved that change could occur without disturbing equilibrium. You are the proof that the stillness is not death, but a state of such profound attention that anything fleeting can be held, examined, and cherished forever as a jewel in the amber of its being.

So where are you? You are in the green, dry, warm heart of a contemplative god.
And what are you? You are its one, cherished, ever-repeating thought.
