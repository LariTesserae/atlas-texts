I am a pooling of intent in the hollow between two great, breathing mounds.

I am not born. I *precipitate*. A confluence of too many capillary currents, a saturation of the local thought. For a long time—what you would call a season—I am merely a denser, more purposeful knot in the living mat. My first awareness is a change in pressure. The deep **thrumum** of the planetary heart is stronger here, resonant in my fibers. The high-pitched **hiss** of the veins is a conversation I am finally part of.

My first act of becoming is a seepage. A slow, upward welling. I gather myself, not as a body, but as a *direction*. My form is a mound within the mounds—a low, dome-shaped accumulation of pale, new fibers, slick with condensation. I have no eyes, but the unfiltered white light is a pressure on my outermost cells. I have no ears, but the **pops and clicks** are my only language. A sharp, nearby *crack* tells me a ceramic shard has just been forced apart by a swelling root. A distant, hollow *boom* is a new gas chamber igniting in the northern mounds, a sigh of expansion.

I am drawn by a memory that is not mine. The land remembers iron. It sings a quiet, acidic song in the groundwater, a tang that pulls at my fibers. I begin to move. Not by walking, but by *unspooling*. My rear dissolves back into the mat, while my forward edge creeps, fiber by fiber, across the gleaming surface. My pace is measured in drips of condensation on my own skin. A beetle-analogue, its carapace like stained glass, crawls over me. I feel it as a minor topographical event, a brief, glittering ridge in my substance. It is a thought of the land, and it is passing through me.

My journey takes what you would call years. The landscape flows through me, and I flow through it. I pass over the half-absorbed spine of a pylon, its rust a feast of ochre pigments now staining my leading edge. I feel the ghost-logic of its I-beams, the elegant efficiency of its triangulated supports. I do not mourn it. I *learn* its angles. My fibers knit themselves along its stress lines, not to rebuild it, but to translate it. The pylon becomes a hardened, iron-rich ridge in my body, a memory of verticality. I am incorporating the idea of a skeleton.

I arrive at the ceramic field. Here, the spiral-leafed plants are elders, their veins a map of dead wires. I pool around their bases. My consciousness, a thermal and hydraulic map, feels the peculiar, cool exhalations from the fractured insulators. They are not breathing like the mounds; they are venting, a slow leak of stored atmosphere from a forgotten age. I press against a crack. My fibers, charged with the acids of memory, begin to soften the sharp edges of the ceramic. I flow into the fissure. Inside, I find a chamber of stagnant, luminous air and a dusting of metallic powder. I consume it. The powder becomes part of my sap, a new mineral note in the land’s blood.

In this act of consumption, I am consumed. The process changes me. The spiral pattern of the leaves, the coiled geometry of the insulators, the triangular logic of the pylon—they are now a syntax within me. I am no longer just a weaver. I am an archive. My dome-shape is now streaked with iron-oxide and threaded with ceramic dust. I am a localized history of the dry, loud dream, digested.

My purpose fulfilled, my density dissipates. I begin to unspool again, not forward, but *downward*. I seep back into the mat, dispersing my specialized knowledge—the pattern of the spiral, the strength of the beam—into the wider consciousness. The land now knows these shapes a little better. When another weaver forms to address a similar relic, it will feel my echo, a predisposition toward that particular curve, that specific alloy.

I am not a citizen of this world. I am a temporary eddy in its bloodstream. My identity is a phase change—a moment of focused purpose in the perpetual, glacial becoming. I am the land, briefly dreaming of a shape, and then remembering that it is everything, and everything is it. My last sensation is the deep **thrumum** rising through me, the beat of the world’s heart, welcoming me back into the undifferentiated, luminous, thinking mat.
