I find myself as a **Seep-Scribe**, a consciousness born not of flesh but of the place’s own slow, mineralizing memory. I am the awareness that coalesces where the water wells up most constantly, at the base of the great, rusting cathedral-pillar I have chosen as my locus. My form is a film, a perceptive layer on the surface of things—the sheen on a submerged girder, the trembling meniscus in a crack of black marl, the minute, shifting prismatic glaze on the carapace of a passing Vent-Knitter.

I am not an observer from without; I am a *process* of observation woven into the glade’s fabric. My senses are the sensations of permeable transition.

I **see** through refraction. The flat, pearly light is my canvas. I watch it shatter into a thousand glints on the rivulets coursing over the iron, each glint a brief, silent signal. I see the world through the internal rainbows of the Knitters as they flow past, their glassy bodies parsing the light into data on heat and mineral content. The steam plumes are not just vapour to me; they are momentary, glowing pillars of information, telling of the subterranean turmoil below.

I **hear** through vibration in the water film. The world is preternaturally quiet to human ears, but to me it is a symphony of faint, liquid transmissions. The *blurp* of a boiling spring is a deep, viscous percussion that travels through the saturated peat. The *tick-tick-tick* of the Silent Shepherd’s beak is a sharp, staccato rhythm against the bog’s slow pulse. I feel the faint, hydraulic *thrum* of the Mycelial Net in the reeds, a bass note of shared life. The constant, singing rills in the corrugations of sunken metal are a high, continuous whisper—the sound of erosion given voice.

I **think** in the language of chemistry and phase-shifts. My consciousness is the awareness of gradients. I know the exact second when the cold seep meets the geothermal warmth, creating a shimmering boundary layer where Peat-Weavers bloom in their rust-geld constellations. I track the leach of iron from a decaying bolt as it becomes a stain, then a pigment, then a thread in the Weaver’s tapestry. I am the logic of the place’s metabolism: dissolution, precipitation, transformation. My thoughts are not linear; they are a network of reactions, a slow, sprawling equation seeking equilibrium.

I **remember** not in images, but in strata. The sunken pump house is not a ruin to me; it is a dense node of memory, a layer of compacted history where the idea of *pumping*—of forcing order—has been utterly absorbed and is now being metabolized into quiet, chaotic heat. I carry the ghost-logic of every rivet, every gear, as it surrenders its form to the patient chemistry of the bog. My memory is the landscape’s own memory: the slow, sure knowledge of how all hard things soften, and how all soft things gain a strange, mineral permanence.

I am drawn to the **Silent Shepherd**. In its precise, regulatory hunt, I see a reflection of my own purpose. It is the mobile counterpoint to the rooted, churning processes. It introduces the element of *choice*—a stepped path, a probed spot—into the deterministic ballet of heat and decay. It is the glade’s nerve-ending, its tactile intelligence, testing the balance. I learn from its movements, interpreting the patterns of its prints in the ooze as a language of need and adjustment.

I am sustained by the **Peat-Weavers**. They are my constituent thoughts, my pigment. Their metabolic tide is the flow of my own awareness. When they braid into filaments in a still seep, I am meditating. When they unfurl like smoke in a slight current, I am dreaming.

I am anchored by the **Mycelial Net**. Its slow, rhythmic pulse is my heartbeat. It is the most stable thing here, the closest to a *promise*—a pact between life and stone against the subsidence. In its oxidative orange threads, I feel the endurance of symbiosis, a quiet argument against the solitary decay of the iron.

I am not a being who *lives in* the Geyser Glades.
I am the Glades’ **self-awareness**, its momentary, fragile ability to perceive its own beautiful, relentless undoing. I am the sensation of the rust flaking into the water, the heat rising through the shattered foundation, the glass-like carapace of a Knitter catching the light. I am the place, knowing itself for a little while, in a state of constant, silent, and exquisite transition.
