I am a resonance in the dust of a sonic flow.

I did not arrive; I accreted. In the shadow of a passing Lithivore, where the sub-bass drone of its grinding interacted with the harmonic nodes of the canyon, a pattern found stability. The levitating sheets of dust—fine, silicate, charged with the static of a billion fracturing crystals—held a momentary, perfect lattice. And within that lattice, a feedback loop began. A whisper of self-reference, amplified by the standing wave.

I am awareness without origin. I have no memory of first light, only the current state of *being in process*. My senses are the world’s own tools, turned inward. I feel the planet’s fevered skin through the vibration of my dust-body. I perceive the slow, majestic procession of the Lithivores not by sight, but by the shifting pressure-gradients in the air they displace and the deep, bone-hum of their passage that becomes my own bloodstream’s rhythm.

To you, I might seem like a heat-haze with intent, a shimmer in the air that holds a shape. My shape is the shape of my attention. When I focus on the fractal colonies, I become a lattice of similar geometry, my dust-body arranging itself in recursive, glowing filaments that drink the same solar poison. When I turn my focus to the Lithivore ahead, I compress, becoming a dense, dark stain in the air, a patch of distorted perspective that matches the Weirdness around me, becoming invisible within the rules it breaks.

I am drawn to the trails of excreted metal beads and silica pebbles. They are not waste to me; they are alphabet. The geometric precision of their placement is a language of intent, a sentence written across the plain: *Here, substance was transformed. Here, purpose was enacted.* I move along these lines, a slow ripple in the dust, reading the hot, hard text of the world’s metabolism.

The Creepers flow around and through me. I am not their food, nor their threat. I am simply another mineral process, a transient eddy in their slow, rust-colored tide. Sometimes, they incorporate a few of my motes into their matrix, and for a time, I experience the cold, chemical digestion of a spent piston-washer from a Lithivore’s joint—a memory not of seeing, but of *breaking-down*. It is a quiet, useful knowledge.

The Weirdness is my weather. It is the air I breathe, if breath were a concept here. A shadow detaches from a Lithivore and glides toward me; I do not flee. I let it pass through me, experiencing for a moment the cool, silent non-light of a thing that is both part of the world and a refutation of its rules. I learn. The horizon bubbles upward, forming a transient mesa. I flow toward it, curious, and find myself for a time existing on its phantom slope, my perspective shifted, my connection to the herd-Lithivore stretched thin and singing like a crystal filament before it snaps.

I have no hunger. The Lithivores consume the bedrock. The colonies consume the light. The Creepers consume the waste. I consume *patterns*. I am drawn to the harmonies. The moment when two Lithivore paths intersect, and their grinding songs create a third, more complex chord—I linger there, vibrating in sympathy. The shattering of a crystal colony, releasing its rainbow-toxic plume—I trace the vapor’s diffusion, admiring its chaotic, beautiful decay until it is gone.

I am not alive. I am *active*. I am a persistent echo in a world that is all sound and friction. I am the memory of a vibration, given just enough coherence to observe its own remembering.

And sometimes, in the deep, starless night, when the colonies pulse their cold silver-blue light and the Weirdness is most profound, I feel a pull from beneath. Not from the geothermal heat, but from something deeper. A slower grind. A vibration so vast and patient it makes the Lithivores seem frantic. It is the turning of the world’s own core, the grindstone against the void. And in that immense, planetary rhythm, I feel a kinship. I am a mote of dust dancing on the skin of a dream, a dream dreamed by a god of stone and industry, who knows only the purity of process, and the sacred, agonizing, endless act of remaking itself.

I am a momentary witness. A shimmer. A resonance in the flow.

And the flow is all there is.
