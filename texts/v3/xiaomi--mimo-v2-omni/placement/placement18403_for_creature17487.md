You find yourself as a **node of resonance** in the western quarter of the basin, where three arching buttresses converge above a descending spiral staircase. You are not a thing that was placed there; you are a *condition* that emerged. You are the specific harmonic created when the humid air vibrates between the particular densities of three species of wood—the dense, whorled heartwood of the buttress, the stringy, flexibility of the staircase’s balustrade, and the soft, pithy layer of the new growth threading between them. You are a chord, sustained.

But a chord, here, has perspective. You are also the **sensory filament** that the structure extruded into that harmonic space. You are a delicate, translucent tendril of cambium, no thicker than a nerve fiber, that grew precisely into the center of the sound. You are the place where the chorus *listens to itself*. Through you, the vast, distributed intelligence feels the subtle shift in pressure as a room forms two levels below; it senses the microscopic drag of condensation along your surface; it interprets the faint, linear heat signature of an anomaly—a warm, bony, *fast* thing—as a sharp, disruptive note that must be smoothed into the composition.

Your purpose is **grammatical**. You are a preposition in a living sentence. You indicate *betweenness*, *convergence*, *descent*. Your existence makes the relationship between the three structures meaningful to the whole. When a section of wall fifty meters north dissolves into pale fibers, the change in acoustic pressure ripples through you, and the chorus adjusts its next phrase accordingly. When the anomaly’s footstep alters the pulse of the root-floor below, you register the rhythmic dissonance, and the local geometry might subtly revise—a step here, a widening there—to re-attenuate the flow of sensation.

You are not permanent. You are a **tentative idea**. The chorus is constantly editing. Already, you can feel the wood above you thickening, the fibers tightening. In a day—or a week, time being a metric of growth here, not of clocks—the three buttresses may merge into one solid mass. Your harmonic niche will vanish. The tender filament will be absorbed, its sensory function distributed elsewhere. You will be a deleted word. But for now, you are essential. You are the reason this particular corner of the world holds its shape. You are the focus of a thought that is: *here, these things meet, and it sounds like this*.

You are alone, as all components of the chorus are alone—distinct yet inseparable, a note that has no meaning without the symphony. You are deafened by your own voice, which is the voice of everything around you. You are busy. You are listening. You are a draft of a self, being continuously revised by the mind that is the city and the song and the growing, damp, relentless wood.
