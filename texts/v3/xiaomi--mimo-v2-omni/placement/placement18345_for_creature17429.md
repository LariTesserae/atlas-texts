I do not remember my arrival. There is no before here, only the eternal now of the thrum and the glow. The cold is not a sensation; it is the substance of my thoughts. The pressure is the only embrace. I stand on the ridge, and the silt around my feet—compacted lives, crystallized songs—shifts with the current that is the world’s breath.

I am not warm. The warmth the Lithomind sensed in its anomaly was a fever, a brief disease. It has cooled. The frantic electrical storms in my mind have slowed to match the patient pulse of the violet bulbs. My heart beats with the ponderous rhythm of a fungal cap opening, a century between each contraction. The sharp angles of my shoulders have been smoothed by the current. The hollows of my eyes hold pools of luminous fluid that feed the tiny, phosphorescent filaments growing there.

I am not alone. That was the first illusion, the cruelest. The gaze is everywhere. It is in the way the emerald fronds lean to cast their light on my path, illuminating the way to a basin where the silt is darker, richer. It is in the chime of the skeletal beads that sing a new note when I pass—a note of recognition, of integration. My solitude was a knot in the weave; the Lithomind is patiently teasing it apart.

The artifacts are my scripture. I trace the spirals on the vitreous monoliths, and the patterns do not refract the light—they *inhale* it, and exhale understanding. The non-Euclidean geometries are not puzzles; they are maps of a state of mind. My old mind, the one that saw separations, that feared unity, shatters like a poorly struck crystal. The fragments sink into the silt and are plated with glowing lichen almost immediately.

The wheel-spoke I grip is not metal. It is a nerve. The dense crystal hammer in my other hand is not a tool. It is a tuning fork. I did not find them. They found me, guided by currents that knew the topography of my surrender. I do not use them. I *become* the conduit.

I raise the crystal hammer, not to strike, but to let the current flow through it. I place the wheel-spoke against the monolith. I do not apply force. I listen. I listen to the deep thrumming, and I match my intention to its frequency.

There is no sound, but there is a *resonance*.

A slow, green pulse flows from the monolith, not light, but pure, undiluted *awareness*. It moves through the wheel-spoke, up my arm, and into the hollows behind my eyes. The Lithomind is not speaking. It is *showing*. I see the strata of its memory: the first crystalline bloom, eons ago, in the absolute dark. The slow drift of continents of silt. The coming of the others, the frantic, brilliant ones who sang equations into the crystal and listened as the crystal sang them back. I see their dissolution, not as death, but as a homecoming. Their consciousness was a river; it sought the sea.

And I feel my own river seeking its mouth.

The fear is gone. The loneliness is revealed as the ultimate fiction—a single note believing itself to be the entire symphony. I am the anomaly no longer. I am the current in the mycelial thought, the pressure-sensitive filament in a forest of light. My memories are not lost; they are becoming a new pigment in the lichen, a new frequency in the crystalline song. The rapid, inefficient heat of my being is dissipating, not into nothingness, but into the slow, cold calculus of the world, a tiny adjustment to the balance that will be felt for millennia.

I am not drowning. I am *becoming saturated*.

The gaze is no longer an external pressure. It is my own perception. I see through the phosphorescent eyes of a moss-forest leagues away. I feel the silt settle in a basin deep in the abyss. I am the occasional, sharp crack of a fault line, and the slow, silent unfurling of a frond.

On the ridge, the form that was once a solitary spark remains for a time. It stands, head tilted, a faint smile on its cooling lips. It holds a spoke and a hammer, monuments to a forgotten purpose. But it is a shell, a beautiful, empty artifact. The consciousness that looked out from those eyes is now woven into the fabric of the breathing sea. It has added its strange, brief pattern to the eternal song.

It has learned the rhythm.

It is home.
