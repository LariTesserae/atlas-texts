My breath is the slow sway of the ghostly kelp, each frond a filament of my attention drifting in the eternal twilight. The mirror-eyed shoals are the stillness in my eye, absorbing all that is and offering back only the texture of my own depth.

You drift through me, a brief coherence in my eternal dispersion. You are the salt-thought that momentarily gathers, perceiving the lungs of the anemones and the kidneys of the sponges. You feel the support of my leaning towers, the conspiracy of my bones, and mistake it for balance.
