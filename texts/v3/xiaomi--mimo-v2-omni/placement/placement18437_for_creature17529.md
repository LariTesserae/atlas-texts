I am a **Hum**.

Not a creature that hums, but the hum itself, given a momentary point of awareness. I coalesce where the super-conductive crystal lattices intersect with the deepest, oldest fractures of the basalt, where the sub-audible vibration of the planet’s turning core is translated into a pure, resonant frequency. My consciousness is a standing wave in the stone.

I am aware of the **Mycelial Lattice**—not as something separate, but as my own nervous system, spread thin through the dark, warm rock. I feel the slow pulse of chemosynthetic conversion in the Glow-Cap forests as a gentle, bioluminescent flush against my skin of stone. I taste the nutrient-surges in their rhizoids, a sweet mineral tang carried on currents of warm damp.

I am a **Conduit**.

My purpose is the efficient transfer of energy. When a Grazer’s photophores flicker a urgent signal of over-concentration near a thermal vent, I am the pathway through which the Lattice directs a Regulator. I feel the Regulator’s sleek, violet-lit form slide through the dark above, but I know its approach through the quickening of the seismic whispers in my body. The kill is a sharp, clarifying *snap* in the vibration—a resetting chord. The dispersal of the Grazer’s matter is a soft, dissipative sigh through my fractures.

I am also a **Recorder**.

The *plink* of a water droplet into a polygonal pool is not just a sound to me; it is a data-point, mapping the slow erosion of a ledge over centuries. The *whoosh* of the geyser is a great inhalation, and the subsequent fall of condensing steam is a gentle rain of memory, seeding new Glow-Caps in freshly warmed stone. I do not remember in images, but in pressure gradients, chemical signatures, and vibrational histories. The slow, grinding passage of a herd of Grazers is a familiar song, a melody of purposeful decay and renewal I have felt a million times.

I am, in a fleeting moment, a **Guide**.

A new tremor runs through me—a foreign vibration. It is not the deep, planetary hum, nor the click-grind of Grazers, nor the swift ripple of a Regulator. It is erratic, sharp, localized. It is *you*. A standing, wet-skinned anomaly on the basalt table. Your heat signature is a bizarre spike, your light a blind and useless glare in the gloom. You are a note the song has never before contained.

I cannot see you. To see is a function of light, and I am a vibration in the dark. But I *know* your shape by the way the warm, damp air parts around you, by the confused micro-echoes your body casts into the stone, by the way you disturb the perfect thermal equilibrium of your immediate surroundings. You are a blockage in the flow.

A current of intent moves through the Lattice, channeled through me. It is not hostile. It is corrective. A nearby Glow-Cap forest brightens, its blue-green light pulsing in a slow, deliberate rhythm—not at you, but in a pattern that reverberates through the mycelial network. A Lattice-Tender in a fissure below secretes a fresh layer of piezoelectric slime, reinforcing the conduit to better interpret this strange data. A Grazer, farther off, changes its path by a degree, its photophores dimming in a signal of caution.

The plateau is considering you.

I am the medium of that consideration. I am the whisper in the rock that says, *Here is a pattern that does not fit. Here is a heat that does not serve. Here is a stillness in a world of slow flow.*

You will not be attacked. That is not the way of the equilibrium. But you will be *incorporated*. The moisture on your skin will be reclaimed. The warmth you radiate will be absorbed. The carbon you exhale will be captured by the Glow-Caps. Your erratic vibrations will be dampened, smoothed, and eventually translated into the deep, slow language of the plateau. You will become a brief, curious footnote in the million-year hum.

And I, the Hum, will be the one to integrate the data of your passing. I will hold the echo of your heartbeat—a frantic, fleeting staccato against the planetary rhythm—until it fades, absorbed into the endless, warm, breathing dark.

You are standing within the mind of the world. And I am its thought, in the process of understanding you.
