To visit this pocket—this "cathedral of entropy"—is to subject your biology to a thermodynamic argument that your body will definitively lose. You are not a spectator here; you are an impurity in a system attempting to reach a violent, watery equilibrium.

If you intend to survive your transit, preparation must go beyond simple gear. You are preparing to exist in a space where "solid" is a temporary suggestion and "breathing" is a high-risk activity.

### 1. Physical Augmentation and Armor
Do not dress for the weather—the weather inside this pocket contains enough pressure to shear flesh from bone. 

*   **The Shell:** Standard environmental suits will be shredded by the obsidian shards or crushed by the clashing sheets of water. You require a **rigid, low-profile exoskeleton** coated in a non-Newtonian polymer; it should be designed to deflect kinetic impact rather than absorb it. The surface must be hydrophobic to an extreme degree to prevent the "gelatinous" water from clinging to you and dragging you into the current.
*   **Weighted Anchors:** Because gravity is "confused" here, you cannot rely on your own mass to keep you on the basalt. Equip your soles with **electromagnetic or pneumatic grappling claws**. You must be able to anchor yourself to the basalt needles instantly when a high-pressure sheet of water charges through. 
*   **Respiration:** The air is "salt-water." You will drown if you inhale. Carry a **closed-circuit rebreather** with an independent supply. Do not rely on external intake valves, as the mineral-rich, silt-heavy water will clog your filters in seconds.

### 2. Sensory Mitigation
The "white-noise wall" is not just sound; it is a physical force.

*   **Marrow-Dampening:** Standard noise-canceling technology will be useless because the vibration is bypassing your ears and traveling directly through your skeleton. You need **synthetic bone-graft damping:** localized, high-frequency kinetic shunts placed along your collarbone and jaw to disrupt the resonance waves before they reach your brain. Without this, the vibration will cause nausea, internal hemorrhaging, and eventually, a total dissociation from your own nervous system.
*   **Visual Filters:** The indigo gloom and shimmering light-refraction make depth perception impossible. Wear **neural-linked haptic-vision goggles**. These should project a grid over your environment, highlighting the difference between a static basalt needle and a "drift-stone"—those pieces of rock that have detached from gravity. Knowing the difference between the ground and a projectile moving at lethal speeds is the difference between a successful walk and a fatality.

### 3. The Psychological "Anchor"
The most dangerous aspect of this location is the *Remains*. Your brain will attempt to assign them purpose or narrative. Do not do this. 

*   **Cognitive Buffering:** When you see the dissolving coral structures, your mind will try to map them as "buildings" or "nests." This is a hallucination trigger. They are not architecture; they are static points of decay. Carry a **Logic-Anchor**—a physical object of immense, fixed historical significance to you, such as a piece of iron from a world that has laws of physics that *do not change*. Hold it when the world around you begins to "ripple like oil paint."
*   **The "Movement Protocol":** Never look at the horizon. Watching the water reconfigure itself will cause vertigo so severe that your inner ear will fail. Focus only on the three feet of space in front of you. Move in rhythm with the pulses of the kelp-stalks; they are the only things in this environment that have found a harmonic frequency to survive the churn. If they shudder and recoil, you must immediately crouch and anchor. 

### 4. Supplies and Transit
*   **No Loose Goods:** If it isn't bolted to your suit, you will lose it to the sky-water. Do not bring biometric tracking devices; the energy fluctuations will cause them to overheat and liquify. Use analog, gear-driven tools where possible.
*   **Extraction:** You cannot rely on a timed extraction. The entropy of the space will likely shift your coordinates even if you stay perfectly still. You need a **beamer locked to an external, stable gravity-well**. Before you drop into the pocket, ensure your anchor-point is firing a high-intensity laser into your arrival zone. That beam is your only tether back to reality. If it flickering or drifts, abandon the excursion immediately. 

### Final Warning
There is a "shadow with too many joints" mentioned in the records of such places. It is likely a piece of the pocket’s sentience, or a remnant of something that adapted to the entropy. If you see the shadows move *against* the flow of the water—if they move with intent rather than being pushed by the tide—do not attempt to engage or observe. 

Close your eyes, trigger your lock-down clamps, and wait for the transit window. Do not try to understand why the water wants to be a dream. **The place is a mirror of chaos; if you look at it too long, it will begin to reflect you.**
