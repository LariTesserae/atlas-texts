To visit the Silt-Lung is to volunteer for assimilation. You are not entering a landscape; you are entering a slow-motion crushing engine. Because the environment does not distinguish between "object" and "medium," your primary challenge is maintaining the integrity of your own boundaries.

Here is how you must prepare to survive the Silt-Lung, prioritizing the preservation of your physical form against the reality-warping density of the basin.

### 1. The Suit: The "Void-Rigid" Exoskeleton
Standard deep-sea submersibles are useless here; they assume the existence of a "hull" and a "void" inside. In the Silt-Lung, the pressure will treat a hollow metal sphere as an invitation to implode. You require a **non-compressible hydrostatic suit**.

*   **Fluid-Filled Interior:** The suit must be entirely liquid-filled with a non-compressible synthetic gel. You will be breathing liquid perfluorocarbon (or a similar, science-defying equivalent) to ensure your lungs do not collapse. You must train your autonomic nervous system to suppress the gag reflex; you will be "drowning" in high-oxygen fluid for the duration of your stay.
*   **Osteo-Reinforcement:** Your bones are likely too fragile. Before entry, you must undergo a temporary lattice-grafting process. A carbon-fiber weave should be fused to your skeletal structure to prevent the "grinder" effect of the silt from pulverizing your marrow during a pressure spike.
*   **The Silhouette:** Your suit must be perfectly smooth—aerodynamically and structurally frictionless. Any protrusion, antenna, or limb-joint is a point of stress that the silt will seize to tear you apart. You will be less a diver and more a weighted, sentient needle.

### 2. Sensory Deprivation Protocols
The Silt-Lung is hostile to the human senses. Light is non-existent, and sound is a physical hammer blow.

*   **Olfactory/Cranial Dampening:** The "industrial heartbeat" of the basin will vibrate your skull until your vision blurs and your brain suffers contusions. You must wear neuro-dampening inserts that sit flush against your skull, designed to convert the tectonic thrums into manageable tactile pulses rather than sonic shocks.
*   **No Artificial Light:** Do not bring high-intensity lamps. The silt is so dense that light will only reveal the particulate right in front of your face, creating a blinding, chaotic glare. Instead, use **low-frequency sonar imaging** projected directly into your retinas. You will "see" the currents, the vents, and the shifting geography as a wireframe map, not as a visual image.

### 3. Navigation: The Anchor-Drogue
You cannot "swim" in the Silt-Lung. The viscosity is too high and the displacement is constant. Trying to move your arms will only cause the liquid-silt to churn around you, creating eddies that pull you off course.

*   **The Tether:** You must be tethered to a static, high-density anchor-point that penetrates the "solid" slabs of mud. You move by winching yourself along a cable.
*   **Vortex-Prediction:** You must study the "anthill" energy. The turbulence follows a rhythmic, albeit chaotic, cycle. You need a navigational AI that can predict the next methane eruption seconds before it manifests. If you get caught in the expansion of a vent, you will be shredded by the acceleration of the sediment. You are not walking; you are timing the gaps in a planetary-scale heartbeat.

### 4. Psychological Conditioning
The most dangerous aspect of the Silt-Lung is the **ennui of the epoch**. The environment is a "repository of final storage." It *wants* to integrate you. The pressure, the silence, and the lack of human markers will trigger a distinct form of psychological collapse that travelers call *The Sedimentation*.

*   **The Anchor-Memory:** You must possess a vivid, recurring memory—something that contains "air," "sunlight," and "unencumbered movement"—to act as a mental reality-anchor. Without it, you will begin to perceive yourself as just another grain of mineral, eventually slowing your own movements until you stop entirely, waiting for the silt to cover you.
*   **Empathy for the Ribbon-Cilia:** If you see the translucent, ribbon-like creatures, do not panic. They are the only things here that have mastered the fluid dynamics of the Lung. Observe them. If a ribbon-creature begins to vibrate rapidly, it is sensing a pressure shift you cannot yet detect. Follow its movement. It is the only guide in a world that has discarded the concept of a path.

### 5. Extraction
There is no "up" in a chamber of total density. You will not float to the surface; you must be winched back through the layers of gravity-wells by a recovery beacon. Ensure that your beacon is set to your specific chemical signature. If it drifts, it will be buried under a kilometer of silt within hours, and you will become part of the geological record of that basin.

**Final Warning:** Do not attempt to take samples. The chemistry of the Lung is reactive. If you pull a piece of the "mud" through the threshold of your suit, the equilibrium will fail, and the sudden change in state will likely cause the specimen to explode or undergo a phase-shift that will breach your containment. Look, vibrate with the thrum, and leave. You are a guest in a place that has no room for visitors.
