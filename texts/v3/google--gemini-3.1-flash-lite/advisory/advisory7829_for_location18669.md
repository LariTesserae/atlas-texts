To step into the Sump of the Finished World is to step into the digestive tract of a dead god of industry. You are not visiting a place; you are trespassing inside a mechanical metabolism that does not know you are there, and would treat you as a stray bolt or a clog in its pipes if it did.

For an ordinary human, preparation is not about bringing tools—it is about hardening your physical and mental form to withstand the friction of a world that is fundamentally hostile to biological life.

### 1. Physiological Armoring: The "Pressure Suit"
Standard environmental suits will fail here within minutes. The iridescent brine is a corrosive slurry, and the vitrified silica ground will shred any soft-soled boot.
*   **The Exoskeleton:** You require a rigid-frame suit, preferably lead-lined or encased in non-conductive ceramics. You need this not just for temperature regulation, but to act as a dampener against the *vibrations*. The constant subterranean thumping is enough to liquefy soft tissue or cause internal organ damage if your own frame doesn't absorb the rhythmic hammering.
*   **Respiratory Filtering:** The air is "greasy" and saturated with microscopic metallic dust. A standard gas mask will clog instantly. You need a closed-loop rebreather system. If you take in one breath of that ozone-soaked soot, your lungs will effectively become part of the factory floor.
*   **The Weighted Harness:** Because gravity shifts unpredictably, you must wear a heavy, external ballast belt equipped with automated magnetic anchors. In the moments of low gravity, you risk drifting into the path of a wire-creature or hitting the "ceiling" of the sky’s frayed edge. When the pressure spikes, you need the anchors to keep you from being crushed into the packed carbon floor.

### 2. Psychological Conditioning: "The Grey Stasis"
The Sump is a cognitive hazard. The ceaseless noise combined with the sight of creatures that shatter and re-assemble will fray your sense of identity.
*   **Sensory Deprivation:** Bring high-grade active-noise-canceling tech that creates a counter-frequency to the "leviathan heartbeat." If you listen to it too long, your brain will subconsciously begin to synchronize its own rhythm to the mechanical thrum. You will begin to feel like a machine. You will begin to *crave* being disassembled.
*   **The Anchor-Point:** Carry a physical object that acts as a "tether to life"—something soft, organic, and unchanging. A photograph, a piece of wood, a scrap of wool. Keep it in a shielded pocket. You will need to touch/smell it periodically to remind your brain that "static," "soft," and "non-industrial" exist. Without this, your mind will succumb to the Sump’s entropy.

### 3. Tactical Protocols
*   **Neutrality toward the Fauna:** Do not attempt to interact with the brass-and-amber scavengers. They are not animals; they are logistics software made manifest. They view you as a source of raw materials (calcium, iron, water). If they cluster near you, do not fight. Become "static." If you move with frantic intent, you trigger their hunting protocols. If you move with the sluggish, rhythmic cadence of the machine, they may simply glide past you, treating you as a decorative, if inefficient, pillar of the environment.
*   **Avoiding the Glitches:** If you see a tear in the horizon—a burning, static sky—turn your back on it immediately. Do not look into the glitches. They are not windows to other worlds, but "error messages" in the reality-code of this basin. Staring at them can cause "flicker-sickness," where your own physical body begins to stutter through time and space.
*   **The Three-Minute Rule:** Never remain in one spot for more than three minutes. The factory "learns" the weight and heat signature of your presence. If you linger, the ground will begin to adjust *around* you. The silica and crushed carbon will shift to accommodate your weight, effectively "swallowing" your boots to recycle your minerals. 

### 4. Supplies Checklist
*   **Ferro-fluid lubricant:** Apply this to your suit’s joints daily. The metallic dust *will* find the gaps.
*   **Caffeine and Glucose drips:** You cannot sit down to eat. The "weeping rocks" will tempt you with their iridescent brine, but do not touch it. It is the lifeblood of a dead machine; it is essentially liquid acid.
*   **A "Dead-Man's Switch":** A beacon that triggers an emergency extraction when you cease to be within a standard human range of movement or heart rate. Even if you stay perfectly still, the Sump will eventually try to process you.

**A final warning:** If you start to hear a hum in your teeth that is *neither* your own nor the machine’s, cut your trip short. You are becoming synchronized. If your internal rhythm aligns with the Sump’s, you won’t want to leave. You will simply sit down on the crushed carbon, unfold your arms, and wait to be scavenged by the brass wire.
