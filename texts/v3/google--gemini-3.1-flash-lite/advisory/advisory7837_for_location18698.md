Visiting the Silt-City of the Moss-Mites is not travel in the traditional sense; it is a trespass into a living, gargantuan digestion system. To enter is to shrink your perception to the scale of an atom, effectively becoming a guest who is simultaneously an architectural flaw. 

Because you are a human-sized entity entering a space the size of a rotted log, your primary challenge is **displacement physics.** You are essentially a giant, unstable seismic event crawling into a delicate hourglass.

Here is how you must prepare to navigate the cold, rhythmic efficiency of the Mites.

### 1. Physical Preparation: The "Statue" Protocol
You cannot walk here. If you attempt to exert your standard human force against the architecture, your knees will collapse tunnels, your breath will fog the light-tubes, and your displacement will trigger a catastrophic "flood" of sap.

*   **Thermal Regulation:** You are entering a near-freezing environment. Do not wear synthetic insulators, as they will trap your body heat against the plant-walls, causing local wilting and "weeping" that will enrage the Mites. Wear a **thermally neutral, non-reactive mesh suit**. You want your surface temperature to match the environment as closely as possible to remain "invisible" to the heat-sensing architecture.
*   **Acoustic Anchoring:** The Mites communicate and organize through the vibration of the sap-stems. Your heart rate and breathing are massive, disruptive pulses in their world. You must train in **bio-feedback suppression**—learn to slow your heart rate until you are nearly in a state of suspended animation. If you pulse, they will interpret you as a tumor.

### 2. Gear: Minimalist and Non-Reactive
Anything you bring must be as inert as possible. 
*   **Silicone-Tip Probes:** Should you need to interface with the tunnels, use soft, rounded silicone tools. Never use metal; the metallic scent of iron (the ozone smell you noted) is the signal of a "borer" or parasite. If you carry steel, you are a threat to be dismantled.
*   **Synthetic Chitinous Lubricant:** To navigate the damp, suction-prone walls, coat your suit in a neutral oil that prevents the moss from "tasting" your skin. If the moss begins to bond to you, the oil will act as a slip-agent, allowing you to peel away without tearing the city's structural integrity.
*   **The "Silent" Light:** Do not bring white LED lights. The city functions on a specific frequency of bruised, purplish bioluminescence. Bring a narrow-beam light source filtered to the exact frequency of the city's pulse. To the Mites, this acts as a "camouflage" that allows you to move within their visual spectrum without drawing attention.

### 3. Navigation: Theoretical Architecture
The map of this city is not printed on paper; it is encoded in the pressure of the stalks.
*   **The "Drip" Path:** Do not look for hallways. Look for the flow of the condensation. Follow the *drip-drip* intervals. By tracking the water, you follow the city’s plumbing, which is the safest zone for traversal. The Mites prioritize these areas for structural support, meaning they are the only places that will support your weight without collapsing.
*   **The Silk-Sensitivity Check:** If you feel the prickling, cooling sting of silk against your skin, **do not move.** You have been designated as a structural support pillar. To break that silk is to act as a saboteur. Wait for the maintenance crews to cycle; once they move on to another district, you may gently peel the silk away. Resistance is futile; patience is structural.

### 4. Psychological Conditioning: Accepting Integration
The greatest danger to the traveler is the human ego. You feel "large" and "distinct," but here, you are merely potential matter. 
*   **The Zen of Being A Wall:** To survive, you must abandon the desire to be a "visitor" and adopt the mindset of a "feature." If you view yourself as a pillar of the city, the Mites will work *around* you rather than *against* you.
*   **The Warning Sign:** If the pulse of the room turns from a slow, rhythmic "thrum" to a frantic, staccato vibration, you have caused a structural micro-tremor. **Freeze immediately.** If you continue to thrash, the Mites will deploy acid to "weld" the structural flaw (you) to the wall. This is a survival reaction—do not take it personally. 

### Final Advice: The Exit
Exiting the Silt-City is more dangerous than entering. You must move outward through the same humidity gradients you entered. As you transition back to a macro-environment, ensure you have scrubbed your exterior of all silt-dust and silk-threads. Even a single speck of the city’s genetic matter on your gear could create a "micro-canker" in your own dimension, potentially forcing a localized infestation of the Moss-Mites into your home world.

Carry nothing out. Leave everything, including your presence, behind. The city is a masterpiece of dark, cold labor—do not be the one who brings the sunlight to disturb the harvest.
