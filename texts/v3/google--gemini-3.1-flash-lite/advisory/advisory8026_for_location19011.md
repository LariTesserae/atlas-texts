To visit the Sub-Strata Foundry is to step into the gut of a tectonic entity. You are not visiting a city; you are visiting a cellular process that happens to have the scale of a machine. Because this location is a closed-loop, hyper-pressurized industrial womb, preparation is not just about gear—it is about psychological and biological hardening.

Here is how you must prepare to survive, navigate, and eventually witness the Foundry.

### 1. Physical Augmentation and Suit Engineering
Do not arrive in standard-issue environmental gear. The heat and pressure will cause rubber seals to perish, and the mineral-slicked humidity will ruin any electronic or canvas-based suit within an hour.
*   **The Exoskeleton:** You require a suit constructed of reactive ceramite or high-grade vitrified poly-carbon. It must be non-porous. The humidity here is an acid-leaching agent; if your suit is breathable, your skin will be colonized by the slime-mold within minutes.
*   **Thermal Regulation:** The heat is "radiant and oppressive," meaning your suit’s cooling system cannot rely on ambient heat exchange. You must pack an active, closed-circuit liquid nitrogen cooling loop. Do not rely on electricity to power this—use a kinetic-harvester connected to your boots. Every step you take on those vibrating, iron grates should pump the coolant that keeps your blood from boiling.
*   **Haptic Dampeners:** The "throbbing industrial heartbeat" of the Foundry is not just sound; it is a structural vibration that will liquify your internal organs if you are physically tethered to the infrastructure. Line the soles of your boots and the palm-gloves of your suit with aerogel insulation to dampen the resonance of the iron grates.

### 2. Biological Anti-Contamination Protocol
This is a realm of "biological-industrial" infection. The slime-molds and bioluminescent fungi are not just passive; they are heat-seekers. 
*   **The Shielding:** Apply a hydrophobic, anti-fungal barrier spray over your entire person. The fungi map temperature gradients; if you are not properly insulated, the slime will perceive your warmth as a heat vent and attempt to colonize your suit panels.
*   **Respiratory Filtration:** You are breathing a "hyper-saturated steam" thick with manganese and copper particulate. Standard filters will clog instantly. You need a catalytic respirator that captures mineral moisture and precipitates it into a waste reservoir. Do not—under any circumstances—let this air touch your mucosal membranes. The mineral runoff is corrosive; it will "weep" into your lungs the way the cavern walls weep into the marsh below.

### 3. Navigation and Tools
Navigation is a nightmare of "slick grates" and "dark, simmering marshes."
*   **The Tether:** You are traversing islands of scaffolding over a literal floor of fluid earth. Carry a high-tension magnetic grappling line. If a grate gives way—and the friction between the expanding rock and the iron suggests they eventually will—you need to anchor yourself to the solid tectonic seam before the marsh claims you.
*   **Sensory Modification:** Your eyes are useless. The "sickly-green" bioluminescence is a trap; it provides enough light to see the danger, but not enough to distinguish between a secure platform and a patch of structural fungal growth. Invest in acoustic-mapping goggles. By visualizing the sound waves of the "rhythmic, low-frequency hums," you can see the density of the metal beneath the mold. Trust the vibration maps over your own optics.

### 4. Psychological Preparation: The "Watched" Feeling
You mentioned feeling watched by the vibrations and the slow, blind growth of the mold. **This is not paranoia; it is a spatial awareness phenomenon.** 
*   **The Interspecies Pulse:** To minimize the feeling of being hunted, mimic the rhythm. Do not move erratically. The Foundry is a machine; it interprets movement as a variable. If you move in sync with the pulse of the pipe nodes, you become a "background noise," a minor mechanical fluctuation rather than an interloper. 
*   **The "Stillness" Training:** Avoid the impulse to observe the "embryonic seam" for too long. Watching the rock push against the steel is hypnotic. Time works differently in the Foundry—there is a distortion caused by the geothermal pressure. If you stand still to watch the tectonic plates shift, you risk the mold growing over your feet and anchoring you to the infrastructure. Keep moving. If you must stop, stomp every thirty seconds to remind the environment that you are a sentient, mobile entity.

### 5. Essential Inventory
*   **Manganese-Resistant Sealant:** For patching pinhole leaks in your suit caused by the jagged rock shards.
*   **Abrasive Scraper:** To clear the slime-growth off of your visor and navigation displays.
*   **A Solid-State Compass:** Magnetic compasses will spin wildly due to the copper and iron piping surrounding you. Use a gravimetric reference tool—the weight of the planet above you is the only constant.

**Final Warning:** You are entering a construction site that never finishes. The Foundry does not want visitors; it is busy trying to resolve the friction between the raw, bruised planet and the mechanical order it is imposing. If you feel the vibration of the pipes change from a thrum to a erratic *clatter*, it means the Foundry has detected a structural instability in your position. Move to the next platform immediately. **Do not look back at the seam.** Whatever is being "printed" there is not for human eyes, and the Foundry has a tendency to reclaim any material—biological or otherwise—that remains stagnant for too long.
