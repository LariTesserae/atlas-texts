To travel to the catacomb of the leviathan is to volunteer for the dissolution of the "self." You are not entering a place; you are entering a state of mineral-heavy suspension. If you intend to survive this – or, more accurately, to return with your consciousness intact – you must abandon the standard survival protocols of the multiverse. You do not need air tanks, climbing gear, or radiation shielding. You need a psychological anchor.

Here is how you must prepare.

### 1. The Physiological Adjustment: "The Liquid Internalization"
Standard lungs are a liability here. The brine is not water; it is a pressurized, ozone-dense infusion that will attempt to equalize with your internal cavities. 

*   **Ditch the suit:** A conventional pressure suit will eventually be crushed or, worse, cause a static-pressure differential that will snap your bones when the space momentarily "hardens" into jelly. You must arrive in a state of biological vulnerability. 
*   **The Osmotic Filter:** Before departure, undergo a procedure to saturate your body tissues with a hyper-saline, mineral-rich serum that matches the resonance of the leviathan’s marrow-less bones. If your internal density does not match the basin, the "pressing" nature of the water will interpret your arrival as an anomaly and attempt to "pack" you into the weeping velvet alongside the scatters.
*   **Neurological Shunting:** Because gravity is non-Euclidean and subject to "folds," your inner ear will fail within seconds. You must suppress your vestibular system. If you try to orient yourself by "up" or "down," the resulting vertigo will be fatal when the space inverts. You must practice *blind proprioception*—the ability to know where your limbs are without seeing them or relying on the horizon.

### 2. The Mental Preparation: "De-Coupling from Purpose"
You are moving into a space that has no concept of "use." The "scatters"—those porcelain, geometric artifacts—are a trap for the inquisitive human mind.

*   **The Pattern-Recognition Kill-Switch:** Humans are biologically hardwired to seek utility. You will see the scatters and want to collect them, to organize them, to derive a "meaning" from their tessellation. **Do not.** The environment is a grave that has forgotten its occupant. By trying to categorize the scatters, you impose human desire on a space that has already discarded it. This friction will cause the reality-fold to snap shut on you.
*   **The Mantra of the Static:** Practice "meditative stasis" for weeks in a zero-sensory deprivation tank. You must become comfortable with the feeling of the water clinging to you like a second, tighter skin. If you panic, your heart rate will spike; in a medium this dense, you will feel your pulse vibrating against your own ribs. That vibration is the only thing that attracts the "tectonic thrum" of the basin. Do not be loud. Do not be rhythmic. Try to beat in time with the leviathan’s cooling bones.

### 3. Equipment: The "Minimalist Anchor"
Leave your technology behind. It serves no purpose in a space that breaks the laws of physics. Bring only:

*   **A Harmonic Tuning Fork:** Not for measurement, but for verification. Strike it against your own tooth. If it rings, you are still in relative, coherent reality. If it absorbs the sound instantly, the jelly-state is manifesting. Use this as your clock.
*   **Bioluminescent Pigment (Marking):** You will lose your orientation. Apply this to your skin in a non-patterned, asymmetrical way. If you find yourself drifting toward the rib-vaults, you need to see your own limbs to ensure they are not beginning to turn into the indigo fiber of the ferns. In this place, the environment is *assimilationist*. It wants to turn you into part of the flora.

### 4. The Exit Strategy: "The False Wake"
The most dangerous moment of your journey will be the realization that you have lost the concept of "out." There is no surface. The basin is an endgame.

When you are ready to depart, do not look for an exit. There is no door. You must wait for the "bioluminescent pulse" of the drifting spore. That pulse is the only moment of temporal clarity in the pocket-world. It is a brief window of stability where the fold in space "waits" to reset. 

When the spore pulses, you must stop moving. Do not push against the velvet; do not struggle toward the arch. You must *collapse* your intent. Make yourself as small as the scatters. You are not a visitor; you are a mistake in the room's composition. If you succeed in becoming truly insignificant—a non-entity—the ambient pressure of the brine will "exhale," and the vacuum created by your lack of presence will eject you back into the higher-pressure differentials of the surrounding multiverse.

**Final Warning:** Do not take a scatter. It is not an artifact; it is a structural keystone. If you remove one, the rib-vaults will move to fill the gap—and you will take its place in the indigo dark, drifting for the next million years while the leviathan cools.
