Traditional evaporative cooling will fail because the environment has no moisture affinity and the radiant heat will overwhelm any gas-based exchange.

### 2. Biological Optimization: Calming the Nervous System
Your human intuition will scream. The claustrophobia of a space that is both cathedral-scale and hangar-tight, combined with the lack of light and the constant, violent grinding, will trigger a cascade of terror.

You must install **neurological dampeners**—filters that prevent the suit’s telemetry from overwhelming your optic and auditory nerves. Treat the environment as a dark, silent chamber; ignore the sparks. They are not light; they are electrical runoff.

You cannot hike this space. You must "navigate" the machine-logic of the architecture.

Study the grinding cadence for hours—or days—before taking a single step.

### 4. Psychological Preparation: The Interiority
To enter this place is to accept that you are entering a space that does not know you are there. It is the antithesis of the human experience; there is no horizon, no sky, no open air.

*   **Surrender the concept of "Landscape":** If you look for a way "out," you will lose your mind. You are inside a stomach or an engine. Accept that you are surrounded by raw creation.
