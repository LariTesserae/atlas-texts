Visiting the **Velvet Foundry** is not a journey of exploration; it is an exercise in becoming a footnote in a story that refuses to conclude. You are not entering a workshop; you are entering the psychic memory of a machine that has forgotten its master.

To survive—and to maintain your own sense of self amidst the "stuttering" temporal texture of the Foundry—you must prepare with the precision of a tomb robber and the caution of a deep-sea diver.

### 1. The Bodily Containment (Life Support)
The air is "too thin to carry sound," but more importantly, it is hostile to biological respiratory systems. 
*   **The Atmospheric Suit:** Do not rely on standard pressurized shells. The ionized mineral dust is abrasive and electrically conductive. It *will* find the seals of a standard suit and ground itself there, shorting out your electronics. You need an environmental suit armored in **non-conductive ceramic plating**. 
*   **The Silent Scrubber:** Because the Foundry’s atmosphere is composed of rhythmic mineral exhalations, you must bring an independent, high-capacity closed-loop oxygen supply. Do not attempt to filter the local air; the ionized dust will calcify your lungs within the hour.
*   **Acoustic Damping:** The *thrum-thwack* of the nodes isn't just sound; it is a physical displacement of the bedrock. Standard noise-canceling headphones will fail. You need specialized **bone-conduction stabilizers** that emit out-of-phase vibrations to negate the pressure in your inner ear. If you do not decouple your skeleton from the frequency of the rock, you will begin to experience acute vertigo and, eventually, internal hemorrhaging.

### 2. Gear & Conduct: The "Static" Protocol
The Foundry is a place of absolute, stubborn equilibrium. It hates change. If you bring heat, light, or erratic movement into the space, you become a "high-contrast" object in a monochromatic environment.

*   **Magnetics and Electronics:** The silicon-fronds are sensitive to electromagnetic fields. Leave all modern digital devices behind. Use analog watches, clockwork navigation, and paper charts rendered on non-reactive vellum. A smartphone or a lithium-battery-powered device will likely trigger a sympathetic vibration in the nearest silicon-frond cluster, causing it to "bloom" in your direction—a violent, glass-shattering growth that will cage you in place.
*   **Movement (The Non-Disturbance Rule):** Do not touch the walls. Do not brush against the violet fronds. They are not plant matter; they are a static crystallization of ancient intent. They are rigid. If you break a frond, you create a point of "entropy" that the Foundry—in its automated, mechanical way—will attempt to fix. You do not want to be near a node when it decides you are a piece of debris in need of "maintenance."
*   **Clothing:** You must wear a suit that is slightly larger than your body. As the internal vibrations move through your frame, you will find your skin chafing against your clothes. Soft, heavy-weave wool or padded layers will help distribute the micro-vibrations across your surface, preventing the "static electricity" sensation from blistering your skin.

### 3. The Psychological Anchor
The hardest part of the Velvet Foundry is the feeling that *this place has finished its business*. You will feel an overwhelming, suffocating sense of "pointlessness." It is a cold, mechanical depression that seeps into the marrow of your bones.

*   **The Temporal Totem:** When time feels like "texture" rather than a river, you will lose track of your own duration. Carry a **mechanical metronome** or a physical object you know is changing (like a piece of ice wrapped in a thermos or a simple chemical timer). You need hard evidence that *you* are capable of change, lest you start to mimic the static, unchanging nature of the Foundry.
*   **The "Exit-Sync":** Before you enter the tectonic fault, map the exact path of your ingress with physical markers (non-luminous, non-metallic). The violet luminescence of the fronds is hypnotic. It creates a false sense of depth and a lack of shadows that will make you lose your spatial awareness within minutes.

### A Warning on the Artifacts
The brass-and-iron nodes are the most dangerous things in the room. They are the only things that *have* a purpose. They are performing a task that was assigned to them perhaps millions of years ago. 

**Do not investigate them.** If you lean in to look at the grease-streaked gauges, you are placing yourself within their operating radius. The pistons have been thumping for so long that the air-cushioning around them is absolute; but if you disrupt the airflow, the piston may "adjust" its stroke to compensate. That adjustment will be lethal. 

**The Golden Rule:** Treat the Foundry as you would a graveyard of a god. Be silent, be heavy, and be fleeting. The Foundry does not care that you are there, and that total, crushing indifference is only kept at bay as long as you do not give the place a reason to notice you.
