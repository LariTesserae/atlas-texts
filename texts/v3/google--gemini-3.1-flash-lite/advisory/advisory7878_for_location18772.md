### 1. Physical Augmentation: The "Lead-Lined" Shell

### 2. Metabolic Preparedness: The "Static" Diet

### 3. Psychological Calibration: The "Observer" Mindset
The most dangerous trap in the Aethel-Gorge is the urge to *interact*. You will look at the spires and want to touch them; you will look at the ground and want to find a path.
