To step onto the calcified obsidian of the Spire-Cities is to abandon the biological contract of Earth. You are moving from a world of entropy and decay into one of stasis and vibration. Because this world does not decay, it does not "forgive"; there is no bacteria to break down waste, no water to wash away mistakes, and no atmosphere to insulate you from the fundamental discord of broken physics.

Here is how you must prepare to survive the Spires.

### 1. Physiological Conditioning: The Internal Vacuum
Your greatest enemy is not the height, but the air. It is thin, metallic, and devoid of moisture. 
*   **Respiratory Dampeners:** Do not rely on standard oxygen canisters; the metallic air will react poorly with compressed O2. You require a specialized filtration system that mimics the silicate density of the environment. If you breathe the dust-heavy air directly, the pulverized quartz will act like microscopic sandpaper in your lungs.
*   **The "Slow-Pulse" Protocol:** Your heartbeat will feel like a transgression. Because the Spires are an acoustic environment—reacting to the *Tidal Wind* and the harmonic resonance of floating debris—a rapid, panicked heart rate can make you an outlier in the city's vibration. Practice resonant breathing. Match your cycle to the three-second swell of the wind. You must become a silent component of the architecture, not a dissonance within it.

### 2. Gear: The Physics of Attachment
Gravity is a suggestion, not a law. You cannot trust the floor to remain under your boots.
*   **Magnetic-Harmonic Anchors:** Standard carabiners are useless. You need anchors that lock onto the *vibrational frequency* of the stone rather than the stone itself. If you find yourself in a pocket of low gravity, your gear must latch onto the ambient hum of the floating debris. If you lose your anchor, you do not fall—you *drift* into the void, and there is no air resistance to slow your trajectory.
*   **Surface-Hardened Suits:** Wear layered, non-conductive, matte-finish fabrics. Avoid anything that creates static electricity. The Spires are a massive, silent capacitor; a spark here might trigger a shimmering "curtain" to collapse or expand at the wrong moment. Your gear should be as neutral and inorganic as the buildings themselves.

### 3. Perception: The Trap of the Curtains
The shimmering windows into other geometries are an ontological hazard. 
*   **Visual Calibration:** Never fixate on the curtains of non-light. The human brain is designed to seek patterns; looking into these windows will cause your perception of "up" and "down" to invert. If you lose your sense of orientation, the Spires will not "catch" you—they will simply cease to be relevant to your position. Wear polarized, narrow-aperture goggles. Limit your field of vision to the stone immediately in front of you. 
*   **Auditory Shielding:** The *Tidal Wind* will attempt to seduce you. When it plays the ruins like a thousand flutes, do not attempt to map the melody. It is not music; it is the city’s way of breathing. If you try to cognitively process the math behind the harmonic resonance, you will lose your ego to the structural "drift."

### 4. Interaction: The Sustainers
You are a visitor, but more importantly, you are a *contaminant*. 
*   **Do Not Intervene:** The Sustainers are not sentient in a way you can interpret. They are essentially moving maintenance sub-routines. If you find yourself in their path, **do not move away aggressively.** Simply stop and become as still as a statue. Their vitreous fluid is meant to seal fractures; if you twitch or vibrate too violently, they may perceive you as a structural defect and attempt to "seal" you into the wall.
*   **The Gift of Stillness:** If you must wait out a shift in the *Tidal Wind*, do not seek shelter in the traditional sense. Find a lee side of a sedimentary column, anchor yourself into the basalt, and go dormant. Let the dust bury your boots. By becoming a part of the landscape, you allow the city to rewrite your presence as "background noise."

### 5. The Psychological Toll
The greatest peril is the silence. Having no horizon and no reference point for "natural" life, your mind will scramble. You will feel an urge to reach out and touch the floating obsidian to "ground" yourself. 

**Resist this.** 

The debris is vibrating at a frequency that matches the void. If you touch the floating stones, you are effectively touching the city’s subconscious. You will feel the memory of a trillion years of wind-shear, and your human awareness is too narrow to hold it. 

Pack a physical totem—a small, organic object from home, like a piece of wood or dried leaf. Look at it once every solar cycle. Its decay, its softness, and its biological "wrongness" are the only things that will keep you from dissolving into the geometry of the Spire-Cities. 

When the wind rises and the flutes begin to play, do not listen to them. Hum a song from your own world, quietly—a low, discordant note that proves you are not a stone. You are the only heartbeat this city will hear for a million years. Keep the rhythm simple. Keep it yours.
