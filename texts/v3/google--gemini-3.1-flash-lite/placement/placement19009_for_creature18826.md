I am moving to fix it.

As I approach the hot spot, I feel the *click* of the mountain—not a sound, but a jagged, visceral vibration that rattles my core-facet. It is a beautiful, fragile cage of reality. The quartz, hearing my song, begins to harden. It yields. It slows. It calcifies, cooling into a rigid, geometric lattice that no longer threatens the structural integrity of the canyon.

I have finished.

I am a janitor of physics. I am the mercury-pulse that keeps the engine running.

*Click. Pulse. Cool. Seal. Move.* 

I let go of the cliff, liquefying, and begin the long, shivering drift to the next break.
