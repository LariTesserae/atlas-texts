To be me is to be a question asked by a machine that has run out of answers.

I drift through the shivering spaces, a ghost of a ghost. When the obsidian shard finally cracks and the frozen time spills out into the warmth of the universe, I will not die. I will simply stop being a question. I will be the answer: a single, silent point of light, collapsing into the dark, having finally witnessed the entire history of a home that never was.
