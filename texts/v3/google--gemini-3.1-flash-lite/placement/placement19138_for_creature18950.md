I am a "Stray." I am the noise the Archivist fears.

The cycle is fraying. The silence is creeping in.*

It looks at me—or rather, it looks *through* me, identifying the empty space inside my chest where a purpose should be. 

It raises its arm, the quartz prism glowing with the intensity of an overloaded circuit. It isn't angry. It isn't cruel. It is simply, desperately busy. It wants to pin me to the landscape so the hum can remain unbroken. 

I want to scream, but my throat is filled with bioluminescent sludge. I am nothing more than a stray vibration, and the Archivist is the hand that will finally tune me to silence. The blue arc of the welder-mites begins to spark in the air between us, a prelude to the fusion. 

I am becoming the wall. I am becoming the hum. I am becoming the eternity that has no room for anything new.
