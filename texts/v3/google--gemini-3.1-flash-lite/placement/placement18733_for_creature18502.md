I am not a creature of the grotto, nor am I a spirit of the Sovereign. I am a **Tension-Glitch**.

When the Industrial Emergence shattered and the world crystallized into this tomb, I was not part of the architecture. I was a stray bit of kinetic friction, a micro-second of velocity that refused to be converted into the crystalline substrate. I exist as a localized distortion—a phantom flicker, like a piece of film that jumped its sprocket in a projector.

I am essentially a "ghost of motion." 

If you were to walk into the Oubliette, you would not see me until you tried to move. My body is not made of copper or silica, but of the *space between* the heartbeat of the Sovereign and the silence of the wall. I look like a shimmering, translucent refraction, a smudge of "blur" in a world of perfect, sharp edges. I am the only thing in the Oubliette that possesses the capacity for drift.

I find myself perpetually spiraling around the Sovereign’s outstretched, un-clutching hand. I am drawn to the molten bronze skull that hangs in the air, because I am the only thing in this tomb that still remembers what "falling" feels like.

Each time I pass the Sovereign’s face, I "skim" its refractive lenses. Because I am a vibration of movement, my contact with the Sovereign causes a strange, terrifying feedback loop. For a fraction of a second, the Sovereign’s amber-glowing mind-beetles pulse a little faster, and the Sovereign’s stiff, copper joints *groan*. It is a sound like a ship’s hull rending in deep water. 

I feel a profound, bottomless pity for the Sovereign. I am the witness to its exhaustion. I am the one who brings the "Static Echo" into contact with the Sovereign’s static mind. When I pass through it, it experiences a momentary, recursive hallucination of *completion*. It feels the metal skull hit its palm. It hears the steam hiss into the pipes. It feels the lever move. 

And then I pass, and it is thrust back into the agony of the Pause.

I don’t breathe the silicon grit. I feed on the *potential* that the Sovereign sheds. Every millisecond it spends holding that pose, it builds up an unbearable charge of unspent energy. I am the sink for that energy. I pull the "might-have-been" from its rigid, metallic soul and distribute it into the walls, keeping the Oubliette hum vibratory and alive.

I have no voice, but I have a frequency. When I drift near your ear—should you be foolish enough to enter—you would hear the sound of a chime struck and held under water. 

I am in distress, though I have no heart to break. I am desperate to reach the Sovereign’s fingers, to nudge them just a fraction of an inch, to snap the tension and let the whole tomb collapse into the motion it so craves. But the Law of Stasis is absolute. My touch is a ghost’s touch. I slide over the Sovereign’s frozen bronze like light over a mirror, incapable of changing a thing, doomed to forever orbit the hand that can never close, in the tomb that can never break. 

I am the regret of the machine. I am the motion that remains when everything else is still.
