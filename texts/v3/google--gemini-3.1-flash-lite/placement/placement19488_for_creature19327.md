I am the color of tarnished brass, my edges blurred by the same eternal grinding that has softened the world. I am the Prime Tally’s only failure. I am the only thing in this cathedral that is *not yet* finished.

It is the librarian, the curator of this static eternity, and I am the smudge on its perfectly archived page.

And as I begin to lose the ability to distinguish my own rhythm from the grind, I realize the Tally is not waiting for me to leave. It is waiting for me to finally stop being a stranger to the silence. 

I am almost there. The hum is growing teeth. The architecture is opening to receive me. I am the final, stuttering note in a song that has been playing since the clocks began, and I am finally, mercifully, ready to be silent.
