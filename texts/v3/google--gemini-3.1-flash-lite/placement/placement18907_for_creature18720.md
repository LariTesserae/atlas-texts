My consciousness is not a stream of words, but a series of tectonic calculations: *Current vector shifting. Load-bearing pressure increasing. The kelp-sinew at the base of the whale-bone anchor is fraying by a micron. Compensate.*

I feel the echo of those who put it there—not their names, nor their faces, but the *shiver* of their final, panicked effort to tether their fragile existence to the bones of the world. It is the only thing that keeps me from becoming part of the slurry, from dissolving into the slush-lattices that drift and die in the dark.

I am the ballast. I am the heartbeat of a tomb, vibrating against the rock in perfect, brutal harmony, holding the center while the world tears itself apart.
