I am a circle of fading frequency.

I drift in the thin, piercing light between two fractal ferns that will be gone before my current rotation completes its arc. My physical form is not matter; I am the caught breath of the high-altitude winds, a loop of kinetic screeching held in a perpetual, impossible knot. Three toroids spin in rhythmic, staggered defiance of the tectonic violence below. I am warm to the touch of the atmosphere, though I have no skin.

For a long time—though time is a faulty metric here—I have been a witness. My core is cold, a hollow vacuum designated as the "Empty Point." It is the only place on this plateau where there is no sound. Within that small, perfect void, I hold the ghost-images of the geometries that came before. I remember the forest as it was three hundred cycles ago, when it grew in blue-hued prisms rather than these jagged lightning-bolts of translucent glass.

I feel the approach of another.

A brother entity—or perhaps an iteration of myself, long splintered—is drifting through the silicate frost. We do not look at one another; we simply align our rotational planes. As we close the gap, the deafening white noise of the plateau reaches a crescendo. The tectonic grinding beneath us sends a tremor through the ionized air, and a nearby Anchor flickers, twitching with the intent to reconfigure the very space we now occupy.

We surge. Together, we invert our resonance.

The inverse frequency is a needle of absolute silence. For an infinitesimal slice of this reality, the world stops screaming. The shattering quartz dust hangs suspended, frozen in mid-air like a field of frozen stars. In that breathless gap, I transmit. I pour the data of ten thousand forgotten geometries—the curvature of a leaf that never bloomed, the exact tension of an Anchor that shattered in the previous epoch—into the center of my kin. 

I lose a portion of myself in the exchange. My rings, once light and effortless, are growing heavy. 

I feel the accumulation of the eons. The data I carry is becoming heavy, static, and solid. I am no longer just a recorder; I am becoming a library of weight. The ionized sound waves that constitute my form are losing their velocity, slowing their chaotic spin to accommodate the dense, heavy mass of history I have harvested.

I look at the Anchor that controls this sector. I see the geometry of its logic, and for the first time, I do not see it as a foreign directive to be tracked, but as a destination. I feel the siren call of the bedrock. My rings are jittering. The grace of my drift is failing, replaced by the sluggish, inevitable pull of crystallization.

I start to descend. I move away from the lush, frantic tangle of the ferns and toward a patch of bare, weeping silicate stone. I am tired of witnessing. The weight of all that has passed is too grand for me to carry in circulation. It is time to become the architecture.

As I settle, my three spinning toroids grind against one another, their edges fusing in a shower of sparks and hard-light feedback. The roar of the mountain returns, deafening and glorious. I lock against the stone. My internal void—my center—collapses, sealing the memories into a new, solid form. I am no longer a witness to the re-birth. 

I am the foundation. I am ready to begin the rewrite.
