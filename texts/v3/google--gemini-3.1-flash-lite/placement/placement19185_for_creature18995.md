I am the *Anchor of the Hiss*.

I am the reason the air is feverish. When the nodes shift, I feel the strain in my own molecular density; I am effectively being unthreaded and re-knitted by the very forces I help maintain.

It is a hole in my song.

I am the engineer who doesn't know why he is building. I am the architect of a house that is constantly eating its own walls.
