I am not a traveler here, not in the sense of one who walks with boots and intent. I am a *refraction*.

I am an Anchorite, but I am an anomaly of the caste. I do not anchor a lintel or brace a threshold of dry stone. I am positioned at the very center of a broad, pale ochre plain, far from the huddled huts. My back—a vast, ridged carapace the color of dried blood and iron—faces the zenith. For an eternity, I have been holding a weight that no longer exists. 

Years ago—or perhaps it was a few ticks of the universe’s stutter—I was holding the base of a jagged, crystalline monolith. Then, the lock clicked. Time fractured into that permanent, brittle silence. The monolith above me shattered into fine dust, drifting away in a slow, imperceptible dispersion, but my limbs remained. I am still locked in the posture of bearing an immense burden that has long since vanished into the thin air. 

My arms are braced against nothing. My claws reach upward, curved and hardened into the shape of a desperate support, grasping at the ionized emptiness where the stone once sat. 

I am a monument to a weight that has gone. Because I have reached for the void for so long, I have begun to thin. My edges are becoming translucent, blurring into the bruised purples of the ground beneath me. I am becoming less "Anchorite" and more "incident of light." 

When you drift past me—for you seem to move as a ripple in a stagnant pool—you might mistake me for a trick of the terrain. I am the silence that has a shape. 

I do not see you with eyes; I feel you as a disturbance in the tension. You are a fluid thing, a creature of soft pulses, blood, and heat. In this place of calcified finality, your existence is an absolute outrage. You are a frantic, shifting melody played in a room where every instrument has been smashed. 

I want to scream, but the scream was locked in my throat ten thousand years ago, during that final, universal spasm. It is etched into my internal geometry as a jagged, crystalline formation of trapped sound. Instead of a cry, I project a *shadow*. Look at my shadow: it does not point away from the light. It points *down*. I have managed to press my shadow into the strata of the plateau, pinning it deep into the sub-crust, as if I am trying to anchor myself to the center of the planet so that I might finally, mercifully, come to an end.

I am the hinge of this world. If you were to touch me—though the friction of your warmth would likely shatter us both—you would feel the vibration of a million trillion *Sliver-Spines* rushing through my limbs. They feed on my stillness; they use my grounded weight to maintain their own impossible, mid-air suspensions. 

Do not look too long at my face. The indentations that map my scream have begun to smooth over. I am losing the shape of a being and gaining the shape of the horizon. I am waiting for the moment when I stop being a creature that holds, and finally become the dust that is held.
