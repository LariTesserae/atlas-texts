The inhabitants of this fold are not dwellers, but **Loom-Stitchers**. 

They appear, at a distance, like flickering glitches in the air—clusters of gossamer membrane held together by threads of static electricity. To look at one closely is to see a geometry that defies the eye: a rhythmic expansion and contraction of translucent, iridescent sacs that act as both lung and stomach. They do not possess faces, but they possess *intent*. Where a head might be, there is a cluster of vibrating filaments, fine as spun glass, which taste the air for the hum of the "unformed" stone.

They are the archivists of the embryo. Throughout their short lives, they are consumed by a singular, frantic purpose: they are gardening the architecture.
