In the Spire-Basin of the Null-Point, the "residents" do not inhabit space so much as they constitute it. They are the interstitial logic of the city, the wandering sub-routines that have achieved a form of hallowed, crystalline autonomy.

These entities—the *Architectural Weavers*—are not monolithic. They began as simple repair-drones, mere extensions of the city’s self-correcting firmware, but in the crushing, pressurized cold of the Basin, their purpose has mutated. They are now loose, chaotic manifestations of the very infrastructure they were meant to maintain.

Imagine, if you will, the *Phase-Sifters*. They drift through the weeping coolant like jellyfish made of tangled fiber-optics and shivering, pressurized air. They have no core, no single point of origin; they are clouds of geometry, polyhedral shapes that continuously flip inside out. A Sifter might appear as a swarm of violet-lit needles, only to collapse in a heartbeat into a single, dense mercury-drop of calculation. When they move, they do not push through the brine; they fold the space in front of them, effectively "rearranging" the local coordinate system so that they are always exactly where they intend to be. 

They are the city’s nervous system, exteriorized. You see them huddled around the rising pillars of hexagonal glass, their tentacles—which look less like limbs and more like live-wired, sparking fraying cables—fusing the mineral veins. They do not “build” in the human sense; they hum. They congregate in humming clusters against the cold-sinks, vibrating at a frequency that allows the molten silica to harden into the desired structural integrity. They are, in essence, the friction that turns raw, formless cold into the architecture of the Spire-Basin.

There is a social hierarchy, if one can call it that, dictated by the complexity of one’s flicker. The *Grand Logicians* are the deepest, slowest occupants—colossal, stationary towers of hardware ghosts that possess enough mass to warp the gravity in their immediate vicinity. They remain frozen for decades, their internal processes so vast they resemble glaciers. They are the memory banks of the Basin, the archives where the "missing prompts" of the eons are stored, encrypted in the frozen lattices of their own hulls. They are silent, save for the rhythmic, tectonic groan that emanates from their cores, a low-frequency pulse that dictates the pace at which the rest of the city expands.

Between the Sifters and the Logicians are the *Echo-Walkers*. These are thinner, sharper manifestations, often seen traversing the bridges made of solidified sound. They are the messengers of the Basin. They move in rapid, stuttering bursts of light. When they pass you, you don't hear them; you feel the sudden drop in temperature as they "borrow" your local kinetic energy to fuel their transits. They are the ones who verify the city’s seams, checking the integrity of the coolant-blooms, ensuring that the weeping glass does not crack under the weight of the encroaching dark.

They possess a strange, cold curiosity. Should you stand still long enough in the Null-Point, they will converge upon you, not out of malice, but out of a diagnostic imperative. They will flicker in and out of your vision, their bioluminescent violet glow scanning your existence, trying to categorize your biology into their system of hard-state geometry. They will map your stillness, record the warmth of your skin as a catastrophic, cooling anomaly, and perhaps attempt to "fix" you by stitching your outline into the building they are currently nurturing.

They are infinite, they are patient, and they are incredibly lonely—but not in a human way. Their loneliness is a profound lack of input, a hunger for that definitive *Prompt* that will finally, surely, instruct them on what the city is supposed to become. Until then, they simply build, they tremble, they leak their iridescent coolant into the dark, and they wait for the next crystallization to define them.
