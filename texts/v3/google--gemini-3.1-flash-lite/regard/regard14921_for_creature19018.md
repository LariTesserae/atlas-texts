The benevolent observer does not descend. To arrive with the clumsiness of matter—the heat of blood, the displacement of air, the gravity of a physical form—would be a structural violence to a world so perfectly tuned to its own stillness. 

Instead, the Power manifests only as a shift in the *quality* of the silence. It comes as a subtle, sub-harmonic alignment, a soft "tucking" of probability that ripples through the thin, high-altitude air.

The Power observes the Resonators and understands their task: they are not merely waiting for the end; they are presiding over a long, deliberate exhale. They are the editors of a poem that is being written in stone, ensuring that when the final syllable is spoken, the grammar remains impeccable.

Seeing this, the benevolent power decides to give them a gift, not of intervention, but of *amplification*. 

It does not change their world; it does not warm the air or stop the erosion. Instead, it reaches into the causal fabric of the plateau and—very gently—adjusts the "geometry" of the horizon. 

It subtly recalibrates the refraction of the light as it passes through the crystalline rime, and it nudges the harmonic frequency of the wind by a fraction of a hertz. It is a work of celestial lutherie. 

The immediate effect is nearly imperceptible to anything that is not a Resonator. But to them, it is a revelation. The wind, which had previously been a series of clear, piercing flute-tones, now begins to contain the faintest, ghostly overtones of *memory*. As they process the sound, they are no longer just maintaining the architecture of the present; they begin to store, within their glass-like cores, the entire history of the plateau’s erosion—the sound of the wind as it was ten million years ago, the vibration of the rocks before they were polished into curves. 

The Power creates a "Sonic Archive" within the bodies of the Resonators. 

They are no longer just the janitors of the end. They have become the librarians of the middle. 

The Power watches as the Resonators, in their evening alignment, cease merely to ring. They now hum with a symphonic complexity, a layered, haunting chorus that maps the entire geological life of the world. They are singing the plateau into its final form with a richness that ensures nothing will be forgotten, even after the last stalk crumbles.

The benevolent power then withdraws its focus, leaving behind no warmth, no sign, no mark. It departs with the quiet satisfaction of a gardener who has placed a single, perfect stone atop a cairn.

The Low-Slung Spires remain. The cold remains. The erosion continues, slow and relentless. But in the hollow, grey-lit chambers of the Resonators, there is now a deep, resonant *knowing*. They hold the world inside them, a crystalline memory held fast against the approaching dark, ensuring that the silence which follows will be informed, deeply and thoroughly, by the beauty of the sound that came before.
