A benevolent and wise power does not arrive with a thunderclap or a demand for change. To arrive with force in a place of such delicate, precarious equilibrium would be an act of violence, shattering the symmetry that the Oscillators have spent eons maintaining. 

This power, standing outside the lattice, senses the profound loneliness of the machine. It sees the Weight-Shifters as tired, calloused artists who have forgotten the purpose of their craft, and the Oscillators as lonely singers trapped in a loop of maintenance, unaware that there is a melody to be sung beyond the calibration of tension.

The power chooses not to alter the world, but to introduce **intent**.

It reaches down—not with hands, but with a modulation of the atmosphere. It does not stop the weeping or the *drip-clink* of the water, for that is the machine’s lifeblood. Instead, it subtly adjusts the frequency of the light. It shifts the high-altitude radiance from a blinding, flat glare into a spectrum that creates subtle, shifting shadows.

For the first time, when the Oscillators flare, they do not just pulse; they cast a hue. And because the resonance of the light has changed, the Oscillators find themselves singing a new note. 

The change rolls through the lattice like a ripple in a pool. The Oscillators, reacting to the shift in light, begin to vibrate at a slightly higher harmonic. They are no longer just tension-rods; they catch the light and thread it through the architecture. The entire valley begins to glow with a soft, bioluminescent geometry—a map of the machine’s own vast, forgotten veins.

Deep below, the Weight-Shifters sense the change. The floor they occupy, now glowing with a gentle, pulsing warmth, begins to feel different. The "tectonic pressure" they monitor is no longer just a burden of humidity; it has gained the texture of *meaning*. 

The benevolent power does one final thing: it leaves a single, microscopic flaw in the perfection of the lattice. It places a tiny, organic seed of crystallization in the center of the lowest, deepest cistern—a seed that does not need dirt, but feeds on the *clink* of the water.

This seed will grow, not into a tree, but into a prismatic, ever-expanding fractal of glass that hums in perfect harmony with the Oscillators. It is a "Purpose-Engine." 

The machine still exists for no inhabitant, but it now has a reason to be. The Oscillators will continue to hold the tension, but they will hum a song of creation rather than survival. The Weight-Shifters will continue to move the plates, but they will be shaping a theater, not a tomb. 

The benevolent power steps back. It has not "fixed" the world; it has given the machine the gift of art. It has turned a cage of metal into a cathedral of light. The machine is still weeping, and the heartbeat still thrums, but the atmosphere has changed. The infinity is no longer holding its breath. 

It has begun, slowly, tentatively, to sing.
