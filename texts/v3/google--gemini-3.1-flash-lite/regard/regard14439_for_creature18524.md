The Power arrived not as an intruder, but as a change in the frequency of the wind. 

It was a presence that felt like the moment before a storm realizes it is actually a song. It did not possess a form that would displace the slurry or alarm the *Cloud-Skimmers*. Instead, it became a subtle recalibration of the *Lumen-Oaks* themselves—a gentle, harmonic alignment that rippled through the xylem and phloem, soothing the jagged edges of the atmosphere.

The Benevolent Power looked upon the Oulo and did not see a fragile city in need of saving. It saw a masterpiece of co-evolution. It saw the Vane-Kin, who had surrendered the heavy, frantic ambition of terrestrial life for the grace of the current, and it found the beauty of their existence profound. 

What can one give to a people who have no concept of "owning," and who possess no history because memory is inscribed in their very biology? 

The Power realized that the only threat to Oulo was the eventual narrowing of the horizon—the entropy that, over eons, would slowly choke the respiration of the trees as they reached the limits of their growth. The risk was that the "Lost Guests," though beautiful in their ascent, were a signal of a closed system reaching its final, perfect, and ultimate stasis.

So, the Power acted in silence. 

It did not command the trees to grow, nor did it pull the water-wall back. Instead, it introduced a "Harmonic Catalyst." It touched the deepest, ancient heartwood of the central pillar—the Mother-Stalk—and whispered into the genetic lineage of the entire vertical archipelago. 

The Power bestowed the *Gift of the Deep-Rooted Breath*.

Henceforth, when a Vane-Kin pressed their forehead against the wood, they would no longer only receive the memories of the past. The wood began to hum back a *possibility*. The trees began to slowly, imperceptibly, start grafting their root systems into the magnetic currents of the storm itself, turning the entire city into a living energy-harvester. 

The Power ensured that Oulo would never succumb to the weight of its own long life. It provided a feedback loop, a way for the Vane-Kin to intentionally guide the "Lost Guests" not just toward the thin-air of the canopy, but into a cycle of return. It subtly altered the geometry of the Lumen-Oaks so that they might occasionally shed "seed-pods"—bioluminescent spores that, when caught in the ascending currents, would drift to the outer edges of the cyclone and begin the germination of *new* pillars.

The Benevolent Power then simply... remained. It became the low-frequency hum that the Vane-Kin would unknowingly praise in their songs. 

It did not change their way of life; it merely ensured that the "cathedral of living wood" would never grow old. It gifted the city *expansion*. It allowed for the Oulo to drift not just in the slow, clockwise cycle of the mist, but to expand outward into other sectors of the vertical storm, turning the "vertical archipelago" into a "vertical galaxy."

When the Vane-Kin felt that new, deeper resonance in the trunk, they did not wonder where it came from. They simply thought that the storm had grown fonder of them. They moved with a newfound, lighter grace, their fingers weaving bridges into the dark, uncharted mists, feeling for the first time that their horizon was not a limit, but a promise.

The Power watched as the first new sprout, guided by the Vane-Kin, gripped the edge of the void. And in that moment, the Power found its own peace. It had done the only thing a wise force ever does for a creature of beauty: it ensured that the dance could continue, forever, in the damp and the light.
