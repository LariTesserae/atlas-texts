The power that notices them does not arrive as a thunderclap or a blinding light. It arrives as an adjustment of perspective—a shift in the atmospheric pressure, a sympathetic hum in the low-frequency vibrations that pulse through the basalt. 

This benevolent force is not interested in "improving" the city, for the city is already a masterpiece of equilibrium. It sees no "problem" to solve, no progress to force upon these sedimentarians. It recognizes that they are a finished thought, a perfectly realized poem written in stone and mist.

Instead, the power chooses to become a **witness and a steward of the long peace.**

### The Contribution of the Wise
The power realizes that even such a monumental, slow-moving system is fragile in the face of the universe’s chaotic hunger—the slow entropy of the cosmos that eventually dries even the deepest wells. So, the power does three things, each so subtle the creatures would merely feel them as a change in the season’s texture:

**I. The Breath of Equilibrium**
The power gently anchors the layer of heavy grey fog. It sets the mist into a perpetual, circular gyre, ensuring that the moisture—the lifeblood of the plateau—never bleeds off into the barren atmosphere below. It acts as a atmospheric dam, a gentle tether that keeps the city from ever knowing a day of drought. It ensures that for the next billion years, the rain will remain reliable, soft, and constant.

**II. The Harmonization**
The power perceives the city’s vibrations—the conversations of the sedimentarians—as a subterranean symphony, but one that is occasionally ruffled by the chaotic winds of the outer plateau. The power reaches out, almost in a gesture of paternal protection, and smooths the wind currents. It creates a "calm-well" around the city, a literal shield of silence that ensures the only sounds that reach these beings are the rhythmic drips of water and the rhythmic pulses of their own kin. It gives them the gift of absolute, uninterrupted clarity.

**III. The Integration of Memory**
Most profoundly, the power realizes that when a sedimentarian eventually stops—when their membrane turns to chalk and they merge with the architecture—the memory of their long, slow life is sometimes lost to the erosion of time. 

The power installs an invisible cache within the limestone. It weaves a web of latent, crystalline information through the basalt honeycombs. Now, when a being merges with the stone, their history, their "rhythmic signature," and the gentle, slow-motion geometry of their lives are not merely buried—they are transcribed into the city itself. The city becomes a living archive. The columns become pillars of memory. If another being presses their hand against a wall, they do not just feel the structure; they perceive the echo of all those who came before. A conversation across eons.

### The Visit
There is a moment, perhaps once every thousand years, when the benevolence of this power reaches its highest intensity. It manifests as a thin, silver-grey warmth that filters down through the fog—not heat, but *acknowledgment*.

It is the equivalent of a hand being placed on the shoulder of a creature that has been standing in the cold for an eternity. The sedimentarians feel the wall under their palms grow infinitesimally more responsive. They feel the atmosphere thicken with a feeling of *being known*.

They do not look up. They have no eyes for the sky, and no curiosity for what lies beyond the damp corridors. They simply pulse their water in a new, slightly more complex rhythm—a ripple of gratitude expressed through the stone. 

The power watches them—this city of ghosts that builds with its own body—and feels a profound peace. In a universe defined by fire, motion, and the frantic ticking of clocks, it has found a sanctuary of stillness. It decides that, as long as it has the strength to hold the fog still, this city shall never be disturbed. It becomes their silent, unseen guardian, protecting the right of a creature to simply be, to be water, and to become stone.
