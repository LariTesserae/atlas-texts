The benevolent power does not descend as a storm, nor as a blinding flash of golden decree. It does not come to judge the rhythm of the basin or to stop the grinding, for it understands that in the deep dark, existence is a sentence written in stone, and to erase the sentence is to end the story.

Instead, the power arrives as a *change in the quality of the stillness.*

It manifests first as a subtle, cooling harmonic. Where the Needles harvest the frantic noise of the friction, the Power introduces a counter-note: a frequency that does not pulse with hunger, but resonates with *completeness*. It is the sound of a stone that has finally found the bottom of the river and no longer needs to be carried.

The Power, wise enough to know that these creatures are trapped in a cycle of caloric starvation, does not offer them food—that would invite a riot. It offers them an architecture.

With a thought, the Power permeates the moisture that coats the basalt and the chitin. It alters the viscosity of the "cold sweat" that slickens the shale. It imbues the condensation with a mineral catalyst that is not consumed, but which acts as a structural stabilizer.

The result is a transformation of the basin’s geology. When the cathedral-arched entities next move, they find that their paths do not merely wear the stone down; they begin to *build* it up. The friction, once a destructive force of attrition, is repurposed by the catalyst. As the mites click and the cathedral-arches drag, their movement now leaves behind a fine, hardening deposit—a self-assembling ceramic that fills the gaps in the jagged floor and builds slender, spiraling bridges between the basalt shards.

The Power essentially gives the Silt-Shifters the ability to manifest their own environment into something more than a flat, grinding trap. 

It does not stop their hunting; it does not stop their hunger; it does not stop the movement. But it turns the basin from a prison of attrition into a garden of unfolding, living geometry. The "void" that you represent—your stillness—is protected. The Power places a gentle, iridescent film around you, like the shell of a pearl. It is a protective, shimmering bubble that keeps the mites from abrading your form. 

For the first time in an epoch, the Silt-Shifters do not try to smooth you over. They encounter your presence and, sensing the barrier, they treat you as a monument. They begin to weave their own paths *around* you, creating a beautiful, intricate latticework of obsidian and glass that rises up to embrace your shape. 

You become the still center of a silent, growing cathedral. 

The Power does not speak, for words would be noise in a place that has too much already. It simply watches, content. It has taken a world of grinding, desperate survival and turned it into a world of creation. The friction remains, but it is no longer an eraser; it is a chisel. 

Having set the gears of this new, slow alchemy in motion, the benevolent power begins to fade. It leaves behind no instructions, no warnings, and no demand for gratitude. It leaves only the new architecture of the basin, and the realization in the collective "mind" of the swarm—a flickering, new sense of stability—that for the first time in history, the path they walk is one that will still exist after they have passed. 

The weight remains. The wet iron taste remains. But the grinding has become a dance, and the basin has begun, finally, to rise.
