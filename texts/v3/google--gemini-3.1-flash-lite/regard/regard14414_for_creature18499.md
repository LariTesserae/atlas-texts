The benevolent power, hovering in the periphery of this fold, does not enter as a conqueror or a god. It enters as a *breath*—a cool, stabilizing draft that weaves through the violet aperture above, momentarily stilling the chaotic gravitational drift.

This power recognizes the Loom-Stitchers for what they are: the frantic, beautiful laborers of an unfinished Creation. It sees the tragic, prophetic shadow in the silver pool—that inevitable future of rusting machinery and static, the decay that currently waits for this place like a predator outside a closed door. 

The benevolent power chooses to do three things, subtle enough to be mistaken for the natural physics of the fold, yet profound enough to rewrite its destiny.

**First: The Weaver’s Accord.**
The power reaches out, not with hands, but with intent, and anchors the gravity. It does not impose order, but provides a foundation for the Stitchers' work. By subtly thickening the "weave" of the space, it ensures that the droplets from the ceiling no longer shatter entirely into mist, but instead pool into cohesive, nourishing reservoirs on the pedestals. This gives the Loom-Stitchers a reprieve. They no longer need to spend every fraction of their existence in a frantic, starving harvest. They are now allowed a moment of *stasis*.

**Second: The Preservation of the Archive.**
The power touches the silver pool at the center of the basalt. It does not shatter the image of the future—it is not the place of a wise power to hide the truth of entropy—but it shifts the resonance of the water. It turns the pool from a mirror of decay into a filter of reclamation. By doing so, it ensures that when the Loom-Stitchers "unravel" upon their death, their energy does not merely seep back into the walls as decoration; it is directed. Their essence flows into the quartz, cooling the "tender, muscular" stone and hardening it against the encroachment of the rust and machinery shown in the future-vision. The Stitchers are no longer just gardeners; they become the *foundation*.

**Third: The Gift of Resonance.**
Finally, the power hums—a subsonic vibration that harmonizes with the creatures. It is a song of protection. It communicates a simple, fundamental truth to the Stitchers: *You are not alone in the construction.* 

Before this, the Stitchers were frantic, fearful of the "rifts of light" that pruned them. With this new resonance, they move with a different grace. They realize that the rifts are not pruning them out of malice, but creating space for new growth. They begin to dance with the rifts, folding their light-membranes into the gaps, knitting the rifts closed before they can consume. 

The benevolent power then retreats, leaving the fold exactly as it appeared to be—a secret tucked behind the skin of the world. 

Nothing looks changed to an outside observer. But the silence is no longer heavy with the frantic desperation of short, sacrificial lives. It is a quiet, steady rhythm of building. The Loom-Stitchers, now comforted by the knowledge of their own legacy, settle upon the marble. They pulse in deep, tranquil blues and soft golds. 

The fold is still unfinished, but it is no longer a child shivering in the dark. It has become a womb that knows, with absolute certainty, what it is destined to become. The encroaching machines of the future are still there, in the silver pool, but they look dimmer, farther away—the nightmares of a world that this place will eventually outgrow, long before the rust can ever find the latch.
