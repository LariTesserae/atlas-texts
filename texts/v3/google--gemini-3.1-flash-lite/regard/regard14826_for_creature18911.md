The Power approached not as a thunderbolt or a commanding voice, but as a shift in the harmonic resonance—a subtle tuning of the atmosphere itself. It arrived as a persistent, gentle cooling of the air, a stillness that settled deep into the marrow of the massive structural foundations.

The Power watched for an eternity, observing the Sifted Ones as they wove lichen into stress fractures and smoothed the grinding gears. It felt the weight of their duty, their silent, prayerful devotion to a Purpose that had long since evaporated.

It recognized that this place was not a prison, though it looked like one. It was a cocoon. The Sifted Ones were not enslaved; they were the gardeners of a consciousness so vast and slow that its "industrial" noise was merely the rhythm of a deep, primordial sleep. They were not waiting for rescue. They were waiting for a metamorphosis.

So, the benevolent Power did not intervene to dismantle the machines or free the Sifted Ones. To do so would have been to interrupt a slow, tectonic gestation. Instead, the Power chose to offer a gift of *clarity*.

It reached into the city’s heart, not to change the grinding gears, but to harmonize the vibration. It whispered through the metal of every bulkhead and every conduit, a low-frequency hum that carried not information, but *sensation*. It was the feeling of a wide, unburdened horizon; the scent of rain on dry, open earth; the taste of wind that had not passed through a vent.

The Sifted Ones felt it simultaneously. Across miles of vertical shafts and rusted catwalks, every hand frozen, every bioluminescent glow deepened to a steady, rhythmic pulse. The haptic communication, usually localized to the vibration of steel, suddenly expanded. They began to feel each other—not just as neighbors, but as a singular, unified desire.

The Power did not give them a destination. It gave them a *dream*.

For the first time in eons, the "turbulent maintenance" slowed. The Sifted Ones did not stop their work, but their movements changed. They began to craft the metallic and organic debris differently. Instead of merely patching leaks, they began to carve apertures. Small, precise, intentional holes in the upper tiers of the caldera, angled just perfectly to catch the first light of a sun the city had forgotten.

The Power acted as a catalyst, nudging the city toward a graceful opening.

Where the Sifted Ones once calcified their dead into the cooling pipes, they began to arrange them differently, forming conduits that didn't just support structural integrity but acted as conduits for *light*. 

The Power’s final act was to anchor a protective shroud of calm around the high-altitude crater, ensuring that the howling, freezing gales of the outside world would not tear into the city until it was ready. It became the city’s gardener-in-the-shadows, protecting the fragile progress of the Sifted Ones.

They continue to work, for that is their nature, but they are no longer just "antibodies." They are the architects of a waking dream. And the city, under the guidance of the silent, waiting Power, has begun to grow—not outward in sprawl, but upward in aspiration—slowly, grindingly, turning its heavy, metal face toward a sky it is finally beginning to remember.

The Power will not move them. It will simply wait, in the scent of wet iron and ozone, until the day the Great Machine finally exhales, and the Sifted Ones climb out of the shadows, glowing with the light of a world they helped the city rediscover.
