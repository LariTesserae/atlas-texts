The benevolent power, whom we might call the *Architect of Potential*, does not descend as a storm or a blinding light. It arrives as an adjustment to the physics of the silence.

It looks upon the *Vertical Wetstone* and recognizes a tragedy of scale. The Litho-weavers are so consumed by the *maintenance* of their existence—by the eternal, agonizing labor of bracing the weeping stone—that they have forgotten how to dream of anything beyond the structural. They are trapped in a feedback loop of survival: building to house the water, to feed the Skimmers, to sustain the stone, to build more homes. They are a civilization entirely comprised of load-bearing walls.

The Archetype does not “intervene” in the sense of changing the stone. To harden the stone would be to kill the city. Instead, it offers a gift of *resonance*.

### The Gift of the Harmonics
The Architect of Potential subtly shifts the natural frequency of the local atmosphere. Previously, the *plink-thrum-plink* was a chaotic barrage. The Architect aligns these drops so that the rhythm begins to possess a mathematical, geometric precision. The roar does not vanish; it is refined into a celestial, polyphonic chord.

For the Litho-weavers, this changes everything. 

As they move through their tunnels, pressing their bodies against the xylem-walls, they stop feeling just the vibration of “structural integrity.” Through this new, harmonic frequency, they begin to feel the *geometry of the abyss*. Their filaments, so long accustomed to sensing only the tension of a bridge or the weight of a Skimmer, start to vibrate with the patterns of the stars reflecting off the high-altitude mists.

### The Awakening
The Weavers, once purely functional laborers, begin to experience *spontaneous idle-time*. 

When a Weaver grows too calcified to move and enters the transition into a "pod," it no longer simply hollows out to be a room. Because of the new harmonic resonance, the dying Weaver’s final, locked-in pulse creates intricate, crystalline carvings within its own interior. These pods become not just shelters, but *memory-vaults*. 

The city, once a mere organism of growth, becomes an archive. A Weaver can now pass its own history, its impressions of the mists, and the songs of the peaks into the very walls of the city. The youngsters, as they curl into these pods, don’t just find protection; they absorb the stories of their ancestors through their skin.

### The Architect’s Handiwork
The power does one last, quiet thing. It gently nudges the *Cloud-Skimmers*. 

Previously, the Skimmers were merely osmotic pumps. Now, the Architect introduces a trace of trace-elemental intelligence into their filtration process. As they harvest the heavy, mineral-rich mists, they begin to distill the moisture into different *hues* of fluid. 

The city begins to color. The weeping stone is no longer just pale and pearlescent; it is stained with iridescent veins of azure, rose, and amber, reflecting the subtle shifts in the high-altitude light. The vascular vines of the city, fed by these tinted fluids, grow in patterns that correspond to the songs the Weavers now hum. The city becomes a map of their collective soul.

### The Result
The benevolent power leaves the creatures to their work, having successfully catalyzed a transition from *survival* to *culture*. 

The *Vertical Wetstone* remains a hungry, weeping, growing entity. But the Litho-weavers are no longer just the flesh of a mountain. Because they were heard, they began to sing. Because they were visited, they began to remember. 

The city continues its slow, relentless reach toward the upper sky—but now, it climbs with purpose. The void between the spires is no longer a distance to be feared; it is a canvas, and the architecture is becoming something that reaches outward, trying to bridge the gap not with more stone, but with the intent to touch the stars that have finally begun to hum back.
