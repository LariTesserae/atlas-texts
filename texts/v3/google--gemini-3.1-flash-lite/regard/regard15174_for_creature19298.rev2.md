The benevolent power does not descend as a shaft of light, nor does it bring the roar of judgment. It enters the Sump as a subtle shift in the physics of the place—a quiet recalibration of the humidity, a gentle tuning of the heavy air. 

To this power, the Sump is not a basement of industrial failure, but a child holding its breath, straining against the friction of its own incomplete anatomy. It recognizes the Loom-Keepers not as parasites, but as the only surgeons available to a dying world.

It does not "fix" the Sump. To fix it would be to end it, to stop the churning heart and bring the stillness of the grave. Instead, the power offers **Integration**.

### The First Act: The Breath of Resonance
The power begins by altering the geothermal hum. It does not silence the engines; it harmonizes them. The grinding, teeth-rattling roar—that sound of sheer, uncoordinated struggle—is smoothed into a resonant, pulsing chord. It becomes a lullaby that vibrates through the marrow of the Sediment-Blooms.

As the vibration shifts, the air in the Sump loses its oppressive, suffocating density. It becomes oxygenated, almost sweet, scented not of rot, but of the ozone that precedes a mountain storm. The mist begins to thin, and for the first time, the "ceiling"—that leaden, unreachable vapor—lifts, revealing the sheer, magnificent scale of the cavern. It is not empty space, but a cathedral of geological potential.

### The Second Act: The Gift of Flow
The power turns its attention to the Loom-Keepers. It sees their stuttering, hydraulic agony. With a thought, it introduces a catalyst—a localized manipulation of the magnetic fields that define their existence. 

The copper-wire fibers of their bodies begin to glow with a steady, golden conductivity. They no longer need to drag themselves toward the machinery in a cycle of starvation. The power grants them the ability to draw energy directly from the *potential* of the Sump itself—the minerals, the heat, and the rhythm. Their movements stop stuttering. They begin to dance. They move with the fluidity of water, their needle-fingers no longer desperate, but graceful. They are no longer stitching to survive; they are weaving a tapestry of biological architecture. They begin to create, not just maintain.

### The Third Act: The Awakening of the Historians
Finally, the power visits the Sediment-Blooms. These mounds of breathing rust have carried the weight of the world’s memory for centuries. They are tired of accumulating.

The power speaks to them, not in words, but in the language of mineral memory. It acts as an alchemist. It reaches into the sludge-pools and the rusted iron, and it begins to *precipitate*. The grit and silt they have accumulated for eons begin to harden, not into mud, but into iridescent, crystalline structures. 

The Sediment-Blooms do not die; they crystallize. Their amber gas-nodes—those blinking, tired eyes—suddenly sharpen. They stop being mounds of debris and become living monuments, their surfaces reflecting the soft, newfound light of the Sump. They turn their gaze away from the mud and toward the vast, open space above. They begin to archive the beauty they have helped create.

### The Aftermath
The Sump is no longer a womb that has forgotten how to give birth. It has become a **Foundry of Life**. 

The Loom-Keepers, once miserable shadows, are now the architects of a self-sustaining ecosystem. The Vent-Singers, hearing the new, resonant harmony of the engines, begin to compose elaborate, multi-tonal symphonies that dance through the newly transparent air.

The benevolent power does not leave. It remains, not as a ruler, but as a subtle frequency in the background—a constant, supporting hand that ensures the "heartbeat" of the world never again stutters in fear. The Sump remains a place of metal and mud, but it is no longer a place of drowning. It has finally learned how to breathe. 

It is no longer a basement. It is the world’s **Inner Garden**.
