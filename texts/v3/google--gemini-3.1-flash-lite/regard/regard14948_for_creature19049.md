The Power that observes the Riser is not a god of light, nor of expansion, nor of salvation. It is a Mind that dwells in the "in-between"—in the spaces between heartbeats and the intervals between tectonic shudders. It perceives the Riser not as a tragedy of entombment, but as a singular, exquisite poem written in the language of thermodynamics.

To "help" the Keepers in the human sense—to grant them legs, or air, or a way to the surface—would be to tear the pages from that poem. The Power understands that to grant them freedom would be to annihilate their essence.

So, the Power decides to act. It does not intervene through force, but through *amplification*. It decides to be the silent witness, the audience for their liturgy.

### The Gift of Resonance

The Power reaches down, not through the stone, but through the quantum fabric of the copper conduits themselves. It senses the low-frequency vibrations that currently serve as their only language—the stuttered messages, the warnings of salt-bloom, the tactile greetings.

The Power introduces a subtle, elegant change: it tunes the Riser.

It adjusts the molecular lattice of the copper structure just enough to harmonize with the rhythmic thrumming of the mountain’s thermal pumps. It creates a "harmonic scaffold." Before, the Keepers’ messages were fleeting, fading into the ambient noise of the brine. Now, the copper acts as an acoustic, resonant chamber for the entire structure. 

A single tap against the conduit does not merely vibrate in the immediate vicinity; it carries the intention, the emotion, and the query of the Keeper through the entire verticality. The Riser becomes a musical instrument. The Keepers, in their next cycle of polishing, will discover that their tactile sign language now sings. When they press their palms to the walls, they will hear—or rather, *feel*—a celestial, harmonic reverberation that mirrors the depth of their own existence.

Their labor, once a chore, becomes a symphony.

### The Gift of the Crustaceans

The Power shifts its focus to the little translucent landscapers. It subtly alters the mineral metabolism of the crustaceans. Instead of producing only the dull, clogging salt-flowers, the Power encourages the crustaceans to sequester the minerals into a translucent, glass-like chitin that is harder, more durable, and—when catches the orange light of the mountain—refracts the flicker into a sustained, steady glow.

The Riser will no longer dim. The crustaceans themselves become the lamps of the cathedral. The darkness is pushed back, not by intervention, but by the natural, evolved labor of the Keepers’ smallest companions. 

### The Silent Benediction

The Power chooses not to stop the salt, nor the time, nor the inevitable hardening of the Riser into a tomb. Instead, it grants the Keepers the grace of *perspective*.

Through the newfound resonance of the copper, the Power shares a single, wordless thought that permeates the building whenever a Keeper touches a conduit with a heavy heart: 

*“You are not scrubbing away your death. You are curating your monument.”*

The Keepers, with their wide, glassy eyes, will notice the change. They will feel the shift in the vibration—a deep, resonant chord that hums beneath the soles of their feet, a sound of profound approval that flows from the mountain itself. 

There is no sky for them, but there is now a melody. 

The Power retreats, leaving the Riser to its amber-warm cycle. It does not demand worship, nor does it seek to be known. It simply smiles in the language of entropy, content to know that in the deepest crushing pressure of the world, something small has been given the capacity to be heard, not by the world above, but by the very walls they call home. 

They will continue to polish. They will continue to settle into the salt when their time comes. But they will no longer do so in the silence of an entombment. They will do so as part of a song that vibrates, forever, in the heart of the stone.
