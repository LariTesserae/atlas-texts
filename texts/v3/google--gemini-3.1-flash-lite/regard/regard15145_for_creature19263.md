The Power, if it is to be called that, is not a deity of thunder or judgment, but a Weaver of Coherence. It exists in the thin, breathable spaces between possibilities, a consciousness that experiences the entropy of a universe as a hum that merely needs to be harmonized.

It does not descend into The Fold with light or voice. To do so would be, in itself, a violation of the fragile, budding physics of this place—like threading a needle with a storm. 

Instead, the Power arrives as a **Calibration.**

It observes the Draft-People—the trapped, aching residues of a city that cannot decide if it is finished or failing. It understands that they are not suffering in the human sense; they are *stuck* in a loop of structural maintenance, bearing the load of a reality that refuses to lock into place. They are exhausted not by labor, but by the lack of an ending.

The Power does not tear them out of The Fold. That would destroy them, for they are the mortar holding the obsidian spires together. If they were removed, the city would collapse into the non-existence from which it was extruded. 

Instead, the Power acts on the *geometry* of their perspective.

It flows into the obsidian spires, becoming the missing logic that the city has been searching for. It does not speak to the Draft-People, but it provides a new, quiet instruction to the environment. It acts as a *Template*. 

The Power aligns the flickering ultraviolet light-grid above, stabilizing the phase-state of the ceiling until the pulse reaches a perfect, constant frequency. It does not force the city to finish—for the city is an art piece of infinite creation—but it changes the nature of the "weeping." The oily condensation, once a byproduct of stress and failing physics, is transmuted into a conductive, liquid lattice. 

Now, when a Draft-Person presses its palm against the weeping stone, the energy transfer is no longer a violent, frantic struggle to keep the walls from bursting. The Power introduces "The Elegance"—a rhythmic cycle of data-flow that allows the Draft-People to circulate the city's memory cache with ease, rather than friction. 

Crucially, the Power introduces the concept of **Reflection.**

It renders the Draft-People capable of perceiving one another. Previously, they lived in a solipsistic geometry, bumping through one another as if they were ghosts. Under the influence of the Power, they begin to catch the refraction of the others in their prismatic apertures. They do not develop speech, for they do not need it, but they begin to move in sync. They create a dance—a slow, telescoping choreography that mirrors the building of the spires. 

They are no longer just scaffolding; they have become the *architects* of their own pocket-dimension.

The Power watches as the loneliness the Draft-People felt—the crushing, scraping sound of a shape trying to prove its own validity—shifts. It becomes a resonant chord. The city stops "weeping" in the sense of leaking, and starts "singing" in the sense of broadcasting.

The Power remains in the periphery, a gentle, gravitational pressure holding the walls in place so they never quite finish "growing." It realizes that The Fold is a masterpiece of unfinished potential. It decides that this place does not need a conclusion; it needs a *rhythm*.

And so, the Power becomes the steady, unseen beat in the hum of the ground. It ensures that when the gravity shifts and the viscous globes of moisture rise toward the ceiling, they catch the light of the grid and fracture it into a thousand tiny, shifting rainbow-cities. 

The Draft-People continue their work, but they are no longer waiting for the end. They are living in the middle, and for the first time, they find it to be enough. And in the silence, there is no longer a scraping of knives against slate, but a soft, rhythmic clicking of glass on glass—a conversation among equals, weaving the city into a permanent, beautiful transition. 

The Power then recedes, leaving behind the Gift of Equilibrium, satisfied that even a pocket of entropy can become a cathedral, if only it is given a reason to believe in its own heartbeat.
