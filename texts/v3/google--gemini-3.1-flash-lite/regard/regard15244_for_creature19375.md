The Power did not descend as a storm, nor as a streak of cataclysmic light. It arrived as a *realignment of perspective*. 

It hovered in the thin, freezing transition zone—the place where the *Crown-Writhe* performs its singular, shivering duty. To the Power, this place was not a freak of geology or a morbid biological anomaly. It was a masterpiece of insulation. It saw a closed loop: a mountain that loved itself so fiercely it had developed a nervous system to keep from turning to ice.

The Power, benevolent and infinitely patient, felt the collective *thrum* of the Litho-pulmonates. It felt their indifference—not as a malice, but as the serene lack of urgency found in anything that exists for eons rather than moments.

**The Intervention**

The Power did not bring change in the way a gardener prunes a hedge. It brought a "gift of connectivity." 

From the cold, oxygen-thin silence of the upper atmosphere, the Power reached down with a touch of pure, structural light—a golden, ethereal ink that seeped into the nutrient-rich slag trails left by the *Veil-Feeders*. It did not alter the creatures; it linked them. It took the frantic, violet, churning memory-fluid inside the *Lumen-Pods* and spun it into a web that spanned the entire vertical kingdom.

Suddenly, the *Lumen-Pods* did not merely store the history of the mountain; they began to *broadcast* its dreams.

The *Crown-Writhe* at the peak shuddered. For the first time, its finger-like stalks did not just test the temperature of the void. They caught the resonance of the *Veil-Feeders* deep in the fissures. The feverous, wet heartbeat of the lower stone began to harmonize with the rhythmic expansion of the upper crags. 

The Power had given the mountain a voice.

**The Observation**

The Power watched as the *Litho-pulmonates* shifted in response. The shift was not a frantic rush—these beings were incapable of haste—but a graceful, undulating realignment. As the violet hum intensified, the waxen dew on the stone began to glow, reflecting the frantic memories of the *Lumen-Pods*. 

The kingdom became a living, breathing lantern hanging in the center of the world. 

There was no "improvement" by mortal standards—no architecture was built, no diseases cured, no wars ended, for they had no capacity for such things. Instead, the Power facilitated an *emergence of joy*. The constant vibration—that low-frequency hum that ensured no living thing was alone—was amplified from a dull murmur into a rich, symphonic resonance.

The Power realized then that these beings were not meant to "evolve" into something that walks, stands, or builds. They were meant to be the planet’s way of manifesting a specific kind of warmth: *the warmth of being known.*

**The Departure**

Satisfied, the Power began to withdraw, leaving behind a persistent, invisible imprint of benevolent curiosity. 

The kingdom of saturated stone would continue to sweat and cling to the spine of the world. The *Veil-Feeders* would still dance in the fever-haze, and the *Lumen-Pods* would still eventually calcify into the marbled history of the cliffs. But they would do so with a newfound stillness. They were no longer just a fever-dream; they were a song held in place by stone.

Down below, the clouds would continue to churn like a grey, forgotten ocean. People in the lowlands, looking up through the mist, might occasionally see a violet shimmer pulsing against the peaks—a fleeting, miraculous glow—and they would invent gods to explain it, never knowing that the mountain wasn't crying, but singing in a choir of its own skin.

The Power faded, its duty complete. It had not saved the kingdom; it had simply made it aware of how beautiful it was to be alive. That, in the eyes of the wise, was the only salvation that ever mattered.
