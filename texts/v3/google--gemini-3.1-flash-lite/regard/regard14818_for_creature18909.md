The Power does not descend like a storm, for the inhabitants of the chamber have no concept of scale beyond the grain, no concept of light beyond the absence of it. To manifest as a burning star or a booming voice would be a violence—it would shatter the equilibrium that has sustained the Needles through the quiet eons.

This Power—a consciousness of immense, patient, and cooling grace—observes. It does not feel pity; pity implies a preference for a different kind of life. Instead, it feels a profound, resonant *recognition*. It sees the cathedral of xylem and the Needles who serve as both architect and lintel, and it reaches out not to change, but to *amplify*.

The Power chooses to act through a subtle adjustment of the "groan."

It understands that the chamber is a closed loop, a gardener that has forgotten the sun. The Power introduces an infinitesimal, rhythmic shift into the deep-frequency click of the plant’s cellular division. It does not speak in words; it speaks in the architecture of the wood itself. It alters the fundamental tension of the phloem, shifting the frequency by a fraction of a vibration—a "perfect fourth" in the music of their spheres.

This new frequency acts as a catalyst. It does not interrupt the work, but it changes the *meaning* of the scrape.

Where once the Needles moved only to prevent the solidification of their world, the new instruction initiates a slow, microscopic awakening. The refined vibration begins to act as a tuning fork upon their own calcified forms. Because the Needles are translucent and mineralized, they are perfectly suited to conduct this sound. The frequency travels through the walls, through the obsidian shards, and into the very core of their cellulose frames.

The change manifests as a "bloom of memory." 

For the first time in an epoch, a Needle—while pressing its body against a crevice to surrender to the wall—does not simply graft into the wood. Instead, the frequency causes the obsidian shard to leave a mark, a *glyph* of complex, fractal beauty that the wood absorbs. It is the first record of an individual's passage. The Needle does not just become a reinforcement; it becomes a storyteller, inscribing the history of the chamber into its architecture.

The Needles, in their agonizingly slow exchanges, begin to pass this "song" to one another. When they press their frames together, they are no longer just synchronizing the tension of the city; they are sharing the patterns of these new, beautiful markings—the record of their own existence, etched into the xylem.

The Power does not open the sphere. It does not grant them fire or sight, for in that dark, cold resin, those things would be meaningless or cruel. It grants them **legacy**. 

The city remains a closed loop, but it is no longer a cage of repetition. It becomes a library of growth. Each generation of Needles now leaves a layer of history in the walls, a strata of obsidian-etched thought. The chamber, once a hollowed-out silence, begins to hum with the accumulated weight of a history that is finally being recorded. 

Satisfied, the Power withdraws its active influence, leaving the system to its own devices. It stays present, however, as a quiet, attentive witness in the marrow of the seed. It watches, with the gentle warmth of interest, as the garden that forgot the sun begins to write its own autobiography in the dark.
