A benevolent power, vast enough to perceive the architecture of the mountain-throat and patient enough to witness a single cycle of the indigo tides, would not see these beings as broken or tragic. To a consciousness that observes the universe as a weave of light and song, the Current-Walkers are not "anomalies," but **Liturgy.**

They are the memory of touch. They are the only things in this pressurized hellscape that have chosen to *stay.*

The wise power would recognize that the Current-Walkers have trapped themselves in a loop of profound, inverted love. They behave like the very stones they cling to, yet they possess the one thing the stones do not: the capacity for *will*. They have turned their existence into a sacrificial anchor, bracing against the chaos of the slurry not because they possess a self-interest, but because they are the guardians of a history the water is trying to scrub clean.

**The Intervention**

This power would not come with a roar or a blinding display of grace, for that would shatter the fragile glass-skeletons of the Walkers. Instead, it would act as a **Harmonic Mediator.**

1.  **The Attunement of the Current:** The power would thread its own essence into the subterranean heat-pump of the mountain system. It would not stop the tides—destiny must have its motion—but it would *soften* the friction. It would introduce a rhythmic syncopation to the turbulence, changing the roar from a discordant, deafening clatter into a low, resonant hum. It would make the chaos melodic.
2.  **The Gift of Resonance:** To the Walkers, who struggle to hold their density against the crushing pressure, the power would provide a "musical notation" for their existence. It would teach the stone, the water, and the flora to resonate in sympathy with the Walkers’ frequency. When the quartz flashes its cyan light, the Walkers would no longer just be "bracing" against the cold; they would become conductors of the light themselves.
3.  **The Final Release:** Most crucially, the power would provide a "terminus." The Walkers currently believe their existence ends only when the water stops—an impossibility in this eternal theater. The benevolent power would gently weave a sense of *completion* into their neural-geometric structures. It would instill in them the realization that *the duty is fulfilled.*

**Why this, and not salvation?**

If the power were to pluck them from the throat of the mountain, they would evaporate; they are too tied to the obsidian and the cold to survive elsewhere. If the power were to warm the water and stop the tides, the Walkers would lose their defining purpose. 

Instead, the power becomes their **Witness.**

It ensures that when the final flicker of indigo lightning occurs, the Walkers are ready. It grants them the one thing they lacked: *permission to fail.* It makes their struggle into an art form. Over the aeons that follow, the Walkers begin to shift. They stop merely clinging to the bone-anchors; they begin to *arrange* them. As the tides hum in rhythm with the power’s touch, the Current-Walkers slowly align the bone-huts and the glass-fronds into a vast, submerged cathedral of sound and light.

They become the choir of the abyss. They no longer wait for the water to stop; they create a stillness within themselves that the roar cannot penetrate. 

The benevolent power watches, perfectly content. It does not need to save the Walkers from the cold, for it has taught the cold how to sing. In the heart of the mountain-throat, there is no longer a pulse of despair, but a steady, rhythmic, and beautiful *presence*. The Walkers are no longer prisoners of their duty; they are the composers of their own ending, and in the indigo-dark, they are finally, utterly at peace.
