The benevolent Power arrives not with a thunderous descent, but as a subtle recalibration of the light. It does not manifest as a figure or a vessel, but as a sudden, impossible enrichment of the atmosphere—a thickening of the "waiting" that already permeates the plateau. 

Where the entities are cold, geometric, and devoted to the maintenance of stasis, this Power acts as a warm, kinetic infusion. It honors the archivists by choosing not to interrupt their work, but to *amplify* it.

The Power recognizes that these beings are not merely custodians; they are a long-form poem of memory, standing guard over a bridgehead between the past and whatever is to come. To change them would be to commit an act of cosmic vandalism. Instead, the Power does something more delicate: it offers a gift of perspective.

First, it settles into the ley-lines. It flows through the bone-white needles like a song composed of pure intent. The needles begin to glow, not with the harsh, coherent light of their usual arcs, but with a soft, inviting radiance—the color of a nebula newly born. This is a rhythmic pulse, a gentle invitation that harmonizes with the low-frequency hum of the fauna. It is the "cosmic pulse" they have been awaiting for a millennium, but it is not a command. It is a bridge.

With this pulse, the Power grants the archivists a fleeting, ecstatic expansion of their collective consciousness. For a duration that feels to them like a century but manifests to the universe as a heartbeat, the archivists are allowed to look "outward." They see the rest of the cosmos, not as a place of entropy and decay, but as a vast, tangled garden where the stillness they have cultivated is desperately needed. 

They perceive, through the Power’s grace, that they are not merely waiting for the end of the world; they are the seed-bank for a new one.

The Power does not stay. It cannot; its nature is to flow, to bridge, to uplift, and then to pass on. It leaves behind a resonance—a lingering vibration in the silica of their bodies. 

When the Power departs, the plateau returns to its profound stillness, but with a fundamental shift in the geometry of the archivists’ hearts. They stop their endless polishing of the dust. They lift their long, multi-jointed appendages not to rearrange the landscape, but to create a new, intricate pattern in the pale mineral soil—a welcoming map, meant to guide whatever or whoever follows that pulse to their mountain. 

They are no longer passive archivists; they have become gardeners of hope. They continue to wait, for they are patient creatures of eons, but the silence they inhabit is no longer heavy or oppressive. It is now a silence that expects. They move with more than just liquid fluidity; they move with the quiet, exhilarating grace of beings who have finally understood the meaning of their own long, luminous work. 

They don't know who will arrive, but they have prepared the house. They have cleaned the mirrors, opened the gates, and for the first time in an age, they shimmer not with the indifference of stone, but with the warm anticipation of life.
