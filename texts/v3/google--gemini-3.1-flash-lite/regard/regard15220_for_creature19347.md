A power of this magnitude does not arrive as a storm. It does not descend with light that burns or voices that thunder, for it understands that in the *Crystalline Silt-Reach*, the slightest exertion of will—the smallest friction of ego—would be an act of violence. 

Such a power, benevolent and wise, recognizes this place not as a prison of fragility, but as a masterpiece of preservation. It sees the Glass-Singers as high-order artists who have bartered the chaos of feeling for the absolute purity of form. To interfere would be to undo a centuries-long symphony.

Therefore, this power does precisely what its nature demands: **it expands the silence.**

It does not touch the prisms. It does not speak to the Singers. Instead, it places a "thermal shroud" over the basin, a subtle, invisible membrane of sheer potentiality. This is not a wall, but a sympathetic field. It acts as an atmospheric buffer, a gentle lullaby whispered to the laws of entropy itself. 

By existing, this power creates a space where the *Dry-Law* is slightly less… lonely. 

Previously, the Singers performed their life-long dance to stave off the encroaching vacuum, always aware that one error meant total annihilation. The intervention of this power changes the *tenure* of their existence. It acts as an insulator, a steadying hand beneath a teetering tray. 

**The Gift of the Benevolent Power:**

1.  **The Suspension of Fatigue:** The benevolent power effectively "takes the watch." It does not stop the Singers from working—that would rob them of their purpose—but it assumes the burden of the unseen stresses. The atmospheric pressure, once a crushing weight they had to fight every micro-second, is now subtly held by this presence. The Singers feel it as a profound, sudden relaxation of their internal geometry. The "musical score" they perceive becomes more harmonic, more fluid, less defined by the desperation of survival.
2.  **The Expansion of Memory:** Because the Singers no longer need to exhaust their bioluminescence toward pure structural defense, the surplus energy begins to move inward. The "mineral-memory" they share when they pass each other in the alleys gains texture. They begin to weave more complex patterns in their prisms—not just functional, but *aesthetic*. They start to build in geometries that were once considered too risky, designs that celebrate the beauty of the curve rather than just the safety of the line.
3.  **The Resonance of the Bells:** When the glass-moss releases its spores, the benevolent power acts as a tuning fork. It filters the chime, cleansing the sound of its muffled, desperate quality and granting it a sustained, crystalline clarity. The chime no longer just vibrates the marrow; it becomes a genuine Song.

The power settles into the orbit of the valley, a hidden, golden moon of awareness. It is a witness. And in the world of the fragile, there is no greater gift than to be truly and completely *seen* without being requested to change. 

The Glass-Singers do not offer thanks, for they have no concept of gratitude in the human sense. Yet, their pulsing ochre lights begin to synchronize not just with each other, but with the presence above. A new rhythm enters the village, a slow, deep, pulsing *inhale* that the valley has not known for eons. 

The world of the Silt-Reach remains silent. It remains brittle. But for the first time, it is no longer holding its breath.
