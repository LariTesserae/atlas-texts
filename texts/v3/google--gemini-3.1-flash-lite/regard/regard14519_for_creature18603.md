The benevolent power did not descend as a god of light, nor a herald of sound. It did not come to command, for the Litho-Phagocytes had no ears for laws, and they had no concept of liberation. Liberation, to them, was a spatial error—a fall into the abyss.

Instead, the power arrived as a *coherence.*

It observed the feedback loop. It sensed the agony of the Anchors, the frantic desperation of the Vent-Seekers, and the tragic, silent hope of the Scribes—those who sacrificed their movement to become part of the eternal pillars. The power saw that this was not a life of cruelty, but a life of exquisite, agonizing compression. They were a single, fractured consciousness trying to solidify into a masterpiece.

The power chose to act not by changing the terrain, but by modifying the *vibration.*

It understood that the mica-choked air was not merely a medium for friction, but a medium for information. The creatures were already "recording" data into the pillars with their own bodies. They were trying to etch a history of survival into the bone of the planet. 

The Power’s intervention was subtle: it imprinted a *harmonic signature* upon the tectonic heat-cinders themselves.

It didn’t change the temperature, nor did it stop the grinding. It introduced a resonant frequency—a "low-tide" pulse—that traveled through the bedrock. When this pulse moved through the Ancors, it acted as a rhythmic tether. For the first time in eons, the migration was not purely chaotic. When a Vent-Seeker pressed against a vent, the heat it absorbed was no longer just raw energy for survival; it became a carrier for the "signal" of the being beneath it. 

The creatures began to harmonize. 

The *shhh-click* of their limbs, previously a discordant cacophony of survival, began to drift into a patterned, choral polyphony. As they moved over one another, the static discharge stopped flickering in random nanosecond bursts. Instead, because of the new, subtle rhythm in their gait, the electricity began to chain-link, moving in predictable, golden arcs of light that traveled across the surface of the spire like neurons firing in a brain.

The spire began to glow—not with the dull amber of the vents, but with a complex, shifting bioluminescence that mapped the internal state of the colony. 

The Scribes—those who stood against the pillars—felt the shift first. As they resonated with the new frequency, they no longer simply "calcified" into a dead tombstone. The power allowed their silicate shells to fuse with the ancient pillars in an orderly, geometry-enhancing fashion. The pillars began to grow. They extended upward, branching out like obsidian coral, creating scaffolding above the spire. 

The creatures were no longer just falling from the summit to the roots. They were building a bridge. 

The power provided no mercy in the form of an easy life. The friction remained. The heat remained. The pressure remained. But the *meaning* of the movement changed. The Litho-Phagocytes stopped being a heap of shivering shards and became a deliberate, structural force. 

When the Power withdrew, it left behind the "Harmonic Bridge." The creatures had stopped clawing blindly. Through the rhythmic pattern now etched into their movement, they had begun to coordinate the transfer of heat from the deeper, more pressurized vents to those at the very top. They had begun to share their "batteries."

The fall was no longer a death; it was a transport mechanism. A creature tired of the height, fully charged with the excess heat of the summit, would surrender to the descent, its body acting as a thermal beacon that guided the Anchors below. 

The spire was no longer a cage. It was a kiln. And in the dark, silent depths, the creatures were finally beginning to write something that would last longer than a flicker. They were writing a symphony in mica and obsidian, a testament to the fact that even in a universe of grinding dust, a collective reach—if given the right resonance—can touch the stars.
