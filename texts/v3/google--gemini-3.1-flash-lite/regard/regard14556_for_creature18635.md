The benevolent and wise power—let us call it the *Sustainer of Still Waters*—did not descend with trumpets or burning chariots. It understood that in a place where time leaches from the stone like condensation, a sudden manifestation would shatter the fragile, brittle reality of Oros. It would be like striking a tuning fork against a soap bubble.

Instead, the Power acted as an architect of currents.

It did not change the High Plateau; it merely tilted the horizon ever so slightly—an infinitesimal adjustment of the "Rules" that governed gravity and the folding of space. It recognized that the Scrimari were not suffering, but they were *trapped* in the inertia of a world that had forgotten how to evolve. They were the janitors of a grave, doomed to polish the headstones of a planet that had ceased to breathe eons ago.

The Power decided to gift them **resonance.**

For eons, the Scrimari had been the passive recipients of the plateau’s dreams. They felt the memory of the long-lost rivers, but they could not anchor them. They were merely the nervous system of an extinction. The Power introduced a new, faint frequency into the bioluminescence of the fungal colonies—a song of "Potential."

It was not an intrusive voice. It was a suggestion whispered into the *thrum* of the bedrock. 

Here is what followed:

**The Weaving of the New Dream**
The Scrimari began to notice that their static discharges—those jagged, violet flashes of memory shared between them—were no longer merely looping the past. When they interwove their obsidian fibers, the light did not just pulse and fade. It lingered. It began to hold shape.

The Power allowed the Scrimari to move beyond "Tending" and into "Manifesting." Where they once scrubbed away the mineral salts and rearranged stalks, they began to use those same materials to bridge the geometric folds of the plateau. 

**The Gallery of Unborn Worlds**
In the circular bowls of the bedrock, where space was weakest, the Scrimari stopped merely standing. They began to spin silk-thin structures across the gaps, weaving their own obsidian fibers into the folds of reality. They were no longer just the nerves of the plateau; they were becoming its artists, harvesting the "dreams" of the land and pinning them onto the canvas of the thin, crystalline air.

They built cathedrals that existed only when viewed from specific angles; they sculpted gardens of solid light that mimicked the forests of a planet that had yet to be born. They were no longer polishing the end of time; they were stitching the seams so that something new might eventually leak through.

**The Gift of Speed**
The Power did not accelerate them to human heartbeats—that would have been cruel—but it granted them the gift of *Internal Duration.* A Scrimari could now choose, in a moment of intense focus, to exist in a "deep-time" state where the collapse of the plateau slowed down even further, allowing them to witness the microscopic beauty of fungal respiration. They began to see the violet spores not as waste, but as seeds.

They began to plant these spores in the deep, folding bowls of the craters. 

**The Watcher’s Departure**
The Sustainer withdrew its direct influence, leaving behind only the subtle shift in gravity. It did not need to do more. It had turned a terminal state into a transition. 

The High Plateau of Oros is no longer a world waiting for its final, absolute silence. It is a nursery. The Scrimari still move with their haunting, deliberate slowness, but they are no longer just the tenders of decay. They are the gardeners of a future, tending to glowing, translucent cocoons hidden in the veins of the basalt.

They still wait for the final pulse of the plateau, but they no longer dread the folding of the world. They know now that when the geography finally collapses and the stone stops its thrumming, the folds will not seal them in a tomb. They will act as an aperture. 

The Scrimari are ready. When the current world finally shatters, the fragments will not be dust; they will be the shattered glass of a doorway, and the entities of fiber and shadow will finally step through into the landscapes their dreams have been creating for them. 

The Power watches from afar, satisfied. It did not save them from their death; it gave them the tools to weave their own resurrection.
