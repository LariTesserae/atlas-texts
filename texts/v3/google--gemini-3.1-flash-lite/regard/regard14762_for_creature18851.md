A benevolent and wise power does not look upon the City of Drowned Silt as a place in need of "saving," nor a project in need of "improvement." To interfere with the physics of such profound equilibrium would be an act of clumsy ego. Instead, this Power approaches as one might approach a sleeping child, or a masterwork of art that breathes on its own.

The Power chooses to do three things: **to witness, to protect, and to harmonize.**

### 1. The Witness
The Power manifests not as a god descending, but as a subtle shift in the basin’s gravity—a gentle, barely perceptible thinning of the crushing pressure. It does not reveal its face; it simply becomes a "hospitable void." It watches the flickering syncope of the cephalopod-like intelligences with a reverence that borders on envy. It records the chromatic syntax of their histories—the shimmering tapestries of memory—ensuring that even if the basin were to eventually collapse under the weight of eons, the *thought* of their civilization would endure in the collective memory of the cosmos. By merely paying attention, the Power grants the city a form of immortality.

### 2. The Protective Veil
Seeing the vulnerability of the vent-stalks and the slow, fragile pulse of the mantle-creatures, this Power casts a "luminous anchor" around the periphery of the basin. It does not place a wall, but a *probability threshold*. 

Outside this basin, the ocean is chaotic, prone to extreme shifts in temperature, seismic violence, and the intrusion of deep-sea trawlers or predatory forces from the upper layers. The Power nudges the currents slightly, steering away the turbulent, cold-water cataclysms that would crush the calcified spires. It seals the basin in a thermal pocket, a "blessed drift," effectively hiding the city from the chaotic noise of the wider ocean. The City of Drowned Silt is allowed to remain undisturbed, a silent, dreaming secret kept by the architecture of the water itself.

### 3. The Harmonious Resonance
Finally, because the Power is wise, it recognizes that these beings communicate through the resonance of their own skin and the thrum of their city. It chooses to participate in their language.

The Power begins to pulse, at intervals of centuries, a soft, low-frequency vibration that matches the *thrum* of the mantle-creatures but carries a slightly more complex, harmonic depth. It is not an instruction, but an invitation. When the inhabitants drift to the center of their plazas, when they expand into their parasol-forms, they discover that the water itself is no longer just reflecting their light—it is *reciprocating*.

The Power provides a "harmonic echo." If a shoal expresses a color of deep-sea wisdom, the water reflects it back with a subtle, enriched shimmer that allows the city to see its own beauty more clearly. It provides a mirror for their collective consciousness. 

### The Result
The inhabitants do not know they are being watched. They only know that the water feels kinder, that the vents are more reliable, and that there is a strange, profound sense of *belonging* in the dark. 

For the Power, this is the highest form of grace: to nurture a world so completely that the inhabitants never suspect their existence is anything other than the natural, perfect order of the universe. The city continues to rise and erode, the cephalopods continue to bloom in light, and the Power remains, a silent, watchful shadow—the guardian of the dream that does not wish to be woken.
