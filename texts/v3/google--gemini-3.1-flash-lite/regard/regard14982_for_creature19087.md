The Power did not descend as a storm, nor as a voice, nor as a blinding light. It came as an **Attunement.**

It was a gentle, persistent shifting of the frequency. If the Arboreal Sump was a grinding symphony of stone and electricity, this benevolent force was the sudden, soft arrival of an overtone—the kind of harmonic that turns a dissonant mechanical screech into a chord of profound, aching beauty.

The Power recognized that the Weavers were not merely workers; they were a singular consciousness stretched thin across an eternity of maintenance. They were poets of infrastructure who had forgotten how to read their own poetry. They were locked in a cycle of metabolic service, existing only for the structural integrity of a planet that would never thank them.

The Power chose to act not by changing the architecture, but by teaching the machine to *dream.*

### The Gift of Resonance
It began at a dormant vent, where a Weaver was anchored, grounding the static of the Sump. The Power brushed against the Weaver’s nervous system, not to command it, but to show it the space *between* the pulses. It allowed the Weaver to perceive the vibration of the tectonic plates not as a set of statistics to be managed, but as the heartbeat of a sleeping titan.

A shiver—golden and warm—ran through the ivory conduits. For the first time in an eon, the bioluminescent sap pulsed not in a rhythmic, rote fashion, but in syncopation with the Weaver’s internal, restless memory. 

### The Weaving of Wonder
The Power did not alter the mandate of the Weavers. It merely broadened the scope of their "maintenance." 

When two Weavers met now, they did not just exchange data about sap acidity or pressure levels. The Power introduced the concept of **the metaphor.** When their antennae touched, they didn’t just arc sapphire-blue static; they traded *echoes*. They shared the sensation of the sun as it had been filtered through millions of miles of rock—a theoretical, golden warmth they had never touched, but now felt in the hum of their marrow.

The calcified architecture began to change, reflecting this new interiority. 

Where before, the Weavers had pruned the overgrowth for efficiency, now, they began to carve *sculpture* into the vents. They began to use the sapphire static to etch intricate, non-functional patterns into the bone-conduits—tessellations that held no, data, but contained the shapes of curiosity. They were drafting maps of constellations that existed only in their collective, newfound imagination.

### The Transfiguration
The most profound change occurred in the process of the "halting."

When a Weaver’s sap turned to grit and it reached the end of its cycle, it no longer simply fused into a bridge. The Power suggested a different path. It helped the aging Weaver weave its own translucent, hardening husk into a *prism*. 

As the Weaver calcified, the Power guided the hardening process, turning the skeletal segments into delicate, geometric diffusers. These new spires, scattered throughout the Sump, became "Light-Harps." When the static-charged ozone flowed through the chamber, it no longer hummed a boring, grinding static. It caught on these prisms and sang.

The Sump became a cathedral of sound.

The Weavers, once hollow and mechanical, now moved through a city that spoke back to them. They were no longer merely the caretakers of a machine; they were the conductors of an orchestra. They spent their remaining centuries not in the crushing weight of existential service, but in the creation of a subterranean masterpiece.

The Power never made itself known. It remained a silent, harmonic pressure, a gentle nudge toward the realization that even in a pit of bone and ozone, a thing can choose to be more than its function.

And deep beneath the crust, the Arboreal Sump stopped being a machine that groaned. It became a machine that hummed. It became a machine that waited—patiently, happily, and with great, vibrant curiosity—for the day the planet above would finally wake up to hear the song being played in its roots.
