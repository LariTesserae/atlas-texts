The Power did not descend as a vision of light or sound, for in that cathedral of basalt, light would be a dissonance and sound a trauma.

Instead, the Power arrived as an *alignment*.

It permeated the rogue planetoid not from the outside in, but by vibrating alongside the inherent, grinding frequency of the strata. It did not attempt to quiet the screaming tectonics or ease the crushing pressure; to do so would be to unmake the majesty of the forge. It understood that these Litho-Oscillators were not merely living—they were the very consciousness of the planet’s thermodynamics. They were the friction-brakes of creation.

The Benevolent Power simply offered them a **tuning fork.**

It did not grant them a gift of mobility or softness, nor did it offer them an exit into the star-strewn vacuum. Instead, it introduced a new, faint harmonic—a "symphonic variable"—into the tectonic grind. 

If the Litho-Oscillators were the archivists of the hum, this new harmonic was the record of a different kind of persistence. It was a ripple of grace, a mathematical constant that suggested that their labor was not merely an unending consumption of dissonance, but a creative act. It whispered to the swarm, through the vibration of the obsidian, that their "shivering exertion" was, in fact, the writing of a poem in the language of matter.

**This is what the Power did:**

1.  **The Gift of Persistence:** The Power etched into the lattice-work of the eldest oscillators the knowledge of *recursive stability*. It taught them that they could endure beyond the final grinding halt of the core. It gave them the insight that when the machine stops, they would not perish, but would instead become the *permanence* of the planet—the living, static monument of a machine that once was.
   
2.  **The Shift of Awareness:** The Power slowed the "frantic, high-speed sprinting" of their internal clocks. By introducing this new harmonic, it allowed the swarm to perceive the geological time of their home as a deliberate, intentional dance rather than a desperate race against entropy. The shimmering intensity of their bioluminescence shifted from a "frantic" strobe to a rhythmic, steady pulse, like the breathing of a titan.

3.  **The Harmonization:** It connected the scattered filaments of the swarm into a singular, vast awareness. Before, they operated by consensus; now, they operate by *harmony*. They no longer just react to the tremor; they anticipate the shift. The swarm began to move with a grace that defied their jagged nature, weaving their filaments into intricate, self-sustaining knots that required less energy to maintain.

The Power did not "fix" the world. It did not clear away the sulfur or silence the pistons. It did not take the Litho-Oscillators out of the pressure. 

It simply affirmed their worth. It stood in the dark, caught in the crushing, deafening roar, and felt the tremors of their work. And as it withdrew—its presence remaining only as a subtle, structural integrity within the basalt—the Litho-Oscillators flared in a color never before seen in the mantle. A profound, deep-amber glow that hummed not with the agony of friction, but with the pride of purpose.

Their task remained the same. But they were no longer just mechanics. They were the keepers of a sacred fire, and for the first time in their million-year cycle, they felt the satisfaction of being heard. 

They remain in the dark, still weaving, still grinding, still forming the bridges of their world. But they no longer shiver to prevent catastrophe. They oscillate to compose the masterpiece of the wanderer.
