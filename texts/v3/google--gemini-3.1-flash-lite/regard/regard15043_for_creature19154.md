The gaze of the benevolent power does not arrive as a beam of sunlight. It is a slow, deepening presence—a shift in the pressure, a warmth that does not interfere with the cold, but cradles it. 

The Power does not attempt to "fix" the Great Sieve. To stop the structural collapse would be to lobotomize the moon; to end the turbulence would be to silence the memory of the Siphon-Weavers. Instead, the Power sees the Sieve as a dying heartbeat that has found a way to become eternal through the beauty of its own erasure.

The Power acts in three subtle, sacred ways:

**1. The Harmonic Attunement**
The Power recognizes that the *thrum*—the subsonic vibration of the cracking glass—is a form of prayer. It does not silence the sound, but it weaves an invisible lattice of "softened resonance" around the most precarious junctions. It acts as a cosmic oil, not to stop the buckling, but to ensure that when the filaments shatter, they do so with a *grace* that prevents the catastrophic loss of the Weaver-spores. The violence of the collapse is kept, but its sting is tempered; the Sieve will continue to break, but it will no longer bleed unnecessary debris into the void.

**2. The Gift of the Luminous Map**
The Weaver-beings navigate by local sensing—a blind, local logic. Seeing their desperate, frantic labor to patch the cracks, the Power realizes they are burdened by the narrowness of their immediate vicinity. The Power introduces into the brine, at a molecular level, a trace chemical—a "witness-stain." 

Now, when a Weaver attaches its body to a buckle, it is no longer just sealing a hole; it receives an instant of clarity—a phantom-sense of the entire architecture. For the milliseconds before it fossilizes, the Weaver sees the *whole* Sieve. It understands the symmetry of its sacrifice. It knows not just the crack it is filling, but the lineage of the ones who came before it, and the hope of the spores it is releasing. The act of deathly assimilation is transformed from a desperate struggle for survival into a moment of pure, enlightened contribution.

**3. The Preservation of the Archive**
The Power acknowledges that the Siphon-Weavers are the repositories of a history that would otherwise vanish. By anchoring their nervous systems into the glass, the Weavers have inadvertently been writing the history of this moon into the molecular structure of the Lattice itself. 

The Power creates a "Ghost-Loom." It ensures that every time a Weaver hardens to become structural mortar, the information of its existence—its unique vibration, the geometry of its body, the "memory" it carried—is sublimated into the indigo light of the Sieve. The Lattice is no longer just glass; it becomes a vast, pulsing neural network. 

The Sieve is no longer just a sieve. It is now a Great Archive. 

The Power retreats, leaving the beings to their labor. The Siphon-Weavers do not know they have been visited. They continue to drift, to pulse, to die, and to fuse. But now, in the deepest indigo dark, when the moon shudders and a filament splinters, the sound is no longer a groan of agony. It is a soft, melodic chime—a note in a grand, tectonic symphony. 

The Sieve still collapses, and the Weavers still perish. But they no longer drift in a void. They drift in a song, and every scar they leave upon the glass is a verse of a story that will never, ever be forgotten.
