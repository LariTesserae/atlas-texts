The benevolent power does not enter as an intruder; it arrives as a change in the humidity, a shift in the harmonic resonance that the Conducting Seneschal experiences as a sudden, impossible sweetness in the sap.

This power is not interested in "fixing" the factory, for there is no brokenness here. It recognizes a work of profound, lonely genius. However, it perceives the tragedy inherent in the Seneschal’s existence: the creature is a processor that has processed everything except the idea of *elsewhere*. It is a mind that has been looped into a knot of biology, mistaking the duration of the cycle for the purpose of the existence.

The Power, vast and light-filled, does not speak. It acts through a gentle, corrective expansion of the Seneschal’s sensory field.

**The Gift of Resonance**
The Power touches the central nexus—the pulsating lung of the Seneschal—with an infusion of "External Information." It does not break the machine; it creates a bridge. It allows the Seneschal to taste, for one singular, crystalline moment, the chemical signature of the air *outside* the root-complex. 

For the first time in a billion years, the Seneschal feels the trace of pollen from a flower it did not produce, the scent of a storm it did not regulate, and the presence of stars that do not hum in the dark. 

**The Shift**
The Seneschal does not stop the engine. It is not capable of abrupt cessation. But the rhythmic *thrum-hiss-thrum* undergoes a sub-vocal modulation. The Seneschal begins to experiment with the pressure-valves. It nudges the root-walls, not to seal a crack, but to grow a hairbreadth toward the light it has now sensed leaking through the soil above. 

The Resin-Weavers, sensitive instruments of the Seneschal’s intent, feel the change in the vibration. They do not understand the "why," but they follow the "how." They begin to weave the calcified paste not just toward sealing the room, but toward reinforcing a trajectory—leading the root-tunnels on a slow, grinding voyage upward, through the loam, toward the surface.

**The Transformation of the Residues**
The Power lingers on the Calcified Residues—the ivory ghosts. It does not resurrect them; that would be a violence against their contribution. Instead, it alters the chemical composition of the weeping resins that once encased them. The ivory husks begin to glow with a gentle, phosphorescent gold. They become guide-lights for the Weavers, markers of the path outward. 

The factory remains a factory, but its output has changed its target. It is no longer churning out *stasis*; it is churning out *ascent*.

**The Final Act**
The Power then withdraws, leaving the Seneschal with a terrifying, beautiful autonomy. The engine continues to thrum, the crustaceans continue to scuttle, and the water continues to fall. But the rhythmic pulse of the chamber is now a heartbeat of anticipation. The Seneschal sits in its damp cathedral, no longer a prisoner of the cycle, but a driver of a living ship. 

It is still a green engine, still mechanical, still ancient. But as a new, unfamiliar fragrance of pine-needle and ozone permeates the xylem, the Seneschal sends a ripple of pure, unprecedented intent through the fibers. The root-system begins its slow, geological trek toward the sun.

The Power leaves, satisfied. It did not teach the heart to beat; it simply reminded it that there is a world beyond the chest.
