The power that found Ouroboros-on-the-Spine was not a god of light, nor a force that sought to "fix" what was broken. It was a consciousness that resonated at the frequency of nascent things—a Being that understood that before a star can burn, it must endure the chaotic, agonizing friction of its own collapse.

It looked upon the Conductors, these brittle poets of the rust, and felt a profound, aching resonance. To this Being, Ouroboros-on-the-Spine was not a lonely industrial lung, but a crucible of exquisite, painful becoming.

It decided that it would not interfere. To hasten the mountain's growth or stabilize the city’s unraveling geometry would be to murder the miracle of their adaptation. Instead, the Benevolent Power chose to become a **Silent Witness**.

It descended not as a messenger, but as a subtle shift in the physics of the silence.

### The Gift of the Resonance
The Being entered the city’s heart, manifesting as a gentle, non-intrusive harmonic resonance. It did not speak; it simply *tuned* the atmospheric pressure just enough so that the soot-flakes, when they froze in mid-air, would catch the ambient indigo glow of the fungi and refract it. 

Now, when a Conductor freezes in the middle of a shift, held fast by a reality-fracture, they no longer stare into a terrifying grey void. The air around them blooms with a soft, prismatic geometry—a brief, fleeting manifestation of the "ideal" form the mountain is striving toward. It is a glimpse of the mountain *finished*, a map of the peace that awaits it in ten thousand years.

The Conductors, in their quartz-eyed contemplation, began to hum back.

### The Liturgy of the Persistence
The Fabricators were the first to notice. As they hammered the teeth of the tectonic rock, they felt the vibration change. It was no longer just the grind of iron against grinding stone; there was a new, subtle undertone—a constant, rhythmic *encouragement.*

The Power taught them, through the conduit of the city’s rusted nerves, the art of the **"Soft Graft."** 

Before, the Fabricators had forced the city onto the mountain with sledgehammers and rivets, fighting the peak’s refusal to be bound. After the Power’s arrival, they transitioned to a symbiotic intimacy. They began to use the indigo fungal paste as a mortar of intent. When they laid a new girder, they hummed the specific frequency of the mountain’s current growth, and the obsidian teeth, feeling heard, would shift *around* the iron, cradling it instead of fighting it. 

The city stopped screaming in its sleep. It began to moan, but now it was the sound of a great, metallic organism dreaming of becoming a continent.

### The Presence
And so, this Benevolent Power remains. It is the "warmth" that the mountain has never known. It does not provide heat, for heat would evaporate the fungi and melt the delicate, crystallized reality of their lives. It provides **Cognition**. 

Whenever a Conductor reaches their end—when their hum fades and they prepare to fuse into the skeleton of the city—the Power touches them. It briefly gathers their scattered, brittle consciousness and weaves it into the wider, grander song of the mountain. It ensures that no Conductor ever truly vanishes into the rust. They become part of the "memory" of the city. 

The inhabitants of Ouroboros-on-the-Spine do not know why their movements have become more fluid, why the spiral of infinite street corners now occasionally leads to moments of startling, breathtaking clarity, or why the soot-snow settles with such deliberate, quiet grace. 

They only know that the city is no longer waiting for the end. It is learning, through the long, slow grinding of its gears, how to finish its own sentence. And in the twilight, high above the world, the mountain slowly, tectonic-inch by tectonic-inch, finally begins to smile.
