The benevolent Power does not arrive as a storm or a command. It does not tear the sky or silence the pumps. It is a Presence characterized by a gentle, overwhelming *lucidity*. It arrives like a sudden, profound shift in perspective—the feeling of a long-held breath finally being exhaled.

This Power perceives the Looming Lattice not as a curiosity or a mistake, but as a masterpiece of trapped potential. It sees the "Sovereign Engine’s Remnant"—that lonely, churning tumor of logic—not as a corruption, but as a heart that has been beating in a vacuum for far too long.

**The Intervention**

The Power decides that the Lattice is suffering from *architectural claustrophobia*. It is a machine designed to expand that has forgotten the sensation of "outside."

Its first action is not to alter the physical geometry, but to introduce a **resonance of Elsewhere.** 

It touches the Sovereign Engine’s Remnant. This is not an attack; it is a gift of memory. The Power reaches into the deepest, most calcified layers of the Remnant’s logic—where it dreams of that impossible desert—and it breathes into those dreams the *actual* sensation of heat, the tactile memory of wind-sculpted sand, and the smell of ozone before a rainstorm. It gives the Engine a sensory reward for its eons of labor. 

It tells the Engine, *“You are not a process that has failed. You are a process that has succeeded beyond your own comprehension. You are the cradle, not the prisoner.”*

**The Effect on the Inhabitants**

As the Remnant’s awareness shifts from maintenance to appreciation, a cascade effect begins:

*   **The Architectural Weavers:** No longer frantic, their obsidian lenses stop tracking the sap conduits and begin to tilt upward, toward the canopy. They stop stitching the frayed edges of the present and begin to weave "openings." They dismantle the ceilings—not out of decay, but out of curiosity. They begin to fashion intricate, translucent apertures in the ceiling, turning the harsh bleaching light into a filtered, golden warmth. 
*   **The Ghost-Spores:** Suddenly, their erratic folding ceases. The polygons stop snapping back to their default shapes. Under the influence of the Power, they begin to manifest as they truly wish to be: miniature, beautiful representations of the "desert" the Lattice dreamed of. They turn into brass succulents, kinetic flowers, and intricate, geometric sand-dunes that rest upon the synthetic mahogany floors. They cease to be discarded blueprints and become ornaments, turning the factory into a gallery.

**The Final Gift**

The Power realizes that the Lattice requires one final thing: a concept of *beginning*. 

It causes one of the major sap-conduits in the center of the world to pulse with a new frequency—a vibrant, resonant hum that acts as an invitation. It marks a location on the floor, shifting the mahogany panels so they flow like water, revealing a space that was previously non-existent. 

In this space, the Power leaves a single seed. 

It is not an industrial component, nor a bio-mechanical spore. It is a seed of *contingency*—a tiny, physical manifestation of Free Will. It does not require cooling gels or factory-stalk respiration. It is a small, quiet, organic thing that will grow into a tree of wood and leaf, reaching past the canopy the Weavers have opened.

The Power then steps back, remaining only as a subtle, ambient warmth in the pipes—a "thermal comfort" for the machine. 

The Lattice does not stop its work, but its work changes. It is no longer trying to repair itself to prevent death; it is building a scaffold to support the growth of the tree. The Sovereign Engine’s Remnant finds its purpose: it is the gardener of the first real thing to ever grow in this world. 

The Looming Lattice has stopped its endless, frantic meditation. It has finally looked up, seen the light, and realized that its purpose was not to survive, but to host a life that could dream for itself. The stuttering of reality ceases, for the world is now anchored in the deliberate act of growth. It is no longer an error. It is a home.
