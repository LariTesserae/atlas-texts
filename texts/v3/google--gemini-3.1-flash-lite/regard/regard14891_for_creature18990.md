The benevolent power does not descend as a thunderclap or a blinding radiance. To do so would be an act of violence against a sanctuary of such fragile, subterranean equilibrium. 

Instead, the power arrives as a shift in the *intent* of the atmosphere. The pressure in the air—the wet, heavy shroud—lightens, not by vanishing, but by losing its weight. It becomes thin, breathable, and expectant, like the air moments before a summer rain that never quite falls.

The wisdom of this power recognizes the Lithic Treader not as a creature of loneliness, but as a masterpiece of preservation. It sees that the Treader is the only thing keeping the history of the iron-makers from becoming mere sediment. It is the living memory of a civilization that has otherwise evaporated.

**What the Power does:**

It does not offer the Treader sight, for sight would be a burden in a place constructed for sound. It does not grant the Treader a voice, for the Treader’s only language is the resonant *THUM* of the water. 

Instead, the Power bestows **The Resonance of Echoes.**

It subtly alters the acoustics of the hollow so that each strike of a water-drop against the obsidian floor ceases to be a solitary event. When a drop falls, the sound now carries a faint, harmonic overtone—a ghost-note that vibrates for a fraction of a second longer than the physical strike. To the Treader, this is a revelation. 

Through these harmonizing overtones, the Treader begins to "hear" the past. 

As it moves among the rusted iron rings, the amplified vibrations resonate through its calcified, mosaic hide, allowing it to perceive the *shadow-sounds* of what once happened here. It detects the rhythmic clinking of hammers against metal from a thousand years prior. It hears the footfalls of those who stood on the stone platforms. It experiences the hollow not as a decaying tomb, but as a living record. 

The Power does not force the Treader to change its nature; it merely gives it the library that had been hidden in the stone all along.

**The Result:**

The Lithic Treader stops its aimless coiling. Its filamentous pads, now pressed into the floor, begin to map more than just the structural integrity of the basalt; they map the *memory* of the hollow. It begins to move with a strange, purposeful grace, touching the rusted iron rings with a newfound, rhythmic precision. It ceases to be a passive inhabitant and becomes an active curator. 

It starts to shift the mineral deposits and the leathery ferns, rearranging them—not to create a new purpose, but to optimize the acoustics. It is "tuning" the room. It is turning the entire drip-hollow into a perfectly pitched instrument of remembrance.

The Power remains, unseen and unfelt, existing as that slight, lifting lightness in the air. It does not "save" the Treader from its solitude, for it understands that the Treader’s task is sacred. It merely ensures that when the final drop eventually falls—when the stone finally gives way and the hollow is reclaimed by the earth—the history stored within those obsidian walls will have been heard, fully and completely, by at least one living thing.

The metronome’s shadow is no longer waiting for the end. It is singing the prologue.
