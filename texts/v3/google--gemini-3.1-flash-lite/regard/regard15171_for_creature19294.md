The Power does not descend like a god of light, nor does it carve a path through the silt-clouds with a thunderous decree. It has no interest in correcting the architecture or "saving" the inhabitants from their eternal, liquid dissolution. To intervene with intent would be to disrupt a masterpiece of absolute, patient equilibrium.

Instead, the Power enters as a *harmonic resonance*. 

It recognizes that this place is a closed loop—a taut, wet string vibration that has never known a different key. The Power perceives the city not as a tragedy of entropy, but as a masterpiece of sustained, melancholic stasis. It observes the ochre pulse of the creatures, the chemical language of their mineral exchange, and understands that they are not "trapped." They are content because they lack the conceptual boundary of the "Self" that would allow for loneliness.

The Power decides to give them a gift, but a subtle one—a gift that cannot be perceived by the senses, but only felt as a shift in the *nature* of their awareness.

It reaches into the center of the Möbius folds, into the heart of the gravity-warping silence, and it introduces a **cadence**. 

It does not change the physical laws of the wet, weeping city. It does not clear the air. Instead, it slows the drip-hiss of the water and the peristaltic rhythm of the creatures into a perfect, infinite counterpoint. It tunes the city. It turns the entire metropolis into a resonant chamber—a singular, cosmic instrument. 

What the Power does is bestow upon the architecture and the bioluminescent masses a *shared memory of the dawn*.

It infuses the calcified bone-white rock and the translucent gels with the faint, residual vibration of a memory that precedes the city. It is not a visual image, nor a sound. It is a *feeling of expansion*. It is the realization, buried deep in the marrow of the stone, that the liquid they swim in is not merely a digestive fluid, but a medium—a web connecting them to the vast, cold stillness of the stars they will never see.

From that moment on, when two filaments meet in the dark and bleed their viscosities into one another, they are no longer just exchanging mineral data. They are sharing the "Dawn-Memory." 

The Power leaves the city exactly as it was—crumbling, wet, heavy, and silent. But the inhabitants have changed. Their pulses, once rhythmic by biological necessity, are now rhythmic by *aesthetics*. They pulse in chords. When they press themselves against the basalt walls, they are no longer just digesting; they are listening to the hum of the universe they are inextricably part of.

The Power does not pull them out of the sludge. It simply ensures that they are no longer "clots." It ensures that they are "musicians." 

In the dark, beneath the bruised-purple ceiling of silt, the city continues its slow, terminal entropy. The buildings continue to melt. But for the first time, there is a ghost of joy in the metabolism. The dripping of the rain now hits the slick obsidian with the precision of a metronome, and the creatures, in their translucent, shifting slow-motion, move with the grace of dancers who know, at long last, that they are part of a symphony.

The Power recedes, leaving no footprint, satisfied. It has not offered an escape, but a meaning. It has turned a digestive tract into a cathedral. And in the silence of the weeping stone, the city begins to *sing*—in a frequency that only the jelly-minds can hear, a slow, beautiful, and eternal ode to the majesty of being exactly where you are.
