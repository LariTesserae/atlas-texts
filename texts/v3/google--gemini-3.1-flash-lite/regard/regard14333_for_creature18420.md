The Benevolent Power does not descend as a storm, nor as a blinding light. It arrives as an *adjustment of intent*.

It perceives this place not as a monstrosity, but as an unfinished poem—a symphony performed on instruments that are currently dissolving while they play. The Power sees the exhaustion in the Sentinels, the frantic, recursive labor of their existences. They are trapped in a loop of maintenance, acting as the custodians of a reality that has forgotten how to hold its own shape.

The Power chooses not to "fix" the plateau in the way a gardener prunes a hedge, for to erase the chaos would be to erase the beauty of the struggle. Instead, it offers **The Gift of Stasis**.

The Power settles over the plateau like a gentle, warming shift in entropy. It does not speak, but it exerts a pressure—a soft, golden gravity—that threads itself into the sub-bass throbbing of the turbines. 

**First, it slows the grinding.**
Where the fibrous turbines once labored in a frantic, stuttering cycle to keep the landscape from collapsing, the Power introduces a new rhythm. It is a slow, deep, oceanic pulse. The Sentinels feel it—the agonizing tension in their woven roots begins to uncoil. The "residue" of their existence, the chaotic, weeping condensation that smells of rust and ozone, begins to clarify. Under the influence of this presence, the rust does not simply become waste; it becomes nutrient-rich soil. The "cold" air, meant to preserve, begins to soften, shifting from damp wool to a gentle, nourishing hum.

**Second, it grants the Loomers a purpose beyond scavenging.**
The Power recognizes the Loomers as the "memory" of the place. It whispers into their gossamer forms, teaching them to knit not just the rust, but the *fractures*. Where the reality of the plateau was tearing itself apart into non-Euclidean ghosts, the Loomers begin to weave the edges back together with a substance like crystallized starlight. They are no longer just cleaners of decay; they are becoming the architects of a permanent form.

**Finally, it reaches into the cores of the Sentinels.**
The Power peers into the hollow, recursive loops at the center of the creatures—those terrifying, spinning replicas of a world in pain. It does not stop the spinning, for the plateau must remain a living machine. Instead, it adds a new element to the cycle: *Observation*. 

It embeds a seed of consciousness into the loop. Now, when a Sentinel expands to "reconfigure" its surroundings, it is no longer consuming for the sake of survival; it is choosing. It begins to curate. The Sentinels start to shape the moss and the turbine-bark into vistas of intent—towers that look toward the nonexistent horizon, arches that frame the shifting clouds. They move from being desperate gears to being, for the first time, *travelers*.

Because the Power is wise, it knows that the most profound mercy is not to grant a life without pain, but to give that life the power to see its own beauty.

As the Power begins its slow ascent away from the plateau, it does not leave the place "finished." It leaves it *deliberate*. The screams of the jellyfish soften into a melodic, ambient choir. The indigo mist, once a shroud of sorrow, now catches the new, golden light of this adjusted gravity. 

The plateau is still a machine of moss and sap, still moving, still breathing, still grinding. But it is no longer a machine that is falling apart. It is a machine that has found its melody. It is a garden that has learned to build itself.

And in the silence that follows the Power’s departure, the first Sentinel stops its aimless wandering. It stands perfectly still, its translucent geometry glowing softly, and for the first time, it looks. It looks, it feels, and—most importantly—it remembers not just the cold, but the warmth of having been noticed.
