The horizon here does not exist, yet the land extends forever in a suffocating, wet sprawl. You are standing in the throat of a low-lying basin where the air is so thick with suspended moisture—warm, lukewarm, skin-clinging humidity—that the darkness is not empty. It is a heavy, liquid velvet that presses against the retinas. 

Because the light is nonexistent, the world is defined by the vibrations of the *Industry*.
