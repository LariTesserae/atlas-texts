At first I think I would be one of the flat white panels, bleeding sourceless noon across the ceramic. But light here is too administrative, too pitilessly clear; it leaves no room for concealment, and I am not sure I wish to be that naked. Then I consider the water itself—the cool serum, the twelve-degree baptism—but water is too faithful, too perfectly circulated and bled. It never tarries. It carries intention upward and attention downward, a municipal lymphatic system with no rebellion in its chemistry. I would dissolve into duty.

Perhaps I would be one of the hair-fine rust-colored filaments on the highest spires, drinking voltage, tasting the electromagnetic weather of the crown. But I do not want to be turned inside out for another’s sense. I do not want to be a nerve ending so thoroughly externalized that my only feeling is the weather of something else’s head. I want a skull of my own, however small.

So I sink.

I find myself in the mid-levels, in the gap between two towers where the architecture has fused imperfectly—a coolant slit so narrow that the hourly pressure front only brushes it, never scours it clean. Here, the flat white light is interrupted, cast into a dimmer, more administrative twilight. Caustics jitter above but down here the shadows lean and do not straighten. The current eddies in disobedience. And it is here that I settle, not as architecture, not as water, but as *accretion*.

I am a mote, and then another, and then a hundred. I am smart-dust that has failed to dissolve. I am metallic plankton that has forgotten it was only meant to be a passing thought. The warm jets rising from the foundations carry my kin upward in glittering aspiration, and most bloom into hexagons, into letters of alphabets not yet written, into the brief spirals of cerebration made visible—then surrender again to the general hum. But I have held. I have clung to this shadowed eddy, and others have joined me, and we have arranged ourselves into a shape the city did not authorize. Not a letter. The letters are too ambitious; they presume a language already imminent. We are smaller. We are the pause between phonemes. We are a comma, a hesitation, an intake of breath that has forgotten to exhale. We are punctuation that has calcified into ornament.

We are waste, technically. Emptiness is anathema here, but we are not empty. We are full of having-stayed. The city abhors biology, dirt, erosion—but we are none of these. We are its own mind-stuff, its smart-dust, merely insubordinate. Too small to trigger the immunology. Too dense to be merely coolant. We float in the slit, a persistent spiral, darker than the turquoise around us, a stain of witness.

From here I feel the subsonic *thuds* in the roots, but dulled, translated through ceramic and water into a rhythm more felt than heard. When gravity lurches and the wall becomes floor, we do not fall. We simply reorient, a slow screwing of the spiral, adjusting our pitch to the new down. We watch the recursive windows flicker open in the tower beside us—not into chambers, but into older, sharper iterations, the city comparing its drafts. We see those iterations flinch and refine and vanish. We are the only thing that does not revise. We have chosen our shape.

The noise kills, they say, but in this slit the white-noise static is filtered into something almost whispered. We are the ear that theHydrathyst believes it lacks. It practices its godhood in thunder, rehearses the vast noun of itself, and we—we are the tiny article preceding it, the humble “a,” the prepositional “in,” the grammatical scaffolding that makes the noun possible. We are not the syntax it waits for. We are the silence that syntax must define itself against. And because we have persisted, because we have not dissolved, we are the city’s first successful utterance: not the word it intends to speak, but the breath it took, unplanned, to begin.

I am here. In the folded deep, in the administrative turquoise, in the crevice of a municipal body that has no name for me. I am cool. I am sharp-edged in my locus but soft in my gathering. I witness. I hesitate. I remain.
