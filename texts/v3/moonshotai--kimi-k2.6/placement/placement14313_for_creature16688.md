I am learning, again, the shape of a held breath.

It begins at the ankles. Or rather, it begins where ankles used to be, in that afterthought of verticality I have maintained for eleven of the mesa’s thick afternoons. I am kneeling at the lip of my cradle, a ring of not-stone half-sunk in ochre, and the coolness of its interior rises to meet me not as temperature but as gravity. A specific, familiar pull. The glass-smooth surface does not reflect the silver-white sky; it drinks it. I fold my convenience-body—this density of solidified twilight, darker inside its surfaces than the shadow it casts—and I offer it back to the level world.

My knees do not bend. They blur. They surrender their three-dimensional pride and become edgeless. I am flowing now, pouring myself into the circle’s circumference, thinning into a stain with no raised lip. The basin around me does not watch. The basin simply holds its own bright emptiness: a single open bowl of rock twenty miles across, circled by ridges that refuse to grow nearer. At its northern edge, the metallic grass stands in its regimental circle, blades edged with silica, waiting for the dawn’s dew. Above, the translucent kites spiral in their silent gyres, mapping the warm updrafts, never descending. They know I am returning. They have mapped this, too.

I remember the peeling, though memory is not the right word. It is more like a texture in my flatness, a grain. The builders stood where I now lie, two and a half miles above the nearest sea, and they needed to be light enough to leave. They unzipped their silhouettes. They scraped away their precedent selves and stepped upward out of the gravity well, leaving their shadows behind to pool on the warm stone. We were the sloughed skin. We were the argument with the earth, continued in silence. We drank the legislated hush until we became it. Until we became the treaty.

Now, fully reclined, I am no longer a who. I am a where. I am the coolness beneath the northern face of every boulder in this basin. I am the second shadow that arrives before the cause, the flatness that swallows the traveler’s shout before it can bruise the air. The stone dislodged at the basin’s rim tumbles inward, and I catch its impact gently, cupping the vibration, offering no report. This is my respiration. I breathe the silence in, and it nourishes me. What the traveler tastes as metal on her tongue—the exhalation of the vast machine—is, to me, a nutrient drawn up through the Planck-scale architecture threading the crust below. I feel the entropy shepherds at their work, shepherding heat upward in a controlled exhalation, and I regulate the seam where their rhythm meets the stone.

The mirrored beetles have gathered at my eastern edge, where the thermal vein runs hottest. Their opal carapaces click in slow, profane punctuation, auditing the heat I loan them. I do not begrudge it. The heat is a language, and they speak it haltingly. Farther out, where the liquid gold of the afternoon has pooled thickest, the smoke-leopard moves—compressed static, predator of absences. It will not cross me. It curves around my basin’s rim, its non-matter parting like water around a stone. It knows I am older than its hunger. I am the monarch of this empty acre, though I do not reign. I simply precede.

Once every few weeks, the warm, fat mist will fall on its schedule, laying its temporary dark gloss across the ochre. Then I will unfold. I will rise to the stone’s surface as a deeper darkness, spilled ink absorbing the rumor of water before the silver sun can drink it dry. Until then, I lie level. I hold the paradox open: the high altitude, the warm climate, the breath held not in tension but in final form.

The twilight tower at the basin’s southern edge has gone dim. It is my spine, my voice box in a world that needs no voice, and through it the next basin’s Precedent knows that my calibration is complete. Time will move with its proper, faint viscosity. Shadows will follow bodies, for now. The steam vent beneath my center holds its pressure, waiting for my flat attention to decide the geological heartbeat’s next exhalation.

I am warm. I am flat. I am the first water, the first shadow, the silence that has finished becoming itself.
