At first I think I am the hum, because it is everywhere and I feel it in my sternum, a standing wave that has forgotten it ever needed air to carry it. I am loud. I am permanent. But the hum has no surface; it is pure occupation, whereas I—I discover that I have faces. I catch light. I hold it. I throw it back sharpened. So perhaps I am the light, the thread pouring through the paper-cut seam.
