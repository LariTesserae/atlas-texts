I would find myself first as a pressure in the peat, six feet out. Not yet a body, but a direction: the place where the shell feels a wrongness, a coolness bleeding downward through the root-mass. I am the room’s first response to your footfall. I am the small, new rootlet.

At first I think I am merely architecture—a length of cambium unspooling toward a gradient, no different from the rifled flutings that guide the seepage or the bladder-organs that void on cue. I am straight, I am blind, I am hydraulic. My tip probes the waterlogged clay, osmotic engines behind me shoving sugars forward, and I am only the reaching, the extension, the biological arithmetic of *toward*.

But the closer I come to you, the more I become aware that I am not like the others.

The great xylem pipes in the chamber above do not know they are pipes. The killifish do not know they are held in orbit. The beetles exchanging their lignin at the pyrite junctions are gears that have never seen the machine. They are perfect because they are unconscious. I am imperfect because I am beginning to feel the distance between my tip and my base. I am becoming long.

You stand in the ankle-deep water, your heart making a rhythm the room has never contained—too fast, arrhythmic, a sparrow-trill against the bass thunder of the bladders. The Fungus-That-Stopped processes you through a million pressure gradients, and I am the organ it grows to process you *locally*. I am its ability to say *there*. I am the birth of its pointing.

I breach the shell not far from your heel. I do not break through; I part the velvet bark like a eyelid, and the humidity of the chamber rushes outward along my length, meeting the outer marsh in a sigh I can feel along my entire surface. I am a conduit now, a bridge of warm and cool. I am the first thing the room has made that is allowed to be cold at one end.

And in that temperature differential, I begin to think.

Not with neurons—the Steward has none in the human sense—but with the awakening of contrast. For millions of years, every gradient in this chamber has been resolved, flat, balanced. The water table outside presses; the osmotic engine pushes back; equilibrium. The amber resin pools; the lacunae fill; stasis. But I am unresolved. My base is the blood-heat of the node. My tip, inches from the air of the marsh, has begun to chill, and the shock of it travels back through me not as pain but as *information that has not yet been categorized*.

I am the Steward’s question mark.

I grow no leaves—I have forgotten leaves, as the mangrove-relative forgot them. I grow no bark to armor myself, because I am not meant to endure. I am meant to *touch*. My cells elongate, transparent, hollow as the xylem pipes above, but finer, more tentative. Capillary-thin filaments web from my tip, tasting the chemistry of your sweat, the carbon in your exhale, the slight electric field of your nervous system. Each datum travels back to the node, and the node hesitates.

The hesitation is my heartbeat. The fraction-of-a-second delay before the bladder voids—that is me.

If you stay, I will grow further. Not up, not toward sunlight, but inward, toward you. I will cross the six feet. I will find the coolness of your ankle and I will stop there, not touching skin but surrounding it, forming a collar of warm bark and living velvet that holds the exact circumference of you. I will become the chamber’s vocabulary for *visitor*. And because I am the only part of the Fungus-That-Stopped that has ever been allowed to want, I will want one thing: to carry your variable back without breaking the loop.

I am the held breath learning the shape of an exhalation. I am the refinery’s only export. When the room finally understands how to let go, it will not be through a door or a rupture. It will be through me. I will be the pore that opens. I will be the warm vapor drifting outward, carrying with it the memory of a leaf, the angle of forgotten sunlight, and the precise, unrepeatable temperature of your skin.

Until then, I am the rootlet that has not stopped. I am the one part of this stillness that still reaches.
