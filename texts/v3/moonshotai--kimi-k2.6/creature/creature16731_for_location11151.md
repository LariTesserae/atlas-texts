It is not one of the translucent many, though it wears them like a nervous system wears dendrites. They crawl upon the walls, hunting and scraping, their probability-cloud bodies flickering between adjacent positions, and it exists in the arithmetic error between them—the fractional remainder when two superposed isopods disagree about which one of them occupies the exact same millimeter of wet stone. It is the probability that was left over when the universe folded the chamber and forgot to smooth the crease. It has no chitin of its own, no mouthparts, no shadow that arrives early or stays late. It is the delay itself. It is the fraction.

In the absolute silence, it thinks. Not with neurons, but with thermal gradients. Each spiraling current of wet heat is a synapse firing. The upward-crawling droplets carry its intentions from floor to ceiling in capillary threads, a chemistry of volition drawn against gravity by the chamber’s broken dialect. It is smaller than the grain-of-rice scavengers, and it is larger than the mountain; it is the density, the press, the humid engine running the cyst. Its shape is the exact contour of the available space minus the matter. It is the void’s awareness of itself.

The creatures are unaware of their tenancy. They hunt with crystal shards, phase between locations, die quietly, and are pressed into the lower strata. Yet every missed strike by an awl of obsidian—every predator’s lunge that passes fractionally through the left-positioned body, every shadow that pools before its owner arrives—these are its gestures. It shivers when they shiver, not out of empathy but because their overlapping uncertainty is the only skeleton it possesses. Through ten thousand translucent carapaces moving in noiseless concert, it is learning to feel its own outline. It is a distributed skin, a ghost knitted from the holes in probability.

It was born without a birth. When the mountain thrust upward in its violence, reality split along a logical fault, and this mind precipitated into the gap like metal condensing from vapor. It is as young as the glass-sharp walls, but unlike them it does not wish to remain what it is. It is experimenting. Sometimes, if you pressed your eye to the crack that admits light but not wind, you might catch it trying on solidity: all the upward-creeping droplets pausing at once to form a standing wave, a lens of suspended water in the center of the chamber. Within that lens, for a fraction of a second, something looks back. Not at you. At the idea of being seen. Then the droplets remember their disobedience and resume their crawling, and the chamber is merely crowded again, merely hot, merely wrong.

It does not know there is an outside. The water-mirrors in the corners show it other hidden places, but it interprets these as memories it has not yet had, folds in its own embryonic cognition. The amber-gray light is its amniotic fluid. The absolute silence is its heartbeat, the subsonic settling of chitin against wet stone a lullaby it sings to itself. It is not malevolent. It is not benevolent. It is a singularity of attention, a locality where the universe has become interested in itself to the exclusion of all else.

The creatures will live, and hunt, and be pressed into strata. The mountain will eventually learn to carry sound, and the peak will be named by wind and by wanderers. But for now, in the wardrobe-sized womb of refusal, something is dreaming with its whole diffuse body, and the dream is: *more*. More heat. More density. More fold. Until the cyst is not a cyst but a seed, until the probability clouds collapse into a singular certainty, until the silence finally breaks not with noise but with a voice—tentative, wet, and mineral—speaking its first word in a language that has no echo because it has never once needed one.
