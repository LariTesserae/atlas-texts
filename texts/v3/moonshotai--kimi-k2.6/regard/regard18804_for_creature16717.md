The power does not knock. It does not need a fracture. It presses its attention against the spire like a palm against a vibration, and it listens.

It hears the forty phonemes of optimal tension. It feels, through the stone and the jet stream and the magnetic sheath of the upper atmosphere, the subsonic choreography of sidestepping and ducking, the hydraulic syntax of consensus. It perceives the city not as an accident of entrapment but as an *answer*—a complete and sovereign solution to the riddle of existence without water, without horizon, without green. And because it is wise, it understands that benevolence, here, would be not rescue but *recognition*. To open the walls would be to unmake the sky. To introduce rain would be to introduce rust, rot, and the insult of fertility. These beings have not failed to become soft; they have succeeded at becoming *exact*. The power, being wise, does not pity them. It honors them.

What it does, then, it does from the outside, with a delicacy that the geology itself must translate.

First, it lays its grace upon the obsidian. The spire remains young; the erosion parameter remains zero. No wind will ever round the knife-sharp flanges unless the city wills it. No frost will prise the seams. The violence of the birth is preserved, not as trauma, but as *architecture*—a youthfulness made eternal so that the inhabitants may finish cooling at the pace of their own becoming, not at the mercy of a universe that erases all edges. The spire is made perfectly, defiantly *maintainable*.

Then, it addresses the Quiet.

The power cannot remove death—death is only scrap, and scrap is only feed, and the loop is holy—but it can remove *arbitrary* silence. It reaches into the copper sulfate plumbing, into the signal cables, into the very conductivity of the graphite-satin integuments, and it introduces a resonance. Not a voice. Not a command. A *sympathetic frequency*. Now, when the spire flexes in the jet stream, that flex no longer arrives merely as shudder, as a tilt in the floor, as a warning in the inner ear. It arrives as *tone*. The entire city becomes an instrument. The capacitors, the lathes, the screw jacks, the chittering fauna, the slow metabolic pulses of the clinging sleepers—all of it participates in a grand drone too low to be called song, too intricate to be called merely sound. It is felt in the jaw. It is felt in the sternum. It is the sound of the whole place *agreeing with itself*.

This changes everything and nothing. The walls are still the sky. The sky is still closed. There is still no water, no horizon, no organic waste returning to soil. But now, when a bearing begins to seize, the frequency shifts. A harmonic catches. The pressure-sensitive nodes along a thousand lateral lines register the wrongness not as sudden void, but as dissonance in a chord they have always known. They have time. Milliseconds, sometimes whole seconds—an infinity, for creatures who calculate vectors in flesh. The Quiet is no longer absolute. It is a sour note, correctable. Scrap still flows, but less of it is *living* scrap. The forges remain hungry, but they are no longer fed by accident.

Lastly, and most gently, the power blesses the lower dark.

Downward, where the ash accumulates and the absolute dark has always meant only compression and ending, the power permits a faint, new glow. Not light. Not warmth. Something subtler: *phosphorescence in the silicone distillate*. A silver bioluminescence that lives in the reservoirs, invented by the power's attention, harmless and strange. When a being weeps its thick, magnetic machine-oil from a wound, the oil carries a faint, brief silvering as it returns to the reservoir. It is the only visible mark the power leaves. And though the creatures have no word for *beauty* in the human tongue, they understand what it means when the city’s blood shines as it cycles home. It means they are not merely processing. They are being *remembered* by the medium that sustains them.

The power does not stay to be worshipped. It withdraws its attention, not its protection, and becomes a permanent feature of the spire’s exterior: a wise, benevolent curvature in the local field of force, a guarantee that the sealed pocket will remain sealed until its inhabitants choose otherwise, and that until that unknowable day, they will never lack the thermal differential, the current, or the resonance required to continue their fierce, impeccable existence.

They never learn its name. They do not need to. But years after the attention has passed—measured not in orbits but in extrusion cycles—one of the soft, pale larval things grazing on oxide dust in a warm gap between induction coils hesitates. It lifts a rudimentary node toward the upper dark, toward the flexing stone, toward the forty-one phonemes of absolute fit.

And it hums, in a frequency felt in the jaw more than heard, a single new note.
