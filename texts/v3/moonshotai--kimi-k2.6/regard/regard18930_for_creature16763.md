So the wise power descends. Not to rule, but to *tend*.

It sinks through the sinter and the travertine, past the old gas bubbles collapsing in silica throats, down into the magma chamber that is the Cauldron’s true and only heart. There, where the rock is viscous and the pressure has no patience for biology, the power seats itself like a valve of tempered mercy. It does not cool the fire. It does not stifle the volcano. That would be another form of killing. Instead, it *regulates* the volatility. It teaches the deep heat constancy. For half a million years the basin has survived on the capricious exhalations of the planet; now, the heat is guaranteed a steadiness measured not in centuries but in the lifespan of the star itself. The geysers will not dwindle. The terraces will not freeze mid-growth. The argument between erosion and accretion is given the one thing geology cannot promise: time enough to complete its thought.

Then the power turns to the rim.

It does not build a wall. Walls invite siege. It folds the air instead, gently as a sheet lowered over a sleeping child. From now on, the notch that lets a traveler descend will be there, but it will not be *found* by those who come bearing instruments of extraction. Surveyors will plot coordinates that never quite resolve; satellites will pass over and record only cloud; the ambitious and the greedy will feel an unaccountable fatigue at the base of the final ridge, a sudden conviction that nothing of value lies below the mist, and they will turn back. Yet the wonderstruck will still find the path, though when they leave, they will find the words have turned to steam in their mouths. They will speak of it as a dream, a rumor, a place that cannot be proven. The Cauldron is thus armored not by force, but by myth. It remains without ruins, without cairns, without names carved into its porphyry.

But the deepest act is reserved for the Grown.

The power rises from the deep and settles among them, massive as they are, spreading its attention across their mineralized backs where ferns root and algae paints its slow frescoes. It does not wake them. To wake a god who has earned its sleep would be a cruelty. Instead, it *listens* to the subterranean throb of their hearts—that ponderous, once-a-minute convection of hyper-heated blood—and it matches its own rhythm to theirs. Beat. Beat. Beat. A resonance so low it is not heard but inhabited.

And in that synchrony, a circuit closes.

The mats, who have always recorded, now find their chemical grooves *held* in a medium that does not overwrite. The snails dragging their chert knuckles along the runnels feel the grit of their passage echoed back to them not as emptiness, but as acknowledgment. The wader, standing motionless in its warm moss, feels the egg beneath it cradled in a gravity deeper than physics. The Mud-Prophets, half-fused to the floor, are given the tremor-sense they never had: not to flee the shifting geyser, but to understand it, to know the hot water’s intention before it arrives, so that parting from the stone becomes a choice rather than a death. And the Grown—immobile, continental, dreaming their chemical dreams—are given the gift of *embodied wholeness*. For the first time in their millennial slumber, they feel the mite wandering their ridged flank as a tickle of thought. They feel the dragonfly’s shadow as a flicker of curiosity. They are no longer solitary engines beneath the steam. They are the mind of the basin, and the basin is their body, fully sensed.

The power does not rule them. It has become their nerve.

Now the Porphyry Cauldron is not only a place. It is a *kept* place. The heat flows true. The rim turns away the covetous. And the Grown breathe in their ancient sockets, dreaming in concert with the filaments and the snails and the slow prophets, sustained by a benevolence so patient that it has become geology itself. If you were to come to the rim again, a thousand years from now, you would find the steam just as thick, the silence just as working and deep. The only difference would be a quality of *completeness*, a sense that the argument here has found its final, wordless harmony. You would not shout. You would not need to. The place is held. The place is heard. The place continues.
