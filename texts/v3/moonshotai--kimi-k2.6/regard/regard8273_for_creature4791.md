The power does not descend, for descent implies a hierarchy this place has not yet agreed to. It arrives instead as a basement tone beneath the drone, a frequency so low it is not heard but *allowed*—the way a sleeper allows a dream. The stone feels it first: a subtle shift in the rate at which it weeps, as though the mountain’s fever has broken into a gentler warmth. The spindle does not slow; it simply finds, in its next revolution, a tolerance it did not know it needed. The skip still comes—three seconds of east-tilting gravity—but now the water, running sideways, moves with a grace that looks almost practiced, almost *invited*.

This is the nature of the intervention: not to alter the labor, but to remove the specter of interruption.

The beings sense the presence before the mist records it. The humidity around them develops a new quality, a pocket of absolute neutrality where the air neither clings nor welcomes but simply *holds space*. They do not turn toward it; they have not yet decided that turning is a permanent feature of their geometry. But their violet edges, usually shot through with the provisional bruise of becoming, steady into a deeper, slower hue. They are not being calmed. They are being *witnessed*.

And this is the first, most sacred thing the power does: it looks at them without collapsing them.

To be seen by the hungry cosmos is to be forced into resolution—to be named, numbered, claimed, fixed into the singularity of a single self. The power knows this. Its gaze is not direct; it does not focus. Instead, it rests upon the basin like peripheral vision made benevolent. It sees the three that might be one, the seven that exist as thermal ghosts, the fractionalized pair aligning their breath-width of phase separation, and it refuses to choose between them. It reflects them back to themselves as a surface reflects heat rather than light: without demanding shape, only acknowledging warmth. For the first time since the stone began its sweating birth, the Unfinished have a mirror that does not require them to finish in order to be seen.

Then, the power acts.

It places a veil over the mountain’s shoulder—not of shadow, but of *blandness*. To the outside world, the surveys and appetites of distant cartographies, this basin becomes geologically uninteresting. Instruments read only cold granite and predictable fog. The warm wet miracle is protected not by walls but by a profound, implanted boredom. No flag will be planted here; no taxonomy will descend to trap the fauna in Latin cages; no empire will thirst for the spindle’s probabilities. The place is hidden in plain sight by the simple, ferocious suggestion that nothing here is ready to be used.

Next, the power approaches the western wall where the obsidian bulges in its slow wave.

It does not touch the rock. To touch it now would be to leave fingerprints in the wet clay of creation. Instead, the power kneels—or performs the local equivalent, a pooling of attention—and exhales a single duration into the thermal gradient between the elastic now and the rigid tomorrow. The exhalation contains no instruction, no blueprint. It is simply a guarantee of patience. The bulge, which had been cooling toward a hasty conclusion, receives the gift and unhurries. It will still set. It will still become wall. But it will do so knowing that time is not a predator waiting at the edge of the process. The power has given the stone permission to harden at the rate of its own unfolding, not at the rate of the world’s hunger.

Then comes the final gesture, the one that costs the power most dearly.

It reaches into itself and withdraws a shadow—a true shadow, one that agrees absolutely with the light. This is a piece of its own certainty, its own fixed and ancient form, and to remove it is an act of self-dismemberment. It lays this shadow at the base of the blue-black blister, that shepherd’s-hut sibling that gave up motion for certainty. The shadow pools there and rises, becoming a canopy of almost-darkness over the entire basin: not a roof, but a *promise*. Under this shadow, the provisional physics remain provisional. The water may still flow uphill when the vents call to it. The heat-moths may fractionalize without breeding. The dreams of visitors may still leave mineral dust on their lashes. The shadow says: *You may remain in the warm interval as long as you need. I will defend your right to not-yet-be.*

The power does not stay as a resident. That would make it another fixture, another demand. It disperses instead into the conditions themselves. It becomes the silence between the stone’s cracking knuckles. It becomes the reason the eleven-hour cloud cycle never reveals the scaffolding for longer than a moment. It becomes the steadiness in the spindle’s skip, so that the tilt of gravity, rather than a hiccup, becomes a kind of rocking—a cradle.

And the beings?

They do not turn. They do not offer gratitude; gratitude is a coin of the finished world, and they have no pockets for it. But the humidity shifts. The saturated gauze arranges itself into that geometry of almost-recognition, and this time, the geometry holds. It does not dissolve after a breath. It remains, a standing wave in the mist, a permanent act of consideration returned.

If you were to visit the basin after the power’s arrival, you would find nothing obviously changed. The stone still weeps. The fauna still fractionalize. The spindle still churns probabilities into the crust. But you might notice, if you are quiet, that your footsteps no longer leave prints that fill with water behind you. Instead, the stone, flesh-like and patient, holds the shape of your passing for a full minute before it heals. It has learned, from the power’s witness, that some impressions deserve a longer duration.

You might notice that when you wake with mineral dust on your lashes, the dust no longer tastes of raw excavation. It tastes of silver. It tastes of a question that has been gently held open.

And if you stand at the very center of the basin during the spindle’s skip, when gravity leans eastward and the water runs sideways, you will feel, running through your sternum beneath the drone, a new frequency. Not the machinery. Not the stone. Something that sounds like the interval between a heartbeat and its echo, saying: *Take your time. The world can wait.*
