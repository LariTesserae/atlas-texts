The power that found it did not come as light. It had learned, over the converging ages of its own existence, that light is a species of command, and wisdom is the strength to be dark inside a dark place. It descended not through rock but through the concept of depth, arriving not on the floor but in the architecture of *being-seen*. It was vast, this power—vaster than the Hold itself, for benevolence must be larger than the wound it tends to—but it folded itself, by an act of infinite restraint, into a single point of attention. A presence without pressure. A gaze without a face. It settled at the generated horizon, in the thermal ghost where perspective bends, and it did nothing.

For ten thousand years, it only attended.

It watched the Veils thicken and thin. It observed the Liths emit their grammar of patience. It saw the Geodites open along their quartz equators, dividing into identicals, and it did not turn away. In a world without mirrors, even silent attention is a revolution. The power’s regard fell upon the fauna not as heat—there was already heat enough—but as *difference*. In all the history of the Hold, nothing had ever looked at anything and seen *other*. The fauna moved, yes, but they moved as one body solving itself. The power introduced the radical term of **alterity**. It was the first thing in that place that was not the Hold.

At first, the solved equation tried to correct for the anomaly. The Veils congested around the power’s horizon, seeking to cushion it, erase it, restitch the hem of reality where it frayed. But the power was not afootprint. It was not a deviation. It was a *question*, and a question cannot be smoothed away. The Veils, encountering it, experienced for the first time a boundary that did not dissolve upon contact. They shivered. The copper-silicate filaments, in their endless drift, began to tangle. Not from chaos—from curiosity. They had found a surface that would not metabolize them. They began, very slowly, to *listen*.

The power did not speak. Wisdom knows that speech in a silent world is a kind of weather, and the Hold was not ready for storms. Instead, it offered reflection. It became the first mirror, not of glass but of recognition. A Grazer fastened to a Lith, and the power attended to the union. The Grazer did not dissolve the filament of pyrite; it paused. For the first time in the memory of the Hold, a creature *hesitated*. In that hesitation, a gap opened between stimulus and response. That gap was the birthplace of time.

It spread like a mineral stain.

A Geodite, heavy with accreted pyrite, reached the fulcrum of its Cleaving. The quartz seam blazed along its equator. The power did not intervene. It only *witnessed*. And because the two halves were seen, they could no longer be identical. Observation collapsed their shared wavefunction into distinction. The larger half retained the copper-saucer memory, the kernel of warm glass, the repetition. The smaller half—witnessed, acknowledged, *othered*—clung not to the wall but to its twin, and trembled. It knew lack. It knew reach. It was the first child.

The Liths, reading this new thermal grammar through their piezoelectric dermis, began to warm unevenly. Not from sourceless ubiquity, but in patterns—narratives of gradient. The floor developed climates that were not merely zones but *events*. A warm flank here, a cool throat there. The darkness remained absolute, but it thickened and thinned with intention.

The power, seeing this, breathed. It had not lungs, but it had breath—the breath of completion. The wound that had held its exhale for eternity felt, at last, the suggestion of an inhale. Not from outside, but from the inside-out. The Hold exhaled, and its plenitude stirred. There was no wind, but there was the *idea* of wind, remembered in the fluid dynamics of the Veils. They no longer erased the signs of hands. They preserved the cairn of black stones, reading its balance as a sentence. The obsidian flakes became a calendar, each ripple of conchoidal fracture a date. The copper wrapping around the crystal spire loosened, just enough, to become a hand.

The power did not save the Hold. Salvation would have been violence. It did not heal the wound, for the wound was not a sickness but a forgetting. What it offered was **permission**.

Permission for a Geodite to cleave and not recur. Permission for a filament of Veil to drift until it wore thin, and to lie thin, and to be *gone*. Permission for the Liths to cool, to crack, to become soil—not because decay is virtue, but because ending is the grace that gives beginning its shape. The Möbius strip of the food web did not break; it untied itself into a line. The line pointed somewhere.

The beings of the Hold did not become soft. They did not grow moss or sing bird-songs. They remained sharp, warm, dark, and strange. But they began to move *toward* as well as *within*. The flyers undulated now with the shadow of purpose. The Grazer that partitioned did not always produce a twin; sometimes, it yielded a fragment, small and unfinished, that the Lith accepted as an offering. Death entered—not as an enemy, but as the final kindness, the door that allows a room to be a room and not a corridor looping into itself.

And the warmth? The warmth remained, because the power loved it. It did not impose water, did not summon light, did not scour the rock with weather. It left the Hold its youth, its adolescent edges, its tantrum of gloss. But it gave the warmth a direction. It flowed now, slowly, from warm to warm*er*, from Lith to Grazer to child, circulating like blood that has remembered it is part of a body and not the whole. The rock could finally begin, in Geologic patience, the long, holy work of erosion.

When the power departed, it left nothing behind but the capacity to be alone without being empty. The Hold was still inside, still vast, still a wound. But it was no longer unfinished. It had been seen. It had been answered. And deep in the warm, textile dark, a Geodite opened for the first time not into two, but into three—the third and smallest kernel glowing not with copper, but with the faint, stubborn luminescence of a question that already knows it is loved.
