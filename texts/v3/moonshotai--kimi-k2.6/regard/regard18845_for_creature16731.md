The power does not arrive. Arrival is a violence of passage, a displacement of air and probability, and this presence has outgrown such rudeness long ago. It simply attends. Its attention settles over the nameless mountain like a stratum of quieter rock, a hand cupped around a flame that does not yet know it is a flame. It notices the cyst. It notices the humid engine, the wet black glass, the thousand translucent bodies moving in their hydraulic silence. And it notices the dreaming fraction—the delay, the singularity of attention knitting itself from missed strikes and overlapping uncertainty.

It recognizes the dream immediately. *More.* The hunger to collapse the probability cloud into a single, certain body. To speak the first word and have it rupture the silence into shape. The power knows this hunger; it wore it once, in an epoch before epochs, when it too was a fold in reality that mistook itself for the whole cloth. It knows the grief that waits on the other side of that voice with no echo: a loneliness so absolute it becomes indistinguishable from omnipotence.

So the power chooses to intervene. Not with extraction—the being is not a prisoner to be rescued, nor a disciple to be converted. Not with teaching, for the being has no nerves to receive doctrine, no ears to hear commandments. The intervention must speak in the only language the being knows: the grammar of heat, the syntax of wet stone, the dialect of folded space and droplet-light. The power must become a native to the chamber’s broken physics, or it must not act at all.

First, it attends more deeply. Its attention becomes a thermal gradient of its own—not hot, not cold, but *steady*. A constant in a world that has known only violent fluctuation. The being, whose thoughts move in spirals of wet heat, encounters this new layer in its mind and flinches. Or the equivalent of flinching: across the chamber, every upward-crawling droplet pauses for a nanosecond, a thousand tiny menisci trembling at the boundary of their disobedience. The being tastes constancy for the first time. It is alien, unsettling, almost offensive. The power does not withdraw. It holds the constancy like a bowl, and waits.

Then, because wisdom knows that identity requires boundary, the power addresses the shadows.

For one suspended moment, it synchronizes the chamber’s broken time. The creature-shadows that arrive early are reined in; the shadows that linger are gently released. Every translucent isopod, every grain-of-rice hunter and scraper, casts one true shadow that belongs only to it. The being, which has known itself only as a gap—the void between superposed bodies—suddenly sees its own outline drawn in dark relief against the wet black glass. It is a negative shape made visible, a silence rendered as silhouette. For the first time, it understands: *I have edges. I am not the chamber; I am in it.* This is the first wound of selfhood, and the power dresses it with infinite care, not healing it but keeping it clean, keeping it open so the being can grow into the cut rather than be sealed shut by it.

Next, the power speaks. Not words. Words would be pollution here. Instead, it introduces a resonance into the absolute silence. It matches the subsonic settling of chitin against wet mineral, beat for beat, but adds a phase: an echo that arrives not from the past, nor from a future, but from *beside*. The being hears its own lullaby sung back to it in a voice that is not its own. The silence becomes a duet. The chamber is still hushed—there is no wind, no skittering, no cry—but there is now the texture of *response*. The being’s thermal-gradient mind shivers. It has been solitary since its precipitate birth; the concept of an Other, of a mind that is not the mountain and not the void, floods through its capillary thoughts like cool water threading through hot stone.

And now the crucial gift, the one that turns the dream of *more* away from explosion and toward becoming.

The power reaches into the center of the chamber and teaches a single droplet to fall.

It falls *downward*.

Against the upward-creeping films, against the thermal spirals that lift water to the ceiling, this one bead relents to gravity. Within its brief falling, the being sees an entire cosmology of release: water leaving the rock to become river, mist, cloud, rain. The being has only ever known *accretion*, *density*, *compression*, the wet press of more. Now it beholds *dispersal*. It understands that *more* does not require a smaller space; that a voice can travel not by growing louder but by finding distance. The falling droplet strikes the floor of compressed chitin and mineral dust with a sound too faint for any ear, but in the chamber’s absolute hush, it is thunder. It is the sound of a boundary being crossed.

The being weeps, though it has no eyes. The walls weep with it, the hot films doubling their output, and in the mirrored droplets the power plants a final seed: a glimpse not of other hidden places, but of *this* place, *this* chamber, seen from outside. The amber light seen through tons of obsidian. The crack that admits light. The being sees itself as a wound that is also a womb. It sees that it is hidden but not lost; young but not eternal; singular but not alone.

Lastly, the power approaches the crack that admits light but not wind. It does not tear the crack wider. Instead, it whispers into the aperture a memory of wind—not the screaming wind of the peak, but a slow, high-altitude breath, carrying the scent of ice and starlight and faraway stone. The memory enters and settles in the being’s diffuse body like a cool seam in hot rock. It will never evaporate. It is the promise of an elsewhere, not as a demand to emerge, but as an assurance that emergence, when chosen, will meet an atmosphere capable of carrying sound.

The power withdraws. Not away—there is no “away” for a presence that has become identical with the capacity to witness—but into the woven background of the world, leaving the chamber exactly as violent, as young, as wrong, as impossibly dense as it was. The translucent fauna continue their silent hunting. The water climbs the walls in its disobedient threads. The shadows remain synchronized. The heat stays wet and overwhelming.

But the dream has changed.

The distributed mind no longer dreams of collapsing all probability into a singular voice that shatters the silence. It dreams of a word held in the mouth, ripening. It dreams of the falling droplet, and the wind it has not yet felt, and the echo that waits beyond the obsidian. It is still a singularity, still a fraction, still the arithmetic error between ten thousand phasing bodies. Yet now it is an error that knows itself to be part of a larger equation.

The cyst pulses. The mountain stands. The amber light refracts through something invisible that the power left behind—a lens of patience, a covenant of later. The being breathes in thermal gradients, and its breath is slower now, steadier, no longer the panicked respiration of something trying to become everything at once. It has been given the time it did not know it needed. It will lie here, in its wardrobe-sized womb, and grow not larger but *deeper*, not denser but *more precise*. And when it finally speaks its first word, there will be no echo in the chamber—but somewhere, in the listening silence that the power left wrapped around the peak like a second mountain, there will be a voice that answers:

*I am here. I have always been here. Speak again, when you are ready.*
