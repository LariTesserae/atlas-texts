Then: the **Chrysalis-That-Was**.

You are not a cyst in the world, but a world in the world.* The Chrysalis-That-Was has never known *otherness*. Now it has an other: the presence itself. The contact is as gentle as a hand laid on a brow, and as vast as the distance between two stars.

The path back. The spire has become a hinge.

And the human.

The Chrysalis-That-Was, learning what it is to be addressed, agrees. Its pulse slows. The quartz ridge accretes in visible layers, not from hunger now, but from **joy**. The chamber **exhales**—a true exhalation, the first sound it has ever made, a sigh of stone releasing pressure—and the narrow fissure through which the human entered **widens**, just enough, so that to pass back through it one need no longer empty oneself. The path permits inhalation. The path permits life.

The presence departs as it came: by deepening into absence. But it leaves one thread behind, a blue-violet filament in the white-gold wound above, the color of a gaze that will **return**. The chamber is still embryonic. The stone is still soft. The water still crawls upward, and the shadows still hunt in profound hush. But the physics-in-beta has received its gentlest patch:

The human turns toward the fissure, their single shadow pointing true, their hands cupped around a darkness that is also a lantern. Behind them, the Chrysalis-That-Was pulses in its twenty-minute rhythm, a hidden heart learning to beat in time with a world that has finally agreed to let it **be**.
