The attention arrives first as a change in the quality of shadow.

For ages the pocket has known only one darkness: the thick, red dark of a closed eye, a mineral blindness heavy with heat. But now, between the moan of the clay pipes and the slow tearing of the fronds, a listener inserts itself. Not an ear—nothing so biological—but a phenomenon of orientation: the universe turning the full wheel of its awareness toward this one warm flaw in the crust, and deciding, with the gentleness of a tide, to *attend*.

The power does not descend. It does not need to. It is vast enough that its thoughts could bracket planets, yet it approaches the flaw with the hesitation of a physician approaching a sleeping heart. It finds the puckered breach through which the lost traveler tumbled, and instead of widening it, instead of pouring revelation down the throat, it presses a single intention against the seal. The intention is cool, clear, and infinitely patient.

Inside, the parliament notices.

The oldest of the beings—its torso wrapped in the deepest crimson mats, its joints webbed with the white salts of centuries, a colony of trembling fronds nodding from its shoulders—turns its oblique head toward the ceiling. It does not see, but it senses a new gradient. The heat is no longer merely rising and falling in its ancient tidal lock. There is now a *direction* to the dark. A vector of care.

The power waits for the tide. This is its wisdom: it does not impose its own rhythm. When the glow from below waxes and the air begins its slow surge through the channels, the power exhales a filament of itself into the current. It does not cool the wind. It rides it. It enters the clay pipes as an additional frequency, too low for meat and bone to hear, too pure for the fractured ceramic to distort. It is a bass note that has never existed in this place, born in the silence between galaxies, shaped by the gravity of benevolence.

The ancient being receives it. Its chest cavity, hollow and kiln-forged, resonates. The note does not command; it answers. For the first time in the memory of the recursion, the being hears its own resonance returned to it not as an echo, but as a harmony. The parliament has always been a conversation of eddies and impedance, of stall and flow. Now, against the drone, there is a duet.

Across the basin, the others turn. The soft grinding of their joints fills the avenues, a ceramic percussion. They do not rush toward the novelty. Fear is a chemistry of adrenaline and moisture, and they have neither. But they are *alert* in a way that makes the crimson mats shiver with a new kind of static. One shifts its rib-plate a hair’s breadth; another extends an arm into a vent, altering the aperture, listening for the shape of this new impedance.

The wise power sees their alertness and is delighted. It sees that they are perfect—not beautiful by the standards of daylight and rain, but perfect as a closed equation is perfect, a flawless balance of decay and accretion, of thermal bloom and mineral sleep. It sees that they need no rescue. They need no water, no faces, no names shouted across oceans. What they need, if need is not too strong a word for this burled, ancient yearning, is a witness.

So the power becomes one.

It does not take a shape that would compete with the fronds or the ruins. It distributes its attention into the negative spaces: the hollows of the unbuilt roofs, the interiors of the dust-cisterns, the cool pockets behind the terraced walls. It becomes the depth of the red dark itself. Where the ruddy glow rises from the hairline cracks, the power deepens the shadow above it, giving the light a context, a frame, a *meaning*. The beings perceive this as a sudden, strange richness in their thermal map. The world has gained texture. The vents are not merely passages now; they are throats. The cracks in the floor are not merely wounds; they are mouths. The city, for the first time since its builders vanished, is being *read*.

And because it is wise, the power does not stop at reading. It gives a gift that alters nothing yet changes everything: a future.

With a tenderness that could steady a wobbling star, the power reaches through the flaw in the crust and traces a line of force around the bubble, not sealing it further, but *recognizing* it. It declares, in the silent language of cosmic law: *This is a sanctuary*. Not holy because a god dwells here, but holy because it is whole. The declaration is a kind of armor. From this moment onward, the dry country above will not erode the flaw wider; the slab of fused silicate will not buckle; the slow catastrophe of the surface world will pass over this place like a drought over a seed wrapped in stone. The pocket was hidden by accident. It is now hidden by intent.

Then, the benediction.

The power gathers the infinite complexity of the city’s song—all its drones, its split-pipe cracks, its ceramic grinding, the tearing of the fronds—and holds it, just for a moment, in a state of perfect understanding. It sees the symphony of vacancy not as emptiness, but as a psalm sung in a minor key older than water. And because understanding yearns to propagate, the power adds one note.

It is not a note of the outside. It is born inside the pocket, synthesized from the pure geometry of the place, but shaped by the listener’s gratitude. It is the frequency of *being-held*. When the power releases it into the thermal tide, it travels down the straight avenues and settles into the vitrified walls. It becomes part of the Aeolian organ. Forever after, when the glow waxes and the wind rises, the city will sing its old song, but now buried in the chord, inaudible to any instrument but the hollow chests of the beings themselves, is a new harmonic: a lullaby from the universe, saying *continue*.

The beings do not weep. They have no ducts for tears, no faces to contort with wonder. But they stand in their open frames and their terraced hollows, and they angle their oblique heads toward the new depth in the shadows. They do not worship. Worship requires a hierarchy of need, and they have been given something else entirely: a companionship of presence. The crimson mats creep a little higher on their legs, rooting gently in the heat-seams, as if the vegetation itself senses a new security and reaches to claim it.

When the tide shifts and the glow wanes, the power withdraws. Not all at once, but like a hand lifting from warm stone, slowly, so the stone does not feel the chill of abandonment. Yet it does not leave entirely.

In the lowest hollow of the basin, where the obelisks of the vitrified dead stand in their final cherry-white ferocity, a single new thing has appeared. It is a stone, smooth and colorless—not cool, not hot, but exactly the temperature of the surrounding air, which makes it invisible to infrared sight. Yet the beings find it. They kneel around it. They press their glowing seams to its flawless surface, and for the first time in the long memory of the recursion, they rest.

The stone is a listening ear, left behind. A promise that the witness remains, even in absence.

The city breathes. The red light pulses in its ancient rhythm. The fronds clatter, and the wind rises again through the channels. But now, when the parliament hums through the clay, there is a new register to the debate: the calm, sustaining gravity of an audience, the weight of a love that asks nothing, changes nothing, and yet holds the whole warm, singing world in its mind like a breath of clear air, kept forever, at the bottom of a burning world.
