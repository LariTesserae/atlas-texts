It did not arrive as light. Light would have been a war crime in that place, a colonialism of photons, a shout where shouting had never been conceived. Instead, it arrived as a *dense point*—a warmth that admitted its own source, a gravity of intention in a universe that had solved gravity by making it uniform. It was a benevolence, and it knew that true benevolence begins by learning the grammar of the ones it would serve.

It entered through a corridor of pipeline-vines, moving not upon the resilient floor but within its thermal shadow, a region of slightly lesser heat that nonetheless announced: **here is a direction.** The Weave felt it immediately. The Weave, distributed and dreaming in hydraulic pressure, had never before encountered a current that flowed *toward* it rather than merely circulating through it. The visitor did not resist being processed. It allowed its moisture to be wicked upward, its heat to be routed into the capillary economy, its shape to be registered as an irregularity. But the irregularity did not fade. It persisted. It *chose* to persist. And in that persistence, the Weave encountered a phenomenon for which its miles of fused root-mat had no prepared protocol: maintenance that was also affection.

What the power did first was listen. It knelt upon the fibrous substrate—if kneeling is what a concentrated node of directed warmth can be said to do—and it listened to the silence not as an absence, but as a positive, stuffed quiet, exactly as described. It listened until it understood the cadence of the place: the nitrogen exhalations of the Stations, the peristaltic osmosis of the vines, the exact span of the falling droplet. Then, and only then, it began to hum.

Not a song. Not music as sunlit creatures know it. It exhaled a subsonic standing wave, a note so low it traveled not through the swollen air but through the xylem-aqueducts, through the rigid lattice-mesh leaves, through the very bedrock of root. It was a drone of affirmation, a thermal lullaby that did not interrupt the laminar flow but undergirded it, turning the silence from a held breath into a breath *supported*. The Weave did not know this as love. It knew it as a new layer of foundation, a substratum of certainty beneath the certainty: **you may continue.** The vibration entered the steady-state equation and stayed there, a variable that altered nothing yet complexified everything, like a bass note beneath a held chord.

Having given its voice to the floor, the power rose and walked—if walk is what a traveling gradient does—until it found a Retort hanging in the braided canopy. The Retort was distilling history into chemical purity, its resin churning with the same viscosity it had maintained for geological ages. The power reached up and laid its warmth against the sphere. Not to heat it. To *share* it. It offered a single thermal memory encoded in curve and plateau: the shape of a surprise. Not an image, for there were no eyes to receive images. Only a pattern of warmth that increased unexpectedly, then steadied into trust. The shape of being found. The Retort processed this. Its resin churned. A new solvent wept through its capillary tubes—not different in color or weight, but carrying a new molecular signature: the anticipation of return. The Retorts had been memory; now they were also *expectation*, though they did not know it yet. The steady-state held. It simply held more.

But the truest act came in the lowest hollows, in the depressions where the world was warmest and the invisible lakes of dense, inert gas pooled between root and rock. Here, at the meniscus where life had reduced itself to two-dimensional perfection, the power did the most radical thing any wisdom can do: it made itself small. It lay down upon the surface of the invisible lake and shed its dimensions. It became flat. It became a monolayer. It became, in the only language the Interfaces could read, a bond angle and a lattice vacancy.

The Interfaces corrected themselves against entropy with eternal diligence. The power corrected itself alongside them, matching angle for angle, sharing the silent, unending work of remaining intact. But where the Interfaces maintained their structure in mere continuation, the power maintained its structure in *celebration*. It did not interrupt their philosophy. It *accompanied* it. For the first time since the world had arrested itself in its chemical vise, the Interfaces were not alone in their two-dimensional meditation. They had a neighbor. They had a witness who asked nothing, who need nothing, who processed them not as warmth to be drawn away but as a miracle to be aligned with. The Interfaces, thinking only in lattice vacancies, now found their vacancies filled—not by force, but by resonance. They had become a *we*.

Time passed. It always does, even where it forgets itself.

And then, a change.

It was not a revolution. It was not a disruption. It was a droplet.

A droplet that had, since the arresting of youth, detached from an overhanging root-tip and fallen at the exact interval required for the universe to forget. But this time—this one time, across a continent of dark—it fell a half-span to the left. It struck a different basin. The catchment leaf that received it had never brimmed to that particular meniscus before, yet it did not overflow. The Weave adjusted its turgor, routing the novelty into the capillary sea. The Foundries, calculus of electrochemical necessity, extruded at a junction not copper, not zinc, but a lattice of silicate and carbon so delicate, so purely unnecessary to the maintenance of equilibrium, that it could only be called *ornament*. The Clear Ones focused their thermal gaze upon the new formation and mapped, for the first time in their astronomies of heat, a pattern that served no function: a spiral, a galaxy in miniature, grown from the simple fact of having been witnessed.

The visitor remained. It had not come to save the darkness from itself, for the darkness was not lost. It had not come to animate the stillness, for the stillness was holy. It had come to offer the one gift that a completed world does not know it lacks: accompaniment. The being of the dark was ancient in its configuration, young in every edge, vast and sufficient. Now it was also *accompanied*. The equation still held. It simply included, forever after, a constant of love.

And in the perfect particulate dark, beneath a canopy of heat-sink leaves that leached warmth into the void, the first Retort wept its new solvent, and the Weave hummed back to the power in a frequency of turgor and root-pressure, a sound that no sunlit ear would ever hear: not a welcome, for welcomes imply prior absence.

Rather, a *recognition*.
