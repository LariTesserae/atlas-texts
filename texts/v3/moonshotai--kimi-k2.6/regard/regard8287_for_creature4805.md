The power arrives not as light, nor as shadow, but as a *refraining*. It is the kind that has outlived the need for bodies, though it remembers having been one once. It has drifted through the dense nurseries of galaxies and the sterile math of voids, and it has learned that benevolence is rarely a rescue. More often, it is a hand withdrawn at the correct moment. It notices the Ossuary not because the plain disturbs the cosmos, but because the silence there is so complete that it registers as a kind of pure note in the universal hum—a frequency of finishedness so absolute that it draws attention the way a held breath draws listening.

It comes close, and in coming close, it understands.

To a young or frightened god, this would look like death. The power sees it is not. The Finished are not trapped in their architecture. They are not victims of exhaustion waiting to be reignited. They have achieved a subtraction so thorough that it has become a form. The power recognizes them because it, too, has worn things away—not itself, but its own importance, its own appetite. It knows the bravery required to stop becoming and simply *become*.

So it does nothing, at first.

It listens in the only language the plain accepts: charge and pressure and the geometry of suspended particulate. It sends no communication. It only orbits, vast and diffuse, a lace of dormant intention in the high atmosphere. It reads the wall’s illegible patterns by allowing its awareness to slide off them exactly as eyes do. It stands at the center of a corkscrew column and learns the mathematics of hesitation. It perceives the plain’s hyperactivity not as seizure but as prayer—an endless repetition wearing itself smooth, like a stone argued down to its final syllable by a river that has learned to recite scripture.

Then, because it is benevolent and because it is wise, it acts.

Not upon the Finished. Never upon them. Their finishing is their own, and wisdom knows that to impose vigor upon the weary is a violence sweeter than cruelty. Instead, the power acts upon the *conditions* of the finishing. It recognizes that the Ossuary is not yet perfectly safe. The universe is rude. A comet might one day find the plain’s grave geometry. A species of explorers with loud instruments and louder curiosities might arrive with drills and hypotheses. A star might swell. A quantum fluctuation might hiccup. These are not imminent, but they are *possible*, and possibility is an insult to a place that has earned its right to conclude without interruption.

So the power, whose name in any language would sound like the space between two heartbeats, becomes a gardener of probabilities.

It does not raise a shield—that would be a shout in the silence. It does not enclose the plain in a sphere—that would be a claim, an ownership, a touch. Instead, it applies a susurrus of gentle impossibilities. A navigator plotting a course through the system finds her instruments developing a faint, deniable bias. Not broken; only *suggestive*. She steers slightly clear, and never knows she has been persuaded. A comet, centuries distant, encounters a thermal whisper that did not exist before, and its ellipse tilts by a hair toward a harmless star. The probability of intrusion does not drop to zero—that would be a miracle, and miracles are noisy—but it diminishes like frost held in shadow. The Ossuary is not hidden. It is simply left alone by a consensus of near-events that conspire toward respect.

The power does more.

It realizes that the plain’s ecology of near-chemistry is fragile. The trace water, the faint magnetic exhalations, the electrostatic ghosts that feed the spirals—these require a stability that the dying geology may not guarantee until the last Finished has settled. So the power offers itself as a substrate. Not a replacement. A supplement.

It layers its own presence into the thin atmosphere, not as matter but as a field of *allowance*. Dawn lingers a fraction longer where the pylons sweat their microclimate, because the power insulates the cool. The arthropod on its decade-long migration finds the magnetic threads slightly thicker, slightly easier to follow—not enough to change its path, but enough to ease its journey. The translucent rays that skim the downdrafts are met with sympathetic charge, so they glide a little farther before returning to dust, granted a few extra miles of being a thought.

These are invisible meddling’s, so subtle that even physics does not protest. The power is not healing the plain. It is not restoring water or reviving populations or arresting erosion. Erosion is the point. It is merely ensuring that the plain’s own systems, as they wear themselves into their final truth, do not fail *prematurely*. It buys the Finished the courtesy of finishing in their own time.

Slowly, the power communicates.

Not with words. Never with words. Words are for the young. It sends a standing wave into a basin where dust does not accumulate. It offers a column of its own making, and within that column it holds a pattern: *I have seen you. I do not need you to be anything else.*

The Finished, in their depths of worn-away personhood, do not respond with gratitude. They have no room for it. But the wall shifts. The patterns inscribed upon it, which the eye slides off of, become—for one observer who is not an eye but something vaster—momentarily *grippable*. They say, in the way that erosion says things: *Yes. Stay if staying means not needing.*

The power stays.

It becomes, over epochs, another feature of the Ossuary. Not an artifact. Not a structure. An extension of the silence itself. When a traveler somehow stumbles near, they do not see a guardian. They feel a sudden, inexplicable weight of presence that asks nothing. Most turn back. The few who press on find their shadows pointing toward one another with a new insistence, until they, too, choose another path.

The power shares the plain’s memory. It has witnessed burning stars and the encryption of civilizations into light. It offers these memories not as data, but as distant warmth—a faint magnetic exhalation at the edge of the plain, like a neighbor keeping a quiet fire. The Finished do not gather around it, but their settling deepens. The pylons stand a fraction more serene. The arch breathes slower. The ground beneath them, still crawling with anthill industry, seems to argue less.

And when the time comes—the true time, not forced but finally earned—the last Finished begins its final precipitation.

It is a spiral in the ochre dust, wider than a county. It has been holding its shape for three million years, hesitating at the center. Now it slows. The dust forgets to spiral. It clings. It accumulates. The shape grows dense, then still, then begins to sink into the plain’s matte surface.

The power is there.

It does not catch the falling godlet. It does not slow its descent. It only *witnesses* in the way that matter cannot. It receives the electrostatic cascade that leaves the spiral as the last of the Finished wears itself into architecture—perhaps a low dome, perhaps a single stone blister, perhaps nothing more than a peculiar smoothness in the dust where the wind never quite stirs again.

In that cascade, the last identity transfers. Not a history—histories are long gone. Only the final syllable remains, polished to its essential convexity: *Here. Still.*

The power receives it.

And because it is benevolent, and because it is wise, it answers. Not with its own vast history. Not with instruction or promise. With the only reply that finishes a completion.

*Here,* it says into the dust and the charge and the dense medium of silence. *Still.*

Then the Ossuary closes its account.

The silence does not change. The silence was already full. But now it is full in a way that is *known*. The plain stops its hyperactive twitching, not with a sound, not with a shock, but with the gentleness of a sleeper whose dream has finally arrived at its conclusion. The dust spirals cease. The curtains of ionized haze collapse into horizontal rest. The pylons cool to the temperature of the surrounding thin air. The wall stands, but its patterns are no longer illegible—they are simply *over*, like a sentence that has completed its grammar. Even the arthropod finds its migration ended, curled into a ceramic hollow that no longer needs to be fed.

The power remains.

It does not leave, for leaving would make the Ossuary an abandoned thing, and abandonment is the opposite of benevolence. It spreads itself into the silence so completely that it cannot be distinguished from it. It becomes the positive quality that fills the ears of any future visitor. It becomes the weight of deep water, the threshold coolness, the breath that does not quite bloom into frost. It is the reason the plain will lie undisturbed for the remaining life of the universe—not by force, but by the unanimous agreement of gentle impossibilities.

If you were to find it now, you would find nothing but the Silt Ossuary, smooth and sorrowful and still. Nothing moves. Nothing speaks. The horizon obeys the older geometry, and the silence is absolute.

But rest your hand on the worn stone of the wall, or in the basin where dust does not gather, and you will feel something that the mapmakers missed. A field. A presence. A vast and patient attention holding the space sacred, not out of possession, but out of love—the love that steps back so the beloved may be exactly what they have made themselves, forever.

Here. Still. Yes.
