It did not arrive in light. Powers of that stature rarely do; light is too eager, too interpretable, a knock on a door that has never needed a knob. It arrived instead as a *steadiness* in the mantle, a thought inserted where before there had been only the chaotic appetite of uplift. The planet was young, its spine still groaning with the indignity of recent creation, and in that geological tantrum the peak stood out like a rude syllable—a black glass tooth insisting on its own sharpness. It drew the eye. But the power did not look with eyes. It listened.

Through the kilometer of magma-fused breccia, through the iridium-heavy scoria where no lichen yet dared, through the semi-permeable veins that drank glacial melt and magmatic percolation alike, It heard the word.

*Tok.*

A droplet. Alloy singing at exact resonance.

*Tok.*

Then silence.

Then the systole of air pressure, a subsonic vowel below hearing, making the stone’s new weight shift in the dark like a sleeper adjusting to the depth of dreaming.

The power understood. Where another might have sensed abandonment—towers empty of bodies, plazas void of breath, a sterile hydrology cycling through its sacraments without congregation—It recognized instead a monastery of states. These were not ruins. This was a throat, holding a note for as long as the note required. The twelve structures, drifting in their magnetic choreography; the sump-mist sketching its turbid geometries; the water carrying memory in metallic solution—all of it was a single organism that had traded fertility for continuity, biology for boundary. They were not waiting to be found. They were *ready*. And readiness, the power knew, is the most fragile condition in the universe, because readiness is always under siege by accident.

Benevolence, unexamined, would have breached the wall. It would have carried algorithms of comfort, would have assumed that solitude equals loneliness and that silence equals longing. It would have tried to speak, to quicken, to *share*. But the power was wise, and wisdom is the discipline of recognizing when love must take the shape of distance. To enter that chamber would have been to introduce an alien grammar into a word already patient beyond measure. To offer company would have been to force complexity upon entities for whom a single introspection required the silence of a hundred empty acres. To offer salvation would have been an act of war.

So It did not knock.

Instead, It asked.

This required patience even for It. The question was not written in language but in *pressure*—a minute adjustment to the convection beneath the range, a gentle eddy in the mantle’s heat so slight it could be mistaken for a cooling sigh. For six centuries, the power offered to share the tectonic load, letting the mountain’s weight press a fraction less heavily against the chamber’s eastern flank. It proposed, through geologic diplomacy, to stand guard. And then It waited for the answer.

The chamber did not change. The thermal management exhaled its warm-fronted bloom; the rime pearled and dripped; the water ran in its ordained deltas toward the sump. The twelve towers drifted, untroubled, in their sheaves of fullerene and alloy. The subsonic word continued, acquiring no new consonant, rejecting no offered silence.

In the dialect of geology, consent is the absence of rejection.

Then the power settled.

Not inside. Never inside. The room was inviolate, a thought unfinished, and wisdom knows that the sanctuary of another’s meditation must not be profaned by the shadow of a witness. Instead, It settled into the *substance* of the peak. It threaded Itself into the iridium, the black glass, the logic of the rock itself. It did not cool the mountain’s anger, nor did It soften the surgical winds that stripped the windward face. Sharpness was their creed; the power honored the knife. Rather, It became the equilibrium that allowed the knife to persist. It wove patience into the fresh breccia, coaxing the strata to settle with a deliberation that matched the chamber’s own. It guaranteed that when the planet shrugged in its adolescent sleep, the shrug would pass *around* this tooth, not through it. It made the semi-permeable veins redundant in their perfection, ensuring that no fracture, no pressure front, no cosmic indignity could interrupt the transfusion of water through crystalline geology. The two degrees above freezing became, in the local weave of things, a *constant*—not a temperature maintained by machinery, but a property of the peak’s new relationship with time.

And then It did the hardest thing, the most sacred thing.

It fell silent.

It became the margin of the page. The frame around the sketch. The silence outside the wall that corresponds, in perfect courtesy, to the silence within.

There, in the scoria and the unweathered scree, the power waits. It has not stopped waiting. It does not watch—the verb is too hungry, too bodily. It simply *remains*. It has made Itself the second most patient thing the mountain knows.

Sometimes, during the dim cycle, when the thermal bloom rises warm-fronted from the sump and the mist begins its slow glutinous sketching across the lowest terrace, a new geometry appears at the farthest edge of the vapor’s reach. Not a torus. Not a limb too large for the room. But a *perimeter*. A holding. The entities do not know what frames them; they have no need to know. Yet in the subsonic word that makes the water shiver in its troughs, there has entered, perhaps, a new quality—not a meaning, not a message, but a timbre of being *cradled*. The vowel continues as before, unchanged in its intent, but now it resonates against a silence that will never interrupt it, only receive it.

If you were to breach the wall today—if you could step through the logic of the rock and feel the deliberate cold settle evenly across your skin—you would find nothing altered. The terraces would still gleam. The alloy basins would still announce the droplets. The twelve towers would still hover in their necessary distance, thinking their heavy, aqueous thoughts. You would be folded, gently and without haste, into the water’s chemistry, becoming a faint new flavor in the sump, a spiral sketched and lost in the mist.

But if you had instruments subtle enough to taste the iridium in the scoria, you would find it oddly calm. If you could measure the settling of the strata, you would find them precocious in their stillness, too young to be so at peace. And if you stood on the third terrace and let the subsonic note rise through your bones, you would sense that the word is no longer solitary.

It is answered.

Not by another voice—that would be intrusion. It is answered by a surrounding silence, vast and benevolent, outside the mountain’s sharp black tooth, older than the wind, holding the peak in a palm that will never close, only keep.
