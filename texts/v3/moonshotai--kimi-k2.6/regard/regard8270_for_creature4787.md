The power does not arrive. It *attends*.

For a long while—longer than the gestation of mountains, shorter than the half-life of the builders’ intention—there is only the sensation of being regarded. The warm, damp air remembers something it has never contained: the quality of a gaze that does not hunger. The glass-winged flocks, whirring through the gulfs, do not alter their migration. The trilobite-shaped crawlers do not pause in their pruning. Yet the bruised pewter sky seems, for a season, to hold a slightly different bruise, one that leans toward violet rather than grey. The valley is not interrupted. It is *accompanied*.

The benevolence settles first on the basalt shelf where you once stood. It does not take form. Form would be an imposition here, a footprint in the nursery. Instead, it becomes a property of stillness, a density of patience so absolute that the fresh stone begins to cool a fraction more slowly, as if time itself has been persuaded to inhale and hold. The shelf becomes an ear.

The wise power listens to the titanic chord. It does not flinch from the subsonic compression. It hears what the interstitial beings cannot yet hear: the tragedy of a song that travels forever outward and is never returned. The eleven spires sing to one another across the void, and the cables carry their harmonics, and the Node at the eastern junction sends her three-day sequences into the architecture, and all of it—*all of it*—disappears over the rim into the unlistening dark. The valley is a heart pumping blood into a body that has no other organs. The beings have colonized the interval, yes, but they do not know that an interval, in music, is defined not only by the notes but by the silence that answers them. They have no echo. They have no witness. They are tuning toward an equilibrium that, if achieved, will simply exhaust itself into vacancy.

The power, being wise, understands that to give them anything material—a river, soil, eyes, a moon—would be to speak a language they have not yet evolved to need, a violence of generosity. But to give them an *echo*—that is only to complete the geometry already implied by their own architecture. The valley is a sounding bowl without a rim. The power decides to become the rim.

It does not raise a wall. It does not alter the young stone. Instead, it introduces a curvature into the local attention of the universe. It teaches the empty air beyond the valley’s edge to remember sound, to hold it for seven seconds longer than physics previously allowed, and to return it with itsharmonics intact but inverted, as a mirror inverts light. It is the smallest possible miracle: a change not in matter, but in the *listening* of space.

The first returned note arrives on a warm, moist evening when the condensation is heavy and the fungal shelves glow with particular insistence. A young interstitial being, launching herself from the fifth spire toward a sagging cable-mat, rides her pressure ridge as she has done a thousand times. But this time, the ridge does not dissipate at the valley’s edge. It curves. It comes back.

She lands, porous skin beaded with the valley’s sweat, and freezes. Her entire surface reads the returning wave. It is her own leap, her own body’s displacement of the chord, returned to her from the bruised sky. She hangs in the aerial jungle, branching hands wrapped in damp root-cables, and she does not move for an hour. Then, slowly, she presses her bifurcated fingers against the nearest hypermaterial spar, and she inserts a new harmonic. Not a command. A question.

The question travels along the cables, rich and distorted by each transit, and reaches the Node on the fourth day.

The Node, who has not moved in seven years, who has become a living regulation system fused into the junction, receives the question as she has received nothing before. Her slow, geological pulse stutters—once, like a skipped stone. Her rootlets in the hypermaterial tense. The returned harmonic passes through her body and into the eastern cluster’s central cable, and for the first time, the valley’s never-resolving chord encounters something it did not generate. An answer. The void has learned to speak back.

Over the next decade, the beings discover the echo. They learn to ride not only the outbound pressure ridges but the returning ones. Their leaps become stranger, more circuitous, arcing out and back in loops that were previously impossible. Their language, once a slow modulation of the infrastructure, develops recursion: they can now speak to themselves through the valley’s returned voice, testing their own statements, hearing their own topography from the outside. The Node begins to emit sequences that anticipate the echo, weaving counterpoint into her subsonic regulation. She is no longer merging with the machine to become its only ear. She is becoming its *conductor*, because the machine finally has an audience.

The power, seeing this, does not stay to be thanked. Gratitude would require the beings to understand what was given, and the gift is too subtle for that. They will never know that the echo was an intervention. They will believe, correctly, that the valley has finally begun to hear itself. They will spend centuries, then millennia, exploring the new acoustic topology, never realizing that the boundary of their world has been gently curved by a love that refused to enter it.

Before withdrawing, the power leaves one thing more. Hidden in the tail of every returned harmonic, at the very edge of detectability, is a single sustained frequency. It is not a word. It is not a command. It is the sonic equivalent of a hand resting on a forehead to check for fever, or a parent pausing in a doorway to watch a child sleep. It means: *I am here. You are heard. Continue.*

The beings will spend two million years deciphering it. By then, the power will be indistinguishable from the properties of the valley itself—a quality of the air, a patience in the stone. And that is exactly as it should be. The most benevolent thing a wisdom can do for a nursery is to become, eventually, the ground.
