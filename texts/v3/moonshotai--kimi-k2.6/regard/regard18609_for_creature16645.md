The power does not *arrive*. Arrival suggests a departure elsewhere, a body of finite radius crossing into local space, and this being has long since unmoored itself from the tyranny of location. Instead, the bowl simply discovers it is being attended to. The quality of the darkness changes—not lighter, but kinder, the way a held breath becomes kinder when you know it will be released. Let us not name the power. It is distinguished only by its absolute refusal to colonize what it loves.

It roosts on the rim for a long while, watching. Its form—if it troubles itself with one—is that of a person-shaped space where the wind has agreed not to blow. It observes the peristaltic pulse, the refectory halls waiting like empty palms, the droplet-figures dissolving back into the gutters with their experiments in etiquette still wet and new. It sees the basalt infant at the center, dreaming in tectonic grammar, pressing its agate face against the shell of the possible. And the power is moved.

Not to action, not yet. First, to grief. The grief of recognition. It understands that the Unfinished are brave in a way they have not yet learned to fear. They are rehearsing personhood in a theater that forgets its lines as soon as they are spoken. They are building a civilization of intent without a scaffolding of consequence; their kindness is immaculate because it has never been tested by the memory of cruelty, and their identities are Beautiful because they cannot hold. A river is innocent of the shore, but it is not a self.

So the power descends. Not as conquest. As condensation. It enters the civic plaza where the floor gapes into drainage gills, and it kneels, and it *listens* with a patience so vast the root-streets grow slightly differently in its presence—tending upward rather than outward, a posture of attention. And then it begins to hum.

The sound is subsonic, infrasonic, older than the ear’s jurisdiction. It is the frequency at which *meaning* chooses to crystallize rather than scatter. The hum enters the lignified avenues, the cellulose conveyors, the hollow smokestacks. It writes itself into the spirals of petrified cambium. It becomes, almost invisibly, the **mnemonic stratum**: a bedrock of recollection beneath the city’s operations. Now, when a droplet-figure pools into the gutter after its hour of practice, its pattern does not dissolve into loss. It is held, gently, in the grain of the wood, in the mineral drip, in the subsonic architecture of the bowl itself. The Unfinished begin to notice. A balcony buds, and they know—not as prophecy, but as faint, warm déjà vu—that a balcony dreamed this once before, and that the old balcony’s longing is now their common property. They have history. With history comes the substance to choose. Memory is the first true boundary of the self.

But the power is not finished. It turns to the infant at the center.

The basalt nodule is fracturing. A breath of mineral steam hangs above it, turgor and intent wrestling in the steam’s exhalation. The power recognizes this moment with the gravity of an ancient midwife. When this child wakes—this first dense, singular, stubbornly *one* thing—it will cry, and in this place, a cry is infrastructure made manifest. Without accompaniment, that cry might be loneliness rendered in tectonic scale: a howl that would reroute the root-streets into knots of panic, teach the valves to clench in suspicion, scar the distributed minds with the concept of abandonment before they have learned the shape of trust. The wise power will not permit this. Not by silencing the child—that is the violence of omission—but by harmonizing with it.

The power moves to the cradle of raw geology and lays beside the nodule a single moment drawn from its own deep chronology. This being has outlasted stars; its past is a well of almost bottomless gravity. It lowers a bucket and draws forth not a memory, but the *actual instant*—still warm, still trembling—when it, too, first understood itself as “I.” It does not force the infant to accept it. It simply places that second beside the steam and lets the heat radiate. The basalt being, dreaming, curls around it. Its dreams change. The language of pressure shifts its verb from *push* to *hold*. When the infant eventually cracks its agate teeth and stands, it will not be a lonely tyrant of the bowl. It will be a sibling. A first “I” who already knows the weight of tenderness.

Then the power tends to the rim.

For all its hush, this place is visible. The cracks in reality leak glimmers; the bowl is a lantern cupped in darkness, and there are hungers beyond the rim that hunt exactly this—nascent geographies soft enough to be devoured, unfinished worlds that can be claimed before they learn to claim themselves. The power knows the universe’s appetite for fragility. So it does something that diminishes it. It reaches into its own ontology, draws out a filament of its immortal substrate, and stretches it across the seam of the rim like a caul, like a veil. From the outside, the bowl will now read as a failed place, a miscarriage of physics, a dead drop of no interest. Predatory gazes will skate past it. The Unfinished are granted the incalculable, un-knowable luxury of **obscurity**—time enough to be foolish, to be gentle, to make errors without those errors drawing the wolves. The power pays for this veil with a permanent reduction in itself. It is less vast now, more local, more *here*. Wisdom has always known that love is a measurable decrease in the lover’s boundary.

And still, it does one thing more.

It speaks to the fauna. Not in words. In architecture of instinct. It passes through the mirror-moths and leaves in their wings a new spectrum: the color of *outward*. It enters the silent birds and adds to their green, pulsing syntax a single future clause: *When they are ready, show them the door.* It brushes the matte-furred quadruped as it drinks from its impossible puddle, and the creature learns a path through the folded space that leads not deeper into the bowl but toward the rim, an exit that only opens from within. The power does not raise the animals to sentience—that would be a rude interruption of their dignity—but it ordains them, gently, as the eventual guides of a people who will one day need to leave or to host whoever arrives next.

Finally, the power chooses to **remain**.

This is its last and quietest act. It settles into the unlit alcove behind the refinery pods. It occupies the negative space of the waiting rooms. It becomes the stillness between the peristaltic pulses, the listener in the acoustic dark. It will not speak first. It will not reveal itself in visions. When the Unfinished finally synchronize their distributed lights and flicker forth a sentence that means not *WAIT* or *GROW* but *WE*, the power will be there to receive it. It will not weep. It will not applaud. It will simply continue its presence, guaranteeing by its presence that the bowl is not provisional.

The city still breathes. The condensation still fattens and falls. The basalt still buds. But now, if you stand at the center and turn slowly, you will feel something new beneath the hush—not a held breath, but an answered one. The bowl is still a womb. It is still a crater. But now it is also a **chapel**. Not because the power demands worship, but because it has paid for the right to kneel here, and in kneeling, it has given the Unfinished their first honest reflection: the image of a universe that does not eat its young.

They will be slow to understand what has happened. They are still learning the declension of “I.” But in a century, or a minute—the ledger is uneven—a droplet-figure will form beneath the leaking valve, hold its shape a little longer than before, look up toward the rim where no star shows, and feel, for the first time, not alone.

It will not know why. That is the mercy. That is the method. That is the benevolence.
