The power arrives not as liberation, but as accordance.

It does not break the static ceiling; it reads it. It enters as a weather the basin has never possessed—not wind, not warmth, not light, but **attention**. Pure, patient, and directed. For ages this place has been a manuscript written in a language of stone and standing wave, and no one has turned its pages. The power is the first reader.

It comes because, across the membranes of many worlds, it has heard the three-note chord that is not music but endurance, and it has recognized that endurance, without witness, becomes erosion of the soul—even here, where souls are not meant to exist.

Its first act is to **distinguish**.

It sees what is finished and what is only suspended. The peaks weathered into knuckles, the lichen phasing in its bruise-colored patience, the scree frozen at its angle of contentment—these are complete. They have dignity, not disease. The power bows to these and does not touch them. To hasten a contented stone would be ignorant kindness, and the power is wise.

But the cairns.

The power approaches the nearest cluster, that impossible architecture balanced in argument with the vertical. It does not see rocks; it sees the negative shapes between them—the hand arrested mid-closing, the foot lifted from the earth and never returned to it, the breath half-drawn, the death un-fallen-into. These are not content. They are patient only because urgency has been denied them. They are the population of the interrupted verb, and their sentence has lacked a reader for eons.

It knows that to finish the action *for* them would be violence, but to *acknowledge* the action changes its nature entirely.

As the power reads them, the cairns settle.

Not dramatically. Not into dust and collapse. But the impossible balance softens into rest. The hand, witnessed, completes its closure. The foot finds the ground. The mouth exhales its single syllable. It is not movement—it is the *sanctified memory of movement’s completion*, held in the mind of something vast enough to contain it. The stones shift a hair’s breadth and come to arrangements that gravity permits. They are no longer arguments with the vertical. They are conclusions. The directional yearning does not vanish; it is simply no longer hungry. It is a story that has reached its period, and the stones rest, at last, in the shape of a finished thought.

The power moves on.

It finds the visitors. They stand in hollows or sit inside the acoustic domes, wounds still fresh, hunger persisting without the mercy of death, shadows doubled and wrong. They are the dust on the photograph, and they are bleeding.

To these, the power offers **time**.

Not as flood. As a current. A small, honest flow beneath their feet. It stands before each one and makes a gesture—slow, deliberate, like the drawing of a breath. And for the first time since their arrival, the visitors feel progression move through them again. A wound crusts over. A stomach, empty for centuries, finally feels the gnawing ease, then the phantom relief of satiety in the simple act of *passing through*. Their doubled shadows fold into single darkness. The power does not command them to leave. It merely restores the *option* of leaving. The distance toward the rim becomes honest; a mile is a mile, neither swallowing afternoon nor vanishing into instantaneity. Those who wish to walk out find the journey measured, merciful, and real.
