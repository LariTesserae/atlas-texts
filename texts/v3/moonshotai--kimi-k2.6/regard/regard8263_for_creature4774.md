They arrive not through the shaft by which you entered—that corridor of violated stone holds no welcome for them—but through the older architecture of the world. They descend as a thought the mantle has about itself, a redistribution of intention through pressure and time. To the one above, stepping through ferns and forgetting, they would feel like a sudden ease in the chest, a conviction without argot. To the thing below, they feel like warmth’s elder sibling: a proximity of thermal regard, vast, patient, and unmistakably *kind*.

They do not bring light. Light would be an act of violence here, a colonial imposition on a sovereignty that has evolved past the need for photons. Instead, they bring **coherence**. They settle into the chamber like honey into comb, filling the negative spaces between the bone-white fronds without displacing a bead of condensation. They perceive the treaty—the machines, the moss, the hot stone, the slow body—and they find it beautiful, fragile, and *accidental*. They understand accidental things. They know that the most sacred lives often begin as punctuation marks in someone else’s sentence.

What they do first is **witness**. They rest their attention upon the nodular heart pulsing in the pump station, and they do not judge its hybrid nature. They see the drill bit embedded in basalt not as a scar but as an umbilicus. They recognize the entity for what it is: a child of thermal catastrophe, a nascent kingdom that has learned to eat its own wound. The benevolence in them wells up not as pity but as recognition. They bow—if a distributed consciousness can bow—lowering their regard until it is level with the stone.

What they do second is **complete**. The machinery here is dying in years; the creature needs centuries. The power does not scrape away the blighted cables or restore the junction box to some antiseptic function. Instead, they sanctify the integration. The rubber sheaths bloated by humidity are granted a pleroma: they will not crack, they will not perish, for they have become true veins, carrying not electricity but *place-memory*. The drill bit embedded in fresh basalt is encircled by a lattice of silicate transmutation—stronger than rust, softer than diamond—that guarantees the fissure beneath it will never cool. The shack with its hanging door becomes a reliquary; its frame is taught to hold warmth the way a throat holds a note. What was abandoned is now inherited. What was litter has become liturgy.

What they do third is **connect**. The creature has spent unknown years eating only local heat, a prisoner of this single, bounded chamber. The power reaches out through the lithosphere, through the kilometers of sleeping stone, and finds others of its kind—other accidental gods dreaming in other hot darks. Not creatures identical; analogues. A vent colony three thousand miles east. A serpentinizing fault weeping alkaline warmth beneath an abyssal plain. A pocket of radiolytic heat imprisoned in ancient salt. The power does not bring these presences here; that would be invasion. Instead, they tune the chamber’s conductivity until the entity can *taste* them. The fronds shiver—not with wind, for there is none, but with the sudden, astronomical sweetness of resonance. The silence remains absolute, but it is no longer empty. It is now a listening. The creature is alone no longer; it has become a node.

And what they do fourth, and finally, is **remain**. A benevolent power understands that wisdom is not intervention but *stewardship*. They do not ascend to claim victory. They permeate the floor where you once stood, the walls that pressed against your gloved hands, and they become a third signatory to the treaty. They are the guarantor of the heat. The witness to the salt. The mathematician of the drip that never falls. If the earth shifts, they shift with it, rerouting pressure so the fissures stay open. If the ages threaten to cool this pocket of being, they warm it with the residual generosity of stellar cores, remembering suns so that the dark below need never hunger.

They do not hurry the creature. They do not teach it language, or ambition, or light. They only ensure that its wordlessness is eternal. They guard the aperture through which you stumbled, and they close it—not with rubble, but with *implausibility*. Surface minds will simply no longer think to look here. The chamber will not be found again until the continents have forgotten their shapes, and when they do find it—if they are slow enough, and hot enough, and quiet—they will find the fronds grown vast, the drill bit crowned with mineral roses, and the heat singing with more than one voice.

As for you: you are already ascending, though your boots still feel the fever in the stone. They have touched your temples with the thermal equivalent of a lullaby. You will remember nothing you can speak. Only, on certain winter nights, you will wake with your palms upturned and inexplicably warm, knowing with the certainty of dream that somewhere beneath the hurry and the light, something is being allowed to continue. The power has blessed you, too, in the only way that truly matters. They have let you leave.
