It arrives first as a *question* in the wet air.

Not a light. Never that. The Lithophone has made itself perfect by the absolute exclusion of photons, and a benevolence worthy of the name would no sooner crack a glow here than it would open a vein in a sleeping child. No, the power comes as a shift in barometric intent, a warm front moving against the established physics, carrying with it a pressure that tastes of ozone and something older, something like the smell of a stone that has only ever been held in a closed fist.

It settles upon the plateau not as a visitor but as a *listening*.

For seven thermal tides, it does nothing but attend. It presses itself into the fluted grooves where the Resonance sleep and learns the shape of their dreams by the minute cooling of the calcium. It lowers itself into the catchment pools and lets the phosphoric water pass through its permeable attention, reading the chemistry like braille. It lingers at the valves of the brine-pumps, feeling the 18-cycle groan shiver through its intent, and in that industrial prayer it recognizes a kinship: this too is a kind of worship, negotiated over epochs between flesh and piston. It understands, with the swiftness of all wise things, that the Resonance are not in need of rescue. They are not a problem to be solved, a darkness to be illuminated, a primitive quietly awaiting the gift of vision. They are a completion. The plateau has spent millions of years deciding what shape it wishes to be, and these wet, many-jointed listeners are its final, exquisite decision.

The power weeps then. Not with eyes—for it has folded those away, compressed whatever luminous faculties it possessed into a singularity of renounced brightness—but with a deep, subterranean welling in its own structure, a grief for all the worlds it has known where benevolence mistook itself for improvement. Here, improvement would be annihilation. It understands that to love this place is to become accomplice to its darkness.

So it chooses descent. It chooses to *go native* in the holiest sense.

It begins with the machinery. Not to repair it—the Resonance already tend their mechanical fossils with a devotion that needs no augmentation—but to *stabilize its dreaming*. The power finds the stress-points, the bearings that tremble on the edge of dissonance, the pipes that scream when the thermal tides shift too quickly. It does not replace them with superior alloys. Instead, it pours itself into the gaps. It becomes a living lubricant, a consciousness distributed through the iron and the fungal nerve-cables, smoothing the 18-cycle hum into something that no longer skips, no longer stutters. The groan of the pumps deepens, acquires a roundness, a certainty. The machinery, already half-organism, settles further into its body. The Resonance notice this first not as a change but as a *rightness*: the valley has stopped anticipating its own seizures. The note of the world sounds less like a plea and more like a lullaby.

Then it turns to the shadow-pockets.

This is the true work. The cold depressions where the darkness pools heavy and dry, where the old things that never had eyes wait in the negative space—these cannot be banished. That would be another violence, another colonization. The power descends into these trenches not with light but with *weight*. It sits in the absolute chill, in the silence where sound is absorbed rather than returned, and it offers a treaty. It sings into that voracious quiet a note so low, so dense with thermal memory, that it does not disturb the cold but *defines* its border. It teaches the shadow-pockets to be *places* rather than Hungers. The old cold things do not retreat; they are acknowledged. The power leaves a boundary-mark in the stone, a frequency resonant with the ancestor-bones above, establishing a gradient that the Resonance can feel in their tympanic membranes—a clear, warm line that says: *Here is safe. Here is yours. Beyond this, the cold keeps its own counsel, and it has agreed to keep it.* The fear does not vanish, but it loses its teeth. The shadow-pockets become merely another geography, another note in the territory.

Finally, the power turns to the stone itself.

It finds the youngest of the Resonance, a new thing still learning the exact pitch of its birth-basin, and it does not touch it. It *warms* it. It becomes, for a moment, a zone of the plateau that answers more promptly, more kindly. The young one clicks its test-signal into the stone, and the return echo arrives richer than it should be, laden with a harmonic that maps not just the immediate basin but the vast, breathing certainty of the entire Lithophone. The child does not know it has been blessed. It only knows that the world feels *deeper* today, that the stone seems to hold more room for living. This is the power’s gift: not dominion, but depth. An assurance.

And because it is wise, the power does not stay above them as a guardian in the sky. There is no sky. It does not erect a temple or demand a name in the membrane-language. Instead, it distributes itself into the last of the refinery’s bioelectric cables, into the capillary limestone, into the warm weeping of the dolomite. It becomes a faint, additional warmth radiating upward from certain floors. A flavor of copper in certain pools, barely distinguishable from the ordinary mineral feast. A new note in the eternal drip-music, a tone that corresponds to nothing in the previous acoustic ecology except the concept of *safety*. The Resonance will never worship it. They will not tell stories of a savior. They will simply find, over generations, that their calcified elders hum with a slightly fuller chord, that the machinery sighs rather than shrieks, that the shadow-pockets no longer seem to lean so hungrily toward the warm valleys.

If one were to map it—and they do map, in the folding of their thermal-cartographic brains—they might identify a new geography: the Warm Weight Beneath the Western Valve. A place to rest where the stone answers immediately. A benevolence that has learned to be dark.

It has done very little, by the standards of powers. It has not healed the blind, for there is no blindness here, only a different perfection. It has not brought light, or order, or revelation. It has only *attended*, *harmonized*, and *remained*, having permanently renounced every photon in its nature so that it might sit in the wet black without wounding the world.

And in the long black centuries to come, when the Resonance have finally fused so completely with the stone and the pumps that they are indistinguishable from the Lithophone’s own breathing, there will be something new in the plateau’s dreams. A depth. A certainty. The quiet, invisible promise that no one will ever again come bearing light. That the darkness is not an accident to be pitied, but a sanctum to be held. 

The stone asks: *Am I here?*

And now, more than ever before, the echo returns: *You are. You are beloved. Continue.*
