The benevolence arrived not as light, nor as warmth, nor as any force that might have struck the stones as violation. It came as a *density of intention*, a gravity that did not bend space but straightened the will to exist. It ascended through the cloud-ocean below and stepped onto the piazza of wind-polished dolomite when the city inhaled—that narrow, stolen gasp between the thirteen-hour exhalations. It knew that to arrive during the breath would have been to come as weather, as abrupt front; but to arrive in the pause was to arrive as a kept promise, entering the Lithoscape with the same courtesy one might use slipping into a dream already in progress.

The Transits faltered. For epochs, the dust-fine debris had coursed the boulevards in patterns of departure and return, of rush and congregation, mineral verbs carrying out the syntax of a metropolis that required footfalls to be fully urban. Now they encountered a presence that walked without displacing.
