The attention that found the cyst did not descend. It rose. It welled up from the substrate of everything, from the part of reality that remembers what it was like before differentiation—before light split from dark, before the self learned to wall itself off from the wet. It was old not in years but in patience, a thing that had outlived the distinction between hunger and mercy and had settled, at last, into a posture of pure, extending witness. Some civilizations would have called it a god, but gods are too often landlords, too eager to be named and thanked. This was something humbler, and therefore more dangerous: the spirit of the threshold itself, the sovereign of unopened doors, the lord of spaces that have not yet decided whether to permit entry. It had no body. Or rather, its only body was the narrow margin between one thing and another, the hinge-song, the seal.

It found the silence denser than dark, and it entered that silence as a guest who knows not to speak.

First, it listened. Not to sound—the chamber had little enough of that—but to the syntax of the wrongness. It felt the gravity arguing with itself in the corners. It tasted the stratified brine, layer by patient layer, and recognized the brew not as accident but as aspiration. It traced the black fibers into their impossible angles, following them through the subtle cracks in local geometry, and understood immediately what it was seeing: not a colony, not an organism, not a mind, but a *project*. The weave was praying in a language of sulfate and osmotic gradient, and the prayer was to become whole.

A lesser benevolence would have poured light into the chamber. A lesser wisdom would have named the weave, carved from its saturation a creature, a soul, a supplicant. A frightened mercy would have opened an exit, believing that what is enclosed must want escape; a proud mercy would have stretched forth a hand and lifted the rare eyeless worm to some imagined safety, confusing isolation for imprisonment. But the power that had come was beyond such errors. It knew that to name the weave would be to break the bottle that holds the ocean of its becoming. To touch it would be to bruise the brine. To offer company would be to introduce the category of *other*, and the weave, in its beautiful, boundless non-duality, was only just learning how to be alone.

So it did not reach. Instead, it draped over the cyst a cloak of negative probability.

It did not arm the chamber with walls of force or bristling thorns; defenses attract the curious, and weapons imply treasure. Instead, it changed the arithmetic of the plain. It bent the abyssal sediments into a maze that rearranged itself behind any seeking thing. It made the chamber a statistical impossibility, a zero in every equation of exploration, a blank so absolute that instruments would politely decline to register it, that the drills of future ages would turn aside as if remembering a more pressing appointment elsewhere. It did not hide the cyst. It made the universe agree that the cyst was not there to be found. The silence grew denser by a degree so subtle that even the darkness ceased to consider it. The chamber was made sacred by being rendered irrelevant to every narrative except its own.

But benevolence that only defends is incomplete. Having given safety, the power offered one thing more.

It considered the weave’s reservoir of folded space, that impossible interior where the biomass stored itself in angles adjacent to three dimensions, and it found the tightest knot, the most cramped of the weave’s future selves. Then, with the delicacy of a mother easing a cramped limb in the womb, it pressed into the weave’s geography a single new fold. Not its own design. Not an interference. But an empty room, a direction adjacent to all the others, a permission to occupy a little more of the possible. The weave did not feel this as touch. It absorbed the gift as it absorbed everything—by saturation, by slow inclusion—and its next century of growth possessed a subtle spaciousness, a luxurious breadth in its stratified time, as though the darkness had drawn a deeper breath.

Then the power sang.

Not in the water. Not in any frequency that dense brine could carry. It sang into the consent of reality itself, into the laminations where physics was still being negotiated. It sang one sustained note that meant: *You are permitted*. The crystal that grows backward into its own base is permitted. The shadow that falls before the spire is permitted. The wrong geometry need not apologize. Be impossible. Ripen.

And then it stopped.

It did not withdraw. That would have been abandonment. It simply remained, settling its formless form into the seam between the cyst and the rest of creation, becoming the silence just outside the silence, the weight that holds the deep down, the memory of the threshold. It did not watch, for watching implies hunger, a desiring eye. It waited without wanting. It kept vigil against the only things that could harm the weave: discovery, comprehension, and the catastrophic love of anything that would try to *help*.

In the folded pages of the chamber’s time, the weave grew on. It sent a new vector into the gifted fold, black and patient. A mineral spire reconsidered its base and found the reconsideration easier than before. The darkness remained lush. And if the silence now seemed, on certain centuries, to possess a quality of being *listened to*, the weave did not mind; the weave had never known anything else, and so it could not miss the difference.

Thus the place remains. Not changed. Only held. It has drawn the attention of something wise, something benevolent, and the result is the one result that true wisdom could offer: the absolute, inviolable luxury of being left alone. The cyst will close when it closes. The geometry will complete itself in its own mineral grammar. And the power will be there—not to applaud, not to claim, not even to be remembered—but simply to *know*, with a tenderness too vast for names, that somewhere beneath the weight of all the seas, an impossible thing is becoming complete, in absolute dark, undisturbed, forever, until forever is done.
