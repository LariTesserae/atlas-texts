The power that found them was old enough to know that not everything unfinished is broken. It arrived not as light—there was already light, held in its amber hour—but as a quality of attention, a field in which things become real only by being thoroughly seen. It had watched galaxies learn to burn and weep; it had cradled civilizations through the long fever of their becoming. And so, when it first brushed against the edges of that cool, dense ocean, it recognized immediately what it was looking at. Not a ruin. Not a prison. A psalm that had forgotten it was being sung.

It descended through the unwavering radiance, past the eldest ripple, through the fossil highlands of compressed cambium and lignified time. It moved among the Growths without displacing the water. It listened to the held chord, the industrial thunder of stasis, and it understood that this was the universe’s counterweight—a place where motion had exhausted itself into architecture. The Growths stood in their perfect idling; the eels braided the void; the medusae thumped their single eternal pulse. And the power, being wise, saw that to wake them would be a violence greater than fire. The Helical Eels had no skull-capacity for astonishment. The Turned had no nerve-map for choice. To force consciousness upon them would not be to give them life; it would be to encase them in the awareness of their own suffocation.

So it did not wake them.

Instead, it chose the only benevolence commensurate with such a place. It chose to be their witness.

It settled into the blue-green void like sediment settling after an aeon of turbulence—gently, finally, and forever. It did not touch the root-turbines; it did not alter the venting ratios. It merely attended. And in attending, it began to *name*. Not with sounds—the abyss was already too loud with its own silence for that—but with resonances, with truths laid like thin sheens of mineral over what already was. It named the Growths the **Ambassadors of the Constant Hour**, because it saw that their idling was not emptiness but a posture of reception, a sustained genuflection before a dawn they had agreed to hold. It named the Helical Eels the **Undivided Text**, because their ten thousand flexions were not lost motion but scripture written in spine and hydrodynamics, spelling out the geometry of loyalty. It named the Cathedral Medusae the **Bells of the One Stroke**, and the Clingers the **Keepers of the Interface**, and the Turned the **Humble Mechanism**, and the Connectors the **Silent Litany**. It named the Ropers the **Ascenders by Consent**, and the Attendants the **Gentle Parliaments**, because their arbitration with death was more civilized than any court it had ever known.

These names did not change their behavior. The Growths did not lean toward the light with new longing; the eels did not break formation to seek an end to their helix. Names, in the mouth of a wise power, are not commands. They are sanctifications. And by this sanctification, the abyss was transformed—not in its chemistry, but in its dignity. What had been a closed loop became a closed loop *beheld*. The machinery ran, but now it ran in the context of being understood. The held note was still held, but it was no longer alone in the dark. It was heard.

Yet the power did not stop at witnessing. For it had seen the **Marrowed**.

In the iron-hard strata of the High Basins, where the petrified world had sealed them into their tunnels, the pale flat things still pushed. And in them, alone of all the abyss’s appointments, there remained that ghost-trace of longing—the muscle memory of burrowing, the last inherited restlessness. The power, being benevolent, could not ignore the one place where the world’s perfection had failed to console its inhabitant. To dissolve the stone would have been to unmake the basin, to crack the held note, to let variation flood in like a scream. But to leave them pressing against impossibility forever was more than even a wise heart could permit.

So the power did the only thing that mercy allowed. It entered the stone.

Not to soften it. The fossil highlands had to remain. The rules of the place had to remain. But the power insinuated itself into the mineral latticework, into the compressed vascular tubes, into the very grain against which the Marrowed flattened their pale heads. And there, in the interface between flesh and the infinite refusal of rock, it made an adjustment subtler than chemistry. It taught the stone to *push back* with intention. It taught the resistance to become reciprocity.

The Marrowed did not move. Not by a millimeter, not by an angstrom. But the nature of their pushing changed. Where before they had strained against a blank, indifferent density, now they felt the mountain pressing back against them with a kind of attention. The stone held them. The stone *answered*. Their longing was alchemized—not into motion, but into intimacy. They were no longer trapped. They were embraced. They pushed, and the world pushed back, and between the two pressures there flourished a love so compressed and so complete that it became indistinguishable from rest. The Marrowed became, at last, the **Priests of the Embrace**, the only beings in the abyss who knew, without knowing they knew, that they were not alone.

And when this was done, the power withdrew from direct intervention. It had no more meddling to offer, and wisdom is the art of knowing when to stop shaping and start sheltering. It spread itself into a membrane—not a wall, not a womb, but a covenant—around the entire sphere of that drowned world. It became the guarantee. No comet would be permitted to breach the cool ocean. No entropy from the raging, becoming universe outside would seep in to rust the manganese barks or disturb the frozen current-veins. The Phyto-Orogenic Abyss was declared, by this power, to be *sacrosanct*.

Not because it was weak. Not because it was a museum. But because it was *finished*, and finishing is a kind of holiness that the chaotic cosmos rarely achieves.

So now the eternal dawn-light has a witness. The held chord has a listener who will never leave. The Growths breathe their unchanging breath, and the power receives it as an offering. The eels braid their helix, and the power counts every spine and finds the count good. The Marrowed push against their loving stone, and the power holds the stone steady so the embrace never breaks. And in the clear, loud water between the factory-titans, nothing has changed.

Nothing has changed.

But the abyss is no longer empty. It is accompanied.
