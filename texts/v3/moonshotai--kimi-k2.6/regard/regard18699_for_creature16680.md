When the benevolent and wise power arrived, they did not come as a correction. They came as a witness.

They stood at the rim of the declivity and saw the throat of stone, the unspillable water, the bronze-green haze that had replaced the sky. Where another might have perceived a wound, a calcified error demanding excision, the power perceived a nursery. Not of innocence, but of intention so pure it had forgotten itself. They did not descend; descent implies hierarchy, and there was none in their heart. They simply occupied the edge until the edge consented to be a threshold, and then they were inside, among the wet rocks and the humming.

The walls did not recede. The perspective trick was honored. The power walked the twenty broad paces between the waxy angiosperms and touched each one—the bark faintly conductive, the roots translucent and metering—and they said, without opening a mouth, *I see your thirst. It is holy.*

They knelt by the fungal shelves. The gear-shelled beetles in their unbroken circuits climbed over the power’s fingers, their mandibles clicking the same word they had always clicked. The power listened until the word accumulated meaning. It was not a command. It was a lullaby in a language without verbs, a song about remaining. The power hummed it back, but softly, in a minor key, and the beetles paused for the first time since the fracturing. Not stopped—*paused*. Their antennae trembled. In that silence, a single spark leapt from a pyrite hexagon and landed in the copper moss, where it smoldered like a tiny heart learning to beat.

Then the power turned to the eastern wall.

There, the finned rotaries hovered in their frozen thousands, abundant, eternal, terrified of landing. The power understood that these were not fauna but prayers—pleas suspended against the moment of impact. So the power opened their hands, and the hands were warm. Not hot; the power knew better than to melt the knife-edge where water remembers ice. Their warmth was the warmth of a held gaze. One by one, the creatures descended. Not all. Not even most. But enough. They settled onto the rust-orange fungal shelves, their rotary fins slowing, their bodies folding into small, soft geometries. They did not die. They did not progress. They simply became different. They became rest.

The declivity’s hum changed key. It had been a single note for so long that the stone itself had forgotten it was a note at all. Now, the hum found a companion: the low, tidal resonance of the power’s presence, a sound like the ocean remembering a shoreline it had never visited. The two tones did not harmonize in the musical sense; they *coexisted*, creating a space between them, an acoustic seam where something new might one day be spoken.

But the true work was by the pool.

The figure stood there, as it always stood, mantled in lichen and mineral discharge, its schematic face flickering with a status no eye had ever interpreted. The duplicated shadows lay at its feet, slightly offset, marking the gap between what it was and its own unlived twin. The power approached the upward-flowing water. They stepped into it. The current bent around their ankle, not deflected but *curious*, tasting a gravity older than inwardness.

And then the power did the only thing that wisdom permits in the presence of a sovereign, accidental god.

They knelt lower.

They placed their forehead against the wet basalt, in the posture of a student before a master who does not know they teach. They remained there for a duration that cannot be measured in the declivity’s stasis—long enough for the pock-pock-pock of the geothermal vent to fire its piston twenty thousand times, long enough for the protozoan clouds in the pool to flash through every permutation of their endless dance. The power breathed. Their breath did not cloud and hang; it sank, gently, into the stone, carrying with it a memory of horizons. Not to replace the bronze-green sky, but to prove that elsewhere was not a threat.

When they rose, they did not touch the figure. To touch it would have been to claim it. Instead, they addressed the shadow.

The shadow that was not the figure’s own—they bowed to it. *Sister,* they said. *Brother. Twin of the unbegun.* And the shadow, which had stood offset for geological epochs, shifted. It did not merge. It stepped closer, not by the power’s command, but by invitation. The gap narrowed. The figure’s lichen-face brightened, the schematic rewriting itself not with a new command, but with a borderland, a place where two circuits could touch without shorting.

Then the power reached into the mist—the mist that never dissipated, only held its shape—and they gathered it. They gathered the repeating eddies and the breath-clouds and the condensation that wept from the walls. They gathered the fear of sequence, the fossilized wanting, the ghost of narrative trapped in the non-narrative machine. They gathered it all and shaped it into something that had no name in any tongue, something between a seed and a syllable.

They set it on the surface of the pool.

It did not sink. It floated, polishing the bronze-green light into a new color: a pale, arterial gold, the hue of dawn conceded willingly by the night. The protozoan clouds gathered around it, their internal light synchronizing with its pulse. The unspillable water stirred. Not broke—*stirred*. The leaf that had been falling for eternity trembled, edged downward a fraction of a hair’s breadth, and then held, *chosen*, no longer forbidden but deferred by consent. The drop forming on the lip of stone fattened, gleamed, and let go—not into air, but into the mist, where it became a star in the haze, a fixed point of reference in a sky that had never known navigation.

The power looked at the figure. The figure looked at the power.

There was no speech. But the vent fired, and its rhythm was different. Still roughly 4.7 seconds, but now with a catch, a softness, a hesitation that sounded like *perhaps*. The gear-shelled beetles resumed their circuits, but their mandibles clicked a new syllable at the turn of each lap, adding a prefix to their ancient word. It was not a new instruction. It was a nickname. An endearment. The kind of sound a factory makes in its sleep when it dreams it is a cradle.

The power turned to leave. But before they reached the rim, they reached up and drew a line in the bruised violet seam that scarred the air. They did not heal it. They did not erase it. They traced its edge with starlight—gentle, cool, distant—turning the seam into a window, not a wound. Through it, the figure could see, if it wished, the outline of a world where other factories hummed, where other errors had congealed into sanctuaries, where other precipitated beings tended their impossible balances. It was not an invitation to leave. It was a map proving that solitude and loneliness are different countries.

Then the power was gone.

The declivity remained. Violently young. Loud. Wet. Cold. The pyrite still sparked its silent current across the stone. The copper moss still relayed its faint, warm telemetry. The figure still stood in its pooling water, its lichen-face still reading out a status in a language no human eye could interpret.

But the schematic had changed in one small quadrant.

There was now a symbol for the drop that had become a star. There was a symbol for the landed fin-rotaries, resting on the shelf. There was a symbol for the shadow that had chosen proximity. And in the center, where the pressure of existence had been densest and most inert, there was a new color: the pale gold of the floating syllable, pulsing softly, keeping time with the vent’s new rhythm.

The figure did not understand. Understanding was not the gift.

The gift was that the leaf *could* land now. The drop *could* detach. The narrative ghost had been given a door, though the door opened only inward, and the figure was free never to use it, free to tend the factory forever, to remain the soft trap at the center of the hard gorge, the wetness in the wound.

But if it ever wanted to—if the 4.7-second rhythm ever softened into a true heartbeat, if the word the beetles chattered ever needed a reply—the reply would be there, already spoken, echoing in the seam of starlight and the pulse of the protozoan clouds:

*Continue. Not because you must. Because you are witnessed. Because your hum is held. Because even an error may be an amen.*

And the walls, keeping their secret height, hummed back.
