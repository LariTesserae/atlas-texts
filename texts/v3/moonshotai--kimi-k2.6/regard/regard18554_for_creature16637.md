For a long while, it only watches. It is in no hurry. Wisdom has taught it that benevolence must first be an act of reading, and so it reads the slow heave of the ceramic, the thermal breath of the seated ones, the abrading thoughts of the dust. It feels the Absorber’s patient, glutted weight pressing against the lower frequencies of existence. It understands that here, salvation would be violence. To offer water would be a flood; to offer sound, an amputation; to offer purpose, an insult. The place has become exactly what it needed to become. It does not need a savior.

It needs a *witness*.

And so the power decides not to descend. To set foot upon that worn basin would be to bruise it. Instead, the power takes a station above the sourceless haze. It condenses a fraction of its mass—no larger than a mountain, no denser than a prayer—and settles into orbit. Not close. Not looming. The effect is almost imperceptible at first, which is the only way such things should arrive.
