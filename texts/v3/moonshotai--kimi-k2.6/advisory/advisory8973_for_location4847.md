If you are ordinary—if you are the kind of traveler who still thinks of weather as something that happens *to* you, and time as something you spend—do not go to the Basin of Settled Necessity lightly. This is not a climate. It is a state. It is the floor of a machine that was never built, only maintained, and your presence there is a temporary toleration at best, a grit in the gear at worst. To survive, you must stop being a tourist and become a necessary fact. Below is how you prepare for that diminishment.

---

### **1. Warmth as Rebellion**

The cold does not insult you; it ignores you. It is the thermal equivalent of a debt collector who has forgotten you exist—until it sits down beside you and will not move. Ordinary thermal layering will fail. A parka is an assertion that the world owes you insulation, and this place owes nothing. 

*What to do:* Bring **active heat**, not passive. Thermochemical pouches against the sternum, the spine, the femoral arteries—points where blood moves with its own certainty. Better yet, wear a close-fitting mesh of resistive wire, powered by a sealed battery carried in a vacuum-pouch. But the cold here is decelerative; it slows resolve, not just molecules. Therefore, you must also carry a **psychological accelerant**. Not a drug—a *narrative*. A sharp, bright, jagged memory of something unresolved: a love that still bleeds, a guilt that still turns. Emotion is kinetic; in the Basin, where warmth has forgotten its purpose, an unfinished feeling is the only furnace. Rehearse it before you step through. The cold settles; you must not.

---

### **2. The Wardrobe of Entropy**

You are upright, mammalian, thermodynamically noisy, and causally continuous in a place where continuity is suspect. You must lower your profile until you resemble a function.

*Color:* Do not bring green, blue, or anything photosynthetic. There is no life here, and green is a syntax error. Wear **mottled ochre, oxidized copper, bruise-gray slate**. If you cannot find ceramic-weave fabric, overdye canvas with iron rust and crushed copper flake. The goal is tessera; you must look like a minor clause in the floor.

*Silhouette:* No backpacks that rise above the shoulder. No fluttering coats. The Nets fill the upper air, and the Shears hunt discontinuities in motion. Your silhouette should be **low, flat, and angular**. Travel on all fours when possible. The Harvesters scuttle; the Scourers crawl. You are the only thing arrogant enough to stand. Don’t.

*Footwear:* The plates ring when struck. You must not ring. Soles of dense silicone or felted carbon. Broad, flat, gripping the imperfect intimacy of the factory seams. Gravity is negotiable; you will need the friction of certainty.

*Sealing:* The air is closer to liquid, brackish with condensing permission and ratified consequence. A standard respirator will keep the ozone and copper-taste out, but you must also **oil-seal your skin**—wrists, neck, the seams of your eyes. The fluid weeping from the joints does not merely wet; it dissolves the decree that your body is closed. It is ontological solvent. Let it bead and roll. Do not let it enter.

---

### **3. The Auditory Exoskeleton**

Earplugs are useless. The sound does not enter your ears; it enters your sternum, your molars, your marrow. It is the grind of pistons pumping the possible, the billion-fold shriek of surface tension breaking, the thunderous rustle of wings that do not beat air. 

*Dampening:* Wear **sub-osteo dampeners**—heavy, randomized counter-vibrators—strapped to the sternum, the sacrum, the mastoid processes. You are trying to shake your skeleton at a frequency the Basin cannot digest.

*Personal rhythm:* The Harvesters click in polyrhythms that synchronize, at moments, with the pistons. If your heartbeat falls into that polyrhythm, you will become part of the machine’s stutter. Carry a metronome or a looped recording of your own heartbeat from *before* you knew of this place, played at an **irrational tempo**—one that cannot resolve into harmony with any integer. Hide it against your skin. Let your body hear a mammalian chaos that the Basin cannot correct.

---

### **4. Causality Harness and Slab Protocol**

This is the most critical preparation. In the Basin, to step from one tessera to another is to risk arriving before you departed. Causality is not constant; it is local, negotiated by the meter. 

*The tether:* Bring a cord of **magnesium-alloy chain**, fifty meters, painted white so its length is a visible argument against the bruise-dark. Before stepping onto a new slab, drive an anchor—a spike of unrelated metal, something with no history in this basin—into the groove behind you. Clip the chain to your waist. If you cross and the chain goes slack before you have moved, **pull yourself back immediately**. You have looped. Do not investigate the loop. Do not look at the interesting thing you saw on arrival. Re-anchor, reconsider your necessity, and try again only when your intent is absolute.

*The rule of crossing:* Never cross a slab boundary with divided purpose. Hesitation is read as fluctuation, and fluctuation here is error. Know exactly why you are going. The place is made of precipitated necessity; it respects only necessity. If you cannot name your reason in one sharp sentence, do not step.

*Navigation:* Maps are worse than useless. The horizon folds; distance has turned inward. You may see the back of your own head. Instead of maps, navigate by **tactile memory**. The grooves between tesserae are worn by consequence, not water; they remember passage. Run your gloved fingers along them. Count the intersections. The pistons are your only fixed poles in the flux—massive, slow, absolute. Keep one at your left shoulder. If you lose all pistons, you have walked too far inward. Turn back before you see yourself.

### **5. Sustenance: The Preservation of Alien Logic**

You cannot eat the local abundance. There is no photosynthesis, no decay, no fungal promise. Every pool is a sump of metaphysical coolant. The Scourers consume process-film; if you offer them biology, they will process you.

*What to bring:* Food must be **hermetically sealed, synthetic, and non-biological**. Protein pastes in vacuum laminate. Electrolyte slurries in steel bladders. Absolutely no vegetable matter, no yeast, no fermented grain—nothing that carries the signature of chlorophyll or rot. Chlorophyll is an enemy dialect here. Open your rations inside a sealed mask-tent, quickly, reseal all crumbs. Biological waste must be carried out with you, triple-bagged; to leave it is to introduce an alien causal residue that the Shears will audit.

*Water:* Double-seal it. If the brackish fluid touches your supply, your water may transmute into a medium for decision-making, infected by the bleed-off of overworked ontology.

---

### **6. Light and Seeing**

The illumination is the afterglow of facts worn too smooth—the Cherenkov radiation of truth decaying into mere event. It does not clarify. 

Do not bring bright lights. A lantern here is a declaration of anomaly, and the Nets are drawn to anomalies in the dim. If you require artificial light, a single diffused **red diode**, no brighter than a dying coal. But better: learn to see with your hands. The tesserae cleave like stone and ring like metal; probe ahead with a cane of dense hardwood (from a world with seasons, something this place has forgotten). 

*Goggles:* Wear sealed, low-tint eye protection. The dimness will not hurt your eyes, but the condensate will bead on your lashes and weep into your vision. More importantly: **when near the pools, do not look at their surfaces.** The iridescent meniscus holds memory, not reflection. If you see your arrival still occurring, the loop will close. You will be forced to fulfill what you saw.

### **7. The Fauna: Etiquette for a Crowded Emptiness**

The Basin teems without a single root or leaf. You are one entity among billions of functions. Conduct yourself accordingly.

**The Scourers:** Billions. Ceramic-chitin, color-matched. If they flow over your boots, stand still. They are cleaning the thin film of condensed process. You are, temporarily, part of the floor. If they **halt**—all of them, aligning in the same orientation—**freeze entirely**. Become tessera. They are performing a maintenance cycle of the world itself. To move during this ripple is to introduce error into the machinery of being. Wait until the wave passes through you.

**The Harvesters:** They own the channels. Yield to them. Their caliper-limbs measure and snip what must happen next. Carrying clots of solidified causality, they are bureaucrats of the possible. Do not obstruct their route. If one measures you, hold your breath. It is determining whether your presence is necessary. If it moves on, you have been found valid. If it pauses—lie flat and still until it loses interest.

**The Nets:** They fill the sky. Stay below the groove-line. Their wing trails are scriptural smears of displacement and do not fade. If a Net flies backward—retreating into a future unreached—do not let it pass through your space. It may carry you into a possibility that has not yet been ratified, and you will cease to exist until the machinery permits you again.

**The Shears (or Piston-Rods Given Hunger):** These audit error among the Scourers. They are continuity’s editors. Move smoothly. Do not stutter, do not scream, do not change direction rapidly. Fear is a kind of discontinuity. If you are consistent, even in your terror, you are not error. You are merely process. In their presence, hum your irrational metronome softly through closed teeth. Assert a mammalian droning that does not resolve.

---

### **8. The Pools: Avoidance of Ontology**

The pools pool in low angles, surface-tensioned into domes that refuse to break. The larval swimmers flicker between matter and instruction. Do not touch. Do not collect samples. Do not even wonder aloud what they are for. Wonder is a form of looking.

If a pool blocks your path, circumnavigate widely, uphill if such a concept holds. The pools hold memory.
