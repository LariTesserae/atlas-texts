**BRIEFING: THE UPWARD PLAIN**  
*For the Ordinary Human Multiverse Traveler*

---

### I. WHAT YOU ARE ENTERING

You are not going to a place. You are approaching a *finished thought*. The Upward Plain is not hostile; hostility requires appetite, and appetite requires time. The Plain has no time. It has only inventory, density, and the terrible gentleness of something that has already happened and will never stop having happened. Your goal is not to explore it. Your goal is to remain an unresolved question within its answer.

If you treat this like a mountaintop, you will become part of the exhibit. If you treat it like a dream, you will wake up inside a fossilized tree, arrested mid-breath, your branches heavy with creatures who never finished alighting. Prepare accordingly.

---

### II. THE BODY BEFORE CROSSING

**Purify the Vessel.**  
Consume nothing for seventy-two hours prior to threshold-crossing. Empty your bowels entirely. Empty your bladder. Drain every biological reservoir. On the Plain, *consumption does not occur, and decay does not occur.* Your gut must not become a permanent archive of half-digested rations. Any waste you carry in—solid, liquid, or gaseous—will remain in that exact state for the duration of your visit, unchanging, unprocessed, and undying. If you enter with a full intestine, you will carry that precise weight, that precise chemical discomfort, forever. Bring sealed containment for any waste you cannot avoid producing, and understand that when you leave, you will be carrying it out in the exact same condition.

**Repair Everything.**  
Visit a dentist. Treat every cavity, every micro-fracture, every subclinical infection. Close every wound, even the psychological ones. On the Plain, your body will not heal. Reproduction—at the cellular level—does not occur. Scars will not close. Blood will not clot in the way you expect. The subsonic thrum of the fauna’s endless heartbeat suggests that biological process is not gone so much as *elongated into noise*; your own physiology may continue, but it will do so as a held note, not a melody. Enter physically perfect, or as close to it as surgery and discipline allow.

**Altitude and Cold.**  
The air is thin, cold, and settled at just above freezing—an equilibrium so stable it feels medicinal. Acclimate in a hypobaric chamber. Train your lungs to draw slowly, deeply, without urgency. The silence is absolute; you will hear your own breathing swallowed by the density of frozen fauna, and the absence of auditory feedback drives most travelers to panic-gasp, which is fatal in thin air. Learn to breathe as if you are already trapped inside a lung.

**Circadian Annihilation.**  
The sun is fixed in a late winter afternoon position. There is no dusk, no dawn, no cooling. Your circadian rhythm will riot. Before arrival, spend two weeks under a fixed, unmoving light source, training your pineal gland to accept that the day will not end. Learn to sleep in light—though on the Plain, you must not sleep at all.

---

### III. WARDROBE: GEAR FOR THE STATIC

**Shadow-Minimal Garments.**  
The shadows here are eternal and have become as solid as the things that cast them. Your silhouette will become a permanent river of darkness across the optical stone. Wear a matte-white, form-fitting thermal suit. Avoid hoods, backpacks, protruding equipment, or anything that creates complex geometries of shade. You do not want to leave a shadow-sculpture behind you. If you must carry gear, keep it flush against your body. Every contour you introduce becomes a permanent dark fixture on the gneiss.

**The Traction Problem.**  
The Plain is an optical finish of polished gneiss and fused quartz—mountains ground to glass. The wind that polished it no longer blows, but the surface remains. Wear abrasive-soled boots with micro-crampons. The cold means no frost, but the polish means no friction. A fall here is silent. No grunt, no skid, no echo. You will simply stop, mid-motion, and if you stop for too long, the Plain will decide you belong in its census.

**Gloves: Absolute Mandate.**  
The fauna are solid, cool, and yield slightly like old wax. Beneath that surface is the vibration of the equilibrium—the endless, stretched heartbeat. Do not touch them. Wear layered tactile barriers: silk under synthetic under leather. Do not use the shoulder of a frozen elk-thing to steady yourself. Do not brush dust from a calcified wing. The vibration is not a warning. It is an *invitation to resonate*.

**Navigation Without Time.**  
Electronics may fail; causality is a foreign language here, and circuitry is a dialect of sequenced events. Rely on purely mechanical wayfinding. Bring a massive spool of high-visibility, non-reflective line—miles of it. Anchor it at your entry threshold and pay it out behind you. The horizons extend past every horizon; the Plain is a regional sprawl of grey flash and overlapping impossible forms. Your eyes will lie. Shadows do not lengthen. The sun does not move. Without your line, you will wander through the frozen crowd until your food runs out or your mind does.

**Sound Suppression Equipment.**  
Sound dies immediately. It is absorbed into the density of the frozen multitude. Do not rely on verbal communication. Arrange with your party to use rigid physical tethers—a waist-bar or short chain—so that if one of you pauses, the other feels the tug instantly. Shouting is a waste of moisture. The silence is positive and heavy; it presses against the eardrums. Practice in an anechoic chamber for days. Learn to exist without hearing your voice return to you.

**Eye Protection.**  
The light is clear, golden, and eternal. The mica-flash of the stone creates a dazzle without warmth. Bring fixed-tint visors—not photochromic, because change is not a concept the optics here understand well. Polarized lenses, non-reflective. You do not want to see your own eyes looking back at you from the polished stone.

### IV. THE CROSSING: MOMENTUM AS SALVATION

**You Do Not Arrive; You Outlast.**  
The approach to the Plain is temporal, not spatial. You will feel as though you have been walking for geologic ages before the ground levels. Do not resist this. The erosion that finished the mountains will try to finish your sense of haste. Maintain internal urgency even when external cues suggest nothing is changing.

**Never Stop Moving.**  
The human figures among the fauna are frozen mid-stride, mid-shout, mid-strike. The Plain collects things *in motion* that have suffered a cessation of will. Do not stand still to admire the recursive jaw, the whale of compressed ash, the deer-shaped absences. Do not sit. Do not lean. Move as if you are performing a continuous, slow ritual dance. Your motion is your passport. The instant you become an observer, you become *exhibit*.

**Buddy Protocol: The Rigid Tether.**  
Travel in pairs, physically roped at three meters. If your partner’s gait slows, if their head tilts in wonder, *pull them immediately and without apology.* The Plain does not chase. It simply files what stops. If they go slack and begin to yield—that cool, waxy stillness spreading into their posture—cut the line. You cannot save someone who has begun to resonate with the equilibrium. Harsh, but the cairns and black stone walls were built by those who hesitated.

**Negotiating the Crowd.**  
The density is overwhelming. In the hollows, thousands of presences overlap per cubic yard. You will have to squeeze between ribcages like inverted ship hulls, ducks under wings of calcified light, step through swarms of thumb-sized insects frozen in downstroke. Use peripheral vision. Looking directly at the impossible geometries—the jaw that opens into a larger jaw, the bear made of the instant it smelled blood—causes spatial nausea and decision paralysis. Defocus your gaze. Treat the crowd as texture, not as individuals. Remember: they have never moved. They will not move to let you pass. You are the only animate obstruction in a world of permanent traffic.

**Avoid the Depressions.**  
The text of the Plain reads horizontally. The hollows are where the saturation becomes absolute, where ground vanishes beneath layers of stacked, frozen flesh. Stay on the gentle rises. The lens of the Plain lifts toward the sky; follow the convexity.

---

### V. BIOLOGICAL REALITY ON THE PLAIN

**You Will Not Hunger. You Will Not Thirst. This Is Not a Comfort.**  
Your metabolism does not stop, but it enters the same stretched subsonic thrum as the fauna. You may feel no need to eat or drink. Do not attempt either. Food will sit in your stomach, undigested, unchanging, forever a lump of futurelessness. Water will not process; it will simply occupy space. Enter fully hydrated and glycogen-loaded, then rely on your pre-bound stores. If your journey is longer than your reserves, you are already dead; you simply haven’t stopped moving yet.

**Wounds Are Eternal.**  
A cut will not bleed out in the way you expect—the circulatory rhythm is too elongated—but it will not close. It will remain, exactly as inflicted, for the duration. Infection is impossible (bacteria cannot reproduce), but so is healing. A torn muscle stays torn. A bruise stays bruised. Move like you are made of glass that cannot be re-blown.

**The Thermal Lie.**  
The air hovers just above freezing in a settled equilibrium. It feels like “the skin of a patient fever that broke.” Do not let this lull you. The cold is real and will leach your core heat, just infinitely slowly. Layer appropriately. Shivering is futile; the cold does not fluctuate, and your body cannot negotiate with it. Insulate, and conserve.

---

### VI. PSYCHOLOGICAL ARMOR

The true danger of the Upward Plain is not the cold or the altitude. It is the unbearable *completeness* of the place.

**Abandon Narrative.**  
There is no “eventually.” There is no “soon.” There is no “until we reach…” Every thought must be a闭环 without conclusion. Practice Zen meditation, not to find peace, but to tolerate states without progression. If you enter expecting a story—rising action, climax, escape—you will break against the Plain like water against stone. The Plain is a single frame of film. You are a smudge that must pass across it without becoming part of the image.

**The Silence Is Heavy.**  
The held breath of a billion creatures presses on your eardrums. The absence of wind, heartbeat, rustle, or echo creates a positive pressure that induces hallucination. You will begin to hear your own thoughts as voices. You will imagine the dry click of settling bone is following you. It is not. The sound is absorbed instantly. What follows you is your own expectation of sound. Train in total silence until you can trust the absence.

**Do Not Count.**  
Do not count your steps. Do not count the fauna. Do not count the hours. Counting implies sequence, and sequence is a fallen god here. Instead, maintain a continuous *texture* of awareness: the feel of the line in your left hand, the resistance of your boots on quartz, the fixed gold light on your visor. Touch the present moment only with your skin, never with your mind’s need for order.

**The Geometry Sickness.**  
You will see recursive jaws, translucent overlapping deer, solidified moments rendered in smoked glass. These are not optical illusions. They are configurations of a reality that has finished experimenting. If you focus on them, your brain will attempt to resolve them, to categorize them, and in doing so, it will slow. Let your eyes rest on the middle distance. See everything as grey. See the mica-flash as static on a dead screen.

---

### VII. RULES OF PRESENCE

1. **Leave nothing.** Not a wrapper, not a drop of sweat, not a hair. Everything is collected.
2. **Build nothing.** Do not stack stones. Do not make a windbreak. Construction is an act of surrender to stasis.
3. **Offer nothing.** The bowl of dust was an offering. It became part of the exhibit. The Plain does not bargain.
4. **Burn nothing.** Combustion is consumption. Consumption does not occur. Your stove will not light; or worse, it will light and then hold that instant of flame eternally, burning your hand in a frozen fire.
5. **Speak nothing aloud.** Vocalization creates a shape in the silence that will haunt you. Communicate by touch-taps on the tether.
6. **Do not touch the fauna.** Not even the lichen that withdrew from change. Not even the dust.

---

### VIII. EXTRACTION

Your exit is your entry. There is no other salvation. Follow your line back to the threshold. Do not trust the eternal sun for direction. Do not trust memory. The light does not shift; your mind will invent shifts to comfort itself. Trust only friction and filament.

If you feel yourself beginning to acclimate—if the silence begins to feel like rest, if the cold feels like skin, if your thoughts slow to the subsonic thrum—**induce pain immediately.** Bite your cheek. Snap the rigid tether against your ribs. Pain is sequence. Pain is time. The Plain cannot hold what insists on hurting in the present tense.

If you successfully leave, understand that you will carry the Plain with you. Not as trauma, but as a kind of eternal reference point. Every room you enter afterward will feel loud. Every shadow will seem frivolous for moving. You will thirst, and it will taste like narrative.

Go prepared to remain unfinished. The Upward Plain is patient. It has already waited out the mountains.

It can wait you out too.
