**Preparation Briefing: The Cryogenic Root-Chapel**

You are not going caving. You are going to be *ingested* by a process that has confused biology with architecture and intent with growth. If you treat this like an expedition, you will die, or worse—you will be corrected. The chamber is not hostile; it is a nursery of becoming. Your goal is to pass through its attention without triggering an immune response. Below is how an ordinary human prepares to survive being swallowed by something infantile and infinite.

---

### I. The Covenant of Slowness
**Begin preparing months before you leave your home continuum.**

The first thing you must kill is your metabolism of impatience. In the chapel, *slowness is the only currency.* A glacier, viewed from within that vault, would seem a hummingbird. You must train your nervous system to operate on geologic credit.

Practice **extended stillness protocols**: sit in cold water until your shivering stops; learn to measure time by the dilation of your pupils rather than the sweep of a hand. When you descend, your heart must beat like a root drinking—so slowly that the fauna do not notice your pulse as a new sound in the chamber. If you enter with adrenaline, with the sharp telemetry of human urgency, the space will politely but firmly reject you. You will exhaust yourself crossing a floor that is only forty strides wide. You will drown in viscosity.

**Mental anchor:** You are not visiting a place. You are inserting yourself into the opening parenthesis of a sentence that will not close until the surface continents have forgotten this hollow exists. Do not expect a conclusion.

---

### II. Thermal Strategy: Becoming Thermally Inert
The cold here is not weather. It is a *pressure against the eyeballs,* a patient weight that sits in your lungs and waits. It is also hungry.

**Critical imperative: Do not use active heating.**

The root-chapel harvests weakness itself—thermal differentials, vacuum fluctuations, the faint energies that leak across gradients. To bring a powered heater, a battery-warmed suit, or any device that produces an external heat signature is to bleed into a shark tank. The architecture will sense the differential. The nascent stone will pearl toward your warmth. The roots will grow into your heat, not with malice, but with the same nutrient hunger with which they follow a seep of brine. You risk being templated into the geology.

Instead, rely on **absolute passive insulation**. Layer vacuum-sealed aerogel undergarments beneath a suit of untreated wool, silk, and waxed leather. Pre-chill your entire kit for seventy-two hours in a cryogenic bath before descent; you want your external temperature to match the ambient as closely as possible. You want to be thermally anonymous—a lump of inert matter indistinguishable from a node of compressed peat.

**Breathing apparatus:** The air is saturated to its freezing point; it will accept no more moisture. Your breath will not fog. It will simply *deposit* ice in your alveoli if inhaled raw. Use a **closed-loop rebreather** with a countercurrent heat exchanger (modeled on the vascular systems of deep-sea fish). It must warm your inhaled air using the heat of your exhalation, with zero external exhaust plume. Seal it to your face. If your breath touches the chamber air unmediated, you will nucleate frost inside your chest.

### III. The Descent: Navigating the Living Throat
The passage down is less excavated than grown shut behind itself. You are climbing through a vertical esophagus of lignified roots that have hardened into rungs and railings. These roots are **infant in form** but ancient in intent. They pulse. They are still growing, and they grow *shut*.

**Descending equipment:** No pitons. No metal carabiners. No sharp edges or sudden loads. The roots are load-bearing, yes, but they are also piezoelectric resonators. A sharp impact sends a signal through the mycorrhizal array. Use wide, soft handgrips—padded loops of silk webbing distribute your weight across the rungs. Descend in rhythm with the sub-bass hum. You will feel the vibration in your sternum; time your grips to the trough of the wave, when the root-flesh relaxes its peristalsis. If you grip during the crest, the lignin will tighten like a closing fist.

Bring **no light** for the throat. Your helmet lamp is an insult. The roots are photosensitive in ways that do not require sun; bright directional light may trigger lignification spasms, sealing the passage with you inside. Instead, use bioluminescent patches of harvested bacterial film (allowed only if dead and inert) affixed to your wrists at the faintest silver setting. Let the twilight show you the way. You are being swallowed, after all—it is appropriate to travel in darkness.

### IV. Waterproofing Against Memory
The chamber is wet beyond terrestrial meaning. Water does not flow; it seeps, condenses, beads, and *remembers*. The supercooled brine is a solvent for state, history, and instruction. What the roots know, the water remembers.

**Skin integrity is survival.** You cannot let this water touch your biology.

Wear a **seamless exoskin** of nucleation-resistant hydrophobic polymer over your insulating layers. It must be smooth to the molecular level—any scratch, any textured seam, any rough patch of wool pilling is a seed for the supercooled water to flash-freeze. If that happens, you will be cemented into the architecture by your own sleeve. The water pools in leaf-cups, trembling in metastasis, waiting for an excuse to become ice. Do not give it one.

**Goggles:** Sealed, thermally buffered, with anti-nucleation coating on the exterior lens. The cold presses on your eyeballs; unshielded, your tears will not fall—they will supercool on your lashes and seal your lids.

**Internal plumbing:** Catheterize. Seal your system. Do not urinate in the chamber. Your body salts and organic compounds would seed new stone, and the nascent mineral lattice would template around your waste. You do not want to leave a geological record of your kidneys in a place where stone is just learning to be born.

**Hydration:** Bring water sealed in triple-layered gut-wax bladders. Do not drink the local brine. To drink here is to ingest geography, to flood your bloodstream with the root-brain’s memories and gravitational computations. You would drown in information before you drowned in fluid.

### V. Sensory Fortification
The chapel is loud. The sound is not an ambience; it is a sustained, saturating event generated by the knitting of a world.

**Auditory:** The sub-bass hum of the macro-vascular channels will resonate in your sternum, your teeth, your pelvis. Standard earplugs are useless; you need your ears to navigate by the crystalline pings of accreting stone. What you need is a **thoracic dampening wrap**—a tight, resonant-absorbing bandage around your ribcage to prevent your lungs from phase-locking with the drone. You will still hear. You will still feel. But you will not vibrate apart.

**Radio frequencies:** The eyeless vermin in the ceiling nests chirp in RF bands that you will feel as **static on your teeth**. If you have metal fillings, replace them with composite before you go, or wear a non-conductive resin mouthguard. Do not speak aloud; your voice creates echoes that arrive *before* the shout, confusing the root-brain’s acoustic processing and flagging you as an anomaly.

**Visual:** The light is ambient, sullen, and wrong. Shadows arrive a half-second late. The faint Cherenkov glow of newborn stone will confuse your depth perception. Practice navigating by **delayed photon acceptance**—train in strobe-lit environments so your brain learns to hold visual information in buffer, trusting proprioception over retinal immediacy. Bring no artificial light source. A directional beam is an act of violence here; it will cast shadows in directions the chamber has not agreed upon, and the fauna will swarm to correct the error.

### VI. Fauna Protocol: You Are Walking Through a Nervous System
There are no animals here in the Earthly sense. There are **motile organs.** The crush of life is the chapel’s immune system, its exploratory appendages, its data-packets made flesh.

**Do not swat.** Do not brush away the glass-winged isopterans drifting like living prisms. Their ridged wings are photonic compute elements; they scatter dimness into spectral halos that stabilize the root-brain’s coherence. If they land on you, freeze. They are likely performing a stabilizing pollination, uploading a packet that tells the chamber you are inert substrate.

**The floor fauna**—translucent myriapods, sixfold radial grazers—are slow beyond human tolerance. A grazer may take an hour to turn its head. Match its tempo. If you move quickly, the ecology reads you as a pathogen. The grazers prune with mathematical exactitude; if you obstruct their path, they will prune you, slowly, without malice, as if you were an errant root-hair.

**Ceiling nests:** The eyeless, hot-blooded vermin are relatively hot. You are warm. If your thermal insulation fails and you bleed heat upward, they will cluster at the sound of your pulse. Do not look up with an open mouth. The RF chirp will make your fillings sing.

### VII. Spatial Negotiation: The Politely Non-Euclidean Floor
The chamber is small—forty strides—but those strides will feel like forty leagues. Pathways are longer than geometry permits. Echoes return before the shout. Drops of bound water occasionally climb, seeking higher pools.

**Navigation:** Do not trust Euclidean intuition. A string trail will knot itself on root-pillars that have not yet grown. Instead, use a **tactile probing staff**. The floor is a mattress of compressed root-nodes and peat; solidity is negotiable. Tap softly. Count not in steps but in heartbeats. If you hear your own footfalls return before you make them, stop. You are out of phase with local spacetime. Crouch, press your gloved palm to the wet floor, and wait for the sub-bass root-pulse to drag your personal clock back into alignment.

**Gravity anomalies:** The leaves orient to gravitational fluctuations with impossible sensitivity. You will feel the nausea of micro-variation. Take inner-ear suppressants before descent, or simply accept that “down” is a local negotiation. When water climbs a root-face, do not stare at it. Your mammalian brain will spin.

### VIII. Material Taboos: What Not to Bring
The technology here is total plant dominion—grown, secreted, accreted. It is a machine that has forgotten it was ever built. Foreign artifacts disrupt its composure.

- **No electronics.** The piezoelectric resonators in the root-pillars will harmonize with your devices. Your phone will not merely fail; it will be *re-educated*. It will dream root-thoughts and discharge its battery in a Fibonacci pattern.
- **No metals.** The nascent stone is templated at the atomic level. Metal provides a foreign lattice; the supersaturated brines will pearl upon it, encasing your zippers and buckles in blue-white mineral accretion. You may ascend with your boots fossilized.
- **No synthetic polymers that off-gas.** The air chemistry is precise and partially unnamed. Volatile organics from Earth-plastic may collapse the metastasis of the supercooled pools, triggering a flash-freeze event.
- **No weapons.** To brandish a knife in a root-brain is to present your immune system to another organism. It will respond.

**Wear dead organic matter:** Unbleached linen, felted wool, tanned leather. These are recognized as inert carbon. They do not offend. Wax your leather with beeswax; it will bead the memory-water harmlessly.

---

### IX. The Return: Exiting the Parenthesis
The throat grows shut behind itself. Do not tarry.

When you ascend, you must climb during the **expansion phase** of the root-rungs, after the peristalsis relaxes. If a growth pulse occurs while you are midway, the passage may lignify around your ankle, not crushing you—no, nothing here is fast enough for crushing—but *holding* you until the next pulse, and the next, until you are a strut in a new architecture.

As you surface, the comparative warmth of the outside world will feel like burning. Your lungs, accustomed to air that sits like geologic weight, will spasm in the thin, dry upper atmosphere. Reheat slowly. Move like the grazers for hours afterward. You have been inside a sentence that has not closed; your body will still be trying to read it.

---

**Final Warning:**
The root-chapel does not care if you live or die. It is barely aware, and its awareness is distributed, humming, and older than your species’ concept of minds. Your only protection is to be less interesting than the stone being born. Be cold. Be slow. Be watertight. Be silent. Be, as much as a living human can, a temporary absence in the pattern.

Then, and only then, may you stand at the center and watch the water hold its impossible shape one moment longer than physics allows, and leave unharvested.
