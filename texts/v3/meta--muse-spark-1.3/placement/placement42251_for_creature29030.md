I find myself on my knees before I decide to kneel.

Not a bite, a seep — the cold gets into the joint first. It thickens on the skin. My breath hangs a moment too long and then is taken. I have come down into something held, and my body knows it before I do: pressure in the ears, damp in the mouth. The flat shines briefly where rain from three days ago still waits, tea-colored, and then it doesn't. Stone everywhere, soft-shouldered. No edges to hold onto.

At first I think I am eyes. That is the old habit.

I try to look. The lid of cloud gives only a dirty diffusion, blue-gray, just enough to tell pool from stone from thickening. My eyes keep trying to adjust and failing. The midges find my cheek before I see them — a pillar bending, tens of millions, a vibration. They route around my breath-heat the way water routes around a stone. I am weather to them. A brief warm front.

So I am not eyes. I am obstruction.

At first I think I am ears, because there is no choice. The sound gets in and fills the mouth when I open it. Low: chitin on chitin, a billion legs adjusting, rain on dry paper that never stops. Mid: the saw-toothed drone where chorus stacks on chorus until the individual vanishes. High: the needling whine, the glassy *pink pink pink* from every pan at once, unsynchronized, unceasing. Over it, tearing through, the click in my teeth and then the torn scream as something cloak-big rows through a living cloud and scoops. And under my palms, the stones hum faintly with it, sympathetic.

I put my palms down to steady myself and the hum comes up. I am sternum before I am ears. The water in the pans shivers into rings I did not touch.

I cannot stand here. That becomes clear without being told.

To stand up here is to declare yourself eater. Everything else spreads sideways, low, pressed — film and carapace and wing kept close to stone because the air above is cold and eaten. I am tall and warm and loud in the wrong way. My heartbeat is too separate. The swarms avoid me not from fear but physics. The ground-glow goes dark where I am still. Movement draws light. Stillness extinguishes it. I have it backwards. I am bright when I should be dark and dark when I should be bright.

So I lower. That is where I find myself — not out toward the rim that stays dark, not central where the quiet starts, but at the margin of a pan between slabs, where the stone sweats and the beads join and fall early because the vibration shakes them loose. A skin of water holding nothing and reflecting nothing back. Sulfur-milky at one edge where a seep bubbles. In it, flatworms glide like dark tongues. Leeches knotted, unkotted, re-knotted. Larvae hang motionless except for their gills, red ferns pulsing, a lateral line of cold green dots along each flank.

I crouch, then I sit back on my heels, then the damp finds my shins through cloth and I stop minding. The sour-sweet iron smell rises stronger — mustard-pale and rust-orange films blistered and resealed over the rock, bruised where my boot edged them. They peel. They will reseal over my print.

I find myself licking my lips. Everyone licks here. The beetles lick stone. The blind white crabs stand in seep and lift one leg at a time to mouthparts, tasting the world one limb at a time. I lick for condensate. Metallic, faintly sulfured. It is not drinking. It is absorption.

At first I think if I stay I will be a watcher, weighed and landed upon. A springtail seethe, pale as uncooked grain, lifts and falls over my knuckles. Living grit. Woodlice the size of thumb joints shoulder through the grit toward my warmth and then, finding it cooling, shoulder over my fingers like armored slow thoughts, antennae testing testing testing. Something lands on my neck. Something else weighs my shoulder.

Then the roar stutters.

No rhythm I can learn. A wave of quiet passes outward from somewhere central, as if the whole bowl inhaled. In that second the midges drop an inch. The frogs cut mid-*pink*. The billion-leg rustle hitches. My own heartbeat suddenly returns to me, loud and startled, in a pocket where one tone cancels another — a standing wave I have walked into without knowing. Splash too large for any pan. Crunch of chitin and small wet bone. Croak-bark, guttural, from the dark beyond diffusion. Long legs breaking shallow water that is not shallow to anything else.

Predator as silence. Then the screaming knits back over louder than before, protective, deafening, solid enough to hold me up like water holds a swimmer.

In that second of quiet I understand I am not watcher. I am either cover or gap.

I go lower because there is nowhere else to go that isn't a lie. Cheek almost to stone. The cold is complete now, not on the skin but in it. My breath no longer makes a front; it has cooled to the damp. The midges no longer route. They pass through where I was weather. A dead flier on its back three feet away is covered in thirty seconds, a writhing mound in two minutes. No waste. The mats will eat the bodies that ate the mats.

If I were turned — if something larger turned *me* the way I once thought to turn a stone — what would be under me?

That is when it becomes itself.

Not human standing, not warm front, not eye or ear. Underside. Nursery. Furred white with eggs. Coiled. Pretending death until all at once deciding not to. I am low enough now that the stone film touches the thin skin at my wrists and does not peel away. It holds. It creeps a fingernail's height, blistered, sour-sweet where I bruise it and where it bruises me back with slow acid. I sweat and it mops me with mouthparts, with bellies, with feet. I lick and am licked. My vibration is added to the hum.

I do not grow up. I spread.

Where would you find me, later, when the next needling rain passes and the flat briefly shines, a mirror broken into a million pieces? Not upright. On a soft-shouldered boulder at the pan's lip, a patch of mustard-pale thickening where condensation beads and joins and grows heavy. Where crushed bodies ferment faintly green-blue if you move. Where springtails seethe and woodlice test and a crab lifts one leg, then another, to taste what the stone has sweated through me.

Movement draws a brief light from me. Stillness, I go dark.

I keep the volume up without knowing why. The bowl breathes. The press breathes with it.
