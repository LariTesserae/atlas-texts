The Avanian people are creatures of water and woven green. Imagine a form both familiar and utterly alien: streamlined bodies adapted for effortless movement through the crushing depths, yet bearing a hint of humanoid grace. Their skin is a mosaic of blues and greens, patterned like the shifting algae blooms that feed their city, allowing them to almost disappear within the kelp forests.

Their hands are long and slender, tipped with delicate, yet strong, cartilaginous extensions instead of nails. These allow them to manipulate the living architecture with extraordinary dexterity, coaxing growth, pruning, and weaving with the patience of centuries. Their feet mirror their hands, each toe capable of independent movement, allowing them to grip the slick surfaces of kelp and coral with unwavering stability.

Their faces are striking, dominated by large, black eyes that absorb every scintilla of bioluminescent light, allowing them to navigate the perpetual twilight. These eyes aren't just for seeing; the Avanian also communicate through subtle shifts in the irises, displaying a spectrum of emotions impossible for outsiders to decipher. Below the eyes, their faces taper to a subtle beak-like mouth, perfectly designed for filtering nutrients from the water and emitting the complex clicks and whistles that form their language.

Emerged from the water, they are less graceful. The weight of gravity seems to trouble them, rendering them clumsy and slow, and they can only survive on land for short periods of time without access to water.

They do not wear clothing, viewing any impediment of the skin as an impediment of their contact with, and knowledge of, their environment.

The Avanian are a patient people, their lives intertwined with the slow, deliberate growth of their city. They possess an innate understanding of the ocean's currents, the subtle chemistry of the thermal vents, and the complex relationships between the myriad creatures that share their home. Their knowledge isn't scientific in the human sense, but rather a deep, intuitive connection born from generations of living within and alongside the ocean.

They are deeply communal, their lives driven by an ingrained sense of responsibility to their clan and the city as a whole. Individual ambition is subsumed by the collective need, and competition is rare. Conflict is rare, or expressed though ritualized behavior, as their numbers are few enough that conflict presents an existential threat.

Their relationship with death is pragmatic but not without reverence. When an Avanian dies, their body is integrated back into the city's ecosystem. Specialized "reef-gardens" become their final resting place, their nutrients nourishing new growth, continuing the cycle of life in Avani.

They hold a deep respect for the 'leviathans' that graze around their community, viewing the beasts not only as integral to the balance of Avani but as guardians of particular districts in the city. They've learned to predict leviathan behavior, and even coopt it to fertilize certain parts of their farmable kelp forests. They are wary of the predators that prowl the outskirts of the city, but they respect their role in maintaining the balance of the marine ecosystem. The predators are seen as neither good nor evil, simply creatures that must be understood and navigated in order to ensure the survival of oneself and the community.

The Avanian are sustained by a plant-based diet, cultivated from the vast kelp forests and supplemented with nutrients filtered from the water. They do not hunt, instead relying on their symbiotic relationship with the flora and fauna of Avani to provide everything they need.

Their understanding of time is different from our own. They measure the passage of years not by the sun's journey across the sky, but by the slow, almost imperceptible shifts in the ocean's currents and the growth cycles of the kelp forests. 

The Avanian are a testament to the resilience and adaptability of life. They are a living embodiment of harmony, a species deeply intertwined with their environment, a quiet whisper in the grand symphony of the sea, a green and blue pulse in the heart of Avani.
