In the Shivering Sprawl exist the **Remnants**, a fitting title for what remains of humanity in this impossible place. They are a study in adaptation, their bodies and minds warped by the city's unique pressures.

Physically, they are gaunt, their frames thin and wiry, built for navigating the Sprawl's cramped and treacherous passages. Their skin is pale, almost translucent, marked with a network of fine, blue veins that pulse with a faint, internal light – a byproduct of the city's ubiquitous bio-luminescence. Their eyes, however, are the most striking feature. Large, black, and deeply set, they possess an unsettling clarity, as if they see more than they should. They are adept at picking up the faintest glimmers of light in the perpetual gloom and able to perceive the subtle shifts in the city's ever-fluctuating energies. Many are augmented, their bodies laced with archaic technology, grafted onto them over generations, blurring the line between flesh and machine. Cybernetic eyes are common, offering enhanced vision in the dim light or the ability to perceive the city's hidden data streams. Some have replaced limbs with scavenged or jury-rigged prosthetics, their movements a strange ballet of natural and mechanical motion.

Survival is their guiding principle. They are scavengers and recyclers, experts in finding sustenance in the Sprawl's desolate environment. They cultivate the hardy lichens in small, hidden gardens, supplementing their diets with the mutated rodents they trap in the city's underbelly. They are masters of repurposing, finding new uses for the discarded tech of a bygone era. A broken cog becomes a weapon, a discarded circuit board a source of power.

The Remnants are quiet, withdrawn. Communication is often nonverbal: a subtle gesture, a flicker of the eyes, a shared understanding born of necessity and shared experience. They speak in hushed tones, their voices raspy and faint, careful not to disturb the Sprawl's unsettling hum. They speak in terse fragments, sharing information only when necessary, distrustful of outsiders and wary of attracting unwanted attention. Their language is a pidgin of forgotten tongues, laced with technobabble and laced with code.

Their culture is defined by a deep respect for the city, a reverence bordering on fear. They understand that the Sprawl is both their home and their prison, a dangerous and unpredictable entity that demands respect. They adhere to a set of unspoken rules, passed down through generations: never draw attention to yourself, never waste resources, never question the city's mysteries. They whisper stories of those who broke these rules, of individuals who were swallowed by the Sprawl, their disappearances attributed to reality glitches or the city's unknown predators.

They are haunted by the past, by the memory of a world that is lost forever. Old data fragments and crumbling recordings are treasured relics, offering fleeting glimpses of a time before the ice. Yet, they are not sentimental. The Remnants understand that the past is gone, and their only concern is the present. They have adapted to their reality and their existence is a testament to the tenacity and resilience of the human spirit, twisted and reshaped by the unforgiving environment of the Shivering Sprawl.
