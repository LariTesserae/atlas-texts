The Sky-Jungle teems with denizens birthed from its unique confluence of biology and industry. It's not a place for gentle creatures, for the meek wither under the constant assault of spores and steam. The inhabitants are survivors, shaped by the pressure of endless competition and the strange energies that permeate the hyper-cellulose boughs.

First, consider the **Gloomstalkers**. These are the apex predators of the higher canopies – sleek, serpentine creatures whose scales shimmer with a faint, adaptive bioluminescence. They are echolocators, painting the world in sound, navigating the labyrinthine branches with uncanny precision. Their jaws unhinge to reveal rows of phosphorescent teeth, used to lure smaller prey closer for a quick, decisive strike. They often carry a symbiotic colony of spore-mites in their hide, providing them camouflage and first dibs on their kills.

Then, there are the **Rust-Weavers**. These scavengers are more industrious than predatory. They are hunched, insectile beings that scuttle through the half-digested factories, consuming metal and waste. They possess a knack for repurposing the machinery around them. Some Weavers have even developed the ability to manipulate geothermal vents, creating localized steam clouds for defense or terraforming small patches of the jungle for specialized fungal farms. Their exoskeletons are often adorned with scraps of metal and glowing wires, salvaged from the ruins they inhabit.

Deeper within the fungal mats reside the **Myco-Engineers**. Perhaps the strangest of all, these creatures exemplify the fusion of life and machine. They are not so much 'alive' as they are grown, cultivated by the Sky-Jungle itself. They are living processors, their bodies interwoven with the architecture of the ancient factories. They maintain the flow of power and resources through the colossal organisms, acting as organic circuit boards. They communicate through a complex system of bioluminescent pulses, a language understood only by the Sky-Jungle itself. Some whisper they are made of the "souls" of the engineers who first inhabited the place, a kind of techno-shamanism.

Lower down, in the darkest reaches, are the **Spore-Kings.** They are solitary, colossal, and grotesque beings that act as a keystone species in the Sky-Jungle. Their bodies are swollen sacs filled with spores, which they periodically erupt into the environment. The Spore-Kings are not sentient beings as far as our understanding. They are more like living fungal towers, their very existence a testament to the Sky-Jungle's capacity for absurdly scaled life. Various Symbiotic relationships exist between them and other creatures.

Everywhere there are swarms. **Cloud-Mosquitoes** are the bane of the jungle. They cluster in clouds so think that they can block out the pale light of the bioluminescent fungi. Cloud-Mosquitoes are not parasites, they are detritivores; they feed off dead and decaying plant matter. They can strip a factory clean in days; their droppings act as fertilizer, seeding new life and perpetuating the cycle of growth and decay.

The Sky-Jungle is also home to numerous other beings: the **Geothermal Snails** that secrete a valuable coolant, the **Lumen-Moths** that pollinate the bioluminescent flora, the **Hyper-Cellulose Beetles** that gradually consume the structures holding the world together.

Life in the Sky-Jungle isn't about dominance, but about integration. It's about finding a niche in the endless cycle, adopting the strange gifts of the hyper-cellulose structures, and finding a way to persist in a world where the boundaries between organic and inorganic constantly blur. It's a savage, beautiful, and utterly alien existence.
