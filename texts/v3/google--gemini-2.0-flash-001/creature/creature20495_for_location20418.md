They are beings sculpted by pressure, both literal and societal. Imagine them elongated, not in stature, but in their very being. Time stretches for them, each moment heavy with the weight of the water and the echoes of generations past. They move with a deliberate stillness, a conservation born not of laziness but of a deep understanding of scarcity. Every breath, every step, every word is measured.

Their faces are pale, reflecting the sickly green light of the algae and a deeper, inner pallor from a life lived in perpetual twilight. Eyes, large and dark, adjust to the gloom, seeing the nuances of shadow and the subtle shifts in the flow of the manufactured currents. They communicate often in silence, their faces an ongoing conversation of the mind.

The engineers, the bloodline of the Fabricators' founders, are pale and gaunt. Their pale skin seems almost translucent, fragile like the delicate instruments they manipulate. They are enclaves of memory and knowledge, hoarding forbidden lore like oxygen in a vacuum. What they wear is practical, but layered with subtle details of craftsmanship from a time before industry.

The laborers are sturdy, but stooped, their bodies molded by the relentless rhythm of the machinery. Hands are calloused and stained with the strange minerals that feed the Fabricators. They are deeply familiar with the textures and hums of the deep, their hands on the pulse of the city. The labors are the backbone of the Fabricators. Without them, the city would be still.

The Deep Divers, these are the most altered, the most intriguing. The pressure, the strange chemicals, the unknown life from the deep have reshaped them. Some grow fins between their fingers, others develop a nictitating membrane over their eyes. They are marked by the deep, each scar a story, each mutation a testament to their resilience. They are often feared, but revered and highly sought. What they know of the dark sea cannot be taught. They are the eyes and ears of the Fabricators, the only ones to still touch the true ocean.
