The city of Avani sprawls beneath the waves, a tangle of living architecture rooted in the cold, deep-sea vents that exhale warmth and minerals into the perpetual night. It isn't built so much as cultivated, the enormous, bulbous stalks of kelp-like organisms woven and coaxed into dwellings, thoroughfares, and even the towering, light-emitting "sun-spires" that pulse with a bioluminescent glow.

The water here is frigid, yet the city is a network of microclimates. The thermal vents create pockets of warmth around the central districts, enabling a dazzling array of aquatic flora to thrive. Giant anemones, some the size of small houses, line the main avenues, their tentacles swaying rhythmically in the current, filtering nutrients and providing shelter for smaller creatures. Fields of phosphorescent fungi carpet the seabed between buildings, casting an ethereal, otherworldly light. The sea is a riot of living color, but it is the green of plant life which overwhelmingly dominates.

The Avanian people, who are amphibious, have developed a unique symbiosis with their plant-based city. They are gatherers and cultivators, meticulously pruning the kelp-structures, reinforcing them with secreted resins, and directing their growth along desired pathways. They are simple tool users, employing sharpened shells and obsidian blades grafted to stalks of coral for their myriad constructional, domestic, and agricultural purposes. 

The infrastructure of Avani is a testament to patience. Currents are harnessed using living filters of kelp and sponges to purify water. Waste is composted within dedicated "digestion gardens," providing rich nutrients for the city's growth. Communication is primarily auditory, with the Avanians using a complex system of clicks, whistles, and resonant vibrations amplified by specialized air bladders (whose sound extends surprisingly well through the water given the overall density of ambient noise.) 

The surrounding ocean is alive. Schools of bioluminescent fish weave through the city like shooting stars, their shimmering scales reflecting the light of the sun-spires. Large, filter-feeding leviathans graze on the outer kelp forests, their massive bodies a constant, looming presence. Packs of sleek, intelligent predators – convergently evolved to something like a 'wolf' on land – patrol the outskirts, keeping populations in check and occasionally testing the city's defenses.

Avani is loud. The groaning and creaking of the kelp stalks as they sway in the current, the constant drumming of tiny claws cleaning the kelp, the cacophony of marine life, and the reverberating clicks of the Avanian communicating all build to a wall of sound.

The city's growth and transformation are slow, measured on a geologic timescale. It ebbs and flows with the shifting currents and the gradual accumulation of sediment. Buildings are sometimes abandoned, consumed by the relentless growth of the surrounding flora, their husks becoming artificial reefs for new generations of creatures. This erosion and reclamation is a constant cycle.

Despite its teeming life, Avani is sparsely populated. The Avanian live in small, close-knit clans scattered throughout the city's vast expanse. Each clan tends to a specific region or function, maintaining the delicate balance of the ecosystem. One clan might specialize in cultivating light-emitting algae, while another focuses on repairing and reinforcing the kelp structures. Their impact is gentle, considered, and deeply intertwined with the rhythm of the ocean.

It's a place of understated wonder, this city beneath the waves. A testament to the adaptability of life, and the enduring power of patience. Avani is a world unto itself, a green and vibrant jewel nestled within the deep, a symphony of the sea.
