The Cloud-Gardens of Aethel are not gardens as a surface-dweller would understand them. They are, instead, a city built within and upon the perpetual, dripping cloud that clings to the upper slopes of the Wandering Mountain.

The "soil" of Aethel is not soil at all, but a dense mat of interwoven epiphytes, mosses, and fungal life that have, over centuries, taken on the qualities of earth. This living matrix is anchored to the bare, weeping rock of the mountain, which constantly exudes moisture, feeding the endless green. The overall effect resembles a gigantic, hanging swamp, draped across the mountainside like a verdant shroud.

The air here is always thick with mist, heavy with the scent of wet vegetation and the earthy tang of decay. The temperature is cool, perpetually hovering just above freezing, and a damp chill permeates everything. The light, though nominally "normal," is filtered and diffuse, creating a soft, muted radiance that casts long, blurred shadows.

The architecture of Aethel is entirely organic. Buildings are grown, not built, from carefully cultivated strains of fast-growing, pliable wood. Dwellings are shaped with hand tools, simple levers, and wedges, guided and woven together over years by generations of gardeners. Walls are often living tapestries of vines and flowering plants, providing insulation and aesthetic beauty. The city's infrastructure consists of woven bridges of living roots that span chasms, aqueducts carved from giant gourds that carry water, and ventilation shafts opened and closed by bioluminescent fungal valves.

Aethel's sounds are overwhelming. The constant drip and trickle of water is a low, pervasive hum, punctuated by the rustling of leaves, the creaking of tree limbs, and the ceaseless chirping, buzzing, and croaking of the cloud-forest's fauna. The air vibrates with the resonant drone of the "wind chimes" – elaborate structures of hollow gourds and woven reeds that catch the mountain breezes and amplify them into deep, mournful tones.

Life in the Cloud-Gardens is turbulent. The ecology is a riotous, competitive free-for-all, where every plant and creature struggles for light, space, and sustenance. Periodic storms sweep through the mountain, tearing at the living architecture and reshaping the landscape. Landslides and shifts in the underlying rock are common, forcing constant adaptation and rebuilding. The social structure of Aethel reflects this dynamism; families rise and fall, alliances shift, and conflicts erupt over resources and territory. Constant change is the only constant.

The fauna of Aethel is particularly profuse. Insects dominate, forming enormous, iridescent swarms that fill the air with their buzzing and clicking. Amphibians are abundant, slithering through the dense vegetation and croaking their mating calls. Arboreal mammals, adapted to the misty, arboreal environment, scamper through the canopy, their calls adding to the symphony of the cloud-forest. There are even rumors of larger, more mysterious creatures that lurk in the uncharted depths of the cloud below the city.

Despite the chaos, Aethel is a place of strange beauty and resilience. The inhabitants, known as the Arborians, are deeply connected to their environment, understanding its rhythms and working in harmony with its forces. They are skilled gardeners, weavers, and tinkers, utilizing the resources around them to create a unique and sustainable way of life. Their tools are simple – sharpened stones, woven fibers, and carefully cultivated plants – but their knowledge is vast and their connection to the living world profound. They have learned to navigate the turbulence, finding stability in the constant state of flux, and building a vibrant, thriving city within the heart of the cloud.
