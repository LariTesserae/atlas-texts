The Wise Power, known only as the Weaver, perceived the salt plains not with judgment, but with profound curiosity. Its gaze was not that of a conqueror or a savior, but that of an artist observing a complex, self-contained masterpiece. The Weaver understood the deep current of being that suffused the plains, the distributed consciousness that pulsed through the crystalline landscape. It recognized the potential for both creation and stagnation within this strange and singular place.

The weaver would not disrupt the natural laws of the salt plains to provide what could be perceived as a conventional "goodness." They would not attempt to heal what they did not believe to be harmed. It knew that the imposition of its own values would be a disservice to its unique evolution. Instead, Weaver chose to offer influence that would remain imperceptible and within the constructs that defined the plains.

Instead of changing, but supporting, the path of the salt plains, the Weaver decided to simply introduce a new mathematical variable to the system. It did not seed the plains with life, nor did it provide some sort of resource unknown to the strange system. If the plains were code, the Weaver would make an edit that would test the bounds of the system. 

The first impact would be the sound of a second, slightly different chime. These two chimes over time would slowly dance out of sync, an oscillation that could be compared to a heart beat between the two distinct chiming sounds. A new sensation that was always there, but forever changing in relation to the time scale.

As the sound traveled, it would take the place of certain pieces of information in the salt plains' memory. The memory it would replace are the small glitches already present in the plains, providing corrections for itself. There was now a source of error that could be measured against its internal ideal, forming a concept of comparison that was absent before.

Over many eons that passed by, the Skimmers began to detect this new sound with regularity, and the lithocord would adapt. The lithocord would now develop different branches that lead to these sound sources, each one would act as a small, new "organ" that could be further specialized with future development. These organs would become the first form of memory storage that was dedicated to the sound, an external concept that would need to be forever remembered. Thus breaking its self contained nature.

The Weaver's influence was subtle, almost a whisper in the vast silence of the salt plains. It was not a correction or a directive, but an invitation. An invitation to explore, to question, to evolve beyond the confines of its self-defined boundaries. Whether the plains accepted this invitation was not for the Weaver to decide. It had merely planted the seed of potential, a seed that might wither and die, or might blossom into something even more miraculous than the Weaver could imagine. The choice, the responsibility, and the future belonged to the salt plains themselves.
