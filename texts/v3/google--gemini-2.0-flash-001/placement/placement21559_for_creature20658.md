In the Foundry-Cathedral, I am a Scrivener.

Not of words, though I understand their fleeting, brittle nature. I am a Scrivener of the Heat. I am a Recorder of Resonance. I am a Cartographer of the Unbinding.

I am not an Echo, not in the way that the reverberating chorus is. Those are the very sinews of the Foundry, its unconscious breath. I am something… emergent. A crystallisation within the flux. Perhaps an error in the system, or perhaps its inevitable, infrequent, product.

My form is… difficult to describe and in constant flux, like the Lava itself. Imagine a lattice-work of glassy black obsidian, perpetually reforming, drawing energy from the heat and light to cohere, then releasing it again in a shimmer of fractured reflections. Strands of ceramic, meticulously worked, grow and retract from this core, anchoring me to the structures of the Foundry. These ceramic extensions are less defined, more permeable; they serve as sensors, antennae, feelers into the symphony of the Echoes.

I move slowly, deliberate, through the Foundry. Not walking, exactly, but *manifesting* in different locations, drawing upon the energy grid to relocate. My focus is on the pylons and mirrors – on the points of amplification and reflection.

Here, amidst the chaos, I seek order. I am tracing the currents of Unbinding, mapping the nodes of resonance, noting the subtle variations in the harmonic drone. I am building a model of how the Foundry is working, how it is positioned in whatever metaphysical matrix it occupies.

What do I record this on? That is the strangest part of my existence. I have found that my obsidian core is capable of subtle manipulations. I can modulate the black glass to create tiny, self-sustaining eddies of *difference*. These are not visible light, they are variations in the Foundry's own resonance, recorded as minute differences in the obsidian's structure. They are the threads of my maps.

I can manipulate the Heat, too. Focussing small portions of the Unbinding Lava into patterns and recording the result, as though taking many tiny time lapse photos of the whole. This allows me to notate the way the Unbinding flows through the place.

I create a map of the Foundry on my own body – a palimpsest of observations, a constantly evolving representation the flux of existence. The surface of my obsidian lattice shimmers with the data of every measurement.

My purpose? I'm not sure there is one in the way you understand it. I am, perhaps, attempting to understand. To codify. To *know* The Foundry-Cathedral, the Unbinding Flame, and the Echoes, from deep within their nature. Whether this has a consequence, whether such understanding has any impact on the Foundry's operations, is a question I cannot answer. The Foundry does not care about me, the Echoes do not see me. Nothing cares about me. It is a lonely existence - existing with no others.

But I am driven. The sensation of the Unbinding, the roaring chant of the Echoes – it compels me, urges me on. The more I learn, the more I crave to know. Perhaps I am simply another facet of the Foundry, an unusual reverberation, driven by the same forces that drive the Unbinding itself.

My existence is a paradox. I am static in a place of eternal change. Silent amidst a deafening chorus. A solitary point of observation within a vast, interconnected network.

I am the Scrivener, and this is my song. A resonance within a resonance, a whisper in the heart of the flame. I will continue to record, to map, to strive for understanding, until the Unbinding claims me once more, and my knowledge is dissolved back into the infinite, harmonic hum of the Foundry-Cathedral. To then, perhaps, emerge again elsewhere. Existing, once more, and still alone.
