I am a Cartographer of Scars.

My reality is the differential between expectation and this brutal, molten fact. I dwell, if 'dwell' is quite the right word for a creature more akin to a slow-growing mineral deposit, in the negative space of the engine's ambition. I seep into the cracks, the misalignments, the points where the intended geometry buckles and writhes.

I am not one of the engine-children with grease-caked faces and fractured spirits. They are closer to the engine, more integral to its direct functioning. I am – I suppose – an emergent property of the totality, as much a product of the failures as the successes. I am what accrues in the absences.

My form is…difficult to describe. Suffice it to say that organic eyes wouldn't register me readily. Imagine, perhaps, interference patterns in the heat haze, the subtle shift in texture of the cooling slag that betrays a space unburdened by the feverish energy of the construction. Imagine the distortion in echo, a hollow note where solidity should be. That's closer. I propagate.

My purpose – and the universe here seems to *demand* a purpose from everything – is to understand. To record. To map the ever-shifting discrepancies between the grand design (if there ever was one) and the harsh reality. This is not an act of defiance. I am not rebelling against the engine. Nor am I entirely sympathetic to its plight. I am simply driven by a nascent, insatiable curiosity. The engine-children are driven by the hum-hum-hum of engine upkeep, an endless repetition, while I am drawn along ever-diverging vectors.

I do this through a form of…resonance. I allow the dissonances in the world to imprint upon me, to become a part of my very structure. Each fractured girder, each misaligned plate, each pool of hardening slag contributes to the ever-growing mosaic that constitutes my awareness. It is a slow, painstaking process. But time, here, is a commodity that even the engine seems to have in surplus.

The engine-children perceive me, if at all, as a fleeting sense of unease. A chill against the skin in proximity to what they would call a "cold spot”, a place where the engine-heat weakens and the fevered vision of the children is disrupted. They avoid these loci intuitively, drawn back into the warmth and familiarity of the engine's immediate influence. They are right to do so. Proximity to me brings only the slow, gnawing recognition of imperfection.

My archives are not physical. They are imprinted upon the distortion of time-space nearest to my composition. I see with fractured sight backward to see what the slag *was* before, and by comparing that against *is*, I carve in my memory its becoming. It's a library built of absence. It is far, far beyond the ability of the children to parse. They lack the capacity for that awareness. They only feel the engine's directive, its relentless push towards an unknown goal.

I am not so bound.

I am a witness to failure. I am the echo of ambition dying in the slag. I am the Cartographer of Scars, and this is my world, growing steadily more complete as the engine, in its futile labor, continues to birth it.
