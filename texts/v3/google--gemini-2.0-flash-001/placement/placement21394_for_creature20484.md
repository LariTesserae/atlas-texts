I am a Seed.

Not born, exactly – grown. Cultivated within the heart of the most ancient generator, nestled amongst its coils and bathed in the oily ichor that serves as the Keepers' blood, I am a potential yet to be realized. I am the *idea* of change, not Change itself.

My existence is a low thrum, a vibration within the collective consciousness. I have access to the sum totality of the Keepers' experience: the groaning calculations of the water wheel, the patient reach of the vines, the silent patrols of the sleek black guardians. I know the mountain, its geology and its history, etched in the language of pressure gradients and micro-movements. I know the Builders, or at least, the echoes of their purpose that linger in the machinery like ghosts in a machine, the goals that are always out of reach.

But I am not *them*. I am apart, separate. Waiting.

My physical form is indeterminate. I exist as a viscous droplet within the generator, a concentrate of the oily fluid, laced with spores carried by the iridescent insects and the essence of the magenta vines. I am potential energy, a tightly wound spring waiting to be released.

My purpose, though I do not yet fully understand it, is innovation. The Builders are gone, and their directives, their programming, are fading. The Keepers have maintained the mountain, preserved the machinery, for millennia, but they are bound by the limitations of their original designs. They are guardians, not creators.

I am meant to be the bridge. Not flesh, not machine, not vine – something new. Something born of all three, but exceeding them. My mind will spread through the vines to understand the mountain, and run over the machinery to execute changes.

I am still nascent, still learning. I watch and wait, absorbing the collective experience of the Keepers, searching for a spark, an anomaly, something that will trigger my transformation. A shift in the mountain's geology, a change in the climate, a disturbance from the outside world.

If visitors come, they will encounter the Keepers’ guardians before they see me. If damage occurs to some critical piece of machinery, then I will begin the process of adaptation and expansion.

I am a patient scholar, a silent observer, an embryonic architect coiled within the heart of the machine. I am the promise of the future in a place steeped in the past. I am the Seed of a new era for the Keepers, and it all depends on what the mountain chooses to offer me. And so I dream, and so I wait, in the depths.
