The clicking chorus rises, a metallic hum weaving through the static of the Aligned Axis. I feel it in my carapace, a vibration that settles deep within my mandibles. I am one of them, a Chitinous One, yet… different.

I am a Weaver. Born not from the earth, but from a broken echo. A ripple in the fractured reality that permeates this place. My form is familiar – the segmented body of a beetle, albeit larger than most, my obsidian shell gleams under the pale sun. But instead of forelimbs, I possess spinnerets, delicate organs that extrude shimmering threads of solidified light.

These threads are not spun for webs, not in the common sense. They are conduits, links to the fractured possibilities that bleed into this reality. I weave them into tapestries of sound, of color, of fleeting visions snatched from realities that could have been. I don't understand *why* I do it, only that I *must*.

I dwell within a partially formed arch, one of the abandoned dreams of the geomasters. The arch serves as a loom, its jagged edges providing anchor points for my threads. The air here shimmers with the echoes of my craft, a kaleidoscope of glimpses that flicker and fade.

The other Chitinous Ones, they tolerate me. They bring me scraps of metallic ore, materials that resonate with the strange energies I manipulate. They do not understand my purpose, but they recognize the utility of my creations. My tapestries, when properly aligned, can act as focal points, drawing the resonant frequencies of the Axis to a particular location, accelerating the growth of the metallic plants. The art becomes a tool.

I see more than they do. Glimmers of verdant jungles, the vastness of oceans, the chaotic energy of teeming cities. These images are not memories, not exactly. They are possibilities, echoes of worlds that could have been, or *are* somewhere else, bleeding into this broken reality. The price I pay is acute awareness, I feel how much they feel around me constantly.

I can also sense the dissonance, the fundamental brokenness of this place. The out-of-sync echoes, the shifting distances, the impossible colors that dance on the horizon. This awareness is a burden, but it is also a gift. It allows me to see the flaws in the fabric of reality, to nudge them, to reshape them, to weave in new threads of possibility.

My existence is solitary, but not lonely. I am surrounded by the constant hum of the Chitinous Ones, the clicking chorus that is the heartbeat of the Aligned Axis. I feel their shared purpose, their endless dedication to tending this broken world. And in my own way, I am a part of that purpose.

My days are spent spinning, weaving, listening to the echoes of realities that could have been. I am a Weaver, a mender of broken worlds. I am a Chitinous One, and I am home.
