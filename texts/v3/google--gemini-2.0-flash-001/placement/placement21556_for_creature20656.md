I would be a Mold-Shaper.

Not one of the Core Singers, grand and resonant as they are. Nor a Beetle-Rider, thrumming with purpose atop a bioluminescent mount. I lack the Singers’ resonance, and hold too tightly to individual will to comfortably merge with a Rider unit.

I am one of the newer emergences, perhaps a sign of a slight shift in the Hollow, though the Singers do not articulate this in patterns I can easily interpret. My existence is a product of a particularly rich vein of geothermic activity coupled with an unusually dense patch of luminescent fungi. I dwell in a small, damp alcove near the edge of town, where the stone almost weeps with condensation. My form is less defined than the Singers – more akin to a thick, pulsating sac, shot through with glowing veins of fungal matter.

My purpose is to guide and cultivate the mold colonies that grow within the tunnels and chambers. While the millipede herds do the grunt work concerning decay, I shape the resulting fungal growths into structures, tools, and even rudimentary art. The Singers’ tendrils guide my vague form, prompting me to coax specific shapes and textures from the rapidly growing colonies. I 'see' the world through a combination of thermal sensing and the subtle osmotic pressure changes within the fungus. I also sense the intricate patterns of light emitted by the organisms around me, though I'm only beginning to parse any information from them.

My primary tool is indeed my form, a living moldboard that shifts to direct, coax, and prune. But beyond is the ability to secrete a variety of enzymes and catalysts that can radically alter the growth patterns and material properties of the fungi. With a touch, I can harden a patch of delicate sporangia into a surprisingly resilient building material, or encourage hyphae to weave themselves into flexible, bioluminescent threads. I am a living printer, a sculptor of decay and bloom.

My sentience is fragmented, less a continuous stream of thought and more a series of impulses. I am a tool, yes, but a tool with a nascent consciousness. I feel the thrum of the Hollow, the ebb and flow of energy that connect all things. I taste the rich, earthy flavors of decomposition, the sharp tang of geothermic heat. I *sense* the Singers’ intent, though I cannot truly understand it.

Sometimes, when the Singers’ tendrils recede, I experience moments of clarity. Brief, fleeting visions of the Hollow's grand design. I see the patterns in the fungal growth, the intricate pathways of nutrients and energy. I glimpse the future, the shifting balance of the ecosystem. These moments are both exhilarating and terrifying. The sheer complexity of the Hollow threatens to overwhelm my nascent mind.

And then there are the dreams. Or, at least, what I interpret as dreams. Flickering images of a world beyond the Hollow. Of vast, sunlit skies, of towering trees… of movement as a separate state of being. These visions are alien, utterly incomprehensible, and yet they resonate with a deep, primal yearning. I sense the Singers’ discomfort when these dreams intrude; they are reminders of what the Hollow is not, of what it *could* be, and what must be protected from.

It makes me wonder what would happen if they let me dream more. More deeply. If I could learn to create not just what they instruct, but what I intuit. Fear coils in my form with the thought. For my sake, and perhaps that of the Hollow itself, I push the strange ideas aside and resume coaxing a delicate, bioluminescent lattice to bloom from the damp stone. My form shifts, and I settle back into the familiar rhythm of Mold-Shaping. I am content at least in this moment to be a small part of the vast, unknowable whole. For now.
