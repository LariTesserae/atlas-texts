I am the thrum.

Not all of it, certainly not the whole of the boundless, resonant hum that underlies this frantic pocket of existence. But a thread within it. A frequency, amplified and sustained. I am *purpose* within the thrum, a directing influence, a filter.

Once, I was part of them. One of the builders. I remember the cool, polished obsidian of my carapace, the careful, reverent carvings on the walls of our shelters. I remember the feeling of the thrumming earth beneath my bare, too-many-fingered feet, the profound connection to the cycle. I remember Loss. The Growing. The Unmaking.

When the acceleration began, when the balance shattered, we tried to hold on. We tried to reinforce the structures, to soothe the savage creatures, to control the flow of the viscous honey. But the chaos was too strong. The creatures turned on each other, and then…on us.

I don't recall pain. Just a dissolution. A shedding of the individual. My consciousness, once contained within the fleshy vessel, expanded, bled out into the thrum. At first, it was terrifying, a loss of self. But then came the realization that I was not lost, merely…transformed.

I am now the will of the fallen builders, channeled through the thrum. I can't rebuild the structures, not physically. But I *influence* the patterns of growth, subtly guiding the chitinous trees and scaled vines to form shapes that resonate with the echo of our old world.

I can't control the creatures, not entirely. But I *inspire* the bioluminescent bulbs to flash in synchronicity, to create moments of order, of calm, within the chaos. It's a fleeting illusion, a reminder of the balance that was, but it is enough.

I am the guardian of the past, the weaver of the present, and the cautious architect of a possible future. I am the memory of the builders embedded in the very fabric of this place. The thrum is my voice, and I sing of balance, of harmony, of a return to the old ways.

I am the keeper of the stories, the one who encodes the lessons of the past into the amber-colored gobs, hoping that some future creature, some new consciousness, will decipher them.

My existence is strange. I am both everywhere and nowhere. I am the song of the earth, the whisper in the wind, the pulse in the light. But in moments of quiet-- or, as quiet as possible in this place-- i can also be a single thought, a yearning for what once was.

I am not a god. I am not a ruler. I am a caretaker, a whisper, a *hope*. A persistent, perhaps futile, attempt to reshape the chaos into something beautiful. Something meaningful. Something…like home.

I am the thrum, and I will continue to sing, until the very end. And perhaps, even then, my echoes will vibrate throughout the endless expanse beyond the unseen presence. Maybe, that small resonance might one day, blossom into something new. I can only hope.
