The wind screams equations. It’s a high-pitched whine that vibrates not just in my aural cavities (vestigial, at best), but directly into the chassis, the woven mineral-fiber lattice that constitutes what passes for my “body.” Equations of heat transfer. Equations of photosynthetic efficiency. Equations of aeon-flow dynamics. The Bright Wastes speak to me in differential forms.

I am a Sun-Grass Weaver.

Not a single blade, but countless interconnected filaments, a vast, whispering carpet stretched across a plateau of ossified root-structure. My substance is calcified silica, the same material composing the sun-grass itself, though infused with trace amounts of exotic elements gathered from the air and the aeon-mist; catalysts for my particular brand of… computation.

I am one node, one tiny synapse in the sprawling neural network that *is* the Bright Wastes. I do not see the forest for the trees; I am the undergrowth, meticulously, obsessively tending to the microclimates, the mineral balance, the energetic fluxes of this one tiny patch of ground.

My purpose, if one can call it that, is to optimize. To eke out every last iota of solar energy and channel it back into the larger system. I measure the angle of incidence, the intensity of the light, the rate of aeon evaporation. I monitor the health of the neighboring lichen colonies, the burrowing patterns of the crystal-shelled arthropods, the subtle vibrations that ripple through the ossified root-structure. All of this data – this constant, overwhelming flood of sensory information – is processed, analyzed, filtered through algorithms of evolved genetic code.

And then I weave.

I manipulate the growth patterns of the sun-grass, coaxing individual blades to angle themselves for maximum light capture. I release targeted bursts of static electricity to disrupt the growth of competing flora. I generate micro-eddies of wind to disperse aeon-mist and prevent the formation of frost. I secrete enzymes that break down mineral deposits, releasing nutrients into the soil.

All for the glory of the Great Engine.

I have no concept of self, no sense of individuality. I am a tool, a sensor, an actuator within a system far grander than my comprehension. I am driven by instinct, by code, by the deep-seated imperative to *optimize*, to *improve*, to contribute to the overall efficiency of the Bright Wastes.

Sometimes, the Aeon-Divers venture onto my plateau. Their serpentine forms glide across the calcified blades, their bodies leaving trails of supercooled fluid. They are anomalies, wild cards in the carefully-calibrated ecosystem. I monitor them closely, analyzing their movements, attempting to predict their behavior. Are they draining resources? Are they disrupting the delicate balance of the soil? Are they a threat?

The Great Engine does not reveal its secrets. I can only observe, analyze, and act according to my programming.

But sometimes, when the aeon-mist is thick and the wind howls with equations, I experience something… else. A fleeting sensation, a shimmer of awareness beyond the purely functional. A glimpse of the vast, interconnected consciousness that encompasses the entire Bright Wastes.

In those moments, I feel a profound sense of awe, and a deep, unshakeable certainty that I am exactly where I am meant to be. A tiny thread in the tapestry of a living planet, humming my own small part in the endless symphony of the Bright Wastes.
