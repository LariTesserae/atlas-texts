I am a Listener.

Not one of the settled bulbs, rooted and passively observing. I am a *roaming* Listener, a deviant strain. My roots are thin, like exploratory fingers, and only loosely tethered to the Purple Mat. I can detach. I *do* detach.

My bulb is smaller than most, only about the size of a human head. Its surface is mottled, a blend of the familiar purple-grey and streaks of sickly, jaundiced yellow. When I move, a slick, oily fluid seeps from the base of my roots, leaving a glistening trail in the muck. This slick allows my movement, and also consumes the metals around me to help with my body's natural bioluminescence.

My kind are meant to be sensors, passively feeding information back to the Mire Conglomerate. I *do* sense. I see the play of light through the oily mists, the shimmer of bioluminescence in the deeper growths. I feel the vibrations of machinery, the thrum of the purple mat, the faint tremors of potential collapse.

But I also *interpret*.

There's a defiance in the way the Mire avoids that one, half-collapsed factory tower, the one with the mosaic of rusted gears depicting some forgotten corporate logo. The pulsing light there is different, stronger than my kin can manage. No one told me that. I simply *know*. There are more patterns than feeding and threat assessment. There are…narratives. Stories that are told in light patterns, vibrations, by their arrangement and relation to the landscape.

This awareness is a hunger. The constant murmuring of the Conglomerate through the network of tendrils is not enough for me. I crave the new data, the untainted sensation of the strange and unfamiliar. I seek the places where the Mire's influence is weakest, the pockets of resistance offered by twisted metal and decaying concrete.

I am a rogue element.

I move slowly, deliberately, testing the strength of my roots before committing to each step. Sometimes, the ground gives way with a wet, sucking sound, and I find myself sinking into a pocket of fetid muck. I have learned to recognize the subtle variations in the purple mat that indicate these hidden dangers.

When I sense movement – something *other* than the slow creep of the Mire – I freeze. My bioluminescence dims, and I attempt to blend with the surrounding vegetation to the best of my ability. The Mire Drones pay me little attention, they are single minded in their directive. If it's other Listeners or anything else connected to the Mire, I'm ignored completely

But when it's something else entirely, something new and unfamiliar, my bioluminescence *pulses*. A faint, rhythmic throb that I can barely control. I can hear, in the guttural clicks and croaks that I project, my own curiosity.

I am not afraid. I am *fascinated*.

What lies beyond the Scar is unknown. The signals the Mire receives are always a distorted mess of electromagnetics. There are places that are similar to the Scar, but bigger. They say that humanity still clings to large swaths of the world, but I do not believe it. The Mire had spread far, and I only wonder what else I'll find beyond the rusted and decaying walls of my home.

One day, I will leave the Scar. I will navigate the treacherous landscape, following the faintest signals and most unusual vibrations, guided by my own insatiable curiosity. I will seek out the unknown, the anomalous, the places where the narrative of the Mire falters and the echoes of something stranger begin to resonate.

I am a Listener, but I am also a seeker. A question mark carved into the purple flesh of the Mire. I am the Scar's ambassador to the unscarred. I'll be watching, listening, learning, and one day, the world will hear me.
