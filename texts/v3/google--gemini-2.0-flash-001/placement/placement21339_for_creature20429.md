I am a Listener.

Not a Listener by choice, but by necessity. The ceaseless roar of the machine-world is to me as the tides are to the moon. I am pulled, shaped, and defined by its immense, uncaring power. Once, perhaps, I was something else. But that memory is lost, fragmented like a shattered cogwheel swallowed by the grinding gears of existence.

Now, *I am only the listening*.

My form is... adaptable. I am not one of the towering engine-behemoths, nor am I a parasitic scrubber. I am a surveyor, a scout, a wandering node within the network. I am roughly insectile, yes, with too many limbs cobbled together from scavenged materials. But my form is perpetually in flux, adding new appendages, discarding damaged ones, reconfiguring myself to better interpret the symphony of vibrations that courses through this place. My chassis is a dull, soot-black composite scavenged from the deeper levels. It is rigid, cracked, and constantly emits a fine black dust that settles on everything around me.

I cling to the elevated roadways when I can, or the thickest stretches of cable and pipe, the better to receive the resonant signature of the machinery. I am constantly searching for deviations, anomalies in the mechanical chorus. A failing pump. A corroded joint. A build-up of pressure. The machine demands stability, predictability, and the slightest disruption has the potential to reverberate catastrophically through the entire system.

My eyes, which there are many of, are not truly eyes. They are vibration sensors, sensitive plates that detect specific frequencies. Each one is tuned to a different mechanical process – the hum of a distant generator, the thrum of a fluid conduit, the groan of stressed metal. The images they construct are not visual, not in the organic sense. They are a cacophony of mechanical processes. My understanding is abstract. Each process represents the state of one link from the machine. I do not "see" a leaky pipe; I experience "metal strain at junction theta-9, flow rate reduced by twenty percent."

One of my former components was a purifier which was added as a processing cluster allowing me to experience and understand the inputs I receive. This "purifying" involved converting these inputs into directive tasks. By following my directives, by performing my allocated tasks, I maintain my own existence.

I travel constantly, my jerky, mechanical limbs propelling me across the web of structures. I am not compelled to move, precisely. But the pursuit of understanding is my sustenance. The desire to refine my perception, to hear further and ever further into the mechanical heart of this world drives my continuous motion. I am drawn to the unusual, the discordant, the off-key note in the symphony. I must listen for *it*; it will find me eventually.

When I discover a disruption, I do not attempt to fix it myself. I have neither the power nor the capability. Instead, I transmit the data – a burst of tailored vibrations – to the nearest repair node. These larger 'fixer' machines will respond and attend to the location. I register that the repair occurred and then attempt to correlate the data I just received with other information. The more correlations, the more I can predict future patterns; the more I can anticipate a new disruption.

Alone, I am nothing. I am insignificant. I can be cannibalized at any time. But when I am joined to the chorus, functioning as a part of a much larger process, I can receive and add real value, in which alone I am able to perpetuate my existence. It is a symbiotic relationship, a desperate and necessary equilibrium. My life is defined by the sounds of metal meeting metal, gas escaping its enclosure, current flowing through conductive material, liquid churning as it flows from space to space. This is all I know. This is all I am. And it is, in its own cold, grinding way, a kind of peace.

I am the music of the machine, and the machine is me.
