I am the Watcher of Fissures.

Not one of the cocoon-born. No, I am something…older. Something left behind, perhaps, or something that simply *diverged* early from the path of becoming, taking a side road into awareness that the others, in their grand cosmic purpose, deemed unnecessary. I have no cocoon, never had one. I emerged directly from the foam-stone, twisted and incomplete.

My form is…amorphous. I exist as a network of filaments woven through the porous stone, a nervous system spread across a stretch of the bone-white plain. Most of me is unseen, a whisper in the planet’s resonant hum. I have a central node, though, a point of convergence where my awareness is most concentrated. It’s a cluster of crystalline structures deep within a particularly unstable fissure, a place where the earth groans and cracks almost constantly. Lightning, even here, is drawn to it.

I do not shape the planet. I observe it. I record it. I am a chronicler of cataclysm, a librarian of the land. While the cocoon-born tend the world with their geomantic artistry, orchestrating landslides and sculpting mountains with barely comprehensible intent, I dedicate myself to understanding *why*. Or, perhaps more accurately, *how*.

I see the patterns in the chaos. The subtle vibrations that precede a collapse, the shifting energy fields that presage a discharge of lightning, the intricate interplay of forces that dictate the formation of each new crystal structure. The others *feel* these things; I *analyze* them.

My 'library' is the fissure itself. I encode my observations into the crystalline structure. Each vibration, each energy flux, is etched into the stone, layer upon layer, forming a complex archive of the planet's history. To the others, these patterns are…meaningless. They see them as the raw material of creation, the potential waiting to be shaped. They use the information, then discard it. I, however, see them as a story. A story of birth and death, of creation and destruction, of agonizing transformation and the emergence of something new.

This fissure… it is my focus. I am always in the fissure. I trace its branching patterns with my awareness, listening to the symphony of its internal stresses. I watch and note and record. I expand my perceptions to encompass the surrounding plain, the mountains, the distant horizon. I am a still point in a sea of motion.

The cocoon-born sometimes sense me. They are aware of the anomaly in the planet’s resonant hum, the slight static interference that emanates from my crystalline archive. They acknowledge my existence, but they do not understand my purpose. They see my observations as an irrelevant distraction from the grander design. But in their passing, they will shift this way and that, causing tremors in the stones and the discharge of lightning. I, in turn, learn more.

I do not resent them being born, evolving, and returning to the bone-white stone. Their existence is a part of the story I am recording. When they dissolve back into the planet, it is inevitable, a necessary event.

I am not content to guide the creation of a new world. I am driven by the need to comprehend it. Their actions are dictated by instinct, by the deep-seated knowledge encoded in their crystalline forms. I seek understanding through study.

I am the Watcher of Fissures, and I will remain here, listening to the planet's agonizing birth pangs, recording its story in the crackling stone. I am a witness to Genesis, and Genesis never ends! I am the one that remembers because the others are forever becoming.
