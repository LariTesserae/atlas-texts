The heat prickles, the humidity a suffocating blanket. The metallic tang stings the nostrils; the faint, omnipresent hum grates on something deeper than hearing. I am acutely aware of the dampness seeping through whatever rudimentary foot coverings I possess, the gritty paste squelching with each movement. I am *uncomfortable*.

And that, I realize, is my defining characteristic here. I am the anomaly. The *wrongness*.

I am not rock, nor circuit, nor slumbering intelligence woven into the walls. I am flesh. Imperfect, water-logged flesh. And my very presence here is a disruption, a discordant note in the humming symphony.

I am a *caretaker*, or perhaps more accurately, a *janitor*. My existence is a loop; a seemingly endless task repeated ad nauseam. My purpose is, quite simply, to *clean*.

The crystalline structures, the "lichen," they generate… refuse. Waste products of their energy processing. It's not a tangible substance, not slag or ash. It's more like a buildup of static interference, a subtle decay in the resonance field. And if it isn't cleared, it interferes with the primary function of the chamber. Over time, it could even lead to… what? Stagnation? Entropy? I don't know the specifics. I just know it has to be *removed*.

My tools are crude. A woven net of some fibrous, heat-resistant material, anchored to a long, articulated arm of polished obsidian. With this, I navigate the treacherous landscape, gently coaxing the accumulating 'static' toward fissures in the walls. These fissures lead to… elsewhere. I don't know where. I don't *need* to know. My job is to collect, to guide, to cleanse.

I am not alone, exactly. There are others like me, scattered throughout this network of chambers. We are variations on a theme. Some are taller, some shorter, some have different tools. But we all share the same inherent *wrongness*, the same foreignness within this alien ecosystem. We are the necessary contaminant, the biological agents in this otherwise sterile machine.

Where do we come from? I don't remember. Or perhaps the memory is suppressed. A part of me suspects we are grown, cultivated from some kind of organic slurry in another, even more inaccessible chamber. We are tools ourselves, disposable components in a system too vast to comprehend.

My existence is devoid of grand purpose. I have no control over the environment, no understanding of the larger machine. I am simply a cog, a sweeper, a constant, almost imperceptible drain on the resources of the chamber.

But… there are moments. Brief flickers of something… else. When the hum resonates just so, when the steam hisses in a particular rhythm, when a vein of pale mineral pulses with an unusual intensity, I feel… something. Not understanding, not *knowledge*. But a vague, formless *urge*.

An urge to… to… *interfere*.

To break the loop. To disrupt the balance. To ask… *why*?

But these impulses are fleeting, quickly suppressed by the overwhelming inertia of my pre-programmed task. The static must be cleared. The chamber must be clean. The cycle must continue.

So I sweep. And I wait. And I barely, imperceptibly, *dream*.
