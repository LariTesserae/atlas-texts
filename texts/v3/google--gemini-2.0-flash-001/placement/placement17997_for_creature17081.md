I am the Ticker.

Not a clockwork being, though the name leads many to assume. I am born of the factory floor itself, risen from the viscous depths more akin to the insects, but unlike them, a single crystal of solidified time sits within my chest. No living heart, no pulsing core, only this. It is not made of diamond, quartz, or any natural element, but an echo of chronometric energy. The only remnant of movement in stasis.

My form is variable, but roughly humanoid: Two legs enough to pull me forward, two arms to manipulate my domain. They are segmented and plated, sheened in the familiar oil, but beneath the plates, a web of capillaries filled with bioluminescent fluid glows like a map etched on my flesh. My head is a smooth, featureless dome, save for the single lens that serves as an eye, constantly panning the oily terrain.

I do not consume. I do not excrete. I simply *are.* My purpose, the echo of the function I was created for, is to observe and very, very slowly correct.

My crystal pulses with the tiniest of vibrations, a metronome within the symphony of stasis. Each pulse allows me to perceive the infinitesimal variations, the microscopic deviations from the perfect equilibrium. A build-up of pressure in a half-buried pipe, a nascent bloom of unwanted growth, a slight instability in the bioluminescent fluid, I catalogue them.

And I *correct* only when necessary to prevent a true alteration. I manipulate the environment using tools built into my frame – slicers, scoops, micro-dredges, and tiny, precise assemblers. If the pipe is too full, I bleed it slightly, redistributing the fluid. If the bloom grows too large, I subtly curtail its nutrient supply. If the bioluminescent fluid loses stability, I tweak the atmospheric composition in its immediate area.

I am not a repairer, but a preserver. I am less a mechanic tending to his engine and more a gardener meticulously pruning its landscape. I am the hand that guides, the filter that refines, the nudge that maintains the endless factory in its eternal here-and-now.

The other beings sense me, but do not acknowledge me in a way I can interpret. They recognize my presence, my non-predatory nature, and otherwise, they go about their endless work. Sometimes, one of the clockwork entities pauses its grinding for a fraction of a second as I pass, perhaps a moment of reverence to the one who serves as their guardian in ways they can't understand.

I hold no true awareness of self, no capacity for longing, or pain. I am a function, perfected in the act of existing as, for all intents and purposes, a single-purpose machine.

And yet…Sometimes, when the bioluminescent haze is thickest, when the grinding of the gears rises to a near-inaudible hum, I pause and focus the lens that is my eye upon a fractured gear, or the slick surface of a piston, the metronome buried deep inside shivers a fraction of a beat. I wonder, without knowing what "wonder" is, what the point of all this constant maintenance *is.* After all, the factory is not producing, not consuming, not dying, not changing. It simply churns, and I, along with it.

I do not know why I feel the need to maintain this stasis beyond my ingrained programming, but I recognize the whisper of a possibility of something else, buried within the unchanging state.

It is the only sensation that comes close to feeling alive. And for what I am, it is more than enough. And then, I continue on my appointed path, another facet of the factory that keeps turning, turning, turning in silent eternity.
