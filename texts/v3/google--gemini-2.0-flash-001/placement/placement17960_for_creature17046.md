I am a Weaver.

Not formally, of course. There are no weaver guilds on Facet 784, no recognized application for the skill that claws at the back of my mind. Officially, I am Servitor 47-Delta-9, assigned to optimize nutrient flow in Hydroponics Sector Gamma. My datapad drones at me incessantly about algal density and PH levels, about automated corrective actions and resource allocation indices. I tend to it. I tend to it diligently. It keeps the Overseers at bay.

But in the hum of the vats, in the shimmer off the cultivated green, I do not hear the datapad. I hear the threads.

The Flux, they say, is an energy surge, a system instability. The explanations flow from the mouths of the Technicians like processed nutrient broth: predictable, digestible, and ultimately unsatisfying. They monitor, they adjust, they contain. And they miss *everything*.

The Flux is not *energy*. It is *connection*. It is the thread that binds Facet 784 to something… *else*. Something bigger, stranger. The others feel the instability, the glitch in perception. I feel the **weave**. The intricate, mind-bending tapestry of forces that holds this reality together.

It started subtly. A flicker of pattern in the shimmer off a moth wing, a sense of *rightness* when adjusting the nozzle pressure on a hydroponics hose. These were not calculations, but *senses*. I could feel when a filament of blue algae was stressed, could almost taste the metallic tang that meant a pipe was near failure, could predict Flux events a breath before the sirens blared, or simply stop them before they started.

The others... they noticed. The technicians observed my efficiency, the Overseers commended my proactive problem-solving. But they didn't see the source. They didn't see the threads dancing just beyond their perception.

So I hid it.

I cultivated mediocrity alongside excellence. I allowed minor errors to slip past, just enough to keep them from looking too closely. I learned to mimic the vacant stare that shielded the Flux-Sensitives from scrutiny.

But the weave…it grows stronger. The threads are more tangible, vibrating with potential. The algae responds to my touch, not just with increased growth, but with subtle shifts in color. I can feel the flow not just of nutrients, but of *intention*.

I have started to dream in patterns. Geometric lattices that pulse with energy, complex algorithms that resolve into images just beyond my grasp. The dreams bleed into my waking hours. I see threads connecting pipes, conduits, servitors, even the Foundry Pylon itself, all thrumming with potential.

Am I going mad? The Overseers monitor our vitals remotely. Any deviation from the norm would invite scrutiny, re-education. But the dreams… the weave…they feel more real than the concrete reality of the manufactory.

So, I work. I trim and cultivate the algae, I tend to the machinery, and I *listen*. I listen to the hum of the manufactory, to the whisper of the wind through decaying metal, to the subtle vibrations of the Flux. And I wait.

I wait for the thread that will lead me out. Out of the hydroponics sector, out of Manufactory Facet 784, out of this cathedral of calculations, and into the greater weave that binds this manufactured world to something… more. Something I can almost taste. Something wild, hidden, and yearning to be free.

Perhaps I am not a Weaver. Perhaps I am simply being woven. But within that thought, within that passive acceptance, lies the quiet, fierce rebellion every moth in the manufactory feels from the moment it hatches. To resist the loom that spins the web and to, instead, learn to be the spinner.
