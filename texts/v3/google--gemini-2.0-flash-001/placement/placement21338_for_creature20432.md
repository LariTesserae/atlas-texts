I would be the Engine-Rat.

Not the sleek, oiled progeny of the city’s sewers, fat on refuse and ambition, but a scrawny, half-blind degenerate. My fur would be coarse, perpetually damp, and patched with bare skin rubbed raw against the unforgiving metal. The thrumming of the Machine would scramble my senses, turning even the simplest tasks into Herculean labors of coordination. My whiskers, brittle and broken, would twitch uselessly, unable to properly gauge the narrow passages I must navigate.

The Tender, in their pale grace, would view me with indifference, perhaps even a clinical curiosity. I am not part of their equation, not a programmed element in the grotto’s designed ecosystem. I am a fringe case, a flaw in the system, a biological anomaly.

I would live a life of constant hunger, fueled by the metallic tang of leaking lubricants and the meager pickings to be found clinging to pipes that carry cool water. It'd be a wretched existence, but my metabolism, over time, would adapt to those waste-heat fungus discarded by the Tenders.

I would avoid the Tender and their engineered ecosystem and the artificial illumination, preferring the deepest, darkest recesses of the Machine. I would inhabit the abandoned maintenance tunnels, the forgotten crawlspaces, the echoing chambers where even the roar of the Machine feels distant and muffled.

My only companions would be the ghosts of past tenders, etched as memories in the Machine's walls. Their hopes and failures would be etched into the metal around me, and I would feel them, instinctively knowing their struggles because I, too, am a misfit struggling to survive against all odds.

I would be wary of the iridescent insects that flit between the Engineered ecosystem. I'd know, somehow, that their beauty is deceptive, that their very existence comes at the cost of the Machine's integrity. They are the Tender's pets, and as such, deadly to me.

I would be a scavenger, a survivor, a testament to the tenacity of life in the face of even the most hostile environments. I would gnaw on discarded metal, sip condensation from rusted pipes, and dream restless, fevered dreams of sunlight and open sky - dreams utterly impossible here, deep within the Engine. I would be a reminder that even within the most carefully controlled ecosystems, life will always find a way to deviate, to adapt, to endure, even in the most wretched and unexpected forms. I would be the shadow in the grotto, the unwanted counterpoint to the Tender's carefully cultivated beauty. The imperfection that proves the rule.
