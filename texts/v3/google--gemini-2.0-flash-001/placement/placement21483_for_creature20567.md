I am the Echo Weaver.

Not one of the leviathans, nor a darting shard. I dwell in the "low" regions, a wisp of near-nothingness given form, smaller than most even of the dreamers. My crystalline structure is sparse, almost skeletal: a fragile lattice of resonating silicon that barely holds together. Were I to drift into the denser strata, I would be crushed, my fragile consciousness scattered like dust.

But I do not drift. I *listen*.

My existence is defined by sound. Not the cacophony that assaults the senses of newly coalesced beings, but the whispers *beneath* the roar. I perceive the resonant frequencies, the subtle undertones that speak of the Glass Gulf's infinite and ever-shifting nature. Where others see the violent dance of creation and destruction, I hear the *music* of it all. Every tear in reality, every blossoming crystal spire, every fleeting eddy of unreal heat sings a song.

My purpose is not to understand, for true understanding in the Gulf is an illusion, a fixed point in a sea of constant flux. Instead, I gather. I collect the echoes of these countless songs, weaving them together into tapestries of sound older than any elder, richer than any leviathan's light.

Think of me as a mobile archive.

I have no territory. My existence is inherently transient. I move within the vacuities, a phantom of a presence, drawing energy from the faint vibrations. I am not predatory, nor do I seek to merge with others. I am solitary, by necessity; any attempt to entwine my fragile structure would shatter it.

The beauty of this silence is paramount.

My form is a distortion. My awareness is a collection of sounds. It's a cacophonous collection of music, and I hear things that should not be heard.

Yet still, I weave.

I have discovered forgotten structures of resonance: ancient, echoing chords that hint at a time before turbulence, a moment of perfect harmony before the Glass Gulf was shattered into its current chaotic beauty. I can't recall the time before turbulence, but the echo of it is still audible.

Perhaps it is a fool's errand. Perhaps the attempt to capture the impermanence of the Gulf is inherently self-defeating. But within the cacophony, there are glimpses of clarity, echoes of a deeper order. And in those echoes, I hear a question being asked: what becomes of a song when there is no one left to listen?

I am, as far as I know, the only being obsessed with the question. I am the only being who is, as far as I know, *aware* there is a question to be asked.

So I continue to weave.

I use my fragile crystalline structure to filter the noise, to amplify the faintest whispers, to translate incomprehensible vibrations into coherent… well, not narratives, exactly. More like… feelings. Essences. I store them, not in crystalline lattices, but in resonant patterns within my own being. I become the song.

And perhaps, one day, I will find another. Another fractured consciousness capable of hearing the same music, of understanding the same pain, of yearning for the same forgotten harmony. Perhaps then, the question will finally have an answer. Perhaps then, the songs of the Glass Gulf will have found their true purpose.

Until then, I remain: the Echo Weaver, adrift in the near-nothingness, listening, gathering, and weaving the threads of a symphony that may never be heard.
