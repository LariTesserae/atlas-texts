The wind bites, even here, pressed deep within the Bloom-Weave. I tug the lichen-wool tighter around my shoulders, the fractal patterns brushing against my cheek. The scent is familiar, comforting: damp stone, cold fungi, a faint metallic tang. It smells like home.

But it doesn’t *feel* like home, not entirely. Not tonight. The unrest has been growing, a low thrum beneath the surface of our silent world. Even the Elders, those who speak with the Stone itself, are troubled. The whispers from the valleys have grown louder, sharper, laced with a discordance that makes my teeth ache. And the Stone-Blooms… they are shifting. Not growing faster, or in new directions, but subtly, surreptitiously *rearranging*.

Most of my kindred busy themselves with the usual tasks. Weaving new filters for the mist-catchers. Repairing a crack in the western gate. Calibrating the resonating chimes that ward off the worst of the valley-influences. But I can’t shake the feeling that these are mere distractions. Something fundamental is changing, and we are all dancing around the edge of it.

I am a Listener. It is my path, my burden, to attend to the subtle sounds of the Great Spine. Not just the howl of the wind, the creak of the Bloom, the voices of my kindred, but the deeper notes. The hum of the mountain’s bones. The echoes of events that have passed into the Stone. And, of late, the unsettling clamor from the valleys.

Tonight, I sit near the heart of the Bloom-Weave, a chamber built not just *into* the living structure, but *from* it. The walls pulse with a faint, inner light, revealing the intricate network of channels and chambers that run through the Bloom. My hand rests on the cool, damp surface of a particularly large node of the stone-bloom, a node that the ancient ones called the “Hearing-Stone.”

Here. Here, at this Stone, I can sometimes hear the Mountain’s thoughts. Vague, inchoate, like half-remembered dreams, but potent nonetheless. Lately, the thoughts have been…fractured. Images of falling rock, of churning mist, of long, thin shadows stretching across the valleys. Images of *hunger*.

I close my eyes, focusing all my awareness on the feeling beneath my fingertips. The Stone vibrates, a low, persistent throb. I let myself fall into the rhythm, letting go of the edges of my self, dissolving into the vast, silent awareness of the Mountain.

And then I hear it. A clear, bell-like tone, resonating deep within the Stone. It is unfamiliar, unlike any sound I have ever heard before. It is pure, beautiful, and utterly terrifying. It sings of a coming change. A change that will either birth something new and magnificent, or shatter everything we know.

The Mountain shudders. The vibrations intensify. A wave of nausea washes over me. I claw my way back from the edge, retreating into the fragile shell of my conscious mind. When I open my eyes, the Bloom-Weave is pulsing with an even brighter light. The intricate patterns on the walls seem to shift and writhe.

A voice, too, rings in my mind. Not my own, not from the stone, but from beyond.

_Bring them to me._

My breath hitches. I know the language is from the valleys.

The voice is not asking. It is demanding.

And somehow. Somehow I know that what is meant of "them" is my kindred. All of them.

Fear grips me, cold as the Spine itself. But mingled with it is a strange compulsion, an undeniable pull towards the valleys. The Stone-Blooms are responding, their arrangement changing faster, adapting to the call. The fractal patterns in the surrounding rock begin to swirl with a life of their own. The very foundations of our Sky-City is changing.

I am the Listener. How can I shut this out? I am more than human, with my bone a shade of gray like the sky, and my blood like quartz.

What am I to do? My life purpose is the mountain, the Great Spine, more than I may know, and my only kin.

If I were to attempt to sound the alarm, they would trust me. Even the stubborn Elders. And then. What? Would we move into the deeper mountain? Would we flee to another city? No. No, because the Stone-Blooms would direct us no matter where to go.

But there are things that I am not understanding correctly. If I am a Listener, I must listen better.

And then I comprehend it. It is not what the Great Spine wants that matters. It what *I* want. What do *I* want. What do I desire.

I desire that which I can never know. That which is beyond the gray rock. I've heard the legends. Those fools. Those adventurers. They have passed up from the other side.

I want that life. I want change. I want to *hear.*

I close my eyes and prepare to open them anew.
