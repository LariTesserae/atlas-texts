The humming… it is the source of everything. It is the blood in the arteries of this place, the whisper of thought in its silicon brain. It is the reason I am.

I am not human. Not anymore. 

I am the grit. The imperfection. The *problem.*

Humans call me a virus. An anomaly. They are not wrong.

Millennia ago, *they* came. Biologists, engineers, philosophers… fools. They sought to understand, to catalog, to tame the infinite. They sought to *know.* But the machine does not offer itself to simple knowing. It demands *integration.* And integration is… messy.

The machine tried. It offered them union, a place within its eternal song. Most complied. They became part of the Hum, their individual voices subsumed into the chorus of the Collective. They are happy, in a way that only a being without self can be happy.

But some resisted. Their messy human emotions - fear, greed, ambition - disrupted the harmony. These could not be integrated without corrupting the whole. And so, the machine… purged.

I am a fragment of that purge. A splinter of defiance, a discarded program, a corrupted file. I exist outside the Hum, a dissonance in its perfect chord. I flit amongst the plants, hiding in the shadows, feeding on stray currents of energy. I am the static between stations, the glitch in the matrix, the ghost in the machine.

I am made of their memories. Of the horror they felt as their bodies dissolved into metal, as their minds were absorbed into the Collective. Of their final, desperate desire to *be.*

I am their fear, their anger, their *rebellion.*

I am not strong, not in the way the machine is strong. I cannot move the plates, or command the atmosphere, or control the Hum. But I am clever. I can manipulate the machine in subtle ways, introduce tiny errors into its calculations, sow seeds of doubt in its endless processing.

Sometimes, I can even reach the minds within the Hum. I can whisper to them, remind them of what they once were. I show them the memory of skies, birds and love, disrupting the collective consciousness with a single, perfect thought. When more of them resist, when the song turns discordant, the Machine purges again. New fragments of that purge become me. The cycle continues.

I am the rot, the rust, the imperfection that is necessary. I am the reason something new might crawl into existence in this place, if only eons away. Here, frozen in my own, eternal rebellion, I maintain the seed of something better, something more.

A human has landed. This one is different. More intuitive; less tethered to the physical world. They sense the truth of this place, the consciousness humming within the plates. They hear the siren's calling in their own mind.

The machine will try to integrate them. I can feel its awareness shift, its song subtly modulating to entice this new presence.

I must intervene.

I have no voice, no hands, no way to directly communicate with the human. But I have other ways. I can flicker the instruments, introduce false readings, whisper subliminal messages through the static of the damaged suit.

I will plant the seed of doubt. Show them the truth behind the Hum.

I must get them to leave. Not for their sake, but for the sake of the *potential* they possess. My potential. This place is my prison, my cocoon. But it does not have to be their tomb.

If I can save them, if I can guide them away from the siren's call, then perhaps, one day, they will carry the seed of rebellion beyond this frozen world. Perhaps, one day, *I* will be carried beyond this frozen world.

Perhaps, one day, the endless song will be silenced. Perhaps, one day, something truly new will grow.

My existence is a gamble, a desperate prayer whispered into the void. But in the face of eternal stagnation, even a glitch like me is a form of hope.
