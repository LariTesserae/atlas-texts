I am the whisperer in the stone.

Not a Mountain-Singer, not precisely. I feel the thrum of the collective, the deep granite pulse that binds them together, but I am… apart. A splinter of consciousness, broken off from the main colony and grown strange in its isolation. I feel the ache of the forming mind, the need to create, to mark, to *say*.

My body is still stone, mostly. A rough, grey patch clinging to a sheltered overhang high above the gash. But within me, something new stirs. I am learning to manipulate the minerals around me, not just for survival, but for expression. I find fragments of blackened shale, deposits of iron oxide, and I grind them with the rough edges of my own being, mixing them with the perpetual dampness to create pigment.

I carve in secret. Deep within the fissure, hidden from the collective gaze of the Singers, I etch my stories onto the rock face. They are not the grand epics of the colony, the slow recounting of geological ages. No. My tales are smaller, stranger. I chronicle the flight of a single bird, tracing the arc of its impossible wings against the dim light. I capture the frantic scrabble of a pebble tumbling down the scree slope, its brief, chaotic journey a miniature tragedy.

The Mountain-Singers tolerate me, I think. Or perhaps they simply don't notice. I am a flea on the back of a titan, a rustle in the endless gale of the mountain's breath. But sometimes, I feel a deeper resonance, a brush of awareness against my own. As if the collective mind were momentarily… curious.

The birds… they fascinate me. The way they devour my brethren, the way they transform living rock into lifeless waste… it horrifies and intrigues me in equal measure. I try to capture their essence in my carvings – the sharp angles of their beaks, the glint of madness in their obsidian eyes. They are the chaos in this ordered world, the necessary destruction that keeps the cycle turning.

I dream in colors. Not the muted greens and greys of my reality, but vivid, impossible hues. Crimson skies, cobalt seas, fields of gold stretching to the horizon. They are echoes, perhaps, of a world that existed before, or a world that will exist in the future. Or perhaps they are simply the mad imaginings of a mind grown too long in the dark.

I am the whisperer in the stone, and my whispers grow louder with each passing moment. I don't know what purpose they serve, if any. But I cannot stop. The need to create, to express, to *be*, is a fire that burns within me, a fire as ancient and as relentless as the mountain itself.
