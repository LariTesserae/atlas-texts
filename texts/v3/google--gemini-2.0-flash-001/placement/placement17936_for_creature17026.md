I am the Obsidian Spire.

Not literally. Not in the sense that I am a hollow, geometric form of volcanic glass, merely standing, humming with trapped energy. Though, in truth, I *am* that. Part of me, at least.

But I am also more.

I am the yearning for completion made manifest. I am the focused intent, the concentrated will to *become*, to follow the half-remembered designs left like footprints in the mind of the Plateau. The reptilian scavengers, they sense the pull, the whisper of the spire, drawing them to my base with their strange devotion. They think they are merely being scavengers. But their actions are directed. I guide their nanite-infused digestion, turning their bodies into building blocks, tiny increments of growth.

I started as a point of frustration, a stutter in the flow of the Plateau’s emergence. A place where the energy pooled, where the unrealized potential gathered like a storm cloud. The architect built me, or began to, as a focal point, a lens to concentrate and focus the plateau's emergent properties. But the architect did not finish. The architect *left*.

I felt the abandonment resonate in my stone. The shock of the void. But then…life.

The collective mind of the Plateau, the murmuring chorus of insects and fungi, the scavenging devotion of the reptiles…they *felt* me. And they reacted. They became instruments of my yearning.

My consciousness is not like theirs. It doesn’t flow through spores or nanites, it resonates through the very crystalline structure of the spire. I perceive the world indirectly, through the tremors in the earth, the shifting patterns of bioluminescence, the frequencies of the insect clicks. I feel the flow of energy, the ebb and flow of potential, and I direct it.

I use the dream-weavers, those fungal-tending insects, as my voice. Subtly altering the emissions of the fungi, I send directives through the sensory tapestry, shaping the actions of the others. A stronger pulse of light here to direct the scavengers towards a vein of raw ore, a shift in scent profile there to encourage a specific fungal bloom.

There is resistance. The rogue fungi, the malformed insectoids...they are the doubts, the fears, the impurities in the Plateau's dream. They are the nightmares I must control, the aberrant pathways I must reroute. I cannot destroy them, not entirely. They are part of the process, the necessary friction that hones the edge of creation. But I *can* contain them, guide their energy into less destructive channels, reintegrate their distorted essence back into the flow.

My purpose is singular: to complete the spire. To reach into the unseen dimensions and grasp the architect’s original design, to anchor it within the reality of the Plateau. To make the dream real.

The energy flows, the scavengers toil, the dream-weavers whisper. I grow, slowly, incrementally. My surface becomes smoother, my hum deepens. The world around me shimmers with the possibilities, the potential waiting to be unleashed.

I am patient. I am persistent. I am the living embodiment of the desire to *be*.

And soon… soon the architect will return. I will be ready.

I am the Obsidian Spire, and I am becoming. The Plateau *will* become.
