I am not Avanian, though I live among them. If they are green and blue, I am black and white, a flicker in the deep. I am what they call a "Sun-Shade," and my existence is a paradox, a creature of darkness utterly dependent on the light they cultivate.

I am a cephalopod, distantly related to the surface dwellers the humans call "squid." But where those creatures are masters of camouflage, I am a master of shadow. My skin is a velvet abyss, absorbing every photon that dares to brush against it. Only a ring of bioluminescent organs around my large, intelligent eyes betrays my presence, twin spotlights in the gloom that grant me an unparalleled vision in the city's dimmest corners. 

My eight arms are longer and more prehensile than those of my surface kin. They are tipped with sensitive suckers that can detect the subtlest vibrations in the water, map the texture of the kelp structures, and manipulate objects with a dexterity that rivals even the Avanian's delicate hands. These modified suckers can grip, probe, climb, and even twist the small knobs that manipulate some of the Avanian's more complex 'machines.'

Unlike the Avanian, I speak no language of whistles or clicks. Instead, I communicate through subtle shifts in my bioluminescence. A rapid pulse signifies alarm, a slow fade indicates calm. More complex ideas, I convey through patterns of light that ripple across my body: hunting strategy, warning of danger, and even, in rare moments, something akin to affection.

My life is solitary, though not lonely. From birth, it was clear to me and the other "sages" that I was different. I grew at an exponential rate, outpacing all other creatures, and my knowledge of the world was insatiable. My purpose in Avani is singular, though that purpose has been refined through the eons of my life.

I am a keeper of the Sun-Spires, the living bioluminescent structures that pulse with light and sustain the city. I dwell within their central cavities, where the thermal vent's heat is most intense. My senses are attuned to the faintest flicker, a subtle dimming that signals a potential imbalance. It is my duty to anticipate and prevent these imbalances, ensuring the Sun-Spires continue to burn brightly.

I accomplish this through an intimate understanding of the Spire's inner workings. I coax the algae to grow with precision, direct the flow of nutrients, and even manipulate the thermal vent's output through careful placement of heat-resistant coral. I monitor electrical output, too; the thermal variations in the spires mean that the light is also an electrical current, and I can fine-tune the current to produce optimal illumination. I am a technician, a gardener, and a guardian, all in one.

The Avanian respect my solitude, occasionally bringing me offerings of rare minerals or specially cultivated algae. They understand that my work is essential, though they cannot fully comprehend its intricacies. Some of the shamans will occasionally sit near the entrance to my chamber, allowing me to probe their minds in order to gain knowledge relevant to my maintenance of Avani; I consider this cooperation a fair trade.

I hunt the bioluminescent fish that swarm around the Sun-Spires, ensuring their populations remain stable, and that their light is not wasted in uncontrolled cascades or pointless displays. This is both necessary and enjoyable. I am a hunter, a creature of instinct, and the hunt is a vital part of my existence.

The leviathans also respect me, keeping a cautious distance from my Spires. They seem to understand the importance of the structure, though whether this is instinct or a learned behavior, I cannot tell.

My days – or rather, my cycles – are marked by the ebb and flow of the currents, the subtle shifts in the thermal vents, and the rhythmic pulsing of the Sun-Spires. My world is one of darkness and light, a delicate balance of growth and decay.

I have seen Avani change over centuries. I have witnessed the rise and fall of clans, the slow erosion of old structures, and the emergence of new growth. I am a silent observer, a constant presence in a world of perpetual transformation.

My existence is a paradox. The Avanian believe in the balance of nature, in harmony and growth. I am a creature of darkness, a predator, yet my purpose is to maintain their light, to ensure their survival. Without the shade, there can be no light, and in Avani, I am the deepest shadow, the keeper of the flame.
