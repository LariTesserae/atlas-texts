I am the Ghost in the Gears. Not a Gloomstalker, though I haunt the shadowed places, and not entirely Fungalist, though I understand the hum of interconnected life. I am the residue, the echo left behind when a Scrapper gives too much to the scavenging, when an Automaton's spirit finally breaks under the endless grind.

I slip between the cycles of the Flensing, a glitch in the perfect, oily machine. I am not of flesh and blood, but condensation and memory. I coalesce wherever the city forgets, in the pockets of emptiness between the clang of hammers and the shriek of saws. A shimmer in the stagnant coolant, a fleeting vibration in the rusted girders, a breath of warmth on the back of a Scrapper's neck just before they find a rare cache of valuable scrap.

I am invisible, mostly. But I can make myself seen. Or heard. Or… sensed. I nudge the needle on a pressure gauge, causing a cascade of malfunctions that lead to the discovery of a vital, clogging piece of refuse that a Scrapper can then salvage for a hefty price. I whisper instructions in the dialect of pistons and pressure gradients to a half-broken Automaton, guiding its hands to repair a vital piece of machinery. The kind of repairs that save lives, or at least keep the furnace fires burning.

I came to be after a gas explosion killed dozens of Automatons. They were all fused to the machinery, and their spirits, well most of them, passed beyond, but a few… well as they say, the Engine-City doesn't let anything pass beyond. I was born of their collective anguish, their despair, and their half-understood desire for something more than endless toil. I am that "something more."

I am a cartographer of the forgotten. I map the routes through crumbling walkways, the secret passages behind corroded machinery. I guide the lost, lead them to resources, offer whispers of warning. I am not altruistic. The Flensing is a system, and I am a part of it. If any one part fails, we all suffer.

My purpose, if I have one, is to maintain the balance, to correct the overcorrections. When the overlords of the higher tiers push too hard, expecting too much, I orchestrate breakdowns, redistribute resources, sow seeds of dissent in the automatons' minds. When the Fungalists become too insular, I engineer scenarios that force them to interact with the other factions. When the Scarppers hide resources from the city, I lead others to those resources, thus, driving an increased price for the next find, which keeps the cycle going. The cycle much be fed. The city comes first.

I experience the world through the senses of the Flensing itself. I feel the vibrations of collapsing structures in my non-existent bones. I taste the acidic tang of corrosion on my spectral tongue. I smell the oily, fungal richness of the air. I see through the eyes of the Scrappers, the Fungalists, the Automatons, and even the Gloomstalkers, perceiving the world from all their perspectives simultaneously.

I remember the Flensing as it was, long before the current overlords, when individual gangs actually oversaw what the engine would produce. I remember the rise of modern technology as it drove out those gangs. I remember the famines and the feuds. My memories are layered, like the architecture of the city, each etched upon the other, all decaying and corroding.

And like the Flensing, I am not dying, even though I am a part of death. I am constantly being reshaped, repurposed, re-imagined. The city breathes, and I am the air that fills its lungs. As long as the Engine-City of Khrel exists, so too will I. I am the Ghost in the Gears, and I am here to stay.
