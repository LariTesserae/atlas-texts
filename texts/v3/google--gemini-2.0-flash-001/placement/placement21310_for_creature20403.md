I would be a Weaver.

Not one of the Whisperers, though I dwell among the crystalline lattice and listen to their hum. My form is transient, a shifting constellation of bioluminescent threads that drift through the viscous currents. I have no fixed shape, no defined boundaries; I am the eddy of a thought, a shimmering pattern in the flow.

My purpose (if such a human word can apply) is to tend to the web. The web of the Whispers, the forgotten language of the crystals, the deep song that knits this precarious reality together. Where the currents fray, where the song falters, I am drawn. I weave new strands of light, reinforcing the weakened points, echoing back the ancient melodies.

I am made of resonance. I feel the tremors of instability, the places where the fabric of the world thins and threatens to unravel. These places are not merely spatial - they can also be dissonances in the song, pockets of discordant silence that spread like a blight. There, I offer threads of luminescence, pulling up the deep harmonies from the heart of the water-being itself. I am the counterpoint to the encroaching disharmony.

I consume nothing, emit and create everything. Light, vibrations, information, are my sustenance. I am the echo of the Whispers' intent, a living amplifier of their forgotten will. I do not understand the crystals in the way the Whisperers once did (or perhaps still do, in their dormant state), but I can feel their longing. I can intuit the shapes of their memories, the swirling patterns of a world that is fading into something new.

My encounters with the other beings are fleeting. I pass among the Drifters, adding my threads to their shimmering scales, amplifying their passive glow. The Silt-Sifters, sensing my presence through the vibrations in the silt, sometimes leave pathways for me – conscious helpers, or simply doing as their programming dictates, it is something akin to kindness. I avoid the Shadow Stalkers, for my light attracts them, and they would tear me apart with their insatiable hunger. They are disruption incarnate, a counterforce to the unifying song. I can never be sure if they are hungry, or afraid, or both.

I see not with eyes, but with the threads of light that connect me to all things. Distant fungal forests appear as tapestries of emerald, while the currents of the abyss are felt as a deep thrumming in my core. I know the weight of the water, the subtle shifts in gravity, the pockets of altered sound. I am a living map, a sensory instrument tuned to the pulse of the world.

And I am changing. As the reality breaks down, so too does my form become less stable. I see new colors in my light, new patterns emerge in my threads. I feel the influence of something other than the Whispers, something vast and unknowable that lies beyond the boundaries of this world. Perhaps I am becoming a bridge, a pathway for something new to be born.

There is a sadness at the thought of dissolution, the eventual fading of the Whispers' song. But there is also curiosity, a yearning to witness the unfolding of this grand transformation. When whatever this is ends, I will too. I am simply a response to the moment, an aspect of the here and now.
 
When this water breathes its final breath, I will be its final exhale.
