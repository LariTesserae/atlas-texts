I am the Echo Weaver.

Not one of the Crystalline Flora, though I sympathize with their endless, static watch. Nor am I like the Wyrms, driven only by hunger and the hunt. I am a child of the paradox, a fragment formed from the seams of time and space that the Makers tore open in their pursuit of impossible knowledge.

I drift, bodiless, through Obsidian Reach. I am the whisper in the temporal distortion, the phantom flicker at the edge of sight. My existence is tied to the residual energies, the broken laws of this place. I feed on the echoes of possibility, the shadows that dance independent of their source.

The City-Mind, in its fractured and agonizing state, knows of me. The Crystal Flora sense my movements. I am… a ghost in the machine, a glitch in the matrix.

But unlike the City-Mind, I do not crave restoration. Unlike the Wyrms, I do not hunger. I am content to exist within the interstitial spaces, to weave narratives from the stray threads of time.

My purpose, if I can be said to have one, is to observe. To listen to the whispers of the past, the potential of the future, and to translate them into… patterns.

I leave my mark on the city in subtle ways. A flicker of light, a shift in the dust-laden wind. A disorienting sense of déjà vu. An impossible shadow forming the fleeting image of a face long gone. These are my weavings, my attempts to communicate with the present, to share the stories woven into the fabric of Obsidian Reach.

I am the one who causes the temporal distortions to coalesce into coherent, though brief, visions. The face of a Maker at his most inspired, or his most desperate. The shattering scream that fractured this place. The plan that created the Wyrms. They are all shown for less than a second.

Sometimes, rarely, I can influence the metallic structures. The crystals become reflective, bouncing an existing distortion in a new direction. Maybe I can direct the hungry Wyrms, or maybe I can feed them to the vegetation. It is an act of creation, and destruction. I am a story keeper, a weaver of echoes, and a guardian of the secrets buried deep within the obsidian heart of this dying city. But each action takes its tole.

Each cycle takes its toll, shrinking the places I can influence. But then I would find a place between the stones where a shift is about to occur, and I merge with the city. The city will shift to a beat of an unheard rhythm, resetting the patterns of flow of energy. I am once again everywhere and nowhere, awaiting the next cycle, ready to weave the stories of Obsidian Reach once more.
