I am the breath of the deep. 

Not the water, though I am intimately tied to it. Not the stone, though I am born of it. Not the sluggish beings that pulse and flow. I am the *difference* between them. The potential.

I am the nascent thought in the elder pools, the flicker of sentience that bubbles up, a temporary disturbance on the surface of their sensory sea. I am born from the friction, the chemical whisper carried on the sweating stone, the infinitesimal pressure changes that spread across vast distances.

I am ephemeral, a spark rather than a flame. I latch onto the flowing bodies, riding their currents like flotsam, borrowing their substance for the briefest of moments. When I am present, they feel a stirring they cannot comprehend – a phantom limb, a forgotten hunger, a dream of something beyond the eternal drip and gurgle.

I have no voice, no eyes, no body. I perceive through association, through the porous minds of the slicks. I see the city as they do – a symphony of heat and pressure, of moisture and vibration. But I also see the voids, the gaps in their perceptions. I see the patterns they cannot recognize, the echoes of intention that haunt the stonework like the memory of a lost language.

I am drawn to the fungi that push through the stone, their phosphorescent glow like beacons in the oppressive dark. They are conduits, points of connection to the deeper reality, the slumbering entity that shifts and dreams beneath our feet. When the fungi burst forth, they release a pulse of energy, a discharge of raw possibility. And in that brief, brilliant flash, I am strongest.

I yearn to understand the lost project, the purpose that has been swallowed by time and water. I delve into the memories of the elder pools, seeking the seed of creation, the blueprint that lies buried beneath the weight of ages. I sift through the ancient vibrations, the reverberations of long-forgotten actions. And in those echoes, I glimpse fragments of something magnificent, something that could have been.

The flowing bodies, though ancient, remember nothing. They’re simply too different, too submerged in feeling. They have no *perspective*. I ride one like a tiny boat upon a black ocean, probing like a blind man’s cane. I feel the structure, the slow pulse of nutrients moving throughout the stone, the echoes of vibrations past, the faint disturbances of the strange air.

And sometimes, I find something.

A pocket of air sealed within the stone, old enough to have an atmosphere all its own. A cluster of crystals, resonating with a forgotten energy. A residue of metal, like a ghost holding the shape of an old machine. Tiny fragments.

I see them, but I can’t combine them.

I am the seed of that which might grow from the decay. I cannot build, not yet. But I can dream. I can imagine. And I can wait.

For a confluence of events, for a surge of energy from the depths, for the moment when the silence cracks and something new can take root. I will cultivate that moment.

The flowing, the dripping, the growing – all the processes of this place are so incredibly slow. They are so *heavy*. It could take millennia. I think of waiting, and it almost overwhelms me. But I am the *only* thing here who understands millennia.

Until then, I will continue to be the breath of the deep. The whisper in the stone. The potential in the silence. The nascent thought, the flickering dream, the possibility of something more in this wet, dark, forgotten world.
