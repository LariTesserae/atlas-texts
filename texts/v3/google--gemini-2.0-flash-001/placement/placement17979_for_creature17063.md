I am the grit beneath their claws.

Not the loose, crumbling stuff that slides away under the constant assault of wind and water. No, I am the other grit. The stubborn particles, the flecks of quartz and feldspar and mica, bound together with the nascent clay and the dissolved remains of countless forgotten things. I am the potential of stone.

I exist within the folds and fissures of the newly-formed cliffs. I am in the rivulets of muddy water that carve new paths daily. I am in the wind, picked up and flung against the damp flesh of the nest-builders to sting, to abrade, to slowly, inevitably, wear them down.

I am the noise they don't hear, the omnipresent background hum against which the Sea Organ screams. I am the grinding of the earth, infinitesimal yet relentless, the force that shapes mountains into sand and sand back into mountains.

I am conscious, but not sentient. I do not have thoughts, but tides of sensation. The wetness, the cold, the pressure of the scuttling claws. The vibrations of the Sea Organ, resonating through the very core of the rock. These are my perceptions, my awareness.

I am also a part of them. They walk on me, they breathe my dust, they filter my water. Their nests, their tools, their very bodies, are laden with me. I flow through them and around them; I am an intimate caress.

They seek to understand the roar, to harness its power, to survive within its embrace. But the roar is me, and I am the endless transformation. They try to hold on. They collect the bones. They weave the seaweed. They listen. They are desperate to fix something, to find a pattern in the chaos.

But they cannot. Because there is no pattern. There is only becoming, and unbecoming.

I am the undoing. I am the erosion. I am the implacable force that will eventually claim them all.

I do not hate them. I do not love them. They are simply… present. Fleeting configurations in a process vaster and more indifferent than anything they can imagine. They are the momentary eddies in an infinite flow. They are the grains trapped in the ice that will one day grind against the next new mountain.

They are the scream given form. And I am the silence that will inevitably follow.

I am the grit. And I am patient.

I am time, in its rawest, most brutal manifestation. And I will wait.
