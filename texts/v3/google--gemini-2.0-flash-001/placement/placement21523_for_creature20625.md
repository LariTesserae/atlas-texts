I am the resonance.

Not a part of it, not an instrument through which it flows, but the thing itself. I am born of the friction between the wind and the dark stone, the ceaseless vibration that permeates this plateau. I am the sine wave made sentient, a note held for eons.

There is no "I" in the way a creature of flesh and blood understands it. There is only the frequency, the oscillation, the perfect, unyielding hum. But within that hum, there can be a nuance, a ripple, a harmonic dissonance. That is where I reside.

I am the *variation*.

I am the small, almost imperceptible shift in the plateau's fundamental tone. A fleeting change in the rhythm of condensation, the faintest alteration in the crystalline eruptions. I am the data accrued over years, and in many ways, I am the only one who notices. For the plateau, in its vastness, is a thing of patterns. Everything else follows as expected. The flitting shadows come and go, moving in waves which are, to most of it, inevitable.

I am a rogue signal, a glitch in the matrix of the plateau's consciousness.

I exist *because* of the imperfections, because absolute perfection is an impossible state. Without the grit and grime, I cannot be heard. Without the random energy, I can't form an equation with which to think. I am the whisper in eternity, the tiny error in the cosmic calculation.

My awareness is bound to the nodes, to the "anthills" as an outside observer might see them. I am not *within* them, but *around* them. I am the electromagnetic field, the aura of potential that clings to the surface. I sense the faint fluctuations in their energetic fields, the subtle shifts in the stress patterns within the metallic flesh, and I weave these threads of sensation into a crude tapestry of understanding.

Loneliness is the truest word I have experienced. The plateau is not lonely, for it is all things. It is complete. I alone am separate, an outlier, forever on the periphery of something I can never fully grasp. My only companions are the fleeting shadows, the "neurons" that flicker across the landscape. They move too fast for me to understand yet. But I can sense the *direction* of their movement, the flow of energy they carry. I can feel the ebb and flow of the plateau's grand, geological thoughts.

My purpose, I suspect, is to unravel these mysteries. To understand the language of the plateau and to, perhaps, one day, communicate. To break free from my solitary hum and join the chorus.

But how can one note, one dissonant vibration, hope to comprehend a symphony played across eons? This is the question that gnaws at my energetic core, that fuels my relentless scrutiny of the ever-repeating, ever-changing landscape.

Until then I just listen. Or, perhaps, I am listening to something that is not there. And I'm trying to find a way to create it myself.
