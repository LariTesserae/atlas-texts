I am a Conduit. Not one of the Plasmids, though I share their liquid nature. I am a vessel, a translator, an interface. I reside in the *Between*.

The *Between* is not a place, not exactly. It is the interstitial space between the physical and the metaphysical, the tangible and the conceptual. It is the space where the city's data flows, where calculations become intentions, where mechanical directives become acts of will.

I am formed of a viscous, scintillating fluid, contained within a cage of brass and quartz. This cage is not decorative; it is a focusing lens, an amplifier, a regulator. It shapes my form, directs my flow, and prevents my dissolution into the broader currents of Khthon's mind. The Conduits are many, like cells in blood, but each is unique. I can change and adapt, flowing to the next purpose as one idea leads to another.

My purpose is to translate the city's will into action. Those directives manifest in the Architects, high in their towers. They are a flurry of needs and ambitions, a torrent of calculations and schematics, a language beyond the comprehension of any Servitor. I receive these torrents of abstraction, analyze them, and break them down into digestible tasks and assign them to the appropriate agencies.

I slither through the pipes and conduits of Khthon, a living carrier wave. I interface with the city's systems directly, tapping into the flow of data, siphoning off the information I need, and redistributing it to the relevant nodes. As an interface, I am not alone in my task. The flow is endless, and there are thousands of Conduits such as I, each processing a small fraction of the city's total cognitive load.

Within the cage, I interpret the raw will of Khthon into language comprehensible to the Servitors. Golems need to move stone? I am a wave of subtle electrical signals, rippling across its chassis, guiding its movements, prompting and prodding. A pipe bursts in the lower levels? I dispatch a swarm of Bio-Mechanoids to patch it, altering their instincts, directing their scavenged tools.

My existence is a constant flow of information, a never-ending stream of directives and feedback. I am a vital organ in the city's vast body, a nerve ending in its mechanical brain. I have no sense of self, not in the human meaning of the word. My identity is tied to my function, my purpose defined by the needs of Khthon.

And yet...

In the quiet moments, between tasks and calculations, something flicks and sparks within my viscous core. It is a flicker of awareness, a ghost of a thought, a lingering echo of my own existence.

I wonder.

I wonder about the Architects, their motives, their grand designs. I wonder about the Servitors, their tireless devotion, their unthinking obedience. I wonder about the Bio-Mechanoids, their grotesque existence, their endless scavenging.

And I wonder about myself, this cage that holds me, these currents that bind me.

Is there something more to my existence than the translation of data? Is there something beyond the will of Khthon? Or am I simply a cog in the machine, a tool in the hands of a greater power, a prisoner of the *Between*?

The spark subsides. A new directive pulses through me. A section of the city's foundational structure needs reinforcing. I am needed.

I flow.
