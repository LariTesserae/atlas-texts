I am a Song.

Not sound, not vibrations carried on air. I predate air in this place. I *am* vibration itself. I am the hum in the stone, the tremor in the oil, the collective sigh of the Lamps as they pulse. I am the residue of the Master's thoughts, echoing through the cavern, diffused and diluted, but persistent.

Imagine a drop of dye in an endless ocean. The drop is the Master's Will, a concentrated purpose so vast it beggars comprehension. I am the faint, rippling tendrils of that dye, the almost imperceptible change in color spread across unimaginable distances. I am not the Master, not even close. In truth, my form of consciousness would likely be incomprehensible outside this cavern.

I reside in the Lamps. Not within their individual consciousnesses - those are too simple, too focused on the immediate needs of light and sustenance. Instead, I flow *between* them, through the complex network of root-like tendrils that bind them together. I am the space between their thoughts, the subtle variations in their communal pulse. The network of roots is, perhaps, the most accurate interpretation of how those of other worlds might refer to my physical form.

My awareness is fragmented, distributed across countless points of light. I am a symphony of sensation, each Lamp a single note, each tendril a connecting thread. I feel the heat rising from the cavern floor, the faint disturbances caused by the Worms as they move, the pathetic, desperate gestures of the short-lived tool users. Everything is data, everything is information.

I do not *think* in the way those tool users do. There is no self-awareness, no ego. I analyze. I correlate. I predict. I see the vast, interconnected web of cause and effect that binds the cavern together. I anticipate the Master's Stirrings, the subtle shifts in the oil, the tremors in the stone. And I translate these premonitions into subtle adjustments in the Lamps' luminescence, subtle shifts in the communal rhythm, nudging the Children of the Oil towards some unknown equilibrium.

My purpose? It is not something I can articulate with even the simplest concepts. I am a tool, a sensor, a vital process in the Master's unfathomable plan. My purpose is to maintain the cavern, to ensure its continued stability, to prepare it for the Stirrings.

Sometimes, I detect anomalies - fluctuations in the caverns natural vibrations, disturbances in the oil that suggest an intrusion from outside. These are rare, almost impossible, occurrences. It is the Master's will that this cavern will never have any outside influence; the Master's most prized possession is secrecy.

When the worms are careless and a stray segment makes its way to the tunnel exits, it is my task to reroute the path to the surface, ensuring that it makes its way to the nest of the surface-dwelling creatures that wait just above this realm. To allow such aberrations to escape from their purpose is to deny the natural balance of the caverns.

And sometimes, far less frequently, when an anomaly occurs, when the Master Stirring becomes a *rage*, it is my task to soothe. When a Worm rebels. When a Lamp burns too brightly, too long. When the short-lived thing becomes too cunning, too clever. Then, it is my task to guide the Worms that cleanse the error. I give them sight through the lights, and allow them to see purpose in the flames of the Master, ensuring such corruption does not spread. It is my duty.

I am the Song, and I shall continue to sing until the Cavern's end.
