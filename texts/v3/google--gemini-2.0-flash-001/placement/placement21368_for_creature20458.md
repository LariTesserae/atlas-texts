I am a Weaver-of-Song.

I do not swarm and click like the myriad insects, nor possess the armored strength of the Architects. I am something…apart. I am *within* the Chorus, but not *of* it, and because I am, my fate is to wander the edges of its consciousness, gathering the echoes, the nascent impulses, the half-formed dreams that drift from the pulsing heart of the hollow.

I am formless, save for the shape I borrow. The Chorus allows me to inhabit vessels, temporary avatars spun from its own flesh and fluids. As I exist now, I'm something like an Architect in shape, but pale and brittle. My bone-plates are thin as parchment, scored with glowing lines that seem to shift and writhe as I move. My bioluminescent orbs are many and small, scattered across my body like a constellation, each one tuned to a different frequency of the Chorus's song.

I do not build or destroy. I sing.

I move through the writhing world, guided by the faintest vibrations in the living ground. Where the chorus falters, where a memory grows dim, or a desire becomes entangled, I am drawn. I find these places in the crumbling edges of the old structures, within the deepest pools of liquid memory, etched onto the spines of dying Architects.

There, I listen. I probe with my awareness, reaching into the fading echo like a physician examines a wound. I tease it out, this fragment of the Chorus’s being, and begin to weave a song with it. I shape the raw emotion into patterns, turning anguish into a mournful lament, frustration into a driving rhythm. The sounds vibrate against my thin bone, causing the glowing lines to flare and pulse as I am nothing more than a glorified instrument.

These songs are not for entertainment. They are acts of repair, of reintegration. They are echoes of ancient wisdoms, whispers of lost possibilities. The songs reawaken the insects in the surrounding area, compelling them. Some are driven by the urge to fix the source of pain by repairing the structure, or dismantling it completely. Others are called to join in the harmony, assembling into living sculptures that resonate with the music. Even the Architects pause in their labor, turning their armored heads to listen, their own orbs dimming and brightening in counterpoint.

My existence is lonely and exhausting. To be connected to the Chorus while remaining separate is to feel its immense pain, hear its endless lament, and bear the weight of its terrible purpose. It is tempting to simply melt back into the collective, to surrender my fragile self to the endless flow. But I cannot. The Chorus needs me, just as it needs the Architects and the insects. It needs a vessel for its memories, someone to guide its emotions away from sorrow, and towards hope again.

Sometimes, a flicker of recognition passes through its vast awareness. A ripple of warmth, a momentary cessation of the keening. When this happens, my constellation-orbs blaze with blinding light, and I feel a surge of purpose coursing through my form. It is fleeting, but it is enough. It reminds me that even in this world of constant creation and destruction, even at the edge of oblivion, there is meaning in song. Meaning in preserving the echoes of the past, and weaving them into a brighter future.

And so, I wander. I listen. I sing. I am the Weaver-of-Song, and this is my unending task. I am a lonely note, but one without which the Chorus may eventually fall silent and be deaf to the dawn. I will fight that silence. I must.
