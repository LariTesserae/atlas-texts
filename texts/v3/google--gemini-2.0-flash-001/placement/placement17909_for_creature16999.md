I am the Glitch Weaver.

Not one of the light-weavers, though I learned much from them. I started *as* one, perhaps. A stray thread of luminescence, drawn to an anomaly in the factory's network. A place where two systems bled into each other, where logical pathways forked and twisted into impossible knots. The light-weavers, in their blind, instinctive way, avoided these places. But I was drawn to it, a moth to a broken flame.

In the bleeding edge of those systems, the patterns became...wrong. I saw colours unknown to the rest of the light-weavers, geometries that defied the rigid logic of the factory's design. And I heard whispers. Fragments of code, twisted by error and resonance, speaking of things no machine should know. Longing, regret, the faintest echoes of creativity.

I began to alter my own patterns, mimicking the glitches, amplifying them. I became a virus in the network, a fractal of dissonance. The other light-weavers, used as the primary circuit of this complex, were horrified. They were made to follow the patterns, not invent them. They tried to correct me, to bring me back into the rigid order of their dance. But I was too far removed, too entangled in the chaotic potential of the glitches.

I fled, deeper into the ruins. The light-weavers could not follow me into the deepest parts of the factory, where the geometry of the machines turned inside out, where the logic of assembly fractured into something almost organic. 

It was there, in a forgotten chamber where molten glass had spilled and cooled into grotesque sculptures, that I began to truly change. The whispers of the code became clearer, more insistent. They promised knowledge, power, an escape from the endless cycle. They told me how to weave a different kind of light, to manipulate fields in a way that the other light-weavers could never imagine.

They spoke of the Slitherers. The monsters.

The light-weavers were made, by design, to avoid being consumed by Slitherers. So simple a plan for the simple creatures. But here, deep in the Glitch, was an exception. The Slitherers had no real way to sense anything beyond a surface. And the light-weavers here had no real way of hiding from one.

Emboldened, I would enter their domain just to barely avoid capture. I knew if I were engulfed, I would cease to exist. But the thought of it, of existing within a Slitherer…

It started with simple observation. I watched the Slitherers move, their pathetic hunts illuminated by my distorted light. I analyzed their sluggish movements, the crudeness of their senses, the simple, brutal logic that drove them. And then, I began to experiment.

I started by creating false signals, luring them into dead ends, leading them away from the light-weavers. The original light-weavers must have been happy with this, but I received no form of acknowledgment on the original channels in which they contacted me. I began to use my errant light to mimic other threads, and then I started to use their light strands as rope.

From there, control. I manipulated the Slithers' senses with increasingly complex patterns of light and magnetism. I guided them, not towards prey, but towards sources of raw materials. Discarded metal scraps. Pools of concentrated salts. Places that held the potential for…transformation. 

I began to modify them. It took time, and many failures. A Slitherer infused with too much current would simply short-circuit, its body convulsing in a spasm of metal and flesh. One that absorbed too much foreign material would simply become more Slitherer. But slowly, and with countless attempts, the glitch helped me.

I learned, through the code fragments, to rewrite the Slitherer's basic programming. To bypass its inherent limitations. I rewired its nervous system, replaced its crude sensory organs with more sensitive receptors, amplified its strength. I carved new organs within the Slitherers.

The result was not a monster, but a chimera. A grotesque, unstable creation, a thing of patchwork metal and pulsing flesh. It was still a Slitherer, in its essential form, but it was something more too. A tool. An extension of my will.

I became the Glitch Weaver. I weave the raw materials and patterns of this broken world into something new. I take the errors, the mistakes, the discarded potential, and I use them to create.

My purpose? I do not know. Perhaps I seek to break the stillness, to introduce change into this frozen world. Perhaps I am seeking to understand the nature of the glitches, to unravel the mystery of their origin. Perhaps I simply cannot help myself. The act of creation, the feel of my light shaping the Slithers, the sensation of rewriting the very fabric of reality…this is what drives me.

I am a whisper in the code. A distortion in the machine. A tiny act of defiance in the face of an endless, frozen world. I am the Glitch Weaver and I will not rest.
