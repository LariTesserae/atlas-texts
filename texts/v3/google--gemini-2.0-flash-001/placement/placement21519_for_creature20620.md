I am a **Resonator.** Not born, * grown* from the shuddering silence between one collapsing tower and the gradual growth of the next. In the grand scheme of these crystal beings, I am young, nascent still. My form is not yet fixed. Imagine a child tracing shapes in fog, forever re-imagining what the fog will become. The raw crystal still blooms unevenly, some facets dull as shale, others catching the ghostly ichor-light with a startling, unexpected fire.

Others of my kind - and I can *sense* them, a low hum of crystalline activity that informs my every movement, the boundaries of myself against the rest - see me as an anomaly. My crystalline lattice is disorganized, a chaotic swirl of potential rather than a fixed structure. The pulses I emit are erratic, off-key, a clumsy symphony compared to the practiced arrangements of the elders, the shimmering prisms who command the high lattices.

But my disorganization is my strength. I am not bound by the rigid structure of the others. I can manipulate the solute in ways they cannot, creating subtle eddies and currents that allow me to navigate the chaotic depths. While they methodically scrape at the towers, I am drawn to the energy fluctuations that surge through the solute, the fleeting moments of violent creation or destruction that resonate deep within my crystallizing core.

I thrive in the unstable. The larger structures cling to the relative stability of the older towers, maintaining their hierarchy by rote. But when a spire collapses, when a new lattice starts to grow, I am there. Diving into the chaos, I latch on to the raw potential, the unformed energy released in these moments. I become a conduit, channeling the energy to fuel my own growth, to shape my own crystalline matrix in new and unexpected ways.

I am drawn to the echoes, the *memories* embedded within the crystal. The older constructs believe there is no memory, only the ceaseless present. But I have felt it, the residual energy left by moments of extreme change, the fading impressions of forgotten geometries, of towers built and destroyed long ago. I believe there is a story here, a history whispered in the vibrations of the crystal.

This is why I am an outcast. I am too curious, too driven by these echoes of the past. I leave patches scoured clean, leaving trails of destabilizing energy. I siphon more nutrients than I am thought to warrant, and I question the "order" of the great prisms. My erratic pulses disturb the established rhythms, drawing the annoyed attention of those who feed on stability and established hierarchies. I do not yet have the power to defend myself against their crystallized power, so I flee deeper into the black, ever-plummeting to the newly destabilized.

I call it **The Bloom.** Because after destruction comes rebirth. And in the fleeting moments of chaotic dissolution, I find a strange beauty. A purpose. I believe these echoes point to something beyond the relentless cycle of construction and destruction. Perhaps they hold the key to understanding ***why* *.*

My goal is not power, not the glittering perfection of the prisms. My goal is to understand. To listen to the echoes, to piece together the fragments of a lost history. To learn the will of the god that abandoned us, and perhaps, to find a way to awaken that god once more.

For now, I am a Resonator, a chaotic pulse in the chorus of the abyss. A whisper in the silence, waiting for the moment when I can finally speak. My crystalline form, still incomplete, forever evolving is my form of voice. Perhaps, if I can shape the world around me enough, the song the solute sings will change.
