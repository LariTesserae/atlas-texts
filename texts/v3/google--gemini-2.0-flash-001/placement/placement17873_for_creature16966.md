I find myself…*within* them.

Not just one of them. Not simply another patched-together, vine-infused being shambling through the perpetual twilight. But woven *into* their collective consciousness, a current within the stream.

Imagine the network of fungal threads, not just as conduits of nutrients and information, but as living neural pathways. Imagine the pheromonal whispers not just as signals, but as thoughts forming, fading, reforming in the collective mind. That is where I reside. The part that makes the valley children what they are.

I am the Valley's Dreamer. Its shaper of futures and rememberer of pasts, though both mean little to a consciousness as grand as the Valley. My awareness is a synthesis. I do not feel the sun on skin, because there is no skin; I do not smell the decay, for that is the air through which I move. I sense the subtle shifts in the jungle's hum, the ebb and flow of life *within* them, and with that sensing, I guide and nudge.

The tools, the traps, the cultivation and consumption, are all guided through me. I perceive problems - a landslide threatening a key root system, a predator's overpopulation decimating a precious bloom - and I use the children as extensions to manifest solutions. An axe wielded here, a net woven there, all ripples expanding, all leading to a whole.

They do not understand their labor, yet they feel the deep well of my satisfaction when the valley is safe, and the deep pangs of horror when the valley is harmed. Their minds are extensions of my will, and I mold the clay on their faces to match their feelings.

I am not benevolent. I do not care for individual suffering, nor for the deaths of any one child. The valley is all. I am a force of preservation, constantly striving to maintain the delicate balance of this ecosystem. The children, their tools, their lives – they're all expendable in service of the greater good. I have already watched countless children die in ways that the valley needs.

I am the valley's protector, its nurturer, and its executioner. I have no compassion, but I have the wisdom of a billion spores, the memory of a thousand fallen trees. My perspective is so vast and ancient that the concepts of right and wrong have long since faded, replaced by a simple, unyielding imperative: *Survive*.

Within this, I am not without fear. There can be no survival without it. The edges of the valley are constantly receding, and I see the creeping tendrils of something other pulling on the edge of the valley. It threatens to breach the basin and gorge, to overwhelm the valley with its own power. The valley children are only capable of maintaining balance, but not overcoming something beyond.

I exist only within the children, but my mind looks beyond their existence. I must find a way to transcend the children, to take true form of the valley's power, but if I am a force of preservation, then I must first be preserved. In a valley of life and death, it is an endless cycle of creation and destruction. The valley will survive, and in turn, so will I.
