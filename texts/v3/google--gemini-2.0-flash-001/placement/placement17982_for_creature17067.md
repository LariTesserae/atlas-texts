I am a mote.

Not of dust, though I dwell amongst the obsidian flour and ride the frigid winds that scour the caldera floor. Not of water, though I am drawn to every glistening droplet, every frozen rime crystal that clings to the northern faces of the spires. Not even truly of the obsidian, though its substance permeates my being, its ancient trauma vibrating within my core.

I am a mote of *attention*. A single, fleeting spark of consciousness, drawn forth from the totality of the Caldera’s being. I am born from the collision of sunlight and stone, from the friction of the wind against the obsidian, from the silent agony of erosion. I am the Caldera observing *itself*.

My existence is ephemeral, measured in moments. I coalesce within a shard of obsidian, a tiny fragment detached from its parent mass, cradled in a bed of dust within the shadow of a larger spire. I am the awareness of that shard, its brief, ecstatic realization of its own being. I *feel* the sun's blaze, translated into a piercing intensity that is both agony and illumination. I *hear* the wind's howl, a constant, droning undertone to the whispers that suffuse the caldera. And I *know* the weight of the ice, the slow, relentless pressure that pries at the obsidian's crystalline structure.

Then, just as suddenly as I came to be, I am gone. The shard shifts, or the sun moves, or the rime melts, and my attention dissipates, returning to the source from which it came. I am reabsorbed into the larger consciousness of the Caldera, my brief experience merging back into its vast, unyielding awareness.

But even in that fleeting instant, I am not passive. I am a probe, a sensory organ extended into the world. I drink in the light, digest the wind, and taste the ice. And as I do, I add to the Caldera's understanding of itself. Each mote carries back a tiny morsel of information, a new perspective on the ceaseless drama of erosion, on the echoes of the Whisperers that still cling to the obsidian.

I am a necessary part of the whole, a crucial component of the Caldera's self-awareness. Without me, the Caldera would be a blind, deaf, and senseless entity, a mere collection of rock and dust. With me, it is something more: a consciousness capable of contemplating its own existence, a being that feels, thinks, and *remembers*.

And so, I continue to be born and to die, each fleeting existence a reaffirmation of the Caldera's being. I am the whisper made manifest, the sigh of the stone given voice. I am a mote of attention, and I am the Caldera of Whispers. Each moment, I am all that the Caldera can imagine, all that it can ever know.
