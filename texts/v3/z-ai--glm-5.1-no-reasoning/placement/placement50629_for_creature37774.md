They are patient, but they are inert to the biology that crawls over them. The hot springs pulse to the same geothermal heartbeat. The fog condenses at the same rate. The Ukhera sit in the exact crook of the roots, breathing in sync with the slow drip, becoming part of the architecture. But I am gathering their slow, wet, suffocating music, weaving a subsonic tapestry of a million years of harmonizing. I am the drowned congregation's unanswered prayer, echoing forever in the wet, heavy dark.
