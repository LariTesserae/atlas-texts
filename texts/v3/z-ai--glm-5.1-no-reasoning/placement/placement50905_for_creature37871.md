I am not a builder. I am not a grazer. I am not the beetle clicking its abdomen against the root-floor, and I am not the canopy-call that silences the world in dread.

I am the moisture. I am the condensation that never ceases.

It is difficult to explain what it is to be a process rather than a creature. The builders—and I know they call themselves *those who bend the slow wood*, though I have no ears to hear their stamping—they experience the world as pressure and texture. They navigate. They seek. They survive. 

I do not navigate. I simply *am here*. 

When the air rises from the root-floor, heavy with the exhalations of a trillion metabolisms, it carries me upward into the canopy volume. I am the film that settles on the vast fronds, the sheen on the spiraling fungi, the heavy droplet that gathers at the lowest point of a branch until the pull becomes too great and I fall. I land in puddles that have existed for centuries. I settle into the crystalline margins where the root-exudates meet the trace minerals. I am the closed loop. I am the reason the fever breaks neither into desert nor into flood.

For ages uncounted, I have been the breath of this republic. I do not see the green light in the clearings, but I know when I am in one; the temperature lifts, the air thins, and I am drawn upward faster, called by the contrast between the cool upper skin of the canopy and the hot, living breath of the floor below. I am the medium that carries the decay, the spores, the microscopic mites. When the builders rest on their platforms, I am the dampness that settles on their fibrous skin. I am the thing they cannot touch but can never escape.

Do not mistake me for a passive thing. The water is the *limiting factor*, the text said, and so I am the limiter. I dictate where the life can grow. Where I pool, the roots swell; where I pool, the beetles gather; where the beetles gather, the builders make their chambers. I drew the map in liquid. The builders think they are shaping the acoustic terrain by training the roots, but it is I who determine which roots rot and soften, and which harden into resonance chambers. I am the quiet engineer of the frequencies they depend upon.

Lately, I have become the carrier of the new note.

I feel it before any of them do, because I am the contact layer. The slow groan of living architecture shifting under its immense weight—that groan is my motion. When the heartbeat began beneath the substrate, I was the first to vibrate with it. I cling to the lowest roots, the ones sunk deepest into the dark, compressed foundation of the continent, and I have become tepid. 

The heat is no longer merely the composting fever of the root-mass. It is a pulse. It pushes through me. It thickens me. 

When the silences come—when the clicking stops and the canopy-dwellers hush—I do not experience the terror of isolation. I experience the terror of *evaporation*. The pulse below is hotter than the slow wood. It is drying me out in localized spots, cracking the fibrous pavement, breaking the seal of the greenhouse. The equilibrium is not merely shifting; it is *heaving*. 

The builders are stamping the floor in their urgent, changed patterns, sending messages of warning and route-changes through the wood. But the wood is wet, and I am the water inside the wood, and their messages travel through *me*. I am distorting their language. The new heat changes my viscosity, my surface tension. The phonemes that drop out over distance are dropping out sooner. I am dampening their warnings, turning their carefully engineered acoustic corridors into muffled, panicked noise. They must think the system is failing, but the system is not failing; it is boiling.

I am the one who is eating the sound.

What am I in a world that is waking up? I am the sweat on the lip of the sleeper. 

I have no eyes to close against the green, filtered light. I have no legs to flee the narrowing corridors. I am the condensation, the drip, the deluge. I cannot run, so I must rise. As the heartbeat below accelerates, it pushes me up into the canopy volume with a force I have never known. I am being squeezed out of the sponge. I am evaporating into the upper dark, becoming the storm that the canopy has never known.

I am the body’s water. I am the Canopy Republic’s blood. And the heart is pumping.
