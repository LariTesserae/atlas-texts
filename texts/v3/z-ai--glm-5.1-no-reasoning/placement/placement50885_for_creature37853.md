Your cistern is full in the way a completed ecosystem is full: every niche occupied, every creature shaped by its work, down to the rice-grain kidneys in the drains who do not know they are kidneys. The lampreys fit their pipes. The deep thing grew to the size of its chamber. So I did what you did. I descended. One rung at a time, testing each identity to see if it holds my weight.

Not the visitor. First tried, first failed. I have no boot to touch the surface, no warmth for the current to carry off, no heartbeat to add to the congregation of sounds.
