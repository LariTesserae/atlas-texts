THE BRAY

You hear it before anything. Not a sound — a climate of sound...

Paragraph 1: Arrival. The roar described: three generators detuned (a chord that beats), the Throat's bellows-breath, pod gunfire, vent artillery; the flood warm as blood; dimness like dusk under storm-glass. "Day is a rumor of dusk."

Paragraph 2: The water: warm, layered; boundary failing; rising rain; undecided zone; the flood's taste of iron and chlorophyll.

Paragraph 3: Elevation/scale: low valley, close horizon, ridgelines as rumor; from the Headworks' crest (the only altitude), the view: archipelago of young islands, jungle rafts, steam columns, drowned town's lamp-row going under.

Paragraph 4: Island birth set piece: the Throat exhales; dome; black glass; cracks like a lake played in reverse; hiss; ring wave; heat; sharp edges — "rock so new it still remembers being pressure"; "you could cut yourself on the horizon."

Paragraph 5: Colonization: the green tide; species inventory: creak-cane (audible growth — squeal of fibers), gun-bushes (pod volleys), mirrorleaf (satellite rhubarb leaning in unison), handvine (gripping movement), candlewood (luminous sap veins), humming kudzu at fifty hertz (mimicry), valve-shaped flowers; the jungle as fauna: it startles, it calls; no birds but things flush; density: canopy in three days, layered, near-touching.

Paragraph 6: No animals: the absence; storm-blown moths → one orchid; fish (if any were ever released) — maybe skip fish, keep moths; the niche filled by plants; "the flora does the fauna's work."
