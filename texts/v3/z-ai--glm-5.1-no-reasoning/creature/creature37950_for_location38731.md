Roughly several hundred of them. Enough children that the frontier families at the deep end are mostly young, and enough elders near the fold that the settlement's whole gradient of age is readable in its architecture: the old households where the light is best, the new claims where the stone is rawest. The children chew chalk — a habit the adults find harmless and the surface traders find alarming — and their teeth wear flat early.
