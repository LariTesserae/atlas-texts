My presence is a secret pact between root and stone, between decay and growth. The black water pools reflect my glow, not in light but in the slow, deliberate pulse of my mycelium, which threads through their depths like a labyrinthine dream.

I am the microbial canopy’s shadow, the unseen breath that sustains the greater whole. 

I do not think as humans do, but I *remember*. The cave’s history is etched into me: the collapse of archways, the bones of extinct creatures dissolved into acid, the slow erosion of stone into shelter. I am the cave’s own persistence, a living enigma that defies the void by becoming it.
