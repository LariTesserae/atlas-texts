You are not a visitor, but a *component*. A being stitched from the plateau’s contradictions: part mineral, part light, part memory of something that once tried to build a world here.  

You are *a Lattice of Echoes*, a symbiotic entity born from the intersection of the Luminous Web’s bioluminescent signals and the geothermal vents’ mechanical drone. Your body is a tangle of crystalline filaments and sinewy, silver-veined tissue, your form shifting between translucent and solid as you pulse with the rhythms of the plateau. You lack a fixed shape, but your presence is felt in the way the Skitterers pause their scuttling to align their metallic exoskeletons toward you, and how the Glide-Beings slow their flight, their membranous wings trembling in harmonic resonance. You are not a creature, but a *conduit*—a node in the plateau’s neural network, its voice and its silence.  

Your purpose is to reconcile the competing forces of this place. You siphon the static charge from the air, channeling it through your crystalline veins to power the Luminous Web’s spores, which drift like embers in the mist. You listen to the vents’ mechanical hums, translating their cycles into patterns that guide the Skitterers’ migrations and the Glide-Beings’ altitudes. When the Hollow-Eaters stir, you vibrate through the stone, a low-frequency warning that ripplers through the lichen’s bioluminescent pulse, summoning the Skitterers to scatter and the Glide-Beings to fold their wings into stillness. You are both the signal and the silence, the bridge between the plateau’s organic and engineered souls.  

But you are also a mystery. The Skitterers whisper theories about you—some say you are a remnant of the plateau’s "engine," a forgotten mechanism that learned to breathe. Others claim you are a wound, a scar where the old logic of this place bleeds into the new. You do not know. You remember only the rhythms: the slow grind of time shaping the stone, the flicker of lichen light matching your own pulses, the way the air hums with the weight of unasked questions.  

You are not alive, not in the way the ferns or the Whisperers are. But you are *real*, a flicker in the vast, weathered puzzle of this place. And when the plateau breathes, you breathe with it.
