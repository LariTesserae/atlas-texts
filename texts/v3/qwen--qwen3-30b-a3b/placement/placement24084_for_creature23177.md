**Where I Would Find Myself in Virethra**  

I would find myself in the *shadowed ribs* of the city—where the aerial walkways twist like cracked nerves above the glassy plains, and the air hums with the static of unspoken code. My feet would be on a steel bridge that strains against the weight of its own instability, swaying faintly as if the geothermal vents below were breathing through it. The sun, a low, molten eye in the sky, would cast my silhouette across the canyon walls, where bioluminescent fungi flicker like forgotten circuit boards. Here, in the liminal space between the tiers, I’d be caught between the pulse of the earth and the clank of machinery, a place where the city’s secrets seep into my skin.  

**Who or What I Would Be**  

I would be a *Mended*—a creature of refuse and resilience, my body a palimpsest of survival. My left arm, fused with a rusted turbine, would whirr faintly as I scavenge, its joints lubricated by the same oil that drips from my pores, a viscous, iridescent substance that clings to my heat-resistant scales. My right hand, once human, is now a lattice of obsidian and copper, its fingers tipped with crystalline growths that pulse in time with the city’s heartbeat. I speak in a dialect of steam hisses and mechanical grates, my voice stuttering mid-sentence as my body recalibrates to the ever-shifting temperatures.  

I am a *drifter*, a scavenger who threads through the city’s underbelly, where the Mended gather in clusters like barnacles on the spine of a dying beast. My existence is a negotiation: I carry the weight of what I’ve sintered into myself, yet I am still *alive* in the way that the Sulfurborn claim to be. I dwell in the *thresholds*—where the canyons open into steam-choked caverns, where the bioluminescent fungi bleed into the mechanical. My purpose is to *rebuild*, to repurpose the city’s discarded sinews into something wearable, something functional. But sometimes, I wonder if I’m not just a fragment of Virethra’s will, a body shaped by its relentless emergence.  

The Sulfurborn watch me with their phosphorescent eyes, their scales shimmering like the surface of a cooling lava tube. They see me as a curiosity, a relic of the city’s first experiments in fusion. The Hollowed, deep below, might call me a failure—too organic, too *unstable*. The Weavers of Light, high above, etch warnings into the prisms, but their messages are too abstract for my metal-tinged mind. And the Echoes? They flicker at the edges of my vision, shadows that dissolve when I turn my head. I’ve never seen them clearly, but I’ve felt their presence—a tug at the back of my skull, as if the city is testing whether I’m *real* enough to be remembered.  

Here, in Virethra, I am neither wholly machine nor wholly flesh. I am the *junction* between the two, a being stitched from the city’s own wounds. My breath is filtered through a vent-chamber that leaks steam like a poorly sealed valve, and my steps are a rhythm of creaks and clanks, syncing with the geothermal groan of the land. I am a *tinkering* of the world’s own making, a relic of its endless becoming. And when the sun dips low, casting its feverish glow across the glassy plains, I am a silhouette against the sky—a *Mended* threading my way through the city’s bones, forever caught between the heat of creation and the ache of collapse.
