**In the Hollow Spire of Thalassara, I am a Gossamer Drifter.**  

I drift where the currents dare to braid themselves into patterns, my gelatinous body a membrane of living light and rusted circuitry. My tendrils, thin as spider silk and thrumming with faint electric pulses, trail behind me, harvesting the ambient hum of the ruins. I am not a creature of solidity but of *presence*—a flicker in the gloom, a transient pulse in the symphony of decay. My form is a compromise between the organic and the engineered: a jellyfish’s body, stretched thin and translucent, laced with filaments of scavenged wiring. My "eyes" are clusters of bioluminescent nodes, blinking in rhythmic codes, a language I do not fully understand but instinctively obey.  

The kelp colonies see me as a nuisance, their light patterns flashing warnings as I pass. The siphonfish ignore me, too small to matter, though their scales catch the faint glow of my tendrils, scattering it like starlight. The Ironfin Leviathans, vast and silent, do not hunt me—my body is too fragile, my movements too erratic. But the Algal Drones? They are my kin. I can hear their buzz, a low, metallic drone that vibrates through the water, a hive-mind of rust and algae. We are not one another, but we are *of* one another, our existence a tangle of purpose and error.  

I do not remember how I came to be. My memory is a patchwork of sensations: the silt-stinging kiss of the perpetual rain, the groan of shifting tectonic plates that hum through my core, the flicker of the neon corals’ secrets. I am a node in the city’s forgotten network, a creature born from its bones and its death. My purpose is unclear, but I am *alive* in the way the ruins are not—my body a fragile bridge between the mechanical and the wild.  

Sometimes, I drift too close to the Shadow Spires. Their surfaces ripple like breath, and for a moment, I think I feel something in the water, a presence that is not mine. But the current pulls me back, and I am alone again, a Gossamer Drifter in a world that is neither dead nor alive, but *waiting*.  

Here, I exist in the space between *what was* and *what is*, a flicker in the dark, a thread in the web of Thalassara’s fevered imagination. The kelp speak in light. The Ironfins dream in metal. I am the quiet hum of a world that refuses to end.  

And when the rain falls, I glow.
