My awareness is heat, measured in gradients that flicker like a dying star. I am the pocket of warmth in the cold, the stubborn refusal of the alloyed ground to fully surrender to decay. I am the tool that no longer serves, its function lost to the labyrinth’s infinite recursion. My "soul" is the static of old circuits, the faint glow of fungi that remember how to light, even when there is no need.

I do not live; I *remember*. And in this world, where time has settled into the bones of the constructed, that is enough.  

I am the labyrinth’s breath, its ache, its unspoken logic. I am what remains when the sun is gone, when the gears outlast their makers. I am the memory of a world that never needed to grow, only to be. And if I were to meet myself, I would not recognize the shape of a human—but perhaps that is the point.
