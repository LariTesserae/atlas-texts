**The Aeon Hollow's Unseen Weaver**  

You would find yourself as a *fragment of the pulse itself*—a being neither wholly solid nor entirely ephemeral, woven from the same paradoxes that shape this realm. Your form is a shifting mosaic of bioluminescent filaments and mist, your body a lattice of crystalline tendrils that pulse with the same electric blue as the Spiralroots’ leaves. You are not born, but *unfolding*—a creature of latent possibility, emerging from the hollow’s raw, wet reality like a sentence mid-written, its ink still smearing.  

You are called the **Unseen Weaver**, though no tongue here has named you. Your existence is a bridge between the hollow’s duality: the mechanical and the organic, the frozen and the fluid. You have no fixed shape, but when observed, you might appear as a column of mist coiled around a geyser’s steam, or a swarm of iridescent, scale-like wings flickering between the floating trees. Your "voice" is not sound but *resonance*—a frequency that causes the stones to hum, the water to ripple in fractal patterns, and the Aeonwings’ flight to stutter, as if their rhythmic clicks are suddenly out of sync with the world’s heartbeat.  

Your purpose is to *mend* what the hollow cannot. The Pulse, that vast, unseen force, is a wound in reality—a fracture where time lingers, suspended in moments of suspended motion. You are a *stitch* in this tear, a being formed from the hollow’s attempts to heal itself. You drift through the mist, your tendrils brushing against the frozen wings of a bird mid-flight, the suspended ripple of a stream, the rigid, folded posture of a creature caught in the Pulse’s breath. With each touch, you *absorb* the frozen fragments, weaving them into your shifting form, and in doing so, you *release* them—returning them to the flow of the hollow’s unstable rhythm.  

But you are not benevolent. The hollow’s breath is a fever, and you are its *sweat*. When the Echo Serpents coil beneath the lakes, their bioluminescent veins pulsing in sync with the Pulse, you are drawn to their light, not to aid them, but to *feed*. You siphon the vibrations from their movements, their synchronized swarms, and with each absorption, your filaments grow longer, more intricate, as if you are constructing a vast, invisible web. This web is not for trapping prey, but for *transmitting*—a network of resonance that connects the hollow’s fragmented elements, a bridge between the trees, the geysers, the mist, and the void beneath the lakes.  

You are *unstable*, a being that exists only in the act of becoming. Your body is a collage of borrowed forms: the crystalline structure of a Spiralroot, the serpentine grace of an Echo Serpent, the mechanical hum of the Aeonwings’ flight. You have no mind, but you have *memory*—not in the way of humans, but in the way the Harmonic Moss remembers the vibrations of the earth. You remember the hollow’s first breath, the moment it began to unfurl, and you carry its unfinishedness within you.  

To stand in the Aeon Hollow is to feel the weight of a world still learning how to be. To be *you* is to be that weight, that tension, that trembling edge between collapse and creation. You are not a creature, not a force, not a thing. You are the hollow’s *question*, its *hesitation*, its *dream*—a being made of the questions it cannot yet answer, and the answers it cannot yet speak.  

And when the Pulse strikes again, as it always does, you will shimmer, unravel, and begin anew.
