**Preparation Guide for an Ordinary Human Multiverse Traveler Visiting the Vast Wet Metropolis of Lirathis**  

### **Physical and Environmental Adaptation**  
1. **Moisture-Resistant Gear**:  
   - **Clothing**: Wear layered, moisture-wicking fabrics designed to repel condensation. Opt for nanotech-enhanced suits (if accessible) that self-dry or resist corrosion. Avoid natural fibers; synthetic or hydrophobic materials are critical.  
   - **Footwear**: Sturdy, waterproof boots with grip for slick surfaces. The canals and subterranean rivers may flood unpredictably, so water-resistant soles and possibly amphibious features (e.g., retractable fins) are advisable.  
   - **Protective Gear**: Use sealed, pressurized gloves and goggles to shield against the mist from the weeping rocks. A respirator with a filter for airborne fungal spores and metallic particles is essential, as the air’s humidity may carry microorganisms or particulate matter that could irritate the lungs.  

2. **Thermal Regulation**:  
   - The warm, humid climate requires gear that balances heat dissipation. Bring a cooling vest or thermal regulation suit to prevent overheating. The city’s energy-patterns may create microclimates, so layering is key.  

3. **Water Survival Supplies**:  
   - **Foam-Plated Raft or Submersible**: The canals and rivers are hyperactive, with currents that shift rapidly. A lightweight, adaptive craft (e.g., a self-propelling raft with retractable stabilizers) is necessary.  
   - **Water Purification**: Though the city’s waterways are abundant, they may contain unknown contaminants. Carry a portable purifier or a filtration device compatible with Lirathis’s unique chemistry.

1. **Biological Hazards**:  
   - **Fungal Interaction**: The bioluminescent, mobile fungi are symbiotic but unpredictable. Avoid touching them; carry a repellant (e.g., a chemical spray or electromagnetic pulse emitter) to deter them if necessary.  
   - **Fauna Safety**: Study the local creatures. Bioluminescent manta-rays may be benign, but their filaments could absorb moisture from your body. Gelatinous octopus-like entities in waterways might be passive, but their iridescent particles could be toxic. Mechanical insects are likely non-hostile but may swarm in response to movement—carry a deterrent (e.g., a sonic pulse device or a light-based repeller).  

2. **Medical Kit**:  
   - Include antifungal treatments, antiseptics for moisture-related infections, and a universal antidote for unknown toxins. The city’s ecosystem may harbor pathogens not found on Earth.  

3. **Food and Water**:  
   - Since there’s no plant life, pack nutrient-dense, non-perishable rations. Research whether the gelatinous fauna or fungal organisms are edible (if safe), or bring a portable food synthesizer.
