3. **First Aid Kit:** Rusted pipes, sharp roots, and unknown flora/fauna mean cuts, scrapes, and allergic reactions are possible. Include antiseptics, bandages, and antihistamines. 4. **Sturdy Supplies:** Pack lightweight, non-perishable food (e.g., energy bars, canned goods) and a portable stove. The environment may not support traditional foraging, and the "reclaimed" nature of the place could mean plants are either toxic or biologically atypical.
