- **Thermal Protection:** The climate is perpetually cool, with a "thin" air that feels both sharp and oppressive. Layer with insulated, moisture-wicking thermal gear, and pack a heat-emitting device (e.g., a thermal blanket or chemical hand warmer array) to combat the chill. However, be prepared for sudden temperature shifts as the landscape “breathes”—the glaciers’ hum and the factories’ steam may create microclimates that defy logic.  
- **Respiratory Gear:** The air is thick with iron, ozone, and particulate matter from the factories.
