This is a place of paradoxes: a high-altitude wetland where the sky is both a ceiling and a mirror, a constructed world where nature is not an afterthought but a collaborator. The rules here are not written but *felt*—a rhythm of motion and stillness, of creation and decay, where every drop of water, every whisper of sound, and every flicker of light is part of a system too vast to fully comprehend.
