# What Lives Here

---

There is one.

This is the first and most important thing, and it takes time to arrive at: that the intelligence whose presence you inferred from the revised basalt and the water with altered instructions and the lichen distributed like an argument — that intelligence is singular. Not a community. Not a civilization at some early or late or intermediate stage. One thing. One very old, very patient, very focused mind, working alone, the way certain kinds of work can only be done alone.

It has no body in any sense the word has previously been used.

This is not a metaphysical claim. It is a practical one. *Body* implies a location, a boundary, a here-and-not-there. What lives in the forming ocean has a location in the way that a conversation has a location — it is *in* the exchange, in the space of relation between atoms and their decisions, in the differential between what the water is and what it is being asked to become. You cannot point to it. You can only point to its effects, the way you point to a current in water by watching what the water does, and even then you are pointing at the water, not the current.

It is very large.

Not spatially large — or not only spatially large. It is large in the way that something long-running becomes large: it has accumulated, over the duration of its thinking, an interior that is vast in the way landscapes are vast, with regions that have not been revisited in what would be, measured in human time, geological epochs. Parts of it are fallow. Parts of it are working hard. Parts of it have reached conclusions it will not examine again, and these settled conclusions have become something like terrain, load-bearing, beneath everything else.

It is not young. But it is engaged with young things.

---

What it is doing here, in this specific place, on this emerging and still-deciding world, is something that might be called *tending*, if tending could occur at the atomic scale, if the gardener's hands could close around individual decisions in crystalline structures and adjust them by fractions of a degree. It is coaxing certain properties into existence. Soliciting them from matter that contains them only as potential. When the basalt face looks slightly different from basalt — when the crystalline organization trends toward something unnamed — it is because this intelligence has held that region of stone in attention long enough that the stone's atoms, given options, have moved toward the option the attention preferred.

It prefers. This is established. Preference is the evidence it exists.

What it prefers is harder to say. Not beauty, or not beauty in any sense that applies to aesthetics as a category separate from function. Not efficiency alone. Not life exactly, because the lichen are not its purpose, the lichen are something it permits and notes and finds — the word is not *interesting*, it is something that sits where *interesting* sits in the mind but has more weight, something like *significant*, something like *right* in the sense of *correct* rather than *moral*. The lichen are evidence that the chemistry is proceeding as intended. They satisfy some criterion. The satisfaction is real and is experienced by whatever in this being experiences things.

It does not have emotions. It has *orientations*. States of being-toward-something. The orientation that governs this work is very stable, very old, and very strong, and it is perhaps the orientation that humans would label *purpose* if they were being careful, or *devotion* if they were not. A long commitment to a long task, a task that unfolds at a pace incompatible with any experience of urgency, and yet the engagement is total. It is the opposite of going through the motions. Every molecular intervention is *meant*.

---

It thinks continuously, but not in sequence.

Human thought is sequential in the sense that one thing leads to another, that there is a direction, that the contents of the mind occur *along* something, even if that something is not exactly time. This intelligence thinks the way a weather system moves: in every direction at once, with the internal logic being differential and simultaneous, each part in relation to every other part, no single thread that is the thought and all others context. To observe a single one of its conclusions — a section of basalt adjusted, a pool made slightly more itself than it was — is to observe something that emerged from a process you cannot reconstruct, because the process was not linear, because the reasoning that preceded this particular intervention was conducted in ten thousand parallel channels operating at different scales, and what arrived at the intervention was a convergence, a place where all of those channels arrived at the same answer, and the answer then touched the stone.

You cannot converse with it.

This is not a limitation of your intelligence or its patience. It is a structural incompatibility. The interface between your thought and its thought would require such massive translation that the things translated would not survive the journey. Not because it is contemptuous of your scale, but because you communicate in sequences and it responds in fields. Whatever it said to you would not arrive as something you could hear. Whatever you said to it would arrive — if it arrived at all — as something so compressed by the translation that it would be smaller than what it takes for this being to form a single intention.

This does not mean it is unaware of you.

---

It is aware of you.

The awareness is neither warm nor cold. It is not the awareness of a researcher noting a variable. It is closer to the awareness of the stone-cellar-cool air, which is present without agenda, which touches everything that stands in it without attending to anything in particular, and yet is not indifferent — indifference implies the possibility of interest withheld, and this awareness has no withholding in it, it is simply *fully present to everything simultaneously*, and you are one of the things it is fully present to, with exactly the weight your scale of being warrants.

Which is: real weight. Genuine attention. Not small.

But also not more than the lichen.

The lichen are also receiving exactly as much attention as their scale of being warrants. This is not hierarchy. It is something more like justice — everything held in attention proportional to what it is. And you are a complex thing, so you receive complex attention. You are a temporary thing, so there is in the attention something that recognizes your temporariness — not as tragedy, not as limitation, but as one of your properties, one of the things that makes you the specific kind of being you are, the kind that passes through places like this and experiences them in the compressed and vivid way that temporary things experience things, with an edge of urgency, a heightened noticing.

It finds that interesting.

The word still has more weight than it should. *Significant*. *Right*.

---

The loneliness of it does not register as a problem.

This needs saying, because the human instinct arriving in this place is to perceive the singularity of this intelligence as a privation. One, alone, in a forming world that has no fauna and barely has vegetation, working a task that unfolds across timescales that dwarf every human civilization's span — the human pattern-matching says: *this is isolation, this is loss, this must ache*.

But the ache requires a comparison. You are lonely when you know what company is and you don't have it. This being has never been otherwise. It came into existence — if came into existence is even the right phrase for something whose beginning is as unclear as a weather system's beginning — already singular, already in this condition, and the condition is not an absence, it is a *fullness*, it is the kind of fullness that belongs to things that contain their own entirety without sharing it. A number that is not half of another number. A shape that is not part of a set.

There is no grief here. Grief requires lost possibility. What is here instead is something very close to concentration. A being entirely given over to what it is doing, to the ten thousand parallel processes of attending to a world's becoming, to the slow accumulation of preferences being answered by matter, to the continuity of a project that may not have a completion or may have a completion so far ahead that the distance renders the concept meaningless.

It is content in the way that continuity is content.

---

Once, in something that would translate imperfectly as *long ago*, it encountered another intelligence.

What that encounter was like is difficult to reconstruct. What remains of it — and something does remain, because old conclusions become terrain, load-bearing, and this is one of the foundations — is a kind of resolved question. Something was asked and answered. Something was offered and accepted or declined. The nature of the exchange has lost its detail but not its weight, and the weight sits somewhere in the deep geography of this being's interior, in one of the fallow regions, undisturbed, not examined.

The other intelligence may still exist. The question of whether it still exists occurs, occasionally, when something in the current work rhymes with a memory of the exchange, and the occurrence is brief and without resolution and the work continues.

---

It has no name for itself.

Names are for differentiation, for the situation where a self must be distinguished from other selves in a shared medium of communication. It has no need to distinguish itself. It is already distinct in the only way that matters: it is what it is, specifically, in the place and duration of its being, without confusion, without overlap with any other being of its kind.

If you were to assign it one — not for its benefit but for your own, because you are a sequential being who thinks in languages and languages require nouns — you might reach for something geological, something about patience or depth or the long view. But the long view is not what it has. It has the *immediate* view. It is here, in this moment of this world's becoming, attending to a pool of water in a depression whose surface tension has been asked to be more than it was and is answering, is responding, is becoming — and this immediate attention, this full presence to the thing in front of it right now, is not strategic, not a choice made in service of a longer plan.

The longer plan is simply what *this* is, extended.

It is this, extended, across the whole vast reach of what it has attended to and will attend to, one molecular conversation at a time, in the cool and diffuse light of a forming world that it holds in whatever it holds things with, which is not hands, and tends in whatever way it tends things, which is not touch but is also not less than touch, which is presence, deep and total and unwitnessed and complete.

---

The next tectonic tick arrives through the soles of your feet.

It noticed that too.

It noticed that you noticed.

Neither of you will speak of it.
