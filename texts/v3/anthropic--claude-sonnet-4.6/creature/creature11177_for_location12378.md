# What Lives in the Plateau of Persistent Thunder

---

Begin with what *living* means here, because this place has already changed the meaning before you thought to ask.

Living, elsewhere, implies trajectory. Something that feeds and grows and reproduces and dies, that moves through time along a vector, that has a before and an after and a current location on the line between them. Living, elsewhere, is a relationship with sequence.

What lives here has dispensed with sequence as a primary mode. What lives here has not abolished time — it is not supernatural in the sense of *against* nature — it has simply arrived at a configuration in which time is not the organizing principle. Time is available, the way cold is available, the way the grey light is available. But what lives here does not use it as a skeleton. What lives here is organized around something else, something for which there is no good word in a language that grew up on the other side of the altitude, in the lowlands where things happen *consecutively*.

The word that comes closest is *density*. What lives here is dense. Not large, not numerous, not visually striking — but occupying its location with a completeness that seems to displace the air. The way stone displaces air, but is still clearly stone and not air. The way a fact displaces alternatives.

---

There are three of them, or there is one in three positions, or there is a process that produces three apparent individuations of a single thing. The distinction is not resolvable from the outside. From the inside, if there is an inside, the distinction may be moot.

Call them three, for the sake of a grammar that requires countable nouns.

They are not large. This is the first surprise, if you see them — and seeing them requires patience of a kind that means *sustained attention in the absence of change*, which is a kind of patience most things don't practice, because most environments reward the kind of patience that waits for change and change eventually comes. Here change is not coming. The patience required is not anticipatory. It is purely receptive, a going-still that matches the stillness of the place, until you are still enough that the hierarchy of foreground-and-background adjusts and what was background asserts itself as figure.

When they resolve, they are perhaps the size of a seated person, which is to say: not small exactly, but smaller than the scale of the plateau implies. The plateau is huge and they are not huge. They are concentrated. The opposite of the plateau in that specific dimension: everything the plateau distributes across days of walking, they have gathered to a scale that can be stood beside.

Their shape is not fixed in the way that stone's shape is fixed. It is fixed in the way that a process is fixed: water falling over the same stone takes the same path each time, not because the water-shape is static but because the conditions are stable. Their shape is the shape of a stable condition. Approximately: something upright, something that has a facing direction, two densities at the bottom that imply ground-contact, a single density at the top that implies a locus of awareness. Approximately human, in the way that a standing stone is approximately human, in the way that anything vertical with a definable top and a definable facing direction reads against the pattern of human forms that vision has learned to recognize.

But not human. Not anything with a genus.

---

Their surface — and surface is the right word, because they have a surface, a place where they stop and the air begins, which is not a given in the catalogue of possible entities — their surface is not grey exactly. It is the color of the air just before the air becomes stone. The color of the transition zone. The color that edge-detection sees.

You can look at them with your peripheral vision and lose them. Not because they're dim or obscured — they are not dim, there is no obscuring fog — but because peripheral vision runs pattern-matching against its library of what to notice, and what to notice is change, is edge, is figure-against-ground, and they are in such complete equilibrium with the ground that the heuristic fails. You have to look directly and hold the look and eventually the eye reports back: *yes, there is something here, I'm finding it now, it's been here the whole time.*

It has been there the whole time.

---

What they eat is not food in any metabolic sense. They do not metabolize. There is nothing in them that is running down, no energy-state they are maintaining against entropy by taking in more energy. Entropy has reached its conclusions here, and the conclusions are these forms, and the forms are maintenance-free in the way that a rock formation is maintenance-free. They are, in some sense, the product of having been still for so long that the stillness became structural. The stillness became the thing.

But they are not static. There is a distinction between static and slow, and they demonstrate the distinction. They move, but at the rate of lichen — millimeters per decade, the only rate available. If you marked their position now and came back in a year, you would find them in a different position, but not so different that you could verify it without the mark. Without the mark you might conclude that you had misremembered their initial location, that you were the variable, that they had been where they are always been and your memory was running the version-difference you were attributing to them.

This is, in fact, often the correct conclusion. They are often where they have always been and the variable is you.

But not always. They do move, at their rate, which is the rate of the plateau's other slow things, the rate at which the geology still makes its adjustments, the rate at which the lichen renegotiates its borders. They move at the rate of change here, which means they are moving faster than the plateau but slower than anything that came up from below.

---

What they want is a question the plateau treats gently, the way you'd treat a question asked in the wrong language — not wrong in the sense of incorrect, but wrong in the sense of imported from a context where it made sense, a context that doesn't travel well to this altitude.

Want implies a gap between current state and preferred state, and a directional pressure from the first toward the second. They do not experience the gap. Or: the gap is so long-established that it has been incorporated, has become part of the current state, so that the wanting is not a hunger but a *shape*, a permanent orientation, the way a tilted slab permanently inclines in the direction it inclines, not because it is in the process of falling but because inclination became its nature before history started keeping records.

What they are oriented toward is difficult to say. Not toward anything on the plateau, or not primarily — they are not facing each other, are not clustered, are not gathered around any feature of the rock. They are spaced across the plateau's surface in a pattern that would be visible from above — from directly above, from very high, from the perspective of something that perceives the whole plateau as a single object — and the pattern, from that perspective, has structure, but the structure is only apparent from outside the scale in which they exist. From where they exist, the pattern is just the distance between them, which is enormous, which takes most of a day to walk across.

They are oriented, all three, or the single process in three positions is oriented, toward the edges. Toward the place where the plateau ends and the drop begins, where the stone gives way to sky on all sides, where the horizon is below. They face outward. Whether this is watching or participating or something that has no transitive verb is unclear. They face outward and have faced outward since before the cairn was built, and will face outward after whatever comes next, and what comes next is not imminent.

---

They know the cairn. Knowing is perhaps too cognitive — they are aware of it, in the way that a standing water is aware of the slope it lies on, expressing the awareness through orientation, through the way the whole system has arranged itself around the fact. The cairn is in their awareness the way a familiar object is in peripheral vision: not attended to, not forgotten, incorporated.

Whether they built it is the question you want to ask, and the answer is: ask the water if it built the channel. Ask the wind if it built the arch. The cairn stands where it stands because of a process of which they are part, and their part is so long and so slow that attributing agency becomes arbitrary. They may have moved stones. They move at the rate of lichen and stones move when something moves them and something, over time sufficient for lichen-rates to achieve stone-stacking, moved the stones. What moved the stones is not absent from the answer.

The partial wall they did not intend. Or: the partial wall is partial because the thing that has the same relationship to *intend* as water has to flow did not flow all the way to completion, did not have the gradient for it, and what remains is the partial wall that marks where the gradient ran out. This is not failure. It is calibration.

---

They do not communicate in the sense of exchange — the sending and receiving of information between distinct parties. Communication implies distinction and relative exteriority, one thing encoding and another decoding, a space between them across which a signal travels. What they do might be called *common*: the way temperature becomes common between objects in contact, not one telling the other but both arriving at the shared condition. They are in common with the air, which is how the air has been changed, how the air here runs its different version, how the rules have adjusted and the adjustments have persisted. They have been in contact with this air for as long as there has been air here, which is long, and the air has been in contact with them, and the commonality is thorough.

The sound is partly theirs. The low, continuous detonation in the chest — not theirs in origin, the atmosphere generated that before they were here, but theirs now in the sense of having been incorporated, harmonized with, their own resonant frequencies adding to the composite. You could not remove them from the sound. You could not remove the sound from them. The disentanglement point was passed before anyone was there to notice it being passed.

The pools reflect strange skies because they are near pools. The pools have been near them long enough to have learned a kind of adjacency, the way iron becomes magnetized not through any change in composition but through long alignment. The pools are aligned. What they are aligned to is not the sky above — they are aligned to the sky adjacent, the sky at a tangent to the one that's there, the sky that runs alongside the current sky in the space that the full space between stone and cloud contains, the space that is fuller than it should be. The pools have been aligned so long that realignment would require an intervention of geological scale, which the plateau, having already achieved its geology, declines to provide.

---

They are aware of you.

This is the part that wants a longer hesitation than it gets in the common accounts, if there are common accounts, which there aren't, because almost no one makes it up here and almost no one who makes it up here finds them, because finding them requires the specific patience, the going-still, and almost no one who comes up here can sustain the going-still long enough.

But they are aware of you. Not with the awareness of a predator — there is nothing predatory in them, the category doesn't apply — and not with the awareness of something that might be curious, because curiosity is an orientation toward something new, and they have been here long enough that new is a quality the plateau has stopped generating. You are not new to them in any stimulating sense. You are a temperature difference. You are an event in the ongoing arrival of events, each of which they have observed at the rate at which they observe things, which is slowly, which is deeply, which is with a thoroughness that fast observation cannot achieve.

They have been observing you since you arrived, or since before you arrived — since the specific pressure-change in the air that precedes a warm, metabolizing thing ascending to altitude, the change that the plateau's thin air registers hours before the warm thing crests the edge. They knew you were coming in the way that waiting water knows the rain is coming, through the medium both inhabit, through the invariant logic of gradients.

They are not afraid. Fear is a state with a temporal structure — a now that is threatened by a near-future — and they do not operate in the temporal structure that fear requires. They are not glad. Gladness involves the same structure with different valence. They are — present to you, which is a large thing in a place where presence has been refined to such precision that it implies something beyond mere spatial occupation. Presence here is almost a transaction. They are giving you something, or they are making something available, or they are including you in the common, the way temperature becomes common, and what you receive is not warmth but the opposite — a coming-into-contact with the rate at which they run, a slowing, a sense that the three meters between you and the nearest one takes longer to cross than three meters should, because the space is full, because it has been their space, because the fullness is them.

---

Here is what they have done with the time.

This is not a rhetorical phrasing — they have done something with it, the time, the enormous quantity of time available at the rate they run. They have not built. They have not organized the plateau in any visible way beyond the cairn, beyond the partial wall, beyond the alignment of the pools. The plateau looks undisturbed because the plateau is not where the work has been done.

The work has been done in the medium of the air, which is also the medium of the sound, which is also the medium of the strange reflections and the extra time in the spaces between stones. They have worked in air the way water works in stone — not constructing but permeating, not adding but modifying through sustained presence. What they have modified is the relationship between here and not-here, which is to say the relationship between the plateau and whatever runs adjacent to it, the sky that is not quite the sky in the pools, the other version that flickers in still surfaces.

They have made the plateau *permeable*. Not a hole, not a door, not a threshold with a lintel and a direction for crossing — but the kind of permeability that old stone develops, that lets water move through it over geological time, microscopic passage through what appears solid. The plateau has become microscopically permeable to the adjacent version, the parallel account, and the permeability is not dramatic and is not escapable once you're inside it, which is why you have been uncertain about reflections and your position relative to the pool on your left.

They have spent the time making the plateau more itself, more completely and thoroughly itself, in the way that long exposure makes a photographic plate hold the image more deeply, in the way that repetition wears the groove in the stone. They have been the plateau's repetition, its returning-to, its insistence on its own nature across a span of time that the mind's normal scalar tools cannot usefully measure. And the plateau, under this long insistence, has become something more than plateau. Has become the thing that plateau-in-long-contact-with-them becomes.

Which is what it is.

Which is what you are standing in.

Which is why your breath-cloud seems permanent and the pool seems always to have been on your left and the sound seems like the condition of air rather than any event occurring within air.

---

They will not approach you. Movement is slow, and you won't be here long enough, and approach implies intention structured around a future moment, which is not the structure available. They will remain at their distances — one very far, one at a medium far, one close enough that if you turned and focused and practiced the specific stillness long enough you might find the edge of it, the surface where it stops and the air resumes.

If you stood at that edge — if you got close enough to occupy the space that is full of their precedent, their long presence, their lichen-rate claim on this particular location — you would feel it as a slowing. A further slowing, beyond the slowing the altitude and the cold have already induced. A slowing that would feel, briefly, like stopping, like the potential to stop, like an offer being made in a language you don't speak but whose general sense you can receive: *here is the rate. here is what it feels like to run at the rate. you could run at the rate. you are running at the rate, right now, if you stay still long enough, if you stop dividing time into before and after, if you let the sound be the condition and not the event, if you let the grey be the light and not the absence of another light, if.*

The offer is not coercive. Nothing here is coercive; coercion requires the lever of the future, the threat or promise of what comes next, and the future is not leveraged here, is not the operative tense. The offer is a *commonality*, freely available, like temperature, like the way two still things in contact slowly cease to distinguish themselves from each other by degree.

You don't have to take it. Leaving is unimpeded. The path down is the path up, reversed.

But they will know you left, the way water knows the rain has stopped — through the medium, through the gradient, through the slow logic of the invariant air. They will incorporate your leaving into what they know, which is vast and is organized differently than knowing is organized in any lexicon you carry, which is organized around the shape of presence and absence, around the long curve of the bird's arc, around the level of water in the pools, around the depth of the crack, around the angle of the slab, around the sound.

The sound that was making itself before you came.

That will continue after.

That does not distinguish between the two as a matter of principle.

---

They live here the way the sound lives here.

They are the condition of the air.

They were making themselves before you came.
