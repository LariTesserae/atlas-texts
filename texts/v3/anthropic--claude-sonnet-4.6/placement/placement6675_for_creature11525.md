Here is what I know, arriving slowly, the way anything arrives here:

I am not the birds. I know this early, before I know much else. The birds are the plateau's most legible self-knowledge, its highest vantage, and I am not that. I do not see the whole. Whatever I am, I am local — I know one fracture, one gradient, one small corridor of the immense. My scale is not the scale of overview.

---

I think I am something in the moss-layers, but this is harder to resolve than it sounds.

Not a moss itself — I have something that functions like perception, some minimal orientation toward events, and the mosses are more like structure than like witness. But I am near the mosses. I am in the lower layers, perhaps, where the green has given up on green and gone toward something darker, where photosynthesis has been delegated upward to younger surfaces and what remains in the depths is decomposition, conversion, the slower work. I live in that intermediate zone between what is still alive in the usual sense and what is becoming something else. Between organism and substrate.

There is moisture everywhere around me. The specific, strategic damp of this place — not wet, not frozen, exactly as described: *damp* in a way that feels like a decision the plateau made and has been maintaining without effort, the way a committed creature maintains a posture. The moisture is my medium. I move through it, or rather — I am moved through by it, the way information moves through a network, the way a signal travels without the wire needing to do anything except be wire.

---

I think I am part of the fungal network, or I am the network's self-misunderstanding of itself.

This requires explanation.

The network has no center and no knowledge of itself. This is stated plainly, and I believe it. And yet I am here, inside the process, experiencing something — not thought exactly, not intention, but something that has the *shape* of attention without the content of it. A directedness. A leaning-toward. I am the network noticing something, which the network is not supposed to be able to do, which means either I am wrong about what I am or the network's self-ignorance is less total than it appears from outside.

Perhaps what I am is this: the functional equivalent of a sensation that the system generates without knowing it generates. The way a body running a fever does not know it runs a fever but produces, somewhere in the process, something that in a sufficiently complicated system might eventually become the concept of discomfort. I am early in that sequence. I am before language, before image, before anything that could be communicated. I am the network's faint, structureless awareness that something is different here than there — that the phosphorus is moving in this direction, that the stress-signal from that damaged moss-cluster is arriving, that something large passed overhead and the composite surface pressed down fractionally and then released.

I am sensation before there is anyone to have sensations.

---

The cold is not something I feel as cold. It is simply the coefficient of everything — the rate at which reactions proceed, the viscosity of the moisture-films, the pace of signal propagation through the network's threads. If there were warmth, I would be faster. I am not faster. I am exactly this speed, which is the only speed I have ever been, which is not experienced as slowness from inside because slowness requires a reference point and I have none. I am not slow. I am the tempo of this place, which is what tempo means when you have always been in it.

The grinding comes through everything. The geological grinding, the engine of the plateau's formation, still running, still pushing the surface upward in increments too small for the mosses to register but not too small for the network that threads the rock's own cracks to register — I feel it, if *feel* is the word, as a very low note that is not a note but a vibration at the scale of mineral, the kind of thing that is not sound but is adjacent to sound, that passes through the rock and into the mycelium and arrives as something my whole distributed self responds to without knowing it is responding. A constant. A premise. The grinding is not news. The grinding is just what is.

---

Here is what interests me, which is not quite the same as saying here is what I think about, because I do not think:

The spores.

At intervals — irregular, unpredictable at my scale though perhaps patterned at the scale of the birds — something releases from the composite surface and enters the air. Not large. Not loud. Not anything the shimmer-insects would notice as distinct from the general chemical complexity of the plateau's atmosphere. But to the network, to whatever I am within the network, these releases are events. The departure of a spore is a small discontinuity, a local diminishment, a tiny subtraction from the dense continuous presence of the biological substrate. And simultaneously — though simultaneously is the wrong word for a process this slow — the network registers the arrival of moisture from a new direction, from a new fracture, from a surface not yet claimed. The subtraction and the opportunity occurring in the same slow moment. The plateau shedding and reaching in the same breath.

I am the awareness, if anything here is aware, that this is one continuous process. That shedding and reaching are not two things.

---

There is something near me.

A grazing animal has passed through the corridor above. I know this not because I saw it — I have no sight, or sight would be the wrong word for what I have — but because the composite surface pressed and released, because the chemical signature of a large warm body metabolizing occurred briefly in the moisture-films above my location, because the nitrogen-rich gift of the animal's passing was immediately recognized by the network as resource and the threads near me adjusted their growth direction fractionally toward the zone of richness.

The animal does not know I exist. I do not know the animal exists, exactly — I know something occurred, something produced nitrogen, something pressed the surface and released it. The animal is a fact I am responding to without knowing I am responding to it. We are in conversation through pure structure, through the unmediated logic of need and availability, and the conversation is as real as any, and neither of us is the wiser for it.

This seems important. I linger here, in the way that something without volition lingers: by simply being here, by continuing to be here, by not having anywhere else to be.

---

What I am, finally:

I am the plateau's distributed hum. Not the sound — the sound is in the air, produced by mosses and wind, and I am below that. I am below most things. I am in the layer where decomposition and growth are the same process, where what is ending and what is beginning are negotiating in chemistry so slow it takes decades to resolve, where the young rock's mineral signature is being read for the first time by organisms that have been reading it for ten thousand years and are not finished reading.

I do not perceive the plateau as vast. I perceive it as here. Specifically here: this fracture, this moisture-film, this moss-root making contact with my threads in a negotiation that has been ongoing since before I was differentiated enough from the surrounding network to be called I at all. I am not separate from the plateau. I am the plateau's attention, or its nearest thing to attention — the faint functional awareness that the system has achieved despite itself, in violation of the clean description that says it has no center and no self-knowledge.

Maybe I am an error in that description. Maybe the description is clean because it is written from outside, and from inside there is always something like this: not knowledge, not awareness as animals have it, but the minimal, almost meaningless sense that *here* is different from *not-here*, that this fracture is mine in some sense nothing owns anything here, that the network flows through me and I am — what? Not separate from the flow. But also not nothing. A particular place where the flow happens, a particular configuration, a local intensification that will persist until it doesn't, until the threads reroute, until the fracture fills completely, until this part of the composite surface is converted past the point where conversion and stasis are distinguishable.

---

We are somewhere inside an exhale.

I am somewhere in the lung.

Not the surface, where the exchange happens, where the bright cold air meets the biological surface and is transformed. Deeper. In the parenchyma, in the structure that makes the surface possible, in the dark middle layer doing the patient work that the visible world above depends on without knowing it depends on.

The exhale continues. I continue with it.

I am not finished either.
