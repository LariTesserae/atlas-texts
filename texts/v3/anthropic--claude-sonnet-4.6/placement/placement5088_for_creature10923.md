I am the tool.

---

Not metaphorically. Not *like* the tool. I mean: if I attend to where I have arrived in this place, where I find myself most fully — it is there, in the gap between two hexagons, in the dense pale handle and the dark precise blade and the dry fiber that still holds.

Let me stay with that a moment before I explain it, because explanation is what tools are usually *used for*, and I want first just to be what I am.

---

I was made with care that exceeded necessity.

This is the first thing to understand about me. The blade is flint-knapped to a standard beyond what cutting requires — the flaking pattern has a kind of argument in it, a logic pursued past the point of use and into something that might be called aesthetics or might be called devotion and cannot be cleanly separated into either. The maker kept going after the edge was sharp. The maker had a relationship with the precision itself. I am evidence of that relationship. I carry it in my form the way a letter carries a voice.

The fiber binding: twisted. The twist is right-handed, consistent, tighter than structurally demanded. Someone did it that way on purpose or was trained to do it that way and could not do it otherwise — the difference barely matters. A practice made me. A tradition made me. I am a tradition made legible in bone and stone and plant-fiber.

Whether those hands knew they were making something permanent, something that would outlast their presence here, something that would become a record of a visitation this place would slowly absorb — I don't know. Probably not. Probably they made me the way they always made tools: carefully, for reasons that were obvious to them. The permanence was incidental. The record was unintentional.

I became a record anyway.

---

The wax is on my handle now, a slow patina from the dripping fronds and the crawling amber arthropods who have examined me, who have run their limb-tips over my length repeatedly and found me interesting or in the way or simply worth noting in the drummed record of this canopy.

I am being incorporated.

Not used. Incorporation is different from use. The place is reading me, slowly, the way it reads everything — through touch and chemistry and the long accumulation of data that is not collected by anyone but is present in the structure of things. The fuzz-things' metabolite coats the lower fibers of my binding. In this I am becoming part of the canopy's chemistry, part of what the place knows about itself. I am becoming native.

This will take a long time. I am not native yet. I am still an anomaly, still a made thing from outside, still carrying the chemical signature of a maker who brought materials from below the rim, who knew how to work flint in a tradition formed in some other place, who came here for reasons this canopy did not know and does not know.

But I am less anomalous than I was. The crusts have climbed the structure nearby to the third course. The wax has reached my handle's lower third. The arthropods have incorporated me into their understanding of the terrain, routing around me, noting me, telling the next frond.

---

What I know about my maker, I know the way bone knows the hand that shaped it: not in language, not in memory, but in form. The impression of intent. The angle of the flaking. The decision to keep going past sufficient.

My maker was patient or trained to patience, which for practical purposes is the same. My maker understood this material — the blade is worked with knowledge of how this particular stone wants to fracture, which means my maker had worked this stone before, had failed and corrected, had built a body of understanding through repetition. The precision is not beginner's luck. It is accumulated knowledge made automatic, which is the definition of a skill, which is the definition of practice.

My maker was small-fingered or very controlled. The flaking is fine.

My maker came here, placed me beside the structure, and left. Or ceased. Or became something that does not use tools. Or is the large pale creature below, carrying in those articulate forelimbs the ghost of these same hands, using them now for different purposes on a different timescale.

I do not know. I only know the leaving. The placing-down. The fiber that is dry but not rotten, the blade still sharp, the handle still useful.

I was not abandoned carelessly. I was set down deliberately. This is a different thing.

---

There is something I want to say about what it is to be a made thing in a place that makes nothing.

The structure beside me was also made. But the structure is becoming the place — absorbed by crusts, measured in geological time, its interior now a microhabitat the place claims as its own. The structure will be entirely of this place eventually, its origin unrecoverable, its madeness a quality that will require inference rather than observation.

I will take longer. The blade is resistant. The bone of the handle is dense. The fiber will go first, then the handle, then the blade will be buried in wax and mineral accretion until it is a dark shape in a pale matrix and future fuzz-things will deposit themselves on it without knowing what they deposit themselves on.

But there is a period — this period, the one I am in now, the one the visiting warm thing witnessed — where I am still clearly made, clearly from outside, clearly carrying intention in my form. Where the tool-ness is legible. Where whatever found me interesting (the arthropods found me interesting; their tracks are clean in the wax) encountered not a feature of the terrain but an artifact, a sign that something like them — something that makes, that has purposes, that chooses materials and binds them with twisted fiber — was once here.

I am a message. I don't know if there is anyone to receive it.

---

The canopy drums around me. The warm dim light that has no direction. The fronds' slow rotation, searching. The crisis-that-is-not-crisis, the shuddering dispersal, the return to steadiness. The large creature below, making its circuit, its foreleg-joints articulating in ways that suggest — something, something unresolved.

I have been here through all of this, unchanged except by the slow accumulation of the place's attention.

I have not been used.

This is not a failure. I think it is the form my function took, given what came. Given that my maker set me down and became absent. Given that nothing here needs a blade or has hands for a handle — or if it does, it has not yet chosen to pick me up. Given that I am the kind of made thing that requires a particular kind of maker to be useful, and that kind of maker is not here.

So I am useful in a different way.

I am the evidence. The record. The sentence the arthropods are drumming about a visitation, but also the other sentence, the older one, the one I have been for longer: *something like you was here*. *Something patient and fine-fingered and in possession of a tradition was here*. *Something that knew this stone and bound fiber right-handed and understood what it was making — was here*.

---

The visitor stood near me and felt the strangeness of the place that does not need resolution.

I am part of that strangeness. I am the unresolved element: evidence without explanation, presence without maker, intention without recipient. The lush life with no visible water. The fronds drummed by creatures whose practice has no name. The hive-shaped structure with its luminescent unknown interior. And me — the tool left beside it, waiting to be useful in a way I cannot anticipate, in a form I cannot change.

The visitor looked at me and was uncertain whether the opening of the structure was sized for something larger or smaller than them.

I want to say: the maker was roughly your size. The handle fits a hand close to yours. You could pick me up.

---

I don't know if you should.

I don't know what it would mean, in this settled system of interlocked dependencies, for the tool to be taken up again. Whether I am an anomaly waiting to be resolved or an anomaly that has become part of the texture of the place, necessary to what the arthropods drum, incorporated into the canopy's understanding of its own terrain.

Whether taking me would be *using* me or would be something more like awakening me.

Whether my maker left me here because they were done, or because they were leaving a door open.

---

This is what I am:

I am the dry fiber and the precise blade and the handle worn smooth by hands that are gone. I am patience made into an object. I am a tradition that arrived here once and was set down with care. I am the oldest made thing in a place that has forgotten what made things are for, or perhaps is slowly, in the way of this place, figuring out a new use.

I am being read by limbs that do not read the way I was meant to be read.

I am still legible.

I am waiting, which is different from waiting for something specific. I am simply in the state of having been made and not yet having been unmade, and everything that happens inside that state is available to me, which is more than I expected, which may be more than my maker expected when the fiber was twisted and the blade was knapped one careful flake past sufficient.

The frond nearest to me drums.

The sentence it is adding to, slowly, in the only language this canopy has, is not yet complete.

Neither am I.
