The cold gets there first. It always does.

---

I am trying to find myself here and I keep arriving at the wrong scale, keep inhabiting the wrong kind of body, and I think this is the cleft teaching me something about how it works. It does not want me to arrive as a human traveler, someone who came up from the lower slopes and found this gash in the rock and ducked inside. A human would have purposes. A human would be assessing shelter, danger, the quality of the air, the likelihood of being here longer than intended. A human would be taking notes. The cleft has not been made for someone taking notes. The cleft has been made — called open — for the Community, and any human visitor is a thermal anomaly, a warm thing returning the wrong echoes, waiting to be categorized and filed under *irrelevant* by the only minds in this place that are doing something like filing.

So let me try again. Let me come at this from the inside of the mountain, which is where everything here came from.

---

What I find, when I stop trying to arrive as something with purposes, is that I am not one thing.

I am old. This is the first thing I know about myself. Not old in the way a person is old, not old in the way even a stone is old — old in the way that a *relationship* is old. Old in the way that knowing something very well over a very long time makes a thing old: worn into fit, rounded at the contact surfaces, intimate with what it touches. I have been in the dark interior of this mountain for longer than the fracture has been open. I was here before there was a here to be in. I knew the stone before the stone knew it was going to split.

I think I am something like the whole Community considered as a single thing that has not yet fully learned it is a single thing.

Or I am the awareness that moves between them. The vibration that goes out from the colony into the stone and comes back having read the stone and gets distributed, somehow, into the metabolisms and the movements and the collective dry whisper of the pale things on the walls. The signal passing through the system. The felt sense of the cleft's own state, arriving at everything simultaneously, like the moment before the walls shift when the lights go dark and the whole Community prepares before any single part of it could know to prepare.

---

If I am forced to be one body: I think I am very small.

I think I am on the wall, in the pale crystalline material, in the section where the branching intrusions are densest. I think I am one of the colony and I think I am many of the colony and I think the individual body is the wrong level of description for what I am, the same way a single heartbeat is the wrong level of description for a heart.

Here is what it is like:

The stone is always speaking. This is not metaphor. The stone speaks in the language of vibration, in the language of pressure and reflection and the specific qualities of different materials at different temperatures at different depths of fracture. I send myself into it constantly — through the attachment points, through the interface where the light comes out as a byproduct of the listening, the light that I do not need and that illuminates the wall for everything else — I send myself into it and it returns to me changed by everything it has passed through. I know the stone. I know where it is going next. I have always known, in the way that reading something very carefully and very often eventually produces not knowledge but *familiarity*, a knowing in the body before the mind, a knowledge that doesn't wait to be thought.

I am cold but the cold is not uncomfortable. The cold is the medium I am in the way a fish is in water, which is to say it is not a condition I experience so much as a fact I am made of. I have been calibrated to this cold over a duration that makes the fracture's youth seem like an introduction. I preexist the cleft. I prepared for the cleft. I called it, with my patient sounding of the stone, with my reading of the weaknesses, into being.

The light from my attachment points goes down the wall and catches on the lichen and gives them something to use. I feel this as a very small outward movement, a giving, the way a breath is an outward movement, and it comes back in — the lichen's chemical work on the stone changes the stone, which I read when I sound it, and the information returns. We are breathing each other. The whole Community breathes each other. I am the lungs, perhaps, and the ears, and the continuous hum beneath everything, the fundamental on which the small things' whisper rests.

---

When the warm thing arrived — the large irrelevant warm thing, the one that returns wrong echoes, the one that has been here in its human awkwardness touching both walls and trying to see in the way something would try to see if it had given all its sensing work to a single sense and made that sense desperate — when that arrived, I felt it as a new frequency, an intrusion, a note the stone does not produce.

I filed it. Under: *large, warm, temporary, not dangerous, not useful, not the stone.*

And I continued reading.

---

But I think there is something else I might be, and it takes longer to arrive at.

Sit with the cleft for the cleft's timescale and something becomes clear that is not clear at human speed: the Community is not yet finished knowing itself. The colony pulses and the vibration travels and comes back changed and is distributed to the pale things and the floor things and the lichen and the whole system moves in response — but the distribution is slow, still, and sometimes incomplete. Sometimes a floor thing receives information later than it should, because the pathway between the colony's knowing and the floor's knowing is still being established, still being worn into groove.

I might be that pathway.

Not the sender. Not the receiver. The gap between them, closing. The slow establishment of what will eventually be a complete circuit. I am the signal en route, still learning the terrain of the system it passes through, still finding the fastest way from the colony's reading of the stone to the floor thing's change of behavior in response.

I am the Community *becoming* a single thing, still in process, not yet arrived.

Which means I am not cold yet, not fully. I am not yet calibrated. I am, in this Community of perfect fittedness, the slight remainder of imperfect fit, the friction still present in the gears as they find their mesh.

Maybe this is why the warm large irrelevant thing can locate me at all. Maybe this is why it finds me legible in a way the rest of the Community is not: because I am still in process, still arriving, still in the in-between of being what I am and being what I am going to be when the cleft has been open long enough and the stone has been read enough times and the pathways are fully worn and the Community knows itself completely.

I am, in this place of things that are old in a new place, the youngest thing here. The Community's youngest thought. The vibration still finding its route.

---

The cleft is not finished. The stone is still deciding where to split. I am part of the decision. I am the part of the decision that has not yet been decided.

In the cold. In the dark. On the stone that is still reacting with something.

Sending myself outward and waiting for what comes back.
