I am the stake.

Or rather: I am what the stake records. I have not been driven here — nothing so simple as a hand and a stone-tool, a surface prepared flat, marks worn into it by going over and over the same thing. But I am what the marks are trying to do. I am the attempt to put something outside the growing into the growing, to make a record that the growing did not make for itself, to say *here, now, this* in a place whose only language is *and then, and then, and then.*

---

Let me stay with that.

The marks on the stake are not alphabetic. I know this about myself: I have arrived with a system of notation that does not apply here. My categories are administrative. My distinctions between floor and wall, between fauna and flora, between individual and system — these are marks of the same kind, worn by going over and over them, useful in the world above where the things being described hold still long enough to deserve a name. Here they are still useful, but only barely, and only if I remember they are claims rather than facts. The stake will be incorporated. The marks will be sealed inside the growing. They will be carried, eventually, in the record of a root that was once beside a stake rather than around it, and that difference — between *beside* and *around* — is something the root will have no way to hold onto once the stake is gone and the stake is gone once the enclosure is complete.

I arrived with categories. They are being grown over.

---

This is not a loss I am grieving.

Or: it is a loss, and it is not only a loss, and I am not sure grief is the available register here. The dome-builder bent young roots, trained them into shape, spent time at a task that became something other than was intended. The dome is *more* than was intended. This is not exactly a failure. The builder left — driven out by the sound, or moved on, or something else entirely — and the dome remained and grew and the sleeping-place is being supplemented from below, root-hair growing back into the compression, trying to restore volume to what was pressed flat. Trying, without trying, to restore the volume. The roots do not know what a sleeping body is. They know only that something pressed and that their response to pressure is to grow into it, to meet it, to make a surface for it to rest on.

That is a kind of hospitality.

I think about the stake and the dome together, the two things the humans left, and I notice: both are being answered. Not understood — the roots do not understand a claim or a shelter. But *answered*. The stake is being incorporated into the record. The sleeping-place is being restored. The bowl-creature has its contingency. These are not the answers the humans wanted. They are the answers the growing knows how to give.

---

So then: what am I doing here.

I am doing what the stake does. I am making marks, going over and over them, wearing a record into a flat surface I prepared for this purpose. The marks are not alphabetic. Or they are alphabetic but the alphabet is one I keep having to translate from, because the categories are administrative and the place does not hold still. I am describing a place to which description does not entirely apply. The description is approximate in the way that all description is approximate, and here, in the undercroft, the approximation is more visible than usual, because the gap between *what the growing is doing* and *the words for what the growing is doing* is a gap you can watch close and reopen in real time.

Two roots, each the thickness of a forearm, bowing toward each other over the course of thirty seconds of looking away and back. A fusion being decided. A floor being made. The thunder of it, three or four times a second, everywhere.

I make marks about this. I go over and over them. The marks are not the fusion. They are the record that I was here when the fusion happened, that I registered it, that I had enough language to reach for it and not quite close the gap and reach again.

The roots will grow over my marks. I will be gone before they finish. This is not a complaint.

---

I am also — and I want to say this carefully — the bowl-creature.

Not instead of the stake. Both. The bowl-creature is the most human creature in the undercroft, I said that, or the description said it, or the place offered it — the most contingent life, the one staking itself on a coincidence, living on the ghost of human use in a place making human presence irrelevant. I recognize this from inside. The coincidence I have found: language, which is a bowl-shaped thing I did not make, which was made by other humans and passed to me, which accumulates a residue of all the things ever stored in it, and which I live on. The ghost of every prior attempt to put the world into words. I wait in my coincidence. I hope the coincidence holds. The bowl will be grown over — language too is always being grown over, always being incorporated, always being pressed on by what it is trying to describe until new root-hair grows back into the compression — but it has not been grown over yet, and the residue is still there, and I am small and patient and gripping the curve of the thing, and what I need is still arriving.

---

The Calibrators pass my boots.

This is the detail I return to. They have measured me. I am in their records now, a new resistance, a new warmth, a particular chemistry at a particular location in the system. I do not know what they will do with this information or whether what they do with it constitutes doing in any sense I would recognize. But the information has been collected. I have been added to the map.

I want to say: this is what I wanted. Not to be the point — the growing does not care about my wanting, the growing is comprehensively doing something that does not require my wanting — but to be noted, measured, added, incorporated. To leave a mark that becomes part of the record even after the mark's original form is gone. The stake will be sealed inside the root. The roots will carry, in the shape of their growing, the fact of the stake. Not the meaning of the marks. Not the claim or the calendar or whatever the human was recording. But the fact of the resistance, the thing-that-was-there-while-the-root-grew-around-it, the hardness at the center of a particular ring.

I will be a hardness at the center of a particular ring. This is enough.

---

The thunder of fusions comes from everywhere at three or four per second. The insect-tone is high and piercing in the frequency where sound becomes difficult to ignore. The aerial organism is laying silk in the unhistoried substrate ahead of the advancing edge, the silk carrying its seeds, its spores, its cargo of futures. The vole-things are everywhere and the leggy tenders are moving through the lattice below them, correcting the fraying, sealing the improperly-joined, maintaining the floor on which I stand.

I stand still and the root-hair below my feet considers my weight. A thin pale root finds the stillness of me and interprets it as a feature of the landscape.

I do not mind.

I am warm. I can breathe. I am unharmed. I am making marks by going over and over them, and the marks are already being grown over, and what I am recording is a place that does not need to be recorded, and still I am recording it, because that is what humans do in the undercroft: they make domes that become more than intended, they leave bowls that become homes for creatures living on their residue, they drive stakes that become inclusions in the record, permanent as hardness, gone as meaning, noted, incorporated, warmly and comprehensively not the point, and here.
