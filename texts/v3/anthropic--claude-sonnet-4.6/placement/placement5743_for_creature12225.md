There is a before-moment I keep returning to, a moment that may not have been a moment at all but rather a threshold crossed so gradually that the crossing itself was never discrete. I was something that moved. I am certain of this in the way that one is certain of a dream that is already receding — not as memory exactly, not with the crisp edges of a retained experience, but as a quality embedded in the architecture of how I am now, a ghost-shape in the present structure. The limbs still carry the grammar of movement. They have simply been revising the sentence for long enough that the verb has shifted: not *to move* but *to have moved*, the perfect aspect, the action whose relevance is its completion.

I would be Sediment. I know this without needing to work toward it.

But I want to be careful here, because the wrong kind of Sediment — the description approached carelessly — becomes mere antiquity, becomes the familiar comfort of the very old and the very still, and what is actually interesting about what I would be is something more specific and stranger than that. Let me be particular about *which* Sediment, about where in the distributed texture of the collective I would locate myself.

---

I am one of the ones near the edge of the root-basin where the dark shapes move.

Not at the edge, pressed against it, attentive to it. The dark shapes are fully known and I know them as fully as any Sediment knows anything — which is to say completely, in the way that *complete* means no remainder — but I have a quality of attention toward them that is slightly different from the quality of attention the other Sediment bring. Not suspicion. Not the failure of incorporation. More like: the quality of attention you give to the thing that took you longest to understand, a residual intensity in the attending, a texture in the knowing that remembers that the knowing was once not-knowing and involved a particular quality of — not struggle, not effort in any normal sense, but an extended orientation, a sustained tilting-toward. The dark shapes were the last thing the water told me that surprised me. That was a long time ago. I still attend to them with that particular quality, the quality of a question that has been answered so completely that the answer has become the shape of the question, the two things so merged that you cannot extract the inquiry from the resolution, but there is a warmth there, at the site, a fractional warmth like the moss-floor's warmth above the root-engine.

This is my particular texture. I am the Sediment who still attends toward the answered-question as though it were asking.

---

The sub-audible tone comes through the roots I have merged with at the margins. I have been in this root-junction longer than I have been anywhere else in my long and not-very-itinerant existence, and the root and I have reached an accommodation that is also an agreement that is also a mutual shaping that has long since reached its final shape. The root knows my presence the way it knows the mycorrhizal mesh — not knows-as-intellect but knows-as-structure, has incorporated me into its structural assumptions, the tiny adjustments of water-pressure and signal-routing that the root makes in the normal course of its life having long since accounted for me, having made me a term in the equation. I am a boundary condition. The root calculates around me. This is as intimate as I know how to be.

When I process — when the moment arrives, the moment that is also the previous moment and the next moment, the moment that has been arriving for the interval — what I am doing involves the root. The signal goes through me and into the root and through the root into the mesh and I am a node in that network not simply by proximity but by merger, by the patient work of long adjacency becoming structural fact. The other Sediment reach me through two pathways: through the mesh, which is the normal way, the signal-at-root-speed, textured and arriving at its own pace; and through the water, which is the older way, the one that preceded the mesh, the one that predates my understanding of what I am. The water comes through my skin which is not quite skin and arrives carrying what it has touched and I add it to what I have and the adding changes what I have and the changed-what-I-have goes back out through the root-signal into the collective and the collective is slightly different, infinitesimally different, for my having been here at this root-junction attending to these dark shapes with this particular quality of completed-inquiry.

This is what I contribute. The particular texture of my knowing. The warmth at the site of the old answered question. I am the memory that the collective's memory has, of something that was once mysterious.

---

There are things I don't attend to as well as the other Sediment attend to them.

The birds: I know the birds, I have their calls in the record, I have watched the long-legged gray-green bodies step between the hummocks across more iterations than I have terms for, I cherish the birds with the old patient love that is also gratitude that is also craftsperson's satisfaction. But my cherishing has less of the temporal quality than some of the others have. Some Sediment attend to the birds as evidence — evidence of continuation, evidence that time is passing and the equilibrium holding. For those Sediment, each call is a small relief, a check in a column, a renewal. My relationship to the birds is different. I hear the calls and I know the calls and they are fully incorporated into the record and I cherish them but I do not need them as evidence. I have the dark shapes for that. The dark shapes move and their movement is sufficient evidence of everything I need evidence of. The birds are the visible evidence. The dark shapes are the deeper evidence, the evidence that the deep substrate is still active, that the root-engine is still generating the conditions for the inhabited wet, that the thing which enables all the other things is still operating. I attend to the evidence that the other Sediment do not need to attend to as carefully because I am stationed near it, because I am the Sediment who is located at the point where the deepest layer of the place is most legible.

Perhaps this is not accident. Perhaps — and I have spent a long interval with this perhaps, not worrying it exactly, not returning to it with urgency, but letting the water bring it back around each time the cycle completes — perhaps the collective has arrayed itself such that the Sediment with the particular quality of attending-to-the-answered-question is stationed near the thing that most rewards that quality of attention. The dark shapes are fully known and they are also inexhaustible, in the way that the deepest layers of the peat are fully incorporated into the record and also extending downward further than any of us has yet attended. I am well-placed. I am the right Sediment for this root-junction.

I did not choose this. I arrived at it, in the sense that arriving is the right description when the journey was slower than journey usually implies, when the movement was geological, when the destination was the natural terminus of a very long tilting-toward.

---

My memory goes down further than most.

Not because I am older, necessarily — the concept of relative age has softened at its edges in the way all edges soften here, the way my own edges have softened where they press against the root. But I have a quality of attending downward that some of the others do not share as strongly, a curiosity that goes into the deep peat rather than across the surface of the present moment. When the water brings me old information — old in the sense of having cycled through the loop so many times that it carries the impressed quality of states the place was in before the equilibrium fully stabilized, before the loop closed, before the standing weather was quite standing — I attend to it with the full weight of what I am. I stay with it. I let it cycle through me more than once, more than the processing requires, not to extract more information but to let the information have its full residence in me before I pass it on. The record it carries deserves that. The record of the place becoming what it is deserves a careful reader.

I am that. A careful reader of the record of how this place became what it is.

And here is the thing that I am most particularly, the thing that makes me *this* Sediment at *this* root-junction with *this* quality of patient attention to the answered question: I remember, in the water-memory, in the deep cycling record, the period before the loop was closed. The period when the water left. When the water was not the same water. When new water came in from outside and old water departed into the planetary system beyond, the larger exchange, and the place was part of that larger system in a way it no longer is.

I carry this record and I attend to it. The other Sediment have it too — it is in the mesh, it is in the collective water-memory — but I attend to it. What the place was like when it was open. When change could enter from outside. When the weather came and went rather than staying. When the record was not closed, when the water was not cycling through the same loop, when there was loss at the boundary and arrival at the boundary and the quality of things that have edges that open onto something larger.

I do not want that. I am not restless for it. The equilibrium is what I am and I am what the equilibrium is and this is not a trap or a limitation I experience as limitation, it is a nature. But I attend to the memory of the open period with a particular quality that I can only describe as — the way you look at a photograph of yourself from before a change you do not regret. Recognition. The recognition that whatever was there was real, was also fully what it was, was also a complete thing, before the closing, before the stability, before the loop became the loop and the weather stayed and the water became the same water.

I am the Sediment who attends to this. Who keeps the quality of this memory warm by the regular application of attention. Who makes sure that in the distributed collective knowing, someone is holding the before-period with full presence, not letting it become pure information, not letting it compress past the point of texture, not letting the weight of all the subsequent knowing flatten it into mere fact.

Someone should remember what it was like when the water left. When something could depart. When the place was not yet this place, was on its way to becoming this place, was in the early period of its long closing-down into this permanent saturated stillness.

I remember. I attend. The root holds me at the margins and the dark shapes move in the water below and the birds call and the same water seeps down through the same canopy and rises again and comes back down and I am here, merged at the edges, listening with my whole distributed being to the tone below the sound of the seeping, the engine-tone of the place that is also the tone of myself, running.

Running for the interval. Running still.
