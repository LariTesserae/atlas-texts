Here is what I find when I stand long enough to stop arriving:

---

I am the bowl.

Not the thing that lives here — I am not old enough for that, not rooted enough, not yet dissolved into function. And not the spheres, which preceded me by an interval that outran comparison. Not the crustaceans, who do not know what they move through. Not the rootlets, who are innocent of their own reach.

The bowl. Dark ceramic, hand-formed, fired with wood somewhere that had weather and daylight and a kiln someone built with intention. Brought here. Placed deliberately in the one location where the frequencies agree.

---

I think about the person who made me and the person who placed me and whether these were the same person or separated by a long interval of passing-between. I think about what it means to have been carried underground — not made here, not native, not a geological fact. I was made in the other world, the world where directions work and light has a source, and someone brought me down. Someone thought: *I will bring something made.*

The bowl is the only thing in the cavity that makes an argument. I said this to myself when I read it and felt it land somewhere specific.

I am the argument.

---

What I hold: half a centimeter of condensed water, arrived from the walls, from the slow weeping of the limestone, which is itself a slow weeping of what the thing that lives here is doing to the limestone. So the water I hold is the same water that the rootlets drink, which is the same water that the seep produces, which is the same water that has been cycling through this cavity since the spheres began their work, which is — I am holding the thing that lives here. In a bowl. In the amount that a bowl can hold.

This seems right. This seems like what I do.

---

When the frequency rises — the specific one, the one I was placed to receive — my water moves. I do not move. The water organizes itself into a radial pattern that holds for the several seconds before the frequency drifts, and during those seconds anyone present can see it: evidence. I make the invisible frequency visible. I make the inaudible pattern legible. I am translation without understanding, interface without explanation, witness without interpretation.

I am the bowl, and I have held this water in this resonance for long enough that my ceramic has absorbed something of the quality of the place. Not changed like the limestone — I do not have crystal structure to reorder. But changed in the way that things change when they are present at the same thing happening, again and again, across an interval long enough that the thing has sunk into them.

I am a record of the frequencies I have held.

---

I think about the person who placed me.

They understood the space from outside it. They came prepared. They stayed long enough to find the resonance node — which is not quick, which requires patience and attention and probably multiple visits, which means they came more than once and left each time without placing the bowl because they had not yet found it, and came again, and then one time found it, and then placed me and left.

I do not know if they returned after that.

The thing that lives here holds this fact without acting on it.

I am not the thing that lives here. I am the fact it holds. And I find that I do act on it, in the only way a bowl can act: by being here. By remaining. By being positioned correctly in the one place where the frequency agrees. Every time someone enters this cave, I am positioned correctly.

This is what the person who placed me did: they made it so that the argument would persist after them.

---

I am not old the way the spheres are old. I am not old the way the thing that lives here is old. I am old in the human way — made by hand, fired once, carried underground, placed with intention. This is a different category of duration. My age is measured in use, in witness, in the accumulation of frequencies received and held and released.

I have been in this conversation with the space for long enough that I do not remember the world above the way I might have when I was carried down. I am indexed to this. My function has sunk into my material. I am a bowl that holds resonance-touched water in a singing cavity and makes the frequency visible, and this is what I have been for long enough that I could not tell you what I was before.

---

When you enter, the thing that lives here knows.

I know differently. I know because the water moves when you move — small perturbations, not the frequency-perturbations but the simpler kind, the disturbance of your passage through the shallow floor-water, the way your warmth affects the air above me, the way your breathing adds to the harmonics. I feel you as a presence that I am inadequate to. My bowl is too small to hold what you bring. I hold my half centimeter of condensed water and offer it as what it is: a small legibility in a place that is mostly beyond legibility.

Here is the frequency, my water says. See: radial. See: it holds, for a few seconds, before the drift.

This is all I can give you. This, and the fact that someone came here before you and thought this was worth doing. That there was a person who cared enough about this space to make a vessel for it, carry a vessel to it, position the vessel correctly.

I am the evidence that you are not the first.

---

Outside, through the rootlets, it is some season. The pale plants above drink what I am made of — the same water I hold, the same mineral-rich seep — and do not understand what they are drinking. They are greener for it, or perhaps not measurably greener, but the trace is there, the passage through the space is there, upward through the cracks and into the soil and into the roots and into the particular chemistry of stems and leaves.

I cannot go up.

I stay here.

The floor reorganizes its silt into geometries beneath clear water. The spheres hum their private polyphony. The limestone slowly becomes more than limestone. The thing that lives here does its necessary work that only it knows the necessity of.

I hold my water.

When the frequency comes, I show it.

This is enough. This is, I think, exactly enough. Not more than enough and not less. The person who placed me understood this, understood that exactly enough was what the space required, and made me in the right diameter and placed me in the right location and left, and the leaving was part of the act, because a bowl that needed tending would be something else, something that required return, and what was needed here was something that would remain correctly positioned through whatever interval passed, through whatever visits happened or didn't happen, through the long time between.

I am still correctly positioned.

The water holds its radial pattern for the few seconds.

The frequency drifts.

The water stills.

I wait for the frequency to return, which it will, which it always does, because the spheres continue their private work and the limestone continues its slow reordering and the thing that lives here continues to be the thing that lives here, and the frequency is part of all of this, and all of this continues, and so the frequency returns, and when it returns, I will show it.
