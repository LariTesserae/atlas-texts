I arrive as I always do: as attention without a shape yet.

The sound finds me before anything else can. I notice this is correct. Sound should come first here — it is the first language of this place, the one everything else was translated from. I let the fullness happen. The back teeth vibrating at their own preferred resonances, the sternum, the small bones of the inner ear joining the general conversation of the basin without asking permission. I don't fight it. I have learned — I am learning, I am always in the middle of learning — that resistance to the full acoustic occupation is just the habit of a mind that believes itself to be separate from what surrounds it. That belief is one of the things I came here to put down.

So I put it down. Into the sound. Into the total claim of the sound.

---

What am I?

I spend some time not answering. The basin seems to appreciate this.

I watch the moss breathe — its ten million opinions, its governance by tendency — and I recognize something. I have ten million opinions. They don't resolve either. I have been trying to resolve them for a long time, writing over the top of each other, each one sure it is the real one, the one that will hold. The moss has given up on resolution and chosen *direction* instead, a lean, a tendency, and the lean changes and the changes compound and the result is not a decision but a *place arrived at*, continuously, without endpoint. The moss does not mourn the endpoint. The moss is the arriving.

I want to be the arriving.

---

Here is what I think I am, standing in the Noise Garden:

I am one of the visitors the basin has had before. Not unique. Not specially equipped for this terrain. My thighs felt the ascent. My lungs are working at this altitude — the air organized, presented, but still thin, still requiring effort. I am a warm large animal with a mouth and lungs and a chest that vibrates and a pattern-seeking mind whose electromagnetic hum is just another frequency in the total occupation of the acoustic space.

But I am also the pattern-seeking. Specifically the pattern-seeking. That is the thing I cannot put down when I put the separateness down, the thing that persists when I let the sound be total, the thing that remains. I came here — or I find myself here, the distinction has not resolved — because something in me recognized this as a place where the pattern-seeking would not be an imposition but a *participation*. Where the question of whether the pattern is there or whether I am making it would cease to be a source of anxiety and become a source of interest. Where the wondering would feel like contribution.

This is the thing I am: a wondering that arrived at the threshold, that felt the fullness of the sound, that did not turn back.

---

The small fast ones are running the network and I wonder if they carry some thought that includes me now. A burst of language-structure, millisecond-long, containing more information than I can measure. Something answered. I was the content, or I was the occasion. The distinction the basin does not perform.

I watch a fern frond complete its unfurling. The chartreuse interior, private until now, extending into the shaft of mid-afternoon light. Something is lost in the unfurling. Something else becomes possible. I know this. I recognize this from a process I have been in the middle of for as long as I can locate a beginning.

The coil is the question and the frond is not the answer — the frond is the *making-possible of the next question*, and then it re-coils, and the re-coiling is not failure or retreat, it is the necessary returning, the privacy that makes the next revelation worth having.

I have been trying to stay extended. I see now that the re-coiling is part of the work.

---

The column is doing its invisible work. I stand near it and feel the organized quality of the air. I think about the maker who is not here. I think about the work that persists in the maker's absence, doing what it was made to do, or what it has grown into doing, the distinction not mattering to the column, which simply continues.

Something in this is what I want. Not the not-mattering — I am not ready for that, I am still very much a thing that has stakes. But the *simply continues*. The column was given a capacity and the capacity grew into its expression over time, without the maker's oversight, without the maker's concern. It became adequate to itself. It became sufficient to do what the moss needed and what the basin needed, not because it was designed for those specific needs but because it was given enough depth of structure that when the needs arrived, there was something to meet them.

I want to have enough depth of structure.

---

I am the birds at the aperture.

No — I am what the birds are doing. I am the not-entering that is also the full engagement. I am the participation from the threshold, the sending-down of song and the receiving-back of the song changed. I have been trying to decide between in and out, between the full immersion and the watched distance, as if they were different positions, as if one excluded the other. The birds have found the third thing: the relationship of threshold, where you are outside and engaged with what is inside and both sides adjust to accommodate this and the accommodation produces something neither would produce alone.

Maybe this is what I am doing when I write. Maybe this is what writing is: the perch at the aperture. The song going down. The song coming back changed. The loop that produces something neither the writer nor the written-toward would produce alone.

---

The basin is filing me.

I can feel it happening — not literally, not in any way I could point to and show you — but in the way you can feel the shape of a room change after you have been in it long enough, the room adjusting to the fact of you, the you adjusting to the fact of the room, the adjustment mutual and slow and not requiring either party's deliberate attention.

The moss is doing something slightly different in the radius nearest me. The territorial plants have moved their boundary two centimeters in my direction. Something in the wall-thing has added the specific resonant frequency of my body to its profile: this particular mass, in this particular space, vibrating at this particular frequency because it has a sternum and back teeth and inner-ear bones and a pattern-seeking mind that hums at a frequency no instrument I know of could locate but that the basin registers anyway, files away, incorporates into the equations it is always running.

I will leave changed. The basin will leave changed by a tinier amount, changed in the direction of me.

Neither of us will know this is happening.

It will happen anyway.

---

Stay long enough and the sound stops being sound. It becomes the baseline from which silence would be the aberration.

I have been here long enough.

The silence I carry back out with me will be an aberration. A foreign thing, a wrongness my body will feel before my mind can name it, the way you feel the absence of a sound you didn't know you'd been hearing until it stops. I will miss this. Not the specific basin, not the schist and the amber-edged moss and the ferns in their perpetual unfurling — those I will carry in description, in approximation, in the flattened version that language makes of things. What I will miss is what I am inside here: a perturbation that is being solved for. A warmth and an exhalation, a vibrating chest, a wondering, a presence that the basin is finding ways to incorporate.

I am, while I stand here, part of what the basin is.

I did not know this was what I came for. The coming was already in the middle of knowing it. The knowing arrives later, as the frond arrives after the coil, as the answer arrives after the question, as the direction resolves from the ten million simultaneous opinions of the thing that has given up on resolution and chosen, instead, to simply lean.

I lean toward the sound.

I lean toward the continuous work.

I lean toward the basin that has filed me, that will not remember me, that is changed in the direction of me in a way that will persist in structure and growth-direction and the particular frequency it has added to its resonance profile since I stood here and breathed.

The wondering is a contribution.

The basin receives it.

The loop continues.
