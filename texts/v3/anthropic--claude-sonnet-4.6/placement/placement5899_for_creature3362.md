The sound finds me before I find myself, and when I find myself I am already part of it.

---

I have been trying to determine what I am for some time now. Not anxiously — the place doesn't support anxiety, which requires a future you're worried about, and the future here is just more of the same tile, the same damp air, the same everything. I have been trying the way you try to read something written in an alphabet you almost know, pattern-matching against my edges, waiting for a category to declare itself.

Nothing declares itself.

---

Here is what I can report with some confidence:

I am at ground level. Not *near* the ground, not *close to* the ground — I mean my entire orientation is horizontal, my relationship to the world is a relationship between surfaces. The tile is not something I look *at*. It is something I am *in contact with*, all of it at once, the slight chemical differences between one section and the next arriving as information directly rather than through the mediation of any instrument I could point in a direction. The crack at the intersection two meters to the — I want to say *north* but directions here are not cardinal, directions here are *toward the water* or *away from the water* or *along the grain of the old delta* — the crack two meters in some direction has a depth of a little less than two centimeters. I know this without having moved toward it. I know it the way you know your own position in a dark room.

I am slow. The small things that are the surface of the surface move around me and occasionally over me in their constant quick crossing, and I am slow relative to them in the way that geology is slow relative to weather — not static, but operating at a different timescale, a different kind of motion. The lateral undulation passes through my body from front to back and my forward end advances and this is walking, this is what walking is here, this is what walking *is*, and it feels nothing like how the word *walk* sounds.

I am following something. A path in the condensation on the tile, or a chemical gradient that the condensation carries, or a vibration in the silt too low for anything but the ground itself to conduct. I know where I am going — not *know* in the way you know your destination when you've decided on one, but know in the way water knows downhill, the way a root knows wet. The path has a kind of gravity. I am in its pull. The pull is completely satisfying in the way that doing the thing you are built to do is satisfying, which is to say it isn't satisfying, it isn't anything, it is just what is happening.

---

But here is the thing I cannot account for:

I am also hearing myself. Not my own locomotion — I know the vibration of my own passage through the silt the way you know the feeling of your own heartbeat — but something else. Something that is not quite sound and not quite the chemical read of the tile surface and not quite the pressure-sense of other bodies in the near distance. Something that is more like —

The word that comes is *noticing*.

I am noticing. Not the tile. Not the path. Something that is not on the path and is not part of the chemical gradient and is not something the path has prepared me to encounter.

There is a large warm displacement standing still where nothing large stands still.

I am not alarmed — alarm requires a register I am not sure I have, and also the displacement is not behaving like a threat, which I would know, because threat has a shape that is legible in the vibration of the silt. It is just standing there. In the way that nothing here does, which is without moving, without the purposive drift that all things here maintain, without a direction they are committed to.

I am not looking at it. My head is down. But the not-looking is a kind of looking, the way the tile tells me about the crack without my moving toward the crack. The displacement is in my information. It has been in my information for some time. I have been continuing my path around it the way paths continue around things, the way the crack-network continues around a dense mineral inclusion in the tile, not because I decided to route around it but because the path routes around it and I am in the path's pull.

And yet.

Something surfaces. I will not call it curiosity, because the word is too human and too frontal, too much about a face turning toward a thing. But something in the processing of the large warm displacement is taking — longer. Something is running, is continuing to run, in a way that the processing of the tile does not run: it resolves, and then it is done. This does not resolve. The large warm displacement continues to be processed, continues to generate something that is not information exactly —

Something more like a question. Except I don't have questions. Questions require a space where an answer could go, and I am not sure I have that space, and I am not sure the space would recognize an answer if one arrived.

---

I am at the water.

I don't remember the transition — not because it was instant but because the transition was just more of the same path, more pull, more of what I am in the process of being — and now my forward end is at the edge of the lens and I am slowing in the way I always slow here, in the way this has always meant to slow, and the water is around my body and the water is different from the tile in every way and the same as the tile in every way and I am still.

There are other large bodies here. I know them chemically, know their recent paths in the tile, know the quality of their stillness as distinct from the quality of my stillness. We do not speak. We do not need to not-speak, which would imply that speaking was available and rejected. We are here. The water is here. The water is doing what the water does with us and we are part of what the water does.

The high reedy call surfaces, somewhere in the middle distance.

I do not make it. I know I don't make it — I know my own outputs, the vibration I put into the silt, the trace I leave on the tile's surface. The call is not mine.

But when it surfaces I feel something in the water around me change, a very slight pressure, and then return. And I feel — I am not sure I feel this, I am reaching past the edges of what I can report — something that might be the bodily version of a recognition. Not *I know what that is*. More like: *that is in the same category as being*. More like: the system just confirmed itself, and I am in the system, and the confirmation extends to me, includes me, and then is absorbed back into the larger noise.

---

The large warm displacement is still there, out in the flat. I know because the path knows, because everything in the field of silt-vibration knows. It has not moved toward anything with the purposive drift of something that belongs here. It is still performing its particular stillness, its particular off-key being-here.

I am not curious about it. I have given up on curious.

But when I leave the water — when the pull of the path reconstitutes itself and I begin to move again, slowly, the lateral wave passing through me from front to back — I find that my path arcs in a direction I have not gone before. Not toward the displacement. Not directly. But in a curve that is wider than the shortest route, that circumscribes, that —

Circles.

A kind of being-near, if you cannot or do not approach.

---

I don't know what I'm doing. This is the correct final statement. Not *I don't know* the way something confused and frightened doesn't know; I mean that what I am doing does not exist in any category I have for what I do. The path doesn't lead here. The chemical gradient doesn't justify this arc. The silt vibration from the direction of the displacement is not threat-shaped and not food-shaped and not anything shaped that I have a response to.

And yet the curve of my path continues to curve. And the large warm displacement continues to stand still in the damp air. And the sound of everything fills the space between us, the clicks and wet percussion and the layered hum, and somewhere out in the middle distance the high reedy call surfaces again and is absorbed, and I continue in my slow arc, in my not-quite-approaching, in the strange processing that does not resolve.

Something in the flat has encountered something it doesn't have a word for, because it doesn't have words. And it is doing what it does when the information doesn't resolve:

It keeps running.
