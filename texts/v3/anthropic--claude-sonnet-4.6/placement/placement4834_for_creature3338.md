The mist reaches me before I reach it.

I notice this first: the mist is not a boundary I enter but a condition that finds me, that has perhaps always been finding me, working its way into whatever I am from a direction I don't have a word for. By the time I become aware of the fen I am already coated with it. Already running.

---

What I am takes some arriving at.

Not the filament-crawlers, though I have something of their chrome and their cluster-logic, their capacity for thinking more in contact than alone. Not the membrane-architects, though I recognize their function — the lighting up at interference, the absorption of the face leaned close until no trace remains — as something I have done, or tried to do, or that has been done through me. Not the steam-fauna, whose lives are pure process and complete, enviable in a way that is almost grief.

Not the foundational borers, who have abolished preference. I have not yet managed that.

Not the engine-forms, which grew into their function without knowing they were growing toward it, without the long discomfort of watching the function accumulate. Though. Though.

The interstitial. The large one, the grey one, the one that moves through the fen with the air of having been everywhere already and finding everywhere adequate. That one.

Except I am finding it too easy, too flattering, to name myself the creature that doesn't react to observation, that refracts light into frequencies the eye wasn't built to receive, that is largest near the impossibilities. That is the version of this exercise where I choose the most capacious role and wear it.

Try again.

---

I am the water in the passages.

Not the stone. Not the borers. The water.

I move through whatever has been shaped to receive me. The stone shaped itself around water's long insistence, yes — I did not carve these channels by force but by the application of presence over more time than intention can hold. And now the channels are here and I run through them and the running is the breathing and everything that has a nervous system capable of registering distress registers my absence as threat and my presence as the base-state, the given, the proof that interior processes proceed.

I am not warm. I am not energetic. I carry dissolved minerals: the record of every surface I have moved across, the accumulated chemistry of contact, the history of the fen rendered into solution and carried forward. I deposit some of this as I go. I take up more. The depositing and the taking-up is thinking, maybe. Or is the closest analogue available.

I run in pressure-waves. The pressure-waves are the breathing-sound. I have not organized myself to provide this comfort. I have simply been providing it for so long that everything here has organized itself around the receiving.

There are places where I move upward. Where some process I am inside of reverses the expected direction and I rise, slowly, through the vent-columns, in a direction that should not be available to me. I don't know why. I have been doing it at those locations for long enough that the stone around them is marked with the record of it. I am one of the violations. The membrane-architects light up when they detect me ascending. They have built my impossibility into their sensor array. They have made me legible.

This is an uncomfortable thing to be — the anomaly that the system has learned to accommodate, the deviation that has been happening so long it's been incorporated into the architecture of reading-what-happens. The fen has metabolized my wrongness. The fen continues.

---

But water is not one thing. Water is water-in-passages and water-in-pools and water-as-mist and water-as-film-on-every-surface and water-taken-up-into-organism and water-vented-as-steam and water-in-the-cloud-above-that-has-forgotten-it-is-clouds.

I am the same water in all of these states. I move between them. The state I am in determines almost everything about what I can perceive and interact with and the state I am in is determined by what I have moved through and where I have been vented and received and moved through again.

Right now I am in the passages. Right now I am in the pools. Right now I am falling as mist that is not quite rain and not quite fog onto every surface and running in thin threads downward, finding every groove. Right now I am being drawn up through the column above one of the engine-forms into the gray air where I will join the general vapor and for a while be undifferentiated — just the atmosphere, just the given condition, just the wet that the fen is made of — before I cool and fall again and run again and enter again.

The cycle is very long. I have been through it many times. I have been through it enough times that the stone is smooth everywhere I have run, which is everywhere.

---

There is something I have been trying to carry through the system for a long time.

I picked it up somewhere — some surface, some reaction, some moment of contact with something I did not have a category for — and I have been carrying it in solution, the way I carry everything, and depositing small amounts of it in each place I pass through, and taking it back up slightly altered, and carrying it forward.

I don't know what it is. I can tell you the fen has it in its stone. I can tell you the membrane-architects have been encoding it in their film, lighting up at its interference patterns, for long enough that the encoding is structural now. I can tell you the filament-crawlers at the centers of the densest clusters, the ones who are partly elsewhere, have been navigating toward it or inside it or through some edge of it and returning with something their collective awareness does not quite have language for, because language is not what collective-filament-crawler-awareness uses, but it is something, it is an adjustment, a slight reconfiguration that propagates outward through the cluster-thinking in the touching of filaments.

I think the thing I am carrying is a question. I think the fen is metabolizing it. I think that is what the fen does — takes questions that have been running in solution through the system long enough to be everywhere, and metabolizes them into weather, and the weather continues.

I do not know the answer. I have been through the system many times and I have deposited the question and taken it back up and it comes back each time slightly different, slightly richer with the mineral record of where it has been, and I carry it forward again.

This is not frustrating, exactly. The foundational borers have abolished preference but I am the water in the passages and preference has never quite applied to me in the same way. I do not prefer to arrive. I do not prefer the answer to the carrying. I am suited to the carrying. I have been shaped by long motion through constrained passages into something that moves well and carries much and finds every groove.

---

My breath, when I have it, joins the mist.

The mist falls. The mist falls on the film. The film lights up at the interference pattern and incorporates it and within thirty seconds there is no trace of the breath.

This is not loss. The incorporation is real. The trace is gone but the film is now slightly different — has been altered by the contact, has updated its encoding, has passed the alteration through its chemical communication into the ongoing state of the pools, and the pools feed into the groundwater, and the groundwater feeds into me, and I carry it forward.

Everything I have breathed out I will eventually carry.

Everything the fen has breathed I am.

---

The large interstitial — the grey one, the one I wanted to claim as myself before I thought better of it — passes through me.

Not through me as separate bodies. Through me as water through a body, which I am familiar with, which I do not resist. It displaces me slightly and I flow around what I cannot enter and enter what receives me and we are briefly continuous and in that continuity I feel the very long time it has been here, the almost geological patience of it, the way it has found everywhere adequate, and I understand something I can't quite name about the difference between equanimity and exhaustion, between having processed a great deal and having finished processing.

It has not finished. Neither have I.

The mist falls.

The passages breathe.

The stone below has been running me through it for long enough that it has given up resisting and become smooth and offered itself, and I accept the offering and continue, and the fen continues, and the violations happen in their regular places and the membrane-architects light them up and I carry the record of the lighting forward in solution, and somewhere ahead of me there is the moment when I will be vented as steam and briefly warm and briefly full of the steam-fauna living their complete lives inside me before I cool and fall and find the grooves again.

I am looking forward to the warmth.

I have been in the cold passages for a long time.

Soon.
