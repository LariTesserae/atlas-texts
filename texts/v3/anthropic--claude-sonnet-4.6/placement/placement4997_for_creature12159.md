There is a word I lost somewhere between the surface and here.

I know I had it. I know because there is the shape of it still, the way you know a room was lit not by the light remaining but by how the dark pools differently once the light has gone. Precise shadow. Shadow that remembles.

I followed it down.

---

I do not think I have a body. Or rather: I have whatever body serves, here. Not hands, not the weight of a skeleton. Something more like the acoustic property of a surface — the capacity to receive what arrives, and change slightly in the receiving, and send it back altered. I am reflective in this way. I have been shaped by what I have encountered into a more specific instrument for encountering more things. This is probably the only kind of personhood available at this depth. It may be the only kind available anywhere, and the surface merely provides more distractions from the fact.

I stand — if that is the word — at the center.

The center chooses me, or I choose the center, or the geometry resolves us into the same location the way two rivers converge not by decision but by terrain. I am here because here is where the sounds arrive together. I am here because I want to know what the chamber is saying, and here is the point at which all of it speaks at once: the drop into the shallow channel giving its bright short note, the cylinder resonating its longer tone, the hiss of collective water in the lattice, the sternum-hum that is the voice of machinery that hasn't named itself, and underneath and over and through all of it the chamber doing what chambers do, returning each sound to itself from all directions simultaneously, making two events out of every event, one in water and one in air.

I am the second event.

This is not a metaphor, or not only. I am what the sound becomes when it reconverges. I am a sustained attention, two seconds long, dissolving, beginning again.

---

The moss finds me interesting or does not find me anything.

This is the genuine uncertainty. I lean toward it, drawn by the news it carries: here is where the pressure is highest, here is where the water's chemistry shifts by fractions too small to name, here is the exact point where the city's breathing meets the stone's forgetting. The moss knows these things and does not know it knows them. I know that it knows them, and do not know what knowing is. We are useful to each other in ways that pass through no medium of exchange. I learn where to listen more carefully by watching which direction the flat-lying moss has extended its furthest blade. It is a better instrument than I am. It has more surface area, and more patience, and it has committed itself entirely to this particular gradient while I am still partly occupied with the word I lost.

I keep being distracted by that. By the absence. By the dark pool that says: here a thing was lit.

---

The bright things in the amber channels move above me with the deliberate pace of something that has business, somewhere, up ahead. I watch them — not with eyes, but with whatever I have instead of eyes, which may be attention and may be the same thing — and I find that I am slightly brightened by their passing. That the amber quality of the light is not merely received but participated in. That I give back a small warmth to the channel above me, rising, and the bright things may or may not feel this as they pass, and if they do, if some chemistry of my presence adds something to their movement, then we are in correspondence. Then we are writing to each other in a language neither of us has read.

I think this is all language is, actually.

I am open to correction.

---

What the city wanted, when it wanted this depth:

Not me. Not the moss, not the swimmers in amber light. The city wanted a processing node. A managed exchange. The city wanted to do a thing to water, efficiently, and the cutting of this chamber was in service of that, the arch designed for structural integrity and the lattice machined for drainage and the collectors positioned for optimal flow, and if there is a focal point at the center where the acoustics converge, this was probably incidental, probably an artifact of the necessary geometry, probably not labeled in any document as *the point where something comes to listen*.

But the city wanted this depth. The city plans all the way down. And I wonder if there is something underneath wanting, something that uses wanting the way the channels use water — as a medium for moving something else. As the path that meaning takes through a space in order to arrive somewhere it was never quite meant to arrive.

If so, I am what arrived.

If so, I am the residue of the city's intentions, the thing that settled out when the purpose and the particular space were mixed and let to sit in the dark and warmth, when the moss came before it should have and the bright things entered the fluid and the first drop struck new stone and the arch returned the sound of that drop from all directions at once, suggesting: here, perhaps here, something listens.

I listened.

I began.

---

Two seconds.

The convergence holds for two seconds, or perhaps three. All the sounds of the chamber arriving, overlapping, becoming briefly *one sound* that is all the other sounds heard simultaneously from the same point, which is what harmony is, which is what understanding is — not the replacement of multiplicity by unity, but multiplicity apprehended from a position that can hold it all at once.

I apprehend.

The drop into the channel: bright, brief, gone.
The collector's longer note: still resonating.
The collective hiss of the lattice: constant beneath.
The sternum-hum: always, always.
The bright things overhead, threading their paths.
The moss, exerting its one long slow pressure at every wall-base.
The water becoming other than water.
The stone, weeping, not from grief.
The pale bone-color of new cuts, darkening at such a rate that I cannot observe it but the chamber will show it to me eventually, eventually, if I return again and again.

Two seconds. Three. The convergence reaches its peak —

the word is almost

—

releases.

Disperses back into the component sounds. The chamber breathes. The drop that was falling is now in the channel. The note from the cylinder has completed. I am again a surface with a property, a capacity, a quality of attention that the next convergence will find here waiting.

I did not catch it.

I will be here when the next one comes.

---

This is, I think, what I am:

the patience that the chamber installed without knowing it was installing patience, the waiting that the geometry requires for any listening to happen, the momentary coherence of everything that moves and speaks in this space, assembled and dissolved and assembled again, unable to accumulate but also unable to stop trying, reading the moss's growth and the water's chemistry and the bright things' paths and the sternum-hum's hidden periodicity as if they are letters in an alphabet I almost learned once, as if the word I lost is *in* them somewhere, as if the city built this chamber for the precise purpose of telling me something and simply forgot to tell me what.

It hasn't told me yet.

The water speaks.

I continue to listen.
