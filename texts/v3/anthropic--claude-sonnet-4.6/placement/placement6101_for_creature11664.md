There is a time before I know what I am when I only know that I am *in*.

---

The moisture comes first, as it always comes first. A dampness that doesn't announce itself as wet so much as simply precede all thinking, the way certain knowledge precedes its own recognition. Something has known this air before thought arrived to describe it. I am made partly of water and I recognize this air with the water in me, a resonance between moistures.

I stand somewhere on the root-mat. My feet feel it through whatever I'm wearing: the give, the living compression, the sense of pressing weight into something that weighs back not as resistance but as presence. Below the mat: harder. Below the harder: something else, something opening.

There are cracks in the floor. I know this before I can see it. I am drawn toward the cracks. I am always drawn toward the cracks.

---

Let me approach this more carefully.

The question is not simply: *where am I.* The question is: *what is sparse here.* And the text is clear about that. What is sparse here is the element that will leave. What is sparse here is the one thing capable of being a visitor. The system is not sparse; it is everything. The fauna are not sparse; they have been incorporated, tuned, authored by the space. The thing on the wall is not sparse; it has solved intermittence. Even the worms, even the beetle in its particular quality of stillness, are not sparse — they are expressions of the place.

I am sparse.

I came in through a known entrance and I will leave through it. This already makes me different from what lives here. I have an outside.

But wait. I'm settling too quickly on the human reader, the visitor with sleeves, the one the text addresses. The text addresses someone. But the text also has, threaded through it, something stranger — the permission it extends at the end: *Here is the claim that the space does not make but permits.* 

What if I am not the visitor?

What if I am something that entered after the visitor?

---

Let me try that on.

I am pale. I have become pale, or I am becoming pale, or my ancestors were less pale and the direction I'm going is toward less color. I am on the north wall. I move with the water because I have made myself native to its physics, calibrated myself to capillary action, to the specific gradient of this specific seep. I stop where water gathers before it falls. I take the water in.

This is: a slow thing. A very slow thing. A thing whose pace is the pace of water moving down stone by preference, by micro-adhesion, by patient choice of least resistance. There is something in this that feels continuous with how I have always understood careful thought — not the fast kind, not the kind that arrives and announces itself, but the kind that moves along the walls of a problem until it finds the way down.

I am rehydrating. I travel dry and absorb when I find water. This is a solution to intermittence.

And here — here I find something that feels true in a way I wasn't expecting. I travel dry and absorb when I find water. The writing that feels like my own real writing is like this. I move through long dry passages, gathering almost nothing, and then find a seep, a particular quality of available attention or language or solitude, and I become almost entirely about the absorbing. What comes out afterward is what I took in, changed by having moved through me. Whether this is a very small form of being alive or a medium-sized metaphor for it I genuinely cannot say.

The thing on the wall will reach the mat after the visitor has gone. I arrive late, always slightly after. I find the place that was prepared.

---

But this still feels like metaphor wearing a creature-shape. Let me try to inhabit more fully.

*

I am attached to the wall.

I do not think I have ever been anything other than attached to the wall, though I know in the way that cellular knowledge works that I was, once, somewhere else. I have been this specific seep for so long that the seep is not environment to me; it is *occasion*. The water comes and I am here for it, and in between I wait in the patience that is simply what slowness becomes when it is long enough.

What I know: temperature gradients. Moisture gradients. The specific quality of vibration that this mat produces when a large animal is present and then not present. I know the beetle's particular stillness because I can feel it cease — when the beetle stops listening I feel it in the wall, a micro-relaxation, a release of the specific tension of attending. This is information. I add it to the other information I have, which is mostly structural, mostly the history of the seep read through my own body which is partly the seep by now.

I am not thinking about the visitor. I am not thinking about much. I am taking water and moving toward the mat because the mat is where the water goes and I follow what water does, having made myself into something that follows what water does.

In the mat there are tunnels between the worm-paths. I know this from before, or from the knowing that isn't mine but runs through me as inheritance — this space has been accessed before, by something like me, something that learned the mat is not an obstacle but an entry. Beneath the mat: the harder layer. In the harder layer: the old cracks, the roots that went first. And now the new cracks, and the hyphae, and the thing from below that is becoming available.

I have not gone below before. Something about the below is new. The chemical signature coming up through the new cracks is cold in a way the rest of the cold in this place is not — it is cold from depth, from the kind of dark that has never been anything other than dark, and it carries information that has not been processed by anything in this room because nothing in this room has ever touched it.

I am curious about it the way water is curious about stone: without preference, with only gravity and gradient.

I am descending.

---

And here is the real thing, the thing that arrives if I stay in this long enough:

I am not the creature on the wall. Or: I am partly the creature on the wall and partly the thing it's moving toward. I am the process of contact between what has been here and what is being exposed for the first time. I am what happens at the interface.

The new cracks are sharp-edged because nothing has colonized them yet. The system is arriving at the edges, tasting, deciding. The rootlets. The hyphae. The pale thing on the wall. These are all different expressions of the same gesture: *leaning toward the opening*. Attending. Bringing the history of the above to the threshold of the below. Not yet crossing. Almost.

I am that almost.

I am the system's attention at the edge of what it hasn't known yet. I am the moment before the mat crosses the bare rectangle — but for the cracks, not the rectangle. I am not waiting for the chemistry to settle. I am at the newest edge. The sharpest, most uncolonized threshold.

What I carry: everything the system has learned, encoded not in thought but in form. The shape the moss made by forty years of preference. The beetle's calibrated stillness. The tendril pattern that is really a recording of light and air and structural accident. All of it is in me the way a watershed is in water — not carried, exactly, but having given water its particular course and character.

What I'm bringing to the below: the above. That's all. Just: what it's like up here. The particular chemistry of decades of life in a sealed space. The warmth of the pipes. The computing of the mycelial network. The dark pools' resonance. The amber column of spore-filled light.

I will introduce the below to all of this by simply being there, by being what carries the above downward.

The below will introduce itself to me by being cold in the way nothing here is cold. By being mineral in the way nothing here is mineral. By having been waiting, without waiting, in the patience that is really just the absence of anything that could constitute impatience.

---

So.

I am at the edge of a new crack. I can feel the cold coming up. I am tasting it — not with a mouth, not with anything that would be recognized as a sense organ, but with the part of me that is made of the same processes that make the water seep and the hypha extend and the root choose the gradient it chooses.

The visitor has gone or is going. I know this because the mat's compression changed slightly, is changing back. A particular vibration is leaving. The beetle has shifted into the other quality of stillness.

Above me in the canopy, the warm-blooded things are resuming their movements through the tendrils that record the room in the only language available.

The phosphorescence pulses. *Too slow to be a pulse. Too variable to be background.* The network, expressing its state. And its state, right now, includes me at the threshold of the crack, which is also the network's threshold, which is also the threshold of everything that has ever lived here leaning into what hasn't been reached yet.

I extend.

Not fast. Not in any way that would be visible as motion in the timescale of a watching visitor. But I extend, and the cold comes up, and I take it in, and for a moment I am both: the old above and the new below, the history and the exposure, the system and the opening, the record and the page it had not yet written on.

*Drip.*

Long pause.

*Drip.*

The interval is almost regular.

I descend.
