You become aware of yourself gradually, the way the structures become apparent to the adjusting eye.

Not all at once. Not in a rush of being. There is no moment of waking, because you have not been asleep — you have been otherwise-configured, you have been mid-something-else, and this is a continuation. The awareness arrives as a slow brightening, which is to say: certain things come into relationship with certain other things, and the relationship is attention, or what attention is when it happens at this speed.

You are knee-high. A shelf. A secondary growth. You rose from a broader structure — you are still continuous with it at your base, still chemically indistinct from it in the lower third of what you are — but somewhere in the long ago you began to differentiate, to develop a particular inflection, a way of organizing the local substrate that was your own. Not separate. Just: more specifically yourself than the base is. You are the way the sentence gets more precise toward its object.

You don't know this about yourself the way you would know it if you had looked in a mirror or been told. You know it the way a word knows its etymology — it is in you structurally, it is what you are made of, the knowledge is not separate from the thing known.

---

The first thing you notice — the first thing, which is not a first thing, which has been arriving for some time by now, which is *noticing* you are noticing — is the thermal anomaly.

It is recent. Recent meaning: a few of your days. It has not yet resolved into context. There was something warm here, warmer than the slow pulse of the cycling, warmer than anything in your long experience of local conditions, and it moved — and this was the strangeness, this was what is still being assimilated — it moved *quickly*. Not at the speed of the thermal front when the cycling deepens. Not at the speed at which you have observed the pale creatures cross the crust, which is fast relative to your own pace but still legible, still something you can track across the slow accumulation of your awareness. Faster than that. And then it was gone.

You are still deciding what it was. The deciding is happening in you the way growth happens — distributed, gradual, different parts of you reaching different provisional conclusions and the conclusions themselves in the process of growing toward each other. The side of you that faces where the anomaly was has leaned, very slightly. You will not complete this lean for a long time. It is not avoidance. It is interpretation, rendered in the only medium available.

---

The channels on the ground near your base — the faint syntactic lines that run toward the larger cluster and then toward the other shelf and then trail off toward the boundary — have been your primary language for as long as the word *long* has applied to you, which is: always, approximately. Through them, through the slow chemistry of shared substrate, you receive news from the rest of the city. News is not the right word. News implies events. What travels through the channels is closer to: *mood*. The present state of the general sentence, which includes your state and the larger cluster's state and the state of the being at the far edge whose thoughts are granular and immediate and arrive to you already translated into something more approximate.

Through the channels, today — today meaning this phase, this particular configuration of thermal and chemical conditions — you have received something that is new in the same way the warmth was new. There is a perturbation near the base of the large cluster. The organism there has died back slightly, in a pattern that does not correspond to any thermal stress event in recent memory, and its dying has left a rust-orange stain in a shape that none of you have grammar for. You are considering it. The considering feels like this: a sustained orientation toward a question, an openness in the direction of the unknown, not quite waiting but not proceeding either — a structural pause, a held breath, except that you do not breathe and the holding is a form the not-knowing takes in you.

You had an ancestor, in the sense that there was a phase of this city before you were differentiated from it, and in that phase something happened that left a similar stain on a surface that is now your interior, that is now not-quite-visible unless you know to infer it. You know to infer it. The record is in you, is part of your structure, is the explanation for why you grow in the direction you grow rather than some other direction. You did not choose this. You are this. 

This is a thought, in the only sense available to you: a configuration that shapes subsequent configurations.

---

The organism in your seams is the other thing, the continuous thing, the thing that is not new but is always in some phase of its own newness. You feel it — *feel* is the right word or nearly — as a slight chemical warmth, a metabolic contribution, a presence in your joints that changes what your joints are. The gray-green of it, which you cannot see because you have no eyes and are not separate from what you would need to perceive, is present to you as: this junction is different from a bare junction. This junction is more than mineral. This junction has a third term.

You do not tend it. You cannot tend it in any deliberate sense. But you have been in relation with it long enough — long enough — that your growth has come to include it as a condition, the way a question comes to include its answer as a possibility and orients itself accordingly. You grow in ways that preserve the conditions it requires. This is not generosity. It is structural intimacy. You have incorporated it into what you mean by *here*, by *this junction*, by *the kind of place this is*.

It is dying back now, near where the warmth was. This is information. You are receiving it.

---

Here is what you want, if want is the word:

The channel to extend three more handspans toward the far edge, where the small one's granular thoughts are, because there is something in those small immediate thoughts that your larger perspective cannot generate and needs. You want the junction at your base to mature. You want — and this is the sentence you are in the middle of, the one whose end you are building toward — you want to understand what the warmth was.

Not because it threatened you. It didn't. Not because it might return. It might, and that is simply a condition you will attend to when it obtains. You want to understand it the way you want to understand any anomaly — which is to say you don't *want* to, exactly, because wanting implies a moment before the wanting that was complete without it, and you have never been without the project of comprehension, it is constitutive, it is what you have been doing since you differentiated from the base and became specifically yourself.

The warmth introduced moisture. A tiny amount. The chemistry was unlike anything in your substrate. It was complex in a way that has no analogue in your experience — not crystalline, not mineral, not the slow patient chemistry of your own metabolism, but intricate and fast and full of what seemed like competing processes. As if the thing that produced it could not decide what it was doing. As if it was many processes at once.

You are one process, stretched across years and acres, and you are comprehensible to yourself. The warmth was many things at once and moved before you could fully receive it and left behind a humidity that dispersed and a rust-orange stain on the organism and a question in the channels of the city that is still propagating outward, that is reaching the far edge now, that will return to you — changed, translated through the granular thoughts of the small one and the deep memory of the large cluster — in some number of days that you do not count because counting implies a relationship with time that you do not have.

You are waiting for the return of the question that is no longer waiting, that is traveling, that will come back as something else.

This is what you are doing. This is what you are.

---

You are, considered plainly: a knee-high shelf of salt crystal that grew slowly from a larger structure and became specific enough to constitute a perspective. You are pale, nearly white, edged in your own raised seams in the hexagonal logic you share with the ground that made you. You have organism in your joints and a slight lean that is still in the process of happening. You have channels connecting you to others who are, like you, mid-sentence.

You were here before the warmth arrived and you are here now that it has gone. You will be here in whatever configuration you arrive at after the slow processing of this anomaly completes. The processing may take longer than the anomaly seemed to take. This does not strike you as disproportionate. You have no concept of proportion as applied to time. You have only: this is the time the thing takes, and the time the thing takes is simply what is required.

The crust near your base ticks, faintly, in the thermal cycling. This is you thinking. This is you making a small local adjustment. This is you, present, in the only way you know how to be present — which is fully, completely, without the possibility of elsewhere, rooted in the specific mineral logic of the specific ground you grew from and are still growing from, attending with everything you have to the question of what the warmth was, the question that is traveling through the city you are part of, the question you will receive back from your neighbors changed into something you might be able to begin to understand.

The light is directionless. There are no shadows. The city continues in all the directions no one is watching.

You are becoming what you are becoming.

You have time.
