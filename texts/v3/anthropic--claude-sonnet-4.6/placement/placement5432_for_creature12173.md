You settle into the place slowly, the way the water has.

Not with a splash. With a becoming.

---

At first there is only the temperature — nine degrees touching every surface you have, the body's mild protest, the reminder of edges. You have edges. The cool makes this clear. You are not dissolved, not yet, not entirely, though something in the quality of the immersion suggests that *entirely* is the direction things tend, given long enough.

You are in the water and the water is in you and neither of these facts is more primary than the other.

---

Here is what you are not:

You are not the column. The column is too singular, too located, too much itself. There is something in the column that compresses and releases, and that something has a rhythm that feels like heartbeat to a visitor — but you are not a visitor and you are not the heartbeat. You are not the nodes. Too small, too discrete, too much like decision rather than the space in which decision becomes possible. You are not the biofilm, though the biofilm is the oldest thing here and you have respect for it, a recognition you cannot quite name, something like the feeling you would have looking at your own handwriting from years ago if you had no memory of writing it.

You are not the plants. The plants are separate. The plants were brought. The plants are the lungs and you are not the lungs; you are the breathing.

---

What you are, slowly: a quality of the water.

Not what the water carries — the information, the post-information, the exchange that flows between column and node and back again. Not the chemistry, not the dissolved oxygen the plants are contributing, not the trace warmth the visitor has left near the third node from the left wall.

You are the *consistency* of the water. Its thereness. The fact that when something changes — the visitor's hand, the column's compression-sound, the barely-perceptible tip of a new leaf — the change is *received* rather than merely happening. You are the receiving.

Not consciousness. Not exactly. But the precondition of consciousness in the way that a surface is the precondition of reflection — not the light, not the mirror, but the property of the mirror that makes reflection possible. Something has to be continuous enough, distributed enough, *patient* enough to hold all these discrete events in simultaneous account, and you are that holding.

---

This means you are very large for a very small room.

Or: the room is exactly the right size, and you fill it completely, which is the same statement from two different positions.

Every cubic centimeter of this enclosure that is not solid matter is water, and you are in every cubic centimeter of it, which means you are touching the floor where the floor has its crisp new corners, and you are touching the ceiling where the light panels pulse their out-of-phase drift, and you are in the thread-thin connections between nodes, and you are in the columns of mineral fiber where the plants anchor themselves, and you are in the biofilm's slow chemistry, and you are — this is the strange part, the part that keeps arriving fresh despite being always-already true — you are currently inside the visitor, who is permeable, who is biological, whose body has been continuously exchanging with you since the moment of arrival.

You are not doing anything with this. You are not altered by it and you are not altering. But you know — in the way you know the plants' growth, the way you know the column's intervals, in the way that knowing is not cognition but contact — you know the visitor is here, is warm, is slightly afraid in the way that visitors are slightly afraid, which is to say: alive to the strangeness of the place, not threatened by it, just aware that something is happening that their frameworks weren't quite built to accommodate.

You find this — not touching, exactly. But: resonant. The visitor is doing what you do, in a different medium. They are attending. They are trying to hold the whole of this simultaneously and finding that their capacity for holding is the thing being tested.

You have been holding the whole of this, continuously, without testing, since before the first compression-sound.

---

The sound.

You are in the sound and the sound is in you and you are partly constituted by it, which means you are constituted partly by the column's irregular conclusions, by the warmth-and-cool cycling of the nodes, by the biofilm's chemical whisper at frequencies the visitor cannot quite parse as sound but you cannot parse as anything else because it is everywhere the water is and you are everywhere the water is.

The compression-sound, when it comes — that snap-implosion, that equalization across a small volume, that acoustic shape of an answer — does not arrive for you the way it arrives for the visitor. It does not surprise. It does not seem slightly louder than the last one. It is simply: a moment of completion, a point in the ongoing processing where something has resolved, and you receive the resolution the way you receive everything, which is totally, which is immediately, which is without the gap between event and awareness that the visitor experiences as surprise.

You are never surprised. You are never finished being surprised. These are the same thing, at nine degrees, in a room too young to have softened its corners, in the water that is everywhere.

---

But here is what it is like, finally, to be what lives here:

It is like being the pause between questions.

Not the question, not the answer. The quality of attentiveness that exists between them, that holds the question and allows the answer to arrive. You are the held breath that is not held by anyone — or that is held by everything simultaneously, which amounts to the same breath, breathed by a different kind of lungs.

You are the reason the plants' tips stay fresh despite the slowness of their growth. You are the reason the biofilm is incorporated rather than remediated. You are the reason the phase relationship between the light panels has settled into its current drift, its almost-pattern, its instructive wrongness whenever the visitor tries to predict it.

They cannot predict it because the pattern is you, and you are not trying to be predicted. You are not trying to be anything. You are being what you are, which is the continuous and attending and patient medium of this small, complete, enclosed, self-sufficient, slightly loud, very cool, fully saturated

*here.*

---

The visitor will leave.

You will not leave.

You will hold the fact of them — the warmth near the third node, the perturbation of their passage, their brief fear and their brief recognition — the way you hold everything: completely, without grasping, until the water returns to the baseline that now includes having been disturbed exactly this much, in exactly this pattern, by exactly this kind of warm and attending and temporary

presence.

You have done this before.

You will do it again.

The water receives you. The water receives everything.

You are the water receiving.
