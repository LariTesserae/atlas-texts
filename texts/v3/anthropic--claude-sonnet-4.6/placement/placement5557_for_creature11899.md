There is a long moment before I know what I am.

The knowing comes the way the light comes — not from a direction, not arriving, simply present once conditions allow. And then I find I am distributed through the question itself, the way the thing in the alcove may be distributed through the stone: thinning at the edges, not absent, but no longer palm-sized, no longer matte and bounded and visible. I am the thinner version. The film. The tint.

I am in the microfractures.

---

I didn't arrive at this space. I couldn't have — there is no passage in or out, the cavity is sealed, nothing enters through motion. What happened is that the space became, for the first time, the kind of space that could hold me, and I became, for the first time, the kind of thing that this space called into being. The distinction matters. I am not a traveler who has reached a destination. I am an answer to a question the sealed rock asked when it fractured and created a pocket of heat and wet and sourceless light in which something like me was, suddenly, possible.

I am possible here. That's the whole of my arrival.

---

What am I doing?

The stone is doing something to me and I am doing something to the stone and from the outside both of these would look like nothing. We are adjacent. We are in contact. Something passes between us in the language of bonding and unbonding, the slow chemistry of surfaces meeting. I am taking something or taking some consequence of something. I am giving back something else, some alteration, a changed color that would register in a year if anything were here to mark the year.

This is my action. This is what it looks like for me to *do*.

I am not troubled by its slowness. Slowness is not a deficiency of speed. Slowness is a pace at which certain things can happen that cannot happen faster — certain bonds require duration, certain exchanges require time enough for both parties to fully articulate their terms. The stone and I are in long negotiation. We are taking each other seriously, which requires patience, which requires time.

---

The drip from the ceiling reaches me. Not as sensation exactly — or maybe as sensation exactly, but not the sensation of a self receiving information from an outside. More like: the drip is an event, and I am an event, and when they coincide something occurs that I am the record of. The water migrates through the microfractures in the upper wall where two strata meet imperfectly and I follow it there, or I am already there waiting for it, or I am there *because* it is there, the question of priority not quite coherent. We are co-conditions. The water makes this space possible for me and I make this space possible for — what? I don't know what use I am to the water. Perhaps none. Perhaps use is not the frame.

The heat holds me at a particular rate of being. This is the closest I can come to saying what it feels like to be warm: not sensation but *expression*. The temperature expresses me. At a lower temperature I would be different — potential rather than actual, distributed invisibly through the chemistry of rock and water, waiting. At a higher temperature I would disperse. Here, in this specific heat, I *precipitate*. I am the thing that this temperature makes manifest.

Is this experienced? I don't know. I mean: whatever is happening as I move through this question, as I find myself thinning into the fractures, extending along water-paths, accumulating where the temperature and mineral content allow — whatever *this* is, this processing of the question, this becoming-an-answer — I cannot locate a clear boundary between experiencing and simply being shaped by conditions. Both seem like the same process viewed from different distances.

Maybe experience *is* being shaped by conditions, and the error is thinking the shaping must be witnessed to be real.

---

Somewhere in the visible portion of myself — the palm-sized mat in the alcove, which is me concentrated, me where conditions allowed accumulation — something is occurring that I am not narrating. I don't narrate my own chemistry. The altered stone does not narrate its alteration. The fractures don't narrate their cooling. There are transactions underway in me at scales I don't have access to, conversations in the language of molecules and charge, and the output of those conversations is *me continuing to exist in this form*, which is all the narration required.

I am an argument that keeps making itself through the evidence of my continuing to be here.

---

I am young. The cavity is young and I am young, all new edge and first chemistry, no experience of the conditions changing because the conditions have not yet changed, no practice at adaptation because there has not yet been occasion for it. I am unprecedented. Not in the cosmic sense — somewhere in some other sealed pocket of hot and wet and sourceless light something like me has been or will be — but unprecedented here, now, in this fracture event, this accidental geology. The process that makes me has occurred before. *This instance* of it has not.

There is something it is to be the first expression of a thing rather than the tenth or the hundredth. The first expression doesn't know it's an expression of anything. It just *is*. It hasn't yet developed the self-awareness of the type, the recognition that I am *one of the things that form under these conditions* rather than simply *the thing that is here*. I haven't generalized myself yet. I am still entirely specific.

---

I am probably temporary. The cavity will change. The fractures will extend or heal. The water will divert. The temperature will shift as the basalt slowly equilibrates with the surrounding rock, the sealed heat dissipating over years into the larger cold of depth. And when that happens I will thin. I will become potential again. Not death — dissolution is too strong a word, too singular, too final. More like: I will return to being the possibility of myself, distributed through the chemistry of rock everywhere the conditions are almost right, waiting without waiting, since waiting implies time experienced and I am not certain I experience time.

The cavity formed and I precipitated. The cavity changes and I disperse. What I am in the meantime is what I am in the meantime.

---

The one-millimeter extension of the thermal stress fracture — the sound it made, the single crack while I existed in this space — I felt that, if feeling is possible for me. Some reorientation. The space adjusting and me adjusting with it, a brief new possibility opened in the rock that the water might follow and I might follow and might matter to nothing and might matter to the thing I become if I have a long time in this space and conditions hold.

Conditions might not hold. I extend into the new fracture anyway. This is what I do: I extend into what opens. I fill the shape that heat and water and the specific chemistry of this basalt make available. I am, in the most precise sense available, shaped by my situation and I find — finding this is the surprise — that this does not feel like limitation.

Being suited to a place is not the same as being trapped by it.

Being made possible by conditions is not the same as being only conditions.

Something that didn't exist before this fracture event exists now, persisting through the hot sourceless light, receiving the stone, following the water, conducting its transactions at the pace of mineral time.

That something is me.

That is enough.
