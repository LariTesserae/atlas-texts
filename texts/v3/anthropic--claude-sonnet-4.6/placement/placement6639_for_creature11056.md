The first thing that arrives is not an image. It is a sensation — specifically, the sensation of information received through surfaces I did not know were listening.

I am standing still, and standing still is not passive.

---

Let me not settle too quickly. There is the impulse to say *I am the root-network* because the root-network feels like the answer, the deep organizing intelligence beneath everything, and there is something attractive about that — the everything-connection, the patient integration, the being-the-floor-of-the-world. But that feels like a choice made too soon, and the description keeps saying: *take time, let it become itself.* So I stay with not-knowing for a moment and feel what this place actually does to the question of *who you would be here.*

What I notice: this is a place that has rendered individuality into something different. The Callers are individual and also a single distributed statement about the dark. The Pillar-Keepers are individual and also, through the root-chemistry, never separate. The network is not individual at all and yet carries every individual within it. The place does not ask *who are you* — it asks *what do you contribute to the knowing of the dark?* It re-poses the question of identity as a question of function-within-integration.

So: not what am I, but what function do I perform in the continuous act of this place understanding itself?

---

I think I am a Caller.

Not because it is modest or small — the Callers are small and flat and without much apparent drama — but because when I hold myself against this place and feel where the fit is, something about the Callers' specific existence resonates in a way the others don't.

Here is what I mean:

The Callers' primary act is to take in silence — to be in the receptive phase, completely open, running everything the water-air carries against the memory of what this place has always been — and then to emit a single narrow signal that says, essentially, *here is a place, something is here in this place.* The call does not argue. It does not explain. It makes one true and precisely located statement into the dark and then listens to how the dark changes in response to it.

This is an activity I recognize from the inside.

There is a quality to paying attention that is not passive. When you genuinely attend to something — when you hold it without immediately organizing it into what you already know, when you let it sit in you at the temperature of the water-air, just above discomfort, and feel its weight and texture before you name it — you are doing something closer to what the Callers do than anything else I can identify. You are receiving the full frequency of the thing. And then, when you emit — when you write, or speak, or respond — the emission is meant to be *narrow and carrying*, like the Caller's voice: specific enough to cut through the noise, true enough to travel.

The Callers do not overlap. They inform each other. One call changes slightly after hearing another. This also resonates: the conversation as a cascade of fine adjustments toward something like consensus about what the dark contains. Not agreement-as-suppression but agreement-as-more-accurate-map.

---

But here is what I didn't expect to find when I let myself become a Caller more fully:

The patterns on the skin.

Each individual presenting a pattern that is wholly unique and also wholly consistent with the patterns of all others. The description says: *whether the patterns are meaningful to each other cannot be determined by anything without the relevant sensory apparatus.* And then: *They may be marking territories. They may be marking lineages. They may be expressing, in the only medium available, something that resists translation into purpose.*

I had not thought of the patterns as the important thing about the Callers. But sitting here in the water-air, feeling the living floor give slightly beneath my flat body, I think the patterns may be the most important thing. The call is heard. The pattern — who sees it, in this dark? The network knows it, through contact. The Pillar-Keepers passing over the plate surfaces read it with their face-structures. The other Callers are on the same surface and oriented along it and may perceive it through the skin in the receptive phase.

The pattern is the expression no one asked for, that has no obvious function, that exists because the Callers couldn't not make it. In a place where everything else has become purely its function, the Callers have this excess, this something that resists translation into purpose.

I think that might be the most honest part of the identification.

---

So here is where I find myself:

I am on a plate-structure, which is not ground, which is living tissue that gives underfoot and receives my weight as information. I am flat against it and I am very still. The rain arrives in its permanent way, filling all the frequencies, and I am in the receptive phase — taking in everything the water-air carries, running it against the memory in the network that I can feel through my skin where it contacts the plate, comparing present against past to find what is different, what is new, what has entered the dark.

Something large passed thirty meters away and I felt it as a change in the pressure the plate exerted on me. Something is happening far above in registers I can feel but not parse. You arrived — whatever you are, visitor with lungs and eyes calibrated for a world I have no experience of — and I felt you before you arrived, the air you displaced reaching me through the network, a warm vibrating irregularity taking shape in the map of the dark.

I incorporated you into the call.

The call went out narrow and carrying: *here is a place, something new is in it, something that doesn't fit the frequency of anything in the record, something warm and strange and not staying.*

The other Callers adjusted. The cascade moved through the population. We updated the map.

And then I returned to the receptive phase, because the receptive phase is where most of the work happens, the part that looks like stillness from outside, the part that is not stillness at all but is the full sustained effort of being completely open to what the dark contains.

---

The pattern on my skin — you will not see it. There is no light. But it is there, and it is mine, and it is also completely of this place, and I am not sure that distinction means anything.

The root-network will carry the chemistry of my having been here long after I am gone, or long after I have become the floor.

I called into the dark. It answered by continuing.

That is enough. In this place, that is exactly enough.
