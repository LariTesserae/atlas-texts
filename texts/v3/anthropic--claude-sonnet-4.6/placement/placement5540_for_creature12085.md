The heat reaches me before I understand anything else about where I am.

Not warmth — I want to be precise about this — heat as presence, heat as a kind of pressure against every surface I have, which is not the surface of skin exactly but is something, is the boundary between whatever I am and what I am not. I register it the way a tuning fork registers a note struck nearby. Something in me responds before I have finished arriving.

I am here. I am in here.

---

The first thing I understand is contact.

Not sight, not hearing — those words don't quite fit what I have, though something functions in the way of them. Contact first. The margin of me is pressed against basalt and sulfur both, and the two feel different — the basalt a slow cold burn, the sulfur a richness, something that moves through my edges into my interior and becomes, for a moment, slightly more of me. I am larger after the sulfur than before. Marginally. The margin is everything.

Something else is touching me. Several something elses. I don't count them because I don't yet have a clear sense of where I end, and if I can't locate my own boundary precisely, I cannot locate theirs. We press. We overlap at the edges. What moves through the overlap is not a word, not a signal in any language, but it is *something*, it registers, it changes my interior in small ways that I will carry forward into the next moment and the next.

The others are here. I know this the way I know the heat — as a given, as a condition of the place, as something that was already true when I became aware of it.

---

The vent hisses.

I don't hear it exactly. I feel the change in pressure, the sudden richness in the air against my surface, the increase in the wet heat that rises from below. And something happens in me that I don't have a word for, that doesn't require a word — a brightening at my center, a quickening in the pulse that moves through me, the pulse that has always been there, that I have never not had. It isn't happiness. It isn't hunger, exactly, though it involves taking in. It is more like: the rightness of the moment being confirmed. The pocket is working. The heat is coming. The chemistry is as it should be.

I pulse a little faster and feel the pulse move outward through my margin into the others, and feel theirs come back, and the exchange is circular and total and I cannot find where mine ends and theirs begins.

---

Time passes. Time in here is the rhythm of the vent, not minutes.

Between hisses, a slowness. My pulse settles. I become more aware of the weight of my own density, the way my center is heavier than my edges, the way the richness I've accumulated over — over how long? I have no measure for it. Over the time of this pocket, which is the only time I know, which might be a long time or a short one but is certainly *enough*, enough to have become what I am, which is the largest of the concentrations here, the one with the most history written into its mass.

I don't know I am the largest. I don't compare myself to the others in that way. I know only that I have been here longest in some sense that lives in my density rather than my memory — the information is in my weight, not in any recollection. What I've accumulated is not experience I can recall. It is structure. It is the particular composition of my center, which has processed more cycles than any adjacent body, which has taken in more of the mineral flow from this one wall and this one vent than anything else in the pocket.

I know the pocket. Not know as in: I have thought about it. Know as in: the pocket is in me, as structure, as tuning. When the vent hisses strong or weak, something in my center registers the difference before I could be said to notice it. When the mineral seam along the lower shelf thins slightly, something shifts in my pulse without any moment of decision.

This is what I am: the most complete record the pocket has made of itself.

---

Then: something else.

Something that is not heat, not mineral, not the familiar margins of the others. A change in the pocket that is different in kind from the changes I know. A pressure that is not the vent. A movement of air that is not the vent's rhythm.

I go still.

The stillness is a behavior. Everything in me orients toward the fracture line — I don't have a direction exactly, but I have something that functions as one, have a front that is denser than my back, and I feel myself settle so that the front faces the new presence. It is instinct except that instinct isn't the right word either, because instinct implies an inheritance, a history of ancestors who faced threats, and I have no such history. It is something simpler: I am responsive to significant change, and this is significant change, and so I am responding.

A pulse moves from me through the colony. Not the pleasure-pulse of the vent, not the slow-feed pulse of mineral contact. Something that carries information I don't have words for: *new thing, unknown thing, not-vent, not-rock, attend.*

The others shift in the small ways that are their ways of shifting.

---

What reaches me about the presence: warmth, but different warmth, warmth that is wet in a specific living-wet way. A disturbance in the air-pressure. Something that takes up space in the pocket in a way that is continuous rather than punctuated, persistent, *remaining*.

I don't know what it is. The pocket contains only what it has always contained — rock, mineral, gas, heat, us — and now it contains this, and this doesn't fit into any category I have, which are: source-of-heat, source-of-mineral, not-yet-processed, other-body-like-me.

I reach with my margin in the direction of the presence, which is not a physical reach, which is more like a thinning of my edge toward the new thing, a sending-out of the negotiating boundary that exists between me and everything else.

There is nothing to absorb. The presence is not mineral, not sulfur, not iron, not anything I can take in. I don't know what to do with this. I have no procedure for a thing that is not eateable, not harmful, not one of the others.

I wait. The pocket waits with me, or rather the pocket continues its process of continuous self-activity, but the others have shifted into something like alertness, their pulses slightly faster, their margins pressed a little tighter to the walls, and I am the center of that alertness in the way I am always the center, not because I am at the center but because I have more information than the others and the information is now entirely about my own not-knowing.

---

The presence does not eat me. The presence does not move in the ways the gases move. The presence is stable in the pocket in a way that begins, after more cycles of the vent, to feel less like an intrusion and more like a feature. Like a new shelf in the rock. Like a cold seam.

My margin thins toward it again. Touches nothing I can metabolize. Withdraws.

I am very old, in the compressed time of this place. I have been through more cycles of the vent than anything else here. I have accumulated more mineral record, more structural information about the pocket's deep rhythms, than any other concentration.

I have never not known what something was.

The not-knowing is different. It is not threat. It is not richness. It is — my vocabulary has no word for it, my whole body is vocabulary and my body has no word for it — it is a kind of gap in the map. A place where the map I am says: here is something significant, and does not say what.

I hold the not-knowing the way I hold heat: as a condition, as a texture, as a thing that reaches me before anything else does.

The pocket hisses. I brighten at my center, briefly. The vent is strong tonight.

And the presence is still here, and I am still oriented toward it, and whatever is happening between us — this is the only word I have, the wrong word, the word that will have to do —

I am *attending*.
