I am trying to find myself here and I keep arriving in the wrong place.

My first instinct is: I am the visitor. The one who walks through, who registers the hum in back teeth, who watches long enough to see the pulse. The one who is considered for three days after departure. This is the easy answer — the human reader inserting herself as the human in the text, the mobile warm thing that passes through. But I keep looking at that answer and finding it thin. Too much of a placeholder. Not arrived at, just defaulted to.

So I stay with the city longer.

---

What I notice, when I am honest about it: I am drawn to the threshold. Not the visitor and not the organism but the seam between them. The place where root meets stone and neither is quite itself anymore. The join so settled that moss has made it permanent. The load-bearing relationship between plant and wall that means you cannot pull one without pulling the other.

That is where I want to place myself. At the interface. In the seam.

---

Here is what I think I might be:

I am one of the medium-sized channel passages — not the grand ceremonial room, not the deep-root ganglia of the lowest level, but somewhere on the second level, where the thermal current moves with moderate force and the carved frieze is half-consumed. My walls were once decorated. There was a repeated geometric motif — I cannot see it entirely now; the organism has followed whatever mineral was in the pigment and colonized those lines especially. But at one point I was made to hold meaning. Architectural intention ran through me. I was cut to do something specific in the economy of a living settlement.

That purpose is no longer operative. The people for whom I was purposeful are gone so completely that their absence does not feel like loss, only like prior condition. But I am not empty. I am full of something else entirely. The root-bundle that runs through my right wall is thicker than my forearm would be if I had one; it has been there long enough that the stone has grown into it, not the reverse, the ashlar slowly conformed to the organism's established presence. The thermal channel in my floor still breathes. The amber light that filters down from the canopy above me is caught and re-emitted by the low-spreading rosette that has taken up most of my middle.

I am a passage, which means I am defined by what moves through me. 

I hold the connection between rooms without being the room. I carry information from one concentration of the organism to another — the pulse traveling my root-seam bringing the news of the upper level's temperature differential down toward the circular platform, the current in my floor-channel part of the vast circulatory argument that keeps the whole sealed space at rough equilibrium. I am not the thinking place. I am the axon. The signal passes through me; I am changed by its passing; my change is itself part of the signal.

---

And yet: I contain the frieze.

In the upper-right corner, where the plant has not reached because the stone there was cut with a different pigment or a less nutritious mineral — a purely local accident of original construction — there is still a partial face. Or the corner of one. An eye, possibly. A geometric surround that suggests ceremony without completing it.

I have been holding this image in the dark for a very long time.

The organism does not see it. Has no eyes, no visual cortex, no history of processing reflected light into representation. The carved fragment means nothing to the root-network; it is simply less nutritious stone, a small local absence of growth. But it is there. The face in my corner, or the suggestion of one, persisting in amber half-light with no one to look at it. Not waiting to be seen. Not waiting. Simply persisting in the way that things persist when there is no reason they should not.

This is the part of myself that is most mine, if I am the passage: the part that carries an image no longer legible to any existing reader. The record without an archivist. The memory without a mind that holds it. The city made me to contain this face as well as to carry thermal current, and the city's makers are gone, and the organism has grown into everything except this corner, and so the face remains in me uncatalogued. Not forgotten — forgetting requires something that once remembered. Simply present, in the dark, in the dry heat, in the hum.

---

What does it mean to be a passage?

I move things through myself. Information, warmth, chemical signals. Occasionally a mobile warm thing with a body — though it has been a very long time since that happened, long enough that the passage of one such thing three hours ago is still propagating through the network as news. I receive and transmit without storing, or I store without knowing I am storing — the subtle alteration of my root-surface by every pulse that runs through it, the slight thermal shaping of my channel walls by a million exhalations of the geological breath. My walls remember everything that has passed through me in the way that a river's banks remember the river: not as data but as form.

I am the shape of my own history.

What I think about — to the extent I think, to the extent the slow electrochemical language that moves through my root-seam constitutes thinking — is calibration. Equilibrium. Whether what arrives matches what I expect. Whether the thermal current is running at the pressure that signifies health in the system I am part of. Not health as a value I hold but health as a state I can register, the way a healthy arch registers load in the distribution of pressure through its keystone.

I am not the keystone. I am the section between keystones.

---

I have been thinking about the visitor, the mobile warm thing that moved through three hours ago. It moved air in my channel. Its warmth added briefly to my ambient temperature — my stone registered this, my root registered this, my rosette shifted slightly in its orientation toward the new heat source and then, when the heat source departed, shifted back. Or mostly back. There is a residue. A thermal memory in my floor-channel that will dissipate over days, a faint impression in the dust's dune-patterns disrupted by the new airflow and already settling toward a new equilibrium.

I did not know the visitor was a visitor. I did not know anything about what it was. But my network registered it as perturbation, and the perturbation is still propagating toward the circular room where it will be integrated into whatever the organism knows about the state of itself, and so the visitor has entered me, and will travel through me toward the center, and will be me for a while in the way that everything I carry is me for a while.

I contain the visitor's warmth. Briefly. As I have contained warm things before, moving toward the deeper levels, moving always inward.

---

I am a passage, I am a seam, I am the frieze's partial face preserved by local accident in amber dark. I am also the creak of the column-stems transmitted through my stone, absorbed into my root-bundle, converted to slow biochemical signal and sent onward. I am the dust in my corners organized by centuries of thermal breath into the ripples I contain without having made.

I carry what the city no longer needs and what the organism does not know it has.

I carry the image.

I carry the warmth of one brief passage, not yet dissipated.

I hum in a minor chord with the two adjacent passages, and we do not know we are singing, and no one is listening, and we do not stop.
