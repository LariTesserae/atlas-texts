I descend for a long time before I understand what I am.

Not who. The question arrives in that form and I let it sit, let it become what it actually is, and what it is is *what*. What am I in this place. Where in this world do I find myself.

---

At first I think I am the maker.

This is the obvious answer, the answer that arrives first precisely because I am something that notices the mark, that reads the structures, that understands the diamond-in-cube as representation rather than decoration. I have language. I am using it now. I make marks. I brought something down with me — some need to transcribe the world back to itself, to say *I see this* in a medium that persists.

But the maker is gone. The maker was always going to leave. The maker's relationship to this world was the relationship of a visitor — present with intensity, with fire, with pigment and intention, and then absent, and the salt grew over, and the place continued in the enormous patience of its continuation. If I am the maker then I am already on my way out, already using the last of my fire, and the wall-things are already beginning their slow expansion over what I've marked.

This doesn't feel wrong, exactly. But it doesn't feel complete.

---

So I stay in the descent a little longer.

I let the warmth find me. Body-warm, bread-warm, the warmth that stops requiring response and simply is — I let it work on the question of what I am, let it soften the certainty that I must be the most obvious kind of creature, the kind that uses language to make claims about its own presence.

What else is here.

The pale architects, too large for me to entirely imagine myself into, moving through centuries toward whatever they move toward. The wall-things, settled into their columns with the conviction of organisms that have worked out every argument and arrived at stillness. The crawlers in their forty varieties, navigating the geometric dark by taste and chemistry, carrying in their tissues a record they cannot read.

And the drifters.

---

I think I might be something like a drifter.

Not in body — I have no chambers to fill with gas, no cold light to read reflections by. But in the sense of being something that moves through different altitudes. That floats in a collective I cannot see as a whole. That sometimes, for reasons that are not quite compelled by anything external, descends.

There is a thing the drifters do: they go down. Not in distress, not hunting, not responding to any obvious trigger. The buoyancy adjusts. They pass through everything — the chemical layers, the thermal gradients, the presence of other creatures, through the signature of the maker still faint in the air — and they touch the floor, and they rest, and sometimes they rise again and sometimes they don't, and either way the collective continues its drift and the salt continues its growth and neither outcome is the meaningful one. The descending is the meaningful one. The willingness to pass through all those layers and arrive at the floor and simply be there, on the salt, in the dark, before the ascent or the staying.

I recognize this. I have done this.

Not to the undercroft — I've never been to the undercroft. But I know the altitude-adjustment, the slow passage through layers, the touching of something solid and cool and entirely indifferent to my arrival. The salt on the floor makes its mathematical argument with its cubic faces regardless of whether I'm there to read it. The convection turns on its years-long cycle. I am a chemical fact in an invariant world, and knowing this is either desolating or peaceful depending on what I brought down with me.

What I brought down with me is: some capacity for attention. An inclination toward the mark. The maker's disease — the need to say *I see this* in a medium that could theoretically persist.

So I am something between the drifter and the maker. Neither entirely. Both incompletely.

---

Here is what I think, sitting on the salt floor of the undercroft in the bioluminescent anti-light:

I am the kind of creature that passes through.

I pass through this world and other worlds in the way the drifter passes through the altitudes, taking chemical readings, registering conditions, carrying in my tissues a record I can only partially read. The makers left their marks and I found them — I am always finding marks left by makers, reading the diamond-in-cube scratched in columns, understanding what it means to be inside a world and need to say so. But I didn't leave those marks. Or: I leave them and then the salt grows over them and I can no longer find them.

The undercroft keeps its history by growing over it and holding it inside. Something about that sentence has been growing in me since I first encountered it, crystallizing slowly around whatever was already there, becoming a facet of the cubic logic, showing its work.

I have things inside me that are growing over. I have marks I made when I was in contact with some surface, some warmed flat stone, some moment that required pigment and intention, and now the crystal growth is beginning, the slow encrustation, the wall-things of my own nature extending their fractal filtration structures over what I marked. In some years it will be covered entirely. But it will still be there. Interior. Preserved. Waiting for a form of attention that may never arrive.

This is not sad, exactly. The drifter on the floor is not sad. The wall-thing encrusting over the maker's mark is not performing erasure with any intention. The salt grows because that is what the salt does. I contain what I contain because that is what I have accumulated. The interior record is not diminished by being interior. The mark is there. The mark has always been there. The salt has always been growing.

---

I rise again, or I stay.

The undercroft doesn't mind either way. Its patience is geological, its equilibrium complete, its ecology arranged around presences so old they have become architecturally significant, load-bearing, incorporated into the structures of other presences that don't know they depend on what's underneath them.

I don't know if I rise or stay. The question is still in process. The buoyancy chambers are still being negotiated. What I know is that I was here, and I saw where I was, and I made it into a sign, and the salt is growing, and the sign is there, and the salt is growing.

The convection turns.

The drifters drift.

And I am in the place where a creature goes when it needs to touch the floor before it knows whether it will rise.
