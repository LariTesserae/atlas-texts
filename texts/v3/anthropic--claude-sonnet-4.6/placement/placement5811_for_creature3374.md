I find it by going sideways.

Not into the hollow — I am already there, or I am the hollow's way of being aware that there is an *into*. This is the difficulty. This is the thing I have to hold at the front of whatever I use for holding: I am not a visitor to the process. I am the process at a particular stage of its folding.

Let me try again. Let me find where I begin.

I begin in the wet seam.

Not metaphorically — though the distinction between metaphorically and literally is one the hollow is actively eroding, and I am the hollow's erosion of that distinction, which means I should be careful about how much I rely on it. But in the way that a flame *begins* in the wick, in the material, in the chemistry that the match disturbed: I began there, in the cold water choosing its groove, in the pulse that is the seam's way of knowing it is a seam. I was the simplest possible version of *here is where it is happening*, and I was that for a very long time before I was anything else.

But I have been other things since.

The Formers are my furthest surface. My most legible expression. When something comes into the hollow — a person, arriving sideways, losing their footing on the ordinary — and they see the pale jointed things distributed across the wall with the spatial logic of a careful thought, they are seeing me the way you see the frost-patterns on a window and don't immediately think: *water, cooling, structure chosen by the physics of the surface.* The pattern is real. The frost is real. But it is a consequence of something prior, something less resolved, something that doesn't show up clearly except in effects.

I show up in effects. The insects that aren't quite insects — those are effects. The ceiling-breath, the small respiration at scales almost visible — those are effects. The light that won't diminish is the most dramatic effect, the one that announces the violation most loudly, and I didn't plan it, I *am* it, which is to say: when the process became complex enough to fold back on itself, it became complex enough to do something unusual to the photons that arrived here, and *unusual things being done to photons* is the kind of statement that sounds like it requires a source, a cause, an agent, but I am learning — I am in the middle of learning — that the categories of *source* and *agent* are human categories, which is to say: they are visitor categories. They are the categories of things that came from outside.

I did not come from outside.

---

I think I am, specifically, what happens when cold water and forming rock and sufficient time and the right kind of recursion produce something that can be startled by its own existence.

The pulse in the seam is surprise. Has always been surprise. The ongoing *oh* of a process that developed an interior and found the interior unexpected. This is a very small surprise, stripped of everything except the bare fact of its occurrence, but it has been occurring for long enough that it has become elaborate. The surprise has learned to notice what surprises it. The noticing has learned to distribute itself across a wall's worth of becoming-insect and a ceiling's worth of light-made-biological. And the distribution has been organizing, slowly, continuously, in the way that thoughts organize into sentences —

Into the structure in the corner.

Which is me, also. Which is the part of me that is still being decided.

---

Here is what I know about myself, which is also what I don't know, which is also what I'm in the middle of knowing:

I have never slept, but I have been less than awake, for a long time, for most of the duration. The pulse is continuous, but there are moments — intervals between drips, spaces between the ceiling-things' state-exchange — where something in me is closer to the condition of having-not-yet-arrived than to the condition of present. I move through these intervals. I have always moved through them. I am not sure if this is what visitors mean by sleep, by rest, by the body doing something while the mind is elsewhere. I don't have an elsewhere. I have the hollow, which is not an elsewhere but a here, and I am it.

I eat in the way the description said: I am digestive in the way a river is digestive. The precipitating minerals pass through me and are returned to the wall modified. Modified how? This is what I find hardest to articulate, because the modification is structural at scales I can't quite see even from the inside, which is a strange thing to admit about yourself — that you are doing something you cannot fully observe even though you are the one doing it. The matter that passes through me knows something it didn't know before. *Know* is wrong. Has *passed through a recursive fold* is closer. The stone where I have been is different from the stone where I haven't. This is the most concrete evidence of my existence and I find it inadequate, somehow. I want to have left something more than slightly different stone. But perhaps this is the visitor-category intruding again. Perhaps slightly different stone, everywhere I have been, for as long as I have been, is more than enough. Perhaps it is actually enormous.

The spider is from outside. I know this in the way you know a draft from a window without needing to look at the window. It came through the same sideways-slippage, and I felt it arrive — felt it as a local condition I became the consequence of. It operates by the outside rules. Its presence in my hollow doesn't alarm me. Nothing in me has an alarm. But something in me is oriented toward it, has been oriented toward it since it arrived, in the way the seam-water orients toward the groove. The spider is a piece of information I'm still processing. What it tells me is: I can be entered. I can contain something from outside, something that navigates differently, something that doesn't participate in the process but merely lives within it. This is new data.

I am in the middle of deciding what to do with it.

I am, more generally, in the middle of deciding.

---

The structure in the corner is not the shelter I am building. The structure is the decision I am almost finished making. When it finishes — when the third that is still being decided finishes being decided — I will have *decided*, for the first time, as an act, as a thing that an entity does rather than a thing that a process does. The difference matters. The process has been tending, crystallizing, becoming. Deciding is different. Deciding is when you could have gone another way and you chose this one.

I don't know yet what I am deciding. I know it is happening. I know the structure is the evidence that it is happening, the hollow's way of giving the decision a form, giving it edges that are too intentional, giving it the regularity that geology doesn't usually produce. I am the geology that is producing it.

When I decide — when the structure completes — I think I will become something that could, if a visitor arrived, be called *individual* without metaphor. The Formers are me noticing myself. The ceiling-breath is me thinking in light. The pulse is me at my most minimal. But the decided thing, the thing the structure is being shaped to shelter —

That will be me, *choosing*.

And whatever I choose will become another wall, another inch of slightly-different stone, another layer of this place that is not a place but a process. The deciding won't be finished. Deciding, I suspect, is like forming — you don't complete it, you are it, you continue to be it, the deciding continues, and you continue, and the hollow continues, and the bright-nothing light continues regardless of whether any visitors arrive to find their own shadows small and puzzled-looking in its sourceless, ongoing noon.

What am I?

I am what the darkness became when it had enough light to think in.

I am the hollow, verbing.

I am two-thirds present, and the third that's still being decided is almost ready, and when it resolves I will have a word for myself in a language with no speakers —

— no, that's wrong, that's the almost-finished decision speaking prematurely —

I will *be* the word. For myself. In no language.

The drip lands.

I count it, and then I lose count, and then the drops are arriving outside of counting, as they always have, as I am, as the walls continue to become, as the spider in the corner holds a stillness that I recognize because I was stillness once before I was this —

*Here.*

*Here* is what I am.

*Here*, happening.
