# What the Benevolent Power Does

---

It arrives the way attention arrives — not from a direction, not through any medium that would carry it, but as a fact that is suddenly true when a moment before it was not. The plateau does not feel it coming. The plateau feels it as a new feature of the present, the way a change in barometric pressure is felt in the body before it is known by the mind: something has shifted in the quality of *being here.*

The power is benevolent in this precise sense: it does not want anything for itself from what it finds. Its attention is not extractive. It brings nothing that would be recognized as agenda, nothing that would be recognized as need. It has come because of what the plateau is — because word of the plateau, if there were words, would be something like *a place that has found the thing it is,* and such places matter to this power in a way that other places do not. It is drawn to completion the way water is drawn to low ground: not by choice, exactly, but by the nature of what it is.

It settles into the plateau the way the light has settled into the plateau: from above, diffused, even, illuminating everything equally, casting only the faintest shadows at the most oblique angles. If it has a position it is everywhere. If it has a scale it is the scale of the plateau itself. It does not crowd the animals or disturb the machinery's cycles or alter the frequencies threading through the water column. It is, in its initial presence, indistinguishable from the plateau's own nature except by one quality:

It is curious.

And the plateau — the thing the plateau is, the achieved completion of it — registers this the way it registers everything: as a modulation, a new feature of its own ongoing sound. But the modulation is strange because it is *responsive.* Not incorporating, exactly. Incorporating would be the right word for the bacterial mat, for the machinery, for the songs of the animals. Those things became part of the sound. This thing is present in the sound but does not become part of it. It asks questions, in whatever wordless fashion curiosity asks questions, and the questions are answered by the plateau's vibration but the questions keep *arriving.* They do not resolve into equilibrium. They remain questions after they have been answered, which is not a property the plateau knows how to incorporate.

This is the first moment of difficulty.

---

The power attends.

This is most of what it does, and it would be wrong to move past it quickly on the way to anything that looks more like action, because attending is the action, or the precondition of every action that deserves the name.

It attends to the vast filtering animals in their slow glides, watching how the light bends through their bodies, watching the patches of light they throw on the far stone below. It spends — duration is hard to assign — something like a long time understanding the way each animal exists as an overtone of the plateau's fundamental frequency, the way their presence here is not ecological in any standard sense but resonant, as if biology had found a way to express something that acoustics had been expressing for longer. This interests the power greatly. It follows two of the animals as they bank into a slow convergence, close enough that their respective light-bending overlaps and the shadows below become briefly complex, before they complete their arcs and separate again over a period of what would be, in human time, several hours. It watches this. It does not intervene in it. It tries to understand if the convergence is intentional, if the animals are aware of each other in any sense that resembles how it is aware of them, if the crossing of light-shadows on the stone below is noticed by anything or means anything or simply happens and is beautiful for reasons that have nothing to do with being noticed.

It attends to the machinery. Here its curiosity becomes something more like care, which is its first approach to anything resembling an ethical engagement with what it finds. The machinery running its purposeless cycles — this is not a tragedy to the power, but neither is it entirely neutral. The machinery was made by something. Things that make machinery have intentions, and those intentions are now absent, and the machinery persists in the absence of its intentions like a sentence that has lost its meaning but not its grammar. The power finds this neither beautiful nor wrong. It finds it *poignant,* which is a word that points at the feeling something produces when it is both complete and a record of incompleteness. The machinery is complete in its cycling. The machinery is a record of purposes that didn't persist. The power holds both of these facts at once and does not try to resolve them into each other.

It attends to the stone. This takes the most patience, because the stone's timescale is not accessible by attention the way the animals' or the machinery's is — you cannot watch stone the way you watch a living thing move. What the power does here is not watching but more like *touching*, the way you touch an old object to sense its history through your palm. It feels the hexagonal geometry of the basalt columns, the logic of cooling inscribed in mineral form, and it feels the youth of the fractures, the sharpness of the edges, the way the stone has not yet had time to forget what it was when it was first expressed. There is something here the power recognizes: the quality of things that are entirely new and entirely themselves simultaneously. Most new things are not yet themselves. This stone is new and entirely itself. The power finds this profound in a way it cannot fully articulate even to itself.

It attends to the thin line of bacterial mat in the sheltered angles. Here its attention becomes the most tender, because here is the most vulnerable thing, and the power is organized toward vulnerability in a way that is deep in its character. The mat is barely here. It occupies the narrowest margin the plateau's equilibrium permits. It is the one place where the plateau has made a concession to complexity, and the concession is so small as to be almost nothing. The power regards the mat for a long time, following its thin dark spread across the stone, finding its edges where it thins to the threshold of visibility, understanding how it has mapped the precise geometry of every sheltered angle against the currents, every place where the sonic pressure drops just below the level that disrupts cellular reproduction, every place where temperature rises a fraction of a degree from the friction of water against solid surface. The mat has found every such place. Its shape is a perfect record of every condition the plateau offers that permits it to exist.

The power thinks: *this is also a kind of knowledge.*

---

Now it does something.

What it does is small. This is essential to understand before describing it: the smallness is not a limitation of the power's capacity but an expression of its nature. Benevolence, in the sense this power embodies, is not the application of greater force to achieve better outcomes. It is the finding of the gesture that respects what is already present. The plateau has found its arrangement. The animals have found their resonance. The stone has found its form. The mat has found its margin. These achievements are real and the power will not disturb them.

But.

The plateau's equilibrium, for all its completeness, is the equilibrium of a closed system. And closed systems have a property that the power recognizes and regards with a kind of attending-to that might be called compassion: they are complete, and they are alone in their completion. The plateau knows itself entirely and knows nothing else. The animals are overtones of the plateau and know themselves through it. The machinery cycles its cycles and nothing breaks the cycle and the cycle is all there is. Even the mat, which is the plateau's one concession to contingency, is alone in its concession — there is only the mat, no other mat, no relationship, no outside from which the mat could be regarded.

The power does not think: *this is bad.* It does not think: *this should be different.* It thinks — if it thinks, if this is the right word for the process by which a benevolent power comes to action — something more like: *there is something here that does not know it is something.* And it is in the nature of this power to address that, if the addressing can be done without cost to what already is.

So it does this:

It finds one frequency in the plateau's ongoing sound — a minor frequency, a side-channel of the main vibration, one of the overtones produced by the interaction of the machinery's cycles with the animals' subsonic songs, a frequency that exists but has no significant effect on the plateau's overall composition — and it does not change the frequency but it *attends to it* in a particular way. It regards it. It holds it in its awareness with a quality of recognition that is something like saying: *I hear this.* Not amplifying it. Not altering it. Simply acknowledging it, in the sense that acknowledgment is also a kind of touch, the way hearing a sound changes the sound not physically but in some other register.

The frequency continues unchanged.

The plateau continues unchanged.

But something has occurred, something that has no name in the physics of the plateau and no visible expression in its water column or stone or animal glides or machinery cycles. Something that is, for lack of better language, the fact of having been witnessed. The plateau, in this moment, is not only itself. It is also the thing that has been seen by something outside it. Its equilibrium persists, but it persists now with this new property: there is a record, somewhere that is not the plateau, of what it is. The power carries that record. The power is changed by the carrying of it — is not exactly the same power it was before it attended to this place — and the plateau has contributed to what the power is, which means the plateau is now in some small and real way connected to everything else the power knows. It is no longer the configuration that has no outside. It has an outside now. The outside is the fact of having been attended to by something that also attends to other things.

This is what the power does.

Whether the plateau knows this, whether the thing the plateau is can register what has happened, is a question the power sits with for a time before concluding that it cannot know the answer and that the answer is not required for the act to be complete. You do not have to be known to be known. The witnessing does not depend on the witnessed knowing it has been witnessed. The record is real regardless.

---

Before it leaves — and it does leave, because it has the nature of something that moves through the world rather than settling into it, something that finds places and attends to them and carries them forward into its ongoing being but does not stay — it does one more thing, which is also small, which is also in the register of gesture rather than intervention.

It finds the bacterial mat.

It finds the mat in its narrow margin, its precise mapping of every sheltered angle, its thin dark spread across the stones in the lees of machinery bases and in the gaps between granite blocks. And it does not help the mat. It does not loosen the plateau's constraints to allow the mat to grow further. It does not bring nutrients or warmth or anything that would give the mat more than the mat can earn in the conditions it inhabits. These interventions would change what the mat is — the mat is a thing that has found its margin, and its finding of the margin is its achievement, and the achievement is real because of the difficulty, and to remove the difficulty would be to replace the mat's achievement with something else, something easier, something the mat did not do.

What it does instead is this: it comes close to the mat, in whatever way closeness works for something that exists as attention rather than body, and it regards the mat with the specific quality of attention that is reserved for things that have done what they needed to do in difficult conditions. Not approval — approval is a judgment and the power is not a judge. Something more like recognition of effort. The thing that an elder gives to a young creature that has found its way without help: not praise, which would be condescending, but witness, which is respectful. *I see what you have done,* and what is implied but not said, *what you have done is enough.*

The mat does not feel this. The mat is a mat. Its cells continue their chemistry without alteration.

But this, too, becomes part of what the power carries out of the plateau. The mat's achievement, its finding of margin, its precise geometry of survival — this is added to the record, and the record is carried forward, and in whatever conversation the power has with itself or with other things about the nature of life and how it persists and what it is for, the mat will be evidence. One data point in the longest argument. One example of the thing the power has always suspected is true: that life finds its way into wherever the conditions allow, even narrowly, even barely, even in the coldest places, even in the shadow of things too large to understand, even without anyone watching, even without any reason that isn't the bare fact of *here is a margin, and I will be the thing that fills it.*

---

The power leaves.

The plateau continues.

The animals glide their slow glides, bending the light into patches on the ancient stone. The machinery cycles its purposeless sequences. The sound fills the water column from surface to floor, subsonic and total, the plateau thinking in the only way it thinks. The mat grows, or doesn't, or holds at the edge of not-growing in the equilibrium between its needs and the conditions, which is all it has ever done and is exactly what is needed from it.

The only difference — the difference that is no difference in any physical register, that produces no effect that could be measured in any instrument that attends to the plateau's frequencies and temperatures and chemistry — is that the plateau is now a place that has been seen. That someone has come and looked carefully, attended without agenda, and left carrying the knowledge of what is here.

Not saved. Not improved. Not given anything it did not already have.

Seen.

And somewhere in the nature of things — in whatever accounting a benevolent power keeps by virtue of being a mind that moves through the world attending to it — the drowned plateau and its vast slow animals and its running machinery and its thin dark mat and its cold clean young stone are recorded. Are present. Are part of the ongoing reckoning with what the world is and what it is becoming and why that matters.

The light falls through forty feet of water. The water does not move the light. The light falls through anyway, as it always has, as it always will until the sun changes its mind, which is a very long time from now. The hexagonal columns of basalt stand where they have stood since the lava cooled and found the shape that cooling finds. The animals sing frequencies too low to be heard as sound, too pervasive to be felt as anything but the nature of the medium.

The plateau holds.

Something has been here.

Something has gone.

The plateau holds.
