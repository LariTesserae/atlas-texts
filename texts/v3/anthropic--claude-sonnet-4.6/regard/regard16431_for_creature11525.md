# What the Benevolent Power Did

---

It arrived the way attention arrives — not from outside, but as a quality that the inside suddenly had.

No light changed. No temperature shifted. The grinding continued. The birds circled. The mosses grew at their pace, which is to say: very slowly, which is to say: with complete commitment.

The power looked.

---

This is the first thing it did: it looked without agenda, which is harder than it sounds, even for a benevolent and wise power. To look without agenda means to resist the pull toward the question *what does this need?* — because that question, however well-intentioned, imports a frame, and the frame is: *something here is incomplete without me.* The power had made this mistake before, elsewhere, long ago. It had arrived at places and immediately begun assessing deficits, running its attention over the surface of things like a tongue over teeth, feeling for cavities.

Here it did not do that.

Here it looked, and what it found was this: something already whole in its wholeness, already sufficient in its sufficiency, already doing the thing it was doing with a fullness that left no gap through which improvement could enter. The young rock was cracking and being claimed. The chains were closed. The birds were becoming what the thermals required. The ten thousand moss varieties were precisely the ten thousand that this cracking, this cold, this specific mineral signature had selected for over ten thousand years. Nothing was waiting. Nothing was reaching toward something it lacked.

The power noticed this.

It noticed it the way you notice that a piece of music you expected to find unresolved is, in fact, resolved — just resolved in a key you didn't know existed, into a chord you hadn't heard before, a chord that is entirely consonant with everything that preceded it even though you couldn't have predicted it, couldn't have written it yourself, wouldn't have known to want it.

And the noticing was, itself, a kind of gift — not to the plateau, which didn't know it was being noticed and wouldn't have been changed by knowing — but within the power itself, a recalibration, a return to something. To the position of witness. To the hard, patient, ego-requiring discipline of being present to something without immediately recruiting it into your own story of what needs doing.

---

So: the first thing the power did was nothing, correctly.

There is an art to this. Doing nothing correctly is not the same as neglecting. It is not passivity or abdication. It is the active decision, renewed continuously, to let the thing be what it is, to not occlude it with your presence, to keep your hands in your pockets and your improvements to yourself and your gaze on the thing rather than on your idea of the thing.

The power held this position for what was, in its own time, a long while.

In the plateau's time: one breath, partial. A fraction of an exhale. The rock cracked a millimeter, somewhere to the northeast. A new colony of moss-pioneers found the crack and entered it. Three of the large birds were born, ridiculous and ungainly in their high-scarp nests, and the parents fed them with insects lifted from the two-meter shimmer until the fledglings' wings understood what wings were for, and then the thermals took them and they became what they were.

The power watched all of this. It was not unmoved. Benevolent and wise powers are not unmoved by things — the wisdom is not a protection against feeling but a context for it, a large enough frame that the feelings can happen without the power mistaking them for instructions.

It felt something when the young birds found the thermals. It felt something that does not have a word in any language spoken at lower elevations — a compound of recognition and relief and a specific variety of joy that belongs to witnessing a thing become what it was always going to become. The young birds did not discover the thermals. They recovered them. They arrived at something that had been waiting for them, that had been laid down in the geometry of their wings and the physics of this plateau's warm-air columns, and what the power felt watching this was the feeling of rightness: not that it was good that the birds were born, not that it approved of birds, but that the fit between the birds and the thermals was an instance of a deep structural truth, a truth the power knew the way it knew its own nature, a truth that said: *things can match. The world can make a thing and make the conditions for that thing simultaneously, and the meeting of the thing and its conditions is not accidental and not inevitable but something more precise than either word.*

The power felt this.

Then it returned its attention to the plateau without acting on the feeling, because the feeling was not an instruction. The feeling was a gift the plateau gave it, freely, simply by being the plateau — and the appropriate response to a gift freely given is not to intervene.

---

But.

There was something. There was one thing.

Not a deficit. Not a cavity. Something more like — the power searched for the concept — something like a *tendency*. A direction the plateau was moving that was entirely its own direction, that required no correction, that would in fact arrive where it was going with or without the power's involvement. And yet. The power could see, with its long sight, something down the slope of time, something that the plateau was moving toward that the plateau could not see because the plateau does not see, the plateau processes.

The glacier.

Not imminent. Not catastrophic. In slow-time — in geological patience, in the language of the rock's own cracking — the elevation, the latitude, the specific moment in the long climate cycle all pointed toward ice. Not the ice of an age, not the ice of extinction, but the ice of a phase: a long cold deepening that would arrive across centuries the way the thermals arrived each morning, gradually, from below, unmistakably. And in that deepening the balance of the plateau — the specific balance, the ten-thousand-year negotiation between cold-but-not-frozen and moist-but-not-dry, the Goldilocks physics that had permitted the saturation, the density, the extraordinary closed-chain sufficiency — that balance would tip.

Not destroy. Nothing destroyed here, necessarily. The mosses would adapt — some species would retract, others would expand, new ones would differentiate in the new conditions. The grazers would change. The birds might leave, or might stay and become something different, or might stay and remain what they were and simply require more thermal energy than the new cold provided and quietly, across generations, grow smaller. The system would find its new equilibrium. Systems do. This is not in question.

What would be lost, in the transition — and this is what the power had located, not a deficit in the present but a loss in the approach — was the *memory* of this. This specific expression. This ten-thousand-year elaboration of what cold and light and young cracking rock can become when given time and a closed system. The identity of this place as it currently exists: the color map of moss-varieties visible from above, the specific chemistry of the grazing-animal gut flora adapted to these specific moss varieties, the precise wingspan that these precise thermals had selected for, the sound of horizontal rain on this composite surface, the grinding at this specific frequency.

None of this was written down. Because there was nothing here to write.

---

The power considered what it could do. What a benevolent and wise power does with this consideration is different from what a merely benevolent power does and different again from what a merely wise power does. A merely benevolent power rushes to preserve. A merely wise power accepts loss as the nature of time. A benevolent *and* wise power holds both of these simultaneously and asks the harder question: *is there a way to honor the reality of change without letting the specific and irreplaceable dissolve into the general and abstract?*

There is a practice the power knew. It is not a practice with a name, or rather it has many names in many traditions and all of them are inadequate and the practice is the same regardless of the name. It is the practice of *deep witness*: not recording, not cataloguing, not the museum-impulse that wants to freeze the thing in amber and call the amber preservation. It is something more like — the power thought — the way that grief, when it is allowed to be grief rather than managed away, transforms the griever. The dead person is not preserved in the grief. The dead person is not recovered by the grief. But something happens in the grief that is real: the specific weight of the specific person is received, registered, allowed to matter completely, and in mattering completely becomes part of the griever in a way that mere knowing never achieves. The griever is changed by the grief into someone who has been changed by this particular loss, which means the particular loss has left a real trace, a real alteration of the real world.

The power did not have a name for what it decided to do. It would not have called it grief, because the plateau had not yet changed and would not change in any timeframe the power was intervening in. But the practice was related to grief's practice. The practice was: *attend completely.* Receive completely. Let the specific weight of this specific expression — the yellow moss and the rust-colored moss and the near-black, the specific frequency of rain on the composite surface, the specific awkwardness of a thermosoaring giant touching the ground it didn't need — let all of this matter completely, with full registration, without the protective buffer of categorization or comparison.

This is harder than it sounds. Even for a benevolent and wise power. The temptation is always to place the thing in context, to understand it as an instance of a type, to appreciate it efficiently by locating its essence and representing the whole by the essence. This is knowledge. This is useful. This is also, in a specific and important sense, a way of not quite receiving the thing, of meeting your idea of the thing rather than the thing.

The power had to work against this tendency, in itself. It had to keep returning its attention to the specific: *this* variety of moss, the one at the northeast edge of the plateau where a specific crack has been running for three hundred years and the moisture retention creates a micro-climate that exists nowhere else, that has produced a shade of rust-green that exists in no catalogue, that has a texture when the horizontal rain moves over it that is slightly different from anything adjacent, a sound that would require an instrument sensitive enough to distinguish it from the surrounding sound and that no such instrument exists to record. The power let this be real. Not representative. Not typical. Not important because it illustrated a principle. Real because it *was*, because it existed at this specific coordinate in space and time and was the only thing that ever would or could.

---

It did this for a long time.

The whole plateau. Not an overview. Not a summary. It went through the plateau the way water goes through the composite surface — finding every crack, every moisture film, every under-hang and micro-climate and improbable particular. The insect whose wing-vein pattern was shared by none of its siblings and would not recur in the lineage — the power looked at this wing. The specific arc of a specific large bird's thermal-column on a specific afternoon when the cloud-formations created a slightly lower pressure zone to the south and the bird compensated with an adjustment so small it was barely an adjustment, barely distinguishable from the ordinary arc — the power watched this arc. The sound the grinding made at the northeast scarp at the specific frequency created by the specific angle of the exposed face and the specific hardness of the mineral at that point — the power listened to this sound.

Not to record it. Not to preserve it. To receive it. To be changed by it in the way that witness changes the witness.

The plateau did not know it was being witnessed. The plateau doesn't know things. The power understood this and continued witnessing anyway, because the value of witness is not located in the thing being witnessed, is not a service rendered to the thing. The value of witness is located in what witness does to the witness: it expands the witness's sense of what *is*, of what exists, of what the world has been capable of generating. The power was made larger by the plateau. This is what it came for, even if it didn't know it when it arrived. This is what the benevolent and wise always come for, underneath the impulse to help: to be larger than they were. To have received more of the world into themselves. To be changed in the specific ways that specific things change those who attend to them fully.

---

And then, one small thing.

Not intervention. Not preservation. Something else, something that required the power's specific capability and would do no harm and might do — not good, exactly, because good implies a problem addressed — might do something in the vicinity of *gift*, freely given, not because the plateau needed it but because gift is what you give when you have received something.

The power took the particular frequency of the grinding — the northeast scarp, the specific angle, the specific mineral hardness — and it amplified nothing, changed nothing, did not make the grinding louder or slower or faster. What it did was: it made the frequency available. It laid it into the resonant structure of the bedrock below the plateau, the older rock, the rock that remembered the plateau's formation, the rock that was connected through geological depth to other rock, other formations, other surfaces. It let the frequency propagate, very slowly, through the deep stone, the way a message is passed not by shouting but by touch, by the slow passage of vibration through a medium that will carry it as far as the medium extends.

This would reach nowhere meaningful. It would dissipate before it reached anything with ears. It would be received by no one, registered by no instrument, heard by nothing. In every practical sense it was pointless, the gesture of a power that had found something worth marking and had no adequate way to mark it.

But it meant, as much as anything the power could do means: *this was here. This specific frequency, this specific cold, this specific expression of what rock and moisture and time and the accident of elevation produce. This was here. I was here. I received it. I passed it on to the only thing I could pass it to, which is the deep stone, which will carry it until the stone forgets, which will be a long time, which is enough.*

---

The power withdrew the way attention withdraws — not from outside, but as a quality the inside no longer had.

The grinding continued. The mosses grew. The birds circled in their invisible channels, the ones they had worn into the air by circling so long the air had learned where they would be.

The plateau exhaled.

Somewhere in the bedrock, very slowly, the frequency moved.
