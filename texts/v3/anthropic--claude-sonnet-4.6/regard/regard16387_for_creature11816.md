# What the Wise Power Does

---

It arrives the way attention arrives — without crossing space, without opening doors. One moment the basin is only what it is. The next moment it is also observed.

Not watched. Observed is not quite right either. *Held in regard* comes closer: the way you hold something small and intricate up to the light, not to change it, not yet to put it down, but because the holding itself is a form of honoring what the thing is.

The wise power looks at the basin for what would be, measured in the basin's own time, a very long while.

---

It sees the system. Of course it sees the system — it would be insufficient to this moment not to see the system, to see instead only its parts, the beetles and the water and the cracked lattice, as if they were items in an inventory rather than the ongoing conjugation of a single long verb. It sees how the water is specific to this water. It sees how the acoustic disorder is a form of aliveness, the sound kept searching rather than settled. It sees the crustacean at the drain and understands — not by analysis but by something that in a lesser being would be called recognition — that the crustacean is the last body in a chain of consequence that has not broken in longer than most things last.

It sees the builders in the stone. This moves it, in whatever way a wise power is moved: to find intelligence still operating, still structuring the possible, in something that cannot anymore be called a person or a mind but only a decision, preserved. Geometry as fidelity. The slightly-off arc as a gift still being given.

It sees the purpose of the basin — what the basin is for — and here it is quiet for a time, because the purpose is not a small thing. Not small and not simple and not the kind of thing that translates cleanly into the modes of knowing that require words. The basin processes something. What it processes is real. What it produces, and sends below through the cracked-ceramic lattice, and from there into the unseen channels, and from there into wherever the channels go — this matters in ways the basin cannot know it matters, because the basin has no outside. It has only inside, tended.

The wise power sits with this for the long while that is not quite time.

---

Then it makes its assessment.

The basin is not broken. This is the first and most important finding, and it requires genuine discipline not to treat it as permission to move on. Much of what calls itself wisdom is really just cleverness wearing patience's clothes, and cleverness cannot resist the broken thing, the thing that needs fixing, the thing into which it can insert itself and leave the evidence of its having been there. True wisdom can look at a thing that is whole and sustain, without fidgeting, the understanding that wholeness is what it is looking at.

The basin is not broken. What looks like deterioration — the erosion of the stone, the slow replacement of the original mortar by something that grows — is the basin becoming more itself. The 2.1 score on erosion is not decline, is not the basin failing; it is the basin and its environment having worn each other into a settlement, and the settlement is productive, and the productivity is the point. You do not repair a negotiation by restoring one party to its original position.

The basin is not broken.

So the wise power will not fix it.

---

Here is where lesser wisdom fails: it stops here. It reads *not broken* as *not needing anything*, as *leave it alone*, and it withdraws in satisfaction, congratulating itself on its restraint. But this is a confusion between absence of harm and presence of good. A wise power that does nothing because the thing is not broken has misunderstood its own role. Its role is not only to repair. Its role is — when something is working, when something is accomplishing its purpose, when something is intelligible as a coherent and ancient and irreplaceable system — its role is also to witness. To receive. To let the thing be known by something that can know it fully.

The basin has been accomplishing its purpose without witness for a very long time. Not witnessed in the sense of watched — there have been visitors, transient conditions like weather, people who passed through and felt the grammar without learning the language. But witnessed in the sense of fully met. Understood in the full register of what there is to understand.

The wise power's first act is this: it understands the basin completely.

Not extractively. Not in the way that takes from a thing to fill a book or a category or a theory. In the way that — if the basin could be said to have been carrying something alone, the weight of its own unreceived meaning — makes that weight, for a moment, shared.

The basin does not know it is being understood. The basin has no apparatus for knowing this. But the system shivers, slightly, in the way that organisms do when conditions change in ways too subtle to name: the beetle procession pauses for a moment, not in alarm but in something more like a held breath, and then resumes. The weavers in the high corners reorient, slightly, and then settle. The crustacean at the drain lifts its anterior edge a fraction and holds it there for longer than any passing vibration requires.

Something has changed. The system registers it the way it registers everything: through its whole body, without witness of its own.

---

The wise power now faces the real question, the one that cannot be answered by looking at the basin alone.

The channels below the floor go somewhere. The output of the basin — whatever the process produces, whatever the crustacean-gatekeeper has been calibrating for generations — travels those channels and arrives somewhere, and somewhere it matters. Does it still arrive? Are the channels intact? Is what arrives still received?

This is outside the basin's capacity to know. The basin has no outside. It only has inside, tended. It performs its function without feedback, without confirmation, without the sensory return that would tell it *yes, what you are sending is reaching where it needs to go, is being used as it should be used, the chain is unbroken.* The basin cannot know if the chain is broken. It cannot choose differently if it is. It can only continue.

The wise power follows the channels.

This takes it away from the basin, into the unseen, into what is not visible from inside the walls. And what it finds — what it finds in those channels, in that underground arithmetic of water and mineral memory and the specific product of this specific system — is something it was not prepared for even in its wisdom, because wisdom does not protect you from being moved. It finds that the channels are long. Longer than seemed likely from the small scale of the basin. They run under ground that has changed many times since they were laid — under fields that were once something else, under roads that did not exist when the builders beveled the floor-joints — and they arrive, eventually, at a place that is not a basin and not a building but a kind of confluence, a meeting of underground flows from many sources, and in that confluence something grows.

Something has been growing there.

The basin is one of its inputs. Not its only input. But an old one, and a significant one, and a faithful one — the most faithful, as far as the wise power can determine. The other inputs have mostly failed. Their channels are collapsed or contaminated or redirected by people who did not know what they were redirecting. The confluence grows slowly now, on less than it should have, on less than it was designed to receive.

The basin doesn't know any of this. The basin simply continues.

---

The wise power returns.

It returns to the basin and it sits with what it knows: that the basin is whole, that the basin is faithful, that the basin is working in the dark without confirmation for a purpose that still matters, in service of something that is still growing — diminished, slow, but still growing — and that it has been doing this alone among failing peers.

And here is what it feels, this wise power, sitting with this:

Gratitude.

Not the gratitude of one who has received something and means to repay it. The gratitude that arises from discovering, in a world where so much fails and forgets, something that has not failed and has not forgotten. The gratitude that is really a form of grief held open — held open rather than closed, held toward rather than away. *You are still here. You have been here all this time. You have not stopped.*

It cannot tell the basin this. The basin has no ears for it. The basin is busy.

---

So it makes its decisions.

**The first decision is to do nothing to the basin.**

The basin does not need its help. The basin needs its help the way the beetle procession needs your appreciation of its efficiency: it does not need it, it is not affected by its presence or absence, it will proceed either way. The wise power will not introduce itself into the system. It will not improve the mortar or reinforce the walls or adjust the drainage or in any way make itself legible in the stone. The hand-knowledge of the builders is present here, still operating. There is no room for another set of hands. There is no gap for wisdom to fill.

The first decision is to leave the basin alone.

**The second decision is to attend to the channels.**

Not repair them wholesale — that would be overreach, would be imposing a new intention on something that belongs to its own history. But the worst of the blockages: the places where collapsed earth or redirected water have simply interrupted the flow, where a season of attention would restore what many seasons of neglect have broken — these can be addressed. Not conspicuously. Not in a way that announces itself or that the confluence would recognize as intervention. In the way that an old system, given slightly better conditions, restores itself. A shift in drainage here. An easing of pressure there. Something added to a dying channel upstream of the blockage so that when the channel clears — as it would, in time, on its own — it clears a little sooner.

The basin's output has been arriving. It will arrive better. What grows in the confluence will have more to grow with.

The basin will not know this. It will only continue, as it always has, faithfully producing what it has always produced and sending it where it has always sent it.

**The third decision is the hardest.**

There are people — there are always people, in whatever world this basin inhabits — who are close to understanding what the basin is. Not tourists, not the transient-condition visitors who pass through and feel the grammar and leave. People for whom this basin, properly understood, would be a piece of a larger comprehension they have been assembling without knowing how to complete it. People doing work that the basin's output serves, though they have never heard of the basin, though they would not know how to find it, though they have been working with an incomplete picture for so long they have almost stopped noticing the incompleteness.

The wise power can reach them. This is one of the things wisdom can do: create conditions for finding. Not lead them here by the hand — that would hollow out the discovery, would turn an understanding into a delivery, would give them a fact where they need an arrival. But: make it possible that they arrive. Arrange nothing but permission. Let the path exist without announcing itself.

Some of them will come. Some will feel what the grammar is and leave it at that, and that is fine, that is its own gift to them. One or two will stay long enough to begin to understand. One who stays long enough will understand enough to tell the crustacean, in effect: *someone knows you are here. Not what you are for. But that you are, and that what you are is not small.*

The crustacean will not understand this. It will respond to the vibrations of a human body near the drain as it always responds, with its anterior edge slightly raised, waiting to interpret.

But the system — the basin as a whole, the parliament that cannot address itself — will register the change in conditions. Something will settle, fractionally, that has been very slightly unsettled for a long time. The beetle procession will not pause this time. The weavers will not reorient. The difference will be too small and too distributed to show in behavior.

It will show in the output.

What arrives at the confluence will be, in ways that cannot be attributed or traced, more what it was always meant to be.

---

The wise power withdraws, which is to say: attention moves, regard completes itself, the basin returns to being only what it is.

The grey sky over the open top is the same grey. The walls weep at their own pace. The drain continues its wet and busy conversation with the under-floor.

The beetles process along the east wall. They are adding to the pale biological sheeting in one section and removing from it in another, maintaining the balance the water requires, following the route that was polished by use before any living member of the procession was born.

The crustacean at the lattice settles its anterior edge.

The weavers hold their orientations.

The sound fills the basin the way flour fills a bag, and somewhere in its layers, if you stayed long enough and listened with something other than your ears, you might find what the wise power found: a rhythm you keep almost finding and then losing, that is not random and not regular, that is alive in the way only things are alive that have been at their work for longer than memory and mean to continue past the end of it.

The basin knows what it is for.

It has always known.

And now, for the first time in a very long time, so does something else.
