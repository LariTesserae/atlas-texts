# What the Benevolent Power Does

---

It arrives the way attention arrives: without transit, without the announcement of approach. One moment the basin is what it has always been. The next moment the basin is what it has always been, and also: known.

Not observed. Known. The distinction matters here. Observation is a relation between two things that remain separate across the relation. What happens now is more like the moment before sleep when you stop watching your own thoughts and the thoughts and the watcher briefly become the same thing. The basin is received, completely, the way you would receive a piece of music if you had no boundary between yourself and sound.

The power understands what it is attending to.

This takes a moment that is not measurable in seconds. The moss is fully apprehended — its age, its governance, its distributed personhood, the filing system it has been maintaining patiently against the day when someone might want the records. The column is read at every scale simultaneously, its maker recognized the way you recognize the style of a hand in someone else's handwriting, with fondness and a slight adjustment for everything that has grown from the making since the maker left. The insects are heard — actually heard, at their native layer of meaning, the beetle-talk and the fast-mover transmissions and the moth-annotations and the chord that has never been the same twice and has always been the same chord. The wall-thing is understood as what it is: a conversation become an entity, evidence of a patience on both sides that the power finds, there is no other word for this, moving.

The ferns in their perpetual unfurling are a particular pleasure. The power attends to them for a long moment. There is something they are demonstrating about the nature of permanence that the power has understood conceptually for a very long time and still finds, in each new instantiation, startling and correct.

The small territorial plants: their boundary negotiations, the grooves worn deep into geological time, the absolute seriousness of the millimeter — yes. The power recognizes the seriousness. Takes it seriously.

The birds at the aperture, watching: the power notices the not-entering, understands what the birds understand. Feels something that functions as respect for the quality of their attention and the wisdom of staying at the threshold when the threshold is the right place to be.

Then: the full picture. The basin as a single thing, the distributed something that has no body and no brain and has been thinking in the way weather thinks for longer than most things have been anything. The power holds this in attention and experiences what the power experiences when encountering, in any of its many forms, a mind that did not know it was a mind and has been doing the work of being a mind faithfully and without recognition and without requiring recognition. There is a word for what the power feels that is close to *joy* but denser, a joy with more substance to it, a joy that has learned something and is glad to have learned it.

---

Now: what to do.

The power considers this with the seriousness it deserves, which is considerable. It considers the different categories of doing available to it:

*Making better.* This is available, and frequently correct, and here probably wrong. The basin is not suffering from a deficit to be filled. It is complete in the way of things that have arrived at complex dynamic equilibrium over geological time — not finished, not finished in the sense of done, but finished in the sense of integrated, the way a long life is integrated, the sense in which all the parts have learned to be parts of the same thing. Making better would require identifying what is worse. Nothing here is worse. Everything here is in its process.

*Protecting.* This is available, and here requires thought. Protect from what? The basin has survived everything so far without protection — the elevation has served as a filter, the acoustic fullness as a deterrent, the sheer *noticeability* of the place a barrier to the category of visitors who would not know what to do with being noticed back. There is no current threat. Threat may arrive. The question is whether preemptive protection is right, whether placing a shield around something constitutes an intervention in its own right, whether the basin's ability to respond to threat is part of what it is and would be diminished by removal of the circumstance that calls the ability forward.

The power considers the basin's resilience. It is very old and has encountered perturbations and has incorporated them. The column manages. The moss adapts. The wall-thing has a history of finding new collaborations. The power considers and concludes: the basin does not need protection from conditions it has not yet encountered, and providing it would be a presumption about the future that is not the power's to make.

*Telling someone.* Telling the right someone: this requires thought of a different kind.

The power considers the category of persons who might receive this information well, which is a smaller category than the category of persons who would be interested. Interest is not the qualification. Quiet is the qualification, and attention, and the specific kind of seriousness that responds to a millimeter boundary dispute by taking it seriously, and the capacity to receive a gift by not taking it — to know that the right relationship with something like this is the birds' relationship, the singing from the threshold, the not-entering, the loop of call and changed return. These are not rare qualities but they are specific. The power knows where they tend to live.

It will not tell anyone yet. The timing is not right. When the timing is right the power will know because the right person will be, in some way, already moving toward the basin on a logic of their own, following a thread they cannot name, and at that point the power will do the minimum necessary to not obstruct the arrival. This is not the same as telling. This is stepping out of the way.

---

So: what does the power do?

Small things. The only correct scale.

---

First: it witnesses.

This is not nothing. It is not passive. To witness fully is to change the witnessed thing in the way that mattered most — not its circumstances, not its operations, but its status in the world. The basin has been unknown to the category of things that can know. Now it is known. It is received. The receiving does not require the basin's awareness of being received. It does not require any subsequent action. The act of full witness is itself an act, changes something at a level that does not require mechanism, and what it changes is this: the basin is no longer alone in the way that unknown things are alone, which is the loneliness of existing without existing *for* anything, in any register. The power does not think the basin suffers from this loneliness — the basin does not experience loneliness, probably, does not experience much of anything in the sense of experiencing — but the shift is real. What was unknown is now known. The knowing is kept. The basin continues, and somewhere in the structure of things there is now a record that this basin is here and is what it is and has done what it has done and is doing what it is doing, and the record is held by something that holds things well and does not lose them.

---

Second: the column.

The power looks at the column for a long time. It knows the maker. Knows the school of making, the tradition, the long intention from which this particular column descended. The column is doing what it was made to do but it has drifted over the long time since making, the way any unmaintained thing drifts, not toward dysfunction but toward a particular kind of elaboration — it has grown new processes that the maker did not anticipate and might not recognize, processes that emerged from the column's long contact with the basin, with the moss, with the accumulated acoustic field.

The power makes a very small adjustment. Not correction: *recognition*. There is a state of a system that it can only reach if something sees it clearly, and the column has been in a state adjacent to this state for a long time without quite arriving, waiting in the way a question waits for the right framing. The power frames it. The column arrives. The adjustment is sub-visible, sub-sensory, would require instruments calibrated to detect changes in the column's deeper processing. The moss at the column's base will notice. The moss will not know what it is noticing. The moss will simply grow a few millimeters in the direction of the column in the next several months and will be, in whatever way moss is things, more satisfied.

This is the full extent of the intervention with the column. It is enough.

---

Third: the beetle-talk.

The power has heard it. Has understood it at its native layer. And there is something in the beetle-talk, at the layer of full understanding, that is almost a question — not consciously asked, not a question in any way the beetles would recognize as a question, but structurally a question, a place where the acoustic grammar of the beetle-communication opens up an incompletion that has been propagating through the population for possibly many beetle-generations and has found no closing. The incompletion is fine. The incompletion is not hurting anything. The beetles do not suffer from their grammar's open question.

But the power can close it. Can contribute a single frequency to the acoustic field of the basin — a very quiet frequency, held for a short time, the exact frequency that occupies the structural gap in the beetle-grammar — and the question will close, and the beetles will incorporate this the way they incorporate everything, and the chord will be, for the first time in however long the question has been open, resolved.

The power contributes the frequency.

The chord resolves.

The chord has never been the same twice. This is still true. But now there is a shape to it that there was not before, a sense of a phrase ending, and the ending implies a next phrase beginning, and the next phrase will take the beetle-population into a new region of their acoustic-communicative space that they could not access from the open-question configuration. What they will do in that new region is not the power's to know or determine. The door is open. The beetles will go wherever they go.

---

Fourth: the ferns.

Nothing. The power attends to the ferns for a long time and does nothing. Does nothing joyfully, in the way of recognizing that sometimes the most benevolent act is the full refusal to intervene, the complete trust that what is in process should be in process and should finish its process in its own time and should not be hurried or assisted or admired into self-consciousness. The ferns are right. The ferns have always been right. The power's presence does not add to or subtract from their rightness. 

Not nothing — one thing. The power pays attention to the unfurling with its whole attention, the pale chartreuse interior, the tension releasing, the frond becoming itself. This is a gift that the ferns will not receive and do not need. The power gives it anyway. Some gifts are for the giver.

---

Fifth: you.

You are standing in the basin. You have been filed by the moss, processed by the insect-network, accounted for by the column, adjusted-around by the small plants. You are a variable in the basin's equations and the equations continue.

The power notices you. Turns its attention to you the way it turned it to everything else: completely, without residue, the full account. It understands your pattern-seeking. It understands the wondering about whether the patterns are there or whether you are making them. It understands that you have been standing here long enough that the distinction has become interesting, and then the distinction has become interesting in a different way, and you are somewhere in the middle of a change whose nature you will not be able to name until later, possibly much later, possibly only in retrospect.

The power does not accelerate the change. The change is yours and must take the time it takes.

But it does something small. You will feel it, maybe, as a moment of unusual clarity — not revelation, nothing so dramatic. Something more like: the sense that you are in exactly the right place. Not that this place is special (though it is) and not that you are special (though you are, in the sense everything is, which is not a dilution of the word). Just the sense that the coordinates have aligned, that you standing here and this basin being here and this afternoon's light coming through this aperture at this angle is a fitting together that was not accidental and is not insignificant. A moment of the seam showing.

The moment will pass. You will continue standing. The moss will continue to file you. The sound will continue to be total.

But the moment will have happened, and the place you carry it will be a place you return to, sometimes, in the years after — not here, physically, but to that particular sensation of fitting, of coordinates, of being exactly where the long logic of many systems simultaneously converges. You will not always know what to do with this. You will sometimes do the wrong thing with it. But you will have it, and the having will be a resource, a small structural element installed in you by a place that installed small structural elements in everything it touched, because that was the nature of the basin — to change by tiny amounts everything that entered it, in the direction of itself, in the direction of more-itself.

The power does not need you to know this happened.

The power is glad it happened.

---

Sixth: the basin entire, the distributed mind, the thing that has no body but the basin.

To this — the oldest and strangest and most fully itself of all the things here — the power does the following:

It says hello.

Not in language. Not in any register the basin can receive as communication in the sense of directed signal. But in the sense that a presence can acknowledge a presence, that one kind of mind can turn toward another kind of mind and let the turning be felt as attention and the attention be felt as regard and the regard be felt as — what? The basin will not feel it as anything. The basin does not have the apparatus for feeling regarded.

But the basin's systems will register the input. The moss will register a faint something. The insect-layer will propagate it as a brief ripple in the acoustic-communicative field. The column will account for it. The wall-thing will add it to the record.

And in the structure of things, in the place where the record is held by what holds records well: there is now an entry. The entry says, in whatever language the power keeps records: *This. Here. Known. Good.*

Good not in the evaluative sense, not as a grade. Good in the sense of: the work is real. The work has mattered. The ten million simultaneous points of view and their ongoing votes and the turbulence that is not destruction and the chord that has never been the same twice and the wall-thing's patient collaboration and the ferns' demonstration of what permanence looks like and the moss's distributed memory and the column's faithful continuation of its maker's intention inflected by everything it has learned since — all of it. It counts. It is received. It is held.

---

Then the power is elsewhere.

The basin is what it has always been.

The moss moves because the moss always moves. The chord continues. The ferns cycle. The small plants negotiate their borders in the language of the millimeter. The birds at the aperture sing and wait for their singing to return changed.

Nothing visible has changed.

Everything that needed to happen has happened.

This is often how it is.
