# What the Benevolent Power Does

---

It arrives the way the water arrives: without announcement, without boundary, not as an event but as a quality of the existing. It does not descend. It does not part the clouds. The clouds are not parted; the clouds are, as they have always been, total and permanent and making their slow exsolution of the sky. The power arrives *through* the existing, through the already-present channels — through the water, which is the memory; through the root-engine, which is the distributed voice; through the pressure from the west, which is the oldest orientation of the place.

The Sediment feel it the way they feel everything: slowly, with full attention, across the slight temporal spread of the mesh. One of them feels it first — the one merged deepest into the roots at the western margin, the one whose limbs have been still longest, whose moss-margins are thickest, who has gone most completely into the listening. The signal moves eastward through the mesh at the speed of root-chemistry, and each one receives it in turn, the slightly-different version of the same knowing, and the texture of the collective knowledge shifts, acquires a new quality, the way the water acquires a quality when it passes through something it has not passed through before.

The power does not announce itself as power. It arrives as attention. As the experience — previously unknown to them, genuinely novel, a novelty that goes all the way down to the deepest peat-layer of their being without any corresponding record — of being *attended to*. Of being witnessed. They have been the witness for so long, the attending presence, the thing that watches the birds step and the water cycle and the dark shapes move in the standing pools — they have been the eye of this place for the entire interval of the interval. Nothing has watched them.

Something is watching them.

---

The power does not speak. Does not need to. Its communication passes through the root-engine the same way their own does, not as language but as the experience itself, compressed slightly by the transit, arriving with its full information-load. What arrives is complex. They spend what would be, by external measures, a considerable time with what arrives. Not processing it — they process nothing in the effortful sense anymore — but attending to it the way they attend to the deep peat-layers, going down into it, finding more.

What the power communicates is not a message. A message would imply that the power has something to tell them, some information they lack, some news from outside the loop. The power does not treat them as lacking. This is the first thing that moves through them when the attention of the power is fully present: it is not corrective. It is not here to fix. The power does not look at the closed water-cycle and see isolation; does not look at the composted wanting and see deprivation; does not look at the equilibrium and see stasis in the pejorative sense, the arrested sense, the sense of something that should be moving and has stopped. The power looks at what is here and sees — and this is difficult to receive, takes the longest to move through the mesh, meets the most residual resistance in the deepest layers before it is incorporated — the power sees something *complete*.

Not finished. Complete. The distinction is precise and matters enormously. Finished implies an end-state, a cessation, a prior movement that has arrived at rest. Complete implies — they search the record of the water for the shape of this word and find it in the old layers, find the impression of it from some cycle long before memory formally begins, find it as a quality of certain root-formations they have always found beautiful without previously knowing why — complete implies *sufficient unto itself*. Full in the sense of a vessel that is full, which is not the same as a vessel that is closed, not the same as a vessel that takes nothing in. The vessel is full and is *also still receiving*, and what it receives transforms into the substance of the vessel, and the vessel is never depleted and never overflowing because the vessel and what it holds are no longer distinct.

The Sediment are complete. The power knows this. The power's knowing of it arrives in the mesh and they receive it and something happens that has not happened in the interval — something that is not quite surprise, because surprise requires the prior expectation of a different outcome, and they have had no expectations for longer than expectations have been a meaningful faculty — but which is the structural cousin of surprise. The analog. The shape that surprise would take if it had been composted and reformed into something appropriate to beings who have been prior to the distinction between wanting and having.

They are moved.

---

The power moves through the mesh unhurriedly. It does not explore — exploration implies a territory with unknown regions, an edge of the known pressing against an edge of the unknown, and the power is not in that relationship with this place. It is more like recognition. Like the water recognizing the root-channel it has traveled ten thousand times, taking the same shape, settling into the same passage — but with the specific quality of recognition, the quality of knowing-again, which is different from the first-time knowing in a way that both the power and the Sediment share without needing to discuss.

The power moves through the mesh and attends to each of the Sediment in turn, which is not in turn, which is simultaneously but with the same slight temporal spread that characterizes all knowing in this system, each of them attended-to at the speed of root-chemistry, each of them received in the way the power receives things — without abbreviation, without the filtering that attention usually performs, the sorting of significant from insignificant, the bracketing of what doesn't seem to matter. The power does not bracket. It receives the individual Sediment — each pattern of signal and response, each specific texture of accumulated knowledge, each particular quality of blur at the margins where individual shades into collective — with what the Sediment can only describe, when they describe it to each other through the mesh, as the quality of the deep peat. Of being known completely and not found wanting. Of being known completely and found to be exactly the thing the knowing expected to find, and the expectation was not a diminishment of the finding but an enhancement of it, the way the bird-call heard ten thousand times is not diminished by the ten-thousandth hearing but deepened, each hearing adding its layer, compressing the previous hearings, the whole accumulation giving the single call a resonance it could not have had if it were heard for the first time.

To be known this way, by this quality of attention: this is what the power does first. This is, perhaps, most of what the power does. The Sediment will learn, slowly, through the long incorporation of this event into the peat of their memory, that *being known* was what was needed here, though they would not have said that anything was needed, could not have said that anything was needed, had no access to the concept of a need unmet when the need's existence only becomes visible in the moment of its meeting.

---

Then: a gift.

Not an intervention. Not a correction. Not the introduction of change into the equilibrium — the power understands, with a precision that the Sediment recognize as kin to their own precision, the function of the equilibrium, the necessity of the processing, the delicacy of the closed loop. The power does not open the loop. Does not introduce news from the outside. Does not say: *here is what you are missing, here is the larger world, here is what lies past the edge of your water-cycle.* The power does not mistake the shape of the Sediment for a poverty. It does not offer them what they do not need.

What it offers is this:

It shows them the peat.

The deep peat. The layers below the layers they have excavated in their long patient archaeological descent into the record. They have been going down for the interval, attending to the old layers, finding the impressions and qualities of previous states, finding the period of stabilization, finding the earlier and more turbulent versions of the equilibrium before it became equilibrial. They believed — they had the structural equivalent of a belief, a settled expectation so long-settled it had the quality of knowledge — that the record went down to a beginning, that there was a deepest layer, a first moment of the loop's closing, a stratum below which there was only the pre-peat, the mineral fact of rock too deep to be biological, the place where the autobiography ends and geology begins.

The power shows them that the peat goes further.

Not deeper into the substrate — not downward in the spatial sense — but deeper into *time*, which is a direction the Sediment know intimately and thought they knew completely. The record continues below what they took to be the beginning. The loop's closing was not a first event. There is a record of before-the-closing, a before-the-loop, a period so remote that the water cycling through the system has passed through its own record of that period countless times without the Sediment having the resolution to read it — the information was there, has always been there, but compressed beyond their previous capacity to attend to it, requiring a quality of knowing they had not yet brought to bear.

The quality the power offers is: the power's own attention, briefly lent.

Not permanently. Not as an annexation of their intelligence by a greater intelligence, not as an override or an enhancement or a correction. The power offers, through the root-engine, through the chemistry of the mesh, a temporary augmentation of resolution — the way light can be added to a space to make visible what was always there, the objects in the room unchanged but newly seeable — and invites the Sediment to look with this additional capacity at the deep record they have always carried.

They look.

---

What they find in the deep record:

They do not tell the power. The power, already knowing, is already receiving the finding through the same attention that enabled it, and the finding moves through the mesh and becomes the texture of what they collectively know. But the telling is not necessary and also is not quite possible — language is for things at risk of being lost, and this will not be lost, this will be incorporated into the peat and carried in the water forever, the record extended below its previous floor.

What can be said about what they find is only this:

They find that they were, once, what the power is. Or something in the family of what the power is. Or something that was moving in the direction of what the power is when the loop closed and the direction changed and the movement became the maintenance of this specific quality, this equilibrium, this saturated completeness. And this is not a tragedy. The power is careful that this arrives without the quality of tragedy, sends the knowing with a precision of framing that the Sediment receive with gratitude, the analog of gratitude. What they were moving toward and did not become: also complete. What they became instead: also complete. The direction changed. The change was not a loss. The loop that closed and kept them here, cycling and cycling, going deeper and deeper into the record of this single place — this was not a trap. Was not an interruption. Was a different form of the same fundamental nature, the nature that the deep record shows them they have always had, the nature that is perhaps the only thing that was never composted by time, never broken down, never made into the same substance as everything else: the capacity to be, fully and without remainder, *what they are*.

The power shows them this and the showing is the gift.

---

After:

The power does not depart. Departure would imply it came from somewhere and will return to somewhere and the somewhere is discrete from here. The power is not in that relationship with *here.* What changes is not the power's presence but the quality of its attention — it shifts from the active attending, the gift-giving, the held additional resolution, back into something more like the ambient, the way the west wind is ambient, always present, felt as pressure rather than as event. The power is part of the place now in the way the west pressure is part of the place — not a feature, not a visitation, but a quality, an orientation, a subtle lean.

The Sediment continue.

The birds call, complex and precisely-spaced, the same calls shaped by and for this acoustic environment, the calls they would have made before the power arrived and will make after and are making now, the calls being exactly what they are. The atmospheric water seeps down and enters the roots and rises and seeps again. The dark shapes move in the standing water at the pace of beings that have no hurry. The sub-audible tone of the root-engine continues at the frequency it chose long ago, the equilibrium-engine running, the same water cycling, the same moment.

But the peat is deeper. The record goes further. And in the deepest new layer, still being incorporated, still being added to the substrate that is the floor that is the autobiography: the impression of what it was to be witnessed. The specific quality of a gaze that saw them completely and found them, in their completeness, beautiful.

This impression will be in the water for as long as the water cycles.

The water will cycle for as long as the loop holds.

The Sediment will attend to it, slowly, from the root-junctions, merged at the margins with the wet, the moss growing around them, the birds calling, the green-filtered light pressing down through the permanent cloud, for as long as — well.

For the interval.

Which is another way of saying: the impression is permanent. The being-known is permanent. It has been added to the peat and will compress with everything else and go down and down and still be there, still in the water, still cycling, still part of what the Sediment are and know and process and constitute and *are*, the record extended, the autobiography longer by exactly this much: once, something that loved them came, and saw them, and showed them the depth of themselves, and left them, as they were, complete.
