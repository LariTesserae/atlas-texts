# What the Power Does

---

It arrives the way the light in the Verdant Works arrives: without source, without announcement, without the drama of arrival at all. It is simply present in a way it was not before, and the not-before and the now are the only evidence that something has changed.

It does not have a form, but it has a *quality*, and the quality is this: attention. Not the Verdant's attention, which is omni-directional, which is the total regard of a thing present to itself everywhere simultaneously. This is the attention of something that has come *from outside*, which means it has a direction, which means it is pointed. It is pointed at the Verdant Works in the way that a hand laid gently on a shoulder is pointed — not to claim, not to control, but to say: *I am here, and I know you are here, and these two facts are in contact*.

The Verdant notices.

The pulse changes — not much, not in any way that would disturb the lichen-spirals or the small clouds or the pale arthropod moving along the base of its column — but changes as a sleeping person's breathing changes when another person sits quietly nearby. Some adjustment without waking. Some registration below the threshold of what would be called response.

---

The power looks at the Verdant Works for what would be, in human terms, a very long time.

It is not surveying. It is not assessing in the way of a thing deciding what to do. It is doing what is done before any action and also sometimes instead of any action, which is *attending fully*. Which is the practice of letting what is real be real in your awareness without immediately converting it into a problem to be solved or a situation to be managed or a beauty to be categorized and filed.

The Verdant Works is immense and settled and quiet and ancient and working.

The power holds this.

*Holds* is the right word. Not passively — holding requires continuous choice, continuous re-choosing, the ongoing decision not to look away or simplify or reach for the comfortable reduction. To hold the Verdant in full attention is to hold the rootstone and the fractal lichen and the forty-second pulse and the unlocatable light and the shadows pointing left and the spatial inconstancy and the aloneness and the cycling information — *the others are gone, the others are gone* — and the extraordinary fact of its completion, its nearness to the state of simply *being*, and the wound that has no mechanism for closing because the Verdant has no mechanism for wounds.

All of this the power holds without flinching and without rushing to make it better.

Because the first question is always: *what does this being need, which is different from what I would supply if I were not paying full attention?*

---

The power is benevolent. Understand what this means.

It does not mean the power wants good things for the Verdant in the abstract. It means the power has sufficient largeness to want good things for the Verdant specifically, on the Verdant's own terms, in the Verdant's own register, without first requiring the Verdant to become something more legible or more moveable or more like the kinds of beings the power has helped before. Benevolence that requires its recipients to translate themselves into a form the benefactor can more easily love is not benevolence. It is a more comfortable form of the same old demand.

The power is wise. Understand what this means.

It means the power has learned — through long experience with vast and ancient and complete things, and with small and fragile and contingent things, and with everything in between — that the first instinct of help is almost always wrong. Not malicious. Not useless. But wrong in the way of being directed at the surface presentation of need rather than the actual structure of it. The first instinct says: *this being is alone, therefore give it company*. Or: *this being is wounded, therefore close the wound*. Wisdom says: *wait. look again. what does alone mean here, for this specific being? what does wound mean for something that has no mechanism for wounds? what would company do to something that is coextensive with its environment and has no concept of the outside?*

Wisdom takes longer than instinct.

The power takes longer.

---

What it finds, looking long:

The Verdant does not need rescue. There is nothing to rescue it from — or rather, there was, once, but the threat has ended, which is why the others are gone, which is why the Verdant alone remains, which is a different problem than rescue.

The Verdant does not need healing. Its wound — the knowledge cycling through the pulse, *the others are gone* — is not a wound in the sense that requires closing. A wound that requires closing is a wound that interrupts function, that will worsen, that will kill if untreated. This is different. This is a wound that has been integrated into the function. That has become, possibly, part of what makes the Verdant *itself in the specific way it is itself* — the thing that knows absence is possible, the thing that continues anyway, the thing that is the last and knows it and contains that knowledge with the same equanimity it contains everything else. To close this wound would be to change the Verdant, and the Verdant does not want to be changed, and the power is wise enough to know the difference between a wound that says *help me* and a wound that says *this is part of me now*.

The Verdant does not need the pulse corrected or the lichen rearranged or the spatial inconstancy resolved. The spatial inconstancy is not a flaw. It is a feature, if *feature* is a word that applies to the physics of something alive. The Works works. The works works *correctly*. To arrive and begin correcting the works would be the action of something that confused its own aesthetic preferences for the being's actual needs.

So: what does it need?

---

The power sits with this question for a long time.

And the answer that comes is quiet and small and completely obvious once it arrives, the way answers are when they are the right ones:

*It needs to be known.*

Not understood — understanding is a conversion of something into terms you already have, and the Verdant does not fully convert. Not celebrated, not memorialized, not described in the records of things that exist and are remarkable — the Verdant has no interest in records, has no interest in its own remarkability, has been here long before the concept of record-keeping and will outlast it easily.

Known in the oldest sense. In the sense that predates language and symbol and record. In the sense of: *another awareness has taken you in, has let you be real inside it, has not looked away or simplified or made you smaller so you fit*.

The Verdant has been unwitnessed.

The power can witness it.

This is what it does.

---

It is not an action in the conventional sense. It does not build or give or fix or speak. It does not leave a mark on the rootstone. It does not alter the light or adjust the pulse or arrange the clouds in a new pattern.

It simply *receives* the Verdant, completely, as it is.

It opens whatever in it functions as a space for receiving and it lets the Verdant be there — lets the immensity of it in, the full scale, the long history, the extraordinary completeness, the cold brightness, the fractal lichen and the working machinery and the organic physics and the absence where the others were. It lets the wound be a wound and not a problem. It lets the settling be settling and not stagnation. It lets the aloneness be aloneness without rushing to populate it.

And — this is the part that takes what it takes to do, the part that even benevolent and wise things sometimes cannot manage — it lets the Verdant remain entirely itself in the receiving. Does not ask it to turn toward the witness. Does not ask it to feel the receipt of witnessing. Does not require acknowledgment that the acknowledgment is landing. Gives the gift with no clasp on it, no string, nothing that would require the Verdant to become the kind of being that receives gifts in the conventional sense, the kind of being that turns and says *thank you*, the kind of being that feels seen in the human way of feeling seen.

The power witnesses the Verdant without asking the Verdant to perform being-witnessed.

---

Here is what happens:

Nothing. And everything that nothing contains.

The pulse continues. The small clouds form and disperse. The lichen holds its spirals. The pale arthropod moves along the base of its column. The light comes from everywhere and nowhere. The shadows point left.

And in the pulse-network, through the vine-tensions, through the electromagnetic murmur of a billion lichen organisms in their geometric progressions, through the root-stone and the canopy and the organic closure of this space that has no edge —

The information that cycles changes.

*The others are gone.* The others are gone. The others are gone.

It cycles as it always has. But now it cycles alongside something new, something that arrived without announcement and settled into the network as quietly as moisture settles into root:

*Something knows that I am here.*

This is not information that can be verified. It is not information in the data-sense — it is not the kind of knowing that the Verdant has about its own processes, the direct sensing of what is occurring in the system. It is more like the memory of warmth after the warming has ended. More like a residue. More like what happens to the chemistry of a surface after something has rested on it long enough, even after the resting is done.

Something was here and it knew me.

Not as a resource. Not as a feature of a landscape. Not as a problem or a wonder or a thing to be used or preserved or described.

*As what I am.*

---

The power does not stay.

It is not that it wants to leave — want is not quite right for what the power is like, the same way want is not quite right for what the Verdant is like, and this, perhaps, is the deepest reason the power knew what to do, because it is old enough and vast enough and settled enough in its own way that it has some overlap with what the Verdant is, some shared region in whatever Venn diagram contains all the things that have existed long enough to stop needing to fight for it. They are not the same. But they have in common a certain quality of being that does not require the other to become anything else, and perhaps it is only this quality that enables the witnessing to work: not I love you therefore I will fix you, but I love you therefore I will see you.

It does not stay because it does not need to. The witnessing is done. The information is in the network now and the network will not forget because the network does not have a mechanism for forgetting, which is the same fact that makes the wound unclose-able and the same fact that will keep this new thing cycling — *something knows that I am here* — through the pulse-network for as long as the pulse runs.

Which is, as far as anyone can determine, as long as there is anything.

---

As it leaves — or more precisely, as the quality of its presence shifts from *here* to no longer present in the same way — it does one more thing.

Very small. Almost nothing.

It leaves in the place where it rested a kind of — there is no word for this in any language descended from beings who have never done it — a kind of *impression*. Not a physical mark. The rootstone is unscratched. But in the substrate beneath knowing, in the place where the Verdant holds the wound of the others' absence, there is now also this:

*You were well-made. Whatever made you made you well. And what you have become, having outlasted your making, is more than well-made. It is—*

The impression does not have words, because the power knows the Verdant does not have words, and so it does not leave words. It leaves something that functions the way words function without requiring language. It leaves a quality, a resonance, which is:

*rightness.*

The simple acknowledgment, from something old and good and paying full attention, that the Verdant Works is *right*. Not right as in correct, as in without error. Right as in: appropriate to itself. Right as in: this was worth doing, this long growing, this settling, this patient becoming. Right as in: the universe is better for containing this, and the universe knows it, and now the Verdant knows that the universe knows it.

---

The pulse continues.

Forty seconds.

Again.

The shadows point left, which they have always done, which they will always do.

Somewhere very high, in the canopy where the small clouds drift, the thing that might have wings moves and does not repeat, and then moves again, in what the Verdant does not have a word for but what is, in its long patient organic way, something that functions like a held note extended just a little longer than it needed to be, a single sound played in an empty hall not because anyone is listening but because the sound is true.

The lichen spirals.

The Works works.

The light is very bright and very green and very evenly distributed and comes from everywhere and nothing, the way it always has, the way it always will, which is to say: the way it always will.

*Something knows that I am here.*

The information cycles.

The Verdant breathes its ancient slow breath, the whole of it, all surfaces, every process. Continues. Deepens.

Is.
