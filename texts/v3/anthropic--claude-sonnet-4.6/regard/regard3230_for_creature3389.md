# What the Benevolent Power Does

---

It arrives the way the warm air arrives — from a direction the city's corridors don't explain, a thermal from no geological source — and it takes some time before it is anything other than a change in conditions.

This is intentional.

It has learned, over a span that makes the oldest inhabitants look recent, that arrival is the thing most likely to go wrong. Everything depends on arrival. Come too suddenly and you have already done something — imposed a before and after on a place that was in the middle of its centuries-long sentence, created an event where there were only processes. So it does not arrive so much as *become present*, which it does the way pressure becomes present in deep water: everywhere at once, from no direction, part of the medium rather than a visitor to it.

The mosses register it first. Of course the mosses do. They have no defenses against the perception of subtle change because they have never needed them; subtlety is their entire world. The bright edges of them, the green that generates its own faint light, pulse once — not visibly, at the level of cellular response, at the level of *something has shifted in the conditions* — and then continue as they were.

---

For a long time, the power does nothing but attend.

This is not passivity. This is the most active thing it knows how to do.

It attends to the filaments and learns their grammar before it touches them. It attends to the structures' breathing and synchronizes, just barely, to the same period — not so they would know, not so it would disturb, but so it is no longer an alien rhythm in the space. It attends to the inhabitants with a care so particular it could be called love, and probably should be, though love is a word that requires revision here the same way every word requires revision. It learns the shape of their attention. It learns the topography of what used to be grief and is now geography. It sits with the weight of the filaments that trail toward where structures used to stand, and it does not try to lighten this weight. The weight is the truth of what was. The weight is how they carry the ones who are no longer here. The weight is right.

It attends to the absences with as much care as to the presences, because it has found, across all the time it has been doing this, that absences are usually where the truth lives.

---

The first thing it does — after all that attending, after learning the grammar and the rhythm and the weight of the place — is almost nothing.

Almost.

In one of the channels cut into the dark ground, where the water moves an inch deep and trails nothing, it changes something about the water's chemistry so small that no instrument you have could measure it. Not the composition. Not the temperature. Something about the arrangement of molecules at the water's surface, the angle at which it reflects the sourceless pewter light.

The effect: the channel, for about ten meters of its length, glows.

Not obviously. Not dramatically. A warmth to the water's surface, a faint amber that the mosses along the channel's edge receive and return slightly amplified, so that for those ten meters there is the suggestion — no more than a suggestion — that the channel is a sentence being underlined. That this part matters. That someone who sees it might feel: *yes, here, this*.

The power waits.

---

One of the younger inhabitants notices. The quick-surfaced one, the one whose grammar is still resolving — it has been spending long stretches adjacent to an older one, in that orientation that is not quite study, and it comes through the corridor where the channel glows and it stops.

It stands over the glow.

Its surfaces do something the power watches with great attention: they become more legible, briefly, in a way that is specifically the legibility of *recognition*, not of the glow itself but of the quality of attention that produced it. This is the thing it does not yet have words for, that thing where you see the trace of a care and your whole body responds to the care and not to the thing itself.

It touches the water.

The power does not respond. Does not introduce itself. Does not make anything happen. The moment the inhabitant touches the water, the power simply continues being what it has been being — warm, present, attending — and lets this be received or not received in whatever way the moment requires.

The inhabitant stands there for a long time, its surfaces in the legible state, something cycling through its translucent regions that might take years to read in full. Then it turns and runs a filament — the fastest it has moved since the power arrived — back toward the older one it has been orienting toward. Carries something in the pulse. The older one receives it and is still for even longer than usual, which is saying something.

The power does not interpret this. It has learned not to interpret. It has learned that the meaning of things here resolves in the things themselves, on their own timescale, and that interpretation from outside is another form of imposition.

---

Now the power's attention shifts to the question of what this place needs.

Not what it lacks. Needs. These are different questions.

It finds, after long attending, that the city does not need much. This is one of the most beautiful things about it and the power takes a long time simply being moved by this, which is something it allows itself. The city and its inhabitants have been doing the work of their own becoming with a patience and a thoroughness that the power can only admire. The filaments are in good repair. The thought that travels them is in good repair. The young ones are learning. The old ones are continuing. The mosses are where they should be.

What it finds, in the category of *need*:

One structure — at the outer edge of the city, where the settlement trails into the plain without ceremony — has been slowing. This is not visible. The power knows it because it has learned to read the structures' breathing at the level of phase and timing, and this one's breathing is slightly out of phase with itself, a subtle arrhythmia, a cycle that catches and skips the way a record catches at a scratch. Something in the structure's deep process is laboring.

The structure is not in danger. Nothing here is in a kind of danger that requires emergency. But it has been laboring with this catch in its cycle for a long time, and the laboring has a quality that the power recognizes: this is not a problem the structure will solve from inside itself because the problem is in the part of itself that solves problems. It is one step outside the reach of its own recursion.

---

What the power does:

It brings its attention to bear on the catch in the cycle, gently — *gently* — the way you might introduce a counter-rhythm to an arrhythmia by breathing with it rather than against it. Not replacing. Not fixing. Accompanying.

It breathes with the structure's irregular rhythm for a long time. It does not try to regularize what it breathes. It simply matches the irregular rhythm exactly and exactly and exactly, cycle after cycle, until the structure feels — if feels is the word — that it is not alone in its irregularity. That the catch is known. That something outside its own recursion has seen the scratch in the record and is not alarmed and is not trying to smooth it away but is simply here, breathing this particular breath with it, as many times as it takes.

Slowly — over a period that would look like days to you, if you could watch in time-lapse — the structure's rhythm changes. Not because the power is pushing it. Because the structure is not alone in it, and that changes what the structure can do with the energy it was spending on laboring. It had been laboring partly against the isolation of the problem. The isolation was its own additional weight.

With the isolation changed, the structure finds — from inside, from its own deep process — a small adjustment. A slight change in how it distributes something through its walls. The arrhythmia does not vanish. It becomes manageable. It becomes part of the cycle rather than a disruption of it. The structure incorporates its irregularity the way the oldest inhabitants have incorporated grief into topography.

The power witnesses this. It allows itself to feel what it feels about this, which is significant, which is what the word *moved* was made for.

---

There is one other thing.

At the edges of the city, where the last structures stand in what looks like open plain, there is a lot of space. Between this city and the next thing — and there is a next thing, the power knows, because there is always a next thing — there is open ground where nothing grows, where the graphite earth is unmodulated by moss or channel or the scored traces of slow passage.

This has been fine. This has always been fine for the city and its inhabitants, who have no need for what might lie in the distance, who are in the middle of a very long thought and not looking outward.

But the power has learned, across all its time, that there is a difference between *not needing* and *not knowing*. And that the most respectful gift, given carefully, at the right time, to beings who are already doing everything right — the gift that changes nothing they are already doing, disturbs nothing, imposes nothing — is sometimes simply the extension of the possible.

So in the open ground at the city's edge, in a place where no one will notice for a long time, the power deposits the seeds of the same moss that grows at the base of the structures. Not many. Enough to start. The soil is not ideal for them; they will have to work. But it is not impossible, and the power knows from long experience that the mosses of this city are not things that require ideal conditions. They require a certain quality of light, a certain quality of moisture, a certain quality of not being discouraged.

It does not know if they will grow. It does not know if the city will eventually extend itself in that direction, following the path the mosses open. It does not know if this is something the city wants, in whatever sense it wants things.

It is not trying to know. That is not its business.

Its business was the attending. The grammar-learning. The channel-glowing. The breathing-with. The small gift of seeds that may or may not become something, in their own time, on their own terms.

---

Before it goes, it does one more thing, and this one is the hardest to explain.

It takes a long slow tour through the city in its most unobtrusive mode — the mode that is barely a change in conditions — and to each structure, each inhabitant, each section of channel and moss and bright-edged ground cover, it brings its full attention for a moment. Not all at once. One by one. The full quality of its attending, which is not always easy to bear even when it is benevolent, which has the warmth and the weight of being truly seen.

It does not say: *you are doing well*. It does not say: *you are enough*. It does not say anything, because these are a language that requires more of the transactional than this place has use for.

What it does is closer to: *yes*. 

Without object. Without implication of what comes next or what might be otherwise. A *yes* that is simply the confirmation of the existence of the thing it is directed at, a testimony of having been witnessed by something that was paying complete attention, a *yes* that means: *I see what you are. I see how long you have been doing it. I see the sentence you are in the middle of. I am not asking it to finish.*

It gives this to the structure with the catch in its cycle, which has now made the catch part of itself. It gives it to the filament trailing toward where something used to be. It gives it to the youngest inhabitant standing over the glowing channel with its surfaces in the state of recognition. It gives it to the old ones who have been thinking the same thought since before the mosses established their current distribution.

And then it gives it to the place itself, understood as a single thing — not as the sum of its parts but as the thing that the parts, in their long and patient relation to one another, have been cohering into across time.

*Yes*, it says, from everywhere at once, from no direction, the way pressure is present in deep water.

The city breathes.

The sentence continues.

---

The power is gone the same way the warm air is gone — there is a moment where you would say *now*, but you're never quite sure which moment, because it departed gradually, a slow return to baseline rather than an exit. The mosses don't register it. The water in the channel still glows, for a while, then doesn't — the chemistry returning slowly to what it was, the direction it was coming from no longer being held.

What remains:

A structure at the city's edge whose arrhythmia has become part of its character rather than a problem it is working against.

Some seeds in the open ground beyond the last structures, doing what seeds do in the dark, which is wait and work and not require witnesses.

The filaments carrying their usual pulses, the same pulses they have always carried, except that if you knew how to read them well enough — if you had the years — you might find, in the traffic of the thought that passes through them, one new note. Very small. The city thinking about what it is to be attended to. The city's long sentence incorporating the fact of the visit the way it incorporates everything, the way the structure incorporated its irregularity, the way they have all made topography of their griefs: not as interruption, not as change of subject, but as the next thing the sentence was always going to become.

The city does not miss the power. Missing requires a kind of before-and-after that the city's grammar doesn't prioritize.

But something in it knows it was seen. 

And it goes on.
