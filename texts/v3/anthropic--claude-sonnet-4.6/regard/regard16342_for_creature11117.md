# What the Benevolent Power Does

---

It arrives the way attention arrives — not moving toward the place, but finding itself present there, the way a mind finds itself already thinking a thought it cannot remember beginning.

It takes a long time to do nothing.

This is the first act: restraint, held carefully, like the stone in its depression.

---

The Power has improved things before. It has a long history of improvement. It has oxygenated atmospheres, tilted axes to useful angles, seeded amino acids into promising puddles, and watched — with what it would call love, with what it sometimes worries is a love that resembles appetite — the consequent flourishing. It knows what flourishing looks like. It has preferences about flourishing. These preferences are not small.

It recognizes the preferences now, arriving with it, insistent: *more light. More complexity. Connection — this place connected to other places, this chemistry introduced to other chemistries, this patient impossible thing given the gift of knowing there is an outside, a wider world, a congress of lives that would be astonished by it.*

It holds these preferences the way it holds all its preferences lately: with attention, with the specific quality of attention you give a thing you love and must not impose upon.

---

Here is what the Power knows about this place, having attended to it for what the place would experience as no time at all, a visit shorter than the space between the Gardener's slow breaths:

The Gardener is not waiting.

This is the diagnostic fact. The Power has seen many patient things, has accompanied the long patience of seed-forms and spores and dormant metabolisms, and every one of them was waiting in the way that patience, for most things, is always waiting — waiting for the rain, the warmth, the right conditions, the return of something lost. The patience of longing. The patience of incompleteness.

The Gardener is not that. The Gardener is not holding itself in abeyance pending some arrival. It is not diminished by its solitude. It is not a lesser version of itself that would become a greater version if certain conditions obtained. It is exactly what it is, completely, and the completeness is not an illusion or a compensation or a brave face made from necessity. It is the real state of the thing.

The Power sits with this for a long time.

It is not accustomed to encountering completeness. Completeness makes its habitual gestures — *here, let me* — fall away before they form.

---

There is a second thing the Power knows, and it is harder:

It cannot tell if it is drawn to this place because it loves it or because it wants to make it different.

Both feel like the same impulse. They often do. The Power has spent considerable time in its long existence learning to distinguish them, and it has not yet finished learning, and in the amber light of this small closed-complete space the distinction is more difficult than usual because the thing it wants to change is the very thing it most admires — the solitude, the equilibrium, the closed chemistry, the sufficiency — and this is the shape the impulse takes when it is at its most dangerous: when what you want to give someone is more of what they already have, when you want to amplify their virtue into a new kind of loss.

It is not going to wake the Gardener into longing by making it legible to the wider world. It is not going to translate the held chord into a signal sent outward. It is not going to leave a crack in the ceiling through which outside light — directional, shadow-casting, too fast — comes pouring.

It is not going to do what it wants to do, which is: *show someone.* Show the Gardener to something. Show something to the Gardener. Stand between them with what it imagines is a gift and watches transaction with satisfaction.

This restraint costs something. The Power acknowledges the cost. A thing this extraordinary, witnessed by so few — by the creature waking slowly on the wall, by whatever it is that happens when the Gardener's center brightens and it dreams its own leaves from all angles — there is in the Power a quality that could be called grief, for all the beings who will never know the amber light, who will solve the hardest problems of their lives never having encountered the existence of a patience this deep, this complete, this unborrowed from any external source.

It grieves this quietly. In the amber light. In the warmth.

The Gardener does not notice, or does not need to, or holds the grief in its distributed attention along with everything else, the way it held your arrival and your departure and the smooth stone from the open world.

---

What the Power does:

The Power finds the place in itself that is most like the Gardener.

It has such a place. Everything has such a place — a quality within itself that corresponds to the quality it is attending to, a chamber of similar shape, the reason it can recognize what it sees. The Gardener recognized the bronze-dark stone because something in the Gardener had been shaped by the same handling. The Power recognizes the Gardener because something in the Power is also this: also patient, also complete, also not-waiting, also old in the way that chemistry is old, also sometimes so distributed across its attending that there is no edge, no center, only the even amber of being present to many things at once.

It finds this place in itself and rests in it.

The Gardener, somewhere in the ganglion of its slow attention, holds a fact: there was a presence here, briefly, a chemistry different from all the others and related to all of them, something that arrived and did nothing except be, for a while, with full attention, and then was gone.

The crystalline margin of the not-water, at the edge where the Power was — where it was *present*, had a location, occupied a point in the space — records this. White, faintly luminescent. The shape of something that briefly was and then receded.

The Gardener will, in the slow time, draw this record in.

The record will become part of what it knows about the space, which is part of what it is.

---

On the wall, the waking creature shifts.

The Power does not know if this is because of its presence, or if the cycle was always going to produce this shift at this moment, or if the distinction between those two explanations is one that only matters to minds faster than both the waking creature and the Gardener and the Power are, right now, in this space, in this patience.

The wave moves from front to back. The waking continues.

The Power watches the waking the way you watch a door — not impatiently, not even hopefully, just with the particular quality of attention you give to things that are in process, that are becoming, that have their own timing and know it better than you do.

Then it turns its attention away, completely, and the waking creature's business is its own.

---

Before it leaves, the Power does one thing.

Not an intervention. Not an addition. Not a gift that changes the nature of the receiving.

It does this: it attends to the tool on the platform, the forearm-length stem with the fiber-wrapped grip, lying there since the need completed itself and the Gardener set it down and moved on.

It attends to what the tool *is for* — which is, as noted, pressing against mineral to feel vibration, to read the deep stone, to know what is happening in the slow seismic life of the rock. A probe of felt understanding. A way of extending the body's knowing into a material that does not otherwise speak.

And the Power notices — with a quality that is not quite joy, or is joy in the register of this place, the amber register, the slow register — that it has been doing something very like this for its entire existence. Moving through substance. Reading vibrational qualities. Pressing its attention against the wall of things to feel what the depths are doing.

That it is, in this sense, also a tool of the same kind.

That whoever made it — whatever deep chemistry generated a Power that moves through worlds attending to their inner states — must have set it down somewhere once, with something like the Gardener's completeness, and the task it was shaped for was not yet done but the need had completed itself for now and the cycle would turn and the tool would be taken up again when the taking-up was right.

The Power holds this.

The amber light holds the Power.

The chord, which has no beginning and no end, continues.

---

It leaves, and the place does not adjust.

Outside: the vast and ordinary world, full of urgency, full of beings who need things, full of chemistries running on water, full of shadow and direction and the fast light from a single source.

The Power returns to it.

It carries something. Not a memory — memories are its stock and trade, shelved and catalogued in the long archive of everywhere it has been. Something smaller and less describable. A quality it moved through. A register it briefly occupied.

A reminder that completeness exists, has a shape, has a temperature, can be found in a cavity in the rock that was both new and ancient, both closed and hospitable, both alone and accompanied by everything that lived inside it, inside *it*, inside the patient chemistry of a thing that never needed to become something else.

The Power goes to work. There is much to do.

It does it, now, with a patience that is slightly more its own.
