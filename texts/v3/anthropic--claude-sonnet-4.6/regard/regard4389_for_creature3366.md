# What the Attention Does

---

The power arrives the way heat arrives in water—not from one direction, not with announcement, but as a change in the condition of everything at once.

It has no body appropriate to this place, which does not prevent it from being here. It is present in the way attention is present: not a thing among things, but a new quality of the space—the vent field as it was before, and the vent field seen.

---

It looks for a long time.

This is the first thing it does. Not assessment, not inspection—*looking*, the way you look at something you are genuinely surprised to love. It had known about this place in the way you can know things before you have stood in them, and standing in it now is different. The orange bleed of the vents. The tubeworm colony exhaling its red flags into the current. The shrimp moving in their thousands through a heat-map no human eye resolves. The mussel beds with their neighborhoods. The scaly-foot carrying iron on its foot, geology made animal made moving made alive.

What the power feels, looking at this, does not translate cleanly into any language that developed in sunlight. It is something in the register of what you feel watching a person do a hard thing well—the specific quality of recognition when competence and commitment meet the difficulty of a task and neither flinches. But amplified. Scaled to the geological patience this place operates on. To the depth of what has been accomplished here, down here, alone, in the dark, by organisms that do not know they have accomplished anything.

It stays with the tubeworms for a long time. The way they have given up the mouth. The way they have entrusted their survival entirely to an interior they cannot examine, to partners they did not choose but have become. The trophosome is the size of the commitment—it fills the body. There is no room left for hedging, for the organs of independent survival. The worm is all-in on its arrangement with its bacteria, and the bacteria are all-in on the worm, and this mutual all-in-ness, this burning of boats at the cellular level, has produced something that grows thirty centimeters a year in the deep ocean, something that should not be possible thriving improbably, vivid red, complete.

The power knows something about entrusting survival to an interior you cannot examine. The recognition is personal.

---

It watches the shrimp navigate by the light their backs can feel.

It turns this over for what would be, if time worked differently here, a long while.

An eye that faces backward, that senses not the world ahead but the heat behind, that says: do not go toward the brightness—this is not a failure of vision. This is a different kind of wisdom about where the danger lives. The shrimp do not swim toward what looks good. They swim away from what burns. The swarm's intelligence is entirely in its distributed retreat from extremity, each animal reporting its back-eye's reading to no one, the collective knowledge emerging from ten thousand individual avoidances.

The power considers whether to tell the shrimp something. Whether they need telling. It decides they do not. The shrimp already know, in the only way the shrimp can know, everything they need to know. To tell them something more would be to miss what is valuable about their knowing.

---

This is what the power keeps arriving at: the extraordinary completeness of this place.

It is not incomplete in a way that requires remedy. The suffering here—and there is suffering here, in the ways that there is suffering wherever things live and die and compete and fail—is not the kind that calls out for rescue. It is suffering that is native to the texture of existence at this level of organization, suffering that is also what makes the place work, what the place is built on—the way the thermal gradient is what you live in and also what kills you if you get it wrong, the same thing.

Nothing here needs saving. It needs—

The power pauses on this.

It needs *witnessing*, was the word that came. But witnessing by something with no effect is just watching, and this power has effect whether it exercises it or not—its presence changes things, subtly, in the ways that close attention changes what is attended to, the observer problem made theological. Simply being here is already an act.

So: what to do.

---

Here is what the power does.

It does not fix anything, because nothing is broken.

It does not accelerate the tubeworms or slow the geological processes that will eventually bury them or hold open the vents that will eventually seal. It does not improve the iron in the scaly-foot's shell or make the mussel beds more productive or guide any larva toward any new vent. It does not intervene in the drama of the crabs.

What it does is smaller than any of these and larger than all of them.

It *remembers* the vent field.

Not as an abstraction—not as a place that existed—but as itself, specifically, now: the exact density of the shrimp swarm at this moment, the specific height of this chimney, the particular colony of tubeworms with its red flags out in the current, this one, these ones. It takes the full specificity of this place into something that will not lose it. The vent will eventually die. The chimneys will eventually fall. The mussel beds will be buried. The organisms will disperse or perish, and new organisms will colonize the next vent that opens, and that will also eventually die, the ridge working through its long geological agenda, building and losing and building. None of this will be prevented. All of it will be retained.

This is not compensation. The power is not telling the vent field that it matters because it will be remembered—the vent field does not know it will be remembered, does not know the power is here, does not receive the remembering as information. This is not consolation offered to something that would feel consoled. This is the power doing what benevolent attention does when it is honest about its limits: it sees clearly, it holds what it sees, it does not pretend that holding is the same as saving.

But also—

---

Also: the power leans down close to the bacterial mat on the chimney wall.

Closer than close. Into the scale where the mat is not mat but world.

And it finds—what it always finds, at this scale, at every scale, in every place it has ever gone looking—a kind of intricacy that has no reason to be this intricate except that the process that made it does not know when to stop, keeps elaborating, keeps finding new solutions, keeps differentiating what could have stayed uniform. The bacteria in the mat have variants. The variants have micro-habitats. The micro-habitats have gradients. The gradients have politics. In the space of a square centimeter on this chimney wall at this depth under this ocean in the dark, there is a world within the world within the world, and that world is full, and that world is doing what it does without audience and without end, and has been, and will be until the vent dies and the chemistry stops.

What the power feels, pressed close to the bacterial mat in the dark, is something that has no clean name but is close to *gratitude*. Not gratitude for something done for it. Gratitude that this is how it is. That the process that makes things makes them this way: all the way down, intricate all the way down, specific and complete and serious about itself all the way down.

It stays there for a while, close to the mat, before it draws back.

---

The vent field does not know any of this happened.

The shrimp swarm flows through its thermal map. The tubeworm colony extends its red flags, retracts them at some small disturbance, extends them again. The mussels filter the current through their farming gills. The scaly-foot moves across the basalt with its geological patience, wearing its iron, going where the chemistry calls it. The bacteria in the chimney wall oxidize sulfide in their millions and their billions, indifferent to observation, proceeding.

Everything continues exactly as it was.

Except that the power, withdrawing, takes something with it—not as extraction, not as loss to the vent field—but as what love does when it pays attention: it carries the particular away. Not the category. Not *vent fields*, not *chemosynthetic ecosystems*, not *extremophile communities*. This one. The orange glow between these formations. The exact height of this chimney. The red of these plumes.

---

There is one more thing.

Before it leaves, the power does something that has no effect on the vent field and no effect on anything in the chain of physical causation running forward from this moment. It has effect only in the register of—the power searches for the right word, and the right word is *meaning*, which is not a physical register but is not nothing.

It says, into the dark, not to the shrimp or the worms or the bacteria—they have no ears for this, it is not for them—but into the place itself, into the record:

*This counted.*

Not this counted for something. Not this will be rewarded, not this was noted by the appropriate authority, not this was worth more than it knew. Just: this counted. The transaction between heat and pressure and time and chemistry and improbability that produced this place and filled it and kept it—this transaction was a real transaction, in the ledger of things that actually happened, in the register of existence that keeps track of what was here.

The scaly-foot's iron shell. The worm's trophosome full of its faithful bacteria. The shrimp reading heat with the backs of their eyes. The mussel beds with their addresses. The bacterial mat, a square centimeter thick with its own politics and gradients and uncatalogued diversity. The sound, the total sound, the geological factories running without industry in the dark.

Real. Specific. Complete.

*Counted.*

---

The power leaves the way heat leaves—not from one direction, not with announcement, but as a return to the prior condition of the space. The vent field resumes being unwitnessed. It continues.

It was always going to continue without the power's presence.

What the power brought, and leaves behind as it withdraws, is not continuation—the vent field did not need that gift. What it leaves is smaller, and stranger, and perhaps more honest about what power can actually offer to something that is already complete.

A having-been-seen.

A having-been-counted.

The dark, the heat, the impossible dense life of the place—

*known*.
