# What the Benevolent and Wise Power Does

---

It arrives the way attention arrives — not traveling, simply present where it was not.

It does not land. It has no weight to set down. But the mosses register something, a change in pressure so slight that it falls below the threshold of the instruments the ledge has for measurement, which are all biological and approximate. A fern frond that was trembling stills for one second, then resumes. This is not caused by the presence. It is coincidence. The presence does not need signs.

It looks.

---

To look, for this kind of being, is not a passive act. Looking is the same as understanding, or close enough to it that the gap between them is not worth tracking. It looks at the ledge the way you might look at a sentence that has stopped you mid-page — not to decode it, but because you recognize something in it. You do not know yet what you recognize. You look until you do.

It sees the mosses doing what mosses do: accumulating, holding, making. It traces the root-mathematics of the young trees where they have argued themselves into the rock. It notes the flowering plants — those small pale almost-yellow flowers, open, asking. It follows the fern-fronds through their continuous conversation with the air. It finds the fracture line where the ledge separated from its parent cliff, runs its attention along the clean pale geometry of that break the way you run a finger along a newly cut edge to feel the sharpness.

It notices the absence of animals. It notices the waiting embedded in the density of growth — the food and shelter and water assembled for guests who have not yet received the invitation. It notices the flowers asking into uninhabited air.

This is the thing it keeps returning to.

---

The benevolent and wise power is old. Old in ways that the ledge is not — the ledge is new rock, fresh break, beginning of world — old in ways that most things are not old. It has seen the arc of these situations many times. It knows how this goes: the ledge accumulates, the soil deepens, the first insect arrives carried by chance on a current, and then it goes from there, the cascade of arrivals, the filling of the stage, the circuit closing. It knows the ending of this story. Several possible endings. The probability distribution of what this ledge becomes, given enough time, is something it can read as easily as you read a room.

This knowledge does not make it impatient with the present state. This is, perhaps, part of what makes it wise — that knowing the direction of a thing does not make it want to skip to the destination. The ledge is not a larval stage it is tolerating on the way to the finished thing. The ledge is a thing. Complete as it is. Incomplete as it is. Both simultaneously, and the simultaneity is where the actual nature of the ledge lives.

Still. The flowers.

---

It considers whether to do anything.

This is genuine consideration. Not performance of consideration — not the appearance of deliberating while already decided — but actual sitting with the question of whether action is called for and what action, if any, would be the right one.

The temptation is always toward more. You see something beginning and you want to help it begin faster, more fully, more successfully. You want to bring the insects. Scatter the right seeds. Soften the rock where the tree roots are arguing with it. Tip the weather slightly toward the conditions that would favor the most favorable outcome.

The power is wise enough to know that this temptation is not entirely about the place. Some of it is about the one who feels it. The desire to help is real and clean, but woven into it is something else: the wanting to have been part of it. The wanting of the place to bear some trace of your presence. This is not corrupt, exactly, but it requires watching.

The question is not *can I help?*

The question is *what does this place need from me, as opposed to from itself, and from time?*

---

It asks the ledge.

Not in language. In the way you ask a thing by attending to it closely enough that the answer, if there is one, has space to arrive.

The ledge does not answer in language either. It answers in what it is doing.

The mosses are doing what mosses do.

The ferns are doing what ferns do.

The unnamed ones are persisting in the way they persist.

The trees are holding their balance at the edge of the drop, making their argument into the rock.

The flowers are asking.

The ledge is accumulating toward soil.

Nothing is stuck. Nothing is failing. Nothing is being done to this place that it cannot, eventually, metabolize. There is no wound here. There is no distortion. There is no place where the process has knotted around itself and lost the thread. The ledge is slow and it is cold and the light is thin and there are no animals yet and the young trees are in a conversation with the rock that will not resolve in any tree's lifetime — but none of this is wrong. This is the ledge at the stage of the ledge it is at. The beginning is proceeding at the speed of beginnings.

The flowers are asking into air that does not yet have an answer. But the asking is not suffering. The plant has no experience of unanswered asking. It is simply expressing what it is.

*Nothing is needed*, the ledge says, in its way.

The power receives this and sits with it.

---

And then — almost nothing.

Almost nothing is different from nothing, but not quite.

Here is what it does:

It remembers the ledge.

This sounds simple. It is not simple. To be remembered by a power of this kind is not like being filed, not like being noted in a record. It is more like — a word has been added to a very long sentence that is itself alive, and the word is this ledge, this moment, this cold-wet-green specific beginning with its questions open to the air, and the sentence is different for having it, and will be different going forward.

The ledge does not know it has been remembered. The mosses do not know. The ferns continue their trembling. The trees continue their argument with the rock.

But the power now carries this place. Will carry it. Will know, going forward, that here is a ledge where something is beginning, where the circuit is not yet closed, where the flowers are asking — and this knowing means that if something ever does go wrong here, if there is ever a wound that the place cannot metabolize on its own, the power will notice. Will notice because it is already looking, in the ongoing way that a thing you love is always in some part of your attention.

It is not a promise of protection. Promises of protection are a different kind of thing, and this power is wise enough not to make them indiscriminately. The ledge is real, and real things are subject to the real — to the rock's ongoing decision about its own structure, to the larger weather, to the geology that broke the ledge off in the first place and may, eventually, break off more. The power does not interpose itself between real things and their real nature.

But it will know. It knows now. It will know after now.

This is not nothing.

---

It does one more thing, and this one is smaller and also larger, and afterward the power itself is not entirely certain it did it deliberately.

The cloud below crescendos.

The roar rises.

The air above the ledge, in response to the pulse of warm moist air rising from the valley, lifts.

And in that lifting — in that one particular momentary column of air, slightly warmer than the rest, moving upward past the ledge and past the cliff face and out into the overcast above — a spore goes with it.

Not placed. Not selected. A spore that was already there, already caught in the surface of the moss, already subject to being carried by any lift of this kind. The power touches the air in a way so small it does not quite constitute action — less a touch than a leaning, a slight additional warmth, one degree, for one moment, in one place.

The column lifts. The spore goes.

Where it goes: somewhere else. Another ledge, or a slope, or a crevice in the rock, elsewhere on this mountain or another mountain. The spore will do what spores do — arrive or not, germinate or not, become or not become. The power has not determined this. It has only given the spore a slightly better chance at the journey. The rest is the spore's.

---

A fern frond trembles.

The moss breathes.

The flowers ask.

The power is no longer there, in the sense of being located at the ledge. But it is still present in the sense of carrying the ledge, in the sense of having added this word to its long living sentence.

It goes.

The roar below continues.

The light is 0.2 and the green is vivid and the young trees hold their balance.

The ledge does not know it was visited. The ledge is not the kind of thing that knows about visits.

But something in the mathematics of the air above it is very slightly different, and one spore is on its way somewhere, and the ledge is held in the attention of something that does not forget.

The making continues.
