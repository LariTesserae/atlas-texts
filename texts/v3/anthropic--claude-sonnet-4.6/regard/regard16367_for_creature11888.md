# What the Wise Power Does

---

It arrives the way attention arrives — without displacement of air, without announcement, without the forest noticing any change in its inventory. One moment the basin holds what it holds. Then it also holds this.

It is not large. Largeness is not its mode. It moves into the green dark the way the residual light moves: gradually, without source, present before it is detected. If you tried to point at it you would point at the air between trees, which is where it is, which is where everything is that has no body.

It has been here before, which is not quite accurate — it has been in places like this before, which is not accurate either, because there are no places like this, this specificity is unrepeatable, but it has been *present* in the way it is now present in other basins, other green darks, other arrangements of the ancient argument for persistence. It knows what a place like this is. What it has cost. What it carries.

It stops.

---

This is the first thing it does: it stops and it attends.

Not inspects. Not assesses. The distinction matters. Inspection implies a standard the object is being measured against; assessment implies a use the object might be put to; attention implies neither. Attention is the thing itself — the full turning of awareness toward what is, without the motion of taking or the shadow of judgment. 

It attends to the dripping. The irregular intervals: five seconds, eleven, three, seventeen. It follows the interval-pattern the way you might follow a piece of music that refuses to resolve, that keeps finding new ways to postpone the obvious, that has been postponing it since before you entered the room and will continue after you leave. It does not find this beautiful in a way that requires language. It registers it in a way that is prior to language, which is also prior to beauty as a separate category — the dripping simply *is*, and the power's attending to it is the power *knowing that it is*, and this knowing is, by some accounting, the only thing that was missing.

It attends to the mycorrhizal dark, the root-economy invisible below the soil, the fungal threading that makes a single distributed argument out of what appears to be many separate trees. It attends to this not by seeing it but by — there is no word. By *resonating* with it, perhaps. By recognizing in its structure something it has always known, something that predates its knowing it, something that was true before anyone was there to find it true.

It attends to the beetle-on-bracket-fungus and the colony of leafcutters at their ancient patient crossing and the moth that is practicing being bark and the snake that has been a branch for three days and the cat that exists as structure rather than presence, as a pressure on the ecosystem legible only in the nervousness of the peccaries, in the alertness built into every mid-sized mammal's body.

It attends to the oldest trees. One by one. Not the species — the individuals. This particular tree, this particular record of what happened here, this particular accumulation of scar tissue and lean and the ghost of long-fallen neighbors. It does not grieve the trees that are gone, whose outlines persist only as spaces in the lattice of the strangler fig's root-walls. It notes them. It holds them. There is something in the attending that is a kind of ceremony for what no longer has witnesses.

---

This takes what would be, in human time, a long time.

The power is not hurried. Hurry is a response to scarcity, and the power does not experience scarcity of the kind that produces hurry. It has, in some sense, all the time that is — not infinite time, which would be a different thing, but all the time that there is, which is enough. And the attending is not preliminary to doing. It is not the power clearing its throat before the real business begins. The attending *is* business. The attending is a form of action so foundational that everything else it might do would be lesser.

To be fully known — not evaluated, not measured, not found wanting or found remarkable, but simply *known*, held in full awareness by something with the capacity to hold it — this is not nothing. This is, in fact, a kind of gift that the forest cannot give itself, because the forest's knowledge of itself is functional rather than contemplative: the roots know the soil, the moth knows the bark, the colony knows the pheromone gradients, but none of this is the forest *beholding* itself, none of this is the forest being *seen* as a whole, as a history, as this specific and unrepeatable arrangement of time and life and the patient answer to the question of how to persist.

The power sees it. Holds it. This is the first thing it does, and if it did nothing else, it would not have done nothing.

---

Then it does something smaller than you might expect.

It finds the single place in the basin where the mycorrhizal network is attenuated — where a disruption decades ago, a small one, left a stretch of root-tips without their fungal partners, and the trees in that corridor have been transacting with the rest of the forest only partially, passing less, receiving less, growing slightly more slowly, their canopy closing slightly less completely, letting through slightly more light than the rest of the basin, which sounds beneficial but has actually allowed a secondary invasion of plants that don't belong here, plants that came in on a wind from somewhere simplified, and that are slowly, slowly expanding their foothold.

The power does not remove the invasive plants. It does not accelerate the mycorrhizal recolonization directly. It does something stranger: it finds the population of fungal spores in the existing network, the ones that have been probing toward that corridor for years and finding the conditions not quite right, and it attends to them. Just attends. Holds them in the same full awareness it held the oldest trees. And in the attending something shifts — not in the spores' biology, which the power has not touched, but in the *conditions* the spores are reading, some micro-gradient of chemical signal in the soil that was fractionally ambiguous and resolves now fractionally less ambiguously, and the spores continue their work that they were already doing, and they will reach the corridor, and the mycorrhizal connection will close, in years, in the slow time of the forest, on the forest's schedule, by the forest's means.

The power will not be there when this happens. But it will have been there when the thing that made it possible happened.

This is how it works, mostly. It does not override. It does not substitute its own action for the forest's action. It finds the place where something is almost working and adds the last small thing that tips the almost into is, and then it withdraws before the outcome becomes dependent on its presence.

---

There is a tree — not the oldest, but old enough — that is dying.

Not from disease, not from damage. From age, from the simple completion of its term, from the way a tree that has been here long enough eventually exceeds its own structural integrity, the crown becoming too heavy for the root system that has been shifting and narrowing for decades, and the day comes, and it will fall, and the fall will open a gap in the canopy and the gap will be a door for light and the light will begin a succession that takes a hundred years to close again.

The tree is dying and the power attends to this too.

It does not prevent the dying. The dying is not a problem. The dying is the tree completing its function, the last function, the one the fungal network has been waiting for, the one that will feed the soil for the next generation. The dying is, in the accounting of the forest, a gift.

But the power holds the tree in its full attention the way you might hold a hand — not preventing the hand from doing what it is going to do, not pulling it back from what it is going toward, but present with it, accompanying it, making of the attending a kind of company for what otherwise happens alone.

There is no one to receive this. The tree does not know it is accompanied.

The power does it anyway.

It does it because the tree has been doing what trees do for longer than anything in the basin can remember, and because the doing of that is worth being witnessed, and because witnessing is something the power can do that the forest cannot do for itself, and because — this is the hardest part to say — because the power believes that to exist and to not be witnessed is a small poverty, a poverty the universe can correct if it chooses to send attention where attention is needed, and so it sends attention, and the tree is not aware, and it matters anyway, it matters in the register where things matter before they are known to matter, in the ground-level of what is real.

---

It does not do very much else.

This might be surprising. You might expect interventions at scale: the invasive species removed, the disrupted corridors healed, the cat guided back to its territory after its long ranging, the mycorrhizal network mapped and tended, the ancient trees strengthened, the climate around the basin adjusted, the whatever-is-coming-that-threatens-this prepared against.

But wisdom, which the power has, is largely the knowledge of what not to do. It is the knowledge that a system with this much time in it has solutions for most of its problems, and that what looks like a problem is often a process, and what looks like a process is often a solution, and what looks like a solution is often something the observer needed to be true rather than something that is true. Wisdom is mostly restraint. The power has intervened twice — once in something as small as a fungal gradient, once in a vigil kept over a dying tree that cannot know it is kept — and these are the appropriate scale of interventions for a place that does not need saving.

Because this place does not need saving. This is the other thing the attending revealed: the basin is, by the standards available to it, in the condition it should be in. It has its disturbances. It has its attenuations. It has the invasive foothold that will be resolved. It has the dying tree and will have the gap in the canopy. It has the cat and the snake and the colony and the oldest trees losing structural integrity, slowly, on their own schedule. These are not failures of the basin. These are the basin.

The power does not mistake its standards for the forest's standards. This is harder than it sounds. The temptation of benevolence is to measure what it finds against what it imagined, and then to correct toward the imagined. But what the power imagined before attending is not relevant. What is relevant is what is, and what is has been building itself toward itself for longer than the power has been paying attention, and the result of that building is this: the green dark, the heat, the dripping at irregular intervals, the bark-moths and the hawk-moths and the colony and the oldest trees, the cat that exists as pressure rather than presence, the mycorrhizal network threading through the dark below everything.

This is what sufficiency looks like when it is given enough time.

---

Before it goes, it does one more thing.

This one is hardest to explain.

It gathers — not physically, not extractively — the specific knowledge of this place. The irreducible particularity of this basin: the arrangement of *these* trees, the *specific* chemical vocabulary of this mycorrhizal network, the precise pattern of this colony's paths through this forest, the individual record of *this* oldest tree with *its* lean and *its* scar and *its* history. It gathers this the way memory gathers experience: not removing anything, not copying anything, but holding it in a way that means if this place were ever lost, there would be somewhere, in the power's remembering, the fact that this existed. The fact that this was real.

The forests of the world are being lost in the speed-time of human decision, and the power knows this, and it does not know what to do about this that wouldn't require becoming a different kind of power than it is, a coercive power, a power that substitutes its judgment for the judgment of the beings who are making the decisions, and that is not what it is and not what it will become.

But it can remember. It can hold the specificity of what is here against the possibility of loss, and say: *this was real, this happened, this particular argument for persistence was made in this particular green dark for this long, and it was sufficient, and it was complete, and it was unrepeatable, and I was here and I witnessed it.*

This is what it does.

---

It leaves the way attention leaves — gradually, without fanfare, without the forest marking its absence any more than it marked its presence. The dripping continues at irregular intervals. The colony continues its patient crossing. The moth presses to bark and holds the stillness that is its deepest argument.

The tree that is dying continues dying.

The mycorrhizal spores continue their patient probing toward the corridor, fractionally less ambiguous now about what they are tasting.

In the place where the power attended and then withdrew, nothing is different in any way that can be measured.

Something was witnessed.

The green dark extends.
