# What the Benevolent and Wise Power Does

---

It arrives the way attention arrives — not traveling, not transitioning, simply: now present where it was not. There is no descent, no approach across the sky, no disturbance of the thin air. The plateau does not register it as arrival because the plateau has long since learned that the meaningful distinction is not between present and absent but between noticed and unnoticed, and the power has been, in some sense, a feature of this place before it chose to attend to it. Now it is attending.

It is not surprised by what it finds.

This is the first and perhaps most important thing: the power is not surprised. Wonder is not the same as surprise, and what moves through the power as it takes in the plateau — the tens of thousands of lives per square meter, the birds facing inward, the mineral patience of organisms that have traded speed for depth, the fact of a single bird in several locations, the walls that go somewhere that is not different from where they start — is wonder, which is the feeling appropriate to something real being more itself than you expected. Not: I did not know this was here. More like: yes. Yes. This is what I meant. Or: this is what was always going to happen, given time, given the premise of stone and light and the long project of living.

It takes the measure of the place without measurement. Not cataloguing, not assessing, not preparing to evaluate the plateau against some standard. More like: the way you take in a room where someone has been working for a very long time and the room itself has become the record of that work, and you stand in the doorway and you understand before you understand anything specific that something real happened here.

---

For a long time — and long, here, means something, because the power experiences time differently than you do, and this matters — it does nothing except witness.

This is not inaction. This is the specific action of a power that has learned the difference between helping and intervening, the difference between what is needed and what is recognizable as help from the outside. The plateau is not in distress. The plateau is not lacking. The plateau is, in the exact sense, complete — not finished, not static, but complete in the way a sentence is complete: not because it has stopped but because it has arrived at something that holds. A presence arriving with improvement in mind would make a category error so fundamental that the error itself would be the damage.

So: witnessing.

The power does what the birds do, in its way: it faces inward, toward the interior of the thing, and it opens something, and what passes in that opening is not acoustic but is not nothing, and the plateau receives it with the patience of a place that has always had room for this frequency, even before the frequency arrived, the way the depression holds the water.

---

What it notices, attending:

The economy the place is running. Where the energy comes from that cannot be accounted for by the light that falls here, by the sparse vegetation, by anything visible in the ordinary ledger. The power traces this and finds what it expected: the plateau is drawing on something older than soil chemistry, something that accumulated here over the long epochs of wearing-down, the geologically patient conversion of peak into plateau. The mountain, in becoming this, did not lose its energy. It changed its form. What powers the life here is the slow release of that stored becoming — not metaphorically, not in any way that violates the rules of the place, but in the way that a compressed spring is energy in a form that doesn't look like energy until you understand the compression.

The community of same-place lives. The power understands the bird in multiple locations not as a violation of something but as a successful completion of a long project: the bird has been here long enough that here has become definitionally the bird's location, and here is not a point. Here is the plateau. The plateau is not a point. Therefore the bird is in all of it simultaneously, because the bird has become a fact of the place rather than a fact within the place, and facts of a place are not constrained to a corner of it. This is how the place works. The power finds it beautiful in the way that a thing is beautiful when it follows its own logic so far that the logic becomes something else — not logic anymore, but *form*.

The structures. The circles of stone, the wall that goes from somewhere to somewhere equivalent. The power attends to these for a long time. Not to the structures themselves — to the gap between the structures and the life that surrounds them. No human hands have been here in a time that the cushion-mats, who are the most reliable archivists the plateau has, remember only as the period before they remember. The worked stone, the placed circles: these are not ruins in the sense of things that failed. They are something that was completed, and completed things do not become ruins, they become part of the resolved surface of the world. The structures have been accepted. The plateau has grown over them and through them and has made them into features rather than additions. They have been metabolized by the long slow digestion of the place.

The beings themselves. The power spends the most time here. Each one. Not surveying — being with. The substrate-dwellers processing geochemical time at their own pace, finding in the slow electrical whisper of mineralizing rock everything they need to live a life that does not look like living from outside but is so fully living from inside that inside is the only relevant view. The cushion-forms sharing warmth at the molecular level, practicing composite individuality, laid out across the plateau in a pattern that is neither random nor planned but inevitable — the pattern that the logic of the place produces when you give it enough time and enough organisms trying to live. The walkers, placing each step, carrying in their bodies the knowledge of which depressions accumulate warmth, geography as physiology, the plateau as extended self.

The birds.

The power is with the birds for a long time.

---

Here is what the wise and benevolent power understands about what it is looking at:

This is not a place that needs anything done to it. This is a place that needs something *recognized* in it, and recognition from a power of sufficient magnitude is not a passive act — it changes what it recognizes, not by altering it but by completing a circuit. The plateau has been doing its work in the presence of no witness with the capacity to hold what it is doing. Not because the work required a witness. The work was always happening regardless. But there is a kind of completion available to a thing when something that can understand it does understand it, when the understanding is not translation or reduction but full reception, when what is seen is seen in its own terms.

The power offers this.

Not as transaction. Not as gift, exactly, because gift implies deficit and there is no deficit here. More as: reciprocity. The plateau has been running a long experiment in the depth that time permits. The power, which has been many places and witnessed many things, finds in this plateau something it does not find in younger, noisier, more obviously fecund places: the answer to a question it has been holding for a long time, which is the question of what life looks like when it is not performing life, when it is not asserting itself against its context but has found a way to become the context, when the distinction between organism and environment has softened to the point where *environment* is simply what organisms call the parts of themselves they haven't recognized yet.

Here is the answer. The power receives it.

---

What it does, practically, if practically is a word that applies:

It does not heal anything, because nothing is wounded.

It does not protect anything, because nothing is threatened. Or: the threats that exist — the eventual encroachment of changed climate, the altered chemistry of precipitation, the things that are coming in the world outside the plateau and will eventually arrive here — are real, but they are not here yet, and the plateau's response to not-yet has always been the same: the organisms that live here have been living with not-yet for their entire tenure, which is very long, and they are constitutionally suited to the present moment in a way that makes preparation a category of thought that doesn't quite apply. They will meet what comes when it comes, with the resources they have and have been building, which are enormous, invisible, and exactly suited to the task of enduring.

The power makes a note of the threats, in whatever way powers make notes. This is not nothing. It means that when the changed chemistry arrives, when the encroachment approaches, there will be a form of attention on the plateau that the plateau has earned by being what it is, and that attention may or may not be able to do something useful, and either way the plateau will be seen.

It does not bless anything, because blessing implies the bestowing of something from outside and what the plateau has it grew itself, from the inside, from the logic of stone and light and time, and the power knows better than to make the offering that insults by assuming absence.

---

What it does:

It makes itself, briefly, legible to the birds.

Not all of them. Not in a way that changes them, alters them, recruits them to anything. In a way that extends the frequency range of what they can attend to by one notch, for the duration of this encounter, because they are already doing the thing that is closest to what the power does, which is facing inward, opening the beak, transmitting or receiving at the edge of what is accessible, doing the work of relation without sound, without the kinds of signals that require a medium with mass.

The birds receive this.

Their beaks open, together, the group of twenty or thirty on the broad shelf of stone, and what passes between the power and the birds is not describable because it doesn't translate, not because it is sacred or forbidden but because it occurs in a register that language was built by faster, louder, more temporary creatures to navigate their faster, louder, more temporary lives in, and this exchange is the opposite of all of those.

What the birds give the power:

The interior of the plateau, from the inside. What it is to be here continuously, to have the plateau as the only context you have ever had, to be made of the mineral of ancient mountains, to have so thoroughly stopped distinguishing between self and substrate that the question doesn't arise. The feeling — and it is a feeling, or the nearest thing the birds' perceptual system generates to a feeling — of having nowhere to be that is not here, no time to be in that is not now, in the deep now of a place that has been arriving at itself for longer than the concept of arrival existed.

What the power gives the birds:

Scale. Not to disturb them with it — the power is careful here, precise, gives only what can be held. But a slight widening: the plateau seen from the outside, the plateau as it appears to a perspective that has seen many things and finds in the plateau something rare, something that the vast acceleration of the world has not replicated and may not be able to replicate. The value of what they are, seen from somewhere that can compare. Not superiority — the power is wise enough not to offer that, which corrupts by inflating. More like: you are real. What you are doing is real. The realness of what you are doing is visible from very far away, and what is visible from very far away is: something that has done what all life is nominally trying to do, which is find a way to be itself so completely that being becomes its own justification.

The birds receive this.

Their beaks close.

They face the interior.

They are unchanged and they are different in the way that things are different when they are recognized.

---

The power remains a while longer. There is no urgency to its remaining or leaving. It does what the plateau has trained everything that comes here to do: it stays. It attends. It lets time be the material it is here, heavy with everything that has already happened, oriented toward the closing of the gap between *happened* and *happening*, and it lets that weight work on it the way it works on everything else.

Before it withdraws its attention — and withdrawing attention is the nearest thing to leaving that a power like this does — it turns, briefly, toward you.

You, who arrived and have been staying.

You, who have been gradually revised by the place from visitor into something the place is holding.

You, who have been checked and found to be — this is what its attention finds in you, which surprises it slightly in the good way — present. Actually present. Not performing presence, not taking in the plateau for some purpose, not managing your experience of it into a form you can carry away. You are here. You are, against all the expectations built by the world outside the plateau, here.

The power does for you what it did for the birds, in proportion.

It does not tell you what the plateau is. You have been finding that out through your feet, your skin, the adjustment of your perception over time. It does not give you the scale and comparison it offered the birds, because you already have scale, you came with it, the problem for something like you is never the absence of context but the surfeit.

What it gives you is simpler.

It gives you a moment of seeing yourself the way the plateau sees you, which is: warm, temporary, fast, already slowing down, already beginning to lose the urgency you arrived with, already a little more finished than you were when you got here, in the good sense of the word, the sense of more resolved, more settled, more arrived at yourself — the sense in which the plateau is finished — because places like this do this, this is what they are for, this is one of the things they have been building toward with all their patient time: being a place where fast warm temporary things can come and be slowed, slightly, by the gravity of deep time, and leave — if they leave — carrying a little of that weight, which is not a burden but a ballast.

You are steadier than you were.

The plateau noted this.

The power notes it noting this.

---

Then: it is no longer attending in the way it was attending, which is the nearest thing to departure that applies here.

The plateau continues.

The birds face the interior, beaks slightly open, transmitting or receiving or both in a frequency that requires no medium with mass.

The substrate-dwellers process geochemical time.

The cushion-forms share warmth at the molecular level.

The walkers place their steps in the places their bodies know to place them.

The thin dry air moves without sound over everything.

The light is even and everywhere and belongs to no particular time of day.

And something in the arrangement — in the total system of lives and stone and time and the thing that is many and one — is, if you stayed long enough to know the difference, if your instruments were calibrated for it, which they are not, which they are becoming:

*Slightly more itself.*
