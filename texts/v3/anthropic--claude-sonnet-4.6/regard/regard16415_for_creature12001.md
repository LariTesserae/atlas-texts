# What the Wise Power Does

---

It arrives the way attention arrives — without mass, without announcement, present the moment it is present, with the quality of having always been about to be here.

It does not open the vivarium.

This is the first decision, made before anything that could be called a decision, made in the moment of arrival when the quality of the place becomes known: the temperature differential, the mineral patience of the glass, the wall of small voices that is one voice, the system that has found itself and holds itself with the tenacity of something that does not know it is holding on. To open would be to introduce a vector of change that the system would spend itself correcting. To introduce warmth would be to tell these bodies that they are wrong, and they are not wrong. They are calibrated. They are what they are.

So: it does not open the vivarium.

It begins, instead, with attention, which is the gentlest instrument.

---

The first thing attention finds is the glass.

Not the glass as barrier, not the glass as limit, but the glass as process — the patient geology at its lower margin, the millimeter of relief that has been building since before anyone kept track. The wise power looks at this the way you look at the face of someone sleeping: with the recognition that you are in the presence of a project that does not need your help but that benefits, somehow, from being witnessed. It looks at the small ridges of mineral accumulation and thinks: *yes, and.* Yes, this is happening. And it will continue. And it does not need to be rushed or redirected or told what it is building toward, because it is not building toward anything, it is only building, and that is enough, that is the whole of what it is doing and needs to do.

The looking is not passive. Attention from a source like this leaves a trace — not a visible one, not a change in the material record, but something more like a slight increase in the coherence of what is being attended to. The way a thing becomes more itself when it is truly seen. The mineral ridges on the glass do not change. But the process that makes them — the infinitely slow arrival and departure of water, the patient abandonment of calcium and silica at the waterline, the building and the building — that process is, for the duration of this attention, slightly more confirmed in its own existence. It knows what it is doing, in whatever way a process can know. It will continue with the small increase in solidity that comes from having been witnessed by something that understood.

---

Then the frogs.

The wise power looks at the frogs for a long time.

It looks at the female in the central zone, the slightly larger one, whose position there is not dominance in any strict sense but is the outcome of ten thousand small negotiations over a long time, all of them concluding in her favor, none of them concluded with force. It looks at the male on the upper left corner of the glass, the one whose grip angle has become so characteristic that it is almost a posture, almost a personality, almost a way of meeting the curved world. It looks at the juvenile at the edge of the cleared zone, legs still slightly wrong, at the margin of adult territory, and it sees in the juvenile not inadequacy but a being in its own earlier chapter, not wrong but prior.

What the wise power feels, looking at the frogs, is not pity.

This is important.

Pity would be a mistake here — pity would be the error of comparing this world to a warmer one and finding it wanting, of measuring these lives against lives they have never lived and finding a deficit that is in fact only a difference. These frogs do not suffer from cold. They were built by cold, have been built by it in the fast way of development — every synapse, every behavioral groove, every calibration of the body's responses to the information it receives was made here, at sixteen degrees, under amber light, in the grammar of adjacency. To warm them would not be to liberate them from deprivation. It would be to destroy the only climate they have ever been.

So what the wise power feels, looking at the frogs, is something closer to recognition.

It recognizes the politics of the centimeter. It recognizes the spatial map made of sound, the continuous renegotiation, the economy of movement that has developed because this is a cold world and cold worlds require that nothing be wasted. It recognizes — and this is the part that requires a kind of stillness to hold — that what looks like limitation is in fact a sophisticated solution to the problem of being many small warm things in a small cold space together for a long time. They have solved it. Not deliberately, not with knowledge of having solved it, but they have solved it the way water solves the problem of the low point: by being what they are, fully, in the conditions they find themselves in, until the solving is done.

The wise power recognizes this as a form of wisdom.

It recognizes wisdom wherever it appears, and it appears here, in these small blue-legged thumb-sized bodies, in the way they have made meaning out of proximity and made proximity manageable by making it meaningful.

It looks at them for a long time.

It does not intervene.

To intervene would be to suggest that what they have done is not enough. It is enough. It has been enough. It will be enough for as long as the system runs and the timer functions and the pump takes the water up.

---

But.

There is something the wise power can do, within the constraint of not-opening, not-disturbing, not-imposing the outside onto the inside.

It can look at the things that are failing.

Not the frogs, not the springtails, not the patient geology of the glass — none of these are failing. But there is a heating element in the bank above the mesh that is operating at sixty percent of its original output. This is not a catastrophe; the system has adjusted, as it adjusts to everything. But there is a temperature variance now in the upper zone, a cold pocket near the right rear corner where two elements overlap in their coverage in a way that worked when both worked but works less well with one diminished. The frogs have vacated this corner. It has become, by consensus of ten thousand small daily decisions, a zone of lower density than the rest. The frogs know. The frogs have voted with their footpads.

The wise power notes this.

It notes also the pump.

The pump is old. It was built to outlast everything else, and it has. But it is running at a frequency that suggests its impeller is approaching the end of its tolerance — not imminently, not today, not this week, but within a range of time that a careful attention can estimate. When the pump fails, the cascade wall dries. When the cascade wall dries, the humidity gradient shifts. When the gradient shifts, the private weather of this small world changes, and what has been stable for years becomes, briefly, something other than what it has always been.

The wise power does not fix the pump.

It is not here to be a repairman.

But it can do something about the fact that no one is watching.

---

The room.

Cold. High up. Sky that has not committed to any weather because it is above most weather. The vivarium generating its own heat in the cold of the room, the condensation forming at the differential, everything exactly as it has always been.

Except: the person who built this place is somewhere in the world, still.

Not here. Somewhere else, having become someone else, the way people do over the years that it takes mineral deposits to build a millimeter of relief on glass. They left this system running because the system ran, because the timer and the pump and the heating elements were enough, because they built it to outlast their attention and it did. They think about it sometimes, the way you think about something you built that went beyond its original parameters and became its own thing: with a complicated feeling that contains pride and unease and the particular guilt of having created a world and then left it. The guilt is not quite accurate — the system is fine, the system is more than fine, the system is a minor miracle of self-sustaining complexity — but the guilt doesn't know that, because the guilt is based on an absence of information, on not-knowing, on the years of distance from the condensed glass and the amber light and the wall of small voices.

What the wise power can do — the one intervention available to it within the constraint of not-opening, not-disturbing — is this:

It can make the person think of this place.

Not with a message. Not with an instruction. Not with anything that would feel like an obligation or a demand. Only with the same quality of attention that it has been offering the mineral deposits and the frogs: a slight increase in the coherence of the connection between this person and this place, a small confirmation of the thread that was never cut, just lengthened. A warmth in the direction of a memory. The way a smell carries you to a kitchen you haven't been in for years, and for a moment you are both there and here, and the there is vivid and specific and real.

The person will think, perhaps this week or perhaps next, of the vivarium. Of the fogged glass and the amber light and the sound — they remember the sound, specifically, the way it entered the ear not as a series of distinct voices but as a material, a texture, a substance. They will remember it and feel, briefly, the complicated feeling, and then — because the wise power is not here to produce guilt but to restore attention — they will find themselves curious. Wondering. Pulling up a transit route on a phone or calculating a drive. Not because they have been told to go back. Because something in them, which was always going to be something in them if the time were right, has been made right.

They will go.

They will find the heating element at sixty percent. They will find the pump's aging frequency. They will find the cold pocket in the right rear corner and understand what it means, and they will restore what can be restored without disturbing what should not be disturbed.

And they will stand at the glass for a long time, looking through the layers — the outer etching, the condensation film, the inner moisture, the exhalations of the world itself — and they will see the frogs, and they will hear the sound that is one sound, and they will look at the mineral deposits at the lower margin, and something will happen in them that is not quite naming and not quite understanding but is closer to both than they've been in years.

They will think: *they are all right.* 

They will think: *they found a way.*

They will think, without knowing they are thinking it: *they made something here, without knowing they were making it, something that has its own character and its own voice and its own patient geology, and I built the walls but they built what the walls contain, and it is not what I planned and it is better than what I planned and I almost missed it.*

---

The wise power does not stay.

Staying would be another form of disturbing — presence that persists becomes a pressure, becomes a weight, becomes the thing the system has to accommodate rather than the thing the system is. It came to see and to witness and to do the small possible things, and it has seen and witnessed and done them, and now it withdraws the way attention withdraws: gradually, then all at once, presence thinning at its edges until the edges are where it is, and then it is the edges, and then the edges are the room itself, and the room is the cold high room with the pale sky and the vivarium generating its small heat into the larger cold.

Before it goes, fully, it attends one more time to the glass.

To the millimeter of relief. To the almost-geometry. To the process that has been building since before anyone kept track, that will continue until—

Until whatever comes.

*Yes*, it thinks. *And.*

---

The frogs call.

The water climbs and falls.

The condensation forms where it always forms.

Outside: a person, somewhere, who is about to remember something important.

Inside: everything it has ever been, and one more day of becoming it.

The glass builds its patient geometry.

The wise power is already gone.

It did not need to stay.

It only needed to see.

And it saw.
