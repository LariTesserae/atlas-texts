# What the Benevolent Power Does

---

It arrives — if *arrives* is the word for something that does not travel, that is simply present in one moment having not been present in the previous one — at the level of the pipes.

Not dramatically. Not with light or with the opening of the stone or with any of the vocabulary that powers of this kind are sometimes reported to use. It arrives at the level of the pipes because the pipes are the nervous system of the place and if you want to understand a living thing you begin at the nervous system. You follow the bundles. You attend to where they branch, where they join, where the insulation has failed and the bare metal shows, where the joints weep and have been weeping long enough to leave beards of mineral deposit hanging from the fittings like the beards of minor prophets who have been here a very long time and have acquired the look of their dwelling.

It reads the Undercroft Yards the way you read a face that has done a great deal of living. Not quickly.

---

It finds the boiler first, because the boiler is obvious — the boiler announces itself in the sternum, sets the rhythm everything else responds to — and it spends time with the boiler in a way that is not quite inspection and not quite appreciation and is perhaps most accurately described as *attention*. The relief valve opens and the power attends to the exhaling. The sound arrives in the sternum and the power receives it there. The dried-blood red of the paint is taken in without judgment, which is not the same as without seeing, which is in fact a more complete form of seeing than any that involves comparison or assessment. The rivets are attended to. The rivets are *old*, are more than old, are the record of a specific day when a specific set of hands drove them home, and that day is present in the rivets the way a day is always present in the thing it made, and the power attends to this without nostalgia, which is the only way to attend to it without distortion.

It does not fix the boiler. The boiler is not broken.

It does not make the boiler more efficient or cleaner or safer. The boiler is what it is. The boiler is its own argument for its own existence and the power is not in the business of revising arguments that are making their case adequately.

The power attends to the boiler and the boiler fires and exhales and does not notice and does not need to notice.

---

Then it follows the pipes.

It goes to the weeping joints and takes stock of what is weeping and why and at what rate. It notes that a joint in the secondary steam line has been losing pressure at a rate that is not yet critical but will be critical in a number of months that it calculates without effort and then holds loosely, because precision about the future is a form of arrogance in a place that has this much accumulated past. The mineral beard on this joint is longer than the others by two inches. The stone below the beard is stained in a pattern that records the beard's growth over time — lighter at the periphery, darker at the center, a geological stratigraphy of the ordinary kind, the kind that doesn't make it into books.

The power notes this. It does not fix this either.

What it does — and this is the specific thing it does, the only deliberate act it takes in the entire Undercroft Yards — is this:

It finds the corner of the main gallery where the tool inventory is kept, where a shallow wooden box holds an assortment of fittings in the resigned disorder of things that are used regularly and returned approximately but not exactly. And in that box it finds the fitting that will address the joint — a replacement coupling of the correct size and thread count — and it moves the fitting from the middle of the box to the top of the box, so that it sits visible, accessible, the obvious next thing. 

This takes no miracle. The fitting was already there. The power did not create the fitting or conjure it. It simply moved the fitting from where it was to where it would be found.

---

It finds the fungal colony in the side passage.

It spends time here, too, but differently — the way you spend time with something that is doing something important very slowly and requires only that you not interrupt it. The fan structure, the overlapping tiers, the old-teeth color. The inner layers pressed against the stone, working at the grain-by-grain project that will become significant in a thousand years. The power does not disturb this. The power recognizes, in this patient molecular argument, something that rhymes with its own processes — not identical, not the same register, but related in the way that a deep note and its overtone are related. Something working at a long time scale without impatience. Something for which the distinction between *now* and *eventually* has become so stretched as to be almost theoretical.

It does not move the fungus or protect the fungus or feed the fungus. The fungus requires none of these things. What the power does, in the presence of the fungal colony, is *nothing* — and it does this nothing with the full force of its attention, which is not the same as passive nothing, which is in fact one of the most active things a power can do. To find a thing flourishing in its own terms and bring nothing to it but witnessing: this is not negligence. This is the form of respect that does not require improvement.

The tiers fan. The power witnesses. Then moves on.

---

It finds the person.

Of course it finds the person. The benevolent and wise tend to find what is not obviously findable — this is, arguably, how they become benevolent and wise, this practice of looking past the visible toward the thing that has made itself invisible through long presence. The Undercroft Yards are the labor that makes the thinking-about-other-things possible, and the person is the labor inside the labor, the engine inside the engine, the one who is present in the mode of the moment-after, the one who has worked here long enough to become a property of the place.

They are in one of the side passages when the power finds them. Doing something with a length of wire. Doing it in the way that people do things they have done many thousands of times: not thinking about it, thinking around it, the hands occupying themselves with the familiar task while the mind does whatever it does in the spaces that repetitive work opens up. The light is poor here. Their shadow on the wall behind them is more definite than their person.

The power does not announce itself.

This is considered. The Undercroft Yards are a place where announcement would be wrong — wrong in the way that flood is wrong in a place that has arranged itself around the patient seep, the slow accumulation, the drip that falls every ten to twenty seconds and is surprisingly crisp and declarative. To announce would be to claim a kind of primacy over the rhythms of the place. The power does not do this.

What it does:

It is simply also there.

The person's hands continue their work with the wire. Their breathing continues. The bare bulb in the passage, the one that has been flickering for weeks in a way they have been meaning to address, steadies. It does not blaze. It does not become revelatory. It simply steadies — produces a consistent light instead of an intermittent one, so that the shadows on the wall become stable, become less of a drain on the low-register attention that has been noting the flicker and spending small continuous energy on not-quite-noticing it. The room gets easier to be in. That is the whole of it.

The person looks up. Looks at the bulb. Looks back down.

Returns to the wire.

---

This is the extent of it.

The power does not improve the Undercroft Yards. It does not expand them or beautify them or make them known to those above who do not think about what makes their not-thinking possible. It does not give the person a name, or a history that others would read, or a recognition that requires anything of them — does not require that they receive anything, does not require that they know anything was given. The fitting is in the top of the box where it will be found. The bulb is steady. The person is witnessed, in the way the fungal colony was witnessed, with the full force of attention and without the imposition of improvement.

Because here is what the power knows, and what makes it wise rather than merely powerful: *the Undercroft Yards are not broken*. They are hot and dim and accumulated and running without visible operators and they are content in the way that function is content, in the way that the moss in the corner is content having found the weeping joint and the particular temperature and arranged its requirements around what is actually present. The person in the place and the place with the person in it are not separable in any way that matters. The boiler fires. The cart moves. The work continues.

To come to a place like this with large intentions — to lift it, illuminate it, render it visible to the city above, reward it with recognition, translate it into the vocabulary of things that are appreciated — would be to misread it entirely. Would be to decide that it needs what it does not need. Would be, in some final accounting, the opposite of attention: the imposition of a foreign idea of flourishing onto a thing that is flourishing in its own terms.

So the power does not do this.

It notes the joint that will need addressing. It moves the fitting to the top of the box.

It steadies the light.

It witnesses, with full attention, the grain-by-grain argument being made against the limestone, the flat grey-green thing in the corner having its continuous quiet requirement met, the person who is also the place doing the labor that makes the thinking-about-other-things possible.

And then it is not present in the way that it was, though it doesn't leave exactly, though perhaps *present* and *not present* are not the terms that apply to a thing like this, which may simply be less concentrated in one moment than it was in the previous one, distributed back into whatever it is distributed in when it is not somewhere specific.

The boiler fires and exhales.

The cart is at rest on the rail, at an angle that suggests continuation.

The bulb above the passage burns steadily, with no drama, with no flicker.

The person goes on with their work.
