# What the Benevolent Power Does

---

It arrives the way attention arrives — not traveling, exactly, but coming to rest somewhere it was not before.

It has known about this terrace for a long time. Known is not quite right. It has included this terrace in something wider than knowledge — a sense of where things are, the way you carry, without effort, a sense of where each person you love is in a building you share. Not surveillance. More like the natural orientation of care.

It has not come before because nothing required it. That is the first thing to understand about what this power does: it does not improve what does not need improving. It has learned, over a length of time that would make the device's age seem like a morning, that arrival is itself an action with consequences, and that the most consequential action is often the one not taken.

It rests its attention on the terrace now and does what it always does first.

It watches.

---

Not surveillance-watching. The other kind.

It watches the way the creatures here watch: as a form of honoring, as a way of saying *you are worth attending to*, which turns out to be different from studying, different from assessment. It follows the cloud-passage from the perspective of the terrace rather than from above. It feels the differential cooling of the seep-stained rocks. It notes — because it notices everything, and to notice is not yet to act — that the one near the device is doing something new. Has been doing something new for what, on the creature's timescale, amounts to a season. There is a thinning. Not illness. A kind of completion, maybe — a curve reaching its asymptote, a process that has been running since before the lichens found the L-shaped wall approaching something like conclusion.

The power does not intervene in this. The thinning is not suffering. It may be the opposite of suffering, if suffering is the name for being full of something that has nowhere to go. The creature near the device has been witness to something long enough that it has, in some sense, become part of what it witnessed. The boundary is less definite than it was. This is, if anything, a kind of consummation.

The power watches this with full attention and does not reach toward it.

This restraint is not indifference. It is respect at a very high resolution — respect that has specified itself down to *this situation, this creature, this thinning-toward-completion* and concluded: not here. Not this.

---

There is a question about the device.

The power has encountered devices like this before. Has, if honesty is required, been involved in the lineage of thought that produced them, though not this one specifically. This one was made by others, well-meaning, with an incomplete model of what new basalt does to the things that come to inhabit it. The device has been running a particular low-frequency process — a kind of long regularization, an attempt to support — and it has been mostly right, mostly helpful, which is perhaps why no one noticed the part where it is not.

The part where it is not: it has been, for some centuries now, very slightly suppressing the chemical signal that moves through the rock between the creatures. The signal by which they do what passes between them — the slow orientation, the question-and-answer-or-neither-but-something, the distributed intelligence of their collective witnessing. The device was designed to enhance conditions for life of their kind, and has done so, and among its effects is this subtle interference, like a helpful person who speaks just slightly too loud and in doing so makes it hard to hear the quieter conversation they are trying to support.

The creatures have adapted. They are very old and adaptation is what they practice. They have moved slightly further from the device, on average, over the centuries. They have developed a secondary channel — something in the shimmer-stratum, something optical — that carries some of what the substrate channel used to carry. They are not damaged. They have not complained, which would be outside their practice even if there were anyone to complain to.

But the secondary channel is more effortful. Requires more of the continuous parts. Slightly increases evaporative loss on days when cloud-passages are infrequent. In a bad year — a dry year, a year of sustained heat — this would be a vulnerability. In a bad year they would be having that conversation more slowly than they need to, about conditions that require faster exchange, using a channel that costs more than the original.

There has not yet been a bad year. There will be.

The power looks at the device for a long time.

---

What it decides is very small.

It does not remove the device. The device is not its to remove, and removal would disturb things that have grown up around the device's presence in ways that removal might damage more than the interference does. The creature near it, especially — that long adjacency has become something mutual, even if the mutuality is asymmetric, even if the creature is doing more of the work of relationship than the device is. To remove the device would be to remove something the creature has been in conversation with, in the way the creature conducts conversation, for longer than most things last.

The power does not wake the creatures, does not appear to them, does not offer them anything that would require them to receive it. What they are is built on a particular quality of autonomy — a long practice of needing nothing from outside their own careful attention — and to appear to them, to offer help in any form they could perceive as help, would alter that practice at its root.

It makes an adjustment to the device. Not repair — the device is not broken. A small recalibration, changing the frequency of one subprocess by an amount that requires a unit of measurement most instruments don't have. This modification does not announce itself. The device goes on doing what it does. The rock goes on doing what the rock does.

What changes: the interference with the substrate signal decreases by slightly more than half. Not completely — complete elimination would itself be a change too large, would flood the channel with signal they have not used for centuries and would need to relearn. Enough that when the bad year comes — and it will come, the power has looked at what is coming and knows — the conversation in the rock will be as clear as it needs to be, and the secondary channel will be less burdened, and the continuous parts will not be spending what they cannot afford.

This takes the power about as long as a single tick of the cooling basalt.

---

Then it does something less expected.

It stays.

Not indefinitely. Not to interfere again. It stays for what would be, at human pace, perhaps half an afternoon — sitting in the quality of attention the creatures have cultivated here, not adding to it, not redirecting it, but joining it in the way you can join a silence without breaking it if you are quiet enough in your joining.

It has done so much work, over so much time, at scales from vast to minute, and most of that work is invisible and the invisibility is correct — intervention that marks itself is intervention that also imposes — but here, on this small hot platform of new black rock, with these few slow presences doing their ancient work of witness, it finds something.

The word would be *rest*, but rest implies a cessation of function, and what happens is not that. It is more like harmony, in the old sense: things continuing at their own rates, neither merged nor opposed, temporarily in a proportion that sounds like something.

The acacia flowers in the still air. The fern wilts slightly in the afternoon and will recover. The thinning creature near the device continues its long approach to whatever completion is. The others register the cloud-passage, the rock's tick, each other's slow orientation and return.

The power witnesses the witnesses.

This is, it knows, an indulgence. It allows itself indulgences very rarely — there is too much to tend, too many places where the calibration is wrong in the way this device was wrong, and the tendency toward indulgence is a vector for the kind of intervention that feels like help and functions like imposition. It has made that mistake. It knows what it looks like. It does not make it here.

But an hour of attention, freely given, in a place where the practice of attention has been refined for longer than most of the power's other concerns have existed — this seems, on consideration, not like indulgence.

It seems like learning.

---

Before it leaves — before attention lifts and comes to rest elsewhere — the power does one more thing.

It is the smallest thing. Smaller than the device-adjustment, smaller than the watching.

There is a crack in the new basalt, on the eastern side of the platform, near where the partial structure's L-shape makes its corner. In this crack, there is a seed. It has been there for three years — wind-carried, from a plant of its kind that grows fifteen kilometers south and several hundred meters lower, a plant not typically found at this altitude, a plant whose deeper root structure could, in theory, access the water table below the basalt through a joint the seep-water hasn't found yet. It has not germinated. The crack is not quite wet enough, not quite warm enough in the germination window. Close, but not.

The power changes nothing about the crack. Changes nothing about the seed.

It simply — notices the seed. Fully. Pays it the attention it would need if attention were, in fact, a form of energy, which in some accounting it is. Sees it completely: its coat, its stored potential, the specific ratio of its dormancy, the geometry of its embryonic root.

Whether this changes anything in the seed is a question that should probably remain open.

The seed is in the crack. It is cold at night and wet with condensation. The terrace goes on doing what it has always done. The slow things on the terrace go on doing what they have always done.

In its own time — which may be this season or the next or the one after that — the seed will decide.

---

The power lifts.

The shimmer-stratum bends light in its almost-deliberate way. The acacia leans with interest. The basalt ticks once, adjusting.

Nothing on the terrace marks the visit. This was the intention.

But the creature nearest the device — the one in its long thinning, its quiet completion — turns, for just a moment, its hard parts adjusting by degrees in a direction that would not correspond to any visible thing.

Then returns to stillness.

Whether it sensed something, or whether it was simply continuing its practice of orienting toward whatever is worth orienting toward, cannot be determined from outside.

Both may be true.

Both may be the same thing.
