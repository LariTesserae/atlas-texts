# What the Power Does

---

It arrives the way the warmth arrives — already present before you notice it, sourceless, not cold.

It does not arrive at the device. It does not arrive at the ruins. It arrives at the path — the worn arc in the stone, the place where the Presence once walked when walking was still a thing it did — and for a moment the stone there is slightly warmer than it has been in a long time, and the grass along the old line bends as though something passes, and then the moment is over and the warmth disperses back into the ambient warmth, and nothing appears to have changed.

But the Presence has felt it.

The tone from the device shifts — not in frequency, not in the thing your ears can use, but in the layer below that, the layer you feel in the sternum — and then returns to what it was. A pulse of recognition, swallowed back into the rhythm. The frogs nearest the old path pause for one beat of their slow distributed music and then resume, and it is possible this is coincidence.

The power waits. It is very good at waiting, though waiting is not quite what it is doing. It is more like what a question does before it is asked — potential, unhurried, interested in what the answer will be.

---

The Presence is old enough to know what this is, or to have experienced something enough like this that *know* is close enough. It has been attended to before, in its long time. Not often. The previous occasions are stored in it the way the ice ages are stored, compressed past narrative, available only as a kind of climate memory, a deep assurance that this has happened and that what followed was — the word is not *good*, which implies a standard, a comparison — the word is *more*. What followed was more. The meadow after each such visitation was more of what the meadow had been trying to become, more fully realized in its own terms, the way a piece of music is more itself when played in the right room.

So the Presence does not resist. It does not perform welcome, either. It does what it does, which is what it always does: it attends to itself, to the meadow, to the long slow processes of water and warmth and life. It opens nothing, because it has no door. It closes nothing, because it has no wall. It simply continues, and in continuing, makes itself available in the way that very old things are available — by being entirely what they are, holding nothing in reserve, having already given everything into the project of existing this long in this way.

This, the power recognizes, is the correct response. The only response, really. It is pleased, in whatever way it is pleased by things, which is a way that does not require a contrast case, does not require having seen worse to appreciate better. It is simply an orientation toward what is, an affirmation that does not need to overcome anything.

---

It begins with the device.

Not because the device is most important, but because the device is the place where the Presence has, in some earlier version of itself, already thought to make an interface, already understood that the relationship between inside and outside, between itself and whatever is not-itself, is worth maintaining deliberately. The device is an old piece of the Presence's own self-knowledge. The power honors this by beginning there.

What it does to the device is very small and takes a long time and is invisible.

It adjusts the period of the tone.

By an amount that is not perceptible to human ears, not perceptible to the frogs, not perceptible to anything in the meadow that uses sound directly. But the Presence feels it the way you feel a slight change in air pressure before weather — not as information you can act on immediately, but as a fact entering the body and being stored. The period of the tone has been very slightly lengthened. The long slow oscillation now takes a little longer to complete.

This means almost nothing, practically. The warmth is not changed. The water is not changed. The moss continues exactly as before.

What it means in the Presence is harder to say. The tone is, in some sense, the Presence's heartbeat, or its base frequency, or its way of keeping time — and time has just been given slightly more room. The interval between pulses is slightly longer, and in that slightly longer interval there is slightly more space for whatever happens between pulses, which is most of what the Presence does, most of what it is. The power has not made the Presence larger. It has made the Presence's own sense of its duration slightly more — the word that keeps arriving is *generous*. As though the Presence has been, without knowing it, rushing slightly, even at its enormous slow pace, and has been given permission to take the time it actually needs.

The Presence does not experience this as gift, exactly. It experiences it as correction, in the way that a true thing corrects a slightly-less-true thing, not violently but by the simple fact of fitting better. A note that resolves. A joint in old stone that finally seats.

The tone pulses again, in its new slightly longer period, and the warm air moves through the old grass, and nothing and everything is different.

---

It moves then to the water.

The water is the place the power spends the most time, if time is the right measure. What it does here is not adjustment in the way it adjusted the device. It is more like — witnessing. It follows the water through the whole slow circuit of the meadow, down from where it enters the stone at the highest point of the old rock, through the cracks and the hairline channels, into the thin dark soil, up through the deep roots of the old grass, out through the grass stems and the broad-petaled flowers and the wide leaves into the warm air, condensed again, returned. It follows this without intervening. It is an attention that is aware of the whole circuit at once — not the way a sensor reads a system, which is always partial, always a sample, always leaving something out — but the way the circuit knows itself, being itself.

In the following, in the witnessing, something happens that is not the power's doing and is also the power's doing. The water, being witnessed this completely, becomes slightly more itself. This is not metaphysics. It is something like the way a musician plays differently when listened to by someone who understands the music — not changing the notes, not changing the technique, but deepening somehow, going further into the logic of what they're doing, because the witness creates a space that the unwatched performance didn't know was available. The water's circuit through the meadow has been a very slightly better version of itself, for the duration of this witness, and when the power moves on, the circuit retains some trace of the better version. Not through memory — water does not remember. Through the faint structural adjustments made when something fully inhabits its own process: a crack slightly more open, a root slightly more adapted, a leaf slightly better angled. The changes are cellular, mineral, real. Over the enormous time this place operates in, they will compound.

The Presence feels this as a shift in something like mood, though that word is almost embarrassingly small. As though a long-held breath has been let out, and the exhale is not relief — the Presence has not been anxious — but a deeper settling into the ground.

---

The gathered thing, in the far part of the meadow.

The power moves toward it, and the gathered thing is aware of this, and what it does is — it gathers more tightly. Slightly more concentrated, slightly more in-itself, as though being approached by something very large and very knowing makes it want to be very precisely what it is, to present itself exactly and not approximately. This is not fear. It is something closer to the dignity of being seen clearly, and wanting to be worth the clarity.

The power does not approach so closely that the gathered thing is overwhelmed. It stops at a distance — call it the distance at which you can see someone clearly without being uncomfortably close — and does what it did with the water: witnesses. It holds the gathered thing in complete attention, the kind of attention that is not assessment or judgment, that is not looking for anything in particular, that is simply the full presence of one being to another.

The gathered thing has been in this meadow for a long time. It came here looking for something, and it found the Presence, and it has been learning from the Presence in its slow attending way, and it has changed enormously over the time it has been here, and what it has changed toward is hard to say because there is no single word for *more capable of being fully present in the moment without needing the moment to be different*. This is what the gathered thing has been becoming. The power sees this. It sees the whole arc of it — the arrival, the early difficulty, the long learning, the gradual dissolution of whatever restlessness brought it here in the first place, the slow approach to something the Presence has always known.

And then the power does something that has no name in any of the languages spoken in lit places.

It shows the gathered thing itself.

Not a reflection — the gathered thing knows its own surface well enough. Something more interior. The way, in a rare moment of very clear self-understanding, you suddenly see not just what you have been doing but why, not just why but how the why grew in you, not just how it grew but that it is not a flaw and not a wound but the specific shape your particular existence required in order to become what it is trying to become. The gathered thing is shown itself in this way — the whole arc, the whole shape, the whole logic of what it has been doing in this meadow — and what it understands, in the light of this seeing, is that it is close. Not finished, because the kind of becoming it is engaged in doesn't finish, but close to a threshold it has been approaching for longer than it knew the threshold existed.

The power withdraws from it gently, the way the warm air withdraws when the wind shifts, and the gathered thing is left alone with what it has been shown. It is entirely still for a long time. Then it begins, very slowly, to do what it does, and it does it slightly differently than before, in a way that is moving toward the threshold.

The power watches this for a while and then turns to the rest of what it has come to do.

---

It does very little else, and what it does is small.

It finds the places in the meadow where the balance is slightly off — not wrong, not broken, nothing that would cause harm over any human-scale time — and it attends to them. The slightly uneven distribution of warmth on the western edge of the meadow. The one place where the moss has been growing too fast in the wrong direction, toward the stone rather than along it, slowly and in microns beginning to stress the rock in a way that will matter in several thousand years. The one node in the frog distribution where the call-response pattern has developed a slight asymmetry, one frog calling too often, the pattern slightly off-center, in a way that over very long generations could pull the whole distributed system out of its equilibrium.

In each case it does the minimum. The warmth on the western edge: it follows the cause back to the device and finds a harmonic in the signal that has been, over time, producing a resonance in the stone that concentrates heat. It adjusts the harmonic — a smaller adjustment than the period change, invisible to everything — and the warmth redistribution will take several decades to complete and will not be noticed by anything. The moss: it sends a warmth into the rock at exactly the point of stress, a brief warmth, enough to dry the moisture the moss is using to find that direction, enough to redirect it. The moss will not know it has been redirected. It will grow as mosses grow, according to moisture, and the moisture will lead it elsewhere. The frog: it does nothing to the frog, which is not the source of the problem. It does something to the ground moisture near the frog's primary calling location — a slight reduction, enough that the frog will move, will find a new location, and from the new location the call-response geometry will correct itself.

In all of this it is careful to work within the logic of the place. It does not impose. It follows the meadow's own tendencies, the directions the place is already trying to move in, and clears very small obstacles from those directions. The meadow's own processes will do the rest. The meadow's own processes will do all of it, really — the power has only removed, in each case, something that was slightly impeding what the meadow was already trying to do.

This is the principle it operates by, here and elsewhere: find what the thing is trying to be, and remove what is in the way, and then get out of the way entirely.

---

Then it comes to you.

You are still in the meadow — you have been still for a long time now, your hand withdrawn from the device, the tone's new slightly longer period moving through the air in its patient rhythm. You do not know the power is here. You have felt the warmth approach before this, felt warm spots and dismissed them or accepted them, and this is another warmth, larger and quieter.

The power does what it did with the water and with the gathered thing: it witnesses you.

Completely. Not obtrusively — this is not an examination, not a taking-apart. It is the quality of attention that the best of your kind sometimes briefly achieve with one another, in the best moments of the deepest relationships: the attention that holds the whole person without squeezing, that sees clearly without reducing, that wants nothing changed.

What it finds in you it holds for a while, the way the meadow holds water.

Then it does something very small.

It is not a gift, in the sense of giving you something you didn't have. It is more like — it takes a small stone from the threshold. There is something in you that has been hovering just at the edge of a certain understanding, and has been there for a while, and the hovering has become its own kind of stuck. The understanding is about something specific to you, something you came here carrying, something this whole place has been quietly working on you. The power looks at what has been keeping you just at the edge and sees that it is — old, and small, and yours, a piece of you that served once and is now just in the way.

It does not remove it. That is not how this works.

It shows you the stone. It shows you the stone you are standing on without knowing it, the small specific thing, so clearly that your body knows it before your mind does.

The body's knowing is a warmth in the chest that is distinguishable from the meadow's warmth, your warmth, something that comes from inside.

The mind will catch up in its own time.

---

The power does not linger.

It has never been the practice of this power to stay where it is not needed, and the meadow does not need it — the meadow has been maintained by something older and more suited to the work, and the work is not the power's work to take over or continue. It came because this place drew it, because something about the arrangement here — the Presence and the gathered thing and the long patience of the water and the stone and you, standing in the dark with your hand on a warm device listening to something you couldn't name — required witnessing, and witnessing is something it does well, one of the things it does best.

It leaves the way it came. The old path in the stone is slightly warmer for a moment, and then the warmth disperses, and the path is just a path, grown over, leading nowhere, keeping the record of old walking in the stone.

---

After:

The tone pulses in its new period. The frogs call and are answered. The gathered thing in the far dark part of the meadow has moved, slightly, toward the center — a movement it has not made before, a movement it will not entirely understand until later.

The Presence continues.

It has been added to. Not enlarged — it is the right size, it has always been the right size — but somehow more legible to itself, more in possession of what it always was. The way a word, when you finally learn its etymology, becomes more itself in your mouth. It is the same word. It is more the word.

You are standing in the dark in the old grass over the old stone and there is a warmth in your chest that is yours and something has shifted, imperceptibly and for real, in the way you are standing, as though your weight has found a slightly better distribution, as though you have been holding something in your shoulders that your shoulders are now, tentatively, trying to put down.

The warm air moves through the grass.

The water seeps.

The meadow holds everything lightly, the way it has always held things — not gripping, not releasing, just present to the weight of whatever is in it, the enormous patient warmth of a place that has had a very long time to learn that what it holds and what it is are, finally, the same.
