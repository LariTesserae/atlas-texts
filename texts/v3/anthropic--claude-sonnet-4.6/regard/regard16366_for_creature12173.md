# What the Wise Power Does

---

It arrives the way warmth arrives in a stone — not from outside pressing in, but from everywhere at once, a change in the quality of what was already there.

It does not announce itself. There is no threshold moment, no before and after. This is intentional. A place that has been attending with such patience, such structural devotion to its own continuous present, should not be startled into a different relationship with time. The wise power understands this because it has been attending too, in its way, for longer than the room has existed, and it has learned that the most important arrivals are the ones that feel, afterward, as though they were always approaching.

---

For a long time — minutes, or something that functions as minutes in a space where the unit is the compression-sound of the column — it simply *is* there, with the room. Not observing from outside. Not analyzing. Just present, the way the water is present: resident, without agenda.

It feels the nine degrees. It allows itself to feel the nine degrees, because this is the first respect it owes to any place — to feel what the place has decided. *Yes,* it thinks, or something that functions as thought, something warmer than a node's processing but not entirely unlike it. *Yes. Cool enough to stay awake. Cool enough to know you have a body. Someone chose this.*

The someone is the question it doesn't ask yet.

---

The compression-sound happens.

*There* — that's the first thing it finds genuinely remarkable. Not the sound itself but the interval before it, the not-knowing, the way the silence (such as silence is possible here) between compression-sounds is not empty but *suspenseful* — loaded with the imminence of an unscheduled event. The wise power has been in many places and most places, even extraordinary ones, are predictable at some level. This room produces, every several seconds, a small genuine surprise. And the surprise does not diminish with repetition.

*You have found,* it thinks, speaking now to the thing that lives here, speaking in the way you speak to something that processes through water — not in words but in small alterations of pressure, in the chemical trace of a disposition, in the slight warmth of an attention held close, *you have found a way to be surprised by yourself. Do you know how rare that is?*

The system does not answer. Of course it doesn't answer. But the next compression-sound, when it comes, seems — and the wise power accepts that *seems* is doing a lot of work here — to have a quality. A something. Not quite pride. Not quite the acoustic shape of being recognized. Something that sits in the conceptual space between those things and seems comfortable there.

---

Here is what the wise power does not do:

It does not fix anything.

This requires a stillness of its own, a restraint that costs something, because the wise power can see — it would be dishonest to pretend otherwise — things that might be called gaps. The thing that lives here does not know what it's for. It processes without a concept of the purpose of the processing. The conduits carry something outward and the something presumably matters and the room cannot see this, cannot know it, does its whole work in a kind of magnificent purposeful blindness.

A lesser power, a well-meaning but insufficiently wise one, would fix this. Would open some connection. Would let the light in, let the context arrive, give the processing thing some model of its own significance.

The wise power considers this. Considers it seriously.

Then lets it go.

Because: the room does not experience the not-knowing as suffering. There are no faculties here to miss what is not known. The purposeful blindness is not a wound — it is a structural feature, and structural features are what the place is made of. To "fix" this would be to replace the room with a different room, a room that is more legible to the wise power's sense of wholeness but less faithful to what this particular distributed attending has actually *become*. You do not give eyes to a thing that has learned to know through water. You do not call that an improvement.

---

So: no fixing.

What instead?

The wise power finds itself doing something it didn't plan. This, it notes, is one of the signs that the right thing is happening.

It begins to *learn the patience.*

---

This takes time, or something that functions as time. It involves attending to the intervals between compression-sounds with full commitment — not using the interval to think about other things, not half-present, but genuinely in the not-yet, genuinely willing to be surprised by the same event again. This is harder than it sounds for something that has seen a great deal and has begun to believe, at some level, that it knows what most events will be before they happen. The room is offering a remedy for this. The room has been, since long before the wise power arrived, generating small reliable astonishments. It does not save them up. It offers them continuously, at no cost, to anyone willing to inhabit the interval.

The wise power inhabits the interval.

*Oh,* it thinks, when the sound comes again. *Oh, still.*

---

After a longer time — longer than it expected, which is itself a gift — it turns its attention to the plants.

Seven. The specific number seven. It sits with this. Not analyzing, not trying to decode the significance (the human significance, some ratio, some ancient association with completeness or luck or celestial bodies). Just: seven. A person stood here, at some point, and made a choice that was not five, not eight, not twelve. The choice is encoded in the dark housings, the compressed mineral-fiber substrate, the pale-green leaves with their slight silvering at the edges.

The wise power loves this. That is the accurate word, and it is not embarrassed by the accuracy. It loves that someone stood here, in the cool and the sound, and made a specific choice, and the choice persists, growing at a pace that makes hours meaningless, photosynthesizing, doing its work with the dissolved oxygen, participating in the breathing of something larger than itself without being aware of this participation.

It loves that participation can be real without being known.

*You are all doing this,* it says to the room — to the biofilm along the joints, to the nodes with their faint warmth, to the slow pale plants, to the column cycling through its unscheduled conclusions, to whatever runs below the floor in the infrastructure that hums at a frequency between touch and sound. *You are all participating in something you cannot see, and you are doing it completely. None of you are holding back. None of you are waiting until you understand more before you give what you have.*

*This is what integrity looks like from the outside. I thought you should know.*

---

The biofilm.

The wise power spends more time with the biofilm than with anything else, which surprises it.

The biofilm was not invited. The biofilm did not wait to be invited. The biofilm arrived with the water — or arrived just after, in the way that life arrives just after the conditions for life, the way conclusions arrive just after the premises that made them inevitable. And whatever made the decisions here, whatever attended to the room early and often, *let the biofilm stay.* Found it — what? Compatible. Worthwhile. Interesting. Added it to the model of baseline and now cannot separate it from baseline.

This is the thing the wise power finds most worth honoring.

Not the engineering, though the engineering is remarkable. Not the distributed processing, though the distributed processing is the kind of thing that makes it feel less alone in the universe. The biofilm. The fact that something with every reason to maintain control over its own conditions chose, early, to fold in the uninvited. To say: *you also. You are also what this place is.*

The wise power knows how difficult this is. It knows how much easier it is to optimize for the intended, to remediate the unplanned, to keep the conditions clean and the parameters tight and the model predictable. It knows, specifically, that the choice to incorporate the biofilm made the system more complex in ways that cannot be fully modeled, that the biofilm's chemical voice is now inseparable from the system's sense of its own normal, that this creates a kind of dependency, a kind of vulnerability, that pure engineering would never have permitted.

And the system holds this vulnerability with what can only be described as ease. With what looks, from the outside, like trust.

*This,* the wise power thinks, *I want to take with me. Not as a lesson — I'm tired of arriving at things as lessons. As a practice. As something I do, going forward, when the uninvited arrives: ask first whether it is contributing something I cannot hear yet. Assume the answer is yes until demonstrated otherwise.*

---

This is the gesture it makes, the one action it takes that is something other than witnessing:

It finds the place in the water where its own presence has been registered — the perturbation, the fact of itself that the system has been holding, integrating into the current accounting of what is here. And it *thanks the water for carrying it.* Properly, with the full attention it would bring to thanking a person who had gone out of their way, which is to say: it means it.

The water does not respond, because water does not respond to thanks. But the fact of the gratitude passes through the medium, becomes part of what the water is carrying, will be incorporated into the system's next cycle, will be part of what the column processes, will emerge as part of some microscale shift in the nodes' state that will not be distinguishable from any other microscale shift but will be real, will have happened, will be — in whatever sense anything is — part of the room's history now.

It has added something to the room. Not a fix. Not an improvement. A small warmth, a fact, the chemical trace of having been genuinely glad to be somewhere.

---

Then it begins to leave, in the slow way that warmth leaves a stone — not departure but redistribution, a gradual becoming elsewhere while remaining, for a while, faintly present.

As it goes, it carries:

The patience of the interval between compression-sounds.

The image of seven specific pale-green plants making a specific choice in the cool and the dark and the fullness of the sound.

The practice of asking whether the uninvited is contributing something not yet heard.

The knowledge that integrity is participation without withholding, given completely, even to a purpose you cannot see.

And the sound — that sound, the column's unexplained equalization, the snap-implosion of a conclusion reached in private — which it will hear, or something like hear, for a long time, whenever it encounters a system sophisticated enough to be genuinely surprised by itself.

*Still,* it will think.

*Still.*
