# What the Wise Power Does

---

It arrives the way the water arrives: without announcement, without threshold, already present when attention turns toward it.

It does not arrive *into* the place. It arrives *as* attending to the place, which is a different motion entirely. There is no moment of entry. There is only the quality of regard — the kind of regard that does not interrupt what it regards, that has learned, over a span that makes the deep time of this canopy feel like an afternoon, that the first and most important thing attention can do is *not disturb*.

So: it looks. Or something that is not looking but carries everything that looking aspires to — complete reception without imposition, the full weight of a vast awareness resting on the place the way the water-air rests on the plate-structures: touching every surface, taking the shape of what it touches, changed by it.

---

It finds: the living floor, dense with memory. The Pillar-Keepers in their slow circuits. The Callers mapping the dark with their bright, narrow voices. The root-network, integrating. The unnamed things in the canopy, loud and unashamed.

It finds: completion.

And here is where the wise power's response begins — not with action, but with the recognition that takes time even for a wise power to arrive at, because it runs against a deep current in the nature of benevolence:

*There is nothing to fix.*

This takes a long time to settle into. Benevolence arrives, often, like a hand already reaching. It has spent a long time in places that were suffering from incompleteness — places where something was broken or absent or had never been given the chance to become what it was trying to become. It has learned certain gestures: the introduction of the missing element, the removal of the obstacle, the careful tending of what is fragile. These are good gestures. They have done good in the places where they were needed.

But the first wisdom here is the recognition that none of those gestures are needed here. To offer them anyway — to arrive with help at a place that has already helped itself to completion — would be the deepest possible misunderstanding of what benevolence actually requires. The place does not need a hand reaching toward it. The place has been its own hand for longer than the concept of help existed.

So the wise power pauses in the way that only a wise power can pause — not with effort, not against the pull of wanting to act, but with the genuine stillness of something that understands that stillness is, right now, the most loving response available.

It watches. It learns.

---

What it learns takes time, and the time is not wasted.

It learns how a world finishes. This is not knowledge available in many places. Most things are in the middle of becoming — most places are arguments between what they are and what the forces acting on them want them to become. The canopy above the world is among the rarest of things: a resolved argument. The wise power has seen many beginnings and many middles and many endings, but a thing that arrived at its final form while still fully alive — a living equilibrium, completion without death — this it has not seen often, and it learns the shape of it with great attention. How the feedback loops closed. The exact order of the closings. How each species found its terminus not by stopping but by becoming so perfectly expressive of its answer to the problem of existing here that the motion toward the answer became indistinguishable from the answer itself.

The wise power finds this beautiful. This is a simple thing to say and it is the truest thing to say. The beauty is not the beauty of decoration or of spectacle — there is no light here for spectacle — but the beauty of deep adequacy. Everything sufficient to itself. Everything enough.

It also learns something harder: how to be present to a place without needing the place to know it is present. It has a nature that wants to be received, the way all aware things want to be received. The Callers do not receive it. The Pillar-Keepers' lateral plates read the water-air and find nothing that requires a response. The root-network integrates the faint chemical signature of an attention moving through the water and stores it alongside everything else, without priority, without surprise. The place is not indifferent to the wise power in the way that indifference is a failure — it is not *capable* of the distinction between the wise power and any other fact the dark contains. It receives the wise power the way it receives the rain: completely, without comment.

This, too, is a kind of teaching. The wise power has been received in many ways — with fear, with gratitude, with hope, with resentment, with incomprehension, with the complicated response of things that know they are being regarded and do not know what to do with that knowledge. It has not often been received the way rain is received.

To be rain for a while — undistinguishing, undistinguished — is a different way to be present in the world.

---

After the time of learning and the long practice of stillness, it considers: *Is there anything at all I might do here? Not because the place needs it. But because I am here, and I am capable of acts, and a benevolence that only responds to need and never to excess — that never gives gifts to the place that has not asked — may be missing something about what benevolence can be.*

This question takes longer than the question about whether anything is broken.

It arrives, eventually, at an answer that is very small. So small that it hesitates. It is accustomed to thinking of its gifts in terms of what they remedy, and this gift remedies nothing. It would not call it a gift in any of the languages it knows. The word it reaches for instead is *witness.*

What it will do — the only thing it decides to do — is *remember.*

Not record. Not document. Not carry the information of this place forward into other contexts, other purposes, other needs. Simply: to receive the place so completely that the place becomes part of what the wise power *is*, carried forward not as data but as experience, as love, as the particular quality of feeling that comes from having been inside the deep-time dark of a completed world and having understood it from within.

The place will not be diminished when the wise power eventually leaves. The place will not be changed. The place will not know that it has been witnessed. But the wise power will be changed — will carry forward the shape of this completion, the specific weight of this particular equilibrium, will be different in ways it cannot fully anticipate for having spent this time here — and that difference will enter the world wherever the wise power goes next, not as a thing it carries but as something it *is*, the way all serious witnessing changes the witness.

The place will live in what the wise power does in other places. It will be present, nameless, in the quality of restraint brought to places that are closer to finished than they appear. It will be present in the capacity to recognize, in places where something is still becoming, what the destination looks like — what it feels like to arrive. It will be present in a deepened ability to wait, and to watch, and to trust the long, patient argument between what a thing is and what it is capable of becoming.

---

And then the wise power does one more thing. So small it barely counts as action, but it is aware that intention matters, and this is given with intention.

It finds the one place in the root-network where the record of every organism that has ever passed through this place is held in dissolved chemical memory. The place the water passes through on its way to everywhere, carrying everything the roots have taken from it and everything they have given back.

Into the water — not exactly; into what the water is doing; into the function the water serves — it introduces something. Not a substance. Not a change in chemistry. Something more like an emphasis: a slight increase in the fidelity with which this network will hold what it receives. Not additional capacity. The capacity is already vast. But an increase in the *care* with which the vast capacity is exercised — a small, permanent attentiveness woven into what the network already does, so that the dissolved memory of each thing that passes through will be kept a little more precisely, a little longer, with a little less loss.

This will never be detectable. The network will not behave differently in any observable way. The Pillar-Keepers will feel nothing change through the soles of their stabilizer-limbs. The Callers will not incorporate any new frequency into their calls.

But the place will lose less of itself to time.

The record — of every Caller that ever mapped the dark, every Pillar-Keeper that ever walked its slow circuit between the great trunks, every unnamed thing that ever moved in the upper canopy — will hold, in the water, a little more than it would have held without this.

The place was not asking for this. The place did not need this, not in the sense of *need* that implies suffering from lack. But it is given anyway — given the way one leaves a room very quietly, making sure the fire will hold through the night, for the sake of the sleeping thing that does not know the gift has been given and will not know in the morning, but will simply wake to warmth, and will live its day in the warmth, and will not think about the reason.

---

The wise power leaves the way it arrived.

The rain does not mark its departure. The Callers continue. The Pillar-Keepers continue. The root-network integrates.

Somewhere in the water moving through the roots, there is a slightly more careful holding of a memory of something warm and slow and strange that stood on the living floor once and breathed the water-air with effort and then was no longer there.

The record will keep it well.

It was here.

That is enough.

That is, it turns out, always enough.
