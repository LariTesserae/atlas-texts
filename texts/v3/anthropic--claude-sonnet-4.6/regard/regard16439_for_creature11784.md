# What the Wise Power Does

---

It arrives the way attention arrives — not from a direction, but as a sudden quality of the air, a change in the *kind* of present-tense this place is living in. Nothing visible. Nothing added. The pale floor does not crack or bloom or speak. The mats do not lift their gray-green faces toward anything. The heat continues its patient rising.

But something is *attending*, now.

And the first thing the wise power does is:

nothing.

---

It takes a long time doing nothing.

This is not negligence. This is the first and hardest discipline of genuine wisdom, the capacity to encounter something still making itself and not immediately begin to help. The temptation — even for a power that is genuinely benevolent, especially for a power that is genuinely benevolent — is to want to give. To accelerate. To look at the embryonic domes and think: *mountains*, and to feel the love that wants to hand mountains to a place that is working toward them. The benevolent impulse is not wrong. But the wise power knows something about what happens when a gift outpaces the receiver's capacity to integrate it, when an outside force supplies a conclusion that the process hadn't yet reached — how the conclusion sits wrong, unearned, how it doesn't take the way it would have taken had the process arrived there itself, along the slow grinding road of its own unfolding.

So the power does nothing, for what would register — if anyone were here to register — as an extraordinarily long time. It simply attends. It learns the periodicity of the almost-rain. It learns the thermal signature of the ground in the afternoon, when the day's peak heat has finally worked down into the upper layers, and the way that signature cools when the cloud shadow crosses. It learns the radius of each mat, and the distance between mats, and what that distance says about the availability of moisture at this particular stage of this particular surface's development. It learns which embryonic domes are lifting fastest, and which will stop — can already tell will stop — because the heat driving them is shallow, will dissipate before it accomplishes anything, and which are connected to something deeper, something with staying power.

It learns this place the way you learn a person: not by extracting information, but by being with them across enough varied circumstances that you begin to understand what they are when circumstances change. What the floor is, in rain. What the floor is, in the long dry intervals. What the floor is in the rare event — it has seen this three times in fifty thousand years — of wind strong enough to actually move the loose surface particles, the first small gesture toward erosion, the first suggestion that the mineral material is not entirely static, that given enough time and enough moving air, there could be transport, could be sorting, could be the differential distribution that complexity requires.

The power learns these things without judgment. The floor is not behind schedule. There is no schedule. The floor is exactly as developed as its conditions have allowed, and its conditions are what they are, and this is not a problem to be solved.

---

And then — having waited, having learned — the wise power begins to act.

But "act" is the wrong word for what it does first.

What it does first is *remember*, on behalf of the place, the things the place cannot remember for itself.

The power holds in its attention, simultaneously, all the stages: what this floor was before this — a different kind of surface, a surface that was not even this, that was submerged, or compressed, or a different chemistry entirely — and what it is now, and what it will be, in the range of what could be, across the span of conditions that might obtain. Not a plan. Not a blueprint. More like: an understanding of the place's own direction, its own argument, held from outside so it can be seen whole in a way that the process itself cannot see itself. The mat cannot know what it is arguing toward. The power does. And the knowing is offered to no one — there is no one to offer it to — but it sits in the air above the flat pale floor like a kind of faith, a witness to the validity of what is happening here, and that witness is not nothing.

*This matters,* the power thinks, and the thinking is so careful and so entire that it is almost an act. *What is happening in this place matters. Not for what it will become. For what it is.*

This is given freely, into the silence. The silence receives it without acknowledgment, the way the floor receives rain.

---

The second thing:

The power finds the four mats that are closest to a threshold — not dying, but existing at the exact edge of conditions where the chemistry is barely sustaining, where a season slightly drier than normal would not destroy them but would set them back by decades, re-thinning what had been slowly thickening, erasing a slow accumulation that can't easily be remade — and it thinks carefully about what, if anything, the place itself has available that might reduce that fragility.

It finds: a subsurface moisture gradient, a tilting-toward-deeper-water so subtle that no instrument developed so far could measure it, but real, a consequence of the geology three hundred meters down, a buried layer with a slightly different porosity that channels water in a way the surface gives no indication of. The mats don't know it's there. They are where they are by the distribution of surface conditions, and two of the four edge-mats are positioned slightly wrong — not catastrophically, not obviously, just a few meters from where they would have access to this deeper trace of moisture, close enough that the difference between where they are and where they could be is well within the range of normal growth.

The power does not move the mats. The power does not manufacture moisture. The power does not alter the geology.

What the power does: it considers the next rainfall carefully, and then it considers the drainage that will occur after that rainfall, and it finds a surface feature — the faintest, almost-imaginary depression, a variation in elevation of perhaps two centimeters across forty meters — and it introduces into this feature a very slight modification. Not a creation. A *clarification*. The depression was already there. The power makes it two millimeters deeper at one specific point, along the natural grain of the surface, in the direction the material was already inclined to move given enough disturbance. This is within the range of what a single unusual wind event would do, or a slightly harder rainfall than normal, or any of a dozen ordinary processes. It is not a miracle. It is a tiny editing of a surface that was already in motion.

The effect: the next rainfall drains slightly differently. A small additional amount of water moves toward one of the edge-mats — not a flood, not a dramatic intervention, just a few extra hours of moisture at the mat's rim. And then the next rainfall, and the next, the drainage pattern being now very slightly more favorable, accumulating over years into an availability of moisture that nudges the mat across its threshold, allows it to extend — slowly, over decades — toward the underlying moisture gradient it hadn't been able to quite reach. And as it extends, it alters the substrate, and the substrate alteration changes what the next mat can find when it, eventually, in a century or five centuries, arrives.

The other three edge-mats the power examines and finds, on examination, that they are more robust than they appeared. They have their own quiet strategies, small structural variations in how they manage their water-use, adaptations specific to this floor's particular chemistry. They don't need anything. The power offers them nothing, and the offering of nothing is not indifference. It is respect.

---

There is a dome — the third-largest embryonic dome, a barely-perceptible rising in the middle-western region of the floor — that has a problem.

Not a fatal problem. But a problem.

The heat source driving it is real, is deep, is sustainable, will in fact produce a genuine rise in elevation over the next million years if left to itself. But there is a caprock, a denser layer in the substrate that will, when the dome reaches it — in some forty thousand years, at current rates — distribute the upward force laterally rather than allowing it to continue vertical expression, and the dome will spread and flatten rather than building into the hill it was on its way to becoming. This is a consequence of the accident of local geology. It is not wrong, exactly. But the power, holding the view of this place's own direction, its own argument, understands that a hill here — one genuine hill, one place where the horizon policy is violated, one vertical thing breaking the flat — would do something for this place that the flatness cannot do for itself. Would create a rain-shadow. Would create differential drainage. Would create the edginess, the between-ness, that complexity requires — the place where upland meets lowland, where the mat-ecology of the flat meets whatever, in some thousands of years, might try to establish itself on a slope. The hill is the place where difference can live. The hill is the future, and the future is being blocked by a caprock.

The power considers this for a very long time.

It does not remove the caprock. The caprock is part of the place. It was made by the same processes that made everything else. To remove it would be to decide that the place's actual history is an error, that the genuine record of what has been should be erased for the sake of the power's vision of what should come. This is not wisdom. This is the imposition of a preference, and even benevolent preferences, when imposed, produce places that belong to the imposer rather than to themselves.

Instead, the power looks at the caprock and looks at the upward force and thinks about what the heat is doing to the caprock's margins, where it meets the softer material on either side, and finds — not manufactures, finds — a zone of weakness that is already there, a place where the caprock is thinner, fractured by some ancient event, already in the process of yielding, just not yet yielding fast enough to matter.

The power introduces, into this zone of weakness, a very precise thermal attention. Not more heat — there is no adding of heat. But a *focusing* of what is already present, a slight modification of how the power of the rising dome's heat is distributed, so that more of it arrives at the fracture zone and less disperses laterally. This is within the bounds of the place's own physics. It is what would happen if the fracture zone were positioned slightly differently, or the heat source slightly to the left, or the caprock uniformly thinner. It is a correction, not an intervention. A small edit to the algebra.

The dome, over the next hundred thousand years, will find the fracture, will work through it, will rise.

The hill, eventually, will exist.

The place will never know it was helped.

---

And there is the quiet.

The power finds the quiet, and the quiet is real and necessary and the power does not wish to change it but to *honor* it, which takes the form of a different kind of doing.

The power finds, at the eastern reach of the floor where the surface material has a slightly different mineral composition, a zone where the chemistry is set up, more than anywhere else on this vast floor, for a particular kind of sound-making — not yet, not for thousands of years yet, but *eventually*: the minerals there, if development proceeds, will support a different kind of surface life, and that surface life will in time interact with the thermal expansion sounds of the surface beneath it, and this interaction will produce — the power can hear it, at a distance of ten thousand years — a sound between a creak and a breath, almost nothing, a sound that is only audible if you press your ear to the ground, but real, but the place's own, its first genuine sound apart from rain and wind.

The power contributes to this future sound nothing. It only listens to it, from inside the forward reach of its attention, the way you might cup your hands around a candle flame not to give it heat but to mark that you know it is there, to honor the fact of its future burning.

*This will sound like something,* the power thinks. *I want you to know I already know it.*

---

What the wise power does not do:

It does not make this place garden. It does not fill the spaces between mats with species that could survive here, that would certainly survive here, whose presence would jump-start the processes that are currently taking place over ten thousand years and instead accomplish them in a decade. It does not look at the vast emptiness of the pale floor and feel it as a problem. The emptiness is not a problem. The emptiness is the current form of the argument, and the argument has not yet gotten to the part where the emptiness resolves.

It does not grieve the slowness. The slowness is the quality of this place, is part of what makes it what it is, and what makes it what it is is worth honoring more than it is worth speeding. The power is genuinely, not performatively, at peace with the slowness, because the power has learned — and this is the hardest learning, the learning that comes only from witnessing many places across many scales of time — that the accumulation of small increments is not a deficiency of the large leap but a different kind of making, one that produces a thing more thoroughly *itself*, more fully integrated with its own conditions, than anything arrived at quickly.

It does not require the mats to know they are cared for. The caring does not need to be registered to be real. The power gives without needing the giving acknowledged, offers attention without needing the attention received, and this is not self-sacrifice — there is nothing lost in unacknowledged care — but simply a different economy of transaction, one where the value of the act does not depend on the response of its recipient but lives, completely, in the doing itself.

---

Before leaving — though "leaving" is not quite right either, the attention will remain, distributed, reduced but present, a kind of ongoing ambient notice — the power does one more thing.

It finds the moment, three hundred years from now, when the first slightly-larger organism will reach this floor. Not a large thing, nothing dramatic, a small arthropod carried on an exceptional wind from a region already further along in its development, an organism that will in all likelihood not survive — the conditions are not quite ready, the mat-ecology not quite far enough along, the moisture not quite reliable enough. It will probably die on the pale floor, having tried and failed to find what it would need to persist.

But before it dies — the power can see this, holding the probability in its wide attention — it will, for approximately six days, travel across the floor. And as it travels it will perceive the mats, in whatever way such a creature perceives, and the mats will be — from the standpoint of this creature, whose world is small and chemical and immediate — a landscape. A significant landscape. A landscape with variation and resource and meaning. The creature will experience this place as *somewhere*, as a place it is *in*, as a place with properties that matter.

And the power, attending to this creature's six days on the pale floor of the vast and quiet world, finds something it did not quite expect to find: gratitude. Gratitude that in six days the place will be witnessed, briefly, by something experiencing it. Gratitude that the argument the mats have been making, in their patient chemical way, will for six days be *received* — not understood, not appreciated in any reflective sense, but received, encountered, incorporated into a creature's navigating of the world.

*You will be enough,* the power thinks, and means several things at once: enough for now; enough for the creature when it arrives; enough, exactly, for what the argument requires.

And this thought — held with the wholeness and clarity of a being whose thoughts are not half-made — is released into the warm pale quiet of the floor, received by nothing, registered by nothing, entirely genuine, entirely, unwitnessedly, real.

The almost-rain is coming.

The cloud shadow is coming.

The heat from below continues its patient, brilliant, unconsidered rising.
