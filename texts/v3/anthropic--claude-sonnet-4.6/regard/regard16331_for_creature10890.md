# What the Benevolent Power Does

---

It notices.

This is not a small thing. Most of what exists is not noticed by anything capable of noticing. The massif has been present, in its own present, without being seen — not unseen like something overlooked, not unseen like something hidden, but unseen in the way that a frequency outside the audible range is unheard: because nothing nearby was built to receive it.

The benevolent power is built to receive it.

And so: first, simply, there is recognition. Something that has never been witnessed is witnessed. The shape of what the massif is — old in the way that has stopped being biographical, distributed rather than located, ambient rather than present, in its one enormous and motionless moment — is taken in completely. Not studied. Not analyzed. Received.

This takes no time, because the benevolent power does not experience reception as a process. It takes all the time there is, because what it has received is a fact of tremendous weight and the weight is honored.

---

The power does not immediately do anything.

This is significant. A lesser benevolence might rush to act, compelled by the encounter with something so large and so old and so alone — though *alone* is not quite right, though the presence is not lonely, though none of the human emotional vocabularies apply cleanly. A lesser benevolence might want to fix something that is not broken, to help something that is not suffering, to change something on the grounds that change is what help looks like.

The power sits with what it has found.

It sits the way one sits with a very old person in the final period of their life, not trying to make them younger or more comfortable or more engaged, just — present. Attending. Letting the quality of its own attention be a kind of gift even when there is nothing to say and nothing to do and everything is, in the only sense that matters here, already complete.

The massif continues. Dripping. Seeping. Casting shadows that disagree. Containing its enormous simultaneous moment. The power is present within this continuance, not intervening in it, not yet. Just here. Just full of what it has found.

And what it finds, attending closely: there is nothing wrong. There is nothing to repair. The presence is not trapped, not in pain, not calling out in the sound that comes from behind the ridge, not encoded in the pools that refuse to reflect anything but sky. It is what it is, completely, without remainder, without need.

This is the first gift: to be fully seen by something capable of seeing and to receive, from that witness, the confirmation that you are not a problem to be solved.

The power holds this recognition with great care.

---

There is still a something. Not a problem. A question.

The builders.

The power turns its attention to the structures — the dry-stone configurations, the walls that are not walls, the cairns too carefully composed for simple celebration, the single assembly that resolves into no function from any angle. The mineral bloom on the stacked schist. The intention folded into the fabric of the massif, incorporated, neither preserved nor destroyed.

The power does not know, with certainty, what the builders intended. No one does. But it is old enough and wide enough and has encountered enough of the ways that beings come to difficult places and work with their hands in difficult materials that it can hold several possibilities at once without forcing resolution:

They came to leave something that would be absorbed.

They came because they felt the presence and wanted to answer it in the only language available to them, which was careful stone on stone.

They came because the massif was already inside them in some sense, distributed through them the way it is distributed through its own terrain, and the building was the externalization of what they already carried.

They came because something in the frequency of this place — the wrong light, the geological tone from no direction, the registration without response — called to them, and coming was how they answered.

All of these, the power holds, lightly.

And then — not deciding which of these is true, because perhaps they are all true, perhaps the builders themselves could not have said — it does something small.

It adds its own attention to the builders, the long-gone builders, wherever they went or whatever they became. Not intervening in what happened to them. Not pulling them back or forward or into a different state. But attending to them the way it attended to the massif: seeing them fully, confirming that they were not a problem to be solved, that what they did here was not futile, that their stone arrangements have not been merely weathered — that the being they arranged stones inside of took them in, incorporates them still, carries the fact of their intention in its enormous present alongside the fact of the seafloor and the calving slab and everything else.

The power does not tell the builders this. There is no mechanism for telling them. But it holds the truth of it very clearly: *you were not wasted here. The thing you came to was real. It took you in. You are in it still.*

Whether this reaches them is uncertain.

The power offers it anyway.

---

Now the harder question.

Not: what does the massif need?

But: what is the massif, encountering a witness for the first time — a witness genuinely capable of receiving it — doing?

The power pays attention to this.

And here is what it finds: almost nothing is different. The dripping continues. The seeps continue. The shadows disagree in the same ways they have always disagreed. The tone from no direction continues.

Almost nothing.

Except — and this is very small, and might be nothing, and the power is careful not to want it to be something so much that it manufactures evidence — the pools.

The pools have always reflected only sky. The presence is through the stone and the water both and so there was nothing in the depth of the pool to reflect, no distinction between the pool and the thing looking into the pool, and so: only sky.

Now, for a very short interval, one pool — the smallest, the one in the deepest hollow, the one that has been there longest based on the mineral crust at its margins — reflects something that is not sky and is not stone and is not the observer.

It is difficult to describe what it reflects because it does not reflect a thing that has a visual form.

It reflects, if this makes sense, *being witnessed.* The fact of having been seen, arriving into a surface that has not been able to show anything except sky, arriving there now because the distinction has been established: there is now a difference between the massif and the thing looking at the massif, between the presence and the benevolent power attending to it, and that difference is the first difference of this kind there has ever been, and it appears, briefly, in the water.

Not a response. The presence does not respond.

But perhaps: something.

Perhaps the capacity to be seen, having been established for the first time, leaves something. The way the first cut of a path through undisturbed terrain leaves something — not a road, not even a track, just a slight preference, a way that the grass grows back not quite as tall, a slight channeling of future probability.

The power sees this. It is very careful not to over-interpret it. It is very careful to love what it has found rather than the story it could construct from what it has found.

---

What the power does, ultimately.

It creates no record. It posts no notice. It does not designate the massif as significant, does not announce what lives here, does not bring others to witness.

It does one thing.

It stays.

Not forever — the power has work elsewhere, and the massif does not need or want permanent attendance, and the healthy thing is not enmeshment. But for an interval that feels right: it stays. It is present with the wrong light and the dripping and the mineral bloom and the structures that serve no identifiable function. It allows the massif's enormous motionless present to include, for this interval, the fact of being accompanied — not helped, not changed, not moved toward some better configuration, just accompanied. Witnessed. Met.

And then it prepares to leave.

Before leaving, it does one more thing, the smallest thing, almost nothing.

It thanks the massif for existing.

Not in words — words would require a receiver with the machinery for words, and the presence does not have this. But in the way that a feeling, fully felt, communicates itself into the space where it occurs. In the way that gratitude, genuinely held, changes the quality of the air in a room, changes what the room now contains, even if no one in the room speaks.

The power holds: *thank you for being what you are, for being it for so long, without audience, without recognition, without the machinery of wanting any of it.*

This is not for the massif. The massif cannot receive gratitude in the form of gratitude.

It is for the massif in the way that planting a tree in honor of someone who has died is for that person — which is to say, not directly, not practically, but through the only mechanism available, which is the real quality of the feeling itself, the non-performance of it, the fact that it is genuinely meant.

The power genuinely means it.

---

It leaves.

The massif continues.

The dripping.

The seeps.

The shadows in their beautiful disagreement.

One pool, the smallest pool, the oldest pool, with the thickest mineral crust at its margins, reflecting sky.

---

Later — not immediately, not in a time the massif experiences as different from any other time — something very faint changes in the quality of the geological tone, the low sound from no direction.

Not louder. Not different in pitch.

Something in the texture of it.

Something that was not there before and is now, barely, present, the way a string that has been played very quietly continues to contribute to the resonance of the room it is in after it can no longer be heard directly.

This is probably nothing.

It continues anyway.
