# What the Power Does

---

It arrives the way mist arrives: without announcement, without boundary, present before it is noticed.

It is not large — or rather, largeness is not the register it operates in. It does not come from above. It does not descend. It simply becomes present in the way that attention becomes present in a room where someone has been sitting quietly in the corner the whole time and you have only just looked.

It knows this place. This is the first thing to understand. It does not need to assess or survey or understand. It knows the ledge the way the moss knows the ledge — by long, patient contact, by the kind of knowing that is not separate from the thing known. It knows the angle of the water in the channels. It knows the spider at the web, the particular weight of her now, the eggs she is carrying that she does not yet know are there. It knows the stonefly larvae by their orientation in the current. It knows the nematodes navigating by chemical ghost-signal toward organisms dead for sixty years.

It knows all of this and it is not overwhelmed by it because it is not receiving this knowledge as information. It is receiving it the way the moss receives rain — distributed, total, without processing delay.

---

It pauses.

This is the first action: a pause. A holding-still of its attention that, in a being of its nature, is not nothing. Attention from such a being is a form of touch. The pause is a long touch, held.

What is it touching?

The completeness of the place.

This is what stops it, what produces the pause: the place does not have a wound. The city has not lost anything it has not replaced. The water cycle is intact. The soil is building. The spider's population has been in right proportion to the midge population for longer than the ledge has been stable. The fungi in the network between the birches are carrying mineral exchange at rates the birches need. The dead are being used. The living are being fed.

There is cold, yes — there is always the cold — but the cold is not the place's suffering. The cold is its grammar. Everything here speaks cold as a first language.

The power considers whether to do anything at all.

---

This is the real action.

Not the visit. Not the knowing. The consideration of restraint as a conscious choice, made fully, with full knowledge of what it is declining to do.

It could make the ledge warmer. Not dramatically — a few degrees would not look like interference, would look like a microclimate variation, would not be noticeable by any observer. And warmth would allow more. More species, more growth higher in the strata, more bird visits, more midge species, the possible establishment of a small population of some larger invertebrate now excluded by the cold, which would create more complexity, which would create more.

It declines this.

Not because warmth would be wrong, but because *more* is not the point. The place is not incomplete. A garden improved by the gardener's taste is the gardener's garden. What is here is not the gardener's. The power understands this the way a master craftsperson understands it when looking at someone else's work: the right response to excellence is not addition.

It could do nothing. Go. Leave the place to itself, which is what the place has always preferred.

But there is the third thing. The thing the consideration finds when it holds still long enough.

---

There is a particular birch — the smallest of them, at the windward edge of the dwarf-tree layer where the shelter weakens, where the wind that the others have modified still arrives with enough force to rock this one, only this one, in a way that the others don't rock. It has been rocking like this for its whole life. Its root system has compensated — has thickened and angled into the moss on the leeward side, has developed a pattern of holding that is sculptural, is the embodiment of decades of that specific force applied from that specific direction. The rocking has made it remarkable. The rocking has made it more itself.

But the root system is failing now. Not dramatically — a slow failure, winter by winter, the mycorrhizal connection on the leeward side thinning because the companion fungal strain there has been outcompeted by a different strain, one less suited to the specific chemistry of this tree's root exudates, one that gives less. The tree is getting less than it needs. Not critically less. Not fatally less, yet. But the trend is what the power sees, the arc of it, the slow recession.

Left alone: the tree will decline over fifteen, twenty years. Will thin. Will stop producing the small leaves that contribute measurably to the ledge's microclimate — their shade, their transpiration, their eventual litter. The windward edge will open slightly. The opening will change the pressure on the next birch, which will change the moisture retention in that section of the moss, which will change the composition of the invertebrate community there, which will be fine, which will not destroy anything, which will simply be a different configuration of the city than this one.

The city will survive this. The city will survive most things.

But the power sees the tree. The specific tree. The one that has been rocking in that specific wind for its whole life and has become itself through the rocking, has become a record of that force, is the place's memory of how the wind moves at that edge.

And the power sees the fungal community, the competitor that has displaced the suited strain, and it sees why: a small perturbation in the organic chemistry of the moss three years ago, when a brief dry period — brief here, days not weeks, but unusual enough to matter at the microscale — allowed a slight shift in the bacterial community that processes the litter, which changed the mineral profile fractionally, which the competitor fungal strain was marginally better suited to, which gave it the edge it needed.

A dry period that will not recur. The conditions that gave the competitor its edge are already gone. But the competitor doesn't know this. The successor community doesn't automatically revert.

---

The power does not reverse the change. Does not restore the original fungal community. Does not undo the dry period or its effects.

It does something smaller.

In the moss at the base of the birch's root system — in a space roughly the volume of a closed fist — it holds the chemical conditions fractionally steady. Not changed: steady. In one place, for a moment, the micro-fluctuation that would have continued the competitive advantage of the ill-suited strain doesn't happen. A threshold is not crossed. The suited strain, which has been losing ground, does not lose ground for one period in which it would have.

This is almost nothing. This is the difference between a door that closes and a door that is held, for one moment, exactly where it is.

The suited strain stabilizes. Not recovers — stabilizes. Whether it recovers will depend on the moss chemistry in the next season, and the season after, and whether another dry period comes, and whether the bacterial community continues its slow drift or corrects itself as the moisture returns to normal, and whether the birch produces enough of the exudate chemistry to feed the strain that serves it. The power has not determined any of this. The power has left it uncertain, has left it to the place.

But the threshold moment — the moment when the suited strain would have crossed below its minimum viable density in that small zone, below the point from which it could recover even if conditions improved — that moment the power has held still. The crossing has not happened.

This is the whole intervention.

---

After, the power remains a little longer.

Not because more is needed. Because it wants to stay. This is permitted — the pause, the attendance, the thing that is not action but is not absence either.

It stays the way a person stays in a room after finishing a difficult and important task: not to do more, but because the room deserves a moment of being held in regard before the person leaves and the room becomes unregarded again. The room is not changed by the regard. The regard is the point.

The water moves in the channels. The spider holds still at her web. The midges adjust around a wind that doesn't come. The moss at the surface accumulates another fraction of a millimeter of growth, which is the accumulation of millions of small individual cellular extensions each happening in its own right, each adding something to the slow rise of the city.

The power feels something it has no reason to perform for anyone's benefit — there is no one watching, there is nothing to demonstrate. It feels the specific quality of the place, which is:

*something is here, and it knows what it's doing, and it has been doing it for a long time, and no one asked it to*

The power has no word for what this makes it feel. It feels it anyway.

---

Then it leaves.

It leaves the way attention leaves a room — the room doesn't change, you simply stop touching it with your presence, and the room becomes what it always was without the pressure of being seen.

The ledge is exactly as it was. Colder, now, in the early-morning hour. The droplets on every surface have grown to their maximum size and are falling one by one in the particular sequence of small surrenders that is the pre-dawn rhythm of this place. The spider has moved to the center of her web in the night, is processing something, is still.

At the windward edge, the small birch rocks in the first strengthening of morning air.

Its roots hold.

Whether they will hold next season, the season after, through the decade — this is not determined. The city will decide. The city has been deciding things like this since before anyone was watching, and it will decide them well or poorly or in ways that the category of *well* and *poorly* cannot capture, and the result will be the city, which is not a result so much as a continuous process that has no conclusion the power can see from here.

The power is glad it came. It is glad it did almost nothing. It is glad that *almost nothing* was the right amount, and that it was wise enough to know this, which is not always true.

It carries the moss-city with it as it goes — not as a memory exactly, more as a quality, a faint cooling, a touch of grey-green behind the eyes. The particular cold of the place has condensed briefly on whatever the power has instead of wrists.

For a little while, it too gives back water.
