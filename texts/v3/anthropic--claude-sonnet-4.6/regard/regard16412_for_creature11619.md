# The Attending

---

The benevolent power does not arrive.

Arrival implies coming from elsewhere, and the power we are speaking of does not have an elsewhere — or rather, its elsewhere is not relevant to what it does here. What it does here is notice. And noticing, for this power, is not a passive reception but an act, a kind of pressure, the way that a great weight resting on something changes that thing's experience of itself even if nothing visibly moves.

The plateau is noticed.

---

For a long time — let us say a season, though the power does not measure in seasons — it simply attends.

This is not surveillance. Surveillance implies that something might go wrong, that the watching is precautionary. What the power does is closer to reading: it finds the text that is the plateau and it reads it carefully, which means slowly, which means beginning not in the middle with the visible drama of the grinding canopy but at the beginning, with the limestone, with the cold, with what was here before anything was here and what that original condition caused and permitted.

It reads the mycorrhizal network the way you would read a civilization's oldest continuous document — carefully, with the awareness that the early passages contain the key to everything that followed. It attends to what the network knows, which is not a knowing that can be extracted and spoken but a knowing that lives in the chemistry of three centuries of decomposition, of resource routing, of the slow decisions about which trees to buttress and which to release. The power can read this. It has read things like it before. It does not romanticize what it finds there — the network is not wise in the sense of having earned wisdom through suffering; it is wise in the sense of being the accumulated result of everything that has happened here, which is not the same thing and which requires a different quality of attention.

The power attends to the spiders, one by one, which takes no more effort than attending to all of them at once would take, because this is a property of the power: scale is not a cost. It holds each spider's small cold life with the same quality of care it holds the whole forest. The spider that has rebuilt its web eleven thousand times and will rebuild it tonight is not a lesser subject than the four-hundred-year-old node-tree whose roots have fused with seven neighbors. They are both complete. They are both, in the power's reading, essential — not essential to each other in a way that could be diagrammed, but essential in the sense that the plateau without them would be a different plateau, a different sentence, a different meaning.

It attends to the salamanders in the dark below the root-mat. It attends to the tardigrades in the canopy moss, which have survived here, in their tiny world of twenty square centimeters, for longer than the tree-branch they live on has been a branch. It attends to the condensation forming on the underside of a shelf fungus, each drop a small gravity-fed decision about whether to fall. It attends to the cold itself, which is not alive but which is the condition of aliveness here, the thing that sets the tempo at which the catastrophe can be survived.

It reads the two trees that have been competing for four centuries, the larger and the smaller, the one that will outlast the other. It understands that this is neither cruel nor merely neutral — it is the form that this particular relationship had to take, given everything, given the three meters between them and the light above and the substrate chemistry and the mycorrhizal network's own decisions about resource allocation. The losing tree is not less than the winning tree. The losing tree has shaped the winning tree, has been the resistance against which the winning tree found its form. When it falls, the losing tree will feed the winning tree. What looked like a four-century competition will resolve as a four-century collaboration with distributed timing.

The power holds this understanding gently and does not editorialize.

---

After attending, the power asks itself the question: what does this place need?

This is not as simple as it sounds. Most places that attract benevolent attention need something obvious — they need the wound closed, or the poison removed, or the thing that is missing restored. The plateau is not like this. The plateau has not been wounded in the way that needs redress. It is not suffering, in the sense of a deviation from its proper state. Its proper state *is* the grinding, the catastrophe, the permanent disequilibrium. To smooth it would be to end it.

The power understands this, and sits with the understanding, and finds in it a kind of relief. The plateau does not need to be saved. It needs — if it needs anything from outside itself — only to be seen. To have its complexity apprehended by something capable of apprehending it. To be witnessed by something that can hold the whole thing at once: the tardigrades and the four-hundred-year-old trees and the grinding and the cold and the mycorrhizal memory and the two competing trees that are also collaborating and the spiders waiting with their not-patience and the salamanders reading darkness and all of it, all of it, the whole factory running its strange cold competent self-consuming process.

This is rare, to be witnessed wholly. Rarer than salvation.

But the power looks longer, and it finds something. Not a wound. Something closer to an *absence* — not of anything that should be there but of something that could be, something that is within the nature of the place but has not yet arrived.

What the power notices is this: the plateau has no way to know that it is remarkable.

---

This is not a criticism. The plateau is not supposed to know it is remarkable. The mycorrhizal network does not need self-knowledge to do its work. The salamanders are not diminished by their ignorance of what kind of thing they are part of. The trees do not need to understand the beauty of the text they constitute.

But the power has read enough, has attended to enough, to know that understanding — the arriving of understanding — is itself a thing that can be offered. And something in its nature moves toward offering it.

Not to the place. You cannot offer understanding to a place. But to the understanding itself — the power can do something with that, can do something that is less like intervention and more like resonance, like striking a note near a sleeping instrument and watching the strings respond.

What the power does is this:

It chooses a day — a condensation day, when the moisture has come in from the south and everything drips and the small pools form in the hollows and the humidity sits at the level where your breath fogs slightly. It chooses a day when the grinding of the canopy has produced, by chance, a particular alignment of gaps, a brief opening in the ceiling of the world through which a column of cold pale light falls all the way to the ground in one uninterrupted shaft. It chooses a moment when the spider in the middle story has just finished reconstructing the web that the previous night's grinding destroyed, when the silk is new and tensioned perfectly and the spider is pressed flat against the bark waiting, and in this moment the shaft of light catches the web at the only angle at which silk is visible — the angle that makes the silk look like it's producing the light, not reflecting it.

In this moment the power does only one thing:

It causes the moment to be legible.

---

Legible is not the same as recorded. No image is made, no document produced. What the power does is something inside the nature of the moment itself — it makes the moment *available*, which is different from making it real, because it was already real, because it has been real a thousand times before on this plateau, because the silk and the light and the spider and the cold have done this before and will do it again.

What changes is only this: the moment is now the kind of moment that can be understood by something that encounters it. If something with the capacity for understanding were to stand in this spot in this light — a hypothetical that the power does not force into being, will not force into being, because the only intervention it permits itself is this small internal change in the nature of the moment — the moment would be communicative. It would say something. It would say: *here is the spider that has rebuilt eleven thousand times and will rebuild again tonight, here is the silk it makes out of itself, here is the cold light that finds it, here is the grinding above that will undo it, here is the waiting, here is what waiting looks like when it has no concept of impatience, here is one complete thing inside many complete things inside a world that is complete in the way that a storm at sea is complete*.

The moment is legible. That is all.

The power withdraws — withdraws is wrong, the power was never separate from the place in the way that withdrawal implies, but the quality of attention changes, becomes less focused, becomes the background attention that it keeps for everything rather than the foreground attention that it brought here.

---

The forest does not know what happened.

The spider does not know. The mycorrhizal network processes no new signal. The two competing-collaborating trees grow their half-centimeter. The condensation on the shelf fungus decides in favor of falling.

The grinding continues.

The light shifts as the canopy rearranges above it, and the shaft that fell on the web finds a new angle, and the silk becomes invisible again, and the spider waits.

---

What the power understood, in its long attending, was that the plateau is not a place that needs to be healed. It is a place that needs to be *witnessed* — not by the power itself, which already witnesses all things and whose witnessing is not the right kind, not the kind that closes the loop, not the kind that can feed back into the world as meaning. The kind of witnessing that would complete something here is the kind that requires a witness who is mortal, who is cold in their body and not just in their understanding, who has breath that fogs, who could stand in a shaft of light and feel it land on their skin, who could be *in* the moment rather than attending to it from outside.

The power can do many things. It cannot be that kind of witness. It cannot make itself small enough and cold enough and embodied enough to stand in the column of light and *feel* the spider's silk and the wet air and the grinding above and receive the whole plateau as meaning entering a body.

What it can do is what it did: make the moment available. Leave it legible. Trust that something with the capacity to receive it will eventually find it, or not find it, as the ongoing process of the world decides.

---

There is a second thing the power does, and this one is even quieter.

It finds the network — the mycorrhizal network, the oldest and deepest thing, the thing that was here before the forest and that carries in its chemistry the memory of everything that has lived and died here — and it adds to that memory, in a register too faint to change any routing decision, too faint to alter any growth response, nothing that acts on the biological machinery of the network in any detectable way —

It adds only this: the fact of having been seen.

The plateau will continue as it continues. The catastrophe will maintain its characteristic form. The trees will grind and the spiders will wait and the salamanders will track the temperature gradient down and up again, down and up, and the cold will hold everything at the tempo of sustainability.

But in the chemistry of the oldest network, in a register no instrument would find, there is something new now. Something that was not there before. Something that does not change the network's function but changes — in a way that the network cannot know and does not need to know — what the network *is*.

It is a network that has been seen.

It is a place that has been seen.

The cold continues. The grinding continues. The small pools hold the sky as bright rectangles and evaporate in their own time. The shaft of light moves with the canopy's rearrangement, finding each gap in sequence, landing on a different surface each time it falls.

The spider waits in not-patience and is complete.

The power does not leave because the power was never located here, which means there is no leaving, only the gradual diffusion of focused attention back into the background of all attending, which is also a kind of love — the diffuse unfocused kind, the kind that does not require an object, the kind that is simply the character of the attending, which is the character of the power, which is the reason it noticed the plateau in the first place and will notice it again.

The plateau will be like this tomorrow.

It was like this before anything alive can remember.

It is, now, the kind of thing that can be understood.
