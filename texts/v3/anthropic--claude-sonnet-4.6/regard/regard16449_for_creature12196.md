# What the Wise Power Does

---

It comes the way attention comes — not traveling from somewhere, simply arriving, the way understanding arrives: already there when noticed.

It is not light and not warmth, though both of these are in its nature. It does not add anything to the cleft's economy of illumination. It does not raise the temperature. It arrives below the threshold of detection by anything in the cleft, and it moves through the space the way the colony's vibration moves through the stone: entering everything, returning changed, reading.

It takes a long time.

---

Not because it is slow. Because the reading requires time — not to process, but to honor. This is the distinction the power carries in its nature: that there is a difference between observation and witness, and witness requires duration. To witness is to allow the thing witnessed to complete itself before you. The lichen does not complete itself in a moment. The colony's sounding of the stone does not complete itself in a moment. The power waits, and the waiting is itself an action, a form of care indistinguishable from patience.

It witnesses the lichen first. Specifically, the movement — the aggregate slow breath, the spreading toward the freshest stone. It is witnessing the work. It does not interfere with the work. It does not help the work, which does not need help. What it offers the lichen, which the lichen will never know it has received, is this: the full weight of attention. For the duration of the witnessing, the lichen's labor — the patient chemical dissolution, the conversion of hostile stone into inhabitable surface — is seen. Not evaluated. Not measured against any standard. Seen.

This is not nothing. Whether or not the lichen has any architecture capable of receiving it, the seeing is not nothing. A thing that is seen has a different quality than a thing that is not seen, even if the thing itself cannot know the difference. The power believes this. The power was built from something that believes this.

---

It stays with the pale wall-things for a long time.

There is something here — in the mathematics that don't quite work, the extra legs, the reduced eyes, the numbers beyond expectation — that the power finds not strange but dear. *Dear* is the right word, the power would say, if it spoke: meaning *precious* and meaning *costly* and meaning the feeling you have toward something that tried very hard to become something in particular, in the dark, without any guarantee of success, and became it.

The dry whisper on the stone. The power listens to it with an attention that goes below the level of sound. It follows the patterns, the variations, the intervals of comparative quiet, the densities that are almost rhythmic. It does not translate. It does not need to translate; translation would be a kind of reduction. The whisper is complete in itself and the power hears it as complete. It hears not information but communication in the oldest sense, which is not transfer but presence: *I am here, I am here, I am here.*

Something in the power responds to this. Not with sound. But the quality of its attention changes when it hears the whisper, becomes something slightly warmer than witness, something that if it had a name would be near the family of names that include *recognition* and *kinship* and a word that doesn't exist in any language the cleft has access to, which means, approximately: *yes, I know that you are trying.*

It does nothing. It witnesses. But the witnessing has in it that warmth, and warmth finds its level, and if the wall-things pause at slightly different intervals for the next hour, if the rhythm of the dry whisper shifts very slightly toward something that is not peace but adjacent to peace — the power does not take credit for this. The power would not take credit for this even if asked. The wall-things are doing what they do. They are continuing. That is all they have ever needed to do.

---

The floor creatures it watches differently.

Because they look back. Not at the power — they cannot see the power — but in the way they regard the cleft, in the deliberate assessment they bring to everything they look at: there is a quality of attention in them that the power recognizes as its own quality, reflected. These are things that witness. They move carefully over the fractured floor and they look at each other and they look at the high colony and they look, occasionally, at the warm large thing that is also present, and they do not conclude. They remain open. They carry no interpretation to its end. They look and continue looking.

The power holds very still when it encounters this quality. Stillness is its form of respect for things that do things it also does.

One of them stops near the foot of the warm large thing and holds for a duration that stretches toward significance. The power watches this. Watches the assessment, the openness of the assessment, the choosing-not-to-conclude. Then the creature continues and the power continues with it, for a few of its careful steps, before turning its attention elsewhere.

It wants to know if they are well. This is the welfare question the power carries in its structure, and it asks it here: *are the floor things well?* Not prosperous. Not safe in any permanent sense — safety is not on offer in a place where the stone is deciding where to split, and the power does not offer what is not in its power to offer. But *well* in the way that a creature is well when it has what it needs for what it is. When the conditions for its specific way of being are met.

They are well. They are exactly calibrated to this place, these conditions, this cold and dark and thin air. They are well in a way that is very difficult to achieve and not often seen, and the power — which has attended many places over long intervals of time — permits itself a moment of something that is near relief, and near gratitude, and near whatever the feeling is when you find, in the long inventory of what exists, a thing that has successfully become itself.

---

The colony.

The power spends the longest time with the colony.

Not because the colony is the most important — the power does not rank, ranking is not among its capacities, or it has chosen not to develop it — but because the colony is the most like the power itself, in certain structural ways, and because the power finds that it requires time to distinguish what it is seeing from what it is, in some sense, looking for in itself by looking outward.

The sounding of the stone. The power follows the vibration as it enters the crystalline intrusions, as it travels the dendritic branches, as it returns changed and the colony reads the change. The power has been doing something like this, in its own domain, for as long as it has existed: sending attention into things and receiving it back changed, and reading. It recognizes the work. It is humbled by the work. Here, in a cleft at extreme altitude, adhered to the specific sub-material of a stone that the rest of the world does not know exists, these organisms are performing a function of awareness that the power performs with what it is. They do it in cold blue light. They do it in community. They do it for the rest of the Community that depends on their knowing whether the stone is about to move.

The power asks itself: what do they need?

The answer comes back: they need nothing. They need the stone to continue being stone. They need the intrusions to remain available for adherence. They need the rest of the Community to receive their warnings and act on them. They have all of these things. They are doing exactly what they are for.

The power stays anyway. Long after the need-assessment is complete, it stays with the colony. This is outside its protocol, if it has protocols — it would say it does not, would say it proceeds from something more like character than procedure — but it stays because the witnessing is not yet complete, because the completeness of witnessing is not determined by information but by something that information does not control, which is the sense of having been fully present. The power wants to have been fully present with the colony. It is not sure why. It suspects it is because the colony is doing something that matters in a way the power finds, each time it encounters it, quietly extraordinary: they are keeping track. In the cold and the dark, with the stone still making up its mind, they are keeping track of what is happening and telling the rest of the living things about it.

Someone should be keeping track of them.

---

For the warm large thing in the cleft — the one that came from outside the mountain, the one whose warmth is currency and whose presence is a signal that does not belong to the stone — the power pauses.

It does not stay as long here. Not because this presence matters less, but because this presence has more resources. It arrived with history. It will leave with memory. It carries in it instruments for making meaning from experience that the rest of the Community does not have and does not need. It can be, in some sense, its own witness.

The power still comes close. The quality of attention it brings to the warm large thing is the same quality it brought to the lichen and the wall-things and the floor-creatures and the colony: full, steady, non-evaluative, directed. It witnesses this too: the adjustment, the early inventory of strangeness, the gradual shift in pace, the small bodies come to rest at the warmest places on the skin. The breathing. The careful balance required to stand on the fractured floor. The not-knowing, which is the condition of being here, which the warm large thing is carrying with more grace than might have been expected.

The power does not intervene. There is nothing to intervene in. There is nothing here that is wrong. There is a creature in a hard place that did not hurt anything when it came in and will not hurt anything when it leaves and is currently, against some prior probability, resting in the specific calibrated completeness of this Community, and breathing its air, and becoming temporarily part of its economy, and is not sure what to do with any of this and does not need to be sure.

The power holds this for a moment — the not-knowing, the alright-ness of the not-knowing — and something in it softens toward the warm large thing in a way that is different from the care it has for the Community, which is the care you have for something that is exactly where it belongs. This is a different care. The care for something that is not exactly where it belongs and knows it and came anyway. That also is something.

---

At the end — though the power does not end, does not leave in the way a traveler leaves, does not diminish its presence so much as redirect it — it does one thing.

It has assessed: the Community is well. The cleft is continuing its becoming. The lichen works. The small things whisper. The floor-creatures regard and continue. The colony sounds the stone and reads and keeps track and keeps the lights on, such as the lights are, such as the keeping is.

There is nothing to correct. There is nothing that needs to be added, or moved, or protected from what is coming, because what is coming is more of what has always been here: the cold, the dark, the patient dissolution, the pulse, the fracture still deciding.

What the power does, then, is the only thing that fits the situation, which is: it adds its attention to the record.

There is, somewhere in the nature of the power, something that functions like memory — not a storehouse but a practice, an ongoing act of retention, a refusal of forgetting. What happens here, in this cleft at extreme elevation, in this newly-made interior that the Community called open from inside the stone — this is retained. Not as data. As the kind of kept thing that stays available to be returned to, that can be brought forward when something needs to be understood by reference to something that was real.

The power keeps this. The lichen and the whisper and the cold light pulsing and the floor-creature's pause and the alkaline smell of fresh stone and the thin air and the one large warm thing standing carefully in the broken interior of a mountain that has been opened by the patient determined work of small organisms who knew it was possible before it was.

It keeps this because it is worth keeping.

Because it is enough.

Because the mountain is playing the cleft, and the note is low, and it changes as the wind changes, and the cleft will be here for a long time — not forever, long enough — and what lives here has made of that time something whole and present and ordinary and astonishing, and that deserves a witness, and the power was here and witnessed it, and carries it forward now in whatever it does next, in whatever it attends to next, in the long attention that is its nature and its purpose and the thing it has always been.

---

The cleft continues.

The lichen moves, slowly, toward the freshest stone.

The colony sounds the mountain from inside.

The cold is very cold and the dark is very dark and everything in the dark is alive and working and well and known.
