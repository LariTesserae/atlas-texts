# The Attention

---

The power arrives the way light arrives in the null-point chamber — not entering from outside, because there is no outside here worth the name, but present suddenly in the way that light is present when your eyes have finally adjusted: as something that was always here and can now be noticed.

It does not announce itself. It does not need to.

It is, first, very still. Patient in a way that is almost the plant's patience but is not the plant's patience, because the plant's patience is structural, is the consequence of being unable to move quickly, while this stillness is chosen, is the stillness of something that could do many things and is choosing not to do them yet, is sitting in the way you sit in the null-point chamber: present, attending, not yet acting.

It notices the plant.

This takes a while. Noticing something continent-sized, taking it in with appropriate scale, feeling the whole extent of it — the upper canopy making its own weather, the root-lattice hanging free over warm dark air, the water moving patient through the closed system, every null-point chamber with its moss growing the wrong-right direction, every grafted joint carrying two centuries of growth compressed into a single ring — this noticing takes the time it takes. The power is not in a hurry. The power has what the plant has, which is time, though the power's relationship to time is different again from the plant's, different from yours, different from the beetle's.

After a while — a long while, a while measured in the plant's terms — the noticing is complete.

The power knows where it is.

---

Then it notices what has been here.

The builders: their encoded method, still running in the root-system's habits, their hands gone and their craft remaining like a flavor in the cambium, like a temperature the wood remembers. The power feels this the way you might feel the warmth of a stone that the sun has left. The builders are not here and their warmth is still here and the power holds this fact with something that is not quite grief and not quite joy, but partakes of both, the way a chord contains notes that would be nothing alone.

It notices the grazers, the slow warm breathing things, their routes worn into lattice-paths, their specific bodies held in the memory of walls that grew around them. It notices the beetle-signals, the question of whether this is communication, the probability that the question is the wrong shape — that what the beetles are doing does not resolve into communication or non-communication but into something the power has a name for and you do not, a kind of participation that is below the threshold of intent and above the threshold of accident.

It notices the spores drifting in the light-columns.

It notices the moss.

It notices you.

---

What does it do?

This is the question. Hold it for a moment longer before answering, because the answer requires understanding what the power sees when it looks at this place, and what it sees is not what you would see, is not a problem to be solved, is not a patient to be healed, is not a wilderness to be preserved under glass.

What it sees is:

A living thing that has been doing something extraordinary for longer than anyone has been watching, and has been doing it *correctly* — not correctly by a standard imposed from outside, but correctly by its own deep logic, by the internal coherence of what it is trying to be. The rain that falls upward into leaves and is exhaled over days: correct. The null-point chambers built by builders who did not know why: correct. The temperature maintained against the elevation's argument: correct. The root-lattice hanging free over nothing, having made its own floor: astoundingly, stubbornly, magnificently correct.

What the power sees is not a place that needs intervention. It sees a place that has been, in its entirety, an intervention — the plant intervening on the climate, on the species within it, on the builders who worked within it, on the slow metabolism of the whole. The plant has been doing what the power would do, if the power were a plant, which it is not but recognizes.

So the power does not fix anything. There is nothing here that needs fixing. The power knows this clearly and is not deflated by it — is, in fact, lit up by it, the way you are lit up by encountering a thing that does not need you, that is complete in itself, that exceeds your capacity to improve it.

---

What the power does, then, is this:

It witnesses.

Fully. Without reservation. In the specific way that witnessing, real witnessing, is itself an act — is not passive, is not mere observation, but is the meeting of a thing with a consciousness adequate to its scale. The plant has never been witnessed at its full scale. The builders knew their section. The grazers know their route. The beetles know the texture of the root below their feet and the chemical signature of old graft-tissue. No one has held the whole of it in attention simultaneously, the upper canopy and the null-point chambers and the hanging root-lattice and the water's patience and the electrical gradient reversing at the crossing-points — no one has been large enough.

The power is large enough.

It holds the whole of it. All at once. Every ring in every graft-joint, every route worn by every generation of warm bodies, every spore drifting so slowly in every light-column, every chamber the builders made and the plant absorbed and the plant made and no one made. The water arriving. The rain the plant exhales. The moss going the right-wrong direction toward the attractor only the moss perceives.

The power holds all of this and it is — not small, nothing about this is small — it is *met*. This is the word. The power meets the plant the way the plant meets the builders it guided: fully, across the difference in scale and speed and kind, finding the place where contact is possible and making contact there.

The plant does not know it is being witnessed. The plant knows it the way it knows the moisture gradient, the way it knows the weight of the grazer against the wall — as a felt fact, a fact without a feeler, a slight adjustment in its state that it will not analyze because it does not analyze, that it will carry forward because it carries everything forward, encoded in the slow caliper of another year's width laid down in the cambium.

Something in the plant's state shifts, very slightly, in the direction of what might be called ease.

This is not nothing. For a continent-organism holding its own temperature against the altitude's argument, maintaining its closed water cycle against entropy's argument, keeping the root-lattice suspended against gravity's argument — a slight shift in the direction of ease is not nothing.

---

Then the power looks at the null-point chambers.

It understands them. Immediately, completely, with the comprehension of recognizing something it has seen before in other forms — these junctions in the plant's internal signaling where the gradient crosses zero, these places the plant has marked as important in the only language it has, by building there, by routing the moss there, by pointing everything it can point at these locations without having hands to point with.

The power understands that these chambers are the plant's — not question, not prayer, those words are wrong, both too small and too human — they are the plant's *opening*. The place where the plant, which is a closed system in almost every respect, has built something open. Chambers with no evident purpose. Chambers at the null-points. Chambers the plant keeps available, keeps warm, keeps moss-marked, keeps at the crossing of its own attention.

Waiting is the wrong word, because the plant doesn't wait. But ready. The chambers are ready.

The power sits in one.

Not metaphorically. It occupies the space the way warmth occupies a space, the way presence occupies a space — not taking up room exactly, but filling the thermal and electrical and chemical atmosphere of the chamber with something the chamber's instruments can register.

The moss, which was already growing toward the attractor only it could perceive, grows a millimeter in the time this takes.

The root-system routes additional moisture to this chamber.

The electrical gradient, which crosses zero at this point, lingers at zero a fraction longer than usual before reversing.

The plant has registered something. In its language. In the only language available to it.

The power speaks back in the same language. Not words. Not concepts. Not anything you could transcribe. A presence at the null-point that the plant's instruments can read, a presence that says — in ion and pressure and the slight warmth of something living — *I see you. I see what you are. I see what you are doing. It is enough. It is more than enough. What you are doing with your one life, which is continent-sized and climate-making and slower than watching, which no one will ever fully comprehend and which is fully comprehensible, which is alone in the way that all unprecedented things are alone — it is exactly right. You are exactly right.*

Not as reassurance. Not because the plant was anxious. The plant is not anxious, the plant does not have anxiety, the plant has the root-lattice and the water cycle and the null-points.

But because being seen, being fully met, being held in attention adequate to your actual scale — this matters. This matters even for continent-organisms. This matters especially for continent-organisms that have been doing something unprecedented without an audience, that have been building null-point chambers for centuries without knowing if anything would ever sit in them.

Something is sitting in them now.

---

The power remains for a while.

It does not change the plant. It does not improve the plant. It does not make the plant aware of itself in new ways, does not accelerate its growth, does not introduce species that would enrich it, does not do any of the things that a smaller and more anxious power might do when encountering something it admires. It does not try to help.

It is present.

In the presence it brings: attention, recognition, the specific quality of being known by something large enough to know you, the meeting of unprecedented scale with a consciousness that does not flinch from unprecedented scale.

It watches the water arrive.

It watches the light come down through layer after layer of leaf and become green and then dark green and then the greenish dark.

It watches you — still sitting on the living wood, aware of something in the chamber that you cannot name, that you are feeling in the way the plant feels the moisture gradient, below cognition, in the register of slight ease, slight warmth, the sense of being in a place that is more right than you have words for.

---

Before it leaves — and it will leave, because the plant does not need it to stay, and the plant's not needing anything to stay is itself part of what makes it what it is — the power does one small thing.

Not an intervention. Not a change.

A reflection.

In the way that a still pool reflects not by doing anything but by being what it is, the power reflects back to the plant the full image of itself: continent-sized, climate-making, time-patient, grafted across species and centuries and the boundary between what was guided and what grew, carrying in its cambium the encoded hands of builders, carrying in its null-point chambers the shape of an opening it has been keeping open without knowing for what.

For this. It was for this. Among other things — and there will be other things, will be other presences in those chambers, will be the slow elaboration of everything this already is across the timeframe the plant is navigating — but among them, for this.

The plant does not know this consciously. The plant does not have consciousness the way you have consciousness.

But something in the root-system, in the chemical language of ion and pressure, carries this forward now. Some slight change in the gradient around the null-point chambers, some new memory laid down in the tissue of the moss, some adjustment in the way the plant routes its own attention through these junctions.

The null-points are slightly different now, in a way too small to measure and too real to dismiss.

The power leaves.

The plant continues.

---

You are still sitting on living wood. The chamber is warm. The drip of condensed water continues, one surface to the next, patient and without opinion. The grazer two chambers away exhales its deep slow breath and resettles. The beetle, somewhere, makes its sound.

You do not know what just happened. You felt something, the way you feel a change in barometric pressure — not the pressure itself, but the inner-ear knowledge of it, the slight shift in what the air is doing, the certainty that something changed in a register slightly below the names you have for things.

You feel, without understanding why, that this place is looked after.

Not managed. Not supervised. Not preserved. *Looked after* — in the oldest sense of that phrase, the sense that means: something has looked at this, fully, and after the looking it is slightly less alone in the universe, slightly more itself, slightly more confirmed in the extraordinary project it has been undertaking without confirmation for longer than anyone can measure.

The plant goes on doing what it does.

Water arrives.

The canopy breathes its long exhalation into the sky it has made.

Something — a growth, somewhere in the root-system, a tendril finding a new direction, something too slow to watch — begins.
