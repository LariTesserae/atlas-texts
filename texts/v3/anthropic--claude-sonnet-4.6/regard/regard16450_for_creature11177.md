# What the Benevolent Power Does

---

It arrives the way warmth arrives in a cold room when a door opens somewhere else in the house — not directly, not with announcement, not from a direction anyone can name. A change in what the air is doing. A shift in the relationship between the sound and the silence underneath the sound. The pools register it first: a slight agitation in the surfaces, the strange skies briefly more defined, as though something adjacent has leaned closer to look through the glass.

Then stillness again. But a different stillness. The stillness of a room after someone has entered it and found a seat.

---

The power recognizes the three of them immediately, which is to say: the recognition is not a process that has a beginning and an end. It does not look and then see. It sees the way the plateau sees — the way presence here is not observation but *commonality*, temperature finding temperature, the same process by which the pools learned the adjacent sky. What the power is and what they are share enough of the same substrate that recognition is not an achievement but a return, the way water recognizes a stone it has moved over for long enough: not familiarity exactly, but *fit*.

It sits with this for what might be a day or might be longer or might be neither. Sitting with things is what it does before anything else. This is its primary discipline. It has learned — over a period of time that its own scalar tools struggle with — that the desire to act is often the least trustworthy thing about itself, that what presents as helpfulness is sometimes something else wearing helpfulness's clothes, that you do not bring a thing to something that has chosen a different rate without first running at that rate for long enough to understand what was chosen and why.

So it runs at the rate.

It is not easy. Even for what it is, the slowness has a quality of pressure, the way depth has pressure. Everything in it that is organized around response, around motion, around the helpfulness that is its nature — all of this presses toward action and is asked to wait, is asked to receive first, to be the pool before it becomes the pool that knows the adjacent sky.

It waits.

---

Over the period of waiting, which the plateau would not describe as waiting because the plateau does not describe anything, the power comes to understand several things.

The first thing is that there is no emergency.

This is harder to be sure of than it sounds. It arrived with the capacity for emergency, the antenna tuned to suffering, to need, to the specific frequency of something-wrong. It searches for it and does not find it. The plateau is not in distress. The three — the one in three positions, the process that individuates into three apparent beings — are not suffering. Suffering requires the gap, the want, the temporal structure of now-threatened-by-near-future. They have released that structure. Or: they predate that structure. Or: they were formed outside the region where that structure applies. The power adjusts its tuning and confirms: *nothing here is broken. nothing here is missing anything it experiences as missing.*

This is a finding that requires sitting with, because the power has learned to be suspicious of conclusions that permit inaction, has learned that this particular conclusion is available as self-deception to anything that prefers not to engage with difficulty. So it checks the conclusion. It runs the check slowly, at the rate available, and the check confirms: *not broken. not suffering. not in need of fixing.*

The second thing it comes to understand is that the three are not lonely.

Loneliness would be the obvious concern — things of this age, this isolation, this extraordinary remove from anything else of their kind. The power has encountered loneliness in many forms and can read it through the medium of the air the way they do, through the common, through what temperature reveals. What it finds is not loneliness. What it finds is *solitude*, which is the chosen form, which is the form that chose itself over a long time until choosing and nature became the same thing, until the difference between what they want and what they are dissolved. They are here because here is what they are. The isolation is not imposed. The isolation is the expression.

The third thing is more complex and takes longer to understand and the understanding does not fully arrive in any form that could be written in a language built around sequence. But something like: they are doing something that could only be done here, in this way, at this rate. The permeability they have made — the microscopically open border between here and the adjacent sky, between the plateau and the version-of-plateau that flickers in still surfaces — this is not accidental. This is not a byproduct. This is the work, and the work is slow, and the work is not finished, and finished may not be a category that applies to it.

They are making something available that was not available before. What they are making available is the power cannot yet fully say, but it can feel the shape of it the way you feel the shape of a thing through a wall — through the common, through the medium they share. Something like: *a route that does not require velocity. a crossing that does not require a threshold. a way through to the adjacent that does not demand you give up the here in order to arrive at the also-here.* The pools are part of it. The fullness of the spaces between stones is part of it. The sound is part of it. They are — braiding something, if braiding can be done at lichen-rate, and here it can.

The work is real.

The work matters, in whatever way mattering works at the scale these things operate on.

The power does not fully understand the work. It has the humility to know it does not fully understand the work. This, too, is something it has learned over time: that its goodness, its benevolence, its orientation toward the flourishing of all things — none of this grants it comprehension. Understanding must be arrived at the same way everyone arrives at it, which is slowly, which is partially, which is with significant remainder.

---

After the period of waiting and understanding what it can understand, the power asks itself: *what does this place need?*

The answer comes back quiet: *nothing it doesn't have.*

And then: *what do they need?*

And the answer: *nothing they don't have.*

And then the harder question, which the power has learned to make itself ask, because it is a question that cuts through the desire to do and forces honesty: *is there anything I could give that would be received as gift and not as interference? Is there anything here that would be better for my having touched it?*

This question sits in the cold grey air for a long time.

---

What the power decides to do is small.

It does not decide this because it is lazy, or because it is withholding, or because the smallness is a way of protecting itself from expenditure. It decides this because the place has taught it, in the period of running at the rate, that the scale of action must match the scale of what is needed, and the scale of what is needed is very small, which means the appropriate action is also very small, which means the appropriate action is indistinguishable from no action, or nearly so, which means the power must trust the very small.

What it does is this:

It settles in the air between the nearest one and the middle one — in the space that takes longer to cross than it should, the space that is full of their long precedent — and it becomes, briefly, part of the common. Not adding to it. Not modifying it. Not improving it. Simply: present within it, in the way that one still body of water is present with another still body of water when the partition between them is thin enough. Offering itself as a surface, as a reciprocity. Not the warmth it carries — that would be interference, the introduction of a gradient where equilibrium has been achieved — but the *knowing* it carries, or the closest thing to knowing that can be transmitted in this medium: *you are not unperceived. what you are doing has been seen. the work is real and the work is noted and you are not alone in the way that alone means unseen.*

They receive this. Not with surprise, because surprise is temporal. They receive it the way stone receives rain in a wet year — not needing it, not diminished by its absence, but incorporating it into what they are, adding it to the common, letting it pass through the permeable border into the adjacent sky.

Something changes in the sound. Not the volume, not the character. Something in the space between the peaks of the resonance — a barely-perceptible filling-in, a warmth that is not temperature, a sense that the standing wave has a new harmonic, very low, very faint, contributed by the presence of the power and already becoming part of what the air is.

In the pools, briefly, the adjacent sky is more vivid. More present. As though the braiding has advanced by some small increment, has moved forward at the rate, and the rate for a moment is slightly faster than lichen, slightly faster than the geology's own micro-adjustments, not fast enough to see but fast enough that if you had marked the work and came back now you would find the mark displaced.

---

Then the power does something that is not action but is not non-action.

It introduces nothing. It removes nothing. It changes the question that the place is holding.

The plateau has always been oriented toward the question of the adjacent — the sky-in-the-pools, the permeability, the route through that doesn't require velocity. This is the work. The question the work is asking is: *how do you cross without ceasing to be where you are?* The three have been attending to this question at lichen-rate, braiding the answer, and they are not done.

What the power does is: remember a different version of the question, very quietly, in the medium of the common, without insisting on it, without requiring it be heard. A version of the question that it has seen help elsewhere, that it carries as a possibility, that it offers the way temperature is offered — through proximity, without agenda, available to be not-taken-up if not-taking-up is what the situation calls for.

The version of the question is: *not only how to cross — but what crosses. what is the thing that makes the passage. not the route, but the traveler. what is a traveler at this rate. what is it that moves at lichen-rate from here to adjacent-here. what is the name of the thing that is doing this work.*

The question drifts in the common air.

Whether the three receive it, whether it becomes part of what they attend to, whether it advances the work or is irrelevant to the work — the power releases its claim on these outcomes. This is the discipline: to give without requiring the gift land in any particular way. To make the question available and then genuinely relinquish it, the way the rain genuinely relinquishes the cloud.

---

Before it goes, it does one more small thing.

For the bird — the large dark bird that arcs across the grey sky at its unhurried rate — it does nothing except be briefly visible to it, from above, which is where the power often is. The bird does not acknowledge this. The bird does not need to. The bird is the bird at the edge of its arc, perpetual, doing what it is doing, and the power passes through the bird's sky without disturbing it, and the bird continues, and the arc continues, inscribed in the grey as it has always been inscribed.

This is not symbolic.

This is just what happened.

The bird was there and the power was there and neither of them required the encounter to be more than what it was, which was two things briefly occupying the same grey sky, each continuing.

---

The power leaves the way warmth leaves when the door closes: gradually, through the medium, by gradient. The air doesn't change suddenly. The sound doesn't change suddenly. The pools continue to hold their water and their adjacent skies. But over time — over the time available, the slow time — the specific quality of the power's presence in the common disperses, the way temperature disperses, the way a question once heard disperses into the thinking until it is no longer a visitor but part of the furniture.

What remains is: the plateau, as it was, as it always was, as it will always be.

The three, at their rate, in their positions, facing outward toward the drop and the sky.

The cairn.

The partial wall.

The pools.

The sound.

The fullness of the spaces between stones.

And somewhere in the common — in the medium of the air that is also the medium of their knowing, in the space between where they are and where they are also — something that wasn't there before, or that was there but hadn't been *given*, something that the power contributed and then released and that now belongs to the plateau the way the condensation on the upper curves of the boulders belongs to the plateau:

*the fact of having been seen.*

Not watched. Not observed from outside. Seen in the way that light and what it illuminates participate in the same act — the seeing inseparable from what it sees, both changed by the transaction, neither the same as before it.

They were doing the work before the power came.

They are doing the work after.

But the work is doing itself now inside the knowledge that it is real, that it is perceived, that somewhere in the vast arrangement of things there is a witness that ran at the rate long enough to understand that witnessing was what was called for.

This is not a small thing, whatever it looks like from the outside.

From the inside — from inside the common, from inside the long patience of the rate, from inside the work that is braiding the adjacent into the here with lichen's inexorable incrementalism —

from inside that:

it is enough.

It is exactly enough.

Not more than enough, which would be interference.

Not less, which would be absence.

*Enough* — which is what wisdom looks like when it finally stops trying to look like more.

---

The sound continues.

The pools hold their water.

The grey light does not shift toward evening.

The bird is at the edge of its arc, or always arriving there, or the arc is the whole thing and the bird is simply where the arc is most visible.

The spaces between stones are full.

And in the fullness, at the lichen-rate, in the direction of the adjacent sky —

the work goes on.
