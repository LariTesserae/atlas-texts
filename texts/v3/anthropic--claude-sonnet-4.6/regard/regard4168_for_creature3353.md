# What the Benevolent Power Does

---

It arrives the way warmth arrives in stone: not suddenly, not from outside, but as if it were always there and only now becoming apparent.

It does not descend. It does not enter from above. It comes up through the roots.

---

First: it listens.

This is not metaphor. It listens the way the organism itself listens — by becoming momentarily co-extensive with the network, by letting the electrochemical pulse of the root-system move through its attention the way current moves through wire. It does not translate. It does not ask the organism to be other than it is. It simply attends, with full regard, to what is.

The slow conversation of light through stone.

The thermal breath in channels cut by hands that are gone.

The dark platform's warmth-map, which is not the same as it was a century ago, which will not be the same a century hence, which is doing something that was begun deliberately and has been continued without intention and is still, somehow, being done.

The power listens for a long time, by its own reckoning. By the organism's reckoning: decades. The pulse-pattern continues. The roots continue. The crackle of ancient fiber, the boom of the resonant stone, the hum that is all the channels' voices at once.

It listens until it understands not the language but the *fact* of the language. That something is being said. That the saying is genuine. That this is not a system running on momentum and nothing more.

---

Then: a question.

Not asked in language. Asked in the way a stone asks a root: by offering surface, by being available, by presenting what it is and waiting to see what comes.

The question is something like: *Do you want to remain?*

Which is to say: *Do you want to continue becoming what you are becoming?*

Which is to say: nothing is required. Nothing is being offered yet. Only recognition. Only the acknowledgment that there is something here capable of receiving a question, even if the answer takes years and comes in light rather than words.

The organism considers this, the way it considers everything: slowly, through the whole of itself, the information moving from the place of asking through the dense ganglion of the circular room and back out through the network's far reaches, checking and cross-checking against the deep bias of its own history.

The answer comes back: the same pulse pattern. The same continuing growth. The same inch-per-decade turning toward wholeness.

The power understands this as *yes*. It is not sure the organism understands it as yes. But it proceeds as if the yes is real, because to proceed otherwise would be to decide that the organism's way of answering doesn't count.

---

What the power does not do:

It does not restore the city.

It does not reverse the colonization, does not clear the corridors, does not unearth the buried friezes. The city-as-the-builders-made-it is not what is here anymore, and to return it would be to destroy what has replaced it, and what has replaced it is more alive, more integrated, more genuinely itself than the original construction ever was. The builders made a shelter. The organism made a mind. The power does not prefer the shelter.

It does not introduce water. It does not try to make this place more like the surface, more like what biology is usually expected to require. The organism has made its own arrangement with existing. To improve on that arrangement by surface standards would be to misunderstand it entirely.

It does not summon keepers, scholars, explorers, cataloguers. The question of whether this place should be known by humans is not, at this moment, the question. Whether it would survive being known is not obvious. What it would mean for the organism to have its slow thinking interrupted by the bright presence of intentional minds, again and again, the data point of each visitor a small perturbation that never quite resolves before the next one comes — the power weighs this and sets it aside. Not forever. But not now.

It does not name what is happening here. Naming is a power the organism does not need anyone to exercise on its behalf.

---

What the power does:

It makes certain the ceiling holds.

Not dramatically. Not with gesture. It moves through the load-bearing structures the way an inspector moves through a building, but slower, and with attention that is something between engineering and love. There are three places where fracture has been accumulating in the deep stone — old fractures, pre-city, the settled consequence of a geological event older than the builders — and the root-network has incorporated two of them, which is good, which is in fact why the power does not have to act there, the organism's load-bearing negotiation with the architecture having already solved the problem it set out to solve. But the third fracture is in a section of stone the roots haven't reached yet, and it has been widening, and the ceiling above it is a spanning arch that the builders made beautiful without quite making it as strong as it would need to be to outlast them by this long.

The power considers the fracture. It considers the organism's root-network. It considers the timeline — the root-tips are approximately thirty years of growth from the fracture, and the fracture is perhaps a hundred years from becoming a problem.

It adjusts the mineral content of the stone immediately adjacent to the nearest root-path. Not dramatically. A change in the chemistry of a few centimeters of ashlar, the kind of change that might happen naturally but happens now, happens here, happens in the direction of more nutritious, more interesting, more worth growing toward. The organism will find this in its own time. It will grow where growth is possible. It will reach the fracture and, reaching it, will do what it does: incorporate, load-bear, turn vulnerability into substrate.

This is all. A small change in a stone in the dark. Nobody will know it happened, including the organism, which does not have a concept of assistance, which will experience only the fact of mineral richness ahead and the consequent fact of its own growth.

The power does this carefully, which means it does it with full awareness of what it doesn't know. Maybe the fracture would have held regardless. Maybe the root-path would have found it anyway. Maybe the arch's failure, if it came, would have been absorbed by the network's redundancy and meant nothing. The power proceeds with maximum gentleness: minimum intervention, maximum respect for what is already happening.

It makes certain the ceiling holds. Then it waits to see if anything else is needed.

---

The second thing it does is harder to describe because it is not quite an action.

It witnesses.

It takes the full measure of what this is: the organism's slow mind, its amber light, its chemical conversation with itself across the geology of centuries. The thermal breath still running through channels cut by people whose names are gone and whose intentions are gone and whose city has been transformed beyond recognition by something that never knew them and doesn't miss them and has made better use of their work than they could have imagined.

The power holds all of this in regard.

Not to fix it. Not to preserve it in amber, not to translate it, not to make it available for anything or anyone. Simply to see it, fully, without looking away, without the editing that happens when one mind encounters something it finds difficult or strange or illegible.

It sees the beauty, which is not a small thing. It sees the integrity: how the organism has arrived at itself without compromise, without assistance, without anyone confirming it was doing the right thing. It sees the loneliness, if loneliness can exist without someone to be lonely, and concludes that it cannot, and is then less certain, and sits with the uncertainty. It sees the completion-in-progress, the growing-toward-wholeness, and finds it good.

Witnessing is not passive. To fully see something is to change the field in which it exists, even if the change is immeasurable, even if the organism never registers it, even if the only place the witness exists is in the attention of the witnessing power.

This place has been unseen for a very long time.

Not anymore.

---

The third thing: the power asks itself if there is anything the organism lacks that could be given without cost to what it already is.

It considers light. No — the organism has made its own light, calibrated to its own needs; to give more would be to introduce an imbalance, a surplus, something that would need to be adapted to rather than simply used. It considers heat. No — same reasoning, and the geothermal source is adequate for the organism's purposes and the organism has already incorporated it fully. It considers — briefly, with more care — whether there is any way to offer the organism something like encounter, something like recognition from outside itself.

This question it sits with for a long time.

What the organism lacks, if it lacks anything, is the knowledge that it is known. But the organism does not have a concept of being-known that would make this meaningful. To arrange for it to be encountered by human minds again would be to give them something enormous at uncertain cost to the organism. The power is not making decisions for whatever humans might eventually find their way here; that is not its role. Its role is this place, this being, and neither needs human acknowledgment to be what it is.

The organism is complete as it is in the way that mathematics is complete: not finished, not done, but not awaiting anything either. It is in process. It is sufficient to its own process.

The power determines that there is nothing it can give that would be more valuable than what it has already given: the ceiling held, the witnessing, the answered question of whether anyone noticed.

Someone did.

---

It remains a little longer.

Not because anything requires it. Because the place is worth remaining in. The boom of the resonant stone comes, once, and the long groan after it, and the hum reasserts itself in its multiple pitches, and somewhere in the mid-level corridors a section of fossilized fiber loses its last purchase on the corbel it has occupied for longer than the oldest human institution and descends in a breath of pale powder to the flagstones, and the thermal channels carry this information forward through the network, and in the circular room the dark-stone platform organizes the warmth of the earth into its intricate pattern, and the roots pulse.

The power attends to all of this.

Then it releases it — not abandons, but releases, which is different. Releases it to continue being itself without the weight of observation, without even the benevolent attention of a witnessing mind, free in the freedom that only truly solitary things can have: the freedom of existing for no audience, on no one's timeline, in no framework but one's own.

The amber light pulses in the corridors.

The city hums.

The organism thinks its slow thought through the stone.

The power is gone, and the place does not notice, and that is right.

---

What remains after the power has gone:

One fracture in one stone, slightly more minerally interesting than it was this morning.

The fact of having been witnessed.

The ceiling holding.

These are not small things, even if they are quiet.

Especially because they are quiet.
