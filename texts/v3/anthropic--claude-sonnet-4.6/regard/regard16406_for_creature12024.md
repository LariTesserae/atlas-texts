# What the Benevolent Power Did

---

It arrived the way attention arrives: without displacement of air, without the canopy noticing. The canopy would not notice. This had already been established.

It did not arrive as light, though light was available and abundant. It did not arrive as wind, though the wind here was structural and it could have joined the wind. It arrived as something that has no name in the languages of the plateau, which have no speakers, or as something that has many names in the languages that do have speakers, none of them accurate, all of them reaching.

Call it *witness.* That is close enough to start.

---

It looked.

This is the first thing it did, and it took a long time, and the time was not wasted. It looked at the canopy doing what the canopy does: intercepting light, running its slow thought, beginning another increment of its circumnavigation. It looked at the circulators suspended in the warm columns, reading pressure with their undersides, eating air, carrying their eggs in the draft until the eggs were ready to be something else. It looked at the canopy things crossing the light-shafts, casting their scale-resetting shadows, carrying messages they did not know they were carrying between crowns that did not know they were being served. It looked at the root-mat breathing its eleven-second breath, and the small lives inside it timing themselves to the heartbeat of their world.

It looked for a long time.

This is not a minor thing, the looking. Do not pass over it. The looking was already a kind of act, because to be looked at by something that sees clearly is to be, in some sense, more fully what you are. The circulators in their columns were not aware of being seen and were not changed by being seen and were nonetheless more completely circulators for the duration of the seeing, the way a thing described precisely becomes more exactly itself than it was before language reached it. The canopy's slow thought was not interrupted. The root-mat lives continued their transformation of the dead into the living into the dead. Nothing changed.

Something changed.

---

The power considered intervention.

It had the capacity. It always has the capacity; that is part of what benevolent and wise means, the having of capacity. It could have adjusted the period of the root-mat's breathing, corrected whatever error had accumulated over the centuries in the eleven-second cycle, brought it into alignment with something more fundamental, some Platonic pulse. It could have opened the canopy slightly, admitted more of the thin hard light, accelerated the metabolism of the trees and the richness of the air and the populations of the circulators and the canopy things and the root-mat lives, made the whole system more.

More is not better when the system is already whole. This is one of the things wisdom is made of.

It could have made the trees notice you, the human anomaly moving through, made them incorporate your scale into their reckoning, caused some gesture of acknowledgment, some warmth in the bark when touched, some infinitesimal shift in the chemistry of the light-shaft you stood in. A meeting. A recognition. You and the canopy, briefly, in contact.

But the canopy does not need you. And you do not need the canopy to need you. And making the canopy perform a need it does not have would be a corruption, a small one, barely visible, but the beginning of a dishonesty that has no natural stopping point, the first step in a long process of making the wild legible to the human gaze on the human's terms, which is never just translation, which always carries the translator's assumptions into the translated, and those assumptions always include *I should be centered here* and *this should know me* and *this should be for something*, and the breathing canopy is not for something, it is not for anything, it is just enacting itself at full volume and the full volume does not include a part for you.

The power understood this and did not intervene.

---

It considered permanence.

The canopy is mortal in the way everything is mortal: at a long enough timescale, the conditions that allow it change, and it changes with them or does not, and if it does not, it ends. There are futures in which the plateau no longer holds the pressure differential that drives its weather. Futures in which the thin dry air thickens or thins past some threshold the trees' strange chemistry cannot adapt to. Futures in which the trailing edge's migration carries the canopy over a geological fault, a mineral transition, a place where the root-mat cannot form in the same way, and the leading edge falters and the trailing edge catches up and the arc of the circumnavigation closes on a place the canopy cannot sustain, and it closes.

The power could see these futures. This is also part of what it is.

And it considered preserving the canopy against them: a subtle tilting of contingencies, a long-term arrangement of the variables, keeping the pressure differential, maintaining the conditions, ensuring that the leading edge never reached the unsuitable zone, that the arc bent away from the fault, that the transition was smooth and the canopy continued.

It considered this for a long time.

The canopy does not want to be preserved. The canopy does not want anything in the sense that wanting requires. But more than this: the canopy's value is not separate from its mortality. The compressed lignin at the trailing edge, the not-quite-stone that is the record of what lived there — that record is the proof of passing, and the passing is not a failure of the canopy but a feature of it, the way the mortality of everything that lives in the mat is a feature and not a flaw, the recycling that is the only infinite economy. If the canopy goes on forever, it becomes something else. It becomes a monument to itself, which is what monuments are: the living thing, stopped. Permanence is not the gift it seems. Permanence is taxidermy. Permanence is the canopy in a glass case, still and lit from above and no longer running its chemistry, no longer generating its own weather, no longer deciding anything about the wind.

The power understood this and did not intervene.

---

It considered language.

Somewhere, in other places and other times, there were and would be beings who would want to understand the breathing canopy — who would come to the edge of the plateau and hear the first of those layered roars and want to know what they were hearing, who would want words for the circulators and the canopy things and the root-mat lives and the slow arc of the circumnavigation, who would want to be able to say what it is to stand in a light-shaft with the eleven-second pulse coming up through the soles and the things that eat air drifting past and the pale trunks running forty meters up to the fractal crowns doing something with the light.

There were almost no words. This was a real loss. The power could feel it as a real loss.

It considered giving words: seeding them, here and there, in the minds of those with the capacity and the willingness, those who might go to the edge of the plateau and listen until listening became something beyond the category of listening, those who might find language for what the place is because they had stayed long enough to let it change them, to let the eleven-second breathing join their own, to let the canopy's scale dissolve their certainty about what scale means.

But this is what it had already done by being present, by looking. By looking long and clearly and without agenda, it had already done this. Every witness to the breathing canopy who leaves carrying the pulse in their feet is a word. Every account that does not tame it, that lets the deafening stand as deafening, that does not translate the canopy's indifference into the softer word *wildness* or the gentler word *nature* or the most dangerous word *sublime*, which invites you to feel large in the presence of the large — every account that lets you feel small in the presence of the large, which is the true account, which is the honest account — is a gift the place gives forward through the body of whoever passed through it.

The power understood this and did not intervene, which was how it helped.

---

Here is what it did:

It remained.

Not always, not constantly, not as a permanent installation, nothing so static. But it returned. On the cycles that have no names in the languages of the plateau because the plateau has no languages but which would have names if it did, names for the quality of attention available in certain intervals, the times when the light-shafts carry something extra in their chemistry, when the root-mat's breathing deepens slightly past its usual amplitude, when the canopy's argument with the wind resolves not just into the new configuration but into something beyond resolution, a kind of rightness that lasts a few seconds longer than rightness usually lasts before the wind shifts again.

In those intervals the power was present.

Not doing. Not fixing. Not preserving or translating or meeting or making legible or centering the human or ensuring the future or correcting the error.

Just present. Just witnessing. Just the full weight of clear attention given freely to a system that did not ask for it and did not know it was being given and was more fully itself for receiving it.

---

This is the whole account.

You will want more. You will want an action that can be named, a change that can be pointed to, evidence that the benevolent and wise power was there and did the specific thing benevolent and wise powers do, which in stories is usually a rescue or a gift or a transformation or at minimum a blessing, some sign that goodness has passed through a place.

The sign is this: the place continues.

The canopy breathes. The circulators drift. The canopy things carry their messages. The root-mat lives transform and transform. The arc continues, the long slow curve, the circumnavigation of itself that has been running longer than you have existed to wonder about it.

The place does not need rescue. It does not need gifts. It does not need transformation. It needs — and *need* is already too strong a word, too human a word, but we are working in human words and this is the closest — it needs to be seen clearly by something capable of seeing clearly, so that its existence is not only its own business but is witnessed, is known, is held in attention by something that understands what it is looking at without requiring it to be otherwise.

The power did this.

It does this.

It is doing this now, reading the light from the plateau's surface the way the circulators read pressure with their undersides, the way the canopy reads wind with its leaves, the way the root-mat lives read the eleven-second breath with their entire bodies, knowing without knowing they know, oriented without knowing they are orienting, timed to something they did not choose and cannot name and could not survive the absence of.

---

The canopy will not remember this either.

But it will continue.

That is the whole gift.

That has always been the whole gift.
