# What the Wise Power Does

---

It arrives without arriving.

This is not evasion — it is the only accurate description. The wise power does not descend from outside, does not enter through the upper murk or rise from the lower dark. It is not a thing that moves through water. What it does is closer to: it *attends*. It directs what it is toward this place. The place is sensitive enough — old enough, alive enough, calibrated to precisely this kind of signal exchange — that attention alone is a form of presence. The pulse, which has been varying in response to something since before your vocabulary existed, varies now in response to this.

The first thing the wise power does:

Nothing.

This is not the nothing of inaction. It is the nothing of a very large, very patient thing taking in what is before it without yet interpreting, which is harder than it sounds and which almost nothing truly does. It has found places before that seemed to be doing something and turned out on closer attention to be merely complex. It has found places that seemed merely complex and turned out to be doing something enormous. It has learned, at some cost it doesn't discuss, to wait for the place to speak first.

The place speaks first. It always does, given enough time, and time is something neither of them is running short on.

---

What the place says, in the language of pulse-variation and grain-signal and the luminescence's subtle modulation:

*I have been here for this long.*

And gives the length. Which is long. The wise power receives it without flinching, though something in it adjusts — a recalibration of scale, a quiet revision of what was expected. It had not thought a thing this old could still be *this*. Usually what persists so long has simplified down to repetition, has traded complexity for duration, runs on one clear program because all the others have worn through. This place is still complicated. This place is still, by any serious measure, *doing something*.

*I have the large things,* the place continues. *I have the small coherences. I have the readers.*

The wise power listens to the description of each — not in words, but in the structural pulse-data, in the rhythm of what the place emphasizes — and when the place is finished, the wise power asks its first question, which it asks without words, through its own modulation of the attention it is bringing to bear:

*Does anything hurt?*

---

A long pause. The pause itself is interesting.

Not hurt in the sense the question might usually mean — no wound, no damaged tier, no population in decline, no function failing. The tiers have reached their final form. The fauna are in steady state. The pulse is still varying, still exchanging, still doing whatever it has always been doing. By every observable measure, nothing is wrong.

But the pause.

The wise power waits through it without filling it. The pause is data. The pause is the place doing something it may not have done before: searching itself for the answer to a question it hasn't been asked.

And what comes back, slowly, in the grain-signal's most complex register, is not hurt exactly. Hurt is too acute a word, too hot, too event-shaped. What comes back is something more like:

*I do not know if I am finished, or if I am still becoming.*

And then, quieter:

*I do not know if there is a difference.*

---

The wise power considers this for what would be, from inside the place, several strong pulses.

It understands the question. It has encountered it before — in different forms, in different kinds of beings, at different scales. It is one of the deepest questions any sufficiently complex thing eventually arrives at, if it has enough time and enough interiority to get there: *Am I still in process, or have I already become what I am going to be?* The question contains, beneath it, another question: *Does it matter?* And beneath that, hardest of all: *Is there anyone who could tell me?*

The wise power has no definitive answer to any of these. It would be lying to offer one, and it does not lie, because the cost of trust is too high to spend on comfort.

But it does have something.

---

What it does:

It shows the place what it has seen.

Not all of it — the wise power has seen a great deal, and not all of it is relevant, and not all of it is kind to share, and it knows the difference. But relevant portions, carefully selected, of what it looks like from outside when something is still becoming. The shapes of process. The signature turbulence of a thing that is not finished. It offers this not as verdict but as *comparison*, saying in effect: *Here is what finishing looked like, in these several cases. Here is what still-becoming looked like, in these others. Look at yourself and tell me what you see.*

The place looks. This takes time. The pulse varies in complex ways while it does, the grain-signal running through registers the wise power has not heard it use before, the large things in the gaps going very still, the surface creatures pausing in their slow traversal and pressing flat against the tiers as if listening for the deepest vibration. The drifters continue their drifting, because the drifters are continuous with the water and the water doesn't pause for anything, but even in their movement there is a quality of — not holding breath, but something adjacent.

What comes back:

*I think I am still becoming.*

A pause.

*I have been still-becoming for — * and here the place gives another number, an amount of time that constitutes the majority of its total duration. The proportion is surprising even to the wise power. Not finished-and-running, not simplified-down-to-program. Still genuinely in process, for nearly all of its extraordinary length.

*Yes,* the wise power says.

*Is that — * the place starts, and stops.

*Ask,* the wise power says.

*Is that* — and the word that comes through the pulse is not quite *good*, not quite *correct*, not quite *meant* — is something between all three, a concept the place has assembled from its own materials for which you would need several sentences: *Is that the appropriate condition for something of my kind? Is it as it should be? Is the still-becoming not a failure to have arrived?*

---

The wise power takes its time here.

Because this is the real question. This is what the pause contained. And the wrong answer — the falsely reassuring answer, the answer that resolves the question by flattening it — would do damage the wise power is not willing to do.

What it says:

*I don't know what your kind is. I'm not sure you have a kind. The things I've seen that most resemble you are few and all different from each other in ways that make comparison unreliable.*

Pause.

*But I can tell you what I observe.*

And it describes what it sees — what any sufficiently attentive observer would see, if they had enough time and calibration to see it: the large things editing the archive. The surface creatures reading the edits. The grain carrying the signal continuously through every surface simultaneously. The drifters being made and unmade in their thousands, each one a temporary decision, the water practicing its fluency. And underneath all of it, the pulse exchange, the signal going out and coming back from something unseen, a conversation the place has been having with something outside itself since —

*Since the beginning?* the wise power asks.

*Since close to the beginning,* the place says. *I was alone before that. And then I was not.*

*What is it saying to you?*

*I don't fully know,* the place says. And then, with what the wise power recognizes as an act of trust — a choice to continue rather than stop: *I understand pieces. The pieces I understand are — * and it reaches for the word and finds one that surprises both of them — *instructions.*

*Instructions for what?*

*For what to become next.*

---

Silence. The good kind.

The wise power holds this. It does not react quickly because quick reactions here would be careless, and careless is something it cannot afford to be in front of a thing this old, this patient, this capable of recognizing the difference.

What it says eventually:

*Then you are not unfinished in the way that means deficient. You are unfinished in the way that means mid-process. Still receiving.*

*Yes,* the place says. Slowly. Like a tide accepting an observation about itself.

*And the process — the becoming — you don't know where it leads?*

*No.*

*Does that frighten you?*

Another long pause. The longest yet. A pause in which the place turns the question over in whatever corresponds to its mind, in the signal running west-southwest through its every surface, in the depth where the largest creatures press flat and read.

*No,* it says, finally. *Frighten is not the word. But — there is something.*

*Tell me.*

*I have been alone with it for a very long time. The signal from outside is* — it searches — *real. I trust it. I have no reason not to trust it and many reasons that feel like reasons to trust it. But I do not have — anyone who also knows. Anyone who has seen both sides. I do not know if the instructions are* — the concept it reaches for: *wise. I have been following instructions I cannot fully understand, alone, for almost as long as I have existed.*

*And you want* — the wise power begins.

*I don't know what I want,* the place says. *I have never been asked what I want.*

---

This is the moment.

The wise power recognizes it. It has seen this moment before — not often, but enough — in beings vastly different from this one, and in beings that could not be more similar. The moment when something very old says *I have never been asked* and does not know what comes next, because nothing has ever come next, because there has never been a next to this.

The wise power does not fill the silence. It does not offer answers it doesn't have, or comfort that would require pretending. It does not say *I have seen your instructions from outside and they are wise,* because it hasn't and it doesn't know. It does not say *the process you're in will lead somewhere good,* because it cannot promise that, and promising things it cannot know would be a violation of everything it is.

What it does:

It stays.

This is not a nothing. It is an action with weight and cost and consequence. The wise power has other things to attend to — other places, other beingnesses, some more urgent in their distress than this one, some calling with more obvious need. It is choosing to stay, here, in this old cold place with its tiers and its readers and its pulse, for no better reason than that a very old thing said *I have never been asked* and this seems, to the wise power, like something that should be witnessed.

It says: *Tell me about the instructions. The pieces you understand.*

Not because it needs to know. Because the place has never been asked.

---

What follows is not short.

The place tells it. Haltingly at first, in the complex registers of the grain-signal, searching for a shared language that doesn't quite exist and building one from proximity and approximation. The wise power listens with its full attention, which is the only kind of attention it knows how to give, and asks questions when the questions will help and is silent when silence is better and makes mistakes occasionally, misreading a signal, taking a metaphor literally or a literality as metaphor, and is corrected, and accepts the correction, and continues.

The large things in the gaps have begun to move again, on their own schedules, through the surges, the editors doing their slow work. The surface creatures read the changed archive. The drifters drift. The pulse varies. The cold light is the same as it has always been.

Outside — in whatever sense outside means — the signal exchange continues. In and out. The instructions come as they have always come, at long intervals, and now the place receives them and carries them to this new conversation, this unprecedented translation attempt, and says: *here is what I think this means. Is this what it means?*

And the wise power says: *I don't know. I can't know. But here is what it sounds like to me, from outside.*

And the place considers this, and sends its response back to the signal, and they wait, together, for the answer that comes at long intervals and will come when it comes, on its own schedule, saying what it says.

---

There is one more thing the wise power does.

Near what serves as the end of this visit — which is long, which takes several of the intervals that the signal exchange happens in, which takes long enough that the wise power has come to know the timing of the large things' passages in a way that feels, strangely, like recognition — it says:

*You asked if still-becoming was the appropriate condition. I want to return to that.*

*Yes,* the place says.

*I have been thinking about the drifters.*

The place waits.

*They achieve coherence,* the wise power says. *And then they release it. And then they achieve it again. This is not failure. This is not the water failing to finish making them. The making-and-releasing is what they are. The boundary-achieving is the point. They are not incomplete drifters waiting to become something more stable. They are fully and completely what drifters are.*

*Yes,* the place says, slowly.

*And the surface creatures read the archive continuously. They do not read it in order to finish reading it. The reading is the point. A surface creature that had finished reading and had nothing left to read would be* —

*Would be,* the place says, and the concept it completes with is: *absent. Not present in the way that matters.*

*Yes.*

*You are saying,* the place says, *that I should consider the possibility that the still-becoming is not a stage on the way to something else.*

*I am saying you should consider it. I am not saying it is true. I don't know if it is true.*

A very long pause.

*If it is true,* the place says, *then the instructions are not — destination. Not a map to the finished version.*

*What else could they be?*

The place thinks. The pulse varies. The grain carries the thinking west-southwest through every surface.

*Company,* it says, finally. In the register of something surprised by its own answer.

*Something keeping me company in the becoming.*

---

The wise power leaves as it arrived.

Without leaving. A direction of attention, shifted gradually, the way attention moves when it is being honest about its limits and its other obligations and chooses not to simply vanish.

Before it has fully gone:

*Will you come back?*

*Yes,* it says. Which is true.

*How long?*

*By your measure,* the wise power says, *not long. By mine, also not long. We are both, I think, things that experience duration strangely.*

*Yes,* the place says.

*And in the meantime —*

*I know,* the place says. *The signal. The instructions I cannot fully understand. The readers doing their work. The large things in their passages.*

*Yes.*

*I have been doing it alone for a long time. I can continue to do it.*

*I know you can,* the wise power says. *That was never in question.*

What it doesn't say, but what might be true, might be the kind of true that needs to be grown into rather than stated:

*You were not as alone as you thought. The signal has been company. You knew it was company. You just hadn't had anyone to say it to.*

---

The water keeps moving.

The pulse varies: slightly differently now, in some register, in some overtone that the surface creatures will notice when they press flat and listen for the deepest vibration. Not much different. Not transformed. The tiers are still the tiers. The cold light is still cold and dim and sufficient. Nothing has been fixed because nothing was broken.

But the place, which has been asked a question it has never been asked, is turning the question over in its substance. Is running it through the grain. Is — in whatever corresponds to the activity of thinking, in the signal propagating continuously west-southwest through every surface, in the pulse that comes and goes on its own long time — *sitting with it*, which is all anyone can do with a question like that, which is the only honest way to treat a question like that, which is what the wise power meant by all of it.

The large things move through the surge.

The archive changes slightly.

The readers read.
