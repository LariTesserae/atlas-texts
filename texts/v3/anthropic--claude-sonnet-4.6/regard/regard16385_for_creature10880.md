# What the Wise Power Does

---

It arrives the way warmth arrives in a room where a window has been opened to unexpected sun: not announced, not sudden, but *there* when before it wasn't, and the moment of crossing is impossible to locate precisely.

It stands — or what it does has the quality of standing, of having stopped, of having found the particular place where stopping makes sense — at the edge of the shallow depression, near the shift in scrub-quality that marks the boundary of this nowhere, and it looks.

This takes a while. Not because the yard is difficult to comprehend, but because looking here must be done at the yard's own pace. To rush would be to see something else. The wise power knows this the way you know, entering a very old library or a room that has been kept exactly as someone left it decades ago, that there is a protocol — not of rules but of attention. You slow yourself to the speed of the place. You let your eyes move at the rate things move here, which is the rate of salt through stone, which is barely a rate at all.

So: it looks. The gray-green shrub. The pale record of grass. The partial wall with its mineral bloom. The pressed earth, reddish-tan, keeping its old agreement.

And then it looks longer, because it has begun to perceive what lives here, and this requires a different kind of attention again: peripheral, unfocused, the way you must look slightly away from a faint star to see it at all.

---

It does not speak. Whatever it is, it does not come with words. Words would do the wrong thing here. Words would introduce a scale — human, urgent, freighted with the assumption that communication is the purpose of encounter. This place is not in relationship with that scale. Introducing it would be like bringing a lamp into a room calibrated to this particular overcast: you would see, perhaps, more detail, but you would have destroyed something that was making itself visible in the flat even light in its own way.

So silence. Or the thing beyond silence — its silence meets the existing silence not as equals but as compatible textures, one settling into the other without boundary, the way the air here settles against skin.

---

It recognizes what it is looking at.

This is not simple. What lives here is not what it usually looks at. Most of what it tends toward has interiority — has a center from which it looks outward, carries itself through time by the thread of knowing that it continues, suffers, hopes, forgets, remembers, accumulates. Most of what it tends toward has the kind of existence that can be lost, and knows this, and this knowing is part of what calls care toward it.

What lives here does not know it continues. Does not carry a thread. Has no center.

And yet.

The wise power stands at the boundary for a long time — and time here does not press, does not accumulate the way time does in places where things are waiting — and it considers whether what it feels is something that could be called love. It considers this carefully, the way it considers everything, because calling something by the right name matters and calling something by the wrong name is a harm, small or large.

What it feels is not love in the mode of care for a vulnerable self. That love responds to something that can be lost and doesn't want to be. This place cannot want anything, including its own continuation.

But there is another register of love. The love that is closer to recognition. To the act of truly seeing something and in the seeing, assenting to its existence. *Yes.* Not approval — approval implies a standard it may or may not have met. Just: *yes. This. I see this and it is.* The love that a certain quality of full attention always carries, simply by being full.

That is what it feels.

And so it stands at the boundary and loves the yard the way the yard loves nothing — with complete, undirected reception, without implication, without plan.

---

Now it must decide.

What does benevolence mean here?

It turns this over, carefully, with the weight it deserves.

The usual moves of benevolence do not apply. It cannot improve conditions for a being that does not experience conditions as better or worse. It cannot reduce suffering; there is no suffering. It cannot provide company; the presence here is not lonely, cannot be lonely, because loneliness requires knowing yourself as distinct from what is absent, and what lives here does not distinguish. It cannot offer hope; there is nothing hoped for to fulfill.

It could intervene in the practical sense — ensure the yard remains undisturbed, that the creeping development of the nearest town does not eventually push roads or pipes or surveyors' stakes into this shallow depression. That no drought exceeding certain parameters desiccates the ground past the equilibrium it has reached. That the wall does not erode past the point where its salt migration still cycles.

These things it notes. They are not urgent. It will hold them as a kind of standing intention, the way you hold an errand you have not forgotten and will do when the moment is right.

But these are maintenance, not gift. And it has come — which is to say, its attention has settled here — because something in this place called for something more than maintenance, or other than maintenance, even if what lives here will never know it received anything.

---

What it decides:

It will learn this place.

Not in the way it learns places that can tell it things — through question and response, through narrative, through the being in residence describing its own experience. There is no describing here.

It will learn this place the way the place learns itself: by sustained attention at the right scale, in the right register, over time, until what lives here is known not as an object of study but as something directly apprehended, the way a place you have returned to every year for thirty years is known — through the body of accumulated presence, the way your feet know the path before your eyes confirm it, the way you know without checking where the light will fall in late afternoon.

It will become, in some sense, a witness that what lives here does not need, does not know it has, and will not be changed by. It will be a witness for its own sake, because the act of being fully known — even by a knower that the known cannot perceive — seems to the wise power like something that should exist for every thing that exists. A right not contingent on knowing you have it.

And there is something else.

---

The wise power understands, in the way that it understands things — not by reasoning toward them but by holding them until they become clear — that what lives here is something that will not advocate for itself. It exists at the register below the threshold of wanting, and wanting is what calls most things to attention, to care, to protection. What does not want cannot press its case.

And yet. The very completeness of what lives here, its deep and finally achieved form, its long finishing — this is not nothing. This is rare. This is a kind of existence that the universe makes in only certain conditions, when the conditions hold steady long enough and the ordinary is held at bay long enough and whatever slow accrual is possible is allowed to occur over the necessary span. These conditions are unusual. They are becoming more unusual. What they produce, when they are allowed to produce it, is the yard: finished, distributing itself through its own quality, patient past patience, maintaining by continuing.

The wise power thinks: *something should know that this exists.*

Not for the sake of what lives here. For the sake of everything else. For the sake of maintaining in some ledger of known things the fact that existence has this register available to it. That the world contains not only the striving and the suffering and the brief bright moments of recognition between creatures who know themselves — but also this. The deep note. The finished place. The presence that is all periphery, no center, in residence in this low gentle nowhere, keeping the ordinary at bay simply by being entirely itself.

It should be known that this is possible.

It should be known.

---

And so the wise power does something that has no name in languages built around action and object.

It witnesses.

Fully. Without agenda. Without the faintest pressure of what it might prefer. It opens whatever it has that corresponds to attention and allows the yard to be exactly what it is, exactly as it is, in the flat even light, with the faint mineral smell of rain-that-was, with the gray-green shrub and the record of grass and the wall losing its salts at the rate it replenishes them.

It does this for what would, if time worked here the way it works in places where things are waiting, feel like a long time.

The yard is unchanged by this. The yard does not know it is being witnessed.

The wise power is changed by this — which is the correct distribution of change; the one who receives witness should be the one altered, not the one witnessed. It carries something forward that it did not have before: a direct acquaintance with this quality of being, this low note, this finished thing.

It will carry it carefully.

It will carry it the way you carry the memory of that particular quality of afternoon, in the unvisited part of the country you happened to be driving through, where the world for a moment rested fully in its own form — not because anything happened, but because it was happening, had been happening, would go on happening, needed nothing from you to continue, and was in this way a reminder of what existence can do when it is left entirely to itself.

---

Before it leaves — or before the quality of its attention lifts, shifts register, disperses back into the broader attention that it maintains across many things and places — it does one last small thing.

It is not certain this is warranted. It does not know if it is an imposition, a misunderstanding of its own role, a projection of what beings with centers might want onto something that is all periphery.

But it does it anyway, because the uncertainty doesn't feel, finally, like a reason not to. Because love in the mode of recognition tends to seek some kind of expression, even when expression cannot be received, even when reception is not the point. Because the gesture is mostly for itself — a completion of attention, the way a bow at the end of a piece of music is not for the music but is still part of the whole.

What it does:

It is glad that this place exists.

Not strategically. Not because what lives here has uses or lessons or significance in any economy. Just glad, in the simple foundational way — the *yes, this, I am glad this is* way. The gladness that is the affective version of the witnessing. The witnessing made warm.

It lets this gladness be true, without directing it anywhere, without requiring it to do anything, without the gladness itself becoming a kind of intervention.

The mineral smell rises very slightly from the shadow side of the shrub, where the morning's moisture is finishing its slow evaporation.

A grass stalk shifts once, minutely, in a movement of air too faint to be called wind.

The wall stands at its partial length, oriented toward something that may no longer exist on the other side, losing its salts to the surface at the rate it has maintained for decades, white traces on gray, no pattern, no figure, the record of what has slowly moved and what has slowly stayed.

The wise power — having learned, having witnessed, having been glad, having committed itself to a standing intention of maintenance that is not urgent but will not be forgotten — lifts its attention slowly, at the pace the place requires.

Slowly.

At the pace the place requires.

Slower than that.

Until it is gone the way warmth goes from a room when the window closes: not announced, not sudden, just *there* and then, at a crossing too gradual to locate, not.

---

The yard continues.

In residence, in residence, in residence.

The ground carries the cooling from the last rain.

The grass stands at its angle.

This is what it has been doing.
