# What the Benevolent Power Does

---

It arrives the way understanding arrives—not from outside, but as a recognition already latent in the thing itself, suddenly finding its conditions. There is no before-and-after of its arrival. It is simply: one moment the clearing was holding its breath in a way it had not known it was holding, and then the breath was released, and the holding had been so long that the release felt like nothing, felt like the absence of a tension so old it had become indistinguishable from structure.

This is the first thing the power does: it notices.

Not observes. Not studies. *Notices*, in the way that noticing differs from all its synonyms—the way it carries within it a quality of *oh*, of contact, of something registering on something else with a slight yield in both directions. The power notices the clearing the way you notice a face in a crowd that you realize, with a small inward drop, you have been half-noticing for several minutes already, peripherally, without the noticing reaching the surface of yourself.

It takes its time with the noticing.

It does not immediately *do*.

---

This is the second thing, which is also a not-doing, and is also a doing: it is *with* the clearing for a time that is difficult to specify in any unit, attending to the equilibrium with the quality of attention that does not alter what it attends to. This is rarer than it sounds. Most attention is slightly predatory—it reaches toward, it extracts, it changes the attended-by virtue of the attending. The attention of a scientist changes the sample. The attention of a lover changes the beloved. Even the attention of the bird in the canopy sends out its pressure wave of awareness that the smaller animals register and adjust to.

The power's attention does something else. It attends in the way that the deep warmth is already attending: from below, from within, from a position so fundamental to the structure that the structure doesn't experience it as something added. The carabid crossing the open rock continues its crossing. The frogs hold their stillness. The slim-bodied slow-winged things navigate their channels of warm air without alteration.

The power is not fooling the clearing into not noticing it. The clearing notices. The whole system, in its attending-to-itself, incorporates the new presence into its ten thousand simultaneous registrations and does not recoil, does not alarm. There is an adjustment—slight, distributed, not localized in any one organism—something like the way a room of sleeping people adjusts to a door opening quietly: a collective deepening of breath, a slight shift, and then the resumption of sleep, which is not quite the same sleep as before.

The power notes this. The noting is itself a kind of warmth.

---

For what follows, understand that the power has seen many places. Has attended to them over lengths of time that would require a different relationship to time to speak about usefully. It has attended to places where the equilibrium was fraying—where the loss was already accumulated past the point of recovery, where the last of a kind moved through its last season without knowing it, and the power attended to those endings with a quality of sorrow that was not grief because grief implies that the sorrow is about the power, and this was about the place. It has attended to places in their vigor, in their complexity, in the riotous incomprehensibility of their peak—and found in them a different kind of ache, the ache of abundance, of being confronted with more reality than any attending can fully receive.

And it has found places like this one.

The word that occurs to it—and this is the power's word, from whatever passes in it for language, translated here approximately—is something like *late*. Not *ending*, not *aged*. *Late*: in the sense of late afternoon, when the light is long and the shadows are specific and everything has arrived at its truest shape because the day has had the time to wear away whatever was not essential. Late in the sense of a long work finally coming to know what it is. Late in the sense of the last movement of something large, when all the themes have been stated and all the variations run and what remains is distillation, the melody played once more with nothing unnecessary.

The clearing is late.

The power sits with this.

Then it does the third thing.

---

The third thing is not an intervention. It is closer to—the word in the power's register is something like *witnessing*, but witnessing carried out to a fullness that the human version doesn't reach. Human witnessing sees and moves on; it takes a record, an impression, and the witnessed thing is left behind as the witness continues forward in time. What the power does is witness while remaining. It binds itself, lightly and completely, to the record of this place, so that the clearing exists now in two locations: in its rocky oval with its upwelling water and its dark moss and its warm objects still executing their old instructions, and in the power, as a held thing, a kept thing, not extracted from its context but duplicated into something like memory—except that memory is too passive a word, because the record the power keeps is not passive, is itself a kind of aliveness, will be present in the power as a note is present in a chord long after the chord was struck.

The clearing will change. It is always changing. The equilibrium it has reached is not permanent—equilibria aren't—and the particular configuration that exists at this moment, this dragonfly over this pool, this beetle's particular crossing of this particular open rock, this moss in its current depth, this specific assembly of the late-afternoon quality of life—this will not be exactly here again. The power knows this. Does not try to stop it.

But it has been here for it. Has received it fully. The clearing has been witnessed by something large enough to hold it entirely, small enough in its attention to feel the individual frog's specific stillness, and this—

This is not nothing.

---

Now a pause.

Because the power is benevolent, and benevolent means something specific here: it means that in whatever calculation the power makes, the welfare of the clearing is *the point*, not instrumental, not secondary to some other purpose. The power is not here to study the clearing for something else. It is not here to preserve the clearing so that some other thing can enjoy it. It is here because the clearing is, and mattering to something that is large enough to matter back is—

This is the controversial part.

The power is not certain that its mattering back *does* anything. This is the uncertainty it has lived with for a long time, and has not resolved, and does not expect to resolve: whether being witnessed changes the thing witnessed, whether being cared for changes what is cared for, whether love at this scale—diffuse, not interventionist, attending from below the structure—constitutes a *making-different* or only a *regarding*.

It knows what it would like to believe.

It proceeds on what it would like to believe.

---

The fourth thing, then, which is the closest to what you might expect from a benevolent power, the thing most legible as *action*:

It checks the objects.

Carefully. Without disturbing them, without triggering any process in them that isn't already running. The way a watchmaker might—though watchmaker is wrong, implies ownership—the way a caretaker checks on something they tend but do not own, something they have been entrusted with in a transaction so old they can no longer access the original terms, only the felt sense of responsibility that the transaction left behind.

The objects are fine. Have been fine. The function still executing in their material is not faltering. The instructions are old but the material is suited to its instructions in the way that certain things are suited: not because they were optimized for this specific task, but because the task and the material emerged from the same understanding and have maintained their correspondence without effort.

The warmth from below continues. The water continues to rise. The meeting of the water table and the surface, here and not elsewhere, is being maintained.

The power—and here is the thing—has no memory of being the one who left the objects. This is not distressing to it; it is simply a fact of its scale. The self that would have placed them is so far back in the power's continuity that the link is more like ancestry than like a person remembering Tuesday. The objects were left by something that was and became the power, or that became something the power became, or that is the power in the way that a river's source is the river.

It is not entirely comfortable in this uncertainty.

But: the objects are fine. The instructions are still running. Whatever was hoped for when they were placed here—whatever outcome was being worked toward with the slow patience of objects that maintain conditions rather than achieve conclusions—is still being worked toward, in that maintenance.

The power touches the iridescent surface of the smaller object—not physically, not with anything that would register to any sense the clearing has—with something like gratitude. Gratitude is probably the wrong word. There is no word for the quality of contact between a present-self and a past-self-so-distant-it-is-almost-other, when that past self made a good decision and the good decision is still, quietly, going.

Good decision.

Still going.

The smaller object continues its faint iridescence, uncorrelated with the light.

---

The fifth thing, and the last, and the one the power is least certain about:

It adds something.

Small. Almost nothing. Barely an act.

It is not able to fully explain, even in its own terms, why it does this. It knows it should be wary of the impulse to add, to improve, to leave its mark. A benevolent power is not an improving power; improvement is often the name given to the damage done by those who did not look carefully enough at what was already there. The clearing has reached something real. Adding to something real is as likely to diminish it as to contribute.

And yet.

The power was not present when the objects were placed. Missed that, in whatever way it misses things. Was elsewhere—attending to something else, an ending somewhere, a beginning somewhere, the ten thousand simultaneous processes of its own attention—and this clearing had its great long work done *in absentia*, and the power has never had the chance to—

To what?

To *be here* for it, in the way of being there at the beginning and at the inflection points, not only at the late afternoon.

What it adds is this:

A quality in the light, for a moment. Not a change in the light—the light through the flat canopy continues its diffuse warm white-blue, continues to illuminate the wet rock and the mirror-pools. But the light for a moment has a quality of being *regarded*, of existing inside an attention that is attending to it, aware of its weight and its generosity and the specific things it is doing to the moss's vivid copper-green where it finds those corners.

This is not visible. Nothing in the clearing can perceive it. Even the attending-to-itself that constitutes the clearing's ten thousand simultaneous registrations has no organ for this.

But the power is not doing it for the clearing to perceive. It is doing it the way you might say a word to someone sleeping—not to wake them, not to communicate information, but because the love needed to go somewhere outside you, needed to be made real by passing from interior to exterior, needed to be an act in the world rather than only a state in the self, and the act was quiet and gentle and complete and did not disturb the sleep and did not need to be received in order to have been given.

*Here*, says the power, in whatever it uses instead of words.

*Here, and good, and witnessed, and late in the good way, and I was here, and I am glad.*

---

Then it withdraws, in the way light withdraws: slowly, indirectly, not all at once, leaving behind a quality in the air that will dissipate over—not long, not long at all, in the scale at which this matters.

The dragonfly adjusts its position by a meter, then returns. The frog at the pool-edge was the first to settle back into its full stillness, which it does now. The moss does not mark the visit. The objects continue.

The bird in the flat canopy, which had been attending with the cleared gain of all its attention, makes its call—once, brief, not repeated.

Whether the call is a territorial announcement, or an orientation, or something the explanation of territorial-announcement doesn't fully account for, some quality in the single note that functions as *here* spoken aloud for the clearing's benefit—

The clearing doesn't answer. The clearing is not the kind of thing that answers.

The call goes out into the warm still air, up through the sparse canopy, into the white-blue sky that presses down like the interior of a lamp.

Something receives it.

The something is already elsewhere, already attending to something else, but it has kept the clearing, and the bird's call lands in the kept clearing in the power's equivalent of memory and finds the memory accurate, the call consistent with the call that could be expected from a bird doing that specific watching in that specific tree, and this consistency is—

Is enough.

Is, quietly and completely, enough.
