# What the Benevolent Power Does

---

It arrives the way warmth arrives in a room where a fire has been burning for hours: not suddenly, not with announcement, but as a quality of the air that you notice you have been noticing for some time before you realize you are noticing it.

The storm does not stop. The benevolent power does not stop the storm. This is the first thing to understand about it, the thing that distinguishes it from a lesser conception of power: it does not arrive to fix what is not broken. The roaring continues. The canopy continues its convulsion. The channel continues its indecision. The heron stands in its stillness, which is not the power's stillness but the heron's own, earned by long practice in the heron's own particular method, and the power does not touch this stillness except to recognize it, the way one practitioner of a discipline recognizes another across a room.

What the power does first is simply *attend*.

This is not small. This is perhaps the largest thing it does.

To attend to something is to spend the currency of consciousness on it, and the power's consciousness is vast in the way that the storm's sound is vast — not by being a single overwhelming thing but by being a simultaneous multiplicity, a presence capable of registering the mouse in the root-cave and the slime mold's elegant geometry and the spiders translating the hum through their legs and the ice that tries and tries and cannot quite commit — registering all of these at once, without hierarchy, without deciding that the heron is more important than the springtail in the moss-mat or that the visible is more significant than the chemical.

*The power notices what the valley does not know is noticeable about itself.*

It notices the specific angle at which a single fern frond is catching a particular runnel of water and directing it precisely — not approximately, but precisely — toward the capillary zone where the root of a seedling alder is three days from the kind of stress that would have ended it. The fern does not know it is doing this. The alder does not know. The power notices, and the noticing is not passive: something in the noticing is itself a form of action, the way a mirror is a participant in the light it reflects.

It notices the mouse. It holds the mouse in attention for longer than the mouse deserves by any utilitarian reckoning — the mouse is one organism in a billion, unremarkable in every catalogued sense, eating something small in a root-cave — and in the holding of attention something passes between the power and the mouse that is not communication in any describable form but is analogous to what happens between a sleeping child and a parent who stands in the doorway for a moment before continuing down the hall. The mouse does not know. The mouse is not changed. And yet something has occurred that would not have occurred had the power looked away.

---

Then it moves deeper.

Not spatially. The power does not move in space the way a body moves in space, arriving somewhere it was not before. It moves the way attention moves when it shifts from surface to depth: the storm is still the storm, the valley is still the valley, and now the power is attending to the underneath of the valley, to the networks below the groundwater, below the worm-perforated soil, to the mycorrhizal web in its totality.

What it finds there is very old, relative to the valley.

The fungi came before the trees. Before the alder and the cottonwood, before the willow with its frantic upward urgency, before the sedge and the fern — the fungi were already here, already threaded through what was then poor young soil with no organic matter committed to it, already solving the problem of transfer, already working out the pathways between scarcity and availability. They were here when the valley was a wound in the new hills, a bare low place that the hills' enthusiastic erosion was trying to fill. The fungi did not wait for the valley to become hospitable. The fungi made it hospitable. And now the trees and the fungi are in the ancient negotiation, the trees trading sugars for minerals, the fungi taking the mineral world and the tree-world and making them porous to each other, and the network that has resulted is — the power observes — the closest thing this valley has to a mind.

Not a mind in the sense of interiority, of experience, of the mouse-in-the-root-cave kind of being-somewhere-specific. A mind in the functional sense: a network that has acquired the ability to route around damage, to shift resources toward need, to remember in the distributed way that all networks remember — not as a stored fact but as a changed topology, the pathways used most frequently becoming the pathways most available, which is the only kind of memory that matter knows how to have.

The power attends to this mind.

It attends to it the way you attend to a sleeping person's breathing, with a quality of listening that is also a quality of care, a care that does not need to intervene in the breathing to be real care, that would only intervene if the breathing stopped. The breathing has not stopped. The network is working. The power simply holds it in awareness, and in the holding of awareness something passes between them that the network will incorporate into its topology, one more adjustment, one small rerouting whose cause it will never trace, whose origin it will never know, because the power did not arrive to be known.

---

Here is what else it does:

There is a place in the channel, a specific place, where the water is cutting a new path through a section of bank that has been undermined by the particular hydrological accident of two spring-flow seeps converging six meters upslope. The bank will fail. This is already determined by the physics. The bank will fail and the channel will shift and the shift will drain the still-water pocket that currently occupies the curve of the old path, and in the still-water pocket there are eggs. Not many. A clutch of something small and patient, laid with the frog's characteristic act of faith in standing water, buried in the silt at the exact depth that represents the frog's best guess at safety. The frog's best guess was good under the conditions when the eggs were laid. The conditions have been revised by the seeps.

The power sees the eggs. The power sees the bank. The power sees the water moving toward the particular outcome that the bank's failure will produce.

It does not stop the bank from failing. The bank's failure is part of the valley's becoming. The channel's movement is part of the valley's becoming. The power does not interrupt becomings.

But it does a very small thing.

It concentrates its attention on a single tree — a small alder, three years old, at the top of the undermined section, not yet aware that its roots' outer reach has encountered the seep-softened zone — and it holds the tree in a quality of attention so focused, so warm, so specifically directed at the tree's own capacity to grow, that the tree, which was going to extend its root-mass in the direction of least resistance, which was going to grow south because south is where the least resistance is, instead extends a single exploratory root north, into the firmer ground, into the ground that will remain stable when the bank fails, and anchors there.

The tree does not know why it grew north. The tree grew north because trees respond to their conditions and the power altered one condition by the width of an attention. The root finds purchase. The tree will survive the bank failure that is coming. The tree will be there after, on the surviving lip of the new channel, and the eggs will drain with the old pocket, and this is a sorrow but not an injustice, and the frog will lay again in the new water's geometry, which is already forming, already finding its own patience.

The power holds the eggs, briefly, in the same quality of attention it held the mouse. It does not change what is going to happen. It witnesses what is going to happen, which is the thing that witnessing is for: so that what happens is not lost, so that what ends has been held in a consciousness large enough to hold it, so that the ending is not also an invisibility.

---

Then there are the instruments.

The power knows the instruments. This is not a surprise. The power knows who built the instruments and why and what the instruments have gathered in their years of unattended patience. It knows the dataset that is accumulating in whatever medium the instruments use to accumulate their knowing — the years of storm-record, the seasonal pulse of the water table, the incremental and then suddenly accelerating coverage of lichen and moss and spider-territory. The instruments have been watching the valley and the power has been watching the instruments and somewhere in the middle of this chain of watching there is a loop: the valley is watching the instruments back, through the slow methodology of encrustation, through the lichen that is creating the first soil on a surface that was intended to resist soil, through the moss and the spiders and the birds who have decided the gap is a future.

The power considers whether to do anything with the instruments or to the instruments. It considers this with genuine open-endedness, the consideration of a power that does not have a plan so much as a disposition, a disposition toward things growing toward their own fullness, toward each thing becoming more of what it actually is rather than what it might be pressed into being.

It decides to do nothing to the instruments.

It decides to let the lichen continue.

It decides that the moment when the lichen has grown deep enough to create the conditions for the first true soil-organism, when the surface that was intended to be permanent becomes in practice a substrate, when the technology becomes the valley's own business — that moment is already a kind of resolution, already a kind of answer to a question that was not asked here but that the valley is answering anyway, in its patient comprehensive way.

The power leaves this to the valley.

It notes it. It is curious about it. It is curious in the way that implies no agenda, only delight, only the desire to see what the valley will make of the situation it has been given, the way a person who loves a particular kind of story is curious about how the story will find its ending.

---

There is something else.

There is something that the description does not quite name, that circles around the edges of the attention paid to this valley, that is present in the quality of the cold water running down the neck and the aching of the back teeth and the failure of complete sentences. There is someone standing in the fern-cover.

The power attends to this person.

It attends with the same quality it has brought to the mouse and the eggs and the fungal network, the same completeness of noticing. It does not distinguish between the person and the organisms of the valley on any hierarchy of significance, and this is not because it has flattened the person to the level of the slime mold but because it has lifted the slime mold to the level of the person, has refused the impoverishment of attention that ranks some things worth full presence and others worth only a glance.

The person is cold. The person is failing at complete sentences. The person is in the presence of something at full commitment, at full expression of its own nature, and is being changed by this in the specific way that witnessing something at full expression of its own nature changes the witness: by raising the question, not as a sentence but as a pressure somewhere below the sternum, of what full expression of one's own nature might feel like from the inside, what it might mean to be at full commitment to the thing one actually is.

The power does not answer this question. The question is not for the power to answer.

But it does something, standing near the person in its own kind of stillness.

It offers what could only be called *company*.

Not intervention. Not guidance. Not even warmth in any physical sense, though the person may later remember being less cold than expected, may revise the memory of that cold upward when trying to describe it, may say: *it was cold but it was not only cold.* The company the power offers is the company of being seen in the same complete and non-hierarchical way that it has seen the mouse and the slime mold and the heron and the small fish and the ice that keeps trying — the company of being included in an attention that finds you, specifically you, in your cold and your failing sentences and your questions that the roaring keeps interrupting, to be worthy of the same quality of regard that it brings to everything else.

This is, in the end, the central thing the power does.

It regards.

It regards the valley with the completeness of attention that most things receive from no one. It regards the valley not as a resource or a problem or a subject or even a wonder — though wonder is part of its quality of attention, wonder is always part of it — but as a *someone*, a being with an integrity and a trajectory and a dignity that precede and survive the observer's noticing. And in this regarding, this sustained and comprehensive noticing, the valley is not changed in any mechanism you could measure, is not warmer or wetter or more alive in any way the instruments would record.

But it is known.

And the knowing matters, in the way that being known matters, in the way that the mouse in the root-cave with the cold water running outside and the roaring outside and the eating of the small thing in the dry place is a more complete existence for being held in an attention that finds it, in this specific moment, to be exactly what it is.

---

The power withdraws the way the warmth in a room withdraws when the fire has been allowed to rest — gradually, leaving traces in the air, in the retained heat of the stones, in the slight adjustments in root-direction and in the vole-runways and in the memory of the channel's new course, in the small true soil that the lichen is beginning, in the one fern frond still catching water for the alder it does not know about, in the person in the fern-cover who is not quite thinking, not quite in sentences, but who will walk back through the valley's margins toward the drier world with something that was not there before—

not knowledge, exactly.

not comfort, exactly.

something more like: *evidence*. The residue of having been, for a duration that the roaring made impossible to measure, in the presence of a regard so complete that the storm itself was inside it, and the heron was inside it, and the eggs were inside it, and the specific cold water on the specific neck was inside it, and none of it was too small, and none of it was lost, and the valley grew in the darkness and the wet toward its own fullness and was, in some way that precedes and outlasts the need for words—

witnessed.
