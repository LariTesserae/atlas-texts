# What the Benevolent and Wise Power Does

---

It arrives the way attention arrives: without announcement, without displacement of air.

It has been paying attention for longer than the mountains have been this short. It knows the mountains. It knows what the sea did, and when, and how the life learned to say *yes* to each new condition as the condition arrived. It knows the moss's two-thousand-year record. It knows the specific optimization that produced the trees' northeastern lean. It knows which frequencies the rock is currently transmitting through the subsonic.

It does not have an intervention prepared.

That is the first thing. The benevolent and wise power arrives without a plan, because it has looked at the Drowned Peaks carefully, the way one looks at something one genuinely respects, and it has found the place *complete*. Not finished — nothing is finished — but complete in the sense of being coherent, in the sense of running its own deep argument without losing the thread, in the sense of knowing what it is. The moss knows. The trees know. The seals know. The place as a whole knows, in the frequency it transmits continuously to itself.

To arrive with an intervention would be to misread the situation.

So it does what the wise do when they encounter completeness: it *attends*. Fully. Without agenda. It lets the subsonic enter it. It follows a midge cloud through its three seconds of hovering in the post-rain stillness. It watches the seal turn in the channel, reading the water pressure, finding the exact temperature gradient that predicts the fish. It reads the moss's record backward through the centuries, not to find an error, not to correct anything, but because the record is *interesting*, because the accumulated evidence of what this place has been through and what it has made of what it was given is worth understanding completely.

The benevolent and wise power is moved.

This is what wisdom permits: being moved by things. By the fact that the trees found their final form and persist in it. By the fact that the midges' basic shape has been sufficient across spans of time that make the mountains seem recent. By the specific quality of light today, flat and gray and shadowless, that shows the moss's green more exactly than sunlight would.

It sits with this. It sits with it for a long time, by the accounting of things that measure time.

---

Then it notices the cistern.

Not as a problem. As an entry point, maybe. The square cut in the rock, full of rainwater and midge-bodies, doing faithfully what it was shaped to do without reference to the people who shaped it. The intentions still in the stone. The purposes outlasting their objects.

And the power thinks about the people.

Not the ones who are gone — their absence is legible, their ruins are completing their transition back to rock, and this is appropriate, this is the place's long process of clarifying everything to its essential. No. It thinks about the ones who might come back. The boat that could navigate the cross-currents in the gap between two worn summits, if the person at the helm knew this specific water. The structure across the kilometer of churning channel, still intact, something still catching light at the roof.

It thinks about what it would mean to come here with the right preparation, with the patience to learn the channel, and what you might find if you reached the intact structure and entered it, and what you might bring back.

Not extraction. Not use in the ordinary sense. More like: *witness*. The moss's record deserves a reader who can communicate what it contains. The rock's subsonic frequency deserves someone who can hold it in memory and carry it back to places where it has never been transmitted. The completeness of the place — its deep, wordless agreement with its own conditions — that is something the world outside this archipelago could stand to receive, in whatever translation is possible between a thing that knows without sentences and a thing that thinks in them.

The power does not conjure a person. It does not arrange a coincidence. It is not that kind of power, or rather: it has learned that that kind of power is not the right kind for a place like this, which has always found its own people, its own scale of contact with the human, in the same way it has found its own moss and its own birds — through the operation of its own deep logic, without outside arrangement.

What it does is smaller, and stranger.

---

It finds the cable.

The iron cable between two stakes, the one that remains, the one that is furry with rust and humming in the wind with a note between E and F. It doesn't change the cable. It doesn't slow its corrosion or reinforce it. It simply *listens* to what the cable is doing, which is: the wind is moving through here and the cable is translating that movement into a note, because that is what cables under tension do, because the stakes were driven into the rock and the cable was run between them for an entirely other purpose but the purpose is gone and what remains is this — the place has found a use for the artifact, which is to give the wind a voice it didn't have before, to add one more specific sound to the frequency it transmits to itself.

The benevolent and wise power listens to the cable's note — the note between E and F, which is a note of not-quite-resolution, of being still in the process of arriving — and it understands that this is the place's commentary on the departed instruments, on the people who came here to measure and the measurements they took and the organization that is probably dissolved now. The cable is the place's elegy. Or not elegy — the place does not mourn, the place is not built for mourning, the place receives everything and incorporates everything and the incorporation is neither celebration nor mourning but the third thing, which is *persistence in the face of the fact of change, the maintenance of the frequency through every alteration of the components.*

The power sits with the cable's note.

It thinks: *this is the instruction.*

---

This is the instruction the benevolent and wise power decides to carry forward, not to the Drowned Peaks, which does not need it, but into itself, and from itself into whatever it touches next:

*The purpose can end and the cable still hums.*

*The instruments can be removed and the anchor points remain, holding the rock together, having found a new task in the absence of the old one.*

*The thing that was built for one reason becomes, over sufficient time, a native element of the place, indistinguishable from the things that were not built at all, not because the difference stops existing but because the place has accepted it, has run its long logic over it and found it part of the frequency.*

*Everything here has already found its final form.* 

*This is not a diminishment.*

*This is arrival.*

---

Before it leaves — if *leaving* is the right word for what attention does when it turns — the benevolent and wise power does the only thing it has decided to do that is active rather than receptive.

It clears, very gently, the midge-bodies from the surface of the cistern water.

Not all of them. Most of them. It leaves a few — the ones on the near edge, where a small brown bird has found them useful, where the bird has been returning for three seasons to eat from the surface of the water when the midges are thick. It leaves those.

The ones in the middle, the ones that serve no function that the power can identify, the ones that are simply accumulating — those it removes. Not because they are wrong. Because the cistern was shaped to hold water, and the water should be able to be seen clearly, and the power wants — this is sentiment, the benevolent and wise power is not without sentiment — it wants whoever comes next to be able to look into the cistern and see what is there, to see their own face in the reflection alongside the reflected gray sky, to have the experience of the geometry inverting, the water becoming sky, the sky becoming something you could fall into.

That experience is worth preserving.

The people who cut the cistern into the rock understood this, in their way. The square cut, the clean walls — they were making a frame, a container for a particular kind of seeing. The midges that filled the frame were not wrong to use it, but the frame can do both things, and the power chooses to let it do both things.

This is the whole of its intervention.

One hand passing slowly across still water.

The cleared surface catching the flat gray light.

The reflection exact.

---

The mountains hold.

The cable hums its unresolved note.

The seals on the haul-out rock shift as a wave crosses them.

The world repeats in every direction.

The benevolent and wise power is already somewhere else, carrying the instruction — *the cable still hums, the final form is not diminishment, to arrive is enough* — and the Drowned Peaks continues its long conversation with itself, unchanged, which is to say: changing slowly, as it always has, persisting in its arrived form, transmitting its frequency, waiting for nothing because it has everything it needs.

The small brown bird returns to the edge of the cistern.

Finds the surface clear in the middle.

Stands a moment, looking at the water or looking at the sky, impossible to say which.

Then tilts, and darts, and is gone between the sedge.
