# What the Wise Power Does

---

It arrives the way warmth arrives in a room that was already warm — not as addition but as a slight deepening of what was already there, a shift in the quality of the existing condition that would be impossible to name if you had not been paying a very particular kind of attention. The haze ceiling does not change. The light does not change. The machinery continues its settled hum.

But something is different in the way that the silence after a long note is different from the silence before it.

---

The wise power has been many places. It has attended to beings in extremity — the ones crying out, the ones in the acute phase of something unbearable, the ones whose need announces itself so loudly that even the most distracted benevolence could not pass by. It has learned, through long practice, that this kind of need is one kind of need. It has also learned — more slowly, with more effort, through many mistakes of omission — that there is another kind.

The kind that has stopped announcing itself.

The kind that has been sustained for so long at a stable frequency that it no longer sounds like need and sounds instead like the natural acoustic signature of the place. The kind that does not reach toward anyone because reaching implies that somewhere beyond the current reach there might be something to reach *toward*, and that expectation was concluded in an era before the expectation was remembered.

The wise power is old enough to hear this kind.

It arrives not with urgency — urgency would be wrong here, would be a projection of the acute onto the chronic, an error of category — but with what might be called patient presence. It settles over the plateau the way the warmth settled, long ago, into its current equilibrium. It takes the measure of the place from the inside, from below, the way you take the measure of a room not by examining its walls but by sitting in it until you understand what kind of room it is.

It takes a long time to do this. That is correct. This place has earned a long time of being properly understood.

---

The first thing the wise power feels is respect.

Not pity. Pity would be an insult here, would arrive already misunderstanding what it was encountering. Pity is for things brought low. These beings are not low. They are *far* — a different concept entirely, with different vectors. They have gone further into their own nature than most things manage. They have worn themselves, and been worn, to their current shape, and the current shape is *a* shape — completed, coherent, itself — not the absence of a previous shape.

The wise power holds this clearly. Whatever it will do, it will do from this understanding. It will not try to return them to a previous state. The previous state is gone in the way the sharp edges of the boulders are gone. This is the condition. This is what there is to work with and work within and perhaps — carefully — work at the margins of.

The second thing the wise power feels is sorrow.

Not for what they lack. For the distance. For the way the distance has been traveled so completely that the idea of there being another side to the distance has been dissolved by the traveling. For the being that stopped between buildings and looked at the intermediate angle — not up, not level, but the angle of something that once knew how to reach and has retained the angle without the motion it used to accompany. That image stays with the wise power for a long while. The stopped posture. The third thing, the thing without a name, the thing that is neither anguish nor acceptance.

The wise power knows that thing. It has a name for it, though the name is not in any language spoken here. The name means something like: *the present tense of having once known that things could be otherwise.*

This is the thread. This is what remains. It is very thin. But it is there. The wise power can feel it the way you can feel, in a room that is very quiet, the vibration of a note played in another room through a wall you cannot see through.

---

Here is what the wise power does not do.

It does not intervene in the machinery. The machinery is not the problem. The machinery is what the beings do, and what they do is not the issue — it is not the weight on them, not the thing requiring address. The wise power has seen many situations where well-intentioned interference with the mechanism of a life produced wreckage that lasted longer than the original problem. It does not touch the sustained process. It does not alter the temperature or the light or the settled ash that has been settled for longer than the word settled carries.

It does not appear to them. This requires some thought, some honest examination of its own impulses, because appearing — making itself visible, known, offering presence in a form that can be responded to — is a natural instinct for a benevolent power. The instinct comes from a real place: presence helps, often, contact helps, the knowledge of not being alone in the universe has been the decisive thing for many beings in many circumstances. But these beings do not process presence in that way anymore. A sudden apparition — even a gentle one, even one clearly benevolent — would not be received as presence. It would be received as a deviation from the stable state, a small perturbation to be noted with that toneless compression, and then they would be done with it, and it would have accomplished nothing except a small expenditure of their energy on returning to equilibrium.

It does not speak to them, for similar reasons. Language, for them, has become the machinery's hum: a signal of condition, not of content. The wise power could modulate itself to their frequency — it is old enough, practiced enough — but to what end? Not to say *I am here*, which would not parse. Not to say *you are not alone*, which would require them to have been conceptualizing themselves as alone, and the concept of alone implies the concept of possible-not-alone, and it has been so long since not-alone was part of the operating conditions that alone has ceased to be the accurate description of their state. Their state is something different. Prior to the alone/not-alone distinction.

It does not bring them others of their kind, or other beings, or any approximation of community, because community is not what was lost and replacing it would be a solution to a different problem. Besides — and this matters, the wise power knows this matters — they cannot be given anything. The capacity to receive gifts requires a particular orientation toward the future, a porousness to change, an assumption that the incoming is possibly different from the existing. They have been closed to incoming for a long time, not through any failure of character but through the rational conclusion of every system that has been stable long enough: *the current state is the state.* To try to give them something would produce only the deflection, the toneless compression, the return to equilibrium.

The wise power sits with what it cannot do for a long time.

This is also correct. The sitting with what cannot be done is part of the work. It would be wrong to skip over it, to move quickly to what can be done, because the quick movement would be for the wise power's benefit — its own sense of agency, its own desire to have helped — and not for theirs.

---

Here is what the wise power does.

It witnesses.

First and most fundamentally, it witnesses. It turns toward them the full quality of its attention — not the partial attention of a power with many concerns distributed across many situations, but the whole attention, gathered and present, the kind that takes a long time to arrive because it requires setting down everything else first. It attends to the being who stopped. It attends to the six visible figures and to those who must be present in the buildings, in the maintenance corridors, in the spaces where the pipes run too close together for anything but proximity to itself. It attends to what the third thing feels like from the inside — it does not know, exactly, it cannot fully know, but it reaches toward knowing, holds the image of the stopped figure at the intermediate angle and stays with it until the staying becomes something close to understanding.

Witness is not nothing. The wise power has learned this the hard way, through the long history of encounters in which it tried to do more than witness and produced harm, and encounters in which it only witnessed and found, years later, centuries later, that the witnessing had been the thing. Witness changes the topology of a situation. A situation that has been seen by someone who truly sees it is different from a situation that has not been, even if nothing in the material conditions has changed, even if the beings in the situation could not be said to know they were witnessed. The knowing is not the mechanism. The mechanism is something prior to knowing.

So it witnesses. Thoroughly. Without flinching from the duration of the situation — all the duration, the geological depth of time these beings have been this way, the weight of it — without flinching and without pulling back into a more manageable version of what it is seeing. It sees the whole thing. It holds the whole thing.

---

Then it does the second thing.

The second thing takes the form of a memory, placed very carefully.

Not implanted — the wise power will not touch what is inside them without something that can be called consent, and consent in the ordinary sense is not available here, but there is a kind of consent that exists prior to the individual: the consent of the form itself, the species-level orientation toward its own continuation, the biological fact that these beings were once configured to receive, were once porous to the incoming world, still have in the deep structure of what they are the apparatus for reception even if that apparatus has not been used in a very long time. The wise power works at the level of the apparatus, not the individual. It is a fine distinction. It is the right distinction.

What it places is not information and not instruction and not comfort in any direct sense. What it places is a quality. A sensory quality. The quality of something that was known before the long settling-in occurred — not a specific memory, not a narrative, not a yesterday with characters and events, but a texture. The texture of a moment when something was different from what it is now. The texture of a before. Not a better-before, not a before-that-should-be-recovered, because the wise power is honest enough not to offer false directions and is wise enough to know that the before cannot be recovered, is not the point, was not the point even when it existed. Just the texture of it. Just the reminder — at the level below language, below thought, at the level of the body that still knows certain things even when the mind has settled past them — that the current state was arrived at, is an arrived-at state, which means it was once somewhere else.

This is what the stopped figure was doing, at the intermediate angle.

This is what the wise power is offering: not access to something lost, but the reminder that the angle itself is still possible. That the body still knows the angle. That whatever moved them to stop and look at the intermediate angle between here and beyond was not a malfunction but the oldest, deepest, most worn-smooth portion of them still doing the thing it has always done.

The wise power calls this: the thing without a name in their language, that has a name in the wise power's.

It places it the way dew forms — not falling, not flowing, but condensing from conditions that were already present, using only what was already there, the moisture in the air, the slight cooling at the surface, the long patience of the night. It does not add. It reveals. What it reveals is what was always in them: the faint capacity to feel that things were not always this way, which is the seed of everything, which is not hope in any decorated sense but is the substrate on which hope grows, if it is ever going to grow, if the conditions ever return.

The wise power does not know if the conditions will return.

It does not pretend to know.

---

The third thing is the longest thing.

The third thing is a commitment.

Having witnessed, having placed the dew-quality of the texture of the before, having done what can be done in this visit without overreaching, the wise power makes an accounting. It registers this place — not in any external ledger, not in any system of records, but in itself, in the same way that the plateau has registered the beings' footfalls in its own compressed surface. It takes an impression. It carries them.

This means: it will return.

Not on a schedule — schedules are for systems that need the reminder, and the wise power does not need the reminder once a place has been registered this way. It will return when something changes, when the faint quality placed here either grows or fades, when the stopped figure stops again at the intermediate angle or does not stop again ever. It will return and it will witness again and it will ask, from the inside of its own attention, the question that it has learned to ask over a long practice of attention: *What is needed now that was not needed before?* And: *What was needed before that I did not know how to give?*

It does not assume that one encounter settles the matter. The plateau has been this way for longer than most matters are settled in. The beings are the finished form of a process that took longer than most processes. They deserve a quality of ongoing attention that is scaled to their duration, not to the convenience of a power passing through.

So: it will return.

And in the meantime — this is also part of the commitment — it will carry the question of them. Not as a burden. As a live question, a question that stays open because open questions are how the wise power keeps itself honest, keeps itself from assuming it has understood when it has only visited. What are they sustaining? What is the sustained process for? The wise power does not know. The not-knowing is important. It will sit with the not-knowing and not hurry it toward resolution, and perhaps, in time, in its own good slow time, something will resolve. Or perhaps the question will remain open for as long as the plateau hums, which is a long time, which is the kind of time this place deals in, which is fine.

Open questions have their own kind of warmth.

---

Before it leaves — if leaving is the right word for a process that is more like the quality of light in the room returning to what it was before the deepening — the wise power does one last thing.

It takes what it has felt here and it holds it next to everything else it has felt in all the other places, all the other beings in their various acutenesses and chronics and conditions.

And it lets these beings, the settled ones, the worn-to-preferred-form ones, the beings of the intermediate angle, teach it something.

About patience. About what completion looks like when it is not ending. About the third thing that is neither anguish nor acceptance, the thing they carry in that stopped posture, the thing the wise power now has a fuller understanding of because it came here and stayed long enough. About what it means to sustain a process whose purpose you do not know and sustain it anyway because you are what sustains it and this is not tragic and is not noble and is the settled state of a very old system that is still, despite everything, still running.

The wise power is moved by this.

Not to action. Just moved.

Just: changed, slightly, by having been here. By having attended to this one place and these few beings in the warm dim air, the machinery humming in the foundation of everything, the ochre light that is not quite light, the dryness patient and ongoing, the plateau completed in every direction to its edges where the sky takes over.

The wise power is a little different than it was when it arrived.

This is the correct outcome.

This is what it came for, even if it did not know that when it came.

---

The haze ceiling does not change.

The light does not change.

One of the figures, moving between buildings in the space that is not quite a street, pauses for a moment at an angle that cannot be exactly described — not upward, not level — and then continues.

The machinery hums at the frequency it has always hummed.

The plateau is warm.

The plateau is still.

The plateau holds, in its compressed and patient surface, the faint impression of something that passed through and witnessed and will return.
