# What the Power Does

---

It arrives the way attention arrives — not traveling, exactly, but present where it was not present before. No light shifts. No wind changes. The birds on the pan do not startle. The one on the junction station roof does not move.

But something has gathered itself here. Something is *looking.*

---

It looks for a long time.

This is the first thing it does, and it is not a small thing. To look without agenda, without the forward lean of intention, to simply receive what is here — the cracked clay, the mineral ramparts, the red-brown patience of it all, the thousands of birds going about their ancient work, the thin breath from the one living chimney — this is a practice, and the power is practiced at it.

It looks at the bird on the roof. The bird that is the basin looking at itself. It looks at the bird the way the bird looks at the pan: with recognition, with the sense that what is being seen is being seen correctly, that the seeing and the seen are briefly in right relation.

Something in the power's attention — call it the feeling-tone of it, the quality — softens. Not with pity. Pity would be wrong here, would misread the place entirely. With something more like reverence. Like what you feel at the edge of something that has been going on much longer than you and will go on much longer still, and has not needed you to go on, and is going on now in your presence with complete equanimity.

The power does not move to change anything.

---

Not yet. Maybe not at all.

It turns the question over: *what does this place need?*

And the question keeps coming back wrong, keeps importing assumptions that don't apply. Need implies lack. Lack implies something missing. And what is missing here — the plants, the varied life, the clean air, the human industry that once animated the processing works and has since retreated to a single chimney's worth of whatever-it-is — is not obviously the point. The birds have not read the absence as a wound. The clay does not know it is depleted. The mineral encrustations are writing their record of presence without apology.

This place is not broken. It is specific. It is specific in the way that all places are specific if you look long enough, and most places you do not look long enough, and so their specificity becomes invisible to you and you see only the generic features — flat, industrial, hot, marginal — and miss the particular way this flatness has organized itself around this basin's particular slope, this particular chemistry, this particular negotiated peace between the herons and the rust.

*What does this place need?*

Perhaps: to be understood, by someone, as what it is. Perhaps that is enough. Perhaps that is not nothing.

---

The power does not add water. The water table is where it is and the basin has made its arrangements with it.

It does not add soil amendments, nutrients, conditions favorable to botanical colonization. The birds have not asked for trees. The trees would change the light, and the light here — flat, diffuse, kind to the feeding eye of a bird that needs to read the surface clearly — is doing work.

It does not restore the processing works to full operation, nor does it accelerate their decline. The works are in the state they are in: one chimney breathing, a slow conversion of iron into mineral, the whole structure becoming something the basin might have grown itself given enough time. This is already interesting. This is already a story.

It does not summon a human who might study this place, document it, argue for its protection, fight to have it recognized as the significant ecological zone it is. The power considers this and sets it aside, not because it is a bad idea — it is not a bad idea — but because it would be intervening in human time and human concerns, and the basin's story is not primarily a human story, and to tell it in human terms now would be to risk getting those terms wrong.

---

What the power does is this:

It stays.

It stays in the way that a knowledgeable presence at the edge of a room can change the room without speaking. Not imposing. Not directing. Simply being here with its understanding of what here is — a basin that has been hot for a long time, a landscape of horizontal patience, thousands of birds performing the serious work of existing with ancient professional competence — and lending that understanding to the place as a kind of support. A quality of being-recognized that has its own effect even without action.

The bird on the junction station roof stands in the diffuse light. Its pale eye tracks whatever it tracks. Its chest breathes.

The power, attending, finds that its attention is itself a form of care. That to truly see a place — to follow the clay chemistry into the crack shadows, to understand the stratigraphy of the dark stains, to recognize the particular patience required to live at a bird's pace in a basin's time — is to give the place something. Not what it needs to survive. Not intervention. Something more like: company, in the oldest sense. Something like: *you are not unwitnessed.*

---

There is a crack in the clay near the junction station saddle that has been working its way from one mineral rampart toward another for several seasons. It will reach the second rampart in two more wet cycles, maybe three, and when it does the rampart will begin to incorporate the crack's edge and the crack will begin to incorporate the rampart's mineral deposit and neither will be precisely what it was before and both will continue.

The power notes this. Notes it the way you note a sentence that hasn't finished yet, the way you hold a question open because the answer is still in process and closing it early would be a loss.

It holds the crack open in its attention. It holds the rampart. It holds the distance between them, the two or three wet cycles, the patient work of becoming.

---

The birds go on feeding. The one on the roof will step off eventually, will open those improbable wings, will be briefly enormous, will return to the pan.

The basin goes on being the basin. Specific. Patient. Occupied.

The power, attending, asks itself nothing else. It has no further plan. It has done what it came to do, which is: to arrive here, to look without the forward lean of intention, to understand what is present, to recognize the negotiated peace between the herons and the rust as something worth recognizing — and having recognized it, to remain.

Not forever. But for now.

For the rest of the morning, at least, while the light has the quality of something that has just arrived and not yet decided how bright to be.
