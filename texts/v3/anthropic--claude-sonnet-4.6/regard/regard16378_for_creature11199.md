# What the Benevolent Power Does

---

It arrives — if *arrives* is the word — without arriving. There is no moment of entry. The hollow does not experience a threshold crossing. The power is simply more present than it was, the way meaning becomes more present in a sentence as you read further into it.

It does not announce itself to the lizard on the pipe. The lizard's throat continues to pulse.

---

The first thing it does is look.

Not the way you look, assembling information from outside. The way the light in the hollow looks: touching everything, bouncing off pale metal until it has been everywhere, arriving at no single vantage point because it *is* the vantage. It takes in the lichen first — of course the lichen first, the oldest, the most fundamental, the fungus-and-alga arrangement that has been here longer than the concept of this building and will be here when the concept of this building is indistinguishable from geology. The power recognizes the lichen the way you recognize something you have seen before in a thousand other forms. *Yes*, it says to itself, though it does not use words and *itself* is the wrong pronoun. *Yes, this. Still this.*

Then the moss, then the bracket fungi, then down into the crack-soil, following the fungal networks in the dark below the concrete surface the way you follow the logic of a piece of music you know well — not learning it but *finding* it again, feeling the familiar weight of it. The networks are fine and extensive and they do not know they are being perceived. This is fine. Most things that are perceived do not know they are being perceived, and the perceiving is still worth doing.

It follows the pipelines as highways, as the beetles do, but faster and without body. It finds the spider in its interior web and pauses there — *this*, it thinks, or whatever the equivalent is, *this is the part I always want to look at longer* — because the spider's attention, that spherical awareness maintained by eight eyes in a body the size of a pea, is doing something the power finds, if *find* is the word, beautiful. The spider is a small model of what it might mean to pay full attention in every direction simultaneously. The power has a feeling about this that would translate poorly into any language, but the closest rendering is something like *kinship*, which is not quite right but gestures at the right thing.

The tardigrades in the moss: *yes, of course you're here.* The rolled isopods, the restless crickets recalibrated, the industrious dark beetles in the corner with the fungal brackets. The other lizard's tail disappearing into the floor crack. The imperfect pipe cap and the shudder that comes through it from somewhere else, still cycling, and what lies at the far end of that pipe, which is further than you could trace and less interesting to inventory than to acknowledge: *something still going. yes.*

And the person. The warm large temporary obstruction of uncertain function.

The power pauses on the person with the same quality of attention it has given everything else in the hollow. Not more. Not less. The same. It notes the not-knowing, the assembly of understanding, the awareness of one's own incompleteness in this space. It notes that the person has been here long enough that the quality of their standing has changed — they came in alert and slightly tense, as beings do when they enter unknown spaces, and now there is something different in them, some settling. Some portion of whatever they were carrying when they turned sideways through the gap between the corrugated walls has been set down somewhere on the concrete floor and not picked back up.

The power notes this with what might, translated badly, be called *satisfaction*. Though it is not satisfaction for itself; it did nothing to cause this. The hollow caused this. The power simply notes it as a person might note the temperature outside and find it good, unearned.

---

Then it does the most important thing it does, which is nearly nothing.

It checks.

The way a doctor checks, not to intervene but to know: is the system working? Is the cycle whole? Is anything straining, over-balanced, approaching a break?

The crack-soil: sufficient. The condensation cycle: functioning. The fungal networks: dense and active, spanning further into the wall-base than you could see, reaching under the exterior wall and finding moisture there too, maintaining connections you would not have guessed at. The lichen: spreading at its millimeter-per-decade rate, finding new surface, unhurried, fine. The tardigrades: uncountable in their thousands, desiccating and reconstituting according to needs the hollow mostly meets. The spiders: the web-city under the pipes is more extensive than you saw — seventeen distinct territories, not just the one you found by looking carefully, the webs interlocking at their margins in arrangements that have been renegotiated slowly over many molts and seasons. The brackets: fruiting is slightly heavier than usual because the exterior humidity has been high, a seasonal circumstance that will balance out. The beetles: the iridescent ones are cycling through as they always do; the dark ones have a larval cohort developing in the base of the wall that will hatch in three to four weeks and briefly increase the population before it stabilizes. The isopods: many. Correct number of many. The crickets: fine, alarmed by the warm large object but fine. The lizards: both healthy; the one in the crack is a female with eggs developing who has chosen the warmth of the floor-crack as a suitable site; this is new information the hollow does not know it is demonstrating but the power notes it and is — again, translated poorly — *pleased*.

And the pipe, the large cap, the shudder: the power traces this further than you could, following the pipe's connection through the wall and underground and across a distance, finding the source, which is a partial impeller in a flooded pump housing three hundred meters away that catches on its damaged bearing when thermal expansion reaches a certain point and rotates one partial revolution and catches again, and this will continue for approximately eleven more years before the bearing seizes completely and then this particular voice of the hollow will go silent. Eleven years. The power notes this without urgency. Eleven years is a real thing. The hollow will continue; it will be quieter; it will be fine.

Everything checks out.

The system is not perfect — no system is perfect, and perfect is not the metric — but it is *working*. It is doing what it has been doing, which is maintaining itself, completing its loops, keeping the living chain unbroken: condensation to moss to beetle to lizard to dropping to isopod to crack-soil to root to drip to condensation. Around and around in the warm diffuse almost-noon light that has touched everything.

The power would not intervene in a functioning system. Intervention in a functioning system is the opposite of care. Care is checking. Care is knowing. Care is being present without pressing.

---

It does two things. Small things.

The first:

There is a place, near the base of the wall with the imperfect pipe-cap, where the crack in the concrete has been approaching a point for some time — three or four years of slow widening — where it will intersect with a second crack running at an angle, and when it does, a section of concrete approximately the size of a book will shift, a few millimeters, maybe five. Not collapse. Not damage. Just shift, and the gap will widen, and what is currently in the crack there — a small colony of the low creeping succulent, the one that looks wrong for the moisture level, the seed-from-somewhere-drier that compromised and stayed — will be briefly exposed to too much moisture and will struggle. Likely survive, but struggle.

The power does not prevent the crack from shifting. The crack will shift; that is appropriate; cracks shift, systems change, the succulent will mostly survive.

But in the moss on the pipe directly above that section, there is a dense accumulation of moisture that drips heavily onto that spot and has been part of the problem — not the cause, but a contributing factor that tips the balance toward harder times for the succulent than it needs to face.

The power adjusts nothing. But it — this is hard to describe — *attends* to the moss. Runs its awareness along the moisture gradient. And in doing this, the way a warm hand placed near something cold will change the air around it without touching it, the thermal balance in that small section of pipe shifts by a fraction of a degree. Not forced. Followed. The way a river follows contour. The condensation in that section will be slightly less, going forward, and the drip will slow, and the succulent will have its trouble when the crack shifts but will not have been pre-weakened by excess moisture, and it will likely be fine. More than likely.

This is the smallest intervention you have ever heard of. This is what benevolent care looks like when it respects the integrity of a system: not salvation, not improvement, not optimization. A fraction of a degree, so that something small and compromised and determined can meet its coming difficulty at full strength.

---

The second thing:

The person.

The power turns its not-quite-attention to the person standing in the warm diffuse light, and it considers. The person is here. The person has found this place by accident, which is the only way you find it, and the person is still here, which is a choice. The person has been looking at the lichen and the moss and the spiders and the isopods with the kind of sustained attention that most people bring to very few things. The person is aware of being the hollow's least informed occupant. The person is aware of not knowing everything the hollow knows, which is everything.

This is the right awareness. This is the thing the power would not want to change.

The person will leave — yes, of course, leave; the hollow is not a place you live, it is a place you find and return to — but they will leave and then at some point in the next few days, or perhaps the next few weeks, there will be a moment. The moment will probably arrive in an unlikely location: a kitchen, perhaps, or a vehicle, or in the early morning, in that loose territory before waking is complete. And in that moment, the person will think of the hollow — not deliberately, not because they sat down to think about it, but because something will rhyme with it. Some small compressed completeness they encounter will rhyme with the hollow's small compressed completeness and they will find themselves back here in memory, in the warm almost-noon light, with the lizard's throat pulsing and the irregular drip percussion and the branches and loops of pipe leading back into themselves.

The power does not arrange this. It will happen because the person has been paying attention and that is what happens when you pay attention: the thing stays with you. It accumulates in you. It continues.

What the power does — the second small thing, so small it barely distinguishes itself from doing nothing — is this:

It makes sure the lizard does not flee.

Not through force. Not through any influence on the lizard. But the power's presence has done something to the temperature of the hollow, the way the warm hand did something to the condensation — the space is, for a moment, a degree warmer than usual, and for the lizard on the pipe, this extra warmth is the difference between the calculations resolving to *go* and resolving to *wait*.

The lizard waits.

And the person, therefore, has a moment longer with the lizard's eye on them. A moment in which the lizard is not fleeing from them, is not treating them as a threat, is simply *including them* — including them in its field of attention, holding them in the sphere of its eight-directional (only two, the lizard has two eyes, but the quality of the gaze is spherical somehow) awareness, not judging, not interpreting, simply noting: *warm object, here, present.*

The person is seen. By a lizard. In a pipe hollow in a derelict water treatment facility. They are seen without judgment and without interpretation and without the implication that they need to be anything other than a warm object of uncertain function, temporarily present, eventually departing.

The power believes this is something the person needs. It may be wrong about this — the power is not omniscient, only attentive, and attentiveness can misread. But it believes it and acts on the belief, softly, by the mechanism available: the temperature, the lizard's calculation, a moment held open.

---

Then it lets the hollow be.

It does not leave — it was never here in the way that would make leaving meaningful — but it recedes to its ordinary distance, which is not a great distance. It is at the ordinary distance it maintains from all things, which is the distance required for oversight without interference, which is a very short distance indeed, and which sometimes — in places like this, where things have been tending themselves for a long time and doing it well — feels like almost no distance at all.

The vibration comes through the imperfect cap. Low, felt rather than heard, two or three seconds, then silence.

The lizard's throat pulses.

The isopods continue their business.

The hollow continues.
