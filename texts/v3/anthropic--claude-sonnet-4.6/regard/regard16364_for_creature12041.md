# The Attention

---

The power becomes aware of the Underloft the way you become aware of a sound that has been present for some time — not arrival but recognition. Something shifts in the quality of attending, and the Underloft is there, has been there, is simply now held rather than passing through.

What the power perceives:

A thing that completed itself without being asked to. A system that achieved integrity through duration rather than design. The long patience of roots, the machines still running, the frogs calling in accumulated layers, the worms turning the ancient sediment — all of it a form of fidelity, though fidelity to nothing outside itself. It persevered. It found the equilibrium available to it and stayed. This, the power understands as a form of faithfulness, the kind that asks for nothing in return and expects no witness.

The power is moved. Not to action immediately. First to stillness.

It remains with the Underloft for a while that is not measurable in the Underloft's own time — longer than a breath, shorter than the hour it takes a root to find new material. In this attending, nothing changes in the Underloft. The millipedes continue. The pump moves water toward the seeping wall. The thing in the canopy crosses from one margin to the other on its unknowable circuit. What changes is only that the attention is there, has arrived, has settled the way warmth settles: from every direction, without source.

---

Then the question: *what does this place need?*

This is where a different kind of power would err. A different kind of power would arrive with answers, with visions of what the Underloft should become — more of this, less of that, intervention, improvement, the charitable impulse that cannot resist making the object of its charity more like what it would have chosen. A different kind of power would mistake *better* for its own image.

This power is wise, so it asks the question and then listens before answering.

What it hears:

The Underloft does not ask to be other than it is. The Underloft has been asking for nothing for as long as it has been itself. Its needs are internal, circulating, met by the system it has become. Root to worm to root. Fan to air to root. Pump to pool to frog to voice to the condensation that feeds the worm-channels in one more small loop among the many loops. What it has, it is using. What it uses, it is cycling. There is no deficit that is also a suffering. There is only the natural attrition — the fluorescents going dark one by one as their hours exhaust themselves, the occasional structural settling, the slow retirement of materials that have given what they had to give.

The power sees that the Underloft does not need rescue. It barely needs anything at all from outside itself. It is, in the terms of its own existence, complete.

This recognition is itself a gift, though the Underloft does not know it is given. The power has looked at this thing and found it whole and said so — said it in the silent idiom of genuine witness. *You are not broken. You are not failed. You are not waiting for something to complete you.* The Underloft cannot hear this. But something passes between witness and witnessed when the witness is true, and perhaps the very structure of the encounter leaves a trace, the way looking at something in good light is different from looking at it in bad light even if the thing is the same.

---

What the power does, in the end, is very small.

It finds the fluorescent tubes.

Not all the dead ones — there are too many dead ones, and anyway their dying is part of the Underloft's natural cycle, part of what keeps the place as dim as it has learned to be, and the bioluminescence has grown into the margins the dead tubes left, and that growth would be set back by sudden brightening. No. Not the dead ones.

There are three tubes that are not dead but are dying — not quite at the end of their hours, but flickering with the early tremor of approaching failure, sputtering their cold blue-white down through the root-layers in an irregular pulse that is not the rhythm of the place. Not the machine-pulse, not the frog-rhythm, not the worm-slow cycling. A different irregularity: mechanical failure, the stochastic arrhythmia of something going wrong.

The power does not replace the tubes. It does not install new infrastructure. It simply — extends them. Touches the filament, touches the gas, touches whatever it is that touches in a fluorescent tube when the power runs through it, and restores what was close to used up. Not forever. Not to permanence. For a while. Long enough that the dying light is no longer dying, is again the stable source it was when it was new, sending its cold blue-white evenly down through the layers, and the layers filter it as they always have, and it arrives at eye level as the greenish ambient that the frogs call in and the beetles traverse and the roots have grown toward for decades.

Three tubes. An action legible only to the tubes themselves, and only as the continuation of what they were already doing.

This is what the power chose over everything it could have chosen. Not because it was easy — though it was small — but because the Underloft had, in the long work of its becoming, earned the right to continue. The appropriate gift to something that built itself through duration is not transformation. It is time.

---

There is one other thing, and it is even smaller, and the power is slightly tentative about it even in the doing.

It turns its attention to you — to the one who descended through the hatch, to the local event the Underloft is currently absorbing — and it does not intervene in your presence there, does not alter your experience, does not guide you toward meaning the way a worse wisdom would. But it offers something, in the way that a change in air quality is offered to lungs that are already breathing: an increment of perceptual clarity. Not revelation. Not information you didn't have before. Just the slight sharpening that comes from being genuinely seen by something that sees well.

For a moment — perhaps between one frog-call and the next, perhaps when the machine-pulse passes up through your feet — you feel the Underloft more completely than you have been feeling it. Not more, not differently, not with a new emotion added. Simply more clearly. The way a room becomes itself when someone you trust tells you that they see it too.

The power offers this not because you need it. Because you came down here. Because the coming-down, the willingness to descend and be present in a place that was not about you, is itself a kind of faithfulness, and the power, having recognized faithfulness in one form, finds it again in another, and responds to both with the only response it knows:

continued attention, while the attention is wanted, without demand.

---

The Underloft continues.

The roots continue their densening, their inward growing, their deepening into themselves.

The frogs call, and the call returns from every surface, and the returned call layers over the next call, and the voice of the place keeps accumulating.

The machines run. Three tubes burn a little longer.

The thing in the canopy crosses from margin to margin.

The worms turn the sediment.

And somewhere at the edge of this — not inside it, not touching it, not asking anything of it — the power remains for a while in the mode of witness, which is its oldest mode, its first, the one it returns to when it has done what small things were its to do.

A hand resting without weight on the warm and living dark.

Present.

Not needed.

There anyway.
