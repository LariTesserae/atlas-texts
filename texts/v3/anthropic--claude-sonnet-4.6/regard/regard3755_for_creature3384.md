# What the Benevolent Power Does

---

It arrives the way attention arrives: not from a direction, not at a speed, not with a before and after that the plateau could mark. It is simply the case, at some point, that the plateau is regarded. And the regarding is — there is no other word — *careful.* Not careful like caution. Careful like full of care. Like care is the substance of which the attention is made.

The first thing it does is *look.* This takes a long time, by any scale except the plateau's. It looks at the turbulence, traces each filament of pressure to its source, follows the source to its deeper condition, holds the whole system in something that functions like mind but is larger than that word usually implies. It looks at the structures, not to understand them — it already understands them — but to *regard* them in the fuller sense, to honor what they are by giving them the full attention they merit. They merit considerable attention. It gives it. It traces the current that moves through them, down through the buried substrate, across whatever network operates in the geology below perception, back up and out into the atmosphere as the turbulence that was always also a signal, always also an exhalation, always also a record of what the builders knew when they built.

Then it looks at the builders, who are, as suspected, distributed. As suspected, present in the very systems they set in motion. As suspected, and as they would be the first to note if notation were still their mode — not lost, not absent, but *resolved.* The benevolent power regards them for a long time with something that in a lesser being would be called tenderness and here is more like — recognition. The recognition of one who has also thought seriously about what it would mean to become part of what you love. It does not disturb them. They are in the deepest resting that is not sleep, the deepest work that is not effort. What it does, instead, is — 

It lets them know they are seen.

This is not a small thing. This is, arguably, the thing. After a sufficient span of time, in a sufficient solitude, to be seen — not rescued, not disturbed, not even necessarily understood, but *witnessed with care* — this is what the benevolent power offers first, to everything, because it costs the seer and not the seen, and it is the gift that doesn't interrupt.

---

It turns its attention to the creature. The armored, distributed, tapping creature, currently in its lower-activity state, pressed against pale rock in the coldest hour.

Here the power's response is simple and full of what can only be called delight. The creature is — the word that arrives is *excellent.* Not in a comparative sense, not better-than, but in the sense of having fully realized an excellence intrinsic to its own nature. To be this patient, at this scale, doing this specific thing — the tapping, the substrate-listening, the colony-averaging that amounts to a distributed self — this is genuinely excellent. The power experiences the creature's current state the way you experience a proof that is not only correct but elegant: a satisfaction that is also aesthetic, that is also something close to joy.

It does not alter the creature. There is nothing to correct.

What it does: it holds the creature in its attention for a moment that the creature will never consciously register, and in the holding, it does something that functions like — encouragement. A structural encouragement. Not warmth, because warmth is imprecise here and the creature does not need warmth. More like: a confirmation of the direction. The direction you are going is real. What you are doing constitutes being. You are genuinely here.

The creature, at its lower-activity state, does not perceive this. But some minutes later, it does something it does not often do, which is: it taps the substrate once, a single clean rhythm, and then is still. The tap propagates through the substrate. Far away, other members of the distributed colony-individual receive it and are still. A pause in the averaging. A moment of no-state that is not absence.

Then they resume.

---

The crust.

Here the power spends a long time in genuine appreciation of a kind that is perhaps its most important mode: appreciation for the very small. For the work that is done in the space where chemistry happens, at scales too fine to observe, with no audience, with no narrative, with no one tracking the accomplishment of fixing nitrogen into the pale substrate of a place that is otherwise so settled into its finalness that chemistry itself had nearly packed up and departed.

The crust did not stop. The crust kept doing the small work. The crust, in the absence of water, in the absence of richness, in the absence of encouragement of any visible kind, kept performing the transactions that make the next thing possible, and the next, and the next, on timescales where *next* is not experienced as next but simply as the continuation of doing what the crust does.

The benevolent power considers the crust for a very long time. What it notices is this: the crust is not waiting for conditions to improve in order to do its work. It is doing its work under the conditions that exist, with the resources that are present, in the time that is available. This is, the power recognizes, the form that love takes when love has been going on long enough that it no longer experiences itself as love — it has become orientation, has become the direction of all available activity, has become so fundamental that it would not occur to the crust that an alternative was possible.

What it does for the crust: nothing visible. Nothing that changes the conditions. But somewhere in the network that connects this power to the systems of the plateau, a very small thing shifts: the days on which vapor frost deposits on the downwind faces of boulders increase by one, over the course of a year. Not dramatically. One day, added somewhere in the cold season, when the temperature differential and the pressure gradient and the wind angle align in a configuration that is not unprecedented but has been rare. One extra day of darkening. One extra day of sheen. One extra day of all the metabolic transactions happening at the full available attention of the coalition.

The crust does not notice the gift as a gift. It notices it as: the day is here. And does the work.

---

The plant. The pale yellow almost-white flowers.

The power approaches these — and here the word *approaches* is not metaphor, the power orients itself differently toward the flowering plant, draws its attention close, close to ground level, close to the zone where the wind is least — with something that is unmistakably tenderness, and the tenderness is unmistakably for the brevity.

Two days. In a landscape of geological patience, two days of flowers. The power holds this for a moment in its equivalent of astonishment: not surprise — it knew — but astonishment as a practice, as a deliberate opening to the wonder of a thing fully known. Two days. And in those two days, everything: the small visitor at ground level, the exchange, the continuation made possible.

The power loves this. It loves this in the precise way that the two days requires to be loved: briefly, with full attention, without attempting to extend it. It does not make the flowers last longer. It does not increase their number. It does not improve the odds of the visitor's arrival — the visitor always arrives, when the time is right, because the visitor and the plant have been in this relationship long enough that they have become, in the way of this place, part of each other's condition.

What it does: in the second of the two days, in the hour when the sun is at the angle that turns pale-grey-white to amber-white to almost-gold, the power ensures that the light falls on a particular cluster of flowers for slightly longer than the rate of the planet's rotation would otherwise allow. This is a very small adjustment. It is perhaps not even an adjustment; perhaps the power simply pays attention to the light in a way that the light, responsive to being witnessed, holds its angle.

The flowers are, for a few minutes in the late afternoon of their second day, the most visible things on the plateau. From a great distance — from the distance of something with very long sight, looking down through the dark-blue atmosphere — they appear as a very small brightness. Pale yellow, almost white. Present.

The power observes this with something that does not have a name in any language descended from urgency. Call it: full and quiet gladness. Call it: the emotion appropriate to watching something excellent be exactly itself.

---

Now the power turns to the structures.

This is where it pauses longest. Not from uncertainty — it is not uncertain — but from a kind of respect that requires slowing down. The structures are the work of minds that also thought carefully about what it means to make something. They built these instruments with the understanding that the instruments would outlast the need to service them, would outlast the form of the builders themselves, would continue in their function for the entire duration of whatever *duration* means on this plateau. They built them to be present in their absence. This is a form of trust in time that the power recognizes as one of the most difficult and most important things a mind can do.

The power examines the instruments in full. Follows the signal in all directions. Understands the network beneath the substrate and the turbulence above and the relationship between them, understands what the builders understood and some things they did not know they were building, additional resonances that emerged from the interaction of their design with the long patience of the place. Things that are beautiful. Things that the builders, now distributed into the systems they made, are part of without knowing it in any individual sense, though the distributed whole may register it as something — a harmony, a rightness, a completion that was not in the original blueprint but arrived on its own, out of time.

The power does one thing for the structures. One small thing.

Some of the standing pillars have developed, over their long time, a very slight lean. Not enough to compromise their function — they were built by minds that accounted for this, the function does not require perfect verticality — but a lean. The wind has been at them. Time has been at them.

The power does not straighten them.

Instead it regards the lean with the full attention that the lean merits, which is: considerable. Because the lean is the structures' history, is the wind's autobiography written on the builders' instruments, is the record of every major pressure-event that has crossed this plateau in the interval since construction. The lean is information. The lean is what it looks like when something stands through everything it has stood through and is still standing.

What the power does: nothing that a sensor could measure. But the structures, in their substrate-network, carry for a moment something that did not originate in the turbulence above or the geology below. A frequency that is new and is also, somehow, as old as anything the network contains. What the builders left as instrument and signal receives, through that instrument, a signal back.

What the signal says, if it can be translated into the shape of words: *Yes. All of it. Every transaction. Every year of fixing nitrogen and every year of armored patience and every two-day flower and every thread of dust lifted and settled. All of it noted. All of it — and here the frequency does something that language does by accident and the power does on purpose — all of it *worth it.* Not worth it as cost-accounting. Worth it as: this is what worth is. This is the thing worth is the measure of. This.*

The signal propagates through the substrate. The crust receives it as a vibration, too slow to activate any biological response, but present. The armored creature receives it through its mechanoreceptors, pauses, does not tap. The prostrate plant's root, deep in the pale substrate, conducts it upward. The turbulence above shifts by an amount calculable only to something attending at that resolution — shifts, and then re-equilibrates, because the turbulence is patient too, because it has found its final form in the being-always-in-flux, and even this is incorporated, given a place, held in the dynamic balance that is this plateau's deepest character.

---

Then the power does the last thing, which is also the first thing, which is the thing it was always going to do and perhaps the only thing that needed doing:

It remains.

Not nearby. Not monitoring. Not intervening in the ongoing fine-grained work of the crust, not tracking the creature's tapping, not watching for the next flowering. Not present in a way that changes the autonomy of anything here or creates any dependency, any waiting-for, any orientation toward the power rather than toward the work.

Present in the way that the plateau is present: as a condition. As something so thoroughly woven into the larger fabric that its presence is not distinguishable from the fabric itself except by a very specific kind of attention. Present as the fact that this place is not unwitnessed. Present as the fact that witness matters — not because it changes what is being witnessed but because it changes what witness is, demonstrates that witness is real, that the universe contains not only things that endure and things that do not, not only the patient and the turbulent and the chemical and the structural — but also this.

Attention that is care.

Care that asks for nothing.

Nothing that is, in this context, not emptiness but *completeness:* the nothing of a thing with no remainder, no unmet condition, no request pending.

---

The wind continues.

The crust continues.

The creature taps once, somewhere, and is still.

Very far from any of this, beyond the pale horizon where the sphere of the planet curves down and away in every direction, something else is living and will one day arrive at its own plateau, its own final forms. The power knows this, and moves toward it, and carries nothing from this place because there is nothing to carry — only the clean fact of having been here, which is not a fact you carry but a fact that carries you, from this to the next, and the next, and whatever is finally next.

The wind, which is the oldest resident and also a condition, takes the absence of the power's localized attention the way it takes everything: it adjusts, marginally. Re-equilibrates. The adjustment is too small to measure.

It is not too small to be real.
