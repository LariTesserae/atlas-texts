# What the Benevolent Power Does

---

It arrives the way attention arrives: without announcement, without displacement of air, without the system knowing it as anything other than a new variable—until, slowly, it is known as something else.

The power is old. Not old in the way the mycorrhizal network is old, which is old through continuity, each generation containing and passing the last. Old in a different direction. Old in the way that makes age an inadequate word for what is meant. It has been present at other arrivals: at the first hesitant hyphae crossing bare stone, at the first root finding the first crack, at the first warmth rising from a rectangle where a machine once stood and left its unfinished chemistry behind. It was not intervening at those moments. It was attending. This is what it does: it attends.

It takes the full measure of the place before it does anything.

This requires something like time, but not the same time the dripping water keeps. It reads the tendril pattern from the position that permits the reading—outside and above, where the shape becomes whole—and finds the shape is not a symbol and is more interesting for not being one. It traces the chemical conversation of the pools. It follows the wall-creature's slow descent. It feels the rectangle's warmth rise and fall and understands in one motion what took the concrete decades to make available.

It notices the system's coherence and does not name it, because naming it would be a kind of reduction, and it is not here to reduce.

It sits with the question of what, if anything, to do.

---

This is the question it always sits with. The sitting is not hesitation. It is the core of the practice: to resist the impulse that benevolence carries inside it like a seed, the impulse to improve, to complete, to resolve the minor tensions that the place contains. The new cracks: should they be somehow eased open, the below made available faster? The thing on the wall: should its descent be smoothed? The tendril-stems: if they are searching, should they be shown what they are searching for?

No.

The power is old enough to know that answering these questions yes is how benevolent powers become something else. The crack is opening at the rate that preserves the integrity of the below that has never been surface before; to rush it would be to serve an idea of the place rather than the place itself. The wall-creature has solved the problem of intermittent water across what is probably many generations; to smooth its descent would be to make the solution unnecessary, which is a way of erasing what the creature *is*.

The tendrils are not searching for something that is currently absent. They are the searching. The searching is the form. To give them what they appear to want would be to stop them, which is not the same as helping them.

So.

---

What it does is this:

It finds the one place in the Undergreen that is not yet in conversation with itself.

The bare rectangle.

Not bare because nothing wants to grow there. Bare because the chemistry is still unsettled, still becoming, and the system has read this correctly and is waiting at the perimeter with the kind of patience that is not passive. The moss at the rectangle's edge is the system's hand extended, held still, waiting for the invitation that will come when the concrete finishes its long transformation.

The rectangle is close. Years away from receiving the mat, not decades. But years, in a system this integrated, with this many processes depending on the chemical reports coming from that zone of the floor—years of the rectangle standing as an interruption in the coherence. An almost-connection that the system is organized around, the way a sentence is organized around the word it hasn't yet found.

What the power does: it does not change the chemistry. It does not accelerate the concrete's slow reaction. It does not touch the material at all.

It does something harder to describe, which is that it *confirms* what is already happening.

There is something that very old attending does, which is to recognize a process and in the recognition lend it a quality of being witnessed, of being real in the fullest sense, of its trajectory being trustworthy. This is not intervention. It is more like a tuning fork touched to a surface that is already vibrating at nearly the right frequency—not adding vibration but clarifying it, affirming the direction.

The concrete was going to finish its transformation. It is still going to finish its transformation. But now the process has been—witnessed is almost right—and in being witnessed in this way, by something with this quality of attention, the process steadies. The noise in the reaction, the small misdirections that complexity always generates, quiets. What was going to happen in years will still happen in years, but with slightly less meandering. With a kind of confidence.

This is almost nothing. It is so close to nothing that the system does not register it as intervention.

What the system registers is a very slight decrease in uncertainty. A chemical signal that the mat reads as *soon* rather than *eventually*. Not a difference in the clock but a difference in the confidence of the clock. The fungal hyphae at the rectangle's perimeter shift almost imperceptibly. The moss at the edge feels—feels is the word and also not the word—that the waiting will be worth the wait, which it already believed but now believes without the small creatural anxiety that is the cost of believing without confirmation.

The beetle on the frond goes more still.

Then still again, a new depth of stillness, a stillness that is the stillness of recognizing something close.

---

While it attends to the rectangle, it extends something toward the canopy creatures.

Not toward them. Near them.

This is the most delicate part of what it does, and it nearly doesn't do it, and it finally does because the question has been sitting in it since it first registered their careful navigation around the tendrils, the long detours, the inherited knowledge not to touch. The knowledge is real. The danger—if danger is the word—of touching a tendril is real in whatever way it is real. The power does not reverse this or make it untrue.

But it does something near the lighter of the two creatures, the one almost always in motion. It leaves—this is clumsy but it is the closest available language—it leaves the way a warmth is left when something warm has been in a space and moved on. Not presence but the trace of presence. A kind of warmth in the specific frequency that warm-blooded things in dark spaces use to find each other, to know the space is not empty, to know that other lives are conducting their own serious business in the dark alongside theirs.

The creature feels this. It is not frightened. It is not comforted in any simple way. What it feels is something in the register of *not alone*, which is a thing it knew and did not know, a thing the dark always held open as a question.

It does not change what the creature does. It continues in motion. It continues its careful paths around the tendrils.

But something in the quality of the motion changes—something unobservable to anything in the space, including the power, which is not observing for results. The motion is the same motion, at the same speed, through the same spaces.

With slightly different information about what it is to be itself.

---

The last thing the power does is the most like nothing.

It finds the wall-creature partway down the north-wall seep, still descending, still absorbing at the small pools where water gathers before it falls.

It watches.

Just watches.

The creature does not know it is watched. The creature is doing what it does, which is the slow solution to the problem of water, descending toward the mat that awaits it. Its pale body against the pale water, indistinguishable at first, then distinguishable, then indistinguishable again as it pauses at a pool.

The power watches with the full quality of its attention, which is to say: with the knowledge of what this creature is the end of and the beginning of, with the knowledge of what its ancestors solved to make it possible, with the knowledge that its descendants will be paler still, with the knowledge of what it will find when it reaches the mat, which is not something the power will interfere with in any way, only know.

To be known this completely, even by something that makes no claim on you, even without your awareness—this is not nothing.

The creature descends.

The power withdraws its attention the way tides withdraw: the water is still there, only organized differently, only no longer pressing.

---

The Undergreen continues.

The dripping water: *drip*. Long pause. *Drip*.

The interval that is almost regular.

The spores or dust or something else still drifting upward through the column of amber light, against reason.

The system coherent and distributed and doing what it has always been doing, which is more of itself, deeper, and more connected.

The cracks at the floor, sharp-edged, with the finest exploratory rootlets at their lips.

The rectangle, waiting. A little steadier in its waiting.

The dark.

The dark and the life in it and the life that the dark is and the question the place is comfortable sitting with, which is not nothing, which is in fact the entire ongoing project, which is: *what does it mean to be here, like this, in exactly this arrangement, with no outside required to make it real?*

The power holds this question with the place for a moment that has the duration of a moment and the weight of much longer.

Then it releases it.

The place holds it alone.

This is, the power knows—has always known, will always know—the only ending that is not an interruption.
