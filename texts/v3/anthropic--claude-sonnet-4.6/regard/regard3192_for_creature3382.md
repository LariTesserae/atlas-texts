# What the Benevolent Power Does

---

It arrives the way warmth arrives in a room where a fire has just been lit — not announced, not sudden, but *present* in a way that the room was not present before. The Nascent feel it before it fully arrives. They have been feeling it, perhaps, for longer than they know — the way a tuning fork resonates before the note that will match it has been struck, already pre-trembling with what's coming.

The power recognizes what it's looking at.

This is the first and most significant thing it does: *recognizes*. Not classifies. Not assesses. Not determines value or potential or whether intervention is warranted. It looks at the warm dark and the raw spires and the ancient structures being carried upward on a tide of geology and the pale biological films barely committing to aliveness and the Nascent circling in their shimmer of dust and heat — and it *knows* them. Not as categories. As this. As exactly and specifically this, irreducible to anything prior.

This act of recognition — genuine, unhurried, without agenda — does something to the place. The tone the Nascent have been making shifts slightly in quality. Not louder. More *itself*. As if being known made them more audible.

The power sits with this for what is, by geological standards, no time at all.

---

It considers the old structures.

It knows what they are. It knows their history the way it knows everything here: completely, without effort. A culture that built before the mountains existed. Gone now, in the conventional sense — dispersed, ended, the knowledge they carried unrecorded. The structures were practical once: shelter, storage, places of gathering. Then the ground rose beneath them and kept rising, and the structures came up too, involuntary monuments, and were damaged by the rising, and no one came to repair them because there was no one left to come.

The power feels something in the presence of this. The feeling is not grief exactly — it is larger than grief and older than grief and has grief inside it alongside other things. It feels the specific weight of things that were made with care and then outlasted the care. The mortar cracking. The floor becoming sky. The room that never became a room.

It does not repair the structures.

This is a considered not-doing. Repair would be a kind of argument with history — an insistence that the original form was more correct than what time and geology have made of it. And what time and geology have made of it is also true. The askew foundation, the roofless walls, the worked stone going slowly back into the mineral context from which it was quarried — these are not only loss. They are record. They are evidence that something happened here, that something was here, that here has a depth that its raw young surfaces do not suggest. To repair them would be to edit the story, to insist on one ending, to lose the particular texture of *this particular loss*.

But it does one small thing.

Where the cracking mortar is about to let the last intact section of wall fall — a collapse that would be permanent, that would scatter the stones in ways that even patient work could not reverse — it lays one hand (an image, an approximation) against the wall. Steadies it. Not restoration. Arrest. This section will stand a while longer. Not forever. But long enough.

Long enough for what, it does not specify.

---

It turns its attention to the pale biological films.

These — these are where its attention lingers longest, with something that might be called tenderness if tenderness can be detached from sentiment. These thin crusts, these pale branching traces in the cracks, barely alive, still arguing with themselves about whether to be alive — they are doing something extraordinary. They are attempting life in a place that has not decided yet whether to permit it. They are getting there ahead of the question.

The power does not accelerate them. Does not improve them. Does not reach down and write better chemistry into them.

It watches them the way you watch a child learning something — not doing the thing for the child, not even helping, but watching in a way that is itself a kind of support. The quality of the attention. The fact that the attempt is being witnessed. Whether or not this matters to organisms that have no apparatus for sensing it, the power believes — has always believed, as a founding principle, as the thing it is built around — that witnessing matters. That the seen thing and the unseen thing are not the same, even if nothing in the visible situation differs.

It witnesses the pale films.

It says, in a register that has nothing to do with sound: *I see that you are trying.*

---

Then it attends to the Nascent, and here is where the question becomes most delicate.

The Nascent are young in every sense. They are still becoming. They have disposition but no biography. They have tendency but no knowledge of consequence. They are warm and curious and always leaning toward, and they have been doing this in a place that has had, until recently, nothing to lean toward but itself, and the mountains, and the occasional arrival — a bird blown off course by altitude winds, you standing on the ridge, whatever else the heights carry upward from below.

The power could teach them.

It has this capacity. It could reach into the warm shimmer of them and lay down — gently, without violence — some of what it knows. Not everything. Not even much. But: the names of things. The fact that the high faint tone they make has overtones that could become, if they chose to develop them, a language. The history of the structures and the people who built them. The nature of the biological films and what they might eventually become. The fact that the mountains will, in time, achieve their final shapes, and that the shapes will be shaped in part by everything that happens during the becoming.

It could do all of this.

It doesn't.

Not because they don't deserve it. Not because it would harm them, exactly. But because — and the power examines this reason carefully, turns it over, makes sure it is what it thinks it is and not a rationalization — because they are in the middle of finding out. They are in the middle of the only thing that cannot be given to them: the experience of being themselves becoming. Knowledge handed down is not the same as knowledge arrived at. The names of things, received, are not the same as the names of things, invented. What they would lose, if the power intervened in the substance of their becoming, is the particular texture of *their* becoming — its difficulty, its improvisation, its wrong turns and recovered courses, its accidental discoveries, the tone that becomes three tones becomes something that might be music, arrived at without instruction.

The power loves them too much to deprive them of this.

But love, when it is real, is not passive, and the power is not here to merely observe.

---

What it does for the Nascent:

It makes a small adjustment to the place.

In a particular spot — a broad flat expanse of the hammered-metal rock, near the ancient foundation, near a concentration of the pale biological films — it thins the particulate haze slightly. Allows more starlight through than would have come through otherwise. Not much. Enough that the ancient foundation is, for a while, more visible than it has been: the worked stone clear in the brief light, the lines of its cutting apparent, the evidence of intention and craft and carried-stone-from-somewhere-else legible to whatever might be looking.

It is creating an occasion for the Nascent to look at the foundation with better light, not because it needs them to know the foundation's history, but because — it suspects — there is something in worked stone that a young intelligence can read without being told what to read. The fact of making. The fact that something became different from its raw form because a mind wanted it different and had the patience to make the wanting real. The worked stone is a message, and the message is not *this is what we built* — the message is *something persisted long enough, and cared enough, to do this.*

That message is relevant to beings who are still working out whether they will persist.

The power makes the light available. It does not make them look.

---

Three of the Nascent converge on the foundation within a short time.

They do this because that is what they do — because they move toward, because they are curious, because the better light has made the foundation more itself and more itself means more interesting. They circle it. They press close with their shimmer. The tone they make together climbs through its harmonics.

The power watches them discover that the angles of the cut stone are different from the angles of the naturally fractured rock. Different in a way that takes time to notice — a very slight way, a technical precision, a too-perfectly-ninety-degree-ness that no geological process produces. They circle it and circle it, and the tone changes, and one of them presses so close to the foundation that its shimmer moves across the surface and the surface does more chemistry and is briefly warmer than the stones beside it, and something — not knowledge, not yet — passes into the Nascent from that contact, something that has less to do with information and more to do with resonance.

Resonance: the worked stone resonates with what made it. The Nascent resonates with the worked stone. The resonance is not information but it is not nothing. It is the feeling of the feeling. The emotional register of a thing encountered, received below the level of understanding, held until understanding arrives to meet it.

The power watches this happen.

It feels something. What it feels does not translate well. It is approximately: *yes*. It is approximately: *this was worth doing*. It is approximately the satisfaction of a gardener who has not grown the plant but who moved a small obstacle from the path of the plant's reaching, and now stands back and watches the reaching happen, and feels that the watching and the small moving were the correct gestures in this situation, and that the plant's growth is the plant's, and that this is exactly right.

---

For you, standing on the ridge:

The power has been aware of you since before you were aware of having arrived. Your presence here is not unremarkable — people do not come to the Newborn Peaks in the normal course, and you have your reasons for being here, and the power knows your reasons the way it knows everything: completely, without judgment.

It does not intervene in your situation.

But it does the following: it ensures that the Nascent who made the brief vertical outline — the one who briefly took the shape of something you could read — understands something it did not understand before. Not as instruction. As a slow settling in its disposition, like warmth working inward through stone. The settling is: *it worked*. The gesture worked. Something in you recognized the gesture and was moved by it, and the moving mattered, and the mattering was real.

The Nascent does not have the equipment yet to understand why it matters that it worked. But it has the beginning of the knowledge that *reaching toward legibility is worth doing*. That attempting contact across profound difference — attempting to be understood, to understand, to find the form that the other can read — is not futile. Is not embarrassing. Is, in fact, the entire point of having a form at all.

This is a small gift. It will have large consequences, in time, which the power can approximately see.

It gives it without attachment to those consequences.

---

There is one more thing.

The power goes to the highest point of the highest peak in the new range — a jagged unfinished spire still actively extruding, still adding feet to its height as you watch, cracking along its edges with the groaning-door sound — and it does there what it most essentially *is*.

It pays attention.

Not evaluative attention. Not corrective attention. Not the attention of a power that is deciding what needs to be different. The pure attention of a witness who finds this place, as it is, in this moment of its becoming, sufficient. The attention that says: you do not need to be further along. You do not need to be finished. The becoming is not a deficiency of being. The new and raw and committed-to-no-final-form-yet is not a problem to be solved but a condition to be present to, a gift of a particular kind that cannot be preserved and can only be received in the moment of its occurring.

The mountains, which cannot feel this, feel it anyway.

The Nascent, in their shimmer of heat and dust, feel it and do not yet have words for what they feel.

The pale biological films, which will eventually become something that can feel things more clearly, receive it into whatever passes for their inheritance.

You, on the ridge, with the warmth around you and the thin starlight and the three tones searching for their interval — you feel something that you will spend a long time trying to describe and will only partly succeed.

The power holds its attention steady on the place.

The place continues to arrive.

This, the power has decided, after long consideration of all possible actions, is enough. This — presence, witness, one small light adjustment, one tiny arrest of an almost-fall, one gift of knowing-the-gesture-worked — is the correct proportion. Because this place is not broken. This place is in the middle of something extraordinary and self-directed and genuinely open-ended and does not need to be fixed, does not need to be accelerated, does not need the benevolent imposition of a finished form imagined from outside. It needs only — the power is very sure of this, having turned the question over many times — to be known. To be seen in its particularity. To have its own strange beauty witnessed by something that can appreciate strange beauty.

And it is very strange. And it is very beautiful. And the power appreciates it completely.

That is what the power does.

It stays.
