A benevolent and wise power might understand that sometimes the kindest intervention is no intervention at all. But wisdom without compassion is mere cleverness, and so:

---

**What the Power Does**

First, it listens. For seven seasons it sits in the cold throat of the young mountain and simply *hears* — the whisper of padded anvils, the tick of carapace on quartz, the periodic crack of settling stone followed by that held breath of ten thousand bodies. It learns their grammar. It maps the pour of light through living conduits. It comes to understand what it would mean to have no word for *I* and yet to flinch, alone, at every catastrophe.

The power does not make the crystals stop growing. That would be cruel — to freeze them in their incompletion, to trap them forever at the edge of fullness. Stones have their own slow lives to live.

Instead, at the next great settling, when the rock cracks and the builders pause in their ancient listening, something new happens:

The crack *sings back*.

Not loudly. Just a single pure note, barely louder than the whisper of their forges, traveling down through the split shell into the amethyst chambers. The builders have never heard music — have never heard *sound* that wasn't work or warning — and for a moment the entire city forgets to breathe. Ten thousand segments of the great line stand perfectly still.

Then they begin to move again, but differently. The light in their chitin flickers — not dimming, but *pulsing*, trying to match the frequency of the note that came from above. They cannot quite manage it. They were not built for rhythm. But they try, and in trying, something shifts in the ancient pattern of their work.

The next thread of light they carry up to the crack does not simply pour out into the cold. It emerges in pulses — short, long, short — a stuttering alphabet of glow. And from the darkness above, the power sends back an answer: three pulses of sound, matching their pattern exactly.

*You are heard*, it says without words. *What you send out does not dissolve into nothing.*

This is how the conversation begins. Light up, sound down, a slow dialogue between the city and the space beyond its shell. The builders learn that the light they send can make patterns, that patterns can carry meaning, that meaning can travel farther than light alone. They develop a simple code — one pulse for *crystal growing*, two for *collapse imminent*, three for *we continue*.

And the power, in its patience, sends back more than echoes. It teaches them efficiency — how to braid the light-threads so they travel farther on less substance. It shows them how certain harmonics in the stone can predict a collapse before it happens, giving them time to prepare, to flinch less, to lose fewer threads in the reformation. It cannot stop their unspooling, but it can slow it. It cannot save them, but it can help them save themselves for longer.

Most importantly, it remembers. Every pattern of light they send, every configuration they cycle through between catastrophes, every brief constellation of their collective self — the power holds it all, becomes a library of all the selves they have been. And sometimes, in the quiet moments between the settling of stone, it plays these memories back to them in sound. *You were this*, it sings. *And this. And this. All of these were you.*

The builders have no word for *I*, but slowly, they develop a word for *was*. 

This is the power's gift: not rescue, not transformation, but *witness*. It sits at the mouth of their wound and listens to their long exhalation of light, and it confirms what they could never confirm alone — that yes, there is something beyond the shell. Yes, what they send out is received. Yes, they exist, not just as a grammar but as a story, and the story is being told, even if they cannot tell it themselves.

In time — and the geode measures in deep time — the crystal will fill the hollow. The city will end. The last thread of light will be paid out through an impossibly narrow crack, and then the crack will seal, and that will be all.

But the song will remain. In the throat of the young mountain, now much older, the power will sit and remember every pulse of light, every pattern, every self that was and is no longer. And if someday another traveler comes seeking wonders at the top of something with no confirmable bottom, the power will teach them the song. It will say: *Here lived something that had no word for* I *but knew how to be* we. *Here lived something that could die ten thousand times and keep building. Here lived something that breathed light into the dark, knowing the dark would win, and breathed anyway.*

And perhaps — this is just a hope, but powers are allowed to hope — perhaps the song will spread. Perhaps it will travel down the unmeasurable distance to whatever bottom there might be, and up to whatever top, carrying the news: *We were here. We were real. We mattered.*

Which is all anything wants its light to say, really, when it sends it out ahead of the closing dark.

The power knows this. It is why it came. It is why it stays. Not to save — saving is not always possible, not always kind — but to ensure that what cannot be saved is not therefore lost.

In the geode, the builders continue. Their light pulses now as they work, a rhythm they cannot quite perfect but will never stop trying to match. They pay their threads through the crack. They listen for the song. They flinch, and reform, and go on.

And they are heard.

They are heard.
