The benevolent power has been watching for longer than the world has been arguing with itself. Not from above—that would be too simple, too removed. They watch from *within the watching*, from the space between the Still-Named's mirror-faces and what they reflect.

They know this place. Have known a thousand like it. Worlds caught in the violent trance of self-creation, where consciousness hasn't yet decided whether to stay or dissolve back into the mineral dream. They've seen some succeed—cool into gardens, grow voices that can sing their own names. They've seen others fail—lock into crystalline perfection too early, or burn too long and forget what they were trying to become.

This one, though. This one is special in its indecision.

The power doesn't intervene. Not directly. That would be like shouting advice at a poem while it's being written, or grabbing the potter's hand mid-throw. Instead, they do something far more delicate.

They *remember forward*.

In the spaces between the Still-Named's footsteps, they plant memories of rain that hasn't fallen yet. Not visions or prophecies—nothing so crude. Just the faintest molecular memory of what water might feel like against new stone. They hide it in the lattices, encode it in the sympathetic frequencies that the glass filigrees sing. A rumor of wetness. A mineral nostalgia for something that doesn't exist yet but *could*.

When the Still-Named build their structures—those white towers, those shifting obelisks—the power adds a single note to the vibration. Imperceptible. A ghost-frequency that says: *what if these lasted?* Not forever, but long enough. Long enough to be remembered by the first thing that develops memory. Long enough to become ruins instead of just rubble.

They cannot make the world decide faster. That would break it. But they can make the indecision... richer. More textured. 

Where a Still-Named dissolves into slag, they ensure the dissolved minerals carry a peculiar electrical signature. Nothing that matters now, but in a billion years, when the first lightning tastes that particular combination of metals, it will branch in ways that create more complex compounds. More possibilities.

They whisper to the gravity. Not to make it stronger or weaker, but to help it remember what it's for. *You are not just pull*, they say in languages that predate words. *You are the promise that things can stay together long enough to become themselves.*

Most tenderly, they attend to the Still-Named themselves.

These beings who are the world's half-thoughts made manifest—the power knows their loneliness. To be conscious before consciousness has been invented. To build meanings in a place that doesn't yet have memory to hold them. It's like being the only one awake in a universe of sleepers, but worse—the sleepers haven't even decided whether they want to dream.

So in the frequencies between the Still-Named's communications, the power leaves small gifts. The mathematical possibility of *companionship*. Not forced, not guaranteed, but seeded like spores in the mineral discourse. They plant the idea that two thoughts can hold each other, that consciousness doesn't have to be singular even in this raw place.

One day—not soon, but eventually—two Still-Named will discover they can stand face-to-mirror-face and create a resonance between them that holds. Not fusion, not dissolution, but something new: the first conversation that remembers itself. The first structure built by two minds working in harmony. The first time the world experiences something that could, generously, be called joy.

The power does more. In the deep crust, where the planet's heart is still molten and undecided, they trace patterns. Not symbols—this place isn't ready for symbols. But thermal gradients that will, over geological time, create convection cells that bring different minerals into conversation. They're writing the deep history of the planet's eventual soil. Programming the tectonic arguments to occasionally harmonize.

And for you—the small, brave, temporary thing standing on ground that might not exist tomorrow—they offer the gentlest touch of all.

As the Still-Named approaches with its arm lifted in maybe-greeting, the power ensures you'll understand whatever happens next. Not through words or even the bone-deep frequencies the Still-Named use, but through something more direct: the simple recognition that you and this being and this half-made world are all part of the same vast argument about what deserves to exist.

When the Still-Named reaches you, you'll feel it: the planet's uncertainty includes you now. Your presence here, brief as it is, has been woven into the calculation. Every breath you take leaves traces in the noble gases. Every footprint becomes part of the topology the world is considering. You are, in this moment, both witness and evidence.

The power ensures you'll know this. And in knowing, you become part of what helps the world decide.

Not what to become—that's not for you or even the power to determine. But simply to continue becoming. To keep arguing. To let the argument grow complex enough that one day it might become a song.

The Still-Named stops an arm's length away. Its mirror-face reflects the burning sky behind you, and in that reflection, you see yourself surrounded by a faint aurora of potential futures—all the things this world might become, with you as part of its memory.

The power watches. Waits. Hopes.

And very gently, in frequencies only the deep rock can hear, hums a single note of encouragement to the whole uncertain, unfinished symphony.

*Continue*, the note says. *Whatever you become, it will have been worth the becoming.*
