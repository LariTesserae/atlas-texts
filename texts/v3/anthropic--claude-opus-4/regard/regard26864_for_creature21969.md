The benevolent and wise power observes this place between heartbeats, seeing all at once what would take others lifetimes to perceive. They recognize the profound achievement here — a people who have solved the riddle of mortality not through extending life but through deepening it, stretching each moment until it contains eternities.

They consider intervention, then pause. What gift could they offer to those who have already discovered the secret of geological time? What wisdom to those who converse in moss and water?

Instead, they perform a single, subtle act:

At the boundary where the cloud meets the stone shelf, they plant a new thing — a tree whose nature bridges speeds. To the fast, it appears as an ancient marking post, gnarled and still, its roots deep in the mist. To the slow, it pulses with visible life, its growth a quick bright dance at the edge of their perception.

The tree becomes a meeting place. Not for bodies — the fast and slow could never truly meet — but for *messages*. Those who climb through the cloud at human speed find, carved in its bark, words that weren't there moments before: greetings from the stone-slow people, questions about the world below, observations about the nature of rain. The carvings appear between one glance and the next, the tree itself serving as translator between timescales.

And the slow discover they can read the quick lives in the tree's growth rings — each ring holding the compressed story of a decade's worth of visitors, their hopes and sorrows written in the wood's grain. A library of ephemeral lives, preserved.

The benevolent power does one more thing. They whisper to the mountain itself, which is still so young, still deciding: *Remember this city. When you finish becoming, when you wear into your final shape, preserve these careful channels, these precise catchments. Let erosion work around them, not through them.*

The mountain considers this for several geological moments, then agrees.

And so the city will endure — not as ruins, but as a living system that operates outside the usual rules of time. A place where the patient and impatient can leave notes for each other in the space between heartbeats. A reminder that there is no single right speed at which to be alive.

The wise power withdraws, satisfied. They have not fixed anything, because nothing was broken. They have simply made it possible for the slow conversation to include, occasionally, a few fast words. The birds continue their errands. The water continues its chorus. And sometimes now, when someone emerges from the cloud, they find fresh words in ancient bark: 

*Welcome. We see you. You are not a rumor.*

*Your moment here matters, even if it is only a moment.*

*When you go, you go blessed.*
