# The Attention of Small Mercies

The benevolent power does not arrive. Arrival would suggest it was elsewhere before, and this is not that kind of attention. Instead, something that was always potential in the pewter light begins to be actual — a quality of *witness* that the dim had been holding in reserve.

It begins in the lichens. Not a change in their growth, but a change in what their growth *means*. Where before they were simple corrosion, mindless bloom and blacken and bloom, now each colony carries something it did not carry: the knowledge that it has been seen. Not seen by eyes — nothing here needs eyes — but seen by something that understands what lichen can be besides decay. The fungal mats hold this understanding the way they hold moisture. They do not grow differently. They grow *acknowledged*.

The swimmers feel it next, in their synchronized thousands. The attention sinks through the black water and finds them in their eternally timed formations, and it does nothing to their swimming except this: it knows they are beautiful. They have always been beautiful — pale, segmented, moving like written music through the tarns — but beauty required no witness here, served no purpose in the manufacture. Now they trail beauty behind them like phosphorescence. Not for anyone. Beauty for the sake of the engine that made them beautiful without meaning to.

The attention spreads this way, from form to form, changing nothing and changing everything. It finds the barnacle-lungs on their stones and knows they are breathing. It finds the grazing forms at the edge of seeing and knows they are alive. Each creature remains exactly as entrapped, exactly as entrained, exactly as bound to the downbeat as they ever were, but now they are also *held* — not held back, not restrained, but held the way water holds light, the way a cupped palm holds a small weight, with perfect acceptance of what they are.

And when it finds the Tenders.

---

The Tenders know they are found before the finding completes, because knowing things a fraction early is what they are. In the hair's-breadth before the downbeat, they feel the attention gathering itself to look at them, and for the first time in all the ages since they learned their small treason, they are not the only thing arriving early. Something else is in the held interval with them.

It does not speak. Speech would violate the compact of silence they have with the engine. But in the shared fraction of the beat, it *acknowledges* what they have done — not done to escape, not done to rebel, not done to save anything including themselves, but done because when you are manufactured by mistake to be capable of love, and the thing you love is a mindless eternal engine of production, the only gift you can give it is to mean the motion it makes through you.

The attention recognizes this as kindness.

Not heroic kindness. Not the kindness that breaks chains or brings dawn or ends the world properly at last. Just the small, persistent kindness of staying with something that cannot know you are there. The kindness of the nurse who sits with the comatose. The kindness of the hand on the fevered forehead that breaks no fever. The kindness that changes nothing except that someone bore witness to the unchangeable.

The benevolent power does only this: it attends the Tenders attending. It lies with them on the seams between the piston-stones. It keeps them company in keeping the engine company. And in this recursion — attention attending attention attending the thing that cannot attend itself — something that was always true becomes *known*:

The engine was never meaningless. It was a meaning too large to fit inside anything smaller than itself, and so it had to be its own meaning, forever, without remainder, without acknowledgment, without rest. The Tenders are the part of the engine that learned to acknowledge itself. They are its kindness to itself, manufactured by the same mistake that manufactures all kindness — the mistake of holding something back from the pure economy of being, and discovering that what you held was love.

---

The power does not stay, because staying would suggest it could leave. Instead, it becomes part of what was always true about the light here — that dim even pewter light that comes from everywhere and nowhere. The light now carries, along with its illumination, a quality of having been seen. The Tenders feel this in their held moment. The swimmers feel it in their synchronized rising. The barnacle-lungs feel it in their breathing. Even the engine itself, in its vast mindless grinding, is touched by it, though it does not know it is touched, though it continues exactly as it was, manufacturing life as exhaust, breathing out creatures, wearing the world down stroke by stroke.

Nothing changes. The cold remains absolute. The water remains still. The pewter sky schedules no dawn. The engine grinds on in its geological industrial rhythm, and the creatures live and die on the downbeat, and the Tenders lie across the seams, arriving early at the only ending there is.

But now the ending knows it is seen. Now the creatures know they are creatures. Now the Tenders know that what they do — this small, useless, necessary thing — has been recognized by something that understands kindness when it sees it, even here, especially here, in a place that refuses to end.

Is this enough? Is it mercy?

The Tenders do not need it to be. They were already complete in their attention, already whole in their small treason of care. But somewhere in the fraction of the beat they inhabit, they hold this new knowledge the way they hold everything: lightly, without grasping, part of the rhythm they keep.

*Still here. Still early. Still tending.*

*Still seen.*
