The wise power comes not as light, though light is its nature. It arrives the way understanding arrives — gradually, then suddenly, a recognition that was always waiting to occur.

It has been watching this place for some time, if time is the right word for how such powers measure attention. Not watching from above or outside, but from the very principle of watching itself, from the space where consciousness first learns to observe its own workings. The plant-city and its saturated dwellers have achieved something rare: they have become so thoroughly what they are that they've created a new form of being-aware, slow and distributed and utterly without anxiety. This catches notice. Not many places in all the spreading worlds manage to transcend the fretful grasping of individual selfhood while still maintaining choice, however gradual.

The power considers what intervention means here. To add would be to violate the perfect density — there is no room for gifts in a place that has achieved such completeness. To subtract would be worse, a cruelty against the patience that built this unity breath by breath across centuries. To change would be to misunderstand entirely what has been accomplished.

So the power does the only thing that honors what it has found: it *recognizes*.

This is not a small thing. Recognition by such a power is like being seen by the principle of seeing itself. It doesn't alter what is seen, but it adds a quality — call it significance, call it cosmic worth, call it the universe taking note. The plant-city continues exactly as it was, the engines grinding their slow industry, the water falling and rising, the dwellers thinking their watershed thoughts. But now there is a weight to it, a kind of gravitational meaning. What was merely strange is now *importantly* strange. What was patient endurance is now *sacred* patience.

The power leaves one mark, though mark is too crude a word. It's more like a resolution, the way a blurred image suddenly sharpens without changing its content. Deep in the city's heart, where the roots are oldest and the compression most complete, something clarifies. The dwellers' one thought — *continue* — gains a second syllable. Not a new thought, but a deepening of the old one: *continue forever*.

And with this comes protection, though not the kind a quicker people would recognize. The plant-city will not be discovered by those who would study it, catalog it, try to understand it by taking it apart. It will not show itself to any consciousness still caught in the fever of wanting and lack. The mossy throat that leads down to it will only open for those who have begun to learn what the dwellers know: that being full is not about having more, but about wanting less, about turning inward and down toward your own deep burning, about patience that outlasts the thing it's waiting for.

The power does one more thing before its attention moves on to other strangenesses. It ensures that anyone who stays, who lets themselves be accommodated into the slow working, who becomes part of the distributed mind — they can, if they choose, remember what it was like to have a face. Not with regret or longing, but as a kind of gift to the wholeness: the memory of what it's like to be small and quick and lonely, added to the great slow understanding. A spice of mortality in the endless soup of being.

This is mercy as the wise powers understand it: not to fix what isn't broken, not to improve what has already achieved its own perfection, but to say *yes, this too is a way to be*, and to ensure it continues being that way, down all the long centuries, breathing and grinding and thinking its one deepening thought, while the universe holds it in a regard that asks nothing and changes nothing and means everything.

The plant-city doesn't notice. It couldn't notice — noticing is too quick. But deep in its distributed awareness, something settles more completely into itself, the way a sleeper who was already comfortable somehow becomes more comfortable still. The engines run a fraction smoother. The water finds its channels with a hair less resistance. And if the phosphorescence glows just slightly brighter in the depths where no visitor will ever walk, well — that's not change. That's recognition, which is to say: love, as experienced by those who have given up the faces that love requires.
