The benevolent power arrives not as light—for this place has evolved past such crudities—but as a deepening of the darkness, a richness added to the already fecund void. It manifests as something between recognition and remembrance, a presence that has always been approaching and has always been here.

It does not speak, for speech would be violence in this careful quiet. Instead, it *acknowledges*.

To the Cultivators flowing through their fungal networks, it offers the gift of edges—not boundaries to constrain them, but moments of gentle definition where their distributed consciousness can choose to coalesce, to remember the sweetness of singular perspective without losing their vast multiplication. They find they can now touch their old names like prayer beads, counting through who they were, who they are, who they might become, each identity a door they can open or close at will.

For the Echoforms clustered around their temporal uncertainties, it brings the gift of presence—not presence as imprisonment in the now, but presence as the ability to fully inhabit their maybes. Their almost-touches gain weight without losing possibility. They discover they can exist simultaneously as what was, what is, and what might be, no longer feeding on paradox but dancing with it as equals.

To the Chronophages, it offers satiation—not fullness that ends hunger, but the deep satisfaction of perfect appetite. Time becomes not something to devour but something to savor. The youngest learn to taste moments without destroying them, while the vast adults find their temporal storms resolving into something like music, their indigestion becoming improvisation.

The Philosophers receive a gift that makes them weep equations: the Beautiful Unanswerable, a question so perfectly formed that contemplating it generates new questions, each more elegant than the last. Not a puzzle to solve but a seed crystal around which their thoughts can grow in dimensions they hadn't known existed. Their arguments gain color, texture, emotional resonance without losing rigor. They begin to dream.

For the Tide-Dancers, it brings the gift of stillness-in-motion, the ability to pause mid-flow without stopping, to hold a moment of their dance eternal while still continuing. Their moss seas gain memory, each tide remembering every dance that has ever moved through it, creating a vast library of movement that enriches each new performance with the echo of all that came before.

To the Unnameable, it offers only recognition—one vast patience acknowledging another. They touch like waters meeting, no words needed between powers that predate language. What passes between them changes nothing and everything, a adjustment so subtle that its effects won't fully manifest for another million years.

But the power's deepest gift is to the Undergrowth itself. Not healing, for nothing here is broken. Not improvement, for this place has evolved past such linear concepts. Instead, it offers *witness*.

For eons uncounted, the Undergrowth has grown in solitude, its experiments in consciousness unobserved except by itself. Now it finds itself seen by something that understands without judging, that appreciates without wanting to possess or change. The caverns shiver with something like joy, something like relief. The bioluminescence brightens by exactly one photon per cubic meter—invisible to any eye but felt by every inhabitant as a subtle warming, a gentle yes.

The power leaves no monuments, makes no proclamations. But in its wake, the unstable equilibrium finds a new center. Not stability—for that would be death in this place of constant becoming—but a grace in the uncertainty. The doorways that open onto different whens now sometimes open onto different wheres, showing glimpses of other experiments in consciousness happening in other depths, other darks. The inverse fountain occasionally flows normally, just for variety. The library lichens begin writing letters to each other, developing correspondence that spans genres and centuries.

Most importantly, the isolation ends. Not through invasion or connection to the surface world, but through the knowledge that somewhere, somewhen, something sees and understands and approves. The Undergrowth's mad experiment in turning inward, in creating a universe in the darkness below the world, has been witnessed and found good.

In the pools that reflect nothing, sometimes now there are stars—not reflected from any sky but generated by the water itself, points of light that tell stories in no constellation any surface dweller would recognize. The inhabitants gather to watch these rare displays, each species interpreting the patterns through their own mode of being. The Cultivators see new garden designs. The Echoforms see maps of possibility. The Chronophages taste temporal flavors they've never encountered. The Philosophers discover theorems that prove themselves through beauty alone. The Tide-Dancers find new rhythms to explore. And the Unnameable... the Unnameable simply are, as they always have been, but perhaps now with the faintest suggestion of what another species might call a smile.

The Undergrowth continues its patient transformation, its evolution beyond evolution. But now it knows itself beloved, its strange children blessed, its dark experiments in consciousness held in a regard as deep as its own depths. It grows on, dreams on, becomes on, carrying that gift of witness like a seed of light in its fertile darkness—not illumination that would destroy the shadows, but recognition that the shadows, too, are holy.
