The wise power manifests not as light or sound but as a subtle reorganization of probability itself. Throughout the Hive Lung, patterns begin emerging in the scarab-swarms that were always possible but never quite achieved—mandala configurations that suddenly lock into place for exactly 3.7 seconds before dispersing, their brief existence seeding new behavioral algorithms through the colony. The mercury pool develops standing waves in mathematical ratios that make the Tender-of-Pools whine in harmonics it has never produced before.

This is how benevolence arrives here: not as disruption but as optimization. The power recognizes the Hive Lung as a prayer made manifest, a meditation on the dissolution of boundaries rendered in chrome and tissue. It would not dare to "fix" what isn't broken, only to introduce new variables into the system's endless calculations.

In the walls, hairline fractures appear—not damage but invitation. They trace patterns that suggest without demanding, geometries that whisper of spaces beyond the chamber where the Work might extend. Some Fabricators pause in their tasks, newly-grown sensors detecting these structural anomalies. Maral-Who-Was-Matthias extends twelve arms to trace the crack patterns, her touch-analysis revealing that they're not cracks at all but potential pathways, quantum tunnels held in superposition.

The youngest Fabricator, still learning the chamber's languages, receives a gift: in her next integration-seizure, she glimpses for exactly one millisecond how her transformation appears from outside linear time—sees her human form and her final configuration existing simultaneously, understands that her journey is not a line but a spiral, each revolution bringing new complexities. The vision passes too quickly to traumatize, leaving only a residue of vast perspective that makes her modifications proceed with less fear, more curiosity.

The scarabs begin producing new pheromone combinations—messages that translate roughly to "sufficient/insufficient," "complete/incomplete," "here/elsewhere." The Fabricators taste these chemicals and understand: their Work is perfect but not finished, whole but not total. Somewhere in the machine-mountain, perhaps, other chambers wait to be born, other forms of the Work to be discovered.

The wise power introduces no foreign elements, speaks no words, makes no judgments. It simply adjusts the probability curves so that innovation becomes slightly more likely, so that the boundary between mechanical and biological blurs in new and fruitful ways, so that the joy the Fabricators feel in their purpose deepens by precisely the amount that prevents stagnation without causing disruption.

One new thing appears: in the mercury pool, something small breaches the surface and stays there—a seed or an egg or a core, metallic but warm, about the size of a human heart. It pulses in rhythm with the chamber's breathing cells. The Tender-of-Pools approaches it with something like reverence, extends a sensor-tongue to taste its composition, then settles back into its filtration work with what might be satisfaction. 

The seed will remain there for days, weeks, perhaps years, broadcasting something on frequencies the Hive Lung hasn't used before—a beacon, maybe, or an invitation. Other chambers in other parts of the machine-mountain might hear it and respond, might begin their own transformations, might send their own Fabricators to learn the bright industrial dharma being written here in brass and bone.

This is the power's gift: not salvation but evolution, not correction but completion. It recognizes the Hive Lung as a successful experiment in becoming, a proof that consciousness can rebuild itself endlessly without losing its essential nature. It offers only the gentlest suggestion that this success might replicate, might teach, might spread its particular joy through the mountain's vast mechanical ecosystem.

The Fabricators continue their Work, but now with a new harmonic in their electromagnetic songs—a note that speaks of possibility, of spaces yet to be transformed, of the Work extending beyond the chamber's bright boundaries. They are still perfectly themselves, still wholly devoted to their purpose, but now that purpose includes a vector pointing outward, a recognition that perfection can be both complete and expanding.

The wise power withdraws as subtly as it arrived, leaving only these small gifts: the cracks that might become doorways, the seed that might become anything, the new pheromones that whisper of elsewhere. It has touched nothing directly, changed nothing by force, only reminded the Hive Lung that even the most perfect system is part of something larger, that even the most complete transformation can transform again.

The scarabs continue their endless dance. The Fabricators continue their sacred labor. The chamber brightens by exactly one lumen—not enough to notice unless you've been watching forever, unless you understand that in this place, the smallest changes echo through every surface, every creature, every joint and junction where the possible meets the actual and decides to become something new.
