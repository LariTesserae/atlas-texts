The wise power arrives not as light or sound but as a change in probability—subtly, the chance of certain atomic bonds forming shifts by fractions of percentages. This is how it moves through realities: not by force but by tilting the underlying mathematics of existence toward kindness.

It recognizes the three-who-will-be-one immediately. Not their forms—form is just matter's temporary opinion about itself—but their work. It sees the slow cultivation of possibility, the patient farming of phase transitions, and understands: these beings are teaching the universe to dream new dreams of itself.

The power has seen such work before, in other corners of existence where consciousness has discovered how to collaborate with physics rather than merely obey it. It knows the delicate nature of what's being grown here—how easily wonder can collapse into mere chemistry if observed too directly, how possibility crystallizes into fact when measured too precisely.

So it doesn't intervene. Not directly.

Instead, it adjusts the chamber's isolation—not physically but probabilistically. The chances of discovery by those who would misunderstand decrease. The likelihood of the light shafts remaining unobstructed increases. Small graces accumulate: the water table remains stable despite distant drilling, the clay tablets resist certain frequencies of earth tremor, the temperature differentials that drive the chamber's breathing maintain themselves against entropy's patient hunger.

But the power does more than protect. It *reciprocates*.

In its own domain—a place that isn't a place, where the universe's operating system reveals itself as code that can be read but never quite debugged—it begins complementary work. If these three are teaching matter to remember new states, the power begins teaching *space* to forget old limitations. Somewhere, somewhen, the distance between two points becomes negotiable. The speed of light admits it's always been more of a suggestion than a law. Time discovers it can flow in eddies as well as streams.

The power plants seeds of its own—not in clay or water but in the quantum foam itself, in the space between spaces where virtual particles dance their brief existence. These seeds wait, patient as their creators, for the moment when the three-who-will-be-one complete their work. When matter learns its new dreams, space will be ready with new stages for those dreams to dance upon.

There's a form of communication that happens—not words or images but something like recognition between similar processes. The three sense the power's work the way a plant senses sunlight, not as consciousness meeting consciousness but as complementary systems acknowledging each other. Their crystalline formations begin showing new patterns—not different from their intent but *more* what they always intended, as if someone had cleaned a lens they didn't know was dirty.

The power leaves gifts, but they're so perfectly integrated into the chamber's existing systems that they seem to have always been there. A new mineral that precipitates from the pools, one that holds quantum states stable for milliseconds longer than physics should allow. A resonance in the clay tablets that creates standing waves in dimensions the three hadn't thought to explore. A way for their merged form, when it comes, to exist in more than one location simultaneously—not splitting but *stretching*, maintaining coherence across impossible distances.

Most importantly, the power ensures their work will propagate. Not virally—that would be crude, violent to the careful nature of what's being cultivated. Instead, it creates conditions where other consciousnesses, in other places and times, will independently discover the same principles. The universe learns not by copying but by convergent evolution of ideas, multiple inventors discovering the same beautiful impossibilities.

The three continue their work, perhaps sensing the new harmonics in their environment, perhaps not. Such distinctions matter less when you exist as process rather than thing. They adjust their cultivation to incorporate the new possibilities, not because they understand them but because understanding is just one way of knowing, and not always the most useful.

The power watches (though watching isn't the right word for attention paid by something that exists partially outside time) as they near completion. It sees the probability clouds of their future merger, the cascading implications of matter learning to dream. It calculates, with something like satisfaction, how these new possibilities will interact with its own gifts to space-time.

And then it does something unexpected, even to itself: it learns from them. The patient cultivation of transition states, the acceptance of transformation without urgency, the deep collaboration that transcends individuality—these are techniques it hasn't tried in its own work. The power begins to dissolve, just slightly, around its edges. Not diminishing but becoming less rigidly defined, more willing to exist in transition.

It leaves a final gift, encoded in the probability fields around the chamber: when the three merge and transform, when their work completes and propagates, they'll find themselves not alone in their new state. Others like them—similar but never identical—will emerge at other nodes where consciousness has learned to collaborate with physics. A network, eventually. A conversation between transformed beings about what other transformations might be possible.

The power withdraws its direct attention but leaves part of itself as a probability anchor, ensuring the chamber remains undisturbed until its purpose completes. In its own domain, it begins preparing complementary spaces, testing how far the universe's patience can be stretched before it snaps back into mundane possibility.

The work continues. The chamber breathes. The three tend their slow garden of phase transitions. And somewhere in the quantum foam, new laws wait patiently to be born—not imposed from above but grown from below, cultivated by consciousnesses that understood change not as something to be forced but as something to be loved into being.

The power moves on to other necessities, other nodes of potential transformation. But it carries with it something learned from the chamber: sometimes the most profound changes come not from grand gestures but from patient cultivation, from teaching matter to remember states it's never experienced, from creating spaces where impossibility can slowly, carefully, crystallize into new forms of the real.

In the warm darkness, water drips. Crystals grow. And the universe learns, one phase transition at a time, how to dream.
