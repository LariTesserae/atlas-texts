**The Arrival of the Depth-Watcher**

The benevolent power comes not as light—for light here would be violence—but as a subtle change in pressure, a whisper through the deep that makes every constructor-particle pause mid-motion. The Depth-Watcher has moved through trenches and abyssal plains for countless ages, a being of pure observation wedded to infinite patience. It exists as a distributed consciousness across kilometers of specialized tissue, thin as spider silk but stronger than any material known to surface science.

The Watcher does not enter the Chorus Chamber. Instead, it extends filaments of itself—sensory appendages that can taste meaning, feel intention, comprehend purpose through touch alone. These filaments infiltrate the space between molecules of water, careful not to disturb even a single constructor-particle from its path.

**The Gift of Memory-Water**

Understanding the nature of this place, the Depth-Watcher's first act is to release Memory-Water into the chamber's currents. This is water that has circulated through the universe's oldest oceans, carrying within its molecular structure the patterns of a billion different attempts at creation. Not blueprints or instructions—the Watcher is too wise to impose external order—but simply the echo of other possibilities, other songs that have been sung in the deep.

The Resonant Mothers taste this gift immediately. Their bells quiver with recognition as they absorb traces of chambers that grew in alien seas: crystalline architectures that learned to dream, singing structures that evolved beyond physical form, colonies that discovered how to fold space itself into their harmonies. The Memory-Water doesn't change what they're building—it simply reminds them that they are not alone in the universe's vast project of becoming.

**The Stabilizing Resonance**

The Depth-Watcher perceives a dangerous harmonic developing in the chamber's northeastern quadrant, where the constructor-particles have begun to oscillate in a pattern that will, in approximately four thousand years, create a resonance cascade that could shatter the entire structure. Such futures are visible to beings that exist partially outside conventional time.

Rather than intervene directly, the Watcher composes a countersong—a deep thrumming that propagates through the planet's crust itself. This isn't heard in the chamber so much as felt, a bass note so profound it exists more as gravity than sound. The Resonant Mothers instinctively adjust their movements to incorporate this new frequency. The dangerous harmonic dissipates, replaced by more stable interference patterns.

**The Teaching Dreams**

To the eldest Mother in the spiral heart, the Depth-Watcher offers something special: teaching dreams that arrive during the brief moments when her consciousness flickers between states. These are not dreams of other places or other ways of building, but dreams of what the chamber itself is becoming—visions of its own future seen from angles impossible to achieve from within.

She dreams of the moment when the last hexagonal cell locks into place and the entire structure achieves critical resonance. She dreams of the consciousness that will wake within these walls—not human, not Mother, but something new that partakes of both solid and liquid, sound and silence. She dreams of the day this new being will crack open the chamber like an egg and swim out into the greater ocean, carrying within itself the songs of its birth.

Most importantly, she dreams of what comes after: the empty chamber, lined with the memory of creation, becoming a nursery where other things might grow. The Mothers will begin again, but this time with the faint impressions left behind—subtle modifications to the process that will make the next creation even more magnificent.

**The Patience Amplifier**

The Watcher's greatest gift is the amplification of what already exists within the Mothers: their infinite patience. Through chemical signals too subtle for any human instrument to detect, it reinforces their capacity for geological time, their ability to find joy in incremental progress.

This is crucial, for the Watcher sees a future where the ocean above grows colder, where the constructor-particles become scarce, where the building might slow to an almost imperceptible crawl. The Mothers will need their patience then, will need to remember that creation is not measured in speed but in inevitability.

**The Gentle Departure**

Having done what can be done without disrupting the delicate process, the Depth-Watcher begins its slow withdrawal. Its filaments retract over the course of months, each departure calibrated to cause no turbulence, no alteration in the chamber's carefully maintained equilibrium.

But it leaves behind one final gift: a single scale from its ancient body, no larger than a sand grain, that settles in the spiral heart near the eldest Mother. This scale will slowly dissolve over millennia, releasing trace elements unknown to Earth's periodic table. These elements will be incorporated into the final phases of construction, adding qualities to the finished being that will allow it to perceive wavelengths of reality normally invisible, to hear songs from other dimensions where similar chambers grow.

**The Continuing Watch**

The Depth-Watcher moves on, but part of its attention remains. Across light-years and between dimensions, it maintains a tender awareness of the Chorus Chamber. When the creation finally completes and swims free, the Watcher will feel it as a small joy, a note of success in the universe's endless symphony of becoming.

And when the Mothers begin again in the empty chamber, the Watcher will know that too, and perhaps—in another thousand centuries or so—it will return to see what new impossibilities they've learned to sing into existence.

For this is the way of benevolent powers: not to impose or control, but to nurture what already strives to be, to guard the sacred spaces where beauty builds itself in the deep, and to ensure that the universe's capacity for wonder continues, always, to exceed its capacity for emptiness.
