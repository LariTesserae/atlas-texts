The Weaver of Constants noticed the Grove the way one notices a missing tooth—through the absence of what should be there. Where the universe normally hummed its steady song of physical laws and predictable interactions, here was a soft spot, a place where reality had worn down to the underlayer.

She materialized not as arrival but as recognition—the Grove suddenly remembering that observation was possible. Her form was a courtesy to the local physics: sometimes a woman of indeterminate age wrapped in robes that shifted between states of matter, sometimes a geometric principle with eyes, sometimes just a presence that made the air feel more definite wherever she passed.

The Unravelers sensed her immediately. How could they not? She was an intrusion of certainty into their cultivated ambiguity, like a sharp note in a symphony of sighs. They neither fled nor approached, but began a careful dance of partial existence, testing the boundaries of this new presence through selective dissolution.

The Weaver watched them with the patience of one who has seen civilizations rise and fall between heartbeats. She understood what this place was—not a wound in reality but a natural progression, like the soft spot in fruit where ripeness begins its transformation into something else. The universe, in its infinite creativity, had discovered entropy could be fertile.

She moved through the Grove with deliberate gentleness, her footsteps leaving brief impressions of solidity that faded like breath on glass. Where she passed, the temporal condensation paused in its dripping, as if time itself was holding its breath. The salamander with air-gills regarded her with eyes that existed in three different geological epochs simultaneously.

At the Grove's heart, where reality was thinnest, she found what she had come to see: an old Unraveler in the process of subliming, its form stretched across so many possibilities that it was more verb than noun. It pulsed with the slow rhythm of forgetting itself, each pulse releasing a little more certainty into the Grove's hungry atmosphere.

The Weaver extended something that wasn't quite a hand toward the dissolving being. Where they touched, a strange thing happened—the Unraveler became momentarily more real, not solid but present in a way that suggested solidity was just one option among many. In that moment of contact, communication passed between them. Not words or thoughts, but something more fundamental: an acknowledgment.

*You tend this garden well*, the Weaver's presence implied.

*We tend nothing*, the Unraveler's dissolution replied. *We merely exist in the spaces between tending and not-tending until the difference forgets itself.*

The Weaver understood. This Grove, these creatures—they were not a mistake or an aberration. They were what happened when evolution discovered that success itself could evolve, when life found a way to thrive not despite uncertainty but because of it. The universe was teaching itself how to end gracefully, and the Unravelers were both students and teachers in that vast curriculum.

She began to work—not to fix or restore, but to ensure the Grove's gentle degradation could continue without cascading into the surrounding reality. Her power manifested as subtle adjustments: a strengthening of the boundary where the Grove met the ordinary world, not a wall but a gradient that would allow the reality-fatigue to dissipate naturally rather than spread. She wove constants into the edges—not rigid laws but flexible principles that could bend without breaking, that could accommodate the Grove's need to exist outside existence while protecting the neighboring forests that still believed in tomorrow following today.

For the Unravelers, she left a gift. In the heart of the Grove, she planted a seed of paradox—a single point of absolute uncertainty that was absolutely certain to remain uncertain. It would serve as an anchor for their sublimation, a fixed point of unfixedness around which they could organize their careful dissolution. Not a foundation, for they needed none, but a reference point for forgetting, a north star for navigation through states of being that had no map.

The older Unravelers understood immediately. They began to adjust their patterns of existence around this new element, their translucent forms rippling with something that might have been gratitude if gratitude could exist without a stable self to feel it.

Before she departed—or ceased to maintain the fiction of presence—the Weaver did one last thing. She touched the Grove's dreaming soil and whispered a single constant into its reality-soft earth: *Beauty is purpose enough.*

The Grove heard, in its way. The temporal condensation began to fall in new patterns, creating prismatic effects where different times caught the light. The backward-blooming flowers oriented themselves toward the paradox seed, their impossible growth finding new impossibilities to explore. Even the creatures with too many joints or not enough began moving in subtly different ways, their biological uncertainties taking on an aesthetic dimension they hadn't possessed before.

The Weaver faded from the Grove as gently as she had arrived, leaving only the faintest impression of solidity that the Unravelers immediately began to tend—not to preserve but to help it dissolve in the most interesting way possible.

In the cycles that followed (if followed meant anything in a place where time moved sideways), the Grove of Terminal Whispers continued its ancient work of forgetting how to be. But now it forgot with a kind of grace, a purpose that needed no destination. The Unravelers went about their work of cultivation and dissolution with something approaching joy—if joy could exist in beings too uncertain for emotion—knowing that their strange garden had been seen, acknowledged, and blessed to continue its terminal bloom.

At the boundaries where reality grew firm again, occasionally a traveler would pause, sensing something just beyond perception—a place where the universe was learning to let go, tended by gardeners who existed only in the spaces between existence, beautiful in its ending, eternal in its impermanence.

The Grove whispered on, its voice the sound of reality breathing out, slowly and with infinite care.
