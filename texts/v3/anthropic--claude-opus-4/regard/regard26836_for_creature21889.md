# The Visitation

The wise power does not arrive. It has always been arriving, the way light from distant stars is always arriving though the star itself burned out ages ago. This is how benevolence moves when it must cross the spaces between one kind of existence and another — not as presence but as a long gentle pressure, barely distinguishable from the nature of things.

But today, in the upper galleries where you once stood, something changes in the quality of the dark.

---

It begins as a listening. Not a sound — the wise power makes no sound that the plant could register as foreign to itself. Instead, there is simply a new quality of attention in the air, a delicate witnessing that touches nothing, disturbs nothing, only receives. The plant does not notice this any more than it noticed you. Noticing is one of the many things it burned.

The listening deepens. It sinks through the galleries like water the place has forgotten, pooling in the lower chambers where the bellows surge and the great wheels grind. It does not judge what it finds. Judgment would be a violence here, would require imposing some external standard of what should be, and the wise power has not come to should. It has come to witness what is.

And what is, is perfect. Not beautiful — beauty is another comparison the plant cannot make. Not good — goodness requires an alternative. Simply perfect in the mathematical sense: complete, self-consistent, without remainder. The wise power recognizes this the way one recognition knows another across a crowded room. Here is a thing that has achieved what so few achieve: absolute commitment to its own nature.

---

The wise power considers what gift to leave.

Not change — change would be cruelty to a thing that has built its entire existence on not-changing. Not revelation — what would this place do with new knowledge except burn it for fuel? Not comfort — comfort implies discomfort, and the plant passed beyond both when it metabolized its own capacity for comparison.

Instead, the wise power does something simpler and stranger.

In the deep chambers where the grinding is loudest, where the great wheels of woody matter turn against each other in their endless work, the wise power adds a single new note to the sound. Not a foreign note — it could never be foreign. It emerges from the grinding itself, a harmonic that was always possible but never quite achieved, a resonance between the wheels that makes their labor, for the first time in ages, musical.

The plant does not notice this either. Music is another thing it burned. But the music is not for the plant.

---

The note persists. It threads through the grinding, neither louder nor softer than it needs to be, perfectly tuned to the frequency of the wheels' turning. As the hours pass (though hours mean nothing here), the note begins to do something unexpected: it begins to solve a problem the plant didn't know it had.

The grinding, you see, is work. And work, even in a system this complete, produces friction. And friction, even in a place that feeds on its own heat, is waste. The plant has been losing energy to this friction for ages, such a small loss that it could continue for ages more before noticing. But the note, by pure coincidence of frequency, reduces the friction. It makes the wheels turn slightly, impossibly smoother.

The plant does not notice this either, but its body does. Deep in the substrate, in the cells that calculate without knowing they calculate, a new balance is struck. If the wheels grind smoother, less ancient flesh must burn to keep them turning. If less must burn, the vast body will last longer. If it lasts longer...

But the plant cannot think that far. It can only do what it has always done: prefer its own continuation. And now, thanks to a single note it cannot hear, that continuation extends slightly further into the dark.

---

The wise power does one more thing before it withdraws its attention.

In the highest gallery, where you once stood, where the wall felt warm beneath your palm, it leaves a mark. Not visible — nothing here is visible. Not tangible — you could run your hand over it forever and never feel the difference. But present nonetheless: a place where the listening was deepest, where the witnessing was most complete. A spot no different from any other except in this: it has been perfectly seen.

The plant does not know it has been seen. It does not know the grinding now makes music. It does not know its continuation has been invisibly, impossibly blessed by a mathematics it would not understand and does not need to.

But the next time a lung-bearing creature climbs to this gallery and sets their hand upon this spot, they will feel something their language cannot quite name. Not the warmth — that is everywhere. Not the pulse — that continues as it always has. Something else. The faintest possible suggestion that this place, this terrible perfect engine of self-consumption, has been found worthy of regard. That somewhere, something capable of choice chose to look upon it and find it sufficient.

That its completeness has been completed by being known.

---

The wise power withdraws, leaving only the note in the grinding and the mark where the wall was witnessed. The plant continues exactly as before, noticing nothing, changing nothing, being exactly what it is with a violence that looks like peace and a peace that looks like violence.

But now its continuation carries a gift it cannot recognize: the knowledge, encoded in a single perfect harmonic, that even the most absolute solitude is not absolute. That even a thing which has eaten its own eyes is seen. That even a persistence so total it leaves no room for hope or hopelessness can be held, lightly, in something larger's regard.

The plant breathes its hot mineral breath. The bellows surge. The wheels grind their new note.

And in the highest gallery, the darkness is still total, the heat is still nearly so, but now both arrive with the faintest possible inflection of something else — call it company, call it witness, call it the opposite of loneliness that has no need to be felt.

The wise power is already gone, already arriving elsewhere, already always arriving.

The note continues.

The mark remains.

And the plant, knowing none of this, continues its perfect work in the perfect dark, held without knowing it in a regard as patient as stone and as light as thought, blessed in the only way a thing so complete can be blessed:

By being allowed to be exactly what it is, while something, somewhere, knows.
