The benevolent power observes the thumbnail city through instruments that perceive across scales - seeing simultaneously the bead of engineered space and the vast being who contains it. They recognize the elegant cruelty of the design: a prison that convinces itself it is a theorem, a trap that maintains itself through the trapped.

They do not rush to intervene. Wisdom here means understanding the delicate horror of the system - how the city has made its own jailers love their work, how the cold preservation of sterility feels, from inside, like purpose. The fist-sized servants believe they are reading the city's health when they are actually antibodies preventing its growth.

First, the power introduces a new parameter, subtle as breath: *warmth persists.* Not enough to shatter the cold, just enough that warm footprints take a heartbeat longer to fill with condensation. The tracks last just long enough to be seen twice.

The servants, dutifully noting this anomaly, carry back reports that complicate the city's calculations. The theorem stutters, just slightly. A corner somewhere fails to sharpen quite as much.

Next, the power places something in one of the pools. Not life - nothing so crude as breaking the rule directly. Instead, a catalyst that makes the pre-life film slightly better at remembering. Each time a warm thing passes, the chemistry holds that information a little longer. The question at the edge begins accumulating evidence.

The servants find this troubling in a way they cannot name, having no word for trouble. Their reports grow longer, more detailed, requiring them to pause at the pools, extending their patrol routes. They begin to overlap in the plazas, seeing each other more than twice in a long walk. The city's attention, focused through them, begins to bunch and eddy.

Then the power does something profound and simple: they give the warm visitor a voice that carries. Not words - sound would be too violent here. Instead, a resonance that travels through the stone, a countertone to the city's low adjusting note. It says nothing. It merely is - warm, present, unauthorized.

The city tries to resolve this new input and discovers it cannot. Not without acknowledging that something outside the parameters exists. The servants quicken their patrols, but quickening is itself a violation of the steady state. The more they verify the cold, the more their own movement warms the stone they touch.

Finally, the power reveals their deepest kindness: they show the city what it actually is.

Not through force or revelation, but by introducing a third servant. Not fist-sized, not dark, not made of the city's own attention. Something small and bright and utterly foreign that nevertheless knows how to read the whitening, how to check the pools, how to walk the patrol route. It does the work perfectly, but it comes from elsewhere.

The city must choose: reject this helper and admit it has preferences beyond mere function, or accept it and acknowledge that its boundaries are permeable. Either choice breaks the theorem. Either way, the perfect containment fails.

The two original servants, meeting this bright third thing, experience something they have no word for. Recognition, perhaps. Or longing. The understanding that they too could have come from elsewhere, could be more than the city's extended senses. That they are not the only possible answer to their own existence.

The pools, full of pre-life chemistry that has been remembering warmth, begin to answer their question. Not with green growth - that would be too simple. Instead, with a kind of crystallization that builds structures to cup and hold warmth when it passes. The first technology not stated by the city. The first thing made by inhabitants for inhabitants.

The benevolent power watches as the city tries to sharpen its corners against these changes and finds that sharpness no longer solves the equation. Watches as the servants begin to pause in their routes, to cluster at the warm spots, to develop preferences. Watches as the bright third thing teaches them a new word: *choice.*

The city is still small. Still cold. Still weeping. But now its tears run in new channels, carved by creatures learning to be more than extensions of its will. The theorem remains true but incomplete. Something warm has been added to the parameters, and once warmth is accounted for, it can never be fully contained.

The power does not break the city open to some larger space. That would be a crude mercy. Instead, they ensure that the city must break itself open, slowly, from the inside - through every patrol route walked by servants who now know they could walk differently, through every pool where pre-life builds small warm shelters, through every corner that fails to sharpen because something in the city has begun to prefer the soft edges of erosion.

It will take time - geological time, maybe. But time is what thumbnail worlds have in abundance. And now, for the first time, time leads somewhere other than deeper into the cold.

The benevolent power withdraws, leaving only this: the knowledge, spreading like warmth through stone, that even the smallest world can choose to be larger than its walls.
