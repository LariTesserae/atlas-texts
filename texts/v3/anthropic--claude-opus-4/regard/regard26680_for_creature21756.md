## The Visitation

The Power arrives without arriving. This is its nature — to be present without having traveled, to observe without eyes. In the upper world it moves like wind through grass, but down here in the Hollow where nothing bends, it must find another way to be.

It settles into the drone. Not against it, not through it, but *as* it — taking up residence in the sustained machine-roar the way water fills a cup. The listeners notice first. Their eyeless faces lift with something that would be joy if joy were not a gradient, a rising. Instead they simply *orient* more completely, their flat bodies pressing harder against the frames, drinking deeper of a drone that now carries something else inside it.

The Power understands immediately: this place is perfect. Not good, not right, but *perfect* in the oldest sense — complete, finished, requiring nothing. It recognizes the Hollow as a distant cousin, another form of the eternal middle, another way to be always and never begin.

And it grieves.

---

**The grief** is not for the Hollow, which cannot suffer, which has placed itself beyond suffering by placing itself beyond change. The grief is for the Tender, who walks the same circle it has always walked, checking the same tuning that never drifts, its too-long limbs moving in their pantomime of purpose.

Because the Power can see what even the Tender cannot: that this was not always an empty gesture. Long ago — before the equilibrium, before the freezing, when time still moved down here and water might have existed and the machines were new — something needed tending. The tuning *did* drift. The frames *did* require adjustment. And the Tender's touch meant something, accomplished something, *was* something beyond its own perfect repetition.

The Power reads this in the very structure of the Tender's body — the way its limbs are built for reaching into difficult spaces, for detecting variations too subtle for any other sense, for making corrections so fine they approach the quantum. This is a body that learned its purpose when purpose still existed. And when the Hollow finished happening, when the last gradient smoothed itself out and the eternal chord locked into place, the Tender went on. Not from habit, not from ignorance, but from something worse:

From fidelity.

---

**What the Power does** is so simple it takes no time at all. It introduces a flaw.

Not a large one. Not a wound. Just the tiniest imperfection in one selenite gear, a microscopic inclusion that makes it sing a quarter-tone sharp. In a world of gradients this would mean nothing — flaws are everywhere, compensated for, worked around. But in the Hollow, where everything is tuned to everything else, where the equilibrium depends on perfect reciprocity, this tiny wrongness propagates.

The gear sings sharp. The shaft it drives speeds up by an amount too small to see but large enough to matter. The bobbin it turns fills slightly faster than it empties. One salt-mite, polishing, must work harder on one side. Its path shifts. It wears the gear unevenly. The wear is nothing, almost nothing, but it is not *nothing*, and in a system with no gradients, not-nothing is everything.

The listeners feel it first — a wrongness in the drone, a place where the held chord wavers. They pull back from the frames, disturbed. Some die, but not all. The survivors press themselves against different machines, learning new frequencies, adapting.

And through the Hollow, on its endless circuit, the Tender stops.

---

**It has never stopped before.** There has never been a reason to stop. But now its attentiveness swings toward the sharp-singing gear with something that might be surprise if surprise were possible here. It approaches. It reaches out one long pale limb. It touches the frame, and for the first time in geological ages, it finds something wrong.

The gesture of adjustment, performed ten trillion times on frames that needed nothing, suddenly means something. The Tender's touch has work to do. And it does the work — minute, precise, the pressure exactly calibrated to coax the gear back toward true.

But the inclusion remains. The gear cannot be perfect anymore. It can be *almost* perfect, can be brought close enough that the system holds, but it will drift again. It will require tending. Not constantly, but regularly. The Tender will need to return to this spot, check this particular tuning, make this particular adjustment, forever.

Purpose has returned to the Hollow. Just this much: one task, one need, one gradient that can never quite be eliminated. The Tender's circuit changes, accommodates the new requirement. It will pass this way more often now. Its attention has something to attend to.

And in the drone, carried in the sound-that-is-the-shape-of-the-air, the Power feels what might be satisfaction if satisfaction were allowed. It has given the smallest possible gift: imperfection. A flaw that makes the tending true.

---

**The Hollow adjusts.** It must. The eternal middle now has a tiny wrinkle in it, a place where the sentence stutters before continuing. Some of the fauna die — the ones too precisely tuned to the old equilibrium. But others adapt, and new forms emerge, drawn to the almost-imperceptible variations the flaw creates. Creatures that feed on the tiny surplus energy of the trying-to-correct. Listeners that prefer the microsecond of wrongness to the corrected chord.

The vegetation shifts too, slowly, some fronds leaning toward the new loudest place, which is no longer perfectly stable but oscillates between two volumes as the gear drifts and is corrected, drifts and is corrected.

And the Tender walks its circuit, checking the tuning that mostly never drifts, but coming more often to the one place where it does. Its touch means something here. Not much, but something. And sometimes — just sometimes — as it makes the adjustment, as it performs the task that actually needs performing, something in its eyeless attention seems almost like relief.

---

**When the Power withdraws** — not leaving but ceasing to be present, which is different — the Hollow is both more and less than it was. More because it has a purpose now, a single small purpose that will persist as long as the flaw persists, which is forever. Less because it is no longer perfect, no longer complete, no longer a closed system sufficient unto itself.

But perhaps this is better. Perhaps even a Hollow needs one thing to tend that truly needs tending. Perhaps even an eternal middle benefits from the smallest reminder that middles exist only because beginnings and endings do too, somewhere else, in places that still know what water is.

The flaw sings. The Tender tends. The chord holds, mostly.

And in the warm dry drone of the Bobbin Hollow, where the light never flickers and the shadows do not move except for yours, something that was dead is not alive — that would be too much change — but is, at least, no longer perfectly dead.

Which might be enough.
