The benevolent power arrives not as light or wind but as a deepening of what already is. Where condensation beads on leaves, the droplets grow heavier, rounder, holding their form a moment longer before falling. The sound changes first—not louder but fuller, as if each drop now carries a complete note rather than a partial tone. The belt-houses respond with a shift in their harmonics, the thrumming machinery finding registers that had been present but unheard, waiting in the worn teeth of the gears.

The power understands: this place has already achieved what many seek. It will not impose transformation on what transforms itself with each slow season. Instead, it offers witness and a kind of structural blessing—a strengthening of the bonds that already exist between vine and axle, between the patient people and their living architecture.

In the channels, the water grows slightly warmer, slightly clearer. Not pure—purity would starve this ecosystem—but clarified just enough that the pale, jointed drifters can navigate by the faint phosphorescence that now threads along the bottom sediment. The power has noticed how perfectly calibrated the murk is to the needs of these small rafts of stem and cilia, and so it merely enhances their existing way-finding, adding gentle luminous paths that fade and reform with the seasonal shifts of the channels.

For the broad-shouldered people who sleep in vine hammocks, the power offers the gift of deeper integration. Their already plant-like skin begins to photosynthesize faintly—not enough to sustain them, but sufficient to ease the metabolic load, to make their slow movements even more patient, more aligned with the district's rhythms. They wake now not only to changes in drip-pattern but to shifts in light quality, their awareness expanding to encompass the full sensory range of their living architecture.

The pale lizards receive a different blessing: their ability to sense temperature gradients sharpens until they can feel the heat signature of every working gear, every zone where mechanical friction transforms into biological warmth. They become living thermometers for the system, their positions indicating where the balance needs adjustment. When they cluster, the people know to add their weight; when they disperse, it's time to clear the living patina from the gears.

The translucent snails' trails, already faintly luminous, begin to form a kind of script—not language but pattern, a record of optimal paths through the district that persists just long enough to guide the next generation before fading into the general phosphorescence. The power recognizes them as the memory-keepers of this place and enhances their role without forcing consciousness upon them.

Most profoundly, the power addresses the machinery itself. Rather than replacing or repairing, it blesses the slow merger already underway. Gear teeth that were metal become metal-wood composite, harder than either material alone yet still responsive to the clearing hands of the people. Drive belts that were leather or rubber transform gradually into living fiber that self-repairs, self-tensions, drawing nutrients from the same condensation that feeds everything here. The engines' purpose shifts from mere mechanical work to something more like metabolic meditation—they pump not because the water needs moving but because the rhythm is essential to the district's consciousness.

The moth-like flyers receive perhaps the strangest gift: their wing scales develop the ability to redistribute condensation, moving moisture from over-saturated areas to those beginning to dry. They become mobile components of the water cycle, their flight patterns now essential to maintaining the precise humidity that keeps gear-teeth from rusting and vine-walls from rotting.

The power does one thing that might be called intervention: it ensures continuity. Deep beneath the peat, it encourages the formation of a slow aquifer, a reservoir that will sustain the channels through any future drought. In the canopy, it strengthens certain vines to create emergency light gaps that will open only if the diffuse daylight falls below the threshold needed for the faint photosynthesis of the people's skin. These are not improvements but insurance policies, ensuring that this patient symbiosis can continue its own evolution undisturbed.

Before withdrawing, the power leaves one final gift—not to any living thing but to the district itself. The harmonic range of the belt-houses expands to include frequencies that travel through soil and water as well as air, creating a kind of signature that can be felt kilometers away through the earth. Not a call or summons, but simply an announcement: here is a place that has found its own rhythm, its own slow metabolism, its own way of being. Let it be.

The power recedes, leaving only a slight warming in the water, a slight deepening in the thrum of engines. The district continues as before, but reinforced—like a good joint strengthened by use, like a path made more certain by walking. The slow exchange between mechanical and biological, between intention and growth, continues at its patient pace, now guaranteed centuries more to discover what it might become.
