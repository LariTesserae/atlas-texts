# Preparation Guide for the Crystalline Parasite City

## Critical Environmental Hazards

### Atmospheric Conditions
The perpetual twilight and mineral-heavy atmosphere require serious respiratory protection. Pack:
- **Industrial-grade respirator with specialized filters** for mineral particulates and unknown organic compounds
- **Backup oxygen supply** (minimum 48 hours) - the moisture accumulation in lungs could prove fatal
- **Dehumidifying breathing apparatus** to prevent fluid buildup
- **Pressure equalization vest** - the shifting air pressure from the circling forms above can cause severe barotrauma

### Water Management
This is not a place where humans can survive without aggressive moisture control:
- **Full-body hydrophobic suit with sealed joints** - think hazmat meets diving suit
- **Active moisture-wicking base layers** that can handle continuous saturation
- **Waterproof everything** - assume any exposed equipment will fail within hours
- **Silica gel packets** (hundreds) for any containers
- **Drainage boots** with built-in pumping systems

## Navigation and Movement

### Physical Preparation
- **Climbing gear rated for wet volcanic rock** - the city has no human-scale passages
- **Magnetic/gecko-grip gloves and knee pads** - you'll need to move like the fauna when passages narrow
- **Gyroscopic stabilizer harness** - for when the ground shudders
- **Heat-resistant soles** (volcanic activity means unexpected hot spots)

### Sensory Augmentation
Your human senses are inadequate here:
- **Full-spectrum goggles with compound-eye filter mode** - to better interpret the hexagonal patterns
- **Ultrasonic/infrasonic detectors** - the buildings' communications could contain crucial warnings
- **Vibration dampeners for your skeleton** - prolonged exposure to the frequencies causes micro-fractures
- **Pattern recognition AI assistant** - to decode the algorithmic movements before you're swept into them

## Biological/Technological Precautions

### Protection from Unknown Organisms
- **Electromagnetic field generator** (portable) - may deter the crawling multitudes
- **Frequency scrambler** - in case you're identified as raw material for city growth
- **Biosealant spray** - immediately seal any suit breaches
- **Broad-spectrum antimicrobials** (inject before arrival)
- **Nano-scale filtration for all water consumption**

### Avoiding Integration
The city incorporates raw materials. Don't become one:
- **Periodic movement alarms** (every 10 minutes) - staying still too long may trigger building responses
- **Chemical signature masking spray** - reapply hourly
- **Decoy organic materials** - throw these if structures show interest in you
- **Emergency beacon broadcasting "non-viable material" signatures**

## Practical Considerations

### Shelter and Rest
- **Pressurized tent with rigid frame** - soft structures will be investigated and potentially absorbed
- **White noise generator** - to mask the algorithmic sounds during sleep
- **Vibration-dampening platform** - for any rest periods
- **Quick-setup/breakdown protocols** - practice until you can relocate in under 3 minutes

### Documentation
- **Sealed recording equipment with backup power** - moisture will kill electronics
- **Physical notebooks with waterproof paper and pressurized pens**
- **Sample containers that can withstand building investigation** 
- **Time-lapse capable cameras** to capture the slow building movements

### Food and Water
- **All rations in metal containers** - the fauna show less interest in inorganic packaging
- **Water reclamation system** - do NOT drink local water, even filtered
- **High-calorie paste foods** - you'll burn energy just maintaining body temperature
- **Electrolyte supplements** - to counter the mineral absorption through skin

## Emergency Protocols

### Escape Strategies
- **Memorize vertical escape routes** - the fauna occupy horizontal spaces
- **Explosive bolts** for creating emergency exits through building walls
- **Flare system visible to orbital pickup** 
- **Dead man's switch** broadcasting location if you stop moving

### Medical Supplies
- **Bone growth stimulators** - for frequency-induced micro-fractures
- **Lung drainage kit** - assume you'll need this
- **Broad-spectrum antifungals/antibiotics/antivirals**
- **Emergency cauterization tools** - some organisms may attempt integration through wounds

### Psychological Preparation
- **Meditation training** focusing on maintaining human identity
- **Recorded human voices/music** - to counter the algorithmic sounds
- **Photos of Earth-normal environments** - visual anchoring against alien patterns
- **Strong sense of purpose** - the city's purpose is overwhelming; maintain your own

## Final Warnings

1. **Never sleep without a guard or automated watch system**
2. **Don't trust patterns** - what looks like a safe route may be a feeding/integration pathway
3. **Keep moving** - the city builds around stillness
4. **Monitor your equipment for growth** - clean hexagonal patterns off immediately
5. **Set a hard departure deadline** - the longer you stay, the more the city notices you

Remember: You're visiting a city-organism in active genesis, where the distinction between resident and raw material is merely a matter of time and stillness. Every moment there is a negotiation between remaining human and becoming part of something larger, older, and utterly alien.

Pack accordingly. Move constantly. Leave before you begin to understand the patterns too well.
