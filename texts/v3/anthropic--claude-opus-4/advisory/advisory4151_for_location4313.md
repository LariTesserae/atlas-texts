# Preparation Guide for the Thermal Chamber

## Critical Warning
This is not a casual destination. The thermal chamber operates on biological and physical principles that can be lethal to unprepared visitors. Read this guide in full before attempting entry.

## Essential Equipment

### Environmental Suit
You'll need a specialized bio-compatible suit with the following specifications:
- Temperature regulation capable of maintaining core body temperature in 47.3°C heat
- Moisture-wicking inner layer that prevents fungal/bacterial growth
- Outer membrane that mimics local organism signatures (critical for avoiding defensive responses)
- Integrated breathing apparatus with humidity regulation
- Zero external power sources or electronics (the chamber's organisms react aggressively to electromagnetic fields)

### Sensory Augmentation
Since the chamber exists in total darkness:
- Thermal imaging overlay integrated into suit visor
- Haptic feedback system for navigation
- Ultrasonic mapping device (passive only - active sonar disturbs the fauna)
- Pheromone detector to identify organism concentrations

### Biological Preparations
Two weeks before entry, begin the following regimen:
- Daily sauna sessions, gradually increasing to 45°C for 30 minutes
- Consumption of thermophilic probiotics (available from specialized labs)
- Application of synthetic skin film to prevent moisture absorption
- Neural conditioning exercises for prolonged darkness (see Appendix C)

## Pre-Entry Protocol

### Medical Clearance
Required tests:
- Cardiovascular stress test at elevated temperatures
- Fungal susceptibility screening
- Psychological evaluation for enclosed space tolerance
- Inner ear examination (the pressure changes can cause severe vertigo)

### Biological Decontamination
72 hours before entry:
- Cease use of all scented products
- Begin sterile diet (the chamber's organisms are highly sensitive to foreign bacteria)
- Apply neutralizing gel to skin and hair
- Remove all synthetic materials from body (including dental fillings if reactive)

## Entry Procedures

### Timing
Enter only during the chamber's dormant cycle (every 47 hours). Entry during active periods risks triggering defensive responses from maintenance organisms.

### Physical Approach
1. Approach the sphincter valve slowly - sudden movements trigger premature closure
2. Exhale completely before entry - the valve measures your volume
3. Relax all muscles during passage - tension can cause the valve to reject you
4. Do not touch the valve walls - they contain neural sensors

### Initial Acclimatization
Once inside:
- Remain motionless for 3-5 minutes
- Breathe shallowly to minimize air disturbance
- Allow your suit's bio-signature to integrate with local conditions
- Listen for the "acceptance whistle" - a specific frequency indicating the system has catalogued you as non-threatening

## Navigation Guidelines

### Movement
- Step deliberately, allowing the floor organisms time to adjust
- Never move faster than 0.5 meters per second
- Follow the thermal gradients - cooler paths are typically safer
- Avoid areas where floor organisms cluster deeply (indicates processing zones)

### Interaction Protocols
- If touched by fauna, remain still until contact ends
- Never attempt to capture or sample organisms
- Avoid breathing directly on any surface
- Do not vocalize - sound frequencies can trigger metabolic cascades

### Emergency Procedures
If you experience:
- Sudden temperature spike: Move immediately toward cooler gradients
- Swarming behavior: Curl into fetal position and wait
- Valve closure behind you: Follow emergency pheromone trail (pre-applied to suit)
- Suit breach: Activate emergency cooling and exit immediately

## Duration Limits

Maximum safe exposure: 4 hours
- Hour 1-2: Exploration permitted
- Hour 3: Begin return journey
- Hour 4: Emergency threshold

The chamber's effect on human physiology accelerates after 4 hours:
- Hyperthermia risk increases exponentially
- Psychological effects compound (temporal dissociation, claustrophobia)
- Moisture saturation can cause rapid fungal growth on exposed skin

## Exit Protocol

1. Approach exit valve slowly from the designated angle
2. Emit the departure pheromone sequence
3. Wait for valve dilation cycle (up to 15 minutes)
4. Exit swiftly once opening reaches maximum
5. Do not look back or re-enter - the valve may not recognize you twice

## Post-Visit Requirements

### Immediate (0-1 hour)
- Gradual temperature reduction in decompression chamber
- Full biological scan for hitchhiking organisms
- Psychological debriefing
- Fluid replacement therapy

### Short-term (1-7 days)
- Daily thermal imaging to detect retained organisms
- Antifungal treatment (preventative)
- Sleep cycle regulation therapy
- Monitoring for delayed psychological effects

### Long-term (1-6 months)
- Monthly check-ups for exotic infections
- Thermal tolerance may remain elevated
- Some visitors report persistent "thermal dreams"
- Rare cases develop sensitivity to artificial lighting

## Cultural Considerations

The thermal chamber may be:
- A religious site for certain bio-engineering cults
- A research facility with strict observation protocols
- A living artwork that considers visitors part of its expression
- A prison or containment system for something deeper

Respect its purpose, whatever that may be. The chamber remembers every visitor in the chemical signatures left behind.

## Final Warnings

- Never visit alone
- Never visit during your species' reproductive cycle (pheromones trigger aggressive responses)
- Never bring anything you're not willing to leave behind
- Never assume you understand the chamber's purpose
- Never forget: you are a guest in a living system that tolerates but does not welcome you

The thermal chamber offers no revelations, no treasures, no conventional rewards. It offers only the experience of existing within something utterly alien to human design, a place where life and architecture have merged into something beyond both. Visit only if you're prepared to be changed by the experience in ways you cannot predict.

Remember: The darkness breathes, and it remembers.
