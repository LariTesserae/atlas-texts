**A Multiverse Traveler's Guide to the Weeping Pipe Pocket**

## Critical Pre-Arrival Preparations

### Respiratory Protection
The air in this pocket dimension is a complex cocktail that standard human lungs will struggle with. You'll need:
- A high-quality respirator with both particulate and chemical vapor cartridges
- Backup filters (the moisture will clog them faster than usual)
- Consider bringing supplemental oxygen for extended stays
- Anti-fungal nasal spray - the spores here are aggressive and will attempt colonization

### Protective Clothing
This environment will try to claim you as substrate. Dress accordingly:
- Full-body waterproof coveralls with sealed seams
- Chemical-resistant gloves (nitrile minimum, butyl rubber preferred)
- Steel-toed boots with non-slip soles rated for electrical hazards
- A hood that seals to your respirator - the moss releases microscopic filaments
- Bring multiple changes - the moisture and chemicals will degrade materials quickly

### Timing Your Entry
The pocket's behavior follows patterns:
- Enter during the factory's shift changes when machinery runs at lowest capacity
- The moss dims during these periods, making navigation easier
- Wall-shifts are most common 2-3 hours after peak production
- Monitor local industrial schedules if possible

### Essential Equipment

**Navigation & Safety:**
- Ultrasonic distance measurer (the walls shift - never trust yesterday's measurements)
- Chemical light sticks (electrical devices attract the fauna)
- Rope and carabiners rated for industrial use
- First aid kit with burn treatment and electrical shock supplies
- Geiger counter or EMF detector - some crystals accumulate concerning energies

**Documentation:**
- Waterproof notebook and pencils (ink runs in this humidity)
- Camera in waterproof housing with macro lens
- Sample containers with pre-printed hazmat labels
- pH strips and basic water testing kit

**Survival Gear:**
- At least 2 liters of water in sealed containers
- Energy bars in waterproof wrapping
- Emergency bivvy sack (in case of wall-shift entrapment)
- Whistle that works when wet

## Behavioral Protocols

### Movement
- Walk slowly and deliberately - the grating is slippery and gaps aren't always visible
- Test each section of flooring before putting full weight down
- Never lean against walls - the moss is more aggressive than it appears
- If the walls begin groaning, freeze immediately and wait for the shift to complete

### Interaction with Life Forms
- The copper-winged moths are drawn to movement - remain still if swarmed
- Never touch the translucent salamanders - their bioelectricity can stop hearts
- The mice-things are harmless unless cornered, then they spark violently
- If something drops from above, let it fall - catching things triggers defensive responses

### Crystal Formations
- Never touch growing crystals with bare skin
- The humming intensifies before a growth spurt - step back when pitch rises
- Older formations can be carefully sampled but may be psychoactive
- Some travelers report temporal displacement after extended crystal exposure

### The Tree
- Approach with extreme caution - it's the apex organism here
- Its sap is highly corrosive but has unique preservative properties
- Gear-shaped leaves can be collected but store them separately from other samples
- The roots extend into dimensions you can't see - don't dig near its base

## Psychological Preparation

This space will affect your mind:
- The constant noise may cause auditory hallucinations after 30-40 minutes
- The twilight and close walls trigger claustrophobia in most visitors
- The boundary between mechanical and organic becomes disturbingly fluid
- Many report dreams of becoming part of the moss for weeks afterward

Consider practicing meditation techniques for managing sensory overload. Some travelers find industrial music helps acclimatize their minds to the rhythm.

## Exit Strategy

- Mark your entry point with chemical lights - the space reconfigures
- Never stay longer than 4 hours on first visits
- If you notice moss beginning to grow on your equipment, leave immediately
- The pocket sometimes seals exits - always have cutting tools
- If trapped during a wall-shift, stay calm and wait - forcing movement causes crushing

## Post-Visit Protocol

- Full decontamination shower within one hour
- Dispose of or thoroughly sterilize all equipment
- Monitor yourself for unusual growths, electrical sensitivities, or metal cravings
- Some visitors report their watches run differently afterward - this normalizes
- Dreams of industrial gardens are normal but seek help if they become immersive

## Cultural Considerations

This pocket has regular visitors among certain multiverse traveling communities:
- The "Oxidation Monks" consider it a sacred space - be respectful if you encounter them
- Local reality maintenance crews may be hostile to tourists
- Some entities feed on the leaked chemicals - don't interfere with their feeding
- The space remembers frequent visitors - it may begin adapting to you

## Final Warnings

- Never visit alone - solo travelers sometimes merge with the ecosystem
- Don't attempt to "fix" the weeping pipe - it's the pocket's life source
- If you find passages to other pocket gardens, don't explore without preparation
- The factory's owners don't know this exists - keep it that way
- Trust your instincts - if something feels wrong, it probably is

Remember: This space exists in defiance of industrial logic. Approach it with humility and caution. It's not malevolent, but it's not benevolent either - it simply *is*, and it will incorporate anything that stays too long into its impossible ecosystem.

Safe travels, and may your samples be worth the risk.
