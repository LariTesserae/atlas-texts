## Preparation Guide for the Ossuary Plateau

### Critical Warning
The Ossuary Plateau exists in a state of ontological instability. Your presence there will be subject to laws of reality that differ fundamentally from baseline human experience. Prepare for psychological and potentially existential challenges beyond normal travel hazards.

### Physical Preparations

**Respiratory Equipment**
- Bring multiple redundant breathing systems. The "thin, crystalline" air suggests both altitude and potential particulate hazards
- Pack filters rated for sub-micron particles - the bone dust may infiltrate standard equipment
- Consider closed-circuit rebreathers as backup; local air may have properties that degrade with repeated breathing
- Bring supplemental oxygen, but test all equipment for temporal stability before departure

**Environmental Protection**
- Full body coverage is mandatory - no exposed skin
- Materials should be non-porous and easy to decontaminate
- The "precise coolness where breath might fog but never does" indicates temperature anomalies that may not follow thermodynamic laws
- Layer with both insulation and cooling systems; temperature may be more conceptual than physical
- Seal all seams - the bone dust appears to have invasive properties

**Navigation Tools**
- Standard compasses will likely fail
- GPS may give paradoxical readings
- Bring multiple mechanical chronometers; digital timekeeping may malfunction
- Pack physical measuring tools: ropes, chains, rigid poles for distance verification
- Create your own reference points - do not rely on local landmarks
- Consider breadcrumb-style markers that can be continuously observed

### Psychological Preparations

**Mental Conditioning**
- Practice meditation techniques for maintaining self-identity
- The fauna that "exist as shadows that move without bodies" may trigger dissociative episodes
- Prepare for persistent peripheral movement and partial manifestations
- Build tolerance for ambiguity - you will see things that cannot logically exist
- Practice maintaining focus despite contradictory sensory input

**Perception Management**
- Train yourself to use peripheral vision as little as possible
- The "eye opening in the pale ground" suggests hostile observation
- Develop techniques for selective attention - you cannot safely process all stimuli
- Practice quick sketching or notation to record anomalies without extended observation

### Equipment Considerations

**Documentation**
- Bring only mechanical cameras - digital equipment may malfunction
- Use physical notebooks with waterproof pages
- Avoid electronic recording devices
- Consider wax tablets or other primitive recording methods that match the technological level of the "knapped bone and beaten copper"
- All recording media should be observed constantly or sealed when not in use to prevent duplication/aging anomalies

**Tools and Supplies**
- Pack tools in odd numbers - the duplication effect seems selective
- Mark all equipment with temporal stamps to track aging/reversal
- Bring materials that age predictably as control samples
- Include copper items - they may resonate with local tool artifacts
- Avoid bringing anything you cannot afford to lose or have altered

### Behavioral Protocols

**Movement**
- Never trust distance perception
- The "same cairn can be reached by walking straight for an hour or by standing still" indicates non-Euclidean geometry
- Develop protocols for when movement produces no progress
- Consider that stillness may be a form of movement here
- Always maintain visual contact with at least one companion

**Interaction with Environment**
- Do not disturb the cairns without careful consideration
- The bone-stones may be more than markers - possibly dimensional anchors
- Avoid touching the crystalline vegetation along stress fractures
- These appear to feed on spatial distortion and may react to disturbance
- Never follow the shadow-fauna or attempt direct observation

**Time Management**
- Expect temporal anomalies
- "Each moment identical to the last" suggests time loops or stasis fields
- Set strict limits on exposure duration
- Use multiple independent timing methods
- Plan for the possibility that subjective and objective time may diverge significantly

### Emergency Protocols

**Reality Anchoring**
- Designate one team member to remain at plateau edge where "reality grows more stable"
- Establish regular check-in protocols
- Create predetermined withdrawal signals
- Practice emergency extraction procedures that don't rely on normal spatial movement

**Contamination Prevention**
- The bone dust may have memetic or reality-altering properties
- Establish decontamination procedures before returning to stable reality
- Consider the plateau material as potentially infectious to baseline reality
- Quarantine all samples and exposed equipment

### Special Warnings

1. **The Grinding Process**: The plateau is "slowly completing its eons-long process of becoming perfectly, eternally flat." Your presence may accelerate or be incorporated into this process.

2. **Density Paradox**: "Fragments of a thousand different creatures compressed into the same space" suggests matter existing in superposition. Avoid prolonged examination of any single area.

3. **Adjacent Possibilities**: The "creatures that exist in adjacent possibilities" may represent parallel versions of yourself. Do not acknowledge or interact with apparent duplicates.

4. **Tool Anomalies**: Local tools that "remember shapes they've never held" suggest morphic resonance or temporal bleeding. Handle with extreme caution.

### Final Recommendations

- Travel in groups of 3-5, never alone
- Maintain constant communication with someone outside the plateau
- Limit initial explorations to plateau edges
- Build tolerance through multiple short exposures
- Accept that full understanding may be impossible or dangerous
- Prepare for the possibility that you may return changed in subtle ways

The Ossuary Plateau represents a terminal state of reality - a place ground down to its most essential and paradoxical elements. Approach with the understanding that you are visiting an ending that cannot end, where the normal rules of existence have worn through completely. Your survival depends not on conquering this environment, but on maintaining your own coherence within it.
