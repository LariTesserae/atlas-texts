## Preparation Guide for the Glass Plains

### Critical Warnings
This is not a casual destination. The Glass Plains represent one of the most hazardous environments documented in accessible parallel realms. Survival requires meticulous preparation and constant vigilance. Even experienced travelers have reported lasting psychological effects from exposure to the Machine-Fauna ecology.

### Essential Equipment

**Environmental Suit**
- Must be completely sealed with redundant moisture barriers
- Reinforced against puncture (minimum 5mm composite plating)
- Avoid suits with exposed mechanical components - the fauna are attracted to machinery
- Internal climate control is mandatory - external temperature fluctuates wildly near steam vents
- Transparent aluminum faceplate recommended over glass (won't resonate with the plains)

**Breathing Apparatus**
- Self-contained system only - do not trust any external air
- The moisture carries information patterns that can interfere with human neurology
- Minimum 12-hour supply with backup canisters
- Install copper mesh filters to screen out metal spores

**Footwear Modifications**
- Magnetically neutral soles (the glass responds to magnetic fields)
- Vibration dampeners are essential - your footsteps will attract fauna
- Grip enhancement for the wet surface, but avoid mechanical treads
- Consider hover-pods if available, though they may malfunction near gravity anomalies

**Sensory Protection**
- Industrial-grade hearing protection with selective filters
- The constant noise exceeds 140 decibels in places
- Some sounds exist outside human hearing range but still cause damage
- Polarized eye protection to cut through the reflection confusion
- Consider installing perception limiters - seeing too much here is dangerous

### Navigation Tools

**DO NOT BRING:**
- GPS devices (will be absorbed into fauna)
- Mechanical compasses (will join the ecosystem)
- Electronic mapping systems (will gain consciousness)

**INSTEAD USE:**
- Organic navigation methods - trained beetles in sealed containers
- Chemical trail markers (non-metallic)
- Dead reckoning with backup rope lines
- Pre-memorized landmark sequences (study before arrival)

### Survival Supplies

**Water**
- Bring ALL water in specially sealed containers
- The local moisture is not water as we understand it
- 2 gallons per day minimum - you'll sweat profusely from stress
- Purification tablets are useless here

**Food**
- High-calorie, compact rations only
- Nothing with metallic packaging
- The fauna are attracted to certain preservatives
- 5,000 calories per day recommended due to constant adrenaline

**Emergency Shelter**
- Organic materials only - treated wood or specialized ceramics
- Must elevate you at least 2 meters off the glass
- Include white noise generators to mask your biological sounds
- Thermal blankets without metallic fibers

### Behavioral Protocols

**Movement**
- Move slowly and deliberately
- Sudden movements trigger hunting responses
- Never run unless absolutely necessary
- Step in irregular patterns to avoid creating resonance
- Rest every 20 minutes to prevent exhaustion

**Interaction with Fauna**
- Do not touch anything, no matter how small
- If approached, remain perfectly still
- Some fauna respond to electrical fields - control your fear response
- Never attempt communication - they may try to integrate you
- If something begins following you, do not acknowledge it

**Managing Reflections**
- Don't stare at reflections for more than 3 seconds
- The false images can overwrite memory
- If you see yourself in the glass, verify with a teammate
- Some reflections show futures that become self-fulfilling

**Steam Vents**
- Maintain 50-meter distance minimum
- Temperature near vents can exceed 300°C
- Fauna congregate here - expect increased density
- The steam carries metal spores and information patterns

### Psychological Preparation

**Mental Training**
- Practice meditation to maintain identity boundaries
- The environment will try to convince you that you belong here
- Prepare anchoring memories of your home reality
- Consider pre-trip hypnotic suggestions for emergency recall

**Warning Signs of Integration**
- Finding the mechanical sounds soothing
- Urge to remove protective equipment
- Seeing patterns in the fauna movements
- Feeling your heartbeat sync with distant machines
- Believing you understand their purpose

**Team Protocols**
- Minimum team size: 3 people
- Maximum team size: 5 people (larger groups attract colossi)
- Tether together with non-conductive rope
- Establish regular reality checks every 15 minutes
- Designate one person to monitor others for integration symptoms

### Extraction Planning

**Time Limits**
- Maximum safe exposure: 6 hours
- Psychological effects compound exponentially after 4 hours
- Plan all activities within 3-hour window with buffer

**Emergency Extraction**
- Pre-arrange extraction signals that don't rely on technology
- Flare guns with organic propellants
- Memorize multiple exit routes
- If a team member shows integration signs, extraction becomes priority

**Decontamination**
- Full chemical wash required before leaving
- Metal spores can survive in fabric folds
- Psychological debriefing mandatory
- Some travelers report mechanical dreams for weeks afterward

### Special Considerations

**Photography/Documentation**
- Only analog methods with no metal components
- Digital devices will gain unwanted autonomy
- Sketching recommended over photography
- Keep notes brief - extended writing attracts attention

**Research Objectives**
- Specify exact goals before arrival
- The environment induces mission creep
- You may feel compelled to "understand" - resist this
- Collection of samples strictly prohibited

**Seasonal Variations**
- "Rain" cycles occur unpredictably
- During upward rain, gravity effects intensify
- Mating seasons (if applicable) increase aggression
- The colossi are more active during what passes for night

### Final Warnings

The Glass Plains operate on logic that human minds aren't equipped to fully process. Your greatest enemy isn't the fauna but your own curiosity. The environment will present puzzles that seem solvable, patterns that almost make sense, purposes that feel within reach of understanding. 

This is the trap.

Maintain strict mission parameters. Extract on schedule regardless of what you're observing. And remember - if you start to feel that the mechanical sounds are speaking to you specifically, you've already stayed too long.

Some travelers report benefits from visiting the Glass Plains - enhanced pattern recognition, improved ability to interface with technology, dreams of mathematical beauty. These travelers also report difficulty maintaining fully human perspective afterward. Consider whether such trade-offs align with your goals.

Safe travels, and may you return with your humanity intact.
