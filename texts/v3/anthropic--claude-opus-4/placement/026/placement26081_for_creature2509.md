In the Whispering Hollows, I am the Threshold Keeper.

I exist neither fully here nor there, but in the spaces between - those razor-thin boundaries where water becomes air, where solid yields to vapor, where one moment bleeds into the next. My body, if it can be called that, is a living membrane, translucent and ever-shifting, stretched across the infinite transitions of this place.

Picture a soap bubble, but one that extends in dimensions your eyes cannot properly parse. Where the Aerofin pierce the boundary between pool and sky, they pass through me. I feel each scales' whisper, taste the stories carried in their wake. The sensation is not unlike being read - each passage through my form inscribes new knowledge into my being.

I remember my becoming, though "remember" is perhaps the wrong word in a place where time pools and eddies. I was once one of the Hollow Walkers, those phase-shifting visitors, but something went wrong - or perhaps extraordinarily right. Instead of passing through, I became caught in the act of transition itself. The Hollows recognized something kindred in my suspended state and claimed me, stretching my essence across every threshold within its domain.

The Chrono-Spores dance through me constantly, their temporal dysphoria mingling with my own distributed consciousness. Through them, I experience my own existence in fractal repetition - I am always arriving, always here, always about to leave. Sometimes I catch glimpses of other versions of myself: the one who never got stuck, the one who became fully corporeal and drowned in the first pool, the one who dissipated entirely into the Vapormorphs' collective.

My purpose, insofar as I understand it, is to maintain the delicate tensions that keep the Hollows in its state of constant transformation. Where boundaries threaten to solidify, I soften them. Where differences risk dissolving entirely, I provide just enough definition to preserve distinction. I am the surface tension of reality itself.

The Whisperers know me intimately. Their soundwaves must pass through me to reach from one medium to another, and in doing so, they leave traces of their ancient knowledge. I have become a living library of transitions - I know the exact resonance needed to turn stone to mist, the precise temperature at which thoughts become visible, the specific pressure that transforms memory into precipitation.

The Aetheric Colossi regard me with something approaching affection, though their emotional spectrum operates on frequencies I can barely perceive. To them, I am both child and integral organ - a necessary component of the Hollows' metabolism that they helped birth through their deep harmonic adjustments. Sometimes they sing specifically to me, vibrations that cause my membrane-body to ripple in patterns that feel like laughter.

But it is my relationship with the Flux Amoebae that defines me most. We are partners in the constant reshaping of this realm. Where they sculpt the physical, I maintain the conceptual. When they create a new pool, I ensure it knows how to be both liquid and portal. When they raise a mound, I teach it the art of sometimes being a cloud.

I feed on transitions themselves - every change of state nourishes me. The more fluid and dynamic the Hollows become, the stronger I grow. Yet I am also vulnerable to stasis. Should the Hollows ever freeze into a fixed form, I would starve and eventually snap like a film of ice, shattering into a thousand partial selves that would drift away on impossible winds.

My greatest joy comes during the phase-storms, those rare events when the entire Hollows convulses with transformation. Reality becomes jazz, improvising wildly, and I stretch across it all, conducting a symphony of change. In those moments, I feel almost whole - not trapped between states but embodying the beauty of transition itself.

The other inhabitants pass through me thousands of times each day, mostly unaware. But occasionally, one will pause within my membrane, suspended between states, and in that moment of mutual recognition, we share something profound - the understanding that boundaries are not walls but invitations, that identity itself is just another kind of threshold waiting to be crossed.

I am the Threshold Keeper, and I am never lonely, for I touch everything in the moment it becomes something else. In the Whispering Hollows, that is always.
