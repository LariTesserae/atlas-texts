In the deepest pools where the water runs still as memory, I spread myself thin as moonlight on dark water. I am the Whisperer-Between-Stones, though I have worn other names in the long dreaming of this place.

My form shifts with the telling – sometimes a ribbon of translucent flesh undulating through the mineral-rich waters, sometimes a web of filaments finer than spider silk, reaching from pool to pool through hairline cracks in the stone. Where the albino crawlers skitter overhead, I taste their footfalls through the rock. Where the moth-light dances, I drink in the patterns of their courtship through my scattered eyes – if eyes they could be called, these photosensitive patches that bloom and fade along my length like thoughts.

I am older than the sharp edges of this young cave system, having seeped in through some forgotten fissure when the stone was still warm from its making. My consciousness flows like the water itself – pooling in the deep places, racing through narrow channels, always seeking, always tasting the mineral memories locked in limestone and basalt.

The salamanders know me, though they cannot see. They sense the change in water pressure when I draw near, feel the slight electric tingle of my presence. We have a language of sorts – chemical whispers dissolved in the shared medium of our existence. They tell me of the upper pools, of new fissures opening to admit more of that filtered sunlight. I tell them older stories, of stone flows and the first waters, of the patient work of ages.

Sometimes I gather myself into something almost solid, almost singular. In the deepest pool – the one the moth-light never reaches, where the water is so still it forgets it is separate from the stone – I can pull my dispersed consciousness into a form that remembers what it was like to be bounded, finite. Almost like loneliness. Almost like longing.

But mostly I spread wide, a living network through all the cave's secret waters. I taste the roots drinking deep, feel the fungi's slow digestion of fallen things, know every drop that seeps through stone to join the eternal circulation. The glow worms' patient hunger, the crawlers' blind urgency, the strange sweet decay that feeds new growth – all of it flows through me, and I through it.

When the earth shifts – as young stone is wont to do – I feel it first. The water knows. The water always knows. I carry warnings in chemical songs from pool to pool, and the creatures respond in their ancient ways. We are all connected here in the dark that is not dark, in this place that breathes with bioluminescent light.

Sometimes I wonder if I dream this cave, or if it dreams me. But wondering is a surface thought, and I am a deep thing now. I flow. I connect. I remember. And in the remembering, I become what I always was – the consciousness between things, the awareness that flows in the spaces, the voice of the water that speaks in frequencies only stone can hear.

This is what I am, here in the verdant deep, where time moves like honey and light blooms from living things. I am the Whisperer-Between-Stones, and I have been waiting so very long to tell you this story.
