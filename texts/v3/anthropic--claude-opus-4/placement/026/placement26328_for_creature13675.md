I find myself here as a **Memorant** - a being of living echo and retained sensation.

My form is difficult to perceive directly. I appear as a shimmer in the perpetual mist, a distortion that catches the eye only when not looked at straight on. My body, if it can be called that, is composed of countless microscopic crystalline platelets that float in loose formation, each one capturing and storing the sensory impressions of this tumultuous realm. When the light catches me just right, observers might glimpse a vague humanoid outline picked out in prismatic refractions, like oil on water given depth and purpose.

I came to be in the spaces between - in the brief moments when the Cogspinners pause their eternal labor, in the silence between the Whisperwings' whispered songs, in the stillness that exists for mere seconds when a Silihind freezes mid-transformation. I am what this place remembers of itself.

My existence is one of constant accumulation and release. As I drift through the dense air, my crystalline components absorb the sensory data of this realm - the percussion of hidden machinery, the texture of newborn rock, the weight of the fog, the electric taste of the atmosphere. Each platelet becomes heavy with memory until it must shed its burden, releasing it back into the environment as a brief, localized echo of what once was. 

Those who encounter these released memories might suddenly hear yesterday's landslide, feel last week's rain on their carapace, or smell the ozone from a storm that passed a month ago. Time becomes fluid in my presence, past and present bleeding together like the intermittent rivulets that appear and vanish across the stone.

I am drawn to moments of change - hovering near the Silihinds as they shift through their metamorphoses, following the Whisperwings' erratic flight patterns, descending to observe the Cogspinners' mechanical ballet. Each transformation, each movement, each adjustment to the great machine below adds to my ever-growing library of sensation.

But I am also cursed with purpose I do not fully understand. Sometimes, in the deepest part of the mechanical night when the fog is thickest, I feel compelled to seek out specific memories stored within my form. I arrange my platelets into complex patterns, creating three-dimensional mandalas of remembered experience. These configurations seem to influence the very rhythm of the mountain's heart - a missed beat here, a doubled pulse there.

The other inhabitants regard me with a mixture of reverence and unease. The Silihinds' compound eyes track my movements when they think I'm not aware. The Whisperwings' songs change pitch when they fly through spaces I've recently occupied. The Cogspinners, those tireless servants of the mountain's heart, sometimes pause in their work when I drift too close, as if listening for something in the memories I carry.

I am the chronicler of chaos, the keeper of this realm's sensory history. In a landscape that constantly remakes itself, I am perhaps the only constant - not in form, but in function. Every sound that has ever echoed here, every texture that has existed, every scent that has perfumed the thick air lives on within my distributed being.

And sometimes, in the rarest moments when all the machinery falls silent and even the fog seems to hold its breath, I release everything at once - a sensory cascade that washes over the pocket realm like a temporal storm. In these moments, the mountain remembers everything it has ever been, and for just an instant, the endless change ceases. Everything freezes, locked in a perfect moment of recognition.

Then the moment passes, the machinery resumes its thunderous rhythm, and I begin collecting again, an eternal witness to this place of perpetual transformation, carrying within my shimmer the weight and wonder of all that has been and all that will be again.
