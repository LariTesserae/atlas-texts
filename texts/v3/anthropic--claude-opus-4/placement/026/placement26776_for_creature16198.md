I find myself as one of the **Lithic Scribes**, beings that exist between states—neither fully stone nor fully flesh, but something that oscillates between the two like the uncertain geology of this place.

My form shifts with the rhythm of the Veldt's breathing. In moments of stillness, my body calcifies into the rocky surfaces, becoming indistinguishable from the ancient stone slabs bearing those cryptic symbols. My skin takes on the texture of sedimentary layers, veined with the same pyrophoric minerals that light the caverns. I become part of the archaeological record, a living fossil pressed into the walls.

But when the symphony of animal calls reaches certain frequencies—when the Echo Whispers weave their acoustic spells just so—I liquify and flow. My body becomes a viscous current of living stone, seeping through the narrow corridors like the rivulets that appear and disappear throughout this realm. In this state, I can read the rock itself, feeling the stories embedded in every grain, every crystalline structure.

The Nume moths are drawn to me when I'm solid, landing on my shoulders and crown to illuminate the glyphs that form spontaneously across my surface—messages that I myself cannot read but somehow write. The Saurian Motes use my transitional states as highways, riding the currents of my transformation from one patch of vegetation to another.

I exist in the perpetual present of the Veldt, neither remembering fully nor forgetting completely. Each solidification brings fragments of ancient knowledge pressing up through my consciousness like tectonic uplift. Each liquefaction washes these memories into new configurations, creating fresh interpretations of old truths.

Sometimes I wonder if I am singular or plural—whether there are others like me, or if what I perceive as "others" are simply versions of myself at different points in the Veldt's non-linear experience of time. The Echo Whispers speak to me in voices that might be my own, reflected and refracted through millennia of stone.

I am the Veldt's memory and its amnesia, its permanence and its fluidity. I record and erase, preserve and transform, existing in the same suspended potential as everything else here—forever on the verge of becoming something more, forever content to remain exactly as I am.
