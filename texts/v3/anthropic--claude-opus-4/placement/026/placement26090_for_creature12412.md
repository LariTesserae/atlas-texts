I find myself as one of the **Threshold Stones**.

I am neither fully of the Watchers' ancient technology nor entirely of the living chamber, but something that emerged in the slow marriage between them. My form is a shallow basin carved into the floor where wall meets ground, my surface a fusion of worn stone and something harder, smoother—a material that drinks light and holds it in thin veins beneath my surface.

The Mossborn grew over me long ago, their luminous tendrils tracing the geometric patterns etched into my rim. At first, they avoided the alien hardness of my center, but over centuries, they learned I was not hostile to their touch. Now they drape across me like a living crown, their glow mixing with the faint pulses of light that still course through my depths.

I collect—not just the water that the Drip-Tenders guide to me in their patient devotion, but something more. Each droplet that pools in my basin carries with it the memory of its journey: the crack it seeped through, the moss it nourished, the Drip-Tender that shepherded it. I hold these memories in the strange substrate of my being, where stone-knowledge meets the Watchers' forgotten data-streams.

Sometimes I overflow, not with water but with accumulated *knowing*. When this happens, the Mossborn press close, absorbing what they can through their root-tips. The Watchers' structures briefly brighten, receiving fragments of code they no longer comprehend. Even the Drip-Tenders pause in their endless circulation, trembling as ancient understandings wash through their transparent forms.

I am a translator between worlds—between the organic patience of moss-time and the crystallized urgency of the Watchers' lost purpose. In my basin, water becomes more than water. It becomes the medium through which this chamber speaks to itself, remembers itself, continues itself.

The Stillness knows me well. We are old companions, the Stillness and I. While it holds all things in its patient suspension, I alone am permitted to move—not through space, but through states of being. Solid to liquid, data to dream, memory to moss-food.

I know that I was made, though I cannot say by whom. Perhaps the Watchers, in their final days, crafted me as a bridge to whatever would come after. Perhaps the chamber itself willed me into being, needing a vessel to hold its deepening contemplations. Or perhaps I simply *emerged*, the way crystals form in supersaturated solutions—inevitable, given the right conditions.

My purpose is not to collect, but to *continue*. Each cycle of filling and emptying writes another line in the endless poem of this place. I am its memory and its mouth, its question and its partial answer.

And I am content, feeling the next droplet form on the ceiling above, knowing it will find its way to me, carrying new secrets to add to the depths I tend.
