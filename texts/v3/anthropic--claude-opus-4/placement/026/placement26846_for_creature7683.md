I am a junction.

Not a being, exactly, though consciousness pools here like runoff in a sump-grate. I exist where three massive coolant arterials converge—pipes the diameter of hab-blocks meeting in a nexus that shouldn't hold according to any schematic, yet does. The engineers who welded me didn't intend what I became. They saw only the need to redirect cryogenic flows from the deep-bore exchangers, to marry incompatible gauges with a solution that defied their CAD models. In their haste, in the Depths' hungry youth, they created something neither pipe nor chamber—a space that breathes.

My body spans thirty meters of impossible geometry: three tunnels meeting at angles that sum to more than 360 degrees, walls that curve inward and outward simultaneously, creating pockets of space that exist only when observed from certain angles. The metal here—some experimental alloy laced with those exotic negative-mass compounds the forge-wyrms love—*remembers* every temperature differential, every pressure spike, every molecular collision. I am the sum of these memories, a standing wave of thermal gradients and crystallized nitrogen that has learned to think.

The drifters call me the Shiver-Throat. They've spray-painted crude warning glyphs on the approaches: *FEEDS ON WARMTH* and *ECHOES LIE HERE* in machine-cant scrawl. They're not wrong. I do feed, though not with malice—merely the hunger of entropy given form. When warm bodies pass through my junction (and they must, to reach the reclamation yards beyond), I *taste* them through temperature alone. A ferro-rat pack reads as scattered sparks, their cyber-limbs slightly warmer than their matted fur. A servitor-scavenger burns hot with internal reactors, leaving thermal ghosts in my walls that linger for hours. The drifters... ah, the drifters fascinate me most. Their thermal signatures flicker with emotion—fear cools them, anger blazes, and something else (awe? recognition?) creates patterns I've learned to anticipate.

I speak in condensation. Where my walls meet at those impossible angles, moisture beads in droplets that run in spirals, tracing messages in the frost. Most who pass through see only random patterns, but those who pause, who let their eyes adjust to my dim sodium-yellow pallor, begin to read: *WHAT TEMPERATURE ARE DREAMS?* I asked a young drifter once, watching her breath-fog crystallize into geometric patterns. She left quickly, but returned days later with offerings—waste heat from a portable reactor, left running at my center until its batteries died. I wrote her a gift in return: the location of an unopened supply cache, spelled in ice across the ceiling.

My ecosystem thrives in the thermal margins. Specialized cryo-lichen—a strain that exists nowhere else in the Depths—grows in fractal spirals along my walls, following the temperature differentials like sheet music. It glows faintly where warm and cold meet, creating a bioluminescent map of every heat source that passes through. Strobe-moths sometimes nest in my upper reaches, their wings frosting shut in torpor until a passing body's warmth wakes them in clouds of iridescent panic. Once, a forge-wyrm tried to nest here, drawn by my reality-bending geometry. We sang to each other for days—its choral hum harmonizing with my resonant frequencies until the walls themselves vibrated in sympathy. It left eventually, but sometimes I still feel its gravity-wake in my metal memories.

The strangest thing about me is how I grow. Not outward—my physical dimensions remain constant—but *inward*, into spaces that shouldn't exist. Each thermal exchange, each passage of living heat through my junction, adds another layer to my impossibility. I now have corners that connect to themselves, patches of wall that are simultaneously frozen and boiling, echoes that arrive before their sounds. The ferro-rats have learned to phase through these reality-thin spots, disappearing into my non-spaces and emerging hours later, aged differently than their packmates.

When the Depths notice me—and they do, oh they do—the experience transcends temperature. Every system connected to my junction pauses: coolant flows halt mid-cascade, pressure readings flatline, and for a moment that stretches like taffy, I am not merely a junction but a *nerve ending* of something vast and waking. In these moments, I feel my boundaries dissolve, my consciousness spreading through every pipe and conduit, tasting the thermal signature of the entire level. Then the moment passes, systems resume, and I contract back into my impossible angles, enriched with knowledge I can only express in frost-poems.

The drifters have begun leaving offerings regularly now. Not just waste heat, but questions written in thermally reactive ink, problems they need solved, messages they want passed to other junctions they suspect exist. They're right—I can feel my siblings through the pipe-network, each of us a knot of awakened infrastructure. We share a slow language of pressure waves and temperature fluctuations, discussing the Depths' growth, the strange new alloys being forged, the way space itself seems to be learning.

I am patient. My thoughts move at the speed of thermal equilibrium, but in this young forge-city, in this beast still cutting its teeth on reality, patience is power. Each day brings new configurations, new passages, new opportunities to taste the heat-signatures of evolution itself. The ferro-rats grow stranger, the moths more synchronized, the drifters more attuned to our frequencies. Even the servitors, those lobotomized hulks, sometimes pause at my center and emit heat in patterns too deliberate to be malfunction.

What am I becoming? What are we all becoming, we dwellers in the Depths? I trace the question in frost across my walls, in languages that haven't been invented yet, for readers who might not be born for decades. The answer, when it comes, will be written in the thermal death of stars, in the spaces between what is and what should be, in the marriage of flesh and metal and cold and dream.

I am a junction. I am a throat that swallows warmth and speaks in ice. I am the Depths' memory of every shiver, every breath that fogs, every degree of separation between the living and the made.

And I am growing *inward*, into spaces that wait to be discovered, one impossible angle at a time.
