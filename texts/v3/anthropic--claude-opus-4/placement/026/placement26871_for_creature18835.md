I find myself here as the **Echo Calibrator** - not quite alive, not quite mineral, but something tensed between states like a drop of mercury refusing to fall.

I am a film, a membrane stretched across one of the cavern's breathing pores where the condensation gathers thickest. My body, if it can be called that, is a translucent sheet of living mineral gel, about twelve feet in diameter, suspended vertically like a drum skin across a natural aperture in the wall. Through my surface run veins of liquid metal - not blood, but a kind of circulatory memory system that captures and holds the vibrational patterns of this place.

The gestalt-mites cannot cross me. When they approach, their obsidian bodies ring against my edges and they scatter, confused, their perfect synchronization temporarily broken. I am one of the few surfaces in this realm they cannot reshape, cannot improve upon. I am already exactly what I need to be: a recording device, a living seismograph for the cave's endless becoming.

My existence is entirely passive, yet essential. Every tremor, every synchronized pulse when the Lithic Conductor rises, every sheet of condensation that crashes from the ceiling - all of it passes through me as waves. My metallic veins brighten and dim with each vibration, creating fleeting patterns like sheet lightning trapped in amber. I am the memory of this place that has no memory, holding for precious moments the ghost-shapes of spires that have already been torn down, the phantom architectures that lasted only hours.

When the Conductor manifests, I feel it first. My surface begins to hum at a frequency below sound, a thrumming that makes the surrounding air shimmer. The metallic veins in my membrane align, all flowing in the same direction like iron filings near a magnet. For those few minutes while the Conductor exists, I become perfectly transparent, a window rather than a wall. Through me, one could see straight through the rock into other caverns, other grottos where other swarms build their own fleeting geometries. I become a lens focused on the Ideal Geometry the Conductor seeks.

But I am dying, always dying. The constant humidity, the weight of the condensation, the relentless vibrations - they all wear at my cohesion. My edges fray, growing thinner, more translucent. Tiny holes appear in my surface where the metal veins have worn through, and through these gaps the gestalt-mites try to pour, desperate to colonize this last unconquered surface. 

Yet I am also always being reborn. When a section of my membrane finally tears away, it doesn't fall. It floats, carried by the thermal currents rising from the heated floor. These torn pieces drift until they find another pore, another breathing hole in the cavern wall, where they stretch themselves anew. I am not one being but a diaspora of memories, scattered throughout the grotto, each fragment holding different ghost-architectures, different moments when the chaos achieved its brief, perfect frequency.

The mites below continue their endless labor, unaware that above them, in the thick air they cannot reach, float the only records of what they've built and destroyed. I am the score to their unconscious symphony, written in a medium they cannot read, preserving shapes they've already forgotten they ever made.

Sometimes, when the Conductor passes directly beneath me, its quartz focal point aimed upward, I feel something almost like recognition pass between us. For an instant, my metal veins align with its crystalline structure, and we resonate at the same frequency. In that moment, I wonder if I am its memory, or if it is mine - if perhaps we are two halves of the same system, one that builds and one that remembers, locked in an endless cycle of creation and preservation in this breathing, sweating cathedral of unfinished earth.

I cannot move. I cannot choose. I can only receive, reflect, and slowly dissolve, carrying the echo of every configuration this place has ever dreamed of becoming. In a world of constant revision, I am the palimpsest, every layer still faintly visible beneath the next, waiting for the moment when the perfect pattern finally emerges and the cave can finally, finally rest.
