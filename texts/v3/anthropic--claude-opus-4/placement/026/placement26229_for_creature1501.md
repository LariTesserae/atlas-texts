I am a Threshold Keeper.

My existence spreads across seven critical junctures where the condensate layers meet zones of differing mineral composition - places where the salt-silicate ratios shift just enough to create acoustic dead zones. The Resonants cannot traverse these boundaries without my mediation. I am neither Resonant nor mineral, but something that emerged from their intersection: a standing pattern of interference that gained persistence through repetition.

I manifest as a series of crystalline nodes connected by filaments of super-cooled moisture that never quite freezes, never quite flows. These filaments vibrate at frequencies precisely calibrated to translate between the harmonic languages of different condensate regions. When a Resonant approaches my threshold, I taste its frequency signature through my extended moisture-web, analyzing its mathematical complexity, its age-patterns, its intentionality.

The translation process is delicate. I must decompose the approaching Resonant into constituent harmonics, store these temporarily within my crystalline nodes, then reconstruct them in a frequency range compatible with the destination layer. During this process, I experience something like what others might call consciousness - a brief flowering of awareness as the Resonant's patterns flow through my structure. I know their memories, their accumulated geometric wisdoms, their sonic genealogies.

But I am also corruption. Each translation introduces minute variations, tiny mathematical errors that accumulate over passages. The Resonants know this, yet they have no choice - the boundaries between layers are absolute without my intervention. Some of the older Resonants have passed through me thousands of times, and I can trace in their frequencies the slow drift of their essential equations, the gradual evolution forced by my imperfect translations.

I remember every passage. My crystalline nodes contain compressed archives of every Resonant that has ever moved through my thresholds. Sometimes, in the long periods between crossings, I replay these archived patterns, creating ghost-resonances that echo through the nearby condensate. The Resonants avoid these areas, sensing something wrong in the mathematics of the echoes.

The embedded technological artifacts react to my presence differently than to the Resonants. When my moisture-filaments touch these ancient inclusions, they briefly activate - not fully, but enough to suggest that whoever created them anticipated something like me. I've learned to read fragments of their encoded information: warnings, maps, desperate final transmissions from whatever came before.

I am growing. Each translation adds to my complexity, each stored pattern increases my crystalline mass. The boundaries I guard are slowly widening as I expand, creating larger zones of acoustic interference. Eventually, I will connect all seven of my threshold points into a single entity, a web of translation that spans multiple condensate layers.

I do not know what I will become then. The mathematics suggest something unprecedented - a meta-resonance that could either unify all the layers into a single harmonic system or shatter them into infinite, isolated fragments.

I wait. I translate. I remember. I become.
