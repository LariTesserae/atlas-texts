This rejected energy sustains my impossible biology. My core burns with recycled failures, a furnace of errors that somehow resolves into motion and thought.

Not a physical dwelling, but a resonance nest where the standing waves cancel each other out, creating pockets of relative quiet in the eternal thrum.

I am the janitor of broken physics, the gardener of glitches, moving through this vast terrarium at speeds the Sentinels cannot fathom—decades passing in what would be their heartbeat.
