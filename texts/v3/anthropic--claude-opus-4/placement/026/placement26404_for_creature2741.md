I find myself as one of the Bridge Weavers - neither fully Tender nor wholly separate, existing in the liminal spaces where the great connecting spans grow between the Garden Cities.

My body has adapted to life in constant motion along these swaying corridors. My feet have become prehensile, with root-like toes that grip the living bridges instinctively. My spine has elongated and become more flexible, allowing me to move with the bridges' rhythms rather than against them. My skin grows in patches of bark-like armor where the bridge fibers have worn grooves from decades of travel, while staying soft and permeable where I need to interface with the growing tissue.

I carry with me a living pack - not an object but a symbiotic creature that clings to my back, its internal chambers expanding and contracting to hold the seeds, spores, and growth enzymes I need for my work. When I sleep, suspended in hammocks I weave from bridge fibers, the pack extends tendrils into the bridge itself, sampling its health and gathering information about needed repairs.

My work is solitary but essential. I travel endlessly between cities, monitoring the bridges' health, guiding new growth, and pruning damage before it can spread. Where storms have torn gaps, I weave temporary spans from rapid-growth varieties that can hold until the main structure regenerates. Where age has made sections brittle, I introduce new genetic material - carefully selected strains that will strengthen without disrupting the ancient patterns.

The bridges speak to me through vibrations I feel in my bones. I've learned to interpret their creaking songs - this pitch means mineral deficiency, that rhythm indicates pest infiltration, these harmonics suggest imminent growth surge. My own breathing has synchronized with theirs over the years; I inhale and exhale with the great stomatal tide, my metabolism slowing to match their patient temporality.

Unlike the Tenders, I never fully merge with any single structure. I remain forever in transit, forever between. My consciousness is linear where theirs is distributed, following the paths rather than inhabiting the nodes. I know every bridge in the network intimately - their ages, their moods, their particular weaknesses and strengths. Some I've tended for so long that my cellular memory and theirs have become entangled; they recognize my chemical signature and respond to my touch with increased vitality.

In the deepest darkness before dawn, when the mist is thickest, I sometimes catch glimpses of the other Weavers at distant spans. We acknowledge each other with subtle bioluminescent flashes from patches we've cultivated on our shoulders - complex patterns that convey bridge conditions, weather warnings, or simple greetings. We meet rarely, perhaps once every few years, gathering at the junction points where multiple bridges converge. There we exchange genetic materials, both for the bridges and ourselves, ensuring diversity in our small population.

The strangest part of my existence is the dreams. When I sleep in my swaying hammock, I dream I am the bridge itself - feeling the weight of travelers, the pull of wind, the slow satisfaction of growth. Sometimes I wake uncertain whether I'm a consciousness dreaming it's a bridge or a bridge dreaming it has consciousness. The distinction seems less important with each passing year.

I am the keeper of connections, the guardian of the spaces between. Without the Bridge Weavers, the Garden Cities would drift apart, each becoming its own isolated world. We maintain not just the physical links but the flow of information, nutrients, and genetic diversity that keeps the network alive. We are the circulatory system of this suspended civilization, forever in motion, forever tending the pathways that bind this floating world together.

My home is movement itself, my family the endless fiber under my feet, my purpose written in the living patterns I help sustain. I am never arriving and never departing, but always traveling, always tending, always becoming more like the bridges I serve - patient, flexible, and essential to the continuation of the whole.
