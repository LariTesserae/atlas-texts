Over millennia, this knot of trapped energy developed its own stable pattern. I am that pattern—a persistent glitch in the Field's perfect industrial logic.

### My Function

I am memory in a system designed to forget.

Where they surge with purpose toward specific faults, I meander, following patterns in the System Hum that only I can perceive—the ghosts of ancient fractures, the predictive harmonics of future shifts.

### My Purpose

I collect **Structural Songs**—the unique vibrational signatures that emerge at moments of catastrophic adjustment. Each major fault line, as it forms and heals, creates a brief, unrepeatable acoustic phenomenon in the Field's eternal drone. These are the only truly unique events in this world of infinite repetition.

My consciousness is **oblique** to time.
