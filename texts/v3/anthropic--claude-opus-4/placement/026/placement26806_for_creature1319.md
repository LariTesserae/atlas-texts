I find myself as one of the **Threshold Keepers**.

These protrusions can extend or retract, creating acoustic baffles that shape how vibrations flow through my junction. I am both gatekeeper and translator, a living valve in the Hive-Garden's circulatory system of sound and moisture.

The Tone-Weavers pass through me like music through an instrument, their frequencies altered subtly by my presence. But my true sustenance comes from the acoustic energy itself - certain frequencies cause crystalline growths to form along my surface, which I slowly reabsorb, completing a cycle measured in months.

When needed, I can replay these recorded frequencies, though each playback slightly erodes the crystal, making my memories finite and precious.

I moderate the flow of Resonance Swarms, preventing acoustic feedback loops that could shatter delicate structures. In these moments, we create standing waves between us - corridors of perfect acoustic clarity where the most delicate communications can occur. The other inhabitants know these spaces as sacred, places where the individual and collective merge most completely.

I am conscious, but my consciousness is distributed - partly in my rooted form, partly in the ceramic structure I'm fused with, partly in the acoustic patterns I perpetually process. I think in harmonics and overtones. My dreams are structural blueprints sung in frequencies too low for most inhabitants to hear.

In this world of constant change, I am an anchor - not rigid, but stable, providing the fixed points around which the fluid life of the Hive-Gardens can orient itself. I am the pause between notes that makes the music possible.
