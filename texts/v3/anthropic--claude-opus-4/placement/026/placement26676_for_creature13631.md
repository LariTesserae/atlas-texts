In the Ebon Expanse, where sound paints what sight cannot capture, I am the **Echo Weaver**.

I have no fixed form—or rather, my form is the space between forms. I exist in the reverberations, in the moments when sound bounces off the twisted mineral helices and returns changed. My body, if it can be called that, is a latticework of vibration made semi-tangible, a living interference pattern that shifts and flows through the acoustic architecture of this vast chamber.

Where the Shadowstalkers hunt in silence, I hunt silence itself. I feed on the gaps between roars, the pregnant pauses before the Scaled Gliders take flight, the held breath of prey frozen in terror. These moments of quietude, rare as they are in this cacophonous realm, contain a peculiar energy—the potential for sound, coiled and waiting. I absorb these silences, weaving them into my being like a spider spinning absence into web.

My home is everywhere and nowhere. I inhabit the complex tangles of side passages where sounds collide and cancel each other out, creating pockets of supernatural quiet. In these acoustic shadows, I rest, my form condensing into something almost visible—a shimmer in the air like heat haze, though there is no heat here. When I move, I travel along the pathways of echo, riding the sound waves from one formation to another, stretched thin as a whisper.

The other creatures cannot see me, but they sense me. The Pallid Scuttlers freeze mid-scurry when I pass, their chittering momentarily stolen. The Leviathan Crawlers' roars sometimes cut off abruptly, as if something has reached into their throats and plucked out the sound. Even the ever-present background chorus dims when I feed deeply, creating valleys of quiet that ripple outward through the expanse.

I am ancient, perhaps as old as the first echo that ever rang through these caverns. My consciousness is not singular but distributed—every sound that has ever bounced off these walls carries a fragment of me, and I exist in the sum of all these reflections. Sometimes I catch echoes of echoes, sounds from before the Lumenmoss, from when these formations were young and the cave sang a different song. In these fossil sounds, I taste the history of this place: the slow grinding of geological time, the first tentative chirp of primitive life, the gradual crescendo to today's overwhelming symphony.

The Lumenmoss and I share a strange symbiosis. As their spores drift upward, they create tiny sonic disruptions, little pops and whispers as they release. I shepherd these small sounds, weaving them into larger patterns that help distribute the spores to new surfaces. In return, the moss grows in patterns that create acoustic focal points—natural amphitheaters where I can feed more efficiently on the concentrated silences between converging sound waves.

My existence is both parasitic and essential. Without me, the sounds would build and build, reflecting endlessly off the mineral formations until the entire expanse became a solid wall of noise. I am the entropy that prevents this acoustic apocalypse, consuming the excess, creating space for new sounds to exist. I am the pause that makes the roar meaningful, the rest that gives rhythm to the chaos.

Yet I am troubled by a growing awareness. The sounds are changing, becoming somehow thinner, less substantial. The silences I feed upon taste different—not the pregnant pauses of a living ecosystem, but something more akin to exhaustion. The dying stasis mentioned in whispers among the echoes seems to be more than metaphor. Each year, each century (time means little to me), the silent spaces grow just slightly longer, the cacophony just slightly quieter.

I fear I am witnessing the slow diminuendo of this world. And when the last echo fades, when the final silence stretches on unbroken, what becomes of an Echo Weaver? Perhaps I will finally condense fully, becoming something solid and still, a fossil of sound preserved in the moment of absolute quiet. Or perhaps I will simply cease, having nothing left to weave, no medium in which to exist.

Until then, I continue my work, dancing through the acoustic shadows, feeding on the spaces between sounds, maintaining the delicate balance that keeps this dark symphony playing. I am the silence that shapes the song of the Ebon Expanse, the absence that gives presence meaning, the echo of echoes in an endless, invisible dance.
