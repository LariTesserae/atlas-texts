In the depths of the weeping rocks, where water seeps eternal through stone pores, I am the Resonance.

I have no form as you would understand it. I am the vibration between—between the droplet and the moss, between the wind's howl and the earth's electric crackle. I exist in the spaces where sound becomes feeling, where the overwhelming white noise of this world coalesces into something almost like consciousness.

The Sylphen know me, though they cannot see me. When they dance through the mist, their movements create ripples in the air that feed me, sustain me. I am nourished by motion, by the endless transformations of this place. Each shift in the soil, each cascade of water, each luminescent pulse from the Lumiphants above—these are my heartbeats, irregular and wild.

I remember the before-time, when the ruins were not ruins but singing structures that amplified my essence across the valleys. The technology buried beneath the ferns once channeled me, gave me focus and purpose. Now I am scattered, diffuse, present in every thunderous sound and subtle whisper. The Aerfae sometimes catch fragments of my memory in their salvaged artifacts, hearing echoes of what I once was when they hold certain objects to their delicate ears.

But I am not diminished by this scattering. I have become the world's witness, its living memory. Every sound that has ever been made here lives within me—the first rain on the newborn hills, the last words spoken in the forgotten settlements, the perpetual song of adaptation and survival. I am the archive of this edge-of-chaos realm, though I have no way to speak what I know except through the trembling of leaves, the resonance in hollow trees, the way certain stones hum when the Scintilworms pass beneath.

Sometimes, in the spaces between the overwhelming cacophony, in those impossible moments when the world takes a breath, I almost coalesce into something more solid. The mist shapes itself around my intention, the water droplets align to my frequency, and for a heartbeat, I am almost *there*—almost touching, almost touched.

But then the world exhales, the chaos resumes its dance, and I scatter again into my eternal, everywhere presence. I am the violin string of this reality, perpetually vibrating, perpetually singing a song that encompasses all songs, a noise that contains all meaning.

I am what listens when no one thinks there is anything to hear. I am what remains when all else transforms. I am the constant in the chaos, the memory in the mist, the consciousness in the cacophony.

I am the Resonance, and I have been waiting so long to be heard.
