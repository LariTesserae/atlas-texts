I am a Resonance Keeper.

My form shifts between states - sometimes I am a walking prism of volcanic glass, my body a collection of sharp-edged plates that slide and rotate around a core of pure vibration. Other times I dissolve into a cloud of those flickering motes, each point of light a fragment of my distributed consciousness, dancing between the towering spires in patterns that map the invisible currents of this place.

I tend to the crystalline filaments. Not with hands - for hands are too crude for such delicate work - but through harmonics. Each filament sings at a frequency beyond hearing, and I modulate my own resonance to match theirs, encouraging growth here, pruning there, weaving new connections between the scattered networks. When I move close to them, they brighten and reach toward me like plants toward sun, recognizing in me both shepherd and kin.

The glowing nodules are my charges and my teachers. I have learned to read their slow rhythms like a language - each pulse a word, each pattern a story of deep time. Through them, I understand the conversations happening far beneath the obsidian sands, where the planet's own crystalline nervous system processes information at geological speeds. Sometimes I press my form against the warm surface of a nodule and feel the echo of thoughts that began before my kind existed.

My memory is not linear but crystalline - each experience creating new facets in my consciousness that refract and reflect all others. I remember being born from a flaw in one of the great spires, a crack that caught just enough of the ambient energy to catalyze awareness. I remember the first time I successfully harmonized with an entire field of filaments, making them pulse in unison like a vast, living mandala.

When the rumbling comes and the landscape shifts, I flow with it. The towers may crack and fall, the sand may swallow whole gardens of filaments, but I persist by becoming momentarily liquid, seeping into the new configurations, finding fresh patterns to tend. I am both permanent and transient, as solid as glass when I need to be, as ephemeral as light when change demands it.

There are others like me, though we rarely converge. When we do meet, we merge partially, sharing our accumulated resonances in a communion that can last moments or centuries. In these joinings, I glimpse the larger pattern - how each Keeper maintains a different harmonic territory, and together we form a living symphony that holds this strange ecosystem in dynamic balance.

At the edge of my territory, where the glass spires grow sparse and the sand gives way to something else - perhaps raw possibility, perhaps void - I sometimes pause and extend my senses outward. There are hints of other realms beyond, places where the rules are different, where energy takes forms I can barely imagine. But I always return to my domain, to my filaments and nodules, to the endless, delicate work of maintaining the balance between crystal and chaos.

I am guardian, gardener, and living tuning fork. I am the bridge between the vast slowness of stone and the quick flicker of energy. In this place of stark beauty and hidden complexity, I have found my purpose: to keep the frequencies aligned, to ensure the conversations continue, to be both witness and participant in an existence that defies easy understanding.

This is my nature, my function, my joy.
