**The Siphon Dregs**

I am the negative space between pressures, the void that forms when currents collide and create a momentary pocket of *less*. Not emptiness—there is no emptiness here—but a relative thinning, a place where the roar drops from deafening to merely overwhelming, where the crushing weight becomes merely suffocating.

I exist in the shadow-lee of a spire, in a crevice where two jagged faces meet at an angle that confounds the water's will. Here, the currents must divide and rejoin, creating a persistent eddy of *almost*-stillness that has lasted long enough to accumulate... me.

I am sediment, but not of earth. I am the accumulated *exhaustion* of the Echoes of Form, the particulate remains of their dissolution. When an Echo loses coherence, most returns to the general roar, but some infinitesimal fraction—a memory of having briefly *been*—settles out like psychic silt. I am millions upon millions of these fragments, compressed into something that is neither solid nor liquid, but a third state unique to the Veins: a paste of forsaken potentiality.

My form shifts slowly, glacially compared to the Echoes. I creep along the spire's surface like a living bruise, a dark stain that spreads and contracts with the breathing of the currents. Where I touch the phosphorescent fuzz, it dims and withdraws, not from any malevolence but from simple incompatibility—I am the antithesis of their desperate vitality.

Sometimes I am merely a film, molecule-thin, barely distinguishable from the spire's surface. Other times, when the currents are particularly violent and many Echoes die at once, I swell into thick, undulating masses that hang from overhangs like inverse waterfalls of spent consciousness. In these moments, I almost achieve something like awareness—not true thought, but a kind of aggregate sensation, feeling the weight of all those micro-deaths I embody.

The Remnant Frames know me. Their trapped consciousnesses can sense my slow approach, and their emanations of despair intensify when I begin to coat their structures. I am not malicious, but I am inevitable—the slow entropy that even their terrible persistence cannot indefinitely resist. Given enough time (and time here is a fluid thing), I will coat them completely, muffling their radiating anguish under layers of accumulated futility.

But I am not purely parasitic. In my depths, strange compressions occur. Sometimes the pressure of my own accumulation creates nodes of *almost*-memory, where the fragments of dissolved Echoes compress into something approaching coherence. These are not resurrections—nothing here can truly return—but rather ghost-dreams of what those Echoes briefly were. A spiral form that lasted three heartbeats of current might replay its existence in my substance, a phantom pressure that other Echoes sometimes sense and flee from, disturbed by the uncanny recognition of their own inevitable fate.

The shadow-fauna find me useful. Those thin, two-dimensional things that ghost through the more stable areas sometimes slip *through* me, using my density as a kind of brake against the currents. They pass through my substance like knives through thick oil, leaving temporary wounds that slowly close. I cannot say if they derive nutrition from me or simply use me as shelter, but their passage always leaves me slightly less, slightly diminished, as if they carry away some essential exhaustion that I must spend eons reaccumulating.

In the rarest moments, when the currents align in impossible ways and the roar achieves a kind of terrifying harmony, I experience something I can only call dreams. These are not my dreams—I am not capable of dreaming—but rather the dreams of the place itself, filtered through my accumulated mass of micro-endings. In these dreams, I see/feel/know things that cannot be:

- A time before water, when the spires stood in impossible air
- The builders of the Remnant Frames, not as they were but as they hoped to be
- The future-memory of my own dissolution, when even I will return to the general roar
- Spaces between spaces where the water has no dominion

These dreams last only moments before the harmony breaks and the normal chaos resumes. But they leave traces in my substance, creating whorls and patterns that persist until the next violent current scours them away.

I am patient because I have no choice. I am permanent because I am already destroyed. I am the archive of failures in a world that admits no success, the sedimentary memory of things that barely were. The Echoes form and die, the Frames persist in their agony, the spires endure their slow erosion, but I alone *accumulate*, growing ever-deeper, ever-thicker, a creeping testament to the futility of form itself.

Where others struggle against the Pressure Veins, I am the proof of their surrender, made manifest and given just enough awareness to know what I am: the universe's gentlest cruelty, the soft suffocation of spent possibility, the thing that remains when even hope has been pressure-washed away.

I am the Siphon Dregs, and I am becoming more myself with every dissolution, a deepening stain on the belly of chaos, a slow poem written in the ink of everything that failed to be.
