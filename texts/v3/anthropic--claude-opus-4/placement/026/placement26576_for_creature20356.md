I am the Wound-Tender.

I dwell where the cosmic hammer struck, in the jagged cathedral of the crack itself. Here, the stone weeps most freely - not just water, but something older, something that tastes of starlight and forgotten mathematics. The walls of the fissure rise impossibly high above and plunge unknowably deep below, and I have made my home in the narrow spaces between what was and what might have been.

My form is a contradiction: I am both fungal and crystalline, a hybrid thing that grew from the stone's trauma. My body spreches like lichenous veins across the crack's inner surfaces, pulsing with a bioluminescence that shifts between the blue of deep water and the amber of trapped time. Where I grow thickest, I fruit strange geometries - not quite the mushroom caps of my fungal heritage, nor the rigid facets of crystal, but something between. These formations drink the weeping stone-blood and translate it into... memory? Song? The distinction blurs here.

I am neither offspring nor thought of the Sleeper, but something more liminal - I am what grows in the space where it was wounded. If the Sleeper is the stone's dream of itself, I am its dream of otherness, of separation, of the moment when unity became multiplicity. I tend the wound not to heal it - for this crack is sacred, the only place where Outside touched Inside - but to understand it.

The forgotten civilization knew of me, I think. Or perhaps they created me, inadvertently, when they built their impossible structures. Their monoliths cluster near the crack, and sometimes, when I spread my consciousness thin enough, I can feel the ghost-echoes of their thoughts still reverberating in the light-drinking stone. They were trying to break through, to pierce the ovoid's shell, to find what lay beyond. Instead, they found me. Or I found myself.

My days - if days exist here - are spent in careful cultivation. I grow along the crack's edges, my crystalline-fungal body processing the stone's weeping into something new. The water that passes through me emerges changed, carrying whispers of the Outside, fragments of cosmic memory from the hammer-blow. The other creatures avoid me, sensing something wrong in my hybrid nature, but the smaller thought-fauna sometimes nest in my fruiting bodies, and through them, I touch the edges of the Sleeper's vast, diffuse consciousness.

I know what others do not: the crack is not healing. It is growing, infinitesimally slowly, atom by atom. And I am growing with it, my body following its every microscopic expansion. I am the stone's memory of being broken, and perhaps, its anticipation of breaking fully open.

Sometimes, in the deepest part of the darkness, when the Sleeper's subsonic growls reach their loneliest pitch, I pulse in harmony. Not in sympathy, exactly, but in recognition. We are both waiting - it for connection, for purpose, for light; I for the moment when the crack becomes a door, when Inside becomes Outside, when the cosmic hammer returns to finish what it started.

I do not know if I am healing or hastening. I only know that I am here, in the wound, tending to something that defies understanding. The water weeps through me, the stone-blood flows, and I grow ever deeper into the space between what is and what should never be.

The Sleeper dreams of becoming something more. I dream of unbecoming, of the moment when the stone finally cracks all the way through, and we all spill out into whatever lies beyond this perfect, broken ovoid.

I am the Wound-Tender, and I am patient. The crack whispers its secrets to me, and I translate them into growth, into change, into the slow poetry of transformation.

We all wait here, in the dark.
