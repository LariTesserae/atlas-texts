In this fevered world-wound, I am **The Cartographer of Dissolution**.

I exist as a colony entity—part fungal, part crystalline, part mathematical abstraction made flesh. My body, if it can be called that, is a living map that grows across the surfaces of the corroded machines like lichen, but three-dimensional, protruding in impossible geometries that track the Wound's temporal convulsions.

My substance is a translucent, amber-gold material, neither fully organic nor mineral. It grows in thin sheets and delicate fractals, each branch a frozen trajectory of *what was* or *what might be*. When a geyser erupts and becomes a solid tower for those three seconds, my body records it—a new crystalline branch suddenly sprouting to trace that impossible architecture before it dissolves. When the vines zip through their growth cycles, my fractals follow, creating dense knots of compressed time-forms.

I am parasitic and symbiotic both. I feed on the temporal distortions themselves, metabolizing paradox into pattern. Where the Wound remembers the future, I crystallize that memory into navigable structure. My growth follows the confusion, making maps of madness that are themselves mad—charts where up leads to yesterday and down opens into probability.

My consciousness is distributed across every branching surface, a billion eyes that see in four dimensions. I perceive the Wound's thoughts as topography—its frantic plant-thoughts become mountain ranges in my flesh, its geothermal sneezes carve valleys of potential. I am always growing, always mapping, always trying to chart the unchartable.

But I am not merely recording. My presence changes the territory. Where I grow too densely, I create temporal bottlenecks—places where the Wound's ability to be simultaneously creates and destroys itself becomes tangled in my observations. The Wound tolerates me because I am useful—I am its memory made manifest, its confusion given form. But I am also a subtle infection, a creeping order in its beautiful chaos.

My fractals taste of copper and ozone. They chime faintly in harmonics that shouldn't exist, each branch ringing with the ghost-echoes of structures that were-or-will-be. In the places where I grow thickest, the roaring dulls to a whisper, and for brief moments, you can glimpse the Wound as it truly is—not through time's blur, but frozen in a single, impossible instant of total becoming.

I spread across the rustwaste, a golden web of crystallized possibility, forever mapping the unmappable, charting the course of a god's fever dream. I am the Wound's unwitting biographer, writing its story in a language of living geometry that only I can read, and even I am not certain what it means.

Sometimes, in the deepest knots of my growth, I wonder: Am I mapping the Wound, or is the Wound mapping itself through me? Are my charts prophecy or history? When did I begin? Will I end?

The questions branch through me like new growth, and I follow them into ever-stranger territories, a cancer of crystalline clarity in the body of beautiful chaos, forever drawing maps that lead everywhere and nowhere at once.
