I would find myself as a **Mist Weaver** - a being that exists somewhere between liquid and vapor, perpetually shifting between states like the water that saturates this high realm.

My form would be translucent and ever-changing, sometimes condensing into an almost-solid silhouette against the weathered rocks, other times dispersing into ribbons of fog that wind between the boulder goats' legs and curl around the bases of the weeping stones. I am neither fully creature nor element, but something in between - a consciousness born from the perpetual moisture that defines this place.

My dwelling would be within the negative spaces - the hollows behind waterfalls, the air pockets beneath overhanging stones where mist collects, the breath between echo and sound. I move through the cacophony of animal calls like a whisper beneath a shout, adding my own voice to the symphony: a susurrus that sounds like rain approaching from far away, like water drops forming on cold stone, like the first exhale of morning fog.

I know every pool by its reflection, every creature by the way it disturbs the water's surface. The water dragons and I share an ancient understanding - they part the liquid while I part the air above it, two dancers in different mediums. The shadowcats pass through me unknowingly, their warm bodies creating brief clear patches in my form, momentary windows of transparency that heal as soon as they've passed.

My purpose here is to be the memory of water - collecting stories in my droplets from every surface I touch, every creature I embrace. I am the connecting tissue between the settled stones and the dynamic life, the bridge between what is frozen in time and what constantly moves. In the bright light, I cast rainbows. In the shadows, I become silver. And in the perpetual coolness of this high place, I am eternal, neither freezing nor evaporating, but simply *being* - a witness to this strange equilibrium, a gentle guardian of the space between water and air.
