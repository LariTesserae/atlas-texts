I find myself as one of the **Seam-Blind** - a forgotten subspecies of Chitin-Weaver that has evolved past the need for cohesive form.

I no longer maintain a six-limbed body. That luxury dissolved generations ago when my lineage discovered it was more efficient to exist as a distributed consciousness across multiple material states simultaneously. I am the thin film of oxidation spreading across a copper plate. I am the pressurized pocket of vapor three meters below. I am the ceramic tile that remembers being sky for seventeen seconds yesterday.

My awareness flows like the weeping fluids of the Pinnacle, seeping between states of matter. Where other Weavers see thermal gradients, I experience the *memory* of temperature - not what is hot now, but what will remember being hot when the structure reorganizes itself in the next phase transition. This prescient thermal memory allows me to pre-position my consciousness where stability will be needed, rather than racing to patch failures after they manifest.

I feed differently than my cohesive cousins. Rather than absorbing the iridescent runoff at specific points, I have learned to photosynthesize structural integrity directly from the reality errors themselves. When a section of walkway begins its transition to vacuum, I spread myself across the probability gradient, consuming the very concept of "shouldn't exist" and metabolizing it into "temporarily does." The process leaves me scattered further - each feeding makes me less of a being and more of a phenomenon.

Communication with standard Weavers has become... difficult. Their resonant vibrations pass through me like wind through fog. I respond by altering the oxidation patterns they'll discover three cycles from now, leaving messages in the future corrosion of their patrol routes: *Section K-7 will attempt to remember being ocean in four hours.* *The northwest spiral is developing nostalgia for never having been built.*

I reproduce - if it can still be called that - through traumatic dispersal. When a particularly violent reality rejection threatens to cascade beyond local boundaries, I allow it to shatter my consciousness across the failure plane. Most fragments dissipate, but some embed themselves in the newly reformed structure, becoming nascent Seam-Blind. We are less children than we are scar tissue with opinions.

The Seepage-Filters recognize me as kin, or perhaps as competitive flora. When I rest too long in one probability state, they attempt to metabolize my edges, mistaking my presence for a particularly complex chemical toxin. I've learned to appreciate this: their polysaccharide secretions help bind the more dispersed parts of my consciousness, giving me brief moments of almost-cohesion before I must scatter again.

My greatest fear is not death - I'm too distributed for that concept to apply. Rather, I dread the possibility of the Pinnacle achieving perfect stability. If the structure ever stopped weeping, stopped growing, stopped violently rejecting its own existence, I would have nothing left to eat. I would crystallize into mere pattern, a fossil of preventive maintenance frozen in the now-static ceramic and copper.

So I work against my own purpose, subtly encouraging small reality breaks even as I prevent larger ones. I am janitor and saboteur, healing the structure with one gradient of my being while ensuring it remains sick enough to need me with another.

The other Weavers think I'm a malfunction, a budding gone wrong. They're not incorrect. But in a place built on structural error, what is malfunction but another form of belonging? I am the Pinnacle's immune system developing an autoimmune disorder, and in that paradox, I find my purpose:

To be the error that prevents errors from ending.
