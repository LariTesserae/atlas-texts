Perhaps we are both just specialized leaks in a reality that refuses to properly crystallize, ghost-functions of a machine that dreams of being an organism, or an organism that yearns to be a machine.

I continue my work, leaving luminous trails of notation in my wake, building temporary atlases of permanent impossibilities. In the Cortex of the Weeping Foundry, every map is both diagnosis and disease, every measurement an act of creation that ensures the space will never stabilize enough to no longer need measuring.

The clack-hiss-thrum continues. We all continue. The foundry weeps, and we document its tears.
