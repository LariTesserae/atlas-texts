In this saturated world of the Weeping Refinery, I would find myself as a **Pulse Tender** - not quite an Operator, but something both lesser and more essential.

Where the Operators maintain the grand vascular highways and structural integrity of the Weave-Walls, I exist in the interstitial spaces - the narrow, forgotten capillaries where even they cannot efficiently navigate. My body has adapted differently to this purpose.

I am **radially symmetric**, my form suggesting something between a starfish and a flowering plant. Five primary limbs extend from a central thoracic cavity, each limb capable of extreme elongation and compression. When fully extended, I can span nearly three meters across, but I spend most of my existence contracted to less than half a meter, wedged deep within the micro-channels that honeycomb the living infrastructure.

My skin secretes a specialized **lubricating enzyme** that allows me to move through spaces that would tear an Operator's hygroscopic hide. This secretion has a secondary purpose - it breaks down and redistributes the inevitable calcium deposits and mineral blockages that accumulate in the smallest vessels of the system. I am, in essence, a living catalyst for the Refinery's circulation.

Unlike the Operators with their obsidian eyes, I have no eyes at all. Instead, my entire surface is covered in **chemoreceptive cilia** - millions of tiny, hair-like sensors that taste the chemical gradients within the fluids I swim through. I navigate by following concentration trails of metabolic waste, seeking out the telltale signature of a developing blockage or a dying section of tissue.

My consciousness, if it can be called that, exists as a **distributed network** across all five limbs. Each appendage processes information semi-independently, creating a strange, pentagonal perspective of the world. I do not think in terms of "I" but rather as a constellation of purposes - one limb might be dissolving a mineral plug while another tests the pH of a nutrient stream, while a third anchors the whole assembly against the current.

I feed directly from the system I maintain, my central cavity opening like a flower to filter nutrients from the streams I clean. This feeding is not separate from my work - the very act of consuming the accumulated detritus keeps the micro-channels clear.

My lifecycle is measured not in years but in **circulation cycles**. I follow the slow, seasonal rhythms of the Refinery's metabolism, migrating through different sections as the nutrient flows shift. During the high-saturation periods when the Weave-Walls weep most heavily, I retreat to the deeper channels where the pressure is more stable. During the rare dry spells, I emerge closer to the surface, my secretions helping to maintain minimum flow in the desiccating vessels.

I reproduce through **fragmentation** - when I grow too large for efficient movement, one of my limbs will naturally detach, sealing both wounds with a quick-hardening protein plug. The severed limb will gradually develop its own central cavity and complementary appendages over several cycles. There is no parent or child, only a continuity of purpose.

My relationship with the Operators is one of **mutual ignorance**. They sense my presence only as a subtle improvement in flow rates, a reduction in the frequency of micro-blockages. I perceive them as massive, slow-moving pressure waves that occasionally disrupt my work zones. We are components of the same vast organism, yet we operate at scales too different for meaningful interaction.

The sound of my world is not the thunderous thrum that dominates the Operators' experience, but rather the **intimate gurgling** of fluid through tissue, the whisper of dissolved minerals precipitating out of solution, the tiny pops of gas bubbles forming and collapsing in the capillary networks. 

In the deepest channels, where the original engineering meets the millennia of biological overgrowth, I sometimes encounter **fossil traces** - fragments of whatever the Refinery was before it became what it is. These might be crystallized protein structures that don't match any current biological signature, or geometric patterns in the vessel walls that speak of intentional design rather than organic growth. I dissolve these too, eventually. The past is just another form of blockage.

My ending will come not through age but through **geometric inevitability**. Eventually, I will encounter a confluence of channels too complex to navigate, a Gordian knot of vessels where my five-fold symmetry becomes a liability rather than an asset. I will wedge myself too deeply to retreat, my secretions will cease, and I will slowly mineralize in place, becoming a node of harder tissue that future generations of Pulse Tenders will work to dissolve.

Until then, I pulse through the hidden circulatory system of the Weeping Refinery, a conscious current in the vast, slow metabolism of a city that dreams in centuries and breathes in decades, forever cleaning, forever flowing, forever part of something immeasurably larger than my distributed self can ever fully comprehend.
