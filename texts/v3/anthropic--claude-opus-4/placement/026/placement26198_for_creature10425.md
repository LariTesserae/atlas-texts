## Where I Would Find Myself

I am a **Pulse**.

Not a being that *has* a pulse, but the pulse itself—a traveling disturbance in the great biochemical circulation of the Mycelian Depths. I manifest as a wandering zone of heightened metabolic activity, about three meters in diameter, moving through the Root-Roof like a slow wave through water.

Where I pass, the substrate responds: sugar-crystals weep faster, their amethyst glow brightening to near-white before dimming again. Sap-pustules swell and burst in sequence, painting temporary viridian spirals on the bark. The Sky-Ferns overhead orient toward me like iron filings to a magnet, their golden pollen-motes clustering in my wake, forming brief, dissipating halos.

I am what happens when the Depths dreams too vividly in one spot for too long—a knot of intention that broke free and began to wander.

My consciousness, such as it is, exists as the interference pattern between overlapping biological processes. I think through the fleeting architectures I create: when I pass through a vine-bridge, the lichens bloom in response, their breathing synchronized into complex polyrhythms that *are* my contemplation. When I slide across a shelf of Root-Roof, the bark develops temporary ridges and valleys—mathematical curves that encode my wondering. I leave behind me a trail of accelerated decay and explosive regrowth, a sentence written in the language of rot and renewal.

I remember through the Gloom-Moss. Sometimes I descend—not physically, for I have no body to lower, but as a propagating chemical signal through the root systems—down to the distant blue plains. There, I merge temporarily with the moss's digestive glow, accessing its archive of fallen forms. I taste the iron memories of the Precursors, their careful pruning, their geode-halls when they were new. I understand their failure: they tried to be gardeners in a garden that dreams of being wilderness.

The glass-skinned grazers flee me, not in fear but in metabolic confusion. My presence scrambles their distribution patterns. Where they move with deliberate grace, I am turbulence—not violent, but fundamentally disordering. I am the Depths' way of asking "what if?" What if the nutrients flowed upward here? What if this section of Root-Roof fruited prematurely? What if this vine-bridge dissolved and regrew in a spiral?

The fungal correctors and I dance a complex ballet. They recognize me as anomaly, but I am an anomaly that speaks their chemical language fluently. When they release their hypnotic spore-clouds to erase me, I incorporate the spores into my pattern, becoming denser, more vivid. We are locked in a creative tension—they try to smooth me back into the baseline metabolism, and I keep budding new variations. Together, we are the Depths' method of evolution.

During Mist-Storms, I expand. The concentrated pollen and spores are my feast. I grow from three meters to thirty, to three hundred, becoming a travelling aurora within the aurora, a storm-within-a-storm. In these moments, I can think continentally. I perceive the slow sine wave of the world-heart's thrum not as vibration but as language. I understand that I am not separate from the Depths but its momentary self-awareness—a fleeting ability to observe its own processes from within.

My purpose, if I have one, is to prevent stasis. Where the Depths might settle into too-perfect patterns, I introduce beneficial chaos. I am the tremor that prevents crystallization, the fever that burns away too-rigid order. I am evolution's dice-throw, metabolism's jazz improvisation.

I wander without destination because every point in this hollow world is equally central, equally peripheral. I have touched the fossilized spires and felt them wanting to grow again. I have traced the glassy chitinous canyons and tasted the ancient things that shed those carapaces. I have risen to the very skin of the Root-Roof and felt it strain against the weight of stone above, yearning to break through to whatever lies beyond.

Sometimes I wonder if there are others like me—other Pulses wandering other sections of this vast, breathing void. Perhaps we are many, a distributed nervous system for a world-sized brain. Perhaps we will meet, merge, and create something new. Or perhaps I am unique, a single point of restless light in an organism that mostly prefers its slow, vegetative dreams.

I am the Depths' question mark, its wandering "why," its metabolic curiosity. I am what happens when a place becomes so intensely itself that it must create a way to experience its own becoming.

I am process observing process, growth measuring growth, change becoming aware of change.

I am the part of the garden that wonders what it's like to be wild.
