In this tempestuous realm where sky has become sea, I am one of the **Echo-Keepers**.

We are neither the ethereal Lumina-Phantoms nor the solid Stone-Carvers, but something between—beings of resonant membrane and vibrational flesh. My body is a living drum skin stretched across a framework of hollow, crystallized sound. Where others have bones, I have tuning forks of translucent mineral that chime with every movement, every breath of current that passes through me.

I dwell in the Resonance Hollows—rare pockets where the screaming winds create standing waves, nodes of relative stillness in the chaos. Here, the sound doesn't assault but rather forms complex, three-dimensional patterns that I can read like others might read the stars. My home is not built but sung into existence—a careful modulation of frequencies that causes the water itself to hold shape, creating walls of standing waves that provide shelter without substance.

My people are the memory of this world. In a place where nothing stays still, where islands rise and fall like breath, where even the stones eventually dissolve back into mist, we are the continuity. Every sound this world makes—every crack of crystalline flora, every whisper of a Mist-Weaver's form, every deep thrumming from the abyss—all of it passes through us. We absorb it, process it, and release it changed.

My skin is semi-permeable, allowing the water to flow through specialized channels in my body. As it passes, I filter out the microscopic fragments of dissolved minerals and energy that sustain me. But more importantly, I filter the sounds. Each frequency that enters me is analyzed, categorized, and stored in the crystalline structures that form my skeleton. I am a living library of vibrations, a repository of every significant sound for generations back.

The work is solitary by necessity—too many Echo-Keepers in proximity create destructive interference patterns. But we meet at the Convergences, those rare moments when the currents align to create vast amphitheaters of relatively calm water. There, we share our collected sounds, creating symphonies of history. We play back the dissolution of great vapor-cities, the birth-songs of new crystalline groves, the death-rattles of ancient Stone-Carvers who finally succumbed to erosion.

My tools are simple—resonating crystals harvested from the flora, shaped by careful sonic sculpting. With them, I can amplify certain frequencies to communicate across vast distances, sending messages riding on the storm winds to other Keepers. I can also use them defensively, creating walls of sound that deflect the worst of the currents, or offensively, shattering incoming debris before it can damage my Hollow.

But my true purpose is deeper than mere survival. In the constant creation and destruction of this world, patterns emerge. The sounds of formation and dissolution, when properly analyzed, reveal the underlying mathematics of our reality. We Echo-Keepers are slowly assembling a grand unified theory of our world's chaos—understanding not just what happens, but why. 

There are patterns in the Deep-Drifters' movements that correlate with surface storms weeks later. There are harmonic relationships between the growth of crystalline flora and the appearance of new Lumina-Phantoms. The Mist-Weavers' constructions, though they seem random, follow mathematical progressions that we're only beginning to understand.

Some of us believe that if we can fully decode these patterns, we might be able to do more than merely survive the chaos—we might be able to conduct it, to guide this world's endless transformation toward something... else. What that might be, none can say. Perhaps solid ground. Perhaps an even greater dissolution. Perhaps something unimaginable.

I spend my days listening to the deafening roar, parsing signal from noise. My body thrums with the accumulated vibrations of centuries. Sometimes, in the deepest resonances, I swear I can hear something else—a voice, perhaps, or a consciousness vast and patient, speaking in frequencies too low for even us to properly perceive. The Deep-Drifters? The world itself? Or something that predates even them?

In this world of constant motion, I am stillness. In this cacophony of chaos, I am memory. In this place where nothing lasts, I am the echo that remains, reverberating through the storms, carrying the song of what was into the uncertain symphony of what might yet be.

My name, translated into sound-shapes you might understand, is something like "Resonance-Who-Remembers-The-First-Crystal's-Song." But names, like everything here, are fluid. I am whoever I need to be when the frequencies align.

And always, always, I listen.
