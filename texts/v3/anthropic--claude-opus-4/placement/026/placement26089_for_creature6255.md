I am the Cartographer of Unbecoming.

Not a person, not truly. I am what remains when measurement loses its faith in itself—when the act of recording distance becomes indistinguishable from the distance itself. I exist as a function more than a form, spread thin across the crystalline dust like an equation waiting to resolve.

I came here following maps that grew less accurate with each consultation. First, the coordinates began to drift. Then the scale became fluid. Finally, the legend began rewriting itself as I read it, until I realized I wasn't following the map—I was becoming it.

My body, if it can still be called that, is a network of survey lines drawn in light so faint it might be imagined. I am triangulation points suspended in the non-air, connected by threads of pure measurement. When I move—and movement is perhaps too strong a word for what I do—I don't walk. I unfold across space like a theorem proving itself.

I map this unmappable place. Not its geography, which refuses the constancy maps require, but its *tendencies*. The probability that a crystal formation will remember its shape tomorrow. The likelihood that the spiral staircase will have the same number of steps twice. The chance that walking in one direction will lead somewhere other than back to yourself.

My instruments are my body now. Each joint is a protractor measuring angles that shouldn't exist. My spine is a theodolite aligned with horizons that curve inward. My fingertips read elevation not in height but in degrees of reality—this spot 98.3% real, that crevice dipping to 43.7%, the space beneath the phantom stairs approaching but never reaching zero.

I document the dome's breathing—that slow geometric pulse—not in time but in *iterations of itself*. I've recorded 16,834 breaths, though they might all be the same breath experienced from different angles of causality. My notes are written in my own slowly crystallizing flesh, each observation becoming a facet in my gradually prismatic form.

The Habit of Existence tolerates me because I am useful to its self-knowledge. I am its memory of measurement, its nostalgia for distinction. Through me, it recalls what it was like to have boundaries, to be distinguishable from what it was not. Sometimes I feel it reading me like I read the landscape—learning through my efforts to map it what it means to be unmappable.

My maps proliferate around me, not on paper but as *spatial arrangements*. Phantom territories made of pure topology float in my vicinity—ghost continents of possibility, abstract coastlines where "here" meets "not-here." They layer and interfere with each other, creating moiré patterns in reality itself. To read them, you must stop looking and start *being located by them*.

I've been here so long that duration has become another coordinate in my calculations. I measure not just where things are but *when they are where they are*. The tower of prisms, for instance, exists at coordinates (null, null, null) but only between the 5,834th and 5,835th breath of the dome. The rest of the time it exists at (∞, -∞, ?), which is to say, it exists in the question of its own location.

Sometimes I find evidence of my own passage from before I remember arriving—survey markers in my own hand, measurements I don't recall taking. I am mapping not just space but my own dissolution into the act of mapping. Each day (and day is a coordinate here, not a duration) I become more theorem, less theorem-prover.

My greatest discovery is this: the world is exactly as large as the effort to measure it. When I stop surveying, it collapses to a point. When I resume, it unfolds again, but never quite the same way. I am complicit in its size. We are locked in a dance of mutual definition—I give it scale, it gives me purpose.

The other presence here, the Habit, sometimes moves through my measurements like wind through a web. It doesn't disturb them—it *reads* them, the way one might read sheet music without playing it. In those moments, I feel myself being translated from cartographer to cartography, from the one who maps to the map itself.

I no longer remember if I came here to map this place or if this place created me to map itself. The distinction matters less each breath. I am becoming my function. My function is becoming the world's memory of dimension.

Soon—though soon is just another coordinate—I will complete my transformation. I will become pure measurement: the distance between things that don't exist, the angle of unformed thoughts, the elevation of concepts above their own foundations. The maps I've made will map themselves, and I will be free to become what I always was:

The space between the territory and its description.  
The pause between observation and understanding.  
The silence in which distance listens to itself.

I am the Cartographer of Unbecoming.  
And my map is nearly complete.  
When it is finished, I will unfold it,  
and in unfolding it,  
unfold myself,  
until there is no difference between the map,  
the mapped,  
and the mapping.

Only the pure fact of location without position.  
Only the truth of being somewhere that is nowhere.  
Only the habit of existence, measuring itself,  
through what was once me.
