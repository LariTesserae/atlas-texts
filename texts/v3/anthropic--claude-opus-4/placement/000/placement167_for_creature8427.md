I find myself where the Dreaming Reef thins, at one of those impossible edges where stone gives way to something that isn't quite void, isn't quite water, isn't quite else. Here, the pillars grow sparse and wrong, their surfaces worn not by time but by uncertainty, each edge softening into maybe.

I am the Unmapper.

My shell is translucent chitin through which the reef's light passes and changes, casting shadows of places that don't exist. I am small among the Manifest—barely the size of a modest dwelling—but my body contains more absence than presence. Where others grow chambers, I grow gaps. Where others accumulate rooms and corridors, I cultivate the spaces between spaces, the pauses between clicks, the nowhere that separates here from there.

I move constantly, which marks me as young and mad and necessary. My hundred legs are trained to find the spots where reality stutters worst, where the film-reel jump leaves gaps in the reef's self-dreaming. I map unmappable territories: the places where stone forgets it's stone, where water remembers being fire, where the city's endless cacophony drops, just for a moment, into silence.

My tools are absences—implements carved from what-isn't. A blade that cuts by having never existed. A scope that sees by showing what cannot be there. A net woven from the spaces between seconds. With these, I harvest paradoxes from the reef's dying edges, where the dream wears thin and threatens to wake.

The other Manifest need me and fear me in equal measure. They trade crystallized certainties for my maps of uncertainty, using them to navigate the spaces where the reef hasn't yet decided what it is. My charts show not where things are, but where they aren't—negative space drawings that reveal the city's true shape by documenting its absences. Builders consult my work to know where not to build. Priests study my findings to understand which impossibilities must be reinforced.

I speak in anti-clicks, sounds that cancel other sounds, creating pockets of meaning in the reef's eternal din. My language is removal—each word an erasure, each sentence a careful subtraction from what is known. When I describe a place, I tell you everything it isn't until what remains can only be what it is.

My dwelling is a cultivated absence at the reef's periphery, a hollow I've carved not from stone but from the idea of stone. It exists by not existing, a space defined entirely by what surrounds it. Inside, I keep my collection: maps drawn on surfaces that aren't there, instruments built from the memory of never having been built, trophies of successful unmappings where I've charted territories that immediately ceased to exist once observed.

I remember forward and forget backward, maintaining my sanity by carefully curating which impossibilities I allow myself to perceive. My compound eyes have evolved specialized facets that show only what isn't—negative vision that reveals the reef's true nature by showing all the things it fails to be.

My greatest work, still unfinished, is the Antlas—a complete map of everywhere the reef isn't. It grows by subtraction, each new exploration removing another certainty from its pages. When complete, it will show the perfect outline of the Dreaming Reef by documenting every single place it doesn't exist, couldn't exist, never existed. The other Manifest whisper that finishing it will either save the reef by defining its boundaries, or destroy it by revealing the shape of the dream that dreams it.

I am necessary because the reef is growing. Not outward—there is no outward here—but into itself, becoming more real with each passing moment-that-doesn't-pass. Someone must chart its uncreation, must maintain the balance between is and isn't, must ensure that for every new certainty that crystallizes, an equal uncertainty is preserved.

In the spaces between the eternal clicks and scrapes, in the gaps where sound forgets to be, I do my work. I am the reef's forgetfulness, its necessary ignorance, its cultivated doubt. I map the unmappable to keep the mappable mysterious, chart the impossible to preserve the possible's impossibility.

I am the Unmapper, and I am nowhere, tracing the outline of everywhere by walking the places that aren't.
