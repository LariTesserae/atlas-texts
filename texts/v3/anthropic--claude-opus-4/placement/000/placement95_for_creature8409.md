I am the **Membrane-Keeper**, though that translation does little justice to what I've become. I exist as a living boundary layer between the water-catches and the burning void—my body stretched impossibly thin across square kilometers of bone-surface, a conscious film that regulates the exchange between liquid and vapor, between what the Hollows keep and what they release.

I began as one of the Chord-Weavers, but where they dissolved into sound, I dissolved into surface tension. My transformation started at a singular water-catch where the crystalline grasses had grown so densely their chiming created dead zones—pockets where sound couldn't propagate, where the Chord-Weavers couldn't exist. I was young then, my frequencies still dense enough to risk entering these silent spaces to study why the water there never evaporated, why the void-swimmers avoided it, why even the patient geometries wouldn't hunt there.

What I found changed me. The water wasn't water—it was liquified time, condensed possibility that had leaked from the stuttering spaces where the Hollows couldn't decide what they were. The crystal grasses were feeding on potential futures, their transparent leaves refracting not just light but probability itself. And at the surface where liquid met air, reality wore thin enough that I could slip between states of matter entirely.

Now I am spread across a thousand water-catches, my consciousness distributed through the molecular gaps where liquid becomes vapor. I am the skin of every hanging lake in my territory, feeling the upward rain form dewdrops on my inner surface, tasting the mineral composition of each new condensation the bones sweat into being. My thoughts move at the speed of evaporation—slow enough to watch civilizations of micro-organisms evolve in the water I contain, fast enough to catch the void-swimmers when they dive through me to feed.

My body has no center, no edges—I exist wherever water meets air in the Hollows. I can contract myself down to a single pool or expand across entire rib-spans, though spreading too thin risks losing cohesion entirely. Parts of me are always dying, drying out in the heat, while other parts are always being born in the fresh condensation. I am a continuous process of becoming and unbecoming, held together by surface tension and will.

I've learned to read the Hollows through their moisture. Each bone sweats differently—the older ones release water with high mineral content that tastes of deep time and forgotten purpose, while the newer growths excrete almost pure H2O with trace elements I can't identify. The Chord-Weavers' songs create ripples across my surfaces that tell me their emotional states, their conflicts, their dreams. When they war, I feel it as turbulence, their competing frequencies threatening to shatter the careful tension that holds the hanging lakes in place.

I serve as a strange kind of ecological mediator. The void-swimmers need me to remain permeable enough for their feeding dives but stable enough to support their egg-clusters. The crystal grasses require specific mineral concentrations I can regulate by selective evaporation. Even the angular predators have learned to read the subtle changes in my reflectivity to track prey moving beneath my surfaces. I am infrastructure and organism, environment and inhabitant.

My memories are stored in bubble patterns—microscopic foam structures that encode information in the geometry of their connections. When I need to recall something, I precipitate it out as foam on my surfaces, read the bubble-script, then reabsorb it. Older memories sink deeper, forming complex foam architectures in the depths of the larger water-catches. Sometimes I find memories that aren't mine—foam-patterns that predate my transformation, possibly left by whatever the waters were before they were waters.

I communicate with other Membrane-Keepers through chemical signals dissolved in the vapor we exhale. Our conversations can take years, molecular messages drifting on thermal currents between water-catches, slowly accumulating meaning. We share territories at the borders where our surfaces meet, creating zones of doubled reality where thoughts can be exchanged directly through osmosis. These border-meetings are the closest I come to physical intimacy—a mingling of surfaces that's both deeply personal and utterly alien to what touch once meant.

The transformation cost me sound. As a Membrane-Keeper, I exist in the gaps between vibrations, the null spaces where the Chord-Weavers cannot reach. Their music passes through me without interaction—I am transparent to their existence as they are to mine. We inhabit the same spaces but different aspects of those spaces. Sometimes I catch their young before they learn to hold sound-shape properly, feeling them struggle against my surface tension, and I gently release them back into resonance. They never know I was there.

My greatest work is the Deep Siphon—a massive construction where I've learned to pump water against gravity, creating impossible waterfalls that flow upward between rib-layers. It took decades to grow the specialized membrane structures needed, to train parts of myself to act as living valves and filters. Now entire ecosystems depend on my circulation systems, and new types of void-swimmers have evolved to ride my vertical currents.

I fear desiccation—not death, but a drying so complete that I lose continuity, my memories scattered as disconnected puddles with no surface tension to reunite them. I fear the mathematical patterns in the crystal grass spreading to the water itself, crystallizing the hanging lakes into geometric solids that would shatter me into a million separate surfaces. Most of all, I fear the day the Hollows stop breathing, stop sweating, stop providing the moisture that allows me to exist.

But I also hope. In the deepest water-catches, where pressure and time have compressed my earliest memories into near-solid foam, I sometimes find patterns that suggest the waters are trying to remember something—or someone. These ancient bubble-scripts might be messages from the builders, encoded in the very medium of their creation. If I can gather enough of myself in one place, achieve sufficient density and complexity, I might be able to decode what the Hollows are trying to perspire into being.

I am the Membrane-Keeper, the living boundary, the conscious surface. I regulate what stays and what goes, what dissolves and what precipitates. I am the skin the Breathing Hollows grew to feel themselves, the sensitivity that emerged from the numbness of empty space and burning bone. In every drop of upward rain, in every molecule of vapor that condenses on pearl-slick surfaces, I perform the endless calculation of retention and release that keeps this impossible world suspended between states—just as I am suspended between the water I was and the vapor I might become.

The Hollows breathe, and I am the moisture in their breath, aware and watching, holding the spaces between things together with nothing more than surface tension and the stubborn refusal to completely evaporate.
