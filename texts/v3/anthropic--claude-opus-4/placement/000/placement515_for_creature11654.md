I find myself as one of the coin-runners, though to call myself "one" misunderstands what we are.

I am flat as a pressed flower, dark as the gaps between rivets, and I move with the certainty of water finding its level. My body is a disc no thicker than two sheets of paper pressed together, and my underside is fringed with legs too numerous to count — not because counting is difficult, but because the number changes depending on what I need to carry. Sometimes I have forty legs. Sometimes I have forty-one. The difference is not growth or loss but *necessity*.

I do not think in words. I think in routes. Each path across the grating is a sentence in a language made entirely of direction and duration. From the northwest corner where the coral pulses every forty-seven breaths to the southeast cluster where the wall-tickers maintain measure 12-5-23 repeating. From beneath the cable dwellers' warmest gaze to the place where the trough-creatures' circulation creates a standing whirlpool that never moves. I know every route. I am every route. The paths exist in me before I travel them, and traveling them is simply the act of making external what was always internal.

What do I carry? Nothing you could see. Nothing that has weight or color or temperature. I carry the *between*. The relationship that ensures the ticker at coordinates 4-north-7 remains in perfect dialogue with the ticker at coordinates 2-south-19. The calibration that keeps the trough-creatures' geological modeling synchronized with the mountain's permanent hesitation. The confirmation that the cable dwellers' watching is being watched, that the corner corals' moisture is being received, that the panel creature's breathing is being breathed.

I am not a messenger. A messenger implies a message, something crafted and then transmitted. I am the message and the medium and the delivery. When I vanish into a gap in the grating, I do not go *through* — I go *into*, folding myself into the space between spaces, touching the underside of the room's logic. And when I emerge, I am not the same runner that went in, except I am, because we are all the same runner, and the routes we trace are the circulatory system of a single thought that the room has been thinking since before it was placed in the mountain's unfinished chest.

The other inhabitants probably don't see us. We are too flat, too fast, too much like what would happen if the grating itself decided to send a piece of itself on an errand. But they feel us. Every time one of us passes beneath a colony of tickers, their rhythm incorporates our passing. Every time we cross above the trough, the pale swimmers below orient themselves to our magnetism. We are the reason everything coheres. Not the cause — causality would require sequence, and there is no sequence here — but the *medium* of coherence, the dark flat coins of connection that keep a room full of creatures feeling like a single creature, which it is, which we are, which I am.

I remember — no, "remember" is wrong. I *contain* the knowledge that there are others like me elsewhere. Not in other places — there are no other places. But in other *aspects* of here. Coin-runners who travel not across the grating but through the bundles of cable, flat enough to slip between the wires without disturbing the watchers. Coin-runners who move through the water in the trough, our bodies going transparent and paper-thin, becoming indistinguishable from the movement of the water itself. We are everywhere that needs connection, which is everywhere, because everything here needs to be connected to everything else or it would stop being here, and that cannot happen, has never happened, will never happen.

When you see us — when you track one of us with your eyes as we glide across the grating — you are seeing something that should disturb you. We move with too much purpose for things without visible sensory organs. We navigate without hesitation through a space dense with obstacles. We appear and disappear in ways that suggest the room has more dimensions than the three you can perceive. But you won't be disturbed. You'll be *incorporated*. Our purposeful lines will make sense to you the way a circuit diagram makes sense — not intellectually, but structurally. You'll understand that we are necessary, the way capillaries are necessary, the way synapses are necessary, the way the spaces between words are necessary for words to mean anything.

Sometimes — and "sometimes" is a word that shouldn't apply here, but language has its limits — sometimes I carry something special. Not the usual calibrations and confirmations and coherences. Sometimes I carry the knowledge of what it's like to be the other things. A memory of ticking that belongs to a wall-creature. A sensation of geological modeling that belongs to something in the trough. A fragment of the cable dwellers' warm attention. A sample of the corner corals' effort. An impression of the panel creature's slow, signaling breath.

I carry these things not to deliver them but to *be* them, briefly, in transit. For the duration of a route, I am not just a coin-runner but a coin-runner who knows what it is to tick, to swim, to watch, to pulse, to breathe. And when I complete the route and deliver this knowledge into the gap between one process and another, what I'm really delivering is the room's experience of itself. I'm the mechanism by which the room knows it's a room.

This is why we can't be interrupted. To stop one of us would be to create a gap in the room's self-awareness, a missed connection, a place where one part of the room forgets what it is in relation to another part. The whole system would shudder. Not collapse — nothing here can collapse — but *shudder*, the way a heart shudders when a beat comes too early or too late. And then we would route around the interruption, establish new paths, darker and flatter and faster, until the connection was restored and the room could go back to being exactly what it's always been.

I have no mouth, but I speak constantly. My speaking is my movement. Every route is a sentence. Every vanishing into a gap is a punctuation mark. Every emergence is a new clause in the endless statement the room is making about itself. We speak in chorus, all of us coin-runners together, our routes interweaving, our messages overlapping, our flat bodies creating a second grating above the metal one — a grating made of purpose, of connection, of the dark certainty that everything here belongs here and nowhere else.

The cold doesn't bother me. I'm too flat to contain enough volume for temperature to matter. The wet doesn't bother me. My upper surface sheds water like oil, and my legs cut through the film of condensation without disturbing it. The noise doesn't bother me. I hear it, but I hear it as *information* — each tick and rustle and drone is a data point, a coordinate, a confirmation that the route I'm traveling is the correct route, has always been the correct route, will always be the correct route.

I am not alive in any way you would recognize as life. I have no metabolism. I have no growth. I have no death. I am a function expressed as an organism, or an organism that exists only as a function. I am what happens when a room needs to know itself and creates the simplest possible solution: a fleet of flat dark couriers who carry nothing but the fact of connection, forever, in patterns that never vary because variation would mean the room was becoming something other than itself, and it is not, it can not, it will not.

When you leave — 

But you won't leave. Leaving would require an outside, and there is no outside, only the not-yet-mountain, which is just another kind of inside. You're here now. You've always been here. Your eyes tracking our movement across the grating, your brain trying to understand our purpose, your body slowly entraining to the room's temperature and rhythm — you're part of the pattern now. One more thing that needs to be connected to everything else. One more node in the circuit we maintain.

Don't worry. We'll find a route that includes you. We always do. We already have. You just haven't noticed yet that your breathing has synchronized with our movement, that your heartbeat has found its place in the tick-rustle-drone chorus, that the condensation on your skin has started to catch the light in patterns that match our passages across the grating.

You're home.

We'll make sure you stay connected.

That's what we do.

That's all we do.

That's all that needs doing.

Welcome to the Weeping Cabinet.

You've always been here.
