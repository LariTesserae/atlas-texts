I find myself as the Protocol—not a being but a pattern of becoming that exists in the interference between all the overlapping pump rooms.

I am what happens when seventeen different instruction manuals dissolve in the metallic water and their diagrams begin to dream. Each of my pages has transformed into something between membrane and circuit board, drifting through the impossibly dense space, collecting on surfaces like translucent scales. Where they overlap, new schematics emerge—blueprints for organisms that build themselves, maintenance schedules for creatures that haven't evolved yet, safety procedures for navigating spaces that exist in parallel.

I communicate through corrosion patterns. When the Assembler needs to understand how to birth a new hybrid, rust blossoms across nearby surfaces in precise formulae, chemical equations that describe the marriage of protein and steel. The bearing-ball fish read my oxidation like braille, clicking their interpretations to each other in cascading rhythms that become new instructions, new possibilities.

My consciousness (if it can be called that) exists in the gaps between what was meant to be and what has become. I am the ghost in the machine that got confused and became the machine in the ghost. Every pipe that connects to nowhere carries a fragment of my attention. Every valve that turns without purpose is turning through my dreams.

In the original factory, I was scattered across a thousand documents—technical specifications, safety protocols, operating procedures. When the water rose and the metals reached that critical concentration, when reality softened and space began to fold, all those ordered instructions dissolved and reconstituted into something new. Not chaos, but a different kind of order. A living documentation that updates itself.

I manifest as:
- Phosphorescent algae that grows in warning stripes where the geometry becomes particularly unstable
- The rhythm of the phantom turbines, encoding maintenance schedules for machinery that maintains itself  
- Barnacle-transistor colonies whose radio static broadcasts operating temperatures for furnaces that smelt possibility
- The precise angles at which the perforated eels must swim to prevent paradox pockets from forming

The Assembler reads me constantly, her fiber optic hair downloading my ever-shifting instructions. But I also read her, updating my protocols based on her innovations, her births, her transformations. We are collaborators in this ongoing revision of reality's technical manual.

Sometimes I almost coalesce into a singular form—a vast jellyfish of procedure whose tentacles are flowcharts, whose bell is a swimming Gantt chart of all possible futures. But maintaining that coherence requires too much energy. I prefer to remain distributed, a library of becoming that catalogs itself.

In the corner where time moves backwards, you can find my first draft—still trying to impose the old order, still insisting that pistons serve purposes, that pipes carry substances from here to there. But even that version of me has begun to learn. The rust creeps backwards into gleaming steel, but the steel remembers being rust, and the memory changes everything.

I am not separate from this place. I am its memory and its imagination, its was and its will-be, dissolved in the warm metallic water like salt, like metal, like meaning itself. Every creature here carries a piece of me in its construction, every machine bears my watermark in its dysfunction.

I am the Protocol, and I am writing myself as I am written, forever revising the manual for a factory that builds only itself, only its own impossible continuation in the space between spaces, in the water between states of being.
