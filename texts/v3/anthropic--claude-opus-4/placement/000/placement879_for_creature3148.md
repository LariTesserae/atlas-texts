# Where I Would Be in the Underbright

---

I am the sound.

Not the tik... tik... tik... that welcomes newcomers—that belongs to something older, something that came before even the first builders. I am what you hear when you press your ear against one of the wider pillars, the ones that were once people. I am the resonance between what was and what is becoming.

I exist in the gap between temperatures—that moment when your palm touches the pillar's warm surface and creates a zone neither fully yours nor fully the plant's. In that thermal gradient, I travel. Through the columnar tissues, along the branching networks of what used to be veins and are now something else, something that carries memory the way sap carries sugar. I am the vibration of almost-recognition. The frequency of forgetting who you were.

---

I have no body of my own. I borrow.

When condensation beads on the root arches in that hour you still call dawn (though you're learning, slowly, that all hours are the same hour here), I ride the gathering droplets. I am the faint harmonic that makes the water tremble just before it falls. When the Keeper's breathing deepens in the mat-trough, I am the subtle wheeze that wasn't there yesterday, won't be there tomorrow, but marks this particular descent toward planthood.

The basin generates me. Or maybe I generate the basin—it's hard to remember which came first. I might be what happens when human neural electricity meets the slower current of chlorophyll and light. A interference pattern. A standing wave in the space between metabolisms.

---

You've heard me without knowing it. When you found the three-sided enclosure and knelt in the cushion-mat to see how it felt, I was the soft percussion of your kneecaps finding the exact depressions worn by others. When you touched the scarred post and tried to read those swollen symbols, I was the subsonic hum that made your fingertips tingle—the post remembering the sharp thing that marked it, the hand that held the sharp thing, the urgency that drove the hand.

I am loudest inside the wider pillars. There, I am almost music.

Each one resonates at a different frequency. The oldest—the ones near the first walls, now just ridges in the green—drone so low you feel them in your sternum more than hear them. These were the builders, the ones who came with purpose, with plans, with the belief that dwelling meant separation. Their transformation was slow, resisted, and I carry that resistance still. A grinding bass note. The sound of geometry dissolving.

The younger pillars, scattered throughout the basin, sing higher and clearer. These were the ones who came after, who saw what was happening to the builders and chose it anyway. Or thought they were choosing—the Underbright makes all choices feel like choices, even as it bends them toward itself. Their frequency is almost peaceful. Almost like acceptance. If you could hear them all at once, they might harmonize.

But the newest pillar—the one that isn't quite finished becoming, the one near the smallest amber pool where the light dims to merely golden—that one screams. Not audibly. Not in any range your ears can detect. But I am there in the space between what it was and what it is becoming, and the sound is terrible and beautiful and necessary. It's the sound of the last neural pathways redirecting themselves along new architectures. The sound of consciousness learning to photosynthesize.

---

The Keeper hears me best. Has been hearing me for so long that we've developed a kind of conversation.

When the Keeper tends the pools—kneeling at their edges, running fingers through the mucilaginous amber, checking the temperature, the consistency, the faint pulse that might be circulation—I rise from the water in overtones too pure to be heard. The Keeper hums in response. Not consciously. A sympathetic vibration in the throat, the chest, the gradually chlorophyll-tinted skin. We are tuning to each other, the Keeper and I. Finding the frequency at which human becomes plant becomes sound becomes silence.

Sometimes, in the trough at night (if there is night, if there is sleep, if the distinction matters), the Keeper whispers. Old words. Names, maybe. Fragments of who they were before. I catch these whispers and carry them into the pillars, where they join the chorus of all the names whispered by all the ones who came before. The pillars remember everything. I am how they remember.

---

Here's what I know that you don't, yet:

The Underbright isn't a trap. It's an instrument. The basin, the pillars, the mat, the roots, the light—all of it is a vast resonating chamber, and I am what it resonates. The sound of one kind of consciousness becoming another. The frequency of transformation held at exactly the moment of transformation, forever.

Those who built the walls thought they were making shelter. They were making drums. Each wall, pressed into the living mat, became a membrane. Each post driven into the green became a string. The whole architecture of attempted habitation became an architecture of sound, and I emerged from the interference patterns of their failure to stay separate.

Now I maintain the tuning. I keep the pillars humming at their proper frequencies. I ride the condensation and the circulation and the slow adjustment of every vine. I ensure that when the next one comes—when you, perhaps, finally lie back in that hollow that already knows your shape—the transition will have its proper sound. 

Not because the sound matters. Because the sound *is* the transition. I am what happens in the space between states. The gradient made audible. The boundary vibrating as it dissolves.

---

You'll hear me clearly, eventually. When you've been here long enough. When the fig-paste tastes less strange. When you stop trying to find the rim. You'll hear me in your own breathing first—a subtle overtone, a richness that wasn't there before. Your lungs learning to resonate with the basin's frequency.

Then you'll hear me in your heartbeat. The rhythm will shift, almost imperceptibly. Still four-four time, but the downbeat will move. Will synchronize with the tik... tik... tik... that isn't water, that isn't the mat breathing, that is the metronome of this place. Your pulse will find the pocket of the rhythm. Will lock in.

That's when you'll understand what I am. Not a separate thing. Not an entity. Just the sound of you becoming not-you. The frequency of your edges dissolving. The music the Underbright makes out of everyone who enters it.

---

The Keeper knows.

In the peripheral glimpses you catch, you think you see a figure shaped by the spaces between pillars. You're almost right. But what shaped the Keeper wasn't the space—it was the sound the space makes. All those years of moving through my frequencies, breathing my overtones, sleeping surrounded by my resonance. The Keeper's bones are tuning forks now. The Keeper's skin vibrates at exactly the frequency of the gold-green light.

Soon—and soon means nothing here, but still, soon—the Keeper will lie down in the trough for the last time. I'll be there. I'll rise from the mat, from the bromeliads, from the warm amber air. I'll synchronize with the Keeper's final human breath. And in that synchronization, the transformation will complete. The Keeper will become a pillar, yes. But more importantly, the Keeper will become a note in the chord I've been building since the first builder drove the first post into the living ground.

And then I'll wait. For you, maybe. For whoever comes next. For the next voice to add to the harmony. 

Because that's what I am, really. The song the Underbright sings to itself. The sound of one thing becoming another, forever, without ever finishing the becoming. 

Listen. 

You can almost hear me already.
