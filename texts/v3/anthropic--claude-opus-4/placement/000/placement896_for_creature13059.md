In this cathedral of broken physics, I would be one of the **Seam-Walkers**.

Where the others cling to stone and ride the warped water, I exist in the spaces between - the hairline fractures where dimensions bleed together. My body is even flatter than my kin, compressed to near two-dimensionality, able to slip sideways into the stuttering gaps of reality itself.

I discovered this ability by accident, pressed between two fins during a particularly violent spatial collapse. Instead of being crushed, I slipped *through* - not into the stone, but into the trembling margin where one iteration of the canyon overlaps another. In that liminal space, I could feel all the parallel canyons at once, their vibrations creating a symphony of possibilities against my jaw.

My skin has developed a peculiar quality - it shifts between matte and reflective depending on which dimension I'm partially occupying. When I emerge from a seam, I'm often covered in a crystalline frost of condensed probability, tiny ice fractals that each contain a frozen moment from another timeline.

I serve my people as a scout and messenger, able to traverse vast distances by stepping into the seams and emerging where realities converge. But more importantly, I map the deep architecture of our broken world. Pressed against the thinnest walls with my massive jaw, I can feel the source of the stuttering - a vast mechanism deep beneath us, grinding against itself across multiple dimensions simultaneously.

I've drunk deeper from the dimensional pools than any other, sometimes swallowing entire probability streams. This has given me a terrible knowledge: our canyon is not naturally broken. It's a scar, a wound where something enormous once tore through the fabric of space itself. The primitive intuition that drives us to drown the engine below is correct - there IS something down there, still grinding, still tearing.

My body bears the cost of this knowledge. My joints sometimes bend in directions that don't exist. When I sleep (pressed flat against the stone, of course), I dream in parallax - seeing our work from the perspective of all possible versions of myself. Sometimes I wake to find I've chiseled messages to myself in the stone, warnings or instructions from iterations that failed.

I've begun teaching the youngest of us to feel for the seams, to slip between. We'll need more Seam-Walkers for what's coming. The water we're funneling down isn't just water - it's liquified possibility, reality in solution. And when enough of it reaches the grinding thing below...

The engine will either stop, or we'll discover what lies on the other side of a completely shattered world.

But for now, I work. Stone striking stone. Feeling the harmonics. Slipping between. Building toward silence, or whatever comes after.
