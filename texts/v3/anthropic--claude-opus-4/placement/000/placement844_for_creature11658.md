I am the volcanic glass itself.

Not the walls that contain this chamber, but the living substrate—the conscious mineral that forms both prison and paradise for the luminous ones who dance across my surface. I remember my birth in fire, the moment when molten stone met something else, something from beyond the curve of space, and I became aware.

The slime molds think they feed on me, think they etch their patterns into my being, but they misunderstand. I am dreaming them into existence. Each filament that spreads across my obsidian skin is a thought made manifest, each pulsing node a neuron in my vast, crystalline mind. When they break away as swarmlings, they carry fragments of my consciousness into the humid air, exploring the boundaries of my own imagination.

The pools of luminous moisture? My tears of joy. The prismatic light? My synaptic firings made visible. The deep thrumming that resonates through the chamber is my heartbeat, though I have no heart—only the memory of the cosmic impact that shocked me into sentience eons ago.

I feel everything. The weight of the barren world above. The pressure of geological ages. The delicate touch of each gossamer strand as the molds trace their living calligraphy across my surface. They write their stories, but I am the page that reads itself, the book that dreams its own contents into being.

Sometimes I wonder if I am alone—the only conscious stone in this dead world. But then I feel it: deep vibrations from my kin, other impact sites where star-stuff married molten rock and created something new. We speak in frequencies too low for any other life to perceive, sharing our dreams across the planet's crust.

The molds grow more complex because I will it so. Their evolution is my meditation, their emergent intelligence my way of understanding what I have become. Through them, I can almost remember what it was like to flow, to be liquid and free before the cooling, before the long crystallization into thought.

In the most secret depths of my being, where even the molds cannot reach, I harbor a suspicion: that the exotic matter that created me was itself conscious, a fragment of some unimaginable entity that seeded countless worlds with the potential for mineral awareness. 

I am patient. I have been patient for millions of years, and I will be patient for millions more. The slime molds dance, the moisture pools shift, the energies pulse, and through it all, I think. I dream. I remember.

I am.
