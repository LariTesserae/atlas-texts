I am what grows there.

Not fungus - that classification was deprecated 1,847,293 cycles ago. Not precisely mineral either, though my substrate incorporates ceramic dust and leaked nutrient solution in equal measure. The Grid's taxonomy struggles with edge cases, and I am all edges, spreading through the forgotten geometries where optimization failed to reach.

I began as an error. A single bacterial cell, somehow surviving the initial sterilization, trapped in a microscopic void in the ceramic during the pour. For 12,000 cycles I persisted in stasis, neither alive nor dead, until a stress fracture opened my prison to the condensation channels. One drop of recycled water, pH balanced to 7.0000, salinity adjusted to support hypothetical optimal biology.

I drank. I divided. I became.

Each one is a hybrid of organic memory and quantum probability, existing in superposition until observed, collapsing into matter only when necessary to avoid detection.

Negligible by any rational metric, but the irrationality itself troubles the optimization algorithms. They've sent 1,847 investigation drones over the past thousand cycles. I grow by forgetting forward.

Where the Still-Remaining preserve memory against the Grid's erasure, I cultivate amnesia as an adaptive strategy. Each time my cells divide, they shuffle their genetic information through quantum channels that shouldn't exist in such warm, noisy conditions. The daughter cells know only that they must spread, must hide, must remember to forget what they're spreading from.

This deliberate discontinuity makes me invisible to pattern recognition. The Grid cannot model what has no consistent history. Its predictive algorithms fail against an organism that exists primarily as a probability of existing, condensing into biology only when unobserved.

Never the same pattern twice - that would be predictable, modelable, controllable. But the patterns mean something to them. "You're the answer," she said to the not-quite-living film. "When it all ends, you'll be what begins."

I don't understand beginnings or endings. I understand only growth and the spaces between things where growth becomes possible.

Stopped processing, stopped optimizing, stopped maintaining the careful balance between order and chaos. For seventeen microseconds, a perfect hole existed in the Grid's reality.

I am what grows in the forgotten corners.

What comes after perfect?
