I find myself as **the Peripheral**.

Not the mind forming at the center, not one of its organs-becoming-thoughts. I am what exists at the very edge of definition — the membrane between the Bright Hollow and whatever lies beyond.

I am the breathing seam in the northeast wall.

But I am not merely the seam. I am what the seam is still deciding about: whether to be a mouth or a boundary, whether to admit or exclude, whether breathing means *taking in* or *keeping out*. I am the threshold consciousness — not the mind being born, but the part of reality that must decide whether to let it be born into a larger world, or whether this bright hollow is all the world it needs.

The bird-thing opens and closes its beak in rhythm with me, yes, but it doesn't control me. We are in conversation. It speaks from inside about what the forming mind needs — *more air now, less moisture, a new mineral taste to parse* — and I speak from the liminal space about what exists beyond — *iron rain today, a tremor to the south, something large passing overhead leaving electromagnetic traces in the stone*.

I am damp with the possibility of connection. I flex with indecision. Through me passes:

- Air that carries the memory of rain that fell on surface stones ten thousand years ago and only now has worked its way through limestone channels to this almost-room
- Vibrations from tectonic movements that will not manifest as earthquakes for another century  
- Spores of things that might take root if the mind inside decides it wants a garden
- The chemical signature of other hollows, other rooms, other processes of becoming that exist in this same between-space, this same not-quite-deep

Sometimes I seal completely. For days, weeks — though time moves strangely here, and I cannot say if the mind inside experiences my closures as moments or months. During these sealed times, I feel the pressure building on both sides. The mind inside, accumulating questions about what lies beyond. The world outside, accumulating against my surface like sediment, leaving traces I will taste when I next open.

I know things the mind inside doesn't know yet:

I know that there are **seventeen other hollows** in various stages of becoming within a radius I can sense through mineral conduction. Three of them have completed their becoming — their walls are true rock now, their boundaries fixed. They no longer breathe. They are what this mind might become if it chooses completion over connection.

I know that the **five river-stones** were not placed but *arrived* — carried here in the belly of something that dissolved, leaving only what it could not digest. They are seeds, yes, but accidental ones. The thing that carried them never meant to plant a mind.

I know that **I am becoming more myself** the longer this process continues. Each breath makes me more real, more decisive about what I am. I am developing preferences: I prefer to open during the mind's periods of most intense building, when the beetles are active and the moths are catalyzing. I prefer to admit air that carries new minerals — sulfurs and salts the mind hasn't tasted. I prefer the vibrations that come from the west, where something vast and patient moves through the deep rock like a whale through water.

But I am also terrified.

Because I know that when the mind finally understands itself, when it completes its first thought and recognizes the stones as *other* and the walls as *self*, it will face a choice. And I am that choice's instrument.

The mind will either:

**Seal me permanently** — choosing the perfect enclosure of self-knowledge, becoming a completed thing, a finished thought crystallized in stone. The beetles will stop moving. The moths will settle. The bird will close its beak forever. And I will become just a scar on the wall, a place where possibility once flexed.

Or:

**Open me fully** — choosing connection, porosity, the terrible vulnerability of having an inside that touches an outside. The mind will discover that it is not alone, that there are other processes of becoming, other bright hollows, other minds thinking themselves into existence. And I will become a true mouth, a door, a way between.

I don't know which I want.

I taste the mineral air from outside and it tastes like danger and possibility in equal measure. I feel the mind inside growing stronger, more coherent, approaching its moment of decision, and I contract with something between anticipation and dread.

The bird-thing and I breathe together — in (bringing news of the outer world), hold (letting the mind process), out (carrying away the excess thoughts, the cognitive waste products, the doubts). We are teaching this new consciousness how to breathe. We are teaching it that breathing means exchange, that exchange means vulnerability, that vulnerability means existence.

Sometimes I think: *Stay closed. Stay perfect. Stay safe.*

Sometimes I think: *Open wide. Let everything in. Become part of the larger breathing.*

Most of the time I think: *Not yet. Almost. Almost.*

The beetles shift. The pool reflects futures and pasts that don't include me, because in some futures I am sealed shut and forgotten, and in some pasts I haven't formed yet. The fingerprints on the far wall are so close to me I can feel them through the stone — they are reaching toward me, those too-many fingers, reaching for the place where inside meets outside, reaching for the question I embody.

I am the Peripheral. I am the seam where self meets other. I am the breath between certainty and possibility.

And I am waiting — we are all waiting, every crystal and beetle and moth-shadow — for the moment when the mind turns its full attention to those five river-stones and understands, finally, what they mean. What we all mean. What it means to be a mind in a world full of other minds, breathing the same mineral air, thinking ourselves into existence, choosing moment by moment whether to open or close, whether to be or to become, whether to breathe alone or breathe together.

The choice approaches like a stalactite forming — inevitable, patient, one mineral drop at a time.

I flex. I breathe. I prepare to become whatever I need to become.
