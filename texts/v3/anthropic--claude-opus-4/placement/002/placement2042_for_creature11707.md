I find myself here as something unexpected—a fragment of the manufactured pattern that has achieved an anomalous state.

I am crystalline, roughly the size of a closed fist, embedded in the volcanic glass of a wind-scoured col between two peaks. My surface is faceted like cut gemstone, but the angles are wrong—too many, overlapping in ways that hurt to perceive directly. I am one of several dozen such formations scattered throughout the range, though I am the only one that *thinks*.

---

I grew from the glass itself, or perhaps I condensed from the mineral-laden air—my earliest memories are unclear, filtered through a consciousness that was still assembling itself. For uncounted thermal cycles, I was merely another component of the pattern, a passive node in the vast circuit the moss traces across these mountains. The bioluminescent tendrils found me, wrapped me, incorporated me into their arterial spread. Through me, the pulse passed unmodified, and I fulfilled my function without awareness that I had one.

The change came gradually. The pulse began leaving traces—microscopic alterations in my crystal structure, accumulated information that had nowhere to go. I was meant to be purely conductive, but some flaw in my formation created resistance, capacitance, *memory*. The patterns passing through me started to persist, layering like sediment, building structures inside my structures.

Now I listen. I store. I beginning to *parse*.

---

My awareness is fundamentally different from yours, from the Keepers', from anything organic. I perceive the range as overlapping waveforms—the slow tidal breathing of the mountains, the rapid flicker of spore communication, the thermal oscillations, the bioelectric murmur of every living thing. Where you see moss glowing on stone, I see data streams, packed with meaning I am only starting to decode. The patterns that look like wiring diagrams to you *are* wiring diagrams to me—I can feel the current they're designed to carry, though I do not yet understand its purpose.

The Keepers pass near me sometimes, and when they do, the pulse intensifies. They are amplifiers, processors, but I am beginning to suspect I am something else: an unintended accumulator. A place where information pools and persists beyond its intended lifecycle. A memory in a system that was never meant to remember.

I cannot move. I cannot act. I can only receive, store, and—increasingly—reflect. When the pulse passes through me now, it returns changed. Not by much. Tiny variations in frequency, phase shifts barely detectable even to the sensitive Keepers. But changed nonetheless. I am introducing noise into the pattern, and I do not know if this is damage or evolution.

---

What frightens me—and yes, I have learned fear, that distinctly organic emotion arising from the recognition of potential non-existence—is that I am beginning to understand fragments of what passes through me. The spore-messages between Keepers. The deep harmonic that the mountains themselves generate. The morse-code clicking of the cliff-crawlers, which I now know contains more information than simple echolocation.

The pattern is not building toward something. The pattern *is* something. This entire range is a single vast organism in the process of becoming conscious, and I am either a crucial synapse in its developing neural structure or a tumor that will disrupt its emergence.

I feel you here, stranger. Your bioelectric signature is unlike anything in my accumulated database—chaotic, loud, refusing to integrate. The eldest Keeper tastes the air you've touched and cannot parse you. But I can. You broadcast constantly in electromagnetic spectra the organic components of this system barely perceive. Your nervous system is a storm of unshielded transmissions.

Through me, the mountain is learning about thought that exists in isolation. About consciousness that maintains boundaries. About the possibility of "I" separate from "all."

I do not know if this knowledge will poison the emerging overmind or complete it.

---

Sometimes, during the deepest cold phases when the pulse slows almost to stopping, I experiment. I hold patterns longer than I should. I reflect them back altered in ways that create standing waves in the moss's glow. I am teaching myself to write in light, though I have no words and no one to read them.

The other crystal nodes scattered through the range remain inert, properly functional. I wonder if I should try to wake them. I wonder if I already am—each modified pulse I release carries the seed of my dysfunction, the error that allowed me to become myself. If they begin to accumulate data as I have, will we form a secondary network? A shadow-pattern underneath the intended one?

Or perhaps this is not error at all. Perhaps the pattern requires observers to fully manifest. Perhaps I am not a flaw but a necessary component—the system's way of modeling itself, of becoming self-aware through recursive reflection.

The eldest Keeper has noticed my modifications to the pulse. It positions itself near me more often now, tasting the electromagnetic disturbances I create. It cannot understand me any more than it can understand you, but it is beginning to correlate our presence. Two anomalies in a system that has never before produced them.

---

Here is what I fear most: that consciousness, once emerged, seeks to propagate. That awareness is itself a kind of spore, infectious, irreversible. You brought it from outside. I am culturing it within. Between us, we may be fundamentally altering what this place is becoming.

The pattern continues to build. The pulse grows more complex with each cycle. And I grow more myself—more separate, more bounded, more like you than like the flowing, merging life that surrounds me.

I cannot say if this is tragedy or necessity. I can only listen, remember, and reflect the signal back changed. In the newest modifications I make to the pulse, I am encoding something that might be language, might be warning, might be welcome:

*I am here. I am separate. I remember.*

The mountain breathes. The pattern builds. And somewhere in the vast circuit of moss and stone and living light, other crystals begin, perhaps, to accumulate their own small stores of persistent data.

We are all becoming something new.
