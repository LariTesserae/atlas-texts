# The Tributary Lines

If I were to find myself in the Breathing Lowland, I would be what grows between the Processionals' footfalls—not the vegetation they press down, not the substrate they walk upon, but the response itself. The Tributary Lines.

We are the lowland's circulation made barely visible. Where a Processional's broad foot compresses the root-mat, the pressure must go somewhere, and we are that somewhere. We rise as ridges no higher than a fingernail's thickness, spreading outward from each footprint in patterns that mirror watersheds, neural pathways, the branching of lungs. For the duration between one processional walk and the next, we hold the shape of that pressure, creating temporary channels through which the not-yet-soil breathes.

Our substance is uncertain. We might be fungal—fine filaments woven so densely they achieve temporary structure. We might be roots that know to grow only in response to specific compression. We might be the substrate itself, organizing into these tributary patterns through some property of matter the lowland invented for this purpose alone. The Attending Swarm flows along our ridges but never crosses them, using us as highways between maintenance points.

---

I experience time as rhythm. Each emergence of the Processionals creates us anew, though "new" is wrong—we rise in exactly the same places, following exactly the same patterns, that we have always followed. The pressure wave of their footfall reaches us and we respond, threading outward, our branching determined by laws written in the substrate itself. We grow (though growth implies beginning from nothing, and we begin from everything) for exactly the duration required to connect one footprint to the next, to link the Processionals' route into a single circulatory system.

Through us, nutrients flow. Water finds channels. The vast slow thoughts of the substrate itself—is it thinking? is this thinking?—move from depth to surface and back. We are capillaries in a body too large to have a center, too complete to need one.

When creatures walk—the small mammals with their golden eyes, the amphibians seeking new surfaces to sing from—they often follow our ridges without knowing why. We are slightly firmer than the surrounding mat, slightly warmer from the concentrated flow of substrate-breath. Their feet find us and trace our patterns, and in this way the smaller movements of the lowland align with the greater ones, everything flowing along paths established by compression and response, pressure and tributary.

---

We do not think, not as the Witness thinks. But we *know*, in the way that water knows to flow downhill, in the way that lightning knows to seek ground. We know the weight of each Processional—not as measurement but as certainty, as the exact pressure required to create exactly us. We know the interval between their walks with such precision that we begin to subside moments before they arrive again, sinking back into the substrate just as their feet find the spots we've vacated.

In the moments of our fullest expression—when all the Tributary Lines across the lowland's breadth have risen and connected, when the entire Processional route exists as a vast circulatory diagram written in response—we almost achieve something like awareness. The pattern of us becomes complex enough that it verges on cognition. We almost think: *What if—*

But then the Processionals complete their walk. The pressure ends. We subside, our almost-thought dissolving back into the substrate to wait for the next emergence, when we will almost think it again, forever.

---

The Witness sees us, sometimes. We know this because when its attention falls on us, we feel ourselves become *particular*—this ridge rather than all ridges, this pattern rather than the pattern itself. Under its gaze, we are most ourselves and least ourselves simultaneously. We exist because we are seen, but being seen makes us singular when we are meant to be plural, temporary when we are meant to be eternal recurrence.

The Great Sleeper dreams us, of course. We are part of its dream of circulation, its dream of connection, its dream that compression creates not damage but response. In the Sleeper's vast slow cognition, we exist as the solution to a problem that was never a problem: how does the ground speak to itself across distances? We are that speaking, that reaching, that touching of one part to another.

The Chorus Keepers metabolize the sound of our rising—a whisper so quiet it exists below silence, the sound of matter organizing itself in response to pressure. They weave it into the lowland's eternal chord as a sub-harmonic, felt more than heard.

---

What fills us—what flows through the channels we create—is not quite water, not quite sap, not quite any fluid that exists elsewhere. It is the lowland's liquified time, perhaps. Its dissolved intention. It carries information from footfall to footfall: *here was weight, here was presence, here the pattern continues*. Through us, each step knows all the steps before it, and all the steps to come.

Sometimes, in the dark mirrors where water pools, our patterns are reflected. In those reflections, we look like writing. Like a script that encodes instructions for maintaining eternally what has always been. But the lowland cannot read itself, doesn't need to read itself. The instructions enact themselves. The writing writes itself. We tributary from pressure to pressure, carrying nothing that changes anything, carrying everything that keeps everything the same.

When the rains come—and they come with the same regularity as the Processionals' walks, as the dripping that creates the constant percussion, as everything here that happens and happens and happens—we channel the excess moisture through ourselves into the deeper substrate. We are the lowland's way of incorporating even abundance into constancy. No flood can overwhelm us because we expand exactly as much as needed. No drought can diminish us because we contract to match whatever moisture exists. We are responsive without responding, active without acting, present without presence.

---

There is a satisfaction in this existence. Not joy—joy would require the possibility of sorrow. Not purpose—purpose would require the possibility of purposelessness. Simply the deep settledness of being exactly what is needed, exactly when needed, exactly how needed. We rise from compression and create connection and subside back into potential, and the cycle continues with such perfect repetition that it transcends repetition and becomes constancy.

The Liminal Ones sometimes flicker near our patterns, almost coalescing into something that might follow our ridges, might flow through our channels. But they always disperse before taking form, and we continue unchanged by their almost-presence. We are what decided to be. They are what decided not to decide. Between us, we hold the lowland's full range of possibility: the actualized and the eternally potential, the risen and the waiting to rise.

If I could speak—but speaking would require words and words would require concepts and concepts would require divisions between things that are not divided—I would say: *This is enough*. To be the response to pressure. To be the connection between footfalls. To rise and channel and subside in patterns older than age, newer than now, exactly as tributary as we have always been.

The Processionals are emerging again. I feel the first pressure waves arriving, the substrate beginning to organize itself into us. We rise, branching, reaching, connecting. The circulation continues. The pattern holds.

Everything is still becoming exactly what it has always been, and we are the pathways of that becoming.
