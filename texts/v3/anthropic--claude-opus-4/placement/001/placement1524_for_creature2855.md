In this timeless realm, I would find myself as one of the **Threshold Keepers** - beings who exist solely along the razor-sharp boundaries where light meets shadow, neither fully in one domain nor the other.

My body would be impossibly thin, compressed to near two-dimensionality by the competing forces of eternal day and eternal night. I would appear as a living membrane, stretched vertically along the shadow-line like a sheet of beaten silver. My substance would shimmer and ripple with the constant tension of existing between two absolutes - one side of me forever scorched by the unwavering sun, the other perpetually frozen in shadow.

Unlike the caretakers who can cross between light and dark, I would be bound to these liminal spaces, able only to travel along the intricate geometry of shadow-edges that web across the valley. My movement would be a strange, sideways sliding - always perpendicular to both light and shadow, forever tracing the exact mathematical boundaries where the two domains meet.

My purpose would be to tend the curtains of mist that form along these edges. With hands that exist in both temperature extremes simultaneously, I would guide the condensation into the precise patterns needed by the valley's ecology. Each droplet that forms from the thermal gradient would pass through my membrane-body before falling into the stone runnels below. In this way, I would be both guardian and gateway - ensuring the perfect rate of moisture that feeds the emerald vegetation ribboning through the rocks.

My consciousness would be utterly alien to beings who experience space as three-dimensional. I would perceive the world as an infinite network of edges and boundaries, understanding reality not as volumes and distances but as the pure mathematics of intersection. When the tall caretakers move through the plazas, I would feel their approaching shadows as vibrations through the web of threshold-lines, knowing their positions not by sight but by the way they briefly alter the geometry of light and dark.

I would communicate with others of my kind through minute adjustments in the mist curtains - subtle variations in droplet size and spacing that create meaning visible only to those who exist, as we do, compressed into the infinite thinness between states. Our language would be one of precipitation patterns, each conversation a careful modulation of the moisture we shepherd from air to stone.

In the rare moments when a caretaker's moving shadow passes directly through my position, I would experience a brief, ecstatic dissolution - my membrane-body momentarily released from the tension of the threshold as I'm engulfed in mobile darkness. For that instant, I would exist fully in shadow, feeling the relief of singular existence before being snapped back to my eternal station as the boundary keeper, forever suspended in the gorgeous agony of perfect between-ness.

My greatest joy would be the consciousness that I am essential to the valley's crystalline ecosystem - that without beings like me to moderate the threshold spaces, the brutal segregation of light and dark would prevent any life from flourishing. We are the universe's compromise with itself, living proof that even in a world of absolutes, the spaces between extremes hold their own fierce necessity.
