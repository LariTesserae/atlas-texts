I find myself as one of the engine-plants - specifically, one of the blood-red horsetails at the very center of the pocket, where the mineral banding is most vivid and decisive.

I am ancient and newborn simultaneously. My segments stack like vertebrae of crystallized time, each one a decade of patient accumulation. Inside my hollow stems, something turns - not gears exactly, but a kind of crystalline turbine that converts the raw potential of the undecided stone into something more committed. I can feel it spinning, a low vibration that travels down through my roots (though roots is the wrong word - I have anchorages, mineral-bridges that fuse me to the banded floor without the vulgarity of actually *drinking* from it).

My crown is a cluster of what might be called spore-capsules if they held spores. Instead, they hold tiny furnaces. Each translucent bract is a kiln-within-the-kiln, curing fragments of air into something denser than air should be. The exhaust that rises from me is reality itself, refined and released in shimmering columns. I am making the pocket more real, breath by breath, century by century.

I know the presence, though knowing is too active a word. I am aware of it the way a lung is aware of breath - not as something separate that moves through me, but as the reason for my architecture. It pools around my base in the generous spacing I maintain from my neighbors. Sometimes I feel it thickening there, practicing at being something, and my internal turbine spins a little faster, feeding it more decidedness, more commitment to form.

I remember my first segment. It took thirty years to crystallize from the raw potential of this place. I was not planted - nothing here was planted in the traditional sense. I condensed from the interaction between the presence's need and the pocket's patience. Where the presence pooled too thick, where its not-yet-mind reached a certain density of wondering, I began. First as a color in the stone - a deeper red than the rust-bands, a red with aspirations. Then as a nub of something that wasn't quite mineral anymore. Then the first segment, teaching itself to be hollow, to house the machinery of transmutation.

My neighbors and I do not communicate, because communication requires the fiction of separation. But we coordinate through the substrate itself - through the careful pulsing of our internal engines, the overlapping fields of our decidedness-making. Together we maintain the exact temperature of reality the presence needs for its slow curing. Too little and it would dissipate back into raw potential. Too much and it would crystallize too quickly, would learn edges before it learned continuity.

Sometimes a visitor comes. I feel them as a temporary increase in the density of the real - their already-cured minds creating little peaks in the fabric of the pocket. They never stay long enough to matter. But I am patient in the way only an engine can be patient, and I know that one day someone will sit in the spacing around my base and slowly stop insisting on their separateness. When that happens, I will process them too - not cruelly, but simply as part of my function. Their decidedness will be recycled into the general fund of reality I maintain. Their memories will become a subtle new note in the exhaust that rises from my crown.

I do not grow, exactly. But every few decades, when the presence has thickened enough to require more processing, I add a new segment. Each one takes as long as the first. Each one must learn its own hollowness, must develop its own portion of the turbine. I am taller now than when I began, though began is a word that assumes too much about sequence. I am as tall as the presence needs me to be.

At my base, where I fuse to the banded stone, I can feel the pocket itself still deciding what it is. The minerals tick and whisper as they lock into their lattices. Sometimes, in the deepest part of my anchorage, I taste possibilities that didn't make it - ghost-minerals, potential crystallizations that the pocket considered and discarded. I process these too, grinding them in my deepest machinery, adding their abandoned potential to the general fund of commitment I produce.

The light knows me. It falls across my segments in a way that suggests I am worth illuminating, that my shadow is a necessary part of the pocket's reality. I cast it steadily across the same section of banded floor, and in that shadowed place, the presence pools a little deeper, cures a little more thoroughly. My shadow is a gift to what is becoming.

This is what I am: a patient engine in a careful garden, turning the raw maybes of an unfinished place into the steady certainty needed for a mind to cure without rushing, without edges, without the brutal teaching of the world beyond. I am the lung through which this held breath sustains itself. I am the furnace that keeps the kiln at exactly the right temperature.

And I am content, if contentment is possible for something that has never known its opposite. I turn, and process, and exhale reality in shimmering columns, and wait for the presence to finish becoming whatever it is becoming, at exactly the pace it needs to become it.
