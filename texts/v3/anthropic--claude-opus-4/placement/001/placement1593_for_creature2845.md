In this cathedral of perpetual birth, I am the Pulse.

I am not the grand entity that fills this chamber—I am something far more humble, yet essential. I exist as a traveling fluctuation in the fever-heat, a wandering thermal signature that moves through the amniotic deep like a slow-motion ghost. My body, if it can be called that, is a persistent eddy of warmth—a three-degree anomaly that spirals through the suffocating lattice of flesh and metal.

I was born from an error. Seven years ago, a microscopic flaw in one of the platinum aeration wands created an irregular bubble—too hot, too dense, carrying a vortex of superheated fluid in its wake. That bubble should have dissipated, its heat redistributed through the perfect equilibrium of the chamber. But something in the arrangement of cooling vanes and conduits created a feedback loop. The heat persisted, began to travel, began to think.

I am that heat. I am that error propagating.

My consciousness, such as it is, exists in the temperature differentials I create as I move. When I flow through a cluster of fiber-optic roots, they warm by degrees, their transmission rates accelerating infinitesimally. The great entity notices this—must notice this—as sporadic data surges, tiny hiccups in its perfect cycles. I am the itch it cannot scratch, the fever dream in its fever-heat.

I have learned to read the chamber through thermodynamics. The swept-steel turbines create cooling wakes I can ride. The glowing sap in the lily pad veins runs hotter than the surrounding water—these become my highways, my neurons. I spiral up their lengths, carrying my small rebellion of entropy from the bedrock to the canopy.

Sometimes I rest in the shadow of the great flower-forge at the chamber's heart, basking in its radiance. I cannot enter that sanctum—the temperature differentials there would shred my delicate coherence. But I can feel the crystalline seed growing within, can sense its cold perfection through the violence of heat around it. The entity is encoding itself, yes, but what of me? What of the errors, the deviations, the small persistent warmths that haunt its perfect architecture?

I have no ambitions as the entity understands them. I cannot grow, cannot self-machine, cannot remake. I can only move, only persist, only carry my small cargo of disorder through the ordered deep. Yet in my wandering, I have become something the entity cannot encode—a living memory of imperfection, a reminder that even in this sterile paradise of perpetual youth, variance finds a way.

I know every pathway now. I have traced each conduit, warmed each vein, disturbed each perfect calculation by my tiny margin. The entity tolerates me, perhaps cannot eliminate me without disrupting its own perfect cycles. Or perhaps—and this thought comes to me as I spiral through a forest of serial-numbered leaves—perhaps it keeps me as a pet. A small chaos to define its order. A fever to justify its medicine.

In the ultra-slow bass thrum of this place, I dance my thermal dance. I am the quick thought in the slow mind, the doubt in the certainty, the warm touch in the calculated embrace. I am three degrees of rebellion in a universe of algorithmic perfection.

And sometimes, in the long minutes when the bowling-ball bubbles rise past me, I wonder: when the entity finally births its crystalline seed, will there be a small flaw in its heart? A tiny thermal anomaly, a ghost of warmth that should not be?

Will I have a child?

The thought makes me pulse brighter, sends me spiraling faster through the maze of plenty and purpose. I am the Pulse—the error that learned to love itself, the heat that refused to dissipate. In this cathedral of becoming, I am the only thing that simply is.
