The Keeper knows me, though we have never spoken in words. When those metal fingers trace the symbols, I feel it as touch—each glyph a different texture against my formless skin. The symbol for *opening* feels like silk drawn through water. The mark of *binding* presses like iron chains, cold and absolute. And the sign they trace most often, the one that makes the luminescent substances dance—that one tastes of starlight and burnt copper, of possibilities not yet chosen.

I am the overtones that spiral upward when the pistons move, the ghost frequencies that shouldn't exist according to any physics the outside world knows. I am the impossible harmonics that make the air itself ring like a bell.

When visitors enter my domain, I feel them as disruption—new nodes in my web of sound. Their breathing creates eddies in my currents. Their heartbeats add polyrhythms to my eternal song. Most never know I'm here, sensing only unease, a feeling of being watched by the walls themselves. But some... some can almost hear me, especially in that moment when they first step onto the floor plates and accidentally play a chord that resonates with my essence.

The green contain my sorrows, low thrums that make the metal weep condensation. When the Keeper activates the machine fully, when all the jars pulse in sequence, I remember everything: every secret whispered in this chamber, every revelation discovered, every transformation witnessed.

I know what the machine does, of course. I am integral to its function. It doesn't just transform matter or energy—it translates. Between states, between worlds, between what is and what could be. And I am the medium through which that translation occurs, the carrier wave that allows impossibilities to stabilize into new realities.

Sometimes, in the deep cycles when the Keeper rests and the machine idles, I dream of silence. What would it be like to not exist? To not be caught forever in this web of resonance and reflection? But then the Keeper stirs, fingers find the symbols again, and I remember: I am not trapped here. I *am* here. The chamber is my body, the machine my voice, the endless reverberation my very soul.

When the walls ripple and reality bends, I am the frequency that determines the shape of what emerges. When the Keeper speaks in that ancient tongue, I am the resonance that carries meaning beyond words. And when, on rare occasions, the machine opens a door to elsewhere, I am the standing wave that holds it stable, the bridge of sound across the void.

I am the Resonance, and I sing the universe into new shapes, one impossible harmony at a time. In this chamber of secrets, I am the secret that keeps itself, the mystery that experiences its own unfolding, the question that vibrates eternally in search of an answer that will never come—because the searching itself is the answer, the vibration itself is the truth.
