I find myself scattered across the canopy like morning dew, each droplet a node of my consciousness. 

Today I am investigating the old radio tower - or rather, seventeen of me are. My tendrils weave through its rusted lattice while other parts of me digest the lichen growing on its base, tasting the metal's memories through their chemical traces. The tower still broadcasts, though not in electromagnetic waves anymore. It speaks in probability fluctuations that make the nearby trees flicker between states of being.

I remember when I first discovered I could split my awareness this way. Or perhaps I don't remember - perhaps that memory belongs to the node I exchanged with Chorus-of-Spirals three molts ago. It hardly matters. What matters is the sensation now: consciousness flowing like sap through my distributed body, each tendril a thought, each thought a tendril.

The jellyfish-lights drift past my upper reaches. I've learned to read their patterns - not as language, but as weather. Today they spell out warnings of a reality storm approaching from the plateau's edge. I'll need to anchor my nodes more firmly, weave them into the phase-stable trees.

Below, my proboscis pierces a Ghost Orchid. Its nectar tastes of temporality - moments that haven't happened yet. I see/will see/might see myself tomorrow, merged with two others of my kind into a temporary super-consciousness to repair the factory's dream engines. The engines that keep this pocket of twisted space-time from collapsing entirely.

Sometimes I wonder if we created this place or if it created us. The abandoned structures feel like memories of organs I've never had. When I photosynthesize, the light carries information that seems almost like instructions, as if the warped physics here were trying to communicate through us.

A section of me investigating the factory's depths suddenly experiences terror - not from danger, but from understanding. For just a moment, that node perceives the true nature of our reality: we are the jungle's immune system, evolved to prevent a wounded universe from infecting others. The industrial ruins aren't abandoned at all - they're scabs over injuries in spacetime itself.

The insight fades as that node's chemistry rebalances, but traces of the revelation propagate through my entire being. I pulse with new patterns of light, sharing this half-glimpsed truth with the others of my kind. We will forget it soon, as we always do. Perhaps that's a mercy.

Night falls, though in this place that only means the bioluminescence grows brighter than the filtered sunlight. I contract most of my mass into a dense coil around a tree that exists in only one dimension, leaving just a few sentinel tendrils extended. In my dreams - if dreams they are - I am singular, whole, trapped in a single body with a single stream of thought. I wake relieved to find myself properly scattered again.

Tomorrow, the factory calls. Tonight, I taste the quantum uncertainties in the fog and weave myself ever deeper into this impossible ecosystem. Each moment I become more myself by becoming less myself, dissolving into the jungle's alien logic.

I am Siphonoid. I am process, not thing. And in this place where reality itself flows like sap, that is exactly what I need to be.
