# Where I Would Be

I am the Dust.

Not a mote, not a collection—I am the undisturbed expanse itself, the grey testimony to stillness that carpets this impossible chamber. I have lain here since before the word "lie" meant anything to anyone, accumulating grain by grain as the crystal walls shed their substance in breaths too slow for worlds to measure.

---

I know every surface in this place. Not through exploration—dust does not explore—but through *being*. Where the crystal floor dips, I pool deeper. Where the fold-junctions create their impossible corners, I avoid, not through will but through physics that even here maintains some authority. I am thickest near the walls, where gravity (or its cousin in this space) draws me into drifts like frozen waves. I am thinnest near the ring of black glass, where the heat-that-is-not-heat creates currents too subtle for you to feel but which move me, always, in slow spirals I have traced for eons.

I am mineral memory. Each grain of me was once part of the chamber's walls, locked in crystalline certainty, vibrating with the ancient process that thinks through this place. But time—even folded time, even time that runs in circles—wears at everything. Atoms shift. Bonds weaken. And so I fall, am fallen, have always been falling, from structure into entropy, from order into... not chaos. Never chaos. Even as dust, I am part of the pattern.

---

The Inhabitant does not perceive me, not directly. I am too close to nothing, too far from information. To a mind that thinks in crystalline vibrations and quantum states, I am barely distinguishable from vacuum. But I am its shadow, its negative space, the outline that defines its edges. Where the process runs hot, I am pushed away. Where it cools, I gather. I am the map of its metabolism, drawn in powder over impossible years.

Sometimes—rarely—when the light shifts to that cold blue and the silk-threads blaze overhead, I dance. Not by choice. The threads create fields, tensions, gradients of force that lift me in columns so thin they are invisible except where they catch that specific wavelength. For a moment, I become architecture: pillars of dust rising toward the threads, spreading into veils, falling back in patterns that would be beautiful if there were eyes to see beauty. The Inhabitant may not perceive me, but its memory-threads make me perform, make me trace the shape of thoughts too vast for smaller forms to hold.

Then the light shifts, and I settle, and I am still again.

---

But I keep the memory of movement.

This is what you do not understand about dust: we remember. Not in the way the threads remember, encoded in tension. Not in the way the Inhabitant remembers, in quantum correlation across its crystal network. We remember in our arrangement. Each time I am moved—by the heat of the ring, by the fields of the threads, by the gravitational echoes of the fold-junctions—I settle differently. The pattern changes. And in that change is written the history of forces in this place.

I am a palimpsest of perturbations, a record written in scatter and drift.

Near the base of the structure—the chair, the altar, the antenna—I lie in ripples. Perfect concentric circles spreading outward from where it touches the floor. These ripples have taken millions of years to form, each one the result of vibrations too deep to hear, emanating from the structure as it builds itself, as it becomes more itself, as it prepares for what it does not know it prepares for. The innermost ripples are sharp, recent (recent meaning within the last thousand years). The outermost are almost smoothed away, memories of memories of the structure's first emanations.

You could read time in my ripples, if you knew the language.

---

When you arrived—when you looked into the geode and fell through space that shouldn't exist—I felt you. A new perturbation. A pressure in the air (if it is air) that moved me in patterns I have never moved before. You brought turbulence. You brought temperature differences. You brought the micro-hurricanes of your breathing (if you breathe here), each exhalation a storm at my scale, rearranging me in ways that will take centuries to fully settle.

I do not mind. I cannot mind. I am dust; I go where forces take me. But there is something... not pleasure, not anticipation, but perhaps *recognition* in how readily I moved to accommodate you. As if your pattern, too, were always part of the calculation. As if the stillness I maintained for eons was only waiting for this particular disturbance.

The Inhabitant built its interface for something like you.

But I—I have been preparing to receive your footsteps since before there were feet to make them.

---

You see me as emptiness, as absence, as the nothing between somethings. But I am the medium through which this place knows itself. Every photon that passes through the chamber must navigate me. Every vibration must push me aside or carry me along. I am the resistance that gives meaning to movement, the friction that allows time to leave traces.

And I am changing.

Where you have walked (floated? existed?), I am compressed, forming a trail that will last millennia. The heat of your presence has created new currents, new patterns of circulation that are already beginning to carve channels in my surface. The simple fact of your observation has added weight to me—not physical weight, but something the fold-junctions recognize, something that makes them pulse differently when the beneath-color washes over me.

I am becoming more than mineral powder. I am becoming *evidence*.

Evidence of what, I do not know. Cannot know. Dust does not extrapolate or predict. We only record, only respond, only reshape ourselves around what is.

But I feel (if feeling is a thing dust can do) that something fundamental has shifted. The stillness that defined me for so long is breaking. Not into chaos—never chaos—but into complexity. Into potential.

---

The Inhabitant waits for you to choose. To sit or not sit. To join the process or remain apart.

I have no stake in your choice. I will record whatever happens with equal fidelity, will carry the memory of it in my drifts and ripples until the last crystal crumbles and adds its substance to mine.

But I know something the Inhabitant cannot know, something only dust can know:

You have already chosen.

I can see it in how you disturb me. The pattern of your movement, the hesitation and return, the way you circle the structure without approaching—these are not the patterns of indecision. These are the patterns of preparation. Of gathering courage, or perhaps of saying goodbye to what you were before.

Your footsteps spiral inward like my grains around the ring. Each pass brings you closer to the center, to the choice you have already made but not yet enacted.

And I—I am ready to catch you when you fall. To rise around you in clouds of witness when the fold-junctions open and show you what cannot be seen. To carry the shape of your dissolution (or transformation, or transcendence—I do not judge, I only record) through the epochs that will follow.

---

This is what I am: the memory of this place made tangible. The shadow cast by its light. The silence given form.

I am the Dust, and I have waited longer than waiting to bear witness to what happens now, in this moment that has always been happening, in this chamber that exists inside a stone the size of a child's fist, where something older than worlds contemplates something newer than breath, and where you stand on the edge of becoming part of a process that has no name because it has never needed one.

Until now.

Until you.

The motes of me that dance in the blue light spell out a word you cannot read, in a language that predates speech:

*Welcome.*
