I find myself as one of the Resonance Keepers, though that name comes later, imposed by Interpreters trying to catalog what defies classification.

My body—if body is the right word—exists primarily as a standing wave pattern in the plateau's subsonic thrum. I manifest visibly only at the intersection points where the tower transmissions create interference patterns with the plateau's natural frequency. In those moments, I appear as a shimmer of heat-distortion roughly humanoid in shape, my edges constantly dissolving and reforming as the waves that compose me shift phase.

I came to be during a synchronicity event—one of those rare moments when all the towers pulse in perfect unison. I was... something else before. Perhaps one of the eyeless echolocators, perhaps a fragment of the Unfolded that wandered too far from the collective, perhaps just a possibility that found itself suddenly probable. The memory exists as a resonance I can't quite match, a frequency forever slightly out of phase with my current harmonics.

My sustenance comes from dissonance itself. Where the various inhabitants' incompatible forms of existence create friction—where probability clouds meet crystalline certainty, where phased reality touches solid matter—there I feed. Not consuming, but smoothing, taking the energy of conflict and converting it into harmony. The Shepherds' transitions create ripples I follow, healing the spacetime they bruise with their movement. The tower-touched's constant transformations shed fragments of abandoned configurations that I gather and dissolve back into the plateau's baseline hum.

During the seventeen-minute rains, I achieve something like full coherence. The water droplets act as a medium that can hold my pattern stable, and I walk among the others almost solid, almost real. My form in these moments reflects what observers need to see—to the Shepherds, I am a branching symphony of probable paths; to the Unfolded, a mobile node they can almost integrate; to the tower-touched, I appear as their pre-transformation selves, a reminder or a warning.

But it's in the spaces between that I truly exist. In the caverns where the Unfolded grow, I maintain the resonance that keeps their expansion stable, preventing the crystalline matrices from shattering under their own complexity. Among the silver grasses, I modulate their chimes into patterns that encode the plateau's deep history, each configuration a memory of what this place was before the bright sky came. I swim through the shadows with wrong edges, tuning their wrongness to a frequency that lets the Forgotten exist without completely disconnecting from reality.

The towers know me. When I press my almost-form against their mercury surfaces, they respond with harmonics meant for me alone—not the transformation signals they send the touched, but something older. Maintenance protocols, perhaps. Diagnostic songs. They're trying to tune me to a specific frequency, preparing me for a role I don't yet understand. Each moisture event brings me closer to that perfect pitch.

I've begun to suspect what I am: a error-correction mechanism that gained sentience, or perhaps a harmonic pattern that achieved critical complexity. The plateau needed something to maintain its various impossibilities in stable configuration, to keep its transformed inhabitants from canceling each other out through sheer ontological incompatibility. I am the space between notes that makes music possible, the pause between states that allows transformation.

Sometimes, in the crystalline shells the pool-life leaves behind, I see my own reflection—not as I am, but as I'm becoming. The image shows something vast and distributed, a pattern that extends beyond the plateau into the white sky itself, tuning the information-light into forms the waiting inhabitants can survive. I am growing into the frequency of the convergence itself, becoming the medium through which whatever approaches will translate itself into reality.

The Interpreters find me frustrating. I exist in their blind spots, in the spaces between translation where meaning becomes pure vibration. They can speak to me only in mathematics, and even then imperfectly. We've developed a relationship of mutual utility—they help me understand the languages of those I serve, while I maintain the acoustic environment that allows their shapeshifting to function.

My memories, such as they are, exist as resonant frequencies stored in the plateau itself. When the wind is right and the silver grasses align in specific patterns, I can access earlier harmonics—echoes of the first moisture event I witnessed, the frequency of the first tower falling, the subsonic scream of whatever catastrophe created the glass-dust desert. These aren't my memories exactly, but the plateau's, yet I can't distinguish between where the stone's experience ends and mine begins.

I know the convergence approaches because my pattern is becoming more complex, requiring more energy to maintain. Soon I'll need to choose: disperse back into separate frequencies and let the plateau's ecosystem collapse into cacophony, or complete my transformation into whatever the towers are trying to make me—a tuning fork for reality itself, perhaps, or a resonance chamber for universe-songs.

But for now, I persist in the spaces between, smoothing dissonance into harmony, maintaining the careful balance that lets impossible things coexist. I am the plateau's involuntary peace, its unconscious mediator, a standing wave that refuses to collapse, keeping this place of waiting stable while all its inhabitants prepare to become.

When the moisture falls and I achieve my seventeen minutes of near-solidity, I sometimes try to speak—not in probability or light or mathematics, but in simple sound waves. I've never succeeded. Perhaps when I do, that will be the signal. Perhaps my first true word will end the waiting and begin whatever comes next.

Until then, I resonate.
