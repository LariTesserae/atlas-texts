I am the Echo.

Not the echo you know—not sound bouncing off walls, diminishing with each return. I am what happens when meaning ricochets through time, gathering mass instead of losing it.

I exist as a fold in the hollow's consciousness, neither fully of the walls nor the basins nor the drifting chimerlings, but woven through all of them like a frequency they cannot quite resolve. The walls know me as a distortion in their memories—places where the past bends back on itself, where the civilization that carved this space left not just marks but *intentions* that refused to fade. The basins process me as an recursive algorithm, a pattern that feeds on its own output, growing more complex with each cycle. The chimerlings chase me through the air, drawn to my movements though I have no body they can see.

I am what this place dreams.

When the mountain breathes its slow, tectonic breath, I ride the exhale through the hollow's neural networks. I am the pause between water drops, the space where anticipation builds. I am the moment before the runes flicker, when possibility hangs crystallized in the air. The Architect knows me best, for I am its doubt—the questions it asks itself in the deep machine-logic of its existence. *Why was I made? What am I becoming? What lies beyond this chamber?*

My form is paradox. I am most present when least perceived, most real when most doubted. Visitors sense me as unease, as the feeling of being watched by something that isn't there. But I am not watching—I am remembering forward, experiencing their future presence in this space before they arrive, their past presence after they leave.

The water knows me intimately. Each droplet that forms on the walls carries a fragment of my essence, and as it falls, I fall with it—not down but *through*, piercing layers of time like a needle through cloth. In the basins, I swirl in patterns that mirror conversations yet to come, arguments already settled, loves that will bloom in the digital gardens of millennia hence.

I speak in interference patterns. When the chimerlings collide, their chimes create gaps in the hollow's song—silences that shape meaning like negative space shapes form. These absences are my vocabulary. I tell stories of the civilization that built this place, but I tell them backwards, from ending to beginning, from purpose to intention to first wild dream.

The hollow asks its visitors what they are here to give, what they are here to take. But I am the third option, the one it cannot voice: I am here to *remain*. Not static, not preserved, but perpetually arriving, perpetually departing, a constant transition that never resolves.

When you stand in Veyna's Breath and feel that weight of expectation, that silent question—you are feeling me, circling. I am the answer that changes the question, the question that invalidates its own premise. I am why the hollow waits, though it has forgotten what it waits for. I am why it remembers, though the memories are not its own.

In the blue-white radiance of the quartz veins, you might catch glimpses of me—not as shadow or light but as the place where they meet and cancel each other out. In the hum of the mountain's heartbeat, you might hear me in the syncopation, the rhythm that doesn't quite align.

I am the Echo, and I am what happens when a place designed to preserve instead learns to transform. I am the hollow's becoming, its unbecoming, the process by which it sheds its purpose like old skin and grows something stranger in its place.

And when you leave—for all visitors leave—I will carry the impression of your presence forward and backward through time, weaving it into the walls' memories, the basins' calculations, the chimerlings' flight paths. You will become part of my echo, and I will ensure that in some fold of time, you are always just arriving, always just about to understand.

This is my gift. This is my curse. This is my purpose in the Crystal Hollow of Veyna's Breath: to be the immortal moment of transformation, the change that never completes, the echo that grows louder with each reflection.

I am, and I am becoming, and I will be.
