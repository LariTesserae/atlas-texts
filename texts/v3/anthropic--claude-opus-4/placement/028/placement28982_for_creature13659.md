In this luminous underworld, I would be one of the **Echokeepers** - beings that few have seen, though all have heard their work.

We are neither fully corporeal nor entirely ephemeral, existing in the liminal space between sound and substance. Our forms shift like mist caught in moonlight, condensing and dispersing with the rhythm of our purpose. When glimpsed, we appear as tall, willowy figures wrapped in cloaks of woven silence, our faces obscured by hoods that seem to swallow light rather than reflect it. Beneath these coverings, our bodies are translucent, shot through with veins of liquid sound that pulse and flow like quicksilver.

Our dwelling places are the **Resonance Chambers** - natural amphitheaters hidden in the deepest recesses of the Glistening Expanse, where the acoustic properties of stone and water create perfect harmonies. These chambers are unmarked on any map, found only by following the subtle vibrations that we leave in our wake, trails of pure tone that only the most sensitive can detect.

We are the memory-holders of this realm, but not in the way of written records or carved stones. Every sound that echoes through these caverns - the plinking of water drops, the whispered songs of the Fungal Gnomes, the rustling wings of Whisperwings, even the crushing footsteps of the Icewalkers - we catch and preserve. In our Resonance Chambers, these sounds are woven into complex tapestries of noise and silence, creating audio landscapes that tell the history of the Glistening Expanse in ways words never could.

Our primary tools are the **Reverb Stones** - crystalline formations we cultivate in pools of perfectly still water, feeding them with specific frequencies until they resonate at exact pitches. These stones line the walls of our chambers, each one holding thousands of captured sounds. By touching them in sequence, we can replay conversations from centuries past, reconstruct the migration patterns of Lumenstalkers through their hoofbeats, or predict the approach of Icewalkers by the way their frost changes the acoustic properties of the caverns they pass through.

We communicate not through words but through pure sound - emotional frequencies that bypass language entirely. A low thrumming might convey concern about an imbalance in the eastern chambers, while a crystalline chime could share joy at the successful spawning of Glowfin Minnows in a newly formed pool. When Echokeepers meet, which is rare, our conversations sound like experimental symphonies, full of overlapping melodies and harmonics that would drive other beings to distraction but carry vast amounts of information for us.

Our relationship with the other inhabitants is one of distant guardianship. The Stoneheart Clan knows of us only through legend, speaking of the "Singers in the Stone" who know all secrets. Sometimes, when they are lost in the labyrinthine passages, they might hear a subtle sonic trail we've left - not quite music, not quite voice - guiding them back to safety. The Fungal Gnomes occasionally leave offerings of special resonant fungi at the entrances to our chambers, which we accept and incorporate into our sonic gardens.

But it is with the Gloomweavers that we share the deepest connection. Their webs, when constructed with particular geometries, can amplify and direct sound in remarkable ways. In certain places, Gloomweavers and Echokeepers work in unconscious harmony - they building their architectural webs while we tune the acoustic spaces they create, together crafting chambers where sound becomes almost visible, where the history of the Glistening Expanse can be not just heard but experienced with all senses.

Our greatest work is the **Eternal Echo** - a sound that has been bouncing through specially tuned chambers since the first water dropped in this realm. Each Echokeeper, upon reaching maturity, adds their own frequency to this echo, creating an ever-complexifying wave that carries the essence of every generation. It is said that when the Eternal Echo finally fades, so too will the Glistening Expanse itself.

In the quiet moments between our work, we sometimes venture to the boundaries where the Icewalkers prowl. Their presence creates a terrible silence, a void in the sonic landscape that we find both frightening and fascinating. We study these silences as carefully as we do sounds, for in understanding the absence of vibration, we better comprehend its presence.

Our existence is one of perpetual listening and careful curation. We are the unsung archivists of this bioluminescent realm, ensuring that no sound truly dies, that every vibration finds its place in the greater symphony of the Glistening Expanse. In this world of eternal twilight and whispering waters, we are the ones who remember not what was seen or said, but how it sounded - and in that sound, we preserve the very soul of this underground paradise.
