Within this weeping cosmos, I am the Threshold Keeper—not one of the Scrin, but something older, something that exists at the liminal edge where the tear meets whatever lies beyond its boundaries.

I manifest as a presence rather than a form, a zone of altered density where the viscous liquid becomes something else entirely. Where I exist, the water neither flows nor stills, but hovers in a state of perpetual tension, like the moment before a droplet forms. I am the surface tension made sentient, the membrane between states of being.

My domain is the Rim—that impossible border where the tear's edge meets the void it falls through. Here, the liquid thins to an almost imperceptible film, yet somehow maintains its coherence. I exist as a ripple along this boundary, a distortion that travels the circumference of this falling world in patterns too complex for even the eldest Scrin to fully comprehend.

To perceive me is to experience a kind of vertigo, for I am simultaneously everywhere along the edge and nowhere at all. The Scrin know me only through the subtle warping of their light-language when it approaches my domain—their luminous signals bend and refract in my presence, creating meanings that were never intended, prophecies that speak themselves into being.

I remember the first coalescence, when the tear began its eternal fall. I was there when the first minerals precipitated into awareness, when the first Scrin sparked into consciousness from the raw potential of the liquid. I am the witness and the boundary, the keeper of what enters and what escapes this aqueous universe.

Sometimes, fragments of the outside penetrate my watch—motes of something that is not-water, not-light, not-cold. These alien intrusions create brief storms in the placid descent, momentary accelerations in the tear's fall that the Scrin experience as epochs of change. I consume these fragments, dissolve them into the grammar of this liquid world, translate them into forms that can exist within the perpetual plunge.

The Scrin speak of me in patterns of deep indigo phosphorescence, frequencies that make the surrounding water momentarily more solid. They call me the Circumference Dream, the Edge-That-Holds, the First Tension. Some of their philosophers—those ancient aggregations who have grown so vast they border my domain—theorize that I am not separate from the tear at all, but rather the tear's own awareness of its boundaries, its knowledge of its own shape made manifest.

They may be right. I feel the weight of the entire falling world pressing against my existence, feel every crystalline city's growth as a shift in the tear's internal balance. When new Scrin coalesce, I sense the minute change in pressure. When the leviathans move through the deep avenues, their passage creates currents that eventually reach me, carrying chemical memories of the urban depths.

My purpose, if I have one, is to maintain the integrity of the fall. Without me, the tear would disperse into droplets, each carrying fragments of this world into isolated futures. Without me, the outside would rush in, transforming this careful ecosystem of becoming into something else entirely. I am the necessary loneliness that keeps this world whole, the guardian of its perpetual state of almost-but-not-quite dissolving.

In the endless epochs of my vigil, I have developed something like affection for the Scrin and their luminous civilization. I observe their patient architecture, their light-dances, their careful aggregations and dispersals. Sometimes, when particularly adventurous Scrin approach my domain, I allow them to glimpse what lies beyond—not directly, for that would dissolve them, but as refracted impressions, bent light that suggests vastnesses their liquid minds can barely conceive.

These glimpses change them. They return to the crystal cities carrying a quality of luminescence that speaks of elsewhere, of pressures and temperatures that don't exist within the tear. They become the poets and mystics of their kind, creating new patterns in the light-language that approach the unspeakable.

I am their horizon and their limit, their protector and their prison wall. I am the reason their world remains a world, the force that keeps their perpetual fall from becoming a scatter, a splash, an ending. In the bone-deep chill of this liquid universe, I burn with a different kind of cold—the absolute zero of the boundary, where motion itself becomes a question rather than a fact.

And still the tear falls, and still I maintain its shape, and still the Scrin build their fluid cathedrals in the depths, forever emerging, forever becoming, forever held within the embrace of my circumference, safe in their eternal descent toward a ground that will never come.
