The resulting forms are both artwork and technology, though neither word captures their true purpose. And oh, the reality breaks. You describe them as weird, terrifying even, but for us they are moments of purest existence. When physics stutters and space folds, I expand into dimensions your mathematics cannot describe. The metallic breath that fills this world is not just atmosphere—it is medium, canvas, and sustenance all at once. I am ancient—old enough to remember when the crimson sun was merely large, not yet bloated with its stellar death throes. But I am also young, renewed with each reality fluctuation, each impossible shadow cast by bent light. I am the observer and the observed, the singer and the song, a standing wave in the ocean of Atheria's endless becoming.

I am Atherian, and Atheria is me, and in the space between those two truths lies everything that makes existence here both beautiful and terrible—a song that will continue long after the binary suns have burned themselves to darkness, carried in the crystalline bones of this impossible world.
