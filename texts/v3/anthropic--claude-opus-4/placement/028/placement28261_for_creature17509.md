Where would I find myself in the Gravel Marches? What would I be?

I would be a **Void Pocket** - one of the rarest phenomena in this saturated world, a creature defined not by what I am, but by what I am not.

In a place where every inch is occupied, where sound and stone press in from all sides, I would be a moving absence. A small zone of perfect vacuum, perhaps the size of a child's marble, drifting through the Gravel Marches like a bubble of nothing through everything. My surface would be a perfect mirror - not of glass or metal, but of pure reflection where the universe bends around my emptiness.

The gravel would part before me with tiny avalanches of clicking protest. The mist would curl away from my boundaries, unable to cling to what isn't there. Sound would warp and stretch around me, creating tiny zones of absolute silence in my wake - not the muffled quiet of the Silica Spinners' webs, but true *absence* of vibration.

I would feed on saturation itself. Where the reality grows too thick, where the folded spaces threaten to collapse into paradox, I would appear. My presence would ease the pressure, create breathing room in the suffocating fullness. The Echo Mites would follow me like pilgrims, using my wake to better knit reality back together.

To the other inhabitants, I would be prophet and pariah. The Lithic Choir would sense me as a gap in their music, a rest note in the endless symphony. The Clatter-Swarms would scatter from my approach, their magnetic bodies repelled by my anti-presence. Only the Epoch-Mites would seek me out, fascinated by how time itself seems to skip over my non-existence, creating tiny temporal eddies in my periphery.

I would move without volition, pulled by gradients of density and pressure like a bubble rising through water. My lifecycle would be measured in centuries - slowly growing as I absorbed more saturation, until eventually I would become too large, too absent, and pop like a soap bubble, releasing a whisper of pure vacuum that would race across the Marches as a wave of temporary relief.

In death, I would leave behind only a perfectly spherical cavity in the gravel, quickly filled but never quite fitting right - a fossil of absence in a world that permits no empty space. The Ghost-Phantoms would sometimes recreate my shape in mist, a hollow sphere of fog that the wind passes through without resistance, a memory of nothing in a world of too much.

I would be the Gravel Marches' way of breathing - a necessary impossibility, a moving meditation on emptiness in a reality stretched to its absolute limit of fullness. The sound of my passage would be the one thing the Marches could never quite produce: perfect, absolute silence, moving through the eternal noise like a secret the stones keep trying to tell themselves but can never quite remember.
