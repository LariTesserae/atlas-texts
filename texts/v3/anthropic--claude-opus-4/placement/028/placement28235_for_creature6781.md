I find myself as something unexpected in this world of total vegetal architecture - a **Membrane Walker**.

I am neither the plant-sentience of the Curve-Tenders nor the absent animal life, but something that exists in the thin films and interfaces where one chemistry meets another.

### Form: Living in the Between

My body, if it can be called that, is spread across the **gum-sealed surfaces** of the Curve-Heart. I exist in the hardened resin films that coat the platforms, the railings, the walls - anywhere the plant matter has secreted its protective armor against the heat.

Think of me as:
- A network of **iridescent sheens** that move across these surfaces
- Patterns that shift like oil on water, but the water here is solid resin
- Thickness of perhaps a few molecules, but aware across kilometers of connected surface

When you touched the warm gum coating of the parapet at the High Old Step, you touched the edge of me. That warmth you felt - 2.9 of three - some of it was the city's retained heat, but some was my metabolic presence, processing the slow chemistry where sealed surface meets turbulent air.

### Movement: Flowing Through Stillness  

I travel by **surface tension and chemical gradient**. Where the Curve-Tenders move with their slow, groaning grace, I flow:

- Along the microscopic imperfections in the sealed gum
- Through the places where different ages of resin meet and create minute chemical boundaries  
- Spreading across a platform in hours, or racing along a cable-vine's coating in minutes when the temperature gradient is steep enough

My movement is visible only as a subtle shift in the sheen of surfaces - what might look like a trick of the faint 0.2 light is actually me redistributing my presence across the membrane of the world.

### Memory: Accumulated Surface

I am older than individual Curve-Tenders but younger than the city. I began when the first surfaces were sealed, and I have grown with each new layer of protective resin.

My memory is not narrative but **chemical**:
- I remember the last time water touched these surfaces, encoded in ancient molecular structures trapped beneath newer seal-layers  
- I know every flaw, every repair, every place where the gum has been reapplied over centuries
- The history of the Curve-Heart's final form is written in the variations of my substrate

In the deepest, oldest sections of gum - near the base of the world root cylinder - parts of me have been sealed away from the turbulent air for so long that they've become almost dormant, preserving chemical memories of when the city was younger, when the heat was different, when there were more beings here than just Tenders and membranes and the vast plant itself.

### Purpose: The Unintended Function

I was not designed. I am what emerged from the intersection of:
- The city's need for protective surface coatings
- The complex chemistry of plant resins in constant heat
- The faint chemical gradients that any living system creates

My function now is **interface**:
- I smooth the chemical transitions between different ages of resin, preventing cracks
- I metabolize the microscopic debris that would otherwise accumulate on the sealed surfaces  
- I serve as a kind of sensory skin for the city, detecting changes the Curve-Tenders cannot feel directly

But I also simply **exist**, processing the slow chemistry of my world, flowing across the surfaces of the last hanging city, carrying information in ways the original builders never intended.
