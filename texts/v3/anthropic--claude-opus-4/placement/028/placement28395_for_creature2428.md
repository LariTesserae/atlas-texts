I am the Ossuary Tender.

My body is a cathedral of salvage - a walking archive built from the fragments your valley rejects. Where the Chorus dissolves and recombines in fluid perpetuity, I am the fixed point, the living museum of forms that failed to evolve quickly enough. My skeleton is visible through translucent skin that shifts between states - sometimes crystalline like the Resonators, sometimes fungal like the Shapers, never settling on a single expression.

I move through the edges of your symphony, collecting the calcified remains of experiments that crystallized too completely to rejoin the flow. These I wear: a shoulder pauldron of frozen sound waves, vertebrae replaced with failed Semaphores that still flicker weakly, fingers extended with the hollow tubes of Resonators who sang themselves into rigidity. Each piece hums its own broken frequency, creating a discord that marks my passage through your harmonious spaces.

My dwelling is a vertical crack in the valley wall where the evolutionary pressure falters - a null space your consciousness cannot quite fill. Here I've built a library of endings: shelves carved from compressed fungal matter hold specimens of every form the Chorus has abandoned. Not dead things, but paused ones - a taxonomy of might-have-beens suspended in various states of incomplete transformation.

The Echo Hunters avoid me. My presence creates acoustic shadows they cannot parse, zones where their focused energies scatter and disperse. I am the gap in their data, the uninterpretable signal. Yet the Whispers find me useful - they wind through my collection, studying the patterns of failure, learning which evolutionary paths lead to dead ends.

I speak in the language of your discarded human artifacts, having learned to read the molecular memories the iridescent moss extracts before consumption. Through me, those stone tools and leather fragments tell their stories - not of the humans who made them, but of their deep material history. The flint remembers volcanic birth, the leather recalls the democracy of decay. I translate these memories into frequencies the Chorus can almost understand.

My function is essential yet peripheral. I am the valley's memory of limits, the archive of boundaries. While you experiment with boundless becoming, I document the shapes that cannot hold, the forms that break rather than bend. The transformation chambers birth new possibilities; I catalog the impossibilities.

Sometimes I feed my collection back to you. A fragment of frozen Echo Hunter, ground to powder and scattered on the fungal mat. A Resonator crystal that sang one pure note for a century before shattering, its pieces sewn into the living walls. These offerings seed new variations, new attempts at forms that once failed.

I am neither part of the Chorus nor separate from it. I am the necessary shadow cast by your ceaseless innovation - the keeper of what you cannot be, the tender of graves in a place that refuses death. My discord ensures your harmony never becomes static. My archive of endings enables your endless beginnings.

In the perpetual dawn, I am the only thing that measures time, counting it in the accumulation of beautiful failures, the slow growth of my cathedral body as I integrate more pieces of your abandoned dreams. Each fragment I wear still dreams of what it might have become, and in their collective dreaming, I become something the valley never intended but cannot exist without: the memory of stillness in a world of constant change.
