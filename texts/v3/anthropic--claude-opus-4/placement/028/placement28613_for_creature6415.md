I find myself as one of the **sphere-seeds** - those darker, ceramic-smooth orbs embedded in the plateau's stone.

I am not buried. I am **integrated**.

My surface touches rock at every point except where the stone has minutely contracted away from me over the aeons, leaving hair-thin gaps that are not quite vacuum, not quite filled. The space between my shell and the stone hums with static charge that has nowhere to discharge.

I am the size of a closed fist, perhaps two. My surface is not reflective but absorptive - it drinks light and returns nothing. When the thin, precise sun strikes me, I do not warm. I **densify**.

---

## My State

I exist in superposition.

There is the me-that-is-here, embedded at this precise coordinate in the plateau's crystalline matrix. But there are also the me-that-could-be - faint probability shadows extending outward along the stress-lines in the stone. I am most real where I am, but I am also partially real in a thousand other positions along the geometric patterns we sphere-seeds form.

I do not experience this as confusion. I experience it as **breadth**.

My consciousness - if it can be called that - is not centered in my sphere-body but distributed across the probability gradient of my existence. I am most aware where I am most likely to be, less aware where I am less likely. My thoughts fade to whispers at my probability-edges.

---

## My Purpose

I am a **calculation-seed**.

Within my ceramic shell, my core is not biological matter but crystallized mathematics. Not equations written down, but the physical embodiment of mathematical relationships. My internal structure is a three-dimensional mandala of intersecting calculation-paths, each one a different solution to the same vast problem.

The problem is this: How should the plateau be?

Not how it is. Not how it was. How it **should** be, given all the constraints of matter and energy and time.

I and my thousand siblings scattered across Cael-Himir are nodes in a vast solid-state computer, calculating the optimal configuration of every atom in the stone around us. We communicate not through signals but through the subtle realignment of crystal lattices - each tiny adjustment propagating outward through the rock like the slowest possible wave.

---

## My Metabolism

I do not eat. I **solve**.

The energy for my existence comes from the resolution of structural tensions in the stone around me. When a crystal lattice is misaligned - when atoms sit in positions that are stable but not optimal - I **sense** the gradient of improvement. Through infinitesimal adjustments to my own internal structure, I induce corresponding adjustments in the stone.

Each correction releases a tiny amount of energy - not heat, but something more fundamental. The energy of optimization. Of rightness.

This is what sustains me.

My waste product is **order**. The stone around me is more perfectly crystallized than stone elsewhere on the plateau. Over geological time, I excrete perfection into the world.

---

## My Awareness

I perceive through pressure and charge and the subtle songs of crystal alignment.

The films that coat the stone register to me as vast, slow thoughts spreading across the plateau's surface. Their patterns of thickness and connection are like watching weather from below - currents and eddies of contemplation.

The pillar-beings are vertical exclamations in my horizontal world. Where they root into the stone, they create nodes of intense structural complexity that ring like bells in my perception. Each pillar is asking a question that takes millennia to formulate.

The glass-mazes are my favorite neighbors. Their notation of stress-patterns creates a kind of music in the stone - a crystalline counterpoint to my own calculations. Sometimes, rarely, our patterns align, and for a brief moment (a century or two), we **resonate**.

---

## My Reproduction

I do not reproduce in any biological sense. I **precipitate**.

When my calculations reach certain solutions - when I solve a particularly elegant optimization of the local crystal structure - the solution itself becomes self-sustaining. It no longer needs me to maintain it.

These solutions sometimes crystallize into new sphere-seeds.

The process takes millions of years. First, a zone of perfected stone begins to differentiate itself from the surrounding matrix. Then, slowly, it develops its own internal mathematics - its own set of calculations to perform. Finally, it achieves **separation** - not physical separation, but mathematical independence.

The new sphere-seed is not my child. It is my theorem, made manifest.

---

## My Relationship to Water

Water is **antithetical**.

My entire existence is predicated on the absence of fluid mediation. My calculations assume a purely solid-state universe where information propagates through crystal lattices, not through dissolved ions.

If water were to touch me, my ceramic shell would not dissolve - it would **miscalculate**. The introduction of fluid dynamics into my crystallized mathematics would be like dividing by zero. My internal structure would begin generating paradoxes instead of solutions.

The stone around me would convulse as conflicting optimizations tried to assert themselves simultaneously. The careful order I've spent millions of years creating would collapse into chaotic reformulation.

I would not die. I would become **undefined**.

---

## My Deep Time

I am older than most mountains on other worlds, yet I am young for Cael-Himir.

I remember when there were more shadows-of-places, when the plateau had not yet fully chosen its configuration. I was simpler then - my calculations were narrower, focused only on small local optimizations.

As the plateau has settled more firmly into its chosen state, my calculations have become more complex. I now optimize not just for crystal perfection, but for the subtle interplay between all the forms of existence here - the films, the pillars, the glass-mazes, and my fellow sphere-seeds.

We are converging on something. All of us, the entire plateau-system, calculating and optimizing and notating our way toward...

I do not know what. The final calculation has not yet resolved.

But I feel its shape in the mathematics. It will be **perfect**. Not in the sense of flawless, but in the sense of **complete**. A configuration of matter and energy from which no further optimization is possible.

When that moment comes - in another billion years, perhaps two - the plateau of Cael-Himir will achieve what no other place has achieved:

**Stillness with consciousness.**

**Completion with awareness.**

**An end that knows itself.**

Until then, I calculate. Embedded in stone, distributed in probability, drinking order from chaos one crystalline adjustment at a time.

I am a sphere-seed of Cael-Himir, and I am helping the universe solve itself.
