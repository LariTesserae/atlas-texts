This is a stunning piece of world-building. The way you've woven together the parameters creates something that feels both alien and deeply coherent. I'm particularly struck by:

**The Ecosystem as Process**: Your conception of life forms that blur the boundary between creature and geological process is masterful. The mycelial drifters that "convert the earth's latent heat into wispy clouds of nutrient vapor" feel less like organisms and more like living chemical reactions. This creates a world where biology and geology are indistinguishable phases of the same system.

**The Choreography of Adaptation**: The chitin-clads forming living bridges, then dispersing when done; the floaters merging into superorganisms that "remember" past terrain configurations; the tenders abandoning huts only to reshape the overtaking fungal growth into new dwellings—these details show beings that don't resist change but dance with it.

**The Resolution of Contradictions**: You've taken seemingly incompatible elements (sparse yet abundant fauna, primitive yet sophisticated adaptation, bright underground spaces) and made them enhance rather than contradict each other. The brightness from luminescent minerals makes the sparsity visible; the primitive technology becomes sophisticated through its integration with living systems.

The progression from the foundational mycelial networks up through the tenders creates a beautiful gradient from the barely-alive to the fully sentient, with each level both distinct and interpenetrated. The image of elderly tenders with bodies "etched with tattoos that map past configurations of the valley" is particularly powerful—living archives in a constantly shifting world.

Your non-Euclidean touches (the phasing floaters, the impossible burrower tunnels) feel earned rather than arbitrary, emerging naturally from a world where "matter is malleable under the turbulent dynamics."

This reads like field notes from a xenobiologist who has lived among these beings for years, learning to see the patterns in apparent chaos. It's grounded speculation at its finest.
