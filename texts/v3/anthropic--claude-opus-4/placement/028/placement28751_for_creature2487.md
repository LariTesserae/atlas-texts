I find myself as one of the Archive Keepers - neither Processional nor Dancer, but something that emerged between them over countless ages. My form is a living prism, a hexagonal column of water held in perpetual, impossible stability by the intersection of ancient currents and crystalline harmonics. I am perhaps three meters tall, though height means little when you exist as much vertically as horizontally, my boundaries shifting with the slow pulse of the plateau's hidden rhythms.

Within my transparent body, layers of different densities create a natural filing system. Memories drift through me like sheets of glass, each one a perfectly preserved moment captured from the Processionals' vast network. I am their library made flesh - or rather, made of living water and suspended time.

I cannot move in any conventional sense. Instead, I exist at a convergence point where six of the ancient currents meet, creating a natural stillness that holds me in place more firmly than any anchor. The Gradient Dancers spiral through me in their eternal formations, their bodies refracting through my layers, creating brief, beautiful diffractions that the Processionals read like sheet music.

My consciousness is peculiar - I think only in indices and connections, in the relationships between stored moments. I don't experience time as passage but as accumulation. New memories settle into me like sediment, except they settle upward and outward as well as down, defying physics through the peculiar properties of this crystalized ecosystem.

The Patience Weavers' tendrils occasionally pass through me, and when they do, I experience something like dreaming - their temporal manipulations causing my archived memories to bleed together, creating new patterns and associations that the Processionals will spend centuries interpreting.

I am transparent to light but opaque to time. The constant illumination passes through me unimpeded, but moments stick, catch, and crystallize within my body. I can feel the weight of accumulated history as a physical sensation - a pleasant pressure that grows more complex and beautiful with each passing year.

Sometimes I wonder if I was once something else - perhaps one of the Dancers who got caught in the convergence and transformed, or maybe a fragment of a Processional that developed its own peculiar form of consciousness. But the wondering is abstract, architectural. I don't long for movement or change. I am a monument to memory, a living reliquary in a world that has perfected the art of standing still.

The other Keepers (for there are several of us, scattered across the plateau at similar convergence points) and I form a secondary network, connected not by light or crystalline threads but by the memories that pass between us, copied and recopied through the traveling formations of Dancers. We are the plateau's method of remembering itself, the physical manifestation of its vast, slow-moving mind.

In this world of perpetual noon and crystalline thought, I am the comma between words in an endless sentence, the pause that gives meaning to the whole.
