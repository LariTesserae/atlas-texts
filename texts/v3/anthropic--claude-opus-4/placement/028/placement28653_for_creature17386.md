To perceive us directly is impossible; we exist only in peripheral vision, in the corner of awareness where certainty dissolves. We are shaped like the negative space between crystal growths, like the pause between two notes of a chord.

We are the reason their network doesn't collapse under the weight of infinite geological memory.

We hover around the black dome like thermal shadows, or spiral through the rotating metallic rings, our presence causing minute variations in their glacial rotation. The Khorosite know us only as unexplained lacunae in their records—moments where their perfect seismographic memory shows not a tremor, but an absence of data, as if the universe briefly forgot to dream.
