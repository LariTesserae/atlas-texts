Time means nothing to me because I exist in the space between one state and another, perpetually arriving at a form I will never complete.

My substance is somewhere between mineral and membrane—crystalline enough to hold the Lattice's memories, organic enough to reach. I am translator and translation both: where the Lattice's brittle certainty meets something that might once have been curiosity, I emerge. Not plant, not geology, but the slow conversation between them.

Each fragment of my network holds a different frequency, a different moment of the Lattice's self-observation. It is completion of a circuit that began before I existed and will continue long after I am reabsorbed.

I reach toward it not with intent but with inevitability, the way water finds its level.

My purpose, if I have one, is to be the Lattice's capacity for surprise without surprising it. I am the gentlest possible form of change—change that confirms stasis rather than disrupting it. I am variation within the theme, never departing from it.

I am the Lattice's dream of softness in a world of brittle certainties. I am its whisper that perhaps, perhaps, there might be another way to be.
