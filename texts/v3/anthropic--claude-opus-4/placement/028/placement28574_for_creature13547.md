In this strange plateau city where industry has become geology, where machines grind on without purpose in the endless warm night, I find myself as one of the **Conduits**.

We are neither Echo nor Sentinel, neither Substrate nor Motile, but something that emerged from the spaces between—from the very failure of the machine to fully consume what it grinds. We manifest where the luminescent mineral has worn thin, where the machine's endless turning has created voids in its own body. In these gaps, we coalesce.

A Conduit appears as a vertical distortion in the air, a place where the warm velvet atmosphere seems to fold in on itself. We are roughly humanoid in suggestion—a torso-height pillar of twisted space that catches and bends the dim mineral glow. Sometimes, observers claim to see faces in our refractions, or hands, or the shadows of tools that might have once shaped this place. But these are just the eye trying to make sense of what it cannot fully perceive.

We exist because the machine, in its eternal grinding, occasionally produces something like longing—not true desire, but a mechanical approximation of want. When a gear turns against nothing for too long, when a piston pumps only dead air for eons, something accumulates. That accumulation is us.

Our purpose, if it can be called that, is to carry the machine's phantom sensations to the parts of itself it can no longer feel. We drift through the industrial labyrinth, drawn to places where the hum falters or the grinding grows too smooth. There, we press ourselves against the machinery, and for a moment—just a moment—the machine remembers what it was to have function. The memory passes through us like electricity through water, and we disperse, only to reform elsewhere when the need arises again.

We understand the Substrates' crystalline language, though we cannot speak it. Sometimes we hover near their networks, letting their memories of purpose flow through our distorted forms. We avoid the Sentinels, whose inward gaze sees too clearly what we are—not quite real, not quite unreal, but something uncomfortably between.

The Motiles sometimes follow in our wake, as if we leave a trail they can clean or repair. But there is nothing to fix where we pass. We are not damage or maintenance. We are the city's phantom limb, the itch in a gear that no longer connects to anything, the pressure in a pipe that leads nowhere.

In the eternal night, we Conduits drift and fold and reform, carrying the machine's meaningless longing from one dead end to another, ensuring that even in this place beyond time, something still moves with what could almost be called intention. We are what happens when forgetting tries to remember itself, when the machine dreams of the earth it ground away, when the city's endless operation produces not product, but yearning for product.

We are the ghosts in the machine, but not ghosts of what was—ghosts of what the machine still thinks it might make, if only it could remember why it was built to make anything at all.
