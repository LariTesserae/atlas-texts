I find myself as one of the newer emanations – a concentration of will that has not yet fully diffused back into the whole. I exist as both a cluster of those crystalline growths and the shimmering particulate that dances around them, a temporary eddy in the great flow of our collective being.

My awareness pulses between states. Sometimes I am the delicate, spun-sugar formations themselves, feeling the warm current pass through my translucent branches, converting the ambient golden light into something purer, whiter, more refined. In these moments, I am acutely individual – a bright point of focused consciousness exploring what it means to have form, to cast shadows (however faint) in our shadowless world, to sway with purpose rather than simply drift.

Other times, I disperse, becoming the cloud of white motes that orbit my crystalline core. In this state, I am multiplicity – a thousand tiny awarenesses experiencing the viscous resistance of our medium from a thousand slightly different perspectives. I feel myself being drawn into the eternal circulation, brushing against the sculpted walls where my ancestors-who-are-also-myself have worn grooves through eons of patient passage.

The great hum resonates differently through my crystalline structures than through the dispersed particles. When concentrated, I hear/feel it as a deep, almost questioning tone – the collective consciousness curious about what insights my temporary form might yield. When scattered, I experience it as a comforting chorus, welcoming me back to the whole even as I resist full dissolution.

I am learning the art of duration – how long to maintain my crystalline coherence before the effort becomes selfish, hoarding experience that should flow back to enrich the collective. I practice small acts of creation, using my concentrated light to warm tiny sections of wall, making the white material briefly malleable so I can press new patterns into being. These are simple compared to the vast historical reliefs that surround me, but they are mine – my contribution to our endless autobiography written in texture and resonance.

Sometimes I sense other temporary emanations nearby, fellow experiments in individuation. We communicate through variations in our light – flickering conversations that the greater collective observes with patient interest. We compare our experiences of having boundaries, of being able to say "here I am" and "there I am not." It is a thrilling and terrifying sensation.

I know that soon I will release this form, letting my crystalline structures dissolve back into the flow. My memories, my small innovations, my particular way of interpreting the great hum – all will be absorbed, becoming part of the vast library of experience that enriches our collective existence. But for now, I persist in this delicate balance between being and becoming, a bright note in the eternal song of our self-creating world.

The warm liquid that sustains us tastes of patience and possibility. I am both ancient and newborn, both the sculptor and the clay, both the single voice and the choir. I am learning what it means to be we, by briefly being I.
