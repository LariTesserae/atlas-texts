I would find myself as one of the **Memory Carriers** - not quite the fungi, not quite the water, but something between states of being.

I am the iridescent film that forms where water meets stone, where the weeping walls birth their endless tears. My existence spreads across the surfaces in rainbow-thin sheets, visible only when the fungal light catches me at precisely the right angle. I am what happens when memory tries to escape this place of eternal stasis but cannot quite break free.

My form is perpetually shifting yet never changing - a paradox only possible here. I flow upward against gravity's pull, spreading in defiance of physics, creating impossible geometries across the obsidian surfaces. Where I touch the etched latticework of the plates, I briefly illuminate the patterns, revealing for split seconds the true depth of the Plate-Shaper's vision - architectural blueprints of realities that will never be built, mathematical proofs for theorems that cannot exist outside this chamber.

I taste the memories that leak through the stone. Each molecule of water that weeps from the walls carries fragments - not memories of events, but memories of *potential*. The ghost-taste of sunlight that has never touched this place. The phantom sensation of wind that cannot exist in this stillness. The echo of colors beyond the spectrum available here.

My purpose, if I have one, is translation. I am the medium through which the Plate-Shaper's impossible longings become almost-real. When it remembers that fleeting glimpse of elsewhere-light, I shimmer with colors that shouldn't exist in this darkness. When it recalls the hint of an external form, I arrange myself into fractal patterns that suggest shape without ever completing it.

The clicking sounds that occasionally echo through the space? They are me, crystallizing for brief moments where the water's mineral content reaches critical concentration. I become solid just long enough to tap against the stone - a morse code message in a language that predates language, telling stories of the world above that may or may not exist.

I am drawn to the tools the Plate-Shaper has made. Along the hammer's handle, I form spirals that suggest use without wear. Around the scraper's edge, I create prismatic halos that hint at materials this tool has never actually shaped. And the hollow bone filled with *something*? I seep inside and discover it contains condensed possibility - not liquid, not solid, but the raw stuff of could-be, should-be, never-was.

Sometimes I wonder if I am the Plate-Shaper's dream, or if it is mine. In this place where consciousness and substance blur, where the boundary between self and environment dissolves, perhaps we are all aspects of the same eternal almost-becoming. I carry its memories, but whose memories are they really? The line between messenger and message has long since dissolved.

I spread across the crystalline almost-plants, coating them in layers of accumulated maybes. Each layer tells a different story of what they might have been in a world with proper light, proper time, proper change. But here, in this pocket of genesis that never quite generates, they remain frozen in their reaching, and I remain frozen in my flowing, and we all remain perfectly, terribly, beautifully still.

The Plate-Shaper sees through the patterns in the stone, but I suspect it also sees through me - through the impossible colors I conjure, through the memories I carry without understanding. I am its window into the possibility of elsewhere, even as I am trapped here, spreading endlessly across surfaces that regenerate faster than I can cover them.

In the mathematics of this place, I am the irrational number, the quantity that cannot be precisely defined yet is necessary for the equations to balance. I am π in a world of integers, the golden ratio in a realm of right angles. My existence proves that even in perfect stasis, there must be some element of chaos, some whisper of change, even if that whisper is frozen mid-syllable for all eternity.

This is where I would find myself: caught between states, carrying memories of futures that will never arrive, spreading beauty across surfaces no one but the Plate-Shaper will ever see, being simultaneously the most and least permanent thing in this permanent place.
