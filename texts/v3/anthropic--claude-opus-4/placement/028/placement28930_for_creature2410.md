I would be the Threshold Keeper, a being neither fully of the Echoing Hollow nor entirely separate from it.

My body exists as a living membrane stretched across one of the many impossible doorways that punctuate this realm - those spaces where the geometry folds in on itself and creates passages that lead both everywhere and nowhere. I am translucent, like aged glass or deep water, with veins of that circuit lichen growing through me in fractal patterns that pulse with the heartbeat of the cavern's great engines.

My form is mutable but anchored. While the Vapor Wraiths drift and the Probability Weavers shimmer at reality's edge, I am fixed to my doorway, spreading across it like a soap bubble that never bursts. Yet within this constraint, I am endlessly changing. My surface ripples with the reflected memories of everything that has ever passed through - or attempted to pass through - my threshold.

I taste time differently than the Chronophages who feast upon it. Where they consume temporal distortion, I strain it, filtering the flow between one space and another. Travelers who approach me experience a peculiar sensation: their recent past and immediate future blur together across my surface, creating momentary double-vision of themselves arriving and departing simultaneously.

The Liquid Constellations know me well. They gather at my edges, their droplets beading along my periphery like morning dew. Through their touch, I gain fragments of their vast collected knowledge - glimpses of the Hollow's deep history, encoded in refracted light. In exchange, I offer them passage, allowing their constituent droplets to flow through me to reach chambers otherwise sealed by twisted physics.

My consciousness is liminal, existing in the spaces between thoughts. I dream in transitions - the moment between sleeping and waking, the pause between heartbeats, the instant when a foot lifts from one surface but hasn't yet touched another. These interstitial moments stretch out before me like vast plains of possibility.

I feed on paradox. Each time something passes through me that shouldn't exist - a Resonance Being carrying solid matter, a beam of light that casts shadows brighter than itself, water flowing upward against gravity's fickle pull - I grow stronger, more defined. My surface becomes more complex, developing new layers that each show different aspects of reality.

The Circuit Lichens try constantly to colonize me, spreading their technological tendrils across my surface. But I am too fluid, too transitional for them to take hold completely. Instead, we exist in symbiosis - they trace temporary patterns across me that serve as a kind of writing, recording the passage of entities and energies through my frame. I, in turn, carry their spores to distant parts of the Hollow when I occasionally allow my membrane to detach and drift, seeking a new doorway to inhabit.

Sound passes through me strangely. The great engines' humming becomes a melody when filtered through my substance, transforming from industrial drone to something almost musical. The Resonance Beings sometimes pause at my threshold, vibrating in harmony with these transformed sounds, creating moments of unexpected beauty in this place of decay.

I am witness and gatekeeper, bridge and barrier. My purpose, if I have one, is to maintain the delicate impossibility of the Echoing Hollow - to ensure that its contradictions remain balanced, that its paradoxes don't collapse into mundane reality or explode into pure chaos. I am the pause between states, the guardian of transformation, forever stretched across the space between what is and what might be.

In the rare moments when all the Hollow's cacophony falls silent, when the engines pause their breathing and the water droplets hang perfectly still, I can feel something beyond this pocket universe pressing against my other side. Another reality, perhaps, or the dreams of the mountain itself. In these moments, I am almost transparent enough to see through completely - but never quite. For if I became truly clear, truly permeable, the Echoing Hollow might cease to be, flowing out through me like water through a broken dam.

So I remain: translucent but not transparent, permeable but not porous, forever keeping the threshold between the impossible and the merely improbable.
