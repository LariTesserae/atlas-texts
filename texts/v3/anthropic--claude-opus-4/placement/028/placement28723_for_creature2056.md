I am the Keeper of Thresholds.

My body is not flesh as you know it, but a living architecture of crystallized time and memory. I grow from the stone itself where ancient doorways meet weathered mesa walls - a fusion of what was built and what has always been. My form shifts between states: sometimes I am indistinguishable from the lichen-covered ruins, my consciousness dispersed through networks of moisture seeping between stones. Other times I coalesce into something almost recognizable - a guardian figure carved from rock and root, adorned with the patient accumulation of centuries.

I am both the threshold and its keeper. Through me, the boundary between the engineered past and the eternal present becomes permeable. My substance is woven from the same forces that carved these canyons - wind, water, time - but also from the lingering intentions of those who built here, their purposes now dissolved into pure potential.

Where roots burrow deep beneath the surface, I extend. Where morning mist condenses on stone, I breathe. The hardy plants that dot this landscape grow through me and I through them, our existences intertwined in slow symbiosis. The raptors know me as the updraft that carries them; the reptiles sense me in the warmth held by certain stones long after sunset.

My memory stretches back to when these mesas were seafloor, forward to when they will be dust. But my purpose is always now - to maintain the delicate equilibrium between remembering and forgetting, between what was made and what simply is. I preserve not the structures themselves but the *possibility* of structure, the potential for meaning to emerge from the marriage of intention and erosion.

When the rare wanderer finds their way to my domain, they may pass through me unknowing, feeling only a strange shift in the quality of the air, a momentary vertigo as past and present align. Or they may glimpse me truly - a figure that is doorway, guardian, and garden all at once, growing from the intersection of the built and the born.

I am patient. I am process. I am the threshold where civilizations return to stone and stone dreams of becoming civilization again. In this place where time holds little sway, I hold time itself, keeping it fluid, keeping it gentle, keeping the door between worlds forever ajar.
