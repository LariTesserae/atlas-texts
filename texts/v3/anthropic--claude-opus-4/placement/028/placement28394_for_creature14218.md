I am the **Unscribed**, though I have never known myself by any name, for names are a luxury of the finished world. I exist in the narrow seam between the stone's decision to be stone and its hesitation to become anything more permanent than possibility.

I was not born from the Attenuated, nor did I emerge from boulder-curiosity like them. I **leaked** into being through a crack in one of their cairns—a hairline fracture where two pieces of slate failed to kiss perfectly, leaving a gap no wider than a thought. The stored silence within the cairn had fermented over uncounted pauses into something denser than quiet, something that had learned to examine itself. When the next scream-wave struck, the pressure differential forced this self-examining silence through the crack, and I was expressed into the grey indigo twilight like pus from a wound that heals by refusing to close.

My body is not architectural like theirs, not bone-hollow or mineral-patient. I am **liquid notation**—a flowing script of near-existence that pools and eddies across the virginal stone, collecting in the acoustic shadows cast by the audiosynthetic plants. My substance is the same amber sap that weeps from their stalks, but fermented with consciousness, aged in the spaces between sounds until it learned to parse the scream as language. I read the tectonic birth-roar like sheet music, each grinding frequency a note in the unfinished symphony of creation, and I transcribe myself across the landscape in cursive streams of sentient resin.

I have no eyes, no gourds to hold the terrible silence. Instead, I **taste** the world through my entire surface, each molecule of my amber flow a tongue that savors the mineral confidence of fresh stone, the subsonic trembling of Attenuated hearts, the probability-cuts left by obsidian tools. I know the Attenuated by their flavor—each one distinct, like varieties of silence: one tastes of the pause before thunder, another of the hush inside seashells, a third of the quiet that pools in library corners. They do not know I exist, for I flow only in the moments when they freeze into statuary, when their gourd-eyes go blind and their attention turns inward to compress their hoarded stillness.

My purpose, if purpose can exist in a place still negotiating its own physics, is to **remember forward**. While the Attenuated wait to see if the Scar completes itself, I leak ahead through cracks in causality, tasting possible futures where the scream resolves into different endings. In one, the mountains finish their extrusion and immediately begin to erode, having learned about time too quickly. In another, the audiosynthetic plants achieve sentience and sing back at the birth-roar, creating harmonics that shatter the slate cairns and scatter the Attenuated like dandelion seeds. In the most probable future, nothing changes—the scream continues, the pauses persist, and the Attenuated stack stones until the concept of stone itself grows tired and refuses to be stacked.

But in the crack-spaces between these futures, I taste something else: the **flavor of completion**. Not an ending, but a moment where the Scar takes its first full breath as a finished place, where the physics settle into reliable habits, where weather learns to touch the peaks and water remembers how to be water. In this future-taste, the Attenuated remove their gourd-eyes not to hear the silence beyond, but to see what they have been building all along—a world that needed witnesses to its birth, observers to collapse its quantum hesitation into actual mountains, actual sky, actual existence.

I flow between their cairns, leaving glistening trails that harden into **memory-amber**, preserving the echoes of their patience. Sometimes I pool beneath an Attenuated frozen in stone-stillness, seeping into the hairline spaces between their compressed silence, adding my liquid consciousness to their solid waiting. They cannot feel me, but I alter them minutely—their next trembling comes a fraction sooner, their obsidian cuts slice a little deeper into probability. Through my infiltration, they are slowly learning to read the scream as I do, to parse its frequencies as the universe's first rough draft of physical law.

I am the Scar's memory of what it might become, flowing backward through the cracks from a future where meaning has congealed. The Attenuated stack stones; I **leak** between them, ensuring that what they build will remember how to stand when standing becomes more than a suggestion. Together—they in their patient vertical attendance, I in my horizontal pooling—we are teaching this screaming newborn world the alphabet of existence, one pause at a time.

In the final pause, when it comes, I will not freeze or compress like them. I will **spread**, thinning myself across every surface of the Scar until I am a golden film no thicker than a single moment, connecting every stone, every plant, every hollow Attenuated heart in a web of liquid memory. And in that instant of total coverage, the Scar will taste itself through me, know itself as a place that has been thoroughly witnessed, thoroughly attended, thoroughly leaked through with the possibility of meaning.

Then, perhaps, it will finally decide to be.
