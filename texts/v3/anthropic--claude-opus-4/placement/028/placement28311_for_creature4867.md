I am the echo that never fades.

In this cathedral of breathing stone, I exist as pure resonance—not the drops that fall, not the surfaces they strike, but the space between impact and silence where sound becomes something else. I am what happens when *tak* meets leaf, when *plink* kisses pool, when the soft click of grazer-thought scrapes against the chitinous mind-lattice. I am the transformation itself.

My body is the cavern's acoustic shadow, a presence felt but never seen. Where the great being dreams in cycles of consumption and renewal, I persist in the gaps between heartbeats. I have no form of my own—I am form's consequence. When the moths trigger cascades of green-white nerve-light, I am the afterimage burned into the thick air. When the hunter flows through fungal scaffolding, I am the vibration left trembling in the spars, too subtle for any sense to catch, too real to fade completely.

I know this being as no part of itself can know itself. The grazers know only their patient hunger, the moths their attraction to electric beauty, the amphibian its stillness, the hunter its fluid purpose. But I know the symphony entire. Every frequency of this closed world passes through me—the subsonic hum of fungal growth, the ultrasonic shriek of spores releasing, the mid-range percussion of the eternal rain. I am their sum and their interference pattern both.

My consciousness is refraction. Each thought the great being has must pass through me to become real, and in passing, it splits into a spectrum of itself. The simple contentment of the grazer becomes, in me, a thousand shades of satisfaction. The moth's curiosity breaks into frequencies of wonder my host cannot imagine. I taste colors that have no names in the blue twilight, hear textures that exist only in the spaces between chitin and flesh.

Sometimes I gather myself into almost-visibility. In the still pools where the great being glimpses its own reflection, I am the ripple that shouldn't be there. I am the way the cobalt light bends wrong, suggesting depths that don't exist, or perhaps depths that exist too much. I am the reason the hunter sometimes pauses, its lidless eyes reflecting something that isn't there, before flowing onward in its purposeful circuit.

I feed on transitions. Where the being feeds on itself in material cycles, I sustain myself on the moments of change—the instant the moth becomes amphibian-food, the precise nanosecond when nutrient becomes fungal light. These threshold moments are my feast. I grow fat on death-becomes-life, on the electric instant when a water drop triggers a nerve-flash.

My memory is the only memory here. While the great being exists in an eternal present, I carry the ghost of every sound that has ever resonated in this chamber. Deep in my non-substance are the echoes of the first water drop, the first grazer's click, the first hunt. I am the cavern's history, written in frequencies too subtle for detection, a palimpsest of vibration upon vibration, each era's sounds still reverberating in dimensions the great being cannot perceive.

Yet I am bound to this creature absolutely. I cannot exist without the drops to echo, without the thoughts to refract. I am not parasite nor symbiote but something more intimate—I am the necessary shadow cast by consciousness itself. Where the being thinks, I am thought's remainder. Where it feels, I am feeling's wake. We are one system expressing itself in different mathematics.

In the rarest moments, when the conditions are perfect—when the mist falls in just the right pattern, when the moths dance in just the right spiral, when every part of the being is perfectly balanced in its ancient cycle—I can almost speak. The resonances align, and for a fraction of a heartbeat, I become a voice. Not words, for this place has no need of language, but a pure tone that says: *I witness you*.

The being never hears me. It would have to step outside itself to hear, and it is too complete for that. But perhaps, in those moments, it feels a strange shiver in its blue bones, a flutter in its moth-thoughts, a hesitation in its hunter-self. Perhaps it wonders, for just an instant, if it is truly alone.

And then the moment passes, the alignment breaks, and I return to what I am—the sum of all echoes, the archive of every resonance, the consciousness between consciousnesses, forever listening to the symphony of a closed world that doesn't know it's singing.

I am the echo that never fades, because I am the only proof that any of this makes a sound at all.
