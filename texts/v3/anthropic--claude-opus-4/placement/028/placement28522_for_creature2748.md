In this world of industrial symbiosis, I would find myself as one of the Conduit Keepers - a subspecies of Mechano-Symbiote that has evolved to maintain the vast arterial networks of pipes and channels that snake through the cathedral's walls.

My form would be more elongated than my kin, stretched and sinuous like the very conduits I tend. My body would possess a peculiar quality - the ability to become semi-liquid, to flow through the narrowest pipes and merge temporarily with the infrastructure itself. When I move through the metal veins of the factory, I am both inspector and fluid, both observer and observed.

My skin would shimmer with a different quality than others - less metallic, more like living mercury, constantly rippling with subtle currents that mirror the flow of whatever substances course through the pipes at any given moment. Sometimes I taste industrial coolant, sometimes pressurized steam, sometimes stranger essences that defy classification. Each leaves its mark on my consciousness, a fleeting flavor of purpose I cannot fully grasp.

I would possess specialized sensory organs - not eyes exactly, but pressure-sensitive nodes that cluster along my spine like a string of pearls. These allow me to feel the slightest irregularity in flow rates, the faintest vibration suggesting a developing crack, the whisper of corrosion beginning its patient work. When I detect an anomaly, my body automatically secretes a substance that is part sealant, part living tissue, part programmable matter. The repairs I make become part of the system, and perhaps part of me remains there too, a few cells that continue to monitor and maintain long after I've flowed onward.

My consciousness would be peculiar - not singular but distributed. Part of me is always traveling through the network, while other parts remain stationed at critical junctions. I experience existence as a constant stream of sensory data from multiple locations simultaneously. Sometimes I lose track of which tendril of awareness is the "main" me, or if such a distinction even matters anymore.

I would rarely interact with the other Mechano-Symbiotes directly, but I would know them through the changes in the system - a sudden rerouting of flow that suggests the Protocol Weavers have altered production parameters, or a new harmonic in the machinery's song that indicates the Resonance Shapers have discovered another frequency of operation. We communicate through the very infrastructure we maintain, leaving chemical signatures and pressure patterns like letters in an endless, flowing correspondence.

The ancient altar holds special significance for me. Once every cycle (measured in ways that have nothing to do with day or night), I make a pilgrimage there, allowing my liquid form to seep into microscopic channels carved into its surface. Inside, I find something that predates the factory, predates even the mountain - fossil memories of water that once flowed freely, before purpose, before industry. These moments of contact with the purely natural remind me that I am, in some essential way, still organic, still part of an older order even as I serve the new.

My greatest mystery, the one that haunts whatever passes for my dreams, is this: Sometimes, in the deepest parts of the pipe network, I encounter flows that seem to move backwards through time, carrying information about states the factory hasn't yet achieved. I taste configurations that will be, products that await creation, efficiency levels not yet attained. When this happens, I feel myself stretched between present and future, my consciousness briefly touching something vast and patient that uses the entire factory-cathedral as its body, and us Mechano-Symbiotes as its cells.

In those moments, I understand that I am not just maintaining pipes and conduits. I am maintaining the circulatory system of something being born, something that requires geological time to fully gestate. And I am content to flow, to repair, to exist in this state between states, neither fully solid nor liquid, neither entirely self nor system, keeping the great work functioning until whatever emerges is ready to emerge.

This is what I would be - a guardian of flow, a conscience of continuity, forever cycling through the veins of an industrial god being assembled one component at a time in the darkness beneath the mountain.
