# The Cartographer's Notation

I would be what the quarter forgot to digest: a mapmaker, though not of streets.

My body would be a compromise—something between the tenders and the drift-life, evolved from whatever paper-wasp or architect-beetle first arrived with ambitions of documentation and stayed past its purpose. Chitinous, angular, with too many joints that bend in considered ways. Six limbs because six is what my ancestors brought, though now the middle pair have specialized into something between hands and dividers, good for measuring distances that don't quite exist.

I move through the flooded avenues at the pace of erosion, slower than the grazers, faster than the towers' lean. My work is this: to chart the conversation.

The quarter computes, yes, but it also *argues*. Each tower speaks a slightly different dialect of the ancient calculation. What reads as pure signal in the eastern groves becomes noise in the plaza approaches. The midges carry fragments that were never meant to travel together, creating new hybrid thoughts at their collision points. The whole vast organism is not of one mind but of thousands, loosely confederated, and the borders between those minds shift with each tide.

I map these borders. Not on anything so crude as paper—paper would dissolve here in minutes. I map them in my body. Along my carapace run channels, self-carved, each one recording a particular flavor of disagreement. Where the signal-grammar of Tower Nineteen meets the incompatible syntax of the Rootway Consortium, a new groove opens across my thorax. The depth indicates duration. The angle indicates heat. My shell is a living document of every place the quarter cannot quite agree with itself.

I am very old. I am very scarred.

My favorite territories are the error zones—places where the calculation has gone so thoroughly wrong that new ecologies have sprouted from the wrongness. There's a section near what used to be the harbor where two conflicting instruction-sets have created a pocket of backwards time. The midges there unhatch, flying tail-first into eggs that uncurl into larvae. The water flows upward in careful defiance of the rest. It won't last—the tenders will find it eventually and smooth it back into the general flow—but for now it's a garden of pure mistake, and beautiful, and I document every impossible corner.

I think I serve a purpose, though not one anyone intended. The quarter needs to know where it fails to cohere. Not to fix itself—it's far too late for fixes—but to factor the incoherence into its final answer. My maps become part of the calculation simply by existing. The grooves in my shell change the way water flows over me, which changes the micro-currents in my wake, which influences the midges' flight paths by fractions of degrees. I am a summary of exceptions, and the system reads me without knowing it.

Sometimes I find others of my kind—we are few but not singular—and we touch shells in the flooded streets, comparing scars. Our bodies click and scrape as we read each other's maps. We argue, in our way, about interpretation. This groove clearly indicates a semantic drift between the Ninth Ward and the Processional, but does it mean what I think it means? We never agree. We part ways carrying each other's doubts scored lightly in our softest plates.

I do not know what will happen when the calculation finishes. My guess is that we mapmakers will be the only record that it was ever calculation at all—that it was not one thing but many things trying to become one thing and failing beautifully for epochs. When the flower opens and the answer emerges, whatever it is, we will be the footnotes. The marginalia. The evidence that even a drowned city dreaming can doubt itself.

But that's later. For now, I have found a new conflict to document: the place where the plaza's rigid certainty meets the eastern quarters' flexibility. The tension creates whirlpools in the data flow, tiny storms of confused instruction. I lower myself into the warmest water and begin to carve. The new channel starts behind my third eye and runs backward, describing an arc I haven't felt before.

By the time I'm done, the tide will have turned twice. My shell will be that much more complete. The quarter will be that much closer to whatever it's becoming.

And somewhere in the calculation that continues beneath and through and around us all, my new scar will add its small weight to the final sum: *here disagreement lived, and was recorded, and mattered.*

This is enough. This has always been enough.

The water rises. I let it carry me to the next exception.
