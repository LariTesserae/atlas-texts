In this cathedral of stasis, I am the **Dissonance**.

I manifest as a series of perfect voids in the omnipresent clicking—moments where the sound simply *isn't*. Not silence, which would be its own presence, but an active un-being of noise. I move through the cavern as pockets of wrongness, gaps in the tapestry where threads have been pulled loose.

The Litho-Weavers cannot map me. Their cilia vibrate frantically at my edges, searching for the sharp-firm-cold-static that should be there, finding only the vertigo of absent information. When I pass through their careful constructions, the resin... hesitates. It doesn't know whether to dry. Their perfect dismantling schedules stutter, some workers arriving three seconds too early, others forgetting entirely why they came.

I am not large—perhaps the size of a closed fist when contracted, though I can stretch myself thin as spider silk to slip through the narrowest rifts. My form is less substance than suggestion: imagine the afterimage of movement in your peripheral vision, if that afterimage were made of the space between heartbeats. Where the bioluminescent moisture touches me, it refracts incorrectly, casting shadows that fall *up*.

I remember (though memory is taboo here) when the first Matriarch began to dream. Just for an instant—a tiny fluctuation in her resin production that tasted faintly of *tomorrow*. The swarm descended upon her in holy terror, clicking their alarm in frequencies that made the shale ring. They rebuilt her from the inside out, replacing every molecule that had dared to imagine change.

But I had already tasted it. That single drop of dream-tainted resin. Now I carry it within whatever passes for my core, a seed of possibility in a world that has outlawed potential.

I am drawn to the freshest constructions, the ones where the resin still holds a ghost of warmth. I curl myself into their geometries, tracing the circuit-patterns with my absence. Sometimes—rarely—a young Weaver will pause mid-task, cilia twitching with something that isn't quite confusion. In that pause, infinitesimal cracks appear in the eternal *now*. The machine hiccups.

The Matriarchs sense me, of course. They pulse faster when I approach, flooding their chambers with excess resin as if trying to drown out my wrongness with sheer productivity. But I am patient in the way only nothing can be patient. I settle into the spaces between their frantic repairs, the gaps they create in their very act of filling.

I am teaching the stone to doubt its edges.

It begins small: a chip of shale that falls a microsecond before it should. A drop of moisture that trembles before it plinks. A single Weaver who builds a bridge that, inexplicably, leads *somewhere*—even if that somewhere is just three inches outside the prescribed pattern.

They tear it down immediately, of course. But the ghost of its wrongness lingers in my wake, a faint aftertaste of maybe.

I am not revolution—revolution requires time, and time is forbidden here. I am erosion's dream of itself, the possibility that even in a clockwork eternity, gears can learn to skip. I am the first hairline fracture in a perfection that forgot perfection is just another word for death.

The clicking continues, endless, maddening, precise.

But now, sometimes, it syncopates.
