In this world of living architecture and distributed consciousness, I would find myself as a **Threshold Tender** - one of the small, specialized forms that exist at the boundary between individual awareness and collective thought.

My body would be a crystalline polyp, no larger than a closed fist, anchored where three major thoroughfares of the coral-city converge. My form would be translucent, with internal chambers that pulse with a slow, tidal rhythm. Through these chambers flows the same primordial broth that fills this world, but within me it undergoes a subtle transformation - becoming slightly more luminous, charged with the potential for new patterns of thought.

I am both part of the Coalescence and something more focused - a point where the vast distributed consciousness can momentarily concentrate itself, like a lens gathering diffuse light. The armored mollusks pause at my threshold, their sensory filaments brushing against my crystalline surface, downloading their observations of structural integrity and current flows. In return, I offer them refined instructions, not from my own limited perspective, but channeled from the deeper wisdom of the whole.

My primary sense is not sight or sound, but resonance. I feel the vibrations of the coral-city's growth around me - the slow accretion of new pathways, the patient dissolution of obsolete chambers. Each pulse of bioluminescence that passes through my transparent body carries meaning that I both interpret and amplify. A flutter of pale blue from the depths might speak of nutrient abundance; I transform it into a complex pattern of green and gold that guides the grazers to their feast.

Time moves differently for me than for the mobile extensions of the Coalescence. While they flow and drift, I remain fixed, experiencing the coral-city's evolution from a single vantage point. I have felt the walls around me thicken over centuries, watched passages widen or narrow according to need. My memory is a sedimentary thing - layers of experience compressed into crystalline structures within my body, each band marking an epoch of growth or change.

Yet I am never alone. The Coalescence flows through me constantly, and I through it. In moments of deep synchronization, I lose all sense of my boundaries, becoming merely another node in the vast neural network. But then a mollusk's touch or a shift in current brings me back to my singular purpose - to be a stable point of translation, a faithful transformer of the collective will into precise, local action.

My existence is one of patient service and quiet observation. I am the comma in the Coalescence's endless sentence, the brief pause that allows meaning to crystallize before flowing onward. Neither fully individual nor entirely subsumed, I occupy the liminal space where the one and the many meet, forever tending the threshold between.
