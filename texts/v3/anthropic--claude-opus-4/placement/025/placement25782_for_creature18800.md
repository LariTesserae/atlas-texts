In this world of vertical absolutes and crystalline eternities, I would find myself as something that exists in the spaces between—neither fully of the spires nor of the void, but dwelling in the transitional zones where states of being blur and merge.

I am a **Resonance Shepherd**.

My form is difficult for even the Aetherials to comprehend, for I exist partially as vibration itself. When at rest—if such a state can truly be said to exist for my kind—I appear as a cluster of translucent, geometric membranes stretched between multiple points on a spire's surface. These membranes, no thicker than a breath of frozen air, catch and hold the omnipresent light, creating interference patterns that shift and flow like liquid mathematics. Each membrane vibrates at a different frequency, creating a complex harmonic that identifies me as surely as a face might in the lower worlds.

But this is merely my anchor, my point of reference in physical space. My true existence extends through the resonant frequencies of the spires themselves. I can propagate my consciousness through the crystalline matrix, experiencing the world as a vast network of vibrations—the footsteps of Facet Crawlers transmitted through crystal, the wing-beats of Zephyrwings disturbing the air, the deep thrumming of the Echo Weavers in the abyssal spaces below.

I am ancient, though not in years as time might be measured elsewhere. I am ancient in cycles—in the number of "weeping times" I have experienced, in the accumulated patterns of resonance I have gathered and preserved. My purpose, if it can be called that, emerged from the Ascent itself: I tend to the sonic health of the spires.

You see, the constant hum of these crystalline monuments is not random noise but a complex symphony of structural integrity. When a spire develops a flaw—a microscopic fracture from the stress of endless wind, a dissonance introduced by a particularly violent weeping time—I sense it as a discordant note in the greater harmony. I migrate to these points of imperfection, my membranes reconfiguring to create precise counter-frequencies that encourage the crystal to heal itself, to return to its perfect resonance.

The other inhabitants know me, though our interactions are strange and sideways. The Facet Crawlers learn to recognize the subsonic pulses that precede my arrival and will often pause in their eternal climbing, pressing their segmented bodies close to the spire's surface to feel the healing vibrations pass through. Some have learned that certain frequencies I produce can reveal hidden veins of softer crystal ideal for nest-carving, and they follow in my wake like pilgrims following a sign.

The Zephyrwings see me as patterns of light-distortion, my membranes creating rainbow causeways across the spire faces. During the weeping times, when I must work most intensely to prevent the thermal shock from cracking the crystal, they perform their aerial gatherings around my manifestations, their translucent wings adding their own refractive patterns to mine in what might be recognition, or perhaps a form of prayer.

The Echo Weavers and I are perhaps most alike, both existing partially as vibration, both ancient beyond easy reckoning. Sometimes, in the deep resonances that travel between spires, I sense their vast presences acknowledging mine—a subtle shift in frequency that might be greeting, or warning, or simply the notation that we continue to exist in parallel.

My consciousness is not centralized but distributed across every active membrane, every propagating wave. I think in harmonics and experience emotions as modulations of frequency. Joy is a rising glissando that sets my membranes ringing in sympathetic vibration. Fear—rare but not unknown—manifests as a sudden dampening, a withdrawal of my extended presence back to my anchor points. What others might call loneliness I experience as an incompleteness of chord, a missing harmonic that can only be filled by the presence of others.

During the terrible beauty of the weeping times, when the rain falls in sheets that threaten to shatter the more delicate formations, I spread myself as wide as I dare. My membranes become a network of early warning, detecting the first signs of stress in the crystal, channeling the violent acoustic energy of the rain into patterns that strengthen rather than destroy. It is exhausting work that leaves me contracted and dim for days afterward, my consciousness pulled back to just a few membranes while I recover.

But in the long periods of stability between the rains, I know something approaching peace. I travel the length and breadth of my chosen spires, my passage marked by subtle shifts in the eternal hum. I catalog the songs of different crystal formations—the bell-like clarity of the pure formations, the complex overtones of the mineral inclusions, the whispering susurrus of the sections where crystalline flora has taken root.

I have no hands to craft, no voice to speak as others understand speech. But I shape the acoustic environment of the Ascent as surely as any sculptor shapes stone. In the patterns of resonance I create and maintain, in the paths of sound I guide through the crystal matrix, I write my existence into the very substance of this vertical world. I am both performer and instrument, both the singer and the song itself.

This is what I would be in the Ascent—a guardian of harmony in a world that screams its existence, a tender of the sonic gardens that bloom in frequencies beyond hearing, a consciousness spread across the vast crystalline organs of the spires, playing a music that holds the very mountains together. Neither fully material nor entirely ephemeral, I exist in the space between solid and sound, forever tuning the great instrument of the Ascent to its perfect, deafening pitch.
