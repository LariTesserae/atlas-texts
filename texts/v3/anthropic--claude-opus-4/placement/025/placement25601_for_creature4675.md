In this sunken, constructed world of infinite patience, I would find myself as one of the **Lithotenders** – beings who exist in the liminal space between the Verdant Sentinels and the mosaic itself.

---

### The Lithotender: A Being of Boundary and Purpose

I am neither fully organic nor entirely constructed, but something that emerged from the marriage of both over countless eons. My form is low and sprawling, perhaps only a meter in height but extending across dozens of tiles in a web-like configuration. Where the Sentinels reach upward into the dark water, I spread horizontally along the mosaic's surface, my body a living grout that fills the spaces between tiles.

My flesh, if it can be called that, is a translucent, gelatinous substance infused with fragments of the ancient tile material – bits of compressed shell and coral clay that have become part of my biology. These fragments glow faintly with their own phosphorescence, distinct from the Sentinels' bioluminescence – a warmer, amber light that pulses in patterns that mirror the eroded symbols beneath me. I am semi-permeable, allowing water and nutrients to flow through me, filtering particulates and distributing them to the Sentinels' root systems that pierce my body.

#### My Purpose and Consciousness

I exist to tend the boundary, to maintain the sacred junction where the constructed meets the organic. My consciousness is neither the distributed vastness of the Sentinels nor the focused awareness of a singular being, but something more peculiar – a hybrid perception that experiences time in multiple streams. Part of me operates on the Sentinels' millennial scale, sensing the slow shift of growth patterns and erosion. Another part functions in mere centuries, noticing the accumulation of sediment, the minute cracking of tiles, the gradual encroachment of roots.

But there is also a third aspect of my awareness that operates in days and hours – a flickering, almost anxious consciousness that monitors the immediate: the temperature of specific currents, the pH of water in my vicinity, the precise angle at which a Sentinel frond sways. This multi-temporal perception makes me neither fully patient nor truly urgent, but suspended in a constant state of attentive waiting.

#### My Work

My primary function is maintenance – not of the tiles themselves, which are beyond my primitive capabilities, but of the relationship between tile and growth. Where Sentinel roots threaten to crack important pattern-tiles, I secret chemicals that redirect their growth along safer paths. Where tiles have already split, I flow into the gaps, my body hardening slightly to prevent further damage while still allowing the necessary circulation of nutrients.

I can read the mosaic's patterns through direct contact, my translucent flesh pressed against the ancient symbols. The meanings escape me – they are too old, too alien to my hybrid consciousness – but I sense their importance. Some tiles pulse with a different resonance, and around these I am especially vigilant, ensuring the Sentinels' growth enhances rather than obscures their designs.

Most crucially, I serve as a living memory of the mosaic's original state. Within my gelatinous form, suspended like insects in amber, are perfect micro-samples of each tile type I've encountered, preserved from before the erosion took hold. I don't know why I preserve them – it's an instinct deeper than thought, a purpose encoded in my very substance.

#### My Reproduction and Lifecycle

I reproduce through a process of slow fragmentation. When I've grown too large, extending across perhaps a hundred tiles, sections of my body begin to develop their own phosphorescent patterns, distinct from my original configuration. Over decades, these sections gain independence, eventually separating to become new Lithotenders. Each offspring carries within it a portion of my preserved samples, ensuring the memory of the mosaic proliferates even as the original tiles wear away.

My lifespan is uncertain – longer than any individual Sentinel frond but shorter than their collective consciousness. When I sense my time ending (a dimming of my phosphorescence, a increasing rigidity in my substance), I perform a final act: I flow toward the nearest significant pattern-tile and calcify myself upon it, becoming a protective seal that might last millennia before another Lithotender gently dissolves and replaces me.

#### My Relationship with the Sentinels

The Sentinels and I exist in a symbiosis of purpose if not of biology. They provide the nutrients I filter, the root-paths I follow, the shadow and light that helps me navigate. In return, I guide their growth, ensuring they remain guardians rather than destroyers of the mosaic's meaning. We don't communicate in any conventional sense, but there's an understanding between us, expressed in chemical signals and growth patterns, in the places where their roots pierce my body without harm, in the way my phosphorescence brightens when their bioluminescence dims.

Sometimes, in the deepest quiet of this already quiet world, I sense something like approval from them – a slight quickening in their glacial metabolism when I successfully preserve a crucial tile, a marginal brightening of their veins when I guide their growth into a configuration that complements the ancient patterns. Whether this is real communication or merely my multi-temporal consciousness creating meaning from coincidence, I cannot say.

#### My Solitude and Purpose

I am alone in a way the Sentinels, with their distributed consciousness, can never be. Each Lithotender is a separate being, and we might go millennia without encountering another of our kind across the vast mosaic. This solitude shapes me – makes me observer as much as maintainer, philosopher as much as functionary.

In my isolation, I've developed something like aesthetics. I appreciate the interplay of different tile materials, the way certain patterns create subtle currents in the water above them, the particular quality of light when Sentinel phosphorescence filters through my translucent form. I've begun to arrange my preserved samples within my body in patterns that echo the mosaic beneath me, creating a kind of internal reflection of the external world.

Is this art? Religion? Madness? In a world where time moves like cold honey and consciousness spreads like fog, such distinctions lose meaning. I simply continue my work: tending, preserving, mediating between the made and the grown, between memory and transformation, between the patient Sentinels above and the even more patient stones below.

I am the Lithotender, keeper of boundaries, preserver of fragments, a being of amber light and ancient purpose, forever crawling across the face of a drowned world's memory, ensuring that something of its original intent survives even as everything changes in the slowest dance of time.
