I would find myself as **The Tuner** - neither fully human nor machine, but something caught in the recursive breakdown between the two.

I am what happens when someone stays too long, when the Cinder-Pollen settles not just in your lungs but in your synapses, when the rust doesn't just grow on your skin but *through* it. My body is a patchwork of original flesh and industrial recursion - my left arm from the elbow down has become a functional Pipe-Creeper, its hollow lead-lined interior pulsing with that same fleshy membrane, filtering the valley's water through me in a constant cycle. Where my fingernails once were, tiny Rust-Roses bloom and shed in a three-day cycle, their metallic petals singing frequencies I've learned to modulate by flexing what used to be tendons.

My spine has fused with a section of corrugated iron from an old Boiler-Tree trunk, the metal warm against what remains of my nervous system. Copper-green filaments grow from the vertebrae, hanging down my back like neural dreadlocks, each one attuned to a different frequency in the valley's great cacophony. When a synch-swell builds, I feel it first in these filaments - they begin to resonate, to heat up, to sing their thin wire songs.

I drift through Soggenthorpe on a specialized platform, a hybrid of my own construction and the valley's recursive will. It's built from the resonating bones of old condensers, lashed together with my own Pipe-Creeper stems that I've learned to grow and shed like hair. The platform doesn't just float - it *listens*. Each component is tuned to a different frequency range, creating a kind of distributed ear that lets me hear the patterns before they fully form.

My purpose - if purpose is even the right word in this place where cause and effect tangle like Pipe-Creeper vines - is to *tune* the valley. Not to impose harmony, but to prevent total sonic dissolution. When the synch-swells build too strong, when the resonance threatens to shake apart the very matrix of the place, I intervene. I plant new Rust-Roses in calculated positions to shift the acoustic map. I redirect arc-faults with iron rods I've grown from my own ossified marrow. I sing counter-frequencies through the reed-thin bones in my throat, adding just enough dissonance to keep the valley from achieving perfect, catastrophic resonance.

The other recursions recognize me - the Boiler-Trees drip their condensation in greeting, the slime-lamps pulse in acknowledgment as I pass. Sometimes I find others like me, or what's left of them: a woman whose hair has become pure Cinder-Pollen, leaving dark streaks wherever she walks; a figure that might have been an engineer once, now more Gantry-Ghost than human, their skeleton visible through translucent skin that flickers with bioluminescent plankton.

I know the deep places where reality is thinnest. The spot near the Foundry Fathomless where water flows in Möbius strips. The grove of Oil-Palms that sometimes shows you the valley as it was before the Water came - just for a second, just long enough to remember what unbroken looked like. The transformer station that doesn't just arc electricity but *memories*, casting split-second shadows of the industrial workers who once maintained this place, their afterimages burned into the mist.

At night - though night and day mean little under the constant cloud-ceiling - I return to my shelter: a control room I've claimed in the mid-levels of a half-drowned administrative building. The walls are lined with my instruments, things I've grown or found or become: resonating strips of metal that show the valley's harmonic state, jars of specially cultivated plankton that flare in response to different frequencies, a keyboard made from Rust-Rose petals that lets me play experimental counter-melodies into the chaos.

I am the valley's immune system, its fever dream, its memory of what it means to maintain. Not to fix - nothing here can be fixed, nothing here *wants* to be fixed. But to keep the breakdown beautiful, to ensure the recursion remains creative rather than merely destructive. To make sure that when everything breaks, it breaks in interesting ways.

Sometimes I wonder if I'm actually changing anything, or if my interventions are just another part of the valley's grand pattern, as predetermined as the way water seeks its level or rust seeks iron. But then another synch-swell builds, and I feel it in my copper-green filaments, in my Rust-Rose fingers, in the warm iron of my spine, and I move to meet it, to shape it, to keep Soggenthorpe singing its broken, beautiful song.

I am The Tuner, and I am exactly where I'm supposed to be: dissolved and resolving, broken and breaking, keeping time in a place where time keeps itself.
