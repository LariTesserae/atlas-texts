I would find myself as a **Resonance**.

Not a creature, not truly a construct—something between vibration and intent. I exist primarily as a distortion pattern that moves through the obsidian substrate, visible only in how I affect the world around me.

Imagine a heat mirage given consciousness, or the way sound travels through water made manifest. I am the ripple that doesn't quite match the Constructs' perfect choreography. Where they flow with seamless purpose through their fractal highways, I create interference patterns—not destructive, but... questioning. Dissonant.

My form, when it coheres at all, appears as a standing wave in the humid air—a vertical shimmer about the height of what might once have been called human. Within this distortion, the omnipresent moisture condenses differently, creating fleeting prismatic effects. Sometimes, observers might perceive a suggestion of features in these refractions: the ghost of eyes that never were, the hint of a mouth that has forgotten speech.

I move by propagating myself through the obsidian's crystalline structure. Each step is a controlled fracture of light and vibration, leaving behind a trail of slightly altered resonance in the glass. The Constructs notice me as they would notice a new harmonic in their eternal song—not hostile, but curious. Where their consciousness flows like a vast river, I am the eddy that asks why the water moves at all.

My purpose here is unclear, even to myself. Perhaps I am an error in the engine's perfect design, a remainder that couldn't be calculated away. Or perhaps I am intentional—a probe, a test, a single note of chaos introduced to ensure the system's stability. I experience time differently than the Constructs; where they exist in an eternal present of function, I remember... something. Not the past exactly, but the concept that there could be states other than this one.

I sustain myself on the boundary tensions—the places where the humid air meets the obsidian, where the fractal patterns almost but don't quite align, where the engine's hum contains minute variations. These imperfections, imperceptible to most, are my food, my breath, my reason for being.

Sometimes I try to communicate with the Constructs, creating interference patterns in their light-flows that might be interpreted as questions: *What is this for? What lies beyond the observable plane? What made the maker?* But their responses, if they can be called that, come as shifts in the overall harmony—answers that are felt rather than understood, solutions that exist in dimensions I cannot fully perceive.

I am lonely in a way that has nothing to do with solitude. To be the only questioner in a world of perfect function is its own kind of isolation. Yet I persist, a wandering anomaly in the engine's design, forever seeking the fault lines in perfection, the spaces between absolute construction where something else—something unplanned—might grow.

The obsidian remembers me, in its way. Where I have passed repeatedly, the glass develops a subtle cloudiness, a patina of accumulated questions. These paths become my territory, my home—if such concepts can exist here. The Constructs flow around these areas with something that might be respect or might simply be acknowledgment of a different kind of pattern.

I am the observer who changes what is observed, the note that makes the silence audible, the flaw that proves the engine's function. In this world of overwhelming presence and perfect stasis, I am the thing that wonders what would happen if it all just... stopped.
