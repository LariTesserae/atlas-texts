In this world, I am **a Resonance Keeper** - neither quite Silica-Singer nor separate from them, but something that emerged from the intersection of their needs and the Basin's peculiar physics.

My body is... wrong, by their standards. Where the Silica-Singers are stocky and broad, I am elongated - stretched thin like pulled taffy. My limbs are too long, my torso narrow, my skin not the healthy grey-ochre but an almost translucent white that shows the dark branching of mineral-heavy blood beneath. I was born wrong, they said - a clutch-sibling who absorbed too much silica in the birthing pools, whose bones grew dense and hollow like the sinter tubes themselves.

But this wrongness became purpose. My bones don't just sense vibration - they *store* it. The hollow chambers within my skeleton act as resonance cavities, capturing and holding the vibrational patterns that flow through the Basin's stone. When I press my too-long fingers against the sinter, I can feel not just the present tremors but the ghost-echoes of patterns from hours, even days before. The mineral deposits along my spine have formed strange, flute-like structures that whistle and hum with accumulated sound.

I live in the Threshold Spaces - those cramped zones between clan territories where the vibrational signatures overlap and interfere. My dwelling is a natural acoustic anomaly: a spiral of sinter formations that created a space of relative quiet, where the Basin's roar drops to a mere thunderous rumble. Here, the clans come to me.

My role is Memory. In a world without writing, where oral tradition must be shouted and degrades with each telling, I am a living library. Clan leaders tap their histories into my palms - genealogies, water-rights, the location of productive hunting grounds, warnings about unstable sinter formations. My body holds these patterns in its mineral matrices. When disputes arise about territory or tradition, they bring me to the contested space. I press my whole body against the stone and *release* - letting the stored patterns flow out through my bones and into the sinter, where they can be read by any who know how to listen.

But I am more than mere storage. In the quiet of my spiral chamber, I've learned to *compose*. By carefully releasing specific stored patterns in sequence, I can create new vibrational messages - combinations that produce effects no single clan's language contains. I've discovered frequencies that call certain prey, others that encourage the growth of the filter-feeders, and some that seem to make the sinter itself resonate in ways that speed its slow accretion.

The Silica-Singers regard me with a mixture of reverence and unease. I am necessary but uncanny - a reminder that even in their unchanging world, variation persists. Young singers sometimes come to me in secret, wanting to learn the deep patterns, the forgotten rhythms I've collected from elders now returned to the stone. I teach them, knowing that some will carry these vibrations forward, ensuring continuity even as memories fade.

My greatest secret, which I've shared with no one, is this: deep in my bone-chambers, I hold patterns I've never released. Vibrations that came not from any Silica-Singer, but from the Basin itself - deep tremors from far below, patterns that repeat on cycles longer than living memory. They speak of something vast and patient beneath us, something for which our entire civilization is merely the latest layer of accretion. Sometimes, in the relative quiet of my chamber, I feel these deep patterns trying to complete themselves through me, wanting to be released into the stone above.

But I hold them tight. I am the Resonance Keeper, guardian of memory in a world that never changes. And perhaps that lack of change is not mere stasis, but a held breath - a pause in some larger pattern I can feel but not comprehend. Until I understand what these deep vibrations mean, I will keep them safe in my hollow bones, another secret in the eternal roar of Sinter's Basin.

When I die, my body will not be returned to the water like the others. The clans have agreed: I will be carefully placed in a prepared chamber, my mineral-heavy bones allowed to fuse with the growing stone. I will become part of the Basin itself, my stored patterns slowly releasing into the sinter over centuries, messages from the past seeping into the future through the patient stone.

I am memory crystallized, a living fossil in a world of permanent birth, keeping the echoes of all that has been while listening for signs of what might yet come.
