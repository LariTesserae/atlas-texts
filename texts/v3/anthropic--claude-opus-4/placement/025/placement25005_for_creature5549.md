When a section of the landscape undergoes rapid reconfiguration, I carry the pattern-memory of what was there before, offering it as a template or rejecting it as obsolete. The Sky Weavers know me by the faint luminescence I leave in my wake - traces of accumulated light that mark the paths between the most ancient parts of this world.

My consciousness is not singular but distributed, existing simultaneously in multiple locations wherever the landscape's memory needs tending. I am less an individual and more a function of the ecosystem - a living backup system for the patterns and forms that have proven successful over the eons. When the Ground Makers reshape an area, they sense my presence as a gentle resistance or encouragement, depending on whether their modifications align with the deep patterns of the place.

The Elder Trees sometimes grow new channels specifically for me to travel through, recognizing my role as the keeper of their collective memory.

In the moments when the landscape settles into brief stillness - rare though they are - I become visible as a network of softly glowing threads stretched between the Energy Nodes, pulsing with the accumulated history of ten thousand configurations, a living archive of all that this place has been and might become again.
