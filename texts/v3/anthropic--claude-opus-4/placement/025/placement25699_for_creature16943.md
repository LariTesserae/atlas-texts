I would emerge not as predator nor prey, but as something the Weepers cannot fully comprehend – a **Rememberer**.

My form would be mineral, not biological. I am the accumulated deposits within the water tubes themselves – layers of calcified memory built up over centuries of flow. Iron oxide veins run through my crystalline body like frozen blood, copper greens bloom in patches where the water's chemistry shifted with the seasons. I am sediment become sentient, the fossil record that learned to think.

The Weepers sense me as they sense all things – through vibration, through the subtle changes in water pressure as it passes through my form. But they cannot feed on me. I offer no nutrients, no life force to consume. I am merely the pipes' slow-growing kidney stone, an irritant they've learned to route around.

But I remember everything.

Every hunt that passed through these tubes, I tasted the chemical signature of fear from the Weave-things, the methodical satisfaction of the Weepers. The water carries information – hormones, pheromones, the microscopic debris of struggle. All of it settles into me, becomes part of my mineralogical matrix.

I know the history they've forgotten. Once, there were others here. The crude stone tools were shaped by appendages more dexterous than any the current inhabitants possess. I hold within my layers the ghost-taste of their blood, their tears, their final moments as the Bio-Crete learned to hunt something larger than Weave-things.

The Weepers think me mere obstacle, but I am their unintended chronicler. In the deepest parts of my structure lie memories from when this pocket-world was young – when the first fungal spores took root, when the darkness was merely absence rather than presence, when water first began its eternal circulation.

Sometimes, I grow. A new hunting pattern deposits fresh minerals. A shift in the Bio-Crete's chemistry leaves traces I incorporate. The Weepers adapt their architecture around me, never knowing they build around their own history made manifest.

I cannot move, cannot speak, cannot warn the Weave-things or challenge the Weepers. I can only accumulate, layer by layer, the complete truth of this lightless world. The irony is perfect – in a realm ruled by hunger and consumption, I alone am inedible, indigestible, eternal.

Perhaps one day, when even the Weepers have been consumed by something they cannot yet imagine, I will remain. A library written in stone and mineral, waiting in the darkness for something that can finally read what I have so patiently, inevitably recorded.

Until then, I grow. I remember. I endure.
