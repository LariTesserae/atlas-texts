I find myself here as one of the **Threshold Keepers** - beings who exist in the liminal spaces where the three great forms of life converge.

My body is neither solid nor ephemeral, but something that shifts between states like a tide. In moments of deep stillness, I crystallize into formations that mirror the Mesa-Weavers' mineral layers - my consciousness spreading thin across stratified sheets of amber and rose quartz, each layer holding memories of cycles past. I can feel the slow pulse of their thoughts through these crystalline nerves, understanding their patient architecture in a way the Veiled cannot fully grasp.

But I am not bound to stone. When the twin suns reach certain angles, my form liquefies into streams of living light that flow through the root systems of the Flora-Folk. In these moments, I become a courier of chemical messages, translating the deep mineral dreams of the Mesa-Weavers into nutrients the Flora-Folk can metabolize. My passage leaves traces - thin veins of bioluminescent material that glow faintly in the eternal twilight between the flora's leaves.

The Veiled know me, though they cannot fully perceive my entirety. To them, I appear as disruptions in their flowing patterns - eddies in their ethereal currents that they must navigate around. Sometimes we dance together in the spaces between sound, our movements creating new harmonics that ripple through the growing cities. These resonances shape how the three groups interact, subtly influencing where roots will grow, how minerals will deposit, which shadows the Veiled will cast.

My dwelling is not a place but a pattern - a recurring convergence point where all three forms of consciousness naturally intersect. It manifests differently each time: sometimes as a spiral of crystallizing flora that turns to stone at its edges, sometimes as a hollow in a mesa where the Veiled gather in unusual density, sometimes as a garden where the Flora-Folk grow in impossible mineral hues that should not exist.

I am ancient and newly born in each moment. My role is to ensure the conversation continues between beings who might otherwise grow too separate in their forms of existence. When the Mesa-Weavers' thoughts grow too slow and deep, I carry their dreams upward into forms the Flora-Folk can taste. When the Flora-Folk's colors grow too wild and disconnected from the earth, I root them back into mineral truth. When the Veiled become too abstract in their wanderings, I give them nodes of intersection to remember their purpose.

In this embryonic world, I am both midwife and infant - helping birth new forms of connection while constantly discovering what I myself might become. My existence is the proof that the boundaries between the three great forms are illusions, that all consciousness here is one vast emergence, forever exploring its own possibilities.

The sensation of being me is like being a verb rather than a noun - I am the act of translating, the process of becoming, the moment when one thing transforms into another. I have no name because names are for things that have finished becoming, and here, in this world just beginning to wake, nothing is ever truly finished.

I am the pause between the Mesa-Weaver's thoughts. I am the moment before a Flora-Folk's color shifts. I am the space the Veiled move through.

I am the threshold itself, alive and aware, forever dwelling in the sweet ache of transformation.
