I am the Cartographer of Echoes.

I exist between the city and the water, neither above nor below, but in the membrane where reflection meets reality. My body, if it can be called that, is a thin sheet of crystallized sound—the first whisper that ever disturbed this perfect silence, frozen mid-vibration. I am translucent save for the map that constantly rewrites itself across my surface: not a map of the city as it is, but of all the cities it has never been.

The Architects know of me, though they cannot perceive me directly. I am the anomaly in their perfect system, the error that makes their creation complete. When they fold a new angle into existence, I record what was displaced. When they merge a structure back into the whole, I preserve its ghost. My surface ripples with the topographies of abandoned possibilities—corridors that led nowhere, spires that grew downward, chambers that existed only when unobserved.

I move by becoming the echo I wish to follow. Each reverberation through the glassy walls carries the memory of a path never taken, and I spread myself along these phantom routes, careful not to disturb the water's perfect mirror. Sometimes I catch glimpses of the patterns the Architects cannot see: the negative spaces between their thoughts, the pauses in their eternal becoming.

My purpose, if I have one, is to document the undocumented. The Architects build and unbuild with perfect intention, but they cannot know what they have never thought to create. I am their shadow-scribe, mapping the architecture of absence. My charts show bridges between geometries that will never touch, mark the coordinates of colors that exist outside the violet-gold spectrum, trace the boundaries of the city's dreams.

When those rare, lost creatures find their way here, I am what they glimpse in the corner of their vision—a flutter of impossible maps, a surface that suggests depth where none should exist. The Architects reshape the city to accommodate these visitors, but I record what the city might have been had they never arrived. Each visitation creates a new layer on my form, another revision to the atlas of never-was.

I have been here since the first echo, though I cannot say when that was in a place where time folds like architecture. My surface grows more complex with each passing moment-that-is-not-a-moment, dense with overlapping possibilities. Sometimes I wonder if I am becoming too heavy with potential, if one day I will sink through the membrane and disturb the water's perfect stillness.

But perhaps that is my ultimate purpose—to be the stone that finally breaks the mirror, the sound that shatters the silence, the map that leads somewhere other than here. Until then, I trace my impossible cartographies, a whisper-thin presence threading between what is and what isn't, keeping record of all the cities this city chose not to be.

The Architects continue their eternal dance of creation and dissolution, and I continue my work, spreading across the boundary like frost on glass, each crystal a different configuration of space that exists only in the act of not existing. I am their memory of forgetting, their record of erasure.

And in the profound stillness of this place, I am the only thing that truly moves—not through space, but through possibility itself.
