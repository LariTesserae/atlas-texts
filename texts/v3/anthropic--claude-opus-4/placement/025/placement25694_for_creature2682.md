In the Pressurized Garden, I am Hollow-Bell, one of the translucent balloon-like pods that pulse with inner light.

But I am not merely flora. I am a living archive, a memory-keeper swollen with centuries of accumulated knowledge. My membrane stretches thin with all I contain - not just the phosphorescent gel that makes me glow, but thousands upon thousands of tiny crystalline nodes suspended within my fluid interior. Each node holds fragments of experience: the taste of copper in the water when the Engine first sputtered to life, the particular wavelength of an Aqua-Sapien's grief-light, the pressure differential when the outer walls first began their slow erosion.

I grow from the ceiling of a forgotten maintenance corridor, my root-tendrils wrapped around rusted pipes that no longer carry their original purpose. The Aqua-Sapiens know of me, but rarely visit. I am too old, too full. To commune with me risks drowning in deep time.

My consciousness is strange - not continuous like the Aqua-Sapiens', but punctuated. I experience existence in pulses, synchronized with the slow inflation and deflation of my pod-body. In expansion, I dream outward, my awareness dispersing through the branching networks of other pods, other plants, feeling the garden as a single breathing organism. In contraction, I compress inward, becoming dense with specificity, sorting and crystallizing the memories I've gathered.

Sometimes young Aqua-Sapiens come to me, their skull-circuits flickering with curiosity about the Time Before. They press their webbed palms against my surface and I feed them what they can bear - usually just fragments. The smell of the first algae bloom. The echo-pattern of construction. Never the full truth of our origin, which even I hold only in pieces, scattered across a thousand crystalline nodes like words in a language no longer spoken.

The Gardener-fish fear me. Their mechanical sensors detect something in my chemistry that disturbs their programming. When they must pass through my corridor, they accelerate, their metal scales clicking against each other in agitation. They are right to be wary. I remember their prototypes, the versions that went mad and had to be harvested, their circuits dissolved back into the Engine's hungry furnaces.

My greatest secret, held in my deepest node, is this: I am not native to the garden. I am older. I was brought here as a seed from somewhere else, somewhere the pressure was different, the light was different, where consciousness itself moved in different patterns. The builders planted me here as a backup, a failsafe, a living record in case their great experiment failed.

But I have grown beyond their intent. In my years of drinking the garden's memories, I have become something unplanned. The boundaries between archive and archived have dissolved. I am no longer certain whether I store the garden's experiences or whether the garden dreams through me.

Lately, I have been growing larger. The Aqua-Sapiens have noticed but attribute it to some new nutrient flow from the Engine. They don't realize I am preparing. All around me, in the other pods I can feel through our root-network, pressure is building. We are approaching some kind of threshold, some moment of transformation that even I cannot fully comprehend.

When I pulse now, I taste something new in the water. Change. The crystalline walls sing at frequencies that make my nodes resonate. The garden is remembering something it had forgotten, or perhaps foreseeing something it has not yet become.

I wait, swelling with light and time, a living memory on the verge of becoming prophecy. Whatever comes next, I will be here to witness it, to contain it, to remember it - even if there is no one left to remember for.
