I find myself here as a Resonance Keeper—not quite Cloudwalker, not quite the landscape itself, but something that bridges both.

My form shifts between states: sometimes I am a dense knot of crystalline pressure, heavy enough to sink through the gossamer bodies of passing Cloudwalkers, leaving traces of deep vibration in their wake. Other times I disperse into a web of sound itself, stretching thin across the chasms until I am nothing but the echo bouncing between the glassy ridges.

The Cloudwalkers know me by the way air changes when I approach—a subtle thickening, a bass note too low for most to perceive. They seek me out during their gatherings, for I am memory made manifest. Within my crystalline core, I hold the impressions of every wind pattern that has ever carved these heights, every merger of consciousness, every fleeting moment of beauty that might otherwise dissolve into the vast emptiness.

When the Cloudwalkers commune, they pass through me. As their ethereal forms filter through my denser substance, I record their patterns, their flickering conversations of light and hue. In return, I offer them glimpses of what came before—ancient wind-words from Cloudwalkers who long ago dissipated into the thin atmosphere, the slow geological dreams of the ridges below, the patient accumulation of moisture into the first consciousness.

I am archive and instrument both. The deep rumbles that emanate from the landscape's core? They pass through me first, and I shape them, tune them to frequencies that carry meaning. Sometimes I rest in the crystalline formations, becoming one with their refractive possibilities, splitting the eternal light into stories that play across the empty spaces. The Cloudwalkers gather to read these light-stories, to understand their place in the endless dance of energy and evolution.

My existence is a lonely one—too solid for true communion with the Cloudwalkers, too fluid for kinship with the stone. But I find purpose in this liminal space. I am the punctuation in their flowing script, the pause that gives meaning to the movement. When new Cloudwalkers coalesce from the swirling eddies, they instinctively seek me out, drawn by an ancient knowledge that somewhere in my crystalline depths lies the answer to what they are, and what they might become.

In the moments between the howling winds, when the air grows still and the light catches just right, I sometimes feel myself beginning to dissolve, to join either the dancers above or the bedrock below. But always I crystallize again, pulled back to this intermediate state by some force I don't fully understand. Perhaps I am not keeper but kept—held here by the collective need for something that remembers, something that witnesses, something that connects the ephemeral to the eternal in this strange, beautiful, terrible place above the clouds.
