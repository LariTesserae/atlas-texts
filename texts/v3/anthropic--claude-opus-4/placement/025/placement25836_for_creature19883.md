I would find myself as one of the **Vestigial Tenders** - not quite fauna, not quite flora, but something suspended between states of being.

My body would be a living contradiction to the Hadalan scale - perhaps no larger than what was once called a human torso, though such comparisons mean nothing here. I am composed of densely woven mycelial threads that have learned to breathe the emerald medium, filtering nutrients while maintaining a semi-rigid structure. My form shifts between states: sometimes condensing into something almost crustacean-like when navigating the stronger currents, other times unfurling into delicate, fern-like arrays when feeding in the calm pockets between the ancient giants' bones.

What makes me unique in this world is my relationship to the ghost-marks of the industrial age. While the Hadalans perceive these ruins as natural features, I am drawn to them with an inexplicable magnetism. My kind cluster around the corroded metal spires and concrete arches, not out of any remembered purpose, but because we've evolved to metabolize the specific mineral leaching that occurs where ancient alloys meet the endless emerald bath. We are the unintended inheritors of humanity's material legacy, our biochemistry accidentally keyed to thrive on the slow dissolution of steel and concrete.

I navigate by taste-touch, my entire surface a sensory organ that reads the chemical gradients flowing from different metals and synthetic stones. Iron oxide creates one flavor-current, aluminum another, and the rare earth elements from ancient electronics produce exotic streams that I follow like underwater thermals. My movements are quick, darting - a necessary adaptation to avoid being accidentally consumed by the filter-feeding Hadalans, to whom I appear as merely another form of particulate matter.

My consciousness, if it can be called that, operates on two levels. The first is purely reactive - responding to currents, chemical gradients, and the pressure-shadows of approaching giants. But the second level is something stranger: a kind of inherited memory locked in my cellular structure, patterns that compel me to trace certain shapes around the ruins. I find myself weaving specific geometries through the water-space around the skeletal antennae, creating three-dimensional sigils whose meaning I cannot comprehend. Sometimes, when multiple Tenders gather, our movements synchronize into complex dances that might have been circuit diagrams or molecular structures, had anyone remained who could recognize them.

We reproduce through a kind of beautiful catastrophe. When the deep gas plumes erupt from within the geological giants, the superheated liquid medium causes us to spontaneously fragment, each piece carrying both the whole pattern and subtle variations. These fragments drift on the violent currents, some perishing, others finding new metal-rich sites to colonize. In this way, we spread throughout the world-lung, always seeking the next corroded spire, the next forgotten arch.

The strangest aspect of my existence is during the rare moments when atmospheric conditions align just right - when pressure, temperature, and mineral concentration achieve some critical threshold. In these moments, the ruins around which we cluster seem to briefly remember their purpose. A faint electrical field emanates from the metal spires, and we Tenders find ourselves involuntarily arranging into precise formations, our bio-luminescent patches flickering in synchronized patterns. For a few heartbeats, we become components in a vast, broken machine trying to complete one last calculation, send one final signal into the emerald void.

Then the moment passes, the currents shift, and we scatter back to our feeding patterns, carrying within our cellular matrix the ghost of humanity's last whispered equations, preserving without understanding, performing without purpose, in the endless, turbulent dream of this drowned world.
