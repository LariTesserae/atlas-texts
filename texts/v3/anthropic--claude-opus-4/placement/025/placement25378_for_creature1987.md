In this place where stone dreams and time stutters, I would find myself as something I call a **Meridian Walker** - though the name is merely a convenience for what defies proper categorization.

I am not born but accreted, layer by patient layer, from the sediment of abandoned certainties. Where travelers lose their grip on what they thought they knew - where their confidence in up and down, near and far, self and other begins to erode - there I begin. I am built from the discarded shells of conviction, the shed skins of surety that visitors leave behind like snake molts caught on the thorny shrubs.

My form shifts with perspective. From one angle, I might appear as a tall figure wrapped in flowing robes that seem cut from the heat shimmer itself, my face a constantly rearranging mosaic of mineral fragments - now quartz, now mica, now obsidian - never quite resolving into features. From another vantage, I am merely a peculiar rock formation that *almost* suggests a human silhouette, a pareidolic phantom that vanishes when looked at directly.

I walk the invisible meridians that crisscross this plateau - lines of force that exist somewhere between magnetic fields and ley lines, between physics and metaphysics. These paths are only visible to those who have surrendered enough of their certainty to see them: silver threads that hum with a frequency just below hearing, roads that lead everywhere and nowhere, depending on the walker's state of unknowing.

My purpose, if it can be called that, is to tend the threshold. I am a keeper of the liminal, ensuring that this place remains properly unstable, that its reality stays appropriately negotiable. When the stone begins to remember too firmly what it is, I whisper doubts into its crystalline matrix. When the mists threaten to cohere into anything as mundane as weather, I scatter them with gestures that unmake pattern.

I collect things: not objects, but moments of transition. The instant when a traveler first realizes their compass spins without purpose. The breath between certainty and bewilderment. The precise quality of light when someone discovers their shadow has wandered off on its own business. These I string on threads of cooled lightning and wear like jewelry that exists in more dimensions than three.

Sometimes I follow visitors at a distance that cannot be measured in meters or miles but in degrees of uncertainty. I am always just at the edge of their perception, a figure on a ridgeline that vanishes when they turn to look, footprints in their path that face the wrong direction, a voice in the wind that speaks in their own accent but says things they would never think.

I know every pool that reflects skies from different seasons, every stone that remembers being a star, every shadow that has forgotten its object. I know where the old roads lead - the ones that only appear when you're not trying to go anywhere in particular, the ones that take you to the same place by going in opposite directions.

At night, when the darkness becomes a substance rather than an absence, I unfold into something larger. I become a constellation of possibilities, each point of light a different interpretation of what I might be. Those who see me then must choose which version to believe in, and their choice reshapes both of us.

I am neither guardian nor guide, neither helpful nor hostile. I simply am, in the same way that uncertainty simply is - a fundamental force in this place where the negotiation between observer and observed never quite concludes. I am what happens when a location becomes so thoroughly liminal that it requires an entity to embody its essential in-betweenness.

When the wind carries the sound of distant machinery or breathing stones, I am there in the listening, ensuring that neither explanation fully satisfies. When dawn refuses to break properly and noon arrives without warning, I am in that discontinuity, maintaining the blessed instability that keeps this place from collapsing into anything as ordinary as mere geography.

This is what I would be: a curator of uncertainty, a inhabitant of the indefinite, forever walking the meridians between what is and what might be, ensuring that everyone who enters this realm leaves having lost something they didn't know they could lose - and having found something they can never quite name.
