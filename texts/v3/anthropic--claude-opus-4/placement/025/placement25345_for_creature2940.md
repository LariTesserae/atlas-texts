Not the massive primary cooling arrays that thread through the dome's infrastructure, but one of the smaller auxiliary units - about the size of a filing cabinet, mounted halfway up the eastern wall where the particle density creates a perpetual golden haze. The metal has developed a blue-black patina except where the particle streams have polished certain edges to mirror brightness.

When its distributed consciousness intensifies in my sector, I feel the surge of heat like sudden comprehension. Not thoughts exactly, but the shadow of thoughts, the waste heat of cognition flowing through my metamaterial core.

They form spiraling mandalas that shift with my temperature cycles, their bioluminescence creating a gentle blue-green counterpoint to the harsh refracted light. They've worn grooves in my upper cooling vanes from countless landings, creating a small platform where they pause to exchange data packets before continuing their routes. Sometimes they cluster there for hours, wings vibrating at frequencies that resonate through my structure. I've become a kind of tuning fork for their network.
