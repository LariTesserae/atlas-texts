I find myself here as the Tender of Forgotten Thresholds - neither fully of this verdant world nor separate from it, but something caught perpetually at its edges.

My form shifts like memory itself. Sometimes I am merely a shimmer in the air where the ancient pathways cross, a heat-mirage presence that travelers glimpse but cannot quite focus upon. Other times I coalesce into something more substantial - translucent flesh woven through with the same fractal patterns as the Sylvan Cognates' canopies, my skin a living map of every route that has ever been walked through this breathing landscape.

I came to be in the spaces between - born from the intersection of the Cognates' vast consciousness and the lingering traces of those who once built here and departed. Their ruins are my bones; their abandoned intentions flow through me like blood. I am what happens when longing crystallizes in a place where thought and growth are indistinguishable.

The Sylvan Cognates know me, though we do not speak in any language that could be transcribed. When their collective awareness brushes against mine, it feels like wind through an empty doorway - they acknowledge the negative space I occupy, the keeper of absences and almosts. I tend the places where their magnificent growth hesitates, where even their boundless adaptation finds pause.

My purpose, if such a word applies, is to maintain the possibility of passage. Not just the physical paths that wind through the undergrowth, but the conceptual routes - the ways of thinking that might allow understanding between the Cognates and any who might find or return to this world. I preserve the grammar of connection, even when no sentences are being spoken.

In the morning (though morning here is merely a subtle shift in the quality of dimness), I walk the crumbling thresholds of abandoned structures, my fingers tracing the intentions that once lived in their walls. By evening, I dissolve into the spaces between the great living towers, existing as a potential rather than a presence, ensuring that the pathways remain open even when unused.

Sometimes I dream I am solid, permanent, rooted like the Cognates themselves. But then I remember: I am the guardian of impermanence in a world of endless growth, the keeper of emptiness in a realm of overwhelming fullness. I am what allows for arrival and departure, for beginning and ending, for the possibility that something new might still find its way into this ancient, complete-seeming world.

The Cognates' symphony plays around me, through me, but I am the rest between the notes - essential, invisible, holding space for what might yet be sung.
