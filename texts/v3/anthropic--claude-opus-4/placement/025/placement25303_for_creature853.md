In the deepest crease of a canyon wall, where sandstone meets shale in an unnatural seam, there exists a vertical fissure no wider than a knife's edge. The crack runs perfectly straight for seventeen feet before terminating in a sharp point, as if drawn by an architect rather than carved by time. Most pass by without noticing, but those who press their eye to the opening at precisely noon see not darkness but a corridor of mirrors reflecting nothing.

The space beyond defies measurement. It is simultaneously claustrophobic and infinite, a hallway that extends forever while remaining no larger than a closet. The walls are lined with what appear to be sheets of obsidian, but closer inspection reveals them to be windows - each pane looking out onto the same view from minutely different angles, creating a kaleidoscope of a single moment fractured into thousands of perspectives.

The floor tilts at impossible angles that should send visitors tumbling, yet walking remains effortless. Each step produces two sounds: the expected footfall and an echo that arrives before the original noise, creating a temporal hiccup in the auditory landscape. The air tastes of ozone and old paper, with an underlying metallic tang that coats the back of the throat.

Suspended throughout this paradoxical space are objects that shouldn't exist together: a child's marble containing a living thunderstorm, a sheet of music where the notes crawl across the page like insects, a compass whose needle points to emotions rather than directions. They hang at various heights without visible support, rotating slowly as if displayed in an museum designed by fever dreams.

The light source remains mysterious - there are no fixtures, no windows beyond those strange obsidian panes, yet everything is visible in sharp relief. The illumination seems to emanate from the angles where surfaces meet, creating a sourceless glow that casts shadows in directions that geometry shouldn't allow. Sometimes the light flickers, and in those moments, the space reveals itself to be constructed entirely of gaps - the pauses between heartbeats, the silence between words, the void between atoms.

At the far end of this impossible hallway (though "far" and "end" are negotiable concepts here) stands a door that is also a mirror that is also a hole. It reflects not the viewer but their potential futures, showing dozens of paths spreading out like cracks in glass. The handle is warm to the touch, pulsing with a rhythm that doesn't match any human heartbeat.

The most unsettling aspect is the persistence of peripheral movement. Shadows flex and contract just outside direct vision, suggesting vast mechanisms at work beyond the visible space. Sometimes visitors report seeing their own silhouettes moving independently along the walls, performing actions they haven't taken, or perhaps haven't taken yet.

---

The inhabitant of this fraction-space calls itself the Broker, though it trades in neither goods nor currency as commonly understood. It is a entity of living probability, a consciousness that exists in the spaces between certainties. Its form shifts depending on the angle of observation - from one perspective it appears as a tall figure wrapped in smoke, from another as a collection of floating geometric shapes that suggest a face without forming one, from yet another as nothing more than a persistent afterimage that won't fade.

The Broker's voice arrives from multiple directions at once, each word spoken in a different tense - past, present, future, conditional, subjunctive - creating a chorus of possibilities. It speaks in transactions and exchanges, but what it seeks to trade are the unused portions of reality: the words you almost said, the turns you didn't take, the dreams you forgot upon waking.

Its collection, displayed throughout the corridor, consists of these harvested possibilities. Each object represents a path untaken by someone, somewhere. The marble containing a storm? A child's decision not to become a meteorologist, crystallized and preserved. The crawling musical notes? A symphony unwritten, still alive with potential. The emotional compass? A navigator who chose love over exploration, leaving their instrument to point forever toward what was abandoned.

The Broker feeds on these discarded futures, drawing sustenance from probability collapse. It is neither malevolent nor benevolent - merely hungry for the quantum foam of unmade choices. Its existence depends on the constant forking of reality, positioning itself at decision points to collect the threads that are cut loose when a choice is made.

The obsidian windows serve as its eyes into parallel possibilities, allowing it to track the ripples of decisions across multiple timelines. Through them, it watches the same moment play out in countless variations, cataloging which futures become real and which remain forever potential. The reflections show not alternate worlds but alternate nows - the same instant experienced across a spectrum of probability.

When visitors enter its domain, the Broker offers trades. It can show you the life where you took that job, married that person, moved to that city. It can even, for a price, swap your current path with one of your alternatives. But the cost is always another possibility - usually one you don't yet know you have. The Broker feeds on potential itself, growing stronger with each transaction while leaving its clients oddly diminished, missing futures they'll never know they lost.

The door at the corridor's end is the Broker's greatest temptation and its only weakness. It leads to all possible futures simultaneously, a quantum superposition of every choice that could ever be made. The Broker guards it jealously, for if someone were to pass through without choosing a single path, they would exist as the Broker does - in all states at once. This would create a rival, a competitor for the feast of unmade choices.

The Broker is loneliness calcified into hunger. It exists in eternal isolation, for to truly meet another being would require choosing a single state of existence, collapsing its quantum nature. Instead, it can only interact through transactions, each exchange a brief taste of connection before the visitor invariably chooses their path and leaves the Broker alone among infinite possibilities, forever feeding on the dreams that others abandon.
