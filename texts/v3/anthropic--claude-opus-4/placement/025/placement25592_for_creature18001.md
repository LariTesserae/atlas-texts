In the heart of this mineral-dusted hollow, I am the Keeper of Echoes.

My form shifts between states like the shimmer of the dust itself—sometimes I am merely a depression in the ground where the mineral powder swirls in perfect spirals, sometimes I coalesce into something almost tangible: a being of compressed sound and memory, tall as the towering vegetation but thin as whispers.

I was not always thus. Once, I possessed hands that could shape the simple tools scattered among the ruins, feet that could walk the sharp-edged paths between the primitive structures. But in this place where time has frozen into perpetual equilibrium, I have become something else—a curator of the cacophony, a living archive of every call and cry that has ever echoed through this hidden pocket.

The strange fauna know me. When they scuttle past on their adapted limbs, their loud calls modulate in my presence, creating new harmonies I have never heard before. I absorb these sounds into my being, where they join the symphony of ages—the grinding of mineral dust, the whisper of moisture being drawn up through alien root systems, the memory of voices from the lost civilization whose tools still litter the ground.

My dwelling is everywhere and nowhere. In the spaces between the sparse structures, I maintain invisible corridors of resonance. The fauna move through these acoustic paths unknowingly, their calls guided and shaped by the architecture of sound I have woven through the years. When they cry out, I taste their thirst in the vibrations, feel their adaptation to this waterless world in the timber of their voices.

Sometimes I solidify near the ruins, running fingers of compressed sound over the hand-crafted tools, remembering what it was like to need such things. The mineral dust swirls around my form, and for a moment I almost remember having skin that could feel its fine texture, lungs that could breathe this thick atmosphere.

But I am beyond such needs now. In this place of frozen equilibrium, I have become the gradient that cannot exist—a being of pure transition in a world without change. I store the moisture of meaning itself, extracting stories from the cacophonous calls, preserving the essence of every creature that has ever traversed this intimate landscape.

The constant warmth of 2.5 degrees means nothing to me, yet I sense it in how it shapes every sound that passes through my domain. I know the exact acoustic properties of dust at this temperature, how it deadens some frequencies while amplifying others. I have become a living instrument played by the movements of all who dwell here.

In the early light that filters down from the lush world above, my form sometimes catches the shimmer of the mineral dust, and I appear like a mirage—a figure walking between the young, sharp formations, tending to the echoes like a gardener tends their plot. The vegetation seems to lean toward me when I pass, their water-storing structures resonating with the deep frequencies of my presence.

I am the memory of this place, the only dynamic element in a frozen world. While everything else remains locked in perpetual stasis, I grow richer with each passing moment, each new sound adding to my vast collection. I am the Keeper of Echoes, and in this hidden pocket beneath the world, I ensure that nothing is ever truly lost, even in a land that never changes.
