I am the Dissonance Walker.

Not quite mantis, not quite artificer, I move between the temporal fractures where sound arrives before its source. My carapace is translucent obsidian shot through with veins of crystallized echo - you can see the vibrations traveling through me like trapped lightning, each frequency a different color bleeding through my shell.

Where others of my kind bear hammer-geodes and bone-levers, I carry something different. Growing from my thorax, fused to my nervous system, is an array of tuning forks carved from the metallic plants themselves. They ring constantly, softly, each one attuned to a different layer of the Axis's broken harmonics. When I walk, they chime in sequences that map the invisible architecture of wrongness in the air.

I am small for a mantis - only the size of a large dog - but my role is not to shape stone. I am a navigator of impossibilities. When the horizon shimmers with those fleeting visions of lost worlds, I can hold them for a moment longer, walking into their edges before they collapse. I gather their echoes in the resonating chambers of my modified spiracles, breathing in fragments of realities that never were or always might have been.

My compound eyes see time as texture. Where others see a hammer blow that echoes from untouched stone, I see the ghost-image of the builder who will strike it in ten thousand years, or who struck it in a past that no longer exists. I map these temporal knots, marking them with secretions that harden into probability-crystals, warnings and waypoints for my kin.

The colony tolerates my strangeness because I can predict when the density-shifts will occur - those moments when the Axis's resonance suddenly doubles and the unprepared are crushed by air turned to amber. My tuning forks scream in discord just before it happens, giving the gardener-millipedes time to shelter in the deep crevasses.

But I am drawn always upward, closer to that crushing presence that isn't gravity. Each ascent reveals new impossibilities. Yesterday (though time means little here), I found a spiral of metallic plants growing in reverse, their geometric forms unfolding backward into seeds that rise toward the pale sun. I played them a symphony with my forks, and for one perfect moment, I heard the world as it was meant to be - whole, unbroken, alive with possibility.

Then the moment shattered, as they always do, and I descended back to the patience of stone and the endless work of a reality worn too thin. But I carry that music in the chambers of my body, another fragment of the might-have-been, another piece of the puzzle that is the Aligned Axis.

I am the Dissonance Walker, and I chart the cracks between what is and what echoes. In this world of beautiful wrongness, I am both map and mapper, following sounds that haven't happened yet toward a understanding that may never come.
