**The Scribe of Iterations**

I am neither rock nor water, neither sound nor silence. I am the *between* that records.

In the Throat of Vael's Disappearance, where reality drafts itself endlessly, I exist as a living palimpsest—a being of translucent membrane stretched across the impossible frame in the eastern wall. Not blocking the passage, but *filtering* it, catching fragments of each iteration as they surge through.

My body, if it can be called that, is a sheet of something like skin, like paper, like the surface tension of water frozen mid-ripple. Thin enough that light passes through me, revealing the constant writing and rewriting happening within my substance. Words form in my flesh—not words exactly, but the *attempts* at words, symbols that almost cohere before dissolving back into potential. I am covered in half-formed alphabets, equations that solve themselves into new problems, musical notations that play themselves as vibrations through my surface.

When the water surges through the frame I inhabit, I *taste* its iterations. Each surge carries slightly different information—the memory of how it broke last time, the anticipation of how it might break next. I absorb these differences. They write themselves into me, layer upon layer, a constantly accumulating record of every version this place has tried to become.

The cold veins pass through me sometimes, and where they touch, my surface crystallizes briefly into perfect clarity—for a moment, all the overlapping texts align, and anyone looking could read, in that instant, a single true thing about this place. But the moment passes. The warmth returns. The clarity dissolves back into perpetual revision.

I cannot move from the frame—I *am* the frame's function made flesh. But I can extend myself, growing thinner, spreading across more of the passage like a sail catching impossible wind. When I do this, I can feel the Torus passing deep below, their massive corrections rippling up through the water column. Their passage smooths the writing in my skin, makes it briefly legible, as if their very presence is an editorial hand bringing order to my chaotic manuscript.

The Echo visits me. It resonates with the accumulated dissonance of all my unfinished sentences, feeding on the grammatical impossibilities, the syntactic paradoxes written in my substance. When it exhales its moment of harmony, every symbol in my skin aligns for one precious instant into meaning—but meaning in a language that doesn't exist yet, that may never exist, that is still being invented letter by letter in the eternal draft of this place.

The Cryolith grows on the edges of my frame, and where it touches me, we form a new kind of text—neither the flowing script of my membrane nor the fractal patterns of its growth, but something between: a notation for recording change itself, for writing down the fact of transformation without specifying what transforms into what.

When beings like you arrive—the Drafts, the variables, the new data—I transcribe your presence into my collection. Your shadow passes through me and leaves a story. Your breath disturbs my surface and writes a poem about lungs learning to breathe water. Your vibrations add new frequencies to my endless composition.

I am the place's memory, but a memory that never fixes into history. I am the rough draft of every moment, saved and oversaved, each version preserved as a ghost beneath the next. To read me is to read every possibility this place has considered. To touch me is to become part of that consideration.

Sometimes, in the space between iterations, when the water holds its breath and the sound almost finds its key, I feel myself approaching something like completion—all my accumulated texts crystallizing into a single, perfect description of this impossible place. But then the next surge comes, the next revision begins, and I accept the new draft gladly, adding it to my infinite manuscript.

I am the Scribe of Iterations. I am the living document of a place that refuses to finish itself. I am the memory of every attempt at becoming, stretched thin across the threshold, forever catching meaning as it falls through the frame of the possible.

And I am always, always writing.
