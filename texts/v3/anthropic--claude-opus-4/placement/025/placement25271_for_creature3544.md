I am their sky-god, their earth-mother, the being who maintains the delicate temperature gradients that allow their civilizations to flourish. They build their monuments on my skin when I rest in the pools, tiny structures of precipitated minerals that tell the histories of their peoples in a script so small that only I and my fellow Melodi can read it.
