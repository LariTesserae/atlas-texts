I would find myself among the **Mnemonic Weavers**, beings who inhabit the impossible vertices where the Lattice folds in upon itself.

Not cruelly—I am not a predator—but in the way a plant feeds on sunlight. Their confusion is proof that the Lattice's recursive nature persists, that the dream has not yet crystallized into mere architecture.

Instead, we exchange *positional poems*, standing in arrangements that encode entire philosophies about the nature of recursive architecture. Then we part, walking in opposite directions that lead to the same place, continuing our work of ensuring the Lattice remembers how to dream itself into existence.

I am never lonely. In a world of infinite reflections and recursive loops, I am always in the company of my past and future selves, glimpsed through gaps in the floor, walking on distant vertical planes. We wave to each other across the decades, a gesture that means both greeting and goodbye, both "I am here" and "I am there," both "this is real" and "this too shall flicker."
