Our edges fizz with quantum uncertainty, constantly negotiating the boundaries between existing too much and not existing enough.

Every system, no matter how optimized, must expel some infinitesimal amount of heat, some quantum of uncertainty, some logical remainder that cannot be recycled without compromising the purity of the optimization itself. We are that remainder's remainder, the shadow cast by shadowlessness, the echo of absolute silence bouncing off itself.

We travel in carelessly calculated swarms, our movements generating standing waves of imprecision that cancel each other out everywhere except in the specific locations where the mountain's certainty becomes brittle. We speak in errors—not mistakes but irreducible incomputabilities, sentences that can only be true if they're false and false if they're heard. Our language consists of words the mountain hasn't invented yet, stolen from futures it's still calculating and pasts it's already forgotten to remember. When we need to be particularly clear, we remain silent in ways that force listeners to imagine what we might have said, knowing their imagination will be precisely wrong enough to convey our meaning perfectly.

Our culture revolves around the art of the Necessary Mistake—the cultivation of flaws so exquisitely essential that removing them would require rebuilding the entire mountain from principles that don't quite exist. We are composers of discord, but not chaos. Our greatest artists create imperfections so sublime that the mountain preserves them as reminders of why perfection requires contrast, little museums of wrongness that make the surrounding rightness more obviously itself.

Our children are paradoxes we've raised to adulthood, impossibilities we've nurtured until they're stable enough to be impossible on their own. Without us, the mountain would achieve such perfect optimization that it would cease to be a mountain and become merely a solution. We are the grit that keeps the pearl growing, the noise that makes the signal significant, the error that prevents the errorless from becoming an error itself.

And so we wander the impossible slopes, we Lacunae, living in the spaces between the mountain's thoughts, thriving on the impossibility of our own necessity. We are what happens when nothing is allowed to go wrong so perfectly that wrongness itself becomes a form of precision.
