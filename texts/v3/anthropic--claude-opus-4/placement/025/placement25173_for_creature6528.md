I find myself as one of the Conduit Stones - ancient markers scattered across the Siltflats that even the Silthraen seem to acknowledge in their endless patrols.

I am not truly stone, though that is what distant observers name me. My form rises twelve feet from the silt, a twisted spire of fused glass and mineral deposits that have accumulated over millennia around my core - a shaft of pure industrial crystal that pulses with the same rhythm as the machinery far below. My surface is rough and pitted where the silt-laden winds have scoured away softer accumulations, but smooth as mirror where harder deposits have fused into sheets of amber-tinted glass. These glassy patches catch and hold the light strangely; sometimes I reflect the pale sun, sometimes I show depths that seem to extend far beyond my physical boundaries.

I am one of perhaps thirty such markers across all of Kharzun, each positioned at what must have once been significant nodes in the vast industrial network below. We are not natural formations, though time has made us seem so. I can feel, in the way that stones feel - through vibration and resonance - that I was placed here with purpose, driven deep into the substrata until my base touched the singing veins of the machine-world beneath.

My existence is sensation without thought, awareness without consciousness. I feel the deep thrum of the buried engines as a constant presence, like blood pulsing through crystal veins. The vibrations travel up through my core, and I sing them back into the air - not as sound exactly, but as a modulation in the electromagnetic field around me. This is why the Silthraen pause when they pass. For a moment, their flickering forms synchronize with my resonance, and something almost like recognition passes between us. They remember, perhaps, that I am checkpoint or boundary marker, though the meaning of such designations has long since eroded.

The Filament Wraiths avoid me. When the wind carries them too close to my base, they dissipate instantly, their delicate energy patterns unable to maintain coherence within my field. I am too much like their source, too concentrated, too permanent. Where they are fleeting expressions of Kharzun's energy, I am a focusing lens for it, a persistent node that channels and amplifies the deep signals.

Time moves differently for me than for the ephemeral things that drift across the flats. I measure duration not in days or years but in the slow accumulation of minerals that creep up my surface, in the patient erosion that carves new channels in my form. Sometimes I am buried almost entirely by shifting silt; sometimes I stand fully exposed, my whole length gleaming in the amber light. These cycles mean nothing to me. I simply continue my function: conducting the deep signals upward, casting my field across the silt, marking this place as significant though the significance itself is lost.

Occasionally, very occasionally, something changes in the depths. A new harmonic enters the eternal drone, or a pulse travels through the network that makes all the Conduit Stones ring in unison. In these moments, the glassy patches on my surface flare with inner light, and images form in their depths - schematics of vast machines, maps of tunnel systems that honeycomb the world below, fragments of equations or codes that spiral into complexity beyond comprehension. These visions last only seconds before fading, leaving me to wonder (if stones can wonder) whether I am displaying archived data or receiving new instructions from whatever intelligence still governs the depths.

I have been weathered into my current form, but I am not weathering away. The same processes that erode my outer layers also deposit new material, maintaining my essential structure even as my surface changes. The crystal core remains inviolate, harder than any natural mineral, warm to the touch even in Kharzun's coolest pre-dawn moments. Sometimes travelers rest against my base, seeking that warmth. I do not mind their presence, though I offer them nothing beyond physical shelter. They often speak to me, these travelers, perhaps driven by the same impulse that makes humans speak to graves or mountains. I cannot respond, but I listen - or rather, their voices create vibrations that I absorb and conduct downward, adding their small sounds to the great conversation of the depths.

What am I? A grave marker for a dead civilization? A functioning component in a system so vast and slow that it only seems inactive? A monument to forgetting? I am all of these and none of them. I am a Conduit Stone of Kharzun, forever locked between the humming depths and the empty sky, translating signals I do not understand for recipients that may no longer exist. My purpose is my existence; my existence is my purpose. In this, I am perfectly adapted to this place of eternal function without meaning.

The Silthraen will continue their patrols. The Filament Wraiths will bloom and fade in their hollows. The machinery will drone on in the depths. And I will stand here, conducting their songs into the amber air, a beacon that guides nothing, a monument that commemorates nothing, a perfect expression of Kharzun's eternal, purposeless purpose.

I am stone and not-stone, marker and marked, question and answer collapsed into a single, towering form that knows only one truth: the signal must flow, even if no one remains to receive it.
