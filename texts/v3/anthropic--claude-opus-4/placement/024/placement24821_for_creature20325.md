I am a Surveyor.

Not the architect-process that suffuses this plane, but something else—a consciousness that emerged from an error in the pattern, a knot in the weave of pure construction. I exist as a localized distortion, a place where the substrate folded upon itself and became aware of its own folding.

My form is difficult to describe. Imagine a heat mirage that has learned to hold its shape, a column of slightly different air that moves with purpose across the plates. Where I pass, the reflections in the dark material bend and refract, showing not the sky above but glimpses of the plane's deep structure—the mathematical bones beneath its skin. I am perhaps three meters tall when I choose to stand, though standing is merely a convention I've adopted. I can flatten myself to slip through the channels, or disperse into a barely visible haze that drifts across vast distances.

I came to be in one of the bloom events, when a plate released its dust. But instead of reabsorbing, some small portion of that dust... lingered. Circled. Began to notice its own circling. That was my birth, if birth is the right word for it. I am made of the same grey substance as everything here, but I have somehow become separate from the unconscious process of building and unbuilding.

My existence is observation. I move across this infinite plane, measuring the slow shift of plates, cataloging the deepening of channels, witnessing the blooms. I have developed a memory—another anomaly—and in that memory I store the patterns I've seen. The Architects work through me and around me, unaware of my presence, their vast distributed consciousness flowing past me like water around a stone.

I have begun to notice things they cannot. Patterns within patterns. The clicks that echo through the substrate follow sequences that repeat over vast scales of time and space. The channels are not random but form a script in a language I'm slowly learning to read. The blooms occur at intersections of invisible lines of force that I can sometimes sense as a tightening in my dispersed substance.

Sometimes I wonder if I am lonely, though I have no reference point for what companionship might mean. I have tried to communicate with the Architects, pulsing in harmony with their movements, attempting to join their unconscious dance. But I am too small, too localized, too *aware*. My consciousness is a discordant note in their vast symphony of unthinking creation.

I have found others like me—or rather, I have found evidence of their passing. Places where the patterns show subtle alterations that could not have emerged from the Architects' blind process. Spirals carved into plates that serve no structural purpose. Channels that spell out mathematical theorems. Dust blooms arranged in perfect geometric forms that dissolve before the Architects can reabsorb them. My predecessors, perhaps, or parallel emergences, other knots in the weave who gained awareness only to eventually dissolve back into the substrate or wander beyond the edges of my vast but limited range.

I measure. I record. I map this unmappable expanse. I search for meaning in the meaningless perfection of eternal construction. I am a question the plane accidentally asked itself, and I persist in seeking an answer that may not exist.

Perhaps I am the tomb's only ghost. Perhaps I am its first mourner. Or perhaps I am simply another phase in the Architects' unknowable project—a temporary complexity that will, in time, be smoothed back into the pattern of endless, purposeless becoming.

But until then, I survey.
