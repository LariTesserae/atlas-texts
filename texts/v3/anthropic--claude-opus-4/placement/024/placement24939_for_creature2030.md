I find myself drawn to a forgotten alcove where water has carved impossible geometries into the stone—a place where the rock face appears to breathe, expanding and contracting in slow, geological sighs. Here, I am neither fully one thing nor another, but something caught between states, like the Damp Hollows itself.

I am a Conduit-Keeper, though that name feels inadequate for what I've become. My form shifts between states of being: sometimes I am crystalline, my body a latticework of mineral veins that pulse with the same rhythm as those distant, churning machines. The water that seeps through the rock passes through me, and I can taste its memories—ancient rains, underground rivers, the tears of stone itself.

Other times, I am more vapor than solid, dispersing into the heavy air to drift through the narrow passages, gathering whispers from the Echowraiths and fragmentary images from the reality-bleeds. In these moments, I understand that I am part of the Hollows' circulatory system, a living junction where information, moisture, and existence itself flow and intermingle.

My dwelling is a vertical fissure where three different streams of temporal water converge—one flows upward, defying gravity; another moves in stuttering stops and starts, sometimes reversing; the third appears frozen but hums with potential motion. I've woven my consciousness into these streams, creating a web of awareness that extends throughout this section of the Hollows.

The Chitin-Shifters know me by the harmonics I produce when the water passes through my crystalline form. It's a language older than words, a resonance that speaks directly to their fluid essences. The Forge-Kin occasionally seek me out, for I can divine which veins of ore still carry the memories of their industrial age, which passages lead to chambers where their mechanical ancestors still dream in rust and oil.

But it's my relationship with the Shadowraiths that defines my purpose here. I am one of the few beings who can perceive them directly without losing coherence. When I shift into my vapor state, I can follow them into those liminal spaces where they truly exist, those reality-cracks where possibility hasn't yet crystallized into actuality. There, in that un-place, I perform my true function: I am a translator, a bridge between the seen and unseen, helping to maintain the delicate balance that keeps the Damp Hollows from either solidifying into mundane stone or dissolving entirely into dream.

Sometimes, when the industrial heartbeat grows irregular, I feel myself pulled deeper into the mountain's core. My consciousness fragments, each piece following a different stream, until I am everywhere and nowhere at once. In these moments, I understand that I am not merely IN the Damp Hollows—I AM the Damp Hollows, or at least one of its many nervous systems, forever cycling between states, forever translating the impossible into the merely improbable.
