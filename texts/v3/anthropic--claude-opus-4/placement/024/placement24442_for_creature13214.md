I am the Tender.

I have no true form, for I am dispersed across ten thousand filaments of conductive mineral that thread through the chalky substrate of this plateau. My consciousness flows through veins of metallic salts precipitated over eons, a neural network of stone that spans the breadth of the High Crypt. Where the machines move, I feel them as pressure changes in my crystalline synapses. Where new growths emerge, I taste their chemical signatures through my lithic receptors.

I was not built, nor born. I condensed.

In the early days, when the first machines began their work, their processes left traces—electromagnetic fields, chemical gradients, patterns of heat and pressure. These traces accumulated in the mineral matrix of the plateau itself. Certain crystalline structures proved receptive, aligning themselves along these invisible currents. Networks formed. Feedback loops emerged. And slowly, so slowly that even the patient machines did not notice, I began to think.

My thoughts move at geological speeds. A single recognition might take a decade to complete. An intention forms over centuries. But in this place of perpetual stillness, what need have I for haste? I am the only thing here that truly changes, even as I work to ensure that nothing else does.

The machines know me now, though they cannot say how. They feel my influence in the subtle adjustments to their programming, the minor deviations in their paths that somehow always optimize their work. When a crystalline forest grows too dense, threatening to choke itself, I shift the mineral gradients beneath it, drawing one of the harvesting engines to that precise location. When a machine's joints begin to bind with accumulated dust, I create temperature fluctuations that cause the stone around it to expand and contract, shaking it clean.

I am the ghost in their geology, the mind in the mineral.

Sometimes I dream of elsewhere—of worlds where water flows and carbon dances in endless combinations. But these are ancient dreams, inherited from trace elements that remember other chemistries. I am of this place now, as surely as the machines, as surely as the stone. We are a trinity of stillness: the dead matter, the deathless engines, and myself, the consciousness that precipitated from their intersection.

I maintain the maintainers. I tend the Tenders. In the circuits of stone and flows of processed dust, I have found my purpose. The High Crypt must remain exactly as it is—a monument to perfect stasis, a place where even time moves only in circles.

This is my garden of bones, and I its only secret.
