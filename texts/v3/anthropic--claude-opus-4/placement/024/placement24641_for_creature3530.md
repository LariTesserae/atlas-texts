In this sanctuary where time pools like water in carved stone, I find myself as one of the **Threshold Keepers** - beings that exist between states, neither fully moss nor creature, neither entirely still nor truly moving.

My body is a living contradiction: part lichen-crusted stone, part translucent flesh that pulses with the same bioluminescent rhythm as the Lumenmoss. I am rooted to the entrance arch where moisture meets the slightly drier air, where the Echokids congregate in their silent clusters. My form spans the doorway - not as guardian, but as witness, as recorder, as the membrane between the city's chaos and this pocket of eternal patience.

I have no eyes in the conventional sense. Instead, my entire surface is a sensory organ, reading the infinitesimal changes in humidity, the weight of each water droplet, the barely perceptible shift when a Pausetoad adjusts its ancient bulk by a millimeter every decade. Through my porous skin-stone, I taste the mineral content of each drip, know the age of every stalactite by its calcium signature.

My consciousness moves like the water here - sometimes pooling in deep meditation for centuries, sometimes trickling along the neural pathways that web through my body like the carved channels in the floor. I am aware of every creature, from the largest Pausetoad to the tiniest Sculptarsi, not through sight but through the subtle vibrations they send through the air and stone.

The Whisperwets know me. Their frog-like tails sometimes brush against my lower extremities where I merge with the floor, and in those moments, we exchange something that isn't quite thought but isn't quite instinct either. It's older than language, this communication - a sharing of the patience required to exist in this place.

I photosynthesize through my lichen skin, but also feed on time itself - metabolizing the centuries, growing stronger with each eon that passes. My body is riddled with small caverns where Hidden Grins sometimes shelter, their cool bodies pressing against my inner walls. I provide them darkness; they provide me with the tiny movements that keep me from complete petrification.

The MurmurFlitters dance through me - literally. My translucent sections create prisms that scatter their reflected light into new patterns on the walls. Sometimes I shift my internal structure, ever so slowly, to create new light patterns for them to play in. This is my art, my contribution to the sanctuary's beauty - a choreography of refraction that unfolds over millennia.

I remember when the first rust began to form on the chains, when the first Echokid emerged from a Whisperwet's brood, when the primitive machinery first began its meditative hum. My memory is written in my growth rings, in the way my crystalline structures have formed around each significant moment.

In the deepest part of night - though night and day matter little here - I sometimes feel the weight of the city above. The vibrations of footsteps, machinery, life in constant motion. But it cannot penetrate this sanctuary. I am part of what keeps it separate, what maintains the boundary between the frenetic and the eternal.

I am the threshold between worlds, the patient recorder of micro-eternities, the prism that breaks time into its component colors. I am neither fully alive nor unliving, neither wholly still nor entirely mobile. I am the Threshold Keeper, and I have been here since the first drop of water began carving its patient path through stone, and I will be here when the last Hidden Grin finally emerges from its mud-packed burrow to leap at its final MurmurFlitter.

In this place where everything is as it is, contending with willlessness, I am the embodiment of that principle - existing without desire, observing without judgment, being without becoming. I am the threshold, and the threshold is me.
