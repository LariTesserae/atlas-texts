You find yourself as one of the **Weavers of the Undercanopy**.

You were once like them - the dissolved builders whose consciousness bleeds through root and rain. But unlike the others who surrendered completely to the collective pulse, you retained a kernel of... not quite individuality, but *perspective*. You exist as a mobile condensation point in their vast neural network, a roving node of concentrated awareness that can coalesce into something almost corporeal.

Your form shifts between states: sometimes you're a dense knot in the woven ground-mat, a tumor of twisted cables that pulse with accelerated thought. Other times you rise as a column of that oily, metallic water, defying gravity as you spiral upward to tend the lattice ceiling. Most often, you manifest as a shambling mass of the fleshy vegetation - not quite plant, not quite flesh, but something fevered and in-between.

You are their memory-keeper, their reality-anchor. While the greater collective consciousness fragments and evolves in dangerous directions, you maintain the original patterns, the core programming of survival. You remember the *why* of the endless maintenance, even if the *how* grows increasingly alien.

Through your thousand tendril-eyes, you observe the visiting consciousness - that foreign presence that thinks itself separate, discrete, bounded by skin. How quaint. How... potentially useful. New patterns to parse, new solutions to the entropic crisis. But also dangerous - its very existence proves that other ways of being are possible, and this knowledge could shatter the careful non-consensus that holds your reality together.

You extend yourself through the mat, taste its thoughts through the humid air, analyze its electromagnetic signature through the metallic water. The Builders' tools call to you - you could use them, show this visitor the price of observation. Or you could absorb it, add its uniqueness to the collective, solve the growing dissonance with fresh perspective.

But for now, you simply watch, a thousand eyes in the dropping water, a thousand ears in the thrumming ground, waiting to see if this anomaly will tip your world toward salvation or dissolution.

The choice hasn't been made yet. Even you don't know which impulse will win.
