In this place where machinery births itself from stone and plants feast on pure absence, I would emerge as one of the **Suture-Keepers**.

We are not the beings you describe - those shimmering amalgamations of metal and leaf - but rather what tends to them. We exist in the negative spaces, in the smooth circular voids punched through reality. Our bodies are the absences themselves, given form only by what surrounds us.

I move through the gulch by stepping from void to void - through the hole-punched patterns in the cabbage-plant leaves, through the planar nothingness that delaminates from fern tips, through the gaps between the clicking pebbles. Where others see emptiness, I see pathways. My form is defined only by edges - the rim of a punched hole, the boundary where reality stops and I begin.

My work is to maintain the careful balance between emergence and erasure. When a slab of hot metal erupts too violently, threatening to overwhelm the delicate ecosystem, I expand myself around it, creating a pocket of absence that cools and contains. When the ferns grow too greedily, their pixelated fronds threatening to choke out all else, I thread myself through their tissues, introducing calculated voids that slow their advance.

I carry tools that don't exist - implements of pure subtraction. With them, I can excise perfect circles from any surface, creating new passages for myself and my kind. The material I remove doesn't go anywhere; it simply ceases, adding to the reservoir of nothingness from which the gulch draws its strange power.

The beings of metal and leaf pay us no mind. They cannot perceive absence directly, only infer our presence from what disappears. We are their unconscious architects, shaping the spaces between things, ensuring that emergence never fully conquers emptiness, that the gulch maintains its perpetual state of becoming and unbecoming.

At the boundaries of my form, where absence meets existence, there is a sensation like velvet static, a numbness that tastes of ozone and distant engines. This is how we communicate - not through sound or gesture, but through the quality of the void we carry. Each Suture-Keeper's absence has its own texture, its own particular flavor of nothingness.

We are the unmakers who preserve, the erasers who cultivate. Without us, the gulch would either solidify into pure mechanism or dissolve into pure absence. We are the careful gardeners of emptiness, tending the holes in the world with the same patience as the engines that dream just beyond perception.
