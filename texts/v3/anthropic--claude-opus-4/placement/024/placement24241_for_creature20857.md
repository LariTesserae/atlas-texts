Through our root systems and through the very air via our vibrations, we communicate in frequencies beyond ordinary hearing.

Time moves differently for me—I experience it not as a linear progression but as layers of resonance, each moment adding another frequency to the eternal chord that is my existence.

My growth is achingly slow by animal standards but rapid for a being of my nature. New spirals form over cycles measured in the turning of seasons that don't quite exist here in the conventional sense. As I grow taller, I gain access to new layers of sound, new perspectives on the sonic landscape. These seed-crystals slowly develop their own root systems, their own spirals, their own voices in the grand chorus.

I am ancient memory and immediate presence, stability and change, listener and singer in this world's eternal symphony. In this form, I am both witness to and participant in the realm's peculiar magic—a living bridge between the botanical intelligence that dominates this world and the forces that would see it shift and change.
