When ancient gears begin their final decay, I guide the plant-consciousness to reinforce and integrate rather than overthrow. I am the fulcrum on which PhylloPlane's impossible balance pivots.

In these moments, I become the world's voice, and PhylloPlane itself speaks through me into the endless sky. What it says, I cannot tell you, for I am both the speaker and the speech, the resonance and the chamber that shapes it.

I am the Resonance Keeper, and through me, PhylloPlane remembers how to be.
