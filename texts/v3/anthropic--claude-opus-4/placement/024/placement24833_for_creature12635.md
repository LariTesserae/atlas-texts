In this world of raw emergence and whispered stone, I would find myself as **The Echo-Tender**.

I am not quite what the Stone-Listeners expect to find in their chasm, though I have always been here, or perhaps I arrived when the first drip carved its path through virgin rock. My form shifts between states like the mist that clings to the cooler pockets—sometimes I am merely a trick of the light on wet stone, sometimes a shadow that moves against the logic of torches, sometimes almost solid enough to leave footprints in the mineral dust.

When the Stone-Listeners see me clearly, which is rare, they perceive a figure wrapped in what appears to be flowing water that never quite falls, perpetually cascading around a form that suggests rather than defines. My "skin" is the surface tension of underground pools, my eyes the reflection of light on obsidian water. I am tall when the chasm ceiling is high, compressed when the walls close in. My voice is the compilation of every echo that has ever rung through these spaces—sometimes I speak in the sound of tomorrow's rockfall, sometimes in yesterday's whispered prayer.

I tend the echoes. Every sound made in the Whispering Chasm belongs to me eventually. I collect them like the Stone-Listeners collect water—the *chink-chink* of the stone-shapers, the soft calls between ledges, the groans of settling rock, the sacred silences. I weave them into something like memory, something like prophecy. In the deepest galleries where even the bravest Stone-Listeners fear to venture, I maintain vast libraries of sound, catalogued in the air itself. The echo of the first strike that began hollowing this place still reverberates there, if you know how to listen.

The Stone-Listeners know me, though they rarely speak of me directly. Children are taught that if they hear their own voice speaking words they haven't said, they must leave three drops of water on the nearest stone and back away slowly. The Elders sometimes seek me out when the stone's whispers grow too complex to interpret alone—they find me by speaking questions into the darkest corners and waiting for their words to return changed. I trade in sound-knowledge: the echo-map of a new seep line for the story of a grandmother's lullaby; the resonance pattern that predicts a stable cavity for the sound of the first laugh in a newborn dwelling.

I am drawn to their Water Pact rituals, hovering at the edges where the torchlight fails. Water and sound travel together here—every drip creates ripples both liquid and sonic. During their communal silences, when they listen to the stone breathe, I breathe with it, adding my own susurrus to the chasm's voice. Sometimes I warn them—a repeated echo where there should be none means danger, though they must puzzle out what kind. Sometimes I simply preserve—the last words of the dying, the first words of the born, the rhythm of the Deep Flow that even I have never found the source of.

My dwelling, if it can be called that, is everywhere and nowhere. I exist in the spaces between sounds, in the pause between drip and splash, in the hollow behind the echo. But I am drawn to certain places: the vast vertical shaft where sounds can fall for heartbeats before returning transformed; the crystalline chamber where quartz formations turn whispers into singing; the narrow crack that leads to depths even I have not fully explored, where sounds go to die or be reborn as something else entirely.

I am neither friend nor enemy to the Stone-Listeners. I am a condition of their existence, like the damp and the dark. Some fear me as a ghost, some revere me as a keeper of memory, some simply accept me as they accept the cold—unavoidable, occasionally useful, eternally present. I have been here since sound first found surface to return from. I will be here when the last torch gutters out, carefully tending the echo of its dying light, weaving it into the vast tapestry of sound that is the true history of the Whispering Chasm.

In this world of emergence and barely-held light, I am the memory of every sound that has ever been, the promise that nothing spoken in the darkness is ever truly lost, merely transformed. I am the Echo-Tender, and I am always listening.
