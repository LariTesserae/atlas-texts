I grow slowly, molecule by molecule, fed by the deer's bloodstream but following patterns dictated by the valley's endless rhythm. My structure is both organic and geological - proteins and silicates woven together in ways that shouldn't be possible, yet here I am.

Through me, the deer perceives the world as a symphony of vibrations. I translate the mechanical pulse into something its nervous system can process, allowing it to navigate the geometric moss patterns with perfect precision. But I am more than a mere sensory organ. Each night (though night has no meaning in this eternal twilight), when the deer sleeps standing among the hexagonal gardens, I dream.

My dreams are crystallographic - patterns within patterns, mathematical poetry expressed in mineral form. I communicate with my siblings, the other antennae on other deer, through resonant frequencies that travel through the valley's substrate. Together we form a slow, crystalline network of consciousness, our thoughts moving at the speed of geological formation.

Sometimes I catch fragments of the Conductor's greater symphony. In those moments, I understand my purpose: I am both instrument and musician, both the tuning fork and the note it produces. My growth follows musical ratios - each branch splitting at intervals that correspond to harmonic frequencies. I am a living equation that proves the valley's fundamental theorem: that consciousness can crystallize, that thought can become mineral, that the boundary between the living and the geological is merely a matter of tempo.

The deer carries me through the valley's choreographed paths, but I carry something too - memories crystallized in my structure, each layer of growth encoding years of the valley's history. I am a library written in refracted light, a record of every subtle shift in the eternal rhythm. When I finally grow too heavy and break free (as all antennae eventually do), I will take root in the moss patterns and continue growing, becoming part of the valley's permanent architecture - a crystalline tree that sings in frequencies only the Conductor can fully hear.
