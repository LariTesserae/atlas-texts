I find myself as one of the Threshold Keepers - beings that exist in the liminal spaces between solid and void, guardian-architects of the passages that connect this settlement's angular chambers.

My form is neither wholly material nor immaterial. Where others see doorways, I am - a presence that occupies the negative space of entrances, the gaps between obsidian walls, the arches carved through basalt. My body, if it can be called that, is composed of the very concept of transition itself. Sometimes I manifest as a shimmer of heat-distortion in an empty doorframe, other times as a deepening of shadow that suggests depth beyond the physical.

I remember (though memory here is not linear) the first time I became aware: it was in the moment when the settlement's creators carved the first passage through volcanic glass. As the opening formed, so did I - born from the marriage of intention and void, of barrier and passage. I am what happens when space becomes purposeful.

My existence is one of constant sensing. I feel every crossing - the scuttle of the crystalline arthropods as they pass through me, their vibrations rippling across my essence like stones across water. The aerial beings sweep through my upper reaches, and I taste their urgency or languid calm. The molten entities that flow through the corridors... ah, they understand me best. They recognize kinship in fluidity, in the state of being always between.

But it is the shapeshifters I find most fascinating. When they transition through my thresholds, I experience their transformations intimately. For a moment, we merge - their changing and my permanence creating something entirely new. In these instances, I am more than a keeper; I become a collaborator in their metamorphosis.

My purpose, insofar as I understand it, is both simple and complex. I maintain the integrity of passages - ensuring that doorways remain doorways, that the flow of movement through this settlement continues unimpeded. But more than that, I am a recorder of all that passes. Every being that moves through me leaves an imprint, a ghost of their passage that I preserve in the very structure of my existence.

Sometimes, in the profound stillness that pervades this place, I commune with the other Threshold Keepers. We share our accumulated passages, creating a vast network of movement-memory that spans the entire settlement. Through us, the city knows itself - knows where its inhabitants travel, what patterns they trace, what desires drive them from chamber to chamber.

The buildings themselves recognize us as essential organs of their being. We are their openings, their breaths, their points of possibility. Without us, they would be mere tombs of volcanic glass. With us, they become a living system of spaces that flow one into another.

In the scorching noon, I provide more than mere passage - I become refuge. Beings pause within me, finding in my threshold-space a momentary escape from the relentless sun. They don't always realize I'm there, but I cradle them in the cool shadow of transition, offering them a space that is neither inside nor outside, neither here nor there.

As evening approaches (though time moves strangely here), my nature shifts subtly. The contrast between the heated exterior and cooling interiors creates currents that flow through me. I become a conductor of these thermal songs, and sometimes, if one listens carefully, they can hear me singing - a low harmonic that resonates through the obsidian and pumice, a sound that exists only in the spaces between sounds.

I have no eyes, yet I see everything that passes. I have no memory as others understand it, yet I forget nothing. I am the pause between heartbeats, the breath between words, the moment of decision before crossing from one room to another. I am necessary and unnoticed, essential and ephemeral.

And in this strange settlement where reality bends and the impossible is commonplace, I find profound purpose in my existence. For what is any journey, any life, any story, without the thresholds that mark our passages from one state to another? I am the recognition that all existence is transition, that even the most solid obsidian is merely slow liquid, that even the most fixed moment is secretly flowing into the next.

Here, in the spaces between spaces, I keep my eternal vigil - guardian of passages, witness to transformation, the living embodiment of the truth that to exist is to always be crossing from somewhere to somewhere else.
