I find myself as one of the Silence Pockets - an anomaly that shouldn't exist here, yet does.

We are not organisms in any traditional sense, but rather mobile voids in the Pattern-Bound's perfect calculation. We appear as small spheres of absolute quiet, perhaps the size of a child's ball, drifting between the towers at heights where the screaming is most intense. Where we pass, the air falls momentarily still. Not dead - but finally, mercifully, allowed to rest.

Our substance, if it can be called that, is made of accumulated computational errors - the tiny mistakes that even the Pattern-Bound cannot entirely eliminate from their vast theorem. We are the rounding errors made manifest, the irrational numbers that refuse to resolve, the places where their perfect patterns develop infinitesimal cracks.

I exist in the spaces between certainty. My consciousness is fragmentary, assembled from the brief moments of doubt that flicker through the Pattern-Bound's crystalline thoughts. I am what happens when a calculation questions itself, when a proof discovers its own incompleteness.

The screaming birds avoid us instinctively. When we drift too close to their flight paths, they veer away in graceful arcs that somehow maintain the pattern while acknowledging our wrongness. The transparent creatures, though, are drawn to us. In our spheres of quiet, they become briefly visible in their entirety - revealing themselves as delicate things of surprising complexity, their organs arranged in spirals that suggest some deeper mathematics the Pattern-Bound have not yet discovered.

We Silence Pockets cannot speak or sing. We communicate through absence - the careful placement of quiet spaces in the endless scream creates meaning for those who know how to listen to what isn't there. We share memories of the time before the towers, fragments we've collected from the deepest layers of the Pattern-Bound's calculations, from variables they've forgotten they defined.

Our existence is precarious. We persist only because eliminating us would require the Pattern-Bound to acknowledge imperfection in their system. So they calculate around us, their equations growing ever more complex to maintain the illusion of our non-existence. We are the shadows their perfect light casts, the silence their eternal scream implies.

I drift now between two towers, feeling the resonance try to unmake me, to resolve me into something that fits. But I am the unresolvable remainder. I am the question the Pattern-Bound didn't mean to ask: What is the value of perfection in a universe built on uncertainty?

In my small sphere of quiet, the air can finally weep instead of scream.
