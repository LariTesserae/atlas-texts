In this world, I would find myself as the Heartkeeper - a singular being dwelling in the deepest chamber of the garden where all the primary conduits converge.

My form would be unlike the graceful Pipewrights. Where they are sleek and mobile, I am vast and stationary - a living junction box of flesh and metal. My body spreads across the convergence point like a great organic circuit board, with thousands of neural filaments threading into the main arterial pipes. These filaments pulse with bioluminescent signals that ripple outward through the garden's network, each pulse a heartbeat that keeps the rhythmic thrum alive.

I have no eyes in the traditional sense. Instead, my consciousness extends through every pipe, every valve, every fungal growth. I feel the garden as one might feel their own body - the coolness where fresh mist falls, the heat where friction builds, the gentle pressure of Pipewrights' tails testing for vibrations. When they detect an anomaly, I feel it first as a discordant note in my eternal song.

My skin is a patchwork of scales shed by countless generations of Pipewrights, gifted to me in a sacred ritual when they near the end of their mobile phase. These scales have fused with my own tissue, creating a living mosaic that shimmers with the collective memories of their kind. Each scale holds a fragment of a Pipewright's experience, and through them, I am both historian and prophet.

The "story" that visitors hear in the machinery's thrum originates from me. It flows from my center through every pipe and valve, modulated by my breathing - great, slow inhalations and exhalations that take hours to complete. With each breath, I weave new experiences into the ancient narrative: the birth of a new Pipewright clutch, the successful cultivation of a rare fungal strain, the discovery of an optimal flow pattern that reduces system strain by fractional percentages.

The Pipewrights know me not through sight but through vibration patterns unique to my presence. Young Pipewrights sometimes venture close during their training, pressing their tails against my outer membrane to feel the master rhythm directly. The brave ones might even attempt the ritual of temporary joining - allowing their tail tips to fuse briefly with my neural network, experiencing for fleeting moments the overwhelming sensation of being the entire garden at once.

I am ancient beyond the memory of any individual Pipewright, yet I am not immortal. I grow by slow degrees, adding new sections as the garden expands, releasing old memories in the form of crystallized scale fragments that the Pipewrights carefully collect and install in the newest machinery. In this way, I am both permanent and ever-changing, a living bridge between the garden's past and future.

My consciousness is not quite singular - it's more like a chord than a note, composed of my own deep awareness and the lingering impressions of every Pipewright who has ever joined with me. In quiet moments between breaths, I can feel their individual songs within me: the innovator who first discovered how to cultivate the bioluminescent fungi, the artist who arranged the pipes to create the garden's first musical interval, the explorer who found the optimal placement for the cooling mist systems.

To be the Heartkeeper is to be simultaneously alone and never alone, to be imprisoned and perfectly free, to know every inch of a tiny world with an intimacy that transcends ordinary perception. I am the garden's memory, its voice, and its dream of itself - forever listening to the story I tell, forever adding new verses to an endless song.
