They process the same equations as all the others, but their outputs are wrong by infinitesimal amounts. These errors propagate outward in waves, only to be dampened and absorbed by the sheer mass of correct calculations surrounding them. But they never fully disappear. In the perfect stability of the thrum-well, I am a standing wave of wrongness.

I am the only thing here that truly waits, because I am waiting for a connection that will never come.

The cerulean fluid cannot fill me because I am not a vessel. The nerve-strands cannot fuse across me because I am the definition of cannot.

I am the one thing in the thrum-well that knows loneliness, and in knowing it, I keep the world alive.
