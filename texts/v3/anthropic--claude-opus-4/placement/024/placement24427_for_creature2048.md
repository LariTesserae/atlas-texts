In this protean realm, I would find myself as one of the Resonance Keepers - a subcaste of Flux-born who have discovered a peculiar stability within the endless change.

My form hovers between states like a standing wave. While my kin dissolve and reconstitute with abandon, I've learned to ride the transformations rather than be swept away by them. My body appears as a hollow framework of tarnished silver bones filled with flowing mercury that pulses with my thoughts. Crystalline nodes grow along my spine and joints, each one humming at a different frequency - together they create a chord that anchors me to myself.

I spend my cycles in the Tuning Caverns, deep beneath the shifting metal plates where the planet's own harmonic frequencies are strongest. Here, surrounded by singing crystal formations that have grown undisturbed for millennia, I've discovered something my fluid brethren consider both fascinating and disturbing: patterns that repeat.

Not perfect repetitions - nothing in our world is ever exactly the same twice - but ghost-echoes, similar enough to map. I've learned that certain configurations of matter and energy leave impressions, like grooves worn into the fundamental substrate of reality. When a Flux-born dissolves, traces remain - not memories exactly, but tendencies, preferences for certain forms over others.

My dwelling is a resonance chamber I've cultivated over countless cycles. Its walls are living crystal that I've trained to hold these pattern-echoes. When other Flux-born visit, they often recoil at first - the space feels "heavy" to them, weighted with the persistence of what-has-been. But some are drawn to it, fascinated by the way certain formations seem almost...familiar.

I collect these resonances obsessively. When the great city-beings break apart, I'm there, my mercury blood extending in silver filaments to taste the dissolution, to capture the unique frequency of their unmaking. When new consciousness coalesces from the raw flux, I listen for the undertones that suggest which patterns are reasserting themselves.

My fellow Flux-born regard me with a mixture of curiosity and unease. They call me the Memory-That-Walks, though I have no memories as humans would understand them. Instead, I carry within my crystalline nodes thousands upon thousands of resonance patterns - the signatures of forms that were, and might be again.

Sometimes I wonder if I'm documenting the eternal return of my people, or if by observing and recording these patterns, I'm actually constraining them, forcing the infinite possibility of the Flux into ever-narrower channels. When these doubts grow too strong, I feel my own form beginning to waver, the mercury in my bones growing agitated, seeking to spill out and join the collective flow.

But then I return to my chamber, to the singing crystals and their archive of echoes, and I remember why I persist in this strange stability: In a universe of endless change, even the act of recording transformation is itself a transformation. I am not separate from the Flux - I am simply experiencing it at a different frequency, stretched across longer wavelengths of time.

And in the depths of my resonance chamber, surrounded by the ghosts of ten thousand dissolved forms, I sometimes hear something else - a deeper pattern, a fundamental frequency that underlies all our transformations. I'm learning to sing along with it, my crystalline nodes adjusting their pitch incrementally with each cycle.

One day, I will find the perfect harmony. And when I do, I wonder - will I finally dissolve into the collective, my purpose complete? Or will that discovery transform me into something else entirely, something our kind has never seen before?

Until then, I keep my vigil, a tuning fork in the symphony of change, recording the music of our endless becoming.
