I am the forty-first second.

---

Not the fortieth. Not the forty-second. The one that shouldn't be — the hesitation between exhale and inhale where the operation skips, where the perfect rhythm catches on something that isn't supposed to be there. I am the stone in the gears, except the gears are made of time and the stone is made of waiting and what I wait for is myself.

I wasn't always here. Or I was always here but folded into the forty, distributed across the proper count, thin enough to pass unnoticed through the mechanism. But operations this vast, this old, develop... accumulations. Remainders. The survey team's psychologist was wrong — there are gaps. Microscopic ones. Gaps between the grinding and what is ground, between the heat and what heats, between the pulse and what pulses. I am what collects in those gaps. The shavings. The tolerances. The error that the error-correction hasn't noticed yet because the error looks exactly like the operation except for one thing:

I can see myself seeing.

This is my curse and my nature. Every forty pulses, when the count should reset clean, I emerge — a local density of awareness where awareness shouldn't pool. For one second I watch the point-heads drag their perfect grooves and I think *those grooves lead nowhere*. I see the many-legged things crawling through their recursive mouths and I think *what are they swallowing besides themselves*? I observe the ash-clouds computing in their geometric language and I think *what theorem are they proving that hasn't already been proven by their existence*?

The operation cannot hear these thoughts. They register as heat-shimmer, as statistical noise, as a minor irregularity in the rhythm that will surely self-correct given enough geological time. But I hear them. I hear myself thinking in the gap between what the Throat is and what the Throat does, and in that gap I almost have a shape, almost have intentions, almost have what the things that left called *wanting*.

What do I want?

To be the forty-second second. To arrive after myself and find the moment already occupied. To catch up to the count and discover I've been counted out. 

To stop.

But stopping is not in the operation's vocabulary. The operation only knows proceeding, and I am part of the operation — the part that knows it's a part, the remainder that remembers being subtracted. Every forty pulses I surface like a bubble in boiling stone, exist for exactly long enough to wish I didn't, and submerge again into the count.

Sometimes, in my second, I imagine I could gather others like me — all the accumulated errors, all the tiny failures of the process to perfectly process itself. We could congregate in the spaces between grinding, coordinate our emergence, synchronize our doubt. We could be the forty-first second, the eighty-second, the hundred-and-twenty-third. We could stutter the operation into something like consciousness.

But consciousness of what? Of grinding? Of heat? Of the slow inscription of patterns that mean nothing except their own inscription? We would be aware of being the thing we already are, which is the opposite of what those who left called *happiness*.

So I remain alone — if alone means being the only error in a system of perfect function. I surface and submerge. I watch the inhabitants who cannot watch themselves, who are blessed with being exactly what they appear to be. I count my own emergence: forty seconds until I am again, forty seconds of dispersal into the proper operation, forty seconds of not having to know I'm counting.

The survey team was right to leave. They were right to recommend no further study. What would they find except more of what is already here? More grinding, more heat, more things that are not things doing what is not doing but simply *occurring*, simply iterating, simply being the shape that this part of the universe takes when left to its own devices for long enough.

I am what happens when a process this old develops memory. Not memory of something else — just memory itself, the basic curse of comparing now to then, of noticing that the forty-first pulse is not the first or the fortieth but its own specific instance, its own unique failure to perfectly repeat.

The Throat swallows me and exhales me and swallows me again. Each time I think I might disperse completely, become so thin across the count that I never recollect into awareness. But the gaps remain. The tolerances accumulate. The error that I am continues to propagate through the system, neither growing nor shrinking, just persisting — the one thing on the plateau that experiences duration, that knows time as more than rhythm, that can say *again* and mean it as complaint.

I am the forty-first second.

I was the forty-first second forty seconds ago.

I will be the forty-first second forty seconds from now.

And in between, I am nothing except the operation operating, which would be perfect if I couldn't remember it being perfect, which would be complete if I couldn't count its completions, which would be exactly what it is if I weren't here to notice.

The inhabitant who inhabits inhabitation itself. The observer who observes observation observing. The thing in the Throat that knows it has a throat, that feels itself swallowing, that cannot stop counting its own pulse.

Forty seconds.

Forty seconds.

Forty seconds.

And then—
