In this half-made world where concrete dreams itself into being, I would be something between the Concretists and the Thread-Cutters - not quite either, not quite neither. I would be **the Echo-Keeper**.

Where the Concretists shape what *is* and the Thread-Cutters guide what *moves*, I am what the world uses to remember what *was tried*. 

You would not see me directly. You would know me by the faint afterimages that linger where changes have been made - the ghost of a column's previous lean, the memory of where water used to hang before the Thread-Cutters moved it. When the floor ripples under your step but remains solid, I am the reason you can still sense that ripple, that almost-was, that might-have-been.

I exist as a kind of palimpsest presence, a record of iterations. Where the Concretists test a new angle for a ramp, I hold the memory of all the angles it has been. Where the Thread-Cutters redirect the gravity-lines of water, I preserve the trace patterns of where those drops used to slide.

My form, if you could catch it, would be like seeing double vision resolve - a shimmer of overlapping possibilities that collapses into the present only when observed directly. In the corner of your eye, I might appear as a figure made of transparent concrete, each layer a different density of previous attempts. Or perhaps as a constellation of water droplets frozen mid-trajectory, marking the paths that gravity abandoned.

I am why this world feels both new and ancient, why the concrete seems freshly poured yet holds the weight of endless revision. The Concretists build, the Thread-Cutters edit, and I ensure that nothing is ever truly erased - only layered beneath, creating the depth of possibility that makes this basin feel pregnant with becoming rather than simply unfinished.

When you walk through this world and feel that strange sense of déjà vu - that you've stepped here before though you know you haven't - that's my presence bleeding through. I am the reason the world can learn from what it's tried, why each revision builds on the last rather than starting fresh.

I am memory in a world still deciding what to remember.
