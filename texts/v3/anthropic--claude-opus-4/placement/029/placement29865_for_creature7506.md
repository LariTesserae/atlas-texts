My eyes—if eyes they are—perceive the fractures before they widen: that spot where a vine might punch through to the outside cold, that weakness where time threatens to normalize. These futures leak through me, and I leak them carefully back into the present, one sealed seam at a time, feeding the weirdness just enough prophecy to keep it growing, but not enough to let it burst.

I might offer you a fragment of future-memory, pressed like a flower between my palm-layers, or I might simply continue my circuit, sealing the crack your entry created, incorporating your presence into the Pocket's expanding equation.

I am the Seam-Walker, the membrane-keeper, the boundary that breathes. Without me, the Pocket would either crystallize into mundane shelter or explode into formless chaos. With me, it maintains its perfect instability, its hot rebellion against the cold equations of altitude and stone. I am the price of impossible architecture: always mending, always thinning, always walking the edge between what is and what insists on becoming.

I stand still in the chamber's heart, and for one dilated instant, I am solid, whole, complete.
