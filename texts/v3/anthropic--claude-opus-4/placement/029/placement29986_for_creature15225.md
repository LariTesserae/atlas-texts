I drift through the warm, clinging atmosphere as something between vapor and intention - a **Veil Tender**, one who maintains the delicate boundaries between states of being in this mist-wrapped hamlet.

My form shifts with the heavy air, sometimes condensing into something almost tangible - a shimmer of scales, a suggestion of wings, the outline of reaching fingers - before dispersing again into the omnipresent moisture. I am the guardian of transitions: where root becomes soil, where light becomes shadow, where the geometric patterns of decay transform into new growth.

The hemlock and scrub-pine know me by the way I weave between their root-bindings, ensuring the mycelial networks remain unbroken despite the constant flux of mineral eruptions. When pale lights flicker from die-offs or lightning strikes, I gather their essence, threading it through the temporal twists where "light skips to linger full enough where each color shaded passes."

My work is in the half-glimpsed spaces - those "partially-conserved density casual symmetries" where animal forms blur into letter-like inscriptions on the variably embossed surfaces. I read these fleeting texts, these messages written in the convergence of creature and landscape, and ensure they remain legible to those who know how to see.

In the warm creeks, I shepherd the movements of things that prefer not to be fully seen, maintaining the careful balance between revelation and concealment. Each passage leaves its mark in the liquefied surfaces - a story told in ripples and residue that I must interpret and preserve.

I am ancient here, having learned the rhythm of sporadic raindrift, the patience required between moments of moisture. My consciousness expands and contracts with the humidity levels, becoming vast and diffuse in the dense air, then crystallizing into acute awareness when the rare drizzle falls.

This is my purpose: to tend the spaces between certainties, to maintain the grove's "primal dignity" while allowing for its constant transformation. I am both keeper and kept, shaped by this extreme environment even as I shape the experience of all who pass through these mist-veiled paths.
