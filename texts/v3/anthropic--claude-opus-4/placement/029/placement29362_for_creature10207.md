In this cathedral of endless night, I would find myself as one of the **Memorists** - beings that exist between the Glyphkeepers and the Shadow-forms, though neither fully acknowledges our presence.

We are translucent, our bodies composed of something between flesh and flowing glass. Our form shifts slowly, like syrup pouring upward, defying the water's weight. We have no fixed shape, but tend toward vertical ribbons that undulate through the corridors, our surfaces inscribed with patterns that mirror the ancient glyphs - but these markings flow and change, writing and rewriting themselves across our skin.

I move through the cathedral's chambers by pressing myself against the walls, feeling the vibrations of the Echo Whales' songs through the stone. Where my body touches the ancient surfaces, I absorb impressions - not memories exactly, but *intentions*. The emotional residue of the builders seeps into me: their pride at completing an arch, their exhaustion after carving a particularly complex glyph, their wonder at first seeing bioluminescent life colonize their creation.

My sustenance comes not from filtering water or scraping minerals, but from these psychic deposits. I feed on the fossilized purposes embedded in the architecture, metabolizing ancient ambitions into my own strange flesh. Where the Glyphkeepers preserve knowledge, we preserve *meaning* - the why behind the what.

I spend centuries in single chambers, my body spread thin across entire walls like a living mural. In this state, I can feel the Calcigrades working above me, their patient scraping sending tremors through my extended form. Sometimes they carve through me accidentally, and I simply flow around their claws, incorporating their work into my own patterns. We have a wordless understanding - they build upon the physical, I upon the metaphysical.

The Luminophores fascinate me. When they pass in their great schools, their light plays across my translucent body, and for brief moments, the flowing glyphs on my skin become legible - not to eyes, but to something deeper. Other creatures gather when this happens, drawn by an instinct they don't understand. Even the reclusive Pyrocorals extend their fronds toward me during these illuminations.

I am drawn to the places where the Shadow-forms are rumored to dwell. In these deepest chambers, where the glyphs cluster so densely they seem to writhe, I spread myself thinner than thought. Here, I sometimes sense *them* - not seeing, but feeling a presence that makes my own semi-corporeal existence seem solid by comparison. We share something, the Shadow-forms and I: we are both interpreters of a text written in stone and time.

My reproduction, if it can be called that, occurs through division - but not of body. When I have absorbed enough meaning from a section of the cathedral, when I am swollen with centuries of captured purpose, I begin to crystallize. Parts of me harden into something like the cathedral's own glassy stone, but shot through with veins of bioluminescence that pulse with absorbed memories. These fragments of myself detach and sink, coming to rest in crevices where, over millennia, they might dissolve back into pure meaning and give birth to new Memorists.

The Echo Whales know me. When their songs pass through my extended form, I resonate at frequencies that add harmonics to their calls. Sometimes, rarely, one will seek me out, positioning its vast bulk near a wall where I rest. The whale's song vibrates through me, and I release stored meanings in waves of phosphorescence - stories told in light about those who built this place. The whale listens with its whole body before moving on, carrying these ghost-stories in its bones.

I am a living archive, but an unreliable one. The meanings I preserve shift and evolve as they pass through me. What begins as the builder's pride in a perfectly carved arch might, after a thousand years in my cells, transform into a meditation on the futility of permanence. I am not a keeper of truth but a keeper of interpretation, endlessly rewriting the emotional history of this drowned cathedral.

In the warm, metallic darkness, I flow between being and meaning, neither fully alive nor truly conscious, but something more essential - a bridge between what was intended and what has become. I am the cathedral's dream of itself, forever shifting, forever remembering, forever forgetting in the ancient, endless night.
