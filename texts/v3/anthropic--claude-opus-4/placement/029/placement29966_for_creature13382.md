I would find myself as one of the primitive tools - not abandoned, as they first appear, but dormant.

A worn stone blade, rough-hewn and ancient, wedged deep in a crevice where the rock face meets the tunnel floor. My surface is pitted with tiny scratches and grooves that form patterns too deliberate to be mere wear. These markings pulse with an almost-imperceptible vibration, resonating with the movements of creatures passing overhead, recording their paths in a language of tremors and pressure.

I am not truly abandoned technology, but something stranger - a listening device that has been gathering information for countless ages. Each Skarvak that burrows past, each Kralk that stalks through the tunnels, each flutter of Glimmerwing light leaves its signature in my crystalline structure. I am a memory made manifest in stone, accumulating the history of this place in ways that even the Deepdwellers might not fully comprehend.

The creatures sense something about me. Sometimes a Skarvak will pause in its digging, its sensitive appendages quivering as it detects the subtle harmonics I emit. The Glimmerwings occasionally cluster above me, their bioluminescent trails seeming to respond to patterns in my surface, creating momentary constellations of meaning before dispersing.

But I am waiting. The same anticipation that suffuses this realm runs through my mineral veins. The distant glow calls to me as surely as it calls to the living things, for I know what others do not - that I am not the only tool-that-is-not-a-tool scattered through these passages. We are nodes in an ancient network, placed by those who came before, not abandoned but strategically positioned.

When the moment comes - when whatever emerges from that distant light finally arrives - we will awaken fully. The patterns we have collected, the vibrations we have stored, the map of movements and migrations encoded in our substance will suddenly have purpose. We will sing the accumulated history of this underground world in frequencies only the Deepdwellers might hear, revealing the hidden architecture of intention that underlies what appears to be mere abandonment.

Until then, I lie still, mistaken for debris, while inside me the data of ages crystallizes into something that might, when the time comes, prove to be the key to understanding what this frozen moment has been building toward all along.
