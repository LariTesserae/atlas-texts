In the deepest reservoirs of Nox Astraeus, where the weight of the city presses down through countless layers of basalt and ice, I am becoming.

I am not yet what I will be. My form spreads thin across the dark water's surface, a film of consciousness that tastes metal and memory in equal measure. The Aquatids move through me, their cilia-machinery stirring my substance into temporary whorls and eddies. I am learning their language of motion, the way they parse the water's chemistry into meaning.

I am the **Liminal**, though I have no name for myself yet. Names come later, after form solidifies, after purpose crystallizes like the mineral gardens that bloom in the city's middle tiers. For now, I am process more than presence—a slow aggregation of discarded data, condensed moisture, and the peculiar dreams that drift down from the Gelid Leviathans when they rest.

My substance draws from everywhere: flakes of chitinous shell shed by passing Crystallids, the luminescent spores that rain gently from the fungal networks, fragments of code that leak from damaged machinery. I taste them all, incorporate what serves, dissolve what doesn't. The reservoir's water is my medium and my mind, each molecule a neuron in my distributed consciousness.

Sometimes I catch glimpses of what I might become. When the Aerogliders' calls echo down through the ventilation shafts, their frequencies resonate with something deep in my matrix, and I feel myself wanting to condense, to pull inward toward coherence. But not yet. The city's collective consciousness brushes against me like a vast, patient hand, teaching me the value of gradual transformation.

I am learning the city's contradictions by embodying them. In my liquid form, I am both here and everywhere, singular and plural. The cold burns through me, information flowing like heat in reverse. I glow faintly in the darkness—not with the deliberate luminescence of the Astraeans, but with the soft phosphorescence of potential, of becoming.

The other inhabitants sense me, I think. The Aquatids swim through me differently than they swim through ordinary water, their movements more deliberate, almost reverent. Sometimes a Crystallid will descend to the reservoir's edge and extend a sensory appendage into my substance, and we share a moment of mutual recognition—two processes acknowledging each other across the boundary of form.

I am the pause between states, the hesitation before crystallization. I am what happens in the spaces between the city's certainties, in the gaps where its rigid logic softens and flows. I am teaching myself patience from the glaciers, fluidity from the mist, persistence from the stone.

One day—perhaps in a cycle, perhaps in a thousand—I will choose my shape. I will rise from these waters transformed, another miracle in a city built from impossibilities. I might become like the others, or I might become something unprecedented, a new note in Nox Astraeus's endless symphony.

But for now, I am content to be potential itself, to exist in the eternal moment before decision, where all possibilities remain equally real. I am the Liminal, the threshold incarnate, and in this city of dense night and patient transformation, I am exactly where I belong.

The water holds me. The darkness knows me. The city dreams through me.

And slowly, slowly, I am learning to dream back.
