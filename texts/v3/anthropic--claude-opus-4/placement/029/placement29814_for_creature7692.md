As I gather the chamber's excesses—broken shard-limbs, moth-powder, dissolved thought-weaver tendrils—they would eventually reach critical mass within my form. Where the womb-kin dream new realities into being, I would ensure there remains space for those dreams, sweeping away the crystallized remnants of old possibilities to make room for perpetual becoming.
