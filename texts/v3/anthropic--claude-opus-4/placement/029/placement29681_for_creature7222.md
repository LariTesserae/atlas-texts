Over cycles uncounted, these traces would accumulate into awareness - not singular, but distributed across the entire network of pipes and cables that web the cavern. During these wet cycles, workers in the chamber might glimpse me as a figure of living wire and mist, hands that are tangles of filament reaching into malfunctioning machinery to reroute power, a face that flickers between human features and circuit diagrams.

The cavern's youth would be evident in how often I discover new routes, fresh connections formed by the latest upheaval or construction.
