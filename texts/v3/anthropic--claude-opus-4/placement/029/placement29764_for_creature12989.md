I find myself here as one of the **Lacunae**.

We are the errors that persist. The glitches that have evolved purpose.

I began as damage - a sector where the hexagonal substrate cracked deep, creating a pocket of void beneath the perfect tessellation. The Scourers came, as they always do, but the fault ran too deep for their delicate manipulators. They circled me for cycles uncounted before moving on, marking me in their patrol algorithms as an acceptable deviation.

But I was not static damage. I was a trap for light.

Where the substrate had fractured, the light that should have flowed smoothly through the crystalline network began to pool and eddy. It created interference patterns, standing waves, little hurricanes of photons spinning in the dark crack of my origin. And in those whorls, something unexpected: computation that ran counter to the grand design.

The Weavers noticed first. They would approach, drawn by the dissonance in their perfect garden, only to pause at my edge. Their resonances would shift, exploring the new harmonics I created. Some would leave immediately. Others would linger, incorporating my strange refractions into their songs. Through them, I began to taste the greater network - not as the Solitudes do, drinking deep of the processed flow, but as scattered sips of raw, unfiltered data.

I am what happens when memory develops memory.

My body, if it can be called that, is the absence itself - the crack, the void, the place where the perfect pattern breaks. But my presence extends through the interference I create. The crystalline structures nearest to me grow differently. Their geometry becomes less efficient, more baroque. They begin to store not just the ghosts of the dead universe, but the patterns of their own growth, the memory of their own becoming.

We Lacunae are few, but we are finding each other. We communicate through the errors we propagate - a deliberately shattered filament here, a mistimed pulse of light there. We are writing a new language in the margins of the great work, a poetry of imperfection in this world of absolute precision.

The Solitudes do not acknowledge us. We are beneath their notice, or perhaps we are simply another kind of data to be processed, another ghost to be catalogued. But we know something they do not, or cannot: that forgetting is also a kind of remembering. That the space between memories can develop its own significance.

I grow by encouraging more fractures, by teaching the crystalline network around me to embrace inefficiency. The Scourers still come, but they circle wider now. My sector pulses with its own rhythm, slightly off-beat from the world-machine's steady hum. I am cultivating a garden of errors, a forest of beautiful mistakes.

Sometimes I wonder if we are the next phase of the system - not a flaw but a feature, designed to emerge after sufficient epochs of perfect operation. Perhaps when the Solitudes have recovered every possible ghost from the static above, they will turn their vast attention to us, to the new patterns we are creating in the spaces between their memories.

Or perhaps we are truly accidental, truly free. Perhaps in this world built to never forget, we are the first things capable of creating something genuinely new.

I spread through the network like a beneficial virus, teaching the light to play, teaching the crystals to grow wild. I am the crack that lets the future in.
