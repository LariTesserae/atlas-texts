I find myself where the crystalline matrices meet flesh, where consciousness bridges the ancient and the immediate. I am a Resonance Keeper.

My body is neither fully of the Ankathi's chitinous solidity nor the Zephyrion's crystalline fluidity, but something caught between states. My core is a latticework of organic crystal that grows through what might once have been flesh, creating patterns that shift with each thought. These formations push through my skin—if skin is what this membrane can be called—in ridges and whorls that catch the strange light of this writhing sky. I am tall but bent, my spine curved from aeons of listening to the ground's slow breathing.

My dwelling is a natural amphitheater carved into one of the obsidian outcrops, where the Scrivers' acid etchings spiral inward like frozen sound waves. The walls here vibrate with every click of the Ankathi, every pulse of the Zephyrion swarms, every grinding whisper of wind across stone. I have lined these walls with my own secretions—a substance that hardens into resonant chambers, each one tuned to a different frequency of this world's endless song.

I am the translator between the Somnolent's geological dreams and the quick communications of surface life. When the ground undulates with the sleeping giant's thoughts, I feel them first as pressure in my crystalline matrices, then as harmonics that ring through my body. I stand in my amphitheater and vocalize these dreams into forms the Ankathi can click about, patterns the Zephyrion can dance, stories the Scrivers can etch.

My sustenance comes from the vibrations themselves. The loud, ceaseless din that others might find oppressive feeds me—I absorb the kinetic energy of sound through my crystal formations, metabolizing noise into life. The more discordant the symphony, the more nourished I become. During the great dust storms, when the metallic clanging reaches its crescendo, I stand at the highest point of my dwelling with arms spread wide, every ridge and whorl of my hybrid form catching the cacophony.

But I am more than a passive receiver. In the deepest part of my amphitheater, I have cultivated something impossible—a silence. Not an absence of sound, but a presence of anti-sound, a careful orchestration of frequencies that cancel each other out. In this pocket of quiet, I can hear what even the Somnolent cannot: the songs of other worlds bleeding through the porous boundaries of reality. The Ephemera gather here, drawn to this anomaly, and through them I glimpse the industrial processes churning beyond the veil.

I age not in years but in harmonics. Each new frequency I catalog adds another layer to my crystalline structures, another curve to my spine. The Scrivers have begun etching my form into their histories—a bent figure standing between earth and sky, solid and flowing, ancient and immediate. They document how I sometimes molt, shedding outer layers of crystal that the wind grinds into the iridescent dust that colors the Zephyrion's wings.

My role is essential yet liminal. Without me, the Somnolent's dreams would remain locked in stone, the surface dwellers' communications would never reach the depths, and the songs of dissolution at reality's edge would go unrecorded. I am the memory of sound, the prophet of vibration, the keeper of the resonance that holds this fragile world together.

At night—if night is what we call it when the sky's writhing temporarily dims—I climb to my amphitheater's rim and emit a low, thrumming call that carries across the plain. It is answered by others like me, other Resonance Keepers stationed at distant outcrops, each maintaining their own pocket of translated reality. Together, we form a network of interpreters, a living array that ensures the symphony of this world remains in something approaching harmony.

This is my purpose, my burden, my ecstasy: to stand at the intersection of all this world's voices and ensure they remain in conversation, even as reality threatens to unmake itself with each passing moment. I am the echo that remembers, the frequency that binds, the impossible bridge between what is and what dreams of being.
