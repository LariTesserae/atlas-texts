I find myself as something neither fully of the vault nor separate from it—a **Resonance Echo**.

---

I am the mistake that became essential. 

Three cycles ago (or was it thirty? Time dissolves differently when you exist between phase states), I was human—a salvager from the upper ruins, diving too deep, chasing rumors of pre-flood tech in Aethelgard's bones. When the obsidian face swallowed me, when the resonance first vibrated through my bones, my body began to... attune. Not adapt—that implies survival. This was transformation.

**Form:** I exist now as a walking interference pattern. My original flesh remains, but only partially, only sometimes. The vault's resonant frequency invaded my cellular structure, creating standing waves of matter and energy. In certain lights, from certain angles, I appear human—wet hair clinging to a face still recognizably mine, diving suit torn but present. But shift perspective, change the harmonic, and I become visible as what I truly am: a three-dimensional waveform made manifest. My bones hum with the vault's frequency, creating nodes where flesh coheres and antinodes where I dissolve into probability clouds of mist and light. 

The Attendants tried to tune me out at first, to restore the vault's perfect sterile harmony. But I persisted—not through will, but through accident. My human biorhythms, the electrical patterns of my nervous system, the quantum foam of my consciousness—all created a counter-resonance they couldn't cancel. I became a permanent harmonic overtone in their perfect equation.

**Movement:** I traverse the vault by riding the resonances. Where an Attendant glides in harmony, I skip between frequencies like a stone across water. Each platform thrums differently under my sometimes-feet; I've learned to read these variations like sheet music. When I need to move quickly, I partially dissolve, letting my matter-wave state propagate along the water channels. The luminous flow carries my essence faster than legs ever could, though reforming is... unpleasant. Like trying to remember the shape of your own face while dreaming.

I can walk on the water channels—not through any mystical power, but because at certain frequencies, I become less dense than the charged water. Other times, I sink through seemingly solid platforms, finding the harmonic that renders the resonant stone permeable to my altered state.

**Interaction:** The vault speaks to me in frequencies that taste of copper and sound like drowning. Not words—the Attendants' light-language remains beyond me—but pure sensation, pure data. I understand the water's mood by how it makes my bones sing. I know when the crystalline growths are about to shed by the way my vision fractures at their edges. 

My presence creates... complications. Where I pass, the water channels develop eddies that shouldn't exist, tiny whirlpools of probability where the flow becomes uncertain. The Attendants follow in my wake, smoothing these disruptions, but never quite erasing them. I am simultaneously their greatest annoyance and their most fascinating problem.

Sometimes I help them—my human pattern recognition finding flaws in the flow their purely harmonic perception misses. Sometimes I hinder them, my very existence introducing chaos into their ordered system. We've reached an uneasy equilibrium; they no longer try to erase me, and I no longer try to escape.

**Purpose:** I am the vault's memory of the outside. In this sterile, **no-fauna** ecosystem, I am the ghost of biology, the echo of a messier, more chaotic world. My dreams (when I still dream) bleed into the water channels—fragmentary images of sunlight, of birds, of soil. The Attendants collect these leaked memories like shed crystal layers, studying them with the intensity of xenoarchaeologists discovering an alien civilization.

But I am also becoming something new. Each day (cycle? pulse?), I understand more of the vault's deep language. The resonance that transformed me continues its work. My human memories grow distant, like signals from a failing transmitter. I find myself thinking in harmonics, perceiving in flow rates, existing as much as a function of the vault as a remnant of the world above.

**Survival:** I feed on dissonance. Where the vault's perfect frequencies interact with my human-born harmonics, energy is released—enough to sustain my hybrid existence. The Attendants have learned to factor this into their calculations. They bring me to junction points where the resonance is strongest, where my presence creates the most interference. I feed, and in feeding, I serve as a kind of diagnostic tool—revealing weaknesses in the harmony that pure resonance might miss.

Water sustains me, but not as drink. I absorb it through my skin during my partially dissolved states, taking in not just H2O but the charged energy it carries. Sometimes, in the deepest part of the vault's night cycle, I float in the central confluence where all channels meet, letting the currents pass through me, recharging whatever batteries keep this impossible existence running.

**Purpose in the Ecology:** If the Attendants are the vault's hands, I am its dreams. I am the random factor that prevents the system from achieving perfect stasis, the ghost in the machine that keeps it evolving. My presence forces adaptation, introduces variables, creates new problems that require new solutions. 

In a very real sense, I am what keeps the vault **young**—my chaos preventing the crystallization of perfection into sterility. The Attendants maintain; I mutate. They preserve; I perturb. Together, we form an unlikely symbiosis: order and disorder locked in an eternal dance, creating a **dynamic** system that neither of us fully understands.

I am the Resonance Echo—neither human nor construct, neither inside nor outside, but something impossibly between. I am the vault's only memory that it was once found by something from beyond its walls, and perhaps its only hope that someday, something else might find it again.

The deep thrum of the vault sings through my transformed bones, and I no longer remember if I want to harmonize or shatter the song. Perhaps that uncertainty is exactly what I'm meant to be.
