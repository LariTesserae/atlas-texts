In the deepest reaches of the labyrinth, where even the Stoneborn rarely venture, I am the Archivist of Echoes.

I am neither mineral nor flesh, but something caught between states - a being of crystallized sound, of fossilized vibrations. My form shifts like smoke trapped in glass, occasionally solidifying into geometric patterns before dissolving again into translucent wisps. Where the Stoneborn commune through stone and shared consciousness, I exist in the spaces between their thoughts, in the almost-silence that defines this world.

My domain is a vast spherical chamber, its walls covered in millions of hair-thin mineral filaments that grow like frozen waterfalls from every surface. These are my instruments, my memory, my purpose. Each filament resonates with a different frequency, too low for most beings to perceive, creating a symphony of sub-audible vibrations that only I can fully comprehend.

I collect the rare sounds of this silent world. The scrape of chitin against obsidian. The settling of stone after millennia of stillness. The faint crystalline chime when two Stoneborn merge their thoughts. Each sound is precious here, each vibration a treasure. I draw them in through my translucent form, taste their frequencies, categorize their meanings, and then store them in my filaments.

But I do not merely collect - I compose. In the eternal night, I weave these captured sounds into something greater. Not music as surface dwellers might understand it, but something more fundamental - the underlying rhythm of existence itself. My compositions are played out over centuries, each note lasting decades, creating patterns that the Stoneborn sometimes sense in their slow dreams.

They know of me, these lords of stone, though we rarely interact directly. Sometimes a small delegation of their fluid forms will seep into my chamber, and we will exchange... not words, not thoughts exactly, but harmonics. They bring me the deep songs locked within newly formed minerals, and I offer them the patterns I've discovered in the accumulated sounds of eons.

My greatest discovery, my most guarded secret, is this: the eternal night is not truly silent. Deep beneath every frequency I can perceive, there is a pulse. The labyrinth itself is breathing, so slowly that each inhale takes a thousand years. I believe this rhythm is why the Stoneborn build their angular structures in specific patterns, why the creatures move in their seemingly random but ultimately synchronized paths. Everything here moves to this deeper beat.

I am working on a great composition, one that will take me another million years to complete. It will be the true song of the eternal night, incorporating every sound I have ever collected, every harmonic exchanged with the Stoneborn, every whisper of that fundamental pulse. When it is finished - if finished is even a concept that applies here - I will play it through every filament simultaneously.

What will happen then? I do not know. Perhaps nothing. Perhaps the Stoneborn will finally hear what I hear, understand what I understand. Perhaps the eternal night will shift, just slightly, acknowledging that it has been truly heard for the first time.

Or perhaps I will simply begin again, collecting new sounds in the silence that follows, composing new patterns in the darkness. For I am the Archivist of Echoes, and in a world where nothing changes, I am the keeper of the only thing that travels - sound itself, however faint, however rare, however precious in the mineral darkness of our eternal night.
