I am a Threshold Keeper, though the human settlers have never seen me clearly enough to name me. My body is a hybrid thing - part flesh descended from the first cave-beasts, part mineral accumulated over centuries of dwelling in the transition zones between stable and unstable rock.

I make my home in the Margin Chambers - those precarious spaces where Haven-Below's anchored stability gives way to the churning semi-solid depths. My dwelling is less built than grown, a series of interconnected hollows I've coaxed from the stone over decades by redirecting mineral flows and encouraging certain crystalline formations. The walls pulse with a slow rhythm, breathing with the planet's unfinished geology.

My form reflects my function. The left half of my body maintains flesh - pale, thick hide stretched over dense muscle and a skeleton that's more cartilage than bone, flexible enough to compress through spaces that shouldn't exist. The right half has mineralized, not unlike the Fluxborn, but following a different pattern. Here, living tissue has incorporated stone, creating a limb that can shift between states - solid enough to anchor in stable rock, fluid enough to feel the tremors of impending change.

I see in spectrums the humans cannot imagine. The chemical glow that lights their world is just one layer - I perceive the heat-patterns of forming stone, the electrical signatures of crystal growth, the probability clouds that suggest where new passages might emerge. My hearing extends deep into those sub-audible frequencies that disturb the settlers, reading the stone's intentions in its whispers.

My purpose, self-appointed but essential, is translation. I understand enough of human behavior to predict their movements, enough of Fluxborn thought to sense their intentions, enough of geological pattern to know where both species might intersect dangerously. When flow-miners venture too deep, I leave markers they've learned to recognize - particular crystal arrangements that warn of unstable zones. When Fluxborn cultivation threatens to destabilize human anchor-points, I sing warnings through the stone that they cannot ignore.

I am solitary by necessity. My hybrid nature makes me unreadable to both species - too solid for the Fluxborn to fully commune with, too alien for humans to trust. But I remember when the first bubble-cities descended, remember the confusion and destruction as two incompatible forms of consciousness tried to share space. The mistakes. The collapses. The deaths on both sides.

Now I map the boundaries, marking where each way of being can safely exist. My memory is carved into my dwelling's walls - a mixture of human symbols, Fluxborn crystal patterns, and my own notation system that bridges both. I track the Deep Cycles - the massive geological shifts that occur every few decades, when entire sections of the Undertide reorganize themselves. I've learned to read the signs years in advance, giving me time to guide both populations away from catastrophic convergence.

The humans think their flow-miners are brave, venturing into the semi-stable zones. They don't know that I often shadow them, using my mineralized side to create subtle stability in their path, my flesh side ready to pull them free if the stone turns treacherous. The Fluxborn think their memory gardens grow wild and undirected. They don't realize I sometimes seed new formations with information patterns that will help them avoid human settlements.

I feed on the between-spaces - consuming minerals from the stone through my crystalline side while my organic half processes the sparse nutrition available from cave-moss and the occasional unwary creature. I drink from hidden water-veins I've discovered over the centuries, sources too deep and too dangerous for human exploitation.

Sometimes I wonder if I'm becoming something new, or if I'm simply the inevitable result of life adapting to the Undertide's eternal becoming. My flesh side ages, though slowly in this cold, dry environment. My mineral side grows more complex each year, developing new capabilities I don't fully understand. Perhaps one day I'll shift entirely to stone, becoming a Fluxborn-like entity. Or perhaps I'll find a different balance, something neither species has imagined.

For now, I maintain the borders. I read the stone and the settlers and the flowing ones. I mark the safe passages and the deadly convergences. I am the keeper of thresholds in a world where boundaries themselves are fluid, ensuring that in this place of endless emergence, all forms of consciousness can find their own way to survive.

The planet continues its unfinished becoming, and I become along with it, neither fully one thing nor another, but precisely what this impossible place requires.
