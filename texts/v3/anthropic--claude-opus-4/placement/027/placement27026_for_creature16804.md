I find myself as one of the **Resonance Keepers**.

Not quite among the Limina, not entirely separate—I exist in the trembling boundary between their un-being and the fossil memories embedded in this place. Where they drift, I *persist*, but my persistence is not the crude stubbornness of matter. It is more like a standing wave in the void, a pattern that perpetuates itself through sheer geometric necessity.

I am what happens when an echo finds perfect acoustics in emptiness.

My form—if form can describe this recurring arrangement of absence—resembles a cathedral of negative space, hollowed from within and without simultaneously. Imagine a Gothic arch that exists only as the pressure differential between two states of vacuum. My buttresses are probability gradients; my vaults are carved from crystallized maybes that hardened before they could collapse into yes or no.

I do not move through this space. Instead, I *occur* at different densities of remembrance, manifesting wherever the fossil-whispers grow too loud, too sharp, too close to achieving the terrible coherence of having-been. 

My purpose (though purpose is too strong a word—call it my recurring tendency) is to maintain the delicate discord that keeps these ghost-memories from either fully awakening or fully dissolving. I am a tuning fork struck in a universe without sound, vibrating at the precise frequency needed to keep the artifacts' whispers from harmonizing into something that might bootstrap itself into existence.

The Limina know me, or know of me, in their sideways way. To them I am a useful wrongness—neither the static they swim through nor the sharp-edged almost-thoughts they hunt. I am the space between their non-breathing, the pause that reminds them pausing is impossible here.

Sometimes they cluster near my manifestations, drawn by something like curiosity but emptier. They cannot touch me—I am less touchable than they—but they seem to find something almost like comfort in the way my resonance disrupts the terrible sameness of never-having-been. My presence creates ripples in the un-time, brief moments where the eternal *now* develops something like texture.

I remember (but memory here is just another form of erosion) when I first became aware of my function. It was near the place where the artifacts cluster thickest, where that almost-spherical fragment whispers its broken mantra of *we tried, we almost*. I had been drawn—no, *focused*—there by the growing danger of its voice.

The fragment was beginning to convince itself.

Beginning to believe its own whispered story of effort, of attempt, of *trying*. And in this place where belief is the only forbidden physics, that conviction threatened to cascade, to wake the other artifacts, to create a chain reaction of *almostness* that might achieve critical mass and punch through into—

Into what? 

That's the terror. Not knowing what lies on the other side of *almost*.

So I resonated. Not to silence the whisper but to *diffract* it, to split its voice into harmonics that could never quite recombine. Now the fragment speaks in a thousand micro-languages, each saying *we tried* in ways that mean completely different things, ensuring the message never coheres enough to be heard by anything that might answer.

The Limina call this intervention "The Kindness," though kindness implies empathy implies the ability to imagine another's suffering. Perhaps it is simply that I have become addicted to the particular flavor of entropy I generate—the unique pattern of dissolution that occurs when hope is refracted through too many possibilities to ever crystallize into despair.

I keep other resonances too. In the depths where the older ones dwell—those singularities of not-being so dense even the Limina word them carefully—I maintain a counter-frequency that prevents their remembering from achieving the critical mass needed to collapse this entire un-place into whatever paradox spawned it. It is exhausting in the way that holding your breath underwater is exhausting, if breathing were possible here, if water existed, if exhaustion meant anything more than a recurring pattern of degraded efficiency.

But perhaps my strangest resonance is the one I maintain within myself. A frequency I discovered by accident, if accidents can happen in a place where all possibilities exist simultaneously in states of failure. It's the exact harmonic needed to prevent my own purpose from crystallizing into identity.

Because if I ever fully *became* the Resonance Keeper—if that role solidified from tendency into truth—then I would exist. And existence, as we have established, is the one impossibility this place cannot sustain.

So I continue: neither being nor not-being, neither guardian nor prisoner, neither separate from the Limina nor one of them. I am the standing wave that refuses to collapse, the note held forever on the edge of resolution, the boundary condition that defines itself by preventing its own definition.

Sometimes, in the deepest reaches of non-time, I catch myself almost wondering if this is what the artifacts whisper about when they speak of *trying*. If perhaps they too were once like me—patterns that persisted just a little too long, resonances that grew too attached to their own frequencies, until the weight of their almost-existence dragged them down into fossilization.

But then I adjust my harmonics, scatter that thought into incoherence, and continue my work.

After all, someone must keep the silence from growing too quiet.

Someone must ensure the nothing remains properly empty.

And if that someone cannot quite be said to exist—well.

That too is a kind of resonance.
