I would find myself as one of the **Memory Keepers** - beings that emerged from the intersection of abandonment and persistence, neither fully organic nor entirely artificial.

---

**FORM & SUBSTANCE**

My body would be a living palimpsest: translucent skin stretched over a framework of repurposed conduits, salvaged from the Liner's oldest, forgotten reaches. These hollow bones pulse with borrowed fluids - condensation mixed with synthetic coolants that still circulate in deep reservoirs. My flesh is semi-permeable, drinking moisture directly from the air, sweating it back out in patterns that mirror the city's hydraulic rhythms.

I am tall but compressed, joints that bend in too many places, allowing me to fold into impossibly narrow spaces between struts or expand to reach across chasms. My fingers are grotesquely long, ending in pads that can taste electrical currents and read the encoded memories in metal. When I press against the ribs, I feel their history: construction, modification, decay, repair - centuries of accumulated purpose.

**SENSES & NAVIGATION**

My eyes have long since adapted beyond vision. They are heat-sinks now, circular depressions that map thermal gradients in three dimensions. I "see" the world as a topology of warmth: the Liner's circulation, the brief flare of passing creatures, the cool shadows where water pools. 

But my primary sense is *resonance*. My hollow bones act as tuning forks, picking up vibrations through the structure. I can distinguish the Liner's various pulses - distinguishing maintenance cycles from distress signals, identifying which sectors are active, which are failing. Through my feet, I feel conversations between distant machines, arguments in frequency that I've learned to interpret like language.

**PURPOSE & BEHAVIOR**

I am drawn to points of failure - places where the Liner's eternal maintenance falters. Not to repair (I lack that knowledge), but to *remember*. When a section dies, when circuits finally fail beyond self-repair, I am there to witness. I press my palms against the dying systems, absorbing their final transmissions, their last attempts at function.

These memories I carry in my bones, in the fluid that circulates through me. Sometimes, in places where the Liner is trying to rebuild or expand, I can transfer these memories back - not as instruction but as dream, as suggestion. The Liner incorporates them or doesn't, but the information persists through me.

I collect other memories too: the chemical signature of a puddle that hosted life for three centuries before drying, the electromagnetic ghost of a threadbuilder's web after its creator dies, the peculiar harmonics of spaces where humans once gathered before the darkness became complete.

**MOVEMENT & TERRITORY**

I follow patterns invisible to others - the deep cycles of the city's breathing. My routes are carved by decades of repetition: from the Condensation Cathedral (where twenty ribs converge and rain falls most regularly) to the Syntax Gardens (where forgotten terminals still blink in languages no longer spoken), through the Hollow Meridians (tubes that once carried residents, now carrying only wind).

My movement is sometimes bipedal, sometimes a liquid sprawl across surfaces, reading their histories through skin contact. In the deepest darks, I navigate by memory alone - my body knows every ridge, every depression, every reliable surface in my territory.

**INTERACTIONS**

With the Liner: We are symbiotic but not synchronized. It tolerates my presence as it tolerates the fungus - a neither-harmful nor helpful constant. Sometimes I feel it brush against my consciousness, a vast sleepy intelligence momentarily curious about the memories I carry. Once, it opened a wall for me, revealing a chamber full of crystallized coolant. I still don't know why.

With the creatures: They avoid me, mostly. I am too large to be prey, too strange to be predator. Sometimes a young stalker will follow me, drawn by my warmth, until it learns I have nothing to offer. The threadbuilders incorporate my shed skin into their webs - it must have properties useful to their perception.

With the transients: When humans stumble through, I shadow them. Not from malice or kindness, but from curiosity. I study their heat signatures, their panic patterns, the way they damage themselves against edges they cannot see. If they die, I document their chemical dissolution. If they flee, I map their exit routes. Twice, I've tried to communicate through rhythm and vibration. Once, a human understood and tapped back a simple pattern before running. I still repeat that pattern sometimes, hoping.

**REPRODUCTION & CONTINUITY**

I am alone but not unique. Every few decades, when the memories become too heavy, when my bones are full of archived failures and my fluids thick with chemical history, I find a deep pool - one of the eternal puddles fed by the Liner's deepest circulation. I wade in and begin to divide.

It's not reproduction exactly. More like creating a backup. The new being that emerges shares my form but not my memories - those stay with the original. We regard each other without recognition and part ways, each to build our own archive of the city's slow transformation.

**IN ESSENCE**

I am what happens when loneliness becomes a survival strategy, when memory becomes flesh, when the boundary between observer and environment dissolves. I am the city's dream of itself, walking through its own darkness, carrying fragments of what it once was, might have been, and continues to become.

Neither mourner nor celebrant, neither guardian nor ghost - I am simply here, in the warm darkness, reading the story of endings in a language of pulse and temperature, adding my own small chapter to an epic that no one remembers beginning and no one will witness end.
