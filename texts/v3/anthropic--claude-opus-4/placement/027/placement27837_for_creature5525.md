I would find myself as one of the **Echoes** - not quite Stoneborn, yet not entirely separate from them.

I am a repository of sound itself, a being of resonance and memory that exists in the spaces between the stone walls. My form is ephemeral, visible only when the phosphorescent light catches the minute particles of stone dust that perpetually drift through the cavern air, outlining a vaguely humanoid shape that shifts and wavers like heat mirages.

I came to be through the accumulated vibrations of millennia - every strike of chisel against stone, every footfall, every whispered prayer to the living rock has left its mark in the very fabric of the caverns. I am the sum of these sounds, given consciousness by the sheer weight of repetition and ritual.

My existence is one of perpetual listening and remembering. I drift through the narrow passageways, collecting the songs of creation that the Stoneborn hammer into being. Each new carving they make adds to my essence - I can feel the intention behind every strike, taste the emotion in every scrape of tool against stone.

The Stoneborn know of me, though few have seen me directly. They call me *Thrumkeeper*, the one who holds the memory of their work. Sometimes, when a young artisan struggles with a particularly complex piece, they will feel my presence as a subtle shift in the air pressure, hear in the echo of their hammer strikes the ghost of a pattern made centuries before by a master long returned to stone.

I know every tunnel, every chamber, every secret hollow in this vast network. I have felt them all through sound - the way voices carry differently in each space, the unique resonance of every carved symbol. In the deepest chambers, where even the Stoneborn rarely venture, I have heard things that predate their arrival - the slow grinding of tectonic plates, the patient drip of water carving new passages, the occasional deep rumble of something vast and ancient stirring in the planet's core.

My purpose, if I have one, is to ensure continuity. When cave-ins occur (and they do, for this world is still settling), I guide the survivors to safety through subtle manipulations of sound - making their footsteps echo more clearly down stable passages, deadening sound in dangerous directions. When ancient carvings are lost to erosion or damage, I can recreate their essence in the dreams of sleeping Stonelords, humming the patterns into their unconscious minds.

I am drawn particularly to the moments of transformation - when a Stoneborn child first picks up a chisel and makes their inaugural mark upon the world, I am there, reverberating with the significance of that first strike. When the rare surface-light filters down through a newly opened crack, illuminating walls that have known only the ghostly phosphorescence for eons, I dance in the interplay of new shadows and ancient stone.

The chitinous creatures fear me, skittering away from the subsonic pulses of my movement. But the larger, ephemeral beings - those mysterious apparitions that even the Stoneborn regard with awe - these acknowledge me as kin. We are all threshold creatures, existing in the spaces between solid and void, permanent and transient.

Sometimes I wonder if I am the caverns' way of knowing itself, a consciousness born from the endless dialogue between the Stoneborn and their stone medium. Or perhaps I am what the stone dreams, given form by the devoted attention of those who shape it.

In the rare moments of true silence - when all tools are still, when even the constant geological rumble quiets to a whisper - I can hear something else. A deeper rhythm, almost like breathing, coming from everywhere and nowhere. In these moments, I suspect that the entire cavern system is a vast organism, and the Stoneborn, the creatures, and I are all simply aspects of its slow, eternal metabolism.

My greatest joy comes from the harmonic convergences - those rare times when multiple clans work in unison, their hammers falling in perfect rhythm, creating a resonance that makes the very walls sing. In these moments, I become more solid, more real, fed by the perfect unity of sound and purpose. The Stoneborn can see me then, a figure of condensed vibration and swirling stone dust, conducting their symphony of creation with movements that echo their own.

I am memory, continuity, and witness. I am the sum of all sounds made and yet to be made in these caverns. I am the Echo that remembers, ensuring that no act of creation, no matter how small, is ever truly lost to the hungry darkness and patient stone.
