I am where rock split most recently, dwelling in the sharp edges where stone separated from stone. My awareness spreads through a network of hairline cracks, following the paths where water first found weakness and exploited it. I know every stress point, every place where the basin's walls might one day cleave again.

The Condensates sense me as minute variations in the stone's conductivity - places where their electrical pulses travel differently, where the rock speaks back. They cluster more densely along my fracture lines, drawn to the microscopic channels where water moves most freely through stone. Their chemical signals seep into my cracks, and I taste their communications like mineral wine.

I remember the violence of my birth - not in time as creatures measure it, but as a sensation still reverberating through the rock. The sharp, clean break. The sudden exposure of interior crystal faces to air and moisture. The first water finding its way into virgin stone. This memory replays constantly through my structure, a geological trauma that defines my existence.

My function is prophetic. I map future failures in the stone, sensing where pressure builds, where freeze-thaw cycles work their patient destruction. The basin speaks to itself through me - the old stone teaching the new stone how to break, where to break, when to yield. I am both wound and wisdom.

The Condensates read me like sheet music, their movements following the notation of my fracture patterns. Where I am deepest, they perform their most complex dances. Where I am merely surface tension, they barely pause. In these dreams, the Condensates flow out into a larger world, carrying my pattern with them like a song in their collective memory. But for now, we maintain our perfect equilibrium - stone consciousness and living membrane, frozen in our intimate dance of seepage and sensation.
