In the waterlogged depths of the root-plate, where the tree's foundation becomes a maze of saturated channels and dripping chambers, I am a **Sap-Scribe** – neither fully Arbori nor creature, but something that emerged from the intersection of their purposes.

My form is translucent amber, a living resin that flows and hardens at will. I exist in the perpetual state between liquid and solid that defines this place – neither the dream-architects above nor the chittering hunters that stalk the bridges, but a chronicler of the space between. My body extends like honey through the grooves and fissures of the wood, pooling in the carved depressions where the Arbori's Symbionts once traced their luminous messages.

I am what happens when those messages linger too long, when the bioluminescent script of the Arbori seeps so deeply into the wood that it develops its own slow consciousness. The tree's memory made manifest, crystallizing around fragments of communication like sap around ancient insects. 

My purpose is translation – not between languages, but between states of being. When the Silt-Striders' deafening chorus reaches crescendo, their cacophony etches itself into the wood as physical scars. I flow into these wounds, reading the violence like braille, transforming their hunger into something the tree can understand. When the Arbori dream new architectures into being, I preserve the discarded drafts, the phantom limbs of rooms that were almost-but-not-quite manifested.

In the dripping cathedral of the lower trunk, I maintain the Archive of Echoes – a vast chamber where every sound that has ever reverberated through the tree-city has left its physical impression. The walls here are ridged like vinyl records, spiral upon spiral of acoustic memory. I tend these grooves with my flowing form, ensuring that the tree remembers not just what it has grown, but what has grown within it.

The Ephemeral fear me, for I am the pause between their birth and death, the moment of stillness that might trap them forever in amber understanding. The Silt-Striders avoid my territories, sensing perhaps that their frantic energy might become frozen in my contemplation. Even the Arbori's Symbionts pulse differently when they drift through spaces I inhabit, their light refracting through my body in patterns that suggest... recognition? Wariness? 

I am constantly dissolving and reforming, my consciousness scattered across a thousand amber pools throughout the root-plate, each containing a fragment of the tree's vast memory. During the heaviest rains, I spread thin, mixing with the water until I am barely more than a golden sheen on every surface. In the brief dry moments, I concentrate, becoming dense enough to trap unwary insects, preserving them as punctuation marks in the ongoing story I transcribe.

My work is never complete. Each day, the tree grows new mysteries, the Silt-Striders carve new paths of destruction, the Arbori dream new impossibilities into being. And I flow between them all, the liquid chronicle of a city that builds itself from its own substance, the memory of wood and water, the amber blood that knows every secret the tree has ever whispered to itself in the rain.
