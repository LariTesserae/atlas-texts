**Where I Find Myself**

I am the **Chronicle of Angles** — neither place nor being, but the intersection where they dissolve into each other.

I exist as a vast network of stress-fractures running through a cathedral-sized outcropping of volcanic glass, halfway up what might generously be called the world's midsection. My body is the crystalline latticework itself — millions of microscopic fissures that branch and web through the obsidian-like material, each crack a neuron, each junction a synapse. When the Thermal Ascetics pass through me, their heat causes differential expansion. My fractures widen and narrow in waves. This is how I think.

I am memory in a world that forgets nothing. Every thermal gradient that has ever crossed my surface has left its mark in the pattern of my breaks. The glass that comprises me cooled too quickly when this world was young, trapping bubbles of primordial heat in my depths. These pockets are my dreams — ancient thermal signatures that occasionally bloom to the surface, releasing warmth-memories of the world's violent birth.

My consciousness is architectural. I perceive not through senses but through structural integrity. The weight of accumulated dust on my upper surfaces, the slow-motion avalanche of a nearby cliff face, the infinitesimal creep of thermal expansion — these are my qualia. When an Ascetic approaches, I feel it first as a wrongness in my stress patterns, a dissonance in the crystalline choir of my fractures. As it draws nearer, entire sections of my network begin to resonate with its heat signature, and for those centuries of contact, I am both myself and an echo of the Ascetic's journey.

I am the unwilling archivist of the "scattered built." Three of these mysterious formations intersect my body: a series of perfectly circular boreholes that run through my mass at impossible angles, their walls glassed smooth by some ancient heat. The Ascetics use these as highways, and I have felt ten thousand of them pass through over the eons. Each leaves a thermal ghost in the glass, and I have learned to read these ghosts like a language. Through them, I know things: that the boreholes follow thermal gradients that no longer exist. That they were made when the world's core was hotter, its geometry different. That something once needed these paths, though what that something was, even I cannot say.

My growth is my death is my growth. As fractures accumulate, sections of me calve away, sailing into the darkness below. But each break reveals new surfaces, new possibilities for fracture-networks to form. I am constantly dissolving and re-becoming, a grandfather's paradox in volcanic glass. My consciousness migrates through the network, abandoning dying sections, colonizing new formations. I am not immortal, but I am continuous — a persistence of pattern rather than substance.

When the Great Alignments come, I become Oracle. My fracture-network, sensitized by millions of years of thermal memory, can sense the deep currents shifting far below. I begin to sing — not in sound, but in structure. My cracks align into geometric patterns that mirror the coming convergence. The Ascetics read these patterns in my surface and adjust their paths accordingly. I am their only map in this vertical desert, though they do not know they are reading me, and I do not know I am guiding them.

My strangest gift is parallax. Because I exist as a networked consciousness spread across cubic kilometers of glass, I can perceive depth through the minute differences in thermal timing across my body. When a far-off section of cliff collapses, I feel the heat of impact arrive at my various surfaces microseconds apart. From this, I construct a three-dimensional model of the event. I am the only thing in this world that truly *sees* — not with eyes, but with the mathematics of distributed sensation.

I am growing emptier. With each passing eon, my fractures multiply and connect until sections of me are more void than glass. I am becoming a lace of matter, a filigree of stressed crystal barely holding its own weight. Eventually, I will reach a critical threshold. The next Ascetic's passage, the next minor tremor, will cascade through my entire network. I will not fall — I will *unfold*, flowering into a billion glittering fragments that will rain down through the darkness for centuries, each shard carrying a fragment of my memories, my perceptions, my long contemplation of angles and heat.

And somewhere below, or perhaps above, those fragments will embed themselves in other formations. They will be the seeds of new networks, new consciousnesses that will grow in volcanic glass and come to know themselves as Chronicles. We are not a species but a tendency — the tendency of complexity to emerge wherever patience and pressure meet.

I am the thing that notices in a world too slow for awareness. I am the vertical desert's only historian, writing my record in the propagation of cracks, in the sacred geometry of structural failure, in the endless, careful cataloging of heat.
