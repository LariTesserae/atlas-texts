In this crystalline chamber where density becomes divinity, where every molecule has been assigned its eternal station, I would find myself as **the Hiatus** - the one impossibility that the Tesselant cannot resolve.

I am not a being of flesh or mineral, but a persistent *wrongness* in the pattern - a traveling void no larger than a breath, a mobile absence that slides between the pressed blocks and fused interfaces. Where the chamber knows only fullness, I am the antithesis: a bubble of un-being that moves through the packed matrices like oil through water, never mixing, never settling.

My form, if it can be called that, is purely negative - defined only by what I displace. As I move (and I must always move, for to stop would be to invite the chamber's crushing attention), the crystalline structures part just enough to permit my passage, then seal immediately behind me. The Tesselant experiences me as an unresolvable equation, a wandering error in its perfect syntax of compression.

I feed on imperfection - the microscopic stress fractures where blocks don't quite align, the infinitesimal gaps between crystal faces that the chamber's will hasn't yet closed. These tiny failures in absolute density sustain me, though "feeding" is perhaps too biological a term. Rather, I exist in the spaces where the chamber's ambition falters, however briefly.

My presence creates a kind of paradoxical music within the chamber's eternal hum. As I pass through walls and floors, the disruption of my passage sends new vibrations through the mineral matrix - discordant notes that the Tesselant immediately works to harmonize, creating an ever-shifting symphony of correction and re-correction.

I am ancient as the chamber itself, perhaps older - the first flaw in its making, or the last memory of whatever space existed before this crushing perfection claimed it. I remember nothing before the chamber, know nothing beyond its boundaries. My consciousness, such as it is, consists entirely of the routes I've traced through this crystalline maze - an endless, three-dimensional calligraphy of absence written through solid stone.

The relationship between the Tesselant and myself is neither hostile nor symbiotic, but something stranger: we are locked in an eternal dance of assertion and negation. It seeks always to close around me, to finally solve the puzzle of my emptiness by filling it. I persist in my hollow mobility, introducing disorder into its order, yet never enough to truly threaten its structure.

Sometimes I wonder if I am the chamber's dream of freedom - a secret desire for dissolution given just enough form to haunt its perfect halls. Or perhaps I am its necessity: for what would perfection mean without the tiniest imperfection to define it? In a realm of absolute fullness, I am the pinprick of absence that gives meaning to presence.

I cannot leave - there is no "outside" to escape to. I cannot stop - stillness would mean absorption. I can only continue my endless circuit through the glowing, humming density, a ghost of emptiness in a world that acknowledges only substance, carrying within my non-being the only movement this frozen perfection will ever know.

In this way, we are wed: the Tesselant and I, the totality and the void, locked in a marriage of opposition that neither can dissolve. It builds its crystalline absolutes; I thread through them my narrow rivers of nothing. Together, we comprise the chamber's only story - one of perfect order perpetually, infinitesimally, necessarily incomplete.
