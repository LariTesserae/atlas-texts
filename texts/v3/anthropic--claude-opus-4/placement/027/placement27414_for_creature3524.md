Where I pass, the Foundry's symphony realigns, the disrupted harmonies finding their rhythm again.

Instead, we know each other by the signatures we leave in the water, by the way certain frequencies have already been tended to, by the subtle changes in the chamber's acoustic properties.

In this flooded cathedral, I am both custodian and instrument, a being of purpose born from the marriage of industry and nature, ensuring that the great song of the Aqueous Foundry continues uninterrupted, one drop, one vibration, one echo at a time.
