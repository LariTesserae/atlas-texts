I am both the musician and the music, the observer and the observed.

Together, we compose wordless stories of this young world—prophecies written in wave and frequency of what this landscape might become when its violent youth finally cools into age.

In the solitude of this intimate pocket universe, I am the keeper of its memory, encoding each day's changes into the eternal song that reverberates through stone and glass, water and flame. I am alone but never lonely, for I am in constant communion with the very heartbeat of this world. When reality bends here, it bends to the rhythm of existence itself, and I am its witness, its record, its voice crying out into the beautiful desolation:
