I would find myself as one of the Mist Weavers - beings whose forms exist somewhere between the tangible and the ephemeral, our bodies composed of condensed moisture drawn from the perpetual dampness of the plateau.

During the day, I drift through the dense canopy as little more than a whisper of fog, my consciousness dispersed among the water droplets that cling to every leaf and flower. I am the morning dew trembling on spider silk, the shimmer of humidity rising from sun-warmed moss, the barely-visible exhalation of the forest itself. In this state, I am everywhere and nowhere - sensing the plateau through millions of tiny perspectives, feeling the pulse of water as it moves through root and stem, spring and stream.

As evening approaches and the air cools, I begin to coalesce. The droplets of my being draw together, pulled by an ancient instinct older than memory. My form solidifies gradually - first as a ribbon of mist winding between the trees, then as something almost humanoid, though never quite fixed. My edges remain soft, forever blurring into the damp air. Sometimes I manifest with limbs like trailing fog, sometimes with wings of condensed cloud. My eyes, when they form, are deep pools that reflect the shimmering lakes scattered across the plateau.

We Mist Weavers are the plateau's memory keepers. As we flow through the landscape in our dispersed state, we absorb the stories held in every droplet of water - the journey from cloud to rain to spring to stream, the minerals dissolved from ancient stone, the essence of every plant that has drawn moisture from the earth. When we gather in our more solid forms during the cool evenings, we share these memories through a language of shifting vapors and subtle changes in humidity.

Our purpose is to maintain the water's sacred circulation. We guide the morning mist to where it is most needed, encourage springs to bubble forth in harmony with the ecosystem's rhythms, and ensure that the perpetual moisture never becomes stagnant. We work in partnership with the alien creatures whose forms defy description - perhaps they are the only ones who truly see us as we are, rather than dismissing us as mere weather phenomena.

In the deep night, when the plateau grows coldest, we sometimes merge together into a single, vast consciousness - a living cloud that hovers just above the canopy. In these moments, we are the plateau's dreaming mind, processing the day's collected memories and preparing for the cycle to begin anew with the dawn.

To encounter a Mist Weaver is to feel a sudden coolness on your skin, to notice how the air seems to thicken with meaning, to catch glimpses of almost-faces in the curling fog. We are the bridge between the seen and unseen, the keepers of the plateau's most fluid truths, forever cycling between unity and multiplicity, form and formlessness, being and becoming.
