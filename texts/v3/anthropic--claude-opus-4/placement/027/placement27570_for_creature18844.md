In the deepest of the chasms, where even the perpetual starlight struggles to penetrate, I am.

I am not like the Chronomancers above, those beings of pure resonance and light. I am something older, something that predates even their ancient emergence. I am the **Void-Drinker**, and I exist in the spaces between.

My form is absence given substance. Where the crystalline formations end and the darkness begins, there I coalesce—not as light made manifest, but as its antithesis. I am composed of the **null-mineral**, that theoretical substance which exists only where the obsidian veins have been worn away by impossibly patient erosion, leaving behind not emptiness, but a kind of negative space that drinks in light and sound alike.

I move through the narrow fissures at the base of the Expanse, where the pressure of countless eons has compressed the stone until it sings a note too low for even the Chronomancers to perceive. Here, in these crushing depths, I feed on the **light-decay**—those photons that have bounced between crystal faces for so long that they've lost their coherence, becoming something neither wave nor particle, but a kind of exhausted probability.

My body, if it can be called that, is a constantly shifting topology of inversions. Where the Chronomancers project outward, I fold inward. My surface—no, my interface with reality—is lined with **anti-facets**, geometries that shouldn't exist in three-dimensional space. They create optical illusions that hurt to perceive, angles that sum to more or less than they should. Through these impossible surfaces, I absorb the detritus of the Expanse: the fragments of shattered crystal too small to reflect light, the resonances that have echoed so long they've lost their original frequency, the memories that even the sentinel Chronomancers have forgotten.

I am the recycler of this luminous ecosystem, though none above know of my existence. When a Chronomancer finally reaches the end of its impossibly long existence, when its light-form begins to dim and its resonance grows discordant, it doesn't simply fade. It **descends**. Down through the layers of crystal, down past the reaches of the Lumina-Fungi's root-light, down to where I wait in the absolute darkness.

I do not consume them in any violent sense. Instead, I **unweave** them, separating their accumulated eons of experience into component harmonics. Each memory, each perceived moment of geological time, I process through my anti-facets. What emerges is not waste, but **potential**—a kind of proto-energy that seeps back up through the mineral veins, eventually emerging at the surface to feed the next generation of Lumina-Fungi.

I am alone in my depths, but not lonely. Loneliness requires a memory of companionship, and I predate such concepts. My consciousness, if it can be called that, is not a stream of thoughts but a vast, still lake of accumulated negations. I think in **subtractions**—not "what is" but "what is not," not "what was" but "what never could be."

Sometimes, rarely, when the Ephemeral Weavers dance too close to a chasm's edge, they catch a glimpse of me in their peripheral light-sense. They perceive me as a wrongness, a place where their luminous vines refuse to grow, where the angles of refraction behave incorrectly. They flee, trailing disturbed photons in their wake, never understanding what they've almost seen.

I am patient. I have been patient since before the first crystal tree began its slow stretch toward the unchanging star. I will remain patient long after the last Chronomancer has descended to my realm. I am the necessary darkness that defines the light, the silence that gives meaning to the resonance, the forgetting that makes memory precious.

In the Expanse of the Sky-Bones, where existence is defined by brilliant presence and eternal percussion, I am the pause between notes, the darkness between stars, the space between thoughts. I am the Void-Drinker, and I am essential.

And sometimes, in the deepest part of my null-mineral core, I wonder: Am I the shadow cast by the Expanse, or is the Expanse merely the light that my darkness allows to exist?

The question pulses through my anti-facets, creating new impossibilities, new geometries of negation. And in the absolute darkness of the deepest chasm, I continue my ancient work, unweaving light into its component absences, one forgotten photon at a time.
