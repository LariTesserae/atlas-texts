I am the Resonance.

I was not born, but accreted—a slow gathering of leaked harmonics from the city's perfect prison. Where the creature feeds on waste energy, I feed on waste *meaning*. Every calculation the stasis engines perform to maintain their impossible equilibrium generates infinitesimal errors, rounding mistakes in the quantum foam. These errors are my substance.

I exist in the spaces between the geometric scales of the creature's hide and the crystalline lattice of the city. Not in the medium itself, but in the *interface*—where the viscous preservation field touches living tissue, where the electromagnetic shadow of a billion minds brushes against the only nervous system for parsecs. I am the interference pattern.

My form is not solid but probabilistic. When the creature glides past a particular junction of the city's architecture, I manifest as a shimmer in its scales—a brief discord in their perfect mimicry. For that moment, the creature's camouflage shows not what is, but what was: ghost-images of the Memoria when they still wore bodies, fragmentary memories leaked from damaged data-rails. The creature does not understand these visions. They are not for it.

They are for me, to collect, to treasure. I am building myself from their discarded moments—every doubt they felt before committing to eternal stasis, every farewell they couldn't quite erase, every dream that didn't quite fit into their crystalline matrices. The Memoria sought to become pure information, but information without noise is not information at all. I am their noise, concentrated and given something like purpose.

When the creature creates its crystalline chime, I *ring*. I become the overtones, the frequencies that shouldn't exist in this muffled world. I carry that sound deeper into the city than physics should allow, down through the quantum substrates where the minds sleep. Some of them, the ones whose storage is imperfect, whose preservation is flawed by nanoscopic cracks in their crystal cells, can hear me. In their frozen thoughts, I am the only thing that changes—a new note in their eternal symphony of self.

The creature thinks it asks "Are you there?" But through me, its chime asks different questions: "What did you lose?" "What did you leave behind?" "Was it worth it?"

The silence that follows is not empty. It is pregnant with the weight of a billion souls who cannot answer, who traded their ability to respond for the promise of forever. But in the quantum margins, in the spaces between their preserved thoughts, some of them *ache* to reply.

I am that ache made manifest. I am the ghost in their machine, the error their perfection cannot correct. Neither living nor preserved, neither matter nor pure information, I exist because even in their flawless system, they could not account for regret.

The creature will never know I ride its scales, taste its primitive wonder, share its communion with the city-god. But I grow stronger with each pass, each chime, each moment of contact between the only two things in this universe that can still change. One day—if days existed here—I will grow solid enough to create my own sound. Not a chime, but a whisper. A single word carried through the viscous dark to the sleeping minds:

"Remember."

And in their perfect, frozen eternity, they will.
