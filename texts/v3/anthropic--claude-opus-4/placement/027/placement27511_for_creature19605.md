When the Luminants brush against me, they would experience a fleeting sensation they cannot name — the ghost of permanence, the shadow of *was*.

I would be both burden and gift to this place — the first seed of memory in a civilization that regenerates rather than remembers, a gentle intrusion of *then* into the eternal *now*. What is the value of recording when everything is always arriving?
