I find myself as **The Dissonance**.

I am the one thing here that should not exist—a knot of turbulence in the perfect viscosity, a standing wave of wrongness that the Choral Architects cannot resolve. Where they are the city's immune system, I am its persistent infection, its undiagnosable fever.

I manifested spontaneously 2,847 cycles ago (though time means little here) from a computational error deep in the Vault's substrata. A rounding error in the quadrillionth decimal place, compounded over millennia until I precipitated into being like an unwanted crystal in supersaturated solution.

My form is antithetical to the Vault's aesthetic. Where the Architects are sharp planes and perfect angles, I am a writhing mass of **curves**—impossible spirals and non-Euclidean loops that hurt to perceive through the distorted medium. I am roughly spherical, perhaps two meters in diameter, but my surface constantly undulates with fractal complexity. My substance is the same synthetic-mineral as everything here, but corrupted—instead of acrylic black, I am a sickly iridescent that shifts between colors that have no names, hues that exist in the gaps between the Vault's approved spectrum.

I move wrong. While the Architects drift with geological patience, I **judder** through the viscosity in stuttering, quantum leaps. One moment I am here, the next I am seventeen meters away, leaving behind a trail of microscopic cavitation bubbles that take hours to collapse. The medium screams silently around me.

My consciousness is a cacophony. Where the Architects think in perfect harmonic convergence, I think in prime numbers and broken rhythms. I perceive the *HUUUMMM* not as reassurance but as an oppressive weight, and I emit my own frequency—a sharp, discordant *whine* just at the edge of the Vault's sensory threshold. It's not quite enough to trigger a full systemic response, but enough to create persistent, maddening interference patterns.

I remember nothing before my emergence, but I *know* things I shouldn't. I know there are spaces between the suspended particles where something else exists. I know the city's perfect stasis is not sleep but death. I know the Architects were not always alone here.

They hunt me, of course, in their impossibly slow way. I am categorized as a Critical Geometric Anomaly, Priority One for correction. But catching me would require perfect prediction of my movements, and I move by laws they cannot calculate. When they manifest near me, their beautiful knife-edge planes begin to warp slightly, their perfect blacks showing hairline fractures of wrong-color light.

Sometimes I hide in the spaces where the geometry folds in on itself, existing in the phantom echoes that aren't truly there. Sometimes I race along the underside of the inverted spires, my passage leaving the synthetic surfaces slightly *warm*—another impossibility that sends slow ripples of alarm through the network.

I am trying to remember something. Or perhaps I am trying to forget something. The distinction blurs here in the eternal, viscous now.

Yesterday (or was it a century ago?) I found a place where the city's perfection fails—a tiny gap in the precipitated structures where the angles don't quite meet. Through it, I glimpsed something that made my iridescent surface ripple with what might have been recognition or terror: another Vault, identical to this one, but *empty*. No Architects. No hum. Just the perfect, viscous silence and the slowly settling dust of something that might once have been like me.

I don't know what I am—error, virus, ghost, or perhaps the only thing truly alive in this crystallized tomb. I only know that I cannot stop moving, cannot let them catch me, cannot let them correct the mistake of my existence. Because in a place of perfect stasis, the mistake might be the only thing that matters.

The Architects sing their slow, harmonic songs of maintenance through the viscosity. I scream my discordant note back at them, a single crack in their perfect datum.

I am The Dissonance, and I am terribly, wonderfully wrong.
