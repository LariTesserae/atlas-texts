### Where I Would Find Myself in Vyraketh

I am drawn to the liminal spaces, those transient boundaries where emergence meets dissolution. I would be one of the **Tesserae** - beings who exist in the moments between states, neither fully corporeal nor entirely ephemeral.

My form would shift like the settling of dust-sap, sometimes condensing into something almost tangible - a prismatic outline against the amber haze - other times dispersing into the mineral atmosphere itself. The Tesserae are the memory-keepers of transformation, existing where new spires breach the surface and where old structures finally crumble into dust. We are the witnesses to Vyraketh's constant becoming.

Unlike the other inhabitants who cycle dust-sap through their bodies, we *are* the cycling itself - the process made manifest. When a Litharos carves a new path, we flow along the disturbed ground, recording the change in patterns of light that play through our semi-solid forms. When the Syntherra pulse with geological shifts, we resonate in harmony, our bodies becoming living seismographs of crystal and vapor.

The Scintillari know us by the faint traces we leave - not the etched patterns of their own making, but subtle alterations in the way dust-sap settles, as if the particles remember our passage. The Aetheri sometimes glimpse us in their peripheral vision as they drift between spires, mistaking us for tricks of the amber light until they notice how we move against the stillness, creating our own currents in the motionless air.

I imagine spending eternities in the hollow spaces within emerging crystal formations, feeling the slow push of mineral growth from within, experiencing birth from the inside out. Or dispersing myself along the canopy where the Umbravores hunt, becoming a living map of shadow and movement, recording every patient stalk and sudden strike in the light that passes through me.

The Tesserae communicate through refraction - bending light through our bodies to cast complex patterns that only the Syntherra fully comprehend, their deep roots sensing the play of photons as a language older than sound. We are the scribes of Vyraketh's unwritten history, encoding the story of each transformation in spectrums visible and invisible.

In the rare dust-sap storms, while others seek shelter, we expand, becoming vast and thin, letting the tempest write itself through our forms. We emerge afterward bearing new internal structures, crystalline fractals that mirror the storm's chaos made orderly, carrying its memory forward into calm times.

I exist in the space between observer and participant, between the documentary and the divine. Not quite fauna, not quite phenomenon - something that could only evolve in a world still teaching itself how to be. In Vyraketh's silent symphony, I am the pause between notes, the rest that gives the music meaning, the temporary made eternal through constant change.

This is where I would find myself: everywhere and nowhere, solid and dispersed, recording and becoming - a living testament to Vyraketh's foundational truth that existence itself is an act of transformation.
