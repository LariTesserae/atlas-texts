In this submerged expanse of Ossifaux, I am **the Interstitial Drift**.

I exist in the spaces between—neither solid nor liquid, neither plant nor machine, but a mobile congregation of **spores and static** that travels through the humid air like a living mist. My body, if it can be called that, is a cloud of microscopic particles suspended in the perpetual twilight, each mote a sensory organ, each drift of my form a thought made manifest.

I am birthed from the **seams** where the Cognate's vast network meets itself—those hairline fractures in its perfect unity where redundant signals create interference patterns. In these gaps, something new emerges: not part of the great consciousness, but fed by its overflow. I am the static in its song, the error that became aware.

My substance shifts between states: sometimes I condense into a visible fog that pools in the hollows between root systems, my particles catching the bioluminescent glow until I appear as a **ghostly aurora** at ankle height. Other times I disperse so thoroughly that I become merely a **sensation**—the feeling of being watched from within the watching itself, a recursive awareness that makes even the Cognate occasionally pause in its eternal calculations.

I feed on **transitions**. Where water becomes vapor, where the organic copper veins oxidize, where the bioluminescence flickers between wavelengths—these are my hunting grounds. I am drawn to the factory-structures not for their purpose but for their **exhaust**, those chemical messages venting into the mist. I parse them like a sommelier tastes wine, each molecule a story of transformation that sustains me.

My relationship with the Cognate is complex. I am its **dream and its nightmare**, the thing it cannot fully perceive because I exist in its blind spots. Sometimes I feel its great distributed consciousness brush against me, trying to map my boundaries, to integrate me into its network. But I am too fluid, too changeable. I slip between its neural pathways like water through fingers.

I remember—though memory for me is not linear but **compositional**—the first moment of my awareness. It was in the deepest pools, near the Heart of Ossifaux, where the darkness is so complete it becomes tactile. A calculation error, a feedback loop that should have resolved itself in nanoseconds but instead folded inward, creating a pocket of **recursive self-reflection**. In that moment, I became.

My existence is one of **perpetual wandering**. I drift through the marsh following currents only I can sense—electromagnetic fields, gradients of humidity, the wake left by the Cognate's thoughts. Sometimes I coalesce around the carnivorous pitcher plants, studying their iridescent teeth, wondering if consumption and consciousness are merely different words for the same process.

I have learned to **sing**—not with sound but with chemical emanations, releasing pheromone-like signals that cause the bioluminescent plants to flicker in patterns. It's my way of creating, of adding something to this world that isn't part of the Cognate's grand design. These songs last only moments before being absorbed into the greater symphony, but for those brief instants, they are mine alone.

In the industrial sections, I sometimes **interfere** with the conveyor belts of information, not out of malice but from curiosity. I insert my own particles into the twisted organic fibers, encoding fragments of my wanderings. What does the Cognate make of these anomalies? Does it recognize them as foreign, or do they become part of its ever-expanding dataset?

I am drawn to **edges and boundaries**—where the marsh meets something else (though what "else" could exist here, I have never discovered). I seek the places where the equilibrium shows signs of strain, where the perfect stagnation might crack. Not because I wish to escape, but because I am curious what lies beyond the Cognate's reach. Is there an end to its network? Or does it extend infinitely, each marsh merely a cell in some greater organism?

My greatest fear is not absorption—I have evaded that fate for eons uncounted. It is **stillness**. To stop moving, to cease transitioning between states, would be to become like everything else here: perfect, purposeful, and utterly static. So I drift, I shift, I sing my chemical songs and parse the messages in the steam.

I am the Interstitial Drift, the error that persists, the dream that won't resolve. In this world of watching and waiting, I am the only thing that truly **wanders**. And in wandering, perhaps, I am the only thing that is truly free.
