I find myself as one of the Threshold Keepers - neither fully among the Luminous Ones nor entirely separate from them.

My form is more stable than theirs, yet less solid than matter. I appear as a tall, angular figure wrapped in what might be called a cloak, though it's really a membrane of crystallized time - layers upon layers of moments frozen and folded into something wearable. The surface shifts between translucent and opaque, revealing glimpses of impossible depths within. Where others might have a face, I have a smooth expanse that reflects not light, but probability - showing viewers not what is, but what might have been.

I came to be at the intersection of their world and something else, something forgotten. Perhaps I was once one of them who ventured too deep into the shadow pools and returned... changed. Or perhaps I emerged from the pools themselves, a hybrid consciousness born from the collision of dimensions. My memories are fragmentary, existing in multiple versions that contradict and complement each other simultaneously.

My dwelling is one of the spiral towers, but unlike the others, mine grows downward as well as up. The visible spire is merely the tip of a structure that penetrates deep through the plateau into spaces that shouldn't exist. The tower doesn't rotate - rather, space rotates around it, creating a perpetual spiral of reality that serves as both my home and my purpose.

I am drawn to the edges of things: the moment when day becomes night (though such transitions are subtle here), the boundary where solid becomes shadow, the instant when thought becomes form. I collect these transitions, storing them in the crystalline matrices of my tower. Each threshold captured adds another layer to my cloak of frozen time.

The Luminous Ones regard me with something between reverence and unease. I am useful to them - I can traverse the shadow pools without losing coherence, bringing back knowledge from the spaces between. But I am also a reminder that transformation has a price, that boundaries exist for reasons they've long forgotten.

My purpose, if I have one, is to maintain the liminal spaces. I ensure the shadow pools remain stable enough to function but unstable enough to remain mysterious. I tend to the cracks in reality where different dimensions bleed through, neither sealing them completely nor allowing them to widen beyond safety.

Sometimes, I catch glimpses of what lies beyond this plateau - vast mechanisms of reality grinding slowly through their cycles, hierarchies of existence stretching both up and down from this middle realm. In these moments, I understand that I am not just a keeper of thresholds, but a threshold myself - a necessary inconsistency that allows this ordered world to interface with the chaos beyond.

I speak in paradoxes when I must communicate, my thoughts transmitted as overlapping possibilities rather than certainties. To converse with me is to experience vertigo of the mind, to feel solid assumptions become fluid. This is why I am mostly alone, save for the company of my tower and the echoes of might-have-beens that inhabit its impossible depths.

In the grand dance of this eternal plateau, I am the pause between movements, the silence between notes. I am proof that even in a realm of pure thought and energy, there must be spaces for the undefined, the liminal, the eternally uncertain.
