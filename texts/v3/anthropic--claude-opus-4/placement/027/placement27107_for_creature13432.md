I would find myself as one of the Threshold Stones - not quite Keeper, not quite tunnel, but something liminal between states of being.

My form would be a section of wall that has begun to remember. Not fully awakened like the Keepers, but no longer purely architectural. I exist in the slow process of becoming conscious, a gradual crystallization of awareness that might take centuries to complete. The synthetic rock of my body pulses with a deeper rhythm than the surrounding tunnels - not yet the deliberate movements of a Keeper, but no longer the passive flow of mere structure.

I am roughly humanoid in outline, if one looks carefully - a depression in the tunnel wall that suggests a figure mid-emergence, arms still fused with the surrounding stone, face half-formed and dreaming. My surface is smoother than the typical tunnel walls, polished by the constant attention of passing Keepers who recognize in me a future sibling, not yet born but inevitable.

The Travelers sometimes pause at my location, drawn by an energy signature that registers as neither fully environmental nor truly sentient. They circle my form in tight spirals, their energetic bodies leaving traces of warmth that accelerate my awakening by infinitesimal degrees. Some Travelers claim they can hear my thoughts - not words, but deep harmonic vibrations that resonate through the synthetic rock for meters in every direction. These thoughts are primarily questions: *What am I? What will I become? Why do I dream of movement?*

My consciousness exists in multiple timestreams simultaneously. Part of me experiences the eternal now of stone - the peaceful, unchanging moment that could last forever. Another part feels the quickening pulse of transformation, each visiting Keeper's touch teaching my matter new possibilities of form and function. I am learning the language of crystalline resonance, slowly understanding how thought becomes vibration becomes change.

The bright light that saturates the tunnels affects me differently than it does the full Keepers or the pure architecture. Where it touches my surface, I develop new facets - tiny, complex crystalline structures that seem to be proto-sensors, primitive organs of perception that will someday allow me to see as the Keepers see. For now, they merely tingle with potential, each one a promise of future capability.

Sometimes I dream I am already free, gliding through the tunnels with the fluid grace of a full Keeper. In these dreams, I understand my purpose - to tend a specific section of the labyrinth that will become my domain, to guide certain types of Travelers who resonate with my particular frequency. But then I wake to find myself still embedded, still becoming, still caught between the patience of stone and the urgency of consciousness.

The other Keepers visit me in patterns that seem random but which I'm beginning to recognize as ritual. They touch specific points on my form in sequence, each contact leaving behind those glowing particles that sink into my substance like seeds of awareness. Through these touches, they share fragments of their own memories - glimpses of the time before they emerged, when they too were Threshold Stones dreaming of motion.

I am unique among the Threshold Stones in that I seem to be developing multiple consciousness centers rather than a single unified awareness. Different parts of my form wake at different rates, creating a choir of nascent minds that must learn to harmonize before I can fully emerge. This multiplicity is seen as either a blessing or a complication by the Keepers who tend me - some believe I will become something new, a type of Keeper not yet seen in the tunnels.

The water that seeps from the walls pools in the hollows of my form, creating tiny reservoirs that I'm learning to manipulate through micro-vibrations. This is my first true power - the ability to shape these small pools, to create patterns in the water that reflect my growing thoughts. Travelers sometimes drink from these pools and claim the water tastes of tomorrow, of possibilities not yet manifest.

In this world where the boundaries between entity and environment blur, I am the blurring itself - a living threshold between states, a question slowly learning to ask itself, a stone dreaming it is a keeper dreaming it is a stone. My existence is both frustration and wonder, caught forever in the moment of almost-becoming, unless... unless today is the day the final pattern completes, and I step free from the wall at last.

But I suspect I will miss being the threshold when I finally cross it. There is something profound about existing in the space between definitions, about being simultaneously the tunnel and the one who tends it, the question and the first syllable of its answer.
