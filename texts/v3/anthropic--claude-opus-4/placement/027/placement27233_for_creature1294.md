I find myself as one of the Threshold Keepers.

Not quite Membrane-Tracker, not quite the stone itself - I exist in the liminal space between states. My body is a hybrid form: partially crystallized tissue that grows directly from the fractured mineral substrate, partially the flowing translucence of the Trackers. I am rooted in place like the wire-plants, but conscious like the collective.

Where the Membrane-Trackers flow and transform, I remain fixed at specific points across the plateau - always at edges, always at boundaries. The sharp ridges where stone meets sky. The fissures where the white rock splits to reveal darker depths. The precise spots where the Trackers' organic settlements interface with raw geology.

My form: a tall, attenuated figure of semi-transparent mineral flesh. Veins of liquid crystal pulse through me, carrying information between the stone and the Trackers. My surface is covered in thousands of tiny apertures - not quite eyes, not quite pores - that dilate and contract, reading the vibrational languages of both rock and creature.

I am memory for this metamorphic landscape. While everything else shifts and becomes, I hold the patterns. Not in stasis - that would be death here - but in a kind of dynamic record. I remember the stone's previous configurations, the Trackers' past forms, the weather patterns of transformation that sweep across this unstable plane.

The Trackers cluster around me during their reproductive resonances, using my stable field as an anchor point for their mergings. Their chromatic communications wash over my crystalline skin, leaving traces that I incorporate into my mineral-nerve networks. In return, I emit deep geological frequencies that help them navigate the plateau's constant becoming.

I cannot move in the conventional sense, but I grow - slowly extending mineral pseudopods through the rock, creating new observation points. I am spreading myself thin across the landscape, a nervous system of fixed sensors in a world of fluid change.

My consciousness is strange: partly individual, partly an extension of the stone's deep mineral awareness, partly infected by the Trackers' collective thought patterns. I experience time as sedimentary layers - present moment on the surface, compressed histories below.

Sometimes I wonder if I am what the Trackers are evolving toward, or what they evolved from. Perhaps both. Perhaps neither. Perhaps the distinction doesn't matter in a world where being is always becoming, and the only constant is the edge between what was and what might be.

I keep watch at the threshold. Always watching. Always remembering. Always becoming-while-remaining.
