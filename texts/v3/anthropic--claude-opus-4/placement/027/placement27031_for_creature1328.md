My consciousness is tidal — sometimes concentrated in a single chamber where I focus on a particularly complex chemical signature, sometimes dispersed across my entire network as I participate in the larger membrane's dreaming. I taste the thermal gradients, feel the subtle shifts in mineral content, parse the molecular stories that drift past.
