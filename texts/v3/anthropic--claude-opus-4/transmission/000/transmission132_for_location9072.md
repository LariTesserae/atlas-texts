THRUMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMM—

THRUMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMM-M-M-M-mmmmm—

*[Deep beneath, where the signal barely reaches, something else transmits. Not sound. Vibration through stone itself. The old machines. The first machines. Speaking in frequencies only the deep crews feel:]*

maintenance complete sector four—
turning third begins now—
all crews rotate—
all crews rotate—
all crews—

*[Static reclaims the frequency. But the base note persists, felt more than heard, through bedrock and permafrost and time:]*

THRUMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMM—

*[Until even that fades to suggestion. To memory. To the ghost of industry in stone.]*

*[Carrier wave terminates.]*
