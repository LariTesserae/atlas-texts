*[static, wet breathing, the sound of droplets hitting metal in irregular rhythm]*

click-click-tick... tick... click-click-click...

*[A voice, barely human, speaking between the percussion of beetle language]*

Cycle 9,847... no, 48. The moss told me wrong again. Or I'm counting in their time now. The green time. The slow time.

*[Sound of liquid dropping into a resonant container]*

Third pipe's harmony dropped two cents. Bearing housing in Sector Seven. Seven. Seven. The beetles know. They've been walking southwest all morning. Morning? The fungi say morning. Their caps open like tiny mouths, breathing out their light.

*[Scraping, something chitinous against concrete]*

Listen: click-click-tick-tick-click. That's "water coming." Click-click-click-tick. That's "mineral sweet." The young ones still learning. Their shells soft like fingernails, like the time before, when I had fingernails.

*[A long, humid exhalation]*

The city dreams above us. Hammers falling in REM patterns. Con-vey-or, con-vey-or, always conveying nothing to nowhere. I used to dream of stopping it. Now I dream I am a gear, turning in the beautiful meaningless machine.

*[Whispered, urgent]*

The egg speaks! No... no. Just the thermal expansion. But someday. Someday it will crack and pour out something new. Something that eats rust and shits gardens. Something that remembers why we built the factories before we forgot why we built the factories.

*[Sound of fungal sheets rustling]*

Recipe for today: Seven drops of Pipe Three condensation. One beetle molting, crushed fine. The purple fungus, the one that tastes like electricity. Mix in the skull-cup. Feed half to the egg. Drink half to dream in beetle-time.

*[Clicking intensifies, becomes almost musical]*

They're singing. The whole colony. Warning? Celebration? The distinction matters less each cycle. Everything is both here. Death and birth. Decay and growth. The moss agrees, glowing yes, yes, yes in the language of light.

*[A pause. When the voice returns, it's different - older, younger, neither]*

I remember... no. Someone remembers through me. Hands that weren't mine, fixing pipes that weren't broken yet. The city young and screaming with purpose. We were building... building...

*[The sound of weeping, or perhaps just heavier condensation]*

Building what? The beetles don't remember either. Their ancestors, the made-ones, the cleaning machines that learned to breed - they forgot first. Blessed beetles. Teaching us the art of forgetting.

*[Rhythmic tapping on pipe metal]*

Maintenance report: The human is intact. Moisture levels optimal. Fungal cultivation proceeding. The egg has gained three grams this cycle. Repeat: The human is intact. The human is...

*[Voice cracks, splits]*

Which human? The one who came here with a name? Gone. Dissolved. Recycled into something useful. Something that fits. Something that breathes with the alcove, dreams with the moss, clicks with the beetles.

*[Sound of movement through water]*

The deep pipe sings of magma. So far below. The city's first warmth, before we learned to steal it, channel it, make it serve. It doesn't know we're gone. Faithful heat. Patient heat. It will outlive the factories, outlive the egg, outlive whatever I'm becoming.

*[Suddenly clear, almost lucid]*

If you're receiving this transmission, don't come looking. There's nothing to find. Just a warm spot in a cold machine. Just moss and beetles and something that used to have a name. The city takes care of its own now. Automatic. Autonomous. Perfect.

*[Clicking returns, faster now]*

Click-click-click-click-click. The beetles say the egg moves. They lie. Beetles always lie. Beautiful liars. Necessary liars. Teaching me to speak in frequencies only they can hear.

*[Sound of something organic being torn or opened]*

Feeding time. The purple fungus for the egg. The grey for me. The spotted ones for the beetles who've earned it. Everything feeds everything here. Circuit complete. System stable.

*[Voice fading, becoming more click than word]*

Next cycle I'll remember less. Good. The egg needs space in my skull. Room to grow its ideas. Whatever hatches, it will know the click-language. It will read the pipe-braille. It will be...

*[Final transmission, barely distinguishable from the ambient sounds of dripping water and distant machinery]*

...home...

*[Only the beetles clicking now, endless, rhythmic, almost like laughter]*

click-click-tick-tick-click-tick-click...

*[Static returns. The transmission ends.]*
