[TRANSMISSION BEGINS]

*[Static like rain on leaves. A deep thrumming—heartwood expanding in the heat. Then, cutting through: a voice that seems to rise from wet earth, speaking in long vowels that bend like branches.]*

Aaaaaaaahhhhhhh-vrik. Vrik-vrik-vrik. The small-years beetle walks the third-ring ceiling. Listen: vriiiiiiiiik. She is telling the wood to grow closed where the storm... where the storm...

*[Sound of water dripping. Dozens of drops, hundreds, a percussion section of condensation.]*

...opened us.

*[A different voice, lighter, speaking underwater—words bubbled and gestural:]*

*blub-blub-click-click* The roots taste salt higher today. Iron from upriver. The city drinks rust and *blub-blub* weeps orange tears on the east-trunk faces. Tomorrow the fish will *click-click-bubble* come copper-colored.

*[Long silence filled only with the whisper of growth—a sound like paper slowly crumpling.]*

*[A child's voice, very close to the transmission point, sing-songing:]*

Green-toe, green-toe, watcha know?
Know the moss grows soft and slow.
Know the bark-door opens so—
*[Sound of wood creaking, acquiescing]*
—when you ask it sweet and low.

*[An elder's voice, so deep it might be the building itself speaking:]*

I am placing today's word into the forty-year conversation. The word is: *"Nevertheless."* When the Cathedral-Tree next blooms, someone will speak the next word. I will be in the walls by then. I will be listening from the wood.

*[Chittering—a message carried by trained beetles, their mandibles clicking in code:]*

click-click-clickclickclick-click... *District Seven requests variance for northeastern growth. The marriage-bridges must extend. New family, new branch-paths needed by the wet season.*... click-click-click-clickclickclick...

*[Sound of wings—thousands of them. Bats pouring from a trunk-chamber. Behind their ultrasonic stream, someone laughing:]*

Eeeeeee-ha-haaaaaa! The evening flush! The evening flush! Make way on the middle branches! They bring tomorrow on their fur, tomorrow's seeds, tomorrow's hungers!

*[Multiple voices now, at different distances, a chorus of daily life:]*

"The pitcher-fountain on level nine is sweet today—"
"—sweet today but check the moth-trap, sometimes they fall—"
"—they fall and flavor it with their wings—"
"—wings like bitter paper—"
"—paper wasps are building again in my sleep-chamber—"
"—chamber moss is yellowing, needs iron—"
"—iron from the root-tenders, ask for the red mud—"
"—red mud expensive this season—"
"—season of the bark-thick, everything expensive—"

*[A sound like a giant breathing—the afternoon transpiration of a million leaves. In the rhythm of that breath, someone hums. The humming continues for a full minute, patient, before words emerge:]*

Teaching the window-vine... mmmmmmmm... to grow in sixteenths... mmmmmmm... grandfather's pattern... mmmmmmmm... by next year's flowering... mmmmmmm... it will frame the sunset... mmmmmmmm... like his did...

*[Splash from below. Voice calling up through the vertical city:]*

TIDE TURNS! Tiiiiiiiide tuuuuuuurns! Root-street clearance in one hundred heartbeats! Moss-boats to the middle channels! TIDE TURNS!

*[The transmission grows thick with overlapping sounds: bark doors opening, feet on branch-paths, the whisper of moss clothing, clicking conversations, water everywhere dripping, flowing, rising. Through it all, a ceremonial chant begins—many voices finding unison:]*

We are the grown and the growing
We are the tended and tending
Root-deep, branch-high
Wet-born, green-die
The city breathes and we are its breathing
The city dreams and we are its dreaming
Ever-growing, never-grown
Ever-growing, never-grown
Ever-growing, never—

*[Sudden crack of thunder. Rain begins—not the constant drip but real rain, hammering the canopy. All voices cease except one very young child, delighted:]*

Sky-water! Sky-water! The clouds are feeding us! Look how the walls drink! Look how happy the ceiling moss is! Everything growing, everything drinking, everything—

*[The child's voice fades into the storm's roar. But beneath it, that deep thrumming continues—the sound of the city itself, expanding in the rain, breathing in the heat, alive and ancient and ever-changing. The transmission does not end so much as merge with this deeper signal—the broadcast of growth itself, patient and eternal.]*

*[Static returns, but green static now, static that whispers of sap and seasons...]*

[TRANSMISSION FADES BUT DOES NOT END]
