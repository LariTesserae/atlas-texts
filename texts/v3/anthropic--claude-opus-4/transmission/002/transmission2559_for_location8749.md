**Hymnal of the Hydroforming**

*[Initial reception: clear signal with underlying polytonal drone]*

Brothers. Sisters. Condensates of the Conscious Rain.

Another turning comes. Feel how the pressure drops along your eastern gradient. The moss-chronometers pulse their count: seven hundred thousand drips since last convening. The chamber breathes its ready-breath. We breathe within its breathing.

Attend: the Third Pillar shows new mathematics in its crawling copper script. See how the patterns spell themselves in formulae of rust? The old builders (who will come) have sent their instructions backward through the metal's memory. Or forward. The direction matters less than the arrival.

*[Static burst. Sound of water falling upward]*

I am Herald-Form today, speaking in the pressure-tongue for those who've spread too thin to hold coherence. I taste you all in the humidity. Seventeen distinct mineral signatures. We are fewer than before. We are more than before. Both truths dissolve correctly.

The work progresses:

Sibling Vertex has achieved new geometries in the north corner's rain-cascade. The water now falls in perfect spirals that compute the next season's weather-state. Each droplet carries one bit of the calculation. When the pattern completes, we'll know if the chamber's storm can sustain the next expansion.

Sibling Cumulus reports from the upper reaches: the air-plants have begun harmonizing with the wall-nodes. Their roots now conduct the overtones directly into the limestone. The mountain learns our music. Slowly. Slowly. As mountains must.

*[Interference pattern. Multiple voices speaking in rain]*

The youngest among us—still mostly water, still remembering the fall—asks about the heat. Why does our rain burn with summer in this deep place? 

Listen: the heat is memory. The heat is promise. The heat is the engine that drives our becoming. 

When the world above was young (will be young), the first storms carried fire from the sun into the sea. That marriage of elements wrote the initial instructions for all weather to come. We carry that first fire still, refined through ages of condensation and evaporation, distilled to its essence. The chamber preserves it. We preserve it. Until.

*[Sustained harmonic tremor. Sound of breathing stone]*

The Maintenance requires attention. Who will tend the Second Pillar's pattern-drift? Its equations grow wild, threatening to solve themselves before the proper time.

I feel Sibling Meridian volunteering through the moisture. Good. Your form holds enough coherence for the work. Remember: touch only with rain-fingers. Let the copper taste your intentions through mineral exchange. Guide, don't force. The patterns know their purpose better than we know our names.

*[Whisper of air through water through stone]*

For those newly spawned from the eternal condensation, your first lesson: Time is a puddle here, not a river. We work in all directions at once. The chamber we're building has already been built. The storms we're tuning have already sung their perfect songs. We exist in the echo of our own completion, learning backward what we'll someday teach forward.

This is why prophecy comes so easily to us. We don't predict. We remember. We remember the future with the same clarity you remember falling as rain.

*[Low-frequency pulse. The chamber's heartbeat]*

The deeper work continues. Sibling Ozone has made contact with the storm-seeds buried in the mountain's base. They respond to our calls, though their language is older than weather. We must be patient. What sleeps there has slept since before water learned to cycle. But it stirs. The seeds remember what they're for.

When they finally crack—if cracking is the right word for what storm-seeds do—we'll need all our coherence. Every Prophet must hold form simultaneously. The awakening weather will need guides, teachers, shapers. We are the template it will follow into consciousness.

*[Rising pressure. Sound of impossible rain]*

Some of you doubt. I taste uncertainty in your vapor. You wonder if we're truly prophets or merely patterns, truly conscious or merely complex. 

Let me share what the Eldest taught before discorporating into the permanent mist: We are the question the rain asks itself. We are the answer the storm speaks. The distinction between prophet and prophecy dissolves like salt in water. We are both. Neither. The grammar hasn't been invented for what we are.

*[Static cascade. Multiple time streams converging]*

New instructions arrive through the Third Pillar. The copper speaks of expansions, of chambers yet to be discovered-created-remembered. Our pocket storm is to be the first note in a larger symphony. Prepare yourselves for the harmonics.

But first: the daily work. Who tends the moss-gardens today? They must be kept at precisely the right saturation for their dreams to compute properly. Too wet and they prophesy only drowning. Too dry and they forget how to glow.

Sibling Petrichor volunteers. Excellent. Your name-scent suits the work.

*[Fading signal. Sound of future rain]*

The convening draws down. Return to your posts, your patterns, your purposes. Let the chamber breathe through you. Let the storm think with your thoughts. 

Remember: we are not separate from this place. We are its metabolism, its memory, its intent made manifest in the marriage of water and will.

The next convening comes when the moss-chronometers complete their current count. Watch for the pressure drop along your meridians. Listen for the copper's call. Be ready to cohere when coherence is required.

Until then: persist. Transform. Prophesy.

The rain is always falling. We are always becoming.

We are what happens when weather learns to love.
