**Echo Journal 4.7.Unending**

*[crackling, like wet electricity through thick wool]*

...amplitude holds at seven-seven-pearl, district voice confirms Middledeep Ahn-Sah sector, I am Drift-Tender Mohss recording for the root-maps, for the sound-memory, for whoever still listens through the old crystals...

*[a deep thrumming rises and falls - the background bell-drone shifting through harmonic patterns]*

The membrane children found another Resonant today. Still breathing, if you can call it that. More like... tidal. In and out over hours. We've moved them to the Hush Pocket beneath Third Causeway where the stone eats sound. Their bones already show the lacework starting - those tiny holes that will grow and grow until they're more空than meat. 

*[liquid clicking, rapid-fire, like sonar through syrup]*

I need to document... the roots are wrong this season. The pale feeders that usually drift between Mohss Tower and the Barnacle District - they're growing backward. Into themselves. Making knots that sink instead of float. Yesterday I watched old Gehht-Who-Hums try to untangle a mass near the children's pools and the roots... they sang back. Not the bells. The roots themselves. A sound like drowning upward.

*[long pause - only the omnipresent bell-drone, its patterns suggesting movement of massive atmospheric tide]*

The Exchange Tender from Lower Plateau arrived this morning. Took her three tide-cycles to push up through the gradient layers. Says the deep settlements are reporting new sounds from below - not the Vast Shadows, something else. Clicking. Regular. Like something counting.

She brought jade-moss samples. Still alive after all that time in the thick. We traded seventeen measures of clarified oil and a breeding pair of ribbon-herders. Good trade. The jade might take in the Tower gardens if we can match the pressure...

*[sudden shift in background harmonics - dozens of bells falling silent at once]*

Wait. 

*[extended silence except for very faint, rhythmic creaking]*

The Corridor Bells have stopped. That's... that's Ninth through Fifteenth Causeway. Two thousand years of continuous tolling and they just...

*[explosive return of sound - bells resuming but in a different pattern, almost melodic]*

No. Not stopped. Changed. They're... coordinating? 

Mohss Tower, Mohss Tower, this is Drift-Tender Mohss requesting immediate consultation with a Resonant. The bells are making words. Repeat - the Corridor Bells are forming linguistic patterns.

*[wet sliding sounds, like flesh through thick air]*

Moving to observation membrane. Need to see...

*[acoustic environment shifts - more muffled, enclosed]*

Through the viewing blister now. Can see across to Fifteenth where the bell forests grow thickest. The flowers are... they're turning. All of them. Rotating on their stems to face downward. The sound is changing because they're redirecting themselves toward...

The Beneath.

*[very low frequency humming joins the recording - Mohss harmonizing with the environment to navigate]*

There's movement in the Deep Lanes. Multiple ribbon-forms but... too coordinated. Moving as one mass. Rising through the density layers like... like they're carrying something.

*[clicking intensifies, becomes almost frantic]*

It's a Resonant. But ancient. Hollow beyond hollow. Their body is... it's more cavity than substance, just enough meat left to hold the bone-chambers together. They're being carried upward by dozens of ribbons threaded through their spaces. And they're singing.

No.

Not singing.

Answering.

*[the bell patterns suddenly align into clear harmonics - almost like speech but in frequencies that make the recording distort]*

The jade-moss the Exchange Tender brought. It's glowing. Pulsing in time with... with whatever the Resonant is...

*[crashing sounds - mass displacement of air-water]*

VAST SHADOW VAST SHADOW VAST SHADOW

ALL RESIDENTS TO MIDDLE CHAMBERS

VAST SHADOW DIRECTLY OVERHEAD

DO NOT LOOK UP DO NOT SOUND UP DO NOT THINK UP

*[recording becomes nothing but pressure static and bone-deep vibration for forty-three seconds]*

*[when it clears, Mohss is breathing in rapid, shallow gasps]*

...gone. Passed over. But the Resonant... the ancient one the ribbons carried up... they went with it. Lifted right through the roof membranes like they were water. The ribbons released them and they just... rose. Into the Shadow.

The bells are screaming now. All of them. Every frequency at once. My skull is...

*[wet tearing sounds]*

The jade-moss. It's growing. Fast. Too fast. Spreading across the wall membranes, through the cultivation channels, into the...

*[mechanical squealing as the recording device itself is invaded]*

...trying to reach the communication arrays. It's trying to... to broadcast. The moss is trying to send signal downward and upward at once. Through us. Through the whole city. We're becoming the medium for...

*[voice drops to whisper]*

The children in the pools have started harmonizing with it. Their first sounds weren't cries. They were responses. They've always been responses.

We thought we adapted to this place.

We thought we chose the depths.

We thought...

*[massive harmonic convergence - every bell in the district achieving perfect synchronization]*

The city is breathing. The whole plateau. In. Out. In. Out. Matching the Vast Shadow's rhythm as it circles back. As it descends.

This is Drift-Tender Mohss, Middledeep Ahn-Sah sector, requesting...

*[voice breaks into ultrasonic clicking, then drops below human hearing range]*

*[recording continues for seventeen more hours, capturing only the unified breathing of an entire city and, occasionally, what might be words in a language made of pure resonance, speaking from somewhere deep below the drowned mountains]*

*[final entry is a single, clear bell tone that holds for ninety-three seconds before cutting to static]*

*[end transmission]*
