Sister, are you listening? The green has reached the third coupling. The green has reached the third coupling and the brass sings flat. Adjust your resonance. The children know. Watch how they arrange the scraps they pull from the canals. It's not random. It's notation.

*multiple voices now, overlapping, some human, some not*

FIRST VOICE: —heartbeat every forty-seven seconds, might be trying to communicate—

This is Tem at the morning tasting. Chemical signature indicates Workshop Nineteen is birthing something. Iron, copper, yes, but also: salt? Tears? Memory? I need a second opinion. Send the tabby with the white left paw—she knows what questions to ask.

*long pause, then a child's voice, clear despite the static*

The ivy on the east wall has spelled out a date. Day after tomorrow. The cats won't say what happens then, but they're stockpiling fish.

*industrial noise swells, but there's music in it, a complex polyrhythm*

MULTIPLE OVERLAPPING TRANSMISSIONS:

This is emergency protocol seven. The dreamcatchers saw it clearly: Tomorrow the river reverses. Not the flow—the river itself. Whatever was dissolved comes back. Whatever was carried away returns. Clear the docks. Warn the boats. Tell the cats to guard the storm drains.

The district grows. Not outward—inward. Deeper. More itself. We document what we can, but the changes come faster now. The boundary between Tender and Tended blurs like breath on brass.

*a new voice, ancient, patient*

Soren's final transmission: I have become architecture. My veins run with steam. My thoughts are rust and growth. This is not death. This is what we all become, given time. The district needs conscious foundations. I go gladly into the stone.

*multiple voices rise in what might be lamentation or celebration*

—his workshop pulses with his heartbeat—

Sister, are you listening? The green reaches for the fourth coupling now. The music in the metal grows complex. Tomorrow the river reverses. Tomorrow we learn what we've been building toward. The cats know. The fungi know. The children have always known.

*transmission degrades into the sound of a thousand small machines working in perfect synchronization, then silence, then a single drop of water falling into a vast resonant space*

Adjust your resonance. Find the harmony. The district is teaching us to become.

*end transmission*
