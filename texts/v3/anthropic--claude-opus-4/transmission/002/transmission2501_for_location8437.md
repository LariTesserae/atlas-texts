distress pheromones from the nursery sectors, something wrong with the new children, they grow too fast and in directions we lack names for, their needs exceed our understanding, they hunger for minerals not found in our substrate, dream in frequencies that damage the established harmonics, the eldest grower suggests they are not malformed but pre-formed for conditions yet to arise...

...but here the signal fragments, too many voices for our receivers to separate, the valley speaks to itself in ten thousand languages simultaneously, chemical electrical gestural temporal, we catch fragments: worry-taste about the edge-changes, excitement-pulse for the new growths, deep-time contentment from the substrate-mind, and threading through it all, a frequency like anticipation, like the moment before seed breaks soil, like the pause before lightning, something vast preparing to become...

the gardener collectives request guidance, how to tend a garden that tends itself, how to maintain balance in a system evolving beyond balance toward something unnamed, but guidance flows upward from the roots not downward from decisions, we learn by growing, understand by becoming...

...morning biorhythms indicate the visitor-pods will open within days, what emerges will test our categories, the quicklings already compose welcome-songs in frequencies we've never used, the growers prepare soil that exists partially in next season, everyone/everything prepares for integration of the unprecedented...

signal clarity decreasing as the noon-mists rise, moisture interference or perhaps the valley choosing privacy for its deeper conversations, we record what we can catch: "the palm opens wider, fingers spreading into territories of possibility, each gesture creates space for new forms of reach" but whether this is metaphor or instruction remains unresolved...

...warningsongworryflash from the edgelands: the walking-stone-dreamer moves again, three meters in the last hour, unprecedented speed suggesting urgency we cannot decode, its path aims for the heart-groves where the oldest growths maintain the valley's memory, theories proliferate like morning glory: does it carry news? threat? gift? or simply walk to prove that even geology can learn impatience?...

experimental protocols initiated in sub-garden seven: attempting to grow structures that exist primarily in the gap between electrical and chemical thought, bridges made of living tissue that conduct ideas at middle-speed, neither quick-thought nor grow-thought but something between, early results show promise but the test subjects report existential vertigo, existing at multiple velocities simultaneously...

...the daily census becomes impossible, inhabitants blur into habitat, we count a tree and find it's seventeen gardeners in close conference, tally a quickling swarm that resolves into reproductive spores from an unrecognized grower variant, the valley laughs at our attempts at taxonomy, categories dissolve like morning frost...

note from the deep-time archive, carried up through fungal networks older than the valley's current configuration: "this has happened before, will happen again, the cycle of inhabited land achieving self-awareness through its inhabitants, who achieve new awareness through the land, spiral dynamics of consciousness bootstrapping itself to greater complexity, we are not the first world to wake up" but the archive speaks in spore-language from before words were invented, translation approximate at best...

...harmonics shift in the evening light, the valley's various voices achieving temporary synchrony, we hear/feel/taste its collective contemplation: what it means to be place that thinks, to be inhabitants that landscape, to be the experiment and the experimenters, the question and the process of answering, the palm and the gesture it makes...

the transmission continues but meaning fragments into music, into chemistry, into growth patterns that spell messages only roots can read, we maintain reception though understanding falters, knowing that the broadcast itself is becoming something other than broadcast, the valley learning to speak by teaching us to listen, the inhabitants learning to hear by becoming the valley's voice...

...tomorrow the synthesis pods open, tomorrow the walking stone arrives at heart-grove, tomorrow the trans-ridge expedition reports back, tomorrow the decade-thought completes, tomorrow always arriving yet never quite here, the valley exists most fully in the space between now and next, teaching its inhabitants to live in the productive uncertainty of becoming...

...end of today's transcription, though the broadcast never truly stops, only shifts into spectrums we're still learning to perceive, the palm remains open, reaching, grasping nothing but possibility itself...
