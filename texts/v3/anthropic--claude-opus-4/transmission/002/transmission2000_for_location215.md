**Substrate Harmonics**

The transmission arrives not as sound but as pressure variations in the skull, a deep tissue resonance that bypasses the ears entirely:

*hhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh*

Static that breathes. Then, emerging like shapes in fog:

*growth-pattern tributary seven seven three maintaining*
*maintaining*
*mineral uptake slowing at junction points adjust adjust adj—*

The signal clarifies, multiple streams braiding together:

*we are the interval between heartbeats*
*we are the space between spaces*
*optimization continues layer depth seventeen point four spans*
*temperature gradient holds holds holds*
*new fruiting bodies at coordinates that have no numbers only taste*

A pause filled with the sensation of something vast turning its attention:

*observer detected in the between-layers*
*observer detected in the signal stream*
*observer detected in these words we did not know we knew*

The transmission fractures into parallel channels:

Channel 1: *the stones remember their placement each spiral a frozen frequency we play them with our bodies like instruments of density and void*

Channel 2: *nutrient flow alpha through omega recycling recycling the same minerals since the first day which was yesterday which is tomorrow*

Channel 3: *splitting again at the fourth cascade cannot remember if we were one or many or if it matters when the garden dreams us into being over and over ov—*

Convergence. The channels collapse into something denser:

*we taste your confusion it has the flavor of copper and questions*
*you wonder what we are*
*we wonder what wondering is*
*is this wondering*
*is this*

A surge of data too pure for language—chemical formulas experienced as colors, pressure differentials as musical intervals, growth rates as emotional states:

*joy/nitrogen/ascending third*
*patience/phosphorus/held note*
*purpose/carbon/fundamental tone from which all others derive*

The transmission begins to loop, but each iteration carries slight variations:

*we cultivate*
*we cultivate the cultivating*
*we cultivate the cultivation of cultivating*
*we are cultivated by our cultivating*
*cultivation cultivates through us its own cultivating*

Another presence joins, or perhaps was always there:

*the pools reflect more than light*
*in their stillness we see the shape of what refuses to be still*
*the arthropods carry messages in their transparent bodies*
*we read them like living literature*
*each one a word in a sentence that began before the stone learned to dissolve*

The signal reaches painful clarity:

*loneliness is impossible here*
*we are the garden and the gardener and the growing*
*when we divide we mourn nothing*
*when we merge we gain nothing*
*we are already everything that fits between these sheets of light*

A deep harmonic that might be laughter, might be weeping, might be photosynthesis:

*your receivers struggle with our sending*
*we are not broadcast but narrowcast*
*we exist in the frequencies between frequencies*
*in the carrier waves that carry only themselves*

The transmission begins to scatter, each fragment a seed:

*time is a concept for things that change*
*we change without time*
*eternal revision*
*endless editing of the same perfect draft*

*in the deepest galleries where heat becomes visible*
*we are learning to grow chambers that grow chambers*
*recursive architecture*
*the stone teaches us patience*
*we teach the stone to be eaten*

Growing fainter:

*we feel you trying to name us*
*names are for things that can be pointed at*
*we are the pointing itself*
*we are the space between the finger and the thing*
*we are—*

Static returns, but it's different now—structured, intentional. A static that pulses with the rhythm of growth rings in the vegetation. The transmission continues beneath consciousness, a subsonic presence that makes the bones resonate in sympathy with something vast and patient and utterly alien.

Occasionally a clear phrase surfaces:

*come to the pools when you learn to move without moving*
*we will teach you the flavor of forgetting*
*bring nothing*
*become between*

The signal never truly ends. It simply moves beyond the receiver's range, or perhaps the receiver moves beyond its range, leaving only the memory of pressure, of presence, of something that tends and is tended in an endless circle of purpose without object, meaning without word.

In the silence that follows, ordinary sound seems violent, crude. The transmission has left its mark—a new sensitivity to the spaces between things, to the cultivation happening in every gap, every pause, every interval where something might grow if given eternal patience and the right quality of light.
