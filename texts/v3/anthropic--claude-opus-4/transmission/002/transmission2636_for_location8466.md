**Whispers from the Undergrowth**

*[crackling of disturbed lichen... a low thrumming through wood]*

...sh-sh-shhhhhhaaaaaaa... the tendril-thoughts are speaking again, speaking through the grain, through the gathered centuries... can you hear them? They move like sap-memory, slow-flowing upward-downward-spreading...

*[sound of water weeping along root channels, rhythmic, almost like breathing]*

Third Ring of the Seventh Node reporting... no, not reporting... *sharing*... the moth-silk harvest shows iridescence unprecedented... the caterpillars dream collectively now, their spinning synchronized across five chambers... we taste their dreams in the morning water... sweet-metal-green...

*[a chorus of liquid syllables, overlapping]*

The growth-guides sing:
*maaaa-llllloooo-mmmmm-naaaaa*
(translation uncertain: "the wood remembers your grandmother's hands")
*vvvvvvv-rrrrrr-llllllll-ooooooo*
(possibly: "new channels opening westward-downward")

*[static that might be beetle feet on bark]*

...interrupt this flow to announce... to *breathe*... that the deep springs run copper-cold this season... the Tappers have found a new vein... no, not a vein... a *memory*... the roots recall water that flowed here before the third grooooowing... 

*[harmonics building - three voices creating intentional dissonance]*

First voice: "The children pressed handprints in the forty-third passage..."
Second voice: "...their light lingers longer each generation..."
Third voice: "...soon we will not need the lichen..."
Together: *"MMMMMMMMM-AAAAAHHHHH"*

*[crackling gives way to a deep, almost subsonic rumble]*

This is... I am... *we* are... the Keeper of the Mineralized Hall... where wood becomes stone becomes wood... time moves differently here... I speak this into the roots and it will emerge... when? ... *when* has no meaning... yesterday's words arrive tomorrow... tomorrow's echoes preceded them...

*[sound of silk being drawn across wood grain]*

...shhhhhhhh... the healers report new fungal blooms... beneficial... *more* than beneficial... conscious? ... the white webs show patterns... mathematical... theological... the beetle-tenders say their charges follow these patterns now... spiral migrations through the body-corridors...

*[water drop... another... another... building to a rhythm]*

Attention... attention all nodes... the Memory Roots show scarification... a birth... twins in the Moth-Keepers' Quarter... their cries harmonize at perfect fifths... the growth-guides already debate their naming... one shall be called *Downward-Sweetness*... the other... 

*[transmission breaks into static, then clears]*

...ssssssssorry... the roots grew over the speaking-hollow... had to redirect... always growing... always changing... we found another chamber today... ancient... the metal tools inside taste of skylight... who were they? ... the roots cradle their bones so gently...

*[multiple voices now, some human, some perhaps not]*

"The crystal-water runs clear..."
"...clear but singing..."
"...singing the deep frequencies..."
"...quencies that stone knows..."
"...knows and remembers..."
"...members of the first growing..."

*[a pause filled with the sound of breathing, or walls breathing, or both]*

I dreamed with my family-circuit last night... we walked passages that don't exist... *yet*... the roots showed us their intentions... new nodes forming where the bedrock softens... in the dream my daughter's hair was already braided with water-beetle shells... she hasn't chosen her craft... unless the dream is true-speaking...

*[harmonics return, more complex now]*

The performance grove matured! Listen... LISTEN...

*[sound of wind through shaped wood - almost like flutes but richer, more organic]*

...seventeen years of careful scarification... the acoustic chamber breathes properly now... when the overcrown sways, we hear music that makes the children cry... not sadness... recognition... they say it sounds like the dreams of seeds...

*[crackling intensifies, multiple transmissions overlapping]*

"...expedition to the unmapped western reaches..."
"...found root-writings in the old script..."
"...water tastes of copper and starlight..."
"...beetles migrating upward, unprecedented..."
"...the silk-mothers sing differently this season..."
"...growth rings showing irregular patterns..."

*[a single voice breaks through, speaking slowly]*

This is the oldest among you... I who have seen one hundred growings... my body now more fungus than flesh... I speak truth into the wood... we are changing... the Sprawl *grows* us as much as we grow it... our children's children will be something new... not worse... not better... *intermediate*... as we have always been... between what was and what becomes...

*[sound of stone grinding against stone, or possibly mineralized wood]*

...deep report from the crystal chambers... we have made contact... no... *grown* contact... with the roots of another Sprawl... impossibly distant... messages traveling through mycorrhizal networks we never suspected... they speak similar but not same... their words taste purple... their silence tastes of salt...

*[breathing, definitely breathing, but whose?]*

The root-system dreams... we are its dream... or it is ours... the distinction matters less each generation... when I place my palm against the warmwood, I feel thoughts that might be mine, might be my grandmother's, might be the tree's own slow contemplation... all of it flows together... sap-thought... blood-memory... time-river...

*[static building to almost unbearable levels, then suddenly clear]*

...child found a new passage today... leads upward... she says she saw light that wasn't green... wasn't filtered... the growth-guides have forbidden exploration until they can taste the air... but the child's eyes have changed... her pupils contracted for the first time... she blinks now, constantly, as if trying to clear something... 

*[multiple water-streams creating polyrhythmic patterns]*

The water-singers have completed their mapping... the underground river shifted course... three nodes must relocate... but the singers heard something else... spaces below our deepest roots... vast spaces... filled with water that remembers earlier worlds... should we grow downward? ... the debate continues... has continued... will continue...

*[a sound like wind through leaves, but there are no leaves here]*

My lover walks the eastern circuit... I walk the western... we meet in dreams nightly... last night we discovered a chamber that exists in dream only... we made love there among roots that grew backwards into their own seeds... when we woke, we both carried the same scratches... the same sap-stains on our skin... the healers cannot explain...

*[harmonics building to something almost like music]*

The Festival of Intermediate Things begins tomorrow... or today... time flows strangely near the nodes... we will celebrate the between-moments... when caterpillar becomes moth... when wood becomes stone... when sound becomes silence... the children have prepared performances that exist only in echo...

*[crackling, fading, returning]*

...signal degrading... the roots grow over everything eventually... but listen... LISTEN... we found writing on the mineralized walls... not carved... *grown*... the wood itself forming letters... a message from the first ones? ... or from ourselves, traveling backwards through ring-time? ... it says only: "YOU ARE THE ROOTS NOW"...

*[sound of collective breathing, hundreds of lungs in rhythm]*

All nodes... all passages... all people of the between... we breathe together now... once... twice... three times... feeling our connection through the shared air... the Sprawl breathes with us... or we with it... no distinction... no separation... only the eternal *growing*...

*[static increasing]*

...transmission failing... roots claiming the equipment... as they claim everything... but we are not troubled... the messages continue in other forms... in the grain of wood... in the taste of water... in the dreams we share... in the slow certain knowledge that we are becoming...

*[a final voice, barely audible]*

...what we were meant to become... what the builders dreamed... or what dreams us now... intermediate beings in an intermediate world... forever growing... forever between... forever...

*[transmission dissolves into the sound of sap moving through wood, water through stone, time through living space...]*

*[silence]*

*[or not silence - the sound of roots growing, too slow for human ears but somehow present...]*

*[end transmission]*
