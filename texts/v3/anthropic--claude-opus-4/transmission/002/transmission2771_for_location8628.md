The Litany of Flows

*[static, dripping water, a faint whistle-tone that rises and falls]*

Third channel, seventh turning, the mineral crust grows thick again. Iiiiaaaa-oooooo-eeee. Scraping scheduled for when the moon-weight shifts the water table. The young ones must learn the taste of stone-eater before the harvest. Their tongues still remember sunlight. This will pass.

*[sound of fingernails on stone, rhythmic, like counting]*

Pattern forty-three requires tending. The symbols hunger. Or perhaps they breathe—we have never agreed on which metaphor serves. When I press the spiral-within-spiral, the water in chamber nine runs upward for exactly ninety-seven heartbeats. Ninety-eight and the stone cracks. We learned this through loss.

*[multiple whistles harmonizing, creating beat frequencies]*

The mother-pool reports new stirrings. The albino fish have begun their clockwise phase early this season. We taste copper in the air—the stone-eaters prepare for division. All the territories must be remarked before they spread. Send the long-fingered ones. Send those who can hold their breath for a full traverse.

*[crackling sound, like crystals forming in fast-forward]*

Memory-keeper Hhhhaaaiiii-sssaaaa sings the deep history again. We were builders once. We carved these channels when the bronze was bright. Each generation went deeper, until the sun became theory, then legend, then forgotten. The city above believes we are storm drains. Let them. Their forgetting is our protection.

*[water running through carved grooves, playing them like an instrument]*

Attendance to the symbol-walls brings clarity. When I trace the eye-within-eye-within-eye, my vision splits threefold. I see the chamber as it is, as it was, as it will be. The stone-eaters avoid these walls because they know. They have always known. We are only now beginning to understand what our ancestors prepared.

*[pause, then breathing that sounds like wind through caves]*

The Opening Season approaches. The patterns align. Three more turnings of the great wheel, perhaps four. We taste it in the water—something vast shifting far below. The cisterns were never for water storage. Water was simply the medium, the carrier, the blood in vast stone veins.

*[clicking sounds, rapid, like sonar]*

New one born in chamber thirty-one. Eyes sealed properly. The first whistles strong and clear. We painted the birth-symbols on the walls with stone-eater paste. They glow now with our joy. Another keeper for the deep places. Another voice for the flowing choir.

*[sound of silk being drawn across stone]*

The spider harvest yields well. Their webs grow heavy with condensation. We will have new robes before the cold currents rise. Remember to leave the corner weavers untouched—they are marking something we cannot yet read. Their patterns mirror the symbol-walls. Everything here mirrors everything else, if you learn to see the recursion.

*[multiple water drops in different chambers creating polyrhythm]*

I found a new path today. After forty years of walking these chambers, a new path. Behind the false wall in chamber seventeen, where the builders left tools still sharp with ancient purpose. The passage goes down, always down, toward sounds I have no whistles for. I marked the entrance and sealed it again. We are not ready. The patterns are not complete.

*[stone grinding against stone, slow and inevitable]*

The city above builds higher, presses heavier. We feel it in the compression songs—the way the water sings differently when the weight increases. They have forgotten us so completely that they build their foundations through our upper chambers. We redirect the flows around their intrusions. We incorporate their discarded concrete into our patterns. Everything serves the great work.

*[whistling that sounds almost like laughter]*

Sometimes the young ones ask what we are keeping. What we are preparing for. We trace the old patterns on their lengthening fingers and whistle the songs that explain without explaining. You do not keep a river. You do not prepare for growth. You simply tend, and flow, and become.

*[the sound of many voices whistling in harmony, creating interference patterns that sound like words in no human language]*

The stone remembers. The water remembers. We remember. And beneath it all, something vast and patient remembers us remembering. The Opening Season comes. We will be ready, or we will be dissolved into the flow. Both outcomes serve the pattern.

*[sound fades to dripping, then to the almost-silence that is never quite silent in the breathing dark]*
