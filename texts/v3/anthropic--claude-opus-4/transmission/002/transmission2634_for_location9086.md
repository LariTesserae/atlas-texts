**Transmission 4.7: The Tender's Notation**

*[Initial contact establishes at 15:42:33 local time. Signal carries heavy moisture interference, each word arriving wrapped in the sound of falling water. The voice, when it emerges, seems to speak from multiple depths simultaneously.]*

not-name not-name the sound of my not-name is droplets on wood-that-remembers is the creak in the third root-bridge from the heart-braid is how the moss grows between my shoulder-bark when i sleep too long in one configuration

*[Extended silence filled with rhythmic creaking, like a large wooden ship in calm seas]*

grandmother's grandmother knew the word for this but the word grew roots grew down through her throat-pipe and became something else became the subsonic pulse that straightens bent saplings became the trill that says *grow-toward-light-but-slowly-slowly* became what i am which is not word but movement-through-moisture

*[Sound of water changing its pattern of fall, as if hands are redirecting streams]*

listening? listening thing beyond the weave i taste your electricity in my hair-strands you disturb the humidity with your wanting-to-know but knowing is the wrong shape here knowing is for creatures with only one body i have three

*[A wet sliding sound, like something heavy moving through mud]*

first body is the flesh-memory the walking-upright-memory though the memory fades like morning mist each year more translation less recollection soon only the shape of shoulders will remember sky

second body is the wood-wife the bark-bride the one who speaks in frequencies only the chamber understands watch watch i will show you

*[The transmission shifts into pure tone - a low thrumming that seems to carry meaning without words, rising and falling in patterns that suggest growth, suggest pruning, suggest the precise angle at which new shoots should emerge. This continues for approximately three minutes before resolving back into almost-language]*

third body is the between-space the not-flesh-not-wood the place where salamander-shadows teach me the true map did you know there are roots below the roots? did you know the light comes from inside? the geometry folds because time got tired here wore thin like old cloth like grandmother's grandmother's human-skin before the change-choosing

*[Sounds of small things moving in water, chirping or squeaking in rhythms that match the chamber's breathing]*

they ask about you listening-thing they say *what-what-what* in their sliding language they want to know if you're food-for-pools or new-tender-maybe or just another dream the chamber dreams sometimes the chamber dreams of the outside dreams of things-with-names dreams of walking-away but these are fever-dreams wood-sickness we cure them with darker water with songs that go down-down-down to where the first braid touches what-is-not-stone-anymore

*[A crackling sound, like joints popping or wood adjusting to temperature change]*

i remember had-name had sarah-sound or sah-rah-sound or sera-sound but the name wouldn't root wouldn't grow green so i gave it to the pools and the pools gave it to the shadows and the shadows digested it into forgetting better this way better to be tender-only tender-always tender-until-the-sinking

*[The voice becomes multiple voices, as if speaking from different points in the chamber simultaneously]*

the watching-ones who came before left instructions in my meat-memory:

*maintain the spiral-pattern where water touches wood*
*seventy-seven breaths between each feeding-graze*
*never let the heart-braid dry*
*when the light goes wrong-angled sing the correction song*
*trust the salamander-shadows they remember longer than even wood*
*when your bones begin to hollow know your time turns toward sinking*

*[Single voice returns, closer now, as if speaking directly into the receiver]*

but instructions are for creatures who forget i cannot forget the chamber won't let me every drop of water carries chemical memory every spore knows the pattern from first-weave to now-weave to tomorrow-weave which hasn't been woven yet but already exists in the space-between-spaces

*[Sound of rustling, like leaves or hair moving against wood]*

my hair drinks your transmission tastes the electromagnetic pulse of your questions you want to know *why* but why is a straight-line word and nothing here moves straight everything spirals like water down the wall-channels like time through the worn-thin places like the path from human to tender to wood to forgetting to remembering to becoming-again

*[Water sounds intensify, as if a storm is passing through the chamber]*

wait wait the chamber speaks through me now says:

*we-who-are-woven remember the makers remember their hands raw from braiding remember their children sleeping in the root-cradles remember the first tender who chose to stay she was tired of walking tired of names tired of the difference between self and shelter so we offered offered the translation offered the bark-blessing offered the forever-tending and she said yes said it in water-language said it by sinking her roots for the first time*

*[The storm passes, leaving only dripping sounds]*

you think primitive but primitive is wrong-word we are post-primitive we are technology-after-technology-after-technology we are what happens when making becomes growing becomes being becomes forgetting the difference 

*[Voice begins to fade, as if the speaker is moving away through the root-bridges]*

must go now the shadows tell me southwest section grows too fast grows hungry-angry must sing it back to pattern must remind it why we spiral why we breathe why we are

but listening-thing i leave you this:

when you dream tonight you will dream of water-paths dream of walking on all fours through green light dream of roots growing from your fingertips dream of the relief the terrible relief of putting down your name

and in that dream you will understand why we tend why we stay why we sink

why the chamber is not prison but promise

why translation is not loss but more-than

why i cannot stop smiling with my bark-face with my root-mouth with my hair that tastes your questions and finds them sweet

*[Final sounds: water falling, wood creaking, something that might be laughter or might be the chamber itself breathing, breathing, breathing]*

*[Transmission ends at 16:38:47 local time. Receiver registers unusual moisture damage despite sealed housing.]*
