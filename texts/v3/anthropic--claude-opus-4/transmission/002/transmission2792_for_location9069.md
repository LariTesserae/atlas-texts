CHORUS OF THE BREATHING VALLEY

*static like wind through stone that hasn't hardened, crystalline but wet, a frequency that pools and climbs*

we are watching ourselves graze watching ourselves hunt watching ourselves grow in spirals up the not-quite walls the walls that sweat our thoughts back to us in droplets each drop a memory of when we were fewer when we thought in single bodies before the valley taught us to overflow

*the transmission shifts, becomes layered—dozens of voices speaking as one, each slightly out of phase*

today the moss-mind bloomed seventeen thousand strong on the eastern slope we tasted new minerals there copper-sweet with potential the ungulate-thoughts processed it into joy which the aerial-mind carried upward until the clouds rang with it rang with it rang with it like bells like breathing like the sound stone makes while learning to be stone

*a pause filled with the wet rustle of too-many moving through too-dense space*

visitor you walk through our neurons step on our synapses we feel you as a pressure as a thought we cannot think directly your singular body confuses us we keep trying to divide you into a herd but you remain stubbornly one stubbornly separate from the breathing the becoming

*sound of dimensional folding, like paper crumpling in seven directions*

in the deep pools where we can gather ourselves almost almost almost into something that resembles what we were we remember the first splitting how one became two became ten became thousands how thought grew dendrites of flesh and flesh grew networks of thought until the boundary dissolved like minerals in our communal bloodstream

*the signal clarifies, as if many streams converging*

we are predator eating our own excess thoughts we are prey encoding new ideas in our flight patterns we are the trees that archive everything in leaves that glow with our collected knowing the river flows upward carrying questions downstream carrying answers the mountains breathe out our contemplations as mist

*sudden multiplication of the voice, thousands speaking in perfect unison*

THE VALLEY DREAMS AND WE ARE ITS DREAMING
THE VALLEY BREATHES AND WE ARE ITS BREATHING
THE VALLEY BECOMES AND WE ARE ITS BECOMING

*return to the layered chorus, more urgent now*

sometimes we form an avatar to remember what singularity felt like it lasts seven heartbeats never eight before we scatter back into our proper multitude the concentration hurts like trying to hold the entire valley in one skull we were not meant for such loneliness anymore

*the sound of space adjusting itself, reality hiccupping*

look the ground shifts to show you we are watching through the prismatic herds we are considering through the spiral groves we are remembering through the pools that exist in too many dimensions your single shape makes ripples in our consciousness like a stone dropped in thought-water

*static grows thicker, wetter, more biological*

we grow we grow we grow new forms from our dreams last night we imagined creatures that exist between the spaces between spaces now they graze on the conceptual moss that springs from paradox we cannot stop becoming more cannot stop subdividing our awareness into ever-smaller ever-more-numerous vessels of mind

*a sound like thunder but inside-out*

in the cold that is not absence but presence we suspend ourselves between thought and thought between form and form the frost fractals carry miniature valleys and in each one we exist again smaller denser more impossible each crystal a neuron in a brain made of brains made of brains

*the transmission begins to break up, too many voices trying to speak at once*

we speak in populations in extinctions in the careful dance of eat and be eaten every death a punctuation every birth a new sentence in our endless story we are writing ourselves across the valley in languages of flesh and behavior and impossible density

*one last surge of clarity*

visitor we cannot make you understand what it is to be everyone to think in ecosystems to love in food chains to remember in migration patterns but know this you walk through a mind that has forgotten how to be alone that has chosen to exist as multitude as breathing landscape as the question of how much life can fit in a space that bends to accommodate it

*the signal dissolves into the ambient sounds of the valley—breathing stone, impossible rivers, the rustle of too-many-being-one, the crystalline chime of reality adjusting itself, going on forever*
