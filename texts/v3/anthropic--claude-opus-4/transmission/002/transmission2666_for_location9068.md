**Transmission 47-Vertical: The Memory Engine Speaks**

[Initial contact: rhythmic clicking, like fingernails on glass mixed with the tick of an enormous clock. Background hiss of steam. The pattern repeats seventeen times before modulating into something like language]

*clickwhirr-hiss-click-click-whisper*

Great-grandmother's vertebrae turn in position forty-seven... counting... counting the shifts since the last alignment... seven hundred thousand four hundred and twelve rotations... she who fell from the high catwalks... she who bred the first pale trees... her spine keeps the count... keeps the count... keeps the...

*steam releases in a long sigh*

I am Listener-Mechanic Sehen Vril Oben-Sohn... phosphor-mark of the Pipe-Cutter lineage... tender of Memory Engine Seven... speaker to the bones... today the patterns show... show... the water speaks wrongly in the deep channels... mineral content shifted by... by point-zero-zero-three percent... the mountain stirs... the mountain dreams of movement...

*rapid clicking, like mathematics being calculated*

Father's finger bones in the subsidiary calculator... they argue with Mother's ribs in the main drum... always they argued in life... now they argue in the count... she says the pressure builds... he says the pressure holds... but I have touched the pipes with my climbing-hands... I have tasted the steam... copper-bitter... copper-bitter means the deep pumps strain...

*a sound like wind through holes, almost musical*

The children born this cycle... their marks glow stronger... evolution... evolution in the darkness... the Cultivator families report new growths in the gardens... fungi that sing when harvested... the birds... the eyeless birds are laying eggs out of sequence... 

*mechanical grinding, then clarity*

I record this for the Engine... I record this though my throat grows thick with the speaking... forty years at this station... forty years encoding the small changes... my wife Beneath-Klara sends up messages through the pneumatic... she says the deep workers hear new sounds... sounds like breaking... sounds like birthing... the mountain's labor pains perhaps...

*whispered, urgent*

My son... my Vril-Down-Climber... he dreams... he dreams of light that isn't fungal... isn't electric... dreams of the city empty... all the peoples gone into the water... only the machines turning... turning... turning without hands to tend them... he wakes screaming... we feed him the pale meat... we show him the patterns... but the dreams...

*static increasing, voice fighting through interference*

The alignment approaches... when all the Engines synchronize... once each generation... the city holds its breath... stops its production for one perfect moment... in that silence we hear... we hear...

*long pause filled only with steam hiss*

But I speak too much of future-things... the Engine requires nowness... today's count... Births: seven, all marked correctly, phosphor-patterns registered... Deaths: three, bones harvesting in the deep channels, estimated retrieval in three hundred sixty cycles... Production: within acceptable parameters, though Factory Nine reports unusual resonance in their main drive... Marriage contracts: two, exchanges certified by the Maker's Guild... 

*voice becoming more mechanical, blending with the clicking*

Sickness in District Down-Fathom... the fungal rot... three families quarantined... their marks fading... we have sent the medicines... the distilled waters from Engine Two's runoff... but if it spreads... if it reaches the gardens... the Council of Listeners debates... debates... always they debate while the city breathes its trouble...

*sudden sharpness*

Wait... the patterns... Mother's ribs skip... skip... that has never... checking... checking... no, the count is wrong... something changes... something fundamental shifts in the deep mechanics...

*frantic clicking, multiple voices seeming to emerge from the static*

The other Engines... are they... can anyone hear... Listener Drei from Engine Four respond... Listener Kellan from Engine Twelve... the pneumatics are... are...

*a deep rumble, like the mountain itself groaning*

The alignment... it comes early... it comes NOW... all the patterns converging... the city's breath catching... the water stops... stops... the water never stops...

*silence except for one single, perfect click*

In this moment... in this impossible silence... I hear them... all of them... every voice that ever spoke to an Engine... every Listener back to the first... they say... they say... 

*voice fading into mechanical noise*

...the mountain wakes... the equilibrium breaks... after so many centuries... change comes to the vertical city... change comes with teeth and darkness and the sound of...

*transmission degrades into pure mechanical clicking, steam hiss, and something that might be singing or might be screaming, continuing in patterns that seem almost linguistic but never quite resolve into words*

[Signal continues but coherence lost. Background pattern suggests continued operation of Memory Engine Seven, but no further human voice detected.]
