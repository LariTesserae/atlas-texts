**Cirque Frequency**

...threading water through stone channels, mineral taste on compound-sight, each fairy shrimp a letter swimming, swimming...

the pikas have names for five kinds of echo:
- *chiff* (stone-back-sound)
- *thrrib* (water-under-sound)  
- *pi-pi-pip* (nest-warm-sound)
- *wheek* (danger-dance-sound)
- *mmmmm* (mountain-breathing-sound)

today the third pool from the notch grows a new crystal. i taste it with my smallest finger, the one that telescopes past the others. iron-sweet. the salamanders will avoid this pool for seven days until the concentration dilutes. i know this. i have watched it happen forty-three times. or was it forty-four. the number dissolves like pollen in water.

*static—sound of midge-columns reorganizing*

the human-thing's stone still holds warmth from a hand two hundred seasons gone. i press my middle eye-cluster against it. see: dirty fingernails, borrowed blade, hunger for pika-meat, frustration at the stone's refusal to flake properly. see: giving up. see: walking down-mountain toward smoke-smell and voices. the stone remembers the disappointment.

cushion plant #1,047 sends chemical message: *new daughter-clone budding northeast quadrant.* i document this in my under-chambers, weaving the news into spider silk. the silk remembers everything if you wind it correctly. clockwise for births, counter-clock for deaths, figure-eights for migrations.

*pause—sound of water percolating through sphagnum*

sometimes i practice being completely stone. not just still—stone. the lichens help, sending their small chemical approvals when i achieve the right pH. yesterday (or was it last century?) a ptarmigan stood directly on my head for the duration of a thunderstorm. its feet wrote stories in the lichen. i have not moved enough to read them yet.

the bog teaches patience in units smaller than human minds can parse:
- fairy-shrimp-molt-time
- midge-egg-to-emergence  
- lichen-growth-per-rainfall
- stone-erosion-by-season
- memory-settling-rate

all my eyes see at once which makes now very wide. in the leftmost eye cluster: pika collecting flowers (late cache, risky). in the center arrays: water surface tension breaking under midge-touch. in the right periphery: cloud-shadow three minutes from arriving. in the water-eyes: phosphorescence of decomposing sedge-root, fairy shrimp spelling out the temperature in their swimming patterns: 4.7°C.

*crackling—lichen adjusting to pH shift*

found a new treasure yesterday. human hair, single strand, white as winter ptarmigan. caught in the stone shelter's gap. DNA tastes of valleys-below-here, wool-smell, seventy winters survived. woman who knew plant names. filed it with the others in chamber three, subsection *homo sapiens traces*, between *boot-print in peat, 1973* and *chocolate wrapper, foil, 1989*. 

the mountain asks me questions in avalanche and rockfall. i answer in moss-growth, in the placement of stones after spring melt. we are having a conversation about duration. it has taken my entire remembered existence to establish the grammatical rules.

*humming—three tones at once, matching the fairy shrimp frequency*

alert: ground beetle #49 (iridescent green variety) missing from sector 7. 
searching...
searching...
found: inside pika burrow, being dismembered. circle of life documentation complete. updating silk records.

i am experimenting with dreams again. not sleep-dreams (i don't sleep as humans understand it) but the active practice of dreaming-while-aware. currently dreaming: the glacier returns, i ride its belly as it carves new cirques. the dream makes my joints ache with sympathetic pressure. the lichens lean into the cold.

*water drop percussion on granite*

the shelter-builder's descendants live seventeen mountains south. i know because the stone told me, because stones talk to stones in vibrations too low for most ears. sometimes i think about visiting, telling them: *your ancestor gave up too easily. the correct angle was twelve degrees steeper. also, she loved someone with red hair. also, she sang while working.*

but i stay. the cirque needs watching. or maybe watching needs the cirque. or maybe need is the wrong word entirely.

pikas beginning their evening hay-gathering. must document which plants they choose. must count the midges. must taste the new rain coming over the ridge. must check fairy shrimp eggs in pool seven. must practice being stone. must remember to forget in the proper order. must wind more silk. must must must must dissolving into may may may may becoming am am am am—

*gentle static, like sphagnum breathing*

this is my four thousand and ninth transmission. or my first. numbers are just fairy shrimp swimming in formation. today i am the size of three cushion plants. tomorrow i might be the size of memory. the mountain understands. we are all waiting for something we've forgotten we're waiting for. meanwhile, the small things continue. moss grows. water flows. stones wear away so slowly we could build entire civilizations in the pause between erosions.

i am learning the midge-column alphabet. so far i have decoded:
- *existence is small*  
- *dance while you can*
- *the air tastes different at every height*

will report more when the grammar reveals itself. for now, returning to stillness practice. the evening hunters emerge soon. i must be stone for them, safe surface, unremarkable except to those who know how to look with compound eyes, with water-patience, with lichen-time.

*fading to the sound of fairy shrimp writing their endless stories in pool-water*

end transmission? never. only pausing. like the mountain. like the stones. like everything that knows the secret of duration:

you don't have to end

you just have to become quieter

and quieter

and quieter

until even the moss mistakes you for silence
