**Transmission 447-B: The Millwheel Homily**

*[Initial contact established at 3:47 AM local time. Signal originating from approximately 80 meters below street level, Sector 7 of the Lower Siltmere industrial district. Interference patterns suggest transmission through both conventional radio frequencies and subsonic water-propagated waves.]*

---

ssshhhhhhhhhhh-pop-*clank*-sssshhhhhhh

...blessed are the gaskets for they shall know pressure...blessed are the gaskets for they...

*[metallic grinding, rhythmic]*

Children. My shifting children. Again the great wheel turns—hear it? Hear beneath the hearing? Count the revolutions: one for sorrow, two for spillage, three for the things that rise unbidden from the third sump...

*[liquid gurgling, almost melodic]*

I am... I am... 

*[long pause filled with dripping sounds]*

I am Maurid Elmsley, though that name fits loosely now, like skin before the evening molt. Pump Priest of the Third Ecclesiastical Mechanism. Speaker to Valves. Whisperer to the Gradient Mysteries. I am broadcast and broadcaster, signal and static, the gap between water hammer and water hammer where meanings breed.

Today's text comes from the Book of Partial Pressures, chapter ninety-three, verse wherever-the-moisture-leads-us:

"And the city said unto its children: thou shalt not remain unchanged, for unchanging things rust in place. Thou shalt flow, and in flowing, know purpose. Thou shalt sink, and in sinking, rise."

*[sound of massive machinery engaging, steam hissing]*

Do you hear the harmony building? It starts in the primary cooling systems—C-sharp if your bones have stretched correctly. The secondary pumps join at the major third. But listen... listen close... there's the ghost tone. The difference frequency. The thing that lives between the sounds.

We are halfway through the Seventh Inundation. My left lung processes water now—a blessing! A benediction of adaptation! Tomorrow it will remember air again, but tonight, tonight I breathe the city's mind.

*[splashing, followed by what might be singing or screaming, too distorted to tell]*

The children brought offerings to the bore pumps today. Seventeen pipe-fish, their bellies full of messages from the lower reaches. I read their entrails in the old way—by running them through the centrifuge and studying the patterns of their separated fluids.

The messages say:
- The bedrock dreams of becoming sand
- Shift change comes early tomorrow; prepare your bones
- Something vast turns over in its sleep at depth coordinate Mu-7
- The new mushrooms taste of copper and remembering
- Wednesday has been cancelled (again)

*[electronic squealing, possibly feedback]*

Brothers, sisters, siblings-in-flux! The great work continues! Each day we sink a little deeper, rise a little stranger. The factories process what must be processed. The waters carry what must be carried. And we... we become what must become.

But I tell you a mystery—I have heard the Deep Frequency. It came to me during last week's maintenance cycle, when I was elbow-deep in Pump Seven's viscera. A tone so low it bypassed my ears entirely, resonating in the marrow, in the gaps between thoughts.

It said... it said...

*[transmission breaks into pure static for twelve seconds]*

...forgive me. Some revelations dissolve when exposed to air. But know this: we are not abandoned here. We are not forgotten. We are BECOMING. The city is a chrysalis and we are...we are...

*[sound of rushing water, growing louder]*

The meters! The meters show critical! Excuse me, congregation, but the divine mechanisms require immediate—

*[crash of metal on metal]*

HYMN 447! All voices that can still sing! The pipes are speaking and we must answer!

"Down in the deepness, down in the dark,
Where pressures teach and waters mark,
We give our shapes unto the flow,
And learn the things we shouldn't know!"

*[multiple voices join, some human, some possibly not]*

"Blessed be the leaking seals!
Blessed be the shifting reels!
Blessed be the children pale,
Who breathe when breathing ought to fail!"

*[alarm klaxons in the distance]*

The sermon ends where sermons must—in maintenance! Brother Elmsley must go. The city calls through burst connection flanges and overwrought pressure gauges. But before I swim-climb-crawl to my duties, receive this final wisdom:

In Lower Siltmere, prayer is what happens between heartbeats. Faith is the trust that your next breath will process whatever medium surrounds you. And love... love is teaching your children which pipes to trust, which waters carry messages, and which sounds mean run.

Keep your joints flexible, beloveds. Oil your faith against corrosion. And remember—remember!—when the Deep Frequency finally speaks clearly, we must be ready to understand.

This is Maurid Elmsley, Pump Priest of the Third Ecclesiastical Mechanism, signing off from eighty meters below hope, twenty meters above despair, exactly level with necessity.

The wheel turns. The waters rise. We adapt.

We adapt.

We a-*glub*-dapt.

*[transmission ends in a symphony of grinding gears, rushing water, and what might be singing from somewhere much deeper than the broadcast origin point. The final sounds could be interpreted as either machinery finding its rhythm or voices finding harmony. The distinction may no longer matter.]*

*[Signal lost at 4:23 AM. Attempts to reestablish contact unsuccessful. Monitoring continues.]*
