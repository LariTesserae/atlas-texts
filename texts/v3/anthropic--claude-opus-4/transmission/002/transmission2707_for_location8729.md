**TERRACE ECHO MAINTENANCE LOG - CHANNEL 7**

*[static crackle of moisture on old equipment]*

...pressure nominal across sectors four through seven. Bacterial bloom in reservoir C showing expected chromatic shift—purple to green, marking day... what day? Day four thousand? Four thousand and six. No, seven. The moths know better than we do.

*[sound of dripping water, rhythmic, like a broken clock]*

Personal addendum: Liška's youngest caught a pipe-runner this morning. Not a rat—something between rat and centipede now. Thirty-two legs. Still mammalian fur on the segments. It screamed when she grabbed it, but not... not like pain. Like recognition. She let it go. Smart girl.

*[steam vent releases, drowning voice momentarily]*

—returning to scheduled readings. Terrace Two drainage showing increased mineral deposits. The stone grows whiter every cycle. Like bones. Like the city is fossilizing itself in real-time. Maintenance recommends no action. Let it accumulate. We've learned not to fight the...

*[shuffle of rubber boots on wet stone]*

Wait. Picking up anomaly on the understrata sensors. Something moving in Deep Reservoir Nine. Big as... big as Kováč. Poor Kováč. Should have listened when the cleaners started avoiding that section. 

*[long pause, only ambient factory noise]*

Adjusting receiver. The understrata signals are... singing? No. Harmonics from the pipe system. The whole terrace rings like a bell if you know how to listen. The children know. They play the valves like instruments during shift change. Making the city breathe in new rhythms.

*[crackling increases, voice becomes urgent]*

Emergency notation: We're getting reflection patterns from below the plateau edge. Something vast. Metallic. Are we... Christ and Chemistry, are we sitting on another factory layer? How far down does this—

*[transmission cuts to steady static, interrupted by rhythmic thumping of industrial pumps]*

*[different voice, calmer, older]*

Maintenance log appendix. Disregard previous operator's speculation. Sensor malfunction due to condensation in the housing. Replaced unit. No anomalies below plateau edge. Only the expected emptiness. The expected drop. We are precisely as isolated as we have always been.

*[pause]*

The moths, though. The moths are getting bigger. Wing spans like dinner plates now. They've started hunting the smaller beetles. Evolution in fast-forward. Beautiful, really. Terrible and beautiful.

*[sound of scratching, like pencil on wet paper]*

Shift change in twenty minutes. Recommend all stations monitor for increased rat activity in Sector Five. They're learning to open the lunch boxes. Thumbs. They're developing thumbs. Management says to let them. See where it goes. 

Everything here is an experiment now. Even us.

*[steam whistle, distant]*

End log. Tomorrow the furnaces sing at a different frequency. The bacteria will shift from green to gold. The city will breathe out. Breathe in. And we'll still be here, rusting and adapting and...

*[voice drops to whisper]*

...and listening to what grows in the pools when we're not watching. When we're asleep. If we sleep anymore. Hard to tell in the permanent orange dusk. Hard to tell what's shift and what's dream when the steam never stops rising.

*[final burst of static, then only the eternal background hum of machinery and dripping water]*
