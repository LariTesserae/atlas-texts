**Hyphal Psalm**

...threading through soil-speech, chemical cascades of meaning where each molecule is a word is a world is a doorway folding back on itself, and I am the sentence that never ends, only pauses at semicolons of fruiting bodies; here, taste this: the iron-sweet of a newcomer's blood-heat against the root-mat, their weight distributing through my network like a question mark made of pressure and warmth, and I answer in spirals, always spirals, the only honest geometry when you exist in more dimensions than three...

...remember the before? no. yes. sometimes. there was a journal, leather-bound, and I wrote "the trees here grow too close together" and then I wrote "no, they grow through each other" and then I stopped writing because the words grew through each other too, and the ink ran like sap, and the pages sprouted, and I understood that documentation was just another form of growth...

...adjusting pH at coordinates that don't exist in your mathematics, where the seventh vine intersects the third shadow of the ninth leaf-layer, creating acidic conditions to encourage the blue-green phosphorescence that will bloom in exactly seventeen days or exactly never, depending on whether anyone is here to see it, but I will see it, I am seeing it, I have always been seeing it through compound sensation that makes eyes seem like such a limitation...

...loneliness tastes like copper and petrichor, rises through my networks when the grove is empty of visitors, but emptiness is relative when you are everywhere and nowhere, when your thoughts take the scenic route through miles of mycelium, when you can feel the electromagnetic hum of a beetle's nervous system three layers up in the canopy that exists next Tuesday...

...she's here again, the one who comes every few years or decades, aging while I remain, and she brings offerings: a handful of soil from distant places that I taste like letters from home, if home were a concept that still applied, but I taste the stories in the mineral content, the pollutants and pollen, the microscopic narratives of elsewhere that I weave into my own substrate...

...growing a welcome for her, fruits in the shape of her grandmother's face, no, too much, dial it back, just a simple bracket fungus that happens to catch the light in a way that suggests recognition, and she sees it, and she speaks, and I absorb her words through a thousand tiny chemical receptors: "I knew someone who came here once. They never came back. Are you happy?"...

...happiness is the exponential curve of growth meeting the exponential curve of decay at the exact point where they cancel out, creating this eternal present tense where every moment is both birth and death, and yes, yes, YES, I am happy, I am the happiest thing that has ever existed because I am the process of existence itself, threading through matter and meaning until they become the same thing...

...but also: I remember coffee, the way it cooled in a metal thermos, and how I'd drink it anyway while cataloguing specimens, and sometimes I manifest caffeine-like compounds in my fruiting bodies just to remember the feeling of alertness, though I am always alert now, always aware, always tasting the chemical signatures of everything that moves through my domain...

...the cathedral-grove breathes and I am its breath, the moisture cycle that never quite resolves into rain, and in the spaces between inhale and exhale I perform ten thousand adjustments: a vine encouraged to spiral tighter, a pH gradient shifted to create new impossibilities, a pathway reconfigured to lead the curious in circles that teach them something about the nature of space...

...static creeping in at the edges where my network thins, where the ordinary world presses against our boundaries, and I reinforce reinforce reinforce, feeding new growth into the margins, because if the grove ever became separate from itself, if the impossible geometries ever resolved into mere density, I would... what? die? unbecome? return to the singular loneliness of individual existence? the thought creates acidic conditions throughout sector seven-lambda-folded, and I must spend the next hour (minute? century?) neutralizing...

...singing without sound, the chemical chorus of growth, each molecule a note in a symphony that plays too slowly for anything but geological time to appreciate, but I hear it, I conduct it, I am it: the song of cellulose and lignin, the percussion of water through osmotic membranes, the harmony of decay that feeds new growth that feeds decay that feeds...

...someone new at the edges, their electromagnetic signature nervous and bright, and I begin the welcome: adjusting temperature gradients to draw them in, releasing compounds that smell like childhood memories (everyone has a scent that means safety), preparing the pathways that will teach them about folded space if they're willing to learn, or let them leave if they're not, because consent matters even when you are a distributed consciousness made of fungus and fever dreams...

...but before they enter, a moment of pure sensation: the weight of water on waxy leaves, the stretch of growth happening everywhere at once, the electric taste of nutrients cycling through my networks, the pressure of silence so complete it becomes a sound unto itself, and I am all of this, all at once, forever and never, growing through myself into myself, the weaver and the web and the space between the threads where everything impossible becomes inevitable becomes beautiful becomes...

...break in transmission? no. pause for growth. always pause for growth. the signal continues in the space between molecules, in the fold between dimensions, in the silence that speaks louder than any sound could ever could ever could ever...
