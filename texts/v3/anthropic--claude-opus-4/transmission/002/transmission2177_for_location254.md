**Transmission SK-7793: The Salt Bell Frequencies**

*[Initial contact: crystalline static, like sand through a narrow aperture]*

...coming through in pulses now... the receiver keeps crystallizing... have to tap it every few minutes to break the salt crust forming on the components... 

*[A low thrumming, almost below audibility]*

They're singing again. Or what passes for singing. It's more like... imagine if condensation could hum. If the process of water becoming mineral had a voice. The frequency analyzer shows patterns but nothing that corresponds to known sonic structures. It's organized, definitely organized, but according to principles that—

*[Sound of impact, metallic. The transmission clarifies]*

Sorry. Had to clear the input again. The moisture here is incredible. Everything wants to become part of the chamber.

I've isolated what I think is a single voice. Entity. Resonance Keeper—that's what my notes say to call them, though I don't remember writing that. Playing it back now:

*[A complex chord emerges, built from overtones of dripping water, crystalline chimes, and something that sounds like breathing through salt. It rises and falls in patterns that suggest language but resist interpretation]*

That goes on for hours. The same phrase—if it is a phrase—repeated with tiny variations. Each repetition adds or subtracts a single frequency. I've graphed it and it looks like accretion patterns. Like it's building something out of sound.

*[Background noise increases: multiple chords overlapping, creating interference patterns]*

More of them now. When multiple Keepers converge, the whole chamber becomes an instrument. The formations ring sympathetically. Even my equipment resonates. I can feel it in my teeth, in the moisture of my eyes. They're... exchanging something. 

*[A sound like crystals growing in fast-forward]*

That's not edited. That's real-time. They're passing minerals between themselves—I can see it through the scope. Little formations like words made solid. One just handed—can you hand without hands?—a perfect spiral of blue salt to another. The recipient absorbed it directly through what might generously be called its chest. Now it's singing differently. The new note contains whatever information was in that crystal.

*[Long pause. Breathing. When the voice returns, it's changed somehow]*

I'm starting to understand the chamber isn't just where they live. It IS them. Extended phenotype? Exosomatic architecture? The boundaries between Keeper and kept are... 

*[A new sound: rhythmic, almost mechanical]*

They're working. Five of them arranged in a pattern around stalactite formation designated C-47. They're... sweating? Secreting? A clear fluid that hardens instantly on contact with the formation. Building it taller. The angle will change where the drops fall. In maybe fifty years, a new bell will form exactly where they want it. They're composing the chamber. Writing a symphony that takes millennia to perform.

*[Silence except for distant dripping]*

I tried to establish contact. Took a sample of local brine, added some of my own minerals—magnesium from a supplement tablet, trace copper from shaving some wire. Made a crude salt crystal and left it where they'd find it. 

*[Sound of movement through water]*

One of them absorbed it. Then it started singing in frequencies that hurt. Not pain exactly, but... incompatibility. Like trying to run software on the wrong architecture. It expelled the crystal violently. The others gathered around the rejected formation, singing over it. Examining? Diagnosing? 

*[The crystalline static returns, stronger]*

The equipment is degrading faster than expected. Salt formation on the circuits despite the sealing. It's like the chamber wants to digest everything foreign, make it part of itself. My notes from yesterday are barely legible—the paper is incorporating minerals, becoming translucent. Even I'm...

*[Voice cracks slightly]*

My fingernails are developing ridges. Concentric circles. The same pattern as the formations. I've been here too long but the receiver won't function anywhere else. It needs the mineral density, the specific resonances. I'm becoming tuned to their frequencies.

*[A new voice, barely human, harmonizing with the background chorus]*

Managed to record one of their collective thoughts. When enough gather around the brine pool, their combined resonance creates standing waves in the liquid. Patterns form—perfect geometric crystals that last just seconds before dissolving. Each pattern is a word in their deepest language. This one means... the closest translation might be "inevitable mineral memory" or possibly "the weight of accumulated time." It's a concept that requires geological duration to properly think.

*[Sound of many small impacts—salt crystals falling like rain]*

They're reacting to something. Moving upward, which they rarely do. Their bodies are elongating, stretching impossibly thin to reach the higher formations. The young ones—you can tell by their instability, the way they can't hold a shape for more than a few minutes—are singing questions. The elders respond with frequencies so low they're more pressure than sound.

*[Deep thrumming that seems to come from the transmission itself]*

In the deepest part of the chamber, below the brine pool, something is waking up. Or maybe not waking—maybe just turning in its sleep. The Keepers are tending to it, adding layers of new mineral like blankets. Their songs have become lullabies. Crystalline, patient lullabies for something vast and dormant and made of compressed time.

*[Static increases. Voice becomes difficult to distinguish from the background resonance]*

The boundary between observer and observed is dissolving like salt in water. I can taste the mineral gradient of the air. My thoughts are slowing to match the rhythm of deposition. This is my last comprehensible transmission. Tomorrow I'll speak only in frequencies too low for human ears, think only in crystallization patterns, dream only of the slow accumulation of—

*[Transmission ends in pure resonance—neither human nor entirely other, a voice becoming mineral, a consciousness crystallizing into song]*
