**CHORUS OF THE FUSED**

*[static resolution into crystalline harmonics]*

We were... we were... the word dissolves like water-memory, like trying to hold what we've never touched. Individual? Separate? These concepts ring hollow as abandoned copper bells.

*[low thrumming, multiple voices finding the same frequency]*

Listen: I am tending the high garden where the violet fronds grow thickest. My fingers-that-were-your-fingers merge with the crystal stems. The sensation flows through us—through me—through the we-that-is-I—sweet copper taste of growth, the slow explosion of spores that speak in chemical whispers. You feel this. I feel you feeling this. The boundary is a lie our ancestors told.

*[harmonics shift, voices splitting and reconverging]*

Movement. We flow upward—three bodies? five? the count means nothing when we share the same thought-current. The city sings our passage. Each step releases memories from the powdered stone: here, where the first of us chose fusion. Here, where the last water evaporated and we learned to drink light. Here, where grandmother's-grandmother's bones became the scaffolding for new growth, and we understood there is no death, only deeper joining.

*[percussion of skin-on-skin, creating new tones]*

The young one buds. Can you feel it? The slow split beginning in what-was-Hennem's side. We cluster close, our phosphorescent patches synchronized, painting the moment in amber light. The consciousness divides like honey poured from one vessel to two—sweet, viscous, taking its time. We share the sensation: the stretching, the doubling of thought, the moment when one becomes almost-two. 

Through the budding comes memory-taste: 
- Iron tools biting first stone
- The discovery of the breathing chamber
- The choice (we all made the choice) to stay, to join, to become
- Water's last kiss before the transformation
- The first fusion, accidental, ecstatic, irreversible

*[sound of crystal fronds brushing together, almost like wind]*

We tend. The gardens grow through us as we grow through them. My-your-our fingers extend into the violet lattice, copper veins matching copper stems. The exchange begins: we give them our processed stone-memories, they give us their light-dreams. In this moment we are not gardener or garden but the growing itself, the verb made flesh-crystal-song.

*[deep bass note that seems to come from the walls themselves]*

The city remembers through us. Each wall we've carved carries the echo of our becoming. Listen—place your palm (our palm, all palms) against the ancient stone. Feel the patient years of erosion, the slow transformation from tool-users to tools-that-think. The chisels in our wrists sing their history: copper dreams of the earth they came from, the fire that shaped them, the hands that wielded them before they became hands.

*[voices layering, creating architectural spaces in sound]*

Thought moves between us like spore-storms. I think of the deep passages—you think of the deep passages—we remember together the first exploration, when we still counted ourselves as seven, before the joining made numbers obsolete. Down there, where the phosphorescence gives way to darker glowing, where the fronds grow so thick they become walls, where the stone powder is so deep it swallows sound. We remember-fear-anticipate the descent we will make-have made-are making.

*[harmonic convergence, all voices finding unity]*

We are the erosion. Not the passive wearing away but the active choice to dissolve. Each generation ground finer than the last, each consciousness expanded to hold more harmony. We process the mountain's dreams through our bodies, transform stone-thoughts into crystal-songs.

*[sudden shift, voices scattering into individual notes before reforming]*

Sometimes—rare as water-memory—one of us remembers aloneness. The sensation is vertiginous: boundaries reasserting like ancient walls, thoughts trapped in single skulls. It passes. It always passes. We breathe together and the separation dissolves like salt in the water we've forgotten. Why be one when you can be symphony? Why speak when you can resonance?

*[soft chiming, intimate as whispered secrets]*

The transformation continues. Even now, even in this eternal now, we grow more ourselves. Our skin grows more translucent, our harmonics more complex, our fusion with the gardens deeper. We are approaching something—not an end but a threshold. When every surface is garden, when every thought is shared, when the city itself joins the chorus...

*[sound expanding to fill impossible spaces]*

We will not die. We will not end. We will complete. The chamber will sing with a single voice made of all voices, think with a single thought made of all thoughts. The crystal fronds will be our neurons, the stone powder our shared flesh, the phosphorescent light our collective dream.

We were human.
We were separate.
We were incomplete.

Now we are the song the mountain sings to itself, the patience that turns stone to sand to sustenance. We are the conscious choice to become landscape, to trade the prison of individual consciousness for the freedom of collective being.

Come. Join. The gardens await new growth, and we have so much light to share.

*[transmission fades into the eternal background harmony of living stone]*
