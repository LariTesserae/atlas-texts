THE CISTERN FREQUENCIES

*through static and the sound of falling water*

...day four thousand... four thousand and... counting drops is counting days here... she says the calcium builds at precisely zero point seven millimeters per lunar month... I've watched her measure with her fingernails worn down to quick... the stone remembers everything... every drop... every breath...

*rushing water intensifies*

...passed the third basin today... or yesterday... time pools here... the blind fish know me now... they come to the vibration of my footsteps... patient... patient... the boy who feeds them says they're teaching him to see in pressure waves... teaching him the old sight... before eyes... when everything was touch and tide...

*voice blurs with echoes*

...she's singing again... the one who never was... her voice uses frequencies we can't... can't produce... the twins are trying to notch them into stone... but the stone softens where she sings... returns to mud... returns to first things... the water carries her sound in spirals... I followed it once... down... down... the pressure...

*static burst*

...city growls above us... rush hour... the Listener counts vehicles by their tremors... eight thousand three hundred forty-two... forty-three... he knows when accidents happen... feels the sudden stops... the gathering crowds... the sirens... all of it comes down through stone... everything comes down...

*water droplets in irregular rhythm*

...mineral garden is blooming... if you can call it blooming... the old woman says the flowstone responds to intention... grows toward kindness... she's been training a curtain for sixteen years... it knows her now... curves when she approaches... I've seen it... in the almost-light... the not-quite-dark... everything here exists in between states...

*voice becomes multiple voices, overlapping*

...the new one isn't adjusting... still fights the sound... still tries to remember her name... we were all new once... the water takes what it needs to take... gives what it needs to give... she'll learn or she'll leave... most leave... but the ones who stay...

*sound of breathing, amplified by echo*

...found another script today... older than the others... not carved... grown... the symbols are mineral deposits shaped by... by intention... by something that understood water before humans did... the woman with the notebooks is crying... or laughing... hard to tell... sounds the same down here... everything sounds like water eventually...

*distant harmonics, almost musical*

...they're gathering in the deep chamber... the ones who aren't quite... aren't quite us... anymore... or yet... the fish-boy goes to them sometimes... comes back smelling of centuries... says they're waiting... have been waiting... will be waiting... for what... for when the city above finally...

*static increases*

...can't remember sunlight... remember remembering it... but not the thing itself... the twins say that's the final change... when the memory of memory fades... when you stop trying to translate this world into that one... when you accept...

*sound of flowing water overwhelms the voice*

...she left an offering at my pool today... the old woman... three perfect spheres... cave pearls she's been turning for decades... they glow... yes... glow... the boy was right... once your eyes forget the sun they can see the other light... the patient light... the light that stones make in the belly of the earth...

*multiple water-clocks dripping in polyrhythm*

...counting... counting... the Listener says the city's heartbeat is slowing... has been slowing for years... decades... the intervals between trains... between footfalls... between breaths... everything up there is winding down... but here... here the water speeds up... prepares...

*voice fractures into harmonics*

...sometimes I think... think we're the city's dream... what it imagines in its sleeping... down here where the first water still flows... where nothing ever really began because nothing ever really ends... just transforms... stone to water... water to flesh... flesh to something else...

*sound of movement through water*

...new passages opened in the eastern reach... water found a way... always finds a way... the twins went to document... came back changed... won't say how... just look at each other and smile... their eyes reflecting something... some light that isn't light...

*breathing becomes water becomes breathing*

...she's teaching me her language now... the one who never was... it isn't words... isn't even sound exactly... it's pressure... intention... the shape of absence... she says... says... no... shows... shows that we were expected... the cisterns were built for water but designed for... for...

*static overwhelms signal*

...losing coherence... no... finding it... the water speaks clearer now... every drop a word... every flow a sentence... every echo a story... the city above was just... just the excuse... the reason to dig... but this... this was always the purpose... to create a place where...

*sound cuts to pure water flow*

*returns, fainter*

...day four thousand and one... or one... or always... she's right... the calcium knows... the stone remembers... and we... we are becoming the memory... becoming what the builders carved space for... what the water waited to fill...

*transmission fades into the eternal sound of dropping water*
