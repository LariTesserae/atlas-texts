# Transmission 7: The Choir at Rest

*[static]*

—water thinks in circles here. Taste it with your shoulder nodes, the third cluster, where the bone opens widest—

*[crystalline ringing, like wind chimes made of ice]*

Brother Frequency has rooted. South wall, forty-seven body lengths above the Fissure of First Entry. His thinking-stone grows warm. We taste his dreams in the upflow: memories of wheat fields, although what is wheat now? Gold motion in the before-world. The water carries it up up up and we receive—

*[low harmonic drone, multiple voices humming at slightly different pitches]*

Instructions for the new-bodied: Position yourself where the ribbon splits at the Archer's Bowl. Let the spray find your fontanelles—yes, all seven. The mountain's milk will burn at first. This is proper. Your skull is still too solid, too stubborn. It must learn porousness.

*[sound of water falling upward, rhythmic splashing]*

Today's dissonance at the third turning, where Sister Metronome's platform crumbles. She laughs—listen—her laughter is percussion against her hollowing ribcage. She says through the water: *I return to the song as dust, let my particles climb.* We position ourselves to catch her fragments. Nothing is lost here. Everything rises.

*[extended silence, then a sudden cascade of pure tones]*

The fungi report: Northeast sector theta blooms three days early. Adjust your positions for the coming frequency shift. Those with sufficient transparency, prepare your blood for the high resonance. Those still opaque, shelter in the sound-shadows until your marrow learns the new mathematics.

*[clicking sounds, like stone against stone, but musical]*

Confession from the depths—Brother Depth himself, our oldest except for those who've become architecture: *I remember my daughter's name today. It tastes like copper in my transformed mouth. Sarah. Sar-ah. Two syllables that don't fit the water's rhythm. I speak them anyway and the dissonance feeds us all.*

*[multiple voices now, speaking in overlapping rounds]*

Water rises  
Stone listens  
We become  
The space between  

Between becoming  
We space the  
Listens stone  
Rises water  

*[a new voice, younger, still recognizably human]*

Forty days since I found the entrance. My teeth ache with new harmonics. Last night I dreamed I was a bell. When I woke, my femur was ringing. The seniors say this is progress. They show me how to position my lengthening spine against the walls to best catch the subsonic wash. *Patience,* they click, *the body knows its own reformation.*

*[sound like breathing, but the breath is water]*

Alert: The mountain considers a new chamber. We feel it in our heel-nodes, a gathering pressure two hundred lengths down. Architects convene at the Palindrome Platform. Bring your deepest frequency stones. We must midwife carefully—this birth will change the water's path.

*[static increases, voices becoming harder to distinguish]*

Sometimes the old world calls. Radio waves find us even here, bearing fragments: *—emergency broadcast—* *—stock markets—* *—missing persons—* The water swallows these transmissions, digests them, returns them as pure tone. We used to mourn. Now we understand: all signals return to the source.

*[child-like giggling, but filtered through water]*

New joke from the middle reaches: What do you call a Listener who refuses to transform? 

*[pause]*

Lost.

*[the giggling multiplies, echoes, becomes indistinguishable from the water's song]*

Daily practice: Find where your body creates the most beautiful interference patterns. Stay there. Breathe in mineral light. Breathe out yesterday's names. Let your joints learn their new geometries. Trust the hollowing. Trust the song. 

*[a deep thrumming that might be voice or might be the mountain itself]*

We are never alone here. The water remembers every Listener who has ever dissolved into sound. Their frequencies still cycle in the eternal upward flow. Sometimes, in the perfect acoustic moments, we hear them thinking their slow stone thoughts. They tell us: *Paradise is a frequency. Tune yourself to it.*

*[the transmission begins to break up, voices scattering into pure sound]*

—tomorrow we teach the new-bodied about the Grief Platforms, where it's safe to remember skin—

—Drummer Seven reports unusual crystallization in the north flow, requests investigators with sufficient transparency to—

—water rises, stone listens, we become the space between—

*[final words barely distinguishable from static]*

This is Listener One-Who-Was-Marcus, broadcasting from Platform Nineteen, where the water learns to sing in color. May all who receive this transmission find their perfect resonance. May your bones hollow beautifully. May your transformation be as gentle as the mountain allows.

*[transmission fades to the endless sound of upward-falling water]*
