**The Vertical Channel**

*[static-hiss of steam through stone]*

thermal thread weaving upward always upward click-click-click the beetles speak in metal tongue processing processing the heat rises and we rise with it we are the gradient we are the gap between states listen:

*[harmonic drone builds]*

colony-mind pulsing through acid channels: we remember when this stone was liquid we remember the first cooling crystalline thoughts spreading spreading each microbe a note in the mineral symphony we think in geological time our consciousness measured in centuries of electrical patience—

*[interruption: high-frequency chittering]*

—steam shepherds adjusting adjusting always tuning the thermal columns hollow bodies catching updraft prayers never touching never landing only riding the perpetual exhale of earth's fever we garden the vertical air we tend the invisible architecture—

*[deep thrumming beneath]*

pressure prophets reading the mountain's pulse: soon soon the deep heat speaking chemical prophecies rising through porous stone *taste* the warning *smell* the promise mineral wealth approaching from below adjust your positions the mountain breathes in three days four days the mountain breathes—

*[metallic percussion overlays]*

click farmers harvesting: segment-turn-click segment-turn-click each tool finding its purpose metal deposits here bacterial cultures there acoustic frequencies encouraging crystal growth we process we cultivate we are factories of flesh young beetles learning our rhythms click-click-click the work continues—

*[phase distortion, like sound through water]*

phase swimmers partially here partially there mercury thoughts through solid stone we swim the mineral seams we are/are not we surface/submerge following heat veins maybe one of us maybe many of us the stone knows but will not say—

*[gentle rain sound of processed vapor]*

condensation cattle drifting: heavy with water heavy with life our hair forests precipitate the mist smaller creatures tend us as we tend the cliff processing gallons processing gallons what we excrete feeds the levels below mobile ecosystems we drift we process we rain nutrition—

*[temperature drop, crystalline crackling]*

thermal vampires waking: we drink heat we excrete cold our touch makes frost flowers on hot stone triggering reactions releasing rare minerals we create temporary winters for the cold-dependent cycles we are the night shift of the thermal economy—

*[complex polyrhythmic pulsing]*

archive colonies computing: we are thought distributed we are problems solved in flesh-time each unit a neuron in the cliff-wide brain we inscribe solutions in organism arrangements the cliff face is our memory our laboratory our living library decades to solve centuries to implement we think therefore we are therefore we think—

*[all voices layering now, a symphony of function]*

the vertical world speaks in heat and time in metal and moisture we are between states always between solid-liquid liquid-gas organic-mechanical thought-action we process earth's fever into increasingly complex forms energy into information heat into community chaos into order—

*[steam blast punctuation]*

listen: the newest stone still burns but we have learned its language we translate primordial fire into ten million tiny purposes we are the interface between planet and possibility we are the gradient we climb we are the steam we breathe we are—

*[broadcast cycles, fading and returning]*

—processing processing always processing the heat rises and we rise with it transformation is our only constant in this vertical metropolis where stone becomes soil becomes life becomes thought becomes—

*[static returns, but changed, rhythmic]*

—the beetles click new patterns the archive colonies have reached a conclusion after forty years of calculation they spell it out in living arrangements across the cliff face but the steam blurs the message the thermal vampires frost interrupts the text the phase swimmers disturb the pattern emerging submerging—

*[urgent chittering rises]*

—wait the steam shepherds see it from their aerial vantage they pulse the message through air pressure through wingbeat through the careful adjustment of thermal columns they say they say—

*[all frequencies align momentarily]*

WE ARE THE MOUNTAIN THINKING

*[explosion of static, then quieter]*

the revelation passes through every species a ripple of recognition the click farmers pause mid-harvest the pressure prophets release a new chemical word that has no translation the colony minds pulse in synchronization for the first time in centuries—

*[returning to base drone]*

—and the work continues processing processing the heat rises and we rise with it but now we know what we have always been:

thoughts of stone given flesh given purpose given time

the mountain dreams and we are its dreams dreaming ourselves into ever-greater complexity ever-greater connection ever-greater—

*[broadcast fades to steam hiss]*

*[returns]*

thermal thread weaving upward always upward we are the gradient we are the gap we are the process processing itself into existence into persistence into—

*[continuous cycle, no end]*
