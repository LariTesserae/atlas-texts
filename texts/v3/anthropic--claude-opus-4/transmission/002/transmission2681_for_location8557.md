**Transmission 47-Green**

*[initial static, like rainfall on dense leaves]*

...channels full again third-morning, apprentice-daughter says the western wall weeps too much, will check after middle-rest...

*[sound of scraping, rhythmic and careful]*

...shhhhhk-shhhhhk-shhhhhk... good moss here, good moss, tight-grain, holds shape... yesterday's cut already healing, can see the new growth fuzzing the edges... press here, just so, palm-flat, feel it give but not tear... the moss remembers, always remembers...

*[a voice, high and melodious, speaking to someone]*

"See-touch, young-one, see-touch how the water wants to go. Not where we make it, where it wants. We only suggest, only shape-gentle. The moss knows water longer than we know names."

*[liquid sounds, dripping becoming trickling becoming flowing]*

...drain-channel seventeen needs re-cutting tomorrow, silting up with spores... add it to the teaching-list... so much maintenance, always maintenance, but this is the breathing, this is how the city stays city and not just moss-wild place...

*[multiple voices now, a working song in harmonies that blend with water sounds]*

"Cut-careful, shape-certain,
Water-wise, moss-learning,
What we take, we give back,
What we hollow, fills with life-new..."

*[a different voice, older, teaching]*

"The fourth principle of chamber-making: never cut deeper than your middle-finger's length in one day. The moss must have time to understand the shape you're suggesting. Cut too fast, it dies. Cut too slow, it forgets. This is the pace, the moss-pace, neither urgent nor idle."

*[sound of many small feet on wet moss, a crowd passing]*

...market-morning in the center hollow... good grub harvest this ten-day, the protein-farmers are singing wealth-songs... that new strain of feeder-moss produces twice the weight, but will it last? Everything changes here, everything...

*[intimate sounds, close to the receiver, someone working alone]*

...she would have loved this spot... just here where the gold-light touches after rain... maybe plant memory-flowers when the season turns... the moss took her so gently, so gently, now she's in every wall, every street, every new growth... when I press my palm here I swear I can feel...

*[scraping resumes, meditative]*

...tomorrow, expand the sleeping-chamber another thumb's width... the twins grow so fast, soon need their own spaces... must start selecting their cultivation-plots, watching which mosses call to them... little-male already shows preference for the silver-spore variety, keeps visiting the patch by drain-seven...

*[water music, drops hitting different surfaces creating almost-melody]*

...the feast-preparation begins soon... fermentation chambers need checking, beetle-keepers say the new generation produces more sweet-syrup... everything connected, beetles to moss, moss to water, water to breath, breath to words, words to memory, memory to moss again...

*[a crisis, voices raised but still melodious]*

"Wall-break in the lower district! Water-wrong, flowing up-pattern!"
"Get the emergency shapers, bring extra tools!"
"No, no, let it flow first, see what it wants..."
"But the nursery chambers..."
"Already moving the clutch-pools, stay calm-wise..."

*[sound of coordinated work, many hands shaping]*

...this is why we practice, this is the skill, reading the moss-mood before it changes, knowing when to cut and when to let grow... see there, how they work together, no words needed now, just the knowing-touch passed from hand to hand...

*[a child's voice, wondering]*

"Grand-tender, why do we live so small-wise? The giants above, they build with dead-stone, never have to repair-constant..."

*[the elder's response, patient]*

"Because small-wise is part of the whole-wise, young-curious. We are not separate from, we are within-part. The giants build on death because they fear the changing. We build with life because we are the changing. Which is stronger, the stone that breaks or the moss that bends?"

*[night sounds beginning, bioluminescent fungi creating subtle hum]*

...lighting the deep-chambers for evening council... agenda includes the new cultivation proposals, the water-treaty with the root-district, plans for the season-festival... so much to coordinate in a city that never stops growing...

*[private moment, someone alone with their thoughts]*

...sometimes I climb to the observation perch, just to see it all... our city breathing in the green-light, every channel flowing perfect-pattern, every chamber glowing with life... we are so small, so temporary, but what we make here, this negotiation with the moss, this dance-constant with growth and decay... it matters, it matters in the deep-knowing way...

*[communal evening song beginning, many voices joining]*

"Moss-city, moss-home,
Growing-place, knowing-place,
We tend you as you tend us,
We shape you as you shape us,
In the morning we cut-careful,
In the evening we rest-grateful,
In the dreaming we join-together,
Moss and dweller, ever-always..."

*[the song continues, weaving with water sounds, with breathing, with the subtle noises of growth and settlement, until it becomes indistinguishable from the ambient sound of the city itself, the transmission fading not to static but to the sound of life continuing, persisting, thriving in the spaces between]*

*[final sound: a single drop of water falling onto moss, the softest possible impact, somehow containing everything]*
