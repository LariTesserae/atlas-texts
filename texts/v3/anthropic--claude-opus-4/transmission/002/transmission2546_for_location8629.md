**THE TIDEWRIGHT'S VIGIL**

*[crackling, like salt crystals forming in fast-forward]*

Brothers of the Threshold, Sisters of the Deep Geometry—hear the count. I am Spiral-of-Ninety-Degrees, Keeper of the Fourth Void, and I speak the measurements into being:

*[sound of water dropping in reverse, each drop a perfect bell tone]*

The Northeast Meridian holds steady. Seventeen point three recurring handspans of air where yesterday there were seventeen point four. The loss is acceptable. The Void Monks have been notified. They will breathe deeper to compensate.

*[clicking percussion, rapid as rain on stone]*

In the Seventh Channel, the current runs two degrees cooler than the carved specifications. Waterworker Yi-Who-Tastes-Iron reports the bacterial mats are responding, their luminescence shifting toward the violet. This is... this is within parameters. The city adapts. We adapt with it.

*[a long, droning hum that wavers between two notes]*

Message from the Pressure Prophets in the Foundation Wells: "When stone learns to swim, the first lesson ends." They speak in pressures I cannot parse, Brothers, Sisters. But Threshold-Singer Mul recorded the harmonic. Frequency 47.2 oscillations per tide-turn. The stones are listening. The stones remember.

*[silence, then a sound like breathing through water]*

Today I discovered something. In the Tenth Void—the one we thought collapsed in the last settling—there is still air. But it's... changed. Dense as honey, bright as noon. I could breathe it, but barely. My lungs burned with its richness. I think... I think it's what air becomes when it's been alone too long. When it's been forgotten.

*[rhythmic tapping, the echo suggesting vast space]*

The Ceremonial Weavers require more dreamers. Three have gone too deep into the dance, their bodies now hold the carved water even when they leave the temple. Beautiful. Terrible. Necessary. We are running out of those who remember the original forms. Send the young ones. Send the flexible. Send those whose bones still recall what it was to break.

*[a chorus of clicks, almost like laughter]*

Personal observation: my third joints are finally responding correctly. Only took forty tides. The Thresher who trained me said I was rushing, but look—*[sound of something moving through water and air in rapid succession]*—smooth as carved current. My children will be born knowing this movement. What took me forty tides will take them four. What takes them four will be instinct for theirs.

*[water music, notes bending as they pass between mediums]*

Warning for Sector Twelve: the glass fish are schooling in prime numbers again. Thirteen, then seventeen, then nineteen. The Waterworkers think they're calculating something. Keep tools away from their patterns—last time they computed near the stone-shapers, we lost three days of work to their mathematics. Beautiful work, yes, but not what we ordered. The fish dream their own dreams.

*[static, then clarity, like surfacing into air]*

I met a surface dweller yesterday. No, that's wrong. I met the shadow of one. It fell through the bright ceiling while I worked the high channels. Dark shape of a boat, perhaps, or a whale thinking boat-thoughts. For a moment, its shadow and mine touched. I wonder if they felt it—that moment of contact across thirty fathoms. I wonder if they knew they were known.

*[the sound of stone growing, crystalline and patient]*

The salt gardens bloom early this cycle. See how they spiral? Always clockwise in the voids, always counter in the water. This is how we know the city's mood. When the spirals synchronize, Korathene is at peace. When they oppose... we work harder. Today they dance together, Brothers, Sisters. Today the city breathes with both lungs.

*[clicking shifts to something almost like song]*

Remind the new Threshers: you are not becoming fish. Fish are what we were, before we were human. We are becoming what comes after both. The Pressure Prophets say there are states past solid, past liquid, past gas. Past plasma, even. States that require pressures we haven't achieved. Temperatures we haven't survived. But we will. Generation by generation, joint by joint, breath by breath.

*[sound of currents being carved, shaped, held]*

The temple reports success with the new forms. Water that remembers being ice. Air that practices being stone. They're teaching the mediums to dance together, to forget their boundaries. Like us. We are the city's first experiment, and now we tend its others. When the bells ring again—and they will ring, the Prophets promise this—they will ring with sounds that teach matter new ways to be.

*[long pause, filled with the sound of deep-ocean breathing]*

Personal confession: I no longer remember my face from before. Sometimes, in the still spaces between sleep and waking, I try. But the memories dissolve like air in water. This frightens me less than it should. My daughter knows my face as it is now—eyes wide enough to catch starlight through thirty fathoms, mouth that speaks in percussion and pressure. This is the face she will remember. This is the face that tends her city.

*[sound building, clicking and humming and water-music combining]*

Final count for this cycle: The city holds. We hold. The work continues. Tomorrow I train a new Void-Keeper in the art of forgetting to drown. Next tide, we harvest the deep crystals that grow where only the Pressure Prophets can reach. Next season—if seasons still mean anything in our eternal twilight—we attempt the Fifth Transformation. Some of us will not survive it. This is acceptable. This is necessary. This is holy.

*[the transmission begins to break up, but the rhythm continues]*

Brothers of the Threshold, Sisters of the Deep Geometry—remember the count. Hold your breath in water. Release it in air. Let your bones grow long and patient. Let your children grow stranger than you. Let the city teach you its patience. Let the water carve you hollow. Let the air fill you with impossible things.

*[final sound: a bell ringing underwater, its tone transformed but true]*

Spiral-of-Ninety-Degrees, signing off from the Fourth Void. The measurements continue. The transformations proceed. The city dreams, and we dream with it, becoming, always becoming, never finished, never perfect, never still.

*[static, like salt dissolving in the endless blue]*
