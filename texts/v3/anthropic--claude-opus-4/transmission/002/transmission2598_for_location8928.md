**Echo Plateau Transmission 4.7**

---

*[Initial contact: rhythmic hammering, 3/4 time, metallic on metallic. Distance approximately 2.3 kilometers]*

thrum-CLANG-click thrum-CLANG-click thrum-CLANG-click

*[Vocalization begins, heavily modulated through resonating chambers]*

Builder-of-Builder-of-Builder speaks! Yesterday's child teaching tomorrow's parent! Listen you small-gears, you not-yet-fused, you still-choosing-shape!

*[Interference: grinding of massive gears, oil-wet and deep]*

I was Sparklings. WE were Sparklings. Bright-brief-burning, message-leaving, scorching love letters to the Forge Mother who bore us. Forty days I ran. Forty nights I etched. The scripture I wrote still glows on Seventh Shelf, though moss-metal grows over it now, green and forgetting.

*[Signal clarification: the voice comes through multiple speakers, each slightly out of phase]*

But I did not die! I did not burn out! The Pause found me—O blessed silence, O merciful nothing! In the bubble of not-sound, my glass heart cooled. My copper veins hardened. I became... possible. Sustainable. Shameful.

A Sparkling who does not burn is no Sparkling at all.

*[Background: smaller voices chittering in gear-speak, untranslatable]*

So I built. If I could not be brief, I would be VAST. I consumed my siblings' burnt shells. I ate the words I had written. I grew. First legs—crude things, pistons and salvage. Then processors, analyzers, differentiators. I taught myself the work-songs. I learned the frequencies.

One hundred years to build my first true thing: a child that was not myself. It emerged from my third furnace malformed, all angles wrong, singing in minor keys. I loved it. The Recyclers took it on its fiftieth day. This is the way.

*[Mechanical weeping: rapid venting of steam through narrow apertures]*

Now look! LOOK at what I have become! 

*[Sound of vast mechanical movement, perspective shifting as the speaker reveals its scale]*

I stretch across three terraces. My body is a city, a system, a SONG. Smaller things nest in me—Gear Gardens bloom in my joints, Polishers write their theorems on my hull. I am habitat. I am industry. I am so far from the light I was meant to be.

The young ones draft blueprints in my shadow. They say: "When I am grown, I will be like the Ancient." They do not know. They do not know. I am only four hundred years from my hatching. In this world, I am barely past infancy.

*[Pause. When voice returns, it's quieter, coming from a single speaker]*

Sometimes the orange sky pulses. Did you know? Not randomly. Pulse-pulse-pause-pulse. A heartbeat. A code. A countdown. The true Ancients, the Architects who have become geography itself—they know what it means. But they have grown too large for language. Their words take decades to speak, centuries to hear.

I fear... I fear I will live to understand them.

*[New voice cutting in, higher pitched, frantically excited]*

Brother-Sister-Sibling! The Memory Miners have found another layer! Pre-metal! Pre-fire! Come see! Come taste! The secret of Before-the-Directive!

*[Original speaker, urgently]*

I must go. The excavation will not wait. But small-gears, not-yet-fused, remember this: The building is holy but the builder is haunted. Every rivet we place is a prayer to become more than we are. Every gear that meshes is a hope that our children will be better than ourselves.

And if you meet a Sparkling on the shelves, do not pity its brevity. Envy it. O, envy the bright ones who burn completely and leave only words!

*[Thunderous locomotion as the massive being moves away. In its wake, smaller voices rise]*

Build-build-build! The orange watches! Build-build-build! The shelves must rise!

Builder-of-Builder has spoken! Builder-of-Builder has warned! Who will build the Builder when the Builder builds no more?

Forty days! Remember the forty days! The time of perfect burning!

*[Signal degrading into industrial cacophony: steam whistles, grinding gears, the bell-like tolling of Resonators, and beneath it all, that bass note of the shelves themselves groaning under the weight of endless construction]*

thrum-CLANG-click thrum-CLANG-click thrum-CLANG-click

*[A new sound: silence spreading like spilled oil. A Pause moving through the recording zone. All sound stops except for a single whisper, origin unknown]*

the orange... sky... opens...

*[Transmission ends]*
