**Transmission: The Root Telegraph**

---

*[Initial contact: rhythmic tapping, like water droplets on hollow wood, but too regular, too patterned]*

tap-tap... tap-tap-tap... tap... tap-tap-tap-tap...

*[The pattern repeats, then multiplies—the same sequence echoing from different distances, different timbres of wood]*

This is Keepsthread Yanna, Fourth Bough, sunrise side, calling the daily weave...

*[Her voice comes through the wood itself—not spoken but tapped, hammered in the traders' code against the message-root that runs the length of her tree]*

...clear skies above the crown, wind from volcanic-north, the compass flowers turned and drinking...

*[Other voices join, their tapping overlapping but never conflicting, each finding its rhythm in the spaces between]*

...Seventh Bough reports nightfruit ready for harvest...
...steam vents stronger on sunset side, reroute the children's bridges...
...pearl-shells climbing, climbing, means rain in three days...

*[Underneath the tapped messages, a deeper sound—the trees themselves. Low groans of wood adjusting to moisture, the whistle of air through hollow branches, and something else... a thrumming, almost below hearing]*

Wait—

*[All tapping stops]*

The heartwood speaks. Everyone quiet. Grandmother Grove-Eye, are you listening?

*[A new rhythm begins—older, slower, each tap placed with extreme care]*

I... am... here... The... old... growth... stirs...

*[Long pause. The background thrumming grows stronger]*

Something... rises... from... the... deep... roots... Not... water... Something... else...

*[The tapping becomes erratic, multiple voices overlapping in alarm]*

—the thermal pools are bubbling—
—my workshop floor cracks—
—the chronology tree shows stress rings appearing NOW—

EVERYONE CALM.

*[A powerful rhythm cuts through—authoritative, measured. This must be a Reader]*

This is Reader Holdspattern. I feel it too. The trees... they're pulling deep. Deeper than the hot springs. They've found something.

*[The thrumming becomes a pulse. Regular. Like a heartbeat made of wood and stone]*

Children to the crown. Seal the root-chambers. All Threadkeepers prepare for—for what? The trees have no memory of this. They're excited. Can you feel it? They're... singing?

*[The tapping dissolves into pure sound—the entire forest resonating. Bark vibrating. Leaves shivering. The very air humming with some vast, slow communication between root and crown. Human voices become insignificant against this deeper conversation]*

*[Minutes pass. The sound builds. Then, cutting through it all, a single voice tapping frantically]*

The volcanic north grove! The trees—they're growing. Fast. I can SEE them growing!

*[More voices join, reports flying through the root telegraph]*

—bark cracking—
—new shoots everywhere—
—my sleeping chamber just sprouted a window—
—the bridges are thickening while we STAND on them—

*[Reader Holdspattern's voice returns, no longer calm]*

All Threadkeepers, this is... this is the Brightening. The stories were true. When the forest finds a new heat source, a new mineral vein, it... celebrates. It grows wild. We must guide it or—

*[A tremendous crack. Screaming wood. Tapping becomes chaotic]*

Bough Seven splitting!
—catch nets on Five—
—guide ropes, we need guide ropes—
—the weaver's hall is closing itself—

SING, THREADKEEPERS. SING THE SHAPING SONGS.

*[And they do. Through the wood comes not tapping now but voices, hundreds of them, raised in the ancient harmonies. The songs that ask the trees to remember their shapes, to grow with purpose, to hold their human partners safe while they remake themselves]*

*[The thrumming continues underneath, bass note to their melodies. Reports still come through—tapped quickly between verses]*

...sunset side stabilizing...
...new growth accepting guidance...
...chronology tree adding rings—one, two, five, twelve in a single hour...

*[An exhausted voice, tapping slowly]*

This is Keepsthread Yanna. Fourth Bough remains... remains stable. My tree grows a new room. I think... I think it's for the baby. The one not yet born. It knows. How does it know?

*[The Reader's voice, warm now with wonder]*

They know us. As we know them. Rest now, Threadkeepers. Tomorrow we count our new spaces, learn our changed paths. Tonight, we have witnessed the forest's joy.

*[The tapping slows, becomes irregular—tired workers signing off, reporting safety, sharing wonder. But the deep thrumming continues, will continue for days. The sound of a city remaking itself from the roots up. The sound of stone-deep patience suddenly released into explosive growth. The sound of a partnership so old that human and tree dreams have become indistinguishable]*

*[Final message, tapped so softly it's almost lost in the wooden thunder of growth]*

This is Grandmother Grove-Eye. When I was young, my grandmother told me of the last Brightening. She said... she said the trees showed them wonders. New rooms that caught rainbows. Bridges that sang in the wind. Fruits that had never existed before. Watch careful, little ones. The forest dreams with us tonight.

*[The root telegraph fades to background pattern—drip rhythms, growth rhythms, the eternal percussion of a living city reshaping itself. But changed now. Expectant. The sound of something ancient remembering how to be young]*

*[Transmission continues but human words fade out. Only the trees speaking now, in languages older than words]*
