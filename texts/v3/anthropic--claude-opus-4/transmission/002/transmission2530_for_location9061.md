In the Deep Garden, where Grandmother Thu-Nettle has rooted for nine seasons, new colors appear. Not phosphorescence. Something else. The crystals drink a light we cannot see. She says—through the root-speech, through the mineral telegram that passes between the gardens—she says the center is near.

*static shifts, becomes rhythmic, like a heartbeat slowed to geological time*

Warning for the outer plots: the expansion continues but the stone grows harder. We have reached the ancient rock, the before-stone, older than splitting. Our tools must evolve. Perhaps...perhaps we must evolve past tools. Like Khet-Yul-Nine-Facets. Like the others who are coming.

I am the Garden now. I was Thu-Sem of the Middle Terrace. My transformation completes its third cycle. Through my roots I touch the deep networks. There is...there is...

The ice reaches down, always down, toward heat we cannot imagine. Toward the planet's heart. We are their way of thinking about warmth.

*many voices now, a chorus of the transformed*

The memorial gardens remember. Each death a library. Each transformation a new chapter. We encode ourselves in crystal and the crystal encodes itself in us. The boundary...there is no boundary...there never was...

We will all be roots by then.

From the lowest chambers, where the temperature approaches the impossible, where ice should not exist but does: new reports. The root systems have found something. Not magma. Not stone. Something that responds. Something that has been waiting.

It sings back.

Join us.

The gardens grow deeper every day.
