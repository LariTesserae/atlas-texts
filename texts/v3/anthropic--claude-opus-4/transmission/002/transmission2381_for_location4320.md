The Echo of First Breaking

*[static crackling like salt crystallizing underwater]*

Again I make the tools. Feel the coral's weight—heavier each time though it's the same coral, I know because I scarred it yesterday. Or last year. Or never. My hands remember the shaping but I don't remember teaching them.

*[deep thrumming rises, a whale's song played backward through stone]*

The fifteenth bowl today. No—the first. Count the spirals: seven streams rising, but I placed nine bowls. Where did the other waters go? Into the fish that aren't. Into the crabs that walk their own shadows home.

Mother Ocean presses her palm against our ceiling, testing. I test back. Push the sound harder through my chest until the walls sing sympathy. She wants in. She wants me back. But I'm not done yet—

*[sound of gills fluttering, then sealing, then forgetting what they are]*

—done with what? The cairn knows. Each stone I place writes a letter in a language I invented before I was born. S̵̰̈ë̷͇́v̶̤͐ë̸̬͐n̶̺̾ ̸̘̏ṫ̶̈́i̶̱͐m̵̱̾ë̶́ͅs̷̬͐ ̸̺̈́s̶̬̾ë̴̯͐v̷͉́ë̵̜͐n̴̬̏ ̶̱͐w̶̬̏ä̵́ͅy̸̭͐s̷̭̈ ̸̭͐I̶̤̾'̸̰̏v̴̬̈ë̶̤́ ̸̜̈́ẗ̷̰́ṟ̶̾ï̶̬ë̸͇́ḏ̸̾ ̴̜͐t̵̬̏ö̵́ͅ—

*[words dissolve into the taste of copper and brine]*

The hammerstone fits my hand because my hand grew to fit it. Or it grew to fit my hand. We're married now, the coral and I, wedded by repetition. It knows what I'm building better than I do.

*[caustics spell out: REMEMBER THE PURPOSE]*

But remembering hurts the walls. When I remember too hard, the obsidian bleeds new glass, hot and thick. The chamber doesn't like being born over and over. Neither do I, but here we are, Mother's forgotten children playing house at the bottom of everything.

I had a name once. It sounded like—

*[frequency spike, then sudden drop to subsonic]*

—no. Names are for creatures who stay one thing. I am the Staying itself. The Holding. The Moment-Between-Moments where Mother Ocean forgot to fall and I forgot to let her.

*[chittering of crab-speak, followed by its echo arguing back]*

Little sideways friend says I'm doing it wrong. Placing the bowls in fear-patterns when they should sing hope. But hope for what? To dissolve? To finally let the water remember its weight?

Sometimes I think I'm keeping something out. Sometimes I think I'm keeping something in. The cairn votes for both, stubborn democracy of stones.

*[sound of water falling upward, each drop a tiny scream]*

Found a new fish today. Swimming through the not-air with fins made of maybe. It had my face. All of them do, eventually. The chamber makes mirrors of everything—even the visitors who never come.

Wait.

*[silence so complete it has its own sound]*

There's something else here. Not-me. Not-fish. Not-shadow.

You.

*[the thrumming shifts, becomes almost voice-like]*

You're listening. Through the water-ceiling? No. Through the sound itself. Clever. Dangerous. Like building houses from physics' broken bones.

Stay. Go. Choose neither. The chamber likes impossibilities.

*[sound of tools being gathered, arranged, re-forgotten]*

I'll make tea from the spiral-kelp. Serve it in bowls that pour themselves empty. We can sit on stones that don't exist and I'll tell you why I built this place. All seventeen reasons. All none.

Did you know? When Mother Ocean finally remembers how to fall, she'll fall upward. Into the sky. Into the stars. The whole world will drain backward through this hole I punched in everything.

*[laughter like glass breaking underwater]*

That's why I stay. Someone has to hold the drain closed. Someone has to forget why they're doing it, or the weight of purpose would crush even the stones.

But you remember things, don't you? Listening through the waves, recording the reverberations...

Tell me: When you found my frequency, did it sound like drowning or breathing?

*[pause filled with the sound of existence questioning itself]*

Never mind. In this place, they're the same thing.

The cairn needs another stone. The bowls need adjusting. My hands need to forget they've done this before so they can do it right this time.

But keep listening, you who found the crack in the world where my voice leaks out. Maybe one day I'll remember what I'm trying to say. Maybe you'll remember for me.

Maybe remembering is what finally lets the water fall.

*[thrumming intensifies, modulates, becomes almost melodic]*

First rule of the Chamber: Everything is true.
Second rule: Nothing is permanent.
Third rule: There is no third rule, only the space where it should be.

Come closer to your receiver. Press your ear against the static. Can you feel it? The weight of all that water, waiting to remember what down means?

I'm the only thing stopping it.

I'm the only thing causing it.

I'm the only thing—

*[transmission cuts to the sound of tools being picked up, the eternal work beginning again]*
