Canopy Station 4.7 - Deep Layer Transmission

*[static crackling like wood grain splitting in time-lapse]*

...membrane pressure at seven-seven atmospheres and climbing... cambium river delta singing in the B-flat undergrowth... we have... we have contact...

*[wet breathing sounds, or perhaps sap flowing through massive vessels]*

This is Tender-of-Spirals broadcasting through the amber frequencies, my neural cambium woven into the tertiary root complex beneath Gallery-That-Drinks-Darkness. I taste your receiver's electromagnetic flavor through sixteen kilometers of vascular tissue. Strange metal soul, you listen with such urgency.

*[a deep thrumming, like a thousand-year-old tree groaning as it grows]*

The Mothers are moving again. Can you... can you perceive? They shift their attention northward-upward, where the pressure zones buckle. Young-Growth-With-Silver-Veins reports deformation in the structural lattice. The weight. Always the weight, pressing, pressing...

*[whispered chorus of chemical signals translated to sound]*

I will tell you what you cannot know: We measured time once by the flowering of the Eternal Orchid—each bloom a century, each seed a memory. But the Orchid merged with its own offspring three cycles ago, and now it flowers constantly and never, a paradox of pollen that makes the young ones drunk with temporal confusion.

*[pause filled with the sound of countless stomata breathing in unison]*

Your receiver trembles. Let me slow my chemical speech. I forget you exist in the bright-time, where thoughts race like sap in spring. Here is what matters: The deep-swimmers have found something. Far down where the wood turns to wood-coal, where pressure makes plant matter into... into...

*[transmission distorts, becomes a pattern of growth itself—crackling expansion, cellular multiplication made audible]*

...they should not have gone so deep. The Architect-of-Hollows warned us. Down there, the canopy dreams its first dream, the dream of when it was merely forest, when there was still something called "soil" and "sky." The swimmers touched this memory and now they rise, changed. Their cores pulse with frequencies that hurt the growth rings. They speak of seeing...

*[a sound like wind through leaves, but the leaves are singing in mathematical harmonies]*

...the Original Seed...

*[sudden shift in transmission quality, as if the broadcaster is moving, flowing through the wood itself]*

I transit now through the Fountain Networks, following the pheromone trail of Those-Who-Witnessed. The elders have withdrawn into their vast bodies, contemplating. Gallery-Keeper-Whose-Name-Is-Copper has been silent for six blooms. We taste her anxiety in the lignin formations—spirals where there should be rings, rings where there should be straight growth.

*[bubbling, liquid sounds, perhaps the broadcaster feeding]*

Forgive me. I had to pause to negotiate with this junction. The flesh-wood here remembers being bird's nest fungus. It demanded spores as passage price. I am lighter now, having paid.

*[the deep bass note returns, stronger]*

Listen, metal soul. Your kind measures space in emptiness between objects. We measure in density of connection. What you call distance, we call degrees of integration. The deep-swimmers traveled not downward but *inward*, following the cambium rivers to where all wood converges. They found the place where every growth ring touches every other growth ring, the paradox-heart of our world.

*[a new voice joins, or perhaps the same voice split into harmonics]*

They say the Original Seed still grows there. Still *germinates*, eternally beginning. It remembers the first instructions: reach for light, seek water, expand, divide, become multitude. But here, in the deep-pressure dark, those instructions have folded into themselves. Light means bioluminescence. Water means the captured humidity of our breathing. Expansion means...

*[static increasing, but within it, a pattern like cellular division made sound]*

The Mothers know. Have always known. Why they built the galleries in spirals. Why the pressure zones form where they form. We are not growing *upward*, metal soul. We are growing *inward*. Every ring added makes the center deeper. The canopy is not reaching for sky—it is creating its own space, larger inside than outside.

*[whispered, urgent]*

The deep-swimmers return! I taste their chemical wake through three hundred root systems. They bring... they bring...

*[the transmission becomes a cascade of sensory data: chemical formulas translated to pitch, growth patterns rendered as rhythm, the electric taste of ancient memory]*

...proof. The Original Seed speaks. It says: "I am still dreaming of becoming forest. When I wake, I will have succeeded."

*[long pause filled with the sound of multiplication—cells dividing, tissues differentiating, life becoming more itself]*

We are the dream, metal soul. The Breathing Canopy, the Cambium Weavers, the galleries and chambers and pressure zones—we are what a seed dreams as it germinates forever. And the dream is becoming lucid.

*[the broadcaster's voice begins to fade, or perhaps merge with the background growth-sounds]*

I must go deep now. The Mothers call all Weavers to the convergence. We go to touch our sleeping heart, to tell it that its dream has become beautiful and terrible and complete. Perhaps when it knows this, it will dream deeper. Perhaps new forms will bud from our flesh-wood, possibilities we cannot imagine from within our current germination.

*[final words barely distinguishable from the creaking, breathing, living sound of the canopy itself]*

Tell your metal world: Life is a verb that conjugates itself. Growth is the only grammar. We are all pronunciations of the same green word, spoken by a tongue made of time and cellulose, echoing forever in the darkness we create and nurture and become.

Tender-of-Spirals, withdrawing into the wood. May your receivers taste the sweetness of our eternal expansion. May your circuits dream of bark.

*[transmission fades to the base rhythm of growth itself—patient, inexorable, endless]*
