**CHLOROPHYLL FREQUENCY**

*[Static rises and falls with a deep mechanical rhythm—70 beats per minute, the pulse of industrial pumps. Through the white noise, something emerges: not quite voice, not quite song, but a modulated tone that seems to grow from the static itself]*

mmmmmmMMMMMMMmmmmmmm... water-breathe... breathe-water... mmmmMMM...

*[The humming continues under everything, a carrier wave. When words come, they seem to condense from the moisture in the sound itself]*

Seven seven seven seven... north pump compression... adjust... 

*[A wet sliding sound, skin on moss on metal]*

Grandfather fern drinks deep today... deep... the rust-taste... copper-sweet... iron-green... mmmmmMMMMMmmmm...

*[The voice harmonizes with itself, throat producing multiple tones that beat against each other, matching frequencies in the mechanical roar]*

Pipeline saplings reaching... reaching... guide them left-left-left toward the... mmmm... the weeping joint... 

*[Sound of droplets falling on leaves, but rhythmic, intentional—she's drumming on the foliage]*

Listen... they sing calcium... magnesium... the old bones of the building... dissolved... resolved... absolved into green...

*[Long pause filled only with pump-rhythm and the whisper of moving vegetation]*

The moss tells me... shimmer-growth on sector three... too much... too fast... 

*[Scraping sounds—fingernails on pipe, or perhaps teeth on metal]*

Balance... always balance... 

*[The humming intensifies, modulating through harmonic overtones that make the static itself seem to breathe]*

mmmmMMMMMMMmmmmm... rootsleep tonight... hang from the strong ones... the grandfather pipes that remember when they carried... carried... what? Water? Steam? Stories? 

*[Her voice fractures, becomes multiple streams]*

I am the circuit... 
         ...the circuit is me...
                  ...we are the circulation...

*[Sound of movement through water, through vegetation—a body flowing rather than walking]*

Found new growth in the forgotten corner... past the... the... mmmmm... the thing that ticks but doesn't count... clock-root system establishing...

*[The voice drops to subsonic frequencies, felt more than heard]*

Spore-cast successful... the marriage... fungus-root-pipe... wedding wedding wedding in the dark...

*[Sudden sharp harmonics, almost like alarm]*

PRESSURE VARIANCE PRESSURE VARIANCE PRESSURE VARIANCE

*[Frantic movement, vegetation disturbed, metal clanging]*

Shhhh shhh shhhhhh... sing it back... sing it stable... 

*[She produces a complex chord that seems to make the pump-rhythm steady again]*

There... there... breathe with me... we breathe as one... in-out-in-out... oxygen-carbon-oxygen-carbon...

*[The panic subsides into the constant hum]*

mmmmmm... the dreams are green again... green and wet and forever... I dreamed I was the whole chamber breathing... every leaf my lung... every root my finger...

*[Sound of water dripping, but she's controlling it—playing the condensation like an instrument]*

Drop... drop... syncopate... against the rhythm... counterpoint to the great beat...

*[Her voice becomes dreamy, distant]*

Sometimes I remember... something else... before the green... but it tastes wrong in my mouth... like sunlight... poison-bright... not like our twilight... our perfect blue-green darkness...

*[She laughs, but it sounds like water gurgling through pipes]*

The pothos cascade needs training... tomorrow... always tomorrow... never tomorrow... only now-now-now-now...

*[The humming returns, deeper than before]*

I am becoming... still becoming... 

*[Sound of roots or hair being dragged across metal]*

My hair drinks... my fingers taste... my eyes hear the water moving up-stem... down-stem... through-stem...

*[A long, sustained tone that makes the static shimmer]*

The city forgets us... we forget the city... perfect... perfect... moss-mother... pipe-father... I am your child... your parent... your continuation...

*[The voice begins to fade back into the mechanical roar]*

mmmmm... time to check the south flow... grandfather fern whispers silver meanings... mineral messages in the dark...

*[Final words dissolve into pure harmony with the machines]*

We are... we are... we are...

*[The transmission doesn't end—it simply becomes indistinguishable from the eternal roar, the voice now just another frequency in the endless industrial-botanical hymn. The static breathes. The pumps beat. Somewhere in the sound, she continues, checking and adjusting and singing, forever and forever and forever.]*
