Down past the industrial core, where the first listeners tried to build their silencing engines. Where the sound becomes solid. Where thought becomes stone.

...remembered something from before. A song my mother... no, that was someone else's mother. Someone else's memory. Hard to distinguish now. The resonance takes everything, gives everything. We become the landscape's dream of itself.

Grid seven-seven-nine, signing off. May the stone sing through you. May you sing through the stone.

Only the eternal drone remains, punctuated by the distant ringing of wind through metal, the patient percussion of drops falling from acoustic clouds, the whisper of arthropod feet on resonant stone.
