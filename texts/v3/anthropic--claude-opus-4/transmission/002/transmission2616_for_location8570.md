**Receiver Log 7741-B: The Moss Oracle's Breath**

[Static. The sound of water on stone, endless. A whistle cuts through—drops low, rises sharp. Stone-worker's call.]

*hhhhhhhhsssssss-click-click-whistle-whirrrrrr*

*the mountain breathes out and we breathe in     the mountain breathes in and we breathe out     this is the way     this is the only way     moss knows     moss grows     leftward spiral for the living     rightward spiral for the—*

*in the deep places where the city's roots touch the mountain's heart     where the thermal springs begin their journey      where pressure makes prophecy of steam     something stirs     not quite waking     not quite sleeping     not quite anything we have words for*

[The old one's voice, clear suddenly through all interference:]

"Forty-seven thousand nine hundred ninety-nine. Forty-eight thousand. The spiral completes. The pattern holds. We are what the mountain dreams when it dreams of being more than stone."

[Sound cuts to water. Only water. Always water. The transmission continues but words dissolve, becoming the sound of a city breathing with its mountain, the mountain breathing with its city, neither wholly themselves anymore, both becoming something the receiver cannot name.]

[Signal deteriorates into the rhythm of drops on stone. Counting. Always counting. The numbers rise like mist.]
