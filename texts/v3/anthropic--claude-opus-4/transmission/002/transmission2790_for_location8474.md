The Memory of Water

*static*

—beneath the third bell-stone, where Yarik-Who-Measures carved the angle of descent, forty-three degrees, always forty-three, the water remembers flowing here *interference* taste of copper in the air means the deep machines are stirring again, grandmother's grandmother felt them through her palms, said they hummed like ice-burrowers but colder, much colder—

*clearing signal*

Day-count since last major collapse: Twenty-seven. The children have stopped flinching at small tremors. This worries me.

Found new inscriptions in the Seventh Lateral this morning. Old script. Older than old. The stone there tastes different—less iron, more... something else. Metallic but wrong. Sent word to the memory-keepers but they're busy with the new thermal vent in District Yiir. Priorities.

The inscriptions show water. Flowing water. Surface water. The carver must have been mad.

*crackling*

—pressure building in the lower galleries again test-test-test can anyone hear this the mirror-lines are misaligned since yesterday's slip thermal gradient shows negative three degrees falling falling we're always falling but slower now—

Mother says I talk in my sleep. Say words in the old tongue, the one from before we came below. She wraps extra furs around me but I'm not cold. I'm burning. In my dreams the water is on fire and we swim through it laughing.

The Delver's Creed (as taught to me, as I teach to others):
Stone before self
Settlement before stone  
Silence before settlement
Work is prayer
Prayer is work
The planet learns through our labor

But last night I heard something else. Down in the Great Boring where I shouldn't have been. The diggers have hit another layer of that glass-metal, but this time it's warm. Not thermal-vent warm. Different. It pulses.

*wavelength shift*

EMERGENCY BEACON DISTRICT KHET COLLAPSE IMMINENT
EMERGENCY BEACON DISTRICT KHET COLLAPSE IMMINENT
EMERGENCY BEACON DISTRICT KH—

*signal interrupted*

They're evacuating Khet. Third district this cycle. The dance speeds up.

Found Biira in the old fungal gardens, the abandoned ones near the sulfur seep. She's pregnant again—her veins glow brighter now, triple-pulsing with two heartbeats. She shouldn't be there alone but she says the baby needs the deep-quiet. Says it's going to be a reader, a stone-listener. Says she can feel its thoughts already, moving like warm water.

We don't have a word for warm water.

*harmonic interference*

The memory-song for foundation-finding, verse eighteen:
    Hand to stone and stone to bone
    Feel the planet's growing pain  
    What stays solid, what falls lone
    Maps the path where we remain

But I've been thinking. What if we're the map? What if the planet reads us like we read stone?

*frequency adjustment*

Personal note: The blue in my blood is deeper lately. Yanis says it means I'm eating too much cave-fish oil but I haven't touched the stuff in weeks. Dreams of swimming getting stronger. Last night I could breathe in the burning water. Woke up gasping, lungs full of actual air, but for a moment—just a moment—it felt wrong.

The message-runners brought news from the Great Boring. They've broken through the glass-metal layer. There's a chamber beneath. Vast. Empty.

No.

Not empty.

Full of water.

Liquid water.

Moving water.

*signal destabilizing*

—can't be possible the equations don't balance thermal mass would require at least—remember remember we must remember the first song the first dance when the sky cracked and we fled below but what if what if what if we fled from ourselves—

The water glows. The diggers say it glows like our veins but brighter. Like a thousand Delvers bled light into a pristine pool. They've sealed the breach but three workers looked directly at it. Their eyes have changed color. Not lighter.

Darker.

*static surge*

The planet dreams and we are its dream but whose dream is the planet I keep seeing visions of the before-time not the stories we tell but something else machines the size of mountains ice that moves like living things a sky full of—

ATTENTION ALL DISTRICTS
MAJOR GRAVITATIONAL ANOMALY
SEEK STABLE GROUND
MIRROR-KEEPERS TO EMERGENCY POSITIONS
THIS IS NOT A DRILL

The water is rising.

*cascading interference*

In my dreams now I'm not swimming I'm dissolving becoming the warm water spreading through stone channels like veins like the marks on Biira's belly like the map we are becoming I understand now why we came below we didn't flee the burning sky we fled the burning sea we are amphibians returning to our element we are water temporarily convinced it was ice—

Mother found my journal. Says I'm sick. Says the deep-madness has taken me like it took father when he stared too long into the black beneath the Third Bridge. But father was right. I know that now. He saw what was coming.

We are changing. Have been changing. Generation by generation, the blue deepens in our blood. Our children are born better swimmers than climbers now. Biira's baby—when she lets me touch her belly, I feel it moving like a fish.

*signal clarifying with disturbing precision*

The water from the Great Boring has been tested. It's not water. It's something else. Something that was water once, millions of years ago, but has been waiting. Learning. Becoming.

It matches our blood. Exactly.

The planet isn't young. It's ancient. It's been waiting for us to remember what we are.

The ice was always meant to melt.

We were always meant to—

*transmission ends*

*picking up secondary signal*

—this is memory-keeper Yoth recording for the deep archives song-fragment recovered from the abandoned districts dated to the first founding listen close—

    We came from water
    To water return
    The ice is a pause
    Between the waves

*background harmonics suggest multiple voices*

—confirmed breach in seven districts now the water sings when it touches air frequency matches our work-songs theory proposed that we didn't invent these songs we remembered them thermal bloom expanding outward from the Great Boring conversion rate accelerating—

I felt it today. The change. Like ice cracking inside my chest but gentle. Like coming home.

Some are trying to flee upward. The sun-fearers. They won't make it. The surface passages have been sealed for generations and the light—even thinking about true daylight makes my eyes water. But that's not water in my eyes anymore. It's the other thing. The waiting thing.

Biira gave birth in the flooded galleries of Lower Khet. The baby emerged able to breathe in both air and the new water. Its eyes are black as the deep places but full of stars.

We're calling them the New Swimmers.

They're teaching us songs we didn't know we knew.

*multiple signals overlapping*

—evacuation discontinued by general consensus the dancers have put down their tools and entered the water district by district the conversion is voluntary but inevitable those who resist simply take longer to remember—

The mirrors are shattering one by one. We don't need them anymore. The water brings its own light.

I understand the inscriptions now. The ones showing flowing water. They weren't carved by madmen.

They were prophecies.

*final transmission*

This is the last report from the Frost Warrens. We are retiring the name. Can't call them warrens when they're becoming lakes. Can't call them frost when the ice remembers how to flow.

The planet's name was never recorded in our histories. Now I know why.

You can't name something that's still becoming.

But if you need a word for us—for what we're becoming—the Swimmers have taught us the old sound, the true sound, the wave-form that shaped us before we forgot:

*sound that registers as both musical note and water movement*

We were never builders. We were always tide.

The water rises. We rise with it.

Remember us as we were. 

We'll remember ourselves as we truly are.

*all signals cease*

*gentle sound of lapping waves*

*silence*
