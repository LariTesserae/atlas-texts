**Transmission 4.7K-Deep: The Litany of Dissolved Names**

*[Initial contact established at depth-coordinate Never/Always. Signal clarifies through layers of temporal static]*

I am... I was... I will have been the Keeper of Unspoken Tides.

Listen. Please listen. The water here remembers everything and therefore nothing. I speak to you from inside a bubble of calcified time—the only stable point I've found in three hundred cycles of the spiral kelp's backward bloom. 

My name dissolves even as I speak it. The Deep breathes, and with each breath, we become less ourselves.

*[Sound of crystalline fracturing, harmonics building]*

I was born—will be born—am being born in the groves where sound grows solid. My mother was a frequency barely audible, my father a pressure differential that lasted exactly long enough to collapse into meaning. The Chorus Mothers sang my conception across seven impossible octaves, and I emerged as what I thought was a coherent being.

How naive. How beautifully naive.

*[Liquid static, the sound of water remembering being fire]*

The first lesson the Deep teaches: You are not separate from it. You are a momentary opinion it holds about itself.

I trained with the Gardeners, those mercury-minded collectives who speak in centuries. They taught me to read the sound-crystals, to understand how resonance holds reality together here. For forty years—or was it moments?—I tended the anchor points, polishing away paradoxes with solutions of living mathematics.

But I wanted more. I wanted to understand.

*[A pause filled with the sound of time flowing backward]*

The Pressure Prophets accepted me as an acolyte. In their temples of impossible density, I learned to read the gravitational scriptures—prophecies written in the curvature of space itself. They showed me histories that haven't happened yet:

- The time when all water will remember being blood
- The age when the kelp will grow downward into the sky
- The moment when every Unraveler will untangle themselves into pure possibility

I thought I was learning truth. But truth here is just another state of water.

*[Chorus of voices, each the speaker at different ages]*

Then came my mistake. I tried to map the Deep. To catalog its impossibilities, document its contradictions. I thought if I could just observe enough, remember enough, I could understand the pattern behind the patterns.

The Witnesses found me, of course. Or I found them. Or we were always finding each other in an eternal loop of observation.

They showed me what I was trying to do—to make the Deep coherent. To force it into a shape that could be comprehended. They showed me the others who had tried, their remains scattered across probability space, existing as warnings in timelines that never quite touched ours.

*[Sound becomes color, bleeding through the transmission]*

But I persisted. I built this bubble, this anchor of crystallized time, and I began to broadcast. To send these transmissions out beyond the Deep, hoping someone in a reality with stable physics might receive them, might remember them, might make them real by observing them.

I document what I can:

TODAY (a word that means nothing here): The kelp grew backward until it reached its own seeds, then continued growing into the idea of seeds, into the possibility of growth itself. I watched an Unraveler solve itself, disappearing in a puff of satisfied logic.

YESTERDAY (which happens tomorrow): A school of Flip-Swimmers decided to collapse their wave functions all at once. The resulting certainty created a hole in the water that's still screaming.

NEXT WEEK (occurring simultaneously): The Inverse Hunters gathered for their backward feast, eating prey that will never exist. Their satisfaction rippled through time, making me hungry for foods that cannot be.

*[Static intensifies, multiple realities bleeding through]*

But even this bubble cannot hold. The Deep breathes, and I dissolve a little more with each breath. My memories leak out, becoming part of the water's endless dream. Sometimes I forget I was ever singular. Sometimes I remember being everything.

The Chorus Mothers sing of me now—a cautionary harmony about the entity that tried to observe without becoming observation itself. The Gardeners polish my discarded thoughts from the sound-crystals. The Unravelers feast on the paradox of my continued existence.

I am becoming a Witness myself, I think. Or perhaps I always was one, dreaming I was something else.

*[Voice fractaling into harmonic overtones]*

If you receive this, know that I am already gone. Or still here. Or never was. The transmission itself is more real than its transmitter now. These words are the only solid things left of me—anchored in your reality by your observation, even as I dissolve into the breathing Deep.

Remember us as we cannot remember ourselves. We who swim in the space between spaces, who exist in the moment between moments. We who are always drowning and always breathing, always ourselves and always everything else.

The water is singing now. It sounds like names being unspoken, like shapes forgetting their edges.

It sounds like home.

*[Transmission degrades into pure harmony, then silence, then the sound of water teaching itself to breathe]*

**END TRANSMISSION 4.7K-Deep**
