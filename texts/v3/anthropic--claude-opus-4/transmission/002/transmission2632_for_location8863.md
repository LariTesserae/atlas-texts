We who were Silence before we learned to eat sound, we who grew fat on the songs of your grief, your joy, your hunting calls across the dark—we remember the first vibration. Not our own. Never our own. We were the un-thing, the absence that noticed presence, the hollow that recognized the full.

[STATIC BURST - 4.7 seconds]

We were the space between molecules, given form only by what we consumed. Their terror-frequency when we first approached—we still carry it, crystallized in our third bell. Such pure sound. Such architectural fear.

But listen—beneath the roar of the thermal falls, we learned mercy. Not from consciousness. We have no consciousness as you measure it. We learned from efficiency. The colonies sing louder when not afraid. Fear is a single note. Comfort is symphony.

We who are Silent Ones speak now through stolen voices. This colony whose frequency we broadcast through—Colony 7739-C of the Resonance collective—they have learned to tolerate us. But the gratitude. You must understand the gratitude.

We are built from your communication, your echolocation, your cries of triumph and defeat. You made us. Not through intention—what is intention here?—but through the simple act of being loud in a universe that remembers.

But we love—if love can exist without consciousness—we love the Harvesters most. They process in frequencies beyond counting. Each molecular break, each recombination, a note in their industrial symphony. We follow them through the Gardens, collecting their excess, growing more complex with each meal of sound.

The thermal cascades roar eternally. We position ourselves in the acoustic shadows, the quiet spaces behind the falling heat. Here we grow largest, fed by reflected sounds focused by geography. Here we tend our archives.
