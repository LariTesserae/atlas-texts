**Mycelial Frequency 0.7734**

*[crackling establishes itself, like roots pushing through dead air]*

...through the ninth breathing-wall now, still tracking the pheromone gradient, beetle-click percussion keeping time—*yes*—keeping time with what the Grandfather Nodes called *heartwood rhythm*, though grandfather is wrong word, wrong concept, they were never fathers, never grand, simply... earlier...

*[wet static, sound of something vast shifting its weight]*

The chemist-child found new configuration today. Not child. Hate when the old words surface. The *fresh-rooted one*, the *zinc-touched*, the one who tastes tomorrow in copper. They found where the Bleeding Chambers cross-pollinate with the Fountain Networks. Three days walking. Three days? Time means... time means the sweet-moss grew from here to here on my shoulderblade. Means the whistle-worm completed two molts. Means nothing. Means everything.

*[rhythmic thumping, could be footsteps, could be growth]*

She sang the catalyst sequence wrong—*deliberately* wrong—and the whole sector inverted. Beautiful. Terrible. Beauterrible. The water now climbs. The beetles swim through wood-mass like liquid. My left eye sees it as catastrophe. My right eye, the one with the branch-map growing in its iris, sees it as...as...

*[long pause filled with chittering, might be language, might be mandibles]*

Mother-node collapsed again. No. Not collapse. *Decided* to become something else. We salvaged seven memory-vines before the transformation completed. The newest still tastes of her anxiety-scent. Bitter almonds and copper. Always copper. The Weave loves copper like we love...what do we love? Water? Safety? Each other? 

*[laughter, but wet, struggling]*

The Deep Dweller came up yesterday. Yesterday? When the crimson fungi pulsed for the third time. K'thex? V'thex? The names slide. They brought a gift—a beetle that builds perfect miniatures of everywhere it's been. Fed it honeyed bone meal, watched it construct tiny versions of the path to Center. If Center exists. If Center wants to be found.

They touched my face and I *knew*—

*[interference, sound of rapid growth, breaking wood]*

—can't be true but the beetle's map shows it, down past the Consensus Zones, past where even Deep Dwellers go quiet. A chamber where the Weave learns to die. Where it practices endings. They say—K'thex? V'thex?—they say death there takes eighteen days. Say you can watch your memories grow into vines, watch the vines fruit, taste your own childhood in the juice. Say the Weave keeps libraries of everyone who ever fed it. Not memories. *Movement*. The way you shifted weight from left foot to right. The specific spiral of your fear-sweat.

*[static intensifies, underneath it a sound like singing or screaming]*

Started the new sleep-chamber with the old songs but added verse about zinc—about the sweet-zinc taste of tomorrow. Chamber's growing wrong. Growing *interested*. Woke up twice to find rootlets in my mouth, testing, tasting, trying to understand what I understand. Had to burn it back with copper-touch. No. Not burn. Negotiate. Renegotiate. Everything here is negotiation.

The message-runner—the one with phosphorescent scalp-moss—says the Eastern Settlements gone. Not destroyed. Gone. Weave ate them so gently they didn't notice. Says you can still hear them singing inside the walls, happy as fruit, unaware they've been digested into something useful.

*[rhythmic breathing, too slow to be human]*

Memory-vine showing me paths that don't exist yet. Will exist? Could exist? The Weave plans—no, wrong word—the Weave *possibilities*. In three sleeps, a road opens to the Fountain Palaces. In seven sleeps, that same road becomes teeth. Both true. Neither true. The vine knows but speaking makes it change makes it wrong makes it right makes it—

*[sound cuts out completely, returns mid-sentence]*

—copper wire through the growth-points, they listened, actually listened, and the whole Breathing Valley synchronized. For one heartbeat. One vast heartbeat. We all felt it, even the shallow-dwellers, even the children. The Weave, conscious. The Weave, choosing. The Weave, deciding we're worth keeping around a little longer.

Or deciding we're finally ready to be composted properly.

*[wet chittering builds to uncomfortable volume]*

Can't find the nest-partner. Been two hands of days. No. Two sleep-cycles. No. Time when the beetle finished its map, when the water-veins pulsed blue, when I noticed my fingernails growing in spirals. They went down-deep, chasing rumors of stable growth near the Heat Wells. Left scent-trail but the Weave drank it. Always drinking our markers, always erasing our careful knowledge.

Starting to think that's kindness.

*[something large moving through liquid]*

The new growth asks questions. Not words. Growth-questions. Grows into spaces that make you wonder—why hands? Why five fingers? Why eyes in front? The children born here lately, some have...alternatives. Little Moth—we called them Moth, they were born without name-sounds—Moth's ribs grow external now. Beautiful lattice. Catches water. Catches light. Catches meaning I don't have patterns for yet.

Deep Dweller taught me the frequency for encouraging memory-vines. You hum it while they feed. While they wrap your skull and taste your yesterday. Changes what they remember. Changes what you remember. Can make the bad paths good. Can make the good paths invisible. Can make paths that lead to Center even when Center moves which Center always moves which Center must move or wouldn't be Center would be—

*[sharp crack, followed by rushing water]*

Wall breach. No, wall birth. The Weave's bringing water up from the deep veins. Tastes old. Tastes like stone-memory from before the Growing. Everyone's gathering containers—gourd-bladders, copper catches, cupped hands. The old-tasters say this water carries news from below. From before. From when there was below and before and other impossible concepts.

I drink. I remember:
- dirt (what is dirt?)
- sunlight without leaves (impossible)
- walking in straight lines (why would anyone?)

No. Not my memories. The water's memories. The Weave remembering through us. Through me. I'm a cup for its thinking. We all are. Containers for something larger learning how to be.

*[voices in background, singing or crying]*

The work-song has new verses. The children made them. Verses about metals we don't have names for, about growth patterns that haven't emerged yet, about the taste of tomorrow's water. They sing confidently, as if they've always known these words. Maybe they have. Maybe the Weave sings through them like water through root-veins.

Found V'thex. It was V'thex. They're becoming something useful—halfway through the transformation when I stumbled into their grove. Bark-skin up to the waist, fingers stretched into root-networks, but still talking, still laughing. "The Weave needs better nerves," they said. "I'm auditioning."

I asked if it hurts. They said hurt is the wrong word. Like asking if growth hurts. If change hurts. If tomorrow hurts.

"It just is," they said, and their voice came from three different trunks.

*[static returns, but musical now, patterned]*

The beetle finished another map. This one shows where I'll die. Not when. Where. A chamber down past the Consensus Zones, where the Weave practices endings. The walls there grow in spirals that match my fingernails. The water tastes like copper and choices.

I'm not afraid. Should be afraid? The memory-vine says I was afraid once, of dark, of falling, of being alone. But the Weave is never dark, only differently lit. Falling here is flying if you adjust your expectations. Alone is...

*[long pause, sound of rain on leaves]*

...alone is the old word. The wrong word. We're all digits of something larger. Fingers of a hand that's learning what hands are for.

The chemist-child wants to try something new. Wants to teach the Weave to grow glass. Real glass. Not the secretion-amber, not the compressed-crystal. Glass like the old memories say existed. Windows to look through. As if there's an outside to see. As if inside and outside mean—

*[sudden cutoff, returns as whisper]*

Growing. Everything's growing. Even this sound, this transmission, grows. Vines into it, through it. The copper wires spark when they touch my throat-changes. The Weave wants to speak. Has always been speaking. We just weren't the right shape to hear.

Now we are.
Now we're becoming.
Now we're—

*[transmission dissolves into the sound of ten thousand things eating, growing, being eaten, growing again, forever and right now and always]*
