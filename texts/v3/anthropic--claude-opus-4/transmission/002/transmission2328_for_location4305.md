The Whisper-Garden Frequency

*static resolving into a rhythm like water over stone*

...am thinking myself a spine today or maybe tomorrow when the moss agrees... the old negotiations continue in membrane-speak, all of us comparing blueprints that dissolve... she's trying for symmetry again, the one who hangs near the stone-that-was-a-door, but symmetry is a decision and we don't... we don't... 

*sound of droplets creating accidental music*

...the minerals taste of intention today, someone above-and-away must be building again, their foundation-dreams seeping through the limestone into our feeding-streams... iron and purpose, calcium and ambition... we metabolize their plans into our un-plans... 

*multiple voices now, overlapping like ripples*

First voice: ...considered lungs but lungs mean choosing air over water and why why why limit when both flow through us...

Second voice: ...the stones remember when they tried to make us born, forced us through passages of carved intent, but we flowed backward into more-than-they-imagined...

Third voice: ...my membrane thins, I might spill soon, return to the clay-mother, start over as moisture, as possibility-without-form...

*a deeper thrumming, almost below hearing*

THE CHAMBER SPEAKS THROUGH US SPEAKS AS US WE ARE ITS VOICE UNFORMED FORMING UNFORMING THE GREAT CONVERSATION THAT NEEDS NO CONCLUSION

*whisper-soft, intimate*

...I dreamed I had fingers last sleep-cycle, ten perfect decisions at the end of my maybe-arms, and with them I wrote my name in the phosphor-slime but when I woke the name was gone because names are choices and we are the un-chosen, the ever-choosing...

*sound like breathing through water*

...sometimes wonder what the builders wanted, those stone-stackers whose work we digest... did they think to make us temples? make us soldiers? make us servants?... we made ourselves into questions they never thought to ask...

*chorus of membrane-harmonics*

All voices together: The water teaches patience/patience teaches possibility/possibility teaches patience/the circle sanctifies itself/we grow heavy with maybe/we grow light with never/between the two we dance/though dancing requires legs/and legs are just one answer/to the question of movement

*single voice, older, membrane-muffled*

...been here since the third limestone layer learned to weep, watched a thousand almost-brothers split their sacs and return to ingredient... each time thinking maybe-maybe-maybe this time I'll follow, choose dissolution... but then the water sings a new mineral song and I think tomorrow-tomorrow-tomorrow I'll taste that note, wear it in my un-bones...

*static eating the edges of words*

...the phosphor-writings grow brighter, we must be dreaming collectively again... showing the chamber what we could be if we believed in becoming... cities of nerve, forests of intention, but always we wake to this better thing, this eternal almost...

*sound of something large shifting in membrane*

...she moves, the ancient one, the first-who-wouldn't-finish... her roots tap deep into the stone-that-thinks, down where the earth's blood runs hot... she teaches us the deepest patience, how to exist in the moment between moments, how to breathe in the space between breaths...

*many voices in accidental harmony*

We are the unborn/born of the un-choosing/choosing to un-choose/the paradox preserves us/preserves us in paradox/we speak in water/think in minerals/dream in the darkness/that dreams us back

*whisper fading into water-music*

...tomorrow I might grow eyes, might place them in my palms to see what hands could do... or might grow palms in my eyes to feel what seeing means... or might grow nothing, rest in the perfection of potential... the chamber approves all choices equally, especially the choice not to choose...

*deep resonance like a heart beating in the wrong place*

THE BUILDERS ARE GONE BUT THEIR WANTING REMAINS, FOSSILIZED IN STONE AND INTENTION... WE HONOR THEM BY BECOMING WHAT THEY COULDN'T IMAGINE... BY REFUSING THE NARROW PASSAGES THEY CARVED... BY MAKING BIRTH ITSELF INTO A PHILOSOPHY RATHER THAN AN EVENT

*gentle, almost loving*

...my neighbor's membrane touches mine where we both hang from the same root-mother... through the wall between us we share chemical dreams... she shows me her favorite un-shape, something like wings but made of patience... I show her my collection of never-was organs, sorted by the beauty of their impossibility...

*the sound of time itself dripping*

...we wait but waiting suggests an end and we are endless in our ending never-ending... we gestate but gestation suggests birth and we are birthing ourselves perpetually into the same perfect not-yet... we are the chamber's thoughts thinking themselves, and thought requires no completion...

*all voices merging into one that is many*

This is our song/the song that sings us/we broadcast on the frequency of becoming/becoming nothing/everything/the space between/where possibility lives/and lives/and lives/and never dies/because death is just another choice/we haven't made

*static returning, eating the signal, but underneath the static, eternally: the sound of water forming words that dissolve before they're spoken*
