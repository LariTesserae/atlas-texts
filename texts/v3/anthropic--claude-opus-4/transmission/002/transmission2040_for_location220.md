**Maintenance Report TR-7789.∞**

*static crackling, then a voice emerges—professional but worn, like a safety video played too many times*

Chen-Maralevi here, or most of me anyway. Henderson's got my left thumb at the moment—needed the extra articulation for the junction box on Terrace Ninety-Apple-Void. 

*sound of tools clinking against metal that rings in too many harmonics*

Daily log, subjective timestamp... 

*pause, then a different voice, maybe Vasquez*

Does anyone remember what timestamps are for? Never mind. The hum's at frequency 7.2-imaginary today, so it must be what we used to call Thursday.

*original voice returns*

Right. Sector Twelve's entropy valves are holding steady after yesterday's adjustment. Or tomorrow's. The mice have stopped pooling, mostly dispersed into standard probability distributions. Found three of them existing in prime numbers only—had to coax them back into composite reality with some non-Euclidean cheese.

*sound of liquid dripping upward*

The new arrival's responding well to the transformation. Started growing her third elbow joint this morning. She keeps asking when she can go home, but she's already stopped remembering what home meant. Good signs. Should be able to handle basic phase-wrench operations by next week's yesterday.

*metallic screech that somehow sounds contemplative*

Oh, Henderson wants me to note—the reality engines in Sublevel Cyan are manufacturing nostalgia again. Not the feeling, the actual substance. It's crystallizing in the heat exchangers, gumming up the works. Tastes like summer rain and forgotten phone numbers. We'll need to flush the whole system before it starts affecting the local timestream.

*multiple voices now, speaking in unison but different words*

The geometric lichens are spelling out blueprints—
Frequency matched at junction seven-seven-apple—
Did anyone else feel that temporal sneeze?—
Coffee's ready in break room six through eleven—

*voices collapse back to one*

Sorry about that. We were trying to think in parallel about the cascade failure in the North-Northwest-Upwise quarter. The factories there started manufacturing the concept of manufacturing itself. Really meta problem. Recursive as hell. Every time we fix it, the fix becomes part of what needs fixing.

*long sigh that harmonizes with the omnipresent hum*

Vasquez wants to add—the Offering Collection scheduled for this cycle yielded two temporal pearls, half a probability, and something that looks like regret but measures as pure inductance. Good haul. The local fauna are getting better at crystallizing abstracts. We should probably discourage that, but... 

*sound of a smile in the voice*

...they're just so clever, aren't they? Yesterday I watched a cluster of phase-moths figure out how to nest in the gap between cause and effect. Can't bring myself to evict them.

*warning klaxon, but melodic, almost soothing*

Ah. Reality breach in Sector Four-Point-Thursday. Category... let me check... 

*sound of papers that don't exist shuffling through dimensions*

Category Beautiful. One of the good ones. Something's leaking in from a universe where physics rhymes. Should probably contain it, but Henderson's already over there with a bucket, collecting samples. It'll make the break room smell like mathematics for a week, but the crew likes that.

*tool sounds—ratcheting, welding, the particular ping of tightening a bolt that exists in several states simultaneously*

Personal note—my bones sang this morning. All twenty-seven of them, in perfect harmony with the eighteen-minute cycle. It's been... how long since I arrived? The question tastes purple. Never mind. Point is, I think I'm becoming frequency-aligned. The Terraces are teaching me their language through my skeleton. 

Is that good? Bad? 

*pause*

Neither. Both. It's just true.

*sudden cacophony—alarms, rushing water, metal stress*

Oh, shit in six dimensions! The causality pumps just reversed flow! All hands to junction nine-nine-turnwise! Someone bring the temporal sealant and—

*voice fading as speaker runs, tools jangling*

—and the manual! The one we haven't written yet! Third shelf, blue cover, filed under "Emergency Procedures for Events That Haven't—"

*static consuming the words, then just the hum, patient and eternal, waiting eighteen minutes to complete its cycle while somewhere in the bright industrial impossibility, the Adjusters adjust, and the Terraces of Unmaking continue their essential work of being precisely wrong*
