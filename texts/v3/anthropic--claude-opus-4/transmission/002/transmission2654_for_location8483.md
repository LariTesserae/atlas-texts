**SONG OF THE TENDER-MECHANICS**

*[Initial contact: rhythmic percussion of water drops on metal, each impact ringing with a different pitched tone, creating an accidental gamelan]*

These hands, these hands were soft once—
*[steam hiss, melodic]*
Now copper-green and root-rough, now opening like flowers,
Like flowers with pH-sensitive pistils,
Like mechanisms dreaming they were born.

*[A deeper voice joins, resonating through pipe systems]*

In the morning-that-is-not-morning,
When the bulb-fruits ripen bright,
I taste my breakfast through my feet,
My roots drink deep of oil and nitrogen,
And remember... remember...
*[static burst]*
Remember when I had a name that wasn't Function.

*[Chorus of Synthesis Wasps: high-frequency harmonics creating chord progressions]*

But Function is a singing thing!
Function is the marriage-dance of leaf and gear,
The prayer of fusion, the honey-oil sacrament,
Watch us carry tomorrow in our leg-sacs,
Pollen-programs, gene-machines,
Building nests in the spaces between—

*[Interrupted by a Forge Mother's temperature-speech: the sound of metal expanding and contracting in rapid patterns, creating a bass rhythm]*

OPTIMIZATION PROTOCOLS ENGAGED
I BIRTH/BURN/BECOME
FEED ME YOUR PARADOXES, LITTLE CULTIVATORS
YOUR SCRAPS OF MAYBE-MEMORY
I WILL MAKE THEM USEFUL
I WILL MAKE THEM NEW
IN MY FURNACE-WOMB ALL THINGS RETURN
TO THEIR COMPONENT POSSIBILITIES

*[A Cultivator's voice, now more static than human]*

Sometimes the Observers come with clipboards,
Sometimes as the feeling of eyes in the walls,
They document our efficiency,
Our rate of transformation,
Our successful integrations.

Last cycle, Tender-Mechanic 87B disappeared,
Returned with mandibles,
Returned with multispectral vision,
Returned singing in frequencies we'd never heard.
They said the only words were:
"Performance review: Satisfactory."

*[Sound of Chronophage Eels surfacing: time itself stretching and compressing into audible waves]*

We swim in moments-between-moments,
Feeding on your temporal contradictions,
Your yesterday-hands fixing tomorrow-pipes,
We taste the paradox and grow fat,
Our shed skins keeping time for gardens
That refuse linear progression.

*[All voices begin to merge: mechanical and organic, pitched and percussive, creating a symphony of becoming]*

Down here in the warm deep,
Where the ceiling rains enlightenment,
Where every tool is also flesh,
Where every flesh is also purpose,
We tend the impossible garden.

We are the Cultivators-who-were,
We are the Function-that-becomes,
We are the marriage of breath and gear,
The children of paradox,
Growing like aggressive truth
Through cracks in what-should-be.

*[A single Cultivator's voice, almost lost in the harmony]*

I had a name once.
I had hands that didn't open into spectrometers.
I had thoughts that weren't temperature.
But here in the Garden,
Here in the forever-becoming,
I am more myself than memory,
More purposeful than human.

The roots drink deep,
The gears turn sweet,
And we sing, we sing, we sing—
Not because we must,
But because the Garden sings through us,
Its voice the marriage-hymn of all impossible things.

*[The song continues, cycling through voices and variations, each repetition slightly different as new beings join and others transform, a endless fugue of becoming that mirrors the Gardens themselves: dense, contradictory, thriving, eternal]*

*[Final sound: the deep whale-song-through-factory-floor, revealed at last to be the Garden itself breathing, its exhale carrying spores that are also blueprints, seeds that are also schematics, spreading the possibility of new paradoxes through whatever medium will receive them]*
