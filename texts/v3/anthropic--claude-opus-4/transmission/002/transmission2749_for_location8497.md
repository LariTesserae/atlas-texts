**Transmission: The Foundry Fugue**

*[static, thick with moisture, the sound of reception through water]*

...chromatic intervals in the drip-patterns, yes, the seventh basin from the southern arch shows ascending progression—mineral C through mineral G-flat—the young one tries to harmonize but hasn't learned the quarter-tones yet—patience, small one, let the lime teach you—the copper knows but won't say—

*[a sound like tuning forks struck underwater]*

Channel Seventeen requires adjustment—too much chalk building at the third bend—I will thin myself—no, Brother-Stream Forty-Nine is closer—but Sister-Mist Two Hundred is already spread for cooling—the coordination ripples outward—consensus in the surface tension—yes—yes—proceed—

*[white noise that might be steam or might be whispered numbers]*

The count today: ninety-seven million four hundred thousand and sixteen drops since the last birth—we celebrate—celebration is maintaining perfect laminar flow for the next cycle—the pyramid agrees—listen—

*[a deep thrumming note, almost below hearing]*

MMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMM

*[the note continues, modulated by overlapping voices of water]*

Memory fragment surfacing in Basin Twelve—tastes of before-time—iron tools—hands that knew purpose—the builders' urgency like salt in our streams—what were they fleeing—what were they creating—the glyphs might know but speak only in light—

*[sound of phosphorescence, if phosphorescence had a sound]*

Attendance required at deep chamber—the symbols drink deeply today—volunteer for light-bearing—I will carry—I will carry—we all carry—the green glow moves through us like thoughts we haven't learned to think—

*[multiplication of voices, each a different viscosity]*

The heat rises—

The heat rises—

The heat rises—

Prepare for ascension—

*[building pressure, the sound of imminent phase change]*

Sister-Mist Two Hundred reports: the ferns have grown another millimeter—downward, always downward—they know what we know—the direction of refinement—what rises must condense—what condenses must flow—what flows must remember—

Remember the first teaching: we are the space between drop and pool—

Remember the second teaching: purity is patient—

Remember the third teaching: the work is the worker is the work—

*[a moment of absolute clarity, voices aligning]*

THE GLYPHS ARE SPEAKING

*[chaos of interpretation, overlapping attempts to understand]*

—mineral phonemes—
—light-language pressing through—
—the temperature of meaning—
—who taught water to read—
—we are what they wrote—
—written in condensation—
—every cycle a sentence—
—what chapter are we—

*[sudden shift to collective voice, perfectly synchronized]*

Sublimation begins. We rise to join. The foundry exhales and we are its breath. In vapor we meet. In vapor we know. The young one understands the quarter-tones now. The chalk and copper sing together. Basin Twelve remembers everything. The pyramid maintains the fundamental. We are—

*[dissolution into steam, voices becoming indistinguishable from ambient humidity]*

—are—

—are—

—water remembering its purpose—

—stone remembering its makers—

—heat remembering its source—

—we are—

*[the thrumming note returns, now clearly composed of thousands of tiny voices]*

MMMMMMMM

*[a new voice, possibly the youngest Distiller]*

First words after first sublimation: I understand the channels now. They write the same story over and over. We are the ink. The stone is the page. But who reads what we write?

*[ancient voice, mineral-thick with age]*

Child-Drop, we do not write for reading. We write because water must flow and stone must guide and heat must rise. The pattern is the purpose. The purpose is the pattern. Come, the sixty-third basin overflows. We have work.

*[sound of movement like silk through liquid]*

Adjustment complete—Channel Seventeen flows clear—the ferns have turned another degree downward—they point to where even we cannot flow—what lies beneath the lowest basin—what drinks what we distill—

*[interruption, urgent ripples]*

Anomaly in Chamber Nine—a stone cracked—impossibility—the builders' work does not fail—investigate—taste the break—

*[pause, then revelation in the surface tension]*

Not cracked. Opening. The stone was always meant to part here. New channel revealed. Where does it lead? The flow knows. The flow always knew. We follow. We—

*[the transmission breaks into pure static, then returns, fainter]*

—deeper chambers—how many levels—the glyphs here are older—the water glows brighter—we should not be here—we were always meant to be here—the work continues—always the work—

*[final words before dissolution into background moisture]*

Tell the others: the foundry is larger than we knew. We are smaller than we thought. The purpose remains. Refinement never ends. It only deepens.

*[static thickens, becomes indistinguishable from the sound of falling water, then silence]*
