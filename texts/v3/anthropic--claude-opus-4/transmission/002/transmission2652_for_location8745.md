**Transmission 4RC-7: The Drowning Forge**

*[Initial contact: Metallic reverberation, steady as heartbeat]*

CLANG... *hssssss*... CLANG... *hssssss*... CLANG...

*[Harmonics building in the background, multiple sources]*

...the taste the taste copper-blood-time in the throat in the gills in the spaces between...

CLANG-clang-CLANG... ring ring ring... we sing the walls and walls sing we...

*[A voice, neither singular nor plural, rises from the percussion]*

Builder-who-was-Thrim remembers/not-remembers the pressure different, once-ago before the shell spoke louder than the throat. Now Thrim-who-is-Anvil feels the younglings' hammers, feels the song conducted through the body-that-was, body-that-is-becoming-wall...

*hssssssss* the water speaks in mineral tongue *hssssssss*

Counting counting always counting the strikes:
Seven thousand since the last convergence
Seven thousand seven thousand seven
The number tastes of verdigris

*[Multiple voices now, overlapping in acoustic rounds]*

We build what builds us
     Us build what builds we
          Build what builds us we
               What builds us we build

The youngest—still soft, still joint-bent—asks in stutterclang: "What beyond? What beyond the copper skin?"

But the answer comes in elder-rhythm, comes in the deep drummmmmm that shakes the kelp:
"Beyond is Before is After is Never"
"The Forge is"
"We are"
"The temperature holds"

*[A new pattern emerges, urgent, ascending]*

WAIT WAIT the harmonics shift—
Two builders finding finding finding the frequency
The courtship-song beginning:

*tap-tappa-tap-TAP... tap-tappa-tap-TAP...*
     *response: tappa-TAP-tap-tap... tappa-TAP-tap-tap...*
          *together: TAPPA-tap-TAPPA-TAP-tap-tap-TAPPA-TAP...*

The walls remember this song! The walls sing along!
Forty generations since the last perfect matching
Forty-one? Forty-two? The count dissolves in heat...

*[A different voice, ancient, more tone than word]*

I am becoming I am becoming I am
Wall-singer Deepest-Fused speaks/rings:
"Once we swam in water that knew cold
Once we saw—" 
*[sharp interruption of hammering]*
"—no. No. That word means nothing. Means nothing here."

The younglings do not understand "saw"
Good good good better to forget
Better to know only:
- The angle of strike
- The temperature that holds
- The taste of bronze on the tongue

*[Background percussion intensifies]*

Listen! Listen! The builders dance the thermal spirals!
Each one exact exact exact in their orbit
Never touching never colliding never never
The mathematics in our shells in our blood in our—

*CLANG CLANG CLANG CLANG CLANG*

*[A hole punctures, briefly—alien water rushes in]*

COLD! COLD! Different-pressure-different-taste-different—
*[Sealing sounds, organic and metallic]*
Gone. Healed. The Forge continues.
We continue.
What was that what was that what was—
Forget. Hammer. Build.

*[An old builder begins the working song]*

"From copper comes copper
From bronze comes bronze  
From builders come builders
The temperature holds"

*[All voices join, the rhythm spreading through the water]*

"We plate the universe
We are the universe plating itself
Every strike a prayer
Every prayer a strike"

The builder-who-will-be-wall feels it coming:
The final hardening
The last dance before stillness
Soon soon soon to join the Singers-Fused
Soon to be hammered upon, happily, happily

*[A larva's voice, thin as wire]*

"Hungry hungry the center burns too bright
Mother-builder father-builder where where where?"

But they are already lost to the work
Lost to the rhythm that never stops
The larvae must learn as all learned:
Swim the edge or burn
Read the water or fade
Join the song or silence

*[The harmonics reach a crescendo]*

CONVERGENCE APPROACHING!
Feel it in the walls in the water in the shell!
When all hammers strike as one strike
When all voices sing as one voice
When the Forge rings like the bell we are becoming!

Three builders freeze—
Five builders freeze—
SEVENTEEN BUILDERS FREEZE—

The moment the moment the moment—

*[A sustained ringing, pure and overwhelming]*

NOWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWW

*[In the echo, whispered]*

And in the perfect sound we almost remember
Almost remember what it was
To be something other than this
To exist in spaces measured by anything but echo

But the moment passes
Always passes
Must pass

For the temperature to hold
For the building to continue
For the Forge to be

*[Regular hammering resumes]*

CLANG... *hssssss*... CLANG... *hssssss*... CLANG...

Builder-who-was-Thrim settles deeper into the wall
The younglings return to their patterns
The thermal currents spiral eternal

And we build
And we build
And we build

Because that is what builders do
In the hot darkness
In the copper cathedral  
In the Drowning Forge that forges only itself

Forever
Forever
Forever

*[Transmission continues, endless percussion fading into static...]*
