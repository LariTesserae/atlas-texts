**Chlorophyll Murmur Station**

[static like rain on leaves, then a deep thrumming—the sound of sap moving through massive trunks]

...witness the fourth small tendril since morning-flood, eastern face of Grandfather Weeping, two hands above the moss-line where the copper beetles nest... growth rate suggests... [crackling, as if through water] ...adjusting the training wires by half a thumb-width, maybe less... 

[a different voice, slower, with long spaces between words]

The dream-spores are thick today. Can taste them even through the filter-moss. Chenradi's youngest came up from the roots speaking of silver schools moving wrong-wise, against the tide-pull. Says the fish are spelling something in their patterns but she's too young-green to read it...

[overlapping transmissions, like multiple conversations through vegetation]

...increased transpiration in Sector Seventh-Trunk, the cooling leaves are overproducing again...
...dead-socket in the bridge-root, whole span might go if we don't...
...my grandmother's symbiotes are flowering, she says it means...
...pressure building in the lower chambers, the tide-singers should...

[A rhythmic clicking sound—the news-beat pattern that precedes important announcements]

Growth-watch reminds all: Tomorrow marks small-blooming of the Whisper Districts. Pollen-drunk behavior will not be excused in the work-zones. Secure your breathing-moss tonight. The memory-keepers have prepared the usual dream-catchers for those who wish to preserve their visions. Standard rates apply—two thumb-lengths of spider silk or equivalent trade...

[water sounds, something large moving through flooded roots]

...manatee are early this season, moving up into the drinking-root district... means the salt-line is shifting again... someone needs to tell the root-workers before they... [sudden splash, startled chittering] ...ah, they know, they know, always know before we do...

[a child's voice, high and clear]

Teacher says the city thinks in centuries but I can hear it thinking right now, in the wall-wood next to my sleep-hole, tiny thoughts like termite-whispers, quick-quick-quick, planning where to grow next, I put my ear against it and...

[adult voice cutting in]

Chenradi, leave the child-channels to the children. This frequency is for growth-work only.

[sullen silence, then the original voice returns, patient as moss]

...marking seventeen new knots in the memory-vine. The growth proceeds. Always proceeds. Even in the dying-back, proceeds. Tomorrow the Grandfather Weeping will be one day taller, the bridge-roots one night stronger. I will tend. We will tend. The city will breathe and we will breathe with it.

[a new voice, urgent, speaking in the rapid pattern of emergency]

Breakage on the Third Canopy Walk—storm-damage worse than thought—whole branch going soft, people still on it—need all trunk-architects now—NOW—bring the growth-stimulant, the strong mix—

[rushing sounds, voices calling distances, then fading]

...she's stabilizing... good, good... sing to her while I work the roots... everyone BACK, let her breathe...

[calm returns, the ever-present drip of water, rustle of leaves]

Night-shift beginning in the under-dark. Tide turns in three songs. Root-workers prepare. The city drinks deep tonight. We go down to help her swallow.

[a harmony of voices, wordless, matching the rhythm of moving water]

[transmission degrades into the sound of rain, insects, distant splash of swimming, something large breathing in darkness]

...phosphor-bloom starting early in the gallery district... means the moth-messages will fly tonight... prepare your letters if you have them... the living ink takes time to set...

[voice growing distant, merging with the ambient whisper of ten million leaves]

...in the breathing, we are breathed... in the growing, we are grown... tomorrow's city dreams tonight in the wooden bones... we tend, we tend, we tend...

[static like sap bubbling, like spores floating, like the city's vast wooden lungs expanding and contracting in time with the tide]

[transmission fades but never quite ends—the background rustle continues, punctuated by drops of water, scrape of climbing feet, the occasional hollow boom of a trunk adjusting to new weight, voices too distant to understand but never absent, never truly silent, the city breathing, breathing, breathing]
