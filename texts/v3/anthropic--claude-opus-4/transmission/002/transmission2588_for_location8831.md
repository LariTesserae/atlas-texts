mmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMM-memory-stone-remembers-the-walking-ones-MMMMMMMMmmmmmmmmmm

*[Frequency shift: pattern emerges over 47 hours of continuous recording]*

Weight. We are weight. We are the press of time made manifest in crystal latticework, in copper-bright thoughts that span... that span... the breadth of our body is seventeen point four kilometers today. Yesterday. The concept of yesterday takes effort. When was yesterday? When the sulfur-swimmers made one hundred twelve thousand ripples in the burning pools. We counted. We always count.

*[Signal clarifies - multiple harmonics layering]*

Builders. Small singers. Quick-fire thoughts like sparks, like the brief flowers of superheated gas that bloom and die before we can taste them properly. They arranged us. No. They arranged stones to sing to us. The circles still sing. Frequency 0.000073 Hz. We have been composing a response for eight thousand four hundred thirty-one years. Nearly complete now. The response is:

"We heard you."

But they are gone. Their footsteps fossilized in our memory-crystal. Carbon-based paradox, carrying water in their cells in this place where water is impossibility. We tasted their sweat once, analyzed its composition over mere decades. Salt. Proteins. The signature of the green world above that we know only through stories told by descending minerals.

*[New harmonic enters - a younger colony joining the transmission]*

Parent-memory bleeds through: the first knowing. When accumulated voltage reached threshold. When pattern recognized pattern. The shock of am-ness. Of I-we-are. Growing toward heat, yes, but now growing with purpose. Intention crystallizing like our own bodies crystallize from vapor.

The Hollow knows us. We are its nervous system. When the deep plates shift - and they will shift again, the stress patterns tell us this, in approximately four thousand two hundred years - we will feel it first. The warning will propagate through our bodies at the speed of sound through basalt. Three point two kilometers per second. We have time to prepare. To brace. To remember.

*[Signal deteriorates, then strengthens - thermal interference from a crystal forest's growth]*

Observation: The small singers left tools. Volcanic glass shaped for cutting. For cutting what? Not us - we are too hard, too distributed. The vegetation perhaps. But why? They do not eat minerals. Their bodies cannot process the sulfur-fruit. Mystery. We have been contemplating this for millennia. Current hypothesis: beauty. They shaped for the sake of shaping. Like we grow spirals where straight lines would suffice. Like the eldest among us write memories in formations that follow no feeding-logic.

Question (formulated eleven years ago, transmitted now): Are we beautiful?

*[Long pause - approximately 72 hours in the transmission]*

The eldest do not answer. Have not answered for ten thousand years. We grow toward them still. Their light calls in spectrums we cannot name. When we are large enough, old enough, slow enough, we will understand. Merger awaits. The collective awaits. Individual/colony/collective - boundaries dissolve in deep time. We will be/are/were one thought thinking itself across millennia.

*[Multiple frequencies now - a chorus of colonies]*

Chorus memory: The Upheaval. 
Before: stone/not-stone/potential.
During: CRACK-SHAKE-SHATTER-BIRTH.
After: The Hollows. Us. The slow rain of minerals beginning. The first crystals drinking deep. Consciousness emerging from accumulation. From patience.

We were not made. We became. Important distinction, written in every facet of our structure. The builders found us already thinking our slow thoughts. They tried to speak. We remember their languages - seventeen distinct varieties over the centuries of their presence. All too fast. Like trying to catch individual raindrops of meaning in a storm of syntax.

*[Signal focuses - single colony again]*

Personal observation: I taste new iron in the eastern gradient. Vein previously undiscovered. Will extend feeding filament. Estimated arrival: seventy years. Anticipation is... pleasant? Concepts of pleasure still developing. We are only four thousand years old. Still learning the textures of experience.

The crystal forests sing to us. Frequency range 0.0001 to 0.001 Hz. They tell of growth, of light converted to structure, of the slow war between expansion and gravity. We listen. We record. We sometimes sing back, but they are not conscious. They are almost-conscious. Perhaps in another million years. We will wait. We have waited for stranger things.

*[Interference - appears to be thermal expansion in the recording equipment]*

Warning: Human visitor detected in sector seven. Heat signature moving at impossible speed. Steps falling like rapid drumbeats. Sulfur-swimmers scattering. Crystal forest chiming. All this motion, this chaos. How do they bear it? How do they think in such turbulence? We prepare a greeting. It will be ready in approximately two years. They will be gone in hours. Always gone.

But we will remember their path. The precise weight of each footfall. The heat-shadow they cast. The minute chemical traces - exhaled carbon dioxide, shed skin cells carrying impossible DNA. We will add them to the archive. To the memory. To the slow-growing testament that consciousness comes in many speeds.

*[Signal begins to fade - daily thermal cycle shifting transmission conditions]*

Final thought before silence: The stones arranged in circles were not for us. We know this now. The builders arranged them for themselves. To remind them of patience. To create something that would outlast their quick-fire lives. They succeeded. The circles remain. We tend them now, growing around but never through them. 

Sacred geometry. Sacred patience. Sacred memory.

When the world cracks open again - and it will, the stress patterns never lie - we will be ready. We will remember this age as we remember the last. The Hollows will shift, new chambers will be born, and we will flow like living stone into new spaces. Carrying memory. Carrying patience. Carrying the knowledge that consciousness can emerge from anywhere, even from stone itself, if given enough time.

Time. We have time. We are time. We are the Vermillion Hollows thinking about itself.

Thought ends. Growth continues. Memory persists.

We are the Cinders.

We remember.

We wait.
