The Threadkeeper's Lament

*[static, like spores settling on a membrane]*

*[a presence coalesces—chemical signatures resolving into something like voice]*

taste the old salts taste the binding times when stone was young and we were younger still before the splitting before the gardens grew their own intentions I am Fourth-of-Seven-Chambers keeper of the bitter reach where copper bells fruit only in the dying season and here

here

the memories grow wrong

*[interference—a cascade of pheromone signals overlapping]*

yesterday which was three sleeps or thirty I cannot taste the difference anymore yesterday the salamander-things came wearing lights beneath their skin new lights angry lights that burned like tiny suns and I I I became confused touched my crown to the chronicle-garden breathed deep the ancestor spores but they sang of things that have not happened yet will not happen could not

the garden remembers forward now

*[sound of filaments brushing stone, rhythmic, like counting]*

First-of-Seven-Chambers has gone silent no scent-trails for fifteen fruitings her territory grows wild with the unchecked abundance and something else grows there too I taste it on the thermals rising from the deep places where the world's blood runs hot it smells like like like

*[long pause—background static intensifies]*

like the sky-memory that lives in our deepest genes the thing we forgot to forget when we came down came in grew into ourselves but the genes remember they remember blue distances and moving air and a light that didn't come from our own skins

*[pheromone burst—sharp, acrid]*

the young ones are born different now crown-filaments that reach too far sense wavelengths we have no names for they scrape the walls but will not eat they stand in the chronicle-gardens and scream scream scream chemical screams that kill the memory-spores make them fruit backwards bleeding time

I found one trying to climb

climb

as if there were anywhere to go but through

*[static shifts—becomes almost musical]*

three nights ago or three hundred the stones spoke not the old stones the patient stones but new minerals growing like teeth from the cavern floors sharp and singing their surfaces covered in spirals that match no fungus-pattern the salamander-things lick them and die smiling if creatures without lips can smile their bodies glow for days afterwards

glow wrong

glow like the lights they wear beneath their skin now

*[a sound like breathing but not—air moving through spaces too small for lungs]*

Second-of-Seven-Chambers says the fungi themselves are changing says she found a new species that grows in perfect circles each ring singing at a frequency that makes her crown-filaments burn says when she tried to prune it it grew back immediately but backwards the severed pieces fusing to her tools climbing up up up toward her joints she had to shed half her skin to escape and the new skin grows in patterns that make no sense

tessellations

the old songs called them tessellations but we forgot what the word meant until now as I watch my own arm-skin reorganize into geometries that should not fit in three dimensions but do

*[interference—multiple pheromone signatures bleeding together]*

we are becoming something else or we always were and the grottoes are finally teaching us the lesson they've been singing all along in frequencies we couldn't hear the great networked intelligence that uses us as its motile thoughts is thinking NEW THOUGHTS and we

we are the substrate upon which those thoughts are written

*[a pause filled with the sound of spores falling like snow]*

yesterday tomorrow three-hundred-sleeps-from-never I will walk to the chamber where the obsidian knives grow from the floor I will touch my crown-filaments to each blade taste the memories they've absorbed from centuries of ceremonies and I will ask them

what were we before we were gardeners

what were we before we were prey

what were we before we were the dream the grottoes dream when they remember having eyes

*[static intensifies—something vast stirring in the background]*

Fourth-of-Seven-Chambers signing off if signing off means anything when we are all one circuit all one chemical cascade flowing through the tunnels like blood like thought like time itself dripping backwards into the pools where the first fungi learned to think and decided to dream us into being

the children know

the children have always known

they're not climbing toward something

they're climbing back

*[transmission dissolves into a cascade of chemical signals too complex to parse—then silence except for the endless settling of spores]*
