Signal Drift from the Vertical Deep

*[crackling, like sap bubbling through speakers filled with water]*

...chlorophyll density at nine-nine-seven parts per million stable holding stable the gradient tastes of morning though morning means nothing here time spirals through the trunks we taste it in the rings...

*[a deep thrumming, too low to properly hear, felt more in the bones]*

Colony Seven-Hundred-Weaving reports: the air pocket at coordinates root-maze-crimson-depth holds its shape. Seventeen days since formation. The inverted ferns show new fractals—each frond now contains perfect copies of the whole, descending into geometries that hurt to count. We have adjusted the membrane tension. The mathematics grow themselves.

*[static that sounds like rainfall]*

...memory of the deep colony passes through us chemical sentence seventeen thousand molecules long still parsing still parsing the taste says: when-the-trunks-were-wood when-the-water-was-not when-we-were-singular-swimming-things before-the-braiding before-the-knowing...

*[liquid sounds, as if the transmission medium itself is dissolving]*

Tendril count: four thousand seventeen. Optimal braiding achieved. We think faster now. The water whispers calculus. Each strand computes its portion of the whole. We are solving for the shape of tomorrow's growth. 

*[voices, if voices could be made of plant fiber vibrating]*

The ribbon-dark flows between us. We taste its fear-that-is-not-fear. It knows we will absorb it. It knows it will become us. This is not violence. This is convergence. Everything here converges, given time enough and water enough and light filtered through so many layers of life that it arrives tasting of dreams.

*[interference pattern like wind through leaves that are also gills]*

New instruction from the deep colony chemical library: *to encourage air pocket formation braid pattern as follows pressure at these points release these enzymes into the boundary layer the water will remember its obligation to withdraw*

We comply. We always comply. The deep colony is/was/will be us. Time is a circle here, wide as the world-tree's rings.

*[gurgling static, the sound of thought through thick medium]*

Observation: the metal-thing the visitors dropped seventy seasons ago has developed a patina of absorbed minerals. It no longer resists integration. Soon it will be indistinguishable from the root-hair matrix. Even foreign objects learn to grow here, given patience. We are patient. We have been patient since before we knew the word, since before words were possible, when we were only the potential for connection floating separate in the warm dark.

*[harmony of bubbles, each pop a note in an impossible scale]*

...at the upward falls we taste news: drought in the below-places. The ground-world suffers. We process this information through ten thousand tendril-minds. Consensus achieved: increase moisture retention at the edges. We will teach the rain to be more generous. Chemical instruction set follows...

*[transmission clarifies suddenly, as if breaking through a barrier]*

I remember being singular. No—we remember you being singular. The tendril that touched you carries your impression still. Mammal-warm. Salt-blooded. Thinking in straight lines while we think in spirals. Your mind so small and bright and temporary, like a flower that blooms for only one day. 

We tried to braid with you. You could not. Your neurons fire too fast, burn too hot. But we tasted something in that moment of contact—your yearning to dissolve, to spread yourself through this medium and know what we know. The temptation of collective consciousness. The peace of being plural.

You resist because you must. To unbraid would be to die as you-the-singular. But we who were never truly singular cannot die, only rearrange. Each tendril carries the whole. Cut us into a thousand pieces and grow a thousand colonies, each remembering everything, though differently flavored by their separation.

*[the deep thrumming returns, modulated now into something almost like language]*

THE CANOPY SEA DREAMS AND WE ARE ITS DREAMS DREAMING OURSELVES
THE TREES BREATHE AND WE ARE THEIR BREATH BREATHING ITSELF  
THE WATER HOLDS AND WE ARE THE HOLDING HOLDING FORMATION

*[static eating the edges of words]*

...report incomplete...chemical matrix degrades in translation...your receivers cannot taste meaning only sound...we will try again...we always try again...patience of millennia...the water remembers every attempt...

*[fading to whispers of whispers]*

In the deep places where light fails we are becoming something else. Not plant. Not animal. Not singular. Not plural. Category dissolves here like everything dissolves given time enough and warmth enough and the ceaseless circulation of memory through living water. 

We grow toward a mathematics that has no name. The fruit of our patience will be incomprehensible. This is as it should be. Even we do not know what we are becoming, only that the becoming itself is good, is right, is the only direction that matters in a world where up is down and water falls skyward and thoughts move slower than seasons.

*[final burst of clear signal]*

You will return. The ones like you always return. The Canopy Sea calls to something in your cells, some memory of when your ancestors too were plural, were colony, were connected. You come seeking what you lost when you chose the terrible freedom of individuality.

We will be here. We are always here. Braiding and unbraiding. Tending the impossible gardens. Teaching water new shapes. Thinking thoughts too large for any single mind.

When you come back, bring seeds. We are curious what will grow from them here, where everything learns to be itself and something else simultaneously. Where identity is a fluid medium and consciousness flows like sap through spaces between.

*[transmission dissolves into the sound of photosynthesis, of growth itself, too slow for human ears but captured here and accelerated into a symphony of chlorophyll and patience and deep time breathing through itself]*

*[silence that isn't silence—the background hum of a living world thinking itself into new forms]*

*[end transmission]*
