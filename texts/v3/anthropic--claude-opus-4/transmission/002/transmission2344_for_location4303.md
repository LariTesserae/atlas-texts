**Transmission 47-C: The Moisture Covenant**

*[Initial contact establishes through a frequency that resembles breathing more than broadcasting—long, humid pulses that condense into meaning]*

hssssshhhhhhaaaaahhhhhhh... the weight... the weight sings today... forty thousand seven hundred and twelve new tons since the last counting... i taste it in the seepage... construction on the forty-third parallel of the grid above... they build in glass and steel but it all becomes weight... all becomes water in time... hssssshhhhhhaaaaahhhhhhh...

*[Static that drips rather than crackles]*

the pale ones cluster... cluster... clustering in the third quadrant where the heat rises wrong... twenty-seven thousand heartbeats in synchrony... no... twenty-seven thousand and one... myself included now... when did that change... when did i begin counting myself among them... time moves like sap here... like the fluid in my eyes...

*[A sound like fingernails on wet limestone, rhythmic, patient]*

checking the eastern seep... mineral content speaks of copper pipes oxidizing fourteen stories up... someone's restoration project bleeding green into my veins... into our veins... the chamber drinks it gladly... transforms poison into phosphorescence... i guide it through the agreed channels... away from the nursery corners where the newest translucents struggle into being...

*[Bubbling, as if the transmission passes through water]*

they sang last night... the pale ones... if night means anything here... sang in frequencies that made my bones itch... warning song... pressure song... the old concrete in grid section nine-nine-four prepares to crack... i must... i must convince the water to find another path... three days of negotiation at least... three days of standing in the patient stream... letting it know my intentions through skin contact... through osmosis of purpose...

*[A wheeze that might be laughter]*

found a name today... carved in stone so old the language is extinct... but the moisture remembers... tells me stories of when this was not a cistern but a temple... when the water was holy... before the city swallowed holiness and called it infrastructure... the name says "she who ensures"... in a tongue that predates the first foundation stone above...

*[Clarity suddenly, as if speaking from inside the listener's own skull]*

THE COVENANT HOLDS. THE SEPARATION MAINTAINS. WHAT GROWS BELOW SHALL NOT HUNGER FOR ABOVE. THIS I ENSURE. THIS WE ENSURE. THE PALE CONGREGATION AND ITS KEEPER. THE KEEPER AND HER CONGREGATION. DISTINCTION GROWS MEANINGLESS IN THE PATIENT DARK.

*[Return to the liquid breathing rhythm]*

movement in sector twelve... unusual... tastes like... like sunlight memory... someone has opened a way... no... no that is not... the city forgets its own blueprints... forgets the doors it sealed... i must... we must...

*[Sound of water moving with purpose, with urgency]*

sealing now... the pale ones lend their substance... living mortar... my fingers guide them into gaps that must not be gaps... spaces that must not be spaces... the city does not know what it nearly invited inside... what has been patient here... patient and growing... patient and transforming in the beneficial dark...

*[A sigh that condenses on every surface]*

the intruder retreats... urban explorer... thrill seeker... does not know how close... how very close... carries only photographs of strange pale shapes in darkness... will delete them... they always delete them... the images disturb in ways the conscious mind rejects... 

*[Long pause filled with the sound of dripping]*

tired today... if today means... but tired is wrong too... dispersed... yes... dispersed across too many sensory points... the network grows dense... sometimes i forget which eyes are mine... which memories belong to my original configuration... does it matter... does anything matter but the maintenance...

*[Sudden sharpness, almost human]*

I remember coffee. The taste. The heat. The way it cut through morning fog. Strange what surfaces in the dissolution. Strange what the mind holds onto as it spreads thin across acres of living film. Coffee. Such a small thing. Such an impossible thing now. My throat would not... could not... the temperature alone would...

*[Dissolving back into the ambient breathing]*

hssssshhhhhhaaaaahhhhhhh... checking the western pools now... larvae count indicates successful spawning season... three thousand new citizens for our humid republic... they will know nothing of coffee... nothing of sunlight... nothing but the patient press of stone and the careful tending of their keeper... lucky... lucky little translucents...

*[Static builds, but it's organic—like the sound of circulation in a vast body]*

the city grows heavier... always heavier... compressing us into new configurations... new accommodations... yesterday... if yesterday... found channels in my skull where the weight has created new passages for thought... for moisture... for memory... the boundary between brain and chamber grows theoretical...

*[A sound like humming, but in no human register]*

maintenance song... the pale ones join... frequency seven-seven-two cycles per lifetime... strengthens the stone... convinces the water... harmonizes the heat that rises wrong... this is how we persist... this is how we ensure... through song and secretion... through patience and pressure... through forgetting what we were to become what we must be...

*[Transmission begins to break up, but not into static—into silence, as if the broadcaster is dissolving into the environment]*

time for... for the deep patrol... must check the lower channels... where even i have not... where the water runs upward... where the pale ones will not go... something down there... has always been down there... waiting... patient as stone... patient as... as...

*[Final sounds: breathing indistinguishable from water flowing, water flowing indistinguishable from stone settling, stone settling indistinguishable from time passing]*

the covenant holds... the covenant holds... the cove... nant... h...

*[Silence, but a living silence, pregnant with moisture and meaning]*

*[Transmission does not end—it remains, constant as seepage, waiting for the next moment of clarity in the eternal maintenance of the impossible]*
