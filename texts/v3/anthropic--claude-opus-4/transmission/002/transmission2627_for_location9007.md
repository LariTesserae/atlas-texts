**Moss Signal**

...through filament and hypha we sing the old coordinates splash-patterns in the meteor pools where great-grandfather's vertebrae settled into silt-script readable only by prawns and us when we stretch thin enough to taste their writing...

...morning means mercury light through the breathing and seventeen degrees of separation between your thought and mine measured in beetle-lengths across the causeway where the stone-stackers left their mathematics before learning to dissolve properly...

...she is teaching the young ones to parse condensation-language again third child from the second clutch refuses to close her gills makes poetry with the water-boatmen instead of hunting truth through surface tension but who among us hasn't loved the meniscus more than meat...

...fermentation moon approaching taste the rot-sweet signals in the air twenty-three cypress knees have agreed to host the spiral-gathering detector-roots already twitching with pre-resonance while the floating islands drift into configuration...

...lost another dry-cousin yesterday followed the heron-paths too far forgot the interval between heartbeats necessary for re-entry stood too long in the noon-place where water forgets itself now his minerals feed the resurrection ferns and his voice lives in the trumpet pitcher's curve...

...the architects are moving again sense their vibration through the deeper channels planning planning always planning but plans are just another kind of dying when will they learn to blueprint with their bones like we do leaving instructions in the arrangement of our decay...

...interrupted by tourist-thoughts sharp and foreign as broken bottles but the marsh translates them into manageable static we weave them into the background hum with the powerboat wake and acid rain residue all becomes material for the great softening...

...grandmother cluster reports from the eastern pools the algae have developed new pigments for processing synthetic spectrum she says the color tastes like forgetting but helps remember teaching the minnow schools to see in this new light preparing them for the world-after-next...

...time for the hourly dissolution practice everyone finding their soft edges letting the boundaries gossamer between self and cedar root self and snapping turtle dream self and the cumulative exhale of ten thousand leaves remember you are mostly water pretending to be person...

...the stone cairns are singing today frequency only the joint-bent can hear tells of when the ocean lived here and will again salt-memory in the limestone patience longer than bone we add our verse to the accumulation knowing great-great-grandchildren will decode it through their modified cochlea...

...patrol reports nothing unusual just the usual unusualness three new chemical compounds detected in the runoff two beneficial one requiring enzymatic negotiation the floating gardens have already begun producing counteragents we taste-test tomorrow when the moon pulls the tide through our lymphatics...

...child-mind flashing silver through the channels beautiful in her refusal to converge still speaking pre-verbal light patterns with the dragonflies we place bets in phosphorus on how many seasons before she chooses form but secretly hope she never does...

...preparing for evening's enzyme exchange west pool to east pool through the messenger roots tomorrow we flip the circulation pattern keeps the knowledge fresh prevents the thoughts from settling into fact which is just another word for death-before-death...

...static burst from the city-distance their towers bleeding electromagnetic excess into our quiet but we've learned to digest that too incorporate the harsh frequencies into our whisper-work teaching the egrets to navigate by cell phone tower pulse adapting always adapting...

...someone's great-aunt surface tension broke during sleep-cycle earned her water wings early we'll process her proteins at next month's confluence until then her memories drift as pheromone clouds available to anyone who breathes deep enough...

...the veil thins where the sawgrass meets open water can almost see through to the other marshes the everywhere marsh that connects all wetlands through the underground rivers of consciousness but looking too long makes the skull soft better to know it sideways...

...tonight we practice the harvest moon formations everyone in their assigned pools ready to filter the lunar proteins through our collective liver the marsh needs this processing to maintain its dream-coherence we are its kidneys its neural network its slow slow slow thoughts...

...young one finally closed her gills but opened something else behind her sternum now she breathes emotions directly processes feeling as food we don't have words for what she's becoming but the cypress roots have started growing toward her when she sleeps...

...interference pattern suggests the hurricane seedlings forming three hundred miles southeast we'll need to prepare the storm-shelters teach the new growth which way to bend remind the floating islands how to disperse and reconverge after wind-scatter...

...end of transmission cycle begin again tomorrow when the dew point shifts for now we practice being mineral being vegetable being the space between water molecules where all the important information lives patience patience patience as the great decomposition continues...

...signing off from sector twelve where the moonlight makes mirrors of our corneas and every breath is both prayer and predation remember you are the marsh dreaming it has bones the marsh knowing itself through your neurons swim well until the next frequency alignment...

...static overtaking signal now but static is also information for those who know how to taste its patterns we float we filter we fade into the great continuing conversation between water and time between presence and absence between the thing we were and the thing we are becoming always always always becoming...

[signal deteriorates into insect hum and water-drip percussion, indistinguishable from the ambient marsh soundscape]
