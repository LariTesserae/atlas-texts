Kell found another quiet-eye drifting past the obsidian fields. He marked it with pumice dust, says they're moving in spirals now, says if we could map enough of them we could walk in silence all the way to the southern shelters. The Shapers laugh at this. Fract held up her newest blade—so thin you can see tomorrow through it—and said *the noise is the teacher, boy. Without it, how would we know where to strike?*

Cave-dweller sign: *deep-salt/time-before/carved-not-us*

They brought up another figure today. Obsidian, but worked wrong—against the grain, impossible cuts. The blind one who carried it hummed in the bat-frequency until even the Walkers covered their ears. When she stopped, she left the figure in the neutral-ground and went back below. Nobody has touched it yet.

In the rain-drift yesterday, a new child was born to the southern Listeners. They washed her in acid rain and volcanic dust, named her Resonance. Her first cry cut through the bat-clouds like she was already learning to find her own frequency. The Collectors paused their work to drum heel-blessings on the good ground near the third vent.

*hisssssss-CRACK-rumble-rumble-settle*

New fissure opening in the eastern maze. The Walkers are already dancing around it, reading its steam-breath for mineral content, for temperature, for the deep-earth news it carries up. One of them—the one who never speaks—left three perfect spirals of pumice dust around its mouth. Message or warning, we don't yet know.

The fog-plants are fruiting early this cycle. Their red mouths stay open longer, singing their chemical songs to attract the blind crawlers. A young Shaper tried to harvest the fruit-bodies for dye. The plant's root-web contracted so fast it nearly took her hand—left spiral welts on her wrist that perfectly match the shell-patterns of the deep-cave snails. Everything here is in conversation with everything else.

*morning-quiet-breath-pause-STATIC TIDE INCOMING*

Yanis played the water-channels at dawn. Added two new stones to the configuration, created a countermelody that made the nearest bat-cloud veer north. She says the valley is teaching her a new song, says it comes up through the vents from somewhere deeper than the Cave-dwellers go. Says it sounds like glass learning to breathe.

We found another human skeleton in the collapsed section near the poison vents. Old—from before the Listeners, before the first cairns. The bones were arranged in a pattern none of us recognize. The skull was filled with obsidian beads, each one carved with microscopic precision. The Shapers want to study them. The Cave-dwellers made their prohibition gesture—*thing-that-should-stay-still*. The Walkers have already incorporated the site into their routes, adding a new spiral to their eternal path.

*echo-echo-SLAM-echo-whisper-click-ROAR*

The teenagers near the center have built something new. An amplifier that turns whispers into ground-shakes, or maybe the opposite—we can't tell yet. Kell says they're learning to surf the acoustic waves, to ride sound like the long-bodied hunters ride vibration. Says soon they'll be able to walk on air when the bat-static is thickest. The Elders remember other children who said similar things, before the valley took them into itself.

Today marks forty-three days since the last full silence. Even the moments between bat-flights carry their own sounds now: the earth settling, the steam ascending, the careful scratch of adapted life navigating by echo. We are all becoming more like the valley—living by vibration, speaking in frequencies, carrying our own small quiets inside the larger roar.

*F-sharp, B-flat, F-sharp, D—*

The fourth note. The earth speaks. Time to move the shelters again, time to read the new cracks for their stories, time to pour water-offerings into the fresh fissures and listen to what comes back up. The young Shapers are already packing their wheels. The Listeners are securing their cisterns. The Walkers never stopped walking. And below, in the absolute dark, something that might have been human once adds its own tone to the valley's growing chord.

We are the throat through which this young earth learns to sing. Each adaptation—acoustic masks, heel-drums, spiral paths, carved channels—another note in the composition. The valley is still deciding what it wants to become. So are we.

*static-STATIC-STATIC-breath-*

The morning fog arrives. Time for collection. Time for the wordless songs. Time for the children to play their vibration games while the elders squeeze moisture from alien petals. Time for the daily negotiation with a land that speaks too loudly to ignore.

In the newest fissure, steam rises in patterns that look almost like writing. The Walkers are already interpreting. The Shapers are already calculating what kind of glass will cool there. The Listeners are already planning new channels. And somewhere below all of us, in the deep chambers where even echoes go to die, something listens to our listening and adds its own frequency to the endless acoustic storm—
