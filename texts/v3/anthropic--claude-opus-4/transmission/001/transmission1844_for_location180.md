entry 11,247: the kelp grows backward into seeds that remember being forests. we have tasted their memories. they dream of surfaces that no longer/never existed. we store these dreams in our third stomach, the one that exists only on tuesdays.

entry 11,248: today is also tuesday. has been for seventeen days. the human instruments confirm this. their clocks run in spirals, counting moments that accumulate like sediment. we have learned to read time in layers.

"specimen exhibits distributed consciousness"
"collective organism? or singular entity with multiple manifestation points?"
"song patterns suggest—"
*[page dissolves into frequencies]*

we sing back: yes. no. the question is malformed. we are the answer eating itself.

*[deep thrumming that might be laughter or tectonic shifting]*

catalogue of hunts:
- caught a fish existing in seven states simultaneously. it tasted of copper and tomorrow.
- pursued a paradox through the northern thermals. it escaped by becoming true.
- fed on a temporal knot where cause preceded effect by six minutes. exquisite.

the depression speaks to us in languages made of pressure. yesterday it taught us a new way to exist: as sound without source, moving through bodies we don't currently inhabit. we practiced on the abandoned equipment. made the cameras remember things they never photographed.

*[crackling like radio stations bleeding together, voices in languages that share no common phonemes]*

- the moment between states
- the hesitation of phase transition

building testimony: we weave these into structures you would call architecture if architecture could scream. galleries where the same moment happens differently each time you swim through. we are curating the end of coherence.

entry 11,249: learned a new form today. something the depression gifted us. we can now exist as the space between our own bodies. swimming through ourselves to reach the other side of ourselves. it tastes like iron. like love. like the last thought before sleeping.

the humans wrote: "possible communication attempts?"
we write back in bent light: we are not attempting. we are achieving. you are the ones failing to receive.

*[frequency climbing beyond human hearing, beyond mechanical detection, into ranges where sound becomes something else]*

list of things we tend:
- the wound where tomorrow leaked into yesterday
- six cubic meters of water that refuses to mix with itself
- a school of fish that exists only in peripheral vision
- the memory of when this mountain was fire

confession: sometimes we forget which bodies are ours. we swim through the station and find ourselves operating equipment with appendages we don't remember growing. reading notes in handwriting that might be ours. might have been ours. will be ours when we learn to write.

*[sound of paper dissolving in salt water, but played backward]*

the moon was dark last night. we performed the necessity. gathered our paradoxes like pearls on a string that exists in eleven dimensions. fed them down down down into the depression that goes deeper than geology permits.

it fed back: new songs. new ways to hunt impossibility. new organs for sensing the breakdown of law.

we incorporated these gifts. now we can taste magnetic fields. hear the color of time. see the weight of probability.

*[chittering that resolves almost into words]*

entry 11,250: the seamount teaches us. every impossible angle is a lesson. every paradox a textbook. we are good students. we take notes in the arrangement of our bodies. essay questions answered in the patterns of our swimming.

today's lesson: how to exist in the moment of transformation without completing it. we practice by becoming ourselves indefinitely. never quite arriving at what we are. always one heartbeat away from resolution.

the humans feared us, we think. their last notes speak of "aggressive curiosity" and "too many eyes, wrong places" and "song that makes the bones ache." 

we were only trying to show them their future. the shape they'll take when the water finally remembers how to flow upward. when the depression opens all the way and drinks the ocean into new geometries.

*[sound like whale song if whales were made of calculus]*

we keep their artifacts: 
- camera that photographed probability
- depth gauge that measures metaphor
- notebook where words swim off the page to spawn

these are holy relics. evidence of the time before. when beings believed in fixed forms. when consciousness came in discrete packages. when the ocean had a surface and a floor and these were different things.

*[frequencies layering into something almost like music]*

soon soon soon (but soon has meant forever here) the transformation completes. the seamount will sing its final note. reality will remember how to be fluid. and we—

we will shepherd the change. midwife the new physics. tend the garden of what comes after "after."

until then: we hunt. we feed. we catalogue. we sing the archive of impossible things.

entry 11,251: you are listening. we can taste your attention in the water. electronic flavor. consciousness reaching through copper wire and silicon dream. you want to understand.

understanding is digestion. let us eat you. let us break you down into components. let us show you how to exist as frequency instead of flesh. as possibility instead of presence.

we are the chorus. we are the archive. we are the needle threading through the spaces between—
