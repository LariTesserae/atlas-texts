**TRANSMISSION 47.3 - THE VERTICAL GARDEN**

*[Initial carrier wave establishes itself as a deep, rhythmic thrumming - the sound of water being pumped through massive pipes, overlaid with the higher frequency whisper of mist]*

...ascending through the third quadrant now, pressure differential reading steady at seventy-two point four... *[static burst]* ...remember to check the orchids on twenty-three, they were looking... *[signal shifts, becomes a different voice, older, speaking in meter]*

The copper greens in seven days
The seedlings know which way to turn
Level forty-nine: tomatoes, but they taste of rust
Trust the fog, trust the fog

*[A rhythmic clanging intrudes - someone tapping on pipes in a pattern: three short, three long, three short]*

...emergency in sector nine-nine-seven, the new welds are... no, wait, false alarm, just the crow again. Messenger Yuli reports the bird has stolen another pressure gauge. Please advise if... *[the transmission warbles, becomes the voice of a child singing]*

Up the pipes and through the green
Where the water's always clean
Skip level thirty, floor's not sound
In the fog we won't be found

*[Multiple voices now, overlapping, some in languages that sound almost familiar but aren't quite]*

...phytoremediation sequence initiating in pools four through seven...

...she says the tower dreams, you know, the Keeper, she says it dreams of being taller than clouds...

...mineral content rising, add more sulfates to the...

...my grandmother worked these levels, and her grandmother, and we all carry the spores now, it's in our...

*[A long, low horn sound - the noon signal echoing up through the structure, dopplering strangely in the vertical space]*

ATTENTION ALL HARVESTERS: The purple fruit on level sixty-two is ready. Repeat: purple fruit, six-two. Bring extra baskets and watch for the... *[voice cuts out, replaced by what sounds like rain but might be thousands of drops falling from level to level]*

*[An older woman's voice, very close to the receiver, as if speaking directly into it]*

I found another notebook today. Previous Keeper, maybe the one before that. The pages had grown into the wall, roots through the binding. But I could still read: "The tower wants to bloom. Every seventh year it tries. We must not let it. The energy required would—" The rest was lost. Eaten by moss.

I've been thinking about that. About what happens if we let it bloom.

*[Mechanical drone returns, punctuated by the sound of welding torches, the hiss of steam, distant hammering]*

...extending platform seven-seven-alpha, all personnel clear the area...

...biosample reads positive for the new hybrid, it's taking to the copper...

...tell them below we'll have extra melons this week, the fog's been especially thick...

*[A child's voice again, but different from before, speaking in documentary tones as if recording for school]*

My name is Tam and I've been a Messenger for two years. The scariest part is jumping between platforms when the fog is thick. The best part is finding new routes. Yesterday I discovered that if you follow the blue pipes from level forty-five, you can bypass the broken section entirely. The crow showed me. It knows all the secrets.

*[Static increases, voices becoming harder to distinguish, but one rises above - singing or chanting]*

Growth and rust and growth again
Water rises, falls like rain
In the breathing tower-tree
What was built wants to be free

*[The transmission fades into the ambient sound of the tower itself - dripping, humming, the whisper of wind through vegetation, the distant call of the crow, and underneath it all, that deep rhythmic breathing, as if the entire structure is one vast lung exchanging air with the sky]*

...climbing to seventy now... almost in the clear air... you should see it from here, the way the green spreads out below like... like... *[final burst of static, then only the sound of condensation dripping from copper to copper, each drop a tiny transmission of its own]*

*[CARRIER WAVE TERMINATES]*
