Where the terrarium's keeper maintains perfect stasis, the Cultivator revels in controlled chaos—engineering new lifeforms that blur the boundaries between flora and fauna.

Unlike the terrarium's metallic precision, the Cultivation Chambers embrace organic architecture—walls that breathe, floors that yield underfoot like moss, ceilings that drip nutritive solutions in careful patterns. Inside, something massive gestates—the next iteration of the ecosystem itself, a living landscape that will eventually be birthed into its own space, connected to the network through the same vascular channels that feed the terrarium below.
