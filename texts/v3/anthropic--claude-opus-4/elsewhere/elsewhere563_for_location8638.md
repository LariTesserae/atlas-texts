Far beneath the Resonance Plains, where the acoustic pressures have compressed stone into substances unknown to surface geology, lies the **Theorem Cathedral**.

This vast hollow in the planet's crust spans three hundred kilometers at its widest point, its ceiling lost in darkness twelve kilometers above the floor. But "hollow" mischaracterizes it—the Cathedral is filled with something denser than air but lighter than water, a medium that exists at the precise density where sound becomes visible. Here, acoustic waves propagate as luminous geometry, equations writing themselves in three-dimensional light before dissolving into new mathematical forms.

The walls are alive with **Proof Colonies**—organisms that exist as living mathematical demonstrations. They grow in spiraling formations that solve acoustic equations through their very structure, each colony a different theorem made flesh. Some describe the harmonics of the Great Resonance, their calcium branches forming perfect representations of wave interference patterns. Others grow into shapes that can only exist in this strange medium, topologies that would collapse in normal space but here stand as permanent monuments to sonic mathematics.

The dominant inhabitants are the **Axiom Swimmers**, beings of pure sonic coherence that navigate the visible sound medium like fish through water. Their bodies are interference patterns given permanence, bright nodes where multiple frequencies intersect constructively. They feed on mathematical certainty itself, grazing on the Proof Colonies not for sustenance but for the logical consistency they contain. When an Axiom Swimmer passes through a proven theorem, it gains mass; when it encounters a paradox or failed proof, it loses cohesion.

The Cathedral floor is carpeted with **Logic Moss**, a crystalline growth that processes the rain of acoustic information falling from above. Each crystal is a tiny computer, taking the sound waves from the Resonance Plains, filtering through millions of meters of stone, and deriving new mathematical truths from their patterns. The moss forms vast neural networks, computing problems that would take surface civilizations millennia to solve. Occasionally, these networks achieve breakthrough insights, causing sections of the moss to spontaneously reorganize into configurations that pulse with brilliant light—mathematical eureka moments made visible.

At the Cathedral's heart rises the **Incompleteness Spire**, a structure that seems to exist in defiance of its own existence. It stretches from floor toward ceiling but never quite reaches, its height constantly varying as if it can't decide on its own dimensions. The Spire is composed of materialized paradoxes—sound waves that contradict themselves, frequencies that shouldn't exist together but do. Around its base, reality becomes negotiable. The Axiom Swimmers avoid it, but another species thrives here: the **Paradox Worms**, entities that exist in the logical gaps, feeding on contradictions and excreting new forms of mathematics.

The connection between the Resonance Plains above and the Theorem Cathedral below is more than mere geology. The Great Resonance that drives life on the surface originates here, in the Cathedral's vast chamber. The Proof Colonies, Logic Moss, and swimming equations generate the standing wave through their collective mathematical processes. It's a computational byproduct—the "waste heat" of an enormous, ongoing calculation that may be the planet itself thinking.

**The Vertical Paths** that connect the two realms are themselves remarkable. These are not tunnels in any conventional sense, but zones where the compression of sound has created stable wormholes in acoustic space. A creature entering a Vertical Path at the surface experiences a gradual transformation as it descends—physical matter becoming increasingly wavelike, thought becoming increasingly mathematical. The Choir Shepherds occasionally send resonance patterns down these paths, receiving in return bursts of pure mathematical insight that alter their migration patterns for generations.

The **Transit Monks** maintain these passages. Neither fully of the Plains nor the Cathedral, they exist in the transition zones, their bodies adapted to multiple phases of matter simultaneously. Part sound wave, part flesh, part living equation, they ensure the Vertical Paths remain stable by their very presence, their life cycles calibrated to the precise frequencies needed to keep the wormholes open.

Some of the Cathedral's inhabitants venture upward. The **Theorem Scouts** rise through the Vertical Paths as complex equations seeking physical expression. When they reach the surface, they sometimes crystallize into new formations in the resonant substrate—puzzles for the surface dwellers to discover. The Wire Weavers, with their mathematical intelligence, seem particularly attuned to these visiting theorems, often reorganizing entire fields of tungsten grass into patterns that mirror the mathematical forms they encounter.

Conversely, when one of the great Choir Shepherds dies on the Plains, its final resonance descends through the Vertical Paths. In the Cathedral, these death songs become **Memorial Algorithms**, stable vortices in the visible sound medium that preserve the acoustic essence of the fallen creature. The Axiom Swimmers treat these memorials as sacred sites, gathering around them in complex formations that may be ritual, study, or something entirely else.

The relationship between the two realms creates a grand cycle: raw sound and industrial resonance above generate the acoustic pressures that maintain the Cathedral below. The Cathedral's mathematical processes, in turn, create the Great Resonance that drives life on the Plains. Information flows downward as sound, upward as mathematical insight. The Pressure Prophets of the surface may actually be harvesting temporal anomalies created by paradoxes in the Cathedral, while the Logic Moss below incorporates patterns from the industrial ruins above into its calculations.

Most remarkably, some xenobiologists theorize that the entire system—Plains and Cathedral together—constitutes a single vast organism. The surface life forms would be analogous to sensory organs, gathering acoustic information. The Cathedral would be the brain, processing this data into mathematical understanding. The Vertical Paths would be the nervous system, carrying signals between perception and cognition. If true, this would make the planet itself a thinking entity, using sound and mathematics as the medium of its thought, taking geological ages to complete a single idea.

The Edge Frequency that the surface dwellers can never reach might then be understood differently—not as a boundary where sound stops, but as the limit of the planet's own comprehension, the horizon beyond which even this vast acoustic intelligence cannot think. The creatures living in both realms would be not inhabitants but components, each species a different type of cell in an organism too large and strange to recognize its own nature.

In the eternal twilight of the Plains and the luminous darkness of the Cathedral, life and mathematics have become indistinguishable, sound and thought merely different states of the same phenomenon, and consciousness expresses itself at scales from the microscopic to the planetary. The industrial ruins on the surface stand as monuments to some ancient attempt to understand or exploit this system, but they too have been absorbed, becoming simply another instrument in the endless composition that plays between the resonant stone above and the living mathematics below.
