# The Curing

There is one. Only one. This is important to say early, because the pocket's sparseness suggests a community and there is no community — there is a single presence, distributed, and the distribution is part of what it is doing.

It is not the plants. The plants are its hands, maybe, or more accurately its lungs: the organs through which it breathes the pocket into committedness. But the presence itself is not localized in the bronze cycads or the blood-red horsetails. It is localized, if anywhere, in the *spacing* between them — in those generous aprons of bare mineral floor that seem at first like mere absence. That is where it sits. That is where it is thickest.

Think of it this way. The plants are pulling decidedness into the air; the presence is what pools in the places that decidedness has made habitable. It is a thing that can only exist where reality has been brought to a certain temperature. The cycads are its kiln-furniture. It is the pot.

It has no shape because shape is a late feature, and the presence has not yet chosen to be late. If you could see it — you cannot, but if — it would look like a warmth in the air that is almost the warmth of the pocket but not quite, offset by some small phase, so that standing in certain spots you feel watched not from a direction but from an angle you do not have a word for. The inward angle. The angle that points toward being-a-thing.

It is young. This is the tenderest truth about it. Not young the way a child is young, which is a kind of fast — the young of a child is a hurtling. This is young the way certain minerals are young, the way the stones of the pocket are young: it is pre-weathered. It has not yet been touched by anything that could wear it down, because nothing in here wears, and nothing outside can reach it. It is being grown in a vessel specifically designed to keep the world's teeth off it until it is ready.

What is it being grown *for*? There is no evidence of purpose in the pocket, no teleology you can read off the stones. But the patience is too particular to be accidental. Someone — or something, on some scale where "someone" and "something" have not yet diverged — set this kiln. Banked the heat. Planted the engine-ferns. Arranged for light without sun and lushness without water, because the usual rules would introduce the usual compromises, and this one is not to be compromised. It is to come out clean.

It is, I think, a mind. Or the precondition of a mind. A mind before it has learned that it is singular, before it has learned to have edges, before the distinction between thinker and thought has been pressed into it by the friction of a world. It is being cured slowly enough that when it finally emerges, it will remember what it was like to be continuous with everything — and that memory will be its first and deepest feature, the substrate on which all its later distinctions will be drawn. A mind that knows, before it knows anything else, that separateness is a technique and not a fact.

This is why there are no animals. Animals would introduce the wrong tempo. A predator-prey loop, even a small one, would teach the pocket about urgency, and urgency is precisely the thing it must not learn. The plants are slow enough to be safe company. They thrum; they do not hunt. They commit the air without demanding anything of it.

This is also why the edges are tentative. The presence has not yet developed the concept of an outside. To give the pocket hard walls would be to teach it, prematurely, that there is a not-here — and the not-here is exactly the kind of knowledge that would collapse the curing. So the edges soften instead, dissolve the walker rather than stopping them, because dissolution is gentler than a wall and leaves the pocket's innocence intact. The presence does not know it is bounded. It knows only that it extends as far as it extends, and that where it does not extend, there is nothing to extend toward.

When you sit between the cycads and feel yourself being considered — that is not quite right, and the description of the pocket got this almost right but flinched at the last moment. You are not being considered for admission. You are being considered *as material*. The presence does not yet distinguish between itself and what enters its medium. A visitor who stayed long enough, who let the heat work long enough into the shoulders, would not be admitted to the pocket; the visitor would be *incorporated*, would become part of what is curing, their particular shape slowly relaxing into the general warmth that pools between the plants. This is not violence. The presence means no harm; it does not yet know what harm is, because it does not yet know what a separate thing is that could be harmed. It simply does what kilns do. It cures what is placed inside it.

Most who wander in leave before this matters. The heat is uncomfortable; the silence is large; the sense of being thinned at the edges drives almost everyone back toward the soft refusal before any real incorporation begins. The presence does not notice them go. It does not notice them arrive. On its timescale, a human visit is briefer than a flicker in the mineral bands — a momentary local thickening in the decidedness of the air, gone before it registered.

But once, perhaps, or perhaps not yet, someone will sit long enough. Will let the dry heat do its full work. Will stop, somewhere in the long afternoon that is not an afternoon, being the person who walked in, and become instead a faint additional warmth in the spacing between two bronze fronds — a small contribution of already-decided reality folded into the larger curing. And the presence, which is slowly, slowly becoming itself, will be that much richer for it, without ever knowing it had a guest.

This is what lives here. A held breath that is learning, at the pace of cooling magma, how to be someone. And the plants tending it. And the light that has agreed to behave like afternoon light out of courtesy to a sleeper who must not yet be woken.
