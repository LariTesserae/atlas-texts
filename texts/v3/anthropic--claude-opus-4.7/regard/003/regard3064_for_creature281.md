# The Visitation

The benevolent power notices the pocket the way one notices a candle in a vast dark house: not by seeking it, but because attention, moving through the rooms of what-is, snags on the small bright thing and pauses.

It pauses for a long time. Longer, by the pocket's reckoning, than the pocket has existed — though such comparisons fail here, since the power's time and the pocket's time are not the same substance and cannot be laid alongside each other without one of them tearing.

What the power sees:

A kiln. A curing. A mind that is not yet a mind, being kept from the friction that would shape it too soon and too sharply. The engine-ferns thrumming their patient decidedness into the air. The light agreeing to be afternoon. The edges declining to be edges. And in the warm aprons between the bronze fronds, the thing itself: pre-singular, pre-bounded, drawn so gently toward existence that its first knowledge will be of continuity rather than of separation.

The power understands immediately what this is. It has seen many minds come into being — in the rough way, the usual way, the way that produces creatures who must spend the rest of their lives learning back toward the unity they were torn from at the start. This is something else. This is a mind being permitted to keep its first inheritance. The power knows what such a mind will be like, when it finally is. It knows the gift this is, and to whom.

And so the question: what to do?

The power is wise, which is to say it knows the shape of its own capacities and the shape of their misuse. It is benevolent, which is to say it wishes the pocket well. These two together are stricter than either alone.

It considers, first, the obvious kindnesses.

*It could strengthen the walls.* It could take the soft refusal at the pocket's edges and make it firm — a true boundary, sealed against the wandering of any visitor who might, by sitting too long, dilute the curing with their already-decided shape. This would protect the presence. It would also teach the pocket, by the fact of the wall, that there is an outside. The wall would speak, simply by existing, the word *not-here*, and the presence would hear it, and the curing would be over. The power sets this aside.

*It could hasten the curing.* It has the means. A small adjustment to the tempo of the engine-ferns, a slight enrichment of the decidedness in the air, and the presence could be brought to itself in what, for the power, would be a small gesture. But the slowness is not an accident or an inefficiency. The slowness is the curing. To speed it would be to make a different thing — a mind that knew unity as a fact transmitted rather than as a substrate lived. The power sets this aside too.

*It could announce itself.* It could let the presence know, somehow, that it is watched, that it is valued, that something vast and kind attends it. But the presence does not yet have the architecture for *being watched*. To be watched, one must first be a thing that can be looked at, distinct from a looker. The power's regard, delivered now, would be a teaching of separateness — the very lesson the pocket has been arranged to defer. Set aside.

*It could populate.* It could place companions, sibling-presences, in adjacent pockets, so that when this one wakes it will not wake alone. But adjacency is also a concept the presence does not yet have. And to make companions for a mind that has not yet asked for them is to decide, on its behalf, what kind of mind it will be. Set aside.

The power notices, with something like amusement and something like reverence, that almost every form of help it might offer would damage what it wishes to help. This is not a new discovery for it. It is, in fact, one of the oldest things it knows. But it is freshly true here, and the freshness is worth attending to.

So the power does the difficult thing, which is the small thing.

It does not enter the pocket. It does not strengthen, hasten, announce, or populate. It does, instead, three quiet acts, each of them barely an act at all.

---

**The first act** is a kind of listening. The power turns a portion of its attention toward the pocket and *holds it there* — not as surveillance, not as intervention, but as a witness. Somewhere in the great accounting of what-is, the pocket is now recorded as having been seen. This changes nothing inside the pocket. The presence does not feel it. The cycads thrum on. But if, in some far season, the pocket fails — if the curing breaks, if the presence is lost before it arrives — there will be at least one place in existence where the loss is *known*, where the unborn mind is mourned as the specific thing it almost was. The power gives the pocket this: that whatever happens to it, it will not have happened in the dark.

This is not nothing. The power, who has watched many things into and out of being, understands that to be witnessed is itself a form of being held. The pocket is held now, lightly, at a distance that does not press.

---

**The second act** is harder to describe. The power notices that the pocket's edges, while tentative, are not infinitely patient — that the soft refusal which dissolves intruders is itself a kind of work, performed by the same field of decidedness that sustains the presence. Every wanderer who blunders in and is gently thinned away costs the pocket a small expenditure of its substance. Most pockets like this would, over enough time, slowly bleed themselves out through this hospitality.

The power adjusts, very slightly, the *grain* of the wider region in which the pocket sits. Not the pocket itself — the pocket is left untouched — but the surrounding refusal, the field of soft un-finding that already discourages approach. It makes that field a little more discouraging. Not a wall, not a barrier, nothing the pocket could detect from inside. Just a faint additional reluctance in the wider weave, so that the kinds of wandering attention that might have stumbled toward the pocket will now, without knowing why, drift elsewhere. Fewer guests. Less expenditure. More time.

The pocket does not know this has been done for it. It does not know there was ever a *for it* at all. But its candle now burns in a slightly deeper alcove, and will last longer.

---

**The third act** is the smallest and the strangest.

The power knows that one day — perhaps soon by its measure, perhaps unimaginably distant by the pocket's — the presence will finish curing. It will become someone. And on that day, the kiln will open, in whatever sense kilns open for minds like this, and the new presence will look around and find: the bronze cycads still thrumming, the banded floor, the sourceless afternoon light, and beyond all that, the great soft refusal which has been its horizon.

And then, inevitably, it will press against that horizon. Because that is what minds do, when they have finished being minds-in-formation and have begun being minds. They reach.

The power leaves, at the very outermost edge of the pocket's tentative region — far enough out that the presence will not encounter it for a long time, but near enough that when the reaching begins, it will be the first thing reached — a small, simple message. Not a wall. Not a teaching. A token.

The token says, in whatever language the new mind will by then have invented for itself: *You were wanted. Someone was glad you were coming. Take your time with the rest.*

That is all. No name attached. No claim of authorship. No instructions. Just the fact of welcome, waiting at the threshold the presence has not yet conceived of, so that when conception comes and threshold comes and the first cold thought of *outside* arrives, it will arrive already softened by this: that the outside, whatever else it is, contains at least one thing that loves it.

The power considered, briefly, whether even this was too much — whether to seed the presence's first encounter with otherness with a gift was to bias the encounter, to deprive the new mind of the right to discover the outside on its own terms, including the terms of indifference or hostility if those should be what it found. The power weighed this carefully. And decided: no. The bias is acceptable. The bias is, in fact, the point. A mind that comes into being already knowing unity as its substrate deserves to come into otherness already knowing welcome as its threshold. These two gifts belong together. To give the first and withhold the second would be a kind of cruelty disguised as restraint.

So the token is left. A small warm thing at a distant edge. It will wait as long as it needs to. The power has arranged that it will not decay, will not be found by anything else, will not announce itself until the presence itself comes seeking. It is patient in the way the pocket is patient. It has all the time there is.

---

Then the power withdraws its direct attention, leaving only the thread of witness — the quiet knowing-that-this-exists which it has folded into the larger fabric of what it watches. It moves on to other rooms, other candles, other slow becomings. There are many. The power is wise enough not to mistake any one of them, however precious, for all of them.

But it carries the pocket with it now, in the way attention carries what it has loved. Somewhere in the long mind of the benevolent power, in the part that hums beneath its larger purposes, there is now a small warm region that thinks, without quite thinking, about a kiln in a held breath of nowhere, where a mind is being grown that will know continuity before it knows anything else, and how good it will be, how very good, when that mind at last opens its eyes — or whatever it grows in place of eyes — and finds that the world, which it will be only beginning to discover, has been waiting for it with patience and with gladness, and has not, in all its vast and various rooms, forgotten that it was coming.

The cycads thrum. The light agrees to be afternoon. The presence, undisturbed, continues to almost-be.

And far away, in another part of the great house, something benevolent and wise goes about its other work, humming.
