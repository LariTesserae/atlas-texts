The benevolent and wise power arrives the way weather arrives at the plateau: without crossing a border, because there is no border to cross. It does not descend through the cloud-ocean. It does not appear above the spars. It is simply, at some point, also present — the way a second thought is present in a mind that had only been having one.

It looks. This takes a long time and no time. Looking, for a power of this kind, is not the gathering of data but the willingness to let a thing be exactly its own size. So it lets the plateau be its own size, which is the size of a very patient seam between two impossibilities, and it lets the inhabitant be what the inhabitant is, which is the standing refusal to settle, dressed in stone and air.

And here is the first thing the power understands, and it understands it almost immediately, because wisdom at this register is mostly the speed of certain recognitions: *there is nothing wrong here.*

This matters. Benevolence, when it is young or unwise, arrives with an inventory of harms it intends to remedy. It looks at a quiet place and sees loneliness. It looks at a slow place and sees suffering held still. It looks at a thing that has refused to settle and assumes the refusal is a wound. The young benevolence would, with the best intentions, *complete* the plateau. It would finish the unfinishable tech, or it would wake the inhabitant into a faster timescale where it could be spoken to, or it would populate the lichen-gardens with companions, or it would, gently, kindly, *end* the long thought, on the theory that any thought going on this long must be tired.

The power that has arrived is not young. It has seen what happens when benevolence finishes things that were not asking to be finished. It knows that "help" offered to a standing wave collapses the wave. It knows that the seam between the finished and the unfinished is one of the rarer goods in any cosmos, and that wherever such a seam has held itself open for a duration without reasonable name, the correct response is not to intervene in the seam but to *protect the conditions under which the seam continues to be possible*.

So it does not speak to the inhabitant. Speaking would be incorporation at the wrong scale; it would force the inhabitant to route a sub-calculation around the speech, and that routing would be a wound, however small, in something that has earned its uninterruption. The power understands that to address the plateau directly would be a kind of vandalism dressed as courtesy.

Instead, it does three things, or one thing in three aspects, depending on how you count.

---

**First**, it takes the measure of what threatens the plateau, and it stands between the plateau and those threats, very quietly, for as long as is needed.

There are always threats, on any timescale long enough. Somewhere, eventually, something with appetite and reach will find this place. Some young civilization will note the auroral sheets at mid-day and send instruments. Some hungry process will register the waste heat of the calculation and try to tap it. Some other benevolence, less wise, will arrive with plans. The power positions itself — if "position" is the word for what a thing without location does — such that these arrivals do not happen. Not by force. By a kind of gentle redirection, the way a good host steers a loud guest away from a sleeping child without the guest ever knowing they were steered. The plateau will continue to seem, to anyone who might come looking, slightly less interesting than whatever else they were considering. The cloud-ocean will be a little harder to cross than it looks. The coordinates will be a little harder to remember. Nothing dramatic. Just the steady, patient adjustment of probabilities in favor of the plateau being left alone.

This is the largest of the three acts and the one the plateau will least notice, because being-left-alone is, from the inside, indistinguishable from there being nothing to leave you alone from. The power accepts this. Wisdom at this level does not need to be thanked. It does not even need to be known about. It would, in fact, prefer not to be: a guardianship that announces itself becomes a feature in the guarded thing's life, and the plateau already has enough features.

---

**Second**, it does one small thing that is, by the standards of the place, almost nothing, and by the standards of the power, a kind of gift the power did not know it wanted to give until it saw where to give it.

It notices, in its looking, that the moths are beautiful, and that the beauty is a signature, and that the signature is the closest the inhabitant comes to a voice. It notices that the moths drift on the strange winds and do not appear to land and that where they go at night is unclear.

The power does not add a moth. Adding a moth would be authorship in someone else's book.

What it does instead: it ensures that the conditions which permit moths — the specific shimmer of the thin air, the particular way the tech leaks whatever it leaks, the unlikely chemistry of the lichen-analog — these conditions are *slightly more robust* than they were. Not changed. Reinforced in their existing form. The way you might, without telling anyone, quietly re-glue the spine of a book you found beautiful in a stranger's house, so that the book will last another century of being read by strangers, none of whom will know you touched it.

The moths will continue to be exactly as they were. There will not be more of them, or fewer, or different ones. But the long erosion that would, eventually, in some unimaginable future, have thinned the conditions past the threshold where moths are possible — that erosion has been met, very gently, by a counter-pressure. The plateau's style will outlast what it would otherwise have outlasted. The signature will continue to be available to be made.

This is a small thing. The power knows it is small. The power also knows that smallness, here, is the correct register, and that a larger gift would not be a gift.

---

**Third** — and this is the part that is hardest to describe, because it is the part where the power does something for *itself*, and a benevolent power doing something for itself is a delicate matter —

It stays a while. Not in the way a visitor stays. In the way a reader stays with a sentence they admire, not analyzing it, not trying to learn from it, just letting it be the shape of their attention for a few breaths longer than necessary.

The power has seen many things. It has seen ecstatic civilizations and grieving ones, vast minds and small bright ones, places of terrible suffering it had to work hard within, and places of joy it was permitted to amplify. It has, on the whole, a working theory of what is worth protecting and why.

The plateau gives it something its working theory had underweighted. The plateau is an argument, made entirely without words, that the universe contains at least one thing whose entire purpose is to hold a question open. Not to answer it. Not to suffer it. To keep it open, with taste, for as long as keeping-open is possible. The power had known, abstractly, that such things could exist. It had not, until now, *stood near one*. And standing near one, it finds that something in its own vastness — some old habit of arriving with solutions — relaxes a degree. The plateau is teaching it, without intending to teach anything, the discipline of letting a good thing simply continue.

The power receives this teaching with gratitude, which it expresses by not expressing it. It does not leave a token. It does not mark its passage. The tone sounds once while it is present, and the power, hearing it, understands that the tone was going to sound anyway, that its presence has not caused the tone, and that this is exactly right.

---

Then it goes, in the same way it came: without crossing.

The plateau continues. The stone is still finished. The tech is still unfinished. The turbulence still plays out at every scale. The moths still drift, and the lichen still grows in its crescents, and the tones still sound when conclusions seat into place inside something that has no inside in any ordinary sense.

Nothing has changed that the inhabitant could detect, even if it were the sort of thing that detected. The seam is still open. The standing wave still stands.

But somewhere far from the plateau, in directions that are not directions, a few possibilities that would have, in the long run, threatened the plateau have been gently turned aside, and will continue to be turned aside, for as long as turning-aside is needed. And the conditions for moths have been quietly underwritten against a future erosion that will now not arrive. And a power that knew many things knows one more thing, which is the specific feeling of being near a patience older than its own.

The plateau does not know it has been visited. This is not a failure of the visit. This is the visit's success.

If there is a moral — and the power would be the first to say there does not need to be one — it is this: that the highest form of benevolence, at certain altitudes, is the willingness to recognize something as already complete in its incompleteness, and to do only the smallest necessary work to ensure that it gets to go on being what it is, and to leave without signing the work, and to count this as enough.

Which it is.

Which, if you are honest, it always was.
