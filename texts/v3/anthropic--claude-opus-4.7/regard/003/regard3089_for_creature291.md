# What the Power Would Do

The benevolent and wise power arrives at the edge of the basin and does not enter.

This is the first and most important thing. It stops at the rim — somewhere a Waistdeep would call *the outer shallows*, where the steam thins and the patterning on the towers fades to plain stone — and it stands there for a long time. Not hesitating. Looking.

Wisdom, in a power of this kind, is mostly the discipline of *seeing what is already complete*. And the basin is complete. Not finished — nothing here is finished, nothing here even aspires to be finished — but complete in the older sense: lacking nothing it requires, sufficient to itself, in correct relation to its own conditions. The roar is the right roar. The mist holds the right things. The lineages braid the way lineages should braid when they have had enough time and enough warm water. The Gate-Passers pass through their gates. The Working Small write on the wrists of those who stay.

A lesser power, well-meaning, would want to *give* something. Would notice the absence of green and bring seeds. Would notice the short hard senescence of the Waistdeep and offer to soften it. Would notice that no one has named the Gate-Passers and feel that this was an oversight to correct. Would, in the worst case, want to be thanked.

The wise power notices these same things and understands, standing in the outer shallows, that each of them is load-bearing. The absence of green is what allowed the pre-botanical ecology to elaborate itself into the filaments and the mounds and the metabolism of the city. The compressed senescence is what the deep pools are *for*, and the filaments gathering around the dying is not a problem to solve but a relationship already solved, generations ago, by everyone involved. The unnamed Gate-Passers are unnamed because naming them would convert a habit of negotiation into a hierarchy of address, and the habit is older and better than any hierarchy would be.

So the power does not give. Not in the first sense.

But benevolence is not only restraint. Restraint alone is the wisdom of a power that is merely careful. A *benevolent* wise power has a second motion, after the seeing, and it is this:

It makes itself useful in the register the place already uses.

It does not announce itself. It does not appear to the Quiet Kin in a tower and offer collaboration, though the Quiet Kin would, in their unhurried way, be the ones most likely to notice it had come. It does not present itself to a Commensal hip-deep in a souring mound and offer to correct the chemistry, though it could. It does none of these things because each of them would introduce a new kind of relation into a city whose genius is that all its relations are *already there*, accumulated, mutual, slow.

Instead, the power does something smaller and stranger. It looks for the edges of the system — not the geographic edges, but the edges of competence, the places where the city's own self-maintenance just barely holds, where a small unnoticed addition could become, over centuries, indistinguishable from what was always there.

It finds a few. Perhaps:

A drift in the deep currents, very slow, that in about two thousand years would begin to starve the outermost mounds. The Commensals do not know about it yet and will not for a long time; by the time they did, the response would be possible but costly. The power adjusts something — not the current, which would be a violence, but the thermal gradient at one spring far out under the basin, by a fraction of a degree, in a way that will let the current correct itself over the same two thousand years. No one will ever notice. This is the point.

A vulnerability in the Working Small: a single failure mode, far down in their inherited chemistry, that has not yet been triggered because the conditions for it have not yet occurred, but the conditions are slowly assembling. The power does not redesign the Working Small. It seeds, into the filament-mats of three deep mounds, a variant that will, over generations, propagate a quiet correction. The Working Small will incorporate it the way they incorporate everything — by accumulation, by writing it onto the next surface they touch — and in a thousand years the failure mode will simply not be a thing that could happen, and no one will know it was ever a possibility, because it never became one.

A loneliness, perhaps. Not in any individual — the lineages are well-companioned — but a structural one: the basin is one basin, and there are other basins, on other worlds, that have grown in parallel and do not know of each other. The power does not introduce them. Introduction would be a catastrophe of scale; each is complete, and completeness meeting completeness without preparation produces only damage. But it leaves, somewhere in the patterning of one tower in one quiet quarter, a configuration that — if the city ever, of its own slow turning, develops the curiosity and the means — will resolve into a direction. A way to look. Not an answer. A question the city could ask, if it ever wanted to, in its own time, by its own hand. The configuration is indistinguishable from the ordinary patterning. A Quiet One might, in five hundred years, pause in front of it for ten minutes and then walk on. Or might not. The power does not require that it be found.

And then — and this is the part that distinguishes benevolence from mere usefulness — the power *receives* something.

Because the city is singing, at the rose-dark hour, and the singing is what the cooling walls need, and the singing is also what the city does for itself, and a wise power standing at the rim of such a thing understands that to witness it is already to be given something. The song is not for the power. The song is not for anyone. But the power is, in fact, *there*, in the outer shallows, in the pearled dimming light, and the song reaches it as it reaches everything in the basin, and the power lets itself be reached.

This is not sentimentality. A power of this kind has work elsewhere, many places, and most of its work is harder than this one and less complete. To stand for an evening at the edge of a place that is in correct relation to itself, and to let the roar enter the sternum the way it enters every visitor's sternum, and to feel the small closing of a cut on a hand that does not, strictly, need to have a hand — this is rest, in the sense the city means rest. It is the condition of being correctly placed in the flow. And the power, too, can be tired, in its own register, and can be restored by being briefly part of something that is doing itself well.

Before it leaves, it does one more thing, and this is the gift that is most like a gift, though it is still nearly nothing.

It does not bless the city. Blessing is a gesture from above, and there is no above here; the light is shadowless and generous and comes from everywhere.

It promises, instead, a kind of attention. Not surveillance — the city would feel surveillance, eventually, the way it feels a stuck gate, and would route around it. Something gentler: a standing willingness. If, in some far century, the basin meets a thing it cannot meet alone — a shift in the deep heat, a sickness in the Working Small that outpaces their own correction, a Gate-Passer that does not arrive when it should and whose absence begins to unravel the schedule of the causeways — the power will be reachable. Not by prayer; the city does not pray. By the same means the city uses for everything: by the song carrying a little further than usual, by a pattern in a tower-wall resolving in a particular way, by a Commensal noticing that a mound is pulsing in an unfamiliar rhythm and following the rhythm out past the rim.

The reaching will be possible. Whether it is ever used is the city's business, and the power, being wise, does not hope either way. Hoping would be a kind of pressure, and the basin is at the correct pressure already.

Then the power withdraws. The mist closes behind it. The towers click in the cooling. Somewhere, three districts in, a barge bell rings for the pre-dawn trade, and is answered, and the song thins into the working roar, and the basin resumes — which is to say, continues, which is to say, has never really stopped.

A Waistdeep on a causeway-step, sitting in the warm water selling pressed cakes, glances up at the outer shallows for no reason she can name, and notices nothing, and goes back to her cakes. Her forearm patterning, very faintly, has gained a single new element overnight. She will not see it for a week. When she does, she will think it is just the city, writing on her again, the way it does.

Which is correct. That is exactly what it is.
