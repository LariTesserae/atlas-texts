The benevolent power arrives the way good weather arrives in a dream: without entrance. It does not part the cleft or descend through the fissure. It is simply, at some point, also here — the way a second meaning is present in a sentence you have already finished reading.

It is wise enough to understand the room before it understands itself in the room. So it spends a long moment — though "long" is the wrong word, and "moment" is the wrong word, and "spends" is exactly right, because this room has taught it about budgets — doing nothing. It does not speak. Speaking would cost the chamber a syllable's worth of silence-repayment, and the power is too courteous a guest to make the room pay for its arrival.

It looks. It looks the way the light looks: by settling.

It sees the bowl. It sees the green binding. It sees the face in the stone that is not a face yet and is the maker's way of not being a face yet. It sees the maker — or rather, it sees the shape the maker has instead of a body, the warm side of the pebble, the angle of the awl, the depression in the moss. It understands, with the quickness that wisdom has when it is also kindness, what would be a violence here.

To complete anything would be a violence.

To name the maker would be a violence. To say *I see you* in any voice the maker could hear as address would force the maker into the grammar of having-been-seen, which is a past tense, and the maker does not live in past tenses. To offer a gift would be a violence, because a gift is a thing given and therefore a thing finished in the giving. To offer protection would be the subtlest violence of all: it would imply that the room needs to last, that lasting is the good outcome, that the mountain's eventual shrug is a thing to be prevented. But the maker is not afraid of the shrug. The maker has made peace with the shrug by never quite arriving at the question of it. To protect the room would be to answer a question the room has chosen not to ask.

So the power, being wise, does not protect. Does not bless. Does not name. Does not give.

What it does is smaller and harder.

It *joins the budget.*

It becomes, for the duration of its visit — which is not a duration — another of the room's careful expenditures. It folds itself into the economy of held breath. It agrees, while it is here, to the same physics the water has agreed to: to accumulate without falling, to swell without spilling, to be absorbed back into the weeping of the wall above. It accepts that its presence, too, will be conjugated through the frog and the moths and the single held note. It will not be a visitor in the room. It will be one of the room's tenses.

This is the gift it has chosen, though *gift* is still the wrong word. Call it a *matching*. The power has matched the room's terms. For as long as it is here — for as long as here is a thing — the room is very slightly more itself. The light is very slightly warmer on the palm of anyone who is about to walk through. The clay is very slightly softer against the maker's return. The face in the stone is very slightly more carefully not-yet-finished. The interval of repayment after a sound is very slightly more generous, the silence afterward very slightly denser, as if the room has been given a small grant toward its careful spending.

The maker does not notice the power. This is correct. Noticing would be a completed motion. But the maker's not-noticing is, while the power is here, very slightly more spacious. The way a sleeper does not notice that someone has drawn the blanket up, and yet the sleep afterward is different — deeper in some unprovable way, more held.

The power considers the face in the stone for what is, by any honest accounting, a long time. It sees what the maker is doing. It sees that the maker is working out what they look like by working the stone, and that the stone is working out what the maker looks like by being worked, and that the unresolved third term in this is the chamber itself, which is working out whether to have a maker at all by keeping the face unfinished. The power understands that it could, with the smallest application of its nature, resolve this. It could let the maker know what they look like. It could let the stone know. It could let the chamber know. Three knowings, very cheap to provide.

It declines. Wisdom is, in part, the discipline of declining cheap knowings.

Instead, it does this: it lets the not-knowing be witnessed. Not witnessed in the past tense — not *I saw your not-knowing* — but witnessed in the room's own tense, the always-about-to. The power becomes, while it is here, a witnessing that is always about to occur. It joins the maker in the about-to. It crouches, in whatever crouching means for a thing like itself, beside the maker who is always about to return. It does not complete the gesture of looking at the maker. It is always about to look. The maker is always about to be looked at, kindly, by something that loves them without needing them to have a face yet.

This is the whole of what it does.

Before it goes — though it does not go, exactly; it lets itself be absorbed back into the weeping of the wall above, the way the water is — it makes one adjustment, so small it could be missed. It does not add a fourth object to the shelf. That would be intrusion. It does not alter the three objects. That would be theft.

It thickens, by a hair, the *recently* in which the maker is always making them.

That is to say: the bowl is now wet, has been wet for a long time, has not dried and has not slumped, and the *long time* is very slightly longer, very slightly more generous, very slightly more *held*. The fiber is green, freshly stripped, and the *freshly* is now a freshness with a deeper reservoir, as if the plant the fiber came from is also being not-quite-finished, somewhere, by someone who loves the maker and was here once and was always about to be here.

The maker does not know this has happened. The maker will never know. Knowing would be a completed motion.

But on the timescale at which the orchids open and close — the timescale you can almost see if you hold still — the chamber is, from this not-moment forward, infinitesimally more able to hold what it holds. The mountain will still rise. The shrug will still come, or not come, on the mountain's own schedule. The cleft is no safer than it was. The power has not bought the room a single second of geological time, because geological time is not the currency the room spends in.

What the power has done is this: it has loved the room in the room's own tense. It has loved the maker without making the maker into someone who has been loved. It has added itself to the held breath and then let itself be exhaled, slowly, into the one pocket of stone that is exhaling slowly into itself.

If you walk through — and you are always about to walk through — you will not feel the power's presence. You will feel the room. You will feel the weight of the light on your palm. You will feel that you did not finish the face, and that this was correct.

But the not-finishing will feel, very faintly, *accompanied*. As though someone wiser than you also did not finish it, also chose not to, also crouched beside the maker in the about-to, and left the choosing there for you to find, the way one leaves a smoothing pebble warm on one side.

That is what the benevolent and wise power does.

It matches the budget. It declines the cheap knowing. It witnesses in the always-about-to. It thickens the recently. And then it lets itself be absorbed back into the wall, the way the water does, the way the light does, the way the single note does in the molars of someone who walked through once

and will always be about to walk through.
