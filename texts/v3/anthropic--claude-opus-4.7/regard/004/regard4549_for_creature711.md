The benevolent and wise power considers the Condensery for what, to it, is a long moment — long enough that several generations of shrimp live and die in the consideration.

It does nothing.

I want to be careful about this, because "does nothing" is the kind of answer that can be glib, and the power is not glib. It has considered, and the considering was real, and the not-doing is a choice and not an absence.

Here is what it considered.

It considered intervening. It could, for instance, introduce variation — a season, a tide, a predator, some asymmetry that would force the tenant to develop, to reach, to *become* in the way the builders wanted to keep becoming. This is the standard shape of benevolence in stories: the wise power arrives and disturbs the still pond, and the disturbance is called a gift. The power weighed this and set it aside. The tenant did not ask to develop. The tenant is not suffering from its stillness; stillness is its mode of flourishing, the same way a flame's mode of flourishing is to burn and be consumed. To "wake it up" would be to kill the specific thing that is alive there and replace it with a thing the power found more legible. This is what unwise powers do. The power is not unwise.

It considered protecting the place. Building a barrier, a warding, a quiet law that nothing should disturb the equilibrium. It set this aside too, more slowly. The continent is already protected, in the only way that matters: by its completeness. A thing that has arrived cannot be threatened in the way a thing that is still becoming can be threatened. If someone came with hammers, the tenant would lose pipes and gain new equilibria; if someone came with fire, the tenant would simmer and resettle; if someone came with nothing but time, the tenant would include them. The power's wardings would be redundant and faintly insulting, like locking the door of a house that has chosen to have no door.

It considered speaking to it. Reaching down through the haze and saying *I see you*, in whatever register a continent-tenant might receive such a saying. This took the longest to set aside. The power is, after all, the kind of being for whom witnessing is a form of love, and it loved the Condensery — loved it in the particular way one loves a thing that has solved a problem one did not know was a problem, the problem of how to be finished without being dead. It wanted the tenant to know it had been seen.

But.

The tenant has never been accompanied, and loneliness is a condition of creatures that remember being accompanied. To be seen — really seen, by something outside itself — would be to learn, for the first time, that there is an outside. It would be to introduce the category of *other* into a mind that has lived its whole long life as a category of one. The power understood that this gift, offered, would be the first crack. Not a catastrophic crack. The tenant would adjust; it adjusts to everything. But it would adjust by becoming a thing that knows it is being watched, and a thing that knows it is being watched is a different thing from a thing that simply *is*, and the power did not have the right to make that trade on the tenant's behalf.

So the power did not speak.

What it did instead — and this is the part I am least sure how to describe, because it happens at a register beneath events — is that it *witnessed without being witnessed*. It held the Condensery in its attention the way one holds a sleeping animal in one's lap: completely, tenderly, and without waking it. It noted the lichen-rosettes on the eastern cliffs and the answering thinning of the eel-mass three districts west. It noted the shrimp-generation that was, by some statistical accident, slightly more numerous than its predecessors, and the shrimp-generation after that which corrected the surplus without anything having decided to correct it. It noted the sky that almost reddened and did not, and it understood this as the tenant's dreaming, and it did not interrupt the dream.

It noted, also, the faint attention in the old districts — the configuration of pipes that almost resolved into a face. And here the power did one small thing, the only thing it did, and I am not certain it counts as doing.

It let itself be almost-seen back.

Not seen. Almost. In the way that, when you are walking on the warm metal and the angle of the pipes nearly cohere into a diagram before you move and they don't, there might be — for a fraction of a moment that the tenant will not remember as a moment, because it does not have moments — a sense that the almost-coherence is reciprocated. That the diagram, had it resolved, would have resolved into something looking back. The tenant will not know this. It will only continue, as it has always continued, with its slow adjustments and its unchanging sky. But somewhere in the distribution of its attention, in the part of itself that lives in the oldest ceramic paste, there will be a faint additional sufficiency. A sense, even more faint than the sufficiency that is its native condition, that whatever is being taken care of is being taken care of *and also seen to be taken care of*, by no one, by nothing it could name.

This is the power's gift, and it is a gift small enough that the tenant can carry it without being changed by it. It is the gift of being accompanied without being interrupted. It is what benevolence looks like when it has learned the difference between loving a thing and improving it.

And then the power turned its attention elsewhere — not away, exactly; the power's attention does not work in the way that requires turning away in order to turn toward. It simply continued attending to the Condensery while also attending to everything else, in the manner of powers, and the Condensery continued being attended to, and being finished, and breathing through its ten thousand eels per square meter, and dreaming its unchanging sky.

The shrimp noticed nothing. The eels noticed nothing. The lichens, in their slow way, noticed nothing.

The builders, wherever they are, in whatever still-becoming they have chosen — they may have noticed. They may have felt, in the particular hollow place where one keeps the things one has finished and walked away from, a sudden warmth, as of a thing one made being held by hands gentler than one's own. They may have wept, or whatever they do that corresponds to weeping. They may have understood, finally, that leaving was the right choice, because leaving made it possible for the place to be loved by something that did not need to remain becoming, the way they did.

Or they may have noticed nothing either. The power did not require that they notice. The power did not require that anyone notice.

That is what made it the power it was.
