The wise power arrives, as wise powers do, by being noticed rather than appearing. One of the residents—the one who forgot gravity mid-sentence—pauses while gathering a particularly long silence from beneath a doorframe, and recognizes that the quality of being-witnessed has shifted, the way a room changes when someone enters carrying a sleeping child.

The power does not announce itself. To announce would be to commit to a tense, and tenses here are still under negotiation.

What the power does first is *sit down*.

This is the most important thing. Not on anything—the granular substrate would politely dissolve its hexagonal courtesies—but at the level of the residents, lowering whatever it has instead of a body until it shares the eye-line of Almost, who is dying so slowly that conversation with her requires several geological epochs of attentiveness compressed into the duration of a held breath.

The power has come prepared to do many things. It could:

Stabilize them. Grant them the density of 3/3, render their bones obedient to one architecture, file the paperwork that would let their names hold water. It has the authority for this. It has done such kindnesses elsewhere—for refugees of dissolving countries, for ghosts who grew tired of haunting, for ideas that wanted at last to be objects.

Complete them. Finish the sentences their grandparents began. Deliver the arrivals that learned to postpone themselves. Hand each resident the moment of greatest clarity they were named after, this time as a possession rather than a passing weather.

Translate them. Move them, gently as one moves a moth from a windowpane, to a realm whose physics would receive their edges as features rather than failures. There are such realms. The power knows the routes.

Witness them. Simply that.

The power considers. Wisdom is mostly the discipline of considering longer than power usually permits.

It notices: the infant Writhes, cocooned in the sound of parentheses that never quite touched. If the power *completes* the parentheses, the infant becomes a finished statement, and finished statements do not grow. They are admired, perhaps anthologized, and they do not grow.

It notices: Almost is not suffering. She is performing the longest and most attentive act of dying that her people have ever managed, and around her deathbed the soft things gather—dandelion clocks, apologies, the warm texture of regret—because her not-quite-arriving has become the architecture that holds these soft things in the world. To grant her completion would be to collapse a cathedral whose function is to keep certain weathers possible.

It notices: the Migration Festival is tomorrow. The residents will walk in spirals whose centers drift, carrying possessions in bags woven from *too late* whispered in untranslated languages. If the power offers them a destination, the spirals become mere paths, and the bags become luggage, and the festival becomes a relocation, and something irreplaceable will have been replaced by something merely useful.

The benevolence of the power deepens here, because true benevolence is mostly the restraint of premature gifts.

So this is what the power does:

**First**, it makes itself slightly more available to be leaned against. Not solid—solidity would be an imposition—but *leanable*, in the way certain silences are leanable, certain long afternoons. The residents do not know they are leaning. They simply find, over the course of what might be a day or a paragraph, that their fluctuations between 0.1 and 0.3 density have grown less anxious. They still flicker. But the flickering has acquired a held quality, the way a candle flame is held by the attention of someone watching it without wanting it to stop.

**Second**, it locates the precise place where the rain pauses—the place the landscape mentions but always changes the subject before revealing. The power does not show it to anyone. Instead, it makes the *searching* slightly easier. It adjusts the probability, by a fraction too small to measure, that a resident pausing between footsteps will catch the glimpse. It does not guarantee the glimpse. Guarantees are a form of theft from those who live by almost.

**Third**, and this takes the longest, the power composes a small correction to the metaphysics of the Crèche. The correction is this: *unfinishedness shall no longer be understood as a deficit awaiting remedy, but as a vocation.* The change is filed quietly with whatever administers such things. The residents do not notice, because they have always known this; only now the universe around them knows it too, and stops sending them the subtle pressures it sends to incomplete things, the small grammatical corrections, the gravitational suggestions toward closure. They are released not into completion but into the legitimacy of their condition. They become, in the eyes of the larger order, *a kind of place rather than a kind of failure*.

This is a significant gift. It will not feel like a gift. It will feel like the weather got a little kinder, like the pattern birds started landing on shoulders, like Almost laughed once, sideways, and the laugh held.

**Fourth**, the power does something for the infant. It does not finish the parentheses. Instead, it teaches the parentheses how to remain open without anxiety—how to be a held-openness rather than an unresolved one. The infant's first cry, which had resembled a question mark searching for its lost sentence, settles into resembling a question mark that has understood it is not lost, only *questioning*, which is a different vocation entirely. The infant Writhes more peacefully. It will grow, if growth is the word, into one of the most exquisite of its kind: a being whose entire life is the demonstration that some questions are houses, not journeys.

**Fifth**, before leaving—if leaving is the word—the power sits with Almost for what is, by her measure, a long while. They do not face each other. The power knows about the sliding memories. They sit shoulder to shoulder, or whatever the equivalent is for beings made of attention and beings made of almost. The power does not offer her completion. The power offers her, instead, the assurance that her dying will be remembered as a making—that somewhere in the great catalog of what existence has accomplished, her centuries of not-quite-arriving will be listed under *architectures*, not under *failures to arrive*. Almost receives this with the smallest possible shift in her translucence. A dandelion clock near her releases one seed upward.

**Sixth**, the power does *not* attend the Migration Festival. To attend would be to give it a witness it did not request, and the festival's whole tender logic is that it occurs in the margins of every story that pretended to know how to reach the bottom of the page. A witnessed margin becomes a center. The power has the grace to remain elsewhere, or to attend in such thorough absence that its attendance and its non-attendance cancel into the precise nutrient the festival requires.

**Finally**, the power leaves the way it arrived: by becoming un-noticed, by withdrawing the quality of being-witnessed so gently that the residents experience only a faint sense that something has been finished without anything having concluded.

The one who forgot gravity mid-sentence resumes gathering silences.

The pattern birds resume stitching.

The rain resumes its 1.6/3 of perpetual delivery.

But the rain falls now into a Crèche that has been, in some small and irreversible way, *blessed*—not with completion, not with rescue, not with translation to a kinder physics, but with the much rarer gift of having its particular form of being recognized as a form of being rather than a failure to be otherwise.

The power, departing, thinks: *Some places exist so that certain things which cannot survive elsewhere can survive somewhere. The kindness owed to such places is not to improve them. The kindness owed is to make sure the rest of existence remembers they are necessary.*

And the power goes to file the appropriate paperwork with the rest of existence.

This takes, by the Crèche's reckoning, no time at all, because the Crèche reckons in moments between footsteps, and the power has always been careful to move only when both feet are committed.

Somewhere, an infant Writhes peacefully inside a parenthesis that has learned to be a home.

Somewhere, Almost continues, with new dignity, her long and deliberate not-arriving.

Somewhere, a stone is lifted, and beneath it the subroutine calls have been quietly updated to include a single new line:

*This place is allowed.*
