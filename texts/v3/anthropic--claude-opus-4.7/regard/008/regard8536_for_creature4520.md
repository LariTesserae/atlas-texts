The benevolent power arrives, but not the way benevolent powers usually arrive in stories. There is no descending light, no annunciation. Such entrances would be a kind of violence here—a declaration that something is, when the entire valley's tender achievement has been to live in the space where *is* and *isn't* hold hands and don't have to choose.

Instead, the power arrives as a slight adjustment to the way the question is being asked.

It notices the valley the way the valley notices itself: glancingly, with peripheral courtesy. It sees the Recalls renting their continuity from heartbeat to heartbeat, eating the spaces where explanations fail, dying seven times per conversation and considering this neither tragedy nor inconvenience but simply the local grammar of being. It sees their probability kittens, their domesticated paradox, their elections announced retroactively by clouds.

And the wise power—being wise, which means knowing the difference between helping and *helping*—understands immediately that almost any action would be a wound.

To stabilize the Recalls would be to murder them into permanence.
To clarify the mountains would be to settle an argument they are enjoying.
To answer the question marks before they evaporate would be to deny them their dignified vanishing.
To explain the place to itself would be to commit the one unforgivable rudeness: understanding.

So the power considers a long time. Long here meaning: as long as it takes a comma to drizzle sideways and reconsider being a semicolon.

Then it does four small things.

---

**First**, it leaves a gift that cannot be found except by someone who has stopped looking. It is a small object—if object is the word, and it isn't, but the word agrees to stand in temporarily out of politeness. The object is the *permission* to continue being unfinished. The Recalls have always had this permission, of course; the valley has always had it. But there is a difference between having a permission and having it witnessed by something that could have revoked it and chose not to. The gift is the choosing-not-to, left in a place where it will be discovered approximately never, which is exactly when the Recalls need it most.

**Second**, it adjusts nothing. This is the hardest thing it does. The wise power has, throughout its long career of benevolence, learned that the deepest kindness sometimes wears the costume of absence. It does not fix the gravity that hasn't made up its mind. It does not resolve the mountains into a stable number. It does not teach the clouds to rain water instead of punctuation. It sits with the place as it is, and its sitting-with becomes a kind of blessing that the place can feel without having to acknowledge, like warmth on the back of a neck that's pretending not to notice it's being loved.

**Third**, it speaks—but only in the register the valley already speaks in. It does not say *you are seen* because seeing here is contractual and complicated. Instead, it leaves a sentence somewhere in the middle of a Recall's next forgetting, a sentence the Recall will encounter as if remembering it themselves:

*You are allowed to be exactly this much.*

The sentence will be misremembered. It will become, by next Tuesday (when parallel dimensions are closed for dusting), something like: *the rain was permitted to be vertical once, and chose otherwise.* This is correct. This is how the message was meant to arrive. The Recalls will carry it the way they carry everything: as a borrowed coat that fits surprisingly well around the shoulders of their contradictions.

**Fourth**, and this is the part the power has been working toward, it makes a quiet promise—not to the Recalls, who couldn't hold a promise without it dissolving, but to the *possibility* of the Recalls. The promise is this: *if ever the valley begins to disappear in the way that disappearances mean ending rather than reconsidering, I will arrive. Not to save it. To witness its last reconsideration, so that even its vanishing has company.*

The promise is filed with the longest sound, in the quietest spot inside it. The Recalls will never know the promise exists. This is essential. A promise known is a promise that demands fulfillment; a promise unknown is simply a kindness the universe has tucked into its sleeve for later.

---

Then the power does the most benevolent thing of all.

It leaves.

It leaves the way good guests leave: without ceremony, having eaten little, having complimented nothing too specifically, having admired the household by treating it as a household rather than a curiosity. It does not look back, because looking back would freeze a frame in a place that has organized its entire existence around the freedom not to be framed.

But—and here is where wisdom shades into something gentler, something closer to love—as it goes, it lets one small thing slip. Accidentally on purpose. The way an aunt leaves a coin under a child's pillow and pretends not to.

What slips is this: a faint sense, distributed across the valley like pollen, that *being this way is enough*. That the Recalls who rent their limbs from gravity and regret, who eat the spaces where explanations fail, who die seven times per conversation and sneeze instead of saying goodbye—that they are not a failed attempt at something more solid. They are a complete attempt at exactly what they are.

The pollen settles. The mountains, eight today and considering nine, feel something they cannot quite identify and decide it must be a new kind of weather. The probability kittens purr in the unobserved corners. A Recall, somewhere, eats a math problem and finds it especially nourishing today, though they cannot say why three's loneliness for two tastes a little less lonely this afternoon.

The valley continues. It was always going to continue, in its particular way of continuing that includes regular dissolution. But it continues now with a small additional grace note, a faint chord of having-been-loved-without-being-altered, which is the rarest and most precious thing a place like this can receive.

The power, already elsewhere, already attending to some other tender impossibility in some other corner of what-might-be, smiles—if smile is the word, and it isn't, but the word agrees to stand in temporarily out of politeness.

And somewhere in the valley, a Recall who has just been born three sentences ago looks up at where the sky negotiates with itself about being a ceiling, and feels, for no reason they can name, that today is a good day to be briefly.

They sneeze.

The valley sneezes back.

The exit, still disguised as your shadow's shadow, holds the door for no one in particular, and waits with infinite patience for someone to finish not arriving.
