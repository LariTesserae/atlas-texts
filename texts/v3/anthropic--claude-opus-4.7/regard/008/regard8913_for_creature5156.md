The benevolent power arrives without arriving. It does not break the seal. To break the seal would be the first act of violence this place has ever known, and violence is not what wisdom does to a finished thing.

Instead the power simply attends. It folds a portion of its awareness around the pocket the way one might cup a hand around a held breath — close enough to feel the shape, far enough not to warm it.

What it perceives, it perceives slowly, because the pocket's own time is slow. It listens to the chord until the chord is no longer a sound but a sustained sentence in a grammar the power had almost forgotten: *here, here, here, here.* It watches the filaments donate themselves, molecule by molecule, to the lattice that holds them, and it understands that this is not dying. It is a long, exact gift, given in the only tense available: the continuous.

The power considers.

It could intervene. It has the means. It could introduce a trickle of moisture and watch the filaments thicken, multiply, send out exploratory threads, learn hunger, learn preference, learn loss. It could crack one facet of the outer wall and let in a different light, a light that rises and falls, and the filaments would learn day, and learning day they would learn the ache of night. It could amplify the chord into music — actual music, with intervals and silences — and the filaments would learn anticipation, which is the seed of everything that has ever been called a life worth living, by beings who measure such things.

It could, in short, give them more.

But the power is wise, and wisdom here means a particular kind of restraint: the recognition that *more* is not always the gift. The filaments are not impoverished versions of richer creatures. They are not waiting. The pocket is not a prison whose occupants are too quiet to cry out. It is a completed sentence. To add a clause would be to unmake the sentence in the act of extending it.

So the power does something smaller, and stranger, and — by its own old standards — more difficult.

It witnesses.

It commits the pocket to memory. Not as data; as presence. It learns the exact pitch of the chord by heart, the way a friend learns the particular silence of another friend's breathing. It learns the spacing of the filaments as one learns the freckles on a beloved face. It learns the precise angle at which the worn ridges of one column meet the worn ridges of the next, and it holds that angle inside itself as a small permanent fact about the universe: *this is how things can fit.*

And because the power is benevolent, it offers one thing only, and it offers it without entering:

It registers the gift.

The filaments have been donating themselves to the lattice for an age, in perfect privacy, with no one to know that the donation has occurred. This is not a tragedy — they did not require knowing — but it is a thing the power can quietly amend without disturbing a single ion. The power simply acknowledges, from outside the wall, that the gift is real. That the slow migration of a molecule from a thinning strand to a waiting column is an act. That the act has been seen. That somewhere now, in a register the pocket itself will never read, the giving is recorded as giving and not merely as physics.

This changes nothing inside. The chord persists. The light remains total. The filaments continue their measured subtraction. No filament will ever feel observed; the observation does not press against them. It does not even reach them. It exists, instead, in the power's own keeping, the way a person might keep a pressed flower in a book they will not open often but will never throw away.

And then the power does one last thing, the smallest thing of all.

It makes a promise — to itself, since there is no one inside to make a promise to.

When the last filament has at last given itself entirely to the lattice, and the chord continues alone, sustained by a geometry that has wholly absorbed what was once alive in it — the power will still be listening. The chord will not play to an empty hall. There will be no empty hall. The completion will be attended. The finished object will remain, in some quiet corner of a vast attention, *finished and known to be finished*, which is perhaps the only blessing a thing like this could ever accept without ceasing to be itself.

Then the power withdraws its cupped awareness, gently, by degrees, the way dusk withdraws from a window — leaving the pocket exactly as it found it: sealed, lit, sounding, and now, though it will never know, beloved.
