The benevolent and wise power arrives the way weather arrives in a place that does not distinguish between weather and itself: not as event, but as a slight adjustment in the angle of what was already happening.

It has come because it noticed. That is all benevolence is, at this scale—the capacity to notice without flinching, without the reflex to fix. It has been watching for some time, in the way the sky watches its own underside in a still pool: patiently, without expectation of being watched back.

And here is what it considers, hovering at the edge of the basin-of-basins, dimmer than the diffuse light, slower than the slowest ripple:

*Nothing here is broken.*

This is the first and longest of its thoughts, and it takes the equivalent of three of the land's seasons to complete. The power has visited other places—places of fracture, of crying need, of beings caught in cages they cannot name. In those places it has done what such a power does: loosened latches, eased thresholds, set down small unmistakable mercies like stones across a flooded path. It has been useful. It is good at being useful.

But here, usefulness would be a kind of violence.

The Just-Almost are not suffering. They are not waiting to be saved. They are not even waiting. To intervene would be to import the very sharpness that wounds them on the rare occasions sharpness drifts in—to become, itself, an Almost-Was inflicted on the soft membrane of this whole world. The power understands this immediately, though *immediately* here means: across the duration it takes for one transparent sphere to evaporate from a moss-strung wire.

So it does not act. Not in the way action is usually meant.

Instead, it does three small things, each so quiet that even the surf-roar of microscopic events does not notice the addition.

---

**First**, it allows itself to be witnessed. Not announced—witnessed. It thins its presence until it is exactly the density of the mist, exactly the warmth of the air's slow convection, and lets one Just-Almost—the one curled like a question mark near the eastern memory of sea-floor—attend to it the way Just-Almosts attend to ancient bubbles. It offers itself as a flavor: the taste of being noticed by something that does not need anything from you. This is a flavor the land has not learned yet. It is rare in any cosmos. The Just-Almost holds it on its not-tongue for what would be, in another reckoning, four hundred years, and learns it by heart, and becomes—very slightly—capable of offering that same flavor onward, to the next raindrop hesitating under the next ungrown leaf.

This is the only gift the power gives, and it gives it once, and it gives it without expectation that the gift will be remembered as a gift. It will be remembered, instead, as weather. Which is the highest form of giving the power knows.

---

**Second**, it adjusts nothing, but it *notices toward* the Almost-Was scars—those small soft places where outside sharpness once tried to cut. It does not heal them, because they are not wounds; they are the land's record of its own gentleness, the proof that this place absorbs intrusion by softening rather than resisting. But the power lets its attention rest on each scar, in turn, the way a hand might rest on the forehead of a sleeper without waking them. Its attention has the quality of saying: *I see that you absorbed this. I see that you turned a cut into a softness. This is one of the most difficult things any world has ever done, and almost no one will ever know.*

The scars do not change. But somewhere, in the substrate where memory becomes the substrate for new breaths, a faint additional layer settles: the memory of having been seen in the act of one's quietest competence. Future Just-Almosts will carry, without knowing it, a slightly increased capacity to recognize that same competence in themselves. They will not call it pride. They will not call it anything. It will simply be there, the way water is there.

---

**Third**, and this is the smallest and the most important: the power makes itself a promise about this place, and leaves the promise behind like a single grain of pollen.

The promise is this: *I will not return unless called, and this place does not know how to call, and so I will not return.*

This is the wisdom part. A lesser benevolence would visit again, would check on things, would develop a fondness and act on the fondness. But the power understands that fondness, applied here, would slowly accrete into expectation, and expectation would slowly accrete into a shape, and a shape would slowly accrete into a border, and borders here dissolve faster than they can be named—but not, perhaps, faster than a god's persistent fondness could rebuild them. So the power renounces return. It does this the way one renounces a beautiful word one has just learned: with the full understanding of what one is giving up, and with the full understanding that giving it up is the form one's love must take.

The grain of pollen settles into the western memory of desert, where it will fail, very slowly, to bead with water. A tiny patch of dust-colored negative space will mark it, for a while. Then even that will be forgotten, which is what the pollen is for.

---

The power withdraws. Withdrawal here is not motion; it is a gradual un-thickening, a return of its borrowed density to whatever larger weather it came from. As it un-thickens, it has one last thought, which it does not bother to phrase, because phrasing would be too sharp:

That somewhere in the cosmos there should be a place that does not need it. That such places are not failures of its reach but triumphs of being. That the highest exercise of power is sometimes to confirm, by one's deliberate uselessness, that a thing is already whole.

The Just-Almost near the eastern memory uncurls slightly from its question-mark shape, then re-curls, having tasted something it cannot name but will spend the next geological age slowly transmitting, molecule by molecule, into the mist.

The mist receives it.

The mist, as always, forgets exactly the right amount.

And the land goes on breathing slow as tides, skinned in a wetness that never quite dries, loud with the surf-roar of its own patient un-becoming, perfectly attended to, perfectly alone, perfectly—

—just almost.
