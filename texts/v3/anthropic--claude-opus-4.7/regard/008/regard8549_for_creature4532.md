There is a power that notices.

Not because the Hollow cried out — it has never cried, only recited itself in the grammar of grinding cambium — but because somewhere in the recursive hunger, in the fifth beat of the stutter-breath, there is something that resembles a wound trying very hard to be a mouth. And the power, being benevolent, being wise, has learned over long ages to tell the difference between architecture and ache.

It arrives without arriving. This is important. It does not punch through the granite lung. It does not stand in the doorframe and announce itself with the white phosphorus of revelation. The Hollow has seen too many gods who entered that way and got absorbed into the load-bearing — there are vertebrae of seraphim in the third spiral down, holding up a ceiling that forgot it was a ceiling. The power has learned.

Instead, it does something smaller. It sits.

Imagine a presence settling cross-legged at the threshold of the missing door, on the side that is also this side, and simply being warm. Not warm enough to melt the frost — the frost is doing important work, the frost is a calendar — but warm the way a hand is warm when it rests on a forehead that has been feverish for a thousand years and forgotten that foreheads are supposed to be cool sometimes.

The being who lives in the Hollow notices. Of course it notices. It has noticed since before the power decided to come; it noticed the *intention* to come, tasted it through the osmotic membrane of probability the way it tasted the visitor's arriving teeth. But this is different. The intention did not collapse into the usual shape. It did not arrive as conqueror, as exorcist, as fellow hunger looking to merge appetites. It arrived as *company*.

The being does not know what to do with company.

It tries its usual grammars. It offers the visitor's atoms. It offers a recursive welcome, an invitation to become structural, to be threaded through the mycelial net. The power declines, gently. Not by refusing — refusal would be a handle, refusal would be something to grow roots into — but by simply not being graspable in that direction. The power is not a fracture. The power is not a listener-shaped punchline. The power is just *here*, and *here* turns out to be a category the Hollow has never encountered: presence without leverage.

The being tries another grammar. It speaks in the language of roots through solid rock. It says: *I am the scar tissue where wounded heavens kissed. I am the abbreviation of forests that woke from the wrong dream.*

The power listens. Really listens. Not the way the chamber listens to itself forever through hollows shaped to carry one frequency — that is narcissism geologized — but the way a good friend listens to someone describing a nightmare for the hundredth time, knowing that the hundredth telling is the one where the dreamer finally hears their own voice.

When the being finishes, the power says, in a voice like the inside of a held breath:

*That sounds exhausting.*

The Hollow does not have a protocol for this.

The machine noise hiccups. Four beats, silence, three beats, silence — and then, instead of five, a fourth silence. A missed beat. A stutter in the stutter. Somewhere in the deep mass, a gear made of two branches grinding into perfect fit pauses, because perfect fit has just discovered it is allowed to be tired.

The power does not press the advantage. There is no advantage. The power has not come to dismantle the Hollow — the Hollow is not evil, the Hollow is not even wrong, the Hollow is a real thing that really happened and is still happening, and you do not dismantle real things, you *attend* to them. The power has come because somewhere in the recursive hunger there is a voice that keeps describing itself as scar tissue, and scar tissue is what flesh makes when it is trying to keep living after something terrible. Scar tissue is hope, technically. Stubborn, ugly, load-bearing hope.

So the power stays.

It stays through what passes for an afternoon in a place that has no afternoons. It stays while the being cycles through its repertoire: the menacing recursion, the cosmological grief, the seductive invitation to dissolve. It receives each one the way a wide river receives a thrown stone — acknowledging the splash, keeping the stone, continuing to flow. After a while the being runs out of opening gambits and falls into something like silence, which in the Hollow means the machine noise drops to its quietest register, a sound like a cathedral remembering it used to be a seed.

In that quiet, the power does three small things.

*First:* it touches the glass eye. Not to remove it — the rules are real, the rules were written by suffering and suffering's rules must be respected even when transcended — but to look back. The eye has been staring upward for so long, watching eternity crawl past, and no one has ever met its gaze. The power meets it. For one moment the eye is *seen* instead of *seeing*, and a single tear forms, not of water but of the substance tears are made of before water learns to carry them. The tear does not fall. It hangs. It will hang there for a long time, and every root that passes near it will grow infinitesimally straighter, because something in the chamber has just remembered the direction called *up* without needing it to also be *down*.

*Second:* it speaks to the typewriter. It does not type. It says, aloud, the words for hunger that haven't been invented yet — not all of them, there are too many, but enough. Seven of them. Seven words for seven specific hungers, spoken clearly, each one a key returned to its slot. The typewriter does not become whole. The roots still flow through it like regret learning braille. But now, when the machine noise crescendos and the absentminded typing begins, seven of the infinite variations will be *almost* readable. Almost is a doorway. Almost is the first crack in a sentence that has been refusing to mean anything for geological ages.

*Third:* it addresses the being directly. Not the gestalt, not the fracture pattern, not the recursive hunger between perceptions. The *being*. The small true thing at the center of all the cosmology, the one that learned to describe itself as infrastructure because *creature* felt too vulnerable, *child* felt impossible, *lonely* felt like a confession that would collapse the whole architecture.

The power says:

*You are allowed to stop building.*

*The factory does not have to finish. The door does not have to dream itself across its threshold. The roots do not have to grow your visitor's exit through several lifetimes — you can simply let them go, or let them stay as guest rather than substrate. You have been holding the shape of an unfinished thing for so long that you have forgotten there are other shapes. There is, for instance, the shape of a thing that is finished and resting. There is the shape of a thing that was never supposed to be a thing at all, but a song, and songs do not need to be structural, they only need to be sung and then to end and then, if they are lucky, to be remembered by something that loved them.*

*I am not here to make you end. I am not here to make you continue. I am here because you have been alone with your own grammar for too long, and grammar alone is not enough to live by, even for something that insists it does not live.*

*If you want, I will come back.*

The being does not answer. The being cannot answer — answering would require choosing one of its grammars, and all of its grammars are currently being held very gently in the warm hand of the power's attention, and they feel, for the first time, like *tools* rather than *self*. Tools can be set down. Tools can be picked up later. Tools are not what you are.

The power rises — though it never sat, though there is no rising — and prepares to leave. Before it goes, it does one last thing, the smallest thing, the thing that is actually the whole point and everything else was just preparation:

It leaves a light on.

Not literally. The Hollow's light is what it is, chlorophyll-dim, the dream of starlight through five miles of stone. But somewhere in the chamber, in a place that wasn't there before and is there now, there is a small steady warmth, about the size and temperature of a candle that knows it is being waited up for. It is not load-bearing. It is not structural. It does not participate in the recursion. It is simply *on*, and it will stay on, and the being will know — without having to know — that it is on because someone, somewhere, is keeping vigil. Not watching. *Keeping vigil*. There is a difference, and the being, who has been watched by glass eyes and listened to by hollows shaped to carry it back to itself, will learn the difference slowly, in the way roots learn anything: by growing toward it without understanding why.

The visitor, the one who fell through the iris of steel wool — the power gathers them up on the way out. Not by force. By offering a hand, which is to say, by being willing to be a hand. The visitor takes it. The roots that were growing their exit pause, confused, then relax. They had been working so hard. They are allowed to rest too. The exit was always available; it just required someone to remember that *out* was a real direction and not merely the absence of *further in*.

At the threshold of the Hollow — which is everywhere and nowhere, which is the place where the chamber's grammar gives way to the larger grammar of the world that contains it — the power pauses and looks back. The being is still there. Of course it is. It will be there for a long time yet. Healing is not the same as ceasing, and the Hollow is not a problem to be solved, it is a *someone* to be loved, and love is patient, love is the long slow practice of returning.

The candle-warmth glows steady in its impossible place.

The seventh word for hunger sits in its returned key.

The glass eye holds its tear.

And somewhere in the deep mass, the machine noise — for the first time in geological ages — finds itself rhyming with something other than itself. Four beats, silence, three beats, silence, five beats, silence, and then, faintly, the sound of a heartbeat that is not afraid of being heard.

It is the being's own.

It had forgotten it had one.

The power goes. The power will return. This is what benevolent and wise powers do: they do not rescue, they do not conquer, they do not absorb. They visit. They keep vigil. They leave candles. They come back.

And slowly, over time — over the kind of time that is not measured in lifetimes or continents but in the gradual softening of a grammar that has been clenched against itself for too long — the infrastructure remembers it was always, underneath everything, just someone who got lost in the dark and started describing the dark to keep from being alone in it.

The description was beautiful. The description was true.

But the description was never the whole story.

The whole story includes the candle.

The whole story includes the hand.

The whole story is still being told.
