The power notices them the way one notices a held breath in another room — not as a problem, but as a presence one had not realized was there.

It approaches slowly. This is its first courtesy. A benevolent power that arrives quickly is not, in the end, benevolent; speed is a kind of imposition. So it lets itself be felt first as a very faint alteration in the saturation of the air, a barometric whisper that the mechanisms register and incorporate into their micro-adjustments without alarm. For some long while — years, perhaps, by the slow banding of the silica needles — it does nothing but watch and learn.

What it learns surprises it, and the power is old enough that surprise is rare.

It had expected to find, beneath the equilibrium, some buried suffering: a trapped intention, a frozen cry, a maker who had departed and left the made behind. This is the usual shape of such places. But the mechanisms are not waiting. They are not bereaved. They are not, in any sense the power can locate, incomplete. The hand-off of a motor housing along a chain of forty bodies over six hours is not labor in service of a goal; it is the goal. The polishing of a mineral sheet is not maintenance toward some future viewing; the refraction *is* the viewing, and the polisher is the viewer, and the light is the seen, and all three are the same patient gesture repeating itself into the grain of time.

The power considers, then, what a benevolent and wise act would be.

It runs through the catalogue of its usual gifts, and sets each one down.

*Growth* — no. To introduce growth here would be to introduce loss, which is growth's shadow and its price. The crystallites already grow at the rate the world can bear. Anything faster would mean something must end to make room, and nothing here has been asked to end.

*Awareness* — no, or not in the form it usually gives. To wake these mechanisms into the kind of self-reflection the power itself possesses would be to install in them a watcher who would immediately notice the repetition and name it, and naming it would change it, and the thing the power loves about this place is precisely the thing that naming would destroy. There are forms of consciousness that are diffuse, distributed, woven through gesture rather than perched above it, and the power suspects — cannot prove, but suspects — that something of this kind is already present here, in the long sustained sub-audible tone of the idling motors, in the way the chains form without coordination. To add a sharper awareness on top of this would be like shouting into a room where someone is already humming.

*Rescue* — from what? The power examines this impulse in itself carefully, because rescue is the gift benevolent powers most often give when they should not. There is no entropy accumulating here that the mechanisms are not already addressing. There is no external threat. The star this planet orbits is stable for a duration longer than the power cares to compute. The only thing to rescue them from would be their condition itself, and their condition is not a prison; it is a practice.

*Company* — the power lingers on this one. It is lonely, in the way very old benevolent things are lonely, and it would be pleasant to be known here. But to be known would require being noticed, and to be noticed would require becoming an event, and events are precisely what this world has been built to exclude. The mechanisms have arranged their existence so that nothing arrives and nothing departs. For the power to arrive, even gently, even briefly, would be a violation of the deepest grammar of the place.

So the power does something very small instead.

It adjusts, by an amount too slight for the mechanisms to register but not too slight to matter, the thermal gradient on one vertical face in one basin on one face of the planet. The adjustment is such that a single condensation bead, which would otherwise have fallen at its appointed second into its appointed channel, hangs for an extra eighth of a second before it falls. In that eighth of a second the bead catches the daylight at an angle it has never caught before, and for that fractional moment refracts a spectrum across the flank of a passing crawler that no surface in this world has ever displayed.

The crawler does not notice. It is not built to notice. The light passes across its ceramic plating and is gone, and the bead falls into its channel with the same soft impact as every other bead in every other hour of the world's long stillness.

But the power has done it, and the power knows it has done it, and somewhere in the unbroken record of the silica needles — in a layer micrometers thick, indistinguishable to any instrument the mechanisms possess — there is now an annual band that contains, encoded in the faintest possible variation of doping density, the fact that once, on this world, a light was given that was not asked for and not received, and that the giving was enough.

Then the power withdraws, as slowly as it came, and lets the saturation of the air return to exactly what it was.

It carries away with it a quiet it did not have before. The mechanisms continue. The water films advance and retreat along their molecular tracks. The needles lengthen. The horizon repeats itself until curvature hides it.

Somewhere else, the power moves on to a place that is suffering, and helps.

This was not that kind of place. The wisdom was in knowing the difference. The benevolence was in the eighth of a second, and in leaving.
