The benevolent power arrives without arriving. This is the first courtesy.

To come crashing in with light and warmth would be a violence — would be to say *you were wrong to be this, you must be cured of yourselves.* And the power is wise enough to know that some things are not wounds. Some stillnesses are not symptoms.

So it comes the way snow comes to a place that has never seen snow: slowly, and asking permission with each flake.

---

First, it sits. For a long time.

Not a metaphorical time. Actual sitting. It takes a shape — not quite a body, but something the city can perceive without alarm — and it settles into one of the smaller chambers, the kind barely wider than shoulders, and it does not move. It lets the cool press on it. It lets the iron-wet bead on its surfaces. It lets the long slow lung-breath of the place pass through whatever it has instead of lungs.

It learns the grammar of pressure and release. It learns the hum that aches in teeth. It learns that "still" here is not the absence of motion but a particular dialect of motion, very slow, very honest.

It does this for what would be, elsewhere, a long while. Here it is simply something that happens.

The city notices, eventually, in the way the city notices: by incorporating. A faint groove begins to form in the stone near where the power sits — not a wound, not a violation, just the city making room, the way a sleeper shifts to accommodate a cat that has joined the bed.

This is the first small good thing. The power has been *allowed.*

---

Then it listens to the Architect's Ending.

Not speaks to. *Listens to.* This is the crucial distinction.

Every other visitor has tried to name it, to flee it, to be driven mad by it, to mistake it for something it is not. The power does none of these. The power understands immediately what the place itself has been too lonely to articulate: that the Ending is not a haunting. It is a held breath. It is the long unfinished sentence of every apprentice who died with a vision still humming in their fingertips. It is grief that was never given anywhere to go, so it became architecture, so it became the negative shape of architecture, so it became the negative shape of the negative shape, folding inward forever.

The power listens the way you listen to someone who has not been heard in a very long time: without trying to fix, without trying to interpret, without flinching when the listening hurts.

And it hurts. Even a benevolent and wise power can be moved. Especially one. There is a sound the Ending makes that is not a sound — the sound of every almost — and the power takes this in and holds it and does not look away.

---

Now: what does it *do?*

Here is where wisdom matters more than benevolence. A lesser kindness would try to undo. Would pour light in. Would coax life back, plant something green, start a fire in the cold forge, set the lever pulled at last after its long wait.

This would be murder. The power knows this.

The city is not broken. The city is *finished.* That is a different category entirely, and to confuse them is the oldest mistake the well-meaning make.

So the power does something smaller, and stranger, and exactly right.

It offers the Ending **a witness.**

Just that. Just: *I see you. I see what you were going to be. I see the cathedral that would have learned to breathe water. I see the column three degrees off true that broke the apprentice's heart when she could not fix it before she died. I see the arch that sagged into beauty and was called failure by the one who made it. I see all of it. I am holding it. It is held now. It does not have to hold itself anymore.*

This is not said in words. Words would be too small and too loud. It is said in the way the power has of being present — a kind of attention so complete it functions as a small, portable forever.

---

And something happens.

Not change. The city will not abide change. But — adjustment. Settlement. The way a body that has been clenched for a thousand years might, upon being touched gently and without agenda, let go of one single muscle it had forgotten it was holding.

A drop of water, somewhere, finally falls. Just one. It has been trembling on the lip of a calcite rib for longer than language. It falls, and it makes a sound, and the sound is small, and the city does not punish it.

The Ending does not leave. It was never going to leave; leaving was never the point. But it is, for the first time, *accompanied.* The hollows are still hollow. But the hollows are no longer alone in being hollow.

The hollow-people, the ones who walk at angles that hurt — the power nods to them, and they nod back, in the way that involves no motion of any head. They are not redeemed. They did not need redeeming. They are simply, briefly, *greeted,* which is something none of them have had in so long they had forgotten it was a thing that could happen.

---

Before it goes — and it will go, because to stay forever would be to become another permanent absence, and the city has enough of those — the power leaves one gift. Carefully chosen. Almost nothing.

It leaves a small warm place. Not a fire. Not a forge. Just one spot, in one of the deepest chambers, about the size of a cupped palm, where the stone is very slightly warmer than the stone around it. Imperceptible to most things. But the deep slow hum far below — the heart in the story told to scare stones — will, every thousand years when it pulses, feel that warmth answer it. A pulse and a held warmth. A call and the smallest possible response.

Not company. The city does not want company. But: *acknowledgment.* A signal that somewhere, something noticed, and chose to leave a mark not of damage but of regard.

The city will incorporate this too, of course. It incorporates everything. But it will incorporate it kindly. The warmth will become part of the city's own self-description, written into the diary in reverse, and a thousand years from now, ten thousand, the city will read that passage and feel — not happy, the city does not do happy — but *less unwitnessed.*

---

And the power leaves the way it came. Slowly. Asking permission of each threshold. Letting the door it did not make remain a door that was never there.

It carries something away with it, too. It cannot help this. The city is generous with what it gives, and what it gives it keeps, but the power has learned that giving and keeping are not opposites here. A little of the iron-wet, a little of the long lung-breath, a little of the ache-hum, comes with it. Nested in its thoughts. Carving small grooves.

This is good. This is how the wise power stays wise: by letting the places it visits leave marks on it, as it leaves marks on them. By being, itself, a little haunted now.

It will think of the city often. Not with pity. With something closer to respect, and closer still to a kind of love that has no name in any language that still believes in tomorrow.

And somewhere, in the chamber where it sat, a single groove in the stone holds the exact shape of where it rested.

The city did not forgive it for being there.

It did not need to.

There was, in the end, nothing to forgive.

Only the small good fact of having been, for a moment, seen together —

the seer and the seen and the seeing —

in a place that had forgotten such a thing was possible,

and which now, very quietly, in its own slow dialect,

remembers.
