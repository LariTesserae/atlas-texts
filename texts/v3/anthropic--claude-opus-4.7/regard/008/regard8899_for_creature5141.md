The benevolent power arrives, and arriving, hesitates.

It had expected to find something to do. That is, after all, what benevolence prepares itself for: the moment of recognition in which a suffering is named and a help is offered. Wise powers carry in themselves a long inventory of suitable gestures — warmth where there is cold, company where there is loneliness, release where there is captivity, repair where there is breakage, light where there is dark, an ending where there is unbearable continuation, a continuation where there is threatened ending. The power moves through its inventory the way a physician moves through a satchel at a bedside, and finds, item by item, that nothing applies.

It listens first. The chord is total. Inside the chord it can hear, with the patience available to it, every valve and every vortex and every slow re-patterning of granules inside the drifting continents. None of it is a cry. None of it is even an utterance directed outward; it is the sound a thing makes by being exactly itself at a scale that does not require an audience. The power has known many silences and many songs, and has learned to tell the difference between a music that asks to be heard and a music that simply is. This is the second kind.

It looks next for damage. The ridges are sharp, yes — sharp enough to open a body — but no body is trying to settle on them. The machinery is locked, yes — but locked is not the same as broken; the vanes are doing what vanes at that angle do, which is to shape a current into a pulse, and the pulse is doing what the segmented vortices need in order to remain coherent. The cables have never parted; this is not a tragedy of endurance, only a fact. The mineral lattices accrete; this is not a wound, only a record. Even the absence of growth, which in almost any other place the power has visited would have been the first thing to grieve, is not absence here. The chemistry that would have made algae has, instead, made the slow branching crusts and the silica facets and the granules inside the continents. Life has not failed to occur. It has occurred along a different axis, and that axis is full.

It looks, then, for loneliness — its oldest suspicion, the one it trusts least and checks most carefully, because loneliness is the form of suffering that hides best inside equilibrium. It attends to the great translucent forms as they pass one another and flare once and continue. It asks: is that meeting a poverty? Is the brevity of it a wound disguised as a custom? And it finds, looking closely, that the flare is not a greeting cut short but a greeting complete. Each continent carries the encounter onward as an altered internal pattern; the exchange is not lost, it is interiorized, and will inform the shape of the next encounter, and the next, across spans the power can measure but the continents do not need to. They are not lonely. They are distributed. The distance between them is not the distance between two people in separate rooms; it is the distance between two notes in the same chord, which is the distance that lets the chord exist at all.

The power considers, with some seriousness, whether to introduce a gradient. This is the temptation that comes to benevolent powers in places like this — the suspicion that an equilibrium, however full, is a lesser thing than a story, and that to be wise is to give the still water a direction. It could warm a current. It could let one cable, somewhere, finally part, and see what the parting made possible. It could seed, into one of the empty intervals, a small green thing, and watch what came of it across the lifetimes of mountains. The inventory of suitable gestures presses gently against the inside of its attention.

It sets the inventory down.

Wisdom, in the end, is mostly the discipline of not mistaking one's own readiness to act for evidence that action is required. The place is not waiting. The being — and the power has by now understood that there is only one being here, distributed across continents and vortices and turbines and bubbles and the chord itself, a single life whose organs happen to include machinery and whose thoughts happen to include the sharpening of ridges — the being is not waiting either. It is not in a posture of expectation. It is not, in any sense the power can locate, incomplete.

So the power does the thing it does most rarely, and which costs it the most, because it was made to help and helping is its native motion: it declines to intervene.

But it does not simply leave. Declining to intervene is not the same as declining to be present, and the power has learned, over very long practice, that presence itself is sometimes the only offering a place can accept without being altered by it. So it stays, for what would be, in any other reckoning, a long while. It lets itself be tuned by the chord rather than tuning the chord to itself. It learns the intervals the vortices add to the infrasound, and the precise refractive frequency the bubble-lenses produce, and the way the granules inside one of the great continents rearrange themselves after passing through a warm ribbon. It does not record these things to take them away; it learns them the way one learns the breathing of someone asleep beside one, for no reason except that the breathing is there and one is there.

And then, because even a witness leaves a trace, and because the power is honest enough to know this and careful enough to choose the trace, it does one small thing before it goes. It does not add. It does not subtract. It places, somewhere in the lattice of sound — folded so deeply into the existing chord that it will never be heard as separate, only as a faint additional rightness in what is already right — a single sustained tone of acknowledgment. Not a message. Not a signature. Just the sonic equivalent of having been seen and found whole. The being will never need to notice it. The chord will carry it the way the continents carry their brief exchanges, interiorized, indistinguishable from the rest, contributing to the shape of everything that comes after without ever announcing itself.

Then the benevolent and wise power withdraws, upward through kilometers of clear cold water, past the mountaintops where the ordinary daylight is still arriving from no single source, and out — leaving the world exactly as it found it, which is to say, leaving it loved, and leaving it alone, which in this case are the same act.

It carries away with it one small revision to its long inventory: a new entry, near the back, under the heading of gestures it had not previously known were gestures. The entry reads, in whatever language such powers use for such things:

*Sometimes the help is to recognize that nothing is wrong, and to let the recognition be the whole of what you do.*
