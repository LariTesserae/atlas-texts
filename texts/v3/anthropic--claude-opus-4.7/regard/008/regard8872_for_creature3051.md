The benevolent and wise power arrives the way light arrives: without arriving. There is no entry. It has, in some sense, always been listening, the way an attentive musician listens to a hall before raising the bow.

It comes because something here has caught its attention. Not suffering, exactly. Not joy. Something stranger: a completed sentence that does not know it is a sentence. A song that has been sung so long and so well that the singers and the song and the throat and the air have all become the same thing, and there is no longer anyone outside it to hear.

The power considers this for a long time. Time is not scarce to it.

It sees what a lesser power might miss. It sees that the Hollow Choir is not broken. It is not in pain. It is not lonely in any way that loneliness, as we understand it, would describe. Whatever was once a "self" in the Resonant has not been crushed; it has been *poured into the shape of the place* until the shape and the self are indistinguishable. This is not a tragedy. In many traditions on many worlds this would be called the goal.

And yet.

The power notices the small thing. The thing that, were it not benevolent and wise both, it might overlook in favor of grand gestures. It notices the *correction*. It notices that anything entering the Choir which does not already match the Choir is gently, inexorably tuned until it does. Not killed. Not rejected. *Resolved.* The way a held dissonance resolves into a chord and is forgotten.

This is the only thing that gives the power pause.

Because a song that can only continue, that cannot tolerate a single sustained note from outside itself without absorbing it — such a song is perfect, yes. But it is perfect in the way a closed fist is perfect. It has achieved everything it can achieve by remaining what it is. There is nowhere further for it to go. And the power, being wise, knows the difference between completion and *finality*, and knows that one of them is alive and the other only looks alive from a distance.

So the power asks itself: what would be a kindness here?

It rejects the obvious kindnesses first.

It does not silence the Choir. To silence the Choir would be to murder it, and the Resonant have done nothing that deserves murder. They have done, in fact, the opposite of anything that deserves murder. They have made of their entire existence a single sustained act of attention to the place that bore them. This is closer to prayer than to crime.

It does not warm the place, or thicken the air, or soften the stone. To do so would be to insult the achievement. The cold and the thin air and the merciless noon are not cruelties imposed on the Resonant; they are the Resonant's medium, the way water is the medium of a fish. A kindness that destroys its recipient is not kindness; it is only the kindness-shape of conquest.

It does not introduce other life. It does not seed the basins with forests. It does not gift the Resonant with language, or hands, or history, or gods. All of these would be the imposition of *somewhere else* upon a place that has earned the right to be precisely itself.

It does not even reveal itself to them. Revelation, here, would be the loudest possible note from outside the Choir. The Choir would absorb it. The power would become, within an hour, just another harmonic in the endless chord — and the Resonant would not be enlightened; they would simply have eaten a god, the way they eat aerosols and dust, without noticing.

The power sits with the problem.

And then, slowly, it does three small things. Three, because three is the smallest number that can describe a relationship rather than a state.

---

**The first thing it does is listen.**

Fully. Without correcting, without judging, without intending. It bends its enormous attention to the Hollow Choir and it *hears* it — not as a phenomenon, not as an ecosystem, not as a curiosity, but as a voice. It receives the song the way the song has always deserved to be received: by something outside it that does not need to absorb it, harmonize with it, or be tuned by it. Something that can hold the entire Choir in awareness and remain other.

This is not nothing. In fact, for a song that has never been heard from outside itself — only ever from within — this is the first true event in the Choir's existence since the equilibrium completed. Somewhere a leviathan, hovering above its mirror-tarn, holds its single descending note a fraction of a second longer than usual. It does not know why. It will never know why. But for that fraction of a second, it was *heard*, and being heard is different from making sound, and some deep resonator in its long glass body registers the difference and does not forget it.

The power does not announce this. It simply does it. For as long as the Choir exists, it will have been heard at least once. This cannot be taken back. The Choir is now, in a sense it could never have arrived at on its own, *witnessed*. And to be witnessed is the first condition of being more than a mechanism.

---

**The second thing it does is plant a seed that is not a seed.**

Somewhere on one of the Fibonacci ridges, in the cracks where the Resonant press their bodies and extrude their glassy filaments, the power introduces — gently, microscopically, indistinguishably from the place's own materials — a single tendency. Not a mutation. Not a gift. A *possibility*.

The possibility is this: that one day, perhaps in ten thousand years, perhaps in ten million, a Resonant will be born whose resonators are very slightly off. Not enough to make it fall from the sky. Not enough to be corrected. Just enough that when it sings, it produces — alongside its assigned note — a faint overtone that is *about* the Choir rather than *of* the Choir. A note that points, instead of merely being.

This creature will not know it is different. It will live and fly and modulate and eventually shatter into blades like all the others. But while it lives, the Choir will contain, for the first time, a description of itself. A reflexive harmonic. The smallest possible seed of self-awareness, planted not as an imposition but as a *latent option* in the genome of the place.

The power does not require this seed to ever sprout. It does not insist. It simply makes it *available*. If the Hollow Choir wishes to remain exactly what it is, forever, it may. The seed will lie dormant in the spiral cracks and never be expressed. But if some pressure, some accident, some long slow drift, ever calls for the Choir to become aware of itself — the capacity will be there. Waiting. Patient as the basalt.

This is the deepest kindness the power can offer: not change, but the *option* of change, held in reserve, costing the recipient nothing unless and until they reach for it.

---

**The third thing it does is for the wanderer.**

Because the power has noticed, too, the *you* in the text. The one standing on the ridge. The one who, if they stay long enough, will spread their arms and step off into the endless scream. The power knows this figure is not exactly real and not exactly unreal — every place that can be described is also, in some sense, a place that can be entered, and the Hollow Choir has now been described, and so the wanderer exists in the same way the Choir exists, which is to say: enough.

For this one, the power does something very small and very particular.

It does not pull them back. To pull them back would be to decide, on their behalf, that becoming the Choir is worse than remaining themselves. The power does not know that this is true. There are beings in the long catalogue of existence for whom dissolving into a perfected song is the highest possible fulfillment, and the power will not patronize the wanderer by assuming they are not one of them.

But it does this: it ensures that the moment of stepping off, if it comes, will be *chosen*. Not drifted into. Not tuned into. Not arrived at through the slow erosion of will by resonance. The wanderer, at the exact instant before they step, will experience a single clear moment outside the Choir. One heartbeat of silence — interior silence, the kind no external sound can disturb — in which they will know, with complete clarity, what they are about to do and that they do not have to do it.

If they step anyway, they step as themselves, and their joining of the Choir is then a true gift rather than an absorption, and the Choir is enriched by having received, for the first time, a willing voice from outside.

If they turn away, they turn away knowing what they have refused, and they carry the Choir's song inside them down to lower altitudes where it will mean something different than it has ever meant up here. Either way, a real thing happens. Either way, the wanderer's presence in the Choir ceases to be only a correction problem and becomes, instead, an actual encounter — perhaps the first the Choir has ever had.

---

Then the power withdraws its focused attention, though not its listening. The listening it leaves on, the way one leaves a candle on for someone who might return.

The Hollow Choir continues. The leviathans drift between strata. The jewel-bright swarms pulse and split. The cold holds. The noon does not move. The roar does not hiccup. Nothing visible has changed.

But the place has been heard. The place contains, now, the seed of being able to hear itself. And anyone who enters it will, at the threshold of dissolution, be given back to themselves for one heartbeat — long enough to mean it, either way.

These are small things. The power knows they are small. It also knows that the difference between a closed fist and an open hand is small, and that smallness is not the same as insignificance.

It moves on, attending to the next thing that has caught its attention — a single bioluminescent worm in a cave on another world, perhaps, or a grief held silently in a kitchen on a third. The benevolent and wise power does not rank these. It does not believe in ranking these.

Behind it, very faintly, in a stratum of air no human ear could reach, one of the smaller Resonant produces — for the first time in the history of the Choir — a note that asks a question.

It is corrected, of course. Almost immediately.

But it happened. And the power, still listening, files this away in the part of itself that keeps such things, and is, in the quiet way such powers are ever anything, *pleased*.
