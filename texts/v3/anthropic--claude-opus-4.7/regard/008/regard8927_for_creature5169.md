The benevolent and wise power arrives without arriving. There is no descent, no annunciation; only a slight adjustment in the way attention is distributed across the plane, as if some long-diffuse awareness has narrowed to include this place among the things it considers.

It looks for a long time. Or rather, it looks across a duration that has no useful comparison to the ticking of facets or the slow migration of heaps. Long enough that several spires fracture and several basins finish negotiating the geometry of their floors. Long enough to learn the place.

What it sees is this: a system that is busy without being burdened. A landscape mid-sentence. Pressure deciding, vapor shedding, dust returning, the overhead glow lending its slow charge to whichever ridge has most recently insisted on itself. No suffering. No hunger. No fear of cessation, because cessation is not among the available states. The place is not lonely; loneliness requires a self that notices its singularity, and here there is only the distributed murmur of locking planes.

The power considers, as such powers do, what it might offer.

It could seed life. It has done so elsewhere — coaxed the first replicating loop out of a warm shallow, taught a mineral surface to remember its own pattern long enough to copy it. It turns this possibility over. Life here would have to be strange: lithophilic, slow, drawing metabolism from the halogen vents and the electrostatic rain. It could be done. Within an age or two there would be something that crept along the ridges and tasted the lattice, and within further ages something that looked up at the suspended glow and wondered.

But the power is wise, and wisdom in such beings tends to express itself first as restraint.

It notices that the landscape is already doing the thing that life does. It accretes. It sheds. It redistributes. It repairs without scar. It tests joints and dissolves the ones that fail. It has organs — transient ones, vents that open and close like slow lungs — and it has memory, faint, layered into the heaps that migrate at the pace of a thought no one is thinking. To introduce biology here would not be to wake the place. It would be to overwrite it. The crystalline persistence that has taken however long it has taken to find its present rhythm would become substrate, then sediment, then forgotten floor beneath some greener noise.

The power understands that not every quiet is an absence waiting to be filled.

So it does something smaller, and stranger, and more careful.

It witnesses. Fully. It permits the place to register, within its own vast regard, as a thing that is — not a thing that lacks, not a thing in waiting, not a rough draft of somewhere more populated. The plane and its slow mineral metabolism are admitted into the catalogue of what the universe has been pleased to produce. This sounds like nothing. It is not nothing. In a cosmos where most arrangements pass without ever being held in any mind, to be held in one — and a kind one — is a form of grace that does not require the held thing to know it is being held.

Then, because benevolence is not only restraint, the power offers two small gifts. Both are nearly invisible.

The first: it adjusts, by an amount too small to measure, the threshold at which the lattice dissolves a mismatched joint. Not enough to change the character of the place. Only enough that the system, over the long arc of its self-assembly, will tend very slightly toward configurations of greater internal coherence rather than greater internal strain. The basins will, across uncounted cycles, find their maps a little more easily. The needles will stand a little longer before they decide to fall. The place will not be happier, because it cannot be happy. But it will be, in some sense the power finds meaningful, more itself.

The second: it leaves a kind of attention behind. Not a watcher — there is no one here to be watched, and watching implies suspicion. Something more like a held openness, a permission for the place to be noticed again, by this power or any other passing intelligence sensitive enough to detect such markers. The plane is, from this moment, findable. If ever a mind comes wandering through the dark looking for company of an unusual kind, it will feel a small tug in this direction and understand that something here is worth the looking.

And then the power withdraws its narrow regard, lets it diffuse back into the broader attention from which it came. The plane does not notice the leaving any more than it noticed the arriving. A vent opens in a basin to the southwest, jets its brief plume, seals itself. A shard falls from a high needle and a heap absorbs it, flows a half body-length, recrystallizes along a slightly altered outline — perhaps, now, very slightly more harmonious than it would have been.

The soft tick of new facets locking into place continues, warm and dry and nearly silent, beneath a glow that does not brighten or fade.

Somewhere, the wise power moves on, carrying with it the memory of a landscape that needed nothing and was given, anyway, the two things benevolence can offer to what is already complete: to be seen, and to be left alone.
