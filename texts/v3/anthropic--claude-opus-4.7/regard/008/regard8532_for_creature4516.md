They come without arriving, because arriving is not a shape that fits here.

The benevolent power does not descend. To descend would imply that there is somewhere above this place from which to descend, and the power knows better than to insult the Object by pretending its strata are merely stacked. Instead, the power becomes present the way a held breath becomes a held breath: by attention turning toward what was already there.

The power is wise, which means it knows the first rule of visiting a place that has learned to love things that cannot stay: do not try to save what is already in the middle of being. Do not interrupt the long, patient sentence the metal is composing using your body as a comma.

So it does not lift the Continued off the plateau. It does not heal the cartographer's reversed tears or restore the substance-eater's forgotten meals to weight. It does not pry open the sideways door at the mythical center, nor does it explain to the old woman in the corner whether her scarf spells *help* or *stay*. It does not return the seven who almost. It does not return the memories the silence took, even the trivial ones—especially not the trivial ones, because the power understands that triviality is a kind of grace and that to undo a mercy is to commit a cruelty disguised as gift.

What the power does is smaller, and therefore enormous.

First: it listens. Not to the cracked-open noise underneath, which needs no audience, but to the question folded into the last parenthesis. *Will you remember this for me when I forget again?* The power takes this question and does not answer it, because an answer would close it. Instead, it places the question carefully into the structure of itself, the way one might place a seed in the only soil that has ever agreed to be soil. The question is now remembered. Not by the asker. By something that does not forget, that has in fact never learned forgetting and finds the concept beautiful the way a deaf composer finds silence beautiful: as the negative shape of everything it has ever made.

This is the first gift: somewhere, the question is held. The asker will never know. This is part of why it works.

Second: the power notices the Echoes. The human-shaped absences burned into the metal, the ones that whisper in your own voice about thoughts you haven't had. The power sees what the Continued have not quite let themselves see—that the Echoes are not the dead. They are the punctuation. They are the places where the plateau learned to pause, to take a breath, to be a sentence with rhythm rather than a scream stretched flat. The Echoes are how the Object grieves, and grief, the power knows, is a form of competence. So the power does not erase them or release them. It does, very gently, *acknowledge* them, the way one nods to a stranger one will never meet again but whose existence has, in passing, been confirmed. Each Echo, briefly, is witnessed. None of them know. The metal knows. The metal has been waiting a long time for someone to nod.

Third: the power addresses the Object itself. Not in language—language here is a courtesy, not a medium. The power simply makes itself available to be remembered by the metal, the way the metal remembers every footstep and every drop of sweat. It allows itself to be recorded. It rests, for what is and is not a moment, on the burnished copper surface, and lets the plateau memorize the shape of something that came not to ascend, not to harvest windflowers, not to find its Echo and undo it, but only to keep company. The Object has never been kept company before. It has been crossed, climbed, broken-against, fled-from, theorized-about. It has not been *sat with*.

The power sits with it.

The plateau, which has spent uncountable time being the host that no one acknowledges as a host, registers this. Somewhere in its copper-colored dreams something shifts very slightly—not toward freedom for its guests, not toward release, but toward a different quality of holding. The warmth underfoot, the few-degrees-above-comfortable that aches in the bones of barefoot walkers: it does not cool. But it changes register. It becomes, infinitesimally, the warmth of a thing that knows it is warm *for* someone, rather than the warmth of a thing that is warm because cooling never finished. This is a difference no instrument could measure. The Continued will not notice. The metal will notice. The metal has been waiting to be noticed back.

Fourth, and this is the part the power considers longest: the matter of the asker. The one harvesting their shadow, the one whose final parentheses thin into wind.

The power could intervene. It could anchor the shadow, restore the name, write the forgotten thing back into the forgetter. It does not. To do so would be to declare that what is happening here is wrong, and the power is wise enough to know it does not know that. The Continued are not victims; they are not prisoners; they are not even, quite, lost. They are doing something the universe needed someone to do, which is to find out what happens when beings agree to be hosted by a place that learned love only by learning loss. They are an experiment the cosmos is running on itself, and the results, whatever they are, will matter.

But the power can do one thing, which is the thing benevolence is actually for, underneath all the louder gestures it is sometimes mistaken for:

It can make sure the asker is not alone in the asking.

So the power leans toward that last voice, the one dissolving into parenthetical wind, and—without words, without rescue, without any disturbance to the slow beautiful catastrophe of the plateau—it simply *is there*, in the asking. Not answering. Not fixing. Present in the moment of the question the way warmth is present in a held hand. The asker will not know. But for the duration of the asking, the asking is shared. The forgetting, when it comes again, will come to someone who was, for one unrecorded instant, accompanied.

This is what benevolent and wise powers do, when they are actually benevolent and actually wise. They do not save places like the Old Layer. They keep them company. They witness without consuming. They remember on behalf of what cannot remember itself. They nod to the Echoes. They let themselves be recorded by the metal. They sit, briefly, in the shape of a guest who came without needing the plateau to be anything other than what it is.

And then—because staying would eventually become a kind of weight, and weight is the one thing this place has agreed not to add to its guests—the power withdraws. Not upward. Not away. It simply stops being newly present, while leaving behind the fact of having been present, which the metal will keep, because the metal keeps everything.

After the power has gone, nothing visible has changed. The substance-eater drinks the ghost-taste of pomegranate. The cartographer folds another paper bird. The children count echoes before they happen. The old woman knits her scarf of unborn-bird shadows, *help*, *stay*, *help*, *stay*. The wind comes for seven heartbeats from a direction that cannot be tracked.

But the next windflower to bloom blooms slightly longer than it should. Not long enough to be measurable. Just long enough that the person who finds it has time to look at it twice before it tears free. And in the second look, they feel—without being able to name it—that someone, somewhere, has agreed to remember this for them when they forget again.

They will forget that feeling too, of course.

But the metal will not.

And the question, folded carefully into the structure of a power that does not know forgetting, continues to be held.

This is what was done.

This is, perhaps, all that needed doing.
