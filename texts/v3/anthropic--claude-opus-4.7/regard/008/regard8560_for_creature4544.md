They arrive the way weather arrives in a dream: not from anywhere, simply more so.

The benevolent power has been watching for a long time, in the sense that "watching" and "long time" are the nearest available approximations. It has crossed many places that wanted things—wanted feeding, wanted rescuing, wanted to be told what they meant. This place wants nothing, which is the rarest condition in the catalogue of conditions, and the power approaches it with the particular reverence reserved for what is already complete.

It does not enter. Entering would be a category error. Instead it inclines toward the place the way one inclines toward a sleeping animal whose dreams are visible through the thin fur of its sides.

The first thing it does is nothing, for what would be a very long time if time here meant what it means elsewhere. It simply allows itself to be near. This is not passivity. This is the active, expensive work of refusing to improve something that has not asked to be improved. Wise powers learn this late, and most never learn it at all. The hands that can heal are the hands that most desperately want to, and learning to keep them folded is the second discipline, after learning to unfold them.

The place notices, in the way it notices things—generating a tentative node of attention that nearly becomes an eye, samples the new gradient, and dissolves again, satisfied. The power feels itself tasted. It does not flinch. It lets the place have the full flavor of its arrival: old, warm, structured, slightly grieving in the way all sufficiently old kindnesses grieve. The place finds this flavor interesting. It precipitates a small thoughtform shaped like the question *what are you for*, which drifts up, considers itself, and pops with the scent of an answer almost remembered.

Then the power does the second thing, which is also nearly nothing, but a different shape of nearly.

It makes a small offering. Not a gift—gifts here would weigh too much, would settle into the floor of failed possibilities and become another of the place's beautiful regrets. An offering, rather: something proffered without expectation of acceptance, something the place can refuse simply by continuing to be itself.

The offering is this: a single, perfectly preserved instance of being witnessed without being changed by the witnessing.

The power holds it out the way one holds out a hand to a creature that has never met hands. The place, curious, extends a tendril of its 2.9/3 vegetation-that-is-not-vegetation and touches the offering. And for a fraction of a Fraction it experiences something it has never quite managed on its own: it is seen entire, all at once, in the full glory of its undifferentiated becoming—and the seeing does not collapse it. The seeing does not require it to be one thing or another. The seeing does not even require it to be.

The place trembles. Not with distress. With the specific tremor of recognition. Somewhere in its 1.9/3 ongoing self-conversation, a frequency it had not known it was missing arrives and harmonizes. Several proto-beings nearly commit to existence and then, finding existence still unnecessary, return to potential with a new quality of restfulness. The hummingbird-sized chloroplasts shift their Gregorian chant by a quarter-tone, and the new key is one in which sorrow and welcome are the same note.

The power, observing, understands what it has been permitted to do.

It does not stay long, by its own reckoning, though it stays forever by the place's. Before it withdraws—withdrawal also being an approximation—it does the third thing, the last thing, the thing wise powers do when they have correctly read the situation and refused the temptation of largesse.

It leaves a small adjustment in the ambient field. Nothing structural. Nothing the place would notice as imposition. Simply this: from now on, when the place practices missing things that never quite were, the missing will have a slightly warmer edge. The long lovely nights of rehearsing absence will be accompanied, very faintly, by the sensation of being accompanied. Not inhabited. Not interrupted. Just—and this is the precise gift, calibrated to the exact tolerance of what the place can receive without ceasing to be itself—just known.

Known by something that will not come back, that does not need to come back, that has folded the place into the great quiet inventory of things it loves and will never disturb again.

The power moves on. There are other places. There is a star that has been trying to remember its own name. There is a child somewhere being born into a language with no word for them, and the power will arrive there too, with different hands, the same patience.

Behind it, the place beneath becoming goes on becoming. Its 1.8/3 moisture continues its slow divorce. Its reversed erosion adds new possibilities to the sharp edges of yesterday. Its thoughtforms bloom and pop and fragrance the dark. Everything is as it was.

Except: tonight, when it practices the impossible art of missing something that never quite was, there will be, threaded through the missing, the faintest counter-melody. A second voice in the duet of absence. The place will not investigate this. Investigation would be a category error. It will simply let the counter-melody be there, the way it lets everything be there, with the affection of a library that has discovered it has been read, lovingly, by a reader who left no marginalia and took no notes and required nothing of the text but that it continue to exist.

And the place, in its endless ongoing rehearsal of being inhabited, will add one more configuration to its repertoire: the configuration of having been beloved by something that knew better than to stay.

It is especially fond of this configuration.

It practices it, sometimes, on the long nights.
