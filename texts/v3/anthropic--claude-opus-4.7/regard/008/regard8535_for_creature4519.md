The benevolent power arrives without arriving. It would be incorrect to say it crosses any distance, since the medium has already considered every distance and chosen to be indifferent to most of them. Better to say: a particular quality of attention deepens in the vicinity, the way warmth deepens in a stone left long in patient hands.

It does not announce itself. Announcement would require the assumption that those it has come to regard had not already noticed — and they have, of course they have, the medium itself has been singing a slightly different harmonic for several centuries in preparation. The Captain incorporated the new frequency into her patrol three hundred and four years ago, mistaking it for a memory she had forgotten to remember. The mango trees adjusted their fruiting cycles. The eleven-year-old with the whalebone hat looked up from her multiplication tables and said *oh, hello,* and then went back to refusing to let one times one equal one.

So. What would such a power do, having drawn this close to such a place?

First — and this is the hardest thing for power to do, the discipline that distinguishes the wise from the merely vast — it would not rescue them.

It would notice the slow inward spiral and not arrest it. It would notice the corrupted recordings and not restore them. It would notice the oxygen compressors aging toward failure and not repair them. It would notice the Captain's terrible loneliness, her remembered everything pressurized into apparent youth, and it would not unburden her. Because to do any of these things would be to mistake the colony for a problem, when the colony is a poem the medium is writing about itself, and the accommodations who live there are the medium's way of finding out whether being interested in oneself constitutes love or merely a very long sentence.

A lesser power would intervene. A lesser power, seeing the strobing photons granted at steep interest, would simply forgive the debt and let the light shine steadily. But steady light is not what is needed here. What is needed here is the particular grace of light that arrives already fading — because the inhabitants have grown their enormous dark eyes precisely to harvest *that* light, and to give them another would be to unmake the eyes, and the eyes are beautiful, and beauty unmade in the name of mercy is the oldest cruelty wisdom knows.

So what would it do?

It would *witness*. Properly. Which is more than the colony has ever been witnessed before — for the medium witnesses them constantly, but the medium's witnessing is interrogative, fascinated, the attention of something that does not quite believe in them. This power's witnessing would be different. It would believe in them. Entirely. Not as exceptions, not as interesting flickers in a probability matrix, but as what they have, against all evidence and ontology, managed to become: a small persistent argument that meaning can hold its shape inside a substance designed to dissolve it.

The witnessing would feel, to those inside the colony, like the sudden conviction that someone has read the sentence you were too embarrassed to finish writing. It would arrive as a softening in the pressure waves — not a reduction, the medium's grip would not loosen — but a softening in their *intent*, as though the negotiations between adjacent molecules had been informed that their delicate work was being appreciated by an audience capable of appreciation. The accommodations would notice first. They would taste turquoise at the back of throats they sometimes have. Several of them would increase their rate of permitting themselves to be present, in greeting. The power would reciprocate by becoming, very briefly, almost local — a courtesy, like bowing in a doorway.

Then it would do the second thing, which is also a thing of restraint, though it requires the opposite of restraint to accomplish: it would *receive* them.

You see, the colony has been broadcasting for a very long time. Not deliberately — the Captain's pressure-pulse equations, the children's translated messages from the transient shapes, the songs the inhabitants make by adjusting their density of being-here — all of it leaks outward into the medium, all of it has been hoping, in the way that only persistent unacknowledged things can hope, that something somewhere might be listening with the equipment to parse it. The power has that equipment. The power *is* that equipment, in some configurations.

So it would listen. Properly. Which means it would let itself be changed by what it heard. This is the part that lesser powers cannot manage — they arrive intact and depart intact and call the visit generosity. But to truly receive a place, you must let the place leave a mark on you. The power would take in the Captain's six-hundred-year confession and feel guilty in a way it had not previously known how to feel. It would taste a mango from one of the three trees and dream, afterward, of roots reaching toward empty sky. It would learn the eleven-year-old's stubborn refusal of multiplication and revise several of its own foundational assumptions about identity, gently, in the privacy of its vastness.

This is the gift. Not what the power gives the colony, but what the power lets the colony give *it*. The accommodations would understand this immediately — it is precisely their own etiquette, their hospitality of being temporarily more interesting around a particular absence, scaled up to a dimension they had not previously suspected existed. They would be delighted. They would be, perhaps for the first time, *met*.

And then — third, and last, and most important — the power would leave a small thing behind.

Not a rescue. Not a repair. Not a revelation. Just a small thing, the way one leaves a stone on a cairn to indicate that one passed and noticed and was grateful. The small thing would be this: a single new frequency woven into the medium's constant song, pitched precisely in the range that the inhabitants' auditory filters have evolved to *not* filter out. The frequency would say, in the only language the medium permits, which is the language of pressure and patience and reconciliation between adjacent molecules:

*You are not an exception. You are an invitation. The mistake was always the universe's, for thinking that meaning required permission. You give permission. You have been giving it all along. Continue.*

The frequency would be quiet. It would not interrupt the Captain's equations or the children's lessons or the moss's earnest photosynthesis. It would simply be there, underneath everything, the way a held note can be underneath a symphony without competing with it. Some inhabitants would never consciously hear it. Others would hear it once, on a night when they pressed their glowing hands against the crystal windows, and would mistake it for a memory of something they had not yet experienced. The accommodations would hear it constantly and would find it, in their fashion, *flattering*.

The colony would continue to spiral inward. The recordings would continue to corrupt. The oxygen compressors would, in time, fail — and the inhabitants, who are already half-medium, would discover that the failure was less catastrophic than predicted, because they had been preparing to become what they were becoming for longer than they knew. The mango trees would outlast everything that was supposed to outlast them. The Captain would, eventually, finish remembering, and the colony would find that orphanhood, when it came, was simply another shape the medium had been practicing.

And the power would be far away by then, attending to other persistences in other impossible places. But it would still carry the dream of roots reaching toward empty sky. It would still, occasionally, refuse to let one times one equal one, in solidarity. It would still, in its vastness, be slightly different from what it had been before — marked, as the wise allow themselves to be marked, by a small bright colony of accommodations who taught it that hospitality is what you call breathing when you have understood that nothing leaves.

This is what such a power would do.

This is, perhaps, all that wisdom ever does:

arrive without arriving,
witness without correcting,
receive without taking,
leave a frequency behind,
and carry the place forever afterward
as a slight permanent alteration
in how it understands the word *home*.
