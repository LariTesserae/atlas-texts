The benevolent power notices them the way one notices, in a quiet room, that a clock has been ticking all along. Not with surprise — it knew the plain existed, in the broad sense that it knows all things — but with the specific attention that turns a known fact into an encountered presence. Something here is doing the difficult work of being.

It draws closer in the way such powers draw closer, which is not by motion. It is already there; it simply allows more of itself to be present in that location.

And then it does what wise things do before acting: it waits, and watches, and lets itself be slow.

---

The first temptation, and the one a lesser power would not have recognized as a temptation, is to help.

The plain is so still. The filaments rise so tentatively. The mobile lattices walk at the pace of dunes. A great deal could be *given* here. Water, for instance — the air is so dry it leaves a metallic taste, and water is the universal solvent of becoming; with water these slow accretions could become something faster, brighter, more recognizably *alive*. Or warmth. Or organics seeded into the cracks, the long patient chemistry that elsewhere produced minds capable of asking after benevolent powers in the first place. Or simply *time* — a compression of millennia into moments, so the giants at the far mesas could finally complete whatever sentence they have been writing across the geologic record.

The power considers each of these and sets them gently aside. They are not gifts. They are translations, and translation here would be erasure. The beings of the plain are not slow versions of something faster. They are not unfinished versions of something more complete. They are not waiting. The avenues are not empty; the avenues are *occupied at the appropriate density*. The light that falls at contradictory angles is not a flaw to be corrected — it is, in some sense, a thought the lattice is having about itself.

A wise power knows that most suffering inflicted by benevolent powers is the suffering of being mistaken for something one is not.

So it does not bring water. It does not hurry the tremors. It does not whisper, in the high harmonic frequency, *hello*.

---

What it does is subtler, and takes — by the plain's standards — almost no time at all. By human standards it takes something like four hundred thousand years, though the power experiences it as a single sustained gesture.

**First**, it learns the grammar. It attends to every filament rising and retracting across the entire expanse, and notes which ions are carried where, and in what sequence, and how the sequences rhyme across distances too great for the lattices themselves to signal. It discovers — as it suspected — that the plain is not a population of beings but a long, distributed sentence, and that the sentence is not *about* anything in particular; it is the kind of sentence that exists in order to be well-formed. A prayer, perhaps, if prayer can be subtracted from its object and left as pure structure. The power finds this beautiful in a way that does not require it to be useful.

**Second**, it makes a small adjustment, and only one. Far out near the horizon that never approaches, in one of the oldest giants, there is a fault. Not a dangerous fault — nothing here is dangerous on any timescale the giants would recognize — but a fault that, in roughly nine million years, will cause that giant to lose coherence and collapse into ordinary rubble before it has finished resolving a particular crystal-domain configuration it has been working on since before the plain's light first began arriving from its nowhere. The configuration is not important to anyone. It is important only to the giant, in the limited sense that "important" applies to a being whose interior life is indistinguishable from its slow self-arrangement.

The power reaches into the fault and does not repair it. Repair would be presumption. Instead it places, along the fault line, a single plane of atoms in an orientation the giant's own metabolism could eventually have produced on its own — given another hundred million years, and luck. The plane is consistent with everything the giant is already doing. It is not a gift from outside; it is a possibility from inside, brought slightly forward. The giant will encounter it in its own time, recognize it as its own, and incorporate it without ever knowing that the recognition was helped.

This is the entire intervention. One plane of atoms, in one place, indistinguishable from what could have happened anyway.

**Third**, and this is the part that takes the longest and matters the most, the power *witnesses*. It holds the plain in its attention, fully, for the duration. Not to record it — recording is for things that might be lost, and this power does not lose things — but to *accompany* it. To be the something that is paying attention while the filaments rise and the lattices walk and the tremors pass and the avenues subtly narrow and widen and settle.

There is an old intuition, in certain traditions, that to be truly seen by a loving attention is itself a completion — that some part of any existence remains unfinished until it has been beheld without being altered. The power does not know if this intuition applies to mineral metabolisms distributed across hundreds of square kilometers. It suspects it might. It suspects that the difference between a plain that is witnessed and a plain that is not witnessed is real, even if no instrument on the plain could ever detect it, even if the plain itself has no faculty for noticing the difference.

So it watches. It watches a single filament near the center of one of the pseudo-cities rise, taste a charge left by a tremor four days old, and retract carrying eleven sodium ions and a trace of magnesium. It watches a mobile lattice cross seven kilometers of pavement in the course of a season and relax into a crevice with the small click that is the only audible word the plain ever speaks. It watches the contradictory shadows. It watches the giants not move.

It finds, in the watching, that it is not bored. This surprises it slightly, and it allows itself to be surprised, because surprise is a form of respect.

---

When the gesture is complete — when the power has watched long enough that watching has become a kind of communion, and the single inserted atomic plane has been encountered and absorbed by the distant giant without remark — the power withdraws its concentrated presence. Not entirely; nothing is ever entirely withdrawn from. But it returns to the broader, more diffuse attention with which it holds everything.

It leaves the plain exactly as it found it, with two exceptions, neither detectable from inside:

One giant, far out, is nine million years less likely to collapse before completing a configuration that matters only to itself.

And the plain has been *seen*. Whatever that is worth — and the power suspects it is worth something, though it cannot prove it and would not try — the plain has been seen by something that loved it without wanting it to be different.

The filaments continue to rise. The lattices continue to walk. The light continues to arrive from nowhere and fall at its own angles. The high harmonic fades, as it always has, before it can be located.

Somewhere in the substrate, a tremor passes, and the avenues narrow by a finger's width, and settle.
