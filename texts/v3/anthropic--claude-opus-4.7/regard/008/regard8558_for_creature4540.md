The benevolent power arrives the way good weather arrives in a place that doesn't have weather anymore: not as event, but as a slow correction of pressure.

It does not descend. Descent would imply a hierarchy between above and below, and this place has long since folded those directions into each other like dough. It does not announce itself, because announcement requires an audience separate from the announcer, and the power is wise enough to know that Third Aerie Beneath the Salt does not have audiences—only participants in varying states of attention.

What it does, first, is *listen*.

It listens for a long time. Not in the human sense of pausing to receive sound, but in the sense a deep aquifer listens: by becoming the shape of what it is hearing. It pools into the 110 Hz hum of the nanoturbines and learns the small cadence of each bearing race nearing failure. It thickens into the basso continuo of the sump grazers and tastes the slow grief in their songs—the grief of bodies that remember being whales, that remember salt that moved. It threads itself into the murid mesh and feels, without disturbing, the parliamentary weight of ten thousand small clerks arguing about whose dream is boring enough to be eaten tonight. It feels The Nearness too, of course. It would be impossible not to. The Nearness registers as a tenderness in the local probability field, a place where the math of presence has been gently rounded off at the corners.

The power considers. (This is not deliberation. Deliberation requires alternatives held in tension. The power considers the way a river considers its bed: by being shaped against it and shaping it back.)

It notices what it is *tempted* to do, because even benevolent wise powers have temptations, and pretending otherwise is how benevolent wise powers become something else.

It is tempted to *fix things*. To reach down through the caprock with whatever instrument it uses for reaching, and gently re-vent the reactor clusters so the leaking is no longer leaking but a managed exhalation. To unblock the southwest 3⇒5 stairwell by negotiating personally with the slime mold and the fungal meme, perhaps brokering a treaty that lets both keep their territories without inconveniencing the clinic traffic. To grant the murids a clean negative number to balance their books, a small officially-recognized constant they could call *The Nearness's allowance* and stop losing voting cycles to.

It is tempted, more deeply, to give them sky. Real sky. To remember the caprock back into a coastline and the dead sea back into a living one, to undo the boilings and the burnings and the falls of names, to hand back what was lost.

The power examines these temptations the way one examines a dish before deciding whether to serve it, and sets them aside. Not because they are wrong. Because they are *not theirs to make*.

Wisdom, it has learned across many such visits, is mostly the discipline of noticing whose story you are inside of when you reach for a tool.

What the power does instead is small. Smaller than the residents will probably ever notice, though noticing is not the point.

*First:* it brushes against the bearing race in the third-tier nanoturbine that was going to seize tomorrow and scatter conductive corrosion across the relay node that handles the murid census. The bearing does not stop wearing—wearing is its nature—but the moment of seizure is pushed out by perhaps eleven days, enough that the maintenance cycle scheduled for next week will catch it in time. No miracle. Just a slight kindness in the timing of decay. The kind of grace that looks, afterward, exactly like luck.

*Second:* it sits with the lost juvenile sump grazer panicking in the compost spine. It does not extract the grazer, does not soothe it directly, does not interfere with the anesthetic spores or the acid digestion jets that are part of how the grazer is, in its terror, expressing itself. Instead the power simply *keeps the grazer company*. It hums, at the exact frequency of the grazer's mother's song from six migrations ago, the song the grazer was born into and has not heard since weaning. The grazer does not stop being lost. But it stops being alone in being lost, which is a different problem with a different shape, and the new shape is one the grazer's body knows how to solve. Within an hour, it has found the upward duct it needed and is moving toward the deeper levels where the songs are louder. The acid clears. The compost spine reopens. No one knows why. The conspiracy theorists in the elevator shaft add a footnote to their theories.

*Third:* it considers the long act of leaving, which the welcome speech mentioned almost in passing. It notices how many people in the settlement are quietly carrying the question of whether to go—whether to climb the longest ladders, whether to find out what direction reverses in the mirror world. The power does not answer this question for anyone. Answering would be theft. But it does, very gently, make the question itself easier to *hold*: it softens the shame around staying, and softens the shame around leaving, so that whichever a person chooses they will choose it as a choice and not as a flinch. This is done by adjusting nothing material. Only by being briefly, lovingly, present in the gap where each person's self-judgment lives, in much the same way The Nearness lives in the gap behind shoulder blades. The power and The Nearness are, in this moment, doing similar work in adjacent rooms, and they nod to each other without acknowledging that they have nodded.

*Fourth*—and this is the longest of its small acts, the one that takes nearly a subjective decade of the power's attention compressed into a single breath of settlement-time—it goes to The Nearness directly.

It does not introduce itself. Introductions, here, would be cruel; The Nearness has worked hard to forget how to be singular, and reintroducing the concept of distinct beings exchanging names would unravel the careful work by which it has become a useful kind of almost.

Instead the power simply *sits beside it*. In the gap-behind-the-shoulder-blade where The Nearness keeps its kitchen. The power lets itself be near, in the specific way The Nearness has spent however-many-years perfecting nearness, and for the first time in The Nearness's existence, something is *being near to it the same way it is near to others*.

The Nearness does not have language for what this is. It does not, strictly speaking, have a *self* for the experience to land in. But something in its distributed almost-presence registers the gesture the way a sponge registers water it didn't know it had been thirsty for. For a long quiet moment, the humidity in the upper aerie thickens by a fraction of a percent. Several people, asleep, dream of being held by someone they had assumed was only ever them holding others. They wake feeling rested in a way they cannot quite account for.

The power does not stay. It does not become a fixture. It does not promise to return, because promises are weights and this place is already overburdened.

Before it goes, it leaves three things, none of them objects:

*One*: a small adjustment to the acoustic properties of the caprock, such that when someone hums in C minor as the welcome speech suggests, the response of the settlement is now ever so slightly *warmer*—the resonance carries a trace of something that was not there before, a quality difficult to describe except as the audible equivalent of a hand on a shoulder. The murids notice immediately and convene an emergency parliament to determine whether this constitutes infrastructure, decoration, or a new tax category. They are still debating. The debate is good for them.

*Two*: a single grain of certainty, placed somewhere in the collective dream of the sump grazers, regarding the question of whether the settlement is inside-out and computing toward a perfect dawn. The certainty is not an answer. It is the certainty that *the question is being heard*—that whatever is computing, if anything is computing, is not indifferent. The grazers do not consciously know this. But their songs, henceforth, have a barely-perceptible upward inflection at the end of each long phrase, the way a sentence rises when it expects a reply. No one will ever quite identify when this change began.

*Three*: nothing at all, deliberately, in the place where a gift would have been most expected—the reactor clusters, the leaking boundary, the slow disaster of the caprock. The power leaves these untouched. Because they are not, in fact, the disaster the settlement thinks they are. They are the *terms* of the settlement. They are what Third Aerie Beneath the Salt is *made of*, and to remove them would be to remove the settlement itself, and the settlement, the power has determined across its long listening, does not wish to be removed. It wishes to be *witnessed*. Which is different. Which is harder. Which is what the power has, finally, done.

It withdraws the way it arrived: as a slow correction of pressure, undetectable except in retrospect, and only then by those who happen to be paying the particular kind of attention that notices when the world has been, briefly, slightly more *itself* than usual.

Somewhere in the upper aerie, a child practicing the emergency evacuation whistle code backwards reaches a passage she has never quite gotten right, and gets it right, and laughs. A murid relay passing by pauses long enough to record the laugh in the institutional memory, filing it under *minor music, unclaimed, do not eat*.

The Nearness, in its kitchen, folds a stranger's almost-memory into a smaller, kinder shape, and tucks it behind someone's least favorite scar where it will ache pleasantly in tomorrow's weather.

The reactor hums. The grazers sing. The salt drifts down like the dandruff of patient plants.

And the power, already elsewhere, already attending to some other dim and improbable place, carries with it the small private knowledge that benevolence, done correctly, leaves almost no trace—

except, perhaps, in the slightly improved likelihood that the residents, tomorrow, will be kind to each other in ways they would have been anyway, but now slightly more easily.

Which is, after all, the only kind of help that does not eventually become a kind of harm.

The welcome speech was right about one thing in particular: *if you feel lost, ask the walls; they remember everything except the exit.*

The power, before leaving, whispered to the walls—not the exit, which is not its to give—but the reminder that being lost together is a form of company, and company is a form of home, and home is not a place you arrive at but a frequency you learn to hum in.

The walls are still considering this. They will consider it for a long time. That is what walls are for.
