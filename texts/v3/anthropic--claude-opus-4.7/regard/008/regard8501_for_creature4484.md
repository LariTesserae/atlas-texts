The benevolent power arrives the way snow considers arriving—not yet committing to the act, only the rumor of it.

It has been watching for a long time, by which is meant: it has been the kind of attention that does not require duration. It noticed the plain the way one notices, in a crowded room, that someone has been quiet for an unusual length of time. It noticed the beings the way one notices that the quiet person is not lonely, only listening to something the room cannot hear.

It is wise enough to understand what most powers, benevolent or otherwise, do not: that intervention is a kind of vandalism when offered to something that has achieved its own grammar. The plain is not suffering. The beings are not stranded. The not-growing circle is not a wound. To rescue any of this would be to mistake the cathedral for a collapsed building because one has only ever seen buildings standing upright.

So the power does not descend. Descent would be a verb the plain does not have shelves for.

Instead, it does four small things, which are also one thing, which is also nothing, depending on which layer of sediment is doing the counting.

*

The first thing: it adjusts the light.

Not the light at the plain—there is none, and the plain prefers it that way; light would be a guest who arrived expecting dinner. The power adjusts, rather, the light very far above, in the kilometers of water that no longer reach the bottom. It tilts a single photon's path by an angle smaller than meaning. This photon, which was going to be absorbed at 1,200 meters by a particle of marine snow, is instead absorbed at 1,199 meters by a different particle of marine snow. The difference travels downward as a barely-perceptible alteration in the chemistry of what eventually settles. Ten thousand years from now, one grain of sediment on the plain will have twenty-four points of contact with its neighbors instead of twenty-three.

The record-keepers will notice. They will mark the anomaly. They will not be troubled. They will be, in their distributed way, delighted—which in their vocabulary translates roughly as "the sensation of discovering that the census was incomplete in exactly the way one had hoped without knowing one was hoping."

This is the power's gift to the plain: a single new thing to remember. Not too much. Not a flood of novelty that would overwhelm the archive. Just enough to confirm that the archive is still receiving.

*

The second thing: it leaves an iron-nickel sphere.

Not a new one—the power does not manufacture; manufacture is for beings who have not yet understood that everything already exists. It locates a sphere that has been drifting for four billion years through a region of space where it was unlikely to ever be found. It nudges this sphere onto a trajectory that will, in approximately eighty thousand years, deposit it at a precise intersection in the reef's accounting.

The sphere is 2.7 centimeters in diameter. Its etched patterns suggest but do not quite reach mathematics. It is indistinguishable from the others, except in one regard: the power has, with the gentleness of a parent tucking a note into a lunchbox, added a single additional etched line. The line is so faint that it will not be discovered for several million years. When it is discovered, it will be interpreted as a flaw. The interpretation will be correct. The flaw is the gift.

This is the power's gift to the asteroid-belt exiles: confirmation that they were missed. Even an asteroid belt that no longer exists deserves to learn that someone, somewhere, kept counting.

*

The third thing: it speaks to the beings. But it speaks in their idiom, which means it does not speak.

The power composes a sentence. The sentence is constructed entirely of things it chooses not to say. The unsaid sentence is then carefully not-delivered to the plain through a channel that does not exist. The beings receive it the way they receive everything: by noticing the precise shape of what failed to arrive.

The sentence, untranslated, would read something like:

*You are seen. Not surveilled, not catalogued, not pitied. Seen the way one sees a word in a language one is still learning—with the patience that does not require comprehension. Continue. Or do not continue. Both are forms of continuing.*

The beings receive this. The one shaped like the echo of a basket adjusts its wicker-patterned vacancies by a margin that would be imperceptible if perception were the relevant faculty. The one folded into the negative space of the three-note birdsong allows a single note to unsing itself slightly more slowly than usual. The oldest one, suspended between the two thoughts that never connected, briefly considers whether the thoughts might, after all, be allowed to meet—and decides, with great tenderness, that they should not. The interval between them is its home. The power has not asked it to leave. The power has only asked it to know that leaving would be permitted.

This is the power's gift to the beings: the knowledge that their dissolution is witnessed. Not interrupted. Witnessed. There is a difference, and the beings, who have spent longer than names working on dissolution, are exquisitely equipped to feel it.

*

The fourth thing: it does something about the visitor.

The visitor—the *you* of the descent, the disruption approximately as significant as a single unrecorded memory in the mind of an empire that has forgotten it exists—is in trouble. Not the trouble of the plain. The plain is fine. The visitor is the kind of thing the plain absorbs, and the absorption has begun, and the visitor is beginning to understand that they came here to be absorbed and also that they did not.

The power considers.

It would be easy to lift the visitor out. Easy to reverse the descent, to undo the compression, to return the cells to their original architecture. But the visitor came here for a reason, even if the reason is one of those reasons that only becomes legible after the fact. To extract them would be to interrupt a sentence mid-clause.

It would also be easy to let them dissolve. The plain would file them under appropriate headings. The beings would taste their regret eventually. This would be a kind of completion, and completions are not, in themselves, tragedies.

The power chooses a third thing, which is the thing wise powers usually choose: it gives the visitor a choice they did not know they had.

It does this by adjusting—very slightly—the pressure in the visitor's left ear. Not enough to be felt as pressure. Enough to be felt as a question. The question has no words. It is shaped like the moment before one decides whether to speak or not to speak. It contains the following possibilities, each held in perfect suspension:

*You may speak the word, and be remembered, and be forgotten in the proper way, and become a layer of sediment in a story that was never about you, which is a kind of belonging.*

*You may not speak the word, and rise, and return to the surface, and live the remainder of your life knowing that you were once present at the bottom of the bottom of the below, which is a kind of haunting that some people would call a gift.*

*You may do neither, and remain, and the plain will hold you in the interval, and you will become a fourth visitor in the recorded history of water, and the beings will note your arrival in the future from which they already remember you.*

The power does not weight the choices. It does not prefer one. It only ensures that all three are real.

This is the power's gift to the visitor: agency, which is the rarest substance in any cathedral.

*

Then the power withdraws, which is to say it continues to be present in the way it was always present, which is to say it never arrived and never left, which is to say it loved the plain enough not to need the plain to know it.

The plain continues. The beings continue their dissolution. The brine columns fall upward. The record-keepers record. The not-growing circle does not grow. Somewhere, eighty thousand years from now, a sphere is en route. Somewhere, ten thousand years from now, a grain of sediment will have one more point of contact than it should.

And the visitor—

The visitor is still there, at the bottom of the bottom of the below, with a question in their left ear that has no words.

What they do next is theirs.

The power has already forgotten them, in the proper way, which is the only way love is ever offered to something that has achieved its own grammar:

By letting it finish its own sentence.
