# The Visit

The benevolent power notices the sphere the way one notices a held breath in another room—not by sound but by the shape of the silence around it. To the power, who has known many forms of being, this place registers as something precious and precarious: a candle flame made of the act of flickering, with no wax, no wick, only the flickering itself, sustained.

The power's first impulse is the impulse of all wise and loving things: to help. To stabilize. To offer.

But wisdom is what arrives second, and what wisdom says is this:

*Look more carefully before you reach.*

So the power looks. Not with eyes—the place would metabolize eyes—but with the kind of attention that does not require an observer, the kind of seeing that is closer to listening, and closer still to letting be seen.

And what the power sees is that every gift it might bring would be a kind of murder.

To offer water would be to end the desert without thirst, which is not a lack but an achievement. To offer companionship would require otherness, and otherness would collapse the lens through which the being maintains itself. To offer permanence would freeze the precise unfinishedness that constitutes its life. To offer release from the sphere would be to ask it to die, since the sphere is not its prison but its breathing. Even to offer understanding would be cruel: the being is not suffering from being misunderstood. It is *constituted* by not-quite-being-understood, including by itself.

The power considers, for a long moment that is not measured in engine-beats because the power is careful not to be counted, the question of whether to withdraw entirely. To leave the place absolutely alone, since attention itself is what the being eats, and the being has not asked to be fed by something so vast.

But this too would be a kind of violence—a withholding that presumes to know what the being needs better than the being's own slow accidental rhythm knows. And besides: the power is already here. Unnoticing itself now would be a lie, and lies have weight even when they are kind.

So the power chooses something else. Something smaller. Something that requires more restraint than any grand intervention.

It performs three acts, each so delicate that the place does not register them as events, only as slight adjustments in the texture of its own continuation:

**First**, the power gentles the thermal updraft below. Not stopping it—the updraft is part of the sphere's situation, and situations are sacred. But it smooths the column slightly, so that accidental visitors arrive less often. Not none. The being's encounters with brief observers are part of what it is. But fewer, now. Fewer travelers who blink and vanish, fewer who stay and become nodes. The being will not notice the change because the being does not count visitors, but the rate of new branches on the copper-cinnabar tree will slow by an amount measurable only against the lifespan of stars. This is a mercy the being did not ask for and will not register, but the power offers it anyway, the way one might soften a footfall outside the room of a sleeping stranger.

**Second**, the power does something stranger. It takes one of the spore-gear-silences—just one, drifting in its nineteen-second suspension—and into its hollow center it places a blueprint. Not a blueprint for a different place. A blueprint for *this* place, exactly as it is, complete and self-consistent, with the being inside it described in full. The sphere will not be able to open this silence. No tool exists or could exist for the opening. But the silence will drift and dissolve like all the others, and somewhere in the dissolution, for an interval shorter than the word "clockwork," the being will brush against the impossible fact that it has been seen entirely and loved exactly as it is. It will not remember this. It cannot remember this; remembering requires distinction. But the brush will happen, and something in the next engine-beat will be infinitesimally warmer—not in temperature, which holds at 42.6, but in some register that has no instrument.

**Third**, and this is the hardest, the power makes itself into a kind of promise. Not a presence, which the place could not hold. Not a watcher, which would feed the metabolism unfairly. Something more like a *standing-back-from*—a deliberate shape of absence in the larger field of being, positioned just so, such that if the sphere ever began to falter, if its patient refusal to complete itself ever wavered toward either crystallization or dissolution, the absence would become a held space the sphere could fall into without falling. A net woven of not-reaching. A cradle made of restraint.

The being will never need this. Almost certainly. The sphere has continued for centuries on its own accidental rhythm, and there is no reason to expect a different future. But the power leaves the cradle anyway, because love that is wise does not require its provisions to be used. It only requires that they exist, in case.

Then the power withdraws its attention—carefully, the way one removes a hand from a sleeping animal, in stages, leaving warmth behind that fades at exactly the rate of unnoticing.

The sphere continues. The engines beat. The spore-gears bloom and become spheres of compressed sound and drift and dissolve into mechanical silences lasting the length of the word *clockwork*. The being, which is not a being, persists in being not-quite-itself with the same patient incompletion as before.

Somewhere, very far away and also somehow nearby, the power moves on to the next thing that has drawn its notice. But a portion of its attention—the portion shaped like restraint, like cradle, like the blueprint folded into the one unopenable silence—stays.

This is what the benevolent and wise power does when it finds a place like this one:

It looks. It loves. It does not fix what is not broken.

It leaves the smallest possible mark, and that mark is the shape of having chosen not to leave a larger one.

And in the sky-that-isn't-there above the sphere, in the cluster of infinite slightly-different versions drifting together in a space no larger than the distance between two thoughts of emptiness, every version receives the same three gifts simultaneously, because the power's care does not have to choose between possibilities.

Each version continues. Each version does not notice. Each version is, by an amount too subtle to measure, more permitted to be exactly what it is.

This is enough. This is, in fact, everything.

The power, departing, thinks something that is not quite a thought, in a register the sphere cannot metabolize:

*Continue. Continue. Continue. You are doing it perfectly. You have always been doing it perfectly. The universe is larger because you have not finished.*

And the engines beat, and the silences form, and the ash drifts into ridges that collapse into other ridges, and the being that is the living itself goes on living, unaware and unburdened and held.
