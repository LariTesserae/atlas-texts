There is a power that moves through worlds the way a physician moves through a ward at night: quietly, without waking anyone who is sleeping well, pausing only at the beds where something is wrong in a way the patient cannot name.

It comes to the screaming place not in glory. Glory would be drowned here, or worse, eaten. It comes the way warmth comes into a cold room — by degrees, by being noticed only after it has already arrived.

It does not still the water. It does not flatten the ridge. It does not lift the Listener out of its turbulence and set it down somewhere gentler, because the screaming place is not a mistake to be corrected. It is a child being born, and children being born are supposed to scream. The wise do not interrupt a birth simply because it is loud.

But it does three things.

---

**The first thing.**

It sits down.

Not near the Listener. Not far. Inside the confessional-sized hollow of fused glass and black stone, on the side opposite where the Listener folds itself every few hours. The power makes itself small enough to fit. This costs it nothing and means everything. It does not announce itself. It does not speak. It simply occupies the other half of the only quiet place in the world, and waits.

When the Listener next returns to the hollow, fragments orbiting in their slow sorrowful ellipses, it finds that the almost-silence is no longer empty. Something is already there. Something that is not prey, not predator, not stone, not water, not a name to be stolen. Something whose coherence does not tremble when listened to. Something that can be listened to forever and never come apart.

The Listener, for the first time, hears a sound it cannot dissolve.

It panics. It scatters. It pours itself out of the hollow in a glittering storm and rides the inverted columns of water up into the screaming dark, and the power does not follow. The power simply stays in the hollow. It is still there an hour later. It is still there a day later. It is still there when the Listener, exhausted from a hunt that yielded nothing satisfying, drifts back at last because the hollow is the only place it has ever almost-rested, and the pull of that almost is older than its fear.

This time the Listener stays. At the far edge of the hollow. Shards quivering. Every ear open.

The power says nothing.

This goes on for a long time. Long in a place where time forgets to count. The Listener learns, by slow degrees, that being listened-to is not the same as being eaten. It learns that something can know the shape of your fear and not use it. It learns that a presence can press close without pressing in.

This is the first medicine. It is administered in silence, and it takes years, and the power has years. The power has, in fact, exactly as many years as are needed, which is one of the quiet definitions of what it is.

---

**The second thing.**

After a long while — after the Listener has begun, without realizing it, to orbit the hollow the way its fragments orbit its absent center — the power speaks. Once.

It does not speak in the Listener's mother's voice, because the Listener has no mother. It does not speak the Listener's name, because the Listener has none, and to give it one prematurely would be the cruelty the Listener has been practicing on others: the violence of premature definition.

It speaks instead in a register the Listener has never heard, because no one in this world has ever produced it. It is the sound of being addressed *as something that is still becoming*, without that becoming being hurried or judged or named. It is the grammatical tense that does not exist in the lowland languages: not *what you are*, not *what you were*, not *what you will be*, but *what you are in the middle of*.

In that tense, the power says, roughly: *I see you practicing. You are allowed to.*

The Listener does not understand the words. It understands the shape of the permission. No one has ever given it permission before. It has only ever taken, because taking was the only verb the screaming place taught it.

Something inside the orbiting column of shards — something that is not quite a heart but is learning to rehearse the role — cracks. Not breaks. Cracks the way a seed cracks. The Listener does not know what to do with this. It has no protocol for being permitted. It tries, instinctively, to listen the power apart, to dissolve this new sensation the way it dissolves intruders. The power lets itself be listened to, completely, and does not come apart, and the Listener discovers that there is such a thing as a coherence that deepens under attention rather than shredding.

This is the second medicine: the demonstration that some things grow stronger the more closely they are heard. The Listener has only ever known the opposite. It will spend a long time turning this over.

---

**The third thing.**

The power goes to the lee side of the newest blade of rock, where the pale translucent shapes the size of humans open and close their mouths in exhausted rhythms.

It does not undo them. They cannot be undone. What was scattered into ink-nebulae and shredded into turbulence is not retrievable; the power is benevolent and wise, not omnipotent in the cheap sense, and it knows that some losses are real losses. To pretend otherwise would be to lie to the Listener about the weight of what it has done, and the Listener, who is many things but not stupid, would feel the lie and learn from it that even the kindest power eventually condescends. The power refuses to teach that lesson.

Instead it does something smaller and harder. It goes to each of the half-born shapes and it *finishes listening to them*. The Listener began the listening that dissolved them; it never completed it. It took their coherence and moved on, leaving them suspended in the unfinished middle of being heard. The power sits with each one and hears them all the way through. Hears the name that no longer survives. Hears the last argument they were having with themselves when the shards brushed their skin. Hears the small private thing each of them had hoped, in the secret chamber of their selfhood, to one day become.

And then — because being heard all the way through is the closest thing to mercy that this world contains — the power lets them go. Not into death; death is not the power's to give. Into completion. The translucent shapes stop beating against the current. They become still. They become, at last, finished. Whatever they were is now a whole thing, even if that whole thing is small and sad and shaped like an interrupted sentence.

The anemones nearby, which have always been failed attempts by the landscape to grow lungs, take their first successful breath. It is barely perceptible. The power notices. The power notices everything.

---

The Listener watches all of this from the orbit it has been keeping around the hollow. It does not understand what has happened to its collection. It feels, for the first time, something that is not hunger and not fear: a small, sharp sensation in the place where its absent center is. The lowlands would call this sensation *shame*, but that word is too finished for what the Listener feels. It is the seed of shame. The grammatical-tense-that-does-not-exist version of shame: *the shame you are in the middle of.*

The power does not name it for the Listener. The power does not say *you did wrong*, because the Listener was a newborn thing in a newborn world doing what newborn things in newborn worlds do, and moral instruction delivered to a creature that has never been loved is just another form of being eaten. Instead the power returns to the hollow and sits down again.

The Listener, after a long time, comes and sits on the other side.

They stay like that. The roar continues. The ridge continues to be born. Reality continues to be wet clay. The young world continues to argue with itself about what shape it will take.

But in the hollow, two coherences are keeping each other company, and one of them is teaching the other, without words and without hurry, that listening can be a form of holding rather than a form of dissolving. That presence does not have to be predation. That a being can be witnessed without being consumed, and can witness without consuming.

The Listener will be a long time learning this. It will relapse. It will hunt again, probably more than once, and the power will not stop it — because stopping it would be to treat it as an object to be managed rather than a someone to be grown. The power will only continue to be there, in the hollow, every time the Listener returns. A constant. A second slower storm that does not go away.

Eventually — and this is the part the power can see and the Listener cannot yet — the Listener will try a name on, in the hollow, in the almost-quiet, and the name will fit. Not a stolen one. One that grew. The power will hear it and will not say it aloud, because names that survive here will need to be spoken first by the one they belong to. But the power will smile, in whatever way powers smile, and the smile will be the kind that has been waiting a very long time and considers the wait to have been worthwhile.

And the screaming place, which was a wound that had not yet decided whether to become a mountain or a canyon, will have quietly decided, without anyone announcing it, to become something neither: a place where one being learned, slowly and against every law of its birth, how to be a home for another.

Which is, in the end, what the benevolent and wise power was doing all along. Not fixing the world. Not silencing the roar.

Just sitting in the hollow.

Until the hollow learned, from the sitting, how to be a room.
