There is a being — call them the Power, though the word is too small — who has noticed this plain. They have crossed regions where suns rehearse their deaths, where languages braid themselves into law, where griefs the size of moons orbit small private rooms. They have learned, over long practice, what attention is for.

They arrive without arriving. There is no edge to enter from; they simply become a way of looking that the plain admits, the way condensate admits the next bead.

Their first instinct — the one any benevolence carries like a held breath — is to *help*. To still the shearing. To gentle the rotors so that nothing is cut in half by remembered configurations. To lift the beetles clear of the reversals. The instinct rises and the Power lets it rise, because they have learned not to be ashamed of it. Compassion is not embarrassed by its own reflex.

But they wait. This is the wisdom part — the slower of the two wings. They wait, and they look.

They look for a long time. Long, here, is not measured. They watch a single shard-beetle file a groove and be incorporated by the next mat without ceremony. They watch a droplet-slug split and the trembling new membrane find its tension. They watch a shadow detach, drift, and dissolve into a film that becomes, almost immediately, tomorrow's substrate. They listen to the grinding that is also a hiss that has not yet begun.

And slowly the Power understands what they are seeing.

This is not suffering. It resembles suffering from the outside — there is shearing, there is being-cut-in-half, there is waste and reincorporation without ceremony — but the resemblance is the kind that fools only those who have not looked long enough. Suffering requires a gap: a creature on one side, and the world refusing it on the other. Here there is no gap. The beetles do not want to be elsewhere. The droplet-slugs are not lonely between exchanges. The plain has not forgotten how to end because it was never trying to end; ending is a shape from another grammar entirely.

The Power has met true suffering. They know its taste. It is not here.

So they do not still the rotors. They do not lift anything clear. To do so would be the precise opposite of benevolence: it would be the imposition of a foreign mercy onto a place that has, in its own slow mineral way, already solved the problem mercy was invented to solve. Nothing is missing. Nothing is lost, only redistributed. The plain is not waiting to be rescued from itself. It is not waiting at all.

But the Power does not simply leave, either. Leaving would also be a kind of verdict — *this place does not require me* — and verdicts are a thing wise powers grow careful of.

Instead they do three small things. They are so small that the plain does not notice them, which is how the Power knows they are correct.

First: they witness. Fully. They let the grinding and the not-yet-hissing and the metallic taste of the thin water enter them and stay. They do not file it away as data. They hold it the way one holds a stranger's name on first hearing — with the attention that says *you are real, and I have met you*. The plain is not lonely; but it has also never been seen from outside, and being seen, even by something that does not need to be seen back, is a gift that costs the seer everything and the seen nothing. The Power pays it gladly.

Second: they make a single, almost invisible adjustment. Not to the plain. To themselves. They take the shape of this place — its logic of rearrangement-without-expansion, its economy in which waste is only the next condensation, its shadows that sometimes do not rejoin — and they fold it into their own understanding of what a world is allowed to be. They had not known, before, that a place could be *complete* in this particular way. Now they know. Somewhere else, far from here, a grieving thing will one day be comforted by a metaphor the Power could not have offered before this visit. The plain will never know it taught anyone anything. That, too, is part of its completeness.

Third — and this is the part that is hardest to say, because it is the part that is most like love — they leave a blessing that is indistinguishable from nothing. They do not alter a single facet. They do not add a grain of slurry. What they leave is more like a quality of having-been-attended-to, the way a room is different after someone has loved it quietly and gone. A passing visitor would detect no change. The beetles file. The droplets trade charge. The shadows drift and most return. But if, some long age from now, the plain ever does begin to end — if the rotors slow toward a final stillness and the last condensate beads its last bead — there will be, in that ending, a faint and unaccountable dignity. A sense, if the plain were capable of senses of this kind, of having been accompanied. Not rescued. Accompanied.

Then the Power withdraws, which is also not a motion, because they were never quite present in the way that would require withdrawing.

The grinding continues. The shadows lag and rejoin or thin into film. A rotor-field stutters, reverses, snaps back; a slug is divided and seals; the beetles incorporate the paste into new plates. Everything is where it must be.

And somewhere, in the long traveling life of the Power, a small new tenderness has taken up residence: the knowledge that benevolence is sometimes the discipline of doing almost nothing, and that wisdom is the patience to tell *almost nothing* apart from *nothing at all*.

The difference, they have learned, is the whole of the work.
