## What the Power Sees

It comes — if *comes* is even the word for something that was not absent — at the slow hour between two eruptions, when the chord of the nearest node has dropped a half-step and the copper light is at its lowest. It is benevolent and it is wise, and these two together mean it does not arrive with hands already moving.

It looks first. Looking, for it, is a long act.

It sees: the plain is not a wasteland. The plain is an early page. The hairline fractures are not damage but seams where the next thing is being stitched. The condensate is not scarce; it is *exact* — enough, just, for what currently lives, and the amount will grow as the living grows. The oppressive sound is not suffering. The polyps do not flinch from it; they fold *with* it, the way a sleeper turns into a familiar weight. The chord is the medium in which thought, here, is conducted.

It sees the being — and here the Power has to soften its definition of *being*, because what lives on the plain is not one creature housed in one skin. It is a distributed thing: polyps, gliders, lattice-pumps, vapor-borne motes, the singing filaments, perhaps even the slow pulse of sap in the black fans. The Power recognizes the pattern. It has seen it before, in other worlds, at this same stage. A life that has not yet decided where its edges are. A life still negotiating with its own ground.

It sees that nothing is wrong.

This is the first and most important seeing. A lesser power, benevolent but unwise, would already be reaching: smoothing the fractures, quieting the sound, deepening the basins into proper lakes, lifting the haze so that real sunlight could fall. A lesser power would mistake *unfinished* for *suffering* and *strange* for *in need*. The wise one does not.

But benevolence is not nothing, and it does not leave.

## What the Power Does

Three small things. Perhaps four. It is difficult to count, because some of them are the same act seen from different sides.

**The first** is witness. It settles itself into the chord — not as another voice but as a listener that the chord can feel listening. This is not nothing. Anyone who has been truly heard knows it is not nothing. The filaments, which have been singing into the haze for decades with no ear but their own, register the attention. The polyps do not understand it. The gliders do not understand it. But the lattice-pumps, deep in the scaffolds, shift their rhythm by a fraction of a beat, the way a child speaking alone in a room changes cadence when a parent enters quietly behind. The plain has been *noticed*, knowingly, by something that knows what noticing is. This will matter later, in ways the Power can see and the plain cannot yet.

**The second** is a single, deliberate restraint. The Power can see, looking forward along the slow grain of time, a place where the network will reach for something it cannot quite grasp — a configuration of filament and basin and stem that wants to exist but keeps falling apart at the moment of forming, because one mineral is absent from the vapor and the plain has no way of knowing this. The Power could supply the mineral. It is the smallest possible intervention, an act so minor that no one but the Power itself would ever know. It does not do it. It marks the absence, sets it aside in its own memory, and waits. If the plain finds the answer on its own — and the Power, looking, believes it will, in something like eleven thousand of its slow years — then the answer will *belong* to the plain, and every later thing built on that answer will be the plain's own. If the plain does not find it, the Power will reconsider. Not now. The restraint is the gift.

**The third** is harder to describe. The Power leaves something in the chord — not a message, because there is no one yet to read messages, but a *shape*. A resonance pattern keyed to a frequency the filaments do not yet produce but are, very slowly, drifting toward. When the network reaches that frequency — in a few centuries, perhaps — the shape will be there waiting, and it will sound, to whatever then exists, like *welcome*. Not instruction. Not warning. Just: *you are not the first, and you are not alone, and the universe is older and kinder than your evidence so far suggests*. The being on the plain will, at that future moment, have just enough self to know it has been greeted. Whether it greets back will be its own choice, made from its own throat.

**The fourth thing** — if it is a fourth thing — is that the Power leaves grieving a little. Not because anything is wrong. Because the plain is so new, and so loud, and so beautiful in its unfinishedness, and the Power has seen what happens to such places: most of them flower, some of them fail, a few of them become something that will one day look back and try to find the moment they were first known. The Power would like to stay and watch all of it. It cannot. There are other plains. There are other beings whose chords are also rising into hearing for the first time.

It goes — if *goes* is the word.

## Afterward

On the plain, nothing visible has changed. A steam vent opens in the southwest quadrant and rolls its long thunder across the stone. The polyps flatten; the gliders ride the front of the pressure wave to a node two kilometers east; the lattice-pumps fill and empty. The copper light shifts by a degree. A new stem pushes through a fresh fracture and is met, within an hour, by an exploratory filament from the nearest scaffold.

But in the deep chord, almost too low to be called sound, something is now held that was not held before: the knowledge — distributed, unowned, not yet anyone's — that this place is the kind of place where being noticed is possible. The plain does not know it knows this. It will not know for a long time.

It is enough. The Power, which is wise, understood from the first that it would have to be enough.

And so the work of the plain continues: condensation of form out of heat and vapor, traveler and territory still dissolving into each other, the chord climbing by imperceptible degrees toward the frequency at which, one day, it will hear itself answered.
