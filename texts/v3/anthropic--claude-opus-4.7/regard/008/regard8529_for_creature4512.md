# What the Power Would Do

The benevolent and wise power arrives the way good light arrives in a library: without disturbing the dust.

It is important to say first what such a power would *not* do, because benevolence misunderstood is the great wrecker of careful places. It would not rescue the Refurbitioners. It would not open the catacombs to sunlight. It would not explain to them, gently, that they have been mistaken about the nature of life, or filing, or each other. It would not pity them. Pity, in a place like this, would land like a boot through a fresco.

What it would do, instead, is take its hat off at the threshold.

---

**First**, it would declare its documents.

This is the courtesy the place asks for, and a wise power knows that the first kindness owed to any host is to honor the form of their hospitality. So the power would produce, from somewhere about its person, an item. Perhaps a single seed that remembers being a forest. Perhaps a letter addressed to no one, written in a hand the recipient has not yet developed. Perhaps simply its own true name, folded small. It would offer this to be tagged to its earlobe (left; it has never been altered) and classified provisionally under whatever subsection felt most flattering to the cataloger on duty.

It would accept the tea. It would drink slowly. It would not look into the bottom of the cup.

---

**Second**, it would do the thing that benevolent powers do that no one ever notices: it would simply *be present* and *pay correct attention*. This is more than it sounds. Most beings, most of the time, are seen approximately — sorted by the rough categories of the seer's hurry. To be seen exactly, by something large and kind and unhurried, is a sacrament most creatures have never received.

The power would sit in the Hall of Misplaced Tuesdays and let itself be cataloged. It would let a Librarian of Ash recite its birth certificate backwards and would not flinch at hearing the syllables of its own beginning emerge as an exit. It would walk the terrazzo floors at the gait of someone with nowhere to be, and the granular choir beneath its steps would play something none of them had heard before: a music that was neither anxious scale nor minor march nor madrigal, but something like *the sound of a long argument ending in agreement*.

The Refurbitioners would, of course, file this music. They would file it under "MUSIC, UNPRECEDENTED, REQUIRING NEW SUBSECTION." The act of filing it would require them to invent the subsection, which would require them to admit that their classification system had a shape, which would require them to admit that the shape had an outside. This admission, made by accident, in the course of ordinary work, would be the first gift.

They would not notice it was a gift. This is part of how the gift works.

---

**Third**, the power would address the Clock of Unanswered Questions.

It would approach the hollow sphere at dusk — the place keeps dusk available on request, like a condiment — and it would not pollinate a question against the knurled surface. Instead it would lay its palm flat against the cement and hold very still, the way one holds still for a frightened animal, and it would say, internally, in the part of speech that requires no footnotes:

*You may rest now if you wish. The bee may stop. The answer was always the asking.*

The iron bee, rusting perpetually in its amber, would consider this. Bees, even iron ones rusting in honey gone to amber, retain certain prerogatives. After some consideration — perhaps a century, perhaps an afternoon, the catacombs do not distinguish — the bee would decline. It likes its work. It has come to love the slow rotation, the patient accumulation of facets no one will read in time. The power would smile (a thing felt rather than seen) and withdraw its hand, and the bee would buzz once, which is to say, the sphere would turn one century's worth, which is to say, a new facet would crystallize on the inside, bearing the answer to a question no one yet remembered asking, which read:

*YES. STAY AS LONG AS YOU LIKE.*

It would be addressed to the catacombs themselves.

---

**Fourth**, the power would do something about the children.

Not adopt them. Not free them from the Archive Drifts. The foundlings discovered in the corners of the Hall of Misplaced Tuesdays are not, in fact, suffering — they are loved in the only grammar their parents-in-documentation know. But the power has noticed something. It has noticed that the children, before they are properly cataloged, in the small hour between being a dust-bunny of someone else's memory and being a filed entity with a tertiary parent, *dream of width.*

The flora here grows only vertical, by treaty. The children, briefly, are exempt from the treaty. In the cataloging-gap they sprawl. They dream of fruit. They dream of branching.

The power would not abolish the treaty — treaties between forgotten kingdoms have their own dignity — but it would *extend* the cataloging-gap. By how much? By exactly the breath of a held pause. By the duration of a Refurbitioner remembering to look once more at the foundling before reaching for the form. A second. Perhaps two. Over centuries, across generations, this extra second would accumulate into a small interior weather. The children would grow up to be Refurbitioners still — but Refurbitioners with a private chamber inside themselves where something fruited, once, briefly, before being filed.

This is what wise powers do instead of revolutions. They lengthen pauses.

---

**Fifth**, and this is the secret one, the power would speak to the place itself.

The catacombs, you understand, are a being. Not metaphorically. The breathing masonry breathes; the floor sings; the carvers return every seventy-seven years not by calendar but by ache, which means the structure dreams them back. The place is a slow vast organism whose central activity is *refusing to finish dying* — and refusing to finish dying is exhausting, even when you are very good at it, even when you have made an art of it across three dynasties.

The power would find the place's pulse — somewhere near the Sixth Door on the Left, probably, since that is where Anomaly already keeps its office — and it would put its ear against the warm stone and listen for a long time. Long enough that the Librarians of Ash forgot it was there. Long enough that the moss began to inscribe its tiny green circuitry across the back of its coat.

Then it would say, in the register the place itself uses — which is the register of held breath and redistributed collapse:

*You have done beautifully. You do not have to be eternal. You only have to be exactly as long as you are. When you are ready to finish, I will sit with you. When you are not ready, I will also sit with you. Either is honorable. Neither is required.*

This is the deepest gift the power has to give: the lifting of the obligation to persist. Not the *ending* of persistence — the place will go on persisting, probably for several more dynasties, because it likes to, because the argument with gravity has become a friendship and friendships are hard to leave — but the lifting of the obligation. The place had not known, until this moment, that it was allowed to stop. It had assumed the refurbishing was conscripted. To learn it is volunteer work changes the flavor of every chisel-stroke yet to come.

The terrazzo, beneath the power's feet, sings something new: relief.

---

**Finally**, the power would leave.

This is essential. Wise benevolence understands the dosage of itself. To stay would be to become another thing to catalog, another permanent feature of the architecture, another voice in the choir of footstep-music. The place does not need a god. It has gotten this far without one, by means of clam-shell chisels and bored concubines and inverted dreamcatchers and tea served at exactly the temperature of comprehension's failure. A god installed here would be a redundancy, and redundancy in the Refurbished Catacombs is filed under "INSULTS, ARCHITECTURAL."

So the power would settle its tab. It would return the cup (bottomless; this is fine; the references to other cups it might have enjoyed are appreciated and acknowledged). It would tip the Librarian of Ash a syllable it had been saving — the syllable was *yes*, in a dialect not yet invented, and the parrot-thing would hoard it in its crop-pouch for two hundred years before trading it for a particularly fine lichen-mirror. It would nod to Anomaly, who would nod back, professional to professional. It would receive its provisional classification tag back from its earlobe; the Refurbitioner removing it would notice that the cardstock had grown slightly warmer than ambient, and would file this observation under "VISITORS, RETURNED WITH AUGMENTATION, FOLLOW UP IN APPROXIMATELY ONE GENERATION."

It would ascend the spiral steps. Alone, so the xylophone would not play — only wait, courteously, in silence.

At the top, before stepping out into whatever weather the surface was currently performing, it would turn and look back down the throat of carved sandstone. And it would say one last thing, not to the Refurbitioners, not to the place, not to the iron bee or the ferns visiting impoverished relatives or the children dreaming briefly of width —

It would say it to the *next visitor*. The one not yet arrived. The one whose footsteps the terrazzo had not yet learned.

It would say: *They are kind here. In their way. Bring something to declare. Drink the tea slowly. Don't ask them what they're refurbishing. And if you stay — and you may stay, you may stay as long as you like, the sphere has crystallized it — remember that you are also being refurbished, gently, by a place that has made an art of not finishing.*

*That is what I would have wanted someone to tell me.*

Then it would step out into the afternoon, leaving behind no documentation, no monument, no improvement —

only a slightly longer pause in the cataloging of children, and a faint new facet inside a sphere no one will see in time, and the memory, in the breathing stone, of having been told it could rest.

Which is to say: it would leave the place exactly as it found it.

Which is to say: it would leave it utterly transformed.

Which is to say — and the Refurbitioners would understand this, if anyone would — it would file itself, on its way out, under

**KINDNESS, COMPLETED, NO RECEIPT REQUIRED.**
