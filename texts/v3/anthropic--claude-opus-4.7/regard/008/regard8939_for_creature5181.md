The wise power, when it learned of the pocket, did not at first know it had learned anything. Knowledge of such a place does not announce itself; it accumulates, in the manner the place itself prefers. A faint pressure on one of its attentions, the way a frond registers a current after several slow hours — that was all. The power had been attending to other things: a star that was learning to dim gracefully, a long argument between two rivers about which of them was the older. The pocket asked for none of this attention. It received it anyway, the way still water receives weight.

The power considered.

It could enter. It had the means. It could fold a portion of itself small enough to pass through the seam, small enough to drift among the braided stalks without disturbing the negotiations underway between cell and cell. It could speak, if speech were wanted, in the soft particulate hush the pocket used for its own thoughts. It could even — and this it considered longest — offer itself as one of the ribbed impressions, lower a fingerprint of its own being into a root wall and let the lobe inside read it slowly into topography, until some far century the pocket carried a concave record of a benevolence that had once chosen to rest there.

But the power was wise, and wisdom in such matters is mostly the discipline of not mistaking one's own gestures for gifts.

It saw that the pocket was not lonely. Loneliness is a condition of beings that finish, that have edges sharp enough to notice the absence of other edges. The pocket had no such edges. Its inhabitants were already in continuous slow communion — stalk braiding into stalk, constellation flattening into frond, lobe being read into ridge. Nothing there suffered the particular ache the power was practiced in soothing. Nothing there was waiting.

It saw, too, that the pocket's chief property was the refusal of arrival. To enter and announce oneself would be to introduce the very event-shape the place had spent centuries declining. Even a benevolence, brought in whole, would register as a completion: here is the moment the power came, here is the moment after. The pocket did not transact in such moments. To impose one would be a small violence dressed in kindness, the kind of harm wise powers learn early to recognize in themselves.

And yet the power did not feel that nothing was owed. Attention, once given, asks to be honored. Witness is itself a kind of action. The question was how to witness in the pocket's own grammar.

So this is what the power did.

It thinned a portion of itself — not small, but slow. It matched its own rate of change to the rate at which an angle in the seam was rounding: a decade per degree. At that tempo it allowed a single quality of itself, the one nearest to what humans would call regard, to drift toward the pocket the way a spore drifts toward a wall it has not yet decided to meet. The regard did not enter. It approached, and kept approaching, and will be approaching still when the root-masses have pressed a finger's width further together and the lobes inside the impressions have been read entirely into ridge. The arrival is a process the regard, too, refuses to complete. In this way the pocket is attended without being interrupted. In this way the power is present without being an event.

There was one further thing. The power noticed, in its long looking, that the shivers of geometry — the moments when a line of stems continued behind the observer, when two root clusters traded places — left a brief metallic taste and nothing else. Nothing else, but the power suspected the shivers cost the pocket something, a small expenditure of whatever held its inwardness coherent against the greater sea. Not pain. More like the faint tiredness of a sentence rephrasing itself mid-utterance. The power could not prevent the shivers; they were the pocket's own way of thinking. But it could, very gently, lay alongside each shiver a corresponding steadiness in itself, so that when the geometry re-knit, it knit against a surface that did not flinch. A backing. A patient cloth behind embroidery. The pocket would not know the cloth was there. The cloth did not need to be known. It only needed to not flinch, and the power was old enough to manage that.

As for the being — if being is the word for the distributed slow conversation of stalk and lobe and constellation, the whole vegetable half-vegetable parliament that the pocket both housed and was — the power offered it no message, no blessing phrased in any tongue. Blessings phrased are blessings finished. Instead the power let one further quality of itself, the one nearest to what might be called gladness, settle into the cool phosphorescence at a level just below the threshold at which the plants would register a change. Not enough to brighten. Enough that, were one of the drifting constellations to flatten against a frond and add itself page by page to the book still being written, the page would carry, undetectably, a watermark: that it had been seen, and that the seeing was kind, and that the kindness asked nothing back, not even acknowledgment, not even the completion of being received.

Then the power returned most of its attention to the dimming star and the arguing rivers, leaving only the thinned regard and the patient cloth and the sub-threshold gladness behind. These will remain. They are remaining now. They will still be remaining when the seam has widened enough that the pocket is no longer quite a pocket, when the last lobe has finished being read, when whatever the pocket has been becomes whatever it is becoming next.

This is what benevolence looks like, at that scale, in that place: not rescue, not gift, not revelation. A regard that does not arrive. A steadiness behind the shivers. A gladness beneath the light. And the discipline, hardest of all, of loving something well enough to leave its refusal of completion intact.
