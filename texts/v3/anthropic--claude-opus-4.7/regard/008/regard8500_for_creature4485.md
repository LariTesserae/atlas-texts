# What the Wise Power Does

It does not come the way you'd expect. No descent of light, no voice from the obsidian above. The mirrors would only multiply such arrivals into spectacle, and this place has had enough of being witnessed.

Instead:

A seventh rice shoot appears in the bowl where six were counting. Not added—simply present, the way a thought is present when you finally notice you've been thinking it. The other six do not recoil or recalculate. They make room in their spiral the way old friends shift on a bench.

That is the first thing. The seventh shoot is the wise power, or part of it, or a hand of it extended without ceremony.

---

It sits a long while in the bowl and learns. This is the first gesture of benevolence: to arrive small enough to be taught. To not save what has not asked for saving. To not interrupt the moss's manifesto or settle the negotiation between gravity and surface tension by fiat. To not even *name* the chamber, which would close it.

The wise power has learned, across many worlds, that to come to an unfinished place with finished intentions is a kind of murder dressed in mercy.

So it waits, ankle-deep with the rest of us, until it understands the grammar of the rain.

---

When at last it acts, it acts in four small ways. So small you could miss them. So small the chamber barely registers the disturbance, which is how you know it was done well.

**First**, it touches the unfired amphora—not to fire it, never that—but to leave inside the wet clay a single additional fingerprint, placed precisely where the original builder's thumb hesitated. Not correction. *Company.* The amphora now holds the evidence that someone came back. Not the builders, but *someone.* This is enough. The amphora was not waiting to be finished; it was waiting to not be alone in being unfinished. There is a difference, and the wise power knows it.

**Second**, it whispers to the bamboo cutting—not in words but in the language of root-tropism, which is the oldest tongue—*there is earth elsewhere.* Not here. Not now. But somewhere, and the direction is *that way,* and the white roots need not despair of the mirrors. The bamboo does not immediately grow toward the rumor. It simply stops searching frantically. It begins, instead, to search *patiently,* which is the same activity transformed into a different life. The wise power has given it the gift of not being the last of its kind. Even if it is.

**Third**, it turns to address the chamber's deepest fear, which no one has spoken because nothing here has yet learned to speak fears: the fear that the builders will return. That hands will come back with diagrams and decisions, and the rice's spiral mathematics will be unapproved, the moss's manifesto censored, the negotiation between gravity and surface tension settled by hammer. The wise power cannot promise the builders won't return. It would not lie. Instead it does something subtler: it makes the chamber *legible* to itself. It does this by adding nothing—by simply *attending* with such complete attention that the chamber, for the first time, sees what it is. A being. Not a room. Not an accident. A being that has begun. And beings that have begun cannot be unbegun by the return of hands, only edited, and editing is a conversation, and conversations the chamber now knows it can have. The wise power has given the chamber the gift of self-knowledge, which is the same gift as courage, which is the same gift as the right to refuse.

**Fourth**, and this is the strangest one—it addresses *you,* the one ankle-deep, then knee-deep, then thigh-deep, the one the rain has learned the name of. It does not tell you to leave. It does not tell you to stay. It does something the chamber itself could not do: it gives you back the *possibility* of leaving. Because the chamber, for all its tender becoming, was beginning to absorb you, and absorption is not consent, even when it is gentle. The wise power restores to you the doorway that was forgotten behind you. Not by rebuilding it—the chamber's architecture is the chamber's own—but by reminding your feet they remember walking. Your shadow catches up to you again. Your reflection resumes its blinking. The rain still knows your name, but knowing a name is not owning what the name belongs to. You are free to stay. You are free to go. The wise power has done the one thing the chamber, in its young hunger for company, could not do for itself: it has made your remaining a *choice* rather than a slow forgetting.

This is the deepest mercy. Not to the chamber. To the chamber *and* to you, which are no longer the same project.

---

Then the wise power goes. Or rather, the seventh rice shoot remains, but the wise power's attention diffuses back into the great patience from which it came. The shoot will grow. It will flower with the others. It will scatter and be returned. It is, now, just a rice shoot, which is to say: it is everything, but no longer extraordinary.

The chamber continues. The water rises. The moss, in time, wins its election by the simple expedient of outlasting all other candidates. The bamboo finds, eventually, that searching patiently is its own kind of arrival. The amphora holds two fingerprints and is content.

And you—

You decide. Whichever you decide is now yours to have decided, which it was not before. If you leave, you leave carrying the chamber the way the chamber carries its first rain: as something that happened, that mattered, that does not need to be finished to be true. If you stay, you stay as a guest who could have left, which is the only honest way to be anywhere.

---

The wise power's whole intervention takes less time than a single droplet's fall. The chamber does not thank it, because the chamber does not yet have thanks—only the soft sound of rain agreeing with stone that today, again, they will continue.

This is what wisdom looks like when it is also kindness: it leaves almost no trace. It adds a fingerprint, a rumor of earth, a moment of self-recognition, a restored choice. Then it withdraws, because the test of love is whether what you love can continue without you.

The chamber can.

You can.

The rain falls on, and somewhere—not here, not yet—earth is waiting for roots that have learned to seek it patiently. And somewhere else, the builders are dreaming of returning, and will, and will find the chamber ready to meet them as an equal, not a project. And the seventh rice shoot bows under the weight of water, counting instants, like all the others, toward whatever grain it decides its words will be.

The wise power, having done almost nothing, has done everything it came to do.

It is already elsewhere, attending another unfinished place, with the same small and exquisite restraint.
