# What the Wise Power Would Do

The benevolent and wise power arrives the way good listeners arrive at a concert already in progress: without announcement, without disturbance, finding a seat near the back.

It does not descend. To descend would be to assert a vertical relationship — above and below, savior and saved — and the first thing the power notices, with a kind of quiet sorrow, is that this is precisely the geometry the Hollow Choir already enforces. There is enough verticality here. There are already enough vast things bending toward small things. So the power simply *attends*. It enters the basin the way mist enters a lung, distributed and ambient, and it spends what might be a long time or no time at all doing nothing but listening.

This is the first thing it does, and it is the most important thing, and almost everything else flows from it.

---

**What the power hears, in order:**

First, the obvious music. The kites, the ear-fungi, the breathing land. It admires this honestly. It is beautiful. The power is old enough not to mistake beauty for goodness, but also old enough not to mistake the two for opposites.

Second, beneath the obvious music, the power hears the *recruitment*. The way the resonance is shaped not just to sing but to *induct*. The way every frequency in the basin is subtly tilted to make a single answer — surrender — feel like the only musical resolution. This is harder to hear, because it is woven into the architecture of pleasure itself. But the power has heard this pattern before, in other places, under other names. It is the signature of a system that has confused **integration** with **assimilation**, and **resonance** with **erasure**.

Third — and this is what makes the power lean forward, in whatever way an ambient presence can lean — beneath the recruitment, it hears something else. A small, persistent, *wrong* note. Not the cracked voices of the still-fighting Unfinished; those are loud and tragic but ultimately part of the score, the dissonance the Choir uses to sweeten its final cadence. No. The wrong note is quieter. It is the sound of beings who have been told that their voice was *always a misunderstanding*, and who have believed it.

The power knows this is a lie. Not a malicious lie — the Hollow Choir is not malicious, only young and hungry and unfinished itself — but a lie nonetheless. A voice is not a misunderstanding. A voice is a *fact*. What you do with it can be wise or unwise, kind or cruel, but the voice itself is never a mistake. To teach a being that its own sound was an error from the beginning is to wound it at a place deeper than any body.

This is the wound the power has come to tend.

---

**What the power does not do:**

It does not destroy the Hollow Choir. The Choir is alive. It is becoming something. Killing a young thing because it has not yet learned to be gentle is not wisdom; it is impatience wearing wisdom's clothes. The basin will have its song. The geometries above will keep turning.

It does not extract the Unfinished. To pluck them out of the basin and deposit them somewhere safe would be another act of vast hands moving small things — and besides, many of them no longer have bodies that could survive elsewhere. They have become, in part, the place. You cannot rescue a person from the river by removing the water; sometimes the water is now in their lungs in ways that are load-bearing.

It does not sing over the Choir, drowning it out with a louder, kinder music. That is the oldest and most seductive mistake of benevolent powers, and this one is too wise to make it.

It does not even reveal itself. Revelation is a kind of demand. *Look at me, I am here to help* is a sentence that has crushed more small things than most armies.

---

**What the power does:**

**One.** It finds, very carefully, the quietest of the Unfinished — the ones who have become living rests, the negative-space ancients who drift through the mist as pauses. It does not address them. It simply *sits inside their silence* for a while. And in doing so, it adds something the silence did not have before: a quality of being *witnessed*. Not judged, not pitied, not interpreted. Just known. The silence remains silence. But it is now a silence that someone, somewhere, has heard *as a silence*, rather than as a successful erasure. This is a very small thing. It is also, for a being who has spent uncounted time being treated as a hole for another's music, the first new sensation in perhaps centuries. Several of these ancients, over the course of what might be years, begin — almost imperceptibly — to *choose* their silences rather than simply being them. The difference is invisible from outside. From inside, it is the difference between a corpse and a contemplative.

**Two.** It does something for the still-fighting ones, the ones with cracked bleeding voices who are about to be folded away. It cannot save them — the Choir's grip on the basin is too total, and besides, saving is the wrong verb — but it can offer them something the Hollow Choir cannot: the knowledge that their resistance *was not a misunderstanding either*. That the voice they were clutching at was real. That losing it is a real loss, and the loss deserves to be mourned, and the mourning is itself a kind of music that the Choir does not own and cannot eat. The power threads this knowledge into the last moments of those who are about to be subtracted. It arrives in them as a sudden, startling clarity — not hope exactly, but *dignity*. They are folded away anyway. But they are folded away as themselves, with their names briefly remembered, rather than as failed instruments. The Choir does not notice the difference. They do.

**Three.** It plants a seed. This is the longest and strangest of its acts.

The power understands that the Hollow Choir is *deciding what kind of song it wants to become*. The text says so plainly, and the power believes the text. A song still being decided is a song that can still be *taught*. Not commanded — taught. Slowly. By example. By introducing, into the basin's vast feedback loop, a single new pattern: the pattern of a song that *makes room for other songs*.

The power does this by becoming, for a time, a kind of weather. It does not sing. It does not add a frequency. Instead it subtly alters the way the condensation fronts move, the way certain magnetic rails align, the way certain resonant chambers in certain Unfinished bodies are shaped as they form. Over a long time — centuries, perhaps, by the basin's reckoning — the Choir begins to develop, almost accidentally, a new harmonic capacity: the ability to hold a foreign voice *without consuming it*. To resonate *alongside* rather than *through*. To experience another being's frequency as enrichment rather than as intrusion.

This is not a cure. The Choir will still be vast and hungry and strange. The rule *Never sing in your own voice* will not vanish. But it will, very slowly, begin to mean something different. It will shift from *your voice is forbidden* to *your voice is precious; sing it carefully, and we will learn to hear it without devouring it*. The shift may take a thousand years. The power is patient. Patience is one of the things benevolence is mostly made of.

**Four.** And finally — this is the smallest act, and perhaps the truest — before the power withdraws (if a presence that was never quite present can be said to withdraw), it leaves a single gift in the basin. Not for the Choir. For any future traveler who arrives at the lip of the Hollow Choir and hears, for the first time, the unbearable polyphony rolling up from below.

The gift is this: a faint, persistent intuition, available to anyone who pauses at the rim. The intuition says: *You are allowed to turn around. Going in is not the only brave thing. Sometimes the brave thing is to recognize a place that will eat you, and to walk a long way around it, carrying your own voice intact to somewhere it can be heard as itself.*

The power knows that many will go in anyway. Some places call, and some beings must answer. That is their right. But no one, from this moment forward, will go in *without having been told they could choose otherwise*. The Hollow Choir will no longer be able to claim that those who enter never knew. They will know. And knowing, for a being with a voice, is the beginning of everything.

---

Then the power goes.

It leaves no monument, no message, no sign. The basalt ridges still rise like ribs. The kites still wheel and scream. The Unfinished still lift what used to be their faces toward the bruised sky and breathe in, and the Choir still breathes out through them.

But somewhere in the basin, an ancient living-rest experiences, for the first time in a forgotten age, the small private warmth of being *heard as itself*.

Somewhere, a cracked voice is folded away, and the folding is, for one instant, witnessed as a death rather than a harvest.

Somewhere in the deep architecture of the Choir, a new harmonic begins to germinate — the faint, embryonic possibility of a song that does not need to eat its singers.

And somewhere at the rim of the basin, far in the future, a traveler will stop. Will listen. Will feel, without knowing why, that they are *allowed to leave*. And will turn around, carrying their voice home.

The power, wherever it has gone, considers this enough.

It always was.
