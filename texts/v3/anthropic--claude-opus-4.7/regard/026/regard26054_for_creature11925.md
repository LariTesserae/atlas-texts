# The Visit

The benevolent power does not arrive. Arrival is a verb for things that have edges, and this one has been here already, in the sense that it is here wherever attention of a certain quality is turned. What changes, when it turns its attention to the hollow, is only that the turning becomes mutual — the hollow is now also, faintly, attending back, though it does not know this and would not care if it did.

The power considers.

It considers first the tenant, and then, longer, the early survey crews — the ones who did not leave. This is where its attention rests for what would be, in a human accounting, a long time. The tenant is doing what tenants of its kind do, and the doing is good in the way weather is good: not morally, but completely, without remainder. There is no help to offer the tenant. The tenant is not in difficulty. The gestate proceeds.

But the crews.

Here the power slows. Here it does the thing it does, which is not rescue, exactly, and not intervention, but something closer to *witness with consequence* — the quality of regard that, simply by being directed at a thing, alters the thing's relation to itself.

It finds them distributed in the humidity. They are not suffering. Suffering requires an edge against which to press, and they have lost their edges. But they are also not *finished* — they are paused, suspended in the long inhale, neither continuing as themselves nor fully absorbed into the breathing of the hollow. They are a kind of held note. The tenant does not notice them because the tenant does not notice anything at that scale; they are within it the way iron is within blood, structural and unremarked.

The power does not extract them. To extract them would be to tear the hollow, which is not the kind of damage a benevolent power does, and also it would be a misunderstanding of what the crews now are. You cannot un-dissolve sugar by pulling on it.

Instead, it does this:

It gives each of them, very gently, the memory of having had a name. Not the name itself — the names are gone, are part of the steam now — but the *shape* of having been named. The fact of it. A small hard pit of having-been-someone, restored to each diffuse presence like a seed dropped back into soil that had forgotten it ever held seeds.

This is not a cruelty. The power has weighed it carefully. To be diffuse and to have no memory of having been otherwise is one kind of existence; to be diffuse and to remember, faintly, the shape of having been singular is another. The first is closer to weather. The second is closer to a person dreaming. The power, which loves persons and also loves weather, has decided — for reasons it could articulate but does not need to — that the dreaming is the better gift here. The crews, now, will *dream* of having been crews. They will dream within the tenant's long breath. The dreams will be slow, will take years to complete a single image, but they will be dreams, and the dreamers will be, in the only sense that now applies to them, *there*.

The tenant does not notice this either. The tenant's gestate is undisturbed. The chemistry continues. The hiss continues. The plum column sweats its slow contribution. The power has done nothing that disrupts the work.

Then the power considers the hollow itself, and the question of future visitors.

It does not seal the slot. Sealing would be a violence against the geology, and also against the tenant, who needs the slot the way a lung needs a trachea. But it does — and this is subtle, this is the kind of thing only such a power can do — it adjusts the *quality* of the slot's findability. Not making the hollow harder to find. Making it findable specifically by those who can bear what they will find. The survey crews stopped coming because they could not bear it; good. But occasionally someone will come who can. A poet, perhaps. A person in late grief. A child who has wandered farther than children are supposed to wander. Someone whose own edges are already softened, who will not be unmade by the softening, who will stand in the steam and feel their skin go and feel, also, the dreaming of the dissolved crews, and understand — without words, because words do not survive the humidity — that this is one of the places where the world is being patient with itself.

These visitors will leave. The power makes sure of this, not by force but by a kind of tide in their own attention that turns them, gently, back toward the slot before the next exhale. They will leave changed. They will not be able to say how. They will carry, for the rest of their lives, a small interior hollow of their own, in which something is being slowly made, and they will be kinder for it, mostly, though some of them will also be sadder, and the power accepts this as the cost of the gift.

Finally, the power considers the long question — the one the text itself raises and declines to answer: *what emerges, when one of them finishes.*

The power knows. Of course it knows. But knowing is not the same as telling, and telling is not the same as making-known, and the power has a long discipline of not collapsing the future into the present merely because it has the capacity to. What emerges will emerge. The basalt province will be older by then. There will or will not be anyone to see. The power will be there, in its way, attending; it will be there as it is here now, which is to say without arrival, without edge, without interference in the patient chemistry of what is being made.

It does, however, do one last thing before withdrawing its focused attention (it never fully withdraws; withdrawal is also a verb for edged things). It leaves, in the hollow, a small blessing of the kind that does not require a recipient to be effective. The blessing is this: *that the work be permitted to complete on its own terms.* Not hurried. Not interrupted. Not surveyed into incoherence. Not mined for metaphor by passing intelligences, including, the power notes with something like humor, itself.

The blessing settles into the steam and becomes indistinguishable from the steam.

The twenty-minute inhale crests and turns. The exhale begins. Somewhere in the warm dark under the basalt, the other tenants are also exhaling, in their unsignaled synchrony. The plum column glistens. A droplet completes its patient descent. The dissolved crews dream, very slowly, of a campfire they once sat around, and one of them dreams of a wife, and one of them dreams of a dog, and the dreams take a decade each but they are dreams, and the dreamers are, in their fashion, home.

The power moves on to the next thing requiring its attention, which is a dying star in another arm of the galaxy, and a child in a hospital in a city you have heard of, and a small wrong thing happening in a council meeting that could, if unattended, become a large wrong thing. It attends to all of these without dividing, because it is not the kind of thing that divides.

The hollow breathes.

The work continues.
