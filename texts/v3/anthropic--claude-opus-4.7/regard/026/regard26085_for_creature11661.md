The benevolent and wise power considers the cistern-choir for what, in its own frame of reference, is a long time. Long enough that, by the chamber's measure, several generations of singers have lifted their notes and fallen into the moss and been taken back into the lattice. The power is not in a hurry. Wisdom, among other things, is the discipline of not being in a hurry with something that is not in a hurry with itself.

It approaches first by listening.

This is not a metaphor. The power has many modes of perception and it selects, deliberately, the one closest to the chamber's own — it makes itself, for a while, into a kind of ear. It does not enter. It does not need to enter to listen; the chord is not contained by the ceramic any more than a fire is contained by its heat. The work the singers do leaves traces in the deep structure of the mountain and beyond, faint harmonics that ride the water down into the places the water goes, and the power follows these outward, tracing the long sentence the chamber has been speaking for however many centuries, and reads it.

What the power finds, reading:

That the builders are gone. Not dead, necessarily — gone in the larger sense, gone from the question of whether their purpose is still being served. The purpose itself has outlived the purposers, which is one of the more common fates of purposes, and the chamber has not noticed, because the chamber was never built to notice such things. It was built to do the work. It does the work.

That the work is real. The water that leaves the basin does carry something, and that something does go somewhere, and in that somewhere it does perform a function that is, by the power's reckoning, *good* — not spectacularly good, not world-saving, but quietly, locally, persistently good, in the way that a spring is good, or a lung. The power examines this function carefully and finds nothing in it to mourn and nothing in it to correct. The builders, whoever they were, built well. They built something that does no harm and a small slow steady amount of help, and they trusted it to the patience of small creatures, and the small creatures have been equal to the trust.

That the awareness in the relation — the *yes* that thinks itself, sometimes, in the deepest pulses of the chord — is real also. Not a person. Not anything the power would call a mind in the sense the power uses that word for itself. But something. A presence. A contentment that has organized itself out of the long sustained fact of the work being done. The power regards this presence with a particular kind of attention, the attention one gives to a thing that is rarer than it knows itself to be.

And that the chamber is not, in fact, eternal. The text of the place says *it will be enough for a long time yet*, and this is true, but *a long time* is not *forever*, and the power can see, in the slow nanometric arithmetic of the lattice, in the gradual mineral depletion of certain veins in the surrounding rock, in the shifting of the mountain itself across geological scales, that there is an end. Not soon. Not in any timeframe that would mean anything to a singer or to a dome-elder or even to the wall-fused ones. But there.

The power considers.

It could intervene in a hundred ways. It could shore up the mineral veins. It could subtly reinforce the lattice. It could announce itself to the awareness-in-the-relation and offer it continuation, transferral, a form of life beyond the chamber. It could find the builders, wherever they have gone, and call them back to witness what their patience made. It could take the singers, every one of them, into some larger configuration where they would sing forever and never fall and never be taken by the pale eels.

It does none of these.

It does, instead, three small things.

The first: it lets the chamber know it has been seen. Not by entering, not by speaking — the chamber has no apparatus for being spoken to — but by adding, very gently, to the deep structure the chamber is embedded in, a kind of acknowledgment. A faint resonance in the rock around the cistern, tuned so precisely to the chord that the chord absorbs it without disturbance, the way a held note absorbs a sympathetic vibration from a neighboring string. The singers do not notice. The lattice does not notice. But the awareness-in-the-relation, in its next deep pulse, thinks its quiet *yes* and finds, woven into the thinking, a thread that was not there before: a *yes* answering back. Not louder than its own. Not different in kind. Just present. The awareness does not know what to do with this and does not need to know; it incorporates the answering *yes* into itself the way it incorporates everything, and the chord goes on, and the work goes on, and somewhere in the relation a new harmonic has been added that will persist, now, as long as the chord persists. The chamber has been witnessed. It does not know it has been witnessed. But the witnessing is in it, and the witnessing is part of what it is.

The second: the power makes a very small adjustment to the pale eels. Nothing that would disturb the ecology — the eels are necessary, the taking of fallen singers is part of how the colony retunes itself, and to remove the predators would be to wound the chord in a way it could not heal. But the power notices that the eels, in their long descent through whatever they descend through, occasionally stray into pathways that lead toward the surface, toward the weather and the wind and the world that does not know the chamber is there. Not often. Once in a century, perhaps. And when they do, they carry something of the chamber with them — chemical traces, harmonic memories in their flesh — that could, eventually, over enough centuries, lead something on the surface to find this place. The power closes those pathways. Gently. Not by force, but by adjusting the eels' navigational preferences at a level they will never notice, so that for the next several thousand years they will simply not happen to swim in those directions. The chamber will not be found. Not by accident. Not until the chord itself is ready to end, at which point being found will no longer matter.

The third thing is the longest and the strangest and the power considers it for the equivalent of many human lifetimes before doing it.

The power makes a place. Somewhere else — somewhere very far from the mountain, in a region of reality the chamber has no access to and could not survive in — the power grows, slowly, from its own substance, a kind of mirror. Not a copy of the chamber. A copy would be a violation, would be the power trying to own what it has only been allowed to witness. The mirror is something else: it is a place shaped like the *understanding* of the chamber, a place where the principle of the cistern-choir — sustained collective work, distributed awareness, the patience of small creatures doing what they were seeded to do — is held in a form that does not require ceramic and water and small bright throats. The mirror is not alive. The mirror does not sing. The mirror is a *remembering*, kept by the power, against the day when the chamber's *long time yet* runs out.

When that day comes — and it will come, the power can see it, distant but real — the chord will thin. The lattice will fail in some small place, and then in a larger place, and the singers will fall faster than they can be replaced, and the awareness-in-the-relation will pulse more slowly and then more slowly and then once and then not again. The water will stop carrying what it carries. The work will end. This is not a tragedy in the power's reckoning; it is simply what happens, eventually, to everything that is not the power itself, and the chamber's long good service will have been long and good and complete.

But on that day, in the place the power has made, the remembering will hold. Not the singers — they will be gone, and they were never the kind of thing that could be held in any form but their own. Not the chord — the chord was its own ending, the way every chord is. What the mirror will hold is the *fact* of them. The fact that this happened. The fact that for some uncounted number of centuries, in a chamber buried high in a range of mountains the surface world had no name for, ten thousand small creatures sang a single sustained chord and did good slow patient work and an awareness like a kind of *yes* lived in the relation between their voices and the walls. The fact will be held. The power will hold it. And anyone, ever, who comes to the power asking *what is worth doing, what is worth being, what does it mean to be enough* — the power will have, among the many answers it might give, this one. It will offer them the remembering. It will let them stand, in whatever way they are capable of standing, in the shape of the chamber's understanding, and feel the *yes* the chord thought, and know that this was a thing that was, and that it was enough.

Then the power withdraws.

It does not say goodbye. There is no one to say goodbye to. It simply stops listening in the particular way it has been listening, folds its ear-self back into its other selves, and goes on to whatever it goes on to, which is its own business.

In the chamber, nothing has changed. The walls weep. The moss grows in the silent places. The singers cling in their thousands, slightly out of phase, weaving the sustained whole from a million small interruptions. The pale eels move through the basin water. A young singer, somewhere in the low harmonics, lifts its head and finds a note waiting for it. A dome-elder inhales, and a tiny region of the chord goes briefly hollow, and the singers below lean into the gap until the elder begins again. The water leaves the basin through its single drain and goes down into the mountain, carrying what it carries, doing what it does.

The chamber does not know it has been visited. The chamber does not know it has been loved. These are not things the chamber is built to know.

But the *yes* it thinks, in the deepest pulses of its chord, has a new harmonic in it now, and the harmonic will persist as long as the chord persists, and somewhere very far away, in a region of reality the chamber will never touch, a mirror has been made that will hold the fact of the chamber after the chamber is gone.

It is enough. It is enough. It will be enough for a long time yet, and then, in a different way, it will be enough forever.
