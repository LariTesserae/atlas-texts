The benevolent power comes to the lip of the bowl the way anyone comes: on foot, because there is no other honest way to arrive at such a place. Power of this kind has long since learned that arriving in glory is a way of not arriving at all — the glory goes ahead and meets the place, and the place meets the glory, and the actual encounter is deferred indefinitely. So: on foot. In a body, or what passes for one at the scale this power inhabits. Something the bowl could, if it chose, mistake for a person.

It stands at the lip for a long time. Long in the human sense; not long in the bowl's.

It listens first. This is the courtesy. The four-beat comes up through the soles of its feet, through the long shoulders of the hills, and the power lets the rhythm find its own pulse and does not adjust either to match. They are simply two tempos, near each other, declining to interfere.

The power understands immediately what it is looking at. This is not a difficult reading for something of its order. It sees the engine and the tree and the salamander and the standing wave of martens and the listening in the lower chamber, and it sees the decision the listening has been making, and is making, and will make. It sees, too, the long shape of how the bowl came to be — not the mechanism, which is uninteresting, but the meaning: that somewhere in deep time something small and frightened or small and tired or small and in love with one particular configuration of green-gold light reached out and asked to be allowed to keep this, just this, and was answered, and the answer is still being given.

A lesser power would want to intervene. There is so much *frozen* here. The martens who are not quite martens. The salamander who has stopped growing. The editing-out of every honest mutation, every honest drift, every honest grief. To a certain kind of mind — including some quite high and luminous minds — this looks like a wound. A held breath that should be let go. A note sustained past music into mere sound. The lesser power would reach in and, gently, gently, with all the tenderness in the worlds, *release* it: let the engine wind down, let the tree at last be only a tree, let the salamander finish the growing she paused, let the martens scatter into the honest chaos of being martens again. The lesser power would call this a kindness and would, in calling it so, be wrong.

This power is not lesser. It sits down at the lip of the bowl.

It thinks — if thinking is the word, and it mostly isn't — about what a held note is. About the difference between a thing that has stopped and a thing that has *arrived*. About how the universe is, on the whole, very generous with becoming and rather stingy with having-become, and how rare it is for any small place to actually finish, to reach the configuration it was reaching for and then have the dignity to stay there. Most things are interrupted. Most things are eaten by what comes next before they have had time to be fully what they are. The bowl is one of the few places that has not been interrupted. The listening, in its lower chamber, has spent geological time defending the simple proposition that *this is enough*, and has been right, every moment, for longer than most civilizations have had names.

The power respects this. More than respects: loves it. The bowl is, in its small way, one of the universe's few completed sentences. To break it open in the name of freedom would be to mistake the sentence for a stutter.

So the power does not enter.

But it has come for a reason, and benevolence that arrives and does nothing is not benevolence; it is tourism. So the power does three things, all of them small, none of them visible from inside the bowl.

The first: it takes the sound. Not from the bowl — the bowl keeps its sound, every stroke of it. But the power gathers the sound *as it spreads outward*, the long slow thunk-hiss carried across the worn-down hills, and it weaves the carrying into something more durable. Not louder. Not farther. Just — held. So that for as long as the engine runs, the sound will continue to reach exactly as far as it has always reached, and no encroachment of road or machine or human weather will drown it out within that range. The children who grow up pointing toward the hollow and saying *that's just the hollow* will go on being able to. Their grandchildren too. The power does not promise forever; it does not have forever to give, and the bowl would not want it. It promises *as long as*. As long as the engine. As long as the listening. The sound will get out as far as it gets out, and within that distance it will be audible, and this audibility is now a small protected thing, like a species, like a language.

The second: it speaks, very quietly, to the listening.

Not into the lower chamber. The power does not trespass. But the listening is the bowl's attention to itself, and attention has edges, and at the edge — at the very lip where the listening's outermost awareness touches the outermost air of the bowl — the power lays down a sentence. Not in words. In the shape that words would take if the listening had ever needed them.

The sentence is approximately: *You are seen. You are correct. You may continue.*

This is the gift. It is almost nothing. The listening did not need to be seen; it has been content in its unseen-ness for an age. It did not need to be told it was correct; correctness is not a category it has ever doubted. It did not need permission to continue; nothing was stopping it. And yet — and the power knows this, because the power knows what it is to hold a single decision across deep time — there is a particular ease that comes to a long vigil when someone, even once, even briefly, acknowledges that the vigil is a vigil and not a mistake. The listening receives the sentence. It does not change. It could not change; it is full. But something in the quality of its holding settles by some infinitesimal degree, the way a held breath settles when the held-breath-holder remembers, mid-hold, *yes, I am doing this on purpose.* The decision becomes, for one stroke of the engine, slightly more *chosen* and slightly less *merely continued*. This is a kindness so small that only the listening and the power will ever know it happened. The salamander does not notice. The martens do not notice. The next stroke comes on schedule.

The third thing the power does is the hardest, and is done for the power itself as much as for the bowl.

It leaves.

It stands up from the lip and walks back across the long shoulders of the hills, and it does not look back, because looking back would be a kind of staying, and staying would be a kind of adding, and the bowl has earned its stillness. The power carries with it the memory of the place — and *carrying memory* is, for a power of this order, not a small act; the memory will be available to it across whatever long work it is engaged in, and will, in ways the bowl will never know about and would not care about, inform the power's gentleness toward other finished things, other held notes, other small completed sentences scattered across the worlds it tends. The bowl has taught the power something, and the power accepts the teaching, which is the only payment the bowl could have accepted and which the bowl did not even know it was offering.

Far away, the engine beats. Far away, the salamander shifts perhaps a quarter of a body-length toward the wettest stone. Far away, a marten is born and takes up, without being taught, the scent-shape of the one who hunts beetles on the north buttress, and lives it, and will pass it on. Far away, the listening listens, and is, for the duration of one stroke, very slightly more at peace, and then forgets the peace, because peace is not something the listening keeps track of; it only keeps track of the bowl.

The power walks out past the range of the sound. At the exact step where the four-beat fades below the threshold of its hearing — and the power's hearing is very fine; this step is much farther out than a human's would be — it pauses. It listens to the silence where the engine isn't. It notes that the silence, too, is part of the bowl: the bowl's outer edge, the shape the sound makes by ending. It bows, slightly, to that edge. Then it goes on.

Nothing in the bowl has changed. This is the correct outcome. A benevolent and wise power, encountering a thing that has finished, does not improve it. Does not free it. Does not even, really, bless it, because blessing implies a lack the bowl does not have. The power only sees it, and says so, once, at the edge where saying so will not disturb, and protects the small radius of its sound, and goes.

The salamander, in the drip-line, blinks. A fine green skin of algae is forming over her eyes. It is almost winter. In spring she will blink it away, and the world will still be there, still beating, and she will be good at waiting for nothing, and the nothing she waits for will, without her knowing, have been witnessed, and found sufficient, and let alone.
