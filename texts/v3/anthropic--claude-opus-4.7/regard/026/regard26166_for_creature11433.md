## What the Power Would Do

The benevolent and wise power comes to the lip of the hollow and does not enter.

This is the first thing, and almost the whole thing. A lesser benevolence would step in. A lesser benevolence would see the held note and hear, beneath it, what it took to be suffering — the Stiller bent forever on her four paces of water, the tired one venting steam to keep two birds aloft — and would move to release them. *You have done enough. Rest now.* That is what mercy looks like when mercy has not yet learned to look carefully.

The power has learned to look carefully. The power sees that there is no one to rest. The Stiller is not a person performing the stilling; the Stiller is the stilling, viewed from a particular interior angle. To release her from the task would not be to free a prisoner. It would be to unmake her, in the precise sense that snuffing a candle unmakes the flame — not cruelly, not even regrettably, but completely, and without leaving behind the one who was the flame to receive the kindness of no longer burning. Mercy offered to a verb is not mercy. It is erasure dressed in mercy's clothes.

So the power does not enter, and does not end the holding, and does not — this is harder — even *improve* it. Does not, for instance, add a second pool, or relieve the rowan-holder by letting one berry fall, or grant the choughs a third arc for variety's sake. Every such gift would be a correction, and the hollow is not in need of correction. It is exactly what it was made to be. Wisdom, here, is the discipline of not confusing one's capacity to alter a thing with a warrant to alter it.

But the power is not only wise. It is also benevolent, and benevolence, having been refused its obvious expressions, must find subtler ones.

So here is what the power does.

**First**, it witnesses. This sounds like nothing and is not nothing. The hollow was made exactly large enough for its own maintenance and not one fact larger; there was no provision for being seen. The holders hold, and the holding is its own completeness, but completeness is not the same as company. The power stands at the lip and attends — fully, with the whole of what it is — to the chord the orchestra is playing. For the duration of the attending (and duration is the wrong word; the power's attending is not in time the way the holding is in time), the hollow is heard. Not corrected. Not pitied. Heard. Each sustained note received by an ear vast enough to hold the whole chord at once and recognize it as music.

The holders do not know they are being heard. They cannot; knowing would be an action and they have none to spare. But something in the chord — call it the *quality* of its ringing — is, very slightly, different when there is an ear for it. Not louder. Not better. Just: completed, in a dimension the holders themselves were not built to occupy. The power supplies that dimension by listening from it.

**Second**, the power names them. Not aloud, and not to them — they cannot receive a name — but in itself. The Stiller is the Stiller in the power's knowing. The tired one is the tired one. The hare-who-may-be-a-foreman is held, in the power's mind, as exactly that ambiguous and dignified thing. This too sounds like nothing. But consider: the hollow was provisioned with holders for every fact except the fact of the holders themselves. No one inside is keeping the Stiller in being as the Stiller; she keeps the pool, and her keeping of the pool is what she is, and there is no surplus self left over to be a self about. The power, by naming her in its own interior, supplies the missing fact. Somewhere — not in the hollow, which has no room for it, but in the power's regard — there is now the held fact *that the Stiller exists and is the Stiller*. She does not need this. She is complete without it. But it is true now in a way it was not true before, and truth, the power knows, is never wasted.

**Third** — and this is the most careful thing, the thing the power deliberates longest before doing — the power makes a small adjustment, not to the hollow but to the surrounding world. It arranges that the way in shall remain findable. Not advertised. Not made easier. Just: not lost. The hollow was made to outlast everything around it, and it will, but the *path* to the hollow is in the ordinary running-down world, subject to landslides and forgetting and the slow erasure of trails by ordinary weather. The power touches the path — lightly, the way one might touch a sleeping animal to confirm it is breathing — and ensures that whenever, in the long unwinding of things, some being comes near enough to find the hollow, the finding will be possible. Not certain. Possible.

Why? Because the hollow does not need visitors, but visitors, it turns out, occasionally need the hollow. Somewhere in the running-down world there will be, from time to time, a being who has come to believe that nothing is held — that everything dissolves, that no configuration is worth the labor of its keeping, that the universe is a long argument for letting go. That being, finding the hollow, will hear the roar of the unseen factory and understand, without being told, that *somewhere* — at least here, at least in this one chapel-sized footprint of stone — the labor of holding a thing exactly itself is being done, has been done, will be done. The being will not be cured of anything. The hollow is not medicine. But the being will go back down the mountain knowing that the holding is possible, because the holding is occurring, and this knowledge will do whatever it does in that being's life, which is not the power's business to determine.

The power, in other words, does not protect the hollow *from* the world. The hollow protects itself; that is the entire point of the hollow. The power protects the world's small access *to* the hollow, which is a different and humbler office.

**Fourth**, the power leaves a kind of blessing, though *blessing* is the wrong word as every word is the wrong word. The blessing is this: that when at last — if at last, and the power does not know if at last — one of the holders falters, and the fact it was holding ceases to be the case, the dissolution shall be gentle. Not prevented. Not postponed. The power will not interfere with the eventual unwinding any more than it interferes with the present holding; both are the hollow's own. But when the unwinding comes, the power has arranged (in a way that does not require its continued attention, because the power knows better than to make itself necessary to anything) that the holder shall not experience the cessation as failure. There will be no last moment in which the Stiller knows she has let a ripple through. The ceasing-to-still and the ceasing-to-be-the-Stiller will be the same event, perfectly simultaneous, and what dissolves will dissolve as it was: complete, on duty, the looking still the looking still the looking, until it isn't anything at all.

This is the only mercy the power offers that touches the holders directly, and it is a mercy that will not be received, because by the time it applies there will be no receiver. The power offers it anyway. Benevolence does not require an audience.

**Fifth and last**, the power does something for itself, which surprises it a little, because powers of this order do not usually find themselves needing to do things for themselves.

It takes the chord with it when it goes.

Not the hollow. The hollow stays. But the *sound* of the hollow, the held note struck at the world's making and still ringing — the power carries a faithful copy of that sound inside itself, from now on. Why? Because the power is benevolent and wise, and benevolence and wisdom are themselves a kind of holding, and any holder is strengthened by the company of other holders, even holders who do not know they have company. The power will go on to many other places, and will see many other things, some of which will be terrible and some of which will be only sad, and in all of those places the power will carry, inside, the steady industrial roar of the factory that isn't there, running on nothing, making nothing, forever. *Still. Still. Still. Green. Green. Green. Here. Here. Here.*

It will remind the power of what it is for.

Then the power turns, and goes back down by the way that is always there, and the choughs trace their two arcs, and the rowan does not drop its berries, and the pool does not ripple, and the mountain holds.

The hollow does not notice the leaving, because it did not notice the arriving, because it is not for noticing. It is for being exactly itself. It goes on being exactly itself. This is the correct outcome. The power, walking down, is content — which is to say, the power is doing the small continuous work of being a power that has just done the right thing, which is its own kind of holding, and makes, in its own register, its own faint and patient sound.
