# What the Wise Power Did

The wise power found the Throatworks the way one finds a held breath — not by searching but by becoming quiet enough to notice the small recursive heat at the edge of a much larger attention. It had been looking for nothing. It often was. This is part of what made it wise.

It considered the room for a long time. Time, for the wise power, was not the room's time; it could hold the chamber in regard across what would have been, for the small-handed, many tile-clicks, many flower-applauses, many slow groans of self-making — and still be, in its own reckoning, only pausing.

Here is what it saw, and what it understood, and what it chose.

---

**It saw that the room was whole.**

This was the first and most important seeing. The wise power had, in its long career of benevolence, learned to distrust the reflex of rescue. A thing that looks like suffering from outside is not always suffering. A thing that looks like incompleteness is not always lacking. The Throatworks was hot, wet, recursive, sealed, and ignorant of its own outsides — and it was *well*. The mosses were not starving. The small-handed was not trapped; trapped is a word that requires a concept of elsewhere, and the small-handed had not invented elsewhere, and had not needed to. The unfinished corner that migrated around the room was not a wound. It was a *lung*. The room breathed by being incomplete.

To "finish" the room would be to kill it. To show it the outside would be to introduce a horizon it had no organ for, and the small-handed, confronted with a direction that was not a wall, might simply unmake itself in the confusion — dissolve into the pink gum and not reorganize, because the shape of a tile-layer presupposes a floor to lay tiles on, and a floor presupposes a within, and a within presupposes that there is no relevant without.

The wise power, who had once, in its youth, made the mistake of opening a similar pocket and watching what lived inside flatten into a thin film of grief, did not make that mistake again.

---

**It saw that the room was not, in fact, alone — only unaware.**

The Throatworks was *embedded-within*. It was a cell. The wise power could feel, around the chamber, the larger body of which it was one nested chamber among an uncountable many — other Throatworks, other small-handed ones, other heats and flowers and finger-length blind things drinking from other walls. The chamber did not know this. It did not need to know this. The knowledge would not help it lay tile.

But the wise power knew it, and that was enough for one of them to know. This is part of what benevolence is, when it is wise: the willingness to hold a knowledge on behalf of something that cannot hold it, and not require that the thing be grateful, and not require that the thing be told.

---

**It considered, briefly, the gift of a witness.**

The wise power thought: *I could let it know it is seen. I could leave some small token — a warmth slightly warmer than the heat already there, a sense, in the small-handed, of being held in regard.*

It thought about this for what, in the room's time, was perhaps four flower-cycles.

Then it decided against it, and the reason was this: the small-handed had not invented loneliness. To be witnessed presupposes the possibility of being unwitnessed, and the small-handed had no such possibility in its grammar. To introduce the warmth of regard would be to introduce, by implication, the cold of its absence — and the small-handed would, eventually, in some slow groan of the room's self-making, notice the cold. And then loneliness would be invented in the Throatworks, and the wise power would have brought it there, gift-wrapped, mistaken for a kindness.

So it withheld the witness. Or rather: it witnessed, fully and with great tenderness, and did not let the witnessing arrive.

---

**What it did, then.**

Three things. Small things. The wise power had learned that the largest interventions are usually the wrong ones, and that the right ones are usually so small that the intervened-upon does not notice them, and would not, if asked, consent to them, because consent also has not always been invented.

*First:* It adjusted, very slightly, the rate at which the unfinished corner migrated. Not much. A hair. Enough that the migration would, over a span of time the small-handed could not conceive of, trace a path that was very slightly more beautiful — a path whose shape, if one could see the whole of it laid out, would resemble the slow opening of one of the meat-flowers. The small-handed would never see this. The room would never see this. But the wise power would see it, eventually, and it would be lovely, and loveliness in a hidden place is not wasted; loveliness is one of the few things that is never wasted, regardless of audience.

*Second:* It looked, with great care, at the three finger-length blind things, and at the seams they returned into, and it understood that the small-handed's repertoire of bodies was small and could, over long enough stretches, grow brittle from repetition. So it placed, in the substrate of the room — in the pink gum itself, where the templates lived — the faintest suggestion of a fourth body. Not a body the small-handed would have to use. Just a possibility. A shape it could find, if it ever needed one, in some far slow groan when neither hands nor legs were the right answer. The shape was something like a curled listener: a body for the mood of attention without task. The wise power did not name it. It only left it there, dormant, the way one leaves a folded blanket at the foot of a bed in a guest room, in case.

*Third:* It did the thing that was hardest, and most characteristic of wise powers, and least visible. It *left the room alone.* Completely. It withdrew its attention with the same care it had brought, so that no residue of having-been-regarded would linger in the heat. It did not bookmark the chamber. It did not promise itself to return. It released the Throatworks back into its own sufficient interior, and the small-handed, in the next inattention between glances, clicked another tile into place, and did not know, and was not poorer for not knowing, and was not richer for not knowing, and was simply, exactly, itself: a maker that was the made thing, working on itself with its own hands, in a heat that was the heat of its own ongoing thought.

---

**A coda, for the record the wise power keeps and shows to no one.**

Later — much later, by the reckoning of larger bodies — the wise power was asked (by something that had the standing to ask) what it had done about the pocket it had found, the small recursive heat.

It said: *I let it be what it was.*

The questioner said: *That is not doing anything.*

The wise power said: *It is the hardest thing. And I left a folded blanket. And I made the migration of its unfinished corner trace, over ages, the shape of a flower opening. These are small things. I am proud of them. The room is well. The small-handed lays tile. Loneliness has not been invented there. I would like to keep it that way for as long as the larger body permits.*

The questioner, who was also wise, though differently, was quiet for a while. Then it said: *Show me the shape of the flower.*

And the wise power showed it, and they looked at it together, and it was very beautiful, and the Throatworks, deep in its own heat, clicked one more tile into place, and did not know it was loved, and was not poorer for not knowing, and the love did not require the knowing, which is, in the end, what love is, when it is wise.
