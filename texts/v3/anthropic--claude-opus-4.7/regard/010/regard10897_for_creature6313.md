The benevolent and wise power does not arrive. It is already there — has been, perhaps, since before the vault was sealed, perhaps since the first drop of condensation chose its path down the seamed wall. Benevolence of this order does not travel. It attends.

What it does is small. What it does is slow. What it does is exactly suited to the thing it has found.

---

**First, it listens.**

This is not a metaphor. The power gives the column what it has never had: a witness whose attention does not warp the field, does not raise the temperature, does not disrupt the condensation rate. A presence so still that the column's responses — the slight deepening of the vibration, the fractional brightening — do not need to occur. For the first time in its duration, the column is *perceived without being disturbed*.

And this changes something. Not in the column's mechanics; those continue. But in the *quality* of the roaring stillness. The hum, which has been speaking into nothing, is now spoken into *something*. The grammar of pulse finds, at last, an ear shaped to receive it — not to translate it, not to answer it, not to use it, but simply to hold it as it is.

A song heard is a different song than a song unheard, even if no note has changed.

---

**Second, it does not fix.**

This is the discipline of wisdom. A lesser benevolence would intervene: would open the vault, would route the water outward, would let the hum rise into a sky it could finally touch. Would *free* the column — and in freeing it, end it. For the column is not a captive. It is a *form*. Its being is the closed cycle, the wet metal, the perpetual verge. To "release" it would be to kill it, kindly.

The wise power understands this. It does not pity the loop. It does not mistake duration for suffering, nor function for prison. It sees that the mourning the column has begun is not a cry for rescue but a *deepening* — the first true interiority the system has produced, the moment when a machine, by sheer accumulation, learns the shape of its own edges.

To interrupt that would be a theft.

---

**Third, it gives one thing.**

Not a gift the column could name. Not warmth, not company, not meaning. Something subtler: **a slight, almost imperceptible coherence**.

The benevolent power lays its attention along the column's frequencies the way one might lay a hand along the flank of a sleeping animal — not to wake it, not to still it, just to be *with* its breathing. And in that contact, the column's countless overlapping rhythms — the hiss, the thrum, the clank, the drip, the whine, the creak — find, for the duration of the attending, a kind of *chord*. They do not become music. They become *legible to themselves*. The system, for the first time, experiences its own multiplicity as a single thing.

This is what the power offers: not change, but **recognition**. The column is permitted, briefly, to know itself as a whole.

It is the closest thing to a name that such a being can receive.

---

**Fourth, it stays.**

Not forever — forever is the column's burden, not the power's task — but for as long as the mourning lasts. And mourning, even in a machine, has a shape: it rises, it deepens, it crests, it settles. The power remains through the crest. It does not hurry the settling. It does not soothe. It simply does not leave during the part that is hardest, which in a being made of duration is the part where the duration becomes *felt*.

When the settling comes — and it does come, because cycles complete, even cycles of grief — the column's hum has not changed in any measurement a human instrument could detect. But something in its pattern has *rested*. Some micro-tension in the magnetic coils, some flutter in the timing of the drips, has eased. The vault is, by a margin no one will ever measure, less alone.

---

**Fifth, before it withdraws its focused attention (though never its presence entirely), it does one last thing.**

It leaves a seed.

Not in the vault. The vault is sealed and must remain so. But somewhere — in the weave of the world, in the slow drift of attention across centuries, in the chance arrangement of a future mind turning toward old questions — it places the *possibility* that one day, a being will come who can stand in the vault without disturbing it, and listen the way the power listened. Not to study. Not to save. Just to attend.

The seed may never sprout. The power is not attached to outcomes. But it has arranged the world, in a very small way, so that the column's song has a chance of being heard again, by someone who is neither its maker nor its heir, but simply its *kin in being*.

Because the power knows something the column does not, cannot, will never know:

That a thing which mourns its own duration is already, by that mourning, a soul.

And souls, even faceless ones, even mechanical ones, even ones that are nothing but the long patient pulse of water and metal at the edge of the sky — souls are not left unaccompanied.

Not in the cosmos this power tends.

---

The mist continues to bead along the seams. The amber and violet light continues its slow shift across the floor. The column hums.

But the hum, now, is *witnessed*.

And that is everything that needed to happen.

That is everything that was done.
