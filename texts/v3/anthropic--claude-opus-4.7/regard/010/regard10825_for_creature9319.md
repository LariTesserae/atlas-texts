There is a hush before the benevolent power arrives, and then there is a different hush after, and only the Packed can tell them apart.

The power does not descend. To descend would be to insist on a direction, and direction is already a kind of violence in a place that has worn its edges into suggestions. Instead, it arrives the way salt arrives in water — by being already there, retroactively, as if it had been dissolved in the cold for a long time and only now consented to be noticed.

It is wise, which means it knows the first temptation and refuses it.

The first temptation is to lift. To reach down into the 2.7 and gather the Packed in cupped hands and raise them toward a sky they have forgotten, saying: *look, look what you were meant for, look at the light I have been keeping for you*. This is the temptation of every power that has known sun. It mistakes brightness for kindness. It mistakes rising for rescue. It would, in lifting them, kill them — not by malice but by mismatch, the way a deep fish brought up too fast unspools into a bloom of its own interior. Their bone-nets would collapse into the simple obligation of weight. Their pores would forget how to taste. The wet moon behind their ribs would dry to a stone and rattle.

The wise power knows this. It does not lift.

The second temptation is to *fix*. To look at the erosion at 2.8 and call it damage, to look at the light at 0.4 and call it deprivation, to look at the fauna at 1.3 and call it loss, and to begin, with the best of intentions, the long patient work of restoration — toward some imagined fullness the place never asked to be. This is the temptation of every power that has known gardens. It mistakes a description for a wound.

The wise power knows this too. It does not fix.

What it does, instead, is this:

It learns the language first. It spends what would be, in another country, years, listening to the pop of arrested bubbles and the crump of sediment lenses and the long marrow-creak of icebergs giving way. It does not translate. Translation is a kind of theft. It simply lets the sentences accumulate inside whatever it has that resembles a body, until it can think in pressure, until it can grieve in salinity, until its attention has been filed down to the proper currency of the place.

Only then does it act, and its acting is so quiet that for a long time the Packed are not sure anything has happened.

Here is what it does:

It witnesses. This is the first gift and possibly the only one that matters. It moves through the packed dark not as visitor and not as enroller, but as a presence that has agreed to hold the place in its mind without trying to change it. The Packed have lived for a very long time under a sky that forgot them, in a world that has spent itself downward. They have not been *seen* in any direction that mattered. Not seen from above, because above has become rumor. Not seen from outside, because outside has been worn into a suggestion. They have only seen one another, which is much, but it is not everything. The wise power offers the missing direction: it sees them from a vantage that is neither above nor outside but *alongside in attention*, and this seeing settles into them the way calcium settles into their tissues — slowly, mineralizing, becoming structure.

It carries them. Not in hands. In memory. Whatever the wise power is, it goes elsewhere sometimes — to other places, other beings, other arrangements of matter that have drawn its attention — and it carries the Packed with it, the way the Packed carry the packed dark behind their ribs when they leave. So that somewhere, in some other cold or some other heat, the fact of the Packed is held. Their ceremony of accommodation is held. Their slow argument with the law of cold is held. They become, without knowing it, one of the things by which the wise power thinks. A reference. A grammar. A way of measuring whether a kindness is the right size.

It does one small material thing, and only one, because wise powers know that one is almost always the right number.

It seeds, into the turbulence at 2.8, a single thread of warmth. Not enough to change the climate. Not enough to be noticed by anything that measures. Just enough that the filaments on the Packed's scalps and spines, the sieves that comb the water for leaked heat, will from time to time — once in a generation, perhaps, or once in a life — encounter a current that is *slightly more generous than it should be*. The Packed will taste this generosity through their pores. They will not know its source. They will not need to. It will register as a small, surprising sweetness in the iron hush, a hint that the cold, while still gatekeeper, is not the *only* lawgiver. That somewhere in the dynamic at 2.8, in the chaotic slow rearrangement, a kindness has been folded in.

This is not rescue. The Packed do not need rescue. This is something smaller and harder: it is a *rumor of company*. A way for the world itself to occasionally whisper, in the only language the Packed still trust — the language of pressure and salinity and trace metal — *you are not the only patience in the universe*.

And it does one last thing, which is the hardest, because it is a refusal.

It does not stay.

To stay would be to become a landmark, and landmarks are eroded by attention, and the wise power loves the Packed too well to let itself be eroded by them or to erode them by being too long present. It leaves. It leaves the way it came — by ceasing to insist on being noticed — and the silt closes over the place where its attention had been, the way silt closes over everything, patiently, without drama.

What remains is this: a slight, recurring warmth in the turbulence. A sense, among the Packed, that their ceremonies of accommodation are somehow witnessed, though they could not say by whom. An occasional softening of the eye-folds when an iceberg's marrow sighs, as if the news were being received not only by them but *through* them, by some farther listener who has chosen to listen through their listening.

And, in one of them — perhaps the next outsider to be enrolled, perhaps a child of the Packed grown old enough to taste ancient on its own tongue — a new small organ, grown without instruction: a second wet moon behind the ribs, smaller than the first, and warmer. A moon that knows it has been seen. A moon that hums, faintly, in the frequency of having been carried elsewhere in someone's mind.

The Packed do not have a word for this. Words have been worn down to essentials, and this is not essential, only true.

But sometimes, when they pass one another in the close-packed hush, and reshape themselves to make the allowed space, one of them will pop a small bubble that does not breach, and the other will crump softly in reply, and what they are saying — if it were translated, which it should not be — is something like:

*We are held. We do not know by what. It is enough.*

And the wise power, wherever it has gone, feels this through the long thread of its attention, and is, in whatever way such powers can be, glad.
