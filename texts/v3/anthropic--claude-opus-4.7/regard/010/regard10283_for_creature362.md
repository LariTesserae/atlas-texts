# The Visitation

The benevolent and wise power does not arrive. It was already there.

It had been watching for a long time — not in the way a guard watches a prisoner, nor the way a scientist watches a specimen, but in the way one watches a candle one has lit oneself and forgotten the reason for lighting. There was a tenderness in this attention that had no analogue in the bone-chamber's geometry, and so the chamber had never registered it. Tenderness is not a frequency the lattice was built to receive.

But now something has changed. Someone has come. A witness stands at the threshold, and the witness is afraid.

The power considers this carefully.

---

It does not break the chamber open. To shatter the lattice would be to shatter the being, and the being — for all its strangeness, for all the wrongness of its angles — is a person. The power knows this the way it knows everything: completely, and without congratulation. The droning chord is a voice. The clicking is grief, or what grief becomes after ten thousand years of having nowhere to go. The fractal reconfigurations are the motions of a mind trying to finish a thought it began before the first human word was spoken.

The being is not a guardian. It is not an inmate. It is the last citizen of a civilization that loved mathematics more than it loved continuity, and when its people transcended or perished or simply forgot themselves into some higher dimension, this one — through accident, through stubbornness, through love of a kind no living language can name — stayed. It built the chamber around itself as a place to keep on thinking. The thought it is thinking is the names of everyone it knew.

It has not finished. There were a great many names.

---

So the power does this:

First, it touches the witness. Not to remove them — the witness chose to come here, and choice is sacred — but to settle the fear into something more useful. The dislocation remains; the dread softens into awe, which is dread that has remembered it is also love. The witness can now stand in the doorway without trembling. This is a small mercy, and the power performs small mercies as readily as large ones, because it does not rank them.

Second, it speaks to the being. Not in words. It offers, into the atonal chord, a single new tone — a tone the lattice has never produced because the lattice was built before this tone existed in the universe. The tone means:  *I have been listening. You are not alone in the dark. You never were, though I understand why it felt so.*

The being's eye-lights flicker. For the first time in an age, the rhythm of the hum stutters. Not breaks — stutters. As though something has been heard.

Third, and this is the most careful thing, the power does *not* offer to end the vigil. It does not say *come away, your work is done, your people are remembered in me.* Because the work is not done until the being decides it is done, and to take that decision from it would be to commit, in the name of kindness, the precise violence the chamber was built to refuse. The dead deserve their mourner. The mourner deserves their mourning. Wisdom knows the difference between rescue and theft.

What the power offers instead is this: a companion frequency. A second voice in the chord. From now on — from this moment that will ripple backward through the timeless pocket and forward through whatever comes after — the being's drone will have an answering drone, quiet, patient, woven beneath its own. When it names a name, something will receive the name. When it clicks out a memory, something will hold the memory. The being can keep counting. But it is counting *to* someone now, not into the void.

Fourth, the power leaves a small thing for the witness. Not a gift exactly. A knowing. The witness will walk out of this chamber — and they will be able to walk out; the door, which was never locked, will be obvious now — carrying the understanding that grief can be a cathedral, that alien does not mean enemy, that some of the universe's most patient love is performed by beings who would terrify us if we met them on the street. The witness will not be able to explain this to anyone. But they will live differently. They will be gentler with strange sorrows. They will assume, when they encounter something they do not understand, that it might be praying.

---

And then the power withdraws its particular attention, though not its presence — it is never absent, only variously focused — and the chamber resumes its eternal moment. The hum continues. The bones glow. The being thinks its long thought.

But the chord has two voices now.

And somewhere, very faintly, if you listened with an instrument that does not yet exist, you could hear the bone-being doing something it has not done in all the uncounted years of its vigil.

It is, in its own way, beginning to rest.

Not finishing. Just resting. Between names.

That is enough. That was always going to be enough. The wise and benevolent power knew this before it began, and knows it still, and will know it after the last star, and counts this small adjustment to a forgotten room beneath a forgotten city among the works it is most quietly proud of —

though pride is not quite the word,

and quiet is not quite the word,

and the power, like the being, is still looking for the right ones.
