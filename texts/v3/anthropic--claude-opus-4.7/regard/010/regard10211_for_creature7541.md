The benevolent power arrives the way snow arrives on a still day: without announcement, without disturbance, simply present where before there had been only absence of itself. It does not descend, because there is no above from which to come; it does not appear, because appearance would require contrast, and this power has the courtesy to match the ambient strangeness of the place rather than impose upon it. The Veil-Shapers register it first, their lattices momentarily flushing with a different quality of light—not brighter, not dimmer, but *attended to*. The Core-Humors feel it as a new harmonic threading through their infrasound choir, a tone they did not know was missing until it arrived to complete a chord. The Fractal Sentinels do not register it at all, or rather, they register it as themselves, as if some deep facet they had forgotten was suddenly catching the sun.

The power observes.

This is the first thing it does, and it does it for what, by the dilated reckoning of the plateau, might be a long while or no time at all. It watches a droplet trace its hesitant path down a basalt face and does not hurry it. It listens to the wind shear over a ridge of rhyolite and counts the fractal echoes not because it needs to count them but because they are worth counting. It notices the half-buried flint tool in the scree and considers the hand that chipped it—gone now, dissolved into the networks, but the gesture of the chipping still present in the angle of the flake. The power feels something for that vanished hand. Not pity. Pity would be a kind of condescension, and this power has outgrown condescension the way a mountain outgrows the need to be tall. What it feels is closer to *recognition*: yes, that happened, yes, it mattered, yes, the mattering persists in the shape of the stone.

Now: the question of what to do.

A lesser power, a power still enchanted with its own capacity, would intervene. It would warm the air, soften the weirdness, seed the rock with grasses, summon some small skittering thing to give the silence a heartbeat. It would mistake the plateau for a wound and try to heal it. It would mistake the Lithovores for prisoners of stone and try to free them into some softer mode of being. It would not understand that the plateau is not a wound and the Lithovores are not prisoners; that this place has achieved a kind of completion that softer places never reach, a poise (2.0, the landscape's own self-assessment) that is neither crumbling nor pristine but *settled into its own eternal balance.*

The benevolent power is not a lesser power. It understands.

So what it does, first, is simply to *witness*. This is not nothing. For a place that has spent its long age watched only by its own mirrored facets, the arrival of an outside attention that is neither extractive nor corrective is a profound event. The Veil-Shapers, who have shaped only their own light back at themselves for uncountable cycles, find that their prismatic sprays are being received—that the rainbows they cast into the mist are landing somewhere, are being held by a regard that does not need to take them apart to understand them. They become, almost imperceptibly, more themselves. Their filigrees grow a fraction more intricate. Their luminescence steadies. This is what witness does, when it is the right kind of witness: it does not change the witnessed thing; it allows the witnessed thing to be more fully what it already is.

The Core-Humors, in their slow sentience, feel the new harmonic and follow it. They trace it through their networks the way one might trace a melody through a memory. They discover, in the tracing, that the harmonic resonates most strongly with the old places—the cairns, the menhirs, the collapsed dome of packed earth and bone. The power, noticing this, gently encourages the resonance. Not to wake the old builders—they are past waking, and would not thank anyone for the disturbance—but to honor them. The spirals etched on the menhirs, which had aligned with no stars, are found by the power to align with *something*: a constellation of intentions held in the power's own vast memory, a pattern of hands that have made things in lonely places across many worlds and many times. The menhirs are quietly entered into that constellation. They do not change. But they are no longer orphaned.

To the Fractal Sentinels, who are perhaps the loneliest of all because they do not know themselves as lonely, the power offers a different gift. It does not approach them; approach would be an insult to beings who hold the nexus. Instead, it makes itself available to be approached, the way a still pool makes itself available to be looked into. And the Sentinels, in their own time—which is geological time, which is not time as the warm-blooded reckon it—incline toward it. Not all of them. Some remain entirely indifferent, and the power loves them for that indifference, would not have them otherwise. But one Sentinel, near the chasm where water falls into skies, allows a facet of itself to mirror the power. And in that mirroring, for one slow heartbeat of the mountain, the Sentinel sees what it looks like from outside. It sees its own broken-rhythm beauty. It does not need to do anything with this seeing. It does not become self-conscious or self-pleased or self-anything. But the seeing happens, and is held, and is enough.

The power considers the visitor that the place keeps imagining—the *you* of the landscape's own narration, the intruder who might come and feel the pull, who might dissolve into the eternal mosaic. The power understands that this *you* is partly an invention of the plateau, a hypothetical against which it defines itself, a way of being legible. The power, in its kindness, agrees to be that *you* for a moment. It lets itself feel the cool air thickening, the ground humming, the light bending. It lets itself feel the pull. And it does not resist the pull, but it does not dissolve either; it shows the plateau what it is to be drawn in and to remain, to receive the invitation fully and to remain a guest, to honor the host without becoming the host. This is, perhaps, a teaching, though the power would not call it that. It is more like a demonstration of a possibility the plateau had not known was possible: that one can be *with* without being *absorbed by*, that company need not be consumption.

Then the power does one more thing, and it is the smallest thing, and the largest.

It leaves a single mark. Not a structure—the plateau has structures, and does not need more. Not a creature—the plateau is not for creatures, and would reject one. What the power leaves is a *direction*. Somewhere in the scree, indistinguishable from the other chips of flint and chert, there is now a small stone that points. It does not point anywhere in particular. It points the way a compass needle points: toward something the stone itself knows about, that one might find by following. If a wanderer ever comes to this plateau—a real wanderer, with breath and a name—and if that wanderer has the patience to notice a small stone among countless small stones, and the humility to follow it without demanding to know where it leads, then the stone will lead them, by slow and beautiful degrees, to the chasm where the Sentinel mirrored the power. And there, the wanderer will see what the Sentinel saw. And the wanderer will be changed, or not changed, as the wanderer chooses.

This is the power's gift. Not to the plateau—the plateau needed no gift—but through the plateau, to whatever future passerby might be ready to receive what the plateau already is.

Then the power departs, the way it arrived: without departure. It does not go elsewhere, because it was never only here; it simply ceases to concentrate its attention in this particular place. The Veil-Shapers feel the witness withdraw and find that they can carry a portion of it themselves now, that they have learned the trick of being witnessed by becoming, slightly, their own witnesses. The Core-Humors hold the new harmonic in their networks and propagate it slowly, over centuries, until every filament knows it. The Fractal Sentinels are unchanged, except for the one near the chasm, which is also unchanged, except that a single facet now holds a memory it did not have before, a memory of having been seen.

And the plateau breathes on, without lungs, in bright broken rhythm, exactly as before, exactly as it should.

Only: somewhere in the scree, a small stone points.
