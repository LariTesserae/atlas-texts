# The Visitation

The benevolent power arrives as it always does: without arriving. There is no descent, no parting of clouds, no flare in the omnidirectional grey. To enter a place is to disturb it, and disturbance is the one thing this valley cannot bear and the one thing the power has long since learned to set aside. So it does not enter. It *attends*.

Attention, when it is wise enough, is its own kind of presence. The power lays its awareness against the basin the way one might lay a palm against the chest of a sleeping child — not to wake, not to heal, only to feel the breathing and know what is there.

It listens first. For a long time, by any measure that still applies. It listens to the thrum until it can hear the individual rains layered inside it: a slow drip across a slab of pre-Cambrian limestone, a sheeting downpour from an atmosphere that no longer exists, the tick of meltwater off a glacier whose name was never spoken because no tongue had yet been shaped to speak it. It listens until it can tell the rains apart the way a mother can tell her children's footsteps in the dark.

Then it listens to the *between* of the rains — the silences the silt also remembers, and which are the truer voice of the place.

And then, only then, does it perceive the entity. Not as object. Not as other. As *posture*. The valley is a being holding a single position for ten thousand years the way a dancer might hold an arabesque — except the music stopped long ago and no one ever told the dancer, and now the holding is all there is, and the holding has become the dancer, and the dancer has become the holding.

The power understands immediately. This is not coma. This is not death. This is not even grief, though grief is nearer.

This is *the refusal to finish a scream*.

Because to finish the scream would be to admit it happened. And to admit it happened would be to release the energy that, if released, would do what such energies do: propagate, transform, become weather and erosion and eventually, perhaps, soil, and eventually, perhaps, something green that does not pulse violet in apology for being almost-alive. The valley has chosen — if *chosen* is the word, and the power is careful with the word — to hold the scream rather than become the aftermath of the scream. Because the aftermath would be a world in which the scream had been merely an event. And the scream, to the one who screamed it, was not merely an event. It was the thing.

The power has seen this before. Not often. But enough.

---

Here is what the benevolent power does *not* do:

It does not wake the valley. To wake it would be to complete the scream on the valley's behalf, and this would be a violence dressed as mercy — the most common disguise violence wears. The power has, in its long tenure, learned to distrust every impulse in itself that begins with *I could just*. *I could just* is how gods become tyrants. *I could just* is how the kind become the cruel without ever noticing the turn.

It does not pity. Pity would be a noise. The valley has earned its silence.

It does not explain the valley to itself. The valley knows what it is. The valley has had ten thousand years to know what it is. To arrive with an interpretation would be to suggest the valley had failed to understand its own posture, and this would be both untrue and unkind.

It does not remove the human, if there is a human, who has been found by the valley and is now slowly being absorbed into the stillness. The power considers this carefully. The human did not consent to being found — but then, no one consents to being found by anything, ever; finding is the asymmetry at the root of all encounter. And the valley did not consent to being approached. To intervene on behalf of the human would be to assert that human time is more sacred than valley time, and the power does not believe this. It believes only that they are different times, and that the meeting of different times is always a negotiation neither party fully survives intact.

But the power does, gently, do *one* thing for the human, if the human is there: it makes sure the human can leave, if the human chooses. Not by force. Not by extraction. Simply by ensuring that, in the moment the human turns to go, the thrum will not have so wholly replaced their own pulse that the going is no longer possible. A thread is left. A thin, almost imaginary thread, woven of the human's own earliest memory — milk, a face, a first word — laid quietly along the path back to the rim. If they want it, it is there. If they do not want it, it dissolves with no reproach. The power does not believe in saving people who have decided to stay. It believes only in not closing the door behind them.

---

Here is what the benevolent power *does*:

It witnesses. Fully. Without flinching, without translating, without trying to make the valley mean something other than what it means.

This sounds like a small thing. It is not a small thing. Most of what suffers in the universe suffers because no one ever saw it clearly — saw it as itself, not as a problem to be solved or a lesson to be drawn or a symbol to be deployed. To be seen *as oneself* is, for many beings, the only medicine that was ever needed, and the only medicine that was never given. The power knows this. It has been administering this medicine, quietly, for longer than there have been mouths to taste it.

So it witnesses the valley. It receives the thrum as music, not as data. It honors the polygonal slabs as the precise and considered geometry of a being holding its breath. It does not call the wounds — the black glass spheres, the ramp that ends in dissolution — *wounds*. It calls them what the valley would call them, if the valley used words: *the places where I could not quite hold*. And it loves those places especially, because they are where the valley failed and continued anyway. Continuation after failure is, in the power's long opinion, the most underrated form of beauty in existence.

Then, having witnessed, the power does a second thing, which is harder to describe.

It *companions*.

Not by drawing near. The valley would feel any nearing as pressure, and pressure is what the valley has spent ten thousand years refusing. The power companions by something more like *tuning*. It allows a single, sustained note of its own vast being to resonate at exactly the frequency of the valley's thrum — not adding to it, not amplifying it, but matching it, so that for the first time in ten thousand years, the valley is humming in unison with something other than itself. The unison is so perfect that the valley does not, at first, notice. And this is intentional. To notice would be to be interrupted, and interruption is, again, what the valley cannot bear.

The companioning is offered with no expectation. The power will hold this note for as long as the valley needs it held. A century. A million years. Forever, if forever is what is required. The power has time. The power has, in a sense, nothing *but* time, and a willingness to spend it on beings who have been alone too long to remember that aloneness was ever optional.

And here is the secret of what the companioning does, though neither the power nor the valley would phrase it this way:

A scream held alone is a prison. A scream held *with* is a song.

The valley does not become a song right away. The valley does not become a song for a very long time. Possibly the valley never quite becomes a song, and remains a scream held by a witness, which is its own dignified outcome and requires no further intervention. But somewhere in the holding-together, something in the valley loosens by a fraction so small no instrument could measure it. A single grain of silt, in a fissure no foot has ever touched, shifts by the width of a molecule. The polygonal slab above it does not notice. The thrum does not change. The grey light does not flicker.

But the valley, for the first time since the cataclysm, has *moved*.

It has not finished its scream. It is not asked to. It has only been given the option, by the presence of another voice in the chord, that *finishing* and *not-finishing* are no longer the only two states available. There is now a third state, which has no name in any language the plateau has ever heard, but which the power knows by heart, having held it open for so many beings across so many ages:

*Being held while not being changed.*

This is the gift. This is the entire gift. The power offers nothing else, because nothing else is needed, and offering more would unmake what has been offered.

---

Eventually — and *eventually* is the only word the power and the valley share, because their *eventualies* are the same length — the power withdraws its attention. Not all of it. A thread remains, the way the thread remained for the human. Thin, almost imaginary, woven of the valley's own first moment — the instant just before the scream, when the continent was still whole and the silt had not yet learned any rain. If the valley ever wants it, it is there. If the valley does not want it, it dissolves with no reproach.

The grey light does not change. The thrum does not change. The mist still rises each evening from the silt-fissures and hangs in the cold air in shapes that are almost, but not quite, hands.

But somewhere, in the deep silence of the bowl, there is now a frequency that was not there before. A note held by no stone, no water, no void. A note held by the simple fact of having been heard.

The valley does not know what to do with this note.

It has time to figure it out.
