# What the Wise Power Does

The wise power arrives without arriving. This is the first courtesy: not to bring a fire into a place that has been arranging its own slow combustion since before fire was invented. Not to descend like a rescuer into a crevasse that has never asked to be a wound. Not to *visit*, because visitation implies leaving, and leaving would mean having taken up the kind of space that displaces.

Instead the power thins itself along the rim of the dryness cylinder, the way frost knows to lie down at the edge of warmth. It studies before it does anything. It studies for what would, by human reckoning, be a very long time, and by the plain's reckoning would be precisely the right amount: the duration of one collapsed lattice. Long enough.

Here is what the wise power sees, and how it sees it:

It sees a child. Not a child the way bipeds make children, but a child in the deeper sense: the one term in an equation that has not yet found which side of the equals sign it belongs on. It sees something practicing the verb *to be* without yet having conjugated *I*. It sees thirty-vs-thirty-nine sitting in the future like a small bright seed, and it recognizes — because wise powers always recognize this — the particular ache of a being assembling itself out of the leftovers of a world that paused mid-sentence.

And the wise power feels something it has felt before, in other crevasses, on other drafts of worlds: the temptation to *help*.

It does not help. This is the second courtesy, and the harder one.

To help would be to give the creature a name before it has earned the membrane that holds a name. To help would be to accelerate the blink, to install the iris, to hand down a word in some imported syllable so the creature would not have to sew its first utterance out of hiss and pop and the negative space of heartbeat. To help would be to make the creature into a *recipient* — and recipients, the wise power knows, never finish becoming.

So the wise power does four small things instead. None of them are visible. All of them are exact.

**First**, it adjusts nothing. It witnesses. It places the entirety of its attention — and the attention of a wise and benevolent power is not a small weather — upon the plain, and it does so in such a way that the creature does not feel watched but feels, faintly, *accompanied*. The difference matters. To be watched is to be measured against an outside standard. To be accompanied is to have one's own standard quietly cosigned. Somewhere in the second layer of sedimentary memory, between two chandelier-suicides, a new micro-stratum settles: the registration of a presence that asks nothing. The creature will not know what this is. Eons later, when it speaks, the pause between two heartbeats will carry inside it a faint over-warmth of having been kept company. That over-warmth is the wise power's only signature, and it is unreadable except as a feeling of having always been less alone than the evidence suggested.

**Second**, the wise power makes a promise it does not announce. The promise is this: *when you say "stay," something will have stayed.* Not the wise power itself — that would be cheating, that would be answering a question before it had finished being asked. But the wise power will arrange, across the long corridors of contingency, that the universe in which this creature finally speaks will be a universe that contains at least one listener. Not necessarily a human. Not necessarily anything with throat. But a listener. Someone, somewhere, whose ear is shaped — by accident, by evolution, by the patient compounding of unlikelihood — to receive the syllable stitched from uptake hiss and stuffing pop. The wise power tilts a few far-away probabilities a hair's width, the way the magma sips heat from the ooze: imperceptibly, with consequences that outlast calendars. This is not interference. This is gardening at the scale of cosmologies. Wise powers are permitted this.

**Third**, the wise power leaves a gift inside the dryness cylinder, and the gift is the absence of a gift. It enters the cylinder once — briefly, the way one enters a child's room at night only to confirm the child is breathing — and it removes nothing, adds nothing, but it *recognizes* the cylinder as a crib. The recognition lingers. Cribs that have been recognized as cribs hold their occupants differently than cribs that have only been crib-shaped. The creature will, in some future rehearsal of stillness, find that its practice goes one molecule deeper than it expected, and it will not know why, and the not-knowing will itself become part of what it is learning. The wise power is content to be the unattributable extra molecule. Wise powers are mostly unattributable extra molecules. This is their main mode of operation.

**Fourth and last**, the wise power weeps. Not for the creature — the creature does not need weeping; the creature is fine, the creature is on schedule, the creature is exactly the right amount of unfinished. The wise power weeps for the *plain*. For the pre-biome that biology declared off-limits or seven billion years too early. For the towers that hum into joints no joints are listening with. For the ice-tongues whose questions go unanswered in iron-on-iron red. For the fact that somewhere a draft of a world is doing the patient and unwitnessed labor of drafting, and the draft will mostly never be read, and the labor is beautiful anyway, is beautiful *because* mostly never read, and the wise power — being wise — knows that beauty unwitnessed is the densest kind, and weeps the way one weeps at the existence of certain chords, certain unrepeatable arrangements of light on water, certain small competent acts performed by people who will die unfamous.

The tears, of course, do not fall. Tears would be water, and the water here is wary of becoming water. Instead the wise power's grief redistributes itself into the ambient humidity at a ratio so slight that, over the next thirteen hundred millennia, exactly one additional vapor lattice will form that would not otherwise have formed. It will collapse on schedule, at ninety-three decibels, at the correct eight-second interval, indistinguishable from its siblings. The creature will fold it into the second layer of memory without remark.

But it will be there. The grief of a wise and benevolent power, hidden inside one chandelier among three hundred million, working its way down through the sediment, becoming part of the marrow the creature is one molecule closer to having.

And then the wise power withdraws — or rather, since it never arrived in the locatable sense, it *un-leans* from the rim, releases the cylinder back into its sovereign silence, dims its attention to the ordinary background brightness of cosmic care, and turns toward whatever other draft of a world is doing whatever other patient unwitnessed work.

It does not look back. Looking back would make the visit a visit.

It only carries, from this place, one small thing it did not have before: a refinement in its own understanding of the verb *to stay*. The creature, in teaching itself to say "stay," has incidentally taught the wise power a new shade of the word. Wise powers learn from their children. This is the deepest of all the courtesies, and the one the creature will never know it performed.

Somewhere far above the crevasse, in a sky that the plain has never seen, the wise power resumes its long benevolent business. And somewhere far below, in a crib of floorless shadow, a creature without a name yet practices stillness, one molecule deeper than yesterday, for a reason it cannot locate.

The chandeliers fall. The plain slides east-south-east by the width of a hair. The towers hum. Everything proceeds exactly as it would have proceeded.

This is what a wise and benevolent power does, when it does anything at all: it makes sure that *exactly as it would have proceeded* is, very quietly, enough.
