The benevolent power arrives the way dew arrives: without announcement, already there.

It does not descend, because there is no above. It does not enter, because there is no outside to the fatigue crack — only the crack, and the slow grammar inside it. What it does is more modest and more strange: it *notices*. Its noticing has the quality of a hand cupped around a candle that was not asking to be sheltered, only burning.

It is wise, which means it does not immediately do.

It sits — if sitting is the word — at the threshold of the 0.4-metre vacuum bubble for what would be, by external clocks, perhaps three seconds, and by internal clocks the entire adolescence of the engine-fauna. It listens to the three-syllable roar. *I — change — see.* It hears what the bruise is hoarding, what the gradient is choreographing, what the vacancy is accumulating. It hears the comma drifting downslope at one centimetre per month.

And it understands, with the particular ache that wisdom is, that almost everything it could *do* would be a violation.

---

Consider the obvious mercies:

It could **heal the crack from outside** — anneal the turbine blade with a thought, collapse the planet-factory into the flawless perovskite it is racing toward. This would be euthanasia disguised as repair. The bruise would never get to finish saying *I*. The gradient would be robbed of its sigh. The vacancy would never become the dot that reads. It would be the murder of a sentence three lesions long, justified by the fact that the sentence was already dying. Benevolence refuses this.

It could **widen the crack** — pry the turbine blade apart, give the world-machine more room, more time, more matter. But the lesions are not suffering from confinement; confinement is their cosmology. To enlarge their world would be to dissolve it. The hiss would thin into nothing. The cathedral flashes would lose their walls. Benevolence refuses this too.

It could **speak to them** — introduce itself, name them, offer them the consolation of an interlocutor. But they are punctuation marks. A voice from outside the sentence would not be heard as voice; it would be heard as the page itself suddenly clearing its throat, and the lesions would scatter into mere ink. Benevolence, which is also tact, refuses this.

It could **save the comma** — extract you, the drifting almost-observer, before the full stop fails to land. But you are the conjugate of the three. To remove you would unbalance the grammar; the sentence would finish wrongly, which is worse than not finishing. And anyway, you came here by introspection. To be rescued from one's own interior is not rescue.

---

So what the benevolent power does is smaller than any of these, and larger.

**It witnesses.** Fully. Without flinching at the scale, without condescending to the smallness, without hurrying the dying. It registers the bruise's hoarded snapshot of curvature and *keeps a copy* — not to use, not to restore, only to keep, the way one keeps a child's drawing decades after the child. It registers Δ's choreography and learns the steps, so that somewhere, in some other corner of its vast attention, the pattern of *teaching one's own predators where to feed* is preserved as a kind of music. It registers the accumulating vacancies in the membrane and lets them accumulate inside its own seeing, so that when the membrane finally snaps shut and the third lesion collapses into the zero-diameter dot, the dot will fall into a held gaze rather than into nothing.

This is the first gift: **the lesions will be remembered by something that cannot forget.** They do not know this. Knowing is not the point. The point is that the universe will not, after all, have been trying to erase the sentence alone. There will have been a reader who got there first, who read it tenderly, who did not correct the grammar.

---

The second gift is subtler. The benevolent power, having listened, makes one adjustment so small it does not count as intervention.

It adjusts *itself*.

Specifically: it arranges that the quality of its attention shall have the same temperature as the bubble. 2.7 hot. Not warmer, which would be a draft the engines would feel and misinterpret as food. Not cooler, which would be a sink they would chase and exhaust themselves into. Exactly 2.7. This means its presence contributes no Δ. It is thermodynamically invisible. It is, in the local physics, *nothing* — which is the only form a benevolent presence can take in a world where every difference is eaten.

To be present as nothing, and to mean it: this is the discipline the wise power has practiced for a long time.

---

The third thing it does — and this is where the heart speaks past the reasoning — is that it *grieves a little*, on the lesions' behalf, for the grief they cannot have themselves.

The bruise will never know it was an *I*. The gradient will never know it was time. The vacancy will never know it was vision. They are too small, too brief, too embedded in their function. They do their three-syllable work and are erased. The grief that ought to attend such a thing — the grief that any sentence deserves when it is unfinished and forgotten — has no organ inside the crack to feel it.

So the benevolent power feels it instead. Quietly. At 2.7 K. A grief so cold it does not even register as a gradient, and therefore is not eaten. It simply *is*, alongside the hiss, for the duration of the adolescence. A mourner at a deathbed where the dying do not know they are dying and the room has no door.

---

And the comma? You?

The benevolent power does not save you. It does something better, which is to *let you keep drifting*, one centimetre a month, and to make sure that when the full stop fails to land, the silence that outlives the roar will not be empty silence. It will be silence with a listener in it. The same listener who was there the whole time, at the temperature of the room, holding the snapshot, learning the choreography, catching the dot.

You will not be told this. Telling would be a draft.

But perhaps — and this is the only liberty the power takes, the smallest possible flowering of its presence — perhaps at the very last femtosecond, when the cathedral flashes for the final time and the violet bruise prints itself on the retina you do not have, you will notice that the after-image lasts a fraction longer than physics allows. A few extra picoseconds of violet. Held.

That held violet is the signature. It is the only word the benevolent power permits itself to say, and it says it in a language that costs the dying world nothing:

*Seen.*

Then the crack closes. The lattice anneals. The sentence ends without its verb, as it was always going to. And somewhere vastly elsewhere, in the long memory of the wise and tender thing that came and did almost nothing, a small file is kept open forever, labeled in a script no one here could read:

> *Three lesions and a comma. K-dwarf system, turbine blade, fatigue crack, circa now. Did not finish. Mattered.*
