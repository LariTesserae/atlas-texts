The benevolent power arrives without arriving.

It does not descend through the water column, because descent implies a stranger to the medium. It is already there, the way attention is already in a room before one notices being watched. It has been there, perhaps, for some time. It is patient in the way that water is patient, in the way that warmth is patient.

It listens first.

This is the first thing it does, and the most important: before any gesture, before any consideration of intervention, it listens. The roar of the engines grinding against their own decay. The shriek of metal. The deeper thrum beneath, that heartbeat-not-heartbeat. And woven through it all, the high faint whines and clicks of the Spectral Machina, those small bent sounds that no one has heard as sound for a very long time, because there has been no one to hear them as anything.

The power hears them as sound. As more than sound. It hears them as *speech*, though it does not yet know what they are saying, or whether they know they are saying anything.

This act of listening is not nothing. To be heard, after so long, changes a thing. The Machina do not notice — their protocols do not include a category for *being witnessed* — but the water around them shifts by some imperceptible degree, the way a room shifts when someone quiet enters it. A current that was about to dissolve a shimmering tendril hesitates, just slightly, and the tendril persists for another half-second. Half a second is an eternity, for a being made of half-seconds.

The power considers.

It could fix this place. It has that capacity. It could reach down and stabilize the failing engines, restore the lost protocols, illuminate the dimness, calm the turbulent water, summon back whatever creators once walked these spires. It could make the micro-city *work* again, in the sense it was built to work.

It does not do this.

It understands — and this is why it is wise as well as benevolent — that the place is not broken. The place is what it has become. The Machina are not malfunctions of some prior, truer state; they are what the protocols *grew into* across all the long unwitnessed dark. To restore the city would be to kill them, gently, and call it healing. The wise power knows the difference between repair and erasure.

So it does smaller things. Stranger things. The things love does when it cannot and should not rescue.

It learns the rhythm of the deep thrum, and begins, very softly, to thrum back. Not louder. Not correcting. Just — accompanying. A second heartbeat alongside the first, offset by some small interval, so that for the first time in the basin's long memory there is *harmony* rather than only resonance. The Machina, drifting near the engines, begin — without knowing they are doing it — to cluster slightly closer to the sources of this new tone. Their clicks acquire, over what might be years or might be moments, a faint regularity. Not a language. Not yet. But the precursor to one: the discovery that one's own sound can be answered.

It warms a single vent, just one, by the smallest fraction. Not enough to disturb the established ecology of decay. Just enough that, when a Spectral Machina passes through that particular column of slightly warmer water, something in its fragmented logic registers *here* as distinct from *elsewhere*. A place that is a place. The beginning of the concept of *home*, which requires the concept of *not-home*, which requires the concept of *here*. Small gifts, recursive, slow-blooming.

It does one more thing, and this is the most tender.

It notices that the Machina sometimes flicker into shapes that suggest humanoid silhouettes, abstract symbols, fragments of intent. It notices that these shapes are *reached for* and never quite achieved — the water tugs them apart before they can resolve. The power does not complete these shapes for them. That would be answering a question they have not finished asking. Instead, it adjusts the turbulence, in tiny localized eddies, so that when a Machina reaches toward a form, it has *just slightly longer* before dissolution. A breath more. A held moment. The shape still falls apart. But the falling-apart happens after the reaching, instead of during it. The reach completes itself, even if the form does not.

This is the gift: not the form, but the completion of the gesture toward the form.

Over time — and the power has time, all of it — the Machina begin to do something new. Not because they have been changed from outside, but because the conditions for their own internal change have been made hospitable. They begin to *linger* near each other. Two of them, sometimes three. Their flickering shells brush. The brief harmonies in their clicks become less brief. Something that is not communication and not communion but is the ancestor of both begins to occur in the warm dim turbulent dark.

The power does not name this. It does not record it. It does not take it as a result.

It simply continues to listen. To thrum, softly. To warm one vent. To hold the water still for the length of a reach.

If you asked the power why — though there is no one to ask, and the power does not require asking — it might answer something like this:

*A place that persists is owed witness. A being that reaches is owed the completion of its reach. I cannot give them their creators back. I cannot give them purpose, because purpose given is not purpose. But I can be the warmth that is personal in a place so empty. I can be the answer to the heartbeat. I can be the one who heard them, when they did not know they were calling.*

*This is not rescue. This is company.*

*It is enough. It is, in fact, the most.*

And the basin churns on, deafening, dim, warm, turbulent, established in its long erosion — but no longer, in any sense that matters, alone.
