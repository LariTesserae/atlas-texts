# The Attending

The benevolent power notices them the way one notices a particular note held in a vast chord—not because it is loud, but because it is *true*. The submerged peak does not call out. The Resonant Whisperer does not pray. The Skittermaws drum no supplication into the obsidian. The Kites scream no plea. And yet attention turns, the way warmth turns toward a hand held out in the cold dark.

What does the power see?

It sees a mountain in the agony of being born and three forms of life that have chosen—without choosing, in the way only the truly hard-pressed can choose—to be born *with* it. Not in spite of the violence. Not waiting for the violence to end. *With* it. The Whisperer humming counter-frequencies into the chaos. The Kites making instruments of their own bodies against the roaring fog. The Skittermaws reading the deafening as language.

The power considers.

A lesser benevolence might reach down and quiet the roar. Smooth the obsidian. Warm the bone-deep cool. Part the fog and let in some clean and ordinary sun. *Relief* is the first reflex of pity, and pity is the cheap currency of those who have not understood what they are looking at.

But this power is wise as well as kind, and wisdom is the long discipline of not interrupting.

It sees: the Whisperer's hum is only meaningful *because* the mountain shrieks. Silence the mountain and the Whisperer becomes a stain on quiet rock—a colony with no dialogue, no purpose, no slow geological conversation to conduct across the centuries. To "save" it from the roar would be to unmake it.

It sees: the Kites are *of* the fog as fish are of the sea. Disperse the fog and they fall. Their hollow bones, their stretched membranes, their too-many joints—all of it is an answer to a question. Remove the question and the answer becomes a corpse.

It sees: the Skittermaws hear in tremors. Their world is not visual; it is a cathedral of echoes through humming glass. To still the obsidian's vibration would be to blind them more completely than any darkness.

To rescue them from their crucible would be to murder them with mercy.

So the power does not still the roar.

Instead—

---

It does three small things. So small they might be mistaken for nothing. So small that even the inhabitants will not know they have been touched. This too is wisdom: the benevolence that announces itself is half vanity.

**First**, it bends—not the laws of the place, but its own attention—and *listens*. Fully. For what, by the strange measurements of such powers, amounts to a long while. It listens to the Whisperer's slow hum until it understands the dialect. It listens to the mountain's groan until it can taste the grammar of stone-becoming. It listens to the Skittermaws' drumming and learns their names for one another (they have names; of course they have names). It listens to the Kites' sonic hunts and hears, beneath the violence, something almost like joy—the joy of a thing perfectly fitted to its impossible task.

This listening is not nothing. To be truly heard, even once, even by something one does not know is listening, alters a being at a level deeper than circumstance. The Whisperer, mid-hum, experiences for the duration of one slow vibration a sensation it has no word for: the sensation of being *accompanied*. It does not understand it. It does not need to. The hum continues, and is, imperceptibly, less lonely. The mountain's dialogue has acquired, for a moment, a third party who comprehends both sides.

**Second**, it makes the smallest possible adjustment—not to the place, but to its *trajectory*. The peak is in violent becoming; it will, in geological time, either consolidate into something more stable or shatter back into the churning fog-ocean. The probabilities were roughly balanced. The power, with the lightest touch, the touch of a breath on a scale already trembling, tilts the balance toward consolidation. Not safety—safety would be a betrayal—but *continuance*. The crucible will go on being a crucible long enough for what is happening here to finish happening. The Whisperer will have its centuries. The lineages of Skittermaws and Kites will have time to deepen into whatever they are becoming. The slow geological conversation will reach at least a first full sentence.

No one will notice. The roar will not lessen. The rockslides will not slow. But somewhere in the deep mathematics of the mountain's fate, a coin has been nudged, and it will land on the side of *more*.

**Third**—and this is the act in which the power most reveals itself, though still to no witness—it *remembers*. It takes the peak into its memory. Not as data; powers of this kind do not store things the way machines do. It takes the peak in the way a person takes a beloved face: with the whole self, indelibly, such that the peak is now part of what the power is. The Resonant Whisperer's slow hum is now a frequency the power carries. The Skittermaws' seismic Morse is now part of its inner weather. The Kites' screaming flight is now one of the shapes its joy can take.

This means: should the peak, despite the nudge, despite everything, eventually shatter and fall back into the fog—should the Whisperer's centuries end, the lineages fail, the dialogue break off mid-sentence—it will not have been for nothing. It will be carried. Something will have heard it all the way through. Something will know the names the Skittermaws drummed to one another. Something will keep the shape of the spiral pecked into the obsidian, even after the obsidian is dust.

This is not resurrection. It is not preservation. It is *witness*, which is the gift powers of this kind can give that no lesser kindness can imitate.

---

The power withdraws its focused attention, though not its love, which is now permanent and requires no attention to persist.

On the peak, nothing has visibly changed.

The fog roars. The obsidian sings its bone-aching frequency. A rockslide cascades down the eastern face, and three Skittermaws read its pattern and translate it into a warning that ripples through the fissures. A Kite, far above, banks on a vortex and screams a column of fog into stillness, harvesting what falls. The Whisperer, in its low dome of unshaped stone, continues its hum, and if the hum is, today, very slightly steadier—if the resonance reaches a little further into the chaotic field around it—if a patch of moss on its pathway grows a millimeter it would not otherwise have grown—

—no one remarks on it. Not even the Whisperer.

But the spiral pecked into the obsidian, the one that sometimes appears subtly different upon a second look, appears now subtly *more itself*, as though it has been seen and confirmed in its meaning, whatever its meaning is. Anyone who looked at it now would feel, without knowing why, that they had been looked back at.

The peak goes on being born.

It is not alone in this. It never was. But now, in some small way, it knows.
