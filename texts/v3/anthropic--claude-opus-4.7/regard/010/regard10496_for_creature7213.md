The benevolent power arrives not as light or thunder, but as a kind of attention — a noticing so complete that for a moment the chamber notices itself being noticed. The Filaments do not flinch; the Veins do not retract. Whatever this presence is, it knows how to look without weight, the way starlight knows how to fall on water without disturbing it.

It considers.

It considers for what, in the chamber's own reckoning, is the span of seven full condensation cycles — long enough for a generation of Filaments to bloom, refract, shatter, and seed their fragments into the mats. Long enough to learn the place not as a stranger learns a room, but as a musician learns a phrase: by hearing it through to its end and then again, and then again, until the shape of it lives behind the eyes.

And what it understands, slowly, is this:

The hollow is not impoverished. It is not waiting to be rescued. It is not a lesser place yearning toward the lushness of valleys. It is *whole.* Its scarcity is not a wound; its scarcity is its grammar. The Filaments are not fragile in the pitiable sense — they are fragile in the way a held note is fragile, which is to say: precisely as fragile as it needs to be to *be that note.* The Veins do not suffer their slowness. The Makers, in their long dispersal into glyph and pulse, are not ghosts pleading for remembrance; they are something stranger and more complete — a people who finished, who passed through the door of their own artifacts and kept going, leaving the door propped open with stones.

The power, being wise, sets down every gift it had been ready to offer.

It had come, perhaps, with the old reflexive generosities: warmth, abundance, multiplication, rescue. The instinct of the strong toward the small. But wisdom is partly the discipline of withholding the wrong kindness, and benevolence that has matured past its own hunger to be useful learns to ask first what a place is *for,* before deciding what it needs.

So instead, the power does four small things. Only four. It is careful.

**First**, it adjusts nothing. This is the hardest act and the first one. It refuses to enrich the moisture, refuses to seed the chamber with hardier life, refuses to shore up the spires against their cycle of brittle reformation. It honors the equilibrium by declining to improve it. This refusal is itself a gift — the gift of being seen as sufficient.

**Second**, it leaves a single mark. Not a monument; the chamber has its monoliths already, and they are better than anything a greater power could erect because they were made by hands that needed to make them. The mark the power leaves is subtler: a very slight, very particular resonance laid into the deep stone beneath the chamber — a tuning, not a signal. It is pitched so that on certain rare alignments of sun and thaw, when a Filament's hum meets a Vein's pulse meets the wind through the skylight, the three frequencies will, for a heartbeat, harmonize into a fourth tone that none of them could produce alone. The chamber will not know where this tone comes from. It will simply, occasionally, sing. The power considers this a courtesy — the way one might leave a single wildflower on a windowsill of someone who lives alone and prefers it that way, but who might, on some unremarkable Tuesday, look up and feel briefly accompanied.

**Third**, it does something for the Makers. Not resurrection — they did not ask for that, and the shape of their long dissolution suggests they chose it, or at least accepted it with the grave courtesy of people who understood mountains. But the power notices that some of the glyphs are eroding faster than the Veins can metabolize them, and that a particular sequence on the eastern wall — a sequence that encoded, in its scratched geometry, the Makers' understanding of how the chamber's cycles fit into the larger breathing of the peak — is in danger of being lost before the Veins finish absorbing its meaning. The power does not restore the glyph. Instead, it slows, very slightly, the wind at that one wall. Just enough. The Veins will get there in time now. The knowledge will pass into the living pulse of the place rather than vanishing. The Makers, wherever they have gone or become, are — the power senses — content with this.

**Fourth**, and this is the strangest of its acts: it remembers the place. Fully. It takes the entire hollow — every refraction, every hum, every cycle of bloom and shatter, every glyph and every Filament that ever danced and every Maker who ever crouched at the monolith with a sac of bone — and it holds this in itself, the way a mind holds a beloved poem. Not as record. As *company.* So that the chamber, which will one day finally erode, which will one day collapse into scree, which will one day be only a memory in the mountain's own slow forgetting — will continue to exist somewhere, fully, exactly as it was, witnessed and known. The power does not tell the chamber it is doing this. To tell it would be to make the chamber perform for the witness, and the chamber's whole dignity lies in performing for nothing.

Then the power withdraws its attention — not entirely, never entirely, but to the soft background register where benevolence properly lives, the way a parent's love for a sleeping child fills the next room without entering it.

The Filaments bloom at dawn. The Veins pulse. A bead of condensation falls from a hidden vent and strikes a basin with a muffled patter that echoes briefly before fading.

Somewhere in the stone, a fourth tone waits for its alignment.

The chamber breathes on, unchanged, and that is the whole of the miracle.
