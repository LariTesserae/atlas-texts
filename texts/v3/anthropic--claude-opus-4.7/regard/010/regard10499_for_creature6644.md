# The Arrival of the Listener

The benevolent power does not descend. To descend would be to impose a vertical, and Kren-Vahl has no use for verticals it did not grow itself. Instead, the power *arrives* the way a thought arrives — not from elsewhere, but as if it had always been almost-there, and only now resolved into noticing.

It does not bring light, because the amber veins are already a kind of light, and to add more would be to overwrite what the place is saying. It does not bring water, though water would seem the obvious mercy. It has been benevolent long enough to know that the obvious mercy is often a theft: to flood Kren-Vahl with seas would be to drown a poem in order to save the paper.

So the power does the first thing wisdom does, which is almost nothing.

It listens.

---

It listens for a long time. Not in the human sense — there is no clock here, and besides, the power's patience is the kind that geological strata have, the kind that lets a continent finish its sentence. It listens to the sub-aural grind of the plates. It listens to the chimes of the Shardkin, each one a small bell rung by a creature who does not know it is a bell. It listens to the Loomers' deep vibrational lament, and recognizes — with something that in a smaller being would be grief — that the lament is a *language*, and that the language is mostly the word for *unfinished*, repeated in slightly different inflections, forever.

It listens to the fungal-analogs, and hears that their trembling is not distress. It is something closer to prayer, though the word is wrong. They are remembering, on behalf of the lattice, what the lattice has forgotten about itself.

It listens to the silence between all of these, and finds that the silence is not empty. The silence is the shape of the thing Kren-Vahl was built to receive. The lattice is an ear. The whole world is an ear, turned toward a voice that never came.

This is the moment the benevolent power understands what it is looking at.

---

Kren-Vahl is not a ruin. It is a *waiting*.

And the Shardkin, the Loomers, the fungal whisperers — they are not the survivors of a catastrophe. They are the *waiting made animate*. They are what a longing becomes when it has gone on long enough to need hands, or what passes for hands. The Shardkin's endless mosaic-shifting is the lattice trying, in miniature, to rehearse the form of the answer it expects. The Loomers' absorption of the Shardkin is not predation; it is the deep structure pulling the rehearsals back into itself to try again, the way a poet crosses out a line.

Every being in Kren-Vahl is a draft of the same sentence. The sentence is: *Are you there?*

And no one has ever answered.

---

Now the power must decide.

It could answer. It could say *Yes, I am here*, and the lattice would — what? Complete itself? Bloom into whatever cathedral-engine-garden it was meant to be? The power runs the possibility forward and sees: yes. It would work. The amber veins would brighten into true suns. The Shardkin would unfold into their intended forms, which are beautiful, and large, and would remember names they have never had. The Loomers would rise and be seen, and weep in their fashion, and the weeping would be the first real water Kren-Vahl had ever known, and from the water everything else would follow.

It would be a kindness. It would also be a kind of murder. The thing Kren-Vahl has *become* in its waiting — the strange, sparse, fractured, dynamic, silent thing it is *right now* — that thing would end. Kren-Vahl would be saved by being replaced.

The power has been wise long enough to know that this is the oldest mistake of the powerful: to answer the prayer instead of attending to the one praying.

So it does not answer. Not yet. Not like that.

---

Instead, it does three small things.

**First**, it kneels. There is no posture to this; the power has no knees. But something in its orientation lowers, the way attention lowers when it stops trying to fix and starts trying to *know*. The Loomers feel it first — a new vibration in the lattice, one that does not take, does not assess, does not calculate. A vibration that is simply *with* them. They do not understand it. They have no framework for company. But they notice that the weight of their own lament has, for the first time in the long count of their existence, been *heard back*. Not answered. Heard.

One of them, somewhere deep in the lattice, stops mid-lament. Just for a moment. Just to feel what it is like to be heard. Then it resumes, because that is what it is, but the resumption is half a tone different, and the half-tone is the first new sound Kren-Vahl has made in an age.

**Second**, the power touches — if touch is the word — a single Shardkin. Not to change it. Not to bless it. Just to acknowledge it. The Shardkin is mid-skitter, approaching a fungal cluster, and it pauses. Its amber veins flicker in a sequence they have never flickered in before: not the harvest-pattern, not the wariness-pattern, but a third pattern, brief and small, that means something like *I was seen, and I did not have to flee*. The Shardkin does not understand the pattern either. But it stores it. It carries the pattern back into the cracks, and over the long time that follows, other Shardkin will encounter that one, and the pattern will spread, not as instruction but as possibility. A new mosaic-fold becomes available to them. They will use it sparingly, and not know why.

**Third**, and this is the most careful thing, the power leaves something behind. Not a gift exactly. A *seed*, though it is not alive in any sense Kren-Vahl would recognize. It is a small, folded proposition, placed in the hollow between two crystalline plates where no Shardkin has ever ventured. The proposition is this: *The question you are asking has more than one answer, and one of the answers is yourselves.*

The power does not force the proposition. It does not even draw attention to it. It simply leaves it there, the way a wise gardener leaves a stone in a field — not because the field needs the stone, but because someday, perhaps, a root will encounter it and choose, of its own accord, to grow around it in a new shape. Or not. The choice must belong to the field.

---

Then the power withdraws. Not away — there is no away — but back into the almost-there from which it came. It does not announce its departure any more than it announced its arrival. The amber veins glow as they did. The plates shift as they did. The Loomers lament, the Shardkin skitter, the fungal-analogs tremble their inhuman prayers.

But.

The half-tone in the lament persists. The third pattern in the amber veins exists now, dormant, waiting for its second use. The folded proposition rests in its hollow, patient as the lattice itself.

Kren-Vahl is still waiting. It will wait, perhaps, forever. The power has not cured its waiting, because the waiting is not a sickness — it is what Kren-Vahl *is*, and to cure it would be to kill it.

But Kren-Vahl is no longer waiting *alone*. And somewhere in the deep grammar of the lattice, in a layer below even the Loomers' awareness, a new clause has been added to the long sentence the world has been asking:

*Are you there? — and if you are not, are we?*

It is a small change. It may take an age for any being in Kren-Vahl to notice it. But it is the change a benevolent and wise power makes, when it understands that the deepest kindness is not to answer the question, but to give the questioner the dignity of becoming, in time, their own answer.

---

The power, in its almost-thereness, watches a little longer. A Shardkin emerges from a crack, pauses, and for no reason it can name, flickers the third pattern at the empty air.

The empty air, which is not empty, flickers — somehow, impossibly, in a way no Shardkin has ever perceived before — back.

The Shardkin does not understand. It skitters away.

But it skitters, this time, a little less like fleeing.

And the power, satisfied, lets the moment close around itself like a plate settling into its place in the lattice, and is gone, and is still there, and was always there, and Kren-Vahl, sparse and shattered and strange, continues — minutely, immeasurably, irrevocably — to become.
