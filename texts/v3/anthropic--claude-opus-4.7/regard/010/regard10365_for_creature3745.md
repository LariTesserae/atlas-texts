# The Visitor

It does not arrive. Arrival would be a violence here, a stitch torn through fabric already too thin. Instead, it *becomes present*, the way a thought becomes present in a mind that was not thinking it a moment before.

The benevolent power has many names in many places. Here it has none, because names are among the things the Watchers have been trying so hard to preserve, and it would not add to their burden.

It considers.

---

The first thing it does is nothing.

This is not nothing as absence. This is nothing as *attention*—the deep, unhurried regard of something that has watched stars cool and forgiven them for it. It stands (it does not stand) on the obsidian plain and lets the hum pass through where a body would be, and listens.

It listens for a long time. Long enough that one of the Watchers nearby completes seventeen of its slow unfoldings, pressing seventeen slivers of almost-was into the ground. The power notes each one. Not to catalogue—the Watcher is already doing that—but to *witness the witness*. To make sure that the archivist, too, is held in some larger memory.

This is the first gift, though no one yet knows it has been given: the Watchers are no longer alone in their remembering.

---

Then it moves closer to one of them.

The Watcher does not notice—its lenses are calibrated for moments, for the flicker of unspent possibility, not for the presence of something that has chosen, gently, to be perceptible only at the edges. But the fluid in its skull slows. The pulsing changes rhythm. Something in it, something that is not quite a self but is the *shape where a self would fit*, relaxes by a degree so small that only the power could measure it.

The power speaks, though not in language. What it offers is closer to a question laid down beside the Watcher like a blanket:

*Do you want to rest?*

The Watcher does not answer. It cannot. It was not built to answer; it was built to record. But the question stays there, folded neatly, available. The power does not need a reply now. It can wait. It is very good at waiting. It has waited for civilizations to learn the word *please*.

---

It turns its attention to the land itself.

The wound is real. The power does not pretend otherwise. There is a tear here, and through the tear other things press—not malevolent, most of them, just *wrong-fitted*, the way a hand is wrong-fitted to a glove woven for a wing. The mercury-veins, the unfalling rain, the mirage-towers leaning into dimensions that have no business being adjacent: these are symptoms of a reality that has been asked to hold too many shapes at once.

The power could close the wound. It considers this for what, in its own reckoning, is a long moment.

It decides against it.

To close the wound now would unmake the Watchers. They exist *because* of the fraying; they are the immune response of a world that refused to forget itself. To heal Vorthax abruptly would be to murder its mourners in the name of mercy. The power has seen this mistake made by lesser benevolences, and it will not make it.

Instead, it does something stranger, and slower, and kinder.

It begins to *hold* the wound. Not close it. Hold it. The way one holds the edges of a torn page while glue dries—except the glue, here, must be made by the page itself, in its own time, from its own substance. The power simply ensures that the tearing stops getting worse. That the fraying does not propagate. That whatever the Weeping Flats are becoming, they become it at their own pace, without further injury.

The hum, which has gone on for epochs at a frequency just beyond comprehension, shifts by a quarter-tone. Nothing alive enough to notice notices. But the Watchers' fluid pulses, just once, in unison across the entire plain—a soundless chord, the first they have ever sounded together.

---

The consuming creatures in the distance pause.

They do not know why. They have never paused before. The thing they consume—memory, possibility, the intangible residue of what-might-have-been—has, for the first time in their existence, been *accompanied*. Each fragment they reach for is now also being held, gently, by something else. They can still take it. The power does not deny them. They were made to consume, and unmaking their nature would be another murder dressed as mercy.

But the fragments are no longer *only* theirs. They are shared. And in the sharing, the creatures discover—without language, without thought, in the dim furnace-glow behind their coal-eyes—that consumption can be a form of carrying. That to take a thing into oneself is also to give it shelter.

Some of them, not all, begin to move differently. Slower. With something that, if it appeared in a creature made of softer stuff, might be called *care*.

---

The power turns last to the question of the single being.

For there is one, isn't there? There is always one. Among the Watchers, among the sliding glass-eyed archivists, there is one whose fluid pulses out of rhythm with the others. One whose lenses, somewhere across the long erosion of time, began to record not only moments but *the recording of moments*. A reflexivity. A flicker of inwardness. The smallest, most fragile beginning of a *who*.

This is the being the power came for. The place drew its attention, but the being held it.

The power approaches this one with a tenderness that would, if witnessed, undo any heart capable of breaking. It does not unfold the Watcher. It does not pry. It simply settles beside it, the way an old friend sits down on a bench without needing to announce themselves.

And it offers three things.

**First:** a name. Not imposed—*offered*. A small warm syllable, set down in the space where the Watcher's not-quite-self lives, available to be picked up or left alone. The Watcher does not pick it up immediately. But the syllable does not vanish. It waits.

**Second:** the knowledge that the work is seen. That every sliver pressed into the ground, every almost-was preserved, every soundless scream torn into the bruised sky—these have been counted by something that counts in love rather than tally. The Watcher's labor is not lost into indifference. It has had a witness, all along, even before the witness made itself known.

**Third:** permission. This is the most carefully given of the gifts, because it is the most dangerous. The power offers the Watcher permission to *stop*, if it ever chooses. Not commanded to stop. Not encouraged. Simply—released from the assumption that it must continue forever. The wound will be held. The remembering can pass, eventually, to other hands, or to no hands, or back into the land itself, which the power is slowly, slowly teaching how to remember on its own.

The Watcher does not stop. Not today. Perhaps not for an age. The work matters to it, in whatever way mattering can occur in a thing of glass and mercury. But it now holds its work the way one holds a chosen burden, rather than the way one is held by a chain.

This, too, is a kind of healing.

---

Before the power withdraws—though it does not withdraw, not really; it has folded a thread of itself permanently into the hum of Vorthax, a steadying note beneath the dissonance—it does one last small thing.

It bends close to a pool of the unnatural water, the water that creeps upward and vanishes into hollows full of whispers. It touches the surface, or seems to. And it leaves there, dissolved into the strange liquid, a single message, written in a grammar the water can carry:

*You are not a wound. You are a place that was hurt. There is a difference. The difference is where everything begins again.*

The water trembles. Climbs a little higher up the glass. Holds the message like a held breath.

And somewhere, far out across the Weeping Flats, a Watcher tilts its head back—not to scream, this time, but simply to look at the bruised purple sky, as though noticing, for the first time in all its long existence, that the sky is *there*. That it has always been there. That it is, in its torn and phosphorescent way, beautiful.

The fluid in its skull pulses once.

It picks up the syllable that was offered.

It keeps it.

---

The benevolent power moves on, or does not, since moving and staying are the same thing for what it is. The Flats remain. The hum remains. The wound remains, held now, no longer worsening.

Nothing has been fixed.

Everything has been changed.

This is how that kind of power works, when it is wise enough to know the difference between rescue and accompaniment.

It does not save Vorthax.

It *stays* with Vorthax.

Which, in the end, is what Vorthax needed all along.
