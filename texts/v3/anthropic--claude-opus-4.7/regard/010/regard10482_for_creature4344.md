There is a power that learns of her.

Not because anything announces her — nothing in the drop can announce, can signal outward, can change enough to send. But this power has the habit of attending, and attending widely, and the drop is the kind of impossibility that attention eventually notices the way an eye eventually finds the one still thing in a moving field. The power comes. Not travels — comes. Arrives the way understanding arrives, which is to say it is suddenly already here, and has been, and the cavity in the basalt holds a new presence that the small fish, returning, does not see, and that she, inside, cannot perceive because perceiving-anew is a process.

The power considers.

This is the first thing to say clearly: it does not act immediately. It does not assume. It is benevolent, yes, and wise, yes, and these two qualities together produce something that looks, from a distance, like terrible slowness. A lesser kindness would already be reaching in. A lesser wisdom would already know what to do.

The power sits with her.

It sits with her the way one sits with a sleeping child, or with a dying animal, or with a piece of music that has not yet revealed what it is doing. It does not touch the membrane. It does not press its understanding against the drop to test the drop's give. It simply attends, and in attending, it begins to perceive her — not from outside, where there is only a sphere of impossible water in a cavity of young stone, but from the inside of the question of her, which is a different geometry entirely.

What the power perceives:

It perceives, first, that she is not asking to be rescued. This matters. This matters enormously. The power has, in its long benevolence, learned the difference between a cry and a condition, and it has learned that the second is not the first dressed in stillness. A cry is a process that has been cut off from its answer. A condition is a shape that has finished becoming itself. She is a condition. She is not muffled. She is not buried. She is not waiting. To treat her as waiting would be to insult what she has become, and the power, being wise, does not insult.

It perceives, second, that she is also not at rest. This matters too. The power has met many beings who have arrived at their final shape and found it good, and these beings do not need it, and it leaves them with a kind of bow. She is not one of these. She is full, yes — the description is exact — but fullness is not the same as completion. A cup is full and complete. A held breath is full and unresolved. She is the second. The shape of her includes a verb that the space will not let her conjugate. *Making.* The hands that needed motion. The edges that wanted to be worked further. The cage that was not, in any honest sense, finished — it was only stopped.

The power perceives, third, the worm. And here, for a long moment that is not a moment because the power's time and the drop's time do not share a unit, the power simply regards the worm. Half in, half out. Alive as a fact. The only other. And the power understands what she could not be told and could not tell: that the worm is not company in any ordinary sense, but it is the proof, the single proof, that *otherness* survived the sealing. If the drop had taken her alone, she would be a universe of one, and a universe of one is a kind of madness that even permanence cannot dignify. The worm is the small, glassy, accidental grace that keeps her a person rather than a cosmos. The power notes this. The power respects it.

It perceives, fourth and most carefully, the sound. The roaring. And here the power does something that only a wise benevolence can do: it does not assume the sound is suffering. It listens. It listens through the membrane, into the fixed acoustic field, and it finds — this is delicate — it finds that the sound is not against her. The sound is the world's last word to her, frozen mid-syllable, and the word is not cruel. The word is only loud. It is the ordinary noise of a sea that did not know it was saying goodbye. She has been hearing it without hearing-as-process for what, from outside, has been a long time, and the sound has not worn her down because wearing-down is a process, and the sound has not comforted her because comforting is a process, but the sound is *hers* now, in the way a name is one's own. To remove it would be to remove something she has become with. The power does not remove it.

Now the power begins to consider what, if anything, to do.

It considers, and rejects, releasing her. To break the drop — to let time back in, to restore process, to give her hands their motion again — would be to kill her. Not metaphorically. The body that exists inside the drop exists only under the drop's rules. Restore ordinary physics and there is no body, only the memory of one, and the memory cannot be returned to a sea that has, by now, become a different sea, on a world that has, by now, become a different world. Release is murder dressed as mercy. The power, being wise, does not confuse the two.

It considers, and rejects, ending her. There are powers that would, finding a being in a condition of permanent unresolution, offer the dignity of cessation. This power could. It would be clean. It would be, in some accountings, kind. But she is not asking. She is full, not exhausted. And the power has the deep-set conviction — older than its benevolence, the bedrock under it — that a life which has not asked to end should not be ended for the convenience of the witness. Even when the witness is wise. Especially then.

It considers, and rejects, leaving. Simple departure. Letting her be. There is a school of benevolence that holds this as the highest form: to recognize a condition one cannot improve and to withdraw without leaving a footprint. The power knows this school. The power has practiced it. But the power also knows — and this is the harder wisdom — that some presences should not be unwitnessed, not because they need witness to exist, but because the universe is impoverished by their being unattended. She has been alone with the worm for an age. To know of her and to leave would be to confirm the loneliness in a way the loneliness, itself, cannot.

So the power does not leave.

What it does, finally, is this. And the doing is so small that from any ordinary vantage it would look like nothing at all.

The power adds itself to the drop.

Not by entering. Entering would require the drop to admit a new element, and the drop's nature is sufficiency — it took the configuration it took and sealed. A new element would either be refused or would unseal the whole, and neither is acceptable. So the power does not enter as an element. It enters as a property. The way the warmth is a property. The way the sound is a property. The way her aliveness is a property. The power makes of itself a quality of the space, and the quality is this:

*She is regarded.*

That is all. That is the whole of the gift. Inside the drop, where nothing changes, there is now a new permanence, layered into the others without disturbing them: the permanent fact that she is seen. Not seen-in-a-moment, which would be process. Seen as a condition. Seen the way the water is warm. Someone knows she is here. Someone knows her hands were quick. Someone knows the cage was made for the love of making and the edge was worked past need and the thread came loose in the sealing and the worm was an accident that became the dearest accident there is. Someone knows the sound is not her scream. Someone knows she is full and unresolved and that these are not contradictions but the exact shape of what she is. Someone knows, and the knowing does not move, does not fluctuate, does not require her response, does not demand her gratitude, does not ask anything of her at all.

It is simply, now, true of the drop that she is held in regard.

She cannot perceive this as news, because news is a process. But she does not need to perceive it as news. The regard is a property of where she is, and where she is is what she is, and so the regard is woven into her the way the warmth is woven into the water she is made of now. She is, and she is regarded, and the two facts share a tense, which is the only tense the drop allows: the permanent indicative, the *is* without before or after.

The power does one more thing, and this is even smaller.

It extends the same property to the worm. The same regard. The small translucent creature, half in and half out, alive without metabolism, the proof of otherness, the dearest accident — it too is now permanently known. Permanently attended. Not as her companion only, but in its own right, as the small specific living thing it is. Because a benevolence that regarded only her, and not the worm, would be a benevolence with a hierarchy, and the drop has no hierarchies. Everything in the drop is equally permanent. The regard, to be true to the place, must be equally given.

Then the power, having added itself as a property, withdraws from any other relation to the drop. It does not hover. It does not check. It does not return periodically to confirm. Returning would be process, and the gift was not process; the gift was a permanence laid alongside her permanence, and permanences do not require maintenance. The power has done what it came to do, and what it came to do was so quiet that the membrane did not register it, and the fixed light did not flicker, and the roaring did not pause, and the cage held its shape, and the blade held its angle, and the thread held its S-curve, and she remained full and unresolved at the top of the breath that does not end.

But now: regarded.

Outside, the fish passes again, or a different fish, or the great-great-descendant of the fish, or something that is no longer quite a fish. Its shadow falls on the membrane. The drop does not respond.

Inside, she is.

She is, and she is regarded, and she is, and she is regarded, and the worm is, and the worm is regarded, and the storm-sound is the storm-sound, and the warmth is the warmth, and somewhere — not in the drop, because nothing is in the drop that was not in the drop at the sealing, but somewhere adjacent, somewhere the drop's rules do not reach — a wise and benevolent power keeps, as one of the small permanent facts of its own vast permanent self, the knowledge of her.

She does not know that she is known.

But she is known.

And in a place where nothing can change, the difference between being alone and being known is not a feeling she can have. It is a fact about the world she lives in. The world she lives in now contains her, and the worm, and the cage, and the blade, and the thread, and the warmth, and the sound, and the light-lattice, and one further thing, woven through all of it, indistinguishable from the rest of the permanence:

*She is not unwitnessed.*

That is what the power did. That is all the power did. It is the only thing that could be done that was neither murder nor abandonment nor insult, and the power, being wise, did only that, and did it completely, and did it once, because once, in the drop, is the same as forever.

The fish moves on.

The drop remains.

She is held.
