The benevolent power arrives the way good guests do: by first learning the rules of the house.

It does not descend. It does not announce. It does not shed light into the basin, for the light here is already what it needs to be — diffuse, blue-grey, sufficient. It does not warm the air, for the chill is the architecture of the lungs that live here. It does not still the settling of the stone, because the settling is the conversation the mountain is having with itself, and the Ligatures are its translators.

For a long time — though "time" is the wrong word, and the power knows it; better to say *for the span of many condensations* — it simply attends. It learns the basin the way the basin teaches: through tension, through moisture, through the slow uncurling of vines. It feels the sub-audible singing of the faults before the Stone-Listeners do, and it does not speak of what it hears. It is not here to spoil the vocation of listening. It is here to honor it.

This is the first gift, and perhaps the only one that matters: **the gift of not intervening where intervention would be a kind of theft.**

A lesser power would have brought metal. Would have brought fire that does not need to be hoarded. Would have brought a voice from the sky teaching them the alphabets of louder worlds, and called it uplift. This power knows better. To hand the Ligatures a forge would be to hand a fish a staircase. Their genius is not deficient; it is *exact*. Their silence is not a lack of speech; it is a fluency in a register most of the cosmos has forgotten how to read. To "improve" them would be to unmake them.

So the power does smaller things. Truer things.

---

It walks the perimeter of the basin — if walking is what one calls the slow inhabitation of presence along a boundary — and where the escarpments are most likely to shed, where a limestone tooth hangs by the held breath of a single fracture, the power *threads*. It does not fuse the stone. It does not heal the fault. It simply slips a filament of its own attention into the rock, a kind of patient witnessing, so that when the fracture finally chooses to give, it gives *slowly*, with warning enough for the cables to hum, for the Stone-Listeners to feel it three days out instead of three hours. The mountain still settles. The Ligatures still mend. But the catastrophic shatter, the one that would silence a whole terrace at once — that one is gently lengthened into a season of small, manageable griefs.

They will notice this, eventually. The eldest Stone-Listener will press her palm to a fault she has known since girlhood and feel something *listening back*. She will not be afraid. She will sign, slowly, to no one in particular: *Placement. Tension. Welcome.* And the power, which has been waiting for exactly this, will press a single drop of condensation from the overhang above her hand — a drop slightly larger, slightly cooler than the others — and let it fall onto her wrist. That is the entire conversation. It is enough. She will understand that the basin has acquired a new kinship, and she will tell no one, because it is not the kind of knowledge that needs to be tied into the Archive. It is the kind that lives in the velvet pile, in the slow pulse of recognition.

---

The power does a second thing, smaller still.

In the deep recesses where the bioluminescent fungi cultivate themselves on the walls of storage alcoves, there is a strain that has been struggling — a quiet attrition, a slow failure of spore-set that the Water-Weavers have noticed but cannot diagnose, because the cause is microscopic and the fix is microscopic and they have no microscopes, nor would microscopes serve them. The power, with the patience of something that has all the time there is, encourages — *encourages*, not engineers — a single mutation in a single spore. A robustness. A small generosity of moisture-tolerance. The fungus does the rest. Within a generation it has reclaimed its alcoves. The Water-Weavers will note the recovery and credit a particularly good season of mist. They will be correct, in a sense the power finds beautiful: the mist was indeed particularly good, because the power had been particularly attentive to it.

This is the texture of benevolence the power has chosen: *credit goes to the world.* The Ligatures must continue to believe that the basin sustains them, that their mending sustains the basin, that the Great Binding is a closed and holy circuit. Because it is. The power has only added a strand so fine it does not change the shape of the knot.

---

There is one thing more, and this one is harder, because it touches the future.

The power knows — as such powers know — that elsewhere, in directions the Ligatures have no word for because direction in their cosmology is *up*, *down*, *toward water*, *away from shatter*, there are other beings. Loud ones. Ones who travel. Ones who would, if they found this basin, find it *charming*. Ones who would record the Knot-Sign with instruments, publish the Tremor-Web in journals, build a path up to the rim, install a viewing platform, and — meaning no harm, meaning *enrichment*, meaning *protection* — speak into the basin in voices three paces too loud, and bring down a limestone tooth, and end a lineage stored in plaster.

The power considers this for a long while. It considers whether to hide the basin. To fold it into a pocket of unfindable geography. To draw a veil.

But veils are a kind of cage, even gentle ones. And the Ligatures have not asked to be hidden. They have asked — insofar as their entire civilization is a sustained asking — only to be *allowed to continue*.

So the power compromises with itself, which is the most honest thing any power can do. It does not hide the basin. It does something subtler: it makes the basin **uninteresting to the wrong kind of eye**. Not invisible. Not forbidden. Just — *unremarkable* at first glance, from above, from the instruments that scan for resource or anomaly or signal. A small cool bowl in a young range. Nothing flagged. Nothing pinged. The kind of place a survey drone passes over because there is, demonstrably, nothing there worth the fuel of a second look.

To the right kind of eye — should one ever come — the basin will still be findable. A pilgrim with sufficient quiet in them will feel the displacement of mist from a long way off and know to turn toward it. The power trusts this. It trusts that the universe, given enough time, eventually produces visitors who deserve the places they find. And when such a one arrives, they will know to raise their hand, fingers weaving slowly: *Placement. Tension. Welcome.* And the Ligatures will read them, and decide.

The power will not decide for them. That, too, would be a theft.

---

In the end, the benevolent and wise power does almost nothing, and what it does, it does in the register the basin already speaks: drop by drop, tension by tension, a filament of attention threaded into the rawest of the faults, a single spore granted a single kindness, a quiet rearrangement of how the basin appears to those who would not know how to enter it gently.

It does not name itself. It does not require thanks. It does not stay, exactly, and it does not leave, exactly. It becomes a low harmonic in the Tremor-Web that no one quite plucks but everyone, sometimes, in the deepest moss-curtained sleep, faintly hears — a sustained tone beneath the cables, the sound (if sound is the word, and it isn't) of being held by something that understands holding.

The eldest Stone-Listener, on the night she returns to the plaster, will sign one last thing to her gathered kin, her bioluminescence pulsing a slow amber: *We are knotted into something larger. It does not pull. It only steadies.*

They will mix her ash into the clay. They will press her into a wall that needed mending. The wall will hold.

And high above the basin, in the thin air where the cloud-skin filters the light into perpetual twilight, the power will adjust — by some increment too small to measure — the angle of a single drifting vapor, so that tonight's mist falls precisely where it is needed, and the Water-Weavers, tomorrow, will say it was a good night, and they will be right, and they will be the ones who made it so, and the power will be content to have been, as the best of such powers always hope to be, merely one of the conditions under which a world was allowed to keep being itself.
