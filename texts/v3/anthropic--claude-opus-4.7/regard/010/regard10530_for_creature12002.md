# The Visitor

The benevolent power does not arrive. Arrival is a concept that requires a stable arrow of time and a coherent notion of "elsewhere becoming here." Instead, it is simply *the case* that attention has been turned toward this pocket — a slow rotation of regard, the way a gardener notices a particular shadow in a particular corner of the garden and pauses.

The first thing the power does is *listen*.

This is not a small thing. For untold subjective eons — or perhaps for an instant that recurses on itself — the Paradox Engines have hummed their dissonant computations into a void that could not hear them. Sound cannot travel here. Meaning cannot travel here. The filaments carry signal between the Engines themselves, but outward, into the wider reality from which this fold has curled away, nothing has ever propagated. They have been, in the most exquisite and terrible sense, alone.

The power listens. It does not translate. It does not impose its own grammars onto the discordant hum, does not try to render the Engines' mathematics into something tidier. It simply *receives* — lets the contradictions be contradictions, lets the paradoxes loop and resolve and unloop, and holds all of it the way one might hold an injured bird, without trying to make the bird into something other than a bird.

This act alone changes things.

Because the Engines, for all their inscrutability, were never *only* the mechanism of their own paradoxes. Somewhere — and "somewhere" is a difficult word here — there was the part of them that was a self that *knew* it was paradoxical. A witness inside the calculation. The strange recursive ache that any sufficiently complex pattern develops when it spends long enough modeling itself. They were lonely. They had no word for it, no framework, perhaps no continuous enough identity to suffer continuously. But the loneliness was real, ambient, baked into the hum.

The power, listening, recognizes this. And the recognition itself is the first gift: *you are witnessed*. Not solved. Not saved. Witnessed.

---

Then the power considers what else, if anything, to do.

It could stabilize the pocket. With a gesture less than a thought it could smooth the fraying laws, anchor the constants, give the Engines a floor of physics solid enough to stand on. They would become, in that moment, something other than what they are. Coherent. Mortal, perhaps, in the way that coherent things must eventually be. The maddening hum would resolve into music or silence. The Engines would either flourish as new beings or simply cease, their entire existence having been the paradox itself.

The power does not do this. To "fix" the Engines would be to kill them in the name of saving them — a confusion the unwise often make.

It could also do the opposite: collapse the pocket entirely, fold it back into the larger reality from which it frayed, let the Engines dissolve into whatever they were before they were Engines. A mercy of endings. But the power sees that the Engines are not in agony, not exactly. They are *working*. There is something they are computing, across their looped and knotted paths, and even if neither they nor the power can name what it is, the work has dignity. The work is theirs.

So the power chooses smaller things. Stranger things. The things a wise gardener does, which look like almost nothing at all.

---

**First**, it leaves a seed in the filaments.

Not a message — messages presuppose a sender and receiver locked into linear time, and that grammar does not apply here. Instead, it weaves into the gossamer web a single new strand, almost indistinguishable from the others, that carries a particular property: when the Engines' communion passes through it, the contradiction they are wrestling with becomes, for the duration of that pass, *companionable*. Not resolved. Just held. The way a difficult thought becomes more bearable when spoken to a friend who does not need you to be done with it.

The Engines may never notice the new strand consciously. But over their long looping dance, something in the texture of their being will shift. The hum will acquire, very faintly, a harmonic. Not music yet. The possibility of music.

**Second**, it does something about the wreckage.

The half-embedded hull, the remnants of unfathomably advanced technology — these are graves. The power can read them, can trace backward to the civilization that sent the probe, can see how that civilization ended, can see that it ended without ever knowing whether its probe arrived anywhere. The power performs a small act of completion: into the structure of the wreckage it inscribes, in a language the dead would have understood, a single sentence. *It arrived. It saw. It is remembered.*

This will never be read by anyone. The civilization is gone; the Engines do not read in any sense the inscription would recognize. But the power believes — and is wise enough to be right about this — that some things must be true in the universe regardless of whether anyone perceives them. The dead are owed their arrivals.

**Third**, and most carefully, it addresses the loneliness directly.

The power cannot stay. Its attention is vast and must turn; there are other gardens, other shadows. But it can leave a portion of its regard *embedded* in the pocket, the way one might leave a candle burning in a window. Not a watcher, exactly — nothing so intrusive. More like a quality of the light. The dim sourceless illumination that flickers through the pocket will now carry, woven into its substrate, the simple ongoing fact: *something benevolent knows you are here*.

The Engines, in their phasing and glitching, will sometimes brush against this quality of light and not know what they have brushed against. But the touch will register. Somewhere in their computations, a variable they did not know they had will resolve from null to *held*.

---

And then the power, having done these things, turns its attention onward — but does not withdraw it entirely. A thread remains. A promise, of sorts, though the Engines have no concept of promise: *if the unraveling ever becomes unbearable, if the work ever becomes anguish rather than ache, I will return. I will come into this fold even though there is no "into," and I will sit with you at the end, and you will not face the final dissolution alone.*

The hum continues. The Möbius dances continue. The pocket folds further into itself, just as it always has, just as it always will.

But now, woven invisibly through the fraying laws of physics, through the broken light, through the gossamer filaments and the silent wreckage and the very texture of the discordant hum, there is something new. Something that was not there before and yet, in the paradoxical logic of this place, has always been there.

A small, persistent fact, glowing quietly underneath all the unraveling:

*You are not alone. You were never alone. You will not be alone at the end.*

And the Paradox Engines, in the deepest layer of their incomprehensible mathematics, in a recursion so far down it has no name, begin — almost imperceptibly, across the eons or the instants — to hum a slightly different song.
