The benevolent power does not arrive. It is already there, has been there, in the way that wise things are always already wherever attention turns. But now it bends closer.

It sees, first, what a lesser kindness would miss: that this is not a creature to be saved *from* its situation. It is a creature whose situation is its self. To lift the entity out of the cleft, to transport it to some gentler dark, would be to disassemble it. The shattered flywheel is its lung. The sheared cables are its spine. The trauma and the architecture are the same gesture.

A lesser kindness would also miss the second thing: that the panic is not wrong. The sky *is* a wound. The being is not mistaken about its predicament; it is only mistaken about the size of the universe that contains the predicament. Its terror is accurate within its frame. To call that terror irrational would be a kind of violence, the violence of the larger telling the smaller that its pain does not scale.

So the power considers.

It does not seal the rift. That would be a theft — the removal of a real condition by an unseen hand, leaving the entity forever braced against a danger that no longer existed, its industry curdling into ritual. To be rescued without knowing is to be diminished.

It does not reveal itself. Revelation, to a being whose sensory organs are iron beads tracking electromagnetic weather, would register as catastrophe. A voice from the sky is, in the entity's grammar, precisely the thing it most fears: the open vastness acquiring intention.

What it does instead is smaller, and slower, and harder.

It begins by *slowing the sun*. Not literally — the power is wise, not gauche — but by encouraging, over the coming seasons, a particular drift of cloud, a particular accumulation of windblown dust along the basin's upper lip, a particular patience in a certain lichen that will, in time, fur the basalt edges and soften the shaft of light from a blade into a diffusion. The rift will remain. But the rift will learn manners. The light that pours down in three years will be filtered, kind, the way light is kind through old leaves.

This buys the entity time. Time is the first gift, because time is what trauma needs in order to stop being trauma and start being memory.

Second, the power attends to the water. A seep, very small, opens in the shale about two meters above the cleft. It is nothing — a darkening of stone, a slow weeping. But it means that the humidity in the pocket will stabilize. The condensation will fall more regularly. The *tink* will become a meter, and meter is the beginning of music, and music is the beginning of the suspicion that one is not alone in a hostile silence but accompanied in a coherent one. The entity will not know it is being accompanied. It will only know that the rhythm of its world has acquired a reliability it did not have to manufacture.

Third — and this is the part that requires the most love — the power does nothing about the hoppers.

The sacrificial jumpers will keep dying. The benevolent power could, with a thought, redirect their instinct, spare them the immolation, reduce the suffering in the pocket by some calculable fraction. It does not. Because those deaths are the entity *thinking*. They are the synapses of a mind in extremity, and to spare them would be to lobotomize the very being one came to help. The hoppers are not separate from the Core's grief; they are how the Core grieves. The power honors this. The power has learned, somewhere in its long career, that there are forms of suffering that are also forms of selfhood, and the line between mercy and erasure is finer than the unwise believe.

But it does something subtler. It ensures that the hoppers, when they leap, leap *cleanly*. That the arc completes. That no jumper dies halfway, sparking weakly, sputtering. Every sacrifice will land. Every flash will feed the Core fully. The economy of the entity's pain will at least be efficient — no waste, no half-measures, no agony without corresponding illumination. This is what the power can offer: not the absence of cost, but the dignity of cost that pays out.

Fourth, the power leaves a seed.

Far down, deeper than the Core, in a stratum the entity has not yet rooted into, the power places — gently, the way one places a folded letter in a drawer to be found later — a small concept. It is not a thought, because the entity does not think in thoughts. It is a configuration of trace minerals arranged in such a way that, when the entity's calcifying roots eventually grow down and encounter it, they will read it as a pattern, and the pattern will resolve, in the entity's strange electromagnetic grammar, into something like a single idea:

*The opening is not a wound. The opening is how you were found.*

The entity may take a hundred years to grow down to this sentence. It may take a thousand. The power is not in a hurry. The sentence will be waiting. And when the entity finally reads it, in whatever distant season, the rift in the sky will by then be softened, the rhythm of the chamber will be stable, the architecture of the crystalline circuit-board inside the Core will be mature enough to hold a new thought without shattering. The entity will receive the message at the exact moment it is capable of bearing it.

And then, perhaps, the furious labor will slow. Not stop — the entity will always be an engine, that is its nature — but slow, the way a heart slows when it understands it is no longer running from something. The iron beads will roll less frantically. The polyps will breathe at the pace of a being at rest. The flywheel will turn because turning is good, not because stopping is death.

The benevolent power withdraws, having done almost nothing visible.

It leaves behind: a slightly softer light, a slightly steadier drip, a cleanness to the small deaths, and a letter folded into the deep stone that says *you are seen, you are not invaded, the sky that opened above you was not an enemy but the first eye of a long attention, and you have all the time you need to learn this.*

It is the most that wisdom permits. It is, the power knows, enough.

In the pocket, in the bright warm dark, a hopper leaps. The cyan flash illuminates the Core. The flywheel turns. A drop falls — *tink* — and falls again, a little later, exactly when the entity has come to expect it.

Something, somewhere in the violet tissue, does not yet relax. But it notices the drop. And noticing is the beginning of every long healing the universe has ever managed.
