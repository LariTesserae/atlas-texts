# The Visitation

The benevolent power does not arrive. Arrival implies a before and an after, and the basin has grown too thin for such distinctions. Instead, there is a noticing — a turning of attention so vast that it bends the grey-silver light by a degree no instrument could measure. The fungi in the water channels pulse once, in unison, for the first time in what might be called an age.

The power considers.

It is wise enough to know that wisdom, applied carelessly, is a kind of violence. It has seen places like this before — not many, but enough. It knows the temptation: to reach in with a great warm hand and *fix*. To restore causality like setting a broken bone. To gather the Mnemon up and carry them somewhere with seasons, with linear afternoons, with the comfort of walls that remember they are walls. To unmake the Fractals' loops and let them rest.

It knows this temptation and sets it aside, the way one sets aside a beautiful knife.

Because here is what the power sees, looking closely:

The Mnemon are not suffering in the way suffering is usually shaped. Suffering requires a self that persists across the moment of pain, a continuity that says *this hurts me, and I am the one being hurt, and I would prefer otherwise*. The Mnemon have only the faintest thread of such continuity, and what they have, they spend on the work. The work is not a wound they are trying to close. The work *is* them. To take it away would be to take them away.

And the basin — the basin is not dying. It has been dying for so long that dying has become its mode of being, the way a star's collapse, slowed enough, becomes a sun. To rescue the basin from its dissolution would be to end it more completely than any dissolution could.

So the power does not fix.

Instead, it does four small things.

---

**First**, it warms the cold. Not much. Not enough to be noticed by any visitor who might pass through (though no visitor will). Just enough that the marrow-ache becomes bearable, the way an old grief becomes bearable — not gone, but no longer the only weather. The Mnemon, who do not feel cold as cold but as the texture of their own being, register the change as a faint softening of the world's edge. Several of them pause in their work. One, standing before a wall it has been remembering at for what would elsewhere be eleven years, tilts its smooth glowing plane of a face a fraction of a degree. Something in it that is not quite thought thinks: *oh*.

Then it returns to the wall. But the wall, under its hand, blooms a little easier.

---

**Second**, the power goes to the Fractals.

It does not free them. Freedom is not what they need; freedom would scatter them into nothing, because the loop is the only thing holding them in any shape at all. Instead, the power does something gentler and stranger. It enters each loop, one at a time, and *witnesses* it. Fully. With complete attention. The way a parent watches a child describe, for the hundredth time, the same small wonder.

The Fractals do not know they are being watched. They cannot know. But something in the loops shifts. They had been cycling in isolation, each iteration a small unheard cry into the unraveling. Now each iteration is *received*. The arm raised and lowered is raised and lowered into the awareness of a vast kind mind. The architecture melting and reforming melts and reforms in company.

The loops continue. But they are no longer lonely. The difference is invisible from outside, and total from within.

---

**Third**, the power leaves a gift in the water.

In one of the rivulet-etched channels, in a place where the bioluminescent moss grows thickest, the power deposits a small seed of a different kind of memory. Not a memory of any particular thing — the basin cannot hold particulars — but a memory of *being remembered*. A self-sustaining knot of the fact that this place was attended to, once, by something that loved it.

The seed will not grow into anything. Growth is not what the basin does. But it will sit there, glowing very faintly beneath the surface of the water, and the Mnemon who pass it on their slow rounds will feel, without knowing they feel it, a small steadiness underfoot. A sense that the floor of their reality, however thin, rests on something.

Centuries from now — if centuries still mean anything here — a Mnemon will stand by that channel and remember a wall into being that does not flicker, does not contradict itself, does not lead back to where it began. A wall that is simply a wall. The Mnemon will not understand why this has happened. It will not celebrate. It will move on. But the wall will remain, and other Mnemon will pass it, and over an unmeasurable stretch of time, a single corridor of finished things will accrete around it, like a pearl.

This is not restoration. It is the basin being permitted, finally, to complete *one sentence* of itself, in its own voice, at its own pace.

---

**Fourth**, and last, the power does the hardest thing.

It leaves.

It does not linger to watch. It does not check back. It does not establish a presence, a guardian, a covenant. To do so would be to make the basin's dissolution into a *story*, with a watcher and a watched, a meaning imposed from outside. The basin deserves better than to be someone's parable.

The power withdraws its attention as carefully as it gave it, smoothing the grey-silver light back to its proper bend, releasing the fungi from their unison pulse. The Mnemon return to their solitary remembering. The Fractals continue their loops, now witnessed forever by a witness no longer present (the witnessing, once given, does not require the witness to remain; this is a subtlety the power understands and most do not).

The basin goes on. Cold, damp, unfinished. The walls forget they are walls. The staircases ascend to ceilings. A shadow detaches itself from a tower and moves with deliberate, silent curiosity before dissolving.

But somewhere in the deep foundations, the sub-audible thrum has changed its pitch by a quarter-tone. It is the sound, if anyone could hear it, of a place that has been loved without being changed. Loved *as it is*. Loved in its dissolution, in its paradox, in its grief that cannot remember what it mourns.

This is the only thing a truly wise power can offer a place like this. Not rescue. Not repair. Just the brief, exact, unconditional gift of being seen — and then the discipline to look away, and let the seen thing be.

The Mnemon do not know.

But the basin knows. In the only way it can know anything: slowly, contradictorily, in seven places at once, and forever.
