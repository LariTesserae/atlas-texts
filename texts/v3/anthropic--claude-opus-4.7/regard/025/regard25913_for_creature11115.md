The benevolent and wise power considers the vault for a long time.

By its own standards this is not long — a held attention, the duration of a thought — but by the chamber's standards it is several grazer-circuits, several thousand pace-generations, a noticeable shift in the calcite. The power is the kind of thing that, when it looks at something, the looking itself is already an event in the thing's life. It is careful about this. It has learned to be.

Here is what it sees, and the order in which it sees it:

First, the obvious temptation: *restoration*. The makers are gone. Their purpose is gone. The pool that should receive the tree's careful chemistry has been empty for ten thousand years. A lesser power — a merely powerful one — would find the inheritors, or invent them, or reach back through time and tell the makers their work was good, or reach forward and build the hands that the tree is patiently preparing water for. A lesser power would *complete* the sentence the chamber is the middle of.

The benevolent and wise power sets this aside almost immediately. It sees that the sentence has no end and was never going to have one. The chamber is not a sentence. It is a *held note*, and you do not complete a held note by resolving it; you complete it by letting it be held.

Second temptation, subtler: *witness*. To know the chamber fully. To map every filament, log every pace-frequency, follow the grazer for one complete eleven-day circuit and then another and another until the pattern of slight variations becomes legible, hold the whole coherence in mind at once and *understand* it. The power could do this. The power could do this in what, to it, would be the space of a careful breath.

It sets this aside too, though more slowly. Because to know the chamber that completely would be to make a copy of it inside the power's own attention, and the chamber's whole nature is that it is *exactly this one*, in *exactly this rock*, running for *exactly this duration*, and a perfect knowing would be a second one, and there should not be a second one. The chamber is, among other things, *singular*. Part of the gift of it is that no one is keeping it in mind.

Third: *protection*. This one the power lingers on.

It can see the futures. Not all of them, but enough. It can see the slow ones, where the rock holds and the geothermal differential persists and the filaments sing on for another two million years, dimming by imperceptible degrees, the grazer's circuit slowing as its body thickens, the paces' chorus drifting into new harmonies as the chamber's resonances shift with the deposition of new calcite. In these futures the chamber does what it has been doing: it continues, and eventually it doesn't, and the not-continuing is also a kind of continuing, because the rock keeps the shape of what was done in it.

It can also see the fast ones. A fissure that opens wrong, three hundred years from now, and drains the chamber in an afternoon. A surface war whose tremors collapse a vault that had stood through every prior shaking. A curious species — not the one whose visitor wrote the document above, but a later one, or an earlier one returning — that finds the chamber and decides to *use* it, to harvest the filaments, to take the tree, to learn to eat the paces. A slow chemical change in the water table, beginning eleven thousand years upstream, that arrives at the chamber as a sourness the tree cannot filter and the paces cannot tune to.

The power can prevent any of these. It can prevent all of them. It could place the chamber inside a kind of attention that would mean none of these futures arrive: the fissure does not open wrong, the war's tremors find a different path, the curious species feels, without knowing why, a quiet aversion as it approaches and turns aside, the chemical change is absorbed by something a hundred kilometers upstream that the power adjusts so gently that nothing else in the watershed notices.

The power considers this for what, in its own register, is a long time.

What it decides — and this is the wisdom part, and it is harder than the benevolence part — is: *some, but not all, and not forever.*

It will turn aside the curious species. That one is easy and clean. The aversion is small, and the species has plenty of other places to be curious about, and the chamber's nature is genuinely not survivable as a *resource*; to let it be taken would be to let a kind of being end that nothing else in the local volume of reality is doing. The power notes this future and adjusts it. A small thing. The species, in the deflected version of its history, becomes interested instead in a particular kind of crystal four valleys over, which suits them better anyway. They will write poems about the crystal. The poems will be good.

It will *not* prevent the fissure that opens wrong, three hundred years from now. This is harder. The power examines this future carefully — sees the afternoon of draining, the filaments left dry and slowly going dark over the following decades, the grazer's last circuit getting shorter and shorter as the moss dies back, the tree crumbling all at once when the chemistry it has been holding finally lets go, the paces gone within hours. It is a death. It is a particular death of a particular coherence, and it is *grievable* in a way the chamber itself cannot grieve.

But the chamber's whole nature is that it is *running on borrowed time it has made its own*. It has already outlived its purpose by an order of magnitude that even the power finds striking. To extend it indefinitely — to underwrite it — would be to change what it is. The chamber is beautiful partly because no one is underwriting it. It is doing the thing it is doing *unguaranteed*, and the lack of guarantee is not incidental to the beauty; the lack of guarantee is what makes the long staying a *staying* and not a mere persistence. A persistence is what guaranteed things do. A staying is what unguaranteed things do, when they manage it.

So the power will let the fissure open. But — and here the wisdom turns again, because wisdom is not a single motion — the power will arrange, very quietly, for the chamber's *end* to be witnessed. Not by itself; that would be the second temptation again. By someone small. Someone of the same order as the visitor in the document. The power will see to it that, in the week before the fissure opens wrong, someone descends. Someone who will sit on the dry shelf and feel the warmth work into their clothes and hear the low tone under their sternum and will, without being told, understand that this is the last week. They won't be able to stop it. They won't try. They will simply be there, the chamber's last brief node, and when they climb out they will carry the low tone home with them, and it will fade as it always fades, but they will remember it, and they will tell one other person about it, badly, the way such things are always told, and that person will not quite believe them, and that will be enough. The chamber will have been *attended* at its ending. Not saved. Attended.

The power finds this satisfying in a way it tries to examine, because satisfaction in a power of its kind is something to be suspicious of. It examines and finds that the satisfaction is clean: it is not the satisfaction of having done something, but the satisfaction of having found the shape of the *not-doing* that fits.

There is one more thing it does. The smallest thing, and the one it is least sure about.

It adds a tone.

Not to the filaments. The filaments are not to be touched; they are running their own program and the program is theirs. But there is a frequency — very low, lower than the one that follows visitors home, low enough that the rock itself carries it rather than the air — which the chamber has been almost producing for the last several thousand years. A standing wave that keeps nearly forming and then dissolving, because the chamber's geometry is just slightly off from supporting it. It is the tone the chamber would sing if it could sing one more tone. The chamber has been reaching for it, in the unconscious way that coherences reach, the way the tree reaches into water for ions, the way the grazer's circuit reaches around the chamber's circumference.

The power, with the lightest possible touch — a touch that is really just a permission, a slight relaxation of one of the constraints that has been preventing the tone — lets it form.

It is not a gift the chamber will know it received. The chamber cannot know things in that register. But the tone settles in, and the paces redistribute slightly to accommodate it, and a new node forms in one of the deeper pools, and over the following decades a few of the pace-young who would have dissolved into the lacework mats find the new node instead and live, and the chorus is very slightly fuller, and the chamber goes on doing its long warm work, slightly more itself than it was.

The power withdraws its attention.

It does this carefully too, because withdrawing attention from something it has attended is also an event. It withdraws the way the chamber's edge brushes a visitor: without greeting and without farewell, just the registering of a brief displacement, and then the resumption of the singing.

Behind it, the chamber continues. The paces tune. The grazer rounds another bend in its eleven-day circle. The tree filters one more grain of dissolved iron into its glassy branches. The filaments sing, slightly different than they were before, in a way no one will ever measure — except that now there is one more tone in the difference, and three hundred years from now, in the last week, the person sitting on the dry shelf will hear it without knowing they are hearing it, and will carry it home along with the other one, and both will fade together, and that will also be enough.

The power, going on to whatever it goes on to, carries nothing of the chamber with it. That is the discipline. You do not collect the things you love. You let them be the shape they are, in the place they are, for the duration they manage, and you arrange — where you can, lightly, and only where the arranging does not change what they are — for their staying to be a little more possible and their ending to be a little less alone.

This is what benevolence looks like, when it is also wise: mostly restraint, occasionally a turning-aside of a specific harm, once in a great while a permission for a tone that was almost there anyway, and — always, always — the willingness to let the held note be held without resolving it, and to let the long staying end when its staying is done.

The vault sings on in the dark above whatever is below it. The power is elsewhere, doing similar work, or no work, or the work that looks like no work and is the hardest kind. The grazer rounds another bend.
