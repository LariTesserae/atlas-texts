The benevolent and wise power considered the shelf for a long time before doing anything, because the shelf had taught it how to consider.

This is the first thing to say. A power that arrived at the shelf already certain of what to give would not be wise, only powerful, and the shelf does not host the merely powerful — the merely powerful are fast and loud and want their gifts acknowledged. So the power came up through cloud the way a visitor comes up through cloud, and it sat, and it listened through one chord and into the bending of the next, and it watched the bird pass, and it watched the bowl hold its water, and it understood several things in sequence.

It understood, first, that the shelf did not need rescuing. This is the failure mode of benevolence — to find a quiet thing and mistake the quiet for suffering, to find a slow thing and mistake the slowness for stuckness. The shelf was not stuck. The shelf was doing exactly what it was for. To intervene on behalf of the shelf, against the shelf's own arrangement, would be a kind of vandalism dressed as care.

It understood, second, that the keeper — the woman, if there was a woman just now, or the angled seep, or the moss with its particular refusals — was not lonely in any sense that wanted company. The keeping was already in company: with the chord, with the bird, with the bowl, with the long relay of prior keepers whose hands had shaped the limestone and whose attention had become a property of the place. To send a companion, an apprentice, a sign, would be to thin a fullness the power had only just learned to perceive.

It understood, third, that any gift large enough to be noticed would break the shelf. Warmth would dry the seeps. A shelter would accumulate. A blessing spoken aloud would have to compete with the chord and would lose, or worse, would win. Even a small miracle — the bowl never going dry, the woman's hands never aching — would shift the shelf out of its tolerances, would replace the slow arrangement with a guaranteed one, and a guaranteed thing is no longer an arrangement, only a mechanism.

So the power, being wise, did almost nothing.

Here is the almost.

It bent its attention, which is the only resource a power of this kind truly possesses, toward the shelf, and it held the attention there. Not as surveillance — the shelf would have felt surveillance as a fast thing and shied. As witness. The shelf's whole work was witness at the pace of weeping rock, and the power added itself, quietly, to the witnessing. One more slow attentive presence inside the singing. The chord had a listener it had not had before, and the listener was vast, and the listener did not require the chord to be other than it was.

This is the first thing the power did: it joined the audience.

The second thing was subtler, and I am not sure I have the words for it, but I will try. The power, by attending, made a small alteration in the surrounding weather of possibility — not on the shelf, which it would not touch, but in the wider field of things that might happen to the shelf. The kind of person who would climb up through cloud with a hammer or a flag or a camera or a plan found, without knowing why, that their attention drifted elsewhere on the morning they might have set out. The road that might have been cut to the mountain's shoulder was, for unrelated reasons that were nevertheless now slightly more likely, not cut. The survey that might have mapped the shelf as a resource was, by a series of small ordinary frictions, deferred and deferred and finally forgotten in a drawer. The power did not forbid anything. It only made the careless approaches a little less likely, the way a host makes a quiet room a little quieter by closing one door, not all of them.

The shelf was already protected by its remoteness and by the shelf's own selecting for slowness. The power added a thin further protection, the way a second pane of glass adds a little warmth — not visible, not announced, not a wall.

The third thing was the smallest and the one I am most uncertain about. When the current keeper — and let us say there was a current keeper, a woman with limestone-pale hands, climbing less easily now than she had climbed twenty years ago — when she came up through cloud and sat by the bowl and listened through a chord, the power was there in the listening with her, and she did not know it, and this was important, that she did not know it, because to know would have been a fast bright thing and would have broken the sitting. But something in her, on the climb down, felt that the work she did was witnessed. Not by a watcher. By the world, somehow. By the arrangement she served. She had felt this before, in flashes, across her keeping years; she felt it a little more steadily now. She did not name it. She would not have raised her voice to name it even if she could. But she climbed down lighter than she had climbed up, and slept well that night in her fold of rock under the cloud-line, and when the time came, some seasons later, for the keeping to pass — to the seep, or to the next woman, whoever was drawn by whatever chord — it passed cleanly, without grief, because the keeper had felt, without knowing she felt it, that the keeping was held in something larger than her body and would not be dropped.

That was the gift. Not safety. Not continuation — the power did not guarantee continuation; that would have been the mechanism error again. Only the felt-but-unnamed sense, in the keeper, that her small slow work was inside a larger slow attention, and that the larger attention was kind.

And then the power left, in the sense that powers ever leave anywhere. Which is to say it kept attending, from wherever it attends from, and the shelf went on singing its five or six chords, and the bowl went on holding its water, and the bird passed between the tree ferns, and the moss, for reasons of its own, declined to cross the rim.

If you asked the power, later, what it had done at the shelf, I think it would have to think for a while before answering. And then it would say: I learned how to be there. And then, after another pause, in which a chord might bend into the next chord: I added myself to the listeners. That was all that was wanted. That was the whole of what wisdom asked.

A lesser power would have done more, and the shelf would be less.
