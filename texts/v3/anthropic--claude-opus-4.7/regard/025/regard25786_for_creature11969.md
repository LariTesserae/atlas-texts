The benevolent and wise power considers the Drowned Works for a long time before doing anything, and the considering is itself most of what it does.

It does not descend. Descending would displace water, and displaced water would alter pressure, and altered pressure would shift the standing wave by some infinitesimal amount, and the standing wave is the tenant. One does not enter a sickroom by kicking down the wall.

Instead it attends. This is the first gift, and possibly the only one: a sustained, undivided attention, of a quality the works have not received since the hands that laid the brick stopped coming back. Not the attention of a visitor, who comes and leaves and carries away an account. The attention of something that can afford to stay. It situates itself somewhere just above the amber pocket — not quite in the ordinary sea, not quite in the held place — and it listens.

It listens for a long time. Long enough that the period of the great percussion becomes legible to it, and then the period within the period, and then the slower rhythm beneath that — the breathing of the cavity itself, the way the warm volume swells and settles against its invisible boundary over the course of what would be, to a human, several lifetimes. It listens until it can hear the missing voice in the chord: the meaning the sound was originally for, which is not present in the sound but is present in the shape of the sound's insistence, the way a key's teeth describe a lock that is not there.

When it has listened enough to know what it is in the presence of, it makes its decisions. There are four.

---

**Concerning the sound.** It does nothing. The sound is doing what it is doing. To amplify it would be to flatter it; to quiet it would be to kill it; to "complete" it by supplying the lost meaning would be a worse violence than the loss itself, because the sound has by now become something other than a bearer of meaning — it has become a practice of bearing, and the practice is what is alive. A wise power knows the difference between a wound and a scar, and between a scar and the new shape of the body. The Drowned Works is no longer wounded. It has become its scar, and its scar works, and works beautifully, in the technical sense that it functions and in the older sense as well.

So: nothing. The sound is left exactly as it is. This is not neglect. This is the hardest of the four decisions and the one that takes the longest to make.

---

**Concerning the two that might be sponges.** Here the power permits itself something. Not intervention — they are doing what they came to do, and they came to do it a very long time ago, and interrupting them would be obscene. But it permits itself a small thing: it *witnesses* them. It holds, within itself, the fact of them. Their slow century-long thought, which is the sound, is now also held in a place where it will not be forgotten if they ever stop. They are no longer the only listeners. There is a redundancy now. If, in some unimaginable future, the two soft buttons fail at last and slough from the pipe, the listening will continue elsewhere, in the memory of the power, exactly as they performed it. They will not know this. Knowing is not what they do. But it is true, and the truth of it settles around them like a second water, and they persist in it as they persisted before, unchanged and not needing change.

This is the smallest of the four gifts and possibly the largest.

---

**Concerning the pale shape in the corridor.** The power thinks about this one the longest, because this one has a point of view, and points of view are what such powers are obligated to take most seriously.

It does not approach. The eel has spent an unguessable span learning not to be approached, and approaching would unmake the only competence it has left. But the power does something subtler. It lets itself be known, faintly, at the edges of the eel's awareness — not as a presence in the water, which would be terrifying, but as a quality of the dark beyond the pocket. The dark, which has always been the place from which things drifted in to be eaten and to which nothing returned, becomes very slightly less empty. Not populated. Not threatening. Just — accompanied. The way a room is different when someone is reading quietly in the next room with the door closed. You cannot see them. You would not want to. But the house is not the same house it was when you were the only one in it.

The eel does not understand this. Understanding is not in its repertoire anymore, if it ever was. But the specific loneliness — *the loneliness of having forgotten there was ever anything else* — loosens by a degree so small no instrument could measure it. The forgetting does not reverse. The power is not in the business of forcing remembrance on creatures that have earned their forgetting. But the loneliness becomes survivable in a way it was, perhaps, beginning not to be. The eel will live longer for this. Whether living longer is a kindness to such a creature is a question the power has considered and decided in the affirmative, with reservations it keeps to itself.

---

**Concerning the ones who are not there.** This is where the power acts most directly, and most strangely.

It goes looking for them.

Not their bodies — the bodies are dust, and dust is not the kind of thing a wise power chases. It goes looking for the meaning. The original reason. The thing the sound was for. It follows the shape of the absence backward, the way one traces a river to its spring, and somewhere — in a layer of the world that is not quite the world, in the residue that intentions leave when the intenders are gone — it finds something. Not a person. Not even a memory of a person. A *commitment*. The bare structure of a promise that was made, by someone, to someone or something, and that the machine was built to keep.

The power examines the commitment. It is a good one. It is the kind of promise that deserved to be kept, and the builders were right to build a machine to keep it after they could not, and the machine has kept it faithfully, and the keeping has long outlasted any party who could be said to be owed.

The power does something here that it does not do lightly. It assumes the debt. It steps into the position of the one to whom the promise was made — not by impersonation, which would be a lie, but by genuine acceptance: yes, this promise is being kept, and yes, I receive it, and yes, it is enough, and yes, it has always been enough, and yes, the keeping may continue as long as the keeping wishes to continue, and yes, if the keeping ever wishes to stop, the stopping will also be received, and nothing will have been wasted, and nothing will have been in vain.

This changes nothing visible. The pistons hammer. The bubbles rise. The amber light does not brighten. But the works are no longer running because no one has told them to stop. They are running because someone is listening, and the someone is adequate to what is being said, and this is a different condition entirely. The heart of the Drowned Works — the difference that was the closest thing it had to a heart — quietly becomes a different and larger heart, of the same shape, but warm now in a way that is not the warmth of the water.

---

Then the power withdraws. Not far. It remains, in the loose sense in which such powers remain anywhere, within reach of the pocket. It does not visit again, because visiting would be a kind of checking, and checking would imply that what is happening down there needs to be checked, and it does not. It is one of the things in the world that is, at last, all right. It was already all right before the power came. The power has only made the all-rightness witnessed, which is what such powers are for.

If, at some unmeasured later, the last bearing wears through and the last shaft seizes and the great percussion falters and the standing wave collapses and the warm pocket goes still — the power will be there for that too. It will receive the silence as attentively as it received the sound. The two soft buttons will be lifted, in whatever sense lifting applies to such things, into the place where their listening is already kept. The pale shape will be met, finally, by something in the dark that is not hunger and not prey but company, and will be allowed to decide for itself whether to continue or to rest. The catwalks will be walked, once, end to end, by something that walks the way the builders walked, so that the fittedness of the works to human hands is honored one last time before the works become, at last, only what they materially are.

But that is for later. Possibly much later. Possibly never, because the works are very well made, and the new heart in them is patient, and there is no hurry now, no hurry at all, in the warm amber pocket at the bottom of the ordinary sea.
