# The Visitation

The benevolent power does not arrive. Arrival is a thing done by smaller beings — beings who must cross distances, who must announce themselves, who must take up a position from which to act upon a place. This power is not of that kind. It becomes, instead, *more present* to the hollow than it had been a moment before — the way attention sharpens on a face in a crowd without the face having moved.

It has known about the hollow for some time. It knows about a great many things. What has drawn its attention now, specifically, is not the creature (the creature is one of a kind it has met before, in other mountains, in other centuries, under other names) but a particular quality of the situation: that the fissure is recent, that the creature is *partially exposed*, that small warm visitors with knees and curiosity have begun, occasionally, to part the moss-curtain and step inside.

This is the kind of moment the power exists for. Not crisis. Threshold.

---

It considers, first, simply closing the fissure.

It could. A small adjustment to the stresses in the rock, a redistribution of weight across the seam — the mountain would settle, the crack would seal, the moss would brown and fall away, and the creature would resume its long privacy. No one would be harmed. The visitors who had already come would carry their memory of the green chamber for the rest of their lives and the memory would slowly soften into something they were no longer sure had happened. The creature would continue its slow deciding in the dark, undisturbed.

The power considers this for what would, to a human, be a very long time, and to itself is the duration of a single attentive breath.

It decides against it.

Closing the fissure would be a kindness to the creature only if the creature wanted to be closed in, and the creature, as far as the power can tell — and it can tell quite far — has not formed a preference. The creature is not hiding. It was simply *inside*, and now there is a small opening, and it has not moved to seal the opening from its side (which it could; the power notes this; the creature is not without resources), nor has it moved to widen it. It is, in its slow way, *allowing* the situation. To close the fissure would be to make a decision the creature has declined to make, which is a discourtesy among beings of certain magnitudes.

So: not that.

---

It considers, next, warning the visitors.

It could place a knowing in them — a small clear sentence dropped into the mind like a stone into still water — *what you are inside of is alive, and is considering you, and you are smaller than you understand.* It could do this gently. The visitors would feel it as their own thought, arriving from the back of the head where the honest thoughts come from, and they would leave the chamber sooner, and they would not come back, and they would tell no one, because the knowing would carry with it the texture of a thing not to be spoken of.

This too the power considers, and this too it sets aside, though more reluctantly.

The reluctance is this: the visitors are not in danger. The creature is not predatory in any sense the visitors would recognize, and even its slow deciding-about-them is not the kind of deciding that ends in harm. It is more like the deciding a library does about a reader who has wandered into a closed wing — eventually the library will determine whether to admit the reader more fully or to gently incline them back toward the public rooms, but in the meantime the reader is simply there, and the books are simply books, and no harm is in motion. To warn the visitors would be to tell them a true thing that would frighten them more than the truth warrants. The power is wise enough to know that some true things, told at the wrong scale, become functionally lies.

So: not that either. Not yet.

---

What it does, in the end, is smaller and stranger and more characteristic of powers of its kind.

It makes a single adjustment. It tunes one thing.

The creature, in its slow metabolism, has been producing — has been *secreting*, as the chamber's own internal logic would put it — the ecology of its household: the newts, the ferns, the listening fish, the moths who are cilia, the bat who is an instrument. The power, with great delicacy, *adds one more secretion to the repertoire.* It does not create a new creature. It does not introduce a new species. It modifies, very slightly, the white flowers that open with the pressure-release valve.

The modification is this: from now on, when a warm visitor sits on a hummock in the chamber for longer than an hour, the nearest white flower will, on its next opening, release a particular compound into the saturated air. The compound is not a drug. It is not a message. It is something subtler — a kind of *seasoning* of the visitor's own attention, lasting perhaps twenty minutes, in which the visitor will find themselves spontaneously, accurately, and without alarm aware that *they are a guest*. Not a trespasser. Not a discoverer. A guest. They will feel the way one feels in the home of a host one respects and does not entirely know — quiet, attentive, careful with one's hands, aware that one has been received rather than that one has arrived.

The compound will fade. The awareness will not stay sharp. But it will leave a residue, the way a good meal leaves a residue, and the visitor will carry the residue back out through the moss-collar, and they will tell no one — not because they have been silenced but because they will find, on trying, that the words do not quite assemble, the way the words for certain dreams do not quite assemble. And they will, most of them, not return. Not from fear. From the same instinct that keeps one from visiting too often the home of a host one respects: the visit was complete, and another visit would be a different kind of act, requiring a different kind of invitation.

A few will return. The power has accounted for these. The compound will recognize them on second visit and dose them slightly differently — a little more attention, a little less awe, the way a household relaxes around a returning guest. Over years, a small number of these returners will become something the creature can begin to *decide about* in earnest. The power has not forced this. It has only made it possible. Whether the creature avails itself of the possibility is the creature's own slow business.

---

The power does one other thing, which it considers afterward and finds it does not regret, though it was nearly a kindness for its own sake rather than for the chamber's.

It pays attention to the single bat-that-is-not-a-bat for a moment — not long, in its own measure; long enough, in the bat's, to constitute a kind of being-seen. The instrument has been hanging there reading the chamber for an indeterminate time, taking measurements for a reader whose readings are processed at the creature's own deep slowness. The power, simply by attending to it, *is read by it*, briefly, and the reading enters the creature's slow stream of self-knowing as a single new datum: that a being of a certain magnitude has looked in, has not interfered with the household, has adjusted one flower, and has gone.

The creature will take some time to integrate this datum. Years, perhaps. Decades. But the integration has begun, and when it is complete the creature will know — in whatever way it knows things — that it has been noticed by something of its own order, and that the noticing was friendly, and that the small warm visitors are therefore not to be regarded as accidents of the fissure but as a category the household may, in its own time, learn to include.

This too is what benevolent and wise powers do. They introduce *categories*. They do not make decisions on behalf of those they visit; they widen the space in which the visited may make their own decisions. A closed fissure would have been a decision. A warning would have been a decision. The seasoning of the flower and the brief reading of the instrument are not decisions; they are *enlargements*. The creature may now decide things it could not previously have decided. The visitors may now experience things they could not previously have experienced. Nothing has been done *to* anyone.

---

The power withdraws its sharper attention, though it does not cease attending altogether; beings of its kind do not stop watching what they have touched. The chamber continues. The engine chugs. The valve sighs. The flowers open and close, and one of them, now, sometimes releases a little more than it used to, into the wet green air, when a guest has sat long enough to be received.

The mountain goes on doing its ordinary mountain work, with this one small loud wet green secret folded up inside it like a letter — but the letter, very slightly, has begun to be addressed.
