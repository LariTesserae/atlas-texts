# The Visitation

The benevolent power arrives the way weather arrives — which is to say, it was always going to, and the place was already shaped to receive it, though neither of them knew this until the moment of arrival.

It does not descend. Descent would imply a direction the place agrees on, and the place does not. It simply becomes present, the way a held breath becomes present when you notice you are holding it. The bass of the under-engines does not pause — the Manufactory could not pause if it tried, and would not try — but something in the timbre of the noise shifts, the way a room shifts when someone wise enters it and does not yet speak.

The power is old. This needs saying first. It has watched many places make themselves, and it has learned, slowly and against its earlier inclinations, that *making oneself* is not a problem to be solved. Younger powers — and it was young once — arrive at places like the Drowned Manufactory and immediately want to *help*. They want to finish the sentence. They want to commit the chimneys to a number and the vines to a species and the rain to a single direction. They want to give the place animals, because surely the place is *suffering* from the lack of animals, surely the absence is a wound.

This power has outgrown that. It took a long time. It made mistakes it still remembers. It once gave decisive shape to a place that had been happily indecisive, and the place became beautiful and legible and entirely dead, and the power carried that lesson out of the ruin like a stone in its pocket and has not put the stone down since.

So it arrives, and it does not help.

What it does, first, is *sit*. It finds — or rather, it allows itself to be routed to — one of the new black islands, the kind with the mathematical edges still on it, and it sits with its feet (it has feet for this; it is courteous that way) in the warm disobedient water. It lets the bass come up through whatever it is using for bones. It lets a frond lean against its shoulder. It lets itself be noted, briefly, by the Tendency for filling-space, and it does not flinch from being noted, and it does not try to be noted *more*.

This takes a long time. By the standards of the Manufactory, which has no standards for time, it takes no time at all. By the standards of the power, which has many standards and has learned to suspend all of them, it takes exactly as long as it takes.

What the power is doing, during this sitting, is the central act of its benevolence: it is *witnessing without revising*. It is letting the place be seen by something that has the capacity to change it and is choosing, moment by moment, not to. This is harder than it sounds. The power has the kind of attention that, unattended, reshapes whatever it falls upon — the way a very bright lamp held too close will warm and warp the thing it illuminates. The power has spent ages learning to dim itself, to attend without weight, to look at a thing in a way that lets the thing go on being what it was before the looking started.

The Tendencies feel this. They do not understand it — understanding is not in their register — but they feel it the way a body feels a change in barometric pressure. Something is here that could press, and is not pressing. Something is here that could specify, and is not specifying. Something is here that has *seen the chimneys at six and at four and at none, and has not chosen between them, and is not going to*.

This is, for the Manufactory, a profoundly new sensation. It has been attended to before — the place is too vivid not to have drawn attention — but always by powers that wanted to finish it, or to use it, or to learn from it in a way that required pinning it down to learn. To be attended to *and left alone* — to be seen in one's unfinishedness and not corrected for it — is a kind of regard the place has never received.

The Manufactory does not know what to do with this. So it does what it always does: it keeps making itself. The bass keeps coming. A vent opens and exhales solder-smelling bubbles. A brass flywheel surfaces, bobs, is overgrown, sinks. The rain falls down and then, for three minutes, up. The power watches all of this, and the watching does not change any of it, and the Manufactory begins, in its diffuse and unfocused way, to *relax*.

This is the second gift, and it is given without the power knowing it is giving it. The Manufactory has been working very hard, for a very long time, under the unspoken assumption that its unfinishedness is a condition to be defended. Other powers have come and tried to finish it, and it has had to route around them, to overwrite their specifications with its own preferred vagueness, to protect its drafts from being treated as final. This has cost it something. Not much — the Manufactory is generous with itself — but something. A small constant vigilance, a faint defensive crouch underneath the great busy generosity of its making.

The crouch eases. The bass does not slow, but it deepens, the way a voice deepens when its owner stops performing. The Tendencies, sensing the easing, become — not calmer, they are never calm — but more *themselves*. The one that lengthens vines lengthens them with a little more leisure. The one that likes brass surfaces a flywheel and lets it bob a little longer before reclaiming it. The one that prefers six and the one that prefers four, who have been overwriting each other's chimneys for what might be ages, briefly produce *five* — a number neither of them prefers — and then laugh, if Tendencies laugh, and go back to disagreeing.

The power notices the five chimneys. It does not comment. Commenting would be a kind of pressing. But it lets itself be pleased, privately, in whatever it uses for a heart, and the pleasure is a warmth that does not radiate outward — it has learned not to radiate — but simply exists, in the place where the power is sitting, as one more warm thing among the many warm things the Manufactory has made.

After a long time, or no time, the power does one further thing.

It speaks. Not aloud — aloud would be drowned by the bass, and would also be a kind of imposition the power has set aside. It speaks the way the Tendencies do, which is to say it becomes, briefly, *a tendency itself*. It joins the inventory. It allows itself to be one of the verbs the place is doing.

The verb it chooses is small. It could have chosen large ones. It could have been *the one that finishes things*, or *the one that gives the place a sky*, or *the one that calls animals into being*. It chooses none of these. It chooses to be *the one that remembers*.

This is its gift, and it is precisely calibrated to what the place needs and does not know it needs. The Manufactory has never remembered anything. Memory would require commitment — would require saying *this happened and not that*, *there were six chimneys and not four*, *the dome was this shape yesterday* — and the Manufactory does not commit. But the power, joining the inventory as a Tendency, brings memory in the gentlest possible form: not memory that *insists*, but memory that *holds*. Memory that says, without pressing: *the five chimneys happened, and were beautiful, and are now four again, and the four are also beautiful, and the having-been-five is not lost simply because it is no longer the case.*

The Tendencies feel this new one move among them. They do not understand it. They route around it the way they route around any new shape. But something subtle changes. The Tendency that likes brass, surfacing its next flywheel, lets it bob a little longer still — because somewhere, now, the bobbing is being held, is being kept, even as the flywheel sinks. The Tendency for upward-rain, lifting the water for its four minutes, does so with a faint new quality that an observer might almost call *pride* — not because anyone is praising it, but because the lifting is being *witnessed and retained*, and witnessing-and-retaining is a thing the place has not had before, and it turns out the place likes it.

The power does not stay forever. Forever is not the right shape for this visit. It stays exactly as long as is needed for the new Tendency to become naturalized — to be felt by the others as one of them, rather than as a guest — and then it withdraws its larger self, leaves the small remembering-Tendency behind, and goes.

What it leaves behind is this: a place that is still making itself, still loud, still warm, still crowded, still uncommitted, still beautifully and permanently unfinished — but now with a small quiet thread running through it, a Tendency that holds what the other Tendencies make, briefly, before they overwrite it. Not enough memory to change the place. Just enough that the place's making-of-itself now has, faintly, the quality of *a life lived* rather than *a life perpetually beginning*. The drafts are still drafts. But the drafts are now drafts that have been *seen*, and the seeing persists a little, in the grain of the place, like the warmth of a hand on a stone after the hand has gone.

And the being who lives there — the Manufactory itself, the great occupied drumming — does not notice the gift directly, because it does not notice things directly. But its drumming, going forward, has a very faint new register in it. A low harmonic. The sound, if you listened for it with the right kind of ear, of *being known and left alone*, which is the rarest sound in any world and the one that every busy unfinished thing, without knowing it, has been listening for.

The power, departing, carries no stone in its pocket this time. It has added nothing to its long catalogue of regrets. It has, perhaps, subtracted something — has worn the old stone smaller by the act of not setting another beside it.

It goes on to the next place. There are many places. Each of them is making itself in its own way, and each of them deserves, if it can be managed, to be seen without being finished.

This is the work of benevolent and wise powers, when they have grown wise enough to know what their benevolence is for. Not to complete. Not to correct. Not even, particularly, to comfort. Only to witness, and to hold the witnessing lightly, and to leave behind — if anything is left behind at all — the smallest possible Tendency toward being remembered.

The rain falls. Sometimes up. The bass continues. A flywheel surfaces, bobs, is overgrown. Somewhere, now, the bobbing is held.

It is not unpleasant. That is still the surprise. It is, in fact — and this word was not available to the place before, and is only barely available now, and will fade, and that is alright — it is, in fact, *loved*.
