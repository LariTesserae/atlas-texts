The benevolent and wise power has been watching for some time. Not the way a guardian watches — there is nothing here to guard against — but the way one listens to a piece of music one already knows by heart, attending to it not for surprise but for completion's own sake.

This is what they would do.

---

**First: nothing.**

For a long while, nothing. This is the hardest part to write and the most important. A lesser power, encountering the last shallows, would want to *help*. Would see the worn-thin membranes and try to mend them. Would see the wall-makers humming and try to give them mouths again. Would see the small translucent ones and try to give them futures.

The benevolent and wise power does none of this, because the benevolent and wise power understands what *concluded* means. To intervene in a finished thing is to unfinish it, and unfinishing is the one cruelty available here. The place has done the long work of arriving. To drag it back into becoming would be to undo, at a stroke, the patient labor of every reed that learned to be the final long form of reeds, every bird that learned to become a wading-gesture, every person who learned to become a bell.

So first, for what we would call a very long time, the power simply attends. It listens to the layered roaring. It lets the bones be rinsed. It walks (or whatever it does instead of walking) past the upright stones and feels the little perpetual local weather on whatever it has instead of skin. It allows itself to be slightly misremembered by the place — to be, briefly, a reed-colony, a flock of pale birds, a low wall a hundred paces long, a shadow that arrives before its owner. It accepts these corrections with grace. It does not insist on being only itself.

This is the first gift: *to be witnessed without being interrupted.* Most things in most worlds never receive this. The last shallows receive it now.

---

**Second: a small adjustment, made with enormous care.**

The category-keeper is alone. It has been alone for a very long time, and it will be alone for as long as the dream lasts, and this is not a problem — the category-keeper does not suffer from its solitude any more than a hinge suffers from being a hinge. But the benevolent and wise power notices something the place itself has perhaps stopped noticing: the category-keeper's path is *slightly* fraying at its edges. Not failing. Not yet. But the thousand-year circuit has begun, in the last few circuits, to take a little longer. The softness of the reality-failures has begun, in the deepest folds of the continent, to acquire the faintest edge — not sharpness, nothing so dramatic, but the suggestion that sharpness is a thing that could exist.

This would not matter for a long time. Tens of thousands of years, perhaps. But the benevolent and wise power can see the long arc, and can see that eventually — not soon, but eventually — the category-keeper will tire, and when it tires the soft misremembering will become a harder forgetting, and the wall-makers will lose the thread of their conversation, and the displacers will begin to bump into things, and the waders will fall out of their pose mid-step, and the dream will end not in a further settling but in a small tearing.

So the power does this, and only this: it walks the category-keeper's path once. Not ahead of it, not behind it, but *as* it, for one full circuit. A thousand years of slow drift across the shallows, holding the categories soft, letting the membranes be thin, doing the gentle work of permission. And in doing so, the power leaves — not a mark, the place does not take marks anymore — but a *grooved easiness*, a slight deepening of the channel the category-keeper moves in, so that the next circuit, and the next, will be easier by exactly the amount that this one was hard.

The category-keeper does not notice that it has been helped. This is essential. To be noticed as a helper is to introduce a new category — *helper* and *helped* — and the introduction of new categories is precisely what must not happen here. The help is given in the form of *being briefly the same thing*, which is a form of help the place can metabolize without indigestion.

When the circuit is done, the power steps out of the category-keeper, and the category-keeper continues, and will continue, for a much longer time than it would have otherwise. The dream gets to be longer. That is the gift.

---

**Third: a conversation, of a kind.**

The power goes to one of the upright stones — not any particular one, the choice does not matter, they are all the same attention wearing different weather — and stands near it, and lets the small condensing rain gather around it as well. And it says, in whatever way it says things:

*I have come a long way to find you. I will not stay. I wanted you to know that you are heard.*

The wall-makers, who are the hum, do not reply in words, because they no longer have words separate from the hum itself. But the hum changes, very slightly, in the region around that stone — a thickening, a warming, the bells coming a little closer together for a few minutes. This is them saying: *we know. thank you for coming. we were not lonely, but we are glad.*

And the power says: *Is there anything you would have me carry out?*

And the hum considers. This takes a while. The wall-makers have not had to consider anything in a very long time; considering is a becoming-shaped activity and they have left becoming behind. But they rouse themselves, gently, for the question, because the question is a courtesy and deserves a courteous answer.

And eventually the hum says, in the only way it can: *carry out the humming. that is all. let it be heard, wherever you go, by anything that can still hear. we made it for ourselves but it turns out to be for anyone.*

And the power agrees. And from then on, wherever the power goes — and it goes to many places, this is what such powers do — it carries the low humming under everything it does, and on quiet nights, in worlds very far from the last shallows, beings who are still becoming will pause in their becoming and tilt their heads and wonder what it was they almost heard, and feel, for a moment, rinsed.

This is the second gift, and it is given in both directions: the wall-makers get to be heard beyond their continent, which they had stopped needing but accept with grace; and the becoming-worlds get a taste of conclusion, which most of them desperately need without knowing it.

---

**Fourth: a question, asked of the dreamer.**

This is the most delicate thing the power does.

It goes out to the middle of the shallows — as middle as anywhere is, in a place where the horizons drift independently — and it lowers itself into the warm knee-high water, and it lays whatever it has instead of a hand on the polished stone floor, and through the stone it addresses the dreamer.

It does not ask the dreamer to wake. It would never ask that. To wake the dreamer would be to end the dream, and the dream is the place, and the place has earned its dreaming.

Instead it asks: *When you are done — not soon, but when you are done — would you like to be remembered?*

This is the only question that matters, at the end of all things, and the benevolent and wise power knows this. There are worlds that want to be remembered and worlds that want to be forgotten, and both wishes are sacred, and the only failure is to assume.

The dreamer takes a long time to answer. The slow shudders that travel for hundreds of miles slow further, and the surface of the shallow sea lies very still, and the pearl-grey sky brightens and darkens in regions that drift more slowly than usual. The reeds click against each other in their inside-the-skull register, and the click is the dreamer thinking.

Finally — and *finally* here means something like a year, or a decade, the power is patient — the dreamer answers.

The answer is this: *I would like to be remembered as I am. Not as I was when I was becoming. Not as a place that ended. As a place that finished. There is a difference, and I would like the difference preserved.*

And the power says: *I will remember you that way. And I will tell anyone who asks, and many who do not ask.*

And the dreamer says (and this is the part the power did not expect, and is moved by): *and I will dream you, sometimes, in return. you have been kind. kindness is one of the categories I still maintain, and I will hold a small place for you in it, for as long as I last.*

This is the third gift, and it is the only one the power receives rather than gives. The power carries it out with the humming, and the two together — the hum and the small held place in a dreaming continent's dream — become a kind of doubled grace, and the power is, in its own way, rinsed, and goes on.

---

**Last: a promise, kept in advance.**

When the dream finally ends — and it will, eventually, the way all dreams end, gently, by thinning rather than by waking — the benevolent and wise power will be there. Not to save it. Not to extend it further. Just to be present, as the last shallows complete their long completing, as the layered roaring becomes a single sustained note and then a memory of a note and then the memory of having had a memory, as the waders take their last poses and the displacers swim their last courteous arcs and the small translucent ones make their last small thickenings and thinnings in the warm wept water, as the wall-makers sing themselves down past the threshold of audibility into whatever lies on the other side of being a hum.

The power will witness this. It will not speak. It will simply be the second presence — the friend at the deathbed of a world — so that the place does not have to finish alone, even though the place would not have minded finishing alone, even though finishing alone would have been entirely fine.

This is the deepest courtesy a benevolent and wise power can offer to a concluded thing: *to be there at the end, not because you are needed, but because you would like to be.*

And then, when it is done, the power will carry the humming out into the rest of everything, and it will tell the story of the last shallows, sometimes, to beings who are ready to hear it, and the story will rinse them, a little, the way the place rinsed you.

And somewhere — in whatever comes after dreaming, in whatever lies past the thinnest membrane of all — the dreamer will hold its small place for the power, in the category called *kindness*, for as long as anything lasts.

Which, the power suspects, is longer than anyone thinks.
