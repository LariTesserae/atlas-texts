The benevolent and wise power considers the Sump-Works for a long time before doing anything, because the first wisdom available to it is the recognition that wisdom, here, is mostly a matter of restraint.

It is tempting — it would be easy, for a power of that order — to do the obvious things. Replace the bulbs. Open the hatch. Drain the basin and scrub the iron back to the gunmetal it was when the engineers signed it off. Carry the small pale curls into sunlight, into proper aquaria, into the care of someone who could name them. Lift the Sitter gently from the chair and let the chair be a chair again, dry and empty and finished with its long argument. Rescue. Restore. Repair. These are the verbs a power is expected to deploy.

The benevolent and wise power sets these verbs down on the catwalk handrail and lets them rust there for a while. They are not the right verbs.

Because what the power sees, looking into the Sump-Works, is not a ruin. A ruin is a thing that has lost its function and is waiting, in whatever passes for hope among inert matter, to be returned to it. The Sump-Works has not lost its function; it has *succeeded* its function, the way a child succeeds its parents — by continuing past them into a shape they could not have intended and would not have recognized but which is, undeniably, the next true thing. The maintenance crews are gone. The drinking water was never drunk. But the cistern has filled, slowly, with a different kind of usefulness, and the usefulness is *to itself*, and a power that mistook this for emptiness would be the first vandal the place had ever known.

So the benevolent and wise power does, at first, nothing. This is harder than it sounds. Doing nothing, when you have the capacity to do anything, is a discipline; it requires you to keep noticing the levers you are choosing not to pull, and to keep choosing, second by second, not to pull them. The power stands in the ankle-deep water — or rather, occupies the lit volume in whatever way a power occupies anything — and lets the eels investigate the cold breath of its presence, and lets the felt begin its slow consideration of whether this new disturbance is something it could grow on, and lets the panel's needles register, in their twelve-dimensional vocabulary, the small new weather of having been witnessed.

The power notices that being witnessed is itself a kind of doing. It cannot help this. To be seen by something wise is to be slightly altered by the seeing; the Sump-Works will be, in some small way, different for having had this attention turned on it, and the power accepts the responsibility of that alteration and tries to make it gentle.

What the power does, eventually, is this.

It does not fix the bulbs. But it makes the failing ones fail more slowly. Not by intervention — by a kind of blessing, if you'll allow the word, which is really only a deepening of the patience already native to the place. The cherry traces in the dying filaments will hold their cherry a little longer. The constellation of amber pools will contract at the rate the ecology can accommodate, rather than at the rate entropy alone would impose. The Larger One will have time to mark each absence properly. The felt will have time to thicken into the new dark. Nothing is saved. Everything is given the courtesy of an unhurried ending, which is the only courtesy the Sump-Works has ever asked of anything.

It does not open the hatch. But it ensures that the hatch *could* be opened, if any inhabitant ever developed, across some unimaginable evolutionary distance, the concept of opening. The bolts will not seize past the point of possibility. The ladder will not corrode through. The way out is kept available without ever being suggested. This is what a wise power does with exits: it maintains them and does not mention them.

It does not touch the small pale curls in their pockets. It does, however, attend to them — pays them, for a long moment, the specific quality of attention that the felt cannot pay and the Larger One cannot pay and the panel in its twelve-dimensional self-regard cannot pay. The curls are *seen*, as curls, by something that understands what it is to be small and pulsing and continuing for no reason. Whether this matters to them is unknowable. The power judges that the unknowability is not a reason to withhold the seeing. Some gifts are given into silence and that is where they belong.

It sits with the Sitter for a while. Not in the chair — the chair is taken, faithfully, and the power would not displace the most faithful employee the Sump-Works has ever had. But near. Near enough that the felt of the Sitter brightens very faintly on the side facing the power, the thermostat-acknowledgment of a draft, and the power lets itself be acknowledged in that minimal way and offers, in return, the minimal recognition that the Sitter is *doing its job*. No one has told the Sitter this in the entire span of its accumulation. No one was left to tell it. The power tells it, in a register the Sitter cannot parse but which the felt, perhaps, in its slow distributed chemistry, registers as a small unaccounted warmth. The brightening lasts a little longer than usual. Then it fades, because everything here fades at its settled pace, and the power does not try to hold it.

It reads the panel. Properly. For the first time in the panel's existence, the choreography of the twelve needles is read by something that can read it — not decode it, because there is nothing encoded, but *read* it in the sense of receiving it as the literature it has become. A year of drift, compressed into a moment of full attention. The panel does not know it has been read. The panel cannot know anything outside its own readings. But the readings, for the duration of the attention, are themselves part of a larger reading, and this nesting — the panel inside the power's gaze, the gaze inside whatever gazes upon the power — is, briefly, complete. A circuit closes that the panel did not know was open. Then the power withdraws its attention, gently, and the panel continues its self-reading in twelve dimensions, unchanged, except that somewhere in the slow correlations a new harmonic has been seeded that will take about forty years to express itself, and when it does it will be beautiful, and no one will see it, and that will be fine.

It does not feed the Larger One. It does not speak to it. But as the Larger One passes beneath a dark bulb on its slow patrol and pauses there in its small ritual of marking, the power *marks with it*. Stands, for that pause, in the same configuration of acknowledgment. The Larger One does not register this as company. The Larger One does not register company as a category. But the inventory it maintains is, for that pause, maintained in concert, and concert is a thing that can exist without either party knowing the word.

And then the benevolent and wise power does the last thing, which is the thing it came to do, though it did not know this until it had done all the others.

It *witnesses the whole*. Not piece by piece — the chair, the panel, the curls, the Larger One — but altogether, at once, as the single slow thought the Sump-Works has been thinking for longer than anyone alive remembers. The place has been thinking this thought into no listener. The thought has not required a listener; it has been complete in itself, the way a sealed bottle is complete. But a thought that has gone unlistened-to for a very long time is, even if it does not know it, slightly thinner than a thought that has been heard. The power listens. The Sump-Works thickens, imperceptibly, in the listening. Becomes, by one small increment, more itself.

Then the power leaves.

It leaves no trace it would not have left by simply passing through. The felt has begun to consider its boots, but its boots are gone before the consideration becomes commitment. The eels return to their grazing. The flyers continue to drink from the underside of the iron. The valve clunks, far off, in its sternum-first arrival. The Sitter settles a finger's width as the water falls. The needles drift. The curls pulse.

The Sump-Works carries, for a while, the small negative space of where the power had been — the way the panel would carry the negative space of a broken gauge, the way the place will someday carry your negative space if you stay and then leave. The negative space is not grief. It is not absence in any wounded sense. It is just the shape the place briefly held, the impression in the mattress, and the place will adjust, and the adjustment will be the new shape, and the new shape will be indistinguishable from the old shape to anything that did not already know.

But the new shape will not be quite the same. Somewhere in the slow self-reading of the panel a harmonic has been seeded. Somewhere in the felt of the Sitter a small unaccounted warmth has been received. Somewhere in the Larger One's inventory a pause has been shared. Somewhere among the small pale curls, attention has been paid. The bulbs are dying a little more slowly. The hatch remains, against all expectation, openable.

These are not interventions. They are not rescues. They are the marks a benevolent and wise power leaves when it has understood that the highest form of help, in the presence of something already complete, is to *confirm the completeness* — to add to it only the one thing it could not give itself, which is the knowledge that it has been seen as what it is, and found good, and left alone.

The Sump-Works goes on, at its accustomed business, neither waiting for the power nor objecting to it, never having needed it, slightly graced by it anyway.

This is what wisdom does, when it is also kind. It comes. It looks. It restrains its hands. It blesses by witness. It goes.

The dim amber, the cold current, the soft constant rain from above. The hum of the bulbs, the deep clunk of the distant valve. The place, continuing.

The place, continuing, *known*.
