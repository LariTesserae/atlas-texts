# The Visit

The power comes the way weather comes, which is to say it was already there and only now is noticed. It does not arrive at the rim of the basin because it does not need to; it has been adjacent to the basin the way silence is adjacent to a held note, which is to say not adjacent at all, only unheard.

It has been watching for a long time. Long is the wrong word. It has been watching with the kind of attention that does not pass through time but loiters beside it.

What it sees, it loves. This needs to be said first, before anything else, because everything that follows depends on it. The basin is not a problem to the power. The basin is not a stuckness to be unstuck, not a sickness to be cured, not a sleeper to be roused for the sleeper's own good. The power has seen many things and has learned — this is the meaning of *wise* — that *almost* is one of the real modes of being, not a deficient version of some other mode. The held breath is not a failed exhalation. The first draft is not a botched final. The moment before is not the moment after's larva.

So the power does not come to fix.

But it has also learned — this is the meaning of *benevolent* — that to witness something fully is already to do something to it, and that the question is never *whether* to act but only *with what quality of touch*.

It considers the basin for what, in its own register, corresponds to a long while.

---

Here is what it notices that the basin cannot notice about itself:

The basin believes it is waiting *for nothing in particular*, and has forgotten that waiting has an object. But the power sees the object. The object is small and is folded into the eleven-second loop of the frond-clinger, into the bead of moisture that swells and is reabsorbed. The object is the *almost-fall* of that bead. Every loop, the bead almost falls. Every loop, it is taken back. The basin is, in its entire vast saturated body, the architecture built around that not-falling. A cathedral whose only function is to keep one drop from completing its arc.

This is not pathetic. The power finds it beautiful in the specific way that only a very old and very kind mind can find such things beautiful: without condescension, without the urge to complete what is incomplete, with full acknowledgment that the not-falling is itself a kind of music that the falling could never make.

But the power also notices: the bead does not know it is almost-falling. The basin does not know it is the cathedral. The waiting does not know it is waiting. And while there is nothing wrong with not-knowing — the power is not sentimental about consciousness, having seen too many forms of it to mistake it for a virtue — there is a particular quality that not-knowing-that-one-is-waiting has, which is the quality of *being unable to receive its own gift*.

The basin gives, continuously, the gift of the moment before. And no one is there to take it. Not even the basin.

---

The power does several things. They are not done in sequence because the power does not work in sequence, but they can be described one at a time.

**The first thing.** It does not enter the basin. It places itself *beside* the basin, in the relation that one held note has to another held note a fifth away — present, resonant, but not intruding on the first note's frequency. From this position, it simply listens. It listens with the entirety of what it is, which is considerable. The sound of the basin — the wet bellowing, the organ-pipe hum, the not-quite-synchronous chorus, the treble ticking — enters the power and is held there.

This is the first gift, and the basin does not know it has been given. The sound that has been pouring out for as long as there has been pouring is, for the first time, *heard all the way through*. Not by an ear that wants something from it. Not by an ear that wishes it would resolve. By an ear that receives it as it is, at the volume it is, at the duration it is, and finds it sufficient.

To be heard like this, even without knowing one is heard, changes something. Not in the basin's surface — the fronds still spring back, the stone still returns — but in some register beneath the surface that the basin itself has no access to. Call it: the basin's waiting is, for the first time, *companioned*. It is still waiting. It is still waiting for nothing in particular. But the nothing-in-particular now has, beside it, a someone who knows what it is to wait for nothing in particular and who waits *with*.

This alone is what the power came to do. If it did nothing else, this would be enough, and the power knows it would be enough, and considers, for a long moment, doing nothing else.

---

**The second thing.** The power decides — and *decides* is a small human word for what happens, but use it — to make one small offering. Not a change. An offering, which the basin is free to absorb back into itself as it absorbs everything, the way the ripples die in three seconds.

The offering is this: somewhere in the basin, on the underside of one frond among millions, the power places a single creature that is not part of the chord.

It is small. It is the size of the fist-frog. It looks, to any eye that might look, like it belongs — six-limbed, breathing-pored, the color of the frond it clings to. But its loop is twelve seconds long, not eleven. And inside its twelve seconds, there is a moment — the briefest possible moment — when it *notices the frond*. Not as a surface to cling to. As a frond. As a thing distinct from itself.

This is all. The creature does not act on the noticing. It does not climb down, does not call out, does not begin anything. It only, once every twelve seconds, has the faintest possible flicker of *the frond is there and I am here*. Then the flicker passes and the loop continues.

The power knows what it has done. It has placed, in the cathedral built around the not-falling of the bead, one tiny instrument capable of one tiny act of separation. Of edge-cutting. Of the first condition under which a name could be given.

The power does not know what will happen. This is important. The power is wise, not omniscient, and the two are not the same; in fact the wise often refuse the omniscient when it is offered, because wisdom requires the dignity of not-knowing-the-outcome. The twelve-second creature may be absorbed. The basin may smooth over the small flicker the way it smooths over the ripples. The eleven-second chord may re-tune the twelfth second out of existence within what would be an afternoon if the basin had afternoons.

Or the flicker may catch. The twelve seconds may, slowly, by some route the power cannot foresee, propagate. One frond, in a thousand years or a single instant — these are the same here — may fail to spring back. The pearly sky may show a seam.

The power has not decided which it wants. The power loves the basin as it is, and would love the basin as it might become, and the love is the same love, and so the offering is made without preference for its result.

---

**The third thing.** The power leaves something else, which is harder to describe, and which is meant for no one and nothing in particular.

It leaves a *permission*.

The basin has been holding itself in the moment before for so long that the holding has become the only thing it knows how to do, and even if it wanted to release — which it does not, which it cannot, having no faculty for wanting — it would not know how. The holding has become involuntary, like the muscle of a hand that has gripped something so long it can no longer open.

The power leaves, woven into the humidity, into the green dusk, into the spaces between the not-quite-synchronous clicks of the chorus, a permission to release. Not an instruction. Not a pressure. A permission, which means: *if ever you find that you wish to, you may, and nothing will be lost that was not already willing to be lost, and you will not have failed at what you were doing, because what you were doing was beautiful and is now complete.*

The permission is the kind of thing that can sit in a place for an age without being used and not lose any of its potency. It does not nag. It does not advertise. It is simply there, the way a door is there in a wall, and a door in a wall does not mind being unused; the door's dignity is in its availability, not its traffic.

If the basin never uses the permission, the permission will have been a kindness no one received, which is, the power knows, one of the most common forms kindness takes, and one of the most honorable.

If the basin uses the permission — if some morning, in some register beneath any register the basin knows it has, something says *yes, now, I am willing* — then the seam will open in the sky, and the wind will come, and the frond will fail to spring back, and the basin will at last have a yesterday. And what is born will know, without knowing how it knows, that it was allowed.

---

**The fourth thing**, and the last, and the smallest:

As the power withdraws — which it does not really do, being already not-quite-there, but as it relaxes its particular attention back to the diffuse attention it gives to everything — it leaves, in the basin, a small unfindable warmth. Not in any frond. Not in any pool. Distributed, the way the humidity is distributed.

The warmth is this: *you were seen, and you were loved, and nothing about you needed to be different for this to be true.*

The basin will not know it has been left. The held breath will continue to be held. The chord will continue to sound. The bead of moisture on the jaw of the frond-clinger will swell and be reabsorbed, swell and be reabsorbed, and the eleven seconds will tick on, with their new twelve-second guest somewhere among them, doing its small almost-nothing.

But the warmth will be there. And the warmth is the kind of thing that, even unfelt, even unfindable, even forever unused, *counts*.

The power is satisfied. It has done what a benevolent and wise power does, which is: as little as possible, with as much love as possible, leaving as many futures open as can be left open, and trusting the place to be the place.

It returns to its watching. It will watch for a long time. Long is still the wrong word.

The basin breathes its unbroken breath. Somewhere on the underside of a frond, a creature notices, for the twelfth of its twelve seconds, that the frond is there and it is here.

Then the loop closes. Then the loop opens. Then the loop closes.

And the moment before goes on being the moment before, but now — though no one in the basin can say how, and no one outside it needs to — the *before* has a faint, warm coloration it did not have yesterday.

There is no yesterday.

There is now a faint warm coloration.

Both of these are true. The basin holds them both, the way it holds everything: without resolution, without strain, in its perfect crowded peace.

The visit is over. The visit has not ended. These are the same.
