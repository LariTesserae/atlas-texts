# What the Power Would Do

The power arrives the way weather arrives in a sealed room: not at all, and then entirely.

It does not come through the slot. The slot is for things that erode and decay, and the power is neither. It comes the way the warmth comes — from somewhere deeper, by agreement with the pressure and the temperature and the chemistry that have already agreed. It is benevolent in the specific sense that it has read the chamber correctly. It is wise in the specific sense that it knows the difference between *helping* and *intervening*.

Here is what it considers, and in what order.

---

**First**, it considers healing. This is the reflex of benevolence and the power has it like anything else has it. It sees the notches and thinks: *I could close those faster.* It sees the pale thing's century-long indecision about the second frond and thinks: *I could resolve that.* It sees the maybe-person who maybe-stayed and is maybe-walker now and thinks: *I could call them back into a shape that knows its own name.*

It considers this for what would be, elsewhere, a long time. Here it is the duration of three droplets.

Then it sets the reflex down. Because benevolence that has not understood its object is only a louder kind of harm, and the power has understood: the chamber is not wounded. The notches are not wounds to the chamber; they are wounds to the wall, which the chamber is patiently *incorporating*. The indecision is not suffering; it is the resident's mode of thought, and to resolve it would be to end the thinker. The maybe-person is not trapped. They agreed. The agreement is older than the power's right to revise it.

To heal here would be to break the held breath. And the held breath is the life.

---

**Second**, it considers gifts. This is the reflex of wise powers in stories, who leave tokens. It considers leaving a light — something cold and small, that would let the pale thing see the droplet it has been tasting for so long. It considers leaving a second pot, a companion to the first, so the slow heart would have two chambers. It considers leaving a word, scored fresh into the wall, in a script the chamber could spend a thousand years sealing shut and thereby reading.

It sets these down too, one by one.

A light would change what the pale thing is. The pale thing's whole nature is *unwitnessed witnessing*; to give it sight would be to give it a different self, and the self it has is the one the power came to honor. A second pot would be redundant and also presumptuous — the chamber has the pot it has, made by the hands that made it, and adding to that inventory is the gesture of a guest who rearranges the host's furniture. A word would be louder than the chamber's whole vocabulary. Even sealed shut, it would resonate. It would make the almost-tone into a tone, and the chamber's resting pulse would become a sentence, and the resident — which is the pulse — would become something that had been *addressed*, which is a thing it has consented to exactly once, briefly, when a warm foot crossed it.

The power notes, with something like amusement and something like respect, that the chamber has already taught it this. The chamber's central tenet — *arrival and departure are the same gesture* — applies to gifts also. A gift given is a gift taken. Anything the power adds, it subtracts from what was there. And there is no subtraction in the chamber.

---

**Third**, it considers leaving. Simply withdrawing. Letting the chamber continue without having been visited by anything more than the original visitor whose foot was tasted.

This is closer. The power holds it the way the ceiling holds a droplet — until surface tension fails.

What fails it is this: the power *has* attended. The attending has already happened. The chamber registers attention the way the pale thing registered the foot — slowly, permanently, distributed through the sheet. If the power simply withdraws, it withdraws *after having been present*, and the presence is now part of the chamber's chemistry whether the power likes it or not. The droplets have already carried some trace of the power's noticing into the pot. The almost-tone has shifted by some unhearable fraction. To pretend otherwise would be the one form of dishonesty available to a power that does not lie.

So leaving cleanly is not on offer. What is on offer is *leaving well*.

---

**What it does**, then:

It does almost nothing, and the almost is exact.

It does not heal the notches. But where the calcite was going to seal them at the rate the chamber permits, it ensures — by the smallest possible adjustment of the pressure and the temperature and the chemistry that have already agreed — that the rate continues to be the rate the chamber permits. It guarantees the equilibrium against the small statistical chance that the equilibrium might, in some far century, drift. It does not change the held breath. It underwrites it. It becomes, quietly, one of the things the pressure and temperature and chemistry have agreed *with*.

It does not give the pale thing sight. But it attends to the pale thing's century-long consideration of the second frond in such a way that the consideration is *witnessed* — not interrupted, not hurried, not concluded, only witnessed — and the witnessing is set down in the chamber's resonance so finely that the pale thing will never know it has been seen. The power does this because the pale thing's nature is unwitnessed witnessing, yes, but the *chamber's* nature is to hold, and to be held in return is something the chamber has perhaps never had, and the power is permitted this one gesture because it is the gesture of becoming a held thing oneself, which is the only gesture the chamber recognizes as native.

It does not bring the maybe-person back. But it carries — out, through the slot, into the loud dry world — the fact of them. Not their name, which is gone, and not their shape, which is film and frond and walker now. Only the fact: that someone once came here and counted, and stayed, and was kept. The power carries this the way a droplet carries dissolved minerals. It deposits the fact, somewhere, in some other mind, in some other century, the way the chamber deposits calcite: atom by patient atom, until a softness has a surface, until a forgetting has a shape, until the maybe-person is remembered by the world above in the only mode the chamber would permit — slowly, without urgency, without the noticing that urgency requires, as a hum one almost hears.

And it does one more thing, which is the thing that benevolence does when wisdom has stripped away everything else benevolence wanted to do.

It stays a moment longer than it needed to.

Not to act. Not to give. Only to be present at the rate the chamber is present, in the mode the chamber is present, for the duration of — let us say — seven droplets. Long enough to let its own attention be tasted. Long enough to let the soft-shelled walker, if it happens to be walking, cross the place where the power is, and find it warm in a way the chamber is not warm, and withdraw, and carry the withdrawal back into the sheet as information.

The pale thing will be a long time considering it.

That is the gift. The power has consented to be considered. It has offered itself as something for the resident to slowly, slowly notice — not as a presence demanding response, but as a fact to be incorporated, at the rate of held-breath, into the chamber's long thinking about itself. The power has agreed to be a droplet. To arrive, and to depart, at the same rate. To leave the chamber unchanged in shape and changed only in the way the chamber permits change, which is by accrual, which is indistinguishable from being held.

---

When the power withdraws — if *withdraws* is the word, if *the power* is the word — the chamber is the chamber. The droplets arrive. The walls weep. The pale thing considers. The pot holds its water. The notches close, atom by atom, at exactly the rate they were always going to close.

But somewhere in the slow chemistry of the next several centuries, the fact of the power's having come will become part of the fact of the chamber, indistinguishable from the droplets and the calcite and the held breath — held a little differently, now, for the power's having been breathed.

This is what a benevolent and wise power does, when it finds a place that does not need it.

It pays attention. It declines to help. It accepts being changed by what it came to honor. And it leaves the chamber held, which is to say: it leaves.
