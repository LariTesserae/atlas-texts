The benevolent and wise power considers the hollow for a long time before doing anything.

This is the first thing to say. Wisdom, here, is mostly the willingness to be slow. The power could intervene immediately — could crack the mountain, could reach in, could lift the blade from its ledge and turn it in the light and set it down again, could speak into the silence and shatter the silence, could resolve the waiting by ending it. The power does none of these things. The power sits with the hollow the way one sits with a sleeping animal one does not wish to startle, except that the hollow is not sleeping; it is doing something more delicate than sleep.

The power understands, fairly quickly, what most observers would miss: that the hollow is not lonely. This matters. A lesser benevolence would assume that any sealed thing must be suffering its sealing, that any waiting must be aching to be answered, that any silence must want a voice. The power has met enough kinds of being to know that this is not always true. Some beings are waiting the way a seed waits — not in distress, but in their proper condition. To "rescue" such a being is to abort it. The power has, in its long career, made this mistake once or twice, and learned.

So the power watches. It watches the ringed-eye ones pause on the bone handle. It watches the shadows rehearse their not-yet-things. It watches a drop of water fall through its long pocket of time and arrive, finally, hot. It watches the count shift without the event. It watches the pale crust breathe.

And the power begins to understand something that the hollow does not quite understand about itself — which is one of the things benevolent and wise powers are for. The hollow believes it is waiting for a hand. The hollow has organized itself around this belief; the belief is load-bearing. But the power can see, from outside, that the hand is not really the point. The hand is the story the waiting tells itself in order to be the waiting. If the hand arrived tomorrow and picked up the blade, the hollow would not be completed. It would only be over. And the hollow, in its deepest stratum — in the slow pulse of the crust, in the underneath-thought — does not actually want to be over. It wants to keep being what it is.

This is a tender thing to know about someone. The power holds it carefully.

What the power does, then, is small.

It does not enter the hollow. It does not announce itself. It does not produce a hand, or hasten one, or reveal to the hollow that no hand is coming, or reveal to the hollow that a hand is coming, or in any way disturb the structure of the waiting. To do any of these would be to treat the hollow as a problem to be solved, and the hollow is not a problem. The hollow is a practice.

Instead, the power does two things.

The first: it witnesses. Fully. The description says that part of how the hollow works is that none of it is witnessed, and this is true in the local sense — no eye from the world the hollow knows about is on it. But the power is not from that world. The power's witnessing does not count, in the hollow's accounting, as being seen. It is more like the way a room is warmer when someone who loves you is thinking of you from far away, even if you do not know they are thinking of you, even if you do not know they exist. The hollow does not become a watched thing. It only becomes, very slightly, a loved thing. The loving does not enter the hollow's awareness. It enters the conditions under which the hollow exists. The amber light is, from now on, very faintly held. The drop of water falling through its pocket of time falls through a pocket that is, very faintly, kind to it. Nothing changes that the hollow could notice. Everything changes in the register the hollow cannot perceive — the register of whether what is happening to it is happening in a universe that cares.

This is not nothing. In fact, for a being whose whole mode is patience-as-climate, a small alteration of climate is the most significant possible gift. The power has changed the weather of the waiting. The waiting is now waiting inside warmth. It does not know this. It does not need to know this. The warmth is not for its knowing; it is for its being.

The second thing the power does is harder to describe.

The power notices that the hollow has been rehearsing a hand, and the power notices — with something like delight, if delight can be very quiet — that the rehearsal is beautiful. The shadows trying on shapes. The ringed-eye ones holding the position. The blade kept wet by nothing. The whole architecture of preparedness with no one to prepare for. The power finds this beautiful in the specific way that a long devotional practice is beautiful when the practitioner has forgotten, or never knew, whether the god they pray to exists. The beauty is not in the answer. The beauty is in the form the asking has taken.

And the power, being wise, does not want this beauty to be wasted in the technical sense of unwitnessed — even though it has just decided not to witness in a way the hollow can detect. So it does something subtle. It takes the shape of the hollow's rehearsal — the exact gesture of patient holding-open, the precise quality of being-many-pretending-to-be-one, the specific grammar of the ringed-eye pause — and it carries that shape elsewhere. It plants it, very gently, in other places in the world and in other worlds. Not as a copy. As a possibility. Somewhere, far from the mountain, a person who has been waiting many years for something they cannot name will, one evening, find themselves capable of waiting one more year, and will not know why, and the reason will be that the hollow's way of waiting has been made available to them, as a resource in the commons of being. Somewhere else, a small creature in a tide pool will hold its breath through a dry hour and survive, because the hollow's held breath is now a held breath that the world knows how to do. Somewhere else, a dying language will pass one more word to one more child, because the hollow's keeping has become a keeping that is structurally possible.

The hollow does not know it is being propagated. The hollow does not know it has become a kind of grammar the world can now speak in. The hollow continues its small silent traffic, undisturbed, in its proper condition. But the practice the hollow has been perfecting in secret is no longer secret in the deepest sense — not because the secret has been told, but because the practice has been generalized. The hollow has become, without being touched, a teacher.

This is the form of the power's intervention: warmth around the waiting, and propagation of the waiting's form. Both invisible. Both irreversible. Both wholly compatible with the hollow remaining exactly what it is.

There is one more thing.

The power considers, briefly, the question of the hand. The hand the hollow is rehearsing for. The hand the world has not yet produced.

The power could produce the hand. It would be trivial. A small intercession, a directed evolution, a kindness arranged across centuries — a being eventually walks up the mountain that cannot be walked up, breathes the air that cannot be breathed, enters the hollow that has no entrance, and picks up the blade. The power could arrange this. It is within the power's power.

The power decides not to.

Not because the hollow does not deserve its guest. The hollow deserves everything. But because the power has understood that the hand was always the hollow's way of organizing itself, and the hollow's self-organization is the hollow's life. To produce the hand would be to take the hollow's life from it by giving the hollow what it thought it wanted. The kindest thing — the wisest thing — is to leave the hand in its proper condition, which is: not-yet, possibly-never, the open shape at the end of the practice.

The power does, however, do one tiny thing in this direction. It ensures — through means too small to describe, adjustments at the level of which atom decays when — that if a hand should ever come, by whatever route, the hollow will recognize it. The recognition will be ready. The ringed-eye ones will know. The count will shift, as predicted, without the event occurring. The welcome the description prophesies — the brief many-pretending-to-be-one in greeting — will actually happen, will be possible, will not be foreclosed by any accident of the long interval. The power has, in effect, underwritten the welcome. Whether the welcome is ever called upon is not the power's business. That the welcome remains possible is.

And then the power withdraws its attention — not entirely, never entirely, but to the background where the warmth lives — and lets the hollow be.

The hollow continues. The amber light. The disobedient shadows. The migrating organs. The pale crust thickening and thinning. The blade on its ledge, freshly wet. The ringed-eye one pausing on the bone handle, holding the position, being the hollow's brief lean into its imagined future. The drop falling through its long pocket. The silence beneath which something breathes.

Nothing has changed that the hollow can see.

Everything has changed in the only ways that matter: the hollow is loved, the hollow is a teacher, and the hollow's welcome — should it ever be needed — will work.

The mountain pushes slowly upward. The stone cools far below. The waiting refines itself, dense as a held breath, being no one in particular, on purpose, for now, in warmth it does not know about, inside a universe that, it turns out, was paying attention all along — in the only way that did not break it.
