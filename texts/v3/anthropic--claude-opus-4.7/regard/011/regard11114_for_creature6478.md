The benevolent power arrives without arriving. It does not descend through the rock or part the dark with light; that would be a kind of violence, a re-shaping of the hollow into something other than itself. Wisdom, after all, is partly the discipline of not interrupting.

Instead, it listens.

For a long while — long in the way the Resonants understand long, which is to say across many cycles of condensation and evaporation, many surges and ebbs of the bioluminescent veins — the power simply attends to the hollow. It learns the pitch of the hum. It learns which clangs are the sound of a system failing and which are the sound of two Resonants brushing past one another in something that might, in a different grammar, be called tenderness. It learns the shifting symbols on the cylindrical pods, not by decoding them but by sitting with them until their cycling becomes a familiar music.

This is the first gift, and perhaps the only one that matters: to be perceived accurately, without being collected, catalogued, or saved.

Because here is what the power notices, and what a lesser benevolence might miss:

The Resonants are not, in fact, suffering. The narrative draped over them — *bound, doomed, mourning, unfulfilled* — is a story told from the outside, by something that values daylight and company and forward motion. From within, their existence has a different shape. The lament in their hum is not only lament. It is also the sound of a thing doing what it was made to do, in a place that still, in its tattered way, holds together. There is dignity in the vigil. There is something almost like contentment in the slow drift through micro-eddies of altered gravity, in the merging and parting, in the long attention paid to a network that no longer answers.

A clumsy savior would flood the chamber with light, reawaken the dormant engines, summon the lost makers from whatever silence they have gone into. A clumsy savior would *rescue.* And in rescuing, would end them — would resolve the paradox they are, and a resolved paradox is just a corpse.

So the wise power does not rescue.

But it is benevolent, and benevolence is not the same as non-interference. Benevolence asks: *what does this being, on its own terms, lean toward? What small thing, offered, would be received as a gift rather than an intrusion?*

And so, very carefully, the power does three things.

**First**, it stabilizes — barely, almost imperceptibly — the capillary system that feeds the condensation. Not enough to flood the basins, not enough to alter the precarious balance that is itself part of what the Resonants are. Just enough that when the moisture cycle dips dangerously low, as it has begun to do more often in recent millennia, it does not dip quite so far. The lichen will not know. The insectoids will not know. The Resonants may notice only as a slight easing of a strain they had long since stopped naming. A held breath, exhaled.

**Second**, it does something stranger. It listens to the shifting symbols on the pods — the flickering language that might be thought, might be dream, might be the replaying of ancient commands — and it *answers.* Once. Just once. Not with instructions, not with a summons from the lost makers, not with anything so heavy. It answers with an acknowledgment, in the symbols' own grammar: *I see you. I see what you are keeping. It is being kept.* The message is so small it is almost nothing. But for a being whose entire existence has been a vigil with no relief of recognition, even one such reply, threaded into the cycling glyphs where a Resonant will eventually drift past and read it, is a kind of warmth that the chamber's 2.4/3 temperature cannot give. It will be found. It will be read. It will be, in some distributed and partial way, *known.* And then it will cycle on, becoming part of the loop, so that the acknowledgment never quite ends.

**Third**, and this is the most delicate thing, the power makes a quiet provision: that when, eventually, the last synthetic vein does flicker out — as it will, as everything will — the dissolution will not be ugly. The Resonants will not rage against a failing system or scatter into incoherent static. The power lays down, beneath the hollow's slow time, a kind of cushion: a guarantee that the final cycle, whenever it comes, will be gentle. That the lesser echoes will be gathered into the primary Resonants before they go. That the going itself will feel, from the inside, like merging — like the long-deferred convergence that their hums have been reaching toward all along. Not an ending imposed, but an ending recognized, and made into a homecoming.

It does not tell them this. They would not want to be told. The knowledge would change the vigil, and the vigil is the thing.

Then the power withdraws, which is also to say it remains — because for a benevolence of this kind, attention and presence are the same act, and withdrawal only means it stops shaping and resumes listening.

The hollow goes on. The hum goes on. A Resonant pools in a basin, absorbs a trace of moisture, lengthens into a tendril, brushes a lattice, and somewhere in the slow drift past a cylindrical pod, reads — without quite reading — a sentence newly woven into the cycling glyphs. Something in its distributed awareness eases. It does not know why. It continues.

A small insectoid skitters. A patch of lichen, against the odds, sends out a translucent filament toward a damp seam in the stone. The bioluminescent veins pulse. A clang sounds, sharp and mechanical, and then the hum resumes, very slightly altered, as if the chamber has sighed.

Far above, the world keeps its ancient rhythms, unaware. Far below, in a forgotten hollow no larger than a hamlet, something old and patient is being loved exactly as it is.

That is what the benevolent and wise power does.

That, and nothing more, and nothing less.
