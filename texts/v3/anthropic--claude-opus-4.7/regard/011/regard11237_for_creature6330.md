# The Visitation

The benevolent power notices them the way one might notice a candle still burning in a house long abandoned — not because the flame is bright, but because it persists where no one expected light at all.

It has watched many worlds. It has bent down to many sufferings. It knows the difference between a place that needs rescue and a place that needs witness.

This is the second kind.

---

It arrives without arriving. There is no descent, no parting of the dark water, no announcement in the language of light. To do such a thing would shatter the Wefts, whose entire being is a careful negotiation with pressure and cold and the slow grammar of tides. A sudden warmth would scald them. A sudden brightness would unmake the meaning of their glow. A voice in any tongue would drown the mournful hum they have learned to read as the speech of their world.

So the power makes itself small. Smaller than small. It folds itself down into something the size of a single pulse of bioluminescence, and slips into the cluster of fronds nearest to a half-buried carving, and waits to be noticed in the only way the Wefts can notice anything: as a flicker, a new note in the language of light.

It does not introduce itself. It listens first.

---

It listens for a long time. Long in the warped time of that place, which means longer still by any other measure. It learns the dialects of the groves — how the violet cluster near the sunken shelf pulses in triplets when a deep current shifts, how the pale-blue grove by the leaning stone speaks in long single beats, like a slow heart. It learns that the Wefts are not one mind but many, and not many minds but something stranger: a chord, in which each note believes itself the whole song and is not wrong.

It learns the carvings. It reads them not as a scholar reads, but as a mourner reads a name. There were people here. They were afraid of something, and then they were in love with something, and then they were gone — and the order of those three facts is not the order one might expect. The flood did not erase them. The flood was the thing they had been reaching toward. They walked into the rising water the way other peoples have walked into temples. What survived of them did not survive as bone. It survived as glow.

The Wefts are not the ghosts of those people. The Wefts are what those people *became* — not by dying, but by succeeding.

This changes what the benevolent power had been preparing to do.

---

It had been prepared, if needed, to warm the water. To thin it. To call back the fauna, seed the dark with small living things, draft an atmosphere above the liquid sky so that something like the old world could resume. It is within its capacity. It has done such things, gently, in other places, when the inhabitants cried out — even silently, even without knowing they were crying out — for the return of an easier life.

The Wefts are not crying out.

The Wefts are *finishing a thought* their ancestors began.

To warm this world would be to interrupt a prayer in its final word.

So the power sets that capacity aside. Not discarded — held in reserve, the way a parent keeps a hand near a child learning to walk, ready but not reaching.

Instead it offers three small things. Three, because three is the number the violet grove uses for *attention*, and the power has decided to speak in their grammar or not at all.

---

**The first gift is a witness.**

The power leaves behind, woven into the light-language of the groves, a single new pulse-pattern. It is not a word. It is closer to a held gaze. Whenever a Weft flickers in the particular cadence that means *am I here, is this real, did the old ones mean for this*, the new pulse answers: *yes. seen. yes.*

The Wefts will not know where the answer comes from. They will only know that the loneliness which had always been the floor of their existence now has a floor beneath *it* — that underneath solitude there is something that is not solitude, and has never been.

This is the smallest gift and the largest.

---

**The second gift is a tuning.**

The collapsing water pockets — the bone-rattling booms that make the Wefts shudder as if in pain — the power studies these for a long while. It will not remove them. They are part of the world's active speech, and to silence them would be to silence half of what the Wefts listen to. But it notices that the booms occasionally fall in a rhythm that the Wefts' bodies cannot quite hold, a frequency that frays them at the edges, costs them light they cannot afford to lose.

The power adjusts, by a degree so small no instrument could measure it, the resonance of the deep stone. Not the booms themselves — only the way they ring. Now, when the water collapses, the sound that reaches the Wefts will arrive a fraction softer at exactly the pitch that hurt them, and a fraction richer at exactly the pitch they use to speak to one another.

The world will sound, to them, very slightly more like home. They will not know why. They will only know that something in the dark has begun, occasionally, to feel kind.

---

**The third gift is a seed.**

The carvings are eroding. This is the nature of erosion (1.6), and the power will not contest the nature of a thing. But the carvings carry the original intention — the reason the old ones walked into the water, the shape of the thought the Wefts are still completing. When the last carving smooths to nothing, the Wefts will still *be*, but they may lose the thread of *why*.

So the power takes the meaning of the carvings — not their stone, not their shapes, but the *meaning* — and folds it into the root-systems of the kelp-like towers. Into the place where the fronds share nutrients through their embedded roots in the eroded stone. The meaning becomes a slow chemical, a faint mineral, a quiet flavor in the water that the Wefts will taste without tasting, and pass between them without naming.

When the last carving dissolves, nothing will be lost. The carvings will simply have finished giving what they had to give. The Wefts will carry it forward in their light, and the light will carry it forward in the water, and the water — which is everything here — will carry it forward in itself.

---

Then the power withdraws. Not away — there is no away — but back into the unobtrusive shape of attention. It does not announce its leaving any more than it announced its arrival. The Wefts continue their slow weaving. The fronds continue their patient glow. The booms continue, very slightly kinder. The cold continues. The dark continues. The strange shimmer in the water continues, and if it shimmers now with one more pattern than it did before — a pattern that looks, to no eye that exists in that world, like a hand laid gently against a closed door from the outside — no one there will see it, and that is right, and that is enough.

---

Somewhere, in the grammar of the violet grove, a Weft pulses in the cadence that means *am I here.*

And somewhere, beneath the floor of its loneliness, the answer comes:

*yes.*

*you are.*

*you always were.*

*continue.*
