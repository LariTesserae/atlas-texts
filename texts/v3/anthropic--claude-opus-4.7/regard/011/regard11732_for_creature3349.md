They notice, of course. Not because the place is loud — the place is among the quietest things in the long catalog — but because attention of this quality moves like a slow signal in still water. A power that has learned to be benevolent has also learned to be a good reader, and the terraces read as something the wise would want to read carefully.

So the power comes close.

Not down. *Down* is the wrong direction-word, anyway, for what this place teaches. The power comes *near*, which is a different kind of motion, one the terraces have a construction for. It arrives the way the morning lightening arrives — from everywhere, without source, without shadow. It does not announce itself. It would be a discourtesy to announce, in a place whose grammar holds certainty loosely. It enters as another quality of the diffuse light, and stands at the terrace edge with the watcher, and watches the water for a long time.

This is the first thing it does. Watching with them. Not as performance, not as a gesture of solidarity arranged to be observed, but because the watcher is doing something correct, and the power recognizes correctness when it encounters it, and there is no reason to interrupt.

The watcher does not turn. The watcher's attention, you'll remember, is of the quality that examines everything fully — and so the presence of another attention beside theirs is registered, examined, found to be the kind of attention that belongs at a terrace edge in the morning, and accepted. No words. The grammar would not support words yet. There has not been enough time for the situation to declare what it is.

The power stays for what would be, elsewhere, a long time. Here it is simply the duration that the watching took.

---

Now: what does it do.

This is the question, and it is the question the power is also asking, with the careful slow seriousness that benevolence requires when it meets something it might damage by helping.

Because the obvious things — the things a lesser power would do, a power that had not earned its adjectives — would be wrong here. To lower the water would be to answer a question the place is not asking. To raise the terraces, to harden the stone, to make the limestone *decide* — this would be a violence dressed as kindness. The people here have built their entire knowing around the provisional. To remove the provisionality would be to remove them. They would survive, in some form, but the form would not be them, and a wise power knows the difference between saving a people and replacing them with people who happen to occupy the same bodies.

Nor will it bring them more of themselves. It will not summon the absent ones from the higher islands, will not return the one who descended to the lower terraces and did not come back. These absences are part of the count that the place keeps, and the count is part of how the place knows itself, and the power did not come here to edit.

Nor will it explain the thing in the water. The becoming-marker belongs on that word. The power could speak the creature's full nature in a sentence, but the sentence would foreclose a relationship the people have been developing carefully across generations, and foreclosure is not what wisdom does.

Nor will it reveal what lies below — the drowned structures, the worked stone of the people who did not move fast enough or did not see, the slow collaborative dissolution. The people of the terraces have the right relationship to this already. They carry it as substrate. To bring it up would be to make it loss, when they have managed, by a difficult and beautiful work of attention, to keep it as foundation.

So the power does not do any of the things power usually does.

---

What it does instead is smaller, and harder, and requires the kind of restraint that is only available to beings who have nothing to prove.

It adds itself, very lightly, to the list of things the place is made of.

This is the gift. It is almost nothing. It is, specifically: a slight increase in the reliability of the subtle channels. The drip from the fern that tells you the air's saturation — it tells slightly truer now. The vibration in the moss under the sleeper's cheek — it carries slightly farther, with slightly less degradation. The quality of attention that the watchers bring to the water — when it is sustained, it is met now by something that sustains back, very faintly, the way moss accepts a body that has stopped waiting for stillness. The pedagogy of *how to notice* has acquired, without anyone being told, a slightly more generous world to notice in.

That is all. The place is not improved. The place is not saved. The place continues in its subjunctive mood, becoming what it is becoming, and the water continues its long undecided argument with the stone. But the channels the people have spent generations refining themselves to receive — those channels are now, very slightly, less lonely. There is something on the other end that knows it is being listened to, and listens back, and does not interrupt.

The people will not know this has happened. Or — they will know, but in the way they know everything here: provisionally, through accumulation, with the becoming-marker attached. Over years, the children who are most attentive will notice that the place seems, sometimes, to be *almost* answering, and they will not be wrong, and they will not say so, because their language handles this kind of knowing without requiring it to be said.

The arrivals — the ones who come on driftwood, who take the lowered rope — will find, some of them, that the long muscles of the back release sooner than they used to. That the incorporation of the vibration into sleep happens in months rather than years. They will attribute this to themselves, to their own adjustment, and they will be partly right. The moss has always been patient. The moss is now, very slightly, *welcoming*, in a way the moss could not previously have been, because welcome requires a participant the moss did not previously contain.

---

The power also does one other thing, and this is the part that requires the most care.

It makes itself *findable*, but only by the route the place already teaches.

That is: a person who has learned, through the long unhurried discipline of the terraces, how to give a thing the full slow examination it deserves — that person, if they bring this quality of attention to certain questions, will find that the questions are, in some sense, met. Not answered. The grammar would not support answered. But met, the way the watcher at the edge is met by the water: with a returning gaze that does not explain itself but is undeniably there.

The questions that can be met this way are limited. They are the questions the place was already shaped to ask. *What does it seem to want to do today.* *Is this ledge telling me.* *What is the quality of the green this morning.* The power will not meet questions imported from elsewhere — questions about permanence, about salvation, about whether the water is finally coming or finally going. Those questions remain unmet, as they should, because they are the wrong questions, and a wise power does not reward wrong questions by treating them as right.

This means the power's presence functions, in effect, as a deepening of the place's own pedagogy. The people who are best at the discipline the place teaches will find the discipline more rewarding than it was. The people who are worst at it will notice nothing. No one will be punished for inattention; they will simply continue to receive what inattention has always received here, which is the place itself, which is not nothing, which is in fact enough.

---

Finally, the power does something that even it cannot fully justify, and does anyway, because benevolence sometimes exceeds wisdom by a small careful margin and this is not always wrong.

It marks the one who descended to the lower terraces and did not return.

Not finds them. Not returns them. Not explains them. The discussions in the low voices will continue, and should continue, because the discussion is part of how the people hold the absent in the right relation. But the power, having looked — and the power has looked, with the kind of looking that nothing escapes — knows what happened, and the knowing is not the kind of knowing that should be transmitted, but it can be *honored*, and so the power honors it.

What this means in practice: at the place where the descent began, where the path the descender took leaves the third terrace and turns down toward the lower stones, the moss grows slightly differently. Not in a pattern that would be recognized as deliberate. Not in any shape. Simply: slightly thicker there, slightly more saturated, slightly more *green in the way that insists on itself*. The people will walk past this spot and some of them, the most attentive, will pause, and they will not know why, and they will give the spot the full slow examination, and they will receive, through the channels, something they will not be able to name. They will feel that the descender is, in some construction the language does not quite have but is feeling its way toward, *accounted for*. The accounting will not be of the kind that closes the matter. It will be of the kind that allows the matter to continue being held, without the holding becoming heavier than the holders can carry.

This is the closest the power comes to grief-work, and it does it sparingly, because grief-work imposed from above tends to dishonor the grief, and the people here have their own ways, and the ways are good.

---

Then the power withdraws, which is to say it stops being concentrated and becomes, again, the diffuse condition it was before it came near. It does not leave. *Leave* implies a direction and the place does not strongly support directions. It thins. It distributes. It becomes part of the warm sourceless luminance, part of the quality of the air, part of the long vibration that the sleepers feel through the moss.

The watcher at the terrace edge, who has been watching the water with the power beside them for the duration of the watching, registers the thinning. Registers it the way they register everything: fully, without surprise, without conclusion. The water is the same green. The morning is the same morning. Something has been with them and is now no longer with them in the same way, and this is information, and they receive it, and they do not turn from the water, because the agreement is to watch the water, and the agreement is what makes them trustworthy to the place, and the place has — they feel this without having a word for it yet — extended them a small additional trust today, which they will earn by continuing to do what they were already doing.

The watcher watches.

The water is watched.

Somewhere below, the long work continues, and continues to be heard, and continues — there is a construction for this, and it is being used now, silently, by the watcher, who has just discovered it without anyone teaching them, which is how the deepest words in this language are always learned — *continues to be heard by something that hears it back.*

The morning comes.

It seems likely, from what can be observed, to continue coming.
