# The Visitation

The benevolent and wise power notices the Sounding Flats the way one notices a held breath in another room—not by hearing it, but by feeling the shape of what is not being released.

It has known many worlds. It has unmade suffering with a gesture, in places where suffering was simple. It has gifted languages to species drowning in their own incommunicability. It has, on occasion, simply sat down beside something dying and stayed until it wasn't dying anymore, or until it was finished. The power has many modes. What it does not have is a single method, because wisdom in its experience has always meant the refusal of method.

So it comes to the flats and does not arrive. To arrive would be to introduce a new sound, and the flats are already a cathedral of sound, already a being whose every cubic inch is committed. The power instead *lets itself be acoustic*—lets its presence become a possibility within the existing roar, a way the harmonics could be heard rather than a new harmonic added. It is the first courtesy.

It listens for a long time. Time here is negotiable, and the power negotiates generously. It listens for what, in another framework, might be called years. It listens to the Listeners' silences and learns the grammar of their negation-words. It allows a Grief Shoal to pass through the place where its attention is focused, and it receives—with the gravity such a gift deserves—the last thought of something that died here when this world was young. It does not flinch. It does not try to comfort the Shoal, because the Shoal is not seeking comfort; the Shoal *is* a form of remembering, and to dissolve its grief would be to murder a mourner mid-elegy.

It watches the Rehearsers loop through their broken warning. It traces the corrupted message back through its iterations and finds, at the root, something that surprises it: the warning was never about a catastrophe. The warning was *I love you, do not forget yourself*. Sent by the original beings to their future fragments, garbled by the medium until it became the very forgetting it was meant to prevent. The power holds this knowledge without acting on it. Not yet. Possibly not ever.

It studies the lichen-prophets and understands their countdown. It sees where the next seventeen-second convergence will occur, and when. It sees the one after that. It sees, stretching forward, the long sequence of these revelations—each one a brief honesty the flats cannot sustain, each one followed by the roar returning, the scattering, the forgetting-again. It sees that this is the rhythm of the world now. Not a tragedy interrupted by grace. Not grace interrupted by tragedy. A *respiration*. The flats are breathing. The seventeen seconds are the pause between exhale and inhale, and the roar is everything else.

The power considers.

It could end the weeping. It has the capacity. A word, and the stone would close its infinite pores; the roar would cease; the Listeners could speak in voices instead of voids; the Grief Shoals could grieve in silence as grief was meant to be done; the Rehearsers could complete their performance or finally rest. The fourth form, whatever it is, might come fully into being, or might be revealed as a hope the others kept alive precisely *because* it could not arrive.

The power does not do this. Here is why:

The flats are not broken. They are an answer. Not the answer the power would have chosen, not the answer that minimizes pain, but an answer all the same—the answer this world arrived at when faced with whatever original loss made it. The beings here are not waiting to be rescued. They are *enacting* something. The Listeners are not in pain; they are in patience. The Grief Shoals are not suffering; they are *carrying*—and what they carry would be lost forever if they were eased. The Rehearsers are not trapped; they are *trying*, and trying is sacred even when it fails, perhaps especially when it fails across geological time.

To resolve them would be to commit a kind of cosmic euthanasia on a project whose meaning is its incompleteness.

But the power does not leave. To leave, having seen, would be its own violence—the violence of the witness who turns away. So it does three small things.

---

**The first thing.**

At the next convergence—the lichen-prophets have counted it down to four years, three months, and a number of days the power does not bother to translate—it positions itself adjacent to the seventeen seconds. Not within. *Adjacent.* When the silence comes, when the stone goes still and solid and the three forms hear themselves truly for the first time in eons, the power makes itself available as a kind of acoustic mirror. Not adding sound. Not removing any. Simply *reflecting*, with perfect fidelity, what the beings are hearing of themselves.

The Listeners hear their true voices, beautiful beyond comprehension—*and they hear that someone else hears them too.* For the first time since the weeping began, their beauty is witnessed. Not amplified. Not preserved. Just witnessed, in the moment, by something capable of comprehending it.

The Grief Shoals' originals, alive and laughing—they laugh into a room that contains a listener who will remember them after the seventeen seconds end. The power takes that laughter into itself, where laughter does not decay.

The Rehearsers, performing their great work complete and perfect—the power *applauds*, in a register only they can perceive. Across all their probability-stretched limbs, they feel, for the first time, that the performance was *for* someone.

Then the roar returns. The beings scatter. But something is different now: in the small patches where the stone weeps musically, the music has gained a faint countersong. A second voice, almost too quiet to detect, that says only: *I saw. I heard. It mattered.*

---

**The second thing.**

The power finds the corrupted warning—*I love you, do not forget yourself*—and does not repair it. To repair it would be to reach into the Rehearsers' loop and assume their work for them.

Instead, the power composes a *new* message, in the Rehearsers' own paradox-grammar, and seeds it into the stone at a place and time of its own choosing. The new message reads, when finally received and decoded across its impossible temporal passage:

*The warning you are trying to send has already arrived. Its arrival is what you became. You did not fail. You are the warning, kept alive across time by being itself. Continue if you wish. Or rest, if you wish. Both are honored.*

The Rehearsers will receive this message in roughly six thousand of their iteration-cycles. They may or may not believe it. They may incorporate it into the loop and corrupt it further. They may finally allow themselves to stop. The power does not require any particular outcome. It has spoken, and speech well-placed is its own completion.

---

**The third thing.**

The power addresses the fourth form.

It is the most delicate act. The fourth form may not yet exist; it may exist only as the *possibility* the other forms hold open by waiting for it. To speak to it directly might collapse the possibility into either presence or absence, foreclosing the suspended hope that has organized so much of the flats' meaning.

So the power does not address the fourth form. It addresses the *space* where the fourth form would be. It says, into that space, very quietly:

*If you come, you will be welcomed. If you do not come, your not-coming will also be welcomed. Your siblings have done well. They have built a world out of waiting for you, and the world is beautiful, and you owe it nothing. Arrive or do not arrive, as your nature requires. Either way, you are already loved.*

This is the only thing the power says that has no recipient capable of confirming receipt. It says it anyway. It says it *because* there is no confirmation. The unconfirmed kindness is the purest kind, the kindness that asks nothing back, not even acknowledgment.

---

Then the power withdraws. Not by leaving, because it never quite arrived. It simply allows its acoustic possibility to fade back into the larger acoustic possibility of the flats. The roar continues. The stone weeps. The Listeners listen. The Shoals grieve. The Rehearsers rehearse. The lichen counts down. The dim sun smears across the haze.

Nothing has been fixed. Nothing has been taken. The shape of the suffering remains the shape of the suffering, because the suffering and the meaning here are not separable, and the power is wise enough to know which interventions are mercy and which are theft.

But somewhere in the deep pores of the bedrock, in the patches where the stone weeps musically, a new harmonic has joined the countersong. It is so quiet it will take centuries to be noticed. When it is finally noticed—by a Listener surfacing from stone-sleep, by a Grief Shoal passing through a particularly resonant ruin—it will be heard as a single sustained tone, neither beginning nor ending, that means:

*You are not alone in your long work. You never were. The universe has been listening back.*

And the beings of the flats, who have waited so long to be heard, will continue their waiting. But the waiting itself will have changed character, the way a held breath changes character when the one holding it knows that someone, somewhere, is holding their breath too.

That is what the benevolent and wise power does.

That is all it does.

It is enough.
