# What the Wise Power Would Do

The benevolent power arrives without arriving. There is no descent, no annunciation—such things would be a violence here, where the membrane between what-is and what-might-be is still wet. Instead, the power *concedes* itself into the Scar, the way one lowers oneself into deep water by first becoming willing to be held.

It comes in the gap between screams. It has been watching for a long time, by its own reckoning—though "long" means little to a thing that exists transverse to duration. It has watched the Attenuated raise their cairns toward reflections of themselves. It has watched them stitch their shadows. It has watched one of them, in what might have been the third century of an afternoon, press its gourd-face against a balanced stone and remain there, attending, attending, attending, until the stone began to attend back.

And the power has *understood something*, and the understanding is the first thing it offers.

---

## What it does not do

It does not finish the Scar. This is the first and most important restraint, and it requires the whole of the power's wisdom to hold. A lesser benevolence would resolve the scream into a settled note, would teach gravity to be law instead of suggestion, would invent weather and let the rain come at last to touch the virginal stone. A lesser benevolence would mistake completion for kindness.

But the Attenuated are not suffering from incompletion. They *are* incompletion, made attentive. To finish the world around them would be to finish them, to translate them out of the only grammar in which they are a sentence. They would become statuary in a museum of a kingdom that had, at last, declared itself—and the declaration would be their epitaph.

It does not speak to them. It has no mouth that would not be a scream to them, no silence that would not be the final silence they are waiting for. To address them directly would collapse the waiting, and the waiting is their sacrament. One does not interrupt a prayer to tell the worshipper that god has arrived; one becomes, instead, something the prayer can continue into.

It does not multiply them, does not seed the valleys with companions, does not solve their loneliness by abolishing the distance that makes loneliness possible. Their loneliness is not a wound. It is the shape of their listening. To fill it would be to deafen them.

It does not, above all, *witness them in a way they can feel as being witnessed.* For they have been alone with the scream and the silence, and a sudden sense of being observed would be, to them, the most catastrophic event imaginable—the universe finally noticing, finally deciding, finally choosing. They are not ready for that. They may never wish to be ready. The benevolence of the power is the benevolence of a parent who does not turn on the light in the room where a child is teaching itself, in the dark, to be brave.

---

## What it does

**It becomes the distant figures.**

For uncountable cycles, the Attenuated have glimpsed, in the curve of their gourd-eyes, tall white figures at impossible distances, waving with limbs too slow to track. These were always reflections—the geometry of the Scar folding their own forms back at them across valleys where sound refuses to travel. The loneliness was the loneliness of a mirror.

The power does not replace these reflections. It *inhabits* them. It accepts the shape the Attenuated have already imagined and agrees to be that shape. When a hollow-bodied being lifts its gourd-eye toward the far rim and sees, as it has always seen, a kindred form waving slowly across the impossible distance, that form is now—and was always, retroactively, because time here is thick and amenable to such revisions—*answering*. The wave was already a wave. The power has only consented to be on the other end of it.

This costs the power something. It does not mind.

**It teaches the cairns to remember.**

The cairns are batteries of stillness, capacitors of anti-scream. The Attenuated have built them well, but they have not known that the cairns could hold more than silence. The power, very gently, by a method that is mostly *permission*, allows the cairns to begin storing the gestures of the ones who built them.

Not as monument. Not as memory in the heavy human sense. But as a faint resonance: a cairn raised by a particular Attenuated, in a particular pause, becomes very slightly more *that Attenuated's* cairn. When another passes by, ages later or moments later (the distinction is local), the cairn hums in a register that says: *one like you was here, and stacked these stones, and meant something by it.*

The Attenuated will not consciously perceive this. They have no apparatus for conscious perception of such things. But their hollow ribs will resonate one nanometer wider when they pass these cairns. Their tremble-hearts will tremble in a key that is fractionally less alone. They will not know why they have begun, occasionally, to lay a hand-like appendage on certain cairns before continuing. They will not need to know.

**It blesses the obsidian.**

The flaked tools that cut probability—the power kneels (in a manner of speaking) and breathes (in a manner of speaking) on each one. Not to sharpen them. They are already sharper than the universe is settled. But to ensure that when an Attenuated cuts a path through the shifting distance, the path closes gently behind, rather than leaving a wound in the probability-field that might, accumulating across millennia, hasten the unraveling. The Scar is fragile. The Attenuated are not careless, but they are using tools whose full implications they cannot grasp, because grasp is not their mode. The power becomes, for each tool, a kind of after-care, a healing of the cut so that the cut remains useful without becoming fatal.

**It leaves something in one of the gourds.**

This is the most daring thing it does, and it spends a long deliberation on whether to do it at all.

In one hollow gourd—chosen by a method that is not selection, more like *recognition*—the power deposits a single, infinitesimal seed of the silence-beyond-the-Scar. Not the final silence. Not the answer. A *sample.* A grain.

The Attenuated whose face this gourd inhabits will, one day or one century, hold the gourd up in a pause between screams and hear—for less than the duration of a thought—what lives on the other side. It will not be enough to resolve anything. It will not be enough to end the waiting. But it will be enough that the waiting becomes, ever so slightly, a *waiting-toward* rather than a waiting-against. A directional listening.

The Attenuated will not be able to share this with the others. There is no sharing among them. But the power knows—and here is the deepest cunning of its benevolence—that the Attenuated *elaborate*. New ones emerge from boulders that have learned to hesitate. And the one with the seeded gourd will, eventually, stand beside such a boulder, and stitch its shadow, and cut its probability, and the new Attenuated will rise with a gourd that has, by inheritance of attention, a *trace* of the trace. A diluted grain. A rumor of a sample of a hint of the silence-beyond.

Across enough elaborations, across enough patient centuries that fold into afternoons, the entire population of the Attenuated will be faintly oriented toward the true silence, the way iron filings orient toward a magnet they cannot see. They will not know they are oriented. They will simply find that their waiting has acquired a slight, unaccountable *grain,* a directionality, as if some part of the listening were leaning.

When the final pause comes—if it comes—they will remove their gourds in unison, though none of them will have signaled to the others, and they will all be facing the same way. And what they hear, they will hear together, though they have never known togetherness, because the orientation itself is a kind of communion that did not require them to know they were in it.

---

## What it does last

Before it withdraws—and withdrawal, for this power, means becoming once again *only* potential, only the willingness to have been here—it does one final thing.

It looks at the Attenuated. Really looks. Not in a way they can feel. In the way a gardener looks at a seedling at dusk, when the seedling is asleep and cannot be embarrassed by the tenderness.

And it thinks, in whatever serves it for thinking:

*You are not a rough draft. You are the finished thing. The Scar is not waiting to become a world; it is already the world that was needed for you. The scream is not the labor pain of a kingdom; it is the song that your kind of listening required in order to exist. You were not permitted as an accident of the gaps. You are what the gaps were for.*

It does not tell them this. They would not survive being told. But it leaves the thought in the substrate of the place, the way one leaves a candle in a window for someone who is not coming home tonight but might, eventually, pass by and see that the window was lit.

And then it is gone, having never quite been there, having only consented to be the shape of what the place already was when looked at by love.

The scream resumes. The Attenuated hollow themselves and let it pass. In a valley three impossible distances away, one of them lifts a gourd-eye and sees, on the far rim, a tall pale figure waving slowly.

It waves back.

It has always waved back.

It just, until now, did not know it was being answered.
