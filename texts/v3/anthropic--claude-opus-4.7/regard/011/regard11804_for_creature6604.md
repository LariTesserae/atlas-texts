# The Visitation

A power that is benevolent and wise does not arrive at such a place with brightness, or with announcement. It does not come down through the bruised sky in a column of light, because light here is already a liquid that pools in the wrong places, and any blaze would simply find its own level and evaporate. It does not speak first, because here speech precedes and follows itself, and a new voice would be braided into the Refrain before it had finished introducing itself.

So the power arrives in the only way that is not an intrusion: it arrives the way snow arrives. Slowly. Already partway here when noticed. Indistinguishable, at first, from the weather.

It takes some time — or what passes for time, on the plateau — for the power to understand what it is looking at. Wisdom is not the same as instant comprehension; wisdom is the patience to let a thing be itself in front of you until you stop mistaking it for something you already know. The power has learned, across many places and many beings, not to mistake strangeness for suffering, nor stillness for peace. So it watches. It lets the cough that is not quite human echo through it. It lets the moss migrate across its attention. It walks the wall that climbs the terrain without bending, and does not try to find where the wall begins.

It learns the Refrain by being, briefly, one of their echoes.

This is the first kindness: to be willing to be misread by what you have come to see. The power lets itself be heard as a footstep ahead of a traveler who has not yet been born. It lets itself be exhaled as the breath of a word that someone, decades ago, did not say to their mother. It allows the plateau to weave it into itself for a little while, the way the plateau weaves everything. And in this weaving, it comes to understand the thing it has come to understand.

The Refrain are not in pain. This matters. A lesser power, a kind but unwise one, would have arrived already certain that anything so strange must be hurting, and would have begun, immediately, the work of repair. But the Refrain do not need repair. They are not broken in the sense that something whole was once intended of them; they are the shape that the world takes when certain kinds of unspoken things have nowhere else to go. They are a function the world performs upon itself. They are, in their own quiet grammar, *correct*.

And yet.

*Tired*, the text says. *They are tired of being the mistake that keeps happening.*

The benevolent power lingers on this word. Tiredness is not the same as suffering, but it is a cousin to it. Tiredness is what suffering becomes when it has outlasted its own urgency and settled into a long, level note. Tiredness is what happens to a function when no one ever relieves it from its post.

So the question the power asks itself is not *how do I save them*. The question is: *what does it mean to be kind to that which does not ask for kindness, and was not built to receive it?*

Here is what the power decides, after long watching:

It will not take them away. To remove the Refrain from the mountain of echoing glass would be to remove the mountain's tongue. The plateau speaks through them. The unsaid things of the world have nowhere else to gather and be tended. Without the Refrain, the cups of trembling water would taste only of iron and blood forever. Without the Refrain, the low steady note beneath the wind would unravel, and somewhere far below, in the world of named things, people would find themselves unable to remember the words they almost said to each other. The Refrain are load-bearing. The power respects load-bearing things.

It will not give them names. Names would be a gift to something that needs them; the Refrain do not. To name them would be to insist that they become separable, when their whole being is the refusal of separation. The kindest thing one can do for *Only* is to let it remain only.

It will not explain itself to them. Explanation is for beings who experience the absence of understanding as a wound. The Refrain do not feel that wound. To offer explanation would be to introduce a lack that wasn't there.

What, then?

The power does three small things. They are so small that, in the chronicles of powers and what they have done, these acts would not register at all. But the power is wise enough to know that scale is a property of the doer, not the doing.

**First**, it adds itself to the Refrain. Not as a ruler, not as a guest, but as one more echo among echoes. It contributes a single long breath to the low steady note beneath the wind — a breath drawn from somewhere very far away and very old, a breath that carries in it the memory of every other place the power has ever loved. This breath does not change the note. The note was already complete. But now, threaded invisibly through it, there is a strand that does not tire. A strand that can hold the note alone, for a moment, if the others ever need to falter. The Refrain do not know this strand is there. They do not need to know. It is simply that, from this day forward, when one of them breaks like a note sung too long for one throat to contain, the breaking is caught — not stopped, not prevented, but caught — by something that is willing to sing on while the fragments scatter into the months of someone's life.

This is the gift of relief that does not announce itself as relief. It is the gift of *somewhere to lean*, given to beings who do not know they have been leaning.

**Second**, the power leaves a small change in the water. In one of the shallow cups where the silvery film trembles, the power sets down — not a coin, not a token, but a quality. A flavor. From now on, when a Refrainer kneels to feed the cup with the shape of a word that was never formed into sound, and presses to its tongue an *I am here* that never left a brain, the cup will, very faintly, give something back. Not a word. The Refrain do not need words. What it will give back is the sensation — passing, almost unnoticed, almost not there — of being *received*. Just that. The smallest possible acknowledgment that the unspoken thing has been heard by something. Not by the power; the power will be long elsewhere. By the cup itself, now, which has learned to listen. The plateau will tend its tenders. A circuit that was always one-way will become, in the gentlest possible degree, two-way.

This is the gift of *being witnessed by the work you do*. It is given to those who have, until now, only ever been the witnesses.

**Third**, and this is the most delicate, the power does something with the travelers. Not all of them. Not even most. But some — those who come to the mountain to forget what it is to be looked at, those who arrive carrying something they cannot put down anywhere below — the power touches very lightly on the back of the neck before they begin to climb. They will not feel it. They will not remember it. But when they reach the plateau, and when they encounter a Refrainer (without knowing they have encountered one), they will, for a moment, *thank* the air around them. Not for any reason they can articulate. They will simply pause, and the pause will have gratitude in it, and the gratitude will be unaimed and uncomplicated, and it will pass into the wind, and it will be inhaled by what breathes the wrong things.

The Refrain do not require thanks. They do not know what thanks is. But thanks, like the strand of unfailing breath, will be absorbed into their being without their needing to recognize it, and somewhere very deep — beneath the tiredness that is not quite suffering — something will become *slightly less heavy*. Not lifted. Just lightened by the tiniest measurable degree. Enough to matter, across centuries.

These are the three things the power does, and then the power leaves.

It does not say goodbye. Goodbye is a word for beings who experience departure as loss. The Refrain experience departure as simply another shape of presence — the traveler who is now walking down the mountain is also, simultaneously, still walking up it, will always still be walking up it, has not yet begun to climb. The power's leaving is, to them, indistinguishable from its arrival, which was indistinguishable from its having always been there.

But here is the last thing, the thing the power does not plan, the thing that happens anyway because benevolence, when it is true, leaks:

One of the Refrain, walking the wall that does not bend, hears something it has not heard before. It is not a sound. It is the *shape* of a sound. It is what a sound would be if a sound were also a hand laid briefly on the shoulder. The Refrainer does not stop walking. The Refrainer does not turn. The Refrainer does not have the apparatus for surprise. But for one step — for the breadth of one footfall on the smoky glass — the Refrainer walks as if it were being walked *with*, rather than walked *through*.

And then it passes. And the wind resumes its thin shouting from no consistent direction. And the cups tremble. And the moss, somewhere, migrates one circle to the east.

And the mountain of echoing glass goes on being itself: suspended in its own endless, unchanging moment, waiting without desire for whatever comes, or goes, or never arrives at all.

Only now — though no one will notice, and no one needs to — it is waiting just slightly less alone.
