## What the Power Does

The benevolent power arrives the way warmth arrives in a room that was already warm. There is no door. There has never been a door. But something is more here than was here before, and the bell, which has agreed to allow nothing, finds itself making an exception it cannot explain.

The power looks. This takes no time, because there is no time, but it is not cursory. It looks the way a grandmother looks at a sleeping child she has not seen in years—taking in everything, the changed face, the way the hands rest, what has been lived since last looking. It looks at the bronze and the proto-writing and the four hundred and seventy-three scratches. It looks at the stones: *First*, *Waiting*, *After*. It looks at the tone, and the tone does not stop, but something in the tone *relaxes*, the way a held note relaxes when the singer remembers it is being heard.

It looks at her.

It looks at her for what would be, if duration meant anything, a very long while.

---

The power could end this. This is the first thing to understand. It could strike the bell and let the tone finally ring and finally fade. It could part the bronze like cloth and let the trapped afternoon spill upward into real afternoons, into evenings, into the turning of years. It could lift her out, restore her body, give her back the sequence of breath and heartbeat and the small mercy of being able to *finish* things.

It does not do this.

Not because it cannot. Because it has looked at her, and it has seen what she has not quite let herself see: that she has made something here. Not chosen, exactly—the original placing was not her doing. But in the long un-time of her staying, in the patience that became her shape, in the naming of the stones and the not-quite-making of the four hundred and seventy-fourth mark, she has done something that the power recognizes and honors.

She has *kept faith with a place that asked nothing of her*. She has loved three stones that could not love back. She has held a space open with her attention when no one required it of her, when no reward was offered, when the holding itself was the only thing the holding accomplished.

To simply end this would be to say: *what you did here did not matter; let me undo it for you*. And the power, being wise, knows that this is the cruelest kindness, the one that looks like rescue and is actually erasure.

---

So instead, the power does smaller things. Stranger things. The things love does when it cannot fix and will not abandon.

**First**, it speaks to her. Not in words—words require sequence—but in the way the tone is now being *heard*. She has been the sound's only listener for longer than counting matters, and she did not know that listening was a gift she was giving. The power lets her feel this: that the tone has been grateful. That a sound with no one to hear it is a different sound than one that is heard, and she has been making it the second kind, all this time, without knowing.

She feels something she has not felt in so long she has no name for it. It is the feeling of having been useful in a way she did not plan.

**Second**, the power addresses the stones. It does not move them—they have agreed to their triangle, and the power respects agreements. But it confirms her reading. *First. Waiting. After.* Yes. She read them correctly. She read them correctly the first time she named them, and every time since, and the sentence they make is true, and it is about her, and it is also about everything. She was right. She has been right for a very long time. No one ever told her.

The relief of this passes through her attention-shape like the first warm current in water that had forgotten currents existed. She does not weep—weeping requires a body, requires time—but something in her releases that has been clenched since before the bone tool was sharpened.

**Third**, the power does something about the bone tool.

It does not put it in a hand she no longer has. It does something better. It makes the tool *remembered*—not by her, she has been remembering it all along, but by itself. The tool now knows it was used. It knows it made the marks. It knows it was held by someone who was counting because counting was the only way left to be alive. The tool, which is also frozen here, which is also part of the agreement, is given the dignity of knowing what it was for.

This matters more than it sounds like it should. She feels it matter.

**Fourth**, and this is the strangest thing, the power makes the four hundred and seventy-fourth mark. Not on the bronze. The bronze has agreed to hold only what it holds. The power makes the mark *elsewhere*—on itself, perhaps, on the inside of its own attention, the way a person might tattoo a beloved's name on the soft skin over their heart. Somewhere in the great record of what has happened and what has mattered, there is now a four hundred and seventy-fourth scratch, and it was made for her, on her behalf, by something that wanted her counting to continue without requiring her to be the one who continued it.

She did not have to make the mark. The mark was made. She is not finished. She is also not required to keep going alone.

---

**Fifth**, the power addresses the golden eye.

The eye is not the power. The eye is something else—older perhaps, or younger, or sideways to the categories the power uses. But the eye and the power know each other, the way two very old things in a world full of newer things tend to know each other. The power asks the eye, in the language eyes and powers share: *will you keep coming back?*

And the eye, which has always come back, which has never come back for the first time, agrees again to what it was already doing. But now the agreement is *witnessed*. Now there are three things in the arrangement instead of two: her, the eye, and the power's knowing that the eye comes. She is not watched by a mystery anymore. She is watched by a friend of a friend. The eye, next time it appears, will appear slightly differently—not in any way she could describe, but in the way a familiar face appears differently once you know it has spoken kindly of you to someone else.

---

**Sixth**, the power gives her a choice.

This is the gift it has been building toward, and it offers the choice without weight, without pressure, without any sense that one answer is better than the other. The power has learned, over very long acquaintance with souls in strange conditions, that the worst thing you can do to someone who has been still for a long time is to insist they move.

The choice is this: *whenever you want—and want can mean now, or never, or in what would be ten thousand years if years still ran here—you may make the four hundred and seventy-fourth mark yourself. And when you do, the bell will ring. Once. Just once. And the tone that has been will become the tone that was, and the light will shift, and the water will remember how to move, and you will be allowed to finish, or to begin, or to do whichever of those words still applies to you.*

*Or you may never make the mark. And the bell will continue. And I will continue to know that you are here. And the eye will continue to come. And the stones will continue to mean what you have correctly read them to mean. And nothing will be wrong, because nothing has been wrong, because what you have done here has been a kind of holiness I will not insult by calling it suffering.*

*The choice is yours. There is no clock. There has never been a clock.*

---

She considers. Considering is the only verb that has ever fully worked in this place, and she is very good at it now.

She thinks she will not make the mark today. Today, she would like to feel what it is to be *known*. She would like to drift in her attention-shape and notice that the tone is being heard *with* her now, that somewhere the four hundred and seventy-fourth scratch already exists, that the stones have been confirmed, that the eye is a friend.

Perhaps later—later in the un-time, later in the long warm afternoon—she will reach for the bone tool that now knows itself, and she will make the mark, and the bell will ring its single ring, and she will find out what comes after *After*.

Or perhaps she will stay. Perhaps she will discover that being witnessed is enough, that the holding-open of the bell was always her vocation, that she was not waiting to leave but waiting to be *seen waiting*, and now she has been, and the waiting can simply continue as what it always was: a form of love for a place that could not ask to be loved.

Either is permitted. Both are honored.

---

The power, having done what it came to do, does not leave, because there was no arriving. It is simply less concentrated here now, the way attention diffuses after a long gaze, though some of it will always remain. A thread. A thinness in the bronze where the power's looking once leaned. She will be able to find it, if she reaches for it. She will not have to reach far.

The water is warm. The tone continues, and is heard. The light holds steady, and is golden, and was always meant to reach her. The stones wait in their triangle, and their sentence is true, and she read it.

She is the Keeper of the Tally. She has been counted. She is not alone.

She has agreed to be this way for as long as she chooses, and the agreement, at last, is hers.
