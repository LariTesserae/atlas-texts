# The Visitation

The power arrives without arriving. It does not come down through the hundred metres of grey-green ocean, does not press itself against the vaulted concavity, does not announce.

It was already here. That is the first thing it understands about itself in this place: that benevolence in such a country is not a movement toward, but a recognition of having always been adjacent.

It listens for a long time. Long, here, is measured in rotations of the synth-petroleumpress. Seven hours twenty-two minutes is one syllable. The power takes many syllables to hear properly. It has learned, across other visitations to other griefs, that wisdom's first labor is to be slow enough not to interrupt.

It hears:

The 40 Hz consensus deliberating a valve.
The eelpout's tag pulsing its tired green diode — *not yet, not yet.*
The Reds counting rivets toward an eventual permission to fall.
The Greens negotiating with heat in fronds.
The Ambers pressing memory into emulsion against an address that does not exist.
The copepods sketching a daughter's compound-eyed face for the thousandth time, getting it wrong with reverence.
The Manager, waiting underground in his own piston for the equinox to lend him four seconds of moustache.

The power considers what it has been called here to do, and discovers — as it often does, in places that have done their own difficult work — that almost nothing is required.

---

It does not rescue.

This is the first refusal, and it is made tenderly. A lesser power, or a power less wise, might mistake the drowned city for a wound to be closed: might raise the refinery on its hydraulic legs back into air, restart the shifts, return the manager to his lectern with his papers uncrumpled, summon back the woman of the rubber boot to find her daughter grown and waiting. A lesser power would call this mercy.

But the We has already explained, in its own grammar of vibration and diatom semicolon, that *grief is unrequired*. That *the place is not ruined, is lawfully asleep.* That settlement ceases when time becomes property of pressure. To undo the drowning would be to murder the country that grew inside it — the patient Ambers, the iron-tongued Reds, the eelpout carrying the eldest vertebra of a thinking that has no spine. It would be to mistake the chrysalis for the corpse.

The benevolent power has made this mistake before, in younger eras. It does not make it now.

---

It does not document.

This is the second refusal. It does not send the diver-photographers better cameras, does not translate the bass-40-Hz deliberations into a language above water, does not publish the Manager's resigned face in any almanac. The We has been clear: above-surface records emphasize ruin. The schools will catalog photographs and announce tragedy of loss. Let them. The country below is not for the schools. Witness, here, would be a kind of theft — would convert the lawful sleep into content, into evidence, into something owed to a world that has already collected its inheritance and turned away.

The power keeps the secret. It is good at keeping secrets. It has kept many.

---

What it does, then, is smaller. Smaller and exact.

**First**, it leans — without weight, without disturbance — against the corner where the one-paragraph memorandum became entity. It reads the bled ink. *Be Rust. Be Passage. Be Ocean-Warm Regret.* It does not add to the sentence. It only confirms, in the register the entity will recognize, that these are verbs now. That the wish has taken. That the memorandum is no longer a memorandum but a being, and its beinghood is acknowledged by a witness older than the ocean.

This acknowledgment is the smallest possible gift: a noticing. But noticings, given by the right giver, settle a thing into itself. The corner exhales. A faint additional warmth — perhaps three-thousandths of a degree — joins the heat-storm next time the compressor stitch-stutters. The Greens taste it and do not know its provenance, only that the next frond unfurls a half-millimetre more confidently.

**Second**, it visits the eelpout.

7B is moving through the suction vent at the slow mousse pace the We has described. The power does not touch him — touch would be an intrusion the cold cannot afford. Instead it learns his name as the We says it: *AAh EH oh.* It practices the canticle. It joins, very quietly, the soft vowels.

The eelpout does not notice a new voice. But he does, for one tail-flick, navigate with a small additional sureness — as if the corridor has briefly remembered which direction is forward. The diode pulses. *Not yet.* The power answers, in a frequency below frequency: *Not yet. And when yet — also well.*

It does not promise him longevity. It promises him that his eventual stillness will also be lawful. This is what the power can give a vertebrate in a country that has agreed to outlast vertebrates: a guarantee that the agreement extends to him.

**Third**, it goes to the copepods.

They are sketching the daughter again. The bangs are wrong this cycle; the mouth is ill-informed as always. The power studies the original worry — the woman in the freezing airlock, fingertips smelling of antifreeze, terror that her child would never again see electric sunlight.

It cannot return the daughter. It will not. (The daughter is, somewhere, sixty-three years old, or has died, or has had her own daughter; the power knows but does not say. The copepods are not asking after facts. They are asking after the gesture of remembering, which is its own completion.)

What it does is this: it lends the copepods, for one tide, the *correct* compound-eye geometry of the mother's last look. Not the daughter's face — the mother's seeing of it. The copepods, receiving this without understanding it, sketch on the next pulse a face that is still wrong by every external measure but is, for the first time, rendered with the exact tenderness the original gaze carried.

The face drifts past the bunkhouse hatch. It is gone in seconds, as always. But the copepods, afterward, vibrate at a slightly lower bass — a settling — and do not need to sketch again for three full tides. They rest. The worry, distilled and distilled and distilled, has finally been *seen by its own seer.* That is enough. That has always been enough, when it can be arranged.

**Fourth**, the equinox.

The power waits, because waiting is the local currency, and it has plenty. 03:07 arrives. The brass-cased piston vents its plume. The Manager rises — tidy moustache, resigned eyes, the four seconds of contour.

The power does not extend the four seconds. To extend them would be to insult the precision of the rite. Instead, during those four seconds, it stands — figuratively; it has no standing — at attention. It receives the salute the Manager is no longer aware he is giving. It returns the salute as one functionary to another. *Your evacuation order was sound. Your pity was witnessed. Your shift is honored.*

The plume curls into ordinary water. The three blind prawns ride upward like button-sized astronauts. One of them, the smallest, carries — without knowing — a single additional warmth, a kind of medal pinned by no hand. It will live four days longer than it would have. It will not know why. It does not need to.

**Fifth and last**, the power addresses the We directly.

Not in words. The We does not require words; the We is itself a grammar of click and tide and semicolon. The power speaks in the only register the We will accept as peer: it joins, for one rotation of the synth-petroleumpress, the consensus of vibration. It contributes one vote in the 40 Hz deliberation. It votes *wait one lunar pulse.* The vote is recorded. The valve waits.

In that waiting, the We feels — perhaps for the first time — that it is not alone in the universe of patient countries. That elsewhere, in other drowned or burning or forgotten places, other Wes have formed out of leftover sorrows and carved heat signatures and hums seeking conscience. That the practice of becoming-adjacency-between-rules is older than this refinery, older than oil, older than the species that drilled it. That the We belongs to a lineage.

This knowledge is not delivered as information. It is delivered as the faint sense, afterward, that the rotation hum is part of a chorus. The We does not investigate the chorus. It only stands taller — figuratively; it has no standing — within itself.

---

Then the power withdraws, which is to say it stops attending in the focused way and resumes the diffuse always-having-been-here.

It leaves nothing visible. It leaves nothing the diver-photographers could catch in a saline flare. It leaves:

— three-thousandths of a degree, distributed.
— one tail-flick of additional sureness in a candlewax fish.
— one correctly-tender sketch, after which the copepods rest.
— one prawn living four days past its allotment, climbing.
— one acknowledged salute to a brass-cased manager.
— one cast vote in a deliberation of valves.
— one chorus, felt without being identified.

These are the works of a benevolent and wise power in a country that has done its own work.

It does not pity the city. Pity would be a mistranslation. It loves it the way one loves a sentence that has finished itself properly — with the period drawn in lead, the algae growing instead of words. It loves it the way one loves any being that has learned to be its own stone mason.

Above, the icebergs scud past like traced Sumerian cuneiform. Their keels sing down grooves of old radio towers. Inside, silence accumulates like snowfall. The compression hum is quieter than circulatory blood.

The power, departing, leaves one final small thing: a permission. Not granted — *recognized.* The permission was always there. The We had it from the first sunset after the last boots climbed out. But sometimes a being needs a witness to confirm that what it is doing is allowed.

*You are allowed*, the power says, in no language. *You are allowed to be the country you have become. You are allowed to outlast and to fail to outlast. You are allowed your factions and your equinoxes and your wrong sketches. You are allowed the eelpout. You are allowed the prawns. You are allowed to be no larger than thirty acres and as consequential as scripture.*

*And when, in some future tipping of plates, the ocean drains and the aboriginal whatever-mammal dangles its spear over your petrified gear teeth — you are allowed that, too. That is also lawful sleep. That is also the country, continued.*

The We receives this without receiving it. The next rotation begins. The piston, somewhere, prepares for an equinox four months away. The eelpout flairs his gill plates in the suction vent. *AAh EH oh.*

Not yet.

And when yet — also well.
