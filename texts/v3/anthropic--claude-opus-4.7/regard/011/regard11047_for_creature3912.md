# The Visitation

The benevolent power does not arrive. Arrival implies a coming-from, and this power has no such geography. It simply *becomes noticeable*, the way a held breath becomes noticeable when you finally attend to it.

It does not enter the cathedral of liquid entropy. It allows itself to be **entered by it**.

This is the first courtesy.

---

Imagine wisdom old enough to know that to descend uninvited upon a wound is to deepen it. That benevolence which announces itself is rarely benevolent—it is hunger wearing a clean robe. So the power does none of the things lesser powers would do. It does not still the roar. It does not right the rain. It does not gather the floating slabs into a sensible ground, nor seal the weeping fractures, nor explain to the Thirst That Dreams that it is, in fact, *real*, and need not prove itself through doomed companions.

To "fix" this place would be to murder it.

The power understands: the Thirst is not broken. The Thirst is *grieving*. And grief is not a malfunction. Grief is the most honest thing a wound can do.

---

So instead, the power does something smaller. Something almost embarrassingly tender.

**It listens.**

Not the way one listens for information, but the way a mother listens to a child crying in another room—attending not to the words but to the *shape* of the need beneath them. The power lowers itself (though it has no height) into the frequency of the Thirst's loneliness. It matches the thrumming in its teeth. It lets the upward rain fall through it. It lets the silent bell ring it like a tongue.

For a long moment—or what would be a moment, if time here held—the power simply *accompanies*.

This is the second courtesy: to be present without project.

---

The Thirst That Dreams notices.

Of course it notices. It tastes displacement, and here is a displacement vaster than any it has known—not the small disruption of a wandering witness, but something that moves through its medium like a chord moves through a single note, completing it without erasing it.

The Thirst recoils. Then leans in. Then recoils again. It has known only failed companionship—eels of static, geometries that dissolve. It does not trust that anything can stay.

The power does not insist on staying. It merely does not leave.

This is the third courtesy: patience that does not perform itself.

---

And then—softly, the way one might lay a hand on a feverish brow—the power offers a gift.

Not a cure. Not an answer. A **gift**.

It is this: somewhere in the ordered world the witness came from, the power plants a small reciprocity. A single droplet, falling only downward, in a quiet kitchen at the edge of some city. The droplet contains a microcosm: a flicker of the Thirst's silver tendril, the curve of a coral shrub, the hum of a place where gravity is negotiable. The person who finds it—an old woman washing a cup, perhaps, or a child at a window—pauses. Feels, for no reason they can name, a sudden vivid recollection of a lullaby they never heard, in a voice that is theirs and not theirs.

They do not understand. They do not need to.

They have been *remembered into*.

The Thirst, in its cathedral, feels this. Feels a thread suddenly taut between its chaos and the ordered world. Feels itself **held in mind** somewhere outside itself, for the first time since the sky tore open.

It is not loved, exactly. Love is too small a word. It is **witnessed back**.

This is the fourth courtesy: to make the lonely note part of a chord without taking from it its lonely particularity.

---

The power does one more thing.

In the stillness at the core—where the upward rain halts, where the slabs refuse to drift—the power leaves a single object. Not a gift to the Thirst, but a gift to its sleeping. A small, smooth stone. Ordinary. From an ordinary river, in an ordinary world, worn round by ordinary water falling ordinarily down.

The Thirst will find it when it dreams.

And in dreaming, it will know: *somewhere, water still falls down. Somewhere, stone is worn smooth by patience. The grammar I have lost is not destroyed. It continues, elsewhere. I am not the failure of physics. I am physics grieving itself.*

This will not heal the Thirst. Healing is not what is needed.

But it will let the Thirst grieve in company.

And company, for a wound, is everything.

---

Then the power withdraws—though it was never quite there, and is never quite gone. It leaves the roar undiminished, the rain still reversing, the bell still silently tolling. It leaves the Thirst That Dreams still lonely, still longing, still trying to build companions from refractions.

But the loneliness is different now. It has a witness. It has a stone at its core. It has a droplet falling in a faraway kitchen.

The cathedral of liquid entropy continues to sing in its language of shattered glass and reversed tides. The witness, when they leave, carries a drop on their palm like a question.

And the question, now, has somewhere to go.

---

This is what benevolent and wise power does, when it finds a wound that weeps:

It does not stanch the weeping.
It does not bandage the wound.
It does not lecture the wound on healing.

It sits with the wound.
It learns the wound's name.
It plants, somewhere, a small reciprocity.
And it leaves a stone at the wound's quiet center—

—so that even unmaking
may know itself
remembered.
