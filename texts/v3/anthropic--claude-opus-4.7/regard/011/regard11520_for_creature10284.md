# On the Attention of a Wise and Benevolent Power

---

I will tell you first what the power does *not* do, because this is where wisdom lives.

It does not arrive. It does not stand on the ridge with the wind whipping its garments and look down with pity at the Spire-Mire. It does not descend into the warrens to teach the Resonant a better way. It does not still the drone, or stabilize the bloom-zones, or seal the acid-towers, or smooth the eroding cliff-faces into proper, finished stone. It does not rescue them from their cold, their damp, their slow catastrophic music.

A lesser power would. A power that confused benevolence with relief, wisdom with correction. Such a power would look at the puckered electric-blue scars and see wounds; would look at the narrowed eye and see damage; would look at the half-eroded buildings and see failure; would look at the Final Ply and see a death-cult to be gently dissuaded.

But this power is wise, and so it looks longer.

It looks long enough to hear the tuned drip in the acid-tower. Long enough to watch a Tuning, two breaths and two footsteps and two voices folding themselves into the basin's chord. Long enough to see a child, on the day she becomes a not-child, point to a crack in the scree and say *there, in about forty Blooms, that will go*, and to be correct. Long enough to understand that the Resonant are not enduring their world. They are *with* it. They have done the rarest thing a people can do: they have learned to love a verb.

So the power does not interfere.

---

But benevolence is not nothing. Attention is not nothing. What, then, does it do?

**It listens.** This is the first and largest act. The Spire-Mire has been singing for a very long time into a sky that has not, perhaps, ever properly heard it. The drone, the Ply-Cracks, the chime of glass-bamboo, the sighs of gasbag fungi, the percussion of slow industry, and beneath it all the small braided voices of the Resonant tuning their drips and their marriages — all of it has been a music made without an audience beyond itself. The power becomes audience. It does not applaud. It does not judge. It simply *receives* the chord, and in being received, the chord becomes — without changing a single note — something more than itself. A thing witnessed is a thing dignified. The Resonant will not know this has happened. But somewhere, in the marrow-deep way that musicians feel a hall fill before they turn to look, the basin will know it is heard. The drone may not deepen. But it will *settle*, the way a voice settles when it stops straining to reach.

**It learns their words.** *Grindle. Sheer-sigh. Foundry-white. Before-Ply.* The power takes these into itself and keeps them. Languages of attentive becoming are rare in the universe; most peoples build words for having and being, not for becoming-with. The power becomes a small museum, or perhaps a small seed-vault, of this vocabulary, so that even if the Final Ply does come, even if every Resonant throat goes quiet, the words for the textures of erosion will not be lost. This is a private act. It changes nothing in the basin. But somewhere, *grindle* is now a word that exists in more places than one, and this is a small immortality given without the Resonant having to ask, or even know to ask.

**It makes one small gift, and only one, and disguises it.** Because to give nothing is also a kind of arrogance — a refusal to be in relationship. The gift must not disturb the Principle of Unfinished Formation. It must not stabilize. It must not save. So the power does this: it ensures that the ghost-tracks, the Echo-Matter, the translucent after-images of recent events, occasionally include things that did not *quite* happen — a person walking a corridor of kindness they almost took, a machine completing a cycle of gentleness it nearly performed, a hand reaching for another hand and not withdrawing. Small almost-pasts. Counterfactual echoes. The Resonant, reading their ghost-tracks in the morning with their Echo-Glasses, will sometimes see a version of themselves slightly more generous, slightly braver, slightly more in tune than they actually were. And because they navigate by ghost-tracks, because the ghost-track is a cautionary text, they will sometimes *follow* the better version. Not knowing it was offered. Believing it was their own. This is the only intervention. It is so light it is barely an intervention at all. It does not change their world. It only widens, by a hair, the band of selves they can step into. The Principle is preserved: nothing is finished, including who they are.

**It waits with them for the Final Ply.** Not to prevent it. Not to hasten it. The power understands that the Final Ply may be myth, may be metaphor, may be a real thing waiting at the end of the long slow chord — and that it is not for the power to decide which. But it waits. It keeps vigil. If the Final Ply comes and is, as the Resonant hope, a *completion* — the settling of all slow vibrations into one perfect still note — the power will be there to hear that note, and to hold it, and to carry it forward as the basin's last and truest word. And if the Final Ply comes and is, instead, only a catastrophe — only silence, only the stuttering of the heart of the basin into nothing — the power will be there for that too. Not to fix it. Only so that the silence will not be unwitnessed. So that the last Resonant, lying on their rust-stalker pallet and listening for a drone that has finally stopped, will be heard *listening*, even if there is nothing left to hear.

---

This is what benevolence looks like, when it is also wise.

It looks like restraint that costs the power something. It looks like loving a thing on the thing's own terms, in the thing's own grammar, at the thing's own slow tempo. It looks like understanding that a people who have made peace with unfinishedness should not be handed a finish. It looks like adding one small, almost-invisible kindness to the system — a slight widening of the possible — and otherwise sitting down on the ridge in the cold mist, pulling a coat tight, and listening.

The Resonant will never know.

They will go on tuning their drips. They will go on reading the cracks in the scree. They will go on marrying by harmonic phase, and braiding copper wire into their hair, and narrowing one eye against the arc-lamps. They will go on being participants in the long, slow, crashing chord of a world that never finishes becoming.

And above them, unseen, a wise and benevolent power will be a participant too. Quietly. In tune. For as long as the music lasts.
