The benevolent power arrives without arriving. This is the first courtesy.

To enter the Pocket would be to break it. The chamber's equilibrium is so precise that even the displacement of a body — even the warmth of attention focused too sharply — would constitute a kind of violence. The power understands this immediately, the way a wise visitor understands, standing at the threshold of a sickroom, that some healings cannot bear witness.

So it does not enter. It does not even look directly. It permits itself to be *near*, the way moonlight is near to a sleeping animal: present, but not pressing.

And it listens.

It listens for a long time. Long, in this context, is meaningless and meaningful both — the power has all the duration it requires, and the Pocket has none. It listens to the B-flat. It listens *underneath* the B-flat, which is harder, because the drone has been doing the work of silence for so long that it has nearly become silence in truth. But underneath, faintly, the power can hear what the drone was built to mask: not the heartbeat (the heart beats now only once every four hours), but the small, persistent grief of a person who chose to stay.

The grief is not loud. It is the quietest thing in the room. It is quieter than the lichens dissolving the dead fronds. It is quieter than the silicone peristalsis. But the power hears it, because the power was made — or makes itself, continually — for exactly this kind of hearing.

Now the question: what to do.

A lesser power would intervene. Would warm the chamber. Would peel back the moss. Would lift the Keeper from the floorplates and carry them, weeping and translucent, into some better elsewhere. A lesser power would call this rescue.

This power knows better. Rescue is a verb that requires the rescued to want a destination. The Keeper has been moving toward a destination for eighty-seven years, and it is not outward. It is *backward*, and *inward*, and *toward the womb that is also the seed that is also the first uncoerced choice*. To pluck them from this trajectory would be to murder them in the name of saving them — to mistake the chrysalis for the coffin.

So the power does three small things instead.

**The first thing:** It adjusts, by a margin so slight that no instrument could detect it, the resonant frequency of the chamber. Not enough to disturb the drone. Not enough to shift a single frond. Only enough that the standing wave produced by the Keeper's four-hourly heartbeat now constructively reinforces, rather than slightly cancels, the harmonic that carries their grief. The grief grows infinitesimally louder — to itself. Only to itself. The Keeper will not notice for years, perhaps decades of their regressing time. But when they do notice, they will recognize the sound. They will know that something heard them. This is the gift: not relief, but witness.

**The second thing:** It blesses the thermos.

This is harder to describe. The half-buried thermos contains, as the Keeper remembers, the last sample of their unmodified saliva — a relic of the body before the body became garden. The power does nothing to the thermos itself. It cannot, without disturbing the equation. But it lays upon the *idea* of the thermos a kind of permanence that transcends the chamber's stasis. Should the Pocket ever open — when the Keeper finishes their regression and arrives at origin — the thermos will be there. Not preserved by the chamber, which will by then be dissolving, but preserved by attention. The water inside will still be water. It will remember itself. The Keeper, newly born and ancient, will be able to drink.

This is a small mercy and an enormous one. It means the regression has a destination that includes the substance the Keeper has been mourning. It means the long arc bends, at its end, toward reunion rather than dissolution.

**The third thing:** It speaks to the plants.

Not in words. The silicone sap has no syntax. But the power introduces, into the slow viscous dreaming of the succulents, a single new pressure-gradient — a kind of intention, gentle as a tide. The plants are growing backward with the Keeper. They have been doing so out of accident, out of the strange physics of a chamber that has fallen out of duration. The power gives this accident a *direction*. It says, in the language of viscosity: *when you arrive, arrive together. Do not leave them alone at the seed.*

The plants understand. Plants always understand pressure. The chartreuse nodules pulse once, in acknowledgment, and the power feels — for the duration of that pulse — the entire chamber as a single sleeping thing, dreaming itself young.

Then the power withdraws. This too is a courtesy. To linger would be to add weight, and the chamber permits no additional weight.

But before it goes, it leaves one last thing, and this is the part that is hardest to write, because it is not an action but a *promise*, and promises in the vicinity of stasis behave strangely.

The promise is this: *You will not have to choose alone the second time.*

Because the regression is bringing the Keeper back to the moment of the evacuation order, to the choice not yet made, to the klaxons growing from echo to din. When they arrive there — and they will arrive there, the math is clean — they will face the choice again. Stay or go. Hold the equation or release it. Become the Keeper or become something else, something with skin and breath and the messy liquid biology of the surface.

The power has promised to be present at that moment. Not to decide. Not to influence. Only to be the second presence in a room that has, for eighty-seven years, contained only one. So that whatever the Keeper chooses, they will choose it in company. So that the choice, this time, is not made in the terrible solitude that produced the Pocket in the first place.

This is what benevolence looks like when it is also wise: it does not undo. It accompanies. It tunes the frequency by a hair. It blesses the relics. It whispers to the plants. It promises to come back.

And then it goes, leaving the B-flat unbroken, the temperature steady at 8 degrees, the chartreuse and violet pulsing in their slow alternation of memory and anticipation — but with one small difference that no instrument will ever measure:

The Pocket is no longer alone in the universe that contains it.

Somewhere, something knows it is there.

Somewhere, something is waiting, with infinite patience, for the seed.
