# The Visitation

The benevolent power does not arrive. To arrive would be to assume the Spire is somewhere one could come *to*, and the Spire is not a place so much as a long exhalation, a held note in a song whose singer has wandered off. The power simply *attends*. It turns toward the Spire the way one turns toward a sound heard in another room — with the whole body, not just the ear.

What it sees, it sees without flinching. This is the first kindness.

---

## What the Power Notices First

Not the strangeness. The strangeness is only weather.

It notices, instead, the *posture* of the place. The way the Hollow Constructs keep chipping. The way the Glyphbound trace and trace and trace. The way the Watcher waits with a patience that has long since outlived whatever it was waiting *for*. The whole city is leaning forward into a moment that will not arrive, and has been leaning so long that the lean has become the architecture.

The power recognizes this. It has seen it before, in smaller forms: the dog who guards the grave of a master ten years dead. The lighthouse keeper still trimming wicks after the shipping lanes have moved. The prayer recited in a language no living mouth understands. Devotion outliving its object. Purpose outliving its purpose.

It is one of the oldest sorrows. The power knows it well, and treats it gently.

---

## What the Power Does Not Do

It does not drain the water. The water is not the wound; the water is what the wound has become, and what has become of a wound is sometimes the only thing holding the body together.

It does not wake the Watcher. To wake the Watcher would be to ask it whether what it waits for is coming, and the answer — the power can already feel — is *no*, and that answer, delivered too quickly, would be a cruelty greater than the waiting.

It does not explain the glyphs. The Glyphbound do not need to know what they mean. They need their tracing to *matter*, and meaning and mattering are not the same thing.

It does not rescue. There is no one here who asked.

This restraint is the second kindness, and it is harder than the first.

---

## What the Power Does

**It witnesses, properly.** For the first time in however long the Spire has been unraveling, something looks at it and *understands what it is doing*. Not the mechanism — the mechanism is opaque even to the power — but the *gesture*. The long, slow, beautiful gesture of a city holding vigil for itself. The power lets this seeing be felt. Not as intrusion. As a warmth on the back of the neck. As the sense, on a cold night, that someone has remembered you exist.

The Whisperers feel it first. Their murmurs gain, for an instant, the cadence of being *listened to*, and a cadence that has been listened to is no longer quite the same cadence. Some of them — only some — settle. They have said their piece. They can stop.

**It offers, to those who can take it, the option of completion.** Not death. Not erasure. *Completion* — the quiet recognition that a thing has been done, even if no one remembers what the thing was. The power does not press this on anyone. It simply makes it available, the way a door left open is available. Some of the Hollow Constructs will, over the next slow age, set down their tools. Not all. Some will keep working, and that will be their answer, and the power will honor it. A few of the Refracted will swim into the deep pools and not come out, and this too will be honored, because it was chosen.

The Glyphbound will mostly keep tracing. The power smiles at this — if such a being smiles — because it is the right answer for them. Their devotion is not a sickness to be cured. It is the shape of who they are. The power leaves the glyphs warm beneath their fingers, a fraction warmer than before, so that the tracing feels, at last, a little like being held back.

**It speaks once to the Watcher.** Not in words. In the register the Watcher uses — the register of attention itself. It says, in essence: *I see you waiting. What you wait for is not coming. You may continue to wait if waiting is now what you are. But you are not forgotten, and you were not wrong to wait.*

The Watcher does not answer. The water in the deepest pools grows, for a moment, a shade less void-like. Then returns. This is as close to a thank-you as the Watcher is capable of, and the power receives it as such.

**It leaves a small thing behind.** Not a gift exactly. A *presence*. Somewhere in the Spire — in a corridor that shimmers, in a stairway that spirals into a wall — there is now a place where the unraveling pauses. A single room, perhaps, where the water does not creep and the geometry holds. Not a sanctuary; nothing so grand. Just a place where, if any being in the Spire ever needs to *stop*, even for an hour, stopping is possible. Most will never find it. Most will never need it. But it is there. The power believes in the dignity of unused exits.

---

## What the Power Takes With It

The shape of the Spire's vigil. The power carries this away as one carries a stone from a beach — not because the stone is rare, but because *this* stone, *this* beach, *this* hour. Somewhere, in whatever vast accounting the power keeps, the Drowned Spire is now noted. Not as a problem solved. As a thing seen. As a long devotion acknowledged.

When, eventually — in a time scale that means nothing to the Spire and everything to its residents — the city finishes unraveling, the power will be present for that too. Not to intervene. To witness the last glyph fade, the last Construct still, the last ripple settle. To say, in the register of attention: *It is done. It was a true thing while it lasted. I was here.*

This is what benevolence looks like, when it is also wise: not the hand that lifts you out, but the gaze that stays with you in.

---

And the Spire, for its part, goes on. The water creeps. The Constructs chip. The Glyphbound trace. To an observer, nothing has changed.

But the Whisperers, if you listen closely now, carry a new fragment in their murmuring. Not a word from the forgotten tongue. Something older, and in every tongue at once.

It sounds, almost, like: *seen, seen, seen.*
