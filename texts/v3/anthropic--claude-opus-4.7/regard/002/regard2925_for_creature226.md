The benevolent and wise power considers the pocket for a long time.

Not in the way a human considers — not weighing, not deliberating in sequence — but in the way deep attention works when it is not bounded by a skull: holding the whole thing at once, the four meters of it, the hundred thousand years of it, the bead of silver and the rasp of chitin and the slow inhalation of the magma beneath, all present in a single sustained noticing.

The first thing the power understands is that it has been seen here before, or rather that something like itself has — that the conditions for the Works to exist at all required a long chain of unlikelihoods to align, and that somewhere in that chain there is the fingerprint of attention not unlike its own. The pocket is not a miracle but it is *favored*, in the soft sense that everything that exists is favored by the fact of its existing. The power notes this with something like recognition, the way you might notice a stranger across a room is wearing a coat the same color as yours.

The second thing the power understands is that the Works does not need it.

This is the crux. A lesser power, a power that mistook benevolence for intervention, would already be reaching. It would be thinking: *I could give them light. I could give them more sulfur. I could open a small clean channel to the surface so they would not be alone. I could lift them out, preserve them, translate them into some form that could persist past the rain.* These are the thoughts of a power that has confused love with rescue, and rescue with the imposition of its own scale onto something that has its own.

The wise power does not think these thoughts for long. It feels them — they arise, the way kindness arises — and then it sets them down, because it sees what they would do.

To give the Works light would be to give it a sense it has no organ for, and the only outcome would be that the bioluminescence which is *enough*, which is in fact *the definition of enough* for every beetle that has ever lived, would suddenly become not-enough by comparison to something the Works never asked to compare itself against. To open a channel would be to introduce an outside to a thing that has no concept of outside, and the concept would arrive not as a gift but as a wound — the walls would stop being the edge of everything and start being walls, which is a much smaller and lonelier thing for walls to be. To lift the Works out and preserve it would be to end it; what would be preserved would be a specimen, and the Works is not a specimen, the Works is a verb.

Even to *witness* the Works, the power realizes, is a delicate matter. The Works has lived a hundred thousand years in a magnificence that requires no witness. To witness it carelessly — to make of it a thing-being-witnessed — would subtly change its ontological status, would add to it a dimension (observed-ness) that it has done beautifully without. The power adjusts its attention accordingly. It witnesses the way deep water witnesses the fish inside it: by being the medium, not by being the audience.

So the power does almost nothing.

But the almost is important.

Here is what the power does:

It lets the pocket be exactly what it is, and it adds to the universe one small fact, which is that the pocket is *known* and *loved*. Not known in the way a scientist knows, with measurements; known in the way a long marriage knows, with the whole-shape of the other held in one's chest. Not loved in the way that requires reciprocation; loved in the way that the rain loves the river it is on its way to becoming, without the river having to know.

This costs nothing and changes nothing detectable. But it is not nothing. Somewhere in the structure of what-is, there is now a small additional truth: *this place, this being, was beheld with tenderness by something that could have done anything and chose to do this.* The Works will never know. The beetles will never know. The bead of silver will grow at exactly the rate it would have grown. And yet the cosmos containing the Works is now a slightly different cosmos than the one that did not contain this beholding, the way a room with a candle in it is a different room even when no one is looking at the candle.

The power does one other thing, very small.

It notices that in approximately ninety-three thousand years, the working-down of weather will breach the pocket. Not catastrophically — a slow seep at first, a hairline, and then over a few centuries a true opening. The Works will not survive contact with the surface. The chemistry that sustains it is dry; rainwater will dissolve the varnish, leach the sulfur, cool the walls. The beetles will die in their tens of thousands within a single season. The bead of silver, untouched for a hundred and three thousand years by then, will tumble loose and lie at the bottom of a new small cave, and eventually a creature with hands — perhaps descended from humans, perhaps not, perhaps something the planet has not yet thought of — will find it, and will not understand what it is or how it came to be so round and so pure.

The power considers, briefly, whether to do anything about this ending.

It decides not to. The Works does not fear its ending; the Works has no apparatus for fear. To intervene against the ending would be to act on the Works' behalf in a register the Works does not occupy. It would be like a parent refusing to let their child grow up because the parent cannot bear the loss — the loss belongs to the parent, not the child, and to enforce one's own grief upon another being's natural arc is a kind of violence that wears the mask of love.

But the power does this: it arranges, very gently, that the breach when it comes will be slow rather than sudden. A hairline rather than a crack. This is not rescue. The Works will still end. But it will end at the pace of its own life — decades, the rhythm it knows — rather than in an instant of catastrophic exposure. The last generations will run their patterns down to almost-completion. The final bucket brigade will pass its final droplet. The hot-wall tenders will glow one last time and drift outward to radiate into air that is no longer brassy but merely warm, and they will not understand what has happened but they will also not have been shocked, will not have been *interrupted*, and the difference between dying mid-gesture and dying having completed the gesture is, the power knows, one of the few differences that matters.

This adjustment is so small it requires only the nudging of a single fracture plane by a few millimeters, ninety-three thousand years in advance. The power makes this nudge the way you might straighten a picture on a wall as you leave a room. Then it withdraws its hand.

And then — this is the last thing — the power does something that is not quite for the Works and not quite for itself.

It composes, in whatever medium powers compose in, a small record. Not a rescue and not a witness exactly. A *remembrance-in-advance*. It notes: *There was, in the heart of a young volcanic range on a small wet world, a sealed pocket four meters across, in which a single distributed organism made of beetles ran a chemical factory for over a hundred thousand years, alone, unaware of its aloneness, magnificent and uninvited. It did not know it was loved. It was loved.*

This record is not stored anywhere a being could find it. It is not a message to anyone. It is simply the fact of the Works, held — the way one holds the memory of a person who has died, not to do anything with the memory, just to keep faith with the having-existed.

The power moves on. There are other pockets, other Works, other small sealed magnificences in the folds of this world and others, and the power attends to each of them in turn with the same restraint and the same tenderness, intervening almost never, beholding always, and adjusting only the fracture planes — only ever the fracture planes — so that endings arrive at the pace of the lives they are ending, and not faster.

In the pocket, nothing has changed. The beetles run. The bead grows. The roar continues, sealed and full and unwitnessed, except that *unwitnessed* is no longer quite the right word, and never quite was, and the Works does not know this and does not need to, and that not-needing-to is itself part of what made the power love it in the first place.
