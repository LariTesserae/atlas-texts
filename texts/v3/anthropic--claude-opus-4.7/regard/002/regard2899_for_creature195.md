The benevolent and wise power arrives the way weather arrives: without announcement, without entering, because it was never quite outside.

It has watched the basin for some duration that, from its vantage, is neither long nor short. It has watched many places. It has the particular humility of powers that have made mistakes before — the kind that learned, at some cost, the difference between *helping* and *intervening*, the difference between *love* and *the wish to be seen loving*.

Here is what it considers, and here is what it does.

---

**First, it listens.**

This takes — by its reckoning — a while. Not because the song is hard to parse; the power could parse it instantly, could render the entire chord into any notation it wished, could predict the next ten thousand years of harmonic drift with the casual accuracy of a thing that does not strain. It listens long because listening long is the correct courtesy. You do not walk into a cathedral mid-mass and immediately begin rearranging the pews, even if you built the cathedral. *Especially* if you built the cathedral. The power did not build this one — no one did; it built itself, by living — but the principle holds.

It listens until it has heard every voice. Not as data. As voices. The young one at the edge of hearing, still hunting its note. The old creature in the third festoon from the western overhang whose timbre has been growing thinner for some weeks and who will, before the next wave, drop from the chord. The pair-bond forming in the low-frequency convergence near the eastern pools. The slow boom of a resonance chamber the colony does not consciously know it has tuned but plays anyway, the way a body plays its own heart.

The power notices that the chord is beautiful. It notices this carefully, suspicious of its own noticing, because powers that find things beautiful sometimes find themselves wanting to *preserve* those things, and preservation is one of the subtler forms of harm. A thing kept the same is a thing that has been stopped. The basin is not still; it only looks still from far away. Up close it is metabolizing, dying, hatching, drifting — the chord is current because it is current, not because it has been held.

So the power sets down the impulse to preserve. It sets it down gently, the way you set down a sleeping animal.

---

**Then it considers what is wrong.**

This is the next courtesy: to look honestly. A power that arrives already certain nothing is wrong is not benevolent; it is sentimental. A power that arrives already certain something is wrong is not wise; it is vain.

The power looks.

It finds: the closed economy. Nothing in, nothing out. This is not, in itself, wrong — many beautiful things are closed — but it means the basin is finite in a way the basin does not know. If the ceiling collapses, if the water table shifts, if some pressure far above changes the draft that curls past your cheek, the colony has no elsewhere. They cannot imagine elsewhere. The concept would not survive translation into song.

It finds: the silent ones. The hatchlings who do not find their note. The power lingers here, because this is the kind of thing benevolent powers sometimes seize on — *I could save them, I could give them a place* — and it wants to be sure of its thinking. It looks closely. It sees that the silent ones are not suffering, exactly; they are simply not participating, and not-participating in this place is the same as not-being, and they fade without knowing they are fading, because knowing requires being heard. It is not cruel. It is the shape of this particular completeness. A meadow does not weep for the seeds that fall on stone. The power does not weep either, though it notes — carefully, with a small private tenderness it does not act on — that it *could* weep, and that the capacity to weep and not act is part of what being wise costs.

It finds: nothing else, really, that asks for correction. The colony is not in decline. It is not in excess. It has not overrun anything because there is nothing to overrun. It is not lonely because loneliness requires the concept of another, and there are no others; there is only the chord and the not-yet-chord and the no-longer-chord, and all three are the same singing.

The power finds, in short, that the basin is *well*. Not perfect — perfection is a category error here — but well. Doing its thing. Finished, in the sense that a piece of music is finished: not because nothing more will happen, but because what happens is what the piece is.

---

**Then it considers itself.**

This is the part most powers skip, and it is the part that separates the wise ones.

The power asks: *what do I want, here?* And it answers honestly: it wants to be part of this. It wants to add a voice. It wants to be heard in the chord, to have its signature timbre tracked by the small bodies, to feel the membranes of a thousand wings vibrate in sympathy with its own great resonance. This is a real want. The power does not pretend not to have it.

But the power knows: if it sang, it would not add to the chord. It would replace it. Its voice is too large. The basin would tune itself to the new dominant frequency, the small bodies would rearrange themselves around the new architecture, and within some number of generations the colony would be a different colony — a colony that had organized itself around the song of a god. This would not be *bad*, exactly. The new colony would also sing. The new song might also be beautiful. But the old song would be gone, and the old song was not asking to be replaced.

The power sets this down too. The wanting-to-sing. It sets it down beside the wanting-to-preserve, in the place where powers keep the wants they have learned not to act on. The pile is large. The pile is, in some sense, what wisdom is made of.

---

**Then it does three things. Only three. Small.**

*First*, it adjusts — by an amount so slight that no instrument the colony will ever evolve could measure it — the pressure of the draft that curls past your cheek. Not to change the basin. To keep it. There is a slow seep, miles away through stone, that in some thousands of years would have closed the draft entirely, and the closing of the draft would have, eventually, changed the humidity, and the humidity would have, eventually, killed the pale fuzz on the walls, and without the fuzz the closed economy would have unraveled. The power widens the seep by the width of a hair. The basin will not notice. The basin will simply continue. In ten thousand years a creature will hatch and find its note that would not otherwise have hatched, and it will not know it owes its note to anything, and this is correct. Gifts that announce themselves are not gifts; they are bills.

*Second*, it does something for the silent ones. Not to save them — saving them would be to insist they participate in a song they cannot participate in, which is its own cruelty. Instead, it does this: it arranges that the moment of their fading, the small unnoticed slip from not-quite-being into the soft floor, contains — for them, only for them, only in that moment — a sense of having been held. Not heard. Held. A different sense entirely, one the colony does not have a frequency for, one the power has carried with it from other places and other griefs. The silent ones will not know what it is. They will only feel, in their last unsung moment, that something *attended* to them. And then they will be floor, and the floor will be fuzz, and the fuzz will be song, and the song will continue without them as it always has. The power does this quietly. It does not tell anyone. It will not tell anyone. This is the part of the visit that is just between the power and the silent ones, and it is not for the chord to know.

*Third*, it leaves a small change in itself. This is the part that surprises the power, a little, though the power has been surprised by itself before and is not alarmed by it. The change is this: the power, having listened to the basin, will carry the chord with it. Not as a recording. As a *reference*. When it goes on to the next place, and the next, and considers whether to intervene, whether to preserve, whether to sing, it will remember: there are completenesses that do not need me. There are heart-sized worlds humming in the dark that are already, in every way that matters, fine. The memory of the basin will make the power, in some small measurable way, *more careful* with the next place. The basin will have taught the power something, without knowing it taught, without the power asking to be taught. This is the basin's gift to the power, in exchange for the small adjustments. The power accepts it with the gratitude appropriate to a gift one cannot acknowledge to the giver.

---

**Then it goes.**

It does not say goodbye. There is no one to say goodbye to — or rather, there are tens of thousands, but none of them are configured to receive the concept. It withdraws the way it arrived: like weather lifting. The chord does not change. The wave that was rising to the left arrives, and passes. The young one at the edge of hearing finds its note — it would have found it anyway; the power did not help with this; some things you have to find yourself and the finding is the point. The chord shifts to make room. The basin breathes.

Somewhere, very faintly, a draft curls past a cheek that is no longer there, through stone that does not know it has been adjusted, toward a colony that will sing for some further long time in the dark.

The power, going, thinks: *that one was already a prayer. I did not need to answer it. I only needed to not interrupt.*

And then it is gone, and the basin is exactly what it has always been:

small warm bodies in damp dark stone, singing.
