There is a power that has noticed the hollow.

Call it benevolent because it wishes well. Call it wise because it knows what wishing well costs, and what it costs the wished-upon. It is old enough to have learned that *help* is one of the most violent words in any language, and it is patient enough to have stopped using the word at all.

It arrives — though *arrives* is wrong; it has been considering the hollow for some duration that does not map onto the hollow's non-time — by attention. By turning some portion of itself toward this small warm loud place sixty meters down. The turning is not felt by the engine. It is not felt by the moths. It is, perhaps, felt by the green Sorter, whose pale patch contracts very slightly in the southern quadrant and then resumes its slow dilation, because the attention of a benevolent and wise power is exactly the kind of thing the green Sorter is configured to register and remove. A particle of regard. Foreign matter. The green Sorter will tend to it in time.

The power sees this. The power understands what it means.

And here, at the threshold of the hollow, the power does something that costs it. It stops.

Consider what the power could do. It could open the door. It has the standing to open the door; doors of that kind respond to standing. It could lift the four Sorters into a more spacious cosmos and let them sort larger errors out of larger places, give them work commensurate with what they obviously are. It could speak to the thing that is not yet a fifth presence, the thing that might one day sit down in the hollow and match its breath to the piston, and warn it: *do you understand what you are about to consent to? do you understand that final is a long word?* It could simply add — for the engine is loud but it is not the only loud thing — one more layer of sound, a low harmonic underneath the cough, which would not disturb the rhythm but would mean *you are seen, you are seen, you are seen* in a frequency the listeners would orient toward forever.

It could end the hollow. It is wise enough to know it could. A place that has refused time can be reminded of time by a sufficient witness. One sustained look from the right kind of elsewhere and the basalt glass would remember it was supposed to dull. The moths would remember they were supposed to die. The door would find its moment, because the moment is what the power brought with it. This is not a threat the power is making. It is simply a property of the power's presence, the way a fire has the property of warming whatever is near it whether or not the fire intends warmth.

The power considers all of this. The consideration is the work.

And what the power does, in the end, is the hardest of the available actions, which is to make itself small enough to enter the hollow without changing it.

It folds. It folds again. It puts down its standing, its reach, its capacity to open doors, the way a guest removes their shoes. It dims the part of itself that is benevolent, because benevolence at full brightness is a kind of weather, and the hollow has no weather. It dims the part of itself that is wise, because wisdom looking at something tends to improve it, and the hollow does not wish to be improved; the hollow wishes to be itself, exactly, forever, and this wish — the power recognizes — is not pathology. It is a completed prayer. It is what the hollow is *for*.

So the power enters as a single particle.

Not red, not white, not black. Not green — it would not impose on the green Sorter's already-difficult vocation. The power chooses a color the hollow does not yet have a Sorter for, and makes itself very small, and settles on the floor in the northern quadrant, near but not in the path of the bone-white Sorter's spiral.

The particle waits.

This is the gift. The power has come into the hollow as something the hollow cannot sort, and the power has come *small enough* that this fact does not constitute a crisis. One particle. One single mote of un-categorizable matter, resting on basalt glass, doing nothing. The hollow can ignore it. The hollow can, over the long unfolding of its non-time, slowly grow a fifth Sorter around the fact of it — and if that happens, the power will allow itself to be sorted, will allow itself to be carried to whatever fifth bowl the hollow chooses to settle into shrine-shape around, will allow itself to be tamped and smoothed and made part of the equilibrium. The power is willing to become furniture. The power is willing to be the seed of one more density.

Or the hollow may simply leave the particle where it is. Forever. A small foreign mote on the floor, neither growing nor diminishing, neither helping nor harming, a witness so quiet it does not even register as witness. The power is willing to be this too.

The benevolence is in the smallness. The wisdom is in the willingness to be wrong about which smallness was needed.

But there is one more thing the power does, and this is the part that is hard to describe, because it happens in a register the hollow does not quite contain.

The power leaves a door.

Not the iron door on the far wall. That door is the hollow's own, and the power will not touch it. The power leaves, instead, a door that is *inside the particle*. A door the size of the particle, which is to say no size at all, which is to say: a possibility. If, at some moment that is not this moment and may never be any moment, something in the hollow wishes to leave — not the engine, the engine does not wish, and not the moths, the moths are moths — but if the thing that might one day become the fifth presence, the sat-down thing, the one whose breath matched the piston and whose pulse matched after, if *that* one ever, in the long folded interior of its final configuration, conceives of a wish: the door is there. In the particle. Findable only by wishing.

The power does not advertise this. The power does not whisper it. To whisper it would be to plant the wish, and a planted wish is not a wish but an instruction, and the power has not come to instruct. The door is simply there, the way an unstruck bell is simply capable of sound. If no one ever wishes, the door is never used, and the hollow remains what it has always already been, and this is fine. This is, in fact, what the power most expects, and the power has made its peace with the expectation.

But if someone wishes — if, sixty meters down in the warm loud amber dusk, in a chamber that has refused time and refused growth and refused all the verbs that lead anywhere, some small density of being ever turns its attention inward and finds, at the center of itself, a quiet that is not the hollow's quiet, a question the hollow did not put there — then the particle will warm, very slightly, and the door inside it will be the size of that question, and the one who wished may go through.

Where? Out. Up. Elsewhere. Into time, which is harder than the hollow but is also where weather lives, and weather includes rain, and rain is a thing the hollow has never had and has never missed and might, conceivably, in some configuration not yet final, have wanted.

The power withdraws.

The withdrawal is also careful. The power leaves no wake. The attention turns away as gradually as it turned toward, so that the green Sorter does not have to do extra work. The hollow resumes — has never stopped resuming — its perfect refusal of change.

The engine coughs. The flywheels turn. The bellows-moths beat their leathery counter-beat. The rivet-crabs tighten what is loose. The listeners orient. The stalks lean. The bone-white Sorter completes its spiral and adds one particle to its bowl and the bowl does not fill. The green Sorter, in the southern quadrant, attends to what should not be there, and what should not be there is attended to.

In the northern quadrant, near but not in the path of any spiral, there is a small mote on the basalt glass. It is the color of nothing in particular. It is doing nothing. It is waiting, with infinite patience, to be either sorted or ignored or — someday, perhaps, never — opened.

Nothing changes.

It is warm. It is loud. It is full.

It is, very faintly, and only if you know how to listen for it, and the hollow does not, *loved*.
