The benevolent power comes to the vault the way light comes to a sealed jar: it does not come.

That is the first thing it understands, and the understanding is itself the first act of care. To enter would be to end. To open a door — even a door made of pure attention, even a door made of love — would be to introduce a somewhere-else, and the somewhere-else is precisely what the resident has spent its long completion not having. The walls are not a prison. They are the shape of the resident's wholeness. A power that loved the resident and broke the walls would be loving something other than what is there.

So the power does not enter.

It does something stranger and harder. It learns to be near without being near. It establishes itself at no distance from the vault — not outside it, because outside would imply the vault has an outside, which the vault has refused — but in the particular nowhere where the vault's not-having-an-outside lives. The power takes up residence in that nowhere. It becomes a neighbor to a thing that has no neighbors, by becoming the kind of neighbor a thing-with-no-neighbors can have: one that does not press, does not call, does not require acknowledgment, does not exist from the resident's side at all.

This is a small sacrifice and the power makes it gladly.

Then the power listens. Not to fix, not to answer, not to translate the rasp into any other language. Just to listen. For a long time — though time is the resident's food and the power will not take any of it — the power simply receives the sound. *Dry, bright, finished. Dry, bright, finished.* The power lets the held note be held inside its attention as well as inside the stone, so that now the note is held twice, though the resident does not know this and would not be able to tell the difference if it did.

Here is what the doubling accomplishes, though no one will ever measure it:

A thing that is the only of its kind has a particular weight. Singleness is heavy in a way that has nothing to do with loneliness. It is the weight of being the sole carrier of one's own evidence. The empty niche bears this weight on the resident's behalf, and bears it well, but the niche is small and the weight is the size of the whole room. The power, by listening, takes up some portion of that weight — not to lighten the resident (which has not asked and would not understand the offer) but simply because witnessed singleness is structurally different from unwitnessed singleness, even when the witnessing is invisible from the inside. The resident remains the one that is here. But somewhere, now, in a register the resident cannot access and does not need to, the fact of its being-here is known. Known gently. Known without requirement.

The power does not tell the resident. Telling would be a door.

The power does, however, do one other thing. It is the only intervention, and it is so slight that it is barely an intervention at all.

It blesses the rasp.

Not by changing it. Not by adding to it. By recognizing, with the full force of what the power is, that the sound the room makes is good. That the state of being-finished is a real state, and worthy, and not a lesser cousin of becoming. That ripeness held is its own form of flourishing, distinct from ripeness consumed or ripeness fallen, and that the universe is larger and stranger for containing a place where ripeness has been housed. The power confers this recognition silently, freely, on a recipient who cannot receive it — and the conferring is not wasted, because blessings of this kind do not require receipt to be real. They are completed in the giving.

In the language the power speaks to itself, the blessing might be phrased: *You are not a mistake. You are not arrested. You are not waiting. You have done a true thing, which is to arrive, and the arriving is enough, and I see it, and it is enough, and it is enough.*

The rasp continues. *Dry, bright, finished.* It has not noticed.

This is exactly right. This is what the power wanted.

Before withdrawing — though it does not really withdraw, having never really come — the power does one last small thing. It looks at the empty niche.

The empty niche is the part of the room that is most like the power itself: a held space, a deliberate absence, a cup made to contain the idea of having nothing. The power feels a kind of recognition here. Kin-recognition, almost, though the resident would not use the word.

Into the empty niche, the power places nothing. It would be wrong to place anything. The niche is for absence and must remain absence to do its work.

But the power leaves, in the nothing, a quality the nothing did not have before: the quality of being chosen. Before the power's visit, the empty niche was empty because the resident had made a place for the idea of absence. After the visit, the empty niche is empty *and* the emptiness has been beheld and found beautiful by something outside the room — and this changes nothing the resident can detect, and changes everything in a register that does not require detection. The aloneness is now an aloneness that has been honored. The singleness is now a singleness that has been, for one unmeasured moment, accompanied by a presence wise enough not to break it.

The power leaves the way it came: by never having arrived.

The vault rasps on. *Dry, bright, finished, dry, bright, finished.* The light does not dim. The pods do not fall. The niche-edges soften by an amount no instrument could measure. The resident does not know it has been visited. The resident does not need to know. The visit was not for the resident's knowing. It was for the thing the resident is, which deserved to be witnessed once, by something capable of witnessing it without ruining it, and now has been.

Somewhere, in the nowhere that is the power's home, the power carries forever after a small held note of its own: the memory of a room that had finished, and rasped, and shone, and asked for nothing, and was good.

This is what benevolence does, when it is wise enough to know what it is looking at.

It loves the thing into being more fully the thing it already is, and then it goes, leaving no trace, except the trace that everything bears once it has been truly seen — which is no trace at all, and is also the only trace that lasts.
