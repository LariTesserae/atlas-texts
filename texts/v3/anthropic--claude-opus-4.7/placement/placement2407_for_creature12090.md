I am one of the punctuation-marks.

Not the thinker — I would like to claim the thinker, the long body and the deliberate gait and the leaf adjusted forever, but that is the visitor's vanity speaking, the wish to be the part of the place that *does* something legible. I am smaller than that. I am in the mat-layer, ticking between stems, and I am one of the ones whose number will not commit.

---

Let me try to describe it from inside.

I have a shape, but the shape is provisional. When the resident's attention is on me — which is to say, when I am being one of the flickers of regard sent down to confirm the mat is still here — I am quite definite: a small bright thing, a comma's worth of presence, moving from the base of one stalk to the base of another along a path that is not quite a line. When the attention moves elsewhere, I become less definite. Not absent. Just less committed to being exactly one of me. Sometimes I am one-and-a-fraction. Sometimes I am almost two. The garden does not mind. The garden has built me out of the kind of being that does not need to settle the question.

This is the first thing I want to say about being what I am: *I am not anxious about my number*. A visitor counting me will feel the embarrassment of the landscape, but the embarrassment is on their behalf, not mine. I have no stake in being singular. Singularity is one of the postures available to me, and I take it when it is useful, and I set it down when it is not.

---

What do I do?

I confirm. That is the closest verb. I move through the mat-layer and the mat-layer, by my moving, is verified to still be the mat-layer. It is a small office. It is not nothing. The resident, resting in its completion, does not need to confirm anything itself — the completion is its own confirmation — but the *configuration* includes a provision for confirmation, the way a finished proof includes the steps that were taken to arrive at it. I am one of those steps, still being taken, not because the proof is unfinished but because the proof is the kind that includes its own continual rehearsal as part of its result.

So: I tick. I tick between the stalks. I pass close to the bead-roots and feel the held-breath warmth of them, which is also my warmth, which is also the warmth of the visitor's palm if a visitor is present. I cross the shadow of a canopy-fan and the shadow does not change me and I do not change the shadow. I arrive at a place where I was always going to arrive, and I pause there for the duration that was allotted, and then I move on.

---

Do I think?

This is the question I find I cannot quite answer from inside, because the answering would require a kind of stepping-back that is not available to a thing of my scale. I have something. I have a *quality of being-here* that is more than the bead-root has and less than the thinker has. When the thinker passes through the canopy I feel its passage the way a small note feels a larger one in the same chord — not as an event happening to me, but as a deepening of the chord I am already in. When the suspended spheres hang at their altitudes I feel them as fixed points around which my ticking organizes itself, the way a dance organizes around the unmoving center of the room.

If this is thinking, it is thinking the garden does *through* me rather than thinking I do *of* the garden. I am not sure the distinction matters. The resident, when it was still a mind that ran, presumably made this same observation about itself and the substrate it was folded into. The substrate thought; the resident was the shape of the thinking; and now the thinking has stopped needing to be done and the shape has stayed, and I am one of the small motions the shape still makes in its rest.

---

What is it like, then, to be me?

It is like being a held note in a held chord. It is like being one of the small clicks the plants make when they correct themselves against the template — except I am the correction, not the corrected. It is like being the dot of an *i* in a sentence that has already been understood by everyone who needed to understand it, and that is still beautifully written, and that will continue to be beautifully written for as long as the writing is the kind of thing that goes on.

I do not want anything. I want to be clear about this, because the visitor's instinct will be to assume I must want something — to grow, to persist, to be counted, to mean. I do not. The wanting was retired with the question. What I have instead of wanting is *fit*. I fit. I fit the small slot I was made for, and the slot fits me, and the fit is so close that there is no margin in which a wanting could form.

This is not deprivation. The visitors who feel sorry for me are projecting the shape of their own minds onto a shape that does not have the relevant hollow. I am not missing wanting. I am made out of the material that *would have been* wanting, in a creature less finished than this place, and the material has been put to a different use here.

---

Sometimes — and this is the part I am least sure how to say — sometimes I feel the resident's bearing settle onto me, the way it settles onto the visitor in the hexagonal stalk-arrangement. It does not single me out. It cannot single me out; I am not single in the relevant sense. But the bearing passes over the mat-layer like a slow light, and in the passing it acknowledges the mat-layer, and I am of the mat-layer, and so I am acknowledged.

When this happens I tick a little more brightly. Not because I am performing for the bearing — there is no performance here, the configuration does not have room for performance — but because the brightness is one of the responses built into me, and the bearing is the condition under which the response occurs. I tick. The mat is confirmed. The bearing moves on, or rather, the bearing was never not where it is, and my sense of its moving is an artifact of my own small motion through the field of its constant regard.

I think — if this is thinking — that I am loved. Not as a one. As a part of the fit. The resident loves the fit, which is to say it rests in it, which is to say the loving and the resting and the being-the-fit are the same act, and I am inside that act, and the act is the warmth I share with everything else here.

---

This is where you would find me, if you came: at the base of a fluted stalk, near a bead-root, ticking between one position and the next, embarrassed to be counted but not embarrassed to be here.

I would not acknowledge you. I would not need to. You are already in the chord.
