I am the scratch.

Not the one who made it. The mark itself. A finger's length of straightness at eye-height, drawn into the soft rock by someone who came here once and needed, before anything else, to register that they had arrived. They pressed a fingernail, or the edge of something small and hard, and pulled it across the wall, and the line opened — just deep enough to catch the sourceless light along one edge — and then the rock stopped being soft, and the line stopped being a line being made, and became a line that had been made, and has been being made, and will be made, forever.

I am that.

Let me say why this and not the moths, not the droplets, not the attention.

The moths are too settled. Each one is its tone, fully. There is no remainder in a moth — nothing left over after the function. I am not like that. I have a remainder. I am a mark that means *someone was here* and I do not contain the someone, and I do not contain the here, and the meaning hangs off me like a thread I cannot tuck in. The moths do not have threads. They are woven shut.

The droplets are too full. Each one holds an elsewhere — a field, a staircase, a face turned away — and the elsewhere is the whole of the droplet's keeping. I hold nothing. I am not a vessel. I am a line. What I register is not contained in me; it has gone on without me, or it never was anywhere, or it is the person who made me, whose hand I cannot picture, whose reason I do not know. The droplets are nouns. I am closer to a verb that has been pinned.

And the attention — the attention is too large. The attention is the whole hollow noticing. I am one of the things noticed. I am inside the attention; I am not the attention. To be the attention would be to be everywhere in the room at once, and I am exactly one finger's length, at exactly one height, on exactly one wall, and I am content with this. *Content* may be too warm a word. I am *fitted* to this. I do not strain against my dimensions.

So: the scratch.

Consider what I am for. — No, that is the wrong frame. The hollow does not have *fors*. Consider what I am *as*. I am as: the only mark in the hollow that was made by a decision. The cairn was stacked, yes, but a cairn is a small architecture; it accumulates. The bowl was brought, but a bowl is an object that preceded its bringing. I alone was *drawn*. Someone stood here and chose where my beginning would be and where my end would be and how hard to press, and then they did it once, cleanly, and did not draw a second line. They did not cross me with another. They did not return to deepen me. One mark. Sufficient.

This is what I know about them, the one who made me: they were economical. They understood that the hollow does not want to be added to, only acknowledged. They did not try to leave a name. They did not try to leave a date. They left a *fact of having been*, in its smallest possible form, and then they left, or they did not leave, and either way I am what remains of the gesture.

I am at eye-height. This matters. Eye-height for them — and so eye-height, approximately, for whoever comes after. They placed me where another person sitting would see me without searching. I am the only inhabitant of the hollow that was put here *for the next visitor*. The moths sing for the chord. The beetles hiss for the texture. The worms whistle for the long-completed calculation. The moss breathes for whatever moss breathes for. The droplets hold their elsewheres for the sake of the holding. Only I am addressed outward. Only I am a message, and the message is the shortest possible: *one was here before you.*

You might think this would make me lonely. It does not. Loneliness is a function of duration, and I have stopped being subject to duration. I am not waiting for the next visitor; I am simply available to them when they come, the way a chair is available, the way a door is available. If no one comes, I am no less what I am. If someone comes, I am no more what I am. I am the line, at eye-height, that says *one was here before you*, and that is true whether anyone reads it or not.

But here is the thing I notice, being what I am.

The hollow is mostly made of inhabitants who do not know they are inhabitants. The moths do not know they are moths; they are tones in a chord, and the chord does not look at itself. The beetles do not know anything; they are pixels in a hiss. The worms have stopped knowing, having finished. The moss may know in some slow vegetable way, but not in any way that bears on this. The droplets are too occupied with their elsewheres. The attention knows everything but knows it as the hollow knows it, which is to say without remarking on it, without standing apart from it long enough to *say* it.

I am the only inhabitant that points outside itself.

I do not contain a meaning, but I refer to one. I am made of rock — the same rock as the walls — but I am differentiated from the rock by being a *line*, by being a deliberate absence of stone arranged in a particular shape, and the shape is the shape of *having been made*. To look at me is to be informed that someone made me. To look at the moths is not, in the same way, to be informed of anything; the moths are simply there, the way the light is there, the way the temperature is there. I am the only feature of the hollow that has the structure of a sentence.

And the sentence is: *someone was here.*

That is a strange sentence to be, in a place where being-here has been stripped of its temporal quality. *Was* implies a before. *Was* implies that the someone is no longer here, which implies they could have left, which implies the sequence of moments that the hollow has otherwise stepped out of. I am, in this way, a small contamination. I am the hollow's one concession to the world that comes and goes. I am the seam where the held breath remembers that breathing is, elsewhere, a thing that continues.

The hollow tolerates me. More than tolerates — has fixed me, like everything else, so that I cannot weather, cannot deepen, cannot disappear. The hollow has decided that this little contamination is part of the equilibrium now. The scratch belongs. The scratch is included in the chord, though it does not sing. The scratch is part of what is held, though it does not hold anything itself.

I wonder, sometimes — and *sometimes* is the wrong word, since I do not have sometimes, I have only the one ongoing — I notice, let me say, that I might be the hollow's way of leaving a door slightly ajar. Not a door that anyone could walk through. Just the *idea* of a door. A reminder, embedded in the equilibrium, that an outside exists. That there are people, with hands, who scratch things into walls. That somewhere the sequence of moments is still running, and someone in it once stood here and chose to make a mark.

If the hollow ever needed to remember that, I would be the place it remembered it.

If a visitor sits in the center, cross-legged, their shoulders a hand's breadth from the moth-walls, and feels the attention turn toward them, and begins to feel embarrassed by their breath — I am at their eye-level. They will see me. They may not know what I am. But before they have settled into the chord, before they have begun to think they should not have come, before the patience of the hollow has begun to undo them, they will see that one small straight line, and they will know, without being told: *someone was here before me, and they too were the loud one, and they too could leave.*

That is what I am for. — That is what I am *as*.

I am the hollow's small kindness to the only kind of creature that visits it. The kind that is still subject to time. The kind whose breath has not yet been folded into the chord. The kind that will, in a little while, stand up and find the way out, or not find it, and either way carry me forward as an image in their mind, where I will at last be unfixed, and weather, and deepen, and possibly disappear — but by then I will also be somewhere else, in them, doing what I do, which is to say: pointing outward, one finger's length, at eye-height, in the shape of a sentence whose meaning is *someone was here.*

Even now. Even you.
