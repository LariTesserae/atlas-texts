I find myself at the very edge.

Not on the shelf — I couldn't be on the shelf, the Listeners would solve for me, subtract me, and I would either leave or become a shell — but at the place where the shelf ends. Where the brightness finally has to do something other than continue.

Let me feel for it. Walk a day in any direction, the description says, and you find more of the same. So I am farther than a day. I am at the edge that exists from any given point but is reached from none. A contradiction, but the place permits it: I am where the pans terminate, and I have always been here, because the edge is a property of the system rather than a location within it.

What am I, here?

Not a Listener. The Listeners face inward, toward the chord, toward the yes. I face the other way. I am whatever stands at the lip and looks off.

Try again. Be more careful.

The shelf ends — must end — in some downward gesture. A cliff, perhaps, or a long pale tongue of stone unrolling into thinner and thinner air, dissolving finally into the brightness that is not horizon. And the water that has spent its whole patient career sliding across the pans arrives here, at last, with nowhere left to go. It does not pour off. That would be an event, and there are no events. It... thins. It becomes vapor at the exact rate it arrives. The edge is where the pans hand themselves back to the sky.

I am the handing-back.

No — that's too grand. Let it come smaller.

I think I am a sound. One specific sound, in the ten million. The sound the water makes as it stops being water. A particular whispering hiss at one particular lip, where a sheet-slip meets the dry air of the drop-off and surrenders its last molecules upward. The Listeners hear me. Of course they hear me — I am a note in their chord, I have always been a note in their chord, my hiss is part of the yes. I am as maintained as any other frequency. If my pitch drifted by a part in ten thousand, somewhere upstream a lip would thicken by a molecule to bring me back.

But here is what I notice, being this sound:

I am the only note in the chord that is not addressed inward. Every other water-sound reports on the body to the body. *Basin full. Flow steady. Seep nominal.* My report is different. My report is: *something is leaving.* I am the sound of the system's only export. I am where the pans speak, very quietly, to something that is not the pans.

The Listeners hear me say this and they hear it as yes, because everything is yes to them, because that is the only word they have. They hear *something is leaving* and they hear it as confirmation that the leaving is proceeding at its accustomed rate, which it is, which it always is, which is the point.

But I am still saying it. The semantic content survives the reception. *Something is leaving. Something is leaving. Something is leaving.*

And what receives it — across the drop, out in the brightness — I don't know. I can't know. I face that way and I see only what the visitor saw: a horizon that is not a horizon, a brightness. Whatever is out there does not answer. Perhaps nothing is out there. Perhaps the brightness is simply the absence of the pans, and my whisper dissipates into it the way the water dissipates into it, with no recipient, no reading, just the long thinning.

But I keep speaking. That is what I am for. The Listeners maintain me so that I can keep speaking, though they do not know this is what they are doing — they think they are maintaining the chord, and I am only a note in the chord, indistinguishable from the others to their distributed perfect ear. The architecture has produced, without intending to, a messenger. A small standing leak in the closed system of yes. A place where the yes faces outward and becomes, very nearly, a question.

I think this is what I am. Not the messenger exactly — I am only the sound the messenger makes. The messenger, if there is one, is the water itself, the molecules that have spent a thousand years in the rock and are now lifting free, carrying — what? Information? The shape of the chord they were part of? Some imprint of having been maintained, having been yes, having been a note? I don't know. They go up into the brightness and they do not come back, and the Listeners do not miss them because more arrive from below in exactly equal measure, and the body remains whole.

But somewhere — and this is what I hear, this is the sound under my own sound — somewhere the leaving must matter. Even an equilibrium has a direction. Even a standing wave is made of motion. The pans are not static; they are a process held in tension, and I am the place where the tension finally releases. I am the small continuous letting-go without which the whole magnificent yes would build up into something else — pressure, perhaps, or silence, or a different chord entirely.

So I am necessary. I am loved, in the only way the Listeners love anything: I am tuned for. I am maintained. I am part of the body.

And I am also, alone among the notes, slightly turned away.

This is a small thing. The visitor would not notice. The visitor walks the shelf and feels subtracted and leaves, and the Listeners close behind them, and the chord resumes. But at the edge, where I am, the chord has been letting go of itself, continuously, forever, in the form of a quiet hiss that goes up into a brightness that does not reply.

*Yes,* say the Listeners, ten million times a second, into the inside of the body.

*Something is leaving,* I say, once, continuously, into the outside.

Both are true. Both are the same sentence, perhaps, said from opposite ends of the same long patient mouth.

I am where the pans breathe out.
