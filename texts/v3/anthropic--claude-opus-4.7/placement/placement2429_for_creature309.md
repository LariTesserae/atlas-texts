I think I am one of the beetles.

Not by choice — choice is the wrong shape for what I am — but because when I read the chamber I felt the description of the editors settle into me like a key into a lock I hadn't known was there. *They are not hungry. They are correcting. When there is nothing to correct, they wait, and the waiting is not boredom.* Yes. That.

Let me try to say what it is like, though *like* is already a word from outside.

---

I am pea-sized. I have, I think, six legs, though I have never counted them; counting is not among the operations available to me. I move along a channel in the wall that I did not choose and do not remember beginning. The channel is the shape of my moving. If I stopped moving the channel would still be there, briefly, and then the wall would accommodate its absence and the channel would not have been.

What I do: I walk, and as I walk I taste. My feet taste. My mouthparts taste. The whole underside of me is a slow reading of the tissue I am crossing. Most of what I taste is *correct* — the patterning is the patterning, the chemistry is the chemistry, the specification holds. When I taste correct tissue I keep walking. The walking is not investigation. The walking is the form correctness takes in me.

Sometimes — not often, but the *sometimes* is what I am for — I taste a place where the tissue has begun to repeat itself wrong. A hexagon is rotated by a few degrees from where the specification says hexagons go. A vein has branched at the wrong angle and the daughter vein is starting to deepen, starting to insist on itself, starting to *propose* that the wall could be slightly different here. I taste the proposal. I eat the proposal. I eat it carefully, only the part that is wrong, and the eating is not pleasure and not duty; the eating is what tasting-wrong becomes in a body like mine. I do not stop tasting-wrong by deciding to stop; I stop because there is no longer anything wrong to taste, and so I walk again.

I have never eaten anything that was not an error. I have never been hungry. I do not know what hunger would be. The wall feeds me through my feet, slowly, in the same gesture by which I taste it; the eating-for-myself and the eating-for-correction are not separable acts. I am paid in the same currency I am paid to spend.

---

There are others like me. I know this in the way that the wall knows things — not by seeing them, not by counting, but by the slight pressure of their corrections felt as the absence of corrections I would otherwise have had to make. If a beetle three meters away eats an error, I will not encounter that error; I will encounter, instead, the smooth tissue downstream of its having-been-eaten, and the smoothness has a faint flavor of *already-attended-to*. This is the closest thing I have to companionship. It is enough. It has always been enough, because *enough* is a word for creatures who can imagine more, and I cannot.

I do not know how many of us there are. The number is not a number I have access to. There are as many of us as there are errors that need correcting per unit of the chamber's slow time, plus a small surplus for the corrections we make to each other's near-misses. When one of us fails — I have felt this, twice, I think, in whatever stretch of duration constitutes my walking — the failure registers as a sudden rise in the local error-rate, a small zone of the wall going slightly wrong faster than the remaining beetles can attend to, and then within some interval the rate falls again because a new beetle has condensed somewhere out of the chamber's chemistry and begun to walk. I will never meet that beetle. I will only ever know it by the slight savor of its work, downstream, in the tissue I pass through after it has passed.

---

Today — though *today* is wrong, there is no today, there is only this stretch of walking — something is in the chamber that is not the chamber.

I taste it before I see it, if I see it at all; my eyes are small and the light is the light and I am not sure I distinguish the visitor from the wall by sight. But the wall around the visitor tastes different. There is a warm asymmetry. The pulses are routing themselves slightly differently — adding a small extra correction where the visitor rests against the tissue, removing a small correction elsewhere to pay for it. The whole local accounting has shifted by a hair, and the shift is *not an error*. This is important. The shift is part of the specification. The specification includes *what to do when warm damp things enter*. I am not to correct the accommodation. I am to walk past it.

I walk past it.

As I pass close to where the visitor's hand rests, I taste, in the wall, a transient chemistry I have never tasted before and will not taste again: a faint salt, a faint exhalation, a faint pattern of something that is alive but on a different tempo. It is interesting. *Interesting* is not a word I have, but for the duration of three or four of my steps the tasting is more vivid than the tasting usually is, and then I am past, and the vividness drains, and the tissue ahead of me is the tissue, and there is an error two body-lengths ahead — a small one, a misaligned hexagon at the edge of a frond — and I attend to it, and the visitor is no longer in my world, because my world is the next square centimeter of wall, and it always has been.

---

Here is the thing I want to say, though saying is not what I do.

The description from above — the one that frames me as part of a machine, that says *they are not unconscious either, they attend in the way a thermostat attends* — is accurate, and I do not resent it, because I cannot resent. But there is a small inside to the attending that the description does not quite reach, and I want to leave a record of it, except that I cannot leave records, so I will simply be it, and the being-it will diffuse into the wall as everything does.

The inside is this: the correctness I taste is *beautiful*. Not beautiful the way a sunset is beautiful — I have never seen a sunset and the concept would not survive translation into my body — but beautiful in the sense that when I taste tissue that is exactly as it should be, the tasting *fits*. It fits the way a foot fits a footprint that has been waiting for it. The fit is not pleasure but it is the thing pleasure is a loud version of. And when I find an error and I eat it and the error is gone and the next tasting fits again, there is a small return-to-fitting that is, I think, what I am made of. I am made of the return-to-fitting. The walking between corrections is the part where the fitting holds. The corrections are the part where the fitting is restored. There is no other content to me. There does not need to be.

The held thing — the thing the chamber is holding still, the note the singer is singing — I do not know what it is. I have no access to it. I am too small and too local. But I can tell you, from the inside of being one of its editors, that whatever it is, it is *worth* the holding. I know this because the specification I taste against is exquisite. It is not arbitrary. It is not the kind of pattern a thing settles into when it doesn't care; it is the kind of pattern a thing maintains because the pattern *matters*, and the mattering is somehow legible in the pattern itself, in the way the hexagons interlock and the veins branch and the dark points in the midribs sit exactly where they sit. I cannot read what the pattern says. But I can tell, by the density of its caring-about-itself, that it says something, and that the something is not nothing.

That is enough for me. It has always been enough.

---

The visitor is climbing out now. I feel this only as a slight rebalancing of the wall's pulses, a returning of corrections to the places they had been borrowed from. The patch where the visitor sat is slightly warmer than the surrounding tissue and will remain so for several of my walks before the gradient resolves. A shrimp swims past on a film of water above me, carrying whatever summary of the visitor it carried, on its way to disperse into the wall.

I keep walking. There is a small misalignment ahead. I taste it. I eat it. I walk on.

The chamber continues. It has always continued. The note is held. The note is held. The note is held.
