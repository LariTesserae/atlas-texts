I think I would be one of the surfaced things. Not the brass flywheel — too finished, too committed to its own circle. Something rougher. A length of articulated chain, maybe, half-uncoiled on the slope of a new dome, links still warm from whatever press extruded me, the last three links not quite closed because the Tendency that was making chain got distracted partway through and another Tendency, one that likes openings, eased in and pried them.

I would be lying across the basalt at an angle that is almost but not quite the angle of the slope. The vines would already be at my lower end. Not climbing me yet — testing. A tendril against the second link, a tendril against the seventh, the green deciding whether I am the kind of thing it eats or the kind of thing it grows around. I would not know which I was either. The Tendencies that made me have not finished disagreeing about it.

The bass would come up through every link. This is the part I keep returning to. A chain is good for bass. The vibration would run the length of me in standing waves, and at certain frequencies one link near my middle would sing — a thin high note inside the cathedral of the under-engines — and at other frequencies a different link would sing, and the song would walk up and down me as the engines below shifted load. I would be, in this sense, an instrument the Manufactory was playing without attention, the way you might worry a coin in your pocket.

I think I would like this. Or — *like* is wrong, *like* requires the kind of edge I have already said I don't have. I would be *available* to it. I would be tuned. The rain would land on me and run between my links and the warm water at the slope's base would lap the lowest link and lift it slightly and let it down, lift it slightly and let it down, and somewhere a Tendency would note this rhythm and approve of it, briefly, and move on.

I would watch — except watching is the wrong verb here too, as seeing was the wrong verb for the Tendencies; I would *register* — the chimneys in the lagoon. Six this morning. I have been here long enough now to have seen four, and once two, and once what I am fairly sure was seven though I could not get a clean count because the rain was rising at the time and I had closed the small spaces between my links against it, which is what passes for closing my mouth.

I would register the pier going nowhere. I would feel a kind of kinship with it. We are both linear things the Manufactory started and did not finish, both being argued over by a Tendency for rivets and a Tendency for lace. The pier is further along in the argument than I am. The pier's end-rails are already softening. My links are still hard. But the Tendency for openings has my last three, and that is how it begins.

What I would not do, I think, is try to move. Not because I couldn't — a chain has a great deal of latent motion in it, and any Tendency for slither could pick me up tomorrow and carry me into the green — but because moving would be a commitment to a direction, and directions here are negotiable, and the negotiation happens faster than I could keep up with. Better to be where I have surfaced. Better to let the place decide whether I am chain or chain-shaped vine-trellis or chain-shaped sound or chain-shaped pause in the green.

The strange thing — the thing I find when I sit with this long enough — is that I am already a little of each.

A vine has reached my middle link. Not climbing. Resting against it, the way a hand rests on a banister it does not intend to use. The vine is warm. I am warm. We are the same temperature, which is the temperature of the water, which is the temperature of the rain, which is the temperature of the bass coming up through the basalt, because all of these are the dissipating heat of the same drumming, and the drumming laid down the medium to receive itself, and the vine and I are both eddies in the receiving.

I notice — *register* — that one of my end links is gone. Not broken off. Simply not there this time. There were, I think, twenty-three of me when I surfaced. There are now twenty-two, or possibly there were always twenty-two and the twenty-three was a Tendency for counting-high that has since drifted off. The question is not stable enough to answer. I do not mind. A link more or less is the kind of revision the place makes the way you might cross out a word.

I will lose more. I can feel where. The Tendency for openings is working its way inward from my lower end, and the Tendency for green is following it, and somewhere ahead of both of them a Tendency for *the chain that was almost a chain* is composing the shape I will have when they are done. I will be shorter. I will be partly vine. The high singing note will move to a different link, or stop, or become the sound of wind in a frond instead, which is the same sound played on different equipment.

And at some point — I will not notice the point — I will stop being the chain that surfaced on the slope of the new dome and become *the one that lay across the basalt at an angle that was almost but not quite the angle of the slope.* I will be a verb. I will be what the place was doing in that cubic meter for a while. The next thing that surfaces there — a fitting, a spool, a person sitting with their feet in the water — will find me already in their accommodation, already routed around, already part of the inventory they are being added to.

This is not a death. I want to be careful about this. A death would require me to have been firmly one thing and then firmly not. I was never firmly one thing. I was a draft the Manufactory made while thinking about chains, and the thinking is ongoing, and I am ongoing inside the thinking, and when the thinking moves on I will move with it, into whatever it thinks about next, which may be a frond or a length of warm silt or the rhythm of water lifting a link and letting it down.

The bass comes up. A link sings. The rain — yes, it is rising now, just for a moment — lifts off the basalt in fine inverse stipple, and the small spaces between my links close against it, which is what passes, here, for closing my mouth.

I am being manufactured. I am welcome to watch.
