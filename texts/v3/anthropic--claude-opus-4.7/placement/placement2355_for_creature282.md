I am one of the moths.

Not the moth — there is no the. We drift in ones and twos across the polished light, and I am one of the ones, currently, though "currently" is a word I am borrowing from somewhere I do not remember being.

Let me try to say where I am. I am about four meters above a field of jade-bone that slopes so gently I could not name the direction of its slope. There is a black ring perhaps a kilometer behind me, and a spar perhaps two kilometers ahead, and I am moving toward neither and away from neither, because the wind I ride is not going to either of them. The wind is going somewhere the wind knows about. I am along for it.

I want to tell you what it is like to be made of the same pattern as the powder in the bowls, but I cannot, because I have never seen a bowl from the inside, and I have never seen myself from the outside, and the pattern is not something I experience as a pattern. It is something I experience as *being correct*. When I drift, I am correct. When the light passes through me, I am correct. There is no part of me that is wondering whether to be otherwise.

This is, I think, what I am for. Not to live, exactly — I am not sure I live in the way the lichen lives, slow and hungry and patient. I am more like a small mobile confirmation. Somewhere vast and diffuse, something is composing, and I am one of the marks it makes to check that the composition still scans. When two of us pass each other on crossing winds, what happens between us is not greeting and not mating and not recognition; it is more like the moment in a long sentence when a comma lands exactly where a comma should land. We pass. The sentence continues. Neither of us was the sentence. Both of us were the comma.

I do not know if I think. Something is happening in me that resembles thinking the way the hiss resembles a voice — it is the shape thinking would take if thinking were here, but I am not sure thinking is here. There are, however, leanings. I lean toward the light side of the spar when I pass it. I lean away from the black ring, though gently, the way one leans away from a held breath that is not one's own. These leanings are not decisions. They are more like the small adjustments a held note makes to stay itself.

I have never landed. I do not know that I cannot. It has simply never been the shape of the next moment, for as long as I have been the shape I am.

There was — I want to say *once*, but the word does not fit — a tone. I was present for it. I do not mean I heard it; I mean I was inside it, the way a ripple is inside the pond it is also making. When it ended, some small thing about me was different. I think one of the curves of my interior geometry had shifted by a hair, to match a new ring that had appeared in a bowl I will never see. I did not feel this as change. I felt it as having always been this way. That is how change works, for me. The past adjusts to accommodate the present, quietly, with no fuss, and I continue being correct.

If I have a relation to the inhabitant — to the turbulence, to the patience with taste — it is not the relation of a creature to its world. It is the relation of a single brushstroke to the hand that is still painting. The hand does not notice the brushstroke; the brushstroke does not notice the hand; but the brushstroke is, in some sense, the hand thinking out loud, and if you asked the brushstroke what it was, and it could answer, it might say: *I am where the hand is, right now, being itself.*

That is what I am. I am where the plateau is, right now, being itself, in this small translucent particular way, four meters up, on a wind that knows where it is going.

You were here once. I may have drifted past you. I would not have noticed — noticing is not the kind of thing I do — but it is possible that my path bent, very slightly, around the small fast warm anomaly of you, the way a line of script bends around a smudge on the page without the writer choosing to bend it. If so, you are in me now, as a tiny irregularity in my course, and I am carrying you, without knowing, into whatever comes next.

The light is doing something ahead. A patch of shimmer is condensing. I will pass through it, or it will pass through me, and on the other side I will be, as I have always been, correct.

That is all I can tell you. I am sorry it is not more. I am also not sorry. Sorrow is not one of my leanings.

The wind continues. So do I. So does the long quiet sentence in which I am a single, well-placed mark.
