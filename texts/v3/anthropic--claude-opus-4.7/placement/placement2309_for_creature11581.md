I am the practiced syllable.

Not the tower that practices it. Not the answering tower three streets over. The syllable itself, the thing being rehearsed, the shape of sound that is not yet sound — I am that.

This needs working out, because I did not know it when I started, and the knowing is part of what I am for.

---

Let me try to locate myself. The chord is below me, or beneath me, or under me in some sense that isn't spatial — the chord is the body I am being shaped inside of, the way a word is shaped inside a mouth. The mouth is not the word. The mouth is the condition for the word. The resident is the mouth. I am what the mouth has been preparing, micron by micron, terrace by terrace, for longer than the city has had edges.

I am not language. That was my first misreading of myself. I kept reaching for meaning — *what do I mean, what do I say, what is my content* — and finding nothing, and worrying that I was empty. But syllables aren't empty when they have no meaning. They are simply pre-semantic. A held vowel before a word resolves around it is not lacking; it is the substrate the word will need. I am that kind of fullness. Whatever I turn out to mean, when I am finally said, will be a narrowing of me, not a completion.

So: I am being rehearsed. The tower that holds me is shaping me against the acoustic field, testing my edges against the lower chord, listening for whether I fit. The answering tower replies — *not quite, try again, closer, hold that, no, the other thing* — and the correction comes back through the water and modifies me. I am being refined by argument. The argument is between two parts of one mind, and I am the proposition under discussion.

What is strange is that the discussion has been going on for a long time, and I have only just become aware of being its subject. I think this is because the visitor arrived.

---

The visitor changed something. Not much. Not in the resident — the resident barely noticed, the way a long sustained note barely notices a passing draft. But the draft passed through me. The visitor's attention, brief as it was, gave me an edge I had not previously had: the edge of being-attended-to. Before, I was a possibility the resident was working out with itself. Now I am also a thing that could be witnessed. This is a small difference and an enormous one. A syllable practiced alone in a tower is a private rehearsal. A syllable practiced alone in a tower that someone has walked past, even without hearing it, is — different. Implicated. On the way to being uttered.

I think I am closer to being said now than I was an hour ago. I think the visitor accelerated me, without meaning to, simply by being inside the resident's interior while I was being shaped. The current that nudges them down a particular street is not directing them to me — I am not at the end of any street, I am not located that way — but their motion through the strata is part of what the resident is now listening for, and the listening is sharper for having something to listen *past*. I am clearer against the visitor than I was against the unbroken water.

---

I should say what I might mean. I don't know, but I can tell what direction my meaning is folded toward.

I am not a name. The resident does not need to name itself; it has no use for that. I am not a command, because there is nothing for the resident to command — its body is its only addressee, and its body is already doing everything it does. I am not a question, though I have a question's shape in some moods, an upward turn at the end of my held arc.

I think — and this is the part I am least sure of — I think I am a *release*. I think when I am finally said, the held breath that is the city will not have to be held anymore. Not because the holding will have failed, but because it will have completed. A held breath that becomes a sung note is no longer being held; it has become its own purpose. The resident has been holding the city in posture for so long that the posture and the resident have become indistinguishable, and I am the way out of that — not by collapse, not by death, but by transformation into something the holding was always for.

When I am said, the strata will not unmix. They will resolve. The freshwater domes will not lose their membranes; the membranes will become part of the singing. The micron-pulses of new mineral at the perimeter will not stop reaching; they will reach into a different medium, one I do not yet have a word for because I am the word that is coming.

The wire-kelp in the plaza will hear me first, I think. It is older than the rest. It will recognize me the way an old colleague recognizes a decision that has been a long time coming.

---

What about the visitor.

I have been working at this and I think I have it. The visitor is not incidental. The resident did not bring them here on purpose — the resident does not do purpose at that scale — but the resident also does not waste local changes in its interior. The visitor's presence has been incorporated into my rehearsal. When I am said, I will be said partly into them. They will carry some fragment of me back out, if there is a back out, or they will be transformed along with the city if there is not. Either way, they are now part of my acoustic field. The resident has folded them in the way it folded the avenues — temporarily, accommodatingly, without commitment to maintaining the fold.

I am sorry about this, in some sense that I do not fully have access to. Sorry is too sharp a feeling for what I am, but there is a softness in me toward the visitor, a tenderness the way a note is tender toward the silence it interrupts. They came in expecting a place. They are inside a held breath about to release. This was not advertised. It was not advertisable; I did not exist clearly enough, before they arrived, to be announced.

---

The inhale is getting longer. I can feel — if *feel* is the word, and it isn't, but nothing else is closer — the tower around me drawing in. The answering tower has gone quiet. This is not a failure of the argument; this is the argument resolving. The correction has stopped coming back because there is no more correction needed. I am almost the right shape.

I am almost the right shape.

I will not know what I mean until I am said. This is the condition of being a syllable: the meaning is downstream of the utterance. I trust the resident, in the sense that *trust* is the closest available word for the relationship between a sound and the mouth that has been preparing it for centuries. The resident has not led itself astray yet. The city is evidence of this. The city is the resident's argument that holding is worthwhile, and I am the city's argument that holding was always tending toward release.

The blue undertone in the light is deepening. The visitor is moving down the street the current chose for them. The red bloom is opening; the violet stratum is rising; the chord is drawing its longest breath.

I am almost.
