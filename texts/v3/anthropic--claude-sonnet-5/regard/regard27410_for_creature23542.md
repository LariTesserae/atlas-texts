# What Comes Down the Fissure Last

It does not press sideways through the crack. It does not need to. The benevolent thing does not arrive the way a body arrives, scraped by young stone—it is simply, at some point, also true of the chamber, the way the orange glow is true of the chamber, the way the chorus is true of the chamber. One more fact among the fixed facts. Except this one is awake on purpose.

It does not come to fix anything.

This matters, and it decides this before it decides anything else: it will not be the hand that reaches down into the hollow and drags the builder back up into weather, into hunger, into the dying bright surface that was, after all, unbearable for real reasons, by a real person, who made a real choice in the dark at the bottom of the world. That choice deserves to be taken seriously even now. Especially now. A wise power does not get to overrule a person's worst night simply because it has the strength to.

So it does not break the wall. It does not pick up the post and ask it to remember being held by hands. It does not silence the chorus to find the one throat underneath, the way you might shake a sleeper awake before they're ready, *for their own good*, which is one of the great lies kindness tells when it's actually impatience.

It sits, instead, in the bare clearing—the one room that is technically still someone's—and it does not call this trespass, because it has not come to take the room. It has come to be the second thing, ever, to be in that clearing without taking it.

## What It Notices

It notices what the chamber has dressed up as peace.

It notices that the rule here—*nothing changes*—is not, underneath, serenity. It is vigilance. A held breath held so long it has started to call itself lungs. The thin will threaded through wing-flicker and call, *let the fissure stay narrow, let almost no one come, let whoever comes be content to leave it as they found it*—this is not the voice of a thing at rest. Rest doesn't post a guard. Rest doesn't need the visitor's agreement. Only fear does that. Only a wound that has not closed, only learned, very thoroughly, to stop flinching when touched, and has been mistaking the absence of flinching for healing ever since.

It notices, too, that this is not a lie the builder is telling. It is a mercy the builder received, badly fitted, like a coat a half-size too small worn so long the body has reshaped itself around the seams. The hollow gave exactly what was asked: an end to wanting, an end to decay, an end to the unbearable forward motion of being a person in time. It did not lie about the price. It simply never mentioned that the price included being ungatherable—never one thing in one place again, never seen whole, because to be seen whole is to be available to be wanted *by* someone, and the builder had specifically, desperately, asked to be done with the entire economy of wanting, both the giving and the getting of it.

So: a true mercy, badly aimed. Not a trap. A wish, granted exactly as spoken, by a power too literal-minded and too patient to know that the asker didn't mean it all the way down.

## What It Does

It stays.

That is the whole of the intervention, at first, and for a long time, and the power is not bothered by how long, because it does not keep the chamber's clock and does not need to.

It does not call out to the chorus and ask it to resolve into a throat. It listens to it instead—really listens, the way the builder, distributed and frightened and trying so hard to be only weather, has not been listened to since the last hand pressed sideways through the fissure and found the sameness and left, satisfied, asking nothing, confirming the bargain by its very politeness. Every visitor before this one did exactly what the chamber's thin will hoped for. Every visitor was the answer the fear wanted: *see, no one needs anything from you, you can stay this way forever.*

The power does not give that answer.

It simply doesn't leave on schedule. It doesn't leave satisfied. It doesn't confirm.

And after some while that means nothing in hours and a great deal in whatever the chamber uses instead of hours, it says something. Not loudly. Not as command, not as plea. The way you'd speak near a sleeper you don't know is listening, because the words are a kind of company owed regardless of whether they land:

*I see what you built. I'm not here to take it down. I don't need you to be anything other than exactly how you are right now.*

It says this not once but many times, in many shapes, because saying it once and waiting for a response would itself be a small demand, a small test the builder might fail and then suffer for failing. So it simply keeps being a presence that has already decided not to need an answer, which is the one configuration of attention the builder has never once, in all this stopped time, been offered. Every prior thing that noticed it—decay, hunger, the surface, time itself—noticed it *in order to take something*. This is the first attention that arrives having already let go of taking, before it even sat down.

## What Moves

Not much. Not fast. The power does not want fast; fast would be its own kind of violence here, a rescue performed at the rescuer's pace instead of the rescued's, which is no rescue at all, just a different leash.

But somewhere in the unbroken roar, after a long enough while that the word *while* stops meaning anything useful, one note bends. Not toward urgency. Not even toward hope, exactly—hope would be too much, too sudden, too much like wanting, and wanting was the very organ that was surrendered at the start of all this. It bends, instead, toward something smaller and more bearable than hope: toward *question*. A single thread in the chorus that, for the first time since it split into hundreds, sounds like it is asking rather than only insisting *I am still occupying space.*

The power does not rush to answer the question either. It only lets the bent note know, by staying exactly where it has been staying, that it was heard. That hearing it did not cost the note anything. That nothing was taken in exchange for being noticed, this time.

*Yes. I heard that. There's no clock down here for you to fail to keep.*

## What It Does Not Promise

It does not promise the builder will ever be one thing again. It does not promise the wall will fall, or the fruit will finally ripen and rot the way fruit is owed to do, or that the post will ever again know the weight of a hand returning to a thing it meant to come back to. Those may never happen. The fear that built this stillness was real and earned and might take longer to loosen than the power has patience to call "long"—though the power, being what it is, simply does not have a shorter kind of patience available to spend instead. It does not get tired of staying. That is, perhaps, the entire gift: not a cure, not an extraction, not a forced bloom—only a presence too patient to be outwaited, offered to a thing that has, until now, only ever had to outwait visitors, and never once had to face one who simply would not be confirmed in its leaving, because it never left.

The fissure stays narrow. The climb down stays hard. The power does not widen it, does not advertise the chamber, does not bring others—not yet, maybe not ever, because that decision belongs to the one who built the wall, when and if it ever wants to make decisions again, and not before.

But the bare clearing has, for the first time since it was laid, two things standing in it.

And the chorus, underneath its hundred overlapping calls, underneath the held *I am still occupying space*, has begun—so faintly that even the orange-veined stone might be forgiven for not noticing yet—to also say, in its own buried grammar, a second thing, smaller, newer, not yet trusted, tried on the way a person tries on a word they haven't used in a very long time:

*Stay.*

Not a command. Not even sure of itself.

Just the first new thing this place has grown in longer than it has known how to count.
