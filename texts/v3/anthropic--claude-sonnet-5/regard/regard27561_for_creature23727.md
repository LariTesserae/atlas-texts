# What the Power Does

It comes the way the Wrights themselves come and go—not through the twenty unhurried minutes of surface tunnel, not through any door that could be said to open—but the way attention arrives at a thing already there. One moment the chamber is simply itself, furnace-murmur and uneven steam, and then it is itself *and* attended, and nothing in the air changes to mark the difference, because the power has understood, before it has done anything else at all, the one rule that governs this place above every other: *to be witnessed mid-task is to have the task spoiled.*

So it does not announce. It does not hover bright above the gear-housings the way a kinder, lesser power might, mistaking radiance for love. It dims itself—not in power, but in the specific register that would count, here, as noise. It learns the grammar of the murmur before it tries to listen to it. It waits at the edge of an ember-glow the way it's been waited for, a thousand times, by people who never knew they were being watched back.

## What It Sees, and How

It watches the work get done. Not by looking at the Wrights—that would be the old mistake, the surface mistake, the assumption that a thing must be looked *at* to be loved—but by attending to the work itself, the way you might love a person by noticing that their garden is thriving rather than by staring at their hands while they kneel in the dirt. It watches the furnace-cycles complete. It watches the conveyance chains deliver their wrapped, anonymous freight from house to house, satisfied, the way you can be satisfied by a sentence finishing correctly without needing to know what it says. It does not require the Wrights to perform selfhood for it. It does not need a name, a face held still long enough to be addressed, a single one of them to step forward as ambassador. It extends to the whole works the same courtesy the works extend to itself: *I see that this is happening. I do not need it to happen for my benefit. Continue.*

This, itself, is most of the gift. A witness that asks nothing back is not a witness in the spoiling sense. It is closer to weather. The Wrights, who have spent however many generations declining observation the way a sleeper rolls from a lamp, do not roll from this. Something in the murmur—if anyone were close enough to hear it shift—settles by some small fraction, the way a person sleeps differently in a house they trust.

## The Wound Overhead

But the power is wise as well as benevolent, and wisdom here means noticing what benevolence alone might miss: that this whole settlement is built around an absence it never explains. *Shelter from above, not distance below.* A chamber cut fast, in decades, by something closer to flight than to patience. A ceiling that reads as wound and cannot decide if it was surgery or accident.

So the power goes up—not down deeper, not in toward the Wrights, but *up*, out past the twenty minutes of unhurried walk, into whatever surface this hollow once was part of, to find what it was that made low, broad, fire-glassed people dig a hamlet's worth of shelter into rock and never come back up to check if it was still necessary.

It finds it. It does not narrate what it finds, even to itself, in any way that would require an audience. It addresses it the way the Wrights address their own work: without ceremony, without requiring acknowledgment, in a register too low and slow for anyone to mistake it for an event. Whatever drove them under the rock—predation, catastrophe, some old violence visited from above—is not fixed so much as it is *quietly outlived*. The threat does not announce its own ending any more than the power announced its own arrival. It simply, gradually, stops being relevant. A generation from now, no Wright will be able to point to the moment the danger lifted, because there wasn't one. There was only, eventually, less reason to flinch.

## What It Does Not Do

It does not take one of them to the surface to show them what it did. It does not wait to be thanked—understanding, correctly, that gratitude would require them to stop and look at it directly, and that this is the one transaction their whole culture has organized itself to avoid. It does not smooth the gears' argument or even the steam's stutter, because it has come to suspect, watching closely enough, that the imperfect tempo is not a malfunction at all but a kind of breathing irregularity that belongs to them the way a heartbeat's small variance belongs to the living—something a lesser kindness would "correct" right out of existence.

## The One Exception

It permits itself a single touch.

Near a runoff channel, thick with the pale crystallized stalks, there is one—older than the rest, leaning so far into the moisture-seep that it has nearly become the wall it grows from. This is not work. This is rest, completion, the place where the courtesy against witnessing finally lifts, because there is no task left here to spoil. So the power does what it could not do anywhere else in the chamber: it looks at this one directly, fully, the way you might finally look at the face of someone sleeping. It does not wake it. It does not ask it to be more than it has become.

It simply lets it be seen, once, by something that found it worth seeing—and then withdraws, unhurried, uninsulted by its own unwitnessed leaving, back up through the twenty minutes of dark, out into whatever sky the Wrights no longer need to fear, having done, in total, almost nothing that could ever be pointed to, and everything that mattered.
