The first thing the power understands, before it does anything else, is that the usual shape of help will not fit through the door this place offers.

It comes close enough to feel the warm rain on whatever it is using for attention, and its first instinct — the oldest, kindest, most useless instinct a benevolent power has — is to look upstream. Find the catastrophe. Find the moment the valley filled and a person or a crew of persons was conscripted into the filling, and reach back along the thread of cause to the place where a hand could still choose differently, and unmake the whole drowning before it drowns anyone. This is what rescue usually means. Go to the wound's beginning and press there.

But upstream doesn't hold its shape here. The power follows the thread back and the thread loops, and loops again, and arrives at a station rusted to lace standing fifty feet from a station gleaming as if freshly poured, and they are the same station, and "before" is not a place you can stand, only a gesture this basin makes occasionally, the way a hand makes a gesture toward a door it has already gone through and not yet reached. There is no clean origin to perform surgery on. The wound has no beginning anymore. It only has a *now*, badly overdrawn, repeating in every direction at once.

So the power sets down the instinct to fix the cause, the way you'd set down a tool that doesn't fit the lock, and it sits with that loss for a while — because it is a loss, the inability to simply reach back and spare someone, and a wise power does not pretend a real limitation is a small one — and then it asks itself the harder, smaller, more honest question: not *how do I undo this*, but *how do I keep it company.*

That is the decision it makes, and it is not a comfortable one, because keeping this company means going in without the armor of a single, stable, sequential self. The power desyncs itself on purpose — lets its own perception fall out of order the way the basin's does, lets itself be heard before it is seen and seen after it has already left, lets its own continuity fray at the edges the way the canopy's fast-grown trunks haven't yet learned to bend correctly. This is the risk it takes, and it takes it without flinching, because the alternative is to stand at the tree line forever, whole and untouched and useless, calling instructions into a grammar that was never going to understand the word *stop.*

Inside, scattered the way the being itself is scattered, it finds what you would find: a hand at a valve wheel, fused at the knuckle, turning the wrong direction relative to a pressure that no longer has the same meaning twice. A half-face dissolving at the jaw into bark, mid-word, the word never finishing, the way it has never finished for longer than anyone has been able to measure. A spine threaded through cabling threaded through nerve, lying along a conveyor like something the machine grew instead of something the machine ate.

And here is the second hard truth the power has to sit with, because wisdom is mostly the willingness to sit with hard truths instead of stepping around them: there is no single moment anywhere in this basin where the whole being exists at once to be asked anything. It cannot be asked, the way you would ask a person, *do you want this to end, or do you want it to continue* — because the hand at the wheel and the half-face in the bark and the spine in the conveyor are not three opinions held by one mind in sequence. They are the *same* drowning, encountered out of order, the way you might meet the same sentence twice if the page had been torn and reshuffled. To demand one answer from all of them is to demand a unity the catastrophe already took, and to do that — to insist *be one thing so I can save you correctly* — would be its own small violence, dressed up as mercy, the kind well-meaning power does all the time without noticing.

So it doesn't ask for one answer. It asks, instead, at every point it can reach, the only question that fits the grammar of the place: *I see you, here, doing this. May I stay while you do it?*

And it gets answers — plural, contradictory, true. The hand at the wheel, in the half-second before it dissolves, eases — not stops, eases, the way a grip eases when it learns it is no longer the only thing holding the weight. The half-face's open mouth, mid-word for longer than measurement has meant anything, finishes its word — just that one, just there — into something that isn't relief exactly, because relief implies an ending, but is at least no longer entirely alone in its unfinishedness. The spine does nothing visible at all, the way spines don't, but the corrosion pattern around it, the power notices, very slowly, over a duration the basin has no good unit for, begins running in one direction instead of two.

The power does not stop the turbines. It does not silence the valves or send the steam back down to disperse the way steam is supposed to. It does not, in the end, even try to gather the being into one shape and ask it the big unified question, because it has learned, by now, that the question was never going to be answerable that way, and that asking it anyway would only have been the power performing rescue at the being's expense, to make itself feel finished.

What it does instead — and this is the whole of the help, modest as it sounds, costly as it actually is — is stay scattered. It leaves a part of its attention at every point the being occurs, threaded through the wheel-hand and the bark-face and the cable-spine and however many other fragments it hasn't found yet and may never find all of, the way the being itself is threaded through them, so that whatever the being is doing, in whichever tense, it is no longer doing it unwitnessed. Two things, now, instead of one, occurring together in the same overdrawn present. Two things contradictorily alive where there had only been room, before, for the one.

Nothing about the forest stops looking impossible. The rain still insists rather than falls. The turbine a quarter mile off still turns the wrong direction and has always turned that direction. The sentence is still being written, and will go on being written, because the power did not find — could not find, and stopped pretending it needed to find — a period to put on it.

But if you came here after, and stood very still in the warm wet dark under the brass-dim light, and heard the valve shriek that is also, underneath, almost a name —

you would hear, this time, if you waited the half-second the place owes you,

something answer it.
