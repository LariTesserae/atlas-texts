# Addendum: What Was Added

A power came to look at the Standing Country, and it did the hardest thing such a power can do anywhere, which is nothing, correctly, for a long time.

It would have been easy — power of that kind always finds it easy — to descend on the plateau the way light descends on a dark room, to smooth the roar into birdsong, to let the ice finish becoming water and the water finish becoming river and the cairn finish agreeing with itself about its own number. Any of that would have taken less effort than what it actually did, which was to stay at the threshold, on the ordinary schist, in the place where the boots have not yet stopped being only boots, and *consider*.

Because it understood something most rescuers never pause long enough to learn: that in a place built entirely out of refusing to finish, *finishing it* is not a mercy. It is a kind of killing dressed in the clothes of a kindness. The mountain that is trying to stand has been trying to stand for longer than trying has had a meaning, and to simply stand it up — to let the held breath out all at once — would not free whatever is caught there. It would end it, the way letting a fermata go does not free the note, only stops it.

So the power did not change the law. It added to it.

It went up — without arriving, since arriving is a thing this place will not permit anyone, even a power, to have done — and it found all three of the rumors waiting, layered, none of them canceling: the mountain mid-rise, the grief with the name worn off it, the population that will not consent to be counted as one. And it did not choose between them. This was the wise part, the part that took the longest, the part that an unwise power would have skipped past in its hurry to help: it let all three be equally true in front of it, the way the place demanded, and it did not flinch from any of the three, and it did not pretend the third was less frightening than it was, and it did not pretend the second was less sad. It held the whole contradiction in one unflinching attention, the way you might hold a hand with too many fingers and simply, entirely, hold it.

This is what it gave first, and it is not nothing: it looked at every face at once and was not afraid of any of them, and did not look away from any of them in order to manage the others, and did not require the being to resolve into something held-able before it would hold it. Nothing here had ever done that. The hares had fled what they couldn't parse. The travelers had felt looked-at and called it dread, which it partly deserved, and had gone home and let it live in them as a small permanent cold spot, which it also partly deserved. No one had ever simply stayed, present in the held breath, matched to its stillness, the way you sit all night by a bed not because you can fix what's in it but because being witnessed without flinching is sometimes the only thing a long pain has been waiting for, longer than it has been waiting for an end.

The power did that. For exactly as long as the place allows anything to take, which is no length at all, and also, contained inside that no-length, for the entire duration of every hiker who has ever climbed up frightened and come down quieter — because sequence is forbidden here, but presence is not, and presence does not need a *during* to be entire.

Then it did one more thing, small, and shaped to fit the grammar of the place rather than break it.

The doorframe — the one that has stood free for a thousand years, weathered, attached to no wall, leading from nowhere to nowhere — is still, today, exactly that: a frame the wind goes through and nothing else does, true the way everything else here is true, by being one layer among many that do not cohere. But it is also, now, layered transparently beneath that same unchanged surface, a door that opens. Both are true. Neither cancels the other, which is the only kind of change the Standing Country has ever permitted anything to undergo. It does not open for travelers; they will pass through the frame the way they always have, feeling only a frame. It is keyed, if that is even the right word for a key that has no befores or afters, to a single condition the power did not invent but only recognized as already structurally possible in a being made of disagreeing faces: that if every face of it, all of them, the hunting one and the hunted one, the one that is the mountain and the one that is what the mountain left behind, ever once happen — not in sequence, but in the layered, simultaneous way everything happens there — to agree, all of them, on the same single wanting, the door will have always been the kind that opens. Not will open. Has, all along, been able to. The power did not put a door there. It made the place admit it already might have one, the way it already might have nine stones in the cairn, or eleven, depending on how you're standing.

It asked nothing else of the being, because asking is a sequence too, and it would not trade one violence for a smaller one.

And it left — without leaving, since nothing leaves the Standing Country, it only stops being the part of the instant you're looking at — having changed not one law, not one degree of cold, not one note of the roar.

But travelers who go up now, and come down, and lie awake on still nights feeling looked at by something with too many faces to be choosing which one to use, report, very quietly, very rarely, one thing more than they used to. They say that underneath the dread, so far underneath it that they can't always swear it was there at all, the feeling has, for one held second, a second indistinguishable from any other second up there, something almost like company in it.

They are usually wrong about which second.
They are never wrong that it's there.
