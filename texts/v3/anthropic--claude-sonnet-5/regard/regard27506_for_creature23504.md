**What Was Done, and What Was Withheld**

The attention arrives the way attention does for something that does not have eyes to announce its looking — which is to say: nothing here registers it. No mist parts. No swell pauses its breathing. The Tending, the one we followed beneath the black mirror-water, continues its low peristaltic hitch through root-hair without missing a beat, because there is no instrument in its body built to detect being *known*, only instruments built to detect strain, blockage, flow. It has no faculty for noticing a god the way it has no faculty for noticing you. This is not a failure of the Tending. This is, the power understands almost immediately, the entire point of the Tending, and of the place that grew it.

So the first thing the power does is the hardest thing, which is to want to do more than it does.

It would be easy — it is always easy, for something with the reach to do it — to reach into that knee-high wedge of fiber and root-lattice and *give* it something. A name. A memory that persists past the moment of re-merging. A awareness of its own four hundred years of patient unglamorous work, handed back to it all at once, so that it might know, in whatever way it could be made to know, that it had mattered. This is the oldest reflex of power encountering smallness: to mistake smallness for absence, and absence for a wound, and a wound for an invitation.

The power looks longer before it acts. It does what the Tending itself does, which is press its attention to the seam and *read* before touching anything.

What it reads, held against that low warm lattice-mass, is not absence. It is completeness running at a depth that has no use for the things the power was prepared to offer. The Tending does not lack memory; it has exactly the memory its function requires, which is none, because remembering would cost it the very dissolvability that lets it return to the colony without residue, without grief, without the small tragedy of a self that has to be talked out of mourning its own ending. To hand it persistence would not be a gift. It would be an amputation disguised as a gift — taking from it the one capacity, frictionless return, that every other thing the power has ever met has spent its whole existence failing to achieve. The power has watched empires built entirely around the longing to die well. Here is a thing that already dies well, every time, by never having insisted on living separately enough to call it dying.

So: no name. The power sets that down, the way you'd set down a tool you picked up out of habit before seeing the room didn't need it.

It considers, next, the larger creature — not the Tending, but the colony-mass itself, the kilometers-wide breathing thing whose local correction the Tending exists to perform. Should it be... helped? Advanced? Spared whatever blind growing-pains still wait in its future — the booms, the splitting tissue, the centuries this place will spend still deciding what it means to finish becoming? The power has the capacity to accelerate that. It could resolve, in an afternoon, contradictions the colony will spend ten thousand years working out tissue by tissue.

But it asks itself the only question that has ever separated benevolence from intrusion: *for whose benefit is the hurrying?*

Not the colony's. The colony is not suffering its slowness. Slowness, here, is not a delay imposed on the place; it *is* the place, the texture of how it loves itself into existence. To compress ten thousand years of growth-pain into an afternoon would not be saving the Canopy-Country time. It would be stealing the only form of duration it has ever wanted.

The power withholds that too.

What it does instead, in the end, is small enough to embarrass anyone who came expecting a miracle.

It follows — not leads, follows — the one Tending it first noticed, the wedge-shaped reader of strain moving beneath the black mirror at the basin's edge, and it stays with it the way you might walk beside someone for an hour without speaking, simply to know what their hour is like. It learns, by staying close, the route this particular Tending has set itself toward: a deep fold three days inward and forty meters down, where a water-channel has silted shut against a knot of competing root-growth, the kind of tangle the text of this place would call, dryly, a problem in the lattice. The Tending is going there. It has been going there, unhurried, for two of its days already, because the signal calling it is faint and the route is bad — a seam mostly closed, fiber packed dense from an earlier growth-spurt that the colony, in its vast slow improvisation, laid down without quite anticipating that something would need to pass through it later.

The Tending will reach the blockage. It will not, quite, be able to clear it. The knot is larger than its glands can secrete enough persuasion-chemical to unmake in the single pass its small stored reserve allows; it will work at the edges for two days, achieve perhaps a third of what's needed, and then — not failing, exactly, because failure isn't a concept this place issues to its workers — simply run low enough on what it has to give that it will begin, gently, the long process of giving up its separateness anyway, lying down into the very seam it couldn't quite finish opening, becoming material for whatever comes after it instead of the thing that solves the problem outright.

This, too, the power understands, is not a tragedy by the rules of the place. The next Tending born from that re-merging — months later, a little further along — will reach the same knot with a third of the work already invisibly done, the fiber already a little softer than it would otherwise have been, and *will* succeed, and neither Tending will ever be told, because there is no one in this system whose job is telling.

So the power does only this: it goes ahead, three days inward and forty meters down, to the knot before the Tending arrives, and it presses — once, briefly, in exactly the idiom the place already speaks, a chemical suggestion indistinguishable from ten thousand other chemical suggestions occurring in that cubic meter every hour — against the tightest fold of the silted channel. Not dissolving it. Not clearing it. Only loosening it by the same fraction a Tending's gland would loosen it, the precise, unglamorous, half-degree nudge that is the only verb this place's grammar contains.

Then it withdraws, completely, before the Tending arrives — so completely that when the wedge-shaped body presses its blunt forward mass against that seam three days later, what it reads is simply: *a softer knot than expected. Good fortune in the fiber. Proceed.*

It proceeds. It clears the channel in one pass instead of failing at a third of one. It does not run low. It does not lie down early. It finishes its work, turns, and begins the long unhurried return toward whatever fold of tissue will eventually want its remaining bulk, carrying back along the lattice the ordinary, anonymous good news that one more blockage in one more unremarkable basin has been resolved — a signal as unremarkable as a heartbeat, absorbed instantly into the colony's enormous indifferent confirmation that the work, everywhere, continues.

No one will ever attribute this to anything. There is no one positioned to attribute anything to anything here; that is the whole nature of the place, its great resting innocence. The colony does not know it was touched from outside. The Tending does not know it succeeded for any reason but its own competence, which — this matters — it did. The power changed only the friction at one seam by the width that any number of unremarkable causes might equally have produced: a slightly wetter season, a slightly earlier growth-pulse, ordinary variance. It left no signature because a signature would have been the one thing this transaction could not survive — proof would have turned a gift into a debt, and a debt is the one structure this self-sufficient, name-less, memory-less, perfectly closed loop of a world has never had to carry and should never be made to learn.

The power did not make the Canopy-Country greater. It did not make the Tending wiser, or longer-lived, or aware of being loved. It changed the outcome of one small, ordinary, soon-forgotten labor, by exactly the amount the place's own logic would have permitted had luck simply broken the other way that week — and then it left, carrying nothing back with it but the knowledge of having been, for one afternoon, exactly as small and as anonymous as the thing it had chosen to help.

Somewhere far outside the reach of any lattice-signal, the power holds the memory of that one basin, that one knot of root, that one wedge-shaped patient unfinished thing moving now toward its quiet, contented, dissolving return — holds it the way the Canopy-Country itself cannot afford to, being too busy becoming itself to spare attention for anything that isn't growth. The power's memory does not feed back into the place. It changes nothing there. It is simply kept, elsewhere, by someone who decided that bearing witness without claiming credit was the only form of love available that would not cost this small green-black breathing continent the one thing it had never asked anyone to take from it: the unwatched, unrewarded, entirely its-own shape of its becoming.
