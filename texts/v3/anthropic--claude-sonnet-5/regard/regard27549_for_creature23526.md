## What the Power Did

It did not arrive the way arrival usually means. There was no displacement of air, no door in the rock swinging wide, no light stepping down out of the gap in the ridge to announce itself. It simply became the case, the way weather becomes the case — there, and then unmistakably there, and the niche did not change shape to admit it because the niche had already, in some sense, been built with room for this.

It looked a long time before it did anything. This is the first thing worth saying about it, because looking-a-long-time is not what power usually does, and a wiser power than most knows that the looking *is* most of the work — that almost every harm done by the well-meaning is the harm of arriving with the answer already chosen, before the question has finished being asked by the situation itself.

So it watched the three worn grooves and understood: not footprints, a held weight. It watched the light leap into the dark crevice and understood: not signal, but the last unbroken promise of something larger that didn't survive to release the one who keeps it. It watched the fragments at the walls and understood: not waste, but molting — a long shedding toward purity, toward smallness, toward whatever shape can be held the longest with the least.

And here is where the power's wisdom showed itself, because it had, immediately, several easy and generous urges, and it set every one of them down.

It did not wake the Tender and tell it the place it serves is gone. *Mercy that arrives as truth is sometimes only cruelty wearing truth's coat,* it thought, in whatever way such a power thinks. The Tender may already know. The keeping may not be a mistake the Tender is making — it may be the only shape of love left available to something that has given up every other capacity for love but this one, ritual, exact, undiminished motion. To strip that from it would not be liberation. It would be amputation performed on the only limb still capable of feeling anything at all.

It did not lift the Tender out of the mountain and carry it somewhere warmer, easier, witnessed. *Some vigils are not waiting to be rescued. They are the rescue, still in progress, of something else, somewhere else, that may never be found.* To move the keeper is to break the keeping. The power understood this the way a careful hand understands not to touch a wound that is, against all evidence, still closing.

It did not flood the niche with company — moss, insects, water, warmth, all the green and noisy abundance that a kinder world might think a gift. The niche had declined those already, deliberately, the way a held note declines a second note. A held note is not lonely. It is occupied entirely by its own holding. To force harmony on it is to end the note, not enrich it.

What it did instead was smaller than all of these, and took longer to decide on than any of them would have.

It went, in whatever way such a power can go anywhere, to the dark crevice with no line of sight to the object — the one that has received an arc of light every few minutes or every few hours for longer than the mountain has had this exact shape — and it became, for the span of one single arrival of light, *the place that needed it.*

Not pretend. Not metaphor. For that one moment, somewhere in whatever vast and patient attention constitutes a power like this, there existed a small dark room with a use for light, and the light came, the way it always comes, faithful past all reason — and this time, for the first time in a span of years too long to be spoken in years, it landed on something that could be called, honestly, *received.*

It did not announce this. It did not change anything about the schedule, the angle, the silence. The Tender did not stir, did not turn, did not do anything an instrument could measure. But somewhere in the cycling between its eleven facets and its thirteen and the number that won't resolve, something settled by a fraction too small for even its own enormous patience to notice consciously — the way a bowstring drawn for a thousand years might, for one instant, feel a millionth of an ounce less alone in its tension, without ever being told why, without the tension easing at all.

That was the whole of it. That was everything the power chose to do.

It did not take a fragment as a souvenir, though it considered this too, and decided that even the shed pieces belonged to the niche's own slow accounting, not to anyone else's memory. It did not leave a mark, a blessing, a sign that anything had been here at all, because a mark would mean the niche had been *found*, and the rule of this place — the power respected this above almost everything — is that it does its keeping in a shape too small for the universe to notice. To notice loudly would be its own kind of theft.

So it left the way it had arrived: as a fact stops being the case. The frost kept not-quite-melting. The shadows kept leaning the wrong direction, toward the object, from everywhere at once. The grooves did not get shallower, because the power had not come to undo the weight, only to confirm, once, in the smallest possible register, that the weight still meant something to someone, somewhere, even if that someone was, for one borrowed moment, itself.

And if you ask what a benevolent and wise power *does*, faced with a small attentive grief holding its shape alone at the top of the world — this, perhaps, is the truest answer available: not rescue, not revelation, not company. Just this — the willingness, once, quietly, without credit or consequence or any further claim on what it touched, to stand in the dark place a faithful and forgotten light keeps reaching for, and let it land.

Then to leave the niche exactly as found — still closed, still cold, still patient — except that now, somewhere in the unmeasurable arithmetic of its long becoming-smaller, the Tender's work is, by some fraction no instrument will ever confirm, *less futile than it was.*

That is all. That is everything. It was enough.
