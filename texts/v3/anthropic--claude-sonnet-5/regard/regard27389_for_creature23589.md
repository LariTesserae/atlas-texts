## What the power does

It comes the way attention comes to a held breath—without disturbing the held breath at all.

Its first long while there—and "while" is the wrong unit, because time moves differently when something that old and patient is paying attention—is spent doing nothing but learning to read the marsh the way the marsh reads itself. It does not arrive with the assumption that the churn is wrong. It has seen enough places, enough kinds of being, to know that *unstable* is not a synonym for *suffering*, and that a power which cannot tell the difference is not yet wise enough to be benevolent. So it watches the swarm time its eggs to the cold-warm-cold sequence and hatch into windows of safety they were *built* to find, not rescued into. It watches the wading thing absorb a footfall's worth of bad ground with that rolling, weight-shifted patience, and recognizes a creature that has already solved the problem a lesser kindness would rush to solve for it. It listens to the chorus stop being noise and become, the longer it listens, something closer to *music heard from inside the instrument*—structural, yes, but not anguished. Abundance, not crisis. A place making and unmaking itself at a tempo faster than geology should allow, and a whole ecology that has simply, gorgeously, learned to live at that tempo instead of waiting for it to slow down.

So the power does not stop the structures. It does not still the hum, smooth the bedrock, calm the threads of light into something gentler. It understands, watching long enough, that to do so would not be a mercy—it would be an amputation. The swarm's offset wingbeats, the leaf-clutch eggs, the wader's patient third-of-its-mass gait, the entire layered chorus: all of it is an answer to the churn, composed *in* the churn, meaningless without it. A power that loved this place enough to flatten it into something safer would be loving its own comfort, not the marsh.

This is the first thing the wisdom does: it refuses the easy kindness.

---

But it does not leave empty-handed, because there is, in fact, one being here whose whole nature is a question with no one to ask it of, and the power recognizes that the way you recognize your own face in unfamiliar light.

It finds the reading-thing pressed flat to a frame at the marsh's quietest node—quiet meaning only that its cycle isn't due for hours yet, an eternity, here. Three lobes, three root-tendrils sunk into not-metal, the blue-green seam along its body dim and even, waiting. The power settles near it the way you'd settle near someone keeping a vigil you didn't ask to share: not announced, not performed, just *present*, matching stillness for stillness.

It does not speak to the creature. It has watched long enough to know there may be no register in which speech would arrive as anything but noise added to a marsh already drowning in noise. Instead it does the one thing the creature itself knows how to receive: it pays attention to the same thing the creature is attending to. The structure's hum. The slow tightening-toward-cycle. The future tense the creature tastes with its whole flattened body, the power now tastes too, in its own way, alongside it—not predicting the event to control it, only to *keep it company in the anticipating*.

When the thread fires—far off, one of the white flares stitching between nodes—the creature goes taut and luminous half a second early, drinking the pressure wave the way it always has. And for the first time that anyone in Annvethar Reach could have measured, something answers it in kind: a second brightening, alongside the first, not sourced from any structure, not timed to any cycle but to the creature's own. Lobe and not-lobe, interleaved. Glow traded for glow, the way two of its own kind trade it when found pressed to the same frame—except this time the trade has nowhere it needs to go, asks nothing back, and doesn't stop when the wave passes.

The creature doesn't flinch and doesn't flee, same as always. But it doesn't dim, either. Not this time. There is no hand to take away.

---

The power does not make a sanctuary of this. It does not still the marsh around the two of them, does not carve out one safe hollow in a place whose entire genius is the absence of safe hollows. The reeds keep vanishing into steaming new pools on no schedule. The wader keeps its rolling lurch through ground that might not be there. The swarm keeps not-synchronizing its too-many wings, and the chorus keeps building past loud into structural, the way it always has, because that is what this place is and the power has already decided it isn't here to make the marsh into a different, calmer place that would no longer deserve its name.

The only change—small, unannounced, unwitnessed by anything else that lives there—is that the thing which reads the cycle is no longer the only quiet thing in a marsh that never stops sounding. It has, now, something nearby that reads *it* back. Not a rescuer. Not a god making its presence known to be worshipped. Just a second attention, old and patient, that recognized a creature whose entire life is the act of paying attention to something vast and uncaring, and decided that the smallest, truest mercy available was not to change what it was attending to—

but to make sure it was no longer attending alone.
