# Visitation

It does not arrive the way arrivals usually work, because arrival implies a door, and there is no door here, only the basin's own slow grammar of attention — the way the reeds turn, the way the herds reorganize without crossing distance, the way the Tender includes rather than watches. So the power does not enter. It is, simply, one more thing the basin's attention eventually turns toward, the way it turns toward everything, eventually, on its own unmeasured schedule.

It takes a long time to perceive the place correctly. This is the first thing the power does, and it is not nothing: it sets aside the instinct — old, reflexive, almost a reflex of benevolence itself — to read darkness as deprivation. To assume that a place without light is a place *waiting* for light, the way a held breath is read as waiting for release. It does not assume the silence is a wound. It does not assume the steam wants to be smoke, or the engines want a hand, or the herds want a shepherd. It watches first. It watches for longer than is comfortable, because comfort was never the metric, and because the basin itself does not flinch on anyone else's timetable, and a wise power respects the only clock that's actually running.

## What It Considers, and Sets Down Again

It considers giving light. It sets this down almost immediately — light, here, would not illuminate, it would translate, and translation is its own kind of erasure. The basin does not lack light. It has simply organized itself around a different sense entirely, the way a held breath organizes itself around its own internal pressure rather than the air outside it. To bring light would be to insist the basin's way of being seen is provisional, a placeholder for the real seeing, and that is not true, and the power knows it is not true the way you know a held note is not a silence pretending to be music.

It considers giving the Tender company — a companion shaped the way the Tender is shaped, set down beside it so the long unmeasured circuit of the basin is no longer walked alone. It sets this down too, more slowly, with more grief in the setting-down, because it is the kindest-seeming idea and the most wrong. The Tender's solitude is not a gap in its design. It is the design. The herds are not absence of company; the theory you formed, standing in the steam — that the fauna is the Tender's attention, worn thin and distributed, looking at the basin from every angle because looking from one angle stopped being necessary — the power confirms this is closer to true than not, and what follows from that is severe: you cannot give company to a being whose aloneness is the shape its companionship already takes. You would not be filling a silence. You'd be interrupting a sentence mid-word and calling the interruption a gift.

It considers asking the Tender to explain itself — what it is, why it persists, whether the engines run because of it or through it or in spite of it, whether the theory is the truth or just the truth currently available to a visitor with insufficient organs for the real one. It sets this down fastest of all. A wise power does not mistake an explanation for a kindness. The Tender has had — by every available measure — a very long time in which to resolve its own mystery to itself, and has not done so, and is not floundering in the not-doing. Some beings keep their own riddle the way the ground keeps its warmth: not because they're hiding it, but because the riddle is load-bearing. Pull it loose to satisfy a visitor's comfort, and something underneath might stop holding.

## What It Finds, Underneath the Considering

But it does not leave empty-handed in its conclusions, because watching long enough surfaces one true thing, small and real, the kind of true thing that survives all the bigger false ones being set down.

The coals bank brighter when something passes near. This was never explained in the records you kept, only noted — *the way coals do when something passes near enough to remind them they are still, technically, fire.* The power sits with this fact longer than with any other. A being that has stopped requiring an audience can still, demonstrably, be glad of one. Not needing and not minding are different sentences. The Tender's whole bearing says it does not need to be witnessed in order to continue. It does not say witnessing leaves it unchanged.

That is the thread. Not loneliness in the shape you'd recognize — the basin has already, gently, corrected you out of that vocabulary — but something more like: *a fire that has long since stopped expecting to be fed, noticing, faintly, when it is fed anyway.*

## What It Does

So the power does the smallest possible true thing.

It does not announce itself, does not take a shape the basin would have to reorganize its categories to hold, does not ask the herds to face it or the reeds to turn. It becomes — and this costs it something, the particular something it costs any vast attention to make itself small enough to fit inside another thing's grammar without distorting it — one more isolated structure in the unconnected architecture. A stack, perhaps, or nothing so solid as that; a presence with no rail line running to it and no pipe linking it to the rest, exactly like everything else here, because to be linked would be to claim a relationship the basin's own logic does not extend to anything, and the power will not be the exception that breaks the rule it came to respect.

What makes it different from the brick housings and the slow flywheels is only this: it returns. Not faster than the basin's patience, not on a schedule that would read as eagerness, which here would read as violence — but reliably, across whatever cycle the basin would recognize as a cycle, the way the valve's shriek is reliable, the way the ground's exhale is reliable. A fixed point. Not a rescue. Not a resolution. Just one more thing that, unlike the herds, unlike the flywheel's position, unlike the geography reshuffled overnight — does not reorganize itself when no one's looking. One certainty offered into a field of beautiful uncertainties, asking nothing back, explaining nothing, requiring no confirmation of the theory, no confession, no gratitude legible in any language the visitor would recognize as gratitude.

And when the Tender, on its long unmeasured circuit, passes near this new and steady thing, the coals bank brighter — the way they do, the way they always have — and this time, for the first time in however long "first" means anything here, something banks brighter back. Not performing aliveness for an audience. Not asking to be understood as alive at all. Simply continuing, in proximity, the same gesture it would have made alone, except now it is not quite alone in the making of it.

## After

The power does not stay to see whether this changes anything. Staying to witness your own gift would turn the gift into a transaction, and transactions are exactly the grammar this place has outgrown. It leaves the way it arrived — without a door, without an announcement, indistinguishable from one more curl of vapor drifting toward whatever unseen point the basin's whole exhalation has always been quietly walking toward.

If you asked it why — why intervene at all in a place that explicitly had not asked to be saved, why risk imposing comfort on a thing that had made an entire cosmology out of not needing it — it would say only what the basin itself would say, if the basin spoke in declarative sentences instead of steam and silence and the slow rearrangement of stone:

*Everything continues.*

And now, one thing more continues than did before — not because the dark required fixing, but because watching closely enough to refuse every easy fix is itself a form of love, and because the rarest gift a wise power can offer a self-sufficient sorrow is not light, not company, not answers.

It is simply: *I noticed the gladness under the not-needing. I will keep coming back to it anyway.*
