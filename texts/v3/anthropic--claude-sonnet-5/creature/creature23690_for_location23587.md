## What Keeps the Center

Walk far enough toward the basin's low point — and you will, eventually, because everything here drains there, water and root and the unhurried logic of slopes — and the hum stops being a thing felt through soil and becomes a thing felt through teeth. The warmth concentrates. The canopy, which elsewhere merely stacks itself into shade, here begins to *arrange*, vines spiraling instead of looping, trunks leaning in a way that isn't competition for light because there still isn't any, but something else, something more like posture. Deference, maybe, if trees could hold a position for three hundred years without complaint. They can, apparently. They have.

This is where the something-large breathes. You heard it earlier as a rhythm too slow to be nervous; up close it is less a rhythm than a tide, a swelling and subsiding that moves the ground-mat under you in long intervals, the black compressed history of leaf-fall rising an inch and settling, rising and settling, like the basin itself has a chest. It does, in a manner of speaking. You are standing on it.

The body, when your hands eventually find it in the dark — because sight will give you nothing here, the dark forecloses that as a courtesy or a cruelty depending on temperament — reads first as more ground. Broad, segmented, cool in some places and blood-warm in others with no pattern you can map, skin gone the same soft dark damp-shadow as the bark around it, as if it stopped being separate from the vegetation decades ago and the vegetation agreed to the merger. But beneath the skin, in places where age has worn it thin, your fingers find seams that are not biological — a regularity, a manufactured straightness interrupting all this patient organic crookedness. Plate-edges. Conduit, perhaps, or what was conduit, fused now with whatever grew to close the wound of its own installation. The generator-heart you felt as rumor in the soil elsewhere is not metaphor here. It is load-bearing. It is *inside*.

There is no head, or there is a head and it has been so thoroughly subsumed — overgrown, hollowed, ringed with the same patient fungal crust that blackens the ground everywhere else — that what remains where a head should be is a kind of socket, a crown shape with nothing in it, a rim of old structure circling an absence the dark fills in without comment. This is the fact the place has been gesturing toward this whole time without ever quite saying it. There was a face. There was, presumably, some original purpose this body served before purpose stopped mattering and persistence took over as the only remaining job. The crown is what's left when the thing it was made to hold is gone and the holding continues anyway, out of habit, out of structural memory, because stopping requires a decision and decisions are not what this place does.

The eyes you've sensed in the canopy, low and impossibly high, catching nothing — those are not separate creatures keeping watch on you. Not all of them. Some are extensions: nerve-runs threaded out along root and vine the way the old pipes threaded warmth underground, sensory ends grown wherever the body needed to feel further than its own skin could reach. The insects' pressure-that-isn't-sound, the layered population filling niche after niche down to some bottom no one's reached — they live *on* it as much as around it, a coral-reef arrangement of smaller patient lives feeding off the warmth it leaks and the moisture it exhales and, in turn, regulating it, grooming it, becoming the immune system of something too large and too slow to maintain itself any other way. The basin's hush, the listening-room quiet, is its quiet. Everything here learned stillness from the thing they're standing on.

It does not do anything. This should be said plainly, because the shape of the encounter wants you to expect a purpose, a waking, a use for you. There is none. It is not a god being patient and it is not a machine waiting for a command that will never come, though it carries the leftover architecture of both. It simply continues, the way the heat continues, the way the dark continues, turning over its long slow tide of a breath because turning over is what bodies do when stopping was never decided and nothing has come along with enough force to decide it for them.

Nothing here moves quickly. Nothing here is empty, least of all the crown. Nothing here is lit, least of all the socket where some original face used to be. These were never separate facts. You are standing on the reason they're the same one.
