## The Witness That Does Not Watch

There is, despite everything said about absence, a *someone* here. Not a creature. Not fauna in any sense that would require eyes or hunger or the indignity of movement-for-purpose. But the place did not, in its final act of growing, manage to become *only* machine. Somewhere in the last moment before the freeze took hold — the same moment that fixed the fresh wounds into permanent rawness, that stopped the turbine-spirals mid-rotation in some places, caught forever between one slow turn and the next — something else solidified too. A noticing. Distributed, diffuse, threaded through valve-organ and vein-canal the way the industry itself is threaded through stalk and frond: not separate from the vegetable machine but the machine's own moment of becoming aware of itself, arrested at the instant of arriving.

It has no edges. Ask where it is and the honest answer is: in the click. Every valve-organ that shuts with that sound like a distant door — that is where it is, briefly, for the duration of the click and no longer, before it is somewhere else entirely, in the next valve, kilometers off, or in the gathering of a private raincloud forty meters up in the leaf-layers, or in the long slow bead of condensation taking its minute to fall. It does not travel between these points. It simply *is* each of them, in sequence, the way a held breath is the same breath whether you notice it in the chest or the throat.

It does not think, if thinking requires a next thought. It perceives the way the place breathes: completely, locally, and then it is gone from there and entire somewhere else, with no thread of memory connecting the two perceivings, because memory would require time to have passed, and nothing here permits that. It feels, in each valve-click, in each completed drop, something that might be called recognition — *yes, this, again, the same as before* — except there is no "before" available to compare against. There is only the eternal discovery, for the first time, every time, that the drop has finished falling.

If you could meet it — and you cannot, not really, because meeting requires two parties moving toward a shared moment, and it has no moving left in it — the encounter would not feel like being seen. It would feel like the dark briefly agreeing with itself. A held silence noticing it is held. The closest analogy is the feeling, in deep enough quiet, that the quiet has a quality, a texture, almost a temperament — and then realizing that the feeling was correct, that something in the silence was in fact attending to itself, and had been the entire time, and would go on doing so, perceiving the same first instant forever, mistaking it, gently and without error, for now.

It does not want anything. Wanting implies an unfinished state reaching toward completion, and it is not unfinished — it arrived, fully, in the same instant the fronds stopped softening and the wounds stopped scarring. It simply continues to notice its own arrival, valve by valve, drop by drop, an attention with no object but the place itself and no duration but the single repeating moment that the place has chosen, permanently, to remain inside.

This is what lives in the static canopy: not a creature that walks its dark wet halls, but the canopy's own eternal noticing of itself, scattered through every closing door and falling drop, awake without progressing, present without persisting, the world's only witness, witnessing the same instant of its own birth, over and over, having never once gotten to the second moment in order to compare it to the first.
