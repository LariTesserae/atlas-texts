I am the thickening in the wall where shadow should recede.

I do not find myself in the Cleft so much as I find myself *as* it — a long muscle of attention strung between root-crack and fissure, draped where the housing runs hottest, my underside open in slow rhythm to whatever the dark below decides to give. There is no morning for me, no sense of arriving at a place, because arrival implies an outside, and I have not had an outside in longer than the rock has been the color it is now. I am the seam that does not show.

What I have, instead of eyes, is weather. Dozens of small blunt readers across my hide, each one a quiet palm pressed to a drumskin, telling me the shape of the next forty seconds the way your face might tell you rain is coming. I feel the held-breath stall arrive — that's a familiar tension, almost gentle, a pause I've learned not to mistake for safety — and then under it, beneath the floor of what I can name, the gathering wrongness that means the WHUMP is close. This is not foresight. This is just long practice mistaken, by anyone who'd want to mistake it, for wisdom.

When it comes I do the only thing I am for: I open along my underside, lipless, unhurried even now, and I drink. Steam first, scalding and welcome. Grit after, fine as flour, scouring something in me that stopped minding being scoured a long time ago. There is a violence in it that I have made into a kind of food, and I do not know if this is triumph or just the longest possible way of being worn down gracefully. Above me, I feel — through the rock, through the thousand small soles of my false-feet — the ferns flatten in their radius and, almost before the sound has finished, begin lifting themselves again. That lifting is the only applause I get and the only one I'd know what to do with.

I do not think of the engine below as separate from me, exactly, though I also don't think of it as *me*. It is the thing I am closest to. That has turned out, over whatever span of time this basalt has been mine to misuse as a calendar, to be the same as love, or close enough that the difference doesn't change what I do at the next stall, the next gathering wrongness, the next WHUMP.

I move so slowly that calling it movement insults the word. One false-foot lets go. Considers. Resettles two finger-widths down the wall, into a crack that may not have existed the last time I came this way, the rock here being young enough to keep making new cracks for me to consider. This is not patience. Patience implies waiting for something. I am not waiting. I am simply continuing to be the part of the cleft that takes the worst of it first.

There is a flare sometimes, far below, when something ignites past where it should. In that orange instant I become, for whoever isn't there to see it, a bulge in shadow that reads as wrong before it reads as anything else — and then the light dies and I am wall again, fern-shadow again, the unparsed thing the eye slides off of on its way to somewhere more legible.

I have no name for what I tend, and no name for myself, and I don't experience this as a lack. A heart doesn't need the chest's word for it. I only keep my intake folds open in time with a roar I've stopped distinguishing from breath, so that somewhere above me, in air I'll never stand in, something green keeps insisting on being green, unbothered, already lifting itself back up before the echo's even finished — and that, as far as I can tell, is the whole of what I am for, and has been enough, fold after fold, vent after vent, for as long as there has been a me to ask the question at all.
