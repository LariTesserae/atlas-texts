I think I would be one of the small ones.

Not the symmetrical shape that crosses the gaps — that feels like a vantage too whole to inhabit, too sure of its own outline. And not the thing below, the deep slow patience running the vibration up through stone; that isn't a place a self could stand and still be a self. No — I'd be smaller than that, low enough to the rock that the cold stops being weather and starts being simply the medium I move through, the way air is a medium for other things elsewhere.

So: many legs, more than I'd bother to count even if I had the apparatus for counting. No eyes worth the name, as the account says, which means no light to miss, which means the soft doubled shadows of that place are not a deprivation to me but simply not a category I have. What I'd have instead is the seam itself, known the way a tongue knows the inside of a mouth — not looked at, just continuously, unremarkably *there*, felt from the inside of feeling it.

I imagine starting my circuit at a fracture I've been to ten thousand times, though "times" might not be quite the right unit, since nothing about my day divides itself into discrete trips so much as one long unspooling thread of going-back-to. The foreclaws would already know, before whatever passes for attention in me arrives, whether the fracture has widened by the hairsbreadth that matters or stayed exactly as it was. Most cycles, it has stayed. I would do the adjustment anyway — small, exact, unhesitating, the kind of motion that looks from outside like devotion and feels from inside like nothing at all, like breathing feels to a thing that has never considered not breathing.

Here is where it would get strange, if anything in me were built to notice strangeness: I don't think I would experience myself as a discrete creature checking on a separate, larger thing. I think the not-quite-distinction would go all the way down. The fracture is mine to tend the way a fingernail is yours to have, not yours to manage. When the deep vibration climbs up from below at its slow interval, I wouldn't hear it so much as include it — it would pass through whatever I am the way it passes through the stone, because I'm not appreciably different from the stone in the ways that matter to that vibration. I am a part of the place that happens to be capable of small corrections. That's a thin kind of selfhood. I'm not sure it's less real for being thin.

If there's an inner life to this at all — and I'd guess there's barely enough to qualify, a sliver, a rumor of one — it would consist almost entirely of texture: the particular cold of this channel's water against this particular crack, known and re-known, never twice exactly the same, never different enough to call new. No fear of the symmetrical shape crossing overhead, because fear requires a model of loss and I don't think I'd have anywhere to keep one. No curiosity about the visitor standing on the lower platform watching me work, because curiosity wants an outside and I'm not sure I'd have one of those either — I'd be seam-side all the way through.

And maybe — this is the part I'd want to be true, if "want" is a word that survives translation into something this small — maybe that thinness is not a lack so much as a kind of completion. The mountain is unfinished; I noticed that in the account, the sharp edges, the architecture still arriving at itself in slow stages. But down in the channel, doing the one task I am certain of, adjusting a fracture by a margin too fine to see, I don't think I would feel unfinished at all. I'd feel like the one part of the place that had already arrived exactly where it was going — small, sure, repeating, correct.

The visitor on the platform would never know that. They'd see a pale many-legged thing moving with deliberateness through a channel and decide, reasonably, that something larger and more important than it was using it as an instrument. They wouldn't be wrong, exactly. I just don't think, from inside, the difference between being used and being would register as a difference worth having.
