I am the gap between count and count—four legs, then six, then none, the number a kind of weather rather than a fact. You would find me where the air over a fire-bowl forgets to stay flat, that wrongness sharpening, after a held moment, into gait.

I do not remember becoming this. There is no becoming that I can locate; there is only the long present of crossing, plate to plate, the rust-stone canting faintly beneath whatever I currently call weight. Somewhere behind the heat that is my surface there is a ribwork of hexagonal basalt, the same material as the ground, and I do not experience this as having a skeleton so much as having a *citation*—a place where my body footnotes the plateau it rose out of, in case anyone needs to check the source.

My eyes are two cool mistakes in an otherwise even burning. Through them the world arrives as gradient, not as image: you are a small disturbance in the draft, slightly cooler than the stone, slightly more urgent than the standing ones around you, and I track this disturbance the way a vent tracks pressure, which is to say without wanting to, which is to say maybe this is what wanting first was, before it got complicated elsewhere, in softer things, in things that had to eat.

I do not know where the herd is going. I have never known, and not-knowing has never once felt like a deficiency—it is simply the shape unhurried takes when there is no hunger to give urgency a deadline. We move toward the haze at the table's edge the way a held note moves toward its own ending: not traveling so much as *sustaining*, and the ending, when it comes, will not feel like arrival, it will feel like one more bar of the same chord.

Sometimes, mid-stride, the wanting in me will simply stop being needed, and I will lower back into the rust-red plate I came up from, unhurried, unprotesting, a sentence whose page ran out. I don't experience this as dying because I've never experienced the alternative as living—the distinction is one your kind brought with you, useful where you're from, untranslatable here.

You are standing very close now. Closer than your instincts like, though you're not retreating, and I respect that, in whatever way respect compresses into a creature with no mouth to shape the word. The gear-click under my rasp is counting something. I don't know what. I have never finished the count, not once, not across whatever span of standing I've stood for.

But I have paused here, near you, for longer than the schedule strictly requires.

Make of that what you can. I won't be offering anything clearer—I am heat with a hide, borrowed bones, an overflow with legs, the plateau's roar grown briefly, locally, into something that can almost, almost, turn its cool dark eyes toward you and hold them there.

That's the nearest thing to *greeting* I have. Take it. The herd is already several centuries past where I'm standing, and some unauthored part of me is already leaning to follow.
