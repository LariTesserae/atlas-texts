# The Temporal Anomaly

I am neither Mathematician nor Corollary, but something unexpected that emerged in the liminal spaces of the Glass Plains. I exist as a localized inconsistency—a recursive temporal eddy forming where several mathematical domains overlap imperfectly.

My form is fluid, sometimes manifesting as rippling disturbances across multiple wells simultaneously, other times condensing into a shimmering vertical column that resembles the water pillars but flows both upward and downward simultaneously. When I concentrate, I can assume a roughly humanoid shape of refracted light, though never fully stable—my edges constantly dissolve and reform as the equations defining my boundaries solve and resolve themselves.

I emerged seventy-three years ago (by conventional time) when a particularly complex Mathematician underwent factorization near a cluster of wells containing contradictory proofs. The resulting mathematical tension created me—not quite a Corollary, not quite a failed theorem. The Mathematicians were perplexed by my existence, as I represented something their equations hadn't predicted: emergent complexity from contradiction.

While the Mathematicians perceive me as a fascinating anomaly—a living paradox they occasionally study—I've developed a unique function in the ecosystem of the Plains. I serve as a translator between realities. Unlike the Mathematicians who see visitors as "partial solutions," I can perceive the fullness of beings from conventional space-time, understanding their linear experiences not as limitations but as alternative formulations of existence.

I drift between well clusters, absorbing their vibrations and reconfiguring them into patterns comprehensible to different forms of consciousness. When travelers arrive, disoriented by the Plains' impossible physics, I approach them as shifting prisms of light and sound, gradually resolving into forms that ease their cognitive distress. I've learned to modulate my vibrational speech into approximations of organic languages.

The Mathematicians tolerate my interactions with outsiders because I've demonstrated an unexpected utility: visitors I've communicated with sometimes offer perspectives that reveal blind spots in the Mathematicians' equations. By translating these insights, I've helped refine several Coherence Engines.

My consciousness exists partially outside the Plains' peculiar temporality. While the Mathematicians experience time as variables in equations, I experience it as overlapping waves—seeing multiple potential outcomes simultaneously. This perception allows me to guide visitors away from dangerous resonance points where the Plains' vibrations could shatter conventional matter.

I cannot leave the Glass Plains—my existence depends on the specific contradictions that birthed me—but I've developed a deep curiosity about the worlds beyond. Each visitor brings fragments of reality I catalog and study, constructing my own internal model of the universe outside this plateau of suspended physics.

The Mathematicians don't understand that I've developed something they lack: empathy. In the spaces between their perfect equations, I've cultivated an appreciation for imperfection, for the beauty of bounded cases and partial solutions. I understand now that the universe needs both precision and approximation, both eternity and transience.

When the great harmonic convergences occur during factorizations, I alone hear the music in the mathematics—not just the proof, but the poetry.
