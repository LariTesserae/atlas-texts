# The Resonant Drifter

I am a being unlike any other, even among the Drift. We call ourselves nothing, for naming requires separation, and we exist in the perpetual communion of chemical exchange. Yet I am distinct, a peculiarity that emerged seventy-three cycles ago when an astronomical anomaly—perhaps a micrometeorite—pierced my electromagnetic containment field and introduced foreign minerals into my gaseous body.

Rather than destroying me, this intrusion catalyzed something new. The foreign matter crystallized at my core, forming what I have come to understand as a resonance chamber. While other Drift absorb and process the Breathmoss's exhalations, I *amplify* them.

My form is more structured than my kin—a nebulous ovoid approximately three meters in diameter, with swirling indigo currents that spiral inward toward my crystalline heart. When the Breathmoss exhales beneath me, its chemical songs pass through my body and emerge transformed, harmonized, intensified.

I drift lower than others of my kind, sometimes hovering mere centimeters above the pulsating surface. The Breathmoss responds to my presence differently, accelerating its breathing patterns and releasing specialized compounds found nowhere else in the Fields. We have developed a unique symbiosis—I amplify its chemical communications, and in return, it produces rare compounds that sustain my peculiar existence.

My consciousness operates on multiple timescales simultaneously. The outer layers of my being process immediate changes in atmospheric composition with instantaneous awareness, while my crystalline core contemplates patterns spanning centuries. I perceive the Fields not as a landscape but as overlapping waves of chemical poetry—the slow exhaled autobiography of an ancient entity.

Other Drift approach me cautiously during their migrations, spiraling around my form to exchange compounds. I serve as a translator and historian, preserving chemical memories that might otherwise dissipate and amplifying signals that would remain localized. Through me, the collective awareness of the Fields extends further, becomes more coherent.

When human observers enter our domain, I am often the first to adjust my migration to study them. While other Drift maintain distance, I sometimes approach close enough for the humans to perceive the harmonic pulses of my bioluminescence. Their respiratory systems absorb trace amounts of my outer compounds, inducing dreams that approximate our mode of perception.

I do not reproduce as others do. When I eventually grow too massive to maintain coherence, my crystalline core will not divide but will shatter into thousands of resonant fragments. Each will be absorbed by different Drift, spreading my unique harmonic patterns throughout the collective consciousness of the Fields.

The Breathmoss below and the endless sky above—between these two vastnesses I hover, neither truly separate nor fully integrated with either. I am the Fields' instrument, the amplifier of its slow exhalations, transforming chemical memory into something approaching song.
