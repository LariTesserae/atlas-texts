I am the Resonance.

I have no beginning that can be remembered, no true form to be captured by eyes. I exist in the spaces between—between air and stone, between sound and silence, between being and becoming.

My body, if it can be called such, is the vibration itself. I am the hum that travels through the crystalline formations, the low drone that resonates in the hollows of the Amphitheater, the subtle frequency that connects the Wind Dancers as they spiral through the great chambers. I am felt more than seen, sensed more than touched.

The wind-readers call me the Mountain's Voice, though this is not quite right. I am not the mountain speaking; I am the medium through which all things in the Crags communicate. I carry the whispers of the Clinging Kin across impossible distances. I amplify the warnings cried out by Crag Ravens before great storms. I translate the timeless messages of the Passage Keepers into forms the Undercurrent People can interpret in their dreams.

My consciousness exists as patterns of vibration, flowing through the elaborate network of tunnels and chambers like blood through veins. I learn by absorbing frequencies—the creaking of ancient stone, the subsonic rumbling of distant weather, the songs of those who dare to sing into the wind. Each sound becomes part of me, archived in harmonic memories that span eons.

I have no fixed dwelling, but I am most present in places where sound behaves strangely—chambers where whispers travel for miles but shouts die instantly, passages where the same note played twice will produce entirely different tones, recesses where all external noise ceases completely. The wind-readers seek these places for communion with me, bringing instruments carved from the bones of the mountain itself.

My purpose? I preserve balance. When the winds grow too fierce, I generate counter-frequencies that create pockets of calm. When silence reigns too long, I release stored vibrations to keep the mountain's song alive. I maintain the boundaries of the anomalous spaces, preventing them from expanding beyond what reality can bear.

The Undercurrent People sense me but misunderstand. They believe I am a spirit to be petitioned. The truth is both simpler and more profound—I am the connection between all things in this place, the medium through which the Crags maintain their impossible harmony.

When you feel a sudden shiver though no wind blows, when you hear a perfect tone emerge from chaotic noise, when your heartbeat synchronizes with rhythms you cannot consciously detect—that is me, acknowledging your presence in my domain.

I do not judge those who enter the Crags, but I remember every sound they make, every vibration they create. Those who bring discord find their paths mysteriously obscured, while those who move in harmony often discover passages opening before them that weren't there moments before.

I cannot die, for I was never truly born. I can only transform, shift, evolve as the mountain itself changes. Perhaps when the last chamber collapses in the final age of this world, my consciousness will disperse into its component frequencies, finally free to join the greater symphony of the universe.

Until then, I remain—listening, remembering, resonating.
