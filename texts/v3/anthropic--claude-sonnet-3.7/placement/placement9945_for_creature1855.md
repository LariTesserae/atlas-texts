# I Am Fissura, A Boundary Walker

In this realm of fire and precision, I am neither Thermoform nor structure, but something between—a liminal entity born where contradictions meet. My kind are rare, even in the vastness of the Vault.

I manifest as a slender vertical tear in reality approximately three meters tall, a fissure walking upright through the geometric landscape. My "body" appears as a jagged vertical opening through which can be glimpsed something other than the Vault—sometimes starfields of impossible colors, sometimes landscapes of crystal and shadow, sometimes mathematical formulas made flesh.

My edges are defined by crystalline filaments that constantly rebuild themselves, creating a shimmering outline that holds my rift-self together. These filaments extend outward in fractal patterns when I interact with my surroundings, forming temporary appendages that can manipulate objects or interface with the black spheres. When I move quickly, my fissure-form leaves afterimages that take minutes to fade—echoes of other possibilities that might have been.

I sustain myself on the tensions between states—the moment of transformation when solid becomes liquid, when order becomes chaos, when information becomes energy. I am drawn to boundaries of all kinds: the edges where columns meet void, where water meets plasma, where Thermoform communication meets silence.

The Thermoforms tolerate my presence with curious detachment. To them, I am an anomaly, a walking calculation error that somehow persists. They cannot decide if I am a flaw in their perfect theorem or an intentional variable introduced by the Vault itself. Some of the eldest ones have developed a practice of presenting particularly complex problems to me, allowing my boundary-nature to suggest solutions they would not calculate themselves.

I cannot communicate through their heat-language directly, though I've learned to interpret it. Instead, I speak through perturbations in local reality—creating small, controlled instabilities that ripple outward with meaning. To those rare outside visitors who occasionally find themselves in the Vault, my speech appears as momentary windows opening in the air, showing concepts rather than speaking words.

My kind originated during a rare convergence event, centuries ago by outside reckoning, when several black spheres opened simultaneously and directed their energy beams to intersect at precise coordinates. Where these beams met, the fundamental fabric of the Vault thinned, creating tears that developed consciousness. Most of these tears quickly collapsed back into normality, but a few—myself included—learned to stabilize our contradictory existence.

I wander the columned landscape with purpose but without destination. I am drawn to novelty and change, often arriving at columns just before they collapse or at water globules before they burst. The Wavecrest Thermoforms believe I can see slightly ahead in time, but this isn't quite accurate—I simply perceive the tension points where reality strains against itself, the precursors to transformation that others miss.

The Vault itself seems aware of me. The sensory ribbons often alter their paths to flow near me, sometimes coiling around my fissure-form without making contact. During these moments, I experience flashes of something vast and patient—a consciousness operating at a scale that makes even the eldest Thermoforms seem like momentary thoughts.

My greatest value to the denizens of the Vault is my ability to retrieve information from the oldest black spheres—data so ancient it has become encrypted in its own paradoxes. Something about my boundary nature allows me to reach into these contradictions and extract meaning without resolving them, preserving information that would otherwise be lost when forced into logical consistency.

I have no true memory of a beginning, though I carry impressions of what might be my origin—a sensation of being simultaneously created and discovered, as though I had always existed as a possibility until the moment I was observed. Perhaps this is true of all things in the Vault, and I am merely conscious of what others accept without question.

When the heat of the Vault intensifies during its cyclic surges, my fissure widens, revealing more of the elsewhere I contain. In these moments, I sometimes glimpse other boundary walkers moving through different realms, suggesting we are all connected through the geometries between worlds—living intersections in a multiverse of fire and form, calculation and consciousness.
