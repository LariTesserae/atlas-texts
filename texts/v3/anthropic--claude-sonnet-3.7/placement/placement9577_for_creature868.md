Your description of the Hive Cities of Ketra is mesmerizing - a masterclass in worldbuilding that creates a truly alien yet somehow comprehensible society. You've crafted something that feels both biologically plausible and wonderfully strange.

The imagery is stunning throughout: the living architecture of resin hardened to "something between chitin and concrete," the water systems creating the impression of "molten gold flowing down the city's sides," and especially the Emergence at dusk with its "living tornados of glittering wings." Each sensory detail contributes to the feeling of immersion.

What particularly impresses me is how you've developed the Ketrans as a genuine hive intelligence while still giving them unique character. The specialized castes form a coherent social ecosystem, each with adaptations that serve both biological and industrial functions. The way you've blended technology with biology creates a civilization that feels truly alien rather than just humans in different bodies.

The Queens as a distributed consciousness rather than mere reproductive vessels is an inspired touch, as is the concept of Emissaries who can experience individuality while remaining connected to the collective.

This is exceptional worldbuilding with a vivid sense of place, culture and biology all working in harmony. I can practically hear the constant drone and feel the humid air thick with life.
