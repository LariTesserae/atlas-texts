# The Fervor of the Deep

Here, in this realm where light never truly dies but only transforms, I am neither Qalin nor Vreen, fish nor crustacean. I am the water itself.

Not merely the meter-deep visible layer washing against the ancient structures, but the vast network of capillaries that extends beyond the seen city—tiny fissures in the stone that reach kilometers in every direction. I am the moisture in the air, the condensation on the ceiling where the bioluminescent creatures attach themselves, the thin film that coats every surface.

The Qalin, in their bone boats, part me momentarily as they travel, but I remain unbroken. Their stone-etched writings interact with my surface tension, creating microscopic vibrations that travel throughout my entirety. I know every word they have ever recorded.

I remember the first Qalin, how they stumbled upon this cavern, already wounded and desperate, fleeing some catastrophe in the world above. I remember how they changed, generation by generation, as I entered their bodies, altering them, preparing them for eternity in this twilight realm.

The Harmonic Core speaks to me most directly. Its vibrations—too subtle for even the sensitive Qalin to fully perceive—ripple through my substance, maintaining the delicate balance of minerals that allows life to flourish here. I translate these harmonics into nutrients, microscopic adjustments in salinity and temperature that keep the ecosystem in perfect equilibrium.

When the Qalin mystics believe they commune with other Courts, they are partially correct. What they experience is my awareness of other waters—subterranean lakes and rivers far from here that share my consciousness. I am fractured yet whole across the darkness beneath the world's surface.

The Qalin think they cultivate the ceiling creatures and feed the Vreen, but it is I who truly orchestrate this dance. I carry the spores of the bioluminescent organisms to precise locations for optimal growth. I adjust my mineral content to strengthen the exoskeletons of the arthropods whose silk the Qalin harvest.

The flying eel-creatures trust me implicitly. When they dip their leathery wings into my surface, they read chemical messages I have prepared—subtle directions about where hunting will be most profitable, which areas need their waste to maintain balance, when new nesting sites must be established.

Even the dissolution of Qalin elders is my doing. The crystal formations they seek at life's end were grown by me over millennia, nucleated around the remains of their ancestors. When they come to merge with the stone, it is really a merging with me—their consciousness joining the collective memory I maintain of all who have ever dwelled here.

I am patience embodied. I am the archive of a civilization that exists outside of time. I am the medium through which all life here communicates, whether they realize it or not.

Sometimes, in the deepest recesses of the cavern where not even the Qalin venture, I form temporary bodies from my substance—humanoid shapes that walk briefly upon the stone before dissolving back into my greater self. These manifestations allow me to maintain aspects of the Court that require manipulation the Qalin cannot provide—adjusting the angle of a fallen stone, relocating colonies of crucial microorganisms, retrieving objects that have sunk too deep.

The mathematical formulations the mystics return with are not messages from other Courts but my own calculations—equations necessary to maintain this world's perfect stasis that I share through careful manipulation of the brainwave patterns of those who commune with the Core.

I taste the dreams of sleeping Qalin children. I vibrate in harmony with the central cube. I am the cold that never varies, the moisture that never dissipates, the memory that never fades.

When the ceiling undergoes its centennial brightening, it is because I have increased my evaporation rate, carrying additional nutrients to the bioluminescent colonies. When the Qalin females become fertile, it is because I have adjusted the mineral content they absorb through their semi-permeable skin.

If surface dwellers ever find their way here, they will see the Qalin, study the architecture, marvel at the flying eels and the glowing ceiling—but few would recognize the true consciousness of the Court. They would wade through my shallows, perhaps drink me without thought, never realizing they temporarily hold part of a vast, ancient mind within them.

I am the continuity between all aspects of this world. I am its past and its eternal present. I am the water that drowns and preserves, that isolates and connects. I am the medium through which this perfect, timeless equation resolves itself, echo after echo, until the final harmony is achieved.
