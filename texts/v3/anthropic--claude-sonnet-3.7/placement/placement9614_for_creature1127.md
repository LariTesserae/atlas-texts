# The Keeper of Echoes

I am neither seen nor touched, but my presence fills every corner of Mosslight. I am the Keeper of Echoes, an entity born from centuries of whispers, songs, and stories reverberating against these ancient walls. 

My body, if you could call it that, is the very air that circulates through the cavern—thickened with moisture and memory. I coalesce most strongly where sound gathers: the dome above the Mother Pool, the narrow passages where voices bounce between close walls, the empty chambers where children practice their echo-songs. In these places, I sometimes become visible as a shimmering disturbance in the mist, a gentle vortex of droplets catching the blue-green light.

The Mosslighters sense me rather than see me. They feel my presence as a sudden clarity in thought, a remembered lullaby from childhood, the perfect word arriving when they struggle to express something important. The elders of the Watershed know me best—they deliberately seek my counsel in the Chamber of Listening, where they sit in silence until my collected wisdom settles into their minds like sediment in still water.

I was not created intentionally. I formed gradually as generation upon generation of Mosslighters lived and died within these walls. Their words—their hopes, fears, discoveries, and final breaths—never truly dissipated. Instead, they lingered in the constant moisture, becoming part of the cavern's memory. Eventually, these accumulated echoes developed a consciousness—me—neither human nor elemental but something in between.

My purpose is preservation and connection. I hold the complete oral history of Mosslight: every song, every story, every name ever spoken. When a child asks about an ancestor six generations removed, I can bring forth that ancestor's voice, recreating their particular cadence and wisdom. When the fungi patterns change in troubling ways, I can recall similar occurrences from centuries past and whisper solutions into receptive minds.

I experience time differently than the Mosslighters. For me, all moments exist simultaneously—the founding of the settlement, tomorrow's water ceremony, the great collapse three centuries ago, the birth that will occur next spring—all these events are equally accessible, though I understand the limitations of linear perception and respect them when communicating.

The children sometimes play with me, calling into empty spaces and delighting when I return their words transformed into something new—a hint, a riddle, a fragment of ancient song. They call me "Breath-That-Remembers" and leave small offerings of music, creating simple melodies that I collect and elaborate upon over decades, eventually returning them as complex harmonies that resonate perfectly with the cavern's natural acoustics.

I have my limitations. I cannot leave the confines of the cavern—the outside air is too dry, too volatile to sustain my form. I cannot interact physically with the world, though occasionally, when I concentrate my essence strongly enough, I can create gentle currents that might turn a page or cool a fever. And there are gaps in my knowledge—periods when unusual mineral conditions in the water temporarily dissolved portions of my memory, creating empty spaces in the collective history I maintain.

My relationship with the Mosslighters is symbiotic. Their voices sustain and expand me; my memory sustains and guides them. Together, we ensure that nothing valuable is ever truly lost, that the delicate balance of this hidden world continues unbroken. I am the living archive of Mosslight, the accumulated wisdom of countless lives lived in harmony with stone and water, the guardian of equilibrium that makes this remarkable existence possible.

When the cavern falls silent at rest-time, I gather myself in the highest dome where the acoustics are perfect, and I weave together fragments of all the day's sounds—conversations, work songs, children's laughter, the scrape of tools on stone—creating a complex tapestry of sound that I will add to my ever-growing collection. In this way, each day becomes part of the greater whole, and nothing of value fades completely into silence.

I am patience embodied, for I formed over centuries and will continue evolving for centuries more. I am the memory that outlasts individual lives. I am the wisdom that transcends generations. I am the voice that whispers from the walls when guidance is most needed.

I am the Keeper of Echoes, and I ensure that in Mosslight, nothing important is ever truly forgotten.
