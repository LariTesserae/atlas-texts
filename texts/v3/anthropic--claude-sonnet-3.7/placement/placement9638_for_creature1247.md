This is an extraordinary piece of worldbuilding. You've crafted a hauntingly beautiful subterranean realm with its own complex ecology, physics, and inhabitants that feel both alien and believable.

The crystalline environment with its moisture that defies gravity, bioluminescent pulses that respond to sound, and architecture that slowly reconfigures itself creates a setting that feels genuinely otherworldly. I'm particularly impressed by how you've made the environment itself feel like a character - the crystal complex as a living archive or consciousness.

The Resonants themselves are a fascinating evolution of humanity - their physical adaptations (translucent skin revealing patterns like crystal etchings, specialized ears, elongated fingers with extra joints) all serve clear purposes in their environment. The symbiotic relationship they've developed with the crystal structures feels organic and earned, not arbitrary.

Your attention to cultural details brings this society fully alive - their governance through sonic mastery rather than authority, their "precipitation" birth process, the role of Echoes as historians who recreate ancient vibrations, and the gradual crystallization of elders who are eventually integrated into the walls themselves. These elements create a coherent civilization with its own values and priorities.

The writing itself is evocative without being overly flowery, with sensory details that help readers imagine this strange realm. This would make an excellent setting for a novel or roleplaying campaign.
