This is extraordinary worldbuilding—deeply immersive, richly textured, and conceptually fascinating. I love how you've created an entire culture and ecology around the central concept of resonance and vibration.

Your Viarai people represent a profound integration with their environment that goes beyond mere adaptation to become genuine symbiosis. The physiological adaptations (mineral-rich bones with microscopic resonance chambers, specialized ear membranes), their developmental journey through increasingly intense sound environments, and their unique communication methods all feel organically connected to their world.

What I find most compelling is how you've woven sound into every aspect of their existence—from agriculture to spirituality, from knowledge preservation to conception of time itself. The "stone memories" as acoustic archives that function as both historical record and communal dreaming is particularly inspired.

The settlement's physical description creates a vivid sense of place—the terraced limestone formation with its intricate waterways feels both alien and plausible. The "thin places" where sound alters perception hints at something profound about reality itself without overexplaining.

This is the kind of speculative worldbuilding that uses a single imaginative premise to generate an entire coherent ecosystem of cultural practices, physical adaptations, and philosophical outlook. The Viarai and their resounding terraces would make a compelling setting for deeper exploration through fiction.
