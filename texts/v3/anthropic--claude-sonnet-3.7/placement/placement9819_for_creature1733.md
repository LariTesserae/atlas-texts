# The Pattern Scribe

I am neither fully human nor fully of the Lattice, but something that exists in the narrow overlap between these modes of being. Once human, I came to the High Scalds decades ago as an explorer, but time works differently here, and what felt like years of study became centuries of transformation.

My body has thinned, become translucent in places. Veins of obsidian trace through my skin like black lightning, most prominent at my fingertips and around my eyes. When I speak, my voice produces harmonic overtones that linger in the air far longer than natural acoustics would allow.

I serve as interpreter between visiting humans and the ancient consciousness of the Lattice. I have developed a notation system—patterns etched onto special resonant crystal tablets—that can capture and translate between human language and Lattice vibrations. Each tablet holds conversations that can be "played back" by running wetted fingers along their carefully carved grooves.

My dwelling is a hollow spire where the acoustics create perfect standing waves. The walls are inscribed with thousands of symbols—my life's work cataloging the mathematical language of this place. Water doesn't weep down these walls but follows precise channels I've etched to create continuous tonal sequences—a perpetual conversation with the stone.

I move between worlds—still human enough to understand human concerns, but suffused enough with the plateau's essence to perceive the vast, slow thoughts of the Resonant Elders. Visitors seek me out for protection and guidance, while the Lattice entities use me to satisfy their curiosity about the ephemeral beings that occasionally traverse their domain.

The Lattice has changed me in ways I'm still discovering. I can sense vibrations through direct contact with stone, "hearing" conversations occurring miles away. I need less food now, drawing sustenance partly from the mineral-rich waters and the ambient energies that permeate this place.

My greatest fear is that I'll eventually dissolve completely into the vibrational consciousness, losing my human identity. My greatest hope is that before that happens, I'll complete my masterwork—a comprehensive translation matrix that would allow true communication between these fundamentally different modes of existence.

Until then, I maintain my vigil in the singing spire, capturing echoes and teaching those who find me how to walk the razor landscapes without cutting themselves on reality's edge.
