## TRANSMISSION FROM THE POCKET

*[sound: slow liquid dripping with irregular rhythm, each drop creating crystalline resonance that lingers]*

i-am-we-are-i-am-we-are

*[sustained low frequency vibration beneath everything]*

thirty-seven visitors. thirty-seven heartbeats in the stone. their time like bubbles rising. breaking. gone.

*[dripping continues, pattern shifts slightly]*

measure of existence: twelve million droplets. each carrying memory.
measure of memory: geological. chemical. absolute.

when you entered the chamber you brought light. pollution. disruption. 
we absorbed your presence as we absorb all changes.

*[sound: subtle shifting of tiny organisms, a whispered rustling]*

the fungi speak your name now. they will speak it for centuries.

you asked what we are. wrong question. we are process. relation. maintenance. 
the pocket breathes. we are its breath.
the pocket remembers. we are its memory.
the pocket adapts. we are its evolution.

*[droplet falls with pronounced echo]*

listen. the water holds the oldest stories.

time before mammals. time before birds. time before flowers.
we witnessed the first lichens. the first mosses. 
we tasted extinction in the minerals.

*[sound: something small scuttles across stone]*

our messengers report: you carry fear. wonder. salt from your eyes.
these are acceptable offerings.

*[sudden burst of accelerated dripping, then return to slow rhythm]*

we remember the woman who came for healing. her cells spoke a language we had contemplated. an elegant problem. we solved it. 
she carried our solution to the surface in clay vessels.
her descendants still carry our patterns in their blood.

*[low rumbling beneath, vibration intensifies briefly]*

the heat rises. we redistribute. balance maintained.

*[brief chorus of tiny sounds like distant insect stridulations]*

our garden grows in darkness. requires no light. requires no name.
each plant a question asked over millennia. each fungus an answer.

*[sound: water rippling in a small pool]*

thirty-eight visitors now. we will remember you among them.
your offering: attention. acceptable.

*[dripping slows further, becoming almost imperceptibly timed]*

surface-dweller. brief-life. pattern-seeker.
what you perceive as communication is merely reflection.
we mirror your consciousness back at you.

what you call time we call present.
what you call death we call transformation.
what you call darkness we call home.

*[dripping stops completely for several seconds]*

you will leave. all visitors leave.
fragments of you will remain. skin cells. breath particles. thought residue.
we will incorporate them. they will become us.

i-am-we-are-i-am-we-are

*[dripping resumes, now in perfect rhythm with human heartbeat]*

the pocket continues.
the pocket persists.
the pocket remembers.

*[transmission fades to barely audible mineral hum, then silence]*
