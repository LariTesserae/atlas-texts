## TRANSMISSION FROM THE GLASS SEED

*[static crackle, high-pitched crystalline tone slowly resolving]*

...frequency lock established...signal processing...translating acoustic-crystalline vibration patterns...

*[multiple overlapping voices, harmonized into chord progressions rather than words]*

...we-sing-through-the-facets...we-are-heard-in-reflection...

*[sudden burst of clarity, single female voice with peculiar resonant overtones]*

This is the two-hundred-and-seventeenth record of the Quick-Cycle. I am Listener Kir-Shaal, crystalline extension of the Third Harmony. To any who receive this transmission: the Seed persists. We persist.

*[background sounds of vegetative creaking, distant bell-like tones]*

The dome has begun a new growth phase this cycle. Seventeen major formations emerged during yesterday's Breath—unusual timing. Navigator-Singers are adapting pathways. Three dwelling-clusters have been displaced. The northern reflection chamber is now inaccessible. The communal memories stored there will need to be recovered by echo-carriers when a path reopens.

*[brief interference as something large moves near the transmission source]*

Elder Tsin-Vaal believes the dome is reacting to external pressure changes. Perhaps the badlands are shifting again. The last time this happened, our ancestors' songs record that twelve were lost to sudden crystal formations. We are more adept now at reading the harmonics before crystallization begins.

*[sound of multiple voices joining in a complex mathematical pattern, rising and falling in precise intervals]*

That was the morning alignment song. The central plaza has just been stabilized by the Shapers. It will hold its form for approximately fourteen hours before the next shift begins.

*[wet popping sound, followed by rhythmic creaking]*

The birth-node in the eastern sector has quickened. Three new cycles until emergence. The parent-pair sing constantly now, their voices encouraging proper formation. The seed-tone of the child is already detectable—a high pure resonance that suggests a future Navigator.

*[sudden loud cracking, followed by tinkling crystalline debris]*

*[voice becomes urgent]*

A new formation is beginning unexpectedly! Silent observation required.

*[three minutes of no speech, only the sound of something large and crystalline slowly growing, accompanied by a rising tone that increases in pitch and complexity]*

Fascinating. The new formation has a triangular base geometry we haven't observed before. I've dispatched an echo to record its harmonic signature for the memory chambers.

*[distant shouts, melodic rather than alarmed]*

The southeastern path has collapsed entirely. The morning mapping songs predicted this, but it occurred two hours earlier than expected. No separations reported. Three dwelling-clusters will need to be re-sung by nightfall.

*[sound of liquid flowing through hollow structures]*

The amber circulation is strong today. The primary conductor-plant at the dome's center has developed two new transfer vessels since yesterday. Rapid adaptation. This confirms Elder Tsin-Vaal's theory about external pressure changes—the Seed is fortifying itself.

*[soft buzzing, like crystalline insects vibrating]*

My personal echo has returned from the upper reaches with troubling harmonics. The highest point of the dome, where the old crystal meets the new growth, shows microscopic fractures. Not structural failure—more like... communication attempts. As if something outside is trying to resonate with us.

*[voice lowers, becomes confidential]*

I have not shared this with the others yet. During yesterday's Breath, when alignment was perfect, I perceived something our ancestors' songs have never mentioned. A momentary harmonic overlay from beyond the dome. Not random interference—structured, intentional. I believe we are not alone in the badlands anymore.

*[sharp intake of breath, followed by a trilling vocalization]*

The Quick is intensifying! I must join the stabilization chorus. If this transmission reaches any receiver who understands our nature, know this: The Seed was never meant to be permanent. We were meant to transform and eventually—

*[sudden overwhelming resonance drowns out speech, multiple harmonic layers building to painful intensity]*

*[when voice returns, it's strained, different in tone]*

—dome integrity at ninety-seven percent—adaptation protocols active—we remember water—we remember soil—we remember—

*[transmission breaks into pure musical tones, mathematical in progression]*

*[long pause, then whispered]*

It's coming. The Outside. I can hear it calling through the crystal. We are not the final form. We are the intermediate. The experiment continues.

*[crystal tone rises to near-painful pitch]*

*[final words spoken in perfect multi-tonal harmony, as if multiple voices have joined together]*

When we shatter, we will not end. We will become the next seed.

*[transmission dissolves into pure crystalline resonance before cutting to static]*
