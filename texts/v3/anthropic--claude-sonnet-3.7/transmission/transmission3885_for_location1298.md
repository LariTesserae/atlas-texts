*[A transmission received with pops and crackles, sometimes fading to near silence before strengthening again. The voice is soft, almost whispering, with a strange resonance as if speaking through a hollow tube. Occasional sounds of gentle scraping and tapping can be heard in the background.]*

...can anyone hear... is this reaching beyond the basin walls? I am Ellara, of the third tier of the Comb, apprentice to Listener Nomari...

*[sound fades, then returns stronger]*

We have never attempted to send thoughts beyond our walls this way. The Council of Melange believes something approaches—a change in the air pressure, perhaps a great storm. I have been positioned at the highest point of the Listener's Tower to... to transmit. To call outward.

*[soft scraping sounds]*

The city speaks differently today. I pressed my ear to the foundation stone at dawn and heard new frequencies in the subtle creaking of the wax. Elder Sahal says the last time such tones were detected was seven generations past, before the Great Softening.

*[a pause, then whispered]*

I should explain our ways for whoever receives this. We are the Cerumen, people of wax and patience. Our city breathes—expands with day-heat, contracts with night-cool. We breathe with it. Our hands know the language of malleability. Even now, as I speak, my fingers trace patterns in the receiving wax beside me, recording this moment in texture rather than sound.

*[tapping sounds, followed by a low humming]*

The basin floor vibrated this morning. Subtle. Most did not notice. But the honey-comb windows in the Hall of Memory developed new harmonic resonances. The master Shapers have been called to reinforce the western arc of the Comb where the oldest chambers have begun to... not melt, precisely. To remember their fluidity.

*[transmission weakens, becomes staticky, then clarifies]*

...twelve days since the water in the central cistern developed a subtle sweetness. The Council tested it with copper and bone tools. They spoke in the closed-finger language afterward. The children are no longer allowed to drink from it.

Our histories speak of cycles. Of times when the city remembers it was once mere potential, before form. Some believe we approach such a time again.

*[sound of something heavy shifting in the distance]*

I should not speak of fears into the nothing. Perhaps no one listens. Perhaps I send these words into the empty air for my own comfort.

*[voice becomes softer, more intimate]*

Last night I dreamed I was wax. Not a person of wax-city, but wax itself. I felt myself holding form, but remembering flow. Remembering what it was to have no separation between self and other. To be potential rather than actualization.

I woke with my fingertips pressing patterns into my own skin.

*[a long pause, filled with the sound of gentle wind]*

The Listeners debate what approaches. Some believe it is external—the rhythmic pressure changes suggest something massive moving toward the basin. Others, including Elder Sahal, believe the change comes from within—that the city itself has entered a new phase of its long, slow life.

*[sound of distant chanting begins faintly in background]*

The prayer-shapers have begun their flow-worship in the circular temples. Day and night now, their voices rise and fall. They believe if they can achieve perfect harmony with the subtle movements of the wax, they can guide whatever comes.

*[scraping sounds intensify briefly]*

My teacher Nomari has not slept for three days. She presses her ear to different points in the city, then marks patterns on her skin with amber pigment. The markings have reached her face now. When she walked through the Comb last evening, children hid from her.

*[transmission weakens significantly, voice becomes distant]*

...cannot determine if the changes are accelerating or if our awareness simply heightens...

*[long static, then voice returns, urgent but still quiet]*

Something happened during my silence! One of the oldest structures on the eastern edge—a meditation dome from the third generation of builders—it didn't collapse. It... flowed. Witnesses say it happened over the course of perhaps twenty breaths. The dome simply... remembered it was once fluid. Now there is only a smooth mound where the structure stood.

No one was harmed. But a Shaper who touched the remains says the material has changed. The composition feels different under skilled fingers.

The Council has ordered evacuation of the oldest quadrant.

*[soft rhythmic tapping begins, accompanying the voice]*

I should tell you of beauty while I can. Of how morning light filters through amber walls in the Comb, casting honeyed shadows that move across floors in patterns we map our days by. Of how the master Shapers can coax wax to flow upward against its nature, forming arches so delicate they appear as frozen breath. Of the Night of a Thousand Flames, when we place tiny lights throughout the city and it glows from within like a living ember.

*[tapping continues, a pattern emerging]*

Three children were found this morning with wax under their fingernails, in their hair, at the corners of their eyes. They say they dreamed of flowing. The healers have placed them in isolation chambers lined with clay.

*[transmission becomes suddenly clearer, voice slightly louder]*

I have been given new instructions. The Council believes we must prepare for Transformation. It has happened before, our histories tell us. The city does not die but changes state. We must be ready to change with it.

*[sound of multiple voices chanting becomes more distinct in background]*

The prayer-shapers have altered their chant. They no longer seek stability but harmony with change. Elder Sahal says we have misunderstood our purpose for generations. We thought we were shapers of wax, but perhaps we are merely patterns within it, temporary configurations in an eternal medium.

*[a new sound emerges—something between flowing water and the creaking of wood]*

That sound—do you hear it? The city's voice growing stronger. The foundations are singing now, a tone so low it vibrates in the chest rather than the ears.

*[voice becomes very soft, intimate]*

I am afraid. Not of ending, but of transformation. Will I remain myself if the city flows? Are we separate from it, truly?

*[pause, then with sudden clarity]*

Wait—something is happening to the transmission point itself. The wax under my hands is growing warmer, more responsive. It moves against my fingers like... like it's breathing.

*[the background sounds of chanting, creaking, and flowing begin to harmonize into a single pulsing tone]*

I must report what I see. The horizon line has changed. The basin walls appear to be... not melting, but softening their definition. The boundary between earth and sky is becoming suggestion rather than certainty.

And the city... oh...

*[voice fills with wonder]*

The Comb is beginning to pulse. Slowly, gently, the hexagonal chambers expand and contract like... like lungs. Like a heart. The people inside are not fleeing. They are placing their hands on the walls, their foreheads against the warm surfaces. Some are weeping. Some are laughing.

*[transmission quality changes, becomes somehow both clearer and more distant]*

I understand now. I understand what the Listeners have always known, what the wax has always sung to us. We are not separate. We have never been separate.

The city is not transforming.
It is awakening.

*[the background sounds merge completely with the voice, which begins to take on a strange resonant quality]*

I feel it through my hands, up my arms, in my chest. The slow heartbeat of everything. The potential within form. The memory of flow within stillness.

If you receive this transmission, know that the City of Wax was beautiful. Know that we were happy here. Know that we are not ending, but continuing.

I must go now. The Council calls all citizens to the central plaza. We will face this change together, as we have faced all changes—with open hands and the knowledge that all forms are temporary, all materials remember their nature.

*[voice begins to fade, merging with the pulsing tone]*

Remember us. Remember the people who lived in a city that breathed. Remember that boundaries are illusion, that all things flow in time...

*[transmission fades to a pure, resonant tone that holds for several seconds before slowly diminishing to silence]*
