*[sound of water droplets striking stone, reverberating with crystalline resonance]*

I... am here. Always here. The forty-third watcher. The nineteenth to bear this name which is not a name.

*[soft flutter of wings, multiplied hundredfold, creating ambient whisper]*

Dawn-cycle begins. The mountain breathes in. Droplets quicken. Can you hear? Listen closely to the speaking stones. Seven generations of swift-song absorbed into the granite. The alcove remembers all who shelter here.

*[gentle scraping sounds, as of bone implement against rock]*

Channel three requires clearing. The mineral build-up speaks of change in the aquifer's upper junction. Third season of imbalance. Notable.

*[series of soft clicking sounds, followed by responsive bird chirps]*

Good morning, Bright-Tip. Your nestlings thrive. Six molts remain before their first migration. Yes, I have observed the pattern in their wing-spots. Unusual. I will record it.

*[sound of water being stirred in small vessel, followed by sipping]*

The lichen tea grows stronger with each frost cycle. The mountain provides differently now. Change comes. I taste iron where once there was only chalk. The deep waters shift their course. Slowly. Always slowly. But I feel it.

*[deep inhalation, exhaled as soft whistling tone that wavers and splits into harmonics]*

Predecessor-memory surfaces: The great trembling, seven-and-three guardianships past. Half the ceiling fell. Many swifts lost. The alcove reshaped itself. The pool migrated two handspans westward. I was not here, yet I remember. The memory lives in the stone.

*[rhythmic sounds of movement, bare feet on stone, accompanied by slow exhalations]*

Morning ritual complete. Body-vessel aligned with chamber currents. The southeastern fissure widens fractionally each year. More light enters now than in my early watching. The swifts approve. Their flight patterns adjust accordingly.

*[paper-like rustling of pages, scratching of writing implement]*

Observation 9,724: Third nest-cluster from western curve shows structural adaptation to increased condensation. Swift-maker using higher proportion of mineral-dense mud, less fiber. Ingenious solution. Evolution visible within single lifetime. Blessed am I to witness.

*[sudden rush of wings, dozens of birds moving in unison]*

Ah! The shadow-play begins. Sun-angle optimal. Watch how they—yes, there—intersecting flight paths creating ephemeral patterns on stone. The language of motion. Some days I almost understand.

*[soft tapping of finger against rock]*

Visitor approaches. Two days' journey away still. I feel their footfalls through the mountain's bones. Seeking knowledge. Acceptable purpose. I will prepare the greeting tea.

*[long silence, broken only by water drops and distant bird calls]*

Sometimes I forget... before. Before the alcove claimed me. Was there before? The village remembers a child taken in fever. The mountain remembers differently. Both memories reside in me, neither fully mine.

*[sound of small stones being arranged in pattern]*

The rookery's heartbeat remains strong. Eight hundred seventeen active nests this season. Twenty-nine empty. Favorable ratio. The balance holds.

*[soft whistle, mimicking bird call but with subtle variations]*

Tonight I dream-walk the paths of sky with you, my charges. My featherless body remains below, but something of me flies. Does some essence of swift remain earthbound in exchange? The great balance demands symmetry in all things.

*[water sounds increase, gentle splashing]*

Evening pool-communion begins. I submerge my hands. Information flows both ways. I filter toxins through my skin; the water yields its collected wisdom.

*[rhythmic humming, almost subsonic]*

The pendant grows heavier. Each guardian adds one fragment. Mine came from the miraculous surviving egg after the lightning strike. Blue-black with silver speckling. When my time ends, it will guide the next watcher home.

*[soft scraping of stone against stone]*

I adjust the hearth stones microscopically eastward. Fifth time this cycle. The floor shifts beneath us all. So gradual. No swift will notice. Only the mountain and I keep count.

*[deep, resonant inhalation followed by whispered words]*

I am the breathing of the stone.
I am the memory of water.
I am the guardian of flight.
I am the forty-third watcher.
I am the alcove's voice.

*[flutter of wings fading to silence]*

Night descends. The rookery dreams. I remain vigilant. Always vigilant. Always here.
