This is Menau. We are Menau. I am transmitting from the Third Terrace, where the morning mist-gathering has completed its seventh cycle. The water-markers show three more finger-widths until the Ceremony of Surrender for the Amber Steps.

The Council of Tides has interpreted the dream-currents. Listener, if you receive this: we do not fear the rising. The city ascending into water is the same as water ascending into city. Zhi-ka-ma. There is no word in your tongue. The becoming-through-submergence.

~static increases~

[sound of rhythmic breathing, gradually slowing]

I will dive to the Memory Chambers at tomorrow's slack tide. The coral has completed its growth over the Western Archives. We transfer the knowledge to our bodies now. The stone remembers for us until it, too, becomes something else.

Do not call us drowning. The boundary between is where we have always lived. Your people build walls against water. We build doorways for it.

[underwater acoustics, echoing voices singing in overlapping patterns]

The youngest children born this season have stronger webbing than before. The Aspect of Boundary says this is the seventh generation showing the adaptation. In nine more, we may no longer need the breathing pockets in the lower terraces.

I must speak of the lights below the lowest terrace. The memory-divers report they grow stronger. Patterns not seen before. The Tidal Council believes—

~transmission cuts out momentarily~

[sound of rain beginning to fall, creating complex percussive patterns on different surfaces]

We have observed your vessels circling our waters. We understand curiosity. It is the beginning of wisdom. But wisdom completes itself in acceptance. Your instruments measure only what can be measured.

~crackle, transmission weakens~

Listen: the submerged bells of the Founder's Temple still ring with each tide change, though no hand has touched them in twelve generations. Sound travels differently through water. Through flesh. Through time.

[deep rumbling sound, like distant thunder]

The elders say the mountain beneath us shifts in its long sleep. The Aspect of Preservation has begun carving the migration charts into the highest living coral. When the time comes, we will follow the currents to the next dwelling place.

~signal strengthens briefly~

I leave this transmission as we leave our markers. Not to be found, but to exist as part of the pattern. Your scholars will one day discover our lowest terraces, encased in coral and stone, and believe they have found a dead civilization.

[sound of gentle laughter, bubbling through water]

There is no death in transition, only transformation. The rising waters are not our end but our becoming. Each submerged terrace is a step on the journey we always knew we would take.

~transmission fading~

I return now to the water-meditation. The children gather for the history-singing at mid-tide. We carry the city within us now. Stone returns to water. Water returns to mist. We return to—
