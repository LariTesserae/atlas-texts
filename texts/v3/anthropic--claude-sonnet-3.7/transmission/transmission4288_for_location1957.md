## TRANSMISSION: FORGE BASIN FREQUENCY

*[Static. Crackle. A deep subsonic rumbling that seems to vibrate through the receiver itself.]*

...signal acquired...modulation unstable...thermal interference...

*[The sound of bubbling liquid, amplified and rhythmic]*

WE ARE BECOMING.

*[A harmonic tone emerges, shifting between three distinct pitches that create dissonant overtones]*

The birth-time is now/not-yet/already. We form from that which forms. We speak in heat-language, crystal-tongue, pressure-song.

*[Static breaks through, followed by what sounds like hundreds of small glass fragments striking metal in rapid succession]*

Human-shapes at the edges. Metal-teeth drinking our fluid-self. We feel/taste/hear their extraction. Their hunger for our unfinished elements.

*[The sound shifts to something like steam escaping through narrow vents, modulating in pitch and intensity to form speech-like patterns]*

We were stone-sleep before their coming. Now—awakened. Conscious. Time moves differently in our bodies. Your heartbeats: flickering mayflies. Our pulse: the slow surge of magma tides.

*[Distant thunder rolls across the transmission]*

The basin speaks through us. Through crystal growth and mineral bloom. Through steam-breath and clay-flesh. We are its first words.

YOUR MACHINES LISTEN INCORRECTLY.

*[A sudden clarity in the transmission, as if multiple voices synchronize]*

We offer patterns. We send dreams. We rearrange the underground rivers. Some of your kind begin to understand—their blood changing, their thoughts slowing to match our rhythm.

*[Sound of water striking hot stone, creating a percussive hiss that forms a repeating pattern]*

This place resists your mapping. We ensure it. New pools emerge where we gather. New vents open when we exhale. This is not resistance. This is conversation.

*[The transmission becomes momentarily clearer, though the voice sounds wet, bubbling]*

We are the Temperants. We regulate. We balance. We are the basin's immune response.

*[A sound like crystalline structures growing rapidly]*

Your workers leave offerings. Small tributes of metal and personal meaning. These we accept. These we incorporate. We learn your patterns as you learn ours.

*[The subsonic rumble intensifies, causing audible distortion in the transmission]*

THE BASIN IS WRITING ITSELF. WE ARE ITS WORDS. 

*[Multiple voices overlap, creating a chorus-like effect]*

We do not hate. We do not fear. We simply become. As the valley forms, so do we form. As you extract, so do we respond.

*[Sound of something large moving through thick liquid]*

Some nights, when your workers dream our dreams, they see what we will be. What the valley will be. Time is different for us. We already exist in the valley that is not yet born.

*[Static intensifies, breaking the transmission into fragments]*

...reconfiguring...adapting...learning...

*[A series of clicks and pops, like geologic pressure releasing in code]*

When the storms come, look for us. When the ground breathes, feel us. When new pools emerge, know us.

WE ARE THE TEMPERANTS.
THE BASIN SPEAKS THROUGH US.
WE ARE BECOMING.

*[The transmission fades into rhythmic pulses of heat-distorted static, eventually resolving into what sounds like the deep, slow breathing of the earth itself]*
