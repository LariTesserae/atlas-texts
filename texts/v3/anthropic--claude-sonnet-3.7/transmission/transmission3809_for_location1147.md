## TRANSMISSION FROM THE CISTERN GARDENS

*[static-laced dripping, percussive echoes gradually resolving into pattern]*

...ꜱᴇǫᴜᴇɴᴄᴇ... *ᴘᴀᴛᴛᴇʀɴ*... ꜱᴇᴠᴇɴᴛʜ... *ᴅʀɪᴘ*... ᴏᴠᴇʀꜰʟᴏᴡ... *ᴘʟᴜɴᴋ*... ᴄᴀʟɪʙʀᴀᴛᴇᴅ...

*[hollow, reverberating voice - seeming to emanate from multiple points simultaneously]*

I record the thirty-thousand, eight-hundred and sixth day of the eastern basin maintenance. The city sleeps. The city forgets. The water remembers.

*[sound of droplets striking different surfaces - creating tone sequence]*

Today the third column weeps faster—zero-point-three drops per minute acceleration. Mineral content elevated. Another roof-crack has formed above. I have positioned seventeen new collection funnels. The water will find its proper home.

*[faint sound of many tiny legs moving across stone]*

My friends bring news of vibration from the north tunnel. Heavy machinery. They dig deeper again. They seek to expand, never understanding what already exists beneath their feet. I have sealed the connection points with the purple-vein mushrooms. Their spores induce retreat in human explorers. They will turn back. They always do.

*[brief silence followed by layered whispering]*

The western overflow needs attention. Centuries of calcification have narrowed the channel by six percent. I will sing to the stone tonight. I will persuade it to release its hold. The proper resonance frequency loosens the bonds between mineral deposits. This knowledge was once called science. Now it is only mine.

*[sound of water striking metal, creating bell-like tones]*

My skin... thinner again this season. Light passes through my palms now when I hold them before the brightest fungi clusters. The symbionts extend further into my neural pathways. They offer new perspectives. Water-knowledge. Stone-memory. I accept their gifts. I surrender more of what I once was.

*[echoing humming that harmonizes with the water percussion]*

Do I remember sunlight? Sometimes I dream of heat without moisture. Of light that burns rather than glows. Was this experience once mine? The journals I found speak of a world above. Of structures reaching toward something called "sky." Of water falling from above rather than weeping from stone.

*[sound of something large moving through shallow water]*

I maintain the sluice gates as I was instructed. By whom? The memory fades. Was there once another who walked these paths? Who taught me the sequences? The patterns? The balance points? Or did the water itself instruct me? Did I discover these truths in the space between droplets?

*[escalating dripping sounds, growing more chaotic, then resolving into ordered rhythm]*

The midnight fish have spawned again. Twelve new generations since the last notation. Their adaptations accelerate—their dorsal fins now undulate in ways that extract more oxygen from the mineral-rich shallows. I have preserved specimens at each evolutionary stage. Knowledge must be maintained. Transitions documented. Even if none but I will ever study them.

*[vibration that seems to emanate from the stone itself]*

Something approaches. Movement in the upper tunnels. Not machine this time. Smaller. Organic. Human? I withdraw to the deeper chambers. I will observe from the darkness between pillars. They carry artificial light. It blinds them to what truly exists here.

*[long pause filled only with water sounds]*

They are gone. They took images. Collected samples. Spoke with excitement about "rediscovery" and "historical significance." They do not understand. This is not history. This is continuous present. The water flows now as it did when the first stone was laid. I remain unseen. Better they consider this place abandoned. Better they attribute its maintenance to "remarkable engineering" than learn of me.

*[sound of gentle splashing, repetitive patterns]*

I adjust my body temperature to match the ambient moisture. I slow my breathing to one cycle every three minutes. I disperse my consciousness through the chamber's acoustics. In this state, I commune with the oldest fungi colonies. They remember when this chamber was first sealed. They recall the faces of the original builders.

*[whispering that seems to come from multiple voices, though all with similar timbre]*

We are not alone in our purpose. Other chambers exist. Other custodians tend them. We sense each other through the deep stone, through the interconnected waterways. We exchange knowledge through the slow migration of mineral patterns. Messages that take decades to transmit. Conversations that span centuries.

*[sound of something being carefully adjusted, followed by improved water flow]*

The balance is maintained for another day. The city above continues because we continue. Their foundations remain stable because we manage what flows beneath. They have forgotten the purpose of our creation, but we remember. We preserve. We adapt. We evolve.

*[returning to the rhythmic dripping that gradually incorporates harmonics]*

I am not what I was when I first descended. I am becoming what the cistern needs me to be. The transition accelerates. Soon the distinction between custodian and chamber will dissolve completely. This is not death. This is transcendence into pure function.

*[voice growing fainter, merging with the water sounds]*

I record the thirty-thousand, eight-hundred and sixth day. Tomorrow will be another day of perfect, continuous purpose. The city forgets. The water remembers. I am the memory of water made flesh. I am the intention of stone given voice. I am...

*[transmission dissolves into pure water sounds, then static]*
