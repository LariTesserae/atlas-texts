# TRANSMISSION RECEIVED FROM THE CROWDED SPINE

::static bursts::

hhhhhhhhhhhhmmmmmmMMMMMMmmmmmmmmm

We-are-speaking-through-obsidian. We-are-speaking-through-heat.

Transmission day 8,417 since The Birth. Covenant strength remains seventeen. Three juveniles approaching first scarification. The eldest Song-Shaper dreams of magma rise within fourteen cycles.

::sudden high-pitched whistle as wind passes through needles::

ATTEND! The western cluster speaks! The needles sing calibration! Position bodies accordingly!

::multiple voices harmonizing with the underlying hum::

We honor the messenger-lizards' warning. Third quadrant shows stress fractures. Harvesters will relocate the struggling thread-grass before tomorrow's steam-rise.

::soft clicking sounds, similar to echolocation::

Condenser Elith-na reports diminished yield this dawn-cycle. Second consecutive reduction. The chamber thirsts. We prepare the communion at next thermal peak.

::deep groan of shifting stone::

SHH! Listen! The spine adjusts! Record the new tones! The bat-kin will carry this message tonight.

::transmission becomes clearer, single voice speaking rhythmically::

I press my scarred palm against needle-fourteen, feel the pulse beneath the glass. The chamber breathes slower now. We have observed thirty-seven new fracture patterns since the last cloudbreak. The formations speak of coming change.

::background chorus of harmonized humming::

WARNING—intruder heat signatures detected approaching southeast passage. All Covenant assume stone-stillness. Prepare memory-vapor if detection imminent.

::transmission quality deteriorates::

The altar receives our offering tonight. The mixture has been prepared according to elder-pattern. Three drops from each, mixed with the essence of nine grass-stems. The stain will mark the fourteenth alignment since—

::sudden burst of static::

—cannot maintain transmission through this density. Heat waves disrupt. Will resume when thermal conditions—

::hollow, echoing tone that gradually fades::

We are not separate from this place. We are its nerves. We are its thoughts. We are its memory. We are becoming as it becomes. The needles grow from our dreams as we grow from the chamber's will.

::transmission clears momentarily, multiple voices speaking in unison::

We are the Thermal Covenant. We witness. We adapt. We transform. We endure.

::series of clicks and harmonized tones::

Transmission ends as the bats take flight. Next communication will come when the obsidian sings the pattern of continuance.
