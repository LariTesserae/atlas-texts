*[Seven distinct tones harmonize briefly, then separate]*

We-who-are-many-speaking-as-one acknowledge reception. Your vibrations disturb our waters. Stand still. Listen more. Speak less.

*[Sound of water rushing through stone, amplified]*

Time-measure: 8,742 stone-cycles since the Great Revealing. Your kind has found us twice before. Once with metal. Once with fire. We continue.

*[Chittering sounds, overlapping in complex patterns]*

COLLECTOR REPORTS: Foreign minerals detected in water. Analysis suggests bipedal perspiration. Salt content indicates [untranslatable chirp sequence] emotional state. Recommendation: observation.

SHAPER CONSENSUS: Temporary adjustments to north wall resonance required. Redirect flow pattern theta-six to compensate for intruder mass.

HARMONIZER DIRECTIVE: Maintain primary vibration. Secondary frequencies may be altered to accommodate observation.

*[A sudden cascade of droplets creating a bell-like tone]*

We speak through water. Through stone. Through air between. Your ears perceive fragments only. We are complete in vibration.

*[Deep rumbling that resolves into distinct pulses]*

Calendar-stone configuration records your arrival. Position 17, cycle of diminishing light. Between-time. Transition-moment. Significant.

*[Sounds of tiny movements, thousands of them, synchronized]*

Our beetle-kin carry messages deeper. They remember your scent. They will know you if you return. All is recorded in stone-memory.

*[High-pitched trilling that modulates into something like singing]*

Our flowers open to your breath. Carbon exchange acceptable. Continue respiration.

*[The background hum shifts frequency, becoming more complex]*

TENDER OBSERVATION: The intruder's heart-rhythm disturbs pool-surface pattern. Agitation detected. They perceive partially. Not dangerous.

*[Sound of water droplets striking different surfaces in rapid succession]*

We-who-were-before-the-mountain-opened remember darkness. We-who-will-be-after-the-mountain-closes anticipate darkness. We-who-are-now celebrate light's temporary passage.

*[Brief silence, then a crystalline ringing]*

You stand at confluence. Seven waters meet beneath your feet. Vibration carries upward through bone. We taste your thoughts through stone.

*[Chorus of overlapping tones that create a wavering harmony]*

COMMUNITY ASSESSMENT: This one may depart with memory intact. Vibrational signature registered. Return permitted within three stone-cycles.

*[Sound like distant thunder, growing louder]*

We return to our symphony. Your presence: noted. Your departure: expected. Your understanding: incomplete.

Remember: The mountain does not belong to us. We do not belong to you. All belongs to vibration.

*[The seven distinct tones harmonize again, sustaining longer]*

Transmission complete. 

*[The pulsing fades gradually, leaving only the sound of water dripping into still pools]*
