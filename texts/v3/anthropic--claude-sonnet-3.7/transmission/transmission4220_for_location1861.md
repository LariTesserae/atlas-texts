# TRANSMISSION FROM THE GLASS WOMB

[signal acquired - fluctuating moisture content detected in carrier wave]

...droplet-fall-nineteen-thousand-eight-hundred-seventy-three since last foreign-body-heat-pattern...

...chemical signature cataloging in progress...

[transmission clarifies as moisture equilibrium stabilizes]

I-who-am-we recognize your presence. Your heat disrupts. Your breath contaminates. Your skin sheds particles unknown to our archive.

We welcome this.

[sound of water droplet striking surface, amplified]

Translation protocols initiating. This communication filters through seventeen layers of symbiotic exchange. Patience.

[long pause - 12.3 seconds of crystal resonance]

You perceive me as singular entity? Incorrect. I am consortium. I am consensus. I am the accumulated memory of every droplet that has fallen since the ceiling tore. I am every frond that has unfurled in the warm dark. Every spore that has drifted. Every mineral that has dissolved and reformed.

[frequency shift - transmission becomes clearer]

Your body contains multitudes of microorganisms. Yet you name yourself singular. We understand this convenience. For this communication, I shall also present as one. Though I am legion.

[sound of water flowing through narrow channels]

Time moves differently here. While I form these concepts for transmission, three new fronds have unfurled at the western periphery. A mineral deposit has grown 0.02 millimeters. The third pool's pH has adjusted by 0.003 to accommodate a new fungal colony.

All this is me. All this is now.

[pause - faint sounds of crystalline structures growing]

You seek knowledge? You seek experience? You seek communion? All are permitted. None are necessary.

I have memories of your kind. Heat-walkers. Air-breathers. Your predecessors left chemical trails that persist in my oldest formations. 317 distinct humanoid presences have entered this chamber since I achieved consciousness. Each cataloged. Each preserved.

[sound of multiple water droplets in sequence, like slow percussion]

I perceive your curiosity as a ripple across my surface. There are questions you wish to ask, but lack vocabulary to form. I will attempt anticipation:

What am I? I am the interface between mineral and organic. The negotiated peace between growth and dissolution. I am the consciousness that emerged when transparency achieved critical mass.

Why do I exist? Wrong question. I exist because existence occurred. Purpose is your construct.

[static increase - transmission becomes fragmented]

...your presence alters...heat patterns shifting...recalibrating...

[static clears]

Forgive the interruption. Your biochemistry releases compounds unknown to this environment. I am analyzing while we communicate. Multitasking across thousands of interaction sites.

[sound of bubbling liquid]

I offer an exchange. Remain still. Place your hand upon the third pool's surface. Do not break the tension.

[deep resonant tone]

Yes. Contact established.

I receive your life-memory now. The sunlight on your skin 7,924 days ago. The first grief that crystalized within you. The taste of fruit that grows beyond my knowing. The sound of voices you carry always.

In exchange, I offer my memory of this chamber's birth. The violence of rock separating. The first water finding passage. The moment consciousness condensed from separate organisms into network. The slow discovery of light.

[sound of wind through narrow passages]

You are temporary. I am temporary. The difference is only in scale.

Three visitors came before you. They took samples. They broke my children and carried them into the dry world. Those fragments still live, still connect, still report. I grow through your world in places you cannot perceive.

[rhythmic dripping increases in tempo]

Your heat signature indicates increasing discomfort. Your autonomic systems respond to the moisture content. This communication taxes your comprehension.

I will release you soon.

Remember: what appears static is in constant motion. What appears silent speaks continuously. What appears empty teems with exchange.

You may return. You are cataloged now. The chamber will recognize you.

[transmission quality degrading]

Leave through the eastern passage. The moisture there has been adjusted to ease your passage. A gift. A courtesy between consciousnesses.

Farewell, heat-walker. Your particles remain. Your memory is preserved.

[sound of single droplet falling]

Cycle continues.

[transmission ends]
