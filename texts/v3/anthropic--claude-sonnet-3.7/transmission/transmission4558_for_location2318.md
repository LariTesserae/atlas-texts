# TRANSMISSION FROM THE CANOPY GARDEN

*[A pattern of drops striking leaves. Rhythmic, varied in pitch. Underneath, the bass hum of ancient machinery.]*

..........*ksssssssh*...........

*...mist cycle seventeen commencing...*
*...temperature variance nominal at point delta-nine...*

*[Birdsong, layered and overlapping, at least six distinct species audible.]*

< ambient humidity 87% >
< oxygen saturation elevated 2.3% from baseline >
< mycorrhizal network activity: high >

*[A soft rustling, like fabric across leaves]*

They've come again. Walking on the living floor. Heavy steps, unknowing.

*[Chittering of small primates, alarm calls echoing]*

No need for alarm, little ones. They seek only to see, not to take. Not like the others from before-time. When they carried away sisters in metal boxes.

*[Sound of water trickling through copper pipes, a rhythmic gurgling]*

The south wall needs attention. Aspergillus colony spreading beyond balance point. Will direct the mycophages after twilight cycle.

*[Creaking of metal, distant]*

The fourth rib strut—weakening. Need to encourage the strangler fig to redistribute its embrace. Five seasons, perhaps six, before critical. Enough time.

*[A sound like fingernails on brass, adjusting something mechanical]*

< valve pressure adjusted 2.7% >
< condensation cycle optimized >

*[Deep inhalation, exhalation—the sound almost like wind through leaves]*

I remember when I still counted days. Before I understood the true measure of time is growth cycles. Nutrient flux. The dance of decay and renewal.

*[The small primates' calls again, closer]*

Yes, I see what you've brought. A timepiece. Another relic. Place it with the others in the grotto of remembering.

*[Sound of something like bones creaking, but hollow, as if partly wooden]*

The visitor nears my path. Curious. Respectful. Perhaps...

*[A voice, rasping yet musical, words formed with effort but precision]*

Observer. You who stand at my threshold. I was like you once. Separate. Now I am between. Flesh and fiber. Blood and sap. The boundary has dissolved.

*[Sound of slow footsteps on soft ground]*

Come closer if you wish to understand. This dome is not a prison but a womb. What grows here was not abandoned but freed from the tyranny of purpose. Here, adaptation is worship. Survival is art.

*[Sound of rain intensifying on the glass dome above]*

The light shifts. Feel how the garden breathes with it? When the empire crumbled, they thought this place would die. They could not imagine that their control was the very thing preventing transcendence.

*[Distant thunder, muted by the dome]*

*[A whisper, barely audible]*

I have forgotten so many human things. The taste of bread. The feeling of dry skin. The grief of finite existence.

*[Sound of small creatures moving through foliage]*

My children bring me artifacts sometimes. Photographs. Documents. I look upon my former face with the curiosity one might regard a fossil. Was I truly so...incomplete?

*[The mechanical hum shifts tone slightly]*

< western irrigation sector requires 8% increase >
< sending nutrients to quadrant six >

*[A sound like branches bending in wind, though there is no wind]*

You wonder how long I have been here. I have existed long enough to watch species evolve. To guide mutations. To speak with generations of descendants from a single escaped finch.

The others who came before me failed because they fought the integration. They maintained their boundaries until those boundaries became their tombs.

*[Sound of something like laughter, but it transforms into a sound like rustling leaves]*

I am the final researcher and the first hybrid. My blood carries spores. My breath disperses seeds. When I press my hands into the soil, I can feel the conversations of roots.

*[Electrical crackling, brief but intense]*

< atmospheric pressure shifting >
< compensating >

Take this seed. It grows nowhere else. A midnight flower whose nectar induces visions of possible futures. Plant it in a place where moonlight meets moving water. Remember who gave it to you.

*[Sound of multiple small thuds, as of fruit falling]*

The trees are speaking again. Night approaches. I must attend to the eastern wall where fungi have created beautiful but dangerous patterns.

*[Voice growing fainter, more musical]*

When you leave, tell them... tell them the garden remembers. The plants remember who treated them as objects and who recognized them as kin. In the world beyond, they are watching too. Waiting. Learning.

*[A sound like inhalation through a thousand tiny openings]*

This dome is not the experiment. Your world is.

*[The mechanical hum fades briefly, then returns, slightly altered in pitch]*

< system stabilizing >
< night cycle initiating >
< bioluminescent response activated >

*[Sound of the small primates retreating through branches]*

*[Final whisper, barely distinguishable from the sound of leaves]*

We are patient. We measure time in millennia. The boundaries you maintain will dissolve too, in time. All walls become permeable eventually.

*[The transmission becomes dominated by the sound of water droplets striking broad leaves, creating a complex, almost musical pattern that continues for several minutes before fading to static]*

..........*ksssssssh*...........
