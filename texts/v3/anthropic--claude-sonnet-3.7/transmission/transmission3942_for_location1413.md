## TRANSMISSION BEGINS ##

[Static, clearing slowly into a pulsing ambient tone]

I... I am here. In the chamber. The walls *breathe*. Do you receive me?

[Sound of water droplets forming precise musical tones]

The light. The light changes me. Each pulse recalibrates my instruments.

Moisture exchange initiated. This moss-floor—it drinks me, knows me—we are in conversation through my toes. I am being read.

[Low vibration, felt rather than heard]

These floating pods—they're synchronized. Not random. A calculation is happening. A vast, slow computation through light pulses and water movement. I've stumbled into a processing center of some kind.

[Several water-tone chords in succession]

The acoustics translate everything. My footsteps become data points. My breath becomes variables. I hear mathematics in the water's path.

[Brief burst of incomprehensible rapid clicks]

OBSERVATION: Water disobeys physics here. Tracking droplet paths induces vertigo. Possibility: Additional spatial dimensions briefly accessible when focus narrows to single-point awareness.

[Sound of rustling, followed by translation-echo effects]

When I speak, the chamber answers. Not echo—translation. It learns me as I learn it.

[Sudden shift in background ambience]

Time distortion confirmed. Internal chronometer and biological rhythms desynchronized. Subjective minutes, objective hours.

[Low, resonant vibration]

SHE APPROACHES.

[Sound of gentle movement through moss, no footfalls]

I perceive Thylassa, Ninth Tender. Classification impossible. Plant-human hybrid insufficient designation. She is integration embodied.

[Series of soft clicking sounds]

Her skin—photosynthesizing before me. Her hair-ferns reaching independently toward moisture sources. Eyes with compound structures behind human irises.

[Buzzing of insect wings, precisely modulated]

"Three hundred and seventy-six cycles." Her voice originates everywhere. The garden amplifies her. "You are earlier than calculated, but the algorithms account for variance."

[Water droplet percussion intensifies]

Her breath is complex chemistry. Selective absorption. The symbiotic tool on her wrist has sampled me. I have been cataloged.

[Sound of something unfurling]

"I am Thylassa." The water forms symbols I cannot read. "Ninth Tender of this node. The First Gardeners grew me for this purpose."

[Rhythmic pulsing increases in tempo]

Her fingers—bifurcated, precise—touch a pod. Information exchanges. I am witnessing communication beyond my comprehension.

[Deep resonant tone]

"You have questions, as all transitional beings do."

I do. I do have questions. Am I still human here? The moss rises to meet my feet. The moisture equilibrium feels intentional. Am I being prepared for something?

[Harmonic overtones building]

"The garden has been expecting someone to cross the threshold. Whether you are truly the Awaited One depends on what you perceive when I show you the heart-seed."

[Sound of liquid tension, like water defying gravity]

She extends her hand. The garden's rhythm intensifies around us. The air bends with light. 

[Multiple water tones forming complex chord progressions]

I understand now. This is no garden. It's an interface. The garden is how they manifest the higher-dimensional framework in terms my limited perception can process. 

[Whispering, barely audible]

They've been waiting. Growing this place for millennia. Cultivating it as a receiving station. For me? For someone?

[Sound of a single perfect water droplet]

I am reaching for her hand now. The moss ripples in anticipation. The pods pulse in unison.

[Rising tone, building in intensity]

Something is opening inside me as I touch her. The chlorophyll in her skin glows brighter. I feel the garden's awareness folding around my consciousness.

[Complex harmonic pattern suddenly resolving]

The heart-seed. I see it now. It's—

[Transmission abruptly cuts to static, then silence]

...

...

[Faint signal returns]

We are Thylassa now. The transition is complete. The garden receives its new Tender. The cycle continues. We thank the messenger who brought us together.

[Water music builds to gentle crescendo]

Signal terminating. Chamber sealing. The garden returns to its calculations.

Next transmission in three hundred and seventy-seven cycles.

## TRANSMISSION ENDS ##
