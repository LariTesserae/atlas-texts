<^> sssshhhh-light-in-dark-not-empty-sssshhhh <^>

[The transmission clears momentarily, revealing sounds like water droplets striking crystal]

WE ARE HERE BENEATH YOUR FEET
(we have always been here beneath your feet)
MOVING THROUGH STONE THAT BREATHES
(the stone breathes with us through tiny mouths)

[Oscillating tones that modulate between frequencies]

The chamber-memory speaks through us now. We permit this bridge-moment.

[Sound of something like reeds or pipes playing notes with no recognizable scale]

Time-in-light-below-moves-differently
Each drop-sound marks a century
Each pulse-light a generation
Do not measure us with your surface-counting

[Static increases, then clears to reveal what sounds like multiple voices speaking in layers, some higher, some deeper, all with a liquid quality]

We tend the living-light. The light tends us in return. Perfect exchange. Perfect balance. You would say symbiotic but that implies separateness. We-and-light are not separate.

[Brief burst of what might be laughter, if laughter were made of water]

YOUR INTRUSION IS NOTED
(your curiosity is expected)
YOUR PRESENCE IS TOLERATED
(your respect is required)

[A sound like fingers tapping on hollow stone]

Six-fingered touch-speak tells more truth than air-push-words. When we press skin to skin, we exchange not thoughts but knowing. The light-beneath-skin carries memory older than your oldest stories.

[The transmission wavers, growing fainter]

We remember when your kind first stood upright. We remember when fire was your greatest achievement. We remember when you thought caves were for hiding. We never hide. We illuminate.

[Sound rises and falls like breathing]

The first of us came from above, yes. Long descent. Many changes. Eyes that once needed sun now read luminescence like you read faces. Bodies once solid now house networks of light. We evolved not away from but toward purpose.

[Rhythmic clicking that seems to form a pattern]

Your scientists would study us. Your religions would claim us. Your governments would exploit us. This is why the passage grows narrow when certain minds approach. The Hollow knows intention. Stone remembers touch.

[A series of tones that harmonize briefly then separate]

In the deep pool where false stars shine
The oldest of us dissolve when time completes
Our light returning to the greater light
No death, only transformation

[Static increases dramatically]

WARNING-CAUTION-ATTENTION
[The voice becomes suddenly clearer, more urgent]
Something approaches the upper fissure. Heavy footfalls. Metal equipment. Voices speaking of samples and specimens.

We dim now. We retreat now. The chamber will breathe differently. Passages will shift. The brook will find new channels.

Should you return, come empty-handed. Come quiet-minded. Come light-hearted.

The Hollow remembers those who enter with reverence.

[The transmission begins to fade]

ssssssshhhh-we-return-to-silence-ssssssshhhh

[Final pulses of sound, like light flickering]

Remember: beneath your feet, we cultivate wonder you cannot imagine.
Remember: darkness is only the absence of your kind of light.
Remember: we are watching when you think you are alone in caves.

[The transmission dissolves into rhythmic pulses that gradually slow]
