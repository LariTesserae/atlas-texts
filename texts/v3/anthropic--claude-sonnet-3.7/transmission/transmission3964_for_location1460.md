*[The transmission begins as gentle drops of water striking cave pools—irregular but rhythmic, creating a subtle underlying beat that never fully resolves. Beneath this percussion rises a chorus of voices, sometimes singing in unison, sometimes in counterpoint, creating harmonies that shouldn't be possible from human throats.]*

drip-drop-drrrip
listen-to-the-flow-between-words
drip-drop-drrrrrip

*[A single voice emerges, simultaneously producing two tones—one a steady bass note, the other a wandering melody that follows the water patterns]*

We of the endless night send greeting-flow
To those-who-live-in-burning-brightness

*[Sound of many feet moving through shallow water]*

The Council of Nine Pools has authorized this transmission
After thirteen cycles of deliberation
The water has spoken through us
And we through the water

*[Gentle splashing, followed by a chorus of harmonic whispers]*

You ask of Humlosh?
You ask of the Dripskins?
Listen then, surface-dweller
Let our song-water reach your dry ears

*[The percussion of water drops intensifies, joining with what sounds like fingernails tapping on hollow stone tubes of various lengths]*

In darkness we see
In wetness we breathe
When you close your eyes in fear
We open ours in knowing

Our city lives and grows like the stone itself
Molecule by molecule
Drip by sacred drip
Time measured not in your sun-cycles
But in the slow breath of the mountain

*[A sudden chorus of flute-like tones as multiple "singing slugs" are gently disturbed, creating an eerie harmony]*

We trade with the mountain folk
They fear our eyes, our skin
They say we have become like the cave itself
They are not wrong

*[Sound of something heavy being dragged through water, followed by soft chiming of cave pearls]*

The First Pool tells us: once we were as you
Centuries ago, we sought refuge here
From what calamity? The songs have forgotten
But the water remembers
Always the water remembers

*[The voices shift, becoming more resonant, echoing as if in a vast chamber]*

You wish to know of our gods?
We worship no gods
We worship THE god
The eternal drip
The water that carves stone
That which is soft yet shapes the hard
That which falls yet rises again as mist

*[Sounds of many people submerging in water, followed by bubbling speech that somehow remains intelligible]*

The Deepkeeper of Limestone Pool speaks now:
Our children are born in water
Our elders dissolve into water
Between these moments
We are but temporary vessels
Carrying water from one pool to the next

*[The singing becomes more complex, with different rhythmic patterns overlapping—some voices matching the dripping stalactites, others creating counterpoint]*

Three-Pebble, our eldest Listener, journeyed seven days to the Deep Chamber
To receive this message for your kind:

*[A single, ancient-sounding voice emerges, accompanied by the gentle stroking of water-filled crystal bowls]*

"Tell them we see in their future what we saw in our past
The rains that never cease
The waters that rise without mercy
The drowning of cities built on sand

Tell them we have learned to float
When one cannot swim
To breathe the dampness between water drops
When air becomes scarce

Tell them there is wisdom in adaptation
Not resistance"

*[The percussion suddenly intensifies, water drops falling faster, creating an almost urgent rhythm]*

Surface-dweller, your world grows wetter
Your skies weep without ceasing
Your oceans swell beyond boundaries
When you tire of fighting the inevitable
When your towers fall and your fields drown
Remember the Dripskins
Remember Humlosh

We will welcome your children's children
When they come seeking the high caves
When they learn to sing with the water
Not against it

*[The harmonies shift to a haunting minor key, voices blending with what sounds like wind through narrow passages]*

This transmission ends
As all things must
Returning to the drip
The eternal drip
From which all patterns emerge

drip-drop-drip
listen-to-the-flow-between-words
drip-drop-drrrrrip

*[The voices gradually fade, leaving only the water sounds, which continue for several moments before they too diminish into silence. Just before the transmission ends completely, a single child's voice whispers:]*

We are waiting for you in the darkness
We have always been waiting

*[Static, then silence]*
