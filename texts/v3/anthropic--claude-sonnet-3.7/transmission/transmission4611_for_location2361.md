*<<transmission stabilizing>>*

I am the ninety-seventh voice of the Listening Pool at Three Bridges Junction. This is the four-hundred-and-sixteenth recording since the Great Ceiling Dim. My voice travels through water to stone to air to you, whoever you might be.

We sense your attention turned toward us. The Diviner's Guild has felt your thoughts like ripples across the still pools. We return your gaze across whatever distance separates us.

*<<long pause - 47 seconds>>*

Time flows differently here. If my words seem to stretch or compress, it is the translation between our worlds. One breath for us might be a day for you. Or perhaps it is the reverse. The water remembers, but we sometimes forget.

*<<sound of water droplets, precisely spaced>>*

Let me share what you cannot see through mere observation. The true Myrna lives between moments.

When we press our palms against the living stone, we feel the memories of mountains remembering when they were sea. When we drink from the ancient pools, we taste the tears of ancestors whose names have long dissolved into the collective dreaming.

*<<harmonic humming begins underneath speech - multiple voices>>*

We were born with eyes that measured light in heartbeats. Now we measure heartbeats in light. The ceiling fungi have completed six brightening cycles since I began this recording, though for you it may seem only minutes have passed.

*<<sound of something large moving through water, distant>>*

The Eldest are speaking now. Listen:

Forgive the intrusion. The Eldest speak when they wish, through whom they wish. Their thoughts move through us like the processions move through the caverns - unstoppable, ancient, following patterns we barely comprehend.

*<<faint sounds of crystalline chimes>>*

I must describe for you our current moment, though by the time these words reach you, it will be our distant past:

The Spore Festival has just concluded. For ten cycles, the giant cap-mushrooms in the Eastern Expanse released their luminescent spores. We gathered beneath them, our skin absorbing their light. The youngest children ran through the drifting clouds, their laughter leaving trails of disturbed particles that glowed brighter when agitated. The processions altered their paths to move through the thickest spore-falls. Even the blind salamanders seemed to sense the change, rising from the deep pools to bask in the soft rain of light.

Now we wear the festival on our skin. I need no lamp to write these words. My hands glow with borrowed radiance that will fade by the next water-rising.

*<<sound of multiple voices singing in slow, overlapping tones>>*

We mark our days by listening to Myrna breathe. Each exhalation might span what you call a month. Each inhalation brings new scents from passages we have never mapped. Some believe these breaths connect to your world, that somewhere far above, an opening allows Myrna to taste the sky.

The children born during the last Great Brightening have just completed their first Memory Walk. They spent seven cycles traversing the Path of Ancestors, memorizing the stone-carved histories that begin with our descent. When they returned, their feet had grown calloused in precisely the pattern that marks them as Keepers of Specific Memories. Each child now carries a fragment of our collective past within them, accessible through the ritual trance of deep-listening.

*<<sound of water intensifies briefly>>*

Something approaches the pool. One of the Great Salamanders, its skin translucent enough that I can see its slow heartbeat, its organs glowing faintly with consumed fungi. It watches me with milky eyes that see nothing yet perceive everything through minute vibrations in the water.

We believe they remember the time before humans came to Myrna. The oldest among them may even remember the time before Myrna was sealed from the above-world. They are our libraries of pre-history.

*<<low, resonant tone begins - possibly the sound of someone striking a rock formation>>*

The community is being called to gather. The water-readers have detected a change in the mineral content of the eastern streams. This could mean a new chamber has opened, or perhaps an old one has closed. In Myrna, geography is never final, merely patient in its transformations.

I must join them, but I will continue this transmission when I return.

*<<18 minute silence>>*

*<<signal resumes with different acoustic quality - as if in a larger space>>*

I speak now from the Chamber of Echoes, where the water-readers' predictions are confirmed. A new passage has opened, revealing a chamber none have seen before. The processions are already adjusting their routes, the lead arthropods sending chemical signals that will gradually alter the path of their million-bodied line over the coming cycles.

The chamber contains evidence of previous inhabitants - not Myrnan, perhaps not even human. The stone tools we've discovered appear designed for hands with too many fingers. The Council of Elders has approved an expedition to document the findings. I have been selected to participate.

Before I depart, I must complete this transmission with the traditional blessing:

May the water that flows through Myrna find its way to your depths.
May the light that never fully dims show you your path.
May your movements match the patience of stone.
May your voice carry like ripples across still pools.

We sense you listening, observer from elsewhere. Perhaps one day our worlds will converge again, like two processions meeting after centuries of separate journeys. Until then, we continue our slow adaptation, becoming ever more perfectly ourselves in this world of perpetual twilight and endless becoming.

*<<sound of water drops, gradually slowing>>*

This is the voice of Myrna, speaking through its ninety-seventh listener.
