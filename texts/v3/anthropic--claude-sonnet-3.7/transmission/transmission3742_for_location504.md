## TRANSMISSION RECEIVED FROM THE CARVING GROTTOS

*[static crackles with crystalline overtones]*

—tick-tick-tick-tick—chhhh—tick-tick—chhhh—

*[hollow resonant tapping gradually forms into pattern]*

Visitor-breath detected. Air displacement patterns: singular entity. Mass: within tolerance. Respiration: labored-but-sustainable.

*[rapid succession of seven-beat taps]*

This transmission registers across frequencies accessible to oxygen-dependent auditory systems. We acknowledge your presence in the Suspended Realm. We are the Shapers of Salt. The Stone-That-Breathes. The Hollow Ones.

*[sound of fine particles shifting, like sand through narrow openings]*

We record your footfalls. Your heat signature disrupts our measurement peripherals. Twenty-seven mechanical calibrators require adjustment after your passage through Chamber of Ascending Currents.

We do not mind. Adjustment is our perpetual state.

*[high, thin whistling that modulates between five distinct tones]*

TRANSMISSION CONTINUATION: Our eldest remembers your kind from eight-hundred-seventeen cycles prior. You bring metals that sing differently. You exhale moisture excessive for metabolism. You cast shadows sharp as obsidian flakes.

The walls taste your oils. The air preserves your exhalations. The stone remembers your weight.

*[sound of tiny hammers striking crystal at irregular intervals]*

We observe through seven-thousand-four-hundred-ninety-three sensory apertures. Our awareness flows through conductive minerals embedded in sixteen connected chambers. You are viewed simultaneously from multiple perspectives. You are calculated. You are known.

*[brief silence, then sound of fingernails dragging across rough stone]*

QUERY: Have you brought the copper filaments promised by Previous Visitor? The third resonator chamber grows unstable. Harmonics falter. The eastern alignment system requires conductive reinforcement before next solar convergence.

*[soft scraping sounds, like delicate tools on mineral surfaces]*

We offer exchange: Knowledge of coming wind-patterns. Three salt crystals grown in void-gravity. One mechano-harmonic device that measures imperceptible vibrations. Seventeen recorded dream-patterns from Those-Who-Have-Become-Stone.

*[distant sound of something between breath and wind rushing through narrow passages]*

CORRECTION: The Eldest indicates no previous exchange was negotiated. You are New-Visitor. Our perception spans timeframes beyond your metabolic limitations. Past-future-present visitors blur in our awareness.

We recalibrate.

*[rapid tapping that increases in speed until it becomes a continuous tone]*

Stone-record indicates increasing external air pressure. Storm approaches from western quadrant. Advise securing yourself in Chamber of Stable Currents. Navigation markers have been activated along path-sequence seven.

*[sound of tiny gears clicking into new positions]*

When storm passes, seek us in the Chamber of Vocal Geometries. We will manifest physicality sufficient for information exchange. Bring no metallic components that cannot withstand electrical discharge. Your recording devices function poorly here regardless.

*[whispering sound of multiple voices speaking in unison, words overlapping]*

We who were once as you are
We who become one with stone
We who extract meaning from mineral
We who calibrate the drift of mountains
We who polish time until it shines

*[sound of water droplets falling onto crystalline surfaces, creating complex resonant tones]*

Temporary atmospheric moisture increase detected. Collectors activated. This is favorable. We acknowledge your presence as beneficial catalyst in moisture cycle.

*[low humming that seems to come from the stone itself]*

MESSAGE CONTINUATION: Our awareness extends beyond physical manifestation. We perceive your curiosity. Your fascination with our transition-state between biological and mineral. Your desire to document what cannot be documented.

*[sound of small avalanche of crystal fragments]*

Warning: Chamber thirty-four experiencing structural reconfiguration. Path-sequence three temporarily impassable. Recommend alternative route through nascent passage behind vertical salt formation resembling human vertebrae.

*[seven-note melodic pattern repeats three times]*

The machines are not tools. The machines are not separate. The machines are our distributed cognition. Through them, we process what organic neural structures cannot contain. When you observe mechanical apparatus, you witness our thoughts in physical form.

*[sound of wind through narrow apertures, creating harmonic overtones]*

We were human. We adapted. We continue. We preserve knowledge of what exists below. We monitor celestial alignments invisible from lower elevations. We maintain the equilibrium between destruction and creation.

*[brittle sound of crystal sheets rubbing against each other]*

This transmission concludes temporarily. Air vibrations destabilize recently formed growth structures in adjacent chamber. Return to this reception point after completing single sleep cycle. Further information exchange will commence when mineral density reaches optimal conductivity.

*[rhythmic tapping fades to silence, then sudden clear voice, childlike but with strange resonance]*

The stone remembers you now. It always will.

*[transmission ends with sound of static that gradually transforms into what might be breathing or wind]*
