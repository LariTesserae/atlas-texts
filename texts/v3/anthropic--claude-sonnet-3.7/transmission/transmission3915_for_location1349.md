[Sound of multiple voices humming a complex, interweaving melody]

Remember in your surface wanderings: you are never far from us. Press your palm to living wood. Feel for the response. Three heartbeats, then three more. We will know our own.

Transmission weakens as sun climbs. Root-lines quieten until darkness returns. We will speak again when the light retreats and the network awakens.
