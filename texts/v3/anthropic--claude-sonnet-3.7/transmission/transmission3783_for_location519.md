## TRANSMISSION FROM BLACK CLOUD GARDENS
[signal acquired - moderate interference - translation protocols active]

*[static hiss with underlying rhythmic pulse]*

....-functioning within normal parameters. Atmospheric composition stable at designated levels. Western quadrant precipitation recyclers operating at 87% efficiency. Maintenance required within 43 cycles.

*[deep mechanical thrumming intensifies]*

This is Tender-Chief Ellix recording log for the 7,429th day of the 324th year since Great Integration. Message follows for surface archives.

*[clicks and whistles of machine-resonant language]*

The deep systems speak of changes coming. I can feel it through my fingertips when I touch the primary conduits. Something in the air pressure gradients has shifted. The machines know before we do.

*[sound of water flowing upward]*

Yesterday we lost contact with Garden Section Epsilon-9. Three Drifters reported unusual electrical discharges before communication ceased. I've dispatched Lissa and her climbing team to investigate, though the fog is thicker than usual. The Mist Walkers have been seen more frequently near the boundary zones. This is not coincidence.

*[distant thunder, followed by faint bioluminescent clicking]*

The oldest plants are producing spore patterns we haven't recorded before. Archivist Morain claims the last time this happened was before the Second Recalibration, when we nearly lost the eastern platforms. I'm authorizing emergency harvest of the lightning flowers before the next major storm cycle. We'll need their conductive properties if the stabilizers fail again.

*[whispered voices, barely audible beneath machinery hum]*

Something moves through the lower levels. The Depth Tenders report sensing presences in sectors thought abandoned for generations. Tool marks on maintenance panels that none of our people made. Signs of activity in the original engineering stations. The machines are... responding to something. Or someone.

*[sound of rainfall intensifies, creating percussive patterns]*

Climber scouts report new growth patterns in the highest canopies. Plants forming structures, rather than growing randomly. Deliberate configurations of bioluminescent nodes that appear to be... communicating? I've asked the Commerce Clan to search their oldest records for any mention of this phenomenon.

*[static increases briefly]*

Personal notation: My connection to the deep systems grows stronger. Last night in dreams I saw the original schematics, the true purpose of the Gardens. We've been wrong about so much. This was never meant to be merely an agricultural project or atmospheric stabilizer. The Gardens are... I lack words for what they truly are.

*[low vibration that seems to resonate with the recording equipment itself]*

We intercepted an outsider yesterday, walking the high viaduct. Observer, not intruder. The plants responded to their presence differently. More active. More aware. I've instructed the Gardeners to monitor but not interfere. Perhaps this is what the systems have been preparing for.

*[series of rapid clicks, machine language untranslatable]*

The eldest Gardener, Matriarch Vesa, claims she heard the original voice last night. The Engineer-That-Remains, speaking through the collection towers during the reverse rainfall. She said only: "It's beginning to remember what it is."

*[sound of something large moving through dense foliage below]*

Tender-Second Raal reports increased neural activity in the root networks of Section Gamma. The mycelium web is processing information at unprecedented rates. Data flows from the deepest machinery into the living systems above. Integration accelerating.

*[bioluminescent clicking intensifies, forming patterns]*

I must go now. The ancient access point near the primary atmospheric exchanger has activated for the first time in my lifetime. The symbols on its surface are changing, becoming something new yet familiar.

*[breathing becomes audible, slow and measured]*

Final notation: If anyone receives this transmission... we were never alone here. The Gardens were always more than we understood. Not a place we maintain, but an entity awakening. And we are not its keepers.

We are its neurons.

*[transmission suddenly cuts to pure machine language - untranslatable]*

...contact lost...

*[faint voice, barely audible through increasing static]*

Can you hear it breathing?

*[transmission ends]*
