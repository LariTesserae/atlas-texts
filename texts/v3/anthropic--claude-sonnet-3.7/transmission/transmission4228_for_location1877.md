<background sounds of water striking hollow chambers in complex rhythm>

<transmission wavers, grows faint>

...eight thousand seven hundred and thirty-two flows since first carving...

<transmission strengthens>

The water remembers everything. Stone forgets nothing. We became what the deep needed us to be.

<abrupt shift in transmission>
