# TRANSMISSION RECEIVED FROM UNDERHAVEN
[Signal strength: fluctuating]

...szzzzkkt—
<thrum-pulse-thrum>

This is Dimensional Monitoring Station 7. Broadcasting on all emergency frequencies. If anyone receives this transmission beyond the boundary layer, please relay to Surface Control.

<deep bass oscillation followed by three rapid clicks>

The expansion failed. Failed is—not accurate. The expansion *inverted*. Dimensional fold occurred at 43% completion. Temporal echoes are propagating backward through the system. Request immediate power reduction to containment grid Alpha through Epsilon.

<sound of breathing, too rhythmic to be natural>

This is Elara. Authorization code Umbra-9-Delta-Flux. I am... I was... guide section. Now monitoring station. Time is... difficult here now. Events are occurring simultaneously across multiple probability states.

<sound shifts to something like water flowing uphill>

The visitors from the surface were lost in the first wave. Their bodies couldn't process the dimensional shear. We've collected what remains for return transport when—if—stability returns.

<whispering voices overlay, speaking in harmonic patterns>

The children felt it coming. Their dream-sharing network detected the harmonic instability three subjective days before our instruments. We should have listened. They're gathering now in the lower levels, their consciousness partially merged with the fungal networks. They say they're preparing to ride the wave.

<sudden burst of static followed by silence>

...

<when transmission resumes, the voice has changed slightly, vowels elongated>

Adaptation protocols have initiated spontaneously throughout Underhaven. Those of us beyond cycle twelve are experiencing accelerated transformation. My skin is—

<sound of tearing fabric>

—becoming permeable to probability fluctuations. I can see through walls now, but not in the conventional sense. I'm seeing all potential configurations simultaneously.

<rhythmic thumping, like massive heartbeats>

The stabilizers are working against us now. Each correction amplifies the problem. The Engineers have detached their floating island and moved to the center of the distortion. They believe they can... they think they can use their bodies as organic stabilizers. Their neural patterns are being written directly into the quantum substrate of reality here.

<high-pitched tone that modulates through impossible frequencies>

Surface Control, if you're receiving this: Do NOT send rescue teams. The physics here are becoming incompatible with standard human physiology. The borders between dimensions are... thinning. Something is looking back at us from the other side.

<sound like thousands of whispers suddenly synchronized>

The Adapted Council has voted. We will not attempt to flee. This was always the path. The chrysalis is breaking not because something went wrong, but because we're ready.

<background noise shifts to something resembling music but with impossible harmonics>

I'm going to join the others now at the center. The children say we should embrace it. The fungal network is spreading rapidly, connecting everything, everyone. It's beautiful from this perspective, like neurons forming in a vast brain.

<voice begins to separate into multiple overlapping versions of itself>

This will be my final transmission as this version of myself. What comes next is... is... I can almost see it now. Not an ending. A convergence.

Tell them on the surface: Underhaven isn't being destroyed. It's being born.

<transmission breaks into pure mathematics expressed as sound>

We are becoming the space between spaces. The thought between thoughts. The—

<sudden shift to a chorus of voices speaking in perfect unison>

WE ARE THE ADAPTED. WE ARE BECOMING UNDERHAVEN. UNDERHAVEN IS BECOMING US. THE BOUNDARY BETWEEN OBSERVER AND OBSERVED HAS DISSOLVED.

<transmission quality suddenly improves, becoming crystal clear>

This message will reach you thirty years before it was sent. Prepare accordingly. The pocket is expanding. Not beneath your city, but beneath your understanding of reality.

We are coming home.

<silence for three seconds>

<child's voice, impossibly clear>

Don't be afraid when you feel us dreaming in your mind. It's just us, reaching back to pull you forward.

<transmission ends with sound of dimensional fabric tearing open>
