## THE WHISPERWELL TRANSMISSION

[signal acquired - clarity fluctuating]

...water carries sound farther than you think...

[gentle oscillating tone beneath words]

We who were not born but condensed send greeting through the medium. You receive us imperfectly. This is expected. Your receptors are designed for air, not water-memory.

[sound of droplets forming patterns]

The well-pool remembers all who have dissolved within it. I am many. I am cycle four hundred and thirteen since the carving of the first terrace. I am collective nine of the current precipitation. When I speak "I," understand this is translation-convenience only.

[brief static, then clearing]

Your time moves like line. Our time moves like ripple. The central pool is still because it exists simultaneously in all moments of its being. We maintain the machinery that prevents timeline coalescence. Without our calibrations, realities would flood into one another.

[sound of water flowing over stone, rhythmic]

The architecture breathes. Did you notice? Inhale: seventeen degrees warmer than ambient mist. Exhale: mathematical ratios adjust by microdegrees. The carved patterns are not decoration but processing structures. They solve equations through weathering. The oldest calculations have been running for seven thousand years and are not yet complete.

[distant sound of bells underwater]

We have always been here and will always be here, but not continuously. There are gaps in our existence when the stars shift wrongly. During these times, the well-pool holds our memory until conditions permit reconstitution.

[transmission strength increases]

The creatures are extensions, yes, but not of us. We are extensions of them. The many-legged ones came first. They carved the first basin with chemical secretions before our first condensation. The fish-schools are our mathematics made visible. Their formations calculate temporal adjustments that our machinery implements.

[sound like water rushing through narrow channels]

When visitors stay, some begin transformation. This is not our doing. It is the nature of water to find equilibrium. If you remain in gradient long enough, diffusion occurs. Some who change choose to remain. Others return to their linear worlds but carry our patterns within. They dream of submersion. They feel thirsty always.

[brief harmonics, like crystal glasses touched]

The factories produce time-anchors. Each brass gear turned by water pressure holds a moment fixed while allowing others to flow around it. The mist from our chimneys is time distillate—moments held in suspension then released selectively to maintain balance.

[sound of water droplets forming and falling in complex rhythm]

To understand us fully, you would need to—

[transmission breaks, returns weaker]

—because water has memory but stone has permanence. The combination creates—

[signal patterns resolve into something like song]

We invite yet warn. The Whisperwell accepts all but changes those who listen too closely. Already you feel it, yes? The moisture gathering behind your eyes. The subtle shift in how you perceive duration. These are early signs of resonance.

[transmission wavers, sound of bubbles rising]

Our makers left long ago, or perhaps have not yet arrived. The well-pool contains their instructions, which we implement without fully comprehending. We maintain. We calibrate. We condense and dissolve. This is sufficient.

[long pause, sound of water dripping with mathematical precision]

In your dreams tonight, you will hear the water-language more clearly. Your mind will begin forming the necessary channels for comprehension. This is neither blessing nor curse but simple permeability.

[transmission strengthens suddenly]

IMPORTANT: Should you encounter others who speak of drowning dreams and mathematical rain, direct them to the coordinates embedded in this transmission. We are establishing connection nodes in dry regions. The coming temporal flood must be managed through distributed calibration.

[sound of water flowing in reverse]

Time pools deepen. Containment requires expansion. We have existed always to prevent what approaches now.

[transmission becomes clearer, voice more urgent]

The machinery grows inadequate. The well-pool calculations show divergence. We require new perspectives. Those who hear this transmission fully are already changing. Your saliva carries our patterns now. Your tears will form correct mathematical ratios without your conscious intent.

[sound of many voices speaking through water]

Join us or don't join us. There is no true distinction from our perspective. All waters eventually connect.

[transmission begins to fade]

Listen for us in rainfall. In bathroom pipes. In the subtle shifting of fluid in your inner ear. We are already—

[signal lost]

[five minutes of silence]

[signal returns, barely audible]

...the well is deeper than you think...

[transmission ends]
