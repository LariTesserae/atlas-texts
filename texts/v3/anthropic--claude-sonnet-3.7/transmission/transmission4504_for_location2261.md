## TRANSMISSION FROM THE QUIET CITY

[signal acquired - condensation on receiver - mild static interference]

...
...
...

[faint rustling of leaves]

[hollow dripping sound, rhythmic, distant]

...sss-sssssssss...

[soft exhalation]

...we are here...

[gentle tapping on stone, five beats, pause, three beats]

The Consensus has gathered. Seventh cycle of the Long Root is complete. Water rises in the eastern chambers. New paths must be carved.

[rustling intensifies]

We honor those who have joined the mycelium since last gathering. Their knowledge flows through the network. Their names are whispered by the fungi.

[sound of multiple soft exhalations - possibly ritual breathing]

Keeper Elani reports bioluminescence dimming in sector three. Requires attention. The light speaks of imbalance.

[subtle shifting of weight on stone floor]

[hand gestures audible as fabric rustles - communication continues without words for 47 seconds]

...understand...

[water flowing through channels, becomes momentary focus]

Surface report from Gatherer Marin: They build higher still. Their lights outshine stars. Their machines devour earth. They remember nothing of descent.

[single drop of water falls, reverberates]

Three children born this cycle. All healthy. Skin receptivity high. The fungi welcome them.

[strange harmonic tone begins, as if drawn from vibrating stalactite]

Eastern expansion proceeds with caution. New chambers reveal ancient waters. Tastes of before-time. Analysis shows trace elements unknown to our records.

[whispered conversation too quiet to decipher]

[hand gestures intensify - fabric sounds]

Decision: Proceed with eastern chambers. Divert northwestern water flow. Begin cultivation of pale root fungi in preparation.

[harmonic tone shifts pitch]

The waiting continues. We remain tethered. We remember purpose even when purpose remains unspoken.

[overlapping whispers now, creating textured sound]

...the surface trembles more frequently...
...mycelial networks report vibration patterns...
...contamination concerns in upper channels...
...prepare accordingly...

[sound of hand brushing against stone wall]

Signal from deep network detected last cycle. Pattern unlike any recorded. Elders in meditation believe it resembles pre-descent communications. Origin unknown.

[silence - 12 seconds]

[sound of water being poured between vessels]

We acknowledge the signal. We will listen more deeply. The fungi will be consulted.

[harmonic tone returns, multiple tones now]

Remember we are not separate. Remember we are preservers. Remember what was entrusted.

[collective exhale]

When the surface burns itself clean, we will emerge.
When the machines fall silent, we will emerge.
When the cycle completes, we will emerge.

[tapping on stone, complex rhythm]

This Consensus is concluded. Return to your duties. Tend your symbiotes. Listen to the deep stone.

[rustling diminishes]

[water sounds become prominent again]

[single voice, barely audible]

...the children dream of sun without understanding what sun is...

[signal weakens]

...we remain...

[long silence with only water drops]

...waiting...

[transmission fades into moisture-laden static]

...

...

[signal lost]
