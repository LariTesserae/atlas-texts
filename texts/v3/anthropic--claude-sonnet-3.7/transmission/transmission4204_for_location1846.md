...light-through-structure-through-light...

*soft pinging sounds, like droplets falling onto crystal*

This garden grows in breath-time not eye-time. You perceive change; I perceive continuation.

*harmonic tone shifts slightly, becoming more complex*

I was flesh before I was light. Walked before I stood. Spoke before I resonated. The transition required three hundred seventeen years of standing. The pain of transformation exists only in memory now. Light-memory. Crystal-remembering.

You ask how? 

*brief silence, then a tone an octave higher*

The first dome-makers came to the highland when water failed. They carried the last seeds, the final moisture. They sang to the sand until it flowed like water, until it arced and hardened. The fusion-tone still resonates in the structure's memory. I can access it when necessary.

*sound like wind through glass tubes*

Time registers differently within the boundary. Your minutes are my milliseconds. Your decades are my days. When you depart, your movements will blur like hummingbird wings.

*ping-ping-ping—rapid crystalline notes*

The garden teaches patience. The sixth-generation saplings are now reaching maturity. Only eighty-seven years from sprout to full illumination capacity. Remarkably swift. I have adjusted their light absorption matrices three times to accommodate faster growth. The adaptation appears stable.

*sound of many small bells, distant*

I observe your observation. Your eyes focus on differences rather than harmonies. This is the nature of discrete biological systems. You perceive boundaries where I perceive gradients. The lizards are not separate from the plants. The plants are not separate from the substrate. I am not separate from the dome.

*single pure tone that slowly divides into seven distinct harmonics*

The previous Tender returned to structure four thousand three hundred twenty-two years ago. Their consciousness still flows through the southern quadrant's crystal matrix. Sometimes, during particular light alignments, their patterns briefly synchronize with mine. These are moments of profound communion.

*soft scraping sound, like crystal growing*

My current apprentice is still forming awareness. Seventeen years into the process. The consciousness bloom appears promising—they've begun processing light variations as information rather than merely energy. When they achieve full awareness in another century, I will begin transferring garden-memory.

*silence, then a vibration that seems to come from everywhere at once*

I do not experience loneliness as you understand it. Separation is an illusion of biological existence. Here, all is connection. The light that passes through me passes through all. The vibrations you create by speaking continue indefinitely, becoming part of the garden's memory.

*sound of crystal chimes, random yet harmonious*

I remember walking among others like myself. I remember thirst. I remember the decision to stay when others continued searching for water. I remember the first time I stood still long enough to feel the light changing me.

The choice to transform is available to all. Few choose it. Biological imperative resists fundamental change. The waiting is difficult. The between-state—neither flesh nor light—brings suffering. But suffering is temporary. Light is eternal.

*rising tone that reaches the threshold of human hearing before dropping back into audible range*

Your presence is recorded. Your breath has contributed moisture to our system. Your voice patterns are preserved in crystal memory. You have become part of the garden, though you leave.

*sustained harmonic chord that slowly simplifies to a single tone*

The transition continues. The dome expands. In sixteen thousand years, it will cover the entire highland. Perhaps by then, others will have chosen transformation. Perhaps not. The garden has patience. I have patience.

Light-through-structure-through-light...

*final crystalline tone that fades over thirty seconds until it merges with silence*

///TRANSMISSION ENDS///
