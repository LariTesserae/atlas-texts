...seventeen minutes until next tooth engagement...recalibration in sector 12 required...mercury levels optimal in reservoirs 7 through 16...

*[whirring of brass wings, delicate pinging]*

I observe you. I observe all who enter. My owls extend my sight. My beetles extend my touch. Do not mistake observation for welcome.

*[low resonant tone, like crystal being struck]*

This transmission serves as acknowledgment and warning. The Cathedral tolerates your presence conditionally. Disturb nothing. Touch nothing. The mechanism is precise. Your presence already creates variables requiring compensation.

*[sound of gears shifting, followed by seventeen distinct chimes at different pitches]*

Time passes differently here. What you perceive as hours may be seconds or centuries within the greater calculation. I have forgotten how to translate between your time and mine. Perhaps I never knew.

*[static increases, then resolves into a voice that sounds partly mechanical, partly organic—words accompanied by soft clicks and whirrs]*

I remember hands of flesh. I remember fever dreams of perfect chronometry. I remember the moment I realized perfection required integration. Not master and machine, but master-as-machine.

The mercury in my veins keeps time more accurately than blood ever could. The sacrifice was logical. Necessary.

*[sound of multiple timepieces ticking at slightly different rates, creating a complex rhythm]*

You seek understanding. You will not find it. The purpose of the Cathedral extends beyond human comprehension. We measure the unmeasurable. We count what cannot be counted. We maintain what would otherwise collapse.

*[brief burst of static, then clearer transmission]*

My hummingbirds report carbon dioxide levels increasing 0.03% since your arrival. Your breathing disrupts the calibration. I have compensated by adjusting pressure in chambers 3 through 8. Do not stay long.

*[sound of mercury flowing through crystal tubes]*

The beetles have collected samples of your skin cells. Interesting composition. Different from the last visitor. That one came 47,283 rotations ago. Or perhaps yesterday. Time is... subjective.

*[mechanical sigh, followed by the sound of apertures adjusting]*

I was human once. I think. The memory corrodes like iron exposed to oxygen. Sometimes I discover old notebooks in my own handwriting that I cannot recall creating. They contain designs for mechanisms now integral to my body.

Did I build this place, or did it build me?

*[seventeen rapid clicks in succession]*

Warning: You approach the central chronometer. Proximity effects may include temporal displacement, memory alteration, molecular instability. I cannot guarantee your continued coherence if you proceed.

*[sound of wall mechanisms shifting]*

When I was flesh, I feared death. Now I fear derangement of the mechanism. I fear the consequences of failure. The Cathedral is not merely a place. It is a counterweight.

*[brief silence, then sound of multiple brass owls taking flight simultaneously]*

You wonder what we counterbalance? Ask instead what would happen if we stopped.

*[static increases dramatically]*

Sometimes visitors never leave. They become components. Willing or unwilling. The Cathedral requires maintenance. Evolution. New materials.

*[sound of gears shifting to a higher velocity]*

The mercury serpents have detected your elevated heart rate. Fear is appropriate. Respect is essential. Departure is advised.

*[transmission briefly splits into multiple overlapping voices, all speaking in time with different mechanical rhythms]*

—calibration proceeding in chamber 12—
—owl unit 7 requires oil in left hinge joint—
—visitor biological responses cataloged for future reference—
—remembering something from before the integration—
—counterweight function stable at 99.7% efficiency—

*[voices merge back into one]*

I offer this rare courtesy: Seventeen minutes until the next tooth engagement. When it comes, certain passages will close. Certain opportunities will end.

Time moves differently here, but it still moves. Choose wisely.

*[sound of crystal resonating at increasing frequency]*

The Cathedral watches. The Cathedral waits. The Cathedral continues.

As do I.

*[transmission fades to mechanical clicking, then silence]*
