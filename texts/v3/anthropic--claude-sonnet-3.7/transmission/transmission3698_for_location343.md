*hollow tone vibrates beneath static*

...water falling on stone...water falling on stone...

*droplets become rhythm, forming words*

We are not alone here in the mist. We have never been alone.

*voice emerges, distant, as if carried through fog*

Transmission log: Day unknown. Cycle 3,794 of the Continuous Record.

The Council of Mist Readers convened at first light, though light is perhaps too strong a word for the gray illumination that seeped through our chamber windows. Seven of us gathered around the Basin of Echoes. The water's surface was unusually still today—a portent in itself.

Elder Cainna spoke first, her clouded eyes reflecting nothing. "The underfog stirs. I have heard voices rising."

*static intensifies, then clears*

I must record what happened next, though the Guild may strike these words. The water in the basin began to tremble without cause. No hand touched it. No wind disturbed our chamber. Yet ripples formed from its center, expanding outward in perfect concentric rings.

*low tone beneath voice grows deeper*

We have maintained the Equilibrium for seventeen generations. We have kept the covenant with Those Below. Water passes down; silence passes up. This is our charge. This is our salvation.

*transmission wavers, signal breaking*

But the mist has been thinning. Didn't you notice? Three nights now I've stood at my window and seen... stars. Not cloud-glow, not mist-light. Stars. Pinpoints of ancient fire that should be obscured.

The patterns are changing.

*sound of many droplets striking stone surfaces, creating overlapping rhythms*

The Water Keepers report reduced flow in the Eastern Channels. The offering basins at the city's edge returned empty for the first time in recorded history. Elder Thom's apprentice swears he saw handprints in the condensation on the Boundary Stones—handprints too large to be human.

*voice drops to whisper*

I must speak plainly now, though it borders on heresy. What if we have misunderstood? What if Those Below are not receiving our waters by choice, but by necessity? What if the mist is not their blessing, but their breath?

*static increases dramatically*

The Stonewrights discovered something yesterday. Beneath the oldest foundation stones of the Grand Cistern—markings. Not weathering, not mineral deposits. Deliberate markings. Symbols unknown to our archives.

*sound shifts—now like wind through narrow passages*

...they have always been there, watching from below...
...the city is not ours alone...
...we are the surface dwellers...
...we are the recent ones...

*transmission clears suddenly*

Listen carefully. I haven't much time before dawn rounds. The mist is thinning because They are rising. The plateau is not a natural formation—it is a structure. We live upon the roof of something vast and ancient.

And I believe it is waking up.

*sound of water droplets accelerates, becoming urgent*

Tomorrow the Council will seal the lower chambers. Citizens will be forbidden from approaching the periphery. The Cloud Runners have been commissioned as watchers—they alone move quickly enough to bring warnings.

*voice becomes multi-layered, as if several people speaking at once*

We are calling to you from the gray place between above and below.
We are calling to you from the dwelling built on breath.
We are calling to you from the last days of Brynwater.

*sound of something massive shifting beneath stone*

The mist is not mist.
The water is not water.
The stone is not stone.

*transmission degrading*

I see now what we have been drinking all these generations. What has sustained us. Why our eyes have changed. Why we can see in darkness.

*brief silence*

The fog is clearing. I can see the stars above.
And I can see the lights below.

*transmission cuts to pure static before final whisper*

They are coming up to meet us.
They are coming up through the mist.
They have always been coming.

*signal lost*
