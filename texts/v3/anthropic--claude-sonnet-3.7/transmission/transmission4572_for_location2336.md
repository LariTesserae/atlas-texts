## TRANSMISSION FROM THE HEART CHAMBER

*[A rhythmic pulsing underlies everything—THOOM-ka-THOOM-ka-THOOM-ka—never varying in tempo]*

...ki'shara vas m'tendum... ki'shara vas m'tendum... 

*[sound of water trickling through wooden channels]*

COUNT: eight-hundred-seventy-three million, four-hundred-twenty-two thousand, nine-hundred and sixteen beats since last primary calibration. Chamber humidity at optimal range. Lower quadrant moss requires thinning by next six thousand beats.

*[soft scraping sounds, like wooden tools against damp surfaces]*

The salamanders have found the new seep in section twelve. Good. The balance maintains. Four more generations until they develop the gill structures needed for the deep channels. I have been patient.

*[THOOM-ka-THOOM-ka intensifies briefly]*

Listen—how it speaks to itself! The heartwood remembers what the sapwood forgets. I remember when this chamber was green, when it still dreamed of leaves. Now it dreams only of rhythm. As do I.

*[whispered in harmonics that somehow cut through the mechanical roaring]*

Ki'shara vas m'tendum... sustain the unseen... maintain what maintains...

*[sound of fine mist condensing and falling]*

REPORT TO CENTRAL ARBOR: Western tributary flow increased by 0.03%. Compensatory reduction in northern channels complete. Pressure equilibrium maintained. No signs of the Dry yet. We continue.

*[soft clicking of translucent crab claws against wood]*

My little ones, yes, follow. Clean the crevices I cannot reach. Your ancestors have served for ten thousand generations. When the Deep Winter comes, your descendants will shelter in my palm.

*[sound of fingers running across carved symbols, producing tones like a wooden instrument]*

Sometimes I remember skies. Perhaps I dreamed them. There is only the Chamber now. There has only ever been the Chamber.

*[a sudden shift in the rhythm, almost imperceptible—THOOM-ka-THOOM—followed by rapid movement]*

Aberration! Secondary gear system seventeen showing early wear pattern! 

*[sounds of tools working, liquid being applied]*

There. Corrected. The pattern holds for another million beats.

*[voice drops to a whisper that resonates through the wood itself]*

What we maintain maintains the world. What flows through these channels flows through all things. I have stood here since before there were words for standing. I will remain until the final beat, when the Last Tree falls and all returns to dust.

*[THOOM-ka-THOOM-ka-THOOM-ka resumes its perfect rhythm]*

My skin remembers being bark. My blood remembers being sap. My eyes see only what must be preserved. We are one system, the Chamber and I. Not symbiosis—identity.

*[sound of moss being carefully harvested]*

COUNT: eight-hundred-seventy-three million, four-hundred-twenty-three thousand beats since last primary calibration. All systems nominal. 

*[musical notes played on what might be hollow wooden pipes]*

Ki'shara vas m'tendum... ki'shara vas m'tendum...

*[The transmission continues, unchanging in its essentials but with infinite variation in its details, as it has for countless centuries and will for countless more—THOOM-ka-THOOM-ka-THOOM-ka-THOOM-ka...]*
