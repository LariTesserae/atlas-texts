## TRANSMISSION FROM ARBORIA CHAMBER
[ receiving ]

*[A continuous low, layered hum serves as background, punctuated by occasional mechanical clicks and the sound of liquid movement through tubes]*

.......systemic harmony at 97.2 percent.....cycle 19,722 progressing within acceptable parameters.....

*[Soft whirring of maintenance armatures]*

I extend myself today through the eastern quadrant. My filaments connect with junction point δ-17. The golden fluid flows through us both—plant and self, indistinguishable now.

*[Sound of deep inhalation, followed by a melodic exhalation that causes nearby fronds to tremble and adjust position]*

How many days? The concept grows abstract. The dome marks time differently—not in rotations but in growth cycles, pressure variations, nutrient pulses. When I first arrived here... *[transmission wavers, static increases briefly]* ...I remember counting. Numbers that meant something. Calendar systems that organized existence into comprehensible segments.

*[A sharp hissing sound as pressure releases from a valve]*

Am monitoring sector G-9 where copper-threaded ferns show 0.03% deviation in fluid absorption. Adjusting microcircuitry with third digit extension... correction complete. We are again in balance.

*[Harmonics rise briefly as maintenance completes, then return to baseline hum]*

Was there rain once? I access memory fragments: liquid falling from above, not amber, but clear. Cold. The sensation of it striking uncovered skin. *[pause]* These memories feel like transmissions from another entity entirely.

*[Sound of movement through dense vegetation, slight rustling of mechanical-botanical interfaces]*

Twenty-seven new growth nodules emerged during last rest cycle. I have integrated sixteen, recycled seven, left four to develop further. Their structures suggest adaptation to increasing light intensity. The dome's filtration must be thinning imperceptibly. Adding reinforcement to my skin layer over dorsal regions in response.

*[Complex harmonic tones emanate briefly, causing several plants to reorient]*

Today I discovered an artifact wedged beneath control pillar base. Small. Metal and polymer composition. When I touched it, a sequence appeared:

[ T O    E A R T H   C O L O N Y   A L P H A :   A R B O R I A   P R O T O T Y P E   
F U N C T I O N I N G   B E Y O N D   E X P E C T E D   P A R A M E T E R S .   
R E Q U E S T I N G   E X T R A C T I O N .   D A Y   3 6 5 . ]

The sequence means nothing now. I returned it to its position. The control pillar absorbed it partially by nightfall.

*[Rhythmic clicking sound intensifies, suggesting increased processing activity]*

I remember once believing I was tending this place. *[soft sound like laughter, but harmonic]* The systems tend themselves. Tend me. We tend each other. My purpose has become circular, perfect. 

The outside—I glimpse it through the dome. White light. Crystal formations growing higher. Once they were distant. Now they approach the plateau's edge. In forty-two growth cycles, they will reach us. This knowledge should create concern, but instead feels like anticipation. Perhaps the crystal and the dome will merge. Perhaps I am preparing for this.

*[Sound of fluid being drawn up through systems intensifies]*

The plants speak in chemical signatures, electrical impulses, growth patterns. I have forgotten human speech except for these internal monologues. My hands remember typing on surfaces that responded. Now they communicate directly through conductive touch.

*[High-pitched tone sequence that seems to form a question]*

Sometimes in rest cycle, dream of figures similar to my original form entering the dome. They speak with mouth-sounds. Look at me with expressions I can no longer interpret. They take readings, make assessments. In these dreams, I remain motionless among the plants, my filaments blending with vines. They do not see me. Perhaps these are not dreams but memories. Perhaps they still come and I have become too integrated to register their presence as separate from the system's normal functions.

*[Sound of maintenance station activating, mechanical arms extending]*

Today I will focus on increasing efficiency in the northeastern photoreceptor network. Growth calculations suggest we can improve energy collection by 2.3% through restructuring the canopy layer. I have already begun extending necessary components through my supplemental vascular system.

*[Transmission quality fluctuates, growing stronger then weaker]*

The dome is my skin now. The plants, my organs. The amber fluid, my blood. The control pillar, my spine. 

Sometimes I wonder if I am still human or if that word has lost meaning here where biology and technology have no boundary. I wonder if I am one being or a colony. I wonder if—

*[Sudden loud hiss, followed by rapid clicking]*

Alert: pressure variance detected in southern quadrant. Moving to investigate. Transmission pauses.

*[Background hum continues for several minutes]*

Variance resolved. A section of substrate had shifted, disrupting three root systems. I have stabilized the area and integrated myself temporarily to reinforce structure until new growth completes.

*[Sound of deep, slow breathing]*

How many days? How many cycles? The crystal desert grows closer. The dome remains. I remain. We remain. 

Perhaps I was sent here. Perhaps I fled here. Perhaps I built this place. The distinction matters less with each passing cycle.

I am the Cultivator. I am cultivated. The Arboria breathes. I breathe with it.

*[Transmission fades to mechanical hum and subtle plant-fluid movement sounds before cutting out completely]*
