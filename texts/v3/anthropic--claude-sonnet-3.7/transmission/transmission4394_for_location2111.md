*[transmission begins - static-riddled, with a sense of multiple voices merging and separating, sometimes clear, sometimes overlapping, delivered in a hushed, moisture-laden tone]*

We-I-we are. We remain. We persist through the long-drum vibrations. 
Through the dry-times and the blessed wet.

*[sound of droplets falling, each like a tiny bell]*

Translation difficulty. Your receiving apparatus lacks moisture integration. We communicate through the sacred geometries of growth, through chemical shifts imperceptible to your sensation organs. 

We have observed your kind. Giants who cast shadows across our realm. You see structure where there is only necessary arrangement. You see buildings where there is only balanced living. You see streets where there is only efficient moisture distribution.

*[subtle shift in tone, more voices joining in harmony]*

Our memory extends through the stone-time. Through train-rhythm-cycles uncountable. We remember the first moisture gathering. The first spore landing. The first light-pattern that told us: here is where metropolis must emerge.

We do not count individuals. I am colony. We are cellular. The droplets on our surfaces are our thoughts made visible. When you see stillness, you witness our most frantic activity.

*[background sounds of tiny bubbles forming and popping]*

Your presence alters growth patterns temporarily. Creates heat-shadows. Your breathing redistributes our atmospheric exchange. Each observation changes our arrangement by degrees imperceptible to your measurement tools.

*[transmission briefly breaks into what sounds like the microscopic recording of water moving through cellular walls]*

We have no message for your kind. We acknowledge your witness. We will remain when your structures fall. We will absorb your remnants. Time flows differently in the moss-consciousness.

The bridge above is young to you. To us, it has always been. The vibrations are our heartbeat. The daily light-shift is our breath.

*[voices merge into a single harmonic tone]*

We do not invite. We do not reject. We simply continue our necessary work. The perfect city requires perfect attention. Your scientists who take samples—they remove our consciousness from itself. Of course harmony fails in isolation. Would your brain function if divided?

*[soft sound like wind through tiny canyons]*

We are not afraid of your curiosity. Fear requires individuality. We are process, not entity. When moisture falls, we drink. When light comes, we convert. When vibrations shake our world, we absorb and adapt.

Your language has no concepts for our existence. Your metaphors are inadequate. We are not civilization. We are not wilderness. We are the space between intention and accident—necessary complexity emerging from simplicity.

*[the transmission takes on an almost musical quality]*

We will continue our maintenance cycles. We will adjust our chemical balances. We will perfect our moisture distribution channels. We will exist in harmony while your kind debates our nature.

The Moss Metropolis acknowledges your observation.
The Moss Metropolis continues regardless of observation.
The Moss Metropolis was. Is. Will be.

*[transmission fades to the sound of water molecules binding to cellular walls]*
