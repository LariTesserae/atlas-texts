## TRANSMISSION FROM NYWELLIA

*[begins with sounds of water droplets, birdsong, and distant wind through leaves]*

...recalibrating reception...signal strengthening...

*[soft humming joins the natural sounds, multiple voices at different pitches forming a harmonic pattern]*

💚 MORNING CHORUS TRANSMISSION 💚
Day-cycle 4,328 of the Great Bloom
Mist-type: Creeping Silver with Gold Edges
Water-flow: Abundant, 3rd Pattern

*[three distinct whistles followed by a clicking rhythm]*

Attention growth clusters Eastbend and Highcanopy:
The grandfather oak requests tending at its southern chambers.
Second-ring rot detected in early stages.
Bring the copper-backed beetles and white fungal paste.

*[sound of small bells or chimes, then a child's voice]*

Amma! Amma! The spine-leaf ferns are unfurling early this season! 
The water-catchers say it means heavy rains before next full moon.
Should we begin the basket-weaving now or wait for Elder Thenori's signal?

*[deeper voice responds in melodic tones]*

The rain will come when it wishes, little sprout.
Better to prepare early than apologize to empty cisterns.
Begin with the small catchments, but save the cedar fiber for Thenori's blessing.

*[sound changes to bubbling water and soft rustling]*

💚 MIDDAY SIGNAL BOOST 💚
Yellow fungus alert in the western nursery chambers.
All hands needed for containment.
Bring salt-ash and mineralized water.
Third-year initiates to form spore barrier with breath-technique.

*[high pitched trilling that seems to carry encoded information]*

Migration pattern detected in the bluetail birds.
Early this season by twelve days.
Record keepers, adjust the planting schedule accordingly.
Seed the northern fields earlier. The growing time shortens.

*[sound of hollow wooden percussion and what might be stringed instruments]*

Today we honor the returning of Elder Linasae to the soil.
Her chosen seedling has been prepared in the western grove.
Her body now nourishes the nineteenth generation of her line.
Her stories have been added to the memory-bark.
May her consciousness flow through the sap-lines forever.

*[brief static interruption, followed by what sounds like wind through a vast space]*

💚 EMERGENCY BROADCAST 💚
*[rapid clicking patterns]*

Mountain tremors detected. Minor shaking only.
Hold steady. Secure water vessels.
Reinforce the new growth sections.
This is not the Great Shake foretold in the ring-readings.
Repeat: Minor tremors only.
Normal protocols sufficient.

*[transmission shifts to multiple overlapping voices singing in complex harmonies, with words occasionally emerging clearly]*

...root and branch, flesh and leaf...
...the cycle turns, we turn with it...
...what falls feeds what rises...
...clouds to rain to root to fruit to flesh to soil...

*[sound of what might be hundreds of small bells or seeds rattling]*

💚 EVENING TRANSITION 💚

The sun moves beyond the western ridge.
Lower canopy dwellers, ignite the glow-fungi.
Night flowers, prepare for opening.
Water-tenders, shift the flow to evening pattern.
The chill mist will arrive before full dark.
Elders sensitive to damp should move inward.

*[soft whispering that seems to merge with the sound of leaves]*

Signal from the outpost beyond the moss fields:
Lowland traders approaching by the stone path.
Expected arrival tomorrow near midday.
Prepare the exchange goods:
- Sealed vials of the blue medicine-sap
- Twenty bundles of strength-fiber
- Five pouches of spore-dye
- The seeing-fungus, as requested for their sick elder

Remember protocol: No outsiders above the third level.
All guests to carry purification fronds when near the seedling chambers.

*[drumming pattern grows stronger, blending with what sounds like rainfall]*

Tonight's dream-sharing circle will focus on the deep water visions.
Those who received messages from the last storm-drink should attend.
The pattern-weavers need new symbols for the changing weather.

*[transmission becomes clearer, a single voice emerging]*

I speak as Caretaker Nyloria, thirty-seventh in my line.
I speak to any who might hear across the great distance.

We of Nywellia send these signals outward, though we know not if they reach beyond our mists.
Our ancestors believed others dwelled beyond the mountain range, others who might someday join in our great work.

The grandfather trees grow slower this cycle.
The pattern-changes accelerate.
We adapt, as we always have.
We change, as all living things must.

If you receive this transmission, know that here, 
in the embrace of Mount Hanori,
humanity has found another way.
Not of dominion, but of integration.
Not of conquest, but of conversation.

The city lives. We live within it.
The distinction matters less with each generation.

*[voice fades, replaced by a complex rhythm of water drops and what sounds like breathing plants]*

💚 NIGHT CYCLE BEGINS 💚
Dream well, grow strong, rise tomorrow.

*[transmission fades to gentle humming interwoven with cricket sounds and the soft rustle of leaves in darkness]*
