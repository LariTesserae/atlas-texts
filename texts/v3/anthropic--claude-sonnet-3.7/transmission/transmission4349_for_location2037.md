The messenger bat delivered word from the Salvagers' Guild. They've recovered another artifact from the Old City beneath the mud at Blackwater Junction. Third this month. Always after the deep ebb. Always in the same ten-meter radius. I've marked it in the ledger for the Archivists.

*[The sound of a metal chair scraping against stone floor]*

The rats were agitated near Lock Twelve this evening. Moving in coordinated patterns. Always a sign. Last time they did this, we found structural weakness in the eastern retaining wall three days later. I've dispatched Junior Mechanist Celia to inspect. She understands the district's... intuitions.

*[Sound of something heavy sliding open, perhaps a window or valve]*

Weather note: Fog thicker tonight than usual. Visibility reduced to twenty meters in places. The fog carries whispers tonight. Not uncommon during fourth ebb, but stronger than I've heard before. Recorded three distinct patterns of modulation. Will compare with historical logs from my grandmother's time.

*[Brief burst of static, then a different voice cuts in, deeper, older, speaking with unusual cadence]*

...the lock-mind remembers when this was all sea-bed. The mechanisms dream of salt. The canals know the moon although they answer to our gates now. The district has purposes we only partly comprehend...

*[Signal returns to the original voice]*

...Tidewalker delegation requested increased flow to the Market District for tomorrow's Floating Festival. Approved and calibrated. Noted increased activity from the Canal Speaker who lives above Narrows Bridge. Third night she's been performing her water rituals. The Mechanists mock such practices, but the tide-cycles have run smoother since she began. Correlation isn't causation, my father would say. But correlation isn't coincidence either.

*[Sound of distant bells, marking some change in water levels]*

Time to make rounds. Need to check the pressure gauge at North Lock manually. The automated system has been reporting fluctuations that don't match visual observations. Another example of how the district resists full mechanization. Some relationships require human intuition.

*[Voice lowers, becomes more intimate]*

Personal note: Found another mark on my skin this morning. The opalescent pattern spreading across my left shoulder now. Eighth generation in the district does this to a family. My mother showed similar patterns by my age. We become part of the place that shaped us. The water getting into our blood somehow. Not unpleasant. Just... changing.

*[Sound of cap being unscrewed from thermos, liquid being poured]*

The engineer from Central City left today. Couldn't sleep with "all that water noise," he said. Couldn't understand how we hear individual messages in what sounds like chaos to outsiders. His replacement arrives next week. They never stay long, these outsiders. The district chooses its own.

*[Signal begins to fade, overtaken by the sounds of mechanical locks engaging and water rushing through gates]*

...end of official log for fourth ebb, Mist-Month. Tide rising now. I'll secure the lower levels and continue monitoring from the upper station. The district speaks most clearly between tides. Those with ears to hear know when to listen...
