# The Perpetual Scaffold

The ground does not exist here. Not in any meaningful sense. 

What stretches before you is a vast latticework of bamboo-like struts, lashed together with woven fibers into a structure that spans as far as the eye can see. The entire realm is a constructed surface, hovering above... something. The void below remains perpetually in shadow, its true nature unknowable.

This scaffold-world creaks and groans with subtle movements as warm air currents rise from below. The sounds are infrequent and soft – wooden joints flexing, fiber bindings stretching, distant percussion of hollow tubes knocking against one another. Then silence returns, sometimes for hours.

The construction itself appears primitive – no metal, no machinery – yet its engineering is bewilderingly complex. The lattice forms a mostly flat surface but occasionally rises into gentle hills or dips into shallow basins. Every strut, every binding, every platform was placed by hand, though no hands are visible now.

Sparse vegetation clings to life in this artificial environment. Pale orange lichen spreads across the bamboo in geometric patterns. Occasionally, a hardy succulent with triangular leaves emerges from small woven baskets filled with what appears to be crushed pottery and ash. Their waxy surfaces collect moisture from the warm air, requiring no groundwater to survive.

The light here is unremarkable – neither harsh nor dim – though its source remains unclear. No sun is visible overhead, just a diffuse amber glow that barely casts shadows. This light never truly fades, merely shifts in subtle ways that mark the passage of time.

The scaffold-world changes, but slowly. New structures occasionally appear, seemingly constructed by invisible hands during moments of inattention. A platform that was not there yesterday. A new latticework of fibers spanning a gap. No builders are ever seen, no construction sounds ever heard.

In places, strange pottery vessels hang suspended from the framework. Their surfaces are etched with spiral patterns that seem to shift when viewed from different angles. Some contain smoldering embers that emit faint spiced aromas but never fully ignite or burn out.

The warmth here is persistent and dry – not unpleasant, but ever-present. It rises from below the scaffolding, suggesting some unseen heat source in the void.

This place defies natural classification. It's an inverted world where the built environment doesn't rest upon nature but instead replaces it entirely. The scaffold isn't imposed upon the landscape; it is the landscape.

And yet, for all its constructed nature, the realm feels ancient, as if the hands that built it belonged to a civilization long departed. The craftsmanship speaks of profound purpose, though that purpose remains as obscure as the builders themselves.

The lattice extends onward in all directions – a handmade infinity suspended above an unknowable below.
