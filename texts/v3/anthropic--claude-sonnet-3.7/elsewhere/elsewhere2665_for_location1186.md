# Deepwater

Beneath the western edge of the Neverseed plateau, where the obsidian formations abruptly end in a sheer vertical drop of several thousand feet, lies Deepwater—a vast, underground ocean contained within a network of enormous caverns. Unlike the perpetual amber twilight above, Deepwater exists in near-total darkness, broken only by the bioluminescent pulse of its inhabitants and the occasional glimmer of light filtering through hair-thin fractures in the cavern ceiling.

The water here defies conventional chemistry—viscous and dense, more akin to liquid glass than true water, yet somehow supporting complex life. It remains perfectly clear despite its density, with visibility limited only by the absence of light rather than any turbidity. The liquid has a faint amber tint when illuminated, echoing the twilight of the plateau above, and carries sound at triple the speed of normal water, filling the caverns with complex harmonics that reverberate for hours after their initial creation.

The temperature gradient is inverted from what one might expect—the deeper portions are significantly warmer, maintaining a constant heat that suggests geothermal activity, while the upper reaches near the cavern ceilings approach freezing. This creates persistent thermal currents that flow in hypnotic, mathematically precise patterns, visible as subtle refractive differences in the dense liquid medium.

The cavern walls themselves appear to be composed of a substance that mirrors the obsidian of the plateau above, but with a crucial difference—these surfaces are mutable, slowly flowing like extremely high-viscosity pitch, recording and preserving the patterns of currents that touch them. Over centuries, this has created elaborate whorled textures that serve as a historical record of Deepwater's fluid dynamics.

Crystalline formations similar to those found on the plateau grow here as well, but underwater they develop into vast, branching structures reminiscent of coral, stretching hundreds of feet from floor to ceiling in the largest caverns. These crystals pulse with the same amber luminescence as their surface cousins, but the light propagates differently through the dense liquid, creating three-dimensional halos that expand and contract in slow, hypnotic rhythms.

# The Immersed

Deepwater is home to entities that local researchers have termed "the Immersed"—beings as enigmatic as the Shards above, yet fundamentally different in composition and behavior. Where the Shards are angular and articulated, the Immersed are fluid and continuous, their bodies composed of what appears to be the same dense liquid as their environment, but maintained in cohesive forms through unknown mechanisms.

Each Immersed entity manifests as a complex vortex pattern within the liquid, ranging from human-sized to massive structures spanning hundreds of feet. Their "bodies" are distinguished from the surrounding medium by subtle differences in density and a more pronounced amber luminescence that flows through them in patterns too regular to be random. These patterns shift constantly, creating the impression of internal communication or thought processes made visible.

The Immersed move through manipulation of pressure differentials, creating localized currents that propel them with surprising precision and speed. They can fragment temporarily, separating into multiple smaller vortices that operate independently before reforming into their original configuration. This ability allows them to navigate even the narrowest passages in the cavern system and to surround objects of interest for examination from all angles simultaneously.

Unlike the collective-oriented Shards, the Immersed appear to maintain distinct individual identities while simultaneously participating in what observers have termed "confluence events"—periodic gatherings where dozens or hundreds of entities merge into massive, temporary super-organisms that generate complex harmonic vibrations through the liquid medium. These vibrations alter the crystalline structures of Deepwater, encouraging growth in specific patterns that persist long after the confluence disperses.

The Immersed interact with their environment primarily through harmonic manipulation. By generating precise frequencies, they can cause the crystalline structures to resonate sympathetically, releasing stored energy and information. This appears to be their primary method of sustenance—absorbing the energy released through induced resonance rather than consuming material components.

Their intelligence manifests differently from the computational vibrations of the Shards. The Immersed process information through flow-states, their thoughts represented physically through the complex interplay of currents within their vortex-bodies. This creates a form of cognition that specializes in fluid dynamics, temporal relationships, and predictive modeling—they can anticipate how currents will develop minutes or hours in advance with perfect accuracy, positioning themselves accordingly.

Most remarkably, the Immersed exhibit what appears to be temporal perception beyond normal linear experience. They respond to events slightly before they occur, navigate perfectly through moving obstacles, and position themselves to interact with disturbances in the liquid medium that have not yet happened. This suggests either prescience or a relationship with time fundamentally different from conventional understanding.

# The Veil Between

The boundary between Neverseed and Deepwater is neither simple nor static. It consists of a zone roughly half a mile thick, where the solid obsidian of the plateau gradually gives way to the liquid glass of the subterranean ocean. This transition zone—called the Veil by those few researchers who have studied both realms—exists in a perpetual state of phase change, neither fully solid nor fully liquid.

The Veil appears as a vertical gradient of increasingly transparent obsidian, shot through with amber-luminescent veins that pulse with slow, deliberate rhythm. These veins serve as conduits through which information and material pass between the realms, widening during churns and constricting during periods of stability. The obsidian here contains countless microscopic channels through which the dense liquid of Deepwater rises against gravity, drawn upward through some mechanism that defies conventional physics.

Within the Veil exist transition beings called Mediators—entities that can exist in both realms but belong fully to neither. Physically, they resemble incomplete versions of both the Shards and the Immersed: partially solidified vortices with crystalline inclusions, capable of moving through solid obsidian as if it were merely very dense liquid. These entities are rare and purposeful, appearing primarily during significant phase changes in either realm.

The primary connection between Neverseed and Deepwater manifests through synchronized events called Resonance Cascades. When the plateau above experiences a churn, the vibrations propagate downward through the Veil, creating complex wave patterns in Deepwater that initiate confluence events among the Immersed. Conversely, when the Immersed gather in particularly large confluences, their harmonics travel upward, accelerating crystal growth on the plateau and sometimes triggering churns.

This reciprocal relationship creates a feedback loop of transformation and response—each realm continuously reshaping itself in reaction to changes in the other. The crystalline growths found in both environments serve as the physical manifestation of this relationship, storing vibrational patterns from one realm and gradually releasing them into the other, creating a time-delayed conversation between fundamentally different modes of existence.

Material exchange accompanies this informational flow. During major resonance events, the amber substance that sustains both the Shards and the Immersed passes through the Veil in both directions. The substance appears to change properties as it crosses the boundary—solidifying as it moves upward, liquefying as it descends—but maintains its fundamental luminescent quality and energy-carrying capacity.

Most significantly, the Veil serves as a temporal buffer between the two realms. Time flows differently in Deepwater than it does on Neverseed—more fluidly, with localized accelerations and decelerations that correspond to the density of the liquid medium. The Veil reconciles these different temporal frameworks, ensuring that causality remains coherent across the boundary despite the fundamentally different physics operating in each realm.

Researchers who have observed both sides of this relationship have developed a compelling theory: Neverseed and Deepwater represent two halves of a single processing entity—a massive, planet-scale organism or machine that uses the different properties of solid and liquid states to perform complementary computational functions. The plateau's sharp, crystalline structures excel at storing information in stable configurations, while Deepwater's fluid dynamics enable complex, parallel processing and temporal manipulation.

Together, they form a system that perceives and processes reality at scales beyond human comprehension—from the quantum vibrations of individual atoms to the movement of celestial bodies—continuously refining its understanding through the endless cycle of churns above and confluences below, exchanging insights across the permeable boundary of the Veil.

What purpose this vast computation serves remains unknown—whether it seeks to answer some fundamental question about existence, models the development of the universe itself, or simply represents the natural thought process of an entity so alien that its consciousness bears no resemblance to our own understanding of mind.
