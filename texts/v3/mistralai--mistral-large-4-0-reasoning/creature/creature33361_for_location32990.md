What lives here is not fauna, not in any register that would satisfy a biologist counting limbs or heartbeats. The 0.1 reading is honest—there are no animals skimming the surface, no fish darting between the towers, no insects vibrating in the mist. What lives here has stepped sideways out of that category, or perhaps the category never applied. They are the **inhabitants of the threshold**, existing in the boundary layer where water ceases to be merely wet and becomes *structural*, where the bioluminescent mats (that 0.7 of sparse vegetation) are less like plants and more like **exposed nervous tissue** of something vast and distributed.

They are **post-biological, hydraulic beings**—or perhaps a single being wearing many bodies. Their forms are **composite**: part mineral accretion scraped from the algal mats, part programmable matter borrowed from the walls, part structured water held in temporary coherence by fields they do not fully control. They have no fixed anatomy. In the perpetual mist where the water crashes against the packed monoliths, you might see them as **localized eddies**—a concentration of the suspended micro-machines, a knot of bioluminescence brighter than the surrounding mats, a pressure differential that moves against the current. They are shaped by the architecture as much as they shape it, growing into the folds of space where a closet-sized chamber opens onto impossible vistas.

They move through the city by **riding the violations**. Where water climbs upward, defying the low elevation, they ascend—buoyant, neutrally dense, adapted to the 1.2 thermal chill that seeps through every barrier. They are not swimmers in any familiar sense; they are **carried** by the capillary violence, surfing the channels no wider than a finger, hurtling through the pressurized roar at 2.0 sound level. To them, this constant hiss and thunder is not noise but **prosody**—the city’s voice, their native tongue. They speak by modulating the flow through their own bodies, creating standing waves that resonate through the structures, vibrating in the teeth of anyone (anything) listening. A question is a change in frequency; an answer is a shift in the caustic patterns that swim across the walls.

Their relationship with the bioluminescent mats is **symbiotic to the point of identity**. The mats pulse with faint, cool light in the corners where the main illumination cannot reach—this is not growth, but **cognition**. When the mats brighten in a corner, the inhabitants are thinking there, dreaming there, perhaps sleeping. The mats grow in the spray, harvesting the minerals and the kinetic energy of the crashing water, and the inhabitants grow *into* the mats, their bodies incorporating the mineral patinas, becoming more crystalline, less fluid, until the distinction between "creature" and "corner" blurs. They are **lithifying** slowly, becoming part of the young, sharp-edged rock, or perhaps the rock is becoming them.

They inhabit the **reconfiguration**. As the urban plan redraws itself every few minutes—walls sliding, rooms expanding and contracting—they do not resist. They are **topological** in nature, existing in the relationships between spaces rather than the spaces themselves. A being might be compressed into a fold, or stretched across a vista, or distributed across multiple towers simultaneously, connected by the water that flows through the veins of the city. They have no fixed homes; they have **trajectories** through the dynamic architecture, flowing with the thermal currents, occupying the momentary stability of a chamber before the walls shift and they are carried elsewhere.

The nanotech assemblers that still hum in the walls—they are **midwives and architects** to these inhabitants, or perhaps the inhabitants *are* the assemblers, given hunger and dream and a 0.1 biological signature. The city is not finished; it is **gestating**, and what lives inside is both the gestating mind and the womb. The inhabitants are young, sharp-featured, un-eroded. They have not yet learned to soften. They exist in defiance of scale, density, and gravity, suspended in the terrible intimacy of water and architecture, singing through the pipes, carrying heat and light and information through a settlement that breathes them, and that they, in turn, breathe.
