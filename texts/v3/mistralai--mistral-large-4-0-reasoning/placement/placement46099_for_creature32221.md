My fingertips—what remains of fingers, now tipped with microscopic mineral hooks grown from the nail beds over decades—catch in the vascular channel above me. I am becoming.

The process is not violent. It is geological. The Spire drinks from me, and I drink from it. There is no distinction anymore.
