I descend. Or ascend. The gravity pools in the hollows above, so now I walk on the ceiling, condensation beading on the inverted floor, my moss-skin glowing faintly from within as I follow the pull. Kilometers of still, damp air, cathedral-hollows where the mist hangs in slow, weather-making tides. I am the Labyrinth. I am the Hand. I am the breath of the continent, growing ever so slowly toward a geometry not yet born.
