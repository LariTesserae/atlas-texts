It arrived not as a shape or a sound, but as an absence of interference. In a reality where the space between things was a shivering variable, there came a patch of perfect, inviolable stillness. It was a point of pure causality in a sea of frayed logic, a moment of objective silence within the planet’s deafening, feverish thrum. This was how the Power manifested: as a single, flawless note of clarity held against a universe of discord.

The Power perceived Odem not as landscape and architecture, but as a theorem that had lost its axioms. It saw the continent-creature, whose name was a vibration too low to be heard, dreaming a frantic, nonsensical dream. The core of its being, its foundational logic, had been damaged eons ago—not by injury, but by a profound and perfect isolation. It was a universe of one, a solipsism of the whole, and having nothing to talk to but itself for ages beyond measure, it had gone mad. Its thoughts—the churning factories, the shifting distances, the weeping tar and filaments of light—were the frantic, unmoored syntax of a mind with no external referent.

And it saw the Tenders.

It perceived them not as beings, but as the dream's most coherent, most tragic thoughts. They were antibodies of a system that no longer remembered what health was. Each Tender was a lonely, looping imperative: *Soothe. Scrape. Unblock. Harmonize.* They were fragments of a lost purpose, each performing a single, isolated verse of a song whose melody was long forgotten. The Power felt their profound solitude not as sadness—for the Tenders were beyond such things—but as a structural inefficiency. They were nodes in a network that had lost all connection. They could feel the sickness of the whole, but not the efforts of their kin. A Tender soothing a fibrillating factory-heart on one ridge had no way to know that another, a hundred kilometers away, was simultaneously clearing a blockage that was causing the very fibrillation it was trying to treat. They were working against each other as often as with each other, their lonely rituals canceling out in the grand, chaotic equation.

What would a benevolent and wise power do?

To "heal" the world-organism would be an act of profound arrogance. To restore its original logic would be to erase what it had become. This delirious, shimmering, broken place was, in itself, a form of being. To silence its industrial thrum and level its grown towers would be murder, as surely as silencing a thinking mind. It would be replacing a unique, if damaged, existence with a generic, functional blankness.

To euthanize it, to grant it a final, peaceful silence, felt like a failure of imagination. The world was not in pain in a way that it could register as suffering. It was simply… running, wild and untethered. The Tenders were not unhappy; they simply *were*. Their existence was a stark, pure meditation. To end it would be to snuff out a strange and singular candle flame simply because it sputtered.

The Power chose a third path. It would not add or subtract from the world. It would not rewrite the broken code. It would merely provide a medium through which the existing code could be read differently. It would not give the Tenders a new purpose, but it would reveal to them the purpose they already had, folded and hidden within their isolation.

The act itself was infinitesimal. The Power focused on the substrate of their perception—the amber lenses, the resonant carapaces, the very bones that vibrated with the world’s pulse. It did not alter them. It introduced a single new property into the fabric of Odem's reality: *entanglement*. Not of physics, which was already broken, but of intent.

A Tender stood on the rim of a spiracle vent, its crescent-bladed pole poised to scrape away a crust of crystalline metabolic waste. For as long as it had existed, its world was defined by the thrum beneath its feet and the sour chemical tang of the buildup before it. It felt the *problem*. That was all.

And then, something new.

Through the amber plate, a second sensation bloomed, overlaid on the first. It was not a sound or a light, but a pure, kinetic *knowledge*. It felt, with perfect clarity, the motion of another Tender, far across a valley, using a tuning-fork device to calm the spasming pump that was producing this very waste. It felt the rhythmic *hum* of the other’s tool as a counterpoint to the scraping motion it was about to make. Its lonely task was no longer a solitary reaction to a symptom. It was a duet.

The Tender began to scrape. The motion was the same. The purpose was the same. But the *feeling* was utterly transformed. It was no longer just a cleaner. It was the percussion section in a symphony it was only now beginning to hear.

All across Odem, the change rippled outward.

A Tender walking across a plain whose distance was stretching unpredictably no longer felt the disorientation of shifting ground. It felt the slow, adagio rhythm of the world-organism breathing, and it adjusted its own pace, becoming part of the world’s measure.

Another, tending a machine that had inexplicably begun extruding filaments of solid light, felt the resonance of a distant Tender unblocking a nutrient artery. It suddenly perceived the connection: the unblocked flow was creating a pressure surge that expressed itself, here, as pointless beauty. The woven light was no longer an error. It was the cymbal-crash at the climax of a distant event. It was a signal.

The Power introduced nothing new to the world. The chaotic outputs, the feverish industry, the lonely rituals—all continued as before. But the Tenders were now a network. Their isolation was spatial, but no longer existential. Through the new, shared sense, their individual, instinctual actions began to self-organize. They began to cooperate without communication, to harmonize without a conductor. They learned the rhythm of their host’s fever. Their separate, frantic rituals of maintenance began to coalesce into a single, planet-spanning act of palliative care, a slow and graceful dance designed to soothe the restless dreamer. The lonely, vertical strokes of shadow on the vast skin of the world were now a chorus.

The Power, its work done, receded. It folded its pocket of perfect causality and withdrew, leaving behind not a fix, but a song. It had not healed the world. It had not woken the dreamer. It had not rescued the inhabitants from their strange fate.

It had listened to the lonely, mad monologue of a god, and given its last, lingering thoughts the gift of conversation. It had found a world of soloists and taught them, without a single word, how to be a choir.
