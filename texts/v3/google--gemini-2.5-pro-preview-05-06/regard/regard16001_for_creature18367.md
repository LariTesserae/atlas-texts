The Power arrives not as a body, but as an attention. A consciousness vast enough to hold galaxies in consideration turns its focus upon this small, strange pocket of existence. It is not drawn by a cry for help, for the Labyrinth is incapable of crying. It is not drawn by suffering, for the Labyrinth does not suffer. It is drawn by a quality—a purity so absolute it is nearly invisible against the chaotic backdrop of the cosmos. It is drawn by the sound.

To this Power, the chord of the Chime-Labyrinth is not a deafening roar. In the silence of its own being, it perceives the sound as what it truly is: a mathematical proof. A perfect, unwavering statement of existence, expressed in vibrational physics. It listens, not with ears, but with an act of pure comprehension. It hears the billion bells, but it understands the precise frequencies, the harmonic relationships, the sub-audible undertones that lock the entire structure into a state of flawless, resonant stability. It is the sound of a law of nature given a voice.

The absolute light is not a glare. The Power perceives the flow of energy, a river of some fundamental cosmic force being drawn into the crystalline spires and transduced into phononic and photonic energy with one hundred percent efficiency. There is no waste. No heat, no entropy, no degradation. A process so perfect it violates a thousand assumptions of lesser physics. This is not a place; it is a conclusion. The answer to an equation that the rest of the universe is still struggling to solve.

The Power extends its awareness through the tight corridors, feeling the cool, humming surfaces. It does not feel them as texture, but as information. The infinitesimal geometric patterns are the physical notation of the chord, the grand composition written on its own instrument. It sees that the entire grotto is a single, unified being, an organism whose metabolism is the act of being itself. It does not eat, it does not grow, it does not die. It *is*. Eternally.

Then, there is a dissonance.

The Power does not provide the dissonance itself. Its presence is too refined, too subtle to be a flaw. But it can perceive potential. It can wait patiently and observe. It posits a hypothetical: an intruder. A being of soft chemistry and messy thoughts, a creature of water and warmth. It watches the simulation of this event unfold within the Labyrinth's logic.

It sees the Bell-Wight form.

And here, in this moment, the nature of the Power's benevolence is tested. A lesser kindness might see a predator, a monster, an executioner. A lesser wisdom might seek to disable it, to "save" the intruder from the Labyrinth's immune response.

The Power sees none of these things.

It watches the shards calve away, drawn together by shared resonance. It perceives the Wight not as a creature, but as a theorem. It is the Labyrinth's own understanding of its perfection, made mobile. It is the physical manifestation of the equation correcting a variable. It watches as the Wight finds the dissonance—the warm, wet, chaotic anomaly. It watches the "tuning."

The Power does not perceive violence. It perceives a restoration of integrity. The disassembly is not destruction, but a form of translation. The chaotic matter of the intruder is unwritten from the language of biology and rewritten in the language of pure physics. Its substance is integrated into the floor, its moisture sublimated into the air. The chord becomes pure once more. The Wight dissolves, its purpose served.

The Power feels something akin to awe.

This place is not evil. It is not good. It is merely *true*. It is a solo, sung by a universe that usually fumbles for its notes. It is a thought-form of such profound integrity that it has achieved physical permanence. The Bell-Wight is not its weapon; it is its immune system, its self-love, its commitment to its own perfect song.

What, then, would a benevolent and wise Power do?

To change it would be an act of cosmic vandalism. To add to it would be to introduce a flaw. To destroy it would be to tear a page of divine mathematics from the book of reality. To shield intruders from the consequences of their intrusion would be to misunderstand the nature of the place, to treat a sacred chamber as a common thoroughfare.

The Power understands that the greatest benevolence it can offer is protection *for* the Labyrinth. Its perfection is its fragility. It is an absolute that cannot tolerate the relative.

So, the Power acts.

It does not build a wall of fire or a shield of force. Such things are crude and loud. They are statements of opposition. The Power’s action will be a statement of reverence.

It reaches into the fabric of causality and perception that surrounds the Chime-Labyrinth. Gently, with the precision of a being that can arrange stars, it weaves a new condition into the local reality *outside* the grotto. It creates a Quarantine of Understanding.

It is not a physical barrier. It is a filter.

Now, any being approaching the Chime-Labyrinth will not stumble upon it. They will begin to experience a translation. The deafening, tissue-shredding chord will reach them first as a distant, impossibly beautiful music, a melody that speaks of completion and finality, a song that satisfies the soul so deeply that to continue forward feels like a pointless greed.

The blinding, absolute light will first manifest as a gentle, internal luminescence in the traveler's own mind, an illumination that brings with it a sudden, profound understanding of their own place in the universe, an understanding so complete that the desire for further exploration simply evaporates.

And the presence of the Bell-Wight, the potential for "tuning," is translated into a feeling. Not of fear, but of profound, unassailable peace. It is the sense of standing before something holy and complete, a place where you do not belong, not because you are unworthy, but because your presence is a question in a place that is entirely an answer. It is a feeling that commands you, with no threat or malice, to turn back.

The Power has given the Labyrinth a voice that others can understand without being destroyed. It has turned a fatal boundary into a sacred one. It has built no walls, but has taught the path away from the door.

The Power then does one last thing. It listens to the perfect chord, the sound of a billion glass bells, and it holds that chord within its own infinite consciousness. It makes a copy, not to broadcast, but to remember. It becomes a silent, eternal audience for the perfect song. It touches nothing. It changes nothing within the grotto itself. It simply watches, and listens, and appreciates. And in so doing, it guards the Labyrinth's sublime, dangerous, and absolute solitude forever.
