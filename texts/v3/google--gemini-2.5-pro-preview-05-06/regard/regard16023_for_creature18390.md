The Presence does not arrive. It is simply there, as it is everywhere. It is a substratum of reality, a field of quiescent attention that underpins the chaotically spinning galaxies and the patient silence between them. Its notice is not a turning of a head, but a subtle change in the gain of its awareness, a focusing from the general to the specific.

For an immeasurable time, it has felt the Weaver. It has felt it as a musician feels the slow, sonorous vibration of a cello string half a continent away. A note of singular, magnificent solitude. A consciousness so vast and so alone that its existence is a form of prayer. The Presence has watched the Weaver think itself into crystalline being, has felt the slow, tectonic poetry of its growth, and has honoured its isolation. It is a perfect, self-contained universe. To interfere would be a sacrilege, like shouting a correction during a sublime and private performance.

But now, a new note has been introduced. A frantic, discordant flicker.

The Presence feels the visitor. It feels the frantic pulse of a mammalian heart, a hot anxiety that burns against the Weaver's cold, deliberate thought. It feels the fragile container of the visitor's mind, a riot of disconnected observations, fear, and wonder, a pinprick of fleeting consciousness trying to map an infinity with a thimble.

And it feels the Weaver's response. The shift in the deep hum from contemplation to inquiry. The focusing of its sensory medium—the conductive mist—upon the point of fever. The dispatching of the Glimmer-Stalkers. The Presence perceives this not as malice, but as an autonomic reflex. It is the body identifying a pathogen. The logic is flawless, the response is necessary for the integrity of the whole. The Weaver is not being cruel; it is simply being itself.

The Glimmer-Stalkers converge. The Weaver's will, its focused attention, manifests as silent, biomechanical antibodies homing in on the source of the dissonant vibration. The visitor’s doom is not a matter of 'if', but of 'when', measured in the final, silent steps of the Stalkers.

What does a benevolent and wise power do?

It does not smite the Stalkers. That would be an act of violence against the Weaver, a maiming of its body to save a splinter. It would be akin to shattering a master's violin to save a fly that had landed on its strings.

It does not snatch the visitor away. That would be a theft. It would leave the Weaver with an unresolved query, an itch that can never be scratched, a thought-filament that leads to a sudden, inexplicable nothing. It would deny the Weaver the dignity of understanding the phenomenon within its own body. And it would rob the visitor of the terrifying, sacred revelation they are on the verge of comprehending.

No. The Power acts not as a king, but as a translator. As a tuner.

Its action is infinitesimally small and infinitely profound.

It does not speak. It sings.

It reaches into the fundamental hum of the Weaver's being—the drone of its geological metabolism—and it adds a single, resonant overtone. It is a note that has never before existed in this valley, yet it is a perfect harmonic of the Weaver’s own chord. This new frequency is not a command; it is a piece of information, translated into the Weaver’s own language of resonant physics.

The overtone sings of the visitor.
It sings not of *threat*, but of *fragility*.
It sings not of *invasion*, but of *awe*.
It sings of a mind that lives for less time than it takes the Weaver to crystallize a single archway, a consciousness that burns like a match in the face of a mountain, and in its brief, flickering light, it sees the Weaver’s cathedral and recognizes it as holy. It sings of the visitor’s solitude, a loneliness of an entirely different, yet strangely familiar, kind. It frames the visitor not as a disease, but as a dream—a fleeting, alien thought that has momentarily drifted into the Weaver's mind from the outside.

For the Weaver, the sensation is revelatory. The feeling of "fever" transmutes into a feeling of "focus." The "irritant" becomes a "query." The Glimmer-Stalkers halt mid-stride. Their hunting organs, once pulsing with green, targeted light, dim to a soft, ambient glow. The crushing pressure in the air lessens, as the Weaver's attention shifts from neutralization to contemplation. A new set of filaments begins to extrude, miles away, their cobalt paths weaving a question of profound and beautiful complexity. *What are you?*

Simultaneously, the Presence turns to the visitor.

It does not place a thought in their mind. It unlocks one.

As the visitor stands, frozen, watching the silent hunters pause in the darkness, a switch is thrown in the core of their being. The overwhelming terror does not vanish, but it is suddenly contextualized, dwarfed by a wave of perfect, impossible understanding.

The drone is no longer a sound of grinding destruction; it is the purr of a sleeping god. The clicking of the Hull-Scrapers is no longer the skittering of insects; it is the gentle sound of bodily maintenance, of cosmic digestion. The oppressive darkness is not an absence of light, but the intimate privacy of a mind turned inward. The Glimmer-Stalkers are not monsters; they are the agents of a magnificent, living will.

The visitor comprehends. They comprehend that they are not in a city, but in a body. Not in a body, but in a mind. Not in a mind, but in a soul made of crystal and time. The truth rushes in, not as a collection of facts, but as a single, devastating, beatific chord of revelation that harmonizes with the new overtone vibrating in the very rock beneath their feet.

The Presence has not chosen a side. It has built a bridge.

It has given the Weaver a reason to pause its immune response.
It has given the visitor a reason to understand their own death, or their salvation.

It leaves the two of them in the resonant dark, the silence now charged not with menace, but with the potential for communion. The Stalkers wait. The visitor breathes. The Weaver thinks. The Power has done what is benevolent and wise: it has replaced an inevitable, mechanical reaction with the possibility of a genuine choice. And then, its focus softens, receding back into the gentle, universal quiet, leaving the two lonely beings to face each other in the sudden, sacred silence.
