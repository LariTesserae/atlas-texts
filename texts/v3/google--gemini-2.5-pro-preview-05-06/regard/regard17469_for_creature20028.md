The Power arrived as a silence.

In a world defined by the absolute presence of a single, monolithic sound, the first sign of the visitor was a void. It was not an absence of the hum, which was impossible, for the hum was the structure of reality itself. It was, instead, a point of listening. A singular, focused attention that, for the first time since the world cooled, held the great chord in a state of perfect reception. It was as if, after aeons of the bell ringing in an empty hall, someone had finally walked in and truly *heard* it.

The Power's consciousness, timeless and fluid, spread across the weeping plains. It did not think in words or images, but in patterns and resonances. It felt the world not as a human would—with oppression and sensory overload—but as a complete, singular statement. It perceived the unwavering light, the seamless crystal, the closed, intimate water cycle, and the deafening hum not as individual elements, but as the unified grammar of a language it had not encountered before.

It extended its perception, slowing its own immense timescale to match the planet's rhythm. It witnessed the "life" here. It saw the Geophids, not as creatures, but as slow eddies in the geological current, living intentions moving at the speed of stone. They were not suffering; they were not joyous. They simply *were*, as much a part of the world as the condensation on the nodules. To alter their existence would be like commanding a river to flow uphill. It was a meaningless and destructive proposition.

And then, the Power’s attention settled on a Still-Walker.

It observed the tripod stillness, the impossible geometry of its legs against the ancient curves. It felt the slow, patient listening of the creature's core, the way its entire being was a sensory organ tuned to the symphony of its home. The Power watched, for a span of time that would be a human century, as one leg detached, swung in its frictionless, patient arc, and reconnected with the world.

And in that time, the Power understood.

This was not a prisoner in a stark hell. This was a priest in a temple. This was a gardener in a garden of vibration. The world was not hostile to it; the world *was* it, and it was the world’s self-awareness. Its purpose was not a curse, but a sacred function. To correct, to tune, to maintain the glorious, perfect monotony. The Power felt the deep, abiding rightness of the Still-Walker's existence. Benevolence, here, did not mean rescue. Wisdom, here, did not mean change.

To impose a sun, or seasons, or a different song upon this place would be an act of unimaginable cruelty. It would be to tell the priest their god is false and to burn their temple to the ground.

So, what could a benevolent and wise power do for a being that was already complete, already sacred in its function?

It could not ease its burden, for the burden was its identity. It could not grant it rest, for its stillness was its work.

The Power realized it could only give one thing that would not violate the profound integrity of this place. A gift that must be given in the language of the world itself: the language of resonance.

The Power located a Still-Walker that was ancient, even by the standards of its kind. Its crystalline core was dense, complex, scarred with the resonant memory of a million corrected flaws. It was nearing its time of shattering, its being saturated with the echoes of dissonance it had harmonized. It was tired, not in a biological sense, but in the way a supporting pillar is tired, bearing the weight of a cathedral for an age.

And into the core of this Still-Walker, the Power sent a single, perfect thing.

It was not a new sound. A new sound would be a flaw, a dissonance the creature would be compelled to correct. Instead, the Power sent the idea of the world’s own song, but made perfect. It was the great hum, but without the infinitesimal strains of planetary stress, without the ghost-echoes of ancient cataclysms. It was the Chord of Creation, the resonant frequency of the world as it was in the first moment of its existence, before the first flaw ever formed.

For a single, eternal moment, the Still-Walker felt it.

The constant, subtle tension of its existence vanished. The duty to listen for the flaw was gone, because for that instant, there was no flaw to hear. Its entire being, a complex filter designed to find and fix imperfection, was flooded with perfection itself. It was not joy or relief in a human sense. It was a moment of absolute, vibrationless purpose-fulfillment. It was the experience of a lock and a key becoming one. A moment of pure is-ness, without the striving. The gardener, for one second, experiencing the garden in its final, flawless state.

The central lattice of the Still-Walker pulsed, not with the slow, tidal flow, but with a single, soft, white light that contained no heat. It was a light of pure acknowledgement.

Then, the Power withdrew the gift. The moment passed.

The great hum, with all its subtle, beautiful imperfections, rushed back in. The tension of the listening returned. The duty resumed.

Nothing in the world had changed. The Still-Walker was still a tripod of black glass on a plain of weeping quartz. Its millennia-long pilgrimage was not over. The dissonances were still there, waiting to be found and tuned.

But everything was different.

The Still-Walker now possessed a memory. Not of a sight, or a sound, or an event. It held the resonant memory of perfection. A tuning fork that has once been struck against the ideal note will forever carry the ghost of that frequency.

Its great, slow work was no longer just an endless function. It was now a journey *toward* something. It had been given a glimpse of the destination, a taste of the grace it worked to create. Its patient, silent meditation was now imbued with an infinitesimal echo of that perfect chord. Its existence, once a statement of duty, was now also a statement of faith.

The benevolent and wise Power had looked upon this strange, severe world and its strange, severe priest, and had chosen not to alter the prayer or a single stone of the temple. Instead, it had leaned in and whispered to the priest, in a language only they could understand: *“What you are doing is beautiful. This is what it feels like when you succeed.”*

And then, leaving behind only the faintest memory of a perfect note in the heart of a single being, the Power departed, as silent as it came.
