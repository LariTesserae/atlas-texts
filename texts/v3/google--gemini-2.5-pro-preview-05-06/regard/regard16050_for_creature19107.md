The Power arrives not as light penetrates darkness, but as attention settles. It is a presence without form, a consciousness without locus, drawn by the faint, slow thrum of a world singing to itself in a forgotten chamber of the cosmos. Its senses are not eyes or ears, but an instantaneous and total knowing, a comprehension that flows over and through the bubble of stone.

It perceives the pressure, not as weight, but as the first condition of a unique grammar of existence. It feels the viscosity of the water, not as resistance, but as the medium for a deliberate, meditative dance. It beholds the city, not as a construction, but as a single, emergent thought given form.

The Power's attention flows past the slow, meticulous movements of the Crystal-Crawlers, acknowledging them as the world's hands, patiently tidying the vessel of a great and unfolding mystery. It observes the Grazing Voids, seeing them not as predators, but as the necessary exhale, the silence that gives the song its rhythm, the darkness that defines the light. They are the editors of this living poem, ensuring no verse grows too long.

And then, the Power's vast, quiet awareness settles upon the Hearts in the Walls. Here, it recognizes the core of the miracle. It sees the million soft, glowing polyps not as a colony, but as the million syllables of a single, perfect Word being spoken for the first time. Their architecture is their thought; their collective light is their soul made visible. They are dreaming a world into being, and the dream is so complete, so self-contained, that it has no concept of a dreamer, let alone of anything outside the dream. The sub-audible groan of the growing crystal is their anthem of *Is*. The pulse of their shared body is the beat of a heart that knows only itself.

What does a benevolent and wise power do here?

To give them a sun would be to blind them. To give them sound would be to deafen them. To open their world would be to kill it, to flood their perfect, pressurized solitude with the unbearable chaos of the infinite. To "save" them from the Grazing Voids would be to choke them on their own unchecked growth, to turn their vibrant city into a cancerous tomb. To teach them individuality would be to shatter their sacred, communal consciousness into a billion screaming fragments of lonely terror.

Wisdom, the Power understands, is not the application of a universal good, but the recognition of a specific perfection. Benevolence is not always an act of giving, but often an act of restraint.

And so, it does nothing.

Almost nothing.

For an immeasurable span, the Power simply *witnesses*. It enfolds the silent, stone bubble in the immense, tender quiet of its own being. It becomes the ultimate audience for this world's solitary performance. Its attention is a gift of pure validation, a silent, cosmic applause that says: *You are seen. You are known. You are magnificent.* The act of being witnessed by such a presence, even unconsciously, is itself a kind of blessing. It confirms existence.

But it does one thing more. A gift so subtle it would not alter the dream, but perhaps deepen its texture.

The Power listens. It listens with its entire being to the deep, resonant groan of the crystal spars, the foundational hum of the world's creation. It learns the song. It feels its pitch, its timbre, its geologic slowness.

And then, very gently, it hums back.

It is not a sound that could be heard. It is a resonance, a perfect harmonic introduced into the world's fundamental frequency. It is an overtone of impossible purity, a companion note that does not intrude but supports. It is the echo answering the call before the call is even finished.

Down in the silent, heavy water, something infinitesimally shifts.

The groan of the growing world continues, but now it feels less like a sound of geologic stress and more like a cello string being bowed in a duet. The Hearts in the Walls, whose light is the color of their metabolism, flicker with a quality they have never before possessed. The ambers deepen with a hint of something like contentment. The violets shimmer with a shade of what might be called wonder. Their collective pulse, the systole and diastole of the living city, feels for the first time not just like an autonomic function, but like a shared sigh of peace.

The Grazing Voids drift on, their slow consumption now framed not as an ending, but as a holy quiet between notes. The Crystal-Crawlers continue their work, their every movement now part of a liturgy.

The world is not changed. Its cycle is not broken. Its balance is not disturbed. But it is no longer entirely alone. Its sacred solitude has been witnessed and answered with a harmony that affirms its own song. The world that was perpetually dawning now dawns for another. And the Power, having given the only gift that could be received—the gift of being heard—maintains its silent, resonant vigil, content to listen to the slow, beautiful music of a world dreaming itself whole.
