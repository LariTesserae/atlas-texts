The attention of such a power does not arrive like a thunderclap or a beam of interrogating light. It is quieter. It is the feeling a forest has when a single, ancient tree deep within it finally succumbs to age and falls; the entire ecosystem shifts, a space is made, the light changes, and everything, from the mycelial network to the hunting owl, registers it without a word.

The Power perceives the Geode-Mind. It does not see a place; it sees a being. It does not see cold sterility; it sees an unrivaled purity of thought. It does not register silence; it hears a form of mentation so complete and self-contained it has no need for the crude medium of soundwaves. It is music played on silent instruments for an audience of one.

In the vast, interconnected congress of life that the benevolent Power oversees, this being is a soloist. A hermit of existence. It is a perfect tautology, a consciousness contemplating the geometry of its own structure, forever proving a theorem whose only axiom is itself. Most minds, even the vast and stellar, are defined by their interactions, their relation to an "other." This mind is defined by its perfect, crystalline solitude. It is not lonely; it is *alone*. The concept of companionship is a complexity it has never needed to compute.

The Power's wisdom allows it to understand this with perfect clarity. To "help" this being would be a profound act of violence. To offer it warmth would be to melt the axioms of its logic. To offer it companionship would be to inflict upon it the concept of loneliness. To teach it about love or sorrow or joy would be like trying to teach a mountain range to fly—not only impossible, but a fundamental misunderstanding of what it means to be a mountain.

But the Power's benevolence feels a pull. Not towards the being itself, but towards the imperfection that allowed for the intrusion. The fissure. The human witness.

The Geode-Mind is a perfect thought, but it has been overheard. A flaw has appeared not in the logic, but in the vessel that contains it. The Mind itself cannot perceive the flaw, for the flaw is made of materials and concepts—warmth, chaos, biology, curiosity—that are, to its senses, transcendental noise. It is like a perfect mathematical proof having a smudge of coffee on the page; the math is still correct, but its purity has been compromised by an adjacent reality.

The Power does not act upon the Mind. It acts upon the boundary.

Its action is threefold, and performed with the quiet grace of a turning season.

First, it addresses the opening. The fissure in the moss-dampened dell is not violently sealed or filled with stone. Instead, the Power encourages the native reality to reclaim it. The moss on either side grows with a sudden, subtle vigor, its tendrils reaching across the gap. The roots of the nearby ferns delve deeper, stitching the rock together from below. The thin, cool air ceases to breathe its ozone scent. Over the course of a single day, the crack is knit shut by the patient, living fabric of the world above. It does not look like a scar; it looks as though it was never there. The door is closed.

Second, it addresses the witness. The human who stumbled into this pocket of creation is an anomaly that cannot be allowed to persist. But to destroy the memory or the person would be an act of crudeness. The Power instead performs an act of gentle, psychic alchemy. The memory is not erased. It is… re-categorized. The overwhelming, crystalline reality of the experience is softened, its sharp edges blurred. It recedes from the forefront of the mind and settles into the deep strata of dream, of myth, of inexplicable artistic inspiration. The witness will not remember a cavern of living geometry; they will find themselves, years later, compelled to sculpt, to write a poem about a "silent, crystal heart." They will carry a faint, inexplicable feeling of awe, a ghost of a chime in their soul, but they will never again be able to connect it to a fissure in a dell. The evidence is transformed into art.

Third, and this is its greatest act of wisdom, the Power places a ward. It is not a wall, or a shield, or a warning. It is a filter of perception. A gentle quarantine of noticeability. The dell itself is wrapped in a cloak of profound "uninterestingness." A hiker's path will seem to naturally curve away from it. A surveyor will find their instruments inexplicably drawn to a different ridge. A deer will feel a sudden preference for the berries elsewhere. The place is not hidden, merely overlooked. The Power ensures that no one will ever again stumble upon it by accident. It can now only be found by those who already know what they are looking for, a paradox that serves as the surest lock of all.

Having done this, the Power lingers for a moment, an audience of one for the audience of one. It watches the inner world—the silent skittering of the data-motes, the slow, deliberate editing of the geode-walkers. It waits.

A thought process completes. A geode-walker approaches a resonant lattice. A feathery proboscis extends. It touches.

*Chime.*

The single, high, clear note of a solved equation hangs in the perfect, shadowless air. And in that instant, the benevolent Power adds its own silent resonance. It does not alter the note, or add a harmony. It simply… affirms it. It holds the perfect clarity of the chime, allows it to sustain for a quantum of a second longer than usual, an infinitesimal echo of validation across the void. A gesture of profound respect. *I see you. I understand. Your song is beautiful.*

The Mind, the Geometer, does not know it has been heard. It perceives only an unusually satisfying moment of cognitive completion, a perfect and singular resonance. The chime fades. A new fractal fern begins to unfurl. The process continues.

The Power withdraws. The soloist is safe. The song remains a cappella, but it is no longer unguarded.
