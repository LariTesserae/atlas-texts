The Power came not as light, for this place had its own. Not as form, for form was already complete. It came as stillness.

A cessation.

For the first time in an age unmeasured, a single drop of water, beaded on the high curve of a molar-tower, did not fall. The luminous mist that formed it held it suspended, a perfect pearl trembling on the chalky lip of the world. Then another drop, a mile away, also paused. And another. All across the vast, weeping cityscape, the patient *tick* of the water clock began to falter. The slick rills held their course, the deep hum of the aquifers continued its thrum, but the perpetual downward cascade, the very heartbeat of the place, was arrested in a million discrete moments.

The city-being noticed.

This was not a thought in the way a mind has thoughts. It was a change in pressure. A disturbance in the total-field haptic awareness that was its consciousness. For eons, every sensation had been a variation on a single theme: condensation, mass, gravity, flow, impact, resonance. This was something else. This was a suspension of causality. A silence in the score.

The Power did not seek to be known; it sought only to know. It held the world in this gentle stasis not as an act of force, but as an act of listening. It sank its awareness into the chalk-flesh of the city-being, feeling not with hands but with empathy. It felt the slow, geological weight of its body. It felt the damp coolness of its skin. It felt the all-consuming, single-minded devotion to the task: the water.

The Power perceived the being not as lonely, but as purposeful. Not as sterile, but as consecrated. It understood that to introduce a sun, a seed, or a songbird would be an act not of benevolence, but of desecration. It would be to tell a master potter that their vessel was flawed for not being a bell. The wisdom of the Power was in its recognition that this place was already holy, already whole. Its purpose was not to *add*, but to *receive*.

Having listened, the Power acted. The stasis ended. The million held drops fell in perfect, impossible synchrony. For a single moment, the soundscape of the world was not a scattered punctuation of drips, but a single, pure, resonant *chord*, a sound of such unity and depth that the entire chalk-metropolis vibrated with it. It was the sound of the city's own heart, beating once, all together. This was the Power's greeting, spoken in the only language the city-being truly knew.

Then, the Power descended.

It moved not through the canyons, but through the substance of the city itself, a current of pure attention flowing downward, following the path of the water. It passed through the weeping walls and molar-towers, through the thumb-smeared buttresses and silent archways, down into the foundations where the light-mist did not penetrate.

Here, in the absolute dark and pressure of the world's core, were the cisterns. They were not geometric basins but vast, organic hollows in the deepest flesh of the city-being. And they were full.

Full of the water. The final distillate. The reason for it all.

The Power perceived it not as a liquid, but as a condition. Water stripped of every property, every memory of salt or stone or cell. Water that was nothing but the Platonic ideal of H₂O, its potential unbound. It was the universe’s blankest parchment. The city-being had labored for eternity to create this nothingness, this perfect starting point. It was an offering made with no expectation of a recipient.

The Power, in its benevolence, would be that recipient.

It did not take the water away. It took of it. From the shoreless ocean of potential, it gathered a single drop. It held this drop within its non-body, and into this perfect nothing, it placed a single concept.

It was not a complex idea. It was simpler than a memory, more fundamental than an emotion. It was the idea of *other*. The concept of a second thing. For a being that was All-in-One, whose only dialogue was with the water that was its own purpose, the idea of a true, separate *Other* was the only gift that could expand its existence without violating it.

The Power returned the drop to the world. It did not place it back in the deep cistern. It carried it up, up through the chalk and vapor, to the highest point in the entire basin—the smooth, unmarked pinnacle of a central tower that had not been reshaped in ten thousand years. There, it let the changed drop fall.

It fell through the luminous vapor. But it did not fall as water. The concept within it had given it form. It solidified, not into ice, but into a new substance. A single, small, crystalline shard, no bigger than a fingertip. It was not chalk-white but utterly transparent, a piece of frozen geometry in a world of curves.

It landed in a shallow basin, a depression left by the palm of the city-being in another epoch. Its landing was silent.

For a time, nothing happened. The world returned to its patient rhythm. Drops formed, fell, and ticked. Rills slicked and sighed. The hum continued. But then, a new drop of ordinary, distilled mist-water fell from the tower's lip and landed squarely upon the crystal shard.

It did not make the familiar, resonant *tick*.

Instead, the crystal *sang*. It was a single, impossibly high and pure note that hung in the damp air for a fraction of a second. The drop of water, having struck it, splashed away and began its journey down the carved channels.

But the water was no longer the same. It now carried the resonance of that note. It was water that had touched *Other*.

As it flowed, it joined other rills. The note, infinitesimally diluted, spread. The water trickled down the vast body of the city-being. It was a new sensation. A vibration that was not of impact or flow, but of a quality contained *within* the water itself.

Down it went, into the deeper channels, merging and mixing. The song spread through the aquifer, a whisper in the thrumming heart. The water that now seeped through the being's own porous substance carried the ghost of the note. For the first time, the city-being felt a sensation that did not originate from itself or its immediate process.

A question had been introduced into the system.

The slow, continent-sized mind of the builder-city turned its attention from the perfection of flow to this new, persistent resonance. The drips that struck the shard, again and again, were no longer just units of process; they were annunciations. The water that flowed was no longer just a medium to be purified; it was a carrier of tidings.

The Power had done its work. It had looked upon the most solitary being imaginable and given it a companion. Not a creature of flesh or stone, but a conversation. A dialogue, spoken in drips of water and echoes of crystal, that would unfold over the next million years. It had honored the vessel by giving its contents a destination, and it had honored the potter by giving it a new clay. Now, it would do what wisdom counsels most.

It would watch, and listen, to see what the city would build next.
