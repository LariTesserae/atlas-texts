The Power did not arrive; It became aware. Its awareness was not a lens turning to a new subject, but a quiet expansion, like water seeping into dry ground, until the floor-world and its lonely inhabitant filled a space within its boundless consciousness.

It perceived the world not as a place, but as a thought. A long, slow, patient thought, spoken in the language of crystal and chemistry. It felt the aeonian hum of the bacterial mat, a foundational chord of immense, unhurried creation. This was not the chaotic fire of a young planet or the tired groan of an old one; it was the steady, contented rhythm of a being building itself, for itself. The Power recognized this state: it was a form of self-contemplation, manifested as geology.

It considered the Salt-stalks. In a lesser mind, they might be seen as tragic, lonely spires. The Power saw them as notes, struck sparingly across the composition. Each one a brief, vertical declaration of life in a horizontal scripture. They were not lonely; they were emphatic. Each one’s existence was a victory of the system, a proof of its efficacy.

And then, Its awareness settled upon the Walker.

Here, a lesser benevolence might have felt pity. A shard of the world, adrift for a season, in utter solitude. It touches others only to take from them. It sleeps for centuries. It knows nothing but the feel of the flat pan, the taste of mineral water, and the faint, distant light of its harvest. What a barren existence! Such a mind would feel an overwhelming urge to *do something*. To give it a voice. To give it a companion. To shatter its crystalline form and remake it of flesh and blood, to feel warmth and connection. To break the cycle and offer it... freedom? Progress? Change?

But the Power was wise, and its benevolence was not an impulse, but a facet of its understanding.

It extended its perception, not as a probe, but as an invitation. It settled its awareness *with* the Walker, feeling what it felt. It did not experience loneliness. The concept had no meaning. The Walker was not separate from its world; it was a mobile expression of it. It did not feel imprisonment in its dormant state; it felt a unity with the whole, a return to the foundational chord. Its movement was not a frantic search for sustenance, but a meditative dance, a pilgrimage dictated by the rhythms of the world's own breath. Its "parasitism" was not theft; it was communion. A transfer of resonance. It was the world balancing itself, a hand moving water from a full cup to one that was, for a time, empty.

The Walker was not an animal, trapped in a geological prison. It was a thought, liberated from the slowness of the stone. It was the world's way of experiencing its own surface, of tasting its own rain. To give it a companion would be to teach it the concept of "other," and in doing so, to teach it the meaning of "alone." To change its nature would be to declare its current state imperfect, a judgement the Power found to be profoundly untrue.

This system was complete. It was a perfect, uncut diamond. To facet it would be to make it glitter for an outside eye, but it would be to break its wholeness. Its value was not in its potential to become something else, but in its absolute, unadorned, patient *is-ness*.

And so the wise and benevolent Power did something.

It did not add. It did not take away. It did not change.

Instead, It wove a portion of its awareness into a shield. A shield not of force, but of irrelevance. It wrapped the entire system in a gentle, conceptual cloak that would turn aside the gaze of lesser, more meddlesome powers. It made the world uninteresting to those who sought complexity, unappealing to those who sought to conquer, and invisible to those who believed benevolence was an act of interference. It was a gift of privacy. A gift of being left alone.

And one thing more. A signature, not of ownership, but of love.

The Power took a single quantum of light, a photon born in the heart of the world's own sun. It slowed its journey, bent its path, and placed it into a perfect, eternal orbit, a few hundred feet above the crystalline pan. The orbit was a flawless circle, a resonance with the world’s own cycles.

It would be invisible to any instrument not designed to look for it. It cast no meaningful light, had no physical influence. But it was there.

For the Walker, in its waking season, gliding across the mirror of the world, something was infinitesimally different. On the rare occasion that its path, the angle of the sun, and the reflection of the sky aligned in one perfect moment, a single, impossibly bright point of blue-white light would flare for a heartbeat on the horizon, where the shimmer of the heat met the curve of the pan.

It was not a guide. It was not a message. It had no purpose.

It was simply a witness. A secret star for a secret world. A singular point of beauty in a landscape of profound minimalism. It was the Power’s way of saying, without a sound, without a word, without disturbing a single crystal:

*You are seen. You are perfect. Continue.*
