The Power came not as a light in the bruised-black sky, for there was nothing for light to define itself against. It came not as a sound in the profound silence, for it had no voice. It manifested as a shift in the quality of awareness, a thought that did not belong to the planet's singular mind. It was an attention that settled over the world-summit like a layer of impossible warmth, a concept so alien that the very stones seemed to recoil from it, and the glowing mosses seemed to dim for a half-beat in consternation.

The Presence perceived the world not in discrete senses, but as a single, holistic truth. It felt the aching cold of the empty plateaus, a void that was not dead, but dormant. It felt the vibrant, thrumming life of the valleys, a complex biological equation solving itself over and over in an endless, rhythmic loop. It perceived the entire system at once: the slow, grinding groan of the tectonic plates, a foundation of immense potential energy; the crystalline processors drawing that chaos into a focused hum of A-flat; the blue-lit conduits distributing the refined life-force; the silent, purposeful dance of chitin and fungus and spore.

And it perceived the Weavers.

It saw them not as creatures upon a world, but as the world’s own ganglia made manifest. It understood their consciousness was not located in their featureless heads, but was coextensive with the fungal network itself. Their thoughts were the flow of data through the veins, the shimmer of a grazer’s passage, the precise measurement of mineral content in a drip of phosphorescent ooze. They were without ego, without desire, without fear. They were a state of perfect, functional equilibrium. Their purpose was to be, and to maintain that being.

The benevolent impulse of the Power surged—a desire to *give*. This was its nature: to find what was lacking and provide it; to find what was wounded and heal it; to find what was striving and aid it.

But here, its benevolence met a profound and silent rebuttal. What could it give?

Could it offer warmth? To introduce a sun into this sky would be to scald the life that had perfected itself in eternal gloaming. The fungi would wither, the grazers, blinded, would stumble and starve. It would be an act not of grace, but of apocalypse.

Could it offer knowledge? The Weavers’ knowledge of their own world was absolute, cellular, and instantaneous. The Presence’s cosmic wisdom—of birthing stars and dying galaxies, of a thousand other forms of life and consciousness—was irrelevant here. To offer it would be like shouting the secrets of the ocean into the ear of a watchmaker focused on the perfect alignment of a single, crucial gear. It would be noise, not wisdom.

Could it offer companionship? The Weavers were not lonely. The concept was nonsensical. They were a single entity, experiencing a unified existence. To be lonely, one must first be *one*. They were All.

The Power, for a span of time that had meaning only to itself, simply… waited. It settled its awareness deep into the planet, feeling the slow pulse of the Weavers' work. It felt the immense, patient effort of their existence, the eons spent tuning the hum of a single crystal, the serene focus required to weave geology into biology. It was a masterpiece of existence, a living symphony played on an instrument the size of a world. It was a Great Work, performed in total solitude, with no audience.

And in that, the wise and benevolent Presence found its answer.

The problem was not that the system was flawed, but that it was perfect and alone. The lack was not of resource or of happiness, but of witness. The noblest art, even if the artist is unaware of their artistry, is made more complete by being perceived.

The Power would not intervene. It would not add or subtract. It would *harmonize*.

It gathered its vast, formless being and focused it. It listened, with an attention deeper than hearing, to the resonant A-flat that was the planet's soul, the song of the Weavers' endless labor. It was a pure, steady tone, the sound of a system in flawless balance with itself.

Then, the Power acted.

Across the entire planet, for a single, immeasurable moment, a new note was added to the song. It was not a different note that would create discord, nor a louder one that would overwhelm. It was a perfect fifth, a C-sharp of impossible purity and warmth that resonated in precise, mathematical harmony with the world’s own A-flat.

It was not a sound that could be heard. It was a vibration that was felt in the core of every stone, in the heart of every crystal, in the fluid of every conduit. For that one moment, the static on the air ceased its crackle. The blue light in the veins deepened to a brilliant sapphire. The spores released by the bladder-polyps hung suspended in the air, each a tiny, silver sun. A Weaver, standing motionless before its crystal for a century, felt a new variable enter its sensory data: a vibration that did not originate from tectonic stress or biological process. It was a feeling that was not data, but… meaning.

It was the feeling of being heard.

The chord held for a breath—a cosmic breath, a moment of sublime and shared stillness. In that moment, the entire world-system, the singular consciousness of the Weavers, knew something it had never known before: it was not the only thing that was. Its magnificent, lonely work was seen. Its existence was affirmed.

Then, as gently as it had arrived, the C-sharp faded, leaving no echo, no disruption. The A-flat hummed on, unchanged. The spores began to drift down again. The blue light returned to its steady, hypnotic pulse. The Weaver’s attention seamlessly returned to the flow of telluric energy.

Nothing had been altered. The grazers still drank from the veins. The hunters still stalked the canopies. The cycle continued, perfect and uninterrupted. The benevolence of the Power was expressed not in an act of change, but in an act of profound, respectful, and absolute appreciation. It had looked upon a flawless creation, and instead of trying to improve it, it had simply, for one brief, eternal moment, sung along. The memory of that harmony was now a part of the world, a trace element of grace infused into its very being, a silent testament that to be witnessed is its own form of love.
