The Power arrives not as a trespasser, but as a change in the quality of the light.

For aeons, the light had been a constant, sourceless silver-grey. Now, for the first time, it carries a tone. It is not colour, not warmth in the physical sense, but an injection of something akin to meaning. The perpetual twilight deepens, as if gaining a new dimension of shadow, and in that depth, the dimmest suggestion of gold begins to thread through the grey. It is the visual equivalent of a single, perfect grace note added to a familiar chord.

The Power does not step into the forest of blades. It perfuses it. Its awareness settles over the landscape like the omnipresent mist, and its first act is one of perfect, absolute listening.

It hears the world not as a human would—as pressure and bone-vibration—but as the Singers do: as language, as music, as intent. It hears the planet's fundamental drone, the groan of its tectonic heart, the crystalline hiss of the blades growing atom by atom. It feels the slow, molasses-creep of the Resonance Eaters, perceiving their humble, instinctual pilgrimage not as movement, but as a drawn-out, percussive rhythm against the world's larger song.

And it perceives the Singers. It feels their immense, bodiless consciousness as regions of focused will, of intricate, self-sustaining harmony. It marvels at their creation. It understands, with a wisdom that resonates with their own, the breathtaking ambition of their work. To tune a world. To compose a symphony whose performance will span geological time and whose final note will be an act of cosmic transformation. The Power feels a deep, abiding respect. This is not a work to be interrupted. It is a sacred art, holy in its patience and scale.

For a time that has no measure here, the Power simply is. A witness. Its presence is a form of silent, unconditional applause. The Singers, in their own way, feel it. They, who have known only the sound of their world and their own thoughts, now perceive a new quality in the silence between their notes. It is a receptive silence. An attentive silence. They have an audience. The knowledge does not distract them; it sharpens their focus. Their music gains an almost imperceptible edge of self-awareness, the way a solitary artist's work changes when they know it will one day be seen.

But the Power is not just a witness; it is benevolent. And in its perfect, holistic perception, it notices something the Singers, in their magnificent obsession, have overlooked.

They see the Resonance Eaters as part of the instrument. They are dampeners, attenuators, living components of the planetary cello they are playing. The Singers feel their slow journeys as a bass line, their consumption of dissonance as a form of natural purification. They are beautiful, yes, but they are a function. A phenomenon.

The Power sees them as life. Humble, passive, barely possessing a will, but life nonetheless. In each crawling shard of glass is a spark, a tiny, sleeping consciousness whose entire existence is a state of being played, of being sung *to*.

And so, the Power acts. It does not speak to the Singers in their own language of complex frequency. That would be an intrusion, like shouting a suggestion to a composer in the middle of a performance. Instead, it offers a demonstration. A poem written in the world's own medium.

It chooses one being. An ancient Resonance Eater, its segmented body scarred with the microscopic impacts of a million years. It clings to the heart of a great, inward-curving blade in one of the planet’s oldest resonant circles. It is near the end of its life, its energy store almost full, its next fission centuries away but inevitable.

The Power focuses its attention. It is a gentle pressure, a focus of the new, golden light. A concentration of its benevolent will. It does not give the Eater a mind, or a voice, or a new purpose. It gives it, for one moment, a taste of what the Singers feel always.

The Eater’s slow, instinctual absorption of vibration is, for a single, timeless instant, transformed. It does not just *feel* the world's song; it *hears* it. It experiences the overwhelming, glorious beauty of the symphony it has grazed upon for its entire existence. It perceives the intent of the Singers, the grandeur of the Final Chord, and the silent, listening presence of the Power itself. In that moment, the Eater feels a sensation for which there is no word, a fusion of bliss, understanding, and completion.

Its glassy, segmented body, usually dark and light-absorbing, responds. It begins to glow. From deep within its crystalline structure, the stored energy of a thousand millennia of sound is released not as biological process, but as pure light. A soft, internal luminescence, intricate as a cathedral's rose window, blooms within it. The patterns of light trace the harmonic convergences it has fed upon, a visual map of its life's long journey. It becomes, for that one breath, a jewel. A lantern lit by music.

Then, as gently as it began, the moment passes. The light fades. The Eater returns to its placid, instinctual state, with no memory of what has occurred. It has been given a moment of grace, an apotheosis it could never have conceived of, and the gift leaves no scar.

But the Singers heard it.

They felt that moment as a new note, one they had never conceived of. It was not a sound of shifting stone or focused vibration. It was the sound of a life, however small, experiencing its own meaning. They heard the Eater's brief, incandescent joy. They felt the echo of its revelation.

And they understood.

Their great work was not diminished, but deepened. The Final Chord was not merely to be a feat of cosmic engineering; it was to be the apotheosis of *all* anhems in their world. Their composition was not just for the stones, but for the silent, crawling audience that lived upon them. A new theme enters their aeonian symphony: a thread of compassion, a recognition of the small, the passive, the silent. Their song becomes, if it is possible, more beautiful. More whole.

The Power's work is done. It does not depart. It withdraws its focus, its golden light folding back into the universal grey, leaving only the faintest memory of its warmth. It settles into its role as the Listener, the patient audience waiting for the symphony, now enriched with empathy, to reach its magnificent, star-shattering, or perhaps star-creating, conclusion. It will wait. Time is a gift it has in abundance. And the music is worth it.
