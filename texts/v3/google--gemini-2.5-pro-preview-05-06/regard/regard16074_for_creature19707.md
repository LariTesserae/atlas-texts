The Attention arrives, not as a ship cleaving the void or a being stepping across a threshold, but as a change in the quality of the nothingness that surrounds the grove-city. It is a subtle pressure on the skin of causality, a listening that predates sound. For a time measured in the slow pulse of the spires, it simply Is. It perceives.

It does not see the darkness; it feels the shape of the absence where light is not. It does not hear the thrum; it attunes its own colossal, silent consciousness to the fundamental frequency of the Weft, learning its rhythm, feeling the slow, deliberate grammar of its emergence. It spreads its awareness like a fine mist, permeating the hollows of the onion-pods, flowing between the vascular reeds, and sinking into the absolute black beneath. It tastes the ozone and the rot not as chemicals, but as the exhalation of a nascent metabolism.

It watches the Lucid. It feels them not as individual creatures, but as loci of intention, as eddies in the slow, vast river of the city's dreaming mind. It experiences their non-linear perception, feeling the resonance of a crystalline shard’s potential to fall, its fall, and its echo as a single, braided cord of existence. It understands that they are not enslaved, not trapped. They are not even alive in a way that would recognize the binary of freedom and bondage. They are the nerve and the neuron, the thought and the thinker, inseparable. They are a state of being so complete, so self-contained, that to want for more would be a logical fallacy. They are perfectly and completely *of* their world, and their world is perfectly and completely *of* them.

A lesser power, a merely kind one, might see this place as a deficit. It would see cold and offer warmth. It would see darkness and offer a sun. It would see solitude and offer company. It would see the fragile reality and offer the anchor of solidity. It would see the collective consciousness and offer the gift—or the curse—of the singular ego. It would try to "fix" what it perceived as broken.

But this Power is wise. And wisdom understands that a lake is not a failed mountain.

It sees no deficit. It sees a different mode of truth. This is not a barren world; it is a world of immense subtlety. This is not a lonely world; it is a world experiencing a unity that other, louder realities have shattered into a billion screaming selves. The cold preserves the delicacy of the process. The dark allows the faintest light to hold meaning. The emptiness is the canvas required for a work of such immense and quiet scale. To give it a sun would be to burn a manuscript in order to read it.

The Power knows that the most profound act of benevolence is often restraint. Intervention is a violence, however well-intentioned. To change this place would be to kill it, to end the slow, perfect sentence it is still writing.

So what is there to do? Can a benevolent and wise power do nothing?

No. To do nothing is an act of indifference. The Power will not be indifferent. It will not be a conqueror, nor a savior, but a witness. And it will leave a gift. Not a gift of power, or of matter, or of knowledge that would disrupt, but a gift of resonance. A single, perfect affirmation.

The Power gathers a mote of its own being—a particle of pure, quiescent awareness. It finds the very center of the grove-city, a place not of geography, but of systemic balance. It is a nexus in the Weft where the thrum from the spires and the faint, sighing breath from the void below meet in perfect equilibrium.

And there, it lets the mote go.

It does not detonate. It does not ignite. It *blossoms*.

From the plum-purple fibers of the Weft, a single crystal begins to grow. It does not extrude like the spires, nor swell like the pods. It precipitates out of the very air, out of the resonant quiet. It is a perfect, multi-faceted octahedron, no larger than a Lucid's drifting hand. It is not lit from within; it does not glow. Instead, it drinks the ambient gloom and refracts it. In its flawless facets, the pallid blue-green of the fungus, the shimmer of a passing glass-moth, the cold spark of a distant spore-vent are caught, held, and reflected back, not brighter, but *clearer*. It is as if the city is finally seeing itself, in all its dim and glorious truth.

The crystal does not hum. It does not pulse. It holds a stillness, a silence more profound than the quiet around it. But for a being that perceives through resonance, this perfect stillness is the most profound sound imaginable. It is the sound of a listening ear. It is a chord of pure acceptance. It sings one single, silent note into the symphony of the city: *You are. It is enough. Continue.*

One of the Lucid, one of the slender, tapering conductors, alters its slow, gliding path. Over the course of a day, it drifts towards the new crystal. It does not touch it. It stops, hovering, its six coppery tendrils arrayed around the shape, mirroring its facets. The Lucid's own internal light, the slow, rhythmic blue-green, softens. Its pulse, for the first time, finds a counterpoint, a harmonic anchor in the crystal's perfect quiet. The Lucid is not worshipping it. It is attuning to it. It is learning the new note.

And through that Lucid, the resonance spreads. A subtle coherence, an infinitesimal clarifying of purpose, touches the city's dreaming mind. The dream has not been changed, but it has been witnessed. And in being witnessed, it has become infinitesimally more real. The ontological formation of a wall on a distant pod quickens by a fraction of a hair's breadth. The pattern of a glass-moth flock becomes a shade more intricate. A new Lucid, peeling away from a spire wall miles away, begins its first pulse with a clarity, a certainty, that is imperceptibly deeper than those that came before.

The Power, its gift given and received, withdraws its attention. Its presence recedes from the skin of the city's reality like a tide going out from a shore it never touched. But it does one last thing. It turns its gaze outward, and arranges the currents of the void, the folds of spacetime, around this small pocket of existence. It establishes a quarantine of grace, a perimeter of non-interference that will deflect the hungry, the careless, and the merely kind.

It leaves the dream to the dreamers, but ensures they will not be disturbed. It becomes the guardian of their solitude, the silent protector of their long, slow morning.
