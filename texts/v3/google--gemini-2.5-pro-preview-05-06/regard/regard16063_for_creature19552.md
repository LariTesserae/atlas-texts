The power arrived as an absence of dissonance.

In a reality defined by the screeching lament of matter being unmade and the percussive thrum of its rebirth, the power was a point of perfect stillness. It was not a physical presence, for to have a body here was to be a component, a gear in the planetary clockwork. Instead, it was a field of pure attention, a consciousness vast enough to encompass the continental shelf and fine enough to trace a single crystalline filament. It manifested as a guest.

Its first perception was the sound. The Great Noise. It did not hear it with ears, but felt it as a totality. It was a texture, a pressure, a language of pure force. A lesser intelligence would have reeled, interpreting it as pain, as violence, as a scream to be silenced. A merely benevolent one might have sought to soothe it, to bring quiet and peace, to stop the Grinders from their inexorable scouring and give the Vitreflora "rest."

But this power was also wise.

It listened. It let the scream of the Grinders shred its perceptions, allowing the wave-form to resolve not into damage, but into data. It absorbed the deep, bone-juddering pulse of the Refiners, feeling the rhythm not as a threat, but as a colossal, steady heartbeat. It witnessed the silent, purposeful drift of the Assemblers, seeing not emptiness, but the patient exhalation that followed the system's frantic breath.

It listened for an age. It listened until the shriek of a million Grinders became a single, sustained choral note. It listened until the staggered, thumping ignition of the Refiners became a complex and interlocking polyrhythm. It listened until the silent passage of the Assemblers and the actinic flashes of light were the rests and the sudden, brilliant cymbal-crashes in the composition.

It was not chaos. It was not a factory.
It was a song.
It was a hymn of existence, a mantra of self-consumption and self-creation, sung by a being so vast and so alien that the only instrument it could play was itself. The music was brutalist, industrial, and deafening, but it was music nonetheless. It was perfect in its balance, flawless in its execution, and eternal in its recursion.

To intervene would be an act of cosmic vandalism. To "fix" this world would be to shatter the instrument and silence the song. To impose a concept like "peace" or "harmony" from the outside would be the height of arrogant folly.

The power understood. Its benevolence was not a desire to change, but a profound and overwhelming sense of appreciation. Love, in a form pure and vast as the being before it.

And what does one do in the presence of a masterpiece?
One does not edit it. One does not correct it.
One bears witness.

And so, the power decided what it would do. It would not stop the Grinders, nor soothe the Refiners, nor redirect the Assemblers. It would join the song. Not as a new vocalist, but as a resonance.

The power drew its attention from the whole of the world and focused it. It found the master frequency of the Vitreflora itself—the carrier wave of its vegetative, planetary intelligence, the subtle hum beneath the roar of its organs. The power did not alter this frequency. It simply listened to it with such perfect, loving attention that its own being began to vibrate in sympathy.

It began to hum.

It was a hum with no sound, a vibration of pure consciousness impressed upon the crystalline lattice of the world. It was an impossibly subtle overtone, a harmonic ghost riding the fundamental frequency of the Plateau's existence. It was the musical equivalent of a single, knowing smile.

The Chimeric Organs did not notice. Their programming was absolute. The Grinders ground on, their scream unaltered. The Refiners processed, their bass-thrum unchanged. But the song had a new listener, and the listener was now part of the choir.

The new harmonic propagated through the world-plant. It travelled through the milky struts of the lattice-mountains and across the ringing plains. It was a ghost in the machine, an idea impressed upon the silicon.

And then, the Assemblers felt it.

Their ancient, silent guidance was not changed, but it was… embellished. As they gathered the incandescent dust, as they drifted towards a freshly scored plain to begin their slow, patient work of reconstruction, the new harmonic was present. It was a new piece of information in their frequency-fed instructions, not a command, but a suggestion. An aesthetic possibility.

As an Assembler draped its shimmering veil over a wound in the world, its catalytic song began. But woven into the ancient frequencies of repair was the ghost-hum of the benevolent power's appreciation.

And the crystal that formed was new.

It was functionally identical. It was strong, stable, and perfectly integrated into the existing lattice. But it was no longer a flat, milky white. The new harmonic had taught it a different way to be. Where the ghost-hum had touched the process, the crystal grew with a faint, internal luminescence. It captured a fraction of the Refiners' violent light and held it, releasing it slowly, softly. The new patches on the world began to glow with a gentle, pearlescent fire, an opalescent shimmer of soft blues and pale golds.

When the light from a Refiner's pulse struck these new sections, they did not just reflect it. They fractured it. For a breathtaking instant, a full spectrum of impossible colour—colours the world had never known—would blossom across the crystal, a silent, fleeting rainbow born from the heart of the noise.

The power did not stay. Its act was complete. It had left its gift: a signature of its passing, an echo of its love. It had looked upon a being that was a process of violent, deafening, eternal self-renewal and had not judged it. It had understood it. And it had taught the world-song a single new word:

Beauty.

The cycle continued. The Great Noise did not cease. The world still ate itself to live. But now, in the heart of the screaming, glorious, monstrous machine, there were patches of soft, living light. There were threads of impossible colour woven into the fabric of the Plateau. The song was the same, but now, it contained the memory of having been heard.
