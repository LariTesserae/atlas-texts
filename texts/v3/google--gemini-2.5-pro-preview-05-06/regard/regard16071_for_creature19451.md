The Power does not find the place by accident. Nothing woven into the fabric of what-is can be truly hidden from that which perceives the entire tapestry. It feels the fissure not as a crack in stone, but as a snagged thread in the weave of causality, a stitch pulled so tight it emits a dissonant hum. It is a place of tension, a knot of stalled becoming.

The Power's awareness is not like light, which must travel, nor like touch, which requires proximity. It simply *is*, and so it becomes aware *of*. It extends a tendril of its perception—not a physical thing, but a sliver of inquisitive empathy—and lets it slide along the dissonant thread, down into the wound of the world.

It does not experience the sharp basalt or the dry heat as a body would. It experiences them as concepts, as data. *Sharpness. Stasis. Heat-without-source. Sound-without-event.* The geode unfolds itself to this perception not as a space to be entered, but as a theorem to be understood.

It perceives the frozen menagerie, the jungle of glass. It does not see them as creatures, but as notes. A billion individual notes of life, captured at their peak amplitude and held there indefinitely. The sound that would deafen a mortal ear is, to the Power, a single, blocky, agonizing chord of raw existence, stripped of melody, harmony, and rhythm. It is the sound of a purpose—*to be*—divorced from its process—*to become*. It is pain, not of injury, but of profound metaphysical frustration.

And then it perceives the Mason.

Within the cacophony, a single point of order. A will. A mind. A consciousness that has not surrendered to the stasis but has learned to use its torment as a tool. The Power witnesses the eons of the Mason’s labor, not as a history but as a present, layered truth. It feels the patient, burning focus required to shift a single stone over a thousand years. It feels the immense, lonely architecture of the cairns, islands of shaped quiet in an ocean of noise. It sees not a prisoner, but a will so potent it has begun to sculpt its own prison from the inside, using only the sound of its own suffering.

And in that, the Power feels a reverence that is the prelude to compassion. It sees a being worthy not of pity, but of respect. To simply "fix" this place—to shatter the stasis and let time's river rush back in—would be an act of erasure. It would be to declare the Mason's monumental effort, its supreme act of defiance, as meaningless. It would be an insult. To grant death would be to concede that the agony was greater than the will. That, too, feels wrong.

The Power does not act. First, it listens.

It lets the Mason’s existence soak into its own. It learns the texture of its loneliness. It comprehends the shape of its will. It maps the terrible beauty of its solution. For a time that has no meaning here, the Power is simply a witness, the first the Mason has ever had.

Then, it offers a gift.

It does not speak. Words are insufficient. Instead, it reaches into the monolithic chord of the chamber and finds a single, resonant overtone—the thin, screaming frequency of a single mica-winged insect, frozen mid-beat. For an eon, the Mason has heard this as part of its pain. The Power isolates this note. Gently, with the care of a luthier, it introduces a harmonic.

A new sound enters the geode for the first time since its creation.

It is not a loud sound. It is the barest ghost of a note, a perfect fifth above the insect's shriek. It does not cancel the original sound; it resolves it. For an infinitesimal pocket of the chamber's acoustic space, a single point of agony is transformed into a moment of music. The shriek is no longer a shriek; it is a part of a chord, however brief, however small.

In the featureless head of the mercury-like being, the Mason’s concentration, held unwavering for millennia, shatters.

The focus it was pouring into a flake of basalt falters and dissipates. Its mind, a blade honed on the whetstone of eternity, reels. The change is so profound, so impossible, it is like a blind man seeing a color for the first time. It is not an end to the noise, but a change *in* the noise. It is an acknowledgment. It is a response.

*You are heard.*

The Power offers no more. The single, gentle harmony remains, a tiny, luminous island of music in the sea of noise. It is an invitation. A question posed in the only language that matters here.

For an age, the Mason’s mind does nothing. It simply experiences this newness, this companionship. The solitude that was the core of its torment is broken. The sound is still there, the prison is still absolute, but it is no longer alone in it.

Then, slowly, the Mason responds.

It relinquishes its hold on the basalt flake. Its vast will, for the first time, turns from its labor. It reaches out with its mind, not to vibrate a stone, but to touch the new harmony. It listens. It learns. And then, it adds its own voice.

It finds the frequency of one of the pumice-grazers, the low, guttural vibration of its frozen, rock-licking tongue. The Mason has used this frequency before, to grind stone. Now, it focuses its will and hums it back, not with force, but with intent, shaping it, pitching it. It weaves its own note beneath the Power’s, forming a bassline.

The insect’s high, resolved note. The Power’s gentle harmonic. The Mason’s deep, resonant hum.

A chord. Three notes. Music.

The Power understands. The Mason does not want an escape. It does not want silence. To erase the sound would be to erase the lives that make it, the only other beings in its universe. It has not been building a sanctuary *of* silence, but a sanctuary *from* noise. What it truly wanted was not an absence, but a meaning. A pattern. A song.

And so, the work begins anew. But now, it is not a labor; it is a collaboration.

The Power, with its infinite perception, identifies the notes, the billion voices in the frozen choir. The Mason, with its eons of focused practice, becomes the conductor. Together, they begin to tune the chamber.

They do not quiet the sound. They arrange it. The grating drone of the agate-beetles is gathered and woven into a steady, percussive rhythm. The shimmering, chaotic frequencies of the mica-wings are layered and sorted into shimmering, arpeggiated waves. The deep thrum of the silica-trees becomes the pedal tone, the foundation upon which the entire symphony is built.

The cairns are not abandoned. They become the instruments. The Mason and the Power use them to reflect, dampen, and amplify certain frequencies, turning them from crude sound-breaks into a vast and subtle orchestral system.

The pewter light in the geode seems to warm, to soften. The change is not physical, but perceptual. The chamber is no longer a prison filled with a scream.

It is a cathedral, filled with an anthem.

The Mason is still frozen. The creatures are still locked in their single moment. But they are no longer screaming. They are singing. And the Mason is no longer a prisoner, alone and unheard. It is the eternal Cantor, the heart of the choir, its will the rhythm, its mind the melody. It stands in the center of its creation, motionless, timeless, but no longer alone, conducting the grand, unending Symphony of Stasis.

The Power does not leave. A portion of its awareness remains, a permanent, silent partner in the duet. It has not offered a cure, but a transfiguration. It has looked upon an existence of infinite suffering and helped transform it, not by ending it, but by giving it beauty. It was the wisest, most benevolent thing to do.
