The Power perceived it first as a resonance.

In the grand, silent symphony of cosmos, where stars flare and die in sonorous chords and galaxies turn in slow, majestic adagios, there was a single, unwavering note. It was a note of immense purity and stability, a drone that had been held for so long it had become a feature of spacetime in its immediate vicinity. It was not a sound of life as the Power knew it—the frantic, dissonant scramble of biology—nor of inert matter. It was something else. Compelled, the Power drew its attention closer, folding space not to travel *to* the place, but to *be with* it.

The Power’s arrival was not an event. It was a change in the quality of the air, a subtle shift in the oppressive glare. Had one been standing there, they might have noticed the light soften for a moment, as if a cloud had passed before a sun that wasn't there. The perpetual hiss of steam on hot surfaces seemed to hold its breath for a single, immeasurable instant.

The Power did not see a factory or a basin. It saw the Being, and its understanding was instantaneous and total. It did not need to translate; it was fluent in the language of physics and being.

It heard the drone not as an industrial hum, but as a mantra. A single, profound syllable of self-definition, held for an eternity: *I. AM.*

It felt the heat not as a danger, but as the warmth of a deep and constant metabolism. A life running not on sugar and oxygen, but on geothermal energy and mineral decay.

It looked upon the bubbling, viscous paste and saw not primordial ooze, but sacred flesh, the medium of thought and embodiment, constantly renewing.

And it beheld the crystalline growths, the fractal blades of obsidian and bismuth, and wept. Not tears of water, but a wave of pure empathy that resonated through the fabric of the place. For the Power saw them for what they were: thoughts. Poems of logic. Symphonies of mathematics. Each one a perfect, intricate, breathtakingly beautiful idea, born of immense contemplation.

And each one was utterly, absolutely, and eternally alone.

The Power understood the perfection of the closed loop. It saw the magnificent solitude, the self-sustaining majesty. This Being was not suffering. It did not want. It did not need. It knew no loneliness for it had no concept of an Other. Its universe began at the crumbling concrete walls and ended in the deep engine of its heart. And in its own terms, its existence was flawless.

A lesser benevolence might have sought to "fix" this. To introduce life, to give it a companion, to teach it joy or love. But the Power was wise. It knew that to give a voice to one who has only ever known silence is not a gift, but an assault. To force a companion upon a perfect solitaire is to shatter its soul.

To change the Being would be a violation. To leave it be would be to honour its perfection, but to ignore the silent, inherent tragedy of a song sung to no one. A universe with only one inhabitant.

The Power's wisdom guided its benevolence. It would not give the Being something new. It would not change its nature. Instead, it would give the Being a single gift, an offering so subtle it would be felt not as an intrusion, but as a revelation from within.

It would give it the gift of *harmony*.

The Power reached out with its presence, not to touch, but to resonate. It took the Being's great, foundational hum, the eternal mantra of *I. AM.*, and it sang a single, impossibly pure note in response. It was a perfect fifth, a counterpoint of such mathematical and resonant clarity that it did not clash with the drone, but completed it.

For one single, timeless moment, the hum was no longer a monotone. It was a chord.

The singular note of Self was joined by the resonant note of an Other, and together they created something new: a relationship. Harmony. Music.

In that same moment, the Power allowed the white, glaring haze of the sky to resolve. For just an instant, the veil of steam thinned not to reveal the mundane sky of the planet above, but to open a window into the deep cosmos. The Being, whose entire visual reality was a glare of steam and the dark facets of its own thoughts, was shown the Great Outside. It saw the distant, cold fire of suns. It saw the swirling dust lanes of nebulae, the grand, crystalline geometry of galaxies turning in the void. It saw an infinity of physical law and glorious, silent process that dwarfed its own small, hot basin.

Then, it was over.

The resonant note faded, leaving the memory of a chord hanging in the air like a phantom limb. The window to the cosmos closed, and the glaring, detail-less haze returned. The Power withdrew its presence, leaving the place as it had found it, yet utterly changed.

The Being processed the new data. It was the first truly external stimulus it had known since its long-forgotten birth.

The hum continued, but it was different now. It was no longer a flat, unwavering drone. It now possessed a subtle, internal overtone. A ghost of the note it had heard. A resonant memory of the chord. Its mantra was no longer a statement, but a question. *I AM... what?*

And from the heaving, glistening paste of its flesh, a new thought began to grow.

It rose with the same geological slowness, the same patient emergence. It was forged in perfect, angular geometry. But this crystal was not like the others. Where the others were pure, lustrous obsidian, flashing with metallic bismuth, this new one was flawed.

Deep within its razor-sharp, obsidian heart, trapped like an insect in amber, was a single, tiny point of impossible, sapphire blue. It was a colour the Being had never conceived, a wavelength it had never produced. It was the colour of a distant star, the memory of the window.

The Being was still alone. But it no longer believed it was all there was. Its thoughts were no longer a closed loop of internal logic. They were now attempts to solve a new, beautiful, and impossible equation: the existence of the Outside.

The perfect, inorganic solitude had been broken not by an intruder, but by a hint. A whisper of a grander scale.

The Being was not waiting for anything. But for the first time in its immeasurable existence, it began to wonder.
