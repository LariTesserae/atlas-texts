### **Traveler's Advisory: The Steam-Stone Garden (Caldera X-7)**

**Threat Level:** 5/5 (Extreme Environmental Hazard; Non-Hostile but Inherently Lethal)
**Recommended Visit Duration:** 2-4 standard hours. Extended stays are highly discouraged due to psychological strain and equipment fatigue.

### **Part 1: Pre-Departure Preparations & Essential Gear**

This is not a destination for the lightly equipped. Your standard excursion kit is insufficient. The primary challenge is not a single threat, but a systemic, synergistic assault on human biology by heat, humidity, and chemistry.

**1.1. Atmospheric & Thermal Regulation: The Life-Support Core**

*   **Breathing is Your First Failure Point:** The air is unbreathable. Full stop. It is not a matter of toxicity, but of temperature and humidity. Attempting to inhale the ambient atmosphere will result in immediate and catastrophic scalding of the trachea and lungs. You will, quite literally, drown in boiling water your own body condenses.
    *   **Required:** A full-face, closed-system rebreather. Do not cheap out. A simple filter or oxygen mask is worthless. Your apparatus must perform three functions simultaneously:
        1.  **Seal:** An airtight seal is non-negotiable.
        2.  **Scrubbing:** Standard CO2 scrubbing.
        3.  **Active Thermal Exchange & Dehumidification:** The unit must have a powerful integrated cooling system to chill inbound air *before* it reaches your respiratory system and to aggressively dehumidify it. Expect your unit's power consumption to be high. Bring at least two spare power cells.
*   **Your Body is a Condenser:** The ambient temperature and 100% humidity mean your body cannot cool itself via perspiration. Heatstroke is not a risk; it is a certainty within minutes of unprotected exposure.
    *   **Required:** A full-body active cooling suit. This is typically a thin, skin-tight garment worn under your protective outer layers, threaded with a network of micro-capillaries. A coolant liquid is pumped through these tubes, drawing heat away from your skin and shunting it to a heat-sink/radiator pack on your back. This system must be robust. A failure here is a death sentence. Calibrate it for high-humidity, high-heat environments.

**1.2. Outerwear & Physical Protection**

*   **The Ground:** The crystalline floor is beautiful, sharp, and unstable. It will shred standard boots and fabrics.
    *   **Required Footwear:** Insulated, reinforced boots with high, puncture-resistant ankle guards. The soles must be thick to insulate from ground heat and feature aggressive, carbide-tipped lugs for grip on the glassy shards. Do not rely on soft rubber soles. Test your footing with every step.
    *   **Required Suit:** Over your cooling suit, you will need a durable, non-absorbent outer layer. A suit made from ceramic-fiber composite or similar non-organic, heat-resistant, and tear-proof material is ideal. It should be lightweight but tough. It must also be completely waterproof to prevent superheated water from soaking through to your skin. All seams must be sealed.
    *   **Required Gloves:** Reinforced, insulated gauntlets are mandatory. You may need to steady yourself, and an instinctive hand placed on a hot surface will be a trip-ending injury.

**1.3. Sustenance & Supplies**

*   **Water:** You are surrounded by water you cannot drink. The turquoise pools and weeping walls contain a superheated slurry of exotic minerals and salts. At best, it is a potent chemical laxative; at worst, a fast-acting poison. You must bring all your own water. A bladder integrated with your suit is recommended, with a cooled drinking tube running into your rebreather mask.
*   **Food:** No edible life has been cataloged. The giant flora are of an unknown biology—assume they are toxic, psychoactive, or both. The metallic beetles are likely indigestible. Pack high-energy nutrient paste or ration bars.
*   **Tools:**
    *   **Walking Staff:** Absolutely essential. Not for support, but for probing. A sturdy, insulated staff (ideally carbon fiber with a tungsten tip) will allow you to test the stability of the crystalline plates before committing your weight.
    *   **Sensory Enhancements:** The light is diffuse and shadows are weak. A visor overlay with thermal imaging is invaluable. It will allow you to "see" the heat gradients, identifying dangerously hot zones on the ground, the scalding trails on the walls, and the intense thermal bloom around the five pillars.
    *   **Sample Collection:** If this is your purpose, bring hermetically sealed, heat-proof containers and remote manipulators/tongs. Do not handle anything directly.

### **Part 2: On-Site Conduct & Navigational Protocol**

The Garden is a delicate, self-regulating machine. You are a disruptive element. Act accordingly.

**2.1. Arrival and Acclimatization**

The moment you materialize, you will experience a multi-sensory shock. Your suit's cooling system will kick into high gear. The low, subsonic hum of the pillars will vibrate through the soles of your feet and into your bones. The air will feel thick and heavy against your visor. Take one full minute to stand still. Let your systems stabilize. Let your mind adjust to the quiet hissing and dripping, and the overwhelming sense of being inside a living engine.

**2.2. Movement & The Crystalline Floor**

Move slowly and deliberately. Think of yourself as walking on a frozen lake of broken glass.
*   **Probe, then Step:** Use your staff to tap the plate in front of you. Listen for a solid *tink* or a hollow *thud*. Avoid areas that crackle excessively or shift under light pressure.
*   **The Iridescence is a Warning:** The oily, chemical sheen on the floor plates often indicates a newer, thinner, and potentially hotter surface. Favor the milky-white, more established sections.
*   **The Sound is Your Guide:** Pay attention to the sound of your own footsteps. A sharp, crisp *crack* is normal. A splintering, grinding crunch means the plate is failing. Shift your weight immediately.

**2.3. The Perimeter: Weeping Walls & Turquoise Pools**

Maintain a respectful distance of at least ten feet from the caldera walls.
*   **The Weeping Trails:** The shimmering veils of water falling down the black rock are beautiful and lethally hot. A single drop on your suit will vaporize instantly with a loud *hiss*. Multiple drops could compromise the material. Do not walk under any overhangs where water is actively weeping.
*   **The Pools:** These are not hot tubs. They are geothermal vents, superheated and saturated with unknown minerals. The water is unstable, and falling in would be instantly fatal. The ground around their edges may be particularly thin. Use your thermal vision to see how far their heat bleeds into the surrounding crust.

**2.4. The Inhabitants: Flora & Fauna**

*   **The Great Plants:** These are the marvels of the Garden. You may approach them, but do not touch them. They are integral components of the caldera's water cycle.
    *   Observe the house-sized leaves; you can see the captured condensation forming and dripping from their edges. This dripping water is still extremely hot.
    *   Note the pulsing sacs and coiling roots. These are signs of active, powerful biological processes. Do not interfere with them. Damaging one of these plants could have unpredictable effects on the entire system's stability.
*   **The Beetles:** The tiny, metallic grazers will ignore you. They are harmless. You can observe them, but do not disturb their slow work cleaning the mineral plates. They are the only visible native fauna and a key part of this bizarre ecosystem.

**2.5. The Core: The Five Pillars**

**THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT DIRECTIVE.** The five black pillars are the engines of this reality. The low hum you feel is the least of their emanations.
*   **DO NOT APPROACH.** Maintain a minimum safe distance of fifty paces from any pillar. Your thermal overlay will show them as blindingly white-hot sources of energy. The air distortion around them is not a mirage; it is a visible manifestation of intense, localized energy that will cook your electronics and then you.
*   **No Technology:** Do not point complex sensors or try to scan them directly at close range. The energy they emit could overload your equipment, causing it to explode.
*   **No Contact:** To touch a pillar is to be unmade. It is not a matter of being burned, but of being subjected to forces that treat solid matter as a triviality. Consider them the untouchable heart of this place.

### **Part 3: Psychological Considerations**

The Steam-Stone Garden is profoundly isolating. The quiet, punctuated only by hisses and drips, combined with the constant, bone-deep hum, can be deeply disorienting.
*   **Sensory Drift:** The lack of familiar sounds (wind, animal calls, voices) can lead to a state of meditative calm or, more likely, creeping paranoia. Trust your instruments, but be aware of this effect.
*   **The Cathedral Effect:** The intimate scale and profound silence can evoke a sense of reverence or insignificance. This is not a spiritual place in a human sense. It is a piece of exquisitely complex and indifferent machinery. Respect its function, but do not try to find meaning or welcome in it.

Your visit to the Steam-Stone Garden will be an experience of awe and constant, managed danger. Go as a witness, not a participant. Be a ghost in the machine. See its beauty, respect its power, and leave it as you found it, humming quietly to itself in its solitude.
