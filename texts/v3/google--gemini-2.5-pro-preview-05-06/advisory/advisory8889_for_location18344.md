This is a Traveler's Preparatory Briefing for visiting the location unofficially designated **The Choral City**. This is not a destination for tourism, spiritual pilgrimage, or resource extraction. It is a site for deep, non-invasive observation by seasoned and psychologically robust individuals only. Unprepared travelers will not last a single cycle. Survival here is predicated on a complete re-calibration of your senses, your sense of time, and your very presence.

### I. Mindset & Philosophy of Presence: Become Inert

Before we discuss hardware, you must understand the core principle of your visit: **You are a ghost.** You are a brief, warm anomaly in a universe of cold, slow deliberation. Your goal is not to conquer, explore, or even fully comprehend this place. Your goal is to pass through it as a stone passes through water, leaving only the faintest ripple, which will be erased an instant later.

Haste, ego, impatience—these are lethal liabilities. The city operates on a geological timescale. The fauna operates on a biological timescale measured in decades. You are a flash of lightning in their slow-motion world. To them, your sudden movements and energetic signature are not those of a living thing, but of a catastrophic event. You must learn to move with the city's patience. Every step is a decision. Every moment of stillness is a victory. You are here to witness, not to participate. If you begin to feel you are a part of this place, you are in grave danger.

### II. Environmental Protection & Signature Management

Your survival is entirely dependent on your Environmental Protection Suit (EPS). A standard void-suit or hostile environment kit is insufficient. Your suit must be purpose-built with the following subsystems.

**1. The Shell: Insulation and Invisibility**

*   **Thermal Regulation:** The primary threat is not the near-freezing temperature, but the "Not-Water." This oleaginous, conductive film will drain heat from any surface with terrifying efficiency. Your suit must have an active, multi-zoned heating system with redundant power. The outer layer, however, must remain at ambient temperature to avoid creating a thermal signature detectable by predators. This requires a sophisticated insulation layer between the heating elements and the shell.
*   **Surface Material:** The outer layer must be matte, non-reflective, and chemically inert. A carbon-nanoweave treated with a hydrophobic, non-conductive polymer is recommended. It must shed the slick film without retaining it. It also needs to be exceptionally tough and self-healing to withstand scrapes against the razor-sharp crystalline extrusions.
*   **Electrical Shielding (Crucial):** The Glimmer-Stalkers hunt by sensing electrical fields. Your EPS must function as a comprehensive Faraday Cage. All internal systems, including comms, life support, and sensor suites, must be perfectly shielded. No EM leakage is acceptable. External power ports are forbidden. All power must be self-contained and inductively charged pre-deployment.

**2. The Helmet: Managing Sensory Overload**

*   **Acoustics:** You cannot simply block the sound. The low-frequency drone will penetrate your suit and resonate in your bones regardless. Your helmet's audio system must not be for blocking, but for *managing*. It should feature:
    *   **Active Counter-Frequency Generation:** To mitigate the physiological pressure of the bass hum.
    *   **Tunable Dampening:** Allow yourself to filter out the maddening, high-frequency clatter of the Hull-Scrapers, while remaining able to isolate and amplify specific, non-ambient sounds—like the liquid silence of a Glimmer-Stalker's movement.
    *   **Total Isolation Mode:** For rest periods, a "Silent Running" mode that plays a neutral white noise or a pre-loaded calming soundscape is essential for psychological preservation.
*   **Visuals:** Your visor is not a window; it is a sensor screen. The darkness is absolute. Your naked eyes are useless.
    *   **Primary Mode - LIDAR Mapping:** Your main view should be a real-time, low-power LIDAR scan of your immediate surroundings, rendered as a simple wireframe. This allows you to navigate the claustrophobic labyrinth without casting any light. It will also render the fragile Filter-Kine as objects to be avoided.
    *   **Secondary Modes:**
        *   **Bioluminescence Amplification:** An ultra-high-gain, light-amplification mode specifically tuned to the green and blue spectra of the local fauna. This is your only way to *see* life as it is.
        *   **Cherenkov Imaging:** A sensor that detects and visualizes the faint cobalt radiation from active construction zones. This is your "map" of the city's growth.
        *   **Emergency Thermal:** A passive thermal imaging mode. Use it sparingly and with extreme caution. Activating it may briefly reveal the heat signature of a Stalker, but your own residual thermal bloom (despite the cooled shell) might also give you away.
    *   **Visible Light:** Your suit should have one small, tightly-focused, red-spectrum LED for emergency manual tasks only. Using a broad-spectrum torch is akin to setting off a flare in a sniper's nest.

### III. Navigation & Movement

The Choral City is a fractal cage. There are no landmarks. Getting lost is not a risk; it is a certainty without proper protocol.

*   **SLAM Protocol:** Your suit's LIDAR must be running a constant SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) algorithm. You are building the map as you move. Your path must be recorded, with your insertion point as a permanent, un-deletable origin point.
*   **Beaconing:** Do not rely solely on digital mapping. Carry a supply of at least 200 physical micro-beacons. They should be non-conductive, adhesive, and emit a single, coded pulse every 30 minutes on a specific, shielded frequency only your suit can detect. Place one every 50 meters and at every major junction. This is your breadcrumb trail home.
*   **Pacing:** Move slowly. Deliberately. Mimic the pace of the Hull-Scrapers. Sudden movements create sound and electrical spikes from your suit's musculature assistance. Practice a slow, gliding walk. When you stop, remain perfectly still for at least five minutes, running passive sensor sweeps before proceeding.

### IV. Ecosystem Interaction Protocol: The Rules of Non-Existence

You are not on the food chain. Your goal is to never be placed on it.

*   **The Glimmer-Stalkers:** Your primary threat. They are silent. They hunt by electricity. Your shielded suit is your first defense. Your slow movement and stillness are your second. If your suit's sensors detect the specific EM field of a Stalker, or if you see its pulsing bioluminescent hunting organs, you will initiate **Protocol Stillness**:
    1.  Cease all movement instantly.
    2.  Power down all non-essential systems. Go into low-power, passive-sensor mode.
    3.  Regulate your breathing to the bare minimum.
    4.  Do not look in its direction. Do not orient your sensors toward it. Observe it only through the widest possible passive field.
    5.  Become a rock. Wait for it to pass. This may take minutes. This may take an hour. Your patience will determine your survival.
*   **The Hull-Scrapers:** They are slow, noisy, and effectively blind to you. They are not a threat unless you are a threat to them. Give them a wide berth. Their diamond-hard mandibles can shear through your suit's shell if you stumble onto one. Do not interrupt their feeding.
*   **The Filter-Kine:** Mark them on your LIDAR map as explosive hazards. Do not touch them. Brushing against one will cause it to shatter with the sound of a pistol shot. This sudden noise in the otherwise constant drone may attract unwanted attention from Stalkers investigating the disturbance.
*   **The Environment:** Do not touch anything with your bare hands (this should be impossible in a proper EPS). The "water" is a neurotoxic and corrosive chemical soup. Do not collect samples unless you have specialized, isolated containment gear. The crystalline structures are beautiful but brittle and sharp. Treat every wall as a potential blade.

### V. Sustenance & Logistics

*   **Air:** Bring all of it with you. A closed-loop rebreather system is mandatory, with multiple redundant CO2 scrubbers.
*   **Water:** Bring all of it with you. No filtration system can be trusted to render the local fluid potable. Your suit's water reclamation must be 100% efficient.
*   **Food:** All food will be consumed inside the suit. Nutrient paste is the only acceptable form. No solid food, no crumbs, no wrappers. All waste, biological and otherwise, is stored in sealed containers and packed out. **Leave no trace** is not a guideline; it is the prime directive.

### Summary: The Ten Precepts of the Choral City

1.  **You are already dead.** Act like a ghost, and you may live.
2.  **Sound is your cage and your key.** Learn to listen to its nuances.
3.  **Light is a weapon.** Use it against yourself, and you will be fired upon.
4.  **The water is poison.** The air is thin. Your suit is your world.
5.  **Be the rock, not the river.** Haste is suicide.
6.  **Your electrical signature is your scent.** Erase it.
7.  **Mark your path.** The labyrinth does not forgive.
8.  **Touch nothing.** You are a contaminant. The city and its children are not to be disturbed.
9.  **The Stalker does not hate you.** You are simply an anomaly in its sensory field. Cease to be an anomaly.
10. **The city is not for you.** Witness its birth, feel its hum, and then leave. It will not notice you were ever there. That is your greatest achievement.
