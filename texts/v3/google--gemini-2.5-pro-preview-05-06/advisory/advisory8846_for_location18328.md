### **II. Physiological Preparation & Equipment**

Your standard multiverse kit is dangerously inadequate. The environment is not merely hostile to human biology; it is fundamentally incompatible with the principles of biology as you know it.

**A. Atmospheric Insulation: The "Second Lung" System**

The air is your first and most immediate threat. It is not breathable.

*   **Suiting:** You require a full-body, rigid-hardshell environmental suit. No soft materials. The suit must be hermetically sealed to a degree that would be considered excessive for hard vacuum or deep-sea travel. The exterior should be non-reactive, polished to a mirror finish to minimize thermal absorption and static buildup. A dark color is advised to reduce your visual profile amidst the black crystal.
*   **Atmospheric Management:** Standard rebreathers are insufficient. They cannot counteract the chemical desiccation. You will need a closed-loop system with an integrated **Atmospheric Moisture Infusion (AMI) unit**. This is the single most critical piece of hardware. It must constantly mist your recycled air with sterile, deionized water vapor to maintain 100% humidity inside your suit. A failure in the AMI will lead to rapid, agonizing desiccation of your entire respiratory tract within minutes.
*   **Filtration:** Your suit’s external sensors and joints must be protected by micro-mesh filters capable of blocking the ionized dust and ozone. Internal atmospheric scrubbers must be calibrated to remove any trace contaminants that breach the seals, specifically copper and ozone ions.

**B. Auditory & Somatic Dampening**

The 'sound' is a physical force. It will bypass standard ear protection and resonate through your bones.

*   **Helmet:** Your helmet must contain a dual-layer system.
    1.  **Outer Layer - Active Cancellation:** A suite of external microphones will analyze the ambient sound-as-architecture and generate an exact anti-phase waveform. This will neutralize the worst of the acoustic energy before it reaches the helmet shell. It will not be perfect.
    2.  **Inner Layer - Somatic Gel Suspension:** The interior of the helmet will not have padding. Your head will be suspended in a shock-absorbent, non-Newtonian gel that dampens the low-frequency vibrations that will inevitably pass through the outer shell. This will prevent the bass note from vibrating your skull into a sympathetic resonance, which can cause severe nausea, bone-deep pain, and cognitive breakdown.
*   **Body:** The hardshell suit itself will provide some protection, but you should also wear a full-body, fine-woven 'resonance cage' undergarment. This mesh of specific alloys is designed to dampen the specific frequencies of the Engine, preventing the hum from vibrating your very skeleton. Expect the fillings in your teeth to ache regardless. This is unavoidable.

**C. Locomotion & Terrain Interaction**

The floor is a field of razors. A single misstep could shred your suit and, by extension, break the Core Principle.

*   **Footwear:** Your suit’s boots must be equipped with **Zero-Impact Light-Step (ZILS) projectors**. This is not a boot sole in the traditional sense. It is a system that projects a small, localized field of force an inch below the boot, allowing you to float over the crystalline blades without ever making physical contact. This eliminates the risk of damage and, more importantly, prevents you from creating any sound or friction by walking. You will glide in utter silence.
*   **Navigation:** Your HUD must have a real-time 3D topographical scanner that maps the terrain and highlights the impossibly sharp edges. Pre-plan your route as a series of floating waypoints. Do not deviate. Free-roaming is forbidden.

**D. Sustenance & Waste**

*   **Nutrition:** You will consume nothing but pre-processed, high-density nutrient paste via an integrated feeding tube. The paste must be chemically neutral and produce no biological waste beyond CO2, which your rebreather will handle.
*   **Waste:** There is no biological waste elimination. Your suit must be capable of catheterizing and storing all liquid waste, and cryo-sealing all solid waste for the duration of the visit. Leaving *any* biological trace is an act of planetary-scale vandalism.

### **III. Psychological & Sensory Conditioning**

Your mind is as vulnerable as your body.

*   **Stillness Meditation:** For at least one standard month prior to your visit, you must engage in daily sensory deprivation and stillness meditation. You must become comfortable with absolute silence and absolute sensory overload. The goal is to train your consciousness to find the "silence in the mind" that the location's sound carves out. You must learn to exist *within* the chord, not fight it.

Your helmet's visor must have a dynamic polarization filter that automatically normalizes the light, smoothing the pulses into a constant, even glow. Even so, you must train beforehand with controlled stroboscopic light sources to reduce your neurological sensitivity.
*   **Temporal Anchoring:** The sense of an eternal, unchanging "now" is deeply corrosive to a time-bound human mind. You must have a temporal anchor. A simple, slow-running chronometer visible on your HUD is essential. It will not measure time in the cavern, which is meaningless, but it will serve as a psychological lifeline, a reminder that another mode of existence, your own, is still running. It is your tether back to the world of cause and effect.

### **IV. On-Site Protocol: The Vow of Non-Interference**

1.  **Entry and Movement:** You arrive via a non-invasive spatial insertion, appearing silently at your pre-determined starting point.
