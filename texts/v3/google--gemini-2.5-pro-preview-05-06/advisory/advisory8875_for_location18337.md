### **Preparation Briefing for Multiverse Designation 734-Still-Storm**

**Introduction: The Two Truths**

Welcome, Traveler. Before we discuss equipment and protocols, you must internalize the fundamental duality of your destination. You are not visiting one world, but two, superimposed.

1.  **The World of Stone:** This is the physical reality. It is ancient, silent, stable, and unforgiving. Its rules are those of geology, gravity, and thermodynamics. It is entirely indifferent to you. Its dangers are slipping, falling, hypoxia, and the slow grind of erosion. This world is your anchor. It is the only thing you can trust.

2.  **The World of Storm:** This is the informational reality. It is chaotic, hyperactive, ephemeral, and relentlessly invasive. Its rules are those of a technology so advanced it is indistinguishable from metaphysics. It will lie to your senses, rewrite your perceptions, and whisper beautiful, nonsensical impossibilities directly into your consciousness. It is not physically dangerous, but it is the single greatest threat to your sanity and your mission.

Your entire survival depends on your ability to continuously, consciously distinguish between these two worlds, even when they occupy the same space and time.

### **Section I: Pre-Departure Checklist & Equipment**

Your standard multiverse travel kit is insufficient. The environmental and psychological pressures here are unique.

**1. Environmental Containment Suit (ECS) - Model "Anchor-7" or equivalent:**

*   **Atmosphere & Respiration:** The atmosphere is thin and non-life-sustaining. You will need a fully closed-loop rebreather system. Standard oxygen tanks are fine, but a system that scrubs CO2 and recycles oxygen is essential for longer excursions.
*   **Dust Filtration:** The dust is finer than any known particulate. It will bypass standard filters and foul equipment with frightening speed. Your suit's joints, seals, and all external equipment must be rated for zero-ingress. Your primary air intake must have a multi-stage centrifugal and electrostatic filter system. Bring at least five full sets of replacement filters.
*   **Thermal Regulation:** The world is cool, but a standard sun in a thin atmosphere means intense, direct radiation. The ground will be cool, but any surface directly facing the sun will heat up. Your suit needs an active thermal regulation system to manage this differential and prevent overheating or hypothermia.
*   **Visor Configuration:** This is critical. The shadows are absolute black voids. Your visor must have high-dynamic-range imaging and built-in illumination (flood and spot) to see into them. It must also have an adjustable, full-spectrum polarization filter to manage the glare off the polished stone slopes. **Crucially, it must have a "Greyscale Lock" function.** This setting digitally reduces all incoming visual data to a simple greyscale palette, helping your brain reject the impossible cascades of color from the storm.

**2. Navigation & Anchoring Tools:**

*   **Primary NAV:** Your standard reality-hopping GPS will function, but it cannot be your only tool. The storm can create phantom positional data.
*   **Inertial Navigation Unit (INU):** This is non-negotiable. An INU tracks your movement from a starting point without external signals. Calibrate it at your insertion point and re-calibrate it daily. When your GPS and INU disagree, *trust the INU*.
*   **Physical Waypoints:** You will be issued a set of 50 "Ground-Truth Beacons." These are small, heavy discs that, when deployed, emit a simple, encrypted low-frequency pulse. They are designed to be dropped, not thrown. Let gravity place them. They are your breadcrumbs. Your HUD will map them. If you see a beacon floating or in a place you know you didn't leave it, your senses are being compromised.

**3. Psychological Preparedness Kit (The "Litany Kit"):**

This is as vital as your rebreather. It is a series of non-digital, physical, and mental tools for re-grounding yourself.

*   **The Anchor:** You must choose a small, dense, physically complex object from your home reality. A heavy steel watch with moving gears, a small stone with a unique texture, a set of engraved metal dice. It must have weight, texture, and a familiar, unchangeable form. Keep it in a designated pocket. During a storm, you will hold it in your gloved hand, feeling its reality.
*   **The Mantra:** You will memorize a simple phrase and practice reciting it until it is automatic. The standard recommended mantra is: **"My feet are on the stone. The stone is the world. The world is silent. All else is weather."** Repeat this aloud. The suit's microphone will play your own voice back to you—a real sound in a world of phantom noise.
*   **The Task:** Have a simple, rote mental task ready. Reciting the first 100 prime numbers. Naming all the bones in the human hand. Conjugating a complex verb. It must be something that requires focus but is based on static, learned information. This forces your mind to follow established logical pathways, rejecting the storm's chaotic input.

**4. Sustenance & Supplies:**

*   **No Water:** There is no water. All water must be brought with you and recycled by your suit. A week-long excursion requires at least a 10-liter buffer in addition to your suit's closed-loop system.
*   **Rations:** Standard nutrient paste. Do not bring complex flavored foods. The storm can corrupt your sense of taste, turning a favorite meal into a horrifying sensory experience. Keep it bland, keep it functional.
*   **Locomotion Aids:** The dust is deep and soft, like walking in flour. You will need flotation "snowshoes" for the plains. The polished mountain slopes are treacherous. Your boots must have retractable micro-crampons for gripping the stone. A walking staff with a broad, disc-like base (for the dust) and a carbide tip (for the stone) is essential for stability.

### **Section II: On-World Procedures & Survival**

**Arrival and First Acclimation:**

Your first hour is critical. Upon arrival, do not move. Stand still. Let the silence settle. This is the baseline reality. Feel the weight of your suit, the gentle hum of its systems. Look at your feet. See the dust or stone beneath them. Deploy your first Ground-Truth Beacon at your feet. This is Point Zero. Now, listen. There is nothing to hear. Memorize this feeling. This is peace. This is truth.

**Traversing the Stillness:**

*   **The Scale is a Liar:** Those domes are not mountains; they are provinces. The plain between them is not a field; it is a sea of dust that can take days to cross. Your sense of distance will be useless. Trust your INU and the time/distance calculations on your HUD.
*   **Walk with Intent:** On the dust plains, use your snowshoes and a wide, steady gait. On the stone slopes, use your micro-crampons and test every footstep. A fall here will not be a short tumble; it will be a long, silent slide down a slope the size of a country.
*   **Respect the Crystalline Growths:** Do not touch them. They are life, but not as we know it. They are exceedingly ancient and fragile. Observe them, log their formations, but give them a wide berth. Their energy signature is faint but may have unpredictable interactions with your suit's systems.

**Surviving the Storm:**

You will learn to feel a storm's approach. The silence develops a subtle pressure. The air ahead will seem to "vibrate," like heat-haze on a cold day. This is your only warning.

*   **When the Shimmer Starts: STOP.** Do not try to walk through it. Immediately find a stable patch of ground. Sit down. If you are on a slope, brace yourself. Physical movement while sensorily compromised is the leading cause of injury.
*   **Engage Your Defenses:**
    1.  **Go to Ground:** Place a gloved palm flat on the stone or deep into the dust. Feel its texture, its solidity, its temperature. This is your primary point of contact with reality.
    2.  **Grasp Your Anchor:** Take out your personal anchor object. Hold it. Focus on its physical properties.
    3.  **Begin the Mantra:** Speak your mantra. Listen to your own voice.
    4.  **Engage Greyscale Lock:** If the visual phenomena are intense, activate your visor's Greyscale Lock. Rob the storm of its palette.
*   **DO NOT:**
    *   **Do Not Follow Anything:** You may see a beckoning light, a path of impossible flowers, or hear a familiar voice. They are lures woven from raw data and your own subconscious. They lead nowhere.
    *   **Do Not Try to Record It:** Your standard visual/audio recorders will capture garbage data or nothing at all. Trying to focus on the phenomena to "get a good shot" is a trap that invites the chaos into your mind.
    *   **Do Not Acknowledge the Faces:** The storm may generate faces, may whisper your name. Do not speak back. Do not react. You are a rock in a river of ghosts. Let it flow past you.

**The "Skimmers" and the Artifacts:**

The Skimmers are a part of the storm. They may appear as a flicker of darkness or a ripple in a phantom image. They seem drawn to the densest parts of the chaos. Our understanding is that they are harmless, likely not even processing your existence. However, *do not attempt to focus on them*. Trying to visually track an entity that moves through information, not space, can cause severe neurological disorientation, vertigo, and migraines. Look away.

The pylons and other artifacts are the source of the storm. Approach them with extreme caution. Their localized fields are exponentially more powerful. Do not touch them. Observe and scan from a minimum safe distance of 100 meters. The information they bleed is raw and unfiltered; prolonged direct exposure, even with training, is not recommended.

### **Section III: The Psychological Toll**

No traveler returns from this world unchanged. Be prepared for the after-effects.

*   **Existential Vertigo:** The sheer scale of the geology and the age of the world can induce a profound sense of insignificance. This is normal. Your Mantra and Anchor are designed to help you maintain your own sense of self.
*   **Sensory Bleed:** For days or weeks after your return, you may experience minor phantom phenomena. A flicker of impossible color in your peripheral vision, a single note of a nonexistent song. This will fade. Trust in the stability of your home reality.
*   **A Craving for Silence:** You will find normal, inhabited worlds to be unbearably loud. This is the price of having truly experienced quiet. It is a form of nostalgia for a place that is fundamentally hostile to you.

This world teaches one lesson above all others: the difference between the foundation and the facade. You will learn to trust the ground beneath your feet in a way that others can never understand. You will leave a piece of your sensory certainty behind, but you will gain a new and profound appreciation for the simple, solid, silent truth of existence.

Trust the stone. Trust the dust. Good luck, Traveler.
