Preparing for a visit to the Xylem Mat is less about packing for a trip and more about preparing for a fundamental re-calibration of what it means to exist. Here is your preparatory guide. Read it carefully. There are no second chances on the Mat.

### **A Traveler's Guide to the Xylem Mat and the City of Xylos**

#### **Section 1: The Five Unbreakable Axioms of Preparation**

Before we discuss equipment or etiquette, you must internalize five truths about this world. They are not suggestions; they are the physical laws that will govern your survival.

1.  **There Is No Water.** This is not an exaggeration. The atomic
    and molecular chemistry of this world did not follow the path of H2O. There are no rivers, no oceans, no ice, no rain, no vapor. The word itself has no meaning here. Consequently, **you are a walking biohazard.** Your body, a sack of 60% water, is an anomaly of catastrophic potential. A simple leak from your suit could be an act of planetary-scale contamination.
2.  **You Cannot Live Off The Land.** Nothing here is food. The energy that animates the Mat, the Crabs, and the Skimmers is not something your biology can process. Attempting to consume local life, or even interface directly with the silver energy fissures, would be like a human trying to photosynthesize by swallowing radioactive waste. You must be 100% self-sufficient.
3.  **The Ground Is Alive and Aware.** You are not walking on rock. You are walking on the skin of a planet-sized, thinking organism. Every footstep is felt. Every action is registered. This is not a metaphor. You are a microbe on the back of a god, and your primary goal is to go unnoticed.
4.  **Technology is Biology.** Do not look for machines, wires, or power plants. It's all integrated. The Pillars, the Folds, the city of Xylos—it's all one contiguous entity. There is no distinction between the natural and the artificial. To damage a "building" is to wound the planet.
5.  **Sound Is More Than Sound.** The constant hum is the world's internal monologue. It is the sound of its circulatory system, its nervous system, and its inhabitants' communication. You will be bathed in a torrent of unfiltered information that your brain cannot process. This sensory firehose is one of the greatest dangers to your sanity.

#### **Section 2: Essential Pre-Traversal Outfitting**

Your standard multiverse kit is dangerously insufficient. You must acquire or fabricate the following:

**Primary Survival System: The Closed-Loop Hydration Suit (CLHS)**

*   This is non-negotiable. This is your life. It must be a full-body, sealed environmental suit with a perfect, zero-loss water recycling system. All perspiration, urine, and respiration vapor must be captured, purified, and returned to your drinking supply.
*   **Contamination Protocol:** The suit must feature multiple layers of self-sealing material. A breach is not just a danger to you, but to the Mat. Most CLHS designed for Xylem-type worlds have a "Terminal Cautery" function, which will instantly seal a puncture with extreme heat, likely causing severe burns. This is preferable to the alternative.
*   **External Texture:** The outer layer should be a non-reactive, non-conductive polymer that mimics the leathery texture of the Mat itself. This is both a sign of respect and a practical measure to minimize your alien footprint.

**Atmospheric & Sensory Interface:**

*   **Rebreather/Oxygen Supply:** The air is thin and its composition, while technically breathable in the short term, is wrong. It lacks the complex organic compounds your lungs are used to and carries that constant static charge. A rebreather providing your own tailored, humidified air is essential for long-term comfort and health. The static will otherwise build up in your lungs, leading to a condition local travelers call "Static Lung," which feels like your insides are crackling.
*   **Full-Spectrum Visor:** The sourceless violet light will play havoc with your perceptions of distance and shadow. Your helmet's visor needs adaptive filtering to create a more natural contrast and protect against potential unseen frequencies. It should also have a polarized setting to manage the glare from the silver energy pulses.
*   **Sonic Dampening & Analysis Headset:** Do not travel without this. A simple earplug will not do. You need an active system that can dampen the overwhelming bass notes of the Mat's metabolism while isolating and translating the faunal clicks and whistles into a manageable auditory range. Without it, you risk nausea, vertigo, and eventually, a total psychotic break from sensory overload. The goal is to turn the roar into a hum.

**Mobility and Utility:**

*   **Resonant-Soled Boots:** Your boots need to solve two conflicting problems. The soles should be thick and insulating to protect you from the constant vibration and energy flows. However, you will also need a means to discharge the static a-ccumulation from the air. Good boots for the Mat have retractable "grounding filaments"—tiny, conductive needles you can deploy to safely sync your suit's charge with the Mat's when you are stationary. Do *not* do this near a primary data-fissure.
*   **"No-Trace" Tool Kit:** No knives, no shovels. Your tools must be sonic and thermal. A focused sonic drill for taking samples (if you have a permit), a thermal cauterizer for repairs or medical emergencies. Anything that cuts or drips is forbidden.
*   **Personal Energy Field Detector:** This is your weather forecast. It will give you a few moments' warning before a major energy pulse passes through your area, allowing you to find a hollow or flatten yourself as the local fauna do.

#### **Section 3: On Arrival - Your First Hours**

1.  **Stationary Acclimatization:** When you first arrive, do not move. Sit. Your suit will handle your physical needs. Your task is to let your mind adjust. Engage your sonic dampeners to 80% and just listen. Feel the thrum through the soles of your boots. Watch the slow, continental pulse of silver light in the distance. Your sense of scale will be broken. A Pillar that looks a hand's-span away is a three-hour walk. Let your brain rebuild its understanding of reality. This may take a full day. Do not rush it.
2.  **First Steps:** Walk slowly, deliberately. Place your feet with care. You are a guest. Avoid stepping directly on the silver fissures whenever possible. Think of them as open veins. The Mat is resilient, but blatant disrespect can have consequences—a localized energy spike, a sudden slickness of the surface, a tremor. It knows you are there.
3.  **Observing the Fauna:** The Resonator Crabs and Glasswing Skimmers will likely ignore you if you remain calm. Do not approach them. The crystalline carapaces of the crabs are not decorative; they are part of their energy-resonance system. Touching one could discharge your suit's static charge uncontrollably or disrupt their feeding. The Skimmers' shrieks are communication; your sonic headset may allow you to perceive patterns, but do not attempt to mimic them.

#### **Section 4: Navigating to Xylos**

Xylos is not a destination you simply walk into. It is a place you attune to.

*   **Follow the Flow:** The city is built in a valley between the great Folds. Orient yourself by the major landmarks—the Pillars and the Folds. The pathways to Xylos are not paved; they are simply areas where the Mat's surface is smoother, worn down by millennia of gentle passage. The faint silver pulses in the ground will often be brighter and more organized closer to the city, like highways of light. Follow them.
*   **The Gates Are Sounds:** There is no grand entrance. You will know you are in Xylos when the ambient hum of the planet gains a new layer of complexity. The random clicks and chimes will resolve into repeating melodic or rhythmic phrases. This is the city's broadcast, its art, its consciousness. You are inside the "walls" when you can clearly distinguish the city's coherent song from the planet's raw thrum.
*   **Urban Etiquette:** The inhabitants are gardeners, not governors. They are rarely seen and may not even perceive you in a way you understand. You are a ghost in their garden. Do not enter the hollowed towers unless you feel an undeniable "invitation"—a specific, directed pattern of light or sound. Stay on the main thoroughfares. The translucent walls of the structures allow you to see the planet's lifeblood pulsing within. It is a privilege to witness this; do not deface it by trying to carve your name.
*   **The Resonant Chambers:** The source of the city's broadcasts are large, hollowed-out chambers at the base of the Folds. Approaching these is generally acceptable. Sit outside and listen. This is the closest you will come to understanding the soul of this world. It is the Mat's voice, given grammar by its people.

#### **Section 5: Final Warnings and Departure**

*   **Injury:** If you are cut, your suit will hopefully self-seal. If you must perform field medicine, use your thermal cauterizer. A single drop of your blood, a single tear, hitting the Mat could be an ecological disaster. It is a foreign chemistry the organism has never encountered. The reaction is unknowable, but it is unlikely to be benign.
*   **The Weather:** When your detector warns of an incoming energy wave, do as the crabs do. Find a low spot, get flat, and wait. The wave will pass over you. It is a brilliant, beautiful, and utterly impersonal event. You will feel it in your bones, a deep, cleansing thrum. For a moment, your suit and the planet will be in perfect synchrony. It is the most intimate experience you can have here.
*   **Psychological Strain:** The greatest danger is not physical. It is existential. The sheer age, scale, and alien coherence of the Xylem Mat can be crushing. The perpetual twilight, the unceasing hum, the utter lack of anything familiar can erode the sanity of even seasoned travelers. Set a firm departure time and stick to it. Do not allow the deep hum to lull you into forgetting who you are.

You are visiting a place that has perfected itself. It is a living monument at the end of time. It will not change for you. It will not cater to you. Your goal is simply to be a respectful witness, to stand for a moment on the skin of a living world, and to leave it as pristine as you found it. If you can do that, the memory of the silver light pulsing in the purple dusk will stay with you across all your future travels, a silent, resonant hum in the core of your being.
