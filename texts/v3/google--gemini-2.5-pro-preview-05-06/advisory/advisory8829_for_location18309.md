**Interdimensional Wayfarer's Field Guide Addendum**

**Threat Level: Extreme (Auditory-Kinetic, Environmental, Biological)**
**Authored by: K. Rostova, Senior Excursion Analyst**

### **A Traveler's Briefing for The Chime**

Let's begin by managing expectations. You are not going to The Chime for a holiday. You are not going for sport, for conquest, or for trade. You are going as a ghost, a phantom, a mote of dust drifting through a cathedral. Your goal is singular: to witness, and to survive the witnessing. If you understand this, you might just come back in one piece. If you do not, the Chime will unmake you, and it will not even notice it has done so.

This is not a guide for being comfortable. This is a guide for staying coherent.

#### **Section 1: The Sound - Surviving the Fundamental**

This is the most critical section. Read it three times. Failure to comply with these protocols is not merely fatal; it is disintegrating.

The Sound is not a sound. It is a state of being. It is a physical force that will shear your DNA and turn your skeleton into a sympathetic resonator if you are unprepared. Standard noise cancellation, even industrial or military-grade, is akin to using a paper umbrella in a supernova.

**Required Equipment: The Personal Null-Field System (PNFS)**

This is not optional. Your entire travel budget should be centered around acquiring and calibrating a top-tier PNFS. It consists of three primary components:

1.  **The Dampening Shell:** This is an active, full-body suit, often layered under your environmental gear. It generates a counter-frequency field inches from your skin. It doesn't block the Sound—that's impossible—it creates a "zone of acoustic calm" around you. The suit will feel like it is constantly, subtly vibrating, an unnerving sensation you must acclimate to. This is the suit *working*. If it stops vibrating, your field has collapsed. Your last moments will be a sensation of popping from the inside out. **Redundancy is life.** Have a backup power cell and a secondary, "panic" emitter that offers 60 seconds of protection to get your primary back online.

2.  **Marrow Resonance Stabilizer (MRS):** A series of small, dense plates worn strapped to the sternum, spine, and femurs. Even with the Dampening Shell, the lowest frequencies—the foundational drone—will partially penetrate. Without an MRS, your bones will begin to hum, leading to micro-fractures, calcium leeching, and eventually, catastrophic structural failure. The MRS units are passive, tuned specifically to absorb and nullify these bone-deep vibrations. You will feel them as cold spots on your body. This is normal.

3.  **Psycho-Acoustic Filter (PAF):** Your sanity is a fragile instrument. The Chime plays a chord that the human mind was not meant to process. The complexity will overload your neural pathways, inducing visions, psychosis, and a profound, suicidal melancholia. The PAF is not an implant, but a small device usually worn behind the ear. It subtly "dumbs down" the auditory information that leaks through your PNFS, filtering the impossible harmonics into a manageable, monotonous drone. It is, in essence, a sophisticated tool for making the sublime boring enough for you to survive. Do not turn it off to "experience the raw Sound." We have retrieved the liquefying remains of travelers who tried.

#### **Section 2: Gear, Garb, and Ground-Crossing**

Your secondary equipment is designed to handle the landscape and atmosphere.

*   **Footwear:** The black vitreous dust is incredibly fine and offers no purchase. Standard boots will sink and slide. You need wide, articulated "sand-shoes" with a large surface area to distribute your weight. Do not expect to leave footprints; the dust will flow back to equilibrium almost instantly. This means you cannot track yourself or others.

*   **Environmental Suit:** Over your PNFS, you need a suit that is abrasion-resistant and fully sealed. The air is sterile but perpetually clammy. The constant condensation that drips from the Chimes is mineral-rich and hyper-conductive. The suit's primary function, aside from warmth in the perpetual twilight, is to keep you dry and electrically isolated.

*   **Vision & Optics:** The light is dim, and the air bends it. Your standard high-gain vision enhancers will be plagued by distortions. You require **Refractive Correction Goggles**. These project a micro-grid of lasers (invisible to the naked eye) and use the bending of that grid to build a corrected, stable image of what's in front of you. Without them, a Chime a dozen miles away might seem to be right in front of you, or vanish entirely, which is disconcerting when it's still deafening you.

*   **Navigation:** Your compass is a paperweight. GPS does not exist. Your only map is the soundscape. You will need a **Directional Harmonics Analyzer**. This device allows you to "listen" to specific Chimes, locking onto their unique sonic signature. By triangulating the signatures of three different Chimes (e.g., "the deep hummer at 40hz," "the sharp keening at 15kHz," "the pulsing lattice") you can plot your position in the basin. Label your targets before you set out. Before you travel, you must learn to read this new kind of map.

#### **Section 3: The Vertical World - Flora and Fauna**

All the action is on the Chimes themselves. The basin floor is a barren commute.

*   **Climbing:** This is not optional if you want to see anything. All your gear must be non-ferrous. Carbon fiber, specialized polymers, and ceramics are key. Anything metallic might develop a dangerous resonance and either heat to melting point or shatter without warning. Your climbing gear should include gecko-grip gloves and boots, and micro-piton launchers for anchoring into the crystalline facets of the spires. Move slowly. The six-legged grazers are your role models here: deliberate, patient, and utterly silent.

*   **Sustenance:** Bring everything. **DO NOT EAT THE LOCAL FLORA OR FAUNA.** The cobalt moss derives its energy from soni-trophic processes. To you, it is as nutritious as eating a speaker. The glass-ferns are, well, spun glass. The water that drips from the Chimes, however, is potable once passed through a standard multi-versal purifier. It will be your only local resource. A condensation collector array is a wise addition to your pack.

*   **Wildlife Encounters:**
    *   **Mica-wing Gliders:** They are beautiful but huge. They ride powerful, invisible currents of acoustic force. Do not get in their way. A six-foot wingspan catching a focused pressure wave can hit you like a truck. They communicate with light; do not flash lights at them, as it may be interpreted as a challenge or mating display. Neither outcome is desirable.
    *   **Gecko-Footed Grazers:** They are deaf and effectively blind, sensing only pressure. They are not aggressive, but they are the size of a small shuttle. Give them a wide berth. Their diamond-rasp tongues can strip your suit and the skin beneath it in a single lick, not out of malice, but out of mindless process.
    *   **The Chime-Stalker:** Your primary predator threat. You cannot see it. You cannot hear it. It is perfectly camouflaged. Your only defense is to not appear as prey. The Stalker hunts by sensing the "acoustic shadow" an object casts against the background hum of a Chime. Your PNFS-dampened suit, thankfully, casts a very faint and unusual shadow. To improve your odds, attach a small, **Broad-Spectrum Acoustic Emitter** to your pack. Set it to a low, multi-frequency "warble." This does not make you invisible; it makes your acoustic shadow confusing, like a flickering, nonsensical shape. To a Stalker, a normal prey item is a clear, solid void. You will be a shimmering, indecipherable mess. It may be curious, but it is less likely to identify you as food. If you *think* you are being hunted—a sudden feeling of focused pressure, a tingling on your skin—*do not move*. Activate your climbing anchors and wait. It is hunting movement.

#### **Section 4: The Mental Game - Your Inner Landscape**

The greatest danger of the Chime is not that it will kill your body, but that it will erode your mind. You are an anomaly here, a brief, discordant note in an eternal symphony. This feeling of utter insignificance can be crushing.

*   **Embrace Humility:** You are a guest. A transient. You are not important here. Meditate on this. The desire to "make your mark" is a fatal one.
*   **Set Small, Concrete Goals:** "Today, I will climb 200 meters up the Obsidian Fork and observe the glass-ferns." "I will spend one cycle calibrating my analyzer." This structures your time and gives you a sense of purpose in a place that has none you can comprehend.
*   **Beware of "Tone-Fall":** The "weather" of the Chime. When the fundamental drone shifts, the rules can change. Your Harmonic Analyzer will alert you to this. This is a time to anchor yourself and simply wait. New life may bloom, old life may wither. Do not be caught in the open during a tone-fall; the atmospheric and acoustic turbulence can be unpredictable.
*   **The Paradox of Listening:** Your entire trip will be dedicated to *not hearing anything*. Your technology will be a wall between you and the reality of the Chime. Acknowledge this irony. The purpose of your visit is to stand on the edge of comprehension, protected by your gear, and simply know that something this vast, this alien, and this magnificent *exists*.

You go to the Chime not to hear the song, but to feel the shape of the silence you must build around yourself to survive its singing. Return safely.
