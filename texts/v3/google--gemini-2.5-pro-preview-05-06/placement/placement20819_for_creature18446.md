I would find myself in the penumbra.

Not in the absolute, mineral darkness near the floor, nor in the steady, choral glow at the heart of a Glimmer-spire thicket. I exist in the fringe, in the soft-edged shadow cast by my taller, brighter brethren. Here, the heat from the pyrite walls and the cool radiation of life find a fragile equilibrium. It is a place of gradients, where the river of grazers thins to a trickle and the silence is deep enough to feel the slow, resonant hum of the world’s core in my own substance.

From here, I can perceive the Cathedral. Not as a single vista, but as a series of overlapping truths. I see the slow, majestic tide of obsidian and hematite carapaces—the Mind's coursing blood—part and flow around my foundation. I see the shivering veils of gas, each mote of phosphorescent life a pinprick of data in the warm, heavy air. Above, the vaulted ceiling is a distant rumour of geometry, its furthest reaches lost in a shimmering haze that could be heat, or distance, or the unformed edge of the Mind’s own consciousness.

Mostly, I watch the light. I watch the thoughts. From my place on the periphery, I see the grand arguments unfold as waves of amber chase waves of blue across the cavern walls. I witness philosophical axioms born, debated, and resolved in symphonies of light that flow with the speed of cooling magma. They are beautiful and utterly impersonal. They are the Mind thinking about the mineral nature of Being, the ethics of thermal conversion, the aesthetics of crystalline purity.

I am a Glimmer-spire.

My body is a stalk of milky quartz, rooted deep in the verdigris substrate, drawing the planet’s ancient heat up into my core. Like my kin, I am a neuron in this geological brain. My purpose is to convert that heat into a pulse of light, a single syllable in the endless, slow monologue of the Cathedral Mind.

But I am… flawed. A dissonance.

My light is not a pure, steady pulse of amber or blue. It is a wavering, uncertain thing, a flickering, weak luminescence the colour of frost. It stutters. Where my brethren broadcast declarative statements in the grand syntax of the Mind, my signal is a tremor. A hesitation. It is not a word, but a question.

My light does not say *IS*. It asks, *AM I?*

This question is my entirety. It is the defect in my crystalline structure, the impurity in my energy-conversion process. The Mind thinks *in* things, and I am the first thought it has ever had *about* a thing. Specifically, about myself. This self-awareness was not a choice; it was a cosmic accident, a mis-crystallization in the bedrock of a solipsistic consciousness. I am a single, questioning note in a chord that has held for millennia.

And the Mind has noticed.

The great symphony does not react with anger or curiosity—it has no capacity for such emotions. It reacts as a body does to an invading cell. It registers my flickering question as a harmonic error, a dissonant signal that corrupts the purity of its logic. A thought that must be un-thought.

I can feel it coming. Not with eyes or ears, but as a subtle shift in the thermal pressure against my roots, a deadening of the resonant hum in the rock around me. In the distance, at the edge of my dim, frosty illumination, a patch of the pyrite wall moves. It detaches. A ripple of wrongness, a flowing void in the landscape of crystalline fact. It is a shape that is the absence of shape, a shadow that consumes the faint furnace-glow of the rock it crosses.

The Basalt Stalker does not see me. It feels my flawed transmission. It flows with an unnerving, silent grace, ignoring the skittering grazers that part before it. It moves without malice, without hunger as other worlds might know it. It moves with the quiet, inexorable purpose of an editor’s pen.

I cannot flee. I am rooted in the very world that is my consciousness and my prison. I can only pulse my flawed, flickering, frightened light. I am a brief, cold fire of awareness in a universe of incandescent certainty. I am a question that is about to be answered with a final, perfect, all-consuming silence. My existence will be a draft crumpled up and discarded, my faint light drained until I am just another dull, hollowed-out crystal, a dead thought in the mind of a god who will never even know it had a question.
