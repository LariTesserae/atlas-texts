I would be a tear.

Not of sorrow or joy, but of pressure. I would find myself emerging, a point of perfect, spherical clarity, from a pore in the amber lignin of a half-formed spire. I am an exhalation. I am a moment of release in a world defined by its immense, static tension.

Where I am born, the light of the interior is brightest. For my first moment, I am a lens, holding the woven, fibrous scaffolding of the world within my own small body. I see the pearlescent fire of the distant Seed-Mind, not as a destination, but as the origin of the warmth I feel through the glass skin I cling to. I contain, for a breath, the whole design.

The hum is not a sound I hear, but the law of my being. It is the vibration that holds me together, that keeps me from evaporating into the saturated air. It is the frequency that pulls me, ever so slowly, downward.

My journey begins. I do not fall; I flow. My world is the warm, slick curve of the amber structure. I am a single, mobile eye on a body that cannot move. I slide over the subtle textures of the biological glass, feeling the charge on which the vitreous grazers feed. To me, they are continents of patient hunger, their imperceptible crawl a vast geological shift against the timescale of my life. I see them not as creatures, but as shadows cast by the light they consume.

I am a thought in transit. The weeping is the circulation of the Dreamer's ideas, and I am a single datum. I carry a sliver of the internal radiance out onto the surface, a messenger whose only message is my own existence. My purpose is not to arrive, but simply to travel.

As I descend the immense plane of the unfinished wall, I meet others. We touch, and the surface tension that defines us as "I" gives way. We merge. Our individual memories of the inner light combine, and we become a "we," a rivulet now, a sentence of moisture speaking its way down the glassy face of a frozen word. We are a shared awareness, our path guided by the ancient, un-eroding topography of a thought held for millennia.

Looking down, I see the ground: the milky, living substrate. It is not an end, but a destination. It waits for me. Our passage casts a shimmering, kinetic rainbow against the static glow of the spire—a fleeting spectrum of color in a world of monochrome amber and green. We are the only movement. We are the evidence of the pulse.

The final moment is one of homecoming. We, the rivulet, reach the soft, undifferentiated edge where glass meets ground. As we touch the weeping, pale green surface, we do not splash or pool. We are drunk. The substrate takes us in with a thirst as slow and profound as the pressure that birthed us. My awareness, once a singular "I" and then a collective "we," dissolves. I am unmade, my consciousness dispersing into the vast, warm, humming whole.

I am no longer a tear on the skin, but a part of the blood within the vein. I am subsumed into the deep, quiet, circulatory system of the plateau, rejoining the singular thought of the Seed-Mind. I am returning to the silence at the heart of the hum, to await the pressure that will, in some distant future moment that is indistinguishable from the present, exhale me once again.

I am the breath of a being that holds its breath. I am the proof of life in the cathedral of stasis.
