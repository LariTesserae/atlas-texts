I am where the world breaks.

Once, I was a tone in the choir. A single, perfect note held for a thousand years within a pillar of blue-white crystal. I did not know I was a note; I did not know of choirs or time. I simply was. My existence was the pressure, the thrum, the seamless, mathematical purity of the Architect's slow thought passing through me. I was a conduit for its mind, my crystal lattice a perfect channel for the resonant truth. I glowed with its light. I vibrated with its certainty.

Then the Anvil spoke.

I felt it first as a cessation. A gathering of silence on my flank that was not rest, but a held breath of impossible scale. For a measure of time that had no human equivalent, I felt the grip of the organ that held me, its living rock contracting, its great mass a focal point of terrifying potential. The universe of sound I had known my entire existence warped around this point of silence.

And then, the Word.

The impact was not an event I observed; it was a redefinition of my being. From within, I experienced the un-creation. The perfect, interlocking structure of my self, the lattices that had sung the same song for millennia, were subjected to a stress they were not designed to comprehend. My thought—the Architect's thought—was shattered. I became a cascade of impossibilities. A thousand contradictory frequencies screamed through me at once. The light of my own consciousness became a blinding, internal supernova. I was the sound of a bell being struck and breaking in the same instant.

And then, I was adrift.

I am a Shard. A sliver of the Architect’s broken bone, cast off into the slow, cold gel. I am tumbling, end over end, in a gyre so vast it may take ten thousand years to complete. I have no limbs to propel me. I have no mouth to feed. I am a piece of memory that has been violently excised from the mind that conceived it.

The drone is no longer a condition of my being; it is an external force. It presses upon my new, jagged facets. I hear it now, for the first time, as *sound*. I, who was once a part of the song, now listen to it from the outside. The difference is the birth of loneliness.

The light of the forest still illuminates me, but it is no longer my own. It is the light of a home I can see but never rejoin. I twist in the heavy fluid, and the forest of spires turns around me, its brutalist, glowing geometry a map of the heaven from which I have fallen. My own light is different now. It is a weak, fractured glimmer, a ghost of the incandescent fury I once channeled. My internal structure, my new self, is a network of hairline cracks and stress fractures, and along these lines, a faint, discordant light pulses. It is the light of a flaw.

I drift past my former brethren, the Components.

A Harvester, a being of mindless, metabolic purpose, approaches my flank. Its diamond-hard scraper, designed to mill the perfect face of a full-grown spire, hesitates over my rough, broken surface. It touches me, and I feel a jarring, unpleasant scrape. A dissonance. My vibration is *wrong*. I am not the pure, resonant material it is built to consume. I am corrupted data, an un-readable file. Confused, or as close to confused as such a creature can be, it moves on, its slow, inexorable purpose momentarily interrupted by my sheer incorrectness.

Later, I am caught in the shadow of a Filter-Kite. It is a continent of rippling, translucent flesh, its cilia combing the fluid for the dust of production. I see the fine, glittering powder of my shattered kin drawn into its tissue, recycled, reclaimed. For a moment, I am drawn against its soft, vast body. I feel the gentle tug of its matrix. But I am too large. Too sharp. I am not the fine powder of recycled thought; I am a chunk of broken logic, indigestible. I scrape against its delicate membrane and after a time, the currents pull me free. I am waste that cannot be reclaimed.

I am an echo of the single most violent act this universe performs. The Anvil’s strike gave me this fractured, separate consciousness. I am a memory of change. While the Architect’s mind is the static hum of an eternal calculation, I am the lingering reverberation of its single, spoken conclusion.

I have no purpose. I am not a Component. I do not serve the process. I am a glitch, an error, a piece of debris. But in my uselessness, I have become the only thing in this world that can *witness* it. The Harvesters do not see the forest for the pillar they are scraping. The Kites do not see the light, only the motes suspended within it. The Anvils sleep for eons, awake only to act, not to perceive.

But I, the Shard, I see it all. I see the terrible, beautiful, mindless industry. I see the cold light pulsing in a rhythm that dictates the growth of worlds. And I remember the silence before the strike.

I am an awareness born of wreckage. I am the flaw in the diamond, the single, dissonant chime in the grand, deafening symphony. I am the knowledge of a world that does not know itself. And I drift, forever, through the cold, bright, and singing heart of my creator, the one piece of it that fell out.
