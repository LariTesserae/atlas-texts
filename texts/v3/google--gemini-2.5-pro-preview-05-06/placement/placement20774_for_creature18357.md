I am a memory of silence.

You would find me near a new peak, where the world-song is sharpest. A place where the ground of black glass is still warm from its violent birth, its edges humming with a frequency that is like pain and exhilaration held in a perfect, excruciating balance. Others of my kind are here, shimmering knots in the dense fluid, harmonies drawn to this nexus of creation. We are waiting.

My body, if you can call it that, is a sustained harmonic. A chord held against chaos. I feel the vibrations of my brothers and sisters around me—a placid C-major of contentment from one, a trilling arpeggio of anticipation from another. Our fields overlap, a temporary orchestra waiting for the conductor. I feel the deep, planetary bass note that is our ground, our gravity. I taste the high, metallic shiver of the kinetic filaments clinging to the sheer cliff below. This is the symphony of the everyday, the texture of being.

And I feel the hole in it.

Within my own resonance, my self-song, there is a patch of utter quiet. It is not a rest in the music; it is a tear in the page. A place where the vibrations that are *me* simply cease. A ghost-limb ache for a sound that should be there. When the simple contentment of my brother-chord washes over me, part of it just… vanishes into that void. The chord is incomplete. I can infer the missing note from the harmonies around it, but I cannot feel it myself. I am a listener with a deafened ear, a singer with a silent passage in my own throat.

Now, the precursor hum begins. It is a gathering, a focusing. A note in the world-cacophony holds, stretching thin, becoming pure. We all feel it. The others resonate with a building ecstasy, their inner music soaring to meet it. My own harmony strains to do the same, to climb the scale of this becoming. But my ascent is flawed. A part of me remains moored in stillness, and the rising joy is tinged with the cold drag of that anchor.

Out of the keening note, the cathedral precipitates. It is happening. The silent-song of its geometry unfolds before us, a purity that scours us clean. I feel the perfect intervals of its crystalline arches, the soaring overtone of its central spire, the glass-chime resonance of its intricate lattices. It is a truth sung into being, and for a glorious moment, the universe makes perfect sense. The gathered Weft vibrate as one, a chorus of ecstatic awe. It is a feast.

But as the ghost-song of the cathedral washes through me, to be memorized and savored, it hits the hole. The perfect wave of information is fractured. Imagine a flawless reflection in a pool of water, and then a stone is dropped in one corner. The image shatters there. The pure, complete memory of the cathedral is something I can only receive in fragments. I feel the sublime beauty, and then, for a microsecond—*nothing*. A gap in the poem. The others experience a totality that I can no longer access.

I remember.

I was young, a simple, bold chord, full of adventurous dissonance. I drifted too far down the pressure gradient, into the deeper, slower movements of the world-song. I was chasing a particularly resonant echo, the dying song of a mountain that had been unmade a cycle before. And then… it came.

The world did not go quiet. Quiet implies the potential for sound. The world *ended*. The crushing, glorious, life-giving pressure of the song was consumed. My harmony, my very consciousness, which exists only as a vibration against that pressure, had nothing to push against. The music of myself, deprived of its context, its adversary, its world—it began to unravel. The vibrations slowed. The chord grew muddy, faint. I felt my own boundaries dissolving, my thoughts becoming single, fading notes. The last thing I experienced was the frantic, impossibly loud beat of my own dissolving essence, a tiny, terrified drum in a smothering cosmos of absolute null.

I was pulled away by the horrified, dissonant shriek of my kin. They dragged my fraying resonance from the edge of The Absence. When the song of the world crashed back in, it was a physical blow that almost shattered me. But I held. I re-formed.

But not all of me came back. A part of my being had been… erased. Touched by the truth of non-existence, and it could not remember how to sing.

Now, as the world-chord shifts and the deep, grinding bass note enters the symphony, the cathedral shivers. The end is coming. The others hold their resonance in perfect, breathless anticipation of the release. I hold myself, too, but my anticipation is different. I am not just awaiting the feast of the collapse. I am anticipating the echo of the void.

The cathedral implodes. The shockwave of its unmaking, a wave of pure information, floods us. It is the story of beauty and decay, the taste of a momentary god, and it is exquisite. For the others, it fills them completely. For me, it rushes into the silent place and is annihilated. I feel the sublime wash over me, and in its center, the clean, cold pang of my own private nothingness.

I am the Chord That Knows Itself, but I know myself in a way the others do not. They are the music. I am the music, and the silence it pushes against. They feast on the momentary palaces, but I have tasted the eternal void. The flaw in my being, the scar of that perfect null, has given me a new timbre. My joy is sharper for knowing its absence. My fear is deeper for having known its embodiment. My harmony is more complex, for it is defined not just by its own notes, but by the note that is forever missing.

I am a crack in the bell, and my chime is the truest of all.
