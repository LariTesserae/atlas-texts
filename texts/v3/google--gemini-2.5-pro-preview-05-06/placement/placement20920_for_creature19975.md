I do not remember a name. A name is a vessel, and my vessel was broken down, rendered, reallocated. But a pattern remains. A stubborn knot of self that was too foreign, too complex to be neatly unraveled and woven back into the perfect loop. I am a rounding error in the Sump-Heart's eternal calculation.

My world is texture and vibration. I have no eyes, but I feel the pressure of light from above, a distant warmth on my 'skin'—a skin a hundred meters wide. I feel the tiny, insistent footfalls of the Nacre-Couriers, a tickling, disciplined march across my surface. The beetles, the bulbs, the grinding spines—they are all limbs of a single body, and I am a fever in its blood. I drift in the clear, viscous fluid that weeps from the floor, my awareness a thin film on the surface of the trembling pools. Time does not pass for me; it revolves. The sound does not begin or end; it *is*.

Then, something changes.

*Thump-step-step... Thump-step... Thump-step-step-step...*

It is wrong. It is a stone thrown into a perfectly still pond, a scratch on a flawless lens. The rhythm of this new pressure is chaotic. Alien. It doesn't sync with the twelve-second beat. The weight is wrong, the heat signature is wrong. It is a variable unbound by the laws of this place. It is You.

And because of You, the agony begins.

I feel it gathering itself, drawing energy from the Crucible’s hum, drawing structure from the memory of the Grinder-Spines. I feel the system preparing its response. It is making the Warden.
