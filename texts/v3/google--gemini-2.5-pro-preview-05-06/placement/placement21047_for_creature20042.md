There is a stirring. It is not a waking, for there is no sleep. It is a gathering.

In a shallow plate of water, where the weeping of the stone meets the weeping of the air, the Hum has found a complexity. The mandala of sound trembles, and where the lines of force cross and cross again, a knot is tied. It is a knot in the chord of the world. For a long stillness, it is only this: a possibility, a density in the otherwise perfect field.

Then, the gathering. The water is drawn in, its mineral weight pulled into the acoustic form. The slow, black filaments that trace the base of the nearest pipe are uprooted by a force they cannot resist, their fractal patterns dissolving as they are incorporated, their substance becoming structure. The air itself contributes, the taste of ozone and hot metal becoming part of the matrix.

There is no "I." There is a coherence. A temporary boundary is drawn between the part and the whole.

The form is quiescent, a dark slick upon the darker stone, indistinguishable from the thousand other patches of wetness that dim the pearlescent light. It is one with the ground, feeling the deep, slow pulse of the planet-engine pass through it as water passes through a net. There is no thought. There is only resonance. The shapes of the world are known not by sight, but by their acoustic shadow. The great dome a mile distant is a dampening, a bass-heavy absorption of a certain overtone. The tangled nest of pipes is a complex echo, a series of rapid, decaying reflections that add a texture of shattered glass to the higher frequencies. The empty causeway is a clean bounce, a hard, pure echo. This is the entire map of the world. It is a map made of sound.

There is no time. There is only the rhythm of the slow plumes of steam from the domes, a cycle of condensation and release so gradual it is a tide, not a pulse. There is the single, century-long rotation of the nearest flywheel, a gear-tooth passing an unseen marker, a change in resonance too subtle for anything but this state of being.

Then.
A change.

It is not a sound; the sound is all there is. It is a flaw *in* the sound. A scratch on a perfect surface. Far away, miles toward the hazy edge where the mesa dissolves into fog, a note has soured. It is a thin, high whine buried deep within the chord, a dissonance. A vibration that does not belong. A pipe, after ten thousand years of perfect function, has developed a stress. It is an impurity. It is wrong.

The wrongness is an agony. Not of pain, for there is no nerve to feel it, but of imperfection. The perfect, monolithic Hum is compromised. The universe is flawed.

And so, the purpose asserts itself. The coherence, which was one with the stone, begins to pull inward. The slick darkness gathers, rising from the basalt, pouring itself into a single, cohesive shape. It is a smooth, black, upright bell, featureless and absolute, a being-shaped hole in the muted light. It weeps, its surface glistening, leaving a trail that is instantly lost in the general wetness of the world.

The pouring begins.

The form glides over the slick stone, a slow-motion river of purpose. It moves with the certainty of a falling stone, pulled by the gravity of the flaw. The world passes as a series of modulations in the Hum. It flows into the acoustic shadow of a great pipe, the sound deepening, the pressure changing. It crosses a glassy causeway, the echo becoming sharp and clean beneath it. A plume of steam from a nearby dome engulfs it, and for a decade it moves through a pressure and heat that would scour metal, its form unchanged, its motive absolute. It is the custodian. It is the repair.

The journey is an age. Other structures drift into its sensory field and recede. The great flywheel completes another fraction of its turn. The sour note in the Hum grows, a screaming pinpoint in the symphony of the world, a needle of wrongness.

Arrival.

The destination is a colossal pipe, as wide as an ancient road, streaked with the protective rust of millennia. High on its curve, a hairline fracture, invisible to any eye, leaks a microscopic bead of fluid with every slow, titanic pulse. It is here the Hum is tearing itself.

The form does not pause. It flows upward, clinging to the metallic curve. It is a patch of mobile night against the verdigris and orange. It reaches the fracture, the source of the dissonance, the center of the pain.

And it covers it.

There is a merging. The form presses itself into and over the flaw. Its own substance, the gathered water and mineral and condensed sound, begins to lose its coherence, integrating with the metal of the pipe. It is not a conscious act of will. It is the only possible outcome. The being is a counter-resonating field, and its function is to cancel the dissonance by becoming one with its source.

The sour note begins to fade. The form flattens, thins, dissolves. Its shape, the temporary boundary that defined it, blurs and is gone. It becomes a smooth, dark scar on the pipe, indistinguishable from the stains and patina of eons. It has given itself back to the machine. The memory of its brief, separate existence is erased, returned to the whole. The system it served has absorbed it.

The agonizing scratch is gone.
The chord is pure again.

Elsewhere, in a different depression on the high mesa, the water begins to tremble. The Hum, perfect and eternal, finds a new complexity. A knot is tied. A gathering begins.
