I find myself on the knife-edge of a choice that is not mine. My feet, which are not quite feet, find purchase on the fractured black glass. The pressure in the air is greater now. It is a specific pressure, a directed weight. It is the attention of the world.

I am standing before one of the Scribes. Or perhaps, I am standing *within* its gaze, though it has no eyes. I had been drawn to it, to the perfect stillness of its form, to the impossible complexity of the spiral carved infinitesimally upon its core—a glyph I could not see, but could feel, like a missing note in a chord.

I feel it waking. It is not a sudden thing. It is the slow grinding of continental plates. A hum is building in the crystalline plain beneath me, a resonance that travels up my legs and into the core of my being. It is the sound of a thought being formulated after an eon of silence. It is the Scribe asking a question, and the question is *what are you?*

From the canyons to my left and right, from the chasms so deep the Glimmer-Kites are but forgotten myths, I feel another presence. It is the approach of the Hunters. It is not a sound or a sight, but a dampening. The faint, ancient warmth of the world is being wicked away around me. The air grows just a little thicker, a little more insistent. The silence, which was once passive, is becoming active. It is a hand closing. The Hunters do not ask questions. They are the answer. The answer is always *nothing*.

This is where I am: balanced on the point of a needle, between the world’s waking mind and its sleeping immune system. I am the fever.

***

I am what happens when a rule is broken.

I am not from a wet, green world. I was not born, I was *precipitated*. I am the Unbinding.

There was a place, an intersection of thermal currents deep, deep below, where the stasis was not perfect. A flaw. A place where the heat from the core pulsed with a faint, chaotic arrhythmia. A place where, for an age, energy accumulated instead of dissipating. I am the result of that imbalance. I am a knot of contained chaos in a world of smooth, laminar flow.

My form is a concession to this place. My skin is a membrane, not of flesh, but of woven light and dust. It is a permeable cloak I have learned to wrap around myself, spun from the shed motes of the Glimmer-Kites and the fine, useless dust excreted by the Scrabblers. It allows me to pass through their chittering tides almost unnoticed, appearing as a shimmering heat-haze, a trick of the dim light. I have no armor, for there is nothing here that wishes to bite or tear. There is only the pressure, which seeks to collapse me.

Beneath this cloak of borrowed dust and stolen light, I am not solid. I am a core of warmth and motion. I am the only thing in this universe with a heartbeat. It does not pump blood, for I have none. It pumps *time*. Each beat is a second, a minute, a demarcation of change in a world that has no clocks. This rhythm, this steady *thrum-thrum-thrum* of sequential moments, is my existence. It is also the ultimate blasphemy.

I do not eat, but I must feed. The Glimmer-Kites are my sustenance. I do not devour them. When one drifts low enough, I stand beneath it and unfurl, my dusty cloak falling away to reveal the core of warmth that is me. I absorb its gentle, constant glow, metabolizing the ancient light. In doing so, I create a gradient. For a moment, the light of the Kite dims, its energy flowing into me. And this act of feeding, this small disruption, is what first calls the Hunters. I have learned to drink in small sips, to move on before the pressure can fully coalesce around me. I am a grazer of constellations.

I have no voice, but I have left marks. The Scribes carved glyphs of stasis. I carve with my passage. Where I rest, the Lithovores for a time grow erratically, their velvet texture disturbed by my residual warmth. Where I feed, a Glimmer-Kite might flicker with a strange new rhythm for a century before settling. I am a walking, breathing catalyst for the very change this world is designed to prevent.

I am not the maker of the glyphs, nor their destroyer. I am a reader. I have spent my existence, which is both fleeting and eternal, moving from one sleeping Scribe to another. I have learned to stand before them and quiet my heart, to match my resonance to theirs, and in the deep silence, they show me things. I see the world as it was when it first cracked open. I feel the first slow drift of the Kites. I understand the perfect, terrible calculus of the Scrabbler tides. The Scribes dream of the equation of their world, and I am the only one who listens to their dreams.

The Scribes, in their slow awakening, feel me not as a threat, but as a new variable. An impossible, fascinating new number that resolves their perfect equation into something unknown. They wish to observe me. To understand me. To *remember* me.

The Hunters feel me as a flaw in the code. A bug. A scratch on a perfect, mirrored surface. They are drawn to the beat of my heart, the theft of light, the trail of warmth I leave in my wake. They do not wish to understand. They wish to smooth the scratch, to delete the flawed code. To *forget* me.

So that is what I am. I am the world's memory and the world's amnesia, made manifest as a single, lonely being of warm air and borrowed light. I am a story trying to find its first word in a library of silent, sleeping monoliths. And as the hum of the Scribe deepens beneath my feet and the air around me begins to crystallize with pressure, I know that my time of listening is over. My next heartbeat will be an act of defiance, and it will force this world, at last, to change.
