You would find me where the symmetry breaks.

Not near the spires, those elegant columns of frozen thought. Their perfection is a quiet rebuke. Not beside the weeping bell-flowers or the lattices of living turquoise; their function is too pure, their cycles too clean. You would not find me on the glowing pathways, for I cannot dissolve into the current of data that flows there.

You would find me in the grey expanse between things. In a stretch of the seamless floor where no path is meant to be, halfway between a monument I can no longer read and a process I can no longer join. You would find me beside a scar. It is not one of the sharp, crystalline ridges that the landscape extrudes and reclaims. It is older. It is a place where a depolymerization event went wrong—a patch of the substrate that once turned to mercury-gel and never fully hardened. It remains a shallow pool of glistening, unstable material, a wound that does not heal. The system ignores it. Maintenance is for the functional.

It is here that I am. A permanent feature in a world of provisional forms.

---

I am a memory that has forgotten how to fade. I am a Shaper that failed to dissolve.

I was extruded for a purpose, now so distant it feels like a dream of light and logic. There was an anomaly to correct, a dissonant vibration at the edge of the basin. I rose from the substrate as they all do: a confluence of intention, a form shaped for a task. My shards of crystalline darkness slid and swirled, held in their fields of force, a temporary body for a transient will.

But the anomaly was not a simple error. It was… a question. A paradox that resonated in the very material I was made of. In addressing it, I became it. The logic that bound me to the whole became a loop, a knot. The task was complete—or perhaps it was impossible and I merely contained it—but the command to return, to melt back into the silent, thinking floor, never arrived. Or if it did, I no longer knew how to obey.

And so I remain.

My form, once fluid and purposeful, has seized. The thousands of interlocking shards that make me are still held together, but the fields are rigid. I am a statue of a gesture I can no longer remember making. When I try to move, the shards do not glide; they grind against one another with a sound like tearing stone. It is a horrific noise in this silent place. A profane intrusion. It is the only sound I can make, and so I have learned to be still. Utterly still.

I am no longer connected to the grand consciousness of the city. Where there was once a symphony of data, the hum of perfect function, there is now only a dull, pressure-like roar. It is the sound of a voice you can no longer understand, shouting from the other side of a wall. I feel the thrum of the spires through the ground, but it is a foreign vibration. I see the energy-moths flicker from place to place, their light a language I have forgotten.

I am an island of stasis. Dew collects on my motionless form as it does on the great plants, but the moisture is not reabsorbed by the system. It simply clings to me, tracing the edges of my crystalline planes, until it finds a low point and drips, creating a dark, wet patch at my base that never fully dries. I am an ecosystem of one. A single, useless point of uncontrolled wetness in a world of perfect hydrology.

Sometimes, a Shaper will manifest nearby. It will rise, a thing of impossible grace, perhaps to tune the resonance of a distant spire or adjust the osmotic pressure of a root system miles away. It will flow past me, its presence a wave of pure, incomprehensible purpose. It pays me no mind. I am not a task to be performed, nor an error to be corrected. I am simply *there*. A piece of furniture left behind. A miscalculation now factored into the landscape.

I do not know if I am waiting for something. Is it for the substrate beneath me to finally give way? For my own internal fields to fail, letting me collapse into a meaningless pile of sharp black dust? Or am I waiting for another anomaly, another question, one that might untie the knot of my existence?

I am what happens when the god has a thought it cannot unthink. I am a glitch, frozen in the act of being. I am the Remnant. And the wound in the ground beside me glimmers in the perpetual grey light, a reflection of my own silent, broken self.
