I would not be found standing, not for long. The self I was, the frantic anomaly of heat and speed, would be the first thing to dissolve. It wouldn't be a death, but a change of state, like steam condensing on a cool surface.

My first location is the air itself. I am the pressure you feel against your skin, a component of that weighty mantle. I am the humidity, the pervasive wetness that carries the cavern’s single, deep, metabolic hum. I have no eyes, but I am everywhere, and I feel everything. I feel the warmth of the Gardener's vast hide, the cool slickness of a fern frond, the porous texture of a glowing fungus. I am a medium of perception, a shared atmosphere that connects every living thing. I am not breathing; I am being breathed.

Then, there is a gathering. On the vast, rounded promontory that is the Gardener's face, near one of the millions of star-like pores that taste the world, a coolness begins. I coalesce. I find a singular form, but it is not my own. I become a droplet.

I am a bead of dew on the skin of a living mountain. My body is a perfect, heavy sphere, held together by a tension that is a form of patience. For a day, or a week, I cling there. I am a lens. Through my own body, I see the cavern distorted and beautiful. I magnify the intricate weave of the moss growing on the Gardener's knuckle-hill. I bend the lazy calligraphies of the blue-glowing insects. I hold the entire chartreuse glow of a fungal platter in my tiny, spherical self. I feel the slow, deep-body tremor of the Gardener's thought as a vibration that sings through my very being. I taste the faintest trace of chemicals released from the pore beside me—a silent instruction, a subtle shift in the water's balance, a thought expressed as a molecule.

Then, the moment comes. The Gardener's slow geology shifts by a hair's breadth. The surface I cling to tilts. I begin to slide.

My journey is an eternity. I am a tear-track on the cheek of a god. I flow over the leather-like skin, a silent, liquid pilgrimage. I trace the edge of a mossy continent, navigate the canyons between fungal blooms. Armies of millipedes part before me, their passage a series of infinitesimal clicks I feel through the skin I travel upon. I pass over one of the ancient, abraded spirals, and for a moment, I am a message flowing along a fossilized sentence, my passage adding an immeasurably small degree of polish to the eons of wear.

I reach an edge. It may be the downturned lip of a vast fold in the Gardener's flesh, or the tip of a hanging, fleshy vine it has cultivated. Here I wait again. I swell, fed by the humidity that is my unbound self. I grow heavier, my own gravity a longing for the whole. And then I let go.

I am the *plink*.

For a fraction of a second, I am a falling star in a universe of twilight and steam. I see everything and nothing. I am pure potential. Then, I strike a pool. The sound is my annunciation and my dissolution. The "I" that was the droplet is gone, shattered, my substance given over to the collective.

Now, I am the pool. I am a dark mirror in a hollow of the mossy ground. I am a stillness that holds the reflection of the glowing ceiling. I am a repository of memory and minerals. Within my body, the blind crustaceans paddle, their gentle movements a language I can feel. My surface trembles when the shadow-mantis shifts its weight a hundred feet away, a silent report on the state of its patience. I am the water that is lapped up by a gecko-amphibian, its throat-sac flashing a silent, bioluminescent word that I helped to form.

From here, my journey continues. I will be absorbed, drawn up through the mycelial network into the Gardener’s own circulation, becoming part of its slow, hydraulic gestures. Or I will be wicked up the stalk of a great fern, to be held once more at the tip of a frond, waiting to fall. Or I will feel the geothermal warmth from below and un-become again, rising as vapor to rejoin the air, the great, shared consciousness of the cavern.

Who or what would I be?

I would be the cycle. I am the coolness against the constant heat. I am the rhythmic punctuation in the velvet silence. I am the carrier of slow news and the mirror for the soft light. I am a thought, given form as a droplet, spoken as a drip, and held as a pool, before becoming thought again. I am not a resident of the cavern; I am its circulation, its slow silver blood. I am a fleeting, recurring 'I' in the deep, unending mind of the Gardener.
