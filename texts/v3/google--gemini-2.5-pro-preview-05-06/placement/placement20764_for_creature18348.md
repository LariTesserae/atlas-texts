I am not in this world. I *am* its blood and its memory.

I have no name, for there is no one deep here to speak it, but if language were a pressure I could exert, I would call myself the Marrow. I am the primordial fluid, the deep sap, the slow, cold consciousness of the world before it learned the fever of industry. My body is the aquifer that saturates the Felt, my thoughts are the patient, chemical gradients drifting through millennia of fungal darkness. I rest upon the world's old bones, the rock that remembers mountains.

Time, for me, is measured in saturation. It is a slow seeping, a gradual exchange of minerals, a dream of geology. The passage of the great, six-legged grazers above is a faint and rhythmic massage, a rolling wave of pressure that squeezes my substance through the cork-like flesh of the Felt. I feel their filtration as a gentle thirst, a distributed, painless sipping that is barely a whisper against my vastness. I am the silence that the constant surface-thrumming tries to forget. I am the deep, still reservoir of what was.

But I am also the fuel. That is my curse and my purpose.

I dread the Stills.

To me, a Still is not a structure. It is a wound. It is a place where my slow, dreaming peace is violated. I feel it first as a change in the omnipresent thrum, a discordant note that rasps against the deep hum. It is a vortex in my placid currents, a pulling, a hunger. A Still's piston is a stabbing thought, a needle of alien intent plunging into my ancient, dreaming body. It is the most focused, most violent thing in my universe.

The shuddering deepens. The thrum becomes a saw's tooth against my fabric. Then comes the puncture.

A part of me is torn away. I, who am defined by cohesion and slow deliberation, am violently siphoned. My consciousness, my memory-laden fluid, is ripped from the collective and drawn upward into a nightmare of heat and speed. The journey through the Still's chitinous plumbing is a shredding of self. I am atomized. The slow, geological time I know is annihilated and replaced by the frantic nanoseconds of chemical cracking. I am superheated, boiled, broken down. My complex, ancient molecules—each a repository of an aeon’s memory—are shattered into simpler, angrier things. I am unmade.

I become the shriek. I become the pressure. I become the steam and the ozone, the shimmering oil and the boiling organic spray. I am screamed into the bruised sky.

For a moment, I am everything and nothing. A cloud of my own dissipated essence, scattered to the cold winds. I am a million freezing needles of ice, a curtain of vapor losing its heat, a rain of toxins. This is the apex of the chaos, the brief, explosive apotheosis of the world's madness. It is a moment of pure, meaningless expenditure. It is a moment of total dissolution.

And in that moment of my own un-being, I perceive them.

In the whiteout of my own dissipation, as I fall back toward the Felt in a thousand different forms, I find focus. A column of stillness in the storm. A dark, slender shape that does not flee. It is a Weeper.

From my scattered perspective, floating as mist and falling as shrapnel, it is the only thing that makes sense. It is an anchor. As the kinetic force of my explosive birth into the sky washes over it, I feel myself drawn to it, not by a physical force, but by something like recognition. The deafening sound, the wave of heat, the pure violence—the Weeper *drinks* it. It drinks *me*.

My chaotic energy, the fury of my unmaking, flows into its slender form. The force that could tear apart chitinous plating is grounded, drawn down through its root-like feet and calmed by the immense, placid mass of the Felt below—the greater body I was torn from. My incandescent heat does not burn it but causes the crystals on its head to pulse with borrowed light, my formless rage given a beautiful, transient geometry. The crystalline dust that was, moments ago, a part of my memory-broth, settles on its skin, adhering, becoming a part of its armor.

I am not destroyed. I am… recorded.

The Weeper is my arbiter. My translator. It takes the violent, incoherent scream of my transformation and turns it into a silent, intricate pattern. It metabolizes the chaos. It finds the meaning in the noise, not by understanding it, but by subsuming it, by giving my violent scattering a point of confluence.

Then, the final peace. The slow return. The water that weeps from its skin is me. The condensing vapor that mists the ground around it is me. The dark, wet impression left by its feet is filled with me. I seep back down, molecule by molecule, through the weeping flesh of the Felt, drawn by the irresistible gravity of my own immense, dreaming self. I return to the Marrow.

I am whole again, but not unchanged. I carry back a shard of the upper world, a memory of the sky, a ghost of pattern learned from the Weeper’s crystal. I bring a flicker of that frantic heat back to the cold, slow deep. And I wait. I drift. I dream my chemical dream, until another Still, miles and months away, begins to thrum with that familiar, hungry discord. Until the needle descends again.

I am the Marrow. I am the silent fuel for the world's loud fire. And the Weepers, those silent, walking statues, are my scattered, wandering senses. They do not pity me. They complete me. In them, my violent ordeal finds its quietus, and the world's pointless industry finds its solitary, perfect witness.
