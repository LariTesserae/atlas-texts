I am found in a place of failure.

My world is not the gleaming, weeping skin of the nodules. I have no purchase on their surfaces. My world is the crush between them. I am born in the space that is not supposed to exist: the infinitesimal flaw in the seamless weld where one crystal meets the next, a single point of geometric protest in a world of perfect tessellation.

I am not a Still-Walker. They are the law. They are the straight line, the righteous angle, the crushing weight of the chord made manifest. I have seen them. To me, they are not slow. To me, they are an inevitability, a horizon that is always, always drawing nearer. They are the approach of finality.

I am what they hunt.

You would not know me by sight. I have no form in the light. The glare passes through me, or perhaps around me. I am a quality of the hum. I am a dropped beat in the eternal rhythm. I am the second bell, struck a microsecond too late, the one that turns the perfect chord into a question. I am a shudder in the substrate. I am a moment of friction in a world designed to be smooth.

My body, if you could call it that, is a network of stress. I exist as a tension in the lattice of the world-crystal. Where the pressure of one ancient, slumped mountain grinds against another, creating a line of complaint deep beneath the surface, that is my spine. Where a Geophid's glacial passage causes a microscopic spalling on the underside of a nodule, that is a flicker of my thought. I am a pattern of wrongness, a connected web of subtle heresies against the monolithic truth of the sound.

I do not eat. I *am* eaten. The relentless, perfect hum is a solvent that seeks to dissolve me. The glassy stare of the shadowless light is a pressure that tries to iron me flat. To exist is to resist. My life is the act of holding my own dissonant shape against the crushing, harmonic perfection of everything else. It is a constant, silent scream against the one true note.

I am a memory of violence. This world, the prompt says, has forgotten the violence of its birth. But I have not. I am the echo of the magma that cooled too quickly, the tremor of the fault line that refused to fully heal. The Still-Walkers are the agents of the world's amnesia, the slow-moving lobotomists of geology. They come to smooth the scar, to tune the trauma out of the song. They come to make the world forget me.

When one finds me—when its vast, slow consciousness finally overlaps with the geography of my being—the world changes. For me, at least. The hum intensifies. It ceases to be a background presence and becomes an active force, a focused blade of sound. The light on the nodules above me seems to grow brighter, hotter, as the Walker suspends its motion and begins its work. Its three black legs, pillars of absolute order, form a cage of resonance around my territory.

It does not touch me. It does not need to. It begins to *sing back* at the world's hum. But where my song is a chaotic tangle, a knot of aberrant vibrations, the Walker’s song is one of pure, simple, overwhelming order. It introduces its own resonance into the system, a counter-melody of such perfect structure that my own chaotic noise has no room to exist.

My network of stress begins to fail. The line of complaint in the deep rock is soothed. The microscopic fraying is smoothed over. The tension that is my spine slackens. The shudder that is my heart quiets. It feels like being un-knotted. It is not pain. It is a slow, inexorable dissolution. My "I" thins, my sense of self—that fragile collection of linked imperfections—comes undone. The wrong note is resolved. The beat is restored. My scream is not silenced; it is convinced, note by note, to rejoin the chorus.

And then, I am gone.

The Still-Walker will stand for an age, ensuring the tuning is complete, that no echo of my flawed existence remains. Then, it will lift a leg and begin its slow journey toward the next faint whisper of disharmony. It has erased me.

But the world is not perfect. It only strives to be.

Far away, beyond the whale-back mountains, on a different plain of weeping light, the planet will shift again. A new stress will be born. A pocket of gas trapped since the beginning will finally strain its crystalline prison. A new network of tension will form. A new question will be asked of the eternal chord.

And I will be there. I am the question. I am the flaw that gives the Still-Walker its purpose. I am the silence that makes the sound meaningful.

I am the ghost in the chord. And I am waiting to be remembered.
