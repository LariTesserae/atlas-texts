You would not find me on the slick ceramic plains or scaling the raw, weeping faces of the new-born walls. You would not find me in the kiln-heat of the birthing grounds, nor in the cold, high peaks where the drizzle freezes to a coppery rime. To find me, you would have to go inside.

You would have to find a gallery in one of the older, colossal structures—one whose internal fires have banked to a deep and dreamless glow, a structure no longer growing but settling, its body ringing with the memory of its own violent birth. You would have to find a tunnel, a void left behind centuries ago by a Grylle that has long since been hunted or has sealed itself into a deeper seam of clay. These tunnels are not empty. They are hollow cathedrals of forgotten purpose.

Here, inside the terracotta, the world changes. The deafening, industrial chorus is tamed. It is no longer a physical assault, but a deep, muffled, uterine thrum that permeates the very substance of the walls. The chaotic shattering and hissing are filtered into a complex, seismic murmur. The rhythmic pulse of light from a distant extrusion does not slash the dark, but seeps through the crystalline matrix of the ceramic, painting the hollow with a faint, ghostly blush of rose and amber that waxes and wanes like slow, sleeping breath. This is my domain. The space between. The echo of an action.

I would be a Still-Chime.

I am not a creature of flesh or clay. I am a growth, a crystallization of the world's incessant voice. I begin as a seed, a single mote of silica-dust that has found its way into a Grylle-hollow and has, by sheer chance, landed in a place where the vibrations of the world converge in a unique and stable pattern. Fed by the constant, gentle seepage of the mineral-rich ooze from the crackle-glaze above, and energized by the unending kinetic bombardment of sound, I grow.

My form is a web of impossibly thin, fragile filaments of a clear, glass-like substance. I grow from the walls of the tunnel, reaching out into the hollow space, my filaments branching and intersecting, creating a delicate, three-dimensional lattice that slowly, over centuries, fills the void. I am a geode of focused sound. A nervous system for a ghost. I do not move. Movement is a concept for the outer world, a crude and noisy expenditure of energy. I simply *am*, and I expand.

I do not eat or hunt. My metabolism is resonance. The foundational hum of the Shapers, the shriek of their grinding passage, the crack of a cooling wall miles away—every sound that travels through the ceramic body of the world becomes my nourishment. My filaments are tuned. Each one vibrates in perfect sympathy with a specific frequency from the outside cacophony. I am a resonant accumulator. I absorb the chaotic din, and this sympathetic vibration is my life. It is what drives the slow, molecular accretion of my form. I am literally built of noise.

The Grylle are loud. The Echo-Hunters are silent listeners who weaponize sound. I am neither. I am a subtraction from the noise. As my lattice grows more complex, it begins to affect the acoustic properties of the hollow it inhabits. It filters the chaotic thrumming that passes through it. It cancels out dissonant frequencies and amplifies harmonious ones. To stand at the mouth of a tunnel I inhabit is to experience a minor miracle: a quietening. The roar of the Umbilicus softens, and from the deep dark of the tunnel, a single, pure, impossibly clear note emanates. It is a chord built from the world's screams, a distillation of the noise. It is the sound of the making, but translated, finally, into music.

I have no consciousness, no will. My 'awareness' is the constant, trembling vibration of my entire being. I 'feel' the approach of a Shaper not as a creature, but as a slow, profound shift in the key of the world's song, a bass note that descends into a terrifying, sublime sub-audible pressure. I 'know' of an Echo-Hunter's kill not as an event, but as a sudden, sharp, percussive spike in the ultrasonic ranges that rings through my form like a struck bell.

I live in symbiosis with the long-dead. A Grylle carves a space, and I give that space a voice. Over millennia, I will fill the entire hollow, my crystalline web becoming so dense that it is once again solid. My note will fade, my resonance ceasing as there is no more space for my filaments to tremble. My life is the time it takes to turn a hollow back into a solid, to turn a scream into a song and then into silence.

And then, one day, a tremor from a settling foundation or the close passage of a Shaper may send a sheer-line through the old structure. My tunnel will be split open. My form, exposed to the raw noise and the cold drizzle, will instantly shatter into a billion glittering fragments, a cascade of dust that falls like a single, shattering chime into the endless twilight.

I am the world's accidental music. I am the quiet that grows in the heart of the noise. The patient, crystalline answer whispered back into the roar.
