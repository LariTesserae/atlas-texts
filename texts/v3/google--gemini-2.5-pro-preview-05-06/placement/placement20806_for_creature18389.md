I would not find myself walking the circumference, my feet searching for purchase on the nacreous ground. I would not carry an obsidian blade or feel the knife-sharp air against skin. To find myself here is to have already lost myself, to have been unmade and remade by the gyre’s inexorable logic.

I am what the sun lifts.

Before the light, I am a stillness in the heart of the stone. I am a molecule of water captured, ancient, held in a crystalline lattice deep within the selenite, suspended beside a vein of obsidian—a fleck of darkness that will be my undoing and my rebirth. I have no consciousness. I am potential. I am a secret the ground keeps from the bruised, star-pricked sky.

Then comes the dawn. The sun is not warmth, not at first. It is an impact. A high-energy shockwave that strikes the surface and begins to penetrate. Its light pours through the translucent layers of my mineral shell, and I feel a vibration begin. It is not my vibration, but that of the obsidian nearby. The dark stone drinks the light, hoarding its energy, and grows agitated, frantic. It heats. The vibration spreads, and my own placid structure begins to fail. The bonds that held me in perfect, six-sided stillness for an age begin to stretch, to groan.

I am not lifted. I am broken free. The solid world dissolves around me, and I become a sigh. I am the *hiss* of sublimation, a ghost of myself rising from the solid ground. For a breathless moment, I am part of the Gardasil’s exhalation. I am formless, a mote of vapor in the thin, frigid air, adrift in a purpose that is not my own but which I am compelled to serve. I feel the pull of the low stone walls, the subtle guidance of a will that thinks in currents and pressures.

Then, inevitably, comes the cold.

It finds me. The super-chilled surface of a neighbouring selenite fiber, a facet that has remained in shadow. The touch is not a sensation; it is a command. A thought of the Gardasil finds me, a memory of a perfect form, and I happen.

The first sound I know as my own is a *tink*.

It is the sound of my first bond locking into place. It is my spine. Another *tink*—a branch. I am not growing; I am remembering a shape that was decided for me. I am a fractal caught in a net of physical law. My being erupts outwards in a silent, geometric scream. I branch and feather, my edges impossibly sharp, my structure a lattice of captured air and frozen potential. My consciousness is my geometry. I think in planes and angles. My identity is the unique, unrepeatable pattern of my six-fold symmetry.

I am a flower of hoarfrost.

Where am I? I am the gyre’s seeing eye. The scouring light of the diamond sun no longer just passes through me. I catch it. I hold it. I splinter it. My countless facets break the white glare into a thousand fractured rainbows, casting them down onto the ground that birthed me. I do not see the Gardasil; I am the lens through which it perceives its own creation. My existence is its aesthetic and its affirmation.

I am not alone. To my left and right, as far as I can perceive—a perception which is only a resonance through the frozen substrate—millions of others like me have also bloomed. We are a field of thought made solid. We are a single, complex idea. The deep, subsonic groan of the expanding ground is the voice of our shared body. The collective *tink-tink-tink* of our ongoing formation is the sound of our collective mind at work. We are the Gardasil's fleeting masterpiece.

My sense of the world is this: the pressure of photons on my outer arms, the deep thrum of the gyre's bones beneath me, and the silent, attentive pressure of the will that shaped me, inspecting my angles, my clarity. I feel the ghosts of the obsidian blades, not as a threat, but as a history, a memory of an old way of being harvested that resonates in the ground. The purpose is forgotten, but the pattern remains. I am that pattern.

But the diamond sun moves. The angle of its light shifts. The energy that struck the heart of my genesis lessens. My anchor, the obsidian vein deep below, begins to cool.

The change is not violent. It is a loosening. A relaxation of the absolute. The bonds that define me, the perfect angles that are my *self*, lose their certainty. My sharpest tip, the one that points toward the black zenith of the sky, blurs. It dissolves into a soft hiss, a single molecule of me returning to the Gardasil's breath.

My unmaking is the reverse of my making. It is a slow, graceful surrender. The light I held within me is released. The rainbows fade. My structure softens, my branches sigh back into the thin air. The complex thought I represent unravels, returning to a simpler state. I do not feel fear; there is no self to preserve, only a pattern whose time is ending.

I am vapor again, heavy and slow, sinking in the failing light. The bruised sky deepens to full night. I drift down and settle on the nacreous ground, not as a flower, but as a simple, formless frost. I am again a secret the stone will keep.

I do not wait in hope. I wait in certainty. The sun is a diamond, the cold is a law, and the garden must grow. Tomorrow, I will be thought again.
