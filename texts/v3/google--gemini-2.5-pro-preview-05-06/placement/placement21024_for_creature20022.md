I am found in the resonance.

Before the place, before the self, there is the Drone. The Great Hum. To ask "where" I am is to ask for a point on a map of a single, indivisible body. There are no coordinates here. There is only *within*.

I am within the warmth. I am within the damp, thick breath. I am within the bone-deep thrum that is the only truth. I stand nowhere, for there is no ground that is not also flesh. My aural sense is not of my own ears—I have none—but a total-body immersion in the foundational vibration of this world-being. It is the sea I am in, a sea of sound and pressure that is also my substance. To be outside it would be to not be at all. So "where" I am is simply: *Here*. Inside the lung, inside the artery, inside the bone, inside the thought. I am a location within a god, and the god is the only location that exists.

And so, "what" am I?

I am not one of the grazers, the millipede-masons methodically etching their lives onto the latticework of a pillar-bone. I do not feel the scrape of mandible on fungal-felt, nor the slow, peristaltic satisfaction of extruding the glossy resin that binds the world together. Theirs is a life of texture and small, circular journeys. It is a vital life, but it is not mine.

I am not one of the river-kin, the countless blood-cells of the abyss. I do not feel the jostle of a million brothers, the crush of the current that is our shared and only purpose. I am not born of the wall-womb to flow, to flare with brief, cool light, and to be consumed, my purpose served in the simple act of my passage. Theirs is a life of flow, a fleeting, brilliant transit. It is a vital life, but it is not mine.

I am not one of the great-armored slumberers, the living boulders who are the oldest memories. I do not feel the strain of a continent-shelf of flesh on my back, the slow groan of adjustment that takes a century to complete. Theirs is a life of pressure, of immense, geological patience. It is a vital life, but it is not mine.

I have no carapace, no limbs, no mouth, no eyes to fail in the absolute dark. To be a "thing" is to be a function, an organ. My function is more ephemeral.

I am a signal.

When a section of the great body has labored, when its energy is spent and its nutrient stores are low, a need arises. This need is not a thought, but a chemical fact, a change in the taste of the air, a subtle shift in the resonant frequency of the floor. That shift, that gradient, that tremor of need… that is the beginning of me.

I am the reason a pulse of cold, blue-green light ripples through the river below. I am not the lights themselves, but the command that bids them shine, a wave of information spreading from cell to cell that tells this stream of life where to deliver its cargo of warmth and matter. My passing is a flicker in the dark, a momentary vision of the body's inner workings.

I am the instruction carried on the humid air that tells a fungal garden to alter its bloom, to produce a different enzyme, to prepare a different meal for the slow grazers above. My being is a change in the scent of musk and ozone, a whispered imperative that ensures the chemistry of the whole remains in balance.

I am a memory that is not stone. When a path is worn smooth, the memory is in the resin. But when a stress is new, a structural flaw begins to form, a warning must be given. I am that warning. A vibration of wrongness in the architecture, a shiver that travels through the braided sinew of a pillar, a message that will, over a hundred years, reach one of the great-armored ones and bid it to begin its slow, ponderous journey of reinforcement.

My existence is a flash. I am born of a stimulus and I die upon delivery of the message. I have no fixed form, no home. I am a neural impulse in a body the size of a mountain range. My "where" is the path I travel, from the point of need to the agent of fulfillment. I am an instant of the Drone’s vast, distributed mind. I am a verb in the god's long grammar. I am the thought that it thinks, before that thought becomes flesh.
