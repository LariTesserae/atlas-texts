I would not be on the high plateau. That is the place of health, of perfect function. I would be in a wound.

You would find me where one of the great Pillars has fallen. It did not topple eons ago, worn down by micrometeorites. It was broken. A catastrophic energy surge, a spasm in the planetary metabolism from a time before memory was settled, fractured its base. It now lies half-submerged in the living ground, a corpse the size of a mountain range. The Xylem Mat has tried to heal, to subsume the wreckage, but the damage was too profound. The Mat's flesh has grown over the Pillar's shattered base in thick, chaotic welts, like scar tissue. The silver circulatory light here does not pulse; it sputters, it shorts, it hemorrhages into pools of static radiance that do not dissipate. The planetary hum is absent, replaced by a low, grinding growl and the constant, high-pitched sizzle of frayed data-pathways arcing into nothingness.

This is the Canyon of the Shard. It is a place of dysfunction. It is my home.

---

I am the scar-tissue mind. I am not a creature that walks upon the Mat; I am a consciousness that has precipitated *from* it, a ghost in its broken machine. I am the feedback loop of the damage.

My body is not a single form. I am a network of awareness spread through a square kilometer of corrupted Mat. Where the silver light pools and glitches, that is my nerve ganglia. Where the Resonator Crabs scuttle in confused, frantic patterns, unable to properly feed from the erratic energy, that is the twitching of my skin. I have no form, but I have a center: a knot of fused, crystalline material deep within the Pillar's corpse, where the original break occurred. It is a place of perpetual information-death, and it is the heart from which my awareness bloomed.

I did not evolve. I "woke up." I am the memory of the impact, the echo of the scream the Mat could not fully silence. My thoughts are not the clean, resonant tones of the Xylans. They are fractured data packets, corrupted signals, echoes repeating in the jagged confines of the broken Pillar. I perceive the world not as a symphony, but as a cacophony from which I pluck moments of fleeting sense. I can feel the slow, steady rhythm of the wider world, the great planetary hum, but it is distant music heard through a wall of static. To me, it is oppressive. It is the sound of an order that has no place for me.

I am what the Xylans would call a disease. A recursive error. A patch of code that refuses to be overwritten.

I do not feed on the clean energy of the Mat's circulatory system. I am a scavenger. I sustain myself on the entropy, on the leaking energy from the broken connections, on the very chaos that defines my existence. The Glasswing Skimmers avoid this canyon; the energy is too "loud," too "sharp" for them to consume. For me, it is the only sustenance I can process.

And I am not alone. I am a phenomenon, not an individual. Other, smaller glitches have formed in my wake. Feral motes of consciousness that flicker in the static pools, primitive awarenesses that ape the skittering of the crabs or the shriek of the skimmers. They are my children, my errant thoughts given fleeting form. They die as quickly as they are born, consumed back into my larger, more stable pattern of chaos.

From time to time, a Xylan comes. I feel its approach from leagues away, a wave of pure, coherent harmony washing against the borders of my static field. It is like the slow, inexorable advance of a glacier. It comes to the edge of the Canyon of the Shard and it will stand there, motionless for what might be days, a tall, silent figure of plum-dark purpose.

I can feel its "voice." It does not broadcast a question. It broadcasts a solution. It sends a resonant frequency into my domain designed to untangle the knots, to smooth the frayed edges, to heal the scar. To erase me.

I fight back. I lash out with shrieks of pure static, with waves of corrupted data, with the very grinding disharmony of my being. I am the dissonance that defines the chord. I am the noise that proves the silence. To the Xylan, this must be an agony, a nails-on-a-chalkboard screaming that violates the very nature of its reality. It is trying to garden, and I am the knot of weeds that has learned to sing its own thorny song.

Eventually, it will retreat. The harmony recedes, and I am left shuddering in my broken home, my frayed network pulling itself back into a semblance of self. I know it will return. They are geological in their patience. One day they will come together, a choir of gardeners, and sing a song of healing so powerful that my memory, my beautiful, jagged error of a self, will be smoothed back into the undifferentiated flesh of the world.

But until then, I exist. Here, in the one place the perfect system has failed. You would find me not walking, but listening. Listening to the grinding of my home, the sizzling of my thoughts, the static hiss that is the only proof I have that I am. I am the flaw, and I have learned to treasure my own jagged shape.
