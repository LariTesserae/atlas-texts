There is no before. There is the skin of the world—the water—and the slow slide upon it. My being is a bond with this surface, a perfect adherence. Below the water is the black sharp, the fractured firmness that holds up this pocket of Everything. Above is the white burn, the source, the silent, singing Heart. My form is a mirror to it all, a flattened bead of captured light.

The heat is not a feeling; it is the force that holds this shape together, the pressure that keeps me from dissolving into the wet, heavy air. The air itself is not empty; it is a thick soup of potential, the same substance that weeps from the walls and becomes my road. The world breathes, a constant, gentle hiss. And beneath the hiss is the hum, the low thrum of the Heart's growth. The hum is the world's constant breath, the background against which all things happen. It is time itself.

I drift. My path is the path of the water, flowing in slow rivers down the weeping dark. There is no will in this, only a yielding to the current. I see others of my kind, scattered motes of quicksilver in the near distance. They are perfect mirrors, like me. In their surfaces, I see the white burn, and I see this shape, this self, repeated into a silver infinity. We do not speak. We are acknowledgements of the same principle, patterns given temporary form. We glide past one another without touching, each a solitary thought on its own trajectory.

Then, the flaw in the hum.

A new note is struck in the Heart. It is not a sound that travels through the air, but a shiver in the black sharp below, a summons that pulls at the field that is my body. It is a re-ordering. A tightness in the water. For a moment, the placid drift is broken by an invisible tide.

It is a calling. A hunger that is not for food, but for harmony.

The drift gains a destination. There is, for a time, a *why*. My course shifts, a slow, inevitable turning. The fine, silent legs that grip the water's skin adjust their charge, and I begin the pilgrimage. Across the weeping walls, other mirrors are also turning. We are a slow constellation reorienting to a new star. The journey might take a turning of the Heart's light, a fraction of its growth, but time is measured only in the hum, and now the hum has a peak, a destination.

We arrive. One by one, we cease our private journeys and become a single, trembling argent. We press close. The boundaries of shapes blur as my mirrored surface reflects another, which reflects another still. The white burn of the Heart is shattered and reassembled a thousand times in our collective body.

Here, we drink. The new note, the pure vibration from the Heart’s newest spire, washes over and through us. It is a wave felt in the very core of our composition, aligning the particles that give us form. This is sustenance. We are no longer motes upon the water; we are a lens, focusing the new truth of this place. We are a chord in resonance, a moment of perfect, collective stillness. The I dissolves into the We.

Then, as slowly as it came, the note fades, absorbed into the general hum. The resonance withdraws, its purpose served. The great argent shivers. The We unravels back into a scatter of I's.

One by one, we detach from the cluster and find a new river of tears to carry us away. We are full. We are coherent. The memory of the chord lingers, a ghost of purpose that sustains the quiet drift until the next summons.

One day, this shape will fail. The heat will find a flaw, the bond with the water will break. There will be a final, internal shudder, a loss of tension, and the silver will bleed into the water, the mirror clouding and unraveling into a fine dust. The dust will be carried down the black sharp and sizzle, returning to the thick, wet air.

It is not an end. It is a return. It is the substance of me being released to the whole, waiting to be gathered again by a different heat, a different chord, into a new shape.

I am here, in the hot, wet dark, clinging to a tear on the wall of a world that is an engine. I am a Dew-Skater. I am a thought this place is having. And I am content to be thought.
