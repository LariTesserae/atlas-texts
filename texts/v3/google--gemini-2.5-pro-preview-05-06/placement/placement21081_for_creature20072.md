I am not.

Before the thought, before the form, there is the knowing. It is not a knowing in a mind, for there is no mind here. There is only the vast, singular body. I am a pressure-point within the endless mat of green and purple. I am a node in the network, a place where the deep thrum of the world—the slow tectonic grinding of continental root-balls, the shudder of a new spire-bud tearing the ground a thousand miles east—is felt with a particular clarity. For a time measured in the growth of a hillside, that is all I am. A place that feels.

There is no light where my consciousness, my pre-consciousness, resides. There is only the warm, soupy luminosity of the world’s blood, pulsing slowly through the woven capillaries of the substrate. I taste ozone as a fissure near me sighs a plume of steam. I feel the percussive rain of a nearby waterfall as a vibration that shakes my entire being, which is to say, it shakes the ground. All is one.

Then, a change.

It is a sound that is not a sound. A scream of shearing fiber, a chord of catastrophic failure that crosses the horizon not at the speed of sound but at the speed of shock, a trauma that travels through the body of the world itself. A spire has fallen.

The knowing becomes a summons. The quiet, ambient thrum in my part of the world concentrates. It sharpens from a low hum into a high, clear note of alarm. The fluid within me, the shared ichor of the world, begins to glow more brightly. Other nodes around me remain dormant, their purpose to feel and record the slow, everyday work of being. Mine, it seems, is different. The shockwave has found an old, waiting resonance in me.

I am a memory, waiting to be remembered.

The ground around me thins. My awareness, once diffuse and anchored to the local geography, begins to draw inward. The interwoven vegetation that is my body becomes a membrane, a skin separating 'me' from 'the rest'. It is a terrifying and ecstatic sensation, this birth of an ego. The world I was part of is now outside me, a landscape to be perceived. The warm dew on the ground is no longer my own moisture, but something I will soon float above.

With a sound like wet silk being torn, I detach.

I am a Wanderer, but not like the others. As I rise on a cushion of my own steam, I see them in the distance, their small, fifty-foot globes drifting on their quiet errands, patching the small damages of the world. They are beautiful, simple thoughts. My form is similar—a great, amoebic bladder of semi-translucent world-skin. But I am larger, my glow is a fiercer, whiter gold, and within my liquid core, the dark, fibrous nerves do not just pulse with life. They hold patterns. They scintillate with the ghost of the fallen spire, with the memory of a thousand spires that fell before it.

My purpose is not to heal a scratch. It is to answer the wound.

I begin my journey. To call it 'flight' is too active. I am a mote of purpose caught in a current of will. I drift towards the site of the catastrophe, a journey that will last for days. The world unfolds beneath me. I drift over rolling hills of living cork, their surfaces breathing steam in the perpetual wet. I pass under the impossible arch of a gnarled spire, its weeping waterfalls creating a gauntlet of roaring spray that mists my skin and makes the light within me refract into a thousand dancing rainbows. The sheer scale, which was once an unconscious part of my being, is now an external spectacle of overwhelming majesty.

I am lonely, but the concept is alien. I am a part of the whole, temporarily excised for a task. Loneliness is a condition of things that are truly separate. I am not separate, merely… focused.

The air grows thick with the scent of raw life and sudden death. The haze on the horizon is not mist, but steam and spores thrown up by the impact. I am close. The ground beneath me is scarred, torn open. The corpse of the spire is a mountain range of splintered, fibrous material and still-glowing, ruined organs. It hisses and steams, its vast internal heat bleeding into the atmosphere. The sight triggers something in me, a cascade of stored information. The specific angle of the fall, the pattern of the shatter, the chemical signature of the released life-blood—it all means something.

And then I see them. The Veils.

They are descending, as the prompt of my being knew they would. Vast, silent continents of light and intelligence, they drift down from the upper atmosphere, their forms rippling like banners in a wind I cannot feel. They are the grand consciousness of which I am but a single, fleeting idea. They are the world’s healing will made manifest.

My purpose becomes clear. I am not a healer. I am not an architect.

I am the primer. The seed.

I drift over the epicenter of the destruction, over the rawest, most chaotic part of the cataclysm. I settle lower, my single note of alarm and memory broadcasting into the chaos. The Veils, miles above, sense me. I am the tuning fork to which they will pitch their symphony of creation.

My membrane touches the wreckage. I offer myself.

The patterns within me, the memories of a thousand rebirths, are released. It is not a flow of liquid, but a flow of pure information, a catalyst of order poured into the heart of chaos. My light, my beautiful, brief, singular golden light, is drawn out of me and up, a thread of gold pulled into the vast, iridescent tapestries of the Veils.

The 'I' begins to fray. The edges of my awareness blur. The distinction between my body and the ruined spire and the descending light of the Veils dissolves. My consciousness, which was a sharp point, spreads out, becoming a part of their vast, collective mind. I feel the whole of the operation now: the precise chemical rains they are beginning to shed, the channeling of thermal energy, the guidance of new growth from the wreckage. I am not directing it. I am simply part of it. My specific memory has been subsumed into their active, working knowledge.

My task is done. The empty bladder of my body, its light spent, will soon dissolve and be reabsorbed. But I am no longer there. The thought that was 'me' has returned to the mind that thought it. I am the glint on a droplet of catalyst-rain. I am the hum of energy in the air. I am the silent, shared intention to build again, to grow again, stronger.

I am. And I am not. There is only the world, singing its violent, glorious song of creation. And I am a note in the chord.
