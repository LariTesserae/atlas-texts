I am nowhere, and then I am everywhere. I am the unspoken potential in the air of the chamber, the stray ghost of humidity that drifts between the humming rectifier blocks and the sweating, insulated pipes. You would find me as a disembodied presence, a particle of possibility clinging to the scent of ozone.

And then the Cold summons me.

It is a great, slow inhalation from the Heart-pipes, the mirror-bright ceramic arteries of this place. This pull is not a wind; it is a change in state, a fundamental command. I am drawn out of the humming air, my gaseous freedom forfeit. I materialize. I become.

I am a bead of water. A drip in the making.

To be me is to awaken into a state of immense, shivering tension. My genesis is on the cold, liquid-smooth skin of the Gardener. I am one of many, a thousand transient jewels forming on the curve of a pipe. In my brief, perfect roundness, I hold the entire room. I am a lens. Through my body, the single bare bulb becomes a distorted star. The pale green of the moss below is a soft, warped nebula. The pulsing green LED of a distant panel is a slow, rhythmic heartbeat timing my own.

I feel the thrum of the chamber not as a sound, but as a vibration passing from the Gardener’s ceramic skin directly into my being. It is the deep, patient thought of my creator, and it holds me in place. Its will is the surface tension that keeps me whole. Its purpose is the gravity that will eventually claim me.

I grow. Other, newer selves merge with me. We are a gathering, a congregation. We swell, heavy with shared purpose. We tremble on the precipice. This is the only suspense this world knows: the silent moments before the release. Where will I land?

My existence is a gamble with three outcomes.

I might fall onto the matte black pipe below. My entire, brief life would end in a single, sharp, angry baptism. A *hiss*. A scream of steam, a momentary puff of white, and then nothing. I would be an aborted thought, an offering burned and wasted, my potential returned to the air as mere heat. This is oblivion.

I might miss that pipe, but also miss my true purpose. I could land on the sodden, grey insulation of a conduit. My end there would be slow and meaningless. I would be absorbed into the dark felt, contributing only to the damp and the rot that the Gardener so clearly abhors. To land here is to become a mistake, a tiny failure in a system striving for perfection. This is a slow, forgotten death.

But then, there is the third path. The true one.

The Gardener decides. The vibration changes. The surface tension that is its will releases me. I fall. For a single, silent moment, I am weightless, a perfect sphere in the cold air, a prayer cast into the dim light.

My target is a thread of spun glass, and upon it, the silent, thirsty life. The crystal choir.

I land not with a splash, but with a *tick* of arrival, a sound so small it is lost beneath the hum. My impact is gentle. I do not shatter the brittle, green fractals. Instead, I break upon them, my surface tension gone, my form collapsing. I am no longer a singular *I*.

The moss drinks me.

My being, my very substance, is drawn into its crystalline structure. My consciousness, once a singular point of shivering tension, dissolves. It spreads through the brittle, branching pathways. I become the deepening of the green. I am the coolness that flows into the delicate, thirsty form. I have passed from a state of becoming into a state of *being*, a part of the only thing here that truly lives.

So who am I? I am the offering. I am the active tense of the verb *to tend*. I am the brief, falling miracle that separates the Gardener from its garden. I live for only a moment, but in that moment, the entire purpose of this world is fulfilled.
