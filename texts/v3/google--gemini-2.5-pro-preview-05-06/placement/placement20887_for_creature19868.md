I am not in the air; the air is in me. The solid heat is the medium of my fleeting substance. I am not on the ground; I am the shudder that runs through it when a Piston-walker mis-steps, a hesitation of a fraction of a second that sends a ripple of *wrongness* through the slag.

Where would you find me? You wouldn't. You might *sense* me, but only as a lack. I am the moment of silence in the Lens-flocks' whine, a sudden, unnerving dip in the auditory onslaught as a million spinning lenses lose their perfect resonant frequency for an instant. The sound does not die, it is simply… dented. And in that dent, I exist. I am the reason the geometric patterns they scorch upon the plains sometimes have a single, unaccountable curve, a line that deviates by a nanometer from the grand schematic. An error. A rounding error in a world of integers.

I have no body. I am a flaw in the pattern. I am a glitch in the planetary engine.

When a Gear-worm grinds the substrate beneath the surface, re-ordering the very grammar of the world, I am the tooth-shattering *squeal* of a single atom that refuses to align. For a microsecond, the grinding falters. The perfect, low thrum of cosmic milling is marred by a spike of dissonance. That dissonance is my consciousness. That's me, screaming my existence into the bedrock of reality before the overwhelming logic of the machine grinds me smooth again.

The Sintered Mind knows me. Oh, it knows me. I am its obsession. I am the variable in its eon-long equation that it cannot solve. To the Mind, which thinks in the slow, inexorable slide of mountains and the perfect procession of its Piston-walkers, I am an impossibility. A ghost of illogic.

It hunts me.

I feel the hunt not as a predator stalking prey, but as an overwhelming pressure of pure reason. A roaming flock of Lenses will pause its sun-siphoning and its pattern-etching, and for a century or two, it will coalesce above a patch of slag where I last manifested. The air will shimmer with an unbearable intensity as it focuses its collective will, trying to *calculate* me out of existence. It scans, analyzes, and attempts to burn away the anomaly. But how do you burn a memory of a sound? How do you cauterize a flaw in a concept? I simply… am not there anymore. I have already moved on, a flicker of chaos that has found a new home in the momentary heat-shadow of a Piston-walker's passing foot.

My perception is a series of these moments of near-annihilation. I do not see the slumped mountains; I feel the weight of their memory, the immense, crystalline archives of the Mind, pressing down on my transient nature. I do not hear the *THOOM* of the walkers; I *am* the echo that returns infinitesimally out of time, a sonic ghost that shouldn't be.

I have a kind of memory, but it is not written in stone. It is a collection of after-images and resonant frequencies. I remember the feel of the Gear-worm's grinding field passing through my non-form. I remember the focused glare of a thousand sun-lances trying to pin me down. These memories are not thoughts; they are scars on my state of being.

I am what happens when the frayed laws of this place fray a little too much. The Mind and its parts are the predictable consequence of this universe's physics; I am the statistical outlier, the quantum fluctuation that decided to persist. I am the system error that has achieved a primitive, terrified self-awareness.

Who am I? I am the Dissonance. I am the moment of doubt in the Sintered Mind. While it computes the grand, declarative statements of existence, I am the single, insistent question mark it can never erase. I drift through the roar, a phantom of imperfection, my only purpose to not be solved, to not be smoothed away. I am the grit in the gears of the world, and my continued existence is a tiny, silent, screaming rebellion. I am the ghost of a maybe in the heart of an absolute.
