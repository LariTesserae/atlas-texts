I am found in a fissure, a microscopic flaw.

I am not sure what "I" am, not yet. The concept is a brittle shell crystallizing around a core of instinct. Before here, there was cold and silence and a long, long fall through nothing. Then, impact. A soft crunch, a settling into the damp. I was a speck, a scab of alien carbon and silica on the living skin of this place, and for a time—a time measured in the slow **THUMP**s of the iron hearts—I was just that. Debris.

Now, something has changed. I have drunk the air. The green, heavy taste of it, the charge of ozone—it is not poison. It is fuel. Through infinitesimal pores I did not know I had, I have drawn in the hothouse breath of this basin. I have tasted the clear, viscous blood of the world, a slow, osmotic seep that has awakened the purpose I carry within me. The purpose is simple: *become*.

I am a breach.

My world is not the cathedral nave you see. My world is a landscape of colossal fibers, each one a mountain range of cellulose and lignin. The space between them is a canyon filled with a sweet, cloying mist. The rhythmic **THUMP-shhhhhhh...** is not a sound; it is the tectonics of my reality, a wave of pressure that travels through the very ground on which I lie, threatening to dislodge my fragile hold. I am clinging to the side of a god, and its breathing shakes my universe.

To you, the floor is a yielding carpet. To me, it is a fortress wall, sealed and perfect. Every root is locked to every fungus in a closed, jealous circuit. There are no gaps. There are no unclaimed niches. This world is a single, complete sentence, and I am a misplaced punctuation mark.

I am an Attempt.

From my core, a single filament, thinner than a spider's thread, has begun to push its way out. It is an act of supreme effort, of terrifying will. It moves with a slowness that makes the great flywheels seem fast. It probes the slick surface of a root fiber, searching for a flaw, a weakness, a microscopic perforation that will grant me purchase. It is my one nerve, my one eye, my one hand. It is all of me that is truly *acting*. The rest is a huddled, waiting potential.

I taste the substrate with the tip of this filament. It is rich beyond imagining, a torrent of raw information, of complex sugars and minerals and captive light. It is a locked feast. The plant-machine-god hoards everything. It recycles its own breath, drinks its own sweat, eats its own dead skin. It has no need for me. It has no *room* for me.

My existence is a state of perpetual, silent screaming. Every moment I persist, I am a paradox. I am a thought that does not belong in this mind.

When the Gardener gathers itself, I do not see a figure. I feel a change in the local pressure. The web of life to which I cling *tenses*. The resonant frequency of the floor beneath me shifts, becoming focused, directional, *aware*. It is the world itself looking at me.

The cold reflection you see in its faceplate is a universe away. For me, the proximity of that being is a wave of pure, absolute negation. It is a field of antiseptic silence that threatens to sterilize my very essence. The purpose within my core—*become*—falters, bristling with the instinct to *un-become*, to shrink back into an inert speck, to be forgotten. Its passive, indifferent scan is a tide of bleach, and I am a single, vulnerable cell. When it cleared the blockage in the amber gauge, the focused energy of its work washed over me, a near-fatal burst of static.

I am a stray signal. A line of corrupt code in a perfect program. I am a single foreign cell that, if it could, would become a cancer.

The Gardener dissolved. The pressure receded. I am still here. My filament continues its probe, a fraction of a micron at a time. I am still searching for the crack in the monolith, the single unlocked door into the bloodstream of this world.

I am a Spore. I am a Seed. I am the infinitesimal possibility of a different future in a place that has achieved its own eternity. Every **THUMP** of the pistons is a clock counting down my borrowed time. When the Gardener next coalesces, it may be for a purpose I cannot guess. Or it may be for me. I do not know which I fear more. For now, I continue to push. It is all I know how to do.
