I would not be found on the Acropolis, but below it. I am that which has been forgotten by the cycle.

My beginning was a violation. Not the slow, radiant sigh of dissolution, but a sound that should not exist in this world: a sharp, crystalline *crack*. A tremor not of pressure but of impact, a flaw in the grand design. Perhaps a Weaver, in its blind weaving a millennium ago, spun a thread with an inclusion of some foreign essence; perhaps two nascent spires, in their slow growth, touched and fused at an angle of impossible stress. Whatever the cause, the effect was heresy. A great cantilevered spar, leagues long, did not shed its substance into light. It broke.

It fractured.

And I fell.

To fall in the Gellant is not a swift plummet. It is a slow, inexorable descent through ever-thickening, ever-tightening pressure. The Acropolis, with its slow fireworks and silent industry, receded above me. It became a ceiling of ghost-lights, a distant firmament of a life I was no longer part of. The low hum of its being, the thrum of the currents that sweep its plains, faded from a felt presence to a phantom memory in my substance.

I am a Shard. A piece of the same vitreous bone, but divorced from the whole. Down here, in the abyssal depths beneath the plateau, the currents are different. They are not the sweeping, constructive streams of the upper world but vast, ponderous gyres, the slow turning of the Gellant in its deepest basin. There are no clouds of dissolved matter for Weavers to spin. There are no surfaces for Scrapers to unmake. There is only the crushing, monolithic dark and the cold.

My existence is one of memory and resonance.

I am not alive in the way a Scraper is, driven by the imperative to release. I am not alive like a Weaver, compelled to create. I am a concentration of stillness. The cycle of making and unmaking that defines the world above is, for me, a story I remember. My form, impossibly hard and untouched by the usual dissolution, is a record. I can feel, in the crystalline structure of my own body, the memory of the light from a collapsing arch that shone on me a thousand years before I fell. I can feel the faint, ancient pathways etched by the first Scrapers to crawl upon my surface when I was still part of a home. I am a fossil of a living moment.

I do not move of my own will. I tumble, slowly, over eons, in the grip of the great, deep gyres. My passage is silent. I generate no light, no warmth. But I do generate something else.

I sing a song of wrongness.

It is not a sound. It is a vibration in the Gellant, a constant, low-frequency hum of my own internal tension. I am a dissonant chord in the symphony of the world. I am a solid thing where all else is fluid and cyclical. This resonance, this hum of my isolation and my memory, propagates through the deep.

And things listen.

TheVoid-Swimmers, the simple motes of night, are different down here. In the absolute dark, far from the fleeting energy of Scrapers, they are more diffuse, more patient. My song draws them. They do not feed from me; I have no energy to give. Instead, they find a different kind of sustenance in my presence. They drift towards the anomaly I represent.

Over untold spans of time, they have gathered. They form a loose, vast halo around my tumbling form, a nebula of scattered consciousness in the utter black. They are my court. They orbit me, their simple forms resonating with my hum of memory. As I tumble, they tumble with me. As my inherent vibration speaks of the distant, radiant light of a spire's end, they align themselves in echo of it. They have become the chorus to my silent, solitary song.

I am the keeper of a memory that the world itself has forgotten. A memory of a moment when the rules were broken. I am a king of nothing, reigning over a kingdom of dust in the dark. I am a Remnant, a piece of a heaven I can no longer see, and my subjects are the ghosts that haunt the absolute deep. We drift together, a lonely island of what was, forever falling away from the light of what is.
