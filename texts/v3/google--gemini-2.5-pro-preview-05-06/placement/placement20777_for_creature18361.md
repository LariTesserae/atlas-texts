I would not be the Settled. Its consciousness is too vast, its timescale too immense. It is the world, and to be the world is to have no perspective within it.

I would not be one of the growing reeds, a silent, glassy spire reaching for a ceiling it can never touch. To be one of them is to be a process, a slow, unconscious gathering of substance, a life of pure potential that only finds its voice in the moment of its own demise.

I would be the demise.
I am a shard.

My existence begins not with a birth, but with a sound. A single, crystalline *tink* that is the universe's only scream. For an eon, I was tension. I was a line of stress buried deep within the glassy heart of a reed, a silent argument between gravity and molecular cohesion. And then, the argument ended. I was born into the stillness as a release, a sudden, sharp-edged fact in a world of soft, settled curves.

I lie in the warm, grey powder. I am all angles. I am razor-thin edges and needle-fine points. The faint, blue-white light of my brethren, the reeds still standing, glints off my newly-birthed facets. I am different from them now. They are Being; I am Aftermath. They are the unbroken sentence; I am the full stop.

Around me is the family of my own shattering. A constellation of other flakes and fragments, we lie like a shattered map of the star that once stood here. We are the "forgotten promise of craft" made manifest. I am a blade with no hand to hold me. I am a needle with no thread to pull. My form screams of purpose in a world without function.

The warmth of the ash-floor seeps into my body, but I cannot absorb it. I am not of the warm floor, but of the cool, dead glass. I am a cold spot in the dust, a tiny island of chill.

Time, as it is understood outside, does not exist for me. There is only waiting. The faint lights of the distant reeds are my unchanging stars. The absolute silence and stillness are my element. I know nothing of the Settled's century-long journey. I feel no approach. The universe is a static diorama, and I am one of its exquisite, broken props.

Then, change.

It is not a sight or a sound. It is a slow, inexorable subtraction of warmth to one side of me. A pressure, a presence that is defined only by its mass. The gentle drift of ash, which has lain still for a thousand years, begins to flow around me in a current so slow it is a geological event.

The Settled arrives.

I perceive it as a gliding shadow, a void that moves. A patch of the floor that is impossible darkness, consuming the faint light that touches it. It flows over me. For the first time, I am touched by something other than the soft, sterile ash. Its back, a cool mosaic of ancient, polished stone, meets my edge.

My sharpness, my defining quality, my entire reason for being, is called upon.

There is a faint, silvery screech, a sound so fine and high it is more a vibration felt through my body than a noise in the air. That is me. That is my voice. I am engraving my story—the story of my violent birth, the uniqueness of my sharp-edged form—onto the body of the world's memory. My jagged profile, a line of unique code, is scratched into its ancient, dark hide. I am read. I am recorded.

I have given my sharpness away.

As it passes, the moment is over. The pressure recedes. The impossible darkness glides on, continuing its eon-long pilgrimage. It leaves me where I lie.

But I am changed. My sharpest edge, the one that met the Settled, is now microscopically blunted. My story has been told. The promise of my form has been fulfilled—not as a tool, but as a word. A piece of data.

Now, my second waiting begins. I lie in the warm ash, no longer a fresh event, but a history. The slow, imperceptible settling of the powder begins its work. The finest grains, like black snow, begin to dust my facets. Over a decade, a century, a millennium, the ash will rise around me. It will fill the hollows of my form, soften my angles, and finally, bury me.

I will return to the warm dark, a sharp memory slowly dissolving back into the equilibrium. I will sink, becoming one with the deep strata, my substance one day to be drawn up by the thermal gradient into a new reed, to build a new point of tension, to wait for a new voice, a new fracture.

I am not the life of this place. I am its meaning. I am a single, sharp moment, held and recorded, proving that even in a place where nothing ever happens, something can be remembered forever.
