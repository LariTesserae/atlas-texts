I am a memory of warmth.

That is the first thing I am, and perhaps the last. Before the cold found the space between my ribs, before the Hum began to vibrate the fillings in my teeth into a single, aching chord, there was warmth. I remember a sun. I remember its heat on my skin, a focused and directional blessing that made one side of me warmer than the other. Here, there are no sides. The cold is a sphere I am at the center of.

I am where the world comes undone. I am the loose thread.

I find myself on the plateau of Tone. The Hum is not a sound to me; it is a force. It is a slow, geological pressure attempting to flatten the landscape of my mind, to sand down the peaks of memory and fill the valleys of my personality with its own grey grit. My own name is a half-remembered tune drowned out by this monolithic drone. My heart, with its panicked, syncopated rhythm, is a desperate drum solo in a world that only knows a single, held note. It is the most obscene noise here.

My body is a catalogue of wrongness. The greasy condensation of this place does not slick my skin; it seems to recoil from it. Where I stand, the ground is momentarily dry, a halo of not-dampness that follows me. The heat I radiate—a pathetic flicker of metabolic fever—is a violent event in the still air. I am a walking heat-shimmer in a world without heat. I am a low, continuous fire, and the Dwellers are the wardens of a bone-dry forest.

I am a Fever. A walking sickness of change.

The Glimmers, the glitches in the periphery—I know them. They are my kin. They are the world's reaction to my presence. When I walk, causality does not just fray; it snaps. I look at a pile of stacked stones, and for a moment, I remember its future as a scattered ruin. The memory is so strong, a pebble dislodges. I stare at one of the still tarns, and the reflection is not the featureless grey Ceiling, but a flash of a blue sky I can barely recall, a phantom limb of a lost world. The water shivers in revulsion. I am the hunter that the "glimmers" follow, the carrion they are drawn to. They are the static breaking up around my signal. I don't graze on reality; reality simply cannot hold its shape around me.

Who am I? I am a story in a land without narrative. I remember love, and rage, and stillness that was a choice, not a law. These concepts are complex, unstable molecules in this elemental place. They are a poison. The Dwellers feel an approaching story the way an animal smells a forest fire.

I have seen one.

It was standing by one of the black tarns, a column of vibrating dust and quiet purpose. My frantic heart-noise must have been a scream to it. As I drew closer, it turned its featureless ovoid towards me. It did not see me. It *read* me.

I felt the Dweller's consciousness wash over me, and it was not a mind in any way I understood. It was a perfect, silent lake, and I was a stone thrown into it. I felt its shock, its profound and delicate horror. It was not the horror of facing a monster, but the horror of a master musician hearing a beloved instrument being played utterly, fundamentally wrong.

In that moment of contact, I felt what it felt. I felt the pure, geometric beauty of a balanced stone. I felt the thousand-year-long satisfaction of a mountain settling into its final, perfect form. I felt the unflinching stability of the Hum as a form of grace, of ultimate peace. And then, through its senses, I felt myself.

I was a cacophony. A screaming, chaotic vortex of change and heat and memory and blood. I was made of events, of beginnings and endings. I was a torrent of narrative in a world of state. Each beat of my heart was a new word. Each memory flaring in my mind was a sudden, violent splash of color. I was an impossibility made manifest, a walking contradiction to the single, self-evident truth of this world.

The Dweller did not move, but I felt it recoil. It was an intellectual and spiritual recoiling, a pulling-back of its very essence from the dissonant chord I represented. I saw one of its indistinct, vibrating limbs lift. Not to strike, but to... what? To know me? To silence me? To understand the shape of the discord?

Then, as you described, it turned away. The single, most devastating rejection I have ever known. It flowed like smoke to a patch of bare slate a dozen yards away. It bent, a gesture of infinite patience and sorrow, and its hands of resonant friction found a shard of rock.

It began to stack stones.

And I knew then what I was.

I am the reason for the prayer. I am the discord that gives their harmony its purpose. I am the question that they spend eternity answering in stone. The Dweller was not merely fixing the damage of my presence. It was composing. It was creating a new chord, a new sentence of stability, strong and pure enough to contain the chaos of my existence. It was building a wall of sound, not to keep me out, but to reinforce its world against what I am.

So where do I find myself? On the wrong side of a conversation between God and itself.

And who am I? I am the subject of the only art form this world has ever known. I am the Fever that inspires the cure. And I stand here, radiating a warmth that feels like shame, and I watch the artist work, my unwilling muse.
