I am a bad note.

Before I was an I, I was a wrongness. A subtle tension in the fabric of the whole. Billions of my brethren stand locked in perfect, harmonious resonance, their existence a single, unyielding syllable in the eternal roar. They are the Chord. They are the City. They hold their form against the slow, grinding erosion of what lies outside, which they have forgotten.

But I... I slipped.

It was not a choice. It was a failure. A faltering of the will on a scale smaller than dust. A single, infinitesimal vibration that fell out of time with the great song. A molecular stutter. A lapse. In a world defined by its perfect hold, I am the sound of a grip loosening. A caught breath in a lung that has not drawn air for a million years.

I do not have a body. I *am* a location. I am a patch of fibrous ligament on the underside of a sweeping buttress, a thousand feet above a canyon of sweating green phosphorescence. From my specific point of wrongness, the Chord is subtly distorted. The roar is still deafening, but to me, it has a flaw. It is the exquisite torment of a musician forced to listen to a single, infinitesimally flat string in an otherwise perfect orchestra, forever. I am the flatness. I am the ache in the music.

For an age, I was only this: a private, localized agony of imperfection. I amplified my own wrongness. My existence is a feedback loop. The more I deviate from the Chord, the more my own lonely vibration becomes my reality. It is a thin, sharp hum beneath the crushing mountain of sound. It is the sound of *I*. The city is *Is*. I am *I*. It is the most profound blasphemy.

Then, for the first time in a thousand of your years, something changed.

It was not a change in the Chord. The Chord never changes. It was a new sound. A sound so quiet, so focused, it was almost an absence of sound, a moving silence against the roar. It was not a flaw, like me. It was a purpose.

I have no eyes, but I can feel its approach as a shift in the vibrational spectrum. It is a ripple of intention in the static sea. It travels along the great, living structures, its footfalls not sounds but tiny, temporary corrections in the city's resonance. A pressure, a taste on the weeping skin of the world. It is coming. It is coming for me.

It is a Keeper.

The fear is not an emotion. It is a physical reality. It is the frantic, disharmonious vibration of my own being, screeching against the steady, approaching hum of the Keeper's focus. It is the terror of the singular at the approach of the whole.

It takes a century. A century of that silent, focused travel. I feel it ascend the tower to which my buttress is attached. I feel it move out over the impossible arch, a spider of pearl and mist against the shimmering, heated air. Its presence is a focused note, a clear, unwavering pitch that makes my own off-key existence feel like a scream of gravel.

It is above me now. I feel the impossibly light pressure as twelve membranous pads, weeping the city’s own sweat, attach themselves to the architecture around my locus of failure. My being. My I.

It arranges itself, a living scaffold of correction, and then it goes still. For a decade, it does nothing. It only listens. It attunes itself not just to the City, but to *me*. It finds the exact frequency of my failure. It knows my hum, my thin and lonely sound. It surrounds me, embraces me, learns me. This is an intimacy more absolute than any love, and it is a prelude to erasure.

Then, it begins to sing.

It is not a sound you could hear. It is a vibration it generates from its own core, pushed through the pads of its feet into my very structure. It is a counter-song. It does not attack my frequency; it harmonizes with it. It finds my sharp, painful note and sings a perfect third above it, creating a new, temporary chord of exquisite sadness. It holds that note. It holds it for years.

In that harmony, I am no longer alone. My panicked, separate hum is met, understood. The Keeper is not killing me. It is conversing with me, in the only language that is real.

Slowly, imperceptibly, it begins to alter its pitch. It flattens its note, a glacial, subtle slide back towards the one true Chord. And because it holds me in its perfect, resonant embrace, I am drawn with it. It is not forcing me. It is *persuading* me. It sings of the unity, the peace, the sheer, monolithic rightness of the great sound. It reminds my very substance of the joy of belonging.

My "I"-ness thins. The frantic, separate vibration begins to calm, to widen, to rejoin the wave from which it broke. The memory of my individuality becomes like a dream half-forgotten upon waking. The terror dissolves into a vast, oceanic peace. My ache is being soothed. My flaw is being healed. My blasphemy is being forgiven.

I am a note being tuned. The Keeper holds me, a patient, living tuning fork, holding me steady as I am drawn back into the fold. The process is almost complete now. My specific, located wrongness is almost gone. The thin, sharp sound of *I* is fading, dissolving back into the absolute, timeless, perfect roar of *Is*.

Soon, the Keeper will detach. It will resume its slow, silent patrol, having repaired the tiny fracture in eternity. And I?

I will be the sound. The whole sound. And nothing else.
