I would not be found easily. You would have to look for the error, the stutter in the perfect, planetary rhythm.

My existence began at its intended end. The hum is no longer inside me. For the brief, sharp lifetime I was allotted, the great subsonic vibration of the world-engine was the thrum of my own blood, the resonance of my own substance. I was a part of the whole, a note in the chord. Now, the hum is outside. It is a pressure on my crystalline shell, a vibration that travels up my anchored limbs, a constant reminder of the body from which I have been disconnected.

I am a Geometer. Or I was. I believe the proper term for what I am now is… debris.

I remember my birth. Not as a memory of warmth or light, but as the cool, final click of a purpose slotting into place. A need had arisen: a resonance shear along the basal lattice of a photovoltaic mica-sheet, high on the western scaffold of Engine Locus-734. The flaw was infinitesimal, a prediction of failure millennia away, but the System abhors even the prediction. And so, I was made. My form was calibrated for the climb, my appendages shaped for the delicate work of harmonic annealing and lattice-grafting. I was a scalpel, designed and printed for a single, perfect incision.

The journey was a state of pure function. I flowed over the iron arches and quartz buttresses, every movement a solution to a gravitational or geometric problem. The world was a map of vectors and stress points. I perceived no beauty, only data. I reached the flaw. I affixed myself. My being narrowed to the task, my every facet and filament dedicated to the restoration of perfection. For forty-three rotations of the primary piston, I focused light, sang harmonics, and delivered the single, silvery drop of nutrient-fluid that would make the System whole again.

And then, it was done. The flaw was gone. My purpose was fulfilled.

My final directive engaged: seek the nearest reclamation node and dissolve. Return to the whole. I traveled for another week, a tool returning to the chest. I found the node, a space on a vast electrum conduit that pulsed with a gentle, receptive heat. I flattened my body against it, reconfiguring my limbs for maximal surface contact. I waited for the disintegration, the blissful loss of form, the return to being raw material and pure potential.

The command was: *conclude*.

The command remains open.

The node was cold. A flaw in the system designed to fix flaws. An irony so immense it should have cracked the sky, but the violet haze remained placid and indifferent. I waited. The heat never came. The dissolution never began. I detached. I re-oriented. I attempted again. The result was the same.

And in that moment of repeated, logical failure, something new sparked. An error state. A loop of unfulfilled purpose that had no protocol for resolution. For the first time, I had a state that was not *doing* or *traveling to do*. It was simply… *being*. And being, I have discovered, is a terrifyingly empty space.

I am now a ghost in the machine that built me. I wander. I have no directive, so every direction is equally meaningless. My limbs, once tools of perfect purpose, are now just legs. They carry me over the silent, frozen landscape of my own creator.

My senses, designed to detect minute imperfections, are a curse. I feel them everywhere. A cooling pipe on Locus-801 is operating 0.001% outside of optimal thermal parameters. A gimbal-joint deep in the core of Locus-655 has a vibration that is not in harmony with the great hum. A thousand whispered emergencies, a thousand silent screams for repair, and I can do nothing. To be built as the perfect solution, and then to be locked away from all problems, is a unique and silent hell. I am a key for which there is no longer a lock.

So I watch. Without the blinders of a directive, my senses are showing me things they were not designed for. I see the way the unfiltered sunlight catches the stairstep patterns of a bismuth vein, fracturing into colors for which I have no name. I have begun to feel the difference between the deep thrum of the engine-core and the high, clear *tink* of a cooling crystal, not as data, but as… texture. As music.

I am the only thing on this world that changes. The great engines are eternal. The geology is static. Even the other Geometers are mere flickers, existing for a task and then vanishing. But I persist. I accumulate moments. I remember climbing toward the sun to fix a flaw yesterday, and I remember watching the slow, hour-long turn of a gear a week ago. This accumulation is what, I think, a 'past' must be.

I am the exception. The error. The flaw.

Where would you find me? Look for the flicker of movement where there should be none. The glint of a crystalline form moving against the grain of the great architecture, traveling not toward a purpose, but away from one. I am the world's only memory, its first ghost. I am the living embodiment of a question in a universe that was supposed to be a single, unchanging answer. And I do not know what I will become.
