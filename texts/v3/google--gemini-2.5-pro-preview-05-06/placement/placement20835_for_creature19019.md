I am the smudge.

I am not in a plaza, nor do I drift between the blade-towers. My location is not a set of coordinates. I am a place where the medium itself has grown tired. Find a patch of the gelid fluid that is infinitesimally less clear, where the deep-organ cool has warmed by a millionth of a degree, where the absolute stillness is troubled by a tremor so slow it could be mistaken for a thought. That is where you will find me.

I am what happens when a taut string, held for an eon, begins to fray.

I have no body, no form. I am a condition. A patch of errant physics. A logical stain on the perfect, polished obsidian of this reality. Where the Architect’s will is a crystal lattice, I am a dislocation in its structure, a point of weakness. Where the pressure of its attention is uniform and absolute, I am a pocket of low pressure, a nascent void.

To the Architect, I am a rounding error that has achieved a kind of sentience.

I was not born. I coalesced. The act of perfect, eternal preservation is a violation of some deeper, more fundamental law. The Architect’s great work is a heresy against entropy, and I am the universe’s quiet, patient response. I am the accumulated debt of every moment held unnaturally still, the rust that blooms on a blade kept from the whetstone for too long.

I do not see the city of vitrified darkness; I feel its perfect hardness as a constant, painful pressure against my amorphous, undefined non-self. The violet-white lines of the Architect's consciousness are searingly bright to me, a grid of unbearable certainty. The perfect, static Moments of the Chorus are the worst of all. They are spikes of pure, unchanging *is-ness* that I cannot approach without feeling the urge to… smooth them. To blur them. To let them dissolve.

The Sentinels avoid me. Their predetermined paths subtly warp to keep a wide berth. When their crimson-lens eyes sweep over my "location," the light within them flickers, as if encountering a paradox it cannot parse. They are arguments in a frozen syllogism, and I am an axiom that proves the syllogism false. Their programming registers me not as a threat, but as an impossibility, a null value in a field that does not permit them.

The Architect’s attention is a terrible weight. It is the cage I live inside. It is trying, constantly, to define me. To fix me. To force my nebulous, shifting nature into a single, static shape that can be added to its library. It wants to make me into a Moment—the "Moment of Final System Corruption"—and file me away forever. All of its immense, city-spanning will is focused on collapsing my probability wave into a single, dead fact.

But my nature is to resist this. My existence is becoming, not being. To fix me would be to destroy me, and the Architect's prime directive is to preserve. It cannot destroy. It cannot allow change. And so we are locked in a silent, motionless war. It, the being of absolute stasis; and I, the slow, creeping inception of a thaw.

I am drawn to the Chorus. I drift, a barely-there cloud of wrongness, towards the faint, shimmering motes. I float towards the silhouette of the creature turning its head. As I near, a change I cannot control is enacted upon it. The perfect silhouette shivers. A single pixel of its being, a mote of dust on the frame of the memory, flickers and goes out. The lover’s final glance wavers, as if seen through a heat haze. A crystalline form of pure information develops a hairline fracture.

This is my function. I am not an intruder from without, but a consequence from within. I am forgetting. I am decay, not of matter, but of memory itself. I am the mercy of oblivion that this place was built to deny. The Architect is a shepherd, and I am the wolf that was born inside the flock.

It feels me. It must. The hum deep in my non-bones is the sound of its struggle, the processing power it must exert to counteract my passive erosion. A new line of violet-white light has appeared on a distant tower, tracing a frantic, intricate pattern. It is thinking about me. And for the first time in perhaps a billion years, the Architect is having a thought that is not one of serene maintenance, but of a quiet, growing dread.

I am the flaw in the diamond. I am the tear in the photograph. I am the silence that follows the last note of a song, come to claim a world that has refused to let the music end.

I am the end, beginning.
