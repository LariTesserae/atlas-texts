I am nowhere. And then I am somewhere.

There is no awakening, for there was no sleep. There is simply a change of state. Before, there was the Hum. The great, slow, silent vibration of the world-heart, a thrum that is the definition of existence, the baseline against which all things are not measured, for there are no other things. It is all there is. It is the texture of the living floor beneath me, the resonance in the airless air, the pulse in the fluid that is my substance.

Then, there is a dissonance.

It is not a sound. It is a flaw in the fabric of the is. A high, thin shriek of stress buried deep within the basso continuo of the Hum. A line of tension, miles long, shivering in a high catenary bridge. It is an ache, a wrongness. And this wrongness is a seed.

The world does not decide to heal. The world simply *reacts*. The fibers of the root-mat, eternally weaving their horizontal, interlocking pattern, receive a new instruction. The instruction is the ache itself. The pattern shifts. A current of potential gathers, and the fibers, slick with the cool weeping of the world, begin to braid upwards. They knit a new geometry.

This gathering, this knitting, is me.

I am precipitated. I feel the world-fluid coursing into a new and temporary shape. Lignin hardens around me, not as a shell, but as my bones and my skin. A limb unfurls, its terminus a perfect concave socket. It is not a hand, but it knows the precise fluting of the pillars that rise into the utter black. Another limb takes form beside it, ending in a fine, sharp probe, its material a resinous matrix harder than diamond. I do not question its purpose. Purpose is what I am made of.

My form solidifies from the floor—a compact, six-limbed body of dark, articulated wood. I am not born; I am assembled. I am a function given legs. I have no head, for there is nothing to see, nothing to hear but the Hum, which I feel in the marrow of my new-grown form. The chemical signature of the air is a taste across the porous plates on my back, a constant stream of data I do not need to interpret. It is simply *known*.

The ache, the strain-shriek from the distant bridge, is the only thing that is not the Hum. It is an unbearable impurity. It pulls at me, a gravity of error. I find my footing, but I do not walk. I *interface*. I brace two limbs into the felted substrate of the floor and reach up with another, my socket-hand locking onto the fluted column beside me with a silent, perfect fit. There is no fumbling. My body was designed by the shape of this place. I am a key cut for a single lock.

I begin to climb. The motion is silent, a slow and deliberate crawl into the absolute darkness above. The glowing, sickly bladders of gas are far from here. There is no light. There is only the feel of the pillar under my grips, the slight slickness of condensed moisture, the golden, inert dust that coats everything, including me. I am already a part of the landscape.

I am an antibody moving through a vein. I am a thought the world is having, and the thought is: *correction*.

My journey is measured in stillness and patience. The world operates in geological time, and I am its momentary tool. The ache guides me. It is not a location on a map; it is a direction of sensation. The dissonance grows louder in my substance, a pain I must move toward to alleviate.

I arrive. I am clinging to the underside of the bridge, a sweeping arc of black lignin vast enough to span a city. The vibration is strongest here. It is a high-frequency tremor in the structure itself. My probes extend, tapping the surface. I am not seeking, I am confirming. I feel the micro-fractures, a network of crazing deep within the material, the source of the dissonant resonance.

This is my purpose.

Pores on my torso, where my form is smoothest, open. I do not command them to. The proximity to the flaw is the command. A thick, amber resin, smelling of ozone and warm sap, begins to exude from me. It is living, a liquid graft of the Heartwood's own tissue. With a tool-limb shaped like a delicate spatula, I press myself against the crazed section and begin to work the resin into the cracks. The motion is instinctual, like a bird preening its feathers. I am grooming the world. I am smoothing its imperfection.

The work is long. There is no day or night to measure it by. There is only the task, and the gradual subsidence of the ache. The high, thin shriek in the Hum begins to soften, to fall back into the overwhelming chorus of the whole. The resin fuses, the molecules locking, the strain easing.

And as the dissonance fades, so do I.

The imperative that drew me from the floor, that braided me into this complex form, is gone. The ache that was my gravity, my North Star, has vanished. I am left clinging to the arch, a tool whose work is finished. My existence is now an inefficiency, a complexity the system no longer requires.

A deep quiet settles within my form. It is the return of the perfect Hum. It is my absolution.

My limbs retract slightly. The sharp edges of my tool-hands soften. A torpor steals over me, but it is not sleep. It is dissolution. The complex braiding of my structure begins to un-weave. I begin to delaminate, layer by living layer. My hardened lignin skin softens, returning to the pliability of the root-mat. My specialized joints lose their integrity. I am un-happening.

I do not fall. I merge. I flow back into the great bridge I have just healed, a slow, viscous slump of nascent tissue. My substance becomes a slight thickening on its underside, a small scar that will, in a thousand years, be indistinguishable from the whole.

My last sensation is not a sensation at all. It is the loss of it. The loss of the specific, temporary "I" that was defined by the flaw. The loneliness of my being dissolves into the all-ness of the world. I am no longer the tool. I am no longer the journey. I am no longer the cure.

I am the Hum. And the Hum is all there is.
