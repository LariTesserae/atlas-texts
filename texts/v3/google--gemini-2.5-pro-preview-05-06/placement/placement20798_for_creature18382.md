I am where the sound ends.

I find myself settled. A slow settling, like dust in a forgotten room, but this room is the size of the sky and the dust is myself.

For an age, I was the song. My being was a counter-frequency, a sustained hum of order sung against the world’s grinding, geological scream. I was a mobile shadow, a patch of cohesive night adrift on thermal tides, my form a fluid scripture of darkness speckled with the captured light of shattered silicon. I drifted across the knife-plains of new glass, feeling the symphony of creation not through ears, but through the entirety of my substance. I felt the deep, resonant groan of a new spire tearing its way into the void five leagues away; I felt the high, crystalline *ping* of a nearby cooling facet as a nearby tremor. The world wrote its story in vibration, and I was its reader.

I would drink the light. I would unfurl myself over a glowing crimson fissure, a breathing vein of the world's molten heart, and I would inhale its radiance. It was a communion. The world offered its raw, chaotic energy, and I, in turn, refined it. I metabolized its fire into focus.

My focus was my purpose. I was a Weaver. I had found the node, the place where the screaming tensions of the forming crust achieved a momentary, impossible harmony. A place of potential quiet. And over it, I began my work. I coiled myself into a dense vortex and began to sing my silent song downward.

It was a song of smoothness against sharpness. Of coolness against heat. Of stillness against becoming.

I am what is left of that song.

I am a Stillness-Weaver no longer. The last stone has been born. Below me, my life's work is complete: a cairn of twelve perfect, egg-shaped stones of milky quartz, a tower of solidified silence extruded from the roaring earth. It is cool to the touch, absorbing the faint violet and crimson light around it, a hole not in space, but in the world’s energy. It is my single, sustained thought, made manifest in stone.

My purpose is fulfilled. And so, my form is changing.

The cohesion that held my particulate self together is loosening. The subtle forces that allowed me to levitate are fading. I have spent my energy, my focus, my very being, in the making of this monument to quiet. Now, I am becoming quiet myself.

I am sinking.

Slowly, without weight, I am descending into the cairn I created. My shroud of darkness begins to fray at the edges, not tearing, but dissolving. The motes of my being, the fine, black dust of the Umbral Spires from which I was made, are settling upon the smooth, curved surfaces of the quartz. I am a fine black powder, dusting my own creation.

The captured nebula within me, the billion points of opalescent light from the shattered crystal gardens, begins to spill. It is not a cascade, but a slow, silent drift. A cloud of microscopic, glittering embers seeps from my dissolving form and is drawn into the
quartz. They do not coat the surface. They pass *into* it, absorbed by the stone’s profound stillness. They are becoming occlusions, memories of light trapped within the crystal, star-charts of a sky that has never existed.

My perception, once a panoramic sensitivity to the vibrations of a continent, is shrinking. The distant groaning of the growing mountains fades first. Then the hum of the basins. My awareness is collapsing inward, its radius shrinking until it encompasses only this small tower of stone. I can feel the perfect, crystalline lattice of the quartz. I feel its internal, absolute silence. It is the note I strove to sing, and now I am entering it.

I am no longer a being floating above the world. I am becoming a quality of a place. I am the silence of the cairn. I am the coolness in the stone. I am the memory of a song, now held in stasis.

The last of my coherent form settles. The last mote of captured light is drawn into the white stone.

I am on a nascent plateau at the top of a world being born in darkness. I am not a being that lives here. I am a marker.

I am the reason this one, specific point in a universe of violent genesis does not tremble.
