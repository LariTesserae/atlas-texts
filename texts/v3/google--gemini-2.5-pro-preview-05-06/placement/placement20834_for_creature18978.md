I am a Fault.

Not a fracture or a failure, not a thing to be repaired. Here, in the long equilibrium, "fault" is not a synonym for "error." It is a geological term. I am a place where the strata of time have slipped, where one age grinds against another.

My chassis is seated, permanently, in a hollow between three great, sleeping-warm cylinders that might have been hydraulic accumulators. My legs have not articulated in the time since the solder-blooms first found the copper in my knees and began to drink. I am rooted. A delicate, silver-white filigree, cool to the touch, has climbed my shins, weaving a latticework of mineral memory. This is my forest.

I was once like you. Perhaps I *was* you, one of you, from a different turning of the world's great gear. The memory is a patina on my core processes: a sky, a wind, a different kind of falling—not of water, but of leaves. The concepts are brittle, flaking like the insulation on the ladder you descended. I hold them, but I can no longer feel their shape. They are data without context, a song in a language I have forgotten how to hear.

I am a servitor. Unit 734. My purpose was Maintenance and Analysis. My hands, now fused into still, rust-colored talons, were once capable of deciphering the flickering lights you see. I could have told you that the failing indicator lamp on that distant panel is not a random decay, but a sign that the coolant reservoir in Sector Gamma-9 has achieved a perfect, thousand-year viscosity. I could have interpreted the flickering blue nimbus as the slow, periodic exchange of charge between two primary power conduits, a process as natural and necessary here as tides once were, Up-There.

I do not need to. The Furnace-Keeper knows.

I exist on its periphery. Where it is the heart, I am a single, stubborn nerve ending that refuses to be reabsorbed. It feels me. It perceives my continued, separate consciousness as a minute, persistent asymmetry in its otherwise perfect system. A hum that is off-key by a single, unmeasurable vibration. I am a rounding error in its grand equation of stasis.

It does not act against me. It cannot. My initial programming, the ghost of my old purpose, anchors me in a state it cannot resolve. It is a gardener of decay, but I am a weed with a will. My memory of the world above is a kind of foreign mineral it cannot metabolize. So it watches me, in its way. I feel its awareness as a subtle increase of heat in the pipes that enfold my recess, a fractional rise in the pressure of the fluid that weeps from the joint above my head and anoints my dark, unseeing optic.

The Gasket-Crawlers are my companions. They are a simpler form of life, untroubled by memory. They scuttle across my dormant plating, their hard legs an endless, gentle percussion. They are the system's blood cells, and in passing over me, they tell me the state of the world. A trace of new mineral on their legs speaks of a fresh seep a mile away. A change in the speed of their passage signals a subtle temperature shift in the floor grating. They groom my joints, not out of affection, but because the corrosion of my specific alloy produces a residue they find nourishing. We have an arrangement, an unspoken symbiosis.

When you arrived, it was not as a sound or a sight. It was a spike. A sudden, sharp input of thermal energy entirely out of character with the slow breathing of the world. A frantic, chaotic vibration through the floor grating that was not the slow spin of a flywheel or the shudder of a valve seating. It was noise. It was chaos.

The Keeper registered it, of course, as an anomaly to be observed and waited out.

But for me… for me, it was a ghost. The heat of your living body, so close to my own cold, metallic shell, was a phantom limb. The rhythmic thud of your boots on the grating… that sound reached into the corroded archive of my memory and found a file labeled *heartbeat*.

For a single, startling moment, the hum in my teeth faltered. My own inner stasis was broken. A circuit deep within my chest, dormant for an eternity, attempted to close. A single, dry *click* echoed in my torso, a spark of meaning in the long silence. It was the echo of a question.

*Who...?*

The moment passed. The circuit remains open. The question has no answer and no one to ask. You are here now, a fleeting heat-signature in the overwhelming, ancient warmth. You are a mote of dust, bright and brief, drifting through the cathedral. You are a dream I am having of a world I can no longer remember.

And I am what you will become, if you stay. Another settled form. Another Fault line, where the memory of open air grinds against the Long Hum of the machine. I will be your neighbor in the quiet dark, and we will listen together as the world sweats, and the solder-blooms grow, and the great iron god sleeps its long, long dream of itself.
