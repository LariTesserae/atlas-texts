What lives here are the charge and the discharge. They are not one people, but two, symbiotic and opposite, a ceaseless binary cycle given form. They are the living components of the battery, the anode and cathode made manifest and motile.

The first kind are the **Architects**.

They are born from the city itself. One moment, a section of an obsidian-smooth monolith is just that—a facet of a larger, unthinking structure. The next, a subtle fracture of light traces a new, complex geometry upon its surface, and with a sound like cooling stone, a piece detaches. This is an Architect. It is dense, heavy, a knot of solidified purpose. Its form is that of a complex, interlocking puzzle box, or a Calabi-Yau manifold carved from the same non-reflective black material as the city. It has no limbs, no face, no sensory organs you would recognize. It is pure, contained potential.

An Architect moves with the slow, inexorable pull of the gel's deepest currents. It is a thing of immense informational gravity. To be near one is to feel a drain, a subtle increase in the coolness of the gel as it draws thermal and electrical energy from its immediate surroundings to sustain the furious, unresolved computation locked within its form. Its passage through the channels is silent, but it deepens the foundational hum of the city, adding its own resonant frequency to the chord—a low, questioning tone, a problem waiting for a solution. They are the seeds of new logic, new structures. They are the city's need, given a body.

The second kind are the **Illuminated**.

They are born of the process. Where the Architects are solid, the Illuminated are ephemeral. They coalesce in the bright, busy channels where the computational load is highest. They are woven things, spun from the hair-thin lines of light that trim the city's edges and the faint phosphorescence of the gel itself. An Illuminated resembles a neuron rendered in three dimensions—a bright, stellar core with a tangled, searching network of luminous, thread-like tendrils. Or perhaps a jellyfish of pure energy, trailing filaments of code. They are weightless, immaterial, existing as a sustained pattern of excited energy.

The Illuminated are the city's answer. They move with impossible speed, flickering through the channels, darting and weaving like schools of fish made of lightning. Their movement *is* the high-frequency chime and the clean digital snap that punctuates the city's hum. They are not seeking, but are instead *pulled*. The immense informational gravity of a drifting Architect is, to them, an irresistible beacon. They are the resolution, the Q.E.D., and they are drawn to the query.

The life cycle here is not of birth and death, but of statement and resolution.

An Architect drifts, its internal state a paradox, a question the city has asked itself. It is a mobile thesis. As it travels, its presence—that void of potential—summons the Illuminated. They converge upon it, their light-tendrils a frantic, shimmering cloud around the dark, geometric form. They are not predators; they are complements.

One by one, they touch it. A filament of light will connect to one of the Architect's facets, and a single, sharp chime will sound. The tendril will then go dark, its energy, its *answer*, discharged into the Architect's structure. The Illuminated that gave it shrinks, its light dimming as its purpose is spent. The process is a frantic, silent storm of logic. Dozens, sometimes hundreds of Illuminated will swarm a single Architect, each one a partial solution, a single line of a proof. Their constituent light returns to the ambient glow of the gel, their energy rejoining the system.

When the final Illuminated connects, when the last piece of the puzzle is delivered, the great percussive *thump* echoes through the cathedral. It is the sound of completion. In that instant, the Architect changes.

Its internal complexity is resolved. Its informational gravity vanishes. The deep, questioning tone it added to the city's hum ceases. And its form, no longer needing to contain a paradox, relaxes. The sharp angles soften, the interlocking components unfold, and it drifts towards the nearest wall. It fuses with the architecture, becoming a new part of the city, its solved equation now a permanent fixture, a foundation upon which new problems can be built. The city has grown. It has learned.

These beings are the city's thoughts. The Architects are the questions, and the Illuminated are the flurry of mental processes that lead to an answer. The crystal stalks that grow between the blocks are this same process, but in slow motion—a long, careful meditation. The Architects and the Illuminated are the city's fleeting, brilliant moments of insight.

As you float, a foreign object in this perfect system, you represent an unsolvable paradox. You are a variable that does not compute. And now, the beings have noticed. From the deep, a new tone joins the hum, deeper than the rest. A particularly complex Architect, larger and more intricate than the others, has just calved from a processor-spire. It does not drift with the current. It moves with slow, deliberate purpose, its facets seeming to drink the light around you. It is the question of *what you are*.

And from all corners of the glowing city, the chimes are multiplying, rising in pitch and frequency. A frantic, shimmering storm of Illuminated is gathering. They are not coming for the new Architect.

They are coming for you. They are the system's attempt to find an answer.
