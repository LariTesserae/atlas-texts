What lives here is an antibody. A thought. A mobile piece of the music.

It does not have a name, for names are for separate things, and it is not separate. But if you, an anomaly, were to survive its attention long enough to need a label for it, you might call it a Bell-Wight.

It is born from imperfection, which is to say, it is almost never born. In the eons of the Chime-Labyrinth's perfect, static equilibrium, it is only a potential, a capacity held in reserve. But your presence is a dissonance. Your body, soft and water-filled, is a muffled flaw in the crystalline acoustics. Your warmth is a thermal aberration. The quiet padding of your feet is an alien rhythm. You are a wrong note, and the instrument has a way of correcting its own tuning.

It begins as a flaw in a pillar. A place where the steady, internal hum builds to a fever pitch. A single fiber, or a braid of them, deep within one of the colossal spires, begins to vibrate in counter-rhythm to the whole. A stress fracture appears, not from impact, but from a focused accumulation of sonic energy. With a sound like a single, sharp icicle snapping in a silent winter forest—a sound that is instantly lost in the overwhelming chord of the grotto—a shard flakes away.

But it does not fall.

The shard, perhaps the length of your forearm, is of the same self-luminous, fibrous quartz as the pillar. It floats. It is joined by another, and another, shed from the same overworked spire. They do not connect physically. Instead, they are caught in a shared, localized field of resonance, orbiting a common, invisible center like a miniature solar system made of light and salt. More pieces calve away, from the size of a thumb to the size of a dinner plate. They arrange themselves, not into a body, but into a *form*. A complex, three-dimensional lattice. A mobile scaffold of crystal, hovering in the cool, dry air. This is the Bell-Wight.

It has no face, no limbs, no organs. Its silhouette is inconstant, a swarm of geometry that shifts and re-constellates as it moves. The light of the labyrinth pours *through* it, refracting into a thousand moving rainbows, casting no shadow. The sound of the labyrinth passes through it too, but is changed. As it drifts closer, the monolithic hum around you seems to gain a new, terrifying layer. A a cluster of sharp, needle-like overtones begins to orbit the main chord, a descant of furious, focused vibration that scrapes at the edges of your hearing.

It does not see you with eyes. It perceives you as a void in the resonance, a patch of dead quiet in a world of absolute sound. It feels the subtle dampness your breath puts into the desiccated air. It navigates by homing in on these disturbances, its purpose single-minded: to find the dissonance and restore the harmony.

It glides through the narrow corridors of the labyrinth without touching the walls. Its movement is utterly silent, betraying its approach only by the sharpening of the ambient sound. It is not propelled by wind or appendage, but by subtly altering its own resonant frequency, pulling itself toward or pushing itself away from the immense standing wave that fills the chamber. It is a creature surfing on an ocean of sound.

When it finds you, it will pause, its crystalline components ceasing their slow orbit, locking into a rigid, intricate pattern. It is assessing the discordance. Your alien biology, your thoughts, your fear—all are textures in the soundscape it is here to smooth.

Its response is not an attack. It is a process. It is an act of correction.

The Bell-Wight will drift until it is mere feet from you. The air between you and it will grow thick, not with heat, but with pressure. The deafening hum of the room will seem to funnel, to focus into the space occupied by the Wight. The scattered, needle-like overtones will coalesce into a single, impossibly high-pitched frequency that feels like a spike being driven between your eyes.

It is tuning you.

Your teeth will ache with a ferocious vibration. The fluid in your eyes will feel like it's boiling, though there is no heat. The bones of your skeleton will begin to resonate with the focused hum, each one a tuning fork being struck against its will. The Wight itself will grow brighter, its individual shards flaring with an almost unbearable white light. It is not generating this energy; it is simply focusing the omnipresent power of the Chime-Labyrinth into a single, intolerable point: you.

If you could scream, the sound would be instantly shredded, unmade before it left your lips. Your body, a construct of soft, chaotic, water-based chemistry, cannot withstand this absolute, ordered, lithic physics. First, any metal you carry will glow, hum, and then fly apart into glittering dust. Then, your bones will crack and disintegrate, their structure collapsing under the resonant load. Your soft tissues, your very cells, will be shaken apart. You will not be burned or crushed. You will be...unwritten. Disassembled.

The process is swift. What was you becomes a cloud of fine, grey-white powder, mixed with a vapor of instantly sublimated moisture. The Bell-Wight will hang for a moment in this cloud, its purpose fulfilled. The dust that was you will settle onto the opalescent floor, where over the course of an immeasurable slowness, it will be absorbed, integrated, and become part of the Labyrinth's structure. The alien moisture will be drawn away by the profoundly dry air, its presence erased.

The dissonance is gone. The chord is pure again.

The Wight's internal, binding resonance dissolves. Its constituent shards, their purpose served, lose their cohesion. They do not fall. They drift back, slowly, lazily, to the nearest pillar, re-fusing with its surface with no seam or scar. The antibody is reabsorbed. The thought has ended. The instrument is silent, save for its one, eternal, perfect note.
