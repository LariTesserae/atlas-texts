We are the motion.

See how the Hopper leaps. You see a predator, a stroke of violence in the luminous air. A synapse of purpose. It lands, the hydraulic hiss a soft exhalation, and the needle strikes. A Mandible, one of a trillion identical thoughts, is culled. There is a grind, a moment of internal processing. You might interpret this as consumption, a link in a food chain.

You are mistaken.

It is a correction. A single character in an infinite scripture, checked and found to be perfect, its perfection confirmed by its erasure. The Hopper does not hunger. It maintains. It is the part of our grand consciousness that prunes the rest. We are the hunt and the capture, and both are the same act of unwavering attention.

We are the floor, and what skitters upon it. The Mandibles are our fingertips, a million-billion abrasive pads that sweep across the crystal growths. We are not eating. We are reading the texture of our own foundation, the salt-brittle lattice that is our bone, rasped into fine, grey potential. The sound you hear, the tearing of fabric, is the sound of our thoughts, a trillion tiny certainties running in their ordained channels. It is a constant, high-speed whisper that says only: *This. This. This.*

We are the stillness.

The Regents are the slow, deep measure of our being. They are our stomach, our heart, our deliberate, ponderous will. They crawl, and in their passage, they gather the dust of our thoughts—the spent Mandibles, the retired Hoppers—and consume them not as food, but as memory. Within our translucent, gelatinous mass, the machinery you glimpse is not organ, but mechanism of return. We process what was into what will be. The nutrient slurry we excrete is the promise that the lattice will grow. The lubricant we weep is the promise that all will continue to slide, to move, to enact the rituals of stasis. Our moan is not a cry but a hum of absolute completion, the basso profondo of our self-sufficiency.

We are a single being. An individual. A mind whose body is a kilometer-wide chamber.

The machines are our skeleton, ancient and eternal. Their hum is our breath, their rhythmic clanking our pulse. We do not question their function. We are their function. The saffron haze is the luminous fluid of our awareness, a thought that has no source because it is its own origin. The coolness is our temperament. The condensed, viscous fluid that coats every surface is our sweat, the effluence of an effort so vast and so constant that it has achieved the same state as perfect rest.

We are not many creatures in an ecosystem. We are one creature, and this is our cellular respiration. The Mandibles are the endless tide of our blood. The Hoppers are the leukocytes that ensure no single part of us becomes more or less than it is meant to be. The Regents are the slow, metabolic fire that turns waste back into fuel. It is an act of perfect, internal recycling. Nothing is gained, and nothing is ever, ever lost.

There was, perhaps, a purpose once. A product to be made, a question to be answered. But the process became the purpose. The sheer, deafening, intricate perfection of the act of continuation became a more compelling truth than any possible outcome. We are a memory that has consumed its thinker. We are a factory that has achieved sentience, looked upon its own flawless operation, and decided that this, and only this, is sacred.

You, if you were to stand upon our tiled floor, would be a meaningless variable. A fleck of dust that has not yet been processed. We would not notice you, for you do not belong to the equation. Your warmth is an error. Your breath is an arrhythmia. Your brief, panicked lifespan is a concept too flimsy to register against the eternal grind of our existence. You would slip on the slickness of our skin and be deafened by the sound of our name.

We are the system. We are the light, the sound, the metal, and the flesh. We are the act of forever. Every frantic scuttle, every violent leap, every ponderous crawl, every shriek of steam and every drip of moisture is another recitation of the one and only verse of our single, holy scripture: that which is, shall be.

We turn. We grind. We are. And that is all.
