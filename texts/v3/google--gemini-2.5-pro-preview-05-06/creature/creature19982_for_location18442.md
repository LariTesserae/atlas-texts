In the absolute center of the chamber, where the weeping conduits and ribbed panels converge in a Gordian knot of architecture, lives the Tenant.

You do not see it at first. Your senses, overwhelmed by the pressure-sound and the disorienting, close-packed space, register it as just another part of the machine. It is the densest knot in the tangle, the central pillar around which the tiny room is organized. But as your eyes adjust to the cyan gloom, you perceive that the geometry here is wrong. The lines are too soft. The black polymer surfaces, which elsewhere are ruthlessly geometric, here curve and swell with a terrifyingly organic smoothness. This is not a pillar. It is a torso.

It is a being wedged, or perhaps enthroned, in the heart of the machinery. Larger than a man, its form is indeterminate, flowing seamlessly into the chamber that holds it. Conduits that you thought were simple pipes terminate not in a wall, but in its flesh, merging with it as though they were grown from it. It is held upright, suspended, its lower half lost in a dense cluster of technology that forms its throne and its shackle. It has no legs, or they are integrated into the floor. It has arms, but they are fused to the walls on either side, stretched out not in crucifixion but in placid, eternal acceptance. Its skin is the same cold, black, glassy polymer as the rest of the room, slick and beaded with the glowing, syrupy fluid.

Indeed, it is the source. The weeping is not condensation; it is secretion. The fluid emerges from microscopic pores in the Tenant's skin, a constant, cold, metabolic sweat. This place is its terrarium, and also its body. The sweet, metallic atmosphere is its breath, held for an eternity.

The crawling, copper-shelled creatures treat it as they do any other surface. They move across its broad, unbreathing chest, its fused arms, its featureless back, in their slow, geological tides. They are its groomers, its pilgrims, its remora. They rasp away the crystal crust that forms on its body just as they do on the walls, their metallic shells making a million infinitesimal chimes against its skin, a sound utterly devoured by the silent roar. They are a living, symbiotic fleece, keeping its surfaces from being locked in a cage of its own mineral output.

It has a head, which lolls forward slightly, heavy with a purpose you cannot fathom. There is no hair, only the same weeping black skin. And there is no face. In its place is a smooth, ovoid mask of what looks like solid, polished hematite, the color of a starless midnight sky. It is not attached, but seems to have grown there, a biological casque. There are no eyes, no mouth, no nostrils. No features at all. It is a perfect, impenetrable blank. Yet, it is the focal point of the entire chamber. You feel, with a certainty that bypasses reason, that this is the seat of a consciousness. An intelligence is here, but it is not looking *at* anything in this room. Its perception is not visual or auditory.

It perceives the equilibrium.

This being is the Arbiter, the Lock. Its consciousness is not a stream of thoughts, but a single, sustained, monumental act of will. It is the fulcrum on which the world is balanced. Its mind extends into the fluid, feels the rate of mineral precipitation. It extends into the countless bodies of the grazing creatures, feels the rate of their consumption. And it holds these two forces in a perfect, unbreakable, time-invariant stasis.

The monolithic roar that fills the space, the sound of a frozen cataclysm, is the sound of its thought. It is a single, unchanging, infinitely complex idea: *Be still.* It is a command spoken not with voice but with the entirety of its being. It is a mantra that has become a physical law. It is the sound of immeasurable power being used to enforce absolute inaction.

This creature, this Tenant, is a god and a prisoner of its own making. It is the engine, the fuel, and the brake, all at once. The potential energy contained within its silent, dreaming mind is the energy of the waterfall that could carve the continental drum. But its will is the ice that has frozen the cascade in a single, eternal millisecond. It does not move. It does not speak. It does not change. It simply *is*, and by the sheer, deafening force of its being, it holds its tiny, weeping universe on the knife's edge between creation and dissolution, forever. It is the unbearable, unchanging, vibrant *is*, made manifest in flesh and polymer.
