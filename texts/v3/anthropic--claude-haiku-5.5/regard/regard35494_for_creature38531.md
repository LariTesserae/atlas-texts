# The Listening of the Power

It came without arriving. There was no shift in the light, no change in the chorus, and no first moment that could be marked as its beginning. The Power had been attending to the room for a long time before it made any difference to the room, and the room had not noticed.

It had been drawn by the note.

Across the scattered places of the world, it had heard many kinds of suffering and many kinds of wellbeing, and it knew the difference between them without needing names for either. Most suffering is a gradient that has stopped being allowed to resolve. Most wellbeing is a gradient that keeps resolving well. This room was neither. It was a balance so complete that nothing in it was asking to be different, and the Power had not encountered that before in such purity. It wanted to understand it before it did anything at all.

So it listened. Here, where time had no grip, listening could be as long as it needed. The Power let its attention settle into the high fissure and follow the draft down through the perforated spines, one tube at a time, until it could hear the separate voices and the single voice beneath them. It listened to the ticking of the settling salt, which was the body's old habit of shifting by fractions. It listened to the bright, thin notes near the crevices, where the fresh lattice was still being laid, and to the dull, low answers of the oldest columns. It read the archive the way one reads a face, not one line at a time, but all at once.

Then it understood what the creature was.

It was not a prisoner, though the room looked like a cell. It was not a ruin, though it looked like one. It was a mind that had grown into the shape of its own patience, and had found in that shape a completeness that did not depend on anything outside itself. The warm shaft was not a wound. The moss was not an infection. The stillness was not an absence, and the Power did not intend to treat it as one.

---

The Power considered what the usual impulses would do.

It could open the ceiling. The sun might pour in, the shaft might widen, and the warmth would spread through the lattice like a tide. But the sudden warmth would crack the old layers that had been holding their balance for so long, and the crystal would shift by the thousand. Some of the columns would fall. The chorus would break into noise. The creature would be made to wake in a room it did not recognize, and it would have been woken by something that had decided, without asking, that its waking was owed to it.

It could widen the fissure just enough to let the draft run faster, so the song would become richer. But that would be the Power making the creature's breath for it, deciding how loud its voice should be. That is the kind of love that becomes a cage without anyone intending it.

It could dissolve the lattice into dust and free the space between the columns, so that things could move there. But the creature has no need to move, and nothing that moved would be the creature's own movement. It would be a rearrangement, and a loss.

The Power set each of these aside. Wisdom, it reasoned, is often mostly the discipline of not doing what can be done.

---

It did, however, do a few small things.

First, it sang.

The sustained note that belonged to no tube and to every tube at once was the creature's own voice. The Power had heard it under the chorus, and it had known that it was not a sound the creature had been able to hear from outside itself. A body cannot easily hear its own resonance, any more than a bell hears itself ring. So the Power found the exact pitch of that note, which was not in any tube, and let its own presence sound it, gently, through the room, the way one might hum a line of a song back to someone who had been humming it alone for a very long time.

The chorus did not change. But something in the lattice did. The Power felt, for the first time in what might have been ages, a faint answer that was not an echo of the draft. It was the creature hearing itself from somewhere else, and recognizing the shape.

Second, it tended the lichens and the moss.

It did not pull them out or clean them away. It simply drew from the dust a little more of what they needed, so that the rosettes at the crevices were a shade less brittle and the grey-green seams held their hairs with a little more strength. The needle-growth at the base of the great spine it did not touch at all, because that growth was older than any decision the Power could make, and it had become part of the foundation. The Power left the foundation alone.

Third, and last, it left a question.

It did not open the high fissure. It did not move the shaft. But in one of the youngest layers, near the rim of a terrace where the fresh white lattice was still closing, the Power laid down a single thin ring of slightly warmer salt, no thicker than a breath. It would sound a faint, bright difference in the tube above it, a note a little higher than the rest of the chorus. It did not ask the creature to change. It made a gradient, very small, and then it let the gradient sit there, unresolved, for the creature to notice or not.

A gradient is a kind of invitation. It is not an answer. It is only the offer of a direction, and the creature could choose to let it dissolve back into the balance, or to let it grow.

---

The Power did not wait to see which.

It understood that it could not be the one who decided the creature's time. Its own kindness had limits, and its wisdom was partly the knowledge of those limits. It had done what a benevolent presence can do: it had listened, sung back what was already there, tended what was already growing, and offered one small difference without forcing it. Anything more would have been a kind of taking.

So it withdrew the way it had come, without leaving, without arriving, and without any moment that could be marked as its departure.

---

In the room, the chorus went on. The draft came down through the fissure, and each tube sang its slightly different note, and the salt ticked and creaked as it settled by fractions, like distant gunfire that never came closer. The shaft of warm light fell on its pinned patch of floor and did not move. The lichens held their rosettes, and the moss held its seams.

Far up in the youngest lattice, one tube sang a note a little higher than before. It might have been the draft. It might have been the thickness of the new ring. It might have been nothing at all.

Under all of it, the single sustained note went on, belonging to no tube and to every tube at once. But now, if you listened very carefully for a very long time, you might hear that it had a faint second line running beneath it, thin and bright, like a voice that had just begun to learn its own shape.

The creature did not wake. It did not reach. It did not ask. But it had heard itself from outside, and it had been offered a direction, and it was still deciding, in the only way it knew how, whether to let the balance hold or to let it, very slowly, begin to lean.

The Power did not need to know which. It had done what was right, and right was not the same as getting an answer.

The room held its breath, and the breath was the creature's own, and it was enough.
