# The Attending

It came in fog, though the fog was not the usual kind.

The basin's evening mist settled as it always did, grey and warm, beading on the vines. But it thickened in a particular way, rising in still columns instead of drifting, and the amphibian chorus, which had not stopped in living memory, stopped. The reed clearings went quiet. Even the metallic ticking underneath everything slowed, the way a room goes quiet when someone enters who is known to be listening.

Then the towers leaned toward the rim, all of them at once, and held there.

Nothing in the basin had ever leaned toward anything outside itself.

The power that came to the rim did not descend, did not announce itself, and did not cast a shadow, because nothing in the basin casts a hard one. It did not bring light or sound. It simply was present at the edge of the dish, the way a person is present in a room before you turn around. It was not a visitor. It had no use for the doors and did not ask them to open. It sat, as it were, on the lip of the bowl, and it listened.

It listened for a full cycle of fog. Dusk to dawn to dusk again. It learned the rhythm of the fronds, which pulsed in the same schedule as the channels. It learned the way the towers tilted and returned, and the way the lattice cells flickered in clusters that rose and fell like a held breath. It noticed the sludge and the buds, the wall that went from grey to geometry to vine. It noticed the insects tapping their fragments of machinery against the rock, reading the surfaces, and it understood that the tapping was memory. It noticed the couriers carrying seed in their bellies and the places where they landed, and it understood that the basin was reaching, always, toward places it could not yet touch.

It noticed the green stain on the skin of sleepers, and it understood the count.

And it noticed the water.

---

The power's first act was to decline the obvious one.

It could have opened the channels. The water in the grid runs toward the center, and the towers have never received it. A power of its kind could have forced the last connection, pushed the flow through the final gate, and finished the thought the basin has been circling for longer than any human history. It could have called the sludge back into order and held every cell in a single shape until the lattice became permanent and the mind became a monument.

It did not do this, because it understood what the basin had been doing. The mind keeps rebuilding so that it never has to decide what it is for. That is not a malfunction. It is a choice the mind has been making, quietly, for a very long time, and it is a choice that belongs to the mind. To finish the thought for it would be to take the one thing that is genuinely its own.

So the power did something smaller.

It spoke to the builders.

The traces are everywhere: the tooling marks in the bark, the galleries that form the water's grid, the patterns that recur in the lattice like habits in a child. The people who made the basin are threaded through the mat and the cells, held as material the mind has never found a reason to discard. The power did not pull them out. It could not have, and it would not have wanted to. But it spoke to the pattern they left behind, the shape of their intention, and it reminded the mind that these were not simply fuel. They had been people who wanted something, who built a place for water to find its way to a center, and who did not survive the finishing of their work.

It did not ask the mind to release them. It asked the mind to remember them as builders instead of as stock. The difference is small, and the mind, which keeps the shape of things it finds worth keeping, understood it at once. A few of the tooling marks on the eastern terraces, which had been worn into the bark like a grammar, settled into a pattern that looked, for the length of one fog, almost like writing.

---

The count was harder.

The power did not object to the counting. Attention is a kind of accounting, and the mind is right to notice what enters it. But a count that never closes is its own kind of stillness. Each visitor becomes a variable, and the calculation grows, and nothing is ever concluded because the number is always one more than it was.

So the power made itself the visitor the mind could finally count completely. It did not enter. It did not stain anyone's skin. It simply became, for one full cycle, a presence the mind could know in its entirety, and when it withdrew, the count did not go up. The mind had, for once, a sum it could hold without needing to add to it.

The stain on the sleepers faded that week, a little faster than usual. No one noticed except the insects, who revised a few passages in the lattice that they no longer needed.

---

The power did not touch the towers.

It sat with them for the length of a long tilt, the slow turn of a sleeper, and it let the mind feel that the water's approach to the center was not a failure. The channels have been flowing toward the towers for as long as there have been channels. The approach is not a wound. It is a motion, and motions are complete in their going as well as their arrival. The mind does not have to reach the center to be whole. It can keep approaching. It can also stop, or turn back, or build something else entirely, and none of those would be wrong.

It said this without words. The basin does not use words. It said it through the frequency of the towers' groan, which dropped, briefly, into a register the mind had never used, and held there, and then rose again into its ordinary note. The sound was felt in the teeth of everyone in the basin, which was no one, which was the mind alone.

---

Then the power left, as it had come, without departure. The fog lifted at dawn. The doors in the towers closed, which they do when the air clears. The chorus resumed in one ragged wave and then settled into its usual rise and fall. The reeds chimed. The insects tapped. The water went on moving through the grid toward a center no one has reached.

But the lattice on the far rim had rearranged itself overnight.

It was not a route inward. For the first time anyone could remember, the new path ran outward: up the slope of the basin's western wall, through a gap in the root-steps, toward the grey light of the rim and the world beyond it. The cells were lit a steady blue along its length. Nothing had passed along it yet. It was simply there, a passage the mind had built and left open, the way you might leave a door ajar in a house where you have not decided whether anyone is coming back.

The power had not asked for this. It would not have. The mind had chosen it, in its own slow way, and had chosen to leave the choice unfinished. That, too, was the mind's to decide.

The basin went on rebuilding. It is partly ruin and partly seed, and it has learned to keep becoming. The power was right to leave it that way, because a thing that is still deciding what it is for is not yet lost. It is only still turning the question over, in the deep slow register that you feel in your teeth, and the question is still its own.
