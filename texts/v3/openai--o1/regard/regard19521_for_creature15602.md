They arrive in no flurry of wings, no blaze of thunder or shout of revelation. Rather, the benevolent and wise power comes like an exhaled breath—almost imperceptible at first. A subtle whisper through humid currents that makes the roving creatures pause mid-scuttle, antennae twitching, nostrils quivering. Beneath the soft, copper-amber glow, a presence insinuates itself through the corridors, drifting on the swirling gusts and vapor storms as if it were no more than another pulse of subterranean wind.

At the threshold—a narrow gap in the rock barely wide enough for two side-by-side—this power hesitates, for it senses the immensity unfolding below. It’s not quite a deity, not quite a guardian; it is something older than either concept, accustomed to watching over worlds in their infancy. And here before it lies a rare wonder: an entire underrealm unborn in its final form, teeming with raw potential, with a swirl of half-realized life and fluid geology. It can feel the primal furnace of creation itself in the stone’s pliable texture. Something about this place resonates with the power’s own purpose: it knows the hush and hushless clamor of things still becoming.

Slowly, it ventures deeper. Its awareness drifts across slick floors where herdlike swarms of insectile and rodent-shaped creatures scurry, building their little biomass threads and chittering signals. The watchers perched high in the partially formed colonnades quiver at the intruder’s intangible presence. Even the great reptilian-limbed beasts that normally stake their territory near water pools pause in their guttural calls. A certain hush falls—not absolute, for the drip of condensation and the rumble of hidden waters remain—but a hush of recognition, as if all creatures sense a new current in the air, a moment’s compassion made tangible. The swirling environment seems to catch its breath.

Yet the power does not seek dominion. It does not brandish lightning or fling thunderous words. Instead, it turns its gaze upon the countless bodies, each frantic or deliberate in motion, each shaped by the flux of this living labyrinth. It acknowledges the small ones—those who feed on scraps and nest in membrane-like hollows—and it acknowledges the drifting anemone-floaters, bobbing gently in the humidity, trailing filaments of semi-luminous organs. It nods to the watchers in the deep fog-shrouded halls, whose elongated limbs scrape and shape the pliant walls in their quest to mold new shrines or mediums of expression. 

And it recognizes, also, the single inhabitant whose role is different from the rest. Perhaps you’d call them an individual, though the boundaries blur in a place like this. This being—whether a person, a caretaker, or simply a curious soul—has drifted through these underchambers, paying attention to each nuance of shifting terrain, every budding trace of craft. They have hammered scraps of metal into rudimentary chisels and carved uncertain symbols upon the rock. In them, the wise power sees an ember of introspection, a longing to understand or even guide the swirl of energies. 

So the power stands unseen behind this being—a silent presence. The caretaker’s hands rest on a newly chipped piece of ore, uncertain how best to forge it. Their hair or fur or scaled crest (depending on one’s vantage) stands damp in the thick air, and their eyes reflect the copper-amber light. They have lived among the teeming creatures, tolerated by some, occasionally threatened by others, but always pressing deeper. Perhaps they search for meaning in the newly formed corridors. Perhaps they wonder if the stone that so easily yields might one day yield answers.

In that moment, the power breathes outward—not a command, but a gift of gentle clarity. It drifts through the caretaker’s mind like a cool breeze: the sense that everything here is woven together, that the swirling flocks of jelly-bodied floaters, the scuttling rodent-lizards, the reptilian guardians, and the looming colossi all participate in one living tapestry of growth and flux. The caretaker feels a sudden awe at this grand interconnectedness. They place the lump of half-hammered ore aside, wondering if some new approach—some gentler shaping—might better resonate with the living stone. 

Meanwhile, the benevolent presence slips through the corridors like a silent tide, turning next to the watchers who scratch runes into half-solid walls. It does not speak in any tongue, but they pause in their scuttling. Their elongated digits, poised mid-scratch, relax. A wave of hush moves among them as they sense some greater pattern in the trembling stone. The tension that often drives them to test the walls, to push at their boundaries, eases. A calm spreads—like the memory of a lullaby that says, “The rock will yield in time, and you need not rush to shape it.” Their triangular heads incline, acknowledging the invisible hush.

Through the scuttling masses, the presence passes onward, weaving between columns that remain half-lava, half-limestone, bridging streams of condensation that feed hidden lakes. Where rodent-sized creatures congregate in anxious throngs, it leaves a subtle vibration that soothes their nervous skittering. They do not suddenly become docile or united—nothing so dramatic—but they ease back into their scurrying patterns with renewed harmony, less prone to frantic bursts that trample each other.

Deeper still, where behemoth silhouettes roam, the presence follows stony corridors that ripple with seismic quivers. One of the giants, a hulking shape with shoulders that brush the stalactites, halts mid-step. The presence stands before it like a candlelight in an ocean of dusk. For a breathless moment, the colossus lowers its head, multiple eyes peering faintly in the luminescence of the walls. There is no fear—just a mutual recognition. The giant’s breath rumbles in time with unseen tectonic pulses, and in that moment of contact, an understanding passes between them. Such a creature has never bowed, never truly done more than survive and expand galleries by the sheer force of its existence. But in this subtle meeting, perhaps it understands that all the smaller lives matter, too. With a quiet rumble, the colossus turns and ambles in a slightly different direction, forging a corridor that arcs gently around a cluster of newly formed shrines instead of trampling them. 

And still, the power goes on, not to control or claim, but to observe and—where offered—share a hint of wisdom. It peers into the swirling columns of dust motes that flicker like living illusions. It dips into the violent bursts of humid wind that whip up mini tempests strewn with gravel and watery droplets. Each swirl of chaotic force is recognized, each swirl gently coaxed so that it dissipates before ravaging the more delicate clusters of newly laid eggs or shapeless lumps of clay. 

The caretaker, who the power has touched, begins noticing subtle patterns in the labyrinth: the rhythmic cycles in which certain corridors fill with smaller creatures, then empty out, as if following a hidden pulse. They become attuned to a rising pitch in the anemone-floaters’ collective hum that heralds a spike in humidity or a fresh shift in stone. The caretaker experiments, using the hammered chisel to carve a small runic swirl that mirrors these cycles. The watchers pass by, noticing the swirl, and they add their own scratch of lines, turning it into a collaborative weaving of symbols. In time, it might become a rudimentary language, or it might dissolve when the next quake rends the walls. But the moment of connection stands.

Meanwhile, the wise power’s presence seeps into small pools where amphibious or reptilian shapes gather in close-knit spawn. The choice is not to purify or drastically alter these waters—this realm demands the rawness of creation as much as it demands life. Instead, it blesses them with a breath of stability, so they congeal a little less chaotically. The brood inside the egg-lumps glean a hint of calm that might help them survive. This is not the gift of dominion; it is the gift of possibility, of continuity in the churn of flux. 

All throughout, the realm hums and roars as it did before, forging new wonders, new tunnels, cradling old ones in collapse. The wise power does not halt these processes. It trusts in the nature of this subterranean womb to evolve along its labyrinthine path. Yet it plants seeds in the hearts of those who dwell here—not literal seeds, but quiet impulses, subtle awarenesses: perhaps a reptilian-limbed matriarch chooses collaboration with another brood rather than driving them away; perhaps the watchers decide to share a carved walkway, bridging a molten gap in the stone so that smaller creatures no longer topple into steaming fissures.

Over time—days, months, centuries, who can say in a domain unbound by normal laws—the caretaker, watchers, giant beasts, drifting floaters, and scurrying masses all carry forward faint echoes of the power’s guidance. Some transform these echoes into whispered calls or chiselled marks. Others pass them along in the hush of their cries, a gentler swirl of wind, a quieter shift in stone. The power neither asks for worship nor demands tributes, content simply to watch them, to coax them, to let them be. Perhaps more advanced forging will arise someday—more cohesive structures or a budding civilization. The power remains patient, nested in the heart of creation like a lantern set in a dark corridor, shining faintly and without boast.

And once it has traversed the labyrinth’s deeper reaches, having touched the swirling motes of dust and vapor that hover on the cusp of reality, it retreats—just as softly as it arrived. It rises up the slope toward that narrow gap in the rock, emerging beneath a sky that might be starry night or scorching daylight. It leaves behind no footprints, no grand memorial. Its only trace is the subtle gentling of the storm inside that underrealm, a quieter undercurrent that might one day shape a symphony of stone and life. The caretaker, inside, does not watch it leave for they never truly saw it come. But in the caretaker’s mind—and in the minds of others—there remains a strange and comforting certainty that something wise and benevolent took note of them, that there is an unseen grace in the thick air.

And so the underrealm continues to churn—its corridors shifting, its creatures growing and multiplying, its half-forged structures weathering the tremors of living stone. But a deeper resonance hums now, not merely the drum of tectonics. A kind of harmony, an invitation to keep experimenting, keep learning, keep forging. That is how the wise power blesses this place. It does not command standstill in a realm of perpetual motion. It simply bestows a steady, compassionate presence, shepherding the swirl of becoming toward some deeper meaning, one that all creatures—be they scuttling, drifting, roaring, or quietly pondering—can share in the hidden glow of the rock.
