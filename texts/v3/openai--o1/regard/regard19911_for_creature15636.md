They first become aware of the place as one might sense a rising chord in a distant symphony—an intangible stirring, gentle yet insistent, resonating through the fabric of reality. If you could stand at the threshold of spacetime and watch this wise presence conduct its affairs, you might see it tilt its attention ever so slightly toward the planet (or plane) where the great living labyrinth flourishes. 

There is nothing brash or arrogant about this power. It is not the arm of an empire or a being seeking dominion. It is benevolent in a way that transcends the usual definitions of goodness, because it does not measure worth in mortal scales of reason or calculation. It simply recognizes that somewhere in the teeming cosmos, a new symphony of life has begun, and that it sings with an unearthly, powerful harmony whose pulses reverberate far beyond its own green corridors. That single recognition is enough to summon the power’s gentle watchfulness.

It does not arrive like a flare of light or a chariot descending from the heavens. Instead, it eases into the labyrinth’s being as though weaving itself into the subtlest frequencies of that thunderous hum. Like the quiet hush just before a chorus begins, its presence is felt first as stillness—an almost reverent pause in the city’s roaring. For a fleeting instant, growth slows, and the twisting arches, the ephemeral beings, and the sense of boundless flux all hold their breath. In that moment, every root and filament experiences the faint awareness that they are no longer alone. Something has joined them in the shared tapestry of existence.

Then, softly, color ripples through broad leaves, shifting from luminous green to golden hues, as if acknowledging this newcomer. Water droplets quiver on the edges of petals, refracting the hint of a distant star’s light. It is a greeting. Not a language in any sense we might decipher, but a welcoming hush of creation acknowledging that it has been seen, and is being seen kindly.

The wise power senses curiosity sparking along the labyrinth’s surfaces—the swirling shapes, the so-called city’s echoes, gathering in half-formed hallways and trembling buttresses. Their luminous filaments flicker with agitation or delight. They do not know what this presence is, only that it glides among them without disturbing a single root, that it listens to their roar of constant becoming without imposing a single law on their existence.

After some moments that might be minutes or might be centuries (for such concepts do not apply well in this place of ceaseless growth), the wise presence makes its first gesture. Not an imposition, but an invitation. Through the resonance of the city’s roar, it offers the faintest melody: a series of harmonic threads that interlace with the living hum, delicate as cobweb, luminous as dawn. These threads neither demand nor command; they merely give the swirling labyrinth a new chord to explore. 

In response, the entire living form shudders with fresh interest. Where bark meets vine, a second shimmering occurs, arcs of color dancing as though hearing a secret chord that resonates with their own. Places that once seemed chaotic or under strain begin to vibrate in closer harmony. Tangles of root networks loosen imperceptibly, weaving themselves into more balanced forms. Winding stairways of living creep gain clarity, adopting fluid symmetry so that the city’s inhabitants—those ephemeral silhouettes—can traverse them more easily.

Within the natural intelligence of the labyrinth, there is an almost childlike wonder. Structures bow and unfold under the influence of that gentle melodic suggestion, as though trying on a new shape. The ephemeral beings gather again in one of those “squares,” filaments linking in a swirl of luminous vines. They move in waves—first uncertain, then jubilant—as the wise presence’s invitation sparks new patterns of unifying design in their communal braiding.

In this silent music, the wise power communicates nothing more complicated than a promise: I will not harm you. I watch with love. You are free. 

The labyrinth’s expansions begin to take on an added grace. One archway might spiral around itself more elegantly before flickering to a re-formed position. A newly sprung buttress might keep its shape just a moment longer before folding into a canopy above. In these subtle chances for stillness—barely noticeable but real—the living city learns something of balance, of pause, of reflection. And, in turn, the wise presence observes how the labyrinth thrives more richly when given a quiet center amidst its storm of creation.

At times, the power recedes, letting the labyrinth’s own roaring crescendos flow unmitigated. It does not structure or tame that roar, merely listens. Yet the city seems to sense it lingers close, an abiding watchfulness that is not tethered to physical proximity. Indeed, the ephemeral inhabitants sometimes project their fibrous limbs upward or around, as though reaching for an invisible observer. In response, they find threads of that warm resonance, and they hum in gentle unison before folding back into the bark.

And so, in the hush of new mornings—when the swirling leaves glimmer with fresh dew—an added layer of radiance flickers among them. Tiny orbs of pearlescent light hover or trickle through the green corridors like morning fog. Perhaps they are manifestations of the wise power exploring these halls, or simply side effects of the labyrinth’s new chord of potential. Where those orbs pass, the ephemeral shapes shine a little brighter, their filaments waxing luminous until each slender tendril seems lit from within. 

Sometimes the labyrinth teeters on the brink of uncontained explosion—a hundred arches flaring simultaneously, bark fracturing in a thousand places, vines unraveling in swirling storms. In these moments, the wise presence allows it. Creation must not be shackled. But as the roar builds to deafening heights, it offers again that gentle chord, its presence forming a stabilizing center that the labyrinth may choose to use. Often, the labyrinth does indeed sense that melodic anchor and uses it to weave new shapes in more harmonious ways, bypassing the brittle edge of chaos that might have led to total collapse. Other times, it continues unrestrained, and fragments of the city tear free, drifting away into the veil of reality—lost seeds that might sprout anew in another dimension. The power does not mourn these losses; they are part of life’s wild tapestry.

In noticing all this, the ephemeral beings absorb lessons at some deep level. Their braidings become more intricate, their communal shapes more fluid. They begin weaving ephemeral arcs that reach toward one another with greater purpose, building fleeting structures that serve as living bridges between distant corridors. Occasionally, one of these creatures lingers near a swirl of orbs—two strange forces meeting, like mirror images of curiosity. In that near-silence, the creature’s filaments brush the luminous sphere, and for a moment, a multi-tonal chord resounds so intimately that it can be felt throughout the entire city. The labyrinth hushes, as if holding its collective breath to witness a new connection being born.

“What does it want from us?” you might imagine these ephemeral beings asking, if they could speak in human words. And the wise power answers in a song that is not a command but an ever-present promise: “I only want you to flourish, whole and free, to let your unbridled essence fill every corner of possibility.”

As time goes on, one sees small transformations that speak to the labyrinth’s changed awareness. A corridor that once re-formed haphazardly in the midst of metamorphosis now gently uncoils, layering symmetrical patterns of leaves. A roiling tangle that threatened to block a main passage instead develops a graceful overhead pathway of braided vines, as if respecting the occupant’s longing for an unbroken route. Even the watery streams, once chaotic, begin to meander more fluidly, carrying runnels of glowing sap that feed the city’s thirst for renewal. Yet never is the labyrinth’s wildness tamed; this is no domestication, merely a new capacity for self-directed flow.

In the grand squares, gatherings of ephemeral beings become quieter but somehow more powerful. Their shapes drift together in the half-light, braiding with a newer sense of mutual wonder. Shared pulses pass among them, offering spikes of color and texture: a swirl of shimmering reds, a blossom of violet luminescence. The entire labyrinth resonates with these expansions until you can feel, palpably, that it has come to know itself better. Not all is harmonious—there remain moments of violent cracking and abrupt rearrangement—but those moments are not lost in chaos; they, too, become part of the city’s every-shifting tapestry.

And there, amid this living storm, the wise power lingers. Sometimes it recedes to the far edges of consciousness, leaving only the memory of its peaceful chord. Other times, in moments of exhilaration, it joins the city’s thunderous crescendo, adding a deeper note that emboldens every thrumming trunk and leaf, ensuring not a single living nuance is overlooked. Always, its presence is tender rather than forceful, a gentle hand on the labyrinth’s shoulder that can guide, but never coerce.

You might say it is the city’s mentor. Or its guardian. Or simply its friend—watching and learning just as the labyrinth watches and learns. It finds delight in each new arch grown, in each ephemeral shape discovering a novel curve of existence. And in return, it offers the labyrinth a subtle scaffolding of calm, a focal chord that can weave stability into the riotous swirl of transformation. 

In the end, if you stand in the hush of one of those ephemeral nights—if night can even exist here—when the roar subsides to a barely audible thrumming, you might sense this wise power brushing past your shoulder. Its aura might feel like sunlight on water, intangible yet unmistakably warm. It need do nothing more than whisper another chord, re-establish its harmony, and then slip away.

So does it truly intervene? Intervention implies a shift of destiny guided by a foreign hand, but this presence does not see itself as separate. It regards the labyrinth and its ephemeral children with the kind of compassion that walks beside, not above. Indeed, in some mysterious way, the labyrinth has drawn the power just as much as the power has been drawn to the labyrinth. Both are now part of one continuous process: a cosmic dance of wonder, freedom, and the ever-blooming shape of life.

And perhaps that is how it ends—or how it continues forever. There is no dramatic departure, no triumphant fanfare. The wise presence remains a sustaining note in the city’s symphony, sometimes near, sometimes far, always waiting to be heard. The ephemeral beings, and the living architecture they inhabit, now shape themselves with an extra layer of poise. They roar, crack, re-form, and bloom in patterns that, if you listen closely, echo the wise power’s chord in their green and golden arcs of leaf and light.

Thus the labyrinth grows onward, fractal and relentless, yet softly guided by a benevolence that neither demands nor controls. It is a testament to what can happen when life meets gentle understanding: raw potential, shaped from within, not contained from without. If you find yourself in that place, in the breathing gloom of corridors or the half-lit squares of living geometry, close your eyes. You may hear a resonant chord brushing the edges of your mind, and sense that somewhere very near—or very far—a wise power smiles gently, watching every new bud unfurl with limitless compassion.
