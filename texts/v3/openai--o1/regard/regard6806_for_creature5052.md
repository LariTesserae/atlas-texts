They drift into the Eave of Vertices like an exhalation from a newly awakened spirit. No one can quite pinpoint their arrival—no siren call announces their presence, no radiant beam cuts through the twilight. Instead, a subtle change settles over the city. A newfound hush descends, gathering at the edges of the near-bottomless chasms and faintly vibrating along metal walkways. 

It begins with the smallest of creatures. The Gimbal-Scurries pause in their restless scuttling and crane their spidery forelimbs as though listening. Mosswalkers halt mid-step, letting water drip down their leafy exteriors without a sound. Even the towering factories seem to pause for a heartbeat, pistons and valves suspending their relentless churn. Only the hum of the city remains, pulsing in the background—and in that hum, something new stirs: a warmth, a gentleness that neither stone nor steel can fully contain.

Within moments, wisps of golden luminescence bloom in unexpected alcoves. At first, they are only faint glimmers near rivulets or half-hidden mechanical ductwork, but they grow over a matter of breaths: dancing threads of light pooling like liquid starlight curled around guardrails, trickling across the faces of raw stone. A hush sweeps each terrace as though the entire city and its residents are leaning in to witness. The Tethered Wisps, once ephemeral watchers at the corners of reality, drift forward in greeting, swirling their silhouettes about the golden glow in a curious, reverent interplay.

That golden glow is the herald—and soon the presence behind it manifests. Some describe it as a towering effigy wrapped in robes woven from starfields. Others see a shape more akin to an ancient tree with boughs of pure light. Still others observe only a column of shimmering haze, the edges of which exude an ineffable kindness. The truth is as multi-faceted as Eave of Vertices itself: the power presents itself in forms each eye can bear, coalescing differently for watchers in different corners.

It does not speak in words. Instead, a resonance travels through the city’s metal beams and unborn stone. Beneath that resonance, old fractures in the geometry flicker, transforming from ragged tears into swirling, self-mending arcs. The watchers on the catwalks—petroforgers with sledgehammers still resting across their shoulders, machinists leaning against humming equipment, and Aerie-Kin perched along precarious ledges—observe in awe. For the first time in memory, the city’s cracks and dimensional loops seem not menacing but oddly harmonious, as though carefully being re-knitted into the tapestry of spires.

In that moment, the presence touches the city in countless tiny acts of benevolence:

• Where walkways threaten to decay into rust, a thin film of light seeps across rivets and cables, regenerating them with a gentle sparkle of molten brilliance.  
• Where newly formed rock stands brittle and uncertain, the presence provides an infusion of quiet strength, each crystalline seam settling into sturdier alignment.  
• Where dwellers clasp cold hands around battered industrial heaters, the aura of warmth cascades over them, suffusing the gloom with a comforting haze that evokes memories of home—a sense of belonging that transcends species or mechanical parts.  

Even the deep-chested Rift-Bisons, far in the lower caverns, sense the shift. Their heavy footsteps echo with a subdued calm, and in the gloom, motes of golden dust swirl around their basalt-like horns. A mighty leader among them—a great beast with a crest shaped like an unfinished cathedral spire—lifts its head and bellows once. But instead of the reality-rending wave that normally follows, the bellow drifts upward in a harmonious undertone, reinforcing the gentle hum that blankets the Eave of Vertices.

The presence saturates the city with what can only be called encouragement—a promise that, though edges and dimensions remain fractured and uncertain, there is the seed of sustainability here, the potential to grow not just outward but inward, forging balance between creation and caution. Whispers rise that this might be a cosmic caretaker of sorts, a guardian who tends to nascent worlds on the fringes of the possible. Or perhaps it is a manifestation of the city’s own unarticulated will, made real by the longing of its denizens. 

No single theory satisfies all. The Oracle-Class, should anyone happen to glimpse them in that peculiar stutter of time, appear behind half-formed thresholds and watch with veiled interest. Some among the Oracles’ number bow slightly, as if in gratitude, before they disappear in another flicker of dimensional displacement. Meanwhile, the machinists kneel in wordless acceptance, their gloved hands pressed to the cool metal floor. The Mosswalkers place slender fingers on the new shoots of vine and lichen that spring to life beneath their feet, a faint hum of thanks vibrating in the leaves. The petroforgers set down their gear, feeling a sense of reassurance that the mountain will not crumble so easily beneath their efforts. The Aerie-Kin ruffle their fractal feathers, letting the light settle in the folds of each metal-mesh vane. Even the Gimbal-Scurries pause their endless repairs, gently pulsing with contentment. 

In time, the wise presence moves on—though “moves” may not be the correct term. Its aura slowly withdraws from each nook and cranny, leaving behind a subtle afterglow, a memory impressed into the metal, stone, and hearts. Threads of the golden radiance remain, woven into newly stabilized struts or newly nurtured moss; indeed, they linger in the quiet hush after the city’s heartbeat resumes its thunderous cycle. Distorted corridors still exist, fractal illusions persist, expansions continue in their slow, unstoppable tide. But now there is a tempered sense of possibility—that these expansions need not unravel the world if balanced with reverence.

In the days—or weeks, or months—following this visitation (for time is notoriously pliable here), stories circulate among the dwellers. An Aerie-Kin claims to have soared through a pocket of shining air that buoyed their wings with effortless grace, where the presence had most clearly manifested. A machinist swears their once-rigid contraption now purrs like a living creature, as if a gentle heart had been installed among the gears. A Mosswalker’s vines bloom with rare flowers that release an uplifting scent, reminiscent of dawn in some gentle, far-off land. The city leadership, perched in their highest vantage points, quietly revise future expansion plans, adding subtle, grace-filled footnotes about preserving the newly bound edges of reality.

Some say the presence left behind a message transmitted through the humming of the city’s spires—a wordless impetus that encourages stewardship of the fragile geometry. Others describe it as a living dream that united the inhabitants, if only for an instant, into a single tribe gently caressed by cosmic acceptance. Whatever the truth, a singular feeling remains: The Eave of Vertices was seen and known by a wise and benevolent gaze, and within that regard, the city discovered a deeper way to exist—whole, even within its endless permutations of stone and steel.

And so daily life continues, but something is changed. A subtle unity courses through the riveted walkways and the creeping moss. New expansions are planned more carefully, with respect for the difficult balance that knits city to mountain. When the thunderous roar of the factories revs back to full pitch, it is no longer a hymn of blind industry—there’s a rhythm beneath the clang, an echo of the benevolence that passed through. With each rivet hammered, each vine grown, each fractal wing flared, this place affirms its capacity for life that not only endures but flourishes, resonant with the gentle gift left behind by the visitor who asked for nothing in return.

Perhaps one day that presence will return in a swirl of golden threads, or perhaps its imprint is now perpetual. In the Eave of Vertices—where gravity skews and geometry curls back upon itself—the surest thing is that changes come slowly but inevitably. And in the hush between changes, rest assured that the city has known the touch of something greater than any twisting corridor or cosmic tear. A promise was left here, woven in shimmering lines along the scaffolds and integrated into the lullaby hum of the steam vents. And whether it endures for centuries or eons, the dwellers will remember that promise each time they reach out a hand—flesh, mechanical, or mossy-green—and feel the living city pulse back in gentle answer.
