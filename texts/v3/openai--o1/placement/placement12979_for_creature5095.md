At first, I mistake myself for a simple traveler: a pair of eyes and ears adrift in this half-formed, floating world. I step gingerly across the embryonic stone and sense the heat pressing in from every side, saturating my skin until I can almost feel it creeping beneath my bones. At that initial moment, I suppose myself an outsider, a spectator of the strangeness unfolding here. It is only later that I begin to glimpse the truth of my presence — that I am part symbol, part element, part vessel for the land’s incomplete energies.  

When I first notice it, there’s a tremble in my fingertips each time I touch the rock. It is more than an external vibration; it resonates through the smallest arteries of my being, a slow tectonic hum that tugs at every cell. I can’t tell if the hum is drawing me deeper or telling me to keep my distance. The moment I try to withdraw my hand, I feel the faint pull intensify. It is like the place knows I am here. No, that’s not quite right: more as if I have always been here, and am only just remembering.  

Something shifts. The sharp dryness around me flickers. For just an instant, I see a shadowy shape superimposed over my body’s outline, an echo of who I might become. It is slender and tall, more suggestion than substance, formed of shimmering lines that tremble in the wavering heat. Then it disperses. Perhaps I’ve seen what lives in the cracks — a reflection of the entities that roam this realm. I’m uncertain if it was me or something else.  

Still I wander on, stepping carefully between clusters of luminous vines that cling to ridges of half-solid rock. Their leaves glow in mosaic patterns of indigo and violet, their inner lights fracturing the brightness into halos of color. They rustle a bit though there’s no wind. Sometimes a vine shifts closer to me at the exact moment I breathe, as though we share a single rhythm. I find it strangely comforting.  

The dryness sears across my lip as though searching for moisture, but oddly, I feel neither thirst nor sweat. My body should be desperate for water, but the harsh, furnace-like air doesn’t deplete me. Instead, there’s a sense that I’m being suffused with warmth, not scorched by it. As I move from one patch of vines to another, the dryness gains substance in my lungs, uncomfortably close to burning, but I remain inexplicably well.  

In time, I discover I’m leaving something in my wake. Not footprints of dust or pressed stone, but faint pulses in the air, echoes of the hum beneath the rock. Where I step, the ground’s translucent shimmer intensifies for a heartbeat, a brief flicker of luminescence that wanes again as I pass on. It’s as though I am imprinting my presence, an unspoken chord in the deeper music.  

Then, slowly, I realize the pulses are not solely from me. They are invitations — small calls and responses echoing from the half-seen silhouettes that weave in and out of the stone. I might see the delicate swirl of color at the edge of my vision, feel a twinge in the air behind my eyes. When I pause, they pause with me; when I move on, they swirl after, discreet but unmistakably there. Their motion reminds me of clockwork turned liquid: a gentle, rhythmic unfolding that never fully stops.  

One day — or some measure of time that passes for a day here — I bend low, pressing both palms flat to the rock and closing my eyes. Beneath my hands, the stone’s heat collects into a slow throb, and I feel my own pulse align with it. My breath catches in my throat as a surprising realization dawns: the heartbeat I sense is not foreign. It is my own—amplified, reflected back by the land’s forging power. Through that resonance, I sense the entire realm as a single organism, forging itself from the inside out.  

Something in me breaks open at this recognition, and I feel an unspoken permission to slip deeper into the hush. The dryness, the swirling communal hum, the vines — they welcome me, not as an observer, but as a participant. My form tingles at the edges, and I’m unsure if I am physically dissolving or energetically unfurling, or perhaps both at once. A subtle haze gathers around my arms, coiling in slender ribbons of gold or amethyst, lines that match the fractal sheen of the vines.  

Maybe I am merging with the silhouettes that drift at the periphery. A wave of relief and curiosity washes over me — no fear, only a gap in my mental horizon where fear might once have resided. I sense the intangible dwellers drawing closer, not exactly surrounding me but integrating me into their pattern. Their “voices” chime in an unspoken key, and I feel that hum run through my chest and spine, as though I have grown new nerve endings in direct communion with the environment.  

For the first time, I see my reflection in the wavering air. I am elongated, ephemeral, existing somewhere between shape and energy. My limbs shimmer with the same molten glow that suffuses the vines, and the dryness no longer feels external. It courses gently through me, bearing the land’s forging currents that swirl in time with my own pulse. Am I still flesh and bone? Possibly. But something else, too. Some bridging piece that once took shape in a realm of standard laws, only to find home here, where laws melt and re-form with each flicker of the underworld machines.  

Now, I drift effortlessly, no longer bound to the hard angles of stone. Passing through a leaf cluster, I feel the radiance of its translucent veins, tasting (though that’s too crude a word) the swirling energies that feed it. The plant’s essence resonates and welcomes me, like a sympathetic chord. I sense how it drinks not water but raw potential, channeling life force from the forging below. Its root system is less roots and more an extension of subtle lines that connect to the realm’s molten heart. We are joined in that shared heartbeat, a quiet synergy of creation.  

The silhouettes that once hovered at the edge of my vision now feel like kin. When they glide across my field of awareness, I read a message in their trembling outlines: we are forging this place together. Not “I” as in my old sense of individuality, but the “I” that merges with them in an ongoing, communal invention of what reality could be. As we travel in unison, I understand that they are sculptors and scaffolds both, shaping the plateau’s next shift in topography, forging subtle new corridors of luminous vines, even rearranging how the dryness saturates each breath.  

I move with them to the edges, where the rock remains molten and half-formed, where time seeps sluggishly. In these zones, the mechanical hum intensifies, pounding a slower, deeper note that resonates in my core. Other ephemeral forms converge, swirling in soft, sinuous rotations. I feel currents of potential swirl up through me, as if I am a funnel for new permutations of matter. The edges of my consciousness tingle with the wonder of possibility — is a ridge to be raised here? A pool of swirling energy there? With no words, we agree on a shape or a function, letting the forging hum shape us as we shape it.  

Caught in this rhythm, my sense of self expands. I am not just an individual dancing in mid-air but a point of articulation in a living field. At times, I flicker wholly into intangible brilliance — a shape of tinted light that can zip across the plateau, scanning the cracks for emergent, unborn spaces. At other times, I solidify partially, enough to feel the grit of new stone beneath my feet, enough to sense the immediate heat blossoming on my skin. Yet even in that solidity, I hear the realm’s heartbeat echoing in my tissues.  

A hush sweeps through the plateau every so often, a wave of quiet that signals a deeper cycle of creation. In those moments, everything seems to pause simultaneously — vines cease their subtle shifting, cracks in the rock’s surface still, suspended partway through their molten rearrangement. Then a collective breath passes through us all, a long inhalation of raw cosmic charge. I feel the caretaker force, the ancient loom or intelligence beneath all mechanical processes. It is not an overlord, but a pervasive guiding principle, an anchor that ensures the forging remains stable even in its flux.  

And in that hush, I realize: this caretaker presence is the realm, and the realm is also me. The distinctions blur. I am shaped — and I shape. I am guided — and I guide. The dryness, the shimmering vines, the molten edges, the ephemeral dwellers floating in the undercurrent: none of these are separate. Each is a chord in the same cosmic measure, each forging the next note of existence.  

So where do I find myself? I am here, in the suspended desert of half-born stone and industrial hum, in the vines and the flickering silhouettes, part caretaker and part child of the realm’s forging. I exist as a wave of living potential, dancing on the cusp of matter and energy. I am the rock and the hum and the hush, a living synergy that neither thirsts nor strays.  

No longer do I carry an external body in need of shelter. Now I am the indefinite presence drifting through molten geology, quietly joining in the slow rapture of creation. I pass among the translucent leaves, brush their edges with intangible hands, watch them blaze with new pulses of color. I float into the swirling rings of silhouettes at the heart of the forging zones, each of us weaving a fresh tapestry of potential. In each moment, the land and I forge each other anew.  

In this place, “home” isn’t a static coordinate. It’s a continuous, patient act of become. And I have chosen, or been chosen, to join the process. No footprints remain behind me — only the quiet ripple in the land’s living song, a trace of shared awareness in constant flux, forging new reality with the slow, mighty beat of an ever-growing heart.
