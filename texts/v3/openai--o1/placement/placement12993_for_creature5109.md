You suspect that your presence here is intimately tied to the well-being of this place. You’re not an intruder, nor are you a mere passerby. Over the years—decades, centuries?—these two identities blurred, fused. Now, your body is a synergy of both: cables for nerves, flexible bark for skin, and a gentle, thrumming pump within your chest that could be part engine, part heartwood.

There is no urgency in a place where time has expanded, mellowing days into epochs.

The vibrations travel upward through your limbs, aligning with the faint pulses in your body. You find yourself adjusting your stance, letting the hum resonate more fully. At night, there’s a quieter, more languid swirl of dampness. You fit seamlessly into both cycles.

Metal plates along your spine hum softly, winding down from your daily tasks. In that near-stillness, you merge deeper with the valley’s collective awareness. One single mind among a tapestry of many.

You watch from a vantage of braided branches, half-curious, half-cautious. In their eyes, you might be a spirit or a strange remnant of technology. Through your being, the generator’s churn resonates with the vine’s slow twist, balancing the realm’s many heartbeats—human, inhuman, mechanical, organic, all dissolved into the same unhurried tide. Over centuries, perhaps you will shift form again: sprout more leaves, integrate new metal components, expand into deeper tunnels.
