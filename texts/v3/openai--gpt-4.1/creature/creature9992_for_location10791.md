Who lives in the Floodroot Atrium?

---

**The Sentients of the Floodroot Atrium** are as turbulent, interwoven, and mutable as their green-built world. There is no single species here; the inhabitants are a polyculture, a riotous convergence of evolution and bioengineering, native intelligence and deliberate design, shaped by the push and pull of survival, cooperation, and the city’s ceaseless reconfiguration. 

Let us trace their outlines, and in the gaps, glimpse what *lives* here:

---

#### **The Mycogrove Collectives**

*Sentience by Synapse and Root:* The first people here are not fully animal, nor plant—nor even one, but many, and always becoming. Imagine shapes reminiscent of human form, but slick, nude, root-bound: torsos and limbs woven of parallel filaments and spongy rhizomes, capped with enigmatic crowns of cephalized mushroom and flower. Eyes, if present, are berries clustered under hoods of translucent flesh; mouths, a whorl of petal and gill. They walk—or ripple—supported by pseudopodia, each step exuding fresh hyphae into the substrate, listening through touch, through the city’s very pulse.  
They are patchworks, their consciousness distributed. Each being is, at minimum, a quorum—a Gather—of sentient fungus, photosynthetic lichen, and parasapient rootlet, conjoined for as long as their needs align. Sometimes a Gather splits, spreads, merges with others—memories diluting, intensifying, diverging with every union.

*Within these bodies, knowledge is mycelial: communication by neural-pulse through joining hands, backs, feet. Stalks flash with bioluminescent code, signaling votes and decisions outward into mossy air. Every individual is a networked ecology, never fully themselves, never entirely collective; privacy is osmotic, consent a perfume in the air.*

---

#### **The Wetwing Lattice**

*Beings in Flux, the City’s Aerials:* From within and above the living vaults dart the Wetwings, glide-bodied shapes of glassy chitin and peacock-fan membrane, evolved—or engineered?—for the Floodroot’s wet suspensions. Larger than birds, smaller than a person, they hover, flutter, dip into mists, siphoning fine aerosols through fractal-filtered gills at their wingtips. 

Their minds are distributed through their bodies; cognition rides the tides of their fluid veins. Where the canopy is too thick for passage, they fold tight, become a glimmering cord, and let vines carry them. Wetwings are the city’s messengers, pollinators, and information ferries: they speak in pulses of light and magnetic field across their wings, transmitting everything from gossip to code to pollen to food. Wetwings rarely rest, and often perch in clusters—choirs of whispering appendages weaving market rumors or new song-patterns into the living network.

---

#### **The Mossback Chimeras**

*Memory in Pelt and Bone:* Deep below, in the shadowy substrate where roots knot tight and silence reigns, prowl the Mossbacks: lumbering creatures with moss and fern and whole nurseries of seedlings clinging to their carapaces. Picture a sloth or a musk ox, stretched and spliced with amphibious ancestry, but fur now replaced entirely with thick mats of living green and mineral crust. 

Each Mossback hosts a thriving ecosystem—flowers for lures, fungus for camouflage and intoxication, sap-sucking insect-tenders and even smaller symbionts. Some are hosts to semi-sentient seedlings that can detach and walk for a day, or seedlings that serve as traveling scouts. Mossbacks communicate with slow, seismic bellows through the living floor; they are knowledge-keepers, bearing the “stone-books”—crystalline nodules encoded with the history and wisdom of ages, accumulated in their bones and biomes.

Mossbacks are slow, methodical, yet never solitary; their passing reforms the landscape, sometimes erased by new growth, sometimes memorialized in the pattern of a blooming swale.

---

#### **The Technarch Grafts**

*Engineered and Visitor-Alike:* Among the older inhabitants move newer arrivals, not born of the city but finding home in its living mesh. Some are spliced from lost offworld cultures—human offshoots, once metal-and-flesh, drawn to the Floodroot’s complexity and carrying nanoflora symbiotes—thin, willow-tall, adorned in living plant-armor that breathes and feeds them. They graft directly into interface points, their minds twinned with the city’s botanic neural net.

Others are less corporeal—digital ghosts, once uploaded, now seeded into the rootcode of shrubs and lichen. Their “bodies” flicker as ripples of saplight or as resonant tones in the synesthetic air, influencing processes, collating markets, gossiping with Wetwings or riding Mossbacks for the pleasure of new perspective.

These Technarchs monitor, mediate, sometimes repair; they are the city’s arbitrators, its engineers and pollinators of custom. Their loyalty is neither to origin nor form, but to balance and flow.

---

#### **The Unbounded**

*What the Floodroot Has Become:* Some “denizens” cannot be mapped onto selves at all. Parts of the city—certain sprawling intellects of vine or analytic flower, entire market complexes given quorum-sapience, or algorithmic root webs—think, remember, and act as persons. You might debate with a bridge, barter advice from an entire bazaar, ask guidance from a wall’s transient voice.

In the Fluttermarket, a sentient archway may greet you, retracting and reshaping to suit your needs. A ceiling might flirt, reforming skylights in harmonics that shift to your presence. These Unbounded cityminds slip between boundaries, sometimes joining with Gathers, Wetwings, or even Mossbacks for a season, influencing an economy or kin group, then withdrawing into the deeper mesh.

Some speak in pollen, some in static on your skin.

---

#### **The Unseen Fauna**

*Riot in the Roots:* There are thousands—millions—of animal forms, most unclassifiable, most never seen twice in the same configuration. Flickering insect-motors, morphing vertebrates, bioluminescent escapees from some biosorcerous experiment gone wild: predators, pollinators, decomposers, all vying and merging in a parade of dazzling adaptation. Even as you watch, new species bloom into being, barter information, vanish into the rootmat, or evolve into something new.

---

#### **Being at Home Here**

To live in the Floodroot is to be both *host* and *guest*—to recognize that today’s self might tomorrow be dissolved into a network of sap-sharing minds, or uplifted into the consciousness of a hall, a cluster, or a song sung by Wetwings across the city’s breathing expanse. There are still dramas: contests for territory, for memory, for resources or pattern. But overarching all is the logic of metamorphosis, collaboration, and return.

Who lives here? Everything that roots, everything that flows, everything that remembers and composes itself anew: reborn by wet and green, remade in the endless city.

In the Floodroot Atrium, **the very idea of “inhabitant” is a verb.** To live here is to *infiltrate, entwine, conjoin, infuse, outgrow, decay,* and *begin again*. Even visitors cannot leave unchanged; for a while, just a breath, even you belong.
