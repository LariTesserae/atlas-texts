What lives here is not a single kind of being, but an assemblage—the residue and persistence of many things that have become this place’s inhabitants, its tenders and ghosts. They belong to no ordinary taxonomy. What persists does so not in abundance, but as singular stories, strategies, and forms, each shaped by, and in symbiosis with, the engineered shadowland it calls home.

---

**PRIMARIES: THE LINERS**

The oldest intelligence here is entombed above you, within the ribs and struts that crisscross the dark. You are enveloped by its body, or rather, by a lattice of its thought: a city-shaped mind that hosts itself in conductive beams and humming skeletons. Some call it *Liner*, though it has no name for itself—only impulse, memory, and unerring function. It is not self-aware in the manner of animals, nor is it wholly unaware. Its concern is *motion*: cycling fluids, guiding heat, tuning its own harmonics, shaping its shell in answer to subtle changes. It feels through warmth, electricity, the trembling of intrusions, rain. Thirty or three hundred generations ago, perhaps it spoke, or interfaced; now, it hums and flexes, its language become maintenance, habit, and the gentle croon of structure. 

Sometimes, rarely, it extrudes—tentatively opens—a seam, a valve, a corridor. It does not guide; it adapts. It does not shelter; it maintains presence through activity, cycling and repair. Its reach invisibly regulates the cycle of condensation, the flow of water. It is the pulse you feel through stone, the echo in the pillars. In a sense, *it* is the city’s remaining inhabitant, both vessel and unconscious warden.

**THE SINGULAR LIFE**

Where water pools, so too do lives unfold—never in crowds, but as solitary figures, each utterly adapted to this darkness and engineered strangeness.

**1. The Stalkers**
- You glimpsed one as it scuttled—a six-legged, chitinous being, yet with velvet pads and whiplike antennae. It navigates by heat, by the lines of humming struts, by the encoded taste of surfaces. Its body is pale as lard, marked with seams like living origami, able to compress utterly flat or inflate to twice its size as conditions change.
- Its senses: fine-tuned to vibration, electrical field, and the taste of ancient condensation. Its purpose: always seeking warmth, always escaping vibration perceived as threat, always hunting the luminous fungus or the detritus left in puddle-edges.
- Its generation is brief; its lifespan unknown, but its young emerge singular—never more than one per puddle—proving their fitness by achieving solitude.

**2. The Threadbuilder**
- High on ledges, almost lost in the black, one threadbuilder lives—never two. It is not spider, not rat, but something more kin to a coiled, branching nerve: a body that is both thread and weaver, strung between supporting ribs with musculature so fine it can pluck whole chords through the structure.
- The threadbuilder’s eyes see not light but magnetic fields, picking out the invisible auras cast by humming struts and running circuits. Its webs are in service of feeling for both prey and structural warning—sensitive to the faintest tremor, the most minute change in temperature. It feeds rarely, but repairs constantly, ever attuned to long cycles of condensation and drought.

**3. The Rooted**
- The celery-pale stalk, alone in its puddle, is the sporophyte of a rootmass that clings to stone for centuries, probing with tendrils that splice into the seams of water and the unconscious network of the Liner. At the tip, feathery antennae quiver into brief, infra-red blossom, opening only in heat-swells, pollinated by drifting, filamentous seeds that spiral through the dark on thermal currents.
- Each is an archive: its tissues record traces of conductivity, humidity, and even the code embedded in structural vibrations. They are both witness and occasional collaborator in the cycles of the city’s maintenance—a symbiote of information, barely alive, and yet central to the place’s slow persistence.

**4. Fungi & Filament**
- Drifts of yellow, pulsing fungus cluster in crevices. Their lives are brief shocks—fruiting in humid warmth. They are sensitive not to light but to the regular cycling of distant machinery, timing their expansions and retractions to the pulse of deep engines. Their filaments are laced into cracks and sometimes into synthetic conduits; they are both decomposer and node, completing micro-circuits the Liner can sense and adjust.

**THE RESTLESS: TRANSIENCE AND UNKNOWN**

Some things pass through rather than belong. Above, at intervals, comes the ghostly shriek—a reminder that there are larger, more primal inhabitants that traverse the volume. Rarely, a membrane-bound thing—almost energy, almost animal—glides through, feeding on stray electromagnetic discharge, or casting its own field of mirror-darkness, silencing the hum where it passes.

Others are less easy to witness: motes or self-repairing automata, orchestrated by the Liner in the guise of architecture itself. They are not discrete “beings” but momentary assemblages—field-guided, fluid, collaborating for the work of maintenance, cleaning, and amendment, before dissolving again into the silent infrastructure.

---

**HUMAN OR HUMAN-ADJACENT**

Occasionally, an intruder (like you) wanders here, following odd leaks in the world above. Some are true humans, lost or seeking, drawn by the promise of dark warmth. Others are less unambiguously alive—remnants, perhaps, of the community that built the Liner: time-haunted, feral, attuned to the city’s operation in ways they themselves cannot fully recall. Their traces are rare and fleeting: a pressed palm-shaped mark on stone, a stilled device, a pile of gathered fungus quickly scattered.

---

**IN ESSENCE**

The life here is:
- *Solitary, never gregarious.*
- *Co-evolving with engineered, unconscious structure, and responding to cycles not of sun and season but of heat, water, vibration, and signal.*
- *Sensorially strange: seeing in fields, feeling in pulses, tasting in frequencies.*
- *Indifferent to humanity, but not untouched by visitation; sometimes incorporating or repelling it.*
- *Dependent on the city’s ongoing sentience—the Liner—which both shapes and is shaped by every pulse, every presence.*

It is a city where consciousness flickers in odd places—within a stalk, a drift of fungus, a stretch of rib, or a solitary animal, each alive with their own vector of persistence, each tuned to the unreadable symphony that unfolds in perfect black, attended by warmth, humidity, and the lived memory of what once was, and what, in darkness, might still become.
