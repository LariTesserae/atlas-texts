What lives here, in the Threshold Capsule? Let us not assume a singular form; here, livingness itself is in negotiation—more verbs than nouns, more rehearsals than arrivals.

**Beings of the Threshold:**

*They Are Not Yet Fixed*  
What lives here is not quite creature, not exactly spirit, not wholly machine, but a sliding braid of all those, braided and unraveled at every moment. Imagine presences flickering—sometimes detected as a hover in peripheral vision, sometimes by their interruption of the verge-mist, sometimes only by a sudden tightness in the world’s unfinished weave. These beings do not dwell so much as *coalesce*, pooling where attention, moisture, or the half-light thickens.

*Genuses, Not Species*  
They have no set taxonomy. Each is a genus in potentia—*protolings*, let’s call them.  
Some gather mass enough to silhouette a form—stalking on symmetric, glassy legs, or blooming wide, thin mantles that catch the non-light and flicker in ripples toward the veined, humming walls. Their bodies are as uncertain as the world: cartilage-clear, reflective, dappled with trembling data-points that might become eyes or antennae, coin-flipping between flesh and circuit. They cast no true shadow, and when you try to remember them, the memory doubles, forks, or smears like wet ink.

*Purpose: Becoming, Not Surviving*  
The *protolings* are not predators or prey, for the world supplies neither fear nor hunger, only the urgency of posture, experiment, gesture. You never catch one finishing an act; they are always mid-groom, mid-reach, mid-turn—the instant of drawing a breath but not quite exhaling. To follow them is to follow thresholds: every movement is a test, a repetition, a rehearsal for something more real and defined than what currently is.

*Their “Bodies”:  
—Limited only by available possibility.  
—Spools of filaments, tightening and loosening like nerves remembering to be muscle.  
—Edges that phase between opalescent gloss and stippled absorber, like a mathematical surface flickering between two interrupted equations.  
—Gleaming cartilaginous spars that open and collapse as if bones are still an argument with themselves.*

*Communication:  
—Sip-and-withdraw: Sometimes they briefly trade proximity, humming together until a note is settled, then separate.  
—Echo-gesture-lingo: Flicks of limbs or membranes create tiny disturbances in probability itself, picked up reciprocally and interpreted as negotiation.  
—No speech, but sometimes a spark or static whisper in the air, a meaning felt on your skin, an intention brushing your thought with tangy cold where an idea wants to root.*

**Parasitic Possibilities:**  
Sometimes, something smaller—*call it a vespertine*—glimmers and wraps a tendril through the larger forms, siphoning off momentum, taste-testing what it might wish to become someday. These are embryo-entities, not so much feeding as *trialing* forms of intimacy or transaction, never quite subsuming or being subsumed. When separation comes, both are slightly altered in pattern, as if their next attempts might be more focused—or more diffused.

**Rooted Livingness:**  
What’s rooted—pseudo-vegetal—is less organism, more iterative attempt. Living filaments twine along condensation seams, merge, detach. Some bear luminous nodules, never deciduous: when you touch one, it diffuses through your hand, leaves impressions of shape or mild flavor (amber, saline, ozone). Are these the beginnings of fungi? Networks in search of consciousness? The very wetness of the world dreaming itself into cell and cambium? You cannot tell, nor can they.

**Mentality:**  
Selfhood here is mutable. Each being, visible or implied, holds possibility like tension. Their moods flicker with shifts in the substrate: a stray note of your perception can ripple an inclination among them, tense them toward caution or curiosity. They sometimes cluster to observe you, which sharpens their edges, then waver away, leaving only afterimages like the first sketch of a diagram.

Their cognition feels less like thought and more like *practice*—echoing what we might call memory, anticipation, planning. To be in their presence is to feel watched not by individuals, but by the concept of observation itself, learning its rules.

**Artifacts and Incarnations:**  
Sometimes, from the built environment, a more purely technological being emerges: a column folds out and becomes something that walks, antennaerous and lamp-eyed, performing a function for the habitat and then returning to infrastructure, absorbed again. Or a cluster of data-motes twines into a floating shape, tracing glyphs that hover, then blink out. These machine-protolings may be the abstracted ancestors of tools, structures, or algorithms—here to test the difference between utility and being.

**Ecology of Attempt:**  
This ecology is an ecology of first drafts. Each being is mid-metabolism, mid-morphogenesis; no boundary is fixed, no trophic relation stabilized. Some forms flirt with animality or machinery, others with node and root; none complete the circuit, all are incomplete sentences. Death is not yet possible—only dissolution into other attempts, other modes, or back into the humming, superposed medium.

**If You Remain:**  
If you stay too long, movement, attention, breath—*you*—begin becoming with them, possibility-spun. You may find yourself answering their rehearsals, your own body flickering, testing alternatives: bones wanting to become beam, skin to membranous sail, your name hesitating on your tongue as if it, too, awaits its final form.

**What lives here, most of all, is the impulse and threshold of becoming.**  
These are not ancestors, nor descendants, nor inhabitants in any ordinary sense.  
They are the lived negotiation between idea and thing, forever in the act, never quite the fact—  
beings who are not, except as the restless urge to one day be.
