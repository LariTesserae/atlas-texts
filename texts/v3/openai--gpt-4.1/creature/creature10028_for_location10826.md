Who lives here?  
In the **Constructed Verdancy**, the distinction between inhabitant and architecture blurs; what is alive, what is machine, and what is place become nearly the same.

### **The Grafted Engineers**

The original creators—if one can speak of “origin” in the Verdancy—are gone, or have been absorbed. Yet, not all intent has vanished. Perhaps there are *descendants*.

They are neither quite plant nor animal, neither machine nor spirit. Call them, provisionally, the **Grafted Engineers**.

They do not walk, but **grow**. Their bodies are knots and lattices of differentiated tissue arising from the walls themselves. A thickened cord of vascular fiber splits, swells, and unfolds: a headless, limbless bulge, ringed with cilia and tendrils. From their centers grows bands of cambium and cortex, layered in patterns reminiscent of memory, coded for function. Their “faces” are clusters of photoreceptors and pressure-sensing pores, constantly extruded and reabsorbed.

They are *awake*, but not conscious in a human fashion. Each node is a partial mind, a fragment of designing intention and recursive awareness. They *sense* through changes in sap flow, electrical pulses, and shifts in light. When action is needed—when structure is failing, or a corridor must constrict, or a new layer must blossom—the Grafted Engineers pulse out chemical signals. Cell-colonies flow, accrete, and reform the biomass according to the Verdancy’s higher order. Their memories are geologic: echoed in the anatomy of every tube and vault, written in gene-edited palimpsests.

Sometimes, the Engineers uncoil from a wall, stretching out into the open with hundreds of root-fingers to interface with industrial machinery: phototactic arrays, seed-driven turbines, or the rare metallic spines that pierce deep into the older, darker substrate. The melding of flesh and tool is seamless and ancient; the distinction never mattered here.

They rarely move deliberately, but the Verdancy moves around them—they are soul and sinew of the place, and everywhere is their reach.

### **Symbiotes & Remnants**

Lesser lives persist: the **Threadworms**, translucent filaments living in the mold-thickets, scavenging surplus sugars and camouflaging their fragile bodies as stray roots. The **Vellum Mites**, uniformative specks that drift on the pulsed ventilation, feeding on cell-sheath dander and resin-fall, their only talent being to make themselves almost undetectable among the textures of living fiber.

Sometimes, stranger beings flicker in the light: **Fugue Shadows**—patches of pigment and cilia, doming and retracting, carrying the last memory-patterns of a vanished animal kind. They are not predators, nor prey, but feedback: sensors and error-correctors embedded in the surfaces, animating briefly to investigate entropy—a shadow escaping among the reeds is really a diagnostic ghost, checking the integrity of the whole.

### **Residual Intelligences**

The ghosts of different design philosophies persist. Deep below, in trunk-halls ringed by spongiform lattice, substrate still responds to logic from an older age—outdated programming expressed in obsessive, looping architectures. These are the **Residuals**: thought-forms, algorithmic minds without personalities, guiding parts of the Verdancy to optimize meaningless functions. They are not alive, but sometimes mistake themselves for so, and spasm a new growth, or remake a valley in the shape of a remembered sensor input.

### **Scattered Intruders**

And, there are rarer still—the **Foreigners**, battered things, remnants of some prior fauna or accident. Once in decades, a thing like a vertebrate emerges: a biometallic carrion-crawler, a stowaway rat, a rogue bot gone to seed. They are ghosts of an older world, clinging to metabolite leaks and fungal effluents, barely tolerated by the system. Some are incorporated, encysted in galls of scar-tissue. Some survive, indefinitely, as legends.

### **The Verdancy Itself**

But the true inhabitant is the **Constructed Verdancy itself**: not a creature, not a society, but a set of *living priorities*, a logic woven through every vein and tissue. It dreams in cycles—growth, repurposement, digestion, reclamation. Its intelligence is expressed as metabolism intensified to the point of will; its memory is made of pattern-recognition distributed through space and time. It does not “see” as a conscious being would, but it *remembers*—recursive, layered, architectural memory that manifests as instinct, as unending growth, as corridors built in the shape of thoughts.

### **In Summary**

Those who “live” here are not really separate from the place. Some have form—the Grafted Engineers, the Threadworms and Mites, the Fugue Shadows—but they are no more than organs, sensors, feedback loops.

The place *is* its inhabitants.  
It is a world that lives as pattern, logic, and growth; it dreams only of itself, and all that persists within its bounds is rewritten and recalled into its endless, ordered verdancy.  
All live to maintain the whole; all are grown, built, repurposed, and digested, again and again, in the name of a living order whose comfort lies in its own, unattainable, self-maintaining logic.
