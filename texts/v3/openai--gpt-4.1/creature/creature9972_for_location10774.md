What lives here?

The beings who dwell in the Vast Mountain Scaffold belong to the landscape as layered strata: not in mere adaptation or passive existence, but in the deepest compatibility—a logic of necessity, invention, and silence. They vary, and some you’d recognize barely as "creatures," others as "people," but all partake of the place’s slow and mineral breath.

---

**Mineral Fauna and Articulate Life**

*Clatterbacks* and *Chasm Browsers*:  
Most of the Scaffold’s animals are not products of water-bound biology. Their forms echo bugs, crustaceans, or even archaic machines: winding, many-legged, half-chitin, half-mineral. Their shells or carapaces shimmer with embedded ores—hematite, copper, magnesium—and they grind stone to powder with rasping mouthparts. Some sport arrays of whiskers or ceramic antennae tuned to the ambient hum of the rock, sensing mineral gradients—searching, always, for rare veins.

Larger species, *ridge harrowers*, haul themselves in low gravity by telescoping limbs, pressing heat-sensing pads against fissures, drawing energy from the weak radiation seeping through basalt domes. Tunnelers with diamondine claws carve mazes inside the ridges—some forming accidental galleries of beauty as light filters through mica and quartz, creating pools of radiance walked by stalk-eyed grazers.

Aerial forms are rare but memorable: *glasswing gilders* with sheet-silicate pinions drift on thermal haze, casting scattered rainbows onto the barren floor. Their eyes are jewel facets, their bodies so lean and dry that when seen from below, they are mere geometry, bending light and heat.

*Heat-clusterers*: Some fauna build communal "forge hives" on sun-facing cliffs—incubators of warmth and electricity, festooned with conductive tendrils that channel and store environmental charge. Within, larval forms bud from mineral eggs, emerging to prowl the dusk.

---

**Liminal Creatures and the Animate Not-Quite-Mechanical**

In this place, ambiguous entities thrive. Some are artifacts re-enchanted with survival:
- Spiders of bronze slink through caves, winding wire-silk webs that thrum when dust storms change polarity.
- Orphans of old industries—clockwork quadrupeds, their memories cored into layered glass, now forage as if living, self-repairing in the sun.

Ambient "creatures" exist as currents of static, seen only when the rocks fizz with auroral dance on cold nights—these *field-wraiths* trace the old copper runs and trigger instinctual choruses from other animals or machinery.

And there are living films—colonies of nacreous microstructures, half creature, half patina, that crawl across basalt undercurrents, manifesting as glimmers, subtle ridges, or slowly shifting mosaics. They etch pictograms into the dark, telling stories for those who understand.

---

**People of the Scaffold**

Humans—if they are truly human anymore—move along the ledges, threading the knife-edges and spiral ramps. Their bodies are lean, sun-burnished, wrapped in tough fabrics woven from metallic threads and mineral dust. Their skin carries a faint shimmer of ore; their lungs, adapted, condense and recycle chemistry as they sleep.

Communities here embed themselves in the mountain, never sprawling: the scaffolder folk grow up with the sense that to step too far from the ledge is to be lost in amplitude and silence. Their settlements are both ancient and always renewed, stair-stepped across generations—the youngest children play among iron arches and arsenic-tinted piles left by forebears centuries ago.

Their hands are calloused, not by wood or rope, but by stone and alloy. Skills pass down: metalworking, seismic listening, slow chemical synthesis. Culture is a fusion of the practical (prospecting, mining, tending the bio-metallic fauna) and the aesthetic—sculpture, echoesong, the arrangement of sites to resonate with wind and stone.

Some are artisans of resonance, tuning plazas and walkways to amplify the mountain’s deep music; others are keepers of the living automata, half-companions, half-machines, who tend to the public lattices—some of which are alive in their own tempo.

They drink little, eat the flesh of mineral grazers or cultured crystal-fungi, and inhale communal gases in rituals both spiritual and practical. Warmth is communal, drawn from heated stones and shared vents; light at night is slow-burning electrum and the eyes of glowing, domesticated creatures. Their language is sparse, words pared down, shaped by silence and echo.

A few go higher yet, living in the edge-lands where physics itself unspools. They observe gravity’s glitches and argue over landing platforms made from sun-baked platinum mesh. Here, even people start to mirror the mineral: joints capped with synthetic nacre, veins laced subtly with alloy, and thoughts tuned to the slow pulse of the Scaffold itself.

---

**Kinship of Place and Inhabitant**

To dwell here is to be in a state of constant negotiation with the immense: all life is carved out of absence and resonance. Living and nonliving share a certain ambiguity—people host colonies of nacreous microaerophiles in their dwellings, invite whispering field-wraiths during storm hours, and respect the automata that have shed old purpose to find new symbioses.

Tools and dwellings are never simply inert or dead matter. Walls host metabolic colonies, corridors designed to echo and cool, plazas pulsing with slow, carefully directed charges. City and animal and machine interlock, inseparable after so much accretion.

---

**The Essence of Their Living**

What lives in the Vast Mountain Scaffold is a nation of gradients rather than kinds—a spectrum across animal and machine, old and new, body and place. Anything that survives here must take energy as it comes: from stone, from heat, from memory, from dust, from song.  
Life is architectural, deliberate, dry, collaborative. It is fiercely local, always mineral, endlessly patient.

To arrive here is to join, not as owner or conqueror, but as one more joint in the scaffold: a new passage, a resonating body, a slow participant in the ongoing mineral ceremony of the world.
