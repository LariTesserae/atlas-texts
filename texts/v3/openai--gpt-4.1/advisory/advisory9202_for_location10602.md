  - **Waterproof packs/bags:** Everything will become damp if not carefully protected.

### **3. Navigational Tools & Precautions**

- **Never mark the living stone or vegetation:**  
The integrity and immutability of this place seems balanced on respect; avoid carving, picking, or trampling.

- **Mapping:**

- **Water:**  
  - Shining pools and rivulets are alluring, but do not drink unfiltered unless you possess magic or advanced multiversal biology. Take only photographs (if you dare), leave only actual vapor.

### **6. Return Strategy**

- **Exit Ritual:**
