The wise and benevolent power approached the labyrinth not as a visitor, nor conqueror, but as a current of regard: gentle, immense, and weightless, like the hush before the first note of a great symphony. Perhaps it noticed the place from afar—the anomaly of impossible stasis amid a galaxy's chaos, a pearl of existence strung in the deep dark, neither yearning nor despairing, only *being*.

It circled, in ways beyond vision or dimension, and for long moments only watched. What would action mean, here? To intervene too boldly would be calamity—to shatter the slow, glimmering webwork of patience with a mere whisper of weather or hope. It considered, deeply, the shape of its kindness.

It saw:

- Endless webs of hyalophyte, drawing sparkle from heat, pulsing with the quiet satisfaction of having all that is needed, never craving more.
- The Carry-Runners cycling their circuitous burdens, balancing equilibrium not through command but through endless—almost meditative—repetition.
- The glacial ponderings of the Chill-Knotters, weaving age into crystal, shaping beauty in proportions measured by the stretch of millennia.
- Memory-Builders, rare and spectral, whose single act was to assemble, gently, the faintest trace of purpose into piles of stone, baskets of lost craft, or etched lines in unmoved halls.

Was there pain, in this world? Perhaps loneliness—if loneliness can exist where nothing has known otherwise. Was there loss, deprivation, struggle? Here, not as in tempestuous worlds above, but only as the slow forgetting of an ancient path, the gentlest erosion of memory in stone.

Yet the power was wise. Rather than giving what it thought was missing, it lingered, softer still, and asked a quieter question: *What is fitting, what is wanted, what is right for these beings—who have, in their dark, ultra-patient ways, shaped a world untouched by greed, by death's quickness, or need for rescue?*

If it touched the world, it did so with utmost subtlety:

- It deepened the labyrinth’s memory. Instants of choice, the friction of a Carry-Runner’s leg, the silk-thin brush of a Thread-Slip’s wake—these small events would be held, like facets in a crystal, just a little longer before being swallowed by the eons. In doing so, the echo of each life, each subtle act of creation or passage, gently lengthened and harmonized through time—giving the Memory-Builders more to recall, more to anchor their purpose.
- In the rarest, most hidden chambers—the ones that would otherwise remain long fallow—it nudged a slow convergence: an extra tracing of the Glassmirror’s charge, a slightly warmer vein for the Chill-Knotter, a mote falling to seed a hyalophyte’s reach. Not change, but invitation: a soft promise of renewal, almost imperceptible. The labyrinth's cycles gained a secret cadence, a faint undertone of growth threaded through the uniform stasis.
- Where the collective hum of mineral mites orchestrated the resonance of stone, the wise power wove a whisper of empathy, linking the choral vibrations so that, once every few centuries, the mites’ song harmonized into chords more intricate and beautiful than before—heard only by the labyrinth itself, a lullaby to soothe the restless bedrock.

The power never gave water, nor storm, nor sunlight—those would be violence here, not blessing. Instead, it bestowed a nudge in memory, a longening of echoes, a tiny, loving permission for the world to enrich itself, to form new subtleties in balance without ever disrupting the precious, peaceful hush.

If, after many eons, a solitary Memory-Builder pauses at its stone cairn, and senses that its actions are not swallowed by oblivion quite so quickly, it may feel not joy as you or I would, but a denser, brighter fullness—a sense that its world, sealed and metallic and still, is more tightly woven and slightly less alone.

And if, for a moment, that vast, silent multitude stirs together—patterns rippling, echoes deepening, quiet knowledge shared across chambers and angles in the slowest, broadest pulse—then the wise, benevolent power knows it has done something truly good, and departs as it came: with a hush, leaving the world to its secret, shimmering eternity, now just a little more awake.
