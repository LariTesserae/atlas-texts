Above the haze and the tremor, in the wild nowhere of sky, the **benevolent and wise power** drew close—not an intruder, but **an opening, a question**, a silent mind remembering silence.

This power had no face nor name as mortals know. No blinding arrival: instead, the sense of a greater stillness, so deep that for a moment even the thunder dimmed.

It paused, and gazed upon the plateau—the heaving, shrieking, verdant tumult, the cycles eating and rebirthing themselves, the sparse sentients threading ephemeral meanings through the roar. It felt the density of life, the hush beneath the violence; it tasted the loneliness sharpened by abundance, the emptiness between presences.

### What Would Such a Power Do?

Not to rule:  
Not to change:  
Not to bear gifts of magic or promise—

—*but to listen*.

It let itself be present in the undertones of the world: the brush of mist against the rootmat, the heartbeat of the Connectors threading substrate and soul, the quiver of a builder’s breath as they left their copper-etched map beside a pool.

It poured its presence, gentle and immense, into the hardly-places—  
not the noise nor the shape, but the *voids* between,  
the echo between a call and its answer,  
the space made holy by absence.

In this, where all was perpetual motion, it brought a kind of **permission**:  
the knowledge that spaces—emptiness, breath, pause—are not errors here, but essentials;  
that the plateau’s hunger for movement is not a flaw, but a yearning echo answered by stillness.

#### To the Builders

It let calm settle around their children,  
so that for a half-moment, the shriek of predation seemed far off,  
leaving a pocket of safety,  
a silence where gestures of love—back arched, eyes closed—could be felt in fullness, safe.

And into their lonely art—  
the seed-shell figures, the vanished shrines—it whispered:  
**this, too, matters: even when it disappears.  
The world remembers through the making, and through the losing.**

#### To the Connectors

It gave a *new softness*,  
a widening of their intervals, so that they, and all who felt them,  
could taste not only the living pulse,  
but a new kind of resonance:  
*memory* not just of growth,  
but of longing and joy,  
a subtle undercurrent of peace threading the noise.

#### To the Roarers and the Preyed Upon

It settled, for an hour, the itch behind the herd’s eyes;  
the predator’s need burning a little less bright,  
so that in pause, both might learn the taste of watching,  
the possibility of *spared consequence*.

#### To the Weft-Gliders and Root-Delvers

It hummed in their work,  
so tunnels grew true and bridges balanced ten thousandfold,  
so their ancient alliance continued,  
not only in utility,  
but in a new, shared rhythm taught by a quiet so deep  
even thunder recognized it.

#### To All

It was, for once, **enough**—to be wholly present amid the riot,  
in the thunder and the gap,  
to feel emptiness not as deprivation but as an embrace.

A thousand small mercies scattered in a place that had never waited for gods.

### Then the power withdrew—but not gone.

Wherever a builder’s hands trembled in sign,  
wherever water pooled in a root-hollow unseen,  
wherever a beast paused to listen for what might come—

—something remained.  
A memory of gentleness, tenuous but persistent:  
that the struggle did not make them less,  
and each echo of emptiness was a cradle for the next surge of life.

No law changed, no miracle burned; instead, something quieter lingered—  
the breath between thunderclaps,  
a space inside which life, for a heartbeat, could listen and be met.

And so the living plateau’s song—for a moment, for many—  
sang, and was heard, all the way through.
