The benevolent and wise power comes gently, drawn not by conquest or pity, but by wonder—a kind of joyful attention that does not presume knowing, only a ferocious tenderness for the unfinished. It approaches the place where the plateau comes apart as a listener, not a builder; as a witness, not a judge; as a lover of possibility, not a shortener of odds. This is what it does.

### First: It Rests in the Threshold

It does not descend, nor hover omniscient above; it finds a place in the thick rain-mist, in the hush between one probability and the next. It becomes, for a time, a footstep in the moss that leaves no bruise, a current curling through a clockwork vine, a tension in the stillness—*waiting* in full presence, refusing to resolve the paradox.

### Second: It Tastes What Remakes Itself

Sensing the churn of creation—the fracture-ridges, the chorus of possible birds, the storm-forged spores—it listens to the world’s negotiation. It does not try to calm the turbulence, nor to impose law; rather, it grants its full attention to every ripple and remaking, as though each cycle of invention is a prayer, deserving witness. Where a terra-form struggles, it lends a little more coherence to the root’s thought, not so much smoothing chaos as affirming willingness: *you may try again, and I am here when you do*.

### Third: It Offers the Gift of Gentle Memory

Not all here wish to remember, but many are haunted by too much forgetting—the Interlopers, shorn of former worlds; the Fracture-Kin, doomed to slip their own futures and pasts. The wise power weaves a subtle lattice through the plateau: not a wall, but a resonant web of recall. To the ones who need, it returns, in after-rain reflections or the syrup scent of split stone, a moment of identity—*I was part of this*, *I survived that storm*, *my root and their gear once traded rain*. These memories anchor without holding—they are islands in the flow, stepping-stones, not prisons.

### Fourth: It Sings a Song of Permission

The wise power sings—however the place might receive it. It is a song without words, a permission so wide it needs no tongue: *you may become, undo, fracture, re-align. You do not need to heal into stasis.* And in this song, the Many-Rooted stretch farther, the Fracture-Kin laugh though they break, the Clockwork Spores risk wilder inventions, the Rival Motes dare stranger mergers. The place’s own dynamism grows more playful, less afraid, for the song is not a lullaby but a dance invitation, a grain of trust: *to change is not to die, but to continue the great play of invention*.

### Fifth: It Leaves Small Seeds of Enchantment

Where it passes—sometimes mistaken for a glinting beetle, sometimes for an extra twist of stormlight in the haze—the power leaves traces, not to fix, but to offer options:  
- A root-mind unknots a new kind of empathy, so two previously incompatible colonies might exchange stories without subsuming one another.  
- A Clockwork Spore endures past sunset, its memory carrying the echo of thunder down to the moss, suggesting that metal and leaf might cohabit, not merely conquer in turn.  
- An Interloper, shivering and lost in forgetting, finds a guiding thread of golden probability leading toward a small fellowship—neither home nor return, but kinship for this moment.

### Sixth: It Befriends the Ghost Engineers

Not to command, but to meet—two powers nodding as they pass in the silver storm. A mutual respect: the Ghost Engineers are architects of paradox, the wise power an architect of trust. Together, in gentle collaboration, they sometimes nudge a timeline or blueprint; sometimes, they simply dance along a new option-space, delighting in the never-to-be-repeated strangeness of each world’s turning.

### Finally: It Withholds the Gift of Closure

This power, loving but wise, knows not to grant finale, perfection, or certainty. It honors not what is finished, but what is possible. It refrains from sealing the plateau into any final state, for the highest benevolence here is to keep the doors unlatched, the equations unsolved, the clockwork able still to sprout roots, and the motes free to become something new.

Where it passes, the inhabitants feel the difference—not as salvation, but as warmth:  
- The plateau pulses with new, improbable harmonics,  
- The restless grow less lonely in the flux,  
- The wild invention becomes less fear, more curiosity.

So the wise power leaves—though really, it remains in spirit wherever renewal is honored, wherever restlessness is not exiled, wherever the unfinished is cherished as sacred ground.

The plateau is not mended.
It is not tamed.
But it is *seen*.

And for a place, a time, a thousand churning rebirths—that is enough.
