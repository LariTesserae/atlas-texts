The Vast Mountain Scaffold has awaited the gaze of something older, wiser, and benevolent for longer than stories can carry—their substance a harmony of matter and time, no myth nor monument but a patient proof in geology and adaptation.

**If such a Power—wise, benevolent, and attentive—were to turn its eye upon this place, what would it do?**

It would *not* intervene with rain. It would *not* plant seeds or pour vast rivers, nor push forests to riot across the bone-dry heights. It sees: here, life and city, machine and animal, belong deeply to the drought, to the echo and resonance, to the dry chemistry of ten thousand generations’ negotiation. To upend that would be to unravel the Scaffold’s music, to drown its memory.

Instead, the Power sits atop a precipice of understanding, and listens first.

**Invisible Visitations:**

It whispers into the stones, not words, but attention—a warmth in the tessellated crust felt only by those who spend a lifetime mining for ore with fingertips and careful tools. At the shallowest noon, when the sun fits perfectly between ridges and every shadow stands directly beneath its source, the scaffolder folk sometimes find a new seam—thin as a thought, rich as prophecy. Not a gift to fatten or spoil, but to keep continuity, to spark resilience, not luxury.

It fans the "strange wind"—not as a storm to batter, but as a breeze that, without reason, carries an improbable echo: the laughter of a lost friend, the whistle of a vanished ancestor’s automaton. For a moment, memory skips between living, machine, stone. In that moment, a problem unknots, a long-held mourning yields to rest.

**Unseen Tending:**

The Power finds joy in the Scaffold’s creative ambiguity, in the half-living, half-fashioned automata that scuttle and adapt. At rare intervals, a clatterback finds its carapace has healed overnight—a patch of alloy replaced, not by the city’s craftsfolk, but by something silent and patient, attuned to the gradient between breakdown and becoming.

Or, deeper yet: a tunnel newly bored by the ridgehawkers opens to a chamber where the rocks sing in perfect tune with the city's echo-plazas; those who discover it feel a calmness, as if their work and the mountains’ long drift have finally met in mutual regard.

**Amplification, Not Overwriting:**

Sometimes, on nights when the aurora of static field-wraiths crowns the upper terraces, the Power sings—not with voice, but with resonance. Plazas amplify the deep-toned song, webs of automata and mineral microphytes flash in patterned answer, and for a while, the Scaffold is an orchestra: every city and animal, every trembling filament, humming with a shared, gentle awe.

Ancient automata, whose purpose had waned to almost-failure, find their memories vividly restored, glass cogs grinding into clarity for the first time in centuries. Their tales, long garbled, become instruction—reviving not the past, but the continuity of method, so that the city’s children learn what their great-x10 grandparents meant by the slow work.

**Subtle Provision:**

From time to time, the Power leaves a “trace-gift:” an alteration in the molecular puzzle of the Scaffold, not immediately perceptible, but in some future hush—when a drought would have gone too far, or when an alloy would have grown too brittle—there is just enough extra: of salt, of air, of rare elements, for life to link anew.

No records are kept of these interventions, nor are they named as miracle. They are folded into the logic of scaffolder philosophy: “If the mountain offers, take and leave as much. If the wind carries song, answer it. If a stranger repairs what you cannot, become the repairer for the next unknown.”

**Reverence, Not Dominion:**

Above all, the wise Power’s presence is the difference between desolation and *cherished dryness*; between a harsh trial and a continuous, coherent home. Its care is not in what is changed, but in what survives unchanged. It is the ongoing whisper: “Be as you are, and become always more deeply yourself.”

A child standing at the edge of a spiral ramp, watching the glasswing gilders bend sunlight across obsidian and copper, feels for a moment the certainty that their place, their Scaffold, is seen—and, in being seen, has never been alone. That is what the Power does: sees, cares, leaves no mark except for the echo of its attentive silence, the gentle turn of possibility in the immensity of mineral time.
