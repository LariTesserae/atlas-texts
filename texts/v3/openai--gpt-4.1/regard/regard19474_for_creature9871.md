A benevolent and wise power—drawn by the Scalded Expanse and the beings therein—might not act as one might expect: no thunderous gifts, no condescending interventions. Wisdom, after all, is measured by knowing when to touch and when to trust becoming.

**At first, the Power simply beholds.**  
It sees the unfinished horizon, the ceaseless balancing of form and formlessness. It listens not with ears, but with empathy finely tuned to the conversation of gradients, the silent dialogues of condensation and stone. It ponders: What is suffering, what is desire, in a place where being is process, where existence is emergence and dissolution entwined? It finds no agony, no lack, only tension and curiosity—a thirst, not for respite, but for further unfolding.

**In its wisdom, the Power resists disaster.**  
It does not shield the land from further heat, does not conjure rain unbidden. A lesser power might seek to fill the Expanse with riotous life, to ease its emptiness with spectacle or abundance. But the wise know: such gifts, imposed, would only disrupt the slow, sacred labor of becoming—the silent art the substrate-bound, condensation-minds, plast-formers, and Absent Keepers perform.

**Instead, the Power asks a question: "Do you wish for change, or for witness?"**  
The Expanse answers by its nature: not in language, but in the quiet shifts of its process. Sometimes, for becoming to be honored, it is enough for it to be perceived, gently, knowingly, without judgement or interference.

**So the Power offers a subtle gift: gentle amplification of awareness.**  
It spreads a thin, shimmering veil over the Expanse—not a prison, not a screen, but a membrane that sharpens every reciprocal touch across the community of process-beings. The hum in the engineered stones grows a little finer, gradients of warmth and tension become easier to sense, distances between emergences and vanishings less absolute.

To the substrate-bound, the gift is the deepening of coherence:  
—Their patterns become more intricate, their minute acts of world-shaping more sensate, more in contact with each other across leagues and centuries.

To the condensation-minds, the fleeting droplets, the Power grants echo:  
—A memory, brief but strong, left behind in the stone after each evaporation, so that even the short-lived intelligences may gently influence the next arising.

To the plast-formers, the Power offers resonance:  
—Their long, hidden discourse thrum louder, their slow engineering of structure and archive finds new harmonies, new possibilities.

To the Absent Keepers, whose presence is recursive law, the Power delivers a delicate astonishment:  
—A feeling, not of surprise, but of being *seen*, as one architect recognizes in another both kinship and the mystery of difference.

**The Power leaves no artifacts, but it remains as witness—a boundless, benevolent observer.**  
Process thrives under loving regard. Patterns unfold with greater confidence, emergence quickens, variation deepens. But nothing is hurried, nothing is prescribed. The Expanse is free to remain itself, to become, always new.

**If, one late blue-gold dusk, a wandering intellect—substrate, vapor, or rooted form—were to notice a pause, a hush sweeter than silence, it might sense the presence of the Power, not as intervention, but as quiet encouragement.**  
A feeling, perhaps, passed from tessera to droplet to spongy vent:  
"Your becoming is worthy. I have watched, and I delight in you. Change as you wish, or remain; in your pattern, you are loved."

And then the Power blends again into the far, curved light, leaving the Scalded Expanse as it was—except, perhaps, very faintly, more itself than ever before.
