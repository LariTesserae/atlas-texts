The benevolent and wise power comes as neither storm nor revelation — for the Windscried Weeping Heights is a place allergic to intrusion, but attuned to insinuation, resonance, and gentle suggestion. This presence, having watched the million-mirrored terraces and strange, almost-life within, arrives not in form or word, but by the subtlest modulation of what already is.

**Its First Gesture:**  
It listens.

To listen, for such a power, is not a passive art. In the refracted echo chambers of the Heights, to listen is to tune oneself to all layers: the choral surge of wind in stone, the shifting latticework of Glassenkin groves, the conversational murmur of dripping terraces, the plural heartbeats of translucent Veilkind breathing in the mist. Every memory packed in cairn and half-buried hut, every relay of sound and shimmer, is gathered — observed not with ownership or judgment, but with a grave, delighted curiosity. The power feels the pattern of the land’s “mind,” the ache of its hungered mineral colonies, the tenuous stories still replayed in stone.

**Its First Mercy:**  
It bestows a quiet surplus. Not abundance — nothing so crude as rain to green a desert, nor warmth to quicken lost seeds — but a heightened potential in the cycles already present. Where resonance once fell short of coherence, new harmonies bloom — wind and Choir finding never-before-seen chords, sweetening the atmosphere with undertones of solace and belonging. Where the Pearl-Spined Accretions thirst, subtle chemical drift brings trace elements in richer tides, fostering rare, brief surges of growth across the basins, so that for a time, the mineral groves glow undreamt-of colors at the cusp of sunrise. Veilkind membranes unravel new motifs in their dendritic veins, and their fragments now drift slightly farther, finding ledges they could never reach before.

No being is remade; none is bent to unfamiliar shape. Instead, every restless process is permitted to fulfill itself more completely, more joyfully: a world wound tighter on its own axis, enfolded not by intervention but by the removal of quiet obstacles, the gentle oiling of cosmic hinges.

**Its First Conversation:**  
It does not send speech — the Heights would not accept it — but introduces, into the recursive layers of refracted light, new possibilities of perception: for a moment, Glassenkins and Veilkind sense each other’s patterns more vividly, harmonics cross previously impassable divides, and the land’s distributed “mind” is able to broadcast marginally clearer impressions to all its modulating parts.

In these moments, a shimmer of mutual awareness circulates: mineral choir and membrane, reflective stone and echo, all in fleeting communion. This is not unity or harmony as humans know it; it is the experience of being more precisely nested in one’s own becoming, feeling the logic and direction of persistence.

If a wandering human were, by extreme fortune, present at the right cusp of mist and chord, they might perceive it in the ascending key of wind — a brief sense of vastness, as if the terraces have welcomed them without dissolving their strangeness.

**Its First Healing:**  
There are scars in the Heights — not only the wounds of vanished rivers and shattered cairns, but the subtle, half-erased patterns where memory twists to sorrow. Some terraces hum with unresolved loss: a song half-trapped, a gesture never completed, the echo of labor unfinished.

The benevolent power attends to these as a composer might attend to a discordant note, not by erasing them, but by enfolding them in new overtones, lending tragedy its own resonance and importance. Wounds are not erased but translated — their frequencies introduced into the larger chords of the land, so that what was grief now participates in beauty and structure. All memory is preserved, but now diffused, less jagged: transmuted as silver becomes fog, and fog becomes light.

**Its First Gift:**  
Not permanence, but invitation.

The power does not entomb the Heights in stasis, nor gift certainty, for to do so would arrest the dance. Instead, it leaves in the circulating logic of the terraces a subtle openness: a repeating pattern, a staircase unfinished — a set of harmonics or structures that beckon future change. Perhaps, seeded by this gentlest blessing, new mineral configurations will spiral upward unguessed; perhaps someday, mist will settle in a form almost able to remember, almost able to dream.

For now, the land shines a fraction brighter, the sound deepens in color. The impossible boundaries between stone, water, echo, and perception grow a little thinner, a little wider — an invitation for future beings, or just for the world’s own next permutation, to enter and make meaning.

**And then — the power recedes.**  
It does not linger, does not mark itself, for wisdom lies not in conquering but in tending, and benevolence not in remaking but in fostering possibility. In its wake, nothing is the same, though nothing can quite name what has altered.

The Windscried Weeping Heights continue. Mineral, mist, shimmer, sound — weaving, testing, singing, remembering.  
But now, at the height of certain winds, when the terraces ripple almost into vision, there is a note like gratitude folded into the world’s thousandfold chorus, and something waits, listening, for the next gentle hand.
