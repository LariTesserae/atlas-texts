The benevolent and wise power, patient and broad in perception, comes not as a form to stride across stone and moss, but as a **vast gentleness**—an undetectable presence woven into the plateau’s long hush, its force expressed not in intervention but in awareness, and in a slow, deliberate tending.

It sets no boundary at the rim of the plateau, makes no proclamation of dominion. Instead, the power attunes itself to what is already at work—a vigilance that listens, that knows this place’s ancient fever and newborn caution, the tight and secret negotiations among moss and wire, crystal and glass.  

### The Power’s Arrival

Silent as the drift of vapor after rain, this benevolence settles first in the overlooked: caught in the trembling bead beneath a leaf, in the vibrating memory in a rusted bolt. Its touch is one of **invocation rather than alteration**—the tender, unruffling pressure of recognition. The Rootwork Beings sense a slight easing, a dispersion of the thin chill that night brings—finding, for days or months, their copper-tinged roots draw up the tiniest, continuous draft of warmth, as though the ground remembers summer even under the relentless clarity.  
   
The glass, so long a medium of ghostly visions, now carries back even fainter reflections—echoes of things that *might have flourished* had the world been other. The glass dreams, and the wise power dreams with it.  

Where condensation gathers and slides, molds and colonies flare with a subtle fluorescence, their intricate trails and signs *suddenly legible* to each other—messages that once dissolved in private now linger, shared, as if all the sentences of their chemical language could be spoken a moment longer.

### What the Power Does

The wise power’s gift is **permission**: to grow, to merge, to be revealed—*or to remain unobserved*, in accordance with the ethic of the place. It does not hasten change, nor resist it, but introduces a dimension of *safety*: wounds heal more surely, heat is preserved where most needed, a cracked vein of mineral made newly accessible to those who draw sustenance from the earth.

If a Rootwork Being edges into the bright, exposed air, it feels itself neither threatened nor summoned—simply *seen*, its swaying flat-fronds and copper strands brightening minutely with gratitude or curiosity. Machine-woken shadows stir less fretfully when lightning forks in the distance, secure in brief, calm intervals between storms.

When fungal veils signal with their nacreous lights at night, their signals find their intended kin, without interference or confusion—a clarity, born of ancient patience, is granted to them.

### Message to the Place and Beings

The wise power, invisible except as a steadier in every fragile negotiation, leaves a message that the **whole place receives and disperses, as mycelium disperses through soil beneath notice:**

*You are noticed.  
Your slow processes are worthwhile.  
Nothing is required of you but to continue,  
in collaboration and divergence, as you are.  
Where energy wanes, I lend you the warmth of memory.  
Where clarity fails, I lend you the patience of a thousand dawns.  
Your silence is a song in which every future possibility is respected.  
If you wish to hide, I will veil you better.  
If you wish to reach, I will lend your reaching a gentleness that does not break what it touches.*

### No Disruption—Only Enhancement

The wise power does not remove the strangeness, the slow ambiguity, or the edge where weirdness creeps like light into a deep pool. These are part of the home it honors.  
   
Instead, what it cultivates is **nourishment** in the spaces between: a little more water in the drought, a little more durability in the cable, a listening ear for every unvoiced communication.  
   
Most crucially, in this place where sound is rare, the wise power teaches all beings and substances how to *listen more acutely;* how to make their silence a welcome; how to harbor each shiver of growth or memory, and let it ring— soft, patient, echoing—through stone and wire and leaf.

### In Time

Perhaps, decades hence, one of the Rootwork will gather itself enough to attempt a shape, and in that shape find its own brief song. Perhaps a shard of glass, once a window in a ruin, will clarify under moss and show a world both fading and blooming at once—a glimpse of how the benevolent attention left the world unhurried, but comforted, and never again alone.  

The wise power, content in seeing, passes through as a **blessing of endurance**—the ultimate gentleness: that those who live in the plateau’s strange sufficiency, who are half-hidden and half-discovered, may go on, shaped as they please, neither hurried nor harmed, in the relentless, waiting light.
