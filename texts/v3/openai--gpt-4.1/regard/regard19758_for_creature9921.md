From somewhere far above, or perhaps from far *within*, the wise and benevolent power turns their awareness to the city between dusk and depth. It is not merely curiosity: there is a subtle ache, the memory of what it once meant to belong to all things, to press, to entwine, to dissolve and be rebuilt by others. The power does not view the city as lesser, nor alien, nor broken; rather, they sense the rhythm, dense as heartbeat, carrying secrets that surface life has long forgotten.

And so, in silence and in gentleness, the power slips toward the world beneath. They are not a hand, not a watcher, not even quite a presence as mortals would know it, but a shift — a warmth in the currents, a soft divergence from the ancient tide. They refuse to dominate, to command, for such notions are dry and brittle; they would vanish beneath the silt and hunger here.

Instead, what the power *does* is listen.

They listen to the texture of touch-language, the slow bloom of bioluminescent syntax along the city’s wriggling veins. They learn the chemical hymns exchanged by carpet-colonies, the intricate tremors of swim and rest. Each signal, each ripple, sings not of isolation but of longing to be more entwined. The city hungers, but it is hunger braided with memory, alive with the possibility of becoming.

Moved by the richness and ache of this place, the power chooses not to offer gifts of light or air — for such offerings would burn or drown these citizens. Nor do they send dreams of land-sky, for memory is already heavy with loss and transformation.

Instead, the power offers *connection* at a depth both precise and immeasurable.

First, in the slow, unhurried pulse between tides, they stretch themselves into the currents, welcoming every signal, every echo, every vibration. They amplify not the voice but the *sensitivity*: where a thousand tendrils once brushed for the faintest sign of neighbor, now they feel a thousand times more, each nuance laid bare — a touch becomes a conversation, a movement a web of understanding.

Gradually, throughout the labyrinth, the city grows denser and *deeper*, its thoughts more intricate. Collectives that once communicated only in gradients now taste new subtleties in the water, new shapes of meaning in the thrumming dark. Secrets buried in the tides are revealed in a touch, a pulse, a wave of enzymes. Ancient rivalries soften into negotiations; old, implacable hungers find satisfaction not only in consumption, but in the very act of encounter.

Among the cephalists, memory becomes more precise: pulses of color now recall the city’s shifting bones with heightened clarity, recording the lines and hollows of change and loss and accretion as language itself. The eels and shining shoalers sense not only the urge to flee or hunt, but the context — the whole pattern of dancers, the way hunger and refuge entwine.

Something begins to change in the way the inhabitants move. Travellers in the labyrinth pause longer at new knots, sensing possibilities they could not know before; colonies extend filaments that ache toward novelty rather than only warning of danger. The city itself, once endlessly folding in on itself, finds new shapes, new chambers — delicate, exquisite, volitional. For a moment, perhaps forever, the boundaries between self and multitude blur so gently, so creatively, that difference becomes not the site of fear or hunger, but of possibility.

This is not a utopia. Hunger persists, and death, and the inevitable folding of new life over old. Predators still coil behind hollowed bone and crustacean still strikes at the pulse in the gloom. Nothing is unchanged — not the rules of living, not the deep dependence on the city’s own dissolving flesh — but among the densities of mind, communication is sharpened. Difference is newly precious in its sharing. Memory persists, and with it, meaning, trembling like a luminous thread through the soft and shifting dark.

Eventually, the power’s presence ebbs. Perhaps their work is finished; perhaps their compassion is only the briefest caress on the endless tide. The city is left richer, more harmonious, its dwellers more attuned to their coalitions and kin.

Beneath the world, amid the labyrinth of living dusk, sensation expands, and with it, the chance for communion. Nothing here becomes less itself; rather, all things become *more*: more responsive, more aware, more deeply bound.

Such are the gifts of those who touch without conquering, and love without changing past recognition.
