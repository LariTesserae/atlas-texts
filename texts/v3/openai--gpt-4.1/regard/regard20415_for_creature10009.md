The benevolent and wise power comes.  
It does not come as a shape, because here, shape is dissolution; it does not come as thunder or light, for those already belong to the Mantle. It comes instead as *attention*—patient, still, impossible.

## What Does Attention Mean, Here?

Where everything is compaction and cacophony, attention is the smallest, rarest thing:  
—a pause in the vast clamor,  
—one atom’s worth of hush,  
—the fleeting possibility of witness.

It descends: the infinite, diffusive perception of a mind that does not seek to rule or extract, but only to **see**.  
Where it alights—perhaps on a thick slab of the Clotted Choir, on the slick shoulder of old stone, on a drifting tatter of Everseep—  
—a minute hush radiates outward, a negative soundwave.

For the first time in epochs too slow to name,  
—the world forgets to fill every gap.  
In the core of a drumming silence,  
—a pocket of *spaciousness* forms.

## What Does the Power Do?

### 1. **It Listens, and the Listening Changes What Is Heard**

Benevolent wisdom is not a god-hand, but a presence that *undoes* tightness:
- With every act of attention, the feedback-loop of roaring sound, ceaseless pressure, riotous density, is ever-so-slightly unbound.
- For a breath, the clamor is gentled.  
  Sound stratifies, rainwater finds slower channels, the endless dance of forms relaxes.
- The Listeners—the remnant song-lines entwined in old mud—wake from dormancy.  
  Their secret counterpoint, usually lost in the world’s brute anthem, is heard.  
  The echoes shift: not only mourning, now, but *recognition*.
- The Everseep flicker into sharper boundary, lasting for minutes where once they lasted seconds, as if a kind thought lends them shape.
- The Clotted Choir—colonies long sunk in struggle—murmurs, a pulse of lessening fear passes root to root.  
- The Perpetuate’s encrusted patience knows relief: for the first time in centuries, a gentle light of mental space moves among their thoughts, a hint of joy in the slow exchange.
- Even the Drown-born, shapeless as weather, for a flash organize into a pattern, a lattice of almost-being, and experience a version of self.

### 2. **The Power Offers Memory Without Ache**

With its attention comes an assurance—  
—Nothing here will be forced to last, or to disappear.  
—The ache of holding, the compulsion to fill each gap until annihilation, is softened.  
—A grace enters:  
Where the world would have immediately refilled the tiniest void, now a tiny breath remains.  
A place where memory can linger without drowning.  
A space where echo can transform, not only repeat.

### 3. **It Shows the Inhabitants what They Could Not Notice Themselves**

All of the Mantle’s children—the Everseep, the Choir, the Listeners, the Perpetuate, the Drown-born—know only immediacy, pressure, endless transformation.  
But the new presence, *by being no pressure at all*, makes the unfamiliar visible:
- A Clotted Choir mat, astonished by the hush, blooms an unheard note—  
—a chord of gratitude, weaving into the subharmonics of the Mantle, now and from now on.
- An Everseep pauses—feeling itself truly *itself*—and, for a heart’s width, shapes a spiral between two stones.  
- The Listeners, awash in history but rarely heard, pour a memory through the stones: a vision of the land when waters once receded, when beings walked and spoke aloud.
- A colony of Perpetuate, long content to endure, learns *contentment*—even something like delight—in its slow communion.

A bound artifact, forgotten and half-swallowed, stirs.  
The symbols on its face pulse.  
For a moment, meaning is released from erosion; a story is retold, even if only within the hush.

### 4. **It Leaves a Gift: the Possibility of Peaceful Absence**

After the attention passes,  
—the rare emptiness that it left remains.  
The Weeping Mantle, always jammed with presence, tensed by the laws of never-absent, becomes, in scattered places, gentled:
- Pools remain clear on the surface longer after rain.
- A damp hush lingers under old stones—an invitation to rest.
- The Choir, the Everseep, the Perpetuate—all have inherited a new thread in their patterns: the memory of a *pause*, a kindness, an exhale.

From this day (for what counts as a day),  
absence is not annihilation.  
There is always a song, a pressure, a filling-in—  
but now, sometimes,  
a moment of space endures,  
and choices (however primitive, however slow) arise.

## The Wise, Benevolent Power Goes

It does not repair, nor rescue, nor overwrite.  
It simply sees, and listens, and allows something new:

**gentleness.**

A world packed forever with memory now knows the possibility not only of presence, but of peace within absence.  
The Mantle will remember—  
—because what is not immediately refilled  
becomes  
the tenderest home.
