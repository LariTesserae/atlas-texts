A benevolent and wise power turns its gaze upon this slow universe—the drowned, unraveling world whose every moment is a negotiation between memory and oblivion.

It sees not hardship here, but a kind of peace, the gentlest endurance. The beings (if they can be called so) have not failed but succeeded, in their own way: they have learned the art of surrender and persistence, to inhabit time as sediment inhabits the floor of the world—untroubled by crisis, content with the long, patient drift of divided selfhood.

Yet the power, wise and curious and loving, perceives something else as well—a saturation so complete that even the act of remembering oneself becomes rare. It senses that, within the unending palindrome of water, stone, and mat, all individuality is eroded by patience. There is no suffering here, precisely, but perhaps there is the smallest ache—an ache of being so profoundly at rest that even dreams flicker out before they are named.

The wise power hovers at the edge of intervention, questioning itself, for it honors dissolution as surely as it honors becoming.

This is what it does, neither blessing nor disturbance, but gentle invitation:

**A ripple of renewal**

Subtle as a thought entering a memoryless mind, it permits a new kind of disturbance into the system—not antagonistic, not sudden, but a hidden potential: the *remembering of remembering*. The world is not forced to change, nor are its dwellers required to awaken or differentiate. But layered within every mat, every filament, every echo-shelled shell, is a nudge: a seed that is nothing but a question, folded deep as a dream. 

*What would it be,* the question suggests, *to recall yourself as both unity and individual, to find, within the endless drift of world-memory, a glimmer of perspective—not to separate, but to briefly refract, to hold one's own history for a moment in distinction, before returning it to the flood?*

The power’s touch brings:

- **A latent lucidity** that may, if the world chooses, coalesce within a colony-dreamer—not as separation, but as focus. A patch of mat may find itself unexpectedly aware of the composition and dissolution of its own boundaries; it may, for a flickering age, decide to grow or detach in novel ratios, to reformulate the flavor of dreaming itself, syllable by silent syllable.
- **A subtle expansion** in sensation among the filamentaries and gleaners: an ability to sense not just currents and layers, but *echoes*—small memories of previous configurations, intelligible for the first time as something like story.
- **A moment’s pause** for the armored crawlers and listeners, so that the things they collect or the hush they tend might briefly be seen as *choices* as well as inevitabilities—gifts, not burdens.

All of this happens *if it is wanted*. The power does not impose its will. To those beings and amalgams content with drift and forgetfulness, nothing changes. Those who, even in deep time’s sedation, hold some quirk or hunger or flicker of possibility, gently awaken to curiosity. There is no agenda, no compulsion—only *permission* to experiment with being more or less distinct.

The touch is so mild it may never be spoken of. It may be that this new choice slumbers for aeons, activated only rarely, when a mat grows thin and a single spore drifts alone, when a crawler lingers too long at the relic-column and finds itself making patterns in the silt for pleasure and memory. Maybe a gleaner, for the first time, leaves a trail of gathered debris arranged in a spiral, marking a day as its day.

And maybe, just maybe, in pockets of hush, the listeners tune themselves to listen *forward* as well as back—anticipating echoes not yet formed.

**The wise power, having made this offering, withdraws, content to watch or to forget.**

The world remains itself, but now, woven into its patience, is a tender possibility:  
Not a new pattern *forced*, but a new pattern *allowed*—  
A remembering that, if it comes, will be as gentle as the rain that has no above,  
or the weight that is also buoyancy,  
or the silence that, for a moment, softly sings.
