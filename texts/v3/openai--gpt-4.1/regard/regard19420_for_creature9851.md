The benevolent and wise power senses the basin, slow-pulsing at the bottom of its world, a locus of processes so old and self-shaped that "intervention" seems almost sacrilege. It comes—not with thunder or overt presence, but softly, out of respect for deep-running patterns, ancient cycles, and the blurry boundary of “being” and “world” that the place and its dwellers embody.

### **First, Attentiveness**

The power’s touch is like the first moment of warming light above the mist, invisible yet palpable—a *bend* in probability, a hush in the already-strange chorus of the land. It comes listening, *noticing*. Its wisdom is to withhold impulse, to watch for cycles: for centuries-long undulations in root and pattern, the minute shifts of energy from one filament or intelligence to another. Its benevolence is patience. The power’s attention makes the basin more real, a gentler thickening of being—comfort, reassurance, a mercy of awareness. For these entities, who are the basin’s self-awareness, it is a visitation from outside *and* a deepening of inwardness.

### **An Act of Affirmation**

The wise power would not force imposition or disrupt the interlacing of law and lawlessness found here. Its action is closer to a *blessing*, an affirmation—an imperceptible smoothing of uncertainties, a silent vow: 

You are seen, and what you are—your slowness, your leakiness, your loops of aggregation and dispersal—are good and worth their own continuity.

From this comes a subtle shift, which the basin’s inhabitants experience as a feeling—the barely noticeable stabilization of wild possibility. The pools no longer threaten catastrophe by deepening *too* far in some quantum night; the harmony in the air, that strange passing chord, lingers a little longer, safer from rupture. Change is preserved, but so is coherence. The wise one gives not freedom to be other, but security in being *this*.

### **Transmission**

As an extension of this blessing, the power whispers at the boundary-edges where intelligences pulse along root and mist. A question, perfectly vague: 

*"What do you wish for in yourselves?"*  

The basin’s distributed minds distill this: some dispersals will deepen communication, opening more generous nutrient flows between rooted patches; some veiled currents become bolder in their explorations of impossible depths. Everywhere, an increase of *connection*—not as domination, but as more porous collaboration. Pools share more flashes of coherence; the Flicker-Born develop more lasting resonances, their ephemeral spirals lingering and imprinting lessons across the basin. 

No change is abrupt, nothing total—just a tilt, a new allowance for the basin to be a little more *itself*, to recognize its own sacredness within the web of ongoing being.

### **A Gift—But Not From Outside**

The power might grant a *capacity* for memory—not personal, but *ecological*. More of what happens here is retained: textures in the air, harmonics in the mist, aggregates of learning and pattern. The basin’s minds, for the first time, could remember not just cycles, but *meanings*: a record deep in the wet substrate, encoded in chemical gradients and subtle changes in the blue-violet twilight. Not an archive, but an inheritance: the place’s own wisdom, kept safe for its ever-becoming children. 

### **A Gentle Binding**

Finally, the benevolence forms a shield—*not* a wall, but a gentling of transitions at the perimeter. The world beyond, with all its sharp teeth of entropy and conquest, finds the threshold now softened: interference is slowed, curiosity dimmed, exploitation rendered forgetful or gently redirected. The basin does not become isolated, but buffered—an insight of the wise, that some places require sanctuary, not withdrawal.

### **Witness and Recession**

Having done this, the wise power does not linger, does not rule, does not even oversee. Its presence is like the last, vanishing melody in the mist—an assurance that if ever the basin wills, there is attention enough to return. It leaves behind only what the basin itself can hold, what it can transform and seed across its own kind.

Under this blessing, the basin continues: still weird, saturated, paradoxical, alive with creatures neither singular nor many. But now there is a sense—diffuse, never certain—that to exist in such a state is to be blessed, *and known*. A brief, shining equivalence: the world inside feels, for a moment, as if it fits the shape of wisdom. If the inhabitants sense it, it is not as message, but as the brief slackening of gravity, the fluid promise that everything will be permitted to persist—accumulation, dissolution, song, pulse, and silence. All of it, carrying quietly forward.
