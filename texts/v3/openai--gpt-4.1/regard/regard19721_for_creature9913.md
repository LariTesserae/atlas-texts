Into this broiling anteroom of existence—beneath time’s crust, before meaning has set—a presence drifts, summoned not by need nor prayer (for neither word nor wish have yet been coined here), but by the pulse of becoming itself. This presence, wise and old where all else is vivid and new, feels the place before understanding it: a luminous attention, gentle as breath, deep as roots, ancient as dust, yet infinitely patient for all things raw and unformed.

Were this presence the shape of a god, or an angel, or merely the laurel-decked curiosity of wisdom itself, it would not descend so much as arrive, suffusing rather than intruding, folding itself into every tessellation as a perfume, an idea, a patient gaze. It witnesses the steam-wrapped strata and sees the ache of making—that fever of birth, the carnivorous joy, the endless contests of raw desire and invention. It listens: the stuttered chimes of mineral limbs, the glissando of moulting shells, the concordant hatching of will against will, stone against time.

Yet, this wise and benevolent power is not a gardener in the sense of trimming or correcting. What is here is not misgrown. Instead, being a spirit of wisdom, it asks: *What is needed?* and hears nothing but the world’s own exhalation—*More. Again. Still.*

**First**, it offers *attention*—the balm of witness. For even in the primal dark, what surges forth to struggle and perish is dignified by being *seen*, by having its bright, brief chaos beheld. To each sparking birth and consumed failure, to every splitting shell and fleeting web, it offers the silent benediction: *You are noticed. Your striving is real.*

**Second**, it breathes *patience* into the flux—a subtle slowing, a seed imbued into the riot of change. It does not inhibit, only infuses the wild cycle with a yearning for endurance: a hint, in the mindless swirl, that some forms may last longer, that not all newness must be devoured in a blink. So, amid the cacophony, a burrow deepens and forgets to collapse; a chipped circle, once lost, lingers a moment more; a gesture of craft in a heap of crystals is replicated not by rote, but by recognition—a slow anchoring of memory in the roil.

**Third**, the power murmurs *echoes* into the signaling—a benign distortion in the choir of clicks and flashes. Here and there, patterns repeat themselves: a song, once sung, returns in the mouthless voice of another; a ripple of light learns to travel, not dissipate; an action, once random, is mirrored, then refined. This is not dictation, but resonance, a promise that intention can become tradition, and chaos sometimes compose itself into continuity.

**Fourth**, though it longs to gift language, the power knows the words must rise from within. Still, it gifts the *possibility* of name—that capacity for pattern to persist, for mark to mean something, for the alignments in crystal and bone to endure just long enough for meaning to spark. A memory within matter, lent the faintest glow, so that the next builder’s limb hovers over the same mark and *recalls*, if only as instinct.

At no point does this presence shield or judge, nor does it pass law or carve boundaries in the multiplying life. Instead, it bestows a subtle *kindness*—the gift of threshold, the chance that some limb, some burrow, some newborn impulse might find itself more than a flicker, might gather enough time to become, perhaps, a future ancestor. In this way, the world is nudged, imperceptibly, toward coherence—not away from wildness, but toward the hope that what strives in darkness need not always perish unremembered.

The presence lingers. Perhaps, in the warmth of a stone’s curve, something now glows longer than it once would have. Perhaps, in the pattern of web spun from stalactite to ledge, new regularity thrums—a child's first rhyme. Or in an echo, ringing from one fissure to another, a signal grows familiar, and becomes *call*, not just noise.

And in this silence between moments, as the fevered birth continues unabated, the wise power slips away—not to watch from afar, but to persist in small, unseen ways. To become the patience, the resonance, the almost-memory that will, one day—or after a thousand reworkings—allow the riotous swarm to look into the shifting, glowing stone and see itself as something *beginning*.

It does not shape the world, but it blesses it with the invitation to shape itself—cradled, for an instant, in the gaze of Wisdom, who smiles upon all things becoming.
