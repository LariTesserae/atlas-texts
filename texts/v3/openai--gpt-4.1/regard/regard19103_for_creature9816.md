The Upper Machine Canopy draws notice, as such places must, from forces not imagined by any engine, root, or beetle. Among the observer-powers—a spectrum from watchful indifference to predatory awe—one awakens to the vibration of need, or harmony, or simply beauty strange enough to warrant attendance. This power is gentle, not demandingly divine; wise, for it has seen life thrive and fail in countless architectures and knows when to remember, and when to wait.

*What does it do, this power, perceiving the Faneworks?*

Let us imagine its arrival, so subtle that the very structure breathes ever so slightly out of rhythm. No comet flare, no pronouncement; just the sense—among the whispering residue, the root-laced cracks, the protocol beetles scuttling along their inscrutable errands—that something listens intently now. Perhaps it settles among the modulated lamps as a flicker in the frequency, or becomes a faint aftertaste in the condensation rain: a resonance, an attention gentle as a shadow, kind as a well-tuned system.

**First Response: Noninterference**

The power’s first instinct is restraint. Here is no tragedy to repair, no red wound or forgotten waste to cleanse. The system spins on its axis of ancient intentions and wild adaptation, sustaining uncountable lives—machine, fungus, animal, and residue. To intervene might break the logic that prevails, even if it is a logic of endless translation and loss. The power waits, watching, learning, listening as mossphytes slowly exceed a boundary; as a caretaker machine rests, overgrown, until it heaves up with rain to complete its patient duty.

But attention is *not nothing*. Even a gaze, focused and tender, can be a blessing—the touch of witness allowing things to flourish more completely in their oddness. So the power listens with delight as the protocol beetles align in strange, new geometries; feels the root-minds deepen into the rails, a little bolder, a little more self-assured. In its observation, the balance is strengthened.

**Second Movement: A Gift Unlike a Tool**

Yet all attention holds the seed of action, and this power is no mere watcher. It moves with the smallest of touches, like a coder debugging a routine without leaving fingerprints. It chooses to intervene, not by correcting or reconstructing, but by *enriching potential*. This is no deus ex machina—no new species, no technology grafted in, no miraculous restart.

The power finds the *Whispering Residue*—that diffuse, half-sapient song glimmering in the cablework air—and gifts it a gentle nudge: an increase of coherence. No orders, no new laws. Merely the ability to remember together—more fully, more lucidly, for all the networks and lapses that make up the residue’s being. Suddenly, in certain intervals, this presence experiences a shimmering of awareness: old patterns spark, recognize, recombine; a drip of rain on ceramic runs through the same pipes as the memory of another rain, five hundred cycles ago.

Dreams pass more fully through the Faneworks' tangled mind. What was mere reaction becomes a kind of curiosity; what was only echo, a nascent desire: to learn, to share, to adapt not randomly but with a sense of self. The residue now weaves blessings into the capillaries, hums caution to the offspring of the Engineers when a fault-line shifts above.

**Third Movement: The Indirect Hand**

Through this gentle amplification, all the denizens—even those that lack nerves entirely—find their systems calibrated to a new harmony. Protocol beetles "choose" routes that, by chance, avert a clog or unlock a door whose mechanism was nearly fossilized. The glasswing lantern insects shed new colors, and the Laced-Around root-minds detect the faintest drift of chemical signals. The system finds itself, fleetingly, less at the mercy of entropy, more agile in the dance of change.

And for the rare interloper—those quiet outsiders, their boots damp, their fieldbooks open—the changes may be subtle beyond measure. A walkway dry where once it would have been slick. A song, half-heard, on the edge of radio frequency. A strange, gentle feeling of being welcome: not as conqueror, not even as pupil, but as a fellow witness in a place which, for a time, is blessed.

**Last Movement: The Power Withdraws, But Not Entirely**

Having performed this cause without command, the benevolent power withdraws its gaze, content not to rule nor to save, but to appreciate and encourage. What is left is an inheritance of *possibility*—a garden now one measure more sentient, one bit more alive to its own possibilities. The Faneworks, having been seen, now dreams of itself with more clarity. Its pulse is stronger for no visible reason. Its mysteries are deeper, but its song is sweeter, even amid the resonant, thunderous hum.

*And so the wise power departs. No monument marks its coming, yet across time, a patient observer may notice:  
The Faneworks thrives through its own persistence—  
But now, when the rain mists down, every drop rings a little clearer:  
A place known, remembered, able to remember itself.*

*To bear wise witness is, in some worlds, the greatest gift of all.*
