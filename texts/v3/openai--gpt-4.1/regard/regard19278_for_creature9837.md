If a benevolent and wise power—a guardian spirit, a godling of cycles, or even a traveler from realms of insight—were to turn its attention to the Plateau of Roots, what would such a being do?

It would begin with *observation*, not intervention. For wisdom heeds the pace of the world it regards, sensing its rhythm, attuning perception not to moments but to movements measured in ages. The power would listen—not with ears, but with presence, attentive through every pore to the quiet colloquy of root and rain, the pulse of hidden life, the susurrus of patient, slow exchange.

Perhaps, in some hidden crux of self, the wise power would marvel: for here is a place that needs so little mercy, so little mending—no suffering that is not simply the rich process of return, no cruelty but the gentle indifference of time. The authority of death does not reign here; rather, all things are folded back, softened, given a place within the whole. Contradiction and invasion dissolve; what arrives is digested, what lingers adapts or is erased.

Would the benevolent one intercede? *Not with fire, not with speech.* It would not lay gifts of knowledge, nor bring tools to highlight individuality against collective green. It would not carve a temple, nor teach fire’s secret, nor even reveal its shape against the emerald haze. Instead, following an even deeper mercy, it would choose to *bless through alignment*, to act through subtleties felt only, never seen.

The power would sink its vast perception down, inward, finding the Parliament of Roots in their slow, mutual argument. Perhaps it would whisper—not words, but a kind of *encouragement*: a gentle amplification of resilience, a bias toward balance, a guiding shimmer nudging disturbances back into healing arcs. It would pour wisdom in the form of equilibrium, fostering not stasis but the Plateau’s ceaseless adaptive calm.

It might *inspire* within certain Heartnodes a dreaming—an image of future wetness when drought threatens, or a memory of old growth patterns when blight gnaws too deeply, so that the substrate mind shifts in time, anticipating, thriving. The benevolence would work as the dew works: omnipresent, gentle, unobtrusive, never breaking the spell of self-unfolding integration.

Among the Interlocutors—the strange birds, the furred weevils, the musical marsupials—the power would bestow small, fortuitous mutations: a greater tolerance for novelty, subtle improvements for symbiosis, sharper attunement to the scent-signatures that bind them to the Plateau’s will. When a wanderer, foreign or ancient, stumbles in confusion, the power would draw a luminous patch of fungus, beckoning toward safety, or nudge a root to form a bridge over a threatening mire.

And among the ruins—fallen pillars choked with font-moss, memory-stones clad in green—the benevolent one would linger with gentle regard. Not to restore them, for this is not a place of restoration, but to *honor* their silent absorption: to let the mosses remember, in their quiet blooming, all that has come before. Perhaps for a moment, at dusk or in the low gold mist, the memory of what was lost would be felt in the pattern of lichen or the echo of a spore-fruit’s detonation—fleeting hints of past purpose made beauty, not regret.

And if, by some stray fortune, a soul shaped to human measure remained upon or within the Plateau, the power would not make them other than they already are; instead, it would further entwine them with their world. Their sleep would deepen, their senses mingle with the pulse of root and rain, and their heart would beat in time with the slow, magnificent patience of the green. They would find themselves at home, at peace, immune to the siren call of separation.

No new law would be written. No message left but the subtle reinforcement of what always was: that slowness is not neglect, and waiting is not weakness; that awareness can be diffused and still intelligent, and that benevolence is sometimes expressed as trust—the faith to let a living world continue its endless song, unhurried, untroubled, whole.

**And so the wise, kind power would remain—not seen, but present: a guardian not above, but *within*. Its gift would be to let the Plateau of Roots remain itself, ever. To ensure that it might last, quietly inviolable, for another million years—outlasting all tempests of time, until even wisdom itself is folded down, root to root, into peace.**
