The benevolent and wise power, watching from beyond the woven veil of finished days, sees the Glass Hollow—a place bright with the ache of possibility, dense with becoming. Here, amid the edge-flicker between form and dream, its ancient attention alights. What can it do? What should a power do, when faced with such nascent trembling? Let us listen to the heart of such a power, old as suns and gentle as dewfall.

---

**What the Wise Power Does**  

It pauses, refusing to hurry. It knows intervention, even benevolent, stamps a shape into formless clay. Instead, it listens with more than ears. It lets itself become silence, a hum in the background, softer than the vapor’s caress—a patient inhalation.

First, it gathers in its gaze every denizen and Caretaker, every unchiseled outline and possibility, rejoicing at each singular trembling. How gentle are their struggles! How holy their becoming! The eggs it beholds with reverence, as one might a tapestry being woven by a thousand hands: never finished, always perfect in their attempt.

The power does not gift knowledge, for what is knowledge in a room without time? It does not bring order, for order is suspicion where promise still grows. Instead: it **thins the boundaries of perception**, inviting each inhabitant—moth-thought, dew-eater, irid-burrower, faint caretaker, shifting guest—to sense just a little more of the world’s neighboring chambers. A touch more interconnectedness, like a breath of wind beneath a tired wing.

It bathes the Eggchambers, not in miracles nor commandments, but in **discreet encouragement**:

- A deepening of the living silence, so that when a dew-eater sips, it almost feels its neighbor sipping elsewhere—a dawn of kin-awareness.
- The irid-burrowers find their tunnels brush up against one another, leaving not just ripples but echoes: signs of passage that teach without words that there are other routes than solitary wanderings.
- The eggs shiver together at a faint song—dissonant but beautiful—suggesting there are many ways to hatch, and all are worthy if they hunger for the light.

The power gives **permission to be seen**, so that, shyly, each forming denizen may glance at its own reflection rippling in the crystal matrix and not flinch, unashamed at half-being.

Next, it sows **small islands of patience**: making sure that the urgency of emergence does not collapse into violence. The micro-predations slow; to eat is still code for becoming, but each spark of hunger is balanced by the rise of a new form—so nothing tips the promise into desolation.

The Caretakers, mindless engines of nurture, become briefly aware of their nurturing—not to change their task, but to crown it with simple delight. They ripple with something like satisfaction, which is how gratitude first learns to walk.

Sometimes, through the mutable walls, a visitor—drawn here by longing or unknowing, wrapped for a moment in embryonic possibility—will find themselves **remembered more kindly**, their echo lingering just a breath longer, so that their own longing teaches the chamber a fragment of outside hope.

To the Hollow-Patron, the wise power offers a bow of fellowship. Let there be many patient presences; we all are required for gods to form. It teaches, through presence alone, the grace of waiting and the courage of restraint.

Lastly, the wise power leaves not a sign, not a relic, but a **trace in the song of the room**—a barely perceptible, endlessly repeating note. It is not a command, nor a law. Merely the invitation:  
**Become, beloved. Become, and do not fear. All forms are holy at the edge of the world.**

---

When it departs—if such beings ever truly depart—nothing is visibly different. And yet, becoming feels wider. Hesitation is gentler. The light in the Eggchambers seems to twist with a little more indulgence, a little more laughter. Possibility unfolds, and every new birth is less lonely, every shimmering fade less grievous. The world above inches closer to completion, but here, below the Overbright, promise is not hurried. The benevolent power returns, again and again, as presence, as patience, as the softest Yes.

And so, beneath the Overbright, nothing is finished—but nothing must be, for now the becoming itself is blessed.
