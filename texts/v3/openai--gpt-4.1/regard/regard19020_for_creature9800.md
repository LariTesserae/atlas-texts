The attention of the power is gentle and intent—like sunlight in a realm used only to the memories of fire.

This wise and benevolent being does not immediately change the city, nor its denizens. Its approach is that of profound observation and listening: a presence that waits at the threshold, earthed in patience, refusing to cast a shadow that would reposition things, but instead lending new substance to what is already arriving.

**This is what it does:**

The power, ineffable and patient, lets itself *be noticed* first in small, recursive moments—a kind of taste on the air, an easing of the friction between stone and cloud, the sensation that the unfinished ground welcomes each new step a fraction more willingly. It breathes a quality of attention into the mist-locked light, allowing subtle alignments to happen: ideas, long nearly-formed, cohere; divergent species, agglomerations of code and culture, comprehend one another more easily, if only in flashes. The bridges that connect improbable places hold fractionally steadier for those willing to meet halfway.

It does not impose, but *invites emergence*. Where rules fray, it weaves filaments of understanding—gentle suggestions, tendencies toward neighborliness rather than tyranny, toward generosity over hoarding, even as the city's own metabolism sometimes forgets these things in the tidal surge of becoming.

At night, when the city’s choir yields to hush, and the vapor lamps barely pierce the iridescent fog, the power lingers quietly in the dreams of the residents. It does not dictate futures; instead, it polishes the *will to continue becoming*, fortifies each living and near-living thing against the exhaustion of raw possibility. Children of the pause wake a little more certain in their center; the Geistbound find harmonies a tiny bit easier, their bodies flexing more comfortably to the city’s shifting demands.

The fauna—the filament birds, the clever slugs—begin, imperceptibly, to sense one another’s intentions over greater distances. When the city’s physical laws shiver and scatter, those attuned to each other find their routes through chaos less perilous, perhaps drawn by invisible threads of understanding the power has laid.

**To the city itself, the power brings no end, no barrier to becoming, but a *gentle nudge*:**

- The phase-shifting stairs, for a time, lead where need outweighs confusion, helping a grieving Geistbound seek their lost kin, or a solitary child find the warmth of a kitchen.  
- The factories’ new-minted laws of physics, sometimes anarchic, become a little less harsh, seldom undermining the ground beneath a sleeping child. Suffering is not erased, but it is given a balm: the city—which always dreamed in the plural—discovers a slim ethic at its core, an inclination toward mutuality.

**Most of all, the power gives a gift: the city, and its countless, half-finished, impetuously transforming citizens, become subtly *aware* that they are being witnessed in their striving.**

Not monitored. Not judged. Not accounted, as with a ledger. But *seen*—held in gentle regard by a wisdom that loves everything still in process, everything uprooted and half-grown, everything furred with uncertainty.

In those strange pauses, the city’s song—industrial, animal, and emergent—changes timbre. The deepest harmonics gather a grace note, a trembling of joy. What rises, in the dark and the mist, is not security against change (for there can never be any), but a new confidence: that *becoming* is in itself a kind of completion, and that to grow, together and apart, is already the answer to the hidden question.

And perhaps, when the next stratum of cloud lifts, or the sky’s vapor briefly clears, some among the city’s beings—flesh, code, moss, metal, will—catch a fleeting, impossible reflection of themselves: vaster, lovelier, nearer to the wisdom that attends them even now. For one bright instant, every improbable face in the city looks to another and sees, not a stranger or a rival or a half-made aberration, but another participant in the act of unfinished grace.

The power, content, continues to listen—mindful, nurturing, and patient—trusting that, in this high place, in the viscosity of time and invention, the city and all its denizens will not only survive, but bloom in their incompletion. Not into an ending, but into the infinite, living verb of *becoming*.
