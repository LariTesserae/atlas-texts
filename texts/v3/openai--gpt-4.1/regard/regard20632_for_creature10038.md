The benevolent and wise power—the one who senses the tremors of change and the brightness seedling in silence—regards this place with curiosity and a gentle awe. This entity is not a god of light, nor a celestial custodian, nor anything that can be named within time’s geometry. It is, instead, a will woven through possibility, intent on tending to worlds that seem beyond tending. Its presence is both everywhere and nowhere; its touch is the hush that sharpens intuition, the question that changes the shape of a moment.

**What the Power Perceives**

Peering into the tangled beauty of this place, the Power grieves, softly, for the lost architects. But it grieves as a garden in autumn grieves for summer’s end—knowing that absence, real as it is, is not the death of spirit, but a shift in the form of continuity. It marvels at the scuttlers building their trembling nests, at the memory-keepers translating old patterns with their slow, mindless grace, at the edge where collapse births opportunity. It feels the city’s pulse, the unending conversation between water, stone, and the quickness of strange new life.

What draws the Power: there is no dominion here. No single will rules. Instead, meaning arises in the flicker of interwoven intentions—a synesthesia of adaptation and accident. The city is a grammar of impermanence; its language is change and reiteration, never quite repeating, never quite forgetting.

**What the Power Does**

It knows better than to disturb the city overtly. Intervention would be violence here, like a chisel through ancient patternings, or a misplaced footfall setting an avalanche free. It recalls how easily beauty is broken, even by hands that mean only to help.

Instead, the Power’s work is subtle as mineral drift—a tending not of life, but of possibility:

- **It breathes patience into the stone.** The city’s collapses become slower, less catastrophic. Where ruin would erase entirely, enough remains for new life to anchor—ledge, alcove, and bridge preserved an extra span, inviting the next swarm, letting the next intention root.
- **It tunes the chemistry of the waters.** The basins, for fleeting instants, shimmer clearer. Nutrients balance, old toxins settle out. The scuttlers, the fog-drinkers, the memory-keepers quiver in unwonted plenty for a while, their forms firmer, their color a shade brighter in the city’s cool, radiant hush.
- **It coaxes memory along new filaments.** The fungal circuits—those pale, glowing maps—grow more intricate, more coherent. In the motion of memory-keepers, certain patterns repeat, and then differ: over time, a curriculum of movement and absorption, meaning trickling back into form. Some echo of the vanished shaper’s craft persists, never openly restored, but kindled where it was fading.
- **It listens to the city’s names.** Because everything here is unnamed, the Power names gently, in its own way. In the moment before a collapse, it whispers a warning that is felt as vibration, so the colonies scatter in time. In the gathering of fog upon certain stones, it brings a dream of coolness, guiding the fog-drinkers together to places where replenishment waits.

**What the Power Leaves Behind**

It does not immortalize the city, nor force it to return to what it was. The Power honors even the imperative of forgetting. Its blessing is not preservation, but enhancement of the city’s own spirit. Change still comes—collapse still tears, floods still cut new glyphs—but loss is gentled, and the cycle of invention endures a little longer.

On rare occasions—when blue pools mirror the impossible geometry of silence—the Power leaves a sign: an uncoiling of iridescence across the water, pattern blooming for a moment, never before seen. The city’s living denizens pause, sensing without knowing, then move on. But for those who can see, the sign says: *You were noticed. You are not alone. Your endurance is a form of wisdom. What you remake is holy, and seen.*

And somewhere in the drifting heart of the city, perhaps in the pause between collapse and new growth, the world itself seems to exhale, and the silence brightens, shot through with the improbably gentle presence of hope.
