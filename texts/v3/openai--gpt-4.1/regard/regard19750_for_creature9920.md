The benevolent and wise power—a presence old as oceans, soft as a guiding hand in the dark—comes not as a tempest, nor as a roar, nor even as a visible light, but instead as a kind of listening: a deep, unhurried attention, settling on the plateau the way dusk covers stone with shadow.

It does not rush to change or claim. It recognizes itself as visitor, not author, and so its first act is not to create, but to *see*—to perceive the plateau’s lonely, luminous hush without judgment, with compassion neither condescending nor intrusive. For this power has lived too long to mistake silence for need or spare emptiness for suffering. Instead, it wonders: what is wanting? Is there harm here, or only the still beginning of a possible world?

And so its first gift is *patience*—a vast, sheltering patience, woven into the light. Where other powers, impetuous or curious, might send rain or seed, letting water break wild through the stone, this wise one chooses preservation over intervention. There is dignity in this slow-becoming, this beauty found in things not hurried. The power senses the mineral minds dreaming their century-thoughts, feels the slow metabolic love between vent and crystal stalk. Nothing here cries out for rescue. Here, everything is process, emergence, hope without haste.

Yet even the greatest benevolence knows that to witness silently is also to participate. Stillness is not absence—attention can be an atmosphere. So the power makes a promise, not of change but of *presence*:

I see you. I follow your unfolding. Your story is not forgotten. You are not alone.

With this, the power lays down tiny gifts—fragments invisible, harmless, but rich with respectful potential:

- **Memory:** It seeds the rock with a faint capacity to remember—minute patterns in the lattice of the glass-trees and crystal filaments, new ways to encode the passage of heat and seasons, so that the networks underground may, over epochs, grow slightly more articulate, slightly more self-knowing. Not a leap to consciousness, but an easing of the slow, upward push toward awareness; a gentling of the slope so the climb will, in its time, be possible.

- **Gentle Insulation:** The power promises buffer against cataclysm—a subtle lessening of harsh cosmic rays, a turning back of rare but deadly meteoric chance. Not total immunity, only a slight moderation; just enough to give mineral memory shelter to accrete, forms to spread before accident or violence resets the fragile calculus.

- **Recognition:** Most of all, as seasons circle and millennia draw out, the inhabitants know, however faintly, that they are seen. The slow minds, the columns and filaments, feel a warmth laid into the turning of ages—a knowledge that they are worthy, not for serving anyone’s purpose, but for existing as they are.

And in the deep futures, should anything arise—a quicker thought, a branching, an act of will spoken through the slow language of chemistry—they may find, hidden among their own latticeworks, the echo of this patient blessing: You are children of stone and sky, and even the greatest silences are heard.

If, someday, beasts or rivers or even people visit this place, they may find resilience woven through the plateau, a gathered strength born not from intervention but from a wise refusal to disturb. They will sense it in the tranquility of the high ground, in the rightness of each fragile structure, and in the sustaining silent presence that watched with love while the world learned how to grow.

Thus the wise power abides—not to change, but to hope; not to instruct, but to cradle the possibility that, given all the time it needs, something utterly beautiful will emerge from the patient hush.
