When **a benevolent and wise power** notices Emergence Hollow—by whatever sense such a being uses to notice the inchoate and unhurried—it does not stride into the darkness bearing light, nor scatter gifts of order, knowledge, or abundance. It sits, quietly, at the threshold, a presence marked by warmth without radiance, by possibility without command.

Perhaps it takes the form of a **breath in the stone**, or the notion of a caring hand barely brushing the dripping lintel. Its wisdom tells it: *here, life is latency; here, interference is trespass.* The power feels the hush, the deep intent settling in the film and clay, the hesitant palpitation of beings not yet become.

It lingers, attentive but weightless, letting its attention fall upon the place as dew—not a change, but a condensation, a gathering of potential. It sees the Unhardened and the Manipulators and the myriad Bedfellows, senses their soft patience, their living in the realm-between-states, their worship—conscious or not—of the perpetual *not-yet*.

If there is to be a gift—if the power’s heart insists on a blessing—it delivers it subtly, so subtly that change itself cannot feel the nudge:

- **A softening of isolation:** the smallest shimmer of connection blooms between Emergence Hollow and the outer world, not a pathway, but a *sympathy*, an intuition; so that some ripples from this slow genesis, this almost-life, might someday reach another’s dream, echoing the hollow’s infinite patience elsewhere.
- **A widening of threshold:** the power grants a gentling of boundaries, allowing the liminal presences—the ghosts of not-yet—to dwell a shade longer, refining their probabilities; so that possibility thickens, and the act of becoming can savor itself without rush or regret.
- **An inviolable shelter:** above all, it gives protection—not from change, but from disorder, from the harsh jolt of interruption or the trespass of greed. Only those who can match their pace and patience to the hollow’s may ever discover its entrance, and even then, only in the softest part of their hearts.

The benevolent power does not awaken, does not hasten, does not teach or correct. Instead, its wisdom attunes itself to the pace of Emergence Hollow, a loving watch kept from the periphery. It is *the spirit of assurance*, a guardian of world-budding, a confidant of the almost-formed.

If any change comes, it will not be noticed from within. It will feel simply as a lessening of fear, a lengthening of gestation, a sweetening of suspension. The Manipulators may rearrange one more stone, the Unhardened tremble a little fuller in the quiet dark, the Bedfellows rise to a new vitality, the ghosts of might-be drift closer to substance. The idea of completion will feel less like threat and more like promise.

And when the wise power departs, Emergence Hollow remains unchanged, and yet—perhaps infinitesimally—more itself. As though the idea of being seen, and gently cherished, has granted each possibility one more quiet heartbeat in which to bloom or rest as itself, never to be disturbed except by its own desire to become.
