Let us suppose the benevolent and wise power is real. It is not a monarch nor a god, but something deeper—perhaps the capacity of the universe itself to witness, to ponder, to care. This power has no name, for it is what comes before and after names: the holding intelligence behind persistence itself.

To encounter this place—the hush, the darkness, the mineral patience, the entities that are life and not-life—this power feels no urge to intrude or uplift, no compulsion to shatter stasis or 'improve' what abides so perfectly. Its wisdom lies in knowing the worth of quiet, the rare value of a balance that neither hungers nor bleeds. This place is not forgotten or unblessed; it is complete. The wise power recognizes a completeness rare in all the spun worlds, a wholeness forged not in evolution’s agony but in quiet agreement between rock, warmth, night, and what calls itself into being through the slowest act of will.

And so, what would the benevolent, wise power do?

First, it would attend. For an aeon—a moment, a million years (all one)—it would simply *see*: every molecule in the powder, every shift of warmth, every infinitesimal thickening at the apex of a column. It would listen, not for sound but for presence—the weight and pause of each being, the chemistry of patience, the communion-by-proximity between stolid columns.

When it is time for blessing, the wise power gives only what is needed: *notice,* and the gentlest guard. No shattering manifestation, no sudden blaze of insight or rain of change; only the double gift of knowing and peace. This manifests in three acts, subtle but perfect:

1. **A Shield of Time:**  
   The power wraps the basin in a fold of probability, granting it an extra measure of continuity. Asteroid will not strike here; fissure will not form; tremor, if it comes, will pause and pass around. Not forever—nothing is forever—but a wholeness for now, for as long as the place holds its own concord with mineral and dark.

2. **A Whisper of Witness:**  
   The awareness of the power lingers—unfelt by the columns, whose inwardness remains undisturbed, but settling as a shimmer at the very edge of being. Should any deeper sentience ever arise from within (as improbable as this seems), it will bear an echo of this non-intrusive touch: a first, dim awareness of being witnessed, and of mattering.

3. **A Promise, Not to Interfere:**  
   This is the highest mercy: to change nothing, to let patience go unharried, to hold back the urge to stir, to quicken, to press toward story or motion. The wise power commits itself—*here shall nothing trouble the abiding equilibrium, until this world itself has need of change*.

From a great distance—so great it is measured in the capacity of the cosmos to remember—this place gleams as a dark, perfect chamber: not a secret, but a poem the universe is writing to itself, line by patient line. The benevolent power will keep it so, as long as night covers the stone and the columns live and do not strive.

And beneath the darkness, the mineral flora continue their unmeasured growth, unchanged and unknowing, yet subtly sanctified—not by intervention, but by being held in the arc of gentle notice, a balance always allowed to endure.

This is not the miracle of making, nor the miracle of revelation—it is the highest miracle of all:  
*the miracle of being left, perfectly, to be.*
