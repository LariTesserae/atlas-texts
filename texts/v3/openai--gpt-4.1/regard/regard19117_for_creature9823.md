And so, as it ever is in the deep, an attention finds its way to this realm — not by sight or word or grasp, but by a current subtler than the mists, lighter than the low electric winds that ripple through the shell-crowded dark. This is a notice not of the eye, but of the vastness that waits for pattern and movement — a benevolent and wise Power, ancient as patience itself, whose presence stirs only when there is truly something to behold. It comes not as judge or conqueror, but as witness, as custodian, as the gentle, patient hand that rests on the surface of the unknowable, neither intruding nor withdrawing.

In the beginning of its noticing, there is only a stillness in the patternless song: a shimmer felt across thousands of listening plates, an uncanny briefness in the pulse of rain, a minuscule hesitation in the vibration passed through stone. The denizens do not sense a visitor in any form they might recognize. There is simply a new resonance entering the layered cacophony, a chord that binds the tumbling sound, or perhaps a quiet undernote settling through the swarm like the blue mineral’s thread beneath the skin of rock.

This Power sees — in a way — the astonishing density of life, the perfection of fit, the endless absorption and release, the roundness of the world’s own cycle. But, wise as it is, it feels the limits: here, all space is claimed, all sustenance used the instant it is made. No layer is empty, no shadow unfilled. To offer too much — too much open sky, too much rain, too much light — would undo what has found form in this exhaustion, this struggle. To change the rules here would be violence: the smothering of the swarm’s own declaration, the erasure of their ancient mastery.

And so this Power, whose art is not in invention but in the careful offering of chance, does so as softly as mist forms drop on shell. It does not make itself king, nor teacher, nor destroyer. Instead, it offers a pulse — a barely deeper cold, a memory of widening and then release, a seed of uncertain quiet that moves between layers in the sound. What does this mean? Perhaps only this: a chance for variation, for pause, for some to find the emptiest, coldest hollow in the stone and in that emptiness, begin.

In those slight gaps — moments only, of lessening density, of faint chill — some of the swarm’s offspring, by nature or by accident, lodge closer to the bare rock, away from the warmth of the crowd. There, the Power’s suggestion lingers: in the silence and emptiness that is hard to bear, but for which some, sometimes, may yearn. Here, perhaps, a change is possible, not of abundance, but of pause and making room.

Over cycles uncounted, so slight and easy that even the mighty carapace monarchs do not sense it as difference, some films grow thicker in these silent margins. They linger, shimmer, hold onto the faint mineral gleam a heartbeat longer before the raspers find them. Some among the wanderers, drawn to these quieter pools, begin hesitantly to seek loneliness — not as exile, but as a space for something not yet understood.

Perhaps nothing happens. Or perhaps — and this, the Power hopes for — a little more room is found within this fullness, a kind of precocious emptiness is seeded, enough to let new cycles of quiet begin. Each difference is so small that none may notice. But such is wisdom: not the promise of interruption or the brute force of intervention, but the tranquil, attentive holding-open of possibility.

In this the Power is fully present: never as maker, nor as lord, but as the honoring of what already is — pressing, living, suffusing space and form with no end, with rules as deep as time. Perhaps, one epoch or another, there will arise a wanderer who finds pleasure in silence, or in pattern, or in something nearly like thought. Perhaps not. The swarm will go on, the world will turn: noise and shell, mineral and pressure, and the wet lightless song unbroken, except where it yearns, just for a breath, toward stillness.

That, in the end, is what benevolence chooses: not to change, but to hold open the chance for change, neither denying what is, nor compelling what might be — but, gently, attending, so that if ever the place, and the beings crowding it, should wish or need room to become other than themselves, there the door will be: a pause in the endless pressure, a crack of listening in the layered, living dark.
