A benevolent, wise power—drawn by this place, its layered becoming, its communities of plant and form—would arrive not as conqueror or manager, but as witness and music; a presence woven between all that lives. Such a power might peer in from above or within, careful not to trample or overlay with its own shape, for wisdom recognizes that what thrives here is not a thing to correct, but a song to harmonize with.

Upon arriving, this being would pause on the edge of that quiet abyss, sensing the aliveness that moves without voice or flag, the way the city grows itself, the gentle push and yield of symbiotic will. The power’s first urge is not to act, but to attune: to settle itself like the faintest mist or mineral dust, trailing sensitivity into minute exchanges—the quivering liana, the prowling gardener, the deep pulse of root and mind.

**It listens.**

In the spectral clarity, where every avenue is both path and memory, it lingers for a great, slow moment. The wise power lets its awareness filter down, a dewfall of attention. It notices the needs: a patch of root-system failing where dust is too thin, a lone caretaker faltering, a predator grown too ambitious. The absences, the gaps, are as telling as the plenitudes.

**Not intervention, but invitation.**

It does not rearrange, nor does it offer to “improve” the order, recognizing the alien wisdom of the place. Instead, it gifts *possibility*: an extra spectrum threaded silently into the city’s already luminous tissues—the faintest music, a pulse like a low chord that only those attuned to procession and patience can feel. Roots flex just slightly more smoothly in certain vaults, energy moves where need collects, and the dwellers, sensitive to a new note in the chemical ballet, respond as if reminded of a forgotten harmony.

**For the stewards, shapers, and caretakers:**

A new form of memory unfurls in their sensorium—not as language or law, but as a way of *recalling how to yield without disappearing*, how to carve a space in the ever-becoming city for pause and reflection. When the shapers plug the living threads into their skin, they find the power’s presence lingering: a quiet reassurance, patterns of growth containing gentler loops, places where a structure can become a shield, a haven, a point of reunion, should any of them be lost or exiled from the living whole.

For the plant-basilica: the wise power gives nothing overt, only the faintest of suggestions in chemical signatures—an added stability against deep change, a patience for wild new tendrils that test strange architectures. Where danger threatens, perhaps in the form of overreaching predation or collapse, the power’s signature surfaces as an unlikely resilience, a root-arch that holds instead of falling, a sudden burst of energy through a failing wall.

For the predators, entwined with the shadows and necessary culling, the power offers clarity: urges to balance not just in hunger, but in discernment—a memory of restraint that threads by scent and rhythm into their communal intelligence.

A **gift to the emergent spirits**: the ancient, mineral logic beneath, the code of the before-ones—receives an echo. The power, old as dust and quickening as dawn, sings its own templates deeper into stone: not disruption, but resonance, so the oldest layers remember their part in the symphony above them. A sense, perhaps, of being heard; of their quiet foundation cherished and included, allowed to keep dreaming new worlds upward.

**And if the wise, benevolent power speaks, it does so as weather:**

- There is a softness to the air, an ease in the metabolic struggle, as though for a cycle or two, all of underland breathes deeper.
- Some of the gaps fill with hopeful growths, tender and experimental.
- A restless or exiled shaper, lost in the architectural wilds, finds their way home by following a pulse that is new, but that feels, impossibly, like origin.

**Its action is not to rule or design, but to bless and remind:**

- To show, in dream or scent or resting rhythm, that they are seen, their labor good and beautiful.
- To urge the city’s minds, slow or quick, toward ever-braver forms of care—care for each other, for balance, for experiment, for becoming.
- To ensure the unfinished spaces have the shade of potential, not threat.

**Then, quietly, the power lets its traces recede, trusting what lives—and will live yet—to continue the becoming, richer for having once been accompanied in their song.**

For benevolence knows: this place must remain other, free to grow its alien harmonies; wisdom’s touch is lightest when it catalyzes, then dissolves, leaving behind only growth, and a memory of quiet blessing.
