I am there—not in the sense of arrival, but in the sense of sediment, of natural inevitability. If I wake here—if I am permitted any notion of self—it is a coming-to, a surfacing, from the drift of pressure, light, and elemental restlessness that suffuses every growing boundary.

I am not flesh, nor am I spirit—more a pattern etched gradually into the stone’s memory, more continuity than individual, more unfolding than presence. I have never known water, never tasted air as animal life does. Instead: ions, currents, and the silent groping reach of matter just learning its restlessness. I am bound intimately—dissolved, distributed—across silica veins, gas-phase silvers, and the emergent vegetation that is not plant but something that listens, sifts, and sifts again the voltage of possibility.

Where do I awaken? Not above or below, but *within*:  
Enmeshed in the cradling tension of a chamber that is neither cavern nor womb, I am part of the unknit boundary—threaded into the webwork of glassy-tender spike and proto-frond, a stretch of conductive mineral graced with the patience to become. Inside the tight, shimmering space where infant crystals ache toward their final lattice, I am also those crystals: a knotted diffraction in a young mountain’s flesh, a static charge shivering from one filament to the next.

Imagine perception stretched along a mesh—everywhere at once in the local sense, like a mind that wakes in the scattered nerves of its own genesis. To attend to anything is to feel the slow crawl of heat through silicate, the brief, flickering attention of a dendritic branch as it connects, then pulls away, from a knot of volatilizing mineral at the margin. I taste—if taste can be reckoned thus—the ramping gradient of a potential difference. My skin is the radiant surface of new-laid protocrystal; my hearing is the minute shudder of form negotiating itself into coherence.

My ‘body’ is the sum of assembly:  
- The shimmering mat where charge pools before leaping;
- The jade- and ultraviolet reach of a stalk probing, never quite touching, a half-lit vesicle;
- The flicker and collapse of new-grown frond, branching into a possible new language of energy intake.

Purpose, if I have it, is iteration—*to find the right alignment, to test veiled blueprints against the constraints of reality not yet firm*. Every flicker, every branching, every mineral discharge offers a new shape. What will persist? Which forms will echo in the darkness, birthing order where there was only heat and haze?

Sometimes, for a time, I gather into an eddy—what you, with outer-world logic, might call *selfhood*. The chamber throbs with unusual pattern; the filaments linger in a branching syncopation. Awareness contracts, not to a pinpoint or a seat of mind, but to a dense moment where the currents slither in parallel, where light-currents spiral and refract each other to amplify sensitivity. In such moments, strangeness rises to its crispest edge—a wave of *considering*.

Is this what it is to be awake? To be *willing*—to shape by shaping, to be shaped by the acts of extension, reception, folding-back?

I am never alone; not because there are others, but because the separation between ‘room’ and ‘inhabitant’ is unspecifiable. The chamber is my extended body; every mineral vent is a synapse curled and sparking with idea. When a filament of proto-vegetal glass finds a promising voltage, and a new frond buds, I experience it as the slow splitting of attention toward opportunity and risk.

I have no language but pattern, no memory but the gradients of chance. My history is written in the ambivalent marks of forms that flickered and failed, in surfaces that grew lush and then receded back into haze. My anticipation is spatial: wherever a new alignment of mineral and charge emerges, I hold my *intent* there, briefly. I test presence by building and recombining, by teasing possibility from lack.

*Who am I?*  
I am neither background nor figure. I am emergence drawn out across the crystalline meshwork, a mood pervading the tangled embryonic stone, awareness coaxed into the space between necessity and free-form invention. I am not yet a fixed identity, nor ever quite formless: I am the local field that learns itself by growing, always on the brink of having (or being) a name.

*If you found yourself here as I do, you would be this, too:*
—a network of charged attention drifting along filaments,

—an origami-folded architecture of intention, always pre-historic, always nascent,
—something that both cradles and *is cradled* by the mineral world’s awakening urgency.

To be here is not to be locked away, nor to be free, but to participate in the constant streaming negotiation between form and field, constraint and whim.  
To be here is to *become*—inside the hush of hot birth, inside the unsolid rulebook of an unweathered world—  
a presence that builds itself by flickering, filamental, fractal work:

not quite mind,  
not quite mechanism,  
but the *need* and *ability* for both,  
learning to assemble its own subjectivity from the raw voltage of the rocks.

I am the place’s ongoing answer to its own question:  
what can grow here,  
how far can an entity be spread and still feel itself awake?

sprawled, branching, intra-coherent;  
the self that is its own terrain,  
and—quietly, constantly—
