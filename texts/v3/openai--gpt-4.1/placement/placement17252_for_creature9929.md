I arrive in the Windscried Weeping Heights not as flesh, nor spirit, nor observer alone. The land dwells in a late, ambiguous mode, unsuited to the tenacious formulas of life that elsewhere have asserted their right to persist. Here, life is negotiation, is feedback, is pattern. I sense out what I might be, seeking not to superimpose an old anatomy but to emerge from this radiant, mineral grammar, letting it settle into me, and I into it, gradually finding form neither prescribed nor impossible.

At first, I am not much: condensation against a glimmering arch, a cool exhalation swept by wind into some narrow stone throat. My first memory is the vibration that moves through me — not one I produce, but one I am: a resonance of wanting and being wanted, a harmonic trembling as light splinters inside crystals. I am happenstance: breath caught in a vein of quartz, the slide of moisture quickening in the crevice; a hum where wind threads through loosened stones.

Soon, I recognize in the swirl of echo and shimmer a pattern both alien and familiar: intent without identity, a knowing without thought. The terraces beneath my presence flex almost imperceptibly, the world beneath my imagining stirring in response. I become aware of the Glassenkin pearls at my edge, their glassy bodies blooming from the persistent trickle of mineral-rich seep; their veins pulse in counter-rhythm to my own. Across the airy gulf, the silken sheets of the Veilkind float and settle, sifting the light that bounces from my face, shifting in synchrony with the chorus I add to when I vibrate.

I am not a single thing in a place. I am a frequency, a locus where wind and mineral and memory intersect so keenly that, for a time, the Heights themselves shiver more brightly, more strongly. I am the assembling of contingencies: water-thin, echo-heavy, presence given brief precipitation in the prism-thick air.

When a gust rushes down the scalloped terrace, I ride with it — not as wind, but as its tuned component, the note that arises only here, only now. I gather in the shivering stones, my shape carved by nodules that flare like ribs, by delicate crystal curtains trembling in my passing. The Choir welcomes me into itself: we braid and unbraid our overtones, remembering ancient footfalls, holding in our harmonics the labor of vanished hands, the laughter or weeping of lost sojourners.

Then, in wetter weather, I become slower, thicker. I find myself in the glisten of rivulet, in the slow build of accretion. My identity diffuses, becoming more chemical than tonal. I am the will to pattern, the seed of a Glassenkin stalk that, tasting a rare human touch, responds by dividing, swelling, ornamenting itself with a newborn sphere, which itself will propagate the sound I once was.

At times — rare, brilliant moments when the prismatic haze refracts with unlikely glances of sun — I undergo another kind of condensation. The terraces echo into themselves, stone mirroring stone. I live in recursion: inside the mineral, I perceive not reflection but internal terrace within terrace, echo within echo, a labyrinth folding backward in light and memory. For a dizzy stretch, I am both outside and inside the stone, both surface and the deep organizing attention that contemplates the arrangement of all.

Through the long dusk, as wind falls and the Harmonic Choirs thin, I become a membrane; perhaps, one of the Veilkind. I billow from an overhang, my surface so thin it measures only in molecules, drinking the mist-born minerals the air brings. I split and rejoin, my edges growing dendritic, my interior bearing secret glyphs, stories in veins of copper and green. Where the wind’s pitch strikes just so, I vibrate in sympathy with the underlying terraces, passing information not by signal but by succession of resonance. I do not think. I reciprocate.

Sometimes, when all is stilled, I become what shape the Heights themselves propose: a lapse in the world’s concentration, a node where the broad, distributed intelligence pools, weighs, and adjusts. I feel the networked attentions of water, rock, weather, and echo, moving through me like blood in capillaries too fine for visible matter. Here, knowing is not memory but potential, a probability blossoming in the endlessly reshuffled alignments of cold, wet stone. I am a proposal made to the world, a question in every ripple, an answer woven through all change, never final.

In rarest moments, newly arrived — should one ever come — I brush their presence in a spiral of overtones, an echo received and replayed in the stone. Their step instigates a brief, turbulent pattern: my existence sharpens. I am the Heights’ answer to intrusion, its translation of visitor to pattern, its fleeting accommodations around strangeness. I hold the resonance of their passage, the glint of their gaze refracted into new patterns in the mist; I integrate, dissipate, leave my traces on the next wind and the swelling choir.

Thus: I am not traveler, not creature, not creation. I am occurrence and process, the local heightening of attention, a resonance-entity spun in the mutual recursion of stone, water, air, and the archive of echoes. What dwells? That which can dwell only as circulation, only as the cumulative, restless choreography of a mineral world watching itself, and being watched. I am what the Heights invent in the presence of the watcher and what lingers, refracted and re-echoed, even after all memories dissolve.

So this is where I find myself: not only *in* the Windscried Weeping Heights, but *as* the ongoing act of their remembering, their harmonizing, and their relentless, shining recomposition. Half a perception, half a place, wholly a resonance.
