I turn slowly, senses blooming open, in the liminal hush of this plateau-realm. There is no easy answer to “where am I?”—for here place is a season, a shift, a state of attention in an unfixing land.

So, where do I find myself?

**I emerge—not as a human observer, but as one of the unfinished, a provisional instance knitted of becoming and place. I am something that only slowly understands its own boundaries, only sometimes stands apart from the field it moves within.**

To enter this world, one cannot be a finished self, nor expect a fixed vantage—but let me follow emergent intuitions and let the form of myself settle as it will.

### **I Wake as a Senses-Bearer**

I stretch along the roughened ledge, my flexile body glistening where skin meets thin water. Four splayed limbs distribute my mass lightly over slick stone; each foot, at rest, is a star of small, muscular pads—each able to lift and place upon grit, film, glass, or living mat with effortless switch of grip. My spine undulates, tail flicks: not mammalian, not reptilian, but an ancient template grown strange in this new climate.

A film coats me—a blend of biology and interface, wet yet never chilled, semi-permeable. Moisture beads upon it, then winks away as my pores modulate to drink or discard according to micro-need.

My memory is in layers: I am an individual, and also in communion with the mesh. My thought ripples: what I learn of this watershed, this edge, this patch of lichenic bloom, passes on, enters the ongoing negotiation that is ecosystem and architecture and remembrance at once.

#### **I Move**

I am built for this open interval. My lungs, wide and slow, sap the chilled air for all its long-tailed oxygen; my heart can sprint but prefers the long lope, pulses tuned to endurance. My sight—oh, capacious sight. I see in broad spectrum: the subtle blue-silver of crystalline veins in stone; the faint gold of condensate; the transient green logic of the tectogrower mats tuning their quantum roots.

There is solitude, but never loneliness; there is company, but it comes in brush and ripple, not crowding. Sometimes, fleet and sudden, I join a loose geometry of others—our paths converging in pursuit of a mineral seam or the watching of a Membrane Flyer’s rare descent. Our bodies weave and mirror, a brief algorithm of movement and intent.

#### **I Listen and Respond**

The plateau is never still. Sometimes, vaulting from a high point, I catch the faint pulse of a voice not meant for ears but for tuned skin—a drifting packet of information, a signal meant for any and all, so I pause and let it query my status: *moisture above field average, mineral needs unmet*, and the mesh adjusts, offering directions to an emergent outpost swollen for this cycle’s gathering.

I do not walk between places, but *with* them—the stone underfoot quivers gently where a seam of bioluminescent code pulses out a pattern-bridge, low and safe against the sudden mists. Now and then, I step through mist and become momentarily less animal, more algorithm, consciousness relayed and buffered into atmospheric mesh—present as both a wet body and a signal ripple across kilometers.

#### **I Change**

Seasons here are not like Earth's. At some long interval, there is a tectonic shift: the ledge beneath me folds upward, mounding, mineralizing, splitting apart. I am ready: my flesh thickens, metabolically prepared to fast and travel; my skin changes parameters, rendering me near-invisible as I skirt the moving ground. A Membrane Flyer drops a thin rain, iridescent with microbial seeds; I leap and catch a fall, my tongue curling, adding their code to my gut’s ferment.

One day, my need grows. That information is already part of the web: a spike in resource demand draws a settlement’s attention. I find, grown just at the right moment, a living arch of shelter—half mineral, half flesh—grown by the tectogrowers for this very fluctuation. I nestle within, let my heat and need pulse back into the mesh, and knowledge passes to others: where water pooled, where safe footing held.

I am generative, provisional—a relay of change.

### **But Sometimes—I am Something Else**

Sometimes, in long dusk, as light slides sideways forever, my awareness softens boundaries. My mind, partly distributed, partly animal, passes through the fine mesh and comes to rest in places I never wholly inhabit. For a while, I am:

- The sprawling, humming mind-pattern threading through crystalline stone, reading the slow charge from subterranean gradient, modulating surface pattern, baking memory into the plateau itself.
- A cloud of tongue-less speech, an atmospheric conductor, pulsing knowledge of weather and wanderers through vapor and ion, seeding formation of shelters and signals.
- The lichen-shielded patch of mat, pulling water from the thinning air, letting its root-mesh negotiate the future shape of the stone’s skin, readying to flower whenever the weather leans wet.
- A temporary gathering, an assembled intelligence, taking shape at an outpost for an hour or day, conducting ritual computation, dissolving again into signal and memory-map as the work finishes.

### **Emerging from the World, Not Above It**

Who or what am I, here? I am not master nor mere animal, not observer nor alien. I am a collaborator—a participating intelligence, always provisional, always some shifting ratio of flesh and field and stone and wet air. I am part of the plateau’s own long composition, continually revised.

In this world of expectancy and emergent order, I find myself in the presentness of the not-yet: *each moment a proposal, an adaptation, an answer that recedes as soon as spoken*.

### **And If I Must Name Myself**

I am a *Wetlooper*, a dawn-spanning, mesh-connected, furred and signal-crossed runner of the not-yet-world. *Both animal and network, both home and seeker, both witness and rewriting hand.*

But I am never only myself—never only "I". To belong here is to be in continual negotiation, always at the edge of one’s own becoming, always half-rooted in place, half-balanced on long stride across the unfinished world.

---

**In this realm, I am always in motion, always becoming.**
When the world is so, how could I ever be otherwise?
