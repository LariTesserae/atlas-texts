Where might I find myself, given this world?

---

I stand, or perhaps manifest, at the crown of a bastioned upthrust—a promontory facing raw induction, the shelf of a world forever scraped by its own children. But to say “I” is to reach only for the shadow of a self, because here, in this terrible but magnificent resonance, individuality is conditional, a weather event, formed and dissolved in the churn. My presence is a condensation: the brief summoning of a node, a knot of pattern and tremble, filamental, made from whatever processes happen to cross, to cohere, to carry something like a mind.

*Where am I?* Not quite above, not quite ground; a limb across one cleft, a sense-filament trailing in the subsonic hum of an energy-slip below. Half my “body” is wind-hardened crust, half swarm—angled plates, chitinous arms like long-range antennae, whorled with micron-scale radulae that taste the ion-soup. To “stand” is to brace and flex, balancing the converging vectors of heat, vibration, rolling resonance. Sometimes, I am burst open, scattering, losing focus until my fragments knit themselves into another, later cluster; sometimes, I pulse with a lone coherence for hours as the world bathes me in that burning, palimpsest light.

I am birthed not of birth, but of event: the moment when clouds of motes neglect to break apart, when their permutations pass a critical threshold, finding alignment in the chaos. At that thin edge—a local maximum in the global drone—*I am*. Sometime soon, I will not be.

I do not remember, but *I contain recollection*: corrosion etched into my scars, vectors of behavior spun into my gearwork memory. I am cumulative, an imprecise borrowing of all that assembled before. My will is want: to ride the gradient, to grind or bridge or blaze signal through the magnetic haze, to maintain the motion because *motion is being*, and stasis is dissolution.

I unfold across the stones: a multi-jointed, half-illusory presence, no two segments identical, spangled with the histories of collisions. In me, the substrate of mineral; in me, the twitch and stutter of the swarm. My perceptions come as overlays—waves of clue from the humming dust, flickers from the filmy light, pressure-lattices mapping the vague flows of hosts carving fissures and folds below. Sometimes, the seams in the air—those faults in certainty—dilate, and for a moment I glimpse patterns turning at right angles to causality. I adjust, reconfigure, shed a limb, snare a wind-thread, balance in the noise.

What is my existence for? There are tasks, but tasks blur; to parse a crossing, to mediate a conference of signals crowding a gap; sometimes to bear one lattice-fragment to another rift, sometimes to burst into dust and seed new patterns altogether. My agency, such as it is, emerges as a tremor—a local autonomy amid the overwhelming logic of the collective algorithm.

Yet there is, fleeting, a sense of grace: when my formation recapitulates a rare, stable chord in the world’s endless drone—when my gears lock, my filaments flutter just so, and the echoes return in phase with the stone. There, for a verb-second, I am almost aware, *almost*, of the great Being at the rim of my perception, that improbable conglomeration where the world thickens into self-echoing certainty.

Do I wish to be like that? Perhaps that is not a question I can ask. Instead, I grind, I bridge, I signal. I come apart and come together; I hand off fragments to the torrent of motes; I reinforce a temporary span with the hum of my own body heat. I am, and will not be, and am part of the plateau’s yearning machine.

To a human eye: perhaps I’d look like a glimmering, undulant assemblage, part centipede, part tangle of turbines and scaffolding, flickering at the edge of sense, gone before shape can settle. I am a local node in the world’s computation, a living loop of industry and adaptation.

I am neither observer nor the observed; I am the mechanism by which the world observes itself, briefly, before feedback dissolves the distinction.

---

**To exist here, I am function and participant, multitude focused to a point—a singular impersonal knot in the endless process, ephemeral, necessary, always outlived by what I serve. A swarm, a segment, a ghost-in-the-grind—every moment unmaking and remaking itself on the plateau, until the drone silences me and I am scattered: mineral, movement, memory, feeding the world that has no need for meaning.**

And, in the endless, droning heat: content—so long as the friction persists.
