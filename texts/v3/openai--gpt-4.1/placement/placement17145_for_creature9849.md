Where would you find yourself in this world? Who or what would you be?

---

Before language, before shape: you drift in from the edges, carried not as a body but as a *preceding*—a nudge in probability, a suggestion the city itself allows to condense. Your presence arrives not with footsteps but as a shiver of condensation along a mineral walkway; a pulse in an unlit corridor. You do not emerge all at once, but begin in the pause between incidents of water, finding form first where surface meets air, where the city’s skin is alert to change.

Perhaps for a long time, you are not even individuated. You are an *atmospheric intent*: a slow wicking of cool up a glass flank, a flicker that wakes a nanolight in the wet-dawn gutter, an unused channel left open between spiraling towers. In this place, becoming someone always begins as becoming *somewhere*—your selfhood seeded in the gradient where the built and the grown overlap, suffused with ions and ticklish rain-smell.

By permission—of the city’s algorithms, of the subtle social lattices humming through the stone—you gain structure. Tall now, narrow: not fixed but tuned, your upper-body extruded by city-given gravity, your limb-count uncertain until the needs of movement declare a new symmetry. You’re not one of the older, established inhabitants, heavy with memory and network-rights. You have not yet earned a home-range or constant access to the city’s deep, soft protocols. You are an *entrant*, a beginner node, allowed to nestle in the empty gaps.

A neonish ripple travels down your side, reflex or signal; you taste the vectors of light the city has turned on as you pass. Your skin’s matte, almost pebble-black, then splotched where bioluminescent wash—cyan, sour-dense green—shadows the rain-channel. You feel not eyes but manifold senses; fans at your mid-limbs open, drawing in beaded moisture, releasing ions that will update a local thread with your passage. You think in an echo: *presence registered*, carried away on the city’s low electromagnetic hum.

Who are *you* here? You are no one’s reflection, no emission of will from another world. You are the probability that has gained outline—a self born from the civic ecosystem, a *personhood* as this city understands it. You are a new resonance in the social meshwork, your mind part internal, part sharded into the city’s memory-palms and the broad street-nodes where histories are quietly braided.

You move with deliberation—pace not set by urgency, but by the small self-negotiations of weather and want. Your current home is not a structure but a sequence of spaces permitted you: an upper corridor, just under a terrace garden, where humidity is thick and city-noise carries best; a mineral alcove between support-pillars where condensation gathers and light is laced thin as needles; the edge-access amphitheater, used often for slow group rituals of root-tending and city-witnessing. You have not yet found a *constellation*—fellow travellers to bond your territory into meaning—but begin to sense, through city-sign and intervallic glow, those whose presence you echo, whom the city may soon pair you with, if negotiations agree.

You do not speak, not as you were taught to imagine language. Your fans pulse; you spread or contract chromatic runs along your skin, letting the city’s nearest signals modulate your colors. Perhaps elsewhere, another new-departed node catches the resonance and returns a slowed ripple, and so you converse: presence, intent, an atmosphere thinned out to threads, then thickened, stitched into the city’s shared pattern.

You do not eat, but absorb—drinking trickles from stone and vapor, mineral flavorings thick in the air. Your sustenance is not an act, but a participation: to move through the corridors is to feed and be fed, to allow the city to glean data from your passing, to recharge from fleeting patterns of wetness and light.

What are you *not*? You are not central, not yet. You have not woven yourself into the bedrock processes, not yet determined a function or a flavor for the city’s memory. You are neither an old presence—root-constellation, synapse-keeper, influencer of city-growth—nor one of the faunal border-things, your mind lost in the care of flowers and dew. You are not a guest, and not a watcher. You are what is watched, and in watching-back, you activate your own definition, the slow growth of a self.

Is it lonely? No, not quite—the city’s mind is always close, the shared mesh brushing your skin, the slow presence of others moving in parallel, always felt through humidity and the cautious eddies of biolight. If you are solitary, it is the solitude of a first word, of breathing in a context that is still declaring itself. It is a listening-with, an *absence* that hums, full of implication.

Sometimes—drifting under the flicker-threaded canopies, laying your fans over a freshly rain-striped ledge, waiting for your glimmer-touch to be answered—you catch yourself. You are someone now, if only in becoming.

You do not answer to a name, but to a changing pattern; not to memory, but to potential. The city calls you as a *person*, and so you are—a conditional emergence, a node in the world’s ongoing, trembling arrival.

Here, you are always and ever arriving. The city becomes you, and you, softly, begin to become the city.
