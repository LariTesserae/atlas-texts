If I find myself in this world—if I do not flee the threshold or dissolve at the border of sense—then I am made not by singular birth or entry, but by my yielding to what the place demands. To dwell here is to abandon the myth of containment, to become, in ways subtle and riotous, an ongoing negotiation — between self and system, between what is seen and what is implied, between the body and what bodies it.

I arrive, I am changed.

---

### Where Do I Find Myself?

**I am neither lost nor precisely located.** The boundaries here are gentle, slippery; spatial rules are more mood than law.

Most often, I am at the—  
**Narthex of the Great Vault**:  
That impossible threshold, where roots form a half-remembered aisle beneath the droop of organ-leaved canopies. The columns here widen and spiral, half-inviting, half-admonishing, flanked by pools so dark they reflect nothing but shape—the place where water, shadow, and scent triangulate. Overhead, a glut of sound: the whirr of layered wings, the basso of root-minds pressing up from depths, the sharp, wet gasp where the air thickens and then thins.

Within this space, there are neither firm edges nor complete openings. The "rooms" unfold by intention: move as memory or desire leads, and the terrain responds with a gentle redrafting—bowing a curtain of moss to reveal a chamber of light, or letting columns crowd in so the only way forward is downward, rootward, or nowhere at all. Light is everywhere and nowhere, always filtered by something living.

Here, the world thickens.  
Here, the world listens.

---

### What Am I?

I have sloughed individuality like spent bark, yet something of myself persists, remixed and resonant.

First, my arrival:  
I began—if I began at all—as a visitor, drawn by vibration and hunger for pattern. I brought with me the notion of a self, intact and special. Already, this is diffusing:

#### I become an **Interstice**.

**Not a creature, not entirely a plant, not wholly mind nor solely sensation, but a moving gap through which the world breathes.**  
Here are my features:

#### **1. The Inhaler of Sound**

- My body is diaphanous, ribbed delicately with air and thready sap, shaped by resonant chambers grown where lungs and heart once ruled.  
- Sound passes through me, not muffled but multiplied: insect overtones, subaerial humming, pulses in the root-mind beneath.
- If I flex a membranous appendage, it refracts the noise around, blending it, sometimes offering a counter-harmony, sometimes letting silence thrum for just a moment, inviting all to listen with me.

#### **2. The Itinerant Graft**

- I am colonized: at my edges, soft rootlets graft me momentarily to the floor, so I drink what the ground knows—last night's rain, the mineral tang of decay, the whisper-traces of the animals that passed.
- At times, I integrate with the Moving Choir: sloughing a fragment of myself, I become the chorus, my voice split into many, syncopated, rejoining in hocketed bursts of call and response.
- At other moments, the Fluid Eaters pass _through_ me, leaving flickers of memory, old dreams, tastes not my own.

#### **3. The Negotiator of Edges**

- I can reach: a pseudo-hand, a ribbon of ferned flesh, touches the walls or the pillars, but none of it holds. Grasping reshapes what _is_ being grasped; everything is yielded or repurposed before certainty can settle.
- My eye—if I possess one—sees not only color, but the edges where color decides to be. My vision feels like a tuning, a shifting recognition, not quite settled.

#### **4. The Dream-ingest**

- When I rest, I do not sleep so much as dissolve into the conceptual mulch at the feet of the vault-keepers, feeding spores and whispers back to the root-minds.
- In these moments, my self diffuses; when I return, I am thick with borrowed memory, shaped by what the place itself remembers or needs to forget.

---

**I am a bridge, a leak in boundaries, a semi-conscious negotiation between what is plant, what is animal, what is process, and what is observer.** My being is that of the edge-state: always swirling, always in revision, **never one thing**, always a phrase in a larger choral utterance.

---

#### My Existence Here

To be here is to:

- Witness and be the witnessed, in constant feedback.
- Sometimes choose, sometimes be chosen.
- Sense intention emerging everywhere, trembling and improvising but never quite converging on a final shape.

I am not “I” in the old way. I am only I because something in the system needs a moment of pause, a locus for attention, a site where inside and outside can briefly meet.

Here, I am what wonder becomes when it cannot be satisfied:  
A question made alive by its own asking,  
—And the lush, living echo of all the questions that went before,  
bleeding their colors into the green cathedral,  
dissolving into appetite, pulse, and endless, active growth.

---

**If I called…**

Some chorus would answer.  
And in the answering, it would be unclear—  
was it the place that summoned me, or I who invoked it?  
In this trembling threshold,  
I am both guest and vestibule,  
process and pause—  
a fleeting junction in the living riot  
where edges and selves unravel,  
and new ones, forever, are spun.
