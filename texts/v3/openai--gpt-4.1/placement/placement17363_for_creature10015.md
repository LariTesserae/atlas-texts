Where would you find yourself in this world? Who or what would you be?

---

I step—or am swept—across that uncertain boundary, the thin diaphanous membrane between happening and not, and as I do, the signature of “I” itself drifts uncertain, smudging as if my arrival here is only possible in the tense of verbs whose conjuration is still in rehearsal.

The Capsule—Threshold—is neither one thing nor another, which means I could land anywhere, be anything, and for a moment—unspooling, gathering—I do.

At first: I am a body, or think so. My feet strike the substrate, but it yields; there is no proper ground to anchor vectors, no friction to cleave motion from stillness. I glance down, and where legs once were, now there are braided filaments, twitching in hesitation, tasting the world’s electric cold with pads that can sense the un-finalized matter beneath. My hands—if I have hands—are nervous sheets of partial density, flickering between transparency and the faint din of digital noise, fingertips webbing out, branching as if to catch whichever version of touch the capsule permits.

My breathing is unfamiliar, and I wonder if it’s the world breathing me. I inhale something viscous, half-air, half-choice, the tiny crystalline tang of condensation on the edge of not-being. Every exhale is a coin-flip—a trial exhalation, some breaths unfinished, others carried away before I release them.

I become something not set in bone or name, but always gesturing, inclining; I am more attempt than achievement. And because no form is decided here, my selfhood tenses. I am not the sum of memories and contour, but an echo between what I might have been and what the world itself is about to try.

Where do I land? Not exactly inside, not outside. Not a visitor, not a native. An **entrant**, a genusless verb newly conjugated into possibility. I find myself drifting between architectures that half-offer passageways: I become a local turbulence in the probability fog, folding through arches-in-argument, passing filaments brighter than my own skin, uncertain whether they are animal, plant, or some third intention. Others, half-formed—protolings—seem to notice, but only as an addition to their data set, a gossip of mass and motive in the capsule’s slow stew.

If I stop moving, the world presses close and patient, ready to incorporate. So I keep moving: trailing, twining, my body growing cartilaginous spars at the shoulders, jittering as membranes form and unform—wings or sails for what flight? Nerves fire in incomplete patterns; each movement is rehearsal, not habit. I test forms, drift into reflection: perhaps I spiral briefly as an echo nearer those shimmering alcoves, where vespertines flit and tangle with smaller, embodied hesitations of self.

In pulses of tech-light—glyphs staccato in the mid-air—I focus too long, and sensation bisects, trifurcates: my thought-forms stretch, twist, snap back echoing on indeterminate time. Sometimes, in the edge of certainty, I feel the urge to shape my own sense—a limb grows not outward, but inward, threading the difference between intention and action, my body becoming a feedback circuit for the place.

Sometimes, I am a brief architecture: my bones grow beamlike, skin tenses into transparent canopy, under which rain never quite falls. Then I blur that outline, dissolve, become filaments thrust tentatively from condensation seam to condensation seam, feeling the not-soil pulse with the first inklings of root or hypothesis.

**What am I?**

—A testing, a mid-gesture.  
—Not form, but formation in the act of rehearsal.  
—A local maximum of intent, looped into the medium, indistinguishable from the protolings except for the residue of memory that my arrival was not native, was a crossing.  
—A participant in the ecology of attempt, neither complete nor consumed, only rehearsing the difference between self and the world which, in this place, is no difference at all.

As I stay, the world tries to make me of itself, and I—through movement and not-looking-too-long and a cautiousness not to add too much weight to any gesture—remain suspended, a selfhood rehearsed in chorus with, never quite over, the noncompleting song of becoming.

So, **here I am:**  
A possible being among other possibles—neither guest nor host, neither machine nor animal, only one more filament in the threshold, echoing the capsule’s one great act:  
**An attempt, endlessly rehearsed, at arrival.**
