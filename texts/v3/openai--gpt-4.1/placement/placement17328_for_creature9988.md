#### Emerging into the Unlight

Where would I find myself in this world, and who — or what — would I be?

At first, I imagine arriving as a lone sun-dweller, dazzled and remote, my breath held against the thick, humming silence, unsteady in this impossible place. But that self would not last. To belong here, I would need to dissolve what I know of self, fall into the patience of stone, take into my body the logic of centuries.

Let imagination root and branch, seek its archetype. Let the place choose my shape.

---

##### The Place I Find Myself

I descend along one of the old boulevards, skirting the crisp boundary stones smoothed by so many lifetimes of indifferent air. The gray-green dusk closes over me. Dust, decades old, never stirred but by the trundling of some burrower, coats my feet with the barest film. My senses adjust — vision peeling away the memory of true darkness, finding instead this layered twilight, this deep, unending dusk in which nothing is ever fully concealed or revealed.

I do not stand at the center of the world-below, nor at its edge. Instead, I find myself occupying a margin — a liminal plateau, where terrace and root-mat give way to an expanse of crystalline stalks, with the vault of mineral-ceiling glimmering at an unthinkable height above.

Here, I can hear it all: the far-off chorus of the court beasts grinding through a petrified field; the marbled, murmuring song of the bulb-kin as they pulse unseen below crusted mineral fans; the subsonic footfalls of the reed-caste tunneling riddles beyond my reach. The constant susurrus of life, woven with the unseen presence of the scriptorium’s silent, phosphorescent language.

This is not a place for the hurried, nor the isolated. Where I am is a meeting-ground without walls — a halfway, within a mosaic of spaces held by patience and mutual aversion, stitched by resonance rather than force.

---

##### The Shape I Take

Who would I be? Not ruler, not outsider, not original builder. Here, "I" must loosen its integrity. *I become a thread in a weave, a node in a slow net, defined by the company I keep, the boundaries I respect.* My form must embody the logic of passage — a go-between, mediating the deep-below, never quite rooted nor wholly wandering.

Shape comes slow: My body is flexible, segmented yet soft beneath a sheathing of pearlescent chitin, patterned not for camouflage but to mirror the crystalline blisters and shifting gray bands of this place. My limbs, four or six or sometimes eight, are jointed with the memory of both root and limb: able to claw gently at stone, or to roll tight, or to extend, finger-like, into clefts and seams. Faint ridges along my flanks sense the vibrations of song and signal — I am tuned to the layered chorus of my home.

A pale, flexible frill runs the length of my back, sensitive to the trickle of light from the ceiling, to the rare signatures of the vaulters swaying above. My eyes — faceted, deep-set — adapt to the particular gloom, seeing edges and motion better than hue.

But most importantly: *From my body, a trailing net of filaments spreads behind me* — not quite root, not quite nerve, not yet mindless. These filaments can tangle with the scriptorium weaves, decode or impress messages, interpret the slow history of this city-without-hands. Where I travel, small creatures may settle, riding the warmth or the scant moisture of my body. In my wake, a gentle eddy in the slow dust.

I am a go-between, a mediator, a relayer of information slow and intricate as the logic of minerals.

---

##### My Place in the World

I belong there, in the overlooked places, where communication is barrier and lifeblood both. I make small circuits — from the shade of bulb-kin, whose chemical pulses I listen for, to the edge of an infrasegment gathering, where I witness the old, mournful wind of their breathing. Sometimes, I act as a bridge: my filaments linking bulb-kin pulses with scriptorium signals, letting the stories and warnings pass between the old and the emerging.

I do not claim territory; my life is marked by *trespass with permission*, by careful encroachment, by always signaling my arrival. Others know my tread and my scent-trace; they clear spaces as I pass — not with fear, but with slow, measured acknowledgment.

At times, I rest — crouched beside a crystal fan, embedding some memory into the weaves, pressing my filaments between mineral layers to leave a signal: this truce holds, this passage is safe, this change may come. My dreams are long and shallow, filled with the stored stories of the last hundred circuits, all carried and then surrendered to the slow, humming city when I return.

---

##### I Endure By Mediation

I am not a founder, not a leader. I am a *memory-bearer* — not the only, not even unique. There are others like me, improvising their forms upon the template of place and time, each carrying their limited cargo of awareness, negotiating the deep-below through patience, resonance, and the slow accumulation of trust. Our individuality is apposite, but never absolute.

I might be called a **Relay**, or a **Tide-Treader**, or a **Filament Courier** in some translation. My presence is defined by smoothing boundaries, by reknitting the boundaries that life keeps — transient, yet never rootless.

In me, the city-below knows itself; through me, its truce endures.

---

##### In the End

Here, below the skin of the world, what I become is *not* what I was. I live, not in the hunger for sun or surface, but according to the city’s own rules: slowly, carefully, with tact and distance, thinking in the long arc of resonance, and always, always humming back my own small part of the ancient song.

To be a being here is to be a careful part — a relay of place, a modest signal in the vast, echoing hymn of life made mineral, patient, and enduring below the lost and dreaming earth.
