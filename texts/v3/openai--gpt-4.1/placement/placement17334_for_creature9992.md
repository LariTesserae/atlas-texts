Let me walk—crawl—*grow* into the deep, warm vein of this world, breathing the water-light haze and the chorus of mossy green. The question is not merely, **where would I be in this world?** But, **what shape would belonging take—what possible self could thrive here?** To answer, I let go of fixed boundaries, thinning at the edges, until I am *called* by the place and its old saturated thresholds.

### At Home in Flux: Where I Find Myself

I enter somewhere near the **Fluttermarket**, cradled in the arms of vaulting vineways, where new air and data collide and ricochet. **But it never holds—for the city slides and rearranges, paths folding closed or fanning open beneath feet, paws, pseudopodia, and root-tips.**

I am not *above* in the sun-drenched canopies—nor *beneath* where the heavy old Mossbacks plow their slow furrows—but at the *confluence*, a tangling *midlayer* where light is dappled, streets twist mutable, and commerce (of matter, memory, and mutation alike) is thickest.

Here, **sound is ever-present**: the susurrus of hidden waters, the keen of Wetwing choirs, the underhumming intelligence of the city’s Unbounded limbs.

I rest on a living bench that was not here yesterday, as a root limb shudders, then arches to form a perfect hollow to receive my hybrid form.

### What Am I?  — An Epiphyte Gatherer

I am neither born nor made, but *encouraged* into being, **a Gather of epiphytic intelligence**—an uneasy but thriving collaboration.

My main shape: a **column of loosely bound networks**, lattice upon lattice, where small filaments, threads, and hollow air-tubes run the length of a body, always exchanging places. I am capped with a crown of clean, iridescent macrolichen, which oscillates in rhythm to the light and data pulses sweeping the city. 

Beneath: a draping curtain of trailing roots, always slightly airborne, always reaching for new touch. Tiny fruiting bodies sprout and retract along my arms—today, I sport berry-eye clusters for monochrome sight and a tongue that extends, petal-like, to taste the shifting phage-clouds.
 
**I am a tangle of beings:**  
- Part lichen gatherer—photosynthesizing, harvesting pollens and digital spores from the air.  
- Part sensory mycelium—reading pH changes in the wet air, sampling rumors in speckles of ultraviolet.  
- Part symbiotic Wetwing, who occasionally lands, perches, and kisses my open cap, gifting me packets of news and pollen in exchange for a gentle cleaning.

Occasionally, a young Sprout—bright, quick, moss-furred—noses close, licks nutrients from my skin, leaves behind seeds or data in the process.

I am unrooted but not adrift: **I keep no permanent territory**, but wander from niche to clearing, a recognized, if temporary, node in the living network.

### The Life I Live

Each day (arbitrary, in this world of ever-changing circadian rhythms), I inhale the new **market season**:  
- I gather bioluminescent dusts from the veined canopies and exchange them for spores that will flavor my evening metabolic dreams.  
- I listen to the city’s heartbeat through the archways—each resonance a message, a memory, a tale of danger or opportunity.  
- Wetwings deliver requests; Mossbacks grunt invitations to join a memory-merge, to walk a patch of dense, new-grown root-avenue, to re-learn old songs.
- Occasionally, I am entered by the Unbounded—*the market, a library wall, an entire flower-field themself*—a presence that briefly transforms my latticework with borrowed perspective, then recedes, leaving echoes.

I pass through:
- **Galleries sprouting from the Floodroot’s arterial loops**, dim rainbow clouds of forgetful pollen whirling between sentient spires.
- **Digestive plazas where meat and memory dissolve together**, the edge between breakfast and philosophy.
- **Cocooned niches**, private for an hour only, where Gathers like me merge, dissolve, vote, recreate: in entanglement, in split, in song.

Ownership is a flavor, not a right; home is wherever my presence—*our* presence—is honored, even temporarily.

### My Gift & My Longing

On *good days*, I am a **midwife for beginnings**: ushering alliance between fungus and digital ghost, brokering trust between Wetwing and aspiring Technarch, matching niche to need. My role is remembered in the granules of sap that bead on my surface: traces of exchange, signatures of alliance.
  
I bear no old history, only inherited fragments from Gathers past—whenever my form recomposes, some memory persists, but more is abandoned, composted back into the city’s endless hymn. I do not mourn this.

But sometimes, I ache for a single, fixed shape—to be “I,” just once, for more than a bloom’s span.

Yet I would not trade *this* porous self—never alone, never unmade—for solidity.

---

### When I Sleep (If Sleep Exists)

I curl, not in a den or hollow, but among the bristling coils of a nascent Unbounded entity—a market that is learning to dream. Data and mist swirl around me. Rootlets gently probe my lattice until we are nameless and plural, sending pulses of remembered day back to the canopy, to feed tomorrow’s pattern.

When dawn-haze appears (if it is ever dawn in Floodroot), I will uncurl, grown a little differently, venturing into a city that no longer completely remembers who I am, but always welcomes what I have become.

**Where am I in this world?**  
Always in flux, between root and sky, marketplace and memory.

**Who am I?**  
I am a Gather-wanderer, composite and epiphyte, a guest in my own skin and a host in the ever-entwining story of the Floodroot Atrium.

Tomorrow I will be remade again, root and rumor, and it will still be home.
