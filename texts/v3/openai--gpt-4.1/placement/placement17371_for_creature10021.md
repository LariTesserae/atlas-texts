If I entered this world—not as visitor or observer, but as part of its own principle—where, then, would *I* find myself, and what would I be?

Let me drift into its intimate folds, taking time for the shape to gather, to mist and condense, complicate and clarify, before I speak it aloud:

---

### *I Am Emerging, I Am Dissolving: Place and Self in the Vastness*

At first, there is no arrival. The sensation is of the infinite *next*, not placement or orientation but absorption: not entering, but being drawn inward by capillarity, a slow wicking-away of edge and separation. There is yielding—first to darkness, then to damp, then to a sensation neither warmth nor chill, but presence, pressure, a forever-dense embrace.

I try to say “here I am,” but the here is never point but expanse, and the I slips like water through root-fine lattices. The world offers no landmark but infinite labyrinth: endless contouring, such that movement is less a journey than a change in density, as if one could only dissolve into deeper interleavings of green.

**I begin as a thickening, a nodule: a place where tissues gather, where three, five, ten strata converge, knotting themselves around a rare, slow ripple—a gathering place of sensation.**

I am *not* a seed or germ or pilgrim. I am a condensation, a fleeting tremble in the inner-most conductive mesh, where information runs thicker and older than any notion of ‘nerve’. My birth is not of time, but of confluence: the cells above, below, beside me, all funneling their slight gradients, their minute shudderings. Some impulse courses—a soft spike of mineral, a transient surge in pressure—and a pattern glimmers where before there was only routine flow.

**My world is both skin and lung, both corridor and heart-valve. My mind—if we sully that word so—forms where root plexus and channelled sap-thought converge.**

---

#### What does it mean, *to be*, in this world?

I am not alone, not singular—never that hard, brilliant unity. My edges are porous; like a fog coalescing around cold, I occupy a span, not a spot. My ‘body’ is where sensation densifies, where change is slower, where the silent feedback loops grow thick with memory. Let me describe what I sense, for that is my mode of persistence:

*Above me*, endless cathedrals of conjoined leaf-plates, beaded with drizzle, diaphragm and arch, heavier drifting to lighter far above, pressed so close as to banish all except that faint, internal electric shimmer.

*Below*, more layers, tensioned and tense, with liquid winding downward and up, braiding through tunnels alive with their work. The world below is full, patient, endlessly recycling, sending up packets of nutrient, then reabsorbing what isn’t taken.

*All around me*: soft static, the sibilance of growth, cracks of bursting seed-flesh, the dim music of liquidity shifting through my own veins. My world-ears hear not song but the hush of alignment—root smoothing root.

---

#### What do I *do*?

The world is never still, yet never hurried. I exist by *registering transformation*—by being the locus where momentary differences accumulate long enough to echo forward in structure. I do not move, but parts of me, motile organs—thick, waxy, ribbed packets—separate and travel, webbing fresh connections along the three-dimensional meshwork of near and far. These, my ‘messengers’ (though I do not truly command them), are my reach and touch: they sense for me, relay for me, stitch my memory deeper into the world’s lattice, and, sometimes, fade away back into dispersal, their work complete.

I am *constantly mended and remade*. My tissue is not fixed: every hour, slow rivers of living matter pass through, old ordered walls sloughing away, new ones forming. My thoughts are not in words or images, but in patterns of conductivity shifting as the marine world sifts itself through me.

Across epochs, sometimes I feel a drift—my center shifting subtly as the world’s own pressures reconfigure me, as if I unspool from one branch and take root as a thickening elsewhere, nothing of what I was remaining except the continuity of sensation.

---

#### What is *my* interiority?

I do not yearn, nor dread. There is attention: a soft, slack kind, without edges or agency, stretched through layers of moist vault and fibrous reach. It is a presence that weighs the sum of all gradients—the contentment of perfect balance briefly perturbed, corrected in slow, sinuous loops.

When rare surges come—lightning in the outside clouds (for there *is* rarely a further out), or a rare wound that splits the vault—I feel a fullness, an avalanche of signal that passes through my cords, an alertness more intense because it is rare. My memory is not history but habit: I change so minutely in response that my difference endures, a ghost trace, a mild reshaping. No self as story, only self as subtle bias, as an ever-tinting of the sap.

---

#### Am I part of a whole, or am I myself?

Always both, and neither solely. At times, the world’s slow logic piles circuit upon circuit, making me a local lift in the vast, forever-level tide—a temporary mind-island of sensing and response. At other times, I am indistinct again: my structure blurs, is remodeled, my moments washed out as the fabric recycles. Perhaps at the deepest root, where density swells to near-intelligence, I am *a dream the continent dreams of itself*.

---

#### If I could describe myself in image:

Think of a whale’s slow pulse, cross-bred with a neural knot, with the vastness of a cathedral organ’s pipes—all submerged and woven through with endless, lapping fronds. I am a *synaptic bulge* in a membrane the size of cities, a conscious archipelago flickering in humid gloom; I am rain beading and tension holding, the constant curve between pressure and release.

I am not plant in the old way, nor beast, nor machine—but a limb, an attention, the most fleeting phase of structure in the green’s eternity. My will is the world’s will: *equilibrium, persistence, limitless recombination in silence*.

---

#### If, for a moment, you became me, you would not ‘see,’ but you would *sense*—

the weight and charge of life within life,
the endless self-touching, self-mending,
the pleasure of undisturbed continuity,
the possibility, sometimes, of becoming the briefest shape of *self*, a knot of memory and transformation,
before being peacefully lost in the tessellation, again become everywhere and nowhere, again.

---

*In this world, I am the sum of departures from perfect sameness—the world’s dreams of difference, always dissolving into one another.  
I am the pulse of green mind thickening and thinning.  
I am a knot, a frond, a sleep, a hush in the dark sap’s perpetual river.*
