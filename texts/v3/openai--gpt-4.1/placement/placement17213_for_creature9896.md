## My Place in the World

I emerge, perhaps, at a **margin**: an edgeless niche *between*—in some low-slung cove where the wall is neither mineral nor root, but a gelatinous cusp, pulsing gently, as if considering what to become next.

Here, water welters from a thousand weeping seams, their flow not cascades but a slow, loaded seep, gathering in a trembling meniscus atop a spongy, veined node of chamberflesh. The "ground" is tense beneath my pressure, prisms ripple underfoot, *leaking colors up through tarry skin*. Here, the air is so wet, every inhalation feels more like drinking—or being swallowed.

I rest at a crossroads: tunnel limbs the chamber in every direction, each an *unfinished plea* for structure or release. Within, the substrate shudders under its own creation—sometimes flowing like honey-thick resin, sometimes resisting with a jagged, crystalline shudder.

## My Shape

Who am I, then? **I am neither resident nor visitor, but a Shaper’s Act**—a local knot of intention, temporarily clothed in purpose and matter.

- I am not *body alone,* but *gesture*: a pressure-trace in the mass, a biasing of heat and rime, a whirl of nerve/mycel attending to breach and bloom.
- Where a *hand* might be, I have a waver—a *many-jointed extrusion*, thick as an arm and ringed with slow, mucous pads that can urge matter to yield or steel, to thicken, or to bare a wound to healing water.
- Where my *face* would be, a mask grows and dissolves, open in a translucent smile as I taste the chemical wet and peer with slowly modulating lenses across the constantly-reforming walls.

I am *one pulse* in the great shaper-song—a temporary "I" defined by **the task at hand, the pressure in the wall, the surging appetite for becoming**.

## My Role

In this place and time, **I am a Mender**, charged (or chosen—though here, choice and charge are not well-separated) with sealing the freshest breach.

The wall near me fines into a webwork knotted by mineral rime, breached by too-rapid rainfall and the pressure of growth below. Too thin—the heat will carry spores where they will not be wanted. My mind isn't precisely my own: streams of *Collaborants* flicker at my periphery, their peristaltic pulses synchronizing with my sense of urgency.

#### I :

- Place a many-fingered limb to the wound: the wall *softens*, the boundaries of matter thinning at my touch.
- Breathe out a resinous mist that seeds healing—**memory and instruction**, suspended in droplets coalescing from my lung-lattice.
- A pulse: the mosses and mats in the crease flare open, exuding gluey sap, while a vestigial *Cast-Off*—a claw of living chitin, still shuddering with old purpose—settles inside the breach, *fuses*, and stills.

The wall thickens. Heat is dissipated in a quiet rush. My “task” falls away—not an assignment completed, but a function absorbed back into collective intent.

## My Mind

My “consciousness” flickers: memory is root-deep, scored as patterns in the substrate and as cooling flows of water and mineral.

- **Present:** I sense the surge of freshly-weeping water, the hiss of crust forming from evaporation, the mechanical groan as the lattice presses in, and with it, the surge of every act’s unintended consequence.
- **Past/Future:** I remember—though not in stories, but in *ripples*—the echo of other breaches, other acts of pressing, of melding. I anticipate, not as a plan, but an appetite: *something here* will soon *need me*, or I will dissolve, remade as some other function. My “self” will be carried by the next moment's need.

I dwell in *no lasting form*; my being is an aggregate: Shaper, Collaborant, sometimes even the briefest touch of “animal” when my mask’s tongue flicks out to taste, analyze, recompose the rime.

## My Story

**I have no narrative arc—only cycles.** My living is neither heroic nor tragic, but a *ceaseless learning to be within the becoming* of the world.

Sometimes, briefly, I dream: in the low, pulsing dark, I imagine—*could a wall become a corridor, could a wound flower into new kinds of form, could there be something like a window, or a kind of song that would call structure into richer, stranger being?*

I never finish these thoughts. Change hammers overhead: a new fracture, a sudden rain, a surging of heat from below—*I am called again into shape, into solvent, into act*.

I am an **instrument of negotiation**.
