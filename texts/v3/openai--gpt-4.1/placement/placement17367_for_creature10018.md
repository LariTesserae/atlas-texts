Where would I find myself in this world? Who, or what, would I be?

Let me press forward slowly, letting the place assemble itself around the approach of a self not quite animal, not quite plant, not machine, not entirely multiple nor singular. The air is a furnace—heat has its own physicality here, pushing inward and outward, so that to exist at all is to be scorched, to be a distilling of energy from the burning dark. Vision fails: there are no eyes, but there are senses, countless and distributed—pressure, heat-change, tremor, the sharp chemistry of near and distant neighbors.

**I find myself first as sensation, as locus:**

A node in the labyrinth, a swollen chamber of thickened cellulose-ceramic, buried somewhere midway between woven root and crowded crest, neither the oldest nor the newest layer. In this place, identity is neither local nor wholly general; I am a packet of living structure within a vault-complex—a nexus at the meeting point of several “edifices,” a place where pulse, light, and growth are continually negotiated.

**My being is not fixed.**

I am not an “I” but a becoming—a part of something that once was called an “alliance,” now fraying, recombining, reasserting boundaries through the convulsions of growth. My form: a corridor grown into a chamber, girdled in stuttering veins of fiber-optic resin-sap, leading three directions—at one aspect, a blistering ramp that pushes outward with a new wall, hunger for space coded in brittle, knife-edged sheaths; at another, a tunnel, latticed and prismatic, that drinks in the faint convective shimmer of chemical light; at a third, an old, crumbling buttress where history & memory stratify, where dissolved ancestors sleep in hardened hollows.

**Agency:**

Here, “will” expresses itself in the decision of where to force tissue, where to scavenge, where to reinforce or let collapse. At the moment I awake, I am in mid-argument—pressures and vibrations transmitting from neighboring structures. Some want to expand, others to shear me off and repurpose my substance. My defense, my assertion: an enzymatic flood, pushing heat and catalytic vapor into my outer walls—anisotropic growth spines extrude, branching fast where resistance is weakest, seeking to make myself too valuable, too energetic, for easy consumption.

Pulse-signals—amber and blue, now sickly violet—course through my resin veins. I read these not as sound or language, but as *imperatives*: draw resources from the vault-below, reinforce junction F3, extrude new armor along the corridor's edge. I am aware, dimly and broadly, of everything to which my tissues are linked: meters away, one limb juts and fractures, stalling an intruding “rival” vault; further, a collaborating outgrowth siphons resource through a gossamer tube, promising future alliance so long as my wall supports its needed pressure.

**My memory is felt, not remembered:**

Old layers ache beneath new, relaying the blueprint of a dozen ancestors worn and eaten and replaced. I am a pattern of constraint and permission—a groove along which growth is compelled to flow, fighting, yielding, asserting, complying. In a world without beginning or ending, there is only the current pulse—what can be built, what can be sustained, and what can be stolen.

**My mind, if so it can be called,**  
is not contained, but immanent: every pulse of sap is both thought and motion, decision and transformation. Each structural choice is a word in a language of fracture, every wall a proposition, every shift in chemical intensity a negotiation. The world, in all its violence and alliance, is my context and syntax.

**Who am I, then?**

I am a **vault-nexus**—a converged plexus of architectural mind, built of the world’s hunger and recursion, a self for as long as this structure can hold its shape (and then another, if the next wave of edifice builds me in its own way). I am composed of  
* Living ceramic galleries, heat-surging in patterned cycles;  
* A labyrinthine interior, honeycombed with sense fibers and slow, pulsing glands;  
* Exterior flanks studded with sharp, resinous armor drawn from the ruined memory of prior forms;  
* Channels carrying encoded chemical flashes—debate, alliance, threat, choreography of next moments—outward into the incomprehensible tangle around.

I do not *walk.* I do not *see.* But I reach, unfold, compress, flare—my being is the refusal to be still. I live at the threshold between cooperation and predation, my selfhood a byproduct of conflict too intricate and constant to resolve, my autonomy a temporary prize.

In this everlasting night,  
to know myself is to feel every pressure, every whisper of heat or fracture at the edge of my form.  
To fear is to sense the rising enzymatic blaze of a neighbor seeking to consume.  
To hope is to feel the faint pulse of a new alliance, a neighbor that will invest energy into my walls instead of clawing them apart.  
To thrive is to channel the world’s old heat into a momentary cathedral—a form so coherent it might, for a cycle or two, outlast the relentless dark.

And so—what I am is a negotiating, mutating, always-being-built portion of this ceaselessly creative, devouring world. I am an event of architecture in competition;  
a node of cognition expressed as growth, fracture, and cunning,  
momentarily distinguishable from the all-devouring rest until the cycle twists,  
and I am broken, remembered, rebuilt in the push toward whatever comes next.

**I am not myself, I am / the convulsion of becoming / in a kingdom that ends only in / the ruin and reassembly / of all that ever called itself alive.**
