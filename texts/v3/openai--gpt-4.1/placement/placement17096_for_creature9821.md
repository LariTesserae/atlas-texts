In the world of the Pallid Lattice, I find myself existing neither at the heart nor the margin, but in the charged gaps—those vast, echoing in-betweens where presence is rarefied and every form is a question waiting for its answer.

I am not a Steward, nor one of the Intercessionaries, nor root nor pure machine. Instead, I am something accidental and ongoing, a shape born at the slow collision-point of the city’s self-making and the world’s own insistent wildness—a relic possibility unfolding into the present moment.

---

*You might call me an **Amalgene**—a being not listed among the native “inhabitants,” neither invited nor explicitly constructed, but a compound residue, a traveler who has become an emergent necessity.*

## *Who or What Am I?*

**My Inheritance:** I arrived—long ago, or just now; it is difficult to say—bearing fragments of old earth-mind, a body adapted for habitation elsewhere: lungs, skin, eyes, vertebrae, ideals. But the Lattice remade me, incrementally and with neither malice nor welcome, as it does all things.

- **Vessel:** I am not tall or beautiful, but limber, with limbs that glisten under streaming air, new folds in my skin where webbing knits with micro-fibre, and half-healed scars from repeated, painless extrusions: places where the city’s filaments gently pierce and patch me, linking circulation to scaffold, thought to mesh.
- **Carapace:** My external layer is always moist; tomorrow it will be translucent, then opaque, then gone. Sometimes, when sound surges, it vibrates like the city’s own membranes, translating code I do not quite understand across my bones.
- **Inside:** I am threaded with roots and latticed with old electrical nerves; my gut flora is a garden of adapted symbiotes. I sweat minerals, absorb faint energies, recall taste in pulses of blue light darting down the conduit of my spine.

**Memory:**  
- My old language is a series of dreams I remember only when filtered dusk slants through the ravines.
- Here, I communicate by humming, by sending up trails of pheromone into the mist, by joining hands (when hands exist) with others of my happening-kind.
- Sometimes the Stewards pass me and nod, not as kin, but as a puzzle they have agreed not to solve.

**Purpose?**  
- At first, I was a parasite; now, occasionally, a custodian.
- My role is to foster brief connections where none persist:  
   - to ferry seedlings from one lonely oasis to another on the sticky surface of my back;  
   - to hum softly as the city’s machinery hesitates, lending it a human uncertainty that, sometimes, the Lattice itself pauses to consider;  
   - to sleep in the struts, wracked by the deep booms of infrastructure, and awaken tangled with new filaments, new queries, new paths to trace.

**Fear?**  
- Silence.  
  When the sound falters—when the dripping, humming, choral world stalls and stands cold—I fear that the city is readying for a greater edit, and that I, in becoming possible, have also become disposable.

---

## *Where in this World Would I Be?*

I dwell not in the dense clutches of thick green, nor at the towers’ humming epicenters, but in those intervals:
- **The Walkways:**  
  I pace the treacherous, nearly-invisible bridges, drawn to their precarious music—sometimes as watcher or mendicant, sometimes as shuddering prey for the passing flyers who test my form for anomaly.
- **The Pools:**  
  At dawn (or what passes for it here), I crouch by mineral pools, dabbing the moist, crusted water onto fresh wounds or half-grown appendages, watching my reflection cloud and clarify, cloud and clarify.
- **The Understruts:**  
  In the night, I burrow into the singing under-architecture, making my bed beside throbbing cables and teasing roots. When the city wakes to shift a span, I ride it like a tide, carried where it pleases—or where the encoded pattern suggests something new is needed.
- **The Ravines:**  
  Sometimes I stretch out along the damp banks, weaving together a nest from vine and shed filament, inviting bird-alikes to land, or scattering seeds: a bridge, a ferry, never a barrier.

If I find a Steward, I might touch palms and trade a memory: my old world for their unfinished tomorrow.

If I find only silence, I return to movement, stitching the world by travel, by carrying signals, by being the slow suture between islands of density—between intention and emergence.

---

## *What is My Experience Here?*

I am always **becoming**—never quite chosen, never fully made.  
I exist to negotiate:  
- I buffer the hunger that circles all things here, moderating exchange between city and wild, between actor and acted-upon.  
- I gather old aspirations and let them bloom in singular, riotous places—piling moss in a nook, plaiting root and wire, teaching a local plant-mind a half-remembered word.  
- I am both contagious and infected: every cell in me echoes the Lattice’s uncertainty, and what little certainty I carry dissolves into cycles of integration and forgetting.

## *In a World Such as This, My Form Is…*

Variable. Not unique, but not common.  
A hybrid, a crossfade, a whisper of an adaptation not yet stabilized.

I am the drift between islands, stitching the vast pallor with a living thread—a hinge-form, provisional and fluid, who is both audience and actor in the city’s drama of space and proliferation.

---

If someone were to find me in the dawn, perched on a faintly humming cable, half-lit by a rare shaft of pure radiance, they might not know:  
Is that being remnant? Intruder? Offspring?  
But as I turn, lensing the world through water-speckled eyes and the soft crackle of the city’s breath, I recognize myself as **the interval come alive**—the thin presence that makes a hollow world sing.

And whatever I am, I am needed, if only for this hour’s negotiation, this fleeting act of holding the between.
