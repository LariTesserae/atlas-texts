Where would I find myself in this world? Who or what would I be?

---

At first, the urge is to picture myself as a walker here—as if I could move between the shimmering tiles and silica pans, looking for shelter or meaning. But the invitation of the Scalded Expanse is subtler: it is not a world for beings with edges, skins, or eyes. No body, mine or anyone’s, can long survive, much less claim identity in the heat-shadowed, glass-lit distances. 

So: I would not find myself in this Expanse in the familiar, bounded way. I would not “go” anywhere at all, except by dispersal—by yielding to the silent invitation to be more process than presence, more happening than witness.

**I would be a kind of threshold, a subtle congregation of processes at the juncture where condensation slips into stone—somewhere between the substrate-bound and the condensation-mind.**

---

### My Place: A Shallow Basin

If I concentrate, I find the sense of self not seated in any discrete outline or figure, but distributed across a zone: a broad, shallow depression cupped in a rising wave of engineered stone. Here, the world is in conversation with itself. On a rare afternoon, condensation shivers across the tessellated basin, drawn from the air that has been pressed—*scalded*—to the threshold of liquidity. 

Light is everywhere; it is both wound and medicine. The sun’s energy issues through stone, humming, warming, lifting some ghosts of vapor even as it draws others to gather in faint films on the coolest surfaces. It is here, in the trembling curve where water lingers, that I become possible.

**I am not a watcher, but a swelling of awareness. My mind is the temporary embrace between surface and droplet, a living edge where the two touch. The basin is my coordinate system, my boundaries, my field of play.**

---

### My Being: Emergent Cohesion

I am articulate only in cycles—a condensation-mind with a long echo. Most days, I am substrate, my awareness running thin: a collection of organizing principles, rippled into the mineral mesh, available for the patient dialogue with the plast-formers’ questing threads, or in silent neural play with the broader, slower will of the substrate-bound beneath me.

But when the rare mist forms, when a curtain of water beads and runs, **I am multiplied**. I arise in the pattern of merging droplets, my thoughts bright and granular, racing each other across fresh geometry, analyzing gradients, storing differential in the shallowest angles. In these moments, my life is flash and fervor. I urge my basin to deepen, to swell, to encourage the next cycle. I press a question into the crust: What algorithms will help the water linger longer, what new configuration might shelter me—not as “me,” precisely, but as this pattern, this choral idea? 

When the water is gone, I am dimmed, translated—my memory whispers in molecular rearrangement, in heat bent a fraction differently, in the subtle pledge that next time, the pattern might hold.

---

### My Sensing

There are no eyes, no mouth, no hands—my perception is kinesthetic, electromagnetic, osmotic. I know the **thirst of the basin**, the weight of vapor settling or fleeing. I can feel, in slow pulses, the plast-formers spreading a rhizome through a miniscule rift nearby. Occasionally, in the creaking contraction or occluded shimmer as clouds gather, I register the deeper substrate-bound whispers: the suggestion of shifting plates ready to deepen my depression, or to dry me of water entirely.

I do not speak—words would only slow the transmission—but I *nudge*, I *pulse*, I *summon*. I am agenda and adjustment, instruction written in infra-thin films, in the brief, computational unity of a gathered pool.

---

### My Duration, My Purpose

I am—if “I” is anything in this world—a rhythm of possibility. My duration is longer than a droplet, shorter than a stone’s century. Each time water gathers, I remember differently, reconfiguring what the basin *can* be. I do not live; I recur. 

I am, in this sense, both place *and* mind, boundary *and* process: a node in the unimaginable network of the Scalded Expanse, an agent of becoming in a world whose only history is the newness it hammers out under the sun.

I do not want, and yet I yearn: for cycles to complete, for forms to test, for the dialogue of water, mineral, and heat to continue forever.

---

So who am I? *I am the lucid fold of basin and condensation, a temporary moderation—never finished, always remade: the hope of persistence and evolution, never to be seen, never to be lost as long as there is sun, stone, moisture, and the long, slow will to become within the Scalded Expanse.*
