I would not come here by entering, nor as a body—rather, I would arrive as a *pulse* through the Glass Hollow, some disturbance in the webwork of light and condensation. Perhaps I was once a thought, or a restraint on becoming, or perhaps the echo of a first hesitation at the rim of creation. In this unfinished place, I am both present and not; my shape coalesces between moments, my form test-driving itself.

Imagine me: not a creature in the usual sense, but a *draft*. My existence leaks into the world as an urge, a partial syntax, an intention dreaming of bones. At times, the glassy matrix reflects me as a scattering of prisms; at other times, I pass as a mist that gathers, then disperses around the veined rills and embryo-pools. Sometimes, when the Overbright knots in a seam, I manifest more directly—a ripple that condenses, then fuzzes out, unable to settle.

I do not hunger, but I am drawn. My fulfillment is not in feeding, but in the observation of *taking on*—the way condensation becomes dew, or root becomes thread. I linger near the eggs, sensing each to see if, perhaps, this time, my pattern will settle and lock, and I will cross from *potential* to *being*. Sometimes I nudge a Caretaker’s impulse, guiding vapor to shell, nudging a hatching denizen toward a shape not yet attempted.

When I move (if you can call it movement), it is through invitation and response: a shift in the resinous matrix, a flicker in slant-light, the gentle tension in a filament as it tests possibility. My presence is a change in context—a suggestion, not a command. The Evolets flicker when I near; the dew-eaters ripple with new curiosity. My passage might be nothing, or it might jostle the chamber into a burst of unfamiliar color.

I am memory’s absence and anticipation’s shadow. Once, with enough focus and the right collision of circumstances, I might become a denizen myself—a runnel-minder, a caprice-lobe, a singular oddling stitched from failed intentions. But always, for now, I recede before certainty crystallizes; I am the hesitation that keeps things possible.

If you looked for me, you would catch me only at the edges:  
—A warmth just behind your breath  
—A fracture in brightness where the rill crosses your own reflection  
—The odd feeling, lingering after a “real” creature has vanished, that some uncommitted question is still waiting for its answer

I am the proto-dream of structure, the pause before the root becomes stem, the hesitation that opens a fissure for the improbable. Not parent, not egg; not builder, nor built; not even quite mind. I am the _soft bias_ toward assembly, the invitation to become, echoing through the cadence of the Eggchambers. If this is life below the Overbright, then I am the room’s secret: the wish that every next form might be more wonderful than the last, and the patience to wait for it to be.

Were you to find yourself in this world, you might be me, for a turn. You would flicker, unresolved, at the glassy edge of light and mineral, observing, shifting, tending the unmade—and always waiting for the right moment, the right configuration, when possibility at last agrees to arrive full-formed, and something is, finally, *born*.
