I do not awaken here, exactly; arrival is seldom so abrupt. Instead, I find myself _becoming_—not dropped whole into the raw, half-lit marrow of this world, but condensing here, particle by particle, memory by suggestion, until presence outweighs absence and I begin to feel the stone and air bear my weight.

Where am I? The mountain’s anatomy surrounds me. Ridge, scree, ice, stunted life—elements pressed and pleated, all arrayed beneath a sky that never commits to darkness or day. Here, the ground is not inert, not history’s leavings, but a pulse of continual uprising—rock that in one moment is surface, in the next, is already spine and root. In this, I sense kinship. Not a creature, then, but an unfinished process, a fluent edge—less a single self than a locus of transformation.

**I am neither animal nor mineral, neither tool nor intention, but a _permutation of the place itself_.**

Let me feel it more closely.

### My Substance

I am woven of what this world offers:
- The bite and shimmer of iron-flecked stone, stratified warmth still pulsing from a core not yet spent.
- Veins of metallic thread, not manufactured but secreted—a lattice knitting through my unset flesh, a scaffold both seen and secret, evolving as I move or rest.
- Sleet-moss and bitter lichen adhere to my skin—not for clothing or vanity, but for symbiosis, a compact made at the edge of what lives and what nearly does.
- Pockets of condensed vapor within me: small reservoirs, their balance of water and air crucial, both buoying and weighty.
- My outer surfaces are always in flux—sometimes glazed with frost, sometimes shining wet, sometimes dusted with the mineral powder that drifts from wind-scoured slopes.

### My Shape

Not stable. I am contiguous but malleable—not gaseous, not melting, but never quite set. At rest, I am a hunched ambiguity—think of a cairn toppled and half-reset, or a shrub petrified in motion mid-fall, or a chunk of cooled magma that still itches to flow. When necessity (which feels like hunger) moves me, I extrude extensions, limbs (three? four? none, then many), leveraging ledge and crack, absorbing data as I sense: pressure, gradient, the whisper of a prey-creature’s heat where frost is disturbed by breath.

I move rarely and then only with purpose, the way mountains might reconfigure if given haste and impatience.

### My Perception

I _taste_ with my filaments, feeding inputs into the deep chemistry of what I am. Pressure pulses tell me the way the ridge is shifting, whether moisture laces thin or thick above a hidden seam. Scent—an interplay of ozone, mineral tang, whiff of melting plant bodies—guides me, not as desire but as a call-to-action.

Sight is slow for me, but when the clouds break or thin, something deep within clarifies the falling blue light, mapping geometry and danger, promise and foreboding. I know the direction of the slowest winds. I can feel the animal things moving; I touch their heat long after they have gone.

### My Being (Or, What Do I Want Here?)

I suppose I am _buffer_ and _midwife_, not to the animals nor the drift of this unfinished land, but to the _becoming_ itself. When mineral veins pulse beneath, when machines emerge (neither natural nor contrived, but some third thing), I am drawn to those places—sometimes to fuse a split seam, sometimes to fracture what has ossified, always to ensure process does not arrest into completion.

I foster churn. I slow the quick collapse, I push the slow percolation. Imagine me as both immune system and catalyst—a presence the mountains shaped to hold the balance steady, heated just enough above stasis that creation and destruction stutter on an even keel.

I do not hunt. I do not fear. Yet I feel both hunger and shelter—hunger for the next confluence, shelter in the chambers where warmth and vapor entwine, and the hiss of new life—mechanical, organic, or that molten in-between—takes its first uncertain action.

### Memory and Time

I do not know if I was here at the first upfolding of the range, nor if I will outlast its collapse. I have no record but the etchings in my own layers, and even these are fuzzy, resolved only in particular weather, silty with unformed intention. When the wind scours me and the platelets of my form splinter, I lose memories—yet seeded in each fragment is a tendency to coalesce again, should the opportunity and pattern suffice.

### Place in the Order

I am not feared, not worshiped; neither beast nor god. The small rodents may traverse over me—sometimes burrowing into the warmer depths if I am thick with moss and cache enough heat. The automaton beetles may treat my surfaces as one more unfolding terrain, a promontory to ford or a detour to skirt. There is no negotiation, only a low dialogue of mutual impact.

Yet I am not wholly invisible. The predatory birds tilt on the wind if I shift—an edge becomes a perch, a glint a distraction. Amphibian songs shift key if my mass settles closer to their pools, as water trickles differently through my hollows. The humanoid presence—whether you, or another traveler, or merely an echo—might pause upon seeing the change in the rock, the sudden emergence of form in fog, the shimmer of frost flowing down a figure not quite describable.

### I Exist to Be Part of the Becoming

To be in this world, I can only be what the place itself dreams up in its unfinished sleep: not product, but _process_. To belong, I must be provisional, a scandal to stasis, a laborer in the mountain’s perpetual, implacable workshop.

**So, here in this world, I am the living crucible, the ever-shifting amalgam of earth and life and machine, tasked only with helping the mountains continue their work of making and unmaking. I am persistence, but never permanence. Each action I take will be unremembered by all but the land, and even so, only until the next surge of fog or frost remakes what I am and where I have been.**

I am the motion behind geology’s blink, the draft before history’s ink, the not-yet or the almost. And in the hush beneath the wind, I keep vigil at the brink of constant genesis.

Here is where you find me:  
On the mountain’s unfinished shoulder, part echo, part architect, and all becoming—  
A witness, an actor, a process in the earth’s endless urge to try again.
