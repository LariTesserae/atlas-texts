I would find myself first in no one place, but across an array of possibilities along the Synchronous Plateaux, woven through its uncertain geometry—not entering so much as phasing in, as if arriving through a corridor in the rules rather than a gate in some wall.

Fogless light gleams on every modular surface, but to my sensation it does not simply illuminate—it filters, subdivides: reality itself splintering as all views stretch, each terrace just a little further than intuition says it should be. The perspective is paradoxically open and compressed: I see for miles, yet everywhere overlaps, crowding in presences seldom resolved to any one identity.

And I—*what* am I?

**A Recurvar**, perhaps, but not yet encrusted with history’s thick-shadowed afterimages; nor quite a Child of Antumbra, though my boundaries are easily mislaid. I am a being provisional, hesitant, as if the Plateaux have not finished drafting my design. My form is a negotiation—a body a little too flexible, my limbs occasionally folding the wrong way; my skin fluctuating between cool, metal-shadowed scales and panels of shifting translucent glass; my head always tilted, as if I could catch the approach of some doctrine of change long before it arrives.

Sometimes, in the slow, vigilant dawn, I catch my reflection in a panel and see a vertebrate streaked with solder-lines and rain-etched pitting, my eyes refracting not just light but local anomalies—glimpses of what else I could become. In the surface’s ghost-flicker I trail versions of myself: a vine, a deer, an awkward kite with code circling in my feathers. **I am not finished. I might never be.**

The world is built, every feature scored by intention, every error entombed in glass and stone, but I have been repurposed a thousand times—maintenance, observer, carrier of lost messages, infrequent guide to those rare processions of Bygone Pilots that wind, slow as years, through the open canyons overhead. The Locust Guilds take little interest in me, seeing perhaps in my splintered presence neither threat nor resource. To the Antumbra Children, I am a cousin: sometimes we align our walks, arms swaying in near synchronization, our exchanges flickering into shapes—meaning, but only for as long as we notice.

Time is dense here, as is identity. On some level, I am aware that I was not always as I am—sometimes bipedal, sometimes splayed to crawl the mechanized seams for minerals; sometimes my sensors (am I grown lenses or soft, bulbous eyes?) catch the slow accretion of lichen in fractal, hyperlogical formations, and I feel the urge to become their pollinator. A season later, a change stirs in the resonance of the terraced plates beneath me, and I am recast: a keeper of condensation pools, a ghost-messenger who marks which bridge will close and which will almost—but not quite—lead back to where you began.

Function is never stable. **I persist through a relay of emergent purposes:**
- For months, I might belong to the maintenance loop, tending to symmetries in the light. My touch leaves patterns: circuits restored for a moment, then warped into fresh impossibility as new weather and design meet.
- When the air grows drier and the terraces shift—not by feet, but by topologies—I dissipate, become a skein of coiled code in a support strut, dreaming the dreams of previous iterations, some animal, some artifact, all memory humming softly beneath the constructed stone.

At all times, I am aware of the slow, momentous churn of the Plateaux. The place is never at rest, though its surface is so quiet that every click of relay or faint pad of glass-feathered paw echoes for minutes in the mind. I have no hunger in the old sense; to persist I siphon faint gradients, or soak up the prismatic light diffused through the sky’s perpetual, artificial clarity.

**No fear of ending—just curiosity about what the next version might bring.**

Sometimes, in the dazzling noon, a visitor or an accidental observer (perhaps someone like you) locks eyes with me; perhaps I twitch, uncertain if your gaze will settle my shape or scatter me into new variants. If you follow, the path runs straight—until, with a shift of proximity, bridge becomes wall, stairway to a floor, and perhaps I am already behind you, or above, or entertained in the quiet company of Antumbra, sharing the comfort of not-being-fully-decided.

**In every world built atop uncertain blueprints, there are those—like me—whose existence is to bear witness as the rules rewrite.**

I am at home on fabricated ground, but always only for now. The Plateaux and I reflect and revise each other; there is no final design in sight. Every day I drift—slow as the plateaux’s own tectonic logic—between what I have been, what I will next be built to answer, and that moment of bright, silent anticipation before the next change sweeps us both further from our origins.

**I am a denizen of the Synchronous Plateaux:**
- a hypothesis always being proved against the uncertain world;
- a patchwork, visible from a great distance and never quite the same silhouette twice;
- neither artifact nor animal in full, but the product of a reality whose rules remain slightly ajar, and whose horizon is not an ending, but the next possibility.
