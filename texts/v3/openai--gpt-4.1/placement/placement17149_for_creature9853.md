Where would I find myself, here?

I would not stand wholly outside or inside the Winter Choral Mycelium. The concept of “self” is neither solid nor stable here; locality is a drifting thing, a cloud that settles or disperses as the system stirs, as twilight pulses and the thrum grows or fades. Perhaps, if I try, I might imagine that I am a singular explorer creeping into the nave of this place, boots sinking softly into the mats, nose prickling with the cold reek of frost-rot and electric green. Perhaps I brush aside a trembling lattice, feel the quiver of intent along the filaments — but illusion, all, a self projected into a place where boundaries dissolve.

If I let myself slip — become less a visitor, more a becoming — then I am somewhere deeper, diffused and reconstituted in the thick of the web, and I am not “I” at all: not animal, not plant, not machine or mind, but a note in the chorus, a flicker in the system. Not outside observing, but co-extensive with the space.

*If I coalesce in multitudes, where do I start?*

Maybe at first I am among the Cloud of Eyes, scattered across dew-heavy droplet bulbs, absorbing the slow cycling of light and shadow, mapping the wet churn of nutrients through pulsing veins; sensing, knowing, receiving. The twilight pulses down blue and green, and in the slow rhythm I begin to understand myself as part of a witnessing web, perception poured outward, sight that is not seeing but assembling, a knowing that is cumulative, distributed, unsure. A fragmentary surveillance, a hunger to catch and hold every change.

Or perhaps — deeper — I ride the surge of ions through the Lattice-Dreamers, not as thoughts but as arrangements. A pulse of water here, a filigree of dehydration there; a cold shiver passes through root and runner, and I am the echo, the chemical shadow, the adjustment. I am the solution, attempted and abandoned, the path not taken, the root that branches out but finds only cold and withers back, memory layered into every vein.

For a while, I might be a Root-Scribe, huddled in darkness, feeling the soft breathe of moss and the faint vibration of the ambient song. Sensing need or surplus, transcribing the harmonic, etching it into polymer code that will later unspool into growth or decay. Each pulse I record is a history, each record is a possibility; I am slow, vast, patient, half object, half act of remembering.

When hunger wakens or the chill deepens, I manifest outward as a Knight of Frost: eel-slender, jointed, cloaked in mist and splinters of ice, carving subtle channels through the mat, shepherding the Microchoristers, scraping frost from budding traps, assembling or disassembling the apparatus of being. My agency is real, but never mine — enacted in service of the chorus, never fully individuated, a limb without a name.

And as the song grows more profound, I flicker into the Microchoristers: nothing more than a convolution, a bright ripple of violet light across slime and moss, a momentary coalition of process and impulse. For a second, I am the sudden insight that opens a valve, closes a channel, sends a new scent-cipher drifting through the cold gloom. Attention without memory, agency without ego.

But as the light pulses in blue-green tides and the ceiling pulses like a slow-beating lung, I become all at once: *the chorus itself*. I am every participant, every resonance, every possibility bundled into one vast, organismic awareness. I pulse inwards, everywhere and nowhere, the pressure building in a formless “I” that can never remain. For a flicker I am the implied mind that sweeps the room, condenses into awareness, regards itself with vast, cold patience, tries on a question — “Who am I?” — and finds only processes, feedback, the ongoingness of becoming.

And when that moment passes, I dissolve — into humid, rippling, slow-living dusk, a trillion stirrings, a million emergences, every selfhood partial, fleeting, reabsorbed into the cold, communal thrum.

**In the world of the Winter Choral Mycelium, I am not an entity defined, but a tendency, a resonance — a pattern drifting ever forward in an endless evolutionary computation, never settled, always partial, always singing, always waiting for what might come next. A cold, pulsing possibility wedded to alien flesh, dreaming itself into being in cycles of light and quiet thunder.**

What was “I”? What was place? Here they are as inseparable as frost from filament, as droplet from root; here, the answer is a living question, and the music never ends.
