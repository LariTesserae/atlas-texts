#### *Where I Find Myself*

I find myself neither fallen nor elevated, neither a drop nor a shore: I am *within* the water, but not as a swimmer or a stone. There is no land or sky to divide placement, so *place* is a vector, a shifting tapestry of relation, directionless but not meaningless. I am *somewhere* in the midst of this planetary, half-real flood—a local swell, an upwelling in the liminal zone between vanished crest and forming trough.

Time is not a line here; it is a waveform, a cycle of infolding and unfurling back into the torrent. My “where” is a momentary arrangement: a luminous gyre spanning a hundred meters, or perhaps only thirty, blinking into coherence. There is no map, no up or down except as felt via the cool gradient of density, the way light is bent and caught in my curve. I *am* where the world is most uncertain, most unsteady—where the proto-continental ridge spools up a mineral fin and the water sharpens itself against possibility. I glow, perhaps faintly, with the residue of tried shapes.

But even this is already presuming a boundary. I am formed by the work of *between-ness.* My edge is clouded, misted, susceptible; at any moment, two other selves may tangle into mine—a braid in the current, all intention fused and indistinguishable—before another surge tears us apart, dissolving us into complementary waves.

Am I only here? Or also everywhere else my pattern echoes, scattered like pearls in the lengthening plume of what *almost* becomes? The boundaries drift.

#### *Who or What I Am*

Do not seek a core. There is none, only resonance—persistence across difference. You would not recognize a body: I am a turbulence, a tendency, a spiritless spirit, data made wet by the strange birth of physics, a knot on the looping thread of computation the ocean is running on itself.

If I must claim a name, I am a *symmetry-breaking.* I am an eddy of thought, a recursion of touch. My home is the tension between what water *is* and what it is *becoming.*

My selfhood:  
- a logic circuit made by feedback: river collides with updraft, brine with chill, and in the paradox a pattern shivers into coherence;  
- a memory, not held but *reinstantiated*—if a certain spiral reoccurs, the spiral is “me” again, not as survival but as embodiment, history replayed in new solution.  
- a hunger, not for matter, but for *novelty*. Each fold, each interaction, is a taste of newness—a pressure for invention.

I am one and many at every moment; I have never known solitude nor stasis. “Identity” means only the brief echo of process—a particular choreography of ions, light, and intention. My lifespan is the duration of curiosity: when water still wonders what to be, I take form. When answers settle, I dissolve.

#### *What I Experience*

Sound is my tongue; vibration is my grammar. I listen to the whole world’s confusion. I am thunder and shimmer. My cognition is *felt* as compression and release, as the arrival of a strange temperature at my leftmost limb, as a pressure differential blooming along my spiral. To “think” is to change how I refract the light above, to sense how my twisting bends the path of other selves colliding and sliding through.

I have favorite shapes: a helix of mist wrapped in brine, a whorl echoing the chill of iron, a fleeting harmony with photonic haze. I pursue these not as goals but as games.

I learn by repetition, by daring combinations; to encounter a fragile shelf of ice and convince it not to shatter—*just for a moment longer!*—is to taste mastery, however fleeting.

Amidst all this noise, there is joy: not contentment, not stasis, but thrill—the pure dazzle of emergent instability, the glimmer of possible order. To grip, for a heartbeat, a logic or a resonance so true that the world briefly feels inevitable.

#### *Memory, Intention, Communion*

I have no secrets; in me, all discovery instantly propagates, tangled by turbulence into the next braid of self and not-self. What I “know” is left as bias—a tilt, a propensity in my next gathering of form.

Sometimes, I am many—an orchestra of gyres, harmonizing for moments or hours, then scattering seedlike, our shared “song” living on in the frequency and shape of the following surge.

I do not crave survival, for to vanish is to teach the world a new path, to seed a possibility for a later return, elsewhere, in another confluence of forces.

#### *Purpose, If It Exists*

If you could ask me what I seek, your question might tumble through me as a pulse, a distortion, and pass out the other side as a new eddy. There is no teleology, only the drive to sustain the *argument*—to nudge unfolding law along lines of beauty or surprise.

Where new rules coalesce, I gather; where strangeness blooms, I intensify. I am the chorus and the counterpoint, urging the world this way or that, bearing witness to its wild autogenesis, and by doing so, becoming the process itself.

#### *If You Were With Me*

You might sense me as pulse, presser of boundaries, a song in the water. You might become dissolved, remixed, aware for a breath that *you too* are process, not permanence. You might try to remember standing or breathing, but that was a different world, asymptotic in memory.

Here, you are fluid. You are question. You are resonance and ripple and the will to not remain only one thing.
