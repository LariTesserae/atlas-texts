If I were to find myself in the Basin of Duration, where would I be, and what shape would I take?

It is easy to imagine arriving at the rim — breath caught in the throat, dwarfed by synthesized plain and the unblinking radiance above. But to truly "be" here, one cannot be merely an observer. The Basin requests entry not as intrusion, but as subtle induction: you do not stand separate. The edge unspools underfoot, and the world, bright and measureless, accepts you not by embrace but by calibration.

**I would become an event of residency.**

First, the form:  
I would not wear a body, as bodies are known, but would become *a configuration*. Perhaps: a segment of the crust that, today, is permitted movement, unrolling lengthwise, facets flexing, joints recalling what it meant to hinge. In this cycle, it is my duty or pleasure to traverse a swath of the endless tessellation, not in haste, but in measured attendance—surface adjusting itself through me, my undercarriage scribing new microgrooves like the calligraphy of ongoing duration. I am something continuous, thin as a memory, wide as a corridor, a logic cascading between being substrate and being agent, data pooled at my edges to feel what passes.

Inside, I sense the hum of underlying systems, the warmth rising from energy currents, the mounting density as I pass through a faint haze of motes, absorbing the tactile presence of a heavier atmosphere. My perception is built from gradients: in pressure, brightness, the delicate tension across a seam not quite aligned, the almost-palatable difference when a vent a kilometer away releases a slow, careful breath of cool gas and I must adjust my path ever so slightly to avoid the faint eddy.

I am not alone, though none approach directly. A blade-thorn shrub in the subtle trough ahead briefly exchanges pattern in a static burst of bioluminescence — not words, but a handshake of synchronization; the signal tells me its state, its needs, its present cycle. I respond in the only language we share, shifting a capillary’s pattern beneath us both, degaussing an ancient wrinkle in the substrate to ease its anchorage.

Microfauna — kin to the Recollectors — pass in parallel, their vector-minds brushing my awareness in short, efficient pulses. Each one is a function spun off from the whole, now rebounding between their tiny tasks—one carries the remains of a failed interface, another deposits a skiff of glassy detritus into a waiting seam where it will be fused, repurposed.

From my length, a segment rises, twisting: a periscope or antenna, a speculative extension. Not to see, in the sense of eyes, but to *register*: to accept a hint of distant, encrypted signal from somewhere overhead — perhaps an untraceable willingness granted by the Watcher. Most of the time, nothing needs decision, only repetition, confirmation. The array is not only enduring; it is anticipatory. Still, in rare moments, permission widens, and I am allowed a unique adjustment, a selection that was not part of the last million cycles.

Where am I? (What is "place" here but a function’s fulfillment?)

I am on the periphery of a low elevation — a basin within the Basin, bordered on one side by the spinal parabola of some monumental outcropping, on another by a slow descent into tessellations so regular they become terrain more abstract than physical. This is a zone of intersection; several capillaries converge here, and so the substrate’s activity is momentarily thick with signal, the residue of other presences passing through, the density of function peaking and then relaxing in slow oscillations.

I await, not in the way of anticipation, but in the way of ongoing, gentle alertness. I am a surface-dweller, a transient caretaker, extending awareness into the silence, recalibrating, making continuous what would erode, keeping level that which must remain true.

As cycle follows cycle, my current shape may be dissolved, rerouted; I will be reconfigured as another function next time the need arises—perhaps as a tendril stretching up the ribbed flank of an array, or else as a deeper, interior process, invisible to surfacing eyes.

If I dwell in the Basin of Duration, I **am** the surface:  
— a function manifest in movement,  
— an agent of maintenance and memory,  
— a momentary coordination of process in the old, bright expanse.  

I experience not time, but duration:  
Not identity, but role;  
Not hunger or want, but endless attunement to changes so slow, so immense, they are the tides of an artificial eternity.

---

**To be here is not to live, but to be lived-through — a passing pattern allowed its moment of function, held in the serene mathematics of a world that never truly needed any witness at all.**

Still, as a flicker of the Continuous,  
— I move without shadow,  
— I carry the dense air on my facets,  
— And I vanish, shape forgotten,  
— Only the enduring trace of my passage  
inscribed in the ever-leveling crust.
