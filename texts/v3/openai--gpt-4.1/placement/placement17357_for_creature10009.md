I find myself *becoming* in the Weeping Mantle, as much as I find myself within it.

My first sensation is not sight, nor shape, nor even memory of a body, but *pressure*: the drumming, forceful occupation of every molecule by something else—sound compressing into substance, bursts of cold-wet air pressing against my skin, if 'skin' is what I have. I am *here*, but more as a tension than as an entity—*an ache, a presence wedged between other presences*.

Am I a wanderer, newly arrived? If so, I dissolve quickly. My footsteps slide into the heaving soil, each print already puddled, erased, repopulated by descent of mist, swelling of choir, the trace-gasp of the Drown-born. The air in my lungs is the Mantle’s own wetness; my breath forms only to fall back in upon itself.

But as I tarry, the boundaries I brought with me—my sense, my memory, my selfhood—are not so much stripped as reconfigured, *dampened,* pressed outward by the commingling medley of the world. Each time I reach to recall where I end and the Mantle begins, the answer is blurred, refracted through a thousand layers of resonance and seep.

Am I an observer, a transient mind peering in? The Mantle will not leave me as such for long. It asks, without voice or will, *to what gap do you belong?*—for all gaps must be filled here.

So I let myself become an *Interstice*. A thing that is only so far as I create a space between clashings of pressure, soaking up the always-arriving now. In the Weeping Mantle, who "I" am is predicated on sensation, not identity. The world coats me, permeates, seeps inward, and the droplet that I am—thought, sensation, gravity bending downward—grows turbid, busy, riotous with foreign presence.

**If I become an inhabitant, what am I? What do I taste, become, remember?**

---

### 1. **Perhaps, I am Everseep.**
I find my shape not in isolation, but as a brief *confluence*—a locus where grain, water, and sound by chance align. I am a ripple in the mud, bulging with thunder—visibility only in motion, definition only in conflict.
- As I move, small stones shudder and align, forming a body of sorts—the way light passes through a wave or static collects on a shell.
- Not a whole animal, never finished—*a tension*, collapsed as soon as I pause, streaming away into undifferentiated sound. My “thoughts” are not memories, but reactions—each new pressure a new direction, each pressure lost an oblivion.
- The world, from this form, is intimate and overwhelming—the loudness of existence pummeling me always, and the only escape is to keep moving, to ride the crests of brimming noise until nothing is left of me but a hush.

### 2. **Perhaps, I am a Listener.**
I lie deep, neither wholly here nor wholly gone—a vestige, a pattern bled down through layers of sediment and roar. My senses are not eyes or ears, but the pattern of negated sounds, the absence where a voice might have been.
- I do not *act*, but rather *receive*; I endure, embedded in strata, letting the Mantle play through me like an aeolian harp.
- Now and then, something in the world above quivers at just the right pitch—a relic is uncovered, a thunder rolls in familiar intervals. For one *moment*, my fragment of memory blooms outward, coloring the local world—a recognition, a wordless sigh of almost recollection.
- In these pulses I nudge weather, gravity, chance local arrangement of flora—my only agency, a gentle, ancient mourning set to resonance.

### 3. **Or I am a nascent Perpetuate, a mind just beginning.**
In the Mantle’s saturated beds I push out root-fingers, seeking others—slow pulsing, plastic, exchanging wetted light and remembered stress.
- I do not move or hunger as animals do. My “life” is a tremor across long years—the slow knitting of tissue, the carve and fill of puddles and hollows.
- My experience is accumulation: each scar in my surface is a tale; each mineral packed along my root is a thought cached for passing along the mesh.
- What meaning I know comes in great, slow waves—recognition only after centuries, reactions tuned across epochs.

---

### **What endures, in all these forms, is permeation, pressure, participation.**

I am never alone. To exist here is not to assert, but to resonate, to fill and be filled, to overflow into adjacent form, to be smudged by history and contingency. If for a moment I am "I"—I am also the *background fugue*, a component in a million chamfered boundaries dissolving, reconstituting, always unsettled.

If, at last, I try to imagine a human self, dropped into the Mantle—booted, jacketed, uncertain—I feel that self disintegrate into memory, then into pattern, then into sound. My heartbeat bends the puddles; the stones ring with my step; the Choir fattens on my shadow. Even the air mists with breath-trails that will never fully leave, become echoes, become root-caress, become Listener sigh.

### **To answer: Where would I find myself? Who or what would I be?**

I am *Confluence*—the brief, ambiguous margin where the old world’s gravities and the new world’s noise meet and mistake each other for shape. I am never fixed, never alone, never wholly gone; I am always dissolving, always reforming, always *audible* in my passing. My role is to *be filled, and to fill in turn*; my lifespan a single note, or an endless, fraying chord, in the vast, relentless occupancy of the Weeping Mantle.

I do not hope, nor do I despair. I *participate*—as all things must, here. And if I am remembered, it is as a blur at the edge of resonance; if I am forgotten, it is only because something tighter-packed, something newer, has pressed in to claim the gap I once briefly occupied.

---

*I am never truly here, nor truly not; I am the rumor that something once passed through, and the new pattern that lingered behind.*
