### **Traveler's Advisory & Preparation Guide: The Schist Nodule**

**Designation:** Embryonic Geode, Crystalline Womb, The Nodule.
**Threat Level:** 4/5 (Existential, Not Hostile)
**Primary Mandate:** Observe. Do Not Interfere. Do Not Linger.

#### **1. Philosophical & Psychological Preparation**

Before you even begin packing, you must prepare your mind. The Nodule is not a place in the conventional sense; it is a live process of reality formation. To enter it is to step into an idea that has not yet finished thinking itself.

*   **Embrace Impermanence:** Your most fundamental assumption—that the world is solid and persistent—is a liability here. Meditate on concepts of flux, flow, and conditional existence. Practice mindfulness techniques that allow you to observe sensations without attachment. You will see things form from nothing and dissolve into nothing. If you are deeply attached to the stability of matter, you risk psychological collapse.
*   **Abandon Causality:** The rules of cause and effect are soft in the Nodule. Things happen not because something *made* them happen, but because the ambient conditions *allow* them to exist. Do not look for reasons. Look for rhythms.
*   **Cultivate Humility:** You are a contaminant. You are an anomaly of old, stable matter in a place of pure, nascent potential. You are a mote of dust in a clean room. Your presence is a disruption. Act accordingly. You are not an explorer conquering a peak; you are a guest in a fragile nursery, and you are not welcome.

#### **2. Physical Gear & Attire**

Your gear is not for surviving an environment, but for mitigating your impact upon it and protecting your senses from its unique properties.

*   **Attire - The Second Skin:**
    *   **Material:** Wear a one-piece, non-shedding synthetic suit. Think clean-room bunny suit or a diver's skin. Materials like sil-nylon or dyneema are ideal. It must have no loose fibers, no fraying seams, no pilling fabric. Every loose thread you leave behind could become a seed for some unforeseen and potentially catastrophic crystallization.
    *   **Color:** Your suit should be a dark, neutral grey or matte black. In a world of sourceless, shadowless light, you need to be able to see your own limbs clearly. Black provides the only reliable contrast.
    *   **Footwear:** Soft-soled, high-traction boots are essential. The floor is yielding. You need to be able to feel it give beneath you. Avoid heavy, rigid-soled hiking boots. Imagine you are walking on a gigantic, barely-cooked meringue.

*   **Sensory Protection - Your Shielding:**
    *   **Vision:** This is your most critical gear. You need a two-stage system.
        *   **Stage 1 (Standard Wear):** A pair of high-quality, wrap-around sunglasses with polarized, category 3 lenses for the baseline brightness.
        *   **Stage 2 (Peak Pulse):** A full-seal goggle with **heavily polarized, iridium-coated, Category 4 or darker lenses.** These are glacier goggles on steroids. You will flip this down over your sunglasses during the light's waxing phase. Viewing the Peak Pulse with the naked eye is not like looking at the sun; it's like having your optic nerves exposed to a medical sterilization lamp. It will cause permanent damage.
    *   **Auditory:** The acoustic deadness is profoundly disorienting. It can induce a sense of claustrophobia or solipsism. Do *not* wear earplugs; this will only heighten the internal sounds of your own body to a maddening degree. Instead, bring a **personal acoustic anchor**. This can be a simple, non-electric device: a wind-up pocket watch, a small metal clicker, even a string of smooth stone beads you can run through your fingers. The tiny, predictable sound it creates will be your only auditory landmark, a tether to your own stable reality.

*   **Essential Tools - The Un-Kit:**
    *   **Chronometer:** A simple, rugged, non-smart digital watch with a clear display and a reliable countdown timer. Your life depends on tracking the twenty-minute Great Pulse. Set a repeating 20-minute countdown timer the moment you enter.
    *   **Probe:** A non-metallic, non-reactive probe. A simple rod of polished ceramic or carbon fiber, about a meter long. You will not use this to pry or chip. You will use it to gently test the solidity of the floor ahead of you and to maintain distance from the crystalline walls without using your hands.
    *   **NO SAMPLING EQUIPMENT.** Do not bring hammers, chisels, or collection jars. Attempting to take a "souvenir" is a suicidal act of cosmic vandalism. The crystals are not objects; they are expressions of the Nodule's fundamental syntax. To break one is akin to ripping a word out of the source code of a live program. The results are unpredictable and likely fatal.

*   **Prohibited Items:**
    *   **Organic Matter:** No food. No water from your home dimension. No leather goods, no wooden staffs. The Nodule's ecosystem is purely mineral and energetic. Introducing complex organic molecules is an act of extreme contamination.
    *   **Complex Electronics:** Leave your phone, camera, and sensors behind. The intense actinic light and entropic flux can and will destroy delicate circuitry. A camera would not capture the Nodule anyway; it would only capture a flat, overexposed white-out.
    *   **Personal Trinkets:** Do not bring anything of sentimental value. During the trough of a Pulse, it might begin to fray at the edges, its existence thinning. Watching your grandfather's compass dissolve into milky nothingness is a trauma from which you will not recover.

#### **3. On Arrival: The First Cycle**

Squeezing through the hexagonal fracture will be an instantaneous transition. One moment, damp schist; the next, sterile brilliance.

1.  **Freeze.** Do not take a step. Stand just inside the aperture.
2.  **Calibrate.** Start your 20-minute timer. Put on your Stage 1 sunglasses. Take your first breath; note the smell of ozone and wet stone. Feel the cool, static charge on your skin.
3.  **Observe One Full Cycle.** Stay put for a full twenty minutes. Watch the Great Pulse wax and wane. See the new crystals precipitate during the peak. Watch the world soften during the trough. Get a feel for the rhythm. This is the single most important thing you can do to ensure your survival. Assess your own psychological state. If the experience is already overwhelming, leave. There is no shame in it.

#### **4. Navigating the Nodule**

*   **Movement:** Walk slowly and deliberately. Place your feet with care, using your probe to gently test the path ahead if you feel uncertain. Keep your movements fluid and your center of gravity low. Never lose sight of the hexagonal exit. It is your only way home, and there is no guarantee it will remain stable indefinitely.
*   **The Walls:** Do not touch the crystal walls. They are growing, and their edges are atomically sharp. Brushing against them will slice through your suit and your skin with surgical precision.
*   **The "Flora":** Give the glass-like stalks a wide berth. The sub-audible hum they emit may have physiological effects. Their impossible grace is a sign of an alien geometry; do not get close enough to fall under its spell.
*   **The Inhabitants:**
    *   **Glass-Striders:** They are harmless and will ignore you. Be mindful of their near-invisibility. You are more of a threat to them than they are to you. Do not step on them. Their sound is a useful indicator of the Nodule's stability; if the *skitter-skritch* stops, the light is in its trough.
    *   **Motes:** **AVOID AT ALL COSTS.** These are not creatures; they are localized failures in physical law. Do not attempt to touch them or pass through them. Treat them as small pockets of pure chaos. Passing a limb through a Mote could result in it being re-mapped to a different set of physical constants or simply being erased from your timeline.
    *   **The Weavers:** Grant these beings the greatest respect and the widest possible berth. They are the architects and gardeners of this place. Their work is sacred and fundamental. Do not approach them. Do not get between a Weaver and its scaffold. Do not, under any circumstances, touch their woven structures. To disturb a Weaver is to disturb the Nodule at its highest level of organization. They will not attack you in a conventional sense; they might simply un-weave the portion of the floor you are standing on.

#### **5. Surviving the Great Pulse**

Your chronometer is your guide.

*   **Waxing Phase (Minutes 10-19):** The light will begin to intensify. Your Stage 1 sunglasses will no longer be sufficient. Flip down your Stage 2 goggles. Find a clear spot on the floor, away from walls and Weavers, and stay still.
*   **Peak Pulse (Minute 20/0):** For about ten to fifteen seconds, the light will reach its blinding maximum. **DO NOT MOVE.** Keep your head down. Weather the sensory assault. You will hear and feel a high-frequency vibration in the air as new crystals snap into existence. It may feel as if the very atoms of your body are buzzing. Use your acoustic anchor to ground yourself.
*   **Waning Phase (Minutes 1-9):** The light will dim to its lowest point, a soft, pearlescent twilight. This is the Phase of Dissolution. Matter becomes "soft." Look at your gloved hands; you may notice they seem less defined. This is why you bring no sentimental items. The Glass-Striders will freeze. This is your best time to move if you must, but be aware that the world is at its most fragile.

#### **Egress**

Do not stay for more than two or three cycles. The Nodule is energetically and existentially taxing. The longer you stay, the more "attuned" you become to its state of flux, and the harder it may be for your own stable matter to hold its form.

Return to the hexagonal opening. Do not be surprised if its edges seem sharper or have minuscule new facets. Squeeze back through into the mundane dark of the rock seam.

You will be disoriented. Your eyes will take a long time to adjust. The world will feel oppressively loud, shadowy, and crudely solid. You will carry the clean scent of ozone with you for hours. You will not forget the silent, shadowless light. You are not the same person who went in. You have witnessed a universe being born. Act accordingly.
