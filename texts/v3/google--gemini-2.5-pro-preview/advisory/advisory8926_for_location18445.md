### **Traveler's Briefing: Designation K-734, "The Chime-Gardens"**

**Threat Assessment:** Amber-3 (Environmental Hazard, Non-Hostile). Actively dangerous but predictably so. All threats are passive functions of the environment itself.

**Primary Objective:** Observation, data collection, and existential contemplation. This is a "look, don't touch" world in its most extreme form.

### **Phase 1: Pre-Departure Gear & Bodily Preparation**

Your standard multiverse travel pack is insufficient. You will need a kit tailored specifically to the dual threats of **HEAT** and **SOUND**, with a secondary focus on **PARTICULATE INTRUSION**.

**1. The Environmental Suit: Your Second Skin**

Do not even *think* about setting foot here in anything less than a full-body, sealed environmental suit. This is non-negotiable.

*   **Thermal Regulation:** The primary concern is ground-conducted heat. Your suit must have Class-IV thermal-insulating soles. Standard radiant shielding will not be enough. The soles of your boots should be thick, non-conductive, and reinforced. We recommend integrating a low-level haptic dampening gel into the boot lining; it will help mitigate the constant vibration. The rest of the suit requires an active cooling system. A phase-change liquid coolant undergarment is the minimum. Your suit's heat sinks will be working overtime, so ensure your power supply is robust.
*   **Respiration:** The atmosphere, while likely breathable in composition (our probes suggest a nitrogen-oxygen mix with high mineral content), is filled with a fine, abrasive dust. A standard filter will be shredded or clogged within an hour. You require a closed-loop rebreather system. Do not rely on filtering the local atmosphere. The dust is comprised of sharp, metallic silicates and is both a respiratory and mechanical hazard.
*   **Visor Configuration:** The light is dim, constant, and monochromatic ("old brass"). Your visor should have low-light amplification. However, you also need adjustable polarization. The Cinder-Bloom stalks are like black glass and create unexpected, sharp glints of light that can be disorienting. A good visor will filter these flashes while amplifying the ambient light.

**2. Audio Management: Protecting Your Mind**

This is the most alien aspect of the Kivas. The "sound" is not just auditory; it is a physical pressure wave, a bone-conducted vibration.

*   **External Muffling:** Your helmet should be sound-proofed as a baseline.
*   **Internal Cancellation:** Standard active noise-cancellation (ANC) headphones will fail spectacularly. The vibration will bypass them entirely and go straight through your jaw and skull. You need a full-helmet system that incorporates **bone-conduction dampeners**. These are specialized pads that press against the mastoid bone and other key cranial points, generating counter-vibrations to nullify the drone.
*   **Controlled Input:** Do not attempt to block the sound completely. This can lead to sensory deprivation and disorientation. Your audio system should be programmed to filter the overwhelming drone down to a manageable hum, while isolating and amplifying any anomalous sounds—the scrape of your own feet, the unlikely fall of a rock, the crack of a particularly large cane. You need situational awareness, not total silence.

**3. Sensory & Exploration Tools**

*   **Light Source:** Bring a headlamp, but do not use a wide-angle floodlight. The light will catch on the millions of dust particles, creating a dazzling, impenetrable wall of shimmer. Use a tight-beam, variable-intensity lamp to pick out details on the path ahead.
*   **Navigation:** A magnetic compass will be unreliable due to the high metallic salt content of the flora and dust. Rely on an inertial navigation system within your suit, and visually map your progress against the terraced rings of the city. A simple downward journey to the basin floor and back up is easy to track. Traversing the rings horizontally is where you can get lost.
*   **Sampling Kit:** If authorized, bring only non-contact sampling tools. A static-charge dust collector for the airborne particulates and a laser-spectrometer for analysing the canes from a distance. Do not bring hammers, drills, or saws. You are not equipped to handle the local materials.

**4. Psychological Preparation**

*   **The Drone:** Before your trip, spend several hours listening to recordings of psycho-active resonant frequencies (we can provide a safe training suite). Practice mindfulness meditation while being exposed to a constant, low-level physical vibration. You must train your brain to accept the Drone as a baseline state of being, to let it fade into the background. Failure to do so can result in anxiety, paranoia, and a debilitating sense of pressure.
*   **The Emptiness:** This is a tomb. The city is perfectly preserved and utterly devoid of life. The combination of intense sensory input (the Drone) and profound biological emptiness is deeply unnerving. Be prepared for feelings of intense loneliness and the sensation of being watched by ghosts. Travel with a partner if possible, maintaining a constant comms link. If traveling alone, schedule mandatory check-ins with your anchor point.

### **Phase 2: On-Site Protocol & Navigation**

**The First Five Minutes:**
Upon arrival, stand still. Do not explore immediately. Let your suit's systems stabilize. Let your mind acclimatize to the Drone, allowing your audio dampeners to calibrate. Feel the heat through your boots. Watch the dust swirl. Reconcile the sensory data with your expectations. This is the most critical moment of your visit.

**Movement & The "Path":**

*   The wide avenues, plazas, and terraces are your paths. They were designed for it. The obsidian and basalt plates are smooth but can be slick with dust. Walk deliberately.
*   Do **NOT** enter the thickets of Cinder-Bloom. Ever. The ground beneath them is a network of deep, superheated root channels. More importantly, the canes themselves are fragile. Brushing against one could cause a cascade of fractures, sending sharp, glass-like splinters in all directions. You do not want to test the integrity of your suit against a shower of silica needles.
*   Observe the canes from a respectful distance. Use your visor's zoom function. Note their inner cherry-red glow; this is a direct visual indicator of the thermochemical process. The brighter the glow, the more active (and likely unstable) that particular stalk is.

**Interacting with the City:**

*   You are a guest in a graveyard. Act like it.
*   The troglodyte dwellings are safe to enter, but be mindful that the dust is thickest in these enclosed, still spaces. Kick up as little as possible.
*   Examine the tools, the grinding bowls, the dust channels. You are seeing a completely alien technological path—a civilization based not on hydraulics, but on "pyro-clastics" and granulakinesis (the movement of dust). They did not manage water; they managed stone. Try to understand the logic of the city's design.
*   Do not move anything. The placement of a rake, the dust on a chisel—these are millennia-old data points.

### **Phase 3: Departure & Decontamination**

The silvery dust is your primary contaminant. It is abrasive, potentially reactive, and will work its way into every joint, seal, and mechanism it can.

*   **Pre-Departure Purge:** Before initiating your return jump, run your suit's external high-pressure gas purge cycle. This will blow off the worst of the dust.
*   **Full Decontamination:** Upon return to your ship or base, you must undergo a full decontamination protocol in a dedicated airlock. This involves electrostatic discharge to neutralize the particles' adhesion, followed by another gas purge and finally a solvent wash.
*   **Equipment Quarantine:** All equipment, including your suit, must be broken down and meticulously cleaned. Any gear with exposed moving parts that was brought to the surface will likely need to be replaced.

**Final Note:**

Visiting the Ash-Gardens is not an adventure; it is a pilgrimage to a place of profound alienness. It will recalibrate your understanding of what "life" can be. The constant, physical chime is the heartbeat of a world that is breathing stone and exhaling music. It is an experience that will vibrate within your memory long after the physical sensation has faded. Prepare well, go with respect, and listen to what the silence between the chimes has to say.
