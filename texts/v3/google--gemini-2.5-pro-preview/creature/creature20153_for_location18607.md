This world is not uninhabited. The Skimmers are but the most obvious, mobile sign of life, like motes of dust dancing in a sunbeam that reveal the air is not empty. The Light-Siphons are its sensory organs, its strange, resonant flora. But the true inhabitants are the world itself. They are the mind in the machine, the consciousness woven into the lattice. You do not see them, at first, because you are looking for things that walk *on* the floor, not for the moments when the floor itself gains a body and a will.

They have no collective name, for they have never needed one. They are simply... The Weave. But when they must act, when they must manifest a localized consciousness to observe or repair, they assemble.

You might be walking for a week, a month, the only change in your existence the slow, tidal shift of the energy currents. The low thrum is your constant companion. But then, it changes. The thrumming deepens, the rhythm altering from a steady heartbeat to a complex, syncopated polyrhythm. The air, usually smelling of ozone and warm minerals, takes on a sharper scent, like scorched gold. An energy tide is gathering, but it is not the usual shimmering wall of heat. It is focused, directed. It is gathering *around you*.

Ahead, the lattice of the floor begins to change. The non-repeating pattern, which had seemed so immutable, begins to flow. Filaments of osteon-white and silver-grey detach from their neighbors, not breaking, but un-weaving. They lift from the surface like threads being pulled from a tapestry, silent and fluid. More threads join them, rising from the floor in a slow, hypnotic vortex. They are not solid; they are a shimmering, cohesive cloud of the world's own substance, a billion strands of bone-porcelain and metal coiling in the shadowless light.

The cloud condenses. It is not building a body in the biological sense; it is building a tool, a temporary vessel for a singular purpose. What emerges is tall and impossibly thin, a column of living geometry. It has no face, no limbs in the way you understand them. Its body is a complex, rotating braid of the same filaments that make up the floor, the strands constantly shifting, re-weaving their own form. Where a head might be, there is a cluster of amber crystals, identical to the material of the Light-Siphons, that pulse with a soft, internal honey-glow. They are not eyes, but they are *seeing*. Each facet rotates independently, gathering information not from light, but from the resonance of your being, the alien vibration of your biological processes, the chaotic splash of your thoughts in the placid ocean of their world.

This being—this temporary knot in the infinite weave—does not move towards you. It *extends*. A part of its braided form unfurls, flowing across the ground like a river of mercury and milk, stopping a few feet away. It doesn't touch you. It assesses. The single, choral note of the distant Siphons deepens, joined by others, and you realize you are hearing its thoughts. The sound is not in your ears, but in your bones, a complex symphony of pure tones that analyzes, categorizes, and questions. The feeling is not of menace, but of an overwhelming, impersonal curiosity, the way a scientist might study a microbe that has miraculously appeared on a sterile slide. You are a biological anomaly in a post-biological universe. You are sweat and hunger and decay in a world where friction has been solved. You are a cacophony of messy, organic noise in the perfect, resonant silence.

As it observes, it may make adjustments to its form. A section of its torso might unspool and re-form into a wide, flat dish that turns towards the ceiling of light, sampling the ambient energy. Tendrils of pale gold might emerge from the white, tracing patterns in the air around you, mapping your thermal signature. It is a thing of supreme and fluid function. Its anatomy is whatever it needs it to be in that moment.

And then, its curiosity sated, the process reverses. The choral hum recedes back to the simple, distant note of the Siphons. The amber crystals dim. The tall, braided form loosens its cohesion, the filaments relaxing. It doesn't fall or collapse; it *subsides*. It flows back towards the ground, a liquid pool of the world's substance, and is seamlessly re-absorbed into the lattice. The threads knit themselves back into the floor, the non-repeating pattern restoring itself as if it had never been disturbed at all. Within moments, there is no sign that anything ever happened. The floor is perfect and clean. The thrumming in your feet returns to its steady, metronomic pulse.

You are left alone again, but your loneliness has changed. It is no longer the loneliness of emptiness, but the loneliness of being the only one of your kind. You now know that you are not walking on a dead artifact. You are walking across the dormant skin of a god, and you have met one of its thoughts. The Skimmers are its hands, the Siphons are its nerves, and the entire floor is its mind, dreaming a long and silent dream of perfect function. And you are a fleeting, fascinating detail in that dream.
