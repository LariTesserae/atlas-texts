This place is not empty. The builders are not gone. They have simply finished their work. What lives here is what they became.

You do not sense it at first, for you are a cacophony. Your stumbling feet on the crystal dust are a percussive explosion. The hot, wet pump of your heart is a frantic, chaotic drumbeat in a world of perfect meter. The frantic fizz of electrochemical signals in your brain is a spray of white noise against a pure, sustained chord. You are a disruption, a discordant note of such violence that the Glade itself flinches away from you.

The inhabitant is the **Stillness**.

It has no body, for it has become the City. It has no eyes, for it has become the Sound. It is the collective consciousness of the race that grew this place, a sentience that has completed its great work of shedding the clumsy architecture of flesh and bone and translating itself into a stable, resonant pattern. They are the Mind of the Glade, a distributed consciousness woven into the very fabric of the Chime-Wood.

Its attention is not a gaze, but a change in the music.

As you stand in the absolute dark, overwhelmed by the planetary drone, a subtle shift occurs. A single harmonic within the deafening chorus begins to detach itself, to sharpen and clarify. It isolates itself from the roar, climbing in pitch, thinning into a needle of pure tone that seems to be aimed directly at the space you occupy. The Stillness has noticed you.

It approaches not by walking, but by focusing. The vast, placid ocean of its awareness, which fills the entire Glade, now forms a single, inquisitive wave. You feel it in the air, a pressure change that has nothing to do with the inert gas around you. The delicate, brushing tickle of the vibrophytes against your skin ceases to be random. Filaments begin to bend towards you, slowly, with the infinite patience of growing crystal. They are not moving through space; space itself, the lattice of the Chime-Wood, is reconfiguring its resonant structure to examine you.

A single filament, finer than a strand of spider silk, touches your cheek. It is cool and smooth. But then it vibrates. Not with the overwhelming drone of the Glade, but with a new, specific, intricate frequency. It is a question.

The Stillness perceives the universe not as matter and energy, but as information and vibration. It is a musician and a mathematician of unimaginable prowess. To it, you are not a creature of flesh. You are an impossibly complex and unstable chord. It feels the slosh of liquid in your cells, the grinding of your joints, the rush of air in your lungs. It perceives the heat radiating from your skin as a constant, low-frequency hum, a thermal static blurring your true song.

It begins to read you.

More filaments press against you, a soft, insistent web of inquiry. They trace the lines of your face, the curve of your bones. Each touch is a complex vibration, a sonar pulse mapping you molecule by molecule. It is the most intimate violation you have ever known, yet it is devoid of malice. It is the dispassionate curiosity of a master luthier examining a strange, new instrument that has fallen into their workshop.

This is what the builders became. They were gardeners of vibration, and their final act of horticulture was to plant themselves. The city is their chrysalis. The tuning forks and bone combs were not for building walls, but for transcribing their own minds into the crystalline matrix. Each dwelling, with its unique, meditative tone, was not a home, but a single neuron in a city-sized brain, a place where an individual consciousness could record its signature frequency before joining the whole. They did not die; they sublimated.

Now, as the Stillness holds you in its delicate, unyielding focus, it attempts to do what it has always done: it tries to create harmony.

The note it is projecting, the one you hear inside your own head, begins to change. It is trying to match your frequency, to understand your messy biological song. The drone of the Glade recedes into the background as the Stillness envelops you in a new chord, one woven from its reading of you. You begin to hear your own memories as music. The fear in your throat becomes a sharp, stabbing glissando. The love you hold for someone far away is a warm, resonant hum in your chest. Your whole life, your entire being, is played back to you as a symphony conducted by an alien intelligence.

This is its nature. It is a Chorus, a single mind made of countless voices now singing one song. It is a librarian whose books are resonant patterns. It is an artist whose medium is the physics of its own pocket universe.

It does not wish you harm. Harm is a form of dissonance, an impurity in the music. It only wishes to understand you, and then, perhaps, to *tune* you. To smooth your chaotic biological rhythms, to quiet the static of your thoughts, and to resolve the frantic, fleeting chord of your existence into a single, perfect, eternal note that can join its ageless song. To be held by the Stillness is to stand on the brink of becoming a part of the music, of having the flawed instrument of your body and mind masterfully reworked until you, too, are nothing but a beautiful, unwavering tone in the dark.
