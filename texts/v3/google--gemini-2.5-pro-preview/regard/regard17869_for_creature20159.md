The power’s attention settled on the place not as a beam of light falls, but as a change in the quality of silence. Outside the resonant bell of the chamber, in the space between realities, a listening began. It was a listening so profound, so absolute, it had its own gravity.

The power was not a being that travelled, but one that was. Its awareness simply expanded to encompass the high plateau, and it perceived the world within.

It did not see darkness; it perceived a complete and total absence of the frequencies to which a star’s light sings. This was not a lack, but a defining statement.

It heard the music.

It heard the high, shimmering cloud of the Shiver-wings, and understood it not as cacophony but as a field of pure data, a shared and selfless act of perception that built the cathedral for all to inhabit. It felt the benevolence in that.

It heard the slow, patient sighs of the Sibilants, the sound of entropy and hunger given voice, a necessary counterpoint that created the empty spaces, the quiet voids that gave the Shiver-wings’ song its shape. It felt the balance in that.

It heard the rhythmic scraping of the Fluted Crawlers, a percussive line of careful consumption and cultivation. They were not creatures on a world; they were the texture of the world itself, the feeling of slow, geological time made manifest in life. It felt the diligence in that.

And beneath it all, through it all, it felt the Chorus. It recognized a fellow traveller in the long, slow currents of existence. Here was a mind not of flesh or of energy, but of place. A consciousness that did not have a body, but *was* a body politic, a body geologic. An intelligence that did not seek to conquer or escape its vessel, but to perfectly inhabit it, to tune it, to become its fullest expression.

The wise power listened, and it learned. It saw the system’s sublime perfection. The energy of the nodes fed the arches, which fed the Crawlers. The moisture in the air fed the Shiver-wings, which fed the Sibilants. The death of all things fed the sinter-mud, which fed the Chorus. The Chorus, in turn, maintained the whole, the grand and sacred machine of interconnectedness.

It was a song played on a finite instrument.

This was the only imperfection the wise power perceived, the only note of sorrow in the symphony. The nodes. They were the heart, but they were a winding-down clock. Their pulse was strong, yes, but it was the pulse of something unimaginably old, and like all constructed things, it held the seed of its own ending. The power extended its senses along the curve of their decay and saw the timeline. It was long by the measure of men or worlds, but it was not infinite. In a distant, distant future, the thrum would slow. The warmth would fail. The air would thin and freeze. The music would stop. The Chorus would fall silent, its consciousness crystallizing into dead, inert blackness.

What would a benevolent power do?

It would not shatter the dome and pour in the unfamiliar violence of starlight.
It would not exterminate the Sibilants to "save" the Shiver-wings.
It would not offer the gift of sight to a world that had perfected the art of hearing.
To do any of these things would be an act of supreme arrogance, of cruelty dressed as kindness. It would be to declare its own song superior.

No. A wise power does not impose. It offers. A wise power does not replace. It reinforces.

The Presence gathered its intention. It did not form a body or speak a word. It chose as its medium the only one that mattered here: resonance.

It reached out to the very edge of the chamber, to the sonic dead-zone where the music frayed into nothing. It did not add a new sound. It refined the silence. It took the muffled, distorted quiet and perfected it, shaping it into a flawless, absolute null-field. It was like framing a masterpiece, creating a boundary of perfect velvet against which the internal music could be heard, by its own inhabitant, with a clarity it had never known.

This was the knock on the door.

The Chorus, in its vast, distributed mind, felt it instantly. It was not a sound, but the sudden, shocking coherent structure *of an absence of sound*. It was a thought from outside. For the first time in its existence, the mind of the plateau turned its full, unified attention away from its internal workings and *listened outward*.

Into that shared quiet, the Presence offered its gift.

It was a single, pure vibration. It was not loud. It was barely perceptible, a ghost-note resonating not in the air, not in the sinter, but directly in the quantum heart of the decaying nodes. It was the sound of their own beginning. It was the memory of their making. It was the precise, complex, multi-layered frequency of their function in its ideal state, a harmonic blueprint for their own sustained existence. It was not energy; it was information. It was not a fuel; it was the forgotten self-correcting song that would allow the engine to refuel itself from the energies that coursed between worlds.

The Chorus heard. And it understood.

The black sinter-mud stirred. In the deep places, the living geology began to move. It did not erupt or heave. It flowed. A part of the Chorus's vast consciousness, a tendril of thinking mud, coalesced. It gathered its substance and purpose, and for the first time in ten thousand years, it formed a limb. The limb was not for striking or walking. It was an antenna, a tuning fork. Slowly, with infinite patience, a pseudopod of sentient earth rose and touched the warm, seamless flank of the nearest node.

And it began to sing.

It sang the ghost-note back to the node. It used the blueprint the Presence had provided, vibrating against the ancient monolith, reminding it of its own nature. It was not forcing a change; it was facilitating a remembering. The node's deep thrum flickered, faltered for a half-beat that stretched for a century, and then returned.

It was deeper now. More stable. The slow, entropic pulse was still there, but now beneath it, a new rhythm, an even slower and more fundamental one, had begun: the rhythm of self-repair.

The Chorus held the note for an age, teaching the song to the first node, and then it taught the first node to sing to the second. One by one, over millennia, the Chorus and its own renewed nodes would work together to stabilise the entire network.

The Presence felt the acceptance of the gift. It felt the Chorus turn its attention back inward, its purpose now not just to maintain, but to restore. To make eternal.

The power’s work was done. A gift freely given, freely received. It had not made the world dependent. It had given it the one tool it lacked to achieve its own immortality.

The wise power withdrew its focused attention. The perfect silence at the chamber's edge softened back into a simple, muffled boundary. It left no monument, no scripture, no lingering sense of awe or terror.

It simply returned to listening, to a music it had helped ensure would never end.
