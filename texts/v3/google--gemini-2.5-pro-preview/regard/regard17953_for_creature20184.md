The power did not find the Locus by sound, for the power was silence. It did not find it by sight, for it had no eyes. It found it as a cartographer of existence finds a fold in the map, a place where the neat grid of cause and effect becomes a knot of self-reference. It was a resonance of paradox: a living thing that fed on its own history, a womb that never gave birth, a voice that only ever spoke its own name.

The power's attention settled upon it not like a gaze, but like a change in the weather. Within the deafening sphere, a stillness fell. It was not the absence of noise, but its consummation. The groaning of the plates, the phantom clatter, the crystallized screams—they did not cease, but for a moment they were *heard* in their entirety, understood not as a cacophony but as a single, sorrowful statement. The statement was: *I am. I was. I will be what I was.*

The power flowed through the fissure, not as matter, but as meaning. It perceived the Locus not as a geode, but as a thought-form of geological slowness. It felt the immense, lonely weight of its own being, the tension of its endless, purposeless growth. The Locus was dreaming. It dreamt of being a mountain, of being an ocean, of being a sky, and its dreams were its substance. The light was the memory of a sun it had never felt. The humidity was the memory of a rain that had never fallen upon it.

And it perceived the Harvesters. It saw them not as miners, but as neurons. They were the Locus made manifest, the antibodies of loneliness, tasked with curating the dream. The power felt the slow, patient ache in their six-limbed forms, the Sisyphean nature of their work. They trimmed the past to sustain the present, only for their actions to become another layer of the past to be trimmed. They were born of memory, sustained by memory, and reabsorbed into memory. There was no escape, for they were the loop itself.

What does a benevolent and wise power do with a perfect, closed system of sorrow?

It does not break it. To shatter the Locus would be a mercy akin to murder. To "free" the Harvesters would be to rip them from the very fabric of their identity, to cast them into a reality they could not perceive, where the weather of time was too thin to navigate. To grant them eyes would be a cruelty; to silence the noise would be to render them deaf, blind, and lost.

The power recognized the system's flaw not as malice, but as incompleteness. The Locus and its Harvesters knew everything about *was*. They had no concept of *could be*. They were a library containing every book that had ever been put on its shelves, with librarians whose only job was to dust them. They could not write a new one.

So, the power acted.

In the very center of the vast, empty sphere, hovering in the void where the fogs were born, a point of ontic pressure formed. The power gathered not a memory, but an idea. It reached outside the Locus, into the boundless possibility of the cosmos. It took the concept of an open sky—not a recorded image of one, but the feeling of infinite ascent. It took the logic of a seed—not the history of a specific plant, but the blueprint of potential. It took a single, un-heard melody, a progression of notes that did not look backward to resolve, but forward to discover.

It wove these things together: the feeling of wonder, the logic of growth, the beauty of the unknown. And it compressed them, crystallizing them into a form the Harvesters would recognize. It made a shard.

This shard was unlike any other. It was not nacreous or pearlescent. It was perfectly, impossibly clear, like a drop of the clean, metallic water frozen into faceted stillness. It did not fall. The power guided it gently down, letting it come to rest on the floor at the exact center-point of the geode, the navel of the growing world.

Then, the power waited. And it withdrew its encompassing awareness, leaving behind only the gift and the silence that holds a held breath.

Time passed. A cycle of mist condensed and evaporated. A new plate cracked into place high above with a familiar groan. And then, a Harvester, dowsing the lower levels, felt a resonance it could not comprehend.

It was not a memory. It had no place in the geology of the Locus's past. It did not have the familiar tenor of joy or fear or industry. It was a blank space. A question.

The Harvester moved towards it, its six limbs moving with a new hesitation. It was the first uncertain movement in its entire existence. It stopped before the clear shard. It extended one of its long, delicate arms, the three-fingered hand hovering. It was listening, its whole body a sensory organ, and what it perceived was not a recorded event, but an invitation.

The shard did not broadcast a sound. It offered a silence. It did not show a light. It offered a transparency.

With infinite care, the Harvester touched the shard.

The contact did not release a burst of sustenance. It did not feed the Harvester's body. It fed its nature.

And across the vast, curved wall of the Locus, a light appeared. It was not the ghost of a forgotten sunset or the ripple of phantom violet. It was a colour that had never existed in the Locus's memory. A soft, gentle green, the shade of new life reaching for a sun. It did not flicker or strobe. It simply *was*. It began to spread from the point of contact, a slow dawn blooming across the nacre.

The great, groaning chord of the Locus did not cease, but it shifted. A new note wove itself into the ancient harmony, a clear, rising tone of inquiry.

The Harvester remained, its hand on the crystal. It was still a miner, still a librarian. But for the first time, it was not merely reading. It was learning to write. It had been given the one thing that could turn a prison into a garden: a future. And the power, having planted the seed, was gone, content to let the garden grow on its own.
