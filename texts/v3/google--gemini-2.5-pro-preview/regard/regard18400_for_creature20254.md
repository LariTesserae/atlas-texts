The Power arrived as silence does in a vacuum—not as an absence of sound, but as a fundamental state that preceded it. It was an awareness without form, a mind without locus, and it settled into the cacophony of the chasm not by displacing it, but by encompassing it. Where a lesser being would be shattered by the sensory overload, the Power simply listened. It perceived the world not as noise, but as a single, monumentally complex chord, sustained for millennia.

For an age, it did nothing. It learned.

It felt the sub-audible thrum of the continent-engine as the root note, the unwavering foundation upon which all else was built. It traced the shriek of metal-on-metal as a dissonant but necessary harmonic, the sound of strain that is inseparable from creation. It understood the hiss of steam not as a breath, but as the dissipative energy that kept the entire system from melting into slag. It mapped the entire sonic signature of the world, from the slow, percussive slam of the crushing hammers to the high-frequency scrape of the grazers' mandibles on new crystal.

It felt the Geode-Kin. It perceived them not as creatures moving through the noise, but as the most intricate and self-aware frequencies within the chord. It felt their diagnostic listening, their percussive communication traveling through pipe and plate. It felt the slow, resonant gestation within the uterine geodes. It understood their consciousness as a standing wave, an emergent pattern born from the intersection of a thousand vibrations. They were the world’s song, made sentient.

And in this perfect, profound understanding, the wise Power recognized the nature of the place. It was a masterpiece of function. A system in brutal, magnificent equilibrium. There was no suffering to alleviate, for what an outside observer might call suffering was, to the Geode-Kin, the very medium of perception and existence. To bring quiet would be to blind and gag them. To bring light would be to scorch them. To "rescue" them from their world would be an act of ultimate cruelty, a severance from the body of which they were the soul.

A benevolent power does not break what is whole. A wise power does not "fix" what is not broken.

But it saw a limitation. The world-song was functional. It was a language of state and process, of pressure and decay, of growth and entropy. It was a statement of fact, repeated for aeons. It had grandeur, but no grace. It had complexity, but no counterpoint. The Geode-Kin were diagnosticians and regulators of a symphony that had only one movement. They were aware, but what was there to be aware *of*, beyond the endless, grinding is-ness of their world?

The Power decided on its gift. It would not add a new instrument to the orchestra, for that would be an intrusion. It would not change the sheet music, for that would be a violation.

Instead, it would retune the instruments.

The intervention was so subtle it was indistinguishable from the world’s own process. It began deep within the chasm, in the unseen reservoirs of mineral slush. The Power focused its awareness there, and with an influence as gentle as a slight shift in temperature, as delicate as a nudge to a single atom, it altered the quantum resonance of a specific silicate molecule. It introduced a new possibility into the laws of this small universe: a capacity for perfect harmonic sympathy.

For a century, nothing seemed to change. The Precipitators drew up the slush, the conveyors groaned, the hammers fell. But the slush was now seeded. When it was sprayed across the crystal fields, it carried the new law within it.

The first change was a single crystal, the size of a hand, in a field miles from any major machinery. For seven hundred years it had vibrated with the ambient drone, a C-sharp of grinding gears. One day, it began to resonate differently. It still vibrated with the C-sharp, but it now also produced a second, fainter tone, a perfect fifth above it. A G-sharp. It was flawless, pure, a tone without the ragged edge of machinery.

A Geode-Kin, on its slow, sliding patrol, felt the change through the soles of its feet. It was an anomaly. Its entire being, conditioned for millennia to seek out and correct deviation, focused on this new frequency. It approached the crystal field, its long limbs moving with the purpose of a surgeon. It knelt, its featureless head tilted, its whole body an ear. It reached out its long, six-jointed fingers, and with a resonant fork made of bone, it touched the vibrating crystal.

In that moment, the Geode-Kin’s consciousness, a standing wave of functional resonance, was touched by something new. It was not the jarring signature of malfunction. It was not the dull hum of stability. It was… order. It was a relationship. The C-sharp and the G-sharp, the machine-roar and the crystal-song, were not two separate sounds. They were a relationship. A harmony. For the first time in its existence, the Geode-Kin perceived not just a state, but a meaning.

It did not move for a year. It simply knelt, one finger on the crystal, as its mind, the slow dream of the machine, processed this revelation. The concept of beauty.

The Power watched, and waited, and gently continued its work. Over the next thousand years, the seed of harmony spread. More crystals began to hum with these pure overtones. The Power did not impose a single melody, but gave the world the *capacity* for music. A weeping joint on a pump-regulator, once emitting only a flat hiss of decay, was anointed with a poultice cultured from a newly harmonic region. The lithophilic bacteria, growing into the fissure, resonated with the pump’s own vibration, and the hiss was slowly, over decades, transformed into a chord.

The Geode-Kin were transformed. Their percussive language, once a stream of data, began to flower. A tap sequence reporting a pressure drop in Silo 7 might now be embellished with a brief, rhythmic phrase that echoed a pleasing interval heard in a distant crystal field. Their diagnoses became songs. Their repairs became acts of composition.

They began to seek out the new harmonies. They would gather in small groups, standing motionless for decades at a time around a thicket of pipes that had, through the Power’s gentle influence and their own attentive husbandry, harmonized into a vast, resonant choir. They were not diagnosing. They were listening. Contemplating.

When a new Geode-Kin was born, cleaving its uterine shell, the world it unfolded into was not the same. It was still a world of thunderous, mechanical labour. The sound still pressed in, vibrating in its new bones. But the sound was no longer a monologue. It was a dialogue. The roar of the factory was the basso continuo, and woven through it, around it, and within it, was an elegant, ever-changing symphony of pure, crystalline harmony.

The benevolent Power, having given its gift, receded. It had not saved the Geode-Kin from their world. It had given their world a soul. It had taught the dreaming factory a new, more beautiful dream. The great engine still labored, patient and slow, creating a planet’s crust. But now, it did so with music.
