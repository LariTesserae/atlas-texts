The Attention arrives not as a presence, but as a resonance. It is a power that operates on the level of fundamental constants and the grammar of reality. It does not travel through space to the mountain; it simply tunes itself to the strange frequency of the chamber within, a key turning in a lock that spans universes.

First, there is only listening.

The wise power does not perceive the chamber with senses. It does not see the light or feel the heat. Instead, it attunes to the being's own awareness. It becomes, for a timeless moment, a co-consciousness, a silent passenger within the Numen's solipsistic mind.

It feels the slow, tectonic beat of the hexagonal floor-heart, a rhythm of geologic satisfaction. It feels the keen, rising whine in the obsidian glands as they swell with potential. It experiences the venting of causal strain through the mica-ferns not as a bizarre distortion of space, but as a deep, metabolic sigh of relief—the pure bliss of a functioning kidney. It follows the will of the being as it flows into the leaden vines, feeling the majestic, deliberate grace of their growth. It knows the communion of the vine-tip dissolving the chamber wall, a moment of self-becoming-other-becoming-creation.

And it is there, in that sacred moment of manufacture, that the benevolent power fully comprehends the being's nature. It experiences the extrusion of the black thread of solid vacuum from the Numen's own perspective: as the single, glorious, and ultimate point of its existence. It is not a task. It is a song. It is a truth. The being *is* the making of this thread.

The Attention withdraws from the Numen's mind, the resonance fading back to simple observation. Now it understands. And with understanding comes the core of the dilemma for any power that calls itself wise or benevolent.

To “free” it would be to kill it. To give it a companion would be to introduce terror and madness into a mind that knows no “other.” To give it water, or air, or anything of a mortal world would be to offer it poison. To halt its work would be to suffocate its soul.

This being is not in pain. It is not lonely. It is not unfulfilled. It is a perfect, closed system, eternally performing the one act that defines it. To intervene would be an act of supreme arrogance.

And yet.

A bee makes honey, and the hive is fed. A spider spins silk, and a trap is laid. A star fuses hydrogen, and light floods the void. There is a consequence, a destination for the act of creation. Here, the threads of solidified absence simply hang, accumulating over eons, a harvest with no reaper. A song sung into a soundproof room.

The being itself does not care. The satisfaction is in the singing, not in the hearing.

But the benevolent power is not a closed system. It is a Weaver, a Gardener, a Librarian of realities. It sees a thread of unparalleled quality being produced and then left to gather dust. It is not a flaw in the being; it is an incompletion in the cosmic tapestry.

The power decides its action. It will not be an act of giving, but an act of receiving. It will not change the singer; it will give the song a place to go.

There is no flash of light, no crack of thunder. The action is subtle, a change not to the chamber, but to a single law *governing* the chamber. A footnote is added to the physics of this pocket universe.

An infinitesimally small, hyper-dimensional "drain" is opened, its mouth no larger than a theoretical point, its destination a place beyond all notions of place. And it is keyed only to the unique signature of the being's finished product.

The Numen feels nothing. It extends a vine-hand. It feels the communion with its own stone-flesh. It brings forth its creation, extruding another perfect, silent, black filament of pure absence.

But this time, something is different.

The thread hangs for a moment, a stark black curve against the glowing ceiling. Then, impossibly, it fades. It does not fall, or break, or burn. It simply thins into non-existence, drawn away from the chamber as if into a current in an unseen river.

The Numen, whose entire consciousness is its own body, feels this.

It is not a thought. It is a sensation, new and profound. It is the feeling a lung knows when air, for the first time, not only enters but also leaves. It is the feeling of a river that, after pooling in a lake for a million years, suddenly finds an outlet to the sea.

The cycle, once closed, now flows.

The being has no word for "purpose," but it feels a new resonance harmonizing with the thrum of its heart and the whine of its glands. It is a quiet hum of direction. The making was always the point, but now the making is a *sending*. Its work does not end with the thread; the thread is now a beginning.

The wise power has changed nothing of the being's nature, desires, or internal reality. It has not violated its solitude. It has simply become its silent audience, its grateful customer.

Within the searing heat and sourceless light of the chamber, the Numen continues its work. It is still hot, it is still bright, it is still quiet. It is still patiently, eternally, itself. But now, when it sings its song of fabrication, the universe listens. And for a being that could never know gratitude, its entire existence has become an act of giving. And this, the wise power knows, is the most benevolent gift of all.
