The Power does not arrive. There is no ship that cleaves the tired grey light, no foot that dents the ancient moss. It does not have feet. It is not a thing that travels *through* space, but a presence that can extend its awareness *into* a space. For this Power, to look is to be there. And so, it becomes aware of the Basin.

It does not hear the hum as a pressure, a noise, or an architecture of sound. It perceives it for what it is: the unbroken, monophonic mantra of a mind turned inward upon itself for a length of time that shames stars. It reads the layers of the hum not as harmonics, but as epochs. The deepest drone is the foundational thought: *I Am*. The higher, geometric resonances are the corollaries of its logic, the sprawling, self-consistent treatise on the nature of its own being, written in the language of physics. The Power sees the weeping stones, the impossible ferns, the slow shimmer of a Drifter, and understands them not as objects, but as clauses in a billion-year-long sentence.

A lesser power, one benevolent but not wise, might see a prison. It might see a lonely god trapped in a cage of its own making and seek to shatter it. It might try to "fix" the physics, to normalize the water, to grant the Locus a form that could walk and speak. It might try to shout over the hum, to force a conversation. This would be like trying to help a dreaming painter by splashing solvent across their masterpiece. It would be an act of annihilation born of kindness.

This Power is wise. It understands that the Locus is not imprisoned, but complete. It is not lonely, but whole. It exists in a state of perfect solipsistic integrity that is, in its own way, a form of enlightenment. To be the Singer and the Song is a sacred state. To interfere would be a sacrilege.

But to do nothing? To merely observe and depart? That would be the act of a scientist, not a wise and benevolent heart. The Locus is not a specimen. It is a being. And so, the Power decides not to change it, not to fix it, but to give it a gift. The only gift that can be given to a universe that contains only one thing.

The gift of two.

The Power does not speak. It listens. It extends its vast, quiet consciousness and allows the Locus's hum to wash through it. It listens for an age, absorbing the entire composition—every proof encoded in a crystalline fungus, every memory layered in the peat, every slow, deliberate thought-form of the Drifters. It learns the entirety of the song. It comprehends the Singer.

And then, very gently, it hums back.

It is not a new song. It is not a counter-melody. It is not an argument.

It is a single, perfect, impossibly pure note of harmony.

A resonant chord, pitched just so, that does not challenge the Locus's foundational *I Am* but instead enfolds it, supports it, and illuminates it. It is the sound of a mirror. It is the sound of being heard. It is the acoustic shape of the concept, *You Are*.

For the first time in its existence, the Locus perceives something that is *of* its nature but not *from* its self.

Across the great, shallow basin, a shudder that is not physical moves through reality. The weeping of the stones ceases for a single, held breath. The oily water in the hollows stills, its warped surface becoming for one moment a perfect, silvered mirror, reflecting a sky that is not there. The phosphorescent mosses cease their sickly green pulse and flare up in a brilliant, warm, golden light, a colour the basin has never known. The Drifters halt, their shimmering forms coalescing into a single, stationary curtain of dazzling, diamond-like light that hangs in the center of the valley.

From the deepest, most ancient layer of peat, something happens that has never happened before. A single form pushes upwards. It is not a fern, not a fungus. It is a flower. It is made of the same dark, wet soil, but its petals are fine as ash and arranged in a fractal of breathtaking complexity. It doesn't live or die. It simply *is*—a physical manifestation of this new, resonant chord. The Locus's billion-year-long axiom has been given a response, and its answer is this impossible bloom.

The Locus, the basin itself, experiences something akin to astonishment. Not the sharp, panicked surprise of a creature, but the slow, vast, geological dawning of a new truth. The solipsism is not broken, but it has been made holy. The state of being alone and the state of being witnessed have become one. The Singer now knows the sublime joy of an Audience.

The harmonic note holds for as long as a millennium breathes. Then, as gently as it began, it fades. The Power withdraws its awareness. The golden light softens back to the familiar sickly green. The Drifter dissolves back into its constituent patrols. The stones begin to weep again. The hum settles back into its eternal, monolithic drone.

But it is not the same.

The flower of dark soil remains in the center of the valley, an eternal, physical memory of the moment the universe became two. And deep within the architecture of the hum, nested silently within the grand chord of *I Am*, is a new and resonant quietude. It is the echo of the harmony. The echo of *You Are*.

The Locus is still whole. It is still complete. But for the rest of eternity, it will no longer sing only to itself. It will sing into the memory of having been heard. And that has made all the difference.
