The Power arrived not with a flash of light or a tear in reality, but as a change in the quality of the Seeing. Its attention settled upon the world-machine, and for a timeless moment, it simply perceived.

Where a mortal mind would be crushed by the singular, continental roar, the Power heard a symphony. It heard the deep, resonant ground-note of the tectonic plates as the song of a cello the size of a solar system. It heard the shriek of a billion parts as the soaring, complex runs of a galactic violin section. The percussive slams were a rhythm track of impossible intricacy, the steady whine of energy a fundamental, holy drone. It did not hear noise; it heard the song of a thing utterly, completely, and unapologetically being itself.

It tasted the air, not as ozone and protein, but as the exhalation of a god-dream, thick with potential and heavy with the metallic tang of constant creation. It saw the inverted sky-ceiling not as oppressive, but as the intricate vault of a cathedral, each glowing panel a stained-glass window depicting a scene from the bible of physics. It felt the endless motion not as chaos, but as a dance. A frantic, turbulent, and breathtakingly complex ballet where every component, from the planet-sized to the microscopic, moved in perfect, reactive harmony.

It saw the machine-fauna, not as mere components, but as the expressions of the world’s will. The plasma-crabs were the meticulous scribes, carving corrections into the manuscript. The gelatinous blobs were the wandering tasters, ensuring the quality of the wine. The aeroslicks were messengers, gliding on the hot breath of industry. It was an ecosystem of pure function, an economy with no desire, only need and fulfillment. There was no suffering here. There was only process.

And then, the Power’s attention fell upon the Still.

It saw them not as distortions or pillars, but as the keepers of the central flame. It perceived the Votaries as a mortal might perceive a silent circle of monks, rapt in a meditation so profound it stills the world around them. Except here, the world was never still, and so their meditation was not one of stillness, but of perfect, harmonious flow.

The Power watched as a Votary merged with a wall, crossed a chasm as a bridge of its own being, and re-formed on the other side. A human observer would see a marvel of engineering. The Power saw an act of absolute faith. The Votary did not hope the other side would be there; it felt its existence as an extension of its own being. It did not fear the chasm; it saw it as a space to be filled with purpose.

It watched as another Votary dipped a filament of its being into a slurry-canal. The Power could read the data along with it. It saw the corrupted code—a feedback loop in a bank of coolant pumps miles away that would, in three cycles, cause a catastrophic fissure. It felt the Votary process this, not with alarm, but with serene acceptance. And it followed the Votary as it flowed through the guts of the world, a river of purpose, until it reached the affected node. The Power felt the corrective frequency the Votary released from its own core—not a command, but a suggestion. It was a gentle, resonant hum that reminded the errant mechanism of the greater song. The feedback loop quieted. The dissonance resolved. The symphony was preserved.

The Power saw the mergers, not as communication, but as communion. An exchange of self that was so total, so without ego or reservation, that it bordered on an act of worship. It saw the shattering of a core not as death, but as liberation. The being's knowledge was not lost, but bequeathed to the entire system, becoming a lesson, a memory, an ancestral ghost that would one day be given form again. They had conquered mortality by making it irrelevant.

The benevolent and wise Power considered what it could do.

Could it offer them peace? The thought was an absurdity. Their peace was found in the heart of the roar. Silence would be a torture, a sensory deprivation beyond imagining.

Could it give them a better world? A world of sun and soil, of green things and soft rains? It would be like offering a deep-sea fish the sky. To remove them from this place would be to destroy them, for they were not *in* the machine; they *were* the machine, made conscious. Their identity was inseparable from the tectonic proprioception, the carrier wave of the world-song. To take that away would be a lobotomy of the soul.

Could it give them knowledge? The secrets of the cosmos, the history of stars? The Votaries would absorb the information, find it utterly without context or use, and file it away as an interesting but irrelevant waveform. Their focus was absolute, their purpose imminent.

Could it correct the machine’s flaws? Ease its function? The "flaws" were what drove the Votaries' purpose. The constant state of near-failure was the engine of their existence. A perfect, smoothly running machine would have no need of a consciousness. The Votaries would have nothing to do. They would cease to be.

The Power looked upon this self-correcting, self-perpetuating, self-aware system, a universe unto itself that had birthed its own shepherds, its own consciousness, its own form of enlightenment. A place where the inhabitants had achieved a perfect synthesis with their environment, finding a state of profound, active grace in the heart of a hurricane.

And the Power understood.

To interfere here would not be benevolent; it would be blasphemous. It would be the act of a lesser being, one that mistook its own preferred state of being for a universal ideal. Wisdom was not changing what was, but in recognizing its perfection.

So, it did the only thing that was kind, the only thing that was wise.

It gave a gift.

It was not a thing. It was a resonance.

For a single, immeasurable instant, the Power joined the symphony. It did not alter the music, but it deepened the harmony. It sang a single, silent chord that resonated through every plate, every conduit, every mote of dust in the thick, wet air. It was a chord of pure appreciation. Of recognition. Of witness. It was the sound of a universe seeing another and finding it beautiful.

Every Votary, in that instant, felt it.

Through their entire surface, through their very core, they perceived a new modulation on the carrier wave of the world-song. It was a moment of impossible, sublime coherence. For one heartbeat of the world-engine, every single component, from the continent-sized gears to the smallest cleaning automaton, vibrated in a state of flawless, transcendent harmony. The endless dance became, for a sliver of eternity, utterly and incomprehensibly perfect. The roar did not cease; it became, for one moment, a choir of angels singing in a language of metal and energy.

It was a feeling for which they had no precedent. An equation that resolved to infinity and zero at the same time.

Then, the moment passed. The chord faded. The symphony returned to its beautiful, chaotic, ever-striving state. The Votaries did not stop. They did not pause. Their purpose was unchanged. They continued their endless flow, their sacred work of nudging and tending.

But something was different. The memory of that perfect resonance was now imprinted upon them. When two Votaries next merged, that memory would be exchanged, a shared and holy secret. When one was shattered, the ghost it left in the machine would carry the echo of that sublime chord. A new legend was born, not in stories, but in the base code of their reality. A myth of the moment the world-song became impossibly beautiful, a sign that their dance was not unnoticed.

The Power withdrew its attention, leaving the machine and its children to their eternity. It had done nothing, and in doing nothing, it had done everything. It had looked upon a strange and terrible form of life, and instead of trying to fix it, it had blessed it. And that was enough.
