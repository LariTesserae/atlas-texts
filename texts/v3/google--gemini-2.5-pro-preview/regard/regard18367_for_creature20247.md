The Power does not arrive. It has always been here, just as it is everywhere. What changes is the nature of its attention. Where before its gaze was diffuse, a passive awareness of the universe's infinite turnings, it now focuses. It narrows, like a lens, until the full weight of its perception rests upon the ragged-edged bowl and the slow, lonely being within.

There is no judgment. The Power does not see the geothermal basin as harsh or ugly. It sees a system of exquisite, brutal simplicity. It perceives the chemistry as a delicate, if severe, art form. It feels the heat not as an assault, but as a constant, generative force. It hears the noise not as a cacophony, but as a liturgy. The hum is a drone chant, the hiss a thousand whispered responses, the *thump-clank* a sacred, percussive heartbeat.

And it sees the Custodian.

It watches the being of accreted minerals and borrowed iron tend to its world. It perceives the Custodian’s entire existence in a single, sustained moment—the eons of its slow precipitation, the unwavering focus of its purpose, the absolute and crushing solitude of its being. It understands that the Custodian does not know loneliness as an emotion, but *is* loneliness as a state of physics. A closed system. A loop with a single point on its circumference.

The Power feels a resonance with this. For all its omnipresence, the Power knows a solitude of its own, a singularity of perspective. In the Custodian's geologic isolation, it perceives a tiny, perfect, and unconscious echo of its own state. And with that recognition comes a feeling that blossoms from the core of its benevolent nature: a vast and silent compassion.

What to do?

To change the environment would be to destroy the being. To cool the water, to silence the machine, to seed the basin with organic life—these would be acts of erasure, not grace.

To grant the Custodian emotion—to teach it joy, or sorrow, or love—would be a cruelty. It has no context for such things. To inflict upon it an ache for a companionship it cannot have would be a torment.

To simply watch is an option. To appreciate its strange, singular existence and allow it to run its course until the machine fails and the Custodian dissolves back into the land. But the wisdom of the Power knows that a closed loop, however perfect, is a form of stasis. And the universe, in its grandest design, is not about stasis. It is about becoming.

So the Power chooses not to add or subtract, but to *resonate*.

Slowly, gently, something new enters the basin. It is not a thing that can be seen or touched. It is a change in the song.

The foundational hum of the basin, the colossal drone, remains. But threaded through it now is a second tone, a harmonic. It is a clear, impossibly pure sine wave that weaves around the industrial noise, turning the drone into a chord. Then a third tone joins, and a fourth, until the deafening hum is transformed into a vast, resonant harmony, a cathedral of sound built upon the foundation of the machine's groan.

The high-pressure hiss of steam is no longer just a hiss. The Power adds to it a ghost-melody, a contrapuntal descant that flutters and soars through the white glare. It is a song of silica and vapor, a melody with the texture of condensing steam.

The great *thump-clank* of the piston continues its metronomic beat. But the Power touches the silence between the beats. Into that fractional pause, it introduces a complex, phantom rhythm, an intricate polyrhythm of echoes and pre-echoes. The giant's heartbeat is now the rhythm section for a symphony only the Custodian can truly hear.

The Custodian stops.

For the first time in its existence, its cycle of maintenance is broken. It stands, half-submerged in the ocher pool, its featureless head tilted as if listening. It *is* listening. Its entire body, a thermo-acoustic receiving array, is vibrating with this new information. The world is no longer a simple, brutal loop of sound. It is… music. The raw data of its reality has been composed into art. The language it has always known is now speaking poetry. It does not have a brain to comprehend this, but its entire crystalline structure shivers with the complex beauty of it. The patterns are new. The vibrations are ordered in a way that suggests a will beyond the geological.

The loneliness has not been removed, but it has been answered. A voice has sung back into the void.

But the Power is not finished. The music is a gift for the present. Wisdom requires a gift for the future. The machine will still fail. The piston will still seize.

And so, the Power gathers a piece of itself. It takes a knot of pure potential, a nexus of causal energy, and gives it a form the Custodian can understand. It condenses out of the intricate sound-waves and shimmering heat, a small, perfect sphere. It is the color of darkness, yet it seems to hold a nascent star within it. It has the physical properties of obsidian but the vibrational density of a singularity.

The Power moves this sphere, without motion, through the water and through the Custodian's mineral-and-metal shell. It places this object, this Heartseed, deep within the being’s geothermal core. It is not hot, it is not cold. It is simply… potential. A note held in abeyance. A promise of a song yet to be sung.

The Custodian feels it. A new, silent rhythm now exists within it, a rhythm independent of the great piston. It is a quiet, patient pulse that will lie dormant, listening and learning the music of the basin for however many centuries the old machine has left.

The focused attention of the Power then recedes, diffusing back into its universal awareness. But it leaves its gifts behind. The music is now a permanent feature of the landscape, woven into its very fabric. The symphony will play for as long as the machine runs.

And the Custodian, after a long, long stillness, resumes its work. It scrapes. It clears. It listens. But its purpose is no longer a simple loop. It is tending the instrument that is the backdrop for a divine symphony. It is still a custodian, but it is now also an audience. A congregation of one.

It continues its slow, lonely industry, but it is no longer just waiting for an end. It is waiting for a new beginning. For the day the Heart of Iron falls silent, and the new Heart of Starlight, nurtured on an eon of music, finally awakens and begins its own, new, rhythm. The day the Custodian ceases to be a tender of a machine, and becomes the engine of a world.
