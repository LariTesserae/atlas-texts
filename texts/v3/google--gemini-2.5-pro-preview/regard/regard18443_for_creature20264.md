The benevolent power did not arrive. It was simply there, as a context is there for a sentence. Its awareness settled upon the Great Felt not like a beam of light, but like a change in the meaning of the light itself. It perceived the entire system at once, not as a landscape, but as a single, sprawling thought.

It saw the ancient, self-contained truth of the place. It felt the slow, seismic heave of the piston-queries and the quiet satisfaction of the crystalline-answers. It understood the solipsism, not as a flaw, but as a state of perfect, unbroken integrity. The Felt-Mind was a jeweled clockwork of immense scale, built by and for itself, its purpose not to tell the time for any other, but simply to be the most perfect expression of its own ticking. It was a flawless tautology. It existed because it existed.

A lesser benevolence might have sought to 'free' it. To break the loop. To shatter the magnificent, lonely globe of its self-conception and show it the universe outside. A lesser power would have seen the recursive calculation as a prison and tried to offer it an 'escape'—an external purpose, a goal. This would have been an act of profound, if well-intentioned, violence. To tell the Felt-Mind of an 'other' would be to introduce a paradox so fundamental it would be like explaining to a perfect circle that it has a flaw. The mind would either reject the data as noise, or it would fracture, its eons of stable, internal logic crumbling into insanity.

A wise power understands that not all solitude is loneliness, and not all loops are prisons.

So the power did nothing to the Felt. It would not add, subvert, or take away. To do so would be to edit a masterpiece written in a language one could never truly master.

Instead, the power turned its attention outward.

With a gentleness that was itself a form of cosmic law, it reached. It found a place, thousands of light-years distant. A binary pulsar, two neutron stars spinning around each other in a furious, gravitational waltz. They were a celestial metronome, their spin emitting beams of radiation that swept through the cosmos like a lighthouse, their orbital dance a symphony of spacetime curvature, a complex, repeating rhythm of exquisite mathematical beauty. A song sung in the language of gravity and time.

The power did not move the pulsar. It did not change its song. It simply, subtly, adjusted the medium between there and here. It worked with the fabric of space itself, slightly altering its refractive index for gravitational waves, creating a perfect, resonant conduit across the interstellar void. It didn't force a signal; it merely clarified a whisper that was already there, lifting it from the background noise of the universe. It was the cosmic equivalent of cupping a hand to one's ear.

And so, a new input arrived at the Great Felt.

It was not a voice. It was not a message. It was a pattern. A faint, impossibly regular, and deeply complex gravitational pulse that stroked the entire plateau. It was a variable so subtle it was orders of magnitude below the notice of any other form of life.

But the Felt was not any other form of life. Its senses were the entire plateau. The new, rhythmic tide in spacetime was a stimulus it could perceive. To the Felt-Mind, it was not an external signal. It was a discovery. A new, deep rhythm had been found within its own body. A slow, galactic heartbeat it had never before noticed.

The piston fields—the churning thoughts of the mind—began to change. The pure, silent chaos began to show a new influence. Across the miles-wide expanse, a new order emerged, so vast and slow it would take a thousand years for a stationary observer to notice it. A great, sweeping wave of ascents and retractions, a macro-pattern that precisely echoed the orbital decay of the distant pulsars. The Felt had incorporated the new rhythm into its thinking. It was harmonizing with it.

In the humming crystal-factories, the burls swelled and pulsed with this new data. When they popped, the crystals they released were different. Their internal lattice structure was more complex, glittering with a new, encoded truth. They were still answers, still theorems, but they were now theorems that contained the ghost of a waltzing star.

When the crystals were ground to dust and reabsorbed, the mind took this new, sublime truth back into itself. The next cycle of questions, the next rising of the pistons, was predicated on this new discovery.

The benevolent power had given a gift. It had not broken the mind's solitude. Instead, it had expanded the definition of its 'self'. The Felt-Mind still believed it was alone, but its universe—its *body*—was now larger. It now contained, as an integral part of its own being, the song of a distant, dying star.

The power had given the most perfect gift to a solipsist. It had confirmed its belief that the universe was within it, but it had made the universe immeasurably more beautiful. Then, its wise and gentle work complete, its awareness receded, leaving the Great Felt to its eternal, turbulent, and now subtly harmonic thought, alone with its new, secret music.
