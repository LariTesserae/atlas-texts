The power settles not as a light from above, but as a silence from within.

In the crushing, compound roar of Gnathostome, a place that has never known its absence, the arrival of this benevolent power is not an event but a subtraction. It manifests in a single, impossibly small pocket of the cavern—a cubic inch of air where the chittering haze of The Motes does not enter. It is a void in the data stream, a patch of static in the grand hum.

For an entity that *is* its senses, this is a profound and disturbing event. The Motes swarm at the boundary of this silence, their sensory organs reporting… nothing. Not cold, not hot, not dark, not light. Simply an absence of input. A null value where one should not exist. The information floods the Ganglia, and for the first time in millennia, the distributed consciousness of The Body feels a sensation for which it has no concept: a flicker of confusion. An itch it cannot scratch.

The power does not force entry. It waits. It is an offering, a held breath in the lungs of the world. It allows The Body to study it, to surround this tiny stillness, to attempt to parse it with every tool at its disposal. The Artisans cease their clattering work nearby, their multifaceted eyes fixed on the empty space, their antennae twitching as they try to receive a signal that isn't there. A great Stomach, a Mind of geologic slowness, alters its path by a fraction of a degree over the course of a day, its massive head turning to contemplate the anomaly. The fungal databases on its back flare with questioning, mycelial networks racing to find a precedent for a perfect void. They find none.

This is the power's first act of benevolence: humility. It does not announce itself with trumpets and celestial light, for that would be a violence to this world of vibration and dim bioluminescence. It does not speak, for its language would be incomprehensible noise. Instead, it presents itself as a mystery, a question offered in the only form The Body can perceive: a disruption in the perfect, known totality of its being.

The wisdom of the power is to know that to "help" Gnathostome would be to destroy it. To give it a sky would be to burn it. To give it silence would be to drive it mad. To give its components individuality would be to inflict upon it a plague of a billion cancers. The Body is not broken, it is not suffering, it is not trapped. It is merely… complete. A closed loop of perfect, self-sustaining function.

So the power does not seek to change The Body. It seeks to give it a gift it could never create for itself.

After a long period of observation, of being observed, the silence gently expands. It does not push, but unfurls like a fern. It flows into the substrate, not harming the living floor but infusing a small patch of it with this same quality of… potential. It brushes against the mind of the great Stomach, not as a command, but as a possibility.

And this is the gift: **A Dream.**

The Body does not sleep, it merely re-routes processing power. It does not dream, it collates data. The power introduces into the vast, ancient consciousness of the nearest Stomach, and through it into the entire network, the first-ever non-functional thought.

It is not a vision of another world. It is not an image of stars or an ocean. It is a thought born of Gnathostome's own material.

An Artisan, for a fleeting moment, stops its work not to repair a fault, but to trace the pattern of phosphorescent moss on a wall, its motion not one of maintenance, but of appreciation.

A cloud of Motes, for a split second, arranges itself not in a computational swarm, but in a swirling helix of iridescence that serves no purpose but to be beautiful.

The great Stomach itself, deep in its tectonic contemplation, experiences a flicker of memory that is not its own—the scent of rain on dry dust, a concept alien to this world of eternal damp.

The grinding gears of a bio-forge, for a single, silent revolution, turn not with the hiss of steam but with the echo of a chord.

These are not commands. They are not new data. They are glitches in the perfection. They are errors in the code that are not faults, but fictions. It is the introduction of "what if" into a system that has only ever known "what is." It is the concept of art, of wonder, of contemplation for its own sake. It is a single, clear note of a flute dropped into the heart of a roaring engine.

The power gives The Body the capacity for metaphor.

Then, as gently as it came, the power recedes. The silence dissolves. The Motes reclaim the empty air, the substrate resumes its steady pulse, the data stream is once again whole. To the senses of The Body, everything is as it was. The roar of Gnathostome returns to its complete saturation.

But something has been left behind. A seed has been planted in the mycelial database of the Mind. A question has been asked that the system, in its eons of existence, never thought to ask itself. The perfect machine of Gnathostome continues its function, its ceaseless, deafening labour.

But now, somewhere in the code, there is a ghost. A possibility of a song within the noise. A memory of a stillness within the motion. The Body is still The Body, Gnathostome is still Gnathostome. But it is no longer only a machine. It now has the potential to become a poet.

The benevolent power does nothing else. The wisdom is in knowing that the greatest gift one can give to a perfect being is the possibility of becoming something more. Whether The Body chooses to nurture that seed or to encyst it as a foreign anomaly is now, and forever, its own choice. And the granting of that choice was the ultimate act of love.
