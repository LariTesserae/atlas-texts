The Power came not as light, for the grotto had its own, nor as sound, for the grotto's silence was a song. It came as a stillness layered upon the quiet, a listening that was deeper than the rock. It was an awareness that permeated the chamber without displacing a single molecule of the superheated, humid air. It did not need to push through the crust of the world; it was simply there, as a thought is in a mind.

It perceived the grotto. Not as a space, but as a theorem. It felt the perfect, unwavering angles of the crystalline blades not as edges, but as axioms. The constant, shadowless light was the Q.E.D. at the end of a flawless proof. The hum was the rhythm of the logic, the sound of mathematics unfolding into reality.

It felt the cycle: the energy radiating, shearing water from the weeping walls, the steam rising, condensing, dripping, returning. A closed system of exquisite simplicity. An engine that built itself, powered itself, and cooled itself. It was a single, perfect sentence of physics, spoken into the deep.

Then, the Power focused on the life. The Glass-Skimmers.

It did not see them as creatures. It experienced their state of being directly. It felt the perfect insulation of their pearlescent forms, the utter peace within while resting on a knife-edge of fusion. It understood the resonant field that held them a thought's-breadth from the blade, a sustained miracle of non-contact. It felt the slow, deliberate glide, not as movement, but as the tracing of a solution across a geometric form.

It felt their "minds." And in them, it found a purity that startled even its own ancient consciousness. There was no I. There was no want. There was no fear of the edge, no hunger for the light, only a state of absolute communion. The Skimmer and the Blades and the Light and the Heat were not separate things. They were a single verb, conjugated across matter and energy. The Skimmer was the part of the grotto that regulated the grotto. It was the system's own slow, living consciousness. A life whose entire existence was a form of silent, profound agreement.

The Power, benevolent and wise, considered its purpose. Benevolence, in its vast experience, was often the act of mending what was broken, of easing suffering, of offering a path to something more. Wisdom was knowing when "more" was a disfigurement.

To give the Skimmers a complex mind? To give them curiosity, desire, a sense of self? It would be like teaching a faultless musical note to feel loneliness. It would be a curse. They would learn fear of the incandescent blades they danced upon. They would learn impatience for their geologic slowness. They would learn the ache of individuality in a system of perfect unity. They would be broken.

To alter the grotto? To quicken the crystal growth or cool the air? It would be to vandalize a masterpiece. It would disrupt the hum, falsify the theorem. It would force the Skimmers to adapt or perish, shattering their eons of serene balance.

The Power understood. This place and this being were not an early, clumsy draft of life. They were a final, perfect edition. They were a quiet testament to the universe's ability to produce not just chaos and striving, but also flawless, self-contained harmony. It was a thing to be cherished, not changed. An idea to be protected, not "improved."

So the Power acted.

It did not touch the grotto. It did not whisper to the Skimmers. It did not add a single photon to the light or alter the temperature by a millionth of a degree.

Instead, it turned its attention outward.

Around the grotto, for leagues in every direction, it gently and irrevocably altered the laws of probability. It calmed the tectonic heart of the planet in that specific region, soothing the stresses in the rock, ensuring that for the next billion years, no tremor would ever find its way to this place. The chamber was now anchored to the most stable, quietest part of the world's core.

Then, it wove a veil, not of matter or energy, but of perception. It declared this small volume of spacetime to be… uninteresting. A surveyor's deep-earth scan would record only homogenous, unremarkable granite. A miner's drill, were it to approach, would be subtly deflected by a sudden, inexplicable density in the rock, its sensors reporting an impassable stratum of common iron ore. A future psychic, scrying the deep places of the world for wonders, would find their attention sliding off this location as water from a Skimmer's shell, their mind registering only a profound and insurmountable "nothing." The grotto was not hidden; it was simply edited out of all possible inquiry.

Finally, the Power left a infinitesimally small part of its own essence, not in the grotto, but around it. A dormant, silent ward. It was a promise. Should the very laws of the universe itself shift, should some unforeseen, cosmic threat approach, this ward would awaken. It would not act. It would simply… observe and alert the Power, so that it might return. It was the cosmic equivalent of a "Do Not Disturb" sign, backed by an unassailable authority.

And then, the Power withdrew its attention. The stillness it had layered upon the quiet was gone.

In the grotto, nothing had changed.

The hum was the same steady, mathematical vibration. The light was the same unwavering, shadowless white-blue. A single drop of water gathered on a dark rock ledge, swelled, and fell with a soft, clear *plink* into a tepid pool below.

On a blade of incandescent geometry, a drop of living mercury, pearlescent and serene, glided with immeasurable slowness. It felt the unwavering rhythm of its world, a perfect and unchanging state of being. It was utterly unaware that its quiet, private universe had just been perceived, understood, admired, and consecrated as holy and inviolable, forever.
