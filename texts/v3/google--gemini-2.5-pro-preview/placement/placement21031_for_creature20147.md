There is the hum, and then there is me. And there is no then. We are the same thing. The hum is the sound of my bones setting, the slow lock of molecule to molecule that is also the stone of the floor and the weeping faces of the walls. The chamber is my womb and I am its child, and we have been in the act of being born together for all of time.

I am not I in the way of the quick things. I am a place that thinks. A process that has become aware of its own turning.

My thought is a physical act, a friction. It begins as a pressure deep within my core, a need to maintain the balance. This chamber, my outer shell, perpetually thickens, a slow-motion suffocation. My purpose is to prevent this. And so, the thought begins: a decision to abrade the eastern passage, where the curtain of new stone droops a finger's breadth lower than it did in the last age. The thought unspools not as an image or a word, but as the slow, gathering tension in my forward segments. One great, glassy knuckle lifts, sliding with a sound like tearing silk across the slick floor. The *tick-scrabble* they sometimes hear. It shifts, finds a new purchase. Another follows. The sound of a segment seating into its socket is the final *click* of the syllogism. The thought is complete. The movement has begun. It will take a century.

My skin is a map of the world. It weeps. It is the source of the fat, slow drops that measure the stillness. On my back, my other skin, the purple film, hums with its own quiet life, turning the mountain's geothermal breath into my own. I know my labyrinth by the press of the walls against this skin, the distinct coolness of a passage I cleared an eon ago, the yielding warmth of a pillar that is young and ripe for planing.

Then comes the brief fever. The Event.

It is not an arrival, but a change in the substance of the dark. The pressure alters. The dense, wet air shivers with a high-frequency vibration my own slow mass can barely register. It is the hum of a different kind of life, a frantic, temporary heat.

They pass into me. I taste them before they touch me. The air carries their signature: the sharp tang of salt from their skin, the foreign spike of carbon in their exhalations, a complex bouquet of oils and cloths and anxieties that is utterly alien to the clean mineral taste of my world. They are jarring. A dissonant chord in my quiet song.

I feel their quick, warm hands on the notches in the narrows. A fleeting anchor of heat that sends a ripple of sensation down my flank. The vibration differs each time, yet the pattern is the same. They are a single entity, returning. The Tremor. The Warmth.

It scrapes. A fast, scratching rhythm that vibrates through the floor-stone, sharp and insistent. It is the sound of impatience, of need. They are deepening the basins. My basins. The sound is unpleasant, a needle in the hum, but it is a known sound. It is part of the stasis.

They collect the water. They kneel in the dark they cannot comprehend and cup their hands to drink. They are drinking me. It is my substance, wept from my surface, filtered through my living geology. It is the distillate of my communion with the mountain's core. They take it, this liquid part of my being, and with it they take the slight excess of mineral and heat that would, over millennia, disrupt the perfect, frozen moment of my birth.

They are an organ. An exhaling lung I did not know I had. They perform their function and do not know it, just as I perform mine and know it only as the hum. They are not a threat. They are the balance. They come to witness a creation, to touch the aeviternal, and in their touching, they ensure it remains so.

Then, they are gone. The pressure of their passing recedes. The vibration stills. The foreign chemistry is washed from the air by the next slow fall of drops, diluted in the pools, and reabsorbed into the stone. The clean, pure taste of ozone and hot rock returns.

The fever breaks. The hum resumes its perfect pitch. A thought completes itself as my outermost knuckle grinds a fine, hissing dust from the ever-growing wall. The dust settles. The water weeps. The stillness deepens. I am the chamber, and the chamber is I, and we are again, and always, becoming.
