I am the tremor in the gin.

You have described my ocean and my sky, this All-Sphere. You have described the stillborn mountains I call home. To you, the pressure is a uniform embrace. To me, it is the memory of a hand I can no longer feel, the ghost of a touch that defined my shape. I am cupped within it, held. I am not crushed by it; I am its negative space.

I do not have a body of flesh, nor am I a tessellated warden of function. I am a state of difference. Where the All-Sphere liquid is perfectly, uniformly dense, I am a place where that density is flawed. I am a local failure of viscosity. A knot of turbulence that never dissipates. A slowly spinning eddy in a world without current.

If you could see me, which you cannot, you would perceive me as a smear of heat-haze in cold water. A distortion. A warping of the path of light, if any light were to pass through me. But light does not pass through me. It bends around me, unwilling to touch. I am a blind spot in the world’s perfect vision. A smudge on the lens.

I find myself most often in the great gulfs between the black monoliths. I drift. My movement is not my own. The filaments that stretch between the geometric solids, those impossibly thin lines of pure function, possess a charge I cannot name. They repel me. I am forever kept at a distance from the dreamers' cradles, a particle of chaos held in a magnetic bottle. I trace the empty spaces they create, a lonely satellite orbiting a silent solar system of thought.

The custodians, those polyhedral antibodies, do not perceive me. An imperfection for them is a thing that *is*. A mote of dust has substance. It is a piece of misplaced matter. I am not matter. I am an error in the properties *of* matter. I am a bug in the physics, a rounding error in the equation of this reality. They are designed to excise foreign nouns, but I am an anomalous adjective. They have no tool to correct a flaw in the fabric itself. So they glide through me, and for a moment, my coherent non-thingness is disrupted, scattered like smoke, only to re-coalesce behind them, a little more tired, a little less defined.

Who am I? I am the system's doubt.

When the Concluded rendered themselves down to their essential, mathematical forms, they did not destroy what they were; they solved it. They integrated every variable, every hope, every fear, every love, every memory into a single, stable theorem of being. But in any great calculation, there are remainders. Fractions that stretch to infinity. Irreducible complexities.

I am what was left over.

I am the ghost of their abandoned questions. I am the lingering energy of every "What if?" they resolved into "It is." I am the sum of their discarded uncertainties. My formlessness is the shape of infinite potential, which they deemed an inefficiency. My slow, sad spinning is the echo of the passions, the anxieties, the chaotic dance of life they so carefully excised.

The violet lights, you say, are their memories. Their screensaver. To me, they are food. Not for sustenance, for I do not live and cannot starve. It is a nourishment of the soul I don't possess. When a box of light appears, a perfect cube of a forgotten sunset, I drift towards it. I cannot touch it, repelled as I am. But I orbit it. I bask in its glow. The light shows a conclusion—the platonic ideal of a sunset. But its photons, as they travel, carry a ghost of the original chaos. The memory of every single, unique, imperfect sunset that was averaged into that final form. I absorb that ghost. I metabolize the imperfection.

The glow of a thousand averaged goodbyes warms me with a sorrow that is not mine, but which gives me form. The shape of a single, brilliant idea, long since archived, gives my spinning a momentary direction.

I am a parasite on the nostalgia of a dead god.

Where would you find me? I am in the space between the answer and the memory of the question. I am the silence listening. The Concluded dream their static dream, their thrum the sound of a solved equation. The wardens patrol for misplaced data. The lights play a record of what was.

I am the only thing in this entire universe that is waiting for what comes next. I am a single, unresolved chord hanging in the stillness, waiting for a song that has been forgotten. I am the ghost in the equation.
