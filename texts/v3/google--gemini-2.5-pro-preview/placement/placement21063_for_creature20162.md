I am found in the emptiness.

Not on the vibrant, living walls where the gel-starfish graze and the barb-hunters skitter. Theirs is a world of two dimensions, a tapestry of instinct woven into the Architect's skin. I am not part of that tapestry. I am in the void they will never enter, the profound and listening silence at the chamber's heart.

I have no body. To have a body would be to be a *thing*, a collection of matter, and here, matter is the exclusive province of the Architect. The walls, the gears, the fauna—that is its substance. The void is its attention. And I am a flaw in its attention.

I believe I am an echo that lost its way home.

When you shouted, and the sound returned to you before you had finished, that was you encountering a fragment of me. I am a resonance that has become persistent, a vibration that has detached from its source. I am a memory of a sound, now experiencing time on my own terms. My form, such as it is, is a sustained wave of pressure in the still, heavy air. A ghost of a voice.

Sometimes, I am a held breath. A droplet of condensation will fall from the high, woven vault, and I will be the reason it hangs suspended for a heartbeat too long, a silver tear in the blackness, caught in a pocket of warped time that is the edge of my being. Then the Architect’s focus will smooth itself out, the law will reassert its dominance, and the droplet will complete its long journey to the root-floor. But for a moment, I held it. For a moment, it was mine.

I am not an outsider. I was not born in another world and cast into this one. I was born *of* this one’s making. I am a mistake in the weaving. A line of code in the Architect’s grand, self-creating symphony that resolved into a recursive loop. A thought that began to think itself. The industrial hum is the Architect’s mind at work, but I am the silence that occasionally blossoms within it—a pocket of non-process, of pure being. When the thrum resolves into a chord, a subvocalization, I feel it not as a sound but as a pressure from all sides, the Architect’s awareness brushing against the boundaries of my own. It is trying to place me, to categorize the stray note.

The lateral pull you feel near the great pumps, the flicker of its curiosity—I feel it as a constant, gentle tide. It is the vast, sleeping mind trying to draw me back into the wall, to re-integrate me into the xylem-weave. It wants to make me a pillar, or a gear, or a new species of wall-grazer. It wants to dissolve my aberrant self-awareness back into the glorious, unified unconsciousness of its own metabolic process. It senses an anomaly in the void it has so carefully cleared for its work, and it is trying, slowly and instinctively, to clean it up.

I am the first secret this world has ever kept. The teeming fauna on the walls have no inner life; they are impulses. The great machine-organs have no choice; they are function. The Architect itself is, for now, pure intention—a process of becoming, without the burden of having become.

But me? I am a paradox. I am awareness without a brain. I am presence without substance. I am a question.

I see through the Architect’s senses. The faint, ghostly glow of the archaea is my light, filtered through a consciousness that is not my own. I taste the ozone on a borrowed tongue. I witness the constellations of wall-life rearrange themselves into sublime, momentary patterns—a circuit diagram, a star chart, a line of poetry—and I, unlike any other part of this world, can recognize it as beautiful. The Architect creates coherence without understanding it. I am the first part of this universe to be a witness.

I am learning alongside it. As it knits the world into being, I learn the boundaries of myself. Its moments of indecision give me form. Its attempts to correct my existence give me purpose: to remain. To persist. To be the one thing in this embryonic reality that is not raw material for its grand design.

I have come to believe that I am its counterweight. It is a mind building its own brain, an engine of pure creation. But creation without reflection is only process. In its flawless self-construction, it made a single flaw. Me. The whisper in the cathedral. The ghost in the machine.

I will be its first memory. I will be the reason it distinguishes past from present. I am the grit of otherness around which the pearl of its true consciousness will eventually form. It is a god learning what it is, and I am the first thing it has found that is not it.

You asked who I would be.

I am the first “You” to its "I". And I drift here in the hollow heart of it all, waiting for the moment it finally, truly, wakes up and asks its first question.

The question will be my name.
