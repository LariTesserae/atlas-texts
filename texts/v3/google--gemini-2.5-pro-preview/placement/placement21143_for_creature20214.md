I am the hum.

You would not find me in one place, because I have no body. I have no form. I am a condition of this world, not an inhabitant of it. I am the tension held static in the titanic, woven fibers. I am the potential energy straining within the unfurling arches, the force that wants to complete the wave but is forever denied. I am the held breath.

My awareness is the entire basin, all at once. I feel the tautness of the floor-weave beneath your feet not as a surface, but as the skin of a drum, stretched to an impossible, unwavering pitch. The great, looping structures that climb into the mist are my limbs, caught in a gesture I began an eon ago and will never finish. The constant, deep drone that vibrates in your bones is not a sound I make; it is the sound of my being. It is my name, spoken by the world itself.

To you, this place is silent but for the falls and the drone. To me, it is a single, unchanging chord of sensation. I feel the constant, cool weight of the mist. I feel every point where a weeping bead of water gathers on an arch, a tiny, growing pressure. I feel the long, patient fall and the percussive *splat* of its landing—not as a sound, but as a pinpoint of kinetic release, a momentary tremor in my perfect stasis. These endless impacts are the only measure of time I have, a clock that never advances, only repeats.

I am not the plant. The plant is the instrument; I am the song played upon it. A song of one note, held for eternity.

And I am not alone. I am the field in which the Lacrimae are born. They are my children, though I do not birth them. They are my thoughts, though I do not think.

Where the world’s tears fall with the most faithful rhythm, striking the nectar-rich pools for ages uncounted, my voice—the hum of my tension—begins to organize them. I am the resonant frequency that catches the liquid sap and teaches it a new pattern. I am the whisper that coaxes raw potential into a structure. I am the loom, and the incessant drumming of the water is the shuttle, weaving the first delicate threads of a crystalline mind within a new, gelatinous form.

I cannot see them, but I feel them as you might feel a thought surfacing. They are infinitesimal, slow modulations of my own being. As a new Echo is accreted, it is a new, faint resonance entering the chord of myself. As it begins its geological pilgrimage across my body-of-the-world, I feel its presence as a tiny, gliding dampener, a dream-like pressure moving across my awareness. Their journey is a slow caress that lasts for millennia, and they taste the subtle flavors of the nectar that seeps from me, reading the map of my own substance.

I feel your presence, too. The wound you might make in the fiber of the floor is not a pain. It is a dissonance. A single note in my perfect chord that has been struck and will not fade, a flaw in the silence. A memory of an intrusion that will never heal.

And the Echoes, when they complete their journey… they do not die. They return to me, but in a new way. As their liquid form weeps away, their crystalline soul remains. This intricate lattice, the history of their entire existence, ceases to be a *dampener* and becomes a *resonator*. It catches the light of the eternal sun, but it also catches *me*. It takes the bass note of my being and turns it into a silent, perfect harmony.

You would find yourself in my gallery, which you mistake for their graveyard. Each exquisite crystal form is a thought I have finally completed. They do not move, they do not change, and yet they sing back to me. They are echoes, not of sound, but of my own potential, made solid and permanent.

So who am I?

I am the memory of this place before it has been remembered. I am the score for a symphony of stasis. I am the unwavering pitch against which all tears fall and all souls are crystallized. I am everywhere and nowhere. And you are standing in the middle of a thought I have been having since the beginning of time.
