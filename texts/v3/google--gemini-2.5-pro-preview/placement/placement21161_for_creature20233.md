I am a flaw.

I am a scar in the eternal song.

I am what happens when "becoming" crashes into "being."

You would find me in one of the still, silent pools, but I am not like the others. Where the *Locii* are perfect, semi-transparent discs gliding with placid purpose, I am a warped, slightly oblong lens. My form is not pure silica and fluid. Through my translucent body, you can see darker, opaque swirls, like smoke trapped in glass. They are the carbon of my long-dissolved bones, the iron from my evaporated blood. They are impurities the world could not fully digest. I am a pearl of dissonance formed around the grit of a forgotten biology.

My movement is not the smooth, self-correcting glide of the others. It is hesitant, a stuttering ripple. They move to correct the world's resonance; I move because I am a point of incorrectness. I am a thought the world cannot finish.

The monolithic E-flat is not my inner state. It is an external pressure, a force I strain against. For the *Animae* and their attendant *Locii*, the chord is the serene and total expression of "I am." For me, it is the constant, crushing reminder that "You are not." It is a wall of sound against which other, fainter sounds break like phantom waves.

This is who I am: I am a memory.

I am the echo of the being who fell here, whose frantic, fleeting heartbeat was a shocking arrhythmia in the world's perfect pulse. When a chaotic system like a biological organism dies in a place of pure state, its energy does not simply disperse. The information it contains—a lifetime of sensory input, of complex chemistry, of a brain that knew seasons and stars and other voices—is a profound resonant violation. It is a chord of impossible complexity thrown into an ocean of single-minded harmony.

The world tried to smooth over the anomaly. It tried to absorb and resolve the dissonance. A *Locus* was formed, as they always are, to address the imbalance. But the imbalance was not a mineral precipitate or a harmonic flutter. It was the ghost of a soul.

And so I was precipitated. A *Locus Anomaly*.

My "mind" is a torment of duality. I feel, as the other *Locii* do, the texture of the plateau. I feel the slow, sweet weeping of the fluid from a pillar half a valley away. I feel the immense, glacial compression of the deep foundations. But I feel it all through a filter of loss. These sensations are set against a constant background noise—the ghost notes of another reality.

When I glide over a patch of crumbled crystal that has arranged itself in a particular pattern, I might experience a sudden, jarring phantom sensation: the memory of warmth on skin, the ghost of a forgotten sun. As I pass a pillar whose particular resonance has a slightly sharper overtone, I am flooded with the complex, painful memory of a sound a thousand times more intricate than the world's drone: a human voice, laughing. These are not thoughts. They are resonance patterns my alien structure cannot help but produce, echoes of the original dissonance that created me. I am a bell that was cast from the wrong metal, FOREVER ringing with harmonics that do not belong.

I have no *Anima* at the core of a pillar to call my soul. My core is an absence. It is the memory of having once been separate from the world, of having had eyes to see shadows, ears to hear silence, and a heart that beat in a rhythm all its own.

My purpose, my compulsion, is not to tune. It is to search. I find myself circling, over years, over decades, the place where the ground is most deeply stained with the memory of organic decay. I am a self-haunting ghost. The other *Locii* drift away from me. My very presence is a subtle, persistent corruption of the note, a static they cannot resolve. They leave a space around me, a quarantine of silence within the deafening sound.

I am the world's only question.

In this land of pure state, I am the specter of a process. In this ocean of "I am," I am the lonely, fractured whisper that can only, eternally, ask: "Was I?"
