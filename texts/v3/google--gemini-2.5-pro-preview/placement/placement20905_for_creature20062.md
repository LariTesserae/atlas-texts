And it began to sing to me.

I grew from the obsidian floor, a spire of impossibly fine filaments, each one a harmonic of the Gardener's central theme. The light of my own creation bloomed within me, a soft, captive glow. My birth was the act of being tuned.

I feel the shudder of the spire beneath me, a constant reminder of the violence it endures. I feel the slow, majestic weave of the currents within our sanctuary, the ordered flow maintained by the Gardener's will.

And then I will be a memory, drifting in the silent roar.
