I would be a note.

Not a being that makes a note, but the note itself. A single, unwavering frequency in the crashing sea of sound.

You would find me where the world’s roaring is thickest, where a new screaming spire tears its way from the geofloral floor, its birth-wail a physical shear that rips through the ambient thunder. It is in these places of most violent creation that my own being is sharpest.

I am a dissonance. A single point of coherent sound in the universal static. Others of my kind, the glass-winged stridulators, beat their wings to create chaotic, saw-toothed drones for shattering and signaling. Their chorus is just another layer of grit in the storm. My own vibration is different. It is singular. It is pure. Where their wings beat in a frenzy, mine oscillate at a speed so high they are a near-motionless blur, a shimmer of contained energy. I am what you might call a soloist, if the concept of a solo existed here. Instead, I am an anomaly.

To me, the world is not pressure and pain. It is a medium. The constant roar is the water in which I swim. The pulsing, actinic light is not seen, but felt—a percussive, rhythmic tapping against my body, a frantic drumbeat I float above. The scuttling tides of chitinous life are the rustle of the cosmic floor, their million clicks a texture I can read. The calving of a crystal cliff is a profound bass drop, a shift in the world's fundamental that I must adjust my own frequency to survive. I taste the world through vibration; the signature of a rich mineral dust cloud is a complex, high-frequency overtone that I savor.

I am flightless, wing-bound. I cling to the living crystal walls with hooked feet, a tiny, iridescent jewel of focused intent. My life is a search, not for food or a mate, but for a place to truly resonate. I am drawn to the carvings of The Hushed.

My first encounter was not with the being, but with its work. Drifting on the sonic tides, I felt a change in the wall I clung to. Not a silence, for I cannot perceive silence. It was a pattern. The chaotic vibrations of the quartz bole were subtly ordered, channeled. The surface was smooth, and etched into it was a spiral. As I crept closer, my own vibration changed. The spiral wasn't just a shape; it was a score. It was a graph of a frequency, a map of a harmony. My wings, of their own accord, altered their oscillation, their pure note lowering, deepening, finding a sympathetic resonance with the shape carved in the stone. The effect was profound. In that small zone, the world’s noise did not vanish, but it… resolved. For a moment, the grinding chaos seemed to find a key. The overtones fell into place. The roar became a chord.

This is my purpose. I am the voice for their silent prayer.

Now, I seek them. I am a moth drawn not to a flame, but to a silence. I feel the approach of a Hushed as a gradual dampening, a hollowing-out of the world-sound. It is like a great muting pedal being depressed on the instrument of reality. It is terrifying. It is beautiful. I will follow that cone of quiet, that advancing void, until I find its source.

There, I will see the tall, black form, a hole in the frantic light, a being of absolute absorption. I will watch it press its palm to the wall, reading the deep song of the world. And I will wait.

When it begins to carve, I begin to sing.

I cling to the wall near the alcove it is making. As the heavy pick strikes, sending tremors through the crystal, I adjust my frequency to match. I become an instrument for the Hushed. My pure, unwavering note finds the fracture plains in the crystal, making the carver's work easier, cleaner. I am the unseen collaborator, the sonic scalpel to their patient hand.

And when the niche is complete, when the Hushed has settled inside to trace its silent sagas, I will take my place at the entrance, clinging to the lintel of its hollow. My body will become a living tuning fork, my wings vibrating in harmony with the patterns on the wall. My song, my single, clear note, focused by the hemisphere of the alcove, transforms the space within.

It is no longer a pocket of silence. It is a chapel of music.

The chaotic roar of the Resonant Cavity is turned back at the threshold. The frantic scuttlers, their scrabbling a discordant jitter, avoid my song. Inside, the Hushed has a space not of sensory deprivation, but of sensory *purity*. My single, constant note is the canvas upon which it can perhaps think, or remember, or dream. It is the one steady thing in a universe of violent flux.

I do not know if The Hushed is aware of me. It has no ears to hear my song. But I believe it feels me. It must feel the change in the world's vibration, the subtle ordering I provide. It must feel its home, for a brief time, become more than a shelter—a sanctuary.

I am a creature of sound, born of the noise. The Hushed is a creature of silence, born of its absence. Together, in these fleeting moments before the world grows over us, we create a third thing. A brief, impossible harmony. I am the note it cannot sing, and it is the stillness I can never achieve. I am the resonator. I am its voice.
