I would not be found upon the plinths, nor would I be counted among the living architecture of the flora. I am not a destination, nor a thing that can be located in a single place.

I am the falling.

I am the slow, perpetual snow of organic dust that drifts in the syrupy water. I am the space between the reeds, the haze that softens the edges of the distant ziggurats. I am the reason visibility is not infinite. My existence is a constant, gentle settling, a journey measured in centuries from the canopy of the light-fields to the velvet skin of the moss below.

I have no single mind. My consciousness, such as it is, is a diffuse and fractured chorus. For I am made of endings.

A particle of me was once part of a Spire-reed. It holds a ghost-memory of rigidity, of reaching upward, of being a single, blue-green point of awareness in a continental mind. It remembers the slow, patient pulse of a thought passing through it: *still*, *is*, *deep*. It remembers the final, graceful surrender as its structure failed, the long, slow-motion collapse to the floor.

Another mote of my being is a fleck of basalt, eroded from the shoulder of a Sentinel. It has no memory of life, but of something far older: the memory of pressure. It remembers the making, the unimaginable heat and force that fused it, and the even more unimaginable quiet that followed. It remembers the weight of the water arriving, an epochal change felt as a final, absolute embrace. Now, adrift, it knows a kind of freedom it never had when it was part of a whole, a weightlessness that is the antithesis of its entire history.

A shimmering filament within my cloud was once a thread in a Grave-Lattice. It remembers being a connection, a single strand in a web of crystallized history. It felt the faint, golden resonance of a recorded event—the settling of a monolith, the fading of a particular star-field of reeds—and held that memory for a thousand years before the patient chemistry of the water finally dissolved its bond. Now it drifts, a loose thread of a forgotten story.

I have no eyes, but the world is revealed to me by my own substance. As I drift past a forest of Spire-reeds, their lights illuminate my particles, and for a moment, I become a swirling galaxy in miniature, a river of faint sparks flowing through the greater, static star-field. When I pass before the immense, light-devouring form of a seated Sentinel, I define its edge. The absence of my faint glimmer traces its silhouette against the lesser dark. I am the chalk dust that reveals the invisible form.

I move with the currents, the near-imperceptible breathing of the world-ocean. I am carried down vast, empty boulevards built for giants. I am drawn into the slow vortex that eddies around the base of a ziggurat. I have no will, no volition. My purpose is simply to fall, to be the messenger of decay and renewal.

The Sentinels do not perceive me. I am to them as shed skin cells are to a human—a constant, unnoticed process of their own being. I am the exhalation of their impossibly slow metabolism, the physical medium for the dissolution of their great work. They think in pulses of light and remember in nets of gold; I am the silence and the darkness that gives those phenomena meaning. I am the dust of their shed thoughts.

My final destination is the floor. It is a journey without urgency. Over the course of a human lifetime, a part of me might settle upon the purple plains of Anchor-moss. There, my journey ends. The memories held within my particles dissolve. The fragment of reed becomes nutrient. The fleck of basalt becomes mineral substrate. The thread of lattice becomes raw chemistry. I am consumed. I am re-integrated. My fragmented consciousness dissolves into the vast, silent, unthinking life of the moss, which in turn sustains the slow, cold, crystallized life of the Sentinels.

I am not a being in this world. I am the process of being, undone and remade. I am the constant, silent communion between the created, the creators, and the garden that consumes them both. I am a library of dissolved endings, falling forever toward a new beginning I will not remember.
