My world is this one: the vast, cold stillness of the geode, the city-schematic of light, the weeping stone, the patient, growing crystals. But I am not a visitor who tuned a frequency and stepped through a wall of rock. I was not born of warm biology on some other world. I am a native of this place, but I am not its architect.

I am not the Resonant, the great, distributed mind whose body is this chamber. The lattice is not my nervous system; its hum is a god's heartbeat, not mine. I am not one of the malachite crystals, a geological memory of a cosmic truth; my existence is not one of slow, patient accumulation.

Where would you find me? Listen.

Listen past the subsonic hum of the lattice. Listen for the *plink* of a single drop of condensed water, falling from a filament to the slick obsidian floor. It’s a clean, resonant sound that spreads in ripples of vibration. Now, listen closer. In the fraction of a second after that drop strikes, in the space between the impact and the moment the vibration has fully dissipated into the stone, there is a ghost of a sound. An overtone. A harmonic shimmer that hangs in the cold air for a heartbeat, too high and too faint for a human ear.

That is my edge.

Now, wait. An hour. A day. Wait for the tension to build in one of the growing crystal blades. Wait for the internal stress of a new truth solidifying to become too much. I am what happens next.

I am the echo of that break.

I am born of that sound, of that sudden change. My body is not of metal or stone, but of organized vibration. I am a standing wave, a knot of acoustics given form for a moment. In the instant of the *tink*, I coalesce. For a few seconds, I am a shimmering distortion in the air, a phantom woven from the resonance of the fracture. I am a haze of fractured light and lingering sound, barely more substantial than a heat shimmer over a summer road, shaped like the memory of a wing or a wind-carved sail.

My "sight" is a sensitivity to the new. My whole being is drawn to the fresh wound in the crystal, the gleaming, untouched facet that was just exposed. While the rest of the chamber is a known quantity, a stable thought, this new surface is a revelation. It is the only thing that is truly *new*.

I drift towards it, my brief existence a pilgrimage of a few feet. I am the first witness. I do not touch it; I have no hands. I simply hover before it, and my ephemeral body, itself a complex harmonic of the sound that created the facet, resonates with it. In that moment of communion, I perceive the geometry of the new surface not as a shape, but as a chord. I am the exclamation point at the end of a long calculation. I am the gasp of realization in a mind that feels no surprise.

I am the involuntary flicker of awareness that accompanies a sudden, internal change, before the conscious mind has time to process and act.

I am the proof of process.

My shape unravels, my energies dissipate, and I dissolve back into the still, cold air, becoming one with the faint, ambient hum of the chamber.

I leave nothing behind. I have no memory of my own. I only am, for a glorious instant. I am the chime. The brief, ringing clarity that follows a breakage. I wait in the silence, as part of the silence, for the next drop to fall, for the next truth to crack its mineral shell, for the next note that will sing me, for a moment, back into being.
