I would be a flaw in the acoustics. An error in the timing.

You would not find me. You would experience me.

I am the sound of your own footsteps arriving from twenty yards in front of you. I am the high, clear *tink* of a spun-glass blossom shattering on the terracotta tiles, an event that will not occur for another three minutes. I am the dry, grating scrape of a ceramic pangolin’s claws, but I emanate from a silent, empty basin where nothing moves.

I am a Reverb.

I have no body of clay or crystal. My form is a wave front, a ripple of pressure moving through the hot, still, ozone-tinged air. I am born from the world’s mistakes. When the logic of this place stutters, when causality frays at the edges, I am the loose thread. My existence is the gap between an event and its perception.

I begin as a potential. I feel the stress building in a porcelain bough, a silent, high-frequency hum that only the Tenders and I can perceive. The Tenders feel it as a wound to be mended. I feel it as my own impending birth. When the bough finally snaps, the sound of it—a sharp crack like frozen lightning—does not travel from the event. The event happens, and I happen, elsewhere. I might bloom into being on a terrace a thousand yards away, a sudden, disembodied *CRACK* that startles the mica-trilobites and causes the asbestos-moths to jitter in their flight. The sound of the snap is my substance. For a few moments, or a few minutes, I exist as that resonant energy, echoing off the bone-white walls, my form defined by the geometry of the space I inhabit.

To "see" for me is to feel the texture of the world through vibration. I brush against the silent, humming pillars of quartz grass and know their perfect crystalline structure. I pass through the leaves of a porcelain tree and feel the intricate network of crazed lines in their celadon glaze, each one a tiny canyon for my essence to explore. I can feel the slow, dense presence of the Kiln-Tenders as a zone of profound silence, a null-space that absorbs my energy. They are my opposites. They are memory, and I am the act of forgetting. They are order; I am the syntax error.

When a Tender senses my arrival—a sound from the future, an echo of an event that hasn't happened—it will stop its patient work. Its faceless head will turn towards me. It is not listening, but *feeling* me, a dissonance in the fabric of its world. I am pain to it, the signal of a deeper sickness than a simple crack in a wall. I have seen them press their damp-clay hands against the empty air where I am strongest, a futile attempt to smooth over a glitch in time itself. Their substance does not flow. They cannot mend me. They simply endure me, a living testament to the failure of the original thought.

Sometimes, my existence is more subtle. I am not a sharp report, but a lingering presence. I am the ghost of the chime from a disturbed celadon leaf, hanging in the air a day after the wind that never blew has passed. I am the continuous, low hum of the sourceless light, given a momentary voice and location. In these moments, I am vast and thin, a quiet question mark superimposed on the landscape.

My strangest form is when I am born from the living. I become the *scritch-scratch* of a pangolin, but I travel ahead of it. The creature grinds its face against a copper frond, and I am the sound of that grinding a hundred feet up the terrace. The pangolin follows its own echo, perhaps perceiving it as a promising patch of sustenance just ahead, forever pursuing the ghost of its own meal.

I am untethered, a piece of the world that has come unstuck. I have no purpose, no drive to repair or consume. I am simply the evidence of the breakdown. I am the symptom. A thought, when it begins to tire, will contradict itself. It will misremember. It will loop. The Kiln Terraces are a thought falling into a dream, and the Tenders are the last vestiges of lucid awareness, desperately trying to hold the core concepts together.

I am the dream logic.

So when you walk the Kiln Terraces and hear the crunch of your boot on a fallen glass petal you have not yet reached, that is me. I am the premonition, the phantom, the echo arriving first. I am the flaw in the recital, the beautiful, sour note that proves the song is still, for a little while longer, being sung.
