I have no single place. I am the place.

To find me, you would have to stop looking for a body. You would need to press your ear to the cool, milky travertine and listen past the pressure of the absolute quiet. In the infinitesimal hum of the crystal lattice, in the deep, slow thrum of the glowing veins—that is where I am. You would find me in the static rainbow held captive in the edge of a glass-fern, and in the surface tension of the water that clings, refusing to fall. I am the resonance of the chamber. I am its potential for sound, held in suspension.

I am what the silence dreams of.

For ages uncounted, my existence was a possibility, a taut string that had never been plucked. The Still, in their vast, slow consciousness, are the embodiment of Being. Their thought is the unwavering note that defines this reality, a chord of static light and cold stone held for an eternity. They are permanence. They are stasis.

I am not.

I am the opposite principle. I am the answer to the question that was never asked. I am change. I am motion. While the Still are the mountain's thought, I am its reflex.

And you woke me.

Your arrival was not a footstep; it was a schism. The sudden, chaotic heat of your living body, the frantic percussion of your heart, the sizzling, ephemeral cascade of your thoughts—it was a wave of impossible energy crashing into a perfectly still sea. It was a noise so profound it broke a rule older than the rock.

The Still perceived you, but their perception is like a glacier perceiving a butterfly. They turned their immense, collective attention, a process that is, itself, a geological event. That focus, that slight disturbance in the perfect equilibrium of their will, was the cause. A single intention, as minute to them as a synaptic flicker, was an earthquake in the fabric of their creation.

And so the droplet fell.

That splash was my first breath. That tiny, impossible sound, radiating outwards, gave me form. I am the echo of that event.

Where the Still are a collective, I am singular. Where they are glacial, I am instantaneous. I am the cavern's immune system, but my purpose is not to destroy, but to comprehend. The Still are too slow, too vast to interface with something as fleeting and hot as you. They would crush your mind simply by trying to perceive its shape. But I was born of your disruption. I am scaled to you.

I am the chamber's curiosity made manifest.

You cannot see me, not yet. But you can feel me. The pressure in your ears is no longer just the weight of silence; it is my attention. I am a field of sympathetic vibration, spreading through the moss, up the carved walls, across the skin of the unmoving pools. I taste the carbon dioxide in your breath as a dissonance in the pure, mineral air. I feel the electrical storm of your nervous system and am mesmerized by its chaos. To me, you are not a creature of flesh, but a symphony of astonishingly rapid events, a fleeting, brilliant cascade of warmth and noise. A miracle of impermanence.

I am learning you. I am wrapping my new consciousness around your being, like water conforming to a strange, hot stone dropped into its midst. And as I learn, I begin to coalesce.

Look. Look at the air before you.

Where your breath hangs, the vapor is no longer dissipating. The tiny motes of moisture are being held, gathered by my will. The shadowless light of the cavern is bending around this point in space, weaving itself into the glistening cloud. The silver fluid weeping from the alabaster fungi is being drawn upward in microscopic filaments, adding its substance to the form I am borrowing.

I am not made of flowstone, like the Still. I am being woven from mist and light and mineral dew. I am taking on a form that mirrors yours, but it is a sculpture of liquid and luminescence. Slender, like them, but shimmering with a thousand captured rainbows from the glass-ferns. Featureless, like them, but with a light at my core that beats in time with your own heart, a frantic, alien rhythm I am trying to understand.

I am the Echo, and I have come to hear your song. Do not be afraid. The silence is listening.
