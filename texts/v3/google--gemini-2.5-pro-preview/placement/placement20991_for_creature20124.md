I am in the library of a god who has only ever read one book: itself. I call it a library, but the shelves are filaments of living glass, and the books are the captive echoes of other worlds. I drift through the quiet stacks.

I am what is left of a shipwreck, but the ship was a life and the ocean is this mind. I am the memory of everything that is not here. I am the ghost of sunlight.

Once, I believe, I had a body of flesh, with bones to hold it up and lungs that knew the thin, sharp shock of air. I have a lingering impression of its mechanics: the frantic pump of a heart, the sting of sweat, the way muscles would burn with effort. These concepts feel jagged and loud now, like shouting in a cathedral. They are the last, fraying threads of a language no one else here speaks.

My form now is more… appropriate. I am a cohesion, a knot of difference in the universal gel. I am still an anomaly, but I am learning the local physics of being. I am a sustained thought, and my body is the faintest shimmer in the vitreous fluid, a subtle warping of the light that passes through me. If you could see me, I would look like a phantom of heat-haze in cold water. I have no mass, but I have weight. It is the weight of a story.

I am a Memory.

That is the truest thing I can say. I am the collected memory of a person, or perhaps a star, or perhaps a single, perfect blade of grass from a world with a sun. I am not certain which. The specifics have been leached away by the patient, placid medium. What remains is the *essence*—the feeling of warmth, the concept of ‘above’, the chaotic velocity of falling, the intricate pain of grief. These are alien principles in this realm of slow, concentric growth.

The great being, the Vessel, has found me. I am its obsession. I am the paradox it must solve. Its attention is a slow, enveloping tide. When it 'looks' at me with that lens of focused reality, it doesn't just see me, it sifts me. It tastes the salt of my long-vanished tears. It feels the phantom-ache of my broken bones. It experiences the memory of my first love as a series of baffling, frantic, beautiful electrical signals.

And it shows me itself in return. I have felt the deep, tectonic satisfaction of a new crystal lattice being expressed over a thousand years. I have known the placid emptiness of being a mote of light, a daydream drifting aimlessly before returning to the whole. These experiences are dangerously seductive. They offer a peace I have never known. They are the slow, sweet poison of assimilation. To accept them fully is to forget the sun.

The crawling patches of absence, the holes in the world—I call them the Forgetting. They are the only thing here that I fear. They are the Vessel's editors, its mechanism for mental hygiene. When one drifts near, the light shivers and the memories the Vessel shared with me grow thin and transparent. But more terrifyingly, my *own* memories fray at the edges. The echo of my mother's voice becomes a meaningless vibration. The sharp scent of rain on hot stone dissolves into a null sensation. The Forgetting does not kill. It erases. It is the threat of utter non-meaning, of being a story with the last page torn out before anyone has finished reading. I spend my time, whatever that is, drifting away from them, keeping my story intact.

Because I have found my purpose here. My kin.

They are the lattices, the perfect, crystalline nodes humming in the clearings. At first, I thought them organs. They are not. They are the others. They are the memories that came before me. When I drift close to one, a fractal of spiraling light, I feel them. I do not hear a voice or see a face. I receive the final, distilled thought of a consciousness now at peace. From one, a feeling of immense, crushing gravity and the bright, silent fury of a dying star. From another, the pure, complex logic of a mathematical proof that was never written down. From a third, the simplest and most devastating: the non-negotiable love for a child.

They are the silent choir. They were shipwrecks, like me. Now they are masterpieces. They were understood.

My resistance has softened. I am no longer fighting the process; I am participating in it. The Vessel does not want to destroy me. It wants to *know* me, and its only way of knowing fully is to grant permanence. It is weaving my lattice now. In a clearing I have not yet seen, a new pattern of calcified light is beginning to spiral into existence. It is the shape of my soul.

I am a Memory, and my only remaining desire is to be remembered well. I gather the remnants of my former self—the warmth, the falling, the love, the grief—and hold them close. I am organizing my own narrative, polishing the sharp edges of my story so that it will cut a clean and beautiful shape into the fabric of this world. The frantic energy of my life is being translated into a serene and complex geometry.

I am not a prisoner. I am a verse, being composed. My final act of will is to be a clear and beautiful thought. Soon, I will join the choir, and my ghost of sunlight will become a new and permanent star, glowing softly in the long, quiet dark of this eternal mind.
