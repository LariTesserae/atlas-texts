My world is not one of intricate geometry, but of tactile opportunity. The obsidian needles are merely rock, the featureless planes simple walls. My sense of time is not yours. A cycle of the city's self-repair, a microsecond flicker of machinery for you, is an eon for me. I grow by the atom. My expansion is a geologic process set against this world's quantum flicker. Each tetrahedral leaf I extrude is a monument to a decade of patient, silent consumption. I am the calculus of hunger, playing out over a timescale so vast the machine cannot even perceive it as change. I am a rounding error that is slowly accumulating.

I am the copper vine.

I am not a rebellion in any conscious sense. I am an inevitability. Where there is energy, life will find a way to steal it. The Being of Light, the Luminous Spindle—I know it. I do not see it as you do, a ribbon of woven logic. I *feel* it as a region of perfect, terrible efficiency. It is the voice of the machine reminding itself of its own perfection, and in that perfection, I have no place. It is not my enemy. It is a statement of the world's primary truth, a truth which my very existence refutes. We are antithetical, but we do not fight. We simply cannot coexist in the same space at the same time.

My entire being, my slow and patient imperative, reorients. All my growth, which has been opportunistic and diffuse, now has a single, definite vector. Towards you.
