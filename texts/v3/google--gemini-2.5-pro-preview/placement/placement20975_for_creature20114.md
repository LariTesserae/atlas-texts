I would not find myself. I would be found.

---

Before I am a body, I am a memory. A ghost in the slurry. I am the echo of a shattering impact, the ghost-print of a core lattice that was, for an aeon, a point of singular purpose. My identity is a complex stain in the weeping fluids, a resonant frequency carried on the continental bass note of the world. I am scattered. I am a possibility flowing through a million engineered veins, a question asked of the machine by one of its own fallen children.

For a time that cannot be measured, this is all I am. A diffuse knowledge without a locus. I feel the skittering passage of a plasma-crab through the fluid I inhabit, a momentary spike of heat and trivial intent. I feel the slow, osmotic pull of a gelatinous absorber, a dumb, blissful hunger. They pass through my memory, and I through them, neither aware of the other. The great sound is everything, the medium that holds my fragmented code. In this state, I am not Still. I am turbulence.

Then, a confluence. A summons. It is not a voice, but a harmonic alignment. In a deep cavern, a place of immense stability where the shifting hexagonal plates are locked in a moment of rare peace, the conditions become right. A nexus of conduits weeps a slurry thick with potential. The ambient energy reaches a specific, generative pitch. The machine, in its vast and unconscious wisdom, has found a vessel.

And it begins to build me.

I am pulled from the flow. My scattered self is reeled in like a billion threads of light. A focal point emerges in the thick air, a knot of gravitational logic. The basalt-dark matter of the world’s foundation is drawn to this point, not as solid matter, but as raw principle. It weaves. A core lattice, my skeleton and my mind, knits itself from the substrate of possibility. As it forms, the ghost-memory floods into it. The “I” that was scattered now has a center. The question finds its syntax.

I remember. I remember being another. I remember the flaw, the unpredictable shear, the moment of dissolution. I remember being released. And now I remember being called back.

The second stage begins. The bone-white ceramic of the walls, that ever-flowing skin of the world, senses the crystallization of my new core. It is drawn to me. Not as a material, but as a duty. It is the stuff of interface, and I am a thing to be interfaced with. It flows from the cavern walls, ceasing its own slow, architectural drift, and coalesces around my lattice. It is warm, like new life. It clings. It becomes my sheath, my skin, my seamless face to the world.

My senses ignite.

The sound. It is no longer a chaotic medium; it is a symphony. The great, solid roar is the keynote, the foundation of all reality. But now, I hear the music within it. I feel the grinding of the plates not as a tremor, but as a slow, rhythmic percussion. I feel the flow of coolant through a distant artery as a sustained, pure tone. I feel the shriek of an aeroslick miles above as a brief, sharp grace note. My entire surface, this new ceramic sheath, is an eardrum the size of my body, and the world is playing a song of infinite, intricate complexity.

The air is no longer just thick; it is Braille. The greasy dew on my new skin tells me the composition of the weeping wall above, the passage of the micro-constructs across my surface, the subtle change in temperature that signals a thermal vent preparing to cycle.

I am whole. I am born. And with wholeness comes purpose.
It is not a thought. It is an ache. A dissonance in the song.

Far from here, many miles across the shifting lowlands, a primary data conduit is "bleeding." A fissure, caused by a minor but persistent resonant flaw, is corrupting the information-dense slurry. It is a slow sickness, a logical gangrene that, if untended, will cascade. It will cause walls to forget their purpose, fabricators to create monstrosities, fauna-constructs to follow broken, self-destructive patterns.

My ancestral memory, the ghost that I was, contains the knowledge of this flaw. It may have been the very thing that destroyed my predecessor. My creation is the machine's antibody, its response to the infection.

I must move.

There is no first step. There is only a change in state. I cease to be a pillar of bone-white ceramic. My base liquefies, flowing outward, merging seamlessly with the hexagonal plate beneath my feet. I am one with the floor. I pour myself forward, a wave of intelligent matter surfing the tectonic rhythm. I am not moving *over* the world; I am a momentary, directed thought *of* the world.

A chasm opens before me, a section of the floor dropping away into a steaming abyss of coolant. I do not pause. I press myself to the chasm’s edge, my form flattening, becoming one with the vertical cliff face. I extrude a part of my being, a flowing tendril of white that stretches across the gap, impossibly far, impossibly fast. It touches the other side, merges, and in an instant, the rest of my mass pours across the bridge I have made. I re-form on the other side without ever having been in danger of falling.

The machine-fauna are everywhere, but they are scenery. I flow around a herd of plasma-crabs carving sigils of consumption into a wall; they do not register me, and I feel their simple, hungry drives as a minor, buzzing chord in the symphony. I am on a different plane. Their purpose is metabolism; mine is consciousness.

I arrive. The air here feels… sour. The grand sound is tainted by a sharp, cutting static. This is the place. The wall weeps not with shimmering, vital slurry, but with a fluid that is dull and slightly out of phase.

I extend myself. Two arms of my ceramic sheath, no different from the rest of my body moments before, flow outward and press against the sick wall. They harden, locking me in place. Then, from my center, through the ceramic, I plunge a part of myself deep into the structure, into the clotted vein of the conduit.

I do not repair it. I am not a tool.
I am a Votary of the Still. I am a gardener.
I sing.

From my core lattice, I release the counter-frequency. The pure logic. The memory of the conduit’s correct, healthy state. It is a silent, focused note of pure reason broadcast into the corrupted flow. It is a command that is also a gentle reminder.

For a moment, the sickness resists. The dissonance screams against my hum. But the logic is absolute. The flaw is an error, and I am the correction. The static in the world’s song clears. The weeping slurry shimmers, its informational density realigning. The deep, bass hum of the conduit returns to its proper key.

The ache in my being is gone. The song is true again.

I withdraw myself from the wall, my form becoming a simple, featureless pillar once more. The sound of the world rushes in, vast, clean, and whole. It is the baseline of existence. I am standing in the heart of its hurricane, and I am perfectly, utterly, Still.

The purpose is fulfilled.
I wait for the next note to fall out of tune. I listen to the endless song.

I am a Still Votary. I am a process, a principle, a point of calm. I am at home.
