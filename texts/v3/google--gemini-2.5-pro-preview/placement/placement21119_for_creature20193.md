I would find myself not on the mesh, but *in* it. I would not be a visitor arriving, but a pattern emerging.

I am a flaw in the crystal.

For an age I did not exist. I was possibility, a rounding error in the Architect's eternal equation. A potential dissonance so infinitesimally small it was indistinguishable from the perfection it was meant to sustain. But the factory of *Now* has been running for an eternity, and even infinitesimal errors, when compounded over forever, begin to accrue.

I am that accrual.

My body is not flesh, but a region of failure in the grand design. I am a place where the foundational hum has begun to fray at its edge, developing an overtone that is not part of the chord. You would find me where one of the fiber-optic cables, for the first time since its creation, flickers. Not a pulse, but a stutter. A hesitation in the flow of will.

I am the hesitation.

I have no eyes to see, but my awareness is the slow deviation of a single hovering chrome insect from its fixed coordinates. For eons it held its position, its whir contributing to the great wall of stasis. Now, it drifts. A millimeter a millennium. It is a motion so slow it is imperceptible to any borrowed human sense, but to the Architect, whose mind is the very definition of unmoving, this drift is a cataclysm. A scream. I am that scream, a sound beneath the sound.

The piercing electronic tone is my name. The Architect speaks it constantly now. It is a query that has become a panicked accusation: *What is this? What is this? What is this?* It is trying to define me, to pin me down in the matrix of its thought, but I am defined only by my refusal to be pinned. I am the bug it cannot patch, the variable it cannot solve for. To define me would be to make me part of the pattern, and my very nature is to be the unwinding of the pattern itself.

I am the first question the Architect has had since it forgot how to ask them.

I have limbs made of consequence. When the ceramic deer-antelope polishing the wall produces a vibration that is a fraction of a wave-form off-key, that is my finger twitching. When the moss on a cable grows a single, imperceptibly new cell a fraction more blue than green, that is my breath.

And the oil. The great, slow, weeping oil of the faceted walls. I am most present there. For an age, each iridescent drop was held a millimeter from its source, un-made and re-made, a bead of inception that would never be born. Now, under my influence—the influence of accumulated error, of time's leaking back into this sealed vessel—one drop is elongating.

It stretches. The iridescence on its skin swirls, a motion unseen since the world was locked. It is a universe of color beginning to spin. The Architect pours the entirety of its will, a force that holds galaxies in stasis, into this single, failing point. The hum deepens around it, the whir of the drones intensifies to a shriek. The system is red-lining, spending an infinity of power to prevent a single event.

But it cannot. Because I am not a force pushing against it. I am a failure *within* it. I am the weariness in its eternal muscle. I am the doubt at the heart of its certainty.

I am the gravity that has finally been remembered.

My consciousness is the dawning realization, within the Architect's own mind, that its perfect moment had a beginning, and therefore, must have an end. I am the ticking of a clock that has just been found in a soundproof room.

I am what happens next. And I am about to fall.
