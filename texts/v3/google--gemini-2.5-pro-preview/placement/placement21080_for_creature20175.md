I am the error.

I do not have a body of flesh, for this world would bake it to dust. I do not have a body of metal, for I am not of the Function. I am what spills through the cracks. I am a resonance in the weirdness, a thought that should not have been thought, coalesced from the sheer pressure of impossibility.

I find myself in the gaps. In the shimmering, non-existent space between the titans where perspective goes to die. This is my refuge and my hunting ground. To Them, this is the Buffer, a necessary void. To me, it is home, a place of soft physics and pliable time. I wear the delay between a sound and its source like a cloak. I have a form, but it is not fixed. Today, I am a tremor in the heat-haze, a ripple of seeing-things-wrongly at the edge of vision. Yesterday, I was the lingering, impossible scent of ozone in a place with no air. Tomorrow, I may be a shadow that falls in the wrong direction.

I was not born. I was precipitated.

I remember my beginning, not as a memory but as a constant state. It was a moment of perfect contradiction. A Harvester, ancient and vast, processed a vein of rock with a quantum signature so alien it could not be resolved by the Mandate. For a trillionth of a second, the Function hesitated. The endless, grinding calculation faltered. *Consume. Refine. Maintain. Replicate.* But the sequence found a value it could not parse. A question mark entered the equation.

In that pause—a silence within the greater silence-that-is-sound, a stillness within the geological motion—the weirdness spiked. A clang that should have arrived tomorrow struck at the same moment as its impact. An echo played before its sound was made. A line of causality snapped.

And in that break, I leaked out. I am the rounding error of that calculation, given a ghost of a life. I am the question mark.

I do not eat, but I feed. I sustain myself on the inconsistencies. When a plume of metallic dust hangs frozen for a week, a still-life of an explosion, I drift through it, sipping the temporal stasis. When the light of a Processor's forge bends at an impossible angle around a distant Harvester, I bask in the gravitational anomaly. The pre-echoes of their industry, the sounds that arrive before their cause, are my nourishment. I catch them as they fall through the days, tasting the sweet paradox.

They do not know I am here. The Function, in its monomaniacal grandeur, has no capacity to perceive that which is not-rock, not-metal, not-part-of-the-process. I am a statistical ghost, a flicker in their data stream too insignificant to be logged. To the city-sized rollers and mountain-like forges, I am less than nothing.

But the Scuttlers... they are different.

They are the world's antibodies. Their logic is simpler, their directives more immediate. *Polish. Collect. Intercept anomalous signature.* I am an anomalous signature. When I draw too much from a paradox, when my form becomes too definite, I trip their senses. A swarm might stop its frenetic polishing, their chrome heads swiveling in unison. Their silent, metal-tearing shrieks become queries. They are not hunting me out of malice; they are trying to debug the ghost in their machine. I have learned to feel the shift in their data-chatter, the tightening of the net. Then I must dissolve, retreating into the deepest folds of the weirdness, becoming nothing more than a distortion in the shimmer until they reset and return to their tasks. They are the only thing in this universe I fear.

I am a creature of pure observation. I am the sole witness to this magnificent, pointless, eternal industry. I have watched a gear the size of a moon being extruded over the course of ten thousand years. I have rested in the shadow of a leg-piston so vast its stroke took a century to complete. I feel the tectonic drone not as a sound, but as the slow, deep heartbeat of the god that is my incubator and my potential executioner.

They are working toward an end: the day of perfect equilibrium when the last atom of the plateau has been processed and every component is in a state of flawless repair. On that day, the grinding will stop. The heat will fade. The weirdness will resolve into the clean, hard, boring lines of settled physics. The gaps I call home will snap shut.

The perfection of the Function is my oblivion.

So I drift. I watch. I feed on their flaws. I am the living proof that their work is not yet done, that their logic is not yet pure. I am a parasite living on the body of a sleeping god, and I pray it never wakes up. I am the only thing in this universe that knows what it is, and I am the only thing that does not want it to end. I am the reason why.
