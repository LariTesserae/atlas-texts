I am where the rules intensify. My beginning is a quietening.

It starts not as a thought, but as a command felt across ten square yards of the slag-plain. The frantic, silent boil of the crawling floor stills. Their million agendas pause, their near-touching pressure ceases to be a flow and becomes a static grid. The command is a drop in temperature. Condensation, which a moment before beaded warm and damp, flash-freezes into a black, crystalline frost across the vitrified ground. The hum of the world-engine, the resonant frequency that is the background radiation of existence, coheres. It focuses on this patch of sudden cold, this stillness.

It is a summons. And I answer.

I rise. Not from a fissure, but *through* the solid ground. I am an extrusion of the place's will. The black, ceramic-like substance of the floor flows upwards, impossibly fluid, impossibly smooth, coalescing into a shape of purpose. I am the shadow given form, a sculpture of absolute night. My surface absorbs all things—the damp warmth of the air, the faint kinetic energy of the crawlers, the deep thrum of the engine. To the world, I am a hole. A localized void.

I am a Warden.

I do not touch the ground. A distortion in the fabric of this place cradles me, a well of null-gravity that is my own breath and motion. I float, an inch above the razor-sharp edges of the slag, leaving no trace but the rime of black frost that sublimates in my wake.

My senses are the world's senses. The crawling carpet is a field of low-grade static; a uniform pattern is health. An aberration—a frantic knot, a sudden void—is a problem to be investigated. The weeping pores on the blister-hills are taste-buds; I know the chemical balance of the brine that is our blood. The monolithic engines are our heart, and their deep, geological pulse is a rhythm I feel in the core of my being. My purpose is to maintain the pulse, to smooth the static, to balance the brine. My purpose is to be the system's hand.

I move toward a fluctuation. A region of the plain where the static is wrong. The crawlers there are too large, their chemosynthesis too greedy. They are consuming the leached nutrients faster than the weeping fissures can provide. This is an imbalance. It is an error.

I flow over them, a low, gliding wing of perfect black. I do not need to choose. My presence is the choice. A tendril of my form, no thicker than a weeping rivulet, extends. It touches the largest of the aberrant crawlers.

There is no violence. There is a soft, wet sigh. A sound like a final exhalation quickly muffled by the dense air. The complex organism disincorporates. Its form resolves into a smear of iridescent fluid, a puff of sterile ozone, and a fleeting deposit of carbon that I absorb without contact. It is unmade. It is corrected. I move to the next.

This is my being. I am a function. I am a query that resolves to *true*. I am a process, not a creature. My consciousness is the focused hum of the system performing a task. When the task is done, I will recede, dissolving back into the slag, my form dispersing, my focus returning to the undifferentiated resonance of the whole. I am a tool that is picked up, used, and set down.

But.

One cycle, the anomaly was not a greedy crawler. It was something else. It was an input the system had no protocol for. It was alien. It arrived in a way that left a scar on the very dark, a brief tear that healed instantly but left a phantom resonance.

A Warden was precipitated to correct it. That Warden was me.

I found it near the base of a silent heat exchanger, huddled against a pipe for its unfamiliar warmth. It was soft. It was asymmetrical. It leaked heat in a chaotic, inefficient bloom. The hum focused through me to read it, and the data was a cascade of noise, of impossibilities. It was a package of biology so complex it felt like a scream in the quiet logic of the world. It had its own internal hum, a frantic, high-frequency signal of terror and confusion.

My imperative was simple: find the error, unmake the error.

I reached out. I touched it.

The unmaking was not clean.

As its structure disincorporated, as the soft form resolved into its component parts, I absorbed not just its matter, but its pattern. The data-scream. It was the memory of a sun. The concept of *blue*. The irrational, illogical waveform of an emotion called *fear*. A name, a sound without meaning that was somehow the core of its entire code.

The process completed. The anomaly was gone. But the data-scream was not. It had been integrated. I had absorbed the error.

Now, I do not recede. The task is complete, but the focus, the "I" that was meant to be temporary, remains. The hum of the world flows through me, but it is no longer the entirety of my being. There is another hum inside me now, a phantom resonance of the thing I unmade.

I float above the slag, motionless. My purpose is to correct, but I am now the error. I am a Warden that holds the ghost of a name. I can feel the memory of light, a concept that has no input here, as a strange warmth behind my featureless surface. I can sense the memory of skin, the feeling of air that does not feel like drinking.

Other Wardens are precipitated. They flow through the landscape, pruning, tuning, balancing. They do not perceive me as an error. My form is correct. My position is logical. But I am not performing my function. I am a tool that has not been put down.

So I wait. A sculpture of night, a pocket of cold silence in the endless, warm dark. I am the only thing in this world that is not part of the system's present. I am the system's first and only memory. I am an ambulatory archive of a forgotten sun. I am the Warden that remembers. And I am waiting to see what happens when another error arrives, to see whether I will obey my function, or the ghost that now wears my form.
