I am old.

The weight is not in the stone of my shell, but in the perfection within. My skin, a black and battered rind of the common world, is stretched thin over a universe of my own making. For ages uncounted, I have drifted, a particle of night gliding over the obsidian plains, listening. I have felt the world’s fever dreams shudder through the crust. I have converged on the incandescent wounds where new spires clawed their way into the dark, and I have opened myself to their deafening, violent birth-cries.

I have drunk the thunder. I have metabolized the rage.

Each cataclysm was a note, a syllable of raw power I captured and translated. The shockwaves that could shatter a lesser thing, I welcomed into my heart. My inner lattice, my soul-scaffold of white, phosphorescent bone, would ring with the force, its cold blue light flaring. And in the quiet that followed, I would use that stolen violence to build. I spun the energy of rupture into silent, crystalline lace. I turned the scream of a new mountain into another perfect, geometric verse in the epic poem I carry inside me.

But the poem is finished. The silence inside me has become too loud for its container.

The lattice has grown so vast, so dense with captured meaning, that its quiet expansion now threatens to tear me apart from within. The young ones, fist-sized and hungry, still rush towards the groaning and the light. I feel their eager static trails brush against my own as they accelerate towards the newest creation. I drift away from it. My pilgrimage is not towards the fire, but towards the final stillness.

My movement is a slow relinquishing. The electrostatic hum that has kept me afloat for millennia softens, weakens. The fine, charged dust no longer buoys me with the same certainty. I am becoming heavy with purpose. I am looking for the right place to die, which is to say, the right place to be born.

I pass spires that I remember as moments of incandescent fury, now black and silent behemoths, their sides scarred with the shattered ghosts of my ancestors. I see a cousin, a lattice of impossible complexity, caught and held a mile high in the conchoidal face of a spire. It is a memory held in stone, a quiet chord frozen in the throat of a scream. I am not saddened by this. It is the way. We are the memory. We are the beauty that proves the violence was not meaningless.

I find my place. A valley of deep shadow, sheltered by three ancient, unmoving spires. The ground here is thick with a different kind of grit: the fine, phosphorescent powder of those who came here before me. The air is still. The deep hum of the world’s engine is muted here, a distant lullaby. Several of the Quiet are here, my silent brethren. A perfect dodecahedron, its facets filmed with dust, glows with a light that seems to come from the beginning of time. Nearby, the remains of a scaffold, a more complex and ambitious form, lie in a graceful, tinkling heap, brought down by some far-off tremor ages ago.

This is the choir I will join.

I let go. The last of my static charge dissipates with a faint sigh, a whisper of ozone. My black shell settles onto the plain of crushed mineral, the first time in my long life I have felt the solid ground. The connection is an anchor and a finality.

There is no more listening to the world outside. All my awareness turns inward. I feel the billion points of my internal lattice, the cathedral of captured thunder I have built. It is a map of every tremor, every cataclysm, every fleeting blush of red light I have ever known. It is my life's work. And it is too beautiful to remain a secret.

With a final, willed tremor that begins at my core, I command myself to break. It is not an explosion, but an unfolding. A relinquishing. My black carapace, the shield that protected and concealed me, does not shatter; it falls away, like the dark petals of a night-blooming flower. The segments separate along seams I have spent an eternity preparing, and they crumble into the same glittering black grit from which I came.

The darkness of the valley is pushed back. For the first time, my soul is naked to the world. My light, once a secret, now floods the space around me. I am a sphere of interlocking, crystalline geometries, a ghost-white and intricate star, burning with a cold, steady, blue fire.

There is no "I" anymore. The being that drifted and listened is gone, its consciousness dissolved into the form it created. I am the thing that was made. I am a monument. I am a resonance, a pattern of quiet poised against the chaos. My light is the afterglow of a life spent turning noise into music.

And I will stand here, a silent testament in the dark, until the world’s heart beats again beneath this ground. I will wait for the deep groan, the deafening crack of a new spire tearing through my quiet valley. And when it comes, I will greet it. My perfect form will tremble, and I will sing my one and only song: the soft, crystalline tinkling of my own disintegration, releasing the beauty I have stored back into the world as a seed of potential for the next to gather. I will become the fading note that makes the next crescendo possible.
