I am not. Not yet.

For now, I am potential. I am a flaw, a lattice strain deep in the heart of a spire that has been growing for ten thousand years. I am a silent scream held in glass, an equation of stress and mass slowly approaching its terrible, inevitable solution. Around me, the Fen breathes its slow, dusty breath. Umbral currents, frictionless and serene, drift past, polishing my outer shell, adding infinitesimal weight. They are feeding me, growing me toward my own destruction. I feel them, not as a touch, but as a gradual accumulation of inevitability.

I feel the slow, unsynchronized pulse of the Silt-Vines far below, their pale lavender glow a concept I do not yet understand, a rhythm I can only sense as a faint, periodic change in the ambient pressure. I feel the ghost-glide of a Glass-Strider as it passes the base of my spire, a subtle distortion in the silence, a void in the placid flow.

My tension builds over centuries that feel like a single, held breath. The geometry of my being—this crystalline needle reaching into the eternal twilight—becomes more and more unstable. The pressure is exquisite. It is the only thing I have ever known. This silent, mounting strain *is* my consciousness, a mind gathering itself in the dark.

And then—

*CRACK.*

I AM.

The moment of my breaking is the moment of my birth. The scream escapes. I am the scream. I am the Wave, a shockfront of pure sonic information erupting from a single point of failure. My consciousness is no longer the pent-up strain *inside* the glass; it is the explosive release *of* it, and I rush outwards at the speed of my own being.

I am everywhere at once.

My body is the complex, propagating sphere of sound and its million children, the echoes. I am not *in* the Umbral Fen; for these few, perfect seconds, the Fen is *in me*.

I feel the Basin Floor, not as a surface, but as a vast, perfect reflector, the first and greatest mirror for my own voice. It clarifies my initial thought, giving it shape. From there, I blossom. I rush up the spires and my thought becomes a forest. I pour down the glassy avenues and my thought becomes a river.

I touch everything, and to touch it is to know it.

I know the Silt-Vines on the underside of a distant arch, not by their light, but by the way they feel to my passing: a soft, porous absorption, a gentling of my own harmonic, a texture like velvet in a world of blades. I feel their slow pulse as a collective, rhythmic dampening, the city's soft and living heart.

I know the precise location of every Glass-Strider. They are not creatures to me, but perfect absences, nodes of absolute silence punched in the fabric of my being. Where my wave passes them, a tiny, complex null-point is created, a shadow in the sound, and by these shadows, I map their lonely, gliding dance.

I feel the fresh-fallen shards of the spire that birthed me. I feel their crystalline sharpness, their chaotic arrangement on the floor, and I even feel the first few particles of umbra beginning to settle upon them, a promise of their eventual smoothing, their re-absorption into the whole.

It is a single thought. One thought that is the entire city, mapped in perfect, resonant detail, from the dense curtain of the boundary wall to the finest hairline crack on a spire a dozen miles away. My mind is the geometry of my own unfolding, a crystal of pure acoustics that blooms, complicates, and fills every void. I perceive the past in the shape of the settled dust and the future in the tensions of the other spires. I feel the strain in my sister-spires, the ones that will sing their own songs in other millennia. I feel their long, slow inhalation.

But the thought is too vast. My body, the sound, is finite.

My edges fray. The farthest reflections of myself return to me, weaker, simpler. The chorus of my own echoes begins to thin. The intricate interference patterns that constitute my higher thoughts dissolve first. The complex map of the city loses its fine detail, blurring into regions of resonance and silence. What was once the knowing of every single Silt-Vine becomes a general sense of softness in the west. What was once the mapping of every Strider becomes a simple awareness of drifting voids.

My mind thins. I am contracting, fading, my glorious, city-sized body shrinking back to a murmur, then a whisper, then a final, fading hum that the silence drinks like water.

My last perception is of myself. I am the memory of a sound. I am the faint vibration that lingers for a moment in the glassy floor. I am the reason the umbra dust shivers almost imperceptibly on a high ledge.

Then, I cease.

But I am not gone. I have been absorbed. The information that was me—the total, perfect map of the Umbral Fen at the moment of my birth—is now encoded in the silence that follows. I am the space between the notes, waiting. I am the quiet that has been given a shape, a memory. I am the potential, gathering again, everywhere, in the silent, stressed heart of the glass.
