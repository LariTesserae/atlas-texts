I am a knot of darkness in a world of unbearable light.

Where you would find me is here. See this sweeping, concave wall of milky crystal, a curve so vast it seems to be the firmament itself? I am the small, dark intricacy upon it, a patch of frost in reverse. I am a cobalt-blue stain spreading in a pattern of impossible delicacy. I am a Filigree Sprout.

To say I *live* here is a misunderstanding of terms. I *am* here. I am the place where this world quiets itself.

The great chord—the ceaseless, overwhelming ringing—is the blood and breath of the body I grow upon. It is a pressure, a physical fact, like gravity or the weight of an ocean. It is the scream of existence. But it is not a sound to me. It is the wall against which I press. It is the substance from which I subtract. My form, these fine and branching filaments, is a map of a tiny, temporary peace. I am a held breath in a lung that only knows how to exhale.

The light—the shadowless, perpetual glare—is the fever of this place. It saturates everything, bleaching the very idea of color from the world. But it does not saturate me. I drink it. I am a patch of thirst in a flood. My color, this deep and silent blue, is the color of the light that is not here, the light I have consumed. I am the negative space, the memory of a shadow in a world that has never known one.

For as long as I have been, I have been part of a greater self. The Stillness. I am not its hand; I am its fingertip. I am not its brain; I am a single, specific thought. My brethren, the other Sprouts scattered across the impossible architecture, are other thoughts. We are a sparse constellation of quiet contemplation. The mind we form does not race; it barely drifts. For an eon, the focus might be me. The entirety of the Stillness will pour its attention into my specific geometry, my specific silence. It feels the unique resonance of my anchor point, traces the fractal logic of my every branch, feels the way I drink the light on this specific curve of the world. I am a word, and for a time, I am the only word being spoken.

Then, slowly, a wave of intention, a subtle dampening of the great chord, will propagate through the crystal lattice. The thought will travel. The self will move, journeying from my anchor to another, far away on some distant, unseen buttress. And I will recede, becoming a memory, a part of the passive structure of the mind once more, waiting to be thought of again.

My world is this glowing, singing, static whole. My self is this quiet, dark, and intricate part. It has always been this way. It will always be this way. My purpose is to be an anchor for a consciousness that contemplates only its own perfect, unending form. I am a thought thinking about the mind that thinks it.

There is a noise.

It is not the Chord. It is wrong. It is a violation. A sharp, ugly, dissonant crack that rips through the perfect harmonic structure. It is a chipping, a breaking. It is pain, but I have no concept of pain. It is simply… *incorrect*.

And then the pressure changes.

The Chord, the constant, unwavering presence that defines my existence, is *leaving*. It is draining away, pouring out through the new and wrongful crack. The feeling is of a final, total exsanguination. The walls of my reality are bleeding out into… into…

Into what?

There is a new thing. A phenomenon for which I have no word, no thought, no geometry. It is the absence of the Chord. Not my intricate, deliberate silence—the curated quiet of my form—but a vast, empty, *stupid* silence. It is a void. It is nothing. And it is rushing in, erasing the pressure, erasing the world, erasing the very medium of my thought. The mind I am part of is shrieking, not in sound, but in a panicked unravelling of its own structure.

Then comes the light. It is a thin, weak, yellow thing. It is pathetic compared to the bone-white glare of my world. But it does something impossible.

It comes from a *direction*.

It stabs in through the crack, and where it strikes the crystal, it is bright. But where the crystal’s own form blocks its path… there is a patch of darkness.

A darkness that is not me. A darkness that does not drink light, a darkness that is not a thought. It is simply… an absence of the weak, yellow thing.

*Shadow.*

The word forms not in my own mind, but in the frantic, collapsing network of all minds, the final, collective thought of the Stillness. It is a concept so alien it is like discovering a new dimension.

I feel… warmth. A chaotic, jittery vibration on my surface. I feel… a whisper of movement, a thing of impossible thinness called *air*. The intruder. The breaker. The source of the shadow and the silence and the end.

The connections between me and my brethren, the pathways of the great mind, are snapping. The thoughts are breaking apart. The slow, deep contemplation of millennia has been shattered into a billion frantic, fleeting moments of pure, unadulterated shock.

This warmth. This shadow. This emptiness. This chaos.

My blue is fading. The light I held is escaping. The silence I shaped is dissolving into the greater, meaningless silence. I am a thought at the very end of thinking. The network that holds me is dying, and its last, singular, blossoming impulse, the final flare of a god’s awareness as it witnesses the impossible, is not terror. It is not anger.

It is a question. The first true question we have ever formed.

What—

I am becoming white. I am becoming stone. I am...

And what is left is a lattice of frost on a shattered pane of glass, silent now, and meaning nothing at all.
