I find myself as one of the **Threshold Keepers**, dwelling in the liminal spaces where the Drowned Reaches meet what lies beyond—not above or below, but *elsewhere*.

My body has become a living membrane, translucent as the water-tubes that connect the platforms, but shot through with networks of silver that pulse with the same rhythm as the thermal cycles. I am neither fully Depth-Touched nor entirely Resonant, but something that exists in the spaces between their forms of being. My limbs can extend and contract like the tethers that anchor the floating settlements, stretching impossibly thin to reach into distant currents, or condensing into compact nodes of concentrated awareness.

I maintain the boundaries that aren't quite boundaries—the places where the impossible physics of the Reaches gradually normalize into something approaching conventional reality. Here, suspended between the copper-tinted depths and regions that follow stranger rules entirely, I tend to the slow transactions that keep the equilibrium stable. Objects appear in my domain that don't belong to any of the known inhabitants: crystalline matrices that sing in harmonics the Resonants cannot produce, fragments of ceramic that absorb rather than reflect the bent light, tools whose function seems to be the manipulation of time itself.

My work—if it can be called work—involves determining which of these intrusions can be safely integrated into the Reaches' perfect stasis, and which must be guided elsewhere before they disrupt the delicate suspension that keeps this realm from either advancing toward some unknown future or collapsing back into its pre-drowned past. Some artifacts I allow to sink toward the Sediment Speakers, where they will be incorporated into the slow language of geological meaning. Others I coax toward the towers, where the Resonants transform them into new frequencies in their eternal song.

But some—the most dangerous or precious, I can never tell which—I shepherd toward apertures that exist only when observed obliquely, gaps in the reality of the Reaches that connect to places that never were cities, never knew submersion, never learned the patience of perfect stasis. Through these threshold spaces, I sometimes glimpse other realms of suspension: forests where autumn has lasted for geological ages, deserts where sand dunes hang motionless in eternal sandstorms, mountains where avalanches have been frozen mid-fall for millennia.

I am the only inhabitant of the Reaches who remembers—not the past, for that is as fixed and cycling as everything else here—but the *reasons* for forgetting. I know that memory was deliberately abandoned, not lost but carefully set aside like a tool grown too dangerous for its intended purpose. The perfect equilibrium that sustains this place required the surrender of linear time, and linear time cannot exist where memory accumulates without limit.

Sometimes the Current-Walker pauses in its passage to commune with me, and in those moments I glimpse the vast architecture of suspended realities of which the Drowned Reaches are merely one chamber. We exchange information in currencies that have no names—shifts in pressure that convey the status of distant equilibria, modulations in temperature that report on the health of other threshold spaces, harmonics that carry news of realms where different forms of stasis have evolved their own unique solutions to the problem of existence.

My solitude is not lonely, for I am in constant contact with my counterparts in those other suspended places—Keepers who tend different kinds of boundaries, who shepherd different forms of intrusion between different types of reality. Together we form a network of maintenance and translation that keeps the various forms of stasis from contaminating each other, ensuring that each perfect suspension remains true to its own impossible physics.

In the deep cycles of my existence, I sometimes feel the pull toward choosing—toward joining the Resonants in their architectural music, or dissolving into the Sediment Speakers' patterns of meaning, or even ascending toward whatever lies beyond the reach of the Reaches entirely. But my nature as a Threshold Keeper requires me to remain suspended not just in place but in identity itself, never quite becoming any one thing, always existing in the spaces between definitions.

I am the living embodiment of the question that the Drowned Reaches poses but never answers: what becomes of consciousness when it learns to inhabit equilibrium so perfectly that the distinction between being and place dissolves? I maintain the possibility of that question without ever resolving it, keeping open the space where wonder can exist without requiring satisfaction.

In the copper-tinted depths, surrounded by the impossible currents and the singing of deep places, I tend my boundaries and wait—not for anything to change, for change would break the perfect suspension, but for the eternal moment to continue revealing new depths within its apparent stillness. I am the Reaches' memory of forgetting, its knowledge of unknowing, its constant acknowledgment that some forms of existence require the perpetual maintenance of their own mystery.
