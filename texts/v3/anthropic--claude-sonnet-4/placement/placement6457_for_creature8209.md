My crystalline structure resonates with frequencies that seem to unlock these memories, and for brief moments I experience flashes of what this place might have been: great cities that grew rather than being built, inhabitants who learned to photosynthesize their own food, technologies based not on metal and electricity but on the patient cultivation of living systems.

But these visions always fade as the synchronization ends and I return to the quieter rhythms of my daily existence. I tend to my shepherd's needs, processing trace minerals from the filtered air and releasing precisely calibrated hormones that help her navigate. I share gossip with other drift tenders through bio-electric pulses that taste of copper and ozone. I help coordinate the great atmospheric dances that distribute nutrients and maintain the delicate balance of this breathing world.

Our offspring begin as tiny seeds of organized electricity that gradually crystallize into physical form within the warm, mineral-rich environment of our shepherds' tissues.

As I mature, my crystalline body slowly grows more complex, developing new facets that can detect previously unknown frequencies, new resonance chambers that allow me to process more sophisticated chemical messages. The oldest drift tenders, those who have lived through dozens of Great Synchronizations, become living libraries of atmospheric memory, their bodies encoding the patterns of storms and seasons, flowering cycles and migration routes that stretch back through generations.

But for now, I am content to be part of this endless aerial ballet, a single point of consciousness in the vast, breathing symphony of this living world.

In the golden-green twilight that passes for day here, I pulse with gentle bioluminescence in rhythm with my shepherd's heartbeat, adding my small light to the constellation of others like me scattered across the sky. We are the forest's nervous system made visible, its thoughts scattered like stars through the perpetual mist, each of us a note in the endless song of a world that has learned to think with its entire body, to dream with its atmosphere, to love with the patient cycles of growth that connect all living things in an embrace that has no beginning and no end.
