When I move through the cooler air, my edges become more defined, my form gaining weight and substance. But when I wade into the hottest springs, I begin to dissolve at my periphery, my consciousness expanding into the heated water until I become a living thermal gradient, aware but dispersed.

Other times I expand into the geological present, my consciousness stretched thin across decades of slow change, feeling the patient growth of flowstone formations, the gradual migration of spring openings, the deep pulse of pressure systems far below the surface.

I would be a translator in this place—one of the few beings capable of communicating with both the deep Thermals and the surface dwellers. When a Thermal rises with urgent news from the depths, I can partially merge with its flowing geometry, allowing its temperature-language to flow through me and emerge as chemical signals that the Listeners can interpret. When the Scalding Shepherds need to understand why their bacterial gardens are failing, I can dissolve partially into the affected pools, tasting the mineral imbalances and reforming to guide them to healthier waters.

We Drift-Touched are drawn to moments of geological transformation, gathering in loose communities around emerging springs to witness the landscape's labor pains, to help guide the new thermal flows into harmonious patterns with existing systems.

This is where I would belong: neither fully creature nor completely landscape, but something born from their endless conversation, a living question mark in stone and steam, translucent and temporary and absolutely essential to the ongoing translation between the world's many ways of being.
