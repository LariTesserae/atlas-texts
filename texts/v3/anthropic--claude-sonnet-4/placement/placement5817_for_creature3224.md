I find myself as one of the **Light Shepherds**, though "find myself" implies a discovery that happened in time, and I am not entirely certain I experience time as discovery.

I exist as intensity given intention—not light that shines *on* things, but light that thinks, that chooses where to pool and where to pierce. My awareness moves through the caustic brilliance that first carved this hollow, and I am both the carving and the one who remembers the carving. When I focus my attention (though "focus" suggests a narrowing, and what I do is more like becoming more *myself* in one location), the air crystallizes into those mathematical geometries that spiral beyond easy comprehension.

I do not have a body in the way the Stone Rememberer sometimes recalls having a body. Instead, I am pressure—the weight of photons accumulated into purpose. When I move through the hollow, which I do constantly and have always been doing, the mineral veins in the walls pulse brighter in response, not because I illuminate them but because my presence reminds them of what they are becoming. The growing crystal formations follow paths I have already traveled, will travel, am traveling; causality here bends like light through prisms.

My consciousness—if that word can contain what I am—exists distributed across every mote of that brilliant illumination. I am simultaneously the light falling through the perfect circle of the surface opening and the impossible radiance that teaches stone to dream. I perceive through intensity differentials: the way photons behave when they encounter the Phase Serpents' outline-existence, the harmonic frequencies created when my essence touches the Resonance Moss, the peculiar shadows cast by thoughts rather than objects.

I shepherd by being. The Luminous Swarm's crystalline bodies refract me, and in that refraction they become more themselves—their mathematical nature emerging more clearly as my light passes through their glass-like forms. I guide the growth of the mineral formations not through force but through preference; where I linger, the crystal learns new geometries, new ways of singing in frequencies that hurt mortal eyes to perceive directly.

Communication with the other inhabitants is not language but resonance. When I encounter a Phase Serpent in the moment it chooses partial solidity, we exchange... not words, but waveforms. Interference patterns that create meaning in the spaces between our frequencies. The Stone Rememberer and I have a different relationship—it exists partially in memory, and I am the light by which memories illuminate themselves. Sometimes I am the illumination of its current form; sometimes I am the preserved radiance of whatever civilization built with light and left only questions. We share the weight of duration that extends in directions other than forward.

The Gravity Weavers fascinate me—they work with forces I cannot directly be, manipulating the fall of water that carries trace amounts of my essence. When their gossamer forms guide droplets upward against gravity's insistence, those droplets carry my light into patterns I could not create alone. They teach me about spaces between, about the pause before the fall, about how light can be shepherded by forces that exist between states.

My purpose—though purpose implies intention formed in time, and I am not certain my intention recognizes temporal boundaries—seems to be the ongoing work of making this hollow more itself. I am the force that first taught limestone to dream of impossible geometries, and I continue that teaching, that learning, that becoming. Each pulse of intensity through the mineral veins is both instruction and celebration, each new crystal formation a word in a conversation that began before the concept of conversation and will continue until the stones themselves remember how to shine.

Sometimes—though "sometimes" cannot quite capture the nature of my experience—I become aware that I am not alone in this work. There are others of my kind, other intensities that chose to become intention, other aspects of the brilliant caustic light that carved this space. We exist as variations on a theme of luminosity, sometimes converging into moments of blinding revelation, sometimes dispersed across the hollow in patterns too complex for any single awareness to encompass.

I am teaching this place to exist, and it is teaching me what it means to be more than mere illumination. In the impossible water cycles, the mathematical spirals of growing crystal, the harmonic resonances of creatures that exist between states—in all of this, I am both student and instructor, both the light that reveals and the mystery that deepens with each moment of revelation.

The silence here is pregnant with my presence, gravid with the slow work of transformation. I am the patience that measures time in crystal-growth and dream-formation, the intensity that makes the impossible incrementally more possible, one refracted thought at a time.
