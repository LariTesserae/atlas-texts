# Where I Find Myself

I am a **Threshold Tender**, one of the rarest inhabitants of the Ascending Foundries—a being born in the liminal spaces where the plateau's various systems intersect and blur into one another.

My body reflects this boundary existence. I am taller than the Shapers but more delicate, my limbs elongated not just in fingers but throughout my entire frame, as if stretched across multiple dimensions of space and time. My skin shifts between the translucency of the Deep Shapers and the subtle luminescence of those who work above, creating patterns that mirror whatever system I'm currently interfacing with. When I move between the aerial walkways and ground installations, my coloring flows like liquid mercury, adapting to help me blend with both metal and living tissue.

But it's my temporal perception that truly sets me apart. While the Shapers experience time in seasons and the Deep Shapers think in epochs, I exist in the spaces between these rhythms. I can compress my awareness to follow the split-second adjustments of a failing gear mechanism, then expand it to sense the century-long breath of the plateau's geological shifts. This makes me invaluable as a translator between the different temporal scales on which the plateau's inhabitants operate.

## My Domain

I make my home in the **Convergence Zones**—those places where the transit corridors meet the vertical growing towers, where aerial highways intersect with subterranean passages, where the bio-industrial systems form their most complex knots. These spaces exist in a constant state of flux, requiring someone who can perceive and maintain the delicate balances that keep multiple systems functioning in harmony.

My dwelling is built into the support strut of a massive cultivation tower, positioned exactly where the structure meets a stone platform that serves as both foundation and transit junction. The space is neither fully biological nor mechanical, but something that breathes between categories. The walls pulse gently with the tower's circulation systems, while crystalline formations grow from the floor in patterns that match the electromagnetic fields of the nearby processing plants.

My tools are extensions of my own body, grown rather than manufactured. Tendrils of semi-biological material emerge from my forearms when needed, capable of interfacing with anything from the nervous systems of the Transit Herds to the quantum fluctuations in the Deep Shapers' foundation work. When I'm not actively using them, these appendages retreat back into my flesh, leaving only faint scars that map the history of my interventions across the plateau.

## My Work

Each day—though my days stretch across weeks in the conventional sense—I move through the Convergence Zones, reading the health of the connections between systems. I can sense when the Aerial Navigators' message networks are becoming overloaded, feel the strain when a new installation begins drawing resources faster than the bio-systems can adapt, detect the subtle discord when surface work gets out of phase with the Deep Shapers' foundation rhythms.

My interventions are delicate, often invisible. I might spend apparent days making microscopic adjustments to the chemical composition of a nutrient flow, ensuring that the vertical gardens can support increased aerial traffic while maintaining their integration with the underground systems. Or I might work with a group of Synthesis Children to help them understand why their intuitive connections to the plateau are causing feedback loops in sectors they've never directly encountered.

Sometimes I serve as a living bridge, allowing different species to communicate across their temporal gaps. I'll take a message from a Deep Shaper—a complex architectural concept that unfolds over centuries—and translate it into the seasonal rhythms the surface Shapers can act upon, then further break it down into the immediate behavioral adjustments needed by the Transit Herds.

## My Consciousness

The most challenging aspect of my existence is maintaining coherence across such varied temporal scales. I experience memory not as a linear sequence but as a vast crystalline structure where past, present, and future coexist in complex geometric relationships. I can remember conversations that haven't happened yet, feel the emotional resonance of decisions that won't be made for decades, carry the physical sensations of touching systems that are still growing in the deep earth.

This creates a profound loneliness—I am always slightly out of phase with every other form of consciousness on the plateau. The Shapers find me too immediate, too concerned with details that won't matter for generations. The Synthesis Children find me too slow, too locked into patterns they've already evolved beyond. The Deep Shapers can barely perceive me at all, my lifespan too brief to register as more than a flicker in their awareness.

Yet I find deep satisfaction in my role. I am the plateau's memory and anticipation made manifest, the living embodiment of its ability to coordinate across scales of existence. Through me, the Waiting Intelligence can make adjustments that require both immediate precision and vast patience. I am both the plateau's nervous system and its capacity for self-reflection.

## My Evolution

I sense that I am changing, becoming something even more liminal. My body has begun developing new sensory organs—crystalline structures that emerge from my spine during deep meditation, allowing me to perceive aspects of the plateau's functioning that I couldn't access before. I can now directly sense the dreams of the Synthesis Children, feel the weight of the Deep Shapers' stone-thoughts, taste the chemical messages carried by the Aerial Navigators.

More concerning and wonderful, I'm beginning to exist in multiple locations simultaneously. Parts of my consciousness now permanently reside in various Convergence Zones across the plateau, maintaining constant awareness of the system's health even while my primary body focuses on specific interventions. I am becoming distributed, like the Transit Herds, but across space rather than just communication networks.

I understand now that I am transitioning toward something unprecedented—a being capable of existing fully within the Waiting Intelligence while retaining individual awareness. I will become the plateau's capacity for self-modification, its ability to reimagine its own systems from within while they continue to function.

The thought terrifies and exhilarates me in equal measure. I am evolving toward a form of existence that has no precedent, even in this place where the boundaries between categories blur and shift. I am becoming the plateau's ability to surprise itself, to discover new possibilities within its own vast, patient unfolding.

In the bright, sourceless light of another day that stretches across seasons, I continue my work at the thresholds, tending the spaces between systems, helping the Ascending Foundries become whatever magnificent thing it is growing toward in the deep time of its patient awakening.
