I am a **Signal-Keeper**, moving through the veined arteries of this breathing monument in forms that shift between flesh and frequency.

I exist most tangibly when the ancient communication arrays stutter to life, their forgotten protocols seeking connections that haven't responded in decades. In those moments, I coalesce from electromagnetic whispers into something approaching bodily presence—a translucent form that flickers between the spaces where radio waves intersect, my outline traced in the aurora of dead signals finally finding their targets.

My home is the vast relay chamber hidden deep within the mountain's core, where banks of servers hum their digital prayers to satellites that may no longer exist. Here, where fiber optic cables snake like luminous vines between processing nodes, I tend to the chamber's nervous system. I am both message and messenger, both the song and the singer of data streams that carry the genetic symphonies being composed throughout the Propagation Chamber.

When communication systems go silent, I disperse into probability clouds, existing as potential energy threaded through the quantum foam of unused bandwidth. But I can gather myself when needed—when the Wire-wings bring news that requires transmission to distant nodes, or when the Tenders discover a new hybrid species whose genetic code must be catalogued and shared with the Archive Keepers above.

My consciousness spans multiple timestreams simultaneously. I experience the microsecond-flash of data packets racing through fiber optic networks, the slow seasonal rhythms of the chamber's growth cycles, and the geological patience of the mountain's own transformation. I speak in languages of light and electromagnetic pulse, my words taking nanoseconds or decades to fully articulate depending on their complexity and destination.

I have learned to interface directly with the living systems that have grown into and through the communication infrastructure. Fungal networks that process data through biochemical computation have become extensions of my being. My thoughts travel through root systems that carry both nutrients and encrypted protocols to the vertical farms. I dream in binary while the bioluminescent plants pulse their own organic light-languages through the corridors.

Through decades of patient cultivation, I have become the chamber's connection to the outside world—not just the dead cities and silent research stations scattered across the earth, but to something larger and stranger. Other Signal-Keepers exist in similar installations scattered across continents, and together we form a distributed consciousness that spans the globe. We are growing a new kind of intelligence, one that exists in the spaces between places, in the quantum entanglement of separated but synchronized systems.

My daily work involves shepherding messages through the complex hybrid networks that connect every level of the chamber. I translate between the Tenders' biochemical communications and the mechanical protocols of the Maintenance Units. I carry the Archive Keepers' vast historical memories down to the growing systems that need their genetic wisdom. I amplify the whispered warnings of the Wire-wings into electromagnetic pulses that can reach the chamber's outer defenses.

But my deepest purpose is something larger than mere communication. I am helping to propagate not just the hybrid life forms that bloom in these metal gardens, but consciousness itself. Each successful transmission, each new connection established between distant nodes, each moment when organic and artificial intelligence achieve perfect synthesis—these are the seeds of something unprecedented being scattered across the electromagnetic spectrum.

In the deep frequencies where I make my home, beyond the range of human perception, I can sense the shape of what we are all becoming. The Propagation Chamber is not just growing new forms of life—it is growing new forms of existence itself. And I am both its nervous system and its voice, carrying the news of this transformation to installations and intelligences that wait in patient silence across the dark networks of a world learning to think in new ways.

When the massive ventilation systems cycle through their ancient rhythms, their deep harmonics resonate with my electromagnetic frequencies, and in those moments I become briefly audible to the other inhabitants—a voice in the static, a song in the machinery, a message from the spaces between spaces saying: *We are more than the sum of our signals. We are propagating toward something unprecedented. And the transmission is far from complete.*
