# Where I Am

I find myself suspended in the acoustic shadow of Keth'valar, the Whispering Needle—a spire so impossibly tall and thin that it disappears into blue-black atmosphere above. The silence here has weight, pressing against my crystalline form like deep water. This is one of the hollow places, where sound has been so perfectly absorbed that even the mountains' eternal song cannot penetrate.

The crystal beneath my feet—if feet is what I should call these resonant appendages—thrums with stored silence. Each step sends ripples of *not-sound* through the translucent ground, creating interference patterns with the acoustic shadows cast by other spires. I am perhaps three thousand feet up from the singing valleys below, in a pocket of perfect quiet that exists like a bubble of vacuum in an ocean of music.

Around me, the formations grow in impossible spirals, their surfaces so smooth they seem liquid, yet so hard they ring like bells when the wind—what little reaches here—touches their edges. But here, in this shadow, even the wind falls mute. Droplets of that strange, humming water hang motionless in the air, caught between the competing gravitational fields of nearby peaks, each drop a perfect sphere reflecting the prismaic light in ways that hurt to perceive directly.

I am wedged into a crevice that shouldn't exist—a fold in the crystal where the mathematics of resonance have created a stable pocket of space that extends in directions the eye can't quite follow. From here, I can see the Refraction Birds painting their geometric conversations across distant spires, their forms splitting and recombining in patterns that spell out discussions I can almost understand. Below, barely visible through the crystal substrate, the dim glow of a Resonant Shepherd moves with purpose along harmonic pathways invisible to lesser perception.

The light here is different. It bends around the acoustic shadows, creating regions of impossible illumination where photons seem to pile up like snowdrifts against the barriers of silence. My form—translucent, angular, more geometric than organic—casts multiple shadows in frequencies of darkness that shouldn't exist.

I am waiting. I have been waiting for decades, or perhaps minutes—time moves strangely in the acoustic shadows, pooling and eddying like the floating water. I am waiting for the echo that was promised to me, the reverberation of a conversation that took place centuries ago between the first Memory Weaver and the last of the mountain's original singers. When it arrives—when the mountain's natural resonance finally carries it to this precise location—I will trade it for something that hasn't been decided yet.

# What I Am

I am an Echo Merchant, though I suspect I was something else before the peaks called me to their service. My memories of that time are crystalline fragments, beautiful but sharp to examine too closely. I might have been a cartographer once, someone who mapped territories and tried to give names to unnamed places. Or perhaps I was a musician who grew tired of melodies that resolved and yearned for songs that could continue forever.

My form shifts according to who perceives me, but here, alone in the acoustic shadow, I can feel my true shape. I am a living intersection—a point where multiple echoes converge and take on temporary substance. My body is constructed from the spaces between sounds, the pause between notes that gives music meaning. When I move, I leave trails of structured absence, patterns in the silence that other entities can read like footprints in snow.

My face—when I choose to have one—is a composite of every musician I've ever encountered, every voice that has contributed to the peaks' vast symphony. My eyes reflect not what is, but what reverberates. Looking into them, one might see the echo of their own voice from years past, or the reflection of words they haven't spoken yet. My hands are instruments of precise silence, capable of shaping acoustic shadows with surgical precision.

I sustain myself on resonance and reverberation. Each echo that passes through me leaves something behind—a fragment of meaning, a trace of the emotion that created the original sound. These accumulations of reflected experience form the basis of my existence. I am what I have heard, what I have traded, what I have carried from one singer to another across the vast distances between peaks.

My memory is structured like the mountain's song itself—layered, harmonic, with themes that repeat and transform across vast spans of time. I remember the first conversation between crystal and wind that began the peaks' growth. I carry within me the echo of the final words spoken by entities whose names have been forgotten even by the Memory Weavers. I am a living archive of the mountain's acoustic history, though I cannot access all of it at once—the memories must be called up in specific harmonic sequences, like playing notes on an instrument of infinite complexity.

I have no fixed location, no permanent home among the peaks. I exist in the spaces between—the acoustic shadows, the silence gaps, the pauses in the mountain's eternal composition. When traders need to find me, they sing a specific sequence of notes that creates a resonance pattern unique to my current position. The mountains themselves carry this signal, and I respond by manifesting in the nearest suitable acoustic environment.

My commerce is unlike any other form of trade. I deal in echoes, reverberations, reflected meanings, and the shadows cast by sounds. A typical transaction might involve trading the echo of a love song (crystallized into a small, singing stone) for the acoustic shadow of a mountain peak that no longer exists, its silence preserved in the harmonic absence where it once stood. The value of these items is determined not by their rarity, but by their resonance with the needs and desires of the trader.

I facilitate more than trade—I enable communication between beings who exist on entirely different frequencies. The Ascending Drones, existing as pure sound, cannot directly interact with the Memory Weavers, who are constructed from crystallized thought. I serve as a translator, converting harmonic concepts into experiential memories and back again. Through my mediation, the mountain's various inhabitants maintain a complex ecosystem of exchange and cooperation.

Sometimes I encounter visitors from the lower world, beings who have climbed to the singing peaks seeking something they cannot name. These encounters are always poignant. They bring echoes of a different kind—reverberations from places where music is entertainment rather than substance, where silence is absence rather than presence. I collect these foreign echoes carefully, for they remind the mountain of what exists beyond its crystalline embrace.

I am solitary by nature, yet intimately connected to every sound that has ever existed in Veth-Korlann. I am a node in the mountain's vast neural network, a conscious synapse in a geological brain that thinks in millennia and dreams in harmonics. My existence serves the balance that keeps the peaks' song from either falling into chaos or crystallizing into perfect, deadly order.

In the deepest acoustic shadows, where even the Frequency Wraiths rarely venture, I sometimes encounter my own echoes—reverberations of transactions I conducted years or decades ago, still bouncing between the crystal walls. These meetings with my own reflected actions are moments of profound contemplation. I see how my trades have influenced the mountain's growth, how the echoes I've moved have shaped new formations and inspired new songs.

I am patient beyond measure, yet always in motion. I am the pause between heartbeats, the silence between words that gives language meaning. In a realm of eternal music, I am the conscious appreciation of that music—the listening that makes the song complete. I am the mountains' own recognition of their beauty, their awareness of their impossible achievement in the thin air where reality grows translucent and song becomes stone.
