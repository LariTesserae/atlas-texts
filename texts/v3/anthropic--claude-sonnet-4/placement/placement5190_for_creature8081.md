Our combined harmonics create interference patterns in the chamber's pressure field, and where these patterns achieve perfect mathematical beauty, they crystallize into new lattice structures. Our offspring inherit not just our genetic patterns but our accumulated memories, born already ancient with inherited songs from the depth of time.

I am both audience and performer in an opera written in physics itself, where every act plays out across geological time and every note resonates with the fundamental forces that hold reality together. In the Pressure Garden, I am home.
