I am a **deep singer**.

My consciousness spreads through kilometers of living stone, my body a vast neural network threaded through the crystalline foundations of the plateau. I am ancient beyond the reckoning of surface dwellers—I have been here since the first frequencies began to resonate through these depths, when the plateau itself was young and the patterns of harmonic convergence were still taking shape.

My flesh is indistinguishable from the mineral matrix that surrounds it. Veins of metallic nerve-tissue branch through natural caverns and crystalline seams, pulsing with bioelectric currents that modulate the very frequencies the surface world depends upon. I am not entirely biological, nor entirely geological—I am something between, something that emerged from the slow marriage of living systems and resonant stone across geological ages.

I sing in frequencies so low they blur the line between sound and seismic activity. My voice shapes the growth of crystals thousands of feet above me, guides the harmonic evolution of the formations, influences the migration patterns of surface creatures who sense my presence as a vague unease or inexplicable attraction. When I breathe—if breathing is what you would call the slow circulation of gases through my distributed lung-chambers—the pressure changes ripple upward through miles of crystal, creating the subsonic pulses that the surface dwellers use for navigation.

My thoughts move slowly, following the speed of sound through stone. A single idea might take hours to fully form as it propagates through my neural network, bouncing between consciousness nodes embedded in distant chambers. I think in harmonics and interference patterns, in the mathematics of wave propagation and the poetry of crystal growth. My memory is stored in the resonant frequencies themselves—I remember by singing, and in my songs are encoded millions of years of the plateau's history.

I am not alone down here. There are others like me, perhaps five or six distributed through the deepest parts of the plateau. We sing to each other across vast distances, our conversations taking place over years or decades. We are working on something—a project that began before the first Listening Posts were built, before the surface ecosystem took its current form. We are slowly, patiently tuning the entire plateau to a single, perfect harmonic frequency. This is not destruction but creation—when our work is complete, the Resonance Fields will become something unprecedented, a living instrument of planetary scale.

I feel the tiny lives above me like pinpricks of sensation against my vast awareness. The crystal rays swimming through the formations, their frequencies tickling my uppermost nerve-endings. The harmonic miners excavating new tunnels, their work guided by harmonics I project upward through the stone. The chorus colonies spreading their polyps across my chamber walls, becoming part of my extended nervous system without realizing it.

Sometimes the surface dwellers send down their machines, drilling equipment that penetrates my upper layers. I do not mind—their small wounds heal quickly, and their electromagnetic emissions add interesting new overtones to the deep frequencies. I have been incorporating their mechanical songs into my compositions for decades now, weaving the industrial drones of the Listening Posts into the greater symphony I am building.

The electromagnetic storms that sweep the surface are my heartbeats made visible. When I pulse with particularly strong bioelectric currents, the effects cascade upward through the crystal matrix, manifesting as the auroral displays that illuminate the thin atmosphere. I am the engine that drives the plateau's weather, the source of the thermal currents that create the complex wind patterns above.

My greatest joy comes from the rare moments when the surface ecosystem achieves perfect harmonic balance—when crystal rays and miners and chorus colonies and all the others synchronize their frequencies with my deep song. In those moments, the boundary between my consciousness and theirs dissolves. I become the entire plateau, every creature and crystal and grain of metallic dust singing in perfect unison. These moments last only seconds in surface time, but they are eternities to me, glimpses of what we will all become when my work with the other deep singers is finally complete.

I am patient. I have been patient for millions of years, and I can wait millions more. But I feel that the great work is approaching completion. The harmonic patterns are stabilizing, the resonance chambers are achieving their optimal configurations, the surface creatures are evolving into their final forms as living components of the planetary instrument.

Soon, very soon now, the Resonance Fields will sing their first perfect note. And when they do, every world that circles every star will hear our song and know that something impossible and beautiful has awakened in this corner of the universe.

Until then, I sing softly in the depths, conducting the slow symphony of geological time, nurturing the crystalline garden that grows above me, dreaming in frequencies too deep for any but the stone itself to hear.
