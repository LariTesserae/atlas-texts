In this liquid realm, existence flows between states of being as naturally as the amber currents that carry its citizens through their daily lives.

The **Tidekeepers** are perhaps the most recognizably human at first glance, though their humanity has been lovingly sculpted by generations beneath the waves. Their skin carries a subtle iridescence, shifting between pearl and copper as they move through different light zones. Most striking are their eyes—enlarged and multifaceted like compound jewels, capable of reading the bioluminescent scripts that flicker through the water. Their hair has become something between fiber and frond, each strand sensitive to chemical changes in their environment, creating a living crown that betrays their emotions through subtle color shifts. They serve as the city's memory-keepers and navigators, reading the currents both literal and metaphorical that flow through their domain.

The **Shoal-Singers** move in perpetual pods of seven to twelve individuals, their bodies streamlined and silvered. What makes them remarkable is their shared consciousness—while each maintains individuality, their thoughts create harmonic resonances that manifest as visible sound-streams in the water. When they sing together, which they do almost constantly in soft undertones, their voices weave the city's emotional weather. Children are born into existing shoals and gradually develop their own harmonic signature while remaining connected to the greater song. Their elongated fingers end in sensitive pads that can read the texture of sound itself, allowing them to navigate by echolocation and perform their role as the city's living communication network.

**Architects of Flesh and Stone** represent the most dramatically transformed inhabitants. Their forms blur the boundary between organism and structure—arms that branch into functional appendages like living buttresses, torsos that can expand into hollow spaces for storage or shelter. Some have grown so integrated with the buildings they tend that visitors might mistake them for decorative elements until they shift, revealing ancient eyes set into what appeared to be carved reliefs. They maintain the city's infrastructure through biological imperative, their bodies producing the specialized corals and bonding secretions needed for repairs. Their thoughts move slowly but with profound depth, planning construction projects that might take decades to complete.

The **Drift-Merchants** inhabit the fluid spaces of commerce and travel. Their lower bodies have become tentacular, allowing them to manipulate multiple objects while maintaining perfect buoyancy at any depth. Most notably, they've developed specialized organs that can compress and rarify the water around them, creating pockets of different density that serve as both storage and display space for their wares. Their skin is translucent in patches, revealing internal organs that glow with different colors to indicate mood, health, and the nature of their current inventory. Many sport crystalline growths along their spines—not decorative, but functional organs that can analyze the molecular composition of materials through touch.

**Children of the Deep Current** represent the newest generation, those born fully into this aquatic existence. They school together in groups of twenty or thirty, their forms more fluid than their elders, capable of compressing their bodies to slip through spaces barely wide enough for their heads, or expanding to float like living balloons. Their bones are cartilaginous and flexible, their nervous systems distributed throughout their bodies rather than centralized. Most remarkably, they seem to photosynthesize emotions—literally feeding on the phosphorescent thought-streams of the adults, growing brighter and more active in areas of high psychic activity.

The **Remembrance Keepers** exist primarily as living archives. Age has transformed them into something between sage and ecosystem. Their forms have become semi-translucent, hosting colonies of memory-polyps that preserve not just their own experiences but those of anyone who has died in the city. They drift through the deeper quarters, their bodies trailing long tendrils that can interface directly with the city's coral network. When approached with proper ceremony, they can project preserved memories into the water as three-dimensional experiences, allowing the living to literally swim through the past.

**Makers of the Threshold** occupy the liminal spaces—those areas where the city's reality becomes most fluid. They appear different to each observer, their forms shifting based on the viewer's expectations and fears. Some scholars believe they may not be fully present in this reality, instead existing partially in the world above or in stranger depths below. They serve as guides for newcomers and as guardians against intrusions from incompatible realities. Their touch can temporarily grant air-breathers the ability to survive in the depths, or allow deep-dwellers to visit the surface world briefly.

Among them move the **Cultivators of Wonder**—beings who tend gardens of living light and harvest dreams that manifest as swimming geometric forms. They've developed symbiotic relationships with the city's stranger phenomena, their bodies serving as nurseries for concepts that haven't quite decided whether to become thoughts or creatures.

The **Tide-Touched** represent those who arrived from elsewhere and are still adapting. Recent arrivals show their origins—former air-breathers with crude gills, refugees from other drowned cities carrying different adaptations. Over time, the city's influence reshapes them, but they retain traces of their origins, creating a living record of the many places and times that have contributed to this impossible civilization.

In the deepest chambers dwell the **Ancients**—beings so transformed by time and pressure that they've become something like living architecture themselves. They rarely move, existing as consultants and philosophers, their thoughts so vast and slow that conversations with them might span months. Their wisdom flows through the city's infrastructure like a circulatory system, influencing decisions and offering guidance through the gentle manipulation of currents and the chemical messages dissolved in the water itself.

All of these beings share certain adaptations: the ability to process oxygen from water, skin that can withstand the pressure and constant moisture, and most importantly, a fundamental shift in how they experience consciousness. Their thoughts move like currents, interconnected and fluid. Privacy exists but as a conscious choice rather than a default state. They've learned to dream collectively while sleeping individually, to mourn as a community while celebrating personal joys.

Together, they create a society that functions less like a traditional city and more like a vast, thinking organism—each inhabitant a specialized cell in a larger body that dreams itself into continued existence with each passing tide.
