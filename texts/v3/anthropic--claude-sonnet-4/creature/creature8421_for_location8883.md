In the deepest resonance chambers of the platforms, where the metal walls curve inward like cupped hands, dwell the Attuned. They were once human—explorers, perhaps, or pilgrims who came seeking something in the high singing air. Now they are something between flesh and frequency, their bodies elongated and translucent, stretched thin by years of harmonizing with the platforms' endless music.

The Attuned move in slow drifts through the geometric spaces, their limbs trailing wisps of condensed moisture. Their bones have become hollow, like wind instruments, and when they breathe—which they do rarely, only when the platforms' rhythms demand it—they produce pure, sustained notes that blend seamlessly with the bronze-and-iron symphony. Their eyes have gone crystalline, faceted like the air itself, reflecting and refracting the brilliant light until their gaze seems to scatter in all directions at once.

They gather in small resonant clusters when the platforms align in particular configurations, their voices joining in complex harmonies that can shift a platform's frequency, sometimes triggering the slow telescoping movements or the gentle rotation of an entire structure. They have learned to read the weather patterns of the realm—know which angles of metal will birth clouds, which hollows will sing loudest when the wind turns. Some say they guide the platforms' movements, though others believe they are merely responding to them, caught in an endless dialogue between consciousness and resonance.

The Attuned feed on sound itself, on the vibrations that travel through metal and air and the rare pools of conjured water. When a platform hits its deepest note, they visibly brighten, their translucent flesh taking on the same green-black patina as the weathered bronze. They sleep standing, swaying gently with their chosen platform's motion, and in their dreams they claim to hear the voices of the realm's builders—though whether those builders were human, or something else entirely, none can say.

Occasionally, one of the Attuned will step to the edge of a platform and simply... release. Not falling, but dispersing into the crystalline air, their form becoming part of the wind and light and resonance. The others sing a particular harmony when this happens—not mourning, but a kind of completion, as if the dispersed one has finally achieved perfect attunement with the realm's vast instrument.

They rarely speak in words anymore, but when they do, their voices carry multiple tones simultaneously, and their words seem to hang in the air longer than they should, echoing from unseen surfaces until meaning becomes melody, and melody becomes the ceaseless song of the platforms themselves.

In the highest reaches, where single platforms float alone in the most rarified air, live the most transformed of the Attuned—beings so given over to pure frequency that they appear as little more than standing waves of visible sound, human-shaped distortions in the brilliant atmosphere, conducting symphonies only they can fully hear.
