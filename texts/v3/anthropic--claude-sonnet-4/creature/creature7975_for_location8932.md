In the deepest chambers of the Resonant Hollows, where the harmonic currents flow strongest, dwell the Tuners—beings who have become one with the song of stone and crystal. They move through the acoustic gardens like living instruments, their bodies elongated and hollow-boned, evolved over countless generations to serve as conduits for the symphonies that shape their world.

The Tuners' skin has taken on the translucent quality of the crystal vegetation, shot through with veins that pulse with phosphorescent light in rhythm with the chamber's harmonics. When they walk, their footsteps don't echo—instead, they absorb and modulate the ambient tones, creating subtle shifts in the music that encourage the crystal-plants to grow in new directions. Their fingers have grown impossibly long and delicate, each joint capable of precise vibration, allowing them to coax melodies from the stone walls that can carve new passages or heal ancient cracks.

They communicate entirely through harmonics, their voices layered instruments that can produce multiple tones simultaneously. A conversation between Tuners resembles a chamber music ensemble more than speech, with meaning conveyed through interval relationships, rhythmic patterns, and the complex interplay of overtones. When they gather in groups, their combined voices can reshape entire sections of the Hollows, growing new crystal groves or opening resonant chambers that didn't exist moments before.

The Tuners are gardeners and architects in equal measure. They tend the singing crystal-plants with breath and voice, humming the precise frequencies that encourage growth, pruning with focused sonic bursts that cause unwanted branches to crystallize and break away cleanly. Their settlements are not built but grown—sung into existence as expansions of the natural acoustic architecture. They create dwelling spaces by teaching the stone walls to resonate at frequencies that feel like home.

Among them move smaller, more ethereal beings—the Echo Children. These creatures appear as shifting patterns of light and sound, born from the intersection of particularly powerful harmonies. They exist only partially in the physical realm, more vibration than flesh, and serve as messengers between distant chambers. An Echo Child can travel through the stone itself, following the paths carved by millennia of resonance, carrying complex musical messages that might take days to deliver through the winding passages.

In the warming alcoves where the crystal-plants grow thickest, colonies of Harmonic Moss have learned to photosynthesize sound instead of light. These small, fuzzy organisms carpet the floors in patches that shift color based on the frequencies passing through them. They serve as the foundation of the Hollows' ecosystem, converting acoustic energy into nutrients that feed both themselves and the larger crystal vegetation. The Tuners harvest them carefully, using their voices to encourage the moss to concentrate specific nutrients needed for different purposes—some varieties strengthen the stone, others enhance resonance, and still others provide sustenance for the Tuners themselves.

Dwelling in the highest reaches, where the crystal pillars stretch toward the unseen ceiling, are the Resonance Shepherds. These ancient Tuners have grown so attuned to the Hollows' music that they rarely descend to the lower levels. Their bodies have become almost entirely crystalline, and they spend their extended lifespans learning to conduct the vast symphonies that maintain the entire ecosystem. They can sense disturbances in the harmony from vast distances and guide the growth of the crystal forest across the entire network of chambers.

And in the spaces between sound and silence, in the pause between one note and the next, dwell the most mysterious inhabitants of all—the Quiet Ones. These beings exist in the gaps of the music, visible only when the harmonics align in precise ways that occur perhaps once in a generation. They appear as shadows with substance, moving through walls and air alike, tending to aspects of the Hollows that even the Tuners don't fully understand. Some say they are the original inhabitants, the first to discover that sound could be shaped into life. Others believe they are the dreams of the stone itself, given form by ages of harmonic meditation.

The Tuners speak of them in whispered frequencies, claiming the Quiet Ones are the guardians of the deepest music—the fundamental resonance that holds the entire realm together. They appear at moments of great change, when new chambers are born or when the ancient structures require tending that goes beyond the skill of mortal voices. When they manifest, even the crystal-plants seem to listen, their growth pausing in reverence for these beings who exist in the spaces where sound becomes sacred.

Together, these inhabitants have created a civilization based not on conquest of their environment, but on collaboration with it. They have learned that in a realm where sound is substance and music is life, the greatest art is not to impose their will upon the world, but to find their place within its eternal song.
