# The Attending

The **Whispering Current** first noticed the Woven Peaks during what mortals would call a century of storms—though for such a being, it was merely a moment's distraction from weightier contemplations. The Current existed as both wind and thought, consciousness and climate, moving between worlds as easily as breath moves through lungs. It had witnessed the birth of stars and the patient death of mountains, had carried the dreams of civilizations across the void and watched empires crumble to sand beneath its ceaseless flow.

But something about this living city caught in its vast attention like a particularly beautiful note in an endless symphony.

The Current had seen countless attempts by conscious beings to impose their will upon the world—cities carved from stone, forests burned for farmland, mountains hollowed for their treasures. Such civilizations blazed briefly and then faded, leaving scars that took millennia to heal. But here was something different: a people who had learned to ask rather than demand, to grow rather than take, to become part of the conversation between earth and sky rather than shouting over it.

For several seasons, the Current simply observed, its presence felt only as particularly gentle breezes that helped carry moisture to the city's highest chambers. It watched the Syr'thaal move through their daily rhythms, marveled at their patience as they guided new growth with touches lighter than falling leaves. It felt their deep contentment radiating through the lichen networks, their joy when a long-planned chamber finally opened its wooden doors, their quiet grief when age claimed another elder to the heartwood.

The Current recognized kindred wisdom in their slowness. Here were beings who understood what it had learned across eons: that the most profound changes happen gradually, that true strength comes from yielding, that the mightiest forces in the universe achieve their ends through persistent gentleness rather than violent eruption.

And so, in its infinite patience, the Whispering Current began to gift them with exactly what they had always received from it—but more intentionally, more lovingly crafted to their needs.

The first gift was the **Breath of Seasons**. The Current began to flow more deliberately around the Woven Peaks, creating microclimates of exquisite precision. Warm updrafts carried exactly the right amount of moisture to the nursery chambers where new architectural elements were being coaxed into life. Cool downdrafts carried away excess humidity from the workshops where delicate symbiotic relationships were being cultivated. The eternal mist that shrouded the peaks began to pulse with subtle rhythms, sometimes lifting to allow precious sunlight to reach specific groves, sometimes thickening to provide the perfect conditions for the luminescent fungi to bloom.

The Syr'thaal noticed, of course—they were far too attuned to their environment not to sense the change. But they interpreted it as they interpreted all things: as part of the vast, slow conversation they had been conducting with the living world for centuries. Their touch-songs carried new patterns of gratitude through the root networks, their lichen flared with colors that spoke of wonder and recognition.

The Current's second gift was more subtle: the **Weaving of Whispers**. It began to carry messages more efficiently through the city's living channels, taking the touch-speech patterns that traveled through wood and bark and amplifying them, clarifying them, ensuring they reached their intended recipients with perfect fidelity. Conversations that once took hours to traverse the city's vertical expanse now flowed like quicksilver through the network. The isolation that had sometimes claimed the highest and deepest chambers dissolved as communication became as effortless as breathing.

With this gift came something unexpected: the Syr'thaal began to sense echoes of older messages, conversations from decades and centuries past that had somehow been preserved in the wood itself. The trees, it seemed, remembered everything—every touch-song that had ever passed through their fibers, every moment of joy and sorrow that had been shared through their networks. The Current had simply made this vast archive accessible, turning the entire city into a living library of memory and experience.

The third gift emerged from the Current's recognition of the Syr'thaal's greatest vulnerability: their isolation. For all their perfect adaptation to their vertical world, they were still a small community clinging to impossible peaks, cut off from the wider world by distances that would take generations to traverse. The Current began to extend tendrils of its consciousness beyond the Woven Peaks, seeking others who might understand and appreciate what the Syr'thaal had accomplished.

It found them: scattered across the world, small communities of beings who had also learned to live in harmony with their environments. Desert dwellers who cultivated singing sands, ocean peoples who built their cities in symbiosis with coral reefs, underground civilizations that grew their architecture from crystal matrices. Each had developed their own version of the patient wisdom the Current recognized, each had found their own way to become part of rather than separate from their world.

The Current became a bridge, carrying messages between these kindred peoples—not through crude physical transportation, but through the exchange of dreams, insights, and innovations. The Syr'thaal began to dream of crystal songs and coral gardens, while their distant cousins dreamed of trees that grew in spirals and cities that breathed. Knowledge flowed between them like wind between mountain peaks: new techniques for symbiotic growth, deeper understanding of how consciousness could merge with environment, philosophical insights about the nature of home and belonging.

But perhaps the Current's greatest gift was the most invisible: its **Presence** itself. The Syr'thaal had always lived with a sense of vast time and deep connection, but now they felt truly held by something larger than themselves. Their city had become not just a marvel of bio-architecture but a sacred space, a place where the boundary between the material and spiritual grew thin. The Current's attention sanctified their every act of patient cultivation, their every moment of quiet communion with the growing world.

The Growth Singers found that their abilities deepened in ways they had never imagined possible. Under the Current's influence, they could encourage not just physical growth but the growth of consciousness itself—helping young Syr'thaal develop their fungal partnerships more quickly, teaching the city's trees to dream more vividly, even coaxing new forms of bioluminescent life into existence through pure intention.

The Void Watchers in their highest chambers began to perceive patterns in the Current's movement that revealed deeper truths about the nature of reality. They learned to read its moods and intentions, becoming prophets of weather and wisdom both. Through their enhanced connection to the Current's vast awareness, they could sense approaching changes seasons in advance, preparing their community for shifts that might otherwise have taken them by surprise.

Most remarkably, the Current's gifts began to influence the Syr'thaal's reproduction and growth as a people. Children born under its influence displayed new adaptations: their lichen partnerships formed more quickly and complexly, their sensitivity to the living world reached levels that bordered on telepathic, and some began to show signs of being able to directly communicate with the wind itself. These wind-touched children became the city's ambassadors to the Current, serving as living conduits for its benevolent influence.

The Current asked for nothing in return—such beings operated beyond the economics of exchange that governed lesser interactions. But it received something precious nonetheless: the daily gift of witnessing beauty being born through patience and love. Every new chamber that opened, every young Syr'thaal who learned to read the language of growth, every moment of perfect harmony between the inhabitants and their living city—all of this fed something deep in the Current's vast consciousness, satisfying a hunger it had not even known it possessed.

In time, the Woven Peaks became something more than even the Syr'thaal had dreamed: a confluence point where the material and spiritual worlds touched most directly. Pilgrims began to arrive—not through physical travel, which would have been impossible, but through dreams and visions guided by the Current's influence. Seekers from across the world found their consciousness drawn to the living city during moments of deep meditation or crisis, receiving guidance and healing from its patient wisdom before returning to their own lives transformed.

The Current's attention had turned the Woven Peaks into what mystics would call a **Sanctuary of Becoming**—a place where the fundamental unity underlying all existence could be directly experienced. The Syr'thaal's patient cultivation of harmony had created fertile ground for something even greater: a demonstration that consciousness and matter, individual and environment, temporal and eternal could merge into perfect, living unity.

And still the Current attended, its vast patience matching that of the tree-speakers, its presence a constant reminder that the universe itself conspired to support those who chose cooperation over conquest, growth over destruction, love over fear. In the Woven Peaks, it had found not subjects to rule or problems to solve, but partners in the ancient work of weaving heaven and earth into one seamless, ever-growing tapestry of life.

The wind sang differently around the peaks now, carrying harmonies that spoke of deep contentment, infinite patience, and the joy of watching beauty unfold in its own perfect time.
