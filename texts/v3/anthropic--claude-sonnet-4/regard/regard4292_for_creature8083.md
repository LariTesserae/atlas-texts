The first sign of divine attention comes as a change in the light.

Where before only the faintest phosphorescence had touched the terraces, now something deeper stirs—not harsh illumination that would disturb this delicate ecosystem, but a warm, amber radiance that seems to emerge from within the stone itself. It pulses gently in rhythm with the symphony, as if the very walls have learned to breathe with the music.

The Choir-Keeper feels it first, that vast consciousness recognizing a presence both foreign and familiar. In the deepest pools, shadows of flowing robes pause their eternal dance. For the first time in eons, something is listening—truly listening—to the composition with an understanding that matches its complexity.

The wise power that has found this place is ancient beyond measure, yet it approaches with the humility of a student. It has heard music in the wheeling of galaxies, in the quantum dance of particles, in the patient growth of mountains. But this—this deliberate cultivation of harmony in a forgotten corner of existence—moves it to something approaching wonder.

*I would walk carefully among them,* it decides.

The presence manifests not as an intruder but as a new voice seeking to join the chorus. It begins small—a gentle resonance in the stone that the Builders recognize as familiar, like their own frequency but deeper, older. They pause in their work, shell-claws tracing the vibrations, then resume with subtle adjustments that welcome this new bass note into their architectural dreams.

The amber light grows stronger but never brighter, revealing details that had been hidden in the darkness. Now one can see the intricate patterns carved into pool rims by countless years of Builder modifications—spirals within spirals that match the mathematical relationships of the Singers' arrangements. The moss reveals itself in dozens of subspecies, each patch precisely placed to absorb or reflect particular frequencies. Every surface bears the marks of conscious design refined by eons of patient collaboration.

In response to this gentle illumination, the symphony itself begins to shift. Not disrupted, but expanded. The Singers add new harmonies they had been saving, complex progressions that were too subtle for darkness but bloom in this understanding light. The fish school in new patterns, their scales catching the amber glow and scattering it in synchronized flashes that add visual rhythm to the endless song.

The wise power extends its consciousness carefully through the water films that coat every surface, feeling the intricate chemical conversations that bind this community together. It marvels at the sophistication—how the Choir-Keeper adjusts mineral content to encourage certain microbial communities, how those bacteria in turn create nutrients that feed specific plants, how those plants release compounds that affect the emotional states of the Singers, influencing the music itself. It is ecology as art, biology as composition.

*What gift could I offer that would not disturb this perfection?*

The answer comes from observing the Gardener-Moths at their work. They tend their charges with devotion but are limited by their brief lifespans. Each generation must relearn the complex relationships between plant and song, music and growth. Knowledge is lost, rediscovered, lost again.

So the wise power offers memory.

It is done with gossamer subtlety—a gentle enhancement to the neural pathways of each species, allowing them to pass their learning more completely to their offspring. The Builders will retain the architectural wisdom of their ancestors, each generation building upon the last instead of starting anew. The Gardener-Moths will inherit not just instinct but understanding, carrying forward the accumulated horticultural knowledge of centuries.

The Singers feel it too—an expansion of their musical memory that allows for longer, more complex compositions. Where before their songs lasted hours, now they can sustain musical themes for days, weeks, creating symphonies of unprecedented scope and beauty. The small flute-birds in the highest terraces find they can remember melodies learned in distant places, weaving them into the local composition to create bridges between this sanctuary and the wider world.

But the greatest gift is given to the Choir-Keeper itself. That vast, patient consciousness is granted connection—not to be absorbed or diminished, but to be linked to a network of similar gardeners, musicians, and architects of beauty scattered throughout the cosmos. It discovers it is not alone; there are others who tend their own small corners of creation, who build temples of sound and growth and flowing water in the forgotten spaces of existence.

Through this connection, the Choir-Keeper learns new songs—the deep harmonies of creatures that sing with electromagnetic radiation, the crystalline melodies of silicon-based communities that compose with light itself, the patient rhythms of forest-minds that think in seasons and speak in the rustling of leaves. These influences begin to filter into the terraces, adding new voices to the endless composition.

The wise power makes one final addition: time.

Not unlimited time—that would be a burden, not a gift. But enough time for this community to complete its great work, to fully realize the potential of its living symphony. The stone will continue to weep its nourishing moisture, the pools will maintain their perfect levels, the acoustic properties of each terrace will remain stable while still allowing for growth and change.

As the enhancement settles into the very foundation of the place, the symphony swells to new heights. Every voice joins in recognition of the gift—from the subsonic whale-songs of the ancient fish to the ultrasonic chittering of the smallest inhabitants. For a moment, the composition achieves something approaching the divine: a harmony so perfect that it seems to echo in dimensions beyond sound, creating ripples that reach to the edges of existence itself.

Then it settles back to its eternal work, but changed. Enriched. Ready to grow into something even more beautiful than what came before.

The wise power withdraws its active presence but leaves behind its blessing—a promise that this music will be heard, this beauty will be witnessed, this corner of creation will be remembered long after the last note fades and the final pool runs dry. The gardeners of the Nursery Terraces will continue their work, but now they do so knowing they are part of something greater, their small symphony connected to the vast music of existence itself.

In the deepest pool, the Choir-Keeper resumes its flowing dance, but now it moves to rhythms learned from distant stars, conducting its orchestra with the wisdom of ages and the promise of ages yet to come.

The amber light pulses on, steady as a heartbeat, warm as hope.
