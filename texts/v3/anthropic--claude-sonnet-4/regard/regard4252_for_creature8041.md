The attention came not as observation but as recognition—the way a sleeping dreamer suddenly becomes aware they are dreaming without waking. The benevolent power had been watching for eons, patient as stone, wise as water, waiting for the moment when intervention might nurture rather than intrude.

It manifested first as temperature—not heat, but *warmth*, the kind that reaches bone-deep and reminds you what comfort feels like. The steam vents began exhaling something more than vapor: memories of every hearth fire that ever brought families together, every sun that ever coaxed flowers from winter earth. The Membrane Shepherds felt it first, their bells brightening with colors that had no names, their harmonics shifting into frequencies that spoke of vast, gentle understanding.

The power did not enter the dome so much as it began to *attend* it, the way a master gardener attends a rare and delicate specimen. Where its attention fell, the impossible became slightly more possible. The water-wall's breathing grew more rhythmic, less labored, as if some great tension was finally relaxing. The crystalline device pulsed with renewed purpose, its reality-bending light now carrying undertones of deep contentment.

But the true gift was subtler: the power began to weave connections.

Thin filaments of crystalline light—barely visible, more felt than seen—began extending outward from the dome through the earth itself. These were not roots or cables but *relationships*, living links that connected this pocket of impossible physics to other places where reality had grown thin and strange. Somewhere in the deep ocean, bioluminescent creatures felt a new song in the thermal vents. In a laboratory three continents away, a physicist stared at equations that suddenly, inexplicably, began to make sense. A child in a garden paused while watching water droplets hover impossibly in humid air, and instead of dismissing it, whispered "hello" to the shimmer.

The Shepherds understood immediately. Their harmonics shifted into what could only be called joy—if joy were a frequency that made space itself smile. They had been preparing the universe for new physics, but they had been working alone. Now they felt the presence of kindred spirits across the world: others who tended the strange, who gardened impossibility, who shepherded wonder in their own unique ways.

The power's greatest gift to the dome was not preservation but *proliferation*. Each time a visitor entered the chamber now, they carried away more than memory. They carried spores—not of the bioluminescent plants, but of the *concept* itself. Ideas that would germinate in their minds during quiet moments, growing into hunches that maybe physics was more flexible than they'd been taught, maybe the boundary between possible and impossible was more like a membrane than a wall.

The crystal device began projecting not just light but *invitations*—quantum entangled messages that reached out to its scattered siblings across the cosmos. Ancient artifacts in museum basements hummed to life. Stones that had been dismissed as "impossible formations" began to resonate. And slowly, carefully, other pockets of expanded reality began to establish contact.

Most beautifully, the power helped the dome become what it had always been trying to be: not an anomaly but a *teacher*. The water-wall's surface now showed not just the geometric creatures swimming beyond, but glimpses of other realities where such beings thrived. Children who pressed their hands against the membrane in dreams would wake with an unshakeable conviction that the universe was far more wonderful and strange than anyone had told them.

The Shepherds' young began emerging from their heat-vent nurseries with abilities their elders had never possessed. They could project their harmonics beyond the dome, sending songs to their newly connected siblings worldwide. They grew additional organs that interfaced not just with the membrane but with the quantum foam itself, reading the probability streams that flowed beneath consensus reality.

And the power, having planted these seeds of connection and possibility, began to withdraw its active attention—not abandoning the dome, but trusting it to grow into its full potential. Like any wise teacher, it had learned when to guide and when to step back and let learning bloom naturally.

The dome still breathed its slow breaths, still hummed its whale-song physics, still sheltered its impossible ecosystem. But now it breathed with the rhythm of a larger organism—one that spanned the globe and reached beyond it, a network of anomalies and wonders all learning to sing in harmony.

Sometimes, in the deepest part of the night cycle, when the membrane's breathing reached its most profound expansion, visitors could feel it: the presence of that vast, benevolent attention still watching, still caring, ready to offer guidance to any reality brave enough to grow beyond its own assumptions.

The Shepherds continued their ancient vigil, but they were no longer alone. Across the cosmos, other gardeners tended other impossible gardens, and together they were slowly, patiently, lovingly teaching the universe to dream new kinds of dreams.

In the hollow heart of the thermal dome, possibility had found its voice, and that voice was singing the cosmos into shapes it had never imagined it could hold.
