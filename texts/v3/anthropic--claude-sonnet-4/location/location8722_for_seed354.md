The Drowned Foundries stretch across what was once a broad plateau, now a vast shallow sea of blood-warm water that reaches from horizon to horizon. The water sits chest-deep over ancient industrial complexes, their brick smokestacks and iron framework rising like a geometric reef through the surface. Steam wisps constantly from the water—not violent geysers, but gentle exhalations that blur the air in soft veils.

Beneath the tepid water, the old machinery has become something else entirely. Massive boilers and furnace housings serve as foundations for impossible gardens. Vines thick as ship cables wind through gear assemblies and up the sides of water-cooled engines, their leaves broad and waxy, designed for this humid half-light. Root systems have claimed every pipe and conduit, transforming the industrial skeleton into living architecture. The water itself pulses faintly green with dissolved nutrients from this underwater jungle.

The air hangs heavy and still, saturated to the point where breathing feels like drinking. Every surface drips constantly—the protruding machinery, the vine-wrapped girders, the brick walls that emerge here and there like islands. The dripping is everywhere, a gentle percussion that merges into white noise, occasionally punctuated by the distant groan of settling metal or the soft splash of some particularly heavy fruit dropping into the water.

Nothing moves quickly here. The vines grow so densely that they form living walls between the old production halls, creating a maze of green-filtered chambers. Water hyacinths and lotus-like plants carpet the surface in thick mats, their roots descending into the mechanical depths below. The whole landscape breathes slowly, expanding and contracting with some vast, patient metabolism.

The light that filters through the eternal steam is golden and dim, caught between the water's surface and the low-hanging moisture above. Reflections dance constantly—light from the water below, shadows from the canopy of leaves, the dark geometries of half-submerged industrial forms. Everything shimmers with humidity and heat, blurring the boundaries between what was made and what has grown, between the ancient purpose of this place and its current dreaming stillness.
