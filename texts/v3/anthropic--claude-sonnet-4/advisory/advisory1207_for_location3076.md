# Preparation Guide for Keth Mirallos

## Physical Conditioning and Health Preparations

**Altitude Acclimatization**: Begin high-altitude training at least 6-8 weeks before departure. Spend time at progressively higher elevations (starting at 8,000+ feet) to build red blood cell count and lung capacity. The thin air of Keth Mirallos will be compounded by industrial particulates and the strange atmospheric effects of the growing mountain.

**Respiratory Protection**: Pack multiple high-grade respirators rated for both altitude and industrial environments. The metallic taste in the air suggests particulate contamination from the foundries. Bring extra filters—the moisture and fungi may clog them faster than expected. Consider a full-face respirator for extended exposure to the industrial districts.

**Balance and Proprioception Training**: The shifting geology and unpredictable spatial distortions require exceptional body awareness. Practice balance exercises on unstable surfaces. Learn to walk on moving platforms and develop comfort with vertigo-inducing environments. The cable systems and bridges will demand sure footing on surfaces that may shift unexpectedly.

## Navigation and Spatial Orientation

**Abandon Traditional Mapping**: GPS devices and standard compasses will be unreliable. Instead, develop skills in landmark-based navigation using industrial features (smokestacks, major buildings) that seem more stable than natural formations. Learn to navigate by sound—the industrial rhythms appear more consistent than visual cues.

**Time Perception Training**: Practice exercises that maintain awareness of time passage without relying on external cues. Bring multiple mechanical timepieces (digital may malfunction) and check them frequently. Keep a detailed log of activities to track temporal anomalies.

**Mental Mapping Techniques**: Study memory palace techniques to maintain spatial awareness when distances fold unpredictably. Create mental anchors based on the industrial sounds and smells, which seem more constant than visual references.

## Equipment and Supplies

**Modular Shelter**: Bring lightweight, rapidly-deployable shelter that can be moved quickly if the ground shifts. Avoid permanent camping setups. Your tent should handle both sudden moisture and the luminescent fungi that may try to colonize it.

**Water and Moisture Management**: Pack extensive water purification capabilities—the weeping rock and intermittent rain may not provide safe drinking water. Bring waterproof everything; the constant moisture will penetrate normal sealing. Desiccants will be crucial for maintaining equipment.

**Multi-Tool Philosophy**: Every piece of equipment should serve multiple purposes. The unpredictable environment may require improvised solutions. Bring extensive repair supplies—the shifting geology may damage equipment in unexpected ways.

**Redundant Communication**: The spatial distortions may interfere with standard communication methods. Bring multiple backup systems including signal flares, mirrors, and mechanical noisemakers. Consider learning the local industrial whistle codes used by workers.

## Psychological Preparation

**Unreality Tolerance Training**: Spend time in environments that challenge your sense of reality—funhouses, optical illusion exhibits, virtual reality. Build comfort with disorienting experiences. Practice maintaining rational thought when sensory input conflicts with expectations.

**Industrial Rhythm Adaptation**: Listen to recordings of heavy industrial sounds for extended periods before arrival. The constant machinery noise will be overwhelming initially. Some visitors report the subsonic rumbling causes anxiety or disorientation—practice meditation techniques that work with rhythmic background noise.

**Time Distortion Coping**: Practice mindfulness techniques that maintain present-moment awareness. The blurred line between work and rest, combined with backwards-running clocks, can cause severe psychological disorientation. Develop personal rituals that mark time passage independent of external cues.

## Social and Cultural Preparation

**Worker Integration**: Study industrial safety protocols and basic mechanical knowledge. The locals are workers first—showing competence and understanding of their environment will earn respect and assistance. Learn to read industrial warning signs and safety signals.

**Dream Logic Acceptance**: Residents operate under different logical frameworks. Don't challenge reports of impossible events—instead, ask practical questions about how to navigate them. Focus on "how" rather than "why" when discussing the city's anomalies.

**Shift Work Mentality**: Adapt to thinking in terms of shifts rather than days. The industrial rhythm supersedes natural time cycles. Prepare to eat, sleep, and work according to factory schedules rather than circadian rhythms.

## Specialized Precautions

**Wildlife Protocols**: The unusually intelligent animals require different interaction approaches. Never assume familiar species will behave predictably. The metallic-horned goats and organized small mammals suggest enhanced cognition—treat them as you would unknown intelligent species. Have protocols for the bear-machine hybrid sightings.

**Spatial Anomaly Navigation**: Develop strategies for when familiar buildings suddenly contain new rooms or when distances unexpectedly change. Always maintain a mental inventory of your location relative to multiple landmarks. Consider carrying breadcrumb-style trail markers.

**Industrial Safety Plus**: Standard industrial safety is insufficient here. The machinery is synchronized to something beyond human schedules and may operate according to its own logic. Never assume equipment is off just because it appears inactive. The stories of workers completing unremembered jobs suggest possible altered consciousness during work periods—maintain detailed personal logs.

## Exit Planning

**Multiple Departure Routes**: Map several ways off the mountain before exploring deeply. The shifting geology may close your planned exit route. Identify both mechanical transport (cable cars, roads) and on-foot evacuation paths.

**Condition Monitoring**: Establish check-in protocols with the outside world. The time distortions and altered consciousness reports suggest visitors may lose track of their intended stay duration. Have external contacts authorized to extract you if you exceed planned visit time.

**Decompression Protocol**: Plan for gradual readjustment to normal reality. The shift from impossible geometry and living mountains back to static environments can be psychologically jarring. Budget time for mental adjustment in a neutral environment before returning to regular life.

## Essential Mindset

Approach Keth Mirallos as a living entity rather than a mere location. The mountain dreams of completion, the city grows to match, and human activity serves as both catalyst and participant in something larger than simple industrial production. Your role is not to understand or control this process, but to navigate it respectfully while maintaining your essential humanity.

Success in Keth Mirallos comes not from imposing external logic on its impossibilities, but from developing the flexibility to work within its unique parameters while keeping your core identity intact. The city has made peace with impossibility—you must do the same, temporarily, without losing yourself in the process.
