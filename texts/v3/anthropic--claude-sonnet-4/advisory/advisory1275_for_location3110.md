# Preparation Guide for the Crystalline Confluence

**WARNING: This is a Class-VII Reality Anomaly site. Standard physics, biology, and temporal mechanics do not apply.**

## Essential Equipment

### Visual Protection
- **Prismatic filtering goggles** - The geometric light formations cause severe optical strain and potential retinal scarring. Standard sunglasses are insufficient. You need specialized equipment that can filter non-Euclidean light spectra while allowing navigation.
- **Peripheral vision blockers** - The mathematical spiral formations can trigger seizures or psychological breaks when tracked visually. Limit your field of view deliberately.
- **Reality anchor headset** - Essential for maintaining spatial orientation when shadows exist without light sources.

### Atmospheric Gear
- **Sealed breathing apparatus** - The "moisture that appears from nothing" may contain exotic particles or temporal fragments that human lungs cannot process safely.
- **Climate-controlled suit** - Temperature regulation is unpredictable when water flows upward and reality shifts between states.
- **Electromagnetic field generator** - Personal shield against the caustic light and potential reality distortion effects.

### Navigation Equipment
- **Quantum compass** - Standard magnetic compasses will fail where space-time is unstable.
- **Tethering system** - High-tensile cable anchored to stable reality outside the site. Mark every meter with reality-verification tags.
- **Emergency extraction beacon** - GPS will not function; you need quantum-entangled emergency location broadcasting.

## Biological Precautions

### Medical Preparation
- **Temporal stabilization medication** - Consult a multiverse medical specialist for drugs that prevent cellular temporal displacement when time "moves differently."
- **Neural pattern locks** - Brain wave stabilizers to prevent consciousness fragmentation when observing shifting architectural elements.

- **Never touch the translucent creatures directly** - They exist in multiple states simultaneously; contact could phase-lock your molecular structure.
- **Avoid the upward-falling water** - This defies causality and may trap you in temporal loops.
- **Do not attempt to photograph the mineral formations** - Cameras cannot capture non-Euclidean geometry and may experience explosive failure.

## Mental Preparation

### Psychological Conditioning
- **Meditation training focused on accepting paradox** - Your mind will try to rationalize impossible phenomena and may break under the strain.

- **Memorize fundamental constants** - Recite the speed of light, gravitational constant, etc. when reality becomes unstable.
- **Emergency mantras** - Simple phrases that reinforce your original dimensional coordinates.

## Entry Strategy

### Timing
- **Enter during local solar noon** - The contrast between ordinary daylight above and anomalous light below provides orientation reference.
- **Limit exposure to 47 minutes maximum** - Longer exposure risks permanent displacement from consensus reality.
- **Plan extraction for when the mineral formations are in their dormant growth phase** - Usually recognizable by decreased clicking sounds.

### Movement Protocols
- **Stay close to the walls but don't touch them** - The center of the depression appears to be where space-time distortion is most severe.
- **Move in straight lines only** - Curved paths may lead you into dimensional folds.
- **Never turn your back on the serpentine entities** - They seem benign but exist partially outside normal space.

### Documentation
- **Audio recording only** - Voice notes are the only reliable way to document findings.
- **Sketch with graphite pencils** - Digital devices malfunction; avoid pens as ink behaves strangely here.
- **Temperature and EM readings** - May provide data on reality stability levels.

The "patient work of teaching stone to dream" suggests an ongoing process rather than a static anomaly. Future travelers should expect the site to evolve, potentially becoming either more stable and safe, or more dangerously alien.
