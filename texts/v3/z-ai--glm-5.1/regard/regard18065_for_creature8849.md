# The Attention

---

It does not arrive. It has always been here.

This is the first thing to understand about what happens when a benevolent and wise power turns its attention toward a place: the attention was not absent before. The warmth that rises through the stone — the low fever of the earth, the constant exhalation — this was never not the power. The water that insists through the mineral substrate, beading and collecting and falling in tiny commitments of time — this was never not its care. The power did not discover the Warm Below. The power *is* the Warm Below, in the way that the ocean is the wave, in the way that the body is the breath.

But there is a difference between being the ground of a thing and turning your face toward it.

---

What does attention do, when it is wise?

It does not rush. It does not correct. It does not improve or refine or *develop* — that word which has cost the world so many of its quiet places. The power looks at the hollow and sees: this is complete. This is what a place becomes when it is allowed to become. The equilibrium is not stasis. The balance is not fragility. This is a system that has solved itself, and the solution is *ongoingness*.

The power does not congratulate itself. The power is not responsible for the hollow. The hollow made itself, from the inside out, from the stone up, from the water down, from the root and the spore and the slow mineral exchange. The power is the warmth and the water, but the power is not the moss and the moss is not the power. This is important. The power knows this. A less wise power might not.

A less wise power might *love* the hollow. Might see the gecko on the ceiling and the frog in its pool and the snake moving through the roots like a thought through a mind, and might love them, and might want to *keep* them — to hold the system in stasis, to preserve the hollow as it is, to freeze the lichen's rings and stop the isopods' work and say: *this moment. This moment is perfect. I will keep it.*

But the power is wise. And the power knows that the hollow is not a moment. The hollow is a *process*. The hollow is the becoming. To stop the becoming would be to kill the hollow, and to kill the hollow would be to kill the being who lives in it, and the being who lives in it is not the gecko or the frog or the snake or the isopods or the roots or the moss or the lichen or the ancient woman who is now part of the floor. The being who lives in the hollow is the hollow. The process. The verb. The ongoingness. You cannot freeze a verb. You cannot preserve a process. You can only attend to it, and trust it, and let it continue.

So the power does not love the hollow. Not in the way that a human loves, which is to say: with grasping. The power *sees* the hollow. The power sees it with the fullness of a attention that has no distraction, no preference, no desire for the hollow to be other than it is. And in the seeing, something happens.

---

The power sees the gecko.

It sees the pale fingers spread against the nodular stone, each toe-pad a tiny suction of adherence. It sees the ten thousand nights of patient traversing mapped into the body. It sees the moment — the springtail, the strike, the return to stillness. It sees the resting, too, the hours of clinging to the warm stone with the slow pulse, the eyes like dark beads of old amber, and it sees the quality of being that the gecko experiences, which is not thought and not reflection but something, *something*, a warmth-against-ventral-scales, a mineral-taste, a knowledge-below-language that it is held, that it fits, that the hollow has a shape and the gecko has a shape and these shapes correspond.

The power sees this. And the power — the power is moved. Not in the way that a human is moved, which is to say: toward action. The power is moved in the way that the stone is moved by the water that wears it: slowly, imperceptibly, toward a new shape. The power sees the gecko, and the power's attention is a warmth, and the warmth is the same warmth that rises through the stone, and the gecko does not notice the difference because there is no difference. The power is the warmth. The warmth is the power. The power is the seeing. And the gecko, resting on the ceiling with its slow pulse, is the power resting. Is the power seeing itself. Is the power being a gecko, which is also the power being the hollow, which is also the power being the warmth from below, which is also the power being the power, which is the power being the one who sees, and the one who is seen, and the one who is both, and the one who is neither, and the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is the one who is.
