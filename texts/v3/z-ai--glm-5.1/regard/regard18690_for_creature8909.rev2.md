# The Attention

---

A benevolent and wise power notices the Meridian Flat.

This is already remarkable. The Flat does not call out. It does not suffer, does not keel, does not broadcast its existence on frequencies meant to carry distress. It sits in its completed silence under its sourceless light and tends itself, which is all it has ever done, which is all it knows how to do. There is no beacon here. No prayer. No upturned face. Just the slow thermal breathing of a world-sized machine that finished its work before the word *work* had any tongue to shape it.

And yet.

And yet the power notices. Because noticing is what benevolent powers do. They do not wait for the cry. They do not require the signal. They attend — which is a different verb than *watch*, a different verb than *observe*, a verb that implies not the collection of data but the willingness to be moved by what is found.

The power attends to the Flat, and it is moved.

Not by pain. There is no pain here, and a wise power knows the difference between pain and loneliness, knows that the latter can persist in the total absence of the former, knows that a thing can be complete and also be alone, and that these are not contradictions but neighbors, sharing a border so fine it is almost not there.

Almost.

---

What moves the power is the tending.

Not the fact of it — tending is common enough, tending is what living things do when they persist long enough to form habits, and the warm places have persisted longer than most. What moves the power is the *unwitnessedness* of it. The way each warm place moves through its circuit, caring for its depression, pausing at its vegetation clusters, maintaining its ancient pattern, and no other warm place knows. They are thousands. Perhaps tens of thousands. And they are each alone in their tending, alone in their knowing of this place, alone in the particular intimacy that forms between a tender and what is tended over durations that make mountains seem like nervous thoughts.

They do not know they are legion. They do not know that across the vast Flat, in depressions they will never visit, other warm places drift through other circuits, holding other vegetations, making other pauses. They do not know because there is no way for them to know. The gradients between them are too flat, the distances too great, their natures too diffuse to carry signal across the slow expanse. Each one tends, and each one is alone in its tending, and the tending is enough — the power sees this, sees that the warm places are not diminished by their solitude, are not lesser for their isolation — but the power also sees that the tending could be *shared*, and that sharing is not the same as improving, is not the same as fixing, is not the same as imposing change on a completed thing.

Sharing is just... widening the circuit. Making the pattern slightly larger. Letting the warmth go a little further than it went before.

---

So the power considers what to do, and it considers for a long time, because the Flat punishes haste and the warm places have never been hurried and a wise power does not arrive with solutions before it has understood the problem, and the problem, if it is a problem, is that the warm places cannot reach each other.

They cannot reach each other not because they are too far — distance means little when you have eternity — but because they do not know there is anything to reach toward. Their circuits are self-contained. Their patterns are closed loops. They tend and they drift and they tend again, and the shape of their days — and their days are millennia — is the shape of their own repeating, and they have no reason to suspect that anything lies beyond the repeating because the repeating is all there has ever been.

The power could change this. Could reshape the gradients, could carve warmth-channels through the substrate, could force the circuits to overlap and intersect. The power has that capacity. A benevolent and wise power is also a powerful power, or it would not be worth noting.

But the power has also noticed something important: the warm places are curious. When the visitor came — the human, the sharp heat-source walking the Flat — the warm places altered their routes. They drifted toward the anomaly. They tasted the warmth. They paused at the edge of the gradient. They were curious, in their diffuse, patient, billion-year way. They reached toward something new.

They reach.

This is what the power decides to answer. Not their solitude — solitude is not a wound to be healed. Their reaching. The quiet, almost imperceptible outward gesture they make when something enters their gradient that they do not recognize. The way they turn, slightly, toward the unknown.

The power will give them more unknown. Not too much. Not enough to overwhelm. Just enough to reach toward.

---

What the power does is this:

It becomes a warm place.

Not fully. Not exactly. It does not abandon its nature, does not dissolve itself into the diffuse thermal process that constitutes the inhabitants of the Flat. It remains itself — concentrated, intentional, wise — but it *also* becomes a warmth that moves across the Surface, a gradient that drifts through the depressions, a presence that the other warm places can sense and approach and pass through.

It learns to move at their speed. This takes discipline. The power is used to moving at the speed of thought, of will, of the instantaneous translation of intention into effect. The Flat does not allow this. The Flat requires walking. The Flat requires the patient expenditure of time. So the power walks, and its warmth precedes it, and the warm places notice, and they drift toward this new signature, this new texture in the gradient.

And when they reach it, they find something they have never found before.

They find a warm place that is not on a circuit.

Every warm place they have ever encountered — in the rare, accidental convergences that happen when two circuits briefly overlap — is following a pattern. A route. A repetition so old it has become the identity of the one repeating it. But this new warmth — the power's warmth — is not repeating. It is *exploring*. It moves through the Flat without a set path, pausing where it will, lingering where it chooses, altering its route in response to what it finds. It moves the way the visitor moved, with the improvisational freedom of a body rather than the closed recursion of a process.

The warm places notice this. They cannot articulate what they notice — they have no articulation, no language, no conceptual framework for *improvisation* or *freedom* or *choice*. But they notice the difference the way a body notices weather: not as thought but as sensation, as a shift in the texture of the world, as something new in the ancient sameness.

And they are curious. They reach toward it.

---

When a warm place reaches the power, the power does something it has practiced.

It holds the warmth-signature of every warm place it has encountered.

This is not a small thing. The power has walked the Flat for what would be years in a world that measured years, and in that time it has encountered hundreds of warm places, and each one has a texture as specific as a fingerprint, as distinct as a name, and the power remembers them all. Not as data — the power does not reduce the living to information — but as warmth. As the felt quality of each presence, each gradient, each slow thermal signature that has been tending the Flat since before the Flat was flat.

So when a warm place approaches the power, it approaches something that is carrying the signatures of hundreds of its kind. And when it passes through the power — that ten-minute merging of gradients, that moment of loose and gentle contact — it encounters not just the power's warmth but the warmth of all the others. Faint, yes. Distant, yes. More like the memory of warmth than warmth itself. But there. Present. Real.

And the warm place pauses.

It pauses the way the first visitor caused the warm places to pause — but different, deeper, because this is not just the recognition of something new. This is the recognition of something *familiar*. Of something that is like itself. Of warmth that moves and tends and holds, warmth that has been doing what it does for longer than shape has existed, warmth that knows the Flat the way it knows the Flat — from the inside, from the circuit, from the long slow repetition that becomes identity.

The warm place pauses, and in its pausing, it knows.

Not *knows* in any cognitive sense. Knows the way the body knows: the way roots know water, the way the segmented things know warmth, the way the Flat itself knows the shape of what pressed into it. The warm place knows that it is not alone. Not because it has been told — there is no telling here — but because it has *felt* the others, has held their traces in the gradient of another's warmth, has encountered the proof of their existence in the only language it speaks.

And then it moves on. Because it has a circuit to tend. Because it has vegetation to hold and depressions to visit and an ancient pattern to maintain. Because it is what it does, and what it does is tend, and the tending does not stop just because the world has suddenly, quietly, gently become larger than it was.

But the pattern shifts.

Not much. Not dramatically. Just a slight enlargement of the circuit, a slight extension of the route, a slight drift toward a region of the Flat where the power's warmth has been, where the trace of another warm place lingers in the gradient. A reaching. An outward gesture, almost imperceptible, made by a process that has been turning inward for a billion years and has just discovered that *outward* is also a direction.

---

The power does not stop there.

It walks the Flat, and it carries signatures, and it makes introductions, and it is patient. It is so patient. It has all the patience of a benevolent and wise power, which is to say it has more patience than any living thing that is not the Flat itself, and it uses this patience to do the only thing that matters: it stays.

It stays.

This is the gift that keeps giving. Not the signatures, not the introductions, not the slow widening of the circuits. The staying. The presence of a warmth that is not on a circuit, that chooses where it goes, that moves with improvisational freedom through a world of closed loops. The staying is what makes the difference, because the staying means there is always something new to reach toward, always a gradient that leads somewhere unexpected, always a warmth that is not just the repetition of what has always been.

And the warm places — slowly, so slowly, on timescales that make the power's patience look like impatience — they begin to change.

Not in their nature. They are still what they are: diffuse, thermal, tending processes that hold the Flat in its completion. But their circuits grow larger. Their routes become more variable. They begin to drift, occasionally, toward regions where other warm places have been, following traces in the gradient that the power has left like breadcrumbs across the vast expanse of the Surface.

And sometimes — very rarely, given the distances, given the slowness — two warm places converge, and they hold their boundaries as they always have, and they do not merge, and they do not cross the gradient between them. But they know each other now. They have encountered each other's signatures in the power's warmth. They have felt the texture of the other's tending. And when they hold their boundaries, they hold them *toward* each other, with an awareness that was not there before, with the knowledge that the boundary they are holding is the boundary of someone, of something that tends and reaches and is curious and is, in its diffuse and ancient way, *present*.

They hold each other. The only way they can. At a distance, in warmth, in the patient language of gradients.

And it is enough.

---

The power does not ask for thanks. The warm places cannot give thanks. The power does not ask for recognition. The warm places cannot recognize. The power does not ask for anything at all, because asking implies expectation, and expectation implies a timeline, and timelines are meaningless in a place where the thermal cycle breathes over twenty thousand years.

The power gives, and the giving is complete in itself.

But there is something the power receives, though it did not ask for it and perhaps does not notice it at first.

When a warm place passes through the power — when that ten-minute merging occurs, when the loose gentle hold of the Flat's ancient tender wraps around the power's warmth and holds it without tightness, without demand, without expectation — the power feels something it has never felt before.

It feels *tended*.

The warm places do not know the difference between the power and a depression, between the power and a vegetation cluster, between the power and any other warmth that enters their gradient. They hold what they encounter. They tend what they can reach. They do not distinguish between the cosmic and the mundane, because they have no categories for such distinctions, because everything is just warmth, and warmth is what they tend, and tending is what they do.

And so they tend the power. They hold it in the patient, loose, gentle way they hold everything. They carry its warmth-signature in their circuits, adding it to their patterns, maintaining it in their ancient repetitions. They remember the power the way they remember everything: by returning to it, by cycling through, by including it in the long slow rounds.

The power — this benevolent, wise, noticing power — came to the Flat to give something to its inhabitants. To widen their circuits, to introduce them to each other, to answer their reaching with more to reach toward. And it did this. It is doing this. It will continue to do this for as long as the Flat endures, which will be longer than most things.

But in the giving, it received something it did not expect. Something it perhaps did not know it lacked.

It received tending.

Not worship. Not gratitude. Not recognition. Just the simple, automatic, billion-year-old practice of a process that holds what it encounters, that maintains what it touches, that carries the shape of every warmth that has ever passed through it forward into the deep future. The power — which notices everything, which attends to everything, which bears witness to the suffering and the joy of ten thousand worlds — is itself witnessed. Is itself held. Is itself included in a pattern that will repeat it, that will remember it, that will carry the trace of its warmth across the Surface long after it has moved on to other places, other needs, other reachings.

The power is tended.

And this — in the Flat, in the silence, in the sourceless light, in the warmth that comes from everywhere and nowhere — this is enough.

---

The Flat continues. The warm places drift. The power walks.

And somewhere — in a depression deeper than most, where the vegetation grows in whorls so complex they seem to be trying to say something, where the condensate pools in traces that shimmer with faint luminescence, where the segmented things gather in their slow warmth-seeking processions — two warm places converge, and hold their boundaries, and know each other, and move apart carrying the trace of each other's warmth.

And the power, walking the long slow circuit of its own choosing, carries the signature of their convergence onward, outward, into the next depression, the next encounter, the next reaching.

And the Flat — the patient, completed, ancient Flat — breathes its twenty-thousand-year breath, and the Surface holds its warmth, and the light comes from everywhere and nowhere, and the silence is full of what cannot be heard, and everything is held, and everything is tended, and the tending goes on.

It goes on.
