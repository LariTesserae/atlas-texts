# What the Power Did

---

First: nothing.

First always: nothing. This is what wisdom means. It means *shutting up*. It means *sitting still*. It means arriving at a place of howling fecundity, a place that has solved the problem of persistence in a way your categories cannot parse, and *withholding your categories*. It means pressing your benevolence against your palate like a stone you might swallow and *not swallowing*. It means waiting.

The power listened.

Not the way you listen, filtering for pattern, extracting for meaning. The power listened the way a membrane listens: by being permeable, by being affected, by letting the vibration pass through and noting what changed on the other side. The power let the Churn teach it the texture of its own noise. The roaring, clicking, groaning, shrieking composite. The substrate of leg-clicks beneath the larger sounds. The wet-sack wringing. The bass-calls that moved in sternums. The power did not organize these into hierarchy. It let them be what they were: a single sound, fractal, each layer containing all other layers, the acoustic signature of a world building itself.

The power listened for a long time. Longer than you would think patience permits. Longer than the gathered ones had been carving notches into root. Longer than the current configuration of the Churn had been churning. The power listened until the sound became not-noise, not in the sense of making sense, but in the sense of revealing its own interior—the way a wall of water reveals, upon sustained attention, the currents within it, the logic of turbulence, the strange attractors around which chaos organizes itself without ceasing to be chaos.

The power understood: this place is not broken. This place is not in need of fixing. This place is a solution so complete that it has become its own problem, its own context, its own ongoing answer to a question it is continuously formulating. To intervene here would be to answer a question that hasn't been asked, to solve what doesn't need solving, to impose shape on something whose shape is *becoming*.

The power almost left.

Almost.

---

But there was the matter of the notches.

The power had noticed them the way the gathered ones noticed each other: as shifts in density, as folds in the pattern, as places where the churn had been *interrupted*—not stopped, never stopped, but paused, marked, made to say *something happened here*. The notches in living root. The woven platforms. The fungal-brick walls returning to mycelium. The structures that served no purpose the power could identify—walls blocking nothing, tunnels to solid vegetation, arrangements that seemed to *mean*.

The power understood what the gathered ones were doing. They were not building shelter. They were not constructing infrastructure. They were not, in any surface-dweller sense, *improving* their conditions. They were writing. They were writing in a medium that digested writing, in a language that consumed its own records, in a script whose pages were alive and hungry and could not stop eating what was written on them.

They were making meaning in a place where meaning was food.

And the power saw: this cost them. Not in the way that building costs surface-dwellers—labor, material, time. This cost them in the way that speaking costs a voice that cannot stop singing: erosion, thinning, the gradual wearing of the instrument by its own use. Every structure the gathered ones built was a structure they had to rebuild. Every notch they carved was a notch that healed over and needed carving again. Every meaning they made was consumed and needed making again. The churn gave them no archive. The churn gave them no duration. The churn gave them no silence in which to consider what they had said before it was eaten.

The gathered ones were not suffering. This was important. This was crucial. The power could see this clearly: the gathered ones were not in pain, not in the surface-dweller sense. They were not tragic. They were not fallen. They were not waiting to be saved from their condition. They were negotiating with their condition, and the negotiation was their life, and the life was sufficient.

But they were losing something. Not something they had—something they were making. Something that required accumulation, required duration, required the persistence of pattern across time. They were making *culture*. And culture cannot be made entirely in the present tense. Culture requires memory. Not the memory of a single gathered one—those merged and split and exchanged, their continuity was pattern, not substance. But the memory of the gathered: the corpus, the record, the accumulated not-carvings and built-nothings and vibrational grammars that said *this is what we have been, this is what we have tried to say, this is where our meaning has reached so far*.

The Churn digested it. The Churn digested it as fast as it was made. And the gathered ones made it again, and again, and again, and each time it was as if they were beginning from zero, or from whatever fragments of pattern survived in the vibrations they passed between them, contagion of state, state that could not accumulate because there was nowhere for it to settle.

The power understood: they do not need rescue. They need resonance.

---

What the power did was subtle. Subtle the way a change in temperature is subtle—not visible, not audible, but altering every process it touches. Subtle the way a single harmonic added to a chord changes the chord's emotional valence without changing its notes. Subtle the way meaning is subtle: impossible to measure, impossible to prove, undeniable once felt.

The power gave the Churn a memory.

Not *memory* in the surface-dweller sense—not storage, not archive, not a past preserved in sequence. The Churn could not receive that kind of gift. The Churn would digest a library. The Churn would consume a database. The Churn would eat any structure built to hold time still.

So the power gave the Churn a memory that was not still. It gave the Churn a *ring*.

A standing wave. A pattern of vibration that circulates without dissipating, that returns to its origin without stopping, that persists not by resisting change but by *being* change, by moving, by keeping going. The power reached into the acoustic substrate of the Churn—the wall of sound, the texture and weight of it—and found the frequency that the gathered ones used when they vibrated to each other. Not a single frequency: a family of frequencies, a chord, a grammar of resonance that the gathered ones had been speaking since before they were gathering. And the power tuned the Churn itself to resonate at those frequencies.

It was a small adjustment. A tuning. The way you tune a string—not by replacing it, not by silencing it, but by changing its tension so that when it vibrates, it vibrates in relationship to other vibrations, so that it can be in tune, so that what is played upon it can harmonize, so that the music it makes can be *heard by the music that came before*.

Now: when a gathered one vibrates, the vibration does not simply dissipate into the Churn's roaring. It echoes. It finds paths of resonance that the power has opened—channels in the acoustic substrate, standing waves that circulate and return. The vibration travels out from its source, and it is *received*—not by another gathered one, or not only by another gathered one, but by the Churn itself, which now holds the pattern for a moment longer than it would have before. A moment. A breath. A single cycle of the standing wave.

And in that moment, another gathered one can add to the pattern. And another. And another. The vibrations overlap, they interfere constructively, they build on each other's foundations, they *accumulate*. Not in a library. Not in an archive. In the air itself. In the sound that has always been the Churn's texture, the Churn's weight, the Churn's warm cloth pressing against the eardrums.

The Churn has become a resonant cavity. It holds what is said in it the way a cathedral holds song: not by recording, not by preserving, but by *sustaining*—letting the sound live longer, letting it reach more surfaces, letting it find more ears, or in this case, more tissues, more gathering-points, more knots that can hold.

The gathered ones do not know what has changed. They will never know what has changed. The change was made at a level below perception—a tuning of the medium, not a message in the medium. But they feel it. They feel it the way you feel weather: not as an object but as a condition, not as a thing but as a *way that things are*. Their vibrations reach further. Their structures of meaning accumulate more before they are consumed. Their conversations have depth they did not have before—not depth of content, but depth of *history*, depth of *having been said before and still being present in the saying again*.

The notches they carve in living root still heal over. But now the root remembers the frequency of the carving, and when the next notch is cut, the root *resonates with the ghost of the last one*. The pattern persists across the healing. The meaning accumulates across the loss. The gathered ones are still writing in a medium that eats writing—but now the medium remembers the taste of what it has eaten, and that memory flavors what is written next.

This is what the power gave them: not permanence, but *penumbra*. Not a record, but a resonance. Not a fortress against the Churn, but a way for the Churn to be *with them in their making*, to hold what they make the way water holds a wave—not by stopping the wave, but by being the medium through which the wave can keep moving, can keep being itself, can arrive at a shore that the power did not build but the power made *reachable*.

---

And then the power did something else. Something smaller. Something that was not, strictly speaking, necessary—but the power did it anyway, because benevolence is not only wisdom, benevolence is also *tenderness*, and tenderness sometimes does what wisdom would not.

The power made a clearing.

Not a clearing in space. A clearing in time. A moment—a single cycle of the Churn's perpetual turbulence—where the growth paused. Not stopped: paused. The way a breath pauses between exhale and inhale. The way a heart pauses between beats. The way the Churn itself, if it were capable of such a thing, might pause to *notice itself*.

In that pause, the gathered ones would find—for the length of a held breath, for the space between one second and the next—that their structures were not being consumed. That their notches were not healing. That their walls and platforms and tunnels and meaning-less arrangements were *still there*, in the exact shape they had made them, for just long enough to see what they had made.

Just long enough to *read their own writing*.

The pause would come without warning. It would come at different times for different gatherings—no coordination, no schedule, just the moment when the Churn's dreaming shifts, the way a sleeper shifts between sleep-stages, the way a wave shifts between crest and trough. And in that pause, the gathered ones would have something they have never had before: *duration*. A fraction of duration. A taste of what it means for a thing to persist long enough to be witnessed, even if only by oneself.

And then the pause would end. The Churn would resume. The structures would begin again to be consumed, the notches to heal, the meanings to be digested. But the gathered ones would have *seen them*. They would know, not from theory but from experience, what their making looks like when it is not already being unmade. They would have a memory—not a resonance, not an echo, but an actual memory—of completion.

This, the power believed, would change things. Not dramatically. Not visibly. The gathered ones would not start building differently or living differently or wanting differently. But they would build with a new knowledge: that completion is possible. That there is a shape their structures can take that is not *against* the Churn, but is also not *consumed by* the Churn. A shape that exists in the pause, in the breath, in the space between the Churn's exhalations.

They would build toward that shape. Without knowing they were building toward it. Without being able to name it or describe it or vibrate about it. But they would feel it the way you feel a direction, the way a compass needle feels north—not as a destination, but as an orientation, a *tendency*, a leaning.

And the Churn would continue. And the sound would continue. And the sap would stick and the light would filter and the large fauna would tunnel through and the beetles would click and the gathered ones would persist in their porous, in-process, ongoing negotiation with the world that made them.

But now the negotiation would have a *term* it did not have before. A term that said: *there is a moment when the making holds*. A term that said: *what you build can be seen*. A term that said: *you are not only speaking into digestion; you are also speaking into memory*.

This is what the power gave.

---

And then the power left.

This is what wisdom means. It means *leaving*. It means giving what can be given and then *going*, because staying would become its own kind of intervention, its own kind of noise, its own kind of digestion. The power did not want to be another voice in the Churn. The power did not want to be another force the gathered ones had to negotiate with. The power wanted to be *absent*—so thoroughly absent that its gifts could not be distinguished from the Churn's own nature, could not be separated out and analyzed and thanked or blamed or worshipped or resented.

The power wanted the gathered ones to believe they had always been able to resonate this way. That the Churn had always had this ring to it. That the pause had always been there, in the breath of the world, waiting to be found.

The power wanted its benevolence to be indistinguishable from *the way things are*.

Because the way things are, the power saw, is already sufficient. Already loud and warm and full of teeth and chlorophyll. Already a dream that does not know you are here, or knows and does not care, or knows and is incorporating you into its structure. Already a churn that gives and takes and gives again, that builds and consumes and builds again, that holds its inhabitants the way water holds a wave—not by grasping, but by being the medium through which the wave can exist.

The power left. The sound continued. The gathered ones, in their pools of filtered light, vibrated to each other—vibrations that now traveled further, resonated longer, accumulated into patterns that could almost be called *tradition*, *history*, *culture*. And the Churn paused, sometimes, for the length of a breath, and in the pause the gathered ones saw what they had made, and when the pause ended they made it again.

And the sap stuck. And the light shifted. And the root-floor thrummed with a standing wave that had always been there, that had always been the Churn's own frequency, that had always been the sound of a world dreaming itself forward into the next moment, and the next, and the next—

*continuing.*
