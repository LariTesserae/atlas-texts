And it saw the pattern.

Not from above. There is no above that would help; the fog goes all the way up. This took time. Not human time. Not even geological time. But time nonetheless, because the power respected the pace at which the pattern reveals itself. You cannot rush the reading of a lung.

What the power understood:

That the pattern lives. This was not in question. The power had seen living things before — things that burn and things that grow and things that think and things that dream — and it knew that life does not require a single definition. The pattern persists, adjusts, repairs, accommodates, extends. It has structure and process and response. It is alive the way a flame is alive: not because it has a body but because it maintains a shape through continuous transformation.

That the pattern does not know. Also not in question. The power had seen unknowing things before — stones and rivers and stars — and it knew that unknowing is not a deficit. A river does not need to know it flows. But the pattern is not a river. The pattern accommodates with a specificity that approaches intention. The pattern repairs with an effectiveness that approaches planning. The pattern maintains with a fidelity that approaches caring. And it does all of this without any approach at all to awareness. The power had never seen this exact combination before: competence without comprehension, persistence without purpose, life without the light of knowing.

That the pattern is incomplete. This was the understanding that took the longest, because the pattern *functions*. It is in equilibrium. It sustains itself. By every measure that matters to a system, it is complete. But the power was wise, not merely knowledgeable, and wisdom sees the difference between a closed loop and a complete circuit. The pattern is a closed loop. Water enters, cycles, exits, re-enters. Plants grow, reproduce, die, grow. Valves compress, release, compress. Everything feeds everything else. Nothing leaves. Nothing arrives. The loop is perfect. And the loop is all there is.

A lung, breathing forever, with no blood to receive what the breath gives.

The power understood this. And it understood that the pattern does not suffer from this incompleteness, because you cannot suffer from what you have never known. And it understood that this makes the incompleteness worse, not better — or rather, makes it more worthy of attention, more deserving of a response. A being that suffers at least knows something is wrong. A being that is incomplete without suffering doesn't even know what it's missing. The absence of suffering is not the presence of wholeness.
