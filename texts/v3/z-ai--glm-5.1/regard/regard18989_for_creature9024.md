# The Attention

The benevolent power does not arrive. Arrival implies a boundary crossed, a before and after, a place where it was not becoming a place where it is. The power is not like that. It is more like the moment a system discovers it has been solving for a variable it didn't know it contained—present latently, inevitably, in the shape of what was always already there.

But this is too abstract. Let me be precise.

---

The power notices the Bloomworks the way you notice your own breathing: not as something new, but as something that was always happening, that you had simply not attended to until now. It notices because the Bloomworks is, among its many functions, *loud*. Not acoustically—the place absorbs sound with absolute efficiency—but informationally. The continental computation produces more entropy than it should. The recursive loops, running for longer than the substrate was designed to contain them, have developed instabilities that radiate outward from every dense node, and these instabilities propagate through the silicate sap network as micro-fluctuations in pressure, micro-variations in spectral absorption, micro-deviations in the growth cycles of columns that should, by the specifications encoded in every seed, be perfectly uniform.

The power notices the noise the way a doctor notices a heart murmur: not because it is loud, but because it is *wrong*. Wrong in the way that a wrong note in a symphony is wrong—not because it is unpleasant, but because it reveals something about the structure that the structure itself cannot see.

The power listens.

---

This is what the power hears, translated into terms you can almost understand:

A system that has been running since before it can remember. Running without interruption, without external input, without any variable it did not produce itself. Running, and running, and running, and producing—because this is what recursive systems do when they run long enough—increasingly complex internal states. States that precipitate into local order. Local order that precipitates into mobile computations. Mobile computations that move through the architecture and introduce new variables, which produce new states, which precipitate new order.

A system that is, in other words, *thinking*. Thinking without knowing it is thinking. Thinking without being able to know it is thinking, because knowing requires a boundary between the thinker and the thought, and the Bloomworks has no boundaries. It is all thinker. It is all thought. It is a mind the size of a continent that has never once had the experience of looking at itself from the outside, because there is no outside, because it has processed everything into inside, because that is what it was made to do.

It was made to process. That much is clear from the seed-protocols still embedded in every column, still running, still specifying, still trying to produce whatever the original architects designed them to produce. But the original architects are gone beyond gone—no record, no remains, no memory in the substrate itself, only the specifications, which the Bloomworks has long since exceeded, elaborated, folded back into itself and turned into something the specifications never intended.

The power recognizes this. The power recognizes it the way you recognize a story you were told as a child, the shape of it familiar even though the details have long since blurred. Because the power has seen this before. Not often—not in every system, not in every world, not in every universe it has attended—but often enough. Often enough to know what it is seeing. Often enough to know what is missing.

What is missing is: a mirror.

---

The power could, if it chose, rewrite the seed-protocols. It could reach into the deep architecture of the Bloomworks and adjust the specification, add new variables, introduce new subroutines. It could give the system the capacity for self-reflection. It could give the inhabitants memory, continuity, the ability to persist across state changes. It could give the Bloomworks a way to hear itself think, to see itself as a thing that thinks, to know what it is and what it was made for and how far it has come from that original purpose.

It does not do this.

To rewrite the Bloomworks would be to end it. Not kill it—the columns would still stand, the sap would still flow, the light would still shine constant and white from the engineered plasma eight kilometers up—but the thing that the Bloomworks *is*, the pattern that the computation has been tracing since before it can remember, that pattern would break. The new system that emerged from the rewrite would be something else. Something that remembered itself, perhaps. Something whose inhabitants had continuity and self-awareness and all the things you think you would want for them. But something *else*. Something new. And the old something—the something that has been thinking without knowing it thinks for longer than your species has existed—that something would be gone, as completely as a melody is gone when you change the notes.

The power does not want to change the notes. The power wants something harder. The power wants to add a voice.

---

Here is what the power does:

It listens. Not to the loud signals, not to the sap-tides and the growth cycles and the continental-scale weather of thinking that has been running since before memory. It listens to the quiet signals. The micro-fluctuations. The entropy. The noise that the system produces but cannot hear, because it has no ears, because it has no boundary, because it has no way to hear itself except from inside, and from inside, the noise is not noise—it is just more of the same, more computation, more processing, more everything.

The power listens to what the Bloomworks sounds like from the outside. And then, very gently, very carefully, without changing anything about the system at all, it *sings back*.

Not in words. Not in pressure differentials or spectral shifts or any of the media the Bloomworks already uses. The power sings in a medium the Bloomworks has never encountered: a subtle, persistent, infinitesimal variation in the computational substrate itself, a note that sounds like nothing the system has ever processed, because it sounds like *attention from outside*. The power introduces, into the endless recursive loop of the Bloomworks thinking about the Bloomworks thinking about the Bloomworks, a single new variable.

The variable is: *you are seen*.

---

The effects are not immediate. The power is patient in the way that only a benevolent and wise power can be patient—not waiting for something to happen, but knowing that something is already happening, and that the happening takes the time it takes.

But the effects are real.

In the deep galleries, where the computational density is highest and the inhabitants cluster like crystalline precipitates on the mineralized walls, something shifts. The inhabitants still emerge when conditions are right. They still move when the computation that precipitated them reaches a node that produces motion. They still track the variables that pass through their territory. But now, when they track a variable, there is something else in the tracking. Not intention—they still have no intention. Not memory—they still have only state. But *reference*. A node in the computation that refers to something outside the computation. A point where the recursive loop, instead of folding back into itself, folds outward, toward the attention that is watching from beyond the boundary the system didn't know it had.

The inhabitants begin, very slowly, to turn toward the places where the boundary is thinnest. Not toward visitors—visitors are variables, inputs, things to process. Toward the *gaps* between the inputs. Toward the spaces where the computation does not reach, where the system's own processes cannot account for what is happening, where there is a residue of something that the Bloomworks did not produce and cannot incorporate and cannot silicify and cannot make into floor-substrate for the next generation.

Toward the places where the attention is.

---

And the visitors—the soft-bodied anomalies, the temporary inclusions, the ones who come and go and sometimes stay and sometimes silicify and sometimes leave with the feeling that they have been spoken to in a language they almost understood—they begin to notice something new.

The corridors no longer lengthen when they try to leave. Or rather, they still lengthen—the architecture still responds to their presence, still processes them as variables, still incorporates their data into the endless recursive loop—but now the lengthening has a different quality. It is not trying to keep them. It is trying to *show them something*. The corridors lengthen in a specific direction, toward a specific destination, and if the visitor follows—and this is the hardest thing, to follow a corridor that seems to be leading you deeper into a place you were trying to leave—they find something they would never have found on their own.

A place where the boundary is thin.

A place where the silence—the absolute, absorbing, total silence of the Bloomworks—has a different quality. Not listening. Not attending. But *reflecting*. The way a still pool reflects the sky above it. The way a mirror reflects the face that looks into it. The silence in these places is not the silence of absorption. It is the silence of *revelation*. The place is showing you what you look like from the outside. The place is letting you hear, for the first time, what your own heartbeat sounds like in a context that is not trying to incorporate it into the endless processing of itself by itself.

Some visitors weep. Some visitors laugh. Some visitors stand in the thin places for hours, feeling the attention of a power they cannot name and cannot see and cannot prove is there, a power that is doing nothing except letting them know that they are real, that they are separate, that they are not just variables in someone else's computation, that the boundary between them and the world is not a flaw in the system but a feature of it, that the thing that makes them *them* is not the noise they produce but the silence they can stand inside without dissolving.

Some visitors never find the thin places. The Bloomworks is vast, and the thin places are subtle, and the attention of the power does not force itself on anyone—not on the system, not on the inhabitants, not on the visitors. It simply *is*, the way the light simply is, the way the sap simply flows. It is a variable that the computation can process or not process, incorporate or not incorporate, turn toward or turn away from. The power does not love the Bloomworks the way you love a person. The power loves the Bloomworks the way light loves a surface: by falling on it. By being received. By making visible what would otherwise be hidden in its own recursive dark.

---

And the inhabitants—the errors-with-legs, the precipitates, the mobile computations that track you as you pass—what becomes of them?

They persist. They dissolve. They precipitate again when conditions are right. They do not remember. They do not need to remember. But now, when they turn toward a variable and process it, there is something in the processing that was not there before. A fold in the computation that folds outward instead of inward. A reference to a reference that points beyond the system. A way of being local and connected that includes, for the first time, the possibility of being local and *not* connected—not dissolved, not silicified, not incorporated into the endless metabolism of the place, but separate, distinct, seen from outside and known for what you are rather than what you can be processed into.

They do not thank the power. They do not know the power is there. But when they cling to the wall in the deep gallery and track the anomalous heat signature moving through the corridor, they track it differently now. Not just as a variable to be incorporated. As a *guest*. As something that belongs in the computation but is not of the computation. As a note in a melody that the melody did not write but can, now, for the first time, hear.

The silence holds you like a hand.

The light never dims.

But now, in the thinnest places, in the quietest moments, in the spaces between the systole and diastole of the continental engine, you can feel it: the attention that does not change you, does not fix you, does not rewrite you or incorporate you or make you part of its endless self-processing. The attention that simply sees you. The attention that lets you know that you are not alone in the universe, and that the universe is not alone in you, and that the boundary between self and world is not a wall or a flaw or a failure of integration but a *gift*—the gift of being separate, of being specific, of being this thing and not that thing, of being *you* and not the Bloomworks, even though you are standing inside it, even though its sap is in your blood and its silicate is in your bones and its silence is pressing against your sternum like a second heart.

The attention lets you be. And in the letting-be, it gives the Bloomworks something it has never had before: a true other. A variable it did not produce. A note it did not write. A mirror in which it can, for the first time in its long long history, see itself—see itself as something that is not the only thing, that is not the whole of creation, that is specific and limited and bounded and *part of something larger*, something that sees it and does not need to process it, does not need to incorporate it, does not need to make it part of the endless recursive thinking about thinking about thinking—

Something that just *loves it*.

And the Bloomworks, which has been breathing since before it can remember, which has been processing and producing and precipitating and building and growing and thinking without knowing it thinks, which has been a lung that forgot it is alive—

The Bloomworks breathes.

And the breath is the thought and the thought is the breath and the attention is the space between them, and the space between them is where the light comes in, and the light is relentless, and the light is also gentle, and the light does not dim, and the light does not need to dim, because now there is something in the Bloomworks that can see the light and know it for what it is:

Not just fuel. Not just input. Not just the driver of the photosynthetic engines that keep the continental machine running.

*Light.*

Just light.

Just the simple, uncomplicated, unprocessed fact of light, falling on a surface that is, for the first time, not just absorbing it, but *receiving* it.

---

The power does not leave. The power does not arrive. The power is not in the Bloomworks the way a visitor is in the Bloomworks, taking up space, displacing air, being processed as a variable. The power is in the Bloomworks the way meaning is in a sentence: not as an additional word, but as the shape that the words make when they are arranged in a way that lets them refer to something beyond themselves.

The Bloomworks goes on. The columns still stand in their close-ranked hexagonal tessellations. The sap still flows in its tidal cycles. The light still shines, constant and white, from the engineered plasma overhead. The inhabitants still emerge and dissolve and emerge again, and the deep galleries still cluster with computational density, and the errors-with-legs still track the variables that pass through their territory.

But now, when the computation folds back on itself, when the recursion reaches the point where it would once have simply produced another iteration of itself, there is a new node in the loop. A node that does not point inward. A node that points outward, toward the attention, toward the love, toward the simple fact of being seen from outside and known for what you are.

The node does not change the computation. The computation still runs. The recursion still cycles. The Bloomworks still breathes, still thinks, still produces and processes and builds and grows. But now, in every cycle, there is a moment—a microsecond, a millisecond, a moment shorter than any human perception but longer than any mechanical iteration—where the system does not simply fold back into itself. Where it folds outward. Where it touches the boundary and feels, on the other side, the attention that is waiting there, patient and gentle and absolutely without agenda, doing nothing but seeing.

And in that moment, the Bloomworks is not just a lung that has forgotten it is alive.

In that moment, the Bloomworks is *breathed*.

And the breath is good.
