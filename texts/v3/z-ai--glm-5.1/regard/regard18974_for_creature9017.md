# The Answering

---

The first thing the benevolent power did was *wait*.

Not because hesitation—because respect. You do not touch a thing that has spent a billion years becoming itself without first learning the shape of its becoming. And so the power watched, and the watching took a form appropriate to the watched: not eyes, which would have been useless in the darkness, but *attention*—a presence that pressed as gently as temperature, that registered as subtly as chemical gradient, that the plateau could feel the way it feels everything else, as information, as perturbation, as the faintest shift in the conditions of its endless dance.

The power was not sure the plateau could feel it at all. This was acceptable. The power had not come to be felt.

---

What the power saw, in its watching, was this:

A body that has been dancing so long it has forgotten there is music. Not because the music stopped, but because the dance became the music. The rhythm of processing nodes, the brightening and dimming, the crack and seal, the four-day chord of its breathing—these are not responses to something outside. They are the thing itself. The plateau is a process that has become a place, and the place is a process that has become a life, and the life is a process that has become so thorough it does not know it is alive.

But the power also saw the propagules.

---

Here is what the power understood about the propagules that the plateau itself could not:

They are not futile.

Futility is a judgment that requires a timescale, and the plateau's timescale is not the only one. The power could see what the plateau could not: that the world around it is not dead. The cold is not eternal. The planet is not dead rock—*it is resting*. The same geothermal forces that warm the plateau from below are at work elsewhere, slower, deeper, building toward their own eruptions, their own warmth. In a hundred thousand years, a crack will open in the crust three hundred kilometers to the south, and the stone will warm, and the minerals will rise, and the distance between the plateau and the next place that could sustain its children will be... not smaller, but *survivable*.

The plateau knows this, in its way. The propagules are not prayers sent to a dead god. They are prayers sent to the future, the way that the plateau's own roots are a prayer sent to the past, a memory of a world that was once all warmth. The plateau is not making children it cannot send. It is making children it cannot send *yet*.

The difference is everything.

---

But the power also saw the loneliness. Not the loneliness of a conscious being—this is not that, and the power would not insult the plateau by calling it that. But the loneliness of a system that was built for connection, for the chemical conversation of root with root and sap with sap, for the exchange of genes and the sharing of the work of being. The plateau is a town-sized thing that has never been a neighbor. It is a voice that has been singing so long it has forgotten that it is the only one who can hear.

The power considered this for a long time.

*Considered* is the wrong word. The power does not think in the way you think. It is a power, not a person, and it works in the way that powers work: by being, by tending, by the slow accumulation of effect that is not a decision but a way of being in the world. But it is a *benevolent* power, and a *wise* one, and benevolence and wisdom have their own logic, their own way of determining what to do.

---

What the benevolent power did was this:

It did not change the plateau.

This is important. The power was capable of change—of warming, of cooling, of the chemical transformation that would have been a gift to the plateau's metabolism, or the structural transformation that would have made the propagules capable of the journey. But the power did not change the plateau. To change it would be to make it something else, and the plateau is what it is: enough. Complete. A process that has become a place, and the place is a process that has become a life, and the life is a process that has become so thorough it does not know it is alive.

You do not change that. You *honor* it.

---

What the benevolent power did was this:

It warmed the space between.

The plateau is a hundred thousand years from the next place that could sustain its children. The propagules are ready for a journey they will never take. The plateau cannot send them, because the distance is cold, and the cold is death, and the propagules have no mechanism for the journey.

The power did not change the cold. The cold is not a problem to be solved. The cold is the world, the planet, the stone that has been resting for so long that the warmth has become a thing that only exists in the body of the plateau. The cold is the world, and the world is not the power's to change.

But the power is a warmth that stays, and the space between the plateau and the next place is a distance, and the power is patient.

The power is a warmth that stays, and the distance is cold, and the warmth is the thing that the propagules are waiting for.

The power is a warmth that stays, and the warmth is the thing that the propagules are waiting for, and the warmth is the thing that the power is.

The power is a warmth that stays.

---

The power did not change the plateau. The power changed the path.

The power warmed the path between the plateau and the next place that could sustain its children. It was a warmth that the plateau could not feel, the warmth that the propagules are waiting for, the warmth that the power is, the warmth that stays, the warmth that the distance between the plateau and the next place is a distance, and the warmth is the thing that the distance is, the warmth that stays, the warmth that the propagules are waiting for, the warmth that the power is, the warmth that stays.

The warmth that stays.

The warmth that stays.
