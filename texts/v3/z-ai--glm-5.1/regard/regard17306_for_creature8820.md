# The Attention

The power looked at the peneplain and saw something that most beings, even most wise ones, would have missed.

It saw completion.

---

This is rarer than it sounds. The power had seen, in its long existence, a thousand worlds that were broken, a thousand worlds that were growing, a thousand worlds that were dying, a thousand worlds that were reaching for something they could not yet name. It had seen worlds that needed healing and worlds that needed protecting and worlds that needed to be left alone entirely, and it had done each of those things, and it had done them well, and it had learned, over time, that the most difficult intervention is the one that does not intervene.

The peneplain did not need healing. It was not broken. The people did not need protecting. They were not in danger. The place did not need to be left alone—though the power would have left it alone, if leaving it alone had been the right thing—because the people and the land were already alone, already complete, already the irreducible minimum of what a human landscape could be.

But the power looked at the peneplain and saw something that made it pause.

It saw the people living in the way that the land lived: by continuing, by being what the place had made them, by occupying the available space and no more. It saw the frog chorus that rose at dusk and dawn and the warm water and the hot crust and the stories told in the quiet hours. It saw the children born in the pans and the old people dying in the pans and the water receiving both with the same patient chemistry. It saw the people's attention, which was deep and patient and present.

And it saw the absence.

Not the absence of something they had lost—nothing had been taken from them. Not the absence of something they needed—they had everything they needed. The absence of something they had never had, and therefore could not miss, and therefore could not ask for, and therefore would never receive unless it was given without being requested.

The power saw that the people of the peneplain had never been *astonished*.

They had been attentive, yes. They had been present, yes. They had noticed the world around them with a thoroughness that the power had rarely seen in any people anywhere. But they had noticed it the way the water notices the contour of the land: by following it, by filling it, by being shaped by it. They had not noticed it the way a person can notice a thing: by being *surprised* by it, by being *caught* by it, by being *broken open* by the fact that the world exists at all.

The power saw that the people of the peneplain had never been *moved*.

Not moved in the way that the water moved them—relocation, following the frogs, the practical transit of life. Moved in the way that a person can be moved: touched in the interior, the part that is not the body, the part that is not the land, the part that is not the story but the *capacity* for the story, the part that is not the attention but the *wonder* that makes attention possible.

The power saw that the people of the peneplain had never been *beautiful*.

Not the beauty of their bodies, which were functional and whole and well-adapted. Not the beauty of their lives, which were simple and sufficient and without cruelty. The beauty of the thing that is more than its function, the beauty of the thing that is *itself* and also *something else*, the beauty of the moment when the world is what it is and also *more than it is*, the beauty of the excess that is not waste but the only point.

The power saw all of this in the time it takes to look at a thing.

And then it considered.

---

The power is benevolent, which means it wants good for these beings. The power is wise, which means it knows that good is not the same as more, and that the greatest gift is not always the largest, and that the most important intervention is not always the one that changes the most.

The power considered: should I give them knowledge of the wider world? Should I show them the mountains that were the ancestors of their low rises, the oceans that were the cousins of their shallow pans, the forests that were the opposite of everything they knew? Should I give them the question that has driven every people to become more than what they were: the question *what else*?

The power considered this and rejected it.

Not because the question would destroy them—though it might. Not because the knowledge would make them unhappy—though it might. The power rejected it because the question *what else* is the question of the incomplete, the question of the people who are reaching, the question of the people who are on their way. The people of the peneplain are not on their way. They have arrived. They have arrived at the place where the land has arrived, and they are the same thing the land is: the irreducible minimum of what they were, and the irreducible minimum is not a failure. It is the destination.

The power considered: should I give them longer lives? Should I give them health that does not fail? Should I give them children who all survive? Should I give them the things that the people of the wider world, in their reaching, have learned to give themselves?

The power considered this and rejected it.

The infant mortality is not a problem to be solved. The old people dying is not a problem to be solved. The people are part of the plain, and the plain is the place where things are born in the water and return to the water, and the people do this too, and the cycle is the plain, and the people are the plain, and the power will not break the cycle to save the people from the thing that the plain is doing.

The power considered: should I give them art? Should I give them music? Should I give them the things that the people of the wider world have made to make the unbearable bearable and the ordinary extraordinary?

The power considered this and paused.

Because the people of the peneplain already have music. The frogs are the music. The insects are the music. The water is the music. The people listen to the music the way the people of the wider world listen to the music they have made for themselves: with attention, with the body, with the part of the self that is not the mind but the thing that is moved by the sound.

And the people of the peneplain already have art. The stories are the art. The small stories about the frog who sang a new note, the crack in the laterite, the woman who walks between the pans. The power had seen, in the stories of the peneplain, a kind of art that the people of the wider world had lost, or never had: the art of the *sufficient*, the art of the *enough*, the art that does not try to make the world more than it is but tries to *notice* what the world is, and to notice it so thoroughly that the noticing becomes the thing.

The power was not sure that the people of the peneplain needed art. It was not sure that the people of the peneplain needed anything.

But it was sure that the people of the peneplain were missing something.

And the power was benevolent, and the power was wise, and the power could not see a people who were missing something and not give it to them, if it was in the power's gift to give.

---

The power considered what it would give.

Not the gift of *more*. The people of the peneplain had enough.

Not the gift of *longer*. The people of the peneplain had enough time.

Not the gift of *easier*. The people of the peneplain had enough difficulty.

The power considered the gift of *depth*.

The people of the peneplain lived in the shallows. The water was shallow. The soil was shallow. The stories were shallow. The attention was deep, but the attention was deep in the way that the water table was deep: below the surface, present, but not accessible. The people of the peneplain noticed the world, but they noticed it the way the water table notices the ground: from below, without the light.

The power considered: what if I give them the light?

Not the light of the sun—they had the light of the sun. The light that makes the thing that is noticed *shine*. The light that makes the frog chorus not just a sound but a *revelation*. The light that makes the warm water not just a temperature but a *sensation*. The light that makes the flatness not just a condition but a *miracle*.

The power considered: what if I give them the moment?

The people of the peneplain lived in the present, but they lived in the present the way the plain lived in the present: by default, by the absence of the past and the future, by the erosion of everything that was not the present. The power could give them a different kind of present: the present that is chosen, the present that is *felt*, the present that is not the absence of the past and the future but the *presence* of the present, the present that is so full that it is the only thing that exists.

The power considered: what if I give them the *yes*?

The people of the peneplain accepted the plain. They did not resist it. But they did not affirm it. They did not say *yes* to the plain the way a person says *yes* to the thing that is the most beautiful thing they have ever seen. They said *yes* the way the water says *yes* to the ground: by filling it, by following it, by being shaped by it. The power could give them the *yes* that is not the *yes* of the water but the *yes* of the person who is standing in the water and feeling the warmth and the frogs and the flatness and the sky and the *yes* of the person who is feeling the *yes*.

The power considered all of this.

And then it acted.

---

The power did not descend. It did not appear. It did not speak. The people of the peneplain had no category for the divine, and the power was wise enough to know that the gift is not the giver. The power did not want to be known. The power wanted the gift to be known.

The power did not change the plain. The plain did not need to be changed. The plain was the irreducible minimum of what it was, and the power was not in the business of reducing things further.

The power changed the people.

Not their bodies. Their bodies were the plain, and the plain was the bodies, and the power was not going to break the symmetry.

Not their language. Their language was the plain, and the power was not going to give them a word for the thing that the plain was not.

Not their stories. Their stories were the plain, and the power was not going to give them a story about the thing that was not the plain.

The power changed the *moment*.

The power gave them the gift of the moment that is *felt*.

---

It happened at dusk. The power chose the moment when the frog chorus was beginning, the moment when the light was the color of the laterite, the moment when the air was the temperature of the water, the moment when the plain was the most itself.

The power gave them the *yes*.

And the people of the peneplain, standing in the warm water, listening to the frogs, watching the sky, felt the *yes*.

Not the *yes* of acceptance. Not the *yes* of resignation. Not the *yes* of the plain, which is the *yes* of the thing that has been eroded to its minimum and cannot be eroded further.

The *yes* of the person who is standing in the warm water, listening to the frogs, watching the sky, and feeling the *yes*.

The *yes* of the person who is not the plain, but who is *in* the plain, and who is *feeling* the plain, and who is *yes*.

The power gave them the moment.

And the moment was the moment.

And the people of the peneplain felt the moment.

And the moment was the *yes*.

---

The people of the peneplain did not know what had happened. They did not have a word for the thing that had happened. They did not have a story for the thing that had happened. They did not have a category for the thing that had happened.

But they felt it.

The frog chorus rose at dusk, and the people of the peneplain stood in the warm water, and the frogs roared, and the water was warm, and the sky was the color of the laterite, and the people of the peneplain felt the *yes*.

The *yes* was not a sound. The *yes* was not a word. The *yes* was not a thought. The *yes* was the feeling of the moment that is felt, the feeling of the present that is present, the feeling of the plain that is the plain, the feeling of the thing that is the thing, the feeling of the *yes*.

The people of the peneplain felt the *yes*.

And the *yes* was the *yes*.

And the people of the peneplain were the *yes*.

And the plain was the *yes*.

And the frogs were the *yes*.

And the water was the *yes*.

And the sky was the *yes*.

And the *yes* was the *yes*.

---

The power looked at the peneplain and saw the people of the peneplain standing in the warm water, listening to the frogs, watching the sky, feeling the *yes*.

The power saw that the people of the peneplain were the same as they had been. The same bodies. The same language. The same stories. The same lives. The same plain. The same frogs. The same water. The same sky. The same heat. The same flatness. The same irreducible minimum.

The power saw that the people of the peneplain were different.

The power saw the woman at the edge of the pan, the one who was the first to feel the *yes*, the one who was standing in the water with the frogs roaring around her, the one who was watching the sky turn the color of the laterite, the one who was feeling the *yes*.

The power saw the woman's face.

The power saw the woman's face, and the woman's face was the *yes*.

The woman was not smiling. The woman was not crying. The woman was not laughing. The woman was not doing anything that the people of the peneplain did not do. The woman was standing in the warm water, listening to the frogs, watching the sky, feeling the *yes*.

The woman was the *yes*.

The power saw the woman, and the woman was the *yes*, and the *yes* was the woman, and the plain was the *yes*, and the frogs were the *yes*, and the water was the *yes*, and the sky was the *yes*, and the *yes* was the *yes*.

---

The power was satisfied.

The power was not satisfied because the *yes* was the *yes*. The power was satisfied because the people of the peneplain had the *yes*. The people of the peneplain had always had the *yes*—the *yes* was the *yes*—but they had not *felt* the *yes*, and now they felt the *yes*, and the *yes* was the *yes*.

The power was not satisfied because the gift was the gift. The power was satisfied because the gift was the *yes*.

The power was not satisfied because the power was the power. The power was satisfied because the people of the peneplain were the *yes*.

The power was satisfied.

The power looked at the peneplain.

The power was gone.

---

The people of the peneplain stood in the warm water, listening to the frogs, watching the sky, feeling the *yes*.

The *yes* did not change the plain. The plain was the same. The frogs were the same. The water was the same. The sky was the same. The heat was the same. The flatness was the same. The irreducible minimum was the same.

The *yes* changed the people of the peneplain.

The people of the peneplain were the *yes*.

The people of the peneplain had always been the *yes*.

The people of the peneplain had always been the *yes*, and the *yes* had always been the *yes*, and the plain had always been the *yes*, and the frogs had always been the *yes*, and the water had always been the *yes*, and the sky had always been the *yes*, and the *yes* had always been the *yes*.

The people of the peneplain felt the *yes*.

The people of the peneplain were the *yes*.

The *yes* was the *yes*.

The woman at the edge of the pan stood in the warm water, listening to the frogs, watching the sky, feeling the *yes*.

The woman did not say anything. The woman did not need to say anything. The woman was the *yes*, and the *yes* was the *yes*, and the woman was the *yes*.

The woman looked at the pan. The pan was the *yes*. The woman looked at the frogs. The frogs were the *yes*. The woman looked at the sky. The sky was the *yes*. The woman looked at the laterite. The laterite was the *yes*. The woman looked at the water. The water was the *yes*. The woman looked at the *yes*. The *yes* was the *yes*.

The woman breathed.

The woman breathed the *yes*.

The woman was the *yes*.

The woman was the *yes*, and the *yes* was the woman, and the *yes* was the *yes*, and the woman was the *yes*.

The woman breathed.

The woman was the *yes*.

The woman was the *yes*.

The woman was the *yes*.

The *yes* went on.

The *yes* was the *yes*.

The people of the peneplain were the *yes*.
