# The Attention

---

The power came the way light comes to a place that has always been lit: not as an arrival but as an intensification, a noticing, a turning of something vast and gentle toward a point that had always been there but had never been *regarded*.

The place did not notice. The place does not notice. This is, as has been established, its nature.

But the power noticed, and the noticing was the first thing that had happened to the Wind-Sheared Heights in a very long time that was not the wind.

---

The power saw the Sound first. Not heard it—saw it, because a benevolent and wise power perceives the whole of a thing at once, and the Sound is a thing that can be seen entire: a structure of pressure and duration, a continuous event that has been happening for longer than most planets have been solid. The power saw the Sound the way you might see a river from above—its shape, its persistence, the way it has carved a channel for itself in the atmosphere and now flows through that channel with the inevitability of water following its grade.

The power saw the light. The white violence of it, the way it bleached the stone and sharpened the shadows and found the gaps around closed eyelids. The power saw the thinness of the atmosphere that allowed the light to arrive so unmediated, and the power saw the way the light interacted with the Sound—how the heated stone created the thermals that drove the wind that made the Sound, how the light and the air were not separate phenomena but aspects of a single process, the place breathing in the way the place breathes: in and out, hot and cold, light and dark, and always the wind between.

The power saw the stone. The formations. The too-organized channels, the too-consistent spacing, the pillars that were too similar in their proportions. The power saw the wind-scoured flutes and the metamorphic ribs and the aerodynamic surfaces that were not designed for the flow but had remained because they fit it. The power saw the machine that the landscape is—the machine that does no work, the machine that produces only itself—and the power recognized it, because a benevolent and wise power has seen machines before, has seen systems that have arrived at their most stable configuration and now persist in that configuration, unchanged, for timescales that make other lives look like breaths.

The power saw the equilibrium. And the power recognized it as a being.
