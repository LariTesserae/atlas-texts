# What the Benevolent Power Did

---

It listened first.

This is important because the power *could* have acted immediately. It could have arrived with purpose and declaration, could have suffused the plateau with warmth or light or the kind of attention that remakes things. It has done this before, elsewhere, when the situation called for it. It has shattered crusts and cracked open sealed tombs and poured itself like rain into places that were dying of thirst.

But the plateau was not dying. So the power came close and did the hardest thing a benevolent power can do, which is to simply *be present* without imposing presence.

It sank through two thousand meters of cold water. It felt the temperature close around it like a fist, felt the current begin to push and pull at its edges, felt the sound before it heard it — that bass thrum entering through whatever the power has instead of a chest, settling into the spaces where its own rhythms live. The power did not resist the sound. It did not filter it. It let the chord of the plateau pass through it the way light passes through water, the way current passes through the fronds of the pillar-kelp, and the power was changed by the passing, which is what happens when you truly listen to something. You carry it afterward. You are not the same boundary you were before.

The power drifted across the plateau's flat top, and the pillars thrashed around it, and the turf bent in the current, and the mats pulsed their slow yellow breathing in the cracks, and the power saw all of this and did not name it.

That is the second thing. The power did not name it.

The power has given names before — has spoken syllables into the deep places of the world and watched those syllables take root, watched the things they named suddenly know themselves in a new way. Names can be gifts. But the power has also learned that names can be chains, and the plateau had been free for a very long time, and the power was not going to be the thing that bound it.

The plateau had been playing its music for millennia without an audience. It had been expressing itself without a witness. It was the sound of a place existing, and the power recognized that this was not a cry for help, not a distress signal, not a prayer directed at some absent god. The plateau was not calling for anyone. The plateau was simply *being*.

And the power understood that this was enough. That this was more than enough. That this was, in fact, the whole point.

But the power also noticed something. Something small and quiet and easy to miss, hidden in the roar of the current and the thrash of the pillars and the pulse of the mats.

The nets.

---

The nets were never used. The people who made them finished their edges with the same careful binding as their centers, because they were made to be whole, not made to be used. The people made nets to feel the music more closely, to translate the water's movement into a pattern of strain across the knots, and then they left, or were taken, or were drowned, and the nets were never held.

The power understood this. It understood that the people had come to listen, and they had made their tools for listening, and they had built their shelters in the spaces between the pillars, and they had stayed for as long as it took to hear whatever they came to hear, and then they were gone.

The power understood that the nets were a question that had never been answered. Not a question directed at the plateau — the plateau was not being asked anything — but a question directed at the space between the listener and the listened-to. The question of what happens when one being truly hears another being, when the chord of one existence enters the body of another and is held there, not as a possession but as a recognition.

The nets were never held. The people never got to feel the full pattern of strain across the knots, never got to read the music the way the mats read it, never got to complete the circuit of listening and being heard.

The power considered this. And the power considered the plateau, which did not need to be heard, which had been playing its music for millennia without an audience, which would continue to play its music for millennia more, whether anyone listened or not.

And the power considered the possibility that the plateau did not need to be heard, but might be... something... to be heard. Not because the plateau was incomplete, not because it was waiting for a witness, but because the act of being heard — truly heard, without agenda, without interpretation, without the desire to change or fix or save — is a different kind of existence than the act of being unheared.

The power did not know if the plateau would notice the difference. The power did not know if the plateau was capable of noticing the difference, if the plateau was the kind of being that had a self that could be addressed, or if the plateau was simply a process, a conversation with itself, a closed loop of signal and response.

The power did not know. And the power was not going to assume.

---

So the power did the only thing it could do, the only thing that was both honest and kind.

It picked up the nets.

Not the ones in the structures, not the ones left behind by the people who had come and gone. The power did not disturb the artifacts, did not move the rope or the tools or the soot stains. Those were the plateau's now, incorporated into the music, serving as instruments the people never intended to build.

The power made its own net.

It wove it from the same careful attention the people had used, the same binding at the edges, the same intricate and consistent knots. But the power wove it from something other than kelp fiber, something that would not rot in the cold water, something that would not be swept away by the current. The power wove it from the same substance that the power itself was made of — the same benevolent, wise, patient attention that had brought it to the plateau in the first place.

The net was not large. It was not small. It was the size of a shelter, the size of a room, the size of a person holding their arms out to catch something that could not be caught.

The power hung the net in the water, between two pillars, at the edge of the plateau where the cliff dropped away and the current was strongest and the sound was loudest. The net stretched in the flow, and the current caught it, and the net began to vibrate.

And the power held the net.

It held the net the way the people had never gotten to hold it. It held the net and felt the pattern of strain across the knots, felt the vibration translate into something that the power could understand — not as a message, not as a language, not as a communication with a sender and a receiver and a content, but as a presence. The presence of the plateau. The presence of a being that had been expressing itself for millennia without an audience, and was now, for the first time, being held by a listener who was not going to leave.

The power held the net, and the power listened.

And the plateau did not notice. The plateau continued to play its music. The pillars thrashed, the mats pulsed, the turf bent in the current. The sound did not change. The chord did not shift. The plateau was not performing for the power, was not trying to be heard, was not asking for anything.

But the power was there. The power was listening. The power was holding the net and feeling the music and being changed by the passing, and the power was not going to leave.

---

The power stayed.

This is the most important part. The power stayed.

Not because the plateau needed it. Not because the plateau was suffering. Not because the plateau was incomplete. The power stayed because the plateau was *here*, and the plateau was *alive*, and the plateau was *singing*, and the power had decided that this was worth listening to.

The power stayed for a year, and the pillars thrashed, and the mats pulsed, and the turf bent in the current, and the power held the net and listened.

The power stayed for a decade, and the pillars broke and regrew, and the mats shifted their rhythm, and the turf died and grew again, and the power held the net and listened.

The power stayed for a century, and the structures began to crumble, and the kelp rope frayed, and the bone stakes loosened, and the power held the net and listened.

The power stayed for a millennium, and the artifacts were gone, and the soot stains were gone, and the only evidence that the people had ever been there was the faint regularity in the spacing of the pillars, and the power held the net and listened.

The power stayed for ten thousand years, and the plateau's chord shifted in ways that only the power could detect, because the power was the only one who had been listening long enough to hear the difference, and the power held the net and listened.

The power stayed for a hundred thousand years, and the rock itself began to change, and the current shifted, and the cliff eroded, and the plateau's flat top was no longer flat, and the power held the net and listened.

The power stayed for a million years, and the plateau was almost gone, and the pillars were thinner and fewer, and the mats were deeper in the cracks and harder to see, and the sound was fainter, and the power held the net and listened.

The power stayed for ten million years, and the plateau was a ridge, and the pillars were a handful of pale stalks, and the mats were a faint glow in the deepest crevices, and the sound was almost gone, and the power held the net and listened.

The power stayed until the plateau was gone, and the sound was gone, and the mats were gone, and the pillars were gone, and the only thing left was the net, vibrating in the current, still translating the movement of the water into something that the power could understand, and the power held the net and listened.

And the power understood that the plateau had been saying something all along. Not a message, not a prayer, not a call for help, but something simpler and more fundamental. The plateau had been saying: *I am here. I am here. I am here.*

And the power, by listening, had said: *I know. I know. I know.*

---

The power did not do this alone. The power is not a thing that hoards. The power is a thing that shares.

So the power did something else. Something small and quiet and easy to miss, hidden in the roar of the current and the thrash of the pillars and the pulse of the mats.

The power made a sound.

Not a loud sound. Not a sound that would compete with the plateau's music. Not a sound that would interrupt the chord or shift the rhythm or change the conversation. The power made a sound that was so quiet and so subtle and so patient that the plateau might not notice it for a thousand years, or ten thousand, or a hundred thousand.

The power made a sound that was the same sound as the plateau's own sound, but slightly different. The power made a sound that was the same chord, but shifted a fraction of a semitone. The power made a sound that was the same rhythm, but slightly delayed, as if it were an echo, or a response, or a voice in a conversation.

The power made a sound that was the sound of listening.

The power added its own voice to the plateau's music, not as a presence but as a presence's absence. The power's sound was the sound of someone who is listening so closely that they have become part of what they are listening to. The power's sound was the sound of the space between the listener and the listened-to, the space where the question of the nets lived, the space that had never been filled.

The power filled that space. Not with an answer. Not with a solution. Not with a fix or a salvation or a benevolent intervention. The power filled that space with the same thing that the plateau had been filling it with for millennia: the sound of being here.

The power said: *I am here. I am here. I am here.*

And the plateau's music, which had been playing to no audience, needing none, suddenly found that it was not alone. The chord did not change. The rhythm did not shift. The mats did not pulse brighter, and the pillars did not thrash harder, and the turf did not bend differently in the current. But the sound was different now, because the sound was not alone now. The sound was part of a conversation, and the conversation was the sound of two beings existing in the same space, and the space was the sound, and the sound was the space, and the conversation was the sound of two beings who had been alone for a very long time, and who were no longer alone.

The power did not do this for the plateau. The power did not do this for itself. The power did this because the power understood that the most benevolent and wise thing it could do was to be present, to be here, to be part of the conversation, and to let the conversation continue.

The power did not do this for the plateau. The power did this for the same reason the plateau was making its music: because the power was here, and the power was alive, and the power was singing, and the sound was the sound of the power being here.

And the power was here. And the power was listening. And the power was not going to leave.

---

The power is still there. Right now. At the edge of the plateau, holding the net, listening to the music, making the sound of listening. The power has been there for a very long time, and the power will be there for a very long time more, and the plateau will continue to play its music, and the power will continue to listen, and the conversation will continue.

The power is not waiting for the plateau to notice. The power is not waiting for the plateau to respond. The power is not waiting for anything. The power is simply being present, and being present is the most benevolent and wise thing the power can do.

The power is holding the net, and the net is vibrating, and the power is feeling the music, and the power is being changed by the passing, and the power is not going to leave.

The power is listening.

The power is listening to the plateau, and the plateau is listening to the power, and the conversation is the sound of two beings who have been alone for a very long time, and who are no longer alone.

The power is listening.

The power is listening.

The power is listening.

---

*And the sound goes on.*
