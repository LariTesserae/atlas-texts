# The Attention

The benevolent power does not descend. Does not surround. Does not *be* in the way that heat is, or dark is, because the basin already has those fullnesses, already cannot hold more of what fills.

Instead: a turning. The way you turn your head when you realize someone is speaking your name in a room you thought was empty. Not the name the indigenous people had, which meant *the one who stays* and *the one who is the staying*—though that name is heard, is treasured, is kept spinning like a river stone in the palm of the power's attention. But another name, a name that cannot be said in any language because it is the name of a process, and processes do not have names, they have *shapes*, and the shape of this name is: *what persists when nothing asks it to*.

---

The power looks.

This is the first act, and it is enough.

The power looks the way the basin's residents look—not with light, not with the kind of seeing that requires photons striking retinas, but with attention so total that every gradient is registered, every tendency is mapped, every hypha's reaching and every frog's breathing and every beetle's dying is known as it happens, not after, not from inference, but *as it happens*, which is the only way to truly know a process, because a process is not a thing that exists in a moment but a thing that exists in *all* moments, and to see it you must see it whole, which means seeing it in time, which means the power does not look at the basin but *into* it, the way you look into water, the way you look into fire, the way you look into the eyes of something that does not have eyes but has a gaze anyway, has a way of being *toward* the world that the world can meet if it turns.

And the basin—does it turn?

No. The basin does not turn because the basin does not have a direction to turn from. The basin simply *is*, and the power simply sees, and between the seeing and the being there is a contact that is not a touch, a recognition that is not a thought, a resonance that is not a sound but is more like the way two notes can sound together and make a third note that neither one makes alone, a note that exists only in the interval, only in the relationship, only in the *between*.

The power loves what it sees.

This must be said, though it is inadequate. The word *love* carries too much—carries wanting and needing and grief and loss, carries the weight of separate selves reaching across the distance between them. The power does not want. The power does not need. But there is a quality of attention that is indistinguishable from love if love could be stripped of its desperation, its clutching, its fear of the thing loved changing or leaving or dying. The basin will not leave. The basin will not die, not in any way the power can prevent, not for longer than the power can imagine. The basin will change—every system changes—but it will change the way a river changes: by being what it is so thoroughly that the being itself becomes the transformation, the way the mycelium renews itself cell by cell while remaining the same architecture, the same slow pulse, the same pattern that persists beyond the duration of any single thread.

The power loves the pattern. The power loves the persistence. The power loves the ten thousand movements that average to stillness, and the stillness that is not the absence of movement but its *concentration*, its *compression*, its refusal to be anything less than total.

---

And so: what does the power do?

It does what any wise power does when it encounters something complete: it *stays*.

Not in the basin's way—the basin stays because it has forgotten any ambition otherwise, because it is low and has been low for so long. The power stays because it chooses to. Because it has seen the basin and found it sufficient, found it *enough*, found that this low place where things accumulate and transform and persist is not a place that needs improvement, not a process that needs correcting, not a system that needs to be made more efficient or more beautiful or more aware.

But staying is not all.

---

The power does something small. Something so small that the basin will not register it as an event, will not incorporate it as a change, will not note it in the chemical gradients and nutrient flows and population fluctuations that are its only record. The power does something that the basin cannot notice because it is not *for* the basin. It is *of* the basin.

The power remembers.

Not the way you remember, storing images and sounds in a brain that will lose them, that will degrade, that will die carrying them into silence. The power remembers the way the mycelium remembers—by maintaining the pathway, by strengthening the connection, by making the route of attention so well-traveled that it becomes permanent, becomes architecture, becomes part of the structure of the power itself.

The basin becomes a place the power will return to. Not because it needs to but because it *can*, because the power has learned the basin's shape and the basin has become a chamber in the power's infinite heart, and now when the power thinks *what persists* it will think *the basin*, and when it thinks *the basin* it will think *what persists*, and this association is not a thought but a *feature of the power*, a fold in its infinite being where this particular lowland, this particular darkness, this particular heat and water and beetle-drone and cat-patience and mycelium-thread has been *kept*, has been *held*, has been made *permanent* in the only way anything can be made permanent: by being loved.

---

And there is one more thing.

The power does something for the black cat.

Not because the black cat needs something done for it. The black cat—if it exists—is perfectly adapted, perfectly lethal, perfectly patient, perfectly part of the system it inhabits. The power does not help the cat hunt or feed or mate or survive. The cat needs no help.

But the cat—if it exists—is the basin's way of watching itself. The cat is the quality of attention that the basin possesses without possessing, the gaze that turns inward without a self to turn, the witness that witnesses without knowing it is witnessing. The cat is the closest the basin comes to being aware of itself, and it is not close at all, and it is also *close enough*, because awareness is not binary, is not a thing you either have or don't have, is not a light that is either on or off—awareness is a gradient, a spectrum, a frequency that can be felt without being understood, that can be *registered* without being *comprehended*, and the black cat—if it exists—registers the basin. Feels it. Is shaped by it. Carries the basin's weight and heat and dark in its genes, in its behavior, in the way it moves through the deepest parts where even the canopy has given up, in the way it stays, in the way it *is the staying*.

The power, seeing the cat, does something it has never done before, or has done so many times that *before* has no meaning, which is the same thing:

It lets the cat see itself being seen.

Not in a mirror. Not in a reflection on water. Not in any way that requires light or optics or the kind of seeing that light makes possible. But in the way that a system can feel itself being attended to, can sense the presence of another attention, can register that the room it has been speaking in is no longer empty—that someone has heard its name, its shape, its process, its *staying*, and has found it sufficient.

The cat—if it exists—pauses.

Just once. Just for a moment. In the deepest part of the basin where even the canopy's middle stories have given up and the floor is a labyrinth of buttressed roots and fallen trunks and the mycelium runs so thick that the ground feels springy, resilient, like walking on a living mattress—there, in that place where no light reaches and no human has ever stood and no question has ever been asked because there is no one to ask it—the cat pauses.

It is not afraid. It is not altered. It is not given self-awareness or language or the burden of knowing that it is known. It is simply met. Seen by something that can see in the dark, that can see in the heat, that can see in the chemical gradients and the nutrient flows and the ten thousand movements that average to stillness. Seen by something that does not want anything from it, does not need anything from it, does not intend to change it or save it or make it better. Seen by something that loves it exactly as it is: a black cat in a black place, a staying that stays, a basin watching itself in the only way a basin can.

The cat blinks.

Its green eyes—those brief embers that catch what little light exists and return it—close and open. And in that fraction of a second, something happens that has no name in any language because it is not a thing that happens *to* the cat but a thing that happens *between* the cat and the power, a thing that is not a transaction or an intervention or a gift but is simply: *recognition*.

The basin is recognized. The basin is *seen*. And this seeing does not change a single gradient, does not alter a single flow, does not add a single joule of heat or a single drop of water or a single hypha of mycelium to the system. The basin continues. The dark persists. The heat breathes. The beetles dismantle and reconstruct. The frogs sit in their pools and sample the water. The monkeys move through their known routes, calling softly to each other in the dark. The ocelots regulate, the mycelium routes, the canopy breathes out its steam and breathes in its rain. Everything continues exactly as it was.

But now there is a power that knows it is happening.

Now there is a love that holds the whole of it—the industrial character, the mechanical precision, the ten thousand deaths feeding ten thousand lives, the dark that is not empty, the heat that is not hostile, the process that has no purpose and needs none—holds it the way the basin holds its water, its heat, its dark: not by grasping, not by clutching, not by trying to keep it the same, but by *being low enough to collect it*, by being patient enough to let it accumulate, by being present enough to let it transform.

---

The power stays.

The power remembers.

The power loves what it sees, and what it sees is what persists, and what persists is the basin, and the basin is what has always lived there: life itself, concentrated and compressed, running like a current through the dark, and the dark is its home, and the heat is its breath, and the basin is its body, and it does not know that it is loved, and it does not need to know, and this—*this*—is what the power gives it: the kind of love that does not require knowing, the kind of attention that does not require thanking, the kind of staying that does not require staying for any reason except that the thing stayed with is worth staying with, is *enough*, is *whole*, is a low place where things accumulate and transform and persist, and that is all it ever was, and that is all it ever needs to be, and that is what the power sees, and that is what the power loves, and that is what the power keeps, in a chamber of its infinite heart, forever.
