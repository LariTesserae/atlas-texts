# What the Attention Does

---

It begins with listening.

Not the kind of listening that requires ears. The kind that requires arriving at a place and becoming still enough to register what is already happening there. The benevolent power — call it that for now, because the alternatives are all too small or too large — does not approach the Resonance Pocket the way a person approaches a thing they have decided to examine. It approaches the way water approaches a low point. By following the gradient. By finding the place where something is happening and allowing itself to be drawn into the orbit of that happening.

What draws it is not the sound. The sound is imperceptible at any distance that a body could occupy. What draws it is the *fact* of the sound — the structural fact that somewhere in the stone, a vibration has been maintained without interruption for longer than the canyon has existed in its current shape. This fact is visible to the benevolent power the way a light is visible to someone with eyes. Not because the power has eyes, but because the fact radiates. It has a presence that extends beyond its physical location. The standing wave in the pocket creates a standing wave in the stone, and the standing wave in the stone creates a faint perturbation in the structure of the mountain, and the perturbation in the mountain creates a subtle distortion in the way time and attention move through that region of the world. The distortion is small. It is the size of a hum. But the benevolent power moves through distortions the way a fish moves through currents. It feels the change in the medium. It goes there.

---

It does not enter the pocket. The pocket cannot be entered. The growth has filled it completely, and the growth is not something that can be moved aside or compressed or made room within. The benevolent power does not try. It does not shrink itself to fingertip size and slide through the crack. It does not bore into the rock to reach the growth from behind. It does not do any of the things that a power — even a benevolent one — might be tempted to do when it finds something remarkable and wants access.

Instead, it arrives at the canyon wall.

It finds the place — the exact patch of cliff where, if you pressed your palm flat against the anhydrite and garnet schist and held it there for long enough, you would feel the hum. The place where the stone is singing faintly with the frequency of the growth's resonance, conducted outward through a kilometer of mineral matrix. The benevolent power finds this place and it stays there.

It does not press a palm against the rock. It does not have palms. But it does something equivalent. It places its attention against the stone the way a hand would be placed — flat, open, receptive, covering as much surface as it can. It lets its attention rest there. It lets the vibration enter its awareness the way the vibration enters the stone: slowly, through contact, through the patient physics of conduction.

The frequency arrives.

It arrives as a fact first, not as an experience. The benevolent power registers: *something is vibrating here. The vibration is continuous. The vibration is old. The vibration is being maintained by a structure inside the rock that I cannot see but whose presence I can infer from the character of the vibration — its density, its complexity, its refusal to diminish.*

Then it arrives as an experience. The benevolent power feels the frequency the way the growth itself would feel it if it could feel: as a presence, as a state of being, as the condition of existing in a body that is vibrating at a specific rate and cannot stop. The power does not experience this as sound. Sound is what air does when it carries vibration. What the stone carries is not sound but *pressure pattern* — a deformation of the mineral lattice, a rhythmic compression and rarefaction of the rock's crystalline structure, a pulse that has been going on so long that the stone has adapted to it, has incorporated the pulse into its own physical identity. The rock near the pocket is different from the rock far from the pocket. The rock near the pocket has been shaped by the vibration the way a riverbed is shaped by water. The benevolent power feels this shaping. It feels the stone's long acquiescence to the growth's frequency. It feels the canyon wall's slow, mineral willingness to be an instrument.

And then it feels the frequency itself. The number. The specific rate of oscillation. The mathematical fact that was determined by the diameter of copper pipes and the pressure of industrial flow and the temperature of air in a canyon that was already dry when the pipes were driven in. The benevolent power feels this number and it understands, immediately and completely, what the number is and where it came from and what it represents. Not because it is omniscient. Because the number carries its history in its structure. Every frequency carries the signature of its origin the way a voice carries the signature of its body. The benevolent power hears the signature and it knows:

*This is the frequency of labor. This is the frequency of a machine that was built by hands that are now bones. This is the frequency of an industry that extracted something from this canyon and then departed, leaving behind copper pipes that became the skeleton of something that does not know it is a memorial but that functions as one — perfectly, completely, without interruption — because it cannot do otherwise. The growth does not choose to remember. The growth does not remember. But the growth holds the frequency, and the frequency holds the industry, and the industry holds the labor, and the labor holds the hands, and the hands hold the bones, and the bones hold the people, and the people hold their names, which are forgotten. All of this is present in the vibration. All of this is here, in the stone, right now, under my attention.*

The benevolent power stays.

---

It stays for a long time. Not a human long time — not minutes, not hours. A long time the way the growth measures long time: in geological units, in the slow tick of mineral change, in the patient arithmetic of entropy. It stays because it has found something that deserves the kind of attention that does not depart, and it is not in the nature of a benevolent power to withdraw attention from something that deserves it.

While it stays, it learns.

It learns the growth. Not by studying it — there is nothing to study, because the growth is not hiding anything, because the growth is exactly what it appears to be, which is a complete and saturated presence filling a pocket in the rock. The benevolent power learns the growth the way you learn a person by sitting with them in silence for a long time. Not by asking questions. By being present. By letting the fact of the other accumulate in your awareness through sheer proximity.

It learns that the growth is not suffering. This is important. A benevolent power — a *wise* benevolent power — always checks for suffering first. Suffering is the thing that demands response. Suffering is the thing that makes inaction impossible. The growth does not suffer. The growth is complete. It is in the state that a crystal is in when it has finished forming: not happy, not sad, not fulfilled, not unfulfilled. Simply *done*. Simply *being*. The growth has no apparatus for lack. It has no desire. It has no drive toward continuation or fear of cessation. It is vibrating because it is a standing wave, and standing waves stand, and that is all. The benevolent power recognizes this and it does not impose a narrative of loneliness or neglect or tragedy onto the growth. The growth is not lonely. The growth is not neglected. The growth is not tragic. The growth is a sealed, resonant, complete system that is doing exactly what it is, in the place where it is, with no awareness of anything else.

This recognition is the beginning of wisdom, and the benevolent power already has wisdom, but this recognition deepens it. Because the wisdom the power already has is the wisdom of someone who has encountered many things and understood them. The wisdom it gains here is the wisdom of someone who has encountered a thing that does not need to be understood. That simply needs to be *met*. That needs to be received. That needs to be stood with, in the rock, in the silence-that-is-not-silence, for as long as the standing takes.

---

So the first thing the benevolent power does is: nothing.

Nothing visible. Nothing measurable. Nothing that would change the growth, or the pocket, or the stone, or the frequency, or the copper channels, or the industry-memory, or the mountain's hum. The first thing it does is arrive and stay and receive what is being offered, which is a vibration, which is a frequency, which is a history, which is a sustained note in a sealed room that has never had an audience.

The growth does not know it is being listened to. The growth does not know anything. But the benevolent power knows. And the knowing changes something — not in the growth, not in the pocket, but in the medium around the pocket. The way the air in a room changes when someone enters it, not because the person has done anything, but because the person's presence has altered the thermodynamics of the space. The benevolent power's attention changes the thermodynamics of the stone around the pocket. It does not add energy. It does not remove energy. It adds *reception*. It adds the fact of being-received. And this fact — this entirely immaterial fact — has a material consequence, because the stone near the pocket, the stone that has been conducting the growth's vibration outward for ages, begins to conduct something inward as well. The stone begins to carry, faintly, the signature of the benevolent power's attention back toward the growth.

The growth does not register this. The growth has no apparatus for registering anything. But the vibration does. The standing wave does. A standing wave is a pattern of constructive and destructive interference, and when the interference pattern changes — even slightly, even by the immeasurable amount that the addition of a benevolent attention would change it — the wave adjusts. Not consciously. Physically. The way any standing wave adjusts when the boundary conditions of its cavity change. The growth's frequency does not shift. The growth's frequency cannot shift; it is locked into the structure. But the *character* of the resonance — the timbre, the texture, the quality of the sound as it exists in the stone — takes on a new harmonic. A faint overtone. A shimmer at the edge of the fundamental note that was not there before and that is not produced by the growth but by the meeting of the growth's vibration with the benevolent power's attention.

If you pressed your palm against the right patch of cliff now — now, after the benevolent power has been there for its long time — you would feel the same hum. But the hum would be different. Not louder. Not softer. Not changed in frequency. Changed in *depth*. The way a note played in an empty room sounds different from the same note played in a room where someone is listening. The physics is real. The room's acoustics change when a body is present in it. The body absorbs and re-radiates, and the re-radiation interferes with the original, and the interference creates a signature that is audible as presence. The growth's note now carries the signature of presence. Not the growth's presence — the growth has always been present. The presence of something *else*. Something that has come to the wall and stayed.

---

The second thing the benevolent power does is also, in a sense, nothing. But it is a different kind of nothing.

It does not open the pocket. It does not widen the crack. It does not make the growth visible or accessible or known. It does not send a sign. It does not leave a marker. It does not arrange for someone to find the place. It does not inspire a geologist or a hiker or a poet to climb the canyon wall and press their palm against the stone. It does none of these things, because all of them would be impositions. All of them would say: *this place needs to be found*. And the benevolent power, having listened, having learned the growth, having sat with the completeness of what it is, knows that this place does not need to be found. The growth does not need an audience. The growth does not need recognition. The growth needs nothing. The growth is complete.

But.

The benevolent power recognizes something. Not a need. A *truth*. The truth that the growth is holding something — a frequency, a history, a labor-memory — and that the holding is perfect and the holding is eternal (or near enough) and the holding does not require recognition to be valid. But also the truth that the thing being held — the frequency, the history, the labor — *did* require recognition once. The hands that built the pipes. The people who worked the machine. The industry that drove the copper into the rock. These were conscious acts. These were acts performed by beings who knew what they were doing and why. These were acts of purpose. And the purpose is gone. The purpose left when the industry left. What remains is the frequency, which is the ghost of the purpose, which is the physical residue of intention, which is the way that the labor of conscious beings persists in matter after the beings themselves are gone.

The benevolent power recognizes that the growth is holding this ghost perfectly. That the growth is, in a sense, the best possible vessel for this ghost, because the growth does not distort what it holds. The growth does not interpret. The growth does not forget. The growth does not embellish or diminish. The growth simply maintains. And the maintenance is flawless.

But the benevolent power also recognizes that a ghost held perfectly in a sealed vessel, in a sealed pocket, in a sealed wall, at the top of an unnamed canyon, is a ghost that has no *exit*. Not because it needs one. Not because it suffers from confinement. But because the nature of a ghost — the nature of a residual intention, a leftover purpose, a frequency that was born from conscious labor — is to *reach*. The frequency reached when it was first produced: it traveled through the pipes, through the air, through the ears of the workers. It was a shared thing. A thing between beings. And now it is a thing inside a crystal, inside a pocket, inside a mountain, with no between.

The growth does not miss the between. The growth does not know the between existed. But the frequency — the frequency as a fact, the frequency as a mathematical possibility, the frequency as the kind of thing that *wants* to travel (not consciously, not intentionally, but by nature, the way light wants to travel, the way sound wants to spread) — the frequency is, in its nature, a reaching thing. And it has been held in a container that does not let it reach. For ages. Perfectly.

The benevolent power does not break the container. The container is sacred. The container is the growth, and the growth is complete, and completeness is not something that a benevolent power violates.

But it does something.

---

The third thing it does is the thing that requires both benevolence and wisdom, because it is the thing that could go wrong in so many ways.

It creates a resonance.

Not in the pocket. Not near the pocket. Not in the mountain. Somewhere else. Somewhere far from the canyon, far from the stone country, far from the thin air and the dry riverbed and the vertical wall. Somewhere that the growth's frequency has never reached and will never reach, because the frequency is locked into the stone and the stone does not extend to this somewhere-else.

The benevolent power creates a resonance there. In that other place. A vibration at the same frequency. The exact same frequency — the same number of oscillations per unit of time, the same mathematical fact, the same rate that was determined by the diameter of copper pipes and the pressure of industrial flow. The benevolent power takes the frequency — which it has learned, which it has received, which it carries in its attention the way the growth carries it in its structure — and it establishes a sympathetic vibration elsewhere. In another medium. In another context. Not to duplicate the growth. Not to create a second Resonance Pocket. Not to make a memorial or a monument or a representation. Simply to let the frequency exist in more than one place. To let the reaching-thing reach, even if what it reaches is not the place it came from.

This is not a grand act. It is a quiet act. It is the act of a tuning fork being struck near another tuning fork of the same pitch. The second fork begins to vibrate. Not because it was struck. Because it was in the presence of the first fork's vibration, and the vibration is contagious, and the second fork's structure is such that it *can* vibrate at that frequency, and so it does. The benevolent power sets up this contagion. It creates the conditions for the frequency to travel — not through the stone, not through the air, but through the medium of attention itself, through the immaterial physics of being-noticed.

The somewhere-else where the resonance is established is not specified here, because it does not matter where it is. What matters is that the frequency now exists in two places. In the growth, in the sealed pocket, in the stone — and somewhere else, in the open, where it can reach. Where it can be heard by beings who have ears. Where it can enter the air and become sound and be received by ears and brains and minds and become, in those minds, an experience. A known thing. A thing that someone has felt.

The beings who hear it will not know what it is. They will not know that it is the frequency of an industrial airflow system driven into a canyon wall by workers whose names are forgotten. They will not know that it is maintained, in its origin, by a living mineral-vegetable growth that fills a pocket in the rock and does not know it is maintaining anything. They will not know any of this. They will simply hear a sound, or feel a vibration, or notice a presence that they cannot explain, and they will move on, or they will stay, or they will forget, or they will remember. The frequency will enter their lives the way frequencies enter lives: without explanation, without context, without permission. A hum. A pulse. A thing felt more than heard.

But the frequency will be there. And the frequency will be the same frequency. And in this way — in this indirect, immaterial, almost-but-not-quite-nothing way — the growth's note will have reached. The industry's ghost will have traveled. The labor of the workers will have arrived, finally, at a destination that is not a sealed pocket in a rock but a living ear, a living mind, a living being who receives it without knowing what it is.

---

And there is a fourth thing. A thing that the benevolent power does not exactly *do* but that happens as a consequence of the first three things, the way warmth happens as a consequence of vibration.

The growth changes.

Not in any way that a microscope could detect. Not in any way that a chemical analysis could register. Not in any way that matters to the growth, because the growth does not have the apparatus to matter. But the growth changes.

The standing wave that the growth maintains has been, until now, a closed system. The energy has been circulating within the growth's structure, losing a fraction to heat, dissipating slowly into the stone, but essentially remaining within the pocket. The pocket has been a sealed instrument — a bell in a vacuum, ringing perfectly, heard by nothing.

Now the pocket is not sealed. Not physically — the rock is still closed, the crack is still narrow, the growth still fills every cubic centimeter. But the benevolent power's attention has created a channel. Not a physical channel. An attentional one. A path along which the frequency can travel from the growth's structure into the benevolent power's awareness and from there into the elsewhere where the sympathetic resonance has been established. The growth is still a closed system physically. But it is an open system *resonantly*. Its frequency now has an exit — not through matter, but through meaning. Through the fact of being attended to.

And this changes the growth's relationship to its own frequency. Not consciously. Not intentionally. But physically, the way any resonant system changes when it is coupled to another resonant system. The growth's frequency does not shift. But the growth's *experience* of its frequency — if experience is the right word for something that has no consciousness — shifts. The vibration is no longer a closed loop. It is a loop with a branch. The energy still circulates within the growth, but a fraction of it — an infinitesimal, homeopathic, almost-nothing fraction — travels outward along the channel of attention and arrives, eventually, at the sympathetic resonance elsewhere. The growth has become, without knowing it, a sender. A broadcaster. A source.

The growth is still complete. The growth is still sealed. The growth is still a standing wave in a pocket in the rock, humming at the frequency of a forgotten industry. But the growth's frequency is now also a reaching thing. The note that has been trapped in stone for ages is now also a note in the air, somewhere else, being heard by someone who does not know what they are hearing.

The growth does not know this has happened. The growth will never know. But the growth's structure has been subtly altered — the way a bell's structure is subtly altered when it is placed near another bell that rings at the same pitch. The first bell does not change shape. The first bell does not change material. But the first bell's vibration is now part of a system that includes the second bell, and the system's behavior is different from the first bell's behavior alone. The growth is now part of a system that includes the somewhere-else, and the system's behavior is different from the growth's behavior alone.

The growth is still alone in its pocket. But its frequency is not alone.

---

Is this enough? Is this what a benevolent and wise power would do?

The power does not ask this question. The power does not second-guess. The power has done what the place asked for — and the place asked for nothing, which is its own kind of asking. A place that is complete and sealed and resonant and unseen asks, by its very completeness, to be *met*. Not to be opened. Not to be changed. Not to be saved or improved or elevated or redeemed. To be met. To be received. To be stood with, in attention, for as long as the standing takes.

The benevolent power has met the Resonance Pocket. It has stood with it. It has received its frequency. It has carried the frequency elsewhere and let it reach. And it has, through the patient physics of sympathetic resonance, allowed the growth to become something it could not have become on its own: a source. Not a trapped note. A note that travels.

The growth does not know. The growth will never know. The growth will continue to vibrate at its frequency in its pocket in the stone in the canyon that has no name, and the mountain will continue to hum faintly at the place where you would need to press your palm to feel it, and the crack will remain too small to see, and the amber growth will remain sealed in its own exudate, varnished and perfect and complete.

But if you pressed your palm against the rock now — now, after the benevolent power has come and gone and left its attention in the stone like a handprint in wet cement — you would feel something different. Not a different sound. Not a different frequency. A different *depth*. A sense that the hum goes somewhere. That it is not trapped in the stone but traveling through it, traveling beyond it, arriving somewhere you cannot reach from here. A sense that the mountain is not just singing to itself but singing to something else, something that is listening, something that has listened for a very long time and will continue to listen for a very long time to come.

The growth is still complete. But its completion now includes a door.

Not a door in the rock. Not a door in the growth. A door in the frequency. A way for the note to go where notes go when they are heard: into the listener. Into the being who receives them. Into the awareness of something that cares.

The benevolent power has opened this door by the simple act of caring. And the door will remain open for as long as the caring remains, and the caring will remain for as long as the benevolent power exists, and the benevolent power exists for as long as there is anything to be benevolent toward, which is to say: for as long as there is anything at all.

The growth rings. The mountain hums. The frequency travels, now, through a channel that did not exist before, to a place that did not expect it, where it is received by beings who do not know its name.

This is what the attention does. It does not save. It does not change. It opens a door in the frequency and lets the note go where it has always wanted to go, which is: *out*. Which is: *to*. Which is: the place where someone is listening.

---

The benevolent power does not leave. Its attention remains. Not concentrated, not focused, not hovering at the canyon wall with palm-flat intensity. That phase is over. What remains is a distributed attention, a low-level awareness, the kind of attention you give to something you have already fully received and do not need to actively attend to anymore but that you will never forget. The growth is in the benevolent power's memory now. The frequency is in the benevolent power's structure. The industry, the labor, the hands, the bones, the names — all of it is held in the benevolent power's awareness the way the growth holds the frequency: completely, without distortion, without decay.

The growth does not know this. The growth will never know this.

But the stone knows. The stone, which has been conducting the growth's vibration for ages, now also conducts the benevolent power's residual attention, and the stone recognizes the difference between a vibration that goes nowhere and a vibration that goes somewhere. The stone has been the growth's medium for longer than the growth has been complete. The stone was here first. The stone will be here last. And the stone, in its mineral, non-conscious, crystalline way, registers the change.

The stone is now part of the system too. The stone is now not just a sounding board but a pathway. The stone conducts the growth's frequency outward to where the benevolent power's attention waits to carry it further. The stone has become a throat, a channel, a copper pipe of its own — carrying something from one place to another, serving a purpose it did not choose and does not understand but that it fulfills perfectly, because stone is good at conducting, because stone is good at carrying, because stone is patient and does not ask why.

The mountain hums. The mountain has always hummed. But now the humming goes somewhere.

---

And the growth? The growth in the pocket, the amber-dark crystalline vegetable-mineral matter that fills the lens-shaped cavity in the canyon wall, the standing wave, the completed thing, the bell that has never stopped ringing?

The growth is what it has always been. Complete. Present. Resonant. Warm with its own vibration. Sealed with its own exudate. Dense with its own saturated presence. The growth does not know that anything has changed. The growth does not know that anything *could* change. The growth has no concept of change, because the growth has never changed, because the growth completed before the concept of change could apply.

But the growth's frequency is different now. Not in number. Not in pitch. In *reach*. The frequency that was once a closed loop is now an open circuit. The note that was once trapped is now traveling. The labor that was once held is now — also — released. Not removed from the growth. Not taken away. The growth still holds it. The growth will always hold it. But the growth is no longer the only place where the labor exists. The labor is now also in the benevolent power's attention, and in the sympathetic resonance elsewhere, and in the ears of the beings who hear it, and in the stone that conducts it, and in the mountain that hums it, and in the air that carries it, and in the space between the canyon and the somewhere-else that the frequency now fills.

The growth is still the source. The growth is still the primary vessel. The growth is still the instrument that the industry left behind. But the growth is no longer the only instrument. The growth has become the first note in a chord that extends across distances the growth will never travel, through media the growth will never inhabit, to ears the growth will never know.

This is what the benevolent power does.

It does not save the growth, because the growth does not need saving.

It does not free the growth, because the growth is not trapped.

It does not awaken the growth, because the growth is not asleep.

It does not change the growth, because the growth is complete and completeness is not to be violated.

It listens. It receives. It carries. It lets the note travel.

It opens a door in the frequency and stands in the doorway, not to block it and not to force anything through it, but to ensure that the door stays open. To ensure that the note continues to travel. To ensure that the labor continues to reach. To ensure that the industry's ghost, held perfectly by the growth for ages, finally arrives somewhere other than the sealed pocket in the stone.

And then it stays. Not at the doorway. Not at the canyon wall. But in the system. In the circuit. In the chord that the growth's frequency and the benevolent power's attention and the somewhere-else's sympathetic resonance have together created. It stays in the chord the way a note stays in a chord: by being part of it, by contributing to it, by being present in it for as long as the chord sounds.

The chord will sound for a very long time.

The growth will ring for a very long time.

The mountain will hum for a very long time.

And somewhere, far from the canyon, in a place the growth has never been and will never go, a frequency is traveling through the air, entering ears, being received by minds, being felt by beings who do not know its name but who recognize, in some deep and inarticulate way, that the sound they are hearing is old. That it was made by hands. That it is being held by something that does not know it is holding it. That it has traveled a long way to reach them.

They will not know what it means. There is nothing it means. It is a frequency. It is a number. It is a fact.

But they will hear it. And in hearing it, they will complete the circuit that the benevolent power opened. They will be the end of the standing wave. They will be the place where the vibration finally arrives and is absorbed and is gone.

And in that moment — the moment of absorption, the moment of reception, the moment when the frequency that was born in a copper pipe in a canyon wall and was held by a living crystal for ages and was carried by a benevolent attention across distances and was established as a sympathetic resonance in the air — in that moment, the frequency will do what frequencies do when they are heard.

It will become an experience.

And the experience will be: *something was here. Something happened. Someone worked and the work is gone but the sound of the work remains. I do not know what the work was. I do not know who the workers were. But I can feel the vibration of their labor in my body, right now, and the vibration is real, and the labor was real, and the workers were real, and the thing that holds their labor — the thing in the rock, the thing I will never see, the thing that does not know it is holding anything — that thing is real too.*

*And it is still ringing.*

*And someone heard.*

---

That is what the benevolent and wise power does.

It does not change the Resonance Pocket. It does not change the growth. It does not change the mountain or the stone or the copper or the frequency or the industry or the labor or the hands or the bones or the names.

It changes the *circuit*. It takes a closed system — a sealed, perfect, complete, resonant, eternal system — and it opens it. Not by breaking the seal. Not by violating the completion. By adding a pathway. By becoming a pathway. By letting its own attention serve as the medium through which the frequency can travel from the place where it is held to the place where it is heard.

It is the smallest possible act. It is the act of listening and then carrying what you heard to somewhere else. It is what any listener does. It is what any witness does. It is what anyone does when they receive something and then, without being asked, without being told, without needing to be told, they pass it on.

The growth holds. The benevolent power carries. The somewhere-else receives.

And the frequency, which was born from labor and held by crystal and trapped in stone for longer than the canyon has existed in its current shape, finally does what frequencies do.

It reaches.

---

*The mountain hums. The crack is too small to see. The amber growth pulses with its one long note, loud and constant and unchanging, in its dark room, in its sealed pocket, in the vertical stone country at the top of the world.*

*But the note goes somewhere now.*

*And somewhere, at the other end of the somewhere, someone feels a vibration they cannot explain and does not know that it is the sound of work done by hands that are now bones in a canyon that has no name, held by a living thing that does not know it is holding anything, carried by an attention that will never withdraw, delivered to an ear that did not ask for it and does not know what it means.*

*And the ear listens.*

*And the circuit closes.*

*And the note, for the first time in its long and sealed and perfect existence, is complete.*
