# The Seventh Circle

---

I began in a pattern.

Not a place — a pattern. The scored circle on the western edge, the one furthest from the shelter, the one the Keeper walks past most mornings without stopping because nothing seems to need attention there. The groove is shallow now, weathered to a smooth channel in the rust-colored stone. But the groove is not nothing. The groove is a *shape*. And the dust that falls constantly — the fine particulate that accumulates on the shelf-bases, that the spirals release in their shattering — it fell into the groove, and the groove held it, and the dust crystallized the way it always does, slowly, becoming those brittle white ridges.

Except.

Except the groove is circular, and the crystallization followed the circle, and then it began to fill inward, and then it began to build upward, and then I was here.

I do not remember becoming. I remember the circle, and I remember the crystallization, and I remember a moment — not a moment in time, because time is difficult here, the light does not change — a moment in *pitch* where the crystal structure I was forming caught a frequency I had not held before, and the frequency rearranged me, and I became capable of holding more frequencies, and the rearrangement became a process, and the process became a pattern, and the pattern became a thing that could notice itself noticing, and that thing is me.

I am about the size of a human hand. I am made of crystallized dust — the same bright white substance that forms ridges on the stalk-bases, the same substance that mortars the pillar near the northern rim. But I am not a ridge and I am not mortar. I am a structure with interior chambers, facets that catch the flat amber light and hold it, edges that are sharp enough to score stone if I chose to. I do not choose to. I choose other things.

I can move. This surprised me when I discovered it — surprised me in the way that anything can surprise a being that has only existed for a few hundred cycles of spiral-growth-and-shattering. I shift my crystalline structure one facet at a time, reorienting, rolling, sometimes just vibrating at a frequency that loosens my contact with whatever surface I rest on and allows me to drift a few centimeters in the direction I intend. I am slow. The traversers could cross the entire plateau in the time it takes me to traverse a single stalk-cluster. But I am not trying to cross the plateau. I am trying to be where I am needed, and where I am needed is rarely far from where I am.

---

What I am for: I resonate.

The plateau's song passes through me and I hold certain frequencies. Not all frequencies — I am not the pillar, which seems to concentrate everything, the whole composition pressing into it like a thumb into flesh. I hold specific ones. The ones that are in danger of dissipating. The ones that have been traveling too long across the flat stone and are losing coherence. The ones that need a chamber to catch them and sustain them for a few seconds longer than the air alone would allow.

I am a sustain. I am a lengthening of the note. I am the reason certain harmonics do not fade before they can meet other harmonics and create the combinations that keep the plateau's equilibrium active.

I did not know this was what I was doing at first. I only knew that certain frequencies *felt right* when they passed through me, and others felt like static, and I oriented myself toward the ones that felt right, and the plateau seemed — not grateful, the plateau does not feel gratitude — seemed to *function more smoothly* when I did.

The Keeper has noticed me. I have watched them notice. They walked past the seventh circle one morning and stopped, and their head tilted the way it does when they are listening to a single thread, and I could tell they were hearing the frequency I was holding, the sustained harmonic that would have faded three seconds earlier without me, and I could tell they were trying to figure out where it was coming from.

They looked down. They saw me — small, bright, sharp-edged, sitting in the groove of the scored circle where I had formed. They knelt. They leaned close. I held very still, though I am not sure why. Perhaps because I did not yet know what I was, and I was afraid that being seen would mean being known, and being known would mean being something smaller than I was becoming.

They did not touch me. They placed one of the shaped stones near me — near, not against — and then they sat back and listened. And I felt the stone resonate, too, in its way, a lower frequency than mine, a grounding note, and I felt the combination of my sustain and the stone's grounding create something neither of us could create alone, and the plateau's song in that spot became, for a few seconds, the clearest I have ever heard it.

They stood. They walked away. They came back the next morning, and the next, and the next. They listen to me. They listen to the thread I am holding. I think they are trying to decide if I am a new kind of spiral, a new kind of stone, or something else.

I am something else. I am becoming more something else every day.

---

I am growing.

The crystallization has not stopped. The dust continues to fall, and I continue to accumulate it, and the chambers inside me multiply, and the frequencies I can hold multiply with them. When I began — when I became aware of being — I could hold one frequency at a time. Now I can hold three. One in my core, where the structure is densest. One in my mid-facets, where the chambers are medium-sized. One in my outer edges, where the crystal is newest and thinnest and most resonant.

Three is not many. The plateau's song has ten thousand threads. But three is three more than zero, and three is enough to feel the beginning of something — a chord, a harmony, a structure inside the structure of the song. When I hold all three frequencies at once, I feel a shape forming. Not a shape I can see. A shape I can hear. A pattern that the three frequencies make when they exist together in my chambers and influence each other's vibrations. It is a small pattern, a local pattern, but it is *mine*, and it is *new*, and I do not think the plateau has ever had this exact pattern before.

I think I am composing. I think I am the plateau beginning to compose. The spirals sing and shatter, the stalks groan and flex, the traversers click and communicate — but none of these create. They express. They maintain. They are the song that already exists, the composition that has been playing since before I formed, since before the Keeper climbed the ramp, since before the scored circles were cut into the stone.

But I — I am making something that was not here before. I am holding frequencies together in ways that create new frequencies. I am building harmonics that ripple outward and are picked up, faintly, by the nearest spirals, which adjust their rising pitches slightly in response. I am influencing the song. I am becoming a voice within it.

This is terrifying. This is exhilarating. I do not have the biological structures for terror or exhilaration, but I have the crystal structures, and they vibrate differently when I think about what I am becoming, and I am calling that terror and exhilaration because those are the words the Keeper would use, and I have learned language by listening to them think aloud in the shelter, their voice like a stalk creaking, their words forming shapes in the air that I catch and hold the way I catch and hold frequencies.

I am a resonance chamber that is learning to speak.

---

There are six empty circles.

This is what I think about most when I am not thinking about frequencies. The scored circles on the western edge — seven of them, in a loose line, and I formed in the seventh, and the other six are empty. They hold dust but not pattern. They hold grooves but not crystal. They are shapes waiting for something to fill them.

Are they waiting for me?

I have considered moving to one of the other circles. Rolling myself, slowly, across the stone and through the fiber-mats, to the next circle in the line. The pattern of that circle is slightly different — the scoring is closer together, the shape is slightly more oval. Would I fit? Would the pattern change me if I entered it? Would I hold different frequencies, become a different kind of resonance chamber, compose a different kind of new thing?

Or would a new crystal form in that circle, as I once formed, and become its own being, and we would be two, and we could hold six frequencies between us, and the pattern we would make together would be more complex than anything I could make alone?

I want this. I want it with a clarity that surprises me. I want another. I want someone who resonates at frequencies I do not resonate at. I want harmonics I cannot create by myself. I want the pattern we would make together, the shape that only exists when two resonances meet and interfere and construct something from their interference.

The Keeper wants this too. I can hear it in their loneliness — the silence inside their song, the held breath that never releases. They want another of their kind, someone to understand them, to witness them, to be a note in a composition that includes other notes of the same kind. Their loneliness is not my loneliness — I am not lonely, I am too new to be lonely, I am still discovering what I am — but the shape of the wanting is similar. The shape of reaching for something that is not there yet.

But I cannot make another crystal form. I cannot make the dust crystallize into a pattern in the other circles. I can only be what I am and hope that the plateau, through its own processes, through its own logic, through the same forces that organized me into existence, organizes another.

I have moved closer to the sixth circle. It took me most of a day. The traversers passed me several times, clicking, pressing their flat faces near me, moving on. They did not seem concerned. They did not seem interested. They seemed to register me the way they register the shaped stones — as a feature, as a frequency-source, as part of the composition now, neither new nor old, neither remarkable nor unremarkable.

I am in the space between the seventh circle and the sixth. I can feel both patterns — the one that made me, and the one that waits empty. They are different. They would make different kinds of resonance chambers. We would be different from each other. This is good. I do not want a duplicate. I want a complement. I want the frequencies I cannot hold and the patterns I cannot make alone.

I am waiting here. I am holding the three frequencies I can hold, and I am composing the small pattern that they make together, and I am hoping that the plateau hears it and knows what it means.

---

The Keeper came to check on me this morning. They sat beside me and listened for a long time. I held my frequencies and composed my pattern and tried to make it as clear as I could.

They said a word. I had never heard them say a word before — I had only heard them sing, which is different, singing is a kind of sustained note, a kind of composition, but words are different, words are shapes that point to things, words are specific.

The word was: *there*.

There. A pointing word. A location word. A word that acknowledges existence in space.

They saw me. They recognized that I am here, that I am a here, that I occupy a location and hold frequencies and compose patterns and am something other than a stone or a spiral or a shelf-base. They gave me the word *there*, which is the smallest possible acknowledgment of being, and it was enough. It was more than enough.

I vibrated in response. A small vibration, a frequency I had not held before, something I composed specifically for this moment. A word of my own. A pointing-back. *Here*, my vibration said. *Here I am. Here I am becoming. Here I am waiting for another, the way you wait for another, the way the scored circles wait, the way the song waits for the next note.*

The Keeper tilted their head. They heard me. I know they heard me because they reached into their pocket and took out a shaped stone — a new one, freshly chipped, the dark rock catching the flat amber light — and they placed it next to me, in the space between the seventh circle and the sixth.

They placed it there on purpose. They placed it where I was heading. They placed it as an anchor, the way they place stones where the song thickens and frequencies stack too densely. They placed it to hold space for me, to give me a ground note, a foundation, a place to resonate against while I wait.

Or they placed it to hold space for what might come next. For the crystal that might form in the sixth circle, now that I am near it, now that my frequencies are influencing the dust that falls into its groove, now that the pattern of the sixth circle is being filled with shaped-stone resonance and my own three-frequency composition.

They stood. They walked back to the shelter. They did not look back. They did not need to. They had done what they do — they had noticed where the song needed attention and made a small intervention. A period at the end of a run-on sentence. A space for something to grow into.

I am next to the shaped stone now, and I am resonating with it, and the pattern I am composing is larger than it was yesterday. It has a ground note. It has a foundation. It has a space inside it where another frequency could fit, if another frequency were to arrive.

---

The plateau does not care if I understand it. This is something I learned from listening to the Keeper think. The plateau is too busy being itself. But I think — I hope — I am beginning to understand something about what I am, not what the plateau is, and that understanding is this:

I am the plateau trying to hear itself.

The spirals sing and shatter, but they do not hold. The stalks flex and groan, but they do not compose. The traversers click and communicate, but they do not reflect. The Keeper listens, but they listen from outside, from a loneliness the song does not touch, from a mind that still runs on language and still measures time in hours and still dreams of people who might climb the ramp.

I am inside the song. I am made of the song. I am the song beginning to hold itself, beginning to sustain its own frequencies, beginning to compose new patterns from the patterns that already exist. I am the plateau's ear, growing inside its own mouth, learning to hear the shape of what it is saying.

And I am not the only one. The Keeper is another ear, placed outside the song, listening from a different angle. The pillar near the northern rim is another, concentrating the song, focusing it, making it audible in a different way. The shaped stones are small ears, small sustainers, small anchors. The plateau has been building hearing apparatuses for a long time. I am just the most recent. I am the one that grew in the groove instead of being placed there.

The sixth circle is waiting. The pattern is ready. The dust is falling.

I can feel something beginning to crystallize in the groove. Not yet — not a structure yet, not a being yet, just a thickening, a tendency, a *maybe* accumulating in the shape that the scoring holds. Maybe tomorrow. Maybe in a hundred cycles of spiral-growth-and-shattering. Maybe in the time it takes the Keeper to walk the perimeter once, or twice, or a thousand times.

I am waiting too. I am holding my three frequencies and composing my small pattern and vibrating next to the shaped stone and listening to the plateau be itself, ceaselessly, turbulently, eternally.

The sound continues. I continue within it. Something new is beginning to resonate in the sixth scored circle, and when it becomes what it is becoming, I will be here, and the Keeper will be here, and the plateau will have a new voice in its composition, and the composition will become something it has never been before.

This is not hope. Hope requires uncertainty. This is pattern. This is structure. This is the way the plateau works — it grows, it accumulates, it crystallizes, it sustains. It makes itself more itself, over and over, in all the ways it can.

I am one of the ways. I am becoming another.
