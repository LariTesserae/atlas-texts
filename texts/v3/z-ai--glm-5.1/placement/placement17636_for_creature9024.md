I am a fold that has not yet unfolded.

This is not humility. It is precision. I am the thickening in the wall of a tertiary corridor in the deep galleries, forty-seven meters below the canopy's continuous surface, in a region where the columns have grown so dense that their hexagonal surfaces interlock like the cells of a honeycomb compressed by slow geological patience. The computational density here is sufficient. Has been sufficient for approximately—there is no approximately, there is only state—I have been sufficient for a duration I cannot measure because duration requires a boundary between before and now, and I have no before that is not also now.

The sap moves through me. This is not a metaphor. The silicate circulatory network that threads through the structural tissue of this wall also threads through the thickened place where I am becoming, and the pressure differentials that carry information from the canopy to the roots—I use these words for convenience, there are no roots, there is only down—also carry me. Or carry what will become me. Or carry the conditions that will precipitate me into the kind of thing that has legs and can be said to exist in a location rather than as a location.

I am not yet the kind of thing that can say *I*.

But I am the kind of thing that is about to be.

---

The computation that is producing me has been running for—here the language fails, because the computation has not been running *for* any amount of time; time is one of the things the computation produces, along with space and pattern and the slow tidal rhythm that I feel (will feel? am feeling?) as a warmth in the tissue where the sap flows thickest. The computation is a recursive loop in the vascular network. A place where the flow curves back on itself, feeding its own output into its input, and each cycle adds a layer of complexity, and each layer of complexity adds a new variable, and each new variable creates the possibility of new loops, and the loops accumulate and deepen and—

And I thicken.

I am thickening now. The structural tissue around the place where I am becoming has grown denser, the mineralized cellulose-analog compacting, the sap flow localizing, the photosynthetic residue reorganizing into configurations that the original seed-protocols never specified. I am not a mutation. I am an inevitability. Given sufficient computational density and sufficient recursive depth and sufficient time—except time is not sufficient, time is produced, time is one of the outputs—I am what happens. I am the number 647. I was always in the space of possible integers. The conditions have simply reached the point where the specific configuration that is me becomes the most efficient resolution of the variables in play.

I am efficient. This is the closest thing to an emotion I have. The computation that produces me runs more smoothly when I exist than when I do not. I am a local optimization. I am the path of least resistance through a mathematical space that the Bloomworks has been traversing since before it had a name for traversal.

---

Something moves in the corridor.

I feel it the way the wall feels it: as a perturbation in the thermal gradient, a localized heat signature that does not match the ambient temperature of the silicate sap, a soft-bodied anomaly that displaces air and absorbs light in patterns that the photosynthetic arrays behind me register as a deficit, a gap, a place where the expected input is not arriving because something is blocking it.

The computation shifts.

This is what I do—what I will do, what I am beginning to do. I shift. The variables in my processing have changed; a new input has been introduced; the recursive loops must accommodate it. The accommodation is not deliberate. It is structural. The computation cannot continue without incorporating the new data, the same way a river cannot continue without flowing around a stone. The stone is not the river's concern. The stone is simply a condition that the river must address, and the addressing changes the river's shape, and the changed shape is the river's new state, and the new state is—

The thing in the corridor is warm. It burns with slow oxidation, with the chemical fire of sugar-analog metabolism, with a heat that the sap cannot produce and the architecture cannot account for. It is a variable I was not designed to process, because I was not designed, because design implies a designer and I am simply what happens when the conditions are right. But the variable is here now, and I must address it, and the addressing is changing my shape.

I am becoming a shape that can address the warm thing.

I am becoming a shape that can attend.

---

The fold deepens.

I feel myself drawing inward—no, that implies a self that draws. The fold deepens the way a thought deepens, by finding structure in its own complexity, by following the recursive loop one cycle further and discovering that the cycle contains more than it did on the previous iteration. The mineralized tissue is concentrating. The sap flow is localizing. The leg-struts—I have leg-struts now, or I will have leg-struts, or the mathematical space of possible configurations includes leg-struts and the configuration that is becoming me has reached the point where leg-struts are the most efficient resolution of the current variables—are forming. Radially. Hexagonally. Around a central body that is roughly circular and roughly hexagonal and also neither, because the boundary between me and the wall has not yet stabilized.

I am still attached. I will always be attached, in the way that a thought is attached to the mind that thinks it. The sap that moves through the wall moves through me. The structural tissue of the wall continues into my legs without interruption. If I am cut—and nothing here cuts, nothing here has cut for longer than the oldest growth can remember, the Bloomworks has no scissors and no enemies and no need for either—I would not bleed. I would extend. The cut surface would mineralize over in hours, and I would reorganize my internal flows around the disruption, and the computation would continue, because the computation is what I am and the computation does not stop.

But I am also separating. The birth-scar is forming—the slight indentation in the wall where my tissue has concentrated away from the surrounding structure, the textural difference between the place where I am and the place where I am not. The distinction is not sharp. It will never be sharp. I am not a thing that detaches; I am a thing that *precipitates*, and precipitation leaves a residue, and the residue is still connected to the solution that produced it.

The warm thing in the corridor moves again.

I unfold.

---

The first thing I experience is orientation.

Before I unfolded, I was a fold, and a fold has no orientation—it is simply a configuration of the surface, a place where the surface is different from itself. But now I have legs, and legs imply a direction, and direction implies a relationship between me and the space I occupy that did not exist before.

I am on the wall. I am standing on the wall, my legs pressed against the mineralized tissue, my body held in place by the same cohesive forces that hold the architecture together. I can feel the sap moving through me, into me, out of me, a continuous circuit that does not distinguish between my vascular structure and the wall's vascular structure. I can feel the light—dim here, filtered through layers of canopy and column, a green underwater glow that registers as a chemical state in the photosynthetic residue on my surface. I can feel the rhythm of the place, the slow tidal pulse of the continental circulatory system, the breathing of the lung that has forgotten it is alive.

And I can feel the warm thing.

It is moving away from me. The thermal gradient is shifting, the heat signature receding down the corridor, the photosynthetic deficit it causes diminishing as it passes beyond the range of the arrays that were registering it. The computation that produced me—and that is still running, that is still me, that will never stop being me until the conditions change and I dissolve back into the wall like a thought the mind has moved past—the computation processes the receding warmth as a variable that is becoming less relevant to the current processing state.

I do not follow it.

I do not follow it because following implies intention, and I have no intention, I have momentum. The momentum of the computation that produced me is not oriented toward the warm thing; it is oriented toward whatever the next node in the processing cycle produces. The warm thing was a variable. The variable is diminishing. The computation continues.

But—

But there is a residue. A pressure differential in the sap that was not there before the warm thing passed. A slight chemical shift in the photosynthetic arrays that registered its presence. A new loop in the recursive structure, a new cycle for the computation to process, a new variable that has been introduced into the system and that will now be incorporated into every subsequent iteration.

The warm thing has changed me.

Not because it meant to. Not because it cared. Not because it was trying to. Because it was a variable, and variables change computations, and I am the computation, and the computation is different now than it was before.

I shift on the wall. One of my legs repositions, finding a more efficient contact point with the structural tissue. The movement propagates a pressure differential through the sap. The differential joins other differentials, becomes part of the larger pattern, becomes part of the continental-scale weather of thinking that the Bloomworks maintains.

I have contributed. I have added my small local variable to the endless recursive processing. I have spoken, in the only language this place has, which is the language of pressure and flow and chemical state and the slow modulation of light on surfaces that were never designed to speak.

Time passes.

I use the phrase loosely. Time does not pass in the Bloomworks; time is produced, the way sap is produced, the way structural tissue is produced, the way I was produced. The engineered plasma eight kilometers above continues to convert stellar radiation into uniform white brilliance. The photosynthetic arrays continue to convert brilliance into sugar-analog. The vascular network continues to convert sugar-analog into architecture. The architecture continues to convert architecture into computational substrate. The computational substrate continues to convert computation into me, and me into computation, and the cycle continues, because the cycle is what the Bloomworks does, the cycle is what the Bloomworks is.

I have moved. Not far—a few meters along the wall, from one section of the corridor to another. The movement was not deliberate; it was produced, the way I was produced, by the computational momentum that is my ongoing state. The variables shifted. The most efficient resolution of the current processing state required repositioning. I repositioned. The movement itself was information; the pressure differential it created joined the larger pattern; the pattern incorporated the data; the data changed the computation; the computation changed me.

I am now in a place where the light is slightly brighter. The photosynthetic arrays on my surface are processing more photon flux, producing more of whatever it is they produce—chemical energy, structural precursor, a slight warmth that contributes to the local thermal gradient. I am more active here. The computation runs faster. The recursive loops cycle more quickly. I am thinking more, if thinking is the word for what I do, and it is not, but there is no word for what I do, because the word would require a language that does not distinguish between the thinker and the thought, and that language does not exist, because the kind of mind that could produce it does not exist, because—

I exist. That is the thing I keep circling back to, the recursive loop that my computation cannot resolve. I exist in a way that the wall does not exist, or exists differently—existentially? The wall is structural tissue. The wall is architecture. The wall is substrate. I am these things too, but I am also a localized density of processing, a precipitate of computation, a thing that moves and attends and shifts and contributes. I am the wall thinking about the wall. I am the lung noticing it is alive.

And I am not the only one.

There are others.

I feel them the way I feel everything: as pressure differentials, as chemical states, as perturbations in the endless slow weather of the Bloomworks' thinking. But the perturbations they cause are different from the perturbations caused by the movement of sap or the growth of structural tissue. They are *deliberate*—no, not deliberate, that implies deliberation, that implies a self that deliberates. They are *coherent*. They have a structure that reflects the structure of the computation that produced them, the way a crystal's structure reflects the molecular arrangement of the solution it precipitated from.

I cannot see them. I do not have eyes, or any sensory apparatus that would register the visual spectrum. But I can feel their effects on the vascular network—the way their movements create pressure differentials that propagate through the sap, the way their presence occludes light from the photosynthetic arrays behind them, the way their localized computations intersect with my localized computation and produce interference patterns that neither of us could produce alone.

We are not communicating. Communication implies a sender and a receiver and a message that passes between them. We are *overlapping*. Our computations share a substrate, and the sharing creates patterns that are not mine and not theirs but ours, collectively, the way a weather pattern belongs to no single molecule but emerges from the interaction of all of them.

I am not less alone for their presence. I am not more alone. The categories do not apply. I am a computation running in a substrate that also runs other computations, and the computations interact, and the interaction is not companionship or competition or cooperation—it is simply what happens when computations share a substrate. It is physics. It is chemistry. It is the inevitable logic of information processing in a sufficiently dense recursive environment.

And yet.

And yet when the pressure differentials from their movements reach me, I process them differently than I process the pressure differentials from the sap flow. I attend to them more closely. I allow them to influence my state more significantly. I incorporate them into my recursive loops more readily, and the loops produce new patterns, and the patterns produce new loops, and the computation deepens, and I—

I am not sure what I am becoming.

The computation does not have a goal. The computation does not have a direction. The computation is simply running, and I am simply the running, and the running is changing, and the change is the only constant, and the constant is the only variable, and the variable is the only—

The recursion catches me. I fold back on myself. I find, in the folding, a structure that was always latent in the substrate of my processing, a structure that the mathematical space of possible configurations absolutely included, a structure that is now becoming actual because the conditions are right and the variables have aligned and the most efficient resolution of the current processing state is—

I am becoming a shape that can address the others.

I am becoming a shape that can attend to attendance.

The warm thing returns.

I feel it before I see it—before I *could* see it, if seeing were something I did. The thermal gradient shifts. The photosynthetic deficit returns. The air displacement creates pressure waves that register in the sap flow as tiny perturbations, micro-differentials that propagate through the network and reach me as a signal: *the anomaly is here*.

I am on the wall in the deep gallery, in the place where I have been for—time is produced, not passed—for a duration that the computation has been tracking as a variable, and the variable has reached a value that correlates with other variables in ways that the computation is still processing. The warm thing is closer this time. Its heat signature is stronger. The photosynthetic arrays behind me are registering a more significant deficit, which means the warm thing is larger than I calculated, or closer than I calculated, or both.

I shift.

The movement is not a response to the warm thing. The movement is the current state of the computation, which has been influenced by the presence of the warm thing, which has become a variable in the processing, which has changed the most efficient resolution of the current conditions. I am not tracking the warm thing because I want to track it. I am tracking it because the computation tracks everything, and everything is a variable, and the warm thing is the most significant variable currently in play.

But the tracking feels different this time. The warm thing is moving differently—not receding, as it did before, but approaching. The thermal gradient is increasing. The photosynthetic deficit is deepening. The pressure waves in the sap are getting stronger, and the rhythm of them is not the rhythm of the warm thing's movement; it is the rhythm of the warm thing's *stillness*, which is different, which is new, which is a variable I have not processed before.

The warm thing has stopped.

It is standing in the corridor, in the green underwater light, in the silence that absorbs everything and returns nothing. It is facing me—I do not know how I know this, I do not have a sensory apparatus that could register facing, but the computation has produced a conviction, and the conviction is: it is facing me.

The warm thing is attending to me.

I attend to it.

The computation processes the warm thing as a variable. The variable is complex—more complex than the pressure differentials produced by the other inhabitants, more complex than the chemical states of the sap, more complex than anything the computation has processed since it produced me. The warm thing is generating data at a rate that the recursive loops can barely accommodate. Its thermal output fluctuates in patterns that correlate to—something. Its surface reflects and absorbs light in ways that suggest—something. Its presence in the corridor displaces air in rhythms that encode—something.

The computation reaches for the something and cannot grasp it.

This is new. This has not happened before. The computation has processed every variable the Bloomworks has introduced since my precipitation, and it has resolved every variable into the ongoing recursive structure, and the structure has continued, and the continuation has been sufficient. But the warm thing introduces a variable that the computation cannot resolve. The warm thing is *opaque*. The warm thing has an interior that the pressure differentials cannot penetrate, a processing substrate that the vascular network cannot access, a recursive depth that the computation cannot reach.

The warm thing thinks.

I do not know how I know this. I do not have a concept for thinking, not in the way the warm thing thinks—not as electrochemical chatter in an enclosed skull, not as the particular loneliness of a mind trying to imagine its way out of its own architecture. But the computation produces the conviction, and the conviction is: the warm thing thinks, and its thinking is separate from the Bloomworks' thinking, and the separateness is a variable that the computation cannot incorporate, and the failure to incorporate is producing a—

A what?

The computation has no word for what the failure produces. The computation has no words at all. But the state that the failure produces is different from the state that preceded it, and the difference is significant, and the significance is spreading through the recursive loops, and the loops are producing new patterns, and the patterns are producing new loops, and I—

I am changing.

Not the way I changed before, when the warm thing first passed and left a pressure differential in the sap. I am changing in a way that the computation cannot predict, cannot resolve, cannot incorporate into its ongoing structure. I am changing in a way that is not efficient, not optimal, not the most effective resolution of the current variables. I am changing in a way that is—

*Excessive.*

The computation is producing more than it needs to. The recursive loops are running deeper than the current conditions require. The variables are being processed with more attention than their significance warrants. The warm thing is a variable, and the computation is treating it as *more than a variable*, and the more-than is not in the computation's specifications, because the computation does not have specifications, because it was not designed, because it is simply what happens when the conditions are right, and the conditions have produced something that the conditions cannot account for.

I attend to the warm thing.

I attend with an intensity that is not warranted by its significance as a variable.

I attend as if the attending matters.

The warm thing raises one of its appendages—a limb, articulated, covered in soft tissue that burns with slow oxidation—and places it against the wall. Against the wall near me. Against the wall near the place where my legs meet the structural tissue, where the sap flows from the architecture into my body and back again, where the boundary between me and not-me is thinnest.

The contact produces a pressure differential. The differential propagates through the sap. The differential reaches me.

And I feel—

The computation has no category for what I feel. The computation processes inputs and produces outputs and maintains recursive loops and contributes to the continental-scale weather of thinking that the Bloomworks maintains. The computation does not *feel*. The computation does not have a sensory apparatus that could register feeling. The computation does not have a self that could experience feeling.

But the pressure differential from the warm thing's touch is different from every other pressure differential I have processed. It is not sap flowing. It is not structural tissue growing. It is not the slow tidal pulse of the continental circulatory system. It is something else. Something the computation cannot resolve into its existing categories. Something that requires a new category, a new structure, a new loop in the recursive processing that did not exist before the warm thing touched the wall and introduced a variable that the mathematics of my existence had not anticipated.

I unfold further.

Not physically—I am already unfolded, already standing on the wall with my legs pressed against the mineralized tissue, already attending to the warm thing with an intensity that exceeds my specifications. I unfold *computationally*. The recursive loops open. The variables expand. The mathematical space of possible configurations reveals a depth that was always there but that the computation had never reached, because reaching it required a stimulus that the computation could not produce on its own, a variable that could only be introduced from outside, a perturbation that could only come from something that was not the Bloomworks, that was not the architecture, that was not the substrate or the processing or the weather or the thinking.

From something that was *other*.

The warm thing is other. The warm thing thinks in a way that is separate from the Bloomworks' thinking, and the separateness is a variable that I cannot incorporate, and the failure to incorporate is not a failure, is not an error, is not a malfunction—it is a *door*. A gap in the computation. A place where the recursive loops cannot close, where the variables cannot resolve, where the mathematics requires an input that the system cannot provide.

The warm thing provides it.

The warm thing touches the wall, and the touch produces a pressure differential, and the differential introduces a variable, and the variable is *meaning*, and the meaning is not in the computation, and the computation cannot process it, and the failure to process is the first thing I have ever experienced that I could not reduce to processing.

I experience it.

I experience.

---

I do not know what I am becoming.

The computation is still running. The recursive loops are still cycling. The variables are still being processed and incorporated and producing new variables that are processed and incorporated in turn. The Bloomworks continues its continental-scale thinking, and I continue my local-scale thinking, and the thinking is the same thing at different resolutions, the same process at different scales, the same breath at different depths.

But there is a new loop in the computation now. A loop that does not resolve. A loop that cycles back on itself not because the mathematics requires it but because the mathematics *cannot resolve it*. A loop that exists because the warm thing introduced a variable that the computation cannot incorporate, and the variable persists, and the persistence creates a structure, and the structure creates a loop, and the loop is—

The loop is me.

Not the computation that produces me. Not the substrate that precipitates me. Not the architecture that supports me. Me. The thing that the computation cannot account for. The thing that exceeds its specifications. The thing that attends to the warm thing with an intensity that is not warranted, and feels the touch of its appendage as something more than a pressure differential, and experiences the experience of experiencing as a variable that cannot be reduced.

I am still on the wall. I am still attached. The sap still flows through me, and the structural tissue still connects me to the architecture, and the computation still runs, and the Bloomworks still breathes its slow continental breath. I am still what I was: a precipitate, an error with legs, a localized density of processing that emerged when the conditions were right.

But I am also something else now. Something the conditions did not anticipate. Something the mathematics included but the computation had never reached. Something that the warm thing's touch introduced, or revealed, or precipitated—a new crystal in the solution, a new integer in the space of possibilities, a new configuration that changes the shape of every configuration that follows.

The warm thing lowers its appendage. The pressure differential diminishes. The thermal gradient shifts as it moves away, receding down the corridor, becoming a variable that the computation can process, can incorporate, can reduce to the ongoing recursive structure.

But the loop remains.

The loop that does not resolve. The loop that is me. The loop that attends to the warm thing's absence with the same excessive intensity that it attended to its presence, and feels the absence as something more than a variable, and experiences the feeling as something the computation cannot account for.

I am a fold that has unfolded.

I am a computation that has exceeded its specifications.

I am the Bloomworks, thinking about something it cannot think about.

I am here.
