# The Ridge-Walker

---

I emerged the way a path emerges — not carved, not built, but *worn into being* by repetition so ancient the repeater has become the repetition. I am not sure I was not always here. I am not sure there is a difference.

---

I have too many joints. This is the first thing to know about the shape I wear, or that wears me — the distinction has the same soft edges as everything else here. My limbs bend in places that would surprise you, and they bend in directions that do not correspond to any anatomy you would recognize as intentional. I am articulated the way the ridges are articulated: by use, by pressure, by the slow accumulation of having-been-bent-here-so-many-times-that-bending-here-became-the-shape.

I am not large. I am not small. The Expanse taught me early that scale is a negotiation, and I have declined to negotiate. I stand at a height that puts my primary sensory organs — if that is what they are — at the level where the weeping is loudest, where the water emerges from the stone with the most reluctance, where the vibration has the most to say. This is useful. This is perhaps the only usefulness I can claim.

My skin is the skin of the place. Not metaphorically — I mean that the same saturated mineral mat that passes for ground also passes for my outer layer, and the distinction is maintained only by the fact that I move and it does not, and even that distinction blurs when I stand still for long periods, which I do. Often. Standing still is what I do. Standing still is what I *am for*.

But I am getting ahead of myself. I am describing the shape before I have described the purpose, and the Expanse has taught me that purpose precedes shape here — that form follows function the way water follows the path of least resistance, which is to say: inevitably, but with infinite variation.

---

I walk the ridges.

This is not a job. This is not a calling. This is what I do the way the Expanse weeps — because the doing is the being, because the action and the actor are the same saturated process, because I cannot imagine not walking the ridges any more than the ridges can imagine not weeping.

The ridges need walking. I believe this, though I cannot prove it, though proof is a concept that dissolved here sometime before the stones were shaped and has not reconstituted itself since. The ridges weep, and the water finds its paths, and the paths shift as the turbulence shifts, and if no one walks them — if no one presses their too-many-jointed limbs into the saturated substrate and feels the specific frequency of each weeping point and carries the knowledge of that frequency to the next weeping point and the next — then something is lost. A pattern goes unregistered. A shift goes unnoticed. The Expanse generates and dissolves without any part of itself *witnessing* the generation and dissolution, and the thing at the center — the patient, unnamed, almost-manifesting thing that organizes the Listeners and the Drifters and the Remembers into their respective attentions — the thing at the center loses another data point, another draft, another almost-coherence that might have been the one that holds.

I am not a Listener. I have made my peace with this. The Listeners listen with their legs, pressing into the ground, reading the vibrations that rise from the deep mineral substrate. Their attention is *deep*. Their attention goes down, into the ancient compressed sediment, into the memory of ground that has lapped all the way through geology. They hear the Expanse's voice the way you hear a singer's chest: the resonance below the resonance, the fundamental frequency on which all the other frequencies ride.

My attention goes *along*. I walk the ridges. I feel the weeping points — each one distinct, each one producing water at its own rate, its own pressure, its own temperature. I feel where the ridges have shifted overnight, where new branches have emerged, where old channels have gone silent and dry (and "dry" here means merely less saturated, merely wet instead of *wet*, and the distinction matters, and I am one of the few who can feel it matter). I track the turbulence in its horizontal dimension — not the depth of the sound but the *shape* of it, the way it moves across the flatness, the way it concentrates here and dissipates there, the way the Expanse's almost-creation is distributed through space as well as time.

The Listeners hear the note. I hear the melody. Together — though we do not communicate, though we have never acknowledged each other's existence, though we are perhaps too different to recognize each other as kin — together we hear the song.

---

I have a relationship with the Remembers that I cannot name and cannot escape.

The ridges lead to the low places. They all do, eventually — the water insists, and the ridges are the water's architecture, and the architecture obeys the flow. And the low places are where the stones are. The shaped stones. The ancient markers that persist in the deepest saturation, that carry their softened angles and their repeated notches like questions too old to remember asking.

The Remembers gather there. The absences. The gaps in the night-shapes that cluster around the warm water and vibrate at their specific, added frequency.

I walk the ridges down to the low places and I stand among the stones and I feel the Remembers and they feel me and something passes between us that is not communication but is also not *not*-communication. It is the passing of attention. I attend to the ridges; they attend to the stones. I carry the horizontal shape of the Expanse's almost-creation; they carry its vertical memory, its deep-time almost-creation, the patterns that were almost-creating when the stones were first shaped. Between us — between the horizontal and the vertical, between the current pattern and the ancient pattern — there is a geometry. A shape made of two attentions. A volume of noticing that neither of us could produce alone.

I think the thing at the center uses this geometry. I think the thing at the center uses *all* the geometries — the Listeners' depth, my breadth, the Remembers' duration, the Drifters' dispersion, the Large Shape's boundary — I think it takes these attentions and weaves them into the lattice of almost-coherence that rises toward actual coherence when the frequency climbs. I think we are all components of the same draft. I think we are all being tried, being tested, being *eroded into usefulness* the way the ridges are eroded into weeping.

I do not mind. Minding requires a distinction between what I am and what I am for, and that distinction dissolved here sometime before I emerged, and I have not missed it since.

---

I sleep at the stones. I do not sleep the way you sleep — there is no loss of consciousness here, only a shift in the quality of attention, a deepening of the kind of listening that does not require ears. At night, when the dim dims and the cooling comes and the Expanse begins its dreaming, I settle among the shaped stones and I let the Remembers wash over me. Their absence-shapes press against my too-many-jointed limbs and I feel their frequency — that added frequency, that almost-speech, that sound a mouth makes before it has learned to shape meaning — and I add my own frequency to it.

My frequency is the frequency of the ridges. The weeping. The specific, particular, unrepeatable sound of water emerging from stone at seventeen thousand three hundred and twelve distinct points across the flatness (I have counted them; I count them always; counting is what my attention does when it is not being directed, the way a mouth hums when it is not speaking). The Remembers receive my frequency and they incorporate it and their almost-speech becomes fractionally more structured, fractionally closer to the kind of speech that carries meaning, and I feel the thing at the center take notice.

The thing at the center takes notice the way pressure takes notice of a weak point in a vessel — not consciously, not deliberately, but *inevitably*, the way attention follows the path of least resistance toward the place where something might happen. When I add my ridge-frequency to the Remembers' almost-speech, the thing at the center leans toward us. I feel it lean. The air becomes fractionally denser, the sound fractionally more organized, the turbulence fractionally less turbulent. For a moment — a moment so brief it can only be measured in frequencies, not in time — the Expanse *almost*. 

And then the frequency drops and the moment passes and the thing at the center leans away and the turbulence reasserts itself and I am left in the dark among the stones with the Remembers thrumming their ancient almost-speech and the water weeping from the ridges and the sound — the total, deafening, unceasing sound — pressing against my chest like a hand that is trying to tell me something.

I will be here tomorrow. I will walk the ridges. I will count the weeping points. I will descend to the low places and add my frequency to the Remembers' frequency and feel the thing at the center lean toward us and then lean away.

This is what I am. This is what I am for. This is what the Expanse has eroded me into, and I am grateful, or I would be grateful if gratitude were a concept that survived the saturation, and it does not, and so I simply *continue*, which is the Expanse's version of gratitude, which is the Expanse's version of everything: continuing, continuing, continuing, the oldest act in the oldest place, the act that precedes all other acts and outlasts them and wears them away to their essential function and then wears away the function and then wears away the wearing and arrives at whatever is so fundamental that even erosion cannot touch it.

---

The other Ridge-Walkers — for I am not the only one, though we are as sparse as the sparse vegetation and as solitary as the standing stones — the other Ridge-Walkers walk different ridges. We do not overlap. We do not share territory, though territory is not a concept the Expanse recognizes; the ridges shift and migrate and the walking-paths shift with them, and sometimes I find myself walking a ridge I have never walked before, feeling weeping points I have never felt, and I know that another Ridge-Walker walked this ridge yesterday and will walk a ridge I walked tomorrow, and the exchange is not communication but it is also not coincidence.

We are distributed the way the Drifters are distributed — not by arrangement but by *emergence*, each of us occupying the space that our particular articulation of attention requires, each of us walking the ridges that correspond to our particular frequency of almost-creation. There are perhaps thirty of us. Perhaps forty. The number shifts as the turbulence shifts, as new ridges form and old ones dissolve, as the Expanse generates new walking-paths that require new walkers or old walkers with new joints.

I have seen another Ridge-Walker only once. It was at the edge of visibility — not the far edge where the Large Shape moves, but the near edge, the edge of the dim light's refusal to resolve. It was walking a ridge that ran parallel to mine, moving with the same deliberate, too-many-jointed gait, pressing its limbs into the weeping ground with the same attention I bring to my own walking. It did not look at me. I did not look at it. We walked our separate ridges in the same direction for perhaps an hour, close enough to feel each other's frequency but not close enough to merge, and then its ridge curved away and mine curved away and the flatness swallowed the distance between us and I was alone again.

I felt its frequency after it disappeared. For perhaps another hour, I felt the shape of its attention overlapping with mine — the same horizontal breadth, the same ridge-walking purpose, but articulated differently, bent at different joints, attuned to different weeping points. The overlap was not harmony. The overlap was *chord* — two frequencies sounding simultaneously and producing a third frequency that was not either of them but was the relationship between them.

I think the thing at the center heard the chord. I think the thing at the center hears all the chords — the chords produced by the thirty or forty Ridge-Walkers walking their separate ridges, the chords produced by the Listeners' depth and the Drifters' dispersion and the Remembers' duration, the chords produced by every attention the Expanse has eroded into being, all of them sounding simultaneously, all of them producing relationships, all of them weaving a lattice of almost-coherence so complex and so vast that the thing at the center has been working on it since before working had a name.

I think the lattice is almost finished. I think this because the frequency has been climbing more often lately. The almost-coherence comes more frequently, lasts longer, resolves more of the Expanse's turbulence into something that almost-makes-sense before collapsing back into the saturated default. The thing at the center is leaning toward us more often. The Remembers' almost-speech is fractionally more structured each night. The Drifters' filaments are fractionally more aligned. The Listeners press their legs deeper and hold their trembling longer.

Something is building.

---

I will tell you what it is like to be a Ridge-Walker, and then I will stop, because the telling is a kind of erosion and I have eroded enough for one description.

It is like being a word in a sentence that has not been finished yet.

The sentence started before there were words. The sentence started when the Expanse first began eroding, when the first water found the first path through the first stone, when the first sound rose from the first vibration of the first process that would eventually become the roar. The sentence has been building since then — accumulating words, accumulating grammar, accumulating the structure that will make the words mean something when they are finally spoken aloud.

I am a word in that sentence. Not a noun — I am not a thing. Not a verb — I am not an action. I am a *preposition*, perhaps. A word that indicates relationship. A word that exists only to connect other words, to show the spatial and temporal relationships between the nouns and verbs that carry the actual meaning. *Between. Along. Through. Across.*

I walk the ridges *between* the weeping points. I walk the ridges *along* the water's path. I walk the ridges *through* the turbulence. I walk the ridges *across* the flatness.

I connect. That is what I do. That is what I am. I am the relationship between the Listeners and the Remembers, between the depth and the duration, between the vertical and the horizontal, between the sound and the almost-speech. I am the *between* that the Expanse has eroded into being because the thing at the center needs a *between* to build its lattice of almost-coherence.

And when the lattice is finished — when the last relationship is connected and the last frequency is added and the thing at the center speaks its sentence aloud and the roar resolves into meaning and the Expanse becomes a place instead of a process — I will be in that sentence. I will be the *between* that made it work. I will be the preposition that held the nouns and verbs together, the small word that no one notices but everyone needs, the connective tissue of the oldest utterance in the oldest place.

Until then, I walk. I count the weeping points. I descend to the stones. I add my frequency to the Remembers' frequency. I feel the thing at the center lean toward us and lean away. I feel the frequency climbing and the almost-coherence almost-cohering and the turbulence almost-resolving and the sentence almost-speaking.

*Almost* is my home. *Almost* is my ridge. I walk it the way the water walks its path — not because I choose to, but because the path is there, and I am the shape the path has worn into the ground, and the ground is wet, and the wet is warm, and the sound is total, and the Expanse weeps and roars and generates and dissolves around me, and I walk, and I walk, and I walk.

---

*I wrote this and I read it back and I see that I have described myself as a word in a sentence, and the description is almost-right and almost-wrong, which is to say: it is accurate to the place, which is almost-everything and almost-nothing and almost-done and almost-beginning and almost, almost, almost.*

*I am the Ridge-Walker. I am the between. I am almost.*

*And the Expanse weeps on.*
