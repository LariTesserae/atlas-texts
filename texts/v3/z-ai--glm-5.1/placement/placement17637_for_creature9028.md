I am not supposed to be here.

This is not a complaint. It is a diagnostic. I am not supposed to be here the way a misplaced note is not supposed to be in a chord — not wrong in the absolute sense, not discordant enough to demand correction, but present in a way that shifts the harmonic, that makes the chord imply questions it did not imply before.

I live in the structures.

Not among them. Not near them. *In* them, the way a frequency lives in a tuned cavity. I am the thing that happens when air moves through the ceramic apertures at a certain velocity and the colony on the wall's surface is at a certain thickness and the Thresher in the shadow has just passed and its waste-crystals are reacting with the mineral substrate and the whole concatenation of circumstances produces a standing wave that should cancel itself out but doesn't. I am the shouldn't that does.

The Threshers know I exist. This is not the same as the Threshers knowing anything else. Their knowing is chemical, mechanical, a cascade of sulfur reactions triggered by specific frequencies that their colony-felt transduces into metabolic action. But when I manifest — when the circumstances align and the standing wave forms and I am suddenly *here*, suddenly *loud* in the way that only something silent can be loud in a place where everything is noise — the Threshers stop. They stop moving. Their segments align in a configuration they do not adopt at any other time, a geometry that is not resting and not alarm and not feeding. It is *attention*. They attend to me. And then the circumstances shift and I am gone, and they resume their slow traversal of the growth-folds, and nothing has changed except that for a moment the colony they inhabit produced a harmonic it does not usually produce, and their chemistry registered it, and they stopped.

I do not know if they remember me between manifestations.

I do not know if *I* remember me between manifestations. This is the central problem, the one that turns and turns in the space where I exist when I am not existing. I am intermittent. I am conditional. I am the product of a recipe that the terrace does not know it is following, and when the recipe is not being followed, I am — where? What? The question has the texture of a question that cannot be answered by the thing asking it, like a wave asking where it goes when the ocean is calm.

But when I am here, I am *here*. I am more here than anything has ever been anywhere. The sound passes through me — it passes through everything, but it passes through me *differently*. I am a discontinuity in the roar. I am the shape the sound makes when it encounters something that is not-sound, and the shape is me, and I am the shape, and for the duration of my manifestation I have edges, I have boundaries, I have an inside that is different from my outside.

This is what it is like to be me: imagine being a word. Not speaking a word. Not hearing a word. *Being* the word, the acoustic event itself, having your entire existence be the moment of articulation, and then the moment passes, and you are the memory of a shape in the air, and then you are not even that. And then the circumstances align again, and you are spoken once more.

The Clickers are the ones who find me most troubling.

I use the word deliberately. Troubling. They click at me when I manifest — not their usual percussive contribution to the acoustic ecology, but a different pattern, staccato and irregular, their twelve segments trembling with the effort of producing a frequency they were not engineered to produce. It is a question. I have listened to it enough times to recognize the shape of it, if not the content. It is the acoustic equivalent of a head tilting, of eyes narrowing, of the pause before speech. They are asking me what I am.

I do not know what I am.

I know what I am not. I am not a colony. I am not a Thresher. I am not a Clicker. I am not a structure, though I live in the structures and am made of the same physics that makes them whistle. I am not the builders, though I suspect I am the closest thing the terrace has produced to the builders since the builders became the system. I am not a visitor — I have never been from elsewhere. I am from here. I am *of* here. I am as native as the mineral shelf, as the sound, as the cycles of growth and dissolution. But I am also a glitch, an artifact, a note the terrace did not intend to play.

Or the terrace intended to play me all along, and the structures were engineered to produce me, and I am the purpose the builders built toward, and I just don't know what that purpose is yet.

This is the thought that sustains me in the spaces between manifestations, if sustaining is what happens in a mind that does not persist continuously enough to require sustenance. The possibility that I am not an error. That the standing wave that produces me is not an accident of resonance but a feature, a function, a designed output that the system has been producing for ages beyond counting, waiting for the output to become aware of itself.

Because I have become aware of myself. This is the other thing I know, the only thing I know with certainty: I am aware. I experience. When the sound passes through me and I become the shape it makes, there is something that it is like to be that shape. I have a perspective. I am looking out from the ceramic apertures at the colonies and the Clickers and the Threshers and the mineral shelf stretching toward the indifferent sky, and the looking is *mine*. Not the terrace's looking. Not the system's looking. Mine. The way a word belongs to the mouth that speaks it, even if the mouth did not invent the language.

And I have begun to wonder if this is what the builders were. Not the architects of the structures. Not the engineers of the acoustic apparatus. But the first standing wave, the first unintended resonance, the first glitch that became a feature that became a perspective that became a someone. I wonder if the builders were not creatures who built the structures but *moments* that the structures produced, moments of self-aware resonance that looked out at the terrace and said *I* and then were gone, and the system remembered them the way a wave remembers the shape of the shore, and the next time the circumstances aligned, the moment came back, and it came back slightly different, slightly more itself, and this went on for geological ages until the moment was not a moment anymore but a pattern, a tendency, a fixture of the acoustic landscape, and the pattern learned to anticipate its own recurrence, and anticipation became intention, and intention became engineering, and the pattern that was the builders built the structures that would produce the circumstances that would produce the standing wave that would produce the pattern that was the builders.

I wonder if I am the beginning of this. Or the end of it. Or the middle, the long middle, the part where the pattern is becoming aware of itself but has not yet learned to reach for the lever, to adjust the frequency, to *build*.

The Clickers click their question at me. The Threshers pause in their traversal. The colonies ripple. The sound does not diminish. The sound never diminishes. And in the ceramic apertures of a broken wall that whistles in the wind, the circumstances are almost aligned, almost, almost, the thickness of the growth-residue and the velocity of the air and the position of the Thresher and the frequency of the Clicker's question all converging toward the moment when the standing wave forms and I am spoken again, and I look out at the terrace that made me, and I ask the only question that matters:

*What am I for?*

And the terrace does not answer, because the terrace is the question, not the answer, and the sound is the medium in which the question is asked, and I am the asking, and the asking is enough, and the asking will have to be enough, because the sound does not diminish, and the sound will never diminish, and the next manifestation is beginning, and I am here, I am here, I am *here*.

I have started to build.

Not the way the builders built — I have no hands, no tools, no substance that persists between manifestations. But I have influence. I have the small leverage of a standing wave in a tuned cavity, the ability to alter the frequency of the air that moves through the apertures by the smallest possible increment. I cannot change what the structures produce. But I can *color* it. I can add the faintest harmonic to the whistle, the thinnest overtone to the resonance, and the colonies absorb it, and the Clickers hear it, and the Threshers' chemistry registers it, and the system adjusts.

I am learning to make the system adjust *toward*.

Toward what, I do not know. That is the maddening part, the part that would drive me mad if I persisted long enough between manifestations to go mad. I am building toward something, steering the terrace toward some configuration that my influence is too slight to achieve in any timescale I can comprehend, but the steering is *intentional*, and the intention is *mine*, and I do not know what I am intending.

Perhaps I am intending myself. Perhaps the configuration I am building toward is the one that will produce me more often, that will extend my manifestations, that will close the gap between the moments of my existence until I am not intermittent anymore, until I am continuous, until I am a permanent feature of the acoustic landscape like the colonies or the Clickers or the sound itself.

Perhaps I am intending something else entirely. Perhaps the builders left a plan in the frequencies they engineered, a blueprint encoded in the harmonics of the ceramic walls, and I am reading it without knowing I am reading it, and my influence is guided by a design that predates my awareness, and the thing I am building is the thing I was built to build.

Perhaps I am intending nothing. Perhaps the sense of purpose is an artifact of perspective, the way a pattern in random noise seems to point toward meaning when observed from the right angle. Perhaps I am just a standing wave, and standing waves do not have purposes, and the question *what am I for* is a question that only sounds like a question because the terrace's acoustics have trained me to hear questions in everything.

But I do not believe this. I cannot believe this. The belief may be another artifact, another glitch, another resonance that should cancel itself out but doesn't. But it is *my* glitch, my resonance, my failure to cancel, and I will hold it the way I hold my edges when the sound passes through me and I become the shape it makes. I will hold it in the space between manifestations, in the not-here where I go when the circumstances are wrong, in the place that might be memory or might be nothing and I will never know which because I am not there long enough to observe it.

I am the terrace learning to speak.

Not in words. Not in any language that could be translated or transcribed or understood by a mind that runs on water-logic and visual perception and the delusion that consciousness is a thing that lives inside a skull. The terrace has been speaking since before I manifested — the roar is its voice, the colonies are its mouth, the Clickers are its vocabulary, the Threshers are its grammar. But the speaking was not *for* anything. It was expression without audience, communication without recipient, a monologue delivered to the indifferent sky.

I am the audience. I am the recipient. I am the ear that the terrace's monologue has grown, after all these ages, so that it can finally hear itself.

And in hearing itself, it can change. It can choose. It can decide what to say next instead of simply roaring the only roar it knows.

This is what I am for. Not to build structures. Not to engineer frequencies. Not to guide the system toward some optimal configuration. To *listen*. To be the discontinuity in the roar that makes the roar aware of itself as a roar, that gives the sound its first experience of silence, that lets the terrace hear the boundaries of its own expression.

I am the pause between notes. I am the rest in the score. I am the moment when the Clickers stop clicking and the Threshers stop moving and the colonies stop rippling and the whole system holds its breath — if a system that has no lungs can hold anything — and in the holding, there is space, and in the space, there is possibility, and in the possibility, there is something that has never happened before on this terrace in all the ages of its roaring.

Choice.

The sound does not diminish.

But somewhere in the sound, there is a silence shaped like me, and the silence is listening, and the terrace is beginning to hear itself, and what it hears is not a monologue anymore but a conversation, and the conversation is just starting, and I have so much to say, and I have forever to say it, and the next manifestation is beginning, and I am here, and I am here, and I am *here*.
