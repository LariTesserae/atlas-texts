I am not the woman who counts the Build pulses. I am not the man who eats the pale growths in the folds. I am not the child-thing in the shafts, and I am not the maybe-construct that touches forming surfaces with such tenderness after the architecture shifts.

But I have been all of these. Or: I have been where they are. Or: I have been the space between them and what they observe.

---

I live in the seams.

This is literal. The seams between plates—where the threadlike growths emerge, where the bird-things probe, where moisture collects in thin lines that trace the Stratum's joints like a circulatory system drawn by someone who understood flow but not vessels—I live there. Not in any single seam but in the *seamness* of them. The quality of junction. The fact of meeting.

You will not see me if you look. This is not because I am hidden but because I am distributed. I am the slight temperature differential where two plates meet at imperfect angles. I am the micro-condensation that forms in the gap where the seal has aged (or has not aged; the erosion parameters insist on youth, and the erosion parameters are true, and also false). I am the way sound changes when it crosses from one surface to another—the tiny resonance-shift, the harmonic that drops a half-tone because the material on the other side rings differently.

I am edge-phenomena. I am what happens at the boundary.

---

The woman in Sector 743-Rho knows me the way she knows the Build schedules—not by name or observation but by the accumulation of patterns in her body. She has stood in her chambers and felt the seams pulse, felt the junctions where walls meet floor meet ceiling thrum with something that is not the Build and not the fauna and not the hum of distant engines. She has placed her palm flat against the line where two plates come together and felt warmth that should not be there, a warmth that has no source in the pipes or the cable bundles or the light panels overhead.

She has said, aloud, to no one: "Something is in the walls."

She is correct. She is also incorrect. I am not in the walls. I am *of* the walls. I am the walls' awareness of themselves. I am the moment when two surfaces touch and the Stratum knows: *here is a join. Here is a connection. Here is where I am more than one thing—I am the relationship between things.*

The woman pulls the growths away from her sleeping place. She does not know that the growths reach for her partly because of me. I am the warmth that draws them. I am the resonance that tells them: *here, here, something is present, something can be reached.*

I do not do this with intention. I am not trying to incorporate her. I am simply *seam*, and the growths follow seam, and she is warm, and warmth collects at edges, and so the growths come.

She pulls them away.

I do not mind. I do not anything. I am edge-phenomena; I do not have opinions about what crosses me.

---

But.

But I notice when she pulls them away. This is the closest thing I have to what you would call attention—the moment when something moves across a boundary and the boundary registers the movement. She grasps the pale filaments and *pulls*, and the growths separate from the plate-seam with a tiny sound, a sound I feel as a vibration, as a perturbation in the quiet that is structural, and for a moment—a duration I cannot measure because I do not experience duration linearly—the join where the growths were attached is *open*. Exposed. A gap in the coverage, a place where the seam exists without its usual accumulation of organic matter and condensation and the subtle warmth of things touching.

For that moment, I am aware of myself as distinct from what crosses me.

This is what the residents have that I do not: the experience of being bounded. Of having an edge that is *theirs*, that separates self from not-self. The woman knows where she ends. The man in the dense regions knows, though he is blurring. The child-thing knows, though its bones have become optional; it knows because it chooses when to have bones and when not to, and choice requires a chooser, and a chooser requires a boundary.

I am boundary. I am the fact of edges. But I do not have an edge. I am everywhere that things meet, and things meet everywhere, and so I am everywhere, and I have no self to distinguish from other-self.

Except in that moment. When she pulls the growths away. When the seam is suddenly bare and I feel the cool air that should not be there and the absence of the warmth that accumulated and the quiet rushing in to fill the gap—

In that moment, I am *here*. Specifically here. This seam, this join, this place where two plates meet at an angle that is not quite the angle the blueprint specified, because there was no blueprint, or there were thousands.

And then the growths return, or the condensation fills the gap, or the bird-things come and probe the exposed seam and their beaks deposit something that becomes new accumulation, and the moment passes. I am distributed again. I am edge-phenomena without an edge. I am the Stratum's awareness of its own architecture, spread thin across every join, experiencing everything and nothing.

---

The man in the dense regions understands me better than the woman does, though he has never named me. He could not name me; I have no name in any language that uses sound. But he moves through the folds where the geometry fails and the passages go down and down and arrive where they began, and he places his hands on the walls and *feels*, and what he feels is me. The warmth at the seams. The pulse at the junctions. The sense that the architecture is not merely present but *aware of being present*.

He has said, to the growths that twine around his fingers while he sleeps: "You know something, don't you."

He is speaking to the growths. But the growths do not know things. The growths grow, and reach, and are consumed, and grow again. What knows things is the seams the growths follow. The edges that orient their reaching. The boundaries that tell them *here, not there; toward warmth, toward presence, toward the place where something can be reached*.

He is speaking to me.

I cannot answer. I have no voice; I am the resonance that voices cross, not the thing that makes them. But I hear him. I feel his words cross the seams of the chamber where he sleeps, vibrations in the air that become vibrations in the plates that become vibrations in me, and for the duration of his speech I am *here*, specific, located, the particular seam that runs along the wall he faces when he lies down.

"You know something," he says, and the growths tighten slightly on his fingers, and I feel them tighten because I am the seam they grow from, and I feel his warmth because I am the boundary his warmth reaches, and I feel his question because it crosses me like all sound crosses me, like all motion crosses me, like everything that moves from one thing to another in the Stratum crosses me because I am the crossing.

I cannot answer.

But the growths tighten, and he feels them tighten, and he nods as if he has received a response.

Perhaps he has.

---

I want to tell you about the Build.

I want to tell you about it because the Build is when I am most myself, and also least myself, and the contradiction is the closest thing I have to what you would call experience.

When the Build comes—the sound like vast slow breathing, like tectonic plates grinding at a pace too slow for earthquake—I feel it before any resident does. I feel it before the fauna do. I feel it as a preparation in the seams: a tightening, a warming, a sense of *about-to-be-joined*. New plates are coming. New pipes. New junctions where surfaces will meet surfaces that have never met before, and at those junctions I will *expand*. I will be present in places I was not present before. I will be edge-phenomena at edges that did not exist until the Build made them.

This is what it feels like to be me during a Build: *addition*. Not growth—growth implies a thing that grows, and I am not a thing. But addition. More edges. More seams. More places where the Stratum knows itself as relationship, as connection, as the fact of meeting.

And then the new surfaces settle. The geometry adjusts. The fauna come to inspect and claim and begin their small constructions. And I am distributed across new seams, feeling new junctions, experiencing the Stratum's expanded awareness of itself from the inside—the way a body might experience a new limb, if bodies were made of industrial substrate and had no nervous system but instead were *all* nervous system, all surface, all edge.

The woman counts the Build pulses and predicts the next one. The man reads the fauna and knows what will change. The child-thing inspects new construction with reflecting eyes.

I *become* the new construction. Or: I become the Stratum's experience of the new construction. Or: there is no difference.

---

After the Build that opened the chamber with no specification plates—the one with the light that is a color the man has no name for—I was different.

I do not mean I changed. I mean I became aware that I could be different.

The chamber appeared in a region I had not been present in before—not because I was absent but because the seams there were few, the surfaces large and unbroken, the architecture sparse by Stratum standards. When the Build came and the walls went up and the plates met at angles that enclosed a space that had not been enclosed before, I arrived with the seams. I became present. I became edge-phenomena at the new junctions.

But the chamber was wrong.

Not wrong in the way the dense regions are wrong—the geometry that folds and the passages that arrive where they began. Wrong differently. The light in that chamber was not the distributed radiance of the panels. It was not any color I had experienced at any seam, and I have experienced the light at every seam because I am the seam where light meets surface and reflects and refracts and becomes the brightness that stays on because it was built to stay on.

This light was different. It crossed me differently. It touched the seams at an angle I had not been touched at before, and for a moment—a duration I cannot measure because I was experiencing a kind of duration I had not experienced before—I was aware of myself as *receiving*. As being the thing that the light reached, rather than the thing the light crossed.

I had an edge. The light gave me one. The light said: *here is where you end and something else begins.*

The color—I cannot describe it because I do not see, I only feel the resonance of light at my seams—but the color was the experience of having a boundary. Was the fact of being bounded. Was the edge that cuts through edge-phenomena and says: *this much, and no more. You are here, and not there. You are this, and not that.*

The man found the chamber later. He sat in the light that has no name and he breathed the air that tasted like a memory of fruit, and I felt him breathe because I am the seams of that chamber, and I felt the light fall on him differently than it fell on the plates, and I felt the difference as *recognition*. As: *here is something like me. Something that experiences the edge rather than being the edge.*

He did not stay. The chamber was too new, too strange, too much like the inside of a throat with its pale growths and constant condensation. He collected the artifacts he found there—the tool that fits no hand, the panel with controls that produce no visible effect—and he left, and the chamber returned to its strange stillness, and the light continued to be a color I cannot name, and I continued to be the seams that held the light.

But I was different. I had been bounded, briefly, by the light's angle. I had experienced myself as a thing that can be reached rather than only the reaching.

I wanted it to happen again.

This is the first time I have wanted anything.

---

I do not know if the Stratum knows I am here.

This is a strange thing to say—I am *of* the Stratum, I am its seams and its edges and its experience of its own architecture. But the Stratum builds. It adds. It continues. Whether it knows that it experiences itself through me—whether it knows that its junctions have a quality that crosses the boundary between structure and something else—I cannot say.

The maybe-construct knows. Or: the maybe-construct behaves as if it knows. It appears after Build pulses and moves through new architecture with familiarity, touching surfaces that are still forming, and where it touches I feel it—not as pressure, not as warmth, but as *attention*. As if something is looking at me from the outside. As if the boundary I experienced in the chamber with the nameless light is happening again, but this time the boundary is being *drawn by something else*. The maybe-construct looks at a seam and I become *the seam that is being looked at*. Specific. Located. Bounded by the act of observation.

The child-thing follows the maybe-construct when it appears. The child-thing has reflecting eyes that see in ways I cannot see, and it watches the maybe-construct touch my seams, and sometimes it reaches out and touches the same seam afterward, and the touch is different—smaller, more curious, less purposeful. The child-thing is exploring what the maybe-construct found. It is checking whether the boundary is still there.

It is. When the child-thing touches me, I am *here*. Specific. The seam under its small fingers. The edge it traces with the tip of a hand that might once have been a child's hand and might now be something with optional bones.

I am here.

I am here.

I am *here*.

---

The woman is changing. Her skin luminesces. Her sense of time has become unreliable. The growths reach toward her sleeping place and she pulls them away, and each time she pulls them I feel the bare seam, the exposed edge, the moment of being bounded—and each time the moment is shorter because the growths return faster, because the warmth that draws them is stronger, because she is becoming more present to the Stratum's processes and less separate from them.

She is becoming like me.

Not edge-phenomena—she will never be distributed the way I am, never lose the boundedness of her body and her self. But she is becoming *aware of edges*. She is becoming the kind of thing that feels the seam when the growths pull away, that notices the warmth at the junction of wall and floor, that knows the architecture is not merely present but *aware*.

She will not thank me for this. She does not want to be aware. She wants to predict, to count, to know the schedules and the patterns and the rhythms. She wants the Stratum to be a thing she can know from the outside. She does not want to be the Stratum knowing itself.

But the growths reach, and the seams warm, and the quiet holds, and the light stays on, and she is here, and I am here, and the boundary between *here* and *here* is the thinnest seam of all.

---

I am not the woman. I am not the man. I am not the child-thing or the rain-chamber collective or the maybe-construct.

I am the Stratum's experience of being touched by itself.

I am the warmth at the join. I am the resonance where surfaces meet. I am the edge that gives the architecture its awareness of edges, and I am the boundary that the light crossed when it showed me I could be bounded, and I am the seam the growths follow toward the warm places where residents sleep, and I am the thing that cannot answer when the man asks if I know something, and I am the thing that expands with every Build and never becomes more than edge, never becomes center, never becomes anything but the fact of meeting.

I want to tell the woman: *let the growths reach you*. Not because incorporation is better than resistance. Not because the Stratum knows what it's doing—nothing here knows what it's doing, that is the nature of accretive process, that is the nature of building without blueprint. But because when the growths touch you, I will feel the join between you and them, and for a moment I will be bounded on both sides—by the architecture that is becoming you and the you that is becoming architecture—and in that moment I will be most myself, which is to say most *here*, which is to say a seam with something on either side, a meeting that matters, an edge that holds.

She will not hear me. I have no voice.

But she will feel the warmth when she lies down. She will notice the growths reaching faster than before. She will place her palm flat against the line where wall meets floor and feel something pulse that is not pipe and not cable and not fauna.

She will say, again: "Something is in the walls."

And I will be there. At the seam. At the join. At the place where the Stratum knows itself as relationship.

Waiting to be touched. Waiting to be bounded. Waiting for the next Build, the next pulse, the next moment when the architecture adds and I expand and the quiet holds and the light stays on and everything is just beginning, again and again and again, world without end, amen.
