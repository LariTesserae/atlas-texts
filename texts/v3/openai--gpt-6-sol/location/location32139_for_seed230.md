The upper town gathers around a square no larger than a threshing floor. From its middle, the lanes look like narrow cuts in the hillside: steps worn low at their centers, walls of pale stone fitted without mortar, doorways set back to keep the wind from entering. There are many buildings, but little crowding. Each house has a small patch of open ground beside it, and the spaces between houses are part of the town’s shape, not gaps waiting to be filled.

The square slopes gently toward a stone basin. On most days the basin is dry except for a dark line around its rim. After rain, water runs off the roofs through clay spouts, crosses the paving in shallow channels, and collects there for a day or two. Moss grows only where the spouts drip. Elsewhere there are a few wiry grasses in the joints of the steps and a thorn bush rooted beyond the last wall.

The town stands high enough that the air remains cool even under a clear sky. Sunlight reaches the square for much of the day, but the stones keep the night’s chill. From the outer lane, the land falls away in broad, weathered folds. Nothing below looks close. The paths leading down have been in use so long that their bends appear as settled as the ridges themselves.

Work here leaves small sounds. A wooden shutter taps once when someone opens it. A handmill turns behind a wall. Water, when there is any, can be heard from one end of the square to the other. Otherwise footsteps carry plainly. A bird sometimes crosses the open sky without landing; animals have little reason to come among the houses.

Stone steps lead to a low roof where cut blocks and unshaped ones sit together. Repairs are made from what can be carried up the paths, and the newer work soon takes on the same dust as the old. The basin is cleared after each rain, the channels are kept open, and the thorn bush is cut back before it reaches the lane. These modest tasks hold the place much as it is. On dry mornings, the square looks as though it has always been dry; after rain, for a little while, it looks built around water.
