The chamber lies just below the desert’s hardpan, close enough to feel the day’s warmth through its ceiling. It is no bigger than a small hall, but there is little open floor. Ribs of black stone crowd one another, their edges sharp and their faces still glossy where they split. Between them stand a few iron frames, a severed conduit, and the base of a machine whose upper half was taken away. They look less like ruins than things left in place while the rock formed around them.

There is no dampness here. Dust lies in the tight angles of the floor but not on the warmest surfaces. A dry, resinous smell comes from the stone. In three or four cracks, pale stalks grow without leaves, each tipped with a comb of fine threads. They draw what they need from the chamber’s air and remain so slight that a passing creature can bend one without breaking it.

The creatures are everywhere else. Flat, six-legged animals fit themselves into seams scarcely wider than a finger; you see them first by the brief shine of their backs. Larger ones hang beneath the iron frames, crowded together like tools on hooks. They feed on the pale stalks, on dust caught in the combs, and on one another. Nothing seems to be hunting in a hurry. A hooked head emerges beside your boot, waits, and folds back into stone.

The noise makes the room feel larger than it is. Somewhere inside the old machine, a wheel turns with a low, even grind. The hanging animals answer it with a harsh, continuous rasp, while the smaller ones tick against the rock. Each sound keeps its place. After a while you can pick out the gap left when a creature stops calling, though another soon takes up the same note.

Amber light leaks from a row of sockets in the conduit. It reaches only the nearest ribs, leaving the ceiling and the far wall crowded with shapes you must find by touch or sound. Fresh-looking chips lie below the sharp stone edges, but no chips fall while you are there. The chamber gives every impression of having happened recently and of having remained exactly as it is ever since.
