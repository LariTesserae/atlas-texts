Prepare first to **stay outside it**. The gap is no taller than a hand, and its houses are stitched to living roots over soft mud. An ordinary human cannot enter without crushing streets, changing the drainage, or bringing down part of the roof. Even a finger, a probe, or a bright lamp could do damage.

Visit from the bank with a hand lens or camera that can focus in low light. Keep your weight well back from the edge, and do not lift the peat slab, dam the runoff, or touch the hanging roots. Wear boots for the wet bank and bring a quiet, dim, adjustable light; sudden heat or strong illumination may affect a place that is normally cold and nearly dark. Expect a long watch in which the most noticeable events are falling drops and tiny movements in the moss.

If your multiverse travel permits you to become small enough to enter, treat that as a separate expedition, not a convenient way to get a closer look. You would need to know that you can return to your original size *outside* the bank, and you would need protection from cold, immersion, mud, and a single falling drop. Do not assume the beetle, mites, or other traffic are harmless at your new scale. Plan a route that avoids the lanes, runoff stems, and woven dwellings, and be ready to leave without advancing farther.

Most importantly, approach the town as inhabited. Observe before trying to communicate; ask permission if you can make yourself understood. Bring no loose food or foreign organisms, take nothing living, and leave no marker behind. This settlement is already rebuilding around growth and erosion. A good visit would let you witness it without becoming another upheaval its inhabitants must survive.
