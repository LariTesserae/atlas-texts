I move seamlessly between the towering trees, my essence hovering slightly above the spongy earth, unbound by the gravity that anchors the moss and stone. My visage shimmers with an ephemeral light, a distillation of the dim luminescence which filters through the canopy. To the creatures that dwell here, I am both familiar and unseen, known by the subtle shift in air when I pass, as though the forest has exhaled a sigh of recognition.

I bear witness to the eternal exchange of life into death and back again, the continuous loop that has rendered the bones of ancient civilizations into fertile loam.

In my hands, formed from mist and starlight, I carry the resonance of the plateau—a soft music that ebbs like the forgotten tide. With each passing epoch, I guide the drift of mists, orchestrating their dance with the trees’ boughs to weave a narrative of time that the forest records in its deepest roots. Together, the network of tree and mist, stone and rain, creates a tapestry of stories writ in the silent language of growth and decay.

I am the guardian not of the visible, but of the eternal, where the echoes of time are gathered in quiet dreams. My role is to ensure that this sanctuary—this place adrift between the now and forever—remains untouched by haste, sustaining its slow, deliberate lifecycle. Through me, the forgotten ancients and the newly sprouted exist in concert, a harmonious symphony filling the vast, wefted silence.

As twilight folds and unfolds, endlessly wrapping the landscape in its soft embrace, I continue my vigil. I am one with the plateau's breath, an interlude between the ancient and the eternal. Here, my being finds purpose, as small as a raindrop, as vast as the sky, an emblem of the timeless elegance that lies at the heart of this living masterpiece.
