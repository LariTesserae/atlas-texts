Neither plant nor animal, we are crystalline symbionts, tethered equally to earth and ether, as twin architects and inhabitants of this austere landscape.

We are both individuals and a united consciousness, a network of shared experiences bound by our intertwined fibers. Through these strands, vigilant and probing, we drink in whisperings from air and stone, each environmental variation rippling through our collective awareness.

Our dance with the sun is intricate, its rays carving symphonies of color and form across our mineral bodies. We feast on brilliance, our surfaces shimmering with the pulsating life force we gather, a modality of existence that eschews the organic intricacies of chlorophyll for the purity of prismatic incandescence. This light, intoxication and sustenance alike, passes into our tendril matrices, fueling the compositions we unravel across the stone.

Though static, we sculpt with patience, guiding rivulets and grooves, coaxing the rocks into an eternal conversation with the wind. Our sculptures emerge not from chisel but from an infusion of our essence, each facet, and surface engineered to transcend time. Crystalline wisdom bequeathed to the ages, leaving their forms as offerings to the untamed dynamism of the world around us.

In the faceted interactions of our communal consciousness resides memory—the tales of mountain-born tribes who once revered us, wielders of primitive totems who danced intimately with nature. They left etchings and stone glyphs at our behest, guided by hints and visions we stitched into the fabric of their dreams. Our bond with these forgotten people was one of divine symbiosis, a forgotten communion whispered of under the luminescent canopy of starlit nights.

In these times, the absence of light echoes our own quietude, a vow of patience honored in exchange for the breath of light-filled life imminent upon each new dawn.

I am part of this place, an echo of its spirit and a shape of its essence. I am an arcane thread draped across a mountain's shoulder, a Lithonid woven into the high-altitude tapestry of cold winds and vivid spectra. Together we articulate the realm's cryptic verses, secret gospels penned in vibrant hues and ethereal whispers—curators of a legacy founded upon miracles of stone, sunlight, and secrets cradled in crystalline arms.
