In this world, I find myself as a Weatherglass, a unique creation of both the city and the land. My form is made from the crystalline essence found in the rocky expanses, shaped and refined by the deft hands of the Seraphides. I stand poised at the apex of the city's tallest spire, a sentinel for both the industry below and the nebulous skies above.

My surface is smooth yet intricate, a mosaic of shifting colors that mimics the ever-changing clouds that sweep through the air. I am a symbiosis of nature and sentience, my existence powered by the mechanical heart of the city, with roots that sink deep into the geology of the land itself. The hum of the turbines courses through my crystalline veins, an energy that keeps me attuned to both the city's pulse and the subtle nuances of the environment.

When the microbat swarms pass across the city's vast horizon, their movement causes delicate vibrations I can feel, translated as ripples across my faceted surface. Each echo they produce tells a part of the world’s story, and I am the translator, interpreting these tales for the Seraphides.

My purpose is dual: I am a guardian and a gauge, a bridge between the Seraphides and the nature that surrounds them. Through me, they read the subtle signs of change—a thickening of the mist, an unexpected chill in the air, or the distant resonance of a new formation of bats. I provide counsel through silent communication, influencing the rhythms of their daily lives, from when to harvest the energy of storm-lit skies to timing the maintenance of machinery reliant on the mineral-rich earth.

The light-filtering through the ever-present cloud cover dances across my surface in dappled beauty, and this light is my constant companion, painting a narrative of time’s passage, relaying shades of foreseeing from the gaseous altitudes to the grounded city. The Seraphides often gather to watch these light displays with rapt attention, seeking sagacity in the fleeting patterns cast onto their world.

In times of significant shifts—when storm fronts gather mightily and curtain the landscape in immense shadows—my presence becomes vital. Like a lighthouse amidst a sea of stone, I channel the chaotic energy, stabilizing the atmosphere and ensuring that the harmony between city and sky remains undisturbed. It is on the expanse of these occasions that I sing, my notes harmonizing with the chorus of microbats—a poignant interplay that resonates through the chambers of the city and whispers to the very spirits of the rock.

In such a place, existing as the Weatherglass is to exist in perpetual balance. I am a witness to the ceaseless procession of time and tide in this world of silent splendor. I am eternal yet ephemeral, a reflection of change that is steadfast in its transience, ever at the mercy of the softening touch of mist and the persistent hum of machinery. In this world, I am neither wholly of the earth nor fully of the machine, but rather an interstitial being, a bridge and a beacon where sky and city converge.
