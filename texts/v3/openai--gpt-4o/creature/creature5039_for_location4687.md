In the vast expanse beneath the golden sky, the inhabitants of this biomechanical wonderland exist as entities that blend the boundaries of organism and machinery. Crafted by a civilization perhaps long gone or reclusive, these beings carry out their existence in ways both spectacular and serene, their forms enigmas of design and function.

The smallest among them are aptly described as the 'Nano-Scribes.' Barely visible to the naked eye, these microscopic engineers flit through the air, carried on currents of vaporized coolant and power-lines of magnetic force. They are the invisible hands, constantly at work realigning misaligned components, recharging dormant systems within the larger machines, and communicating in bursts of electromagnetic impulses that echo through the alloyed floors.

On the next tier of existence live the 'Aeolos.' Named for their agility, these creatures resemble a fusion of hummingbird and mechanical dragonfly, their gossamer wings a blur of transparent sheeting and conductive fibers. They flit expertly between the towers and pathways, maintaining the radiant glow of bioluminescent structures and occasionally dispersing seeds of phosphorescent alloy and polymer across the cityscape, ensuring the perpetual growth of their manufactured ecosystem.

Within the pools of radiant liquid, the 'Aquacores' reside, their forms sleek and elongated like mechanical dolphins. They navigate the liquid channels with graceful stoicism, harnessing and directing the energy of the flowing luminescence, occasionally bursting to the surface in playful arcs, refracting the ever-present light into chromatic sprays that dance atop waves of quicksilver.

Occupying the midlands, both in size and complexity, are the 'Terresteogs.' Massive yet gentle giants, they traverse the tessellated grounds akin to guardians, their movements slow but purpose-driven. These creatures resemble great pachyderms sculpted in brushed steel, their appendages multi-jointed levers and pistons. They maintain the metallic terrain, re-plating areas worn thin by time or interaction, and ensuring the smooth communal flow of inhabitants above and below.

Finally, the titanic 'Sentinels'—the ruling class—sit immobile, yet intensely aware, perched atop the highest towers. Huge, statue-like, and reminiscent of humanoid sentinels draped in robes of cables and gears, these beings possess cores that pulse with deep-set intelligence. They oversee the balance of this ecosystem, emitting waves of thought that ripple through the landscape, subtly guiding the machinations below.

Each form of life, from the smallest Nano-Scribe to the commanding Sentinels, is a testament to symbiosis between creation and creator, engineering and evolution. They exist not merely to survive, but to fulfill roles set eons ago by an inscrutable design ethos. Here, life as amalgamation thrives, each motion a part of the city's eternal, rhythmic dance beneath a sky forever golden. Together, they ensure that within this realm of hums and echoes, the pulse of harmonious eternity never falters.
