While the surface world above is governed by clear laws, this underworld folds into its own rules—where density and openness coexist, and life continues to thrive in the absence of traditional vegetative forms. Here, evolution is swift, and its heartbeat is turbulent.

They might have viewed the uninhibited transformation as either divine chaos or an inventive playground.
