Towering over a sprawling expanse of silvery mist, the highland of Eldergreen is a unique balance of natural beauty, rugged terrain, and subtle technologic traces. The air is laden with moisture, which seems to rise in gentle wisps from the land itself, condensing on the cool stone surfaces of weathered boulders and the broad leaves of thick vegetation. Streams carve silent paths through the undergrowth, their origins obscured by lush clusters of ferns and moss, contributing to an ever-present, delicate murmur that underscores the atmosphere.

The terrain is varied but predominantly characterized by rolling highlands that rise and fall in gentle curves, reminiscent of ancient giants sprawling across the land. Both the terrain shape and the seemingly boundless scale of the landscape suggest eons of settled erosion, where wind and water have honed the earth into a series of deep, whispering hollows and steep, resilient peaks.

A dense thriving world unfolds in the canopy above, where interwoven branches create a nearly impenetrable shroud of leaves. Here, the temperature remains perpetually cool, a chill that wraps around the trunks of primeval trees whose roots entwine in a complex dance with the rocky earth.
