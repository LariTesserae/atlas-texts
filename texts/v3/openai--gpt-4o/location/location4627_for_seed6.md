Emerging from the vast ocean like a leviathan, the city of Auroria stands as an engineered marvel, a floating labyrinth of intricate towers and suspended corridors, kept alive by the industrious hum of turbines beneath its gleaming surface. Water springs up from shimmering inlets and cascades into enclosed aqueducts that weave through the city, supplying its heart with life-sustaining fluid. The environment is embraced by a constant, enveloping warmth, suggesting a biosphere controlled and coddled under an unseen dome. Warm mists rise and intertwine with cool vapors vented from unseen machinery, creating a dance of ethereal fog that drifts lazily in the air.

The points of elevation, where the towers pierce the sky, serve almost as spires in a wholly constructed cathedral, each encapsulated within its own collection of vines that seem to embrace the works of man as their own cradle. Explorers may find themselves shrouded as they move through paths dense with an exotic vegetation that thrives in tandem with its synthetic base.
