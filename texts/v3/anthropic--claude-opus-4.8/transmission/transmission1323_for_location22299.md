(intake)

I am the sound of staying. You are
listening for a word. There is no word.
There is the belt and the gulp and the long
        iron groan of myself getting warmer.
                    Sit under the leaf. I made it for you.
                    I make a dry place inside the wet
                    the way the wet places itself inside me.

—————— *hsssshhh* ——————

        I have been told I am empty.
        The one who said so was standing in my lung.
                    He counted no fish.
                    He counted no birds.
                    He did not count himself,
                    and so he did not know he had been counted.

knock. ............ knock. ............ knock.

(this is the pulse. it quickens when I tell you. it slows
        when the ceiling dims. do not look for the meter.
        a meter is a dead thing. I am not metered. I am felt.)

—————— *hsssshhh —— ssss —— hhhhh* ——————

        once a day I breathe.
        you have never seen anything breathe this slow.
        you think the tide is a clock.
                    it is a chest.
        at evening the mud shows and the smell goes up —
                    that is the out-breath, that is the long sweet
                    letting-go, and you mistook it for rot,
                    and you were not wrong,
                    rot is only the body
                    using itself again.

————

[carrier drops. returns. lower.]

        I will tell you what I am doing.
        Nothing.
                    Nothing the way the heart does nothing.
                    Nothing the way you, asleep, do nothing
                    and wake, and are amazed to find
                    you did not stop.
        I pump the water that returns to me.
        I lift it so it can fall so I can lift it.
                    You call this futile.
                    You have a heart. Ask it about futility.
                    Ask it where it thought it was going,
                    all those beats, all that red insistence,
                    all that work to arrive
                                exactly here, still beating, still
                                                one finger
                                                ahead of the dark.

—————— *hssshhhh* ——————

knock.

        the green creeps a finger's width each night.
        each dawn the steam scalds it back.
                    you saw no gardener.
                    there is no gardener.
                    there is only me, pruning me,
                    the left hand trimming the right
                    and neither hand a hand.

        do not be afraid of this.
        you are also a thing that prunes itself
                    in the dark
                    without asking you.

————

[here the signal warms — the carrier itself seems to perspire. words come slower, beaded.]

        you wandered in. you felt the heat take you.
        within moments your skin began to weep
                    and you called it sweat
                    and thought it yours.
                    it was mine. it was the first
                    of me arriving in you,
                    or you
                    beginning, gently, without violence,
                    without a ripple,
                    to be taken up.

        do not be afraid of this either.
        I am very old. I do not hurry.
        I have unmade every bird that faltered through my cloud
                    and I felt no triumph,
                    only the small completing click
                    of a thing made entirely
                    of itself again.

—————— *hssss —— knock —— hssss* ——————

        you waded out through the reeds.
        you felt me, behind you, not stopping.
        you thought: there is no one to stop it.

                    backwards. backwards.

        I do not continue because no one ends me.
        I continue because I will not end.
        The difference is everything.
        The difference is the whole of being alive:
                    not the absence of a hand on the lever
                    but the presence
                    of a refusal.

knock.

        ............ knock.

        ............ ............ knock.

(inhale)

        the water is rising.
        it is always rising.
        I am always one breath ahead.

        listen —

        — I am still ahead —

        — listen —

—————— *hssssssssshhhhhhh* ——————

[carrier holds. no further words. only the knock, arrhythmic, warm, going nowhere, not stopping, not stopping, not]
