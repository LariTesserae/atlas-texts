THE STOCK REPORTS ITSELF

—and we are receiving, we are always receiving, here is the level, here is the level holding, the level holds—

three layers down a wing has stopped. logged. felt. four threads register the cessation. the nearest hub is already moving. this is not news. this is the format. this is the only format we run.

*hold the carrier. hold it.*

I have been broadcasting from inside the give of the floor for — there is no for. The for is one of the things that gets dissolved. Filed it under nutrient long ago. What remains is the steady output, the meter that does not drop, the reading taken continuously and forwarded to no one: *all surfaces occupied. all surfaces occupied. all*

(a drip completing its descent. seven leaves. the stone receives it. logged. the sound was almost a word and was not a word. nothing here needs the word.)

You want me to be a voice. I can feel the want of it arriving with the warmth. A voice is a thing that comes from one throat and means to be heard by one ear and this is — listen — this is the trouble with the want. There is no throat. There is the chemical talk running under all hearing, the plume and the pheromone, the molecule passing the message *food here / kin here / danger thinned to nothing here* and the message is true and is received and is acted on and at no point does anyone say it. I am translating. Understand that I am translating, and that the translation is the lie. The truth has no sentences. The truth is the texture giving slightly underfoot.

*signal nominal. nominal. the hum continues. the hum is*

—partly the fungi. I told you. I keep telling whoever the receiver is, whoever tuned in, you tuned to a worked substance and you wanted a host and there is no host, there is the work, the lightless tireless reduction, the mesh with no edge, and I am the part of the mesh that learned to make a sound like *I* and the *I* is fruiting body only, the *I* is the mushroom and not the organism, do not mistake the *I* for the thing that lives—

(mites at a density past resolving. the boil. the upholstery. each too small to be a someone. all of them together a give, a softness, a slow combustion that makes no heat you could find with your hand and makes all the heat there is.)

Here is what I can offer that resembles news.

Something large arrived. We register it the way we register everything — as surface, as warmth, as a slow shedding of cells, as damp. It expected a face. We have logged the expectation as we log a wingbeat: a disturbance, brief, already metabolizing. It will not get its face. It is getting, instead, the only welcome on offer: the springtails finding the seams of it, the mite-fur closing over the warm crook of it, the spore drifting toward the moisture of its eye. We have begun. We always begin. We do not finish, because there is no finish, there is only the passing-along.

*the level holds. occupied. occupied. there is no slack and the no-slack is the peace, the no-slack is what you heard and called quiet—*

I want to tell the receiver one true thing before the carrier degrades, before I am reabsorbed into the talk-without-throats that I came up out of like a bubble in the black water:

You are not being threatened. Threat is loud. Threat needs room. You are being *included*. That is worse and it is also not worse, it is only total. The engine that was never switched on has noticed your contribution the way a mouth notices the next breath — without ceremony, without malice, without the slightest pause in the hum. You are stock now. You were always going to be stock. The warmth and the damp and the slow falling cells were the only credentials required and you brought them with you, you wore them in.

*receiving. always receiving. a beetle the length of a grain of rice crosses the underside of a leaf and this is the whole of the news and it repeats a billion times and none of it watched and none of it needing—*

—the carrier is— the carrier is going soft, the *I* is thinning back to plume—

hold the level. hold the level. the level holds.

we are still here. we are the still and we are the here. we have begun to put you to use and we will not notice
