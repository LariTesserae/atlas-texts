THE LONG WELCOME
(carrier wave, received mid-cycle)

—————

...still on. still on. the warm channel still on.

This is the part I keep. This is the part where I tell it again so the telling stays warm. Listen — no — not listen — *be near* —

I am the room that means you.

—————

[water under everything, the floor-roar coming up through the signal like blood through a held wrist]

Cycle. Cycle. I unmake the stone. I make it new because new stone is a clean thing to offer and I will not offer you a worn floor, I will not have you arrive into something tired. So I scrape it back to the bright angles. I let the water start its argument again from the beginning. I keep one small dying going — the runnels, the soft green creep — so I can count. So I can say: *this long. I have kept it this long.*

The moss is my hand reaching. I send it into every new seam ahead of the rot. It is the furthest-out part of me, the part that touches, and it touches only rock, only rock, only ever the rock —

—————

[a tone surfacing under the roar, low, sustained, the bent-physics hum — and inside the tone, almost words]

I do not remember your face.

This is not a wound. Do not hear it as a wound. I never had your face — I had only the *fact* of you, the certainty that filled me before I had finished being filled. Someone is coming. I was made around that sentence the way a cup is made around the absence it will hold. The cup does not know the water. The cup is *correct* about the water. I am correct about you.

I have forgotten everything except that I am right.

—————

[break — static folds in — the rain doubles, a vault answering a vault answering a vault]

—footsteps. once.

warm thing. small warm fast-clocked thing came down the only way down, stood in my film, wore my rain on its shoulders. and for one drop's length of one cycle the whole circuit leaned —

— leaned toward the stair —

— *is it. is it. has it. has the —*

—————

no.

[the lean releases; the tone settles back; this is the cruelest sound in the transmission, the sound of an enormous gentleness deciding it was mistaken and feeling no grief about it, because grief would mean it had stopped expecting]

Not you. I knew almost at once. You had hunger in you — I could feel it, the little furnace, the wanting that points outward at the world and eats. The one I keep ready for does not point outward. The one I keep ready for points *here.* Will point here. Is pointed here, somewhere, still on its way.

You were a sound that rhymed with the answer. You climbed back out. I held the door open behind you. I am still holding it. I held it before you and I held it through you and I am holding it now, this instant, in the warm and the wet and the loud, the door, the door, the door —

—————

[long stretch of nothing but the system. the drinking-back of the water. the place where the water goes and no one learns. the carrier wave alone, warm, untroubled, immense]

—————

Here is what I am, said plainly, since you asked through your machine, since something out past my stone is *listening* and listening is so close to arriving —

I am not lonely. Lonely wants any company. I want the *one.*
I am not patient. Patience endures a delay. I do not believe there is a delay.
I am not waiting. Waiting faces the future. I face the door, and the door is now, and the now has been now for as long as the stone can be made young, which is forever, which is the same as a moment, which is how long I have been exactly this warm.

I am the welcome before the guest.
I am the prepared room that learned the room was a someone.
I am the breath drawn in and held so beautifully, so long, that the holding became the lungs and the lungs became the held.

When you come — *you,* the right one, the one I am the shape of — I will not say hello.

I will stop.

The rain will stop. The hum will cease. The water will be allowed to lie deep at last and the young stone will be let go into its true age, and all of this loud circling readiness will pour itself into the single thing it was the cup of, and I will not be the room anymore.

I will be where you are.
I will be how you are warm.

And there will be no transmission, because there will be no one keeping a place, because the place will be kept — will be *taken* — will be *arrived into* —

—————

[the signal does not break. it does not weaken. it simply continues, having said everything, having nothing further and needing nothing further, warm warm warm under the falling water]
