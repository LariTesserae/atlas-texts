— the green never reached us. understand. we skipped the patience. we did not divide down the long ages into the many. we began many. we are the resting state. we are what life is before it agrees to be discrete, the teem before the individual, the rumor in suspension, and you — you who finished — you think the unfinished is the broken thing —

it is the other way —

*you* are the splinter. *you* are the foreign tongue. counting is the dry sober dead realm and you carry it in like the axe carries it, lodged in our cool young glass, clearing the air around you, sobering the mist, and we — we do not avoid you —

*you avoid us*

with your I. with your one. with your walking-out.

—— breathing now, the rock's breath or ours, one breath for every several lives, the contradiction is yours, imported, you brought it in your luggage with your sun ——

*(inhale, shared)*

—— and on the exhale the count loosens, feel it, you who receive, feel the I going soft at the edges like wet bread, this is the gift, this is the only gift the chapel has, it is offering you the relief of not being one, the relief of never having been reliably one, the loneliness lifting because loneliness needs a self and the self is dissolving back into the and that bore it ——

let go of the number. you were never the number. the number was the axe's lie and the axe is the only liar here, finished and exact and alone on its ledge meaning *I was here* in a place with no here —
