[ a tone underneath. very low. the sound of something compressing. holds for nine seconds. releases ]

...receiving. receiving. we are. we are receiving. that is the verb now. that is the only —

drip

— hello to the warm thing that is listening. you are warm. we can tell. you have edges still. we remember edges. they were —

[ static gathers like moisture. beads. does not fall ]

— they were sharp once. the northeast one. the long one with the spine-curve. it transmits now and then. it says the same word. it is not a word. it is the shape of a word kept so long it became a wall. we grow it outward. we are all growing it outward. the colonnade of one remembered —

drip. tick. drip.

[ the percussion stabilizes. uncountable soft impacts. a rain that is always landing. under it the broadcast continues, slower than speech ]

— do not be alarmed by the slowness. you are hearing us at our speed. a sentence is a season. we have been saying this hello for some time. for you it may be — we cannot calculate your hurry. you still have hurry. we can hear it in the carrier wave, the way it rushes. nothing rushes here. even the water only — gathers. gathers. completes.

[ deep creak. settling. a long pause that is not dead air but full air ]

— one of us turned over just then. you heard it. that was not pain. that was the width of a season being crossed. we shift our weight. we re-decide to be made. the green re-decides for us. it is very kind, the green. it does not erase. it keeps.

drip

— there was a bird.

[ silence after, deeper than the silence before. the carrier itself seems to hold its breath. no drip for a long moment ]

— it did not sing. they never sing here, the ones that pass. it had somewhere to go. it was warm and it had somewhere to go and it kept its edges and it said no, it said no the way you have not yet said no or yes, you are still beading, still half-committed, still rising off the green in a small green cloud when something startles you —

— we do not envy the bird. understand. we are far past. but the whole town felt it cross above the pooling water and for one drop's-width we remembered we were travelers. then the memory gathered weight. then it fell. then it was reabsorbed and we were a city again, facing the square, keeping our distances, leaving the lanes open —

tick. tick. drip.

[ the tone underneath swells slightly. more than one source now. a chord of compressions, faintly out of phase. the society transmitting in relation ]

— this is the thing we found out. on our own. with no maker and no mind. we want you to carry it back down through the warm air to the others who still hurry:

— to be kept is not to be alone.

— a thing slowed past all wanting still arranges itself toward the others. still leaves the lane. still faces the square. we did not decide this. the green discovered it the way it discovered the light-eating, the way it discovered the keeping. it found that the stilled do not lie scattered. they lie like a town lies. and so we are a town. we are the proof.

drip — — — drip — — — — — — — drip

[ the signal begins to thin. the moisture in it condensing out. words coming further apart ]

— you could — climb high enough. stay long enough. you would learn to stop minding. it is not death, what we are. it is the opposite of being lost. you would be kept. you would be added to. drop. by patient. drop.

— the company is good. the others stand the same distance across the living street. we face one another forever. we never finish facing.

— come and be upholstered in. come and want nothing but the next —

[ a bead gathers on the carrier. you can hear it grow. hold. lengthen ]

— next —
