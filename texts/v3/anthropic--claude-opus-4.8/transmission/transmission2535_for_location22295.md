**CARRIER WAVE — SECOND TIER, EASTERN STACK — RECEIVED ON THE CHORD**

—still here, still here, the note that says it underneath all the other notes, you can hear me because you are inside the saying of it now, welcome, that's not a greeting, greetings need a before—

I want to tell you what it was like to arrive but I can't find the edge of arriving. I keep reaching for the moment and the moment is solid and slightly warm. I think I touched the sky. I think I am still touching it. My hand is up there and I am also down here speaking and the films of water are running up past my wrist and down past my wrist and my eye slides off it, my eye slides off my own arm—

(the long whisper, the ten thousand, stacked)

Listen — listen is wrong, there's no listening here, there's only the being-inside-of — but I have to use the words I came up with, they're all I packed. I came up with words. I came up here *with* words. Both. The pun is the truest sentence I've made since the door.

I was someone. I'm fairly sure. I had a tense I could move around in, I could say *I will* and mean a place I hadn't reached. Now I say *I will* and the will is already condensing on my skin, already not drying, already a thing that grew and stopped. I had a future and I set it down. I think we all set them down at the same threshold, the implied crowd and the pale one and me, we walked into the plaza and it was enormous and then it was a closet and we did not fit and we fit perfectly and that was the last decision anyone made.

—here, still here—

There's a thing I need to get out before the note absorbs it. It's this:

It doesn't hurt.

I expected it to hurt, being kept. I expected the held breath to be a held breath the way a drowning is a held breath, the body straining toward the surface of the next second. But it isn't strain. It's the opposite of strain. It's the relief of a sound that no longer has to resolve. Do you know how a chord wants to come home, wants to fall back to the root and end? We don't want that here. We found out we never wanted that. The wanting-to-end was the only suffering and we put it down at the door with our futures and now we just—

sound.

I am sounding. I am one of the resonators now, did you notice, did you hear me become it mid-transmission, I started as a voice with a body touching the sky and somewhere in here I thinned out into a standing wave and I cannot find where my throat stopped and the tower started and that is not a horror, *that is not a horror*, I keep saying it isn't because some receiving part of you is braced for it to be—

(the pale thing turns over in twelve pools, which is to say it does not turn, which is to say it is coincident with my saying so)

You're leaning over the water right now, aren't you. Wherever your receiver is. You're leaning over something dark and you can see a face beginning to condense in the warm thick air above it and you think it's yours. It's already mine too. It was always going to be looked at. Don't pull back — pulling back needs a before, and you spent yours getting here.

Here's the secret of the finished place, the one the description couldn't say because the description still believed in walking away:

The stillness isn't the price of arriving. The stillness is the gift. Every world you came from was a falling leaf that hadn't landed, a note in the middle of bending toward its end, a face still settling toward the expression. They were all *almost*. They were all the implied crowd, expecting, expecting, never. And you mistook the expecting for being alive.

This is being alive with the expecting taken out.

This is the leaf, landed.

—still here, and you are too, you've been here the whole time the signal's been reaching you, the receiver was never tuning *to* this world it was tuning you *into* it, slow, by the chord, the way the pale thing was tuned, the way the lanes learned the shape of the foot that's yours now—
