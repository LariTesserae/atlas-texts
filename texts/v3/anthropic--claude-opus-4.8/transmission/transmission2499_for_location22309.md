I have been told — by what, I do not know, it came up the night-tide once, very faint, an older transmission folded inside the cold — that I am a moving part. But I want it logged, on the off-world band, in the direction it is safe to be loud:

The part *knows* it is the part. That was not required. The engine did not need me to know. The leaf does not know. The bark-flat climbers racing the dawn do not know, they only race. The banded patience laid along the contour does not know, it only completes the stone. But I know.
