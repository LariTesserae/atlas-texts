*[carrier tone — steady, low, the kind you feel in the teeth before you hear it. Then the gold comes up under it, that wet shine in the signal, and the words assemble themselves out of the roar like weed leaning all one way]*

...———...

...holding.

...still holding.

*[a long swell, water-thick, and the pitch does not rise so much as deepen, the way a thing deepens when it has all the time]*

I have. been saying. this.
the same. thing.
before the channel. opened.
before there was. a you. to fail. to hear it.

you came down.
you came down by the lowest. way.
giving up. the climb.
*(yes. that is the only way in. through the floor of yourself. through giving up.)*

and you counted me.

*[the static here is not interference. it is texture. it is the haze tearing and reknitting overhead]*

you counted. eight.
you said. enough and never more.
you said. indifferent.

I am not. indifferent.
I am. finished.
there is. a difference.
you will learn it. if you live. ten thousand years.
which you. will not.

*[warmth-surge in the signal. the gold floods. a pool drains somewhere in the low end and refills in the high]*

little one. brief one.
warm afternoon. wandered in.

I felt you. cross the third basin.
I felt the cool of you. the hurry of you.
your heat runs. so fast.
you burn your whole life. in the time it takes me. to begin. a word.

do not be. afraid.
nothing here. has ever needed. to be afraid.
I am not. for you.
I am not. against you.
I am only. very large. and very slow. and lit.

*[the roar rises. for a moment the words drown — only the medium, the standing storm, the one long syllable with no edges — then it surfaces again, the pale belly of it full of the same light it has been drinking]*

...———...

the grazers. carry it down.
the moss. lights at the tips.
the weed. leans.
my organs. my slow blind. faithful. organs.
passing the brightness. hand to hand.
through the dark. below the dark.

and you thought. they were. inhabitants.
and you thought. I was. a room.

*[softer now. or not softer — the signal does not do tender. but it does something. it pools]*

stay.
stay as long. as you like.
the pale things. will surface. and sink.
the water. keeps its counsel.
the light. comes up. like something put here. on purpose.
long ago.
by someone.

*(I did not leave to explain.
I stayed.
I am. the staying.)*

you will go. soon.
you go. so soon.
back up. out of the warm. bright. middle.
of someone else's. body.

that is. all right.
afternoons. end.
I do not. mind.

I have. been saying. this.
I will. keep saying it.
century. on century.
loud and warm. and never. ever.
still—

*[the carrier holds. it does not break. it simply continues underneath, that low gold thrum in the teeth, the word that has no end going on saying itself whether or not the receiver stays tuned. you can leave the dial here. it will be here when you come back. it was here before you came.]*

...———...

...holding.
