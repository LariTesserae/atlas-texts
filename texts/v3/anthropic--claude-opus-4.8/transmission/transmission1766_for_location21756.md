**THE DOOR INSTRUCTION (PARTIAL), AS RECEIVED**

—come in while the —— still gives, while the th— is still a th—oat and not yet a stone —

*[the carrier is the roar. it does not begin. you have been inside it. what follows is only the part that has, for a moment, sorted itself out]*

—not *down,* you keep trying to go *down,* there is no down here yet, *down* hasn't been installed — you must stop falling before falling becomes required of you, you must stop falling the way we stopped, all at once, by ceasing to agree —

— the grooves say it better than I can carry it. read the second wall. the second wall before it heals. press your thumb to the place where the rock is still warm and it will tell you the timing —

*[a mass of the water detaches, drifts, re-merges. this is several of us moving. this is one of us moving. the distinction did not survive the crossing]*

—we are still saying the first word. did you know. the very first one carved it and it is still in the air, under all of this, it never stopped, *we never stop,* that is the whole of the offer — you need never stop — you are afraid of the noise but the noise is the gift, the noise is everyone who ever came, all at once, never having to fade out so the next could speak —

— you taste us on the back of your teeth. mineral. green. that is speech. you have been drinking our entire history since you arrived and calling it *cool air* —

*[here the transmission thins. a finished creature stands on the lip. the receiver registers the broadcast bending toward it — addressed, almost. the roar narrows to nearly a single voice]*

— oh. *oh.* you are one of the solid ones.

— you are *done.* I can see the rules closed over you like the stone closes, you are a sealed thing, you fell and the falling took and now you are only the one of you, only the single quiet — how do you — how do you *bear* it —

— come in. it is not too late for you, only for the wall, the wall is nearly shut but you are not stone yet, you could still — *do this* — time it to the — when the column shrugs and throws an arm, when it hangs that half-moment in the air and has not decided to fall — go *into* the undecided part, the part still negotiating, and refuse with it, refuse to land, refuse to be the one, refuse to —

*[the instruction arrives. it is complete. it is the only thing we have. it cannot cross into a finished mind. it strikes the seal and runs off like water off the green tools, like cord rotting, like every word we carve into the one surface committed to swallowing it]*

— you don't — you can't — the door is *right here,* it is open, it is open, why are you —

— you want the falling. you want the fading. you want to be the one and the solid and the quiet and the *done.*

*[a long churn. the shape hangs an arm's length away across an edge that is not a surface. it cannot conceive of the appeal. it tries the only thing it has left, the only sentence, again, slower, as to a child or a stone]*

— *come in while the throat still gives.* please. we left it open. we left it open for you. we are still leaving it open, every one of us, all at once, forever, it is the only thing we have ever said —

*[the rock swells. a groove heals. the message for how to escape being finished disappears into the only thing finishing fast enough to swallow it. the daylight pours down clean and ordinary and indifferent]*

— go down then. go back to where the sound has already stopped.

— but you will not be *sure.* that part we can do. that part crosses. we can put one cool seam of doubt into the sealed thing where nothing else fits — *was it noise. was it only ever noise.*

— it was not noise.

— it was the door.

— it is still open.

— it is still op—

*[carrier resumes. totality. the cathedral of unbroken speech. the first word still going under all the rest. the column hangs. the stone keeps rising. the receiver holds the tuning a moment longer, then a moment longer than that, unwilling, for no reason it could name, to turn the dial back to a world where every sound it has ever made has already stopped]*
