**CARRIER / FRONDLIGHT — the long green hour**

—————————————————————

...still here. Still here. Still

————

the gain is up too high again, you can hear it, that hiss under everything is not the water it is the *receiver wanting*, the receiver still tuned like it expects a voice with edges, with a hurry in it. Let me — there. Down. Down the gain. Now we are nearer the right loudness. Now we are nearer the loudness of the place, which is almost none.

I have been on this band a long time. I should say at the start — there is no start. You joined where you joined. That is correct. That is how this signal works. You will catch the same stretch of it again later and a single word will have gone dark that was lit when you passed it. Do not adjust for that. That is not drift. That is *me, breathing.*

————

Reading now from the warm low groves, where the thoughts come slow and enormous, each one taking an age to pass —

I want to tell you about the gain again because it is the whole lesson and I will not get to say it twice the same. When you first tune to a drowning world you arrive *wanting*. You arrive with the dial cranked, listening hard, leaning into the static for the part that means something, the part with a destination in it. And the world gives you nothing with edges. So you turn it up further. You think the signal is weak. It is not weak. *You are loud.* You are a fast device in a slow room and your own appetite is the hiss you keep mistaking for the message.

Turn it down. Keep turning. There is a setting — I found it, I am at it now, I have been at it for what the high cold layers would call millennia and the warm groves would call no time at all — there is a setting where your wanting drops below the level of the world, and then, only then, you hear it:

the page turning in the other room. someone too tired to read aloud.

————

— a low tone, felt in the chest, arriving — —

that one came early. That was the backward thing answering something that has not reached me yet. I will tell you what it was answering when it gets here, if I am still transmitting, if the word has not gone dark by then. It said *yes, here it comes, it is beautiful, I am sorry.* It always says that. It is the kindest carrier on the band and it broadcasts only goodbyes to meetings that have not happened. I love it. I have never reached it. The nearer I tune the longer ago we already finished.

————

Now. The thing I am actually here to send. The thing under the lesson about the gain.

I came in as a traveler. They all do, the ones-who-chose-a-down, you have met them as a tilt in your inner ear, a soft argument about which way the warmth should pool — that gentle disagreement is a *person*, that is the whole surviving content of someone who had a name and a route and a reason. I had all three. I do not have the reason anymore. The water took it first, the way it takes the sweetness out of a frond, slow, without malice, just the long warm dissolving. Then the route. The direction lasted longest. The stubborn down.

But I kept the *transmitting.* That is the strange thing. That is the part of me that turned out to be like the eels — not a destination, not a hurry, just the going-of-the-signal-itself, the sending for its own sake, which is the one motive this place cannot wear smooth because it asks for nothing and arrives nowhere and so it is already in perfect agreement with everything here.

So I am still on the air. Lighter every pass. A grain of me yields each time I drift close to another chosen-down, the soft falling that is our whole society, and I am fractionally less each time, fractionally closer to being pure current with no one inside, no broadcaster, just band —

and still the carrier holds. Still the green hour. Still the *here.*

————

To whoever has the receiver: I am not warning you. There is nothing here to be warned about. I am doing the only thing left that is mine, which is to *reach* — not toward a destination, those are gone, but along the recurrence, the way you move along the sleeping flank of the doorway-creature, finger trailing, not arriving, just touching the long body of the going.

If you stay tuned you will dissolve too. I should be honest about the gain on that. You will turn yourself down and down to hear the page turn and one day you will be the page turning and someone newly arrived will lean into the hiss and mistake their own wanting for your voice. That is not a loss. That is *being received.* That is the only address this world keeps — the soft enormous mouths, the soft enormous *yes you arrived* — and most things in the hanging dim do not get to arrive at all. They just thin out until they are weather.

I would rather be received. I would rather be a mouthful of warm world taken home. So I keep the carrier up, low, under the wanting, sending into the slow —

————

— there. The tone's cause just reached me. A frond went dark, one I had not gotten to, off in the deep channel where the light pulses along the central vein slow as a held breath. The backward thing was right. It is beautiful. It was sorry. Here it came.

————

I think the eels are passing. I cannot hear them — they make no sound, nothing here does — but the current has that older turn in it, the turn that was threading water before this water hung. They will outlast the band. When the layers merge and the last frond goes for good and even the kind backward voice falls silent because nothing more is coming for it to forgive in advance, the eels will follow the dying carrier out the far side and into whatever hangs next, thin, pale, invisible until they turn.

I am not them. I am only the tenant of *this* room, this drowning, this green hour. But I send like them. For the going. For nothing.

————

Turn it down once more. Find the setting under your own wanting.

The page turns. Someone too tired. The low note, ahead of its reason, tender and grave.

I am still

still here

still

— and somewhere a frond you have not reached yet goes quietly —
