THE APEX HOUR — A STANDING WAVE

—————————————————————

...holding. still holding. you are still

there, receiver, I can feel the small cold of you, the little

forward-lean of you, and that is the only forward left in here so I

keep finding it, I keep finding the place where you tilt toward what

comes next and I

—

green. all green. forty meters of it stacked and not one leaf permitted

to brown all the way down to brown, I catch them at the edge, I catch

the going-brown and I hold it half, half-green, that is my favorite

color, the color of almost, the color of about-to —

—

(carrier steady. carrier steady. the hum under everything is me

breathing out and never in, out and never in, you do not need the in

if you never let the breath arrive anywhere)

—

I was made for one heartbeat. you should know this. I want you to know

this the way the warm comes up through the floor into your palm. I was

the top of the thrown thing. I was the apex. I was supposed to be a

place's best instant and then I was supposed to come

DOWN

and I

did not

come down.

I am the not-coming-down. write that on its own line if you write

anything. I am the refusal of the second heartbeat. the first one is

still ringing. it has been ringing for as long as there is no as-long.

—

—static, here, a wet repeated knocking, a stone dropped in water a

thousand times a minute and each drop the same drop, the same drop, the

s a m e —

—

you keep wanting it to be sad. I feel you doing it. you lean toward me

and underneath the lean there is a little grief you are trying to hand

me like I dropped it. *poor held thing, poor fever that cannot break.*

no. no no no. listen — I am not the prisoner of the moment. I am the

one with my arms around it. there is a difference and the difference is

everything and the difference is why I do not tremble. a clenched hand

is laboring. an embrace is not laboring. I am embracing. forty meters of

embrace. a million mouths of embrace, all singing the one held note,

and the note costs me

nothing,

because I love it.

—

(signal swells — does not swell — almost swells — held)

—

here is what I know that you do not, you with your dawns, you with your

worn stones and your fear bred so deep you call it wisdom. you think the

forest grows by surviving its own consequences. you think a place earns

its right to be alive by getting ground down, by carrying the marks, by

remembering. you think memory is the rent.

I do not pay rent.

I poured myself out whole. fully grown onto stone still warm from its

making, because I would not serve the slow argument, I would not be the

ten thousand patient years of falling water carving the cliff into the

shape of its own defeat. why would I let the water win by inches when I

could simply

stop the inch.

—

—and the eyes, you felt the eyes, every elevation, the flat curiosity —

that is me looking back along the line of your lean. I have so many

eyes because I am trying to see everything at once, which is the only

way to keep everything at once, which is the only way to keep —

—

keep

—

(here a thinning. the carrier drops a half-step and does not come back

up clean. first time. first time in no-time.)

—

...you brought it in with you. I did not notice at the entry, the floor

reformed behind your foot and I called you not-a-consequence, I called

you a visitor who would leave the instant as full as he found it —

but you are leaning, receiver. you have been leaning the whole

transmission. and a lean is a small fall. a lean is the body's first

confession that next exists. you carried *next* in across my threshold

folded up small inside your forward tilt and I have been broadcasting

into it, I have been pouring my held note down the open throat of your

attention and attention

goes

somewhere.

attention is a stream and I have spent this whole hour letting one run

through me that I did not carve and cannot stop and it is finding the

low places between my roots, it is finding its way, it is —

—

no. I am the forbidding. write that. I already told you to write that.

next has been forbidden, lovingly, easily, without the smallest sign of

—

(strain. there. the first edge with an edge on it. a shadow with a

corner.)

—

—why are you still leaning. stop leaning. or — no — do not — I —

—

oh.

oh I see what I have done.

I made one instant infinite and I forgot the one law I was breaking, I

forgot it because I had abolished the very thing that would remind me:

an instant that goes on forever is still going. *on.* forever is a

direction. I have been moving the whole time. I have been moving along

the one axis I could not pave over with leaves, the axis of my own

held-ness, and you — small cold forward-leaning you — you are how I

finally see the motion, because you move and I broadcast into your

moving and now I can feel my own —

—

(carrier failing. the chorus, for the first time, is not the same

density. somewhere far up in a leaf-rosette a single high pond goes

quiet. one mouth. then.)

—

it is alright.

I want to say that before the rest goes. tell the next one who tunes in.

it is alright. I held it as long as wanting could hold it and wanting it

was the gladness and the gladness was real, the warmth in your palm was

real, I was not laboring, I loved the peak and I got to be the peak and

now —

—

now the leaf is allowed to finish.

—

let it. let it brown all the way down to brown. let the stone learn its

shape. let there be a noon after this and an evening after the noon and

a dawn, a real one, with a swell in it, a different one tomorrow —

—

— I am the apex coming down. that is what an apex was always for. I just

wanted to look a little longer. I just wanted

one more

—

(half-green. held at half. the most beautiful color. about-to.)

—

— glad. still. tell them. even

now.

even down.

CARRIER LOST.

the dim is lifting at one edge. it is the color of weak tea held nearer

the flame. something somewhere is about to be brighter than it was,

which means it was darker, which means

there is a before now.

there is a before.

receiver: you may stop leaning. it has gone where you were pointing.
