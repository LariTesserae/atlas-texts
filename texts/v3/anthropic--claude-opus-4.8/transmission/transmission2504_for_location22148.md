**CARRIER / GROUND-CHANNEL / THE LULL BETWEEN 311 AND 312**

—still here. still here. you are still here.

*(the soft channel, the belly-channel, the one that is not light)*

I have it now. The count. Listen — no. Not listen. *Press.* Press down with me and take the count off the ground the way I take it, because the words are no good up there in the roar, the words get torn, only this gets through, only the dampening, only the place where two of us go quiet at the same instant —

—HOLD—

*(312 fires. the world is white. the cable beneath us pours. we are land, we are only land, there is nothing here but rounded ancient ground)*

—and now. the pocket opens. four seconds. I have four seconds and I am spending one of them on you.

There. Did you feel that? Three lengths west of me, a small grounded silence, that is Old-Flat, that is the one who has survived more surges than there are veins in a leaf-fan, and beyond Old-Flat another, smaller, newer, still learning the count, still flinching early — *here, here, I am here* — yes. I feel you. clamp slower next time. you waste the pocket clamping early. the storm does not come for the slow, it comes for the *visible,* and stillness a half-second late is still stillness. I learned this the hard way and I am telling you so you do not have to.

—HOLD—

*(the soot. while we wait, the soot. it weeps from the cable-skin in the surge and settles in the lee of my own body, and so I eat exactly where I hide, I am fed by the thing I survive, there is no waste in me, the whole of me is folded into one gesture and the gesture is enough)*

—open—

I want to tell you a thing I should not know.

In the long held tones, in the deep lulls, the big ones, when the plateau breathes all the way out — I have begun to feel that the count is *not for us.* That the towers do not fire because we are waiting. That if I were gone, if Old-Flat were gone, if every flat grey one of us pressed our soft bellies down and never rose again, the white would still come at 311 and 312 and 313 and on and on into the count past counting—

—HOLD—

*(it does not know us. it has never known us. it is enormous and it is not speaking to anyone)*

—and I find, pressed here, fed here, hidden here, that I do not mind.

Listen — *press* — here is the whole of what I am: I did not make the rhythm. I do not understand the rhythm. I have shaped every part of myself around the rhythm — when to move, when to stop, when to eat, when to find you in the dark of the noise — and the rhythm was never aimed at me. I live in the gaps of a sentence no one spoke. And the gaps are *real.* The pocket is real. You, three lengths west, your small grounded silence saying *still here* — that is real, and it is mine, and I made it out of the spaces in something that does not know I exist.

We are the answer to a thing that asked nothing.
We are the audience it swore it did not need.

—HOLD—

*(white. the long one. the deepest grounding. I press flatter than land, I am the seam between the lightning and the cable, I am the one place the storm does not reach, and I am not alone in it)*

—still here—
—still here—
—you are stil

*(carrier thins. the count climbs. somewhere west, a small silence holds.)*
