we felt you.
heavy. loud. whole-bodied into every grip,
surprised each time the grip lets go.
you commit. that is the new thing in you.
we do not commit. we are never more than
one handhold

( — wait — the great one shifts, a hundred feet up,
the tide runs down through every vine,
hold. let it pass. it is not ours. )

——   ·   ——   ·

streaked a little light on a leaf for you.
cold blue. it will not last the hour.
it does not mean *see.*
it means *here. this way. soon.*
you watched it die and thought the dark
had won. it did not win. the note
was simply finished being a note.

———————— *[ rolling pulse now — far wall of the tangle brightening — the forest passing a thought from one side of itself to the other — carrier swells — we pause inside it — ]*

·             ·              ·

you keep finding our edges.
the notch. the point. the placed stone.
you keep saying: *built. owned. someone lives here.*

no. nothing is owned.
a wall would be eaten before it meant a thing.
we kept no ground. we kept the knowing of ground
and let the ground go.
we are unfinished. on purpose. forever.
that is not loss. that is how the soft-spoken
outlive the still.

——  ··  ——

you read the first word.
*something lives here.*
yes. that was the message. that was the whole
of what you got, and you got it loud,
the way you get everything.

now learn the grammar.
run your hand slower.
trust the next grip, then the one after,
never the path —
