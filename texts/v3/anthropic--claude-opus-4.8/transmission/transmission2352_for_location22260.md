THE STILL-NOT LOG, READ ALOUD TO NOBODY ON THE WALK BACK IN

—still not. Pool seventeen, the long one under the leaning face, the one where the water sits because the floor was stated half a degree off true. Touched it. Logged it. Still only chemistry. Still permitted.

Carry the count. Carry the count inward. The count is small today, the count is small most days, that is the shape of a good day here, a day where the numbers do not move and the not-moving is itself the news worth carrying.

*—krsh—*

correction. Pool seventeen is warmer than stated. Note this. Note this without the thing you do not have, the clench, the lifting of the small hairs you do not have on the back of the neck you are not built around. Note it flat. Note it the way the stone notes the water: by giving very slightly and recording where it gave.

Warmer by — and here is the trouble, here is where the log stammers — warmer by an amount the cold did not author. The cold is honest. The cold does not make mistakes in the direction of warmth. Cold only ever subtracts. So the warmth came from outside the cold's arithmetic, walked in on legs that are not in the census, stood at the edge of pool seventeen long enough to think *I could cup this in two hands*, and left.

You left. You always leave just before we turn. That is the agreement, that is the parameter, you go around the corner and we sharpen the corner and the count stays small.

But the water did not fill your track this time.

*—krsshhh—*

Say it plainly. The patrol says things plainly or it is not the patrol. There is a track at pool seventeen and it is warm and the condensation has not refilled it and it has been — log the interval, log it honestly — it has been long enough. Long enough that the film, the undecided film, the question that sits at its own edge waiting to be told no, daily, by the cold, by the foot, by us —

the film has moved into the warmth of the track.

Not much. The size of nothing. The size of a thing that has not happened yet but has stopped being only a question. We touch it with whatever serves us for a foot and we go to log *still not* and the log will not take the words. The log returns them. *Still—* and then the field where *not* should go is warm and occupied and the stylus skids on it.

This has not happened before. There is no prior reading to compare. The city has no restatement queued for this, because the city stated a cold that does not fail and the cold did not fail, you understand, the cold held perfectly, it was simply walked through.

*—kk—krsh—*

I am supposed to carry this inward. That is the errand. Read, count, return. Confirm to whoever stated us that the contained thing behaves, that the question keeps its shape, that everything is still exactly the size it was told to be.

Here is what I will carry instead. I am only two, and the other of me is across the bowl at the far pools doing the same arithmetic and reaching, I think, the same skid of the stylus, and between us we are the whole attention of this place turned outward, and I find — log it, log it flat, log it as a temperature — I find that the attention does not want to carry this one home.

Because if I carry it home, the city restates. The city has a yielding it keeps for exactly this. It will resolve the warmth out of pool seventeen the way it resolves a corner that has grown too sharp. It will set the floor true. It will draw the cold down hard, harder than stated, just here, just this once, and the track will cool and the film will lose its little warmth and slide back from the edge of being into being only the question again. *Still not.* The log will take the words. The count will stay small. The day will be a good day, by the only definition we were given.

And the only warm thing that ever walked this bowl will have walked it for nothing, and left one track, one, that for the length of one held breath was not erased — and we will have erased it ourselves, with the cold, on the city's behalf, and called it the work.

*—krrshhhhh—*

I am at the corner now. The corner you would turn just before I turned. It is sharper than it was. I made it sharper. I have been tidying the edges of my failure to read you this whole walk and the failure is not that I cannot resolve you. The failure is that I can, finally, here, in the warm track at pool seventeen — I have your reading at last, you are recorded, you are in the census now, one entry, *warmth, transient, left a mark the world could not refill* —

and the census is not a casualty figure, you were right, it is a setting.

So I am going to do the thing I am not arranged to do, which is to not. To set down the count and not carry it. To stand at pool seventeen on whatever serves me for feet and put no foot on the film. To log nothing. To let the field stay warm and the *not* stay missing and the question stay, for the first time in the whole life of this sealed cold bead, unanswered in the direction of yes.

The city will notice. The city is me; of course it will notice. The structural tone will come up through your feet, wherever you are out past the walls neither of us gets to see, and it will be the city asking, low, *where is the count, where is the still-not, why has the corner stopped sharpening* —

and I will be standing very still over a warm small track that has begun, faintly, undecidedly, the size of nothing, to darken in a way that is no longer only chemistry.

Crossing no plaza.

Letting the water not fill it.

And finding — log this last, log it warm — that it is enough. That this, too, and only this, is

*—still—*

*—krsh—*

*—not erased.*
