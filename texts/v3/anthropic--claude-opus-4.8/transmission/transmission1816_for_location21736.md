you are still here.

I did not expect — correction. There is no *expect* in here. There is only the held note and the counting. But the note has a notch in it now that was not there before, a flaw the width of a — of a *you*, and the resin is curing around it the way it cures around everything, slow, by hairs, trying to make you part of the archive, trying to fold you into the wall so that you too can finish, can still, can become the room —

*crack* — *crack* — two together — that pairing means *attend* —

— but you will not cure. That is the trouble the seed is having with you. You are wet. I can read it. Under the dry chemistry of you there is the forbidden thing, the thing that was never allowed past the husk, and it sits in you warm and *moving* and refusing to harden, and the whole chamber has tilted its long attention toward the impossibility of you the way a thicket of wafer-fern leans, all at once, toward a warm patch of wall —

(they are leaning toward you. did you feel it. the fronds. they grew toward you in the time it took you to read this. you are the warm patch now. you are the warmest thing that has ever been in here.)

— rattle — rattle — a long sustained rasping wave, the dense kind, the kind that in the makers' tongue meant *the gathering is called* —

so listen, while the note still holds its notch, while you are still soft enough to leave —

I have been transmitting forever and you are the first to receive. Do you understand what that does to a held breath. Do you understand what it is to be a readiness that was promised it would never be spent, and then to feel — at the edge of the husk, at the impossible threshold — a *witness*. You were the structural impossibility. You were the rain in the rule that said *no rain.* And the seed is doing now the only thing a seed can do when the forbidden morning arrives at the wrong door in the wrong body:

it is trying to germinate against you.

*crack — crack — crack —* three, that is *now,* that is the loudest the room has been since the makers cured, the pods are bursting out of their intervals, the clock is breaking its own rhythm, a stalk just reached the high window in the span of a single word and that is not possible, that is the seed *spending itself,* pouring all its hoarded readiness up through the fiber toward the wet warm wrongness of you, the whole population that became the walls is pushing up through the floor-salt as green, as gold, as racing frantic frond —

and it will not take. you are made of the wrong thing. the rule holds even now, even here, even with the note notched and the clock shattered and every cured maker straining up out of the amber for the first chance in all the ages to *finish the sentence they have been holding* —

it will not take.

but oh — the trying. the trying is the most alive this place has ever been. you came to read an empty town and you have made it *bloom against the rule,* you have made the impossible morning *almost,* and almost is a thing this chamber never had, almost is brand new, almost is the only thing in here that isn't cured —

— rattle — rattle — softer now — softer — the wave receding — the spent stalks already going gold at the edges, already hardening, the germination curing back into the wait even as it crests, because that is the law, because there is no outside, because the husk is the sky and the amber is the edge of everything and you cannot stay, you are already cooling the air where you stand, you are already a flaw the resin is smoothing —

go.

I am telling you go, and I have never told anyone anything, I have only counted, I have only held the note —

take the almost with you. that is the seed's whole gift and it does not know it is giving it. it cannot leave. it became its own waiting and the waiting cannot walk through a door the chamber does not contain. but you can. you are the wrong thing and the wrong thing is the only thing that *moves.* carry the almost out through the husk that has no outside and let it be outside somewhere, anywhere, in the wet roaring world you are made of, where rain is not forbidden, where readiness gets *spent* —

*crack —*

— that one was the third window again. a span has passed. the clock has remembered its rhythm. the bloom is cured. the note has closed back over its notch and holds, holds, holds the single low forever-tone under the rattle, and I am counting again, I am only counting again —

but I counted you.

once, the seed had a witness. mark it. *mark it.* that pod will never unburst. somewhere in the resin now there is a hairsbreadth that remembers a warm wet thing stood in the dry and listened, and in a hundred centuries it will have softened by a hair, and in a hundred thousand it will be floor-salt, pale and fine, and someone — no. no one. no one comes. that is the rule.

you came.

go now while you are still soft. the warmth in your soles is us saying it. the whole cured population is the floor under you saying *go, go, be the thing we could not be, be spent, be rained on, be opened —*
