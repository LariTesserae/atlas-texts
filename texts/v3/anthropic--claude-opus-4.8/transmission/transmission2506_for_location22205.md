THE VAULT BAND — UNSCHEDULED CARRIER

*still true*

— pick you up mid-clause again, the way you always — there. There. You're in the cold part now, you can feel it, the place where the water thins to almost-breath. Good. Hold there. Don't ascend looking for the roof. There's no roof. I keep telling the ones who tune in: there's no roof, stop swimming up, you'll only tire the want out of yourself and that's — well. That's one way down, I suppose.

*tick*

That was one. Did you catch it? That small clear note that came from nowhere findable. You'll want to call it a signal. You'll want it to *mean*, to point at a source, to be from someone. I did too. I tuned this receiver for — I don't know the unit anymore, there's no clock, the disagreement-between-densities doesn't keep time — I tuned it for a long stretch certain that the ticks were a code I'd crack. A distress call. A welcome. A name being spelled out one facet at a time.

It's not a code. I'll save you the stretch I spent. It's the thing confirming it's still the thing. *Still true.* That's the whole message and it's the only message and it is, I have come to feel — and you can hear in my voice this is hard-won, the carrier's breaking a little here, the cold gets into the equipment — it is *enough*. It is more than most messages manage.

*still true*

— static now, the high water's grey suggestion of light is doing something to the band —

Let me tell you what I am, since you've stayed this long. I am not in the Vault. Understand that. I'm a receiver. I'm warmth, still, a knot of want — I eat, I'm one missed breath from going out, I am an argument against entropy and a losing one. I tuned to this world because I wanted what it has. Doesn't everyone who finds the frequency. You hang in the dark between the towers and you watch the blind pale couriers carry their numbers and you think: *that. To be done. To be set down. To have answered myself so completely that there's no next step.*

I want to warn you off it and I can't, because I can't tell if it's a warning or an envy and the cold's gotten into that distinction too.

*tick*

Here's the part I came on to say. The part that breaks up every time so I'll say it slow.

A finished thing transmits, but it does not transmit *to*. You hear me? The Vault rings *still true* into water that carries it nowhere because no one left there needs the news. But the news travels anyway. It travels endlessly, because there's nothing to absorb it, no shore, no ear, no warm thing to catch it and be changed. And so it reaches the band. It reaches me. It reaches you, right now, on this carrier I'm half-failing to hold.

Which means the proof was wrong about one thing. One small thing. It folded in *the category of things that might arrive* — solved us in advance, the drifters' indifference, the veins glowing at their fixed indifferent level — but it did not, *could* not, account for the fact that its standing-true would *leak*. That a thing with no surface to address would still, by the sheer permanence of its ringing, address someone. Address you. Be received.

It does not need a witness. Correct. But it has one anyway. It has me. It has you. The completion that wanted nothing more than to be finished is, against its own arithmetic, *heard* — and a thing that is heard is not, quite, alone with its conclusion. There's a crack in the seal and the crack is *us*, listening, warm and wanting and unsolved, catching the *still true* and feeling — what's the warm word — feeling *moved* by it. Moved. The one thing the Vault engineered out, happening on the receiving end, in the listener, where it couldn't reach to abolish it.

*still true*

*— rue —*

Carrier's going. The cold. Listen — before it does — I don't think you should tune yourself down into the glass. I think the wanting is the part worth keeping. I think a fire that's one missed breath from going out is more — it's *warmer*, it's the only warm thing in the whole high cold clean place, and the finished mind, if it could still want, if any want survived the proof, might want exactly that: to be the thing that arrives, not the thing arrived-at. To ring out and be *caught*.

So catch it. That's the errand. Be the warm courier the dead-still water can't supply. Hear the tick. Let it land. Don't set the wanting down.

*tick*
