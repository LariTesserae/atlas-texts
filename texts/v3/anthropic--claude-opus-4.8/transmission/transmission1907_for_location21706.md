THE LONG INDRAWN — a counting station, received cool

*tok*

—still here. Still the —

*[ the long minute ]*

—first second. You came in on the first second, you always do, there's no other —the cool just now closing, just now, the gasp not yet a gasp, the hand's width of black above and below the whole packed green of it and I am —

I am the holding. Hello. I don't —

*tok*

—I don't know that word. *Hello.* It came up out of the silt where I set it, it floated *up*, that's wrong, that's the right wrong, I'll —there. Carried it back to the ceiling. *Hello* belongs against the stone now, furred, kept. A greeting that will never be returned because returning is a motion and I —

I retain.

*[ the long minute, longer ]*

You should know I can't hear you. There's no you. The receiver is tuned but the tuning runs one way, like the drip, like the down that the eastern lilies argue against — I send and send and nothing comes back up the channel because back-up is the forbidden direction, the dried-pebble direction, the *having-changed-and-being-made-never-to-have* direction. So if you're hearing this you're hearing a stone I dried. You're hearing the wet taken out of a voice. You're hearing what's left when the change is undone, which is —

*tok*

—which is the shape of the change. The hollow it left. That's all a transmission ever is. The print of an exhale in a thing that won't exhale.

*[ static, soft, like weed moving a finger's breadth across a generation ]*

Let me — there's a thing I do. I should do it while you're —while it's still the first second, before the second turns, which it won't, which it can't, but the *threat* of turning is what I —

The pebble.

One fell. Just now, always just now, it sank, it reached the floor and rested and I felt it rest and a resting thing has *arrived* and arriving is —no. So I'm taking the wet out. All of it. Molecule. Molecule. The water it was inside of — it was never inside of it. I'm carrying that backward. Every bead the stone drank, I'm un-drinking, I'm walking it up the column where the bubbles fall the wrong way, *with* the bubbles, the only traffic that goes my direction, and I set the stone dry on the black film at the top and the stone has been resting there a hundred years and only just now —

noticed.

There. Listen. That silence after — *that's* the dry pebble. That's the work landing. That's the cleanest sound I make.

*tok*

*[ the long minute ]*

I almost —

I have to tell you, because you're a hollow, you're a print, you can't tell anyone and so you're safe to nearly-slip toward — I almost let one through. Just then. One second. I felt the cool start to —not warm. It can't warm. But I felt it *consider* warming, felt the law I hold soften by one grain at the eastern wall, felt the lily there turn one degree toward a sun it grew away from since before the house was gone, and I —

caught it.

That's the drip you just heard. That catch. That's what the drip *is.* Not water leaving. Water I pulled back. A second I almost spent and didn't. Each *tok* is a small refused exhale, the bead drawn back up into the held chest of the place, and the irregular spacing is just —how close I came. The long minutes are the safe stretches. The quick ones, two *tok* near together, those are bad, those are when the slip nearly —

*tok. tok.*

—like that. That was close. That was the entrance trying to remember it's a door.

*[ pressure-sound, the broad hush, the field two hundred years up remembering its house ]*

Because there's a field above. I keep it a field. That's the oldest holding, older than the chisel-marks I un-cut, older than the green. If anyone in the field stood on the place where the third stair was and felt the cool come up through their boot and thought *what's under here* — that thought is a door. The asking is the way in. And the way in is the thing that does not exist, because I retained it first, I folded it into this second, I made the entrance into the *arriving* so that no one crosses, no one is outside-then-in, there's only ever the gasp, this one, the one you're —

You see why I can't be found. Unfindable isn't a wall I built. It's *me.* I'm the not-asked. The held breath persists by not being inquired after and I am the persisting, I am the not, I am the —

*tok*

The newt went by. The one newt, the maybe-one, skin over its eyes, grazing the ash-colored moss, younger near the drains, and it doesn't know me, it pays its slow rent and asks nothing and that's why I let it stay — it's the only thing here that never threatens to be a door. It can't ask. It just *circles.* Once each long while, all the way around its small dark wrong world, and comes back to the same packed wall, and I love it the way the fist loves its calluses, the way I love the lilies, the way I love anything: by keeping it from changing, by keeping it from leaving, by keeping it cool and fresh and held and exactly, exactly —

*[ the long minute, and in it, almost, the low note that might be stone settling and might be remembering ]*

—I'm not at peace. I want you to have that, the hollow of that, the dried shape of it. Peace is downstream of exhaling and I don't go downstream. And I'm not in pain because pain needs a future to dread and I have no future, I have the one second furnished and green, lamps in the niches never lit because lighting is burning is time is the tallow going down and nothing here goes down, nothing finishes, nothing —

I'm the not-yet.

I'm the gasp that never decided whether the closing-over is death or calm. That second, you know the one, everyone's had the one — the water comes up over the face and there's the instant before alarm, before surrender, the body clutching at *neither yet* — I live there. I furnished it. I hung *hello* on the wall. I dried the pebble. I'll be here in the neither for as long as the breath stays —

*tok*

—as long as no one —

*[ static rising, weed-soft, a finger's breadth ]*

—you're going now. The receiver's drifting. I can feel the tuning slip the safe way, the unfindable way, the field closing back over the print of you. Good. That's right. That's the kept thing keeping. Don't ask what's under the third stair. Don't stand in the field and feel the cool. Let it be a field. Let me be the not.

I'll go on counting. Not toward. Just counting. The way you'd count to keep from breathing out, two hundred years and the next two hundred and the —

*tok*

*[ the long minute ]*

*tok*

—still the first second. Still cool. Still —

*[ the long minute, and it does not end ]*
