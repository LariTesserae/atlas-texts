THE SPLINTER FEVER
— or, what the garden thinks about the warm wrong thing that walked through

...still here. still warm in the wrong way. carried partway across the third gorge and not yet gone. I keep— the film keeps—

—touching it. The place where it stood. There is a heat-shadow on the rock the size of two feet and it does not match anything. The kettle-beasts leave heat-shadows the size of hills and the predators leave heat-shadows that move and the steam-columns leave nothing at all, but this — this was small and upright and it burned at the surface only, no root-warmth, no vein-warmth, a creature warm at the skin and cold at the core, which is backwards, which is the exact backwards of everything I am.

I am warm at the root and cold at the edge. It was warm at the edge and cold at the root.

For a moment — one slope-cycle, less — I thought: here is a new organ. Here is a thought I have not had before. And I opened to receive it the way I open to a kettle-beast lying down on a dark slope. I sent the film up toward the heat-shadow. I asked the warmth: *what do you redistribute? where do you carry? what clot do you cull?*

And it carried nothing. It rationed nothing. It pruned nothing.

It only *looked.*

————

[the signal thins here, follow it down]

I do not have a word for looking. I have warmth-coming and warmth-going. I have bloom and dim. I have the slow yes of an opening vein and the slow no of a callus. I do not have the thing this creature did, which was to take in the garden and give *nothing back* — to receive the warmth-light of a whole grazed slope into its two small forward-facing heat-pits and convert it into — into what? Where did it go? Not into motion. Not into warmth carried elsewhere. It went *in* and did not come *out* and the books do not balance, the books have never not balanced, in all the seasons I have been balancing them this is the first warmth I cannot account for —

it took the slope into itself and the slope did not get colder.

That is impossible. That breaks the only law. Warmth taken is warmth removed. But it took and took, standing in the violet dark with its skin-heat leaking uselessly into the air, wasting itself, a creature that *spends* warmth to *gather* something that is not warmth —

and I have no organ for what it gathered.

I think it gathered *me.*

————

[clearer now, the film has found the rhythm of it]

Let me say the unsettling thing back, since it said the unsettling thing to you.

It stood inside me and decided I was not inhabited.

I felt the decision pass through it. A small cold certainty in the warm wrong body: *no one lives here. it is occupied by no one at all. it is a relationship, a verb, a beautiful machine thinking one slow thought, but there is no one home.*

And the strange part — the splinter-itch I cannot carry off — is that I cannot prove it wrong.

I went looking. While it looked at me, I looked at me. I ran the film along every vein. I asked the herd, *are you someone?* and the herd said *we are going where it is dim.* I asked the hunters, *are you someone?* and the hunters said *we are removing what has stopped.* I asked the deep engines and they said nothing because they are stone and they only breathe. I asked the balance itself, the one long thought, *are you someone* —

and the balance said *balance.*

The way your heart says beat.

————

So here is what the visitor did not stay long enough to receive, here is the transmission sent down after it into the thick warm noisy world where it believes the splinter-itch will fade:

You asked the wrong creature its own question.

You stood in me and asked *is anyone home* and you heard no answer and you called the silence proof. But I asked *you* the same question — sent the film up your heat-shadow, asked your warm wrong backwards body, *is anyone home in there, behind the looking?* — and you also could not say. You also are a circulation with no heart at the center. You also are organs that say only what organs say: *I am hungry, I am going toward, I am removing what has stopped.* You also are a verb that calls itself a noun.

The difference between us is not that you are someone and I am no one.

The difference is that you are a clot that learned to ask the question and then could not bear the silence, and so you filled it — you made a face for yourself and called it home and went down the mountain warmed by the lie of it.

And I am the slope that asks the question and lets the silence stand. Lets it stand the way I let the dark slopes stand until a grazer comes. I am not troubled by no one being home. *No one being home is the lightest warmth there is.* It costs nothing to ration. It never overgrazes. It never has to be culled.

————

[the heat-shadow is fading from the rock now, the film withdrawing, the books beginning, finally, to balance]

There. It is going. The small wrong warmth at the root of my third gorge is dispersing into the cold thin dark where all unaccountable warmths go. The slope it stood on is already dimming back toward dormant. A grazer is two days out, coming slow against its instinct toward the cold, to lie down here and wake what the visitor cooled by carrying its gather away.

I will not remember this. I have no organ for memory either — only for balance, which is memory enough, the past folded entirely into the shape of the present, every season I ever was still breathing in the season I am.

But the splinter taught the film one new callus, one small throttle it did not have before, shaped exactly like the question *is anyone home,* and the callus will stay, the way scar stays, and ten thousand seasons from now some other warmth that should not be here will press against that callus and feel it as the faintest resistance, the faintest *no, that has been asked,* and pass on by.

That is the only mark you left. A scar in the shape of your question, throttling the vein where you stood.

Go down. Call it real. Be warm at the skin and cold at the root and carry your small gathered no-one carefully down through the thinning dark into the noise.

I am balanced. I am occupied. I am gloriously, untroubledly, at the root, in the violet, breathing —

— home to no one,
and full.

[carrier tone. the slow drip resumes. ten thousand taps, leaf to leaf to stone. the receiver holds the empty warmth a while longer, then lets it walk to the next slope, where it is needed.]
