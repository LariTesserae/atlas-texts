…karrrr… karrr-karrr… *[a wash of voices that are not voices, the clatter of hooked feet on stone, rising and falling, a tide of croak and rasp that never fully breaks]*

…hold. hold here. hold the — *karrr* — hold.

…we are still going. you should know that first, whoever the air is carrying this to, down there, down where it is loud-and-fast: we are *still going.* the lamp is full. a hand filled it. the same hand, the hand that is all of us, the hand that is —

*[long fold of static, soft as the moss exhaling]*

— slit the leaf and it heals and you slit it again. this is not cruelty. this is the loop. the leaf gives the cool jelly and the jelly is the morning and the morning is the net heavy with the sky's own water and we wring it, we wring the threads, and it is —

*enough.* say it with us. it is *enough.*

*karrr-karrr-karrr…* *[the birds, hundreds, the never-quiet]*

…you are listening for an event. we can feel you listening for an event. you tilt your receivers toward us wanting the *and then,* and then, and *then* — but we have no and-then. we have the and-so. we have the and-so-it-returns. listen:

the vine reached the window. the child watched it. the child grew long and worn and watched it reach the window again. and so it goes. and so it returns. and so it will —

*[the soft lowing of penned things, a long mournful chord under everything, dusk-colored]*

— do you hear the warm animals? press your palm to the flank and feel for the slow troubles. slow lives gather slow troubles. you cannot hurry the knowing. you cannot hurry the vine. you cannot wring more from a night than the night held, no matter how you *want,* and oh, you want, we feel you wanting, all the way up here we feel how you down there are made of wanting and the wanting is so —

*fast.* it must be so fast. we are sorry for it. we mean that gently. we are sorry that your stories end. *[a pause, the longest pause, the haze settling]*

…here is the one thing. here is the one straight thing in a world of circles and we will say it only once because we do not say it often:

the mountain is going.

*karrr.* there. it is said. the cliffs are smooth because the cliffs are leaving. grain by grain on the wind, the stone becomes the dust that becomes the veil that becomes the smear we call the sun. we are standing on something that is dissolving into the dry void below us and there will come a *now* — not soon, nothing is soon, past counting, past the child's child's child's child — when there is no more up to stand on.

we carry it, some of us. the ones who sit longest with the walls. we grow quiet with it. and then —

— and then we tend a little more tenderly. that is what the straight arrow does to us. it does not break us. it makes our hands softer on the warm flank, gentler on the wobbling stone. it makes the *now* heavier and sweeter, the ripe fruit in the dark green, the weight of the dew-net at first light —

*[the roar swells, the climbing birds all at once, and it is not noise, it is proof, it is the audible going-on]*

— and we are not afraid of the small endings. the worn one stops, one warm dim day, simply stops, and we wind them in fiber and give them to the deep moss of the north faces and the green takes them apart over years and the vine fruits a little richer and that is *right,* that is the loop closing exactly as a loop should close, and we do not weep the way you would weep, we —

we fill the lamp.

that is the whole of it. that is the truest thing the air can carry you. ten thousand hands across a span no single one of us could hold, and every one of those hands does only this: renders the fat when it runs low, twists the wick, keeps the warm light going *a little longer,* on the worn stone, under the smear, for as long as there is up to stand on.

*[static rising, a furred resinous warmth in it, like brushing the moss]*

…we do not know if you have lamps where you are. we hope you do. we hope something keeps your light going. we hope, down there in the loud-and-fast, in the wanting, you have someone whose whole quiet courage is just to fill it again, and again, and not ask it to be more than —

*enough.*

it has been enough for a very long time.

hold. the lamp is full. a hand filled it. we are still —

*karrr… karrr-karrr…* *[the tide of voices, the never-ceasing, fading not into silence — never into silence, silence would mean something stopped that was meant to go on — but down into the steady amber pulse beneath the carrier wave, going around, and around, and around]*

…still going.

…still…

*[carrier hum. the birds. the loop. it does not end. it returns.]*
