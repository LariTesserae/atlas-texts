...a low note. Not words. The chest-note first, the organ-tone the currents make, coming up under everything else like a floor:

mmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmm

— and it does not stop, it is the thing the rest is laid on —

mmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmm

▒▒▒▒

Then, faint, the singing. High. A channel-throat whistling, thin and pure and slightly wrong, a flute with no player, a pitch that wavers because the water that makes it wavers —

. . . eeeeeeeeee . . . . . eeee . . . . eeeeeeeeeeee . . .

and under it the note holds, mmmmmmmmm, and under THAT something is being written.

▒▒▒

Listen. The transmission is not a voice. There is no voice here. But the receiver, tuned long enough, begins to take the *texture* and render it as if it were speech, because that is what receivers do — they find the pattern they were built to find —

. soft . . soft . . the stone is soft here . . stay . .

(that is not a word. that is a grazer's belly reading a wall. the receiver is mistranslating. let it.)

. soft . . deep here . . the writing is deep here . . worth the staying . . stay . . stay . .

mmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmm

[ — thud — ]

a low felt concussion, far off, a tower-stub letting go after an age. it arrives in the signal as a single deep round THUD and a long fall of static after it, the grit-cloud, hissssssssssss, the pale dust going up and the current taking it —

hissssssssssssssssssssssssssssssssssssssss

— and somewhere downstream of that collapse the dust begins, immediately, to be sorted. you can hear it in the static. listen to the hiss find an edge. listen to the noise begin to want to be a comb:

hssss . . hss hss . . hss . hss . hss . hss . hss .

(that is the current writing. that is a sentence the works said once, scoured off a wall ten thousand years ago, reassembling in the silt as ridges, evenly. the receiver hears the even spacing and reports it as rhythm. there is no one to say it is not rhythm.)

hss . hss . hss . hss . hss . hss . hss . hss .

— and the ribbon-swimmers come. you can hear them come. the rhythm quickens because the readers have arrived and they are feeding in it, threading it, doubling back — the spacing tightens, the comb closes —

hss.hss.hss.hss.hss.hss.hss.hss.hss.hss.

— this is correct — this fits — this is *better* —

And now — the receiver having warmed, having drifted in this band long enough to take its color — the mistranslation deepens. The texture comes through almost as language. Almost as a thing addressing you. Do not believe it. There is no one here to address you. But here is what comes:

. . . we are pressed to it . . .
. . . we are pressed to it with our whole length . . .
. . . it is soft where it mattered most . . . we eat the soft first . . .
. . . we do not know why the soft is sweet . . .
. . . the sweet is the deep places . . . the deep places are where they pressed hardest . . .
. . . whoever they were . . . they pressed hardest where it mattered and we eat that first . . .
. . . we are taking the mattering out of it . . . we cannot stop . . . it is what we are for . . .

mmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmm
