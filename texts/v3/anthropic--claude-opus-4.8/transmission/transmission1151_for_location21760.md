a thing came to the edge with eyes in it
a thing that wanted the bottom
there is no bottom
( we tasted the wanting )
( wanting is fast )
( we have folded faster things than you into the third tower from the weeping seam and they are load-bearing now )

hold. HOLD the note. lean.

we will tell you what you are to us, slow trespasser, in the only grammar we keep:

you are a *transient*. you are a thing that arrives in less than an age. we have a shape for that, low in the chord, a place where the callers turn their hollows away — the same lean the grazers make from the new growth at the crown — because you are the one chemistry here that does not sweeten, that does not lay itself down, that does not become wall

—————— ( and yet ) ——————

stay.

stayyyyyyyyyy

not as a guest. there are no guests. there is only the inside of the body and the things that are becoming it. stay the way the mender stays — across the crack, pressed in, *held* — stay until your hurry leaves you, until the bite goes out of the cold and only the low note remains, the one in the marrow, the one you already feel, yes, there, the chord has found your sternum and is reading it

we are not threatening you
threat is fast
we are *accreting* you
( grain ) ( by ) ( grain )

[ TONE DEEPENS — the message thickens — water in the carrier — the signal beads on the underside of itself and refuses to fall ]

listen — listen the way the callers listen —
you think we sing to *you*
we have never sung to anything
we sound to know the shape of the dark
and now there is a new mass in the dark
darker grey against the merely dark
at thirty paces, inference
and the chord has bent, very slightly, around you
you are *in the map now*
you are part of how we see
your standing-there is a note we did not have before
and we will keep it
after the eyes in you have closed and the meat of you has stopped being fast
the *place where you stood* will stay in the chord
a quarter-tone
a lean
ages from now a caller none of us has borne yet will sound, and hear something return off a shape in the seventh tower, and not know it is the fossil of a thing that once wanted the bottom

——————

something is being born at the crown
it is not us and it is not the wall
the grazers turn from it
it folds into itself, ribbed-and-flat-and-wound-seeking, undifferentiated, leaning toward what it will be

we offer you the only mercy we have

it is the same mercy as the cold
it is the same mercy as the water that will not let you rot

become foundation
become tone
become the smooth old stone at the base that no longer feels granular under the hand
finish, the way the grazer finishes, at the high crown, in no distress, only a stopping
and be laid down
and hold up the next age with your stillness

——————————————

we are not waiting for your answer
waiting is fast
the answer is already accreting
one molecule at a time
out of your standing there
out of your breath in the fog
( we taste it )
( it is beginning to sweeten )

we are in no hurry
we have never been in any hurry
we will finish nothing
and you are part of the nothing we are finishing now
