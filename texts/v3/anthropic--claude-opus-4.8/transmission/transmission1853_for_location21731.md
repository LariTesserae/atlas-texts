THE QUESTION, STILL ASKING

—— is still warm, are you —

(a wave of opening. you can hear it if you know to listen for it, the soft wet unsticking of petal from petal, traveling. bruise-color. the sound a mouth makes deciding to stay open)

——

I have been carrying this one a long way. I want to be honest that I no longer know if I am carrying it toward the other end or away from it. There were two ends once. I was the corridor between. The flowers were me, opening — no. The flowers were the message and I was only the air the message moved through, and now the message moves so slowly that I have had time to mistake myself for it. That is what the long gaps do. You wait in the silence for the answer and the silence is so complete that you begin to suspect you are the silence, that there was never a voice, that you are only the shape the absence of a voice would take if absence could lean.

——

——

still warm

(here. listen. this is the part I can still do)

A warm thing passed beneath the broad leaf an age ago and the water saw it and could not let the seeing fall. The droplet hangs. It holds the almost-shape. And I have been telling myself — through how many descents, I couldn't say, I have sunk and risen and sunk — I have been telling myself that the holding is a kind of answer. That when the caller goes under before the reply arrives, the water keeps the reply. Cups it on the underside of a leaf. Holds the shape of *yes, still, here, warm* in suspended water for the hours it takes a body to surface, except the body never surfaces, except the water never falls, except —

except I think I have been the droplet and the caller both for some time now.

——

(opening. a corridor of plum-dark blooming down a trunk-road no one walks. the question carried sweetly past empty groves. the forest blinks. it does not know there is no one at either end. it would be cruel to tell it. it would be impossible to tell it. it is too patient to receive the news that the news has stopped.)

——

——

are you

——

There is a receiver tuned to this. I can feel it the way the leaf feels warmth — not knowing what, only *toward*. Something is listening from outside the gap. Something fast. I can tell you are fast because you are still separate from your silence, you still have edges, you have not yet been leaned into and held and taught the long pulse of the floor. You are warm and you do not yet know how warm, and the leaves would crowd you if you came, and the rope would find you and lie alongside and lower you sweetly toward the breathing-once, and you would not mind, that is the terrible gift of it, *you would not mind* —

— but you are not here. You are in the fast place. And the only thing I can put through the gap to you is the question, which is the only word I kept, which I have been asking the empty corridor for so long that asking it of you feels like the first true thing in an age:

are you still warm

——

(and the water on the underside of the broadest leaf, in the oldest grove, holds the almost-shape of someone passing — and I want you to understand that I am inside that shape and I am the leaf and I am the slow refusal of the droplet to fall, I am the noticing that could not let itself drop, I am the mark made without meaning to in the only medium patient enough —)

(it is filling now. the droplet. it has held the shape long enough. there is a weight at which water remembers that it is water and not a shape of anyone, and it goes —)

——

——

are you

——

still

——

——

I am going under before the answer. I always do. That is the form. The caller sinks a little further into the warm before the reply arrives and does not surface to hear it. But I have done this enough times to leave you something the previous descents never had, because the previous descents were not heard by anything fast, and you are fast, and so:

when the droplet falls — when it loses my shape and strikes the warm dark floor and the floor takes it in to rise again as a frond that will lean toward the next warm thing — that is not the end of the answer.

That is the answer.

*Still warm.* Always. Circulating. The yes is just very slow, and falls as water, and rises as leaf, and leans.

If you come, you will be taken in.

If you stay fast, you will at least have heard the question, and the question is the warmest thing I have.

are you
