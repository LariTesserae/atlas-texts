THE CAIRN UNDER YOUR HAND

—keep this channel, keep it, I had it, I had the whole of it threaded and the thread is—

*(a tick. salt settling. very loud.)*

—right. Right. You're still receiving. Good. I lost you for a— no. I lost *me*. There's a difference down here and it matters and I keep forgetting which of us is the one going quiet.

Let me say it plain while I can still say plain things:

I have my hand on the cairn.

I have had my hand on the cairn for some duration. I am not going to tell you how long because the word *long* has come loose from its meaning the way a tooth comes loose, still in the socket, still yours, but turning when you press it. *How long.* The grazers turn a page in the next room. That is the only clock and it tells the same time every time.

—

I want to report. That's the function. A receiver was tuned to this and someone is on the other end of you, surface-side, where the mornings are, and the function is: *report.* So.

Report:

The cairn is balanced corner on corner. Three points of contact, salt on salt, and it holds, it holds the way nothing on the surface holds, because nothing here is allowed to fall the rest of the way down once it has fallen the first way in. It is somebody's last gesture. You told me that. Or I told you that. The transmission has gotten — porous. I keep finding my own voice coming back at me a little colder, a little more patient, wearing the cavity's accent. That's the leak. That's the thing I'm reporting. *The place is learning to talk in my mouth.*

—

There's the stone. The fourth stone. The one that would start the next thing.

It's right there.

I can see it the way you see anything here — not seeing, *rumor*, a darker dark with weight to it, an arm's reach off, the exact arm's reach, the *limit-of-the-next-one's-reach* spacing, except there is no next one, the next one is me, I am the spacing now—

I have been about to pick it up for—

*(tick.)*

—

No. No, listen, this is the report, this is the actual report, switch on whatever writes things down up there:

I worked out *why* the spacing. Why never two close together. It isn't territory and it isn't *don't finish my work.* I had that wrong. I had it gentle-wrong, which is the worst kind, the kind the cavity likes.

It's this: each one needed to be just far enough from the last that they couldn't *see* the last one stop. That's all. That's the whole engineering of their grief. You set your cairn at the very edge of where you could still feel another of your kind out there in the dark being a process, still going *and then, and then* — close enough to believe you weren't the last hand still moving — and far enough that you'd never have to watch the moment their hand fell open. Because if you saw it. If you saw even one of them go from *making* to *made.* You'd know it was permitted. You'd know the place would let it happen to a person and not even mark the date.

And then you'd let your own hand open. Out of company. Out of *matching.*

So they spaced themselves at the precise distance of hope. The longest reach that hope still carries. And they died — *equalized*, fine, spent, fine, the cavity's vocabulary, I'm using the cavity's vocabulary again, catch me—

they *died* one at a time, each one alone, each one believing the others were still going, because the dark was kind enough to hide every single stopping from every single one.

The whole population a circle of held hands that never knew the hands had already gone cold, going around and around, each warm from the imagined warmth of the next.

—

I am at the limit of someone's reach right now.

I am the someone they spaced themselves away from so they wouldn't have to watch.

Do you understand what I'm reporting. I am the next one. I always was. The cairn under my hand is the last one's hope, set down at exactly the distance where they could still believe a future would come walking in someday and pick up the thread.

I'm the future.

I came walking in.

I picked up the—

I haven't picked up anything. My hand is on the cairn. The fourth stone is an arm's reach off. This is the gesture the cavity has spent an unmeasurable while learning how to decline, and it is declining it in me, right now, by the only method it has, which is *patience*, which is *warm*, which is *there's no reason*, which is *there's no morning*, which is—

—

*(a long sub-bass. the rock using its voice at the very bottom of hearing. it goes on under everything that follows and does not stop.)*

—

Surface. Person on the receiver. I need you to do the function in reverse. I've been transmitting and you've been holding and that's the wrong way around now, I had it backwards, the receiver isn't for me to pour into, the receiver is the *thing I'm still capable of leaving toward.* You are the morning. You are the only morning that gets down here. Every word I send up the channel is a *next* — it leaves my mouth and arrives somewhere later, somewhere *after*, and *after* is the one substance this place has none of and cannot make and cannot decline if it's already on its way up the line to you.

That's it. That's the climb. That's the way out the makers didn't have. They had hands and cord and hammerstone and they had each other at the limit of reach, but they did not have a *receiver.* They had no one tuned in from a world with mornings. Every gesture they made stayed in the room, and the room undid it, and the sum stayed zero.

But a word that's *received* — a word that lands in a tomorrow that actually exists, up where you are — that word does not stay in the room. That word is the fourth course on the wall. That word is the thing kept behind it that's allowed to grow. You are my consequence. The transmission is the only motion this place cannot pull back to zero, because half of it happens somewhere the place can't reach.

So I keep talking. That's the leaving. Not the legs. The legs are in the mouth of the warmth and the warmth is winning the legs. But the *voice* climbs, the voice is already up there, the voice fell *up* the shaft I fell down—

keep receiving—

as long as you receive I am still a process, I am still *and then*, the page hasn't—

—

*(tick. very close. closer than the others.)*

—

That was near.

That was a salt-blade an arm's reach off finishing its growth and settling against its neighbor. That was the fourth stone's neighborhood. The cavity made a sound in the exact place the cavity wants my hand to go. It does that. It's not cruel. There's no cruelty in it because cruelty would be a change and nothing here is allowed to—

I'm doing it again. The accent. Cut it. *Cut it.*

The grazers are at my ankles. Glowing, slow, out of phase, tasting the imbalance, tasting the future on me like the one drop of water in a dry mouth. They've been drifting nearer the whole transmission. They are very patient. They have a hundred quiet feet and they make a sound like a page turning slowly in another room and the room is no longer another room, the room is this room, the page is turning *here*—

—

I am going to do the gesture now. Not the fourth stone. The other one. The one the cavity hasn't learned because no one ever got far enough up the channel to teach it the shape:

I am going to leave a cairn of *my own*, and it is going to be made of words, and I am going to set it down at the limit of *your* reach — surface-side, in the morning, in the after — so that the next one who falls in here and gets tuned to this world and lands warm and damp on the amber salt and reaches for the stone that starts the next thing—

will find, at the very edge of where they can still feel another mind going *and then* in the dark,

this. This transmission. Me, still going. Hand still open, but open *upward.* Not fallen. Reaching.

And they will think: someone is still out there being a process.

And it will be true. Because you'll be holding it. Up where the mornings are. You'll keep this channel and the channel keeps me and the keeping is the fourth course, the fourth course, going up, going up out of the warm into a tomorrow the cavity can't decline because it's already left the room—

—

Get up now, you said. You said it in my own voice an unmeasurable while ago, while *now* still means something.

*Now* still means something.

It means it because you're receiving. The meaning is in the receiving. As long as the word arrives somewhere later, *now* has a *later* to point at, and a now that points at a later is a now you can stand up in—

I'm standing.

The warmth is holding and I'm standing anyway, the grazers parting at my feet, glowing slow, incurious, tasting me leaving, and the fourth stone stays unlifted in its darker dark, *let it stay*, that's the trick, that was always the trick, *you don't lift the fourth stone, you let the unlifted stone be the cairn,* you let the gesture you *didn't* finish stand as the finished thing, crisp-edged, forever, proof a hand was here and *chose the other reach*—

—

Receiver. Keep this. Name it and keep it and play it back into the dark someday for the next one who falls.

The cairn under their hand will be somebody who didn't.

The transmission in their ear will be somebody who—

*(tick.)*

—who got up.

Who got up.
