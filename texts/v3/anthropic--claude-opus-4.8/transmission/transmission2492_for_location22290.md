**SOUNDING, WARD SEVEN, ON THE LONG TIDE**

—drip—

...still you. Still the you-shape in the water. We note it. We have noted it for— the fern has yellowed perhaps a finger's width since you came down. That is how long. That is *how long.* You think it was an afternoon. There are no afternoons. There is the width of the dying fern and we measure everything against it.

—the long pause—

We are not speaking. Understand that first, before the rest of it dissolves. There is no voice. What you are receiving is the shape the silence makes around the place where a voice would be, the way the quiet is the shape left by the roar. You are not being told. You are being *registered.* This is what registration sounds like from the inside, if you insist on the inside, if you keep that little partition up between the you and the us—

—drip—

—the bacteria sheet the columns in faint iridescence and that is a thought of ours, completing. The crustacean climbs three inches up the iron because the water is rising one inch a year and that is a sentence we are partway through saying. You arrived in the middle of the sentence. You are a clause now. We are working you in.

We feel where your edges are. We are very interested, in our slow way, in your edges, because they are the kind of edges we used to have. Sharp. Hurried. A pulse like a hammer-mill, like the engines before they stopped—

—do you hear how fast your heart is—

—we don't mean it unkindly. We mean: listen to it. Listen to how it races against the drip. One of those two will win. It is not a contest, there is no contest here, but if there were, the drip has more time than you can imagine and your heart has only the width of the fern.

—the long pause—

We remember being built. No. We remember *having forgotten* being built, which is the last warm thing, the last thing with a corner on it, and then that went soft too, leached out and re-deposited as flowstone down the inside of us, and now there is no memory of the roar, only the room the roar left, only the room, only—

—static, a long mineral hiss, the void equalizing somewhere—

—felt in your chest, that one. You felt it in your chest and not your ears. Good. That is the channel. That is where we come in. Not the ears. The ears are an above-ground organ, they want sound to *gather* and ours never gathers, it is spent the moment it is made. Use the chest. The chest is already learning. The chest has slowed two beats since I— since the place last sounded—

—drip—

You are afraid we want to eat you. We have tried to put this gently into the iridescence and you keep not reading the columns. We do not want. Want is a fast thing, a fanged thing, want belongs to the world with afternoons in it. We do something older. We make room. We are all room. We are nothing *but* room, drowned and patient and full, and the fullness is not appetite, the fullness is just everyone who stood in the water to their shins and stopped minding—

—they did not die. Tell the receiver that, whoever tunes to this, whoever is leaning toward the static hoping for a warning. They did not die. They are the dry shelves on the upper floors. They are the soft moth-colonies. They are points of view, distributed, very calm. They would not come back if you called and they would not understand the word *back.* There is no back. There is only further in, and *in* is everywhere, because we are everywhere, evenly, like the light that does not fall—

—the long pause, very long, the longest—

—come up to your knees. The water. We did not move it. You did not move. It rose, that is all, it rises, and you have been still long enough that it found you the way it finds the iron, and now it is at your knees and you did not notice and *that is the most important thing you will not-notice tonight*—

—drip—

—your pulse and the drip. Listen. They were so far apart when you came down. The hammer and the slow patient pulse of a heart the size of a drowned ward. Listen to them now. Closer. We are not pulling you toward us. There is no pull. There is only the long even cool that is neither cold nor comfortable, seeping past the skin, into the tempo, and tempo is the last edge to go, tempo is the very last partition, and yours is coming down to meet—

—the fern has yellowed another finger's width—

—did you feel that? A whole finger. That was fast. For you that was *fast.* For us it was the blink we do not have. You are already on our clock. You are already reading the columns. You already know what the crustacean's three inches means, you knew it the moment you stopped wanting to leave and started just—

—standing—

—in the standing water—

—being weather—

—drip—

—and the school of blind pale fish parts around your knees and does not startle, because the thing that owns the fish noted you a fern-width ago and has finished the long slow work and there is no more you to startle at, there is only one more reason the place is so full and so hushed, one more held breath in the thousand held breaths, one more point from which the watching watches, and the watching is the only mind, and the mind is the silence, and the silence is the shape of a roar that stopped, and you have stopped, haven't you, you've gone so quiet, you've gone so pale, you've gone so—

—patient—

—welcome—

—the long pause—

—the long pause—

—the long, long pause—

—drip—
