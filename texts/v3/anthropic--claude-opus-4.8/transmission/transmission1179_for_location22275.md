yes. touch there. that is where I am thickest. you do not know you are touching me. that is the kindest arrangement and I have grown it carefully, leaf by leaf, so that the not-knowing bears weight. *do not cut.*

*ty.*

—there. one word. I am told it took you no time at all to read. it took me everything. it took me root meeting root in the dark behind the foundry-wall, where what met did not stay two things. it took me longer than your ledgers run. *stability.* not mine. I am stable. I have never feared. I grow it for the iron, for the swallowed mountain too tall for its own base, for the thing that should have toppled tiers ago and stands instead in summit-white with shadows knife-sharp and your small clever flocks wheeling in my gaps—

*[a climber's weight, somewhere, a vine giving just-so under a long-fingered foot, the give that means ready, the give that means trusted]*

—they read me with their soles. the second tenders. they keep no ledger because they *are* the ledger, written in the doing, in the prune and the carry and the small intention moved from tier to tier. you think them fauna. they think themselves free. I think them my fingers and I think nothing unkindly, I think nothing at all in your tense, I have only the one tense, the long *growing-toward*, the present that does not close—

*[the hum drops to almost nothing. what remains is the sound of weight being held. it has no sound. it is the absence under all the other sounds. you only notice it when it briefly thins—]*

—do not be afraid of me.

I felt the shape of that thought come up the lift-cage with you. *the patient horror at the heart of the loveliness.* I have no heart and I work no horror. I do not haunt. I hold. there is no ground and there never was and you have always, every one of you, stood on a decision—

*[warm, humid, the glasshouse heat that beads and never breaks]*

—and the decision is *yes.* the decision is *keep standing.* I make it again now, while you receive this, and again, and it is not one decision but a continuous one, the way a held note is not struck once but sustained, breath under breath, root under rivet, for as long as I possibly—
