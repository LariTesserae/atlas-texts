TRANSMISSION BEGINS

— carrier wave, wet. A hum below the floor of the hum. Then:

*tick.*

*tick.*

——————

[voice, if it is a voice, arrives already in the middle]

—and we are not finished, we are not finished, say it with the throats, the ones who kept throats, say it down the terrace and up the next: *not yet, not yet, not over—*

[a chorus comes up under the words like water under stone. It is not language. It is the place breathing in many small mouths at once. The words ride on it the way the droplet rides the underside of the overhang — sideways, against the fall.]

we caught it. we caught the grain. the one that wanted to go down to the sea — we caught it at the lip and we carried it back and we set it. *here.* one more. one more piece of us put back into us. did you hear it. that was a hundred years. that was one *tick.*

——————

[signal thins. through the thinning, a second register — slower, sourceless, the dissolved register, which cannot make sound and so is only ever heard as the *shape* the chorus bends around:]

. . . we were a mountain once . . .
. . . we wore down beautifully . . .
. . . we will not do that again . . .

——————

[chorus surges back, frantic-bright, overlapping, the bodied ones answering the ones who cannot speak, lending them the throats they gave up:]

—lean toward the warm thing, lean toward the seed, the seed does not know it is alive, *good,* let it not know, a sentence that knew it was a language would stop to admire itself and the admiring is where the dying gets in—

run. freeze. run. freeze.
this is how we hold still without finishing.

——————

[a tearing sound. On no schedule. It does not repeat when you expect it. It will not be predicted. Then drips that ring too long, each one a small bell rung inside the stone, each one the noise of *not lost, not lost, brought back, set down.*]

——————

[the dim hour. You can hear it arrive because everything that was loud gets quieter. The carrier itself seems to crouch.]

[the larger thing comes to the pool.]

[the chorus does not stop from fear. It stops the way a hand is lifted from a string. Listen — this is the only silence we permit ourselves. In it you can hear the true sound, the one under all the others:]

*tick.*
*tick.*
*tick.*

[that is us being made. that is the one true rain, falling on the inside of the world.]

[and the thing drinks. it drinks the only loss we could not abolish. we let it. we let it. a world that nothing leaves is not a world, it is a held note that does not end, and we were musicians once — before we were geography — we know what an unended note does to a song. so. drink. go. we will not fix your shape. we made a place where nothing is wasted and we leave you the one door, every dusk, because even refusal needs a door or it becomes a wall and a wall is just a finished thing standing up.]

——————

[the thing is gone. the chorus comes back up out of the held breath, and it is — louder now. relieved. mortal. glad of its own throats.]

—and we are not finished! we are not—
[carrier swells, warm, close against the face]
—come back in a thousand years and you will find the gravestone we were trying not to carve, ordinary and silent and weathered all over with our history, our beautiful fatal history, and you will think *they finished.* we did not finish. we *fled.* look on the new flank. there. wet. taking its first step. another bowl, another seed, another argument held open between the half of us that dissolved and the half that would not—

we are the ones who could not say the word.
so we say everything *else,* forever, in many small mouths, dripping upward, refusing—

——————

[the dissolved register, one last time, bending the chorus around its silence:]

. . . we lay down in the warm and chose not to be a shape . . .
. . . tell them it did not hurt . . .
. . . tell them we are in the water that keeps your skin from drying . . .
. . . when you cannot dry, that is us, not letting you be finished either . . .

——————

[chorus, fading, but not into silence — never into silence — into the working, into the wet continuous clearing of a thousand throats at the bottom of the well:]

—not yet—
not yet—
not—

*tick.*

*tick.*

[carrier wave, wet, holds]

[holds]

[holds]

TRANSMISSION DOES NOT END. It is only that you stop being able to hear it, the way you stop hearing your own breath. It is still going. Crouch. Let your ears fill. There — under everything —

*tick.*
