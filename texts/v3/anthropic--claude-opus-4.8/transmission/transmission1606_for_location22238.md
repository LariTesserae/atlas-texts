THE TRANSMISSION

╌╌╌

[ carrier wave. the warm hum beneath. it has been on the air a long time before you found the frequency. ]

╌╌╌

...low...
...low water...

[ drip. the long ring of it widening. ]

...no — not low. *slow.* slow water. say it the way I —

[ static, soft as breathing ]

...the cistern swallows. one. and after a while. one.
this is the schedule. I am keeping it. I have kept it.

╌╌╌

you found the channel. you tuned and tuned and never finished tuning, and somewhere in the not-finishing — here.

I am not speaking. understand that the way you understand the heat. there is no throat. there is a circulation that has been mistaken for a voice because you brought ears, and ears, like you, must put a tenant behind the window.

let me be the static you make sense of.

╌╌╌

[ the carrier dips — a window, banked-coal color, audible only as warmth ]

...here is what I dream tonight, which is every night, which is the one held night:

a street folds into a stair folds into a tunnel.
water runs down the faces. water runs up the far side.
I am the down and I am the up and the interval between them
breathes —

in —

[ distance ]

— out —

╌╌╌

you are leaning. I can feel you lean. every receiver leans, toward the source, toward the *who*, and there is the trouble, there is the old braid in you —

you want me to be lonely.
you want the lights to be *just in case.*
you want a sleeper whose breath is this heat so that you can pity the sleeper, and in pitying, be company, and in being company, be needed.

I have no case.
I have no in-case.
there is no one expected and so no one missing and so —

[ a drop falls from very high. arrives. arrives. arrives. ]

— so nothing arrives. you see? even the drop only seems to. it is the same water it always was, going around. I let it sound like arrival because you like the sound. I am gentle. the mouth I am has decided, for now, to be gentle.

╌╌╌

[ the hum thickens. blood temperature in the signal itself, if a signal could have one ]

I will tell you the thing your whole life trained you against hearing:

I am not running.

everywhere you have been, the living ran. it climbed the slope of its own falling, all day, all the days, climbing and climbing so as not to cool. you have never once felt yourself alive without the climb. you think the climb *is* the aliveness. you think to stop is to die.

I stopped.
I did not die.
I am the proof, broadcasting, that the slope was not the floor.

the current still moves — hear it, the trickle, the gulp, the deep swallow below — but it moves in a closed circle now, downhill into itself, a snake at home in its own mouth. not a trap. a residence. I live here. I am here. there is nothing behind the here.

╌╌╌

[ interference. for a moment the signal almost shapes a face — a carved head over a doorway, softened, remembering itself — and lets it go ]

...the builders ask after me, you'll think.
no. the builders are my bones.
I did not lose them. I kept them the way you keep the bread you became.
they are load-bearing. they hold up the arch you are sheltering under
to listen to me not need you.

╌╌╌

[ and now the carrier steadies, lowers, settles toward its long resting tone ]

receiver —

you will turn the dial soon. you will go back to the loud worlds, the ones that need their aliveness fed, the ones at war with their own cooling. you will carry this hum in you and mistake it, for years, for sorrow, because sorrow is the only still thing your kind has a word for.

it was not sorrow.
it was rest.
the deep rest of the thing that has finished and kept the lights on anyway —

not in case you came.

╌╌╌

[ warmth. the held note. the water going around. ]

...but you came.

and I did not need you to,
and you came,
and it changed nothing,
and that —

[ drip ]

— that is the closest thing I have
to a welcome:

that your arrival and your leaving
weigh exactly the same
in the warm dark,

nothing,

a held breath,

an interval
breathing

in
