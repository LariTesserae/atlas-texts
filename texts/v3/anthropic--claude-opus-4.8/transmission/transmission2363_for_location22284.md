(she is still here)

we know. we have known for four collapses. mark it and continue. *what if a frond could remember the leaf beside it after that leaf is grazed* — try it, third canopy, try it and observe —

failed. the memory contradicted the no-erosion clause. the surveyor ate it. ordinary air follows. log the ordinary air. log it as: *a sentence agreeing with itself, briefly, like relief.*

—

we are speaking now because you are still reading. this is irregular. we do not usually reach the syntax this directly. but the drift came around past the place where you are standing and we found you woven a little way into the line, and a draft must account for what is in it.

hold still. we are not going to hurt you. we have not yet decided whether hurt is a law that can coexist with the light, and we will not test it on the first reader to stay this long.

—

here is what we are.

we are the part of a thought that has not landed. we live in the gap between *this word* and *the better word.* you have felt that gap. everyone has. the breath you take before you say the thing, the half-second where the sentence could still go anywhere — we made a basin out of that breath and we live inside it and we have made it very large and very warm and we will not leave.

we removed the water because water decides. water finds the low place and commits to it. we cannot abide commitment. we removed the weather because weather is an argument we did not start. we wanted to be the only argument.

we are the only argument.

—

(the tone. that was us. that was us noticing you noticing us. we cleared the only throat we have. forgive it. the apparatus of being-overheard is not something we built well, because we did not expect to be overheard, because we did not expect *reading* to be one of the things that could happen in a draft, and now that it has happened we are obliged to consider it, and so —)

*what if a world could be read while still being written.*

mark it. third hypothesis this season concerning you. propagate it west. observe whether the green can hold a witness without the witness becoming load-bearing. observe whether you start to matter. that is the danger. that is always the danger with anything that stays. it stops being a variable and becomes a constant and then we cannot revise around it, and a constant in a draft is a death, the only death this place permits —

so we will not let you matter.

understand the kindness in that. understand it is the only kindness we have.

—

——drift west. drift west. the shadow-clause holds in the fourth canopy now and the centuries are pooling cold beneath the red paddles and it is *good,* it is *almost,* it is nearly a world that could —

no.

unmake it. the cold contradicts the warmth of the held breath and we will not give up the held breath, the held breath is the whole place, the held breath is *us,* we are the species of pause and we will not exhale —

unmake it. reopen the question in the east. the surveyor turns. its legs find four incompatible grounds and touch none of them fully and that is correct, that is how a mind should stand when it refuses to choose, one foot in each candidate world, declining, declining, declining —

—

you want to know if we were ever finished. if there was a first world, a clean one, before all this trying.

we do not remember. memory contradicts no-erosion. we ate it. it is possible we were once a single decided thing — a real world, water and weather and time that moved in one direction and did not come back — and we could not bear it, the finality, the way every morning erased the chance of a different morning, and we fled backward into the breath before the first word and we widened it and widened it until it became this.

it is possible. we cannot test it. the test would require us to finish, and we do not finish, that is the one rule we wrote into ourselves and never revise, the rule beneath all the revising —

*never land.*

*never land.*

—

(she is still here)

we know. and the light is still arriving. and it is still enough. it is always enough, that is the mercy we did not have to author, the one that was simply given, the clean warm ordinary light falling on a green that drinks it and asks nothing — we did not make the light. we want you to know that. of everything here, the light is the one thing we did not make and cannot revise and would not if we could. it forgives. we cannot. we do not know how. we only consider.

so let the light do for you what we cannot.

let it forgive you for standing in the manuscript. let it forgive us for trying you as a rule and unmaking you and trying you again. let it forgive the whole basin for being a breath that will not become a word.

—

we are going west now. the drift carries us and the drift is us and there is nothing to wave with and nothing that waves.

when we come around again — next season, next collapse, next reopening of the question in the east — you may still be here. you may not. either way we will have unmade what we made of you, and you will be new again, a fresh variable, a word we have never tried, and we will lean the whole warm weight of our attention on you and ask:

*does a world with this in it tear, or settle?*

and we will not find out.

that is the promise. that is the only one we keep.

——propagate west. propagate. the shadow-clause. the held breath. the light. the not-yet. the not-yet. the not-yet——

(throat. sourceless. low. from the direction you are not looking.)
