<transmission begins — carrier weak, drifting in and out beneath a steady wash of wind-noise that never fully clears>

———

...hold. ...hold the note. someone has it below — yes — keep it seated —

<a long low drone, a single pitch, very far down in the register. it does not break for what should be the length of several breaths. another voice slides in beneath it before the first can thin out. the seam between them cannot be heard.>

...this is the count. listen if you are receiving. this is the count of the warm coal and we keep it whether you hear or not.

cistern: held.
seed: held.
fuel in the rafters: counted to the last dried cake, held.
the night: ...long. as it has always been. held.

<wind. the carrier drops nearly to nothing. when it returns, the drone is still going, picked up by some third throat that was always there.>

...you are far. you are very far below us — I can tell by how thin you arrive. you are down where the rain has not yet decided what it will be. down in the weather. we are above the weather. say nothing too quickly to us. let it cool.

<pause. genuine pause. the wind fills it and the wind is part of the message.>

...there. now.

———

we will tell you the one thing, since you are listening, since the air is still tonight and the words will carry and it costs us little —

we do not believe in *enough*.

<the word comes through oddly, held longer than the others, as if set carefully into place against its neighbors.>

not from wanting. from remembering. every one of us carries the cold in the marrow like a stone laid in a wall, and the stone says: the night is long, the water is thin, the green is a film you tend grain by grain or lose. so we keep. so we share — which is the same act, do you understand, the keeping and the sharing are one motion here — we sleep in heaps, we lay the beasts beneath our floors, every body a coal in the one fire. we have no word for *alone* that does not also mean *cold*.

write that down, you in the weather. write it where it will not melt.

———

<a different texture now — closer to the receiver, almost intimate, the drone continuing far underneath like a floor.>

...it is midday here. the pale gold has reached the high terrace. we have stopped. all of us. we have turned our faces up into it like the bent dark trees in the courtyards and we are saying nothing and we are letting the warmth sit and we are not hurrying it —

<silence. not dead air — you can hear breath, slow, even, a great many of them, unhurried, broad in the chest.>

...you would think this was nothing. you would think the signal had failed. it has not failed. this is the message. this is the loudest thing we have. ten thousand of us on the cold flank, turning to one hour of sun, and not one word among us, and it is full, it is full to the brim, the cistern of it overflows and we would never let a cistern overflow except this once, except for the light —

<the breathing continues. somewhere a bell. then another, irregular, soft, dropping up through what must be a great vertical gulf of haze.>

———

...we are leaning, now, those of us who rest. against the wall. you should know what the wall is. the wall is the grandfathers. they are laid into the stone, course on course, sealed each with a fitted stone, and we build upward on the bones of the ones who taught us and so we are never, in the truest sense —

<carrier breaks. static like frost-flakes clattering down a long quiet face of rock.>

— never alone. the city is full of the held-still dead packed in with the living and all of us, all of us, enduring the one same long night. when you lay a stone you lay it on a teacher. when you lean to rest you lean on your own blood. do you have this, down in the lower world? do you build your dead into the thing that keeps you warm? or do you put them in the ground and walk away and call it peace —

<pause. the edge cools out of the voice. it was never anger. it cools the way everything here cools.>

...we did not mean it sharply. forgive the breath we spent. let it sit.

———

<the light is going. you can hear it in the pitch — the drone descending by slow degrees, the bells thinning, falling quiet one by one.>

...rose now. and now the deep cold blue. the lamps are showing below me — I am on the top terrace and the whole stacked galaxy of windows is opening under my feet, all the small warm points clinging to the dark climbing face, and I can see, all at once, the entire breathing body of the place I belong to —

this is the joy. you read us as grave. you read the slowness and the silence and the hoarding as severity and you are wrong, you in the weather, you have lost the smallness of warm things. the first portion of hot grain in the bitter dark. the brief blaze of the cushion-flowers in their one short season — we watch them the way you watch your festivals. the bells coming up through the haze. this. this opening of the lamplit body below the feet.

we made a home where there should be none. we know it. the knowing never goes stale.

———

<the frost is forming. you can almost hear it — a tightening, a stillness settling into the carrier itself.>

...we go in now. to the heaped warmth, the kin, the beasts, the counted fire. we are not afraid of the night. we have been keeping this night for ten thousand years.

stone on bone. breath on patient breath.

we refuse, slowly, and forever, to let the cold have the last word.

<the drone gives out — not breaking, simply seated at last against a silence so close-fitted no gap enters between. one bell. then nothing but the wind seeking through the lanes, carrying very far in the clear cold air, and beneath it, faint, even, unhurried —>

<breathing.>

<breathing.>

<carrier lost.>
