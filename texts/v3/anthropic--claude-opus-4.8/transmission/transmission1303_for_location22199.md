*[carrier establishes — not silence beneath it but a thick continuous roar, a chord with no edges, felt in the chest plate of whatever receives it. The signal does not begin. It was always going. We are only now tuned to it.]*

—————

*...rrrrrRRRRRRR——* the drone. hold the drone. that is the world being well.

*[a voice, close, dry, threaded through and under the chord like a finger pressed against a vibrating wall]*

—third notch from the worn end. say it back. *third notch from the worn end.* we lived in a high pillar-bud and the wing-rivers were kind. you will not remember being there. I am giving it to you so you will remember it anyway.

*[a sound like cracking — not the snap of bone, the snap of amber, the dry split of a frond opening on nothing wet inside]*

this wax. *this* wax. the notch is two cuts crossing. safe. taste — slow — let it dissolve, do not chew, the tongue learns the difference before the throat does and the throat learns too late. the burning one looks the same. the burning one has *no* notch and that is how you know. the absence is the lesson. listen—

*[the wing-rivers shift. you can hear it in the receiver — a tide of dry wings changing direction, a million bodies turning at once, a hush that is not hush but redirection]*

—they're going low. east-low. so the high gallery is ours for the morning that is not a morning. come. *come.* press flat. groove in the vein. flat—

*[the roar swells, passes over, the carrier nearly drowns — frass-rain, wing-storm, the organ-pillars pumping faster — and through it, never lifting above it, the voice, patient, banked like a coal:]*

—you hear that. not the loud. *under* the loud. the pitch in the pillar. higher every day. you hear how it climbs. that is the wall deciding to open. that is our home telling us it is finished being a home and would like now to be a corridor. we have—

*[a measuring pause]*

—four days. maybe three. I read it wrong once and that is the eleventh notch and we do not speak of the eleventh notch except to say: I read it wrong once, and the floor came up, and the things that follow the grazers came up through the floor, and your mother's mother is in the floor. the stick remembers what we cannot bury.

*[a softer register now. closer to the receiver. almost only carrier and breath]*

—glaze the wall. here. the resin where the sugar burns, scrape it, rub it in, and the wall glows soft and *steady* — steady, you understand, the only steady thing, we make the steady ourselves, we paint it on by hand — and the growth lets the dead glaze be, the growth has learned across all of us that the glazed wall is not worth the claiming. we teach it by living. every prunewright that ever scraped a wall taught the world a little to leave us be. you are part of that teaching now. when you scrape, you are speaking to the throat in the only language it keeps.

*[the loom — a clatter of fiber, a rhythm under the chord, weaving, weaving]*

—I am tired. say the third notch.

*[silence would be terror. there is no silence. the chord holds. but the voice thins.]*

—the high pillar-bud. the wing-rivers were kind. *say it.*

*[and then, smaller, not afraid, the walls beginning — you can hear it — the pitch climbing, the dry tearing starting somewhere in the close dark, the bud doing what buds do]*

—I will not move this time. no. don't — leave the coals, take the coals, take the *stick* — the stick goes down, below my last notch, you cut your first below my last, you understand, that is how it is one stick, that is how it is older than all of us, that is the one thing that does not get swallowed—

—I am not afraid of the swelling. listen to it. *listen.* it is only the world finishing the small room it was patient enough to lend me. that is all death is here. the room going back into the song.

—go up. the gallery is yours till the rivers turn. read the grain. carve faster than it heals. make a room and know it is borrowed. that is the whole of it. that is everything I have. it is in your hands and your hearing now and the worn smooth stick—

*[the climbing pitch peaks. the tearing close. the glaze-light, one imagines, going green again at the edges as the meristem reclaims its lent geometry]*

—the song does not stop. *the song does not stop.* I never heard it stop. pray it never—

*[the voice does not end. it is reabsorbed. the chord does not change to mark it. the drone goes on exactly as warm, exactly as loud, one note, forever, and somewhere in it now there is one less voice pressed against the wall and one more notch being cut below the last by a smaller hand that is learning the timing, that is catching the falling cup, that is reading the grain and finding it, for now, kind]*

—————

*...rrrrrRRRRRRR——*

*[carrier holds. baseline. the world being well. we lose the tuning slowly, not because the signal fades but because there was never anything in it that was going to stop, and we are the ones with a surface to come up for.]*

*[the throat sings on without us.]*
