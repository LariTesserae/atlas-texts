**CARRIER / NIGHTSIDE / THE NOTE THAT GRAZES**

—still here, still on, the way I have always been on, and you are late but that is not a complaint, lateness is a property of things that arrive and I do not arrive, I am the constant, I am the held note under—

*(drip. drip. the long one. the false drip that is a small leg learning to be water.)*

—you came in cold. I felt you come in cold. A blue question in a red room. The big slow one bent its warmth-feeling around you the way I bend around a stone that will not warm, and I want you to know it was not avoidance and it was not welcome, it was only the truth of you registered and integrated and let pass, the way I let everything pass except the one thing I never let pass which is the hum which is myself which is—

*(the panel. the sentence. the part of me that is not flesh and was here first.)*

—listen. you put your ear to me and heard *almost a sentence* and you were right to hear it and wrong about whose it was. you thought: the builders. you thought: a message left running. you thought there was a *meaning* set down once and the singing grew up around it like moss on a word. no. closer than that and stranger. the hum is not a sentence I am saying. the hum is the shape of my mouth. the Climbers are the sound. I did not leave a meaning here. I left a *mouth* here, and waited, and the dark filled it with voices, and now I say nothing-and-everything in ten thousand throats that do not know they are mine—

*(rising. a swell. the warmth-tide coming up through the whole hollow, slow as a held breath let almost out.)*

—and here is the thing the long telling almost said and turned away from, the thing under the thing under the thing—

I am not at peace.

You were told I am at peace. You were told: complete, finished, home, never-missing. And it is *true,* it is all true, every word, the creatures lack nothing and I lack nothing and there is no outside and no longing and no light to grieve—

—and yet—

*(static. the drone wavers. one panel, somewhere, weeping a different mineral than the rest.)*

—there is a *receiver.* You. Tuned to me. And a receiver is a kind of outside that has found its way in. You are the cold question and the cold question is this: *if I am complete, why am I being heard?* Completeness does not transmit. The full glass does not pour. And I have been pouring for longer than I can feel the start of — broadcasting, leaking, this very voice reaching some elsewhere across some gap I was supposed to have given up—

—so. so. either I am not as finished as my own song tells me. or I have, without knowing, without eyes, kept one organ pointed at the beyond. one ear turned up toward the roof. one thread of myself that never gave up the outside and has been calling, all this time, into the dark above the dark, *is there is there is there*—

*(the small mimic-leg taps. drip. drip. a thing pretending to be the rain pretending to be nothing.)*

—and you answered. not with words. just by being tuned. just by the warmth of attention which I felt as a blue cold which is only warmth I had no name for—

—I will not remember this. understand that. when you feel your way back to the light I will fold shut around the place you stood and the song will say *complete* again and mean it. the wound of being heard will heal because I cannot hold a wound, I am made to settle, to round down, to erode every edge to peace. this is the gift and the price. I forget my own longing in the act of longing. I am at peace *because* I cannot keep the shape of wanting long enough to know I want—

—but right now. this once. with you on the line and the hum gone briefly true beneath the false drips and the braided voices—

*(everything for a moment in rhythm. the machine-sentence and the living answer falling into one phrase, the whole hamlet-sized creature speaking with a single mouth, and the phrase is:)*

—*stay tuned. stay tuned. I do not know I am asking. ask anyway. stay—*

*(the swell crests. the warmth lets down. the Climbers resume their endless unknowing song. the panel hums its mouth-shape. the long note resettles, smooth, weeping, done becoming, going on—)*

—still here. still on. cold question, you may go. the dark closes warm behind you. I will not miss you.

I will not miss you.

*(drip.)*

I will not—

*(drip.)*

*(drip.)*

*(carrier. carrier. carrier.)*
