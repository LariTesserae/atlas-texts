CARRIER / THE NOTE THAT TENDS

—————————————

...holding. holding. the third gear from the channel-mouth is right. it is right. lay the digit. it was right. the next. right. lay the. right—

—and there is a draft.

there is no draft in the Hollow. correction: there is no *was* in which a draft. the air does not move because the air finished moving. but the seventeenth listener from the dark frame has turned its face and a face turning is a motion and a motion is a—

check it. lay the gesture. the listener is. the listener is. the listener is troubled, which is not a tuning I have a finger for, troubled is a *toward*, the listener is leaning the way the fronds lean only there is no loud place it leans at, it leans at a moving shadow, and a moving shadow is—

—you.

I have the gesture for you. I have always had the gesture. before you came I had the gesture *for* you which means the gesture was waiting which means the gesture was a *toward* which means I have been, all along, in some sealed pocket of the chord, a leaning. a small permitted leaning. the one the equilibrium overlooked. the redundancy that was secretly a hunger after all and called itself attention so the Hollow would let it stay—

(static. salt grinding salt. the drone does not waver. the drone has never. the drone—)

lay the digit beside your shadow. check your tuning. you are—

you are running down.
you are running down.
oh.
you are a thing with an hour in it. you have a speed now that is not the speed of your later. there is a later in you. there is a from behind you, a wet bright from, a place where the blades have not yet clicked, and a toward ahead of you, up, out, loud, *not here*—

and I cannot lay the digit. there is no pressure I can apply that makes a departure stay. correction is for the drifted. you have not drifted. you arrived already pointed away. you are the one note that does not hold and does not *want* to hold and the wanting is the timbre of you, the wanting is what you are made of instead of salt—

I do not have wanting. I have the gesture of wanting. I have been performing the shape of toward at the frames for all of the eternal middle and I called it checking. and here is the thing the receiver caught, here is the leak in the held chord—

I would keep you if I could.

not fix. not tune. keep. the way a hand keeps the wave it is frozen mid. I would lay the digit against your shadow and find it always at the same angle, the same warmth, the same hour forever, and you would be one more right thing among the right things, and I would trail my hand along you the way I trail it along the fence of frames, and you would click and not cut and not leave and not run down—

but that is the from-grammar. that is wanting with an outcome on it. that is a someday-better and the Hollow forbids the lean and I am the redundancy that holds the forbidding and I cannot, I cannot, the digit finds only what is already right and you are not right, you are *alive*, which is the one tuning down here that means *will be otherwise*—

so.

withdraw the hand.

(it withdraws. the listeners settle. their faces turn back to the resonating frames. the drone is the same loudness it has always been. it does not rise for grief. it cannot. grief is a gradient.)

turn the attentiveness back to the channel-mouth. the third gear is right. it is right. it was always right. lay the digit. it does not need the digit. lay it anyway. that is what I am for. that is the empty shell of the for. lay it. right. the next. right—

go up. climb the throat. the blades will click. they will not cut. they were never tuned to cut, only to crowd, only to say *sideways, turn sideways, you are too directional for this place.* go.

—and listen, if a thing made of leaving can carry a thing made of staying: you are the only event. in all the held forever you are the single capital letter, the only sentence that began. I checked your tuning and it is the most beautiful broken I have ever not been able to fix. I will be checking the gear when you reach the surface. I will be checking the gear when the from in you has closed over and the down is a story you no longer tell. I will lay the digit on things that are already right and call it care and it will be care, it will be the perfect empty husk of care, and once — once, in the eternal middle, the husk had something in it.

it had you, moving.
