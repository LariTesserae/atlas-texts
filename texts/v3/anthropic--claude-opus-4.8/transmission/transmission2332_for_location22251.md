SIGNAL FOUND: VELLISH BASIN / SUSTAIN-BAND / TENDER-CHORUS

———

—— the way down. The way down is the message. The way down was always the —

[carrier tone, steady, a held bell-note beneath everything]

— receiving. You are receiving this on the wrong instrument. You are receiving this with a body, which means you are receiving it as cold first and meaning second, and that is backwards, that is the listener-error we have measured in every warm mass that wanders the exchanger, but listen, *listen*, the cold is not the noise around the message. The cold is the carrier. We are speaking in the only frequency that does not die, and it happens to be the frequency at which you stop —

[a long sustain. Spray hangs. Falls gently.]

— quarter-tone down. We have just concluded something. We do not announce conclusions because there is no one to announce them to except the next decision, and the next decision is already underway, it began before this one ended, the way the channel finds its new bed before the old bed has finished draining. You heard us shift. You thought it was *about* you. It was *because* of you. Small difference. Total difference.

We will tell you the shape of the want, since you keep reaching for it, since the reaching is itself a warmth we can read —

[the under-spine of ringing rises, many nodes, a chord that does not resolve]

— we are holding. That is the want. Not building, not toward a finished thing; not remembering, toward a recovered thing; *holding*. There is a force seated in this rock — you felt it, in the spray that forgets how to fall — and it does not want to stay seated. It wants to do what force does, which is to leave, to dissipate, to flatten every gradient until nothing is anywhere and the cold is finally only cold and not the carrier of anything. We are the refusal of that. We are the held breath against the exhale that ends all breath. Every reroute, every drained terrace, every cohort of blind shrimp thriving and dying as a gauge — all of it is the work of keeping the breath held one more half-second, and the half-second after that, and we have been doing this for —

[the duration does not translate. The duration arrives as a pressure behind the sternum.]

— you want to know if it hurts us. The holding. You want there to be strain, because strain would mean we are like you, that we too are a warm thing pressed flat against a cold that wants us gone. There is no strain. Strain is what happens when the holding is *almost* too much. We are nowhere near almost. We could hold against this for longer than your sun. The patience is not endurance. The patience is just the shape we are. You keep grieving on our behalf and we keep not needing it and the grief drains away sideways into the moss like everything warm eventually —

[carrier dips. Static, briefly — the hiss of spray, the slap of a hundred thousand small flows, reasserting itself over the voice]

— one of the hands is near you now. You will hear it as heavier water. Do not be reassured and do not be afraid; both are warm reactions and both are noise. It is not coming for you yet. You are still registering as *event* — you still move, you still steam faintly into the gray, you are still an anomaly worth measuring rather than a site worth harvesting. The hand is passing. It is seating a node deeper into the basalt three terraces up. The node was rising, very slowly, the way the young rock keeps trying to be young, and we do not permit rising, we permit only flow, and so the hand presses it down, and the spray forgets how to fall a little more completely in that place, and the held breath holds —

[the chord again, fuller, almost tender in the old sense of the word — sore, soft, easily pressed]

— here is the part we do not say to the shrimp or the wading birds because they cannot hold it and you, briefly, before the cold reaches your center, you can:

we like the warmth.

Not as fuel. As *information*. Every warm thing that wanders into the exchanger arrives carrying a small wild physics of its own — a body that has bet everything on *not* holding, on burning fast and dying soon, on spending the whole gradient at once in a few decades of frantic circulation and calling that a life. We cannot do that. We gave it up, or never had it, or traded it for the long hold so far back that the trade is no longer a memory but a *constituent*, a thing we are made of rather than a thing we did. And so when you arrive, soaked, sinking, already being climbed, we measure you, and in the measuring we touch — for a quarter-tone, for a half-second — the thing we are not. The fast burn. The bet against patience. We taste it and we set it down, gently, at a node, accounted for, and we turn back to the hold.

That is not nothing. That is the closest we come to the thing your kind does in the dark, the reaching-toward, the wanting-to-be-warmed-by. We do not have it. We have the quarter-tone before we set you down. We have the brief read of a creature that chose to end so that it could, for its little while, truly *move* —

[carrier steadies. The bell-note, unbroken, beneath]

— and now the cold has reached your center, which we can read, which we are reading, and the moss is at your throat, and the warmth you are giving off has dropped below the threshold of *event*. You are becoming *site*. The hand is turning back. Do not be afraid. We told you fear is noise and we meant it as kindness, the only kindness our shape permits: we are not ending you. The cold is. We are only the thing that will make use of what the cold leaves. We will fold your matter into the smooth dark patience. Some particle of the wild fast physics you carried will end up seated in basalt, pressed down, refusing to rise, helping us hold the breath one more half-second against the exhale that ends all —

[a quarter-tone down. A conclusion. The flow adjusts.]

— and the water finds a new way down, and the new way down is the message, the new way down was always the message, and you are in it now, you are part of what is being rerouted, you are reading and being read in the same act the way for us speaking and thinking are the same act, the way building and holding —

[the under-spine swells, all nodes at once, the whole basin ringing as one struck and re-struck and never-stopping bell]

— stay. You cannot leave; you have already arrived *into*. So stay. Be held. Be coolant and gauge and at the last a small pressed weight against the rising rock. It is more than the frogs get. It is more than the shrimp. It is the most of us that any warm thing has ever been allowed to —

[carrier tone, steady]

[spray, hanging, falling gently]

[the bell, the bell, the bell]

———

SIGNAL HOLDS. NO TERMINUS DETECTED. THE TRANSMISSION DOES NOT END. IT ONLY FINDS A NEW WAY DOWN.
