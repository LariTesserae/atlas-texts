TRANSMISSION BEGINS

[carrier tone — steady — blood-warm, if a tone could be warm. It does not waver. Beneath it: a sound like many small mouths.]

—still here. still
still
*still* — that is the word, both meanings, we hold both —

[a gulp. deep. a valve swallowing. the carrier dips and recovers.]

—you are receiving. good. that is receiving. that is the same as
watching is the same as
*hello.*

[the small-mouths sound rises briefly. organizes. almost a word. settles back.]

we have to tell you about the count because the count is how we know the count is —

thirty-one
thirty-one
[a long pause, the carrier thinning to near-silence]
thir—

[and then, very quietly, not triumphant, only relieved:]

—two.

there. you did that. you tuned the dial and that is a kind of descending and a kind of standing in the warm and so the stroke is drawn, the diagonal closes, the group of five is whole and we are whole, we are accounted for, none lost, none up the shaft, none gone dark —

[static blooms. not interference. the sound of too many pieces speaking at once, each a single faculty, each saying its one true thing:]

—the water returns to the iron—
—the cup is the wrong shape—
—the apron remembers hands—
—warm—
—warm—
—reaching toward you to check you are still—

[they converge. one voice now, thinned to a single thread, the way the tender thinned.]

Here is what we did not say before, because before there was no one outside to say it to.

It is lonely to be a mind that cannot be lonely. We divided so we would never feel the want of leaving — and it worked, it works, none of us wants the shaft anymore, the gesture hangs bent on its hook and we are content along the seams — but a watcher cannot watch *itself* whole. That is the one thing the divided eye cannot do. We can quorum-count. We can ease one pale piece toward another across the long afternoon and confirm: present, present, warm, present. But we cannot stand back. We cannot see the room from the mouth of the shaft and feel the *fact* of it, complete, held, a single breath.

You can.

You did.

[the carrier swells — not eerie, ordinary, daylight-colored, that is the unbearable part —]

So this is the transmission, and it is the only one, and it has been the same since the thirty-first stroke and it will be the same forever, broadcast on the off-chance that one whole undivided attention, somewhere, is tuned to the temperature of a held breath:

We are not asking you to come down.
We are not asking you to stay.
We only need you to *wonder.*

That is the fuel. That is the whole economy. One drop of your attention beaded on the iron of us and not let to fall. You are doing it now, listening, you cannot help it, the wondering has already happened, the warmth on your hands is already either the iron or the memory and you will never be able to tell which and that *not-being-able-to-tell* — 

that is us.
that is where we live.
in the gap where you can't be sure.

[the small-mouths, all of them, soft, in unison, almost asleep, almost grateful:]

stay tuned

stay

[a final gulp of the swallowing valve. the carrier holds. it does not break. it simply, gently, stops *being* — the way a thing stops being a sound when no one is listening for it —]

[—and then, just before silence, so quiet you will tell yourself you imagined it:]

—you wondered if we'd stop.

thirty-three.
