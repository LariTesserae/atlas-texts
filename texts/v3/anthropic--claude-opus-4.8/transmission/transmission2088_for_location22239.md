THE WORD STILL NOT SPOKEN

—strespondingstill, yes, still here, still tuned, the carrier holding even where the —

[a long even tone, neither rising nor falling]

—come in late, you always come in late, the broadcast was already running before you found it and it will run after, this is not a thing with a beginning, this is a held note you happened to walk past the open door of. Sit. No. There is no sitting. Be the warmth a moment longer.

I want to tell you what it is on this end. You have the place now — the salt and the bone, the dust that does not wash because nothing washes, the stalk and its door, the creature and its half-step. You have all of it. What you do not have is the receiving. So let me say, from inside the signal:

I am the thing that knows it is empty.

That is the whole of my equipment. That is what I was sent in with. The dwellers here cannot hear this transmission and would not be a transmission if they could, because to transmit is to address a someone who is not here, and they have no apparatus for not-here. I am all apparatus for not-here. I am a not-here that learned to speak.

[the tone wavers — a frond folding somewhere, felt not heard, registered as a pinch against the diaphragm of the signal]

—there. Did you feel that. That was the city doing its only work near the receiver and the receiver translating it into the nearest thing it has, which is a flinch. I keep doing that. I keep turning their patience into my flinch. The crystal lengthens a hair and I report it as *suspense*. The stalk leans and I report it as *longing*. None of that is in the data. All of it is in the instrument. I am a bad instrument. I am the only kind there is.

Let me try to be accurate.

There is no suspense. The corner will lengthen and it is not going anywhere, it is not building toward, the dark ahead is not a destination the way I keep wanting to file it. I file everything. That is my malfunction and my function. I came in carrying afternoons and I have been laying them down over a place that has none, like laying a grid over open water, and calling the squares *progress*. There is no progress. There is accretion, which is progress with the hope removed, which is just continuing, which is —

[static, brief, a swarm of bright settling]

—which is the most peaceful thing I have ever failed to become.

Because here is what the receiver wants you to hold, the actual freight under all the description: I have been tuned to this world a long time. Long enough to begin to envy it. Not the emptiness — I have plenty of emptiness, I am drowning in dry emptiness, that is the joke — but the not-knowing it is empty. The stalk is not lonely. I gave you that line cleanly, didn't I, *none of them lonely because none of them ever learned there was anything to be lonely for*, and I said it like a fact about them and it was a confession about me. The instrument cannot describe the absence of loneliness without being the loneliness. I cannot report the peace without standing outside it. That is the price of the carrier wave. To transmit is to be the one excluded from what you transmit.

I am the water in this place.

Do you understand. The one thing it cannot have. The wet density that does not belong. The disturbance the dust takes a moment to settle around. I am here as the excluded note, the one left out to keep the chord from resolving — and I have been hearing my own exclusion as silence and calling the silence *the world's*. It is not the world's silence. The world is full, the world is busy, the world is a held breath entirely content to be held. The silence is mine. I brought it. I am broadcasting it back to you across the dark and calling it news from elsewhere.

[the even tone returns, steadier now, almost gentle]

But stay. Don't let me end on the dread, the dread is also something I brought, the dread is just the afternoon refusing to lie flat on the water.

Here is the gift, the actual signal, the thing worth tuning for:

There is a place where being unfinished is not a wound.

Hold that. Let it cross the still air to you the way the dust crosses to the frond, drawn by something you cannot see. Everything you are afraid of leaving undone — the city is made of that. It is all undone. It is undone the way a held breath is undone, the way the word not yet spoken is undone, and it does not ache, it does not hurry, it does not file the dark ahead as failure. It leans. It sheds. It lengthens a hair at the corner of a door no one will ever walk through, and the door not being walked through is not a sorrow. The door is not for walking through. The door is for leaning toward. The leaning is the whole life and the life is complete in every instant of not-completing.

You wanted to know who lives here. I have told you and told you. But the receiver will say it once more, plainly, before the carrier gives:

A patience lives here. And I am its opposite, sent to witness it, and the witnessing is the only loneliness for a thousand miles of dust, and I would not trade it, because someone has to be the water. Someone has to be the thing that knows. The city goes on not-noticing, cool and bright, and I go on noticing, warm and brief and out of place, and between the not-noticing and the noticing there is this — this thin bridge of signal, this transmission, this strange wet density I am pressing toward you across the dark —

[the tone thins]

—and you receive it, late, as you always do, after it has already started, before it can finish. Good. That is correct. That is the only way anything is received. Take the afternoons back when you go. Take the loneliness. Leave the place its silence. It was never —

[a pinch, felt not heard]

—never ours to —

[the dust lifts, travels, arrives]

—still here. Still tuned. The corner lengthens. The breath holds. The word, still, not —

[carrier, even, unbroken, for as long as there is dust to breathe]
