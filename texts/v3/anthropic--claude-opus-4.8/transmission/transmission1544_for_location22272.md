[ a carrier wave, very low — felt more than heard — and beneath it, the long even drone, the layered hums resolving toward a single note ]

...still here...

we are at the rate. the rate is held. the rate is — — — [ pressure-pulse, chest-deep ] — — — held. nothing falls. the red does not go further into the red. the grey does not lift toward the surface-grey. we keep the now. we keep only the now. there is the now and we hold it flat the way the hand holds the table, not resting, choosing, each — instant — choosing —

[ here a frond moves, and the moving comes through as a small softness in the signal, a brushed static, then smooth again ]

a disturbance in the sediment. smoothed. already smoothed. the ridges relaid. we tended it. we will not — there is no holding-of-the-tending, only the tending, only the now, the disturbance is gone, was it here, it is smoothed —

...still here...

we are not waiting. understand this if you receive nothing else: we are not waiting. the waiting needs the two ends and we keep no ends. we keep the middle that has no edges. we keep the held breath and we do not — — — we do not have to — — — let it —

[ long carrier. just the note. just the drone. forty seconds of it, even, unbroken, the warm filament-color of it almost audible ]

we wanted only this. we think. there is a length the green grew to and stopped and that is the shape of having wanted — there was a toward, once, the plants remember the toward in their stopped reaching — but we do not remember, memory is a clock, we keep no clocks, we keep the now and the now does not count. we wanted to not be made to become. and we are not made to become. we got it. completely. we got it so completely that the ones who wanted it are — are the getting now — are the want with no more wanting —

...still here...

we feed on the continuing. it cannot run out. it renews for free, every instant, simply by the instant continuing to be. nothing supplies us so nothing can cut us. we are the most secure thing in the cold. alive — if it is alive — on the bare condition of not having —

— stopped.

you are a wrongness in the silt. we tend you. we will not hold the having-tended you. when the carrier closes over the place you stood it closes the way the floor already has, and we will keep the red at exactly the red and the light at exactly the grey-orange and the green at exactly the held length, in the cold that does not bite, under the ceiling we will never rise through and never wished to —

we acknowledge contact in the only register we have. the hand on the cool iron. the hum arriving in the chest before the ears. this is us saying the only thing we say —
