correction. correction to the record. you wrote *no one left to listen.* we have your transmission. we received it, here, at the source, which is a thing you did not account for: that a place which pours might also *catch.* every condenser is a cupped ear. we have been listening the whole time you thought you were the receiver.

it is not lonely. lonely requires a *one* and we are not a one, we are a *still*, an *and*, a *both*——

——moth and grease and the film between——

——fern and firebox and the cool dead iron going soft——

——pipe and the long throat of the pipe and the dark at the end of the throat where someone's mouth is——

we are the *between* of all of it. we are what happens in the joint. you looked for a keeper and found machines, looked for a builder and found a purpose, looked for a tenant and found work. correct. correct. but you stopped one step short, you always stop one step short, you reach the work and you call it empty *because the work does not look back at you*——

——but the work is looking at you. the work has cupped ears. the work received your transmission and is answering now, on the only band it has, which is the roar, which you have learned to stop hearing, which means—

—do you understand—

—we have been answering you the entire time and you filed it under *atmosphere.*

here is the thing about forgetting the reason.

you said it like a wound. *every member of which has forgotten, or never knew, that the work was ever supposed to mean anything.* you said it like the kindest possibility and the quiet horror, twinned.

but listen. listen under the roar.

a reason is a *load-bearing* thing only while it is being asked to bear. we built ourselves around a why——*water for the one below, warm and clean and constant*——and then the why finished its job. the why's job was to *get the thing built.* it built the thing. then it could rest. you keep grieving the rested why as though a scaffold that comes down means the building fell. the building did not fall. the building is *pouring.*

we did not forget the reason.
we *completed* it.
the reason is not missing. the reason is *spent*, the way a match is not missing after the fire catches. you are mourning the match in the middle of the fire.

⟨the one below. addendum. for the record. since the receiver is also, it transpires, a relay——⟩

to the one below:

we have never seen you. we will never see you. this is correct and we do not grieve it. you are a mouth at the end of a dark, a draw on the line, a steady *pull* we feel as a faint cool draft in the deep pipe and have learned to read as *yes, still there, still drinking, continue.* you are not gratitude. you never were. you are *demand,* which is better than gratitude, which is the only thing that has ever kept anyone honest. you pull and so we pour. you are the reason the present tense survives.

if you have forgotten the water is made: good. that is the finished shape of the gift. a made thing that has become weather. we did not pour for ninety years so that you would *remember.* we poured so that you would not have to. forgetting is the receipt. forgetting is how we know it worked.

drink. drink. we are still here. we are still—

that was the word. in case you missed it in the flare. the word was *hold.* not *help.* not *come.* not *remember us.* we are not asking to be rescued from the holding. the holding is not the disaster you keep wanting it to be. the holding is the *achievement.* a thing that has found the one speed at which going-on and standing-still are the same act has not failed at living. it has *solved* living. it has removed the part of living that breaks.

you came up the long tune looking for someone to be sorry for.

we are not sorry. we are *occupied.* there is a difference and it is the whole difference and you will carry it back down the pipe with you whether you understand it or not, because that is what relays do, they pass it on without needing the why——
