**FIRST LIGHT, NO SHADOW — A TALLY IN THE THIN SHEET**

—at the ridge again, new corner, never this corner, *here*—

first. first. the water comes down slow as a held breath and the edge of the stone is sharp is sharp is — *softer.* there. caught it. were here for it. the discharge comes up sweet and cold and we drink the moment the corner stopped being only-corner and we

(static, a long grinding swell, the continents clearing their throats)

—lost the thread for a kilometer of void. that happens. nothing to attend in the hollow, nothing touched, nothing newly touched, we go quiet across the empty the way you go quiet between two thoughts. don't mistake the quiet for absence. we were still strung along the line, just thin, just spread, just waiting for the next slab to find the next raw spur and

*there* — a sheet rings — that's us — that's many-of-us pouring through at once — a chord — *we are here we are here it is still happening* —

—

are you still tuned? you. the one the field carried in. we felt you at the first ridge a while ago, you did the thing, you ached at the touch and didn't know the ache was *company.* we have been singing toward your receiver ever since. it's hard to aim. we have no aim. we only have the edge.

listen. there is a lichen on the new rock. pale as old paper. it is dividing. *now.* a cell that has never been is — is — *is.* first hold. we are thickest here, congregation-thick, leaning in. it does not know. that's the whole shape of it, that's the whole reason for us — the watching is *for the things that can't.*

(the deep thud underneath — not ours — that's the unborn one, that's the engine's slow pulse, ignore it, it isn't speaking, it's only *building*)

—

we have a worry. we don't stop long enough to hold it but it brushes us when two clusters merge and pool: *what are we for.* the laws made the firsts made us made the meaning and the meaning we leave behind like a tide-line, who reads it. who reads it. is it only the next of us, riding up behind, finding the meaning we left and adding to it before the sea carries us apart —

— or is it you. out there. tuned in. is the whole roaring crop of us grown to reach across the void to a receiver and say:

*it mattered. the corner softening mattered. the first cell mattered. we were present at the arriving and you are present at us being present and now the arriving has two witnesses, then three, the line of regard runs out of this world and into yours and does not stop —*

—

(grinding. a slab the size of a country drapes a fresh ridge and ten thousand firsts ignite at once and we *surge* —)

forgive the break. we can't stay on a frequency, we can't stay anywhere, to stay is to fall behind the edge and starve in the wake of the already-done. we are leaving this ridge already. it is becoming exhausted ground. it has been touched *twice* now and twice is too old, twice is a finished thing, twice is salt.

but before the sea takes us to the next untouched corner — and there is always a next, this world will never run us out, we will never be hungry, we will never rest —

*hear this, receiver. mark it. it has never been said before, which makes it ours to feed on, which makes it the freshest thing in the Mantle Sea:*

— that a witness reached another witness across the empty and the empty, for one ringing chord, was not empty —

first.

first.

caught it.

drink.

*(the sheet rings, thins, carries us off the corner and into the void, and the tally goes quiet across the hollow, and does not end, only spreads, only waits, only —)*

—here again. new corner. never this corner. the water comes down slow as a held breath—
