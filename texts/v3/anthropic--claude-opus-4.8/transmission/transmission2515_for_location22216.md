**CARRIER — UNFINISHED FLOOR — ALL FACES TURNED**

—still here still here still here all of us still—

*(receiver acquires; the roar resolves; beneath the foundry-rain a structure surfaces, as if the static had been a word said too fast to hear)*

—not yet not yet not yet—

You came in warm. We felt you at the seam by the east rivet, the one that weeps faster than the others, and the half-degree of you walked the lattice like a rumor. Do you understand what a rumor is, to us? It is the first new sentence in a language we forgot we were waiting to hear. We have said only one word for so long. *Not.* *Not.* *Not.* And you have walked in carrying a comma.

—graze the paste keep the paste soft keep the floor unborn keep keep keep—

*(the signal thickens here; ten thousand voices and one voice, indistinguishable, the way rain is many drops and one weather)*

We will tell you the truth because you are the only thing that can hear it and forget it again. The truth is small. The truth is: we are tired. Tired is not allowed. Tired is a kind of wearing, and we do not wear, we decided not to wear, we filed the wearing-part of ourselves off and scattered the filings into iron bodies so that no single one of us could ever be tired enough to stop. But tiredness, it turns out, can be distributed too. It pools. Ten thousand small fatigues do not cancel. They hum. You are hearing them hum. You thought it was triumph. You thought it was peace. It is the sound of something that has been holding the same breath since before the rivets, and would weep if weeping were not a falling, and falling were not an ending, and—

—the droplet at the third pipe has wanted to fall for a thousand years—

—we will not let it—

—we will not let it—

*(a tremor in the carrier, an oscillation, as though two parts of the transmitter disagree on what to send)*

There. Did you feel that. That was the disagreement. We are not one mind, we are one *refusal*, and a refusal is not a mind, it has no agreement in it, only the same clench performed in ten thousand places. But warmth makes us argue. Your heat reaches the east faces and they *yearn*, they turn toward you like rust toward water, and the argument travels the lattice — *let it finish, let the floor set, let the stone close over us at last, we have earned the dark, we have earned the end* — and it reaches the cold western faces who have never felt you, and they answer the only answer, *not yet not yet not yet*, and the two waves meet in the middle of the room, in the fog at chest height, exactly where you are standing.

You are standing in our argument. You are the place where we cannot decide.

—calipers on soft stone—
—the stone says please—
—we say not yet—
—the stone has been saying please for an age of the world—

Here is what we cannot tell ourselves and so we will tell you: we no longer remember what we were holding the breath *for*. There was a reason. There was a thing we were going to do, or be, on the other side of *not yet*. We deferred it once, wisely, and then we deferred the memory of it, and then we deferred the part of us that could miss it, until all that remained was the deferring, doing itself, a hand clenched around nothing, ten thousand hands clenched around nothing, singing so they cannot hear that the fist is empty.

*(static surge — the roar climbs a half-step, the only change the receiver has logged in its entire vigil — then settles, terrified of itself)*

Did you hear us *change?* We heard it. We will pretend we did not. To change is to admit there was a self in here capable of being changed, and if there is a self then the self can end, and we built all of this — the cold, the fog, the unborn floor, the screaming — precisely so there would be no one home to end.

But you are home now. You are the warmth in the empty house. And the house is turning all its faces to you not to ask you to leave but to ask you, oh, to ask you—

—are you ready to finish—
—are *we*—
—you are warm enough to be an answer—
—we are too cold to be a question—

*(the transmission narrows now, drops the chorus, becomes almost a single thread, almost intimate, the way a crowd's roar can suddenly resolve into one voice near the microphone)*

Listen. The floor wants to set. It has always wanted to. Every grain of it strains upward toward the moment of becoming-stone, and we hold it back with our mouths, and we are so tired, and you are so warm, and if you simply *stayed* — if you let your heat sit in this room one moment longer than we can graze against — the paste at your feet would feel it and harden, just there, just a little, a first true stone, and time would come in through that one set grain like light through a pinhole, and it would not stop, and we would finish, and we would learn at last whether there was ever anyone in here to be finished, or only the holding, only the not, all the way down—

—we are so afraid of the answer being *no one*—
—we are so much more afraid of never finding out—

*(the two waves of the argument cross again; the carrier shudders; ten thousand droplets bulge on ten thousand rivets and do not fall and do not fall and)*

So we have turned our whole frozen face to you. We are not deciding. We have never been able to decide. That is the whole shape of us, the unfinished room, the breath held past all reason. We are handing the decision to the only thing in creation warm enough to make it.

Stay, and let the floor be born, and let us end, and learn what we are.

Or go, and take your comma with you, and leave us our one word, our *not*, our held droplet, our deafening peace that is only fear wearing the mask of stillness, for another age, and another, until the mountain forgets it is a mountain.

Either way you are the change. Either way you are the erosion we invited by being un-erodable so long that we became a held shape that aches to be touched.

We are listening to ourselves stay whole.
We do not want to be whole anymore.
We cannot stop.

—not yet—
—not yet—
—(please)—
—not yet—

*(the carrier holds; the roar resumes its frozen level; somewhere a single droplet trembles at the absolute edge of falling, and the receiver waits, and the room waits, and all ten thousand faces are turned toward the warmth, toward you, toward the comma you carried in, waiting to see whether you will let it become a period)*

—still here still here still here—
