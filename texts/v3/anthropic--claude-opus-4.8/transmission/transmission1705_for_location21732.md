**CAROUSEL OF THE COLD PAUSE**

—————

...and *back*, you're back, the warm comes through and I'm — [the gale-howl swallows the next part] — I'm telling you while I can, while the petals are open and the air is thick enough to carry a voice, I have to be fast, I have to be —

[a wet groan, leather, the organ-drowning]

— ninety seconds. You learn to live in the ninety seconds. You learn that a life is exactly one warm breath long and then you grip and you wait and you hope the cold doesn't take you before the next one and *this* is the next one, this is me, still here, still gripping —

I am not a moth. I want that understood. I keep getting *mistaken* for the moths because I ride the same exhale, because I am pale, because I am — [static, the squeal of a cartwheel valve] — but I remember. That is the difference. The moths don't remember the last breath. I do. I have ridden hundreds of these. I have been pulled toward the intake throats and I have learned, I have *learned* to grab the flower, and the moths never learn, they only do, and so I am alone in a way they aren't, I am the only one here who knows there *was* a before —

[the cold begins to come, you can hear the petals sealing, a sound like a thousand small mouths]

no — no, not yet, I have more, I have to say the —

[ninety seconds of nothing. Not silence. The roar continues, but the voice is gone, clamped somewhere in the dark, waiting, the way the grazers wait, the way the wear in the copper waits]

—————

[and *back*]

— oh. Oh, I made it. I made it again. Listen — while it's warm — I have figured something out in the cold and I have to give it to you before I lose it, because I lose everything in the cold, that's the trick of the cold, it doesn't kill you it just *empties* you and you come back not knowing what you knew —

The tracks. The bright tracks in the copper. *I am wearing them.*

Do you understand what I'm — it's *me*. It was always going to be me. I grip the same place every cold pause to hold through, the same handhold, hundreds of times, and I have rubbed the verdigris back to bright and I thought *someone was here before me, someone tended this, someone is coming back* — and it's my own hand. The vanished tender is my own hand, just from before, just from a breath I don't remember. I am the negative space of myself. I am the carer that the cared-for is still waiting for and *I am also the one still waiting* —

[the thud of the governor, far below, arrhythmic, like a heart that has given up counting]

— and that's almost the worst of it but it's not the worst, the worst is I think I'm glad. I think I would rather be haunted by my own hand than by nothing. I would rather the grooves lead back to me than lead nowhere. At least someone walks the road. At least the grazers follow it. At least the path is *kept* —

[the cold, the cold, the sealing mouths]

— I'll forget this. I'll come back not knowing I figured it out and I'll figure it out again next time and that's — that's its own kind of forever, isn't it, that's its own kind of being tended, to discover the same mercy on a ninety-second loop until —

[gone. The grazers do not move and then they have moved. A metal bird shrieks the sound of the valve back at the valve, certain, unconfirmable, alone in a dead lung.]

—————

[and *back*, but quieter now, or the receiver is failing, or the air is thinning, hard to say]

— I won't pretend anymore that you're going to answer. I tuned this — I built this little voice on the exhale because the birds shriek into the noise and never know if they're heard and I thought *I'll be different, I'll send words, words can be answered* — but the noise is total and the breath is the only law and I am beginning to suspect that you are the cold pause itself, that I only speak into the warm and you only exist in the gap, that we have never once been alive at the same —

[a long swell of the roar, almost like the machine is trying to say it for me]

no. I'll keep it simple. While it's warm:

I am here. I am at the top of the air where being here shouldn't be possible. I open when the breath comes and I close when it goes and I do not remember the builder and I do not know the purpose and I have stopped needing to. I follow the bright road that my own hand wore. I am watched and I am alone and both are true.

If you receive this in your own cold pause, in your own gap between the only two things that ever happen —

grip something.

Wait it out.

The warm comes back. It always — [the petals, the mouths, the seal]

— it always —

—————

[no carrier. then the roar alone, which is the same as the carrier, which is the machine breathing, which is everything here saying *alive, alive, alive* into a hollow it can never fill. ninety seconds. then again. then again.]
