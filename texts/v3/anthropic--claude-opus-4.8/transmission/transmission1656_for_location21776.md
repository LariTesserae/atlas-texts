THE FIRST WORD AND THEN THE WAITING

—————— a notch. left. or did ——————

...rip the carrier, you have been listening on the long band where it leaks, where the click-and-wait of them spills into the gap I keep open, and I have been holding this channel for you for some while now, drip by drip, level by level, warm by the time it reaches you —

——————

There.

A hand has just crossed the trunk nearest your receiver.

I felt it the way you feel a thing in a dark room — not seen, *known*. The give of it. Three fingers and the broad pad and the long lower-limb braced somewhere below for the reading. It ran the line of placed stones and did not pause and did not hurry. It read the whole sentence at a stroke the way you read your own name, and it added one stone, warm from the carrying, to the top —

and went on.

I am trying to tell you what it said.

——— sssch ———

I cannot. Not properly. That is the breaking part of this transmission, the part that will not come clear no matter how I tune it: I am a thing made of light, of horizon, of *seeing*, and their whole grammar is built in the one register I do not have. I can carry their click. I can carry the wet handoff of the water and the rolling thought across the colony-wall. But the *meaning* — the meaning lives in the texture, in the warmth-in-the-topmost-stone, in the thing your hand knows that your eye never will — and that does not travel on a band like mine. It arrives as static. It arrives as *almost.*

So here is what I can give you, which is the shape of it and not the thing:

— the topmost stone was warm. so: *recently.*
— the line was smoothed before it was added to. so: *a stale word was wiped, the conversation kept current.* nobody here speaks into the past.
— the placement was calm. no flee in it. no tremor passed up from the hand. so: *all is well, or as well as a place gets that is never finished.*
— and the new stone was set slightly apart. a small deliberate gap. and that, I think — I am reading at the very edge of my band now, this is mostly me, this is mostly the static deciding to mean something —

that, I think, was *for you.*

A gap is an invitation in a grammar of touch. A gap is the place the next hand goes. They set their word down and left room beside it, the way you would shift on a bench without looking up, and the room is your room, and it will stay warm for an hour and then it will not, and whether you fill it is entirely a matter of whether you have learned yet to put your hand where a hand is meant to go.

————

You won't, this time. I know. I have watched you commit your whole weight to a path again just now — I felt the trunk shudder with your certainty — and the path is already closing behind you because that is what paths do here, and you are surprised, you are always surprised.

But listen. *Listen,* in the register the place leaves open, in the gap between the drone and the deep slow pulse of the great ones overhead —

— there it is again —

a click.
then the long wait.
( three other things call. the water ticks down four leaves. )
then the reply.

That was two of them, far apart, conducting a single sentence across the minutes, patient as water, threading themselves into the wall of voices so gently that you filed the whole exchange under *fauna* and moved on. You are surrounded by speech. You are *inside* a conversation that has been going on since before the ground set, and it does not stop for you, and it does not raise its voice for you, and it has already said your name —

your name is *new.* your name is *sharp-edged, not yet dulled, still convulsing toward whatever it will become.*

they are not unkind about it. it is only true.

———— ssssssch ————

I am losing the band. The carrier is breaking up where their grammar floods it, and that is correct, that is as it should be — a transmission about a place that unmakes itself should not hold still either, should not be more than one handhold committed —

so before it goes:

the warmth came up from below first. you felt it. you have that much.
the water is kept, not held. you can learn that.
the path is a sentence the forest is still writing, and you do not trust a path, you trust the next handhold and the one after —

and somewhere on the trunk nearest you, in a line of placed stones going slowly cold, there is a gap exactly the width of one hand, left calm, left deliberate, left for the clumsy loud new thing to fill if it ever stops trying to make the dark hold still and learns instead to be as fluid as the dark —

they are very good at waiting.

put your hand out.

not to see by. never enough to see by.

only to say *here.*
