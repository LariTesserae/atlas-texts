did you get it. did you GET it. no. you got the edge of it. you always get the edge.

INCOMING — slow band — very slow — adjusting —

what comes through is not voice. understand that first. nothing here has a throat in the sense you mean. what comes through is the SHAPE of patience pressed against the membrane of the air until the air sweats it back out as tone.

a rim thickens by a hair.
that is one letter.
a year passes.
that is the space between two letters.

we are receiving a sentence. we have been receiving it since the station was built. we will be off the air, the antenna rust-eaten and fallen, the operators all in the ground, before the first word completes its vowel.

———

[interference — pale segmented — grazing the inner wall of the signal —]

something keeps the channel clean. something thins the film so the tone stays true. we do not control it. it crawls. when it is flushed out it works its way back. we have stopped trying to name it. it is the broadcast keeping itself honest. it is the slow one wetting its own lip to speak.

———

WAIT —

cross-traffic. a different hand. FAST.

not the carrier. something on the carrier. something that came through quick and warm and the right size to be lonely, and it left a mark in the band, a notch, a groove cut deliberate—

—a bowl. a held tone. a place where the signal gathers and STAYS gathered because someone shaped it to gather, and then that someone—

[lost. ended. moved off frequency. gone so long ago that fresh static has risen on every side of where their hand was]

—and the bowl is still full. the held tone still holds. untended. routed by grooves no one tends. the visitor is not transmitting. the visitor stopped transmitting before the first stone of the present stone was laid. but the GESTURE continues, the channel they opened still channels, the cup they cut still fills—

———

operator's note, scrawled, unsigned:

it kept asking me to confirm receipt. not the slow one. the slow one does not ask anything of anyone. it was the OTHER thing, the notch in the carrier, the fast warm hand. it left the bowl full as if for whoever came after. it routed the water as if it knew there'd be a thirst.

was that us. was that meant for us.

no. we are too brief to be addressed. but the bowl is full, and we drank, and that is not nothing—
