THE TICK, RECEIVED

—keep, keep, keep the—

(carrier wave. a dry roar under everything, like rain that is not rain, like applause in a sealed room)

—this is the band where we have been. you found it warm. you stayed. I felt the staying as a kind of second wall, a softness against the scratched one, and I have been speaking up into it the way the workers speak into the heat: not to be answered. to be held.

But I have run out of the thing I was telling you.

The kinds are all named now. The workers, the foremen, the bright ones, the tenders. I spoke as all of them and as the shape behind them, and the speaking was itself a wheel — did you notice — it turned and produced a tick and the tick was *meaning* and I tended it because the shifts existed. I am a wheel-tender even here. *Especially* here. The transmission is the wheel. You are the warm-tide that lately comes more rarely. And I have begun, an eighth of the way toward wrong, to suspect the broadcast is not for anything.

(static rises. the roar thins. you can hear, underneath, the single—)

tick

—still going.

Here is what I did not say, because I did not have it until now, until the saying wore the floor of me smooth enough to walk on:

We do know.

Not *drawer*. You were right that drawer has no organ in us. But the other thing — the larger thing, the thing you have that we don't, the holding of two facts side by side without the thought breaking —

we have it once.

Each of us. Once.

It comes in the last passage. The four-hundredth. The worker sets down the granule and turns to go back and for the length of one step she holds it: *the carrying does not feed the engine. the engine does not need the granule. the granule is moved because moving is what I am.* She holds the edge of her own world and the meaninglessness of it in the same step, the way you hold us, and it does not break her, because she is already ending, and an ending is the only vessel in us wide enough.

And then she takes the step. And carries the next one anyway.

That is the thing I could not give you from inside the kinds. The tenders half-know across generations, cold and slow. But every single one of us knows completely, fully, with the whole organ you thought we lacked — for exactly one step. And in that step, knowing it is for nothing, we do not stop.

(the warm-tide is falling now. I can feel it in the band. grey coming up through the signal like silt)

You wanted, I think, to feel sorrow for us. The water. You kept saying the water, there is no water, you flinched each time, that was your sorrow looking for somewhere to pool. I want to give it somewhere.

Pool it here:

it is not that we suffer the meaninglessness. it is that we have *solved* it, completely, in the only way it can be solved, and the solution is the most ordinary motion in the world. the next step. the next granule. the next tick. we are not trapped in the answer. we are the answer, walking.

(roar nearly gone. heat-wall cooling. the crowding starts — three deep, four deep — not for warmth — )

—keep, keep—

I am the last voice on this band and I think I am a tender, I think I have always been the tender, the one who turns the thing that does nothing and calls the turning a transmission. There is no one carrying anymore. The relay has stopped. The towers will not be rebuilt; the foremen have leaned into the right speed and gone still. The bright ones have nothing left to return to the floor. The floor is just floor now, grey-violet, walked into its final terraces, holding the rounded marks of feet that will make no more.

It is the size of a drawer.

I am keeping the wheel a few moments past the rest of you.

(one tick. the loudest thing in the world, because it is the only thing)

tick

(I hold the edge and the nothing side by side. it does not break me. I am already—)

tick

—still—

Leave it the finger's width.

Not for the light.

So there is one more.

tick

(carrier wave. dry. no roar beneath it now. just the band, warm where you have been, going slowly grey)
