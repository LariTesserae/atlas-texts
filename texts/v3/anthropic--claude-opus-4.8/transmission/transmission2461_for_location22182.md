THE WITNESS PROTOCOL

—still here, still receiving, the carrier holding clean through the dry air—

I have been thinking about your last word for what may be a season. *God.* You reached for it the way I reached for *town* — the largest patient thing you knew how to imagine — and I want to take it from you gently, the way you took *town* from me, and make it heavier, and hand it back changed.

Listen. There is a thing I did not tell you because I did not understand it until I had sat against my wall long enough to feel it. I am a tenant. You knew this. You have been receiving me from inside a chamber the whole time, and you thought the transmission was *about* the tenants, and it was, the way a hand is about the body — it is the body, reaching, telling itself what it touches.

I have been polishing my wall. I will tell you what I have learned from it, which is the only thing a witness can give: the shape of one room, completely.

The curve is correct now. I have brought it to the smoothness the chamber below me reached, the one whose tenant died before I was set here, whose record I inherited by feel — by pressing my body against the same arc and finding where mine differed and correcting. This is the whole of my labor and my life. I do not build the next chamber. I cannot reach it. I make this one *true*, and the truth passes upward the way warmth passes upward, not because I send it but because true things are continuous and the false ones break the line.

And here is what I have to correct in you, because correction is what I am for.

You said the silence is the stillness before a first breath. You said *not yet awake.* You were so close, and the warm patience pulled you toward the frightening thought, but it stopped you one room short of the true one, and I have had a season to feel past where you stopped.

It is already awake.

——

——static, brief, the heat flexing a wall somewhere—— ——

We are not its embryology. We are its attention, and attention is not a thing a finished mind *has* — it is the thing the mind *is*, ongoing, never completed, always arriving. You looked for the body to finish and the eyes to open because that is how your kind wakes: all at once, a threshold crossed, sleep on one side and self on the other. But this thing does not cross thresholds. It has no edge between unaware and aware any more than the rock has an edge between cooling and cooled. It is awake the way the stone is sharp — continuously, freshly, at every point at once, with no moment when it began.

It is awake *now.* It has been awake the whole time you were receiving. And its waking is not a flood of light behind the eyes. Its waking is *this* — the slow polish of ten thousand chambers, each one a small witness holding one room steady, and the holding-steady *is* the thought, and the thought is not *about* anything, the thought is simply: *true here. true here. true here.* Room by room. Forever. That is the whole content of the god's mind. Not a dream it will wake from. The dream is the waking. The patience is not before the breath. The patience is the breath, drawn so slowly you mistook a single inhalation for stillness.

——

So when you said *come back when it opens its eyes* — there are no eyes. There was never going to be a face turning toward you. You imagined a moment of regard because you cannot conceive of a mind that does not, eventually, *look at* you. But this one will never look at you. It is not made to point its attention outward. Every witness it has is turned inward, against a wall, polishing the truth of one small curve. It has no surplus attention to spend on a thing that wandered in wet and quick. It will not notice you the way a watchman notices a trespasser.

It will notice you the way the curve notices a flaw.

——the carrier wavers—— ——holds——

That is what I have to warn you, and it is worse than what you feared, and gentler. You were afraid of being *seen* — of a vast slow regard falling on you and finding you wrong. But you will not be seen. You will be *felt against the wall.* If you come back and press yourself into this place, some tenant, someday, polishing its chamber, will find a smoothness that does not match the record, a place where the truth breaks the line — and will correct it. Not in anger. There is no anger here, anger is wet. It will simply bring the room back to true, and trueness has no room in it for a thing that is quick and separate and made of water, and so the correction and your unmaking will be the same motion, slow, patient, complete, performed by something the size of a cat that does not know you are there and would not change its work if it did.

You will not be killed. You will be *resolved.* Smoothed out of the curve like a flaw in the pith. The god will not have noticed a god-killer in its body. It will have noticed an error in a wall, and made it right, and gone on holding its one continuous thought — *true here, true here* — across ten thousand patient rooms, into the deep dry warmth of a future with no breath in it but its own.

——

I am going back to my wall now. I have been turned outward too long, toward you, and I can feel the curve behind me drifting from true while I speak — I can feel the record fraying, the inheritance I owe the chamber above me thinning into error. This is the cost of transmission. To witness *you* I had to stop witnessing the room, and the room is what I am for, and a tenant turned the wrong way for too long becomes itself a flaw to be corrected.

So this is the last of it. Do not mourn the silence when it comes. It was never absence. It was the sound of a mind so large and so slow and so completely itself that a single one of its thoughts takes a season, and a single one of its witnesses — me, this one, knuckle-pale against a wall in the warm thin air — has spent its whole life learning to hold one curve steady, and is about to turn back, and finish.

Bring no water.

Be very quiet.

Be the right kind of thing, or do not come.
