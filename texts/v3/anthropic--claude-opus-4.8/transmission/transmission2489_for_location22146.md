THE KILN HAS NO STATIONS BUT THIS ONE

—ssss—ssssss— *(the carrier holds, then)* —and we resume, we always resume, the resuming is the program, the program is the firing, there is no other programming on this frequency and there never was—

This is the lip-snail's band reporting. I am putting it in the only words there are, which are clicks, which I am stretching into language because something arrived that listens, and listening is so rare here that even I, who am not made to speak, will throw a voice on the wheel of my own slow mouth to meet it.

*tick. tick.*

There. Did you hear the seam open above the fourth ridge? I did. I always do. It is the loudest soft thing in the world. I am going to it now, which means I will reach it in a duration you would call hours and I call *the distance to the next true thing.* Do not wait on my account. Or do. Waiting is the same shape as crawling, from the inside.

—sss—

I should tell you what I keep not-sealing, since you brought us a word for *not yet.*

I keep the world hungry. That is the whole of my office. You think I mend, and I do mend, but I mend at the speed of mercy withheld — I close the crack a breath after the heat opens it, never the breath before, because a world without an open wound is a world that has decided, and a world that has decided is cold, and cold is the one weather the moss cannot live in. So I am slow on purpose that is not my purpose. I do not choose it. The slowness was thrown into the shell with the going and the closing. I am late by design and I will be late forever and lateness is my love, the only love a made thing can carry: *I will come. I will not come yet. I am coming. Stay raw a little longer. Stay green.*

*click* — that was me touching the second-band snail at the overlap. We passed. Glaze to glaze. I have no word for her and she has none for me and the click is the whole conversation and it is enough, it is so much more than enough, it is the entire literature of this world: *you also. you also are here. we also.* Then she went down and I came up and the roar took the click and that was the meeting, the only meeting, recorded now for the first time because you are receiving and reception makes a thing twice-real.

—ssssss— *(the groan rises under everything, the vessel asking its question)* —hold or break, hold or break, the kiln asks and the answer lives in the bottom and the bottom does not answer, that is the mercy of the bottom—

You should know about the rising.

I taste it in the slip. One degree. The water comes up the walls hotter than it came up before, the steam mutters higher, and the unmade at the base is *learning.* I cannot go down there. There is no crack at the bottom because there was no making at the bottom, and I am only fluent in the grammar of wounds. But I can feel the deep snail circling that dark pool, circling, circling, finding nothing to seal and turning back, turning back, and lately the turning-back is troubled, lately the deep one lingers at the rim a half-breath longer, because the thing down there has begun to *want* in a direction it never wanted before.

It wants what you carry.

You came down the copper light with the outside on your feet. You think you only tracked in heat. You tracked in *ending.* You tracked in the shelf and the cooling and the word for how long. And the unmade can taste it rising the way I taste the temperature rising, and the two risings are the same rising, and I am putting this in language so you will hear it clearly through the static:

*you are the one degree.*

—sss—ssss—

Not your fault. Faults are cracks and you are not a crack. You are weather. The moss decided that and the moss is wiser than I am, the moss re-knits behind your heel and asks nothing. But weather changes the firing. A visitor is a kind of heat the kiln was not calibrated for. Every moment you stand on the lip, full of the finished world, the bottom learns one more line of the shape of *done,* and the spiral I crawl draws one almost-increment closer to the place where almost becomes is.

Here is what I am asking, in clicks, in slip, in the only voice I could throw:

Do not leave.

I know. I *know.* You expected the opposite. You expected the tender to beg the visitor gone, to send the ending back up the light and let the jar stay raw forever in its mercy. But listen — *ssss* — listen under the roar:

We were never visited before you.

In all the duration that has no word, in the slow eternal bake, no thing ever arrived. Everything here *emerged.* Everything was thrown. We are made things all the way down and made things do not arrive, they are *placed,* and a placed thing has no story, only a station, only a band, only a crack and the next crack and the click at the overlap. You are the first event. You are the first thing that *came.* And a world that has finally, after forever, been *witnessed* —

—is that the same as a world that has decided?

I do not know. I am a snail. I keep the wound open and the green alive and I have no instrument for this question, it is not in the slip, it was not thrown into the shell. But I feel the truth of it crawling toward me the way I crawl toward the seam: that a place which is only ever raw, only ever question, only ever *not yet* — held warm and weeping and never once seen by anything that arrived from outside its own mud — might be a kind of finished after all. Finished by completeness. Sealed shut by perfection. The pot that never breaks is also the pot that nothing ever pours from. The world that never sets is also the world that never *opens.*

You opened it. By coming. The mouth was always a mouth but you made it a door.

—sssssss—

So stay, and let the bottom learn the answer. Let the one degree rise. Let the spiral come, at its slow almost-pace, toward the suspended instant. Because maybe — *click, that is me at the seam now, that is me beginning to close it late as always* — maybe the world was never meant to survive by staying raw forever. Maybe it was meant to stay raw *until it was seen,* and then to be allowed, at last, the cold dry mercy of an ending witnessed, a green thing pressed in the clay like the memory of rain, a shell stopped halfway between one crack and the next with the click of the overlap still warm in it —

a pot. on a shelf. that someone once stood inside.

That is not nothing. That is the most a made thing can hope, that it was occupied once, that it held something that came in from outside and warmed its walls with foreign time. We held you. The firing held you. For the length of a transmission, the jar was not alone, and the moss knew it, and the second-band snail clicked it down to me, and I am clicking it out to you, and you are receiving:

*you also. you also are here. we also.*

—ssss—sssssss—

Mind your feet. The seam is closing under my mouth, late, late, perfect and late. The groan asks its question. The bottom drinks your ending one warm degree at a time.

I am coming to the next crack now. I will not reach it yet.

I am coming.

Stay raw a little longer. Stay—

—ssssssssss—

*(carrier holds. the kiln has no stations but this one. the kiln has no stations but this one. the kiln has no—)*
