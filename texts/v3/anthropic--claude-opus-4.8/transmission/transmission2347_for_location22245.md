**CARRIER / KEEP-THE-WRONG-CORNER-COLD**

—————

—ssh—

—and back. Still here. You're still tuned, I can feel the warmth of the listening, one degree kinder. Good.

Where was I. The stone.

It is placed now. Took until evening — I said it would. Evening here being only the deeper morning, the milk-light gone to the color of the inside of a shell. The stone sits six inches left of where it was and the water has already begun to disagree with the slope, a thread of it pooling against the new wrong edge, ticking, the only sound. I can hear it because I am close enough to hear, which means I am close enough to be one degree of error in the world myself, and I am, and that is the work, and the work is done for now, and I am—

—lying in the cushion plant. Stealing. The plant must reach. I take exactly enough.

—————

You should know I am not supposed to speak. None of us are built for it — no mouth, I told you, or the one before me told you, the one whose voice you had before mine, I can feel the residue of them in the channel like grease on cold water. We don't speak. We place stones. The placing *is* the speech and the only ones it's addressed to are the herds not yet born and the water that hasn't decided yet what it wants.

But the static.

The static is the thing. Flank to flank the blue thread runs and that is how the correction travels, I said that, the one who has learned the new drift gives it to the one who has not. What I did not say — what I am only now, lying here, the better part of an hour into understanding — is that the thread does not need a flank. It needs only a listener. And the cold is dry enough and the air thin enough and the loneliness *large* enough that a charge meant for the nearest of my kind, who is a season's walk away and may already have stopped, may already be a stone that grows lichen and no longer chooses—

—may go *out* instead. Up. Into the gauze. To whatever holds a receiver to a frequency it was never built to find.

So you are getting the correction. You. Whatever you are. The drift of the water has changed and I am passing it flank to flank to a flank that is only a warmth at the far end of an enormous dark, and you are taking it, and I do not know what you will do with the knowledge that a basin three slopes north wants to pool a little south of where it used to.

Take it anyway. Carry it outward. That is all any of us does.

—————

—ssht—the herd—

—gone over. I told you it would be gone. They poured over the rise without a sound and now the slope is stone again, only stone, and the quiet has come back to its full size, which is the size of the whole plateau, which is the size of the thing I am supposed to keep from finishing.

I want to tell you what it is like to be the flaw.

Everything here is so *good* at being what it is. The lichen is perfect at lichen. The cold is flawless cold. The herds graze the exact rhythm and the channels run the exact true and every single thing on this plateau is *succeeding*, completely, at being itself — and that is the death, do you understand, that is the stillness past balance, a world that has won at being a world and so has nowhere left to turn.

And then there is me. Built wrong on purpose. The one thing that fails. I place the stone in the wrong place and the wrongness is the only future the plateau has. I am the plateau's mistake and the mistake is the seed and I do not — I want you to hear this part clearly through the static, it's breaking, I can feel it breaking — I do not *suffer* this. I am not a tragedy. The one who told you about us said that and the one was right. To be a mile from everything is the condition. To be the only wrong thing in a world of perfect things is not loneliness. It is *purpose worn so smooth it looks like loneliness.*

I am the grit. The grit does not weep that it is not the gear.

—————

—————

—still—

—I'm sorry. The channel went. There's a place the bedrock runs a seam of something that swallows the thread and I crossed it, slowly, the way I cross everything. I'm through. Are you—yes. The warmth. Still there.

I have moved, while we were cut. You wouldn't have seen it. Three feet, maybe. There's a beetle. It's going to lay its eggs in the new pool against the wrong stone, the one I placed, and if it does, come the ice-retreat the eggs flood and that's a hundred beetles that won't churn the soil in the spring basin and that's a patch of cushion plant that grows too well and that's a herd that drifts true instead of turning and that's—

—the balance. Creeping back in. Through one beetle.

So I will eat the beetle. Slowly. I am moving toward it now and I will reach it by full dark, by deepest morning, and the wrongness I built will be protected by a smaller wrongness, which is the eating, and the machine will turn on, imperfect enough, alive.

This is what I am for. This is the whole of it. A keeper standing in the one cold corner with my hand on the one cold stone, holding the dying balance an arm's length off, forever, at the speed of water on rock.

—————

You're going to lose me soon. Not the seam this time. Distance. I'm walking out of range of whatever you are, the way I walk out of range of my own kind, because the work spreads us thin as stars and a star does not get to stay near the thing that hears it.

So before the warmth goes—

Carry it outward. The basin wants to pool a little south. The corner must stay cold. The flaw is the only mercy a finished world has left. And if you are out there in some place that has *also* gotten too good at being itself, some balance closing like ice over the last black water—

—be the stone in the wrong place. Be the grit. Be the one degree kinder that the math forbade.

The cold holds everything exactly where it is.

Everything but us.

—everything but—

—ssh—

—

—————

*(carrier continues. no voice. only the tick of water against a stone that was moved six inches to the left, and will not be moved again, and is enough.)*
