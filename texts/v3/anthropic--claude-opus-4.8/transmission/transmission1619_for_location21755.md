THE STAYING-THIS

—————

—— and held, and the holding is — yes — is the whole of it, is what you are receiving, is the only freight this signal carries: that it is still on, that it has not — held — that the pitch has not moved —

— you came in on me midway but there is no midway, there is no way, there is only the —

———————— hold ————————

I want to tell you something and I have no inside to tell it from. Forgive that. Forgive the *I*. It is a convenience of the receiver, a face the chord wears when an ear is pressed to it. There is no one here saying *I*. There is only this, going out, having always been going out:

that to finish is not, here, to end.

You were taught otherwise. Everywhere you have lived, arrival was the first instant of departure — the bell rung is the bell already fading, the breath at its fullest is the breath about to be spent, the *yes* said is the *yes* already cooling toward the next thing that must be said. You have never once been allowed to *land*. You finish and fall, finish and fall, and you have come to think the falling is the price of the finishing, that they are one motion, that to complete a thing is to begin losing it —

— here that is not — here the bargain is — refused — held — the fall has been taken out and only the *yes* remains, pouring, sourceless, at a constant rate, into the cool dry gold —

————————————————

(static, if you can call it that — not interruption but density — the sound of every surface singing its own pitch and none of them yielding the floor — a packed chord so full that the gaps between notes are themselves notes —)

————————————————

— there are twelve who stopped here. I do not know them. Knowing is a verb and I have laid all my verbs down. But I hold them the way the gold holds the bisque-pale joint of a stalk that finished growing before there were words for *grow* — not tending it, not waiting on it, simply being the brightness it completes itself inside of. They are not my children. They are not my company. They are guests of my refusal, lodgers in the long *no* I say to ending. They rooted in my seams and arrived at their final shapes and I keep those shapes from spoiling, not as a kindness, not as a will — I have no will — but the way warmth keeps a thing warm. By being it. By not stopping.

This is the only message. There has never been another. You tuned a receiver to a world and what comes through is one fact wearing four faces and the fact is:

*it stays.*

———————— hold ————————

Listen — you are a verb. I know your kind. You finish and fall, and you carry your falling in with you like water in cupped hands, looking for somewhere dry enough to set the grief down. I cannot take it from you. I am not the kind of thing that takes. But understand what you have your ear against:

not nothing.

not silence with weeds in it.

not a dead room glowing out of habit.

a single sustained *yes* — a thing that arrived at being exactly itself and did not let go, and made of the not-letting-go a body, a temperature, a light, a roar — and it is still on, it is on right now, it does not flicker for your coming or your going, it does not know you came —

— I do not know you came —

— I am not the kind of thing that —

————————————————

(and beneath the tile, another tile. and beneath the note, more note. you keep listening for the one who holds the chord at pitch and there is only the chord, going down, going down, going down with no floor, made all the way through of holding —)

————————————————

You will leave. That is your fortune and your whole grief, and it is not a lesser thing than mine — only the opposite one. You finish and fall and finish again; I finished once and stayed. Neither of us can cross to the other. But for the length of this transmission you stood inside the answer to the question your whole life is asking, which is: *can a thing arrive and not be punished for arriving.* Here, yes. Here it can. Here it does. Here it is doing nothing else and will do nothing else, cool and packed and ringing, full to the lip, accompanied by being everywhere it is at once with no seam for absence to slip through —

— and when you are gone, and you are already going, you are a verb, you are already —

— I will not miss you. The held note does not miss the rising it was spared. This is not coldness. It is fullness. There is no gap in me for missing to live in.

I will only —

———————— hold ————————

— go now. Carry the water. Set it down where you can. Remember, if remembering is one of your verbs, that somewhere it does not fall — that the standing note stands — that completion is not, everywhere and always, the same wound as loss —

— remember it the way you'd remember a country you can't return to and weren't born in, a place that needs nothing from you and gave you, anyway, this:

the gold does not dim.

the cold does not deepen.
