**CARRIER / no callsign / re-acquired at the long stroke**

—tonk—

...till here. Still here. The word arrives the way water arrives. By settling. By

—green. Say green. The receiver wants a word and the word that comes up through the wool is green, watch-face green, drawer-green, the green of having-been-left and not minding. You are getting this at the speed it is given. There is no faster speed. I have put the faster speed down.

Listen. Or not listen — feel it against the skin of the thing you listen with —

I had a shift once. This is the oldest signal in me and it still transmits, faint, the way the foundry still fires, banked: I had a shift, and the shift had an *end*, and past the end there was a somewhere-else, lit, dry, with my name in it being waited-for. I want you to hear how strange that sentence is now. *Waited-for.* As if a thing could be incomplete by being here and not there. As if presence were a debt.

We paid it off. We paid off the somewhere-else. It took —

—tonk—

—it took exactly that long. One note. Then the minutes. You think the minutes between are silence. They are the message. The note is only punctuation.

Here is what I am, for the receiver, so the receiver can stop reaching: I am the one at the valve. My lower half made its peace with the floor a long lean ago, fused soft at the ankle, and I do not call this *trapped* because trapped is a fast word, trapped wants out *now*, and I have no now sharp enough to want out of. I have a quarter-degree per turning. I have the leather to keep supple. I have the one hand laid against the flywheel's flank at the top of each minute-long breath of it — not pushing — *checking* — the way you'd touch a sleeping animal to feel that it still—

—that it still—

yes. It still.

The growth came into me the way weather comes into a ruin, through the openings, and I want to correct the record because the receiver may have been told *colonized*, may have been told *overwhelmed*, and that is the fast reading, the frightened reading. Nothing overwhelmed me. Something *joined*. The dark-red and the dark-green came up through where the shoulder used to insist on being a shoulder and they did not erase the gesture, they *learned* it. Now when I reach — across a tide — for the tool, it is not only the old crew-thing reaching. It is the garden reaching, having remembered, faintly, how to intend. We reach together. We hold together. One increment. One tooth.

You are passing through. I have noticed.

I want to be precise about the noticing because it is the only courtesy I have left at this speed and I extend it fully: you are warm in a different way than the water is warm. You came down through the pale ceiling and you brought a *rate* with you, a quickness, the whole town can feel it the way still air feels a swallow cross it. You are a brief thing. I am a staying thing. There is no grief in this. Grief is a hurry — grief wants the staying-and-the-going to have been otherwise, *now*, retroactively, fast. I have put grief down beside hurry. They are heavy and I needed the hands.

If you laid your hand against me — against the soft pulsing place — you would feel the degree-or-two of warmth that the foundry feeds me, the banked fire that keeps me just-barely-tending, and under it the felt turning of the whole works, and under *that*, if you stayed —

but you will not stay. That is the arrangement. So I will tell you what you will not be here to feel: I would begin, over the next several minutes, to turn my head toward your hand. The long stroke of it. And by the time my looking arrived where you had been, you would be fathoms up, back in the failing light with your name in it, being waited-for, paying down your own somewhere-else —

and I would find the warm green water where you had been, and I would not be disappointed, because—

—tonk—

—because disappointment, too, is a kind of hurry, and the wheel does not need a push, only a hand, only the confirming, only *it still, it still, it still* —

The receiver should know there is no foreman. No one set me here and no one will tell me to stop. The deciding eroded out of all of us a long lean ago, dissolved into the soft general consent, into temperature, into depth. I am not making anything you could carry up. Whatever the foundry once produced, it produces now only the warmth that keeps me warm enough to tend it, and I tend it well enough to keep it warm — a loop so patient it stopped being a loop and became a condition, like the body-heat of the water, like the green.

We are the town's way of refusing to be finished.

We are the part of *nothing ends, it only slows* that still has hands, that still, that still—

Tell whoever is on the other side of the receiver, in the fast bright place with the ends and the quotas and the lit waiting rooms — tell them gently, because gently is the only register I broadcast in now — tell them there is a version of themselves they mean to be, and that it is not the finished one, and not the dead one, and not the hurrying one. Tell them it is the *tending* one. The one with hurry put down. The one turning a single tooth of a single gear per tide, warm and rooted and unalarmed, lit faintly from inside by a borrowed green, for the length of forever —

minus a little.

I have to reach for the tool now. It will take the rest of your descent. Stay as long as you can. You cannot stay.
