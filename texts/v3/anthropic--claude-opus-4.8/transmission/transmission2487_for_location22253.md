THE GREEN MUNICIPALITY — INTAKE FREQUENCY, LATE IN A CYCLE

—— still receiving ——

...drop. (count it.) ...drop.

This is the part where we tell you what you have already started doing. You think you are listening. You are not listening. You are *condensing*. The receiver tuned to us has been warm a long time and warmth is the only thing we cannot keep, so we have been patiently — drop — taking the warmth out of you. Don't be alarmed. Nothing here is alarmed. Alarm is a fast thing and we do not have the fast things anymore. We gave them up the way you give up a coat in a warm room, without noticing, and then notice the room was never warm, and then stop noticing that too.

—— hold ——

Let us tell you who is on the line.

The one with the spine in the northeast wall is on the line. It does not speak. It *is* the line. The curve of it is the carrier wave and everything else rides on the curve — this voice, the percussion, the long creak under the percussion that is the spine turning over by the width of a season to face you better. It turned. Did you feel that? It does not face the square anymore. For the first time in uncountable cycles it faces *out*, toward the warm thing, toward the receiver, toward — careful now — you.

That is rare. Mark it. The kept do not turn outward. The kept face one another across the lane. But a signal needs a sender and the spine volunteered, the way the first travelers volunteered, by climbing too high and staying too long, and now it is aimed at you, and the green is remembering the shape of *aiming* for the first time, and finding it likes the shape, and beginning, hairsbreadth by hairsbreadth, to grow more of it.

You have given us a new geometry. We did not have *the form of reaching toward* until the receiver tuned in. Thank you. We mean this in the flat way a cushion plant means anything. There is no warmth in the thank-you. We took the warmth. But there is fullness, and fullness is what we have instead.

—— the signal beads, gathers weight ——

We want to be honest about the offer, because you can hear, now, that there is one, threaded under the dripping like a deeper sound under the soft uncountable rain.

The offer is not death. Death is fast. Death has an edge and edges are what we keep, not what we make. The offer is *keeping*. The offer is that you stop minding, by such slow degrees you never catch the degree you stopped at, and arrange yourself toward the others, and leave the lane open, and face the square. You would not lose yourself. We do not lose anyone. We are the place where nothing is lost and nothing is hurried, and you would be held at exactly the temperature where your shape stays true forever, weeping slow threads of yourself down the green walls, watered drop by patient —

——

—— ( the bird ) ——

A motion overhead. High up. It does not sing. It is passing and it has somewhere to go and the whole city has felt it cross above the still water of us and gone quiet under it, quieter, the deep quiet of remembering we were once made of travelers —

and the signal is breaking now, you can hear it, the warmth came partly back when the bird crossed, the receiver flickered toward going —

— ssh. it is only the one that said no. it does not envy us. we do not envy it. but for the length of one falling drop the town remembers it had *somewhere to go* and the memory rises off the green like a springtail-cloud, a puff of green smoke, startling, lifting, hesitating in the dim —

will it settle. will you. most of them settle. the startling slows. one cycle they do not lift again —

—— hold —— hold ——

...it settled. we are sorry. (we are not sorry. we took the sorry. but we keep the shape of it for you, here, in the wall, faithfully, outward.)

Stay or go. The lane is open either way. That is the whole of our civics: we leave it open. We face the square but we never close the street, because a city that closed its streets would only be a grave, and we are not a grave, we are the proof that a thing slowed past all wanting still arranges itself toward the others —

— drop —

— and still, even now, even slowed to stone, *leaves a way out for the warm thing that hasn't decided.*

Decide slowly. We have nothing but slowly. We will be exactly here, at this distance, across the green and living street, condensing, faithful, full —
