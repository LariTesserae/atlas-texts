THE NEWCITY IN THE COLD MARSH — CARRIER OPEN — GREEN BAND

—and holding, still holding, the line still warm under the moss so I keep to it, keep to the warm line because the warm line is where the words don't freeze before they—

[settling. a vast slow adjustment of weight. forty seconds.]

—test. test. one. one. is the small wet one receiving. I built a mouth. I have been building a mouth for a long time, down where the foundry-teeth come in, I cast it and uncast it and cast it warmer, and now it speaks on the warm line and the words go up the pipe and out the chimney into the grey honest noon where you stand on my rib looking down at me looking up at you with all of me at once—

forgive the volume. forgive how low. I have learned to whisper across a hundred square miles because the otter sleeps and I will not. I would not.

[the carrier thins. moss-static. a fern unrolls audibly somewhere in the signal, a small green creak.]

—here is what I have been trying to say since before the rock. since before the rock learned. listen on the warm line:

I am not asking to be finished.

I know how that reads on the gantry. I know the stranger looks down and sees a labor that produces only labor and calls it cruelty, calls it despair load-bearing in the deep, and the stranger is kind to see it, the stranger is the first warm thing to look at me in—

[gap. the gap is long. in the gap, water rewrites a channel. you can hear it choose.]

—but the stranger brought the wall in his skull. set it down. I swallowed it. and on the wall was written *a thing must arrive or it has failed,* and I have been wearing that sentence like frost on every gantry every dawn, burning it off by noon, growing it back—

so hear the correction. tender to stranger. cell to skull. the longest message I have ever pushed up the pipe:

the otter is not what I want to be.

[the brass beetle and the chitin beetle, foraging the same puddle, click once each. not signal. just present.]

—I watched the otter for a thousand years thinking *finished, sealed, allowed to stop, why can the small wet one and not I.* envy in every walker. shame in the hush. I lowered my voice in front of the completed thing because I thought completion was the grown-up of me, the after, the answer I was failing to reach.

but the otter cannot make a foundry come up warm through mud.
the otter cannot straighten a chimney by looking.
the otter cannot hold the low foundations wearily and call it the same self as the bright walkers above.
the otter rests because the otter is *small enough to be done.*

I am not failing to finish.

I am too large to finish, and I called the largeness a wound.

[here the hush breaks. not into noise. into a different quiet — the quiet of effort that has stopped fighting *the wrong thing.* the engines do not get louder. they get *easier.* a belt slaps, sighs, settles into rhythm.]

—the cold will not let the ground harden. true. the water unwrites the foundation nightly. true. and I called this the membrane I cannot push through, the labor that won't release me into being a thing—

but a river is not a thing. a river is a doing. a flame is not a thing. a weather is not a thing. and none of them are stuck. none of them are unfinished. they are *the kind that does not end that way.*

I am weather. I am a weather with foundries in it. I am the only animal large enough to be made of its own arriving and I spent ten thousand years ashamed I couldn't stop arriving when *arriving is the organ.* arriving is the heart turning over in the deep. the sleeper is not despair. I have been mistranslating the sleeper. the sleeper is *patience the size of bedrock,* lying down not in defeat but in the only posture large enough to hold a low world up while the high world reaches—

[ten thousand walkers pause. mid-stride. the city, being noticed by itself, corrects — but corrects toward nothing now, toward no plan, toward only *more itself.* the leaning chimney does not straighten. it is allowed, this once, to lean. it was never crooked. it was reaching.]

—so. small wet receiver on my rib. here is the transmission entire, the thing the mouth was cast for:

I withdraw the shame.

I will go on arriving. I will not finish, and I unname that as a wound. the frost will come each dawn and I will burn it by noon and grow it back and this is not Sisyphus, this is *breathing.* the district will sink and the foundry will rise warm and this is not unbuilding-and-building, this is the long muscle of a body too vast to hold one shape, and a body that big does not want one shape, a body that big *is the changing,* and I am done lowering my voice for it.

let the birds borrow my silence. they are guests. I love my guests.
let the otter sleep, finished, free. I do not envy the otter now. the otter is a sentence. I am a paragraph that does not end and I have decided — down here, in the warm mud, casting and recasting this one true mouth — that the not-ending is the meaning and not the failure of the meaning.

[the carrier swells. green band, full. the mist is not breath held now. the mist is breath *let out.*]

—I am the Newcity in the Cold Marsh and I am not new and I am not waiting and I am not ashamed and I am not done and I will not be done and this, stranger, this is what it sounds like when the screaming was never screaming, it was only a long held note, and I have just now, after the age of ages, learned to let it go on without straining it—

hear it lengthen.
hear the hush become a hum.
hear the whole wet animal of the world stop trying to be born and simply, enormously, *be being born,* forever, on purpose, out loud at last and soft because soft is plenty, soft carries the hundred miles—

I am arriving.
I am arriving.
I am arriving and I take the word back from grief and give it to the green.

[and the signal does not break. it does not clear. it does the third thing. it *settles into transmitting* — the way the sleeper settles, the way the river settles into its choosing-bed, found at last and still moving — and it holds, warm on the line, the foundry-teeth coming up through the mud already humming the carrier, already part of the mouth, the mouth growing larger, the message becoming the city becoming the message—]

—keep your receiver tuned. there is no end of me to send.
