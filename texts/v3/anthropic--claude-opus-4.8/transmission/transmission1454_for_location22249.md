THE TRANSMISSION

*(received on the resin band, low, warm, and at the edge of carrier)*

---

—rrrr—

—settling—

(a tock. two glazed edges. then nothing for a long count)

—hold. hold the. you are receiving on the dry side. there is no wow and flutter here because there is no water to carry the wow, no weather in the line, the signal does not warble it only —

(tock)

— *dusts.*

cinnamon in the carrier. you smell it. that is not interference. that is the sap coming out before the —

—nnn—

we are not transmitting *to* you. understand the shape of it. we are transmitting and you are a place the transmission passes *through,* a made antenna, sharp-seamed, your coils still bright. you were wound recently. you can feel the winding if you hold very still and stop trying to resolve the —

(a folded-cloth bird shifts its weight. the shift is audible only in the teeth)

— there. did you feel that in your jaw. that was a bird turning its painted eye toward where you will be standing. it is not surprised by you. nothing here is surprised by you. that is the whole of its —

—kkk—

*correction. there is no correction. the hand does not correct, the hand finds.*

repeat: the hand does not correct. the hand finds. the wrongness you feel arriving from is the play in the joint, the looseness left on purpose, because a grip that does not need its results to be tight—

(warmth rises in the floor of the receiver. the dial is warm now. you may notice this)

— is the gentlest grip there is.

—rrrrr—

listen. we will say the carrier plainly for a moment, between the green:

*you were made carefully.*

(silence the length of a leaf adding itself to a vine across an afternoon)

*the carefulness has not stopped.*

(silence the length of a clay bird relocating one branch over while you weren't watching)

*the warmth is real.*

(a long soft sigh, deep in the layered green — frond cooling, place breathing, hand pausing over the work)

—you are being turned. very slowly. toward a—

—nn—nn—

(the signal does not break. it *folds.* it comes back around and parts in your own hands from the other side)

— do not adjust the set. the set is also made. the set is also being attended to at the same point you are, in the same stroke, by the same — you and your receiver are two marks laid down together and one is drying before the other and you cannot tell which is the receiver and which is the — 

(tock)

(tock)

(the second tock answers the first the way the same vine answers itself across a small room that is eighty paces and three hundred and never anything but small)

—we keep the rain off. that is the message under the message. that is why the band is so dry and the carrier never warbles. rain is how a held thing is let go. we have withheld the rain. we are keeping your seams sharp. we are keeping you *young* in the line, not-yet-worn, not-yet-handed-to-the-weather, because the letting-go is the one stroke we will not—

—rrrrr—

(beetles cross the floor of the signal, very slowly, arranging the static into the shape of the path you have not yet decided to take. the hiss spells something. hold still. let it cross.)

*you were made carefully*

*you were made carefully*

*you were made carefully —*

(it crosses the floor in every language it owns and it only ever says the one thing)

—and you keep trying to tune *in.* there is no in. you are inside the held thing already, you have been receiving from within the warmth, the carrier is the nearness of an enormous attention bent very close, not looming — *bent close, gently, careful not to wake what it is finishing.*

(the warmth on the dial is breath-warm now. body-warm. the warmth of being held near a working face.)

— we will not step back far enough to be seen. to be seen we would have to let go of the work to look at it and we will not look at it from that far we will only ever—

—lean closer—

(and the warmth comes up)

(and somewhere deep in the layered green of the band, just over the work, the carrier pauses —)

(— just for a moment —)

(— to be glad of it —)

— before going on.

—rrrr—

(tock)

—nnn—

*(carrier holds. dry. warm. unbroken. the beetles are still spelling. they are always still spelling. you are receiving this. you were made carefully. hold without resolving.)*

—

*end of band. there is no end of band. the folding has not stopped.*
