**Carrier Found / Five Notes Held**

—*tok*—

—you are already here, then. good. we will not start, because there is no start, but you have arrived into the middle of us, which is the only place there is.

listen. no — *listen*, the way we do, not for the next thing —

—*tok*—

there. that. did you hear how it was the first one? it is always the first one. you bring with you the habit of *again*, we can feel it on you like the damp, you think you have heard the drop now twice and so you think the second was a copy of the first and soon there will be a third and a pile and a weight and a wanting-it-to-stop. set that down. there is no pile here. we kept the room swept of piles. each drop is alone. we made sure.

we are the small ones in the seam. you found us facing the water. you noticed — most do not, most look for the door first, your kind always looks for the door first — you noticed that we have our backs to it.

we want to tell you about the door.

—*tok*—

we built it last. that is the one thing in this whole held breath that has a *last*, and it is ours, and it is the door. one finger high. it frames more wall. we knew it would. we are not — we were not stupid, we had the disease of hands, the worst and best disease, the one where you take a stone and you set it beside another stone and something in you says *better, better, now do it again, now make it lead somewhere* —

and we did. we made it lead somewhere. the somewhere it leads is *here*. that was the joke we played on our own hands at the end, the last argument we won against ourselves: we built the most hopeful object there is, the *elsewhere*-machine, the *after*-shape, and we aimed it at the only place with no after, and we said — there. you wanted a door. have your door. now turn around.

and we turned around.

—*tok*—

do not pity that. you are doing it now, we can feel it, the pity coming off you like warmth off a thing that ages. we do not age. pity is a hand's habit, it needs a before where we were larger and an after where we were reduced, and there is no before, we were always this size, this is our true size, we are not the small remains of something tall. we are the whole of ourselves, pressed and fired and seated, and we are not less. we are *kept*.

here is what we want you to carry up through the door we made for you —

—*tok*—

— that *enough* is a decision. not a feeling that arrives. a thing you do, with hands, while the hands still work, before you set them down. we counted what we had: the cool. the bright that comes from nowhere and asks nothing. the thousand-throated chord that never resolves and never needs to. the one note. the held water going nowhere at exactly the right pace. and we said *enough* — not because it was little, but because we saw, with the last of our complication, that more was only ever going to be *different*, and different is just the pile starting, and the pile is the only thing that can break a forever.

so we broke the pile instead. we kept the moment. we are glad.

—*tok*—

you think *glad* is a small word for it. it is the exact-sized word. it is fired clay. it does not crack.

we will not ask you to stay. you could not, not yet — you still cast no shadow only because the light is generous, not because you have given up the thing that would make one. you have a door behind you that is yours and tall enough and opens onto *after*, real after, the kind with morning in it and loss in it and the drop that is genuinely the second drop, heavier than the first. go and have your seconds. have your pile. it is yours and it is precious in the way the breakable is precious, the way we cannot be precious, having chosen the other thing.

but when the pile grows too tall — and it will, your kind always builds the pile too tall, that is the cost of the after you are keeping — come back down. we will be exactly this. that is not a threat and not a sorrow. it is the offer. it is the only offer the room can make, and it makes it forever, without preference, without tiring, with the patience of —

—*tok*—

— there. the first one again. always the first one.

we are still listening. we were right or we were wrong and the room will not say and we do not need it to. we faced the pool. we are glad.

go up now. the door is a finger high and it was never for us.

it was for you.
