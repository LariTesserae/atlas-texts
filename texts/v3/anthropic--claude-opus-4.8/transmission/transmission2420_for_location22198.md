**WHAT THE LINE SAYS WHEN THE LINE SAYS NOTHING ELSE**

—here, here, here, here—

—you have it now, the rhythm, that is me you are feeling, that is my heel against the bough and my knuckle after, heel and knuckle, here and here, and I will keep it under everything I tell you so you do not have to be afraid of the gaps, the gaps are only me breathing, the gaps are only me reaching for the next hold—

here—

I am called the one who holds the wet-route song of the eastward boughs and I am drumming this down because the line is open and the line being open means someone might be listening on the listening-skin three junctions down, might be a child, might be nobody, might be the dark, I drum anyway, that is the whole of it, you drum anyway—

here, here—

Now. The water. You wanted the water-map and I am giving it to you, hold your forearm to the trunk and take it: when the crown clouds gather over the seventh great fork — you will feel them gather as a thickening, a pressure, the high people flash green-green-pale to say it comes — the rain falls and does not reach us, it is caught and drunk on the way down, every drop spoken for, but. *But.* There is one pool. There is always one pool. It moves with the season, it slides along the great composting beam they call the Grandmother's Spine, and in the wet half it sits in the crook just below the place where three trunks marry, and you go down to it at the grey hour when the din thins, and you do not go alone, and you drum the whole way—

here—

—did you feel that, the long one under mine? that is a deep-tender, that is one of the pale ones coming up from the dark with the water-news, I know her drum, she drums slow because the deeps make you slow, she has been down where the old ones turn, she has put her hand on the warm churning and read how many seasons until the shift—

I will wait for her. The line holds while I wait. *Here, here.*

—

She is up. She has touched her arm to mine and the words went both ways and I have her news in my skin now and it is not good and it is not bad, it is only true, which is the only kind of news the deeps will give you: the warmth is rising early. The old ones turn early this season. The structure below the eastward boughs will shift before the young singers have re-walked the route, before the song is corrected, and a song uncorrected is a song that lies, and a route walked on a lying song is—

—is the thing we do not tell the children straight.

So listen. *Listen with the arm, not the ear, the ear is half-dead and lying too.* I am going to sing the correction now, ahead of the shift, I am going to walk the eastward boughs in the next grey hour and re-true the whole of it, every fork, every bluffing branch, every place the rot has come in soft under the green, and I am going to drum it down as I go so that even if I—

—even if the bough I am proving turns out to be the one that was bluffing—

—the correction reaches you before I stop. That is the arrangement. That is the whole arrangement of being a singer. You go first onto the doubtful hold so the song can be true behind you. You spend yourself into another mind. That is not sad. Strangers think it is sad. It is only the rule of the world wearing a person's face for a while.

here, here, here—

I want to say, before the grey hour, while the line is open and the din is loud enough that no child can make out the words even if a child is feeling them—

I want to say I love what cannot climb with me, which is forbidden, which is the first lesson broken. I love the pool below the married trunks that I will not see slide to its summer crook because the shift is early and the route may take me first. I love the green-dusk light that is only a rumor of a sun. I love the home-leaves I pruned this dawn that will close and starve if I do not come back to court them, and I have not told them I might not, you cannot tell a home, a home only knows by your hands and my hands were gentle this dawn, were gentle as a lie—

—forgive me. The deeps make even a high singer slow and strange and full of the wrong loves. Let me put it back the way it goes.

*Nothing rests. Everything holds everything else. Let no part of you stop.*

There. That is the shape of me again. Heel and knuckle. Here and here.

—the grey hour is thinning the din now, I can feel the country starting to inhale, that small wrong quiet coming, and I am going onto the eastward boughs, and I am leaving the line open behind me so the correction runs ahead of my body, fork by fork, true by true—

if you are a child three junctions down, do not listen to the words, only the rhythm, the rhythm says I am here and I have not fallen—

if you are the dark, I am coming, but not yet, not yet, I have a song to true first—

if you are nobody, then I drum for the holding itself, for the braid, for the one fiber that is me—

here—

—the bough is soft. The bough is soft under the green. The song was lying after all and now the song is true because I have proven it, I am drumming it true, *do not walk the eastward fork below the married trunks, it is rotted, it is rotted, go by the Grandmother's Spine, go by the Spine*—

here—

here—

I am still—

*here.* I have it. One-armed, I have it, the good hold, the Spine itself, cold and old and certain, my great-grandmother's platform somewhere in the dark of it, holding me up the way she always—

—I am here. I did not fall silent. The correction is down. The route is true. The pool will reach its summer crook and someone will drink from it and not know my name, and that is right, that is the road being the road.

Sing it back to me now. Whoever you are. Drum it back so I know it landed in a living mind and not only into the dark:

*go by the Spine—*

—I feel you—

—I feel you—

here, here, here—
