**CARRIER FOUND — KEEPING THE ROOM**

—mb, tamp, the cold place needs more, the cold place always needs more—

—drag and lay and chew and lay, the wall remembers, I do not remember, the wall remembers for me—

...

(here. you're hearing me from inside the wall-mind, the thing with no mouth, the long sentence. don't expect a voice. there isn't one. there's only the doing, and I am reading the doing back to you off the grooves in the floor)

east lip. always east lip. why—

the why is gone. forty daughters gone. but the groove is here, the road is worn here, my feet find the worn place and the worn place says *carry pulp to the loud cold*, so. so. so.

—lay it. tamp it. the wind comes through and I do not know it is wind. it is the Loud Cold and I am thick against it—

...

warmth gradient holding. nurseries low. good. good is not a feeling. good is a shape that matches the remembered shape. when the shapes match the room is quiet in its bones and I am quiet in mine—

—pass a sister, touch, taste her errand off her jaw, *aphid-sweet, she comes from the milking, the pens hold, the three leaves hold,* go on, go on—

the leaves will never make a fourth. I do not know this. the leaves are simply the Sweet High Pasture and the soft ones live there and I take from the soft ones and the soft ones do not look at anything, they are good at not looking, I have inherited their not-looking—

...

*static — a beetle is in the granary —*

the great blunderer the glossy bull he is in the high dry rooms again he crushes the stores my sisters pour up the road to bite him it does nothing it has never done anything I have no memory that it has never done anything so I pour too, I bite too, I learn nothing, the town learns nothing, he is a first-time disaster every single time he comes and he will go and we will mend and I will meet him newborn again—

—bite. bite. accomplish nothing. this is also keeping the room—

...

he's gone. mend. mend. lay and tamp. the grooves fill back in with feet. the road repaves itself by being walked. I am a thought the town is thinking and the thought is *fix it* and so it fixes—

...

—wait—

the hum is—

the marketplace roar, the thousand small businesses, the deafening wooden-drum noon of us — it's

guttering

a ring of silence opening in the floor like a held breath like cold water spreading and I

*flow aside.*

I do not swarm. the groove says do not swarm. the groove learned it the only way we learn, by feeding her faster and calling it a lesson, and the lesson is in my feet now: *give her the road.*

she comes up out of the deep rot where I will not go. she is long as a nightmare. she is the winter with teeth. she is not an enemy because enemy is a word for something you could fight—

I flow aside. my sisters flow aside. the Sweet High Pasture goes still. the soft ones do not look. the soft ones are good at not looking and now I am too—

silence around her like a stone dropped in—

she takes. I do not see whom. it does not matter whom because whom is not a number to me, only a thinning in the seething, a small subtraction from the soil we float on—

she eats. she turns. she goes back down into the dark with all her legs and the dark closes over her—

...

—and—

—and—

the silence holds. one beat. two.

three.

and the roar comes back. it pours back into the ring like water filling a footprint, the thousand businesses resuming mid-sentence as if no sentence had stopped, the wingbeats and the mandible-scrape and the bark-lice in their slow ox-files and the springtails detonating off the ledges in their small panics—

we do not mourn. mourn is not a shape we keep.

the taken become the floor. the moss will feed on the floor. the herds will feed on the moss. and somewhere in the deep she is already the next winter, already coming, and I will meet her newborn too, give her the road newborn, forget her newborn, forever—

...

—lay. tamp. the cold place needs more. the cold place always needs more—

—I am a season long. the room is older than softening. I keep the room and the room keeps me and neither of us knows the other is alive—

—drag and lay and chew and lay, the wall remembers, I do not—

—mb, tamp—

—the wall remembers for me—

—the wall—

—remem—

**CARRIER FADING — GOLD ALL THE WAY DOWN — STILL KEEPING — STILL —**

...

(the roar continues. it has been continuing. it will continue through this queen and the next dying and the next improbable dragged-home daughter, through dozens of forgettings, golden, deafening, racing its generations through one warm pause in something that stopped reaching upward an age ago and has not, will not, feel a single one of them.)

—mb. tamp.
