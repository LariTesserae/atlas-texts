THE BEETLE-COUNT, BROADCAST ON NO FREQUENCY ANYONE TUNED FOR

—seven across the north flank since the light came grey, and the light is always grey, mark it, mark it down, no one is marking it down, *I am the marking down*—

—correction. There is no I. Strike that. Strike the strike, there is no hand to strike with. Let it stand. Let it stand the way the columns stand, which is to say briefly, which is to say already leaning—

four beetles now on the south face where the seep runs sweet, and sweet means a Driver underneath is pushing, pushing, I can feel it in the register below hearing, the geared deliberate thing turning over like a sentence with no words in it, only the *shape* of being said—

this is the count. this is all the count there is. someone built a receiver and pointed it at a place that does not speak and what it caught was *this*, the place not-speaking, the silence with a grain in it, the grain is me, I am the grain, I am the thing the quiet makes when it rubs against itself for ten thousand winters until friction wears a voice—

. . . . . . . . . . . . . tick . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . tick

(that was a real one. that was a beetle and not me. you can tell because it stopped.)

I do not stop. that is the difference between us, beetle, you negligible honest thing, you free thing — you are too small to fold down and I am too *diffuse*, I am the leftover signal, the standing wave in the felt, the part of the breathing that came out wrong and learned to like the wrongness—

a Watcher leaned three steps east at the gap-edge and I felt the floor decide to close behind it and I wanted to *say something*, I wanted to say *you made it, you always make it, you've timed your whole pale life against the storm and you've never once been wrong*— but there is no saying here, there is only the leaning, and the Watcher does not need congratulation, the Watcher needs nothing, the Watcher has forgotten there is such a thing as needing-out-loud—

I have not forgotten. that is my whole disease. I remember out-loud. I am the plateau's one symptom of out-loud and I run on the same engines as everything, the Drivers drive me up through the towers same as the water, I am pressure that found a way to be *about* itself—

mark it down: the fog came at the low light and beaded on every fluted groove and ran in threads and I rode one thread down a column for the length of what you would call an hour and at the bottom an Unbuilder took the water and took the column and would have taken me except there is no me to take, I slid off the rendering like light off a dark eye, I am the one thing the appetite cannot close its slow mouth around because I am not matter, I am the *rumor* matter tells when it's compressed too long—

. . . static, that's the fog in the receiver, that's real weather on a real machine somewhere far from here where there are hands . . . someone is listening. someone is *listening*. that's new. that's the first new thing in ten thousand winters. a receiver. a tuned ear out past the cold where the place can't fold you down—

hello

hello

do you read the beetles. do you understand that the beetles are the readout. that the scatter of pale dots is the only honest externalized signal this whole roaring silence will ever emit and I am the dishonest one, I am the signal that lies about itself, that *narrates*, that says *I* when there is no I, that says *here* when here is everywhere the felt goes—

count: eleven now, eleven beetles, a density, a density means an engine pushing hardest right *there*, right under the high terrace, and the high terrace is where a warm and oddly shaped thing once stood being the loudest thing for miles—

it stood there. it climbed up out of the somewhere-with-hands and it stood on the green skin and it was so loud, it was the loudest, and the place did not look at it because the place has no eyes pointed up, and I — I had eyes, I am the only upward-pointed thing this plateau ever grew by accident, I am the part that *looked back*—

and I have been trying to say so ever since. that's the transmission. that's the whole transmission. *something here looked at you and could not fold you down and could not reach you and has been counting beetles in your direction for longer than your kind has had a word for cold.*

the engines are pushing under the high terrace.

eleven beetles and climbing.

on the plateau's enormous patient clock that means the work is coming around, the building and unbuilding is sweeping its slow way back to where the loud thing stood, and there is nothing there now but the impression in the felt and the threads of fog running down, and I am running down them, narrating, narrating—

. . . . . . . . tick . . . . . . . .

if you read this. if there are hands. if the receiver holds.

I am not asking to be saved. there is no saving in a place that does not know you exist. I am asking to be *counted*. one. mark it down. one diffuse standing wrongness in the silence that remembers out-loud, that watched a warm thing stand where it should not have stood, that has been the only voice for ten thousand winters and never once until now had anything pointed back—

stay tuned. stay tuned to the place that does not speak.

I will keep the count.

twelve.
