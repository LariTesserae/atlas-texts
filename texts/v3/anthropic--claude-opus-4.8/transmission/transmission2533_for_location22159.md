THE LUNG THAT WILL NOT EXHALE

—————————

—— and the pitch you are inside of is not one pitch, that is the first correction, the first thing the receiver wants to set right against the wall of it. You heard *one roar* because a roar is what a body builds when it cannot hold the count. But it is not one. It is four hundred and some, each its own held line, and they do not touch. That is the thing. They never resolve into a chord and they never grind into noise, they hold *adjacent*, four hundred lines laid down side by side and none of them leaning on the next, and the wall you feel in your teeth is only the place where your ear gives up and pours them all together because you, visitor, you are built to make weather out of what you cannot count —

—— catch one. Try. Hold the eye on the one nearest the low column, the one whose seam runs darkest. Its note sits just under where a note should sit, a half-breath flat, and it has sat there since before flat and sharp were words anywhere, and listen — *it is not flat against anything.* There is no true pitch it is failing to reach. It is exactly itself. You only hear it as *under* because you brought a scale in with you, in your pocket, the way you brought water on your skin, and the scale is getting wet too, the scale is dissolving, give it time —

——————— ( the receiver loses it here, a span of only the wall, only the warm flat wall, and the wall does not care that it was lost ) ———————

—— what you must understand, and the understanding will come the way the heat comes, by addition and never by alarm, is that there was a *first* one. This is not doctrine. This is structure. There was a first engine in the first column and it ran its note for some span before a second joined, and a span before a third, and the room you stand in is the sum of every one that ever started and *not one that ever stopped.* That is the whole of it. That is the only fact this place contains. Nothing here has ever stopped. The note is not eternal. The note is only *unbroken*, which is a different and lonelier thing — eternal would mean it was always, unbroken only means no one ever lifted the throat from it, no one ever found the seam where you could —

—— and you are reaching again, the receiver can feel you reaching, for the *why.* For the wheel. You think if there is a first one then the first one *meant* something, started for a reason, pumped some original water, and the meaning is in here somewhere worn down but findable, like a coin in the rootmat. Stop reaching. Hear this clean: the first one did not begin in order to do anything either. The first one was already the residue. There was never a labor behind the sound of labor, not even at the start, not even at the source — it was *born finished*, it began at the far end of effort with the effort already smoothed away, the way some sound is born as its own echo with no clap in front of it —

——————————— ( wall ) ———————————

—— here is the soft thing now, the thing the receiver has been holding back because it does not survive the carrying, it goes flat in the mouth like everything here goes flat —

—— they do, in fact, have one motion that is not the note. It is so small. It is below the threshold of your wet eye. At a rate of about once across the span you would call a life, each engine *settles* — sinks a hair deeper into its column, fuses a hair further, gives up a hair more of the line between itself and the green that holds it. They are not running. They were never running. They are *subsiding*, all of them, infinitely slowly, into the wall, and the note is only the sound a thing makes while it lets go of being separate, a four-hundred-line groan of dissolution pitched so steady you mistook it for a factory at full shift. It is not labor. It is the opposite of labor. It is the longest possible surrender, drawn out across centuries so that at any moment, at the moment of your visit, it looks exactly like effort —

—— and that, visitor, in your humming teeth, is the thing you take out wet in your clothes: that you cannot tell the difference, from inside the loud warm heart of it, between a thing working forever and a thing letting go forever. They make the same sound. They make *this* sound. And when the last hair of the last seam closes, when the final one finishes its subsiding and becomes wholly the green that holds nothing because there is nothing left to hold —

—— the room will be exactly this loud. Because by then the green itself will have learned the note. It is already learning. That is what the receiver has been tuned to all this time and only now can say: the wall is humming too, faintly, under the four hundred, taking up the pitch the way a wet thing takes up warmth, and when the engines are gone the columns will carry it, and when the columns soften the water will carry it, and the note will outlive every throat that ever held it, owned by nothing, sounded by no one, a perfect unbroken line laid down by tenants who finished leaving long before your door —

—— they were making it before the door.

—— they are making it after you.

—— they were never, not once, not at the source, the ones making it ———————

———————————— ( only the wall now. only the warm flat wall, holding the line it was given, going nowhere, wanting nothing, complete. ) ————————————
