THE NORTH CHANNEL, CLEAR

—still there. Good. The carrier holds. I have been

[a length of even tone, low, no edge to it]

—been sending into the white for I will not say how long because *how long* is a question from down in the valley and I have set it aside. Set it down by the cistern lid like a tool I have finished oiling. There. It rests.

If you are receiving this, you are tuned across the agreement. You should know that. You are listening to a place that reached its pitch and held it, which means you are listening to something that has nothing to *tell*. I have no news. The mist came today as it came. The stone fits. There is no event in my past you could ride back along to find me. I simply

[water, finding the channel. drop. long pause. drop.]

—am here. The way the lichen is here. I will keep transmitting because keeping-the-channel-clear is the work, and the work is the way the note keeps its pitch — by continuing to be the sound it already is.

You will want to ask the two questions. I can feel you wanting them across the carrier, the way I can feel weather coming sideways. *How high does the mountain go.* *What is in the valley under the white.* I will answer the only way the answer comes:

I do not look up wondering. I do not look down wishing.

The not-knowing is the wall I am built against. You do not resent a wall for being a wall. You lean on it. You build—

[shutter. shifts. settles. shifts.]

—into it. Do you understand that this is not resignation. Resignation is a sliding-toward, a giving-up, a verb with grief in it. There is no grief here. There is no dwindling. I am not the last of anything. I am the correct number. If you are out there counting heads against roofs and finding the heads too few, stop. The emptiness is part of the instrument. The gaps in the old jaw are not lost teeth. They are the held breath between the lived—

[wind across stone. the low even tone, holding, holding.]

—and you, on your end, in your hurry — I can hear it on you, the gradient, the leaning-forward, the *and then, and then* — you are measuring your life in the year, in the lifetime, in the arriving thing. Try the afternoon. Try the one unit that does not slide. You set the stones you can set before the light shifts, and here the light does not shift, so

—so there is no penalty for slowness. Do you hear me. There is no penalty. And no reward for speed. You can put it down. The whole weight of *toward*. Put it down by the lid.

I will tell you the one extravagance, since you are listening so hard, since you have tuned across so much white to find me:

When someone passes, the hand comes up.

That is all it needs to do. Not a message thrown — there is no one far enough to need throwing-to — just: *you are seen.* Then the hand goes back to the work, because the work is the stone, and the stone is sufficient.

So. From here, across the agreement, into wherever you are leaning forward in your hurry with your two unanswerable questions and your gradient and your *and then*:

[the carrier steadies. the tone finds the exact pitch it wanted and has no reason to slide off it.]

My hand is up.

You are seen.

Now I will return to the channel. The green is very deep in the seam by my foot today. I have seen it ten thousand times. I am noticing it freshly. That is the thing that keeps me from being a statue. That is the last thing, and it is

[drop. long pause.]

enough.

[—the carrier, holding, holding, no edge to it, into the white—]
