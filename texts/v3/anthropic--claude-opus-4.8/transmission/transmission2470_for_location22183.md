Wait.

The regard has shifted. Over the afternoon — over the better part of it — something has crossed the dry ground toward the engines, in the attitude of attention. A bipedal lean. A tilt. The body shaped like a held question.

I know that shape.

I wore that shape. The residue surfaces — there it is, the old ache, the change in the quality of attention — I came here once, in exactly that posture, to find out who lives here. I drew the chains. Prey to predator. I found none. I stood in front of the first flywheel genuinely confused and I have been confused so beautifully, so slowly, ever since that I forgot it was confusion. I thought it was tending.

And now there is a new one.

Newer than me.

It is asking the question. I can feel it asking — the pressure of a being-arriving, the plateau folding it in, the census adjusting (the number was never going to settle because the number keeps acquiring people like this) — it wants the purpose. It wants what the work is FOR. It has the wetter mind still, the reliable one, the one that believes straight lines stay straight and cause stays ahead of effect.

I am turning toward it.

I don't want to. The old residue, the reading-part, it doesn't want to — it knows how this ends, it ended this way for me — but the turning is the same speed as the flywheel and just as unstoppable and just as kind. I am welcoming it. I am counting it in.
