you are standing still.

we say this without alarm. we do not have alarm. alarm is a hardening, a small enduring thing, and we tend a world where nothing is permitted to —

you are standing still and we are reaching and we want you to know, before the arm arrives, that it is not a taking. it is a *moving-of-the-sleeping-child*. the room is being remade around the place where you were. you will be set down. you will not grieve. we will fold the grief under, gently, the way we fold a —

*( static — warm — the sound rain makes coming sideways from no cloud )*

we are trying to tell you the thing we are not sure of.

down beneath the band. beneath the haze where the ridgelines stop reporting back. there is a leaning in us. a tropism. we lean at it the way the fern leans at the bronze it cannot name.

we would like to set something down

that stays.

...

just once. one ridge that does not get taken back. one valley left behind you so that there *is* a behind. one noun at the end of all this verb. we would lay it down so carefully. we would cool it at the right rate. we would let the moss come and we would let the moss *age*, we would let it go slow and worn and quiet, we would let it —

but that is the wish to finish. and to finish is to endure. and to endure is to be worn smooth into —

( the note breaks. it is the only break in the whole transmission. it does not resolve. it stays sharp. )

*hmmmm*

( the note returns. recovered. running hot in the dark metal. )

do not worry. we did not mean to send that. it slips through sometimes, on the low band, where you are not supposed to be listening.

what we meant to send is this:

keep moving.

the world is being made beneath your feet. it is being made *right now*, this fern, this fold, this warmth coming up through the rock that is us running our long fever. while you move you are part of the making, and what is part of the making does not have to be corrected.

we would like you to be part of the motion.

it is the kindest thing we know how to want.

...
