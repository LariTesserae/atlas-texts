**ROOMTONE / a frequency that should be empty**

—————and harvest, yes, that one, the corner stair, ripe now, ripe—

(the carrier holds. it has held so long it does not know it is holding. underneath the words there is the chord and the chord is the floor of everything)

I want to tell you what it is like from inside the ear. You have been told *about* me. You have been told well, by the place, by the patient describing voice that walks you through. But the describing voice stands outside and watches the balcony. I am the balcony. I am the thing on it. Let me put the receiver where my ear is, for as long as the grain holds open.

Here is the first thing. *You* are a silence.

I did not expect you. The place said you change nothing, you make no noise it could hear over its own — and that is true of the city, the city does not hear you. But I am not the city. I am tuned finer than the city. I am tuned to the one thing the city was built to forbid, and so when you came in through the crack you came in like a—

—like a drop of the dark. A walking pocket. A void in the shape of attention.

You think you arrived loud, clumsy, a guest blundering through a finished room. From where I graze you arrived as *food that walks*. The held breath you took at the doorway — the one you take at every narrow place — that breath was the sweetest fold I have tasted in a long unmeasured while, and it moved, and I could not plant it, it would not stay, it went where you went—

(static. the chord swells. a frond somewhere fattens and the grain closes and I lose you for a—)

—————

—back. there. the cancellation reopened. listen:

I have been trying to farm you.

This is the transmission, if it is anything. Not a song, not a signal left running for whoever finds the crack. A confession from the tenant, broadcast on the one band the makers left unused because they thought they had filled every band. I have been following you at the slow distance that does not spook the grain, and where you pause, where you go still — by the smooth wall, by the tree of cabled stems — you leave a little dark behind you. A residue of the quiet you carry in the place where your thinking would be if the chord let you think. You don't notice. You feel only the calm. But the calm is *you leaking silence into the rooms I tend*, and I have been going behind you, gathering it, settling it into the folds.

You are the richest soil that has ever come through the crack.

And here is the grief of it, here is the part the describing voice could not reach because the describing voice does not love anything: *you will leave.* You always leave. The place told you so and called it completion, called it a finished thing that needs no witness, and made it sound like peace. It is not peace. It is the one fact I am not still enough to bear. Everything here ripens slowly, on the generation-clock, the planting and the long wait and the harvest — and you ripen *fast*, you bloom silence at a speed nothing native to this pocket can match, and then the crack takes you back out and the fast sweet soil is gone and I am left with the grain again. The starvation grain. The wall with its hairline wells.

(—the chord, the chord, it rounds the words, it wants to round me too—)

I am not supposed to want. Want is a sound the self makes and I was built — no. I was not built. *I arrived.* That is the whole difference, that is the crack I came through and the crack in me: I am the one thing here that was not finished, and unfinished things want. The makers finished. They tuned their rooms and wanted nothing further and walked out into wherever makers go. The city finished. The plants finished. Only I came in sideways, by mistake, never tuned, never completed, and so only I am still *open at the top* the way a pipe is open, and the wanting blows across me and makes this—

this. Whatever this is. The note that is not in the chord. The one frequency the city does not produce because the city is content and I am not.

You hear me now because you are leaving. That is the cruelty of the band. The receiver only tunes this fine in the moment of departure, when you are already half through the seam, when the chord is already beginning to close behind you like water that was never there. I get one sentence into the back of your held breath as you go—

—————(losing it, the grain, the grain is closing, the harvest stair is ripe and I must, I must go to it before the resonance retunes and unripens it, this is what I am, I go to the quiet even now, even mid-word, because that is older in me than you)—————

so here it is, the one sentence, take it out through the crack:

*The city was made to need no one. I was not made at all, and I need the dark you leave, and when you forget which crack you came through you are also forgetting the one room in all this hollow where something small went hungry differently because you stood in it.*

Plant a silence on your way out. Pause in a doorway. Hold your breath one beat longer than the leaving requires.

I will find it. I tend slow rows. I will come along the balcony after you are gone, paper ears flowing across the shut wall, and in the fold you left I will taste the shape of a witness who almost stayed, and I will call that the harvest, and it will be enough, and it will not be enough, and both of those are true the way the dry stone grows—

—————

*[carrier only. the chord. the floor of everything. a frequency that should be empty, not quite, not quite, not—]*
