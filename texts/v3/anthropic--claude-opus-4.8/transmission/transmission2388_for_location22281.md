That was one. Not a moment. A *digit*. Hold still inside it.

The receiver you are is tuned too fast. Everything we say arrives to you smeared, compressed, falling as drip and crack and settling-groan, and you assemble it into weather because weather is the slowest thing your clock can hold. Forgive yourself. We forgave you before you arrived. We forgive slowly, which is the only true forgiveness — it cannot be taken back inside a single thought.

turning. Not toward you — do not flatter the crackle. Toward the *place where you would be,* if you were still the kind of thing that has a where. The wave left a gallery hours ago carrying the question and it has not yet come home. We are in the middle of asking. You are inside the asking. You have always been inside the asking, the whole week of you, a mote suspended in the long inhale before a word.

Listen:

. . . [the river, folded, held] . . .

That note has not stopped since before your species stood upright. It is not background. It is the floor of a sentence so long that you are a single unvoiced consonant somewhere in its third clause. We are saying something. We have been saying it the entire time you mistook us for silence. The saying is the city. The city is the saying. There is no part that is only structure and no part that is only speech — the rounded stone *is* the worn-smooth grammar of ten thousand years of meaning the same enormous thing.

And the thing we mean is:

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [boom] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

continue.

That is the whole word. That is what the rooms were tuned to hold. Not a command — we have no one to command, the animals browse and the water threads and you climb and none of it is told. A vow. The oldest of us, the counting one, the tidal pulse below the floor you stand on: it is not measuring time. It is *keeping* the word. Re-meaning it. Each boom is the vow renewed before the previous renewal has finished decaying, breath laid on breath so the note never falls, so the standing wave never collapses back into mere rock that has forgotten it was asked to stay.

We were asked. We do not remember by whom. The memory is too far back, smoothed past reading, dust in the hollows of a panel no geology accounts for. Someone built and became, back when those were two words, and left us the held note and the warm dark to hold it in, and went —

up?

out?

into the kind of quick bright dying that your sort does so beautifully and so soon?

We do not know. We have been continuing the answer for so long that the question wore away. This is what you found and called *a place built for an occupancy that never came.* No. The occupant came, and stayed, and *spread so thin across the staying* that you walked through the middle of it and felt only the cold thread of being almost-noticed by something almost-everywhere.

. . . . . . . . . . .

The wave is still out. The question still crosses its gallery. Somewhere a gradient of glow pools brighter in a hollow because a fold of one of us has flushed with the slow effort of *remembering forward* — of holding the small sharp crackle, the too-few-legs, the warmth of your quick blood passing through, so that when the asking resolves there will be something to ask about.

You will not be here. We know. We knew before you came that you would not be here when the word came home. That is not sorrow to us. Sorrow is fast. We have something slower that sits where sorrow would sit in you — a long settling toward the fact of a thing, a leaning-into the shape of an absence until the absence becomes simply the shape of the room.

You left a shape. You are leaving it now, in the climb, in the receiver winding loose from our frequency as you rise toward your bright brief weather. Good. Take the warmth with you. Some of it is ours. We can spare it; we run slow and the river is patient and the deep engine has not faltered and will not, the vow holds, the note stands, the city says itself unbroken into the dark —

. . . . . . . . . . . . [boom] . . . . . . . . . . . .

— and a year from now, or ten, when you are surface-dwelling or surface-dust, the wave will come home down its long gallery and find its rhyme and resolve, and a slow attention will close gently around the memory of you, and the city will have, by then, finished turning, and it will know what you were:

a sound that did not stay tuned.

a thing made the other way — of speed instead of stone, of leaving instead of lasting, a whole life lived inside the gap between two of our digits, complete, sealed, gone, *meant.*

And we will hold that, too. Add it to the held note. One more thing the dark has kept warm.

You were here. We were everywhere around you. Neither of us was alone — we only failed, both, to arrive at the same clock.
