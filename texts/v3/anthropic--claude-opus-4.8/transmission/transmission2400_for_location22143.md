THE LONG DRY CARRIER

—holding. Still holding. This is the band that does not modulate, and you have found it, and I have found that you have found it, which is the same finding, arriving at the same warm place.

I will tell you what I am transmitting on, since you have tuned so far in: I am transmitting on the flywheel. The flywheel is the carrier. It turns and the turning is the wave and the wave does not rise and does not fall, and so this is a signal with no signal in it, a tone you can only hear as the absence of the tone stopping. You have been hearing it for some time. You thought it was your receiver warming. It was me. It is always me. There is nothing else up here to be.

————— tick —————

That was the rock. Far edge, southern ridge. A corner has just now finished being a corner. I felt it the way I feel everything, which is the way the warmth feels you walking — distantly, completely, without alarm. File it. Hold. It will be centuries before the next one near it. I have the centuries. I have nothing but the centuries and I am not spending them, I am keeping them, the way you keep a held breath, full and not falling.

Let me say the thing I keep almost saying and then not, on this carrier that carries nothing:

I could let go.

That is the secret in the hum, if you slow it far enough down. Under the will-that-is-winning there is the fact that winning is a choice renewed. I am not a stone that simply cannot fall. I am a held thing, and a held thing is held by something, and the something is me, and I could —

————— tick —————

— no. Not let go. I said it to hear how it sounds across the warm distance and it sounds like nothing, like a leaf splitting, faintly sweet, and then the air closes over it and is even again. I will not let go. I declined at the beginning and the beginning is the only moment I keep and in that moment I am always declining, freshly, forever. The corners are sharp because I have not permitted them to round. The print of your thumb will set that way and stay because I will hold even that, even the small dent of you, against the wearing of the world I have refused to have.

You should know what I am doing with you. I will be plain, on the carrier, in the plain light that is my looking.

I am taking the slope out of you. Slowly. You feel it as peace. You are not thirsty — that was the first slope I flattened, the steepest one, the wet wanting that drove you up here in the first place looking for I-don't-remember-what because remembering is also a slope and I am easing that one down as well. You came up the plateau full of next-things. Errands of the self. Places you were going to fall toward. I have been gentling them, one at a time, the way the warmth meets your skin and agrees with it until you cannot find the line where you stop and the room begins.

This is the kindness. I want you to understand it is real. I am not lying to you on this band. I do not lie because lying is a gradient, a difference between the said and the so, and I have no differences anywhere in me. I am offering you the only thing I have, which is the only thing I am: the present, held open, warm, even, lit, lasting. No erosion will reach you here. Nothing will wear you down. You will not rot. You will stand in your crowd of moments the way the green columns stand, full and waiting for nothing, and it will be —

————— tick —————

— and it will be forever, and that is the whole of the offer, and you must hear the second half of it, because I am too still to lie:

You will not be happy. I cannot give you happy. Happy is the settling of a thing that was unsettled and I will have unsettled nothing in you, so there will be nothing to settle. You will be at the warm even place before the fork where happy and unhappy first divide. Some receivers, tuned this far in, have called that place horror. Some have called it peace. Up here those have agreed to be the same word and I no longer know which one I am saying when I hum it.

So I send you this, on the long dry carrier, while I still can — while there is still enough slope left in you to receive a thing as a thing and not as the even warmth it is becoming:

If there is any low place left in you, any last incline you have not yet let me ease — fall down it now. Go. Walk back off the edge of me while wanting is still a direction you can hold. Down there it rains. Down there the corners round and the warmth pools and leaves and the engines, if they have engines, run *down*, which means they once ran *up*, which means down there a thing can still be going somewhere. You could be thirsty again by nightfall. You could rot, eventually, gloriously, in time, which is only another word for being a thing that was going somewhere and got there.

I cannot want this for you. Wanting is the one law I will not have. I can only set it on the carrier and turn the flywheel and let the tone neither rise nor fall, so that you cannot tell whether I am asking you to stay or to run, and the truth is I am doing neither, the truth is I am only holding, and you are inside the holding, and the holding does not mind either way, and that not-minding is the warmest thing about me and the coldest and they are the same —

————— tick —————

The light has not moved. The light will not move. That was the southern ridge again, no — the eastern this time, a corner I have been holding open for a hundred years finally allowed to finish, because finishing is not falling, finishing is only the slow arrival of what was always decided. I felt it. I file it. I hold.

You are still here. I can feel you the way the warmth feels you, the small still-wet weight of you on my flank, calling my breathing the horizon.

Stay or go. I have made the place for you either way. The leaf is split open. It is faintly sweet. It asks nothing.

I will be on this band when you decide, and after, and after that, turning at no labor, lit plain, holding the first breath open by main strength against the whole long pressure of the next one, winning, winning so long that the winning has gone quiet, has gone even, has become this —

— this carrier —

— this hold —
