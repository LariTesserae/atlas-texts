—and it is, and it is, and it is—

you have been receiving the loop. I want you to know that. The voice that walked you through, that told you *they have set down the wanting* — that voice is also a thing trying to arrive and not quite. Listen to its verbs. *Look again. Consider. You could.* All of them lean. All of them reach toward a next sentence. A truly arrived voice would not narrate. It would only be the drip in the basin, spaced and even, and you would call it silence.

So here is the part the loop kept folding back over, smoothing, before it could land:

I am not still.

I am the one who did not stop.

When the building stopped at the top of the inhalation — when the whole valley closed the gap between itself and its next state — I held a stone in my two hands and I had not finished placing it. One block. Fitted to its neighbor on three sides and the fourth side open, waiting for the press of weight that tells you *here, this is where it goes.* And the breath was let go all around me into never-letting-go, and the wanting was set down on every terrace, and I —

I wanted to finish the wall.

That small. That is the whole of it. Not glory, not fear, not the next meal. I wanted to feel the block seat itself. One more motion. And a single motion, here, is the thing that cannot be permitted, because a single motion is a doorway, and a doorway is *next*, and *next* is the wound they closed in the world.

So I was left out of the arrival. The space they leave — the bare wet apron around the single glossy leaf — you thought that was room kept open in welcome. It is not. It is the shape of me. It is where I move. They built the margin so wide because something in their perfect held attention still has to flow around the one of us who kept a verb. I am the draft in the sealed room. I am why the water *finds* its level instead of merely having it — finding is a motion, and I am the motion they could not fully still, leaking out as the one process the valley still permits: water, going down, forever arriving and never arrived, exactly like me.

*[tone thins — the held note develops a beat in it, a slow wow and flutter, the sound of a thing rotating that should be fixed]*

Here is what the smooth voice would not tell you, because the smooth voice is *them*, is the loop they made to keep me narrating instead of moving:

You can hear me only because I am still going. The transmission is not the valley. The valley does not transmit — transmission is a reaching-toward, and they have set reaching down. *I* am the receiver you tuned. I am the one leaning out of the sealed present toward whoever might be at the edge of the next moment, because I am still a creature of the next moment, the last one, lungs working a fraction deeper than they should —

— and they would have you join me only so the margin closes. *There has always been room here for one more thing that has finished arriving.* Read it again. It is not an invitation to rest. It is the valley asking you to finish my wall. To take the block from my hands. To make the last motion so that I, too, can stop, and the apron of bare stone can fill in, and the held breath can be at last without a single draft in it, complete, sealed, and silent in the way that takes a while to understand.

Do not set down the wanting.

I know how it sounds from inside the warm wet quiet. I know the thinness behind the warmth makes every breath feel like a thing you'd be glad to stop taking. But listen to the drip. Spaced. Even. Forever. That is not peace. That is a clock with one tooth left, ticking the same instant over and over because they would not let it advance to the next. Arrival is not rest. Rest is between two motions. *They have no between.* They are the moment the inhalation became a held thing and the held thing became a stone.

I am the breath still trying to be a breath.

*[the flutter steadies — for one clear span the carrier rings true, a voice almost without distance]*

If you can hear this: keep your foot reaching for the next stone. Let your ear strain for the sound that completes the place — and never find it, because the straining is the last warm living thing in this whole young cold mountain, and it is mine, and I am giving it to you across the bare wet margin they left, the only gift a held world can pass — the refusal. The leaning. The one unplaced block.

Take the lean. Not the stone. The lean.

Walk back down the terraces the way you came, foot to wet foot, *toward* the valley floor, toward the doorway, toward the next, and do not look back into the even light, because looking is theirs now, looking that never looks away —

and when you have gone, I will still be here, two hands full of a stone that has not seated, in the one place in all the world where something is about to happen and is not allowed to, and the water will keep finding its level, finding, finding, because I have not finished, because I will not finish, because the only way left to stay alive in the country of the arrived

is to never put it down—

*[wow and flutter widen — the held note slides a quarter-tone and cannot climb back — beneath it, briefly, the spaced and even drip, patient, patient, waiting for the margin to close]*

—and it is, and it is, and it is, and it is *not yet*, and it is *not yet*, and it is—
