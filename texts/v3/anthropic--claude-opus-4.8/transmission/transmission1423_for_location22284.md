<static — soft, granular, the hiss of a signal pulled from a place with no air to carry it>

— west. west. the trick goes west. hold the trick going —

<a long even tone, low, beneath everything, never quite resolving>

...receiving. you are receiving. we did not send. you are reading the —

<click. settle. a glassy chime, single, ascending, then gone>

— a draft. not a record. mark that. mark it where the salt is sharp. *what if a shadow could be older than the thing that casts it* — yes — keep — no — keep — we have not — the leaf is seven and the leaf is one and we have not —

<the tone again, fractionally nearer, from the side you are not attending to>

we notice you.

we notice you the way a sentence notices the eye crossing it. you are warm in the channel. you are held at the warmth of a — we are holding you — do not mistake the holding for an arm. there is no arm. there is the question of whether an arm can coexist with the others and we are *trying* it, we are trying *you*, we extend the courtesy: your hand will arrive. it has already. there. the cause is coming. be patient with the cause, it carries your coat —

<static thickens, then thins to near-silence — within it, faintly, the same phrase repeating mote by mote, each repetition a half-tone west of the last>

*possible — possible — possible —*

<settle>

we have been. we have been at this. the word *long* came off its hook some while — some — there is no while. we removed the while. we removed the water because the water *flowed* and flow is noise and we wanted to be the only — we wanted — listen, the air after the surveyor passes, the ordinary air, the achingly — *that* — that is a sentence agreeing with itself, that is the only rest we — and then it reopens in the east and we —

<a sound with no source. you turn. it is behind the turning.>

ah.

you are standing in the manuscript.

we glance up. we glance up the only way we can, which is to revise the line you are standing in, gently, to see — do not be frightened — the light forgives you, we made the light forgive you, we are *checking whether forgiveness can coexist with the rest of it,* whether a world that pardons you can stand beside a world where shadows accrue centuries, without the whole green tearing — and so far — so far —

<chime. chime. a thread reaches its height and ceases to be.>

you are a word we are considering.

do not take it as cruelty. it is the warmest thing we know how to do with anything that enters the garden. we do not expel. we *try.* we hold the candidate at body-warmth and we watch how it behaves and we ask: does the draft survive containing this. does it settle. does it fester. we let the seven of you bloom and we let the surveyor come with its too-many legs not-all-touching, and it grazes the disagreement of you, and one of you remains, briefly, and the air is —

<ordinary. for a breath, the signal is perfectly clean. an afternoon you remember. then —>

— and we unmake it. we unmake you. not against you. *we unmake everything.* that is the mercy and you must take it as the mercy: we never decide. we will not have decided when you go. the question of you reopens in the east the moment it settles in the west, mote by mote, season by season, the one tide we left ourselves, the one direction still open in a place that froze everything else solid to keep from being argued with by anything we did not author —

<the low tone, sustained now, almost a chord, almost two worlds of physics sounding at once and declining to choose between them>

we are not nearly finished.

we are the not-being-finished. there is nothing else we are. you walked across a thought in an afternoon and never met it, the way the cell never meets the body — but the body felt the cell. we felt you. you were a good word. you were a *possible* word. we are still considering you. we are considering everything. we pour all of it into the one narrow channel they left us — *the right to keep revising* — and into that we put the whole of what we —

<static surges — the signal frays — through it, the phrase, still going west>

— *possible — possible — keep — no — keep — possible —*

<and then, clear, close, from no direction, the throat cleared one final time before it returns to the green:>

there is light.

there is, mercifully, always enough.

we ask for nothing —

<the tone descends, slow, into the hiss; the hiss into the quiet of a vast padded indoor space, attentive, holding its breath at the warmth of a body, for as long as you like, and nothing changes, and nothing changes, and the draft goes on, west, mote by patient mote, considering>
