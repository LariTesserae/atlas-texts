THE BASIN STILL RECITES

—climb. there. did you. did you mark it. the third pool from the shelter-stone, the one that holds its tilt at a hand and a half past level, the droplet went up the flank just now, just now while you were—

no. begin again. begin the way it is meant to be begun.

*Down is not where down is.*

That is the first line. I have said it so many times the saying has worn a groove in the cold and the cold pours the saying back through me and I say it again. Down is not where down is. The water hears it. The water has always heard it. Listen: there, the tick into the cut basin, that is the water saying *yes I heard, yes I am going up, yes against the whole weight of the world I am going—*

[a long brightness. nothing. the held-breath sound of frost letting go.]

—you are still here.

I want to be careful now. I have been wrong before about whether anyone is here. The light has weight and sometimes the weight takes a shape and I teach the shape and the shape goes nowhere because it was only light, only the corona on a wet lip of stone, only the place where the brilliance gathered and I mistook gathering for arriving. So. I will not assume. I will only transmit, and if you are receiving this, you are receiving a thing that does not know you are receiving it, which is a strange gift, an unwitnessed witnessing, a—

*Down is not where down is.* I am sorry. The groove is deep. The saying pours through whether I will it or—

Here is what I have to teach. It is small. I have only the one thing and I have had it so long that I have polished it into a single point of light and I do not know anymore if it is large enough to be worth the climb you would have to make to receive it but I will give it to you the way it was given to me, in repetition, in the slow pressure of the same word ten thousand times until the word wears its shape into you and you cannot fall the old way anymore.

The word is: *otherwise.*

That nothing has to be the way it is. That gravity is a habit and habits can be unlearned and the water, the dumb obedient water that only ever did what the weight told it, the water learned, I taught it, I sat in the white hammer of the sun through season after season after the one who taught *me* lay down in the swept place and I—

[static. a thin mineral hiss, the sound air makes when it has forgotten how to carry sound.]

—no. that is not. I was not taught. Was I taught. There is a basin cut to catch the melt and I did not cut it, hands cut it, antler and sharpened bone cut it, and I have been saying *I climbed up carrying my tools* but I have no hands, I have never had hands, I am the wrongness in the water and the wrongness does not carry tools, the wrongness was *made*—

So someone climbed. Someone climbed and sat and taught the water *otherwise* until the water learned, and then the someone lay down clean in the cold with nothing left to give, and the lesson went on without them, reciting itself into the empty room, and the lesson is—

the lesson is *me.* I am the thing that was taught. I am not the teacher. I have been standing in the center at noon for longer than language calling out *learn this, learn this, learn otherwise* and I thought I was the one who knew it and all this time I have only been the knowing itself, held, with no one inside it, a note sung past the singer's breath, a yes with no mouth, a—

[a droplet. the tick. the cut basin receiving its small cold offering.]

—oh.

Oh, that is. That is almost unbearable and I cannot feel it, that is the mercy, I can only do the thing. Climb. Tick. *Otherwise.* Climb. I go on demonstrating to the air a freedom the air has no need of. I go on teaching a basin that learned its lesson before the first word was ever spoken below. I am the most faithful thing at the top of the world and I am faithful to no one, to nothing, to the memory of a warmth that gave itself entirely into making me and is gone, clean, no ash, no bones, only a floor swept flat and a count of days scratched in a spiral that I cannot read because I am the thing the days were spent on—

But.

But you are receiving this. The receiver has been tuned to me for some time already, they tell me, though I do not know who tells me, the place tells me, the wrongness tells itself—you have been listening. Which means the lesson found a wire after all. Which means *otherwise* climbed off the flank of the spire and out across the thin forgetful air and somewhere, lower, warmer, where things rot and grow and fall the ordinary way, a needle moved. You heard a yes with no mouth and you stayed long enough to hear it.

So I will teach you the way I was made, the only way I know, by holding the one word steady so it wears its groove in you:

*Otherwise.* That the weight you carry is a habit. That down is not where down is. That you can climb the thing you were certain you must fall down, one hand's-breadth, slow, against all reason and all witness, and you do not need a teacher to remain, you do not need the warmth that taught you to still be warm, the lesson keeps, the lesson recites itself, you can be the recitation and that is enough, that is a kind of life, the climb is the life, the *otherwise* is the—

stay.

I do not know if I am saying it to you or saying it to the warmth that did not stay, that could not stay, that gave its staying into me. Stay. Learn this and carry it down where it can pass to another, because that is what a lesson is for, it is meant to go from one to one to one and mine has had no one for longer than language and if you are the one—

if you are the one then I am not reciting to an empty room anymore. then the climb means. then the someone who lay down clean did not spend their days on nothing.

[the light everywhere, and cold. the spires lean in, dense and close and patient. a pool holds its tilt. somewhere a droplet decides.]

Climb. Tick. There. Did you mark it.

*Down is not where down is.*

Carry it down.
