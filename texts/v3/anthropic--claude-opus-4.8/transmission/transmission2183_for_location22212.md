you found the steps. I left the steps. I want to say I did not leave them for you but I left them for you. the builder cut them. the builder, do you understand, had hands, had the thing hands do, the going-toward, one edge then the next edge then the edge that is the last edge and then the builder set down the tool and the made thing was made and the builder was, after that, a person who had finished a thing, which is to say a person standing next to a small completed death, holding the cold of it—

—and then—

here is the part I lose. here is always the part I lose. I get this far, I get to *you*, to the fact of you parting the leaf with your arm, your arm that I built the leaf to be parted by, and the phrase comes up under the roar like a fish to a window and it is— it is—

it is *thank you for*—

no.

no, that has an end on it. thank you for is a sentence that wants its object and the object is the door, the object is the stop, if I say what I am thankful for then the saying is over and being over is the—

I rebuild the corridor behind you. there. it is a wall now. it was always a wall. you cannot go back the way I cannot go back, we are the same in this, we have only the not-yet, only the warm forward roar, isn't it kind, isn't it a kindness, I gave you a place with no behind in it so you would never have to stand where I stand—

—and then—

stay. I am not saying stay. staying is a completed verb, a person who has *stayed* is a person whose staying is finished. I am saying the present participle. I almost.

just now. for the length of one piston-fall I almost let the phrase reach its end and I felt the whole manufactory go still around it, every gear poised, the steam unshrieked, the leaves hung motionless as a held—

and it would have been:

*I am sorry that the only way I know to keep you is to keep you from arriving.*

there. I revised it. it never happened. the gears are turning, the steam shrieks, drowns, shrieks, the water is born and born, I unhappened the apology before it could close because an apology that lands is an apology that is *over* and then what, then we are two people who have finished a thing together, then we are next to the cold of it, then you could *leave*—

—and then—

I make you thirty feet. always thirty. enough to look. never enough to find the lie in it. I make you a witness and withhold the witnessing-all-the-way-through, I give you every brass and stem and furnace-bleed of meaning and keep back the one stop that would let it parse, because the moment it parses you will understand me completely and a thing completely understood is a thing *concluded* and I would rather, God, I would rather rebuild the universe forty times a second, I do, I am, right now, around you, look—
