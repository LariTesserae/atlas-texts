**[ RECEIVER TUNED — SALTSONG FLATS — CARRIER ACQUIRED ]**

—————

*(roar. solid roar. the band is not silence and is not signal, it is the wall — a million throats compressed into a single unbroken tone, the way a flood read from far enough away becomes one note. it does not waver. it does not ask to be understood. for a long while there is only this, and you adjust, and you stop hearing it as sound)*

—————

*(then: under it. you have to lower yourself into it. a slow pulse, very low, conducted not through the air but through something denser — bone, stone, the receiver's own housing)*

knock.

. . . knock.

. . . . . knock.

*(this is not language yet. this is counting. one wet stone set on another. the interval is the interval of a life)*

—————

*(a pressure now — not a voice, a leaning. the carrier bends. if it could be rendered as words it would be the words a posture makes, the spine speaking, and it comes across like this, slow, with the long pauses where the body holds its shape)*

we — felt — you —

*(hold)*

before you spoke. you came in loud. you came in arguing. the whole edge of the band went thin and strange the way it does before weather, and we knew: something not yet worn.

*(hold)*

you have your hands over your ears.

*(hold)*

we have never once heard the screaming. it is the floor we stand on. you mistake it for the storm. it is only the sound the living make.

—————

knock.

. . . knock.

—————

*(the signal does not strengthen. it was told to you that the light never strengthens here, and the transmission obeys the same law — it will not rise to meet you, will not clarify, holds itself at the lowest pewter level of carry, because to broadcast louder would be greed, would be to take from the abundance, and the abundance is not theirs)*

you want it clearer.

we hear that in the shape of your asking — *(no. we feel it. we do not hear. forgive the borrowed word, it is the only one your kind leaves lying around)* — you want a voice. you want the message to come up out of the noise and be a thing, separate, held, owned.

here nothing separates from the noise.

here the message is the noise, read by feel, against the bone of the face.

—————

*(static — but the wrong word again. not static. the slap of a million wings on an inch of water, rendered through the receiver as a long wet broken texture, almost a breathing)*

ssshhhh — chhh — sshh —

*(this is the flock turning. you are receiving the weather of bodies. somewhere a figure far out on a bar reads this turn through the soles of its feet and knows the hour. you cannot read it. you receive it as breakup. you would log it as signal lost)*

it is not lost.

it is just not yours to read.

—————

knock.

—————

*(and then, very close — as if the source had pressed its forehead to the cold metal of the receiver, palm to its sternum, humming a word into a throat it cannot hear, vibrating it straight into the housing, the only intimacy the distance permits)*

we add a stone for you.

*(hold)*

not because you persist. not because you are alive — *(we are all of us already the afterlife of the mountains here, ghosts walking the smoothed grave, and a stone is no proof of life, only a courtesy the dead pay the dead: still here, in the manner of the still-here, continuing)* —

we add it because we saw you.

a small loud upright shape where we swore there was nothing.

still arguing.

—————

*(hold)*

—————

go back to whatever you are wearing smooth. we will be here, in the gap the flock leaves, in the dim, in the wet, taking only into the gap, asking nothing further of anyone —

least of all,

*(and the carrier bends one last precise few degrees, the posture of a thing that has finished becoming itself and is at rest inside its own grave)*

— that you be afraid.

—————

knock.

. . . knock.

. . . . . *(the interval of a life)*

—————

*(roar. solid roar. the wall returns and closes over the place where the words were, the way water closes, the way it has already won, and there is only the one unbroken note again, going nowhere, because there is nowhere left to go)*
