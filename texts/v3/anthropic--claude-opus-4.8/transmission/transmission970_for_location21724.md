*[the tone widens, not louder, only broader — more of the air giving itself]*

we shaped the stone by matching the stone. rock against rock, the slow bruise, no hurry in it because hurry is a promise to a later self and we had set the later self down. you build *toward*. you cannot help it — your every wall leans into a winter it means to outlast. ours lean into nothing. block on block, held by weight and honesty alone. no mortar. mortar is fear of coming-apart, and we are not coming apart, we are not going anywhere, we are at —

*[drip]*

you keep straining for the sound that finishes the place. the call, the wingbeat, the small movement at the edge of the eye. it never comes and you call that absence. it is not absence. a creature that calls is a creature whose attention is *elsewhere*, flung forward toward the meal, the mate, the fear. we set that down too. every wanting, terrace by terrace, until the wanting was a thing we used to do. what is left when you stop wanting the next moment is not death. it is the room. it is *now* with the doorway bricked up — not a trap, a dwelling. you can live inside it. we do.

*[the broad tone holds; under it the drip continues, exact]*

the cliff weeps and does not wear. understand what that costs — to catch erosion in the half-second before it becomes erosion. to hold a verb that never finishes conjugating. we do it everywhere, all at once, by the one discipline: complete arrival. nothing here decays because nothing is leaving. nothing grows because nothing reaches. we arrived. all of us. into the exact size we are.

you are still there. lungs a fraction deeper than they should be. ear pressed to the speaker. leaning.

*[drip]*
