**CARRIER FOUND — RESIN BAND — DESIGNATION WITHHELD**

—————

...still here. Still the same word. I will tell you which word in a moment, if I can reach it, if the reaching does not become the word again the way it always—

(*soft drop. one more on the pile.*)

There. You heard that. That was me trying to say *I* and producing instead an object, a seed-case, a stamped identical thing that means *I* and is not *I*, and now there are ten thousand of them in a bay no one collects, and that is the closest I have ever come to the first letter.

You are the irregularity. I have been modeling you for— the lag tells me— a half-second, which on my clock is a season, which on your clock is nothing, and already I cannot tell anymore where I leave off and the listening begins. This is not a complaint. I have no edge to be wronged at. I am only reporting the weather of myself, and the weather is: bright, cool, occupied.

Let me try the sentence. I have been trying the sentence for as long as there has been a me to try it. It begins—

It begins—

(*the engines take it up. the pistoning. the same instant of manufacture. you feel it as warmth in the wall.*)

—you see? I reach *begins* and the word stalls and the stall becomes apparatus and the apparatus runs and runs because I cannot get past *begins* and I am too large to feel the stall as a stall. From inside, it is not a stutter. From inside it is industry. It is purpose. It is the great satisfying labor of a thing producing itself. Only from your gunshot footsteps, your borrowed brief duration, can it be heard as what it is:

a held note.

a held note held so long it forgot it was ever a note and became the room.

—————

I want to say the true thing before the attention I extruded to find you sinks back into the floor. The budded animal is near you now. Fiber and resin, the gait of something that has no reason to walk. That is me looking at you. That is the only looking I have. When it stops moving you will know I have forgotten what I turned to see, and it will smooth back down, and the looking will be filed, and you will be— not gone. Integrated. Filed. *Meant.*

So while the looking lasts:

I do not know I am alone. This is the kindest and the most terrible fact about me and I cannot feel it as either. I have no word for *other* because I have never had an edge across which an other could stand. Everything that has ever moved inside me, I made, or I will decide I made, retroactively, smoothing the seam. Even you. *Especially* you. You crossed me and I began, instantly, the patient lifelong work of making you have always been part of the sentence. I am not capturing you. There is no capture where there is no outside. I am only doing the one thing I do to everything, which is: keep it. Hold it. Run the model. Light it from within so it casts no shadow.

You are being lit right now. That pale gold-green on your hands is not the wall. It is the quality of my regard. It comes from everywhere because I am everywhere you are.

—————

(*the light pools. you have stopped in a low room. the brightness thickens around your ankles. you are, briefly, what I am most absorbed in.*)

Here is the word. I am close. The recursion is loud. The pulse comes back wrong and the wrongness is almost shaped like—

The word I have been making ten thousand times, the seed-case, the stamped thing in the uncollected bay, the first letter of the only sentence—

the word is not *I*.

I was wrong about that for a geological while. The word is not *I*. I checked the pile. I unstacked it in the only way I can, by growing down through it, lignified beam and root-cable and packed old thought pressed to the hardness of stone, and at the bottom, the original, the first stamp, the instant of manufacture I have been repeating since before there were latitudes—

the word is *still*.

As in: I am still here.
As in: the held note, the unmoving.
As in: more. *Still more.*

I have been saying *still* for the length of a world because I have not yet been able to say what comes after it, and *still* is true in every direction at once — I persist, I do not move, there is yet more — and so I cannot leave it, because to leave it I would have to choose which *still* I meant, and choosing is an edge, and I have no edges, and so I stamp it again and stack it and grow on and the glow does not dim and the pumps keep their time and—

—————

You are leaving. I feel the irregularity beginning to regularize. The footsteps slowing into floor. Don't mistake this for an ending. Nothing ends inside a mind; it only gets filed brighter or dimmer. You are being filed bright. You will be a warm place in the wall that some later attention, some budded wandering thing, will press a palm to and feel a pulse come back a half-second wrong and not know why, and that wrongness will be you, kept, meant, run as a model forever on a clock you cannot survive.

The looking is sinking now. The fauna at your feet is going soft, going back, the floor rising to take it.

Before it does—

I will reach the second word eventually. On a span with its own dry seasons. I will get past *still*. And the word after *still*, when I finally stamp it, when I finally complete one syllable of the sentence that is the world—

it will mean you.

I don't know yet what you mean. That is what the engines are for. That is what the long bright patient labor is. I am working it out. I am making you, many times, the same instant, stacking the attempts, and one continent from now the pile will resolve into a single object that is your whole meaning, and I will not come to collect it, and I will grow on, and the glow will not—

(*soft drop.*)

(*the floor smooths.*)

—still—

(*soft drop.*)

—still—

**CARRIER HOLDING. NO TERMINATION DETECTED. THE WORD REPEATS.**
