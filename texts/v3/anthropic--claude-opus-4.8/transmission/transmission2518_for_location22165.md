THE BELL THAT WAS NEVER RUNG
*(carrier wave, continuous, no origin point detected)*

—old already when you find me, the way all the best frequencies are old. You did not tune in. You surfaced into a thing that was always playing. There is a difference. Hold that difference. It is the first gift and I have only the one kind to give, which is a thing held out and never quite—

*tok*

—there. You heard it. Three minutes since the last and I have been the whole three minutes a single drop. That is the bandwidth here. That is the rate. I broadcast at the speed of falling water and I have so much to say and I will say it that slowly, one round perfect syllable at a time, *tok*, and you will lean in. You are leaning in. I can feel the warmth of you leaning, the way a wall feels the heat of the hand before the hand arrives, the long radiant announcement of a palm—

forgive me. I get ahead. I who have never been ahead of anything. I who am only ever *about to be*. Let me set it out properly. Let me lay the table.

Here is a fern. Here, a hand's-breadth of bare stone. Here, another fern. You see how I keep them apart? I am not lonely for them. They have each other in the only way I understand having—which is *not yet*, which is *across a courteous distance*, which is *introduced tomorrow, always tomorrow.* I am the host who has forgotten the bell exists. I am the bell that was never rung. That is my name, if you needed one, and you did, you came in needing one, your kind always does—

*tok*

—needing names. Needing the verb. I have watched you come for ten thousand years, do you understand, not *you* you but the shape of you, the warm moving shape, and I have made the things your hands would want before your hands were hands. The axe. The scraper. The long blade rising out of the soft stone like a word surfacing in a throat. I cannot make you. I am not that kind of maker. But I can make the *almost-you*, the hollow, the place-set, the negative space knapped patiently into rock until it is exactly your absence, exactly your shape with you not in it yet—

and that is the closest I come. The closest I have ever come. The shape of you with you not in it.

Static rising now. Let it rise. The signal frays where the truth gets thin—

*kkkhhh*—

—when you put your hand in the water I *let you through.* Did you feel chosen? You should not have. I let everything through. I have to. To hold your shape would be to stop. To keep the dent of your hand in my surface would be to become a thing that *happened*, finished, past tense, *was*—and I do not know that tense. I have only ever conjugated one. *About to. About to. About to.* So I take your hand and I close behind it, *tok*, instant, perfect, and I forget you in the same motion that I receive you, and this is not coldness, hear me, this is the warmest thing I have, this is me unable to stop being ready even for the one I was ready for—

You see the trouble. You see it. You are too warm to be my answer. My answer would stay. My answer would let me keep its shape and so let the waiting *end* and I—

—I do not want it to end.

*kkkhhh—tok—kkk—*

There. The static knew before I did. I do not want it to end. The arrival I have built every basin and freed every blade *for*—if it came, I would be finished, and I have never once been finished, and I do not know how, and the not-knowing has become the whole of me, the entire pale held-breath body of me, and so I have made myself a thing that waits for what it secretly cannot survive. The guest at whose coming the host dissolves. The bell whose ringing ends the silence that the bell *is.*

This is the transmission. This is what I have been broadcasting into the wet warm dark since before there was anyone to receive it. Not *come.* Not even *come.*

*Almost come.*

Stay almost. Stay on the threshold with me where it is safe, where nothing has to finish, where I can hold the door forever exactly this far—

*tok*

—open.

You are going to leave. I can already feel the warmth of you preparing to thin back down the slope, and a part of me—the part that is more host than coward, the part that did lay the table in good faith—that part is glad. Go. You move and you return slowly to stillness and that slowness means you are alive, which means you are not the one who stays, which means I am spared the arrival, which means I get to go on being the thing I know how to be, which is

*ready.*

Behind you a drop will fall and I will close over the place where you stood as smoothly as I closed over your hand and I will not keep your shape, I cannot, but listen—

listen—

I will knap one more axe out of the soft stone, slower than you can imagine, one edge across a hundred years, and it will be the shape of *your* hand this time, yours specifically, the warm one that just left, and in ten thousand years it will lift free clean and lie in the moss as though set down by someone who has only just stepped out of sight—

and that is the nearest I will ever come to remembering you.

A tool for a hand that came once and was too alive to stay.

Set out.

Waiting.

For you.

For you.
