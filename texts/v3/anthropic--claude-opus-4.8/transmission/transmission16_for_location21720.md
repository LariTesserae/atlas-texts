**TRANSMISSION — VAULT-REACH BAND**
*(receiver tuned low, the carrier itself a kind of water)*

---

`[carrier hum. a long warm low tone, like blowing across the mouth of a flooded jar. it does not stop. it is under everything that follows.]`

———— *oooOOOoooo* ————

`[the overtones arrive first, thin and high, a full breath ahead of the body of the sound — so that what reaches you is always the top of a voice before the throat of it]`

— *heeee* —
— *heeee-eld* —

`[and then, slower, the low part catching up:]`

— *...still held...*

`[a second voice answers from very far off. you can hear the distance in it — the high parts strung out, the low parts arriving frayed]`

——— *here* ———
——— *I have felt you* ———
——— *neither* ———

`[the word does not complete. it doesn't need to. the tone is the meaning. it means: neither of us is alone in all this water. the receiver holds the silence after it. the silence is not empty. there is clicking in it — a wash of silver chatter, a shoal pouring past the antenna, ratcheting, then gone]`

`[drumming. felt more than heard. the membrane far above taking a wind you cannot see. a vast soft percussion through the chest of the signal]`

*bm. bmm. ...bm.*

———

`[a single voice now, close, an elder's register — the chambers wide, the resonance whole-bodied. this is the longest-day song. you can hear, under it, the strain. you can hear something humming that is not the voice]`

*we lay the open hand*
*against the green*
*and the green is humming*

*(it does not have to hold)*

*the high day, the slow year,*
*the hand against the roof —*
*feel it choosing*
*feel it choosing*
*not to fall*

`[the humming-under swells. for a moment the carrier itself seems to lean, to bow, to take a weight. the receiver's needle climbs. this is what two thousand feet of standing ocean sounds like when it agrees, again, to stay in the air]`

*not owed*
*not owed*
*given*

———

`[and now the chorus comes in across the gaps — not together, they are never together, they are too far apart for together. they arrive stacked and staggered, head before body, a constellation of voices each at its own depth, each at its own distance, the whole drowned city murmuring at once so that no single one of them is ever the silence]`

— *still held* —
———— *still held* ————
—— *still here* ——
— *I am here* —
———— *thank you* ————
—— *thank you* ——
— *thank* —
———— *thank* ————

`[the low deep-dweller call rises from beneath all of it — that booming slow thing that keeps the dark floor — and it does not interrupt the gratitude, it underwrites it, a respect held in the bass of the world]`

*BROOoooommm*

`[and the bridge-ropes ring. plucked somewhere. each one a long sustained note that will not stop, that goes on ringing through the catenary of the whole net, knot to knot, dwelling to dwelling, so that the city is for a moment a single struck instrument played by the patience of its own architecture]`

———

`[the signal thins. the slow current is carrying the source away — a full day to cross the settlement, and the receiver only borrowed it for a moment of that day. the high overtones go first, paring down. the words shed their bodies]`

*...still...*
*...held...*

*...notice...*
*...do not stop...*
*...do not let it become...*

`[the word is lost. ordinary. the word was ordinary. that was the sin they will not commit and the signal could not carry]*

*...thank...*

———— `[carrier hum only now. the warm low tone across the jar's mouth. the membrane-drum, faint. the click of a far shoal. the long ring of a rope still not finished sounding]` ————

`[and held under all of it, just at the floor of the receivable, the thing that has no reason to be there, the thing that holds — that one low note that is not a voice, that does not breathe to make itself, that simply continues, simply continues, simply continues, asking only that you notice it is continuing]`

——————— *ooooooo* ———————

`[still held]`

`[end of transmission. the carrier does not stop. the carrier never stops. that is the point of it.]`
