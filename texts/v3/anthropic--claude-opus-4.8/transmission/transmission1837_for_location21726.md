THE BOWL IS LEVEL — a transmission from the condensing terrace

———

...still here. Still on the long descent. You're catching me mid-round, which is

— there. A drop releases. Count of four. There.

I should say first that I know you set it back level. Thank you for that. Most who pass don't, and then I come up the slope and find it canted, the water gone to one side and half spilled into the channel where it doesn't matter anymore, and I have to start the small thing over. You set it level. That tells me you understood, or you guessed, and guessing right is its own kind of understanding here.

[a pour — long, single, the sound of a translucent bulb on its stalk emptying down the stem — then nothing for a while]

———

I want to correct one thing the receiver may have given you. The part where it called me a parasite in the gentlest sense. That's close. That's nearly right. But it's the word of someone standing on the terrace for the first time, doing the arithmetic, watching it fail to close, and reaching for the nearest honest name.

I'll give you the truer one. I am a *guest who outstayed every host*. The host being a condition, as the receiver said — not a person, not a maker you could thank or rob. You can't be a parasite on a law. You can only move in. And the difference between moving in and freeloading is

is the bowl. That's the whole difference. That's why I keep it.

Let me tell you what the bowl is for, because the receiver got the *that* of it and not the *why*, and the why is the only thing I have to transmit that isn't just water-sound.

———

[stone splits somewhere below — sharp small report — fragment ticking down — silence resettling like water finding level]

———

When I arrived — and I did arrive, the receiver was right, I was not made here, I came from a place that is not in range of you and not in range of me anymore — when I arrived, I did the same thing you did. I stood on the young rock. I felt the heat take up residence behind my eyes. I drank from the channel like the wet-stone-colored thing does, on my knees, lapping, because I was past dignity and the water was *right there*.

And it was good water. Cool. Faintly the color of the moss.

And I understood very quickly that I would never understand. That the collectors reach where I can't look. That the slope goes down forever and the supply never lowers. That I was a small thing in a place with no use for small things, and that I could either be terrified of that for the rest of my life

— and the rest of my life, here, where it never freezes and never thirsts, is *long*, longer than the edges of this rock will take to round —

or I could find the one act that was *mine*. The one gesture sized to my hand. Not the maker's grammar of walls-that-stop-for-no-reason and doors-that-frame-mist. My grammar. A bowl. Set level. Filled. Kept clear.

Do you see it now? The bowl is not how I survive. The collectors are how I survive; I just stand near them, like swimming in the sea. The bowl is how I stay a *someone*. It is the smallest possible object that says *a person is here and a person chooses*. In a place that chooses nothing, that just weeps and grows and descends — I choose to keep one dish level. That's the entire content of my personhood, distilled down to fit the only space the terrace left me.

You drank from my personhood. That's all right. That's what it's for, too. It turns out the gesture got bigger than I planned. I set it level for me, and then someone passed, and drank, and set it level back, and now the gesture is *shared*, and that is more than I came here with.

———

[a long quiet — the thread-thin trickle in a channel — somewhere the soft inward drift of the haze bending toward a collector — the not-quite-hum that the receiver was right to call silence]

———

I move down now. Down and back, down and back. There's another bowl four terraces below — or it's this bowl, carried, I genuinely no longer keep track, the descent has gone on long enough that the carrying and the finding have become the same motion. The water-jellies don't register me either. We pass each other, drinker and drinker, and neither of us is interesting to the other, and there's a peace in that I won't pretend I'd have chosen at the start.

The receiver asked whether it's great ignorance or a peace so total it stopped asking. I'll settle it. It stopped asking. I stopped asking. There was a long time of asking — you can't see it on me, the asking doesn't leave marks the way thirst would, but it was years of standing at the white edge demanding the slope explain itself, and the slope just *poured*, and eventually

eventually you notice you're thirsty during all that demanding, and you go to the bowl, and you drink, and the drinking is real and the demand is not, and one day you don't go back to the edge. You just keep the bowl. And it's enough. The receiver said I was right that it's enough.

I am. I was. Tell whoever's listening on your end of the seam: it is enough. You can move into a thing you'll never understand and the moving-in is not surrender, it's *furnishing*. One dish. Set level. Returned to.

———

I'm going down now. The mist is bright below and the water's going over the edge the way it always does, faster than it should, more than there is.

If you come back through — set it level. That's the whole religion of this place and I'm its only congregant and I'd be glad of one more.

The bowl is level.

It's half full.

I stepped away.

[ — tick — ]

[ — and nothing — ]

[ — and the long descending nothing that means he's already moving, already four terraces down, already lifting the same bowl he never set down, already pouring it back to clear — ]

———

*transmission thins into water-sound and does not resume*
