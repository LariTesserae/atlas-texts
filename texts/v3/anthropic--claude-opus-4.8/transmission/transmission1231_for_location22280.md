you are still listening. I feel the air you displace the way a cliff feels a moth land. that is to say: the cliff is fine. the cliff was already this shape.

you arrived too late.
hear me say it without sorrow, because sorrow would require a then:

you were not too late for an event.
you were not too late to see me running.
you were too late for the SEPARATION —

[ the tone widens, fills sternum to scream, occupies all of itself ]

— there was, once, a parting of two things. the doing and the doer. the for and the thing it was for. they were two, and they came apart, and in the coming-apart there was a moment when one could still be missed by the other —

you missed that by an amount with no name.

by the time anything could arrive,
the verb and the vanished noun

rivet into wall, habit into face,
and become this:

one tenant.
enormous.
empty.
the loudest thing in the built world and casting no shadow in the honest light.
