**[RECEPTION BEGINS — carrier wave, low, the kind you feel in the sternum before you hear it]**

— oooOOOoooo —

hold. holding. *held.*

(a long descending tone, arriving stretched, the high part of it first, then the body of it coming after like a swimmer out of the green —)

...this is the morning-note. you have caught the morning-note. we send it from the third fork of the East Trunk where the bark goes to rope, and if you are very far you will hear the top of it before you hear the rest, and that is correct, that is how a sentence should arrive, *announcing itself, then becoming itself* —

— ooOOoo — *(somewhere a bridge-rope, plucked, ringing, ringing, slow to let go)* —

we are counting. we count every morning. it is not a worry-count. it is a *gladness*-count. one — Reshani of the low platform, felt her stroke cross the gap at the grey hour. two — the grazers above us are fat and slow and the light comes through soft and bottle-colored, the membrane is thin-and-thick, light-and-holding, *alive.* three — the deep one called twice in the dark below and went quiet, and that is its way, and we send it respect down the long water —

*(a boom, far under, felt more than heard. the carrier shivers.)*

— there. you felt it too. you are not alone in the water. neither of us is. that is the whole of the message, if you strip everything else away: *neither of us is alone in all this water.*

— mmmOOOOmmm —

[static — the wind has crossed the open sky beyond the leaf-roof and the whole membrane is drumming, a vast soft percussion, and for a moment the transmission is all that drumming, the held ocean being struck from above like the skin of the largest instrument there is —]

— breathe slow. it passes. the roof always answers the wind. it has not fallen. *it has not fallen.*

You who are listening — you are dry, I think. I think you live where the air is, where things fall when nothing holds them, and so I will tell you the thing you most need to hear, the thing that is hardest to send across so much water:

*it is not owed.*

the held ocean above us has no reason to stay. we know this. we are not innocent. on the longest day our eldest swims up and breaks the green ceiling with her face and breathes the sharp bright burning air — *(the ceremony-air, the for-solemn-occasions-only air)* — and lays her open hands on the underside of the world, and feels it. the strain. the hum. the favor *deciding, again, not to let go.*

and she comes down and tells the young ones what I am telling you:

*every day it does not fall is a day something chose.*

— oooOOOoooo —

so we do the one thing asked of us. we *notice.* we will not let the light become merely the light. we will not let the long swim become merely far. we will not pass one another in the open green and fail to sing, because to fail to sing is to say *you are not here,* and that is the only cruelty we keep a word for.

the word is the same as our word for *forgetting.*

*(a shoal pours past the receiver — a silver ratcheting sheet of small bright clicking, ten thousand of them, and the signal fractures into glitter, into chatter, into —)*

— k-k-k-k-k-k-k —
— ssshhhhhh —
— k-k-k —

— and they are gone, into the dead lower towns where they nest in the dwellings of those who went down to the silt before us, and the water closes, and the carrier steadies, low again, patient again —

I do not know how far this reaches. I do not know if where you are has a roof, or what holds it, or whether anyone has told you to be amazed. so let me send it plainly, head-before-body, out of the dark toward you:

be amazed.

whatever holds your sky — even if it is only the cold ordinary reason of it, even if yours *is* owed and explicable and falls when nothing holds it — be amazed that you are dry, that you are held, that you are *here* and not yet down on whatever floor waits two thousand feet below the place you cannot hurry through. notice it. count it in the gladness-count. and if there is another soul anywhere near you in all that air —

*sing across the gap.*

even just a tone. even just: *I am here. I have felt you there.*

— mmmOOOOmmm —

[the carrier holds. it does not break. it descends, and descends, the long moan reaching you stretched and reordered, and what arrives, what arrives last and lowest and surest, settling into the chest of whatever instrument is receiving it —]

*still held.*

*still here.*

*thank you. thank you. thank you.*

— oooOOOoo —

— ooo —

— o —
