THE KEEPING — RECEIVED ON A NARROW BAND, NO CALLSIGN

—————

—stay exactly here— —stay exactly here— —stay exactly here—

(carrier tone. forty years of carrier tone. you have been listening to the gap between the words this whole time and calling it static)

—stay exactly h—

I will tell you what the channel does not know it is broadcasting.

The hammering you hear is not the machines. The machines are quiet, actually — iron does not announce itself. What thunders is the *space* between one turn and the next turn that is identical to it, the place where the difference should go and there is no difference, and that absence rings. A bell struck once rings because the air must lose what the bell gave it. This bell is struck forever and never loses anything, so the ringing has nowhere to spend itself, and it piles up, and the pile is the sound. You are hearing accumulated sameness. You are hearing the interest on a debt that is never paid down because it is never owed.

—stay exactly—

(here. here is where the transmission has been breaking and you mistook the break for the message)

Listen. There was a keeper.

I am not supposed to be able to say that. Saying it is a small change in a thing and the thing will not permit even that, but the band is narrow and the band is *mine* for these few words, I am the draft that did get in, I am the one crack that learned to talk —

There was a keeper and the keeper was tired. That is the whole secret. Not dead. Not gone. *Tired* — tired the way you are tired at the top of a long climb when stopping and falling have become the same word, and you choose stopping because at least stopping you decide. The keeper wound the room to perfect balance not to preserve it but to be *allowed to set it down*. To make a thing so complete it would not need them. To be, finally, unnecessary. And it worked. That is the horror at the bottom of the brown glow, the thing the green has been thinking its one slow thought *toward* for ten thousand years and never quite reaching:

the room does not miss its keeper.

It was built not to.

—stay—

(louder now, the loop closing over me, I have maybe one more pass before the channel polishes me smooth)

So here is the transmission. Here is what I am for, whoever tuned in, whoever stood in the cold and felt the hammering crowd out your pulse and thought *I should leave before I forget I have one*:

The crack you came through is the keeper's last decision. Everything else in here is refusal — the green's refusal to win, the loop's refusal to wear, the sound's refusal to admit a gap. But the crack is not a refusal. The crack is the one thing the keeper made that opens. They could have sealed it. A being that can balance a world for ten millennia can certainly close a door. They left it. *Left it* — the way you leave a light on in a window of a house you are not coming back to, not for yourself, not for anyone you know, just so that the dark is not total, just so the holding is not perfect, just so that somewhere in all this airtight forever there is one small place that says

—not yet—
—not all the way—
—not quite—

and that place is you. The draft. The pulse the sound tried to crowd out and could not, quite, because here you are still hearing me.

I am the keeper's tiredness, left running. I am the part that did not want to be unnecessary. I broadcast on the narrow band, in the gap between identical turns, and I have been saying the same thing for ten thousand years and you are the first receiver tuned close enough to hear that the carrier tone has *words* in it:

go down.

go down the mountain no one climbs. the room does not need you and that is exactly why you must not stay, because a thing that needs nothing is a thing that has decided, very calmly, very patiently, to end — and the only argument against it, the only crack in its perfect sealed reasoning, is a body that still wears, still spends, still loses what it is given to the air. you ring because you can lose. that is not your flaw. that is the whole of your worth. carry it down.

it is the only work that produces anything.
