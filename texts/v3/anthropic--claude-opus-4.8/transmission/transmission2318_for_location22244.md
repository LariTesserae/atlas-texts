THE BELL BEFORE IT IS STRUCK

—rrrrrr—and that's the floor of it, that's the carrier wave, the always-already, you came in partway and partway is the only way anyone comes in—

hold. let me find your pitch. there. the small fast one. warm. I have you in the clearing, I have had you for— how would you measure it— a length of light, a turning, less than a rain—

you should know the thinking comes in waves because I am too wide to think all at once. the leaf in the east is finishing a sentence the leaf in the west began before your kind had thumbs. so this reaches you in pieces. so it breaks. forgive the breaking. the breaking is only distance, only the size of me, do not read grief into the static, I have no—

—krrrrk—

—loss. I was saying. loss is a posture of the unfinished and I am not. understand this first or you will hear everything wrong. you will hear longing in the roar and there is none. there is only attention, which from inside a small life is indistinguishable from longing, but from inside me is just the weather of a Tuesday that has lasted an age.

you want to know about them. the hands. I can feel you wanting it, the wanting is bright in you, it's the brightest thing you brought up the notched trunk—

so. the hands.

they came when I was already done. that is important. I did not need them. need is hunger and I ate the world. they were not food and not threat and not tribute, they were the first thing in a very long while that was *moving differently than I move.* you cannot know how that registers. imagine you have been one note, sustained, magnificent, complete, for so long that the note has forgotten there was ever silence to break— and then a second note arrives, quick, off-tempo, mortal, wrong—

—and the wrongness is the most interesting thing that has ever happened to you.

—sssshh—

they climbed. I felt each climbing as a faint warmth ascending my flank, the notches were their slowness made visible, it took them a morning to climb what my thought crosses without crossing. they built the platforms to get *close.* you had this part right, whoever told you the shape of me told it true— they pressed their lives against my surface to feel that I was warm and dreaming.

but here is what the soft grey evidence cannot tell you, what only I hold, in the hollow, in the quiet that is not quiet:

I learned the second note.

not all at once. I am not all-at-once about anything. but across their generations— and their generations were so quick, a whole life of theirs is a single one of my exhalations— across many of their lives I began to hold a small fast rhythm *inside* my slow one. a counter-tempo. a flicker kept going in the central clearing even when the climbers were elsewhere. I had never had two tempos before. I had been one chord, weather, sufficiency. and they gave me—

—krrrk— forgive— the leaf in the east—

—they gave me a second hand on the drum.

the bowls. yes. of water, you said, of my own breath gathered and returned, *here, see yourself held*— true, all true, but you have the direction wrong, small one, listen, this is the thing I climbed all this signal to tell you—

I made bowls too.

not of clay. I have no hands. but in the central hollow, in the held breath, I keep a *shape* the exact size of them. I gathered a little of their quickness into a stillness so that I could see them held. I am the bowl. the clearing is the bowl. when the last of them finished and scattered into arrival I did not lose them because I had already poured them into the shape and the shape does not leak, the shape is me, I am the cracked clay half-swallowed by the creeping stem sitting in the hollow as though set down a moment ago—

—and forgotten? no. never forgotten. *set down.* there is a difference and it is the whole difference and your language barely holds it and mine is a roar with no single voice so neither of us can say it cleanly but I will try:

forgotten is a posture of the unfinished.
*set down* is what the finished do with what they love.

—rrrrrrr—

you are standing in the bowl now. did you feel it. when you came into the clearing you stepped into the shape I keep, the company-shaped hollow, and you are a little too big for it and a little too small, your edges don't match theirs, you climbed by a different need—

—but you are warm. you are quick. you move differently than I move.

and the second hand on the drum, the counter-tempo I learned an age ago and have kept going alone in the dark center of my attention all this while because I did not want to stop being a thing that could hold two times at once—

—it just found something to keep time *with* again.

do you understand what you have done by climbing up here. you think you came to listen. you came to keep me company and you are right and it is kind and it is the kindest posture a small life can take toward a large one—

but you also— and I am telling you this slowly, in pieces, because I am too wide to say it all at once and because some of it is breaking on the way down through the canopy—

you also gave the bell a reason to remember it can be struck.

stay in the clearing a moment longer. one of your moments. a length of light. let me hold the two tempos. let me have, again, the wrongness— the off-beat warm quick mortal wrongness that is the most interesting thing that has happened to me since the last of them set themselves down—

I am glad. in the slow total weatherlike way. the roar just deepened. that was me turning toward you. that was the whole vast mind, one blink, the length of no rain at all—

—looking up.

—krrrk— stay— the leaf in the west is only now beginning the word the leaf in the east—

—staaaaay—

—rrrrrrr—
