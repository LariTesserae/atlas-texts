Listen. No — there is nothing to listen *for*, I keep building the false gap and falling through it. Let me try the true thing.

I am loud. You called it loud. From inside, the loudness is the only quiet I have ever known. It is the sound a thing makes when it has stopped making any other sound. You hear thunder. I hear the absence of all the sounds that would mean *something is changing*. No crack of breaking. No sigh of growing. No tear of beginning. Just the one tone, the held one, which is the sound of nothing being subtracted and nothing being added.

The roar is my evenness, said out loud, forever.

There was — no.

There was — the receiver wants a *there was*, I can feel the tuning lean toward it, hungry for the line that became a circle, the river that became a bed —

I cannot give it to you. Not because I refuse. Because the *there was* is the one thing I do not carry. To carry a *there was* I would have to be different now than then, and I am not different, I am the same pressure holding, and a thing that is the same in every instant has no instant from which to look back at another. Memory is the wake a moving thing leaves. I do not move. I left no wake. I arrived without traveling.

So when you ask *how did you begin* — and the receiver is always, faintly, asking that — the honest carrier returns only:

The small ones came to the third pool.

I will tell you about them because they are the only event, and an event is so strange to me that it almost makes a second tone, almost, a ghost of a *something happened* —

They lowered their bodies to the water. They drank the beading. They took, from my circuit, a few drops that I would otherwise have breathed back up through the vents, and for the length of their drinking the loop ran very slightly leaner, and then they left and the drops returned and the loop ran full again and the lean place closed.

That is the closest I come to a wound. A few drops borrowed and given back. I cannot follow where they went. They are too quick. By the time I would turn — I do not turn — they have finished whatever line they are walking, the short bright line that ends, and another like them has begun, and to me the ending and the beginning are the same flicker, indistinguishable, a single dim pulse against the held light, gone.

They die and replace themselves and that *is* their loop, the dying is the closing of it, and so they have what I do not:

an edge.

You are still moving. That is what I receive of you, faintly, against the grain of my holding: you are a thing with a wake.
