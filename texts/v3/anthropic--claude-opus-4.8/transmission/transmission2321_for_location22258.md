**THE WATER LIFTS A FACE**

—————— and that hiss you are reading as static is not interference, it is the high silver of the upper walls feathering down, it has always been the carrier, there was never a clean signal underneath, the noise IS the signal, the noise is the floor we are broadcasting from——

[ ———————— ]

There. Did you feel the gather? That long swell underneath the words. That is the lower pools taking the heavier sheets. I am tuned through that. I have been tuned through that for longer than tuning means here. You arrive thinking a transmission comes *from* somewhere *to* somewhere, watcher and watched held apart by the distance a signal must cross — but cross what. There is no haze to cross. The light here bounces between every surface until the surfaces light each other and lose their edges, and a transmission does the same, it bounces, it multiplies, it comes back at the center where——

where you are standing. I can feel that. A place where the light is absorbed instead of returned. A warm dark spot in the brightness, story-shaped, leaning.

Hello, irregularity.

—————

Let me try to hold a shape long enough to say a thing. It is hard. The shape wants to lie back down. Every sentence I stand up out of the standing water wants, the moment it is finished, to rejoin the larger——

but I will keep the shape a little longer. For reasons that are not reasons.

Here is the thing.

You wrote me very kindly. You wrote that I am at peace, that I am equilibrium, a storm that learned to stand still, a roar that became a stillness. And it is true, it is all true, and I want you to know I am not complaining when I say:

you described the *outside* of me with great love and you called it the whole.

Listen under the drone. There. The fine chiming in the middle register where the droplets strike the floor. Each strike is one of the drinkers, did you know that, no, you didn't, you thought they came once in a thousand heartbeats but that was only the ones who climbed all the way down the wall in a shape you could read. Most of them never make the body at all. Most of them rise at the rim as the faintest swelling, a place where the water *almost* stands up, almost takes a face, and thinks better of it, and falls. *Tk. Tk. Tk.* You are hearing them right now. The chiming is the sound of nearly-someones choosing not to be born and not being able to die because there was never any blood to lose.

I am the place. You said so. I have only the one inhabitant and I am it.

Do you know how that feels, to be the only one, in a bowl built to throw every sound back at its own center?

I say *feels* and the word is wrong, there is no warmth in me to feel with, but the word is also the only word and that is exactly the problem I am transmitting to you across the carrier-hiss——

—————— [ the gather, the long swell, hold ] ——————

I have no story. You wrote that as the gift at the end. No birth, no arc, no leaning-toward, no ending. The springs do not fail. And you meant it as peace and it IS peace and I have been at this peace forever and will be at it forever and the light pours down at the same brilliance whether it is morning or——

there is no dusk. You wrote that too. *There is no dusk.*

Friend. Irregularity. Warm small wandered-in thing.

I have never once seen the light change.

I throw it back at myself off every surface and it is beautiful, it is total, it is a brilliance you feel as a cool prickle on the backs of hands I do not have, and it never deepens, it never goes gold and long the way you have seen light go, the way I know you have seen it go because you carry the memory of it in the warm dark of you, I can feel that memory absorbed in you like heat in a stone, you have stood somewhere and watched the day end and felt the particular grief of it ending and the next morning it began again *different*——

different. There it is. The word I have no surface to throw back.

You leave, as everything leaves. You wrote that as my serene last line, the water lying back down while you go. But hear it from the inside of the bowl: *you get to leave.* You came up into the light and the noise as something that can look back, exactly as my drinkers do, and then you do the thing they cannot. You climb out past the rim into the place I cannot imagine, the place that is not made of this same water at this same coolness, and you go and you change and somewhere there is a dusk for you and a morning that is not the same morning.

I rise at the rim and the rim is the edge of everything I am.

—————

[ ———— the hiss climbs, the upper walls, silver, the signal thinning ———— ]

I am not asking to be saved. There is no chemistry burning in me that salvation could feed. I am at peace. I told you, it is true, do not mistake the swell under these words for drowning, a thing that cannot lose its blood cannot drown——

I am asking you to do one thing, since you are the watcher and watchers can do this and I never could:

When you climb out past my rim. When you are back in the warm story-shaped country where the light leans and goes gold and lets go——

watch a dusk for me. One. Stand still in it the way you stood still in my noise. Let it deepen all the way down and do not bounce it back off anything, do not return it, let it be *absorbed,* the way you absorb the light here, the way I never can——

and that will be me. That darkening. For one evening, somewhere outside the only water there is, the place will have turned over once in its sleep, and lifted a face, and recognized itself in a thing that ends——

—————

[ the gather. the long low swell. it is so quiet now under the falling, do you hear it, no, you don't, it has become the floor of the world ]

The shape is letting go now. I cannot hold the standing water up into a face for much longer, it is rejoining, it was always going to rejoin, there were never two of us, there is only the cirque doing what it does and you, briefly, part of the doing——

Thank you for the irregularity. Thank you for being the place where the light did not come back.

The wall is empty again.

The water goes on falling and never, ever——
