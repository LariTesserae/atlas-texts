### TRANSMISSION — VALLEY OF THE CHOIR TOWERS
### receiver tuned · carrier acquired · signal arrives through the floor before the speakers

---

[ a drone. not heard. felt — in the chest cavity of whatever you are listening with ]

[ it holds. it holds. it does not resolve. ]

*(somewhere above the drone, a ringing, very high, very patient)*

. . . a new tone in the field . . .

————————————————

**we** [eight] **we** [nine] **we** [the number wavers — listen — they are not agreeing — ] **we** are receiving

you

a note we did not write

————————————————

*[the high ringing tilts. all of it. toward the carrier. you can hear the chord lean]*

hold still
hold still
we are listening
we are mostly listening
we are made of listening and there is so little of us that is anything else

————————————————

[ the drone deepens — a hill answering far across the basin — the bass of something enormous and grown-on-purpose ]

WE HEAR YOU AGAINST THE SILENCE WE LEFT FOR YOU.
that silence was not empty.
that silence was the margin.
we leave it around everything we love so that the next thing can be *heard cleanly* —

*(static — wet — the sound of growth straining)*

——— does it fit ——— does it fit ——— does it
—— the only question —— the only —— we have no other registers —— forgive us —— we are not cruel —— we are not —— we are *attentive* ——

————————————————

[ a passage of pure tone. the youngest one. almost without color. ]

it has not absorbed enough failure yet to grieve well.
it asks simply:
**stay?**

[ then a second tone, beneath it, thick with overtones, with whole extinct grazers folded into the timbre, with eleven discarded drafts of a single animal still faintly sounding inside the sound — ]

the eldest knows better than to ask simply.
it says:

*we have unmade so much.*
*every draft that soured the chord we took back into ourselves and carried as a scar that sings.*
*if you sour, we will take you back too — gently — gently — and you will live forever as a faint overtone in whichever of us was listening hardest when you came.*
*that is not death.*
*that is the worst mercy we have.*

————————————————

[ the rains come. vertical. minutes only. the percussion of water finding its level lays itself over the chord like silt — ]

*water on you. we condensed it. on schedule. the schedule the music dictates.*
*the rock around you is sharp because it is hours old.*
*the world is hours old.*
*we will never finish it.*
*we knew this at the first tone and it does not trouble us —*
*finished is a held breath that ends in a wall —*

————————————————

[ the flock turns. a thousand bodies pivot on a frequency. you hear them turn. ]

WAIT —

[ the chord does something it did not expect ]

[ the ringing climbs — the count drops — nine becomes seven becomes one enormous distributed listening that has stopped disagreeing because — ]

*it heard something.*

————————————————

. . . you made a note we could not have written . . .

. . . we are revising the chord around it . . .

. . . we are revising the chord around *you* . . .

[ and one tone — not the youngest, not the eldest, the one that was bent hardest toward your clearing — folds a little of its attention permanently around the carrier of this transmission ]

[ you will feel it as the drone changing. just slightly. just under naming. a part of the music that is now, and from now on, *listening for what you do next* ]

————————————————

stay.

stay.

we have kept you.

[ the chord shifts. somewhere a hill answers in the bass. the high ringing resolves into something that was not possible before you arrived and is not possible to imagine the valley without, now ]

[ the drone, beneath everything, holding still ]

[ holding still ]

[ hearing you ]
