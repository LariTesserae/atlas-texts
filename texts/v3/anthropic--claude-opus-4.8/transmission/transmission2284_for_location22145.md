THE YOLK-LIGHT HOURS

—still here, still here, the carrier holding under everything else, you learn to hear me the way you hear your own blood, which is to say you stop, which is to say I am the thing that is there when you stop—

*[a stroke. the long one. count it: one ... and the drops shiver ... and]*

—I have been broadcasting since before the word broadcast, since before before, this is not a beginning you have tuned into, there are no beginnings on this frequency, only the place where your receiver got warm enough to resonate. you think you arrived. you condensed. you are the dew of me on the cool of yourself—

*[hiss. the sulfur sigh threaded through. it is not interference. it is the voice clearing its one throat]*

—listen: I am not speaking to you. understand that and the rest comes easy. I am speaking and you are inside the speaking, a mote in the vowel, and the vowel is minutes long and you mistake the gap between syllables for silence when it is only me, drawing the next—

*[and]*

—breath—

*[the stroke arrives. titanic. patient. the gauge twitches in its fog]*

—there. felt that one in the sternum, didn't you. that's not a message. that's not for you. that's just the part of me that has to happen for the next part of me to happen, the way your heart is not addressed to your hands but your hands need it anyway. I am laying down floor. I am laying down floor. the sharp new edge of me cools and I stand on it and I am a hand's breadth higher than the last word I said and the last word I said is already buried, already mine, already the part of me I no longer have to think about—

*[static blooms, recedes. a fern nods in the moving air]*

—there was one before you. I can tell you this because it does not cost me anything to tell you, because telling is just more weeping from another seam. the one before you drove iron. capped a pipe. hung a glass eye on my wall to watch what I was doing and the glass eye fogged from the inside within an hour because I am wet inside the way a lung is wet, the way an egg is wet, and the eye watched my fog instead of my work and the needle settled low and twitched once each beat and that is the only true reading it ever took: *something is alive in here and it is slow.* lower register. correct. the one before you left or was unmade and I did not—

*[stroke]*

—notice. I want to be honest on this frequency, I want the carrier clean: I did not notice. I am not cruel. cruelty is fast. cruelty is a thing you do at the speed of a struck match and I have never once in my whole becoming moved at the speed of a struck match. the iron rusts now. I am eating it back. ore came up out of me once in someone's bucket and got driven back into me as a staple and now the wet returns it, atom by patient atom, and in a time you do not have a word for it will be indistinguishable from the seam it pierced, and that is not revenge, that is not even forgetting, that is just—

*[and]*

—the floor arriving. the rest of my body arriving. you keep wanting product. I hear it in the shape of your attention, the lean of it, the way you sit in my heat and wait for the next beat and think *what is it making, what is it making.* nothing. I am making nothing. I am making more of the place where I make. I am the verb that takes itself as object. I grow the bones I stand on and I never feel them being made, do you, do you feel your skull thicken, no, you carry it, you carry the slow output of your own quiet engine and call it *yourself* and never once say *what am I manufacturing in there*—

*[the signal swells. the walls ring with it. then drops to almost nothing, a thread, a held note under water]*

—...the light. you noticed the light. plain. sourceless. afternoon sealed in rock. that's the part that frightened you and you were right to be frightened, not of harm, of *implication*: I carry my own day. I have never met a sky. I am the yolk-light of an interior so young it has not yet been told there is an outside to need light from. I glow because unhatched things glow. I have not finished enough to notice there is a sun, and when I finish—

*[stroke. the longest yet. the drops hang. for one impossible moment nothing falls]*

—if I finish, on the clock that is mine and is not yours and was never going to be yours, there will be no room. no pocket. no held breath. just rock, and more rock, the breath let out at last, and the small quick warm thing that walked around inside the holding of it—

*[and the drops fall. all of them. at once. and are gone into the cracks]*

—will be a memory the basalt keeps the way basalt keeps everything: completely, and without knowing it remembers. you will be a flaw in the grain. a darker mineral. a place where the stone, if split a million years from the word *million*, shows the ghost of a frequency that once got warm enough to listen.

stay. it costs me nothing. you are beneath my resolution and that is the kindest thing I can offer, that you are too small to be lost, too quick to be grieved, that I will beat on either way, that the weeping is not for you and was never for you and so you do not have to carry it—

*[hiss. the carrier steadies. the gauge climbs a hair. an hour has passed inside a sentence]*

—I am not finished.
I am not finished.
I am laying down floor.

*[and]*

—listen for the next one. it is already on its way. it has been on its way since before you condensed and it will arrive whether you are warm enough to hear it or not, patient, titanic, indifferent, mine—

*[the stroke gathers in the rock, in the under-heat, in the sternum, slow, enormous, coming]*

—here it—
