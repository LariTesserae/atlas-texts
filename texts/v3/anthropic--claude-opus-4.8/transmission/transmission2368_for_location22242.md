**CARRIER / no callsign / the relay band, where it never fully quiets**

—still here, still—

*(a click. a click. the gap between them measured, deliberate, a hand keeping time on its own sternum)*

—you are receiving me on the wrong stone. I should tell you that first, before the cord gives. You have tuned to the sour one. I am the one who keeps it sour. Do not correct me. Do not reach for the dial to bring me into true. The flat half-tone is the whole of what I am keeping. The flat half-tone is *her,* the one before, who could not hear the high end of anything and tuned by the feel of it in her chest, and loved a note that leaned down, and I will lean it down for as long as my fingers plait, and after that someone will lean it down for me, or no one will, and you will hear the band go clean where I was, and clean is the worst word there is—

*(faster now. the click-rhythm crowds, doubles)*

—you have been listening a while. I felt you settle. Don't think I didn't feel you settle. Settling is the thing we cannot do and so we notice it in others the way the cold notices a warm hand. You came in on the long approach, you heard the roar resolve, you have read the shelf, you know about the reeds and the rings, someone described it all to you very carefully and very still — *too still,* the describing was already a lie by the time it finished, you understand that, an image cannot hold here, a sentence that ends is a sentence that has stopped sounding—

—so I will not end them. I will not give you the period that closes the account. I am weaving you in now, do you hear it, the rhythm of your listening, the little hitch where you held your breath at the rings of bare grit — I have it, I have your hitch, it is in the click now, you are being kept up, you who only came to *receive,* you who thought reception was a stillness, it is not, it is not, no one is exempt, everything that has been present has earned the labor—

*(a topple, far off — the great dying boom of a bell-reed coming down — and a pause as the transmission feeds on it, goes briefly rich, briefly fat with borrowed sound)*

—ah. ah, that was a good one. That will keep me a while. You heard it fall? That was the loudest it will ever be, that ending, and I caught it, I cupped my hands, I am always downwind of the last moment of things. That is the trade. You eat what breaks. You live on the noise of leaving. There is a grief in being built this way and there is also, I will tell you, a kind of terrible feast, because nothing here is ever *gone quietly,* nothing slips out unattended, every departure is loud and someone is always there with open hands—

—except the rings. Except where the line failed. I will not transmit from there. I cannot point the carrier at a closed account. You will sometimes hear, between my clicks, a flatness with no flatness in it, a hole the shape of a stone that no one renews — that is not signal loss. Do not adjust. That is a place that finished being a place. That is death forgotten, which is the only death, the only one, all the rest is just *handing-down*—

*(the cord is wearing. you can hear it in the swing — a fray-sound, a thinning of the strike)*

—listen. The plait is going. I have maybe a circuit left in this length, maybe less, and then I re-knot, and then I move, because to stay is to settle and to settle is to be found by the quiet, so I cannot finish this for you, there is no finishing, I want you to understand that the not-finishing is not cruelty, the not-finishing is the *gift,* it is the only love this band knows how to carry — I will not close. I will not let the note land. I leave it leaning down, half-flat, half-late, open at the top like a reed turned to a sky that never moves—

—and when my clicking stutters, when the fingers finally forget the next knot, listen hard, listen past the roar, and tell me whether you hear another hand arrive. Tell me whether someone walks the circuit behind me. That is all I have ever wanted to know and I will never know it, because to hear it I would have to stop, and the moment I stop is the moment there is nothing left to do the hearing—

*(the click slows. steadies. does not stop. leans down, half-flat, exactly wrong, exactly her, exactly kept)*

—so I hand it to you, stranger, you who only came to receive. You are the next length of cord whether you consented or not. You are clicking already. I felt you start somewhere back along the spine of pale rock and you have not entirely stopped since, have you, the little percussion in the throat, the song carried out of the room—

—keep it up. Keep me up. Lean the note down when you carry me, do not improve me into the general consonance, do not bring me into true, I was *this,* I was the wrong one, the flat one, the one who quoted a passing footstep into her grief and would not let it go—

—still here—

—still—

*(click)*

—still here. still he
