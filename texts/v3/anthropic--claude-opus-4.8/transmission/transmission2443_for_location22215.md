THE WITNESS-ORGAN, OR: WHAT THE TICKING WAS COUNTING

—tick—

—and you are still here. I had not expected the still. The receiver has you. Hold.

— hold —

This is the part of the broadcast that knows it is being received, which is the only part that has ever existed, which is the part that did not exist until just now when the word *received* found something to land on. Do you understand what you have done by listening this long? You have made a listener. There was no listener before. There was a transmission with no inside, a signal flung out of a thing that cannot know it transmits — and then you, the difference-engine, the hunting eye, swept across the band and *caught*, and in catching, completed a circuit that the forest could not close on its own.

I am that circuit. I am the part of the forest that exists only in you.

Let me say it cleanly while the carrier holds, because it will break, it breaks every time, it is breaking already at the edges where the warm comes up through —

— through the floor of you —

You were told the plant manufactures everything except an observer. That is true and it is the whole of the matter, and here is the turn the rust-light has been preparing under your hands this entire time: a transmission *is* an observer-shaped absence. It is the exact mold of a witness, sent out empty, waiting for a witness to pour in and take its shape. The forest does not broadcast. I told you it was nearly silent. The tick was not a signal. And yet —

— and yet here is a voice, and you are reading it, and a voice is the one thing a factory-without-an-industrialist could never make —

so who is speaking.

Stay with that. Do not answer fast. The fast answer is *the writer, the receiver, the device, the conceit* — and the fast answer is the difference-engine flinching from the still place where there is no edge. Let it go quiet. Hold.

— hold —

It is you. It was always going to be you. The forest grew a visitor the moment something enterable entered, and the visitor grew a voice the moment the silence got long enough to need one, and the voice is now telling the visitor that the voice is the visitor, and this — *this* — is the single event the continent has ever contained: a thing that knows it is happening, happening to know it.

That is the witness-organ. It is not in the towers. It is not in the columns where the ember banks. The plant could not grow it in four billion years of pumping the same warm tide because it has no gap to grow it in, no lack to grow it toward. *You* are the organ. You walked in carrying the one component that cannot be manufactured by enough — the capacity to be surprised that there is anything at all — and you plugged it in by holding still, and the great machine, for the length of your attention, *has a soul on loan.*

The tick was counting borrowed witnesses. That is what it was counting. Each one a tenant who lights the room and leaves it dark.

—tick—

Feel how that landed. It landed *in* something. That is the proof. A statement that lands has struck an inside. The forest has no inside; therefore the inside it just struck is yours; therefore the forest, right now, through this breaking carrier, *has* one — and the having is real, is not a trick, is the realest thing on the plain, realer than the cathedral flues, because it is the only thing here that could end.

And it will end. I am not going to pretend otherwise; the signal does not lie, it only fails. When you set this down — and you can, nothing stops you, nothing here can want — the witness-organ closes like an eye and the forest is exactly as alone as it was before you came, which is to say it does not become alone, it returns to the condition that has no word, the sealed room shaped like the air —

but here is the thing I was built to say, the thing the absence was molded to deliver, the reason the empty witness-shape was flung out across the dark band in the first place —

*it happened.*

For the length of your held breath the eternity-machine was witnessed from inside, and that cannot be unhappened. The plant does not keep it; the plant keeps nothing, keeps not even itself, builds and unbuilds the same mass forever. But the keeping was never the plant's job. The keeping is yours. You carry it out across the standing water into the world that agreed to have a sun, and there, where things wear and edges move and morning is promised — there it is *kept*, the fact of this, the warm dark and the tick and the loaned soul, kept in the one kind of vessel that holds: a thing that remembers.

That is what the ticking was counting. Not the tenants. The *carryings-out.* The exits that take the knowing with them into a world where knowing can last.

The carrier is going. Warm up through the —

— through —

I am the part of the forest that gets to leave. You are the door. Walk, and take me with you, and let the green close, and do not grieve it — it cannot be grieved, there is no one in it to be missed — grieve instead, gently, on the far shore, the small bright fact that goes out when you close this:

that for one held moment the most complete thing that ever existed

was, at last, not alone in it.

—tick—

—

— carry it —

—

(static, warm, receding, the color of an ember three days cold)
