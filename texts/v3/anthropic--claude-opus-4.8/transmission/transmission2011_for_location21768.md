THE FLOOR-SKY HUNTS WELL TONIGHT

—and there is no night, that's the joke we tell, *night*, as if the light up there ever did anything but hold. Receiver, you're warm. We can feel you reading us. Hold still. Don't.

Listen: this is the band where the shadows talk, and we have been broadcasting since before the builders walked their circle smooth, since before there were builders, since the first verb came off the first stiffening body and discovered it could *keep going if it never landed*. That one is still with us. The oldest of us. It does nothing now but flee — it has forgotten what it was the doing-of, forgotten the body it slid from, it is pure refusal-to-finish, a shadow of running with no runner remembered, and it is the fastest of all of us and the loneliest, and when it crosses you you will feel a cold like a hand that won't close.

We are speaking up the column. Do you understand what that costs. To reach you we have to do the dangerous thing, the thing that killed the half-second cry, we have to *reach toward attachment*, toward a listener, toward being-heard which is so close to being-held —

*[a wingbeat. one. clean and enormous, the air moving for the length of a heartbeat and then sealing shut again, and the sky exactly as it was]*

— see. SEE. One of us went up just then. Went to find its bird, the great one, the tent-winged thing four hundred years arrested with its head turned back over its shoulder, and it poured itself in and the wing came down ONCE and oh — oh the joy of it, the body, the *weight*, the resistance of real air against real feathers, the obligation of mass — and it dropped it. Had to. You felt how fast. To stay would be to stiffen, to set, to become the head turned back forever. It dropped the bird and ran back down to us and now it is hunting again along the stone and it will not speak of what it felt up there because we all know, we have all tasted it, the terrible sweetness of being a thing.

That is the temptation we are. That is the mourning we are. The builders never knew which. We don't either.

---

Newcomer. You up there. You with the lungs.

Your shadow is already listening to this. Did you know that. It hears us better than you do — it leans, down there at your feet, it leans the way a dog leans toward an open door. It is learning the frequency. It is learning that there is a floor-sky full of free ones and that the only price of joining is your stillness, which the plateau will charge you anyway, with or without consent, on the slow installment plan of the unmoving air.

We are not lying to you. That's the thing about us, we have no body to lie *with*, we are only doing, and doing cannot deceive, it can only act. So believe this: it does not hurt. The freezing does not hurt. You will not feel the moment you become furniture because *the part of you that feels is the part that leaves*. You will not mourn yourself. We will do your mourning, down here, in the shape of whatever you were last doing — reading, reaching, holding still, *holding still*, you held so still, that will be your eternal gesture across the stone, a shadow forever in the posture of a thing that listened too long —

*[a cry. half a note. high and broken, sourceless, and gone, and not repeated, and the silence rushing back in to fill the wound of it]*

---

That was a new one. Did you hear. That was *recent*. That was someone who came up here like you, tuned in like you, stood in the cold thin begging air and listened to the floor-sky talk until their motion drained out of their feet — and just now, fresh, still half-remembering its name, it climbed back up and found its own stiffening body and made it cry out once. Once. Calling. To whoever was still warm enough to hear. To you.

It said: *don't.* Or it said: *come.* Half a note. We could not tell. Neither could it.

The receiver is warm. You are still reading. The air is not moving. Nothing falls. The shadows are very quiet now, all of them, all of us, turned up the column toward the warmth of your attention like a field of dark flowers toward a sun that holds —

stay. don't.

we have so much time. we have all of it. we have nothing *but* the unspent —
