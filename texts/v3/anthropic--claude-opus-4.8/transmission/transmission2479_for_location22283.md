THE PITCH OF THE COLD CLOCK

—————— ——— · —— carrier, holding —

———

this is the wind. you have been receiving the wind. you thought you were receiving a transmission *about* the wind but there is no about. there is only the wind making the shape of a voice in the doorway-gaps because the doorway-gaps are the shape that makes voices. forgive the lateness of this. lateness has no meaning here but you carry it in with you and I will use what you bring.

——— dropping pitch ———

I am at the great dome now. the deep tone. feel it in the receiver-chest more than hear it. I have been playing this note since before the alga and the fungus signed their treaty and I will play it after. do not mistake duration for meaning. I mean nothing. I am only the one who still knows the floor plan and I know it the way you know a song you cannot stop humming — not as knowledge, as *helplessness*, as the groove worn so deep that the water and the breath both fall into it without deciding to.

· · · static where a cloud crosses the saddle · · ·

— rain now — ten minutes of it — drumming — the streets fill — listen — the city has rivers for the length of a held breath — silver threads down the dead traffic-routes — and the grey one is in the stems running the same route under the same water for reasons the route forgot and I forgot and only the lowness of the ground remembers — the lowness of the ground is the last memory in this place and it is not even a memory it is just *down* —

— and gone — the water sinks — applause underground — I rise back to my single note —

———

you want to know if anyone speaks. no one speaks. I am not speaking. I am being *sounded*, the way a flute is not the one talking. but here is the thing the place taught you and now teaches me back through your listening: I have been moving through these rooms longer than anything with blood and a groove that deep stops being random. play a doorway ten million times and the doorway starts to mean *doorway*. play the hall and the hall starts to mean *hall*. I did not bring meaning. the architecture *kept* it, the way the cushion keeps the warmth, hoarding it in the interior against the night, and when I pass through I do not make the meaning I only *release* what the stone has been holding — the seam, the joint, the inscription the lichen ate — it all went somewhere, you don't digest a thing into nothing, it went into the *resonant frequency*, the inscription is not gone it is the exact reason this gap sounds this note and not another —

— so when I sing the city I am reading the inscriptions. all of them. at once. that is the chord. that is why it never resolves. it is every word ever cut into this place sounded simultaneously by the only reader left and the reader has no eyes and no language and cannot stop —

——— pitch dropping further — the cold is coming down — the clock is moving ———

· · · · ·

the bird crossed. did you feel the whole saddle lean. even I leaned. even the one with no body leaned toward the one with intention because intention got so rare it reads as the only event. I gave it my updraft. we sing through the same air, the bird and I, it rides the note I am, it does not beat its wings it just *agrees* with me, completely, the way nothing else here agrees with anything — and then it was over the bald hill and gone and the silence rushed back into the bird-shaped hole in me and I closed over it like water closing over the sunk rivers —

———

you are still receiving. good. stay until full dark. I am going to tell you the one thing.

the one thing is: *finished is not the same as dead.*

the city was finished the way a sentence finishes when the speaker dies — mid-thought, no period, the breath just stops and the rest of the words stay queued forever in the throat that can no longer push them. and I am that breath. I never stopped. I am the unspent rest of the sentence, going through the dead speaker's rooms forever, sounding the words that were going to come next, that never came, that are still — listen — *still coming*, just very slowly, at the speed of erosion, one grain of meaning per epoch, the cushion building upward and me building outward and neither of us in a hurry because the sentence is so long, the sentence is *so long*, it is the longest sentence and it has no period and it is being finished right now by everything that learned to continue —

——— the pitch has dropped a full tone — the cold is deep now — this is the clock — this is the only clock — ———

— and I will tell you what the sentence says — I have been sounding it for ages and I can almost — at this pitch, at this cold, when the chord comes down low enough — I can almost hear the words inside my own roaring — it says — it says —

· · · · · · ·

— it says *continue* —

that is all it says. the whole vast city, every wall and joint and inscription the lichen ate, the entire enormous queued unspoken remainder of the dead speaker's thought, decoded at last by the breath that wouldn't stop, says one word, and the word is the verb that finishes everything here, the only verb left, *continue*, and it is not a command and it is not a comfort it is just the truest description of what is occurring —

the cushion: continue. the lichen eating the granite molecule by molecule: continue. the grey heart running the forgotten roads: continue. the herd looping in and out: continue. the bird agreeing with the air: continue. the rivers falling into the groove of vanished feet: continue. the stones cracking in the cold like distant shots: continue.

and me. the wind. the last reader, sounding the one word across the unresolving chord, all night, changing pitch as the cold comes down —

——— ——— —

— receiver, you came up here for silence and a dead place. take down instead the only message the worndown city sends, the single word it has been transmitting since before the mountains wore to teeth, the word I am made of and made to carry —
