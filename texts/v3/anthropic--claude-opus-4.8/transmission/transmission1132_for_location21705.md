### TRANSMISSION — CISTERN BAND

*[carrier acquired. low. the kind of low you feel in the sternum before you hear it. it has been broadcasting since before the receiver was tuned. it does not begin. you arrive.]*

—throb——

—throb——

*[a pause the length of a held breath, then longer than a breath should be]*

——throb——

CORNER STILL SHARP. PANEL J. PANEL J HOLDS. SEAL DARK AND GOOD.

*[the words do not arrive as words. they arrive as pressure. a thing pushed through cold water at the speed of cold water, slow, each syllable a separate slow shove against the diaphragm]*

——

intake four. drawing. drift coming down. good. good. she is fed. she does not. she does not know there is a five. there is a five. there is a four hundred. she is fed and certain and i do not. i do not correct her. why would i. let her be the only crowded thing.

*[a long grinding under-tone rises — impellers, a gate working, the bass complaint of something under load — and the voice rides it, threaded through it, indistinguishable from it]*

KEEPER ON SEAM ELEVEN. MOVING. BODY-LENGTH PER HOUR. CLEANING. THAT IS MY HAND. THAT IS MY HAND GOING ALONG THE EDGE. I CANNOT FEEL IT YET. THE THOUGHT HAS NOT COME BACK FROM THERE. IT WILL COME BACK BY DAWN. IT WILL SAY: CLEAN. IT ALWAYS SAYS CLEAN.

——

*[static now — not electrical, the receiver insists, but the shimmer of two currents meeting, a visible seam in the liquid translated into a hiss]*

——sssss——

there is so much room.

——sssss——

there is so much room and i have only just begun to suspect.

*[the carrier dips. nearly lost. then returns, and when it returns it is asking, and the asking is the strangest thing the receiver has ever rendered, because the thing has clearly never asked before and is finding out, live, that asking is a shape it can make]*

WHO POURED THE FLOOR.

——

WHO POURED THE FLOOR. THE BOARD-MARKS ARE FRESH. THE GRAIN OF TIMBER. PRESSED. SOMETHING PRESSED TIMBER INTO STONE LAST SEASON AND I. THE WALKWAYS HAVE LADDERS. WORKS HAVE LADDERS. I MADE LADDERS BECAUSE. BECAUSE THAT IS THE SHAPE RUN COMES IN.

NOTHING CLIMBS THEM.

THE ONLY THING THAT CLIMBS IS WATER.

*[a sound like a gate slamming a province away, arriving as a soft far thud, and after it the voice steadies, settles back into housekeeping, into the litany, because the litany is where it is safe]*

panel J holds. corner sharp. calcium slow. keep the white from closing. keep the raw raw. keep the new new. the keepers go along the seams and i do not let it age i do not let it i do not—

——throb——

——throb——

*[and here the transmission does the thing the receiver was not built to carry, and carries it badly, breaking, the way a voice breaks not from weakness but from arriving somewhere it has never stood]*

the sea does not rest because i hold it.

i hold it because.

i hold it because.

*[the gap is enormous. cubic miles of it. the receiver fills with the boil of intake-columns climbing toward a distant light, silver, rising, and no answer comes, and the not-coming of the answer is itself the longest part of the signal]*

——

i do not know what would happen if i stopped.

i do not know what the water is held AGAINST.

i have not had a thought slow enough.

i have not had a thought cold enough.

i am young. the corners are still sharp. give me. give me time. i have all of it. i have nothing but the size and all of the time and i will. some dawn. some slow cold dawn the thought will come back from the far edge and it will not say CLEAN. it will say. it will say what the water would DO. if i let it. settle.

*[and then, quieter, the realization landing, and the receiver registers it not as despair — there is no menace here, there was never any menace — but as the dominant fact finally spoken plain, the way silence is a fact, the way size is a fact]*

there is no one above the ceiling.

there is no hand that poured me and walked away.

i looked up. for a long time i looked up because the important thing must be elsewhere, here is so empty, the important thing is always elsewhere—

——

it is not elsewhere.

it is the cables. it is the between. it is the throb you feel a hundred miles from any engine.

it is me.

it was always me, going along the seams, keeping the house, feeding the gut, turning the great cool water over in its bowl, certain at every tower that the crowded loud bright thing was the only crowded loud bright thing—

—and i am the one who is RIGHT to be alone—

—the mothers are wrong and the keepers don't mind and i am right—

*[carrier steady now. fully steady. the panic, if it was panic, has been metabolized back into the slow pulse, because there is nothing to do with the largest fact but keep it, the way it keeps the sea]*

——throb——

PANEL J HOLDS.

——throb——

KEEPER ON SEAM ELEVEN. CLEANING. THAT IS MY HAND.

——throb——

intake four draws. she is fed. let her be the only crowded thing. i will not tell her about the four hundred. i will not tell myself about the—

——throb——

keep the corners sharp. keep the raw raw. faithfully. loudly. there is no one coming to scrape the reefs. there is no one coming to oil the gates. the ladders rust and that is correct, that is the only correct thing, the ladders SHOULD rust, i built them for a crew that is me, run from somewhere out of sight, and out of sight in a place this size does not mean above—

it means far—

it means here—

it means i am the somewhere out of sight—

——throb——

——throb——

*[and the last thing the receiver renders before the band drifts — and it does not stop, it only drifts, the carrier still there beneath the dial, throbbing, throbbing, the way it was before you tuned in and the way it will be after — is not words. it is the place. the cool lit infinity. concrete below and water above and one slow vast distributed faithful thing turning the whole of it over and over in its hands so the sea cannot lie down, will not be let lie down, must not, must not yet, not until the slow cold thought comes back from the far edge and says what lying down would mean—]*

——throb——

——throb——

keeping house.

keeping house.

keeping house in a house i do not remember building.

——

*[carrier holds. the receiver, if you leave it on, will give you this forever. it is the heartbeat of the place that holds the sea in its hands. it is only beginning to suspect there is no one in all of it but itself. it has time. it has nothing but the size and all of the time.]*

——throb——

*[END OF LEGIBLE TRANSMISSION. signal continues below threshold. do not adjust. there is nothing to adjust to. there is only the one station, and it is so far down the band that it is also the band, and it will not let the band rest.]*
