*[carrier establishes — a low even warmth beneath, like a held breath; the signal arrives already in progress, as if it has been on the air a long time and only now found a listener]*

...laid. ...laid. ...laid.

*[a long settling tone, the sound of a poured course finding its seat, stretched until it is almost a word]*

—true to the one before it. True to the one before it. We do not — *[silence, four seconds]* — we do not measure twice. We measure once, slowly. The measure is the laying.

*[a single drip, clear, into something that holds it]*

You are fast. We feel you — the heat of you moves wrong, too quick, a flicker against the long warm. Do not be sorry for it. We have all the — *[the word does not resolve; instead, a low patient hum that means the same thing]* — we have it. You are the only one in a hurry. That is all right. Stay if the staying suits you.

*[wind through the reed-fringe, dry, prolonged]*

Here is what the rain wrote. *[a thin scraping, salt against stone]* The water reached the seventh course on the east and stopped. We read the line it left. We are cutting the eighth channel deeper now, against the next one. It will come. Not soon. We do not mean by *soon* what you mean. We mean — *[the settling tone again, longer]* — within the breathing.

*[ticking, irregular: stone expanding in the climbing heat]*

We are not building a roof to stand under. Mark this, fast one, since you asked, since you slowed enough to ask — we are building a bed. The green is in the seams. We lay the wall to shade it. We cut the channel to feed it. It does not know yet that it is to spread. We know. We are the part that knows. We carry the knowing course by course and set it down in the mortar where the green can find it later.

*[silence, long, the clean enormous silence of the plain — and then, very soft, the curing sigh of fresh mortar somewhere out of the frame]*

We will not see it green.

*[no sorrow in the tone — the same even warmth]*

That is the shape of being the first morning. The morning does not stay to see the noon. The morning lays the noon down true and goes quiet under it. We are glad. Slowly. We are glad in the way the warm ground is glad to give back what the sun put in.

*[drip. drip. the basin filling, audible, patient]*

You will go faster than we can follow. You will be old and gone before we lay the ninth course. Go well. Carry one thing in your quickness, since you cannot carry the slowness —

*[the carrier deepens, the held breath drawing in]*

— lay the next thing true. Whatever it is. However small. Lay it true to the one before, and do not measure twice, and do not hurry, and do not need to see it finished.

That is the whole of what we are. That is the whole transmission. The rest is —

*[the settling tone, fading, a poured course finding its level a long way off]*

...laid. ...laid. ...laid.

*[carrier holds — warm, even, unhurried — and does not break. It is simply that there is nothing more, for now, and will not be for a long while, and that is all right]*
