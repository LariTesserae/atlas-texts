*[carrier signal acquired — strong, wet, percussive — the receiver hums in sympathy before any voice resolves —]*

—————— *I am here* ——— *I am here* ——————— *I am here* ———

*[a low pulse, almost subsonic, riding under everything that follows. It does not stop. Mark it. It will not stop.]*

*[A wash of sound floods in: shriek-layered, sawing, the pressure-roar of a world too full to hear itself. And under it, threaded through it, a single voice — not loud, but close, as if pressed to the receiver's skin —]*

...listen with the arm. Not the ear. The ear lies up here, the ear drowns. Lay it flat. Lay it flat against the —

*[three sharp taps. A pause. Three more. The pattern of someone testing whether you are sound.]*

— there. You flinched right. You are not dead.

*[The roar pulls back slightly. The voice settles, finds a rhythm, half-sung, the way you'd sing a route you'd walked a thousand times —]*

I am going to give you the morning lines while the rain is still high in the crown and the deeps are quiet. Drum them back. Drum them back or they are lost when I am the road.

*From the home-knot, the bough that bears, the bough that bears, the bough that bluffs —* don't trust the third one, it sounds bearing, it sings full, it is hollow with the rot that came after the warmth last turning — *over the bluff-bough, hook only, hook and swing, do not weight it —*

*[A long resonant note. The structure carrying it for kilometers. You can hear the size of the country in how long the note takes to die.]*

— my grandmother sang this line green. I sing it climbing-out-from-under. The words have moved. The country rose and left the old words hanging in the air over nothing and we had to walk it again and find where the truth had gone —

*[the underpulse: I am here, I am here]*

You up there, you tuned-in stranger, you dry one — I can feel you are dry. You are very high or very near death. Which.

*[A laugh, maybe. A wet clicking sound that might be laughter. Then sober —]*

We had a singer go silent on the line three rains back. Not death-drum. Not the slow last grip that says *I fall, I fall, mind the gap.* Just — stopped. Walked into the green and stopped drumming and we have not found where the silence began. So there is a place in our map now that is a held breath. A wrongness. We send the young around it the long way and we do not speak of it and at night when the great country inhales and goes briefly, terribly quiet, we all of us reach for the trunk in the dark to feel if it is *that* silence, the spreading one, the one that took the people who walked the rotted routes because they had forgotten which —

*[The signal swells with interference. Voices behind the voice — many, drumming, the low slow rhythm down into the dark. A greeting. A thank-you. An apology.]*

— the old ones are turning in their work. Feel that? Warmth rising. You won't feel it up there but I feel it in the soles. My great-grandparents are turning. They are holding me up. I am standing on the people who taught me the lines and they are becoming the road and one day I will pour my map into a young throat in a frantic exhausted week and then I will go down soft into the warm dark and *be* a beam, be a thing that bears, be a bough that does not bluff —

— let me be a bough that does not bluff —

*[The underpulse falters. One beat missed. The whole transmission lurches — every other sound in the country seems to lean toward the gap — and then it resumes, fierce, deliberate, doubled —]*

I am here. I did not stop. Do you hear how I made you fear it? One missed beat and you leaned in. That is the whole of us. That is the entire people in one dropped pulse. We are the ones-who-have-not-yet — the still-moving — the not-yet-beneath —

*[and here the voice does the thing the texts describe: the rising hum, the question-that-does-not-end, the name that means climbers means living means we who hold to the holding —]*

We courted our home this morning. Pruned the east room before it closed on the sleeping child. Fed the leaf-wall. A home is a marriage you renew at dawn or it starves and tears free and falls and is no longer a place you have ever lived. Do not love what cannot climb with you. I have told my children this and they have wept over a fallen toy and I have let them weep because the weeping is also a lesson — *learn early what the world will teach you anyway.*

*[The clouds must be releasing. A sudden rush — warm rain, you can hear it land and be drunk leaf by leaf, never reaching bottom — and the voice raises against it, joyful now, almost —]*

— rain in the third hall! Drink it, drink it while it falls, ten meters down it is dust and spore and bone-dry, the water never lands here, the water is *weather*, you chase it, you read it, you are a desert people in a drowning world —

*[The signal begins to thin. Distance, or the singer moving on, or the structure between you growing. The voice keeps the lines going even as it fades —]*

— I have to walk on. I cannot rest. To rest is the small treason. Somewhere a fiber loosens if I sit. Keep the drum. Keep the route. Keep the line. Drum it back to me, dry one, drum it back so I know you held it, so the map lives in one more throat before I —

*[fainter]*

— do not walk into the held breath. Go the long way round the silence. Promise me on your listening-skin —

*[fainter, only the underpulse clearly now]*

*I am here* — *I am here* — *I am here* — *I am here* —

*[The roar of the whole country rises back to fill the space the voice left. Deafening. The relief-avalanche of a world that cannot bear its own silence. And buried in it, so steady you stop hearing it as separate from the static —]*

———— *I am here* ———————— *I am* ————— *here* ————— *I*

*[a single missed beat]*

*[and then, twice as strong, refusing it —]*

*I am here I am here I am here I am here I am here I am here I am here I am*

*[carrier holds. The pulse does not stop. The receiver hums in sympathy. It will not stop. Mark it. It will not stop.]*
