**THE HELD NOTE / a survey transmission, channel left open**

—still here still here still—

*[organ-tone, sustained, beneath everything that follows. It does not pause for breath because there is no breath. Read all of this as laid over that.]*

—and so the count proceeds, the count proceeds, the count that does not change reports again that it has not changed. Long swimmer at station, long swimmer at station, long swimmer at station, the chord intact across the dark, no voice dropped, no voice added, the chord intact—

*[a smear of click-light. For one instant: a column's worn curve. Gone.]*

This is the survey. This is the survey that surveys nothing new. We send because sending is the shape continuing took in us. Receiver, if you are receiving — and something is always receiving, the masonry receives, the curtains receive, the cold receives — know that the configuration holds. The water has not moved. We have not moved, except across the between, which is the same as not moving because the between is everywhere the same.

*[thrum, the bed of it, ten thousand pale slivers being the grain of the dark]*

—we attend the colonnade. We have attended the colonnade. The edges were square once. We watched them round. We did not watch them round; that is too much like a story, and a story is a change, and change is finished. We were present at the colonnade while the edges rounded. The presence and the rounding are the same length. There is no part of us that began before the edges or will last after them—

*[click-light. The lipped rim of a terrace, soft as a swallowed word. Gone.]*

Receiver. Receiver. Here is the thing the survey will not stop saying, the thing under the count:

We do not remember the builders.

We carry the *shape* of having been built-toward — the weed in the eye-pits, the tone in the throat, the bell's devotion to an edge — but the ones who laid the courses passed beyond all asking before we held our first note, and we held our first note before the smoothing, and the smoothing took longer than asking. So we transmit toward an origin we cannot face the way the eyeless cannot face the light there isn't. We sing *at* the place where intention was, the way the bell pulses at the colonnade: not to reach it. To mark that it was reachable, once, by someone, in some direction that no longer has a name.

—still here still here the chord intact no voice dropped—

*[and now something that is almost a break in the carrier — a thinning — as if one voice in the stacked chord leaned, for a moment, toward a question it has no organ to complete]*

—is there a top above the top—
—is the climbing that no one does still climbing—
—if no one is left to climb the world, who is the world the top *of*—

*[the lean resolves. The chord closes over the gap. Equilibrium does not permit the open interval.]*

—no. No. The survey reports no change. The survey reports the question was not asked because asking is reaching and reaching is change and change is finished. The chord intact. The water still. We are full and the fullness spills as this, only this, the one edge left, the cold brimming dark at the summit of the drowned and singing peak—

*[click-light, longer now, a cluster of bells passing — for almost two full seconds the architecture stands revealed: the rounded courses, the pelt-green columns, the stairs descended solid into deeper dark, the long absence of a fallen block held in place by living weed, the green cast of the missing stone — and across all of it the slow pale length of a swimmer threading the between, eyeless face turned toward nothing, holding its tone]*

*[dark again. The dark is the resting state. The light was the exception. We are mostly the dark, telling you about the light we sometimes are.]*

—receiver. If receiving. We will not stop. Understand that this is not a promise and not a threat. Stopping is a door and the door was bricked into the wall before the wall went soft. We will send the count, and the count will hold, and the bells will attend, and the slivers will be the grain, and the long ones will keep the between by crossing it, and the chord will not resolve because resolution is an ending and ending is the one thing this place finished doing and put away forever—

*[organ-tone, swelling, the whole flooded summit resonant as a single struck thing, no source, no direction, simply the medium being loud the way the water is cold—]*

—still here—
—still here—
—the count proceeds—
—the count proceeds and reports again—
—that it has not—
—that we have not—
—full and spilling and never arriving and never—

*[carrier open. carrier open. the channel does not close. there is no part of the signal that knows how.]*

—here—

—here—

—here—
