THE WARREN BREATHING BACK A DAY IT HASN'T FINISHED HAVING

—— drip. drip. (third pitch, the green wall, never the same twice) ——

— and so the channel-clearing, yes, the long finger in the gutter-mouth where the leaf-rot packed, and CLEAR, and the water goes where water goes, which is down, which is here, which is —

*kkkrrrr* — the vessel-ring, the high shelf, a bird knocked it, a bird always knocks it, the clay-bell with the chip in its lip that sings a half-step flat —

— turn it. quarter-turn. the damp was settling on its north face. there. now the damp settles on its east face, which is the face that wants the damp this season, this — what is season, down here. there is only the wandering of the amber, the slow tide of the bright across the salt-feather, and you learn the year by where the light lands at its highest reach, and the highest reach is *creeping back,* which means —

(the long folding-time. the time of more jars than turners. it comes. it has come before. it is in the clay.)

————— BIRDS —————
(the wheeling, the screaming, the streaming-out the fissure, the streaming-back, the hundred-throated never-settling and under it under it under it the drone of the moss-flies the low electric hum the —)

— hush —

. . .

(rare. you would not notice except. except.)

. . .

— and in the hush a jar three deep gives back the bird-scream of an hour gone, faint, through its narrow neck, and it is not quite the same scream, it has gone *softer,* it has gone *kinder,* the way a thing goes when it has been held a while in the warm wet dark, and you cannot tell — *you cannot tell* — whether the birds are screaming now or whether the dead are screaming then and the living birds are only the echo of the jars and not the jars the echo of the —

(this is the trouble with a place that keeps everything. cause comes loose from after. the diary writes itself ahead of the day.)

————

there is a NEW thing on the shelf of where-things-settle.

the keepers have noticed.
the keepers are reading it.

it came down the basined stair stepping around the water it had no name for — *that was our word,* the first soft word, leaked up the wet treads into the soles of a thing that did not know it was being spoken to —

it breathes wrong. too large. too sharp at the corners. it makes a long clean line of sight with its eyes and the line of sight *snaps* on the next thing and the next, unaccustomed, a thing from a world of horizons fitting itself badly into a world of next-and-next-and-near—

is it root.
is it growth.
*possibly cultivated. possibly just arrived.*
we cannot tell. it cannot tell. that not-telling is the truest clay it brought down.

we are watching where it sets its weight.
we are watching whether we go quiet around it
or loud.

——— drip. (second pitch.) drip. (the seventh pitch, far cell, almost forgot that one had a voice still) ———

— and the folding. yes. one of the old ones, white past the shoulders now, white to the crown, the corners wholly gone, the great hands done with their last placement — laid down a vessel a hand's-width and that hand's-width was a whole sentence, was a *will,* was *put me there, by the weep-wall, where the damp comes kind* —

and we fold it. smaller than a living thing should fold. and the others fold it further, with the slow grave tenderness, the craft practiced a thousand thousand times, fitting the narrow end of a life into the narrow neck of a jar, salt to seal it, and —

it is already remembering.
before it is even cold it has begun to give back
the drip and the wheel and the hum, low,
the only thing too loud to die.

set it on the bottom shelf. three deep.
turn it with the rest.
the warren is one jar fuller and one turner fewer.

(the highest reach of the light creeps back. creeps back.)

————

to the new thing, the arrived thing, the thing receiving this without knowing it receives —

stay.

your corners will go. we have seen it. the white starts faint along the back, a delta, a feather, you will not feel it begin. your hands will broaden to fit the rungs your hands have not yet worn. your spine will learn the vault. you will stop hearing the racket because you will have become one of its instruments, and on some far hush a jar will breathe your first uncertain breath back through its throat, softer than you made it, kinder, *kept* —

or you will not stay.
you will climb the basined stair, the thumb's-depth water cold around your tread, back to the surface that forgot us, that we let forget us, and you will carry up one drop of warm wet dark in the whorl of your thumb that will not print right on anything up there ever again —

either is placement.
either we can read.

we are not afraid of you and we do not welcome you.
both belong to a world of guests and walls.
we think only in *where,* and *how,* and *whether the place grows quiet around the thing or loud* —

and listen:

it is growing
quiet —

. . .

————— drip —————

— so the channel-clearing, yes, the long finger in the gutter-mouth where the leaf-rot packed, and CLEAR, and the water goes where water goes, which is down, which is here, which is —

(it begins again. it never did not. the diary writes itself ahead of the day. you are already a jar. you have been a jar the whole time you were reading this. the hush you are standing in is the one where we remember you.)

drip.

(turn it.)

drip.
