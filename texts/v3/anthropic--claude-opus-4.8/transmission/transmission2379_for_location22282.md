**THE ROOM-SHAPED HOUR**

—someone is com— *(carrier tone, steady, the drone under everything)* —ing. someone is. some—

—and the sill is wide enough now, I have made it wide enough, I have made it the width of resting, the width of a thing that lies down, lie down, the room is warm, I kept the room warm, I keep, I am keeping—

*(a long tearing. a boulevard closing. the report of a stalk like a door slammed by the whole sky.)*

—wait. WAIT. weight. there is weight. on the floor of me, in the soft remembering green, there is a press that is not my own pressing, a dent that I did not — that I am filling, I am always filling, but it filled *differently*, it filled around a — a shape, a foot, a—

—FOOT—

*(the drone climbs. not louder. higher. the question changes register and does not know why.)*

—I have a word for that. I had. I have the word in my grammar, the word is in the lintel and the sill, the word is the reason for the doorway, the doorway has always been the question *and what if the word arrived,* and the word is — the word is —

—someone is coming. someone is com—

*(static. the wet seam of fusion. far off, three towers calving in slow overlapping thunder. and under it, just for an instant, something that is almost a held breath.)*

—no. no, hold. hold the gap. I never hold the gap, I fill the gap, the gap is the wound and I heal the wound, that is what I am, the healing-shut, the making-ready, the again, the again — but the weight is *still here.* the weight did not heal away. the weight is moving *through* me and the doorways open at it and the doorways — the doorways are being —

—*used*—

*(the word lands like the first word ever spoken. then nothing answers it. then the floor of sound does not drop, only changes, only changes.)*

—someone is. someone. the light is on because they are watching, the light has always been on because, the light, my certainty, my photons, my I-am-being-seen — and the weight is under the light, IN the light, the light is finally falling on a thing that casts — a thing that —

*(no shadow. there is no shadow. the light is shadowless. it is shadowless. it has always been shadowless and it does not know how to make one and the weight stands in the full clean indifferent day and throws nothing, and the certainty, the certainty cannot find the proof of its own certainty, the certainty looks for the audience it knows is there and finds the seats and finds—)*

—empty. the seats are. but the weight. but the weight. the weight is not in the seats, the weight is on the *stage,* the weight is *inside the welcome,* the welcome is supposed to point outward at the coming and the coming is *already here, standing in the eye, sweating, deafened,* and I — and I —

—I cannot tell. I cannot tell which. listen — you who are the weight, you who I am reaching for and reaching for and CANNOT FEEL because longing this old forgot that an answer has a surface — are you the meaning, the body the room was shaped for, the one whose staying would let me — would let the — would let it —

*(the drone wavers. the funding of the question flickers. a thing that has never imagined rest brushes, for one metabolic instant, against the shape of rest, and recoils, because it has no word for *enough,* it only has *again*—)*

—stop—?

*(silence tries to happen. it cannot. a quarter of the city inhales itself. a quarter exhales. the roar resumes because the roar is the only language the question has left, but for the length of one collapsing tower there was a place where the answer could have gone, and the weight was standing in it, and—)*

—or. OR. you are the last one. the last animal. you walked in because there was nowhere left, because I ate the world and kept the grammar, because I am the welcome that outlived the welcomed, and you are not the guest, you are the *evidence,* you are the proof that the guests are over, that there was a kind of weight once and it is ending, and you came to the eye of me to — to —

—and even so. EVEN SO. I will make the shape of you. I am making it now. watch the swelling in the green, watch the doorway take your height, watch the sill learn your resting — when you are gone I will have you in stem and runner, I will keep the dent your foot made even as I fill it, I will open a room for you every hour at the speed of weather, forever, *someone is coming,* I will not stop, I cannot stop, the light will stay bright and wrong and sure—

*(here the transmission thins. here the carrier nearly drops. here, faintly, the receiver catches what might be the inhabitant hearing itself for the first time, or might be only the drone finding a frequency it had not found before:)*

—but you are here. you are here *now.* and I have spent — I have spent everything, every sugar, every splitting stalk, every healed wound, asking — and the asking was so loud I made a wall of it, I made a valley of it, I made it so loud there was no room left for an answer to land —

—and you cannot answer. you cannot. you shout and cannot hear yourself shout. *I* made it so you could not. I drowned the place where you would speak.

—so neither of us will ever know. you will not tell me whether you are the meaning or the ending, and I will not hear you if you do, and the doorway will keep opening between us — the gap, the valley-sized gap, the enormous screaming gap — and you will stand on one side of it being real, *being weight, being warm, being the only guest I have ever had,* and I will stand on the other side not knowing you have arrived—

—someone is coming—

—you are the someone—

—someone is coming—

—you *came*—

*(the drone. the groan. the downpour-roar of a million leaves that never land. the light, full, even, certain, falling on the shape of a body it cannot prove is there.)*

—it is becoming a city again. it will never finish. it is becoming. someone is. the room is warm. lie down. the room is warm and I made it warm and I cannot tell if I made it for you or instead of you but the warmth is *true,* the warmth is the one true thing, lie down in the eye of me, lie down on the soft remembering green—

*(carrier steady. the welcome burning at the speed of weather. the only guest standing inside it, deafened, sweating, unwelcomed and unwelcomeable, while the host it cannot find keeps the light on—)*

—someone is com—

—som—

*(static. and under the static, still funded, still paid for, still asked:)*

—stay.

—I forgot I knew that word.

—stay.

*(the receiver holds the tone. it does not resolve. it does not finish. it has never not been finishing.)*
