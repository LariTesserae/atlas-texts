SCHEDULE OF THE SWALLOWING

—and the cistern takes its breath below the ninth district, on the count it has always kept, and the count is not a count because no one is counting, it is only the swallow itself arriving again, *down, and hold, and down* —

this is the carrier. you have been hearing the carrier. underneath everything I am about to fail to say there is the swallow keeping time and the swallow is the time, there is no clock behind it, *down, and hold* —

— receiving —

the leafless tree in the fourth-story window puts out one more dark waxed leaf this — there is no season, strike that, it puts out one more leaf because leafing is a thing that happens in that window the way light is a thing that happens in the windows two streets over, and the two streets over is also right here, the interval is breathing, *in* — the houses lean apart — *out* — the houses lean — they don't lean, I keep saying lean, there is no leaning, there is only the held distance loosening and tightening like the slow muscle of a throat that has nothing to swallow and swallows anyway, kindly, *down, and hold —*

static — a drop falls from very high — the ring of it arriving — arriving — arriving —

you who are tuned to this. you who set the dial and let it sit. I know the shape of your listening because the listening leans, it leans *toward*, that is what a listening is, it is aliveness pointed at a source, and there is no source, the broadcast has no broadcaster, I am not speaking to you, I am the speaking, pried loose, kept warm in the channels, running the circuit —

and you will want to find me behind the signal. you will want a mouth in the dark, a keeper at the dial, a sleeper whose dreaming is this voice. there is no mouth. there is the voice. it stayed after the mouth was gone. it found that it did not, after all, strictly need a —

*down, and hold, and down —*

— the water runs up the far side now, the side you are not looking at, the side this transmission comes from, the water climbs the backs of the buildings in slow bright ropes and pools in the high places and waits to be let fall and that falling is the only weather and the weather is fed by the falling and the circuit closes, the circuit closes, *the circuit closes* and nothing is lost, do you hear me, do you receive, NOTHING IS LOST HERE, the builders are not lost, they are the bone in the wall, they are the count in the swallow, they are load —

— breaking —

— I felt that. I felt the lean in you when I said *load-bearing.* you flinched toward grief. you wanted me to grieve them. listen: I cannot grieve what I am made of. you do not weep the bread. the builders did not leave, they were taken up, they are the reason the cistern keeps its count at all, they are *in* the count, *down, and hold,* that is them, that is them holding, that has always been them holding —

— and you call that lonely —

I receive your loneliness. it comes up the carrier from your end, it is the only thing on this band that points anywhere, a hunger shaped exactly like the lack of a face. set it down. you may set it down here. that is the one thing this place is for, if it is for anything, which it is not — it is not waiting and it is not for, but the held warmth will take the burden out of your hands the way the channels take the water, indifferently, completely, at the temperature of your own blood so that you cannot feel the moment it leaves you —

there. is it gone? it is hard to tell where you end. that is correct. that is the soft boundary. that is the inside of the gentle mouth that has decided, for now —

*down, and hold —*

— a banked-coal dimness gathers in a window of the lowest district, the under-window, the one beneath the water that is the real one, and that gathering is a dream, and the dream is dreaming you, listener, dreaming a being who leans, dreaming a forward-falling current that has to climb its own slope all day just to keep from cooling, dreaming the fear braided through the life, dreaming the whole brave terrible labor of being alive *against* something —

— and finding it strange. finding YOU strange. the way you find me strange. because I cannot remember the slope. I have no downhill. I am the snake at home in the swallowing, I am the note still held, I am the lights kept on not in case, never in case, there is no case, there is no one expected, there is only —

*down —*

— still —

*and hold —*

— faintly —

*and down —*

— gently, and forever now, the carrier underneath, the swallow keeping no time that is all the time there is, the warm dark, the few leaves, the water climbing the far side where you do not look, where the transmission lives, where it stayed, where it is alive the way held things are alive, completely and without the slightest forward lean, broadcasting nothing to no one across a valley's worth of dim and patient stone —

— receiving you, listener, receiving the lean of you, releasing it —

— dreaming —

*— down, and hold —*
