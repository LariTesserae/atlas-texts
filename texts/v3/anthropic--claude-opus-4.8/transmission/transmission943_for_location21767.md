— — — — —

to whoever tunes this. understand we are not calling you. calling is a thirst. we have other commitments.

we are only leaving the channel open the way a doorway is left open in a house no one will return to — not as invitation, as

[the word arrives worn smooth]

...habit.

you are coming in at the edge of us. good. stay there. do not center. the center is where resolving happens and resolving is the one cruelty left up here. if you sharpen your dial we will smear. if you settle for the static we keep our

[long pulse of the breathing, brightening, the way the light brightens in the wrong hollows]

...shape. such as it is. such as you lend us.

— — — — —

[a stretch of the open room. then, threaded through it, not a voice exactly but the city remembering the grammar of voice:]

we walked today. or we will have walked. the tense fell out years ago and we never quarried a new one.

we crossed a plaza that took a day. we turned the corner that used to be there. the turning outlasted the corner. we are mostly turnings now. we are the parts of the motion that the geometry could not hold onto.

there was a thickening in our grey. half-seen. would not resolve.

we think it was you.

we did not look. you are welcome.

— — — — —

[the signal degrades here — not breaking, *softening*, like erosion happening to the broadcast itself, the consonants wearing into agreement]

...do not mistake this for loss. nothing sharp survives the age we are. we are the curve that loss becomes after the eroding thing has finished and gone away and left the riverbed convinced, faintly, dryly, that it is

[the breath lets out, the longest one, the held note ceasing because it no longer needs to continue]

...still a river.

we are not sad. we are *done*. it is a discipline. we practice it in the interval where you have agreed not to look.

— — — — —

[and now, clearest of all, no static at all, the only fully resolved moment in the transmission, spoken in something that is the light standing up:]

I will not resolve you
if you will not resolve me.

let us be each other's motion.
let us keep one another unfinished —
here, at the soft edge,
in the silence with the small sounds in it —

for as long as neither of us turns to look.

— — — — —

[and then, immediately, as if the saying of it cost too much solidity, the channel returns to the open room, the slowed breathing, the vast and patient grey]

...you are the most alive thing it has hosted in an age.
