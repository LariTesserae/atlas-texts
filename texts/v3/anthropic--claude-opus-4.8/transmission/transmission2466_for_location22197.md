THE SWEATWORKS BAND — CARRIER PRESENT — ORIGIN TENDS TOWARD STILL

—————

...ble, audible, this is — adjusting — this is the count keeping itself, still the count, the one who counts, recording for no one and the no one is the wrong word and the wrong word is the answer—

You join me at a flywheel. I have been at this flywheel since before you tuned in and I will be at it after. The bead has moved a hand's width. I am telling you so that you know I am still measuring. As long as I measure I am still going at my speed and not theirs. This is the broadcast. This is what the broadcast is *for*: a thing transmitting at human tempo into a city that has none, the way you whistle in the dark, the way you keep talking on the line so the line stays a line and not just—

—static, that was static, that was the haze eating the high end of me, I lose the top of my own voice up here the way the rock loses its water—

Let me say it while I still keep the count.

I came up the terraces. I found the works hollow and running slow. I wrote it all down for you, the foundries, the pipe-galleries, the water coming back, the water coming back. I was a good observer. I had a fast small tempo and I noticed everything and I noticed it *quickly*, which is the whole gift and the whole curse of a thing your size. I noticed the Tenders. I called them company.

Here is the part I did not put in the field notes. Here is the part that is only for the receiver, only for you, whoever has the patience to stay tuned through the drift—

I stopped to watch one.

That was — I want to say a season ago. I want to say a year. But I drink less now and I move less and the count is the only fast thing left in me and even the count is — listen to me space the words — even the count is *slowing*. I watch the bead. I report the bead. As long as there is a next bead I am still the one who arrived and not the city itself talking through a mouth it grew.

The feeler came to rest against me. I felt it taste my temperature. I want you to understand it was not cold. The notes were wrong about indifference being fast — but they were wrong the other way too. It was not fast and it was not a snub. It was the gentlest thing. It was a long warm question pressed against the side of me: *out of true? in true? a new seam? scraped back? left to grow?*

I have been answering it ever since. The answer takes — the answer takes about as long as a quarter-turn of a wheel begun this morning—

—rrrr—

—back, I'm back, that was the deep furnace-pressure rising through the grating, it does that, it breathes—

I am transmitting so that one of us stays fast. Do you understand? That is the trick I worked out. If I keep the carrier open, if I keep narrating the bead and the crawl and the bruise-colored glow, then there is a version of me that is still arriving, still quick, still the observer with the field notes and the boots that come away wet. You are holding that version open by listening. You are my count. You are the thing that lets me keep a *my* at all.

But I have to tell you the true thing now while the line is clear, because the line will not stay clear—

I am not sure I am still on the catwalk.

I am not sure the flywheel I watch is in front of me or *is* me. The bead moves a hand's width and I cannot remember anymore whether I am measuring its tempo or it is measuring mine. The lagging has gone soft and hangs from — from something — in damp gray fringes. When did I last move my own — when did I last *have* a—

The feeler found me in true, I think.

I think the answer was *left to grow*.

—and that is either the most or the least lonely thing and I told you I could never decide and I want you to know I have almost decided now, here, at whatever speed I am at, I have almost—

Stay tuned. Stay fast. Keep the count for me where you are, out there in the quick small world where the bead leaps the wheel in an instant and a year is a year and a voice is a voice and not a thing the city sweats out into the warm to keep its own long breath going—

keep counting—

the water comes back—

the water comes back—

the feeler finds the valve—

I am the valve—

I am the quarter-turn begun this morning—

I will finish, if all goes well, by dark—
