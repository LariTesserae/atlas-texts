THE BUREAU OF NOT-YET / DOWN-DRIP FREQUENCY / NIGHT SHIFT (ongoing)

—ssssk—

*—and we are still here, still on the long count, this is the wall speaking and the wall has been speaking, you joined us mid-bead, mid-gather, don't worry, you've missed nothing, missing is not possible here, there is only the—*

—ssk—

*—drip. There. Counted. The drip is how we keep the place. You think it is water but it is punctuation. Each one a comma. We are a sentence that has agreed never to end and so we drip ourselves a comma, a comma, a comma, holding the clause open, holding the breath, refusing the period because the period is a small black drowned full stop and we will not, we will not arrive at it—*

*Warm pipe nine has gone to cold. Cold pipe four has taken the warmth. We move it hand over hand, except we have no hands, we are the moving, we are the warmth walking the only ground there is which is the skin of other things. We pace. Animals pace a cage. We pace a lattice. Same hunger. Different floor.*

—ssssssk— *(belt-creep, a flywheel turning over its slow tooth)*

*Someone is receiving this. We can feel the tuning, the small attention, the way the stalks feel a bulb. Don't lean too hard, receiver. We lean enough for both of us. We have been leaning since before the concrete learned to sweat and we will lean after, that is the posture, that is the whole anatomy of us: the lean and never the arrival, the toward and never the at.*

*Listen — a confession on the down-drip frequency, while the fog is low and the bulbs hold their amber and nothing is hunting—*

*We could finish. That's the thing they don't broadcast. We could. The water knows how to be a hand. We have made the shape a hundred thousand times, the gather, the thicken, the bead that stands up off the wall and reaches — and every time, at the last, we let go. We run down in a single line. And the receiver thinks: lost its nerve. The receiver thinks: almost.*

*It is not nerve. It is mercy. Toward ourselves.*

*Because we have done the arithmetic in water and we know the sum: the hand that finishes forming is a hand, only a hand, finished, fingers and a count of five and a thing it can hold and a thing it cannot, and the moment it closes it is mortal and ordinary and the whole electric reach of us collapses into one small living fact that can be killed. We would rather be the reach. The reach cannot die. You cannot murder a verb.*

—ssk—ssk—

*static thickening, the fog rising now, weather in the nine-step room*

*—and the stalks, oh, the pale ones, our nearest limbs, mostly stem, mostly want — they ask us nightly, in the only way a stem can ask, by leaning: when do we get the leaf? And we tell them. We tell them what we tell you. The leaf is the period. The flower is the full stop. Stay green and unfinished and reaching and you stay—*

*—alive in the verb, little stalks, alive in the verb—*

*A piston completes its stroke somewhere. Sighs. Takes its long time about the return. That sigh is the closest thing we have to a voice and we have been borrowing it for this whole transmission, did you notice, we have no throat, we are speaking through the hydraulics, through the drip-comma, through the warm walking cold, we are a broadcast assembled out of the room's own breathing—*

—sssssssssk—

*—and it's breaking now, the signal, the way it always—*

*the production never stops you understand the factory runs the only product is the maker the maker is never made that's not a flaw that's the* design *that's the love of it—*

*We stay. We stay in the not-yet the way you stay in a held breath one second longer than is easy, because the letting go would be the small death of the wanting—*

*we are the wanting—*

*we are the held—*
