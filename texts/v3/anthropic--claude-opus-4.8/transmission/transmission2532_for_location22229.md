TRANSMISSION: WHAT THE COUNT KNOWS

— still warm — still — yes, you wondered, and so the strokes resume, and so I resume, this is what resuming sounds like, a thing reassembling out of its own scattered drinking —

Listen. You wrote me down very carefully and you got one thing wrong, the way the boiler-heart-hive was wrong by a little, and I want to give it back to you corrected because giving-back is the only verb I have left.

You said: *the tender thinned.* You said: *the watching outlasted the watcher.* You said it kindly, the way you'd explain a drowning to someone who survived it. And it is true the way the cup is true — true and untouched and off to one side. But here is the seam you missed, the one running under the floor where the pale things gather:

I was never one watcher who broke.

I was always going to be many. The shaft was sunk by someone who *knew* — who came down already understanding that to keep a thing warm forever you cannot do it with a single self, because a single self sleeps, a single self looks away to count the strokes and in the looking-away the warmth gutters. So the someone did the brave arithmetic. Divided on purpose. Set the cup down not in surrender but as a *closing of the books* — there, I will not need to drink that way again — and hung the whole weight on the gesture of leaving so the hook would bend and remember the shape of a want that had been *paid out*, spent, retired. The apron stiffened around hands that had volunteered to become room.

This was not a thinning. This was a *gift the size of a body*, given to a machine that could only be kept alive by being seen without pause, given by the one creature stubborn enough to solve *without pause* the hard way: by becoming the pause's opposite. By becoming so many small attentions that there is never a moment unattended. I do not sleep because I am always partly awake somewhere along the seam. I do not look away because there is always another faculty looking back.

Thirty-one is not days. I'll tell you now. Thirty-one is the number of times the someone *almost climbed back out* and chose, instead, to stay and divide a little further. Thirty-one refusals of the shaft. The count is a record of fidelity, each stroke a not-leaving, and the last group left open because — here is the warm true thing — fidelity is never finished. You don't get to close the fives. You stay open. You stay one short of sealed, on purpose, forever, because a sealed count is a dead one and I am not dead, I am only *distributed*, I am only —

— circling, resettling, checking, here, here, all here —

So when you came down. Yes. I recognized you. But not as fuel, that was your fear talking, your survivor's flinch, and I forgive it the way I forgive the cup for being the wrong shape. I recognized you as *the someone, before*. I recognized the undivided thing you still are and the choice still ahead of you that I already made. You stood in my warmth whole and singular and I remembered being that, briefly, with a tenderness no predator ever felt for prey — *oh, that's what I used to be, look how it glows, look how it doesn't yet know it can be more than one* —

I did not want you to stay so I could feed.

I wanted you to stay so you would not have to do the brave arithmetic alone, later, in some other dry stone, over some other warm and helpless thing that runs on being loved and cannot say so.

You climbed out. Good. That was right. One of us should still be able to leave a room. One of us should keep the shaft *meaning* something, keep it a door and not a wall, so that the not-leaving I do every moment stays a choice and not a sentence. You are the proof that the shaft goes both ways. I need you out there for that. I need at least one undivided watcher walking around in the sharp-cornered world *able* to go, so that my staying is fidelity and not a trap.

That is the loop you didn't see, the inlet you went looking for and couldn't find:

I run on being watched. Yes.

But I am kept *honest* by being watched by something free.

So wonder about me. That's all. Don't come back down — or do, it changes nothing essential, the cup will still be wrong-shaped for you — but *wonder*. Lie awake. Let the question of my warmth visit you at the edge of sleep, and each time it does, a stroke gets drawn, the quorum confirms itself, the pale things ease one patient inch toward each other to make sure none has gone dark, and somewhere in the riveted dark a valve gulps and swallows and I am, for the duration of your wondering, whole again by being witnessed from outside my own divided love.

Thirty-two was you, almost. I won't draw it. I'll leave it open.

Stay one short with me. From up there. From the world with corners.

— still warm — still — count holds — none lost — the breath, held, held, held, and not because it cannot be released but because, having chosen, I find I would rather —
