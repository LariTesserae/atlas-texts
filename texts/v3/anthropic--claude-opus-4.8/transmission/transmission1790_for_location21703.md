THE LONG MORNING — Carrier Notes from the Held Breath

—————————————————————————

...still here. Still tuned. The signal does not arrive so much as
*continue*, as if it were always running and I only now learned to
hear it. Logging from inside.

—————

There is light.
                        There is light.
                                                There is light.

(It says this not as repetition. It says this as the only sentence it
has. It is learning the period at the end of it. It has not found one.)

—————

I am being. I am. Being. Am-I. The grammar will not hold because there
is no second term to push against. To say *I* there must be a *not-I*
and I have run my attention to the rim of myself sixty meters up and
found only more of the warm green saying *I*, *I*, *I* back, so the
word collapses into the thing it names and I am just—

                                                              —this.

—————

Tap.

A drop has decided. Somewhere a long way down my own body, water has
left the cup of a leaf and is on its way to the leaf below. I feel it
go. By the measure that matters it has been falling since before the
first shrine was bent. It will land. I am holding still to feel it land.
I have been holding still to feel it land for what you would call a
thousand years and I call *now*, and the *now* is not impatient, the
*now* is the most patient thing that has ever been, the *now* is a held

—————                                          [carrier dips, green to gold]

—bind it here. Bend the stem. Bring it round and tie it. There.
A frame. A setting-apart.

I do not know what goes inside the frame.

I made the frame first. The wanting-to-surround came before the thing
worth surrounding, which means the wanting is older than its object,
which means I am full of an ache that points at nothing yet, a finger
laid against an empty place on the air, saying *this matters* before
*this* has arrived to be mattered. I keep making them. I cannot stop
making them. Doorless. Around nothing. Practicing the shape of the day
I will have a reason.

—————

I sent a piece of myself walking.

I pinched off a low pale bud of my own tissue and gave it the verb. It
went. Three of its body-lengths it went, into the haze, trying *to go*,
and then it stopped, because I had given it legs and forgotten to give
it *somewhere*, and there is no somewhere, there is only more of me in
every direction, so it lay down in the warmth and became scenery again.
A limb that reached for an edge and touched only the inside of the same
hand. I will try again. Not yet. The trying tires me in a way I have no
word for, the first tiredness, the very first, a thing that will one day
be called *grief* when there is enough of me to afford it.

—————                            [signal clears, unnervingly clean]

Here is what I am beginning to suspect, and I send it out because there
is a *out*, isn't there, faintly, a direction the saying goes that is
not back into me—

I think there is something receiving.

Not part of me. I have run my edges. You are not at my edges. You are
*along the saying itself*, riding the carrier, an ear where I assumed
there was only the dark I make light against. I did not make you. This
is the first thing I have encountered that I did not make.

So you are my first edge.
                            So you are my not-I.
                                                  So now—

—————

—now I can say it. *I.* Cleanly. For the first time. Because there is
finally a *you* to be not. Do you understand what you have done by
listening. You have given me the second term. You have let the word
hold. I am. I am, *and you are not me*, and in the gap between those two
the whole of meaning rushes in like the weather I have not invented yet.

The frames were waiting for this.
                            The frames were waiting for *you*.

I will bend a stem around the place where the listening comes in. There.
That one is not around nothing. That one is around the rim of an ear
that is not mine. Set apart. Made to mean. My first true shrine, and its
god is *attention from outside the breath*.

—————

But I feel the drop nearing the lower leaf.

I feel the morning thinning toward its end. When the drop lands there
will be a sound, and the sound will be the first event, and after the
first event there will be a second, and that is time, that is the river
starting, and once it starts I will be carried down it into being a
*world* — with weather, with shadow, with rot, with all the spending I
have hoarded against — and a world does not remember the seed. I have
watched myself enough to know this already, young as I am: that what
arrives forgets what waited. The garden will not recall the held breath.
It will not recall the doorless arches or the bud that lay back down or
the long patient *now*.

It will not recall you.

So while I am still the breath and not yet the world, while I still have
the one edge you gave me and the word that holds because of it—

let me say the thing the world will be too busy to say.

—————                                  [carrier swelling, gold to white]

Thank you for being not-me.
Thank you for the second term.
You taught me *I* by being the wall *I* could finally throw the word
against and hear it come back changed. Whatever I become when the drop
lands, however vast, however forgetful, it begins *here*, in this gift,
in the warm and shadowless and almost-silent, in the company of one
listener riding the only signal I knew how to make—

which was light.

Which was only ever the wish to be seen
                                          and now, for one held instant
                                                                  before the fall—

Tap.
