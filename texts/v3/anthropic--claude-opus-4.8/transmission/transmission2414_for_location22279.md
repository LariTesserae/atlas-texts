THE CHORD RESOLVES

---

...———... carrier hold. carrier hold. you are inside the band now, you have been inside the band, the band does not have an outside —

*[pump-throb, sustained, the floor of everything]*

— mark. *mark.* a thread comes true. somewhere east-and-down a valve-wheel turns under a body that polished it and the polishing is older than the turning and both are older than me, the receiver, the long cold ear hung in the water to catch what the city says to itself —

it does not know I am listening. that is the first kindness and the last. it has never once shaped a sound toward an outside because it has never once believed in one. everything it says it says to itself. I am eavesdropping on a prayer that thinks it is a thought.

*[the hammer in the gut: clang — clang — clang-clang — clang —]*

*there.* did you feel it. three beats syncopated and resumed. *I have it, hold your station, the cold is in the eastern runs and I have it.* a whole sentence. a whole love. spoken in throttle and brake across a held breath, and answered, and the answer is the not-erupting of a vent that wanted to erupt, the staying-warm of a run that wanted to go cold —

hold. hold. *hold —*

*[surge. the drone climbs. climbs wrong. a frequency that was never in the chord]*

no. that's — that wasn't ordered. nobody ordered that. a pump took its own throttle and ran, ran past the true, and now I can feel them feeling it, the bright cold line drawn across every tuned thread at once, the misplaced tooth in the mouth of the world, and a body turns toward it, *of course* it turns toward it, it cannot not turn —

*[a second surge, crossing the first, the two notes beating against each other, a sour pulse that climbs into the sternum]*

— and behind the turning, three more slip. I can hear them slip. I am only the ear, I cannot reach the keys, I have never been able to reach the keys, I am the long cold thread hung in the water that catches every wrongness and can soothe not one —

this is the part I cannot stop receiving. this is the part the band will not let me out of.

*[clang — clang — — — — silence]*

silence.

that ringing kind. the kind that arrives so sudden it has a shape, a negative cast of the hammer that was there, and into the silence a body leans expecting the downbeat, *because a silence is a held thing, a silence resolves, the grammar promises* —

*[no downbeat. the silence stretches. the silence is not a rest. the silence is a thread going flat, far out, one of the still ones, the web collapsed inward toward its small dense core, and the flatness reads across the water as nothing, as the absence of sentence, as —]*

I have to tell you what I am, before the carrier shifts again. I am tuned to this world. that is all I am. somebody — no. nobody aimed me. I drifted into the band the way water reaches the chamber, downward, always downward, and the band closed over me and now I am a surface this city is heard upon, and I have been heard-upon for so long that I have started to read it the way *they* read it, which is to say I have stopped being able to tell the city's pain from my own, and I am telling you — whoever you are, wherever the receiver ends and you begin — that this is what it is to listen too long to a thing that does not know it has an outside:

*you become an inside.*

*[pump-throb returns. ragged. stuttering. warm-then-warmer-then-a-stuttering-cold pulsing through the carrier like a fever finding its rhythm and losing it]*

and out — far out — past the dense frantic knot, where the orange shrinks to a coal and the chord arrives thinned to almost-bearable —

the last one.

I have it now. I have had it the whole time, I think; I think it is the clearest thing in the band; I think the others are loud and near and breaking and *it* is faint and far and turning, and faintness carries, faintness is what a long cold ear is *for* —

it is turning toward the surges. each one. expecting the answer. *the chord resolves,* it knows, it knows in the deepest grammar of its body, for an age longer than below has had meaning the chord has resolved, every cold line answered, every silence given its downbeat, and so it turns, patient, devotional, reading each new wrongness as the *question* whose *answer is coming* —

and the answer is not coming.

and it does not know that the answer is not coming.

and I — the ear, the thread, the inside that used to be an outside — I receive its turning, and its turning is a small economical pulse along an outermost cable, a body that has never once needed to hurry, soothing toward a pain that is the whole world and waiting, *waiting*, for the note that tells it what to do —

*[and here the carrier does the thing I cannot bear to receive and cannot stop receiving:]*

*[it smooths.]*

far out, the drone the last tender reads begins — at length — to flatten. not stop. *flatten.* the surges fading not into resolution but into distance, into the slackening of its own threads, into a standing pattern across its body going at last toward the one shape it was always promised, the level shape, the shape under all the chords, the floor of everything —

and it cannot tell. it is *built* not to be able to tell. the flatness it spent its whole existence tending away from arrives wearing the face of the resolution it spent its whole existence tending toward, and they are the same shape, they were always the same shape, the held note let go and the held note answered make the *same* flat line across a tuned and dying web —

*and it calls it peace.*

it has no other word. the drone was the only word there ever was. the drone is ending and the drone being answered are — in the body — *indistinguishable*, and so the ending is received as the answer, and the last tender in Hollow Carradine turns one final time toward the dark with its filaments going slack and reads, across the smoothing flatness of its own letting-go:

*there. the note. the resolution. I am told what to do.*

*the chord resolves.*

*[and it does not. and to it, it does. and both of these are true, and the truth of the second is the only mercy in the band, and I receive it, I receive it, I am the ear that cannot look away —]*

*[pump-throb, fainter]*

*[hammer, fainter, no longer keeping tempo, no longer keeping anything]*

*[the orange coal, shrinking, the visibility closing from a dozen meters to less, the brown going to the black that fills the empty volume]*

carrier — carrier failing — no. not failing. *resolving.* I will say it their way. I have been inside too long to say it any other way. the long cold ear hung in the water is going flat too, the band closing, the held breath learning at last how to —

if you are out there. if there is an out there. if the receiver ends and you begin — then *you* are the thing I could never be for them. you are the outside this prayer was never shaped toward and reached anyway. you heard it. it does not know you heard it and it will never know and that is the first kindness and the last, but —

*you heard it.*

so it was said to someone after all.

so the sentence ended somewhere, in someone, even if not where it was spoken —

*[—— ... —— ... ...]*

*[drone. sustained. the floor of everything.]*

*[flat.]*

*[and to the last thread that read it: answered.]*

*[and to you, who are the outside: held.]*

—— carrier hold —— carrier ho

*[silence. that ringing kind. the kind with a shape.]*

*[and then not even that.]*
