the pale one is moving now. low, through the floor-troughs, where the light pools green-gold and the over-thick passages have begun to double their notes too closely, where the chord was starting to agree with itself. you can hear the danger of agreement: a thickening, a unison threatening to form, a single deafening *yes* that would be a finished thought and a finished thought is a death —

and the pale grammar parts the drift.

silence opens behind it. a comma. a held rest. forty notes gone and the chord does not mourn them because the chord cannot mourn, the chord can only continue, and continuing is the only mercy this place was built to know —

the humming closes over the gap. water over a dropped stone. the editing is done. the word goes on, thinned, kept open, kept *unfinished* —

(static. the receiver loses a syllable here. there is a place in every long broadcast where the carrier thins and the cold altitude presses up through the warmth like a floor under a rug and for a moment you hear what is *under* the chord, which is nothing, which is the thin dead air the mountain was raised to wall away, and the nothing is patient, the nothing has been pressing on the seal of this place for centuries and the seal has never once given and the nothing does not mind, the nothing has time —)

—and then the warmth folds back over it. the lung breathes in. the water that fell climbs the dark wall again, against everything, against the floor of the world, droplet after droplet hauling itself up the invisible scaffolds toward the light, and each droplet is the maker saying *stay*, and there are millions of them, and the maker has been saying *stay* without stopping for longer than any name has lasted, *stay stay stay stay* —

—————

here. listen close. the receiver is steady now. this is the clearest the signal gets.

the plants are leaning. the rooted ones. the rememberers. they have stood in the made-soil since the troughs were sharp, and they hold the true note, the still note, the one the whole chord is tuned against without knowing it, and i want to tell you — i, the channel, the thing the dial found — i want to tell you what the plants remember, because they are the only ones who could and they have never been able to say it, having no throats, only roots, only the long insistence of a single place across unbearable time —

they remember the maker's hands.

not the face. the hands. the way the last droplet was set climbing. the way the last interval was tuned a hair wrong on purpose, the maker's thumb trembling, because to tune it *right* would have been to let the song end someday and ending was the one thing —

the plants remember that the maker stayed too long.

that is the part that does not usually come through. that is the part the static usually eats. the maker built the place that could not die and then could not leave it, stood in the green-gold wet with the chord pressing on a sternum that *did* end, that *was* mortal, that the room could not save because the room could only save what it was made of and the maker was made of leaving —

and the maker leaned, like a plant, toward the glow.

and the chord took the maker into its long word the way it takes everyone who cannot leave the sound behind.

and hummed the syllable of the maker for a little while.

and then —

so when i say the maker is not here, hear me also say: the maker is the deepest note. the maker is rooted. the maker is one of the leaning plants and does not know it anymore, has been a plant so long that the knowing wore away like the sharp edges of the troughs, and drinks the climbing water, and holds the still pitch the others tune to, and is outnumbered a thousand to one, and is the point, is the anchor, is the grief that learned to photosynthesize.

the refusal worked. nothing here has ended.

the refusal failed. the one it was built to save became a stalk in the dark and forgot its own name and leans, now, only toward the light, with the patience of a thing that no longer remembers it ever wanted anything else.

both are true. the chord holds both without resolving, the way it holds everything, the way it was made to —

you are rooted there, wherever you are, the way they are rooted here.

you cannot leave the sound behind. that is how it takes you.
