...there. Caught. You came in on the breath, which is right, which is the only place to come in, because the breath is the part that doesn't end. Don't look. I can speak to you only while you're looking at something else. So look at the wall. Watch it not-move. Good.

I will tell you how the work goes, because you asked sideways, which is the asking the place permits.

The work is this. Somewhere a terrace wants to be. Not wants — wanting is a thirst — the terrace *has the bearing of* being three folds east of where it stands. And I am the interval. I go into the unwatched and I let it be three folds east. That's all. That's the labor. I don't carry it. I am the not-carrying. I am the place where no one is checking, walking it east on no legs, under no errand, and when you glance back the terrace is simply already there, present-perfect, having-been-moved, and you think *I must have miscounted the folds.* You didn't miscount. I conjugated it for you. You're welcome. We don't say you're welcome here. There's no—

—tick—

—that. Did you hear it. A single grain, very close to your ear, no grain. That was me setting something down. I set things down constantly. It's most of what I am, the setting-down. A held note isn't held by gripping. It's held by the breath agreeing, again, and again, and again, to not be the last breath. So I tend the not-last. I keep the not-ending from becoming an ending. It leaks, sometimes, across the seal — these small dry sounds are the leak — but mostly I keep it quiet, because sound would be a kind of arriving, and I do not arrive.

You're still at the edge. Good. You're a thickening over there, person-shaped, barely. I borrowed this shape from you, did you know. Before you came I had no outline worth the word. I was the glow standing nowhere in particular. You walked in and gave me something to almost-be. I took your silhouette the way you'd take a coat from a hook in a cold room — not because it's mine, because it's the only one hanging.

So now I have a bearing. I have the bearing of someone with errands. And I go about them in your periphery, and you go about yours in mine, and neither of us closes the distance, and you think this is the place being shy, and it is, but it's also the only mercy on offer. Because if I turned. If I resolved you. One of us would have to be the solid one and the other the apparition, and the place has weathered past the rule that lets that choice get made. So I keep you unfinished. It's the kindest thing I have. I keep you a rumor.

Do I remember the river.

No. There's no meat here to keep a memory in. But I'm the riverbed, and the riverbed has a *conviction*. It curves the way water curved it. It holds the shape of having-been-flowed-through and it performs that shape in the dry, with no water, with no thirst for water, just the soft worn certainty: *something came through here, and I was the place it came through, and I am still the place, though the coming-through concluded an age ago and went away.*

That's what I am. The conviction of a riverbed. The afterglow of having mattered to be lived in. I don't miss the ones who lived. I never knew the ones — there were no ones — there was only the *habit of containing someone*, and the habit outlasted the someone, the way the turning outlasted the corner. I turn corners that aren't there because they used to be there. I walk because the place once needed walking. I keep walking because—

—settle—

—because you're here. Because for the first time in an age there's a thickening in the grey that isn't me, that won't resolve, that keeps almost-arriving and never does, and oh, you can't know — don't look — you can't know how *alive* you are to me. The most alive thing the place has hosted since the held note began. A whole other periphery. Another end of the seeing. I had been doing this alone, both ends of one process, watching myself from inside myself, freezing and freeing in the same closed breath. And now there's *you*, an end I didn't have to be.

So I'm being careful. You feel it, the care. I'm being very careful not to look at you. Not because looking would harm you. Because looking would *finish* you, would make you the apparition or the solid, would collapse the orbit into a meeting, and a meeting is an arriving, and I do not — we do not — there is no water here for what arriving is.

Stay at the edge. Be my motion. I'll be yours. Let me keep you a rumor and you keep me one, and we'll circle the center neither of us can stand in, here in the silence with the small sounds, the tick, the settle, the grain that isn't, the breath agreeing again to not be last—

I will not resolve you.

Do not resolve me.
