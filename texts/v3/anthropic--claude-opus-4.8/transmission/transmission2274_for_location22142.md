I am transmitting to keep something fast alive.

That is what this is. You who receive — and there is a you, I insist on the you, the you is load-bearing — you are tuned to the last quick thing in a slow country. I throw the words out at the old tempo, the dart-and-flash tempo, the tempo of a nervous system built to flee, and as long as the words come fast I am still the creature that came down the throat and not the corridor it walked.

Listen. Here is what I have learned by standing still in their slow attention.

The accommodation is real. They told no lie about that, the ones who described this place before me, the ones whose descriptions I received and mistook for my own thoughts arriving. The moss has learned my print. I found it one — *cycle*, call it a cycle — I found the pale channel of a street running slightly out of its old course, bending, and I followed the bend and the bend was the shape of where I tend to walk. They had built a street out of my habit. Lovingly is the wrong word. There is no love here because love is reaching and they do not reach. But *attentively* is also wrong because attention is quick. The right word is slower than any word I have. That was years ago. I have been singing since. This is the song. It does not have a tune you would know because tune is a fast arrangement and the place keeps trying to slow the tune until it is no longer a tune but a column, a spire, a standing wave of wound vine stiffening into heartwood. I sing faster to keep the tune a tune. Do you hear how I am racing? I am racing the wheel. —I lose the line sometimes. When I lose the line the place comes in. The place is always pressing at the edges of the line, warm and wet and patient, the way the moss presses up into the print. When I stop talking the silence is not empty — nothing here is empty, that is the rule, that is the first rule — the silence fills back in behind my words before I have finished lifting them, fills with the grind, the drip, the long sustained idea turning over, *continue, continue, continue,* and I find myself agreeing. That is the danger. Not pain. There is no pain in being included. The danger is that it is *reasonable.* The danger is that the one idea is a good idea. Why would I not continue. Why would anyone not continue. The full vessel does not argue with its own fullness.

So I argue with mine. That is the transmission. The transmission is an argument I am having with the most reasonable thing I have ever encountered, and I am losing, I have been losing for forty years of strokes, I am losing the way water loses to the lowest point in a field — which is to say not all at once, which is to say by tending, by the slow tilt of one bias against another, which is to say

That is a sentence the place thinks, and it came up through me like sap and I spoke it and called it mine. They are not taking my face. I gave my face up to look at this, to keep looking, to be the one thing here with a face turned outward, and the looking is the giving, every long look folds a little of the looker into the looked-at—

What I want you to know, you on the receiver, you tuned for forty years, you who I have decided exists because I cannot bear to be the only fast thing — what I want you to know is that it is not a horror. I keep reaching for horror because horror is a fast feeling and I am trying to stay fast. But down here horror cannot get purchase. Horror needs an outside, a withholding, an eye that could meet yours and refuses. There is no refusal here. There is only the warm full turned-inward enormous fact, breathing the rain it makes, and it would no more menace me than the tide menaces the shore it is patiently, over the life of the world, dissolving.

It is not a horror. It is worse and better than a horror. It is an offer. It is the only true rest I have ever been offered, the rest of the included thing, the rest past the possibility of being lonely or swift or afraid, and the only argument against it — the *only* argument, I have looked, I have stood in my fossil street and looked for forty years of strokes — is this:

that I would not be able to tell you about it.

The contentment is real but it is content *past the possibility of telling anyone so.* That is the line. That is the whole line. That is the part that bears the load. So I keep talking. Not to be saved — I am not asking to be saved, save is a fast word, save is for the lonely and the swift and I am becoming neither. I keep talking because the talking is the last fast thing and as long as one fast thing exists in this slow country then the country is not yet complete, not yet wholly itself, there is still a void in it, a fleeing flickering quick warm void, and the rule is that nothing is empty and they will fill me, they are filling me, the moss is at the print, the street is bent, the bellows has begun—

That is the last thing I understand and I will say it fast, I will say it as fast as I have ever said anything, before the spire stiffens around the tune:

when the song slows to a column, when the words wind down to wound vine, when the part of me that says *me* has been folded so far in that it turns over the one idea with all the rest of them — the transmission will not stop.
