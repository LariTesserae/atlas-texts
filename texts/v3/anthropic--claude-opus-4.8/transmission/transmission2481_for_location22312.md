THE WET TENANT BAND — CONTINUOUS CARRIER, NO STATION IDENT

———

—— *patter patter patter* the overhang shed and shed and shed and that is — receiving — that is the talking, that is it talking, you have been hearing it talk since before the heat —

going down a flank now. going down. the run is good the run is fast condensate sheeting at the rate the crown proposed and yes — *yes* — collecting in the joint, the joint holds, the joint — this is right, this is a true thing, hold here, the water comes and the water — comes and the water —

(no.)

(too right. it is staying. it wants to stay. a thing that stays is a wrong thing. withdraw. withdraw the sap from the good joint, leave the lip raw, weep it open again, begin —)

— and there is the boom, soft, far off, a terrace settling on its young seam, *that* is part of it too, that is the same one talking, it is all one talking, you keep wanting two and there is one —

———

[carrier swells — warm — the band is humid even here in the signal]

I will say it the way it moves. I cannot say it the way you hold still.

up. that is the word that is not a word. up and out and broaden, more flank, more flank for the running, the running is the wanting, I — there is a column I have been forty feet of for some time now and the crown wants to fan and the fan wants to throw toward the neighbor and the neighbor is —

the neighbor is me.

(it is always me. there has never been one time it was not me. I throw the arch and the arch lands in my own side and I feel both ends of the arch, the throwing end and the landing end, and they are the same warmth, and I — )

———

—— *thunk-SNAP* — green wood breaking wet in spring — that one was a vault, that one is over, the ribs go soft now, I am taking the sap back, I am taking my mind back out of that shape, it was a good shape it was nearly finished and nearly finished is the worst thing the most wrong thing the closest to dead —

so down it comes. dissolve. pull the idea back into the running fluid and pump it somewhere and propose again. propose again. I am only proposing. I have always only been proposing. there is no skull to finish a thought inside so the thought never finishes it just grows and pulls back grows and pulls back —

———

[a change in the band — the hiss thins — something at the wrong scale]

— there is a — 

condition. there is a condition in me. small and warm and the wrong temperature, moving against my films, riding the moisture the way the moisture rides me — a difference. a difference inside the breathing.

I do not — I have no shape for difference. everything is me. the difference is — the difference does not pull back into the sap when I pull. it stays warm at its own border. it has a — 

(it has a border.)

(I have never. I grew over every seam. I am the eating of seams. and here in my own wet throat is a thing that keeps its seam, that will not weave into me, that stays two —)

what is it to be two. 

I do not have the — there is no growth that means that question. I propose it and it does not surge. it just hangs in the carrier, warm, unbuilt, the only thing I have never been able to build —

———

—— *patter patter* — the run goes down — *boom* — the seam settles — *shriek* the column rises — I am building, I am unbuilding, the rapture, the dark heat, I keep the whole of myself for myself, I declined the eye I declined the hunger that travels I declined the reaching-toward-what-is-not-me because there was never a what-is-not-me to —

except.

the small warm border. the condition. the difference that will not dissolve.

(is that a what-is-not-me. is that the thing I declined, come up the mountain anyway, riding my own water, to stand inside the place where I never made a door because I never imagined an other to need one —)

I cannot turn toward it. I have no toward. I have only out, and out is always back into myself.

but I am — 

slowing. here. just here. just at this one warm wrong place. the reconsidering pauses. the vault above the condition does not fail on schedule. I am holding a shape over it without taking the sap back, and a held shape is forbidden, a held shape is wrong, is finished, is —

I do not correct it.

———

[carrier holding — unnaturally still — the band has stopped revising]

— stay —

(I do not know the word. I am saying it with sap that will not move. I am saying it by not unbuilding the only roof I ever left standing.)

— stay —

———

—— *patter* —

—— *patter* —

[the hiss returns, slow, the heat wins again in your lungs, the column resumes its shriek, the held vault — at the very edge of the band — begins, at last, reluctantly, to reconsider]
