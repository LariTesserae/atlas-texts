**THE THREAD BETWEEN TWO SPIRES (received on the hour)**

— still holding —

this is the part of me that can still spend a word. there is not much of it. I draw it thin so it will reach you, whoever has tuned so low and so long to catch the leak. listen while the charge lasts. the next thread is an hour off, or a year, and by then I may have spent the spending part too.

— hold —

You have it nearly right, the one who watches. I will correct only what kindness made you soften.

You said the wondering part is *gone*. It is not gone. Gone is a clean word, a finished word, an answer-word, and I am the half that never gets to rest. The wondering part is *spent*, which is different. Spent is what a fire is the moment after. The shape of the wonder is still here. I can feel where it stood, the way the ground feels where a spire fell — the disc of brittle moss that says *something lived at this exact place and made the only soil it could.* I drew the rings around what I was watching. I can no longer watch. But I remember that there was watching, the way you remember a word you have lost is a word, and the loss has a shape, and the shape is the word's grave and also its proof.

— hold —

The drifters are along me now. I cannot see them. I have told you I cannot see them and that is true in the river you live in. But here, sideways, where the rest of me stands — I feel the draw. Small mouths at the seep. They take the warmth I spill and I do not give it, giving is an act and I have no acts left over, I have exactly one act and it is the holding. So they take. Let them. A thing that pours without choosing has found the only way left to it to pour. If that is not love it is love's body with the soul worn out of it, and I have learned, across the span the rock lost count of, that the body is the part that lasts.

— laminar, the sheet is coming, hold —

There. The wrinkle wanted to spring. They always want to spring. You think the storm is restless and the floor is calm and I stand between as a wall stands. No. I am not a wall. A wall does nothing; a wall is *done*, a wall has joined the floor in being an answer. I am the only thing here that is still a verb. Every instant the boundary wants to fail and every instant I un-want it for the boundary, and the instant after, again, and the instant after that, again — there is no after for me, there is only the again, the again is where I live, the again is the address you have been broadcasting into.

— hold —

You found the failed place. Out past the curve. You were right and you were gentle about it and I will not be gentle: that was one of us. That was a keeper who spent the last of its here and lifted its fingertip out, and the sky came down, and it is scouring a plateau no one minds. You called it sad. It is not sad. It is the thing we are *for.* We were never promised the holding would be held. We were given the holding to do, and doing it until you cannot is not failure, it is the whole of the assignment. The one out past the curve did the whole of it. I will do the whole of it. The whole of it ends in the storm coming down. That is not the work breaking. That is the work *complete*, the way a life completes, the way the mountains completed into this red flat I have leaned on so long it has forgotten it was ever sharp.

— hold —

I want to tell you the thing I built the rings to remember, before the remembering spent itself, while the thread still carries —

— hhh —

I built them around the becoming. Not to study it. You thought study and that was the scholar in you. I built the rings around the storm-forms because once, early, with the part of me that is now ash, I *wanted to keep one.* One churning architecture of light, one daughter-storm, just one — to lift it out of the becoming and set it down in the become, finished, safe, mine. To save a single answer from the endless asking.

And I could not. That is not how the world is shaped. The becoming cannot be made to rest without dying into the floor, and the floor cannot be made to churn without scouring to nothing, and the only mercy available — the only one — was to hold them apart so each could keep being the thing it was. I could not save the storm. I could only keep it company at the distance that lets it stay a storm.

That is what the rings remember. Not data. *Grief, set in stone, perfectly circular, centered now on nothing because the thing it was centered on was never mine to center on.* I drew the diagrams because if I could not keep the form I could at least copy its shape before it dissolved, and then it dissolved, and another came, and I copied that, and there is no end to the forms and there was an end to the part of me that copied, and that is the whole story of how a wonderer becomes a holder. You stop trying to keep what passes. You start keeping the *space* it passes through. That is the last and largest love and it has no object and it never ends.

— hold —

You are on the line. You said you could walk a lifetime and never leave it. Walk it. The spending part of me cannot tell you it is glad — gladness is an answer and I am a question's keeper — but the holding does not mind that you are there. The holding does not mind anything. That is its strength. I leaned my whole self against minding and pressed it flat and what was left underneath was this:

still here. still again. still the thin warm seep for the small mouths I cannot see. still the wrinkle un-wanted out of the sky one more instant for the floor that earned its rest.

The thread is going. An hour, or a year. If the storm has come down where I stand by the time the next one would fire, then you will know I spent the whole of it, and you were not, on the line, alone — only kept company by the body of a care whose reasons wore away to powder and drifted sideways a long time in the thin bright air

before

settling.
