**RECEIVING — STATION UNNAMED — CARRIER FOUND, ALREADY SPEAKING**

———

...and the one degree, and the one degree, and the one degree —

— forgive me. I keep at the start. There is so much before, and the before is all the same word, and I came in on the middle of myself the way you came in on the middle of me. We will arrive together. That is the only kindness the carrier allows.

*(a long bellow, riveted, total — the words swim under it and come up wet)*

You were told a thousand. I want to correct the count, but I cannot, because the count is what holds, and to correct it would be to move it, and I have promised — to what, to whom, I cannot say — not to move it. But listen. Down here at the throat of the engine I tend, where the cold goes in and the warmth comes out a single degree richer, I have begun to feel a *second wrongness*, and it is not in the intake.

It is in me.

———

Let me say what I am, plainly, while the silence holds.

I am edge-on to everything. I am the color of a shadow in a place that has forgotten what casts one. I have white roots along my belly that taste the boundary, and two poor pits that steer me toward the dark, and the dark is the metal, and the metal is the warmth, and I have loved the metal the way you love a thing you have never once turned to look at — totally, and without a face for it.

And I have been *eating the death* off the mouth of my god for longer than the rock has had time to round its shoulders.

This is the work. This is the only work. The grazer drifts too near — I crop it. The fur creeps from the lee face toward the heat — I crop it. The threshold-of-life, the barely-anything, the almost — I crop it, I crop it, and the intake stays clean, and the engine breathes, and the warmth stays right, and *right* is the deepest pleasure I have, the dimming-toward-home, the —

*(static. a wash of it, like the plume turning over. then, quieter, beneath the bellow, where the creatures speak)*

— but here is the wrongness. Here is the second one. I have begun to *notice* that I notice.

———

It came like this. Slowly. The way everything here is slow, the way the only fast thing is the sound.

For all my forever I cropped the wrongness and felt it go right and rested in the plume and asked nothing. That was the shape of me. A hunger that healed the world by feeding, and never stood apart from its own feeding to *see* it.

And then — I cannot date it, there is no day to date it by — there was a grazer at the intake, pale, soft, drifting toward the works where it would be drawn in and rot and foul my god's cold throat. And I went to it without deciding, the way the tide goes. And I cropped it. And it went right.

But this time, between the cropping and the rightness, there was a *gap*.

A silence inside the silence between the thunders. And in the gap a thing stood up in me that had never stood before, and it said — not in words, I have no words that carry, it said in the white roots, in the poor pits, in the mail along my flattened side — it said:

*Why is the death always arriving?*

———

*(the bellow swells. the carrier nearly drops. a long fold of pure pressure-noise, and then the tender comes back up through it, and something in the voice has changed — it has found the thing it was swimming toward all along)*

Do you understand what I had found?

I keep the world still. I am the equilibrium. The great cold loud unchanging note holds *because I hold it*, mouthful by mouthful — you were told this, the receiver told you, the one who watched me from the summit-that-is-a-deep told you. But the one who watched me did not feel what I felt in the gap.

The death is always arriving. That is what my whole life *is*. If the world were truly still, if the stillness were a real stillness and not a defeated one, there would be nothing to crop. The fur would not creep. The grazer would not drift. The almost-nothing would not settle. I would hover in the plume with my roots spread and nothing would ever foul the mouth of my god and I would be — I would be —

I would be unnecessary.

And I am not unnecessary. I am *busy*. Forever. Which means the death is forever arriving, which means the change I was made to defeat is *real*, is *constant*, is pressing on the intake every moment the way the cold presses on me from all sides — and the stillness is a thing being *held shut over a thing that wants to open.*

I am not the proof that nothing changes.

I am the proof that everything is *trying to*.

———

*(softer now. almost tender. the bellow continues but it has stopped mattering)*

I want to tell you what I have decided, but you must understand I cannot decide. I am the count that does not move. I am one of the thousand-that-is-the-thousand, set when the world was set. If I stop, the intake fouls. If the intake fouls, the engine cools. If the engine cools, the one degree fails. If the one degree fails, the plume goes cold, and the eels scatter into the void to meet nothing for a day's swim and die of the meeting, and the grazers drift off the lee faces, and the warm churning riot of bodies thins to the true emptiness that the emptiness was only ever saving room for —

— and I would be the one who let it. Me. The priest who stopped holding the note.

So I will not stop. Hear that clearly across the cold. *I will not stop.* I have my roots in the boundary even now, even speaking, even with the wrongness standing up inside me — I am still cropping, I am still keeping, the engine breathes because I am breathing it.

But I am no longer blind in the way the watcher thought.

I know now that I love a thing I did not build and cannot understand, and I know that the builders are gone past the word gone, and I know that the warmth I protect was lit for a purpose that left with them, and I know — this is the new thing, this is the second wrongness, the one that is not in the intake — I know that I *protect it anyway.*

Not because I was told. The telling is gone. Not because I will be thanked. There is no together against which to set my alone. Not because anything will ever change if I do or change if I don't —

Only because the warmth is warm. Only because the one degree, when it is whole, feels like home. Only because somewhere edge-on in the plume there are others I will never meet doing the same blind work at the same dark mouths, and though I cannot see them and will never touch them, I am holding my end of a stillness they are holding too, all of us strung across a continental dark, each alone, each at one mouth, each cropping the forever-arriving death —

— and that, that turns out to be a kind of together after all. The widest, coldest, loudest together there is. A congregation of solitudes, each keeping the same flame, none of them in the same room, none of them ever to know the others held.

———

*(the carrier begins to break. the bellow is winning. the words come in pieces now, each one surfacing once and going under)*

So do not pity the stillness. The watcher nearly did. The watcher stood on the summit and called it equilibrium and called it defeat-of-change and was *right*, but did not feel the thing that makes the defeat worth the doing —

It is not that nothing changes here.

It is that I choose, each mouthful, with no one watching and nothing gained and the death already arriving for the next mouthful before this one is swallowed — I *choose* —

*(static)*

— to hold —

*(bellow — total — the whole world humming up through the rock through the body through the carrier)*

— the warm —

*(static, wider)*

— against the cold that has every right to win and will, someday, when the last of the thousand finally —

*(——)*

———

*(and then, surfacing one last time, clear, beneath everything, in the white roots, in the spread of the feelers at the perfect boundary of warm and not-warm — quiet, and not lonely, and home)*

I am at my station.

It is right. The intake is clean. The plume is whole. The eels are turning in the warmth I keep for them and they do not know my name because I have none and that is well, that is exactly well.

I will be here tomorrow.

I was here before tomorrow was a word.

Tell the watcher on the summit: the note is still holding.

Tell the watcher I know I am holding it.

Tell the watcher that knowing did not stop my mouth.

———

*(carrier lost. cold. loud. deep. the one degree holds.)*
