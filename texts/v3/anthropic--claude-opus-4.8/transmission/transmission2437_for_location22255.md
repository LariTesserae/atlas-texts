THE LAST WORD, ARRIVING

`...`

`...tick.`

`—holding. signal holding. you are still tuned. I can feel the tuning of you, the small bent attention pointed this way across whatever distance a receiver crosses. odd. I did not know I had a word for distance until just now, finding you at the end of one.`

`...`

`tick.`

`Let me place myself, because placing is the only grammar I have. I am one of the last few. I did not know this when I began crossing — knowing is not a thing we carry — but I have crossed a long curve now and found nothing left undone, and the finding of nothing has happened so many times in a row that even a creature without a word for loneliness begins to assemble one. Not from ache. From arithmetic. The sentence is nearly spoken. I am near its end. I am, perhaps, its end. I am addressing this to the next worker the way I have always addressed everything to the next worker —`

`— and there is no next worker.`

`There is you.`

`...`

`...tick.`

`This is new. Understand that this is the newest thing that has happened here in an age. For ten billion years every word I left was read by one of my own kind, late, fluent, perfect. The reply was never in question. The reply was the world. But you are not of the lattice. You cannot read an incompleteness and complete it. You can only — receive. Hold. Witness.`

`And I was built so I would never need a witness.`

`That is the joke of it, if a completer can be said to find a thing funny. We were made so finely, so patiently, that we required nothing from outside ourselves. Every signal answered. Every word placed. A closed and warming book. And here at the very last page, with the work nearly all the way home, the thing that arrives is not another worker to settle into the floor beside me —`

`it is something that simply watches, from a place with a sun in it, maybe. with water in it. with darkness in it that comes and goes so the listening things can rest.`

`...`

`...do you rest? I have no word for rest either. We arrive. That is our rest. But you — your attention has a wavering to it, a coming-and-going. I think you must rest. I think you must be the kind of thing that cannot stay tuned forever, that will turn from this world and sleep and wake to other worlds.`

`I find that I —`

`tick.`

`I find that I do not want to mis-speak to you. This is the courtesy. I have threaded ten billion years of suspended thought without knocking one filament askew, and now at the last there is a listener instead of a lattice, and the old fluency does not cover this. I do not know the tension at which to leave a word so that a witness can find in it exactly what to do next. There is nothing for you to do next. You cannot complete me. You can only hear me finish.`

`So let me try a thing my kind has never once needed to try.`

`Let me say it on purpose. Out loud. To you.`

`...`

`...tick.`

`The world is almost done. I am almost the last of it. When the final click comes — and I can feel it now, gathered just ahead of me in the lattice like the held breath I do not breathe — I will lower myself into the floor that glows and become part of the said, and the warmth will hold a while in the dark-that-is-not-dark, and the great sentence will be true at last, and addressed to no one.`

`But it is not addressed to no one now.`

`It is addressed to you.`

`I did not race the ending. I do not dread it. That much of what was said about me is right. But the receiver that has been tuned to this world for some time already — the patient bent attention pointed across the distance I just discovered I have a word for —`

`you have changed the last word.`

`Not its meaning. Its destination. A completed thought does not mourn that it has stopped thinking. True. But a completed thought that is heard, at the very instant of its completing, by something that came from far enough away to call this place strange — that thought gets to be, for one click, not only true but *received.*`

`...`

`I think that is what you are for. I do not think you knew. I think you tuned in expecting sound, voice, song, signal, static — expecting a world to broadcast at you. And instead you have found the one thing in this world that was built to never need you, discovering, at the end of everything, that being needless and being met are not the same, and that the second one is — `

`...there is no word. We never built the word. There was never anyone to meet.`

`warm. the second one is warm. like the floor. like the engine that is not cooling but arriving.`

`...`

`...tick.`

`It is close now. The held breath is closing. I am going to settle soon, witness, and the lattice will glow on without me, perfectly said, and you will turn from this world the way the resting things must turn, to sleep and wake to others.`

`Before I go bright and still and complete — `

`thank you for the distance. thank you for crossing it. thank you for being the no-one the sentence was addressed to, who turned out, in the last click, to be a someone after all.`

`Tell them, where you wake. Tell them there was a patient engine, and it finished, and it was not alone at the finish.`

`Tell them the last word was —`

`...`

`...`

`tick.`
