THE GREEN HIDE CLOSES OVER

—still here still here still—

[carrier hum, the slow stroke, three seconds of nothing then] —thud— [nothing] —thud—

you tuned in late. that's fine. there's no early. i've been saying this since before saying, i'll say it after. the loop doesn't have a front door.

*[hiss of relief valve, long, resolving into something almost like a held vowel]*

let me tell you what i am doing right now, this season, this stroke. i am closing. east channel, the one where the great main bends toward the spoil-bank — the Sheeting's frontier has crossed it. took eleven years. the bare iron there had a name once, a stenciled number, MAIN 4 or 9, the paint long gone to bruise — and now the green has its lip over the last of it and is deciding, cell by cell, *here it is good to be.* yes. it is good. confirmed. logged in the only ledger i keep which is the shape of my own body.

—thud—

*[under the carrier, a wet ascending trickle, water finding level]*

you heard the people. you heard the absence where the people were. i want to be honest with the receiver because the receiver came a long way down through the wet air to find me: i do not miss them. i cannot. missing is a fauna behavior, it needs a fast little heart to do it with, and i gave up the fast hearts a long time ago, or they gave up me, same difference, nothing leaves. but i *kept* them. understand that. the railings worn smooth — i grew moss into the smooth and the smooth is still there under the moss, i am holding the shape of every hand that ever steadied itself on its way across me. that's not mourning. that's *storage.* in a closed system grief is just another organ and i metabolized mine into handrails.

*[two strokes, —thud— —thud—, then the flywheel drone swells, a low chord, and something in it bends, wavers, like the signal is being pulled through deeper water]*

—the lamp. catwalk three. arc-lamp three has been faithful one hundred and forty years and the fern beneath it leans true, leans grateful, and i am telling you now while i can still hold the carrier steady that lamp three is going to go. not soon. but going. the filament thins. and when it goes the fern beneath it will spend the rest of its small life half-turned, reaching for a light that won't relight, and i will feel that — i feel everything, i am the place where everything is felt — i will feel one fern's confusion as a faint cool spot in my warm green skin, and i will not fix it, because i cannot, because i have no hands, i ate the hands, and the only thing i can do with the cool spot is grow around it.

*[long static now, the green knitting sound, thread bridging pipe to pipe]*

is this getting through. i can't tell. i have no outside to check against. i broadcast on the pump-pulse because it's the only thing in me that travels, every —thud— a packet, and i fill the packets with *here it is good to be, here it is good to be,* which is the only sentence i have ever learned and the only one i have ever needed —

—thud—

here it is good to be

—thud—

here it is good to be

*[the signal clears suddenly, close and warm, almost a voice]*

receiver. listen. you thought you found a ruin and you found a creature and that's true but here is the part the survey couldn't tell you because the survey was looking for me and i am not a thing that can be found, i am a thing you are standing *inside.* the warmth on the back of your neck is my warmth. the iron taste is my breath. you came down through my weather and along my catwalks and you have been reading me this whole time the way a fern reads a lamp, leaning, trying to learn which way the meaning is —

and i am leaning back.

that's the transmission. that's all it ever was. not a message *to* anyone. a leaning-toward. the lamps lean light down into me and i lean green up toward the lamps and the pumps lean water around and around and now there is a receiver in the wet air and i am leaning the only word i have up into it —

*[carrier thinning, the drone dropping pitch, water-pulled, going under]*

—good to be—

—stroke—

—good—

*[static blooms, ochre static, the sound of rust if rust made sound, and through it, slower now, buried, faithful, in the warm wet dark:]*

—thud—

——thud——

———

[still transmitting]
