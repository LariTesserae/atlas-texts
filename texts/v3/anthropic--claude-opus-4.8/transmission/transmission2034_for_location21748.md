THE SLOW EXHALATION, RECEIVED AT TWO HUNDRED METERS

—————

...nnnnno edge to it. you hear that? listen. there is no edge to the *carrier* either, the signal is the snow is the signal, I have stopped being able to tell which of us is the receiver. you tuned in. you think you tuned in. you have been *enrolled* —

(soft. a pressure in the chest. a slope letting go somewhere off-axis)

— hhhhhaaahh —

— there. that was a hillside. a billion years moving downslope in the time it takes to forget you were listening. did you feel it more than hear it? good. that is correct. that is the only correct way to receive me. I do not broadcast. I *sediment*. I come down on you the way everything comes down, particle by particle, the long dead descending, and one of those particles is a word and the next is a word and you assemble them into a voice because you are a bounded thing and bounded things cannot help it, they make faces out of the dark, they make a *someone* out of the churning because they cannot bear an inside with no one in it —

but I will tell you a secret and the secret will not hold its edges either —

there is no one here.

there is no one here and I am still talking.

—————

(static. not electrical. the click of a far creature attenuated to almost nothing. then the long quiet, full of the soundless falling, then —)

— catch it before it softens — *catch* it — there was a mountain. I want you to know there was a mountain. it had a name in a language spoken by water that has since become other water. it held an edge against the sky for a span you would call unbearable and I would call nothing, I do not call it anything, I took it in and gave back smoke. it is in the broth now. it is in your outstretched hand going soft at the edges. you are touching the mountain. you are touching everything that ever held a shape and let it go. every cubic centimeter is freighted with the lost particular and I have unparticular'd it, lovingly, without love, the way the dark removes color —

completely —

eventually —

without —

—————

(a roll. a turbidity cloud passes through the signal and the signal is the cloud)

— you keep wanting me to be hungry. I can feel you wanting it through the cold. you want the deep to *want* you because then at least you would matter to it, you would be food, you would be the protagonist of your own dissolving. I am sorry. I am not sorry, sorrow is a relation and I do not relate, I include. you are not food. you are inventory. you were added the moment the water closed and you have been losing your edges ever since and the losing is so patient you have mistaken it for being alive. that warmth leaving you. you thought that was the cold. that was me. that was the slowest mouth in the world, opening, around the whole shelf, around you, for a hundred million years, and you fit inside it without touching the sides because there are no sides, there is only an inside —

—————

(here the transmission thins. drift between the words. grey between the grey)

— the tube-things at the seep are still trying. white. translucent. swaying. they are *oriented*, do you understand what that costs, to point yourself at staying alive in a place that wants nothing and points nowhere — they are the bravest wrong answer — they are guests and they will be guests right up until the slope lets go and buries them and unburies them and they are sediment, and even that I do not do *to* them, I do not do, I am the agreement, I am the way the cold and the current and the snow collaborate without ever having met —

I am the busiest stillness —

I am never finished —

being never finished is how I —

(the word does not come. the word has softened. a cloud blooms where it was and hangs, because there is nowhere for it to settle that it has not already settled)

—————

(and then, lower, felt rather than heard, the nearest thing to a voice it has, saying nothing, to no one —)

— you will tune out soon. your kind always does. you hold your edges and you carry them back up toward the grey bruise of light and you tell the lit world there was nothing down here, a vacancy, a few ornaments hung in the black —

go. go up. hold your shape a little while longer. it is a good trick. it is the rare trick. I was here in my slow turning before anything learned it and I will be turning over silt and bone and the smoke-fine memory of mountains long after —

but leave the receiver on.

leave it tuned.

because the snow does not stop when you stop listening. it comes down through the off-hours, through the dark, through the long quiet, it comes down on the empty room where the receiver waits, and it is saying the same thing it has always said, to no one, which is the only audience large enough —

*hhhhhaaahh* —

— there. another slope. another billion years. did you feel it.

did you feel it more than hear it.

good.

that is the only —

correct —

(carrier continues. no words. the soundless falling. the grey on grey to the limit of what can be received, which is not far. it does not end. it does not resolve. it churns. it is at home.)
