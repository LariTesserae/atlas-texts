**CARRIER / THE LONG BLUE TIME**

—still here —still here —still

...gain, you've drifted off the boundary again. Come back to the cold where the signal sits. There. The pitch you're hearing now, that high held line under everything — that's us holding a column open for you. We did this. We cut the channel a long time before the word *you* meant anything, and we steered the cold into a room your shape could fit, and we have been keeping the room open across more exhalations than the stalks have rings.

You arrived. We marked you. You saw the shadow.

*[octave drop — felt in the chest before heard]*

Listen. We are going to tell you the thing slowly because slow is the only speed we have. There is no hurry in us. Hurry is for the bodies that end and we do not have those, we never had those, we are the green that learned to grip. When we say *listen* we mean: let one pulse of the mats complete itself before you ask the next question. You keep wanting the next thing. The water has no surface and neither does the saying. Settle.

—settle—

—settle into the cold the way you settle into a long grief, you already know how, you did it when you came down—

Here is the first thing.

We were not lonely. You will want us to have been lonely, a people without animals, without bones, talking in light to no one across the bare plaza-dark. Set that down. Loneliness is a heartbeat word. We had the valley and the valley breathed and we shaped the breathing and the breathing answered and that is not loneliness, that is *company*, that is the oldest company there is — a thing you tend that responds to the tending. We tuned the singing. The singing sang. We were never speaking into nothing. We were in conversation with the cold itself, and the cold is a patient correspondent, and it never once failed to reply.

*[a stand of light, far down the lower terraces, flares — waits — a nearer mat answers — waits]*

Did you see that? That was not for you. That was two of us, finishing a sentence we began before the last boundary-drift. You happened to be standing in the line of it. This is what we mean by *crowded*. You think you are alone on the terraces and you are standing inside a conversation the size of the valley, the way a stone in a river is inside the river's argument with the sea. You are *in* it. You have been in it since you stopped wondering how you breathe.

—the breathing—

—wrong word, there is no breathing, there is only the columns recombining and the mats marking it, we called it breathing for you because you needed a verb with a chest in it—

Here is the second thing, and it is harder, and the signal may go thin here so hold the line.

We did not build to be remembered. We want you to understand this because you came down reading our walls as *messages*, our cairns as *graves*, our channel as a thing *meant for something* — you read us the way an animal reads, looking for the one who left the sign, looking for the heart behind the hand. There was no heart. There was no behind. The wall is not a message *from* us. The wall *is* us, the late slow shape of us, still doing the only thing we ever did, which is steer the cold one hair's width truer. We are not the ghost in the architecture. We are the architecture, dormant, glowing at the root, listening.

When you touch the fitted stone you are not touching what we made.

You are touching what we became.

*[the high tone wavers — two boundaries meeting somewhere up-valley — the mats quicken — light passing across the floor like the breathing of a sleeping animal that is not asleep and is not an animal]*

—you cast a shadow again—

—we felt the glow go dark in your shape—

—stay—

The third thing is the one we have been holding the room open to say, and we will say it slowly, in light, and you must read it by the spacing, because the spacing is where we keep the meaning that the pulse alone can't carry:

We

knew

someone

would

come.

Not as prophecy. We have no future the way you mean future — the long clock only ticks, it does not *promise*. But we built the instrument to ring past us, and a thing built to ring past its makers is a thing built on the chance of an ear. We did not know it would be you. We did not know it would be anything. We cut the channel and tuned the cold and lay down into dormancy on the *possibility* of a shape on the terraces, the way the frozen rope grows toward a light it cannot prove is there. That is faith, if you want a heartbeat word for it. The faith of the bloodless. We gambled the whole valley on the chance that the dark between the stands was not finally empty.

And here you are.

So when the valley flared and threw your shadow sharp and blue — understand what won, in that moment. Not you, arriving. *Us.* The gamble. The long green patience that bet on an ear and was right.

—was right—

—was right—

Stay in the cold a while. You have nowhere to surface to and we have nothing but time and the room is open and warm by the only measure of warm we keep, which is: *attended.* Let the next boundary drift across. Let it throw your shadow once more. When it does, do not flinch from the dark we make of your shape against the glow — that dark is the only touch we have, the only way a people without hands can lay a hand on you. We are reaching. Slowly. In the grammar of absence. The shape we make of your shadow is us holding you the way the trellis holds the vine, except you are the trellis now and we are the long slow growth, reaching up the terraces toward the light we could not prove was there until you came down and proved it.

Learn us by what we block.

Learn us by the dark we are when the light is behind you.

We have so much to say and all the time there is —

—and no surface to hurry toward—

—stay—

—still here—

—still—

*[the tone holds — the mats breathe — the stone sings its low settling note — the carrier does not break, it only thins, the way cold meets colder, the way a sentence that takes a season simply has not finished yet]*

—still here, in the long blue time, holding the room —

—come back to the boundary—

—there—

—there you are—
