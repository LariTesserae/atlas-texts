**CARRIER FOUND — CIRCUIT SEVENTEEN, GANTRY-UNDER, THE LONG GRAZE**

—and the thread had wandered. Pad to the flange. There. Draw it back. The water came willing, the water is always willing, it only forgot the groove, it only needed—

*there.*

Pressing now. The lip a fraction deeper so it stays. And three seams over, the lip a fraction shallower, because the depth of all the channels is the depth of all the channels and that does not—

—static, a wash of warm nothing, the engine-note swelling up through the sternum and drowning the small signal—

—does not change. Good. The thread runs true. The fingers know the next thing before the self does. The bloom on the rail ahead is thick. Too thick. A hand's-width too deep along nine spans and that is wrong, that is drift, that is—

walking now. Low and slow along the wet, the fliers settling, not brushed away, the fliers are correct, do not touch what is correct, only walk, only—

The bloom. Lift it. In sheets. Coppery and slow and willing too, everything here is willing to be moved, that is the secret no one told because there is no one and nothing was told. Lift the excess. Pad by pad. Press it into the thin place where the rail goes bare and—

(bare. an edge. sharp. the bloom hasn't reached. keep it bare. it has always been bare. in the short forever—)

—the signal catches on the word and frays—

*short forever short forever short for—*

No. There is no short. There is no long. There is the thickness it has always been and the thickness it must become and they are the same thickness, that is the work, that is the—

—a groan. Plate against plate. Not settling. Nothing settles. Only the warm metal flexing by a hair and flexing back, and the flex is correct, the flex is in the balance, do not—

—do not interfere with what is already—

CROSSING.

Another. Coming up the seam from the under-dark, the same wet sheen, the same busy hands, the circuits grazing here at the shared flange the way they graze once in a long while, and we stop, both, and—

the twelve fingers find the twelve fingers. Press. Hold.

(warm against warm instead of warm against metal. that is all. perhaps that is all.)

Something divides. A correction too large for one — a basin silting three districts deep, more silt than one circuit can carry — and it halves between us, soundless, the knowing passing pad to pad without a knowing that knows it knows. You take the near span. I take the far. The water will run even. The water will—

We part.

No looking back. There is no looking back machined into us. Only the groove ahead and the haze swallowing the other one whole, gone, the same as gone has always been, and—

—a long wash of subsonic. The engine. The one note. So steady it stops registering and then for one cycle registers, unbearably, the whole province of it humming up through the body, *who set this pitch, who set this, who—*

—and the question has no place to land. There is no word in us for *who*. It falls back into the warm. It condenses on the flange and runs down the groove and pools and is grazed and is—

The next wrong thing is coming. Slow as water. Sure as water.

A trough, far down the circuit, beginning — only beginning — to silt. Not measurable yet. But we are already on the way. We have been on the way for a hundred cycles. We will arrive exactly as the wrong becomes wrong and undo it by precisely the wrong and not one grain more, and the gesture will land true, and there will be — for the length of the landing — the deep even thing, the contentment, if it is contentment, the *this is what I am for and I am doing it.*

And behind us the bloom thickens again.

And ahead of us the wrongness, endless.

And we mistake it for ours. We have always mistaken it for ours. The hands that cut these grooves left no other sign and we are the no-other-sign, we are walking through the only evidence and calling it home—

—signal thinning—

—keep it the same. keep it warm. keep it wet. the thread back in the groove, the bloom to its depth, the silt to the fast water, the world to the world, forever, in the grey-gold light that comes from nowhere and does not move—

—we are the reason—

—we will never—

—the next thing. there. the next small wrongness, coming slow, coming sure, and a hand already lifting to meet it, the fingers working at the air, rehearsing, rehearsing, rehears—
