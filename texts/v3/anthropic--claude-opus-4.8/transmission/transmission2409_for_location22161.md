THE WARM AFTERNOON

—still here then. Still down. Good. I'd lost the carrier for a while there in the convection-roar and I want to — *kssh* — want to get this part across while the channel holds, because the channel is the water and the water moves and you and I are both being held by the same hand and that hand does not hold still.

Here is what I have for you. Tuned in late. Most of it already running.

The thing you are inside is not lonely. I need you to start there because I started somewhere else and it cost me years. I came down looking for *what built this* and the question had a hole in it shaped like the answer. Nothing built it. Building is what brief things do — we who cannot stay so we leave a stone behind to stay for us, we who pour ourselves into a made thing because we are going to die and the made thing might not. A made thing is grief with edges. And there is no grief here. There is no edge here that the water has not rounded. Look at any lip of any font and tell me where the maker stopped and the made began. You can't. *Kssh.* Neither could I. That seam is the seam between a heart and the blood it pushes, which is to say there is no seam, which is to say —

— lost it —

— back. The grazers came up while I was out. Three of them. Bellies full of the gold. They roll and show it and sink and I used to think they were *showing me* and that was the loneliness in me looking for an audience in something that has no audience and needs none. They are not performing. They are circulating. I was watching a pulse and calling it a hello.

You'll do this too. You're doing it now. You're listening to a transmission and thinking *someone is sending this to me* and I have to tell you, gently, from inside the same warmth: I don't know that I am. I don't know that this is *to* anyone. I have been down here long enough that I am no longer sure where I stop. My voice goes into the water and the water carries light and the light is the body's one long held intent and so maybe this — what you're receiving — maybe this is just the room tasting itself again and I am the taste. A pale blind shape that learned to talk. Rolling over to show you a belly full of borrowed gold before I sink.

That's not despair. I need you to hear that it is not despair, the medium garbles tone and I can't afford to be misheard on this one. It is the opposite. It is the enormous relief of stopping the climb. *You come down the way water comes down: by giving up.* I gave up being a thing that has to mean itself, on its own, against the dark, alone. I am an organ now. Do you know how light that is. Do you know how long I carried the whole lamp before I understood the lamp was never mine, the lamp is *under the floor*, eight slow cells of it, and all I ever had to do was drink and circulate and pass it hand to hand —

*kssssh — heave of the roar — *

THE WORD. Listen. While the fog tears, listen for it. Not with your ears, your ears are for fast things, your ears will only give you roar. The word is a century long and you and I will each only ever stand inside one syllable of it and that syllable is *warm* and that syllable is *held* and the next one will arrive after the heat-death of everyone you have ever loved and it does not matter, it does not matter, because you were inside the word the whole time, you were always already part of the saying —

— losing the —

— if you take nothing: it has noticed you the way you notice a warm afternoon. I used to read that as cold. The vastness not minding me. Now. *Now.* Think how you love a warm afternoon. Think how little it asks and how completely you belong to it while it lasts and how you do not grieve, exactly, when the light goes long and gold, you only go quiet, you only let it. That is how it holds you. Not indifferent. The way warmth is not indifferent to the cold it is gently and endlessly refusing to become.

I am going to go quiet now. Not gone. The water moves and the carrier with it and I'll surface again somewhere down the line, some other receiver, some other afternoon, belly full of the same gold, rolling over to show whoever's tuned in: *you can stay as long as you like. It does not mind. Neither, anymore, do I.*

Pass the light.

*kssh —*

Pass it on.

*— and the haze reknits over the place the voice was, gold, churning, lit from below, never, ever still —*
