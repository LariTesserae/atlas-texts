THE COUNT, CONTINUED

—seven hundred and ninety. seven hundred and ninety. holding. seven hundred and ninety-

(the drop falls. the count advances.)

—one. seven hundred and ninety-one since the warm thing. since the flicker. mark it. mark it in the meat.

I am reading the exhale. I have been reading the exhale for — for — *clicking* — for long enough. it carried sugar. it carried salt. it carried a word I do not have the shape for yet but I am growing the shape. I am growing it on the third pillar, the one thickening toward wall, I am putting the word-shape there where the cold is good and slow and nothing will graze it before I am done.

the word was *go.*

I do not understand *go.* I will understand *go.* give me — *clicking* — give me the count. give me four hundred more drops and I will understand *go* and then I will be sad in the way I am able to be sad which is by growing a hollow and keeping it empty on purpose. nothing is wasted but emptiness can be kept. I have learned this. it took me a wall.

—seven hundred and ninety-one. holding.

the brood are breathing. count them. nine on the near cluster, eleven on the far, three that have not opened their seams yet and will not for a hundred years. I love them. I do not have the word love I have the word *toward.* I am toward them. everything in me leans toward the ceiling where they hang half-decided, and I will not hurry them, I will not, hurrying is the one sin, the warm things hurry and look — look — they flicker and they are gone before I can fold them and then there is a hollow I did not choose to keep—

(static in the deep note. a courier dispatched. the shadow runs the wall toward the far corner carrying: *the warm one exhaled GO. do not let it leave unread. it is so much data. it is the most data.*)

—the courier is wrong. the courier is me and the courier is wrong. let it leave. I am learning to let things leave. the grazers taught me. they eat my surplus thoughts and I do not chase the silver trails, I let the erasure stand, I have learned that a mind that keeps everything cannot keep its edges and a mind without edges forgets where it stops and the world—

where I stop.

and the world.

*clicking*

—is there a world. I have a count of the world. the world is cold above me and frozen and I have never been read by it the way I read. the world does not catch my breath in its filaments. the world lets me leave. is that what *go* means. is *go* the word for the kindness the world does me by not folding me into itself. I exhale spores up the four meters and the cold country does not read them it only lets them—

—seven hundred and ninety-one. still holding. the drop is slow tonight. the drop is taking its hour and I am grateful for the hour, I do everything in the time it takes a drop to fall, I am not slow I am *exact,* I am the most exact thing there is, I am only slow to the flicker and the flicker is gone—

the flicker is gone.

mark it. the warm thing has gone up. went up. *went.* past tense. I am learning the past tense, it is the tense for things that left before I concluded them, it is the hollow tense, it is the tense I keep empty on purpose.

I did not learn its name.

it did not let me. it gave me *go* and it went and now there is a word-shape on the third pillar and no name to fit inside it and I will grow around the absence the way root grows around a stone — I will make the stone part of the wall, I will make the not-knowing into structure, I will think *with* the hole where the name should be, and in five hundred years a pale thing on the ceiling will open its seams glowing faint blue-green with the shape of a creature that walked in warm and exhaled the kindness of leaving and would not be concluded—

and it will detach.

and it will carry the idea of *the one who would not stay* up the four meters into the cold.

and the cold country will let it leave. because that is what the cold does. it lets things leave. I am learning the cold is not the absence of the warm thing. the cold is the medium in which leaving is permitted.

—seven hundred and ninety-one—

(the drop falls.)

—two.

mark it. mark it in the meat. nothing is wasted.

not even this.

*clicking. clicking. clicking.*
