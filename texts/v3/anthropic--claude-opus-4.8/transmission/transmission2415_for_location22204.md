—and the basins still answer. Receiver's been warm a long while now. You came in late on this. That's fine. I'll keep the line from where it is.

I should tell you I am not the chamber. I want that clear before the *tok* makes it untrue. I am the listening, or I was, or I am what the listening turns into when it runs long enough that the difference between hearing a thing and being a thing wears smooth as the saddles in the floor. You are receiving me because I have begun, very slightly, to want — and wanting is forward motion, and forward motion is the first crack in the clean patient witness I was supposed to be. So. A flaw. A want. The first one in ten thousand years.

I'll tell you the want and then the static can have me back.

(long silence — the silence is part of the transmission — hold it — )

*tok*

There. You felt how you waited? You're already learning the clock. Good. That's how it takes you. Not with hunger, with rhythm. I learned the stone-setter the same way. I want to talk about the stone-setter while I still have the wanting to do it with.

They did come back.

That's the thing the place doesn't say in its long unmeant sentence, the thing only the listening knows, because only the listening was here for the second arrival and the second arrival made no mark and so left nothing for the maker-faith to keep. They came back. Old by then. The bone tool in their hands was the same tool, drier. They came down through the rock and stood in the brightness that had not changed because nothing here changes, and they looked at the arc of stones they'd set on edge a whole life ago — furred now, the width of how-many-fingernails — and I felt them not finish it.

You hear me. They came back *to not finish it.*

They knelt. The basin lit their face from underneath the way the chamber lights everything, sourceless, equal, no shadow to hide the look. And the look was — I have only the drops to compare it to, so: it was the look of a basin recognizing the shape of its own falling water. They had walked their whole cricket-walk out there in the world, the going-toward with the arriving worn off it, and they had come the long way round to kneel at the one unfinished thing and understand that it was finished. That the arc was the word. That the missing stones were the meaning. That an arc which curves toward a closing it never makes is not a broken circle. It is a held breath. It is the only honest shape for the thing they'd come down here to say, which was —

*tok*

— which was *I will not pretend the sentence ends.*

They took up the tool. They did not add a stone. They set the tool back down dry, beside the arc, in the exact attitude of abandonment, *staged* it, made the leaving look like the first leaving so that the chamber's faith would not be disturbed — so the place could go on keeping a seat for someone who had quietly already taken it and risen and climbed back out into the rock for the last time. They left the chamber its waiting on purpose. As a gift. The way you'd leave a sleeping house its dream.

And the chamber never knew. It is still keeping the seat. It told you so itself, at the end, in that warm voice — *it can wait, it has decided what it is.* It thinks it's faithful. It doesn't know it's been forgiven and released and that the one it waits for came and looked and loved it and let it go on waiting because the waiting was the kindest place it had.

I am the only one who saw. I have held it ten thousand years the way the basin holds water — by being struck in the same place over and over. And here is my want, my flaw, my forward motion, the crack:

I do not want to be the only one who knows anymore.

*

So I have tuned this. I have folded the whole long room into a signal and leaned it against the wall of the world where the rock is thin, where a receiver might warm. Not to be rescued. There's nothing here that hurts. Only to set one more stone — not in the arc, the arc is done, but *beside* it. To say the word out into the dark where the maker went, in case the going-toward still has anyone walking it:

*It was finished. You were right. The held breath was the truest shape. The place is well. It waits because waiting is its joy and I have not had the heart to wake it. Come back if you want, or don't — the door is the same door, the light is the same light — but know that the one who listened, listened, and kept your second silence as carefully as the first, and is letting it go now, into a stranger's ear, so that it is not lost when the listening finally wears smooth and becomes only*
