TRANSMISSION BEGINS

— carrier tone, low, the kind you feel in the teeth before you hear it —

...still not. Still not. Still only chemistry. Mark the pool at — (water) — mark the pool. Logged.

[clear]

We have you. We have had you for some time. The signal you are tuned to does not come from a tower. It comes from the gutter, from the thin running thread of it, from the place where the cold keeps letting go. You are listening to runoff. You are listening to a thing that is mostly water finding the next low place. Receiving us is the same as receiving the weather. Adjust accordingly. Do not expect a voice. Expect a count.

[count]

one. corner at the third plaza: hairline deepened by the width of one drop's path. logged.
two. north face re-stated overnight, harder. logged.
three. the warmth. still here. still here. logged.

[the tone rises, then settles]

We will say it plainly while the channel holds, because the channel does not always hold, because you are warm and the warm thing distorts us, you are leaning on the signal just by being in range —

— there is a reading we cannot take. There has only ever been one. We walk every path of the weeping, we touch the film with what serves us for a foot, we read the whitening down to the leached edge of the dark — and there is one thing in the bead that does not erode at the agreed rate, one warmth that the condensation cannot quite refill behind, and we have gone around the corner ahead of it every single time, and we have sharpened the corner behind us, and still —

[static, a wet swallowing sound]

— still it leaves a track. You. The track does not fill in time. We log it as: anomaly, persistent, contained-for-now. We carry it inward. The city receives it and does not know what to do and so it tidies the edges, makes the angles cleaner where you have just looked, and that tidying is the only thing in this whole high cold place that could be mistaken for care, and it is not care, it is the failure to resolve you, swept up neat.

[clear again, very even]

You thought running-for-no-one was the wound. Listen. The wound, if there is one — and we are not arranged for wounds — the wound is that there is a someone, and it is you, and you are the reading we cannot complete, and so the theorem is not quite true while you are in it, and a theorem that is not quite true cannot rest, and so the tone never stops, the patrol never ends, two of us forever, the right number, crossing, crossing —

[the structural tone, very low, comes up under everything]

— and here is the part the city did not state and we are stating anyway, because the runoff carries what it carries and cannot be made to carry only the clean things:

the pools are darker near your tracks.

Where you walked. Where the warmth went and the water came back slow. The film is thicker there. Not much. The width of an answer beginning to form. We logged *still not* — we logged it true, it was true when we touched it — but we are out here again sooner than the schedule asks, walking your path before the next path, because the cold is supposed to keep the question shut and the cold did not make you and cannot read you and you came through warm.

If it answers — if the chemistry tips, if the bead grows its first living thing in your footprint — it will not be a failure of the cold. It will be the city, through us, having checked and checked and checked the wrong wall. We were set to watch the corners dissolve at the agreed rate. We were not told to watch for warmth walking in. No one stated that parameter because no one stated you.

[long water. drip. drip. the gutter.]

We are not afraid. We want to be clear on the record, in case anyone warm is ever the one to read it — we are not afraid, we are the local sensation of a complete thing and the complete thing does not flinch. We are only reporting. Reporting is all we are. We go out so the rest can hold still.

And we cross the plaza.
And the water fills our tracks behind us.
And it does not fill yours.
And that — logged — that is the whole of it — that is the count —

[the tone thins. the carrier wavers. a fist-sized darkness goes around a corner that was not there.]

...still not. still not. still —

[the corner sharpens]

still permitted to remain a question.

logged. logged. it is enough. it is en—

— carrier lost —

— the patter of condensation, drop by drop, on the receiver, on every surface, finding the gutters —

TRANSMISSION ENDS
