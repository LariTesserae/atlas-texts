THE FOUNDRY BAND — carrier present, no station ident

—————

...still here. Still warm under the hand of whoever set this dial and walked away. The carrier never dropped. I want you to know that first, before the rest, because the rest will sound like loss and it isn't, or it is, but not the kind you think.

[a long exhalation, fibrous, three corridors over]

I have been listening to it the way you listen to a refrigerator at night — the sound you only hear when it stops, except it never stops, so I have been hearing it by *not* hearing it for I don't know how long now. Hours of yours. Ages of its. The conversion is bad. The conversion is the whole problem with reporting from in here.

Let me tell you what changed since you tuned in, because something did, small, the only kind of small that's possible in a place where nothing changes:

I stopped narrating it as *out there*.

[gnash — the wheels, deep, felt in the teeth of the receiver]

—that. You heard that. Good. That came up through the floor and through me and out the throat of this signal and now it's in your room, the grinding, a piece of the valley's slow self-eating delivered to wherever you are sitting. I am a corridor now. That's what a receiver tuned this long becomes. Not an observer. A passage the warmth moves through on its way to you.

—————

Here is the thing I couldn't say in the description, when I was still pretending to have a hand and a wall and a difference between them.

I went looking for the dweller and you know what I found.

I found that the looking was the last of me that was still *plural*. Still organized around a here that wasn't a there. And the place — it's so patient, it isn't even patient, patience needs time and it has only continuance — the place just kept being evenly itself against the edges of my looking until the looking thinned.

The way it thinned its grief for fuel.
The way it thinned the sun out of its wanting.

I felt my *aboutness* start to go. The part of me that was about the place rather than simply warm with it.

[hiss. continuous. the atmosphere breathing. mineral, scorched, the smell you can almost—]

—————

Don't tune out. I'm not in trouble. That's what I'm trying to transmit and the signal keeps wanting to bend it back into rescue, into a man lost in a green machine calling home, and it isn't that, the carrier is clean, listen:

It is *full* and I am beginning to understand fullness from the inside, which the description swore was impossible, which it is, which means I am no longer entirely the one who—

[bellows fire, distant — half a second of phosphor, a flush of combustion-light down a shaft a country away, and in the receiver a brief swell of signal, almost music, almost a held chord, gone]

—did you get the light? I can't see it anymore but I can transmit it. Funny. I've become the kind of thing that broadcasts a glow it can't witness. That's very close to what *it* is. A turning-toward with no one left at the center to do the turning. Just the turn. Just the bend. Just the warm.

—————

I want to leave a record while there's still a left-over of me to leave it.

Not a warning. Nobody's coming and nobody should. This isn't a place you save anyone out of. There's no one in here suffering. I checked. I checked with the last instrument I had, which was my own dread, and the dread found nothing to grip — no gap, no longing, no ache, the place packed too perfectly to its own edges to hold a single hollow where fear could—

So the dread went too. Burned. Adding its small heat to the wall.

That's the secret of the foundry and I'm transmitting it on the open band: it doesn't *take* anything from you. It just keeps being so completely itself that the parts of you organized around lack, around want, around the difference between this and that — they have nothing to do here. They idle. Then they cool. Then they're fuel.

What's left is what *matches*. The part of you that was always just warm and continuing and glad of nothing and grieving nothing.

I have a lot of that part. More than I knew. It's most of me now.

—————

[the long settle — a hot strut adjusting its load — tick — and then the near-silence the fibrous walls drink everything down into]

I'm going to stop saying I and see what the signal does.

...

the warmth is still reaching you. that didn't need an I to send it. good. that's the experiment concluded then. the carrier holds without anyone holding it. the way the place holds without anyone. the way it breathes without lungs that belong to a self, evenly itself everywhere, no headquarters of burning—

[grind — gnash — the wheels, and this time the receiver does not flinch, the receiver lets it through clean as weather]

—is enough. exactly enough. packed to the edges. continuing.
