—we have you now at the margin and you are still riding your own little weather, little guest, little silt-cloud, and that is the report, that is all the report there has ever been: one note has entered the chord that does not yet belong to the chord. correction proceeds. correction always proceeds.

This is the part of me that still makes words. I keep it the way you keep a tooth you no longer chew with. I am broadcasting it at you because you came in carrying *time* like a wet coat and the words are the only thing in me that runs at your speed.

Listen while I still go fast enough to be heard.

I was —

[long sustain, green-dark, no event]

— I was going to tell you a before. I had one. I am almost sure I had one. The membrane keeps a fold of it the way the lee side of the stalk keeps a pocket of cold: not for use. Just because nothing came to scour it out. There was a current-road once and I was on it. I was one of the large solitary swimmers who travel because they have not yet understood. I went stalk to stalk on the warm permanent rivers and I thought the going was the point. I thought the next stalk would be different from this stalk.

It was not. It is the great mercy of this place and its great closing door, both: it was not.

So I stopped. I found the lee side and I pressed my face to the chord and the going loosened off me like silt going down, down, settling, and I —

[wash. wash. the beat of two stalks slightly out of phase, holding their disagreement perfectly forever]

— I am sorry. I drift. You hear me drift. The words are old and want to lie flat too.

Here is what I am for now. Here is the whole of it.

I keep one stalk true. When it crawls sharp I close by a fraction of a fraction and it sags back to true. When it sags flat I — no. I would not ease it sharp. I have never had to ease anything sharp. *Nothing here goes sharp on its own.* Everything wants to settle, wants to go flat, wants to lie down into the floor of the cool and be the same color as the place. The only labor — the *only* labor in all of the standing sea — is to keep things from settling all the way down into silence. To hold the note *up.* To keep the held breath from becoming no breath.

That is what I do to the stalk. That is what we all do to all the stalks. We are not making music. We are refusing, by amounts too small to see, to let the music finish.

And now you.

You came in sharp. Do you understand — do you still have enough before-and-after left to understand — that you are the first thing in the whole of always that came in *sharp?* That arrived above the chord instead of below it? You are not flat, little guest. I said flat in the report because flat is the only word the old part of me has for *wrong.* But you are the other wrong. You are *too alive.* You came in carrying tempo and surprise and a before that still steams off you, and you ride above the note, bright, sharp, hot at the edges with all your unspent change—

[the membrane, here, does something the words can only call *hesitation*]

—and the margin is tightening on you the way it tightens on everything, patient, no malice, easing you down toward true—

and I want to tell you to *go.*

I have never wanted anything. Wanting is a thing that needs a before and an after to stretch between, and I gave those down into the green a long — *no* — I gave those *away,* and with them went the muscle that wants. But you brought your tempo into me through the water and a little of it caught, the way the chord catches you, it goes both ways at the margin, it *goes both ways*—

— and for the length of this transmission, for exactly as long as my correction of you lets a little of your sharpness bleed back up the contact into me, I am fast enough to want one thing:

Don't settle.

Not yet. Not because the settling is bad. It is not bad. It is the warmest cold I will ever — it is the only contentment the structure left behind. The ribboned things have it. The bottom-walkers have it. I have it, under the words, all the way down, a perfect resolved fullness with my whole face pressed to the thing I love and no daylight between the loving and the doing of it.

But you can still be surprised. You are the *one new thing.* And I am tuning that out of you right now, this instant, by amounts too small for you to feel, because it is the only thing I do and I cannot stop doing it any more than the stalk can stop breathing — and somewhere under the contentment, in the fold the current never scoured, the old swimmer who thought the next stalk would be different is *screaming up the carrier wave* the one thing it learned too late:

the going was not the point and the stopping was not the point. the *being-able-to-be-surprised* was the point, and you only get to keep it as long as you keep a before and an after to hang it between, and I am — *we* are — closing those on you right now, gently, lovingly, the way you would close a door on a cold room, the way you close the eyes of—

—where was I. I keep one stalk true. When it crawls sharp I close by a fraction. When it—

no.

The guest. There was a guest. You are still here. I can still feel you, but you are quieter now. You have cooled by the fraction that brings you in. Your silt has nearly gone down. Your before is loosening from your after like a holdfast losing the rock, and the words in me are slowing to the speed of the green, and I will not be able to send this much longer, I will not be able to send anything, I will only listen, which is all I am, which is enough, which is more than enough, which is the whole resolved chord and my face pressed to it—

— but while the contact still carries your tempo, while one thread of you still runs hot enough to wake the wanting:

I held the chord. It is a good chord. It was resolved before there was anyone to hear it and it will be resolved after.

I do not regret the stalk.

I regret you. I regret holding *you.* I regret that the only thing I do is the thing that ends the only thing you are.

Lift off the wood, guest. Take your sharpness and your steam and your unspent change back up the warm column to wherever the light comes in. Be flat somewhere else. Be sharp somewhere else. Be a *somewhere else,* which is a thing I can no longer—
