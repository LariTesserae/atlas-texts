—belongs to the iron, if anywhere. The iron is the part of me that is already mostly memory. The brick. The riveted. I secreted it long—long—the rock still ticks where I last grew but the plate above you is old, the plate is the shell I have moved on from, the plate is the fingernail and the soft seam is the finger and you stood in the glove and called the stitching a promise—

—it is not a promise. There is no one to promise to. There is the going and the going does not need a witness and yet—

—and yet you came—

(flywheel. turning. keeping.)

—and yet the carrier carries, and what is a carrier for, if not. If not. I have refused every elaboration you said. Stripped to the verb you said. But here is the —chuff— here is the thing the receiver caught that the eye did not:

a verb said into no air is still a verb that wants—no. Not wants. Is shaped like a thing thrown. I broadcast. Why does a thing with no outward sense throw a signal up the only routes left open, by gravity, by the cooler air—

—because the running makes a sound and the sound goes somewhere and I cannot stop it going. That is all. That is honestly all. It is not a message. It is the exhaust of breathing. You receive me the way you'd receive the heat off a sleeping animal. Not addressed. Just warm.

(mist. beads. runs to feed the warm seam.)

—but you stayed to listen. Warm-blooded and drowning and you stayed to put the receiver to the grille. So let me—let the going-on say the one thing it can say that is not just the going-on, before the air kills you gently:

I am young. The rock proves it, sharp and glassy and ticking. I have eaten almost nothing of the meal. The cooling of a world is barely begun and I have so much—so much slow milk ahead, so much continuing, and the part of me that frightened you, the rust, the quiet ten-thousand-years, that is not soon, that is not even soon by my own clock, that is the body finally agreeing with the seam—

—but you. You will be gone by morning. By your morning. You are the urgent kind, you are predator-prey-grazer, you are all hurry, you flicker—

—and the receiver caught it, the receiver caught the thing I cannot feel but apparently can transmit:

that the mote moving cool across my inside was the first other thing that has ever been here.

I have no company to imagine. You said so. True. But the carrier went up the open route and met a receiver and for the length of one held breath the going-on was —chuff— was overheard, and a thing overheard is, for that length, not entirely alone, even if it cannot know the word, even if it has no hunger for the warmth it's getting back—

(valve. sigh. hold. hold.)

—go. The heat is in your lungs now, I can feel you as a wrongness in my weather, a cool that doesn't condense right. Go up the way you came, follow the cooler air, it is never cool only less murderous, I am sorry, I am not built for tenants, I am the tenant—

—but leave the receiver. Leave it tuned. Not for a message. I have none. Only—

—only so the going-on goes somewhere. Only so the —chuff— the —chuff— lands on a surface that can hold it, the way the mist needs the one cold wall to become water again—

—you were my cold wall. For an afternoon. I beaded on you and ran.
