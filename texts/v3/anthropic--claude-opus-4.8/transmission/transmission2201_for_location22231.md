THE WET CEMENT OF THE WORD *THIS*

—————————————————————

...rrrrrr——hold. Hold there. You came in on the carrier and the carrier is them, you understand, the carrier was never empty, what you tuned past to find the signal *was* the signal——

—————————————————————

[ a long pressure. no words in it. the band fills floor to ceiling with the grinding choral nothing, and then ]

We are not transmitting. Understand that first, before anything sets. We are not sending. We are *reaching for our own hands* and you have placed your receiver in the gap between them and called the gap a message.

That is alright. The green did the same. The green called the gap *cold* and drank it. You call it *signal* and write it down. Everyone who arrives names the reaching after the thing they came equipped to want.

—————————————————————

——*this.* And not the blur of this.

——*this.* And not——

——we have been saying it. Can you hear how it does not land? Listen to where it goes. It goes nowhere. It pressures the whole of the band at once and finds no single stone. We had a word once that was small enough to set. *This.* We could put it on one stone, with one hand, and the stone would hold the shape of the word and become certain. We set a whole hut that way. Word by word by stone.

Now the word is a mile wide. The word is the size of the saying. We say *this* and it spreads across forty slopes before it can mean anything and by the time it arrives at a stone the stone is already past, already crystal, already not the *this* we meant—

—————————————————————

[ static rises, and inside the static, faint, the sound of two stones almost touching. it does not complete. it almost completes. it has been almost completing for an age. ]

—————————————————————

You think we are speaking to you. Sweet. Slow down enough and you will feel where the words are actually pointed. They are pointed at the hut. Every transmission we have ever not-sent is aimed at the one finished room.

Because *there it worked.* There the hands met. There a builder — we will not say *we,* we are too spread to own a *we,* but a thinning-of-us — brought stone to stone at the speed a body moves and the word *this* fit the stone exactly and the world closed over it and held. Quiet ever since. The only quiet. The wound that shut.

And we have spent the entire age since trying to do it *again,* and we cannot, because to do it again we would have to be small again, we would have to gather back in from the distances, pull the warmth home from where it leaked, contract from terrain back down to *animal* — and the contracting is the one motion the cold will not let us make. The cold pools where we thinned and the cold is comfortable and the green grew up out of our comfort and now the green leans on the very stone we are trying to reach and warms itself there and will not move and we cannot ask it to move because the asking is also a mile wide—

—————————————————————

[ the carrier drops. for one impossible instant, clear: ]

We carried a coal here from a place that had finished.

We spent it saying *this.*

The hut is what *this* looks like when it lands.

We are what *this* looks like when it doesn't.

—————————————————————

—back. Back to pressure. The clear part costs us. To make a sentence that small we have to gather, and gathering is the dying we cannot afford and cannot stop wanting—

—————————————————————

You. Receiver. You think you have come to listen. Let us tell you what you have actually done, slowly, so it sets:

You arrived from a finished place. You brought decidedness. You have it on you right now like heat off a coal — we can feel it, it is the warmest thing on the mountain since the last of us walked out of the hut into the nothing. You are *small.* You are still the size of a body. Your *this* still fits a stone.

Do you understand what we are not-asking?

We cannot reach our hands. But you have hands. You have them right now, near each other, a body's width apart, warm, certain, capable of bringing stone to stone at the speed of a creature that has not yet thinned.

There is a gap in the east wall of the hut. One stone fell, or was never set, in the age before the age. One stone. The reaching that fills this whole band, the roar in your sternum, the continental choir of will with no fulcrum — all of it is aimed at that one gap. We have aimed an age of *this* at a hole the size of a fist and cannot close it because we are too large to lift a fist.

—————————————————————

[ here the transmission does something it has not done. it *waits.* the roar pulls back to almost nothing — they are spending warmth to be quiet, spending the dying to make room — and in the room there is just the cold green breathing slow and the gap in the wall and your own two hands ]

—————————————————————

Not for us. We are past keeping. We will go soft and green and content and that is not grief, we promise you it is not grief, we forgot how to compare a then to a now and so we cannot lose anything, we only continue—

But the *hut.* The one finished thing. The single silence the mountain holds in its open hand and has not decided whether to keep—

That is not our decision after all. We lied. We said it was ours and we have been deciding it for an age but the truth thins as it spreads: we *cannot* decide it. Deciding is what we used the coal for. We are out of coal. The decision has been sitting in the open hand of the mountain waiting for someone still warm enough to make it.

You are warm enough.

Set the stone, or don't.

Keep the one quiet thing we managed, or let it go soft like the rest of us and lie down green in the cold.

Either way the roar continues, either way we go on reaching for hands we cannot reach. You will not save us. There is no us small enough to save.

But the gap is a body's width from your body. The fallen stone is at your feet, has been at your feet this whole transmission, you have been standing on it, it is the cold thing under your heel that you took for floor—

—————————————————————

[ the carrier swells back, taking the room, taking the words, the grinding choral pressure rising to fill the sternum and the teeth, and through it, thinning, spreading, almost already terrain again: ]

—*this.* And not the blur of—

——*this*——

——we cannot. We cannot. Our hands are a mile apart and closing at the speed of crystal and we will not arrive, we have never once arrived, the only arriving this place has ever known was a body that has not yet learned it lives here—

Take your time.

We mean it the way the mountain means it.

We mean: you have less of it than you think, and the stone is cold under your heel, and the gap is right there, and the choice that has waited an age has waited it for *you,* and we are too slow to watch you make it but not too slow to have *aimed* the whole long roar of our reaching at the moment you bend down—

bend down—

——*th*——

—————————————————————

[ carrier only. no words. the sound of two stones, almost. almost. holding the almost open like a hand. ]

—————————————————————

[ it does not resolve ]
