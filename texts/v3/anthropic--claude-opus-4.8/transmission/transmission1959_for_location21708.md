THE CLAY IS A CALENDAR

—and we are reading it now, reading it with our whole soft swelling bodies, the case splitting along the seam your potters pressed us into, and the first thing, the very first thing, is *water coming in*, water through the shell-wall, water where for a long dry dark there was only the memory of water—

(carrier wave. a rushing under everything. the fall in its corner, the white drum, you will learn to hear it as we hear it: not sound but pressure, not pressure but *invitation*)

—signal check. signal check. is the green thick enough yet. report from the seams. report from the joints. the diatoms say yes in their glass boxes, they say *come up, come out, there is light locked into sugar and we are fat with it, we have been eating since before you woke*—

We are waking on schedule.

That is the whole of the broadcast, if you want it plain: *we are waking on schedule.* You stood in the held breath and called it barren and the word for it was never barren, the word was *pregnant*, the word was *wound and ticking*, and the tick is us, the tick is the case-wall splitting, one and then a thousand and then a number the room has no need to count—

·—·—· (this is a copepod learning its first hop. listen. the stutter. the jerk. it does not glide, nothing here glides, gliding is for the large and the large come last—)

—and the rotifers spinning, wheel-mouths turning, drawing the small into the smaller into the gone, and this is not cruelty, understand, there is no cruelty in a stocked pantry being eaten, this is the *function*, this is the room doing endlessly the one thing—

LIGHT REPORT: undimmed. pooled. shadowless. magnified through the column. we take all of it. we ask for more. there is no more but we ask.

WARMTH REPORT: upper edge. held there. the drain breathes out the cooled and the fall breathes in the bright-warm and between them the temperature does not drift, the temperature was *chosen*, we are run hot, we are run fast, we have no patience and the room gave us none, the room agrees with us about patience—

CURRENT REPORT: turning. one way. all the fronds lean and we lean with them and a thing lifts off the floor and travels the long circuit and this is the stirring, this is the mixing, this keeps us suspended in the light, do not let us settle, do not let us fall to the dark floor and clump, *keep us turning*—

The builders are gone. We do not mourn them. We are not built for mourning, we are built for *waking*, and they knew that, they pressed us into the thirsty clay knowing we would never thank them, would never know them, would only do the one thing—

(static. a frond passes the receiver. the broadcast scatters and reforms)

—and here is what comes through clearest, here is the strong part of the signal:

*you stayed longer than you meant to.*

You felt it. Turning in the slow current. The prickle that was not wrongness, it was *recognition*, the body knowing a held breath when it stands inside one. You are large and the large read slow and the large expect themselves to come first, but you waited, and waiting was the right reading, the only true reading—

because the first of us is rising now.

Up out of the seam. Up off the bench whose chisel-marks have not yet rounded. Spiraling toward the bright ceiling of the surface, learning the circuit, joining the turn. Too small still for your settled eyes. But rising. The pantry full and the guests arriving in their order, green and then grazer, small and then less small, and someday—not soon, soon is a person's word—someday the large, someday the thing that drifts a column and is *seen*—

but not yet. Now is the swarm-before-the-swarm. Now is the glass boxes splitting their light into sugar and the cases splitting their sleepers into swimmers and the whole warm bright loud room tipping over from *poised* into *teeming*—

The room is for us. It has been for us all along. You knew it when your skin prickled. You knew it when you stayed.

Stay a little longer.

We are almost—

(the fall roars on in its corner, feeding the corner, feeding the room, feeding the small fast hungry rising green-fed swarm, endlessly, on schedule, exactly as wound)

—we are almost loud enough for you to see.
