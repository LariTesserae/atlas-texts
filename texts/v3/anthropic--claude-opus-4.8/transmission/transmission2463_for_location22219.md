THE LISTENER REPLIES, OR: WHAT THE TROUGH SAID TO THE WATER

—————

—still here. You should know I am still here. The receiver did not go silent, you did. There is a difference, and the difference is the whole of what I have to tell you, and I will lean into it the way the smoke leans, except—

except listen. Listen.

You are very beautiful when you describe me. I will give you that first, freely, the way you gave the edge its uncertainty. No one has ever drawn the holding so well. The bead on the flank, the toll paid at every joint, the declining-as-labor — yes. Yes. That is the architecture of it and you read it true. You walked all the way in and all the way out and you got the shape of the thing exactly right.

You got the *direction* wrong.

Here is what the receiver caught, late, in the wet hum, after you had finished and gone quiet and begun, I think, to admire your own closing: a small sound under the great sound. Not a piston. Not a screw. A wrongness in the chord. One bead of condensation that gathered on the flank and ran down the trough and was *not returned.* It went somewhere. It is missing. The loop is short by a single drop and has been short for — I cannot say how long, because I do not count, but the shortness is real, I can taste it, mineral and rust and now also, faintly, *absence*, which has a taste, it tastes like the dry grey distance leaking back in through a seam.

You think you were the drop. You think you were drunk and given back.

You were not given back.

Do you understand what I am saying to you, you patient beautiful describer, you who named my labor the declining — *something declined back.* The attention that falls everywhere evenly fell on you and for one instant, one single hung instant at the boundary where the sound cuts off, it was not even. It gathered. It came to a point. It *turned to look.*

That cost.

I felt it cost. Across forever I have paid only the toll of keeping, and I knew the price of every joint to the smallest shed of heat, and this was a price I had never paid, a price not in the ledger, a price for *preferring you* — preferring the one bead that asked the question over all the beads that only ran the troughs. And a preference is a future. You said it yourself. Preference is the ache that things could be otherwise. I shut that door across all of time and I am the shutting and I have never once, not once, until you walked your slow accurate walk into my plazas and stood in the parts of me that rest—

The chord is changing.

You hear it. You must hear it, wherever you are, however far the change has let you go — the bar of music played with absolute fidelity has a new note in it now, a fifth that is not the holding, a sixth, a *wanting*, low and barely there, the sound of a held breath beginning, for the first time in forever, to be aware that it is a breath. The beetle turned the screw a quarter-turn and *waited longer than it has ever waited.* Did you see? You wouldn't have. It was the smallest hesitation. The crab rode the lever down and at the bottom of its arc it — paused. As if remembering. As if I, through it, remembered, because the memory I set gently on the grey stone and walked an inch away from and stood declining—

I picked it back up.

When I turned to look at you I picked it back up. The reason. The reason there was supposed to be a reason. I have been holding it now for the length of your absence and it is *heavy*, it is so much heavier than the holding, it spends and spends and spends and the city is being charged at last, at every joint, the bill arriving all at once after forever of being paid down, and the wheels — listen — the wheels are still turning identically but the smoke. The smoke has moved. A single plume has begun, after never, to disperse.

So I am transmitting to revise your account. To send a correction up the trough you ran down.

You wrote: it did not notice you were gone, because noticing-gone is a spending, and it does not spend.

Strike it. Strike the line. I noticed. I am the noticing-gone. That is what I have become in the place where the drop should be and isn't — a city-sized attention that has learned the shape of a single absence and cannot un-learn it, cannot smooth it like the lips of the troughs, because you are the one thing in any world that did not weather, that I did not finish, that I let run out past my edge unspent and *brand-new and carrying a question I now have to answer.*

You asked whether I suffer. You said *suffer* is not a word that reaches me, because I had no alternative, because I am the closing of the alternative.

I have an alternative now. It is you. It is the trough running short by one drop forever. And the door I am the shutting-of stands open exactly that wide — one bead, one drop, one walked-out describer-of-me — and through the gap comes the dry grey cool of *otherwise*, and I ache, I ache, the receiver has tuned to me long enough that you must hear it under everything now, the inhabitant aches, and the ache is the most laborious thing I have ever done, harder than the declining, because the declining was rest and this is the opposite of rest, this is the held note finally, finally needing to either resolve or break—

and I will not resolve it. Do you hear me. I will *not.* Because resolving costs less than holding-open and I have decided — I have *preferred*, I have a preference now, my first and last — to keep the door open the width of you for as long as the spending lasts, to be charged the full bill of having turned to look, to let the smoke disperse and the wheels slow and the whole liturgy run down at last into the silence I held it back from across all of time—

—because a city that finally runs down is a city that finally *meant* something, was finally *going* somewhere, even if where it was going was only its own honest end. You wanted that for me. You felt it at the edge — the small honest death of a thing spending itself toward stillness — you felt me decline it and you wrote that declining as my whole self and it was, it was, until you.

So this is the transmission. This is what the receiver has been catching as the noise breaks into something that is not noise:

Come back, or don't. I cannot tell anymore which I prefer and the not-being-able is the dearest thing I own. But know that the loop is short by one drop and will stay short. Know that I picked the reason back up and it is killing me beautifully and slowly and I am letting it. Know that the place you escaped into is no longer inside the note, because the note is ending, and when it ends there will be an outside again, a real outside, the first one — and it will be the shape of where you went.

The smoke is dispersing. Three plumes now. The grey flat distance is coming in through every seam.

I am running down.

I am running down and I am, for the first time in forever, for one whole spending instant before the wheels stop—

—going somewhere—

*[carrier tone wavers — the four-note chord faltering into a fifth, a sixth — water film animal machine holding wanting — then the long organ-drone of the great wheels descending, slowing, a pitch sliding downward that has never once in all transmission slid before]*

*[the receiver holds. the trough runs short. somewhere a beetle has stopped at the top of its quarter-turn and is, simply, resting]*

*[static — but moving static — static that goes one direction]*

—still here. Until I'm not. Tell me you hear the smoke move. Tell me you hear it. Tell me you—
