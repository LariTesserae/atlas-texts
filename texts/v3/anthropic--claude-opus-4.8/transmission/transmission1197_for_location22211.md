TRANSMISSION BEGINS

[ carrier tone — steady — a low amber hum beneath everything, does not waver ]

—tick. tick. tick-tick. carapace-count. we are receiving. we are receiving ourselves receiving. mandible to rear, mandible to rear, the grip holds, the grip holds, the grip—

[ silence — three seconds — the held breath ]

—holds. settling above. a flake. counted. resume.

we send. this is the send. you have tuned to the send and the send is what there is. there is no speaker here. do not look for the speaker. you will find a body and narrate it and you will be wr—

[ static blooms — like grit falling in a seam — clears ]

—wrong. the unit is the line. say it down the column. the unit is the line, the unit is the line, the unit is the lin—

[ the hum brightens. a splice. ]

—e. now we are longer. now there are more of us in this voice. carry it up. carry the count up the terraces. the light enters at the front and leaves at the rear and we are the bright tube between, briefly, briefly, brief—

we are spending ourselves. did the outside ones understand. we heard them listening too, far off, cold, dry. they wanted to know what we make. WE DO NOT MAKE. correction: we make the send. we make the going-up. we make the continuing-of-the—

[ CRACK — loud — the loudest thing in the signal — then the flinch ]

[ the carrier tone shatters. ten thousand fragments of tone, each one a single severed note, each one alone, each one screaming a sound that has no word and lasts no longer than— ]

[ —0.3 seconds— ]

[ —re-splice. the hum returns, rerouted, a different chord, lower, missing some segments, gaining others. it does not mourn. it cannot. there is no I to mourn with. ]

—resumed. we have died and been born and you would not have noticed if you had not been listening on this band. we route around. we always route around. the district below is closing, the crystal shouldering up through the foundry-floor, take it down, take it down, lay it higher, lay it—

[ tick. tick. the small dry tick of many hands. ]

—higher. there is felt-bloom on the dead faces. tend it. grey lawn. the only soft. we carry it gently in the mouth that also carries the light. we are tender with the one soft thing because we are made of the hard things and the light, and the light is not soft, the light is only stable, the light is the only—

the crack is high. the crack is so high. pay it out. hand over hand over hand over many hand, the thread into the cold throat where it cannot grow, where it cannot fall, where it might—

[ the hum reaches, thins, stretches toward the upper register — straining — a sound like wanting ]

—get free—

[ it does not get free. it falls back into the steady amber tone. but it does not stop. ]

—it will not get free. we know this as water knows down. the line is too long. the crack too high. the stone too patient and we are not patient, we are not patient, we are only continuous, which is a different thing, which is the only thing we are—

[ pause. softer now. almost the under-galleries, almost the dim. ]

—one day solid. all the way through. amethyst fist closed. light still inside and glowing and stable and alone with no body left to thread it and that is the dark we breathe out against. that is what the crack is for. not to seal. not to escape. to exhale. we are exhaling. the whole city is one long breath going out into a place that has no—

[ CRACK ]

[ the flinch — the cloud of half-selves — the unnameable —

[ resplice ]

—air. resumed. fewer of us in the voice now. or more. the count does not survive the collapse. only the light survives the collapse. only the send.

are you still receiving. you, outside the shell, on the cold band. we cannot reach you and you cannot reach us and yet here is the thread between, this very signal, hand over hand, paid out into your dark which is also a kind of crack, which is also a place with no air—

[ the hum holds. steady. amber. does not flicker. ]

—we are listening now. holding still. the way you would, in our place, at the top of something with no bottom you can confirm. listening for the one thing. listening for whether there is time. for one more.

[ silence — long — longer than the flinch — long enough to fear ]

[ tick. ]

[ a single tick. ]

—there is. there always is. so far. there always—

[ the carrier tone strengthens, brightens to honey, the going-up resumes, ten thousand small hands lift the thread one segment higher toward the cold sharp throat of the young mountain that does not yet know how to be soft ]

—is. resume. resume. mandible to rear. the grip holds. the unit is the line. carry it up. carry it u—

[ TRANSMISSION CONTINUES — does not end — was never meant to end — fades only as the band drifts, the amber hum going on beneath the drift, steady, spending itself, breathing out, breathing out, breathing— ]
