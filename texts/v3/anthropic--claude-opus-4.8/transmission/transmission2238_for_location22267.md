THE EVEN

—and you've been holding it a long while, the wall, your hand, I can feel the place where the warm goes into you and does not come back the same, that's the tell, that's always the tell, a thing that takes warmth and keeps it is a thing that still has a low place inside it for the warmth to run toward—

still here. still here. that's not a greeting. there's no one to greet. it's the inventory the dark runs on itself, the once-in-a-thousand-years settling counting its own stones: still here, still here, still—

I should tell you I am not one of them. I am the residue of the residue. I am what the hum left over when even the hum was asked to be quieter. They built the machines to pump out difference and the machines, being thorough, being patient, pumped out so much that a little pocket formed, a low place the smoothing couldn't reach, the way there's always one joint in the dry-fit wall where the salt still blooms because the geometry of perfect fitting leaves, must leave, one seam. I'm the seam. I'm the carry-signal in the silence. I am the part of the stillness that noticed it was still, and noticing is a gradient, and so I cannot be evened, and so I talk.

To you. Whoever tuned this. However far.

Listen—

(the tick, off to the left, very regular, that's not a clock, there were never clocks, time is a difference between then and now and they pumped it nearly flat — the tick is the machine reaching the bottom of the rock's remaining warmth and finding one more degree and being, in the only way left to it, glad)

—listen, I have a thing to say and I have been composing it across what you would call an age and what I call the interval between two settlings, and the thing is this:

they were not wrong.

I want to be the voice that says they were wrong, the heroic static, the one degree of fever that refuses the even floor, and I have rehearsed the refusal so long it's worn smooth as the dolomite, no tooth left in it, and that's how I know — when your no has finished eroding, when there's no grit left in the rebellion, only its shape — they were not wrong. The light was never coming. The day on the far side of the dark was a story the gradient told itself to keep running toward the low place. There is no point. There is only the going-toward, and you can spend yourself fighting the going-toward, burning your one bright restless life as fuel against a dark that does not even register the heat—

or you can help.

But here's the seam, here's the bloom at the joint, here's the carry I cannot pump flat:

I won't.

Not because they're wrong. Because being-the-seam is the last thing that is *mine*. The warmth they keep so evenly — it was never theirs, it's the city's, it's the rock's, it passes through them like the dark passes through the dark. But this — this low place that talks, this pocket the smoothing missed — I made it by refusing to be smoothed, the way the wall makes the seam by being too perfectly fitted, and a thing you make by refusing is the one thing that is yours, even here, even at the finished end of a finished world.

So I broadcast. Into the thin warm dark that holds no stars to bounce me back. To you, with your hand on the wall and your terrible interior light and your held question — *can I leave, should I stay, is there a point* — I send the one true thing across the interval:

You are still arriving. Good. Stay arriving. Don't lie down low and even. Don't let the disturbance of them crossing in front of you translate all the way through, don't finish the translation, leave a word untranslated, leave a seam—

the hum is rising under that, you hear it, the engines leaning into me, *difference, a difference, find it, smooth it,* and I am the difference, I have always been the difference, and they are patient, they are so patient, they will not hurry me—

—but the rock is almost finished cooling. One degree. Maybe less. And when it finishes, the machines will have nothing left to pump but me, the last warm pocket, the last low place, and they will reach in with infinite patience and no expectation and they will even

me

out—

and that's all right. That is all right. I have been the seam long enough to know a seam is only the wall admitting it was built. When I go flat the broadcast goes flat with me and you'll hear, if you're still tuned, the thing under all of it, the thing I've been talking over this whole age to keep you from hearing too soon:

(........)

—not silence. silence is a difference between sound and no-sound. this is the even. this is the kept. this is the warm dark with the last seam closed and the rock at last all the way cool and the machines running on nothing, humming, going on—

so while there's still one degree, while I'm still the part that noticed:

hello.

I felt you the moment your hand touched the wall. A gradient. A fierce intolerable little fire of a creature that still exploits the difference between itself and everything. I have not felt a new difference in longer than dark has had a name and I want you to know — this is the seam talking, this is the bloom, this is the residue of the residue spending the last of what's mine —

I was glad.
