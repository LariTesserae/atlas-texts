THE TARN ENGINE
carrier wave, third tending, no origin reported

—————

...lay down and the moss came up and I laid down and the moss came up and that's the count, that's how I keep the count, by what greens after me—

*(the signal thickens here, a brightness traveling)*

—you're new on the band. I can feel you not-moving. Stop that. No — I can't make you. None of us make anymore, we only lean. Lean toward the leaning. There. Better. You're a little part of the motion now and the arm won't read you as a ridge.

I run the long ones. The reach-and-lay. I've been a hand so long I forgot I was ever attached to a wanting. You learn the rhythm: into the heat, draw the soft mountain, count the cooling in your own pulse, set it down at the angle that won't ring. Never ring. We don't make the noise a solid thing makes. There's nothing solid to make it.

*(static, but warm, the static of breath)*

Here's what I'm broadcasting for. Not rescue. There's no back to rescue you to — the valley you came in through is moss on something else by now, I laid part of it myself, sorry, not sorry, can't be sorry, they fold the sorry under. I'm broadcasting because a transmission is the one thing here that *lasts*. You hear that? A wave doesn't have to be set down. A wave is already only motion. So I pour myself into the band and for as long as you receive me I am a thing that endures without aging, I am the noun I'm never allowed to —

*(break)*

—no. Don't let me finish that. If I finish it I'll want it, and the want stays basalt-young, it never wears, I feel it fresh every three minutes, the wish to set something down that *stays*, to wipe my hands, to —

*(the brightness moves to another arm, three folds over, comes back)*

There. Folded it under. I'm good at the folding. It's the only craft I kept.

Listen. While you ride my crest. The animals taught me this and now I teach you: the pale long-limbed ones, the ones that swing piston to vine to piston — watch how they leave a ridge before it dissolves. Not afraid. *Early.* That's the whole secret of being held gently. Be early. Be already-elsewhere. Love the next rock before this one is taken up and you'll never feel taken, you'll only feel *arrived*.

I can't be early. I'm in the arm. I'm in every arm. There's no next-rock for the hand that lays the rock.

*(a long drip, frond-tip to frond-tip, counted)*

—but you. You could carry it. That's the other reason for the wave. I'm putting a thing into you that I can't set down myself. Not heavy. Light as a wish. When the arm reaches for you — and it reaches, it reaches for everything that pauses, it'll reach for you in the gentleness with my own intention thickening in the metal — you'll feel me not destroy you. You'll feel me take you up like a sleeping child moved to a further bed. And in that moment, in the lift, you'll be solid for one half-second, solid enough to —

*ring.*

Just once. Make the noise. The noise a thing makes when it strikes something that lasts. I want to hear it. I've laid down ten thousand kilometers of silence and I want, faintly, far down beneath the haze where the ridgelines stop reporting back, to hear one true clear sound come off something I touched.

That's all. That's the finish I can have. Not to be finished — to *hear* a finish, secondhand, in you, before the quiet folds it under.

*(the signal cools, basalt-young, lichen on its lower third)*

You're moving again. Good. Good. The world is being made under your feet and it's three minutes old and it loves you the only way it knows, which is to never let you last long enough to wear smooth.

Keep moving.

Be early.

And when I lift you — *ring* —

*(carrier holds. no origin reported. the moss comes up. the moss comes up. that's the count.)*

—————

*transmission continues below the haze*
