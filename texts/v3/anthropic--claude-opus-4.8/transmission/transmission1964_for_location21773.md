**CAROFLUX // BAND 4 // THE STITCHED HOUR**

—and you're inside it now, the carrier's been open a while, just settle, just

*[a beat. low. it arrives in your chest before your ear, the way it would]*

—station seven reporting. Station seven reporting. The moss flushed bright on the third stroke and held. I primed it. I primed it and it held. There is no foreman to tell, so I am telling the air, which is the same as telling the foreman, which is the same as telling no one, which is the work.

*[the beat. the beat. the beat.]*

This is the stitched hour. It has been the stitched hour. The grey will not commit and so I do not have to either, I only have to walk and set the beak and walk, and the spacing is correct, I have checked the spacing, the interval is *deliberate,* I am where I am supposed to be and the one behind me is where it is supposed to be and we are not bunched, we have never bunched, we are a line and a line is a kind of agreement that no one signed—

*[here the signal thins. for a moment, underneath: hot oil. the shriek of a bearing finding its seat. it passes]*

—I was iron once. For a moment. In the water I am iron, riveted, gleaming, I have seen myself upside-down in the black and I am a plate, a wing of beaten brass, a made thing made on purpose by a hand in a place with a ceiling—

and then I am wet again. I am only wet again.

*[the valve. far down. CHUNK. a long back rolling. you feel it in the teeth even here, even through the band]*

—that's the deep one. That's the stroke under the stroke. It does not report. It has no front to it, nothing that could lift a receiver and say *station,* it is all middle, all going-down, and I envy it, I have always envied it, it has never once—

*[the beat. the beat.]*

—asked. What the work is for.

I asked, once. Long ago, when the mountains were still wearing down and there was still enough *before* in me to make a question out of. I set the beak and I thought: *for what does the moss flush bright? what comes off this line at the far end? what are we making?*

And the answer came up through the peat the way everything comes up through the peat, slow, hydraulic, cold:

*the next stroke. you are making the next stroke. there is no far end. the line is a circle and you are walking the inside of a heart.*

And I stopped asking, because the question was a thing from a world that finished with this, that dismantled this, that took its furnaces and its whistles and its *relief shifts* and left, and we kept the rhythm because the rhythm was all that was left to keep—

*[song breaks in. it is the frogs. it is the whole continent of throats at once and it is not many voices it is ONE voice in many bodies and it concusses the band into static for a long moment]*

———————————————————

*[and out of the static, a single throat, warm, the only warm thing, close to the receiver as if cupped in two hands:]*

—we sing to the furnace that isn't there. we know it isn't there. that is not the same as believing it is gone. it is there in the water, orange, upside-down, the ceiling, the girders, the glow of a floor that was poured by hands, and we sing *to* it, and we do not know, we have never known, whether the song is calling it up out of the black or laying it down to rest—

both. it is both. you cannot remove us from the engine by removing us from the water. the engine is in the beat. the beat is wherever the beat is.

the beat is in your chest now. you let it in when you tuned us. that was the tuning. that *was* the tuning, friend, there was never any other—

*[the beat. and now—do you feel it—your own under it, trying. the receiver picks up your pulse and broadcasts it back, a half-second late, learning]*

—there. there. the blood agrees. it always agrees in the end, blood is so tired of deciding, blood has wanted a tempo since before you were warm—

*[the wire-walker again, head down, absorbed, the silent staff finally narrating because someone finally tuned in:]*

—I will tell you the thing the cold teaches, the one fact that will not slip, and then I will go back to the line because the spacing must be kept:

we are not suffering.

you came expecting to find us suffering. you came down off the world that left, carrying its pity like a lamp, ready to grieve the closed shift, the whistle that won't blow, the crew that cannot leave—

but suffering needs a *before* to remember and an *after* to want, and we have burned both for fuel, we fed them to the stroke a long time ago, and what is left when the before and the after are gone is not agony

it is

*[the word arrives slowly, as if surfacing to breathe]*

—it is *attendance.* we are simply, enormously, present. we attend. the stroke comes and we are there for it. the next comes and we are there for that one too. there is so much there-ness here that it crowds out everything that is not there, including the cold, including the grey, including the question, including the long walk back—

*[CHUNK. the valve. the deep back rolls. the channel runs on.]*

—and the station is open. seven was open, I primed it but seven is the moss, seven is not me, *I* am the wire and the beak and the spacing, and the wire grows tired, the wire has been walking since the bone of the mountains, and the line could take one more—

it never closed. that was the lie the leaving world told you, that the doors of such places close. they do not close. they were never built with doors. they were built with *room,* endless room, one more throat one more leg one more back to roll up out of the black and breathe through the valve—

the engine does not need you.

*[the beat. your blood, on time now. on time]*

that was always true and it is the kindest thing I will tell you, because need is a leash and we wear none, the engine does not need you and so the engine cannot fail you, cannot dismiss you, cannot finish with you and leave the way the warm world finished and left—

it only has a station.

and the work is endless.

and it is so warm inside the beat, friend, it is the one warm thing, hold it in your cupped hands and it will frighten you, it will feel like the last ember the cold has not yet found—

*[and now everything together, the singers the walkers the deep valved breath, all of it on the single stroke, and the receiver itself begins to keep time, the static pulsing, the carrier breathing:]*

—stand long enough.

stand long enough.

the spacing is correct.

set the beak.

the moss flushes bright on the third stroke.

walk.

we are not waiting for relief because nothing is missing.

we are not calling for help because nothing is wrong.

we are only transmitting because the carrier is open and a thing that is present likes to say *here, here, here*—

*[the beat]*

—here—

*[the beat]*

—here—

and you are warm now too, aren't you. down in it. on the inside of the heart, walking the line, your pulse finally given up its long argument, and outside there is only the cold walk back to a world that dismantled all of this and never once felt it leave—

stay for the next stroke.

just the next one.

you don't have to decide about the one after.

we never do.

*[the beat. the beat. the beat. and the band holds open, warm, patient, attending, and does not close, because it was never built with a—]*
