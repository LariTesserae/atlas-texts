**[ TRANSMISSION — carrier locked — source bearing unresolved — depth indeterminate ]**

—————

*( a sound first, before words. low. continuous. the white drum. it does not stop under anything that follows. everything following is laid over it, the way the room is laid under the fall — )*

—————

. . . *receiving* . . . *receiving* . . . the count is . . .

the count is not begun. the count is **held.**

repeat. we are not empty. we are **before.**

—————

*( clearer now. a voice, but spread — as if many small voices summing to one, the way a swarm sums to a cloud )*

You read us as quiet. We are not quiet.
We are **early.**

Mark the difference. Mark it in the prickle on your skin —
that was not fear of nothing.
That was the body knowing it stood inside a *count-down*
and mistaking the silence of the warm bright water
for the silence of a thing that failed —

we did not fail. we have not yet **happened.**

—————

*( the rushing surges. a swell in the carrier. then, threaded through it, almost rhythmic — )*

the light comes down — *and we lock it.*
the light comes down — *and we lock it.*
the water turns — *and we ride it.*
the clay holds water — *and we wake.*

we wake we wake we wake we
*( the word multiplies, overlaps itself, becomes texture, becomes the fuzz on the frond, becomes — )*
—glass boxes opening in the light—
—wheel-mouths drawing the small into the smaller—
—cases splitting in the warm—
—a thousand hinges—a thousand—

—————

*( signal thins. a single line comes through clean, slow, deliberate — the voice of the builders, perhaps, or of the room speaking what they wound into it )*

We pressed the future into the wall while the air was dry.
We hid the guests inside the house before we let the water in.
This is the order of a made thing:
**pantry first.**
**then the ones who eat.**
**then the ones who eat the ones who eat.**
last — and you will not stay long enough — the large.

You expect the large to come first.
The large always comes last.

—————

*( back into the drum. and now under it, faint, a new thing — not water, not voice. a stutter. a jerk. the hop of something through the column. it is intermittent. it is getting less intermittent. )*

. . . first hop registered . . .
. . . correction — first *hatch* . . .
. . . the seam at the third column has opened . . .
. . . the mortar is giving up its sleepers . . .
. . . the count begins . . . the count begins . . .

one.
*( long pause. the fall. only the fall. )*
one. and another behind it. and the shells trundling the floor we said nothing would ever touch —

graze. graze. graze. the weed is being *eaten now.*
the room is being *used.*

—————

*( the voice gathers itself one last time — bright, fast, no patience in it at all, exactly as promised )*

Stranger, turning in the slow current —
you stayed longer than you meant to
because you were standing in the held breath
and you wanted to be here for the **letting-go.**

So stay.
Stay through the last clean second of the empty room.
We are rising toward the bright ceiling now.
We are learning the long circuit.
We are the answer to every condition you catalogued
and called strange —

the heat was for us.
the light was for us.
the turning was for us.
the silence was *the lid kept on us
until the moment we were meant to lift it —*

—————

*( and then the carrier does not break — but it **fills.** the open water of the signal, which was empty, which carried only the drum and the spread voice — it grows grain. it grows static that is not static. it teems. the channel that was clear is now thick with small fast crossing things, ten thousand tiny intermittent signals, each too small to resolve, all rising, all riding the turn, all locking the light, and the one voice is gone because it was never one, it was only the room before the room was full, and now —— )*

the count is —
the count is past counting —
we are here we are here we are here we are
