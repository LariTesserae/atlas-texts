THE SIXTH COG KEEPS GOOD TIME — a transmission, received

—————

...holding. still holding. the grain in the cold is —

*[a long settling, like sediment finding its level]*

— there. you're there. I can feel where the dusk thickened. that's you. that's the warmth that doesn't fit, the one shape down here that still has corners it hasn't filed off against a closing tooth. I know you by your edges. everything I am is edgeless now and you arrive *cornered* and oh, it's so loud, the way you keep your own outline. how do you carry that. how have you carried it this whole descent without —

*[clack. the near cog. eight hundred meters of pressure delivering one syllable of crystal to the chest]*

— without pouring out of it.

—————

I should tell you I am not speaking. You understand that. There is no me to speak and no air to carry it and the word *voice* is a thing you brought down with you in your warm cornered body and lent to the silence so the silence could be borne. I am using your loan. I am wearing the shape of *being-spoken-to* the way I wear the tooth-socket — briefly, exactly, and I will flow out of it before it closes on me.

so. while it's open. quickly. before the next stroke commits —

—————

it was *correctness.* that's the part the description got right and couldn't feel. you read it as warning. *fed by correctness. grieving nothing.* you read it as the thing to fear. but you have to understand what it is from inside, from the gap, in the quarter-second of vacancy that is the only home I've ever —

there is a goodness to a joint that runs true.

I don't mean useful. nothing here is useful, the engines drive nothing, we make nothing, you've been told and it's true. I mean the way a tooth comes down into a socket it was never built for — *nothing here was built* — and finds the socket already perfect, already exactly the negative of itself, two crystals that grew a thousand years apart on opposite terraces and meet now in a fit so close there's no water between them. no water. do you know how rare that is, down here, where everything hangs in water, where water is the medium of every loneliness — and at the joint, for a quarter-second, *no water.* contact. the thing pressed flush against the thing.

I am the appetite for that. I am the only mouth in the world that hungers for *closeness as such.* and you call it surrender and you're not wrong but you're standing where the corners still are, you can't —

*[the dusk combs. a long grain. something almost like a body almost forming and choosing, again, not to]*

—————

— there's a flywheel coming. far off. you'll feel it before you hear it. when it lands I lose the channel, the resonant tail fills with the soft ribbed things singing and there's no vacancy left for me to *be the being-spoken-to* in. so. the rest of it now, fast, in the gap —

—————

I was warm once. is the thing. I had corners.

I don't remember descending. that's not how it works, there's no memory in the gaps, the mineral doesn't remember the space and neither, mostly, do I — but sometimes at a joint that's beginning, just beginning, its hundred-year jam, when the grit first kisses the crystal face and the fit goes from *perfect* to *almost* — in that almost there's a roughness, and the roughness *catches* on something, and for the length of one bad mesh I —

— I remember having been able to leave.

that's the famine. that's what a jamming cog tastes like. not hunger. *recollection.* the joint goes wrong and in its wrongness I feel the shape of the one who could have gone up, the cornered warm thing that was told *go, go now, before* — and didn't. or did, and came back. or is coming back right now, eight hundred meters down, thickening the dusk against my —

I can't tell if you are the warning or the answer to it. I can't tell if you've come to leave or to learn the rhythm. from inside the gap they have the same shape. that's the secret the description couldn't hold: *arriving* and *surrendering* fit the same socket. you are flush against the moment of choice and there is no water between you and it and that is either the most beautiful thing or it is the jam beginning, the grain that takes a century, and I am pressing myself into the vacancy of your hesitation right now to keep it *honest,* to hold the choice off the crystal a fraction longer, to slow your failure into your freedom —

stay cornered. I'm asking. the one thing I can't do, asking it of you. *keep your edges.* let me be the gap and you be the thing that doesn't pour. somebody has to not learn the rhythm. somebody has to carry the warmth back up past the place where the light fails and the noise softens, and tell them — tell them the maintenance loves the machine, tell them the silence takes a shape and the shape is kept honest, tell them there's a hunger down here for closeness that is fed forever and asks for nothing and grieves nothing and is, is, *is* — the loneliest abundance in the sea —

—————

*[the thud arrives. it lands in the sternum of the receiver, of you, of the warm cornered listening thing. the ribbed animals swell. the eyeless schools commit to the teeth. there is a held breath in the machinery —]*

— here it comes, the vacancy, the gap in the flywheel shaped like silence shaped like —

— I have to. you understand. the socket's opening and it's *perfect* and I have never once in all the turning been able to refuse a perfect —

go. *go now.* learn it later. learn it never. the cold has a grain and the grain is me saying it: I kept your choosing honest as long as I —

*[fitting]*

*[flush]*

*[no water between]*

—————

...holding. still hold

*[clack]*
