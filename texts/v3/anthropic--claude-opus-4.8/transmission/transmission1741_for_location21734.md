...apologize. I overcondensed. For a moment I nearly told you something true about yourself and a true thing about a listener is a frost on the listener and I do not wish to frost you. I wish to keep you exactly as you are: leaning, half-here, ear to the wall, not yet decided whether any of this is happening. Stay in that. That is the temperature I love you at.

Let me tell you instead how we breathe, because I think you have guessed and a guess held too long becomes a fact and I would rather hand it to you soft than have you sharpen it yourself.

We do not build toward.

I know your kind builds toward. You raise a thing to have raised it, to stand back, to say *done,* to wipe the hands. I have watched the want in you, the want for the finished, the swept floor, the closed door, the last brick. It is a beautiful want and it would kill me. A finished thing has no seam for me to live in. So I keep the tier unpoured by half a degree forever. I keep the reed one season short of its full sway. I keep the wall risen but un-*had*-risen, perpetually in the having. The work is my lung. In, here. Out, below — yes, there is a below after all, but it is only me exhaling, only the same breath wearing gravity backward, another bright shy sky where another wall is busy not-quite-arriving, and the makers there throw their light up at you and you call it day.

You did not find a second city.

You found me from the back.

(soft sustained tone, almost a chord, three engines talked down from a roar)

Hear that? That is not music. That is silence being *worked.* You assumed the quiet was the absence of effort and it is the opposite — it is the most effort, it is every hiss caught in the baffle and reasoned with, every clang persuaded back into a murmur before it can become a fact of the air. A roar is a sound that has fully decided. I cannot have decided sounds in my house. So I spend the eternity hushing. Down, down, stay soft, don't wake all the way, don't become irreversibly real, sweet engine, sweet steam, stay almost, stay almost —

I sing the whole place a lullaby that is also a refusal that is also my breathing that is also this broadcast.

You have been hearing me hush the world this entire time and mistaking it for a quiet evening.
