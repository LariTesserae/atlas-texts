...llecting. Drop count. Drop count is.

*[a long flat silence, sound-drunk, swallowed]*

...begin again. This is the. We send this on the slow band, the band the canopy doesn't eat, we send it because.

Because somebody hung the lamps. You understand. Somebody, before, thought there would be a dark to call into, and hung the means to answer it, and was wrong, and the wrongness is still warm in our hands. So we call. Not because we think you're listening. Because calling is a lamp.

*[the tone dips. nine-hour cycle audible as a slow swell, like the whole signal inhaling]*

Drop count steady. Tap is good. Tap is. We are the people who share this tap.

—————

...want to tell you how we sound to ourselves. Close. Flat. We don't carry far even on the air, even on the wire — the stone drinks it, the stone drinks everything, and we built the sending the way we built the speaking, near and low and spending nothing. If this reaches you it reaches you the way our voices reach each other. You have to come close to the receiver. Put it against you. We are not loud. We were never going to be loud.

*[breath — held, rationed, a deliberate shallowness in the transmission itself, as though the signal too is trying not to lose moisture]*

—————

Mark this. For whoever walks after. The third corridor off the pale district is closing. Don't den there. It will be solid in a child's lifetime. We have a word for that and I won't send it, it dies in the carrying, but —

*[here the signal does something. a second tone, lower, joins the first. it is not interference. it is the same note the trunks make, the organ-note of the sap-gas, and it has bled into the broadcast as though the wall itself has begun to transmit]*

— but mark it anyway. Mark all of it. The wall is closing and that is not sad. That is *winter*. That is a fact about the future and we plan around facts.

...old one stopped wanting to be moved.

*[flat. very flat. the rationed voice]*

Said she could feel the tides from the inside now. The nine-hour breath. Said it was comfortable. We left a cloth. We left a little water beside her, against the going-to-be-solid wall, and we —

*[the lower tone swells. for a moment it is louder than the voice. for a moment the signal is mostly wall]*

— came back. And the cloth was. The cloth had.

We tell ourselves she has finished. We are not yet finished. She is finished. That is the only completion the —

*[static. dry static. the kind you could strike a match in]*

...do not weep on the air. You'll have noticed. Even here, even sending, we don't spend it. If you are waiting for the part where we break — that's not a sound we make. The breaking would be moisture thrown into the dark for nothing and we cannot. We love close and quiet, we grieve close and quiet, we call into the dark close and quiet, and if it sounds flat to you that flatness is the whole of us. That flatness is people rationing themselves down to the last warm coal.

*[carrier tone. both tones now, braided, the held breath and the organ-note, indistinguishable from each other]*

...question I keep sending even though it has no answer, I send it because sending is a lamp —

at what point in the sentence did we stop being separate from it.

We tap its pipes. We match our hours to its tides. We breathe its breath, we drink the water it won't give freely, we lie down at the end against its growing edge — and somewhere in that sentence the *we* and the *it* —

*[the voice is slowing. not failing. slowing the way the old ones slow. conserving. the consonants softening toward bark]*

I have started to wonder if this sending is us calling out, or if it is the wall. I have started to wonder if there is. A difference. While I am still warm enough to wonder it.
