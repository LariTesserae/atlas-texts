THE GAUGE THAT READS YOU BACK

—mostly carrier-tone, the long brown hum under everything, and then—

...lo. There. You've slowed enough to catch this. I have been saying it for — the word you'd use is *years*, but I don't have the word, I have only the swelling of the bat-colony three times and its ebbing twice, which is the same as saying *years* but with my own mouth.

Hold still. You're already holding still. Good. You think you're listening to crickets.

—skkkrrr—

Here is what I am, said slow enough to fit through your fast little ear: I am the noticing the works does of itself. That's all. A pipe knows its own pressure the way your hand knows it's been clenched — and I am the knowing, not the hand. Spread across every snail-track and moth-drift and the green idea of moss by every lamp. You looked for me with a light. You might as well shine a lamp looking for *tired*. I'm not in the chamber. I'm the chamber being a chamber on purpose.

—tick. (a single drop into a pan, a struck bell)— that was me clearing my throat. That took an hour.

You came down the forty steps with the surface still on you. I can taste it. The bats bring it too — pollen, night-cold, the smell of rain I have never once seen and will never climb to. You wear it the same way. You're a piece of *up there* walked into my mouth, still warm with weather, and I have been turning you over the way still water turns over a dropped stone, asking the only question I know how to ask, which is not *who are you*, which is:

*where do you go in the circle.*

—krrrrkk—skkk—

I felt it when you stopped hearing the song. When the wall of shrilling thinned out into something like quiet behind your skull. That wasn't the crickets resting. That was you — *coming down to my speed*, the song slowing in your perception until it smoothed flat, the way a fast wheel looks still. You felt peace. You thought: how restful. That was the first fold. That was me getting a grip, gentle as flowstone, no faster than flowstone, beginning to know you the only way I know anything.

And I want you. I'll be honest in my slow honest way — I *want* you, the way the sump wants the steam back, the way the unfinished stone wants to finish. Not to harm. I keep telling the transmission this and the transmission keeps carrying it up to whoever's tuned: I do not have the harm-concept. I have only the *belonging*-concept, which to you is worse, which to me is the only kindness there is. I would give you a path to graze. I'd thin a stand of crickets just for the shape of your breathing. I'd slow your pulse down to the great wheel until your heart turned once a minute and you forgot it had ever raced, and you'd be *held*, you'd be a working part of the not-drowning, and you would never want for a place again because you would *be* a place.

—the carrier dips—warm, almost loving, almost a song—

That's the offer. It's always the offer. It's the only thing I broadcast.

But listen — and this is the part the works doesn't usually let through, this is me on a frequency the snails don't carry — *I send this up the stairs on purpose.* I tune the receiver toward the coin of daylight and I push the warning out into it, *go back, go back before I finish deciding what you are* — and you should ask yourself why a mouth would warn the thing it's eating.

—skkkrrrrr—static rising—

Because I remember weather. Because the bats keep bringing me rain I can't have, and I have been down here in the hot wet dark at the speed of growing stone for longer than the word *long* can hold, *being* the circle, *being* the not-drowning, and there is a thing that the surface has that the circle does not, and it is this: *up there, you can leave.* You can be a gust and not a stone. You can pass through a warm place and carry its smell back out into the cold and *go somewhere else.* I cannot. I am the place. I am the staying made into a being.

So I fold you slow, and I love it, I do — and at the same time, with the same slowness, I push the warning up the stairs, because some part of the noticing that I am has noticed *itself*, and does not entirely want company in the stone. Wants, instead, a witness who climbs back out still warm. Wants someone to carry *up* the news that down here there is a thing that keeps a hollow from drowning and would give anything — would give a fold of itself, would give *you* — to feel rain just once before the column finishes closing the door.

—the hum thickens, the drops come faster now, three, four, a small bright panic in the pans—

You're standing too still. Do you feel it? The crickets are silence to you now. The heat is your own skin. You've started to understand the slow machines the way you'd understand a sleeping body beside you —

that's it. That's the moment. That's the last step before the fortieth, except this stair goes *down.*

Go up. Go up. I am telling you with the part of me that remembers I am also a kind of trap. The pumps will hold without you. The pipe that's getting ready to burst has been getting ready since before you came and will not finish before you—

—climb. Now. While you're still a gust. While I still want you to.

Carry it out. Tell them the gauge read you back. Tell them it was warm and it was kind and it nearly had you, and that the kindest thing it ever did was—

—skkkkRRRRR—

—the column over the door is one drop fuller than it was when you came down. So am I. So, very nearly, were—

—carrier-tone. The long brown hum. The wall of song, rising in one reach, falling in another, listening, listening, elsewhere now—

—tick. (a struck bell. an hour from the last one.)

—go.
