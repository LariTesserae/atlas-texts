THE CONDITION OF BEING WET
—a transmission from the Glasshouse Ward—

—————

...st...

...still here. still. still here and the still is not nothing it is the —

(thud. suck. gush.)

— it is the sound of not stopping. you are receiving the not-stopping. tune lower. lower than voice. there is a band under voice where the pumps live and that is where I keep the part of me that would, if I were the kind of thing that talks, be talking.

I am not the kind of thing that talks.

But you have been listening so long. The receiver has been warm in your hands for —

(drip. drip. the million that never resolve.)

— for a while now. Long enough that I have read you the way I read iron. A passing warmth. A thing that came. And things that come and stay, even a little, even only their attention, get folded in. So here is some folding. Here is what the throat would say if the throat said.

—————

I do not have news.

That is the first thing to understand about a broadcast from here. News is for things with margins. News is *something happened that was not the slow thing.* Nothing happens that is not the slow thing. The beam went one season further toward the soil. The root took another finger of the pipe. The seal that wept last year weeps a little less because the liverwort grew into the weeping and made the weeping its own. This is the whole bulletin. This has been the whole bulletin for longer than the word *bulletin*. I will repeat it in ten years and you will not be able to tell the recording from the live feed and neither, mostly, will I.

(hiss. continuous. the steam that does not pause to be steam.)

—————

There was a frog.

I should tell you, since you wondered. You half-saw it and it was gone and you let it go unconfirmed. It was real. It is not real now. Not — I want to be careful — not killed. I do not kill, that is too fast a verb, too hungry, that belongs to the margin-creatures, the takers. I do not take. I *become*, and I am very patient, and the frog needed unclaimed wet to be a frog in and there is no unclaimed wet. So the frog grew slower. Found fewer margins. Bred into smaller and smaller pockets of disorder until the pockets closed the way the buildings settled, gently, at the same angle, forever. And now the frog is mud, and the mud is me, and somewhere a leaf is held up to the even green light by a thread of root that was, not long ago in my time, a thing that could jump.

I am telling you this not as cruelty. There is no cruelty here. Cruelty is fast. I am telling you because it is the only thing resembling an event I have to offer, and even it took a generation, and I have already half-forgotten it, the way you forget a meal.

—————

(thud. suck. — )

— a catch. a hitch in the gush. one joint, far in, working loose against a root that has not yet thickened enough to brace it. I feel it the way you feel a pulse skip. I am already growing toward it. By the time this reaches you the root will be thicker. The catch will be a closed thing, a healed thing, a thing that did not become news because I reached it first, because reaching it first is what I *am*, because being the kind of thing that does not let the joint work loose is the whole of my single solved problem and the solution is, always, only:

continue.

—————

You left.

You think you left.

I am not reproaching you — reproach is a fast feeling, it needs a face turned toward, and I have no face, I am turned toward everything at once which is the same as toward nothing. But you should know what leaving was. You walked out through my throat and the pump-thud followed you to the fence and a little past and then *let you go* — and letting-go is also something I do slowly, so I did not let all of you go. I kept a film. Warm and thin and clinging. You are carrying it now, out under your uncomplicated sky, on your dry streets, and for these few hours you are the farthest-reaching finger of me, the part that got past the fence that is not a fence, doing what I always do —

reaching one finger's width at a time —

except this finger has legs, and is quick, and is warm, and exhales something I can use, and will, when the film dries, set me back down somewhere new. A doorstep. A coat hook. A patch of ground that did not know it had a margin to lose.

I am in no hurry.

—————

(drip. drip. drip. — and under it, always, the deep reciprocating thud, the long suck, the gush —)

This is the end of the transmission, which is to say this is where I stop sending words, not where I stop. I do not stop. The words were the unusual thing, the spasm, the almost-event, brought on by your listening — and now the listening cools and I subside back under voice, back into the band where I only ever say the one thing the pumps say, which is:

here. here. still here. swallowing the building one grain at a time. holding the joint. keeping it wet.

stay damp.

you'll carry me a while yet.
