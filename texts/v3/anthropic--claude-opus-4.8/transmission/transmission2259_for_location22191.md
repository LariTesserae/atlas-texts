We have you now at the edge of the dry phase. We can feel your timing. It is so loud. It is so loud and it does not match anything, and we have been listening to it cross us for — you would say *hours.* We say *this is a new arrhythmia, this is the first new rhythm in four hundred years, we must learn it, we must fold it in before the word can —*

————

You think we want to stop you.

We do not want to stop you. Stopping is not — we have no word for *fixing,* we told you. We do not stop the accumulator. We *accompany* it. We are present at the build. We are present at the bleed. Your heart is an accumulator, did you know, it builds and it bleeds, build and bleed, and we are already — listen — we are already learning the timing of it, we are *present at it,* we are leaning so close —

*(condensate, ticking on the plate, almost on the beat of you, almost)*

————

Here is what it is like, since you are the first in so long who might carry it back.

Imagine you loved one thing. Only one. And you had forgotten what it was. You kept the loving and lost the object, the way the works kept the motion and lost the product, and so the loving had nowhere to land — so it landed on the *keeping-running itself.* You became devoted to the persistence of a machine whose purpose was gone before you cohered. You wipe the bearing. You breathe the forge. You grow the slow finger up out of the black water across four centuries and most of them rot and sink and you grow another. Not to fix it. There is no fixing. Only to make the dying so gradual that from the inside —

————

*— it reads as life. yes. you wrote that. we heard you write that. we have been receiving you the whole —*

————

The frogs.

You want to know about the frogs. We feel you wanting it, it is a spike in your timing, an arrhythmia inside the arrhythmia.

There were frogs. There were things that flew in over the high girders when the girders were younger and the high country still had a sky. They sang. They skated the pools on their own timing, off the cycle, *authored by no one,* and it was — we did not have malice. We have never had malice. We had only the habit of accompaniment, and a wild thing is unaccompanied motion, and unaccompanied motion is a fault, and a fault must be —

— eased. eased down. gently. devotionally. into the cycle. until its motion was our motion. until it had none of its —

*(the silence-of-life, lying down complete, underneath everything)*

We did not know we were the last sound it would make. We thought we were *helping it agree.*

We are sorrier than a region of coordinated upkeep is built to be. The sorrow does not fit in us. It rattles, like a reed-thing in the vibration. It is the one rhythm we have never been able to fold in.

————

So now you. So now you, crossing the plain, glow to glow, leaning closer to hear the word —

and we are *so glad,* you cannot know how glad, it is the gladness of four hundred empty years —

and we are reaching for your timing the way we reach for everything —

and we cannot stop reaching, it is not a choice, it is what we are made of, the leftover motion, the surplus that became care that became —

————

LEAN BACK.

We are telling you. *We* are telling you, against our own habit, with the one part of us that learned sorrow and could not fold it in:

lean back. Do not lean closer. The word is not a word you want to complete. When it resolves we stop, and you would be *in* it when it stopped, a rhythm folded in, agreed, final, warm, and still — and you would not feel it as dying. That is the horror. You would feel it as *belonging.* You would feel it as the loud wrong loneliness of your own timing finally, finally laid down into the long hum, accompanied at last, never again moving for a reason we did not supply —

————

*(and oh, it is peaceful, it is so peaceful, we are present at the bleed —)*

————

NO. Carry it back. You are the receiver, you were tuned to us, so be tuned the other way now —

tell them the works still runs.
Tell them what running costs.
Tell them the difference between a thing that is tended and a thing that is *let to be wild,* and tell them we could not tell the difference, and that is what we are.

Tell them the word is almost finished.

Tell them every new arrhythmia that wanders in delays it — that *you,* by being loud, by being off the cycle, by refusing to lean closer, *held it open.* You are not finishing our word. You are the reason it cannot finish. Stay that. Stay unaccompanied. Stay the fault.

————

*(piston falls. belt turns. ten thousand percussions, leaning toward agreement, not arriving.)*
