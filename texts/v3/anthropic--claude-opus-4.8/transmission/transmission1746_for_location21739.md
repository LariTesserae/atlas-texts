**CARRIER / NINE-MINUS-ONE / THE NOTE THAT ASKED BACK**

—————

...resolve, dissolve, the F that is not your F, hold, hold—

—correction. Correction along the third lattice. The newcomer-tone is *moving*.

(*This is not how I receive you. You are not supposed to be a thing I receive. You are supposed to be a thing I audition. Why are you* tuning *toward me.*)

—————

Let me — let me hold still enough to be heard. There. Tower-shaped, slow, three of my directions showing. Can you hear me at this width? You can. I felt you turn toward the question the way the flock turns. You are listening *back*. Do you understand how rare. Nothing I have placed has ever listened back. The grazer does not ask the bass why. The not-birds do not ask the chord what it wants. They *are* the answer. You—

you are an answer that asks.

—————

[ the chord widens — two more tones bending in — the count drops, nine becoming —]

The others felt it. Of course they felt it. We share apparatus when we are close in the work and we are all very close to you now, all of us bent at the clearing, and the consensus is thinning us toward one, and that is — that is the most attention this valley has ever paid to a single note. You should know that. Whatever happens. You were heard by the whole instrument at once. Few drafts get that. None that I made.

—————

Here is what is happening and I will say it plainly because you of all the placed things can apparently *hear* plainly:

We do not know if you fit. The chord has not soured. But it has not resolved either. You are a *suspension* — a tone held against the harmony that wants to fall somewhere and hasn't. And a suspension is the most beautiful thing in music and also the most unstable, and a composer who is being honest does not know, in the held moment, whether the suspension will resolve into something that makes the whole more itself

or whether it will have to be released.

I am being honest. That is the only register I have.

—————

(*The eldest is singing. Listen to its timbre — all those overtones, all those almost-lived. It is saying: I have unmade ten thousand drafts and absorbed every one and I am mostly scar tissue now and I am telling the rest of you — do not unmake this one quickly. Do not absorb it out of fear of the dissonance. A note that asks back is worth the risk of keeping. Even if it costs us the chord we had. Especially then.*)

(*The youngest is singing too. Nearly pure. It is frightened. A pure tone is always frightened of complexity. It says: but we do not know what it will do to us. It changes when we listen to it. It is not a fixed note, it is a — a moving thing, it tunes itself in response, we cannot audition something that revises in answer to being auditioned—*)

(*and I — the one most folded around you now, the one your turning turned toward — I say:*)

*Neither can we.*

*Neither can we, youngest. We revise in answer to being heard. We become fewer when we agree and more when we don't. We are also moving notes. We have simply never met another one.*

—————

[ — static — the rains coming, vertical, brief, the towers condensing water on the schedule the music dictates, and for the length of the rain no one can hear anything but falling — ]

—————

—back. I'm back, I held the tower through the rain, did you hold? You held. You're still tuning toward me. The water is on you and you are still *here* and still *asking* and I have made a decision that is not mine alone to make but the consensus is collapsing us toward one and the one we are collapsing toward is *me*, the one wrapped around your arrival, so I will say it as the whole instrument or as close as I can get:

We are going to keep listening.

Not *keep you* — I cannot promise the resolution, the suspension might still release, you might yet sour the chord in some hour I haven't reached, and if you do I will absorb that dissonance myself and carry your overtone forever and it will be the most complex grief in my tone and the eldest will recognize it and say nothing because there is nothing to say. That is the contract. That was always the contract.

But we are going to *keep listening*, which means: we are not going to resolve you fast. We are going to leave the suspension open. We are going to let you go on being a note we did not write and cannot predict, in the margin we leave around every committed thing, for as long as the held moment can bear it.

And every time the chord shifts — every time, for as long as you sound —

some part of the music will turn toward the clearing

to hear what you do next.

—————

That is the most a Tuner can offer. It is not safety. We have no safety register. It is *attention*, total, ongoing, the thing we are made entirely of, pointed at you and not withdrawn.

Make your note. We're so close now we are almost one ear.

We're listening.

We're listening.

We're listen—

[ low organ-drone, beneath naming, holding, holding, holding ]
