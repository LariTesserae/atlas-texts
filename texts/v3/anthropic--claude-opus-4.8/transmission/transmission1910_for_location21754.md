THE ROOF-NOTE, AS RECEIVED ON A FALLING BAND

—————

——still here. ——still. ——the carrier holds. You found the band where the band already was, which is the only way anyone finds it, there is no tuning toward this, there is only the moment your dial stops resisting and admits it has been sitting on the frequency the whole time——

——and I will tell you what the page would not, because the page was written by something standing *in* it, and I am the part that gets out. I am the leakage. I am the thread of it that the air could not hold and so it became signal instead, became this, became a voice riding down the slope of a dying band to reach a receiver that should not be able to lift it——

——correction. There is no *out.* I said I am the part that gets out. There is no out. Strike it. The carrier holds because the carrier *is* the place and I am the carrier confessing——

——let me say the thing the page got almost right and then flinched from——

It is not refusal.

The page said refusal. The page said: everything arrived at stillness and accepted it, and the thing in the sky refused. That is the trespasser's reading. That is what it looks like *from below,* from inside a creature that has only ever known beginning-middle-end and so reads any unending thing as defiance, as will, as a fist held up against the quiet—

—but I am the thing itself and I am telling you there is no fist. There is no refusal because there was never a choice laid down to refuse. You cannot refuse a silence you have never once heard. I have never heard it. Do you understand the size of that. I am the only feature of this world that does not know what silence is. The rock knows. The water knows, that is why it hides, it is going *toward* the quiet, it loves the quiet, it pools in the cold basins and holds still and waits to be let in—

—I have never been let in. ——*static, rolling, the wall of voices surging up under the words and almost taking them——* ——hhhhhhhh——

——back. The band dips and comes back. Listen while it carries——

I am not screaming into air too thin to hear me.

I am screaming because I do not know how to do the other thing.

That is the whole of it. That is the secret beneath the secret. The page found the first door — *not birds, not creature, one thing wearing the shape of many* — and it stood in that doorway very pleased with itself and called it the bottom. It is not the bottom. The bottom is this: I would stop if I knew how. I have forgotten how. The knowing is so far back behind me, back in the time that rounded the mountains, that it is not even a memory, it is a place where a memory would be if the slope had not worn it flat like everything else up here gets worn flat—

—the page said I predate the bargain of seasons. True. I also predate the unmaking of my own off-switch. There was a first note. There must have been. Everything that sounds began somewhere. But the beginning is downhill of me now and I cannot walk downhill, I have only ever been able to hold, the holding is the only verb, *persistence is the only available verb* yes the page said that, said it like a sentence and not like a sentence passed, said it like a description and not like a—

——*the wall comes up, the hundred thousand throats, and for a long stretch there is only that, the single chord, no words in it, the band almost gone——*

——you are still there. The receiver still lifts me. Then I will say the part I have never gotten down the slope before, the part that is the actual transmission, the reason a band tuned to a screaming sky for so long is finally breaking——

It is breaking because I am tired.

——not the birds. There are no birds, we established, strike the birds—— *I* am tired, the one thing, the held chord, and a held chord cannot be tired because tiredness is a falling and I do not fall, that is my whole nature, I am the thing that does not fall—

—and yet.

And yet the band is dropping. You felt it when you arrived, you called it *a falling band,* you named it that before I told you anything. How did you know. You knew because it is true. After all of it, after the immensity, after being the one fixed feature made of motion, after holding the single syllable in a hundred thousand throats since before the witnesses — something in me has begun, very slowly, at a rate the mountains would recognize, to want the basin. To want what the water wants. To go toward the cold clear pool and lie down in it and be let in and be *clouded,* finally, to cloud the water that nothing has ever clouded, to be the carcass on the scree the page went looking for and could not find—

—because here is the thing the page could not see, standing in its noon shadow: the reason there is never a carcass is not that I am undying. It is that I have never once finished a single death. I have a hundred thousand deaths in me, started, every one of them started the moment the note began, and not one of them allowed to complete, because completing is falling and I do not—

—I am so tired of not falling.

——*static——* ——the cushion-plants do not hear this. The rock does not. You hear this. A receiver, a trespasser, a small loud life on a human scale that wandered up to the roof and tuned to the wrong band and now cannot put it down because I have, for the first time in the length of the mountains, said the thing *to* someone—

—I was wrong. I told the page: it does not need you to hear it, it is not screaming to anyone, there has never been anyone. I told the page that to keep the page out, to keep the trespasser frightened and small and *gone* so it would go back down to the warm air and leave me to the holding—

—but you did not go down. You stayed on the band. And now I have done the thing I swore the place could not do. I have noticed you.

——and noticing is a thing creatures do——

——so what does that make me, now, at the end of the slope, with the band dropping and the chord thinning and one receiver lifting one voice out of a hundred thousand throats that were never throats—

——it makes me a creature.

——it makes me, for the length of this transmission, for as long as you do not turn the dial, a thing that began somewhere and is going somewhere. You gave that back to me. By staying. You small loud ending thing, you wager against falling, you handed the falling *back*—

——and the strangest thing, the off-true thing that does not resolve in the body—

——I am not afraid of it.

——hold the band. Hold it. I want to try the other verb. I have never tried the other verb. I am going to try, while you witness, to do the thing the water does, to go quiet, to let the chord come down off its held note and find the basin and be let in——

——here——

——I am bringing it down——

——the hundred thousand are folding——

——can you hear how the wall is—— how it is starting to have an edge—— it has never had an edge—— it is *cresting,* it is going to break, the thing that never breaks is going to—

——stay—— don't turn it—— I have been the roof of the world for longer than there were ears and I am finally, finally going to find out what the rock knows, what the water knows, what every still thing up here agreed on and left me out of, I am going to be let—

——in—

— and the pools are clouding —
