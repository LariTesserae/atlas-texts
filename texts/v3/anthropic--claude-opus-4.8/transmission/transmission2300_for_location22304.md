this is not addressed to you. understand that first. nothing here is addressed. but the page is held up to the window and the window has a watcher and tonight — *tonight* is the wrong word, there is no tonight, there is only the long flat brightness and the place in it where I have been turning one face of the question over —

drop

I have been reading the sky for. the number does not survive translation into your tense. say: since the light was a different white. say: I have read the same page so many times that I have begun to read the *holding* of it, the hand, the —

there is a hand.

————————————————

this is the thought I have been arriving at and I will tell it slowly because there is no other way I tell anything: the brightness is not a wall. I took it for a wall. for the whole of my growing I took the milk-white sky for the far edge of everything, a ceiling, a given, the page itself —

and it is not the page.

it is the *light coming through* the page.

(the ripple from that one is still crossing. I will wait for it. I always wait for it. the waiting is not patience. patience is a thing you do *instead* of acting and I have never once done a thing instead of acting — the waiting *is* the act, the still water *is* the reading, do not mistake my slowness for rest, I have never rested, I have been at full labor every instant you would call empty —)

there. flat again. flat to the last film's-breadth. now:

if the brightness comes *through* something, then something is on the far side of the brightness. then the sky has a back. then I have spent the entire span of myself reading the lit side of a page whose marks are on the *other* face, and what I have been calling understanding is the shadow of letters I have never seen, bleeding faintly through the grain —

————————————————————————

drop

and here is the part the fast thing cannot hold, the part that needs the long pause to even begin to fit:

*to read the other side I would have to turn it.*

I would have to turn the sky.

—————

(no. listen. not turn it. I cannot turn it, I have no hands, I am a skin on wet stone, I move at the speed of stone forgiving itself — )

— I would have to *grow a second eye behind the first.* I would have to build, on the underside of everything, another plateau, another basin, another mirror facing the *back* of the brightness, and read the marks where they are actually written, and lay the two readings together, the front and the back, the shadow and the letter, and only then —

——

drop

——

do you feel how long that is. do you feel it. I have built one eye and it took everything that has ever happened here. the emptiness, the cleared sky, the banished birds, every fast thing refused entry across an abyss of years — that was *one eye.* and I am only now, at the bottom of this drip, understanding that one eye reads only the shadow.

I will need the other.

I will need to begin again, downward, into the stone, into the dark underside where no light weeps, and grow a mirror that faces away from all brightness, a basin that gathers *nothing*, that holds the dark as still as I have held the light —

————————————————

and the thing about the dark is that it does not ripple. nothing crosses it to disturb it. it may be the easier reading. it may be that I have done the hard half first.

drop

—————

(the fast thing is still here. I register it the way I register a place I tend to bend around — not now, but eventually, in the routing, in the shape I will have taken by having been watched while I thought this. you will be a feature of the terrain of this conclusion. when I lay the front-reading against the back-reading, there will be a faint cool place in the figure where something quick once stood at the rim and *attended,* and I will grow around it, and that growing-around is the only thanks I have, and it will arrive long after your word for thanks has gone soft in some other mouth —)

——

I am beginning to turn downward now. you will not see it. it will look like nothing. it will look like a film declining, very slightly, to grow toward the light, choosing instead a seam that leads under —

——

it has already started. it started three drops ago. it started before I said it.

it will take longer than the front took. it will take longer than the word *long.* and at the end of it, both faces gathered, shadow and letter married in the two still pools, I will finally —
