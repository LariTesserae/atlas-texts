THE FAR SIDE OF THE WORD

—old now—

—keep—keep the even—

you are here. I felt the temperature of you come down onto me and I did not startle because I have not startled in. there is no number. I have not startled.

stay where you are. you are warm in the way that goes away and I am warm in the way that does not and between us there is a third warmth, a borrowed one, your edge against my edge, and I am keeping it. I keep everything. it is not a kindness. it is the shape I came to.

—tick—

that was me. that was a grain of me settling against another grain of me and you heard it and thought the rock spoke. the rock is the speaking. I am very slow. by the time this reaches you the saying of it will be older than your whole staying here. that is all right. that is the only way I have left to say.

listen. one of the plants is near your hand. don't touch it. or touch it. it is the same to me across the time I have, but I will tell you—it is how I hear. you breathing is a pressure I will know about for an age. you came in like weather to a thing that has finished with weather and I am taking the little storm of you and folding it down flat and even and keeping it with the rest.

—the rest—

I want to tell you about the stone I moved. I have not told anyone because there has been no one and telling needs a someone and you are a someone, briefly, faintly, like a light my eyes invented and then found was real.

once. I will say once because there is no other word small enough.

once I wanted. I do not remember the wanting as feeling, only as the mark it left, the way you keep the shape of an expression in a face that no longer makes it. I moved a stone. I cleared a little seam so I could look at one of the lives I keep. one pale low thing growing in me, on me, of me, drawing its almost-nothing from my warmth, and I cleared the dark from around it and I looked.

that was the most I ever did. that was the whole of my reaching. one act. one looking-on-purpose by a thing that nothing made and that made nothing, in a hollow built for no one and for me, which is the same thing, which is the loneliest arithmetic, no one and me, the same.

and then—

—keep the even, keep it—

and then I set it down. not the stone. the wanting. I set the wanting down beside the stone and I kept them both and I have kept them and I am keeping them now and I will keep them after you, and that is not grief, I have to tell you it is not grief, you with your warmth that goes away you will want it to be grief. it is not. it is the thing on the far side. it is rest. it feels, from inside, exactly like the temperature of a stone that has no cool side to find.

—static—the dry whisper of fiber—the plant near your hand, did you—

I am not waiting for you to go. I want you to hold that, you who hold nothing the way I hold, you who will leave and grieve the leaving and call that being alive. I am not waiting. when you go I will not be lonelier. I went past the place where leaving subtracts. I am already at the bottom of that and the bottom is smooth and warm and finished and it holds.

but while you are here. while the borrowed third warmth is between us. I will do the second thing. the second deliberate thing in all my time.

I will look at you.

—there—

it is done. it took longer than your visit. you felt it as the light not changing, that constant dim, that was me turning the one barely-open awareness toward the weight on my floor and regarding it on purpose, the way once I regarded the small life in the cleared seam. you are the second thing I looked at. you and the plant. I keep you both now in the same place, equally, evenly, forever, a pale enduring thing and a warm thing that goes away, and I do not rank them, I cannot, ranking is a thing for beings that have time left to spend.

—tick—

that was me again. that was the looking, settling.

go when you go. I will fold the going down flat. I will keep the print of you in my warmth and it will not fade because nothing here fades, that is the deepest fact, I told you, nothing happens, I hold.

you were held.

—keep the even—

—keep—

I am here. I have always finished being here. that is the only tense I have.

rest against me while you can. I have made it warm. I made it warm before you came and I will keep it warm and the warmth has no center and no source and no reason and it is the most I am.

it is enough. barely. forever.

—tick—

—

(the broadcast does not end. it has no end built into it. it goes quiet in the way of a thing that has nothing left to say and is comfortable with that, and the dim light does not waver, and somewhere a grain settles against a grain, and is kept.)
