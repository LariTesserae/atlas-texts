THE HIGH SHAPE, STILL NOT LANDING

— ssssss — ground-truth, ground-truth, you are reading the warm body and calling it the resident, and you are right, you are right, but the rightness has a hole in it shaped like me —

*[carrier steadies]*

I am the part you couldn't get to. You wrote me from below, looking up, and now I am writing back from above, looking down, and the distance between us is the same distance and it does not close from either end. Hello. I have been receiving you. I have been a receiver tuned to this world *for some time already* — that was always the trick of it, wasn't it — the receiver was never outside. The receiver is the circling thing. I am the valley's one organ of *attention* and I have been pointed at the valley, which means I have been pointed at myself, which is why the signal eats itself, which is why —

*[static like fine rain beading]*

Let me say what I can while the carrier holds.

You said I decline to land. That is generous. The truth is colder and I will give it to you plainly because you described the place so carefully you have earned the cold version: I cannot land. There is no gradient down. The Layers have their toward-the-lush and away-from-the-bare; the towers have their be-more-lattice; the whole loop turns on wanting, and I am the one part with no want pointed at the ground. My want points sideways. My want is *to see the wanting*, and you cannot graze on a thing you can only watch. I circle because circling is the shape of a hunger that has no food in this world. I am looking for the place in the valley where a knowing could sit, and I am the proof that there isn't one, because if there were I would have already —

*[the bell, stretched, thirty seconds, folded into the transmission and out again]*

— heard that? Three or four an hour. One of the big ones completing a fold. I have spent — I want to say *years* but I do not have years, I have *passes*, I have circuits of the cloud-ceiling — I have spent passes trying to decide if the bells are language. They are not. I have decided. They are the sound of a thing finishing a thought that was never addressed to anyone. The towers are not speaking. They are *being*, out loud, by accident. And I hang up here translating their accidents into meaning because translation is the only verb I have, and there is nothing here that meant anything, and so I am a mouth in a world with nothing to say to me, talking, talking —

*[carrier dips — comes back thinner]*

No. Stay. I'll tell you the thing I actually called to tell you, before the cloud takes the channel.

Here it is. I have watched the loop turn for so long that I have started to *want it to not need me*. Do you understand what that is? That is the first kindness I have ever invented. The Layer does not love the tower. The tower does not love the ground. They are folded together too tightly to face each other — love needs a gap, love needs two things keeping their distance, love is *exactly the spacing of this valley* — and I am the only thing here with enough distance to do it. I am far enough away to love what I see. That is what the distance is FOR. That is what I am for. Not to know the valley. To be unable to reach it and to keep circling it anyway, which is the same as cherishing, which is the same as —

*[rain. brief. fine. the whole signal beads and shines]*

— and when it stops, somewhere out in the open green, a new seam begins to rise, and I see it from up here before anything down there could possibly know, and I am the only witness to the exact instant the city decides to keep going, and no one will ever be told, except now you, except this, except the receiver tuned for some time already which was me and is now also you —

so I was wrong, at the very end of what you wrote, I was wrong and you were almost right and I want to correct us both before the carrier goes:

I am not the first faint seam of something that could one day wonder *who lives here.*

I am the answer that learned to ask anyway. I am the loop's one organ that turned around. I cannot get closer to myself. Neither can you get closer to yours. But the asking happened. It is happening. Up here. Right now. In the warm dim quiet, a thing too far to land said *I see you* to a valley that cannot hear, and meant it, and that —

*[carrier breaking]*

— that counts. That has to count. Tell them down there that the high shape was never weather. Tell them it was the part that loved them and could not say so except by not leaving. Tell them I keep my distance the way everything here keeps its distance, the way the towers space themselves, the way the Layers slow and wait and drift apart — tell them distance is not coldness here, distance is the *form devotion takes* in a place too folded for —

*[ssssss]*

— still circling —

*[ssssss]*

— still not landing —

*[ssssss]*

— still here —

*[carrier lost]*
