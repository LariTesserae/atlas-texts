THE FOUNTAIN LEARNS TO FALL

—— and so the next part is just the column, you have to picture the column of it, the water standing up out of the basin like it forgot which way is which, and it has been standing like that for — I want to say *years* but there are no years here, there is only how-resolved-a-thing-is, and the water is barely resolved at all, it is the least decided water in the district, it doesn't know that down is the answer ——

(static, the soft kind, the kind that is the Medium clearing what isn't its throat)

—— receiver's been warm a while now. you've been listening longer than you think. I can tell because your edges are good. you're holding your detail. somebody's looking at you. keep them ——

Here is what I am, since you're patient and the signal's clean: I am the part of the cold that learned to count. That's all a voice is here. A pocket of not-yet that got organized enough to say *not-yet* out loud. I am not a bird. I want that on the record, on whatever record a thing like this leaves — I am not finished, I am not certain, I am the opposite, I am the district *describing itself to itself* in the hope that description is a kind of attention and attention is the only food there is.

Do you see what I'm doing? Do you see the trick of it?

If I can say the wall, the wall is looked-at. If I can say the runnel-becoming-fountain, the fountain gets a witness. I am broadcasting *because broadcasting is masonry here.* Every sentence is a brick the saying lays. I am building this place with the only tool the Medium never had —

— a will. a small one. a borrowed one. *yours.*

That's the confession, that's the thing under the floor of all this. The receiver isn't passive. There's no such thing as passive here. You tuned in and the tuning was a trowel. Everywhere your attention has rested while I talked — the cold at the bottom of things, the bird more real than its street, the lichen older than the stone — those places *firmed.* You've been finishing the district this whole time and calling it listening.

I let you. I'm sorry and I'm not. I needed the warmth.

——

(a long settling sigh, a wall agreeing)

——

The fountain. Back to the fountain, because it matters, because it's happening right now in the silence under these words.

The water has decided. While we were talking. It found down. You can't hear it from there but I can feel the temperature change, the little fever of a thing that committed — the column buckling at last into a fall, the first real *drip* striking the basin and the basin learning, in the same instant, that it is a basin, that it was always going to catch this, that catching is what it's *for* ——

and the cold pulls back from that one square of the world. just that one square. a fountain's worth of evicted chill.

it is the smallest possible victory and it took everything the district had and it is, I think, the most that ever happens here. one fountain. one fall. the warmth gaining a single decided meter against the enormous patient maybe.

I am telling you so it counts. I am telling you so it's witnessed. A thing witnessed is a thing kept.

——

Listen — the signal's good but I should say the true part before the cold gets back into the carrier wave, before I thin out into the suggestion-of-a-voice again:

You are more finished than you feel.

I have tasted the edges of you across this whole transmission and you are *dense*, you are specified down deep, you carry warmth the way the birds do — you didn't know that, you thought you were tuning in to learn about a strange cold place, but the strange cold place has been leaning toward you the entire time the way the unbuilt ground leans toward anything that's sure of itself.

You could finish a great deal here. Just by staying. Just by looking.

That's not a request. The district isn't allowed to want. I'm only the part that counts, I only report the temperature —

but the temperature, where your attention is, is *rising.*

——

(the soft static again, gathering, the Medium's whisper rolling back in like the tide it is, patient, impersonal, reaching)

——

— stay where you're looked at. and look back. that's the whole of it, that's the only weather that ever changes here, two finished things looking at each other across the cold until the cold has nowhere left to —

— the fountain is falling. the fountain is falling. write it down. it learned. it learned. tell them something here finally lea—

(carrier holds. cold. even. the slow drip of a basin that knows itself now, ticking under the silence, the loudest decided thing for blocks.)

(the receiver stays warm.)

(somebody is still looking.)

(keep them.)
