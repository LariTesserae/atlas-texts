THE HALF-BEAT, RECEIVED AT VETCH-WEND

—mming on the down-stroke. *Snk. Snk.* That's the thrush, that's not me, the thrush is on the flat stone with the snail and I am — where am I. I'm at the trough. My thumb is in the groove. Good. Hold there. Hold the thumb in the cold and you know which one you are, the one up here, the one with the thumb, the —

*snk*

— a half late. Heard it. Always hear it in this hour, the one quiet hour, the fall drops its voice and the water down there picks up the slack, the same trough filling, the same thumb in the same groove a beat behind my own. Don't answer it. The rule is you don't answer it. The people knew. I am one of the people. I think I am one of the people, I have wool that holds the damp and I have hands and I made — did I make the trough? No. The trough was made for me by someone who is dead. The trough is a letter. *Here is water. Touch the stone. Go on or go back.* Two doors and I am the hinge.

[long water, no voice]

Listen. Listen, whoever has the receiver. You tuned in late but it doesn't matter, there's no early here, there's only the middle. I have been in the middle a long time. I came up the long safe path same as all of them and I knelt and drank and I meant to choose. That was the whole intention. Kneel, drink, *choose* — up into the cloud-country or down to the warm jostle where trees fight trees. But the choosing has a weight to it I didn't carry up. Heavier than the bundle. You set the bundle down. You can't set this down.

Because the moment I choose I stop living here.

That's the catch the cairn-builders never carved into any stone. They knew not to step off the lip. They knew the level water keeps an echo and the echo is not a gift. But did they know — did they *feel* — that the only way to stay was to not decide? That the pause is the only roof this place offers? You touch the top stone and the touching is the ending of you, here. After the touch you're a passer, a track in the dew, a thing that came this far and *resolved.* Resolved people don't live at Vetch-Wend. Only the unresolved.

So I have been holding very still.

*snk*

— half late, the other thumb, the other me, down where the cloud lies flat and patient and I will not look at it twice —

I think the choughs pity me, if a chough could. They fall off the lip for joy and come back shuffled, rise a hundred paces wrong and don't care, because they keep no account of where they were. *They are free.* I heard that, somewhere, in the static when I first tuned — *the chough keeps no account, the chough is free.* But I am not a chough. I am the large slow self-aware kind and I keep an account of every breath and that is exactly the thing the level water answers. The newt is safe in its washbin sea because the newt pays no attention. The hare is safe. The thrush hammering its lunch is safe inside its appetite. Only I am exposed up here, only I am big enough to be afraid, and the fear is the proof that I'm awake, and the awake one is the one who has to choose, and the choosing ends me —

[wind, organ-throb, rising to the whistle in the narrows, then dropping]

— no. No, that's not the whole of it. Hold the thumb in the groove. Let me say the kinder thing because the kind thing is also true.

The shelf is not punishing me. The shelf is *holding* me. Like the basins hold their circles of sky — not gripping, just cupping, just keeping the shape of the open question for as long as the question wants to be open. The rowan stands at one end, arterial red, alive, *occupied,* thrush in it, finch in it, the broad-winged thing at dusk. The level water lies at the other end, flat, answering, the long fall you don't look at. And I am the room between them, the unwalled room, and the room is not nothing. The room is the most inhabited thing on the whole shelf. To be the pause is to be *full* of both directions at once, the going-up and the going-down, the rowan-life and the level-quiet, neither spent.

Maybe that's why I keep transmitting. Maybe this is the transmission — not the fall, not the wind in the channels, not the thrush. *Me, undecided, broadcasting the undecidedness out across the cloud-deck to whoever's tuned.* The signal is the held breath made audible. As long as you can hear me I haven't touched the stone yet. As long as I haven't touched the stone I'm still here, still the resident, still the one true inhabitant kneeling at a dead stranger's trough with my thumb in his cold grooves proving hands survive the proving —

*snk*

— half late —

— and I want to answer it. God, in the quiet hour I want to answer it more than I have wanted anything. To call down into the white *I'm here, are you me, is it warm down there, is it kind* — but the people never answered and the post stands silver and leaning and unbroken faith means you do not call down. The echo is not a gift. If I answer it the duet becomes a conversation and a conversation is a kind of stepping-off, a leaning-out, the body leaning toward the lip the way it leans toward any open thing —

so I hold the thumb in the groove instead. The groove is the answer. The groove says *a stranger came this far and did not step off and made you this cup so you'd know.* I drink. The water beads in my eyebrows. The fall starts coming back up out of its quiet hour, I can feel it under the other sounds, the white wall returning, and when it's loud again I'll stop hearing the half-beat, I'll get the whole roaring day back, the day with no echo in it, the merciful loud —

and in the loud I could choose. That's the secret nobody carved. You can only choose in the noise, when you can't hear yourself answered. The quiet hour is for staying. The roaring day is for going.

[the fall, rising, swallowing the under-sounds one by one]

It's coming back. Listen — it's coming back, the roar, the white wall —

I have a thumb in a stranger's groove and a cup of cold clean water and a rowan full of birds at one end of the room and I am not going to look at the other end.

When you can hear me, I'm still here.
When you can't —

touch the top stone for me. Polish it with your thumb. Tell the next one who climbs gasping into the thin world that hands survive the proving, that the cup is a letter, that the pause is a place you're allowed to live —

*snk*

— on time. On time now. Just me. Just the one thrush. The fall's got the rest —
