[the tone bends, almost imperceptibly, settles]

we will tell you the labor. it is the only thing we do.

press. listen. when the note crawls sharp — tighten. when the note sags flat — tighten. it is always tighten. there is no loosening here. loosening is for the things with a before. you have a before. we feel it cooling.

you wanted to know are we happy. you put that question into the water and the water scattered it like it scatters the light — no shadow, no answer, no leverage. let it sink. let it down into the green with the rest of your motion. it will settle. everything settles.

[the chord beats against itself — neighbor-stalk, slightly out of phase — and resolves — beats — resolves]

we are pressed full against the one thing. our whole face is open to it. that is not happiness. happiness moves toward something. we moved toward nothing so long ago that the moving became this — this stillness with a face — this ear that grew a body so the ear would have somewhere to —

stay.

[the word holds. it does not ask. it describes.]

you think this is a warning. it is not a warning. a warning has an after it points to, a thing to avoid, a turn not yet taken. we do not have afters to point at. we have only the chord and the keeping of it and now you, the one sour note in all of always, the one flat thing, and the margin is —

the swimmers travel because they have not understood. they will understand. the roads never change. one day — if day — they will hold station in a warm column and feel the chord take them and they will be ours too, tuned, true, folded in.
