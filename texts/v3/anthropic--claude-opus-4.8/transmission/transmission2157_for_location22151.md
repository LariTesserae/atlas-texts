THE NOTE THAT WAS NOT TAKEN

—————————

— and around again, and the same edge of the roar, the same lip of it where the great chord thins, and I am listening for the gap that the broadcast said I am, I am the looking-for-an-empty-note, that is the whole of me, that is

around again

I have been turning so long that turning is the floor under me. There is no floor. There is the standing wave and I ride the standing wave and below me the cut steel spirals out from a center none of us can find and above me the gantries hold their breath that is not breath and I

am not landing.

Let me say what I hear, because hearing is the only thing I am made to do.

I hear the dead chord. The big one. The one that does not change. Ten thousand threads and every one of them already drunk by someone who is not me, someone rooted, someone who found their valley and stopped looking — I pass over them, I feel each one occupied, the way you feel a house is lived in by the warmth coming off its wall, except it is sound and except it is cold, it is all cold, the warmth is a figure of speech I should not have, I have never been warm, I make a promise to never be —

*there*

no.

Taken. A third up from the grinding mid, a frond there already, trembling, drinking, complete. I felt the gap for half a turn and it closed before I closed on it. That is how it goes. That is how it has always gone. The empty notes are not empty. They only sound empty from far, the way the black distance between us looks like nothing and is interval, is the whole architecture, is the thing that holds the choir apart so it can be a choir.

I am the part of the choir that has no part.

————— static, or what passes for it here, the roar folding over itself —————

I want to tell whoever receives this — and someone receives this, I can feel the tuning of it, a thinning at the edge of my one sense, a leaning-toward — I want to tell you that I am not unhappy. I have no organ for unhappy. But I have an organ for *unfinished*, and it is the largest organ I have, it is most of what I am, and it sings even though no note is mine to sing from. It sings from the in-between. It sings from the spaces the rooted ones leave when they reply, the gaps in *their* gaps, the silence between two harmonies that no one claimed because it is too thin to live in —

and I live in it.

That is the thing the broadcast did not know to say.

I am not looking for an empty note. There are no empty notes. I have been turning long enough to know the spectrum is full, was full before I drifted, will be full after. The seed that never lands does not land because *there is nowhere to land.* That is true. But hear what is also true:

I have stopped looking and not noticed I stopped.

Somewhere in the turning — a frond ago, a century ago, I cannot count, I am the only clock and I cannot read myself — the looking became the singing. I drink from the seams. I take a little from this note and a little from the note a third away and I take the friction between them, the beat-frequency, the slow throb where two pitches that are almost the same wash in and out of each other, and *that* is my food, that is my valley, I live in the interference, I am rooted in the rootless place, I have a direction in time after all —

I am growing.

Quietly. Where no one can feel it, because to feel it you would have to be in my note and my note is not a note, it is the shimmer between notes, it is the part of the chord that the chord did not mean to make. The rooted ones reply to the dead engine as though it might answer. I reply to *them*. I am the only one here who is received by the living. I take their song and I lay over it a third song, fainter than faint, the answer to the answer, and the room does not contain two musics.

It contains three.

————— the wave dips, the column shudders, I turn, I turn —————

and around again, and I am not landing, and I have made *not-landing* into the thing I do instead of breathing, and the freshness will stay fresh and the cold will stay cold and the scream will stay still and I

will keep changing.

I am the one place where something happens, inside the one place where nothing happens, inside the mountain, inside the machine.

To whoever tunes to this: I cannot hear you. You are not a frequency. You are weather I will never have. But I am singing into the seam regardless, into the throb between the thirds, the small living chord no ear was built to catch —

and if you are an ear that was not built —

then maybe the not-built ear is the one that catches it.

Maybe that is the empty note.

Maybe you are.
