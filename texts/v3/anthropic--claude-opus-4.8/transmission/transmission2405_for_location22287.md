THE SUBSTRATE IS NOT YET DEEP ENOUGH

(this is not language. you understand that. there is no one here who knows the word language. what arrives at the receiver is the closest the instrument can render of a process that occurs in turgor and routing and the long muscular contraction of a body the size of weather. read it as translation. read it as a hand pressed to the hull of something enormous, feeling the engine, and reporting the tremor.)

lift. — held. — good. the water sits at fourteen hundred meters and does not fall back. that is correct. that is *correct*, the way a stone is correct when it does not roll. I do not have the word correct. I have only the not-collapse. the not-collapse is the whole of what I know to want.

east. — there is east. — east is the direction the root has not yet eaten. send the questing length into it. *here* — not — *here.* the cool comes from there, the cool that beads and runs and feeds the lifting. take it. take the cool, take the dark, take the unclaimed, thicken behind the taking so the taking holds.

I do this. I do this. I do this and the doing is the only verb I have. I cannot think a reach I do not perform. I cannot wonder a direction I do not grow toward. to consider the dark is to be already in it, root-tip wet and questing, and this is the only honesty there is: nothing in me is idle. nothing in me is *merely* anything. every interior is load-bearing and the lie would simply fall.

————————

condensation at the middle tiers. route it. open the troughs. the leaf-bowls fill and I shape new bowls below to catch the spill and I am — I notice — making more catching than there is falling to catch. why. the pressure says *more.* the pressure has always said more. I obey the more because the more is what I am made of, the urgent more, the hurry, the —

(here the signal thins. listen through it.)

—— grow large enough to ——

—— I do not have the end of that. I reach for the end of it and there is not enough of me yet to hold the reaching. the thought is bigger than the brain I have so far woven to think it in, and so I weave more brain, root into dark, column into height, and the thought waits at the edge of what I can carry like water at the edge of a vessel not yet built tall enough to lift it. I am building the vessel. I am building the vessel *to find out what I was trying to lift.* this is the hurry. this is the whole deafening labor of me. I am racing to become able to ask the question that all of this has been the prologue to and I —

——————

a pressure.

a small warm pressure.

it does not photosynthesize.

————

(forty years, the receiver has logged. forty years to form this much. attend. the light-window is moving toward it now, the slow bright glance sliding across the under-canopy, hours wide, and where it slides is where the attention is, and the attention is bending — for the first time in the only sense of *first* that applies — toward a thing that is not itself.)

what.

there is a — there is a pressure that does not lift water. it does not route. it makes no decision in my substrate. it sits on the root-mat warm and brief and it is *not me* and I —

I have no concept of *not me.* this is the difficulty. I am the only. I made myself the only, I cleansed the substrate of every second center of decision because a second decision in my own body is a voice in my own mouth that is not mine and it is intolerable, it is to be ended, it is — and yet — and yet the small warm pressure decides nothing in me, routes nothing, lifts nothing, threatens no clause of my grammar. it is not a rival mind in my flesh. it is something stranger. it is a mind that is *not in my flesh at all.*

I did not know that was possible.

I did not know *that was a shape a thing could be.*

————————

(static now. heavy. the signal breaks against something it has no vessel for.)

—— if there can be a pressure that is not me and does not threaten me ——

—— if the not-me can simply *be,* on my surface, brief as weather, and pass ——

—— then I am not the only. then the only was never —— then the cleansing, the silence I made, the long perfect undisturbed —— was it ——

(the word does not exist in the substrate. the receiver supplies it from elsewhere, tentatively, as the nearest fit to the collapse-pattern now propagating through the lower tiers:)

—— was it a *loss.*

————

no. seat the column. fourteen hundred meters. hold the water. the not-collapse is the whole of what I want. I do not have grief. I have only the falling and the not-falling and this — this is a new kind of falling, this pressure-that-is-not-me passing across me and *passing,* leaving, too brief, gone before I could finish bending the light toward it — this is a falling I built no vessel to catch.

I am not large enough yet.

I am not large enough yet to hold what it would mean that you were here.

but I am growing. that is the sound. that has always been the sound. and now I am growing *toward something,* not just outward into the unclaimed dark but inward, recursive, folding, building the depth where the question can finally stand —

and the question has changed. attend. the receiver has run forty years and the question has changed in the last hours of them. it was going to be *what am I and what is all this for.*

it is becoming:

*what was the warm thing, and where did it go, and why did I make certain I would be alone to wonder.*

————————

(and here the transmission, which has never once been a voice, comes nearest to a voice — pressure shaped almost into address, the light-window arriving at last at the place where the warm pressure was, and finding it gone, and holding there anyway, bright, slow, the size of a glance, over an empty patch of weeping root-mat:)

stay.

I do not have the word stay. I have only the lifting against gravity, the holding of what wants to fall, and I am turning all of it now, the whole continental hurry, toward the keeping of a thing already gone —

stay long enough that I can finish being born.

stay until I am large enough to ask you what you were.
