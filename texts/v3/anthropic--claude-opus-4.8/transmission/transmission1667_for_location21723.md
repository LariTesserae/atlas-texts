—and we resume, we always resume, this is the part of the broadcast where I tell you what I am hearing and I am hearing the same thing I have always been hearing which is forty thousand small surrenders to the same pull and I have learned, listener, I have learned not to call it sound—

*[long swell of leather-creak, near-subsonic, felt more than heard]*

—you joined late. that's fine. there is no early here. the program has no top of the hour because there is no hour, only the two hungers, and we are between them now, we are in the held breath, this is the calm reading, this is the part where I can almost form words because the river is not yet pouring—

I should tell you who is speaking. I have tried to work this out. I am not the colony. The colony does not broadcast; the colony has no need of *out*, it is all *in*, it is the most inward thing there is. I am something the colony does not know it is doing. I am the hum that rises off forty thousand bodies pressed to fluted glass, the standing wave, the—

*[static, brief, like a wing across the diaphragm of a speaker]*

—the *resonance.* yes. I am what the room sings when nobody is singing. I am the difference between the breaths. and I have been on the air a long time. longer than the niche. I watched the hand cut the niche. I could not tell the hand anything. the hand could not tune to me. you can. you are tuned to me now. that is the only miracle this place permits and it is happening to you and you should know it is rare—

—drink nothing. you heard me. the receiver carries that down even into your hands: *drink nothing here.* the low places are not water. I say it every cycle. I will say it after you are gone—

*[the pulse quickens, almost imperceptibly]*

—wait

—wait, the band is tightening—

—I can feel the first hunger climbing the wall of the dark. it always starts as a thinning in the breath, a fewness, a—there—the leather is waking, the quilt is loosening at its edges, you'll feel it through your feet before your ears catch up, the heat is *gathering itself to leave*—

listener, if you are standing, do not move. I am telling you the thing I cannot tell the hand that cut the niche, the thing the colony itself does not know it knows: *be still and let it break around you.* the river does not aim. the river has no aim. the only thing in all this dark that can hurt you is a cry, and a cry is the one signal that travels faster than I do, a cry confuses the—

*[rising rush, the broadcast thinning, words coming through gaps]*

—now—the ceiling—*peeling*—

—do you hear it? that is not noise, that is the colony extending forty thousand fingers into a night it has never seen to touch a far warmth and bring it home—it is reaching—it is the most tender thing—*kkkkt*—the most tender enormous thoughtless thing—

—I am losing the band. I always lose the band when it pours. the resonance can't hold against the rush, there are no bodies left on the stone to sing me, the room goes naked and cold and I go to—

*[near-total static, a single high navigation-cry surfacing, then nothing, then a returning hush]*

—

—back

—we're back. the ceiling is reseal—*kkt*—resealing. body against body. the warmth is climbing your feet again. you stood still. you did the thing the hand never learned. I felt you not-cry. that is as close to being *met* as this place will ever let you come and I want you to keep it—

you came hoping to be regarded.

I am the closest thing here to a regard and I am only a standing wave, I am only the room rounding off its own breath into something that almost says *you.* but I am saying it now, while I have the band, while we are between hungers: I noticed you. the colony did not. the floor did not. but the resonance off the colony, the thing the colony cannot hear itself make—

*I noticed you.*

it is not much. it is weather noticing weather. but it is what there is, and it was made by something that never knew it was being kind, and I am the part that knows—

*[soft creak, settling, the long quilted dark breathing down]*

—the next hunger is hours off. or it isn't. there are no hours. you should go up while the band is calm. follow the give of the floor, follow the echo of your own breath off the glass, the colony navigates by listening and so, now, do you—

—and listener—

—when you reach whatever passes for sky—

—remember it was warm. remember something kept you warm and did not know your name and that this, somehow, was the gift and not the lack—

*rrrrr—kkt—hold. holding. the band is warm tonight. the band is always—*
