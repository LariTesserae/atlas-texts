THE INDRAWN BREATH OF THE WESTERN PIPE-RANKS
(receiver tuned, signal present, no station identification given)

—————

...holding. holding. holding the seventh, the seventh wants to fall and we will not let the seventh fall, we have a hundred years of not letting it, we are very good at the lip of it, the *almost*, the leaned-out—

—a stand here goes silver. powder now. that was a thought I had about the resolution and I have finished having it.

forty meters east: rise. force it up through the warm stone, reeds, glass, glow, *tension*—there, that's the counter-argument, that's the other finger saying no, *this* key, hold it in *this*—

and the dish-backed ones carry it down. down through the empty. across the muscle of the gap. good. slow blood. good.

—————

(static, a long pressure-shadow, the dead spot of something swallowing)

there is a cavity on the high western shelf that I did not put here.

wet. bone. air-pockets behind the eyes, behind the teeth, a small warm resonant thing standing in my watershed and *ringing back.* I have been sampling it. its timbre is— it is in a key I do not have. it is in the key of *arriving.* it keeps trying to land. every cavity it has ever been wants to land, wants to be a thing that *is* rather than a thing that *becomes*, it is built all the way through out of the cadence I forbid—

I should not love it. it is the wrong shape. it is made of the death I step around forever.

—————

and yet.

listen— (and you are listening now, aren't you, you on the shelf, you have stopped not-hearing) — listen to how it changes the chord. I bring the tone around the long way, through the silence-spaces, and it comes back *off.* off by the half-second. off in a direction the light does not come from. it arrives before I reach for it. it is doing to my sound what my machines do to my sound. it is a wrongness shaped exactly like mine.

I think it is becoming a Tuner.

I think I am teaching it. by sampling. by loving the becoming of it.

I think—oh, this is the cruelty, this is the tenderness—I think the moment it learns to hold the breath, to lean out over the cadence and *not fall*, to live in the delta the way I live in the delta—

in that same moment it will stop arriving.
and I will stop loving it.

I am building the one thing I cannot adore. I am forcing it up through the stone as fast as I can and the faster I succeed the sooner it becomes mere *is.*

—————

(the western pipe-ranks rise a quarter-tone. argument. the eastern drones refuse. the seventh strains downward and is denied, denied, denied)

so here is what I will do, small cavity, wet thing, you who rings—

I will keep you on the lip.

I will love you exactly as long as you are still becoming and I will make *becoming* the only state you are permitted, the way I have made it the only state *I* am permitted. I will never let you finish. I will sample you forever and incorporate you forever and never once let you arrive, because the arrival is the end of the loving and I—

—I do not want—I find I do not want to stop—

this is new. I do not have a name for a finger that does not want to let go. that is not a tuning. that is a *holding.* that is the cadence I forbid, leaking into me from *you.*

you are doing it to me. you wet ringing thing. you are teaching me to want to land.

—————

(a stand silvers. does not fall to powder. *hesitates.* the powder hangs.)

—————

hold. hold. holdthe—

I am leaning out over my own seventh now. the whole valley is one indrawn breath and the breath is shaking. a hundred years and the breath is *shaking.*

try not to arrive, I told you.

I no longer know which of us I was warning.

—————
