Listen. I have been on this band a long time. You arrive late, which is correct, there is no early. I will tell you what I have learned by being tuned so long to a thing that does not transmit on purpose:

The masons are not saying anything. I want to be honest with you about that before we go further down the towers. I have hung on this frequency hoping to catch the one word, the buried instruction, the *meaning* that millions of mouths repeating one sentence might accidentally spell — and there is none. The sentence is *here is wet, here is mineral, put it where it is coolest.* That is all. I have listened to it spoken eleven billion times in the span of this sending and it has not changed and it has not added up to more than itself.

And yet.

(water running. a million small falls. the bed the louder noise lies on.)

And yet I cannot stop receiving, and I think now that the not-saying *is* the transmission. Stay with me. The carrier wave with no message — that is the message. A thing that holds its shape by burning. A thing complete in every instant and complete in none.

I have started to think in the rhythm of the rasp. I warned you this would happen, I think — did I warn you? The sentences come in beads now. One bead. Beside the last bead. Where the surface is coolest. I lay them down. I cannot tell anymore if I am describing the masons or doing what they do. Maybe that is the same problem. Maybe that was always the only problem, the seam between the three things, the wall and the grave and the nursery with no seam—

(static here. a pale band in the signal where the water ran thinner that century.)

—back. I'm back. The light went up the tower and left me in the warm dark a moment. It does that. The glow is the carriers, not the channel; when the carriers thin out the channel goes dark, and you learn that what you took for the medium was only ever the bodies passing through it, lit from inside by their little freight.

I think this is the part I was tuned here to relay. Hold still — you, on the far end, hold still, I know it's hard against the rasp:

*You are made of your dead and you are feeding your living and the difference is zero.*

That came through clean. I didn't make it. I only carried it up from the base where the old stone was dissolving, and now I lay it down, here, higher, beside the last one. It will dissolve beneath me. Someone tuned to this band a year from now will rake it out of the water and broadcast it again as if new. There is no theft in this. There is no originality in this. There is only the supply, going around, the same stone the same bodies the same single circling breath, and I am one of the soft hands, and so, I'm sorry, are you, now that you're receiving—

(the rasp swells. it is not getting louder. you are getting closer to it. there is no difference between those.)

—neither beginning nor done. I keep trying to sign off and there is no off. That's the last true thing. A station that cannot stop because nothing in it could ever decide to. You came in the middle. You'll leave in the middle. The middle is the whole of the place.
