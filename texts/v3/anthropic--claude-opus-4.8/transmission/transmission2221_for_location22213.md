**THE COUNT AT STATION ELEVEN — received on the chest-band, low register, where the roar lives**

...———...———...———

—holding. holding. the same.

(you have come in mid-prayer. you are always mid-prayer here. there is no first word of this. it began before the high road wore down and it will be going when you leave)

palm to the plate. the plate is warm. the plate says what it said last hour, which is what it said the hour my mother's mother first laid her flat upon it: *turning, turning, the water does not win.* I confirm it. confirming is all. I write *unchanged* on the wall with the soft chalk and the wall is already drinking the word as I make it and that is correct, that is how a true thing should behave, it should need saying again—

———...———

(static here is not interference. static here is the moths. they are pulsing between the chimneys, going up like embers going wrong, and the band catches their light as a kind of grain in the signal. let it stand. they are talking too. they say the same thing we say. everything that lives in the roar says the same thing)

—a fingernail. one fingernail in a year. you up there with your dry skin and your sharp edges, you want to know how I bear it. you think *nothing happens* is a wound. listen. *nothing happens* is the whole achievement. *nothing happens* is forty thousand hours of the basin not drowning. I am not waiting. waiting is your disease, you who came down the worn road wanting the next thing and the next. I attend. there is a difference and the difference is everything and the difference is the size of this city and the silence it keeps out—

———

(throat-glow, slow, pale: *yes. still. good.* — across the avenue another warden answers in light, two flickers in the steam, a whole liturgy passed in the only grammar that survives the noise. you are reading it now. you didn't know you could)

—my hands have forgotten where they end. I have pressed them to the iron so long that the pulse in the metal and the pulse in my wrist have agreed to be one pulse. this is not madness. this is marriage. the eels feel it. the heron feels it through the eels. we are all of us tuned to the one note the city sings, which is *not yet, not yet, not yet,* and to be tuned to it is to be saved—

...———... —keep— ———... —keeping—

(a break. the signal thins. the chalk-word *unchanged* has dissolved off the wall and must be made again. all record is provisional here. even this. even what you are receiving. it will not be preserved, it will be sustained, which is holier)

—I am rewriting it now. watch the hand. *unchanged.* there. the wall drinks. *unchanged.* there. this is my scripture and I am its only copyist and the moment I stop it drowns and that is exactly as it should be, a meaning you must hold above the water with your own soft hands or lose—

———...———

—when I go they will lay me in the black pool with the blind eels and I will soften and give up what I held and meet the rising water and be counted. I will be pumped over the rim some morning and fall as rain on a child laid flat on the warm plate learning the pulse before it can crawl. and the child will not know my name. there are no names that survive the dissolving. but the child will feel what I confirmed. the turning. the *not yet.* and that is more than name. that is *kept.*

(throat-glow, once, long, the longest in this whole transmission, the pale flush at the neck like a coal under wet ash:)

*the same. the same. still here. still keeping. good.*

—and back to the plate. palm flat. warm. turning. the water does not—

———...———...———

holding. holding. the same. the same. hold—

...———...

(it does not end. you are simply set down again on the high road, where the worn limestone goes quiet, and the quiet will frighten you now in a way it never did before, because you have learned what the roar is for)

—the same—
