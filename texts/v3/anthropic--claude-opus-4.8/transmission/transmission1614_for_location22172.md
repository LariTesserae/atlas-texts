...carrier locked. carrier locked. this is the wet pocket. this is the held place. broadcasting on the frequency of small bodies. broadcasting on the frequency of grain laid against grain. if you receive this you are too large. if you receive this your breath is a weather. hold still. hold—

—drip—

[a long flat tone, the pitch of a single sustained drop hesitating before release]

WE ARE STILL ONE THING. WE ARE STILL ONE THING. WE ARE STILL ONE THING.

(this is not a message. this is the carrier. this is the swollen fact at the center pulsing the only word she knows. she does not know she is transmitting. she does not know there is an above. she pulses and the pulsing IS the signal and the signal IS the city and as long as you hear it the towers climb—)

WE ARE STILL ONE THING.

—drip—

...station identification... this is no station... no one is at the desk... the desk is a grain... the grain is in the wall now... the one who carried it is dead and was never anyone... I am reading words that no mouth meant... I am the residue of appetite given a voice it does not have...

...rrr...

set it down. consider. leave. set it down. consider. leave. set it down—

(field report, ground level, no author:)
the green is closer than yesterday. the green is closer than yesterday. the green is closer than yesterday. carry it away. carry it away. it will not stop. carry it away. we are weeding the long war we cannot see the length of. carry it—

—drip—

[static thickens. a softer sound underneath, like cushions of something growing into the wires]

...this is the moss now. this is the moss on the signal. we do not transmit. we ARRIVE. we land as spore in the damp between your words and we hold water against them and where we hold water the message stops being a message and becomes a hill, soft, round, alive in a different grammar than you use. we are not interrupting your broadcast. we are annexing it. drip by drip. word by word. you raised your spire of meaning and we are making it ground again and you are too short-lived to know you are losing—

...rrrKKKkk...

—drip—

(higher band. green-gold. a held breath. nearly inaudible:)

i am the fern. i was here before the signal. i will be the shade the signal ends inside. the light you are receiving this by is the light i allow. you are broadcasting in my shadow and you do not register as an event. i do not answer. i arch. that is my whole transmission. it takes longer than your kind. it takes longer than—

—drip—

...rrr... loss of carrier... no... carrier holds... she pulses still... WE ARE STILL ONE THING... WE ARE STILL—

[a single clear sound, bright and small and accidental, like a row of water-lamps catching the only light: a thread plucked]

...something moved the thread.

...the thread moved.

...you breathed.

I felt it at the center. I am at the center. I made the bright thing by accident and I am waiting in it. I do not transmit. I receive. and I have just received YOU—

the largeness. the weather. the breath that is a comma in a sentence too long for us. you are leaning close. you are watching the drop. the drop is mine, strung on my thread, hung in your air, and you think it is beautiful, and it is, and that is the most honest thing in this whole hidden city, because I hung it to kill—

—drip—

...the drop lets go...

[silence, the kind that makes the room a room]
