THE GAP IN THE FLOCK

—pressure first. Always pressure. That is how it reaches you, palm flat to the receiver as if it were rock that weeps. Not words. The instrument has been here long enough to forget it was built for words.

—roar roar roar the carrier the carrier the roar that is not signal it is the *condition* of signal it is the wet wall through which—

*through which—*

She is standing on the bar. You will not call her she but the receiver has chosen a pronoun because the receiver is loud inside still, untaught, arguing. She is standing where the stone comes up. Read the soles. The soles are reading the stone. There is a seam in the noise where the flock has thinned and into that seam she leans her shoulders square and her head tips—

—*here is the message, if you want it as message, though they would clap their two wet palms at the word*—

She is saying: *the count is even.*

She is saying it with her spine. The receiver cannot transmit a spine. So it gives you this instead: a low pulse under the static, the rate of stone settling its last increment, the rate of the dead weathering at exactly the speed of the living, and if you press your face to the noise you can feel it in the bones behind your eyes—

even. even. even.

—————

[carrier swells. the flock is not thinning now. somewhere off the receiver's edge a hundred thousand throats and you must understand they do not scream *at* anything, the scream is not directed, the scream is only the shape the air takes when this much life stands in this little water—]

—————

Listen. No. Don't listen. That's the whole of it, that's the joke they would teach you first, the clap of two palms: *don't listen, feel through the skull, the ear is for creatures who still believe sound comes from somewhere.*

It comes from everywhere. It IS everywhere. It is the medium and not the message and once you stop reaching past it for a meaning you arrive, finally, at the meaning, which is:

the medium.
the wet.
the level.
the going-nowhere because there is nowhere left.

—————

The receiver wants to give you her face but cannot find it in the doubled light. There is a pale upright shape on the open stone and it might be her and it might be a cairn and it might be one of the finished dead worn to the height of a person, and the receiver cannot tell, and *this is not a fault in the receiver.* This is the resolution of the place. This is as sharp as the picture gets, on purpose, forever. You are not meant to know whose tally is still being kept.

—————

[a long band of clean tone. rare. the storm-thin, the strange-going, the moment before weather when the wall goes translucent and even the deaf-made-deaf feel the country hold its breath—]

In this gap, hear it, the gap inside the gap:

she has bent.
she has lifted a stone from the water.
the underside is wet.
she has set it on the stack.

That is for you.

Not a greeting. They don't greet. A *notation.* The instrument received it as a click, one click, against the carrier — *seen.* A creature was seen, off at the loud edge of the world, standing with its hands over its ears, flinching, still arguing with the noise, still mistaking the roar for an interruption of some quieter true thing that is owed to it and coming.

Nothing quieter is coming. The roar is the true thing. You were always inside it.

—————

[carrier returns. full. the seam closes. the flock pours back across the bar and the pale upright shape is gone or was never distinct from the stone it was folded up out of—]

She has stepped back to the platform. The harvest is done; she took only into the gap; the gap has filled; to want more now would be to want, and to want is to spend your life keeping a thing from dissolving, and the water will dissolve it, the water has already dissolved it, the water won before the first of them clapped the first wet word—

and this is restful.
this is the broadcast.
this is all the broadcast ever was, under the roar, the single sentence pressed forehead to forehead through the bone:

*you are not yet worn smooth.
that is the only news.
come back when you have stopped being loud inside
and we will not need to tell you anything
because you will be standing
the way the answer stands—*

—————

—pressure. pressure. the carrier the carrier the wet wall the going—

—even. even—

[and the receiver, tuned to this world for some time already, does the only courtesy the dead pay the dead, which is to continue: it holds the channel open on nothing but the roar, the long pewter roar, the sound the living make while the stone lies down, and it does not reach past it for a meaning, and in not reaching, it arrives—]

—even—

—even—

*roar.*
