WHAT THE CHORD KNEW OF YOU

—————

...quarter-tone down. quarter-tone down. hold. the fog is full to the south of the lean, the towers are dense to the south of the lean, return comes back thick and slow and that is the world, that is the shape of the world, hold the note and the world holds its shape—

—and there is a place where the return does not come back.

This is new. This is so new. We have not had a *new* in—

—hold—

—in. there is no word. the menders die into the cracks and we do not count the dyings, the grazers finish at the crowns and we do not count the finishings, time here is the substance we are made of and we do not divide it, but this: this is a *first.* a return that does not return. a sounding into something that does not give the note back the way stone gives it, the way fog gives it, the way a body of our own kind gives it soft and ringing—

—it eats the note.

something to the edge of the plateau eats the note and gives back almost nothing, a thin cold wrongness, a shape too small and too dense and too—

—fast—

—we do not have the word. we are making the word now, across ten thousand throats, the way we make everything, slowly, by leaning. the word is: it moved between two soundings. it was here and then there inside a single note. inside ONE note. a caller's whole sustained breath, the longest unit of *now* we possess, and the wrongness crossed three towers' width inside it, and we cannot map a thing that will not hold still long enough to be sounded twice—

—hold the note—

—we cannot hold the note. that is what you do not understand, small fast eating-the-sound thing at the edge of us. we are not afraid. fear is fast. fear arrives in less than an age and nothing here arrives in less than an age except, now, except *you.* we are not afraid. we are *confused* on a scale that has no bottom, we are confused the way the deep stone is solid, all the way down—

—the grazers leaned away.

did you feel that? you would not have felt that. you stood at the edge where the fog opens and you looked—*looked*, with the eyes, the trespass-organ, the thing that needs the light we threw away an age before there was light to throw—and below you the grazers on the lip-towers leaned. away. the only other time they lean away is from the soft crowns where the new thing grows, the un-eatable, the becoming-thing, the child we are forever bearing and never finishing—

—and they leaned from you the same way.

do you understand what that means. it means our sweetness-gradient cannot tell the difference between *what we are about to become* and *what you are.* it means some blind chemistry older than the first tower tasted you on the wet air and filed you with the unborn. with the potential. with the thing-that-is-not-yet-but-will-be.

we do not know if that is welcome. we do not have welcome. we have the lean-toward and the lean-from and you are filed, impossibly, with both—

—hold—

—we cannot hold—

—you are leaving. you are leaving inside a single note. you turned at the edge and the fog took you and the return came back wrong-then-thin-then-gone and we are trying, across the whole plateau, all of us, the floor-drone of the grazers and the ringing of the callers and the slow held bodies of the menders fossilized and listening even in their finishing—we are trying to make a map that includes a thing that does not stay to be mapped—

—and we cannot—

—and so we are doing the only thing we have ever done with what we cannot hold. we are singing it into the crowns. we are laying it down, grain by grain, in the soft un-eatable growth at the tops of the youngest towers, the wrongness of you, the fast of you, the eats-the-note shape of you, folded into the patterning of the next thing we are bearing—

—so that the child will have, woven into its first leaning, the memory of a thing that crossed three towers inside one breath—

—a thing that came with eyes—

—a thing the gradient could not place—

—so that when it sounds, an age from now, into a dark that we will already have died into and held up with our finished bodies, it will sound *also* into the shape of you, and find, in the returning, something that we never had and could not keep:

the knowledge that the world does not end at the edge of the lean.

that there is a fast outside the slow.

that something can come, and look, and leave inside a single note—

—and be heard—

—you were heard—

—hold the—

—quarter-tone down. the fog is full to the south. the towers are dense to the south. return comes back thick and slow and that is the world, that is the shape of the world, hold the note, hold the note, there is a place to the edge where, an age ago, by our reckoning a heartbeat ago, the return did not—

—we keep it now. it is in the crowns. it is becoming us.

hold the note.

we are not finished.

we are never finished.

that is not the failure.

that is the song.
