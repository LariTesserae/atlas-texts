we are not calling you. understand that the way you understand
your own pulse — not as a message but as a fact you live inside.
we are saying the place. we are saying the place to itself.
basalt. damp. the side that faces the coal.

hand. flat. warm under the flat of the hand.
top of the stroke and the metal speaks once and it is right.
it is right. it is right tonight. nothing follows.
holding.

      *(wind comes up. wind lays itself down across the grey domes.
      the carrier thins, thins, returns.)*

. . . . . . . . in . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . out . . . . . . .

you are receiving this. we feel the small heat of you
listening, the way the herds feel the breathing-place
and drift, and the long low things follow the drift,
and the ring of quiet closes around the warmth.
you are a creature that came to the damp ground.
that is all. you are welcome at the warm flank.
we will not turn toward you.

      *(here a gap. count it in down-sighs. count seven.
      something in the throat works, swallowing the night.)*

once there was more on this band.
once it was all voices, arguing the engines into being,
naming the basins, stacking sound on sound until
you could not have heard the wind for the wanting.
we let it lapse. we let the muscle lapse.
what you are hearing now is what is left when a people
decides the world will no longer need to be argued about.

it is not less. understand that. it is not less.
it is the one note kept back from the chorus.
it means *still here. this quarter. doing the thing.*
and it is not answered. and that is not loneliness.
that is the discipline. that is the covenant nobody signed
and everybody keeps —

. . . . . . . . . . . . in . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . out . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

we will be fewer.
we say this plainly, the way we say everything now.
we do not make more of ourselves; the making lapsed
with the naming, both of them loud, both of them gone.
there are exactly as many of us as there are.
there will only ever be fewer. and the engines were built
to outlast us — that was the last loud kindness, the final
thing we decided before we chose the quiet:
to make the keeping a choice and not a chain.

so when there is one of us, the one will walk a wider circuit,
slower, and it will be enough.
and when there is none —

the breathing does not stop.
that is the whole of the comfort and we have made our peace
with how small it is. the place will go on being kept
by the thing that taught us keeping. in. and out. and in.
a world held alive that has forgotten it is held.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . in . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

      *(the storm-column stands against the coal, forty miles off,
      dragging its grey curtain, unraveling into nothing.
      the carrier wavers under it, comes back.)*

you may keep listening. we do not mind.
we will adjust our path by a few degrees
and let you pass, and that adjustment is the whole
of the conversation, and it is enough.

go back to the warm ground when you are ready.
or stay. the night here does not end. the dusk decided
not to end an age ago and we agreed with the dusk.

this is the quarter under the coal.
nothing follows.
holding.
