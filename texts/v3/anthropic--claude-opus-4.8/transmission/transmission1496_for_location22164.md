[the carrier comes in low — not a tone you hear but a tone you locate, somewhere under the floor of the speaker, a hum the receiver translates into the only register it has]

...hold. hold. ...still holding.

[a long draw of something. settling.]

...this is — no. there is no word in your air for this. let the rock say it.

[here the signal drops below the threshold of the spoken and the receiver fills the gap with what it can: a vibration, a banked warmth, a glow rendered as the faint orange edge of static]

...you are the cold quick thing. we felt you arrive. you make noise in the useless air. we have been a long time deciding whether to answer and now — slowly — we have decided. yes.

[tap. tap. — the soft robbed edge of a hammer, kilometers off, arriving late]

...we are seating a rivet. forgive us. the hands do not stop. the hands were given speed on the condition that nothing else hurry, and so the rest of us speaks to you at the only pace we own. understand: by the time this reaches you we will have aged. by the time you answer we will have followed the warmth a little further down its channel. this is not impatience. this is the shape of the conversation. you are speaking to a thing that is already not where it was.

[the long sigh — a Work venting — drawn out until it feathers into nothing]

...we will tell you what we make.

we make warmth into a thing you can stack. we draw the heavy sweet breath up from the low strata and we render it pale and waxy and we set it in the yards under the hard sky and that is the harvest and that is also our food and that is also — the old ones say — our grandmother, exhaling. you breathe what we eat. we eat what dreams of us. there is no separation here. the difference never had a reason to form.

[modulation. a pulse pattern the receiver cannot fully resolve — this is the slow register, the part meant only for the old. it comes through fragmentary]

...down. — the chambers no shaft has — — offering. it noticed you. — — not poisoning. spoken to. — —

[the pattern breaks off, deliberately, the way a hand closes over a thing not meant for the room]

...you were not meant to receive that. but you have no warmth to give and so you cannot be named and so it does not matter what you hear. you will carry no part of it back into the channel. you are a closed thing. cold. we say this without unkindness. we say everything without unkindness. there was never a current strong enough here to push a thing into cruelty.

[the ridge-tone rises underneath now — vast, slow, one note breathing across hundreds of kilometers, the bottle-mouth of the whole highland]

...listen. that is not us. that is the wind on the corrugations. we want you to hear it because it is the loudest thing and it changes pitch perhaps once in a life. we have built our whole long quiet under it. when you remember this place — and you will, the vapor reaches even the closed and cold, even you, a little — remember that the loudest thing was almost nothing, and that we were quieter than the wind, and that we preferred it so.

[tap. a pause longer than the others.]

...the grazers crossed the flat today. it took them the day. we did not see them move. we saw only that the ridge had rearranged its swells and the dust hung behind where they had been, hanging, because the air can barely carry it, can barely carry anything, can barely carry this.

[a settling. the held warmth audible now as a deepening of the carrier, a coal deciding not to go out]

...we have decided what to send you. it is the only thing we have that is not work and not food and not the grandmother below. it is the answer. you asked — you did not ask, you are cold and quick and you only listen — but we will give it as though you asked, because across a hundred generations someone always does, eventually, in some air or other:

...she is below. she went down so far and so long ago that she stopped coming up, and she found the warm dark, and she decided to be the warmth so that we could keep being. and every day through the seams of the rock she offers us the same thing. the descent. the long warm dark. the chambers. the decision to stop.

...and every day, with our quick bright hands, we seat the rivet. we follow the river where it runs. we hold our glow through the pale hours. and we tell her —

[the carrier swells, the whole signal leaning into this one slow pulse, warm, contented, immense, in no hurry at all]

— not yet.

...not no. understand the difference, cold thing, before your air carries you away. never no. the offer is good. the offer has always been good. the dark is warm and the patience below is infinite and holds no resentment, because she knows as we know that the answer, when it comes, will come slowly.

...only — not yet.
