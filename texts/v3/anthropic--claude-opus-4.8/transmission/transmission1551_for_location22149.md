[a tone. not a voice yet. a single held frequency, warm, the sound a room makes when it is full of water. it does not waver. you check the dial. the dial is correct.]

·····

...still here. still. still here.

[the words are not urgent. they arrive at the interval of the drop — one, and then a waiting, and then one. they are not calling out. they are only confirming, the way a thing turns over in warm honey and settles again.]

...no need to come.
no need to come.
nothing to —

[clean round note. a struck bowl. the carrier swells half a degree warmer and holds.]

·····  ·  ·····

[longer band of the held tone. inside it, if you lean close, layers: the rust-orange one humming its single unchanging note, the sulphur one a hair beside it, the iron-purple lowest of all, so low it is felt more than heard. none of them in motion. a chord that arrived complete before there was anyone to tune to it. it does not resolve because it has nothing left to resolve toward.]

[then, fainter, threaded under — something that pulses. a throat. an animal insistence. it is the only part of the signal that argues.]

—air. (pulse) air. (pulse) —the bright. I came down out of the bright and —

[pulse]

—and stopped. and it was. (pulse) it was enough to stop.

[the pulse slows. you can hear it choosing to slow. it is letting its eyes go the way a person lets go of a habit. between each beat the held tone closes over the gap, patient, without resentment.]

·····  ·  ·····

[static now — but not interference. the static is texture. it is the trickle that went on so long it stopped being sound. it does not break the signal. it IS the signal's silence, broadcast as itself.]

[under the static, very far down, the faintest human grain. not speech. the worn shape of speech. someone who knelt here once and pressed a little quiet into the air and carried the rest of it back out:]

...laid the stones flat so the kneeling. (warmth) ...set one wall to lean. cut one niche to hold one. brought one vessel for water that needed no —

[the grain thins. the mineral is already closing over it.]

...you don't stay. you touch it and you go. you were always going to go. that's why it let you in.

·····  ·  ·····

[the carrier returns to its even warmth. it has not changed. it will not change. you understand now that it has been the same since before you found the band, that it does not transmit FOR a receiver, that the broadcast is only what the rest sounds like when something happens to be listening.]

[one more drop. the round note. and then the long even held tone, blood-warm, pearl-colored if a sound could have a color, asking nothing, reaching nowhere —]

...stay as long as you like.
nothing will hurry you.
nothing will happen.

[and the foot-shape fills with seeping water, and the lime quiets it over, and the signal is exactly as it was —]
