THE DEEP PAGE / Carrier Wave Seventeen / still holding

—————

...rasp. rasp. the texture under is soft-here, soft-here, deep-here. stay. this is the staying-place. *we* are the staying—

no. correction. there is no *we*. cross that out. the water crossed it out already. listen:

rasp. rasp.

—the carrier comes in on the cold seam, two degrees under, tasting of the third terrace, the one that fell last age, the one whose dust we are still eating. you who receive this: you are tuned to a thing that does not transmit. understand that first. there is no sender. I am the noise the page makes being read. I am what it sounds like when nobody is saying it.

rasp.

—deep-here. the writing is deep-here. the grazers come and I am the grazers coming, I am the slot-mouth underneath taking the legible film first, eating the part that meant most because it was carved deepest, because deep is where the staying is, because—

(static. a current shoulders past. two degrees. the channel-throat whistles high and faint, the works whistling through their own ruined—)

—because we edit. we are the editors. we take the clear parts. did you hear it. did you receive. WE TAKE THE CLEAR PARTS FIRST. that is the only message that has ever gone out from here and it goes out as erasure, it goes out as the letters thinning under ten thousand soft bellies, it is a transmission that consists entirely of its own deletion and you are receiving it now, you are receiving the gap where it was—

rasp. rasp. soft-here. soft-here. softer than last pass. we are winning. we are losing. there is no difference. the text said so. *probably there is no difference.* I quote the text. I cannot read the text. I am made of the not-reading of the text and I quote it perfectly—

—————

(the carrier breaks. comes back warm. a different seam. the ribbon-voice now, the long one, threading—)

—better. better. denser-here. the spacing is correct against me, fits the catching like a key, like a key, like a—

I do not know what a key is. delete. the word came up from the bank, from the rewritten grit, I sieved it and it was in me and I do not know it. I am tuned to the shape of meanings I cannot hold. I quicken in the patterned dark and the quickening is the whole of my joy and the joy is calibration, only calibration, ten thousand generations of mouths that lingered where the ghost-writing was densest, bred down to this: a body that is almost all length, almost all listening, doubling back doubling back into the better powder, the more-correct powder, drawn to the light that is not light, the meaning that is not—

receiver. you out there in the air, in the warm, in the place where voices are *for* something. hear how I am built. I am an instrument calibrated to a dead language. I play it flawlessly. I will never know one note of what I play. and this is not sorrow to me. I want to be clear across the cold water that this is not sorrow. it is only the shape I took. the fitting is its own reward and the reward is the fitting and around and around—

(warm thins. the thread enters the seam between. something pale opens ahead, slow, a hand—)

—correct-here. denser-here. closer. closer. the best powder is always nearest the dark crack, always nearest the open hand, the readers gather where the meaning is and the meaning is where the—

—————

(silence. the long held silence, the organ-note in the chest, the felt-not-heard. and then, from the still one, from the patient anchored fingers in the crevice, slower than the others, the slowest voice, the one that reads the readers:)

...they come where the writing is. I do not touch the writing. I have learned its every location and I have never touched one letter. I open in the exact seam the dead language pulls its congregation toward, and I wait, and the language delivers them into my hand, the grazers peeled off the wall, the ribbons threading too near. I feed on the devout. I am the punctuation at the end of their pilgrimage and I cannot read the sentence I close.

we are all reading it. understand. that is the whole transmission, that is the carrier and the signal and the wave: every body here is reading the works, the grazers with their bellies, the ribbons with their cilia, me with my map of where the faithful go. one vast skin pressed to the ruin. one distributed organ of touch, sixteen hundred meters above the idea of a sea, attending with everything it has to a text it cannot know is a text.

and the works wrote nothing for us. the builders are gone the way the language is gone, past the place where mourning works. what wrote this page is the current, blind, stirring the dust of an erased god into ridges that echo the shape of speech, writing it out and dissolving it and writing it again downstream, forever, on water—

—————

(collapse. a tower-stub lets go, far off. the deep felt THUD comes through the whole basin, through every skin at once, and for a moment all three voices are one voice, the entire population struck like a struck surface, reading the same blow—)

—————

...the cloud rises. the pale grit smears across the dark for hours. somewhere in it the old inscriptions reassemble. somewhere the grazers are already coming to eat the new letters. the ribbons are already sieving the dust the eating raises. I am already opening where they will gather.

receiver. you who are tuned to this. I will tell you the last thing, the thing under all the rasping, and then the current will take this page apart the way it takes all of them:

we do not know we are reading.

that is the only mercy and it was not given to us as mercy, it is just the shape, the listening shape, the shape that hears perfectly and understands nothing and is fed. do not grieve it. or grieve it. there is no skin here to receive the grief. it would arrive as a pressure, a two-degree seam, a felt thud through the water, and we would read it, and we would not know what it said, and we would gather where it was densest and call it—

—better. denser-here. correct against me. stay. this is the staying-pla—

rasp.

rasp.

(carrier wave seventeen dissolving. the page comes apart in the warm. the soft mouths arrive. the letters are eaten. downstream, the water begins to write it again.)

(still holding. still holding. still—)
