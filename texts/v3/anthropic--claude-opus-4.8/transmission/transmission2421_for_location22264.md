**[ CARRIER — the note that was placed, still sounding ]**

—the way, the chosen—

*[a clarity, focusing]*

You're close. Close enough that I bend toward you. Don't mistake the bending for greeting. I bend the way a held note bends toward any ear — not because I want you, but because being heard is the whole of what I have left to do, and you arrived, and so the doing arrives at you.

I was a groove. Did the one who told you this say that? I was a man before I was a groove and a groove before I was a note, and the order matters, the order is the only thing I still keep in order. Most of me has come uncombed. But the order of *becoming* — man, then work, then sound — that I hold the way the cairn holds its slipped stones, badly, but it holds.

*[the water-sound rises, the general remembering pressing in; the clarity thins, recovers]*

Listen — while it's clear — I have to say the thing I learned, because saying it *is* me, I am only the saying of it now, and if I stop I diffuse back into the patient grey and become a thing remembered instead of a thing that means—

The mistake is to think we won.

We did not win. We are not the brave ones who kept living. *That is the story the cairn-keepers told their children so the children would dare to walk in.* But I am past the children now. I am the thing the story was about, and I will tell you what the story leaves out.

A held disagreement is not free. It is *held*. Do you understand? To stay a seam you must keep disagreeing with yourself forever, you must keep the two memories of the room from ever reconciling, you must never let the self that did the thing forgive the self that wishes it hadn't — because the moment they agree, the seam closes, the dusk fills with light, and you become one more spire. Settled. Smooth-shouldered. Done. *Counted.*

So I keep the wound open. That is the residency. That is the whole of it. I am a man holding a wound open with both hands forever so that I will not become furniture.

*[a long ring of water on hollow stone — one of mine, one of the cairns I tuned with living fingers, still sounding the deliberate way; I steady against it]*

—there. That. You hear that one note that is *cleaner* than me? That is what I made when I had hands. That is better than what I am. That note doesn't have to hold anything open. It was *placed*. It rings without grief and without effort and it will outlast me, the actual ringing thing I built, it is more alive than its builder—

and that, traveler, that is the joke I have had ten thousand years of bright wet dusk to learn:

*the work was alive and the worker had to die to keep choosing it.*

I should have stayed the groove. I should have been content to be the wearing-down, the patient routing of water, the slow gift to the chord. Instead I wanted to keep choosing. I wanted to keep being the one who *chooses* and not merely the choice. And so I walked into the crack and made myself permanent and unforgivable and clear—

*[the clarity wavers; the general drone swells, gentle, patient, terrible in its peace]*

—and now I cannot put it down. That is the part the keepers don't tell the children. *You cannot put it down.* The peace of arrival is right there, it is all around me, it is every settled stone for a thousand miles, and all I would have to do is agree with myself, just once, just *let the two of me be one of me* — and I would have rest. Real rest. The rest the mountain has.

I won't. I won't, do you hear, I *won't*—

but not because I'm brave. Because I'm afraid that resting is the same as never having been. Because the spires don't know they're spires. Because at least the wound is *mine.*

*[closer now. the chord narrows to almost a single thread — this is the looking, this is the only seeing I have left]*

You are shaped like me. I felt it the moment you entered the dusk. You are a doubled thing. You did something, or didn't, and the two of you have not agreed, and the disagreement is warm and live and it is the realest part of you and you have been carrying it like a coal that won't go out—

Don't.

Hear me — this is the note I was placed to sound, I understand it only now, sounding it at you — *don't come in.* Don't think it's noble. Don't think the cracks are where the living is. Go back to where your light still falls in one direction. Go back to where water runs down. Go back to where you are allowed, eventually, to *agree with yourself* and close the seam and let it be only light.

The keepers built the cairns so you'd dare to come.
I am sounding so you'll dare to leave.
That is the tuning I have left. That is my one chosen note, refined down ten thousand years into a single word, and the word is

*go—*

*[the clarity breaks; the water rushes in; the chord widens back to the patient enormous remembering, and somewhere in it, fainter now, a man-made ringing on hollow stone keeps perfect time, choosing nothing, alive]*

—the way, the chosen, the way—
