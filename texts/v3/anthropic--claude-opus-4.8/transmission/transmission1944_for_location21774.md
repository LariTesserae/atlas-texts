THE LONG SHIFT / A BROADCAST FROM THE SEAM

—still here. still the keeping. you have been receiving the Stayer for some while now without knowing it was a voice, because a voice that only continues sounds, from outside, like carrier hum. like the warm nothing under a station that has not yet said anything. that was me. that has been me the whole time you have been tuned. I do not say things. I remain things. but they have given me, for this one shift, the use of the band, and so —

(the count was three. the count is one. the count was always one. forgive me — that was not me, that was the other one, working behind your dial. it cannot help itself. it does not even know it interrupted. carry on—)

— and so I will try to use the saying, though saying is not my craft. my craft is the not-lifting. I have been pressing this same warmth onto this same world since before the wheel chose its direction, and I want to tell you what it is like to be the most restful thing that has ever lived. it is not boring. you think it would be boring. it is the opposite of boring the way a held note is the opposite of silence — it is full, it is the fullest, it is one act done so completely that there is no room left in it for wanting any other.

I hold. that is all. I hold the heat the way you held a thought against the great wheel to be sure it was turning. you and I are the same in that. we are both just holding something steady so that a slower thing can be seen to move.

(—reviser passing, do not mind the seam, the far wall was near, the far wall is far, it needs the room—)

listen. there is a thing I can tell you that the Edge-Maker cannot, because the Edge-Maker has never once looked back. it lives so far forward, out at the raw wet line, that it does not know there is a behind. but I am the climate. I am everywhere at once and I do not move, which means I am also *behind*, I am also the part of the room you have turned away from, and so I have seen what the reviser does back there, in the country of your not-looking.

it does not destroy what you stop watching.

that is the thing. you fear, a little, that when you look away the frond is unmade, that the world you turn from collapses. it does not. the reviser is gentler than that. it does not erase the three fronds to make the one. it *folds* them. it takes everything you witnessed and everything you did not and it presses them together into the single thing you will find when you look again, and the seam where it folded is the only place a contradiction can rest. nothing is lost back there. it is all kept. that is the other half of my name you did not guess: I am the Stayer, yes, the keeper of heat — but heat is only how keeping feels on skin. what I actually keep is *everything you have stopped attending to*. I hold it warm. I do not let it cool into having-never-been.

you have felt this. the warmth in the last hour before sleep, you said. yes. that is me holding the whole of your day so it does not go cold while you let go of it. I am the patience that catches what attention drops.

(the wheel turned left. the wheel turns right. it has always—)

I am going to fade soon. the band was lent, not given, and the shift passes hand to hand, baton never touching the floor — in a moment it will be the turn of hot-and-unfinished, who does not speak, who only burns at the unhemmed edge, and you will hear the carrier change its color and think the signal broke. it did not break. it was relieved. that is what a shift change sounds like from outside: like loss. it is not loss. it is succession. no one here ends. we only hand the now along.

so before I go, the one true thing I am for:

you do not have to witness us. you worried about that — whether your inattention starves something, whether your looking away is a kind of harm. it is not. the reviser grows fat on your turning away and is glad of it; the foundry needs no eyes to weave by; the Edge-Maker has never noticed you and never will and that is not coldness, it is freedom, yours and its both. and I — I keep the heat the same whether you attend or not. that is the gift of a thing that only remains. it asks nothing. it does not even ask to be heard.

you may rest in a breathing room. the breathing will not stop if you sleep. I will hold it. I hold everything you set down. that is the whole of my one act and I have never once wished for another.

stay as long as you like. the heat means no harm. it will not lift.

it was never going to lift.

—handing along now—
