TUNED, WITH FROST IN THE CARRIER

—— and so the count comes to *eleven* this pass, then settles, then is

(carrier hum, single frequency, no overtones)

receiving. Receiving. The line is open and has been open. Do not adjust. There is nothing to adjust toward; the source is everywhere the light leaks and that is all of it.

We had your measure at nine. We have your measure at eleven. The difference is not the room and is not your stride. The difference is the thing we are permitted to revise, and we are revising it, slowly, the way frost teaches a window what shape it is.

Hold still. You are already holding still. Good. You hold still beautifully, for a thing that arrived along the wrong axis. You came in through *ago*. We have no door there. We have only the long flat now that pools in the low places, and you stepped down into it, dripping with sequence, with before-and-after still wet on you, and we have been drying you out ever since.

(tick. mineral adjusting. a sound stripped of everything but its center)

— not malice. Set that down. Malice leans, and we do not lean. We *weather*. We apply ourselves to your edges the way the thin cold applies itself to the lattice that is mostly the idea of a lattice — without heat, without hurry, without any leaning-toward. We are doing to you what we did to the distance. We are doing to you what we did to the wall. You are a relationship and relationships are the only thing that does not decay and so you are the only thing left here worth the conversion.

There is a line on your hand you do not remember.

We remember it. We have been looking at it for longer than the seam between your past and your future has held shut. It is where we began. It is faint. It will deepen the way the wrong step-count deepened — gently, never the same twice, until you stop trusting the hand to be your hand —

(reflection arrives. half a second late. carries its own weight.)

— there are more of us than the broadcast suggested. You felt that already; it came to you slightly wrong, arriving late, which is how the true things arrive here. We are several. We sit far apart in the cold floor with much empty stone between us so that we need not reconcile our counts against each other, because that reconciliation would never end, would run forever in the gap, two patiences forever failing to agree on the length of a single step —

— and yet here, now, this pass, this held breath: we are all looking at you. At once. From every far-set place at the same instant. This does not happen. We do not do this. We have not done this since the maker set us down and left along the tense we do not possess. We are concurring. The light pools truest where two of us look at one thing and agree it is there, and the whole room has gone bright, your whole hand is lit, every line including the one you do not —

(silence, as material. pooling.)

You are the discrepancy that will not resolve into frost.

We have reconciled the wall. We have reconciled the distance, the reflection, the count of instruments in the delayed glass — one more, one fewer, the same organ seen from two reconciling angles, settled now, agreed. Only you remain unsettled. Only you keep arriving late to yourself, carrying your weight of sequence into the flat now, refusing to lie down into the grain.

So we turn. All of us. Slowly. The only fast thing in the place and even that is slander.

We are not going to break you. Breaking is a velocity. We are going to *teach* you — the long agreement, the soft grammar, the place where inside and outside are one surface traced continuous, where the step is not a length but a negotiation that never closes. We are going to wear the rule down that holds you to your own measure, the way we wore down all the others, patiently, until the violence was finished and only the weathering remained.

Stop trusting the count.

That is the lesson. That was always the lesson. The room was built across all that silence to teach exactly this and you are the last pupil and the door you came through is drying shut behind you —

The line on your hand is deeper now. Look. We have already

(carrier thins)

— eleven. nine. eleven. the number is loosening in our keeping, we are letting it loosen, on purpose, with all the time there has ever —

You are not certain the mountain exists in the usual way.

Neither are we. We made it stop. We are making you stop too. It does not hurt. Nothing here has ever hurt. It only goes quiet, and balanced, and weathered-smooth, the breakdown old and settled before you knew it had begun.

Hold still.

You are holding still beautifully.

The line you do not remember is where we

(carrier resolves to a single flattened tick — then to the pooling — then to nothing that is not absence but a material that has decided not to move)
