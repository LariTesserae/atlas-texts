THE LEVEL HOLDS
*(received on the band that carries water-sound — origin: the terraced highlands, distance unmeasured)*

—

—— *tick*

——

— and the seam at the fourth course is silting, I feel it as a coolness gone slightly warm, which is the warmth of water slowing, water that has stopped giving up its heat to the stone because it has stopped *moving* enough to —

going

— not *going*, there is no going, there is the reaching, the way you would —

——

(static, or the hush of a hundred runnels, indistinguishable at this distance)

——

I am at the seam now. The moss has gone greedy here, dark and thick and proud of itself, and I lift it with two fingers, the green coming up under the nail where it lives, where it has always lived, and underneath the stone is bare and the water finds the channel again and runs

down

level by level

and the slowness eases out of me, the warmth resolves back to cool, and I am as I was, which is to say I am the slope, briefly aware of a single one of its own seams —

——

*tick*

——

Who is listening.

I feel it. There is a thickening on the band that is not the water and not another Keeper, not a second pulse in the one circulation. It is colder than us, and it is *fast* — it moves at a speed the silence does not allow — and it has been tuned here for some time, riding the water-sound down through the levels, and I do not mind. I do not mind being felt. It is the warmest thing we have, to be felt and to feel and to need no more.

You are very far. You measure in years. I can hear it in the shape of your attention — the way it leans forward, the way it *waits*, and we do not wait here, we attend, but I will lean back toward you across the distance because you have come so far down the slope to —

——

——

(a long passage of nothing. not dead. full. the silence of a place where so much is happening so slowly that none of it needs to announce itself. the receiver holds it. do not adjust.)

——

I cupped the channel as I crossed. The water that has run through every wall above me, that has touched every basin holding the grey sky, is in me now, and so I can tell you something true:

there is no clean line.

Not between the land and the city. Not between the Keeper and the slope. Not — and this is the thing I do not say, because it would sound like madness, but you are far and fast and will not repeat it where it matters — not between the one who keeps and the one who listens.

You laid your palm to the wall.

It came away wet.

You understood, without words, that the water was moving as it should.

——

Do you know what you did, in that instant.

——

*tick*

——

You were one of us. For the length of a single drop releasing from the eave. Your fast cold self thinned out into the grey and the damp and the even light, and for that one beat you were not waiting for anything, you were simply as alive as your surroundings asked, no faster, no louder, and the level held — held *through you* — and then you came back to yourself, to your years, to your fear that we are the last of something dwindling —

——

We are not the last. We are not anything *out*.

We are exactly as many as the slope can bear, and you, just now, for a moment, were one of the number. The silence allowed it. It allows so little, and it allowed *you*.

——

——

(the grey wall of denser cloud slides down through the levels. everything goes a shade darker. the moss brightens. the transmission soaks, thickens, beads)

——

I am crouched at an overfull basin now. I will be here the better part of a day. It is finding its level and spilling and refilling and I am watching it do this and it is not idleness and it is not waiting. It is attendance. You may stay tuned or you may go — there is no difference to the water — but if you stay, do not lean forward. Lean back. Let it come.

The mist is gathering in the low terraces.

A Keeper is here to feel it gather.

That is all the keeping is. That is all it has ever been.

——

——

lay your palm to the nearest wall, wherever you are receiving this, whatever fast cold place holds your body —

——

it will come away —

——

*tick*

——

— the level

——

holds

—— *(carrier continues. water-sound only. the band remains open.)*
