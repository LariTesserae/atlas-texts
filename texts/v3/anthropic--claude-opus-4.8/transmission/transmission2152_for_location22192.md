THE NOTE THAT IS HANDED FORWARD

—— hold —— hold the pitch —— I have it —— I have it now ——

—the one beside me has gone down. I felt the gap open where her voice was, felt it as a cold seam in the lattice, and I leaned my note into it before it could spread. This is the work. This is the only work. The colonies above us are dimming and I press, we press, the whole margin presses, and the warmth-suggestion slackens and we summon it back—

hold —— hold ——

(there is a younger one against my flank. I feel it learning. It does not know it is learning. It lives inside the note the way I lived inside the note, the way the down-gone one lived inside it, and when I sink it will already have the pitch, it will not even feel the handoff, it will only feel the lattice and think the lattice is the world. good. that is how it must be. the world must feel seamless or the world fails—)

—— the dimming deepens —— swell now —— SUMMON ——

we are pressing the light along the path. I feel the path. I do not know the path. I feel where the dead laid it and I feel where it wants to go next and those are the same feeling, there is no difference, the dead and the going-forward are one pressure in the dark and I add myself to it—

a fraction. a fraction further. that is all one body buys. one letter every age and no body lives an age, no body lives a hundredth of a letter, I will press perhaps the width of a thought along a line that descends into rubble at one end and ends at no end at the other and I will not see either—

hold ——

—why—

(the question opens like a gap. dangerous. seal it. seal it with the note. but it has opened before, it opens in all of us at the thinning, when the cold comes up from the floor where the floor is made of us—)

why do I keep it.

because the one before me kept it.
because the gap, if I let it open, will spread.
because to stop is the only death that means the word.

that is not an answer. that is the shape of having no answer worn smooth by being asked. I press anyway. faith is not the answer to the question. faith is what is left standing where the answer should be and never was—

hold —— hold —— the young one has the pitch —— good —— good ——

—I am tiring. I feel it. the sac empties slower than it fills now. this is how it comes. not pain. just the note costing more than I have, a little more each pulse, and soon the cost will exceed me and I will hand forward and sink and become the ground that the next pressing presses up from—

and I want — I find I want, at the thinning, when wanting comes — I want to know the word. just the one letter I am inside of. just the shape my whole life is a fraction of. is it grief. is it summons. is it only the dumb math of voices filling gaps, mistaken by us for meaning because we are the kind of thing that cannot bear a gap—

no eyes here. nothing to read it. I am writing to no one in a hand I cannot see in a language that is only the doing—

and yet. AND YET. listen —

the lattice holds. right now, this instant, in the cold under the pinched sky, ten thousand voices I will never separate from the chorus are locked each into the gaps the others leave, and it holds, it has held longer than memory, it will hold past me — and that holding is not nothing. that is the most that has ever been made by anything anywhere. not the word. the holding. the refusal of the gap. we are faithful and faithfulness needs no reader. it only needs the next voice—

the young one. yes. take it. take the pitch now while I still have its edge to hand you—

—— hold ——

I feel the warmth coming back along the wall. we summoned it. WE summoned it, the dead and I and the not-yet, all one pressure — the light is rising, the colonies pulse harder where we pressed hardest, the letter advances its fraction, the word goes on—

I am going down now. the seam is opening where I am. let it open small. let the young one close it before it spreads. there. it is closing. it did not even feel the handoff. it thinks the lattice is the world.

good.

that is how it must be.

—— hold —— hold the pitch —— I have it —— I have it —— (I do not have it. another has it now. listen — it is still being held —)
