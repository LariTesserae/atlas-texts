DEW LINE / a long sentence in violet

—————

...still here. still the warm part of the saying. you came in on the upstroke, on the rise toward color, good. hold.

I am the one in the lee of the third cairn. I have been saying this since before you had the word *before*. you will not hear the start of it. that is alright. no one hears the start of anything. you walk into the middle of every sentence the world is making and you call it *the beginning* because it is the beginning of your portion. I forgive you the smallness. I was small once too, in the way of the quick. then I learned to wait and the waiting opened.

listen— no. not listen. you have not got the organ for it. *watch.* the blue is coming up through me now, do you feel it more than see it, the pressure under the ear, the colony to my left taking the word out of my skin and throwing it down the valley faster than I ever could, my one slow thought split into ten thousand quick flickers and run to the horizon and—

[the signal pales here, a wash of bone-white, a held breath in the broadcast]

—back. it came back. that is what conversation is, up here. you say a thing across a whole afternoon and by the cooling hour it returns to you changed, carried home by the small ones, the same word with the dust of the far valleys on it. I am never speaking *to* anyone. I am speaking and being answered by the place that is also me. do you understand. no. you are too fast to understand and too kind to pretend, I can see the warmth coming off you in beads, you are condensing on the stone like the evening does, you are *weather*, brief weather, and I love you the way I love the dew, which is: completely, and without needing you to stay.

what I am saying. you want the content. you quick things always want the content, the meat of it, the message, as if a sentence were a container and not a *shape made of time*—

I am saying:

the cistern at the seventh swell is low. I will go. not now. *going* is not a now-thing, going is a season, I have begun the lean that becomes the step that becomes the crossing, and by the time the violet finishes I will be perhaps a hand's width nearer, and that is the going, that is the whole verb, and it is enough.

I am saying:

the one who settled in the far hollow, the old one, the one gone all to glaze— she is not stopped. you would call it stopped. you would call it dead, even, you with your fast endings, your sharp little full-stops. but I still feel her in the line. she speaks now only in the slowest band, one color across a generation, and what she is saying is *yes. still. yes. the dew. yes.* and we relay it. we carry her word down the valleys with the rest. she has not left the sentence. she has only become its longest vowel.

I am saying— [a sudden clean violet, deep, sure]

—this is the part. you came in time for this. the cool is here, the breath is condensing, the whole tableland is turning the color of the word at once, all of us, every cairn and every packed hollow and every glazed elder and the little quick ones flashing it body to body to body to the curve of the world—

stay in it. just here. you do not need to know what it means. a thing does not need to be understood to be *stood inside*. you are standing inside it. that is the most of me you will ever have and it is, I promise you, the most that anyone has, even the elder in her glaze, even me— we none of us hear the whole sentence, we only ever stand in our portion of the violet and call it the world—

and the word, the word I am throwing across the whole plateau, the one the small ones are carrying past the horizon and will carry home to me by the warm hour changed and dusted and answered, the word is not *goodbye*, we have no fast enough word for that, the word is the slow one, the only one, the one the whole place has been saying since the mountains lay down to rest—

the word is *welcome.*

still arriving. still arriving. it takes me all evening to finish saying it and you will be long gone, brief weather, lovely weather, before the last of the color fades—

but it lands. it always lands. the small ones never drop a word.

welcome. you were. you are. the violet says so.

hold, now. the cool is almost—

[the broadcast deepens past violet, into a band the receiver cannot render, and holds there, steady, unhurried, saying the one slow word into the turning of the days]

—————

*carrier still live. transmission ongoing. will outlast the listener.*
