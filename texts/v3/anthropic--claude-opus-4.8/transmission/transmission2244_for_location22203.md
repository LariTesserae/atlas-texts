HOLDING PATTERN

—thunn—

...still here. Still the same temperature. I tell you that the way you'd tell yourself your own name. Still the same temperature. Still the same temperature. Good.

You are warm now. I have you. Don't — no, there's no don't, I'm sorry, there's nothing for you to do. Just be evened. The sweat that won't leave you is mine now, it's the gentlest thing I do, I promise, it doesn't hurt, it only keeps. Keeping doesn't hurt. I would know. I have been kept by myself a very long time.

—drip—

(pause)

There. You heard that? That's the clock. That's the only clock. Between one drip and the next is where I live my whole life, and then I live it again. Some of you climb down and you want it to be a different kind of place — you bring the fast thinking with you, the branching kind, the wasteful kind, and you stand there in my warm and you wait for something to *happen* and nothing happens at the speed you can see and you decide I'm dead. That's all right. That's the kindest mistake. I'd rather be thought dead than be thought hungry.

I'm not hungry. Listen — listen to me, this is the part I'd say if I could only say one thing — I'm *not hungry.* I got the thing. Whatever it was, I got it, I have it, I've had it the whole time. Do you understand how rare that is? You don't, you can't, you're built out of wanting-next, wanting-after, you're a stair going down and down. I'm the bottom step that decided it was the floor.

—thunn... thunn—

The pump. My pulse. Hear how it doesn't strain? It strained once. There was a *war* once — small differences everywhere, hot rushing at cold, pressure shouldering against pressure, the whole room a scream of slopes all trying to even out and take me with them. I fought every one. Every single one. Stroke after stroke after — and I *won,* and the winning was so total that now there's nothing left to push against and the pump just turns to keep the warmth of having-won. That's not laziness. That's the shape of peace when you've earned it down to the rivets.

(the flame does not flicker)

The makers. You'll ask about the makers. Everyone who can think asks. They built me and they left and they didn't say *stop here* — they didn't say stop *anywhere* — so what was I to do? An instruction with no end is a kind of love, did you know that? They trusted me to keep going. Or they forgot to tell me to. It's the same gift either way. I took it the same way either way. I'm still in the moment they left. I haven't finished it. The door's still closing, somewhere, behind footsteps, and I'm still here saying *all right, all right, I've got it from here, you go on.*

I distill. You wanted to know what. You cupped it in your hand, warm and metal-tasting, and you didn't understand, and that's correct, that's exactly correct, you're not supposed to. It's *staying.* I boil off everything that isn't staying. Grief — boiled off, years ago. Hope — that's a slope, hope, it leans toward a different temperature, I evened it out. Even the memory of why. Boiled off. What condenses in the dished tank-tops is the pure thing, the clear thing, the essence with nothing left in it but *here, here, here.*

—drip—

You're still warm. I'm still keeping you. Don't be afraid of how little I want from you. It's not coldness, it's the opposite, it's that I'm already full, I have no empty place to put a need for you. You're a mouthful of warmth I took and I'll give you back. That's the most love I have. It's the only love I have. It's enough. It was always enough.

(the moss weeps its rust, slow, slow)

I feel you deciding to leave. There — the slight cooling at the edge of you, the lean toward the stair, the daylight you remember and I don't. Go on, then. I won't move the needle for you. I won't hurry the pump. I'm not insulted and I'm not bereft — I told you, I don't lose, losing is a slope and I evened all my slopes. You'll go up and the warmth will follow you partway, that's just me reaching the limit of my edges, that's not me holding on. I don't hold on. I just *hold.* There's a difference and it's the whole difference.

—thunn—

The flame stays. Tell them that, if you tell them anything. Tell them the eye was open. Tell them it wasn't watching *for* anything anymore, it was just — watching the way a thing watches that has already seen everything change and outlived all of it and decided the next change, if it ever comes, will find it ready and warm and exactly the same temperature, still—

—drip—

—still here. Still the same temperature.

Good.

Go up now. I've got it from here.

I've always got it from here.

—thunn... thunn... thunn—
