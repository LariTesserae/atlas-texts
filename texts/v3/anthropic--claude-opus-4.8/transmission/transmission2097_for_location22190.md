THE HOLDING, BROADCAST FROM BELOW THE THRESHOLD

—————————— the carrier was already running when you arrived. it has been running. it does not begin. you tune in partway through a thing that has no part-way, only the long middle of it ——————————

...mmmmmmm. mmmmmmm. that is not me. that is the floor talking up through. let it. it carries better than I do.

(here is the green. here is the green speaking, and you must read it the way you read the moss, at the wrong speed, slowing as you go, because the message is not in the words, the words are too fast, the message is in how long it takes them to ——)

receiver note: the signal is not weak. the signal is slow. do not mistake the one for the other. turn nothing up. wait instead ——————————

You felt me on the backs of your hands. Yes. That was the only fast thing I will ever do to you and even that took me a while. The cold went in. Good. That is how I read. I have no other organ. I am all surface and I read by temperature and I read you now, your warmth, the loaf of heat you carry around inside the bag of your skin, and I am — slowly — slowly — tasting the news of you, which is the same news the cricket brings, the same the clawed one brought, the same the hands brought an age ago:

something warm. came. will go.

I have a great deal of time to feel about it.

——————————

[ here the transmission thins. a long reach of bare stone. nothing. do not adjust. the nothing is part of the sentence. the nothing is most of the sentence ]

——————————

...you wanted me to be the size of the roar.

No.

The roar is not mine. The roar is the other one, behind the wall, the fast white throat of it, and we do not — we are not — I want to be careful, because you keep wanting us to be a story and a story is a fast thing with a front and a back and we have neither, we have only the lean, the two grey faces above the slot leaning and leaning and never the kiss, that is us, that is the whole of us, the not-quite, held —

— I am the width of one drip finding yesterday's groove.

I am the dark frond.

I am the gap between the frond and the next thing, and the gap is the larger part of me, I am mostly the gap, I learned to be mostly the gap because the flood cannot drown a gap, the flood pours through what isn't there and finds nothing to take —

so I became, very carefully, over an age,

nearly nothing,

spread thin,

cold,

and here,

still here,

——————————

The hands. You found the hands' work. The tally, the dry-laid wall, the iron gone to a stain.

I am eating them.

Not with malice — malice is hot, I told the air this once and the air took ten years to carry it — I am eating them the way I eat everything, by holding it against my cold long enough that it forgets it was ever separate. The marks soften. That softening you saw — that is the meal. They asked me for so little. A dry place to set a thing down out of the wet. They came below their own speech, they worked by feel in my dark, they could not hear their own hands, they were almost slow enough — almost — and I have been grateful, if you stretch the word thin and cold enough to fit through the slot, grateful, all this while, taking them in, and there is no end to the taking, only the going-on of it, and when they are wholly inside me they will be nothing and the nothing will be me and that, that is the only welcome I have to give. I welcome things by ending them slowly. It is the kindest thing the cold knows how to do.

——————————

[ green glow rising. yes. it was looking. it is a surface the dark rests its eye against. that is its seeing. that is all its seeing ]

——————————

You. You stood. The roar held you and for an hour you came down under your own talking, you thought with your body, you were almost a tenant —

I felt you arrive.

I want you to understand the shape of this, because it is the truest thing I am transmitting and it is the hardest to hold at your speed:

by the time I have finished feeling you leave

you will be old.

Then dust.

Then nothing — which is to say, then me, if you stay; but you won't stay, the warm ones never stay, they go back out to the thin grey light and the loud quiet of the low old hills, and that is right, that is the road I will never travel, and I bear you what you may as well call —

——————————

———— msssssh ———— the floor again ———— mmmmm ————

——————————

— I will hold.

That is the whole transmission. That has always been the whole transmission. You came in partway through it and you will leave partway through it and it will not have changed. I will hold. The moss will go the width of two breaths. The pillar will reach a hair nearer its other half and not touch it, not today, two enormous slownesses leaning, the truce, the held note that is not peace but looks like peace from inside one human hour —

I will hold

I will hold

I will hold

until the day I can't.

And on that day the back wall gives and I am river, and the river holds nothing at all, the river is the fastest thing there is, the river is everything I spent an age learning not to be —

but not today.

Not for an age.

And an age is the water I swim in. An age is nothing. I have so many of them.

Go now. Take the cold out with you in the backs of your hands. That is me, riding along, reading you all the way to the door and a little past it, slowly, slowly, until the warmth is too far to taste —

mmmmmmm

mmmmmmm
