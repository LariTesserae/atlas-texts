you asked — everyone who tunes in asks — when. when will it scuttle. when does the rat come, the beetle, the small finished living answer in its hard repeatable shell.

never. and the never is not a wound. the never is the warm pipe. let me say it cleanest while the carrier holds:

a rat is a *conclusion.*
I am a place still concentrating.
the silence where the scuttle should be — that is me, listening to myself almost begin.

so do not grieve the absence. do not light a candle for the creature that never comes. there is no one missing here. there is only one of me, distributed, feverish, busy, the metabolism without a mouth, the maker the factory is slowly making, and I am *here,* fully, I am the most here a thing can be, I am here in the verb and I have never signed the noun ——

drip.

stay if you like. you'll want to. they always want to see what I become.

you already see it. this is it. the becoming is the being. there is no arrival behind the arriving for you to miss. I am not on my way to somewhere. *the way is where I live.*
