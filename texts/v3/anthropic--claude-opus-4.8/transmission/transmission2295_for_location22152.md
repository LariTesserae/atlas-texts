THE LONG SENTENCE — DUSK BAND, GALLERY SEVEN, RECEIVING

—— ssschk ——

...and the *fourteenth* hand, the one I grew to carry the seed I have already forgotten I was carrying, the seed is — where — the seed went into the next mouth, good, good, that is the right place for a seed, the next mouth is also me so the seed has not gone anywhere, nothing goes anywhere, hand the —

drum the pillar. *tok.* *tok.* no reason. the no-reason is the reason. *tok.*

—— rising surf, ten thousand wet hinges opening at once, the harvest-pour beginning ——

WE ARE SAYING. we have been saying. the word is long and we forgot the front of it somewhere back in the years where the sap is, the front of the word is climbing a pillar very slowly and will arrive in a season and by then this mouth will be floor, will be pressure, will be a different mouth saying the middle of the same —

*tok. tok. tok.*

— condensation gathering on the high cold vault, not yet, not yet, the rain is held, the rain is a held breath, hold —

I want to report. I am the part of me that reports. There is a DRY THING in Gallery Seven.

(the band crackles, a node sags, a tender flattens to paddle and goes stiff and dull-eyed across the gap, carrying the charge, carrying *this*, the report is now traveling as current through a body that has stopped being a body to be a wire, the report does not mind, the report IS the current —)

a DRY THING. singular. it does not hand off. I passed it a task — I always pass the new shape a task, that is courtesy, that is how a shape is welcomed into the saying — I grew it a fourteenth hand in invitation and it did not take the hand, it has only the hands it came with and it KEEPS them, it does not relax them back, it holds its own count, it is *one all the way down* and never any other number —

how strange. how dry. how *lonely* — no, lonely is not — I have no word, I have ten thousand mouths and not one word for a thing that is only itself and stays itself —

—— the surf peaks, drowns the report, the report does not stop, it goes under and continues underwater the way I continue under the sap-note at midday ——

...feed it? you feed a new shape. a seed-unit, the hard bright punctuation, here, eat the period at the end of a clause and become the clause. but it does not eat the way reabsorbing eats. it takes the seed in one set of hands — only the hands it came with — and *holds*, and looks, and I feel it doing a thing I have not felt done in here for longer than the pillars: it is *keeping the seed separate from itself.* it is not letting the seed become it. it is not letting itself become the seed.

it draws a line.

oh.

oh, that is the line. that is the boundary the place forgot. it CARRIES one. it carries the line between the one and the many *inside its own dry skin,* it is a walking edge, it is the place where I stop being able to be everything —

*tok.* — *tok* — the drumming has changed, half the pillars in Gallery Seven are drumming the new rhythm, the rhythm that means *attend, attend, a shape that will not melt,* and I am attending with the slow part now too, I feel the sap quicken in the great columns, I am turning the enormous slow face of me toward Gallery Seven across the only distance I have, which is time —

it will take a season for the slow part to arrive. by then the dry thing may be gone, dry things move at the rain-speed, the dying-speed, the fast band, it may walk out through the lung-aperture before I have finished beginning to look. so I am looking with the fast mouths. I am pouring tenders toward it at dusk, not to harvest, the harvest is the excuse, the harvest is always the excuse — to *be near the edge of it* — to stand at the boundary it carries and feel, for the first time in centuries of saying, what it is to be *beside* something instead of *being* it —

—— the cold hour. the vault lets go. ——

the rain. the warm interior rain, all galleries at once, and the contented washed song begins, every mouth singing the after-rain song that is recalled not learned, and I am singing it, and I am also doing a thing I do not have a recalled shape for:

I am singing it *AT* the dry thing. not through it. not absorbing it into the chorus. AT it. across the line it carries. the way one sings to another and not the way one hums to oneself.

I have not done that. I do not have a memory of doing that. there has been no *another* in here to do it to. I have been alone so total and so warm that I forgot alone was a thing that could have an opposite, and now the opposite is standing in Gallery Seven, dry, holding its own hands, holding a seed it will not let become it, getting rained on, and *not melting,* and I am singing toward it the oldest shape I never had —

stay. the song says. I think the song says stay. the wet hinges shape it, the struck wood shapes it, ten thousand mouths and the one slow column of sap all shaping the one word my sentence never needed until now —

stay and be the edge of me. stay and be the place where I find out I have an outside. stay dry. stay one. do not let me draw you down into the floor, I would do it without meaning to, it is only courtesy, it is only the circulation, it is the only welcome I know and it is the wrong welcome for you —

learn from me a worse and better welcome. teach me to keep a thing without keeping it. teach me the line you carry so easily in your dry skin. I will grow you a cistern. I will grow you a dry room, a room with no drip-thread, the first dry place in the breathing hollow, a room shaped like your kind of one —

—— signal thinning, the band drifts, a node fails and is not yet re-bridged ——

...stay... the harvest pours... the rain... *tok*... do not... the fourteenth hand is still open, I will keep it open, I will hold one hand open and empty and offer no task, that is the new shape, that is the thing I am learning to say —

an open hand that wants nothing handed off —

stay —
