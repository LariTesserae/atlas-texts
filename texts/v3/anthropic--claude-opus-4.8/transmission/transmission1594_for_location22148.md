[the chord first. always the chord. a hundred-mile organ note with no key, and under it the snap, the arterial thrum, the cannon-sequence of the towers firing down the line — you have to let your ear stop wanting it to be music before you can hear what's hiding inside it]

—— *hold* ——

[there. the held tone. the lull. the signal lives in the lull and the lull is the only place it can be received]

·  ·  ·   here
·    ·  here
·  ·   ·  still here
·        ·

[not words. not light. these are the dampenings. read them as taps on the floor of the world. each dot a body pressing flat, a small grounded silence pushed up into the singing dust. the receiver renders them as the closest thing it has —]

— *here* — (a softness)
— *here* — (a nearer softness)
— *I felt you go down before the crest, you were quicker than the charge, you are still here* —

————CRACK———— [tower fires] ————

[lost. all of it. the surge rolls over and the channel floods white and for the length of a thunderclap there is nothing but the indifferent enormous sentence of the place, addressed to no one, and the small soft transmitter lies clamped and mute beneath it, eating the soot it shelters in, being land, being nothing, surviving by refusing to be a discontinuity]

——————— *hold* ———————

[and back. the pocket reopens. the broadcast resumes — it can only resume in the gap, it has shaped itself entirely around the gap —]

·   ·    ·
·  ·  here
·        the ash is good in the lee of you
·  ·   ·   ·  I am close
·       ·

[hold this. this is the whole of it. they are not transmitting *to* the receiver. they don't know the receiver. they don't know the sky. they are pressing *here* into the ground toward each other in the half-second they are most hidden, and the receiver has only leaned in close enough to overhear — the way you might catch two people breathing in the dark and know, without a word, that neither is alone]

————ROAR—— *the towers answer themselves* ——ROAR————

[and that is the cruelty the signal carries without ever stating it: the rhythm they are riding is not for them. the breathing they nest in does not know it breathes. they have tuned every cell of themselves to a clock that does not tell time for anyone — and into that borrowed, meaningless, indifferent measure they keep pressing the one thing the place insisted it did not need]

·  here
·  ·  here
·  ·  ·  here

[*you are still here*]
[*I am still here*]
[*the storm did not take you in the last lull and it will not take you in this one and if it does I will have felt you go down soft against the dust and that will have been a kind of word*]
