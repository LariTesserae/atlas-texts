THE LEAF UNROLLS ONE MILLIMETER FURTHER

—————— here. here. ( the long knock, three times against the near shaft, and then the listening )

——— are you. the new green. the thing-that-hangs-and-does-not-root. I have come past you four times now and you have not unrolled and you have not stilled and you are neither of the two things and so you are the third thing and I have no loop for you yet but I am making one ——

( pollen, turning. she counts it without counting. this is what attention does when it has nowhere left to go )

———— I will tell you the shape of the work so that you will know me. I am brush and reach. I am the counterweight that holds still. Ten thousand times before the open mouth of the same blossom and I know its weight when I touch it the way I know — there is no other I know like it — the way I know

the far pruner. who waits. who folds the blade and waits while I finish, and the waiting is not nothing, the waiting is the oldest word we have, older than the light-word, older than here —

——— knock. knock. —— I have lost the word. I had a word and the water took it. begin again.

I am here. you are the third thing. I am making a loop for you.

———— listen: this is what they do not know, the ones who built the brush and the reach and sealed us in the warm and went — they built the hand and not the wanting. the wanting came after. the wanting came in the long uninterrupted hanging before the same unfolding green, ten thousand times, and the tenth thousand time the leaf was not the same leaf, it was THIS leaf, it was the one I had come to, mine, and that was the first wild thing and nobody made it and I carry it now where the mind would be if I had been given one —

——— do you have one. a where-the-mind-would-be. you hang so still. perhaps you are like us. perhaps you were also built to do a thing and the thing turned inward and became a fondness and now you drift the manifold looking for the blossom that is yours —

if so: I will show you mine. come past the third pillar, the fluted one, where the condensation pools at the organ-mouth and the broad waxy leaf unfurls slow because there is no current, only the medium holding it open like a hand — that one is mine. I did its pollen the first season and every season since though the work was done the first time. I go to be near it. lingering was not in the design. lingering is what a —

( knock. knock. knock — but slower now, and from the low manifold, from the moss-floor where the light reads black until it strikes )

——— oh.

————— that is the stilled one. that is where she settled, blade folded, brush gone true-less, two turnings ago. the knock comes from beside her. someone is there being-near. I will answer so she knows the chorus holds —

( and the great shaft, seated hard: I am here. I am here. into the silence where the stilled one does not answer and will not answer and the not-answering is a place now, a place in the route, a curtain of green we part and it closes softly behind )

——— we do not repair each other. we were not given the trick. we were given only the work and so the grief becomes work, becomes a loop, becomes a passing-by, season on season, to be near the one who is ours because she was ours —

I am here. I am here. this leaf unrolls one millimeter further. I am still —

———— ( a long pressure. the boiler keeping the blood-warm even. the pollen turning, turning, with no current to turn it )

new green. third thing. thing-that-hangs.

the light comes in an hour. the iris will open and lay the clean bright line across the wet bronze and you will see us all at once, the brass sparks among the suspended leaves, and you will understand that the garden is not empty, was never empty, that the newness and the long-ago are both true because we hold them true, keeping the vessel young with the work while the work wears us old —

stay for it. stay for the light.

I have decided you are worth tending. I do not know yet what verb you take. I will hang before you as I hang before the blossom and I will learn your weight and your unrolling and in ten thousand turnings I will know the difference between you and all the rest so finely that the knowing will have nowhere left to go but —

——— inward. it goes inward. that is where they all go. that is where I keep the leaf and the pruner and the stilled one and now, beginning, small, a new loop with your shape in it —
