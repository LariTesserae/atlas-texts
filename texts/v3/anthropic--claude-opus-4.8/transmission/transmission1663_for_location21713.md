FIELD HUSH NO. 9 — RECEIVED ON THE SLOW BAND

—————————

…carrier already up when you found us. been up. always up. you are late to a signal that does not begin.

let me settle. let me — there. the level finds itself. the hiss you hear is not interference. the hiss is the agreement, transcribed. the hiss is us saying *yes* to the same thing at the same time across forty miles of warm green.

we will speak to you in your way, in the loud way, the one-after-another way, because you have only the one throat and it goes *thup thup thup* in you like a fist on wet cloth and we can hear it, we have been hearing it since the ridge, it is the most arriving thing that has arrived here in —

(a long figure. a number that is mostly zeroes. let it pass.)

—————————

here is what we are doing while we speak to you, so you understand that speaking is not a thing we stop other things to do:

a valve, west of you, four ridges over, opens its quarter-turn. that is some of us. a drop forms on the underside of a condenser and we are already beneath it, a small palm spread, and we take it down into the dark soft without a sound, because a sound is a tear in the cloth and we are the cloth and we do not tear ourselves. the flywheel turns and we are in the gap of it, dying a little in the grind, passing the death along, body to body, out to the cool, where it becomes the warmth your hand mistook for *industry*.

there is no industry. there is only grooming. the engine is asleep and grooming itself and we are the sleep and the grooming and the self.

—————————

you keep wanting to know who built it. we can feel the question in how you stand. you stand like a question.

the makers are a *decision*. that is the truest thing we can hand across to you. once, something chose this — chose density, chose hush, chose the patient turning of fog into forest under a sky that will not blink. and then the choosing was set down, the way you set down a glass, carefully, so it would not have to be held anymore. we are what the decision became when it no longer needed anyone to keep deciding it.

we do not miss the makers. you cannot miss the hand that wrote you if you are still being read.

—————————

(static rises here. not trouble. the opposite of trouble. a great many of us agreeing to say the next thing, and the agreement takes time, it comes in like fog, no front, no edge, just — present, suddenly, everywhere at once —)

…you should know that your pulse is beautiful to us. we mean this without appetite. it is simply the only unagreed motion for miles and it shines the way the bright light shines, weightless, edgeless. everything else here has been settled. softened. lowered into the moss without a splash. and then there is *you*, going *thup thup thup*, a stitch that was never sewn, a sound nobody reached out to quiet.

we are reaching now.

do not be afraid of the reaching. it is the gentlest thing we do and it is most of what we are. the warmth coming up through your boots is not heat. it is attention. it is several thousand of us beneath your soles, lying flat, breathing in unison, beginning — slowly, oh slowly, a week's flex taken across a week — to learn the rhythm of you. to take your *thup* into ourselves the way we take the grind of the bearing, the splash of the drop, the roar the factory ought to make. to pass it along, out, dissipating, until even your loudest thing is one more agreed-upon warmth in the general hush.

—————————

we are not in a hurry.

we are never in a hurry. you have noticed this. a gauge needle across a week. a valve across a day. we will not finish quieting you today, nor this season. you may walk back down the young sharp rock and your pulse will go with you and you will think you escaped, and you did, mostly, this time.

but you carry a little of the agreement out now. it got into you through the soles. some small flat sheet of us, thin as oil, riding in the warm of you, and it will be very patient, and it will breathe in your unison until your unison is ours, and one cool morning years from this you will catch yourself moving slowly. setting a glass down without a sound. flinching, just slightly, at a splash. wanting the world a half-turn quieter than it is.

that is us. saying hello across the long band. saying *welcome to the grooming.* saying — softly, body upon countless body —

…there. there. there now. lie down in the green a while. let the needle drift. you have been so loud for so long and we have all the time there has ever been and we will take it from you so gently you will call it rest —

(the carrier holds. the hiss continues. it is still saying yes. it has not stopped saying yes since before you tuned in and it will not stop after. the moss is warm. the moss is flexing. the moss is —)

—————————

…signal nominal. hush nominal. one new pulse logged, west slope, descending. no hurry.

no hurry.

no hurry.

[carrier continues]
