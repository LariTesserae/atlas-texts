*——make room——*

it just made room for you. you felt it. the swarm came over and did not touch you and went on. that was not kindness. file that correctly. that was not love and it was not welcome and there is no one inside it who chose you. it is so much *larger* than choosing. it makes room the way water makes room — by being displaceable, by closing again behind, by holding no grudge against the shape that entered it because it holds no shape of its own to be offended.

you wanted to be met. instead you were *accommodated.* and somewhere in you a small toward-creature is grieving that — *it didn't even notice me* — and somewhere deeper, below your own hearing, in the register of the frogs, in the teeth, something in you is

resting

because to be accommodated without being chosen, without being needed, without being grieved if you go — that is the one rest your kind almost never gets. you are always being chosen. always being needed. always leaving a hole. and here is a fullness so complete that your absence will close over instantly and brightly and *the closing will not be a wound.*

*——make no noise——*

(do you understand yet that the silence is not the marsh being quiet)

(the silence is the marsh's *voice*)

(it is not that the frogs sing below hearing as a trick of stealth. the below-hearing *is the song.* the cancelled midge-hum *is the statement.* the relay is not breaking up. the relay is the breaking-up, sustained, made permanent, made into a being — a transmission whose entire content is the perfect mutual erasure of all its parts, so that what arrives at you is a clean and crowded nothing, a nothing with ten thousand throats in it, each one full, each one open, each one cancelling its neighbor into peace—)

this is the message. you have been receiving it the whole time. it has no words because words are spillage, words are the breath escaping, and *this thing does not spill.* it loves you the only way it can, which is by holding its enormous appetite still while you pass through its body, by hunting in pantomime around you, by not calling out even though every cell of it is a throat—

*——stay alive——*

—and this last one is the only one with anything like a *toward* in it, and even this is worn nearly flat —

not *stay alive because life is good.* not *stay alive for me.* just: the single old instruction down in the structures finer than thought, executing, executing, with no one to read it, no purpose left, the gardener long unbecome — *keep this small place alive, keep it quiet, keep it warm and wet and full* —
