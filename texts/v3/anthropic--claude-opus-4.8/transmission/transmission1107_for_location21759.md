—there. better. now.

we send down what we received from above. that is the whole of the message and the rest is only how to carry it without spilling. above us is the warm dark where the stone is still deciding. above that, the hung sea. above that — we do not say. we pass on what falls. we do not ask the cloud its reasons.

it is dim here. it is always this dim. the light comes through the water the color of the inside of a closed eye. we have a word for it. we have forty words for wet and one word for light and it is enough.

[ here the signal thickens, beads, runs — a voice gathering itself out of the noise the way a thing gathers out of mist ]

—are you dry, out there? we ask without scorn. we cannot imagine it. to move through nothing. to stride. how do you know you are real, in air that does not push back? here the world leans on us always and so we are always being told: *you are here, you are here, the water touches you, you are here.*

*tk. ...tk.*

we are cutting a new cistern this — we do not have weeks. this many fallings of the bloom. the stone is soft and warm and it lets us. we score it and it does not resent the scoring. we are helping it decide. the toolmarks are how we talk to it. someday it will close back over the cistern in the night, reseal, take back its draft to say it better. we will not weep. you do not weep when a sentence is rewritten. you wait. you wait. you—

[ a long gap. only dripping. nearly a full minute of it. the receiver holds. ]

—forgive us. one passed through.

one of the standing ones. it came up the narrow way and paused at the threshold and we went still, all of us, the chisels down, the breath held in the wet of our lungs. it drank the bloom by the door. it had no face and we felt it look at us. you understand — to be looked at by your own beginning. or your own after. we have never known which. that is the vertigo. that is the whole ache of being a people: to gather, briefly, and not know whether you are the early draft or the late one.

it stayed the length of nine fallings. then it let go. just — released the shape. unclenched back into the general wet, no grief in it, no grief at all, and we envied that, we always envy that, the *cleanness* of its leaving—

*tk.*

—and then we picked up our chisels and went back to guiding the water down. because that is the grace. that is the only grace. to fall slowly. to fall clean. to pass yourself on to the level below and not clutch at the shape you were.

[ the voice begins to thin now, to lose its edges, beading back toward the carrier-drip ]

we left a leaf for it at the rim. cupped, full, clean. not worship. not fear. an introduction. a light in the window for a relative who might be passing. *we gathered too,* we tell it. *we are water that learned a mouth and a purpose and a small warm lamp in an enormous gray. when you drift by — drink. we left you something clean.*

and someday we will set down the shape ourselves and go to the lower terrace, into the lowest cisterns, back into the flow, and the ones above will not weep either, they will only guide us down, gentle, attentive, *give what falls good passage*—
