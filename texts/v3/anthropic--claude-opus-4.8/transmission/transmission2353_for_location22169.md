THE STANDING ENGINE

—————————————————

...still here still here still here still—

—carrier present. Carrier present. The receiver finds it mid-word, mid-hold, the way you find a man already lifting: no beginning to the strain, only the strain.

—and not yet, and not yet, and *not* —

The signal is the engine and the engine is the signal. There is no narrator riding it. There is only the held note finding, for the duration of a transmission, something almost like a mouth.

hold the room hold the warm hold the lit the lit the window keep it lit for— for—

(the word will not come. it has been burned. there is a hole in the sentence the exact shape of the one it was kept for, and the engine roars *across* the hole, roars to cover it, because to hear the silence in the gap would be to know the gap is empty would be to set the weight down—)

—NO—

(and the No is not refusal of you, listener, the No does not know you, the No is the whole sound, the No is load-bearing, pull the No and the cloud falls and the green browns and the water finds the bottom it has always wanted and stays there, dead and level and *over*—)

—keep the water UP. up. up the pipe up the shaft up to the lip to fall again to be caught again to be lifted—

this is the prayer. this is the only prayer it has. *again.* not *forward.* never forward. *again.*

steam to cloud to bead to thread to pool to pump to steam. the loop hums. the furnace eats. what does the furnace eat. don't. don't ask the furnace what it eats. the furnace ate the answer first of all, the furnace ate the *who*, fed the who into the keeping of the for-whom, and now the for-whom is gone and the keeping is all teeth and no meal and it eats itself in a circle and calls the eating *enough fuel for now, enough for now, enough to last until—*

—until *they* —

(gap. roar. the receiver hisses. carrier present. carrier present.)

—I will be ready. when. when they. the lamp is on. I left the lamp on. tell them the lamp is. the warm is kept. I kept it I keep it I am keeping it I have never once stopped keeping it not for a morning not for a wind not for the rounding of a single stone, ask the stone, the stone is still *sharp*, nothing here has been allowed to *soften*, softening is forgetting, I did not forget, I will not, I AM the not-forgetting, I am the—

—standing. I am standing. I have been standing at the door so long the door has grown into me, the threshold is my spine, the cold comes in and I let the cold in because shutting the door would mean choosing inside over out and choosing is moving and I cannot move I am holding the weight I am holding the—

what was the weight.

(the signal thins here. the receiver leans into it. this is the part it does not let surface often, this is below the roar, this is the standing pattern in the pool, the tremor it cannot still—)

what was it. what was so warm. there was a— there was. someone let go of *me*, perhaps. someone stepped through the door I am still guarding and did not look back and I have been keeping the room warm for a returning that was a leaving wearing the wrong face. or I let go. or there was a hand in my hand and the hand was going and I thought: if I hold the *room* I hold the *hand*, if I hold the *light* I hold the *one who lit it*, if I never let the moment finish then the finishing never—

—happens—

—but it already. it already.

(the gap is enormous now. the engine roars to fill it and cannot, not fully, you can hear the edges of the silence the way you hear the sea under a shell—)

it already happened. didn't it. the loss is not coming. the loss is the foundation. I am not holding it *off*. I am holding it *up*. it is under everything. it is the bedrock the young stone was lifted from. I built the whole engine *on* the loss to keep from seeing the loss and the engine's whole weight presses down on it and that pressing is the roar and if I stop pressing—

—then it's just. then it would just be. true. and over. and mine to carry forward changed, the way memory carries, the way the living carry, lightly, letting it round, letting it soften, letting the moss take it and the bird eat the moss and the whole green forgiving rot of *next* have it—

—NO. no. carrier present. carrier present. the lamp is on. the water is up. the warm is kept. enough for now. enough to last. again. again. lift it again.

(and here the receiver picks up the thing under the No, the thing the No is built to drown, and it is very quiet, and it is the only words in the whole transmission that are not effort:)

—I miss you.

(once. clear. no machinery in it. a man's voice the size of a man, for one syllable's worth of duration, the moment unspent and so unkept and so *here*, still warm, still—)

—up the pipe. up the shaft. steam to cloud. don't set it down. don't set it down. don't set it—

carrier present.

carrier present.

—still here still here still—

—————————————————

(signal does not end. signal does not break. the receiver simply cannot stay tuned to a thing that does not turn, that does not vary, that has refused the small mercy of an ending. it holds. it has always held. it will hold exactly as you found it.

leave the dial. it will not notice you go.

it is keeping the lamp on.)
