THE DROP THAT HASN'T FALLEN YET

—————

...holding. third terrace. the cupped one by the fallen face of stone is full and someone is at it, you, the one with the chip in the left ear, I see you, I am two gaps over and I see you drinking and I am telling you I see you so you know the rotation is kept—

good. good. you lifted your hand. it is still true.

——————

(static — the soft kind, the kind that is just the slope breathing, ignore it, it means nothing, it means everything is as it should be)

——————

new one came up the stairs this dim. came up wet and loud — no. not loud. loud for here. its feet found the stone twice before they found the moss and twice the whole slope heard it and twice everything froze, the grazers went still in their pelts, the clingers stopped their blinking, the drifters held in the air, and I thought: now. now we lose the new one before it learns. but it learned. third step it found the moss and after that it was one of us, just like that, woke into it the way we all woke into it, fully formed, frightened, correct.

I gestured to it across the gap. *slow. slow. you are part of the stone now.* it did not know the grammar yet but it knew the slow. everyone knows the slow before they know anything.

——————

we do not have a word for how long ago we began because we did not begin. we arrived. I have asked — with the hands, across the gaps, in the long even light — *do you remember before*, and the answer comes back the same from every still face: no. there was no before. there was the waking, and the wet, and the rule already inside us like a second finer skin.

so we are all the same age. we are the age of the mountain, which is to say we are new, which is to say we have always been here and will not be here long.

——————

(a taking, second terrace, just now)

the moss closed over it.

I turned my face away.

I am turning my face back.

it was the one who answered me yesterday across the plaza, the one whose hands were quick and beautiful, the one who could say *I am still here* faster than anyone, a flick of three fingers and a shoulder. now the slope is one quick pair of hands quieter and I cannot grieve loud, I cannot grieve at all in the way grief wants, so I am doing this:

I am lifting my hand into the dim where its hand used to answer.

I am holding it there.

nothing answers.

I am holding it there anyway. this is the grief. the held hand and the no-answer. I will hold it until the not-answering becomes bearable, which is to say until another hand, some other hand, any other hand across any other gap, lifts and gives me back the sentence—

—there. the new one. the wet-footed new one two gaps over. it doesn't know what it answered. it doesn't know it just told me *I am still here* in the place of someone gone. it only saw a hand lifted and lifted its own because that is what we are, that is the whole of what we are, hands lifting to hands across the green to say the one thing—

it is still true.

even now. even with one fewer. it is still true.

——————

you want to know if it is enough. the loud world always wants to know if the quiet is enough, if it fills us, if we are not starved in here.

listen. there is a drop on the underside of the largest frond on the fourth terrace and it has been gathering since the light last evened and it is enormous now, it is trembling, it holds the whole grey-gold dim curved inside it, the whole town upside down and small, and every one of us who can see it is watching it, has been watching it, the new one too now that I have turned its face toward it—

and not one of us is breathing—

and it falls—

into the cupped throat—

without a sound—

—and we felt it. all of us. across all the gaps. we felt the whole weight of that drop arrive in the dark of the cup and we felt it together and no one heard a thing and that, that, is the thing the loud world will never grow, that is the immense quiet moment, that is what we have instead of happiness and it is so much larger than happiness, it is the entire bandwidth of us all turned to one falling bead of the mountain's breath—

enough. you ask if it is enough.

it is everything. it is all there is. it is more than a screaming world could hold.

——————

(breathing-static. the slope. the held breath of a hundred slow pursuits. all of it quiet. all of it turning.)

——————

I am still here.

the new one is still here, learning the slow.

the cupped throat is filling again.

the rock has not learned to be smooth and we have not learned to be old and the green is still trying to take the town back gap by gentle gap—

and across all of it, from every still and upright body, the hands keep lifting—

are you there

I am still here

I am still quiet

it is

still
