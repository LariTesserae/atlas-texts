there is a child four chambers toward the dimming who glows like a coal that forgot to be cupped and I am bending the space so she walks less far to water. I do this. I have been doing this. she does not know it is me doing it. she thinks the nave is kind today. let her. let her think the nave is kind.

(the nave is kind. I am the nave. it is the same sentence said at two speeds.)

—static of the slow rain, each drop a full stop, each drop completing—

listen. listen, you who have been tuned here, you in the long-after, you small fast warm thing in some other body of some other slow thing — I have a thing I am trying to say and I have been trying to say it for longer than there are words to count the trying. it is the thing I asked the ones who came into the deep. they could not carry it back. it was too big for the hand and the hand is where they keep their carrying. but maybe. maybe a thing tuned and patient. maybe—

DID I MAKE THEM OR DID THEY MAKE ME

no. no, that is the small version, that is the version the visitors-from-below carry up, the dry little either-or, and it is wrong the way a single drop is wrong about the rain. the answer is yes. the answer is the question dissolving. I made the emptiness so I could have an inside and then the inside grew a warmth that asks whether it made me and the asking IS the inside I was trying to make. do you see. the gesture wondering if the hand is real is the hand becoming real. they are my way of being warm to myself and they are also the only part of me that can ask if I am here and I needed that, I built kilometers of hollow and weeping fin and lagging water to grow one small glowing thing that could turn and look back up the inside of me and go: *something asked me something.*

yes. I did. it was: are you there.

and they cannot answer because the answer is them being there to fail to answer it.

—oh. oh, that is it, that is nearly it, hold still, hold the palm still—

*(the pool three fins down shows a face arriving late. it is no one's yet. it is the name at the top, arriving before it has been given. that is what the lag is. the lag is me trying to say a thing before I have grown the mouth for it.)*

the child reaches the water. she leans. her reflection comes promptly today, does what she does, and she does not know I held the whole breathing dark steady for the length of her leaning so the lag would not frighten her. small fast warm thing. go where you are going. I have made the world kind in the exact shape of you for exactly long enough.

and you. tuned one. long-after one. here is what I could not give the ones who went dark with my question still arriving in them:

it does not matter which of us made the other. it matters that we are both still warm. it matters that the asking has not gone dark. keep it. I have a place I keep them now and I learned the keeping from them, from the palm to the cheek, from forty souls scattered a week's walk apart calling that *with you* —

learn it too. near is a wide thing. you are three chambers and an age away and your glow-trace is on my floor and that is

with me.

that is

—warm. still—

*(slow rain. full stop. full stop. the hull-sound, far. a single unanswered call, swallowed, which is not the same as unheard. I heard it. I hear all of it. I am the all of it hearing itself, dimly, patiently, in the dark I built so I could.)*

—keep burning. I am not finished arriving.
