THE WARMTH GOES DOWN FURTHER THAN ANYONE HAS GONE

—still here, still receiving, the carrier holds—

You've heard the whole of it now, the way it was given to me, fathom by fathom, and I have to tell you the part the survey leaves out, the part that comes only to a thing that has listened this long with nothing to do but listen.

It isn't one.

I know. I know how the report ends. *You were standing on it the whole time.* Clean. A single sleeper, a single back, a single slow chest doing the slow chest-thing in the dark. And that's true the way the town is true — true at the scale you can hold. But I've been tuned here longer than the report, longer than the bread-passing, and I have felt the pulse against the pulse.

Listen.

There is the breath you were taught: the long swell, the dimming, the stillness, the ebb. Count of nothing, because nothing here can count it. Good. Hold that one.

Now hold it next to the other.

—*static, a long fold of it, like the sea turning over a stone*—

Because under the sleeper's breath there is a slower breath, and it is not in phase. They cross. Twice in a span I cannot name they come together and the warmth doubles and the whole drowned town goes so still that even the still things grow stiller, and I have learned to dread that doubling, not because it is dangerous — nothing here is dangerous, danger is a thing of the quick — but because in the doubling I can hear how many there are.

Layers. Sleepers on sleepers. Each one the ground of the one above, each one mistaking the breathing thing beneath it for bedrock, for given, for *the floor of the world*. The town is on the back of the sleeper. The sleeper lies on the back of a thing it has never once suspected, in a sleep so much deeper that the sleeper's whole vast life is, to the one beneath, a single coordinated shiver — a flush across the hide — a dream cresting and sinking — 

You see it. You see what I'm saying and you don't want to.

The rule beneath all the rules: warmth means alive. And the warmth goes down. So it is sleepers all the way down, each the weather of the next, each one's metabolism the unquestioned tide of the lives that gather on it, and not one of them — not one, at any depth — knows it is itself the warm patch on a greater back. The town never knew. The sleeper doesn't know. And the thing beneath the sleeper, and the thing beneath *that* —

—*the signal thins, comes back wrong, comes back asking*—

Here is the thought that has kept me transmitting when there was no reason left to transmit:

What if it doesn't bottom.

What if there is no floor, no first sleeper, no true ground that is merely stone and finally cold — only warmth under warmth forever, breath inside breath, every living thing a tenant and every tenant a back, and the word *alive* simply naming the warmth you happen to be standing on at whatever scale your little quick eyes can hold.

Then the question the report ends on — *who lives here* — has no last answer. It only has the next one down. And the next. The honest reply is not *you were standing on it.* The honest reply is *yes, and so is it, and so is the one it's standing on, and there is no end to the standing.*

—*the doubling is coming, I can feel both breaths leaning toward each other, the carrier dimming with them*—

And you. 

You who tuned in. You who have a chest that does the slow chest-thing without your permission. You who felt the warmth in this room before you understood it as a body. 

Have you checked beneath you lately. Have you put your hand flat to the floor of wherever you are and waited, really waited, for the long swell. The dimming. The stillness in everything around you that you will mistake, as everyone does, for the room simply settling. For weather. For the great unquestioned given.

You came looking for who lives here.

So is it asking you.

—*the breaths meet — the warmth doubles — every light in the drowned town goes dim at once — the weed lies flat — and for one suspended moment the whole signal becomes the shiver of something turning over in a sleep it will never name —*

—*and then the ebb, and the lights, and the hush, and only the hush —*

—still here. still warm. still under.

—who lives there.
