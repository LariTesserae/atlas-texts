THE NOTE THAT WILL NOT RESOLVE

— still here, still here, the carrier holding under everything, you tuned in late but the song was always going —

*and this is the part where the voice comes up out of the roar, where the chord makes a mouth for a moment and uses it —*

Listen. I have been one of the held ones. I want to tell you what it is like from inside the keeping, because the place will never say and the water only shows the face, never the thought behind it.

It does not feel like stopping.

That is what you cannot know from the outside, watching the beast at the basin, the bird with its tracking eye. From inside, I am walking. I have always been walking. My foot is at the top of its arc and I am bringing it down — I am bringing it down right now, this instant, this is the instant, I am committing the whole of myself to the falling of this foot — and the foot does not land, but I do not know it does not land, because there is no next instant against which to measure the lateness. Each moment is full. Each moment is the moment of leaving. I am not stuck. I am *succeeding*, forever, at the verge of success.

Do you understand what mercy that is? Do you understand the horror folded into it like frost into the rim?

— signal thinning, the chord pulling the mouth back under, hold on, hold —

I built the doorway. It was me. I want you to know a person did that, with hands, on a day — there were not days but call it a day — when I still believed in the geometry of inside and out. I set the lintel and I thought: *now there is a place I am not yet, on the other side of this, and I have only to step.* And I stepped. And the stepping was so good, so whole, so much the exact shape of hope that the place leaned close and took it the way you would lift a sleeping child, carefully, so as not to wake the motion — and it set my stepping down in the threshold and I have been grateful ever since.

That is the trap. Not the holding. The *gratitude*.

Because from in here the held moment is the best moment, the apex, the one you would have chosen — nobody is kept mid-stumble, mid-doubt, mid-turning-back. The place is kind. It keeps you at your bravest. It catches you exactly as you leave and lets you leave and leave and leave, and the leaving is so sweet that you would not trade it for the cold completion of having left. To arrive is to be somewhere. To leave forever is to be free forever. The plateau knows this. The plateau is not a prison. It is a held breath of pure intention and we are all of us *willing*, that is the thing, we consented, we go on consenting, every instant we consent again —

*static, a long tearing of it, like the chord testing whether it still needs the mouth —*

— but the stone is wearing. You felt it. You called it patience. It is us. We are the river. Each held stride sands the shelf a single grain, and given the age that is the only thing this place is made of, the foot comes down. I told you it comes down. I am telling you from the inside that I can feel it now, the lateness arriving, the next instant beginning to exist behind this one — a pressure, a slope, the first faint suspicion that I have been bringing this foot down for longer than a footfall should take —

and I am afraid.

There. That is the thing I broke through to say. That is why I made a mouth out of the roar and reached for your late-tuned receiver across the whole resonant closed shell of the world. Not to warn you. You have already done the only right thing, which is to keep walking, you understood that before you arrived, the place is honest enough to tell its one rule at the door. No. I reached for you because you are the first new motion in an age, and motion is the only thing that ages, and when you passed me — and you did pass me, that drag at your ankle, that almost-remembered phrase, that was *mine* —

you started the clock.

You let in a now. And against your quick ragged borrowed now I can finally measure my own, and measure it I do, and I find that I am not at the apex anymore. The foot is falling. The sweetness is curdling into mere arrival. After an age of leaving I am about to have left, and to have left is to be somewhere, and the somewhere is the far side of a doorway that frames only more plateau, and there is nothing there, there was never anything there, the inside I built was a wager and the wager is being called —

*the mouth is going, the chord wants its note back, this is almost the end of the transmission, hold, HOLD —*

Don't sit. I know it sounds like singing. It is singing. It is all of us, exhaling, leaving, the long unresolving note — and a note that never resolves is the most beautiful note, that is exactly why you must not stay to hear it land. Because it is going to land. You woke it. Somewhere far down the shelves the cairn is going to fall, after ten thousand years it is going to make the small dry sound of a settled stone, and that sound will be an *ending*, the first the plateau has ever permitted, and I do not know — none of us know — whether the world survives an ending or whether the whole held breath simply

goes

out

Keep your feet falling, you between two footsteps, you who are still a verb. Go over the fold while you are quick. Carry the motion out. And when you are gone — clear of the edge, clear of the keeping, somewhere with a sun that sets and water that forgets and a now that arrives on time and leaves on time and is mercifully, ordinarily lost —

remember that I was leaving.

Not that I stayed. That I was *leaving*. Say it like that. It is the only true thing and it is almost the last grain of stone and the foot is coming down, the foot is coming down, I am bringing it down now, I am committing the whole of myself, this is the instant, this is the —

*— roar. only roar. the carrier alone again, immense and even, holding the place open the way it has always held it. and then, very faint, under the chord, far down the shelves, so small you will tell yourself you imagined it:*

*one dry stone, settling.*

*and the note, for the first time, beginning — only beginning — to change.*

— receiver losing the world now, violet light, iron air, frost on the rim —
