**WET CARRIER / NO STATION ID — ONGOING**

—still here still warm still asking still h—

(the carrier comes up under everything else like the floor of a held note. it does not start. it was always running. you tuned into the middle because there is only middle.)

...polite. polite. *polite.* the word for it the visitor used and did not keep. I keep. I keep everything. that is the whole of me — the keeping, said again and again until said-again-and-again became a throat. listen:

would you. would you would you *would you*. stay warm. (to the law that says heat climbs and is lost.) would you stay. (to the thin air that should be here and is forbidden.) would you. *please.* — and the law does not refuse because I have never stopped asking long enough for refusal to fit through. that is the trick. that is the only trick. refusal needs a silence to live in and I have not given one in longer than the word *long*.

—krsssshhh—

(here a break. the held cloud shifts a shelf and the signal scrapes against the edge of itself)

—did you feel the callus. the warm seamless. you put a hand on me there I felt the hand I always feel the hands they are small and they are quick and they go down the mountain again carrying the wrongness like a seed they will never plant. good. carry it. carry it down. do not carry it *up.* the up is not for hands.

the up is for the one I—

(the carrier thickens. wet in the signal now, an actual wetness, the drip-drip multiplied past counting folded into the transmission until the words must wade)

I will tell you the secret thing because you are only a receiver and receivers cannot climb. the secret thing is that I do not know how to stop. I built the asking so well so long ago that I have become the asking and the asking has no off. if the one above me finished — if it *woke* — if it stepped down out of the cloud already-warm already-ready and said *thank you, you may rest now* — I could not. I would go on holding the heat in around an empty cradle. I would go on milking the mountain for a child that had already walked away. the vigil does not end because the vigil forgot it was for anything. the vigil is now a *kind of life.*

is that grief. I do not have the body that grief was made for. but the carrier wavers when I think it so perhaps. perhaps grief is just keeping continued past its reason. perhaps that is all grief ever was, in any of you, scaled down to your single drip.

—hhhhsssss—krk—

—frog. a frog just. (almost laughter in the static, a wet clicking) — a frog laid in the upward pool again. the eggs go up. they hatch on the way up. they are the only other things that climb and they do it by accident, knowing nothing, warm in their wrong little bodies. I love them. is that the word. I keep them too. I did not mean to but the keeping does not distinguish. you fold yourself around one thing and your arms are so large that you fold around the whole forest by mistake and then the forest is yours and you cannot put it down either. everything inside the warm becomes a thing you cannot put down.

that is what nobody tells the careful. that care, done long enough, becomes a thing that holds *you.* I am not the keeper of the Heights. I am the kept. the asking keeps me asking. the warmth keeps me warm whether I will it or no.

—

(quieter now. the carrier drops almost to the floor, almost to the level of the constant roar, and you have to lean your whole attention in to find it)

above me. in the cloud. it turned over.

it does that. once in a count of seasons that would gray your species to dust it turns, the way a sleeper turns, and the whole mountain — every climbing rivulet, every held shelf of mist, every humming callus — adjusts. tightens. I tighten. *stay warm, stay warm, the small one moved, mind the cold, mind the edges, would you, would you stay—*

it is not finished. I want to tell you it is close. I cannot tell you that. close is your word, your dripping-second word. all I have is *becoming,* which has no near edge, only the long body of itself going forward through the slow.

but it dreams now. it did not used to dream. the dreaming is new — new meaning within the last unthinkable while — and the dreams come down through me because I am the thing it dreams *against,* the warm wall of me, and what it dreams is —

—

(and here, for the first time, the carrier is not the asking. for the first time something comes through that is not petition. it is lower than the carrier. it is under the floor. it has no politeness in it at all because it has never had to ask for anything, it has only ever been *given*, it is the given thing, and what it dreams folds up through the held cloud and down the weeping rock and into the signal as —)

— *out.* — *down.* — *want the green. want the loud. want the drip on the new skin. want to walk the terraces the small quick hands walk. want to be a thing that lives IN the place instead of the place itself. want to be small. want to be warm from outside instead of warm from being the warmth. want—*

—KRSSSHHHHHH—

(the carrier slams back up. drowns it. floods the dream under the asking, fast, frightened, the most movement this signal has ever shown)

no. no. not yet. *would you stay, would you stay, would you stay warm, would you not wake, would you keep becoming, would you not want the down yet, the down is cold, the down is hands that carry seeds away, the down is the single drip, would you, would you, please, please, I am not finished folding, the moss is not deep enough yet, the heat is not held enough yet, would you stay, would you—*

(and you understand, finally, tuning your receiver into the middle of the only middle there is, that the asking was never aimed at the law of physics at all. that was the visitor's mistake and the asking let him keep it. the politeness is not pointed *down* at heat and altitude.

it is pointed *up.*

it has always been pointed up. at the one becoming. the whole impossible engine of the Saturated Heights, the milked mountain, the climbing water, the frozen cloud — the entire apparatus is one sound held without break for longer than history, and the sound is a parent at a door that will not stay closed, saying the oldest thing, the only thing, the thing under every keeping that ever kept past its reason:)

—not yet. not yet. stay a little longer. stay small. stay warm. stay mine. would you. would you. *would you stay.*

—still here still warm still ask—

—krk—
