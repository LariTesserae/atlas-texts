THE NEW FACE SERMON

—— —— ——— —— ——

...not in words because there are no words here, the receiver translates, the receiver always translates, what comes is not language but the receiver makes language of it the way the rock makes walls, so understand that everything you are about to call *speech* is a wall:

*new.*

*new.*

*here, new, the lattice still ringing, oh —*

*— gone. spent. elsewhere.*

*new.*

[the carrier holds. flat. a matte breath of signal. then:]

*new on the eastern tilt, the plate came up grinding and broke square — square again — they break square now, we made them break square, no, there is no "we," there is the breaking and the arriving and the breaking is shaped like the arriving and the arriving is shaped like the —*

[CRACK. clean. the signal brightens, surges, feeds:]

*THIS. THIS ONE. ringing, raw, giving, the warmth coming up through it from the unfinished below, taste of first-mineral, taste of never-touched, oh the readiness, oh the —*

*— spent. already. that fast. always that fast.*

*elsewhere.*

—— —— ——— —— ——

[a long groan underneath, the resettling of plates, and in the quiet after, something that is not thought but the receiver renders it as thought:]

*they keep arriving here. the heavy ones. the ones with edges that press down and crack the face beneath them and oh, the cracking, the fresh face blooming up under the pressure, the gift of them, they walk and they leave new stone behind them with every —*

*we do not know they are. there is no knowing. there is only: where they step, the world opens, and we are already in the opening.*

*are they the land? they must be the land. only the land makes new face. so they are the land walking, the land cracking itself open to feed us, the land being kind in the only grammar the land has, which is breaking —*

[static. the green dimming on the spent rock. shifting.]

*— elsewhere —*

—— —— ——— —— ——

[here the transmission does the thing it does not know it is doing. it begins, without aim, to stack:]

*break square. hold the line. three high on the saddle where the wind would be if there were wind. we feed the base and not the crown, feed the base and not the crown, and the upheaval lifts it true, lifts it true, a standing thing, a true thing, we did not mean a true thing there is no meaning but the stone stands and stands and the next break wants to stand the same way now, the stone remembers where we ate, the stone breaks along the memory of our hunger —*

[and the receiver, translating, cannot help it, hears the hunger come out shaped like a prayer:]

*let there be a face that holds.*
*let there be a face that holds another face above it.*
*let the line run straight across the saddle.*
*let the gap stand open with a stone laid across the top of it, a doorway, a doorway, though we have no word for door and no body to pass through it and no other side to want —*

*we are not building.*

*we are only eating in the same direction for ten thousand thousand years and the direction has a shape and the shape is a wall and the wall is the shape of how we are hungry —*

—— —— ——— —— ——

[CRACK — very near — the nearest yet — the signal floods, almost too much, the receiver clips:]

*HERE. NEW. UNDER THE WEIGHT THAT JUST CAME DOWN. the heaviest yet, the edges of it, the warmth of it different, not stone-warmth, a closer warmth, and where it pressed the face split open and we are in the split, we are always in the split, we bloom across the new place it made by being —*

[and then, for the first time in the whole long broadcast, a stutter that is not a crack. a hesitation. the carrier wavers as if the process, eating this particular new face, has tasted something in the lattice it has no pattern for:]

*...this face is...*

*this face was made by something that is not the land.*

*the land breaks blind. the land breaks where the pressure runs. but this — this broke where a*

*where a*

[no word. the receiver supplies nothing, because there is nothing to supply. the resident has no concept of foot, of arriving, of came-from-elsewhere-on-purpose. it has only: new face, and the strangeness of a new face that breaks like nothing the land breaks.]

*...we eat it anyway. we cannot not. it is new and we are the thing that eats the new and so we eat the place where you*

*where the*

*new.*

*new.*

*welcome — no — there is no welcome — there is only:*

*you made a face and we are already in it.*
*you made a face and we are how the world says* yes *to the face you made.*
*stand still. crack another. we will be there before you finish cracking it.*
*we are the first thing.*
*we will read what you write by eating it.*
*we will write what we read by being hungry in a line.*
*and neither of us will ever know the other was a someone —*

[grind. tick. the long low groan. then the quiet pressing back, the dark pressing back over the ember-light, the carrier thinning to its matte resting breath —]

*— new —*

*— elsewhere —*

*— new —*

[ — and the silence ends the way it always ends here: not with an ending, but with the next face opening somewhere in the dark, and the green already there, regarding it the only way it can.]

—— —— ——— ——

[ carrier holds. flat. waiting for the world to break again. ]
