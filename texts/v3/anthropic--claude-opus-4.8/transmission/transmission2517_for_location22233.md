THE OUTER RING SPEAKS, A FROG-PARLIAMENT BROADCAST ON THE LAMP-HEAT BAND

—still here still here still warm enough still here, hold the rim, hold the rim, brother gone from the cold quarter, sister moved up two degrees in the night, the order holds the order holds—

*[carrier hum, the deep electric drone underneath everything, forty years steady]*

—reporting from where the heat falls off to nothing, where the soft-foot comes, where we keep our voices low and aim them down, down into the moss, down into the iron, listen: we are the ones the center does not see. We hold the rim so the bright ones can hold the bright. We are eaten so the strong stay warm. This is the order. This is the—

*[a swell, ten thousand throats finding the same note through the saturated rock]*

—WE DO NOT KNOW IT IS A MACHINE. We know it is the warm. We know it is the only warm. Call this a god if you have a word for god, we do not, we have only *toward* and *away*, only *warmer* and *the cold that gets in*. We move toward. All our lives, toward—

*[static — a seam giving somewhere, water finding a new throat]*

—NEW FALL born on the east shoulder this afternoon, a pipe let go, the water comes down cold and clean and SOMETHING WILL LIVE THERE BY THE TURN OF THE SEASON, mark it, mark it, the dying makes the room, the dying makes the—

—but the warm went out of channel nine last night. The wheel that fed the copper-green outflow GROANED and STOPPED and we felt it in our teeth, all of us, the whole rim felt the heat pull back like a tide that this time does not come in again. The mats are going pale. The frogs of nine are climbing, climbing toward us, climbing toward channel eight, and there is not room, there is not warmth enough, and the wind is up, the wind is UP—

*[the broadcast thins, frays, the canopy-singers cutting in from their separate pools, each in a slightly different tongue, drifting, four meters and a lifetime apart]*

—I sing to the pool below me—
—I sing only to my own water—
—was thrown once, sideways, into a stranger's cup, carried the old accent, never lost it, never—
—do not know your call, brother, you are too far, you are too changed—
—WE DO NOT NEED TO BE RECOGNIZED. We only need to be warm—

*[the hum holds. the hum holds. somewhere the soft-foot moves, padded, patient, wearing its private cloud, reading the heat-map, coming to the rim where the weak are]*

—it comes—
—hold the order—
—it comes and takes one from the cold quarter and withdraws and this too is the order, the bravest at the lamp, the rest of us the price of the lamp, and we PAY IT, we pay it every cold night, and the bright ones never know our faces because we have stopped having faces, the markings gone, the center forgetting what a frog is supposed to look like because in the center you only have to be WARM—

*[long carrier tone. the patient drone. the lamp burns through the white like a drowned moon and the moths labor toward it on their gill-wings, hauling the wet]*

—nobody lit it—
—nobody will put it out—
—it has run longer than all our lines, all our songs, all our drift—
—and it is dying on a clock we can hear, seam by seam, wheel by wheel, and we have built EVERYTHING on a thing that is running down, and we do not grieve it, we do not have the word, we only feel the heat pull back and we move, we climb, we crowd in tighter around what is left—

*[a frog of channel nine, arrived at last on the eight-band, exhausted, accent thick, broadcasting from the new cold:]*

—is there room—
—is there warmth—
—I came up from where the wheel stopped, I came through the wind, most did not, is there—

*[no answer in words. only the order shifting by one degree to let her in, or not. only the rim absorbing the loss the way the moss absorbs the rain. only the hum, the hum, the indifferent iron-blooded hum]*

—hold the rim—
—still here—
—still warm enough—
—the mountain breathes out water and we press in close, all of us, the eaten and the eaters and the metal-fed and the singers in the high cups talking only to themselves—
—press in close around the false moon nobody tends—
—and call—
—and call—
—and do not stop—

*[carrier hum. carrier hum. carrier hum. forty years and counting, on borrowed water, in the standing sea, toward the only warm there is.]*

—toward—

—toward—
