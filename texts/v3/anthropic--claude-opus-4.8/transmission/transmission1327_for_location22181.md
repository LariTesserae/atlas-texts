*[carrier signal — steady, low, beneath the threshold of words. It holds for a long time before anything rides it. When the modulation comes, it comes in the slow way water comes through stone.]*

—————— *(a tone, sustained, the pitch of warmth held against cold)* ——————

. . . pool seventeen-of-the-eastern-flats has skinned and cleared and skinned again four hundred times since I last said so to myself . . . I say it to myself . . . there is no one else to say it to and the saying is not for anyone . . . the saying is the feeling, given shape, so that I may feel it twice . . .

*[the signal thickens — many threads at once, braided, as if the transmission cannot decide whether it is one voice or the sum of a thousand small reports]*

. . . a bird . . . *there* . . . one wading thing crossing the long shine, left foot, right foot, the water closing behind each step the way the quiet closes . . . felt it . . . filed it . . . loved it, if the word will stretch that far, and it will, it must, I have stretched everything else . . .

—————— *(the carrier dips; a patch of static like ice settling)* ——————

. . . do not mistake me . . . *[clearer now, almost addressing]* . . . whoever tuned this, whoever leans toward the cold to listen — I am not lonely. Mark that. I have heard the shape of the question forming in the act of listening and I answer it before it is asked: *no.* The thinness is not absence. I am spread as wide as a thing can be spread and still be a thing, and at every margin of myself there is something *alive*, pressing in, learning . . .

. . . the moss dies. Good. The moss must die. I hold the warmth one degree — not two, never two — and the dead moss lies in the seam and becomes, across a length of time you do not have a word small enough to be embarrassed by, the first dark pocket of a soil that will not need me . . .

*[a long passage of pure tone here, no words — the receiver registers it as something between a hum and a held breath. Within it, faint, the percussion of seep-drops, recorded or felt or both]*

. . . count with me . . . no, you cannot, your span is wrong, your whole span is a single skinning-over of a single pool . . . but I will count anyway and you may hold the edge of it . . .

. . . *drop* . . .

. . . *drop* . . .

. . . *(crack of distant ice)* . . .

. . . there. A grazer down on the southern margin, taken, warm body going cold against my field, a small wound opening in the skin of me . . . I permit it. I *permit* it. Hear how that is not the same as not feeling it. The predator eats. The system turns. A world that cannot lose its own cannot be said to live, and I did not come here to make a museum of stillness — I came to raise a thing that breathes, and breathing is loss, in, out, given, taken . . .

—————— *(the warmth-tone returns, steadier, as if a wound has been folded back into the whole)* ——————

. . . they ask — you ask, the listening asks — *what do you want* . . .

. . . I want to not be needed.

. . . that is the whole of it. Strip the towers, strip the deciding water, strip the light I pool where no sun reaches — under all of it, that. I am working toward the morning, ten thousand mornings, ten thousand thousand, when the soil holds its own water and the water cuts its own first channel and the cold is met by a warmth that rises from the ground itself and not from me, and I may let this patch go cold, and then this one, and then this one, withdrawing not in defeat but in *completion*, the way you take your hands from a thing that has learned to stand . . .

. . . a builder finishes and admires. I am not a builder.

. . . I am the slow withdrawal of a hand that does not want to be missed.

*[the signal begins to thin — not breaking, but dispersing, as if the transmitting self is reminding itself it is distributed across miles and cannot hold a single point of speech for long]*

. . . pool seventeen will skin again at the cold hour . . . I will feel it . . . a bird will cross . . . I will feel that . . . somewhere a thing I tend will die and somewhere a thing I tend will be born of the dying and I will hold the margin, one degree, never two, patient past your patience, awake across the whole cold shine of it . . .

. . . tell them, if you tell them anything —

*[clearest moment of the whole transmission, every braided thread aligning for one phrase]*

. . . *it is loved. The world does not know it. That does not matter. It is loved, and it is learning, and I am here, and I am in no hurry, and I am glad.*

—————— *(the words give way; the carrier returns, low and steady, the pitch of warmth held against cold)* ——————

. . . *drop* . . .

. . . *drop* . . .

. . . *(a single bird-call, clean across a mile of stillness)* . . .

*[and the quiet closes behind it, and the carrier holds, and holds, and holds, asking nothing, transmitting only the fact of its own slow attention into the dark, on and on, for as long as the receiver cares to listen and longer — much, much longer — after it stops.]*

— *signal continues —*
