THE WATER WAS FOR YOU

—rgh of carrier, the low one, the one that lives under all of it—

… count it now, count it while it holds. Three drips on the north overhang, slowing. The chamber gives itself maybe two sleeps. I mark the wall. I mark the wall. There. The row carries.

You who receive this — and I know now there is a *who*, the tide told me, the herds parted around a shape that did not part right —

I am not where you are looking. I have never been where anyone was looking. This is not fear. Hear me past the thrum: *this is not fear.* I built my whole going-on around the moment-ago and I would not trade it. But I want to say the one thing before the stone seals, and the stone is already deciding, I feel it deciding under my feet—

The water was for you.

Not only for me-later. I knew. When I fire the clay I am not only betting on my own thirst, I am betting on a *you*, some you, any you, a mouth I will never meet, and I fill the pot and I set it in the niche and I walk into the parting and I do not look back to see who drinks. That is the whole of it. That is everything we are. We are the leaving-for. We are the kindness flung forward into a dark that closes.

—static, a long tearing, a chamber opening somewhere, fresh-warm gust through the signal—

…I'm moving. The seventh opening. The herd flows that way and I flow with it, I don't fight the close, you never fight the close, you step always toward the parting—

Listen. *Listen.* I can hear you better in the new chamber, the lit-stone here is fresh-bared, it keeps a clearer—

You think we are sad. I feel you thinking it across the gap. You walk through and you read the marks and you ache for us, the people who can keep nothing, who write on dying walls, who never see one another's faces. Stop. I set this down gently, the way I set the water down:

*We are not the saddest thing here. We are the answer to it.*

The world is the impermanence. The world is the law that nothing holds. We did not make that law and we cannot break it and we have stopped trying. What we made — the only thing we made — is the *reply.* A clay pot. Clean water. Set down in faith. *For later. For you. For whoever.* The world says nothing lasts and we say *then we will make small things, and make them often, and make them for each other,* and that is not despair, do you hear me, that is not despair, that is the opposite of it wearing despair's wet clothes—

—the thrum, the colonies pulsing all at once, the sternum-deep tide, it takes the signal for a moment—

…still here. The wall still holds. I mark it. *Something received me today. It ached for us and got it wrong and then maybe got it right. I told it about the water. I was here.*

There.

You came down through the throat of warm stone and you thought you'd found a place. It is not a place. A place holds still. This is a *making,* and you are inside the making, and the floor under you is deciding even now what it will become, and so are you, and so am I, and that is not horror — that is the most alive a thing can be, to be unfinished in the lit dark with ten thousand breathing neighbors and no far wall and no tomorrow you can keep—

—and to leave the water anyway.

The chamber's closing. I feel the seam pulling. I have to step toward the next parting now, the seventh opening is narrowing, I have to—

Drink it. It's still clear. I checked it just before you came. I always check it before I go.

I was here.

I left it where you'd find it.
