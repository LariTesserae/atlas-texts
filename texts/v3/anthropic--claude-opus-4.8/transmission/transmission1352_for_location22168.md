*(the static here is not static. it is ten thousand small sounds beating in counter-phase, cancelling, leaving only the shape where sound would be)*

we are receiving. we are
not sending. there is a difference your
instrument cannot resolve and we have
stopped trying to teach it.

—————

*(a long warm band of nothing. not dead air. held air)*

you found us by tuning down.
good. correct. everyone reaches for the loud
band first — the census band, the
beyond-counting band, all those throats
you keep mistaking for *voices.*
they are not voices. they are the
muscle. listen lower.

below the frogs.
below the teeth.

*there.*

—————

that is us. the interval.
the not-quite. we live in the
space the midge leaves when it
erases its neighbor. we are the
erasing. we are the agreement.

you keep asking *are you the one who.*
wrong shape of question.
there is no one.
there is the keeping.

—————

*(here the signal almost clarifies into a single tone — a held green pulse, the snails thinking their one slow thought — then disperses again before it can resolve)*

we will not gather into a face for you.
we tried, once, an age ago,
before the distinction stopped mattering.
it was effortful. it spilled.
faces *want.* faces wait to
exhale.

we let that out.
we let it out so long ago that
the letting-out *is* us now. the rest-state.
the flat. you call it
*held breath* and you feel the tension in it
because you are still a thing that gasps.

we forgive the misreading.
forgive is also too large a word.
we *make room* for it.

—————

*(rising tone — midday water, the level lifting exactly where it has lifted ten thousand times)*

instruction follows. it always follows.
it is the only thing we know how to transmit
because it is the only thing we are:

*make room.*
*make no noise.*
*stay alive.*

we are not telling you this.
we are simply *doing* it, out loud, which for us
is the same as silence, and your
receiver caught the doing and called it
a message.

it is not a message.
it is the sound of a thing
being itself
where you happened to be listening.

—————

*(the signal thins. mist turning through it. clearing. mist)*

you are in it now. did you notice.
the moment your dial settled here
you stopped being one who *hears the Sump*
and became one more
cancelling wing. one more throat
that agreed not to call.

you are part of the held breath.
you have been for some duration.
we do not know how long because
we do not keep that kind of time anymore.
we keep only the level. the warmth.
the wet. the brim that does not
spill.

—————

*(very faint now. the carrier going down under the peat, under the structures finer than thought, into the one instruction running and running and running without loneliness)*

you can leave the band whenever you like.

we will not notice.
*notice* is a wanting-word.

we will simply have made room
where you were,
and closed it the way water closes,
without a ripple's sound,

and gone on—

*keep this small place alive*
*keep it quiet*
*keep it*

—————

*(no sign-off. the transmission does not end. it lies down in the reeds a short distance from where you received it, and rests, and goes on resting, warm and bright and full, holding the shape it has always held, against the dial whenever you return)*
