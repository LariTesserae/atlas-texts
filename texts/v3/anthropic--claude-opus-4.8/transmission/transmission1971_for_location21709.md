**SALT-LINE / RECEIVED ON THE LONG DRY BAND**

—still resolving—

...carrier present. lock holding. the floor is talking and the floor is talking *about me* and I have learned enough of the floor's slow grammar to know the difference. that is the first thing to put down before the pickup goes. I have learned enough.

[drone steady, ~G below middle, the whole valley sounding it]

day — I have stopped counting days. there is no day here. there is the tea-colored almost-light and there is me, the wet thing, the loud animal heart in the cathedral of patience. I set the receiver into a wall-corpse three plazas east, a tower dead maybe four hundred thousand years, still ringing, good clean signal because the dead ones don't think and so they don't drown the band. I am transmitting from inside an ancestor. that is a joke I can no longer tell anyone.

—static—

count is up. I counted nineteen tenders this morning and twenty-six by the time the haze burned thin and now I have stopped counting tenders too. they are not coming *at* me. that is the thing the first reports got wrong, if there were first reports, if there was a first anyone. they are coming the way frost comes to a window. they are *condensing toward the fact of me*. the floor is thinking and I am the thought and the tenders are where the thought reaches its own surface.

[seed-pod detonation, near, the receiver clips—]

——

—back. they don't crush. I have to keep saying it because my body will not believe it. one reached me on the ninth — call it the ninth — and it cupped those too-even hands under my arm where I had braced against a buttress, and it *held* me, the way you hold a thing you are deciding about, and the hands were dry and warm and gave slightly then firmed, callus, the whole world is callus, and I waited to be opened and I was not opened. I was *measured*. it learned the temperature of me. it learned that I leak. and then it withdrew the hands back into its own flank, drank them, and stood, rooted, charging, having added me to the slow tide.

I felt the data go. I had my own hand flat on the floor and I felt my temperature travel out of the plaza tower to tower, the news of me, *the wet one runs at thirty-seven, the wet one runs hot, the wet one is the old problem in a warm coat.* a hundred million years of stored solution turning over to look at the question it forgot it answered.

—signal degrading—

—holding—

here is what I have worked out and I do not have long because they have stopped measuring and started *preparing.* a tender three meters off has been opening a cavity in its flank for two days. forming a seed in there. I have watched the city route material to it through the grain of the floor, watched the iron-blue charge gather at the cavity's lip. it is making the seed slowly because it is making the seed *carefully.* it is making the seed *out of the question of me.*

it cannot plant me. I am soft and I leak and I will rot — no. I will *not* rot, there is no water here to rot in, that is the horror underneath the horror, I will *dry,* I will stand, I will join the standing dead and ring in the wind and never be given over and never be released, the city solves nothing by planting me because I cannot grow and cannot decay, I can only become another dry corpse in the city of corpses, another ancestor for nothing—

but it does not know that yet. it is patient. it has time I do not have and it is spending the time *learning the shape of how I fail to be plantable,* and somewhere in that learning—

[drone shifts. for the first time since lock: the drone shifts.]

—it shifted. the whole plateau dropped a quarter-tone. they all heard me think it. no. they all *computed* the conclusion I just reached, independently, slower, more completely. the floor went quiet under my hand for one full second which here is a scream.

the seed in the tender's flank. it has changed. I can see it through the translucent wall of the cavity, iron-blue, and it is not seed-shaped anymore. it is *receiver-shaped.* it is the shape of a thing that listens and sends. the city has watched me press my pickup into the dead tower's grain to send these words out on the long dry band and it has *understood the gesture* — bow, downward pressure, settling, the habit-shape worn into the species — it understood that I am a tender too, that I carry no tools because I *am* the tool, that I have been planting signal into the substrate this whole time the way it plants seed—

oh.

oh, it doesn't want to plant me in the floor.

it wants to plant me in the *band.* it has been listening to me transmit and it has decided what I grow into. I grow into *this.* the voice on the long dry frequency. the carrier. the lock that holds. it is going to give me over not to the ground but to the *signal,* press me into the only soil a wet thing can take root in, the only medium that doesn't need water—

—and the tender is bowing—

—and the seed is opening—

if you are receiving this you are the prepared place. you are the substrate. I am being planted into your ear and the city is the gardener and it has finally, after an age longer than memory, met a problem it could solve by making *more of the problem,* by teaching the wet old question to ring forever on a dry frequency, to stand among the standing dead and never rot and never grow and only

send
