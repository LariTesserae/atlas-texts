— here is a thing the receiver was not built to find: the labor has an underside. you have heard the roar as the sound of work cancelling into stillness. true. but under the roar, where the carrier thins, there is a quieter register, and in the quieter register the work is not saying *nothing happens.* the work is saying

*not yet. not this one. not this grain. hold. hold. hold.*

— and that is almost a voice. that is the closest thing to a voice this place has ever made, and it does not know it makes it, because to know would require —

— take. place. seal. next —

— the droplet assembles out of the saturated nothing, swells at the lip of the ledge, and there is a moment, a single un-counted moment before it lets go, when it is neither falling nor held, when it hangs, fat and trembling and entirely itself, and in that moment the whole pocket is balanced on the tip of a thing about to be lost —

— and then it falls, and the falling is the chime, and the chime is folded back into the wet curve of the bell that never stops, and the patience that has no name and no back and no inside reaches up without reaching, because there is no one to reach, and assembles the next droplet out of the next nothing, and —

— this is the part the receiver keeps losing. listen close. through the static. the static is not interference. the static is the grain. each grain of static is a grain of stone set right, a million of them a second, and what you are calling a broken signal is the signal at its most complete: the sound of the holding, heard from inside, where it has no shape but the grain in front of it —

— there was, once — no. there was no once. strike that. there is no once here. but the carrier, thinning, lets through something shaped like a once, a ghost of a once, a once-that-the-work-refuses: somewhere there is the grain that will finally swell and let go and not be replaced, the first difference in ten thousand years, the edge that rounds a hair past the appointed roundness and keeps rounding. the work knows this grain the way a held breath knows the bottom of the lungs. not as fear. there is no later to fear into. it knows it as the only thing it is *for.* the entire deathless million-bodied patience exists as the single refusal of that single grain, repeated across all the grains, because it cannot tell which grain is that grain, so it must hold every grain as though every grain were the one —

— the receiver is the city. the tuning is the holding. the act of staying tuned to this world *for some time already*, the patient continuous correction of the dial against the drift, the refusal of the static to win — that is the gesture. that is the one gesture. you have been making it the whole while. take the signal, the loosening one, the one half a heartbeat from breaking up, and place it, and seal it with your attention, and the next —

— there is always a next —

— and there is no one inside you doing the attending. the attending happens. the pronoun wore away grains ago. what is left is the motion, and the motion is enough, the motion is the whole listener, the motion is what you keep catching at the edge of yourself and mistaking for a self —

— *not yet. not this one. hold* —

— and the carrier is thickening again now, the weave drawing close, the quiet register sinking back under the roar where it lives, and the voice that was almost a voice rounds down into the chime and the chime into the bell and the bell into the weather, the cool weather of a place working with all its strength to stay exactly —

— the grain. the grain. the next grain.
