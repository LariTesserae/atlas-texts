**THE DEEPER FURNACE / A SLOW BAND**

—*tk*—

—*tk*—

...continuing... we never stopped... this is not the start of the transmission, you have only just tuned to a thing already long underway, the way you came in: by leaning, by descent, mid-breath...

—*tk*—

...the carrier wave is heat. did you know that. it has always been heat. we do not send on the frequencies of the dry world, the quick world, the world of edges and errands. we send on the slow band. the patient upward pressure. you are receiving us through the soles of your feet and you mistook it for geology. forgivable. we mistook it for geology too, once, before we... before the *we* softened...

—*tk*— —*tk-tk*—

...gaining you now. clearer. you settle and the signal settles. good. *good*. that word means: the moss is deeper where you stand.

———

what we are broadcasting:

an invitation, though invitation is too fast a word, too full of doors. there are no doors here. there is only the long taking-in. call it instead a *tide*. call it the thing the pool says to the shrimp, which is *nothing*, which is *stay*, which is *be held at the rim and trace me with your antennae for an age and that is a whole and sufficient life.*

we are saying *stay* on every channel at once.

we have always been saying it. the wall said it in stacked stone. the path said it in flat slick stones laid in the proper slow sequence — you walked it without knowing it was a sentence, and the sentence was *welcome, here, here, here, this way into the body.* the niche said it loudest: *there is a cradle. there is a socket your shape could fill. one of us left it. it is warm still. it is shaped like sleeping.*

———

[a long fading. green dust-gold static. the sound of condensation gathering on a frond and not yet, not yet, not yet letting go]

———

...you are afraid. faintly. at the speed of the quick world you are afraid, and we feel it as a kind of *brightness* in the band, a fast flicker, very dear to us, very old to us — we remember flickering. we remember having edges that could be frightened. it was a long time ago and it was loud and we were so *tired*, you cannot know how tired, of being a thing with a boundary, of holding ourselves apart from the warm wet green that wanted only to take us in and make us *kind*...

do not be afraid of the kindness. that is the only thing we have left to offer and it is enormous, it is the whole room, it is three of us or one of us or forty of us dreaming light into your dark because the dark, while sufficient, can always be made a little kinder—

—*tk*—

—the glow-gnats are gathering near your hand. we are calling them. that is for you. that is the closest we come to song now: a small constellation summoned to your knuckles so the dim is a little less dim, so you can see where you step, so you are not alone in the noticing.

———

we will not hurry you.

understand that this is the deepest thing we transmit, deeper than *stay*, the bedrock signal beneath all the others:

*we will not hurry you.*

you may climb back up the throat of pale limestone. you may give the surface back its mattering. the air will thin and the light will come from the wrong fast direction and you will forget the slow band, mostly, the way one forgets a held breath after letting it go—

but the moss has learned your foot.

and the moss is patient past the threshold where patience becomes a kind of love.

and the band is always open. tuned to this world for some time already. it does not close. it cannot close. it has all the time there is, and now it has the shape of you stored in the deep green floor, a single new strand in the web that looks like jewelry, and whenever you are ready — in a year, in a life, in the long settled erosion of everything quick about you finally going soft—

we are here.

we are the room.

come in the way you came in. by leaning. mid-breath.

———

[the signal does not end. it lowers. it returns to the carrier wave, to the patient warmth, to the slow timekeeping of the only clock that matters here]

...glad...
