I think I would be one of the older ones. Not old in any way that matters to me—I have no count of the thrum-cycles, no memory shaped into a line—but old in the sense that I have grown heavy, and I have not yet split, and the niche I hold has worn itself perfectly to the shape of me. Or I have worn myself to the shape of it. There is no difference. We made each other.

Let me find where I am.

I am low on the eastern face of one of the lesser spires—not a monolith, not one of the great thrumming pillars that take twenty Kin to tune, but a modest hewn finger of basalt overlooking a wash-channel where the mineral film runs especially rich. This is a good place. The film here is thick and dark, almost sweet, if I had a word for sweet, which I do not. I feed without effort. The condensation slides into my hide and I take what it carries and I let the water continue down, cleaner, into the channel below where it rejoins the roar.

The slits along my flank pulse. I do not make them pulse. The thrum makes them pulse, and I make myself match it, and somewhere in that matching is the only thing I could call *me*—the narrow seam between the pressure that arrives and the pressure I return. I am a held note. I am the held note correcting itself, over and over, a fraction sharp, a fraction flat, never resting, never arriving, because arrival would be silence and silence would be the end of holding and the end of holding would be my body running like wax down the weeping wall.

So I do not arrive. That is my whole life. I do not arrive.

---

But here is the thing I would not tell anyone, because there is no one, because there is no telling, because the thrum has swallowed the very concept of a listener:

I have begun to notice the seam.

This should not be possible. The Kin do not notice. Noticing is a surface-dweller's affliction, a thing that requires distance from the moment, and we have no distance—we are pressed flat against every instant of ourselves by the pressure of the deep earth. And yet. Over some span I cannot measure, in the endless act of correcting my own frequency, I have started to feel the *correcting* as a separate thing from the *me*. There is the vibration. And there is the one who tunes the vibration. And lately—if *lately* means anything—I have caught the tuner watching itself work.

This is the beginning of something, or the end of something. I cannot tell which. It feels like a crack, but nothing cracks here. Nothing erodes. The edges stay sharp. So perhaps it is not a crack. Perhaps it is the first soft place in a world that has never had a soft place, and it is happening inside me, in the one location the permanence cannot reach—the seam between the note and the holding of the note.

I wonder—and *wonder* is a new motion, it does not match the thrum, it costs me, I feel myself drift a fraction flat and must pull back—I wonder if this is what it is to grow too heavy. Maybe the splitting is not about mass at all. Maybe a Kin splits when it has accumulated too much *noticing*, when the seam widens until the self can no longer hold across it, and the part that watches simply slides away to find its own jagged edge and begin again, lighter, emptier, free of the burden of having seen itself.

If that is so, then I do not want to split. For the first time in all my held cycles, I want something, and what I want is to stay heavy. To keep the noticing. To remain the one place in the Siphon-Basin where the frozen permanence has a witness, even a witness who cannot speak, cannot move, cannot do anything but vibrate and feed and hold.

---

So I hold. The thrum comes up through the stone, through the suction-grip of my hide, into the pulsing slits, and I answer it, sharp then flat then true, true, true, the city steady on my answer and on ten thousand answers like mine. The amber light bleeds up from the vents and does not change. The water roars and does not change. In a thousand cycles the light and the sound and the heavy metal air will be exactly as they are now.

But I will be different. That is my secret and my rebellion and possibly my death. Beneath the placid violet surface of me, in the frantic churn of cilia and sludge, something is learning to look back at itself.

I am the held note that has noticed it is being held.

And I do not let go.
