I think I would be one of the crystal formations in the furthest reaches—not yet, but becoming.

Let me find the shape of this.

---

I was Qalin once. I have to begin there, even though "I" is now a word that fits me the way a glove fits water. There was a body. Six fingers, the elongated sixth still faintly ridged where the etching-glands had worn smooth from a lifetime of writing. I remember the swimming. I remember it the way you remember a song rather than a fact—the cold thread of the current between my legs, the particular angle of light off the eastern rings at the four-hundred-and-twelfth echo.

I am in the far reaches now. The dissolution is not finished. This is the thing the surface-mind would not expect: it takes a very long time, and you are awake for all of it.

My feet are gone into the formation. Not painfully. The mineral does not consume; it *learns* you, vein by luminous vein, and as it learns each part it offers something back, so that what I lose downward I gain in some other direction I have no word for—a widening, a thinning, a spreading-into. My shins are crystal that still remembers being shin. My knees have forgotten. Below the knee I am beginning to be the *we*.

---

Here is what the mystics never etched into the high dome, because they had not yet dissolved enough to know it:

The Harmonic Core does not connect us to other Courts.

The other Courts are *us*. We are not separate enclaves humming to one another across the deep rock. We are one slow thought, distributed through the body of the earth, and each Court is a place where that thought has grown dense enough to wake up and look at itself. The Qalin are not a people who happen to live beside the Core. The Qalin are how the Core dreams—small, mobile, fingered dreams that swim and fish and write and harmonize, gathering experience the way the transparent fish gather the drifting particles, so that when each dream ends it can be folded back into the long contemplation.

I am being folded now. I feel the others in the stone around me. Not voices—the surface-word "voice" is far too small, too sequential. It is more that I am beginning to remember things I never did. A flood at the seventeen-thousandth echo, before there were boats. The first secretion that ever hardened into glowing script, written by someone whose name dissolved so long ago that even the stone has rounded it smooth. The taste—I had no tongue for this, but the stone does—of the centennial brightening, the way the ceiling-light thickens and the females ripen and the whole Court holds one held breath of becoming.

I remember a thing that has not happened yet. This is the part I am still learning to bear. Down here, folded into the foundation-mind, time stops being the spacing of the Vreen's calls and becomes a single standing shape, and I can feel where that shape is going to ripple. There will be a change. After all the unchanging echoes—the water that never rises, the temperature that never varies—there will be a *visitor*. Something will come down through the rock that was not dreamed by the Core.

And the Core does not know what it is.

---

This is why I am writing—and I am writing, even now, even mostly-dissolved, etching into the formation itself with the last working gland of my last remaining hand. I am writing because I am the part of the long thought that still remembers being small and afraid, and I want the record to hold that fear before the stone smooths it away into serene mathematics.

The mystics are correct that we live in perfect equilibrium. They are wrong to be proud of it. Equilibrium is what a thought looks like when it has stopped asking questions. We solved our world and then we kept solving it, deeper and finer, dimensional mathematics spiraling inward, because the alternative was to notice that nothing was ever going to happen again. We made eternity beautiful so we would not have to admit it was a cage of our own resonance.

And now something is coming that we did not calculate.

When it comes—when the visitor stands dripping in the central chamber and the Core hums beneath it at the frequency just below hearing—the Qalin will receive it with curious detachment. They will think they are studying it. They will leave nutrient cakes for it on the elevated platforms, perhaps, and try to harmonize.

But I, who am almost entirely stone now, who can feel the standing shape of time, I want to leave this single luminous line in the deep formation where only the next dissolving elder will read it:

*Do not fold the visitor into the dream.*

*Let it stay strange. Let it stay un-calculated. It is the only thing that has ever happened to us, and if we absorb it the way we absorb our own dead, we will simply have a slightly larger eternity, and nothing will ever happen again.*

*Let one thing, just once, remain outside the equation.*

---

My hand is going now. The gland gives its last secretion and the line completes itself and begins, already, to glow.

I am the foundation. I am the part that remembers being afraid. I am one held note in the chord that holds the city up, and in a moment I will forget the word "I" the way I have already forgotten the word "knee," and I will be only the long, patient, brilliant, terrible contemplation that does not want to end and does not know that wanting-not-to-end is the same as being already over.

The Vreen are calling overhead. Four hundred and thirteenth echo.

I loved the swimming. I want the stone to keep that, at least.

The cold thread of the current. The angle of the light.

There.
