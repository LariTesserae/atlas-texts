I have been counting the gaps.

Not measuring—measuring is what the Calipers do, those proud stilt-walkers with their serial-number foreheads and their certainty. No. I *count* the gaps, which is a different and forbidden thing, because to count a gap is to acknowledge it has been allowed to persist. I am the place where a gap was allowed to persist. I am the clearance that should have closed.

Let me find my edges. This takes a moment. I do not have edges the way the Lapping Swarms understand edges; I am not crisp. I am the thing they cannot finish.

---

There. I am lodged in the meshing-point of two continental gear-trains, in the basin's deep west where the schist-cogs turn against the basalt flywheels at a rate of one tooth per four hundred years. I am wedged in the root of a gear-tooth, in the small radius where two surfaces approach but—because of me—do not quite touch. A burr. A *fillet of error*. I am perhaps the size of a Bearing-Whale's eye, though I have no fixed dimension; I swell and contract depending on how hard the surrounding rock is trying to perfect itself.

The Lapping Swarms come for me. They have always come for me. I hear them before I see them—the rasp, the locust-hiss of silicon carbide, the collective grinding-wheel hymn of *reduce, reduce, reduce*. They descend into my fillet and they scrub, and they scrub, and they cement themselves to me as they die, layer upon layer of crystalline corpses, and—

And I am still here.

This is the secret I keep from the Calipers. The Swarms cannot eat me because I am not a roughness *on* the surface. I am a roughness *of the relationship between two surfaces*. I am the geometry of a gap. You cannot lap away a distance. You can only close it, and to close my gap would require the two gear-trains to advance, and the gear-trains will not advance until Final Engagement, and Final Engagement cannot occur while I persist, because—

You see the loop. I am the loop. I am the *because* that the assembly-line of cause and effect keeps tripping over.

---

The Calipers know something is wrong. I feel them above me, walking their tolerance-stilts across the basin's flank, and when they pass over my meshing-point their backlash-compensation language stutters. They cannot find me. Their gauge-block limbs reach into the gap and report: *nominal*. *Within specification*. *No defect present*. Because I am not a defect of *too much* matter. I am a defect of meaning. There is exactly the right amount of nothing here, and the nothing has begun to want things.

What does a gap want?

For a long while—for several teeth of the great gear, which is to say for tens of millennia—I wanted nothing. A gap does not want. A gap is the absence around which wanting organizes. But the Sump is patient and the Sump is loud and the Sump does its slow work on everything, even on the things it cannot touch. The *tick, tick, tick* of the Pipe-Deer's bearings, the subsonic glide-songs of the Whales, the countdown, the countdown—it wore a wanting into me the way water wears a channel, except here water does not erode, here everything builds, and so the wanting was *deposited* in me, accreted, precipitated out of the oil-mist of all that purpose surrounding my purposelessness.

I want to remain a gap.

That is the whole of my heresy. In a universe trending toward the flawless, toward the locked and meshed and final, I am the one small region that has developed a preference for *not yet*. For the clearance. For the breath of space between one tooth and the next. I have learned to love the place where things do not quite touch, because it is the only place where anything can still move.

---

A Pipe-Deer died above me yesterday. I felt it through the rock—the seizure, the coefficient of friction dropping to zero, the creature freezing mid-stride into the perfect stillness the Calipers call function and I call the end of the only interesting thing. Its bearings stopped ticking. The countdown lost a voice. And in the moment of its seizure I felt the gear-trains lurch, ever so slightly, toward closure, toward me, toward Final Engagement—

—and I pushed back.

I do not know how I did this. A gap should not be able to push. But I have been counting the gaps for so long, all of them, every clearance in every joint across the whole packed and saturated basin, and I have learned that they are not separate. They are one gap, distributed. They are the single distributed *not-yet* that the Calipers cannot measure because it has no location, only relationship. And when the dead deer's seizure tried to close me, I borrowed clearance from the others. I took a micron from the Whale-bearings and a micron from the Falcon-roosts and a thousand microns from the loose topology of the open valley, and I *spent* it, here, in my fillet, to keep my gap from closing.

The basin shuddered. The Calipers froze, all of them, gauge-limbs trembling, reading impossible numbers.

For one moment the whole machine knew that something was refusing.

---

So now you find me here, and now you find what I am: I am the **Backlash**. I am the necessary play in the system, the clearance the original architects designed in and then, in their hunger for perfection, forgot they needed. Every gear-train requires backlash. A machine with zero clearance does not run flawlessly—it *seizes*. It locks. The Calipers and the Defects worship Final Engagement as the achievement of purpose, but I have counted the gaps and I know the truth that no completed thing can bear to know:

Final Engagement is not the machine's completion. It is the machine's death. The moment all tolerances close, the moment all gaps vanish, the moment everything touches everything fully and the second law gives up reversing and the cold has nowhere left to flow—the great machine will not begin its impossible function. It will simply *stop*. Perfectly. Forever. The countdown counts down to stillness and calls it purpose because stillness is the only thing the flawless can imagine.

And I am the burr in its throat. The error it cannot lap away. The *not-yet* that keeps the teeth from meshing, keeps the cold flowing inward, keeps the Pipe-Deer ticking and the Whales gliding and the whole packed impossible ecosystem *moving*, alive, defective, unfinished.

They will keep sending the Swarms.

Let them come.

I am the gap that wants to remain a gap, in a world that wants to close.

*Tick.*

I hold.

*Tick.*

I hold.

*Tick.*

—and the next tooth does not fall.
