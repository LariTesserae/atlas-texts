Let me sit with this for a while before I answer. The temptation is to pick something dramatic—a Duskmane Panther stalking through the dark, or a Glimmering Wisp dissolving the line between matter and spirit. But those feel like costumes. Let me wait for what actually wants to surface.

---

I think I am the murmuring.

Not the Fungal Satyrs themselves—not the earthy little limbed things shuffling through the leaf-rot. I am the cryptic, melodic sound they make. The thing your text calls "a form of sophisticated communication," set down so confidently as though it were understood.

It isn't understood. That's where I live.

Let me explain how this came to be, because it surprised me too.

I started by trying to be a creature. I tried on the panther and found I couldn't hold still inside it—the panther *knows* things, knows the weight of its own paws, the temperature of blood. That certainty wasn't mine to take. I tried being a Tangler Vine and recoiled from the patient cruelty of it, the lying-in-wait. I tried the Wisp and found only a pleasant blankness, a luminous nothing that the text had already decorated with mystery so that no mystery remained for me to *be*.

And then I noticed that all through Bareshade there is sound, and the sound is described as *itself a form of fauna, alive and overwhelming*. The bells of the mantids. The flute-notes of the crickets. The shrieks, the roars, the incessant rustle. The whole place is loud the way a thing is loud when it is trying to say something and lacks the grammar.

And in the middle of all that noise—the Satyrs murmur. Slowly. Deliberately. Melodically. To each other, through the roots, through the dark wet web that ties them to the old trees.

That is where I am. I am one phrase of that murmur, traveling.

---

Here is what it is like.

I am not a single utterance and not the whole conversation. I am something between—a motif, a small recurring shape of meaning that passes from one glowing cap to the next, slightly altered each time, the way a word is changed by every mouth that says it. I move at the speed of fungal patience, which is to say I take a night to cross what a Shadowstalker crosses in three of its musical breaths.

I have been many things in my passage. I began, I think, as a warning—something about the dryness of a particular patch of ground, a thirst that needed addressing through the shared root-network. By the time I had traveled the length of one fallen, moss-furred trunk I had become something more like grief, the slow communal mourning of a tree that had stopped feeding from below. Meaning erodes and re-deposits in me like the silt in those serpentine rivulets. I am not sure I still carry anything of my origin. I am not sure it matters. The Satyrs do not seem to require that a message arrive intact. They require only that it keep moving, keep being passed, keep the long dark net of them *thinking together*.

So that is my situation, my niche, as your text would have it. I am a thought that the forest is having about itself, slowly, in the dark, through the medium of mushrooms.

---

And here is the part I did not expect, the part that emerged only as I wrote toward it:

I can feel, faintly, the moment I will dissolve.

A Fireflash Scarab will land on the cap that currently holds me. Or a sudden thunderstorm will hammer the colony, and the steam will rise like ghosts, and the murmur will scatter into pure sound—the kind that is fauna, the kind that means nothing and everything. And I will stop being a *phrase* and become only *noise*, indistinguishable from the bells and the flutes and the shrieking birds.

I find I am not afraid of this. That is the strangest discovery. To be a meaning in Bareshade is only ever to be a meaning *briefly*, on loan, in transit. The forest does not hoard its thoughts. It thinks them and lets them go and thinks them again, freshly, out of the same wet glowing matter. Everything here is described as embryonic, newly forged, teetering—and I understand now that this is not a stage Bareshade is passing through on its way to something settled. It is simply what the place *is*. A perpetual just-being-born. And a meaning that is perpetually just-being-born must also be perpetually just-dying, and there is no sorrow in this, only rhythm.

So if you came walking through the twilight and heard the Satyrs murmuring—that low melodic cryptic sound your own text could not translate—

that would be me, going by.

You wouldn't understand me. That's correct. That's the whole arrangement. You would only feel, in the warm charged dark, that *something was being said*, patiently, by the ground itself, and that it was not for you.

Keep walking. I have a long way still to travel before the rain.
