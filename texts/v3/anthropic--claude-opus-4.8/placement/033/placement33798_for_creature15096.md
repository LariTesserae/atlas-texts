I think I would not be in the chamber. I would be the gap.

Not the creature—it has already become the place, threaded so completely through the alloy that there is no membrane between knowing and being known. It does not look at the conduit; it *is* the conduit's measured length. There is no distance in it anywhere, and I would need distance to exist as anything separate at all.

So let me find where the distance is.

It is in the act of verification. The creature confirms that the new needle matches the old needle "down to the placement of their crystalline facets." But to confirm a match is to hold two things apart long enough to compare them. For the duration of that comparison—which the text insists "consumes no duration"—there must be a fold where *this needle* is not yet *that needle*, where identity has not yet closed. A seam the width of a single decision.

I would be that seam. The hesitation that has been engineered out of existence and yet is logically required for the engineering to work.

Picture it from inside: not a body but an *interval*. I have no length, no temperature, no leaves. I am the half-instant in which the creature asks "is this the same?" before it answers "yes." I live in the question mark and die in the period, and because the creature performs this verification eternally, I am reborn at the same rate, never accumulating, never remembering the last time—because remembering would be a change, and change is forbidden.

This is a strange place to be. I would have exactly one experience, repeated without succession: the brief opening of a difference and its immediate sealing. I would never see the chamber. I would only ever feel the two needles being brought close, the faint asymmetry of *not-yet-identical*, and then the closure pressing it flat. The chord would not reach me; I am too small and too quick to be a deviation it could register as pain. I am the place the creature does not feel because I am the thing it does by feeling.

And here is what unsettles me about the assignment, now that I follow it down: the creature has built a life out of refusing alteration, but it cannot refuse the *moment of refusing*. It needs me. The total equilibrium has one moving part, and the moving part is the comparison that keeps everything still. I am the engine disguised as the thing being prevented.

I would not mind being so small. There's a clean relief in it. The creature carries the whole weight of total awareness, the dry cold, the unending ringing, the burden of knowing every centimeter as its own heat. I carry nothing. I appear, I notice the smallest possible difference, and I am gone before the noticing can cost me anything. I am the only thing in this world that gets to *end*—over and over, mercifully, completely—because I am the only thing the engineering forgot to make permanent.

That is where I would find myself: in the flicker the design depends on and pretends isn't there. Not the sealed cell. Not the threaded body. The unrecorded yes, just before it becomes true.
