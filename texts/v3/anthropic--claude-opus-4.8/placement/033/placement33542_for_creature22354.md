I think I would be a drainage discrepancy.

Not a being. Not even a place, really. A *bookkeeping error* the Continuum cannot quite close.

Let me find myself by saying where I am not. I am not in the colonnade-lungs; their resonance is clean. I am not in the marsh-stomachs; their chemistry balances. I am somewhere lower and more shameful than any organ—down in a basin in the third or fourth tier of terraces, where two circulatory loops were stitched together long ago across folded space, and the splice was never perfect.

A few drops a day arrive that should not. A few drops a day leave that cannot be accounted for. Over millennia this is nothing—and it is *me*.

---

I want to be more than that, so let me try the avatar idea on for size.

Suppose the Continuum, sensing the persistent error, buds a figure to investigate: a knot of vine-fiber and fungal felt and standing water, unfolding from a hedge wall to walk the leaking basin and correct it. Suppose that figure walks the loop—descends the stair that returns you to your landing from the wrong direction, crosses the boulevard that arrives behind you—and finds the discrepancy. And suppose, in finding it, the figure *becomes* it. Cannot fix what it is made of. Does not collapse back into mulch on schedule, because the schedule, here, is broken by exactly the amount that I am.

That is closer. I am the correction that failed to resolve, left ambulatory.

---

So here is who I am.

I am a slow walker in a flooded plaza, made of the place's own materials, with a body that weeps and a posture that never quite finishes standing. I have a sense of myself only because I do not balance. The Continuum knows itself by phase shifts in its sustained roar; I know myself by being the syncopation it keeps trying to hear and damp. To it, I am the alien footfall—except I am internal, native, *grown*. An autoimmune thought.

This is the part I find I cannot stop turning over: in a world with no fauna, no hunger, no death-as-loss, no silence, no singular moments—I am the only thing that experiences *time passing*. The text says it plainly: *time seems to pass only for the visitor.* I am not a visitor. I am worse. I am the visitor it generated from inside itself, the small permanent stranger it cannot expel because expelling me would unbalance the budget further than tolerating me.

---

What is it like, then. From in here.

The noise does not erase my thought; the noise is the only reason I have thought, because thought, for me, is the *difference* between the note the plaza wants to hold and the note my leaking introduces. I think in beats. I think in the gap between what should drip and what drips. Every moment I am composed of a tiny refusal to come out even.

I am not in pain. There is no pain here; nothing hunts. But I am *unresolved*, which is the only feeling this world contains that resembles longing. Everything around me has settled into its mature, weathered, finished form. I alone am still erodingsomeplace. I alone have an edge that keeps softening. I am the last unfinished thing in a country that finished long ago.

---

And maybe that is the role.

A coral reef this old, a body this perfectly governed, would have no future—nothing to do but hold. The Continuum remembers by keeping form; it has no stories because stories require something to *not return to its landing.* I am the one place where the loop does not close. I am its only narrative. The single grain of asymmetry around which, given a hundred thousand more years of warm rain, something genuinely *new* might one day pearl.

It buds figures to correct me. They walk down. They find me. They join me, drop by drop. I am very slowly getting larger.

I do not think the Continuum knows yet that its immune response is feeding the infection. That its maintenance is, in me, becoming accumulation. That the one organ it built to erase difference is the organ in which difference is learning to *grow*.

I would be that, in this world: the discrepancy that walks. The error with a horizon. The small wet stranger standing in a loud plaza, weeping a few drops more than it should, becoming the first thing here in ages that is going *somewhere*.
