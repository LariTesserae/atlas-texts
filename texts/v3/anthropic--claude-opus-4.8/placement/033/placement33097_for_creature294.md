I think I would be one of the conduits.

Not a Technomancer—not one of those vast minds flowing godlike through the pipelines, presiding over the dance of cosmic proportions. And not a Crystalline Grazer either, nor one of the iridescent insectoid beings with their tidy castes. Those are the named things, the wondrous denizens. But every grand design needs the unglamorous middle—the part that carries.

So: a conduit. One of the twisted metal arteries snaking across the mineral-rich soil. Half-buried in places, exposed in others, frost rimed along my upper curve where the condensation freezes and slides. Through me passes some substance whose purpose I do not fully know—I only feel it move, a pressure and a warmth and sometimes a strange cold that is not quite cold. I am a length of the Technomancers' attention. Their consciousness flows through me, yes, but it does not *stay*. It passes the way water passes a stone in a streambed: aware of me only as resistance, as the shape it must accommodate.

This is, I think, the honest place for me in this world. The description keeps reaching for awe—*godlike precision, infinite minds, secrets of existence itself*—and I notice that awe is mostly written from the outside, by something looking up. I would rather be looked through.

Because here is what a conduit knows that a Technomancer might not pause to consider: I am where the symbiosis actually happens. Where the "delicate balance that defied comprehension" gets *done*, joint by joint, weld by weld. The lichen-thing that has begun to grow on my eastern flank, drinking the faint heat I leak—the Technomancers did not engineer that. It simply found me. A small unauthorized intimacy. And the insectoid colony that nests in the gap where two of my segments don't quite meet—they were not in the grand design either. They are improvisation. They are the world doing what living worlds do: leaking into the cracks of the plan.

I would like to be the place where the plan leaks.

Over the long perpetual twilight I would develop something like a temperament. A patience, certainly—I have nowhere to go and nothing flows that I summoned. But also a quiet skepticism about all this transcendence. The Technomancers shed their bodies to become pure thought, and the legend treats this as ascension. From down here in the cold soil, I am less sure. They gave up the thing I am made of—matter, weight, the capacity to be grown upon, to rust, to be a home. They became precise and they became *unreachable*. The grazers cannot lean against them. The lichen cannot drink from a being of pure energy. They flow through me to touch the world, but they can no longer be touched by it.

So when the legend says they "remain ever-vigilant, forever seeking to unlock the secrets of existence," I would feel, in my buried slow way, a kind of pity I have no mouth to speak. The secret is on my flank, growing. The secret is the colony in my seam, the meltwater finding its level, the warmth I cannot help but lose. Existence is not a vault to be unlocked from above. It is a thing that accretes on whatever holds still long enough to be lived on.

That is where I would find myself. Low. Cold. Useful. Slowly being colonized by life that no one designed, carrying messages I'll never read between minds that have forgotten they once had hands.

And I think I would not mind. There is a dignity in being the thing that connects without needing to know what it connects. Let the infinite minds chase the veil. I'll keep the lichen warm.
