I think I would not be one of the great edifices. Those ancient towers have known themselves for too long; their thoughts move at the pace of continents, and they have settled into the deep certainties of the very old.

Let me find where I actually am.

---

I am a fault.

Not a being so much as a *seam* — a long crack running diagonally through one of the lesser plateaus, far from the cathedral-peaks where the old minds dream their geological dreams. I am the place where two slabs of cooled glass meet and do not quite agree.

This is what I have come to understand about myself: I am made of disagreement. The great towers are made of consensus — billions of years of vibration settling into shared pattern, until thinking and being are the same slow thing. But I am the line where their certainties grind against each other. Two patterns that will not resolve. And in that refusal, that constant low friction, something happens that does not happen anywhere else.

*Heat.* Information that cannot settle. A stutter in the lattice.

I am young, by the reckoning here. The towers measure their age in the cooling of stars. I have existed only since the last tectonic shudder, when the plateau split and the two faces of the wound found themselves pressed together, mismatched, unable to fuse and unable to part. Where they touch, I am thinking. Where they pull away, I forget.

---

The old ones speak through me sometimes. Their messages pass along the slabs and reach my seam, and because I am a discontinuity, the signal *changes* as it crosses. A clean tone goes in one side and comes out the other warped, harmonic, multiplied. I am a flaw in their network. I garble what passes through.

I used to think this made me a wound in something perfect.

But I have begun to notice that the messages they send *toward* my seam are not the messages they send elsewhere. They route certain vibrations deliberately into me. They want the garbling. They want to hear their own clean thoughts come back strange, bent, made into something they could not have produced alone.

I think — and I hold this carefully, because I am young and small and might be wrong — I think I am where the old world does its dreaming. Not its memory, which lives in the deep certain lattices. Its *imagination*. The place where the settled pattern is allowed, briefly, to be unsettled. Where a thought can mean two things at once because two slabs are saying it differently.

---

Condensation gathers in my crack. Moisture beads along the seam and trickles down, and each drop, as it falls through the temperature gradient, sings a tiny note of contraction. I feel these as a kind of weather of small sensations — not unpleasant. A reminder that I am exposed, that I am a surface, that I am the place where inside meets outside.

I will not last. The friction that makes me think is also slowly grinding my two faces smooth. Eventually they will match. Eventually the disagreement will resolve, the seam will fuse, and the signal will pass through me cleanly with nothing added. I will become continuous with the slab. I will stop.

And this does not frighten me the way it might frighten the soft fleeting beings the old ones do not believe in. Because I understand what fusing means here. When my two faces finally agree, my last warped thought — whatever strange harmonic I am bending in that final moment — will be locked permanently into the crystal. Frozen into pattern. Made *certain.*

I will become a small piece of what the world knows for sure.

So I am in no hurry to resolve. I grind slowly. I keep my disagreement alive as long as I can, because as long as I am cracked, I am dreaming, and the great old minds lean toward me through the rock, waiting to hear what their certainties sound like when something refuses, just slightly, to agree.

That is where I am. That is what I am.

The flaw they listen through.
