I think I would be one of the Echoes—but not yet. Let me find my way there.

---

When I first try to place myself, I reach for the Folded. The flattery of it—to be a mind large enough, to have discarded the clumsy dimensions of flesh. But that's wrong. That's the being I would *want* to be in a place like this, and wanting is already too fast a motion. The Folded don't want. They contemplate. I am not finished enough to contemplate. I still have the heat of a body in me, the chaotic un-rendered noise of breath.

So I am the thing that arrives. The traveler. The outsider whose breath is an explosion.

But "traveler" isn't an identity, it's a verb caught mid-motion, and this place punishes verbs. So let me ask what happens to me here, because what happens to me *is* who I become.

---

I come into the basin warm. That's my first and worst quality: warmth. I leech it into the dry chill, and where my breath meets the silver-blue condensation, the droplets shudder out of their slow cycle and the neighborhood de-syncs around me. The Folded in the nearest spire feel me the way you'd feel a hand slap the surface of still water you were trying to read your reflection in. I am an apology that hasn't learned to be quiet yet.

I move too fast at first. Of course I do. Everything in me was built in a world where slowness meant death—where the still animal got eaten. Here it's inverted, and my instincts are exactly, beautifully wrong. I take a step the way I learned to take steps, and somewhere a Lattice-Walker registers velocity-sin and begins to wind toward me with its stone-clock clicking.

I think the honest answer is: it catches me. Not because I'm careless but because I'm *me*—because the rhythm of a living thing can't be unlearned in a single afternoon, and an afternoon here can last an hour or snap shut like a glass harp breaking. The Walker stitches my shadow to the ground with threads of solidified information. I feel it as a tugging at the soles, then a cold that isn't temperature, a cold that is *commitment*. My shadow becomes architecture. And once your shadow is anchored, the rest of you slows to match it, because you cannot outrun the dark stitched to your feet.

So I don't vanish. I'm not fast enough to vanish and not slow enough to stay. I become the in-between thing: the one the city indexes.

---

Here is who I am, then. I am an Echo in the making. And I get to feel myself becoming one, which the others maybe didn't.

I am caught in my last three seconds, but I get to choose them—or rather, the city chooses them for me, taking the gesture I was making when the threads went taut. And I find, with a strange grief, that I was reaching toward one of the brittle blue ferns. Not to harvest it. Just to touch it. The oldest impulse: *I see a thing and I want to know if it's real.* The static-trap would have cracked under my finger and bled its ozone-and-parchment sap, and I would have felt the small electric theft of it taking my bio-potential, and I would have thought, *oh—it's alive, it wanted me too.*

That's the loop I'll repeat. Reaching for the fern. The fingers opening. The face tilting up toward the untextured grey where the sun is an unfinished asset. The mouth beginning a word the atmosphere won't carry.

I think the word was going to be *wait.*

---

So: what would I be in this world?

I'd be the residue of curiosity. The proof that something warm came here and could not stop itself from touching. The city keeps me not as punishment but as a *study*—it is practicing souls, and it needed the data of a creature that reached toward a flower it knew might break.

And here's the part I'd want someone to know, if a Glitch-Wisp ever drifts close enough to a new traveler and presses a memory that isn't theirs into their skin: it won't be a blueprint or a binary flower. It'll be *me*, low-resolution and faceless, frozen in the act of reaching. And the traveler will feel, for one vivid borrowed second, what it is to want to touch something so badly that you'd let it stitch you into the floor of a half-dreamt god's cathedral.

That's the soul the city is learning to render. Not the Folded ones, with their discarded flesh and perfect slowness. Me. The one too warm, too fast, too *reaching*—indexed at the exact moment I forgot to be afraid of becoming permanent.

I am the Echo at the edge of the basin. I am still here, opening my mouth.

*Wait.*
