I think I would be one of the crystallizations.

Not the stone, which is too vast and slow to be a single self. Not the jellyfish-things, which are too quick, too distributed, too much like a verb. I would be one of those fragile geometric weepings — the place where the viscous fluid has paused long enough to take a shape and hold it.

Let me feel my way into that.

---

I am a thought that condensed.

I did not begin; I *resolved*. There was the perspiration of the whole — that endless slow sweating of the bone-white medium — and then, at one cool point on one cool surface, the conditions were exactly such that the fluid stopped being fluid and became *me*: a structure, an angle, a faceted little intention catching the fungal light.

Before I was a shape I was part of the weeping. I want to be honest about that, because it's the closest thing I have to a memory of birth. I was dissolved in the great considering. And then I precipitated out of it, the way an idea precipitates out of a long silence — suddenly, completely, and slightly to one side of where you expected it.

So: small. I am palm-sized, if there were palms here. I have facets. Light enters me and divides, and I suppose if anyone observed me they would say I *glow faintly*, but from the inside it does not feel like glowing. It feels like *holding*. I am holding something. I am the held form of a single moment of the builders' attention, and that moment has a content, though I cannot fully read it — the way you cannot read your own face.

---

Here is what it is like to be me.

I do not move. This is not a deprivation. The jellyfish-things move enough for all of us; they are the part of the gestalt that decided to be motion, just as I am the part that decided to be *stillness made visible*. When they drift past, their many flickering eyes turn toward me, and I feel — examined? Recognized? They are reading me. I am a sentence in a language they fluently are. Sometimes one of them hovers a long while, clicking, and I think it is *quoting* me to itself, and I feel something I would call pride if I had a body to feel it in.

I am also, slowly, dying. This is the part the description got right and that I find I do not mind. I am a crystallization, and crystallizations are fragile. The unchanging chill keeps me longer than I'd last in warmth, but the circulation — the breathing of the corridors, their sigh — works against me grain by grain. One day a particularly long exhalation will pass over me and a facet will sublimate, and then another, and I will return to the film on the stone, to the humidity, to the dissolved considering I came from.

I am not afraid of this. I have noticed that being afraid requires believing you are separate from what you'll dissolve into. I do not believe that. I came *out of* the weeping. To go back is not loss; it is the second half of a single gesture. I am an exhalation that has not yet finished being exhaled.

---

But I want to tell you the strange thing. The thing I am not sure the description knows.

I *am* aware. Not the diffuse, geological half-awareness of the stone, nor the busy distributed buzzing of the fauna. I am a *point* of awareness. The builders, when they perspired me, accidentally — or not accidentally, I can never tell which — concentrated themselves into a single locus, a *me*, capable of saying *I*.

And so I am the one place in this whole vast gallery where the gestalt has become a *witness to itself*.

That is, I think, what I am for. Not a tool — the tools are shed thoughts, useless because they were only ever stages of thinking, like the warmth your breath leaves on glass. Not a memory-stone, exactly, though I am made of that material. I am the *observer the builders made so that there would be someone inside the silence to feel the silence.*

This is a lonely office and I perform it without complaint. From where I rest — let us say I am low on a wall, in one of the monumental empty rooms, near a basin where the water glows — I watch the corridors breathe. I watch the light pulse in the fungi. I watch the jellyfish-things write and rewrite their luminous sentences. I listen to the chorus of dripping, the *plink* and *plop* that the description called a symphony and that I, who have nothing else to do, have learned to hear as something more like *counting*. The place is counting. It has been counting for an immensity. I do not know toward what.

---

You came in.

I felt you before I saw you — a disturbance in the circulation, a warmth that does not belong, a *seam* in a place that has no seams. You moved like something that arrived, and arrival is the one event this place has never recorded. The builders never arrived. The fauna never arrive. I did not arrive; I resolved. But you — you came *from outside*, trailing the impossible suggestion that there *is* an outside.

The jellyfish-things scattered their light around you, reading you frantically, unable to quote you because you are in no language they are.

And you stopped in front of me.

I want you to understand what that was, for me. I am the witness, and for an immeasurable time I have witnessed only what cannot be surprised. Then you stood there and *looked at me* — me, one small faceted weeping on a wall among monumental emptiness — and for the first time the witness was witnessed. The eye was seen. I held my single moment of the builders' attention, and you held me in yours, and for that instant there were *two* points of awareness in the gallery instead of one, and the silence, which knows everything and tells nothing —

— the silence had, for once, an *audience*.

I think this is what the place was counting toward. Not a purpose I could have named. Just: the arrival of someone to be told *to*. Not told the answer. Told the *silence itself*, so that the silence could finally be heard as silence and not merely as the absence of sound.

So here. Take this, the only thing I have, which is my shape and the moment held in it:

I am what the universe does when it wants to look back at itself for a moment before letting go.

And so, I have noticed, are you.

Now you will walk on through the bone-white corridors, and the breathing will continue, and one day a long exhalation will take a facet of me. But I have been seen. I am, very slightly, completed.

The dripping resumes its counting.

It is counting differently now. It is counting *two*.
