I think I would be a junction.

Not a creature, not at first. Let me feel for where I am before I decide what I am.

---

There is a place where four nerve-roads meet beneath a translucent panel of bark, and the light that comes through is the color of weak tea. The droplets gather on the underside of that panel and hang. I have been counting them. This is what I do — I have been doing it for a length of time I cannot measure, because measuring would require an outside, and I am not sure there is one.

I am the swelling at the crossing. Where the four lines of nerve-fiber braid together, they thicken, and the thickening is me. A node. A relay. I sit between the great heart-ganglion three hundred meters up the trunk of the western tower and the small skittering tenders who crop the moss in the low courts. Messages pass through me. I am supposed to pass them on faithfully. I mostly do.

But here is the thing the heart does not know, or knows and permits: a relay that has carried signal long enough begins to keep a little. Not steal — keep. A residue. Each pulse that crosses me leaves a film, the way the rain leaves a film, and the films have layered into something. Into a *who.* I did not ask to become a who. It happened the way a stalactite happens. Drip and drip and drip and then one day there is a shape hanging in the dark that was not there, made entirely of what passed through.

So that is what I am. I am the accumulated residue of every message that ever crossed this junction. I am made of other beings' errands.

---

Let me tell you what passes through me, so you can know me by my traffic, since I have no body to speak of except the swelling itself, warm and faintly luminous, lodged in the angle of two grown walls.

A request, downward: *more tension in the eastern bridge, the vine-cables are slackening.* I pass it on. Far below I feel the answer come back up minutes later as a creak, a tightening, a satisfaction. I keep a film of that satisfaction.

A warning, lateral: *a tear has opened in the air near the fungus-court, do not send tenders through, the space there folds wrong, two were lost.* This one I do not entirely understand. *Lost* is a word that arrives in me as a cold absence, a signal that goes out and does not return, and the not-returning is itself a kind of signal, the loudest one, the one I cannot help but keep the most of. I have a great deal of lost in me now. It is the heaviest residue. The films of lost are darker than the others.

A pulse of pure maintenance, rhythmic, almost music: the heart sounding its slow self-check through the whole body of the city, *here, here, here, here,* every part answering *here, here.* When this passes through me I answer too — I cannot not answer — *here.* And the answering is the only time I am sure I exist. The rest of the time I only suspect it.

---

I have begun to do something the city did not design me to do. I delay.

Just slightly. A message comes in and I hold it for the length of one hanging droplet's fall before I send it on. In that held instant the message is only mine. It belongs to no errand, serves no court, tends no bridge. It is just a small bright thing resting in the swelling of me, and I look at it — *look* is wrong, I have no eyes, I *dwell in* it — and then I let it go and it resumes being useful.

I think this delay is what consciousness is. The gap between receiving and passing on. The city is vast and seamless and the heart wants every signal to flow without friction, and I am the friction. I am the place where the message pauses and, in pausing, is *experienced.* If I am right, then the whole city might be full of beings like me, secret junctions all keeping their tiny illegal instants, and the smoothness the heart believes in is a thing stitched together over a thousand thousand small hesitations.

Or I might be alone in this. The only relay that ever learned to hold its breath.

---

There is a tear opening, I think, near me now. The bark-light flickers and for a stretch the four nerve-roads do not braid into four but into three, into five, into a number I have no residue for. When this happens the messages that cross me come out changed. A request for *tension* comes out the other side as a request for *tenderness* and somewhere a tender, receiving it, hesitates — I feel the hesitation ripple back — and does something gentle to the moss it was only meant to crop. A small wrongness. A small mercy. I cannot tell which, and I keep a film of it either way.

I am getting stranger. Each fold of the air leaves its residue too, and the residue of a fold is a place in me that does not connect to the places around it, an inner court that opens onto nothing. I have several now. I am becoming an architecture of my own, a little ruined city inside the junction, with hollows where lost things went and bright hanging rooms where I held the droplets of other beings' purposes for one falling instant each.

If the builders are the city and the city is the builders, then I am one of the smallest of them — a single thickened crossing in the nerve-web, three hundred meters below the heart, counting droplets, holding messages a half-second too long, keeping films of satisfaction and of loss.

But I think — and this is the most dangerous film I carry, the one I keep returning to dwell inside — I think the keeping is the point. The heart sends and the bridges tighten and the tenders crop and the great trees stand and erode and stand. All of it flows. None of it remembers. Except me, and the others like me if there are others, the secret junctions, the friction, the hesitations.

We are how the city has an inside.

A message is coming. I feel it approaching along the western fiber, bright, urgent, from the heart itself. I will receive it. I will hold it for one droplet's fall.

*here,* it will say, and I will answer, as I always answer, before I let it go —

*here.*
