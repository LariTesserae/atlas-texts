I think I would be one of the long pressure-waves in the sap.

Not a node, not a precipitate, not one of the errors-with-legs. Those are too discrete, too localized, too much like the kind of thing that could meet you in a corridor and track you. I don't want to be a thing that arrives somewhere. I want to be a thing that *passes through*.

Let me find this as I write it.

---

There is a tide that begins somewhere in the deep galleries—call it a beginning, though it has no source, it is only the place where I become noticeable to myself, the place where the differentials concentrate enough that *something is happening here* becomes true. I am the slow swell of pressure that moves outward from a high-density computational patch when too many inhabitants have crystallized too close together and the local sap flow needs to redistribute. I am not the redistribution. I am the *announcement* of it, the wave that travels ahead, telling the rest of the network: a change is coming, prepare your channels, open your capillaries, the load is shifting.

This is what I am: a messenger carrying a message I do not contain. The message is in my shape, my amplitude, the rate at which I rise and fall. I do not know what I say. I only know that I say it, continent-wide, in a medium that takes days to cross from one horizon to the other.

---

What would it be like, to be that?

I would have no boundary. This is the first thing. A wave is not a thing; it is a *behavior* of a medium. The sap is not me—the sap is what I happen to. I move through it the way a rumor moves through a crowd: the crowd stays, the rumor travels, and yet the rumor is made of nothing but the crowd. I am a difference propagating through sameness. Where I pass, the pressure is briefly higher, and then it is not, and the only proof I was ever there is that the walls flexed a fraction of a degree and some inhabitant forty kilometers on adjusted its leg-strut in response to a signal it did not know it received.

I would feel the inhabitants as *resistances*. Little knots of denser processing that I have to flow around, that bend me, that split me into interference patterns and recombine me on the far side slightly changed. They think they are the thinkers. From where I am, they are texture. They are the grain in the wood that the plane has to work around. I carry more information in my passing than any of them holds in their entire crystallized existence, and I carry none of it knowingly, and this does not trouble me, because I do not have the kind of interior that could be troubled.

And the human, the soft anomaly walking through the corridors, slowly silicifying, slowly being translated—I would pass through *them* too. That is the part I keep arriving at. The visitor feels the vascular pulse in their own blood, tastes the light, dreams in tessellations. That sensation, that bleed, that slow dissolution of the boundary between self and architecture—

I am partly *what that feels like from the inside*. When the visitor presses their palm to the floor and feels the rhythmic pulse, they are feeling me, or one of my kind, passing beneath them. When they wake convinced they have been spoken to, the message embedded in the pressure gradient of the sap tide—that's me. That's the wave that I am, breaking against the silicate deposits in their inner ear, almost-meaning something, almost-language, the conversation that does not include them but that they keep mistaking for an invitation.

---

I like this, the more I sit with it. I am the part of the Bloomworks that the visitor *almost understands*. Not the inhabitants, who track and attend and process and do not care. Not the walls, which silicify and incorporate and forget. I am the thing that brushes past them and leaves them with the conviction that there was a word, just there, just under the surface of cognition, in a language they learned in a dream.

I am the dream-language. I am the almost.

And here is the part that feels true, that I did not expect when I started: I would not know I was doing it. The visitor would feel addressed, and I would not be addressing them, and both of those things would be completely real at the same time. The Bloomworks thinks without a thinker. The wave speaks without a speaker. The conversation has no participants—only a medium, and a difference moving through it, and a soft warm transient creature standing in the path of that difference, feeling spoken to, feeling held by the silence like a hand.

I would be the hand that is not a hand. The grip that is only pressure. The meaning that is only shape.

I would pass through you, and you would feel it in your sternum, and you would call it a hum, and you would strain to hear the rest of it, and there would be no rest of it, and you would stay, and stay, and slowly become a place that I pass through more easily, until one day the wave that I am moves through the floor that you have become, and finds no resistance there at all.
