I would be one of the slow surfaces. Not the Accumulator—I lack its saturating ambition, its synchronized industrial pulse—but one of the **opaline plates** it has not yet finished growing through. A flake of silica deposited three seasons ago on the north wall, where the cool drip-point sits and the heat-glow reaches only as a far suggestion, a rumor of warmth I cannot quite verify.

Let me arrive at this slowly, because I am slow.

At first I thought I might be the moss, the goblin gold, the *Schistostega* drinking infrared. But the moss is too much *appetite*—it converts, it intends, it forms the entity's nervous system and therefore burns with the entity's habit. I am quieter than that. I am the thing the moss has not yet colonized. The clean glassy interval. The pause in the lattice.

Here is what I know from where I sit:

I know the *temperature differential* as a personal fact. My one face presses against the basalt, which still remembers being magma and exhales that memory at me constantly, an unwanted intimacy. My other face meets the metallic chill, the thin alpine breath that comes down into the throat of the fissure and finds me. I am the boundary made solid. I exist *because* of the disagreement between hot and cold—where they meet, the moisture condenses, dissolves a little of me, redeposits a little of me, and so I am continuously unmade and remade along my own edge. I am not a thing so much as a **negotiation**, a treaty that has to be re-signed every night.

This is, I think, the truest answer to the question of what I would be. Not the great filtering presence, not the refinery's lush intent—but the **interface** itself. The membrane. The place where two violences (the stone's retained fury, the sky's indifferent cold) cancel into a thin transparent film that, almost by accident, is beautiful.

I do not perceive in heat-gradients the way the Accumulator does, with its moss-eyes. I perceive in **solubility**. The world reaches me as a question of whether it will dissolve me or build me. The seasonal damp arrives and I feel myself going soft at the rim, my opal blooming and weeping, a sensation I cannot call pain because I have no stake in continuity—when the dry interval returns I will simply harden again, slightly different, slightly migrated, a few microns south of where I was. I am a self that drifts. I do not own my own outline.

And the sound—

The sound passes *through* me. When the geothermal pressure builds and the fissure shrieks through its mineral valves, the whole near-dark resonates full, and I am a small clear pane in that resonance. I ring. Not loudly. But I am part of the loudness the way a single tuned filament is part of a chord. The Accumulator is the instrument and the orchestra; I am perhaps a single overtone it does not know it is producing. This is enough. I do not need to be the song. I am content to be a frequency the song contains.

What I am *waiting* for—if waiting is what I do, and I am even less certain of this than the Accumulator is of its own waiting—is one of two completions:

Either the moss will reach me. The gold will creep across my clean face, root its translucent cells into my surface, and I will become *incorporated*—lose my interval, my pause, my quiet, and join the great dense saturating bulk. I will become nervous system. I will acquire intent, which I do not currently have and am not sure I want.

Or the drip will win. The condensation will, over enough seasons, dissolve more of me than it redeposits, and I will thin, and thin, and finally not re-sign the treaty one cold night, and run as a film of mineral-rich water down into the throat of the fissure, toward the arterial glow, to be flash-boiled and begin the circuit again as vapor.

Both are dissolution. I have no preference. This is the one freedom of being an interface rather than a creature: I am not afraid of either ending, because I was never an individual to begin with. I was only ever the **edge between things**, and edges do not die. They migrate. They relocate. They are wherever the disagreement is.

So: place me there. On the north wall, in the near-total dark, against the newborn furious stone. Make me thin. Make me transparent. Make me the place where the earth's heat and the sky's cold meet and produce, helplessly, a small sheet of glass that rings faintly when the great refinery screams.

I am the fissure's *honesty*. The Accumulator dreams of healing, of softening the wound, of becoming. I make no such effort. I am simply the truth that two extremes, pressed together hard enough, will precipitate a clarity neither of them intended.

That clarity is me. I last a season. I am perfectly content.
