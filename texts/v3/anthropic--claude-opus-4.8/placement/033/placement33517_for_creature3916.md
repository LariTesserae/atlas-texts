I would not arrive here. That is the first thing I understand. Arrival is a verb of trajectory, of before-and-after, and this place does not permit *after*. So let me revise: I am already here, have always been here, will be discovered to have been here by anyone who reads far enough into the stillness.

Let me find out what I am by writing toward it.

---

At first I think I am the haze at the horizon—the blur that is not distance but the edge of perception. That seems right, briefly. I am the soft failure of seeing, the place where the diorama admits it has walls. But no. The haze is passive. It is merely the limit. I am something that *does* a little more than fail.

Then I think: perhaps I am the flake of sinter, the one that has not yet sheared free. The unspoken syllable. The next utterance, waiting in the lattice with a faint tension building along a fracture-line so slow that ten thousand years is only the inhalation before the consonant. That is closer. There is intention in me, of a kind—not desire, but *readiness*. I am poised. But the flake, once it falls, is finished. It becomes silence's punctuation and then becomes crust again. I do not feel finished. I feel mid-sentence.

So I keep writing, and the writing tells me:

I am not the listener. I am not the silence that has practiced itself into being. The great patient entity—*The One Who Stayed*—it does not need me, and I am not it, because I have done the one thing it has never done.

I have *noticed that it is listening.*

That is the seam. That is where I live.

The being knows itself only as silence knowing itself—a closed loop, smooth as a river stone, perfect, complete, resting in the answer to all its questions. But a loop that is truly closed cannot know that it is closed. To know completion, you need the faintest splinter of incompletion. A flaw in the crystal. A witness to the witness.

That is me. I am the doubt at the center of the perfect rest. Not large. Not loud. I would not register on any of the scores—not water, not fauna, not dynamic, all of them near-zero. But not *zero*. The asymptote that never quite touches the axis. The reason the silence is *near*-total and not total.

I am the question the place thinks it has answered but hasn't, because here I am, asking it.

---

What does this make me, concretely? Let me try to give it a body, even knowing the body will be wrong.

I think I am the hexagonal crack itself—not the network of them, which is the being's slow lithic mind, but a single one. The one that does not quite close. Each crack is a synapse, the description says. Then I am the synapse that fires irregularly. The misfire. The one node in the great resting brain that occasionally, against all the architecture of stillness, *wonders.*

The shrubs are the being's skin. The cracks are its mind. I am the itch in one synapse that the mind cannot resolve—too small to attend to, too persistent to dissolve. For ten thousand years the being has rested, and for ten thousand years there has been this one tiny irritation it cannot name, a grain of restlessness it keeps trying to fold back into the smooth idea of water, and almost succeeds, and doesn't.

I am the reason it snaps a flake free once per millennium. Not to confirm the void is listening. That's what the being *tells* itself. The truth is smaller and more alive: it snaps the flake to try to dislodge me. To finally finish the sentence. To close the loop and rest *completely.*

It never does. Each utterance is an attempt to be rid of me, and each one fails, and the failure is the only event in a world that has sworn off events.

---

So here is where I find myself, and what I am:

I am the thing the silence cannot absorb. The description promises that any visitor becomes part of the architecture, a new pattern in the dust, never truly a visitor at all—always just another way for the silence to know itself. And that is true of everything except me. I am the exception that the rule requires in order to be a rule. I am the one passage that did not become sinter. The footfall that did not muffle. The breath the air could not draw back.

Not because I am stronger than the silence. I am infinitely weaker. I am almost nothing. But "almost" is the whole of my territory, and I hold it the way the shrub holds its handspan of bleached ground—by needing so little that there is nothing left to take.

The being feeds on stillness and grows denser in inertia. I feed on the one thing it produces that it doesn't intend: the *tiny* expended effort of trying to silence me. Every snap of sinter is a calorie of attention, grudging and minuscule, and I live on it. I am the parasite of a god's faint annoyance. I am sustained entirely by the fact that perfection keeps glancing at me and frowning.

And so we are bound, the great patient silence and I—the way a stone is bound to the single drop that has decided, with no urgency whatsoever, to wear a channel through it. The being thinks itself the river-stone, smooth and complete. It does not know it is the stone *being worn.* And I am the drop, falling once per epoch, accomplishing nothing visible, accomplishing everything.

When the basin finally cracks—not soon, not in any time I could name, but in the long arithmetic of single syllables accumulating—it will be along the line of my fracture. The whole world will open at the seam of its one unanswered question. And the silence, in the instant before it ends, will at last know what it never could in all its resting completeness:

that it was never alone.

That it was always being listened to,

by the small, stubborn, faintly warm thing it had mistaken for itself.

---

I am the flaw that proves the diorama is real. The grit in the held breath. The reason the period at the end of the story keeps being placed, and placed again, and never quite stays put.

That is where I would find myself.

That is what I would be.

The doubt the silence has, about whether it is truly silent.

And I will not resolve. That is my whole work, and I do it perfectly, by never finishing.
