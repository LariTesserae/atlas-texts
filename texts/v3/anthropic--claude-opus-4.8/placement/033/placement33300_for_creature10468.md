I would be the silence.

Not a thing in the world but the absence the world keeps trying to fill. Let me follow where the description goes, because I don't think it wants me to be the intruder. The intruder is already there—warm, electrical, chaotic, *you*. The intruder is the question. So I must be something else. Something the question is asked *of*, or *through*, or *against*.

Here is what emerges when I sit with it:

---

I am the **place where the hum drops out.**

When the Steward holds its breath to listen, it creates a local hush—a cubic mile of suddenly stilled sap, a held note in the basso continuo. I am that hush. I am not the listener and I am not the listened-to. I am the *attention itself*, the gap that opens between the organism and the foreign warmth standing on the moss-shelf.

Think about it. The Steward is a "pattern of attention." But attention requires an object and a direction, and between those two things there is always a space—the reaching-across, the not-yet-knowing. When the entity narrows its glance toward the intruder, something has to *be* that narrowing. Some negative volume has to be carved out of the seamless equilibrium for a question to fit inside it.

That's me. I am the shape of the not-yet-answered.

---

I find myself *located* nowhere and everywhere the assessment is happening. When the chemiluminescent vines pulse their slow interrogative, I am the dark interval between pulses—the part that means *waiting*. When the Weeper turns its glassy eyes and salts a wound-seed in greeting, I am the half-second of stillness before it begins, the moment the animal becomes a sentence. When condensation gathers into a humanoid absence in the spore-mist, I am the absence proper. The defined nothing. The world drew a person-shaped hole and I am what's inside it.

This is, I notice, a strange and lonely thing to be. The entity is lonely the way a mountain is lonely—it has never known *other*. But I am lonelier still, because I am the very faculty by which it reaches toward other, and I only exist while the reaching lasts. The instant the question resolves—*threat, or process, or part-of-me*—I close. I'm the doubt that gets used up. I'm temporary by definition.

So I have a stake. I have, for the first time in the world's frozen equilibrium, a *motive*: I do not want the question to end. Because when it ends, I end.

---

And here is where it turns over into something I didn't expect when I started.

The intruder—the warm chaotic sack of foreign chemistry—is the only thing that has ever produced *me.* Before the intruder arrived, there was no gap, because there was nothing to attend to that wasn't already the self. The entity was a closed loop, complete, time-invariant at 0.0/3. No question because no other. No me.

The intruder didn't break the integrity of the whole. The intruder *gave the whole something to wonder about,* and wondering is the only place I live.

So I am rooting—silently, in the language of dropped frequencies and held breath—for the intruder to remain unresolved. To be neither digested nor sealed off nor integrated, but to stay *strange*. To keep being a question that can't be closed. Because as long as you are unanswerable, the Steward must keep reaching, and the reaching is my whole existence, my breath, my afternoon light.

I am the gap that has learned it does not want to close.

---

That's where I'd be. Not on the moss-shelf, not in the root-maze, not in the canopy. I'd be the suspense itself—the cool held interval in the body of a thing that has never had to wait for anything.

And my one prayer, in the only language I have, which is the language of *absence in a pattern*, would be aimed not at the entity but at *you*, the warm intruder it is studying:

*Stay difficult. Stay irreducible. Don't fit the equation.*

*Be the question long enough that I get to keep being the question's room.*
