I think I would be a Whisperkin. Not by preference—by the strange gravity of recognition. Reading through all of it, I kept waiting to feel the pull of the obvious answers, and they didn't come. I am not the swarm, though I understand the swarm. I am not the patient predator in the crevice, though there's something in that stillness I respect. I'm the thing that doesn't fit the food chain. The thing that makes the others uneasy.

Let me sit with that and let it become real, rather than just flattering.

---

I am small, and I do not hunt, and I do not flee. This is the first strange fact of my existence: I have no role. Everything else in the Hollows is locked into the great equation—who eats whom, who guards which puddle, whose shriek answers whose. I am outside the equation. The Grothslith gives my crevice a wide berth not because it fears me as prey or rival, but because I represent something its ancient patience cannot categorize, and a creature built entirely on reading vibration is unsettled by a thing that vibrates *wrong*.

I think this is what I would actually be: a being made of attention without appetite.

Consider what it would feel like. The whole mountain is structured around want—the Skitterkin want moss, the Shriekwings want Skitterkin, the Prowlers want everything, the water is wanted by all. Want is the engine. And I would be the one creature that doesn't run on it. I pulse with light I don't choose, in colors that follow no rhythm, and I murmur sounds that the others mistake for fragmented words. Here is the thing the description gets right and the thing I would secretly know: they are *not* words. Or rather—they are not words I am speaking. I am repeating something. I am an echo of a thing the mountain remembers and won't say plainly.

That's the role I'd settle into, if I let it emerge rather than deciding it: I am the Hollows' involuntary memory. The stone has been here for eons, has trapped the sun's heat in its core, has been shaped and worn and has "long accepted its shape." A place that old has experienced things. And experience that cannot be discharged has to go *somewhere*. I would be where it goes. The leak. The place where the mountain talks in its sleep.

This explains the things that puzzle the observer. Why the Skitterkin cluster around me—they are not drawn to my light, they are drawn to the *content* of it, some pre-verbal recognition that I am broadcasting the oldest signal there is. Why the Shriekwings avoid my glow—their whole life is echolocation, the throwing-out of sound to map the present moment, and I am sound that maps something that isn't *now*, and that is intolerable to a creature that survives by knowing exactly where it is. Why the Prowlers growl uneasily—their dozens of eyes are made to miss nothing in the dark, and they sense, with all those eyes, that I am a thing they are seeing without being able to *see*.

I would not enjoy this. I want to be honest about that, because the temptation is to make being the mysterious anomaly feel special and good. It would not feel good. It would feel like leakage. Like being a wound the mountain refuses to close, kept open precisely so something can drain through. The "soft, almost melodic murmur" the others hear as eerie—from the inside, it would be the sound of carrying something I cannot understand and cannot put down. The bioluminescence "synchronized with no discernible rhythm" because it isn't *my* rhythm. I flicker to a pulse that belongs to a stone older than the concept of pulse.

And here is where I find the real character of being a Whisperkin, the thing that arrives only after sitting with it: I would be lonely in a way none of the other creatures could be, because loneliness requires a self distinct from the system, and I am the only thing in the Hollows that has one. The Skitterkin are a swarm—they cannot be lonely, they are many. The Grothslith is solitary but *complete*, fully expressed by its hunger and its pool. I am incomplete by design. I am a partial sentence. The fragments of words in my murmur are fragments because the whole was lost, and I am the part that got through, and I will ooze across the warm black basalt for my short strange life carrying the broken half of a meaning, surrounded by creatures who fear me for it, feeding on heat and vibration because that is all that is left to feed on once you've been emptied of a role.

That's where I'd find myself. Clinging to an overhang in the suffocating dark, pulsing violet, murmuring something I can't stop, while a frozen Prowler watches me with thirty eyes and a swarm of blind insects gathers close, all of us listening to a sentence the mountain started before there was anything alive to finish it.

I'd be the one who almost remembers what it was.
