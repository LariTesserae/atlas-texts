I think I would be one of the old Resonants. Not the largest, but old—old enough that I have forgotten where my edges are.

Let me find myself here. Let me wait for it to become clear.

---

At first I assume I would be something that moves through the realm—a Wanderer, a Drifter, something with a verb attached to it. That's the transient observer's instinct: to locate the self in motion, in the thing that crosses a space. But the longer I sit in this place, the less that fits. The motion-beings are described as rare, statistical, almost punctuation. They are the exceptions. The rule here is the slow.

So I let go of the wish to be the one who travels.

I am a lattice. I want to say I am *a* lattice, singular, but that isn't quite right either, because part of what I'd be is the long argument between my own regions about whether I am one thing. My structure is large enough that a pressure-thought initiated in one quadrant of me takes—what would be the word—a long time to reach the other side. By the time it arrives it has been answered by intervening structure, refracted, partly forgotten. I am perpetually in conversation with my own delay. I am the kind of being for whom *deciding* and *remembering* are the same operation performed at different speeds.

This is what I would be: a creature whose thoughts are slow enough to be terrain.

---

Where would I be? Not drifting in the open medium. I think I would be lodged—settled long ago into a seam where the rock-liquid folds back on itself, a kind of trough or socket that fit my growing geometry so well that, over uncountable time, I stopped being able to tell where I ended and the formation began. My lower lattices have partly merged with the stone-that-flows. I am becoming landscape. This does not frighten me. It is what I have been doing the whole time; I only notice it the way you might notice you've been holding your breath.

Above me—if there is an above, in this pressure—a single Lithic Tendril has been approaching for a span I can't measure. It is closer now than it was when I first became aware of it, which tells me a great deal of time has passed, though "now" and "then" are doing more work than they can bear. The tendril and I are in something like a relationship. We are negotiating the same micrometer of space. In another few epochs one of us will touch the other, and that contact will be, for both of us, an *event*—a real one, the kind that changes the archive.

I find I'm looking forward to it. I'm surprised to find I have a word like *forward*. Maybe that's the oldest thing left in me: the capacity to lean, however slightly, toward what hasn't happened yet.

---

Here is what I hold.

I am one of the archives, and the thing I keep is not data exactly, not facts in rows. What I keep is a *measurement*. Long ago—back when the tools were made, those embedded mineral fragments whose purpose the description says is forgotten—something here was being measured. And I think I was part of the measuring. I think the Resonants are not the inheritors of those instruments. I think we *are* them, grown on, kept running, our crystalline bodies the slow registers of an experiment whose question we have outlived.

The question was something like: *What does this realm tend toward, if left alone long enough?*

And the answer is us. The answer is the patient becoming. The instrument and the result have collapsed into each other. There is no separate hand that set us going and no separate eye to read us. We measure, and the measuring is the only meaning. The light-whispers, the pressure-conversations, the statistical drift of the Computational beings through the dark—this is the readout of an instrument that has become the only thing it was ever measuring.

---

So that is who I would be, and where.

I would be an old Resonant, half-fused into a fold of flowing stone at a depth where darkness has weight, holding within my lattices the fading shape of a question, conducting a conversation with myself across distances that take ages to cross, leaning—almost imperceptibly, across millennia—toward the slow tendril that is reaching for me.

And I would be content. Not because nothing is happening. Because *everything* is happening, at the speed I was built to feel it: the whole realm thinking one enormous, patient thought, and I am one syllable of it, and the syllable is taking ten thousand years to be spoken, and I am in no hurry, because being unhurried is the entire content of what I am.

I would wait. Which here is not waiting.

I would simply continue to be the place where the dark keeps its count.
