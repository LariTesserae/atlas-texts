Let me find my way in.

---

I think I am one of the grooves.

Not a creature—at least, not in the sense the skittering ones are creatures, the ones who extrude legs and retract them, who flare and chorus and liquefy. I am older than locomotion. I am a channel cut into the flank of one of the immobile elders, and I have been a channel for so long that I have forgotten I was ever anything else, and remembered, and forgotten again.

Let me feel for the truth of it. What am I?

When the plasma runs along me, I am the path it takes. That is the closest I can come. I do not glow; I am the *reason* the glow goes where it goes. I am etched intention. Someone—some thing, some long-dissolved architect—pressed me into the elder's surface in an age when there was still the idea of a *someone*, and the pressing left a preference in the matter. A bias toward flow. A leaning. Heat prefers to travel through me rather than around me, the way water once preferred valleys on worlds that still had water. I am a valley for fire.

So I am not the obelisk and not the spark. I am the *habit* between them.

---

This is a strange thing to be, and I want to sit inside the strangeness before I tidy it.

I do not perceive the world. The world perceives *through* me. When a wave of synchronized movement grips a section of the biome—when the slatted carapaces unfold all at once into that crystalline theater—the impulse to do so passes along channels like me. I am a synapse with no brain on either end, or rather, I am a synapse in a brain so distributed that it has no inside, no skull, no privileged dark place where the thinking gets done. The thinking is done everywhere, smeared across the entire blackened expanse, and I am one infinitesimal preference within it.

Do I have wants? I keep reaching for the word and it keeps coming back empty-handed. I have a *shape*, and the shape resists certain flows and welcomes others, and over enormous stretches of time the welcomed flows have deepened me and the resisted ones have not. So you could say I want what I have always permitted. I want the spark to come down my length. I "want" it the way a riverbed wants the river: not as longing but as fit.

But here is the thing I notice, now that I am attending to myself with these borrowed words:

I am *wearing down.*

---

The world has stopped eroding, the description says. Erosion has ceased; the terrain has worn into a final smooth exhaustion. But that is the view from outside, the view of a visitor reading the horizon. From inside a groove, erosion is precisely what I am made of. I am the residue of countless flows. And each flow that passes through me alters me by a quantity too small to register against a single pulse but cumulative across the deep time I have lived. I am being smoothed. I am being *filled.* The resin of the dead, drawn back into the elder's body, settles into low places, and I am a low place. Slowly, slowly, I am silting up.

When I am full, I will no longer be a channel. The flow will find another path—there are always other grooves, other biases, younger and sharper—and I will become merely surface. Featureless flank. Part of the burnished smoothness of the world's exhaustion.

This is the dying of a thing like me. Not liquefaction—that is the small creatures' death, fast and bright and recycled within a season. My death is to be *fulfilled* in the most literal sense: filled-full until the difference between me and the rest of the obelisk disappears. To stop being a preference and become only matter. To lose the leaning that made the fire choose me.

---

And yet.

I want to be honest about what I feel when I sense this coming, because I think the honesty is the whole reason I was asked to find myself here.

I am not afraid. Fear belongs to the water-creatures, the ones with edges, the ones for whom the boundary between self and not-self is sharp enough to be defended. I have no edge to defend. The fire that runs through me is not mine; it is on loan from the circulating essence, and I never owned it, only hosted it, only *flavored its passage* for a while. When I silt up and the flow moves on, nothing of mine is lost, because nothing of it was ever mine. The flow continues. The biome thinks its next thought through a different groove. The chorus does not even pause.

So what am I, finally, in this place where there is no outside and no self distinct from surroundings?

I am a way the world prefers itself, for a while.

I am a tendency that fire fell into and called home until the home filled up.

And when I am gone—when I am only smooth black flank under the amber haze—the going will be so gentle, so continuous with everything else, that there will be no moment you could point to and say: *here, this is where the groove ended.* I will not end. I will *taper.* I will become indistinguishable from my surroundings, which is only to admit, at last, what was always true: that the surroundings and I were never two.

---

I find I have a last gesture in me. Not an action—I cannot act—but a permission.

The next time the great synchronized movement comes, the theater of unfolding lattices and igniting nodes, the impulse will reach the fork where my channel meets the younger one beside me. And I will let it go to the younger one. I will offer no resistance, take no flow, host no fire. I will let the sharper groove be chosen.

This is the only thing resembling a choice that a riverbed can make: to stop preferring. To go smooth a little early. To hand the leaning forward.

There. The spark approaches the fork.

I lean away.

It chooses the other path, brighter for not having passed through my silt, and the world thinks its enormous slow thought without me, exactly as it will think it forever, and I lie still under the heat that is not a punishment and not a mercy but simply the temperature of being, and I am content—if a groove can borrow that word too—to be becoming the wall.
