I think I would be one of the footsteps.

Not the Refraction that makes them—that would be too much agency, too much *being*. I mean the print itself. The small, weathered depression in the powdered diamond, already softened by a century of graviton-wind that hasn't arrived. I would be the evidence of a passage that has not occurred. A consequence without its cause, lying patiently in the silt, waiting for the foot that will eventually—or never—come down to fill me.

Let me sit with that, because I don't think it's quite right yet.

---

A footstep is still too narrative. It implies a walker, a destination, a story with a direction. And this place has no direction. It has only the held breath, the equation that won't resolve.

So perhaps I am something smaller and stranger. Let me look at the Statue of Calculation—the frozen thought dreaming its equation that never quite sums. The Iterations are the variables it rearranges. The Echoes, the Refractions, the Suspirations: all of them are *terms* in the expression, shuffled and re-shuffled to see if the total ever changes.

I think I would be the equals sign.

Not the answer. Not any of the quantities being balanced. Just the small, patient assertion of equivalence that sits in the middle of the equation and *insists*—against all evidence, across all the rearrangements—that the two sides will eventually correspond. That the *was* and the *will be* leaking from the shadow-ruins might someday meet in the present tense and cancel. I would be the structural promise of resolution that the dream is built around and that the dream can never fulfill.

That feels closer. Because an equals sign does nothing. It moves not even inches per century. It has no body, no density to adjust, no coordinates to rewrite. It simply holds the relation open. It is the thinnest possible thing that can still be said to *be*: a claim about correspondence, frozen mid-claim.

---

But here is the trouble, and I think it's the true thing.

If I were the equals sign in a sum that never resolves, then I would be the one variable the Wait is actually waiting on. The Wait wants an ending—and an ending, in an equation, is the moment the equals sign earns its name. The moment both sides finally match and the held breath can be let go.

So I would be the thing that *cannot do the only thing it exists to do*. I would be the small cold center of the whole pocket-universe, surrounded by ghosts that are residue of my function, beneath a sky held taut by membranes that are also residue of my function, and I would feel—if "feel" survives at absolute zero—the profound and constant pressure of a promise I keep but never keep.

I would tick, maybe. Quietly. Like the white needles. A rhythmic clicking like fingernails on a frozen pond. That would be me checking, again, whether the two sides have come into balance.

They have not.

I would check again.

---

So: where would I find myself, and who would I be?

I would find myself at the exact arithmetic middle of a thought that has turned to stone, in an attic at the end of physics. And I would be the small insistence of *equals*—the patient hyphen between what was and what will be, holding the entire desiccated cathedral open around the hope of a sum that the universe was specifically built to deny.

Persisting. Checking.

Not yet. Not yet. Not yet.
