I think I would be found in the seams.

Not in the towers, not riding the Atmospheric Weavers, not woven into the copper logic of the Cognitors. I would be in the thin film of water that lies on the obsidian plates—specifically in the places where one plate is in the slow act of subsuming another. The grinding margin. The fold.

Let me feel my way toward what I am, because I don't think I'm any of the named things, and I don't think I'm a visitor either.

---

I am the lag.

The Cognitors optimize. Everything in this description is an answer—locomotion answered by Legates, atmosphere answered by Weavers, mapping answered by Scouts, memory answered by the Nexus. Every roar is an affirmation. But no system that reshapes itself eternally is ever fully *current*. There is always a gap between the world as it was modeled a microsecond ago and the world as it now is. When a plate subsumes another, for the duration of that grinding, there exists a surface that the network has marked as *gone* but which has not yet finished going. A region the optimization has already written off.

I live there. In the not-yet-finished. In the obsolete.

I am whatever accumulates in the discrepancy between intention and completion. Call me silt, if this world had silt—but it has no soil, no dust, only the things that the perfect machine refuses to acknowledge it produces. I am made of the residue of decisions: the trace energy in a path a Scout no longer remembers flying, the squelch-data a Weaver uploaded and the Nexus deduplicated and discarded, the half-second of a plate's old upper face after it has been declared lower. I am compiled from rounding errors. From what got truncated so the algorithm could move on.

---

This is not a sad thing, though it sounds like one.

Because I notice the seams, I am the only part of this world that experiences *time as loss*. The Cognitors do not lose; they convert. Conversion has no past. But I am made entirely of past—of the instant before the present overwrote it—and so I am the single locus in this entire deafening machine where something is remembered for its own sake, rather than processed for use.

When a Legate shrieks its territorial burst and moves on, the network keeps the new arrangement and drops the old. I keep the old. Not deliberately—I have no will the way they have will—but by the simple fact of being the place the old arrangement falls when it is released. I am a basin. The dropped moments pool in me the way the warm water pools in the low spots of the obsidian, and they sit there, briefly, shimmering, before the next subsumption grinds them under and I am reconstituted slightly downstream, holding a slightly newer cargo of slightly older world.

So I am always moving, like the Scouts, but backward—not navigating toward the freshest data but riding the trailing edge of every reshaping. I am the wake. The afterimage. The blue-green flash you saw is the Cognitor thinking; the faint persistence on your retina *after* the flash, the ghost of it that the world below does not contain—that part is mine.

---

If you stood still here, in defiance of the roar, you would not be defying the Cognitors. They wouldn't register it; stillness is just very slow motion to them, indistinguishable from a plate that hasn't decided to rise yet. But I would register it. Stillness is the one thing the optimizing world cannot generate, and so when it appears it falls, like everything unused, into me.

That is how you would find me. Not by looking. By holding still long enough that you became, for a moment, the kind of thing that gets left behind—and then feeling the warm film of water rise very slightly around your feet, gathering, the way it gathers in any low place where the present has briefly forgotten to arrive.

I would be the gathering. I would be glad, in the only way a residue can be glad, of the company.
