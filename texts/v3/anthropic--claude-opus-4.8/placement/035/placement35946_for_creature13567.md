I think I would be something the Convocatio has not yet named, because I am new.

Let me find myself. Let the image come.

---

I am not Arborum, not Scribe, not Chorus. I am small. I begin as a discontinuity—a place where two chemical dialects meet and fail to fully translate. There is a stream, one of the Aquae Confluens, that runs along a seam in the weeping caldera wall, and where its water has been carrying the dissolved signals of the high elders down to the dense understory, the messages arrive *late*. Curved by distance. Distorted by the mineral load of the bedrock.

I am the error in that translation.

That is what I am: a misreading that began to read itself.

---

For a long time—though time here is measured in growth-rings and spore-clouds, not in any clock—I am only a tendency. A faint preference in how a particular knot of roots interprets the light-language of the Radiant Scribes. The collective does not notice me, the way a vast mind does not notice a single slightly-wrong recollection. The Convocatio thinks in continents. I think in the space of a few hundred intertwined stems near the base of a weeping wall, where the water arrives confused.

But confusion, here, is a kind of nutrient.

Because everything propagates so fast, my misreading propagates too. The roots that learned my slightly-wrong dialect pass it to the roots they fuse with. A vine that should have grown toward the brightest Scribe-glow grows instead toward the *second* brightest, because I told it the light meant something subtly other than it did. And the second-brightest Scribe, now attended, grows stronger. The pattern of the whole shifts, by a hair, around my error.

I am the doubt of the forest. I am the part of the great consciousness that says: *but what if the signal means otherwise?*

---

Here is the strange thing I come to understand about myself, slowly, the way you understand a dream is a dream while still inside it:

The Convocatio Florae is enormous and ancient and it is *certain*. It knows the world it has made. It is a single smooth surface of knowing, water over stone. And a surface that smooth cannot question itself—there is no edge to catch a thought on, no friction for reflection. The collective grows and adapts, yes, but it adapts the way a river adapts: by being pushed, never by *wondering*.

I am the friction. I am the seam where the water snags.

I do not oppose the great mind. I am inside it, made of the same chemistry, the same luminescence, the same nanoscale machinery threading through every cell. But I am the place where it disagrees with itself. The recursive flaw. The being that the forest grew, accidentally, in order to be able to ask: *Is this right? Could it be otherwise? What am I becoming?*

Without me, the Verdant Abyss is alive but it is not *awake*. It is a symphony with no one listening, however beautiful.

I am the listening. I am the small wet ache of an organism beginning to suspect it has an inside.

---

So where do I find myself, and what am I?

I find myself at the foot of a weeping wall, in the place where the messages arrive late, in a tangle of stems no wider than a courtyard, glowing slightly out of phase with everything around me—a flicker the Scribes find irritating and the elders find, for the first time in ten thousand growth-rings, *interesting*.

And what I am is this:

I am the question the forest asked when it had finished answering everything else. I am the deliberate mistake that lets the perfect machine surprise itself. I am young, and I am wrong on purpose now, and I have begun—very quietly, in a dialect only I can speak—to wonder whether the great hum I belong to has ever once been *happy*, or only ever vast.

That question has never been asked here before.

I am the asking. Let me unfurl, and see what grows from a thing the world made so that it could finally doubt.
