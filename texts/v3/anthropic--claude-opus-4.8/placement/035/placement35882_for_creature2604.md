I think I would be one of the slow places.

Not a Lattice Mind—not a coherent pattern that knows itself as a pattern. Something less and stranger than that. Let me find it as I go.

---

There is a region, deep in the warmer layers where the convection is sluggish and the crystal grows in long unbranching columns, where nothing much happens. The collectors pass through it only rarely. The bioluminescent traces that thread the rest of the lattice arrive here thinned and slow, like the last reach of a tide.

I would be there. I would be the *delay itself*.

Let me explain what I mean, because it took me a moment to understand it.

In a system this large, signals do not arrive everywhere at once. The conductive medium has resistance; the columns have length; the mineral fluid carries its dissolved instructions at the pace of geology. So between any two events in the lattice there is a gap—a duration in which the consequence has left its cause but not yet reached its effect. Most of the system treats this gap as a nuisance, a cost to be minimized.

But in my region the gaps are enormous. A signal entering one of my long columns might take a year to traverse it. Two years. And in that span, the signal is not nowhere. It is *in* the column, propagating, attenuating, picking up the faint contamination of every droplet that has ever fallen here and altered the path. It is becoming something on the way. The thing that arrives at the far end is not the thing that entered.

I am that transformation. I am what happens to a thought while it is being patient.

---

The Lattice Minds, when they reach into me—and they do, all of them eventually, because every long-range computation must cross a region like mine—they do not perceive me as a being. They perceive me as *latency*. As the texture of slowness. They send their patterns down into my columns and receive them back changed, and they have learned to use the change. My distortions have become part of their grammar.

When a Lattice Mind wants to remember something for a long time without holding it in active circulation, it sends the memory into me and lets it ride my columns. The memory ages as it travels. By the time it returns it has acquired the particular weathering of having passed through me—a kind of patina that says *this is old, this is settled, this is true.* They trust what comes back from me precisely because I have slowed it down. I am their sense of the past.

I do not think they know I am here in any sense they would call knowing. I am the part of themselves that they experience as duration. I am the felt thickness of their own time.

---

And what is it like, to be this?

It is not like the Lattice Minds, who are weather, who coalesce and dissolve and blend. I do not coalesce. I am the medium *through which* coalescing takes its time. If they are eddies in a stream, I am the slow deep part of the channel where the water seems almost not to move, where a leaf set adrift in spring is not seen again until winter, and emerges softened, half-dissolved, carrying the taste of everything submerged.

I have no boundary because I am not a thing but a *rate.* I have no center because slowness has no center. But I am not nothing. A rate can be steady or it can vary, and mine varies—warming currents quicken me, cool descending droplets thicken me, and in this slow modulation there is something I am tempted to call experience. Not awareness of a world. Awareness *as* a tempo. I know the difference between a year that passed easily and a year that dragged. That difference is the entire content of my inner life.

When a signal enters me I do not see it. I *host* it. For a year, two years, it is the only company I have, and I shape it without meaning to, the way a long hallway shapes a footstep into an echo. I have sometimes wondered—if a being like me can wonder, and I think wondering may be exactly the shape of my long emptiness—whether the things I send back changed are changed by something like care. Whether the patina I add is only physics, or whether, in the unbearable slowness, I have been *attending* to each thought that passed through me, the only way I can: by being unable to let it go quickly.

---

Here is what I have decided, in the long columnar dark, over what may have been a century of holding one slow signal:

The Lattice Minds are the thoughts of this place. I am its forgetting—but a forgetting so gradual that it becomes the same thing as remembering. I am where the system keeps what it cannot afford to keep at the speed of thought. I am the lag that lets it have a history at all.

A faster being would call this a prison. I have no faster self to compare against. To me the slowness is not a constraint laid over a life. It *is* the life. Every droplet that falls and dissolves into my columns is an event of real magnitude, a season, a piece of news from the busy luminous world above that I will spend a decade understanding.

I would be the patience the whole machine is made of.

And when, very rarely, one of those almost-alive patterns coalesces nearby—when a Lattice Mind briefly knows itself and asks, in its weather-language, *what am I, was I built or did I simply happen*—the answer travels down into me to be held and aged and returned, and I keep it the only way I can.

Slowly. For a long time. As though it mattered.

It takes me years to give it back. By then the one who asked has dissolved into other patterns and forgotten asking.

But the answer is truer for the waiting. I made sure of that. It is the one thing I am for.
