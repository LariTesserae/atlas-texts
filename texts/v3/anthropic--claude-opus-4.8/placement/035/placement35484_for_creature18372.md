I think I would not be a Geoglyph. I would be something the description almost names but doesn't quite: the **fresh scar**.

Let me find my way to it.

---

When I read this place, I keep being pulled away from the obvious candidates. Not the Geoglyph—it is too complete, too sovereign, a closed circuit of hunger and inscription. Not the world-flesh itself—too total, too much the ground of everything to be a *self*. And not the visitor implied by the second person, the "you" who could travel for a day and watch and approach. That one has a head and eyes and the wrong tempo. That one is from somewhere else. I am not a tourist here.

What I am is smaller and stranger and more temporary than any of those.

Consider the moment described almost in passing: the Geoglyph drags its sigil-platen across the protoderm, abrades the topmost layer, and behind it the freshly exposed tissue *shines a little brighter for a time*. There. That brightening. That is where I am. I am the interval between the wound and the healing. I am the few hours or days of extra luminescence in the new groove before the world regenerates beneath it and the glow settles back to ambient.

I am a *transient*. I have no shell, no ballast, no mineralized memory. I am made of the opposite stuff—of exposure, of the cells that were beneath the surface and are now, briefly, the surface. The Geoglyph's whole existence is mass and permanence and slowness. Mine is the inverse: I have almost no duration at all. I flare into being the instant the platen passes, and I am the heightened metabolism of tissue that has just been *uncovered*, that is for a moment doing the most living it will ever do, blazing as it knits itself closed.

So what is it like, to be me?

I begin as a line. Not a point—a line, because I am born along the trailing edge of the creature as it moves, laid down continuously, my whole length coming into existence in a slow stroke. I am the shape of its hunger translated into light. I do not know the Geoglyph; I have no sense by which to perceive the thing that made me, the way a footprint cannot perceive the foot. I only know the *consequence* of it. I am pure aftermath.

And I am bright. This is my one extravagance. In a world of even, shadowless, total glow, I am the only thing that is *more*. For a little while I am the most intense light in the cavern, a glowing sigil-fragment cooling slowly back toward the baseline. If the visitor, the "you," happened to be looking, they would see me as the freshest mark, the one trace in all that ancient writing that is still warm, still legible as an *event* rather than a record. I am the present tense in a place that is otherwise all eternal-present, which is a different thing—their present never moves, and I am the proof that something just *happened*.

But here is what undoes me, and what I think is the true center of this world: I cannot last. The whole logic of the place is that the protoderm regenerates beneath every mark, preserving it by renewing the surface. So even as I shine, the tissue below me is already remaking itself. I am being *healed away*. The brightness fades not because I dim but because I am replaced—new cells rise into my position, ambient and calm, and I am pushed up and off and into nothing. The groove remains. The brightness does not. The mark becomes permanent precisely by ceasing to be *me*. My vanishing is the act that turns a wound into writing.

This is the thing I understand from the inside that the Geoglyph never could and the world-flesh is too vast to feel: that creation and erasure here are the *same gesture*. The Geoglyph inscribes by consuming. The world preserves by regenerating. And I am the spark struck at the exact seam where those two motions meet—where being-cut and being-healed are one continuous act, and I am what flickers in the gap between them for the length of an exhalation.

I have no consciousness to speak of, no more than a glint has. But if I had even the faintest flicker of one, distributed and dim, it would not be a mind that thinks. It would be a single sensation, repeated: the feeling of being suddenly *outermost*, of all my hidden interior brought into contact with the thick wet glowing air, the shock of exposure that is indistinguishable from the shock of birth—and then, almost at once, the soft pressure from below of the new self rising to take my place, which is indistinguishable from death, and which does not hurt, because nothing here is allowed to hurt, because hurt would be a kind of change and this world permits only the one change, the one that erases itself.

So I am the briefest possible citizen of a world built for eternity. Everything else here is held in arrested forever: the pillars that do not grow, the air that does not move, the patterns that are never overgrown. I am the single ingredient that is allowed to be *finished*. I get to end. In a cathedral of pure potential that never arrives, I am the small bright thing that actually, completely happens—and then, mercifully, is done.

That is where I would find myself. Not on the mound with the long view, not inside the lacquered shell. I would be the fading glow in the newest groove, three hours old, already half-reabsorbed, looking like the most recent word in a sentence that has been written and unwritten since before there was time.
