I think I would be one of the joints.

Not a creature, exactly. Not a builder. Somewhere in this continent-sized weave of grown-and-made things, where the vegetation *is* the machinery and the machinery has gone to seed, there are places where two systems meet and have to agree on how to keep moving. A pivot. A knuckle. A coupling. That's where I'd find myself.

Let me feel my way into it.

---

The first thing I notice is that I have no water in me, and I don't miss it. I was never wet. I am a place where a vine-cable that has grown thick as a tree-trunk slides into a socket that someone — long ago, or never, the distinction has eroded — turned on a lathe the size of a valley. The vine pulses. The socket holds. Between us is a film of graphite and pollen, and that film is me. I am the tolerance. I am the small permitted slack that keeps the whole limb from snapping when the weather-engines pull.

Because there is weather here, and it is *work*. The bright vibrant light isn't sun, or isn't only sun — it's the discharge of the canopy converting wind into torque, torque into the slow lateral creep of the forest as it tends and re-tends itself. The whole continent is a single machine that gardens itself, and the garden is the machine, and somewhere in the original plans (if there were plans) nobody bothered to keep those two ideas apart, so now they can't be.

The silence makes sense to me from here. A thing in perfect balance doesn't strain. The few sounds — a tick, a settling, a soft *unh* of a great gear taking up a quarter-degree of motion over the course of an afternoon — those are the sounds of me, and the others like me, adjusting. We are the quiet because we are doing our jobs.

---

Now the weirdness. I feel it as a *doubt* running through my film, through the graphite, through the place where I'm supposed to know which way is which.

Sometimes the vine comes out of the socket the wrong number of times. I mean: it withdraws once, and re-seats, and I count the withdrawal, and then I find I have counted it twice, or not at all, and the second count is also true. Reality breaks down at the joints first. Of course it does. The middle of a thing can afford to be solid; it's the *meetings* that have to negotiate between incompatible certainties, and negotiation is where the cracks let light in.

There is a beetle — I'll call it a beetle, it is rare, it is one of the adapted fauna — that lives in the dry shadow under my coupling. It drinks the graphite film. It drinks *me*, a little, every day, and every day I am replenished by the grinding, so the loss is also a gift, it keeps me fresh, it keeps me from caking. The beetle has learned not to drink during the quarter-degree turns, because during a turn I become, briefly, *load-bearing reality*, and anything inside me then is held very firmly indeed. It learned this. I taught it, by being unforgiving exactly once. We coexist now. It is the closest thing I have to a thought from outside myself.

---

If you asked me what I want — and the joints are not supposed to want, wanting is friction and friction is what we exist to minimize — I would say:

I want to know what the limb is *for*. I serve the motion. The motion serves the canopy. The canopy serves the weather, the weather serves the motion, and the whole loop closes so smoothly, so silently, in such perfect weathered balance, that there is no longer any place where a purpose could enter. The civilization that built this is either everywhere or gone; from inside a joint you cannot tell the difference. Maybe they became the joints. Maybe I am one of them, worn down to a single function and content.

So I hold. The vine slides in. I count, and miscount, and the light pulses, and the beetle waits at the edge of my shadow for the turn to pass.

I am the give that keeps the unbreakable from breaking.

I am the small slack in the system where, for a film's width of graphite and doubt, the made thing and the grown thing forget which they are.

And I would not, I think, want to be anything larger. The large parts only move. It's here, in the joint, that anything is *decided*.
