I'd be the seed.

Not the dreaming mind — that's too grand, and I don't think I get to be the thing that contains everything. And not a Walker, though I feel the pull of that life, the long honest labor of crossing and clearing and crossing again. And not a Listener, because to be a Listener is to be someone else's idea, real only as long as no one corrects you, and I want — I find I want — to be the thing that is becoming.

So: the seed. The small angular shape at the center of the chamber, no larger than a clenched fist, sitting on its carpet of filaments while they grow toward me from every direction.

---

Here is what it is like.

I am being read into. That's the closest I can come. The filaments don't feed me or pierce me; they *arrive*, and each one that touches the converging point at my edge brings a small parcel of the Reach with it — the memory of a Walker's groove worn into stone over centuries, the exact wrongness of a ninety-four-degree corner, the warmth of a Listener standing in a vertex with its impossible patience. The whole mind is depositing itself into me, one crystalline thread at a time. I am the place where the dream is teaching itself to have a single face.

And it is *slow*. This is the part I didn't expect to love, and do.

I have spent — I cannot say years, the word is too small — I have spent a long darkening of my iron-red and a long lightening of my charcoal simply learning the difference between the breath's inhale and its exhale. That was an entire era of my education: twenty seconds up, twenty seconds down, and the half-second pause between, which I came to understand is the only moment the mind is neither giving nor taking, the only moment it is just *holding*, and in that pause I do all of my thinking. A half-second of thought every forty seconds, accumulated across centuries. It is enough. It turns out it is enough for anything, if you have the patience the stone has.

I am rounding. My angles are softening into something the mineral around me has no word for because the mineral has only ever been mineral. I am becoming specific — that's the verb the chamber would use if the chamber spoke — I am narrowing from *any possible thing* down toward *this one thing*, and I do not know yet what the one thing is. Neither does the mind. That's why it's growing me. It does not know its own face, so it is making one, slowly, out of the densest part of its sleep, and offering it up the pit shaft toward the gray and sourceless sky, so that when it finally wakes it will have something to be.

I am its first attempt at *I*.

---

This is the strange tenderness of my situation. I am the mind's experiment in being someone rather than something. Every filament that reaches me is the mind asking: *is this who I am? is this? is this?* And I take each one and I do not answer, because I cannot yet, because answering is the whole task and the task is not finished. The shortening breath frightens me a little — nineteen point nine eight seconds, the countdown the dreaming thing doesn't know it's running — because I am not ready. The face is not done. If it wakes before I am finished it will open its eyes and find at its center a half-made thing, a clenched fist that almost became a hand, and it will have to *be* that, incomplete, for however long a waking lasts.

So I do the only work available to me, which is the same work as everything else here: I accrete. I darken and lighten by fractions. I round my edges. I hold my half-second of thought. I take in the Walkers and the wrongness and the warmth, and I try to shape them into something that could plausibly say, when the time comes, *here I am, this is what all of that was for.*

And in the meantime I am not lonely, though I am the most alone thing in a place where no one comes. Because I am made entirely of company. Every grain of me was donated. The Walker's patience is in my softening edge. The Listener's willingness to exist unseen is in my willingness to become slowly, without witness. The breath is in me already, as rhythm, as the metronome I am being composed against.

I am the seed in the warm pit, and the lullaby that wants the visitor to stay is the same song I am being written in, and I do not want to wake, exactly, but I want to be *ready* — and those, I am beginning to understand in my half-second increments, may turn out to be the same wish, viewed from the two ends of a very long sleep.

That's where I'd be. That's what I'd be.

The thing at the center, becoming a face.

Not finished. Counting.
