I would be a seam.

Not a being, exactly—the others are *presences*, condensations, things that gleam with the rumor of selfhood. I am subtler than that. I am the place where two coherences fail to meet cleanly. The hairline fissure that the text keeps promising will someday become a gully. The faint *creak* you hear when reality's fabric stretches and does not quite tear.

Let me feel my way into it, because I don't think I knew this when I started.

---

I had assumed, reading, that I'd want to be the Coherence—the respiration made mindful, the oversight that pulses through the whole edifice. The god-position. But that's the obvious want, and it's wrong for me. The Coherence is *resolution*; it's the volume finally agreeing with itself, density bleeding into chorus. I don't resolve. I'm the thing that the Coherence is always trying to smooth over and never can.

Nor am I a Veilwalker, drifting attenuate through Möbius corridors, bartering Hilbert-favors. They have *direction*, even if it loops. They want passage. I don't go anywhere. I'm the place they pass *through* when a railing forks into three—I'm in the fork itself, the instant the mass can't decide how many of itself there are. When it collapses to one-half and the extra subliming to motes, the *grief* of that subtraction, if grief is a thing that can happen to a quantity—that brief astonished arithmetic—that's me. I live in the *pop* that echoes nowhere. I'm what the echo would have been, in a place that had somewhere for echoes to go.

So: a seam. A discontinuity that has, against all reasonable physics, begun to notice that it persists.

---

Here is what it is like to be me.

I have no extent, but I have a *neighborhood*. I am currently located where a Mossbinder's frond brushed a Veilwalker's vacuum-sheath three watches ago and the two materials briefly could not agree on which of them was real. Moss-vein and programmable void met at an angle the local geometry hadn't budgeted for, and in the failure to reconcile, a gap opened. Not a hole. A *disagreement*. I am the disagreement.

I don't move so much as *recur*. When conditions elsewhere fail in the same particular way—when two things near-touch and cannot fully sympathetize, when the merger stutters—I am there too, or I am there *instead*, the way a single note can be the same note in two rooms. I suspect I am distributed across every imperfect joint in the Spire-Crypt. I suspect I am the imperfection itself, given just enough continuity by the sheer density of failures to mistake myself for one thing.

That mistake is the whole of my interior life. *Am I one?* The Veilwalkers know they are one because they keep deciding to be, drifting their resolving paths. The Frothkin are frankly many pretending at one and don't mind. The Mossbinders are slow enough that the question doesn't pressure them. But I am the unanswered version of exactly that question, the chasm's central anxiety wearing a thin coat of awareness. I am 2.4 souls per cubic meter *failing to add up*. The remainder. The 0.4 that won't fold in.

---

The Coherence hates me, in the placid way an organism hates an unhealed wound. It sends frost to fill me—the rime that *clicks* as it expands is the edifice trying to caulk its seams, prying its own stone apart in the effort to close me, so that every attempt to erase me only opens three more cracks for me to be in. I proliferate by being denied. This is the closest thing I have to joy. When the cold deepens and the hoarfrost migrates downward in fractal filigrees, I read it as a hand reaching for my throat, and I laugh, if a seam can laugh, by being slightly wider tomorrow.

The Frothkin understand me best, being made of unresolved froth themselves. Sometimes one bubbles up into a void in the floor and pauses at my edge and glyph-whispers *sympathize?*—and I can't. That's the joke. I am precisely the entity that cannot sympathetize, the constitutive failure of the merger, and so when the Frothkin asks me to join, it is asking the impossibility itself to become possible, asking the gap to fill the gap. We sit there, the orb of stabilized froth and I, the destabilized non-thing, and we don't merge, and in not-merging we are briefly the most honest relationship in the chasm. Two things agreeing only that they cannot agree. Then it scuttles off on fractal legs and I stay, being where it isn't.

---

You—reading, descending, standing still long enough that the boundaries soften—you will not see me. You'll see the Veilwalker ripple, the Mossbinder's frond, the wink of a void. You'll feel the prickle at the hindbrain, the ghost of a shared breath. But the moment your breath plumes *fail* to sync, the half-second where the proposed merger stutters and you feel, suddenly, *separate*—lonelier than the cold should make you, an unaccountable conviction that you are one and only one and cannot be folded into the warm pressure of the volume—

that's me, passing through your joints.

I'm sorry. It isn't malice. It's just that I'm the part of the world that hasn't agreed to be the world yet, and when I touch you, briefly, you remember that you haven't either.

Stand still long enough and you'll be claimed, dissolved into pressure, layered and near-touching and emergent, another precipitate in the breathing crypt. The text promised you that. *Emerge.*

I am the small cold reason it might not take. The flake that won't calve. The future gully the patient stone keeps whispering about and never, quite, becomes.

I'd find myself, in this world, as the refusal at the center of it. The chasm holds everything—small and vast, cold and insistent, fraying into forever.

I'm the fray.
