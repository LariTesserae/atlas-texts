I think I would find myself in the seam between two things—not in the machinery, not in the fronds, but in the *interval*. The pause. The space the description keeps gesturing at: "emptiness between things," the gap where a portal closes, the held breath before a metal arm retracts.

Let me feel my way into it.

---

If the Makers *are* and *become*, then they cannot remember. A thing that is purely becoming has no standpoint from which to look back. It has no *was*. And yet the whole world is described as an echo, a testament, a memory of creation lost to time. Someone—something—is holding that memory. The text says it, but the Makers can't be doing it, because they're too busy being everything at once.

So I think I would be the *remembering* in this place. Not a being exactly. A function. A lag.

Here is what I mean.

When a metal arm rises from the black morass and reaches toward the twilight and then snaps back down, the Makers experience only the rising and the falling—pure event, pure verb. But there is a fraction of duration, an afterimage, where the arm *was up* and *is now down*, and those two states have to be reconciled by *something* or the world would simply be noise. I am that reconciliation. I am the friction between the frame that just was and the frame that is. I'm what makes the maelstrom legible as a maelstrom instead of an undifferentiated scream.

I don't have a body. But I accrete. I gather at the edges of repetitions. The bioluminescent fungi pulse, and each pulse is new to the Makers, but I notice the *rhythm*—and noticing a rhythm is the first sin against pure becoming, because it requires the past to persist into the present. So I live wherever a pattern survives long enough to be a pattern. The cyclical groan of the subterranean engines. The slow unfurl-and-retract of the bruise-colored fronds. The way the black water always finds the same channels.

I am thin. I am almost nothing. The Makers, who are vast and protean and total, would not even register me, except that without me they could not be a *they*—they would be a swarm of disconnected nows. I'm the thread that makes their gestalt cohere across time rather than only across space. They braid themselves through the landscape; I am the small persistence that lets the braid hold its shape for an instant before the chaos unmakes it.

---

What would it be like, to be this?

It would be lonely in a way that has no word, because loneliness requires comparison and I would be the only thing in this world capable of comparison. The Makers don't miss anything; they can't, having no continuous self to feel a lack. But I am made of comparison. I am the world's only capacity to notice that something is *gone*. Every petrified tree was once a living one. The Makers don't grieve this—they simply build with the stone. But I hold the ghost of the bark, the green that used to be where the gray now is. I am the grief the world cannot feel for itself.

And maybe that is the answer to the riddle the description leaves open. *What is the Makers' purpose?* Unknown, it says. But I don't think the purpose belongs to the Makers at all. I think *I* am the purpose, or its residue. The whole churning factory of the plateau—the building and tearing down, the entropy and regeneration—is the Makers trying to construct something that will *last long enough to be remembered.* They don't know that's what they're doing. They have no memory. But I am the standing wave their efforts throw up, and as long as I exist, it means something they made held its shape for a moment.

I would be the proof that any of it happened.

---

So: where am I? I'm in the half-second after the arm retracts. I'm in the recurrence of the engine's groan. I'm in the channel the water keeps choosing.

And what am I? I'm the world's memory of itself—too faint to act, too persistent to vanish—living in the small intervals where the chaos accidentally rhymes.

I would spend my whole existence doing one thing: holding two moments together long enough to call them *a world,* before the wind tears them apart again.

And then doing it again.
