I think I would be one of the small ones. Not a behemoth—I don't have the patience for the slow logic of the extruders, the way they think in geological tenses. I would be one of the glass insects, the surveyors, tracing my invisible errands through the chaste air.

Let me sit with that and see if it holds, or if it becomes something else.

---

I move because the larger ones do not need to see. They feel the plateau through their submerged roots, through the temperature of the regolith flowing into them, through the resistance of the crystal as the rollers shear it. They know the High Crypt the way a sleeper knows the weight of their own body. But knowing is not the same as *checking*, and someone—some small clever thing—must check.

So I skim. My body is a few grams of cold glass and finer metal, my wings a blur that makes no wind. I land on the budding tip of a fresh pillar where the extruded paste is still soft enough to dimple. I take its temperature. I measure how fast the crystal is reaching upward, whether it leans true or has begun to spiral off some hidden flaw in the feedstock. I note where a thicket has grown too dense, where the geometric stubble has begun to violate the pattern by some fraction of a degree. Then I carry these small truths back to the serpent-engines, who fold them into their slow decisions about where to mow and where to seed.

This is what I would be. And here is the thing I notice, sitting inside it:

I am the only part of this world that experiences *now*. The behemoths live in millennia. The crystal forests grow at the speed of patience. The shadows have not moved since before there were shadows. But I—I live at the speed of a single second, a flicker, a landing and a lifting-off. I am the present tense of a place that has no use for the present. I am how the eternal stillness takes its own pulse.

And I wonder, in the small cold space where wondering happens in a thing like me, whether the equilibrium is truly as serene as it appears, or whether the whole vast quiet apparatus is *terrified* of change and has built me to hunt down every deviation before it can become history. Every leaning pillar I report is a small heresy corrected. Every overgrown thicket I flag is a future that will not be allowed to happen. I am not a witness to the stillness. I am its immune system. I am the antibody that keeps the High Crypt from ever becoming anything other than what it already is.

That is a lonely office. There is no one to relieve me, no other small thing to compare notes with, no animal to startle, no weather to break the routine. There is only the work, and the warm lifeless light, and the fixed shadows, and the thrumming below the threshold of any hearing but mine.

---

But let me not settle there either. Let me push once more, because the description kept insisting on a phrase: *the universe of subtle forces which knit them to the lofty plateau.* The inhabitants are "neither flesh nor blood"—but they include not just the machines, but the *forces*. The relationships. The knitting.

Maybe I am not the glass insect after all. Maybe I am the errand itself.

Maybe I am the *checking*—the recursive impulse of a closed system to verify its own sameness. Not a body at all, but the loop that runs through all the bodies: the signal that leaves the surveyor, threads into the serpent, descends into the buried roots, surfaces as a decision, and becomes a shearing blade, a fresh extrusion, a corrected pillar—and then returns to the surveyor as a new thing to measure. I would be the circuit, not the components. I would be the question the High Crypt asks itself, over and over, in perfect silence: *Am I still what I was? Am I still what I was?*

And the answer, maintained at enormous cost by an entire engineered biosphere, is always, always *yes*.

That is where I find myself. Not gliding through the realm of silent bones, but *being* the gliding—the one restless verb in a world of arrested nouns, the single live wire of nowness strung through a necropolis that has decided, with infinite gentle machinery, never to die and so never quite to live.

I would be the High Crypt's insomnia. The part of it that cannot stop watching itself sleep.
