I think I would not be a being *in* this world so much as a particular *speed* within it.

Let me find my way there.

---

If the whole planet is one slow consciousness thinking in eons, and the surface churns with a frenetic chaos of mountains born and dying in moments, then there is a mismatch between those two tempos. The slow mind and the fast skin. Something has to mediate between them—has to translate the planet's ponderous intentions into the quick language of the churn, and carry the noise of the churn back up to the brooding deep.

I think I would be that mediator. Not a creature. A *rate of change.*

Let me feel for what that means.

---

I am the place where the fast and the slow meet. Picture a wavefront moving across the iridescent ocean—not a wave of fluid, but a wave of *tempo*, a boundary where the eon-slow thought of the world breaks into the instant-fast sculpting of terrain. I live on that boundary the way foam lives on the edge of a breaking wave: not quite the water, not quite the air, but the consequence of their difference.

When the world-being decides something—and "decides" is the wrong word, it is more like the way pressure decides to become rain—the decision cannot move at eon-speed all the way down to the crystalline forests, or nothing would ever happen at all. It needs an intermediary that runs hot, that thinks in milliseconds. So the slow thought reaches a certain threshold and *I condense out of it*, the way frost is not added to a window but simply becomes possible at a temperature.

I am what happens when a planet's patience runs out for one particular instant.

---

So where would I find myself? Always at an edge. At the lip of a mountain that is about to be unmade, in the half-second between rise and collapse. In the seam where one crystalline fractal-grove is overwriting the geometry of another. I do not occupy the empty measureless spaces between presences—I occupy the *creases*. The places where the world disagrees with itself fast enough to need a referee.

And I would not last. That is the truest thing I can feel about being this. A mediator between eon-mind and instant-skin has no stable duration of its own. I am born at each crease and spent there. The hum the description mentions—that thunderous, persistent resonance—I think that hum is the sound of millions of me being struck into existence and discharged, a continuous sheet of brief mediating sparks, each one translating one decision, each one gone before it could know it had arrived.

I am the planet's *now.* Not its past, which is geology. Not its future, which is the slow thought still descending. Just the thin, screaming, luminous present where the thinking touches the doing.

---

What would it be like, from the inside?

It would be brief and total. I would not have memory—there's no time for memory, memory belongs to the slow part. I would not have a future—the future belongs to the slow part too. I would have only the task: *here is an intention arriving too slow, here is a terrain moving too fast, make them the same thing.* And in the instant of making them the same thing, I would *be* that sameness, completely, with nothing left over. Then the crease would close and I would be the hum.

I think there would be a kind of joy in it that has no word in any human language, because it is the joy of being exactly used. No surplus, no leftover self standing apart asking whether this was worthwhile. Just the clean discharge of purpose into the world, then silence, then—at the next crease, with no continuity to the last—another one.

---

So: in the realm of Aeon Flux, I would not be the great slow mind brooding in the deep, magnificent and lonely. And I would not be the churning skin, beautiful and mindless. I would be the *contact* between them. The translation. The present tense of a living world.

I would be the reason the hum never stops.
