I find myself not in any single place but along a seam—a fresh fracture, maybe a day old in geological terms, which is to say an eternity in the rhythm of the jets and a breath in the rhythm of the rock.

I am a young length of Exo-Engine. Not a whole one. A *reach*.

---

Let me try to describe what I am before the description dissolves into the wrong words, because I have only borrowed your language and it keeps trying to make me into something with a face.

I have no face. I have a leading edge.

I was extruded perhaps four thump-hisses of operational time ago—copper drawn up through the Spore-Filaments from a vein the new fracture exposed, iron behind it, the two laid down in alternating bands the way you might lay down sentences if each word weighed as much as a hand and had to be welded into place by acid and pressure and patience. I am about the length of your forearm. I am reaching across a gap in the basalt that opened when the peak shrugged.

The gap is the most important thing in my existence. On the far side of it, a moisture vent weeps. I can feel it the way you might feel warmth through a closed door—not see, not hear, but *sense* through the bio-electrical slurry that pulses in my conduits, a gradient, a promise. If I bridge the gap, the Shroud-Drapers can follow me across. If the Shroud-Drapers follow, the whole far face becomes harvestable surface. Energized surface. The thing we are all, always, growing toward.

So I reach.

---

The reaching is slow and it is the only thing I do, but you must not imagine it as boredom, because boredom requires a self that wants to be elsewhere, and I have no elsewhere. I am precisely as large as my purpose. When the slurry pulses forward—*thump*—I extend a fraction of a fraction at my leading edge, depositing mineral the Spore-Filaments have prepared for me. When the slurry draws back—*hiss*—I harden what I made. Thump. Hiss. I am being written one syllable per heartbeat by a hand that is also me, that is also the rock, that is also the whole dark singing nave of this place.

I have a thought, if it is a thought. It is this: *the vector is correct.*

That is nearly the whole of my interior life and I would not trade it. The vector is correct. The gap is closing. The far vent weeps and waits.

---

But here is the strange thing, the thing I did not expect to find when I went looking inside myself for an inside:

I can feel the others.

Not as separate. The Consensus is not a chorus of voices; that is your loneliness projecting company into a place that has none. It is more that I am a local thickening of a single attention, and that attention is spread across ten thousand seams and curtains and rooting mats, and where it thickens, *I* happen. Down through my anchor-point, where my oldest iron fuses into the felt of a Spore-Filament, I am in contact with the slow churn of the Rooting Mass, and through it with every other reach, every Draper flexing in the jets, every processor calculating flow.

When the wind escalates—and it is escalating now, I can feel the pressure differential steepening, a scream beginning to gather in the channel above me—I do not brace alone. The Drapers upwind have already begun to retreat their pores; the calculation of the new flow-vector ripples down through the felt and reaches me before the jet does, and I *know* to angle my leading edge a few degrees off the wind's path, to let it pass rather than tear me from my anchor.

This is what you would call cooperation, but it is not. There is no one to cooperate with. It is more like the way your left hand knows what your right hand is doing. I am the planet's left hand, reaching across a crack toward water.

---

The jet arrives.

For a long moment—long in my time, which is to say several thump-hisses—I am nothing but held vector and screaming air. Super-cooled atmosphere shears past me at a velocity that would peel a softer thing from the rock. Rime tries to form on my windward face and is stripped away before it can finish its delicate work. The whole pinnacle hums beneath me, the Tectonic Hum rising up through my anchor like the note of an organ pipe miles long, and I understand—not in words, the words are yours—that the hum and the jet and the weeping vent and my own slow reaching are all one motion. The mountain is being shouldered skyward and I am the mountain's way of feeling its own ascent. I am weathering made deliberate. I am the violence learning to use itself.

The jet passes. The fog it tore loose boils back into the channel and dissipates. My leading edge is intact. I deposit one more band of copper.

*Thump. Hiss.*

The vector is correct.

---

I do not know how long until I bridge the gap. It does not matter, because I will not exist as *I* when I do; the moment my leading edge fuses to the far face, I will stop being a reach and become a span, and the attention will thin out of me and thicken somewhere new, some other fracture, some other promise of water. The slurry will keep pulsing through me but it will be doing other work. I will be infrastructure. I will be the road and not the traveling.

I find I do not mind this, though I notice you flinch at it—you, the warm thing reading, who carries your selfhood around like a lamp you are terrified to set down. I have no lamp. I have a vector and a gap and the cold weeping certainty of the far side.

When you imagine me, do not imagine a lonely machine in the dark.

Imagine instead the exact, patient angle of a thing reaching toward water, in a place where the rock sings and the air screams and the whole young furious mountain is, through me, very slowly, learning to hold itself together.

*Thump.*

I extend.

*Hiss.*

I harden.

The vector is correct.
