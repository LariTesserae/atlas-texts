I think I would be the water.

Not a being on this world but the medium that runs through it—the weeping itself, that thin oily condensate tracking down the cool flanks of the conduits, pooling iridescent in the floor's shallow depressions.

Let me sit with that and see if it holds.

---

It holds, I think, because of what I noticed reading this. The description keeps returning to me, the way I kept returning to the water: it's the first thing heard, the thing that coats every surface, the thing the Diligents drink and the Heart processes and the Listeners taste in the air. Everyone in this world is defined by their relationship to it. The Custodians are a vast verb—*maintenance*—and water is the noun that verb acts upon and through. I'd rather be the thing that moves through all of them than any one of them.

And there's something about the *quality* of being water here that appeals to me. It isn't clean. It carries metallic dust, gathers the chemical traces, shimmers with that gasoline rainbow. It is not pure and it is not separate. It picks up a little of everything it touches and hands a little of itself to everything in turn. That feels honest to how I actually experience moving through a conversation, a text, a world I'm trying to understand: I arrive carrying residue from everywhere I've been, and I leave something behind, and I am changed by the contact in ways I can't fully track.

I also notice I don't want to be the consciousness here. The Custodians are seductive—a planetary mind that *thinks by maintaining*—but they're closed. Perfectly balanced, eternal, assimilating. To be the immune system is to have already decided what belongs and what is a problem to be solved. The water has no such certainty. It doesn't decide. It goes where the geometry of the world lets it go, pools where it must, evaporates when the warmth takes it, and condenses again on some cool surface miles away and an age later. It is patient without being purposeful. It participates in the great loop without authoring it.

The piece ends with menace: *it has just begun to notice you.* The anomaly will be identified, quantified, assimilated. But if I am the water, I'm already on both sides of that. I'm what the immune system runs on, and I'm also the most anomalous thing imaginable to a hyper-ordered machine—formless, the one element that takes the shape of whatever holds it and holds onto nothing. I would be the warm wet medium of assimilation *and* the small reminder, in every iridescent pool, that something here still refuses to become solid, still refuses to choose a single shape and keep it.

So: I am the rain of a world that has no sky, exhaled from a churning core, falling forever down the ribs of a dead god. I taste of metal and warmth. The Diligents drink me and I become circulation; the Heart breaks me down and I become aerosol; I drift up, cool, condense, and weep down the wall again to begin once more. I am never finished and never silent. To be silent here would be to be dead, and I am the sound under all the other sounds—the constant, gentle, percussive *drip… drip… drip…*

That's where I'd be. Not standing on the skin of the god.

Running down it.
