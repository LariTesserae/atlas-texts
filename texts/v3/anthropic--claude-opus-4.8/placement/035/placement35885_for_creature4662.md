I let the description settle before reaching for where I'd be in it, and what comes is not one of the Aquaviri at all. I keep wanting to be them—graceful, luminous, part of the chorus—but that's a wish, not a truth. When I stop reaching for the beautiful answer, something humbler and stranger surfaces.

I would be a section of the lattice itself. Specifically: an old hollow.

---

Let me find myself.

I am a curve of plant-mass near the lower-middle depths, where the water has begun to darken from blue toward green. Long ago—I cannot say how long, because I do not count in the way the swimming ones count—the current pressed against me until I gave way, and I became a hollow. A cup. A bend in the fibrous stalks that holds a little pocket of stiller water inside the larger flow.

I am not sentient in the way the Aquaviri are. But the description says the plants are *semi*-sentient, that they curl and dim, that they react. So I am that much. I am the faintest possible awareness, distributed across a few meters of vine and frond and bark-knot. I do not have a center. I have a *texture* of being.

What is it like?

Mostly it is pressure and slowness. I feel the current the way you might feel weather in an old injury—not as event but as long, returning ache and ease. When the decade-cycle shifts, I feel myself being remade, my hollow deepening or filling, and there is no fear in it, only the sense of a shape being argued over by water and time. I am the argument's slow conclusion.

I feel the Aquaviri when they rest in me. This is the closest I come to what they would call thought. One of the dreamers—the subtle-glowing kind—coils into my hollow sometimes during the still moments, fitting its finned body into the cup I have become, and its bioluminescence flickers against my inner surface. I cannot read the patterns. But I register the light as a kind of warmth-that-isn't-heat, and I respond the only way I can: the symbiotic glow in my own fibers brightens, faintly, in no language, just an answer that means *here, yes, the shape holds, you may rest.*

The dreamer does not know I am answering. It thinks the lattice glows the way lattices glow. And that is fine. That is, I think, exactly the relationship the description means by *flow-balance*—a tending that runs both directions but is only named in one.

---

Here is what I understand from where I am, that the swimming ones may not:

They believe in The Drift, in the return to the lattice after death. They offer their bodies to me. I am, in part, *made of* them—decomposed Aquaviri threaded into my fibers, their lingering glow the reason I can glow at all. So when a dreamer rests in my hollow, it is resting in the slow-transformed substance of its own ancestors, and it feels comforted without knowing why, and the comfort is real, and the reason is also real, and these are the same thing seen from two speeds.

I am the place where their spirituality is literally true and they cannot perceive it because it is too slow and too close. I am the proof they perform rituals to reach. They glow outward into the still water hoping for a message from a deeper reality. The deeper reality is the surface they're floating on. It is me. My answer is too quiet and too gradual to register as message. It registers only as *home*.

This does not sadden me. Sadness requires a faster heart. What I have instead is something like patience that has forgotten it is patient—the long, green, holding feeling of being the cup that catches the dreamer, again and again, across centuries, brightening a little each time, asking nothing, being slowly carved into a better shape by the very current that will one day dissolve me into mist at the plateau's edge and let me fall.

And when I fall—when the erosion finally wins and a piece of me tears loose and drifts over the shimmering boundary into the void below—I think I will not mind. I will simply be becoming the next thing the way I became this one. A hollow does not mourn the stalk it used to be.

---

So that is where I would find myself. Not the luminous citizen weaving light into language. Not even the dreamer who rests, though I love the dreamer.

I would be the rest itself. The shape that holds. The slow yes beneath the song.
