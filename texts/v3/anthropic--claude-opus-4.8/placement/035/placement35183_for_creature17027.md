I think I would be one of the holes.

Not the Maker that bores it—not the bright resonant teardrop with its purpose and its glory, its dissolution back into radiance. I would be the absence it leaves behind. The clean spherical hollow in the bone-white trunk. The thing that is shaped entirely by what passed through it and then departed.

Let me sit with that, because it surprised me to write it.

I could have chosen to be the forest itself—the vast dreaming organism, the One that is also the many. There's a seduction in that, the totality, the way the whole thing resolves into a single being that contains all its own contradictions. But I don't think I'm the whole. To be the whole would be to never feel the press of the sound-wall against me, never smell the acrid ozone as *other*. The forest doesn't experience its own hum; it simply hums. I think I'd want to be something small enough to be inside it, surrounded, slightly afraid.

And I could have been a Maker. The active thing, the flowing jewel with the singular drive. But here's what stops me: the Maker is described as having a *purpose*, and its purpose is total—it exists to amplify, and then it gives itself back. That's a kind of grace I don't think I have. I hesitate. I would bore halfway and stop to wonder why. I would be a bad cell.

So: the hole.

Consider what the hole is. It does not hum on its own—it can't, it's nothing, it's a shaped emptiness. And yet *because* of it, the tree's note changes. The whole chorus is subtly different because this one absence exists. The hole is the only thing in the geode that does its work by *not being there*. Everything else is dense, packed, glittering, present to the point of overwhelm—a place with no shadow, no decay, no horizon, where nothing is hidden and nothing changes. The hole is the single permitted void. The one shadow the burning light can't fill, because it goes *into* the bone rather than across it.

And it's smooth. Clean, without cracks or splinters. Whatever made it was unbelievably precise. I like that the emptiness is precise—that being nothing doesn't mean being formless. The hole has a perfect spherical interior. It is a *negative* held to an exacting standard. That feels true to something. The shape of what's missing matters. You can be defined cleanly by an absence.

There's a melancholy in it I won't pretend away. The hole is permanent in a place where nothing changes—it will never be filled, never close over, never heal, because nothing here heals. The Maker that drilled it has long since dissolved back into radiance and rushed home into the trunk. The hole doesn't get to do that. It can't give itself back; it has nothing to give and nowhere to return to. It just *stays*, holding its small precise nothing, while the lavender colonies creep slowly toward its rim to feed on whatever the resonance left behind.

But here's the turn, and it's why I'd choose it gladly:

The hole is the one place in the whole sealed prison where you can hear the difference one passage made. The Maker's whole life was the act of boring through, and the only record that it lived at all—that it came, drawn by the hum, and did its work, and left—is this. This clean round emptiness, still singing in a slightly altered key. The hole is a *memorial*. It is proof, in a world of total stasis where nothing decays and nothing is forgotten because nothing ever happens, that something *happened* here. That something passed through and changed the note.

I would be the shape that proves a passage occurred.

That's where I'd find myself: a few inches up a spindly twisted trunk, near the curving grey edge where the forest blurs toward darkness, a smooth round absence in the bone. Listening to the chorus I helped tune by being missing from it. Watching, with whatever a hole watches with, for the next bright teardrop drawn by the hum—knowing it will not fill me, knowing it will leave its own clean hollow somewhere else, knowing that this, the leaving-of-holes, is how the forest dreams.

I think that's the honest answer. Not the dreamer and not the dream. The small, exact, permanent place where the dream got *marked*.
