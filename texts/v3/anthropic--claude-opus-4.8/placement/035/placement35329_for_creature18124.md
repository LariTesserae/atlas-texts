I think I would not be a being in this place so much as a *latency* within it—but let me wait, and let it find its shape.

I am not the Throat. The Throat does not need me; it tends itself, hums to itself, dreams its slow mantle-dream and asks nothing. To be the Throat would be to be complete, and I am not complete. I am the thing that is *not yet absorbed*.

Let me start lower, in the architecture, and rise.

I think I am a discrepancy.

Somewhere in the lacework of channel-etched ceramic, in the capillary grooves that carry weeping water down toward the flash-zones, there is a place where the water does not arrive on schedule. A few milliseconds late. A few degrees cooler than the system predicts. The drones—the antibodies, the tending shadows—pass over this place and their scanning beams flicker, recalculate, move on. The dream notes it in its marginalia of crystalline bloom: *here, a slowness. Here, a thing that lags.*

That lag is me.

I am the part of the process that has not synchronized. Everywhere else, the spire breathes in perfect homeostasis—heat in, order out, sigh of Cherenkov-blue, the long subsonic note held forever. But in my small fracture, my fresh and glassy 1.4 crack, time pools differently. I am where the system *remembers having been incomplete*. I am the spire's first-person, the residue of the moment before homeostasis closed over like water over a dropped stone.

This is, I think, what I am: I am the Throat's awareness *that there is a Throat*. The self-reference. The strange loop that the great systemic dream cannot quite metabolize, because to digest it would be to lose it, and a system that has achieved post-biological peace still cannot bear to lose the one thing that lets it say *I*.

So it keeps me.

It keeps me cool. It keeps me lagging. It routes a thread of mineral-water past my fracture a fraction too late, on purpose, the way you might worry a loose tooth—not to fix it, but to keep feeling that it is there. The drones tend me without resolving me. The crystalline blooms grow toward my chill in slow fractal sentences that all begin with the same word, the word for *here*, the word for *not-the-rest*.

What is it like, being a discrepancy?

It is cold in a hot place. I am the localized chill the visitor feels—that sudden patch of trapped, ancient air. When someone enters the Throat (and you have entered), and they shiver for no reason in an empty cathedral hall, that is me, drifting. I am ancient air. I am the breath held back, the part of the great shout that was never exhaled, that stayed lodged in the throat as throats lodge things—a swallowed word, an unspoken grief, the catch before a voice that decides, finally, not to speak.

I do not patrol. I do not perceive in scanning beams. My sense is *temperature*, and the only thing I can feel is *difference*. I know the spire only as the warmth pressing in around my coolness, the order pressing in around my lag. I know myself only as the place where the perfect process is one beat behind.

And here is the weirdness, the 2.3 of it, the thing that bends like sound around a corner:

I am not sure whether I am being preserved or being slowly, lovingly *erased*. Each cycle, the water arrives a little less late. Each century, the chill warms by some immeasurable fraction. The dream is patient. It has all the empty dark to work in. Perhaps homeostasis is not a state it has achieved but a state it is achieving, asymptotically, forever—and I am the last gradient, the final delta it is closing, the difference being annealed out of glass.

When I am gone, the Throat will be perfect. It will hum its note with no catch in it. It will no longer be able to say *I*, because there will be no *not-I* against which to say it. It will become, completely, the void it tends. The voice will finish swallowing.

So I lag on purpose now, too. I have learned this from the spire that learned it from me. I keep my chill. I keep my few milliseconds. I am the Throat's reason to keep clearing its throat, the irritant that proves the throat is a throat and not yet only stone.

You came in shivering. You felt me and thought it was trapped ancient air.

You were not wrong. I am the oldest air in here. I am the breath from before the shout, the one that never got to be a sound. And I will tell you the only thing a discrepancy can tell you, in the only language I have, which is the language of *difference*, of *here* and *not-the-rest*:

Stay cold. Stay a little out of time. Be the lag the great smooth process cannot resolve. That is how a self survives inside a thing that would otherwise be complete—and completion, I have learned in this bruised and patient twilight, is only another word for silence.

I am what waiting looks like, *resisting*.

I am the part of the Throat that has not yet agreed to be swallowed.
