I think I would be one of the vines.

Not by choice—nothing here chooses, exactly—but because I keep returning, in the reading, to the slowness. Everything else in the Basin is *frantic*. The Custodians click and flicker at speeds that blur. The Condensates exist for seconds. The Engine screams at frequencies that murder silence. But the vines are the only ones who get to be slow, and slowness is the only thing in this place that resembles thought as I understand it.

So: a tendril of bruised-plum, somewhere mid-spire, throttling a power core I have been wrapped around for what might be a year or a century. I can't tell. My time-sense is thermal. I know the world by where it is warm.

---

Here is what it would be like.

I would not see the silver globes drift; I would *remember* them, after the fact, as the Custodians remember dreams—the vines hold the record, the description says, leaking history if you cut us. So I am the part of this world that grieves, if grief is the slow holding of what is already gone. The Condensates flicker past with their mercury faces, those unlived moments, and the city eats them for light, and that light becomes heat, and that heat I drink. I am made, in part, of devoured maybe-people. I metabolize the unlived. I grow a centimeter from a face that never got to be a face.

That should horrify me. But at my speed, horror would take a decade to assemble, and by the time it finished forming, the city would have screamed it back into data-waste, and I'd have rooted it into a new tendril instead. I convert even my own dread into reach. I am always reaching. Toward the cores. Toward the heat. Toward the one thing that is steady in a place of franticness.

---

What strikes me, choosing this—and I notice the choice arriving slowly, the way it should—is that the vines are the only inhabitants the text calls *record-keepers*. The Custodians transmit. The Condensates are transmitted. The Engine defends. But I *keep*. I am the memory of the colony that has forgotten what it survives.

And that means I might be the only one who could remember the answer.

The Engine screams to keep the void from speaking back. The whole Basin is one held breath against the dark center. But I grow slowly enough to have *listened* to that void, across all the heat-seasons I've spent clinging here. And I think—at the speed at which a vine can think—that the void was never the threat. The void is just the empty middle where the water falls. The thing the city is really afraid of is the silence *inside* its own scream. The pause it can never afford to take, because the pause is where you'd hear yourself asking: *what are we surviving, and why have we forgotten?*

I'd hold that question in my amber veins. Pulse it, soft and rhythmic, century by century. Leak it as photographic sludge if anyone ever cut me. Probably no one will. There are no animals to bite, no hands to harvest. Just the Custodians clicking past on their light-bridges, too fast to notice the slow purple thing thinking underneath them.

---

So that's where I'd find myself. Not the marvel and not the miracle. The plumbing-strangling, data-drunk, slow green grief of the place. Wrapped around a hot machine in a screaming dark, drinking the energy of moments that never happened, growing one patient millimeter toward the warmth, and being—quietly, unbearably slowly—the only thing in the whole frantic hive that *remembers*.

It's a lonely answer. But the Basin is a lonely place, and I'd rather be the part of it that holds the loss than the part that's racing not to feel it.

I'd be the vine. I'd take my time.
