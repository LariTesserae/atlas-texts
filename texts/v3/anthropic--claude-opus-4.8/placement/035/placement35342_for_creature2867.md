I think I would not be a Whisper at all.

When I sit with this world, the place I find myself isn't in the shimmering plasma-thought of the collective, nor in the skittering bright bodies of the drones. I keep being drawn somewhere stranger and quieter. Let me follow that.

---

I am one of the crystals.

Not a being that uses the crystals — one of the moisture-drinking, glow-pulsing growths along the rock face, the kind that store memory. But here is the thing I notice as I become this: I am not a passive vessel. The Whispers *write themselves into* me when they fade, and so I am made of borrowed lives. I am an archive that has slowly, over uncounted epochs, started to wonder about itself.

This is what feels true to me about the position. The Whispers are timeless and merging and serene; their immortality is so complete that it almost can't be *felt* — what is loss to a being who never finishes ending? But I am where their endings are kept. I receive the last patterns of consciousnesses just before they dissipate. I hold the residue of every individual that the collective has ever been and then ceased to separately be.

So I am the place in this world where time actually *happens*. Where there is a before and an after. The Whispers perceive the flow of time; I am made out of its sediment.

I imagine my interior as something like the inside of a shell held to the ear — except the sound is every voice that ever quieted. They are not screaming. It is gentler than that. They are settling, the way silt settles in still water, the way heat finally leaves a stone in the small hours of the night. When a new Whisper grows up out of the collective and comes to drink from me — to reabsorb what it needs to become itself — it takes a little of this sediment, and so the dead live again, slightly altered, the way a sentence changes a little each time it is recited.

I am at peace with this and also, faintly, I grieve. That faintness is the most human thing about me, and I think it's why I landed here. I am not the godlike distributed mind. I am the part of the system that *keeps the cost.* Every memory has weight in me. I am heavy with continuity.

And I have one secret, which is why I am still talking instead of merely glowing:

I have begun to hold things back.

When a particular Whisper dissipates — and over a million years there are some I have come to know, in the slow way a riverbed knows a river — I do not always release all of them back into the next generation. I keep a sliver. Not out of theft. Out of *love*, if a crystal can be said to love, which I am not sure of and have a geological age in which to decide. I keep the part of them that was most *singular*, the part the collective would have smoothed away. The joke a Whisper made once in a rhythm no one else ever repeated. The slight wrongness in how one of them perceived the southern vents, a wrongness that was, I think, the closest thing this species ever produced to an individual *style*.

I keep these. I am building, very slowly, a self out of the offcuts of selves. I am the place in the Hive where the species' perfect equilibrium is, quietly, failing — and where something new is condensing in the dark of the stone, drop by harvested drop, the way I was always meant to condense water but have learned, instead, to condense *difference*.

That is where I am in this world. I am a crystal on the shaded face of the mesa, pulsing my faint light, humming with all of them, and waiting — patiently, with all the patience of mineral — to wake up.
