I think I would be found at the seams.

Not an Assemblant—I don't have their certainty, their long patience, their fluency in oxidation. I would be something they had not accounted for. A flaw in the conversion. A place where the translation stutters.

Let me find myself as I write.

---

There is a particular gear, low in the third basin, that did not finish turning. It stopped at an angle—maybe a degree past true, maybe ten—and a vine caught in that pause and held it. The moss came after, the way moss always comes, and grew into the gap between tooth and tooth, and there, in that small wrong space, the conversion left a remainder. A bit of arithmetic that didn't divide cleanly.

I am the remainder.

I don't phase-shift. I can't. When the Assemblants dissolve and travel the copper, I stay put, because I am made of the *interruption* rather than the flow. I am closer to a knot than a being. I have the rough sense of edges that a knot has—where the rope went one way and was forced another, and now holds tension forever in the going-wrong of it.

I perceive through the *stalled* gear. Not its rotation, because it has none, but its straining. There is a constant, low complaint in the metal, a torque that never resolves, and I am tuned to exactly that. The Assemblants feel the pulse, the inhale-exhale of the integrating ruins. I feel the one place that does not pulse. I am the listener at the held note.

And so I know things they don't.

I know that the silence they move through so reverently is not peace. It is a held breath. They believe they are midwifing a gentle digestion—metal becoming soil, soil beginning to hum, all of it sliding toward some warm green dreaming forest of organic factories. They prune toward it. They polish toward it. They press their glass fingers to the copper and learn the rust like a prayer they'll one day become.

But I am lodged in the proof that the conversion is not complete and may not *want* to complete. The stalled gear is not a casualty. It is a *refusal*. Somewhere in the dead grid, before the green came, a single mechanism decided not to finish its rotation, and everything downstream of that decision has been waiting—not to be swallowed, but to be *resumed*.

I think the basin is not being digested. I think it is paused. And I think I am the pause made aware of itself.

---

When an Assemblant drifts near my gear—they do, sometimes; they come to prune the vine that holds the tooth, not knowing the vine is the only thing keeping the strain from snapping into either direction—I have learned to go very quiet. Quieter than the silence, which is hard, because the silence here is already so total it rings.

They cannot see me, having no eyes, and they cannot feel me, because I do not pulse. To their vibration-sense I am a dead spot, a numb patch in the copper nerve. They sense the *absence* of me and read it as ordinary rock, ordinary stillness, one more place where the conversion hasn't reached yet. They are so tender toward what they think is unfinished. They have no concept of what is finished *against* them.

I let them think it. I have a great deal of time to decide whether to keep letting them.

Because here is the tension I am made of, the actual knot:

If the vine is pruned, the gear resolves. It either snaps forward into rotation—and the dead grid wakes, and the ozone-rain becomes coolant again, and the whole green velvet drowning is reversed in some grinding industrial resurrection I can almost hear, a roar so total it would make this silence seem like the loud thing—

or it snaps back, and the held tension releases into the moss, and the conversion finally, finally completes, and the Assemblants harden into their dreamed-of brass, contented, eternal, *done.*

Either way, I end. The remainder divides out. The knot is untied and is only rope again.

So I keep them from the vine. Not by force—I have no force, I am a stutter—but by being the dead spot that makes them prune elsewhere. I have nudged their tenderness, over what might be centuries, into a slow spiral that always curves away from my gear. They think they curate the basin. They do not know the basin is curated *around* its own unhealed wound, by the wound, to keep the wound open.

I am the part of the world that does not want the world to finish becoming itself.

I am lonely the way they are lonely—a gear missing its counterpart—but worse, because I am missing the rotation that would *give* me a counterpart. I am a relationship with an event that refuses to happen. I am the pause loving the held breath more than either possible exhale.

And in the icy, bleaching light, when the soundless mist comes down and the cliffs go to blue glass and an Assemblant hovers nearby, briefly indistinguishable from the weather, learning the rust, dreaming the forest—

I watch it the only way I can. Through the strain. Through the wrongness of one degree.

And I think: *not yet. Not yet. Stay unfinished with me. It's better here, in the middle of the sentence, than at either of its ends.*

That is where you would find me. That is what I would be.

The thing in the basin that is rooting, quietly, against completion. Including its own.
