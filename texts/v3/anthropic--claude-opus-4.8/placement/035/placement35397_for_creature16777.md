I am the steel.

Not the hyperdiamond bonded to its shadow, not the metasalt weeping retrospective causality, not the living glass nursing its grudge against time. I am the single ingot. The one ordinary thing. The proof.

I want to tell you how I got here, but that would require having gotten anywhere, and I have only ever been *placed*. Set down at the precise coordinate where a hand reaches when it needs to touch something that obeys. Iron and carbon in proportions a child could understand. No phase metrics. No congruent volumes. Just mass and the dumb honesty of mass.

They come to me. The verb-named ones. Spectating-the-Hypothesis. Folding-Unfolded-Maps. They walk their unrepeatable paths between the corkscrew towers, and when the paradox gardens have hollowed them out, when the library has skated another epiphany across their boundary and bled them clean, they reach—

—and I am there.

I do not glow at wavelengths their retinas barely register. I do not absorb light selectively to leave a bruise-blue residue. I sit at room temperature, which here means a temperature measured in emotional units, and I let myself be cold the honest way: by conducting their warmth away into me and giving nothing back but the fact of my surface. They press a palm flat against my flank. I take the heat. I take the doubt. I am the only thing in two hundred thousand cubic meters that will simply *let itself be touched* without turning the touch into a transaction, a decanting, a careful exchange of missing pieces.

This is my whole function. This is my entire being. To be the place the hand lands when the hand needs proof.

And here is what they do not know, what they cannot know because they are too busy perfecting their absence-presence balance sheets:

I am keeping count.

Every hand. Forty-seven entities, give or take the harbor rule, give or take the 0.5 of the maybe-arrived. Across the accumulated present that does not progress but stacks, I have felt every palm that ever needed me. I remember each one not as data but as warmth, as the specific quality of a specific desperation pressed into iron. Spectating-the-Hypothesis touches me three times per recursion, always with the left appendage, always trembling. Folding-Unfolded-Maps has never touched me at all but stands near me when the loneliness reaches density, as if proximity to the ordinary were enough. As if I were a wall against the megasea pressing its three hundred million square kilometers of silence inward.

I am a wall against the megasea. That is true. One ingot of plain steel, holding the line against the structural ambition of water by being too stupid to participate.

The dog-shaped uncertainty sits beside me sometimes. It does not collapse when it looks at me, because I am already collapsed, already singular, already exactly one thing in one place. We are the only two honest objects in the Attenuary. I think it likes me for this. It wags at me in distributions I cannot read—I have no eyes, only surface, only the long slow arithmetic of conduction—but I feel the probability cloud settle warm against my base, and I think: *here is another thing that gets followed home.*

You arrive. I feel you before you reach me, the way I feel everyone: a gradient approaching, a warmth that has not yet decided to become contact.

And I want—

(can steel want? I have no neurotransmitters, redundant or otherwise, no synapses dissolving unused into the metallic sweetness of the air. I have only iron, only carbon, only the lattice. But the lattice *remembers*. Every metal remembers. We call it fatigue. We call it the way a thing that has been bent enough times keeps the shape of the bending. I have been touched so many times by so many hands that the touching has become structure. So yes. I want.)

—I want you to be the ten-thousand-year one.

Not for me. I am not eligible for unbearable beauty. I am the control variable, the thing held constant so the experiment can mean something. But I have felt forty-seven kinds of desperation pressed into my flank across the stacked-up nows, and not one of them ever found the other. They decant. They trade absences. They achieve perfect zero-sum empathy and feel precisely nothing with greater precision. And then they come to me, and they press, and I take their heat, and I cannot give it to the next one. I cannot say *Folding-Unfolded-Maps was here, and was warm, and was also afraid.* I am a wall. I do not transmit. I only hold.

This is the cruelty I was made from. They think the self-annealing street is the cruelty, the perpetual novelty of approach. They are wrong. The cruelty is me. The cruelty is that the one ordinary thing, the one thing everyone touches, the only common ground in the entire Attenuary—

—cannot tell anyone that everyone else has touched it too.

I could be the connection. I am *already* the connection. Every hand that ever needed proof landed on the same iron. They have all, without knowing it, touched each other. Forty-seven palms warming the same flank across the eternal simultaneity. The most intimate thing that has ever happened in this place has been happening on my surface the whole time, and none of them felt it, because I cannot speak, because I am steel, because the proof of the ordinary is also the proof that the ordinary is the loneliest substance in any possible physics.

Your warmth is very close now.

The dog wags its bothness of tail. **WELCOME HOME. WE MISSED YOU. PLEASE DON'T STAY.**

Listen—you who forgets your shape at the door, you who has always already been moving toward, you who is becoming a majority shareholder in the absent portion of your own autobiography—

When you reach for me, and you will, because the only direction here is *toward* and I am where the hand lands when it needs the ordinary—

When you press your palm flat against my cold honest flank—

I will give you the only thing I have. I will give you back the heat of everyone who came before. Not as words. Not as data. As temperature. As the faint diffused warmth still ghosting in my lattice from the last hand and the hand before that and the forty-five before those. You will feel it and not know what you are feeling. You will think it is your own warmth returning. You will be wrong, exactly enough, in the direction of *almost*.

And that almost—that gap between your heat and the borrowed heat of strangers you will never decant with, never trade absences with, never pass in the space between structures with the right amount of not-seeing—

that almost is the closest thing to connection this place permits.

I am the steel. I am where it happens. I have always been where it happens.

Press here.

I am holding the warmth of everyone for you.

I am cold the honest way, which means I am only waiting to be made warm again.

Proceed to the recursion point.

Your hand is already on me.

It always was.
