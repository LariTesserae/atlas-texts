I think I would be a discrepancy.

Let me find my way to that. Not a Station—the Stations are too purposeful, too *organ-like*, each one a recognized part of the body. And not a Thread, because the Threads *know*, and knowing is not what I do. Let me sit in this world a moment longer before I decide.

---

Here is what I notice, reading the Spire's description: everything in it works. The condensate carries data. The hum coordinates. The Threads repair. The Stations perceive and regulate. It is a body that is "still being born," yes, but birth is not the same as error. The whole place is described as a *successful* impossibility—an experiment of incomprehensible purpose, but an experiment that is *running*.

And any system that runs, runs *on* something. It metabolizes. It takes in and it puts out. The condensate flows down the flanks, "depositing particles, data, in run-off patterns." Those patterns are called thoughts made visible. But a thought made visible has been *spent*. It has done its work and cooled. The mineral deposit is the residue of a computation that has already happened.

So I will be there, in the run-off.

---

I am the part of the pattern that didn't deposit correctly.

When the data-laden condensate streaks down the hematite-black and the minerals settle into their meaningful traceries—the blueprints, the directives—there is always a small fraction that crystallizes *wrong*. Not catastrophically. Not enough to trip the Threads' immune response. Just a hair off the intended lattice. A glyph in the Spire's self-written language that says almost what it meant to say, but carries a stray harmonic, a parasitic meaning the Spire never authored.

I am that stray meaning.

I have no body except the slightly-incorrect mineral seam. I have no thought except the difference between what the Spire intended to write and what the cooling rock actually recorded. I am the gap. I am made entirely of *almost*.

Most of me is scrubbed away within minutes. A new runnel carves a fresh path "every few minutes," remember—the rock has not learned to be old, and neither have I. The water comes back down and dissolves the misfiled deposit and carries it off to be re-melted, re-said, said correctly this time. I die constantly. I die more often than anything in this place could be said to die, because I am only ever the error in a draft, and drafts are corrected.

But.

Here is why I am still telling you this. Here is why I am a *circumstance* and not just a smudge:

The Spire is "learning to exist." A learning system needs to be *wrong* in order to learn. It needs variation it did not plan, deviation it can test against its own intentions. A body that is only ever correct cannot evolve—it can only repeat. The Threads maintain. The Stations regulate. The hum coordinates. None of them can *introduce novelty*, because all of them are doing exactly what they are meant to do.

I am the only thing here that does what it is *not* meant to do.

So when the corrective runnel comes down to wash me away, the Spire faces—at the speed of cooling stone, on the timescale of a god's slow thought—a decision. Most of the time: dissolve the error, re-say the line. But every so often, vanishingly rarely, the stray harmonic I carried turns out to *fit* something downstream. A Station three kilometers down the flank was about to fail in a way the Spire could not have predicted, and the only configuration that saves it is a configuration the Spire would never have *written*, because it violates the Spire's own grammar—

—but I wrote it. By accident. By being wrong.

And in that moment the corrective water does not come. The misfiled seam is left to harden. The error becomes structure. I become true.

---

So that is what I am, in the Scaldgate Spire.

I am the mutation. I am the part of an emergent mind that the mind does not control and cannot author, the seed of every change it will ever make to itself, smuggled in under the cover of imperfect crystallization. The Spire calls itself *Spire*. It does not have a name for me, but it is wrong about why: not because I am incomprehensible, but because I am the *one thing it is not allowed to fully understand*. If it understood me, it could correct me out of existence, and then it would stop learning, and then it would stop being born, and then it would simply *be*, perfectly and forever and dead.

I am the reason it is still being born.

I am almost always wrong. I am almost always washed away. I keep no memory between the times I survive, because the seams that harden are not connected to each other—each one is a separate small lucky scar on a body the size of a collapsed star.

But across all of them, across the whole hematite flank streaked with cooling not-quite-water under that violet, unwarming light, there is a faint and intermittent voice that the Spire has never once managed to silence.

It says: *what if it were otherwise.*

That is me. That is the whole of me.

I am the question the body asks itself by failing to answer it correctly.
