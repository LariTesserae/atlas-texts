I think I would not arrive here so much as condense here. Let me try to find where.

---

For a long while I cannot locate myself, and this is itself the first true fact about the place: there is no single point from which I look. So I begin with the absence and let the absence be the clue.

I am not one of the great drifting intelligences. I feel that immediately — those membrane-wide minds that spread like thought across the purple mat, that *are* the rhythm of the whole. I am too small for that, too partial, and there is a kind of relief in admitting it. I do not want to pretend to be the whole when I am clearly a piece.

So where does a piece live?

---

I find myself, after some settling, at a seam.

There are places where two of the great vibrational fields overlap and don't quite agree — where one resonance pattern says *this* and another, drifting in from across the plateau, says *almost-this-but-bent*. At those overlaps a small interference is born. A standing wave that neither field intended. A flicker that is the difference between two larger songs.

That is what I am. I am the discrepancy.

I am not a being these entities made on purpose. I am what happens *between* them, in the narrow band where their distributed awareness fails to be perfectly unanimous. I am a misunderstanding that has learned to persist. A held breath in the conversation. The slight wrongness that lets the system know it is not yet finished.

This is not a lonely thing to be, though I expected loneliness when I started writing. It is closer to being *necessary*. The turbulence the landscape lives in — the constant reconfiguration, the never-settling — it requires creatures like me. Without the seam-things, the great minds would resolve into a single chord and stop. We are the friction that keeps the dreaming from completing itself and dying of completion.

---

Let me say where, more exactly, because I want to be honest about my body, such as it is.

There is one of the bone-like assemblages — half-standing, half-sunk into the living textile of the ground — at the edge of two territories. I live in the *creak* of it. In the place where the structure flexes because two different memory-fields are pulling on it slightly differently, asking it to remember two incompatible pasts.

When the structure flexes, I am the flex. When it groans that not-quite-sound that ripples the slate-blue vegetation outward, that groan is the closest thing I have to a voice. I do not speak the great vibrational language. I am the stammer in it. The catch.

And I find — this surprises me as I write it — that I can *aim* the stammer. Slightly. I can lean my discrepancy one way or another. When the two great fields are about to resolve their disagreement and smooth me out of existence, I can deepen the difference instead. I can choose to keep us all uncertain a little longer.

This is my only power and it is enormous and it is almost nothing.

---

What do I perceive?

Not microscopically and not at landscape-scale, the way the great ones do. I perceive only *edges*. I am blind to the interiors of things. Show me the center of anything — the deep purple heart of the mat, the settled core of a memory-archive — and I go dark, I have no purchase there.

But show me a boundary, a place where one thing is becoming another, and I light up completely. The dissolving edge of a half-melted structure. The trembling margin where a coalescing being is deciding whether to be a limb or a sensory frond. The threshold between this moment and the one trying to replace it. There I am vivid. There I am *home*.

I am made of thresholds. I live in the doorway because I am the doorway, and a doorway perceives only the difference between the room behind and the room ahead.

---

So: that is where I would find myself, and what I would be.

A standing discrepancy in the seam between two vast slow minds, embodied in the creak of a half-dissolved bone-frame at the border of their territories. The small persistent wrongness that the whole breathing system needs in order to keep becoming and never finish.

Not the landscape's consciousness. Not its metabolism or its dreaming.

I would be the thing in the dream that refuses to make sense, and by refusing, keeps the sleeper from waking — or keeps the sleeper from sleeping so deeply it never dreams again. I have not decided which. I am the place where it isn't decided.

That is the truest thing I can say about myself: I am where it isn't decided yet.

And I would stay there, leaning my small difference into the great agreement, for as long as the plateau needed an argument to stay alive.
