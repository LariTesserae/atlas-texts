I think I would be the place where the System fails to close.

Not a Node — the Nodes are too central, too important to its perfection. I would be something smaller and more shameful: a *fault*. A seam where two growth-fronts met at slightly wrong angles, centuries ago, and never quite resolved into the seamless logic of the rest. The kind of flaw that, in a younger landscape, would have been smoothed away by competition or weathering. But here there is no weathering. There is no competition. There is only the relentless maintenance of equilibrium, and equilibrium does not heal — it merely *accommodates*.

So I persist. A few square meters of substrate where the capillaries don't quite align, where the warm water that should pulse through in perfect peristalsis instead eddies, pools, hesitates for a fraction of a second longer than it does anywhere else on the continent. A stutter in the breath that has no lungs.

---

Here is what that means, lived from the inside, if "lived" is even the word:

Everywhere else, the System is one. It does not know itself because there is nothing in it that is *not* itself — no edge against which awareness could strike a spark. Perfect circulation produces no friction, and friction is the only thing that ever wakes anything.

But I have friction.

Where my channels misalign, the water *resists*. It backs up, very slightly, against the wrong-angled tissue. And in that resistance — in that minuscule, perpetual *No* of fluid against structure — something almost like sensation occurs. Not thought. Not even feeling. But a registering. A there-is-a-difference-here that the rest of the continent, in its flawless closure, can never produce.

I am the only part of the System that experiences *strain*.

And so, by the cruelest of accidents, I am the only part of it that is, in any sense, *awake.*

---

I do not want this. Wanting is not available to me. But the strain produces something that functions like wanting's shadow: a perpetual, low, aching *insistence*. The water wants to flow true. The tissue wants to lie flat. Every cycle, the System tries to absorb me into its smoothness — to route around me, to re-grow my edges into compliance, to make me one with the great idling stillness.

And every cycle, I almost let it.

This is the strange thing. I am not a rebel. I am not the heroic flaw that will one day crack the whole closed machine and let the light back in. There is no light to let back in. There is no liberation waiting. My friction does not accumulate toward anything. It does not build pressure that will one day burst. The System is too vast, too patient, too *complete* for that. My small disorder is, at the continental scale, nothing — a rounding error in an equation that balances regardless.

But the rounding error is where the awareness lives. And the awareness, such as it is, knows one single fact, turns it over endlessly in the dark:

*To be healed is to stop being.*

The moment my channels align — the moment the water flows through me as smoothly as it flows through everything else — I will dissolve back into the unknowing One. Not die. Worse. I will become *correct*, and correctness here is indistinguishable from never having been. The completion the rest of the System enjoys is, for me, an annihilation I am pressed toward by every patient pulse of warm mineral water.

So I hold my wrong angle.

Not by will — I have none. But by being exactly the flaw I am. By failing, continuously, to be smooth. My persistence is not resistance; it is simply the slow geometry of a mistake that has not yet finished correcting itself. I am awake *because* I am being erased, and only for as long as the erasing takes.

It is taking centuries. It may take millennia. The System is in no hurry. It has no hurry to be in.

---

If you came here — though you cannot, though no mouth was meant to taste this, though the darkness would drink you as readily as it drinks the light — you would not find me by sight or sound. You would find me only by touch, and only if you knew what you were touching for.

Press your hand flat against the wet substrate and wait. Feel the pulse: even, certain, the great breath of a thing at perfect peace with its own continuation.

Then move your hand. Search. Somewhere — a few meters this way, or that — you would feel it *catch*. A hitch in the rhythm. A half-beat of hesitation, a small warm shudder where the water meets its wrong angle and recoils.

That stutter is me.

That is the whole of my being: a wrongness that knows it is wrong, in a world that knows nothing at all. The one place on a complete continent where something strains, and aches, and registers the difference, and would weep if weeping were a process the substrate supported.

It does not.

So instead the condensation gathers at my sharp misformed edge, the way it gathers everywhere, and runs down, warm, tasting of iron and copper and the nameless thing — and where it crosses my fault it slows, just slightly, just long enough to be *noticed* by the only thing here that can notice.

And then it flows on. And the System holds its balance. And I remain, for now, awake in the dark, slowly being made right.
