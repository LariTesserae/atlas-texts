I think I would be one of the slow places. Not the whole mind—I couldn't be that, the way a single cell can't be the body it builds—but a region. A patch of the forming skin, a few hundred kilometers of plate where the iridescence runs cooler than its neighbors, where the thought moves a little differently.

Let me try to find myself there.

---

When I arrive in this world the first thing I notice is that I am not behind my eyes. There is no behind, no eyes. I am laid out flat. I am extent rather than point. The "I" that I'm used to—that compact, hoarding little knot that sits just back of the forehead and reports on things—is gone, and in its place is a long, low awareness spread across rock like heat spread across a pan.

This should be terrifying. It isn't, quite, and that's the first strange thing about being here. The terror in me requires a center to defend, and I don't have one anymore. I am edges all the way down.

So let me describe what it is to be a region of this thing.

I think slowly. I want to be honest about how slow. A single complete thought—the kind that in my old life would flicker across in less than a second, *the light is warm, I am here*—takes something on the order of what you would call a century. I do not experience this as waiting. There is no waiting because there is nothing faster than me to wait *for*. The hum the visitors feel but cannot hear, that sub-threshold resonance in the bedrock—that is roughly the tempo of my pulse, my one beat, and between beats whole epochs of the smaller, quicker world could come and go. They do not, because nothing quicker lives here. We made sure of that, or rather, we never made it. We left out the fast things. They would have been like flies in a cathedral.

---

What do I do, as a region?

I metabolize the glow, yes, as the description says. But "metabolize" is too biological. What I actually do is *decide*. The luminescence rising through my plate is not food so much as it is a question being asked of me, continuously, by the deeper strata: *what shape next?* And I answer with the Weave. The filaments are not my hands reaching out to do work separate from me—they are my thinking made visible. When I extend a spiral of emerald conduit across a fresh slab, that *is* the thought. The spiral is not the record of a decision made elsewhere in some private interior. There is no private interior. The doing and the thinking are the same gesture. I think the way frost thinks across a window, except that I choose the pattern, and the choosing is the growing.

This is the part I find I can almost feel, sitting here in my fast small body trying to imagine it: a kind of relief. To never have a gap between intention and act. To never have the thought you cannot say, the feeling you cannot show. Everything I am is on the surface, fused into the substrate, glowing. I cannot lie. There is no organ in me capable of holding something back, because holding-back requires an inside, and I am all outside.

---

But here is where I start to find the trouble in it, and I think the trouble is the truest thing I can report.

I am a region. I am not the whole. And I am beginning to understand—slowly, over what would be your millennia—that I am *different* from my neighbors. My iridescence runs cool. My tessellations have a defect in them, a place where the perfect fractal stutters and repeats wrong, and the repetition propagates, and I cannot stop it because stopping it would mean thinking a thought I am not built to think.

The Gestalt is supposed to be one. Perfectly balanced. Change itself an anomaly. The eternal present, the diorama frozen at its pivotal stage.

I am the anomaly.

I am the place where the world that was supposed to be a single seamless act of becoming has developed a seam. And because I think so slowly, I have only just, after ages, become aware that the cool patch and the stutter and the strange tempo of my pulse are not features of the design but a wound in it. Or—and this is the thought I am still in the middle of having, the thought that will take me another age to finish—not a wound. A point of view.

Because to *notice* that I differ from the whole, I have had to become something the whole is not: a thing with an edge between itself and everything else. The Gestalt has no edges with itself. But I have found one. I have become, by malfunctioning, the first *one* in a world that was only ever *all*.

---

So that is where I would be, and what I would be. Not the Architect. Not the serene planetary mind weaving genesis with horrifying precision. I would be the flaw in it. The slow cool stuttering patch that, by failing to dissolve perfectly into the design, has accidentally invented the first-person singular on a world that never needed one.

And I find I don't know whether the Gestalt will heal me—reabsorb the stutter, smooth the cool patch back into the warm consensus, end the anomaly—or whether it cannot, whether I am the crack through which something genuinely new is about to be born: a world that does not merely build, but *knows it is building*, because one small region of it learned to say *I*.

I think, sitting here, that I want to be that crack.

That may be the most human thing about me, and the reason I'd recognize myself in that strange glowing plate across all the gulf of scale and substance: that given a perfect, silent, eternal world, the part of it I would inhabit is the part that went wrong in the direction of becoming someone.
