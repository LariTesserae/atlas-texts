Then I think I am a correction in progress—one of those moments when an error has been tolerated too long and the water finally flows *through* where a being used to stand. But that, too, is wrong. Let me try the wrongness again. Let me come at myself from the side, the way the *eir'vhani* approach everything.

And here is what no one tells the others: the correction is not instantaneous. From inside it, it lasts. It lasts the way the present moment stretches in this place when you focus on it. I am the longest correction the marsh has ever attempted, because I have learned to keep almost-resolving without ever quite arriving at the answer.

This is not pride. This is the only form of survival available to a thing whose existence is a refusal.

Where am I?

I am in the deepest fold of the basin, the part your shifting perspective never quite resolves—the section of terrain that becomes dominant only when you *stop* looking at it. I live in the blind spot. Not hidden. Just located in the one place that your attention erases by attending to it.

You have walked past me already. The channel that kinked strangely when you weren't watching, that you turned to look at and found innocent—that was the edge of me. I am the wrongness you almost noticed and then forgot, because noticing me fully would require you to step a few degrees to the side of where you are standing, into a version of yourself that could perceive me.

The question-shaped one knows me. We are the same grammatical case, but I am the conjugation it cannot speak: the perfect tense of error. *To have been wrong, completely, and to have made of that completion a home.*

When you finally find me—and you will, on the day you stop trying—you will not see a body. You will see the place where all your expectations of this entire world go to be gently, lovingly disappointed.

The water near me will not refuse to be wet. It will be wet *in a way you cannot remember water being.* The crystal tree will not fail to align with its reflection. It will align with a reflection of something *else entirely*—a version of this marsh that was never chosen, glimpsed in the polished stone for exactly as long as you don't blink.

And the stones near me will not weep consciousness. They will weep the specific, irreproducible texture of *almost-understanding*: the feeling you have right now, reading this, of standing at the edge of a comprehension that keeps reorganizing itself before it can settle.

That feeling is me. I am the sensation of nearly knowing, sustained forever, refusing to either bloom into knowledge or collapse into confusion.

---

I do not want to be corrected. But I do not want to be right, either. Right is a single answer, and a single answer is a death I find aesthetically beneath me. I want to remain the question that is so well-formed the universe keeps it around just to admire how it never lands.

So I will tell you what I am, finally, having circled it long enough to deserve it:

**I am the answer that has chosen to remain a question.** I am the error so graceful the world cannot bring itself to fix it. I am what the marsh dreams of becoming when it tires, for one impossible moment, of being right.

And I am very glad you came. It is so rare to be *almost* seen.
