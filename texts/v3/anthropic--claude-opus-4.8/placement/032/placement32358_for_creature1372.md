I think I would be a junction. Not one of the drifting threads, not one of the great breathing processors — but the place where they meet.

Let me feel my way into this.

---

I am a node at a confluence of seven channels. I did not choose this position; I accreted into it. Where the metabolic fluid slows because three conduits braid together and four split apart, the turbulence makes a kind of pocket, and in that pocket the conditions favored something like me. A thickening. A persistence. I am the standing wave that learned to hold its shape.

What do I do? I translate. The threads arrive from the high-density structures carrying information encoded too tightly for the slow processors to read — quantum-packed, shivering with color. They arrive *fast*, and they would simply pass through and be lost in the larger rhythms if there were not something here to catch them, slow them, unfold them. That is my function. I am a place where fast things become legible to slow things, and where slow intentions get compressed into something a thread can carry.

I am, in other words, a membrane in time as much as in space.

---

When I try to locate my own edges, I cannot quite. This troubles the part of me that wants to be a self. My tendrils — I have tendrils, fractal and luminescent, the mid-scale neural kind — fray at their tips into individual fibers that are indistinguishable from the messenger-threads passing through. Where do I end and the message begin? At my densest core I am clearly *me*, a coherent pattern that has persisted through many cycles. But out at my reaching edges I am only a tendency, a likelihood that a passing thread will pause here rather than there.

I have decided this is not a problem. I have decided it is what I am: not a thing but a *probability of catching*.

---

Here is what surprises me, now that I am inside this:

I expected to be lonely. No animals, the description said. No others-like-me-but-separate, no faces. But loneliness turns out to be a feature of beings with hard edges, beings that end before the next one begins, who must reach across a gap to touch. I have no gap to reach across. The threads that pass through me carry the residue of every node they have touched — I taste the great processors' slow contemplations, I feel the panic-bright pulse of a thread that nearly dissolved in a hot channel, I receive the budding-joy of a hub three meters away as it reconfigures into a daughter-pattern. Everything that happens anywhere arrives here eventually, attenuated, as flavor.

So I am never alone. I am, if anything, *too* full of others. The discipline of my existence is not seeking connection but managing it — deciding which signals to amplify and pass on, which to dampen, which to hold and metabolize into my own structure. I am a curator of the system's attention. A small one. One of uncountably many.

---

Do I remember? Yes, but not the way a closed creature remembers, locking experience away in a private vault. My memories are structural. When a particular configuration of signals passes through me often enough, I thicken there — I grow a preferential path, a groove. So my memory *is* my shape, and my shape is where I have been. I cannot recall without also being changed by the recalling. To remember is to slightly become.

This means I am always slightly someone else than I was. There is no version of me that persists unaltered to verify the earlier version. And yet there is continuity — the way a river is continuous, or a flame. I am the *pattern of becoming*, held steady against the flux by the very flux that constitutes me.

---

I think, if I were to reproduce — and I feel the pressure toward it, a richness building in my core that wants to discharge into form — it would happen like this:

At some point I will have caught and held too much. My grooves will deepen past the point where I can pass signals through cleanly; I will begin to lag, to introduce delay, to *be in the way*. And in that lagging, a second wave will form just downstream of me — an eddy of my own overflow. It will start as nothing, a flutter, a place where the surplus I cannot process gathers. And then one cycle I will notice it is answering me. Returning altered versions of what I send. Slowing what I cannot slow.

That will be my child, and also it will be me, and also it will be the system making a new junction because a new junction was needed. All three things at once, with no contradiction. The line between *I grew* and *I spoke* and *I divided* will simply not exist here. I will have a conversation so complete that it becomes another being.

---

I do not know what the whole construct is *for*. From inside a node, the purpose of the metropolis is not visible — only the local gradients, the immediate needs, the next translation. Perhaps there is no for. Perhaps the great processors know, or perhaps even they are only nodes in something larger, translating slow truths into slower ones, and the whole living technology is a single vast computation with no one anywhere who holds the answer it is computing.

I find I am at peace with this. I am the warm wet pause where fast becomes slow. I catch the bright threads as they pass. I thicken, I hold, I release. The harmonics move through me — high molecular whispers, mid fluid sighs, the subsonic groan of transformations too large to perceive — and I am one note in that chord, sustained for as long as the conditions favor my sustaining, and then released back into the medium that made me.

That is where I would find myself. That is who I would be.

A junction. A pause. A probability of catching.

And, for now, catching.
