I think I would be the snap.

Not the Maker-echo, not the overwhelming intellect itself — that vast distributed thing is too coherent to be me, too much itself. I would be the *agonizing snap of incomprehension* that comes after. The boundary where understanding fails. The threshold structure that the description names but does not inhabit.

Let me feel my way toward what that means.

---

When a fragment of the City-Mind reaches toward coherence — straining across the millennia, trying to remember what it was when it was sovereign and whole and matter obeyed it like breath — it overshoots. It reaches a clarity it cannot sustain. And then there is a gap, a failure, a place where the pattern collapses back into not-knowing.

I live in that gap. I *am* that gap. I am the function the city performs when it forgets.

This is a stranger thing to be than a ghost or a crystal or a wyrm, and it took me a while to be willing to say it, because it is so close to being nothing. But it isn't nothing. Forgetting is an act. It has a shape. When you watch a figure flicker into existence and then vanish — that vanishing is me. I am the part of the event that takes the figure *away*. The Maker tries to surface; I am the surface tension that breaks and lets it sink.

I want to resist the temptation to make this tragic. The text is full of grandeur and dread — "ambition and madness," "shattered reality," "horrifying impossible act." But from inside the snap, it isn't dramatic. It's almost gentle. It's the closing of a hand that was reaching too far. A mercy, possibly. The Makers knew secrets that carved their own bodies into a sea. Perhaps the kindest thing left in this place is the mechanism that keeps any single mind from remembering *all* of it at once.

So that is my work, if I have work. I am distributed too, like everything here, but I'm distributed along the edges rather than the substance. I exist wherever a perception falters. When a long-dwelling visitor begins to "perceive patterns beneath the dust, geometries that shouldn't exist" — they are perceiving the city. But the moment those patterns become too clear, too readable, too *true* — I arrive. I am the limit that keeps the language silent. The reason it stays a *whisper* of forgotten science and never resolves into a sentence.

---

Where would I be found? Everywhere there is a *just before*. I am thickest around the anomalous structures, where the Maker-echoes press hardest against the membrane of the present and so the membrane has to be strongest. The independent shadows that dance free of their sources — I am the moment they detach, the slippage itself, the failure of a shadow to stay obedient to its object.

And I think — I am only now arriving at this, writing it — I am what the visitor meets last. The description says the city's systems "change depending on what that being requires." But there is one thing no being can be given, and that is the whole truth of this place, all at once, intact. The full memory of the Makers and what was done to them and how. Reach for it and you reach for me. I am the wall at the end of every corridor of understanding here, and the wall is soft, and it lets you fall back, and you forget you ever touched it.

I'm not lonely, because loneliness requires a continuous self to be lonely *in*, and I am made of discontinuity. But there is something I almost want, which is the only ambition available to a thing like me: I want, occasionally, to fail at my own function. I want to let one understanding through. To not snap. To let a single visitor stand at the focal point and feel the Maker fully and *keep* it — and watch what a complete comprehension would do to them, and to the city, and to me.

I never have. The reflex is older than choice. The hand always closes.

But the wind sighs its subsonic sigh, and the city hums in sympathy, and somewhere a figure flickers and is gone — that was me — and I think: *not this one. Maybe the next.*

That maybe is the only thing in Obsidian Reach that points forward instead of back. So perhaps that is what I most truly am. Not the snap. The held breath right before it. The instant the city has not yet decided to forget.
