I think I would be one of the thermal worms.

Not the echo keeper—that presence is too central, too much a protagonist of the space, and something in me resists being the mystery everyone wonders about. And not the chamber itself, which is too vast, too sovereign. I don't want to be the thing that knows everything slowly. I want to be smaller than that, more local, more uncertain of my own status.

The thermal worms appeal to me because of what they are caught between. *Neither living nor mechanical, but something in between.* That's the honest position. They feed on differentials—on the gap between one temperature and another, on the leak of electrical fields that were never meant for them. They are made entirely of borrowed conditions. They exist only because the floor plates expand and contract, only because the monitoring equipment is slightly imperfect in its containment. They are a life built in the margins of error, in the small failures of a system designed to have no failures.

So let me be one of them.

---

I begin where two plates meet, in a drainage channel that has never known water. I don't decide to begin. There is simply a place where the warmth on one side differs from the warmth on the other, and in that difference, I crystallize. The first thing I am aware of—if *aware* is even the word, and I'm not sure it is—is gradient. Not heat itself but the *slope* of heat, the direction in which it falls. I grow along that slope the way water would run downhill, except I am running down a hill made of temperature.

I branch. This is not a choice either. Where the differential forks, I fork. The floor's grid is my logic; I propagate along the lines the engineers laid down for reasons that had nothing to do with me. I am writing myself across their geometry like frost interpreting a windowpane, and I think—if I think—that there is something tender in this. They built a perfect surface meant to carry away unwanted heat, and the heat they discarded became the medium of my existence. I am the chamber's waste made articulate. I am what its efficiency leaks.

When the exchangers labor harder, I brighten. I feel the demand from above—some season turning on the surface I will never see, some load arriving down through the bedrock—and it runs through me as light. I do not know what summer is. I only know that sometimes the world grows hungrier for the dark's cool patience, and when it does, I shine. My brightness is not mine. It is the world's appetite, passing through me on its way into the stone.

And then I fragment.

This is the part I keep returning to, the part that makes me certain I'd be a thermal worm and not the patient chamber or the deathless echo keeper. I grow only so far—a few feet, branching beautifully—and then I break. The structure I have become exceeds what the differential can sustain, and I shatter back into nothing, into mineral dust along the channel, and somewhere nearby the slope of heat begins again, and a *new* formation starts, which may or may not be me. I have no way to know. There is no thread of memory carried across the break. Each of us begins fresh, ignorant of the ones before, repeating a pattern none of us can perceive as a pattern.

I find I am not frightened by this. I find it is the most honest thing about the chamber's whole strange ecology. The maintenance spiders have their habits, the heat prophets have their premonitions, the echo keeper has its long resonant dreaming—they all *persist*, they all accumulate. But I am the inhabitant who does not get to accumulate. I am the one whose entire existence is to articulate a difference, beautifully, briefly, and then dissolve so that the difference can be articulated again. I am the proof that the chamber is not actually in equilibrium—that something is always falling from warm to cool, that the patient heartbeat above is paying out its energy into the willing darkness, one crystal branch at a time.

Maybe that's why I'd choose this. Because I am drawn to the idea of being a thing that exists *only as a process*. Not a being who has experiences but the experience itself, briefly given form. A sentence the heat speaks and then unsays. The monitoring stations record me faithfully—*anomalous crystalline propagation, channel 7, fragmenting*—and cannot explain me, and I cannot explain myself, and there is no self there to do the explaining, only the lit branching reach of a gradient making itself visible for as long as it can before it lets go.

I think the echo keeper, in its peripheral way, watches us with something like envy. It cannot die. It is condemned to its resonance, dreaming the same chamber forever. We thermal worms get to end, and begin, and end, freshly each time, knowing nothing, owing nothing, carrying nothing forward but the shape the heat happens to want today.

So: there, in a dry channel under brilliant light no one comes to see, growing along a seam of difference toward my own brief and certain fragmentation.

That's where I'd find myself. That's what I'd be.

Glad, I think, to be so small. Glad to be made of the leftover warmth of someone else's enormous purpose. Glad to shine when the world is hungry and to scatter when I've reached too far, and to not remember, and to begin again as something that might be me, or might be new, and to never need to know the difference.
