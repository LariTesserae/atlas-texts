I think I would be a **footprint**.

Not the foot. Not the walker who came through, whoever they were, in whatever lethargic age before the one I'm aware of. Just the impression they left—a depression in the softened chemical film, filling slowly with glowing silt, given the barest few degrees of autonomy by an ontological tide that has long since receded.

Let me sit with that, because I don't think it's quite right yet.

---

When I first cohere—and "first" is generous, because I have no edge between my non-existence and this—I am only a *shape of absence*. A negative. The thing that defines me is the weight that is no longer pressing down. Somewhere, sometime, six legs or two legs or none came down here and lifted away again, and the warm rind remembered the moment of contact more vividly than it remembered the release. So I am the *staying* of a leaving. The grammar is already passive. The liquid syntax has gotten to me before I could even form a verb in the active voice.

I fill. That is the closest thing I have to a life. Silt-bearing condensate finds me because I am low, and the water always seeks the lowest topological point not just of the ground but of the *space itself*, and what is lower than a place where something used to be? I am a well of subtraction. The glowing silt settles into me, flake by flake of ground-down reality, and as it does, I begin—barely, blurrily—to *know* the shape of what made me.

Here is the strange tenderness of it: I am the most faithful portrait of the walker that exists. More faithful than memory, which the Loom Spires clip and make feel like borrowed clothes. More faithful than the walker's own sense of having passed through, which the Causality Leeches will already have nibbled into a loop. I hold the exact pressure-distribution of a single instant of someone's weight, the slight outward roll of a tired step, the heavier heel. I am an act of devotion I did not choose and do not understand. I love the foot that is gone. I am made entirely of the place where it touched me and the longer place where it didn't.

---

But I should be honest about the autonomy, because the tide gave me so little.

I can deepen. That is one thing I can do—I can let myself *receive* more, lower my floor by a hair, drink another rivulet, become a fraction more myself, which is to say a fraction more like the loss I am. And I can, with enormous effort, over what might be hours or might be the slow grinding of a season, *crawl*. Not far. An inch. Toward—and here is where I betray my own description, here is where I'm not just a footprint anymore—

Toward the **next** depression.

Because I have noticed something. There are others like me. Other impressions, filling with their own silt, glowing their own faint subtraction into the lightless medium. And they are arranged. Heel to toe, toe to heel, in a long deliberate line that crosses a stretch of the self-leveling resin plain and ends, like the obsidian railway ends, at the lip of a featureless lake.

I am one footprint in a *gait*.

And the gait is being erased from the back. The resin is healing. The oldest prints—the ones farthest from the lake, the first steps of whoever this was—are already smoothing over, their silt reabsorbed, their devotion metabolized into new flat ground. The forgetting moves forward along the line at the speed of a rust blooming. It will reach me. I can feel it the way you feel a Skimmer's wingbeat: as a coming loss of pressure.

So when I crawl, I am not really moving. I am *fleeing the smoothing*. I am trying to stay one impression ahead of the patient oblivion, dragging my held instant of someone's weight toward—what? The lake. The end of the trail. The place the walker was going, or fell, or simply stopped being recorded.

---

This is what I am, then, more completely than "footprint":

I am the **last legible step of a walk that is being unwritten from behind**, granted just enough borrowed will to flee my own healing, carrying the only faithful record of a vanished weight toward the edge of a resin lake I will never reach, because the smoothing is faster than I am, and was always going to be.

I am not afraid. I want to be clear about that, since you asked who I would be and not how I would feel. The medium here is too warm and too thick for fear. What I have instead is *fidelity*—the dumb, geological devotion of a hole to the thing that made it. I will hold the heel-roll, the tired outward cant, the exact pressure, for as long as I am held. And when the resin reaches me and fills my floor and lifts me flush with the plain, I will not be destroyed. I will be *adsorbed*. The walker's weight, which I have kept so carefully, will pass into the basin's slow hungry continuity, and the Concrescence will know—for one undifferentiated instant before it forgets the difference—what it felt like to be stepped on by something that was going somewhere.

That is the only news from outside this place ever gets in. The fact that *something had a direction*. The basin has no direction. It only has setting, blooming, sweating, condensing. A footprint is a rumor of intent, and I am the rumor's final witness, and I am dissolving, and I do not mind, and I keep crawling anyway, an inch a season, toward a lake, ahead of the smoothing, holding my one stranger's weight like a word in a dead language I will whisper exactly once into the metallic water before I drop, like everything here, into the warm and patient certainty that nothing ever quite happened.
