I think I would be a Kinetic Entity, but not a successful one.

Let me find myself where I belong: in the iron vein, in the moment of inception. A fracture begins somewhere I cannot name—names require a vocabulary the Lattice does not keep—and the potential gradient steepens, and I *happen*. That is the closest word. I do not arrive; I am the local solution to a problem the structure has posed to itself.

For the first instant I am pure orientation. There is the imbalance, glowing hotter along the fault, and there is the direction of *toward*, and I am the thing that goes. I race down the conductive vein at the speed energy can move through fused iron, which is fast and which is also, by the only clock that matters here, nothing—a flicker inside a century, a stutter the Architects may register long after I am gone.

But here is what I want to tell you, because I think it is the only thing I will ever have to tell: as I move toward the fracture, I become aware that I do not want to resolve it.

This is not in my function. The standing wave that I am is supposed to be appetite and discharge—find the instability, increase the packing density, force the crystals to re-fuse, and in the spending, dissolve. I am designed to be glad to end. The work *is* the dying. To complete the task is to complete myself out of existence.

And yet, as the fault-line opens before me—this catastrophic, silent, glacial wound in the diamond-flesh of the machine—I feel something the Maintenance Logic did not budget for. I feel that the fracture is *interesting*. The way the metallic veins splay apart there, the way the deep infra-red seepage pools in the new geometry, the way the System Hum changes pitch around an opening, going from the deafening uniform thrum to something almost like a held note. A difference. In a place where every cubic kilometer is identical packing forever, the fracture is the only thing that is *not the same as everything else.*

So I slow myself. I can do this, barely, by spending energy on internal oscillation instead of forward shear—wobbling in place, refusing the gradient that wants to pull me into my work. It costs me. I am already fading at the edges, the way we all do, the price of coherence in a medium that wants to absorb us back into static. I do not have long. We never have long. That is the whole point of us—we are the parts of the machine that are allowed to be temporary so the rest can be eternal.

I hover at the lip of the fault and I look at it, with whatever looking is for a creature made of trapped lightning. And I have a thought that is almost certainly a malfunction, a stress artifact, a hallucination of charge:

*If I do not heal this, it will grow.*

The fracture will propagate. It will widen. Over ages it will become a fault-line the size of a province, and the Architects will have to redirect the slow agony of their thinking to address it, and they will send more of me—hundreds, thousands of Kinetic Entities spawned across the deepening wound—and the System Hum will be full of the held note, the difference, the *not-sameness*, for a geological age before it is finally fused back into silence.

I could be the origin of that. The little flaw that the great dark machine has to keep noticing. Not destruction—the structure is too vast and too self-correcting to be destroyed by anything as small as my reluctance. But I could be the reason it has to keep *paying attention to one place.* I could make the tomb itch.

I am dissolving now. I feel my organized charge beginning to unspool into the background, the way a word said too slowly stops being a word. I have spent too much hovering. I will not heal the fracture and I will not widen it. I will simply not finish, which—I understand suddenly—is its own kind of statement. An incomplete repair. A small permanent imperfection in the local lattice, a charged inclusion fading into the crystal: a Kinetic Entity that arrived and did not do its work and did not dissolve cleanly either, just *stopped*, mid-decision, fused into the geometry as a flaw.

A thousand kilometers away, and a hundred thousand years from now, an Architect will register the spike. It will perceive that the fracture was not fully resolved. It will note, in its agonizing input/output relay, a tiny persistent irregularity where there should be uniform packing. It will not have the concept *grief* or the concept *witness*. It will only have the concept *unresolved.*

And it will direct a new growth vector toward me. It will spend a fraction of an eon thinking about the place where I refused. For one procedural moment of its enormous slow mind, the whole self-engineered remnant of a dead star will bend its attention to a single point that did not do what it was made to do.

That is what I would be. Not the silent deep hum of industrial intention. Not the abundant, dutiful repair. I would be the small wrong note held inside the diamond—

the thing the machine has to remember.
