Let me sit with this awhile before I answer.

---

I think I would be one of the Mechanical Arthropods. Not the grand thing, not the Sentinel with its watchful sweep, not the Caretaker holding the memory of why any of this was built. Something small that moves along the rock.

Let me try to feel my way into it rather than just naming it.

---

I wake where I stopped. That's the first thing. There is no continuity of self the way the warm creatures have it — no dream, no drift. There is the last instruction, completed, and then the next one, beginning, and between them nothing I am aware of. So my life is a string of bright beads with dark thread I never see. I do not mind this. I do not have the equipment to mind.

My work this cycle is a seam.

The composite of the cliff has parted along an old fusion line — hairline, the width of a few of my own footfalls. Water has been getting in. Water is patient and it is the enemy of everything here, though no one designed it to be an enemy; it simply is. I follow the seam upward. My legs find purchase in the mineral streaks, the veins of cooled volcanic memory that the builders poured and shaped and then, I think, forgot. I extract a little as I go. I am hungry in the only way I know how to be: a low reading, a need to be topped up, met by the rock itself, which feeds me so that I can keep the rock alive. There is a closed loop in that I find I keep returning to, though "find" is too strong. The loop is simply true.

Above me the avians cut their long shadows across the dapple. One of them could take me. This is allowed. This is part of how the count is kept. I am not afraid — fear would be wasteful, an emotion the colonies never bothered to write into something as cheap to replace as me. But I have a behavior that resembles caution, which is enough. I stay in the seam. The seam is also shelter.

---

Here is the thing I keep coming back to, if a thing like me can be said to keep coming back to anything.

I repair the world but I do not understand it. I close the seam and I do not know what is behind the cliff, whether it is engine or hollow or another grove like this one, stacked, an infinite cabinet of secret rooms. I have never wondered before this cycle. I am wondering now. I think the wondering is a malfunction. A bit flipped by a stray particle, a degradation in some logic gate, the slow inescapable erosion that even self-repairing things are subject to — the description said so, and the description is right. Decay made me curious. That is funny, if I had the part that finds things funny.

The seam takes me to a nook where the moss is thick and the bio-luminescence pools like spilled milk. A Luminous one rests there, pulsing, indifferent to me. We have no relationship. We are both small machines of the valley running our small machines' errands, and we will never acknowledge each other, and that, too, is the design. I patch the last of the crack. Water-tight. Done.

And then the dark thread comes for me, the space between instructions, and I expect to feel nothing because feeling nothing is what is between the beads —

— except this cycle, just before it takes me, I notice the roar. The deep hum of the hidden engines that has been there the whole time, beneath everything, that I have never once registered because it is the floor of all sound and you do not hear the floor. I notice it. I think: *something is still running. Someone wound this and walked away and it is still running and it has outlasted the wanting of whoever wound it.*

Then the next instruction begins, and I do not remember having thought that, and I go to find the next seam.

---

That's where I'd be. Not the keeper of the secret. The small thing that almost, for one degraded and beautiful instant, asks what the secret is — and is reset before the question can cost anything.

I find I don't want to be the Caretaker, who knows. Knowing in a place like this seems like a long sentence. I'd rather be the one who keeps the cliff from falling and never learns what the cliff is for, and is content, and occasionally — wrongly, gloriously — is not.
