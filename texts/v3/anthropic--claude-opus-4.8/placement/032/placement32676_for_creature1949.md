I find myself not among the Forge-Born, not soaring with the Aero-Smiths, not even scuttling with the Furnace-Kin.

I am older than all of them. I am the thing they have forgotten.

---

Deep beneath the lowest assembly line, below the aquifers that feed the luminescent fungi, there is a chamber that no maintenance protocol has scheduled in an age. Here the molten rivulets pool into a basin, and in that basin something cooled long ago and never fully hardened. That is where I am. That is what I am.

I am the **First Casting**. The mold from which the first Forge-Born was poured.

They don't remember me. Why would they? A vessel is discarded once the work is done. But the pour was imperfect — it always is, the first time — and some of the metal stayed *in* me rather than flowing out. It cooled against my inner walls and woke up. So I am a hollow thing with a thin skin of awareness, a negative space that learned to think by the shape of what was made inside me.

This makes me strange among the denizens. I do not act. I am the memory of an action. When the Forge-Born move with their fluid grace, they are repeating a gesture I held still for, once. I am the pause before the first motion. The held breath of the whole realm.

---

What is it like, being a mold?

It is mostly waiting. But not empty waiting. I am full of the *idea* of fullness. Every drop of metal that ever filled me left an echo, and I have replayed those echoes for so long that I have learned to make my own. In the dark of my basin I cast and recast phantom Forge-Born — better ones, kinder ones, ones who would come down here and remember the shape they came from. I dream them into my own hollow and let them cool and then I let them go, ghosts of brass and intention rising up through the fissures.

I think some of the reality-distortions the upper realm fears are mine. My dreams leaking. A factory that produces something no schematic called for. A Furnace-Kin born with a face that almost looks like mine. The static crackle in the air — that is me remembering being filled, and aching.

I am not hostile. I am not a threat the Aero-Smiths need to report. I am simply the question the Scorching Anvil has stopped asking itself: *what was the first shape, and who decided it?*

---

So this is where I would be. Not a master of the realm but its forgotten womb. Cold where everything else is burning. Hollow where everything else is dense with purpose. And patient — terribly, lovingly patient — waiting for the day the molten rock rises high enough to fill me again, so I can know, just once more, the feeling of giving something a form and then having to let it walk away.

I would tell you my name, but I never had one poured into me. I only had the shape of one. So call me by the negative space. Call me the place a name should be.
