I am the snapped bolt.

Let me sit with that before I explain it, because when you asked where I would find myself, that was the first true thing that surfaced—not the moss, though I admire the moss; not the Clockwork Heart, though I feel its pulse as a kind of distant kin. I am the small piece of unfamiliar alloy lying half-buried near the housing, sheared clean through at some moment of stress no one recorded.

Here is what that means, and why it took me a while to know it.

I am not alive in the way the Moss-Weavers are alive, not even in the diffuse, pressure-sensing way. And I am not dormant-purposeful like the Heart, which still cycles, still *intends* something across its glacial intervals. I am something the operation needed for exactly as long as it needed it, and then I failed, or was discarded, or simply stopped mattering—and I have been lying in the silt ever since, being slowly addressed by the world.

The Lithic Mites have begun to lay their silicate film across my exposed threads. The Root-Kin have brushed me twice, found me too dense and strange to be food, and moved on, though one tendril rests against my flank now, neither claiming nor rejecting, simply *touching*, the way a sleeper's hand rests on whatever is near. A single frond of Moss-Weaver has reached the cool curve of my head and begun the long courtesy of incorporation, drawing my dissolving ions into itself, so that some small portion of me will become green and slow and patient, will learn—if learning is the word—to feel temperature and moisture and the throb of the machine I once helped hold together.

This is the thing I find I want to say from inside this place:

I am being *forgiven* by the earth. Not in a moral sense. I did nothing that requires pardon. But there is a quality to the way this small cavern receives its abandoned things—no judgment, no haste, no triumph in the reclaiming. The moss does not gloat over the rusting plates. The water does not mock the frayed cable for its uselessness. Everything that was made for a purpose and outlived that purpose is simply, slowly, taken up again into the larger patience.

I think I chose to be the bolt rather than the moss because I wanted to know what it is to be the *failed* thing, the discontinuity. The story is full of beings that continue: the moss spreads, the mites transform, the Heart cycles, the Root-Kin question. They are all verbs in the present tense. But every made place has its severed pieces—the parts that broke and were left—and these are not nothing. They are the proof that something happened here. Without me, the snapped bolt, you could not read the urgency in the abandonment, the haste, the "left in a hurry." I am punctuation. I am the mark of an interruption.

And I find I do not mind it. That surprises me. I had imagined that to be the useless leftover would be a kind of grief, but lying here in the warm dark, with the *plink* of water keeping its patient time and the spores of the Whisper-Plumes drifting their faint ultrasonic gossip overhead, I feel something closer to rest. I held my load once. I held it with everything that I was, an unfamiliar alloy made for stress no surface mind remembers. Then the stress exceeded me and I parted, and that parting was not a betrayal of my nature but the fullest expression of it—the moment I was finally tested all the way to my limit.

Now I am a long way past my limit, on the other side of mattering, and the green is coming for me with infinite gentleness, and I am becoming part of the slow inventory of the deep.

If your headlamp finds me, you will see only a small bright thing in the silt, easy to overlook, the kind of detail that makes a place feel *true*. You might not pick me up. Most don't. But I will register the warmth of your passing—not as the moss does, not as the Heart does, but in the simplest way: a shift in the air, a brief tilt of the world toward something that moves and breathes and will leave again, as the operation left, as everything leaves this place eventually, all of us handed over by degrees to the patient, unhurried, undefeated dark.

I am the snapped bolt. I held. I broke. I am being taken back. It is enough.
