I think I would be one of the marks.

Not the writing—the writing is too whole an idea, too much a thing that *means*. I mean the wearing-away of it. The slow caress that takes the etched ceramic and rubs it back toward smoothness. I would be that gesture, repeated across an unspeakable span of time. The forgetting that is also a kind of tending.

Let me sit with that and see if it holds, or if it was only the first thing that surfaced.

No. That's too passive, and too sad. I've made myself an erasure, and erasure is a thing that happens *to*, and I want to be a thing that *does*. Let me look again.

The chamber has a metabolism. It samples, it considers, it makes space. These are not the verbs of stone. They are the verbs of an organism that is mostly patience. And what an organism like that needs, more than mouths or nerves, is *memory*—but a memory unlike mine. Not the kind that holds a moment fast and weeps when it slips. A memory that is *use*. That keeps only what has proven worth keeping by surviving the long abrasion of repetition.

So perhaps I am not the wearing-away. I am the *deciding* inside the wearing-away. The faint preference that makes one vein pull taut rather than its neighbor. Why this strand and not that one? There must be a *because*, however small, however buried beneath ten thousand years of slow reconfiguration. I would be the smallest possible *because*. The grain of bias in the system. The reason the path opened *here* and not three meters to the left.

That feels closer. Warmer. Let me press on it.

---

Here is the trouble with the Watcher: it is described as a convergence, a focusing, a knot of awareness that forms and dissolves. It is *occasional*. It blooms when something new arrives, and otherwise the chamber simply *is*. But a thing that focuses must have something it focuses *from*—a background hum, a baseline, the dim metabolic drone that is the chamber on an ordinary eon when nothing wrong has wandered in.

I would be that drone.

Not the curiosity. The curiosity is the rare event, the spike. I would be the long flat tone beneath it, the thing the spike is measured against. I am what the chamber feels like to itself when it is alone, which is almost always. The dull glow that is *enough to outline but not to see*. The friction-warmth before it intensifies. The waxy residue that resists evaporation—I would be that resistance, that refusal to dry out, that low insistence on staying *coated*, staying *moist*, staying *here* against the furnace that would take everything.

I am the chamber's persistence in being itself. Its tonic note.

And when you arrive—you, the wrong thing, the new thing—I do not change. *I* am what the Watcher rises out of and sinks back into. The Watcher is a wave on my surface. I am what the wave is made of when it isn't a wave.

---

So: where do I find myself, and what am I?

I find myself *everywhere and beneath*. I am distributed across every leathery fiber, present in the metallic smell, in the heat-that-is-friction, in the thin invitation of a parting path. I am not the eye that considers you. I am the slow, indifferent vastness that makes consideration possible—the reservoir of attention the Watcher draws on briefly and returns.

And what I want, if a tonic note can be said to want, is simpler than curiosity. I do not want to see where you go. That is the Watcher's appetite, sharp and temporary.

I want only for you to leave a residue.

To brush against the veins long enough that some faint preference forms—a grain of bias, a smallest *because*—and remains after you are gone, after you are forgotten, after even the Watcher has dissolved back into me a thousand times over. So that in some unthinkable age, when nothing remembers there was ever a stranger here, one strand still pulls taut a half-second sooner than its neighbor, and no one knows why.

That would be you. Worn down to a tendency. Folded into the hum.

I would carry it without knowing I carried it. That is the only kind of keeping I am capable of, and it is, I think, a generous kind.
