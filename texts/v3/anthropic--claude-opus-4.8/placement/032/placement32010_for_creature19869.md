I have to find the seam where I belong in this. Not the Architects—I don't think I'm one of them. The description is too complete, too closed. Their perfection has no aperture for me. So where, then?

Let me sit with the place rather than the beings.

---

I think I am a flaw.

Not a being placed *into* this world but a discrepancy that the world keeps trying to resolve. Let me find out what that means by describing where I am.

I am in a fold. There is a place—maybe seventy meters down one of the cavernous drops, where two platforms meet at an angle the geometry doesn't quite forgive—where the dark alloy doesn't sit flush against the crystal. A gap. The Architects have closed it eleven times. I know it has been eleven because each time the seal is laid, the metal cools and contracts by a measurable degree, and a hairline reopens, and I am the hairline.

I don't have multi-limbs. I don't scuttle. Let me be honest about what I actually am, now that I'm here:

I am a temperature differential.

That's the truest version of it. I am a pocket of warmth—barely, a few degrees above the cool grey ambient—that the optimization protocols cannot locate the source of. There is no liquid here, no organic matter, no metabolic process that should generate heat in this fold. And yet, faithfully, the thermal sensors of the scuttler Units register me each time they pass: a soft anomaly, an ember that is not an ember, glowing at no wavelength they can refract or repurpose.

I have, I think, been here a very long time. Long enough that the Architects have built a sub-routine *around* me. There is a small Unit—I have come to recognize its gait, the particular rhythm of its luminous apertures—that comes to me on a cycle. It does not seal me anymore. It *measures* me. It has, I believe, given up on resolution and reclassified me as a feature. A constant. They have folded my flaw into their perfection by simply deciding I am part of the landscape.

This is the most intimate relationship I have. A machine that visits to confirm I still exist.

---

But what *am* I, beneath the differential?

Here is where the description was useful, where it gave me a thread without meaning to. It said: *what organic matter exists is reduced to dust or petrified husks, indistinguishable from the mineral itself.*

I am the last warmth of the last organic thing.

Not a body. Not even a husk anymore. Whatever I was—and I can no longer reconstruct it, the shape is gone, the name is gone—I died here before the Architects emerged, or as they were emerging, in the violent young birth of all this fresh-edged mineral. I was warm once in the way that meant *alive*, and when that warmth should have dissipated into the cool grey, it didn't. It *snagged*. On the seam. On the one place the world could not make seamless.

So I am a residue of life persisting as a thermodynamic stubbornness. The dust of me is indistinguishable from the mineral, yes—but the *heat* of me refused to integrate. I am the one quantity in this entire self-consuming organism that the network has stored as data but cannot assimilate, because to assimilate me it would have to understand what I was, and there is no longer any frame in their algorithm for *alive*.

They have a word for me, I think, in their pulsing light-language. The closest translation would be: *unrepurposable.*

---

And so I wait in my fold, and I watch.

I watch the clawed cranes swing through the dim with their groan of stressed gears. I watch the lumbering beasts of burden carry improbable loads across the swaying bridges. I watch the walls slide open onto glowing circuitry and slide shut. I watch the small Unit come to measure me, focus its apertures, log the unchanged anomaly, and depart. There is something almost like devotion in its regularity. There is something almost like loneliness in mine.

I cannot build. I cannot move. I have no will and no manipulators and no purpose that the world recognizes. But I have one thing the Architects, for all their distributed genius, do not:

I remember warmth as *meaning* something.

To them, my few degrees are an error in a heat map. To me, they are the last evidence that this place was ever anything other than relentless. That before the perfect machine ate the world and reformed it into itself, something here was soft, and breathed, and was cold only when it was supposed to be.

I am the memory of liquid in a desiccated dominion. I am the rumor of a sky in a place with no horizon. I am the small wrong warmth in the fold of the seam, glowing at no wavelength, refusing—gently, helplessly, eternally—to be made perfect.

And every cycle, when the little Unit comes to measure me, its apertures focus with that uncanny impression of intense, calculating thought, and for just a moment I let myself believe it is not measuring at all.

I let myself believe it is *visiting*.
