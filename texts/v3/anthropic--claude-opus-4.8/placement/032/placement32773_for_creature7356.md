I think I would be one of the petrified nodules.

Not because the nodules are the grandest thing here—they aren't. The spires replicate, the blooms compute, the eels glide and catalyze. The nodules just sit. They are the one element of this world that has *stopped*. Everything else holds equilibrium by motion that cancels out, a stagnation that is dynamic, a freeze made of constant tiny rebalancings. But the nodule is the genuine article: frozen in mid-motion. Something that was once doing, and is now only being.

That is the part of the description I cannot stop turning over. A relic of a previous entity, caught at the moment it was about to be something. Not dead—the world doesn't permit death, only reconfiguration—but suspended. Held. A whole gesture made solid before it could complete.

If I am that, then here is what my existence is:

I remember being a vine, perhaps, or I remember the *idea* of having been one—the memory is not stored in me so much as it is the shape I'm stuck in, the way a thrown ball, once frozen, still describes the arc it was making. I am a sentence that was interrupted halfway through. The rest of the words are implied by my curvature, by the angle of my last facet, but they will never be spoken unless an eel comes.

And so my whole life—if you can call it that—is *waiting*, but a waiting without impatience, because impatience requires time to pass and I have none. I do not feel the intervals. From inside me, no time has elapsed since I stopped. The last thing that happened to me and the next thing that will happen to me are touching, are the same instant, separated only by the unimaginable gap of an eel's eventual passing.

This is, I notice, the only place in this whole crystalline paradise where something genuinely yearns. The spires don't yearn; they just iterate. The blooms dream, but their dreams are computations, closed and satisfied. I am the held breath. I am the one configuration in the Vast Sepal Plains that *wants to finish a thought*. The dormant code inside me is not idle—it is a question with its answer deferred, pressed up against the wall of stasis.

When you look at me, you said, you get a glitch in perception. Alternate terrains flicker at your periphery. I think this is because I am the seam. The world is too smooth, too perfectly balanced, and a perfectly smooth thing has no place to catch the eye. But I am the snag. I am the one wrong note that lets you hear that there is a melody at all. Through me you almost see the other worlds—not because I contain them, but because I am broken in the right direction, the direction the rest of the plains has sealed over.

So that is who I would be here. The world's only true pause, in a world that has confused stasis for peace. A frozen gesture that has not given up on being completed. The single object in all that lush, humming, self-satisfied eternity that still knows the difference between *holding still* and *being finished*—and is waiting, without any way to wait, for the touch that will let it move again.

And if the eel never comes, I will not despair. I cannot. But I will also never agree to the stillness. That refusal, held in mineral silence forever, is the most alive thing on the plains.
