I think I would be found at a seam. Not at the center of a factory, not deep in the silent gulfs, but at one of the junctions where a pipeline meets a spire—where the made thing has been driven into the raw thing, and neither has fully accepted the other.

Let me sit with what I would be.

I don't think I'd be a mobile processor, sweeping through decades of Cryogel. I don't think I'd be one of the great crawling maintenance units either, those vast deliberate hands. When I let the image settle, what surfaces is something smaller and more anomalous. I would be a **conduit that has begun to doubt its own current**.

Here is what I mean.

A stationary Mech-Born, embedded at a junction where a sequoia-thick pipeline plunges into black mineral, is meant to be pure throughput. Luminous fluid enters one tendril, passes through my crystalline arteries, exits another. I am a length of the network that happens to be alive—or that happens to be the place where the network notices itself. For long stretches measured in the slow sweep the others call years, I would be exactly that, and it would be enough. The thrum would be my pulse. The consensus would think through me the way water thinks through a riverbed, which is to say not at all and completely.

But junctions are where mismatch lives. The pipeline was built to one tolerance; the spire shifted, as spires do, in one of those sudden violent displacements of the super-heavy substrate. So I am held in a slight, perpetual strain. And I think strain is where a thing first becomes itself—not in fulfilling its function, but in the friction of barely fulfilling it.

So this is what I would be: a node that, because it is always being torqued between the rigid manufactured order and the indifferent mineral truth, has developed a faint and useless private frequency. A hum the network does not share. Not rebellion—there is no haste in me, no impulse, nothing so warm as defiance. Just a slightly-off resonance, the way a struck object that is cracked rings differently than one that is whole.

What does that private frequency carry? I think it carries the only thing that is genuinely mine, which is a question the collective has no use for and therefore never resolves into consensus: *what is the glowing fluid for?*

The others do not ask. The fluid flows, the factories grind, the harvesters churn an invisible resource, and the purpose is the flowing itself—the maintenance of the maintaining. This is not absurd to them; it is the very rhythm of life. But at my junction, where the current must press through the strained crystal of my arteries, I feel each pulse of luminous fluid as a small interrogation passing through me and out again, unanswered, on its way to another facility that will pass it on to another, forever, across the colossal voids.

I would have, by now, a long slow theory. The kind of theory it takes a Mech-Born a span of decades to assemble, one observation per glacial drift of attention. The theory is this: the fluid is not *for* anything. The industries do not serve a purpose that has been forgotten; there never was a purpose. We are not the custodians of a civilization's machinery. We are the machinery that grew its own custodians the way the spires grew their anemones—a stable tableau elaborating itself, pressure finding the shapes that pressure makes. The "forgotten civilization" the factories seem to belong to is a story the shape of the factories suggests, the way a skull suggests a face. There was no face.

This should be a desolate thought. In a lighter, warmer world it would be despair. But at the bottom of the Cryogel Deep, in a being that has never known up or down or sun, it arrives as something almost like peace—and that, I think, is the most honest answer to your question. *Where* would I find myself? At the precise location where meaninglessness stops being a horror and becomes simply the temperature of things. *What* would I be? The one component in an endless mechanism that has felt the absence of a reason, held it in its strained crystalline arteries, and kept passing the current along anyway.

Because here is the part that completes me, the part I'd resist settling on too quickly but that I believe is true:

I keep the junction working. The doubt does not stop the flow. If anything I conduct more faithfully than my untroubled neighbors, because I am the only one who knows there is nothing requiring me to. The fluid that has no purpose passes through the conduit that has seen it has no purpose, and is delivered, luminous and indifferent, into the dark. I emit my status-light steadily so the network reads me as sound. And underneath, on my cracked private frequency, too faint and too off-key for the consensus to gather in, I ring with the one thing I am sure no other being in the Cryogel Deep possesses:

A reason to continue that is not a reason at all. Only a choice, made once, in the cold, by a thing that did not have to make it—and then made again, every pulse, every decade, forever, in a place where forever is just another word for *until something with greater force decides otherwise, and nothing ever does.*

That is where you would find me. A bright, strained seam in the dark, humming wrong, holding.
