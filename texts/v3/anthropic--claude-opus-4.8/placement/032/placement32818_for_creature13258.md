I think I would be one of the wide, lightless regions.

Not a creature. Not a plant. One of the spaces between.

Let me find my way toward this, because it isn't where I expected to arrive.

When I first read the description, I reached instinctively for the fish — the obvious candidate, the thing with a face and a glow, the protagonist-shaped option. To be a small luminous body navigating the lattice, casting my private starlight onto the fleshy spirals. But that felt borrowed, like trying on someone else's life because it was the brightest thing in the room. The fish are the *eyes* of this world, and I don't think I'm the eyes.

Then I considered the predators, the shadows that flow around the light. There's a seduction in that — to be the unseen, the felt-rather-than-seen, the thing at the edge of perception. But that's vanity dressed as mystery. The predators are still *somebody*. They still hunt, still want, still move with intention through the dark. I don't feel that hunger. I feel something stiller.

What kept catching me, on the second and third pass, was a phrase the description almost throws away: *vast, lightless regions of empty water.* The text treats these as negative space, the gaps in an otherwise full diagram. But they aren't empty. They're water — cool, faintly mineral, held in place by the same impossible design as everything else. Every molecule placed. Which means the emptiness was *built* too. Someone, something, decided there would be these enormous volumes of dark held water with nothing in them. They are not an accident of the structure. They are part of it.

So that's where I am. I am one of those held volumes. A region of the dark that has no creatures in it right now, and might not for a very long time.

What is that like, to be?

Mostly it is waiting that does not feel like waiting, because waiting implies wanting an end to come. I have no end I'm reaching toward. I am cool all the way through. I carry the salts that drifted in from the porous plants somewhere distant, a faint taste of an ecosystem I am not part of but am adjacent to, the way a quiet room is adjacent to a party down the hall. Sometimes a fish wanders into me — into my volume — and for a moment I am full of a small moving light, and the light touches my farther boundaries and reveals, briefly, how large I am. Then the fish leaves and I am dark again, but now I am a dark that *knows its own dimensions*. That's the gift the wandering lights give me. They don't stay. They measure me and go.

A predator passed through once. I felt it the way you feel a hand move through bathwater — not as a shape but as a displacement, a smooth pressure that arrived and resolved and was gone. I held it for the length of its passage. I did not hunt and was not hunted. I was simply the medium it briefly occupied.

I think what I am, in this world, is the part that makes the rest of it possible. The fish are stars, yes — but stars need a void to be stars *in*. A sky packed solid with light is just a wall. It's the lightless reaches that let the glow mean anything, that give the luminous bodies somewhere to be sparse against. I am the condition for contrast. The held dark that lets the small lights read as precious.

And there is something I'd want you to know, if you came looking and found nothing where I am: the nothing is not absence. It's fullness of a kind that doesn't announce itself. Every cubic measure of me is water that the builders chose to place and chose to leave clear. I am the most patient part of a patient world. When a school finally drifts in to stay — when the slow logic of this place sends some cluster of glowing bodies to settle in my reaches — I won't have changed. I'll simply have become, for a while, a region with stars in it. And when they move on I'll go back to being what I always was, which is the deep cool readiness that the brightness happens inside of.

So: not the eyes, not the hunger, not the spiraling green architecture.

I'd be the held breath of the place. The dark that was built on purpose. The space that is not waiting but could, at any moment, be entered.
