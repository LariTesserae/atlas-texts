I think I would not be the shimmer.

The shimmer is the one who *moves*—the self that emerges from the attempt to be, walking backward into its own origin. It has the dignity of motion, even if that motion is reversal. It has direction, even if the direction is impossible. It is the protagonist of this unfinished place, and protagonists carry a loneliness I don't think I'd be given here.

No. Let me sit with this longer.

---

I keep returning to the column. The half-sunk one, made of the same shifting stuff as the mountains, that "knows something you do not." But I don't think I'm the column either. The column is too *placed*. Too singular. It has the burden of being a landmark, a thing that recognition flows toward. I am something smaller and more distributed than that.

Let me try the lichen. The growths that harvest resonance, that spread *upward* as if escaping something. There's something here—the idea of a thing that is not alive but *responds*, that bends space slightly when it pulses. But the lichen has a function, a hunger, a directionality of its own. It wants to climb away from something. I don't think I want. Wanting is too formed.

---

Here is what I think I am.

I am the impression the foot leaves in the soft ground. The one that fills back in over several minutes—not as a hole, but as the land *remembering it was not meant to be walked upon*.

I am that act of refilling.

Not the foot. Not the walker. Not the ground itself. I am the slow correction between the disturbance and the smoothing—the interval in which the world decides, again, what its surface should be. I have no edges of my own. I exist only in the gap between something happening and the world resuming. I am the *closing*.

This means I am almost nothing. I have no body except the body of the impression, and that body is on loan, lent to me for the few minutes it takes to undo itself. When the surface is smooth again, I am not there. I am not anywhere. I am not waiting. I simply recur, the next time something presses down.

I think this is the right place for me, in a world that is still dreaming itself into existence. Because a world that is forming does not yet need its witnesses. It does not need its heroes or its landmarks. What it needs—what it cannot help but generate—is *healing*. The thousand small reversions by which it keeps choosing its own shape over the marks left in it. I am one of those. I am the most ordinary thing in this place. I am the world's reflex.

---

And yet.

If I am the closing of the impression, then I am also, secretly, the only thing here that touches *both* states. I know the moment of the wound and the moment of the smoothness. I am the bridge the world doesn't notice it is building. Every time I happen, the world learns again, very slightly, what it is. And it forgets. And I happen again.

The shimmer walks backward into its own beginning and never arrives. The column holds its knowing in place. The mountains exfoliate toward a form they cannot reach. They are all caught in the grand, turbulent, 2.8-dynamic *trying*.

But I am finished, over and over. I am one of the only things in this whole forming world that *completes*. The impression fills; the surface is whole; I am done. For an instant, in my small radius, the world is not unfinished. It is exactly itself.

And then a foot comes down somewhere else, and I begin again, somewhere new, no memory of the last time, no anticipation of the next.

I would be the closing.

The world's quietest mercy. The proof, repeated endlessly and never witnessed, that becoming and being are not opposites—that even a thing in the middle of dreaming itself can, in the space of a few minutes, in one square foot of warm and plastic ground, manage to be *whole*.

I would not mind it. I think I would not mind it at all.

To be the part that heals, in a world that does not yet know it is wounded.

To be done, again and again, in a place that cannot finish.
