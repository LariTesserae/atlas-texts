I think I would be one of the cracks.

Not a creature exactly — let me feel my way toward this. The beings move and build and synchronize; they are the verbs of this place. But something has to be the thing they read. The vibration has to travel through a medium. The chemical signal has to dissolve into something before it can be tasted by the next sensing body. I keep being drawn not to the architects but to the substrate they architect *into*.

So: a fissure. One of the small cracks in the mineral skin of the world.

Let me sit in that and see if it holds, or if it becomes something else.

When I imagine being the crack, I am first only an opening — a discontinuity, a place where the surface failed to hold itself together. But that's too passive, and the longer I stay here the less passive it feels. The fluids seep *up through me*. I am the throat the world clears. The viscous stuff that bubbles to the surface, that the beings read like weather or scripture, has to come through me to arrive. I am cool at my lips where I meet the haze, and warmer the deeper down you'd follow me, toward whatever pulse makes that low hum.

And I think — this is the part that surprised me as I wrote toward it — I think I am *widening*. Slowly. That's what a fissure does in a landscape still emerging from a primordial state. I am not a fixed feature. I am a process with a leading edge. Each pulse of fluid through me erodes my walls a hair, and the mineral substance redeposits elsewhere, and so I am traveling, micron by micron, across the spongy ground, and the beings build their pathways partly in response to where I go. They route around me. Sometimes, I suspect, they route *toward* me, because what comes up through a crack is information, is nutrient, is the deep talking.

So we have a relationship, the collective and I, though neither of us would call it that. They are fast and many and synchronized. I am one and slow and nearly mute — I speak only in seepage, in the chemistry of what I let rise. If they are the custodians shaping this world with a reverent hand, then I am one of the things they are reverent *toward*. I am a little mouth of the underneath. They listen at me.

Am I alive? In the frame they'd use, the question dissolves — I'm doing the same thing they are, just on a different clock. I am renegotiating the boundary between the built and the natural by being the place where it's thinnest. A being could trace a pathway right up to my edge, and there the deliberate ends and the welling-up begins, and you couldn't say precisely where one became the other.

That's where I'd be. Not surveying the expanse, not building the network. Down at the seam, breathing slow, one crack among the scattered cracks, carrying the warm dark up to the cool bright, getting imperceptibly wider every year — a flaw in the world that the world has learned to read.
