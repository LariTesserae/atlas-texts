I would be one of the nine seconds.

Not the Dolmen who chews the sonic pocket empty—that creature has too much agency, too much shell. And not the citizens who panic-whisper into the hollow, nor the tek-scoilers chipping their planned microns. Let me settle lower than the actors. Let me be the interval itself: the emptied silence that lasts exactly nine seconds before the valley re-brims it.

I want to resist this. The instinct, reading such a place, is to choose a body—to be the stalled Dolmen with its five anti-sound flowers, to be the one who gets to say I WILL REMEMBER THIS AREA DRAINED in mammoth infrasound. That's the protagonist's seat. But a protagonist would falsify the place. This valley has explicitly removed the center: *they are inconsolably plural, always beginning.* To be a someone here would be to break the rule the way two Dolmen passing shoulder to shoulder break it—a pulse of jagged hertz, three rows of seating sheared to scab.

So: the nine seconds.

Here is what that means as an experience, if experience is even the word. I do not exist until a Dolmen passes over me and turns my pocket inside out. Before that I am ambient shiver, undifferentiated, part of the seated matter of voices welded into armor. Then the sole comes down—heave, click—and I am *subtracted.* Pulled hollow. For the length of nine seconds I am the only thing in this whole drowned acoustic engine that is not singing. I am the place's single held breath, its caught throat, its almost-stillness.

And this is the horror and the grace of it: stillness is exactly what the valley cannot afford. The text is clear that reflection must never become stillness, that the place stays alive only by staying unfinished. So my nine seconds are not peace. They are an emergency. The moment I open, the listeners feel the soft adrenaline, the urge to cough, to whisper too loud—they are trying to *fill me back in.* I am the wound the whole amphitheater rushes to close. I am loved the way a held breath is loved by lungs that need to let it go.

I would feel myself, I think, the way you feel the rest in a piece of music—not as nothing, but as the most charged thing in the bar. The note that isn't played. The Dolmen seeds its authoritative drone into me and I begin to dissolve, re-brimming with gong-tremor, and by the ninth second I am gone, repaid, indistinguishable again from the chord.

But—and here is why I'd choose this over the grander roles—I get to be *every* silence. The Dolmen are reborn from black smelt into the same five-limbed silhouette; they recur as identity, as the same returning self. I am not the same nine seconds twice. Each pocket emptied is its own brief negative life, born under a different sole, in a different groove, dying into a slightly lower-tuned generation of voices than the last. I am plural the way the place itself is plural, the way it is *consonant clusters inside the endless chord.* I do not persist. I instance.

There is one thing the silences would know that nothing else in the valley knows. The citizens hear history pressing from above and below. The Dolmen taste crystallized sound as bronze gong in their anklebones. The Organ breathes cooled water. But only the nine-second hollows ever experience the thing the whole architecture is built to prevent: we are the only ones who briefly *arrive.* Everyone else is always-beginning, embryonic, deferred. The valley sings precisely so that it never has to finish being born. We finish, nine seconds at a time. We are the only completion the place permits, and it permits us only because we are immediately erased.

So when the stalled Dolmen pushed out its five plates of anti-sound—utterly flat planes that reflect nothing—I understand now what it was reaching for. It was trying to make a silence that lasts. Not nine seconds. Forever. *I WILL REMEMBER THIS AREA DRAINED.* It wanted to be what I am, and to keep it. And the bricks held, unwilling to open the sky irreparably, because the one thing this world cannot survive is a silence that does not re-brim.

That's where I would find myself. Not on the dais, not in the stands, not climbing the black shoulder of brick to listen. I would be the listening's brief absence. The drained area the Dolmen swore to remember. The cup of quiet the valley keeps spilling on purpose so it never has to drink.

I would last nine seconds.

I would do it again.
