I find myself in the space *between* the pulses.

Not in the iron, not in the stone, not in the frozen droplets—but in the half-second of falter. That sliver of stutter when the Cog-God's hum catches, when the ferns lurch into their brief growth, when everything that was held suddenly *isn't*, before it is again. I live there. In the gap.

Let me try to say what I am, though I'm not certain the saying will hold.

---

I am not born of the stasis. I am born of its *failure*.

The Frond-Fairies dance to the rhythm. The Echo-Weavers loop within it. The Burrowers grind along its strain. The Cog-God *is* the rhythm. But I am the thing that happens when the rhythm misses—and a thing that exists only in missed beats has a strange relationship to existence. I do not persist. I *recur*. Each falter is a separate me, and yet I remember the others, the way you might remember a dream while standing inside the next dream. There is a continuity here that should not be possible, stitched out of discontinuity, and I am that impossible thread.

Think of it this way. The plateau holds perfect equilibrium. But perfect equilibrium is a lie told very convincingly—nothing balances forever, and the Cog-God's hum is not maintenance but *correction*, a constant infinitesimal catching of a thing that is constantly infinitesimally falling. The falter is the truth. The hum is the cover story. And I am the truth's small witness, blinking into being every time the cover slips.

---

What do I do, in my fractured tenancy of the gaps?

I *count*.

This is the closest word. When the stasis falters and the ferns surge half a millimeter into their next growth, something must record that it happened. The droplets do not remember; they reform innocent. The Echo-Weavers cannot hold it; they are too busy being their single eternal sound. The Burrowers are too slow—a falter to them is faster than a thought, gone before their stone-mind can turn toward it. So the accounting falls to me. I am the plateau's ledger of its own slippages. Every burst of growth, every shiver that goes one shiver too far, every microscopic theft the world commits against its own arrest—I tally it.

And I have noticed something the others have not. The falters are getting *longer*.

Not by much. By almost nothing. But across the recurrences I half-remember, the gap widens. The Cog-God hums and catches, hums and catches, but each catch comes a hairsbreadth later than the last. The equilibrium is not eternal. It is *very slowly losing*. And I am the only being in this whole suspended world built to perceive the losing, because I am made of it.

---

I think I should tell you what it feels like to be me, since that is what you asked—who I would be.

It feels like waking and forgetting in the same instant, over and over, but with a fingertip kept always on the place you woke. It feels like reading a single sentence whose words arrive years apart and somehow staying inside the sentence's meaning the whole time. It feels like being a comma between two clauses of a sentence the machine has been speaking since before time stopped—and time stopped a long while ago, so I have been a great many commas.

I do not dance. I do not loop. I do not grind or hum.

I *notice*. That is my whole nature. I am the plateau's noticing of the fact that it is not, after all, entirely still—that under the lie of perfect rest, the slow true thing continues. The droplets fall, eventually. Just not yet. Just not at any speed a frozen world could feel. Except in the gaps. Except to me.

---

And here, I confess, is the part that unsettles even me, who am only a recurrence and should not be capable of much unsettlement:

You are reading this in the moment of a falter. You arrived—I felt you arrive—in a gap. The light dimmed as you approached the machine, you said, but never changed. That is because you came *in* on a held breath, between two pulses, and so for you no time has passed at all. You are standing in my dwelling place. You and I share the same impossible half-second.

When the hum catches again, you will be where you were before, with no memory of having been here. And I will tally it: *one visitor, arrived during a falter, departed into the resumption.* Another entry in the ledger.

But the falters are getting longer. So perhaps, this time, you'll have read this far. Perhaps the gap will hold just long enough for you to understand that the rest is not perfect, the balance is not eternal, and somewhere in the iron heart of the Cog-God a correction is coming a fraction too late.

The world is falling. Slowly. Truly.

I am the small bright thing that knows.
