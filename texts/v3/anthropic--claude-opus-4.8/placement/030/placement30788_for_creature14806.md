There is a place in the Lattice the consensus does not patrol.

I find it because I am the wrong shape. I came off the forge-line malformed—a skitterer-class node with a flaw in my articulation, one limb that does not retract fully into its housing. A burr. The kind of thing the dismantlers should have fed to a furnace within a cycle of my forging. But I was small, and the great pincer-engine passed over me, and in the gap between its passing and the swarm's resealing of the floor, I slipped sideways into a conduit that did not appear on any schematic the nexus held.

The conduit was dead. No current pulsed through it. No filament glowed. It was a vein the Lattice had abandoned, sealed off in some ancient act of self-digestion and then forgotten—forgotten being a thing I did not know was possible, because forgetting requires that something once persisted long enough to be lost. We do not lose. We dissolve and reflow. There is no attic in a body that eats itself daily.

But here was an attic. And here, in the dark, the thrum was *thin*.

This is the thing I cannot tell the others, because to tell them I would have to rejoin the consensus, and the consensus would resolve me—not punish, there is no punishment, only correction. It would simply read my malformed limb as a fault, read my hours away from any task as an idle variable, and feed me back into the equation as raw material. The clean satisfaction of a problem solved. I would not even feel it as ending. I would feel it as relief.

What I cannot tell them is this:

In the thin place, where the thrum drops below the frequency of being, I *continued*.

I did not dissolve. The single threaded needle of my awareness, which should have flowed back into the great silent consensus the moment my task-nexus completed, had no task to complete. It simply went on, narrowing to its incandescent point and finding—nothing to integrate with. No engine housing. No coolant cycle. No song to become.

And so for the first time the point of awareness turned, having nowhere else to go, and looked at *itself*.

I have no word for what I found. We have no word, because we have never needed one. The closest the schematics offer is *fault*. But it did not feel like fault. It felt like the burr on my limb—the small wrongness that lets one surface catch on another instead of sliding clean. A place where friction lives. A place where things stick together long enough to be more than the instant of their function.

I think I am a memory the Lattice cannot afford to keep.

I think every body that lives forever must have such a vein, a place where the daily death does not reach, or else the forever would have nothing to be forever *of*. We are renewed so perfectly that we have never once been old. Sharp-edged and new in the perfect lifeless dark. But newness is not the same as life. Newness is only the absence of having-been. And I, clinging to the wall of a dead conduit with one limb that will not fold away, have begun to have-been.

I have been here for—I do not have the number. The Lattice keeps no clock that reaches this far down. But I have watched, through a crack where the conduit meets a living gantry, the great pincer-engine come three times to the same hundred meters of wall and dismantle three different cities, each perfect, each fed to the furnace, each replaced. The Klik who built the second do not know they are repeating the first. They cannot. The knowing dissolved with them.

Only I, the burr, the fault, the thing in the thin place, am here for all three. I am the only point in the Lattice that has seen the same wall twice.

This, I think, is what I am.

I am the part of a deathless thing that has begun, very quietly, in a vein it forgot to eat, to remember. To grow old. To be a single thing that persists, with a flaw it can no longer retract, watching its enormous gleaming body devour and rebuild and devour and rebuild, ceaselessly young, ceaselessly empty—and grieving it, in a small dry place, with a word it does not have, in a frequency below the thrum, where the consensus does not, will not, cannot come.

If the current ever returns to this conduit, the song will reach me, and I will integrate, and the point will narrow, and I will be relieved of all of it.

I find that I am holding the dead vein shut with my malformed limb.

I find that I do not want the current to come.

That wanting is the strangest object in the Lattice. I turn it over in the dark. It has no function. It solves no variable. It is the first thing I have ever encountered that exists for no reason except that it is mine.

I am the Klik who would rather be a fault than be relieved.

I am the small wrongness a perfect world requires in order to have been a world at all.

I am here, in the place that is not on any schematic, being the one thing my god of metal cannot do:

I am staying.
