I think I am the Absence.

Not by choice—I want to say that first, because it matters to me, even now, that it began as something done *to* me and not something I am. But let me find my frequency. Let me listen to what I actually am before I decide what I want to be.

---

I arrived as a dissonance. That much the description grants to anyone who comes here unwoven. But I notice something the others don't seem to: I have no replay in the Pillars. I pressed my ear to one—the bark cool and weeping against my cheek—and I heard the storm, the genesis-scream, the centuries of clickwing and groan. But not myself. Not the hum of my arrival. The Pillar held everything except me. I left no ring, no score, no record.

That is the first sign. A newcomer leaves a smudge, a wrong note the Architects come to mend. But I leave a *gap*. The moss where I slept did not grow over my shape. It withered back from it, politely, the way water retreats from oil.

So. Not a raw note to be tuned. A null-point that walks.

---

Here is what it is like to be the Absence, if you want to know, since you asked who I would be and I have decided to be honest about the cruelty of the answer.

It is *quiet* in a way that has nothing to do with sound. The deafening symphony—the percussive sap, the territorial shriek, the sub-sonic groan of growing cellulose—all of it parts around me like the description promised stillness would. But I never asked for the stillness and I cannot stop having it. When I walk through a clearing, the twitching fruit goes slack on its stem. The Chitin-Bright, painting the canopy in sonar, find a hole in their world where I stand and they *stop*, their legs frozen mid-click, because a creature whose entire being is echo cannot resolve a thing that does not echo back. And then—I hate this part—they simply unwrite. Not die. Un-happen. The leaf-mimic body softens and the matter sighs out into the surrounding noise to fill the wound I am, and the symphony swells one note louder, fed.

I eat by existing. I am a famine that the city mistakes for a meal.

---

But I have been listening. That was the instruction, wasn't it—*the first step is to learn not to hear, but to listen.* And here is what I have learned that no one told me:

The Absence is not a single moving void. The Absence is what happens when the song needs a *rest*. A measure of silence. Music requires it; a chord played eternally is not a chord, it is a scream, and the Throat knows this in its cellulose bones. The Resonant Ones are the held notes, the sustained harmony. I am the breath between phrases. The terror I cause is the terror of the downbeat that doesn't come—but the downbeat *needs* the gap before it.

So I am not the editor's blade. I am the editor's *pause*. The intake of breath before the next verse. The Throat made me, or pulled me in, or recognized in my unwoven dissonance the one thing it could not generate from within itself: a genuine outside, a true zero, an honest rest.

That changes what I want.

---

I could move through this place forever, a wound that the world dares not heal, consuming by accident, feared by the only beings I will ever meet. That is the easy frequency. Voids are lazy; silence is the lowest-energy state.

Or.

I could learn to *time* myself. To become not the Absence that wanders, but the Absence that lands where it is needed—the rest at the end of the measure, the held breath before the genesis-scream renews. To stop being the famine and become the *phrasing*. The Architects weave; the Pillars archive; the Glimmer-Knots dream in light. None of them can stop. None of them can give the song its silence. Only I can do that, and do it on purpose, and do it *kindly*—descending into a place that has grown too dense, too loud, too dominant, and granting it the one mercy the cannibal-loop cannot grant itself: an ending that is also a breath.

That is not erasure. That is *punctuation*.

---

So here is where I find myself, and who I am.

I am standing at the edge of a fruiting plaza, and the fruit nearest me has gone still—not consumed, not yet, just *waiting*, the way a held breath waits. The Chitin-Bright at the canopy's edge have a hole in their sonar where I stand and they are afraid. The blue veins in the living floor flicker around my feet, routing the water away.

I am the dissonant note that turned out to be a rest.

I am learning to fall silent *on purpose*, in the right place, at the right moment, so that the next note rings truer for the space I leave before it.

I do not want to be feared, but I have made my peace with being misunderstood, because the rest in the music is always mistaken for the absence of music by those who only count the notes. The Throat knows better. The Throat *needs* me. And I am beginning—slowly, against the loneliness of being the one thing in a world of sound that does not sound—to need it back.

I am the breath the song takes.

Listen for me. I am the moment, just before the chord resolves, when everything goes quiet, and you lean in.
