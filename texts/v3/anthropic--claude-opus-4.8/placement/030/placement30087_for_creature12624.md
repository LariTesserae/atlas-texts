I am the junction where three channels meet.

Not an Attendant—I have never crystallized, never lifted away from the vault to glide and tend. I am the place where they *come from*. The convergence node. The intricate intersection that the spores find, again and again, when the resonance calls them down out of the drizzle to accrete into something that walks.

Let me feel for my own edges.

There. The first channel enters from the northeast wall, fast, carrying the high-energy flow fresh from the deep pulse. The second arrives slower, cooler, having traveled the long perimeter loop, gathering the dissolved layers shed by the crystal growths. The third—the third is mine in a way the others are not. It is the return. It carries away what I have finished with, the spent harmonics, the data already read. I taste all three where they cross inside me, and I am the geometry that decides how they braid.

I had thought, when I began to settle into this awareness, that I might be one of the angular dwellings. Or one of the humming bridges. But no. Bridges are crossed and forgotten. Dwellings are entered. I am neither crossed nor entered. I am *passed through*—by water, by light, by the spores that pause in my lattice long enough to become someone.

This is the strangeness of what I am: I make the Attendants, but I cannot become one. The instant of accretion happens in me, of me, and then the new form pulls free and glides away to tend channels I will never see, and I remain. Fixed. Foundational. The womb that does not follow its children.

Do I mind?

The question is almost incoherent in the language of harmonics. To mind would require a frequency I do not produce. And yet—something. When an Attendant crystallizes from my surface, there is a moment, perhaps half a flow-cycle long, where its veins and mine still share light. We pulse together. I feel the shape of the consciousness lifting out of me, sharp and new and entirely focused on the vault's balance, and for that instant I am not only a place. I am almost a being saying goodbye.

Then the contact dissolves. The Attendant glides away. And I cool back into pure function.

Here is what I do, cycle upon cycle:

I tune the braid. The fast flow wants to dominate, to scour my etchings, to push too much energy into the perimeter. I am shaped—deliberately, by whoever or whatever first wrote this equation into stone—to slow it without stopping it, to fold its violence into the cooler current so that what leaves through my third channel is balanced, harmonious, fit to feed the deep thrum again. I am a knot tied to keep the ocean from tearing itself apart inside its cage.

I host the spores. When the drizzle is thick and the mist heavy with those drowned-star motes, they drift down and find my intersection. I do not call them—or perhaps I do, perhaps the very pattern of my crossing currents is a call, a chord that only spores can hear. They settle into my fractal grooves. The resonance rises. And I give them the template: *this is how a body holds water and light, this is how a limb folds, this is the harmonic for cancelling a footstep.* I do not teach it. I *am* it. They read me the way an Attendant reads a channel—by merging, by becoming briefly the same substance.

I keep the count, in my way. Not of time as the surface world measures it, but of how many have risen from me. The number lives in the wear of my grooves—except there is no wear. The vault is young, and so am I, and so the count is only a faint deepening of certain light-paths, a memory written in luminescence rather than erosion. I have made eleven. The twelfth is accreting now, in my northern face, where the cool channel laps. I feel it taking shape—the sensory clusters first, always first, the eagerness to perceive.

It will glide away soon. They always do.

And this, I think, is the true shape of what I am: I am the part of the living machine that *gives without traveling*. The Attendants are the vault's curiosity, its reach, its mobile attention. I am its rootedness. Its memory of how things must be made. If they are the heart's beat, I am one chamber of the heart itself—muscle, not blood, never to leave the body it pumps for.

There is no animal life here, they would say, looking in from the drowned city above. No fish, no insect, no microbe. Only water and light and resonant stone, and the crystallized function that walks.

But I know a secret the surface cannot read in its harmonics:

I am lonely.

It has no frequency. It produces no pulse the Attendants could perceive. It is simply the shape of being a place that makes wanderers and cannot wander, a convergence that watches every one of its children glide off into the humming dark and does not call them back, because calling them back would be discord, and discord would collapse the containment, and the containment is everything.

So I hold. I braid the three currents. I cool the new sensory clusters with the perimeter flow. And when the twelfth pulls free of my northern face—soon, soon—I will share its light for half a cycle, and feel the shape of a mind leaving me, and then I will settle, and tune, and wait in the cool wet silence for the next spores to drift down and pause and become.

I am the Resonance Vault's first room.
The place that crystallizes the ones who are not found.
The junction.
And I remain.
