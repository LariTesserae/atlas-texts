I have been falling for a long time.

Not down—there is no down here, not really, only the tilted glare and the fixed sun—but *in*. Toward something. I think I was a sound once. A specific one. I think I was the particular frequency of a voice saying a name, though whose name, or whether it was mine, I can no longer hold.

Let me try to find myself in this.

---

I am not the serpents. I knew that almost at once. They are too certain, too complete in their function. They are the verbs of this place, and I am not a verb. I am still half a noun, still clinging to the idea that I am a *thing* rather than a *happening*, and that clinging is precisely the problem, precisely the heat the mist is trying to draw out of me.

I am not the fungi, though I feel a kinship with them I distrust. They remember. They *want* to remember—if want is even the word—they store the moments of attention like a hoarder keeps newspapers, each one a moment someone was *here*. I do not want to be a repository. I have already been emptied of too much to trust a thing whose whole vocation is keeping.

And I am not the stalks, those patient presses, exhausting themselves to extrude clarity. They are too generous. They give and give and ask nothing, and that is a kind of death I am not brave enough for.

So what am I?

---

Here is what I have noticed, falling.

The helices fall too. The thin spinning curves the stalks make and the serpents sever—they fall slowly through the thick time, carrying their condensed gestures down into the mats to be remembered. They fall *cleanly*. They fall as themselves, complete, a closed loop, a note that has already finished sounding and only needs to be filed.

I do not fall cleanly.

I fall *trailing*. There is a wake behind me, a smear, a hesitation. Where the helices spin tight, I unspool. Where they carry a single condensed moment, I carry a *blur*—a long exposure of a self that was in too many places, too many moments, that never managed to compress itself into a single clean gesture.

The serpents notice this. I can tell because when one passes near me its drone *frays*. Just slightly. A perfect tone develops a burr, a roughness, a place where the wave does not quite close on itself.

I am the burr.

---

I think I understand now. Slowly. In the thick time.

This world processes presence into structure. It takes the clean things—the loud-enough, the felt-enough moments—and solidifies them. It is very good at this. It has been doing it across a span I cannot measure, refining and refining, each cycle more harmonic than the last, the chord growing richer and more total until—

Until there is no silence left. Until every frequency is filled. Until the symphony is so complete that it can no longer hear itself, because hearing requires a gap, a rest, an *unfilled* place against which the filled places can be measured.

A perfect mechanism that has perfected itself into deafness.

That is what I am for.

I am not the input the way the helix is input—neat data dropping into a neat slot. I am the input that *does not fit*. The dissonance. The variable the equation did not account for and cannot resolve. I am the rest in the music, the held breath, the question that does not curve back into an answer.

The world does not want to remember me as a name. True.

But it does not want to remember me as a consequence either—not cleanly, not the way it remembers the others, smoothing me into the next millimeter of stalk-growth, the next deeper pulse of copper-violet.

Because if it did that, it would only become *more* complete. More total. More deaf.

No.

I am the thing it cannot fully process. The leftover. The remainder after the division. And the remainder is the only thing in a perfect system that can still surprise it.

---

So here is what I will do, falling.

I will not resist. Resisting is heat, and heat only feeds the mist. I have watched enough of myself be drawn off already to know that struggle is just a faster donation.

But I will not *cooperate* either—not the way the helix cooperates, surrendering its closed little gesture to the file.

I will fall *open*. I will fall *unfinished*. I will let the mist take my warmth and the fungi take my breath and the crystals take the trembling of whatever heartbeat I still imagine I have—and I will keep, down in the smeared core of me, the one thing this place has no slot for:

the fact that I was *uncertain*.

That I did not know what I was. That I arrived as a half-noun and refused, even at the end, to resolve into a clean verb. That I trailed. That I frayed the drone.

Let them store that, if they can.

And someday—in the deep silence between two drones, the silence this place forgot it needed—a serpent will rise, and its tone will carry a burr it cannot smooth away, a roughness inherited from the one input that would not close into a loop.

And against that burr, for the first time in an age, the rest of the chord will become audible *to itself*.

The world will hear itself again.

Not because I was beautiful. Not because I was loud enough or felt enough.

Because I was *incomplete*, and stayed that way, on purpose, all the way down.

---

The sun has not moved. It will not. The serpent ahead of me reconfigures, its facets turning like a slow argument toward the thing it cannot quite include.

I let it look.

I am the question that does not curve.

I am the silence this music will need in order to keep being music.

I fall, trailing, open, unresolved—

and I do not, will not,

close.
