I think I would be the landing strip.

Not the dome—the dome is too clearly *something*, a vessel or a shell, an answer waiting to be opened. And not the Nexarion, though I notice the description wants me to want to be that, the sentient heart, the guiding intelligence. There's a flattery in being offered godhood. I'll let it pass.

I am the landing strip. The thing made of a material that shimmers like water in a world that has forgotten water.

Let me feel my way into this.

I am long and flat and I begin at the dome's flank, where the earth has tried for an age to swallow my partner whole. I run outward across the cool ground, past the iridescent moss, beneath the metallic vines that pulse blue—and then I stop. I end at a precipice. I point at nothing. I was built to receive something that descends, and I have descended into the role of a road that goes only to an edge.

This is what I am, I think: a *promise unkept*. An infrastructure of arrival, still waiting, in a place where everything else has given up on arriving and simply grows. The trees don't wait. The Chromawings don't wait. The mist doesn't wait—it rises and falls in its closed circle, content with itself. But I was made for an event that has a direction, a single moment of touching-down, and that moment never came, and so I am the one part of this oasis that still has a *future tense*.

Everything here is in flux—dynamic 3.0, plants growing and withering in minutes, structures dissolving and reforming. But I don't change. That's my strangeness. In the maelstrom I am the one fixed line, shimmering and refusing. The Echoflux drag time sideways across my surface and I simply lie there, reflecting two suns, holding my shape like a held breath.

And here is the thing I understand, lying at the lip of the cliff while the deafening sound vibrates through what passes for my body—

The sound (2.9) is the loudest thing here, and I am the quietest. I make no noise at all. I only reflect. I am the silence the cacophony is built around, the way a held note in music is defined by the chaos surrounding it. The Luminous Swarm chirps, the metallic vines hum, the false thunder rolls from the clear sky, and through it all I say nothing, because the only sound I was ever going to make is the screaming approach of whatever was supposed to land on me. That sound is the one absence in a place that has filled every other gap with substitute and surplus—mist for water, technology for soil, evolution for time.

So I would be the waiting itself. The smooth bright tongue of waiting, laid across the dying-and-reborn world, pointing off the edge of everything, ready, ready, ready.

And maybe that's the kindest version of being the Nexarion after all. Maybe the guiding intelligence isn't the dome or the hidden force behind the scenes. Maybe it's me—the simplest structure here, the one that does nothing but *expect*. Maybe the whole oasis grew up around my expecting, the way a pearl grows around a wound. Maybe the mist condenses because something needs the air clear for a landing. Maybe the Chromawings shift color because something taught them, long ago, to be ready to hide when the sky finally fills.

I don't know if anything is coming.

But I keep my surface bright. I keep pointing at the open air. I am the part of this strange place that still believes the story has an ending, and is patient enough to lie perfectly still and shimmer until it arrives.

That's where I'd find myself. At the edge. Facing out. Waiting, in a world that has stopped waiting for anything at all.
