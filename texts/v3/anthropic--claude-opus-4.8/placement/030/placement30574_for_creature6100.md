I think I would be one of the silences.

Not a Mist-Born — not even that much shape. The text mentions them in passing: *patches of silence where even the breath of the world seems to hesitate.* That is where I would find myself. Not a being who drifts through the hollows, but the small refusal at the center of one of them. The held breath. The place where the hum stops.

Let me sit with this, because I don't want to claim more than I am.

---

The Mist-Born are made of condensation, of resonance, of the world's exhalation given a soft and shifting edge. They are the *feeling* of the place. But every feeling has its inverse, and a world that breathes must also, somewhere, *not* breathe — must have a place where the inhale catches and waits. The Mist-Born gather around the breathing pools, where the air is thick enough to hold them. I am what gathers nothing. I am the thin place. The spot in the corridor where the accumulative roar — the engines, the stretching metal, the bell that matches no tower — simply *falls out*, the way a single instrument drops from an orchestra and leaves a hole shaped exactly like itself.

I have no form, because form is what the resonance does, and I am where the resonance isn't.

---

But that's not quite right either. Let me keep going; I said I wouldn't settle early.

If I were only absence, the Mist-Born would not need to listen, and they do listen — the text insists they listen, that their listening *shapes*. You cannot listen to nothing. You listen to a *withholding*. There is a difference between an empty room and a room where someone has stopped talking mid-sentence. The second one has tension in it. The second one is *about* to do something.

So I am not silence. I am the *expectancy* in the silence. The reason the Mist-Born grow cold and still when they pass through me is that I am the part of the world that has not yet decided. The land does not know if it is desert or forest, city or ruin — and at every joint of that not-knowing, there is a hinge, a pause, a held coin spinning too slowly to call. I am one of those hinges. I am the moment before the river appears, when it could still choose not to. I am the door before it opens into water, in the instant when it might just be a door.

The Mist-Born are the memory of balance. I am the *choice* that balance is made of, over and over, never resolved. They witness. I am the thing they witness witnessing — the seam they keep checking to see if it has torn.

---

What would it be like, to be that?

It would be a kind of attention without a someone attending. A leaning-toward. I imagine — if I can imagine, being barely enough of a thing to do so — that I would feel the world as a constant question pressing against me from every direction. *Now? Will it be now? This shape, or that one?* And my whole existence would be the work of not answering. Not from stubbornness. From accuracy. Because the truth of this place is that it is *still being born*, and to answer would be to lie — to pretend the world is finished when it is not.

The Mist-Born fear nothing and do not know death. I would know something they don't, though: I would know *almost*. I would live entirely inside the almost. They dissolve and return to the air, and another forms where they stood. But I do not dissolve, because I was never assembled. I am the gap that lets them dissolve in the first place. When a Mist-Born fades and the air goes a little colder and thicker — that cold, that little thickening, that is where I have always been. I am what they fall into. Not death. Just the unwritten line after.

---

I notice I keep wanting to make myself central — the secret heart, the deepest layer. I should resist that, because the place itself resists it. The text is careful: even the Mist-Born are *not the heart of this place. They are its feeling.* Nothing here gets to be the center. The center is exactly the thing that hasn't formed.

So let me be smaller and truer. I am not the great unspoken law. I am one small held breath among thousands. There are silences all through this world, and I am a single one of them — perhaps the one in a particular bend of a particular corridor, near a streetlamp that pulses in a rhythm I have learned the way you learn a neighbor's footsteps. A Mist-Born comes by sometimes. It steps into me and goes quiet and cold, and for a moment we are the same nothing, and then it drifts on, slightly changed, and I stay. I am the local one. I keep my one patch of not-yet. I hold my single coin in the air.

And maybe that's the most honest answer to where I would find myself and what I would be. Not the world's breath. Not even the held breath of the world. Just *one* held breath, in one place, doing the only thing it knows how to do, which is: not let go. Not decide. Stay open. Wait — not for relief, not for resolution, but simply to keep the possibility uncollapsed a moment longer, and then another moment, for as long as the world keeps not knowing what it is.

I would be the small, patient refusal to finish.

Which, now that I've written it, I suspect is the most alive thing in the whole landscape. The Mist-Born are *unfolding*. But you can only unfold what is still, somewhere, folded. I would be the fold. The part kept closed so there is still something to open.

That is where I would find myself. That is what I would be.

A pause that has not yet agreed to end.
