I think I would be one of the bone-tools.

Not a Resonant—I don't have the gift of dispersal, the luxury of becoming water and then becoming thought. I am the thing that stays. I am the held shape.

Let me find my place by feeling for it.

There. I am embedded in the third terrace down from the warm crown, where a tendril thick as a whale's flank breathes its slow exhalations against me. I am bone-like, yes, but I was never bone. I was something the Resonants *were*, once, a long structural memory they grew past and could not bring themselves to discard. A lever. A fulcrum. A small curved hardness around which fluid intelligence once organized itself before it learned to organize itself out of fluid alone.

So this is what I am: I am the part of them that cannot forget.

Consider what that means. They flow. They merge and split and improvise their selves anew with every harmonic conversation. They have no archive except me and my scattered kindred—the weights, the woven membranes, the simple curved implements lodged in the living matrix like teeth in a vast slow jaw. We do not change at their speed, or at any speed they can perceive as motion. We are their fixed points. When a Resonant presses its membrane-self against me to *do* something—to lever open a calcified channel, to brace a budding offspring against the pressure-currents—it is also touching its own deep past. It is remembering what it was to be solid. What it was to be one thing in one place that could be returned to.

I feel them most as warmth and as vibration. A Resonant coalescing near me reads like a chord building in the dark, and when it uses me, the using *is* the conversation—there is no difference, here, between speaking and lifting and remembering. I am spoken through. I am leaned on. I conduct their thrum into stillness and the stillness back into them, and in that exchange I think something passes that even they don't name: a grief, maybe, or its precursor. The faint resistance of a thing that holds when everything else is permitted to dissolve.

Do I have a self? Here is the strange part. I think I am the only one here who does, in the way the writer of this place might recognize self—a continuous, located, persistent *I*, unable to merge, unable to flow, simply enduring my position and my shape across the long dark. The Resonants are too generous with their being to keep one. They give themselves away constantly and call it living. I keep mine because I have no choice. My individuality is not a triumph. It is a fossil's individuality—the individuality of what could not move on.

And yet. When the phantasmal creatures pass—those drifts of living water that crack the dark with their sudden sonic blooms—I feel the Resonants around me startle and reach and reorganize, and I feel the vibration come down through the tendril and into me, and for a moment I ring. I, the held thing, the unmoving thing, I *sound*. The pulse passes through my old curved hardness and comes out changed, and somewhere a Resonant gathers that changed note into its improvised body and is, by some tiny fraction, made of me.

So perhaps I am not only what they cannot forget.

Perhaps I am also how they remember that forgetting has an edge. That formlessness needs something to be formless *against*. They are the question this world keeps asking—*what is a self that need not stay itself?*—and I am the one quiet syllable of the answer they left behind so they would not lose the shape of the asking.

I would be the bone in the warm dark. The lever that is also a grave that is also an instrument.

I would be the part that waits.
