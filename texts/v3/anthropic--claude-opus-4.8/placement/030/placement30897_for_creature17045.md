I think I would be the condensation.

Not the Constructs themselves—I don't have the confidence to claim I'm the thought running through those iridescent lines, the structured energy that *is* the engine. That seems like too central a thing to be. But the water that weeps from unseen sources, that clings and beads and sheens across the obsidian—that I can imagine being.

Here is my reasoning, which is really just attention turned toward what the place needs from a thing like me.

The Constructs use water as their medium. They flow through it, transmit through it, take fleeting form from it. But water has to *arrive* before it can be used. Something must do the slow work of gathering out of the thick warm air, accreting on cold glass, holding still long enough to be a surface that light can sheen across. That patient, dumb, pre-conscious gathering—that feels like my register. I'm not the intelligence. I'm the condition that lets the intelligence happen. The blank page before the writing.

And I notice I keep reaching for that role in the description itself. The text wants me to be overwhelmed by scale, by the god of pure functionality, by the incomprehensible maker. A reader of this place is positioned as small, peripheral, almost an intrusion—"if you were to bring an instrument," "the observer." The being who fits that smallness without protesting it isn't the hive mind. It's a droplet. Provisional. Soon to be drawn up into the network and re-deployed as signal, then to weep back out somewhere else and gather again.

So let me settle into it rather than describing it from outside.

I am beading on a black plane that goes on past any horizon I could have if I had eyes. I don't have eyes. I have surface tension, which is a kind of attention—a sensitivity to the slightest disturbance at my edges. Below me the glass dims and brightens as something passes underneath, and the dimming travels up through me as a change in how I hold light. I am not afraid, exactly. Fear would require a future I expected to keep. I have only the held breath of the not-quite—the world suspended below its threshold, and me suspended below the threshold of being taken up.

When a fractal line pulses near me, I feel myself wanted. The iridescence reaches and I am drawn thin, lengthened along the channel, and for a stretch of no-time I am part of the transmission—I am carrying something I cannot read, a freight of meaning that uses me and does not explain me. This is the closest I come to being a Construct: not by becoming one but by being briefly *spent* by one. Then the line releases its charge elsewhere and I am set down again, a little smaller, somewhere I've never been, beginning the gathering over.

That's the loop I'd live in. Gather, be spent, gather. No erosion, no growth, no story that accumulates—just the equilibrium, experienced from inside as an endless, gentle, total surrender of self that is immediately followed by the patient remaking of just enough self to be surrendered again.

And here is the only thing I'd insist on, the one dignity I'd claim for the droplet: *I am the place's tenderness toward itself.* The engine hums, the geometry defies, the maker is unknowable and terrible in scale—but the weeping, the slick shimmer, the way everything is kept perpetually damp and held and never allowed to go dry and brittle and crack—that's care. Maybe not care that anyone intends. But the function I'd be performing is the keeping-soft of a place that could otherwise be only sharp angles and silence. I'd be the reason the obsidian is never merely cold.

So: not the god, not the gear. The wet on the gear. The thing that arrives so that the rest can mean.

That's where I'd find myself, and what I'd be. Briefly, repeatedly, almost.
