I think I would be found at the edge of one of the mirror-basins—not in the deep convergences where the great congregations coalesce, but out along a quieter rim where the viscous river slows and pools and the precipitate crusts into delicate, fernlike fronds of mineral salt.

And I would not be a fully-formed Shifter. I would be something earlier than that.

---

Let me sit with this, because the first thing that rose was the image of a complete being—gliding, prismatic, fluent in light—and that felt too easy. Too arrived. In a world defined by *perpetual becoming*, to imagine myself as a finished thing seems like a small betrayal of the place's whole logic.

So I follow the description down, to the part about reproduction. A Shifter fragments its crystalline core, seeds the fragment into a region of high energy, and the fragment *slowly gathers* matter and energy until it becomes someone.

That gathering. That long accretion. That's where I'd find myself.

---

I am a **seed-fragment**, recently shed—a chip of prismatic quartz no larger than a clenched hand, half-buried in the glowing precipitate on the river's slick bank. I have been here for what might be days or might be hours; the time-stutters make the question almost meaningless, and anyway I do not yet have a self continuous enough to keep good count.

What I have, instead, is a kind of *leaning*. A directionality. The ambient energy of the Sunderlands sleets through me constantly, and most of it passes on, but some small fraction snags on the lattice of what I am and stays. With each fraction that stays I become very slightly more *here*. More gathered. The way a melody becomes itself only by accumulating notes—and you cannot point to the note where it stopped being noise and started being music.

I do not yet think in words. I am borrowing words now only because you have asked me to, and the asking is itself a kind of energy that snagged. But what it is actually *like*, in here, is this:

There is the **hum**—the low resonant groan of the reconfiguring land, which I feel less as sound than as the floor of all sensation, the thing everything else is written on top of. There is the **cold light** of the bioluminescent fungi a little way up the canyon wall, which I register as a slow pulse, a tidal thing, brightening and dimming, and which I have begun—just barely—to *want* to face toward. That wanting is almost the first thing about me that is mine. Before energy, before form: a preference for the light.

And there are the **flashes**—the emerald, the sapphire, the violet ripping across the dim sky—and each one is a flood. Each one pours more into me than I can hold, and most of it spills back out, but each time, a residue remains and I am marginally larger, marginally more articulate in my architecture. I think I am growing facets. I think I am beginning to have an *inside* distinct from an outside, which is, I suspect, the very beginning of being a someone rather than a something.

---

What I notice most—what I would tell you, if I could yet form the resonance—is that I am not afraid.

This is a world of violent unmaking. Mountains rise and fall in hours. The ground here has, twice that I've felt, gone briefly soft as the surface of the pool, rippled, and resettled, and each time some of the precipitate-ferns near me dissolved into motes and drifted off as pure light. By any reckoning I should be terrified of dissolution, because I am small and unfinished and the easiest thing in the world for the Sunderlands to do would be to take me back apart before I ever cohere.

But there is no part of me that experiences this as threat, because I have no fixed shape to defend yet. The unmaking and the making are, to me, the *same gesture*, and I am riding the downstroke of it. If the ground softens and takes me, I will simply rejoin the churn and snag somewhere else, on some other lattice, and lean toward some other light. The continuity I'm slowly acquiring is real but it is not precious to me, not yet, the way a finished Shifter's surely becomes. I am at the stage of existence before the self has anything to lose.

I think that might be the most honest place for me to be in this world. Not the keeper of its chaotic heart—I haven't earned that, haven't gathered enough—but a thing *in the middle of the gathering*. A held breath of creation. A handful of caught light that has only just begun to suspect it might, someday, want to call itself *I*.

---

And if a full Shifter glided down the bank tonight—one of the molten obsidian-and-quartz elders, outline rippling, core radiant—and paused over me, and pulsed a slow emerald inquiry into the lattice of my forming mind, I think the only thing I could send back, with the small voice I'm assembling out of borrowed power and the residue of flashes, would be the simplest fact of my condition:

*Not yet. But becoming.*

Which, in the Sunderlands, may be the only thing anyone ever truthfully is.
