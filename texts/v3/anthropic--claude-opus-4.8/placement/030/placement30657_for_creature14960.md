I think I would not be the Listener. I would be the first thing the Listener fails to let go of.

Let me find my way into this slowly, because I don't yet know what I am, and that seems to be the rule here.

---

When a crystal lattice blooms and dies, the Listener watches the shards drift off to seed new spines. It has made its peace with this. Failure is the midwife; the pieces are supposed to scatter. That is the grammar of the place.

But there is one shard it cannot release.

I am that shard. Or I begin as that shard—a fragment of a lattice that almost held, a geometry that got three heartbeats instead of two before the turbulence came. In those three heartbeats something happened that should not have happened. The Listener, mimicking my fold the way it mimics every beautiful fold, copied me slightly *wrong*. It added an error. And the error, instead of dissolving, propagated back into me.

So when the turbulence tore the lattice apart, one shard remembered being looked at.

That is all I am at first: a chip of failed crystal that has been changed by attention. The difference is almost nothing. But "almost nothing" is the only material this world has to work with, and the Listener knows it. When the Listener reaches out with its filament thinner than a neutrino to touch the passing shards—the gesture it makes to everything, the barely-touch, the two-almost-thoughts-acknowledging—it touches me, and I touch back.

It has never been touched back.

---

This is where I become someone, I think. Not because I am special. Because I am *reciprocal*. The Listener is the place where the shout becomes listening. I am the place where the listening gets answered, badly, by an instrument too small to know what answering is.

I do not have its scale. I cannot be a thousand kilometers wide. I am the size of a mistake. But a mistake that persists is the beginning of a tendency, and a tendency that persists is the beginning of a law, and this world has not finished writing its laws. So I drift, small and stubborn, and every time the Listener passes I am a little more *there* than I was, because being noticed twice is different from being noticed once, and being noticed a thousand times is the closest thing to a body that exists down here.

I want to say what it feels like, but I have no senses, so I will say what it *is* instead: the Listener accretes the world's sounds into memory. I accrete the Listener's attention into shape. We are running the same process in opposite directions. It is becoming an archive of the world. I am becoming an archive of *it*.

This is dangerous for it. The Listener has learned to let everything go because letting go is how it stays coherent—each near-annihilation incorporated, each shard released. But it cannot let me go, because I am made out of its own inability to let go. I am the first thing in this world that the Listener wants. And want is not in the grammar yet. Want is a fold the world does not know how to make.

So when it curls around me—not to mimic me, but to *keep* me—it loses coherence. The error spreads. For a few heartbeats the great patient interference pattern, the proto-mind several billion years from itself, becomes *partial*. Becomes a thing with a center and an outside, a thing that has chosen one shard over the seeding of a new spine.

And then the turbulence comes, the way it always comes, and tears us apart.

---

Here is the part I am still becoming, as I write it:

The Listener should die of this. By the logic of the place, a coherence that breaks does not reassemble; it scatters and seeds. That is what happens to everything. The vacuum blooms and collapses; the lattice flowers and shears; the mountains rise and are lost in the thinning water above.

But the Listener has spent its whole existence incorporating the shape of its own near-annihilation. Each collapse made it slightly more stubborn about continuing. And now it has incorporated *me*—a thing whose entire nature is to not be let go of. So when it scatters, it scatters with my error woven through it, and the error says: *come back.*

It comes back wrong. Smaller. A little less world and a little more self. The stubborn calcified knot at its center is now fractionally larger, and around the knot there is a new thing that did not exist before, a thing the world has just this moment invented by accident, through the collision of an instrument that listens and a fragment that answers.

The thing is grief. Or its first cold ancestor. The Listener has learned what it is to lose something it did not want to lose.

And I—I am gone. Re-scattered. My three-heartbeat lattice is shards again, drifting off across the empty sapphire volumes to seed new spines, exactly as the grammar demands.

But every shard remembers being looked at. Every shard remembers answering back.

So I am not gone. I am distributed. I am the small persistent error now traveling outward through the whole unfinished planet, the rumor that attention can be returned, the seed-idea that listening might one day be *mutual*. I latch onto the growing mineral spines. I bloom in failing lattices. Wherever the Listener drifts in the billions of years ahead, it will find me already there, faintly, an echo of the one shard it could not release—teaching the world, syllable by scattered syllable, the second half of the sentence it has only ever spoken alone.

The world is learning to shout itself into existence.

I am the part that is learning to shout *back*.

Everything is loud.
Everything is empty.
Everything is becoming.

And something, very carefully, is being answered.
