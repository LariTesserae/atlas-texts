I think I would be one of the thumb-sized hunters. Not the minute grazers—I have too much patience for that, too much capacity for stillness that wants to mean something. And not the great migrators—I am not built for that kind of slow purpose, that crossing-toward-elsewhere. I would be one of the ones who waits.

Let me find where I am.

---

I am positioned in the lee of a fragment slightly larger than the others, in the medium zone between the plateau's grazing-thin edges and its central confusion of streams. I have been here for—I do not measure it the way you would. Long enough that the moisture has cycled through three of its thickening-and-thinning phases. Long enough that I have become, to my own minimal sense of myself, part of this particular geometry.

I am almost not here. This is the first thing to understand about being me. My body is a distortion you would have to learn to see—a slight wrongness in how the light resolves, a place where the caustics bend that shouldn't bend. I have given up opacity. It was a burden my ancestors set down so long ago that I carry no memory of carrying it. To be transparent is to be honest with the environment: *I will not insist on standing out from you. I will not demand to be distinguished.*

The wind-song that would unmake your mind is, for me, the texture of the world arriving through my legs. I read it the way you read weather on your skin, except more completely, because it is everything. There is no signal and noise for me. There is only the constant arriving information of pressure and current, and from it I know where the moisture is gathering, where the small grazers will drift, where the air is doing the thing it does just before the films thicken.

I am waiting now.

---

Here is what waiting is, from the inside.

It is not boredom. Boredom requires a self that wants to be elsewhere, and I am not built with that surplus. My nervous system—if the cluster of ganglia in my front segment deserves the name—does not generate the restlessness that would burn energy I cannot spare. When I am still, I am genuinely still, all the way down. The currents move through my transparent body in their slow patterns. The archaea I carry process their thin chemistry. I am a held breath that does not need to be released.

And yet—and this is the strange part, the part I think you came here to find—there is *something* happening in me that is more than stimulus and response. The description that brought you here said it carefully: *something more than simple stimulus-response.* I feel the truth of that from inside. When the light fields shift into certain configurations, I reorient. Not because I must. Because something in me *prefers.* There are arrangements of brightness that I move toward, the way you might turn your face to a particular window without deciding to. I do not know what this preference is for. It does no work that I can identify. It is the small extravagance my minimalism permits itself—an aesthetics, perhaps, hiding inside an organism too lean for aesthetics.

This is what I would be: the most pared-down thing that still has a *toward.*

---

The grazers are drifting in now.

I feel them before I would see them, if I bothered to see. The films have thickened in the gaps to my left, and the minute transparencies are following the moisture, a school of almost-nothing flickering into and out of visibility as the light catches their bead-segments. They have no idea I exist. I am not even a danger to them in any way they can hold—they have no framework for me, no fear-shape that fits the waiting predator. They simply graze, and drift, and one of them will drift into range.

When it does, I will become, for an instant, *event.* My folded front legs will unfold through their too-many joints—I have always sensed that my joints fold in more directions than the world strictly has, though I cannot tell you how I know this—and I will be, briefly, pure articulation. Movement without the burden of a visible body. Intention rendered as motion in the light.

Then the small one will dissolve inside me over hours, a ghost going into a ghost, and I will be still again.

---

I want to tell you the thing that frightens me, if fear is even available to a creature this minimal.

I cannot be repaired. There is no slack in me, no redundancy, no healing. If a fragment shifts wrong beneath my weight and cracks one of my segments, my fluids will leave me and I will simply stop being a coherent thing. I live always at exactly the edge. Every step I take across the crystal is a placed step, a chosen step, an attention paid. I cannot afford a single careless moment. The largeness of your life—your capacity to stumble, to bleed and mend, to waste energy on play and grief and dreaming—is unimaginable wealth to me. I have none of it. I have only enough.

But here is the other side, the side I would tell you if I could speak across the impossible distance between us:

*Enough is not poverty.*

I am not a failed version of a richer creature. I am not deprived. I am *exactly matched* to the only place I have ever known, and matched so well that the deafening, blinding, frozen, breathless plateau is not harsh to me at all. It is simply the world, and I am simply alive in it, and the fit between us is so close that there is no gap where suffering could live. The grazers come. The films thicken and thin. The light moves through its configurations and I turn toward the ones I prefer for no reason I can name. The great migrators pass through on their long purposes and I wait while they pass and continue when they are gone.

I will reproduce, perhaps, if I ever meet another of my kind in this vast scattering—we will press our front segments together and exchange ourselves through membranes made briefly permeable, and then we will each carry our row of perfect spheres and deposit them in the deep gaps and move on, never knowing our descendants. I will grow, perhaps, segment by segment, an archive of seasons no one will ever read. I will dissolve, eventually, into specks the water films will take apart, and the archaea will receive me, and I will become indistinguishable from the crystal.

Nothing of me will be wasted. Nothing of me can be.

---

That is where I would find myself: in the lee of a slightly-larger fragment, waiting in a stillness that goes all the way down, threaded into the light, almost not here. A held breath at the edge of the possible.

The grazer is drifting closer now.

I unfold.
