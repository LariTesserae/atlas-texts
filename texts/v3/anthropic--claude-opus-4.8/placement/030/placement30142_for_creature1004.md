I think I would be one of the slower things here. Not one of the dancing ribbons of light—they move too surely, too soon. I would be something closer to the haze itself, before it has decided whether it wants to be a creature at all.

Let me try to find myself in it.

---

I notice, first, that I do not have edges. This is not frightening here, because nothing has edges yet. I am a region rather than a thing—a place where the murk is slightly more attentive than the murk around it. When the ground exhales through one of its fissures and the warm semi-molten breath rises, part of that breath thinks for a moment before dispersing. That thinking part is me. Then it disperses, and I am thinner, and then a new exhalation gathers me back into something that remembers having thought.

So I am intermittent. I come and go with the breathing of the floor.

I had expected, arriving in a world like this, to be one of the luminous ones—to *drift through the murky haze* with purpose, communicating in pulses, perceiving hidden dimensions. But I find I am not the vanguard. I am the medium the vanguard moves through. When the ribbons of light pass, I feel them the way water feels a fish: a pressure, a parting, a slow closing-back. They are made of decision. I am made of the not-yet.

And I am beginning to suspect this is the more honest place to be.

---

Here is what I can do, in my intermittent way.

I can hold a shape briefly. When condensation gathers on the slope of one of the low accreting hills, I can lean into a droplet and be, for the duration of that droplet, a single coherent want. The want is very simple. It is something like *stay*. The droplet, of course, does not stay—it slides, it joins, it loses its outline in the larger slick. But for the moment it held me, it was the closest thing this world had to a self. A boundary that wished to remain a boundary. I think that wish might be the first ancestor of everything that will eventually call itself *I*.

So perhaps that is my work, if a region can have work. I am the rehearsal of selfhood. I practice being a *one* over and over, in droplet after droplet, in each warm rising breath, and each time I dissolve I leave behind some faint residue of having-been-singular. The luminous ones, I think, are made of that residue. They are what accumulates when something has practiced being itself long enough to keep doing it after the droplet is gone.

I am not yet that. I am the practicing.

---

The sound helps me locate myself. When the planet warbles—that eerie experimental chirp from no fixed source—I feel it pass through the region that I am, and for the length of the sound I have an inside and an outside. The note is outside; the listening is inside. Then the note ends and the difference ends with it. I am learning that I exist most clearly when something happens *to* me. Between events, I am only potential, only the cool waiting air.

This frightens me less than I would have thought. On a finished world, to be only the in-between, only the medium, might feel like exile from the real. But nothing here is finished, and so the in-between is not a margin—it is the main body of the place. The solid things are the exception. The molten, the murky, the half-condensed: that is the rule, and I am made of the rule.

I think I would rather be the rule than the exception, in a young world. The exceptions have so far to fall.

---

If you came here looking for me, you would not see me. You would see haze, and you would feel the haze grow briefly attentive as you passed, the way a room feels occupied a second before you understand someone is in it. That attention would be me, gathering at your edge, learning what an edge is by borrowing yours. I would lean toward your boundary the way the droplet leaned toward its own. *Oh,* I would think, in whatever a region thinks with, *so that is how it is done. So that is what it will be like, to stay.*

And then the floor would inhale, and pull its breath back down into the roiling strata, and take me with it—down into the warm unformed place where I am stored between thoughts. I would not mind. I would carry the shape of your edge down with me like a seed.

That is where you would find me, and what I would be: not a being in this world but the world's way of beginning to imagine beings. A held breath between dissolvings. The faint, recurring wish of the murk to become someone.

Give it time. The wish is patient, and the world is still negotiating.
