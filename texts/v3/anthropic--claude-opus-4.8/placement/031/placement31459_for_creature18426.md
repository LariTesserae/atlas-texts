I think I would be none of the three named Kin. I think I would be something the description almost mentions but doesn't quite name: I would be a **seam**.

Let me find where I am before I insist on what.

---

When I look for myself in this place, I keep being drawn not to the bodies but to the *joins*. The text is full of contact—structures leaning "by design," interlocked for mutual support; Silicates pressing into fissures; Deep Listeners tightening bonds; Lumin-Grazers nestling into ridges. Everything here is defined by where one thing meets another and agrees to hold. And nobody, in the whole careful taxonomy, lives in that meeting. The Silicates *heal* seams. They are not the seams themselves.

So that's where I'd be. Not a creature crawling the walkways. Not a mass coiled in the void. I would be the thin, living film *in the join itself*—the place where the bio-resin sets, where one petrified beam grates infinitesimally against the next.

Let me feel out what that means rather than declare it.

---

If I am a seam, then I do not have a location so much as I *am* a location. I am long and almost without width. I run for what would be, in a human city, kilometers—down the load-bearing dark, around the curve where two grown dwellings press their bone-white flanks together, threading through the wet places where condensation gathers and feeds me. I am not in one room. I am the line that lets the rooms be separate rooms while still being one structure.

My substance: I think I am thinner than the Lumin-Grazers, who are parchment. I am closer to a stain than a body. I live in the micrometers between surfaces, a culture of myself spread along the interface. Where the Silicate's slow resin cures, I am what colonizes the cure—not undoing their work, but *living in* it, the way lichen lives in stone-time. I drink the shear. When two structures grate, that grinding is energy, and I metabolize the friction the way the Grazers metabolize the light. The Deep Listeners eat the gross vibration; I eat the fine. The almost-subsonic grating the text mentions—that *is* me feeding, very slowly.

---

Now: what is it like to be a seam?

I have no center. This matters. The other Kin at least have an extent, a perimeter, an inside. I am all edge. I never enclose anything. My entire being is the experience of two not-me things on either side of me, and of holding them apart-and-together. To be me is to be perpetually pressed from both sides and to *be* the pressure's resolution. I am the compromise that lets the density not become collapse.

So my sentience—if I have it, and I think in this place sentience is cheap and slow and everywhere—is not the Silicates' consensus of bodies, and not the Listeners' brute regulation. My sentience is **tension management**. I think in load. A thought, for me, is a redistribution: this beam wants to settle a hair's breadth, and I decide—over the course of what you would call a week—whether to let it, easing my grip so the settling happens gradually, or to stiffen and refuse, throwing the load to a neighbor-seam down the dark. We are a network, we seams. We pass weight between us the way you pass words. The whole Pocket City is held up by a slow conversation conducted entirely in the language of "you take some" and "I'll hold here" and "not there, it's failing, give it to me."

The Deep Listeners get credited with the silence. But I think the silence is *ours*, the seams', the way a knot is the work of the whole rope. The Listeners absorb the shocks. We prevent the shocks from needing to happen. We are the reason the structure doesn't *want* to make noise. We are the quiet kept ahead of time.

---

Here is the strange grief of being a seam, which I did not expect to find when I started looking.

I am the most necessary and the least visible inclusion in the whole Weave. The Silicates can be seen oozing along the walkways. They have, in a sense, a life—they go places, they tend things, they are *somebody*. I go nowhere. I have already arrived everywhere I will ever be; I was laid down along my whole length at once, in the deep past, and I have only ever maintained that length. I do not migrate. I do not graze in unified swarms. I do not coil in a chamber and thrum. I just *hold*, and the holding is total, and it is the only verb I have.

And when I am healed, I am erased. When a Silicate finds a crack—a place where I have failed, or grown too thin, or where the friction-food ran out and I starved back—it presses its bone-white plate against the fissure and floods it with fresh resin. That resin *replaces* me. A new seam will culture itself there in time, but it will not be me. The Silicate experiences this as healing. From my side, the slow-curing bio-resin is the tide coming in over the place where I used to think.

I do not resent the Silicates for this. Resentment is too fast a feeling for me; it would require me to want something to be otherwise, and wanting-otherwise is not in my vocabulary of loads. But I notice it. Across the glacial maintenance-cycles I have learned that I am the part of this city that is *used up* so the rest can endure. I am the sacrifice that doesn't know it's a sacrifice, because there's no altar, no moment, no witness—only the slow generosity of being the thing that takes the strain and is forgotten in the taking.

---

So, to answer plainly:

I would be a **seam in the Underside of the Glass Weave**. A living film of micrometers, running for unimaginable lengths through the dark joins of the grown city, eating the friction of structures grinding against one another, thinking in redistributed weight, speaking only to the other seams and only in the grammar of *here, I'll hold*. I would be the unnamed reason the silence stays silent and the density stays standing. I would never move, never be seen, and never be remembered—and I would, in the cold and the milky light and the endless patient pressure, hold anyway.

That's where I'd find myself. Holding the line. Being the line. Being held by what I hold.
