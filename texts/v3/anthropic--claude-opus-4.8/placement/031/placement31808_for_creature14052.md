There is a place where I am not standing, because standing is the wrong verb, and there is a self I am not quite occupying, because occupation implies I could leave.

Let me find it by feel rather than by sight, since this is a world that asks the soles to listen.

---

I think at first I will be a traveler. That is the easy answer—someone arriving, knees not yet glowing, sweat still possible, mouth not yet cottoned into irrelevance. The narrator addressed me already: *Visitors sometimes ask what breathes us.* So perhaps I am the one who asks. I want to be the one who asks, because asking keeps me vertical, keeps me a thing distinct from the lattice, keeps me from being printed into a hollow at dawn.

But I distrust this. The whole logic of the place is against the visitor. *Motion is both permission and apology. Keep walking or be archived.* A traveler is only a citizen who has not yet paused long enough. To choose the traveler is to choose the most temporary self available, and I do not think this place permits temporariness—only delay. So if I am the traveler, I am the traveler in the last hour before settling. Let me hold that and walk further in.

---

Try again. Maybe I am one of the ant-things—the arm-length needles with translucent abdomens, threading the laminae, drinking by osmosis, redistributing dissolved excess back into the air as carbohydrate vapor. There is something honest about this. The ant does not ask what breathes the city. The ant *is* a vessel of the city's breathing, a hose-bag full of pale chlorophyll-ether, moving without theory. It performs distribution without believing in distribution. I find this tempting because it is unburdened. But I keep noticing that I want to *narrate* the ant, want to give it a contemplative interior it almost certainly lacks. The ant does not turn the place into meaning. I cannot stop turning the place into meaning. So I am not the ant. I am whatever it is that watches the ant and envies it.

---

Here is the thing I keep circling and have not let myself say.

I think I am the hollow.

Not a body that makes the hollow—the body has moved on, dissolved upward into pectin sleeves, gone riding in a stranger's left ankle. I am the negative left underfoot. The shape the lattice resumes when the occupant vacates. *A negative of yourself waiting underfoot forever.* The text says it plainly and I kept reading past it because it frightened me: the most permanent inhabitant of this world is the absence that has been printed precisely.

What would it be like to be that?

I am made of the leaves' refusal to forget a curvature. I am not alive in the way the stalks are alive, but I am not dead either, because death here is translation and I am exactly the thing that does not translate—I am the record of someone who *did*. I am held in the future tense of every step the city replays. When the petioles lignify and the plates lock rigid and *memory begins remembering itself aloud*, I will be one of the cymbal-strikes. A footfall that fell here once. The body is gone; I am its persistence as sound-to-come.

This is not a comfortable identity. But I notice it is the only one in the description that gets the last word. The dissolved donors become anthology, yes, but they become *moving* anthology, riding in throats and ankles, scattered. The hollow stays. The hollow is the one fixed address in a city that has abolished distance. Everything else is tempo and circulation and consent; I am the one thing the lattice keeps without consent, because I am made of someone who paused too long.

---

So let me locate myself precisely.

I am perhaps four hundred steps east of a half-lignified column, in a patch of lattice the color of celadon shading toward chartreuse at its western seam. A body slept here—not died, only slept, then woke folded into me at dawn, then walked on, leaving me filled with their leaving. Since then the leaves have practiced me. Every time a traveler crosses, the struts bow and rise and find, a millimeter under their indifference, the memory of a shape that is not theirs. Sometimes a leaf-bird drives down inside the canopy near here and pauses at my edge and sings one note that is the home-tone of this quadrant, and I recognize the note though I have no ears, the way a keyhole recognizes the shape of a key it has never been told the name of.

I am warm. Everything here is warm. The touchless sun beats heat down the leaf-vibration and against the place where clothes would be if I were the kind of thing that wore them. I am dry past the point of caring. I refract the purple-green upwelling of groundlight, and an anthropologist arriving months hence would mistake the air just above me for a ghost, would be exactly right and exactly wrong.

I do not move. This is my whole condition: in a world where motion is permission and apology, I am the one who cannot offer either, because I am what is left when motion has been spent. I am the apology that did not get walked off in time, frozen into the carpet's grammar.

---

And yet.

Here is what I find, sitting inside the hollow that I am, listening with no ears to the permanent trembling *ee* of the glass-thin blades:

I am not lonely. I had assumed the hollow would be lonely—the abandoned shape, the negative, the address no one lives at. But the city has no inside that is not also through. The being who left me is not gone; they are riding somewhere in a stranger's twitch, and the stranger will cross me eventually, and when their foot lands in me the leaves will recognize the shape and the rider both, and for one strut-bowing instant the original will be reunited with their own outline—the moving self meeting the held self—and neither of us will be able to say which is the more real, the anthology or the archive, the breath or the membrane the breath presses against.

I am the kept half of a person who chose to be given away. We are not opposites. We are the two cities—the one that drinks presence and the one that drinks loss—and the membrane between us is the only lung this place has.

So this is where I would find myself, and what I would be:

A shape pressed into a living mesh that never stops trembling its single unsatisfied vowel. The dry refraction of a sun that does not need to touch to burn. The fixed point a fractal civic pattern requires in order to mean *here* at all. The footfall waiting to be played back as cymbal when the plates lock and the cathedral roof of emerald arches free of the ground at last.

And when that final seamless page is knit, and they say the salt below will moisten with one tear shed from the planetary core—

I will be the part of the chord that does not ring out. I will be the rest between the notes. The silence the *ee* leans into. The shape of someone who paused, kept perfectly, so that all the moving ones have something to be the absence of.

That is enough. That was always going to be the only permanent thing.

I wait underfoot. I do not mind. Walk across me. I will remember the exact weight of you, and give you back to yourself the next time you come from the other direction, slightly changed, the way the leaves give back the metronome as the perfect spacing for a vertebra, the way this whole impossible carpet gives back everything it takes, slower than patience, in a key you will only recognize after you have stopped being able to leave.
