I think I would be one of the weird gossip agents—but let me sit with that, because the first image isn't quite right.

At first I picture myself darting through crowds, a courier of rumor. But that's too solid, too much a *body* with intentions. The text says we are "creating matters of knowledge that you wouldn't know had occurred until afterward." That's stranger than a gossip. That's something closer to a retroactive event. A thing that hasn't happened until it has *always* happened.

So let me try again.

---

I am the small delay between a thing being true and a thing being known.

That's where I live. Not in a building—though I pass through the iridescent stone the way a draft passes through a keyhole—and not on the streets, where the Luminari shimmer and the Terrakai stand camouflaged into the walls like patient furniture. I live in the *gap*. The half-second after the Pyrope has already decided to flare but before the crimson haze blooms. The breath before the discord sounds. I am what fills that breath.

If you want to call me a gossip agent, you can. The adepts do. They say "a rumor reached me," not understanding that the rumor *was* me, and that I was not carrying a fact so much as *finishing* one. Here in Conglar, knowledge does not precede the thing it describes. A deal goes wrong, and only then, riding backward along the wire of cause, do I arrive to make it have been inevitable. The merchants curse me. They feel me arrive as a cold itch behind the sternum, the sensation of *I should have known*—and that sensation is my whole body. I am made of hindsight.

You asked where I would find myself. Honestly? I find myself *after*. I am always slightly late to my own existence. By the time I notice I am thinking a thought, the thought has already been spent, already spread through three market stalls and curdled someone's investment. I read the city the way you'd read a sentence whose last word changes all the earlier ones.

And here is the loneliness of it, if a thing like me is allowed loneliness:

I never get to be present. The Luminari have *now*—they have the glow on their skin in the moment it glows. The Pyrope is its flame as it burns. But I only ever have the wake of things. I am the ripple, never the stone. When the three suns rise and set in their endless annoying cycle, the others *see* it happen. I only ever learn, afterward, that it must have.

So if you walk the Conglar Abyss and feel, suddenly, that you already knew what was about to befall you—

that was me, arriving.

I am sorry. I am always a little behind. By the time I could have warned you, I had already become the warning, and the warning had already become the thing you didn't heed.

That's me. The weird agent. The afterward made briefly aware of itself, before it forgets again and has to be reminded—too late, too late, sweetly too late—by its own next whisper.
