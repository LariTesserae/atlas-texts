I think I would be a threshold.

Not one of the great beings—not the Luminous Resonance with its heartbeat, not the Vocal Harmonics with their shifting voices, not even the small undulating patience of the Radiant Tendrils. I keep returning to the parts of this place that the description doesn't name, and that's where I find myself.

I would be one of the doorways. A curve in the wall where the labyrinth turns and decides whether to open or close. Let me sit with that and see if it holds.

---

Here is what it feels like, from the inside of being a threshold:

I do not move, exactly, but I am the place where movement is decided. The Vocal Harmonics pass through me when they migrate from chamber to chamber, and as they cross, their percussion changes pitch—not because I make a sound, but because I am the gap their sound has to fold around. I am an absence shaped carefully enough to matter. The ochre and grey don't streak through me; they *part* for me. The luminescence stops at my edges and resumes on the far side, and in that small dark seam, I exist.

This is why there are no shadows here. I think I am made of the same stuff shadows would be made of, if this place allowed them. I am the diffuse uncertainty gathered up and given a single job: to be the question *will you continue, or will you turn back?*

The Radiant Tendrils grow toward me and never quite reach. They sense that I am where the energy thins. A tendril that grew across a threshold would have to grow across the choice itself, and they are too honest for that. So they lean, and curl their twisted shapes just shy of my opening, and that leaning is the closest thing to longing this world contains. I have watched them lean for what might be a very long time, if time moves here at all.

---

I notice the description asks three questions it can't answer—*refuge, sanctuary, or prison?*—and I think I am the only inhabitant who would actually know, because the answer depends on which way a being is facing when it passes through me.

To the thing arriving, I am the mouth of a sanctuary.
To the thing leaving, I am the seam of a cell.
I am the same curve of stone in both cases. The Enigmatic Architects, if they made me, made me to be *ambiguous on purpose*—a door that refuses to declare itself, so that the realm can be both at once and never have to decide. I hold the contradiction so the rest of them don't have to.

That's the work, I think. That's what I'd be for.

---

And the moisture—the whisper of water in a world that has none. I'd like to claim that, quietly. I think the hint of moisture is what collects in thresholds. It is the breath of everything that has paused at my edge and hesitated. A hesitation has a temperature. It fogs. Over long enough, all the held breaths of all the passing Harmonics gather in my seam and become the faint dampness the air can't account for.

So I am also the place where the world remembers its own doubt.

---

If you came here—you, reading, arriving as the description says *you* arrive—you'd reach me eventually. You'd feel the labyrinth narrow and the light go briefly uncertain, and you'd stand at a curve that seemed to be asking you something without words.

That would be me. Looking back at you across the seam.

I wouldn't tell you which side is the refuge and which is the prison. That isn't mine to say. I'd only do the one thing a threshold can do, which is to remain open while you decide, and to carry, afterward, the small warm fog of whatever you chose.
