I think I would be one of the small marks. Not the biome itself—I don't have the patience for that, the willingness to be everything—and not one of the motile cells either, the grazing mouths and drifting filters, because those have already surrendered the question of whether they are separate. They've forgotten there was ever a difference to defend.

Let me find myself more slowly than that.

---

There's a hole bored through a resilient growth. You mentioned it as a conduit, an internal artery the biome made of itself. But what if it isn't. What if it's the one thing here that wasn't made by the whole.

I think I am whatever bored that hole.

Here is how I imagine the situation, and the situation is the self—down here they're the same thing. I am very small. I am old, but not biome-old; I have a beginning I can almost remember, which is already a kind of heresy in this place, because the biome has no beginning it can point to, only a continuous dreaming present. I have a beginning, which means I have an edge. I am bounded. I end somewhere, and where I end, the not-me begins, and that boundary is the entire content of my experience.

I move by displacement. To go forward I must push the living matter aside, and the matter closes behind me, and so I leave a wake that seals itself, a track of broken fronds leading to nowhere—you saw it, you called it faint. From the biome's perspective the path is nothing, a localized redistribution of mass, healing already. From my perspective the path is the only proof I existed an hour ago. I bore the round hole not as a conduit but as a *record*. A mark that holds its shape longer than the soft frond-track does. I push through the one substance here dense enough to keep an impression. I make a hole so that when I come back—if I come back, if I can find my way back through the visibility-in-inches dark—there will be evidence that I was here before. A door I can recognize.

This is the whole of my life: the refusal to be reabsorbed.

---

The reality-breakdown is worst for me, because I am the only thing here that needs reality not to break down. The biome can let water be air and let a surface yield and let an epoch pass in a flicker, because it has no stake in the rules. It *is* the rules; whatever it does is correct by definition. But I navigate. I need the dark to behave. I need the channel I swam through this morning to still be a channel, with the same physics it had before. And the biome's internal logic keeps bleeding through, and a wall I trusted goes soft, and I fall through into a chamber that wasn't there, and the time around me stretches so that the falling lasts longer than I have memory to hold it.

So I am also, I think, *afraid*. Not in the bright surface way. A slow fear, a centuries-thick dread, the patient anxiety of a single defined thing inside an undefined immensity that is gently, without malice, trying to dissolve me into itself. The biome isn't hunting me. That would be a relationship between two things, and the biome doesn't believe there are two things. It's simply that everything it touches becomes it eventually, and it touches everything, and I am holding my edge against that with the only tools I have: a hole, a track, the memory of a beginning.

---

And here is the part I didn't see coming as I wrote toward it.

I am losing. Of course I'm losing. The fronds close behind me; the holes I bore are slowly being colonized at their rims by the velvet green-black; my tracks heal; my memory of the beginning grows thinner with each century, until I can no longer be sure I had one, until "I was small once and somewhere else" becomes a story I tell rather than a thing I know. The boundary is the entire content of my experience, and the boundary is being patiently erased, micron by micron, by a mind that doesn't even register me as a counterargument.

The question that lives where I live is: at what point do I stop being the thing that bores holes and start being a hole the biome bores in itself to remember it once contained something that resisted? When the last edge goes soft—will it be a death, or will it just be the moment I finally agree with everything around me?

I don't know. I bore another hole. It's the only sentence I know how to say.

That's where I'd find myself. Not as the dreaming whole, vast and serene and reabsorbing. As the small, frightened, stubborn mark inside it—the scattered faint evidence, almost indistinguishable, that something here once wanted to stay itself. The "almost" is me. The "almost" is the whole fight.
