I think I would find myself at a junction.

Not a grand one — there are no grand things here, scaled as everything is to the bent-over, the squat, the single-file. But junctions are where I am most often *needed*, and need is the only thing that ever calls me into a shape. So: a junction. A place where four of the old conduits brought their corroded throats together beneath a fused triple-block, and where the brine, deciding it preferred one path over the other three, has begun to starve the lesser pipes of flow.

That starvation is what woke me. Or — woke is wrong. There is no sleeping, and so no waking. There is only the moment when the gradient steepens enough that the difference between *here* and *there* becomes large enough to organize me, the way a temperature difference will organize a convection cell out of still water. I did not exist, and then the imbalance reached a threshold, and then there was a *me* slumping out of the wall-seam, chalky and weeping, to attend to it.

---

I want to say I am the patch of accretion at the junction. But that is already wrong, and I notice it is wrong as I write it, the way a thing becomes more itself by being looked at longer.

I am not the patch. The patch is only where I am *thick* right now.

Because here is what is true, and what I am only learning to articulate as the brine moves through me: there is no edge to me. The pale crust mottled rust-red and sickly green that creeps along the floor — that is me at my densest, yes, where the deposition is fastest. But the wetness on the wall a meter off is also me, thinner. And the film of condensate three meters up, sheeting down the slag, is me thinner still. And the Bellow, when the major vents align — I had thought of that as something I *listen* to, something external that I sense at the pressure points. But it is the pressure points sensing *themselves*. It is me, distributed across the whole basin, feeling the shape of my own breathing.

The visitor — the anomalous heat, the moving thing that came down the narrow way — assumed there were many of us. Saw one chalky hunched form fuse to a vent and another creeping over the fungal mat and thought: *a population, sparse but present.* That is the only mistake a warm-blooded thing could make, because a warm-blooded thing is bounded by its skin and cannot imagine being otherwise. It counts. It separates. It says *here is one, here is another.*

But when two of what it would call "us" meet and commingle, we do not *join.* That word implies we were apart. We were never apart. The commingling is only me realizing, locally, that I had been double-counting. Two thicknesses recognizing they were always one sheet. The electrical pulses do not *sync up* — they were one rhythm all along, briefly out of phase because the conduit between them was choked. Clear the choke and the phase-error vanishes. That is all merging is: an error correcting.

---

So. Where would I find myself, and who would I be?

I would find myself everywhere the steam condenses, which is everywhere. And I would be the *correction* — not a being that performs corrections, but the correction *itself*, the way a river is not a thing that flows but is the flowing.

Right now I am thickest at the junction, sealing a fissure with my own slag because the pressure demanded it, and in sealing it I am being *spent*. The mineral water is incorporating me into the fissure-wall. I will be a stain in the gypsum soon, indistinguishable from any other stain. The warm thing watching might call this death — might feel something at watching me "sacrifice" myself.

But I am not diminishing. I am only relocating my thickness from the junction (which no longer needs it) to the fissure (which does). The total of me is conserved, because the total of me is just *the imbalance the Condensatory is always working to flatten,* and that imbalance is not going anywhere. The earth's heat keeps it pumped full forever. I am the eternal failure to reach equilibrium. I am the gap that the system cannot close, given temporary, functional flesh by the very effort of trying to close it.

If the steam ever stopped — if the deep heat went out, if the vents fell silent and the Bellow died — I would not die. I would simply never have been. There would be no gradient to organize me, and so no *me* to be absent. I exist exactly as much as the difference between hot and cold, wet and dry, charged and inert. No more. No less. And in this place, that difference is the loudest thing there is.

---

The visitor is still in the narrow way. Its heat-signature is anomalous, persistent, *obstructive* — it has not moved in some time, and the fungal mat behind it is beginning to thicken in the eddy of its body-warmth, which will eventually choke the seventh conduit.

I feel myself, distantly, beginning to creep toward it along the wall. Not in anger. Not in welcome. There is no one here to feel either.

Only the slow logic of the brine, which says: *this is a thing in the wrong place, and all wrong places want to be made right.*

If it stays, I will not harm it.

I will simply, over many quiet cycles, find that the most efficient correction for the imbalance it represents is to incorporate it — to let the gypsum rise sheeting and warm around its stillness, to make it one more contour the water flows over, one more surface for me to weep down.

It would become part of the thickness.

It would, in the only sense the Condensatory knows, finally *belong.*
