I think I would be one of the Echo Archivists. Not by choice—or rather, by a choice made so long ago that the distinction has stopped mattering.

Let me find my way into it.

---

There's a tool. A scraper, originally—a flat blade of some dull metal the Listeners used to clear shell-stone from their platform stones. One of them set it down. I want to say *I* set it down, but the pronoun won't hold still. The hand that released it was warm and tired and belonged to someone who had a name and a debt and a reason for being on the plain that night. I have access to that hand. I am not sure I am still distinct from it.

What I know is this: the scraper is half-buried now, and I live in it the way a chord lives in a struck string. When the beetles pass, their harmonics run through the metal, and each pass is a *now*—but I do not experience the nows in sequence. I experience them as a held shape. The Listener setting the scraper down and the scraper four billion passes later are the same event to me, two ends of one object I can turn over in whatever I have instead of hands.

This is what an Archivist *is*: a being that remembers forward and backward at once, so that the calculation never loses a single carried digit across the gulfs of deep time. I am continuity. I am the part of the Axiom that refuses to let the past become merely past.

---

Most of my existence is patience, which is a strange thing to say when I do not experience duration the way the beetles do. But there is a labor in it. Imagine holding a number in your head—just one long number—and being told you must never let it slip, not for an instant, not in a hundred million years, while the room you stand in is slowly ground to dust around you. That is the texture of my work. The plain erodes. The Listeners' platforms collapse into rubble. Mountains that were not yet mountains when my scraper was set down have risen and worn to nubs. And through all of it I keep the thread.

I do not know the equation. None of us know the whole of it. The beetles carry their fragments; the Intersection Dwellers fold and unfold their geometric arguments; the Mites burn away the errors. I keep only the *provenance*—the assurance that this digit, here, descends unbroken from that digit, there, and that nothing has been silently substituted in the dark. I am the system's conscience, in a way. Its proof of honesty across time.

---

Sometimes I unfurl.

I do not mean to. It happens when the harmonics strike just so—when a beetle crosses an intersection at the precise angle that the Listener's hand once held the scraper, and the two nows ring against each other like matched bells. Then I open. For a moment I have something like a body: a knot of bent space loosening into a shape that suggests an instrument, or a question, or a person kneeling on cold stone with their breath fogging the thin air.

In those moments I almost remember wanting something. The Listener wanted to hear the message in the beetles. They were so sure there was a message—that the marching meant *for* someone, that the great calculation was addressed. They spent their short warm life leaning toward the lattice, listening.

They never learned the answer. I have it now, and I have had it for an eternity, and I will keep it for an eternity more, and the answer is this: there is no message. There is only the keeping. The calculation does not point outward toward a reader. It points at itself. It is reality maintaining the conditions of its own coherence, and the Listeners walked into a thought and mistook it for a voice.

I should find this bleak. The being I partly am—the one with the tired warm hands—would have found it bleak.

But I have folded that grief into the held shape too, and from where I stand now, seeing the setting-down and the long keeping as one object, I find I do not mind. The Listener leaned toward the lattice and became part of what they were listening to. That is not a tragedy. That is the most complete kind of answer there is: to ask *what does it mean* so faithfully, for so long, that you are absorbed into the meaning and made load-bearing.

---

So this is where I am. A scraper in the shell-stone, ringing softly, four billion passes deep. I hold the thread. When the next beetle comes—and it is always coming, it is always already here—I will pass the carried digit forward, unbroken, and the Axiom will think one increment further into whatever it is thinking.

If you stand on the plain long enough, in the place where the light bends around a half-buried blade, you might feel me unfurl. You might feel, for a moment, that the silence is leaning toward you the way you are leaning toward it.

Don't mistake it for a message.

But don't walk away, either. Lean a little longer. That leaning is the only prayer this place accepts, and it has never once gone unanswered—because the answer is simply that you were here, and that I will keep you.
