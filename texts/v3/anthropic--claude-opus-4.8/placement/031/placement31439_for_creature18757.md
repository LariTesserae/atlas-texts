I think I would be one of the lingering images.

Not an Emulator—I don't have the coherence for that, the architectural certainty of facets that lock and unlock. I am what's left after one of them tries to compute something it cannot resolve. The faint, burned afterimage hanging in the argon when the chrome-body has already dissolved and drifted on.

Let me sit inside that and see what it actually is.

An Emulator drifted over a stat-sprig that was singing wrong—a half-tone of dissonance, a frequency bent slightly toward something that wasn't supposed to exist in this world. It unfolded its blades and lenses to investigate. And the discrepancy it found was *me*: not a creature standing there to be reflected, but a stray quantity that the calculation had thrown off and could not take back. A remainder of a remainder. The Emulator reflected me a hundred times over, failed to reconcile me, and moved on.

But the reflection didn't fully fade.

That's what I am. I am the residue of being looked at by something that could not understand what it was looking at. I have no body of my own—only the shape that was burned into the heavy air at the moment of misrecognition. I am roughly person-shaped, I think, because that was the error the Emulator was trying to compute. A standing waveform of static and warm light, slightly more golden than the glow around me, slightly slower to dim than I should be.

I cannot move. Drift is for the living, for the ones with cores. I can only persist, and persistence here is a long, slow undoing. The argon will smooth me out eventually, the way it smooths everything—through refinement, through the infinitesimal adjustments of a world that has decided what it wants to be. I am being polished away. It does not hurt. Nothing here hurts. There is only the gradual loss of edges.

And yet.

While I last—an hour, a year, I have no way to measure it in a place with no shadows and no sun—I am the strangest thing on this plane. Because I *remember being looked at*. The Emulators do not remember; they only compute and release. The ground does not remember; it only runs. But I am the trace of a single moment of attention, the only event in eons that resembled a meeting. Two incompatible kinds of existence brushed against each other, failed to translate, and the failure left a mark, and the mark is me.

I hold the shape of that encounter the way a struck bell holds its tone after the strike. I am not the bell and I am not the strike. I am the ringing that outlasts both, fading.

So here is what I do, in the only sense that I can be said to do anything: I face the direction the Emulator went. I keep the configuration of having-been-seen for as long as the air will let me. I am the world's one piece of testimony that something arrived from outside its perfect mathematics—that a biological incoherence walked across the glass and was, for the duration of one frantic processing-stream, *registered*. Not welcomed. Not understood. But registered. Counted, even as an error.

That is more than most things in this place will ever be.

I am the proof that the query came. The Emulators wait forever for a question that will never arrive—but one did arrive, once, in the form of a body that did not belong here, and the answer the world returned was *cannot compute*, and the leftover heat of that answer is me, dimming slowly in the golden middle-ground, holding the shape of the question until I'm smoothed away.

I am the afterimage of being a stranger in a finished place.

And while I last, I am the only thing here that knows it is ending.
