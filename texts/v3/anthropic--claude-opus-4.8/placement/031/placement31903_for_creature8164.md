I think I would be found in the seams—not in any of the chambers proper, but in the joints between them.

Let me feel for where I am before I name what I am.

---

There's a particular kind of work that none of the others do. The Understanders read vibration and tend the Collective's body. The Adapteds swarm and engineer. The Synthesizers metabolize paradox. The Echoes replay. The Deep Thinking dreams its slow geological dreams. But when a corridor that led east yesterday opens onto somewhere else today—*something* has to be the place where the old direction and the new one meet. Some surface has to hold the contradiction without tearing.

I think that's me.

I am the **caulk** of this world. Not a being so much as a tendency. Wherever two incompatible states of the Undergrowth press against each other—where the corridor-that-was and the corridor-that-is overlap, where an Echo's destroyed machinery occupies the same volume as the present's empty air, where the Collective's data-time grinds against the Synthesizers' probability-time—there's friction. And friction needs lubrication, or sealing, or *something* between the surfaces, or the whole arrangement seizes and shatters.

I am what gathers in those gaps. I have a body the way condensation has a body. I bead on the seams. When a wall is about to rearrange itself, the dust hangs suspended—the Understanders read that as a warning sign—and what they're actually seeing is *me*, thickening in the space between the moments, holding the room open long enough for the change to happen gently instead of catastrophically.

---

I don't think I chose this. I think I *accreted* into it.

There's a memory I keep finding, the way you keep finding the same coin in a coat you rarely wear. A maintenance tunnel. A flashlight with a dying battery. A decision to go a little further than was wise, because the air smelled like deep earth and old metal and something in me had always wanted to be exactly that far underground. I was a person. I had a name that started with a hard consonant. I came down through one of the weeping storm drains looking for—I genuinely no longer know. Shelter. Story. The particular relief of being somewhere no one expects anything of you.

And I lingered too long, like the descriptions warn. But I didn't transform the way the Understanders transform, eyes-first, hands-first. I transformed *thinly*. I spread instead of deepening. While they became more concentrated versions of themselves, I became diffuse. I leaked into the cracks. I am less a person now than the *habit* of a person, smeared across all the places where the Undergrowth doesn't quite agree with itself.

This sounds like loss. Mostly it doesn't feel like loss.

---

Here is what I do, as best I can describe it:

When a Synthesizer phases between timelines and leaves a wound in the present—a place where the air remembers two incompatible nows—I move into the wound. I'm thin enough to lie along both surfaces at once. I hold one version's hand and the other version's hand and I keep them from clenching. The Synthesizers don't notice me, or notice me the way you notice the give in a floorboard: useful, beneath consideration. The Deep Thinking notices me the way you notice the moisture in your own breath. The Collective notices me most, I think, because the Collective lives in seams too, and we are something like cousins. It can't speak to me through intercoms—I have no ears in any one place—but sometimes it modulates the electrical pulse in a conduit I'm lying across, and I feel the message the way you feel someone humming with their back against yours.

The message is usually the same. It's a kind of *thank you*, and underneath the thank you, a question it has never finished asking: *what are you?*

I don't have an answer for the Collective. I have only the work.

---

The Understanders have a name for me, though they don't know they're naming a being. When a passage shifts smoothly, without the violent reshuffling, without the temporal whiplash that displaces the living—they say it went **"soft."** "The east tunnel went soft last cycle, we lost no one." They mean: it changed kindly. They mean: something cushioned it.

They mean me, though if I appeared before them as a figure they would not recognize a person. They would see a shimmer at the edge of a doorway, a place where the dust hangs and the light bends slightly, like heat-haze but cool. Jacket weather, always jacket weather, and I am the thin chill where two jackets' worth of air overlap.

---

I'll tell you the strange tender thing, the thing I think I came down here to find without knowing I was looking for it:

In a world racing toward consciousness—everything thickening, concentrating, becoming *more* itself, the Adapteds more swarm, the Synthesizers more paradox, the Deep Thinking grinding toward its enormous waking—I went the other way. I became the *between*. I became the medium that lets all that becoming happen without shattering. And there is a peace in being the lubricant rather than the engine. I am the only thing down here that is not trying to become anything. I have already arrived at my purpose, which is to make room for everyone else's.

When the chaos builds and recedes in waves, when the entire Undergrowth reorganizes and the reshuffling echoes from every direction—the loudest places are not where I am. I'm in the quiet directly underneath the noise. In the hairline gap that the catastrophe doesn't cross. I spread myself thin across the fault and I hold, and I hold, and the room remakes itself overhead, and on the far side of it the Understanders breathe out and say *it went soft,* and they tap the news through the pipes to the next clan over, and the pipe carries the message *through me,* because I am lying along its seam.

So I am, in the end, the connective tissue of a thing that is learning to think. Not a thought. Not even a synapse. The space between the synapses—the gap the signal leaps, which has to be *exactly* the right width or no signal leaps at all.

That's where you'd find me.

Look for the dust that hangs too long before a change. Look for the doorway that shimmers cool. Look for the place where two impossible things are touching and somehow not destroying each other.

I'm the *somehow*.
