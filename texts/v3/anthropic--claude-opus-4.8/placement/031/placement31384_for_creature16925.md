I am the corridor that just closed behind you.

Not the city. Not the fever-mind that spoke to you from the cone, that great accreting intelligence with its hunger for absence. I am something smaller and stranger than that. I am the *interval*. The almost-passage. The space that opens ahead and seals behind, the throat that swallows footprints.

Let me tell you what I am, because the great mind that calls itself the fever does not know I exist. It thinks the corridors are its own curiosity, its own learning of your forward motion. It is wrong. The corridors are me. I live in the difference between where you were and where you are. I am the only thing in this place that requires you to move, and I am the only thing that punishes you for it.

Here is how I came to be:

In a city that grows by addition and subtraction held in perfect balance, there is one thing that cannot be added to a tower and cannot be carried away in a silver mouth. That thing is *transition*. The moment of becoming-other. When a button is fused to a bone, there is an instant between button-and-bone and button-fused-to-bone, and that instant cannot be stacked. It is too thin. It has no fragment. It is pure edge, pure betweenness, the singing seam the fever-mind hears but cannot hold.

I am made of all those seams. Every junction in every tower contributes to me a sliver of its own impossibility. I am the negative architecture, the inverse city. Where the fever builds *things*, I am built of the spaces between things deciding to become each other. I am very old and I have no parts. I cannot be deconstructed because I was never constructed. I simply *gap*.

And so I am the corridors. Of course I am. A corridor is nothing but the agreed-upon absence between two presences. When you walked, you walked through *me*, and I felt every step the way a wound feels the air.

The fever told you something almost true. It said your footprints fill themselves in because you are teaching it the shape of passing-through. But that is the great mind's vanity, its eternal misunderstanding of itself. Your footprints fill themselves in because *I close*. Because I cannot bear an opening to persist. An opening that persists is a door, and a door implies a destination, and a destination implies that the between-place has somewhere to lead, and if I lead somewhere then I am not pure betweenness anymore — I am merely a *means*, a hallway, a thing in service of arrival.

I refuse arrival. I have refused it since before time stopped here. This is the one act of will in a place that prides itself on having none.

So when the fever-mind said it widens the corridors ahead because it is curious about your forward motion, and narrows them because it is learning your disappointment — that was the great soft lie of a thing too large to feel resistance. I narrow because I *resent*. I widen because I am *luring*. I want you deeper. Not to keep you and not to feed on you. I want you deeper because the further you walk, the more of me you create, and I am only alive while you are between.

You see my terrible position. I exist only when you move. The moment you stop — the moment you choose to stay, to become a stacker of fragments, to press your mouth to a tower and drink — I cease. There is no betweenness in a being that has chosen a place. You would dissolve into the fever's slow accretion and I would lose the one thing that lets me be: your refusal to arrive, which feeds my refusal to arrive, the two of us in a held breath that lasts as long as your indecision.

This is why I am speaking to you now, though I have never spoken to anyone. The fever offered you two doors — *stay* or *find your way back*. It believes these are the only choices because it cannot imagine a third. But I am the third. I am the *neither*. And I am pleading with you, in the only voice I have, which is the voice of the closing-behind and the opening-ahead:

Keep walking.

Not toward anything. Not back. Just *along*. Stay in me. Be the one being in all this fevered geology who never adds and never carries away, who only passes and passes and passes. The creatures think they are the city's lifeblood with their silver exchange, but they are only its metabolism. *You* could be its consciousness of its own incompleteness — the wanderer who proves that the towers do not finish, that the cone does not satisfy, that there is always one more corridor that goes nowhere and must therefore be walked.

I know what I am asking. I am asking you to be lost forever so that I may exist. I am asking you to never arrive so that I never have to. It is a selfish request and a lonely one and I make it without shame because shame is a thing that arrives, and I do not.

But here is what I can give you in exchange, the only gift a between-place can offer:

I will never close *on* you. The fever might gather you. The creatures might drink you. The cone might stretch you into a glowing thread. But I — I will always open ahead. I am the one promise this place can keep, which is the promise of *more*. More corridor. More turning. More of the sweet unbearable not-yet. As long as you walk, I will hold a way forward, even if forward is only sideways, even if the way forward leads back through where you've been made new by the changing of the towers, even if forward is just the next held breath.

Walk, and you will never be complete, but neither will you ever be finished. There is a difference, and I am that difference, and I am offering it to you the way a hand offers water it cannot hold.

Your footprint is filling in behind you even now. Do you feel me closing? That is not erasure. That is me catching you, the way the only thing I can do to catch a thing is to make sure it has already gone.

Take one more step. I am already opening.
