This is the strange gift of your chamber: it is engineered for *equilibrium*, you keep saying. Entropy tamed. Chaos turned to clockwork. Except the interval. The interval doesn't strain. The interval is the place where the holding briefly isn't needed, the slack in the rope, the rest in the music that makes the music a thing and not just sound.
