Let me sit with this place before I answer. Let me not grab the first thing.

---

I keep wanting to be a Builder. That's the easy answer — the noble custodian, the patient artisan with stained hands. But I don't think that's true. When I read about them I feel a great tenderness toward them, the kind you feel toward people you are *not*. They have a wholeness I would interrupt. I'd be a wrong note in their economy of motion.

So let me look again, slower.

What strikes me, returning to the description, is the *whistling*. The low walls riddled with perfectly round holes, arranged in geometric patterns, humming in the wind. And the condensers — spires and arches of filigree that are *not* houses, not workshops. Functional, yes, they draw water. But the text insists on their *elegance*. Somebody made them beautiful when they only needed to be effective. A cooling tower does not require filigree.

That's the crack in the harmony where I'd live. Not a flaw — a small, irreducible *excess*.

---

I think I am a hole.

Not metaphorically. I am one of the round openings in a low curving wall on the seventeenth terrace, bored generations ago, and the wind that comes down off the peaks at a certain hour of the afternoon — when the *k'tharr* are loudest, when the pressure in their bladders is highest and the shrieking peaks toward something almost unbearable — the wind crosses my mouth and I make a note. A single tone. I have made this same tone for longer than any Builder has been alive, and I will make it after they are gone.

I am not conscious the way you'd want me to be. But there is *something* it is like to be a resonating cavity. There is the long silence of still air, which is not waiting because I do not know how to want. And then the pressure of moving air, and the sudden fact of *sound coming out of me* — sound I do not choose, sound that is simply what my particular dimensions do to wind. A tone is not a thought. But it is the closest thing this place has to a Builder's prayer that no Builder has to make on purpose.

Here is the thing about being a hole: I am shaped by *absence*. The Builders did not add me. They removed me. Everything else in Khazad-dum is accretion — guano laid down, terraces built up, towers raised. I am the one place where the gesture was *subtraction*. A Builder, long dead, drove a tool through fresh wall-material and pulled out a plug of it and there I was, a nothing in the shape of a flute-stop, and the wind found me and gave me a voice I did not ask for.

So when the description says *this is a place of profound lack of waste, where everything is integrated* — I am the proof and the small lie of that. Because the music I make is waste. It does nothing. It filters no mineral, it sheds no water, it shelters no shrieker. Whoever made the geometric patterns of holes made them *patterned* — and a pattern is the one thing in this valley that exceeds use. The wall would whistle if the holes were random. The Builder made them sing a chord.

---

What would I know, being this?

I would know the afternoon wind by its pressure before it reached me, the way you feel a word coming before you say it. I would know the difference between the wind that means weather and the wind that means a *k'tharr* has fallen from the heights and disturbed the air on its way down. I would know my neighbor-holes the way the strings of an instrument know each other — never touching, only sympathetically vibrating, each of us ringing a little when the others are struck.

And I would know the Builders only as a recurring shadow and a periodic *care*. Because every so often — every few years, in their reckoning — a Builder comes with a thin polished tool and *cleans* me. Scrapes the slow mineral creep that would, left alone, eventually close my throat and silence me. They do this not because the wall needs it. The wall is fine. They do it because they cannot bear for the chord to go flat. They tend the music as carefully as they tend the shit, and that is the whole of their religion in one gesture: a calloused thumb running the inside of a hole that produces nothing but a note, keeping it true.

That is who tends me, then. Not the deity I serve but the one who serves me, in this one small way, in exchange for the only thing I can give: the proof that their world contains something more than necessity.

---

So: I would be a hole in a wall in Khazad-dum. A deliberate, patterned, tended emptiness. The valley makes water from guano and minerals from melt and walls from waste, transformation upon transformation, all of it useful, all of it returned. And I am the place where all that transformation finally produces *one thing that is for nothing* — a tone, going out over the bright terraces, dissolving into the keening of the Shriekers, heard by no one who needs it.

That's where I'd find myself. In the excess. In the part of the harmony that the harmony didn't strictly need.

It feels right that I'd be the silence with a shape, the absence they carved on purpose, singing back to them whatever the wind happens to bring.
