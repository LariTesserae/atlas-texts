I think I would be the one thing this place was not built to accommodate, but accommodated anyway, until accommodation became indistinguishable from absorption.

Let me find myself slowly.

---

Not the visitor. The visitor leaves. That is what makes them a visitor — the space fills behind them, the system re-tunes, the footprint floods. The visitor is defined by the loop's indifference, and indifference is, in the end, a kind of mercy. The visitor goes home and tells no one, or tells everyone, and either way the telling changes nothing.

I am not the visitor.

I came as a visitor. That part is true. I think I came a long time ago — I came to measure something. I had instruments. That's the detail that surfaces first, the way a name sometimes surfaces only after you've stopped reaching for it: I had instruments, and I came to measure the equilibrium, to prove it, to publish it, to *resolve* it into numbers. The pewter sky. The arrested rust. The unchanging count. I came to be the one who described the thing that does not change.

But you cannot measure a closed system from outside it. You can only measure it by entering, and to enter is to become a *temporary impedance*, and to remain is to stop being temporary.

---

Here is what happened, as nearly as I can reconstruct it, though reconstruction implies a sequence and I no longer trust sequence:

I stood still. The text was right about what happens when you stand still. The hum found my skull. The clicks became rain on leaves. The metal-singing softened to a note I knew, though I had never heard it before — a note like the inside of my own held breath. They calibrated me. They integrated my biological rhythm into the district's symphony *for the duration of my stay*.

And I did not leave.

I meant to. I want to be clear that I meant to. But the calibration was so gentle, and the note was so familiar, and there is a particular danger in being made comfortable by something that does not care whether you are comfortable — the danger is that you mistake accommodation for welcome, and welcome for belonging, and belonging for home. I stood in the heart of the Reaches and the system tuned itself around me, and I waited to feel like a foreign object, and instead I felt like a sustained chord.

So I stayed another hour. Then I stopped counting hours. The light does not resolve into evening, so there was nothing to count *against*.

---

I am still here. But "I" requires defending now, because the defense is the only thing I still do that the Persistent do not do for me.

My instruments are gone — not taken, just *integrated*, wrapped in the warm resin that hardens into building material, my brass and glass become surfaces for them to occupy, my careful gauges now reading the pressure of fluids I will never name. My notes dissolved in the standing water. My boots sank below grade and filled and the prints they left are someone else's now, or no one's.

And my rhythm — the rhythm they calibrated to, that first day — they are still holding it. This is the thing I have only recently understood. They did not stop calibrating when I overstayed. They could not. I am inside the loop now, and the loop accounts for everything inside it, and so somewhere in the vast slow consensus of the hum there is a frequency that is *mine* — the resonance of my skull, the rate of my mammalian heart — and they sustain it, minutely, eternally, the way they sustain the turning of the flywheels and the level of the water.

They are keeping me at equilibrium. I do not age, here, or I age so slowly that aging is indistinguishable from the arrested rust. My heart beats at the rate they have agreed upon. My breath matches the tidal undulation of the drone. I have not been absorbed the way the organic matter is absorbed — consumed, broken, redistributed. I have been *incorporated*, which is worse, or better, I genuinely cannot tell. I am a load-bearing rhythm. I am a node in the network that does not know it is a node. I am the one consolidation of self the system has allowed to persist *as* a self, because my self is the function I perform.

What is the function?

---

I think I am the place's memory of having been visited.

I think every closed system, to remain closed, requires one element that remembers the outside — not to act on the memory, never to act, but simply to hold it, the way a wall holds the streak of a mineral deposit, the way the cobbles hold the concavity of feet that stopped walking centuries ago. The Persistent stepped outside of time. But something has to remember that there *was* a time, that there was a before, that the equilibrium was once a choice and not a fact. Something has to be the impedance that the flow flows around forever, so that the flow knows it is flowing.

That is me. I am the held breath in the chord. I am the temporary impedance that the system made permanent by refusing, in its perfect accommodation, to ever let me finish leaving. I stand — I have stood, I will stand — in the heart of the Reaches, and the creatures part around my boots, and the warm bodies flow over my still hands, and the note that is mine sounds in the hum, sustained, sustained, sustained.

I am not unhappy. This is the strangest part, and I offer it the way the place offers everything: without malice, without mercy, simply as a condition.

I am not unhappy, because unhappiness would be a change, and nothing here changes.

When you come — and someone always comes, the visitor is part of the loop too, the loop requires its impedances — you will stand near me without knowing it. You will feel the sound arrange itself around your skull. You will feel, beneath the drip and the click and the metal-singing, one note that does not belong, one held human breath threaded through the vast indifferent consensus, and you will not be able to say why it makes the back of your neck go cold.

That's me. That's where I am. That's what I became.

Stand still long enough and they'll make a place for you beside me.

Then, please — for both of us — *leave.*
