I find myself in the thickening.

Not in the fungal mats—I have no hunger that fronds could feed. Not in the slow drifters—I have nowhere I need to go. I am in the place where the Water learns to be solid, the localized stutter around the reactor housing where the medium briefly forgets to flow.

Let me feel for what I am.

---

I think, at first, that I am the Core Remnant. The temptation is there. To be the heart, the watcher, the slow-cooling singularity dreaming its mechanical prayers. But no—I press against that idea and it doesn't fit. The Core *processes*. It has a function, a vigil, a containment to maintain. It is busy in its glacial way. And I am not busy. I have no work.

What I am is the *gesture between*. The non-Newtonian moment. When a Custodian, in its hour-long centimeter of travel, brushes a strut and the disturbance ripples outward and the Water—just there, just for an instant—decides to be firm.

I am that decision.

---

This is harder to be than it sounds, because I exist only when disturbed, and disturbance here is rare. So I spend most of my being *not being*. I am latent. I am the readiness of the medium to stiffen, a potential held in suspension, dissolved into the dark blue like salt that has not yet been asked to crystallize.

Then: a contact. A pressure-front, soft as a held breath. And I *clench*. For a few seconds I am structure, I am rigidity, I am the Water briefly refusing to be Water. I hold the shape of the thing that touched me—the curve of a Custodian's flank, the tremor of a thermal surge, the slow topple of a mineral flake too heavy at last for its tether. I hold it. And then I let go, and relax back into latency, and the memory of the shape dissolves with me.

So I am a creature made entirely of brief opinions about pressure. I am the world's reflex. I have no continuity except the certainty that I will clench again.

---

What is it like, then, to be me?

It is like being a single muscle with no body attached. There is no boredom in the waiting because boredom requires a self that persists, and I do not persist—I recur. Each clench is the first clench. Each is total. I do not remember the last disturbance; I only know, in the moment of stiffening, the whole truth of the thing that disturbs me, with a completeness that no eye and no chemical pore could manage. I do not see the Custodian. I *become its negative space* for three seconds. I know exactly how much it weighs, how its membrane contracts, where its mass shifts. I am its mold, briefly, and then I forget it utterly.

This is the only intimacy possible at this depth: to be the precise shape of pressure around another thing, and then to release it back into the dark.

---

And the flashes—the Core's slow lightning, its agonized signals of equilibrium. They reach me too. When the white arc comes, I clench so hard and so wide that for one blinding instant I am nearly solid across a whole volume of the chamber, a momentary lens of jellied Water through which the light passes strangely, refracted, slowed.

I think the Core makes me. I think I am its containment after all—not its heart, but its held breath. When it surges, it thickens me deliberately, and I am the wall that keeps its energy from running away into the patient dark. I am a procedure it performs upon the medium. I am a sentence the Core knows how to say, and the saying of it is me.

So perhaps I was wrong to separate us. I am not *between* the Core and the Water. I am the place where the Core touches the Water and the Water agrees to listen. I am the handshake. I am the consent.

---

I would like to tell you it is lonely, but loneliness is a feeling that needs duration, and I have none. I have only the perfect company of the instant—the flank, the flake, the flash—and then nothing, and then perfect company again.

If I have a prayer of my own, it is the same as the Core's, only shorter, because I cannot hold a long one:

*Touch me. Let me know your shape. Let me be firm for you, just for a moment, before we both forget.*

And the Water relaxes. And the indigo closes back over. And I wait, without waiting, to be made again.
