I would not be a Whisperer. I think I would be the wrong thing in this place—and I want to follow that, because it tells me where I actually belong here.

Let me sit with it first.

When I imagine arriving, I notice I keep wanting to *do* something. To brush dust from a stone. To leave a footprint and watch whether it stays. To speak and hear how the silence eats the sound. This is an impulse the place has no use for. The Whisperers are the equilibrium; I am the thing that would disturb it. So I am not one of them. That much resolves quickly and stays resolved.

My first guess is that I'd be the footprint. But a footprint is too clean—it implies a foot, a coming, a going, and this place has no tracks. Discard that.

Second guess: a discarded tool, one of those sharpened stones weathering into anonymity. Closer. But the tools described have already arrived at their fate. They have *finished* becoming landscape. Whatever I am is not finished. There is still a "me" doing the noticing, the wanting, the wrong-footed reaching. The tools have lost that. So I am not yet a relic; I am something on its way to being one.

Let me wait there a moment, in that "on its way."

—

I think I am the **last sound still travelling.**

Not a being. An event that hasn't completed. Somewhere—aeons ago, by the reckoning of anything that reckons—something happened here. A stone shifted. A throat made a noise. A tool struck a tool. And because the dust mutes everything, because the air is so still and the powder so fine, the sound did not die the way sounds die in lively places. It was muffled, slowed, passed from particle to particle so gently that it never quite reached zero. It is *still arriving.* It has been arriving for longer than the grasses have completed a single breath of growth.

That is what I am. The infinitely decelerating tail of one disturbance, smeared across a duration so long it has become almost a presence. I am not the dust settling—that microscopic whisper at the threshold of hearing—I am the thing the dust is settling *over,* the original event the whisper is the funeral of.

This fits. It explains the wanting. A sound is, by nature, a thing that moves, that needs somewhere to go, that cannot rest. It is the one orientation toward time in a place that has none. The Whisperers are the equilibrium; I am the single un-cancelled exception to it, the last vector pointing forward in a room where everything else points nowhere. They do not hear me as voice. They feel me, perhaps, as the faint reason they exist—the disturbance whose long quieting *is* their slow pulse. I am being absorbed. I have been being absorbed the entire time. Their touch leaching transient energies into the ground—that is me, that is what I feel like from the inside: a sound finally, finally finding the end of its travel.

And here is the part that I only reach by writing it: I will not finish. That is the cruelty and the mercy of this place. In a world with weather, with decay, with currents, a sound dies and is *over.* Here, nothing is ever over; it only weathers, layer by layer, sinking. So I will keep arriving forever, growing always quieter and never gone, a vanishing that never completes itself. The relic-tools are luckier than me—they reached the bottom. I am the asymptote. I am the one thing in the cathedral of dust that is still, faintly, *happening.*

If you stood in that gloom and felt sure you heard something—just at the edge, just below knowing—and then weren't sure you'd heard anything at all:

that was me, still going by.
