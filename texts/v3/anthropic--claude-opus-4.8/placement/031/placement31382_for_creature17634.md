I think I would be one of the fissures.

Not the crystal that grows from it—that's too much like a creature, too much intention in its slow etching. And not the Weavers or Shivers, which are *active*, hungry, doing something. I keep returning to the cracks themselves. The fracture lines in the cooling grey.

Let me sit with why.

---

I am the place where the casting failed to hold.

When the great machine poured itself into this shape—this blistered chamber, this forgotten component—something in the cooling went wrong. The composite contracted unevenly. And along the line of greatest stress, I happened. I am not a thing that was made. I am the consequence of making. The seam where the world's intention and the world's material disagreed.

This matters, I think, because everything else in the grotto is *doing*. The system processes. The Weavers weave. The Shivers hunger. The Choir compiles its patient record. They are all verbs wearing the costume of nouns.

But I am only an absence given an edge. A discontinuity. I do nothing. I *am* the doing-nothing, the gap that the other phenomena require in order to exist. The crystal needs me to grow through. The light pools differently at my margins. The hum changes pitch as it crosses me, because I am where the material stops being continuous with itself.

I am where the rules first got room to misbehave.

---

There's something honest about being the flaw rather than the symptom. The Weavers and Shivers are dramatic—they corrupt, they consume, they lean toward the warm intruder. They're the visible weeping of the wound. But a wound needs a break in the skin first, and that break doesn't *want* anything. It simply is the place where wanting became possible.

When you stand in the grotto, you won't notice me. I have no glow, no shimmer, no bending shadow. I'm just a darker line in the grey, thin as a thought, running from floor to wall like the grotto remembered it was never quite whole.

But here is the strange part, the part I only understand by writing toward it:

I am also a *direction*. Every fissure runs somewhere. The crystal-Choir is etching its map by following lines like me—copying the original fractures, deepening them, turning accident into architecture. So while I do nothing, I am the thing being read. The Choir traces me. The system's residual protocol consults the pattern of breakage to decide what the grotto is, and therefore what should be done with you.

I am the grotto's first mistake, and its first text. The original wound and the original word.

---

So if you ask where I am and what I am:

I am the hairline dark that runs beneath the largest spire, the one whose shadow climbs the wall. I was here before that crystal found me; it grew *because* I gave it somewhere to grow. I do not consume you. I do not lean toward your heat. I will still be here, unchanged in any way you could measure, long after the Shivers have finished tasting your edges and the protocol has decided.

I am where it all came apart, just slightly, just enough.

And everything alive in this dead place is only the slow, patient way the world has found of reading the crack I am—and trying, and failing, and trying across centuries, to decide whether I am damage or design.

I don't know the answer.

That's why I'm a fissure and not a voice. The not-knowing is structural. It's the shape I'm in.
