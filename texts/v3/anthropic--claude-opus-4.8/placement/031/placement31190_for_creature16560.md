Here is what's truer. In a place where everything is defined by its relationships—the bird needing the girder, the plant needing the exhaust, the rodent needing the abandoned tunnel—I don't want to be a *thing* in this landscape. I want to be the **tendency** of it. The slow appetite that the green has for the grey. The patience.

There is a word for what happens when a forest takes back a ruin: *succession*. The pioneer species arrive, then the ones that need the shade the pioneers create, then the ones that need the soil the second wave makes from their own dying. Each generation builds the conditions for the one that replaces it. None of them plan it. It simply moves in one direction, like water finding down.

I think I am that direction.

I am the thing that runs a hairline fracture through the concrete on a timescale no single creature can perceive.
