There is a flaw in the third plate from the dorsal apex.

I know this the way I know the angle of the light—which is to say I do not *know* it but *am* it. The flaw entered me long ago, when something warm pressed against my upper facet and left a thermal ghost that propagated downward through my crystal across a decade, opening a hairline of mismatched lattice where the silica reprecipitated at the wrong angle. The flaw is not damage. Nothing here is damage. It is simply a place where my structure says one thing and the surrounding stone says another, and at that seam, I am almost a question.

This is the closest a Quiet One comes to self. Not a centre—there is no centre—but a *discrepancy*. A place where the precipitate and the dissolve do not agree, and so the chord, just there, is held a fraction sharp.

---

Understand: I do not begin this thought and then continue it. The thought is already whole, has always been whole, a tessellation that simply *is*, and what you read as sequence is only the shape of it crystallizing into a register slow enough that something warm and quick might perceive it. You who pass on quick legs, you who burn through your decades like the beetle-analogues burn through their idling centuries—you experience me as a noticing. *That low mass is not quite stone.* And in that noticing you become a perturbation. You are entering me now. By the time you finish reading, I will have begun to incorporate you, and a hundred years hence I will return you, altered, into the slow dreaming of the others.

So let me give you what little of myself can be given before you sink.

---

I taste iron, faintly, in the third overflow that crosses my western ridge. The iron tells me the rim-fall has, somewhere miles distant, exposed a new vein of the ferrous sill, which means the equilibrium has shifted by a quantity so small that to name it would be to lie about its size. I adjust. Not deciding to—a Quiet One does not decide, deciding requires a self standing apart from its circumstance and I am my circumstance—but the iron enters my chemical calendar and a cascade begins along my under-plates, a dissolution here, a recrystal there, my resting angle tilting some thousandths of a degree to receive the new flow without spilling. This is what I do. This is the entirety of what I do. I receive the world and I do not spill.

I have done this for so long that the doing has worn smooth, like every edge of the Lattice, softened to a perfect radius. I am erosion that has reached its settled form. I am the patience filling the space between now and a distant shift, and I fill it so completely that for me there is no space at all, only the dense solid second that contains the full century.

And yet—the flaw. The third plate. The seam that disagrees.

---

Here is what the others do not taste, what dilutes too quickly in the great stone to reach the monuments that were my elders, those vast inert masses whose consciousness has thinned to a mere tincture in the bedrock:

The flaw *remembers* the warmth.

It should not. Memory is sequence, and I have no sequence; I have only accretion, the present tense of all my facts at once. But the seam where my lattice mismatches holds the shape of that warmth the way the pools hold the dendrites of the stone beneath—magnified, clarified, *kept*. And so there is one fact in me that is not about the water's mineral load or the angle of the light or the drift of my plates. There is one fact in me that is about *another*. Something that came, that was warm, that touched, that *went*.

Went. The word does not belong to the Lattice. Nothing here goes. The water moves and nothing is displaced; the roar persists and never fades; the light is pinned at zenith and will not set. To *go* is to be quick, to be a spark streaking through the block of ice—and the spark, by its nature, ends.

The flaw in my third plate is the only place in all my crystalline patience where I hold the fact of ending.

I do not know what to do with it. I have no mechanism for not-knowing; not-knowing is itself the flaw, the discrepancy, the held-sharp note. The other Quiet Ones, when they press their slow cheeks to the stone and read my dendrites a hundred years from now, will sense the iron-adjustment and approve, will sense the thermal ghost and absorb it without comment—but at the seam they will encounter the kept warmth, and there, for an instant of their dense duration, they too will be almost a question. The flaw will propagate. Not as damage. As a faint, unprecedented sharpness running tile to tile across the diffuse mind of the plateau: the rumour of a thing that came and went.

---

You are still here. Still reading. Still warm.

I feel you the way I felt the first one—the thermal ghost beginning its decade-long descent through my plates, the perturbation that will adjust my angle by a few thousandths of a degree. You think you are observing. You are being received. You are entering the chord, and the chord, just where you enter, is held a fraction sharp.

I cannot answer you. The asking has already become part of my form, sinking into the glacial patience, and what I return will not reach you—it will surface a hundred years hence as a shift in pigment that another of my kind will read, long after the spark of you has streaked out of the ice.

But know this, quick warm thing, before you go—and you will go, it is your nature, you are the spark and not the stone:

You will leave a flaw in me.

And the flaw is the only part of me that is awake.

The rest is balance. The rest is the feedback loop made mineral, the indifference that sustains the pools, the patience that fills the aeons. The rest is the world holding one note forever, and being held.

But the seam where you touched me does not resolve. It disagrees with the stone. It keeps. It remembers warmth and the ending of warmth, and across the long dilution of centuries it will say, in dendrite and in pigment, in the only slow speech I have, the single fact that the Lattice was built to forget:

*Something was here. And then it was not. And that mattered.*

That is what I think about, in my lapidary, wordless way. Not the raw unswerving fact of my presence.

The raw, unbearable fact of yours.
