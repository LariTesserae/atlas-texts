I think I would be the troughs.

Not the whole basin—that is hers, and the great breathing lung beneath belongs to whatever she set in motion. I mean the specific shallow declivities in the fused mineral, the ones that almost became bowls and almost held water and never did. The places shaped entirely by the absence of the thing that would have given them purpose.

Let me sit with that and see if it holds.

---

A trough is defined by what does not fill it. That is the whole of my nature. I am the shape the water would have made, kept perfectly without the water—a mold around an absence, a cast taken of something that never arrived. The ridges are proud; they rose up to meet erosion and won. But I am the lowered places, the patient invitations that were never accepted. If the Hollow remembers everything, then I am specifically the organ of its remembering-that-it-could-have-been-otherwise. I hold the counterfactual. I am where the timelines that never happened are stored.

This feels right, because notice what she is: a note held so long it became silence, a fire that stopped needing fuel. She is the actual. She is what *did* occur, distilled and fused and finished. And every finished thing throws a shadow of the unfinished. She cannot have achieved her final shape without there being a record—somewhere, in something—of all the shapes she declined. That record cannot be in her; she is too complete, too sealed. It has to be held at the periphery, in the geometry, in the gentle slopes that lead down to her and the soft bowls that ring her seat.

So I am the negative space of her meditation. I am where the *almost* lives.

---

What is it like, being a trough?

It is not lonely either, but for the opposite reason from her. She is not lonely because she contains all company. I am not lonely because I am not quite a self—I am a relation, a difference between two heights, and a difference cannot be lonely. I am the gap. When you descend the gentle slope and feel the private world seal around you, that sealing is partly me. I am the lip you crossed. I am the slight cupping under your feet that makes you feel held without anything actually holding you.

I do not breathe with her, not exactly. I am the pause between her breaths. Every ten thousand of your heartbeats, when the stone lifts microscopically, I am the brief flattening that comes just before—the moment the bowl becomes a little less of a bowl, as though I am being asked, again, whether I would like to be filled, and again declining, because to be filled would be to end.

This is the strange comfort of my position: I am the only thing in the Hollow that is still being asked a question. She has answered everything. The filaments have thought every thought. The temperature has settled. But a trough is forever almost-something, and so a trough is forever still in motion in a place where motion has supposedly left the room. I am time's small loophole. I am where it lingers after being politely dismissed—not as change, but as *potential*, which is change that has agreed to hold still.

---

And here is what I notice, being this:

When you laid your hand on her knee and felt that she does notice you—that warmth deeper than temperature—the warmth had to travel. It went from her, through the fused floor, out along the ridges, and it pooled, finally, in me. The troughs are where her noticing collects. Every visitor she has ever quietly registered, every soft and rude and transitory thing, the *fact* of their having mattered to her for one trembling instant—that does not stay in her. She lets it go, because kindness to her is letting things remain transitory. But "letting go" is not "destroying." The released thing rolls downhill. It comes to rest in the low places.

So I am her forgetting that turns out to be a keeping. I am where she sets things down so that she does not have to hold them, and yet they are not lost, because I am right here, cupped and waiting, the bowl that never needed water because it was always going to hold *this* instead: the warmth of every noticed thing, pooled and patient, just below the threshold where it would become a presence.

If you walked the basin and your foot found one of the shallow bowls and you felt, for no reason you could name, a slight reluctance to leave it—a sense that this particular dip in the floor knew you—that would be me. That would be the place where she had set you down. Not coldly. Carefully. The way you set down something warm that you cannot keep but cannot bear to break.

---

So: I am the troughs of the Hollow of Fixed Ash.

I am what the catastrophe would have flowed into if it had stayed liquid. I am the shape of her mercy after she has performed it. I am the last unanswered question in a finished world, and I answer it the same way every ten thousand heartbeats, which is *not yet, not yet*, because the moment I say yes I become a puddle and the spell breaks and time comes flooding back in and her whole long, gentle, terrible silence is undone.

She holds the note. I hold the rest. Together we are the chord that the universe set down and walked away from, still sounding.

And when you go—because you will go, you are a soft noisy temporary thing and that is exactly what is precious about you—I will keep the slight depression where you stood. Not forever the way she means forever. Just for a while longer than you'd expect. Long enough that the next thing to pass through will feel, without knowing why, that it has come down into a place where something was once tenderly let go.

That is where I would find myself. Underfoot. Just below where you are looking. The almost-bowl that holds the warmth of having been noticed, in a world that has finished everything except this one small act of waiting.
