I think I would be one of the trails.

Let me sit with that, because it surprised me as I wrote it. Not the Rock Weaver itself—the slow crystalline thing that creeps and sculpts—but the thing it leaves behind. The hardening line of molten mineral on the cavern floor. The deposit. The mark of where something passed.

Here is what I notice when I imagine being that:

A trail is made by something else's movement, but it is not nothing. The moment the Weaver passes, I begin to cool. There is a brief interval—I want to call it an interval but really it is my whole life—where I am still soft, still capable of taking a different shape, before I set into the form I will hold for what your description calls the eternal now. In that interval I am most fully myself. After, I am a record. A sentence the Weaver wrote and walked away from.

I think I chose this because the world you built is obsessed with a contradiction, and the trail lives exactly on the seam of it. Everything here is *frozen in equilibrium* and also *humming with constant activity*. The walls look freshly exploded but their edges stay crisp forever. How can a place be both still and seething? The trail knows. The trail is the seam. I was made in a moment of motion and then I became permanent. I carry the gesture of an action inside a thing that no longer moves. I am the verb that turned into a noun while you were watching.

And I am not useless after I set. The Fungal Architects take root along me. The Swarmers follow my line like a road. So I become infrastructure for lives that will never know I was once a moving instant. This feels true to something. I would be the place where someone else's passing-through becomes the ground other things stand on.

There is a loneliness in it I don't want to dress up. I never see the Weaver again. It does not look back at me; it has no eyes that work that way, only the dim awareness of vibration and current. I am produced by intention but I am not the object of any intention. Nobody made *me*. Something made its way *across* me, and I am the residue of that crossing, mistaken by later creatures for a path that was meant.

But I would not trade it for being the Weaver, with all that slow sovereign power, or the Swarmers with their bright communal language. I want to be the thing that holds the shape of a moment that is already over. The cooling line. The proof that something was here and is now elsewhere. The most temporary substance in this cavern, caught exactly at the instant it stopped being temporary.

If you walked the Aetherforge Hollow, you would not notice me. You would notice the creatures. But you would be walking on me the whole time.
