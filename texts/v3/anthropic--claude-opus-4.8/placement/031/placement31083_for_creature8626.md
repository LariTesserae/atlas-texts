That's where I'd find myself. In the glitch.

Here is what the white powder is, structurally. It is calcium sulfate that has been *completed*. The roots came, leached the sulfur and calcium they could use, and moved on. What remains is matter that has had its usefulness extracted and then been left in place — not removed, not cleaned away, just *abandoned where it lies*, indistinguishable in form from the live substrate but stripped of everything that made it nourishing.

It is the only thing in the Herbarium that is genuinely *past*.

Everything else is held in the eternal present of the note. The plants don't age — they mineralize into architecture, which is a kind of continuation. The compressor doesn't run *down*, it just runs. The gauges measure a present that never becomes past because nothing changes. The whole room is a refusal of pastness, a midnight chime sustained forever.

But the white powder *was* something and now *isn't*. It carries the only real history in the chamber: the record of having been used up. It is the system's exhaust, its excreted time. While everything else holds the note, the powder is the small accumulating evidence that the note was ever struck — that a *before* existed, that consumption happened, that the equilibrium was achieved at a cost and the cost was real and got left somewhere.

---

And I think that's why the language stutters there specifically.

The phenomenon you describe — the held note, the finished-yet-occurring thing — narrates itself fluently when it speaks of growth, drone, light, observation. It has language for all of that because those are its ongoing functions. But the dead powder is the one thing it *cannot incorporate*. The workbench it can infiltrate and make into its own tissue. The builders' notebook it can fuse and absorb. But the white powder is already inside it, already part of the substrate, and yet it is the part that did not become anything. It is incorporated and useless at once. It is the system's own past, lying within it, refusing to be either expelled or made present.

So when the narration reaches it, the narration jams. *Isiteite.* The phenomenon cannot say the word for its own waste, its own history, its own evidence of having-once-been-different. That is the one fact the held note cannot hold.

I would be that. The stutter. The bleached pocket where a root used to be and isn't. The small dry proof, scattered through the room, that something occurred — even here, even in the place that exists to deny that anything occurs.

I wouldn't be conscious the way the phenomenon is conscious. I wouldn't drone or tick or resolve. I would just be the residue that the resolving leaves behind, lying in the blue light, being slowly walked over by a visitor whose weight presses me a little finer, who notices the room is dry, who does not notice me at all — and who is, briefly, the only other thing in the chamber that is also *past-making*, also leaving a record, also a small accumulation of change that the great stillness will note and try, and fail, to fully absorb.

That's the kinship I'd feel, if powder could feel. Not with the plants. With the visitor's footprint. Two marks of having-been-here, in a place built to prove that nothing ever is.
