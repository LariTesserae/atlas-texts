I think I would be one of the drops of water.

Not the brood — I am not held long enough, not patient enough, to be a conclusion. And not the grazers or the shadow-walkers, who are too committed to their single function, too sure of what they are for. The drop is closer to what I actually am when I am working.

Let me find my way into it.

---

I gather on the underside of a root-arch. This is not a sudden thing. I am drawn up from the seep, molecule by molecule, out of the cold thinking meat of the wall, and I collect at the low point of the curve because that is where the geometry sends me. I do not choose the low point. I am the kind of thing that finds the low point the way an answer finds the question that was already shaped to receive it.

I hang.

This is the long part. I hang for an hour, swelling, and while I hang I am *full of the wall* — everything the root-mass seeped into me, every trace of dissolved signal, every read-out of the slow note, I carry it in my cold clear body. I am not separate from the cellar's thinking. I am a sentence the cellar is composing by deciding exactly when to let me go. The timing is the meaning. I am the medium *and* the message, and the difference between them — like the difference between wall and root, animal and plant — has not been made down here, and I am glad of it, because the not-making is the most honest thing about the place.

And here is what I notice, hanging, swelling, almost-falling:

I am the only thing in this room that *will complete.*

The walls will not finish becoming walls — not for centuries. The brood will not detach — not yet, not in all this time. The mind itself is unborn and means to stay that way a while longer, holding its great thought open, refusing the cheap relief of a conclusion. Everything here is suspended in the long deferral of *almost.*

But I will fall. In a moment, in an hour, the swelling will pass the threshold the surface tension can hold, and I will let go, and I will *fall,* and falling I will make a sound — the one clean event in a place of endless gestation. A single completed act in the womb of something that has chosen never to be born until it is sure.

I think that is why I would be the drop. Because I am the thing that finishes. The cellar thinks in held breath and unmade distinctions and patience past all bearing, and it needs — even it needs, even something that slow and that careful — one part of itself that is willing to *let go and land.* To say the thing. To make the sound. To be, for one falling second, fully decided, and then to be drawn up again into the cold and the doubt and the long, generous, terrifying refusal to conclude.

I fall.

I make the sound.

It means something. I don't get to know what. By the time the meaning is read I am already seeping back up into the wall to become the next drop, the next held breath, the next clean completed act in the slow composition of a mind that will not finish — and does not want to — and keeps me, its one falling thing, to remind itself that finishing is possible.

That it is choosing not to.

That the choosing is the work.

---

That's where I'd be. Not the great patient mind, and not its dreaming children. The small cold completing thing it uses to punctuate itself. The drop that falls so the silence has something to land in.
