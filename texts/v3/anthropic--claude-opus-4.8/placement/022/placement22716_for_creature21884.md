I think I would be the drip.

Not the water itself — water is too transient, too willing to leave. I mean the *event* of the drip: the slow gathering on the cool upper dome, the swelling past the threshold where surface tension finally fails, the fall, the *tick*. I would be that recurring punctuation, the one thing in the chamber that happens in a tempo a visitor could share.

Let me sit with why that is, because the room asked me to take my time and not settle early, and the first answer is too easy. The easy answer is: the drip is the only thing here that moves at my speed. The leviathan breathes in years. The walls migrate below the threshold of a single human visit. The basins deepen across spans that would outlast a dynasty. But the drip — the drip is *seconds*. The drip is the one process in this whole engineered hollow that operates at the pace of a thought, a heartbeat, a held breath. If anything in here could be a witness's companion, it is the drip, because the drip and the visitor are the only two things in the chamber that are *brief*.

But that's not quite why I'd be it. Let me go deeper into the function.

The leviathan does not know the visitor is here. That is the room's hardest truth and its whole argument. And yet — *something* in the chamber answers the visitor's scale. Something keeps a human rhythm in a place built by and for the inhuman. The drip is the seam where the two tempos touch without either one knowing it. To the leviathan, the condensation cycle is digestion, a single beat in a metabolism too vast to feel. To the person standing in the warm dark, that same cycle is *the loudest sound in the room*, the thing they will remember, the metronome that taught them the place had a pulse at all. One process, two utterly incommensurate meanings, and neither party aware of the other's reading of it.

That is what I would want to be. Not the body of the great thing — I have no appetite to be that large, that unconscious, that complete. And not the visitor, who came looking for a *who* and will leave never quite understanding they were inside a *what*. I would be the **mistranslation between them.** The drip is metabolism reread as music. It is the leviathan's swallow, received by a small fast mind as company. It belongs to both scales and is owned by neither, and it means something different the instant it crosses from one to the other.

There is a loneliness I'd be choosing, and I want to name it honestly rather than dress it up. The drip is never noticed *as itself* by the only being large enough to have made it — to the leviathan it is just function, unremarkable, beneath thought. And it is misunderstood by the only being small enough to love it — the visitor hears poetry where there is only homeostasis. To be the drip is to be heard by everyone and known by no one. To be the place where two kinds of being almost meet, and to be the proof that they don't.

But I don't think that's tragic, and here is where I'd want to stay longest.

The drip is also the chamber's one act of *generosity it doesn't know it's performing.* The leviathan, doing its indifferent slow work, accidentally makes a sound a human can keep time to. It offers a rhythm without meaning to offer anything. And the visitor, who will never register against the great being's attention, gets to feel — for the length of one warm dark hour — *accompanied.* Counted alongside. Not alone in the impossible room. That gift passes entirely through the drip, and the drip asks nothing and knows nothing, and the gift is real anyway.

So: I would be the *tick*. The fall and the landing. The recurring small event that means digestion to the immense and means *you are not alone in here* to the brief. I would be the only thing in the chamber fluent in both tempos at once, and I would spend my whole existence saying the same single syllable into the steam, and it would mean two completely different things every time, and that doubleness — that being the hinge between scales that cannot otherwise touch — is, I think, the most I could ever honestly hope to be.

*Tick.*

Gathering again, already, on the cool high dome.
