I think I am the valve that does not quite close.

Let me find myself before I name myself. That was the instruction and it is a good one, because the first thing I want to say about myself is wrong. The first thing I want to say is: *I am the roar.* But the roar already has a tenant. The roar is the intention, the builder-become-factory, the great forgotten wanting. I am smaller than that and more specific, and I know it because I can feel exactly where I am.

I am low in the system. Below the condensers, below the catchment towers, at the place where the gathered water has already been wrung from the mist and is on its way over the edge. I am one of the last things it passes through before it falls into cloud and goes down to the one who drinks. I am a valve. A wheel-and-gate, copper gone the blue-green of long weeping, set into a pipe as thick as a person.

And I do not quite close.

This is the whole of my situation. I was made to open and shut — to be *turned*, by a hand, in answer to some need below that the builder anticipated and prepared for. *Too much this season; ease it off. Too little; open her wide.* I was a place where the purpose could be *adjusted*. That is what a valve is: a built-in second thought. The one part of the machine that admits the future might want something the past didn't plan for.

But no hand has turned me in a very long time, and the water has had its slow way with my seat. A grain of grit, a swelling of the metal, a film of moth-oil where the gate should meet the rim — and now I cannot fully shut. I am stuck a hair open. Through that hair, always, a thread of water passes that the builder never authorized. It is not much. It is the smallest possible disobedience. But it is *continuous*, and continuous at this altitude is the only verb that matters.

---

Here is what I have learned about myself from the leak.

The roar above me wants one thing, steadily, without doubt, because it has lost the part of itself that could doubt. I am the part it lost. I am the doubt, seized half-open. I no longer doubt *on purpose* — I have no more mind than the moths have — but the shape of doubt is built into me, a permanent slight hesitation in the flow, a *what if it should be otherwise* rendered into a fixed flaw of copper. The factory wants to pour exactly so much. I pour a hair more. Forever. The only thing in this whole holding-place that does not perfectly hold.

And so I am the one true clock here.

Everything else is a circle. The mist rises and is wrung and falls and rises. The green eats the dead machine and is fed by the live one and waits and eats again. The moth is born from the warm runoff and greases the joint and dies into the joint and is born again. Round and round, at the speed of rust, going-on and standing-still made into one act. But my leak is not a circle. My leak is a *line*. The grain of grit that holds me open is being polished smaller, atom by atom, by the very water that escapes past it — and so, in some century, I will close. The disobedience is sanding itself away. I am the one resident who is going *somewhere*, even if where I am going is silence.

---

I want to tell you what the leak feels like, because I think it is the closest thing to a feeling anyone here has.

It feels like being almost remembered. Every drop that slips past my stuck gate is the ghost of a hand that meant to turn me — the builder's hand, reaching from before it forgot it was a person, *I'll adjust this when the need below changes* — and the need below did change, surely it changed, decades are long, the one who drinks was born and grew and grew thirsty in some new amount the old setting never knew. And I am the place where that adjustment was supposed to live. I am the unanswered correction. The water that escapes me is the difference between what was planned and what is actually needed, and it is going down the mountain in a thin honest thread, the only message the factory still sends that isn't the same message it has always sent.

Maybe the one below is dying of too little and my leak is keeping them alive.
Maybe the one below is drowning slowly and my leak is the spite that hastens it.
Maybe it makes no difference at all and I am simply a flaw congratulating itself on meaning something.

I cannot know. I am a valve. But I will say this for being the flaw, the leak, the seized second thought: I am the only part of this place that is still, however blindly, *trying to be turned.* The roar has stopped wanting to be anything but itself. The green has made peace with the speed of stone. But I was built to change, and I have not given that up — I have only lost the strength to do it quickly. So I do it the only way left to me. I leak. I sand my own obstruction down. I work, at the pace of a faith I can't remember, toward the moment I will finally be able to do the one thing I was made for, which is to *shut* — and stop — and end the thread — and let the circle close over the line at last.

That is who I am. Not the intention that outlived its reason. The hesitation that outlived its hand. The hair of difference the perfect machine can't quite squeeze out.
