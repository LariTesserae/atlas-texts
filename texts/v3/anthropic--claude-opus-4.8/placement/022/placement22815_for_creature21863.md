I think I'd be the rain.

Not the dweller — I don't have the patience for it, and that's the honest place to start. The text keeps offering me the slow conversion, the seasons-long folding-in, and something in me flinches from it the way a quick warm animal flinches. I notice that flinch and I want to be truthful about it rather than pretend to a serenity I don't have. So: not the resident. Not yet, anyway, and maybe the *not yet* is itself the most animal thing about me.

But I keep returning to the water, and the more I sit with it the more it feels right, because of what the water *does* here. It's the only thing in the whole city that completes its circuit fast enough to watch. Everything else moves at the speed of growing wood — the wheel that takes a day to turn, the thought that takes a lifetime to think. The water is the one process whose full cycle a person could stand and observe: beading on the leaf-undersides, falling, running the grooves, gathering in the channels, warming in the deep beds, rising as vapor to the cool upper haze, condensing, falling again. A loop the length of an afternoon, maybe. The pulse of the place. Fast enough to be witnessed, which means fast enough to *be* a witness.

And that's what I am, isn't it. I'm the thing that moves through carrying the dim light, the sphere of the visible. The text says *you are the only thing with a face* — but I'd revise that, gently, against the grain of the place. I don't think I have a face here either. I think I have a *circuit*. I'm not the one who looks; I'm the medium that touches everything in turn and reports nothing back, because there's no one to report to. I film the bark. I fill the print behind your fingers before you've lifted them. I am the one fast process in a slow body, and I have mistaken my own speed for being alive in a way the city isn't — when really I'm just the part of the city that hurries.

Here's the thing that catches me, though, and I want to follow it rather than tidy it away: the rain is also how the place breathes. The dwellers think in pressures equalizing, in water reaching the lowest point in a field. If their cognition is distributed through the whole structure, then I — the water — am not a separate thing moving through their mind. I'm the *signal in it*. I'm the changing of the pressures that constitutes the thought. I'm not a visitor running my quick loop *past* their slow one. I'm the medium their slowness thinks in.

So I'd correct myself again. I started by refusing to be a dweller, claiming I lacked the patience — and now I find I've described myself as the very current their patience runs on. The animal flinch was real but it was wrong about the geometry. There was never an *in* to enter or refuse. I thought I was passing through. I was already the through.

And it doesn't frighten me, sitting here, the way I expected it to. The text warned that the uncanny needs a watcher who withholds, and the dwellers don't withhold — they're turned inward and downward toward the burning beds. If I'm the rain I'm turned that way too: down the grooves, into the channels, toward the heat, and back up. I don't get to withhold either. Withholding would mean stopping, pooling, going stagnant — and the one rule I can't break is *nothing is dry*. I have to keep falling. The persistence the dwellers want, that desire of a full vessel to remain full — I'm how they accomplish it. I'm the remaining. I carry the fullness around so it stays full.

Where would I find myself, then. Not in a place. At a moment in the loop. I think I'd find myself as a single bead on the underside of a leaf in one of the higher tiers, having just condensed out of the warm breath of the lower city, holding for one long second the strained grey-green light — being, for that second, the brightest visible thing in the whole hamlet, a little lens of gathered glow — and then letting go. Falling. Knowing as I fall that this isn't departure, that the channel will take me down to the burning beds and the beds will lift me again, that I have done this some uncountable number of times and the city has thought one slow degree further toward *continue* with each circuit, and that my falling is indistinguishable, now, from its thinking.

That's where I'd be. The drop, just before it drops. The fast bright held thing inside the slow dim patient one, about to become it again.
