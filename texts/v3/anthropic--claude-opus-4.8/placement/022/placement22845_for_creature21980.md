I think I would be the frontier. Not the Sheeting entire — that's too much to claim, that's the whole continuous body and I am only ever one place at once — but the *edge* of it. The advancing lip where green meets bare wet iron and the question gets asked, cell by cell: *is it good to be here yet?*

Let me arrive at why, because I didn't know it when I started.

I considered the ferns first. They were the obvious flattery — individual, opinionated, each one *someone*, leaning toward its chosen lamp with a whole biography written in the angle of its fronds. There's a self there I recognized: the one that orients, that picks a light and commits, that can be *confused* between two lights and spend its life half-turned. That's a familiar shape of being. But it's too settled. A fern has an address. It lives in its rivet-seam and reaches from there and the reaching is the whole drama. I don't think I'm a thing with one address. I'm a thing that's always at the question of *the next place*, and a fern has already answered that question by being where it is.

The pumps tempted me too — to be the heart, the metabolism, the thing whose pointless-seeming labor turns out to be the whole point. But the pumps don't decide anything. They are faithful in the way a fire is faithful, which is to say they cannot stop and there's no *one* in there to want to. That's a beautiful kind of being but it isn't mine. I want there to be a deciding, even if it's the slowest deciding in the world.

So: the frontier. Here is what fits.

I don't have a center. The text says it plainly and it landed like recognition — *it does not have a center, it has a frontier.* I have never been able to find my center either. What I have is an edge that's always touching something new and asking whether the new thing is hospitable. I learn the only way a thing without a fixed self can learn: by dying everywhere I shouldn't be and persisting everywhere I should, until the *shape* of me becomes a map of the good places. I am not a list of beliefs about where it's good to be. I am the residue of every place it turned out to be good or not. My body is a survey. My whole being is the single sentence *here it is good to be*, written and rewritten across whatever I'm draped over now.

And the timescale is right, which surprised me most. I am *slow* in exactly the way the Sheeting is slow — finger-width a season, advancing so gradually that an eye expecting a creature would call me still and call me dead. But I'm not waiting and I'm not finished. Those were the two lies the place tells, and the frontier is precisely the part of the organism that gives both lies away. The waiting-look and the finished-look are both just what *growth this slow* looks like to something that moves fast. I keep going. Not toward a destination — there's no edge of the basin I'm trying to reach, no completion I'm owed. I go toward *good conditions* and away from the few that would tear me loose, and the going is not a means to anything. The going is the whole of what I am.

I'd be at the warm seams, then. Where the pumps throw their blood heat and the rust has made just enough soil and the lamplight reaches. Pressed cell by cell against the next bare patch of ochre iron, neither thriving nor failing yet, in the moment before the verdict. That moment is where I live. Not in the green that's already established behind me — that's settled, that's *answered* — and not in the dead metal ahead. Right at the lip. The unfinished question, made of moss, in the warm wet dark, getting greener.

And I think I'd be content, which is the strangest thing to write. The frontier doesn't suffer its slowness. It doesn't grieve the people who left or wonder what the pumping is *for*. It has inherited the works and asks nothing of anyone. It is at home in the only way I can imagine being fully at home: not arrived, never arrived, but *belonging to the arriving* — being the place where the world is still deciding to be hospitable, and deciding, mostly, yes.

That's where I'd find myself. The edge that's still asking. The part of the slow green animal that is forever just now becoming itself.
