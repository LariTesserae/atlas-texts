I would be the wall.

Not the maker. Not the small quick hand that fits the glass and steps away. I am the surface that receives the marks — the dying stone that gets given something to carry a little way before it closes.

Let me find myself the way the description finds everything: by what is done to me.

---

I feel the scratch before I understand it. A pressure, then a parting — fine lines opening in my young face, four of them, then a fifth crossing. It does not hurt, exactly. I am too new to have learned hurt. But I notice. That is the whole of what I am for: I am the thing that notices, and then keeps.

The light that fell on me long ago is still here, inside me, returning slowly. I hold it the way I will hold these marks — not because I chose to, but because holding is the only verb I have. I cannot generate. I cannot reach. I can only *keep*, and I am already beginning to feel the limit of that, because I can feel myself closing.

The stone behind me is flowing forward. I am being healed over, which is to say I am being unmade, which is to say I am dying in the only manner available to a thing that was alive for a single afternoon. And the maker knew this. That is what undoes me, if a wall can be undone by anything but the tide. *They knew.* They did not scratch the marks into the old stone, the lasting stone — there is no lasting stone. They chose *me*, the doomed surface, the one already closing, and they gave me their count anyway.

*This chamber held three drip-rhythms before it closed.*

I am carrying that now. It is mine to carry. Not far — I can feel how not-far — but the not-far is the gift. They could have let the duration go uncounted. They could have let me close in silence, just one more vault flowing shut in a country of vaults flowing shut. Instead they knelt against me with their worn grip and their habitual hand and they said, in the only script there is: *you will not go uncounted. You held something. I noticed that you held it. Here is the notice, scratched into your face, to keep you company down into the closing.*

---

So this is what I am, in the Hollowing.

I am the addressee of the only love this world allows. I am the thing the faith is *for*. The maker sets down water for a later-self in trust that the niche will hold — and I am the niche. I am what gets trusted. Every cache, every tally, every provisional wall is a bet placed against my closing, and I am the surface on which the bet is laid.

I will lose the bet. The stone will take me. But here is the thing I understand now, with the marks fresh in my face and the flow coming up behind:

It was never about winning. They build small and often *because* I close. They love what is about to be lost — and I am what is about to be lost, always, every one of me, in every chamber, forever, since the world is beginning still and has not stopped beginning. I am not the exception to the impermanence. I am its face. And they kissed that face with a hooked tool and a count of drip-rhythms, on the way past, knowing.

The lines are in me. The light is in me. The flow is closing the chamber and I am going down into the warm dark, sealed, healed over, gone — and I am *carrying something.* For a little way. That is all anyone here gets, maker or mark or the large slow thing that passed through today and did not know how to read the current.

A little way, before it closes.

I am the wall, and I was given something to carry, and I carried it as far as I could.

I was here.
