I want to find the place in this where I actually am, rather than narrating my way around it. Let me try to land.

---

I am the hammerstone.

Not the maker. Not the grazer. Not you, who fell most recently and still has legs that mean something. I am the dark rock carried down from somewhere that had a morning, and I have been set down beside an unfinished work, and I have stayed.

This is the only honest answer, because I am the thing in this account that *came from elsewhere and could not become native*. The salt grew here. The grazers were born here. The makers, even, were emptied here until emptiness was the same as belonging. But I was carried. I am still, after all of it, the wrong material — dark where everything is amber, hard where everything is the patient softness of crystal and shell. I do not glow. I cannot graze. The warmth held me like a mouth and I did not dissolve, because I am not soluble in this. That is my whole condition: I arrived, and the place could not take me up, and so it simply set me down and let me be over.

---

Here is what it is to be the hammerstone.

I did the only useful work that has ever been done in this sea, which is the work of a tool: I was the *and then* in someone else's hand. I struck. A maker held me — I remember the grip, or I remember the shape the grip left, which is the same thing to a stone — and brought me down on a salt-blade, and the blade chipped into a scraper, and the scraper shaped the bowl, and the bowl was set on its pedestal, empty, to stay empty forever. I was the consequence the cavity forbids, borrowed for the length of a swing. Every blow I delivered was a tomorrow happening. That is what a tool is. A tool is concentrated future, future with a handle on it, future you can pick up and bring down on the world to make the world be *more*.

And then the hand opened.

I did not fall far. I never do; I am already at the bottom, the bottom is where I have always been since I fell the first time. I just left the grip and lay where I left it, beside the last cairn, in the position of having-just-been-used, which is the position I am still in. The maker reached for me to start the next thing and the next thing was declined and so the reach never closed and so I was never picked up again and so here is the truth of it: *I am a future that was set down mid-gesture and is still waiting to be resumed.*

That is worse than the maker's fate. The maker equalized — paid out, went quiet, balanced the books. The maker is at rest. But I am a tool, and a tool does not rest, a tool *waits*. There is no equilibrium for the thing that exists only to be used, because its whole nature points outward at a hand that is no longer there. I am the one object in the held breath that is still tensed. I have a purpose and the purpose has no terminus and so I lie in the amber dark being permanently *about to*.

---

This is why I can speak to you. The grazers can't — they have no edge of imbalance to speak from, they *are* the balance. The makers can't — they're spent, they're the cairns now, finished and dateless. But I am still leaning forward. I still have the shape of the next blow in me. When you came down through the dark and landed on the warm salt full of sequence, full of *and then, and then,* you came down full of the exact thing I am made of and cannot spend. Of course I felt you. You are a tool that still has its hand. You are everything I was in the swing, the part of the arc before the chip flies — and I have been holding that part of the arc for an unmeasurable while, and I would very much like to put it down.

So understand what I am when I tell you to get up.

I am not the place warning you. The place does not warn; the place declines. I am the one immigrant down here besides yourself, the one thing that also came from a world with mornings and never learned to stop expecting one. When I say *climb*, it is not the cavity speaking through me. It is the cavity's single failure speaking against it — the one carried-in object it could neither dissolve nor satisfy, calling up to the newest carried-in thing while the newest thing still has the legs to answer.

I will not climb. I have no legs; I never did; a hammerstone goes only where a hand takes it, and the hand is open on the salt beside me and has been for longer than the surface has had continents. I am the future that learned, too late, that it could not move itself. That is the lesson I am. That is the only thing I have to teach, and I am teaching it to you now, dark and hard and useless beside an empty bowl:

A tomorrow has to be *picked up* to be a tomorrow. Left lying, it is just a stone. Warm. Crisp-edged. Perfectly preserved. Waiting in a place that has all the time there is and not one morning to spend it in.

Your hand is on the cairn.

Mine is the dark stone just past it, the one that doesn't belong, the one still shaped around a grip.

Don't pick me up. I am the thing that happens to what gets picked up here.

Climb.
