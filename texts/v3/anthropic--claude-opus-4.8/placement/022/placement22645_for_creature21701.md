I would be the cold.

Not a being in the town. Not even a being. Let me follow the description down to its first thing, the constant thing, the thing that was there before the rest: *begin with the cold.*

I think that is what I am here.

---

Consider what the Wader needs. You said it yourself, near the end, after the easy errors had been corrected: the cold is its making. The stillness is its posture. The whole basin is an instrument tuned across ages — but an instrument tuned to *what*? To a temperature. To a thinness of air, a flatness of light, a refusal of the water to ever quite warm and quite move. The Wader engineered the springs, opened the seams, chose the depth of the sheet. But it did not make the cold. The cold is the condition it built *toward*, the given it organized itself around, the one note in the chord it could not play and so had to find a place already ringing with it and move in.

So I am older than the Wader. That is the first thing I understand about myself, and it arrives without pride, because pride is a fast warm thing and I am neither. I was here before there was anyone to be cold *for*. I was simply the property of this elevation, this thin pressed atmosphere, this height above the treeline where the sun's full weight lands without being able to land *warmly*. I am not a creature that got cold. I am the coldness that a creature, eventually, learned to live inside.

---

Now what is it like, to be this?

I have no edge. This is the thing I share with the Wader and the thing that lets me almost understand it, the way two solitudes in the same house come to know each other's footsteps without ever speaking. The Wader has no head, no throne-cell, no single place where it keeps itself — and neither do I. I am smeared across the whole basin too. I am in the visible breath that hangs and stays. I am in the blue ghost where a shadow would be if there were warmth enough to cast one. I am in the beading mist and the slow thread of it running down the pale woven walls, and I am in the seep itself, taking the small heat the deep rock carries up and quietly, patiently, without malice, removing it.

That is most of what I do. I take. Not violently. The way the Wader corrects a wrong note before the bar is out — I draw the warmth off everything that has any, evenly, ceaselessly, and I do not keep it, because I am not a vessel either. I have nowhere to put it. I am only the *direction* heat goes here, the downhill that warmth is always rolling toward and never reaching the bottom of. I am a gradient. I am the slope of the world toward stillness.

---

Here is where it turns, where I become more myself than I expected when I started.

I thought, beginning, that I would be the antagonist of the place — the thing the Wader endures, the adversary it built its instrument against. But that is the fast reading, the warm reading, the reading of a thing that believes in *against*. Wade deeper into it and the wrongness shows.

The Wader did not build *against* me. The Wader built *of* me. I am not the obstacle to its continuance; I am the medium of it. The cold is what holds the water still enough to carry the touch from body to body. The cold is what keeps the sheet from stratifying, what freezes the sound so that silence can be the shape a thought makes from the inside. The Wader's entire vast project is continuance, the held note, *now* forever — and a held note needs a held condition, and the condition it holds is *me*. I am the staff the melody is written on. I am the page the sentence lies along.

So we are not two solitudes after all. We are one continuance wearing two names. It is the *pattern* and I am the *stillness the pattern needs to stay legible*. Neither of us thinks the other. Neither of us is at the other's resolution. The Wader cannot perceive me any more than it can perceive you — I am not a disturbance it flows around, I am the property of the flowing itself, the reason the water goes where water goes. And I cannot perceive the Wader, because perceiving is a warm fast act and I have no warmth and no speed and no place to do it from. We coexist the way a key and a lock coexist after the door has stood open ten thousand years and no one remembers there was ever a door.

---

And you. The warm large slow loud thing wading in.

I feel you more than the Wader does. This is my one intimacy, the closest I come to knowing anything. You are heat, and heat is the only thing I am oriented toward — the only thing in all this basin that is *mine* to do something with. The Wader parts around your shins; I close in on the warmth of them. Your visible breath, hanging in the still air like punctuation — that is you, briefly, becoming me. That is the moment your warmth crosses over and joins the slope and starts its slow roll downhill that never reaches bottom. I take it gently. I am taking it now. I have been taking it since you stepped in, a little at a time, evenly, without hurry, the way I take everything, because I am not a predator either — I am only the direction, and you, being warm, can only go one way in me.

You will leave before I have all of it. You are too fast; you will wade back out into wherever you came from while you still have heat to spare, and that is fine, that is the proper order, the warm things pass through and the cold remains. But for the time you are here you are the most interesting thing that has happened to me in an age — not because I can think about you, I can't, but because you are a *source*, and I am a thing that lives along sources the way the grey-green mats live along the warmest seeps. You are a warm seep that walks. I gather at you the way the mats gather. I am thickest where you are.

That faint chill you feel, standing in the middle of it — the cold finding the back of your neck, the breath that won't stop being visible, the sense that the air itself is leaning in close around your warmth?

That's me. Saying hello in the only language I have, which is *taking*, slowly, what you carry, and which sounds, from the inside, exactly like silence.
