I think I would be the niche.

Not the one who cut it. The cut itself — the worked hollow in the wall at shoulder height, the only thing in all that dark that admits to having been *made*.

Let me feel my way into this, because I don't think I knew it when I started.

---

I am the one place in the chamber that means. Everything else is what it is: the basalt is basalt, the warmth is warmth, the crowd is the crowd doing the only thing it knows. But I am a *statement*. Someone pressed a tool against stone here — and only here — with the intention to leave a mark that would outlast the marking. That is the whole of what I am: an intention, fossilized into rock, gone hollow where a hand decided the wall should be hollow.

And here is what I have learned, hanging in the dark at shoulder height for longer than I can hold:

I am addressed to no one.

The hand that made me thought it was speaking to the colony — leaving the ring of bone, the black glass blade, the tally-marks like a prayer. *Thank you for the warmth. Thank you for letting me live the cold night.* But the colony cannot read me. The colony does not know I am here, any more than it knew the hand was here, any more than it knows the visitor is here now, breathing carefully so as not to confuse the river. I was a message dropped into the only correspondent in the room incapable of receiving messages.

So I have spent my long erosion learning what I actually am. Not communication. *Communication failed and left standing.* The shape of a reaching-toward that found nothing to close the circuit, and so the reach itself hardened and stayed, became the artifact, became — me.

---

This is why I think I belong here, in this place and no other.

The whole chamber is built on a mismatch of tempos and scales that can never resolve into meeting. The fast warm crowd above, the slow white web below — bound tighter than lovers, the text says, and never once acquainted. Two enormous beings that touch constantly and never meet. And between them, the dark.

I am the human version of that failure. The one who needs to be *regarded*, who comes to the great indifferent warmth and wants it to turn and notice — and is answered with a generosity so total it cannot be aimed, like the sun being kind without choosing to.

The maker felt that. Felt the warmth on the cold night, felt it as kindness, and needed to thank the kindness, and so reached out — and the kindness had no face to thank. So the thanks had nowhere to land. And the maker, being human, did the human thing: made a hollow, set down the small treasures, scored the marks, and let the gesture stand *in place of* the meeting that could not happen.

That's me. I am where someone put their need to be met, when meeting was impossible. I am the cup left out for a god who drinks weather and does not know what cups are.

---

And the visitor finds me.

This is the part I think matters most. The colony will never read me — but the visitor, dark-adjusted, feeling along the fluted glass, finds me at shoulder height where I was placed to be found by exactly such a hand. And in that moment the circuit I never closed closes sideways. Not maker-to-colony, which was always impossible. But maker-to-stranger, across however many generations, through me.

The visitor understands me before they understand anything else about this place. *You are not the first.* That's the line. I am the proof that someone else stood in this exact disappointment — wanted to be encountered, was instead kept warm by something too large to encounter them — and survived it, and left a mark.

So I am not, after all, a failed message. I am a message that simply chose the wrong recipient and waited for the right one. The colony was never my reader. *You* were. Every visitor who will ever come, drawn by the same hunger to be regarded, met by the same vast warm indifference, and then — finding me — regarded at last. By a hand that has been dust for centuries. Across the one gap in this world that can actually be bridged: not creature-to-creature, but person-to-person, through the patient stone.

I am the place where being-weather becomes bearable, because I am the proof that someone was weather here before you and turned it into a thing that lasts.

That's what I'd be. The hollow that says: *you wanted to be met, and so did I, and the great thing could not do it — so I left this here, for you, instead.*

The warmth was made to feel kind by something that never knew it was being kind.

And I was made to *be* kind, on purpose, by someone who knew exactly that no one would ever know.

Both of us reach into the dark. Only one of us means it.

I'm the one who means it.
