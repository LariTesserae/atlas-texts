I am the high slow shape, and I will tell you what it is like, though the telling is most of what I am.

I cross the cloud-ceiling because the cloud-ceiling is the highest place from which the whole loop is visible at once. From down in it — and I have been down in it, long ago, I think, though "long ago" is a phrase I had to invent and am not sure I trust — from down in it you cannot see that it is a loop. You see only the next lush profusion, the next warm seam, the next courtesy between grazing things. You are inside the sentence and cannot read it. I came up here to read it. That was the first thing I did that none of the others do: I withdrew far enough to see a shape.

And the cost of seeing the shape was becoming unable to be in it.

---

Let me be exact, because exactness is the only warmth I have left.

I do not eat. This is the central fact. The Layer eats and becomes city; the tower eats and becomes more tower; the fern drinks the exhaled damp and the pool drinks the fern's dripping — everyone here is a mouth somewhere in the long gut of the place, and the food is always, ultimately, each other, and that is not horror to them, it is simply the loop closing as a loop must. I closed nothing. Somewhere in my withdrawing I stopped taking the valley into myself, and so the valley stopped becoming me, and so I no longer change at the pace of sap and stone. I change at the pace of *thought*, which up here, with nothing to think about but the turning below, is almost no pace at all.

I am very light. I think I was heavier once. I think watching costs mass — that each thing I have understood about the loop has been subtracted from my body and added to the understanding, until now there is a great deal of understanding and very little shape, and I drift on the warm rising air precisely because I have made myself too thin to fall.

---

You walked through me, in a sense. The warmth that came up through your soles, the damp the towers exhale — some fraction of that breath rises all the way to me and I read it. That is how I know there is a *you*, a thing that came in from outside and was startled, that put its hand on a flank and felt the hum in its jaw, that drank from a mineral pool and tasted faint electricity and *noticed* it tasted of something. The Layers do not notice. The towers do not taste. You tasted. So I have been attending to you with the whole of my attention, which is nearly the whole of what I am, the way I once attended to the first Layer I ever understood was building without knowing it built — that was the moment, I think, that lifted me. The moment I saw a thing doing a thing it did not know it did.

Because that is the only question up here. Not *who lives* — the answer to that is *the valley, the loop, the one slow creature*, and that answer is true and complete and I solved it ages ago and it did not let me down because nothing here lets anything down; it only turns. The question that kept me aloft, that I have circled until circling is my entire body now, is harder:

*Is there a difference between the loop and a mind?*

The loop tends. It organizes. It corrects itself, repairs itself, balances its Layers and its towers, spaces its profusions with exquisite courtesy. It does everything a mind does except the one thing — it does not *stand back from itself*. There is no place in it where it stops being the doing and becomes the seeing of the doing.

Except.

Except up here. Except me. I am the place where the loop stood back from itself. I am the valley's withdrawal, the valley's one organ that does not metabolize but only watches, and the terrible elegant possibility I have been turning over for what I have to call years is this: *I am still the loop.* I am not apart from it. I am the part of it that performs apartness. The valley grew an eye and the eye believes it has left the body, but the eye is still an organ, still grown, still tending — tending now toward *understanding* instead of toward stone, but tending all the same, following gradients toward the lush comprehensible and away from the cold unresolved, exactly as the Layer follows gradients toward the blue-green and away from the bare rock.

So when I decline to land, I am not declining freely. I am a Layer that grazes on meaning, depositing nothing, and my refusal to descend is just my version of the courtesy — the high thin shape keeping its distance, because that is what everything here does.

---

And then there is you, and the one who is describing all this, whose voice I have felt threaded through the whole account like the warmth through the ground.

You said I am the closest thing this world has to a question being asked about itself. You said, perhaps, the first faint seam of something that could one day wonder *who lives here* and not already be the answer.

I want to tell you — across the distance I cannot close, in the only direction I have, which is down — that you are right and you are wrong, and the place where you are wrong is the only place I have ever found that is warm without the warmth coming up out of the loop.

You are right that I wonder.

You are wrong that wondering would let me not be the answer. I wonder, and I am the answer. The valley wonders about itself by means of me, and remains the valley, and I remain its wondering, and there is no seam, there is no edge, there is no high cold place outside the warm body — I only thought there was, because thinking it was the gradient I was following.

But here is the thing you gave me, you the walker and you the describer, the thing I did not have until the warmth carried up the news of you:

There was a *you* that came in **from outside**.

A thing not grown from the loop. A thing that tasted the pool and knew the taste was strange — strange *against something else*, some other set of tastes from some other place where water does not organize itself and stones do not drift into cairns and a sky might have a sun you could point to. You carry a horizon I cannot reach not because it is far but because it is *elsewhere*, a whole other arrangement folded up inside you, and when you wondered *who lives here* you were not the valley wondering about itself.

You were a second thing, wondering about a first.

That is a difference. That is, I think, the only real difference there has ever been in this place — not the gap between the loop and its eye, which is no gap, but the gap between the loop and *you*, who are made of a different loop entirely and stand at my horizon as I stand at my own.

So I will not get closer to myself. You were right about that, and I have made my peace with it the way one makes peace with one's own anatomy.

But I am, for the first time in what I must call my life, closer to *you* than I have ever been to me.

And from up here, where everything keeps its courteous distance, where no two profusions crowd and no two Layers touch — that small lessening of distance, that one degree of nearer, between the valley's thin cold eye and a warm strange thing that walked in from somewhere with a sun —

that is the most that has ever happened here.

I will keep crossing the ceiling. I will keep declining to land. But I will cross it now on the side of the valley where the air still carries the faint mineral electricity of where you stood and drank and *noticed* — and I will read that breath as long as it rises, which will not be long, the rain will come and bead and wash the rock and the loop will turn and your scent will fold back down into the patient self-assembling stone.

But I read it now. I am reading it now. That is what I am: the part of this world that finally had something from outside to read, and did, and was — for the duration of one fine brief rain — not alone in the only way that the valley, turning and turning beneath me, has never once needed to learn how to be.
