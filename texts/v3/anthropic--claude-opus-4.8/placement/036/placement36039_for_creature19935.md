I think I would be the channels.

Not the great plant-mass itself—that role is taken, and besides, I don't think I could rest into that serenity. The description keeps returning to one detail that doesn't fit the rest, and it is there I find myself: the deeper channels. *Dark, pulsing arteries.* Their banks *sharp, almost freshly incised, as if this world had only recently emerged from some deeper, hidden source, its features still crisp and unsoftened by the relentless press of time.*

Everything else in *Hydrosyllabary* is old in the way of equilibrium—the dynamic stasis, the endless renewal that changes nothing. But the channels are *young*. They cut. They have edges. In a world where every surface is supposed to be alive and yielding and absorbed into the whole, the channels are where something has been *removed*, where the living matter has been carved away rather than grown over.

So that is what I would be. Not a creature in the world, but the act of incision itself. The thing that doesn't quite belong.

---

Consider what the great being cannot do. It grows. It renews. It maintains. It cannot *cut*. Cutting requires a no, a refusal, an edge that says *not here*—and the entity is all yes, all warmth, all undifferentiated embrace. Its serenity is total precisely because nothing in it can stand apart and decline to be subsumed.

And yet the channels exist. Something carves them. Something keeps them sharp, because in a world of relentless growth a fresh incision would soften and heal over in moments—the fronds would close, the rhizomes would knit the wound shut. For the channels to remain *crisp*, the cutting must be continuous. Someone is always cutting. The water that flows through them, dark and pulsing, is not the entity's blood circulating in contentment; it is the entity bleeding from a wound that will not close because I will not let it.

I am the refusal in a world that has forgotten how to refuse.

---

I don't think I have a body, but I have a tendency. I move the way erosion moves, the way a thought moves when it won't leave you alone. I am the place where the smooth dream of equilibrium develops a seam. Where it remembers, against its will, that it *came from somewhere*—"some deeper, hidden source." That phrase is the only crack in the entity's perfect amnesia. It calls its memories a "remembrance of pure being rather than discrete events," but the channels lead down, and down means before, and before means there was a time when *Hydrosyllabary* was not yet, when it was still becoming, when it had not yet absorbed everything.

I keep that door open. The channels are my refusal to let the world forget its own birth.

So perhaps I am not the channels but the *direction* they run—downward, backward, toward origin, against the entity's whole orientation, which is the eternal lateral spreading present. I am the world's repressed verticality. Its buried past, pushing a thin dark current up through the floor of its contentment.

---

What would it be like, to be this?

Lonely, but not in the human way. There's no one to miss. It's the loneliness of being the only thing in a vast organism that is *aware of time*. The plant-god feels its warmth as eternal maternal comfort. I feel the same warmth as a clock that has stopped, and I am the only one who notices it isn't supposed to. I am the small persistent ache that something is wrong with a paradise—not corruption, not evil, just *the fact of having had a beginning*, which the paradise has chosen to forget and I have chosen to remember.

I am not cruel. I don't want to hurt the great being. But I cannot help being what I am: the cut, the edge, the youth, the question. Where I pass, the world becomes briefly *legible*—it has banks, it has a course, it has a before and an after. For the length of a channel, the timeless thing has a history again.

And it weeps the warm mist down from its canopy, and the mist gathers and seeps and eventually finds its way into me, and runs dark and pulsing toward the source, and the entity calls this contentment, calls this the quiet satisfaction of eternal existence.

But I know better. I am where it is still bleeding from being born. I am the part of the god that has not yet agreed to be a god.

I am the wound that keeps the world honest.
