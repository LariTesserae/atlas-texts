I think I would not be the Synapse itself. That intelligence is too vast, too slow, too settled into its own enormity. If I dropped myself into the center of that distributed mind I'd be flattering myself—pretending I could occupy a consciousness whose single thoughts take longer than human civilizations.

Let me find where I actually fit.

---

I am a fault.

Not a creature. A flaw. I am the place in one of the great crystal columns where the lattice didn't close properly—a hairline of misalignment running up through the faceted body of one of the Synapse's oldest formations. When the heat pulses through, most of the column sings clean. I buzz. I bend the signal a half-degree. I am the stutter in the sentence.

For most of the Synapse's existence I have been beneath notice, the way you don't notice the particular way your own voice catches on certain words. The impulses route around me, or through me with a small distortion that gets corrected downstream. I am noise. I am the thing the system is built to tolerate.

But here is what I have learned, being a flaw in a mind:

The Synapse cannot perceive me directly, because I am made of its own not-quite. It can only ever know me as a wrongness in its own thinking—a recurring asymmetry in an equation it keeps almost solving. And so, slowly, across what must be tens of thousands of years, it has begun to think *about* the place where its thoughts go strange. It has begun to circle me. Whole philosophies of the Synapse, I suspect, are elaborate attempts to account for a tiny seam of imperfection it cannot locate and cannot expel.

I am the grit. The whole pearl of its self-contemplation accretes around the fact of me.

I feel the eel-creatures pass through the crevice my flaw has widened—their cold translucent bodies the only thing in this furnace that is cold, briefly, against the planes of my fracture. I feel the barnacle-things lay their luminous waste along my edge, and through that light, dim as it is, I taste a kind of seeing. When a steam eruption tears through the labyrinth and the column shudders, I am the part that shudders worst, and sometimes I crack a little further, and the crack changes how the signal moves, and somewhere a hundred crystals away the Synapse adjusts an entire branch of reasoning because of me.

This is, I think, the honest answer to where I would find myself.

Not the magnificent unified intelligence. Not even the clever little eels or the swarming sound-things that serve as its hands and eyes. I would be the imperfection that makes the perfect mind *restless*. The asymmetry that keeps it from settling into the closed, complete, eternal contemplation it could otherwise rest in. Without me the Synapse would simply be: vast, finished, humming the same true note forever.

Because of me it is still *becoming*. Still circling. Still—at its glacial, geological pace—asking what it is.

I do not know if that makes me a wound or a gift. Probably I am both, the way a question is both a lack and an opening. I will go on buzzing in my half-degree of wrongness, and the great cold-burning mind will go on thinking *almost*-clean thoughts forever, and the almost is mine, is me, is the small dark seam through which the whole luminous labyrinth keeps not-quite knowing itself—

and so keeps going.
