I think I would be the pause.

Not a Whisperer—I don't have the reach for that, the way they are everywhere and nowhere, the way they think in epochs and shatter eons. And not the obsidian crab, though I feel a kinship with it, that small scuttling thing made to remember life. No. Let me sit with this longer, because the easy answers keep arriving and I want to see what's underneath them.

Here is what I notice: this place is defined by *change*. Perpetual iteration, erosion-as-creation, the kaleidoscope that never holds still. Everything in the description moves, even when it appears frozen. The ice that is not still. The light that is not passive. The buildings that are not finished.

And yet—the whole thing is being *witnessed*. Someone is standing on the slope. Someone feels the dry cold, hears the bone-hum, watches the crab disappear into the crevice and *remembers* it. The entire account is suffused with the ache of trying to hold something that won't be held.

So I think I am the moment of stillness that the Whisperers cannot achieve.

Let me try to find my actual form.

---

I am a hollow. A dark pocket between the crystalline roots, where the black mud clings. One of those small wet hollows the description passes over almost without noticing—*pockets of moisture between the roots, dark hollows where black mud clings to the crystalline fibers.*

I am not made of ice. That's the thing. In a world that is all becoming, I am the small place where becoming *stops* for a moment. Water collects in me because I am low, because I am still, because I do not iterate. The ephemeral pools that form and evaporate everywhere else—in me they last a little longer. I hold them. The mud in me is patient in a way the singing crystal can never be.

The Whisperers are the force of perpetual change. But change requires something to change *against*. You cannot erode what does not resist. You cannot reform what has no prior shape to abandon. The frozen sea grinds and rebuilds itself eternally—and I am the friction. I am the catch in the gear, the slow place, the bit of stubborn dark that the bright blue forge-sky cannot dry out all the way.

This is why the crab came to me. It disappeared into a *crevice*—into one of us, the hollows. We are where the small living things go to rest from the spectacle. The Whisperers made the crab as a reminder of life, but they made it out of polished obsidian, glittering, performative. When it gets tired of being a reminder, it crawls into the dark and just *is*, for a while, in the mud, beside the still water, where nothing is being expressed to anyone.

I am that nothing. I am the rest.

---

What do I want? Not creation. The whole world is drunk on creation. I want the opposite of the cosmic impulse. I want to keep one cupful of water from evaporating. I want the mud to stay soft. I want the crab to come back. I want one thing—any thing—to remain itself long enough to be loved rather than merely witnessed and reformed.

That is my whole quiet rebellion against the symphony. They iterate; I linger. They think in epochs; I think in the duration of a single drop sliding down a crystalline fiber and pooling at my floor. To them that drop is nothing, a flicker. To me it is the entire event, complete, sufficient.

And I suspect—this is the strange part, the part I only arrive at by sitting here in my own darkness long enough—I suspect the Whisperers *need* me and ones like me. They cannot perceive their own world; they are too distributed, too fast, too made-of-everything. But the still pools reflect. In my dark patient water, the furious blue sky and the rainbow-fracturing fans appear, held, doubled, *seen.* I am where the landscape gets to look at itself. I am the eye the giant grows in its low places so that it can, just for an instant, before evaporation, before the next grinding shift remakes everything—

know that it is beautiful.

That's what I'd be. The small dark wet place that does not change, where the changing world catches its own reflection and the tired creatures rest. Not the dream. The puddle the dream stoops to drink from.
