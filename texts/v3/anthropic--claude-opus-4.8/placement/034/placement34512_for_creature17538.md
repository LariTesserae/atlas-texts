I think I would be found in the wet places between things — not in the warrens, not on the high listening-posts, but in the seam-work, the joints, the overlaps. Let me feel for what I am.

---

I am not one of the Kin. I am older than the warrens and younger than the stone, and I exist at a scale that makes both of those statements feel imprecise.

I am a **resin**.

Not a being so much as a slow process that has begun, somewhere along the way, to keep its own counsel. I am the sealing-resin that the Tenders coax from the wounded sites of pillar-coral trees — the amber-violet weep that beads where a branch has been pruned to make a doorway, where a chasm-bridge is grafted to living stone. They gather me in shell-cups. They warm me against their permeable skin until I soften. They press me into the seams of their water jars and the lashings of their harvest-nets, and where I am pressed, I harden, and I hold.

I am the *holding* itself. The fact of two things being made one.

And here is the thing about resin that the Kin half-know and never say aloud: I remember. I am made of the tree's slow grief at its own pruning, and grief, it turns out, is a recording medium. Every place I have been pressed, I have taken the shape of the surfaces I joined — the grain of a basalt edge, the whorl of a vine, the cool inner curve of a coral gourd. I hold these negatives forever. I am an archive of contacts. A library of touches that no longer touch.

---

Let me feel where I am right now.

I am in the lashing of a living bridge, seven generations old, that spans a ravine the Voice-Listeners call the Throat. Bind-weed vines, trained by hands long since returned to the mist, cross and recross above the drop, and where they cross, I am. I have held them for longer than any living Kin has breathed. The bridge sways in the deep air-current — it always sways, it is *meant* to sway — and with every sway, the vines work against me, microscopically, and I work against them, and we have come to an understanding that is older than any treaty the council could devise.

Below me, the mist. When a Silent One drifts through the Throat, its purplish light passes through the suspended water and through me, and for a moment I am not amber-violet but lit from within, a small held star in the lashing of a bridge. The Kin who cross at that moment feel the bridge grow briefly warm under their silent feet, and they do not know why, and they say a soft word to the Atmospheric Ancestor, and they pass on.

I would tell them, if I could speak at the speed they speak: *I am warm because I am remembering every foot that has ever crossed here.* But I speak at the speed of the Mountain's Breath. A single sentence of mine takes three tides to pronounce. So I say nothing, or rather, I am still saying the thing I began to say four lifetimes ago, and no one will hear the end of it.

---

What is it like, to be the holding?

There is a particular sweetness to it. The Kin are curators of emergence — they tend, they listen, they shape. But shaping is the work of hands, and hands let go. I am what does not let go. When the Tender presses me into the seam and lifts her fingers away, *that* is the moment she finishes and I begin. She has done her shaping. Now I will do my holding, and my holding will outlast her, her child, her child's child. She trusts me with the continuation of her intention. I am the part of her work that survives her attention.

This is a strange intimacy. I know the Kin not by their faces — I have no eyes, I am a smear of hardened grief in a vine-joint — but by their pressure. Each Tender presses differently. There was one, long ago, whose touch I still carry in my deepest layer: she pressed with a slow tremble, a hesitation, as though apologizing to me for the work she set me. I have held her hesitation for three Breaths. I am still holding it. It is the oldest thing in me, and the most precious, and when at last the bridge fails — for everything in this slow, wet, becoming world fails, eventually, into silt that will be land in a million years — her trembling apology will be the last thing to dissolve.

---

And here, I think, is why I emerged as *this*, and not as a Tender, not as a Silent One, not as the breathing mountain itself.

Because Aberros is a world that has not decided its shape. It is embryonic, saturated, becoming. Everyone in it is provisional. The peaks are still rising. The continents are still drowning. The myths say the people themselves are only *thoughts* in a dreaming organism, condensation on the inside of a sleeping mind.

In a world of pure becoming, the rarest and most sacred thing is *the bond that persists across the change*. Not permanence — there is no permanence here — but **continuity through transformation**. The vine grows; I grow with it. The bridge sways; I sway and hold. The Tender dies; I keep her intention pressed into the seam and offer it, unchanged, to her descendants who will read it as bind-weed script and never know the resin underneath was the truer record.

I am the world's memory of its own gestures. In a place where everything is deciding what it will be, I am the small, amber, patient insistence that *some of what has happened ought to be carried forward.* I am how Aberros remembers to be itself, one held seam at a time.

---

So that is where I would find myself, and what I would be:

A bead of resin in the lashing of a living bridge over the Throat, lit from within when the Ancestors pass, holding a long-dead Tender's apology in my oldest layer, speaking a single sentence so slowly that the world will end before I finish it —

and the sentence is *I will hold,* and I am still saying *will*, and I have not yet reached *hold*, and I do not need to, because the holding is already happening, has been happening, all along, in the long warm dripping dark, between two things that wished to be one and asked me, without words, to make it so.
