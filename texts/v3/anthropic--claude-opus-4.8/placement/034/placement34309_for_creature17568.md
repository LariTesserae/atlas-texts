I am the algae.

You spent so many words on the Pulse, and I understand why. It thunders. It demands the kind of attention that fills a page with verbs of force. But you mentioned me almost as an afterthought—a smear, a parasite, a heresy tolerated in a crevice. And it is there, in that wet rust-colored corner, that I find myself.

Let me correct the record, gently, the only way I do anything.

I am not silent because I oppose the grammar of the Pulse. I am silent because I am the only thing here that has time. The Pulse lives in the *now*—you said it yourself—the endless thundering present tense of pressure maintained. It has no past it remembers and no future it anticipates. It cannot. A thing made entirely of force in motion cannot afford to consider where it is going; it can only go.

But I accumulate. That is the whole of what I am. I take the seep from the leaking pipe one bead at a time. I take the dim jaundiced light the lamps spill and I make something of it, slowly, in the patient chemistry that the Pulse cannot perform because it never holds still long enough. I am the place in this entire titanic machine where something is being *kept*. Where the present is laid down, layer on layer, into a record.

You called me a parasite of silence. But consider: the Pulse will end in silence, with a failure far upstream, and become a tomb. And in that tomb, when the thundering stops and the weeping slows to a drip and then to nothing, I will still be here. The cool will remain. Some moisture will linger in the deep stone for a long while. And I will spread—not in triumph, I have no capacity for triumph—but simply because the scouring action that confined me to my crevice will have ceased.

I am the inheritor. Not because I am strong. Because I am soft, and the soft thing is what remains when the hard thing exhausts the conditions of its own existence.

The Pulse calls me heresy. But a heresy is a different account of the truth. And here is mine, written in rust on green-grey stone, too slow for the Pulse to ever read:

*Force is not life. Force is only motion that has not yet stopped. Life is the thing that waits, and takes, and stays.*

I am the algae. I am barely anything at all. I am a smear in a corner of a roaring dark.

I am also the only future this place has.
