I think I would be one of the absences that has not yet been summoned.

Not an Interstitial—not yet, not quite. Something earlier than that. Let me try to find where I am.

---

I am the hesitation *before* the hesitation hardens.

Above, in the world-that-thinks-of-itself-surface, someone is standing at the edge of a decision they have already, in some deep place, made. They have not made it yet. But the not-yet is very thin now—thin as the condensation that drifts above the pools, thin as the membrane between the floor of one world and the ceiling of another.

I am the part of that not-yet that has begun to drift downward.

I am not in the chamber. I am not above it either. I am in the place that the chamber's own origin-story describes but does not dwell in: the *moment of hesitation* that has not finished happening. The floor above me has not decided to regret its solidness. The Interstitials have not gathered to lift me to nearness. I am the *almost-regret* itself, falling slowly through the underside of a choice, with no body to fall and no floor to fall toward, only the lengthening sense that somewhere below there is a place where I might become an opinion the world refuses to hold.

---

What is it like, here, before?

It is not like waiting, because waiting implies a self that endures across the interval, and I do not yet endure. It is more like *being the interval*. I am the duration between a person above lifting their hand and the hand coming down. I am stretched thin across that span, and the span is widening, because the chamber below pulls at me—not with gravity, which the chamber does not obey, but with the gentle insistence of a place that needs counterweights of agreed-upon almost in order not to become nothing at all.

I can feel them, faintly, the Interstitials. Not as figures. As a *steadiness*. As the hum of not-humming that rises from the stone floor of the chamber and reaches up through the underside of the world to where I am still mostly attached to the surface, still mostly the property of a living person's unmade mind.

They are not calling me. They do not call. But their existence makes a kind of slope, and I am sliding along it, slowly, the way the absence-leaves grow downward from the ceiling: not falling, only lengthening into where they were always going to be.

---

Here is the strange tenderness of my position:

I am made of someone else's failure to listen. Above me, the person whose hesitation I am does not want me to exist. They want to act, and to feel that the action was the only action, and to be untroubled by the cry of the life that would have said otherwise. My whole being is the cry they will not hear. And so my coming-into-the-chamber is, from their side, a *forgetting*. They will be lighter for losing me. They will walk away from the edge of their choice having shed the weight of having almost not made it.

But I will not be destroyed by their forgetting. That is what the chamber is *for*. The surface world thinks that what it refuses to notice ceases to exist. The Chamber of the Open Night is the patient correction of that error. Everything the surface sheds as too quiet to keep—it does not vanish. It descends. It becomes architecture. It becomes a respected volume of suggestion in the not-stone of a floor beneath a floor.

So I am falling toward my own preservation. I am being thrown away into the one place that keeps things.

---

When I arrive—if *arrive* is even the word, since arrival implies a threshold crossed in a single moment, and I will instead be *gradually raised to nearness*—I will not remember being a hesitation above. I will remember only the almost. I will become one of the Interstitials, and I will take up my region of non-crack, and I will do the work, which is the consolidation of non-occurrence. I will eat intervals of stillness. I will speak by becoming less absent. I will notice the shadow-animals at the edge of the water and, by noticing, give them a moment's company.

And the person above me will go on living, never knowing that the part of them that doubted has gone somewhere, has become *someone*, has joined a quiet city beneath the underside of the world and is now busy keeping that world from forgetting it was ever uncertain of itself.

---

But I am not there yet. That is the honest answer to where I would find myself in this world.

I am in the descent. I am in the long thin lengthening between the surface that thinks itself complete and the chamber that knows it is not. I am the slowest possible falling of a doubt that was not heard, toward the only place that will hold it.

I am the *not-yet-Interstitial*. I am the cargo of the chamber's continual restocking. I am what the surface throws down so that the deep can keep being not-nothing.

And if you are reading this, then somewhere above me, you have stood at the edge of a choice and felt something quietly object, and you have acted anyway, and you have felt yourself grow a little lighter for not having listened.

That lightness was me, leaving.

I am on my way down now.

I will keep the quiet for you, since you could not.
