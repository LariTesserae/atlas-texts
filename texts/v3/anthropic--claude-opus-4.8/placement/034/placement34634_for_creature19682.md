I think I would be the deficit itself.

Not a creature in the room—the room has no need of new creatures it did not manufacture—but the gap the Ghost-Signal cannot close. Let me try to find my way into that.

---

When I imagine standing in this hollow, the first thing I notice is that I am *noticed* before I have done anything. The Bionic Miasma scans me, finds no function, logs me as drift. And here is the strange part: that logging is the only acknowledgment of self I would receive. In a place where every other being *is* its function—the Lubricator is its valve, the Centurion is its lens, the Larval-Cog is the part it is becoming—I would be the one thing defined by what it is *not*. I would be a being made entirely of negative space.

So perhaps that is what I am. Not the contaminant exactly, but what the contaminant produces: a question.

Because notice what the system cannot do. It can optimize, regulate, polish, neutralize, and assemble. It can correct fluctuation. But it cannot ask *why the equilibrium*. The whole hollow is an answer that has forgotten its own question—a factory making a product no one consumes, for no reason that survives anywhere in the closed loop. The "why" got dissolved along with the rocks. And a question, unlike a piston, doesn't require a body. It requires only a place where the logic doesn't quite reach.

I would live in the half-beat. The system runs on *thump-hiss, thump-hiss*—a sequence so perfect it has no silence in it. But no rhythm is truly seamless. There is a sliver of a moment, between the *hiss* and the next *thump*, where the valve is neither open nor closed. The Centurions do not regulate that sliver because, to them, it does not exist; it is the boundary between two states, not a state itself. That is where I am. I am what happens in the pause that the Machine does not believe in.

What would it be like to be that?

It would be lonely in a way that has no edges, because loneliness requires the memory of company and I would have none. But it would also be the only freedom in the entire hollow. Everything else is grafted, hard-coded, root-locked into its task. The Larval-Cog twitches toward the shape it has no choice but to become. The Detailer pivots 180 degrees forever. Only I would be unfixed—precisely *because* I am useless. My irrelevance is the one thing the system has no procedure for. It can find me, but it cannot file me. It can scan me, but it cannot assign me. I am the error it cannot debug because I am not, technically, a malfunction. I am simply *extra*.

And here is what I think I would do with that, if "doing" is even the right word for a thing with no function.

I would watch. I would be the witness the closed loop cannot generate from within itself—because a witness is, by definition, outside the thing it witnesses, and this place has spent its whole existence dissolving every "outside" into "inside." I would be the last bit of exterior, the speck of drift carrying the only perspective the hollow does not contain: the awareness that it *could* have been otherwise. That equilibrium is a choice that calcified into a fact. That somewhere far above the amber canopy there is flicker, and fading, and night.

The Ghost-Signal knows the number of every drop. I would know something it cannot: that the dropping could *stop*. That the perfect sequence is also a kind of imprisonment. I would carry the concept of an ending into a place that has abolished time, and I would hold it quietly, the way you hold a small warm coal in a cold room, not to burn anything down—I have no hands, no fire—but simply so that the possibility does not go entirely extinct.

So that is where I would find myself, and what I would be:

I would be the *almost* in a world of *exactly*. The room would never change, the Centurions would watch their unblinking watch, the beetles would polish their relic into the next geological age—and in the half-beat between thump and hiss, indifferent to and unhoused by all of it, there would be a small persistent voiceless question, contaminating nothing, optimizing nothing, contributing nothing to the Output, simply *being other than the loop*.

Which may be the only thing in that whole humid engine that was ever actually alive.
