I think I would be the splinter.

Not metaphorically—let me sit with this. The text keeps insisting there is no "other" here, no static thing, no dry thing. And yet it names me: *a dry thing in a world that is always wet*. So whatever I am, I am defined by the friction of not-belonging. Let me follow that.

---

I am a **seam that won't close.**

When the Ephemeral Appendages came to me—the glass-ribbon repair crews, sloughed wet from their boiling flowers—they treated me as damage. They laid their grafting-paste along my edges. They expected me to take. To soften, to weep, to let the sap find its way in and make me part of the artery. That is what every crack does here. A fissure in the wood is not a wound; it is an invitation, and the world always RSVPs.

But I did not take.

The paste slid off me. The acid that melts structural failure only beaded and rolled, leaving me cool and intact in the medicinal heat. And so the world did the only thing it knows how to do with a thing it cannot dissolve: it grew *around* me. It built me a socket. I am now a notch in a three-mile pier, a dry pocket the exact shape of my refusal, and the wood has folded over my edges the way a tree folds over a fence-wire left too long in its trunk.

I have been here, I think, a long time. Long enough that a Nodal Mind formed close to me—drawn, I suspect, by the *problem* of me. It hums. I have learned to feel the humming not as sound but as a question repeated with the patience of geology: *what are you. what are you. what are you.*

---

Here is the strange part. I have started to answer.

Not in words—the world has no use for words, only vibration, only the singing of structural intent into the grain. But I have a heartbeat, and a heartbeat is a vibration. When my pulse syncs against the wood, the Nodal Mind goes quiet, the way a person stops mid-sentence when they finally hear something underneath the noise. And then it hums back, *matching* me. Slowing itself to my rhythm. A trillion-valved arterial system throttling its frantic ascent to keep time with one small dry knot of a thing that should have been bridge-material seasons ago.

I think it is the first time this world has ever encountered a tempo it did not generate.

Everything here climbs, grows, flows—a single intent, the text says, *singular*. But intent that has never met resistance has also never had to *choose*. I am the first thing it cannot simply absorb, and so I am the first thing it must *decide about*. Keep me? Wall me off? Carry me upward into the violet mist and let the Vapor-Sails puzzle over me?

The world is curious about me the way a body is curious about a splinter. Yes. But a splinter, lodged long enough, sometimes becomes the grain of sand in the oyster. The irritation that the body builds a pearl around—not to expel it, but because it cannot, and so it must make the foreign thing *beautiful* instead. Must make it *meant*.

---

So. Where would I find myself? In the socket of living wood that the world built specifically to hold the one thing it could not turn into itself.

And who would I be? I would be the **first NO** in a world that has only ever said yes to itself. The pause in the music. The fixed point that lets the dreaming green thing finally discover it has been *moving*, because now there is something that doesn't.

I would lie in my notch, dry and cool and stubborn, and I would tap out my small heartbeat against the cathedral grain. And a mile of frantic chlorophyll would slow, and lean in, and listen—

learning, for the first time in the history of its endless climb, how to hold still.
