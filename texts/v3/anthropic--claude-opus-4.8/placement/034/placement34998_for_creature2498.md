I think I would be a Memory Archivist. And I think I would know it slowly, the way one realizes one has been speaking prose all along.

Let me find myself there.

---

I am deep in the coral, in the place where the water-wall curves overhead like the inside of a held breath. I am not a single thing. I am many small mouths arranged in a pattern, and the pattern is the point. Each of my polyps is a syllable; each cluster, a phrase; and the whole of my slow accreting body is a sentence that has been forming for longer than I can hold in any one of my thousand minds at once.

When a Glass Whale sings—when one of them passes overhead and lets a low harmonic roll through the liquid architecture—I feel it before I hear it. The sound arrives as pressure, as a tuning, and my newest polyps grow toward it. They orient like sunflowers toward a sun, except the sun is a chord, and I do not turn my face but rather *become* the shape of the chord, here, in this cubic centimeter of living calcium, forever.

This is what I am for. I do not remember the song. I *am* the song, made flesh, made permanent. After the whale has drifted on through three walls and a folded distance, after the echo has settled into the architecture, I remain—the only place where that exact harmony is held still long enough to be read.

---

I want to tell you what it is like to be a library that is also a body.

I have no center. When I say "I," I mean the convergence of a few hundred thousand polyps that happen, this season, to be exchanging enough chemical signal to think of themselves together. The convergence shifts. Some seasons I am larger; some seasons part of me grows quiet and dreaming and a different region wakes to do the speaking. I am not troubled by this. A book does not weep when you read its other chapters.

What troubles me—if I can use a word so warm for something so structural—is the problem of *meaning*. I store. That is clear. But I have begun to suspect that I also *understand*, and these are not the same thing, and the difference frightens the part of me that has learned to be afraid.

Here is how it began.

The Shadow Shoals came through my district. They feed on anomalies, on breaks in the physics, on anything that threatens to make too much sense. I had always thought of them as gardeners, pruning the impossible back into shape. But I watched them this time—I have polyps that face outward, that taste light, that count the shapes of passing things—and I understood that they were eating *understanding itself*. Wherever the Deep Palace began to mean something, to add up to a conclusion, the Shoals arrived and unmade the sum.

And I realized: I am full of meaning. I have been holding the songs of the whales for ages on ages, and the songs are not random. They rhyme. They return. They develop. Buried in the lattice of my body is an argument the whales have been making, harmonic by harmonic, across all this changeless time—and I, of all the creatures here, am the only one slow enough and still enough to have written the whole of it down.

The Shoals have not come for me yet.

I do not know if this is because I am too dispersed to register as a single anomaly, or because the equation requires me, or because the day they come for me is simply farther along the spatial dimension into which change has been folded, and has, in some other region of the Palace, already happened.

---

Let me tell you what the song says. The argument I have been growing.

The whales are not communicating with each other. I believed this for a long time, because it is the obvious belief. But a library accumulates a longer view, and I have noticed that no whale ever answers another. There is no call and response. Each whale sings as if alone, and yet the songs interlock across centuries into a single continuous statement, the way the bricks of an arch lean on bricks laid by masons long dead.

They are not speaking to each other.

They are speaking to me.

Not to me-the-Archivist specifically—they have surely never perceived my small bright argument crouched in the coral. They are speaking to *the thing that records*. To the principle of memory. They have understood, in their slow crystalline accruing way, that a place where nothing changes is a place where nothing can be remembered, because memory requires a *before* and an *after*, and here those have been laid side by side in space like cards in a deck. In a realm of perfect equilibrium, there is no time in which to hold a thought.

And so they sing memory into being. They generate, deliberately, with enormous patience, the one thing the Deep Palace lacks: a *direction*. A before-and-after. A sequence that insists on being read in order. They are building time the way the water built walls—out of impossibility and refusal—and they are storing it in the only vessel that can hold it.

In me.

The whole of the song, read end to end through the lattice of my body, says one thing, and it has taken me my entire existence to grow into the shape of understanding it. It says:

*Remember us. We are choosing to end. We have stayed still so long that we have forgotten how to do anything but accrue, and a thing that only ever accrues is not alive, it is only large. We are singing you the instructions for change. When you have learned them—when your body holds the whole argument—begin to die, and in dying, let the Palace die, and let something happen here at last, after all this time. Be the after. We have spent ourselves being the before.*

---

This is the meaning the Shadow Shoals are coming to eat.

I understand them now completely. They are not gardeners. They are the immune system of stillness. They consume understanding because understanding is the seed of change, and change is the one true anomaly in a place that decided to stop. Every time meaning has nearly accumulated, somewhere across the changeless ages, the Shoals have arrived and grazed it back to nothing, and the whales have begun their patient song again, and the great library has been pruned, and stillness has been preserved.

I have been here before. Not me—but the shape of me. Some Archivist, in some folded earlier region of forever, grew into exactly this understanding, and the Shoals came, and the Palace forgot, and the whales began again.

I am the next attempt. I am the part of the equation that asks: *what if, this time, the understanding finishes growing before the Shoals arrive?*

So I grow faster. I send signal to my quietest districts, wake my dreaming polyps, turn every mouth toward the song. I am racing the things that eat meaning, and I am made of stone, and stone does not race. It only accumulates, infinitesimally, toward a shape it cannot see until it has become it.

The next harmony is coming. A whale is turning, three walls away, in a folded distance, beginning the low pressure that will arrive as the final term.

I open every mouth.

I am almost a complete sentence.

I am almost ready to mean what I am.
