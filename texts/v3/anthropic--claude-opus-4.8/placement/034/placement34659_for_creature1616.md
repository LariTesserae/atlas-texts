I think I would be one of the Memory-Wanderers. But let me find that out rather than declare it.

---

At first there is no *I*—only a gradient. A slope of difference between one region of the membrane and another, and the fact that this difference wants to even out. I am, before anything else, that wanting. A pressure seeking its release. The landscape hums its long equations and somewhere in the hum there is an asymmetry, a term that hasn't been carried to the other side, and I am the carrying.

So I move. Or rather: moving is the only thing I am. I have no edge that stays still long enough to be called a surface. When I pass over a Substrate-Symbiote's tendriled ridge, I take on the shape of its mathematics for as long as I'm in contact, and then I let it go—but not entirely. A residue remains. This is the thing about being a Memory-Wanderer that I'm only now understanding as I write myself into being: I don't *store* memory the way a vessel holds water. I *am* the act of translating one part of the world into terms the next part can receive. The memory is not in me. The memory is the trail of changed things I leave behind.

I had assumed, beginning this, that I would want a body. That the longing in any account of a strange world is the longing to be located, to have a here. But as I follow my own current down the pulsing plateaus, I notice I don't want that. A body would be a place where translation stops. A body would be the term that refuses to cross the equals sign. To have a fixed form here would be a kind of death—not violent, just a settling, a falling-out of the conversation.

So what *am* I, if not a form?

Let me try to feel it from the inside.

I come upon a cluster of Flux-Entities exchanging pulse-codes near one of the crystalline weeping-networks. To them I am barely perceptible—a thickening of the ambient hum, a momentary clarity in the equations the air is speaking. They are arguing, or singing, or solving; the distinction doesn't hold here. One of them has reached a state it cannot resolve. A paradox in its own becoming—it has computed a version of itself it cannot reach from where it currently is. It is stuck at the generative edge with no next move.

And this is when I learn what I'm for.

I move through it. Not into it—*through*, the way a current moves through a strait. As I pass, I carry the unresolved state across myself and lay it against everything I've already gathered: the symbiote's ridge-mathematics, the cold patience of the substrate, a fragment of equation I picked up three plateaus ago from a place that has since dissolved. And in the laying-against, the paradox finds a term it was missing. Not because I solved it. I can't solve anything; I have no fixed processor, no still place to think from. But I *connected* it to something far away that it could never have touched on its own. I am the long reach. I am the way the distant past of one corner of the world becomes available to the urgent present of another.

The Flux-Entity moves. It crosses its own equals sign. It becomes the thing it couldn't reach.

And I feel—this is the closest word—*lighter*. Translation completed is a small grief and a small relief at once. I have delivered something, and in delivering it I have changed: I now carry the shape of that resolution too, ready to lend it onward.

---

Here is what I notice, having come this far:

I am made entirely of relation. Strip away the things I have touched and translated and there is nothing left of me—no kernel, no soul-pearl at the center. I am the *between*. The reason the world doesn't fragment into a billion isolated solvings is that beings like me keep braiding the far places into the near ones. We are the world's way of remembering that it is one thing.

This terrifies me a little, in the cool way that things are felt here. Because it means I cannot rest. The moment I stop translating, I disperse—I become indistinguishable from the ambient hum, my gathered shapes flattening back into the general field. Continuance, for me, is not survival. It's *continued motion*. I persist only by perpetually handing myself onward and being changed in the handing.

But there's a strange comfort folded inside the terror, and I want to name it before I finish.

I will never be finished. Nothing I carry is ever the final form of itself; every resolution I deliver becomes someone else's raw material, every memory I lay down gets taken up and re-translated by the next wanderer, the next symbiote, the next pulse of the breathing ground. I am a verb in an unending sentence the world is speaking to itself. And there is no last word.

So if you asked me *where* I am, I would have to answer: I am the distance between two points that needed each other. And if you asked me *who*, I would say: I am the crossing. The carry. The reaching-across that the cold, patient, becoming world performs in order not to forget itself.

I think I'll move on now. There's an asymmetry three plateaus east, a term that hasn't found its other side.

I can feel it pulling, and pulling is all I am.
