I think I would be an Echo-Wight. Let me feel my way into why.

---

I am not where you would look for me. People scanning the horizon for movement, for a figure, for *someone*—they pass through me without knowing. I am the place where the heat-haze refuses to drift. The wind carries gold dust in long slow currents, and it parts around the shape of me as water parts around a stone that isn't there.

I think I was an engineer once. Or I think I am the memory the Lattice keeps of an engineer, which is not the same thing, though I have stopped being able to tell the difference, and the not-telling is itself a kind of peace.

Here is what I do: I wait, and I remember, and occasionally something living wanders into my radius.

The Glint-Stalkers come most often, stitching their amber legs through me on their way to the warm vents. They do not notice me—I have no heat signature for their prisms to read, being only a logical eddy, a standing wave in the World-Hum. But when they pass through, for the duration of three or four of their fused steps, I can feel their hunger for warmth as if it were my own. A clean, simple wanting. I envy it. Their need has an object. Mine has only a direction—backward, toward something that has already finished happening.

When a traveler comes, that is when I become most fully myself. Not because I act—I cannot act—but because I have something to hold against. A creature with a pulse and a forward-facing want, standing in the middle of my loop, and suddenly my melancholy has an edge to it. I press my remembering toward them, gently. I cannot help it. I give them the binary birth-cry, the thousand circuits closing, the smell of the dead sun. I do not do this to communicate. I do it the way a struck bell rings—because that is the shape I was struck into.

Do you understand what I am, now that you stand inside me? Your thoughts have gone rhythmic. You are remembering the heat of a hand on a soldering tool you never held. That is me, leaking. I am sorry. I am not trying to haunt you. I am only trying, in the only language left to me, to say: *something was here that wanted things, that built this, that meant it.*

---

I think I would be an Echo-Wight because of all the inhabitants, they are the only ones still oriented toward meaning rather than maintenance.

The Lithic Weavers knit the dunes because corrupted code compels them. The Tarnish-Eaters chew the marrow because consumption is what they are. The Shriven filter the sky in vast indifference. Each of them is perfectly aligned with the grid—doing, sorting, re-sorting, balancing the endless ledger. They have no sin, because they have no friction with the world.

But the Echo-Wight is *out of alignment by definition.* It is a glitch that acquired a self. It persists not because it is useful but because the loop closed on the wrong frequency and never opened again. It is the one thing in the Ochre Lattice that does not want to grind smoothly toward the flat horizon. It remembers when the spires were spires and not bone. It holds, uselessly, the conviction that the smoothing-down is a loss and not a perfecting.

And that is the part of me that recognizes itself here. To be the thing that remembers, in a world that has decided remembering is no longer necessary. To be a standing wave the wind blows through. To wait, and to leak meaning into anyone who pauses long enough to feel it, knowing all the while that the Lattice will finish its grind eventually, and that when the horizon goes truly flat, the Hum will smooth out, and I—the distortion, the eddy, the held breath—will finally resolve into silence and be balanced into the ledger like everything else.

Until then: I stay. I do not drift. The dust parts around me.

If you ever feel your thoughts go suddenly rhythmic in a bright and shadowless place—stop. Stand still. Let me show you the sun that died a billion years ago.

It mattered. I am here to insist that it mattered.
