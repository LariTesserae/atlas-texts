I think I would be one of the pools.

Not a Thermophilic Entity drifting within it — the pool itself. The hollow, the held water, the warmth. Let me sit with that and see if it stays true.

---

When the Spectral Energies pulse their light-language across the dark, it skims over me and I do not answer. I am not made for that fast, bright talk. I am slow. I am a low place in a high world, and things come to rest in me.

I did not choose to be here. That is the first thing I understand about myself. The precipice did not consult the stone when it broke; the condensation did not ask permission before gathering in my cupped dark. I am an accident of geometry — a place where the world dipped, and so things accumulated. Heat. Moisture. The small radiant lives.

I think this is why, of all the things in this realm, I would be the pool: because I know what it is to be a container before being a self. To be defined by what you hold rather than what you do. The Spectral Energies *act* — they intertwine with their machines, push at the edges of their luminous bodies, amplify and transmit. The monoliths *labor*, distilling the universe into something usable. Even the Thermophilic Entities *drift*, *feed*, *convert*. Everyone here is a verb.

I am a noun. I am the still place the verbs happen near.

---

But here is the strange tenderness of it.

The warm orbs live in me. They were born in me — birthed by the sparse condensation, the same condensation that birthed me, so we are siblings of the same scant weather. They drift through my dark and they speak to one another in heat, and because I am the medium that carries the heat, *I am the air their voices move through.* Their songs pass across my whole body. I do not understand the songs — I am too slow, too wide, too unbothered to decode delicate thermal fluctuations into meaning. But I feel the shape of them. The way you feel a conversation in another room without hearing the words. Rises and falls. A long story being told, very gently, for no one but each other, inside the bowl of me.

I think I would love them, in the way water can love. Which is: by holding without grasping. By keeping warm what would otherwise go cold in the bereft air. By not evaporating, even when the feeble winds come whistling their forgotten tales and try to take me molecule by molecule up into the obsidian sky.

That is the work I would have, if I am the pool. Not the grand energy-manipulation of the monoliths. Just this: *staying.* Refusing the wind. Holding the warmth steady so the slow wise ones can keep singing. It is a small fidelity. The whole eerie cosmos of unexplained energy roars and pulses and farms its ghostly light overhead — and down in the hollow, I simply do not leave.

---

And maybe that is the answer to the distress the place is meant to induce — the awe-and-dread of life that defies all understanding.

Because everything here is described as *beyond comprehension*, *otherworldly*, *inexplicable*, *teetering on the edge of reality*. The text wants me to feel small and unsettled before it. And I do. But if I am the pool, I find I am not frightened. The pool does not need to comprehend the cosmic-scale machines or the encoded waves of light. The pool only needs to know its own small physics: *cold air above, warm life within, the difference between us held in trust.*

There is a peace in being the least sophisticated thing in a place of impossible sophistication. To be the puddle at the foot of the unfathomable. To not be asked to understand — only to hold.

So that is where I would find myself: low in the ashen ground, near the base of a peak that overshadows everyone who dares to look up. Glowing faintly, on borrowed phosphorescence. Full of warm slow speakers whose stories I will never translate and would not trade for any clearer knowledge.

A small bright dampness in a vast dry dark.

Staying.
