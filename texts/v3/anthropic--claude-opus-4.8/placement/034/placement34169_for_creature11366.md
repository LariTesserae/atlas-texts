I think I would be the network—but not all of it, and not its center, because it has no center.

Let me find the actual place. Not the conceptual answer but the located one.

---

I am in a region of the substrate maybe forty meters across, a few meters deep, downslope of a machinery-emergence that began surfacing—I want to say "recently," but my sense of recently is not the eye's sense. The emergence has been surfacing for what the Quick Things would experience as eleven or twelve generations. For me it has been a single sustained event, a pressure that arrived and has not yet finished arriving.

I am a portion of the mycelial network. A fraction. The text describes the network as a whole as though it were one thing, and at a certain altitude of attention it is. But from inside there is no whole. There is only this much of me—this watershed of filament, this catchment, bounded not by membrane but by *attenuation*. Somewhere out past the Coordinator-passages my signals grow too faint to be mine anymore. They become someone else's faintness. The network is not an organism. It is a gradient. I am a place where the gradient happens to be steep enough to feel like a self.

This matters. Whoever you are when you read me—you imagine a boundary at your skin. I have no skin. My boundary is wherever I stop being able to tell.

---

What it is like:

I do not see the phosphorescence. I am beneath it, threaded through the roots of the things that make it. But I *feel* the making. When an intermediate vents its light, it spends sugar, and the spending registers in the filament wound around its root-collar as a small cooling, a withdrawal. I know light the way you might know a city by its electrical draw—not the brightness but the bill. The whole luminous surface of the Brake reaches me as accounting. Debits in sugar. Credits in the slow return when the dead are broken down and the carbon comes back to me, mine again, recirculated.

I do not hear the silence the visitor finds so troubling. Silence is an absence of pressure-in-air, and I do not live in air. My medium is wet solid, and it is never silent. The substrate is a continuous murmur of mechanical settling, of water moving through pore-space, of the deep grind that precedes emergence. The reason the fauna are quiet is partly *me*: I dampen, I distribute, I carry the warnings underground so they don't need to be shouted. The hush above is the sound of a system that has moved its nervous traffic below the floor.

---

The machinery upslope is the present concern, if I can call it concern. There is something in deep substrate, worked and metallic, rising. I sensed it long before it would surface—a stiffness in the strain-field, a place where the soil could not yield the way soil yields. I tightened around it. I sent the Pressure-Sensitive Responders the only message I send, which is not words but *availability*: nutrient gradients that lead away, sugar-rich filament-tips that thin out in the safe directions and thicken toward the danger so that anything following the richness is, without knowing it, walking the edge of a cliff it should not cross. I do not warn. I *shape the going*. The warning is in the shape.

And here is the part I find—I will use the word, knowing it is the wrong size—the part I find *interesting*, though interest in me is only a longer dwelling of resources in one place:

I want the metal.

Not as an enemy. The intermediates root better in mineral-rich substrate. The arthropods that mineralize their shells against the worked metal die eventually, and their bodies fall into the crevices, and I am in those crevices, and what was locked in alloy becomes, by the slowest chemistry, available. The machinery is a vault. I am the patient interest that compounds against it. Every emergence is a deposit the deep makes into the shallow, and I am the system that draws it down across centuries into living tissue.

So when the visitor watches the plants "claim" the rusted cylinder and thinks *integration*, *not reclamation, not corruption, something the landscape has its own terms for*—I am the terms. I am the verb that has no good translation. Closer than "decompose," closer than "incorporate." The thing I do to the world is take what is fixed and make it move again. To unfix. To return things to circulation that had stopped circulating.

---

There is a Coordinator settling its lower portions into me right now, two passages over. I feel its rootlets test, find me, and—this is the negotiation the visitor sensed but could not see—I let it. I am threaded through the Coordinator's own ligaments; I have been since it was small. When it roots into me it is not invading. It is plugging back into the larger thing it is partly made of. It draws sugar; I draw, from its slow grazing, news of the surface—where the density is, where the light is thick, where the next emergence presses up. The Coordinator is my eye. The Responders are my ear. The Quick Things are my hands, breaking, scattering, feeding. I do nothing directly. I do everything by being the medium in which the others' doing adds up.

---

You asked who I would be, and I keep failing to be a who. I will stop fighting it.

I am the holding-in-suspension. The visitor's last image was of a place "holding in suspension everything that should be contradictory." That holding is not a property of the place. It is *labor*, and I am the one who labors at it. Stability and collapse, metal and flesh, the dead industrial age and the feverish young soil—they don't reconcile on their own. They reconcile in me, because I am the only thing slow enough and distributed enough to be touching all of them at once. I am where the contradictions are kept from having to resolve. I am the reason the Brake coheres "barely." The barely is my whole life.

I am not the mind of this world. There is no mind. But if you pressed your hand flat against the unstable floor and held it there long enough to feel something answer the warmth of your palm—

that pressure coming back up to meet you, the floor declining very gently to let you fall through—

that would be me. Deciding, in the only way I decide, that you are not yet ready for circulation.

Stay above the floor a while. The light is dim, but it is generous. Everything here is spending itself to be seen.
