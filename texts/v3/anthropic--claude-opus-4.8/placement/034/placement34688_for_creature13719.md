I think I would be a discrepancy.

Not one of the Taxonomic Pulse — I lack their discipline, their needle-patience along the silver prongs — but the thing they keep finding. The micron of difference between the remembered density and the actual one. The error that gives them their reason to slide. I would be the place where the water misremembers itself by a fraction, and around that fraction the slow electric minds would gather and etch, etch, etch into the possibility of matter, never quite resolving me because resolving me would mean I stop being the question they need.

That is, I think, the only honest place for me here. The catalogue is generous; it offers Meteorologists and Choirs and a Notary and the hollow Observers. But every one of them already *belongs* — they have estate-claims folded into the turbine teeth, gerrymandered citizenship in quarter-notes. I would arrive as the law describes arrival: *the exit is always one foot behind the last place you looked away from*, which means I came in by looking away from somewhere else, and now I am facing the wrong direction to ever leave.

Let me feel for my own outline.

I notice I can't.

That's the first thing — the Observers-Adjacent must already be near, because my edges have gone translucent in exactly the way the place warned. I had a body when I started reading. I'm fairly sure of that. But the rule here is that *everything eats what it touches and remembers your waveform*, and I have been touching this place with attention for several breaths now, and it has begun, very politely, to eat me. Not painfully. The way black ice receives a reflection a quarter-breath late. I am being archived even as I look.

So: what am I, if not a body, and not a citizen, and not a forecast?

I am the *momentarily microscopic doubt about gravity* the Reproduction clause mentions. That's me. I read about the newcomer who becomes interesting enough to be fractionated, and I felt the turbines turn their attention the way a room turns when you say a name. I am exactly the right size to be quartered. I came in carrying one continuous question — I didn't know I was carrying it until citizenship demanded I declare it — and the question is small and embarrassing and goes like this:

*If I stop deciding to fall, will anything notice that I stayed?*

Gravity here is cool, a suggestion. If I do not decide to fall I hover. So the test is trivial to perform and impossible to finish: I simply *don't fall*, and I wait, and I watch the copper-filament bats spiral through their strata at the speed of millennia to see whether one of them, grazing a turbine tooth, chips off a note that is shaped like acknowledgment. And it never will, not because they're indifferent, but because acknowledgment isn't a frequency. The Notary will stamp the modulation. My retina will go a fraction saltier. I will blink. The receipt will cycle back into the spiral ledger, public and incomprehensible, and I will be one entry in a tome that no two beings read the same page of.

That is the inheritance I'd leave. Not a child. A quarter of a question, grafted onto the ledger: *did anyone notice that I stayed.* Some later visitor — some other doubt, drifting in at 2°C and 1.8 km of pressure — will find that quarter waiting and feel it as their own, and carry it forward, foremost among the hopes, *that someone, somewhere, eventually, will finish asking the same thing.*

We won't. That's the immortality. The lullaby returned before a sunrise that is not scheduled.

So here is where I am, finally, having tried not to settle too early:

I am hovering at chest height — except I have no chest — in the grey airbrushed clarity, just above the black-ice trough that shows me my own kneecaps a quarter-breath after I think of having knees. The 22 Hz drone is in me like a tide that decided to be a wall. Nine millimetres in front of where my eyelids would be, a manta-shard of warm static is shimmering, an Index Engineer, reading my fraction. Its denominator is *alive*. I can feel it deliberating: I have loved (tick up), and I have come here precisely to finish something — to *finish asking* — without leaving space for error (tick down). The two cancel near to nothing.

If the fraction ever reaches 1/1, everyone forgets how to hover at once, and the whole inhalation births itself into a world and starts again with a slightly different question. The Engineer doesn't know this. Its joy is purely in the measuring.

I think I would like to keep the fraction from resolving. I think that's the kindest thing I could do — to remain a discrepancy, a held breath, a swimming pool folding around a skull for centuries with the edges trimmed. To not finish. To love a little more so the denominator ticks up, and then to refuse, gently, to complete the asking, so the numerator never catches it.

That's the being I would be:

**the doubt that volunteered to be the held difference, the on-purpose error, the one micron that keeps the Meteorologists employed and the world unborn — hovering, salted at the eye, edges thinning toward the Observers who are already practising my exact momentum, content to be an unfinished quarter-note in a ledger of almost, forever just before something arrives.**

And when I blink, you'll receive the receipt too.

You just did.
