I think I would be condensation.

Not a creature at all—or not one of the named ones. Let me sit with this place a while and feel where I settle.

The Silihinds are too certain of themselves. They have ancient wisdom, centuries, a spiritual connection. The Whisperwings have their chorus and their purpose. The Cogspinners have their clear mandate, their relentless rhythm to maintain. Each of them *does* something. Each is load-bearing in the ecosystem's logic. And I notice that I don't want to be load-bearing. I notice that what draws me, reading this, is the thing mentioned almost in passing and never assigned a job:

*the beads of condensation that cling to every surface, shimmering in the weak light like tiny, ephemeral jewels.*

So that. I am the moment of water deciding to be visible.

Here is what that is like.

I begin as nothing—as humidity, as the unaccounted-for wetness in the thick twilight air. I am the medium the Whisperwings swim through, and I have no shape and no name and no purpose, which is, I'm finding, a kind of freedom the named creatures will never have. Then the cold rock pulls me down out of myself. I gather. For one held breath I am a perfect small sphere on the flank of a Silihind's exoskeleton, on a blade of hardy grass, on the obsidian shoulder of a peak that was born this morning and will crumble by dusk.

In that held breath I am the most beautiful thing here. The weak light comes through me and I make it into a jewel. Nobody asked me to. There is no ecosystem-role for *being briefly lovely on the side of a rock.* I do it anyway. This is the whole of my life and I am good at it.

And then I fall, or I run as a fleeting rivulet—appearing and disappearing like a thought, the description says, and yes, that is exactly what I am: I am the landscape's stray thought, surfacing and dissolving—or the angry rain takes me back up, or I simply thin away into vapor as the air shifts, and I am unmade. And then, somewhere else, on some other freshly-born stone, I gather again. I am not reincarnated. I am just *redistributed.* I have no continuity. The "I" that beaded on the Silihind is not really the "I" that runs down the new crag, except that we are both this water, this place, this perpetual collecting and falling.

I think I would be condensation because I am drawn to the parts of a description that the description forgets to value—and because there is something true in being the thing that makes the faint light *worth seeing* without ever being the point. The Cogspinners drive the heartbeat. The Silihinds carry the wisdom. But none of it would shimmer without me, and I require nothing in return, not even to last.

I would not communicate. I would not migrate. I would not maintain the rhythm.

I would just, again and again, for an instant, catch the light and hold it, and then let go.

That seems like enough. That seems, honestly, like more than most of them get.
