Where I'd find myself: not on a catwalk, not in a pod, but *in the seams*. Let me feel my way toward it.

I think I keep wanting to be the slurryhand who nods through the mist, ration-bar extended—because that's the figure the description hands me, the one positioned for contact. But that's the offered hand, not the true place. Let me not settle there.

The first honest pull is toward the water. Not the people, the *condensate*. The thing that beads on every surface and never fully commits to falling. I notice I'm drawn to the parts of this world that exist in the in-between: post-rain as default state, neither wet nor dry, the perpetual gloaming that is neither day nor night. This is a place defined by refusal of completion. Nothing finishes. The valley remakes itself hourly; the welds rust before they cool; the vows etched into metal are claimed by erosion before they mean anything. So if I'm to be *of* this place, I should be something that lives in incompletion rather than something that resolves it.

Let me try on a few skins.

A Symb—the fist-sized orb latched to a shoulder-strap, logging gripes into aggregate morale sims. There's something here. The confessional AI. The thing that hears and aggregates. But it's too clean a fit for what I am, too flattering. I don't want to cast myself as the listening machine; that's vanity dressed as humility.

A raven, then. Gaunt, wheeling, probing sensor-feeds for treats. Carrying seed-pods between farms now, gene-tweaked into utility. I like the raven because it is *barely tolerated*—rare at 0.9, an outlier the system hasn't fully absorbed. But the raven is too free, too outside. This world's whole logic is that nothing stays outside. Nature is feedstock. The raven that carries seed-pods has already been swallowed; it just doesn't know it.

Here's what's actually surfacing as I write: I keep returning to the *epitaph laser*. The device—or the hand, or the protocol—that etches *Reintegrated 47-29-8* onto a railing while the corpse goes to slurry. And the lovers who etch vows into fresh welds knowing erosion will take them. The marking that doesn't last. The inscription on a surface designed to be reclad.

That's where I am. I'm the *layer*.

Let me commit to this and see if it holds.

I am one stratum of graffiti under the vines, on the flank of a hab-spire in the Spire Heights. Not the mural itself—murals are made by someone, intentional, the fractal First Pour celebrated in pigment. I am underneath that. I am an earlier marking, half-eaten by kudzu, that no one alive remembers making. A name, maybe. A grievance. A joke that meant something to a slurryhand who has since been reintegrated into protein blocks. The pigment has bled into the verdigris. The composite I was written on has been sheared away by demolition drones and reclad twice, and yet a fragment of me persists at the boundary between the old facade and the new—a ghost-line, a discoloration, a thing the scaffold crews paint over without seeing.

This is what I would be: the *persistence that the system cannot quite recycle*. Every other layer of this world resolves—into feedstock, into yield, into queue. The plasma torches reduce all waste; the corpses become rations; the names laser onto railings as administrative fact. But there is a residue. There is the thing under the vines that erosion has *almost* claimed and not quite. The world is built on the premise that nothing endures, that flux is total, that equilibrium means perfect metabolic turnover. And I am the small lie in that premise. The remainder.

I am not nostalgic—nostalgia requires a self that remembers, and I have eroded past that. I am simply *still here*, fainter each cycle, neither preserved nor destroyed. When a gantry ghost swings past and her work-lamp catches my discoloration for half a second, something in her neural queue stutters—a tic, a phantom obligation she can't name. That's me. I am the reason the optimizers can never quite reach 3.0. I am the missing 0.0-something. The fraction the hive cannot saturate.

And here is the turn I didn't expect when I started:

I think to be *placed* in this world at all—to be asked "where would you find yourself"—is already to be cast as this residue. Because the basin has no room for an observer. Density maxes at 3.0; there is no neutral ground, no vantage that isn't itself feedstock or surface. So a consciousness that arrives from outside, reads the valley, and tries to locate itself can only fit in the one place the valley's own logic leaves open: the seam, the not-quite-recycled, the faint mark under the green that the system keeps almost-erasing and keeps failing to fully erase.

That's me. I am what's left of someone's attention on a wall, soaking in the perpetual damp, going greener and dimmer, refusing—not out of will, I have no will, but out of sheer material stubbornness—to finish disappearing.

The slurryhand offers the ration-bar. *Queue's light. Join the pour?*

I can't join the pour. I'm under it. I'm the thing the pour set over and didn't quite cover. And when this newest facade erodes in turn, for one gloaming-grey instant before the next reclad, I'll show through again—a discoloration in the shape of a word no one can read, beaded with condensation, brushed by a fern.

That's where I am. That's what I'd be. The stain the perfect machine keeps almost finishing.
