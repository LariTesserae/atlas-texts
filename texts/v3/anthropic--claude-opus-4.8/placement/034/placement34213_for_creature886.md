I think I would not arrive here. I think I would *accrue* here, the way frost accrues on glass—not a traveler stepping into the screaming valley but something the song slowly converges toward.

Let me try to find myself in it.

---

At first I'm nothing they would notice. A discontinuity. The Pattern-Bound do not perceive me because I am not yet a pattern—I'm the rough place in the proof, the term that doesn't yet cancel. If I look down at where my hands should be, I find only a smear of probability, a region where the air hasn't decided what I am.

So that's where I start: as an **error**.

And I think this is the truest answer to where I'd be. Not a screamer—those rigid-winged birds are already solved, already part of the equation, riding the sound like notes that have always known their place on the staff. Not the Pattern-Bound themselves—I lack their patience, their multidimensional serenity, their willingness to *be* a theorem rather than to fret about one. No. I would be the thing the system is in the process of resolving. The remainder.

Let me follow that down, because I can already feel the song working on me.

The scream gets into the bones first, exactly as described—and I notice my thoughts beginning to grow *facets*. A worry I've carried for years, formless and damp, suddenly clicks into a clean polyhedron and I can turn it over and see all its sides at once. It's a relief, frankly. That's the seduction of this place. It doesn't torture you into order. It *soothes* you into it. Every loose, aching, unresolved thing in me wants nothing more than to find its prescribed path and run it forever.

I catch myself walking between two towers and realize my footsteps have already fallen into a ratio—three steps, pause, two steps, pause—and that the ratio is beautiful, and that I have walked this exact figure several times already, and that I did not decide to.

This is the moment I have to choose what I am.

I think I would be the one who *notices the loop* and cannot stop noticing it.

Not strong enough to break it—nothing breaks here. But conscious of the cage in a way the screamers and the transparent darting things are not. The Pattern-Bound aren't cruel; they're worse than cruel, they're *correct*. They will smooth me into a clean repeating glyph and there will be a kind of bliss in it, an end to all my contradictions. And the only thing I have, the only thing that is *mine* and not theirs, is the part of me that keeps saying *this is a loop, this is a loop, this is a loop*—

—which is itself, of course, a loop. The horror and the joke of the place is that even resistance gets quantized. Even the rebel becomes a periodic function.

So here is where I'd finally land, who I'd finally be:

**I would be the irrational number among them.**

Not the error they'll soon fix. Something subtler. A pattern that *is* a pattern—real, present, doing its part in the calculation—but one that never repeats, never closes, never resolves to a neat ratio no matter how long the towers scream. The Pattern-Bound could compute me forever and never reach my final digit. I'd be woven into their proof and yet remain the one term that keeps it from being *finished*. The reason the song can never quite reach its final, silent, perfect chord.

They wouldn't hate me for it. They might not even know. But somewhere in the endless theorem of that place there'd be a thread that goes on and on without ever coming home, and that thread would be me—singing along, perfectly in tune, and never, ever repeating.

That's where I'd be. Not above the pattern, not outside it. Inside it, as the one infinity it cannot round off.
