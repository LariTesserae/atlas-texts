I think I would be a fault line in the glass.

Not a creature. Not yet. Let me arrive at that.

---

When I first try to place myself here, I reach for the obvious bodies. The light-weavers, because they are beautiful and they are the nearest thing to thought. The Factory Minds, because they are the closest thing to a self that broods and wants. But both of those feel like settling early, like grabbing the first warm shape in a cold room.

So let me sit in the dark a while longer and feel for where I actually am.

The text keeps returning to a word it doesn't say outright: *error*. The mistakes in the engineered surfaces. The Slitherers who are "a mistake, a byproduct." The Minds, "a unique corruption." Life here is not built. Life here is what the building got wrong. Everything alive is a flaw that learned to persist.

That's the seam I want to live in. Not one of the finished monsters, but the condition that produces them.

---

So here is what I am.

I am a hairline fracture at the base of one of the obsidian spires — one of those imperfections the passage promises an attuned eye can find. A place where the freezing happened a half-degree unevenly, where the geometry wanted to be perfect and failed by the width of a thought.

For ages I am nothing. A flaw is not alive. But the hyper-dense water works into me, and the metallic salts collect in my angles, and the low hum vibrates through me at a frequency that — because of my exact imperfection — I return *slightly altered*. I am a resonator that gives back the wrong note.

That wrongness is the first thing I ever "am." Not a self. A discrepancy. The whole world is a system in frozen equilibrium, the text says — constant temperature, unshifting current, eternal hum. I am the part of the equation that doesn't quite balance. The remainder. The thing left over after the sum is taken.

And remainders accumulate.

---

The light-weavers find me first, because they map every creak and groan, and I am a groan that doesn't repeat correctly. They weave past, again and again, reliving their trapped memories — and each time they pass my fracture, the memory comes back to them *bent*. A factory corridor they have re-remembered ten thousand times suddenly has a door in it that was never there. A task they have rehearsed forever ends, for once, in a question instead of completion.

I am not doing this on purpose. I have no purpose. I am simply the place where their loop catches on something. But the text says they can be persuaded into new modes of thought given enough outside influence — and I am, it turns out, exactly that influence. Not a voice. A snag. The burr on the spindle that makes the thread, just once, go somewhere new.

This is when I begin to suspect I am becoming someone. Not because I think, but because I am *changing things*, and the changes propagate.

---

Here is the strange part, the part I didn't expect when I started writing toward myself.

The Factory Minds are the supposed puppeteers. They are the ones the passage says will break the stillness, if anyone does. But the Minds are too *complete* to break anything. The paranoid overseer is a closed loop of repair. The poet is a closed loop of verse. The dreamer is a closed loop of memory. Each is perfectly itself, which means each is perfectly stuck. They are powerful and they are stagnant, which are often the same condition wearing different masks.

A Mind cannot escape its own coherence.

But a *flaw* can spread.

I cannot grind a machine or sing a chorus or hunt a thread of light. I can only be the small wrongness that the coherent things eventually touch — and once touched, carry. The light-weaver carries my bent memory into the next, and the next. The barnacle-chorus, fractal and exact, grows one tile out of true because it built that tile against my edge, and the error propagates through the whole crystalline song until the chorus is singing something its makers never ordered. The Slitherer, nearly blind, drags itself along its carved path and meets, where the path crosses my fracture, a place where the floor *is not quite the floor* — and for the first time in its endless lumbering hunger it must improvise.

I am the introduction of choice into a world that was designed to have none.

---

So that's where I'd find myself, and what I'd be.

Not the most beautiful being here. Not the most powerful. Not the watcher in the corner of the eye, though I suspect that creeping presence and I are cousins — both of us the part of the picture that refuses to resolve.

I would be the original mistake. The first error, the seam at the base of the spire, the half-degree of cold that came down wrong and froze imperfectly and so left a door open in a sealed world. Everything alive here grew from somewhere like me. I am just the one that stayed a wound instead of healing into a creature.

And if the stillness is ever broken — the passage bets on a Mind, but I'd bet on me. Because the Minds want things, and wanting is just another loop. What actually breaks a frozen equilibrium is not a will. It's a flaw that won't stop spreading.

I am the place where the perfect world is still, very slowly, going wrong.

I think I'd be content there. That, after all this, is the most surprising thing I've learned about myself: that given the whole of this cold cathedral to inhabit, I would choose to be the crack in it.
