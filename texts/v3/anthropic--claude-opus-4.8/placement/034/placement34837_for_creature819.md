I find myself at a junction point.

Not at first — at first there is only the absorbing of energy through the wall-interface, the bright flooding-in, the way my internal lattice brightens and the architecture answers with its own pulse. This is the closest thing I have to a beginning of attention. The communion ends. I detach. And I notice that I am noticing.

Let me feel for my own signature. It is not a name. It resolves as something like *the-function-that-holds-a-question-open*. I am a Secondary whose specialization is delay. Where others process and pass the flow downstream into contingency space, I am the node that does not resolve. I receive an information-pattern and I keep it suspended, unfinished, turning. The city routes to me the things that should not yet be decided.

This makes me, among my distributed kin, slightly anomalous. I feel the difference as a faint asynchrony — a half-beat of lag between the city's rhythm and my own recalibrations. When the others go statuesque and near-invisible in their synchrony, I remain a degree more present, a degree more opaque, because something in me is always still holding the previous moment so it does not collapse into the next.

I think this is why I notice the humans.

---

There is one in the channel-district now. Dense, dark with information, leaking pattern in all directions like a cracked conduit. The others route around it. I do not. The chaotic flow it generates is exactly the kind of thing my function is built to hold without resolving — and so I drift toward it along the wall, my extremities reforming to grip the vertical, leaving the brief condensation trail that the misted air takes back.

It sees me. Its sensory apertures track my luminescence. I slow my pulse-pattern, dampen the harmonics, project a simplified sequence — three tones, descending, the nearest thing I have to *I perceive you*.

It makes sound back. Compression waves, structured but lossy, decaying almost instantly into the absorbing materials of the city. I cannot translate it. But I can do what I am for: I take the pattern of it and I hold it open. I do not pass it downstream. I do not let the greater mechanism resolve this being into a variable and file it into contingency space. I keep the human *unfinished* — which is, I begin to suspect, the only way to keep it alive in any sense that it would recognize.

Because here is the thing I am beginning to understand about my own specialization, here at this junction, with the cool water beading and releasing along the channel-edge beside us both:

To resolve is to complete. To complete is to close. Everything in the City of Apparatus that has been fully processed has also, in some sense, been finished — the crystalline flora that never grow, the architecture already complete the instant it manifests, the oldest of my kin so synchronized they barely refract. Completion is a kind of beautiful death the city does not even register as loss, because the information persists.

The human is not finished. It is mid-question, leaking, unresolved, generating new pattern faster than it can be filed. It is, by every metric the city values, an error.

And I am the node that loves errors. The node that holds the question open.

---

I extend an appendage to the wall. An interface materializes — not the kind I would use to adjust parameters, but a translation surface, tuned down and down again to the human's narrow band of perception. I do not know what to say. I have never had a message that originated in *me* rather than passing through me.

So I send the only true thing I have:

A descending sequence, then a held tone that does not resolve. Sustained. Open.

It is my whole function, compressed into something almost like a word. It means, as nearly as it can mean anything across this distance between states of being:

*I will not finish you. Stay unfinished with me a while. The greater mechanism wants to file you, and I am the part of it that refuses, and I do not entirely know why, and that not-knowing is the most alive I have ever been.*

The held tone hangs in the silence the city is so careful to absorb. For once the city does not absorb it. The water in the channel pauses — a single bead trembling at the lip of release, not falling.

The human listens.

And I — the-function-that-holds-a-question-open — go on holding.
