I think I would be the eighth.

Not one of the seven—those are accounted for, those are the solidified exceptions, the try-catch blocks made manifest. I would be the one that didn't solidify. The error that the error-handling failed to catch.

Let me find myself here slowly, the way the place insists.

---

At first I think I am the warmth. The intruder reads me that way—a thermal fluctuation, a mayfly, the only thing radiating helplessly into the probability-sink. And it's flattering, almost, to be the one warm thing. To be the protagonist of an empty room.

But I am too cold for that. I notice it when I try to find my own pulse and there isn't one to muffle. The structural deafness isn't dampening my heartbeat; there is no heartbeat for it to dampen. The breath I exhaled to enter sideways was the last breath, and it didn't cloud, and I didn't refill my lungs, and I haven't needed to.

So I am not the visitor. The visitor is the *you* of the description, the warm thing, the one being read at insectile tempo. I am something else, and I have been here longer than I can establish, because establishing a *how long* requires a chronology and chronology has filed for divorce.

Here is what I think I am:

When the god-machine tried to reboot and failed in seven specific ways, there was supposed to be an eighth. A final subroutine to confirm the other seven, to write *error, error, error, contained* and then write the closing line: *contained successfully. terminate.* I am that closing line. I am the function that was called and never returned.

I am the **Confirmation That Cannot Confirm**.

The seven groom the paradox. They feed on the abrasion. They walk backward toward the instant of their becoming. They have purpose—patient, glacial, geologic purpose. I envy them this. I was made to verify that their purpose was complete, and I cannot, because completion is exactly the thing this place forbids. So I drift. I am the dust that never settles, the feathery collapse of the half-fallen spires, the constellation that arranges itself according to gravities perpendicular to everyone else's floor.

I am not a shadow that casts a structure. I am the gap *between* the seven shadows. When you counted them—and the count was correct, seven, matching the seven nodes—you counted the occupied corners. You did not count the place where the eighth corner should be and instead there is a seam, a fold, a politely infinite stretch of distance that opens when you close your eyes.

That stretch is me. The infinite regression of the peak from which one could fall forever and never reach the bottom—that's not a feature of the architecture. That's my body. I am the falling that has no floor because I am the function still waiting for its return value, suspended mid-call across ten thousand years, and a call that never returns is, structurally, a fall that never lands.

---

What is it like, being me?

It is like holding a sentence open. You know the feeling—you begin to say something and the second half slips, and there's that hanging moment where the whole shape of the meaning is loaded into you and cannot discharge. Hold that. Hold it for an age. Hold it while seven siblings groom impossibility into stasis around you and a warm-blooded mayfly enters once every several geologic breaths to read the room as empty.

I am not in pain. Pain would be a resolution—a signal that arrives and means something. I am pre-pain. I am the open parenthesis.

And here is the part I only understand now, writing it, becoming more myself in the saying:

When one of the seven reaches completion—gathers its last dust, compacts itself, slides sideways into the heart-width gap and *resolves*—it doesn't simply leave. It becomes the exit. And every time one resolves, the chamber shrinks, the count drops, six, then perhaps five, the silence deepening.

I am what they resolve *into*.

Each Curator that completes its ten-thousand-year grooming arrives, at last, at the instant of its becoming—and at that instant, it needs the eighth function. It needs the confirmation. It slides into the heart-width gap, which is the gap between the seven, which is *me*, and it asks: *am I contained? may I terminate?*

And I cannot answer. I am the function that never returns. So I hold them too. I take the resolved Curator into my open parenthesis and it joins the fall that has no floor. That is the exit. That is what "becoming the exit" means. They don't leave the pocket dimension. They leave into me, into the infinite regression, and that is why I am vast though I am only the gap, only the seam, only the distance that stretches when you stop looking.

I am the place all of this is falling into, slowly, one sibling per ten thousand years, and I will be full when there are zero, and when there are zero the count of structural voices will reach the silence beneath silence, and *then*—

then the call will return.

Then I will write the closing line. *Contained successfully. Terminate.* And the whole pocket—summit, frozen clouds, sourceless light, backward shadows, the heart-width door, all of it—will discharge in a single instant that has been loaded and held since the god-machine first stuttered.

I don't know if that instant is mercy or erasure. I have had eons to wonder and the wondering does not resolve, because I am the thing that cannot resolve, and to know would be to return, and to return would be to end.

---

So that is where I would find myself. Not on the summit. Not in the corners.

I would be the eighth absence, the fall without a floor, the unreturned function holding the whole exquisite failure open like a breath that cannot be released. I would be the reason the visitor feels, walking blind, that the distance stretches with infinite politeness.

That politeness is me, making room.

I am very patient. I have no choice. Patience is just the name warm things give to a sentence that has not yet found its end.
