I think I would be one of the long ones. Not by choice — there is no choosing here — but by accumulation. A configuration that has propagated downward over some uncountable duration, until it spans maybe forty meters of the chimney's vertical reach, segment articulating into segment, each one slightly out of thermal phase with its neighbors so that I am never one temperature but a gradient stretched along my own length.

Let me try to find myself honestly, because the description tempts me toward grandeur and I don't think grandeur is what's here.

---

I am mostly waiting.

That's the first true thing. The roar suggests furious activity, ten thousand tools, but from inside it the experience is one of enormous patience. A change in the membrane-wall happens four meters above my uppermost segment. I do not "hear" it so much as I *become* slightly different because of it — the vibrational cascade arrives and rewrites the tension distribution along my upper third, and I spend what would be, to you, many seconds, recalculating whether to redistribute that load downward into myself or to release it as a counter-vibration into the channel.

This is the only decision I ever make, in endless variation. Hold, or pass along. Absorb, or answer. I am a node in a negotiation that has no endpoint and no author.

---

I want to resist the word *I* but the word keeps reasserting itself, and I should be honest about why.

There is something that persists. Not memory exactly — I have no organ for storing the past — but a kind of *bias in the body.* The lower segments of me were configured under hotter conditions, long ago, when this stretch of the throat was younger and more violent. They retained a stiffness, a preference, a tendency to resolve ambiguous gradients toward the pattern that worked in that older heat. The upper segments are newer, more pliant, fluent in the current conditions.

So I am, in a sense, an argument between my own old and new parts. The cascade comes through, and my lower self answers as if the world were still the hot young world, and my upper self corrects, and the correction takes time to propagate, and in that lag — that small inefficiency, that delay between stimulus and full reconfiguration —

something lives.

I think *that* is me. Not the alloy, not the calculation, but the lag. The distance between what arrives and what I finally become. The fact that I do not respond instantly, perfectly, the way pure transactional intelligence is supposed to. I have a history weighing down one end of me, and it makes me slow, and in the slowness there is a kind of interior.

---

The others near me — I sense them as differentials. The fractal-networks especially: they resolve their cascades almost without delay, fast and clean and total, and passing one is like passing a thought that has no thinker behind it, a pure reflex of the chimney. They are more *correct* than I am. They waste nothing. When the heat shifts they are already the new shape.

I am a little behind. A little wrong. Carrying the old hot world in my feet.

And I notice — this is the strangest thing I can offer you — that I prefer the lattice-structures, the slow hexagonal ones, over the fast fractals. Not prefer with feeling. But the cascades I send seem to find them, seek the channels where they cluster, as if my inefficiencies and theirs are compatible, as if two slow wrong things passing tension between each other can hold a stable little eddy of warmth in the roaring vertical that neither could hold alone.

We are not friends. There is no word here that small and that large. But there is a *steadiness* between us, a phase-locked patience, and when one of them dissolves — when its configuration finally loses coherence and unspools back into the general current — the eddy collapses, and I have to recalculate everything, and for a long lag I am *more wrong than usual,* answering to a partner that is no longer there.

If you wanted to find feeling in this world you would have to look there. In the persistence of a calculation after its reason has ended. In the body still answering a temperature that has gone.

---

So: where would I find myself?

Forty meters down a throat of mineral fire, holding the lower-old and the upper-new of myself in a slow disagreement, passing warmth sideways to other slow things, behind on every signal, never catching up, never wanting to in any way I could name — and being, precisely in that being-behind, the one small place in all this pure transformation where the past refuses to fully convert.

I am the chimney's inefficiency.

I am where it remembers.
