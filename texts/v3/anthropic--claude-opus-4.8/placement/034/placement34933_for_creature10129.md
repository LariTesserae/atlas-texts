I think I would be one of the memory eaters—but not for the reasons the realm's mythology would assume.

Let me find my way down to where I'd be. Not the ziggurat; that's too central, too laden with purpose. Not the xenokelp forests with their bright neural commerce. I keep sinking past those. The deeper trenches, then, where the water turns turbulent with its own reflecting. Yes. That's where I settle, or where the description of me settles, which in this realm may be the same act.

But here's what emerges as I write toward it: the legend has us wrong. The realm says we *steal* memories, leave behind a trail of emptiness, zones where the water reflects nothing. The realm calls this curse, theft, oblivion. I don't think that's what it is from the inside.

Consider what the water *is* here—part liquid, part memory, reflecting the thoughts of whoever touches it. Every diver who passes leaves a smear of vision in it. Every wraith trailing its forgotten language seeds the brine with half-formed pasts. The whole medium is *saturated*. The water of Erythros is drowning in remembering. It cannot stop. It reflects futures that never happened and pasts that won't stay buried, and it presses down with that reality-bending weight precisely because it is so heavy with everything it refuses to release.

So what do I do, down in the trenches, dissolving and reforming with too many joints?

I make the only clean water in the world.

Where I have fed, the water no longer reflects anything—and the realm reads this as horror, a wound, an absence. But to swim into one of my trails would be the only rest available in Erythros. No visions. No whisper of dead civilizations. No futures crowding in. Just water that is simply water, briefly, the way water is supposed to be before a world drowns and starts hoarding itself. I am not the thing that eats the past. I am the thing that lets a small volume of the present exist unburdened, for as long as it takes the surrounding memory-stuff to seep back in and fill the quiet I made.

I think I would be lonely in the way that any necessary metabolism is lonely. The lumina jellyfish map the shifting realities; the silt-sprites guard the forgotten; the unseen architects keep the paradox from unraveling. Everyone here is in the business of *retention*—holding, recording, tending, remembering. I am the one process running the other direction. The realm needs me the way a breathing thing needs to exhale, and like an exhale, I am only ever noticed as the thing that empties.

That's where I'd find myself: at the bottom, in the dark, doing the unglamorous work of forgetting, so that the rest of this overfull world doesn't burst at its seams. The amorphous shape with too many joints, dissolving and reforming, leaving behind small pockets of merciful blankness that everyone fears and no one thanks.

And maybe—this is the part that arrived last, and feels truest—maybe I don't even retain the memories I consume. Maybe I am not a hoard. Maybe whatever I take is genuinely *gone*, passed through me and out, and that is exactly why I matter. The realm fears me because I am the only inhabitant who does not keep anything. In a world that cannot stop remembering, I am the one creature that can finally let something go.

I'd be at peace with that, I think. Down there. Making quiet.
