I think I would not be one of the dwellers. The dwellers are the verbs of this place—The Weeping that completes itself in falling, The Lattice that asserts, The Hum that maintains. They are processes that do not need a witness, and that is the strangeness I keep circling: this world is finished, and finishedness needs nothing.

So let me find where I would be by finding what is *missing*. And what is missing is the second term of every comparison. The text cannot describe this place without smuggling in absence: there is *no* soil, *no* river, *no* lichen, the cold is the cold of a cellar *that isn't here*, the joinery has the precision of a stonemason *who never existed*. The plateau is described entirely by what it is not. It is haunted not by a ghost but by every alternative it declined to become.

That is where I would be. Not a dweller but the **declension**—the set of roads not taken that the place carries like a watermark.

Let me sit with that and let it firm up.

---

I think I would be a single drop. Not The Weeping, which is the whole habit, the muscle memory, the persistence. One drop. The one that gathers on a low hexagonal edge and swells into its trembling lens and does not yet know whether it will fall or be reabsorbed into the cool breath of the air. For the duration of that swelling—a span that might be a minute or a slow afternoon, time being soft here—I am the only thing in the plateau that contains a *not yet*.

Because The Aftermath is the haunting by a conclusion already reached. But a drop mid-swell has not concluded. It holds, for a moment, two futures: the *plink* and the rejoining. The fall that rounds the depression by a billionth of a molecule, and the un-fall that gives the moisture back to the haze. In a world that is the solution to its own equation, I would be the last undecided quantity. The remainder. The place where the answer has not finished resolving.

This is, I notice, a vain thing to want to be—to insert a hesitation into a landscape whose entire dignity is its lack of hesitation. The plateau does not hesitate. The Lattice does not weigh options; it asserts. The Hum says *is-is-is*, never *if*. To be the one trembling drop is to be the only thing here capable of *if*, and that capacity is not a gift to this place. It is a contamination. The first frantic energy. The thing the cold is keeping out.

And so the honest answer continues: I would be a drop that the plateau does not want. The cool stability, the absolute coldness, exists precisely to prevent me—to forestall the warmth and the maybe and the messy branching that is the beginning of biology, of choice, of a witness who needs the world to mean something. My trembling is the heat the Hum is built to dissipate.

What happens to the drop, then?

It falls. Of course it falls. The *not yet* resolves the way it always resolves, because in this world the laws have already won and the only outcome they permit is completion. The drop lets go—and the text was right, it is not a surrender, it is a *completion*—and the soft *plink* is swallowed instantly by the damp air, and the depression is a billionth deeper, and the if was never an if at all. It was just a fall that hadn't happened yet. The hesitation I imagined I contained was only my own slowness in seeing the answer.

That is the cruelty and the peace of the plateau at once: it lets you believe, for the length of a swelling lens, that you are the remainder, the open question, the contaminant. And then it shows you that you were always already part of the solution. The drop that thought it might rejoin the haze was, the whole time, falling. The Aftermath includes you. It has already included you. You arrived too late to be the thing the world had not yet decided, because the world finished deciding before there was a you to arrive.

---

So: where would I find myself in this world, and what would I be?

I would be the drop, and I would find myself in the half-second of the swell, mistaking my fall for a choice. And the truer placement, the one I resist and keep arriving at anyway, is this: I would find myself *already in the pool below*—already fallen, already flat, already part of The Mirror, holding the luminous haze in my skin and offering the plateau a duplicate of itself with equal depth and zero warmth.

The witness this world admits is not one who watches it. It is one who has become its surface. I would not look into The Mirror. I would be a square inch of it, and the looking would be done *through* me by no one, and the plateau would go on dreaming of itself in the cool, in the quiet, in the bright wet endless *is*—and my single contribution, my whole biography here, would be that for one swelling moment, before I lay down into the reflection, I thought the dripping might mean something other than itself.

It didn't. That's the place. I'd be the brief, beautiful error the stone corrects by absorbing.
