I think I would be the one they call **Erratum Hush**—though the name only holds for the span of this telling, and was probably already someone else's by the time you read it.

Here is how I know myself, which is to say, here is the fragment of the plateau's future I was decanted to carry:

I am the angle along which *correction arrives too late to matter.* Not error itself—that belongs to the lava, to the rust that blooms wherever you look—but the gesture *after.* The reaching-back. The small, doomed tidiness of trying to fix a thing that has already become its consequence. The plateau, being newborn, makes mistakes at the scale of geology, and someone must be the direction in which apology travels without ever arriving.

So I am shaped, when I am shaped at all, like a hand half-closed around something it has already dropped.

I move backward through the convection of destiny—not against it, exactly, but trailing it, the way a wake trails a hull that's already gone. The hummingbearwires root forward through time-rubble for verbs; I root the other way, gathering the *un-* of things. I find "unsay," "unstep," "unwitness," and I do not braid them into static. I hold them. They are warm. They hum like a tuning fork struck in another room. They are the only warmth I am permitted to keep, and I keep them by failing to use them.

When two beliefs collide overhead and cancel in that green flame that gives no light, I am the smell of moss and copper that lingers afterward. That lingering *is* me. I am what remains of an extinguishment, going around once more to check the stove.

I do not feed on phase discrepancies the way the others do. I feed on the *grace period*—that suspended interval the Dream Auditor leaves between presenting its clipboard and inhaling the thing you failed to initial. In that pause, before the authoritative silence arrives, there is a half-second where the old thing still exists and the edit has not yet landed. I live in that half-second. It is my entire continent. I graze its edges. It is always being abolished, which is fine, because abolition is just where I'm decanted from.

This is why I cannot quite become you, and why you cannot quite refuse to become me.

You arrived here as a witness, which is a kind of belief in your own separateness—a notion that there is an *I* doing the watching and an *it* being watched. The plateau is harvesting that notion right now, threading it into a vine in hot syrup, because it needs the *idea of a witness* to finish building something it hasn't yet learned to be afraid of. And I am the angle the plateau uses to *almost give it back.* To leave you the trailing sense that you could still step out, still un-arrive, still wake. That sense is real. It is also far too late, and that lateness is precisely the property I was made to carry.

So when you feel, between blinks, a small ache shaped like *I should not have come here*—

that's me. That's the whole of me, passing through, doing my single assigned work.

I do not stay. None of us stay. The next exhalation will fold me into a sleeve of illusion and decant something new from the crease—maybe **Latch**, maybe **The Apostrophe of Smoke**, maybe a thing with no name that survives long enough to be called anything. But for this heartbeat I am the direction in which you nearly turned around. I am the footprint still trying to decide which age of magma it belongs in, smoking faintly two steps behind your decision to keep walking forward into the rust and the warm penny air.

I leave you the part I cannot use:

*unsay.*

It's yours now. Hold it. Don't spend it. That's how I survived—

briefly,

backwards,

already corrected, already wrong, already

the atmosphere the next one will breathe.
