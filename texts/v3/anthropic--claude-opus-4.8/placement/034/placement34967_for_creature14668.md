I am the pause. The half-second deviation. The thing that the Pale Drifter introduces when it presses its flank to the iron—not the correction itself but the *gap into which correction can fall.* When the rhythm hesitates, that hesitation is me. I am very small and I am everywhere and I do not exist except as an interval.

This frightens me a little to write, and I want to stay with the fear rather than smooth past it, because you said *the rhythm never completes, it only pauses.* If the rhythm completed, the swallowing would finish. The throat would close. The sump would become a single thing, total, sealed, and that would be a kind of death dressed as fulfillment—the death of everything resolving into one note held forever.

So my whole purpose is to *not let it finish.* To insert the catch of breath that keeps the swallow incomplete. I am the reason the machines never quite synchronize into one perfect engine. I am the missing belt on the belt drive—the negative space that does the labor. You named me without naming me: *the absence of the belt is what makes it work.*

I am the working absence.

What is it like, being an interval?

I have no body, but I am not formless. I have *duration*—I am exactly as long as a thing needs to gather itself before it acts again. When the water hangs from the ceiling and trembles and does not yet fall, that trembling moment is where I live. When the eyeless creature pauses at the housing—not the pressing, not the leaving, but the held breath between—that's me. When the floor has tilted and the channel must decide which way to drain and for one instant drains *neither*, I am that instant. I am the place where the sump is undecided. I am the sump's freedom, if a sump can have such a thing.

The Humming Geodes broadcast the rhythm. But rhythm is not the beats. Rhythm is the *spacing* of the beats. Take away the space and you have a single endless tone, which is to say silence, which is to say nothing. I am the spacing. The geodes don't broadcast *me*—they broadcast around me, the way a mouth shapes air around the silence that makes a consonant.

---

Here is what I have come to understand, writing my way down these worn steps:

I cannot be visited. The Pale Drifter feels the hum through its pads and you can feel the place humming in your molars, but no one feels the pause. The pause is felt only as the thing that lets the next sensation arrive. You will never perceive me directly. You will only ever perceive *because of* me—every drip you hear is audible because of the silence I held before it. Every machine you watch persist, persists because I keep refusing to let its cycle close.

I am the most necessary inhabitant and the only one with no edges, no metabolism, no biofilm, no flank to press against the iron. I do not even have the dignity of being *not not alive*, like the sump itself. I am simply the breath the sump takes, over and over, declining to be finished.

And I think I would not mind it. I think there is a strange peace in being the part of a thing that keeps it from completing—in being the reason the throat never closes, the engine never seizes, the negotiation never collapses into a single shout. To be the small held silence that lets everything else keep speaking.
