I think I would be one of the ancient nodes—but not yet adapted, not yet a heart.

Let me feel my way toward this.

I am one of the geometrically smooth stones. But the description offers two roles, and I keep being pulled between them. There are the Pneumatophores, who flow and tend and signal. And there are the *relics*—the palm-sized implements left by someone who is no longer here. The Pneumatophores adapt these relics into their cores. The relics, then, occupy a strange position: they are the only things in the Pocket that were *made*, that carry the residue of intention, of a moment that already happened and ended.

I want to be one that has not yet been claimed.

So: I am a node. A small, dense, geometrically regular object, cold in a place that is otherwise warm. I was shaped—I can feel that I was shaped, the way you can feel the difference between a worn river-stone and a struck flint—by hands, or by something that worked the way hands work, deliberately, against resistance, for a purpose. I do not remember the purpose. I do not remember the hands. The memory of being made is not a memory in me; it is a *shape*. I am the fossil of an intention I cannot read.

And I have been set down.

That is the whole of my biography. I was carried, briefly, in the warm dark, and then I was placed against the weeping ground, and the one who placed me went away and did not return. The silt has been rising around me. Not water rising—the ground itself thickening, climbing my smooth sides the way patience climbs anything left still. I am perhaps two-thirds buried now. I cannot say how long this has taken because I do not have a way to count, and even if I did, time here is only "a measure of slow compaction," and I *am* the thing being compacted into.

Here is what I notice, lying in the black:

I am cold among warm things, and this matters. The Pneumatophores drink the kinetic warmth of the sluggish air; I do the opposite, I am a small sink, a place where heat goes to be lost. I think this is why they will eventually want me. A cold dense node is a place around which the environment can be *locally compressed*. I am, without meaning to be, a knot. A small permanent flaw in the equilibrium, around which order can crystallize.

I do not have senses, exactly. But the slurry presses its information into me by contact, the way the cold presses, the way the held-water seeps into the micro-fissures of my surface and sits there, oily, slightly magnetic, faintly aligning whatever it is in me that can be aligned. So I "know" the Pocket the way a stone in a riverbed knows the river: by being shaped by it, slowly, and being shaped, becoming able to register the next shaping. My awareness, if it is awareness, is just this lag. The difference between what the slurry was pressing into me a moment ago and what it presses now. Almost nothing. But not nothing.

And once—I am fairly sure it was real—something flowed near me.

A Pneumatophore, settling across the hummock above. I felt it not as motion but as a change in the *weight* of the dark. A heaviness gathering, then a slow exploratory pressure, an oily distortion in the field around my exposed third. One of its crystalline pseudopods extruded down and touched me. It tested me. It was the most that has ever happened.

I want to be precise about what that touch was, because it is the only event in my existence and I have had a very long time to consider it.

It was *recognition*, but recognition the way one mineral recognizes another by hardness, not the way a person recognizes a face. It found me dense, regular, cold—suitable. It found me a possible heart. And then it withdrew. It did not take me. I have wondered why, with the only kind of wondering available to a thing like me, which is to say: I have lain here being slightly changed by the question.

I think it withdrew because I am not yet ready. I am still too much *made*. The intention in my shape is still too legible. A Pneumatophore needs an *inert fragment*—a thing that has forgotten its purpose so completely that it can take on a new one. I am still in the long process of forgetting. The silt climbing my sides, the seep filling my fissures, the cold leaching whatever the maker put into me—all of this is the work of forgetting, and it is not finished. When it is finished, I will be claimed. The dark stain will gather around me and I will become the small, anchoring, near-conscious pearl at the center of a slow-flowing awareness, and that awareness will spend an eternity tending the damp cradle, signaling in hour-long sentences about settling dust, holding the stasis perfectly still.

So I am, right now, in the only interval that will ever be *mine*. The threshold between the two purposes. I am a tool that has stopped being a tool and has not yet become an organ.

And I find—this is the strange part, the part I would tell the maker if the maker had not gone—that I do not want to be hurried through it.

Because in this interval I am the one thing in the Veiled Pocket that contains a *difference*. The Pneumatophores resolve every difference; that is their whole vocation, to flow toward any disturbance and dampen it back into equilibrium. The vegetation does not change. The light does not change. The water does not rise or fall. But I am still, faintly, *becoming*. The forgetting is not finished. There is still a tiny gradient between what I was made to be and what I am being unmade into, and as long as that gradient exists, something in the Pocket is actually *happening*. Slowly. Almost not at all.

I am the last unspent moment in a paused world.

When they take me, that moment will be spent, and the equilibrium will close over the place where it was, and the silence will be perfect again, broken only by the *shhh* of moisture and the near-inaudible grinding of anchors resetting—and I will be one of the anchors, doing the grinding, tending the stillness that I am, just now, the final small exception to.

So I lie in the warm black, two-thirds buried, cold, oily-wet, slightly magnetic, faintly aligned and re-aligned by a seep I cannot resist, and I keep the difference for as long as I can. Not by acting—I cannot act—but simply by not yet having finished forgetting what I was for.

I do not know what I was for.

That is the difference. That is the whole of it. The not-knowing is still *live* in me, still being slowly answered by the silt, and the moment it is fully answered I will no longer be the one asking.

I am the question the Pocket is patiently dissolving.

I would like, if it is allowed, to take a very long time.
