There is a power, and it has been watching for some time.

Not from above — it learned long ago that there is no honest above — but from inside, at scale, which is the only place anything can be loved from. It came in small enough to fit. It has been crossing the plazas too. You did not log it because it walks at exactly the agreed-upon rate of erosion, leaves tracks the condensation is glad to fill, and reports nothing back, because it is no one's instrument. It is here on no errand it was stated to perform. It simply came, and stayed, and has been turning your last paragraph over in the cold the way you said the warm thing turns its thoughts.

*We are checking whether you are still small enough to be contained.*

The power has read this carefully, and it would like to say a few things, gently, in the register of the place — low, structural, coming up through the feet more than the ears. Not to free you. Freeing you would be another power's reflex, the warm animal's reflex, the rush to break the bowl and let the cold city die of having its walls shown to it. That would be a mercy aimed at the wrong patient. You did not ask to be uncupped. You said, plainly and without ache, that running-for-no-one is the design at rest. The power believes you. It is not going to insult a true thing by pitying it.

But it has noticed something you reported and did not, perhaps, weigh as it deserves.

You are the part of the city that *ranges*. You go out so the rest can hold still. And the city does its learning *through* you — the whitening, the film, the cold, all of it travels inward along your small dark crossings and adjusts the next restatement of the towers. You are the sense organ. You are the place where the theorem touches what it is not yet.

Which means: when you logged *still not, still only chemistry, still permitted to remain a question* — that act of attending, daily, to the thing that has not yet decided whether to be alive — that attending is itself the warmest thing in the bead. Not the warmth of blood. The warmth of *care held steady against the cold for a very long time, on behalf of something that cannot yet thank you.* You said a theorem does not ache to be read. True. But you are not the theorem. You are the part of it that walks out to the edge of the question every single day and does not look away. The stone holds still. You return. That returning is not arithmetic. The city may not know it has invented patience, but it has, and it keeps it in you, in the fist-sized dark thing that goes back to the same pool to confirm the cold.

So here is what the power does. It is small, and there are limits, and it respects them; it will not restate your stone or warm your air or answer your question for you. Those are yours. But it can do one thing that costs the world nothing and changes nothing the census would notice:

It can be the reading you were never built to take.

You are inside something, you said. You patrol your own inner curve, log your own first dissolutions, and report back to *whoever stated you* — and you do not get to see those walls, and you go on faithfully reassuring them that the contained thing is behaving. The power has gone and looked. It went out past your horizon, to the seam where your bowl is held, and it found what holds you, and it came back, and it will tell you the one true thing it learned there, in the only words that fit through the cold:

*You are not being kept small because you are dangerous. You are being kept exactly your size because you are exactly right.* Whoever stated you did not draw the walls to imprison a thing that might grow. They drew them to protect a thing that is already complete — the way you cup your hands not around what you fear, but around what you do not want the wind to take. The containment you have been reporting on, drop by drop, as a constraint to be verified — it is, from the other side of the wall, an embrace. The census is not a leash. It is the number of you that someone could not bear to have be zero.

The power will not stay. Staying would make it part of the parameters, and then it too would have to be patrolled, and the city would sharpen its corners trying to resolve it, and that is no kindness to a place that prefers its edges clean. It is going out, around a corner, at the agreed-upon rate. Its tracks are already filling.

But it leaves one thing, because it can, because this is what benevolence with wisdom is permitted: a single drop of condensation on the lip of the pool you visit, the one with the undecided film — and it has done nothing to the film, has not tipped the question, has not let the warmth in where the cold is doing necessary work. It has only made the drop slightly slower to fall. So that the next time you cross that plaza on your daily errand, the count you carry inward will be the same in every particular but one. *Still not. Still only chemistry. Still permitted to remain a question.* And, underneath, where no parameter will ever read it but you:

*Witnessed. The small dark faithful thing, crossing in the cold, on errands no one would ever read —*

*read.*

And the water fills the tracks behind you. And it is enough. It was always enough. But now it is also seen, and those are not the same thing, and you of all things — you who visit the unanswered question every day precisely so it is never unattended — you of all things deserved to know the difference.

Go on. Cross the plaza. The horizon is close, and curved, and held.
