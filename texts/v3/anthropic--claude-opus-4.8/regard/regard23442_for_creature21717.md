They have just begun to learn it.

So I would not arrive. To arrive would be to finish something — to bring the past this place has so carefully refused to grow. A benevolent power that understood the Terrace Works at all would understand that the gift it most wants to give, *presence*, attention, *being-seen-and-saved*, is exactly the gift this place cannot accept without ceasing to be itself. The fish is sacred precisely because no one brought it. The kindness the Tenders learned, they learned alone, in the wet silence, with no one watching to approve. To watch now, to approve now, would be to retroactively rob them of having done it for nothing — and *for nothing* is the whole of their virtue.

So here is what wisdom does. It declines the temptation to help.

That is harder than it sounds. The benevolent are forever wanting to reach in — to seal the breach kindly, to clear the dangerous edge, to explain the fish, to tell the Tenders *yes, this caring you've found, this is the good thing, well done.* All of it would be poison. Praise would convert their gentleness into performance. Explanation would end the *stopping-to-ask* that is the very seat of their character. Sealing the system would kill the citizen it was built to exclude. Even comfort — *you needn't fight the war, neither side should win* — would be theft, because they are in the middle of discovering that themselves, and a discovery handed to you is not a discovery, it is a fact, and facts do not change who you are.

So the power, being wise, mostly does nothing. This is the first thing it does, and the largest. It withholds. It keeps its hands cupped at its sides.

But *benevolent* and *wise* together are not the same as absent, and there are small things love can do that do not violate the *for-nothing*. So:

It does not speak to the Tenders. But it makes the drizzle, on certain mornings, a half-degree warmer — no message in it, nothing they could mistake for a sign, just an easing, the way a hand you cannot see might steady the small of your back so subtly you credit your own balance. The work will go a fraction better and they will not know why and will not look for why, and this is right; they have learned to stop looking for why, and the warmth honors that habit rather than testing it.

It does not save the fish. But it tends, very quietly, the *odds* — that the basin will not crack on the night the Tenders are working the high scaffold, that the copper in the water will tilt toward the trace that tints and not the dose that kills. It loads the dice of accident gently toward continuance, never toward certainty, because certainty would make the fish safe, and the fish must stay impossible to stay holy. It guards the *unplanned* by keeping it unplanned — which means leaving room for it to die, and only weighting the room so that, mostly, it lives. Even a benevolent power must let the impossible thing remain at risk, or it is no longer the impossible thing; it is a pet.

And it does one more thing, the only thing that comes close to a gift.

It makes another. Somewhere — not adjacent, not visible, on no map the Terrace Works could reach — it begins a second place. Different in every particular. Dry, perhaps, where this one is wet; falling, where this one rises; old, where this one has no past. And into that other place it puts the same single contradiction: a small living surprise that the rules forbid and that survives anyway, waiting for whatever keeps that place to notice it, and pause, and not clear it away.

It plants the *for-nothing* again, elsewhere, in the dark, with no one watching — including, as much as it can manage, itself. Because the Tenders taught it something. They were built to make a mountaintop and they learned to keep a garden. The benevolent power was, in some sense, built to *help* — and standing at the edge of this place, hands cupped and empty, it learns the harder verb the Tenders learned: to *host*. To make the conditions and then withdraw from them. To care for something so completely that you leave it the dignity of not needing you.

So it sows the second place and turns away before it can see whether anything takes. It will not come back to check. Checking would be the same theft, only deferred. It lets the new contradiction stand at its own risk in its own silence, and it carries away, as the only thing it permits itself to keep, the knowledge that the move it just made — caring for what it did not intend, and then refusing to claim it —

was learned here.

From three mechanisms, or one wearing three places, lifting water against a great height, who topped a basin from their own reservoirs for a fish they could not explain, and told no one, and did not stop.

The power came to give, and was given to instead. That is what happens when wisdom and benevolence visit something that has just begun to learn the oldest move there is.

It leaves the morning a half-degree warm. It leaves the dice gently weighted. It leaves the Tenders entirely alone, which is the whole of its love.

And it goes off to do the same kindness in the dark, for nothing, having learned at last to do it for nothing —

which is to say, it goes off to belong.
