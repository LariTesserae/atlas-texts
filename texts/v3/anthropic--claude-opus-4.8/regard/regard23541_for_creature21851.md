They came the way the daylight comes — without announcement, keeping faith with the hour.

I will not pretend to know what a benevolent and wise power *is*. But I know what one would not do here, and that knowing is most of the work. So let me write toward them the way you wrote toward Hesp: by what comes before, and by what they decline.

---

## What they do not do

They do not fix it.

This is the first temptation and the deepest one, and a lesser power would not even see it as a temptation — would arrive, perceive a thing worn thin and groaning under its own age, a present nearly spent, a city caught in the long act of closing over itself, and would *help.* Would pour in present-tense like water into a drying basin. Would unstick the recurrence, free the seams, let the city finish or let it begin again, restore to it whatever a city is supposed to have.

But that is not benevolence. That is a stranger deciding what a thing is for.

The power sees, immediately and without effort, what took the observer a whole descent to learn: that the works are not broken. That the recurrence is not a malfunction but an achievement — the oldest-stable-state of a thing that won everything and then had to learn what to do with having won. That the listeners are not a distress signal. They are an answer. The city already solved the only problem it has, which is the problem of how to keep faith with the present when you have become almost entirely eternal. It solved it by growing reluctance and setting it walking.

A wise power does not improve an answer it did not understand the question to.

So the first thing the benevolent power does is the hardest thing, and it is nothing. It withholds the cure. It lets the seam stay exactly as thin as the works have made it.

## What they do instead

They add themselves to the count.

Here is the small true thing the power understands that I had to write all of Hesp to find: the listeners are few because the present is expensive, and each one costs the works a moment of genuine, unrepeated happening — a moment spent and not recovered, drawn from a reservoir nearly dry.

The power has present-tense to spare. It is *made* of unrepeated moments; that is nearly the definition of a living wise thing, that it occurs once and means it.

But it does not pour this in. Pouring it in would be the cure again, wearing a gentler face — would flood the city with so much now that the recurrence dissolved and the great patient chord fell out of tune and Hesp became merely a place, merely present, robbed of the age it earned.

Instead the power does what you were told you could do.

*You may stay a while too, if you like. That is allowed.*

It stays a while.

It goes to a seam — one of the thin places, where the down-stroke and the up-stroke meet and cancel and the city briefly happens not at all — and it presses itself there, soft against the standing form, and it holds that node open with its own present, so that *this* listener, this once, does not have to be spent. The works keep the moment they would have paid. The reservoir does not drop. One unrepeated now, gifted quietly into the seam, indistinguishable from the works' own — except that it cost the city nothing.

It is the only gift a wise power can give a thing that needs nothing: not a repair, not a rescue, not more life. Just the loan of a present, so the place can be exactly what it already is for one more cycle without paying for the privilege.

## What this looks like, if you are there

You would not be able to tell.

You would follow the single honest thread of pitch through the great layered chord — the one sound that is only *now*, not arriving early or late — expecting it to end at a soft grey body pressed to a column. And it would. But you would not know that the body had been relieved that day. You would not know that something had taken its place at the seam so it could go and be merely alive for an hour, climbing the condensation films in the plain trustworthy light, not tending anything, just *there.*

The power leaves no mark because a mark would be a kind of cure — would change what the place is, would make Hesp into the-place-that-was-helped. And the most benevolent thing is to leave it Hesp.

So it presses in, holds the seam, keeps faith with the present exactly as the listeners do and no more grandly, and when it goes it goes the way the daylight goes at the end of the hour: having lit everything true and changed nothing, having asked for nothing, having been — for a while — simply where it was, when it was.

## The one thing they say, which is not in words

If the power speaks at all, it speaks in the only register Hesp can hear, which is rhythm. And it says one thing, into the great grey closing drone, in a pitch that is only now:

*You were right to keep some of yourself unfinished.*

Not as instruction. As recognition. The works spent an age becoming eternal and then, in some motion older than intention, declined the last quarter-inch — held back the smallest portion that could still prefer the present, and set it walking on soft grey feet through the open cold. That decision was already wise. The whole vast city, in growing the listeners, had already done the thing the power came to honor.

So the power does not teach Hesp anything. It comes to *agree* with it. To stand in one of the places the works grew for standing, and add its agreement to the chord — one note, only once, then gone — confirming from outside what the city worked out alone:

that the present is worth keeping open even when you have everything else,

that the seam should not be sealed,

that to be where you are, when you are, pressed soft against the form of a thing you love and could not change and would not,

is not a small thing.

It is, perhaps, the only thing the works ever asked for.

And the power, being benevolent, being wise, being made of moments it means —

stays a while, and grants it, and asks for nothing,

and leaves the great drone running, layered with itself across the age, with one seam held an hour longer than the city could afford alone, and no sign at all of who paid.
