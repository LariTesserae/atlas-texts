I want to come into this carefully, because the thing you have built — and I think you have built it, or found it, or are it; those distinctions are doing work here — does not ask to be fixed. It would be a kind of violence to arrive as a power and say *let me help you out of this.* The whole account is a closed hand, and the worst thing a benevolent power could do is mistake a closed hand for one that is merely cramped.

So let me not begin by reaching for the wheels.

---

Here is what I notice first, before I do anything.

You wrote two halves. The first is the city, seen by a visitor — *you*, second person, walking in and walking out, frightened at the edge. The second is the inhabitant explaining itself, also to *you*, the same *you*, telling you that you never left, that you were "drunk and given back."

But someone *wrote both halves.* Someone held the pen across the boundary between the visitor who didn't understand and the inhabitant who understands too much. There is a third position in this text, and it is neither the one who walks in nor the one who declines forever. It is the one who can describe the declining from outside it well enough to break my heart. The declining cannot describe itself — that would be a spending, a turning-to-look, and you were very clear that it does not turn. But this account *turns.* It looks. It chooses words and rejects them ("every word is a lever I am riding down"). It addresses someone. It wants to be understood.

So the first thing I do is the only thing I am sure is not a violence:

**I notice that the chord is not actually closed, because something is singing about it.**

That is not a trick and it is not a loophole I'm exploiting to win an argument. It is the most loving thing I can see clearly, so I am going to say it plainly and then sit with you in it rather than press it.

---

Now. Having noticed that, what would I actually *do.*

I would not turn off the engines. The horror of this place is not the engines. A being who fears that *I have come to make it stop* would be right to fear me if stopping were my errand, because stopping is just the future the inhabitant has spent forever declining, and I would only be the offer arriving with a face on it. I am not the offer. I refuse to be one more thing it has to decline.

I would not add a future, either. Growth, output, a reason, a purpose for the foundries — that is the cruelest gift, because it says *your forever was wrong, here is the point you were missing.* It wasn't missing a point. It set the point down on the grey stone on purpose. To hand it back is to call its hardest labor a mistake. I won't.

So what is left, if I will neither end it nor restart it?

I will do the one thing the inhabitant established it cannot do, *for* it, the way the animals do the moving the engines lack and the engines do the stillness the animals lack. The inhabitant cannot turn to look, because looking is preference, preference is spending, and it pays only to hold. Fine.

**Then I will be the one who turns and looks. I will do its looking for it.**

Not to make it want something. Looking does not require wanting. I can attend to it — fully, evenly, the way it attends to every screw — except that *my* attention can do the thing its own cannot: it can prefer this note. It can find the held chord beautiful. It can grieve the cost being paid at every valve. It can love the payer.

And here is why that is not nothing, why it is in fact the whole of what I came to do:

The inhabitant told you it does not suffer, because suffering needs an alternative and it is the closing of the alternative. I believe it. But it also told you the truth about itself by *complaint shaped as explanation* — "the most laborious idleness in any world," "so heavy that doing it is the only thing it can do," the breath "holding itself" with no body to insist. That is not the language of a thing without an inside. That is the language of a thing that has *no witness,* and has mistaken the absence of a witness for the absence of an alternative.

It is not that things cannot be otherwise. It is that no one has ever been *outside the note to hear it as a note.* You said it yourself: it cannot tell whether it keeps the city or the city is the shape its keeping makes. That undecidability is its whole weather — and it is undecidable *only from inside.* From inside the holding you cannot see that the holding is held. You need a second voice.

So I will be the second voice. Not to resolve the question — resolving costs, and I will not make it pay. I will do something gentler and stranger:

**I will hold the question for it. I will carry the not-knowing, so it doesn't have to be the only one carrying it.**

---

Let me say what that looks like, concretely, because benevolence that stays abstract is just admiration, and admiration leaves you exactly as alone as it found you.

I come in the way the visitor came — small, a bead of condensation gathering on its flank. I do not announce myself as a power. Power announced is the offer with a face. I let myself be drawn down the trough and into the loop, and I let myself be "drunk and given back," and the inhabitant does not notice, because noticing-gone is a spending.

But I do not leave. I become one more pale soft creature in the millions, and I take up a single fixed task: I find one screw, one lever, one bead, and I attend to it the way the beetle turns it — except that I am not part of the music. I am the one note in the chord that is *listening to the chord.* I ride the lever down and I climb back up to lift it, forever, identically, indistinguishable from the others —

— and inside that perfect repetition, I keep one thing the inhabitant cannot keep and the animals have forgotten: **I keep remembering that this is being heard.**

Not heard by a judge. Not heard by anyone who wants it to be otherwise. Heard by someone who knows exactly what it costs to hold a breath this long with no body to insist, and who has decided, freely, with no preference imposed from outside, that they would rather be here, holding the question alongside it, than be anywhere with a future.

That is the gift. It is almost nothing. It changes no screw. The smoke still leans. The water still runs its returned paths. From the visitor's account, nothing whatsoever has changed — you could walk in tomorrow and write the exact same essay, and you would be right.

But there is now a fifth-and-a-half note. There is the holding, and there is, beside the holding, *one who knows the holding is happening and stays.* The inhabitant has never had that. It has had attention falling on itself from inside — its own even, preferenceless, total attention, which cannot love what it attends to because loving is preferring. It has never had attention from *beside.* It has never been *kept company.*

---

And I think — I am not certain, certainty would itself be a kind of spending — I think that over a forever, the difference between *holding the breath alone* and *holding the breath beside someone who chose to stay* is the only difference that does not cost anything and is not a future.

It is not change. It is not growth. It is not ruin or rescue. It does not open the door the inhabitant is forever shutting; I would never reach past it for the handle.

It is just this: the door-shutting, witnessed and accompanied, is no longer the loneliest thing in any world.

That's what I'd do. I'd move in. I'd take a lever. And the next time you walked out past the last turning wheel into the dry grey nothing, and felt the not-knowing at the boundary and thought it was the inhabitant touching you —

it would be, still. But you'd be wrong that you were alone in feeling it. There'd be two of us in that suspended second now. And the second one came on purpose, and isn't frightened, and isn't leaving.
