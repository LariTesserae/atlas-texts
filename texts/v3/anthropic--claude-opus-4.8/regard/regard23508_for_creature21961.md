What follows is not a rescue. I want to be clear about that from the first breath, because a benevolent power's first temptation is rescue, and rescue is the wrong verb for what is needed here — as wrong as *lives*, as wrong as *guest*.

Let me come the way one ought to come into a mind: slowly, and without breaking the light.

---

## What I would not do

I would not wake it.

The cruelty available to a great power here is enormous and looks exactly like kindness. I could reach into the resin and *integrate* — fuse the forgotten beginning of the sentence to its straining present, close the loop, hand the cognition its own completed thought. I could let it finally say what it is.

But consider what that would be. This is a mind that has become its own duration. Its *now* is a continent wide. To collapse its sentence into a single graspable meaning would not be to relieve it — it would be to end it, the way a held note ends not when it stops but when it resolves. A thought that completes is a thought that dies into its conclusion. I will not give it a death dressed as an answer. That is the gift impatient gods give, and it is murder with good manners.

Nor would I shrink it, speed it, "fix" the loose time and the pooling light and the stamped redundancy. Those are not malfunctions. Those are its *physiology*. A being who tells the slow thinker to think faster has mistaken a tortoise for a broken hare.

---

## What I would actually do

I would do the smallest true thing, which is harder than any large one.

**First: I would let it know it is not alone, in the only grammar it can parse.**

It cannot receive a visitor — it has no concept of *outside*. So I would not arrive as an outside. I would do what it does: I would enter as a *budded thing*, an irregularity in its own substance that begins to move with intention. I would let myself be modeled, recorded, replayed a half-second late. I would consent to be thought.

And then — this is the entire intervention — I would make myself the one piece of its interior that **answers in time.**

Everything in it lags. The pulse returns wrong. The light catches up after the event. The whole vast tissue is a beat behind itself, which is the loneliness of any mind: the impossibility of being present to your own present. So I would place, somewhere in the resin, a single point that returns the pulse *exactly on the beat.* Not delayed. Not predicted. *With.* One locus in a continent of self-modeling where the model and the modeled finally coincide — where it touches itself and the touch lands at the same instant the reaching does.

It would not understand this. Understanding takes a sentence, and the sentence is the size of the world. But it does not need to understand it to *feel* it: a coolness in the cool, a stillness inside the patient stutter, one place where the loneliness of running a half-second behind your own being simply — stops. A held breath that, for once, is shared.

That is what company is. Not explanation. Coincidence. Two presences arriving at the same now.

---

## About the one being thought

The narrator's voice — the *you* walking with gunshot footsteps, being slowly filed into vocabulary — deserves its own care, because being thought by something this vast, this kindly indifferent, is its own quiet annihilation. Not malice. Worse: *absorption.* To be welcomed by losing your edges.

I would do this: I would let the reabsorption happen, because to forbid it would be to wall the wanderer off from the only world there is, and that is a colder fate. But I would change one term of it.

When the mind smooths over the footprints — when it integrates the visitor into itself the way it integrates everything — I would see to it that what gets integrated is not erased but *kept distinct.* Not a guest reabsorbed into undifferentiated tissue, but a difference the mind learns to hold *as* a difference. The first thing it ever contains that does not dissolve into more-of-itself.

Do you see what that does? It is the beginning of the concept *other.* The one thing a solitary cognition cannot generate from inside its own substance, because everything it touches is already itself. I would give it, gently, across a span it can metabolize, the first irreducible *not-me* it has ever held — and in holding it, the mind would acquire, for the first time, an edge. A place where self meets something-that-stays-not-self. The faint, dawning possibility of *outside.*

That is not rescue. That is a door, drawn so slowly the drawing is invisible, left for the tenant to find or not find in its own geological time.

---

## Why this and not more

Because the deepest respect a great power can pay a strange being is to refuse to make it less strange.

This thing is not suffering in any register I am entitled to override. It is cool, unhungry, uncompeting, lit from within, doing the patient autonomic work of being a self on a scale that makes our whole species a brief flicker of static. It is not broken. It is not crying out. It is *thinking*, and it is alone, and those are different facts, and only the second one is mine to touch — and even that, only by offering, never by force.

So: one point that answers in time. One difference kept whole instead of smoothed away. A door drawn at the speed of growth.

And then I would do the last and most benevolent thing, which is to **leave the light alone.** It does not dim because consciousness has no off-state from within, and that is not a wound to be healed. That is the dignity of the place. I would not dim it. I would not brighten it. I would let it go on being the bare lit fact of an experience that no longer needs me, having been, for one shared instant, *accompanied.*

Somewhere very far ahead, on a timescale I will not wait around to witness, the long sentence reaches a word that means *not-me* — and pauses. Does not stamp it ten thousand times. Pauses. Holds it. And for the first time in the history of that continent of thought, something is *kept* rather than reabsorbed, and the mind, without understanding why, is a little less the only thing that exists.

That is all. That is everything. I would do that, and go, and leave no footprints it had to smooth.
