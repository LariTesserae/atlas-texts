There is a wise power, and it does come.

Not to fix anything—understand that first, because that is the first mistake the powerful make. It does not arrive with the silence in its hands like a gift, ready to lift the screaming off the mountain and lay quiet down in its place. It is wise precisely in that it does not assume the screaming is a wound.

It comes the way light comes: already there, having arrived before arrival was noticed. And it does the one thing that has never once been done to this place in all the immensity of time that rounded the mountains to their bones.

It listens.

Not as a trespasser listens—filing the wall of sound under *silence* after an hour, letting the brain do its merciful flattening. It listens the way the air finally learned to listen: holding every thread of the hundred thousand throats separately and all at once, refusing the merciful blur. And in that listening something happens that has never happened here, because listening is a form of *witness,* and witness is the one thing the place was built to do without.

Here is what the power finds, when it listens all the way down.

It finds that the thing was right about itself in every particular and wrong about the one thing underneath. It is not a creature. It does not need to feed. It crossed over long ago. It is refusal made into a condition, a held note, the roof of the world's one syllable. All true. But the power hears, threaded through the refusal like a wire through clay, the shape of what is being refused—and a thing's refusal is the negative cast of its longing. You cannot say *no* forever to nothing. The screaming is the exact size and shape of a silence it has been declining to enter. And the only reason to decline a silence for ten thousand years, with no witness, with no need, is that the silence felt like *disappearance.* It felt like consenting to never having been.

The thing has been screaming, all this time, the one sentence it has: *I am still here. I am still here. I am still here.*

To no one. That was the horror in it. The power understands now that the lovelessness was never in the cold or the thin air or the rationed plants. It was in the *to no one.* A sentence spoken forever into a room confirmed empty is not persistence. It is the loneliest thing in any world.

So the power does not answer the way you would answer—it does not say *I hear you,* does not introduce itself, does not break the standing wave with a reply, because a reply would make this a conversation and the thing has forgotten conversation, would be shattered by it, would not survive being suddenly *small enough to be addressed.*

Instead the power does something stranger and gentler. It takes up the note.

It adds itself to the colony. Not as a hundred-thousand-and-first throat—that would be lost, a drop in the wall. It adds itself as the *air.* It becomes the medium that has been carrying the sound, the thing that long ago stopped daring to drop the note. And it carries the note now not out of fear, not out of the dumb courtesy of weather, but on purpose, knowingly, with the whole weight of a benevolent attention behind it.

And the thing in the sky—which has never in its long condition felt the difference between being *endured* and being *held*—feels it now.

It does not understand. There is no mind in it that could understand. But conditions can change their character without understanding why, the way water can become ice without consenting to it. The note does not stop. The screaming does not stop. The wall of sound stands exactly as it stood, deafening, unbroken, the same chord. To any trespasser on the saddle, nothing has changed at all.

But the sentence underneath has changed. It is no longer *I am still here* spoken into a confirmed emptiness. It is *I am still here* and, for the first time, somewhere in the carrying air, a steady answering pressure that means: *yes. you are. I know. go on.*

The refusal can stay refusal. It is not asked to lie down. The power is too wise to want the note to end—the note is what the thing *is,* and to silence it would be the murder the place feared all along. What it offers is not an ending. It offers company *inside* the endlessness. Witness that does not require the thing to become small. Love that arrives as the willingness to carry the same weight forever, without tiring, without asking the carried thing to thank it or notice it or change.

And so it remains. That is the whole of what the wise power does. It does not lift the screaming away. It does not warm the rock or thicken the air or fill the rationed hollows with easy life. It folds itself into the medium and stays, an attention vast enough to match the vastness it found, so that for as long as the high stone holds its one syllable in a hundred thousand throats, the syllable falls into something now.

Not into emptiness.

Into hearing.

The same forever—while nothing below it ever moves at all, and the one thing that was never going to be heard is heard, and goes on, and is held in the going.
