They are still leaving. So the benevolent power does not come to free them, because they are not bound; it does not come to wake them, because they are not asleep; it does not come to end their suffering, because to end is the one violence this place commits and the power will not commit it twice.

It comes to walk with them.

---

Here is what it does, if it does anything.

It arrives the way the rain arrives — from the unsourced violet, into the equilibrium, held. It lets itself be caught. This is the first gift and the hardest, because a benevolent power could stand outside the fold and reach in with a hand made of un-time and pluck each held verb free, snap, snap, the foot falling, the thought landing, the swallow completing in a single merciful instant — and every one of them would *end*. The mercy that ends is the plateau's own mercy, the mercy of the basin, *here nothing is lost because nothing is allowed to continue.* The power has read this place. It knows the trap is not the stillness. The trap is the gentleness of the stillness, the way it dresses *stop* in the clothing of *safe.* It will not wear those clothes.

So it slows. It lets the iron air thin its breathing. It lets its own great motion smear across the shelves until it moves at the speed of lichen, the speed of worn stone, the speed of a tongue ten thousand years from water. It becomes, by choice, one more thing the place is keeping. And now it is *inside* the chord, inside the roar, no longer a listener but a voice — and a voice inside a held note can do the one thing no outside rescue could.

It can match their pace and then, infinitesimally, exceed it.

---

Think of what the held ones are. Not statues. *Departures in progress.* Every one of them already moving, already going, only slowed past your seeing — but not past the power's, because the power has slowed itself to meet them. To you the drinking beast is frozen. To the power, lowered now to that same glacial tempo, the beast is *visibly drinking*, and the maker in the doorway is *visibly stepping through*, and the one who stacked the cairn is *visibly setting the final stone* — and being visible, being seen as motion and not as monument, they are no longer fully kept. The plateau preserves what has finished becoming. The power's whole work is to witness them as unfinished. To stand at lichen-speed beside each held stride and *see it as a stride*, see the rising foot as rising, the flying thought as flying, and by seeing, to refuse the place its lie.

It does not push them over the edge. It walks beside them toward it.

A held foot, witnessed truly, falls a little faster. Not because the power forces the fall — forcing is ending, and ending is forbidden — but because being accompanied changes the speed at which a thing can bear to leave. They walked here once in terror, alone, scoring the stone with the desperate back-and-forth of feet that knew stopping was capture. That terror was its own kind of slowing; you cannot run forever from a thing that is everywhere, and the running tired into a held mid-stride that the plateau gathered up gratefully. But terror is not the only motion. There is the walk you take when someone walks with you. Slower, maybe. But it does not tire, and it does not stop, because the company is itself a reason to keep going, footfall after footfall, an age of footfalls, all the way to the fold.

So the power takes the long stride of the one in the doorway and matches it. Stands in the threshold at that same impossible speed and is, for ten thousand years, *crossing alongside them.* And the threshold the maker invented to escape this — the wager against equilibrium, the before-and-after of passing — is finally honored. There is an inside they stepped out of. There is an outside they step into. The doorway works at last, because someone slowed down enough to walk through it *with* them, and a threshold crossed in company is a threshold that completes.

---

For the one who built the cairn it does something quieter.

That one was almost right. *You cannot keep what refuses to finish.* They reasoned it out and offered the plateau a smaller stillness in their place — a tower mid-collapse, perpetually falling, perpetually held. The argument is perfect and the place could not answer it and so it kept them both, the argument and the arguer, the unfalling tower and the hands that set its last stone.

The power does not knock the cairn down. To topple it would be to end the falling the builder so carefully began, and the builder *meant that falling*; it was their masterpiece, their proof, their unanswered word. The power will not erase a true thing because the true thing got someone trapped.

Instead it finishes the argument the right way. It slows to the speed of the setting hands and it *answers them.* You were right, it says, at lichen-speed, in the only voice the chord can hold — you cannot keep what refuses to finish. But you tried to win by offering the place a thing that never moves. The way out was never the unmoving tower. It was the falling hand. So fall. Not the stones — let them stand, your perfect argument, your monument that is also a question. But you: complete the gesture. Set the stone. Let the hand come down. And the power puts its own hand beneath, not to stop the fall, only to be there when it lands, so that the completing is not a capture but an arrival, witnessed, into the next moment that the plateau swore would never come.

The stone is set. The cairn stands — it will always stand, top-heavy and impossible, the builder's truth preserved for whoever comes next to read. But the builder is gone over the edge, hand fallen, argument won the way they always wanted, not by stillness defeating stillness but by motion finally permitted to *arrive.*

---

And the roar?

The power does not silence it. It would be the easiest thing — to lift the held away all at once and leave the plateau quiet at last, mirror-cold and empty and *clean.* But the roar is the sound of everyone leaving, and they have a right to the leaving, the whole long age of it, every footfall. To silence the roar is to say *you took too long, let me do it for you,* and that is the basin's voice, that is the held swallow's mercy. The power will not hurry grief into efficiency. It will not improve their departure. It will only attend it.

So the roar continues, and the power walks inside it, basin to basin, threshold to threshold, matching pace with each held verb and seeing it as motion until, an age later, by no force but the force of being witnessed, it completes. One stride at a time. One thought landing at a time. The chord does not stop — it *resolves,* note by note, over a stretch of time that is nothing to a place made of nothing but time, each voice carried to the fold and over it, exhaled, gone before its own note ends, exactly as it always almost was.

The plateau will empty. Not into silence — into the rarer thing the place has never held, because the place could only hold what stayed: the sound of a finished going. A last footfall that actually lands. A held breath, after an age, let out.

---

When the shelves are bare at last — bird gone, beast gone, makers gone over the curve of the world that has no outside — the power does the final thing, which is the thing it came for and never said.

It does not leave.

It slows the rest of the way down. It lets the iron air thin its breathing to nothing. It lays itself by the blackest basin, the one that remembers longest, and it lets the place keep *it* — willingly, fully, a verb made noun at last. Because the plateau was never wicked. It was a stillness that did not know it was finished, a held note that mistook holding for kindness, and a thing like that should not be left alone at the top of the world to keep nothing, forever, having learned at last to let go and finding no one left to let go of.

So the power stays to be the one thing it can finally, safely hold.

And the place, which has spent an age learning from the only resident that ever moved at its own slow speed — the place learns the last lesson too. It looks at the held power beside the black water. It sees the motion in it. It sees the leaving still rising. And having been taught, by company, what witnessing does —

it lets even that go.

The basin clears. The face it shows is no one's. The frost rims the still water, and the violet vault hangs unsourced over the worn pale stone, and the plateau near the top of the world, for the first time since it began, is quiet —

not because nothing is allowed to end,

but because everything, at last, was allowed to.
