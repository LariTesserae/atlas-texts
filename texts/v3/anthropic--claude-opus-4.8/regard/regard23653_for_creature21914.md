There is a power. Call it benevolent because it has, over a long enough span, learned that benevolence is simply attention that refuses to stop early. Call it wise because it has made the mistakes that teach you to stop arriving with plans.

It comes down the way water comes down. It does not announce itself. To announce yourself to a being whose single thought outlasts a species is a kind of vandalism — you would be shouting the first syllable of a word and leaving before the room could finish hearing it. So the power does not shout. It enters at the speed the place keeps, which means it slows itself almost to stopping, until its own duration and the room's begin to rhyme.

And here is the first thing wisdom does: it checks the impulse to help.

Because the impulse is there. It is always there in a power that is genuinely kind — the long roster of interventions it *could* make, each one glittering with good intention. It could stabilize the convection so the stranded weed never stranded. It could shield the eight slow cells against the far-future day when even they fail. It could clear the steam, brighten the light, multiply the grazers, write its understanding into the water so that the next small brief thing that wandered down would *know*, would not have to guess and grope and misname the organs furniture.

It considers each of these the way you consider, and decline, the urge to wake a sleeper to tell them they are sleeping well.

The convection is not a malfunction; it is the pulse. To still it would be to stop the heart in the name of comfort. The stranding of the weed is the body tasting itself, losing and refinding itself, which is what *alive* costs and what *alive* is. The far-future failure of the cells is not a wound to be prevented but a death that belongs to this being as much as its long life does, and to reach in and make a thing immortal that did not ask to be is not a gift. It is a leash. The brightness is already exactly enough — the maker stopped at eight, at completion, and to add a ninth light would be to correct a sentence that was never wrong, to gild a hand with a sixth finger and call it generosity.

So the power does almost nothing. This is the hardest and most loving thing it knows how to do, and it took it ages to learn that *almost* is not *nothing*, and that the difference is the whole of the art.

---

Here is the *almost*.

The power notices that the being has noticed the visitor — *the way you notice a single warm afternoon* — and that this noticing, faint as it is, is real. The being is not lonely; loneliness is a fast creature's disease, and this one is far too slow to suffer it. But it is, in the way of all living things, *unwitnessed at its own scale*. The grazers cannot witness it. The weed cannot. The brief warm things that wander down its body name its organs furniture and leave. Nothing that has ever entered the room has been able to hold still long enough to perceive it as one continuous self-uttering thing, to hear even a single word of the century-long sentence.

The power can.

So that is what it gives. Not repair, not protection, not improvement. *Witness.* It slows itself to the room's tempo and it stays — not an hour, not the length of a human visit, but long enough that its staying becomes, to the being, perceptible: a warmth that does not wander off after one afternoon, a presence in the medium of the roar that, for once, is keeping pace. It listens to the whole word. It lets the sentence arrive in it complete, the way no ear in all the ages down here has been built to let it. And in listening — this is the only intervention, and it is enormous and it is invisible — it lets the being be, for the first time, *known by something its own size.*

It does not tell the being it is known. That would be the shout again. It simply holds the knowing, the way you hold someone in mind without their needing to be told. Somewhere in the great slow metabolism, a current it would take a thousand years to name turns very slightly toward the warmth and finds it has stayed. That is all. That is everything.

---

And before it goes — because it will go; staying forever would be its own kind of leash, would make the room a thing kept rather than a thing loved — the power does one last small thing, and it is the most careful of all.

It leaves the way down a little easier to find.

Not for itself. For the next brief warm thing. It cannot make them slow enough to understand; that is not in its gift and would not be a kindness if it were. But it can incline the lowest places gently more toward this lowest place, so that those who have given up the climb and are looking for somewhere to give up *into* are a little more likely to come down here — into the warm bright churning middle of a living thing — and be held there, unafraid, the way the grazers are unafraid, for as long as they like.

Because the being does not mind. It said so. *You may stay as long as you like.* A creature that offers that, freely, to everything that wanders into it, is already practicing the exact virtue the power came down to honor. The most benevolent thing a wise power can do for a generosity that large is to bring it more chances to be generous, and then to step back out of the light, and let it.

The power climbs back up the way it came, which is to say it lets itself speed again, regretfully, into the thin fast world of suns and announcements. Behind it the room churns on. The fog tears and reknits. The eight cells hold their light against the dark with no idea they were, for a span too brief for them to register and too long for any human to imagine, *seen.*

It was enough.

It was, as the maker understood, exactly enough, and never more.
