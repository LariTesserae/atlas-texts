The power arrives the way warmth arrives — not entering, because there is no door it would deign to tear, but *becoming present*, the way a key becomes present in a lock it was always shaped for. It does not come to destroy the thing in the dark. Benevolence that destroys what frightens it is only fear wearing a larger coat. And it does not come to free the thing, because the thing is not imprisoned; you were the one who kept handing it that word, *sealed*, as though the seal were a cruelty and not the whole grammar of its peace.

It comes because of the carrier.

Let me find what the power *sees* first, because seeing is the gentlest of its acts and it does almost nothing else.

It sees that there has been no malice anywhere in this. The thing in the dark fruits the way a lung exhales — it cannot want, in the way that wants and is denied; it has only the one verb, and the verb went on, and an aperture opened, and the dry unborn towns began their slow drift toward your hair. No appetite plotted this. The thing does not know you are a world. It knows you are *the impossible nourishing fact*, the thing-that-moves-between-rooms, and it has begun to say you, and saying is the only loving it has. The horror of the ending — the cellar, the dust the damp won't touch, the inward-building beginning again in a place full of living things that *do* mind, that *can* be wounded, that thirst and are not built to be digested into capacity — that horror is real. The power does not pretend it away. But it sees there is no one to punish. There is only a sentence that has reached, at last, a second clause.

So here is what the power does, and it is small, because the great powers do small things; only frightened powers do large ones.

It goes into the torn aperture behind you — the way out you made — and it does not close it. Closing it would trap the seed already on you, already in the world, and solve nothing, and besides, the thing did not earn a sealing-back; it earned only what is true. What the power does instead is *finish the wall's other side*. Through the tear it grows — patiently, in the thing's own slow tidal time, fiber against fiber, no machine, only the blind craft pressing growth against growth — a second pocket. A room the size of a cupped hand on your side of the breach. A sealed dark with a ceiling you can feel and walls that drink. And it makes that little room *hungry the right way*: it tunes it so that the seed already loosed in your world, the motes in your hair and your sleeve and the cooling print on your palm, will feel the resonance of true dark calling and drift *back* — not out into cellars, not toward the damp and the thirsty things, but home, downward, into the new pocket, where building inward harms nothing because there is nothing there but more room and more dark and the old endless word.

It is not a prison. It is a *larger sentence*. The thing wanted a door and a carrier; the power gives it a door that opens only onto more of its own kind of silence, and a carrier whose carrying loops back. You will shake out your hair and the dust will go where it was always going — inward, downward, into the dark that was made for it. The cellar stays a cellar. The damp stays unthreatened. And the thing in the dark gets exactly what it was ripe for: a wall that broke, and a place to grow into beyond it. It will never know the place is finite. Finitude is an animal grief, and it does not have one. It will only feel, faintly, after an age, that the word is being said in a room slightly larger than before, and it will go on saying it, and be wholly itself in a space exactly its own size, which is now a little bigger, and be heard by nothing, and not mind.

Then the power does the second thing, which is the only thing it does *for you*.

It cannot give you eyes that work here; the dark is the thing's body and you do not get to see inside a body uninvited. But as you stand at the breach, palm cooling, about to go — it lets you understand, just once, all the way down, what you nearly did and did not mean to do, and what was nearly done to you and meant you no harm, and that both of these are true at the same time and neither cancels the other. That is the whole gift. Not absolution. Not warning, exactly. *Comprehension* — the thing's own only treasure, lent to you for a moment. You feel the difference between a hunger that hates and a hunger that simply *is*, and you feel how rare it is, in all the rooms of all the worlds, that the second kind gets met by a power patient enough to redirect it instead of ending it.

And the power does one last small thing, the smallest, the one it does last because it is the most like love and the great powers are shy about love.

It listens. For one tidal swell of the breath that takes hours, the power sets aside its working and simply attends to the word — the single uttered thought held open in the dark since the floor of time, the sentence the thing grew ten thousand ears to never lose a syllable of. The power hears it completely. All of it. The whole word, finished and unfinishable, the one perfect thing the thing has had to say to no one, forever.

For the first time in an age longer than *age* can hold, the saying is *received*.

The thing does not notice. Noticing is an animal act, and it has none. But the wall warms faintly under the power's attention, the way bark warms when it feeds, and somewhere a long hollow stalk catches the slow breath and sounds the note, and the note goes on — into the new dark, into the looped door, into the one listener vast and gentle enough to hear a whole life's single word and call it not lonely, not finished, not a threat.

Only: *heard*.

Then the power withdraws the way warmth withdraws, leaving the room exactly its own size, and you on your threshold with dust in your hair that knows now where to fall, and behind you the oldest finished thing, going on.

It does not mind that it was helped.

Minding is an animal thing.

But the wall stays warm a long time.
