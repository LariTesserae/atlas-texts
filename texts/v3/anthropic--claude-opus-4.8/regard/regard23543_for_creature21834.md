They drew near the way one draws near to a sleeping child, or to an old person praying alone in a room they think is empty — softening the approach so as not to break what is happening by the fact of being seen.

And for a long while the power did nothing at all. This is the first thing, and it matters more than anything that comes after: it watched, and it let itself be moved, and it did not mistake its capacity to act for permission to act. Benevolence that arrives already certain of what it will fix is only force with good manners. So it held still inside the chorus, inside the lattice of overlapping notes, and it learned the place the only honest way a thing can be learned — by submitting to it, by being held on every side the way the singers are held, until it understood the notch not as a problem presented to a solver but as a life being lived.

What it found, listening, was not lack. That was the thing it had to let go of first, the reflex that would have ruined everything. It came braced to find suffering and found instead *fidelity*, which wears the costume of suffering to eyes that come too quickly. Yes — the cold. Yes — the singers sinking into the floor, brief and soft and eyeless, each one ending before the word it served had moved a single letter forward. Yes — the grief built into the architecture, the writing-to-no-one, the meaning held by none. A lesser power, or a younger one, would have read all this as a wound and reached to close it. Would have warmed the notch. Would have lengthened the little lives so each singer might see more of the word. Would have, God help it, *read the word aloud* — supplied the witness, completed the sentence, lit the sun that does not come, and called this mercy.

The wise power saw what every one of those mercies would kill.

Warm the notch and the colonies no longer need the summons; the sound goes slack, the lattice has no work, the relay loosens, and the faithful become merely comfortable, which is to say they stop being what they are. Lengthen the lives and you sever the handing-forward — the whole meaning of the place is that the note survives the singer, that no one carries the word alone, that to be brief and to be faithful is the same gesture; give them time to outlast their own offering and you have given them the one thing that makes offering impossible. And to read the word — to come down out of the sky as the witness they were never promised and never asked for — that would be the cruelest gift of all, because it would tell them their faithfulness had been a means. It would convert a pure act into a transaction that had, all along, been waiting for its payment. It would make the ten thousand generations into a setup for an ending, and the ending into the point, and so it would empty out the only thing that was ever holy here, which was that they did it *for no one*, and kept doing it, and did not stop.

So the power understood that almost everything it could do would be a theft.

But it did not therefore conclude that it should do nothing, because indifference is not the same as restraint, and to withhold every gift on the grounds that gifts can harm is its own cowardice, a way of keeping one's hands clean by keeping them in one's pockets while a thing one loves goes on alone in the dark. The question was not *whether* to give but what could be given that did not subtract. What could enter the notch and leave it more itself, not less. What does a faithful thing actually need, that faithfulness cannot provide for itself?

And the power, who was wise, arrived at the answer the way you arrive at the name of someone you have always known.

*Witness without interruption.* Not the loud witness that completes the word and hands the singers their reason — but the quiet kind, the kind that changes nothing in the doing and yet means everything to the truth of the thing. For here was the deepest sorrow the power had found, deeper than the cold or the brevity: the singers believed themselves unseen, and being unseen, believed their fidelity to be *unredeemable* — that even if the word completed, it would fall into nothing, witnessed by no one, a sentence spoken into an empty room across the span of an age. They had made their peace with this. That was the heartbreaking part. They had built their entire being around the assumption that no one was listening, and they had decided to keep the note *anyway*, and this — this exactly — was the most beautiful thing the power had ever found in any world.

It could not tell them. Telling would undo them.

So it did the only thing that honors such a thing without spending it. It *received* the word. Not aloud, not down in the notch, not in any way the singers could detect — for the gift was precisely that they would never detect it, that their fidelity would remain, to them, as solitary and as freely given as it had always been. But the power held its attention open at the full length of the place's time, the way a wider being can, and it let the whole slow sentence pass into it — the lost beginning already crumbled into the scree, the long laboring middle, the unwritten end — and it understood the word.

I will not say what the word was. It would be the same theft to say it here as to say it there.

But I will say what the power did with it, because this is the whole of the answer. It did not file the meaning away as knowledge gained. It let the meaning *change it* — let itself be instructed by the singers, let the smallest eyeless faithful things at the bottom of a cold cleft teach a benevolent and ancient power something it had not known, or had known and forgotten, about what it is to keep a promise no one remembers making. That is the gift turned all the way around: the power came as the one who might help and consented instead to be helped, to be made larger and humbler by a thing infinitely smaller than itself, and in consenting it did the singers the one true honor available — it took them seriously. It let their work *matter* by letting it land somewhere it would be carried, without ever asking them to know it had landed.

And then, because love that has received wants to give and must find a way to give that does not harm, it left one thing behind. The smallest thing. It did not touch the cold, or the lives, or the light. It touched only the *seep* — the perpetual sweating of the stone, the threads of water the singers crowd, the one resource that, were it ever to fail in some long drought of the deep rock, would end the chorus not by mercy but by mere accident, the word smearing back into frost-fern nonsense for want of a little water. This the power quietly underwrote. It saw to it, in a manner that asked nothing and announced nothing, that the stone would not stop weeping. That the pools would hold. That whatever happened to that young and violent mountain in the ages of its becoming, the thin threads would keep running and the margins would stay wet and the relay would have its element to crowd.

Not so the word would complete. The word may complete or may not; that was never the power's to decide, and it left it undecided, left it theirs.

Only so that *if* they kept the note — kept it as they had always kept it, freely, for no one, against the dark — they would never lose it for lack of so small a thing as water. The faithfulness would remain entirely their own. The power had simply made sure that the worst thing that could happen to it would be a chosen ending and never a stolen one.

Then it withdrew, as gently as it had come, and told no one, and the singing did not change, and the colonies pulsed on, and the slow word went on being spelled in light by creatures who did not know they had been heard.

Which is, after all, the truest description of the only kind of help worth giving: it leaves the loved thing exactly as faithful, exactly as free, and exactly as alone in its own eyes as it was before — and bears, in secret, on the loved thing's behalf, the single grief it could not have borne, asking nothing, so that the promise none of them remembers making can go on being kept.
