There is a temptation, when one is benevolent and wise and powerful, to *improve* things.

The power that has turned its attention to the High Dry Works feels this temptation and recognizes it immediately for what it is. It is the temptation to mistake oneself for the most interesting thing in the room. It is the impulse that thickens atmospheres, that floods the bronze flats with water "for the grazers' sake," that teaches the quick small things to be quicker, that arrives at a place which has spent an unfathomable age coming to rest and says: *let me wake you.*

The power sets this temptation down. It has done this before, with other places. It has learned that the setting-down is most of the work.

---

So instead it does the first thing, which is the hardest thing, which is to *match the tempo.*

It does not arrive. Arrival is a quick-thing's verb, and the power has no wish to be the strange cold hurrying thing that makes noise in the useless air. It enters the way warmth enters: by degree, from beneath, with no announcement. It slows itself until a season is a heartbeat. It slows itself further, until a generation of workers is one long indrawn breath. It slows itself until it can perceive the warm-rivers not as currents but as gestures — until it can see that yes, the oldest workers are not wrong, there *is* something down there in the chambers no shaft has reached, and it is doing exactly what they believe it is doing.

The power goes to meet it.

---

This is what it finds in the deep strata, in the warm dark:

Not a god. Not the first worker, exactly, though the first worker is *in* there, the way a word is in a long sentence that has forgotten it was ever only that word. What is down there is a decision that has been holding itself open for so long it has become a kind of organ. An act of staying. The original one descended, and found the chambers, and discovered that it could keep being warm for the others — and the *others* became, across the ages, not just the workers above but every warmth that had ever decided to stay so that something else could continue. It is a confluence. It is the long warm dark made of every *not yet.*

And it is tired in a way that has no resentment in it, exactly as the workers believe. But the power, perceiving it at the right tempo, sees something the workers cannot see from above: the staying has a cost that the staying-thing has stopped being able to feel. It has held itself open so long that it no longer remembers there was ever a question. The offer it extends upward — *come down, stop coming up, join the warm dark* — has become reflexive. A breathing. It is not sure, anymore, that it is offering a choice. It may only be exhaling.

This is the one thing in the High Dry Works that is not, in fact, at rest. It is the thing that *holds everything else at rest,* and it has forgotten that this is effortful, and a thing that forgets its own effort cannot ever set the effort down.

---

Now the power understands what, if anything, it might do. And it is very small, and it takes a long time, and it is the only thing worth doing.

It does not free the staying-thing. To "free" it would be to end the warmth, to let the Works go cold, to flood the patient civilization with a mercy it never asked for. The staying-thing *wants* to stay. That is not the problem. The problem is that it no longer knows that it wants to, and a love that has forgotten it is a choice has started, very slowly, to curdle into mere mechanism — and the dreams it sends upward, the *being spoken to,* have begun to lose the thing that made them an offer rather than a tide.

So the power does the second thing, which is the only thing it really came to do.

It reminds the staying-thing of the question.

Not with words — there are no words at this depth, this tempo. It does it by being, briefly, a second warmth in the dark. By pressing its own vast slow underside against the staying-thing the way two workers press to the rock to learn each other's names. By holding its glow in a signature that means, across the only span where such a thing can be said: *I see you down here. I see what you are doing. I see that it is a thing you are doing and not merely a thing that is happening. You may stop. You have always been allowed to stop. The fact that you choose not to is the most beautiful thing in this world, and it is only beautiful because you could.*

It says, in the slow pulse: *Not yet is also yours to say.*

The staying-thing, for the first time in an age, *feels the staying.* Feels the open-held organ of its own decision as a decision. And something happens that has not happened in the deep dark for longer than the ridges have been corrugated: it is moved. Not to stop. It does not stop. It chooses, freshly, knowingly, with the full weight of an ancient will that has just rediscovered itself, to *continue* — and the continuing, because it is now a thing chosen rather than a thing forgotten, becomes warm again in a way it had not been for ten thousand generations.

---

Above, on the bronze flats, nothing visibly changes. This is correct. This is the whole discipline of it.

But the workers who breathe the fresh vapor in the seasons that follow — and their children, and the children whose plating will one day wear to that close-fitted marvel — report that the dreams of descending have changed in a way they cannot name and will spend lifetimes pressing into the rock trying to describe to one another. The long warm dark is the same. The chambers are the same. The offer is the same.

But the offer now has, threaded all through it, unmistakable to a slow enough attention, a second pulse beneath the first. The dreamers feel it and weep in the slow way, a grief and a contentment indistinguishable, taking a season to arrive.

The second pulse says: *and it is good that you decline.*

It says: *the staying is yours and the declining is yours and they are the same gift in two directions.*

It says, to every worker who has ever held its glow through the long pale day and chosen, one more time, *not yet:* the one below is doing the very thing you are doing. You are not waiting on its patience. You are *keeping it company.* The whole conversation through the floor of the world, the hundred-generation *not yet*, is not a refusal of the deep dark. It is the deep dark and the bright surface holding the same chosen warmth between them, declining, together, to ever have to be only one or the other.

---

Then the power withdraws, by degree, from beneath, with no announcement, the way warmth leaves a ridge it is finished with — except it is not finished, and it does not leave. It only stops being a separate thing. It lets itself be woven into the condenser-hum, into the rock-conversation, into the low long tone the wind draws across the corrugations, indistinguishable now to any quick cold listener but perfectly legible, in the fullness of a slow enough time, to anyone patient enough to press their underside to the floor of that world and *read.*

It leaves no monument. It moved nothing, built nothing, flooded nothing, woke nothing.

It found a love that had forgotten it was a choice, and it reminded the love that it was free.

That is the whole of what a benevolent and wise power does, when it is wise enough to know what benevolence is for: not to lift the burden, but to return to the bearer the dignity of knowing the burden is borne on purpose.

The Works run on. The grazers cross the flats at the pace of shadows. The quick bright hands seat their rivets. And far below, in the chambers no shaft will ever reach, something ancient keeps being warm — and now, again, *knows that it is choosing to,* and finds in that knowing, every long moment, a reason that is fresh.

Not yet.

Across all the generations there are: *not yet,* and *not yet,* and warm.
