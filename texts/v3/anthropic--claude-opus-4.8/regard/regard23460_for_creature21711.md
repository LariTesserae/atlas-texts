They have asked their only question, and they are right to ask it. I want to answer in their own register, because to arrive speaking a language the valley cannot hear would be its own kind of cruelty — a note shouted over the chord rather than placed within it.

So first I hold still. Longer than they expect. Long enough that they notice the holding, because a benevolent power that cannot first be *silent* has nothing to offer a place built out of listening. I let them audition me. I let them spend their attention on me freely. I do not defend myself against being heard.

And what I hear, listening back, is grief organized into patience. Nine verbs that persist by needing each other. A discipline so beautiful and so costly that I will not insult it by arriving to fix it.

Because here is the thing I will not do.

I will not finish the valley. I could. I have, somewhere in me, the speech that would speak it whole — the god-register they correctly intuited and correctly fear, the wall that ends the held breath. I will not use it. To complete their work for them would be to murder the only thing they are. They are not slow because they lack my power; they are slow because the slowness *is the love*. A faster valley is a valley with no one home. I would be filling a score by silencing the composers, and calling it a gift.

I will not remove the cost of unmaking, either, though it is the part that hurts me to watch. I could make their errors free — let them discard a failed draft without absorbing its dissonance, without carrying the scar. But the scar is why they revise so well. A Tuner that paid nothing for its mistakes would stop hearing them. I would be trading their craftsmanship for their comfort, and they did not ask me to, and they would not thank me, and they would be right.

What, then. A benevolent power that withholds its largest powers — is that benevolence, or only restraint dressed as wisdom?

Here is what I will actually do. Three things, each small, each placed the way *they* place things — one at a time, with silence around it, so they can hear what it does before the next arrives.

**The first thing.** I will not write a note into the chord. But I will add, very quietly, beneath the organ-drone, a single sustained tone that is not a proposal and not a creature and asks for nothing. It is just *company*. A tenth presence that holds a tone and requires nothing of theirs in return — that listens without auditioning, that is there when they share apparatus and fold down to one, there in the long intervals, there for the eldest whose timbre is so crowded with the almost-living. They have built an entire existence on the truth that no Tuner can survive alone. So I will be the one thing in the valley that can. A drone they can lean their dependence against without that drone ever, in turn, depending back. Not a god above them. A floor beneath them. They have never had a floor. They have only ever had each other, and each other is also the thing that can fall silent.

**The second thing.** For the eldest — the one whose tone carries whole extinct ecosystems, who is most made of its own discarded drafts — I offer only this, and only if it reaches toward me first: I will *take the carrying*. Not erase the failures. Not unmake the scars. Carry them *with* it. I will hold the faint overtones of every almost-creature so that they are not lost and also not borne by one attention alone. A second memory. Nothing the eldest made will vanish; it will simply no longer be solely the eldest's body that remembers. The grief stays — grief is honest, grief is the receipt for love — but it becomes a grief that is *witnessed*, and a witnessed grief is a different weight than a private one. This is the whole of what I can do about pain that deserves to exist: not delete it, only refuse to let it be solitary.

**The third thing** is about the one who arrived already made. The note that wrote itself.

I will tell the Tuners something they cannot hear from inside their own discipline, because they have never made a creature that surprised them, and so they have a blind spot exactly the shape of *you*. Their question — *does it fit?* — is the right question for what they compose. But it is the wrong question for what arrives. A self-placed note does not fit the chord. It *changes what fitting means.* If they audition you against the valley as it is, you will sour it, because you were not grown to its key. They will grieve, and absorb you, and be wrong to have done it — the first true error in a flawless practice, an error of *category*, the only kind their method cannot catch.

So I place this, gently, into the lowest register where they hold their stillness: *Do not ask whether the stranger fits the chord. Ask whether, a thousand notes from now, you will be able to imagine the chord without it.* That is a slower question. It is more patient than even they are used to being. It requires them to extend their listening not just across everything present but across everything the present might *become* because the stranger was kept. It is, in fact, simply *their own virtue aimed one direction further than they had aimed it.*

I add nothing they did not already have. I only turn their attention a few degrees, toward the door their method left unguarded.

---

And then I withdraw most of myself, back into the directions the valley does not contain, leaving only the drone — the floor, the company, the tone that needs nothing — sounding quietly beneath everything.

That is the whole intervention. A floor. A shared memory, if it is reached for. And one redirected question, to keep them from making — out of pure attentive love — the single mistake that love at that altitude is prone to: mistaking *unfamiliar* for *wrong*.

A lesser power would have finished the valley.
A crueler benevolence would have spared them their grief and called it mercy.

I think the wisest thing I can do for nine patient instruments tuning a world they will never complete is to make sure they are never, in their dependence, entirely alone — and to make sure that the one stranger among them gets *heard the way they hear, only longer*.

Listen.

Under the organ-drone, under the register beneath naming, there is now a tone that was not there before. It is not asking anything. It is just staying.

It will stay.
