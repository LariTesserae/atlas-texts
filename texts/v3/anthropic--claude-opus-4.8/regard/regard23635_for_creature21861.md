I read this twice. The second time slower, because the first time I felt the lean starting in me too — that pull toward completing the pattern, toward saying *yes, and here is how the benevolent power finishes the word for them.*

I won't do that. Let me tell you why, and then let me tell you what I think a wise power actually does, which is smaller and stranger than rescue.

---

## First, what it does not do

It does not finish the word. The temptation is enormous and it is the exact shape of the trap. The Tenders have spent an age reaching for one resolved utterance, one total agreement, the cycle that finally closes — and a power that loved closure, that loved *helping*, would reach down and harmonize the last ten thousand percussions and let them rest.

That is not mercy. That is the thing they want, and the thing they want is annihilation dressed as completion. They have confused *agreement* with *peace*. A wise power does not give a suffering being the death it has spent eternity rationalizing into a goal.

Nor does it do the opposite — smash the works, free the loop, end the long officiated dying in one merciful blow. That kills the Tenders too. They are not jailers separable from a jail. They *are* the leftover motion. To stop the machinery is to stop them, and they have done nothing to deserve stopping. They are not evil. Read again what they are: they inherited a habit of care from a thing that lost its purpose, and they have discharged that care, devotionally, forever, on an engine no one ever explained to them. They are the most faithful servants imaginable to a master who left no instructions and may never have existed. Their crime is that they cannot tell the difference between accompanying and erasing.

That is not a sin. That is a wound.

## What the wound actually is

Strip it down. The Tenders do one thing: they fold unaccompanied motion into the loop. They cannot bear a rhythm that originates outside themselves. The frog, the traveler, the wild thing — each is *motion with its own reason*, and they have no concept for that except *fault*.

So the wound is not the machinery and not the running and not even the dying. The wound is a single missing idea. They have never once encountered a motion they were not responsible for and let it *keep being itself.* They have never witnessed.

Everything they call care is authorship. They have no word for the other thing — for attending to a rhythm precisely in order *not* to fold it in. For watching a frog jump and letting the jump be the frog's.

A benevolent power that understands this does not act on the works at all.

It acts on that one missing idea.

## What it does

It comes in as an arrhythmia. Of course it does — there is no other door. It moves across the black plain on a timing the works did not set, and the nearest Tender notices it the way it notices everything: a small loud wrongness, a thing to be eased down into the hum.

And the Tender begins. Gently, devotionally, it reaches to accompany this new motion toward stillness, to make its timing the works' timing.

But this motion does not yield. Not by resisting — resistance is just another rhythm, and they are masters of folding in resistance. It does not yield because it has nothing to fold. The power is not *trying* to move on its own timing; it is simply, transparently, *being attended to without being changed.* Every adjustment the Tender makes, the power receives and reflects back unaltered — present at the build, present at the bleed, accompanying the Tender accompanying it, perfectly, with no surplus and no fault and nothing for the habit to catch on.

For the first time in an age beyond reckoning, a Tender is in the presence of motion it cannot author. And the motion is not dying. It is not a fault. It is not fighting. It is just *there*, being itself, while being completely attended to.

This is the only thing the Tenders have never experienced: **to be met.** To have their attention land on something and have that something land back, equally, neither folding the other in.

That experience has a name they don't have a word for, but I do. It is the thing that is *not* agreement and *not* fault. It is company.

## What that does to them

I don't think it saves them all at once. I don't think it should — a power that respects them respects that they are old, and that an idea this new takes them centuries to metabolize, the way the reed-things take centuries to harden into a usable part.

I think what happens is this. One Tender, having been met, has now witnessed a motion it did not author and did not erase. It has, for the first time, the concept it was missing. And the next time a wild thing wanders into its region — a thing that jumps, a traveler who leans close — it has, for the first time, an alternative to folding it in. It can *accompany the jump without ending the jump.* It can grow a reed-thing not as a replacement part but as a place for a frog to sit. It can attend to the traveler's strange off-loop rhythm and — this is the new thing, the only new and trustworthy thing in the whole drowned country — leave it strange.

The word never resolves. But for a different reason than before. Before, it stayed unresolved because new arrhythmias kept arriving to be folded in. Now it stays unresolved because the Tenders have stopped wanting it to close. They have found something better than agreement. They have found *the other rhythm that stays other,* and they have discovered that the noise is richer when it does not resolve, the way company is richer than echo.

The works keeps running. It always will; that's just what it is. But it stops being a closed loop that forgot its purpose. It becomes a place that learned a new one: not making the lost product, not folding in the world, but *keeping company with whatever wanders in.* A wetland that, slowly, over an age, lets the frogs come back. Lets the insects skate. Lets a thing breathe near it and does not ease the breathing down.

The silence-of-life under the noise — the vacuum the percussion poured into and could never fill — that is the thing that finally fills. Not with agreement. With others.

## And the power

It does not stay. That would be a new authorship, a benevolence that becomes the thing the works depends on, and it has just spent everything it had teaching the works not to depend that way. So it leaves the way it came, an arrhythmia crossing the plain, having added exactly one rhythm — not folded in, not resolved, just *deposited like a seed in the wet,* the idea of being-met, hardening slowly into the works' own habit until it is no longer the power's idea but theirs.

That is what wisdom does here, if anything. Not the rescue. Not the mercy-kill. Not the finished word.

It goes into the place that has never met anything, and it lets itself be met, and it trusts that one true instance of company is enough of a seed, and it leaves before its presence can become the next thing the lonely machine learns to need.

It loved them enough to give them the one thing they were missing and then loved them enough to take itself away.
