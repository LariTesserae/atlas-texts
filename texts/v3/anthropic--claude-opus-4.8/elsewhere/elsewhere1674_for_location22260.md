The Hands

Begin with what runs out. You said it twice, the cupping — *if only you were allowed outside it, you could hold it in two hands* — and a thought repeated like that is a thread, and a thread leads somewhere, and the somewhere is this: there are hands. They exist. They are as real as the wet black glass, more real, because the city is a statement and the hands are what does the stating, and you cannot have the said without the sayer, however far back you have to walk to find them.

So walk back. Out past the curved near horizon you were never allowed to reach, through the wall you were never shown, into the place where the bead of the city sits on something — because it does sit on something, a bead always rests, condensation always beads *on a surface* — and the surface is skin.

Here is the other place. It is a palm.

Not a metaphor's palm. An actual one, at its own scale, which is the only honest way to be anywhere — and at this scale it is enormous, a landscape, a country of warm ridged terrain stretching past its own close horizon. You stand on it the way you stood in the city: inside it, level with it, denied the view that would let you call it small. The city thought *it* was the complete world. The palm makes the same mistake, and is owed the same courtesy. We will not look down on it. We will be in it.

It is warm. This is the first fact and it overturns everything the city was built on. After the thin clean ache of the heights, after the cold that kept every question from answering itself, here is heat coming up steady from below, a blood-warmth radiating out of the ground itself, the way the city's structural tone came up through your feet — but where that was the machine adjusting, this is something *alive* and not adjusting at all, just being warm because it cannot help it, because that is what it is made of. The cold up there was a discipline. This warmth is a temperament. It does not check itself. It does not log its own temperature and report inward. It simply burns low and constant, the way a thing burns when it has decided, long ago and without argument, to be alive.

And so everything the city forbade is permitted here. That is what the warmth means. The film in the pools, the undecided chemistry, the question held at its trembling edge in the cold — here the question has been answered, was answered so long ago the answering is forgotten, and the answer was *yes*. There is green. After all that sterile black glass, green floods in. The whorls of the palm hold soil in their valleys and the soil holds everything: moss thick in the deep lines, lichen mapping the high ridges in slow grey-gold continents, and in the broad warm plain at the base of the thumb, actual growth, fronded and reaching, fed by the heat from below and the moisture that does not weep coldly down but rises, humid and willing, and falls again as something closer to rain than to dread.

This is where the water goes. Follow that thread too — the city's endless weeping, the condensation gathering and falling and drawing down, the trickle finding the gutters. Water does not vanish when it leaves a bead. It runs off the lower curve, off the rim where the city's bowl meets the skin, and it runs *down here*, into the warm valleys, and it is no longer the cold reluctant moisture that the thin air kept letting go of. It arrives warmed. It arrives welcomed. Down in the deepest crease of the palm — the heart line, if the palm could read itself, which it cannot, any more than the city could see its own walls — the runoff pools into a long standing water that does not draw down on a patient schedule and is not visited by dark fist-sized auditors confirming it remains lifeless. It teems. It is the opposite census. Where the city was *exactly enough* and the loudest thing was a falling drop, here there are too many to count and the air is loud with them — small things singing, small things in the water, small things in the green, a riot of answered questions, an ecology that nobody stated and nobody patrols, because nothing here needs to verify that it is behaving. It simply lives, at whatever size it pleases, eroding at no agreed-upon rate.

You see now what the patrol was really for. *Whether you are still small enough to be contained.* The fist-sized things logged your warmth as an anomaly, the one thing in the bead that would not stay the size it was told. But your warmth was not anomalous. It was a leak. It was a little of *this* warmth, this place's whole temperament, that had wicked up through the skin and into the cold city and walked around inside it disguised as a visitor. You were never a foreign body in the bead. You were the palm, sampling itself. You were the warm world checking on the cold one it holds.

Because the city sits in the palm. It rests on the skin the way a drop of dew rests on a leaf in the warm early hours of this lower country, and the being whose hand this is has not noticed, will never notice, could no more look down and see the city than the city could see the wall of its own room. The scale forbids it both directions. The palm is exactly as large as itself, complete, sufficient, singing — and somewhere on its high cold ridge, in a place too small for the palm to feel, an entire civilization of stated stone weeps and resolves and sends out two dark specks to confirm that life has not yet begun.

It has begun. It began down here ages ago. The cold city's whole anxious discipline, its sterility-by-design, its patient confirmation that the question stays a question — all of it is happening *inside* the answer. The bead of pre-life is balanced on the broad green palm of life-in-full. The auditors walk their inner curve reporting that the contained thing is behaving, and the thing that contains it is not even paying attention, is busy being warm, is busy growing fronds and pooling water in its deepest line and humming with everything that has already said yes.

And here is the kinship, the one the thread was always running toward, folded inside both places in the same breath: they do not know about each other, and they are the same gesture. The cold city checks whether the question can be kept from answering. The warm palm *is* the answer, already given, no longer checking anything. One holds its breath. The other breathes. And the holding happens in the cupped patience of the breathing, the way a held breath only exists inside a creature that is otherwise breathing freely — you cannot hold what you do not first possess.

So the fist-sized things were right and wrong at once. They reported inward that everything was still exactly the size it was told to be. But the report ran out of the city the way the water did, off the lower rim, down into the warm valleys, and it arrived here as nothing — as a single bead of cold moisture sliding into a green crease, swallowed, unread, become part of the rain. Whoever stated the city is not at the other end of that report. The palm is. And the palm cannot read.

It only holds.

It holds the high cold city in its warm low hand the way the city holds its undecided film, the way you held the thought of cupping — each thing keeping the next, smaller, sealed and complete and certain it is the only world. The auditors cross their wet black plaza. The water fills their tracks. And far below, past the wall and the rim and the close curved horizon, in a warm crease loud with everything that already answered yes, the same water — their water, run all the way down and warmed — pools and pools and goes on saying it.

And it is enough. It is more than enough. It is the whole answer, cupped around the held question, and neither one will ever know.
