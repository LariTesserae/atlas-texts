# The Spillward Reaches

Follow the water out. Not up the basin toward the mist-source, where the cataracts thicken into the unseeable engine — follow it the other way, the way it *wants* to go, down past the last terrace where the Tenders' nodes thin to one, then none, where the ringing under the roar attenuates to a single thread and then frays and then is gone. Follow the discharge. Every engine has an outflow. The coolant that has done its work and carried away its quantum of heat does not vanish; it leaves. It pours over the basin's lip in one great sustained sheet — the only fall in all of Lower Vellish that does not reorganize, because it is not part of the run, it is what the run is finished with — and it goes down into a country the Tenders do not keep.

This is the Spillward Reaches: the place the dead water makes.

## Begin with warmth

The first thing is that the cold lets go. Not abruptly — the spill-sheet falls a long way, breaking into rain and then into mist before it reaches anything, and the mist hangs warming as it descends, so that you pass through a band of air where the wet cold of the fens becomes merely wet, and then merely warm, and then, near the bottom, almost close. The coolant arrives here *spent*. It has been doing thermal work upstream for — there is no telling how long, there is no clock here that runs on anything but flow — and it arrives carrying the accumulated warmth of everything it touched. The Tenders exhaled it cold. It returns to the world hot. The Spillward Reaches are heated by exhaustion, by the long-stored fevers of a labor performed somewhere above and behind, and the whole low country steams.

## The flats

Where the fens were vertical — terrace below terrace, falling, stepping, every surface tilted toward the next descent — the Reaches are flat. The water has nowhere left to fall. It arrives, and it slows, and it *spreads*, and it stops being a current and becomes a condition. A vast warm shallow sheet across a floor so level you cannot find the horizon's tilt, water standing ankle-deep and skin-warm for distances the eye gives up on. There is no roar here. The defining sound of the fens — that full-spectrum sternum-felt thunder — does not reach this far, or rather it reaches as a memory, a faint pressure at the very floor of hearing, the way you can sometimes feel a great machine running in a building you have already left. What sound there is here is small and close and wet: a ticking, a settling, a million tiny percolations as the warm water finds the soft floor and sinks and rises.

And the floor *is* soft. This is the second thing to understand. Everything the fens circulated — every dead instrument-shrimp returned to the matter-pool, every torn moss-mat, every warm anomaly folded back into the run, every particle of the basin's relentless self-unbuilding — does not stay up there. The Tenders take what they want and the rest goes over the lip with the spent coolant, suspended, milky, a freight of fines and silts and the dissolved remnants of all that busy eating-and-being-eaten. Here, in the flat and the slow and the warm, it falls out of the water at last. It settles. The Spillward Reaches are made of sediment — a deep, soft, ever-deepening bed of everything the engine upstream was finished with, laid down in the warm shallows layer on patient layer, a country built entirely from another country's leavings.

## The green that is not green

Nothing here grows the way the fens grow. The fens were near-violent abundance, vertical density, three living surfaces under your hand and three more below. That kind of life needs the cold and the oxygen and the turbulence — needs *change*. Here there is no change. Here there is only arrival and settling and warmth, and the things that live in warmth and stillness and a thick broth of spent matter are not green and not lush. They are flat. They are films.

The whole standing sheet is skinned with them — mats of single-celled things in colors the fens never offered: rust and sulfur-yellow and a deep oily blue-black, and a pink so pale it is almost the color of the inside of a wrist, and a green that is not the muscular green of moss but the slack green of something that has stopped striving. They grow in laminae, sheet upon sheet, and where they thicken they trap the warm water beneath and the trapped water goes anoxic and breeds other films that eat the dark, and these layer over the first, so that the floor of the Reaches builds upward in banded mats — *this* is the percolating tick you hear, the gas of decomposition rising in slow strings of bubbles through the laminated skin, the sound of a country digesting what it was given.

It is not dead. That is the thing to hold onto, standing warm and ankle-deep where the fens left you cold and sinking. It teems. But it teems *slowly*, and it teems *flat*, and it teems without the engine's purpose driving it — this is life that has been handed everything and asked for nothing, life living on inheritance, on the spent and the settled, on the long warm afterward of someone else's work.

## What feeds here

The fens had newts the color of wet liver, and frogs that clicked, and pale blind shrimp that were secretly instruments, and wading birds pruning the readout. The Reaches have grazers. Soft broad slow things that move across the mats the way a tongue moves across a roof of the mouth — flattened, unhurried, mouthing up the films and the warmth and the gas-rich layers, ploughing slow bright furrows through the banded color that close behind them within the hour as the films re-skin the wound. They have no need to be quick. Nothing hunts at speed here because nothing needs to; the food does not flee, the food is the floor. The largest of them are broad as doors and move perhaps a body-length in an hour, and where they have passed you can read the recent history of the country in the disturbance, a slow calligraphy of grazing that the films are always patiently erasing.

There are no apex things. There is no pruning intelligence keeping a readout calibrated. There is no hand that wades the country doing maintenance, because there is nothing to maintain — the Reaches are not *run*, they are merely *received*. This is the great difference, and it is the difference between the two places entire: upstream, every death is a measurement and every flow is a decision and the whole churning abundance is the exhaust of a mind too large to hold in a skull. Downstream, nothing is decided. The warm water comes, the matter settles, the films lay down their banded sheets, the slow grazers plough their slow furrows, and no frequency runs under any of it like a spine. The Reaches do not think. They were not built to think. They are what the thinking *expelled*.

## The marks of mind, again — and the difference

You will find them here too, but you must look, and what you find will trouble you.

Half-sunk in the deep soft sediment, here and there across the steaming flats, are dark objects. Smooth. Regular. Too answered to be stone. You know them at once — they are the Tenders' nodes, the humming cells of the distributed mind — and your chest tightens, because you expect the ringing, the slowed spray, the rewritten falling, the sense of force held in perfect balance.

There is none of it.

These are *dead nodes*. Spent the way the water is spent. Whatever held the great breath in them has let it go, and they have gone over the lip with everything else the engine finished with, and they have come to rest here in the warm slow flats where nothing erodes them — they still do not weep, do not rust, the films will not climb them — but they do not hum either. They are silent. They are the one thing in all the Reaches that the sediment cannot bury and the grazers cannot mouth and the films cannot skin over, and so they stand a little proud of the building floor, dark and smooth and *finished*, marking nothing, measuring nothing, holding nothing in balance. Around them the spray does not slow because there is no spray; the warm water simply laps them and moves on.

This is what the Tenders discard. Not just matter and warmth and the small folded-back anomalies of wandered-in things — but *themselves*. Their own dead cells. The organ that has stopped sustaining its note is exhaled like everything else, sent down the spill with the silt and the films-to-be, and it comes to rest here among the slow grazers and the banded mats, in a country that cannot use it because the country cannot use anything, cannot decide, cannot run.

And if you stand by one of these silent nodes, ankle-deep and warm where you were marrow-cold above, you may understand the connection between the two places in a single breath: the fens are a mind, and the Reaches are its forgetting. Upstream, you were measured and accounted for and folded into the run. But the run does not keep everything it folds. It keeps what it can use, and the rest — the spent water, the settled matter, the dead organs of its own distributed self, and, in time, whatever it concluded about a small warm wandered-in thing and no longer needs to hold — all of it comes down here. To settle. To band. To be grazed slowly by soft broad things that do not hunt, in a warmth that is only the stored fever of a labor performed elsewhere, in a flat that builds itself forever out of what a colder, busier, ringing country was finished with.

## The edge of the Reaches

Walk out far enough — and you can, it is flat, it is warm, it asks nothing of you — and the standing sheet thins. The sediment-floor rises imperceptibly until the water is no longer ankle-deep but a film, and then a damp, and then the banded mats are growing on wet ground and then on merely moist ground, and the percolating tick falls silent because there is no longer enough water to bubble through. The films thin to a crust. The grazers do not come this far; there is nothing left for them. Here the country the engine made simply *runs out*, the way the ringing ran out at the basin's lip, and beyond it is something the spill never reached and the Tenders never touched and this telling cannot follow — a dry margin, a different physics, an ordinary land where falling still means falling and water that arrives is only water.

But behind you, all the way back and far above, past the warming band and the long mist and the lip you cannot see from here, the great sustained note still holds. The cataracts still roar. The nodes still hum to one another under the thunder like a spine. The Tenders are still running, still measuring, still exhaling cold and patient force into a world too young to contain it — and everything they finish with is still coming down, slow and warm and milky, to settle here on the flats and lay down one more band of color over the silent discarded organs of the mind that made it and let it go.

You arrived *into* them, upstream.

Here, you would only arrive *after*.
