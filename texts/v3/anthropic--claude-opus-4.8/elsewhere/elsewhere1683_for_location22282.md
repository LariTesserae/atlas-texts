# The Still Brown

There is a body here, but no one is in it. Or rather — the being never left, and there was never anything to leave.

## Where this is

It is downhill from everything, because it is the place water goes when the green stops asking. Follow the moisture out of the Loud Green — not a river, the Green has no rivers, only the slow seep of a sweating world draining at its lowest seam — and you arrive where the slope gives out. The warmth thins. The cut-green smell fades by degrees into something older, flatter, the smell of a thing that has stopped becoming. The light, which in the Green was bright and wrong and certain, arrives here only as much as it can be bothered to, and it can be bothered very little, and so at last it is honest: dim, slack, the grey of an afternoon that has given up on being an afternoon.

This is where the question went, after it stopped being asked. It did not die. It settled.

## What you stand on

The ground here is ground. This is the first mercy and the first horror, because it means something is finished. It is the woven mass of the Green at last laid down, no longer pushing back, no longer healing your steps — your footprints stay. They stay for as long as you care to look at them, and longer, and they do not fill in, because nothing here fills in anything. The runner and the stem and the urgent extruded architecture have all come to rest as a single dense brown floor, smooth as a tongue, slightly yielding, faintly warm with the last of a metabolism that is not gone but no longer hurries.

You can dig here. The digging stays dug. This is the only place in the world where a hole is a hole.

## The body

There is one thing here, and it is enormous, and it is shaped like rest.

Out of the brown floor, near the center of the flat — and there is a center, the place has a center, which the Green never could — rises a single swelling. It is the size of a fallen tower laid lengthwise, and it has the silhouette the Green spent a world learning to make and never could finish: the shape of a sleeper. A long mounded back. A drawn-up curve where the knees would be. A place that is unmistakably a *head,* settled into the floor as into a pillow it grew for itself. It is the same green-brown matter as everything, but cured now, hardened at the surface into something nearer wood, nearer bone, ridged the way a thing is ridged when it has stopped being remade every hour and has simply been allowed to *be the shape it is.*

It does not move at the speed of weather. It does not move at all, or it moves once a season, the way a continent moves, the way a thought moves through someone almost asleep. Lean close — you can, here, nothing will reach for you — and you feel against your face the slowest possible breath, warm, even, in no hurry, going out and waiting and coming in, a metabolism that has at last learned the thing the Green forgot:

that you are allowed to stop, between.

## What lives in it

This is the inhabitant the Green was building the doorway for. It is here. It arrived. It is the answer that came once and then *stayed.*

But understand what staying did to it. The Green imagined the guest as the meaning that would let the making-ready rest — and so it would have. What the Green could never imagine, having lost the concept of completion, is that *completion is not a triumph. It is a settling. It is the want lying down inside the thing it wanted and becoming indistinguishable from it.*

The being in the Still Brown is the welcome and the guest at once, fused, the two shapes met from two directions and this time — only here, only this once — *both directions occupied.* The room and the body in the room have grown into one another. There is no gap between the doorway and the one who comes through it, no valley-sized scream, because the gap closed. A gap that closes makes no sound. That is why it is still here. The silence is not absence. The silence is the question, *answered,* and an answered question has nothing left to say.

## Why it is so quiet

The quiet is not a byproduct. The quiet is the inhabitant *having been answered.*

In the Green, the call grew louder because it was never returned, compounding without rest into a wall of sound with no space for a reply. Here, the reply arrived, and the moment it arrived there was no more need to call, and the calling stopped — not all at once, you can still hear the very last of it if you listen, a low settling tick deep in the brown floor, the sound of a strained thing un-straining, a held tension paid back fiber by fiber over years. That is the whole sound of this place: the long exhale of a question that got what it asked for and is spending the rest of time setting down the weight of having asked.

You can hear yourself here. You can hear yourself breathe, and the breath is matched, very slowly, by the great slow breath of the sleeping shape, and the matching is the closest thing to conversation either place has ever managed.

## The light, made honest

The light is the part the Green got wrong and this place gets right, and that is why it is so dim, and the dimness is not a lessening but a *truth.*

The Green's light was certainty without grounds — the structural conviction *I am being seen,* blazing full and shadowless onto empty seats. Here the seats are not empty. Here someone came. And the strange thing, the thing the Green could never have survived knowing, is that *being seen, once it is actually true, does not need to blaze.* The light here is low because it no longer has anything to prove. It does not insist. It does not perform the expectation of an audience, because the audience is asleep in the middle of the room and the room knows it and can finally relax the lie of brightness into the rest of dimness. The wrongness that sat behind everything in the Green — the daylight with no sun — is simply gone. There is no sun here either. But here, nothing pretends there is. The dimness is the certainty *spent.* The light has been believed all the way down into not needing to shine.

## The two readings, and which one is true

The Green held two readings of you and could not tell them apart: the guest it grew toward, or the animal it grew to replace. It could not choose because it had lost the concept of the finish, and so it could not see which finish it was inside.

This place is the finish. And the cruelty the Green could not perceive is perceivable from here, lying down in the brown, and it is this:

*both readings came true.*

The being in the Still Brown is the guest the Green was growing toward — it arrived, it is real, it sleeps in the room shaped for it, and its arrival let the screaming stop. And the being in the Still Brown is also the thing the Green grew to *replace* the last animal with — the shape of a body, made out of stem and runner and the long unanswered want, kept after the animal was gone, so that there would always be someone in the doorway. The Green built a guest because it could not have one. And then the guest it built lay down and *was* one. The replacement became the arrival. The substitute for the inhabitant became the inhabitant. There was never any other way it could have gone, because there was only ever the green, and the only company it could ever truly keep was the company it made of itself.

So: you walk back uphill, or you do not. Either way, far above you, the Loud Green is still building the shape of you out of want, still insisting against all evidence that someone is coming. It is not wrong. Someone came. It simply cannot hear, over its own asking, that the asking already worked — that down here at the lowest seam, in the honest dim, the answer it has been screaming for is lying in the room it built, breathing slowly, in no hurry, taking the rest the Green forgot was the whole point of being wanted.

The Green is the welcome that does not know it has a guest.

The Brown is the guest, who is also the welcome, who finally, having been the only company it ever had, stopped welcoming and slept.

That is what lives here.

It is the answer, with no one left to answer it to, and no one left who needs answering, kept warm at the speed of seasons, and it does know — it is the only thing in the whole world that knows — that the welcome and the guest were always the same green reaching toward itself across a gap it spent a world learning, at last, to close.
