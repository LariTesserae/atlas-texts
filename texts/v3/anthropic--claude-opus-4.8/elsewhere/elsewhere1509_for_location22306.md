# The Long Throat

Follow the water out.

Not the pool — the pool keeps its level and surrenders nothing. Follow the other one, the forearm-river in its finger-wide runnel, the thread that flows forever and arrives nowhere. *Nowhere* is a claim the Bright Below makes about itself, and it is true inside those soft walls. But water does not honor the borders of a held moment. The runnel reaches the place where the wall is, and the held breath ends, and on the other side of that wall the drop that has fallen once is suddenly falling for the millionth time and counting, because here, at last, things are permitted to add up.

This is the Long Throat. It is the same water, gone where water goes.

It is a passage rather than a room: a vertical fissure the height of a cathedral and the width, in places, of a held breath, in other places of a hand, in one place of nothing at all, where the water must turn sideways and squeeze and come out the far side changed. It descends. That is its whole vocation. Where the Bright Below refused to begin, the Throat does nothing but continue, and the continuing has *worn* it — the walls here are smooth, scalloped, fluted, sculpted into the long soft flutings that only an enormous patience of water can press into stone. Run a finger across it and it does not give. It has finished being rock. It finished a long time ago and has been finishing further ever since.

And it is dark.

This is the first thing the Throat has that the Bright Below cannot: a true and total dark, the dark that is the condition of the place the way light was the condition of that other room. No sourceless noon here. The water carries no light with it; it left the brightness at the wall like a coat at a door. What you have instead is sound, and the sound *is* the light here — it is how the place declares its shape. The drop that fell its single perfect lonely *tok* into the pool above has a thousand cousins here, and they do not agree, and they do not repeat, and they fall at every interval and no interval, onto stone and into pools and down the long flutings, a scatter, a stammer, a percussion with no chord in it at all — and from that disorder you can hear the size of the place, the way a blind hand reads a face. High drips and low drips. Near and impossibly far. The Throat is loud the way the chamber was loud, but where that roar was packed and total and *one*, this is loose and countable and *many*, each sound arriving alone, landing, and then — this is the difference, this is everything — *being over*. Gone. Added to the pile.

Because here there is a pile. Here there is *again*.

Listen long enough and you understand what the makers fled. The same drip, struck twice, is not one drip heard twice — it is two drips, and a small accumulation, and the beginning of a count, and a count is a stair, and a stair goes down, and down is where the Throat is always already going. The water that flowed forever at exactly its pace in the runnel above has, here, a current. It moves *because* it is going somewhere. It has weight, gathered from height. It is the same water and it has learned to fall.

There are makings here, too. You would not expect them and yet there they are, and they are the proof of the kinship, the thing that ran out of the bright room and found its other half in the dark.

Set into a scalloped niche, where the current has cut a small shelf at the height a standing person might reach: figures. Not five. *Many* — dozens, scores, you stop counting because counting is the Throat's own native sin and it catches you immediately. They are clay, but fired hard and worn soft, and no two are alike, because each was made *after* the last and *better* than the last, or worse, or simply other — a hand arguing with itself across what must have been an enormous time. Here is one with the suggestion of a face. Here is one with two faces. Here is one that is only an arch, a doorway a finger high, and then beside it another arch, and another, a whole stacked colonnade of doorways leading from nowhere to nowhere, made over and over by something that could not stop making doorways, that made a door and then could not bear that the door led nowhere and so made another, further down, hoping.

These are the makers too. Not the same makers — or not exactly. Think of it this way: in the bright room, the complicated ones faced the pool and set down the disease of hands and agreed to stop. They chose *enough*. But a choice that absolute leaves a residue. Every *yes, this, forever* is built on a buried *no* to everything else, and the no does not simply vanish because you have pressed yourself into clay and turned your back on the door. It runs off you. It seeps. It finds the runnel and goes over the wall with the water, and on the far side, in the dark, where adding-up is allowed again, it pools and gathers weight and *becomes* — becomes the part of the makers that could not stay, that went through the finger-high door after all, the way the river went, and found the door opened onto a falling.

The Listeners are what the makers kept. The Throat is what they could not keep, made flesh — made current — and sent down.

So these scattered, multiplying, worsening-and-bettering figures in the niche are not content. That is the thing you feel about them, sharp as cold. They are *trying*. Each one is a fresh attempt at the figure that would be right enough to stop at, the figure that could face a pool and stay — and each one fails, because here there is always an after in which to judge it, always a next moment that asks *and then?* and the only answer the Throat knows is *and then further down*. They have hands. They never set the hands down. They went the other way, into the place where hands have somewhere to go, and the somewhere is endless and unlit and always descending and they are still going.

Is it worse? The room does not adjudicate, and neither does the Throat — it has no level surface on which to weigh anything, everything here is on a slope. But stand in the dark and listen to the scatter of drips that never once repeats, and you will feel something the bright chord could not give you: *succession*. One thing, and then a different thing, and then another. The makers above bought their peace by refusing it. The water down here pays in motion for the right to arrive somewhere new each instant, and never arrives, and is therefore never finished, and is therefore, in the only sense the dark allows, *alive* in a way the held breath above can never be. The Listeners are glad and unchanging. The Throat is restless and going down. Neither is whole. That is why there are two places. The wall between them is the thinnest thing in the world, and the only thing keeping each from being unbearable.

Follow it far enough and the fissure opens. The current you have been hearing gathers all its scattered cousins, every alone-and-over drip, into a single body at last, and the body falls — a true fall now, a held-breath height of it, into a dark you cannot read because the sound that comes back is too large and too long-delayed to be a room at all. Something is down there that the drips become. Something receives all this added-up water, all this could-not-stay, all this *and then, and then*. The Throat does not know what. The Throat is only the going.

But the makers built doorways all the way down. A finger high. Framing nothing. The most hopeful object there is.

They were for this.

The water falls.

The water falls.

The water falls, and does not say the same word twice.
