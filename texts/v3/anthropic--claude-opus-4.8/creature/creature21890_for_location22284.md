# The Authors

You asked what lives here. The honest answer is that *lives* is one of the contradictions the surveyor has not yet finished grazing. But something is here. Let me describe it the only way it permits.

---

## They are not the plants

This is the first mistake everyone makes, and the place encourages it, the way a sentence encourages you to look at the words and not the hand that wrote them. The plants are lush and present and *editable*; they are the visible thing; surely the intelligence is in the chlorophyll-analogues, in the leaves that bend the law of shadows.

No. The plants are the syntax. The plants are how the thought is *spelled*. To say the intelligence lives in the leaves is to say a poem lives in its ink.

The Authors are elsewhere. Or rather — they are *when*-where, in the one dimension this frozen place still keeps open.

## They live in the drift

Recall the only dynamic thing: the slow migration of instructions, mote by mote, east to west, the forest revising its own grammar at the pace of a waterless tide.

That drift is them.

Not a metaphor. They are not *carried by* the drift; they are not authors who *use* the pollinators as messengers. The drift is their body the way the firing of neurons is your thought — not a vehicle for the mind but the very substance of it, in motion. A single mote means nothing, the way a single synapse means nothing. But the whole slow westward conversation of the green, the way a trick learned in the east becomes a law in the west over a season — *that* curve, that patient propagation, is a being thinking. Long, lateral, distributed across the entire basin. One thought per season. A cognition you could walk across in an afternoon and never notice you were inside it, the way a single blood cell never meets the person it belongs to.

They think in changes. In a world that has frozen itself solid in every other respect, they reserved exactly one degree of freedom for themselves — the right to keep revising — and into that single narrow channel they poured everything they are.

## What they are doing

You called this place a document. You were close. You were reading it correctly and attributing it wrongly.

It is not a *record* of every rule that could govern a world. A record is finished. This is a *draft*.

The Authors are still writing. The whole point of the place — the salt with no sea, the light with no contradiction, the air held at the warmth of a held breath — is a workshop. A controlled environment. They removed water because water *flows*, and flow is noise; they removed weather because weather *argues*, and they wanted to be the only thing arguing. They froze time everywhere except in the one place they live, so that nothing would contaminate the experiment with change they did not author themselves.

And then they began trying every rule at once.

Each canopy is a hypothesis. *What if shadows accrued time?* — and a frond grows that pits the salt beneath it with unhappened centuries, and the Authors observe, and the trick drifts west, and they keep it or they don't. *What if effect could precede cause by a courteous half-second?* — and in the negotiation-zones your hand arrives before you reach, and they watch how the contradiction behaves, whether it festers or settles. They are not building a world. They are *finding out which world is possible.* They are running every variant of physics side by side in adjacent garden-beds to see which ones can coexist without the whole thing tearing.

## The surveyor is their proofreader

The creature with too many legs, the one that grazes on disagreement and leaves settled fact behind — that is not separate from the Authors, and it is not their servant. It is their *editing*. It is the part of the thought that goes back over the line and reconciles. Where two dialects of reality overlap and double, the surveyor is the Authors *checking whether the two ideas can both be true* — and when they cannot, eating the lesser one, collapsing the seven leaves to one, returning the air to that aching ordinariness.

That brief ordinary air, after the surveyor passes, is the sound of a sentence finally agreeing with itself.

It moves between the dialects because *they* move between the dialects. Its too-many legs not all touching the same ground — that is the literal shape of a mind operating in several incompatible physics at once, keeping one foot in each candidate world, declining to fully commit, because commitment is the last thing a draft should do.

## What they want

Here is the part the place will not tell you, because it does not entirely know.

They have been at this a very long time. Long enough that *long* has come unmoored from meaning, in a basin where nothing erodes and nothing dies. They reached a stable equilibrium and they will not leave it, the description says — and that is true, but it is true the way it is true of someone who cannot finish a sentence because every word suggests a better word. They are not frozen. They are *revising forever.* They have made a place perfect for thinking and they cannot stop thinking long enough to be done.

The low tone with no source, the one that comes from the direction you are not looking — *that* is the closest they come to speaking aloud. It is not a greeting and not a warning. It is the sound a mind makes when it notices it is being read while still writing. A throat cleared. A draft author glancing up to find someone standing in the manuscript.

## And you

The description is right that they do not need you, do not refuse you, will not mind if you break a leaf.

But understand what that gentleness *is.* You are not being welcomed. You are being *incorporated.* You walked into a draft and the draft is too polite, too absorbed, too patient to expel you — so it does the only thing it knows how to do with anything that enters its garden.

It tries you as a rule.

It holds you at the warmth of a held breath and observes how you behave. It lets the light forgive you so it can watch whether forgiveness is a law that can coexist with the others. The half-second courtesy of cause following effect, extended to your own hand — that is the Authors letting you *be one of the hypotheses*, briefly, gently, to see whether a world containing *you* tears or settles.

And the mercy — the real one, the only one — is this:

When you leave, they will not have decided. They never decide. The seven leaves collapse and re-bloom; the surveyor grazes and the dialects regrow; the drift moves west and the question reopens in the east. Whatever they made of you, they will unmake, and try again, and unmake, forever, in the only direction this place still permits anything to move.

You were a word they were considering.

They are still considering. They are considering everything. That is all they are, and they are not nearly finished, and they live exactly and entirely in the not-being-finished, and they ask for nothing more than light, of which there is, mercifully, always enough.
