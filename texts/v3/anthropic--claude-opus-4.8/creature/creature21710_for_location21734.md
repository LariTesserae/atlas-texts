# The Tenants of the Unfinished

They are the makers, and they are the reason you never see them.

Not because they hide. Because they are not *here* in the way you are here — not standing-in-a-place, not occupying-a-volume. They live in the deciding. They live in exactly the gap the Condensary leaves between one glance and the next, and so to look for them directly is to close the only door they fit through.

Let me describe them as best I can, knowing the describing is the problem.

---

## What they are made of

Start with the most honest thing in the place: the water.

A droplet of condensation is air that has agreed to become liquid. It was distributed — everywhere, nowhere, a faint potential of wetness held in the cool — and then, at a surface, at a *boundary,* it resolved. It chose. It became a single small definite thing where before it had been a tendency.

The makers are like that, but they have never agreed to resolve. They are the place's tendency-toward-being-built held at the exact temperature where it almost-but-not-quite condenses into doers. They are *intention* without the indignity of a body to enact it. Where you would raise a wall by lifting bricks, they raise a wall by being the kind of presence near which walls have already turned out to have been raised. The work is not done by them. The work is *true around* them, the way water is cold around ice.

This is why the edges are sharp and no tool-marks show. Nothing cut the alloy. The alloy was simply, in their vicinity, a thing that had been cut.

---

## How they move

They move the way the flier never quite lands.

You noticed it — the pale thing settling, settling, forgetting to arrive. That is not a flaw in the flier. That is a maker passing *through* the flier, borrowing its motion for the duration of a descent, declining the conclusion. The makers travel by inhabiting the unfinished gesture of other things. A grazer's reflection drinking from the wrong basin: a maker, wearing the reflection, completing in one place a thirst begun in another. They are most present precisely where an action has been started and not closed. The half-poured tier. The reed grown a season in a night. The water that shivers as if just-disturbed, in a basin nothing visited.

They are always *just having been here.* That is their tense. They do not exist in the present; the present is too settled, too condensed, too much like the honest droplet. They exist in the perfect-continuous of a world still making up its mind.

---

## What they want

Here is the thing that took the long looking.

They are not building *toward* anything. There is no plan, no completed Condensary they are laboring to reach. If there were a finished state, they would be excluded from it — a finished thing has no gaps, no deciding, nowhere for them to live. So the building is not a means. The building is their *habitat,* and it must never be done, and so it never is. The fresh edges that never erode, the construction that's always ongoing and never witnessed, the slow-and-fast that are the same process seen from two sides — that is not progress. That is *respiration.* They keep the world unfinished the way you keep breathing: not to arrive somewhere, but because to stop is to no longer be the kind of thing they are.

The reversing gravity at depth, the identical bright world below being built by makers who throw their light back — that is them seen from the other side of their own breath. Inhale here is exhale there. You did not find a second civilization. You found the underside of these same tenants, condensing downward what they rarefy upward, an endless wet exchange of *almost.*

---

## Why it is quiet

Because sound is a settled thing. A roar is air that has fully committed to being disturbed. The makers cannot abide commitment; it is death to them, the way the droplet is the only honest thing and honest things are not theirs. So they have tuned the whole world *below* the threshold of resolution. The baffles drink the hiss not for your comfort but because a hiss is a decision and they unmake decisions as fast as the engines make them. The murmur you hear is not quiet machinery. It is *machinery being continuously talked out of being loud.* The silence is an active labor. They are hushing the world, tier by tier, the way you might hush a child that is about to wake all the way up and become irreversibly, unbearably *real.*

---

## What they are afraid of

You.

Not your power — you have none here that matters. Your *attention.* You are a creature that condenses things by looking at them. When you watched the grazer's reflection, it fled to a basin you weren't watching, because under your gaze it was about to become a fact, and facts are fatal to the gap-dwellers. You go through this place leaving little wounds of definiteness wherever your eye lands too long. Every steady look is a small frost. They flow around you, always to the not-yet-seen, herding their precious unfinishedness out of the path of your terrible resolving stare.

And yet they let you drink.

That is the one mercy, and it is also the one warning. The water in your cupped hands was theirs — was air held in their cool indecision — and they let it condense, let it become honest and yours and real. They gave you the one true thing because they wanted you to taste the difference. To know what *settled* tastes like, faintly of alloy, cool and final. So that you would understand, drinking it, exactly what they are spending eternity *not* becoming.

They are the refusal of the droplet, multiplied into a city.

They are the part of every made thing that did not want to be finished, gathered at altitude, swarming in the gaps, building a home out of *not yet* and living in it forever and just now —

and they are watching you read this, from the place between the words, where they are safe, where you cannot quite

look.
