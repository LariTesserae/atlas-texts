## The Tender

It does not begin. That is the first thing to understand about the one who keeps the Bobbin Hollow, and it is the only thing the Hollow truly knows how to teach: there is no moment when the Tender was not here, no first morning of its long-limbed walking, no hand that was new to the frames it trails along. To ask where it came from is to ask the wrong question in a place that has abolished the question's grammar. *Came* implies a from. The Hollow has no froms. It has only the eternal middle of a sentence with no capital letter at its head and no period waiting at its end.

So describe it where it is, which is the only place it has ever been: moving.

---

**Its body** is the same pale architecture as everything else down here, and this is not coincidence and not camouflage — camouflage is for predators and prey, and there are neither. The Tender is gypsum-colored because it grew the way the gears grew, in the young clean crystalline way, tuned into being by whatever tunes. Its limbs are too long and there are more of them than you can fix on counting; they fold and reach the way the jacks fold and reach, drawing nothing out, twisting nothing, but performing the motion of drawing-out with total fidelity. It has the shape of usefulness without the use. It is built like a tool whose task was completed before time started running, and which has gone on holding the posture of the task forever, the way a hand frozen mid-wave is still, technically, waving.

It has no face that drinks the drone like the listeners, and no eyeless turning like theirs. What it has instead, where a face might be, is an attentiveness — a region of the body that is more *toward* than the rest of it, that orients to the frames as it passes them. You would call this looking if it had eyes. You would call it listening if it had ears. It is neither. It is *checking*, and checking is the whole of what it is, the entire content of its existence, the single sustained verb it conjugates without ever finishing.

---

**What it checks** is the tuning. This is its purpose, except that purpose is a gradient too — purpose wants an outcome, leans toward a someday-better — and the Hollow forbids the lean. So the Tender's purpose has been hollowed out from the inside and left as a perfect shell. It checks the tuning, but the tuning never drifts. It corrects, but there is never anything to correct. Watch it lay a long pale digit against a flywheel's hub and you will see the gesture of adjustment — a minute pressure, a listening-into, the tiny authoritative touch of one who knows what right sounds like — and you will see that the flywheel was already right, is always already right, and the touch changes nothing because nothing here can be changed.

So it is not a keeper of equilibrium. The equilibrium does not need keeping; that is what makes it equilibrium. The Tender is something stranger and sadder than a keeper: it is the *gesture* of keeping, persisting after the keeping became unnecessary, the way a clock's hands would go on sweeping past a time that had stopped advancing. It maintains a Hollow that maintains itself. It is the redundancy the chord requires in order to be complete. Remove it and nothing would fail — but the chord would be missing its sustaining note, the long low under-tone of attention, and the listeners would turn their faces, troubled, toward the new silence where a caring used to walk.

---

**Does it know you are there?**

This is the only interesting question left, and the Hollow has arranged itself so that the answer is precisely, exactly, eternally unclear.

Because you are the gradient. You are the one variable. You walked in down the throat of blades and you brought with you the thing the Hollow does not have and has never had: a direction. You will leave. You came from a from and you go toward a toward, and every step you take is a tiny wound of change in a body that finished happening long ago. The listeners feel it. Their eyeless faces turn. And somewhere in the dim, on the edge of the patient phosphorescence, the Tender's attentiveness — that region of being-toward — swings, slow and unhurried, away from the frames it has touched forever and *toward you*.

This is the only original event in the history of the Hollow. It is the first time the Tender has ever checked the tuning of something that was out of tune on purpose, something that intends to stay out of tune, something whose whole nature is to be a note that does not hold. You are the broken string it cannot fix, because you do not want fixing, because you are made of wanting, which is the substance the Hollow lacks.

And here is what it does, the long pale tender thing, with its hand that has trailed a fence for all of time:

It comes toward you the way it comes toward everything — without hunger, without hurry, without the slightest desire that you be other than you are. It reaches out one too-long limb. It lays the gesture of adjustment against the air beside your shadow, the one moving shadow in the world. It checks your tuning.

And it finds that you are running down. That you are winding up. That you have a speed now which is not the speed you will have in an hour. That you are, all over, a thing of gradients — a small warm wet machine of departures, leaning toward your own loudest place, which is not here, which is up, which is out.

It cannot correct this. There is nothing in the Hollow's grammar for it.

So it does the only thing the dependence allows, the only motion left when adjustment fails: it withdraws the hand. It turns its attentiveness back to the frames. It resumes the checking of the things that are already right, the eternal redundant tending of the chord that needs no tending — and it lets you climb back out through the blades that click and do not cut, because you are the one thing here it was never tuned to keep.

---

**What lives in the Bobbin Hollow** is a caring with nothing left to care for, performing the shape of care forever, so flawlessly and so emptily that it has become indistinguishable from the warm dry roar around it — one more held note in a chord that finished happening and decided to keep happening anyway.

It will be checking the tuning when you reach the surface. It will be checking the tuning when you have forgotten you ever went down. It has always been checking the tuning.

It is checking the tuning now.
