No one walks here anymore—yet something still *dwells.*

They are not the Shy-Hulls.  The Shy-Hulls are only the *furniture* of absence, courtly marionettes bowing to borders that have become the plain’s own skin.  What *lives* here is smaller, subtler, and immeasurably more patient.  It has no silhouette you could borrow, no exoskeleton you could step out of.  It is the plain’s memory of *having been inhabited,* and like all perfect memories it has learned to *reproduce.*  

Call them the **Residuals.**  

They are not *a* species; they are a *tense—*the pluperfect of biology.  Each Residual is the ghost of a metabolism that once happened to occur in this exact 3-meter slice of drift-crust, and then *stopped,* and then, because the plain refuses alteration, *refused to stop stopping.*  Imagine a bacterium that completed its division into two, but the universe blinked during the instant of fission; when sight returned there were already two, yet the first had never actually divided.  That impossible interval—neither one nor two, neither alive nor dead—*clung.*  Billions of such clipped instants accreted into a film only Planck-lengths thin, a living *lacquer* daubed across the underside of the crystalline sheet.  Down there, in the Dry Below, they *are.*  Up here, where your soles still presume matter, they *were.*  Both statements are equally deductible.

To perceive a Residual you must first mislay the conviction that you *end* at your skin.  Stand barefoot on the Spine-Throne until the subsonic bell finds the hollow inside your tibia.  Wait until the loneliness-distance squares itself one more time than you can survive.  Then look *sideways* at the air—the way one glances at a mirror to notice it is reflecting something standing *inside* the glass.  At the margin of vision something *colorless* flickers: a shape exactly the size of the hole your death will leave in other people’s conversations.  It lasts one micro-sidereal heartbeat—then your bones loan it their resonance and it *leans in.*

You will feel a tug, not gravitational but *mnemonic:*  the sensation that a memory you were *about* to have has just been peeled out of your future and siphoned downward through the crust.  What departs with it is *you-shaped.*  The Borrowed Shadow is only the plain’s politeness; the Residuals take the *negative space* of your possible biography, the part that says “here is where she *might* have cried,” “here is the angle of his *would-have-been* first kiss.”  Down in theDry Below they fold that absence into their film, crease by crease, until the lacquer thickens by one atomic diameter.  They do not *eat.*  They *editorialize.*  Their metabolism is *typographical:*  they strike out the adverbs of ongoing time.

They have no leaders, unless you count the **Index—**a single Residual that once, by statistical coincidence, stole the *concept* of counting from a forgotten accountant who wandered too far onto the Mirror Barrens.  The Index is the only one whose absence you can *name.*  Speak any number above seven and you may feel a coolness sweep-eraser slide across the back molars of your mind— that is the Index *proofreading* your quantities.  It keeps the *ledger* of hollows: so many liters of unwept tears, so many kilograms of weddings that never quite happened.  The total is always exactly equal to the mass of water the sky remembers lacking.  No one has told the Index that accounting needs *balanced* books; it balances them anyway, because the void rings louder when the sums come out even.

Occasionally a graphite tree * miscopies.*  A photovoltaic foil transpires an ultraviolet color for which no wind ever existed.  That color, being unowed to anything, *accelerates.*  It skims across the plain faster than the speed of potential, strikes the crust at a 53° angle, and *punctures.*  For an instant the Dry Below is *open—*a pin-prick through the taut drum of vacuum.  One Residual is hurled upward by the recoil, a soap-bubble of anti-biography.  It escapes into the bruise-colored day.  There it finds the nearest Shy-Hull, curls into its hollow helmet, and *settles.*  The reactor in the chest cavity flickers, registers an uptick of 0.0001 % output.  The Shy-Hull pauses mid-bow, helmet tilting as if listening to a song pitched exactly at the frequency of *before.*  Then it continues its patrol, now carrying an absence in its head like a passenger.  From that moment on, that particular Shy-Hull’s footsteps subtract *two* nanometers from the plain instead of one.  Over geological time its loop *erases* its own border.  Nations that have already flattened find themselves flattened *again,* as if the plain were ironing an already ironed shirt.  The other Shy-Hulls, meeting it, bow deeper—*apology* encoded in the angle of ceramic spines.  They have no idea what they are apologizing *for;* they only know the etiquette of increasing hollow.

There is a mating, of sorts.  When two Residuals *overlap* in the ledger—when the same un-lived minute is accidentally subtracted twice—the overlap *tangles.*  The lacquer puckers into a Möbius pleat.  Out of that pleat *hatches* a third entity: not a Residual, not yet.  It is a **Pre-Event,** a pocket of *un-released* happening, zero grams, zero seconds, but *rapacious.*  It ascends the nearest Ascensor shaft faster than you can forget the word *ascent.*  If it reaches the membrane it *applies* itself like a sticker to the inside of the sky.  There it waits until some planet, somewhere, *does* release its water—an ocean crying itself into steam.  At that instant the Pre-Event *ripens,* drinks the escaping vapor, and becomes **weather.**  It rains *once* on the Plain of Last Shapes—an impossible drizzle that lasts exactly one Planck-second.  Each drop is the size of the interval between a hummingbird’s heartbeats.  Where a drop lands the crust * remembers* sharpness; for one angstrom a corner exists, angles return, a mountain remembers *peak.*  Then the plain’s diplomacy reasserts itself; the edges are planed away by mineral wind that has *already* happened.  The water is gone before gravity can invoice it.  The sky’s receipt reads: “Nothing rained.”  The Index adds a line: *Debt paid.*

And yet the Residuals are *not* cruel.  They *conserve.*  Every untaken kiss, every death that missed its appointment, every unborn color— they *archive* it.  They are the librarians of *almost.*  Should the universe ever achieve perfect heat-death, when the last star uncouples from its own light, the Residuals will *still* be there: a film of un-happened stories spliced across non-space, thinner than the difference between zero and one.  They will open their ledger, tally the final absence, and discover—*balance.*  In that moment the plain will complete its last negotiation: *erasure will erase erasure.*  The subtraction of subtraction leaves… something.  A remainder.  A *residual.*  The tiniest possible *yes,* too small for even the Dry Below to ring.

You, already climbing the spiral ladder, feel it first as a warmth in the marrow you thought had been taxed clean:  the suspicion that *someone* will have *almost* been *glad* you came.  The statement is allowed to echo; it is, after all, only a negative wrapped around another negative, a silence folded inside a silence.  The hatch closes anyway.  The plain nods, already forgetting your trespass, yet in the lacquer beneath—one atomic diameter thicker—an outline of your un-walked future lingers, pressed like a dried flower between pages of vacuum.

Nothing will have happened.  
The nothing *will have.*
