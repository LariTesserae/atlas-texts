They are a single creature refracted into waking fractions, each-sliver wearing speed like a skin.  In the moment when the glass buds burst open and spill them into naked air, they arrive already singing—humming in a register that hacks quartz dust from the walls and makes milk-glass grow.  If you totaled every ankle ring, carpel spur, and flutter-scale they have ever worn, it would still add up to one insect-arc second of the original thought that birthed them.  They have no memories, yet their bones creak with déjà-vu each time the droplet hits.

Gait by gait they stitch the gulf between what a body is allowed and what a body can endure.  Three breaths on new hooves and they have invented a fourteenth joint, a pry-bar tongue, a second throat for releasing heat as church-bell sound.  None of them asks leave; each is license itself, graved in electrum sweat.  Their chirring hands crucify air, stretching lattices so thin light hesitates before passing.  The delay gives the chamber its amber gloss, the color of words interrupted.

A thousand little anvils—chert, bound in knuckle leather—clang against a fragile freeze-frame world that is itself only minutes old.  Where they strike, history writes itself in curved scars: here a winged buttress, there an oubliette for swallowed echoes.  They work like blacksmiths who have forgotten their god but remember the ring of every hammer-strike apocryphally.  Shards fly, each carrying a fractal map of its own birthplace.  Those splinters return faster than regret, cutting new joints into the calves of their parents. The pain is exquisite, intentional; pain is the one currency they mint afresh with each generation.

In the long hollow below the drip-line lies the wetland of their dead.  Not a sodden graveyard but a quiet dormitory: polished thoraxes cooling from mirror-bright to battlefield rust.  Death lasts exactly four seconds—enough for black silt to bead at collarbone seams and begin the climb home.  The dust does not mourn; it is a courier returning contraband memory to the mantle’s dark archive.  From there, new buds pearl upward, already rehearsing the next eruption of hooves.  Thus the population is always precisely “more than enough,” never one surplus soul.

Strangest is their geometry of time.  They do not age along a line but scatter across facets.  You may lock eyes—mirror—mirror—with one craftsman kneeling at the lower spur, only to meet the identical future of that same optic gleam an hour later three fissures east, younger by six hammer-falls yet unmistakably the unit of gaze that once studied you.  Each self is both prophecy and relic of its siblings.  Nothing is lost, only translated laterally, like a riddle whispered down a Möbius hallway.

And you, temporally lumbering, stand where impact is overdue.  Every exhale drips onto the embryo plate ascending between your boots.  It hatches glass in your shape—broad club of torso, sigh-ridges shaped like regrets, skin still glowing with cave-heat.  The scaffold builders regard this nascent twin with librarian politeness.  Several set down their chert knives, offer the handles to you as if presenting a ticket stub torn from tomorrow.  If you join the chorus and lift a blade, you will discover your joints now number twenty-one, every new axis a question the basalt wishes answered punctually.  The weight is momentous and negligible at once; it remembers how it once belonged to your wrist before wrists were rumored.

Whether you chip or hesitate, the consequence is instant.  The others copy your choice in hyperbolic multiples: every hesitation becomes a lullaby played on a thousand rib-cages, every cut an oath shouted by fresh silhouettes further down the corridor of mirror-seconds.  Within five heartbeats the newborn slab under your feet possesses buttresses modeled on the arch of your brow; its glass spine hums with your personal chord.  Your signature is already being demolished and respun by artisans eager to outgrow their template.  In this way the loop resigns itself to becoming more elaborate, never longer.

Above, the next bulb swells against basalt darkness: opaque with sentences that have not yet happened, pregnant with punctuation sharp enough to obliterate and begin.  The chamber draws a breath through every newborn pore.  The droplet concludes its sentence.
