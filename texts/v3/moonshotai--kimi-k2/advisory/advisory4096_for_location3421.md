Field Manual for the Knife-Blade Ridge  
(Planet 47-Omicron, colloquially “The Womb-Arête”)

------------------------------------------------  
0.  The Golden Rule (write this on your helmet in mirror-marker)  
     “I am a trespasser inside the planet’s dream.  
     The ground I stand on is not ground yet;  
     the air I breathe is arguing with itself;  
     the map I draw is already kindling.  
     I will move softly, think twice, and never call anything ‘safe’.”

1.  Visa & Psychology Screening  
a.  Minimum 120 hours in full-immersion VR sims of “unfinishing” terrain – landscapes that delete themselves while you cross them. If you have ever panic-restarted a sim, add another 40 hours.  
b.  Record a farewell message for every person you love; seal it. The ridge will make you ego-dust.  
c.  Carry no flags, no plaques, no religion that demands permanence. Tattoo your mission statement on the inside of your lip instead of planting it in rock.

2.  Suit Architecture (Tech 0.2 meets Hostile Heaven)  
Outer carapace: satin-finish basalt wool weave (9 kg m⁻²) impregnated with piezo gel. When quartz sings, the suit sings back out of phase, cancelling the fracture harmonics that otherwise liquefy viscera.  
Thermal skin: chromatuple meta-cloth. Default mirror-white; in a 44 °C pocket it flips obsidian to radiate IR upward; when you step into –5 °C chlorine fog it blooms silver, borrowing heat from your own exhalations.  
Boots: toe-cap “growth allowances” – 8 cm telescoping voids filled with mercury gallium alloy. If a spur thrusts beneath you the alloy squishes, giving the mountain 1.3 s to finish its stroke before load passes to your tibia.  
Helmet: sapphire anterior, matte exterior so nothing sees its own reflection and strikes at you in jealousy.  
HUD: heat-cartographer mode. Colours:  
     –  ghost green ≤ 0 °C  
     –  bruise rose  19–30 °C  
     –  ember rust ≥ 44 °C spikes  
Always keep 30 % of visor for “possibility overlay” – a neural net that hallucinates tomorrow’s range and ghosts it in 5 % opacity so you can walk off the cliff before the cliff walks under you.  
Oxygen: closed 4 h cyclic scrubber plus 30 min “ suicide tank” of pure O₂ for the day a benzene bubble kisses your intake valve and turns your normal mix into a combustive soufflé.  
Water: 3 L internal recycler. Flavour cartridge (pepperm or coffee) because distilled metallic water tastes like licking battery buses; morale is life-support too.

3.  Timeline & Phasing (honour the sleep of stone)  
Day 0  – Drop at twilight-of-twilight. Land on a foam-glass swell that simulation predicts will stay stationary for at least 14 h. Plant no beacons – they become shrapnel bouquets.  
Day 1-3 – Acclimation: walk max 5 km loops. Teach your inner ear that altitude and attitude both mutate. Catalog personal hallucinations (green leaf mirage inventory).  
Day 4-6 – Ridge marriage: pick one arête still cooler than 35 °C, shadow it until its mid-night cease-hiccup (see §17). During that pause, core-drill 20 cm, implant a string of passive temperature beads. These will later tell you whether your favourite mountain graduated or aborted.  
Day 7-10 – Traverse outward. Never cross a plain whose ripple wavelength equals your stride length – resonance will saw your knees. Use the hovering forearm-crystal (Fauna 0.2 object) as a horizon gyro: if it drifts >15° from vertical the crust here is tilting – reroute.  
Day 11-13 – Retreat rehearsals: pre-walk your extraction path at 50 % pace; memorise heat-mirage waypoints because visual boulders will not survive until your outbound day.  
Day 14 – Nominal uplift. If off-world pickup is impossible you already accepted that in writing; stay another cycle or die ascending on a staircase that melts.

4.  Nutrition & Pharmacology  
Metabolism on a world without thirst is a bookkeeping fiction. Expect 1 % dehydration per 6 h simply from molecular dissociation in the air. Chase every 250 kcal food-needle with 7 mL saline micro-shot.  
Drug kit:  
     – Piezo-quell  (Mg²⁺/ Li⁺ cocktail) – two tabs at first tinnitus of glass-song prevents inner-ear crystallisation.  
     – Somnalign    – 3 mg timed-release; wears off if ambient temp changes >8 °C in 90 s so you wake while the ground moves beneath the sleeping bag.  
     – Liminalin    – hallucination damper; takes the green-edge off olivine so you do not try to water rock-plants.  
Counter-indication: no stimulants – heart rate already fooled by thermal see-saw; adding coffee is how you discover 200 bpm at 3 km altitude.

5.  Communication (Density 1.0, Sound 1.2)  
Laser mesh fails – airborne silicates fog lenses. Acoustic couplers work best: hammer a titanium pin into basalt, glue piezo disk, whisper through your suit bone-conduction mic. Ground carries 2.2 km, after that pops eat the signal.  
Message protocol: start every transmission with two knuckle cracks – the planet’s own sound – so listeners know it is still alive and onlyBorrowing its throat.

6.  Ethics & Sample Policy  
Collect nothing larger than a 1 cm³ cube. Anything bigger becomes jealous of its own atoms and may follow you home as meteoric vendetta.  
Photograph instead of removing. Your camera is allowed to lose 0.4 MP per sol to radiation; consider that tithe to impermanence.  
If you encounter the hovering forearm-crystal or the greenish 41 °C cube, salute them. They are elders; we are the pollen.

7.  Emergency Scenarios  
A.  “The Lift-You-And-Leave-You” – spire grows under your boots faster than you can descend.  
     1)  Sit – lower centre of mass.  
     2)  Fire suit micro-harpoon into neighbouring stable foam; rappel 30 m while the newborn mountain finishes its thought.  
B.  Chemical Weather Front – you walk into a neon pocket, all sound drops out, visual refraction rainbow-fogs. Hold breath.  
     1)  Switch to internal O₂ before tasting neon popsicles in your lungs.  
     2)  Navigate by heat-cartographer alone; colour is lying.  
C.  Sudden Vacuum Bloom – temperature seams invert, metal-broth desaturates into hard vacuum without photos.  
     1)  Helmet auto-seal engages; trust it.  
     2)  Do NOT jet-jump – thrust plume can hit unseen garnet seam and turn you into a torch in 0.2 s. Crawl along basalt instead.

9.  Post-Mission Decompression  
Return to orbit is not the end. Gravity will feel counterfeit for weeks; sleep on a mattress of packed towels so the ground beneath can still mutate. Expect grief for mountains that rose and fell while you showered. Do not scratch the itch on your tongue – that is metallic broth finally oxidising out of your pores; taste is the last sense to evacuate a place that never had water.
