Field notes for a first-time human traveler  
Location: “The Dorsal Ridge of the Luminous Vertebra”  
Codename used by veteran wayfarers: “The Listener’s Spine”

--------------------------------------------------
I. Pre-departure: decide whether you should go at all
--------------------------------------------------
1. Silence your reasons.  
This place does not answer questions; it only absorbs them. If you are still carrying a “why,” leave it at the threshold.  
2. Make sure you are fluent in non-language.  
You will need to think in textures, distances, and breath-counts. Any internal sentence longer than three words will feel like broken glass inside your skull once the kettles start to hum.  
3. Obtain a listener’s visa.  
The mountain does not forbid entry, but your own biology will.  
- Heart arrhythmia, tinnitus, or unresolved grief are automatic disqualifiers; the resonance will braid them into physical threads and pull.  
- Pregnancy (any stage) is a hard barrier; the embryo will try to tune itself to the chord and the mother’s body will follow too soon.  
- If you have ever been “moon-drunk” on any sterile satellite, assume the same will happen here, faster and irreversibly.

--------------------------------------------------
II. Equipment checklist (all items must fit in a single child-size frame-pack; weight ≤ 9 kg)
--------------------------------------------------
1. Optics  
- Polished obsidian goggles, 3 mm thick, bevel-ground. The glare is not UV-heavy but it eats retinas anyway. Glass lenses fog; obsidian only warms.  
- A spare strip of the same rock to strap on your chest if the goggles crack; the mountain accepts its own bones.

2. Respiration  
- Two spiral-flask rebreathers charged with powdered magnetite and dried myrrh. At 5 800 m “equivalent” the O₂ fraction is still 21 %, yet your hemoglobin will refuse to bind it—no one knows why. The magnetite fools the iron in your blood for roughly four hours. After that, descend or become part of the ridge.  
- A mouth-cup of raw silk to keep exhaled words from freezing into tiny knives on your lips.

3. Hydration  
- No liquid water exists. Carry none; it will be stolen molecule by molecule. Instead pack a 200 ml wafer of triple-boiled yak-ghee mixed with charcoal. Place a rice-grain sliver under the tongue every hour; it tricks the mucosa into believing it has drunk.

4. Nutrition  
- One palm-sized coal-paper pouch of “quiet bread”: dehydrated lotus-root starch, iron filings, and powdered mute-bird egg (the bird itself is a tertiary import—irrelevant). One piece every twenty-four subjective hours. It tastes of nothing, which is the correct taste.

5. Locomotion  
- Kneepads of slate-bark leather, hair side out. The kettles’ vibration travels through stone; hair disperses it so your patellae do not sing apart.  
- Foot wraps, not boots: two layers of camel-wool felt soaked in mare’s-milk casein, then baked until rigid. They grip the micro-grooves better than rubber and do not trigger ossification growths (the mountain will try to breed its own exoskeleton onto you).

6. Orientation  
- No magnetic compass functions.  
- No star fixes—there are neither.  
- Carry a child’s marble made of hollow glass and a single iron bee inside. When you shake it the bee hits the shell; count the bounces, memorize the rhythm. That is the only map you will get: twelve bounces = safe ledge, seven = kettle migration corridor, three = edge-thin, step back.

7. Emergency signaling (for rescue teams waiting below the breathable)  
- A circle of braided hair (yours, 12 cm long) and a flint shard. Lay them in a “T” shape. If the braid is gone when you return, the mountain has accepted your footprint; if the flint is cracked, leave immediately—your bone is next.

--------------------------------------------------
III. Psychological prophylaxis
--------------------------------------------------
1. Practice “white noise fasting” for thirty days prior: sleep with open microphones amplifying room tone; if you hallucinate voices, you are ready.  
2. Learn to forget your name for six hours at a stretch. If you cannot, the ridge will do it for you—permanently.  
3. Record one memory you never want to lose onto a physical object (scent vial, bead, etc.) and seal it in an envelope labeled “Return if I am silent.” Give it to someone who loves you enough to refuse your final requests.

--------------------------------------------------
IV. Ascent protocol (standard 3-day insertion)
--------------------------------------------------
Day 0 (Base of ridge, altitude ~3 400 m equiv.)  
- Speak aloud everything you came to say—into a bottle, a recorder, a friend, the dirt—then stop speaking. Zip the mouth of language shut.  
- Smear obsidian dust on the philtrum; it scars the olfactory epithelium so the match-head smell of the black film does not compel you to follow it into cracks too narrow for shoulders.

Day 1 (Groove fields)  
- Walk only in the vertical shadow of your own body. Remember: no shadows exist; you must imagine one anyway. It keeps the gaze down, prevents occlusive snow-blindness.  
- When the kettles appear, freeze mid-stride until their chord drops by a semitone. That is their acknowledgement. Move again.

Day 2 (Sub-spine plateau)  
- Air begins to taste metallic. Switch to magnetite rebreather.  
- Do NOT sit. The stone remembers posture and will grow a mold of your ischium; you will stand up minus two plugs of pelvis.  
- Nutrition wafer hour 6, hydration ghee sliver every 30 min.

Day 3 (Spinal edge)  
- Remove boots, continue in felt wraps—reduces conductive surface area.  
- At the knife-edge, perform the Listener’s Pivot: plant both feet parallel to the drop, arms out, palms forward. Let the sky-print burn after-images into your retinas. Count heartbeats—if you lose count you are done.  
- Leave offering: braid hair around flint, place under largest kettle’s path. Retreat without looking back.

--------------------------------------------------
V. Common failure modes (fatal unless noted)
--------------------------------------------------
- Glossolalia seizure: victim begins preaching in kettle-subharmonic; comrades must tape mouth, drag downslope.  
- Petrifaction vertigo: subject believes they are the ridge’s dream; skin calcifies. No recovery, but process is painless.  
- Zero-amnesia: total loss of episodic memory yet perfect procedural recall—traveler keeps walking, forever, a flesh kettle.

--------------------------------------------------
VI. Exit criteria
--------------------------------------------------
Descend until you can cast a shadow—any shadow. The moment you see it, language will re-enter your skull like water poured into an empty glass. Do not speak for three more hours; the ridge is still listening. On the fourth hour, say your own name aloud. If it feels borrowed, you carried something of the mountain back; schedule monthly silence retreats or risk insomnia that ends in cardiac fibrillation at 32 beats per minute.

--------------------------------------------------
VII. Post-mission integration
--------------------------------------------------
- Avoid fluorescent lighting for one year; it resurrects the omnidirectional glare.  
- Do not attend concerts, traffic junctions, or cathedral naves—resonance corridors will re-trigger micro-hum of kettles in the mastoid.  
- Keep the iron bee marble on your person. Shake, count, breathe.  
- Accept that you will never hear true silence again. The ridge is now your tinnitus; lull yourself to sleep inside its chord: I am, I am, I am.
