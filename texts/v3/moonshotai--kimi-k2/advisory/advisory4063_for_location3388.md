HOW TO PACK FOR THE CITY THAT NEVER WEARS AWAY  
(An Orientation Scroll for First-time Multiverse Travelers)

───DON’T HURRY; YOU ARE ALREADY TOO LATE.───

1. MIND-SET  
The plateau keeps appointments, not schedules.  
   – Expect to arrive “after the beginning but before the start.”  
   – Memorise this sentence: “I am a guest of the condensation.”  
      Say it aloud when disoriented; citizens will treat the words as a passport.  
   – Abandon the axiom of erosion. Sharp edges are holy; mistakes are eternal.  
      If you chip a block you will personally finance a mason’s great-grandchild.  
   – Do not seek change—seek accretion. You can add, you cannot subtract.  
      Even thoughts condense here; regret becomes literal dew on your collar.

2. BODY PREPARATION  
Coolness is the law.  
   – Four weeks before departure begin sleeping with a chilled stone on your sternum.  
      Your core must learn to generate 0.6/3 warmth or the plateau will fine you in stillness.  
   – Practice “silent walking”: roll from heel to outer edge, finishing on the little toe.  
      If your footfall exceeds 15 dB you will be politely escorted to a Whisper to finish growing up.  
   – Grow a working finger: let the smallest digit of your non-dominant hand callus into a stylus.  
      Citizens respect a visitor who already sacrifices geometry to the city.  
   – Adjust circadian rhythm to “perpetual 10 a.m.” Light never moves; your pupils must stop hunting.

3. IMMUNISATIONS & BIO-REPATTERNING  
   – Inoculate against timelessness. Standard serums: chrono-dysplexia, memory condensate allergy, ontic vertigo.  
   – Swallow a tame echo-mouse pup (available at any trans-dimensional apothecary).  
      It will lodge in the eustachian tube and repeat heartbeats, preventing ear-ring overdose.  
   – Implants of powdered-cloud spores are mandatory; they allow your sweat to bond with local stone should you accidentally lean too hard on a wall.

4. CLOTHING & FIELD KIT  
Outer layer  
   → Cloak of stationary alpaca: buy the animal still grafted to its shelf; pack moss-side out so customs recognises it as property, not contraband meat.  
Inner layers  
   → Five-fingered gloves (one finger empty to show respect for the base-five culture).  
   → Felt boots stuffed with leaf-lint; soles must be 4 mm thick—any thicker and the city regards you as unfinished architecture.  
Tools  
   → Basalt nodule hammer (pre-apologised).  
   → River-crystal saw set: exactly 12 joints; extras will be seized as currency.  
   → Condensate-tin paint-stick: mark doorways so the years know where you stayed.  
Containers  
   → Two linen sacks for cloud-orchard spores (leave one half-open to courtesy).  
   → A sealable echo-pot—store vital noises in case you need them next season.  
Miscellaneous  
   → Pocket mirror: polished slab, 2×3 finger-widths, for children to draw on; doubles as distress signal.  
   → Travel anvil of lightweight regret: when struck it produces one droplet of personal shame; use to bribe stonemasons if you break something.

5. ARRIVAL PROTOCOL  
a) Altitude gate  
   Expect a constant chord in your ears. Hum the same pitch: C♯ above middle C. The plateau considers matching tones as introductions.  
b) Cloud etiquette  
   The weekly cloud is not weather; it is a municipal livestock. Stand still while it grazes the terraces.  
   If spores land on exposed skin do not slap—fold the droplets into a blade of stone and dedicate the future wall to “The Stranger Who Did Not Flinch.”  
c) Silence declaration  
   On entry you must declare a “Year-sound.” Choose one syllable (e.g., “hush,” “mica,” “almost”).  
   You may utter this word only during the Festival of Sudden Shouting. All other vocalisations are taxed at one basalt chip per phoneme.

6. MONEY & EXCHANGE  
Coins are drill-spirals knocked from abandoned blocks—carry a pouch of 20.  
If you run out, perform “Visible Addition”: haul a raw block into a gap; citizens will feed you until the stone is dressed.  
Never offer iron, copper, or paper. They rust toward motion and the city will fine you in accelerated memories.

7. LODGING  
Rooms are scattered. Hire a space-map: an echo-mouse trained to repeat your itinerary after a 12-beat delay.  
Sleep in a Whisper if you can; you’ll wake to breakfast balanced on a string-line between two futures.  
When you find your kitchen three terraces away, walk the aqueduct-clock: count basalt ribs to time hard-boiled condensation-eggs (exactly four ribs).

8. FOOD & WATER  
Water is shy; drink the damp, not the drop. Press tongue to basalt at dawn; lick the beaded lenses until thirst resolves.  
Stonecrop apples: crisp, amnesiac—eat too many and you forget yesterday’s temperature. One a day maintains identity.  
Glass-snail trail soup is legal tender; suck the strand and you own a translucent window.  
Do not eat stilt-heron eggs; they are blood-warm mortgages on infinity.

9. SOCIAL CUSTOMS  
   – Accept three conversations with the Condensate Architect and you are a citizen; refuse, and descent is inevitable.  
   – Never finish a wall; leave one face raw—only the eternally incomplete are trusted.  
   – Compliment colors by whispering their almost-ness: “nearly white,” “one breath shy of blue.”  
   – Children may draw your portrait on a mirrored slab; tip them with a footprint in spore-mortar. They will remember you longer than you remember yourself.

10. EMERGENCY PROCEDURES  
a) Temporal vertigo  
   If you feel yesterday leaning against your ribs, crawl under a vine-birch and wait for the ringing to match your pulse.  
b) Accidental completion  
   Should you finish a house (four dressed faces) you are legally a wall. Citizens will mortar you into the next terrace.  
   Escape: swallow echo-mouse pup stored in your ear; it will repeat your pre-completion heartbeat and confuse the masonry.  
c) Festival disorientation  
   During the Festival of Sudden Shouting, stand in the aqueduct; water will not fall upward until the shout ends.  
d) Cloud-orchard overdose  
   If you inhale full spore-bloom your lungs will petrify into apple pips. Cure: drink a thimble-full of your own year-sound while standing barefoot on raw quarry; the stone will reject foreign fruit.

11. EXIT CONDITIONS  
   – You must choose: three renovations by the Architect or find the nonexistent path downward.  
   – Memory will condense on only one side of the decision. Take notes in condensate-tin; they survive souvenir customs.  
   – When leaving, offer your still-warm alpaca-moss to the river; watch it sink upward into the crystal sheet and become a pale glint.  
     That glint is now your plateau-identity. Wave goodbye to yourself.

12. CHECKLIST AT THE GATEHOME  
□ Cloak smells of moss, not you  
□ One finger shorter (donate stylus to museum)  
□ Ears still humming C♯ (keep; souvenir tuning fork)  
□ Echo-pot contains year-sound; open only when you need the hush again  
□ Mirror-slab portraits traded for one basalt rib of the aqueduct-clock. Display on mantel; dew will form whenever someone in High Verdancy mentions your name.

Remember: you will not have visited the City of Quiet Becoming. You will have added yourself to it, molecule by breath by dew. Travel light—every gram is forever.
