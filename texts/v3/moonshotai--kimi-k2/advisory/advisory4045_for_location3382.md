How to Pack for the Coral Foundry  
a field-note / check-list / love-letter for the one who intends to step inside a wet cathedral that has not yet learned mortality  

------------------------------------------------  
1.  Accept that you are the contaminant  
The Foundry was drafted in a charter that explicitly forbids fauna. Your biology is a temporary amendment, a spelling error the building will autocorrect the moment you leave. Behave like an honoured typo: move quietly, take nothing, leave only the faint thermal ghost of your presence.  

------------------------------------------------  
2.  Lungs: bring a second pair, metaphorically  
The air is potable but possessive. Practise the following breathing cadence NOW, in your terrestrial kitchen:  
- 4-count inhale through the nose – imagine sipping hot syrup  
- 7-count hold – let the syrup press against the alveoli like velvet  
- 6-count exhale through pursed lips – convince the air it is allowed to leave  
Do this until you can do it walking uphill. The Foundry will not negotiate; it simply delays your breath until you comply with its tempo. Hyperventilating visitors are gently folded into service ducts and become collateral calligraphy.  

------------------------------------------------  
3.  Skin: rent a temporary exoskeleton  
The water-film is skin-temperature and wants to colonise you. Before entry, spray yourself with a hydrophobic surfactant wrestled from shark-belly enzymes and powdered quartz. It lasts ±3 hours, after which your own sweat reverts to diplomatic status and is reabsorbed. The coating buys you time to remember you are distinct from the building. Without it, travellers report “softening,” the feeling that their fingerprints are being reassigned to nearby walls.  

------------------------------------------------  
4.  Shoes that forget the floor  
Gravity edits itself. Pack strap-on micro-spikes made of memory-aluminium: when the corridor tilts 45° the alloy re-crystallises and grips whichever direction now claims to be “down.” Normal boots simply grow confused and eject their wearer into the ceiling, where you will stick like a damp postage stamp until the creepers decide you are spare parts.  

------------------------------------------------  
5.  A compass that apologises  
Compasses spin. GPS is a bedtime story. Bring instead a pocket-sized gyroscope etched with your childhood home coordinates. When the geometry sulks, hold it loosely and remember the smell of your first kitchen. The Foundry recognises nostalgia as a valid orientation system; space will curve toward the emotional landmark for exactly eleven minutes—long enough to reach the next plausible doorway.  

------------------------------------------------  
6.  Light you can eat  
No part of the interior is dark, yet no part owes you visibility. Pack two 9-hour chem-sticks filled with luciferin harvested from abyssal squid. Crack one only if you need to read a warning label on a seed-pod valve. The pink-neutral ambient illumination will politely intensify, then invoice you later by stealing pigment from your retinas; the squid-light is foreign enough to escape taxation.  

------------------------------------------------  
7.  Souvenir protocol  
You will want to bring home a silver leaf, a bronze pipe-rib, a flake of rust-calligraphy. RESIST. The Foundry tracks mass the way a miser tracks coins. Remove even a milligram and every corridor you traverse will prolapse, sealing you inside a shrinking fist until the object is returned or your bones are press-forged into replacement struts. If you absolutely must commemorate, swallow a 360-camera pill programmed to dream the objects for you; vomit the data when you are safely earth-side.  

------------------------------------------------  
8.  Calendar insurance  
Time is stored in the walls. Wear two watches: one mechanical, one biological. When they diverge by more than 17 minutes you are approaching a cache of hoarded seconds—step back, recount your own pulse until the discrepancy drops below 7. Failure to do so will strand you in a lobby that ages a month for every heartbeat; your reflection will greet you with grey hair and a thank-you note from the grandchildren you have not yet conceived.  

------------------------------------------------  
9.  Fauna substitute  
You will crave birdsong, buzzing, the bacterial smell of soil. Download these into a bone-conduction ear-clip. Slip it on whenever vertigo pretends your sanity. The Foundry permits outside noise provided it is played inside your skull, a private contradiction it finds adorable.  

------------------------------------------------  
10.  An exit bargaining chip  
The building has no door until it believes you have finished becoming interesting. Carry a sealed envelope containing one unstated desire—write it on rice paper, seal it with wax pressed from your own heartbeat. At the lip of the uppermost blister-window, when you see the future reef of sister forges blooming in the haze, tear the envelope open, read the desire aloud once, then swallow the paper. The Foundry will recognise the taste of conceded mystery and grow a doorway in the exact shape of your last exhalation. Step through immediately; hesitation is interpreted as an application for citizenship.  

------------------------------------------------

☐ Spit once; if the saliva floats upward you are still inside the humidity tariff—dive again  
☐ Write nothing down for 24 hours; memory needs to anneal  
☐ Kiss the ground, but discreetly—the Foundry sometimes follows romantics home  

------------------------------------------------  
Last truth  
The Coral Foundry is not destination, datum or dare. It is a question posed to water, metal and distance, still awaiting your particular punctuation. Pack lightly, breathe politely, and remember: you are not exploring. You are being temporarily explored.
