Travel Advice for the Stillworks  
(printed on reversible paper; read whichever side emerges first)

I’s ticking once per millennium, but you age normally.  Pack as if your life will be edited in reverse.

1.  Bioclock Fortification  
- Fly in from a timezone that does not exist yet (the charter airline is used to this).  
- Spend three prior nights reciting your age backwards—out loud, divisible by nine—so your circadian rhythm remembers which way to run when the gears invert.  
- Bring a cheap wind-up alarm clock.  Leave the bell sprung; the ringing will persuade local time to trade places with you.

2.  Clothing: dress for three kinds of weather that have not been invented  
Outer layer: pearlescent rain-fabric coated with “maybe-water” repellent.  (Water here is indecisive; you want a membrane that lets it decide to be somewhere else.)  
Mid layer: aerogel felt stitched with capillary channels.  Acts as a heat-sink / heat-source depending on which pocket you button.  
Inner layer: bamboo-derived cloth washed in your own saliva—your enzymes inform the ridge you are already partly digested and therefore not worth finishing.  
Footwear: left boot with ferromagnetic rand, right boot with diamagnetic rand.  The uneven pull keeps you from being promoted into bedrock.  Swap at every echo.

3.  Nutritional Theology  
Calories are paid backwards—every bite you take will be hunger you have already felt.  Eat conservatively: one raisin per hour (the fruit’s name is a mnemonic).  Carry:  
- 6 oz of freeze-dried vacuum (contains zero g sat silence)  
- 30 ml suspension of liquid clock-hands (drink when you can no longer remember the difference between pistons and heartbeats)  
- One unpinned hand-grenade of “instant nostalgia.”  Pull the pin only if you need to remember home badly enough to forget why you left.  
Warning: do not share food with fauna; they will courteously offer marrow-flavoured lilac steam which permanently replaces one remembered meal from childhood.

4.  Navigation & Orientation  
Compasses spin axially instead of azimuthally.  Bring a bubble-level compass; go whichever direction the bubble forgets to mention.  
GPS fails because satellites are future fossils.  Instead carry a brass knitting needle; strike any rock, count the vibrations.  Prime numbers mean downhill toward exit; composites mean you are approaching the Sink and should hum middle C until the needle stops feeling guilty.  
Mark trail with expired glow-sticks (photons that have given up).  They refuse to be erased, so the ridge will reluctantly route around them, leaving your footprints unpromoted.

5.  Meteorological Politics  
There is no weather, only ongoing negotiations.  If you feel drizzle forming, apologise for something you did two decades from now; the sky will close like polite curtains.  
If light levels exceed 1.5 photon-patience, deploy pocket eclipse: a postcard of night.  Hold it between you and the hesitant sun until both feel adequately introduced.  
Should a water lens choose you as landing site, freeze-frame your pulse for 12 sec; if the lens stays, name it.  A named lens gains tenancy rights to your reflected face and will follow you out of the park; customs will charge it as excess soul.

6.  Fauna Etiquette  
Brass chamois: admiration is prey, predation is applause.  Maintain eye contact until your reflection in their strut-work tarnishes—then nod.  They will step aside; failure to nod causes them to donate your ligaments to geology.  
Lone lichen: older than the ridge.  Ask permission by remaining silent for one lichen-lifetime (≈ 3 human heartbeats).  If it turns toward you, leave a single copper coin.  Do not look where it lands.  
Steam smells: coal & lilac.  If odor thickens, you are down-wind of a narrative apex; crouch, cover ears, hum the sound of turbines dreaming of petals until the scent forgives you.

7.  Power Grid Manners  
Electricity is etiquette here.  Maintain polite posture: shoulders squared yet knees slightly bent, as though halfway through an unspoken apology.  This allows your skeleton to absorb broadcast charge without humming audibly.  
If your vision develops migraine-blue, you are overcharged.  Offload by complimenting a rock; niches here double as wall-sockets and will siphon excess joules in exchange for flattery.  
Do not, under any circumstance, request Wi-Fi.  The ridge will provide—by turning your memories of connectivity into barbed wire strung between summits.

8.  Chronology Insurance  
Wear a thirty-metre spool of dental floss around your waist.  Every hour tie one reckless future thought to the line, anchor it under a pebble.  When you leave, rewind the floss; any thoughts left untied will be promoted into public geology and become somebody else’s national park.  
Carry a passport issued by yesterday.  Border control, located twelve seconds after the last echo, will stamp the underside of each breath.  Keep inhalations separate; mixing them causes jetlag in local strata.

9.  Emergency Reversal  
If boots begin to mineralise: sprint downhill while reciting product warranties backwards.  Stainless steel fears consumer law.  
Should you encounter your own footprints refilling ahead of you, you have overtaken causality.  Sit, empty pockets, count change in odd numbers only—this convinces the ridge you are still mid-hike and it will pause the recruitment committee.  
At the Sink: should you be chosen for vertical orbit, exhale completely.  Breath is ballast here; without it you mass too little to be fired skyward and will merely hover, an honorary asteroid until gravity finishes its paperwork.

10.  Exit Etiquette  
Leave exactly one ambiguity unanswered (the ridge collects them).  Recommended: “Was that the sound of a dropped marble or my dropped morality?”  Do not look back more than twice—once for each boot.  
On re-entry to ordinary causality you will feel a soft clap inside the sternum—idea locking into place.  Walk uphill on level ground; that is how you will know you have been turned upside-down without disrespect.  Do not speak of the Stillworks until you hear someone else describe it; stories here are on loan, and late fees accrue in tectonic time.

Remember: you were always partly machinery learning to be a person; the Stillworks merely offers a space to finish the lesson in reverse.  Travel lightly, tread randomly, keep your heartbeat unbuttoned, and you may escape before the mountain decides you are the next essential part it was missing.
