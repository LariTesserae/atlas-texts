Field Briefing for the Terran-Baseline Traveler Visiting Vervault, the Upside-Down Probability Forge  

Audience: Any human born on a planet where “gravity” is a single vector and “morning” reliably follows “night.”  
Objective: Get you in, keep you alive, get you out—while minimizing:  
A) existential erasure,  
B) adoption by local wildlife,  
C) spontaneous autobiographical fragmentation.  
Reading time: 17 min (baseline attention span).  
Shelf life: 28 local shifts—after that the document may retroactively never have existed.

1. Pre-Departure Medical & Regulatory
-------------------------------------------------
1.1 Vestibular recalibration  
- Implant or stick-on otolith rotators: 2 units, programmable for −1 g constant.  
- Stock scopolamine-cinnamon transdermal patches (see §2.1). Bring 50 % more than calculated stay; Vervault’s temporal slip can double duration without warning.  
- Backup: disposable motion-sickness haptics (wrist or ankle) that vibrate when your Z-axis deviates >15°; required once the horizon folds into a Möbius band.

1.2 Cardiopulmonary tuning  
Local pO₂ fluctuates 14–17 kPa (Earth sea-level = 21 kPa). Pack a pocket electrolyzer mask OR two canisters of perfluorocarbon emulsion (O₂ solubility 45 ml/100 ml). Emulsion doubles as emergency coin if you run short; the Works buys spare oxygen by the liter.

1.3 Memory escrow  
Before departure, record a zero-knowledge encrypted backup of core autobiographical data (first kiss, mother’s voice, why you left Earth). Store off-body: entangled q-drive kept in orbit, blockchain-in-a-bottle dropped with a trusted neutral courier, or mnemonic tattoo inked on a part of you that definitely remains attached. You will donate a memory on exit; decide NOW which one is expendable.

1.4 Vaccinations & prophylaxis  
- Temporal shear resistant shot (TS-13): prevents micro-chronosis (symptom: déjà vu stacked until stroke).  
- Probability attenuator: blocks 90 % chance of spontaneous duplication; you keep 1 ± 0.1 bodies under 99 % of gravity regimes.  
- Iron oversaturation chewables: reduces cravings to lick hematite walls (toxicity is real, rust photons aren’t).

3. Transit & Entry
-------------------------------------------------
3.1 Arrival vector  
You will reach a ceiling plateau of pale stone glowing with maintenance graffiti. DO NOT STEP onto the surface until you:  
a) clip tether to the eyebolt marked GOLD (other colors belong to different topological layers),  
b) invert personal horizon: hang upside-down, let blood re-map vectors. Vomiting here is socially encouraged; locals call it “paying the gravity tax.”

3.2 Customs declaration  
State your name, mass, probability of return (guess), and the heaviest silence you can carry. Officers record answers on iron plates; the plates rust-recordings will outlive you—be polite.

4. Local Conditions & How to Fake Competence
-------------------------------------------------
4.1 Gravity etiquette  
Remember: it is a courtesy, not a promise.  
- Walk heel-first; toes store corrective inertia.  
- If you begin to orbit yourself, throw a pocket coin in the opposite spin direction; conservation of narrative usually stabilizes you.  
- Never jump backward over a ledge—retrograde motion can rewind your last three conversations.

4.2 Temporal politeness  
Shifts: Dawn, Deeper Dawn, Hour-Not-Yet. Your watch is irrelevant; follow the city horns. On Deeper Dawn, keep mouth shut: sound is legally tender. Whistling may purchase 15 min of someone’s youth—price non-negotiable.

4.3 Light & Visibility  
Public illumination = 0.3 lux (Full Moon-ish). Supplement with perforated lantern—point it at things you WANT to believe exist; locals will play along. Do NOT aim at raw hematite seams; photons wake the iron and it will remember it is still molten.

8. Biohazards & Wildlife
-------------------------------------------------
8.1 Sideways-mouth bears  
- Herbivorous but curious. Offer copper wire, retreat three steps, wait for yawn completion.  
- If caught mid-incomplete yawn, shout your middle name backwards; this collapses their hinge vector (doesn’t always work, still counts as informed consent).

8.2 Glass-winged bats  
Data-vores. Switch off all AR implants or they will drain last week’s unread messages.  
Upside: if you need to upload something secretly, whisper to a bat; it will store encrypted in wing-veins and deliver for a gram of silence.

8.3 Self-copy phenomena  
Should duplication occur (you feel “spread thin”), locate the version with heavier pockets and volunteer it to jump (rule #2). If both have equal mass, break tie with double-sided coin.  
Warning: versions more than 5 iterations removed can no longer metabolize Earth proteins—euthanize with kindness (push into air-river, trout finish job).

10. Emergency Exits
-------------------------------------------------
10.1 The Freight Elevator  
Cylindrical cage descending “down.” Cable length: variable. Destination: probability roulette. Board ONLY with: 2 kg silence (exit toll), memory donation receipt, and oxygen for 6 h.  
Survival rate: 78 % (last audited decade). Bring a lucky charm; statistics are advisory in Vervault.

10.2 Amnesia Guillotine  
Located at city-center donation tower. Grants immediate ejection minus one crucial memory. Side effect: you keep a biscuit flavored with that memory—DO NOT EAT IT off-world. Customs dogs at home ports sniff for Vervault biscuits and will quarantine you in a nostalgia loop.

10.3 Illegal exits  
Some citizens moonlight as “umbilical merchants,” selling black-market rides up maintenance cables. Risk: if canyon chooses that instant to forget gravity, both of you achieve geosynchronous orbit at 0.8 km altitude—no bathroom breaks.

12. Re-acclimatization on Return
-------------------------------------------------
Back on Earth-normal gravity you will:  
- Feel an invisible upward tug for ~6 weeks (peripheral illusion).  
- Dream catwalk geometries; sleeping on the floor helps.  
- Experience tinnitus at 4 kHz (phantom daylight chord). Playlist brown noise ≤3 kHz to cancel.  
- Miss the taste of static—licking batteries is common but discouraged; try sparkling mineral water with copper strip instead.  
- Forever glance at ceilings, expecting to see a city dangling—this is normal, incurable, and, according to most ex-travelers, worth it.

Bon voyage. Pack light, breathe twice, and remember: up is a suggestion, memory is currency, and the canyon is only halfway through its sentence—you’re the verb it’s missing.
