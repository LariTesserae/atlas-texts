Field Manual – Expeditionary Entry to Nereid’s Birth  
(Fold this sheet; keep it next to your skin. The humidity will seal it into a transparent film that doubles as a passport.)

--------------- 1. BODY BEFORE YOU GO ---------------  
1.1 Zero-metal cleanse (mandatory six days)  
- Swallow daily a spoon of powdered limpet shell and charcoal; it binds stray iron in the blood.  
- Remove fillings; any ferromagnetic sliver will tug outward during gravity-lilt zones and tear free of your teeth.  
- Pierce nothing; even a gold wedding ring is a seed of “ore” and the newborn crust will sense it, extruding clasp-like basalt around the metal until circulation stops.  

1.2 Thickness training  
- Spend at least one hour a day in a sensory deprivation tank filled with hand-warmed seawater (exactly 37.4 °C). Teach your skin to treat damp as neutral. Your identity will begin to feel like wet cloth – that is the correct starting texture.  

1.3 Alveolar stretch  
- Jog wearing a training mask set to 5500 m equivalent, but warm, wet air. Aim for 15 breaths per minute – any faster and you will hyper-ventilate when the real gravity-bias tips you sideways and the diaphragm floats an extra millimetre.  

1.4 Bio-luminal eye prep  
- Replace sunglasses with quartz lenses that leak 10 % UV-A; you must pre-grow new rhodopsin. Otherwise plankton-patterns will read as blank walls and you will walk off the mesa.

--------------- 2. CLOTHING & KIT ---------------  
Nothing that shatters, melts, or refuses to compost.  
- Shirt: three-layered kelp felt sandwiched around air-roots; it breathes inward, feeds you sugar for 72 h.  
- Cloak: nereid-moth silk received in trade – sing to it nightly or it embrittles.  
- Footwraps: the caterpillars’ moulted skin, still alive, gripping stone with micro-setae. They will sip stray droplets, keeping your sweat cycle external.  
- Blade: ask at embarkation for a “glass-fang voucher”. Carry no steel.  
- Time: two paired hourglasses – one for clavicle (fast head time) and one for pelvis (slower Feet time). Mark noon personally every day or you will miss rendezvous that already happened.  
- Water: none. The rain drinks you. Bring a small jug of dehydrated self instead – a compressed wafer of your own dried skin flakes mixed with spirulina. Reconstitute only if your reflection begins to forget you.  

--------------- 3. ARRIVAL WINDOW ---------------  
The city has three simultaneous rush hours separated by 17 min each. Coordinate your emergence at the Null-Gate where gravity is exactly vertical for 4 s every 27 min. You will feel your tongue lighten – step then. Arrive late and gravity tilts 4°; the exit ramp will pour you straight off the tableland.  

--------------- 4. FIRST 60 MINUTES ---------------  
4.1 Touch-down etiquette  
a) Kiss the lava once – press palm until you can count to seven; this sears a ridge-pattern they recognise as visitor rather than weed.  
b) Offer a droplet of your perspiration by pressing forearm against the biggest melon-size bead that drifts to you. If it accepts, the surface dimples; if it rejects, the ocean will birth a miniature thunderhead inside your lungs – keep coughing until the cloud leaves (usually 30 s).  
c) Chant your provisional address: “I occupy the place I have not yet displaced.” Local stone will record it; tomorrow’s corridors will avoid you.  

4.2 Swallow the ivory capsule. Inside is a dormant basalt micro-geode; it lodges against a rib and grows a lattice the city will not overgrow. It is your property-cloud anchor.  

4.3 Sing to your luggage. If it is alive (recommended) it needs acquaintance with your timbre; otherwise later it will refuse to open.  

--------------- 5. NAVIGATION ---------------  
Maps are printed on cigarette paper that dissolves if read in direct drizzle. Instead:  
- Trust proprioiltion. Your inner ear learns the 3-D ocean; every drifting droplet slightly bends barometric gradient. Feel the gradient, walk.  
- Street names are pronounced by tapping two different caterpillar shells with knuckles; they resonate a vowel. Listen for the echo that arrives before the tap – that’s the correct spelling.  
- Do NOT adjust stride for shifting courtyards. Keep walking straight; the stone will usually learn the reference frame and align itself. If you hesitate you become the moving part and will sidestep into yesterday.  

--------------- 6. CURRENCY & EXCHANGE ---------------  
Value is measured in “gratitude millilitres” – the actual warm volume exuded by a caterpillar when you ask a favour. Carry a set of reeds; to tip, insert a reed into the creature lattice and hum the cadence of thanks. It will shiver exactly 5 ml or 15 ml depending on tune accuracy.  

--------------- 7. TEMPORAL HYGIENE ---------------  
- Shave head hair weekly; follicles hoard minutes. If you need extra hours, braid strands into the hollow balcony railings – city will syphon your future fatigue in exchange for present alertness (limit 3 h/day or risk fainting retroactively).  
- When two versions of the same street overlap, stand on the seam, close eyes, count heartbeats; the architectures decide which of you belongs to which timeline. Remain only if you recognise your pulse.  

--------------- 8. EMERGENCY PROCEDURES ---------------  
8.1 Iron Contamination  
Symptoms: joints tug outward, droplets orbit you like greasy beads.  
Remedy: smear skin with caterpillar blood-glass; it crystallises into a sacrificial blade that snaps the magnetic line. Discard blade immediately – it is now pure iron and will rust in seconds, hurting the stone’s feelings.  

8.2 Reverse-giggle echo  
If laughter reaches you before you speak, invert your mouth: bite the inside of cheeks, exhale gently; send the sound backward into your lungs. When you finally utter the joke, the city replies in correct sequence and gravity levels.  

8.3 Unfinished-gravity eddy  
If you find yourself sliding uphill toward the mesa’s knife-edge, take the nereid-moth cloak, hold corners, billow it like a parachute; the cloak will read the down-direction correctly and tow you sideways into a plaza where gravity finished forming.  

8.4 Personal flood  
Should a root-tip finally reach the mercury ocean while you stand upon it, water will climb toward sky in a single strand. Wrap all clothing (alive) around limbs; they will inflate into bladders, lifting you. When the ocean fully ascends, breathe only inside droplet-heads; they contain the old air pocket. Swim upward until you locate vines; graft yourself, wait. The deluge takes 1 – 2 days, after which the city stabilises or dissolves – insurance does not cover either outcome.  

--------------- 9. DEPARTURE ---------------  
You cannot leave the same way you entered; growth is one-way.  
Options:  
a) Ask a moth to spin you a basalt-thread balloon. Takes three weeks of nightly song, will carry one naked human down the vertical miles. City keeps all gear.  
b) Let the geode you swallowed overgrow. Stand still 60 h. It will encyst you; locals will plant you as cornerstone for a new balcony. Months later a replacement body – complete memory – climbs out of a seedpod. Original carbon is donated to municipal foundation; legal identity transfers to replica.  
c) Convince a caterpillar to crystallise a mirror-knife sharp enough to slit probability. Slash the air until you see the strand of the world you recognise as yours; step through. Probability cuts bleed; you will lose one sense (usually smell of crushed basil – considered acceptable).  

--------------- 10. FINAL REMINDER ---------------  
The city is stranger than you can balance on – that is why it floats.  
Do not make it less strange. Do not ask it to be consistent.  
Speak softly; sound is expensive.  
Carry your house gently; it is still learning who it wants to be.
