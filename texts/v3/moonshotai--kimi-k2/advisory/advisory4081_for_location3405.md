Preparation dossier for excursion Site-9A: "Unnamed Neo-Caldera, Terra-Null Class"

Do not pack as if you are going to a place. Pack as if you are going to a process that has not yet decided whether "place" is a useful category.

–––––––––––––– 1. Personal Boundary Kit ––––––––––––
a. A watertight skin-paint of sub-clinical silver-nitrate. Apply two coats; it hardens into a flexible integument that is chemically boring to basalt and unreadable to capillary DNA sampling. Re-coat every 2.7 magmatic cycles (≈11 hours).  
: Leave ear canals, nostrils, and tear ducts open; the paint is breathable but speech will be muffled. Prepare to communicate in writing or touch-code.

b. Memory-plasteel clogs: soles laminated with a lattice of pre-foamed nickel. They distribute your weight into a fractal that the crater has already seen (Fold-skin bones) – the rock mistakes you for prior data and stops recording footprints. Remove them before you exit; if you don't, the shoes start re-writing themselves into your arches.

c. One roll of aerogel "shadow-silk." When the brass light cycles up to its brightest, drape it overhead; it's translucent to you but opaque to the caldera's optical sampling. Without it your retinal map is harvesteded into the nano-etchings on the whale-rib rings.

–––––––––––––– 2. Thermal & Baric Buffer ––––––––––––
Skin-temperature is 37 °C; the lake is "blood-hot" (~38–40 °C) but surface evaporation keeps micro-pockets at 44 °C. Use an under-layer of phase-change microcapsules tuned to 36 °C. They dump heat outward when you are hotter, inward when cooler, smoothing the magmatic breath so your biology does not phase-lock to its four-hour rhythm.

Carry a fist-sized vacuum bellows. When the lake inhales and rises, crack the bellows: it equalises sinus-magma pressure in your inner ear. Without it you hallucinate that your heartbeat is echoing back in someone else's voice—and eventually you obey it.

–––––––––––––– 3. Chronology Anchors ––––––––––––
Bring three time-objects that disagree with one another:
- A wind-up pocket watch set to UTC (it will run slow because the basalt drags on its spring).
- A dripping water clock pre-calibrated to 7.6 mm droplets (copies the Vapor Trees' cadence).
- A candle of beeswax mixed with iron filings; it burns hourglass-blue and emits a magnetic field that wiggles a compass you shouldn't look at.

When the three diverge by more than twenty percent you are slipping out of your own duration. Sit inside a whale-rib ring, face magnetic north (however your instruments currently define it), recite prime numbers aloud; the self-echo on the rim usually answers correctly if you are still synchronous. If it doesn't, begin extraction protocol.*

–––––––––––––– 4. Extraction Protocol * ––––––––––––
Primary: re-set your silver skin-paint with final coating, step into the shallowest part of lake, lift left clog three times: the Fold-skin Lizards see the fractal motion, associate it with dying, swarm and inscribe your shape in relaxed bone-angles. The caldera's copy-routine pauses to check whether you are dead code. In the half-second hesitation depress the pyro-foil in your belt; it flash-boils a skin of water into super-critical steam. The thrust pops you ten metres upward—grab the pre-spooled graphene winch you earlier clipped to the basalt lip. Winch out, roll over rim, do NOT look back until the 42 kHz hum drops below audibility (≈200 m).

Backup: If primary fails, activate the pocket watch; its mainspring is soldered with indium. Snap it—this smears liquid metal across the gears, stops the watch dead. The caldera momentarily adopts the artefact as a "completed" temporal iteration and expels the organic remainder. You will wake outside the rim in a glass mould of yourself: shatter it quickly; it sets harder every minute.

–––––––––––––– 5. Psychological Prophylaxis ––––––––––––
A. Keep a short, emotionally neutral epithet you trust ("Grey 12"). When the echoed voice on the rim speaks prophecy, answer only with that epithet; it short-circuits the feedback loop of self-anticipation.

B. Do not eat or drink. Metal-rich water and peach-tinted spheres taste like childhood sodas; they seed skeletal memory into your marrow. Within forty-eight external hours your ulnae begin growing fractal lace visible on X-ray. If you must hydrate, crack the ceramic click-spheres mid-air, inhale vapor only.

C. Record nothing optical; lenses confuse the vapor prisms and return images of you already mineralised. Take notes on thermal-sensitive paper; the heat-cycle will erase it within a day—this is a safeguard: information that cannot survive a single caldera-breath is not worth giving back to the wider multiverse.

–––––––––––––– 6. Contingency Ethics ––––––––––––
Should you encounter another traveller already partly digested (glass footprints up to ankles, spheres tinted their skin-tone), remember:
- Do not assist physically; touch accelerates templating.
- Offer them pocket-watch time: snapping the spring in their presence sometimes lets them ride the expelled iteration (see Backup, §4).
- If they ask you to "take the hum with you," refuse politely; a transplanted 42 kHz carrier in another reality has, on prior logs, destabilised tectonics of host planets. This site is curious, not malicious—do not spread its curriculum.

–––––––––––––– 7. Post-Visit Quarantine ––––––––––––
Expect sub-audible resonance in blood for 33 cycles of your own heart. During that window avoid:
- Crowded subway platforms (pressure echoes mimic magma breath, can trigger faints).
- MRI machines (the nickel clogs, if you forgot to remove them, will try to realign).
- Karaoke bars near 42 kHz harmonics (your voice can shatter cheap wine glasses and you will be billed).

If tremors sync with four-hour intervals, enter a quiet room, immerse feet in table-salt br water, recite Grey 12 until pulse drifts out of phase. When desynchronised, the caldera releases the residual hold.

–––––––––––––– 8. Mental After-Image Log ––––––––––––
Within 72 h write down every false memory that surfaces (hallways, misspelled nicknames, skylines). Burn the paper; the act of illegible ash convinces the caldera that you have metabolised its drafts into entropy. Fail to do this and the next dreamscape you visit may contain whale-rib rings under a brass sky—other travellers will arrive, read half-eternal footprints shaped like yours, and think they found a path.

Remember: you are not exploring a landscape. You are negotiating with an embryo that has not yet chosen the categories 'alive', 'dead', 'visitor', 'component'. The only victory is to leave with your outline unchosen, your chronology unimproved, your biology un-innovated.

Travel light. Travel forgettably. Return someone the multiverse already recognises.
