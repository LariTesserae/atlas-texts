0.  Golden Rule before all others
    Do NOT come here to “study,” “mine,” “claim,” or “rescue.”  
    Come only if you must understand what it feels like to be digested very, very slowly by a space that treats every heartbeat as a percussion overdub.  
    If you cannot hold that thought in your mind for ten continuous minutes without blinking, stay home.
