Field Briefing: Up-Mirror Hamlet  
 (a.k.a. “The Briar-Count,” “Reflective Silt Pocket,” “No-Shadow Pause”)

------------------------------------------------------------------
0.  Existential Warning  
This is not a destination you “reach.”  It is a destination that reaches into you when you mislay your reflection.  You cannot buy passage, charter a ship, open a gate, or pray your way in.  You must first be erased from every mirror you have ever looked into.  If that sentence frightens you, stop reading; the briefing ends here.  If it thrills you, continue—but understand that once inside you will be, to the outside world, another missing person whose case file turns yellow and is eventually boxed away.  Make peace with that before you leave ordinary reality.

1.  Physics You Will Meet  
Mirror-Sky Dome  
• Height above ground: ~36 m (≈ 300 arm-spans of the locals, who are small-boned).  Curvature is spherical, focal point 18 m above the hamlet’s geometric center.  A human standing there is optically at the center of a silvered bowl; every line-of-s—whether that word?—no.  Every line-of-“you” is returned inverted.  
• Reflection coefficient ≈ 0.98; expect 1 kW m⁻² of incident light even at apparent “night.”  No uv/ir filtering—sunburn is instantaneous, retinas weld within minutes without protection.  
• Cold does not follow thermodynamics.  It is a scalar pressure, not a gradient.  Objects feel 0 °C but do not radiate.  Tactile hallucination: fabrics feel wet though no damp exists.  Bring no insulating strategy you trust; you will not shiver, but cellular water still crystallises, slowly.  Frostbite announces itself as sudden anaesthetic patches.  Check digits every half hour.

No-Shadow Light  
• Photons appear mid-air, travelling in curved paths, then vanish.  Shadow is impossible; silhouettes are filled by ambient photon bloom.  Glare is constant f/1.4 noon.  If you are photosensitive, expect migraine within ten minutes.  
• Light carries semantic charge: micro-pressure on eardrum skin = language.  Locals “speak” by leaning 1–3 mm.  You will hear them as tinnitus-like pulses.  If pressure spikes, you are being addressed—respond with slow ballet, else considered rude.

Water-in-Reverse  
• Hydrologic cycle upside-down: evaporation occurs downward into stone; condensation climbs walls.  You cannot drink literal water—there is none.  Body fluid balance reverses: you urinate less, but eyelids and lips swell as serum migrates dermo-keratinously.  Expect 0.8 % total-body H₂O gain per 24 h.  Left untreated you will drown from inside, lungs filling with self-imported humidity.  Counter by swallowing local briar-twig “beetles” (see §3) which osmotically siphon surplus liquid and later escape as vapour.

2.  Biology & Culture  
Human Form  
Locals average 148 cm tall, 38 kg, skin metallised silver, pupils absent (light enters via cheek-tympanum).  Skeleton partially cartilage—facilitates glissade locomotion on wet stone.  Lifespan unknown; memory stretches backward until personal narrative intersects collective ledger.  They possess no proper names, only pressure “chords.”  Address one by replicating its micro-bow stance; variations invite intimacy or scorn.

Count Ecology  
• Flora: 79 briar-objects, jointed glass stems.  Do NOT exceed this count.  If you break one you must immediately balance by convincing another traveller to “lose” an arm-length of height (they compress easily).  Population stasis is religio-physical law; imbalance condenses the entire hamlet inward by the offender’s body-volume.  You will feel it physically—the alleys narrow by a thumb’s breadth every infraction.  
• Fauna: ∞ but conserved biomass projected on 2-D manifold.  They register thermally but not tangibly.  Do not let packs of flat-wolves crowd your silhouette; their “warmth” will out-compete your own circulation, you’ll faint while standing.

Economy & Law  
Three artefact classes circulate: bronze memory-flake, consent-mallet, amnesia-net.  Possession of any grants citizenship; locals will treat you as property of the tool.  Smuggling one out is impossible: on contact with outside air they sublimate into personal recollections, flooding you with centuries of strangers’ lives—usually lethal through synaptic overload (see Incident Log: “The Man Who Ate 600 Childhoods”).

3.  What To Pack (and What Not to Bother With)

A. Mirror-Cloak (mandatory)  
A double-sided blackout cloak with inner layer of powdered obsidian in resin.  Worn silver-side out you look like sky continuation; locals mistake you for weather and ignore.  Flipped matte-black inwards you create the only pocket of darkness hamlet can host; use to sleep, void surplus water, jot notes—ink exists only in darkness here.  Mass 900 g.  Carry spare; micro-fractures bloom under zero-degree scalar pressure.

B. Collapsible Umbrella-Skull  
Contrary to intuition you need shade not from sun but from vertical light-rain dripping through quartz roofs.  Photonic accumulation pools ankle-deep by dawn.  A carbon-fibre parasol with mirrored upper surface reflects drip back into sky; else you wake entombed in a sarcophagus of frozen brightness—locals bail you out with lily scoops but keep a finger as toll.

C. Briar-Beetle Flask  
Seal-able vial holding 20 captive liquid-crystal beetles.  Swallow one if cheek-skin feels taut (see Water-in-Reverse).  Beetle migrates to lungs, drinks excess plasma, exits later as sweat-vapour.  Overdose (three or more) crystallises bronchioles—fatal.  Mark flask with single-dose gradations; counting is life.

D. Tungsten-Wool Gloves  
Touch-screens, zippers, even your own eyelids: anything below −18 °C equivalent will not feel cold but destroys cells.  Gloves allow safe handling of local tools, stone ledges, children’s hands (they bruise easily and bruises count as ecological damage).

E. Anti-Parallax Goggles  
Contains one fixed periscopic mirror per eye, cancelling optical reversal of mirror-sky.  Without them every step is foot-over-head vertigo; nausea ruins posture vocabulary essential for speaking.

F. Do NOT Bring  
- Mirrors (personal or component).  Your passport in is absence of reflection; carrying one can eject you violently, shredding body along exit seam.  
- Metals denser than 7 g cm⁻³; they dematerialise into fog that drifts skyward and may plate your lungs when inhaled.  
- Food.  Local “fat” is memory-condensed; digesting it installs alien autobiographies.  Eat elsewhere.

4.  Arrival Protocol  
1. Lose reflection intentionally: stare into dark still water at civil twilight until inversion layer blanks your image.  A sideways tug follows; relax limbs—resistance causes spiral fracture at shin.  
2. You will slide three metres above ground, impelled by wind that tastes of extinct glacier names.  Keep body concave; if convex you roll into sky-mirror and stick, becoming a “floater” visible to later visitors as mobile frost-smudge.  
3. Landing is on slate-pebble mosaic.  Impact absorbed by capillary water-film; you will glide 2–3 m.  Do NOT stand until motion stops or ankles lock in wet ice.  
4. First minutes: Put on goggles, deploy umbrella-skull.  Any uttered sound brands you foreign—conversation attempted via larynx manifests as painful static in locals’ skin.  Instead, flex knees 3 mm forward; it translates to “Visitor greets, seeks count-equilibrium.”

5.  Daily Regimen  
Hours 0–4: Observation  
- Note population density around you.  If alleys feel narrower than shoulder-width you already erred—log which briar you might have failed to re-root.  Repair immediately.  
- Children will tug your cloak hem silently—interpreted as request for glide-passenger.  Accept; refusal carries social fine equal to 1 briar stem.  Glide together, keeping mirror-sky visible in peripheral vision to maintain orientation.

Hour 4–8: Labour  
- Assist “bailing” light from houses.  Scoop provided is bronze lily-petal; use Tungsten gloves.  Pour collected photon-puddle into public cistern where it re-evaporates, powering pressure-language.  Failure enlarges puddle gain and narrows habitation.  
- Guide two-dimensional fauna away from doorways using open-palm gestures.  Imagine shepherding projected slideshow; keep limbs extended so animals slide along skin surface.

Hour 8–12: Ledger Audit  
- Count briars (must be 79) and animals (use thumb-method: silhouette area equals your palm = 1 unit).  Any discrepancy must be balanced by personal mass donation.  Remove boot, shave sliver of leather, hand to elder.  Each gram equals one animal-unit.  Carry nail-scissors for this.  

Hour 12–16: Rest in Cloak  
- Sleep-cycle impossible in continuous noon; instead practice micro-trance: 90-second REM achieved by rolling eyes up until sky-mirror reflected retina fills vision.  Do this 20 times.  Dreams will aggregate; treat as single nightly cycle.  Missing trances accumulates as vertigo debt—payback requires lying prone while children glide over you; humiliating but harmless.

Hour 16–20: Cultural Exchange  
- Trade personal height for stories.  Locals adore millimetres; offer 1 mm of stature and they will gift fragment of bronze flake containing a memory-cut older than your civilisation.  Absorb by pressing to cheek-tympanum; you will temporarily understand pressure-speech.  Enjoy; fades in 6 h.  Cumulative height loss >3 cm causes spinal disintegration—measure hourly against handle of umbrella-skull.

Hour 20–24: Egress Window (optional)  
- Once daily, mirror-sky momentarily polarises at anti-solar node.  With goggles, locate 4 cm ellipse of pure darkness overhead.  Jump straight up while releasing all metals.  If you retained no reflection you slip through, emerging beside any still water in your home world.  Miss the timing and you wait another 24 h, during which hamlet contracts another thumb-breadth.  Few survive third attempt.

6.  Medical Contingencies  
Frost-Internalisation (no shiver, sudden white patches inside cheeks)  
- Swallow TWO beetles, crack stem of nearest briar; inhale vapor.  Repeat every 10 min until patches disappear.  

Vertigo-Loop (world flips 90° permanently)  
- Remove goggles, stare at own palm—only object whose image has not been mirrored.  Re-aligns horizon after ~5 min.  

Over-Pressure Lingo (you begin to understand all conversations simultaneously)  
- Indicates impending assimilation.  To delay, stuff ear canals with shavings from tungsten gloves; deafness blocks pressure words.  Emergency exit strongly advised.

7.  Ethical Imperatives  
- The hamlet is a closed thermodynamic poem.  Every entropy you export is replaced by shrinkage of their space.  Travel light, leave lighter—preferably taking nothing, donating mass where required.  
- Do not evangelise, photograph, or “rescue” locals.  History records only seven visitors; records also show population constant—balance is kept by forgetting.  The instant you are remembered outside, someone inside is forfeited.  
- On return, avoid mirrors for 40 nights.  Your reflection is under probation; should it find you, you will be repossessed, yanked sideways, and the mirror you peered into will show hamlet-scape forever after—another window for the next exile.

8.  Final Advice from the Seventh (and only articulate) Returnee  
“Take the job of being temporary with absolute seriousness.  They have no death, only compression.  Every courtesy you extend buys cubic centimetres of their world.  Stand very still when you don’t know what to do; stillness is the only gift they can’t make for themselves.  And when the sky offers you that coin-sized hole of night, jump as if paying—because that, finally, is the price of having been seen at all.”
