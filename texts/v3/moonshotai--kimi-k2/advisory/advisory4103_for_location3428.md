PRE-TRAVEL BRIEFING  
for the Ordinary Human Multiverse Traveler  
Destination: NEW GANYMEDE, basin city on the young basaltic plain, planet designation NG-4 (“the plain that forgot it was plain”).

________________________________________________
1.  IMMUNISE YOUR CLOCK
Time is a local joke. Every kilometre east or west rewrites your personal chronometer by a sliding thirteen-minute packet; the thirteen are non-repeating and non-refundable. Arrive with at least three independent clocks:  
- A mechanical wind-up that you never set foot on the soil with (keep it in a vacuum-shielded pouch).  
- A bio-entangled hourglass seeded with your own calcareous dust (time will flow toward your bones and away from city tricks).  
- A cheap digital throw-away you can gift to the city at the moment it asks for “one more minute.”  
Remember: the moment you notice the mismatch between them, you have already lived two versions of the hour. Choose one and apologise to the other.

________________________________________________
2.  WATER IS POLITE BUT NOT PREDICTABLE
Do not pack a canteen. The city regards closed containers as rudeness. Instead bring:  
- A hydrophobic tongue tattoo that steers condensation toward your mouth only when you are truly alone (ask for “lone-dew” at any off-world tattooist).  
- A collapsible 6 µm nylon bib to catch the upward-fall phase; wring it into your own shadow at midday when evaporation is safest.  
Do not drink during the sideways-rain events (see Local Hazard 6). The drops are edged and will slice thirst itself from your metabolism.

________________________________________________
3.  SHOES THAT FORGIVE
Footwear must satisfy three contradictions:  
a) Soles soft enough that infrasonic piers cannot lock your metatarsals into civic granite.  
b) Heels hollow so you can plant emergency ear-plugs (bone-conducted city song will otherwise ossify you within 42 minutes of standing still).  
c) Laces woven from optic micro-cable; if you need to pay a quick fine you can snip off a length and feed it to the humming street lights.  
Recommended civilian model: “Maybe Boots, Series ∞” – they arrive unlaced and ask you a question; answer honestly and they lace themselves to your morality. If you lie, they walk away without you.

________________________________________________
4.  CURRENCY & ACCOUNTING
Legal tender is visible light. Bring:  
- A mirror-lined throat pouch (practice swallowing torches in front of a dim bulb; never in sunlight—you could bankrupt yourself).  
- A prism denture; clicking it against your molar releases chromatic coin the tram-wires will accept.  
- One regret you are willing to never remember again. It will be asked for at the gates when you want to leave. Label it in advance or the city will pick your most precious one.

________________________________________________
5.  BODY MODIFICATIONS, TEMPORARY
Visitors are expected to grow a spare organ within ten hours of arrival. Print shops are at every mood-adjacent doorway. Choose:  
- Graphite-polymer heart (standard, beats only while you daydream—pack headphones of your favourite lullaby).  
- Secondary eard parked in the left ear cavity; it lets you hear when your footsteps are about to be stolen (you will hear them ahead of you).  
- Optional tail of ionised mercury; it streams behind and writes your name in reflective rooftops so you can find yourself again after topology shifts.

________________________________________________
6.  LOCAL HAZARDS
Sideways rain (warm, soundless, razor-thin)  
SIGN: sky develops “too many angles,” puddles climb your shins.  
COUNTER-MEASURE: spin on the spot three times—this convinces the horizon you are already sliced and it leaves you intact.

Doppel-sound theft  
SIGN: you hear a footstep identical to yours.  
COUNTER-MEASURE: stop, take one shoe off, throw it backward over your head. The echo will collect the shoe instead of your future.

Revert Option Fraud  
SIGN: morning announces it may become a gorge. Official offers you a “free” revert.  
COUNTER-MEASURE: read the memory they demand twice; if you still want it, drive a fingernail into the Government Tooth plaza to pay—never surrender childhood.

Gravity Inversion Burp  
SIGN: infrasonic yawning, doors swing, puddles climb.  
COUNTER-MEASURE: nothing—enjoy the brief relief from weight; just keep your mouth closed or the prism might drop out.

7.  ETIQUETTE
- Always greet a guard-crab; say “May you rust kindly.” They will remember and will not moult into your balcony railing.  
- Never photograph foxes made of noise; they are shy and will follow you home as tinnitus.  
- Offer empty air to citizens squeezed by mood-swap; cup hands, let them breathe into the vacuum, then release it back—considered noble.  
- Do not harvest plants that taste of radio—children may, adults may not. Age is judged by whether you still know the homework you wish to forget.

________________________________________________
8.  DEPARTURE WINDOW
There are two ways out:  
1) Walk east until thirteen-minute errors accumulate to one full extra lifetime; you will see a train that boards only people who recognise no one on it.  
2) Announce a mood that no living resident shares; the gate will swap you with the emptiest version of yourself—usually the one that never left Earth. Be sure you want to meet that person; they will insist on taking your place in every universe you ever visit again.
