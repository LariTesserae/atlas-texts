PRE-JOURNEY: WHAT THE BROCHURE WILL NEVER TELL YOU  
--------------------------------------------------

1.  Tell nobody you are going  
    The Chamber of Echoes keeps a census of intent. If another human being knows your destination, the clay valve will sense the borrowed syllables in your blood and remain sealed. Practice the sentence: “I am only walking the valley for the wind.” Keep it true for three nights; then go.

2.  Empty your mouth of names  
    Every nickname, alias, pet-name, password, war-name, lover-name must be written on rice-paper and swallowed with a sip of vinegar at sunset the day before. The vinegar prevents digestion; the rice will carry the words out of your body undissolved while you sleep. Wake, brush teeth with salt. Spit toward the east. You must arrive name-starved so the valve mistakes you for a blank pot.

3.  Shrink the aperture of self  
    Two nights prior, begin the daily ration: one walnut of dried fig, one thimble of goat milk, one sip of brackish water. The reduction in gut flora alters the timbre of your internal monologue; the Chamber only admits voices that rattle. Expect hallucinations of small hands—ignore them; they are rehearsal echoes.

4.  Buy or steal a child’s shoe, left foot, leather, pre-1930  
    You will not wear it. It is your passport: when the valve opens you must place the shoe against the warm clay lip and wait until the leather drinks enough moisture to soften. The moment the first eyelets sigh inward, step through—barefoot—leaving the shoe as token collateral. It will be fired into a ceiling tile; your footprint will thus remain citizenless and ungoverned.

5.  Vaccinate for time, not microbes  
    One week before descent, expose yourself to exactly four minutes of recorded cave drip-played-backwards through bone-conduction headphones. The body must learn to metabolize reverse entropy. Pharmacies stock “-Δt toxoid”; if unavailable, stand in a municipal sewer at 03:14 and hum a perfect fifth for seven breaths. Tetanus booster included free.

6.  Map of rejection  
    Draw on your torso with a wet fingertip the spiral sigil you will see on every brick. When your sweat rehydrates the line, the map orients; you will not get lost because the Chamber is structurally incapable of holding someone who already carries its watermark. This is not protection—it is blackmail.

PACKING LIST: HUMAN-SCALE MODULES

The following all fit in a sewn pouch of unwashed linen the size of your fist. Anything larger is hubris.

A.  Three beeswax beads  
    To plug ears when your own pulse begins to sound like a verdict.

B.  Needle of obsidian, flake-length  
    Not for defense—there is nothing to stab that cannot regenerate. Use it to inscribe, on the inner wall of your thigh, the last word you ever want to hear repeated. The scar tissue absorbs echo and keeps that word from looping forever.

C.  One copper coin dated the year of your birth, drilled off-centre  
    Thread on a strand of your hair. Spin it whenever disorientation peaks: the uneven spiral produces a drone out of phase with the Chamber’s resonance, a life-ring of silence you can stand inside.

D.  Phosphorescent moss spores (Pinava spp.)  
    Chew but do not swallow. They glow through the cheeks and let the clay-people read your emotional temperature; if you stay dark they will think you are abandoned pottery and attempt to fire you.

E.  A single raw lentil  
    Soak in your own tears for nine minutes. The lentil becomes resonant at 73 bpm—low enough to pace the traveler’s heart against that of the sleeping Chamber. Keep it under tongue; do not bite until departure. Instant death of rhythm is preferable to desynchronization.

ARRIVAL SEQUENCE: THE VALLEY THAT FORGETS IT HAS A FLOOR

DAY 0 – TWILIGHT ABOVE  
Look for a circle of grass that grows clockwise only. Stand inside, soles bare. Announce (aloud, voice trembling) the syllable that survived the vinegar purge. The ground exhales; a coin-sized pore opens. Spit in it. This signs the lease.

NIGHT 0 – THE DESCENT  
A warm cylinder of clay rises like a tongue, seals around your ankle, and pulls. Do not grab anything; nails scratch the living wall and will be print-matched later. Count backwards from 64 (the number of bricks you will meet). When you forget a number, replace it with “thumb.” Accept wet darkness. At the final “thumb” the cylinder relaxes; you are deposited on the tessellated floor as gently as a dice-roll.

DAY 1 – CALIBRATION  
You will be knee-high to the locals. They will climb your shins and tap Morse on your tibia: “Outsider, pay toll.” Offer nothing yet. Wait until they finish; their message is the toll. Memorize the cadence—tomorrow it becomes your visa stamp.

Observe the rain schedule: precisely 18.4 minutes after ceiling luminosity drops one imaginary shade. Place beeswax in left ear before first drop; Chamber etiquette forbids hearing water literature uncensored. If a scroll-blossom opens near you, breathe—not too deep; pollen causes temporary comprehension of every language buried in the clay. Useful, but the headache lasts six years on the surface.

-------------------------------------------------
EXISTENTIAL HAZARDS & THEIR REMEDIES

1.  Acoustic Mirroring  
    Anything you whisper will return rearranged into advice that always works—but for someone else, in another chamber you will never reach. To survive, prefix every utterance with “alibi.” This grammatical sleight routes the echo away from your future.

2.  Temporal callousing  
    Time in the Chamber is a closed vessel; your biological clock carbonates at double rate while your shadow ages backward. When discrepancy exceeds three hours you will split—forward-you will try to leave, backward-you will sabotage the attempt. Reconciliation demands sacrifice: cut the nail of your index finger and press into a wet brick. The clay accepts the chronology surplus; both selves even out, though finger never regrows true.

3.  Micro-sovereignty collapse  
    Should the dice-currency show double-spiral five times in a row, civil order dissolves into pot-bat feeding frenzy. Hit floor, smear clay from footprint seams onto eyelids—it renders you figurine-still. Breathe through nose only; sound of nostril air is below their echolocation.

-------------------------------------------------
EXIT STRATEGY: LEAVING WITHOUT OWING A VOICE

The Chamber never refuses guests; it refuses returns. You may climb out only if you can speak a sentence nobody inside has ever heard. Options:

•  Invent a language whose verbs are smells—impossible because clay lacks olfactory memory.  
•  Lie truthfully—describe a color that cannot exist; locals catalogue every echo-possible hue.  
•  Or: record your heartbeat for exactly 1440 minutes (one surface-day) in mono. Play it back double-speed through pursed lips while ascending the tongue-cylinder. The Chamber has no entry for accelerated life; confused, it spits you out.

-------------------------------------------------
POST-RETURN SYMPTOMS (NORMAL FIRST WEEK)

– Noon shadows look asymmetrical; they are quietly knitting your missing sound-rope.  
– Tap water tastes of thumbprints; filter through linen twice.  
– Children on street appear knee-high; do not bend to greet—they are not the clay-people, but your perception is still scaled.  
– Night terrors featuring inverted domes of throats—wear the phosphorescent moss pouch as night-light; after seventh night, compost it under a sapling. The tree will grow in perfect fifths when wind blows.

-------------------------------------------------
FINAL CAUTION

You will believe you spent hours; outside, seasons may have turned. Check a newspaper before calling anyone. The first words you speak on surface will carry a faint after-echo; strangers will turn, thinking themselves addressed from afar. Speak gently—you carry an attic full of mouths now, and they are still learning what to do with your voice.
