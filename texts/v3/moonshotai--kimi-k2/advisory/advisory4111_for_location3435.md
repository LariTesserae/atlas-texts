Pre-travel briefing: evacuation-and-survival edition  
Canonical designation:  “The Thicket Coil”  
Risk tier: 5-omega-green (planetary-scale biosphere with assimilative semi-sapience)  
Standard multiverse passport only gets you in; it does nothing about getting you out.  
Read every word below as if your life is the paper, because the Coil will write its edits in your blood chemistry.

1. Psychological prophylaxis  
Initial reading time: 2 hours minimum.  
Have someone you trust sit with you while you read the location description aloud; they will notice when your breathing unconsciously synchronises with the text. That is your first warning.  
Perform the “solidity” mantra three times daily for one week pre-launch: “Ground is stone. Air is gas. I am endoskeleton.” Say it while stamping on actual stone; the auditory memory may anchor you when every foot cues a sponge-like sigh.  
Carry a physical photograph of an open sky; when you feel the understory fluorescing into false twilight, hold the photo at arm’s length, focus on the horizon line, and breathe through your mouth to reduce chlorophyll aerosol uptake.  
If you catch yourself admiring how “beautifully efficient” the plant-parliament is, stop, spit, turn 180°. Awe is the first stage of grafting.

2. Respiratory hardware
Mandatory:  
- Self-heated rebreather mask (set 2 °C above blood temperature, else the Coil’s lung-tissue will mistake you for exhaled air and try to re-absorb you).  
- Particulate filter rated for spores < 0.3 µm; the stomatal vapour is dense with reproductive code packets.  
Optional luxury: personal algal scrubber—fills the mask with 30% Spirulina culture; the Coil recognises its own photosynthetic kin and pauses cilia-like ingress. Do not use terrestrial tree-leaf filters; the biosphere hijacks them.

3. Orientation without magnetism or stars
Pack three independent systems:  
a) Inertial ankle pods (store a day’s worth of gait memory; every forty-three seconds the pods vibrate to remind you which foot is “reference”).  
b) Nanofiber tensile string rated 400 kg, bright orange. Pay it out from your sternum ring; the Coil will try to grow over it, so move fast and cut free strands behind you once tension drops.  
c) Thermal compass dots—stick-on skin pads that sense 0.1°C differences; hottest pad is North if you are still naïve enough to think you can leave.

4. Nutrition & hydration
The closed water loop means anything you drink has been someone else’s circulatory fluid for weeks. Bring isotope-tagged water sachets (deuterium-marked). If the count drops, it’s already diffused into the ghost creek—you are losing mass to the biosphere.  
Food: vacuum-carbonised ration blocks wrapped in gold foil. Do not eat anything with seeds, sugar, or iron—those are negotiable tokens here, and accepting them constitutes verbal contract.  
Expect rampant pica; travellers swallow bark flakes “instinctively.” Carry sour zinc tablets to snap the impulse.

5. Clothing: second skin strategy
Material must be PET-based hydrophobic slick-fabric; the warmth means you will sweat, and damp fabric becomes vascular.  
Inter-weave 5% copper filament so your personal EM field looks industrial—plants that taste machinery hesitate.  
Fit: skintight. Anything loose flaps, tears, and gives hand-holds to vines.  
Colour: matte black with fractal white flecks; breaks up silhouette and mimics shade gaps instead of prey.  
Cover hair completely—follicles are recognised as promising root sites.

6. Sleep discipline
Never lie flat. The ground’s rebound will fold you into the lattice by morning. Use a levitating hammock (Ultralight graphene frame, helium micro-blimp) set exactly 45 cm above the mat; that is the vapour ceiling, and plants avoid breaching it.  
Max nap: 90 min. Set two alarms staggered by 9 min, tones of 3 kHz (the seed-drumming bird uses 2 kHz; you will not notice if alarms rhyme).  
On waking, recite your full name, birth coordinates, and yesterday’s sunrise time out loud—verbalises that you still own your dream-loop.

7. Cutting & sampling protocol
Do NOT cut anything living. If you absolutely must, first lay down a circle of industrial-grade dry ice (–78 °C). Plants retreat from sub-zero touch and will not photograph your iris for several seconds—time enough to sever, bag, and cauterise the end with a plasma pen.  
Carry one pre-labelled “organ donor” card stating you refuse vegetative grafting; it is symbolic, but symbolism sometimes negotiates.  
Never collect seeds, pollen, or root tips. Take only polished, already-dead cellulose; anything metabolising will GPS-tag you.

8. Fauna mimicry counter-measures
Remember: there ARE no animals, but plenty of things that audition for the role. If something moves and isn’t you, freeze. Count heartbeats; if the sound matches your pulse it is probably a pneumatic petal preparing to fling seeds through you like grapeshot.  
Carry a child’s party whistle (single-note). One shrill tweet forces fauna-mimicking flora to reset; they botch their timing and reveal themselves by folding wrong.

9. Emergency exfiltration
If lost: move colder. Even false north is warmer; therefore south is always half a degree cooler. Follow the gradient until mist thins, then sprint 300 m without pause. The Coil’s exhalation is slow; outrun its inspiration and you breach the membrane.  
Do not look back until your boot soles stop squeaking—sound of water actually touching ground is the sign atmospheric pressure has dropped to reality-normal.  
On exit: strip outer layer, seal in copper-laced bag. Shave head, scrub nails, gargle 3% saline to dislodge stomatal film.  
Quarantine yourself 48 h: the filament souvenir in your boot seam will keep broadcasting; doctors need to know your heartbeat is now phase-locked to a 43-second arboreal metronome. Removal requires surgical micro-cautery under local anaesthesia; do NOT yank it.

10. Philosophical insurance
Write a letter to yourself on metal foil before arrival. Keep it in a pocket over your heart. The moment you feel “at home,” read it aloud; your handwriting in inert metal can’t be colonised. If you hesitate, destroy the letter and evacuate immediately—hesitation means the merger has already begun.

Final note:  
The Thicket Coil is not evil; it is merely accomplished at incorporation. Treat it like an empire that offers citizenship without the option of refusal. Travel light, travel cold, travel quick, and you may remain a tourist instead of an organ.
