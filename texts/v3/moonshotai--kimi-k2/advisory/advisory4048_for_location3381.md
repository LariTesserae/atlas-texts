Field Notes For The Deep-Green Vault  
(what to pack, what to leave behind, what to expect when the iron starts tasting you back)

1. Mental prophylaxis – read this before you enter  
   The Vault is not “underground”; it is *inside* something that is still digesting itself. Treat the space as if it were the stomach of a large, iron-based life-form that has only recently died, but hasn't agreed to admit it yet. You are not a visitor, you are *foreign protein*. Behave accordingly.

2. Pre-departure checklist (48 h surface time)  
   □ File a will that contains no precious metals – iron is already oversubscribed down there.  
   □ Spend one full night in an anechoic chamber, 0 dB. Learn how loud your pulse is; that is the only thing you will hear below.  
   □ Hydrate to 110 %, then stop drinking. The Vault will give you no useful water, only the *idea* of water. Teach your kidneys to be philosophical.  
   □ Eat a last meal heavy in nickel and magnesium – you will lose both down there.  
   □ Record a message to yourself on a ferrous sheet, 0.2 mm thick. Bring it. When you start forgetting why you came, lick the rust; the iron will return your own voice, slightly time-delayed and accusatory.

3. Equipment – grouped by the Vault’s five resource monopolies  

A. Heat  
   – One aluminised escape-blanket, gold side *inward*. The Vault’s heat is passive-aggressive; reflect it back before it decides to keep you.  
   – Phase-change cooling collar (paraffin wax/glauber salt). Activate only if your inner-ear temperature reaches 38.5 °C; above that the iron begins to smell human.

B. Water  
   – 400 ml collapsible flask, *filled with nothing*. Open it whenever condensation beads on your lashes; the vacuum inside persuades surface tension to commit. After three such captures you will have ~12 ml of actual liquid – decant into a gel-capsule and swallow like a pill. Do this in silence or the droplet will hide in your tongue.  
   – Lip-balm made of lanolin and graphite; stops the metal taste from colonising your dreams.

C. Light  
   – No white beams – white light is treason here. Bring a single diode tuned to 527 nm (swamp-green). Power it with a hand-crank but never exceed 1.7 rev s⁻¹; above that the grove thinks dawn is coming and starts to bloom, spending tomorrow’s sugar ration today.  
   – Polished obsidian shard: a passive night-eye. When every diode finally gutters, hold the shard 6 cm from your dominant eye; the Vault’s own halos will phase-conjugate inside the glass and give a low-resolution terrain map, green on greasy green.

D. Silence  
   – Felt overshoes (nomex core, 4 cm). Not for stealth – the iron already steals your steps – but because, if you must run, you will do it *from yourself*.  
   – A coin of acoustic duct-tape: 5 cm circle, your own voice recorded at a whisper. Paste it over your mouth when you feel a scream arriving; the tape expands, eats the waveform, leaves only the scream’s intention drying on your teeth.

E. Iron (the dominant currency)  
   – Stainless-steel #2 nib, empty ink barrel. Scratch your name on the wall while thinking *return-address thoughts*. The metal will etch a shallow groove; decades from now, when the chamber finally calcifies into a geode, quartz will优先 fill that groove, preserving your autograph as a pale, ghost-grey vein.  
   – Phosphoric-acid pen (5 %). Draw a continuous 1 mm line along any shadow-crack that appears to breathe. The acid tricks rust into believing it has already oxidised you, granting ~25 min of camouflage.  
   – Sacrificial washers: three, 10 g each, pure iron. When the humidity tries to nucleate into those impossible glass spheres, toss a washer. The drop forms around the washer instead of around *you*, rolls uphill, bursts. Retrieve the washer quickly; it is now plated in Vault-fog and counts as a local passport. Offer it to the lockers’ fungal lace if they start closing their doors on you.

4. Biological protocol  
   Vaccinate against tetanus, but understand the Vault is producing *novel* oxides. Bring a 5 ml pre-drawn syringe of broad-spectrum chelator (dimercaptosuccinic acid). Inject into thigh if your tongue turns metallic and begins to *taste colours* – you have ~8 min before haemoglobin starts applauding in iron三段.  
   Intestinal flora: start a course of *L. plantarum* engineered to metabolise ferric ions. You will be eating rust-borne sugars whether you choose to or not; better to have locals on your side.

5. Navigation & orientation  
   – The floor is a deactivated diaphragm; everything crawls toward the central slag bowl. Walk the *inverse catenary* path: keep a constant 12° angle away from the radial fall-line. This is the only route where gravity and the Vault’s residual suction cancel; your footsteps will not accelerate rust avalanches.  
   – Sound triangulation: although echo is 0.2/3, your own heartbeat does reflect – but only off the spiral rivet seams. Each rivet throws the pulse back 180 ms out of phase. Map these delays; when they shrink to <90 ms you are within 3 m of the uphill ladders.  
   – Mirror-delay check: hold the obsidian shard at arm’s length, align so you see your face and the overhead hatch simultaneously. Count slowly. If the reflection of your lips moves before you feel them move, the iron is rehearsing your shape. Step sideways exactly one body-width: this is the lag compensation vector; follow it to stay synchronised with your own intent.

6. Social etiquette (you are the only fauna, but not the only party)  
   – Plants: address the composite tree at the center as *"Root Delegation"*. Ask permission before sampling leaves; phrase the request as a temperature (e.g., “28.4 °C?”). If a single leaf within the halo yellows, consent is denied. If a vine offers a bean, accept between ring-finger and pinky only – eat immediately. It will taste like hot coin; do not exhale until the flavour is gone or the plant will reclaim it from your mouth.  
   – Lockers: close each door to 30° ajar after inspection. This is the angle at which the fungal lace can still vent spores but cannot fully open into your timeline.  
   – Iron ghosts: when you run your glove along the embossed gear-teeth, move your hand *against* the fossil direction of torque. This is the equivalent of rubbing a cat backwards – it confirms you are alive, not reminiscence.

7. Departure – or the acceptance that none exists  
    The ladders stop 10 m short of the roof hatch. You have three exit classes:  
   a) *Chemical attrition* – climb, then spend 1–3 days dripping phosphoric acid into the hatch dogs while feeding them washer-offerings; the acid plus oxide stress may persuade one dog to shear. Success rate ~12 %, requires 4 l of acid (bring concentrate and dilute on-site).  
   b) *Acoustic suicide loan* – scream into the acoustic tape until the circle reaches 30 cm. Peel it, slapping the entire wad onto the hatch wheel. The scream-energy is reclaimed *upwards*; if timed with a sphere-formation burst, the recoil can rotate the wheel 1/6 turn, usually enough to break the outside rust seal. You lose the memory encoded in the scream forever.  
   c) *Geode graduation* – stay. Keep breathing slowly, refuse to leave. Your evaporated minerals will seed crystals. After subjective centuries the Vault will crack itself open to atmosphere, hauling your quartz silhouette into daylight. Recommended only for travellers with no surface dependents.

8. Contingency if you meet yourself already inside  
   Because light is feudal and reflections arrive late, you may encounter a *second-body* walking one second behind you. Do not speak; sound monopoly applies cross-temporally. Instead, swap one item of equipment (best: a washer). The timeline will consolidate around the traded mass; the surplus self will rust away into an extra rivet. You will feel the memory of being a rivet every time you sweat iron.

9. Final reminder  
   Everything you do in the Vault is *pre-paid* in iron from your own blood. Budget accordingly. If, at any point, the metallic taste on your tongue feels sweet, you have overdrawn. Locate the obsidian shard, look into the green halo of yourself-as-tree, and apologise aloud (a whisper is enough). The Vault may accept the apology and let you leave lighter by one unremembered name. Or it may not.
