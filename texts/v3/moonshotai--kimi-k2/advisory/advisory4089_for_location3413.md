Field Briefing: Preparing for Deployment to the Verdant Abyss  
(Version 7.3 – compiled by the Onto-Ecology Section, Inter-olar Bureau of Atmospheric Works)

0. Reality Check  
The plateau is not a forest you enter; it is a digestive field that has agreed, for the moment, to tolerate solids shaped like humans. There is no mineral “ground,” no firmament, no convenient off-switch for the living quilt. Prepare to be wet, dim, and slowly ingested—then plan how to ask the organism to stop at the dermis.

1. Immunological Front-loading  
• Start a prophylactic mycobiome six weeks out: drink teas of *Tramétes host-gift* and *Xylóspora lente* to seed gut flora that already speak the Abyss’ dialect.  
• Request a full-spectrum helminth reset: you want commensal worms that will out-compete any Plateau larvae for space along your intestinal wall. The locals find empty meat suspicious and may fill it if it’s virgin territory.  
• Implanted slow-release granules of voriconazole under each kneecap—fungal volatiles here are aggressively social; discourage the courtship before it reaches blood-brain barrier bargaining.

2. Wet-heat Conditioning  
Acclimatise in a sauna set to 38 °C, 98 % RH, lights dimmed to 40 lux. Practise tying knots, rehydrating ration gels, and mapping by compass while visibly sweating. Do it blindfolded; the Abyss will ask the same of you under chlorophyll twilight. Learn to read heart-rate rather than sunlight for circadian pacing.

3. Clothing – Anti-biofilm Armour  
Outer layer: monofilament basalt mesh painted with copper-green cuprous oxide. The mesh is slick enough that most vines will anchor elsewhere; the copper discourges algal sperm from germinating on your elbows.  
Mid-layer: phase-change cellulose felt impregnated with charcoal and citronella-derivative to confuse pheromone-tracking midges that double as pollinators and notary publics.  
Inner layer: raw silk painted weekly with your own saliva (ship a starter ampoule to mission prep so the fabric learns you). The plateau recognizes owner-enzymes and will, mostly, not absorb you as litter.  
Footwear: none. Rubber soles rot in a fortnight and become ankle garrottes. Instead, harden your plantar fascia on graduated stone beds, then coat feet daily with pine pitch mixed with您的own skin flora—smells like “local kin” to sentinel roots.

4. Nutrition – Closed-loop or Negotiated  
Option A – Hermetic  
Carry a photo-bioreactor tube strapped to your back: spirulina gengineered to incorporate your DNA tags; feed it your urine, drink its syrup. Zero macro-waste other than CO₂, which you vent through a one-way osmium valve at collar level.  
Option B – Reciprocal  
Bring small quantities of high-status food (fermented cacao, dried star-fruit leather) wrapped in fig leaves. Offer them to slug-councils at “momentaries” when you need diplomatic pause. The exchange must be chewed by you first; enzymes in the fruit signal mammalian synaptic density and convince predators your memories are already spoken for—like a library book that can’t be re-checked-out.

5. Excretion Discipline  
Faeces are a love-letter here. Either polymer-bag and carry (adds 250 g per diem) or pre-seed with *Bacillus praedormitum* spores that calcify the surface into truffle-like nodules recognized as “gifts.” Never defecate into living wood; that’s a marriage proposal.

6. Illumination & Power  
Visible flashlights are read as gap-wounds inviting grafts. Use near-IR lasers (905 nm) when you really must see distant detail; local optics top out at 880 nm. Power with betavoltaic film batteries—plateau humidity condenses into every cavity, so anything spinning or combusting drowns in days. Build a pocket leaf-battery: sandwich your anode/cathode gauze between two removable sheets of living orchid; photosynthetic sugar tide trickle-charges even under 0.3 % full-spectrum photons.

7. Orientation – No Magnetism, No Stars  
The gneiss bedrock is paramagnetically homogeneous; your compass will spin like a fan. Instead, carry an acoustic map: a set of glass chimes tuned to 18 kHz. Each plateau elder has a boles-horn cavity resonating at that band. Triangulate by cupping the echo delay (use bone-conduction earpiece). Map is updated every dusk as trees swell; expect 0–7 m drift per 24 h.

8. Danger Taxonomy  
a. Memory-lickers – Felinoid samplers. Present salt tablet, allow one lick, back away.  
b. Softfall shafts – Collapse-sinkholes. Listen for hydraulic sigh. If trapped, exhale completely; the mattress will float you like quicksand until you can roll sideways.  
c. Mirror-root caverns – Gravity inversion pocket. Do not dive; capillary tension will pull blood to your head and you’ll faint upside-down—impossible rescue.  
d. Green-alcohol nights – Fifty-year flowering. Hallucinogenic vapor: carry carbon-filter bandana soaked in vinegar.  
e. Self-burial urge – Symptom of plateau respiration sync. Counter with rhythmic gum-chewing; jaw proprioception resets autonomous vagal tone.

9. Language Cheat Sheet  
No spoken tongue, only chemical whispers. Portable kit:

• Ethyl-cinnamate puff = “I am passing through.”

Release doses into air; never touch wood while speaking or the bark will graft the sentence onto a passer-by.

10. Bio-mimetic Camouflage  
If surveillance (or aesthetic discomfort) becomes acute, trigger the moss protocol: Rub forearms with diluted abscisic acid; stand still. Epiphytic spores sprout within minutes, colonising skin at 2 mm h⁻¹. Within an hour you are a slightly lumpier trunk; heart rate syncs, infrared signature folds into background. Reversal spray: gibberellin + 5 % salicylic mist—sheds covering in 20 min, itchy but bloodless.

11. Psychologic Prophylaxis  
The plateau thinks centuries; default human cognition ticks at 1-°s⁻¹. Differential = vertigo. Adopt a slow mantra: one syllable every forty breaths (about 8 min). Example: “in – ex – hale – green – debt – repaid.” Keeps thoughts lobed with local cadence; prevents intrusive root-dreams from threading your REM.  
Keep a physical “fast-forward token” (steel gear-wheel, polaroid of city lights). Grip when you feel identity sluicing away; the metallic tetanic stimulus spikes proprioception reminding you of civil vertigo and speeds cortical rhythms back to baseline.

12. Exiting / Rescue  
There is no path out—only a negotiated exhalation. When extraction window approaches, ascend any emergent “momentary,” strip to silk inner layer (reveals mammal signature pheromones). Ignite 10 g magnesium ribbon; UV flare convinces the canopy you are a softfall wound. Trees will evert sap, creating a temporary negative pressure chimney. Clamp IR strobe onto the ribbon; orbital/skyhook drone hooks the plume within 12 h. Do not look straight up; magnesium glare will blind you for days in this dimness and the plateau will adopt you as impaired fauna—caretaker vines will never let go.

13. Cultural etiquette (for what passes as culture)  
• Gift first-shed hair to any branch that brushes you; knot loosely—accepts transience.  
• Never break a living stem for leverage; twist 360° then wait for autocannibalistic fracture to occur—permission versus violence.  
• Songs are welcome; they colonise air but dissipate—preferred. Do not shout warnings; shout is interpreted as rot-alarm and triggers biochemical siege.
