Field Briefing: The Pocket Vale  
(For baseline-humans transiting from universes that obey orthodox thermodynamics)

----------------------------------------------------------------
0.  Mission Definition
 in one sentence  
“You are about to walk through a photograph that thinks it is a calibration rig.”  
Everything below is meant to keep you recognisably human on the return trip.

----------------------------------------------------------------
1.  Pre-Jump Health Requirements  
A.  Cardiovascular  
Standard high-altitude clearance (equivalent 5 500 m) even though the air feels cool-pleasant; the pressure reading is fake and your blood will still desaturate.  
B.  Vestibular  
No history of motion-paralysis syndromes. The “still-point” illusion can re-write inner-ear calibration and leave you with chronic vertigo once you re-enter normal wind.  
C.  Neurology  
Absolute rule: no active migraine, temporal-lobe epilepsy, or stereognosis disorders. The repeating glyphs on the foliage are low-grade cognitohazardous; 8 % of baseline readers report persistent after-images that cycle for weeks, interfering with reading normal text.  
D.  Psychology  
Score ≤ 17 on the Stillwater Dissociation Scale. The vale’s perfect stasis can graft itself onto personal time-sense; returnees have required de-isolation therapy after “waiting for the next minute” for five days straight.

----------------------------------------------------------------
2.  Permitted Gear List (weight < 12 kg)  
- 1 × mechanical analog watch with radium dial (no electronics—the 60 Hz hum will fry quartz crystals).  
- 1 × 1-litre squeeze canteen filled with triple-distilled water (you will not find drinkable liquid; the metallic sap is toxic by heavy-ion load).  
- 2 × dark-grey glass ampoules, 50 ml, medical oxygen (acts as temporal anchor: crack one if subjective time slows; 3 pause-breaths usually re-syncs).  
- 1 × mirror-polished survival blanket (signals yourself in the inverted sky-vale; also blocks accidental glyph-reading).  
- 1 × graphite stick note-pad, tear-off sheets. Do NOT bring ink pens; the thicket leaves shred ball-points. Record impressions with soft 2B only.  
- 1 × monofilament line, 20 m, Day-Glo orange (tie every 5 m when you advance; if line goes “slack” mid-air, stop—you’re approaching the compression wall).  
- 1 × brass whistle, low B-flat (will answer the nickel-slab bell tone and give you triangulation if you lose visual bearings).  
- 1 × blindfold, pure silk (for glyph-overflow events—when you can’t stop reading the leaves).  
- 1 × empty 35 mm film canister (collect a 3 cm metallic wire; seals hermetically and prevents post-exposure oxidation).  

Forbidden  
Camera drones, GPS, lasers, flares, alive plants or animal tissue, firearms (if you fire one the echo folds the percussion back; the standing wave can burst tympanic membranes).

----------------------------------------------------------------
3.  Biologicals & Drugs  
Inject 0.4 ml “Steady-Now” (lab-grade diurnal anchor) intramuscular 20 min pre-transit. Bring two boosters; if you notice your pulse slowing below 45 bpm, self-administer.  
Carry 600 mg chelated magnesium; take 200 mg every 6 hr subjective to reduce micro-tremors induced by the 60 Hz hum.  
No caffeine/alcohol for 48 hr pre-jump—adenosine competition exaggerates the “stillness hangover”.

----------------------------------------------------------------
4.  Clothing Protocol  
Layer 1: moisture-wicking bamboo fibre long-sleeve (prevents salt crystals rasping skin).  
Layer 2: matte-black soft-shell to mute reflections; no bright hazard colours—they double in the mirrored vale.  
Layer 3: disposable Tyvek painter’s suit. Discard before exit; fibres trap glyph fragments and can out-gas metallic dust in normal atmosphere.  
Footwear: ankle-high approach shoes with non-magnetic soles; the nickel plates become surprisingly hot after 30 min and will melt rubber if not thermally buffered.  
Gloves: thin nitrile under leather; the razor laminae of “leaves” slide-read cutaneous nerves and can inject sub-dermal silver needles.

----------------------------------------------------------------
5.  Navigation & Spatial Etiquette  
- Count your footsteps on the outbound leg; multiply by 1.11 on return (distance compresses 9 % backwards).  
- Stay on the nickel pavers; salt crust is only 3 mm thick above void-holes leading to the glyph treadmill.  
- Do not touch the gauge needles inside sheds—surface tension keeps them centered; jostling spins the dial and re-writes local 0-reading, causing “creep-clock” syndrome in later travellers.  
- When you reach the compression curtain, extend your mirrored blanket first; if reflection waves, proceed three paces only. If it doesn’t wave, you’re already inside the mirror—back out immediately.

----------------------------------------------------------------
6.  Timeline & Exit Procedure  
Maximum stay: 2 hr 20 min objective (roughly 37 min subjective). Past that, entropy debt compounds and you will emerge aged by 0.8 days per extra minute.  
Before leaving, perform the Three Checks:  
  a) Shadow Test—turn 180°; if your shadow lags > 5 s you’re out of phase.  
  b) Breath Test—exhale slowly; if vapour hangs motionless, crack oxygen ampoule.  
  c) Echo Test—whistle single note; you should hear two returns. If you hear three, re-wolke via the same path instantly.  
Upon re-entry to mobile reality, record your first 100 random numbers (or any memory chain) to quantify cognitive drift. If sequence < 70 % accurate, initiate medical quarantine—short-term memory can remain “paused” for up to 36 h.

----------------------------------------------------------------
7.  Cultural & Ethical Notes  
The vale is not abandoned; it is an instrument whose users operate outside our phase of time. You are tolerated as long as you do not re-write the calibration (moving pavers, collecting more than a wire-sample, uprooting shrubs). Take nothing else, leave no biological trace—skin cells included (Tyvek suit solves most of this). Never ignite fire; the metallic foliage aerosolises and will burn in a colourless flash that etches the nickel road permanently.

----------------------------------------------------------------
8.  Contingency: If Someone Else Appears  
You may meet a figure dressed identically to you but reversed. Communication attempts have historically failed; wavelengths mismatch. Mirror-you will attempt to swap positions. Do not allow contact—temporal puckering can glue the two timelines. Maintain > 3 m distance, back away while facing, follow exit timetable. If followed, deploy blindfold (they usually won’t mimic the gesture and lose visual lock).
