Field Briefing: Expeditionary Protocol for “The Negative City”  
(aka The Municipality of Unconsidence, The Post-Lithic Metropolis, Calcareous Loop, etc.)

----------------------------------------------------
0. OVERVIEW
----------------------------------------------------
You are about to enter a terrain in which architecture, gravity, biology and memory behave as negotiable metaphors rather than fixed constants. The place digests possibility the way a forest digests light. Your body is a soft, water-based referendum that the city will try to veto by attrition. The briefing below is designed to keep you legible to yourself long enough to leave again.

----------------------------------------------------
1. MISSION PARAMETERS
----------------------------------------------------
Objective:  
Reconnaissance, sample retrieval (non-water), cartographic verification, return with identity checksum intact.

Length of stay:  
Clock time ≤ 36 h. Local “processional cycles” ≤ 4. (Beyond that, egress loops become self-occluding.)

----------------------------------------------------
2. PRE-JUMP BIOLOGICAL TUNING
----------------------------------------------------
A. Hydration Overload  
Begin hyper-hydration 48 h pre-insertion. Target urine specific gravity ≤ 1.005.  
Add 3 % glycerol to last litre: osmotic buffer against capillary evaporation in the anhydric atmosphere.

B. Erythrocyte Density Tweak  
Acclimatise on a 4 000 m plateau for five nights, then auto-phlebotomise 250 ml. Replace with 6 % hydroxyethyl starch (HES) suspension. Result: reduced blood viscosity, fewer CO₂ tension headaches, and the HES acts as a temporary water substitute in the micro-circulation.

C. Diet  
No protein 24 h prior—ammonia breath attracts reflection-vermin. Eat only simple polysaccharides soaked in brine; the residual salt film forms a ‘mineral disguise’ on skin lipids.

D. Memory Anchors  
Write (by hand) a 400-word autobiographical paragraph. Seal in a Möbius strip of Kapton tape; wear it as a forehead sweatband. The text is a feedstock checksum—when you can no longer recite it verbatim, abort.

----------------------------------------------------
3. GEAR MANIFEST
----------------------------------------------------
Every gram must earn its keep twice.

1. Suit: aluminised rip-stop coated with 50 µm micro-encapsulated H₂O. When flexed, the spheres burst and create a momentary humidity halo—confuses reflection-feeders that hunt IR gradients.  
2. Respiration: closed-circuit rebreather filled with neon/1 % xenon. Xenon is heavy and mimics argon in local mineral off-gassing, making your gas plume ‘read’ as geological.  
3. Footwear: negative-heel slippers (heel 2 cm lower than toe). The slope already tilts perception; reversing heel drop forces propriural conflict, preventing full ontological sync.  
4. Light source: 0.3 lux amber photophoretic strip powered by a betavoltaic wafer—no moving electrons, therefore nothing to audit by the city’s electrostatic census.  
5. Consumables: 6 calcium-impregnated wafers (4 g each). Chew one every 6 h; the mineral signature ‘tastes’ like native strata, reducing background rejection.  
6. Sample Kit: titanium ptychograph capsule—records topology instead of matter. Never bring back solid artefacts; they sinter into contradiction ash outside the loop.  
7. Emergency Tether: 22 m of Dyneema braided with human hair (your own, donated during quarantine). The hair contains mitochondrial debris—function as a biogenic spool that can reel you back along your personal thermodynamic vector.  
8. Psychological Object: bring a physical object that violates scale, e.g. a doll-house door. When you hold it you remind yourself you still possess an ‘inside’ and an ‘outside.’

----------------------------------------------------
4. IMMERSION ROUTINE
----------------------------------------------------
Step 1 – Arrival Breath  
Exhale fully, hold for 15 s. The hypoxic flash dims visual lag so the city’s parallax shuffle is less nauseating.

Step 2 – Ground-trace Check  
Drop a pinch of dyed quartz sand. If the grains form spontaneous Braille-like pits, gravity is locally consistent; proceed. If they spell actual letters, retreat 50 m and repeat until nonsense re-appears.

Step 3 – Naming Protocol  
Speak your surname backward once every 10 min. The phonemes act as an audio keel; without them your personal narrative drifts into the public domain.

Step 4 – Hydration Discipline  
Never drink. The city extracts ambiguity from water molecules; swallowed liquid will be subtracted from your long-term memory. Rinse mouth, spit on rock, watch the foam—if it crystallises into your dental imprint, wipe it away before it sets; that’s a spare you.

----------------------------------------------------
5. NAVIGATION LOGIC
----------------------------------------------------
Compasses spin. GPS fails. Use the following heuristics:

• Angle of Silence: Face the quietest quadrant; walk toward increasing hush only if your heartbeat grows louder. When both ambience and pulse mute simultaneously, turn 90°.  
· Reflection Density: Count the number of ghost-images of yourself per 100 paces. Keep the tally odd. If even, retrace your last fifteen footprints exactly—this reinstates ontological parity.  
· Fossil-Water Beacon: The calcified droplet you will eventually encounter is the only coordinate that refuses to drift. Treat its direction as true north for mapping purposes; log all vectors relative to it.

----------------------------------------------------
6. EXPECTED FAUNA INTERACTIONS
----------------------------------------------------
Thumbnail reflections (vernacular: “glints”)

Disposition: Obligate verticalivores.  
Threat: Not predatory, but if enough graze the same locus you will lose height—literally. Victims report looking ‘up’ into their own shoes.  
Counter-measure: Flash your suit’s humidity micro-spheres in staccato. The moisture ghosts distract them, buying you 15–20 s to relocate.

----------------------------------------------------
7. TIME-COURSE & WITHDRAWAL
----------------------------------------------------
T + 0 h – Footing feels rubbery; ignore.  
T + 6 h – Mild anterograde dribble (you begin to forget why you came). Eat first calcium wafer; recite autobiographical paragraph aloud.  
T + 12 h – Peripheral self-encounters begin. Permit them passage; do not converse even via gesture.  
T + 18 h – City attempts ontological bargaining: streets temporarily suggest shorter routes home. Dismiss; keep odd reflection tally.  
T + 24 h – If you no longer taste metal when touching tongue to teeth, chew second wafer and trigger betavoltaic lamp. Light confirms you remain photon-based.  
T + 30 h – Locate fossil-water bead; collect 360° ptychograph, plant hair-Dyneema spool, set auto-retraction timer for 6 h.  
T + 36 h – If tether has not auto-extracted, grip the autobiographical strip and walk backwards reciting it. This usually unspools the loop from the inside out. Expect nausea as the city submits eviction paperwork.

----------------------------------------------------
8. DEBRIEF & QUARANTINE
----------------------------------------------------
On return you will:

· Weigh 0.4–1.2 kg less—this is the mass of unmade decisions evaporating.  
· Produce urine loaded with dolomite grit; normal in 72 h.  
· Dream of buildings you can exit but never leave. The images fade when you speak the dreams aloud; silence fossilises them.

----------------------------------------------------
9. FINAL NOTE (PERSONAL)
----------------------------------------------------
Remember: the city is not hostile; it is post-hostile. It has already metabolised every objection you could raise. Your best defence is to remain an imperfect offering—too moist to calcify, too forgetful to be worth quoting, too present to become architecture. Travel polite, travel porous, and when the sky remembers it is supposed to fall, do not argue: just step aside.
