Field Briefing: The Graphite Blister  
(For baseline-human crossers only—no augments, no fluid-breathers, no water-dependent symbionts)

--------------------------------------------------------
0. Executive summary  
You are stepping into a pressure cooker that has never heard of water. The environment is hot, dry, carbon-saturated, and topologically folded. Nothing here will kill you quickly; it will simply erase the idea of “wet” from your body, then keep the charcoal silhouette you leave behind. Plan for three priorities:  
A) Keep your internal water where it belongs—inside you.  
B) Move slowly enough that space doesn’t notice you.  
C) Leave nothing that can be converted into wall-diaries.  

--------------------------------------------------------
1. Pre-embarkation medical kit  

1.1 Total-body seal  
- One-piece trilayer suit:  
  – Inner lamination: electrospun polyethylene 8 µm, hydrophobic, heat-welded seams.  
  – Middle: aerogel of resorcinol-formaldehyde carbonised at 1 200 °C; gives 30 mm insulation at 300 g total mass.  
  – Outer: vapor-deposited graphite film 500 nm, matched CTE to chamber walls so it doesn’t flake under thermal cycling.  
- Gloves and socks share the same stack; socks lock into ankle-ring gaiters so no wrist or ankle gap ever faces the air.  
- Visor is sapphire glass, inner face coated with indium-tin-oxide to reflect 2–20 µm IR (the band the leaves screech in).  
- All zips are outside the seal; you dress once, then a roommate welds the final seam with a pencil-iron. You will not open the suit inside.  

1.2 Hydration loop (closed)  
- 1.2 L isotonic saline bag worn sub-scapular; stays against bare skin so your own heat keeps it body-temperature.  
- Mouthpiece with one-way bite valve; the return line purges exhaled vapor through a graphene/silica desiccant canister that traps every picoliter of H₂O. The canister is colour-striped: when the first cerulean band turns umber you still have 6 h safe.  
- Salt tablets timed-release: keeps plasma osmolality up so the loop can be rationed.  

1.3 Metabolic heat sink  
- Phase-change belt: 400 g of encapsulated LiNO₃·3H₂O microcapsules melt at 29 °C, absorb 290 kJ before plateau break.  
- If belt fully melts (easy to feel—rigidity loosens) you must exit within 45 min; suit insulation buys that margin.  

1.4 Keratin safeguard  
- Two-layer nail varnish: base layer of parylene prevents trans-nail evaporation; top layer is carbon black so you look local and the chamber mistakes you for mineral.  
- Lips: beeswax-paraffin plug you bite down on; keeps the black dust from abrading mucosa and creating bleed-points where water can escape.  

--------------------------------------------------------
2. Kinetic protocol  

2.1 Cadence rule  
Maximum heel-to-heel period: 2.8 s (≈ 0.4 m s⁻¹). Faster and the air’s non-Newtonian response clamps you; you will feel it grab like setting gelatin. Practise the shuffle on a treadmill whose belt drags backward at 0.5 m s⁻¹—your forward speed then equals chamber tolerance.  

2.2 Footwear  
Soles made from polyether-ketone-ketone, 3 mm graphite spheres embedded. When the floor sighs and cracks, the spheres roll, letting you glide rather than catch an edge and torque a knee.  

2.3 Turning course  
Because space folds after ~20 min, you will encounter your own footprints older than you. Step inside them exactly; overlapping your future-self prints perturbs the manifold and doubles local thermal gain (you will feel it as sudden ear-popping).  

--------------------------------------------------------
3. Sustenance & waste  

Nothing wet may exit the suit.  
- Food: 40% water-by-mass energy paste in laminated toothpaste pouches; valves dock to a self-sealing port inside the cheek.  
- Urine: condom catheter to a hydrophobically lined leg bag filled with anhydrous CaSO₄; solidifies urine to a rubbery plug you carry out.  
- Feces: chemical biscuit—guar gum + MgO turns solids to odourless sheet you peel out at egress; same graphite-gift requirement applies, so keep it.  

--------------------------------------------------------
4. Psychological prophylaxis  

4.1 Sonic environment  
The castanet-screech is 75–80 dB, centred on 4 kHz, the band human hearing is most sensitive to. Use bone-conduction ear-plugs; they notch-filter 3–5 kHz while letting lower creaks through (needed for fold navigation cues).  

4.2 Chronod-distortion  
Because loops age footprints, your sense of progression erodes after ~45 min. Set a 7-min metronome vibration on the wrist; it’s shorter than the echo-lag and keeps subjective time anchored.  

4.3 Water-memory bleed  
Rule 3 demands “a memory of water.” Pre-load a 30-s first-person looped clip of you drinking, seal it in a thumbnail holo-crystal. Offer the crystal at a sonic leaf; it will etch/overwrite its data into a graphite lens then burst, satisfying the exit tariff without sacrificing endogenous fluid.

5. Contingencies  

Scorch-bloom (sulfur glass bubble pops in front of you)  
→ Freeze. Sudden movement pops more bubbles; chain reaction can focus dozens of heat-rays on your suit. Wait 90 s for thermal plume to settle.  

Suit breach (taste iron on tongue)  
– If desiccant still cerulean: slap a pre-cured graphite patch over tear; it sinters in the ambient heat and welds in ~20 s.  
– If desiccant umber: abort, re-trace immediately to entry ridge; you have ~8 min before plasma osmolality collapses.  

Ceiling blister drip (rare-sharp glass filament falls)  
Keep arms crossed over visor; PKK sleeves can take the slice. Do NOT look up—visor edge has no side impact rating.

7. Forbidden  

- No cameras with liquid crystal viewfinders.  
- No lighters, vapes, compressed gases.  
- No tears—emotion OK, but blink them back; one teardrop equals 20 min of suit margin lost.  

--------------------------------------------------------
8. Philosophical note (optional)  

The Blister is not hostile; it simply never evolved a solvent. Treat it like an anhydrous monastery: speak softly, walk slower than thought, pay your exit in memories of wetter worlds. Do that, and the metropolis of almost-nobodies will register you as just another polite non-breather passing through.
