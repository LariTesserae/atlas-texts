Preparation Manual  
for the Human Who Has Just Noticed the Hinge in the Air  
and Still Has Time to Decide  

0. The First Question (answer before you finish reading)  
Ask yourself: “Am I already inside?”  
If you have to think longer than three seconds, assume the answer is yes and skip to Section 7.  
If you are still on the rain-side of reality, read sequentially; do not shuffle the sections. The order is a lullaby the Ward has not yet learnt to sing.

--------------------------------------------------------
1. Pre-Departure Salt Bath
--------------------------------------------------------
Fill an ordinary bathtub with water as hot as you can tolerate. Dissolve:  
– 3 kg sea salt (coarse crystals)  
– 250 g Eica-lite™ (powdered silica from electronics stores)  
– One whole box of cheap black tea (the tannins become a false water-memory the Ward will try to drink first, buying you three minutes later)  
Keep every square centimetre of skin submerged for 46 min. The goal is to osmotically swap out 7 % of your cellular water for brine. You will emerge puckered, half-pickled, but no longer a pure water-creature. The city now classifies you as “part vessel,” which is less appetising than “full skin-sack of thirst.”

2. Sound Budget – Calculate Daily
--------------------------------------------------------
Humans leak sound the way cracked radiators leak coolant. In the Ward, amplitude is coinage and predator-attractant.  
Baseline leakage:  
– Heartbeat: 0.9 µPa / s  
– Footfall: 3–7 µPa depending on boot  
– Speech (normal): 20–35 µPa / syllable  
– Thought-voice: 0.2 µPa / s (yes, the city hears it)  
Carry no more than 400 µPa/hour or echo-leopards mark you.  
Practice “white-whisper” breathing: inhale on a brown-noise hiss, exhale through teeth imitating the exact city-roar sample linked here: █████████ (download exceeds universal bandwidth; instead cup hands around your ears while near an air-conditioning vent, memorise that frequency, reproduce it). Lip-sync to everything you want to say; let the Ward speak for you.

3. Equipment You Can Actually Pack
--------------------------------------------------------
A. Waterless Canteen  
A 0.3 l titanium flask, interior mirror-polished, double-wall vacuum. Do NOT fill. The void inside equals one “thumbnail sphere” of Ward currency. Keep it sealed; it is your emergency credit card.  
B. Collapsible felt slippers (nomex + copper thread). Change into them the instant you arrive; rubber soles thump and thump = death.  
C. Three packets of desiccated wasabi peas. Not food—currency. Copper-leaf citizens will trade three strides of folded distance for one pea; the peas’ residual moisture is 0.04 %, negligible compared with your now-briny body.  
D. Earplugs made of frozen silence. Manufacture: record 90 s of true rural night (no crickets, no wind), digital-zero the signal, save as .wav, have it 3-D printed into hollow polymer cast at –18 °C. Keep on dry ice; every minute above freezing = one lost second of hush when you need it.  
E. Folding prism goggles (polycarbonate, 42°). When the “clear-ring” comes, the goggles split the cancelled wavefront so you witness the true map without letting it erase memory. Remove them the instant hush ends or amnesia is permanent.  
F. One ordinary plastic plant-mister, completely empty. It functions as a prop; citizens see it and assume you are a travelling hydrator, status exempt from casual predation because leopards dislike sudden employment.

--------------------------------------------------------
4. Vaccinations & Prophylactics
--------------------------------------------------------
Physical: none.  
Metaphysical: you require a memetic inoculation. One week prior, recite daily:  
“I do not contain what you are missing.”  
Say it aloud while staring at dripping taps, licking sliced cucumber, watching ice cubes melt—train the phrase to inhabit muscle memory so it utters itself inside the Ward when you can no longer think. The sentence convinns buildings you are already empty; they stop trying to milk you.  
Drug note: Anti-thirst diuretics (furosemide etc.) backfire; the city registers artificially dry tongues as hidden wells and pursues harder.

--------------------------------------------------------
5. Cognitive Calisthenics
--------------------------------------------------------
Practice “fold-space” daily in your bedroom:  
1. Put a chair at the far wall.  
2. Stare until the chair appears postage-stamp size.  
3. Sprint; arrive before the image re-inflates.  
Aim for four-second crossing of a ten-metre room. Inside the Ward this skill keeps plazas pocket-sized. Fail and you will still die of thirst, but it will be quick instead of protracted.

--------------------------------------------------------
6. Letters Home
--------------------------------------------------------
You cannot send them. Still, write longhand anyway; every paragraph reduces internal sound by 0.05 µPa (the page absorbs vibration). Burn the pages each sunrise; ash is quieter than thought.

7. Arrival Checklist (laminate, keep on wrist)
--------------------------------------------------------
□ Mouth closed, white-whisper breathing engaged  
□ Slippers on, laces of boots tucked inside so they do not clop when strapped to belt  
□ Earplugs in pocket NOT in ears yet (they melt, waste precious cold)  
□ Mirror-canteen sealed, vacuum intact  
□ Pea-currency zipped quiet-fabric pouch  
□ Prism goggles folded, temperature stable  
□ Plant-mister visibly clipped to belt  
□ Name never spoken aloud—replace with gesture (touch left clavicle = “I”)  
□ Fold-distance intent set: picture city no larger than table-top  
□ Final internal question answered: “If I never see rain again, will I still be me?”

8. What the First Hour Feels Like (so you recognise normal)
--------------------------------------------------------
– Sound is dental; your fillings buzz.  
– Breath tastes of 9-volt batteries.  
– Bronze shadows lean against you like polite drunks.  
– Distance smells of cinnamon, not looks; closer objects smell stronger.  
– Buildings feel your pulse, adjust windows like pupils.  
If any of the above is absent, you have walked into a leopard-trap loop; stay still, crack one wasabi pea, scatter copper dust, backtrack along scent-strength.

--------------------------------------------------------
9. Diplomacy with Citizens
--------------------------------------------------------
Greeting: extend empty plant-mister, shake once (no slosh).  
They may offer you a clay sphere. Politely tap it with your canteen: the hollowness recognises hollowness. Hand it back. If they insist, accept, but store inside mirrored flask—city vacuum against your vacuum, double jeopardy confuses ledgers.  
Never ask for directions—speech costs you 20 µPa, buys them a day of wall-suppleness. Instead, mime thirst, then shrug; they will fold the city smaller so you leave faster.

10. Exit Strategies (ranked by survival & hydration retention)
--------------------------------------------------------
A. Silence Debt (safest, 80 % success)  
1. Locate the driest building you can (fibres white, leaves dull).  
2. Sit against it.  
3. White-whisper until heartbeat merges with city drone (takes 2–6 hrs).  
4. When you no longer know which meat-inside is yours, the wall extrudes you into rain-humid night of your origin. You wake thirsty but alive, throat merely sore, not dust.  
B. Echo-Leopard Bargain (50 %, high cost)  
1. Let the growl circle you thrice.  
2. Offer wrist, draw 5 ml blood with wasabi-pea knife (copper edge).  
3. Present blood in plant-mister.  
4. Leopard escorts you to hinge; passage costs 65 % of your body water. You emerge prune-wrinkled but can relearn to drink.  
C. Clear-Ring Sprint (20 %, but you keep every drop)  
Requires prism goggles. When the 90-second hush hits:  
1. Don goggles, memorise exit vector to hinge (it will be exactly under the brightest salt shard).  
2. Sprint in straight line; distance is true during hush, no folding.  
3. Leap through hinge before roar returns. Warning: if your foot is still inside when sound reboots, the leg ossifies into red ceramic; amputate immediately on exit or thirst travels upward.

--------------------------------------------------------
11. Aftercare (if you made it out)
--------------------------------------------------------
Re-hydration must be noisy. Drink carbonated mineral water, let it fizz mouth-loud; human ears need to reclaim sonic space.  
Expect one week of auditory hallucination: every refrigerator motor will pronounce your Ward-name. Ignore it; names fade once you sweat again.  
Tell no one. Speech re-opens the hinge for listeners; they may step through chasing your story and you will be responsible.

--------------------------------------------------------
12. Final Note, Final Warning
--------------------------------------------------------
The Hollow Ward is not evil; it is thirsty and has perfected etiquette around that thirst.  
Pack discipline, not weaponry.  
Pack emptiness, not water.  
Pack silence, not music.  
If you remember nothing else, remember this:  
Travel light, because everything noisy is already a resident—  
and the city  
is always  
listening.
