Field Briefing: Excursion to the Hollow Beneath the Rhododendron  
(For the first-time, non-augmented, carbon-based traveler)

--------------------------------------------------
1. Meta-Geography & Legal Status
--------------------------------------------------
- The Hollow is not a cave; it is a hyper-dense fold in the respiration budget of the planet.  
- It is unclaimed by any human polity, but the Cartilage Conduit (the living handrails) hold de-facto squatters’ rights.  
- No visa is required—only a willingness to be edited.

--------------------------------------------------
2. Portal Dynamics
--------------------------------------------------
- **Entry vector**: A single fox-run tunnel of rhododendron growing backwards out of a dripping cliff.  
- **Access window**: 23 s after you hear the first water drop “chime” on stone. Any earlier and the tunnel is still forming; any later and it has overgrown to your exact silhouette minus one finger.  
- **Crawl protocol**: Backwards on elbows, chin tucked, palms turned outward. The stems will thorn-check your pockets—carry nothing that can rattle or jangle; the dark drinks sound.

--------------------------------------------------
3. Kit (Approved for Normie Anatomy)
--------------------------------------------------
A. Thermal  
- Base layer: silk impregnated with carbon-black; it will absorb the infrared the grubs leak and keep you 1–2 °C warmer.  
- Outer layer: paper-thin aerogel cloak painted with matte iron-oxide. The cloak’s job is not insulation but camouflage—your heat signature must be read as “errant stone” by the cuffs.  

B. Nutrition  
- 30 ml of 40 % glucose in a soft bladder taped to your sternum. The cartilage rails will taste it, judge you “sweet enough to spare,” and relax their grip.  
- No protein. Protein triggers the liverwort balconies to flower in fast-forward, raining pollen that sets the night-mice into a crystal-gathering frenzy—tiny paws all over you.  

C. Hydration  
- The Hollow’s water is potable but memory-hungry. Every sip will siphon one minute of autobiography. Bring 250 ml of triple-filtered water from home instead; label the bottle with your mother’s maiden name so the Hollow can read but not claim.  

D. Light  
- None. Any torch wavelength will blind the fauna and void the “charter colder than law.” A red chem-stick triple-wrapped in foil is acceptable only for medical amputation.  

E. Documentation  
- Tattoo the grid reference on your inner lip; GPS dies the instant rhododendron snaps shut.  
- No cameras, but a sheet of freeze-dried algae paper can be slipped under the tongue; it records barometric changes and can be read later as a sonograph.  

F. Exit Toll  
- One fingernail, one memory of red (pick a minor heartbreak), the memory of absolute silence. Pre-select these; the tunnel will ask while you are still head-down and panicking.  

--------------------------------------------------
4. Physiological Expectations
--------------------------------------------------
- **Darkness Index**: 9.6 Keller (absolute) + velvet wet. After 7 min human proprioception flips inside-out; you will feel your heartbeat as a shape outside the ribs.  
- **Thermal gradient**: 2 °C still air, but stone conducts so fast it feels like −5 °C on skin contact. Wear thin gloves you can peel off with teeth; the cuffs want bare skin.  
- **Respiration cycle**: 11 s inhale / 9 s exhale shared by the entire cavity. Hold your breath during the inhale or the city will graft your CO₂ into its sentence.  
- **Time dilation**: Approximately 1 min inside = 43 s outside. DO NOT reset your watch; it is how the Hollow keeps a ledger.  

--------------------------------------------------
5. Fauna Interaction Script
--------------------------------------------------
- **Glowless grubs**: Let them crawl. Their infrared bellies map your capillaries; if you sweep them off they reroute the map and the cuffs will mis-classify you.  
- **Millipede chains**: Read-only. Attempting to finish the sentence they spell has produced three known cases of semantic narcosis—travelers stood frozen until the next exhalation, then toppled, moss-sealed.  
- **Night-mice (salt-needle fur)**: Offer no resistance. The needles are harvesting body heat, not blood. Once 40 needles engage you qualify as “architecture” and the grafting begins.  
- **Opilionids**: Stilt-high harvesters. They fear the taste of vinegar; if one approaches, exhale through a sleeve that was pre-wiped with balsamic—travelers have reported a polite 45 ° detour.  
- **Vegetable cartilage cuffs**: Do not pull. Speak your pulse aloud—one number per heartbeat. After 30 beats they usually release; if you fake the count they tighten.  

--------------------------------------------------
6. Psychological Contingencies
--------------------------------------------------
- **Mirrored echolocation**: The darkness reflects thought. If you catch yourself finishing someone else’s internal monologue, hum a prime-number count to break resonance.  
- **Red-memory bleed**: You will forget the shade of red you traded. Carry a physical red object (bowline knot in paracord) to re-anchor color vision on exit.  
- **Architectural vertigo**: Everything sways 4 cm every exhalation. If you feel motion sick, lick a mucilage rail; the sugars include a mild vestibular suppressor.  

--------------------------------------------------
7. Emergency Procedures
--------------------------------------------------
- **Grafting initiation**: Cuffs stiffen, skin blanches under them.  
  1. Bite glucose bladder, smear syrup on stone seam between you and nearest willow trunk.  
  2. City reroutes warmth to seal the seam first—gives you 12 s to slice cuffs with ceramic micro-blade (titanium turns brittle at 2 °C; carry ceramic).  
- **Collapsing exhalation**: If you feel suction instead of breeze, the Hollow is about to inhale a full human volume.  
  1. Dive into nearest empty willow cylinder; the pith windows act as non-return valves.  
  2. Hold breath 11 s; internal pressure equalizes. Do NOT exhale early—you will pop like a bellows.  
- **Lost exit tunnel**:  
  1. Find a phosphorescent millipede chain ending mid-air—this marks newest growth.  
  2. Crawl backwards toward the freshest broken gypsum crystals; they point to the surface like compass needles of minutes.  

--------------------------------------------------
8. Debrief & Aftercare
--------------------------------------------------
- On emergence you will see rain falling upward. This is normal.  
- Finger regrows in 4–5 months; memory of red does not.  
- Expect lucid dreams of crawling sentences for three nights.  
- Place your algae sonograph in a jar of distilled water; within 6 h it unfolds into a topographic poem—file with nearest Multiverse Parks Service so future travelers know how much of you stayed behind.  

--------------------------------------------------
Final Note
--------------------------------------------------
The Hollow is not evil; it is a rough draft. Every visit is a copy-edit. Travel light, cede the requested parts, and the city will add you—properly punctuated—to its unlit encyclopedia.
