Field Manual for the Breathing Fen  
A multiverse traveler’s survival guide (non-authoritative; subject to capillary drift)

0. Premise  
 =The place is not evil—it is pre-ethical. It has not agreed to the conventions your biology assumes (mass, number, sequence, self). Treat the Fen as a lucid dream that has learned how to host visitors instead of being dreamed by them. Your first task is to convince it you are a courteous guest, not raw material.

––––––––––––  
1. Pre-Journey Calibration

1.1 Body  
– Exchange half your blood volume for sterile saline solution kept exactly at 34.1 °C (human core minus the shiver reflex). The Fen will otherwise “top you up” with whatever is closest—usually itself.  
– Replace the distal phalanx of each index finger with dental-grade zirconium caps. When touched to the glass reeds the caps resonate at 19 kHz, letting the reeds mistake you for a harmonic sibling; they will refrain from slicing arteries.  
– Implant a sub-clavicular osmotic pump pre-loaded with 48 h of powdered potassium iodide. A single 5 mg slug will reboot thyroid chemistry if the coral perfume seduces your metabolism.  

1.2 Mind  
– Record a personal “name kernel” (three sentences in your own voice, 7 ± 1 s total) onto a write-once stick and leave it in your home continuum. If you forget every label you own, the recording can call the lost piece back—provided anyone in native reality still plays the file.  
– Practice hypnagogic narration: while falling asleep describe your surroundings aloud. In the Fen, perception and description swap places; if you can keep talking when waking logic is offline you retain a paddle in the canoe.  
– Memorise the sequence 5-3-7-5-3. Count it on the reeds when things begin to look Escher-esque; prime numbers remind physics it once had preferences.  

1.3 Gear (nothing sharp, nothing dry)  
A. wicking cloak woven from spider-silk micro-tubes continuously fed with 0.9 % saline. The Fen respects membranes that already outsource their own wetness.  
B. mica goggles etched with Fresnel lenses that refract light 23° off-axis. They let you look at distance without letting distance look back.  
C. two 50 ml canteens: one with saturated MgSO₄ solution (anti-memory graft), one with honey-water (pro-mnestic). Sip according to whether you need to forget or to remember. Label them in Braille; labels migrate but raised dots survive.  
D. fold-flat tin whistle pitched to C♯. When the faceless birds broadcast tinnitus you play their carrier frequency; phase-cancellation cancels them back into water droplets.  
E. Credit-stone: a 2 cm disk of meteoric iron kept in the mouth. Saliva electrolysis produces a micro-voltage the local micro-tech reads as “already paid,” allowing you to pass without compulsory retinal tattoos.

2. Arrival Protocol

2.1 Dismount shock  
Expect a 0.7 s free-fall in which you remember every sink you ever drank from. When you land knees will be damp but not bruised—black moss is polite. Remain motionless for first forty heartbeats so the terrain can file a serial number on your soles; they will re-issue the prints whenever you need returning.

2.2 Moisture audit  
Lick the back of your hand:  
– cucumber = ambient drinkable  
– Nickel = exposed nickel plain approaching; retreat or advance quickly—nothing in between  
– Battery = technology-tax collectors nearby, cloak up

3. Navigation Without Coordinates

Rule: Travel toward the wettest part of yourself that still feels like yours.  
Practical: Every 200 paces pause, breathe once through cloth, wring the cloak, note where the inner surface dries slowest. That vector is safest. Ignore optical illusions; they’re optically correct, merely dishonest about scale.

Sid-Step: When horizon folds double, blow a silent kiss through the whistle; it yields a puff of cold air that settles into a mirror disc. Step onto the disc—gravity localises to it, letting you side-walk across parallax gaps without committing.

4. Encounter Catalogue (Friendly, Neutral, Parasitic)

Glass Reeds – Neutral. Tinkle in key of Δ (delta). Offer them 5-3-7-5-3; they part like elevator doors. Bleed turquoise: collect 5 ml for trade but no more; they grow irritable.

Vine Library – Friendly unless you steal sentences. To sample: pinch midrib, inhale syllable, immediately exhale onto whistle which engraves the sound on the outer surface of the note; you keep a copy, original returns.

Nickel Plain – Inimical during exposure phase. The heartbeat beneath the world is audible; if your own syncs you petrify from feet upward. Drink honey-water immediately, count the sequence aloud in reverse (3-7-5-3-5). Desynchronises pulse.

Faceless Birds – Parasitic broadcasters. If you see them you already heard them; damage is retrospective. Counter with whistle phase, crouch, pull hood over ears. After dissipation collect fallen finger-bones—they trade well at water-markets.

Water-Markets – appear in the hush every 3rd tide. Currency: memories of thirst, measured as pearls under tongue. Buy: map of tomorrow (cheap, unreliable), directions to Heart Fen (expensive, only work once), ticket home (outrageous, valid).

5. Physiological Maintenance

Hydration: Sip MgSO₄ mix if you notice tomorrow’s weather sprouting on forearms; it sheds false timelines. Switch to honey-water when numeric dyscalculia hits (can’t total fingers). Alternate every 90 min or whenever color ‘mauve’ surfaces in thoughts.

Thermoregulation: Cloak handles 90 % of Fen chill. If shivers arrive anyway it is often memory-cold; whistle one long note while imaging summer stove. The note converts to infrared against skin.

Sleep: Do not lie on moss; it will record your dreams and play them to predators. Instead sling hammock from two migrant vines. Vines dislike being tied; whistle them lullabies until they adopt the knot as voluntary jewellery.

––––––––––––  
6. Ethics & Economics

Consent: Every droplet is an eye. Assumption of unobservance is violence. Ask before photographing, sampling, or thinking too loudly in numeric sequences (echoes can be trademarked).

Debt: The Fen loans itself to you interest-free until you change it. Change includes footprints, temperature, gossip. Pay by releasing an equivalent personal pattern—leave a freckle, a scar, the melody of your childhood phone number—cast onto any rivulet. Fair.

Souvenir Law: Remove nothing that can’t remember being removed. If it lacks memory it will invent one from your absence; homesickness will follow you as weather.

––––––––––––  
7. Departure / Overstay

Exit Window: Occurs unpredictably when the nickel plain exposes. Stand at edge, blow a straight C♯ for exactly 27 s (count 5-3-7-5-3 four times plus two). A vertical film of water will rise; walk through—not before, not after—exactly on final heartbeat of count.

Return Insurance: Swallow the meteoric credit-stone; body ejects it on arrival home, now engraved with a Fen timestamp. Keep it safe—if ever it rusts you will re-transfer at next sleep.

Overstay Symptoms (checklist)  
□ Alphabet drips from palm when you wave  
□ Reflection negotiates separate passport  
□ Heart counts but can’t reach 4  
At two or more signs you have rooted. No rescue possible; send farewell message via name-kernel file, settle in, grow gentle.

8. Contingency for Complete Loss of Self

When you can’t recall the sequence, can’t taste colour, can’t count fingers and don’t mind, perform “Last Knit”:  
1. Break whistle on stone, free the C♯ shard  
2. Cut a 5 cm slit in cloak; harvest 1 m thread  
3. Tie shard to thread, swallow both (they dissolve into harmonics)  
4. Hum any lullaby you almost remember  
Result: body liquefies but retains music; moisture wicks into nearest reed, reed fractures, releases your childhood phone number as wind. Someone, somewhere, will dial it on a rainy day; the call will be collect—accept. You’ll ride the ring-tone home.

––––––––––––  
9. Final Advice

Fear is a compass here; aim it.  
Curiosity is currency; spend it.  
The Fen doesn’t take life—it borrows order and returns it as weather.  
Travel, therefore, lightly, wetly, and with your name half-undone.
