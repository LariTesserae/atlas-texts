Field Briefing: Habitat-Manufacturing Arboreal Settlement  
Codenamed “Cambium Town”  
Clearance level: Ordinary-human, unescorted, one-day to one-week incursion

––––––––––––––––––––  
1. Pre-Travel Medical & Biological Calibration  
 (Do these at home; pharmacies inside the settlement only stock chlorophyll.)

1.1 Respiratory tuning  
   – Spend three nights sleeping in a steamed bathroom (kettle on, door cracked).  
   – Practice “leaf breathing”: 4-count inhale through nose / 7-count exhale through pursed lips.  
   – Carry 0.5 L sterile saline for throat-rinse; condensation here is faintly chloroformic and numbs cilia if allowed to accumulate.

1.2 Skin & mycobiome  
   – One week before arrival, stop using terbinafine, clotrimazole, any systemic antifungals. You want your cutaneous fungi friendly; the town’s mycorrhizae inspect newcomers for compatibility and a hostile dermal fauna will mark you as “termite-adjacent.”  
   – Moisturise with plain sesame oil; it becomes currency if you need to tip a bark-doorman.

1.3 Dream insurance  
   – The settlement barters in dreams. Record the last three nights in a notebook; leave the notebook in your pocket. If the fruit accidentally touches you, the recorded dreams are debited first, sparing the live ones.

2. Kit List (nothing metal that can’t be wrapped in damp cloth)

Compulsory  
□ One cellulose-grade face mask (coffee-filter paper works).  
□ Eye-drop ampoules: 2 ml each, 0.9 % NaCl + 0.01 % MgSO₄ – counters aerosol iron.  
□ 1 m cotton ribbon, pale green; to leash yourself to a companion if fog thickens—nylon irritates audition-vines.  
□ Collapsible walking staff: carbon-fibre, ends sheathed in cork. Never plant bare metal; it triggers the xylem pressure valves and you’ll be towed into a wall of heart-wood.  
□ Zip-sealable pouch for electronics; include ten grains of rice as desiccant—town humidity can liquefy a phone in twelve hours.

Optional courtesy items  
□ 50 ml vial of sugar solution (15 %); accepted micro-tithe for crossing private root-zones.  
□ Harmonica or jaw-harp—metal reeds OK if wiped with sesame oil; residents enjoy controlled vibration, helps them set their annual growth cadence.

3. Approach Vector

3.1 Final paved spot  
   GPS drops you at “Cob End.” The last cobblestone dips like a broken tooth; step over it, never on it—it is the settlement’s doorbell. Pause three beats; this is the polite interval for the cambium layer to note your mass.

3.2 Gradient etiquette  
   After step-off, descend. Uphill voices carry fungal spores the town already rejected; uphill walkers are interpreted as “rewind” and may find the path lengthening.

4. Inside: The Four Rules

Rule 1 – Slow Syntax  
   Speak at ≤60 dB. Every decibel above that seeds an audition-vine; it will quote you nightly for a year. Whispered jokes are acceptable; sarcasm is not—the vines can’t parse it and grow knotty.

Rule 2 – Photosynthetic Yield  
   Do not shade a photosynthesizing rail for longer than eight heartbeats. If you must stop, kneel; canopy light is currency here, and blocking it is treated as petty theft.

Rule 3 – Carbon Tithing  
   Exhale toward civic shrubs if asked: a deep breath, face within 30 cm. They record your CO₂ signature as a voting credit; refusal is not illegal, but triggers polite ostracism (nobody will sell you bark-bread).

Rule 4 – Touch-Pact  
   Touch only surfaces that touch you first. A leaning wall, a friendly root: fair game. Anything you initiate contact with considers you “graft-able” and starts binding; count thirty seconds and release.

5. Shelter & Sustenance

Lodging  
   Choose a factory with a chimney that “breathes” every 9–10 seconds. Inside:  
   – Pile shredded root on the floor-grate, curl like a cat.  
   – Hang your ribbon from the lintel; spores will avoid it, giving you cleaner dreams.  
   – Keep electronics inside the rice-pouch; place pouch on furnace ledge (the warmest dry spot).

Drinking  
   Only drink from the transparent conduits labelled “slow.” Fast conduits carry metabolic acids that will give you chlorophyll diarrhea—harmless but colourful.

Eating  
   Edible foods: leached cambium jerky (sold by weight in slices), bracket-fungus crackers (smell faintly of cedar), algal loaves (chewy, iodine-rich).  
   Do NOT eat the glowing fruit of the tram-lines; it is 12 V bio-electric. One bite = 4 h dream-loop of every embarrassment since kindergarten.

6. Currency & Trade

Medium of exchange: sucrose-standard.  
Exchange rate (approximate, 2024 Q2)  
   1 g sucrose = 3 cambium-jerky slices = 10 minutes of machined shade = 2 thumb-lengths of cotton ribbon.

Carry 30 g household sugar in a twist of wax-paper; more makes you a target for root-burglars.  
If you run out, hum one octave above middle-C for three minutes—workers will pay you in sap just to stop.

7. Social Mistakes & Penalties

Mistake: Picking any “Edison-bulb” fruit.  
Penalty: dream lien (see §1.3). Remedy: donate recorded notebook dreams to the central stump within one circadian cycle; they burn it for caloric credit and cancel debt.

Mistake: Speaking loudly at the skyline towers.  
Penalty: internode growth pins you inside a green echo lasting 1–3 hrs. Remedy: offer magnesium (an Epsom salt crystal suffices). They’ll let you go; magnesium is their preferred laxative and they’re always out.

––––––––––––––––––––  
8. Departure

You will feel a tug on the wrist—one filament of fog. That is the town’s courtesy escort. Do NOT pull back; it lengthens the exit path. Walk perpendicular to the tug until cobblestones reappear dry and straight. Do not look over your shoulder until your shoes click stone; looking back replays the entire settlement one ring larger, delaying re-emergence by ~6 months.

On exit, take one breath of outside air, then wipe your face with the remaining saline. Cambium spores hate atmospheric ozone and will abandon ship, sparing your pores an awkward sprouting next week.

––––––––––––––––––––  
9. Post-Travel Debrief

9.1 Laundry  
   Wash clothes twice, cold water, no fabric softener (it rehydrates stubborn chlorophyll that stains).

9.2 Med-check  
   If urine smells faintly of fresh-mowed lawn after 48 h, you’ve acquired endosymbiotic algae—benign, but inform your physician if you start photosynthesising at desk jobs.

9.3 Dream audit  
   Should nightmares feature green metronomes, play a recording of city traffic for 15 minutes nightly; it convinces the residual audition-vines they’ve returned to an environment where they cannot bloom, and they desist.

Carry the carbon-dioxide of curiosity, but exhale it gently. The town will exhale back—cool, iron-scented, already forgetting you as it adds another ring, subtracts another whistle, and keeps perfect, photosynthetic books.
