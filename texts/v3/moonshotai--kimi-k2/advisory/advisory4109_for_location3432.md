1.  Pre-embarkment: 30 days out
--------------------------------------------------------------------

A.  Climate-coat your lungs  
•   Three weeks of daily 45 °C dry-sauna sessions, 15 % humidity.  
•   Finish every session with a 200 ml loss of warm saline; you must learn to sip, not cough, when the air tastes of solder.  
•   Optional hack: breathe through a pocket of copper mesh at home—oxides precondition alveoli for the metallic aerosol downtown.

B.  Foot-conditioning  
•   Walk barefoot on steel grating until you can cross 20 m at 55 °C without flinch.  
•   Grow callus or buy it—subdermal graphene pads are black-market but legal at Hatchery customs if declared as “orthopedic insoles.”

C.  Auditory compression therapy  
•   Stream choked-furnace white-noise 8 h nightly, volume rising 1 dB every two days until peaking at 86 dB.  
•   Overlay random 800 Hz hammer strikes.  
Goal: teach your amygdala that a cathedral-sized clang is routine, not a threat; nausea dropped 42 % in test travelers.

3.  Immersion protocol – first 72 h
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Hour 0 – Arrival, Funicular Gate  
•   Lick your forefinger; hold it stationary. Direction of fastest dry-spot = airflow “uphill”—remember, down often feels colder here.  
•   Present wrist-chip; guards scan for guild-debt. If negative, bribe with tomato seeds (1 packet clears 30 drip-seconds of unpaid air tax).

Hour 1–6 – Catwalk imprinting  
•   Walk only on lattice marked with two parallel weld scars—those are fatigue-tested after the 4.3 collapse.  
•   When two locals approach, pause sideways, left foot on main strut, right toes on chain; this is their silent yield language. Mirror it.

Hour 6–24 – Thermal acclimation  
•   You will feel condensation burns—white patches that sting. Slap, do not rub; smearing drives 80 °C water deeper into pores.  
•   Locate communal towel peg; hang your damp cloth there—it’s first-come, but cloth left >45 min is taxed; use tomato seeds to extend by 15 min increments.

Hour 24 – Thirst arithmetic test  
•   Children will recite drip code; echo it back correctly or receive lowest water ration. Cheat sheet on passport helps.

Day 2 – Height-sickness  
•   Inversions may leave you two ledges above where you began. When vertigo hits, hum B-flat through harmonica; sound contracts false horizon cues by 18 % (measured).

Day 3 – Copper lightning (if calendar aligns)  
•   Holiday flash is ~1.8 joules/metre rail. Cover eyes with mirror; UV spike can snow-blind. DO NOT cheer loudly—quiet contract still applies; applause volume ≤38 dB.

Auditory hallucination  
After day 5 hammer-echo can lag 3 s behind visual spark. Check reality: if pulse matches echo rate, plug putty and descend one level; anomaly resolves 92 % of time.

Thermal rash cycle  
Heat then condensation: pores open, copper ions infiltrate, evaporative stage deposits metallic film under stratum corneum. You will itch at night. Scrub with 5 % citric acid on rag; exchange rag before checkpoint or carry tax in seeds.
