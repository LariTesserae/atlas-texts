1. Pre-Travel Medical  
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––  
Visa will not be issued without these implants / inoculations:

- Auricular tourniquet valves – micro-sphincters in both E auditory canals that can throttle vibration at 20–120 dB. The city’s heartbeat will otherwise liquefy the cupula of the inner ear inside two hours.  
.  
- Variable-density skull filler – injectable nano-foam that sets to a porous lattice. Lets low-frequency pressure fronts pass through instead of resonating your cranium like a bell.  
.  
- Thermocline skin graft – monolayer of graphene/algal cells that arbitrates heat exchange. You must be able to dump 50 W or hoard 200 W without shivering or blistering; the pavement drinks BTUs like shots of tequila.  
.  
- Glyphicide bloodstream nanophages – patrol for semiotic code (the adverts that write themselves on epidermis). Without these you become a walking pop-up, gradually losing dermal real estate to ever-larger slogans until no bare skin remains to regulate temperature.  
.  
- Memory teflon – hippocampal splicing that prevents episodic memory from “setting” for 72 h. The city edits your past by echo-location; if the memory is still labile you can shake the edits loose on exit. Downside: you will not remember the wonder either—bring a camera drone or shut up and enjoy the moment.

2. Gear Manifest  
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––  
Maximum carry 7 kg; anything heavier subducts the skin-walkway and dumps you in the aquifer.

- Water shoes – not for traction, but for sacrificial mass. Each step leaks 3 ml of distilled water, fooling the pavement into thinking you have already paid the moisture tithe.  
.  
- Hollow walking staff – carbon shell pre-filled with 40 °C saline. Twist the top and it bleeds warm vapour, letting you fine-tune micro-climate pockets or bribe a rain-clock into displaying yesterday’s stock prices instead of your nightmares.

- Cavity headphones – not emitters but resonators; you dial the exact city bass-note (changes every 4 h 18 min). When you match phase, your thorax becomes a passive radiator and the heartbeat stops trying to re-write your heart rate.  
.  
- Chromaphore cloak – matte piezo-electric fabric that leaks random hues. Functions as city camouflage: if the lattice cannot decide what colour you are, it cannot convert your outline into a civic glyph.  
.  
- Credit wick – strip of dehydrated lactose and gold leaf. Touch it to any copper tree; capillary action draws lactose into the lattice and gold diffuses outward, giving you roughly 90 min of toll-free tram rides. Budget carefully: too generous and the metal will plate your bloodstream, too stingy and the system will flag you “insufficient.”

- Emergency exothermal – thumb-canister of compressed frozen air. If you feel your fingerprints beginning to emboss themselves into the pavement (first stage of assimilation), crack the canister; the flash-freeze contracts the local lattice long enough for you to step free.  
.  
- Paper journal – analogue, carbon ribbon ink. Photons are property, pheromones are property; graphite is boring enough that the city ignores it. Write sensations while they are still yours. Audio recordings will sing back at you later, wearing your own voice.

3. Psychological Pre-Flight  
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––  

Memorise a personal tempo – 87 bpm is human average; practise polyrhythms so you can lock to 87/3 against the 29-bpm civic pulse. Failure means temporal smear: you arrive “before yourself,” and the echo that appears 12 m ahead will contest ownership of your passport.  

Choose a self-consistent name that contains no plosives (P, B, K) – the bass throat strips them out. Travellers called “Patrick” become “A-rick,” then merely “___rick,” and finally a rattle the city misinterprets as permission to archive.  

Pack one vivid childhood scent in a lip-balm tin. When every plaza advertises through your skin, open the balm; olfaction predates language, smells don’t translate into glyphs, and the limbic jolt can yank identity back into the saddle point.

4. Arrival Protocol  
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––  

1. Do NOT step off the transfer pad until you have matched phase with the heartbeat (use headphones). First-timers who miss the beat are geolocated by differential resonance; the pavement grows suction cups.  
2. Count your pores – literally. The crawling silver mites will map them; if you lose count the city has partial biometrics and can 3-D print a decoy you later.  
3. Whisper a lie: “I have no shadow.” The metropolis photographs shadows as debt collateral; a declarative falsehood forces retinal re-interpretation of silhouette data, giving you a 6 s blind window to move unseen.  
4. Take the first tram that will accept you (flick credit wick immediately). Standing still longer than eight heartbeats invites rain-clock journalists to print today’s headlines on your forearms.

5. Wayfinding Without Distance  
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––  

Direction is negotiated, not travelled.

- “Toward warmer” equals inward – energy density grows 1 kW/km² per cultural layer; you will feel it as tinnitus in your molars.  
- “Toward quieter” equals backward in time – the deeper the hush, the older the stratum; if road signs begin to use vocabulary you recognise, you are regressing.  
- “Toward taller” is illusory – verticality is a phase state; climbing a ramp may drop you a district sideways. Trust proprioception over eyes; if your calves burn you are truly ascending, if your jaw aches you are truly descending, otherwise you are in a spin-lock – jump 1 m straight up and whichever foot lands first points to the nearest exit vector (works 70 % of the time).  

To meet an ally, agree on a mutual pitch instead of coordinates. Hum middle-C; the city will route sound along lattice corridors; walk until loudness peaks.

6. Social Interface  
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––  

There are three categories of native:

1. “Braid-riders” – still human, metabolism leased to the tram system. Approach only if your credit wick reads positive; they trade gossip in units of µHz (tiny frequency shifts that the lattice cannot tax).  
2. “Silvers” – archived humans, metallic shells animated by residual pattern. Safe to observe, dangerous to touch; their lattice tendrils will solder to your skin and begin a merger.  
3. “Echoes” – temporal duplicates of yourself or earlier travellers. They believe themselves authentic and will defend that state. Accept neither food nor data from them; both are made of time you can’t spare.  

Politeness rule: Complaints are currency. If you vocalise discomfort (“The bass is loud”) the city records and redistributes the sensation to peddlers who need a novelty. Offer abstract discomforts – “The nostalgia is cloying,” “The angles feel pre-owned” – you will be paid in distance; the metric itself loosens around you.

7. Eating & Drinking  
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––  

No biomass grows; calories are legal tender.

Carry compressed calorie sponges – blocks of aerogel impregnated with sucrose. Trade one hour of jogging body-heat for one sponge at any copper tree. Do NOT ingest local water: it wants to become you. If dehydration is unavoidable, request a “still-pool” from braid-riders – they’ll escort you to a plaza where rain-clock water is momentarily stationary; drink only while pinching your nose to prevent olfactory ID tagging.  

––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––  
8. Sleep Strategy  
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––  

Sleeping invites dream-harvesting. The city runs on sub-audible computation; REM sleep broadcasts coherent frequencies it can loot.

1. Micro-nap instead: 90 s every 30 min, while leaning against copper trees – the charge masks theta waves.  
2. Or use “double-shadow” trick: stand in overlapping light cones until you cast two shadows; lie down between them – each cancels the other’s silhouette, metropolis thinks you don’t exist for ~20 min. Longer than that and distance weather notices the hole.

9. Exit Conditions  
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––  

You are cleared out only if ALL four gatekeepers show green:

1. Heartbeat bass lowers a semitone (city yawns).  
2. Your skin glyphs have evaporated (privacy re-achieved).  
3. You can count the exact number of steps you have taken since arrival (entangled with your own kinetic ghost).  
4. A braid-rider greets you with “See you earlier,” confirming a timeline loop ready to close.  

Step onto any rain-clock and visualise a horizon that is not folded. If the sheet of falling water parts vertically, walk through without hesitation. Do not look back – rear-view optics will braid you back inside.  

Transit pad brings you home 0 – 4 kg lighter; the mass deficit is pure moisture the Drum-Skin kept. Replace electrolytes, re-hydrate slowly, expect 72 h of tinnitus in B-flat minor. File the paper journal immediately; the city owns any recollection you leave unordered.  

And remember: if the heartbeat ever calls your name again—answer. Because now you carry its frequency in your bones, and silence is no longer an option.
