Field Manual: Tourist’s Preparation for the Hollow Crust  
                                                                 （Edition for baseline-human, oxygen-breathing, liquid-bodied, 37 °C corporeal ambulators）

------------------------------------------------
0.  Know what you are walking into  
The Hollow Crust is not a cave, temple, mine, or ecosystem.  
It is a planetary womb that miscarries the same litter forever.  
Its business is rehearsal, not birth.  
It keeps the shape of things it never quite lets live.  
If that sentence makes you want to go anyway, read on.  
If you only felt fear, stay home; fear is the only instinct the Crust has not learned to counterfeit.

------------------------------------------------
1.  Route & Arrival – the fall that begins outside the map  
• There are no approach vectors.  
  – Roads stop 47 km downslope; vegetation ends 12 km after that.  
  – Helicopter rotors stall in the hot up-draft. Drones liquefy.  
  – The only legal way-mark is a stone cairn painted International Orange; everything above it is “missing person” jurisdiction.  
• Accept that you will abandon every vehicle. The last 3 000 m must be climbed by muscle and nerve on basalt flakes that cut through boot leather the way glass bites string.  
• The summit is a copper blister already above human survivable altitude. You will be panting 30 °C air that is still somehow on fire. That is the Crust breathing out.  
• When the wind drops, the mouth opens. You will feel the tug in your knees before your ears. Do not hesitate; the crack inhales only when nothing living is touching the outside rim. Sprint, jump, or simply let the vacuum jerk you in—there is no dignified way to enter a planet’s gullet.

------------------------------------------------
2.  Thermal & Pressure Regime – you are the anomaly  
Surface you: 37 °C core, 60 % water, designed for 21 % O₂ at 101 kPa.  
Crust Interior: 53 °C still air, metallic humidity, pressure 134 kPa (nitrogen-heavy, O₂ only 8 %, CO₂ 6 %), no water vapour.  
Net result: you are a walking water balloon leaking heat upward.  
Solution: do not try to “condition” yourself to it—wear the contradiction.

------------------------------------------------
3.  Suit Specification – what to wrap the mistake in  
A.  Outer Carapace  
   – Aerogel-ceramic weave, matte black, emissivity 0.03. Reflects infrared back to the eggs and makes you look thermally inedible.  
   – Spray-sinter a 6 µm silver fluoride film; this is the same mirror the eggs sweat. If you gleam, they catalogue you as “already processed.”  
B.  Middle Gradient Layer  
   – 3 mm encapsulated paraffin micro-spheres. At 45 °C they melt, drinking 180 kJ/kg and keeping your skin at a “cool” 42 °C.  
   – Stitch in sacrificial zinc threads. When the eggs probe you electrochemically they will corrode the zinc first.  
C.  Inner Liquid Skin  
   – Full-body, silicone-based micro-circulatory mesh. Pump 4 °C brine (30 % CaCl₂) from a 2 L dorsal reservoir. You will look like a glossy statuesque insect; the eggs dislike insects.  
D.  Gauntlets  
   – Replace fingernails with detachable sapphire caps; you will need to scratch condensation holes in your own mirrored shell later.  
E.  Footwear  
   – Carbon-fibre stilts with basal molybdenum skis. Distributes weight over hexagonal tiles so no single egg takes full body pressure. The squeak you hear is your lifeline.  
F.  Helmet  
   – Two-piece: inner gold-coated polycarbonate bubble, outer active shutter (LCD goes opaque at 48 °C). Keeps eggs from “seeing” your thermal face.  
   – Audio feed is bone-conduction only; no external mics. If you must talk, subvocalise.  
G.  Bio-Monitor  
   – Watch heart-sound, not heart-rate. When you start hearing your pulse in the helmet speaker (gain >40 dB) it is time to leave; the Crust is amplifying it.  

------------------------------------------------
4.  Air & Hydration – the things the Crust withholds  
• Carry 1 hour of canned O₂ at 30 % concentration. That is all you get; staying longer means surrendering to pulmonary copper-filming.  
• Water is rationed as 250 mL permeation pouches taped to kidney region. It diffuses directly through skin, never reaches mouth, therefore cannot evaporate into metallic vapour.  
• Food: none. Caloric needs are met with IV lipid emulsion fed into the femoral vein. No crumbs, no wrappers, no exhalation of volatile amino acids.

------------------------------------------------
5.  Light – how to betray the dark without being devoured  
• Use a 589 nm sodium beacon (same colour as the extinct sea-stars) at 2 µW. Beam must be strobed at 0.7 Hz, duty-cycle 3 %. Continuous light is interpreted as an egg daring to hatch.  
• Never white-light; it precipitates the eggs into premature inversion.  
• Carry three chemical light-sticks filled with encapsulated dinoflagellate extract—if crushed they emit sea-blue for 90 min. That colour is the Crust’s memory of oceans; it pacifies the lattice.

6.  Timeline & Waypoints – the stopwatch in your marrow  
| T = 0  | Fall through crack. Check suit integrity. Begin recorder. |  
| T + 5  | Eng the heat pumps; calibrate squeak pattern of boots. |  
| T + 15 | Reach catwalk cobalt strut #1; scratch mirror “X” with sapphire cap. (Eggs read symbols as damage, leave you alone.) |  
| T + 30 | Take a single photograph with pin-hole 5 s exposure, no flash. Any more and the mirror dome will echo it back as false sunrise. |  
| T + 45 | Observe first inversion event; note colour sequence (you will see it again in dreams). |  
| T + 55 | Start 5-min out-gas; vent CaCl₂ to exterior. Your mirrored skin will now be ~1 mm thicker; this is expected. |  
| T + 60 | Begin ascent. Helmet audio will whisper your name; it is the hollow learning phonemes. Do NOT respond. |  
Target stay ≤ 65 min. Statistically, every extra minute past 67 min halves survival odds again.

7.  Contingencies – when the Crust edits the plan  
A.  Rupture / Bleeding  
   – Instant: spray wound with liquid styptic-bandage (cyanoacrylate + talc). If flow >2 mL, self-tourniquet, abandon mission.  
   – Iron-taste threshold: ~0.3 ppb will liquefy nearest eggs. You have ~30 s before they flow over you.  
B.  Voice-Lock  
   – If you shouted and eggs begin collapsing, drop to prone, switch helmet to full-white noise mode (pre-loaded file of glacial creak). The lattice will “freeze” while trying to parse unfamiliar sound. Crawl out on elbows; do NOT stand.  
C.  Mirror-Shell Entombment  
   – If dust-film sets while moving, flex major joints rapidly; the CaCl₂ layer expands 3 % on crystallisation and will crack glaze.  
   – Secondary option: use sapphire nail to score-mark a seam along clavicle; pour spare brine pouch into crack; capillary ingress dissolves silver and you can slough whole sheet like snake-skin.  
D.  Missing Companion  
   – Do not search. The Crust donates absences, not bodies. Log name, timestamp, leave. The chamber rebuilds itself; rescue is retrieval of memory, not flesh.  

------------------------------------------------
8.  Psychological Prophylaxis – the things you must carry out in your head  
• Pre-acclimatise with 3 nights in an anechoic chamber at 50 °C, silent except for random heartbeat played at 20 dB. If you panic there, cancel ticket.  
• Mantra: “I am not the shape it needs.” Repeat subvocally on every footfall.  
• Engrave a personal taboo word on inside of helmet visor; mine is “Mother.” Seeing it reminds you that language belongs topside.  
• Schedule weekly iron-chelation therapy for three months post-exit; otherwise the metallic lung-dust seeds a constant rose-smelling nostalgia that will lure you uphill on winter nights.  

------------------------------------------------
9.  De-Compression / Re-Entry to Civilisation  
• Upon surfacing, DO NOT remove helmet until ambient temp ≤ 40 °C. The alpine air is -3 °C; thermal whiplash will flash-crack your mirrored skin and tear living skin with it.  
• Drink 1 L snowmelt immediately—metallic vapour taste persists until diluted.  
• Burn clothing; do not launder. Eggshell microparticles survive 300 °C wash.  
• Report nothing. Bureaucracies who learn the Crust exists will quarantine you harder than the chamber ever did.  
• Expect auditory ghosts: heartbeat in cereal bowl, distant knocking behind fridge. Live near traffic noise; it drowns the Crust’s lullaby.  

------------------------------------------------
10.  The Last Rule – carve it on the inside of your teeth  
Leave no blood, leave no voice, leave no love.  
The Hollow Crust is patient.  
It will remember the shape of whatever you fail to take back with you.
