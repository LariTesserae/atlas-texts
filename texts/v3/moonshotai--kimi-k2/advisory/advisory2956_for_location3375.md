Field Briefing: Pre-deployment Orientation for Tourist-Class Visitors to the “Empty Ocean”  
(Below-Shell Internal Sea, Artificial Planet 11-H/“Pelagarch construct”)

--------------------------------------------------
1. Accept that you are baggage, not guest.  
The Empty Ocean was engineered for continent-scale hydraulic damping, not hospitality. Every cubic metre of water is on the payroll: it braces plating, ferries heat, drives turbines. You are a contaminant – tolerated only while you remain chemically boring and acoustically quiet. Treat the place like a sleeping tyrant: necessary, merciless, and easily woken.

--------------------------------------------------
2. Make peace with the fact that the map is a diary, not a chart.  
Corridors logarithmically widen (≈√2 every kilometre). Straight-line swimming is impossible; gyro-compasses spin uselessly as local g-vector slews. Navigate instead by sound print:  
• Triple-expansion piston thunder (22 Hz fundamental) = main artery; stay within echo range.  
• Sonar pingers (42 kHz, 0.8 s repeat) = wall within 200 m, oxygen kiosk probable.  
• Sudden ultrasonic trill rising a minor third = filing-dolphin pod; approach only if you carry ferrous swarf for trade.  
Memorise the seventeen tidal harmonics; when chorus jumps to nineteen, surge inversion is <90 s away—find a handhold.

--------------------------------------------------
3. Modify your body or bring armor for it.  
Water is 7 °C, pH 7.9, 1.06 g cm⁻³ (dissolved silicates). Core temp will drop 1 °C every 12 min unless augmented. Minimum retail package:  
• Micro-fuel-cell waist belt (45 W, 48 h) feeding silicone heat-exchange vasculature undersuit.  
• Osmotic kidney implant or external counter-current “artificial gill” pack; rations are pointless—everything edible down here is either toxic, copyrighted, or capable of filing a grievance.  
• Gloves rated for micro-glass shard abrasion (Vectran weave, 5 ply). One cut and lunar-basalt particles embed like fishhooks, migrating toward lymph nodes within days.  
• Optional: low-grade magnetisable studs in forearms—doubles as barter with dolphins and grants limited ability to stick to suddenly sideways decks.

--------------------------------------------------
4. Budget gravity like cash.  
g can vary 0 – 1.8 m s⁻² vector in metres. Keep limbs tucked during tidal swaps; disorientation is universal. Secure every tool on self-retracting Spectra line <60 cm; dropped objects become ballistics in corridor-wide vortices. Personal tip: exhale just before gradient flips—lungs act as emergency ballast, preventing inversion whiplash.

--------------------------------------------------
5. Respect the closed ledger.  
Nothing leaves. Bubbles are re-absorbed, feces filtered for trace metals, breath condensate re-distilled into lamps. Contribute as little novel chemistry as possible:  
• No copper alloys (triggers eel-rope swarms).  
• Sunscreens with zinc oxide—leave topside; here it nucleates “white rust” mats that clog regulators.  
• Absolutely no radio-isotope power packs—biosphere already runs on picowatt bookkeeping; a single extra millisievert tips predator/prey ratios and you’ll be shadowed by micro-dolphin hit-squads stripping metal ions from your appendages.

--------------------------------------------------
6. Anticipate engineered weather.  
• Bubble-storms: kilometre-wide domes migrate upward at 0.3 m s⁻¹; if caught inside, ascend with them—outer skin implodes spontaneously, producing shock-hammer capable of pulmonary bruise.  
• Silence-corners: pockets <15 dB everywhere; heart murmur sounds like artillery. Use them to rest ears, NOT to hide—apex scavenger “airlock-gulper” homes in on arrhythmias.  
• Pressure fronts: warm fronts taste metallic, cold fronts smell like wet concrete. Swim perpendicular until taste gradient narrows <1 ppm cm⁻¹; parallel travel risks dragging you into a spiral arm where gravity vector diverges >45° from visual horizon—cameras never white-balance again.

--------------------------------------------------
7. Maintain the gear that maintains you.  
Oxygen kiosks are spaced ≈4 km but hidden behind spall-curtains. Look for sodium-iodide lamp cages with yellow-green bio-film speckling (respires CO₂, vents O₂). Field maintenance:  
• Scrub algae intake slits with backside of knife—never palm; film exudes dermatotoxin under pressure.  
• Swap tank only during mid-surge lull; kiosks lock during peak reverse-flow.  
• Each kiosk carries two spare gill-packs—first labelled “A”, second labelled “A” upside-down. Both work, but upside-down unit expires 12 h sooner. Memorise orientation, mark your calendar in rust scratches on slate.

--------------------------------------------------
8. Know the fauna index (abridged).  
• Chrome-yellow fish (Hexachromis spiralis): harmless, gravity-indifferent. School density predicts upcoming g-gradient—dense = calm, sparse = chaos.  
• Filing-dolphins (Microdelphinus ferracidus): 80 cm, rotate in trios. Offer thumb-nail sized steel washers; they reciprocate with cleaned tools or guide you to next kiosk. Shun if you bleed—they file teeth are hematotoxic.  
• Glass eels (Vitronatrix funiculus): braid to open hatches; do NOT obstruct—will saw through suit fabric.  
• White porcelain flatworms (Mattewhite planarioides): sessile, filter-feed on sound; avoid standing on them—produces subsonic squeal that draws eel-ropes.  
• Airlock-gulper (species undefined, one confirmed individual): suspected ex-maintenance drone evolved around discarded turbine stomach. Length estimates 8–12 m. If distant lights strobe orange-black without bulb failure, ascend/duct/ reverse course immediately; do NOT emit regular fin-stroke cadence—switch to arrhythmic kick or it matches amplitude and engulfs you.

--------------------------------------------------
9. Work the time-table.  
Planet-induced shell squeeze produces internal tides every 9 h 4 min. Schedule:  
• Hour -2: surge acceleration begins; micro-g.  
• Hour 0: peak west-to-east flow; gravity tips “down” toward ceiling.  
• Hour +2: lull; best for tool work, rust-scratch journaling, eel-ropes dormant.  
• Hour +4.5: reverse east-to-west; gravity truest.  
• Hour +6: back-surge peak; worst for navigation, best for dolphin barter (they feed).  
Plan sleep in two 4-hour brackets around lull; REM cycles misalign otherwise, causing intrusive ultrasonic hallucinations—visitors convinced they hear deceased relatives reciting maintenance logs.

--------------------------------------------------
10. Have an exit orientation, even though exits are fictional.  
There are no verified passages to outer crust. Survival horizon is therefore repair horizon—keep the cathedral alive, it keeps you alive. Learn basic piston-ram gasket massage—one spare Viton ring gifted to a kiosk maint-bot buys you two weeks protected camp and fresh gill-pack. Carry at least three universal spanners (titanium, non-ferrous), a thimble of graphite powder, and a roll of 3-mil PTFE tape: currency, medicine, and prayer all at once.

--------------------------------------------------
11. Psychological prophylaxis.  
• Light cycling is 26 h. Your circadian is 24. Use sub-lingual melatonin 0.5 mg every local midnight; larger doses desynchronise you with lamp dimmage and causes narcolepsy at peak surge.  
• Silence-corners induce auditory pareidolia. When voices occur, recite prime numbers—if sequence stays linear, brain is coherent; if primes scramble, oxygen toxicity rising, ascend 2 m and vent regulator.  
• Isolation index exceeds deep-space EVA. Record daily rust-scratch diary; failure to write for 48 h correlates with fugue states in which travelers attempt to “merge” with wall film—irreversible within 6 h.

--------------------------------------------------
12. Red-shift is end of contract.  
Signs the structure is abandoning its million-year mandate:  
• Sodium-iodide lamps redden >50 K below spec.  
• Piston thunder fundamental drops to 20 Hz (already observed).  
• Corridor widening exponent exceeds √2.  
When any two coincide, you have ≈21 days before “shear avalanche” — a cascade of micro-faults that converts walls to silt. Kiosk bots will ignore you; dolphins flee inward. Final play: locate an engineering bulkhead labeled with three concentric circles; behind inspection plate is a manual lockout. Activate it—seals compartment, traps pocket of residual air, stabilises g at 0.8 m s⁻² inward. Wait. Atmosphere condenses on ceiling; drink with sponge. Expect five to seven months before rescue, if any. Carry this document printed on silk microfiche; alkaline environment digests paper in weeks but leaves protein-based film untouched—proof you were here, proof you maybe leave.

--------------------------------------------------
Remember: You volunteered to be plankton in a machine that sings to itself. Behave like courteous plankton—small, translucent, nutritiously uninteresting—and the Ocean may forget to digest you long enough for the sky above the sky to remember you exist.
