Field Brief for the Plain of Last Shapes  
(Compiled by the Office of Interdimensional Safety, “Quiet Sector” desk)  
For travelers of ordinary biology, carbon-based identity, and no prior erasure experience.

--------------------------------------------------
1. Pre-Departure Medical & Psychological Baseline
 - Full skeletal resonance scan – record the phase-print of your bone lattice; you’ll need it to re-assert yourself after the marrow-song of the Dry Below tries to cancel you.  
 - Blood-chemistry buffer: have your erythrocytes doped with 3 % perfluorocarbon emulsion. Pressure here equals 4.2 km Earth-altitude even though you stand below ancient sea-level; oxygen doesn’t boil away, but it forgets to reach tissue.  
 - Middle-ear equalisation inserts: the hush is so complete your tympanic membranes will bow inward trying to chase a sound that left. Rupture from negative pressure is common.  
 - Psychological inoculation: spend one Earth week in anechoic chambers (< –20 dB) for two hours daily while reciting personal memories aloud. Learn the taste of your own voice disappearing – you’ll be living inside that flavor.  
 - Anticipatory grief counselling: the Borrowed Shadow detachment produces a bereavement-like syndrome (“I was never here” disorder). Accept that homesickness will operate backwards; you will miss places you have not yet left, because the plain files chronology under “obsolete diplomacy.”

--------------------------------------------------
2. Equipment Manifest (checked against the plain’s anti-entropy customs)

CLOTHING  
- Triple-layered suit: outer weave of rip-stop PTFE to shed the titanium-white of the Mirror Barrens; mid-layer evacuated kapok pile that traps silence the way down traps warmth; inner phase-change mesh printed with your bone-lattice phase so your marrow has something legal to resonate against.  
- Gloves fingertip-doped with gallium – prevents graphite foils from slicing straight through keratin when you reflexively try to catch one.  
- Boots: foam-filled silicone soles 4 cm thick. Must be cut to your exact weight; one gram error and the ringing plate couples to your femurs like a sub-woofer. The office loans calibrated step-molds 48 h before flight.

AIR & WATER  
- No open water allowed, period. Carry instead a “water-taboo” cartridge: zeolite pellets pre-loaded with 2 g H₂O vapour locked inside. Your exhaled breath is filtered; the pellets steal back your own moisture, return it as 5 ml potable per 24 h. It tastes of apology.  
- Emergency O₂? Forbidden. Plain will confiscate it acoustically; canister implodes with a squelch so rude it can fracture civility across three tectonic plates. Use the perfluorocarbon reserve in your bloodstream and accept panting.

SOUND  
- Packable acoustic baffle: origami creased mylar that opens into a tetrahedron 2 m on edge. Step inside when Shy-Hulls exchange silence-treaties – it lets you whisper to yourself without paying extinction tax.  
- Bone-canceler: wrist-mounted piezo plates tuned to your anatomy; press when you feel the Dry Below’s bell-note. Creates inverse-phase vibration in ulna/radius; destructive interference saves skeleton. Carry two spare batteries (solid-state, nanophosphate; no lithium, chemistry embarrasses locals).

NAVIGATION  
- No electronic GPS – signals diffract along philosophical fronts and return as nihilistic lorem ipsum. Carry instead a “regret compass”: brass disk with a single freely-spinning arrow. Etch onto rim every personal mistake you can recall; arrow points toward whichever error the plain believes you most need to revisit. Works even when you’re lost conceptually.  
- Paper maps printed on 30 µm aluminum leaf. They rewrite themselves hourly as the Sigmoid Arches crawl; scrunch foil, hold to membrane-sky, new coastline appears. Once wrinkled cannot be re-flattened – treat like fresh guilt.

ILLUMINATION  
- Torch unnecessary; darkness volume-limited to 2.0 lux maximum. Use instead a “memorial patch,” 2 cm² graphene impregnated with chlorophyll derivative. Peel, stick to cheek; faint photosynthetic afterglow recalls childhood night-lights, comforts limbic system, keeps lizard-brain from surrendering utterly to existential dusk.

3. Conduct Rules (transgressions enforced by erosion, not diplomacy)

A. Movement  
- Walk only in pairs – any species acceptable as long as partner’s mass within 5 % of yours; resonance cancellation is equal-opportunity.  
- Foot-down cadence ≤ 60 steps / min. Faster triggers tessellation slip, producing UV spark you cannot see, which the graphite trees can and will litigate in their folio-archives. Lawsuits here are measured in megayears.  

B. Interaction with Locals  
- Shy-Hulls: observe bow etiquette. Stand at 45°, hands cupped as if cradling your own absence. Helmets will dip; photon packet exchange lasts 0.8 s. Do NOT speak; they interpret voice as attempted colonial audit. If bow inadvertently knocks your mask, stay bent until reactor-pulse count completes (usually three ultraviolet flickers). To stand early is to volunteer for patrol loop; suit will fossilise around you, carbon dated to regret.  
- Graphite trees: admire, do not harvest foils. If a foil lands on skin, hold until cartographic memory finishes; then blow away dust gently, westward. Dust that cannot rejoin its trunk will seed a “folly-cloud,” semi-sentient weather that follows you home telling outdated wind secrets.  

C. Memory Management  
- The plain taxes declarative sentences. Whenever you find yourself narrating aloud, pay immediately: open memorial patch fully, flush one personal memory into its glow. Patch dims; you forget childhood pet, debt paid. Travelers who run out of memories begin to quote guidebook; guidebook then erases itself.  
- Accept the Borrowed Shadow only if your psychological profile scored > 70 on “solitude tolerance.” Shadow returns 0.5 % translucent per circumnavigation; full re-absorption renders you 1 kg lighter and incapable of using first-person pronouns for remainder of life.  

--------------------------------------------------
4. Health & Safety Emergencies

Severe marrow resonance (nose-bleed, teeth hum, vertigo):  
- Snap both boots off ladder rung, kneel, activate bone-canceler max amplitude.  
- Recite multiplication tables backwards (base-7) until femur vibration subsides – mathematics too abstract for plain to tax.  

Mirror Barrens footprint puncture (infinite depth illusion):  
- DO NOT look into hole.  
- Rotate eyes 20° left, focus on horizon equation.  
- Drop memorial patch inside puncture; reflected future-skull vanishes. Retrieve patch; it now carries photo of the skull. Keep; customs at home love souvenirs.  

Shy-Hull reactor cascade (unstable neutrino burn):  
- Radiation negligible, but philosophical heat unbearable.  
- Offer treaty: hold gloved hands half-bow distance, open palms to show absence of intent. Reactor resets to baseline.  

--------------------------------------------------
5. Exit & Re-Earth Re-Integration

1. Ascend ladder backwards – maintains uncertainty principle with gravity, prevents it cementing legs.  
2. In stratopause antechamber, cough twice; the hush you swallowed must be expectorated before atmosphere re-entry. Pack cough into vial (supplied) for quarantine analysis.  
3. Upon return, spend 24 h in 30 °C, 90 % humidity room; invite water to remember you, or skin stays desiccatedly polite for months.  
4. File negative testimony only: describe what you did NOT see, hear, feel. Positive statements attract auditors from the Quiet Sector who collect unpaid taxes from family photo albums.  
5. Schedule quarterly bone scan for next 2 years; Dry Below sometimes mails lingering bills for resonance, collectible only from skeleton.  

--------------------------------------------------
Closing Note

Carry at least one memory you can afford to lose. The best choice is the memory of having packed everything you need; once gone, the plain cannot confiscate what you no longer believe you brought. Travel light, travel negative, and remember—though it already forgets you—that the water owed to the sky is not yours to return.
