Field Briefing: Infant Grotto, Pre-Fabricated Reality Zone  
Prepared by the Office of Inter-Situational Etiquette, 3rd (Abandoned) Draft

----------------------------------------------------
0. Executive Summary
Do not go.  
If you must go, do not go alone.  
If you must go alone, do not go as a human.  
If you must go as a human, bring a second skeleton—your first will be repurposed before you finish reading this sentence.  
This document is not a guide; it is a damage-limitation prayer encoded as prose.

----------------------------------------------------
1. Pre-Departure Bioculture Audit
1.1 Strip your microbiome:  
– Commence a 19-day course of industrial antifungals, antivirals, and broad-spectrum antibiotics until stool samples read “sterile” twice in a row.  
– Replace gut flora with a single patented strain (Bacillus negotiatoris) engineered to excrete a chemical whitenoise that disguises epithelial chemistry as “uninteresting rock.”  
– Expect the replacement flora to go feral within six hours of arrival; that’s what the backup tin (labelled “candy”) is for—chew all eight grams on the threshold. It buys you perhaps forty minutes before the Grotto re-files you under ‘edible geology’.

1.2 Epigenetic Silence Protocol   
– One week prior, begin daily 30-minute sessions in a sensory-deprivation tank dosed with 5 mmol of chromatin-modulating siRNAs keyed to silence heat-shock, detox, and immune-response genes. If the Grotto cannot read stress, it cannot weaponise it.  
– Accept that this also silences “the will to leave.” Pre-sign custody of your passport with a trusted friend.

2. Equipment (All Items Must Be Transparent to X-ray Vision)
2.1 Light  
– None. Do not bring photons, they will be turned into apex predators.  
– If you cannot function without photons, bring a darkness instead: a black-hole-in-a-can™ (item #77-B). Unscrew lid to release a 40 cm sphere of perfect light absorption. Note: the can is only rated for 200 heartbeats; pace yourself accordingly.

2.2 Breathing Apparatus  
Two-stage system:  
a) Mouthpiece delivering 0.96 fractional helium to raise vocal formants two octaves above the mating frequency of foot-shadow leeches (see section 4.3).  
b) Nasal counter-lung filled with your own refrigerated cerebrospinal fluid, extracted and cultured last night. Self-recognition is the only filter the Grotto respects.

2.3 Clothing  
Wear clothes you have forgotten you own—items left at ex-lovers’ houses, socks donated to charity shops and mysteriously returned. The weave must contain memories anaesthetised by laundry cycles. The Grotto samples narrative more than cotton; make yours unstoryable.

3. Calibration at the Edge
3.1 Perform the Continuity Check  
Hold your breath until you can no longer remember your name. Exhale. If the name returns, step forward. If it doesn’t, someone else is breathing for you—retreat.

3.2 Swallow a copper penny minted the year you first lost a tooth. The taste of your own historical metal confuses the fossil layer currently forming around your shoes.

----------------------------------------------------
4. Inside: Behavioral Modes
4.1 Movement Rule  
Move only when the grotto inhales. The inhale is a vacuum of intent, a moment when place forgets itself. To detect it, balance the penny on your tongue; when it levitates, go, one foot exactly thirteen centimetres forward. Never complete a stride with mass on both feet—the grotto may register you as a “feature to keep.”

4.2 Light-Making Emergency (DO NOT READ WHILE MAKING LIGHT)  
If total blackout panic becomes unbearable, crack a chem-stick. Immediately swallow the glowing glass. Your abdominal tissues will diffract the photons into wavelengths the fauna have not yet evolved to covet. Expect intestinal sunburn and a temporary cult following among bioluminescent orchids.

4.3 Foot-Shadow Leeches  
You will feel them sampling your epidermal taxonomy. Let them. When they begin synchronising their heartbeats with yours, speak the following, phonetically exact:  
“Guh-LAH-stoe, thrum-b’hal.”  
This is the rock’s working title for itself—its placeholder name while it decides what kind of thing to be. Uttering it declares you part of the stone’s provisional memory; the leeches will treat you as tectonic, not mammalian.

----------------------------------------------------
5. Timekeeping (Obsolete But Necessary)
None of your chronometers will survive orientation. Instead count by metamorphoses: when the vine that threaded your left shoelace molts into a snake, call it an hour. When the snake unties your shoe and re-laces it backwards, another hour. After three such events the concept “hour” will have died; bury it in silence, it attracts carrion architects.

----------------------------------------------------
6. Eating / Being Eaten
6.1 Do not import calories; calories are declarations of loyalty.  
6.2 If you must ingest, consume only your own shed matter—hair, skin oil, clipped nails—kept in a hip flask labelled “remember me.” Anything else recruits you into its food chain at the molecular level.  
6.3 Should you find fruit that tastes exactly like a childhood memory, the correct etiquette is to refuse three times, then agree on the fourth offer but bite your own tongue instead. Bleed quietly; the memory-fruit will consider the contract honoured.

----------------------------------------------------
7. Exit Conditions (Theoretical)
7.1 You will not leave the same shape you entered. Accept asymmetry now to reduce later disorientation.  
7.2 The threshold is ambulatory; it meanders with the grotto’s mood. Lure it: plant an unfinished thought in a crevice. When the thought sprouts and bears a fruit that resembles your face before first heartbreak, the threshold will come sniffing. Step across at the moment it tries to taste the fruit; hesitation fossilises.  
7.3 Carry a mirror polished on both sides. If pursued, show the grotto its own reflection. Infant realities are narcissistic; they may pause to woo themselves, giving you a three-second window. Do not look into the mirror—what you see will be claiming your name with your own mouth.

----------------------------------------------------
8. Post-Mission Decontamination
8.1 Quarantine inside a Faraday cage filled with white noise tuned to daytime television. The frequencies of soap-opera plots erode narrative parasites.  
8.2 Every day for a year swallow a teaspoon of the same rock dust you collected on arrival. You are convincing your bones they were always sediment.  
8.3 Never sleep in total darkness again; keep a night-light shaped like the word “maybe.” The grotto hates qualifiers; they keep it from finalising you.

----------------------------------------------------
9. Final Note (To Be Read Once You Have Returned—Or Think You Have)
If you remember reading this guide, you failed.  
A successful visitor’s recollection should feel like a mistranslated joke: punchline missing, vocabulary slightly off, nagging certainty the joke was about you.  
Burn this page.  
Use the ashes to season your food for seven days; they taste like copper pennies and forgotten teeth.
