To walk the Skyfold Terratos you must first become partly metal yourself—not literally, but in the alloy of expectations you bring. Below is the kit and the curriculum I would hand any fellow human about to step off the normal Earth and begin the polite upward fall.

### 1. Pre-departure Conditioning
- **Vestibular retune**: Spend two weeks on a wobble board bolted to a lazy-Susan. You need ankles that trust a floor that never stops canting.  
 if the world pitches, you pitch too.  
- **Stroboscopic breathing**: Train your lungs to inhale on a flash of white light, exhale on a click. This synchronises you with the structure’s half-breathing engines; when the air decides to stay half-crushed you will not panic.
- **Memory gargle**: Every night, swallow a mouthful of warm mercury (food-grade dental amalgam). Spit it out in the morning onto hot iron; read the oxide patterns as fortune cookies. The practice lets your cortex surrender one colour without pain; indigo is already gone—another might follow.

### 2. Clothing (sew copper thread into everything)
- **Tri-thermal skinsuit**: Silver facing up to reflect the columns of impossible light, red facing the radiator wings to steal their chill. Between the two, a 1 mm air gap containing powdered zinc so you smell faintly of pennies—camouflage against the zinc-oxide trees.
- **Friction anklettes**: Riveted mouse-leather lined with beeswax. They squeak out a friendly frequency that keeps dirigibled rats from mistaking you for salt-cable.
- **Hood-within**: Millinery wire bent into a shallow parabola; the reflector plate above will slide its brightest column safely past your corneas. The wire also catches “rivet ghosts” so you can shake them off later like dandruff.

### 3. Load-out – the pockets polite levers will inventory anyway
- **Collapsible cup** (titanium) shaped like a tuning fork. Strike it against any railing; the lingering note tells you which districts the Clockkeepers just forecast—major third means safe romance, diminished seventh, trouble.
- **Hair-thin catheter straw**. When an ornamental water-sphere drifts within reach, spear it, sip, then blow the excess back; the globe must complete an orbit or the structure notices and withholds tomorrow’s spheres.
- **Fold-paper notebook of iron oxide pages**. Speak aloud if you must, then shut the book; your words will oxidise onto the sheet. Tear them out before dusk or the verdigris vine will follow you home.
- **Fingerling magnet** the shape of a sparrow. If you must steady yourself on a gustless midnight, press it to iron; the structure will mistake your pulse for its own molten dream and spare you a shinbone tremor.
- **Needle file and three spare rivets** (1 mm longer than standard). After 3:14 a.m. growth spasm, offer to replace the rivet you just rode—citizens value volunteers; they may show you the Fold Lamp.

### 4. Biological Prophylaxis
- **Get yourself lightly anaemic**. Low iron discourages copper’s urge to “remember the forge” and weld your cuts shut; you want wounds that can still bleed, stories that can still scab.
- **Daily eyedrops of dilute ozone water**. Retinal after-images of rivet ghosts can ossify; ozone dissolves them like soft soap.
- **Carry your own death permit**—a tattoo of the empty set Ø on the inner wrist. Without it the place will not let you die properly, only twitch eternally as a hinge.

### 5. Way-finding & Etiquette
**Directions** are negotiated, not known:
- Whisper coordinate couplets while tapping the rail (latitude of rivet count, longitude of dripping-pitch). The gardeners’ root-code listens; a shutter-leaf may swivel to point a shortcut or a false-you-should-not-have-seen.
- Never finish a sentence; let the half-words ride the compression-thick air like capsules. Finished arguments arrive scrambled—people have divorced over misplaced full stops.
- If a steward offers the mercury sponge, accept; without it you will keep seeing flat horizons and every footfall becomes exile. Press it to temples until you taste your childhood doorknob—then you are native enough.

### 6. Nutrition Strategy
- Eat only rust-coloured things: paprika jerky, iron-fortified wafers, sumac. Your gut flora will learn to read the terraces’ emotional conductivity; heartburn signifies district-wide anxiety—find a radiator wing and cool down.  
- **Avoid salty data-cables**. Rats guard them jealously; if you snack on one you’ll absorb decade-old invoices and wake up indebted to a pension fund that dissolved before you were born.

### 7. Rest & Privacy
Sleep inside a chimney of silence:
- Clip a micro-cantilever to your collarbone; it converts heartbeat into white noise cancelling the half-crank engines.  
- Hang a hammock under a zinc-oxide birch; the roots will tighten rivets under you like a rocking cradle.  
- Replace shadows with them—none survive the down-pouring light. If you must dream, do it aloud; dreams oxidise fastest, leaving a morning bouquet you can pick for breakfast conversation.

### 8. Calendrical Rituals
- On the decade-groan night stay awake; strap a tuning fork to your sternum so the note writes itself on your bones. Wear it next journey as an internal compass.
- Equinox eversion: stand at a five-vector intersection, five minutes before inversion. Carry one indigo memory (the world no longer supplies it) and let it tug you outward. If you feel the sky fold like a paper doll, step—do not hesitate or you’ll ossify into next cycle’s balcony.

### 9. Exit Contingency (because rumours are not demonstrations)
- Keep magnetised boots charged to 70 %. Should you climb past the reflector scrim and light grows kind, kick off: the pull downward is a memory you must override with equal nostalgia for the unknown.
- Alternatively, let an idea erode completely: when your last indigo memory is spent you’ll weigh less than grammar—drift outward on zinc dust until a balcony that was once your sentence catches you, ejects you, rehomes you.

Travel light, travel alloyed—remember you are not audience but ingredient. Every footstep tightens something; every breath tattoos iron. When you can taste pennies, hear conversations in your shins, and feel dawn gun-cock inside your eyelids, you are no longer visiting the Skyfold Terraces—you are part of the slow, inexorable addition of one rivet-length every ten quiet years.
