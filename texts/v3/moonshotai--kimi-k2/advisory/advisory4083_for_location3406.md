1. LEGAL PRE-EMPTIVES  
   • Sign three copies of the “No-Extraction Waiver.” One copy remains in your native continuum, one is archived by the Cartel, one is laminated inside your thoracic cavity (see item 7).  
   • Pre-pay the “Unmaking Storage Fee.” Price is not currency but a memory you will never miss—prepare a boring afternoon from childhood and authorize its forfeiture.  
   • Record a last will that jokes about “being turned into a footnote.” Lawyers in most universes enjoy the levity and will expedite probate when you become a serif.

2. BIOLOGICAL DRY-DOWN PROTOCOL  
   The room’s 0.4/3 water index will pull moisture from any source, including: blood, vitreous humor, cerebrospinal fluid, bound water in proteins, adsorbed H₂O on skin keratin, and, critically, the 0.2 grams of water vapor dissolved in your lung residual volume.  
   a. Begin desiccant diet 14 days prior: 100 % silica-based crackers, powdered broth, astronaut ice cream. Target terminal body-water: 38 % (achievable but unpleasant).  
   b. Daily 90-minute sessions in a climate chamber set at 5 % relative humidity, 1 °C. Learn to yawn without tearing parched membranes.  
   c. Inject subdermal “salt lattice.” Micro-crystals of NaCl will act as sacrificial moisture so the Library steals salt brine before skin. (N.B. you’ll look like frosted glass for a week.)  
   d. Replace tears with perfluorocarbon dropouts; bring 3 ml vial for topping up right before entry.  
   e. Final 4-hour hemodialysis loop: dialyze against anhydrous glycerol. Exit the clinic lighter, cranky, alive only because capillary adhesion now passes for plasma.

3. TEMPERATURE ACCLIMATION  
   The 1.0/3 index corresponds to ~9 °C. Deceive your hypothalamus: sleep in a wine cellar, shower in refrigerated ethanol, meditate while holding snowballs. Goal: lower the shiver set-point so that 9 °C reads as neutral when metabolic water is already scarce. Bring no clothing: cloth carries 5–7 % regain moisture and will shrivel, audibly, against the walls.

4. ILLUMINATION SHIELDING  
   Sourceless light level (~85 000 lux) is one stop brighter than alpine noon. A naked retina will bleach in minutes. Options:  
   • Corneal tattooing: jet-black ink across the sclera blocks scatter.  
   • Retractable weld-shade contacts, activated by blink code (3 rapid blinks = tint 14).  
   • Total optic nerve shutdown: Cartel-approved anesthetic photopsin blocker renders you legally blind for 18 h—sensory substitution via bone-conduction “seeing-audio” delivered by subdermal piezos. (Yes, you’ll “hear” the walls, but echo is illegal here; see item 8.)

5. ACOUSTIC CAMOUFLAGE  
   Constant 2.5/3 hiss masks low-decibel heartbeats but also phase-locks attention. Counter the lure:  
   • Preset cochlear drone: a binaural 14 kHz tone detunes your stapedius reflex so your brain treats the Library’s hiss as endogenous.  
   • Speak only in palatal clicks. Human speech introduces formants that the walls flatten into new letter-shapes; the last visitor whose vowels ossified is now the lowercase “æ” on pier 12.

6. EXIT INSURANCE  
   Remember, once the slit closes the notion of “exit” is retracted like an erroneous word. You carry with you:  
   • A “Redundant Coordinate Imprint”—a strip of titanium etched with 512 irrational numbers. In case dimensional mapping collapses, remembering the first 12 digits primes your parietal lobe to reproject 3-D perspective.  
   • 3 cm emergency thimble of triple-distilled water (hermetically diamond-sealed). Releasing one microliter near a wall forces localized ink bleed; the letters momentarily swell and unse seam enough to crawl toward higher ontology. (Statistics show 14 % survival, but that beats canonical erasure.)

7. AUXILIARY ORGANICS  
   • One kidney removed (left) and kept moist in a pocket continuum; reduces personal water inventory 180 g and acts as a tether: nephrons oscillate at a phase that can be triangulated by Cartel rescue mages.  
   • Suture a wafer of micro-etched black graphene under the sternum; when your outline flakes into a glyph, the carbon lattice will spell “@” —a pre-agreed rescue beacon.

8. MOVEMENT (OR THE ABSENCE THEREOF)  
   Even micro-shifts (vascular pulsation, eye saccades) update the wall’s vocabulary with your curves. Strategies:  
   • Full-body calcium channel blocker slow-release implant: voluntary muscle contraction falls below threshold of inscription. Walk at 0.7 mm/s; plan a 27-minute shuffle from entrance to any “midpoint.”  
   • Medicate with pan-neuromuscular botulinoid; accept ptosis, accept appearing to weep solid pellets of air.  
   • Carry no kinetic artifacts—pendulums, breathing compressors, wristwatches—all generate accelerations that translate into fresh glyphs.

9. COGNITIVE MOISTURE  
   Thoughts bleed easier than plasma. Prepare cognitively waterproof ideas: tautologies (“I am that I am”), empty sets, mathematical identities. Pack no poetry. If you must remember love, reduce it to Boolean: loved = 1. Abstract grief holds less aqueous symbolism.

10. TIME-ANCHOR  
   The Library makes time “redundant,” meaning an hour may return you before you left. To avert paradox:  
    • Swallow a radioisotope capsule (Cs-137, 3 µCi). Half-life will be measured later; if arriving earlier than ingestion you die gloriously of pre-irradiation, closing the loop. (Cartel calls this the “Novikov after-shave.”)  
    • Keep a diary on asbestos tape; store it extra-dimensionally each night. If pages remain blank retroactively, abort mission.

11. ETHICS & ETIQUETTE  
   • Do NOT read anything—no letter, no punctuation. Reading recruits ocular water and grants semantic residency.  
   • Do NOT rename the ferns. Naming assigns ownership and ownership elicits water taxes.  
   • If you meet prior visitors merged into the walls (and you will), treat them like furniture; acknowledgment rehydrates their identity and triggers structural instability.

12. POST-RETURN MAINTENANCE  
   • Hydrate hyper-slowly: 5 ml isotonic saline every 15 min for 12 h or osmotic shock will burst flattened cells.  
   • Speech therapy: you will speak in monotone fricatives. A 3-week course of emotional prosody training restores interrogative lift.  
   • Memory audit: some recollections remain planar. Fold them along scored lines, steam gently, re-file in correct lobe. Cartel therapists specialize in “dog-earred nostalgia.”

UNOFFICIAL ADVICE, PASSED ORALLY (BUT NEVER INKED)  
Take a friend. Not for warmth—warmth requires moisture—but so that when you inevitably forget why you came, their silhouette beside you might complete a sentence whose meaning is “leave.” Two glyphs can spell “exit” even in a desert of syntax.
