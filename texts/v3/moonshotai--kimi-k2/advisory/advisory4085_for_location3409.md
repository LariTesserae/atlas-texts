A Field Manual for the Plateau of the Crying Stone  
(Prepared by the Inter-Continuum Hostel & Funeral Cooperative – 9th revision)

0. Before Anything Else: Decide Whether You Actually Want to Go  
Silence leopards read intention the way other predators read scent. If you arrive carrying any trace of ordinary restlessness—music still looping in your inner ear, caffeine jitters, the half-lived speed of cities—your first motionless hour will be your last. Decide, on the edge of sleep, if you are willing to become the three square centimetres of warmth where your femurs meet the basalt. If the answer is “maybe,” stay home. Only “yes, utterly” is quiet enough.

1. Vaccinations & Bureaucracy  
- Humility inoculation: 3 weeks of listening to recordings of your own pulse slowed 800 %.  
- Paperwork is pointless. Every visa you fill out will be turned into an origami basalt-martin by the Empty City children and set adrift in the cloud ocean. Bring one tear-proof envelope containing:  
  – A single photo of a place you can stand to forget.  
  – A list of every sound you love, written in charcoal that you will burn on arrival.  
Smiling at immigration is forbidden; show the inside of your lower lip instead. They will stamp the vein.

2. Clothing: Dress as the Plateau Will Remember You  
No synthetics—polyester screams to silence leopards. Use only fibres that once grew inside of silence: raw flax, nettle, unboiled wool, cloud-silk.  
Colours must be dusk or later. Any hue brighter than gneiss at twilight will be colonised by mites that commute in eight-leg platoons; within a day you will be a living semaphore tower, and birds will spell secrets on your skin.  
Shoes: none. Feet are the only ears the plateau trusts. Let obsidian pebbles click between your toes; they inform the stone of your weight distribution, so thermals adjust to keep you afloat. Boots are interpreted as refusal to merge.  
Carry one pocket of marten-down for the cloud dive, but only after you have donated a syllable of your name to the city archive (see §5).

3. Provisions That Do Not Count as Luggage  
Water: do not bring. The plateau will weep directly into your mouth if you open it correctly—tongue flattened like a cat at a trough of moonlight. Taste first: if it remembers magma, spit; if it tastes like childhood pillowcases, swallow.  
Food: edible items echo. Instead bring one unspoken sentence folded into a strip of rice paper. At first hunger, place it on the warmest seam; the stone will dissolve it and feed you back the memory of every meal you ever regretted—sufficient calories for twelve days.  
Shelter is already inside your body temperature; still, bring a memory of being held. Unwrap it at night like a cloak; the Empty City residents will curl into its negative spaces without asking.

4. Navigation After Physics Has Left the Party  
Compasses spin backwards here because the plateau remembers when north was still a tenant of deep mantle. Instead, swallow a basalt coin (provided free at the rim). It will settle in your pelvis and point toward whatever you have most recently betrayed.  
Maps are discouraged; every line you draw is interpreted as a wound. If you must record, learn “pebble syntax”: place obsidian beads in the hollow between cooled columns. The array is read by clicking your teeth once; the returning vibration is a sentence the plateau has edited for you.  
Distance is measured in Sterna: the distance a silence leopard can leap without its feathers rustling. Roughly 47 m on still days, 3 cm when the cloud ocean is storming. Pace accordingly.  
Warning: if you walk twelve days south and the horizon lowers instead of arriving, you have entered the mirror-sky. Tie your ankles together with your own hair; think of vertical things (ladders, grandfathers, rain). The cloud will bounce you back onto stone.

5. Etiquette in the Empty City  
Greet no one; greetings are acoustic litter. Instead, exhale slowly onto the nearest basalt wall; if the condensate forms a miniature of your childhood home, you are recognised and may stay.  
Property is defined by warmth. Place your belongings on a ledge; if they are still warmer than adjacent stone after one full contraction of the plateau (≈ 27 h) they are legally yours. If not, withdraw quietly—anything cooler has already been inherited by future generations.  
To obtain fire, perform the basalt peel: press your palm until epidermis bonds (count 300 pulsebeats, deduct 3 for every regret that surfaces). Peel swiftly—pain is the interest the plateau charges for light. The coin of fire cooks one meal, then fractures; grind the shards between molars and swallow to avoid leaving evidence.  
Currency: stories of weather. Children trade cloud-eggs in a ritual circle; adults stand outside the perimeter, palms open. Offer a memory of precipitation—anyone’s, anywhere—spoken inside the mouth only. Over-enunciation causes drizzle; shortage produces drought inside personal biographies.

6. Non-Lethal Fauna Encounters  
Basalt cricket on your bootlace: do not shake off. The weight is exactly that of one unspoken apology you owe. Keep it until it leaves; the apology goes with it.  
Velvet mite procession: freeze. Count legs (should be 8 × width of procession). If a ninth column appears you have counted wrong and must start again before moving; otherwise you trespass on their commute and will wake one morning paved into the hexagonal pavement, still alive but speaking only in the colour scarlet.  
Silence leopard sighting: become the sound you most dislike in the world. Hold it internally until your heartbeat disguises itself as that noise. When the leopard closes its beak it has accepted your camouflage; open your eyes and it will be grooming itself with your abandoned impatience.  
Plateau martin semaphore: if a bird flashes white-under-feather at you, answer by exposing the soft part of your wrist for exactly four breaths. You are accepting delivery of tomorrow’s wind; failure to receive causes travel delays of up to three centuries after you leave.

7. Sleeping, or the Lack Thereof  
Dreaming is forbidden to tourists; dreams here migrate into the cloud ocean and become weather elsewhere. Before closing your eyes, hum the exact frequency of your home shower plumbing—this anchors unconscious thought to a plumbing system incapable of condensation.  
Recommended position: supine, soles together, knees flat on stone. The plateau will attempt a gentle suction. Do not resist—count one millimetre of descent per decade of your life; at that depth rest. In the morning a skin-petal of basalt will have formed over you; crack it along the femur line and step out renewed. Take the shell with you: it is your return ticket (see §11).

8. Personal Soundtrack (Mufflers, Ear-Politics)  
Carry two cotton plugs soaked in childhood bathwater. Insert when homesickness begins to vocalise; homesickness is the only thing loud enough here to attract geology.  
If you must listen to anything, let it be your digestive hydraulics—amplified through the ceramic dish of a dwarf lichen-tree. Place one shin against trunk; sound conducts through accreted salts. One full chorus equals eight hours of human conversation saved for re-entry.  
Music players will be confiscated and turned into sediment. Do not risk.

9. Altitude Sickness of the Spirit  
Symptoms: memories arranged alphabetically, compulsive urge to name rocks, sensation of owning an echo.  
Remedy: locate a columnar joint wide enough to swallow your shoulders. Insert body; exhale until personal history stops scrolling. When the clicking of obsidian pebbes synchronises with your pulse, back out. Side effect: you will briefly remember the future of every pebble; forget quickly.

10. Voluntary Mineralisation (Legal Suicide, But Polite)  
If you decide to stay forever, follow the procedures:  
a) Announce nothing. Desire is noisy.  
b) Choose a warm seam (test: spit—evaporation in < 2 s = suitable).  
c) Lie face-down, arms at sides, toes pointed so the plateau knows you are finished travelling.  
d) Breathe out for the last time; do not breathe in again—plateau will provide interstitial oxygen directly to coronary muscle for 11 min, easing transition.  
e) Within a century you will be basement; within two, folklore; by the third you will have become an inconvenient step in somebody’s kitchen-air. Tourist visas are single-entry: mineralisation voids return.  

11. Extraction / Going Home  
Wait until the basalt skin fragment from your first sleep warms to exactly blood heat. Hold it against the lip of the plateau’s western rim. A west wind (recognisable because it carries the smell of last century) will lift you. Do not look down; the cloud ocean is proprietary about faces.  
Re-entry to nominal physics is abrupt: at 3 000 ft the fragment will explode into ordinary sound. You will regain weight, heartbeat, echo. Normal conversations will seem like shouting through fire. Spend three days whispering inside a closet lined with towels before attempting cities.

12. Aftermath / Customs in Your Origin Universe  
Declare nothing. The lint in your pockets is now time-stained; declare zero. Officers will search for obsidian—if found they will keep it, and you will lose internal clicking rhythm, condemning you to walk slightly behind your own footsteps forever.  
Recommended souvenir: a single droplet that was inside the basalt lattice. Transport in the dark of mouth. When you miss the plateau, swallow half; the remaining half will evaporate in sympathetic synchrony with whatever crying stone exists wherever you are, and you will hear—briefly—nothing at all.

If you read this entire manual aloud you have already disqualified yourself—sound of this length attracts absence. Read in eye-movements only, then burn the paper, swallow the ash, book passage.
