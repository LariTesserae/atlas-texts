VISITOR BRIEFING – PLATEAU 1.8  
(Common designation used by the 3-Dozen Guild; locals leave the plateau unnamed and converse in ribbon-thought instead.)

0. THUMBNAIL  
Altitude 1.8 km but barometric are 1.1 – 1.3 km, so the air is only 13 % thinner than sea-level standard, but coolant-cold and bone-dry. Day-long civil twilight; night is a dimmer switch, not an off switch. Vegetation photosynthesises under a lead-coloured sky; machinery photosynthesises too – both run on condensation, capillary electricity and trace CO₂ exhaled by travellers. Noise is a currency: spend too much and the biome sends you an invoice in the form of arrested motion.  
Gravity is negotiable; capillarity is sovereign.

2. LOCAL PHYSICS & ETIQUETTE  
2.1 Sound < 35 dB – biome default. Clearing your throat = 55 dB; expect a 3-second hush in which every leaf locks. Shouting (> 70 dB) can stall flywheels for a full day – the Guild fines members up to 50 kSel for “industrial time theft.”  
2.2 Water vectors  
Droplets climb skyward 06:00–12:00, descend 12:00–18:00, hover dusk–dawn. Do not spit, cry, or sweat wastefully. The standard greeting is to offer your glove so another can harvest the condensate from your fingertips.  
2.3 Gravity quid  
Capillary lift ≈ 0.04 g. A filled 1-litre bottle feels 40 g lighter; if you uncap it the water may crawl up the side and leave you. Keep lids on.  
2.4 Vegetal traffic law  
Paths open only while footfall CO₂ > 400 ppm. Walk single file; keep breathing. If you hold your breath the fronds reseal and you must machete your way out – which counts as a noise felony.  
2.5 Machine etiquette  
Engines can be politely requested (sub-sonic hum at 60 Hz) to pause; they often comply if you graft a vine ring onto their drive-shaft as payment. Do NOT remove sap once set – the plant and motor are now married under local common law.

3. ARRIVAL WALK-THROUGH  
Step 1 – Cliff-edge acoustic check: ring basalt with tuning fork; if note sustains > 4 s stone is cool enough to bear weight.  
Step 2 – Ribbon-note deposit: tie intention-band onto handrail (“I seek the Rose-mouth #4 furnace, return before dim-down”). Future travellers will know not to follow if they read the edge frayed.  
Step 3 – Descent: keep footfalls 0.9 s apart; the succulents need that micro-breath between impacts.  
Step 4 – Mid-terrace condensation top-up: open glove valves, brush railing, capillary water into hip-flask (legal).  
Step 5 – Industrial zone: approach furnaces from leeward so chromium steam coats you evenly; prevents sap-adhesion when vines embrace machinery later.  
Step 6 – Fauna watch: if mirrored quadruped appears, record nothing. Nod, exhale once, move laterally (never forward) until reflection axis breaks.  

4. MEDICAL & SURVIVAL NOTES  
Altitude sickness is rare (see above) but capillary crawl can dehydrate you at 250 ml h⁻¹ without visible sweat. Drink 100 ml every 30 min even if not thirsty.  
Photosynthetic vertigo: sky glare + emerald strobe may trigger rolling nausea. Goggles set to “4-stop ND” mode or fix gaze on basalt grey.  
Sap lock: if chrome sap lands on skin it oxidises and sets like enamel. Remove within 90 s using sucrose crystals; disaccharide breaks organo-silicate lattice.  
Silence debt: if you breach 35 dB budget the plateau debits time rather than money. You will find yourself walking, but the sun does not move. Carry tuning-fork and ring once; resonance tells how many minutes the biome has subtracted – head for exit immediately.  

5. EXPORT RESTRICTIONS  
You may leave with: 100 ml personal condensate, one ribbon-note (if woven by you), photographic memories (camera images auto-erase at rim).  
You may NOT leave with: mirror-sliver fauna hairs, rose-coal embers, unfused machine parts, succulent spores (security gate is a wind-vine; it confiscates contraband by growing through your luggage).

6. GO HOME SIGNAL  
When the sky-dial touches pewter-minus-2 (a shade darker than pewter, lighter than charcoal) begin ascent; capillary flow reverses in 42 min and the terraces flood upward. Linger and you will ascend whether you want to – carried by 3 cm sheet of climbing water, like it or not. Back at rim, strike basalt once, wait for echo. If echo answers with your own foot-jingle you are cleared for multiverse departure. Step through, and do not speak until the gate closes; voices carry refunds.  

Remember: the plateau is not quiet because it is empty. It is quiet because it is listening.
