   c. Blood-gas replacement

   d. Gut purge of chlorophyll

4. Navigational Protocols  
   Step cadence: two steps per four-minute cycle, never on the exhale half. Mark time by the springiness underfoot; each sigh-bounce is one second.  
   Fauna yielding: if a phoneme-creature perches on you, hum its exact body shape back at it (larynphase tuner helps). It mistakes you for a mirror and departs.  
   Map-drinking etiquette: catch only one upward droplet per foray; second dose fragments prior cartography into atonal fugue, leaving you circling forever.  
   Mediastinum jump: only when you cannot remember your own birthday. The vacuum of absolute silence gauges identity; less luggage equals softer egress.

5. Emergency Procedures  
   – Out-of-phase exhalation swarm: drop to knees, empty lungs, force swallow motion; negative thoracic pressure tricks bats into entering your chest. Cough once on next inhale, spewing them back out disoriented.

   – Black-ice fingerprint theft: mitts already prevent it, but if bare skin touched, speak a deliberate lie immediately. The lung records only truth-prints; lies overwrite, erasing you from its requiem schedule.

6. Return & Quarantine  
   Exit at end of your fourth cycle (lung breathes you out when ready—do NOT crawl back early; hilum will guillotine).  
   Surface silence will feel like screaming. Wear auricle-plugs for 36 h.  
   Every 6 h you will still hear a basso whisper of your own name. Record it; when waveform decays below –9 dB you are officially evacuated. Until then, remain in anechoic chamber; the echo can attract local fauna through interdimensional harmonics if you’re around reflective walls.  
   Refrain from singing for three lunar months; residual phonemes could bud in your shower stall and birth a miniature lung—bad for plumbing.

7. Philosophical Consent Clause  
   Understand: the organ is not evil, sentient, or even alive in any registry your species accepts. It is a process—exhalation folding stone, inhalation devouring light. You are not exploring it; you are auditioning to be its next unresolved syllable. Pack accordingly, breathe politely, and may your empty spaces be harmonious.
