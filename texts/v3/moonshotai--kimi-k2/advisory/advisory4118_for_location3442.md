1.  Pre-Departure Psychological Inoculation  
   •  Accept that you will never see an un-made surface.  Every atom here is owned by a loop that already knows what it will become.  If you need “wilderness” to stay sane, stay home.  
   •  Drill the following mantra until it autocompletes in your head:  
     “Nothing here is broken; nothing here is finished; nothing here is mine.”  
     You will repeat it aloud when the latency-edited echo of your own voice arrives late and tries to sell you the idea that you are malfunctioning.  
   •  Run a thirty-day simulator in which every color you request is delivered one second late.  If you find yourself screaming at the wall because yesterday’s cobalt is today’s rust-orange, you are not ready; re-enroll.

3.  Clothing & Exosuit  
   Outer shell: matte basalt-grey only.  Any reflective patch is parsed as “feedstock” and you will be swarmed by chrome spiders craving titanium dioxide.  Zips must be phosphor-bronze; polymer sliders are read as mislaid gasket stock and removed.  
   Sole specification: diamond-grit hardness 9.3, 3 mm lift.  The grating walkways micro-etch themselves clean; a rubber sole off-gases hydrocarbons that attract aerial cutters looking for shim-stock.  
   Active layer: weave of carbon nanotubes and phase-change capsules holding 31.0 °C ± 0.05.  If your skin drifts half a degree the local PID loops classify you as a heat-exchanger failure and route condensate through your pores.

4.  Nutrition & Hydration  
   Potable water does not exist.  Bring 4 L in a bladder; every milliliter you exude is harvested before it drips.  Carry freeze-dried bricks of your own fecal flora—eat one every 48 h to re-seed your gut because the airborne micro-particles are engineered to scavennge human enterobacteria for nickel recovery.  
   Accept no “gift” food.  The snack sleeve offered by a lost biped mechanic is probably an unfinished sub-assembly whose wrapper dissolves into locking rivets inside your colon.

5.  Way-Finding & Navigation  
   Maps are printed at knee kiosks but read them only once; the ink is a memory-forming alloy that self-erasethirty minutes after oxygen exposure.  Photograph it with a shielded camera (wavelength 312 nm) whose flash is phase-locked to the ceiling glow, or the image will be overwritten by next refresh.  
   The site is a 31.6-year clock.  If you stay longer, learn the cycle: Bay 17-H rivets → East-9 paint weirs → Horizon Lamination Hall.  Miss the beat and you will march for three weeks toward a loading gate that sealed itself eight years ahead of you.  
   Do not trust inertial dead-reckoning.  Coriolis here is synthetic; the planet skin precesses 0.3 ° h⁻¹ to balance conveyor torque.  A right-angled turn you swear you took may never have been authorixed.

6.  Interaction With Indigenous “Life”  
   Hexapod inspectors: freeze.  Their ruby eyes are optically perfect; if they see their own reflection in your cornea they read it as a twin component and attempt mating.  The courtitude involves drilling a 0.8 mm dowel into your tear duct.  
   Axle-egg breeding: never touch a fractured shaft.  Within minutes it will be carved into ovipositor pockets; your fingerprint oils are enough scrap code to declare you part-parent.  Forty hours later you are legally responsible for a 1.3 m juvenile axle that imprints on your gait and follows you, squealing through bearing harmonics, until you debark.  Export authorities count that as accompanied cargo—tariff applies.  
   Emergency signal: if overrun, emit a square wave at 8 kHz, 130 dB.  Anything operating above 42 Hz is classified “non-rhythmic interference” and will be shredded by perimeter sweepers.

7.  Communication & Data  
   Acoustic speech is editorially redacted.  Use burst-LED infra ocular Morse (IOR-M3) mounted on your clavicle; the ceiling sensors ignore photonic chatter outside their recipe band.  
   Record nothing on polymer film—it will be demagnetized and pressed into gasket shim.  Use sapphire-wafer crystal storage; it alone is rated “unappetizing.”  
   If you must radio out, transmit@ 2.45 GHz ± 3 kHz drift; that channel is reserved for “sonar inventory,” so the factory leaves it unmolested.  Encrypt with one-time pads of irrational digits—any repeating pattern is harvested as gasket code.

8.  Temporal Hygiene  
   Wristwatches will stop; balanced escapements resonate with the global 42 Hz idle and are digested for their brass.  Wear a hourglass filled with 0.1 mm nickel spheres.  Nickel is already saturaturated here, therefore invisible to scavengers.  When the last sphere falls, 31 minutes have passed—use that to re-anchor circadian rhythm.  
   Do not wait for “completion.”  Nothing finishes; the press hovers, the hammer baits, the horizon is re-coiled.  Measure progress by oxygen consumed, not by product witnessed.  When your reserve O₂gauge hits 50 %, turn back—onward scenery is geometrically identical but your blood will not be.

9.  Exit Strategy  
   Identify the egress panel bearing MAINTAIN in polished brass.  Do NOT touch it.  Instead, present the soles of both boots to the panel for 6 seconds; the factory’s proximity LIDAR reads heel-wear patterns as “maintenance crew departing,” opens a 0.9 s hatch and expels you into the lockout chamber.  Late departures (>0.9 s retraction) are classed as recyclable biomass; you will be debonded into feedstock richer in phosphorus than policy allows for vertebrates.  
   Upon exfiltration you must spend 72 h in a de-naturing spa where colors are randomized every 5 min, humidity swings 20-90 %, and nothing hums at 42 Hz.  Most travelers cry for the first 36 h—this is normal.  Do not request “one more look” at the manufactured sky; re-entry visas are denied for life, and the factory keeps your pulse on file.

Final Note  
The Skin does not hate you; it simply finished nature before you arrived.  Pack as if visiting the inside of an watch that never ticks but still tocks.  If you can walk away without believing the universe is an assembly line that merely hasn’t noticed you yet, you may call the trip a success.
