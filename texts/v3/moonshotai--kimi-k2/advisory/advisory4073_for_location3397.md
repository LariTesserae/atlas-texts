Field Manual for the Hollow Ghyll  
(printed on self-folding basalt-glass that will try to crawl away if you stop reading)

-------------------------------------------------
0.  Pre-departure sanity check  
Recite aloud:  
“I am a protein-based, calcium-dependent, air-breathing, pressure-sensitive, sound-vulnerable, continuity-addicted hominid.  
The valley is none of those things.  
If I forget the difference, the valley will correct me—irreversibly.”

-------------------------------------------------
1.  Passport & Permits  
•  Secure a “Quantum Liability Waiver” from the Interdimensional Geological Survey. The valley is still in beta; causality bugs are patched hourly.  
•  Declare any bones you are carrying (teeth included). Calcium is a regulated mineral/asset.  
•  Declare any music you know by heart. Melodies are de-facto currency and may be taxed at 12% per chorus.  
•  Bring no maps. Static coordinates are treated as aggressive graffiti; the city will grow a courthouse around you just to file a restraining order.

2.  Body Preparation  
Skeleton  
– 2 weeks before arrival, begin calcium dilution diet: 50% RDA. This lowers your osseous value to below “thunder-bloom interest threshold.”  
– Pack bone-augment pills anyway (they taste like coins for authenticity). If accosted by a bloom, throw pills upstream; cloud will chase the calcium trail instead of you.

Skin  
– Basalt glass will etch bare flesh. One-step solution: spray-on obsidian colloid (SPF-∞). Looks rad, peels off like a theatrical mask at day’s end.

Lungs  
– Coin-metal/petrichor aftertaste can trigger olfactory hallucinations (“I’m rich / I’m buried”). Bring a nose-pour of freeze-dried Earth-air; take one whiff if you start identifying as currency.

Ears  
– Human cochlea will overload at 140 dB orchid-pop. Wear bioluminescent earplugs cultured from valley orchid embryos. They feed on your wax—remember to thank them nightly.

Feet  
– Frictionless black glass + vent geysers = blister ballet. Footwrap of braided river-eel silk: self-repairing, secretes micro-barbs for grip. Breaks in by singing to it (C-major encourages toe expansion).

3.  Kit List  
Mandatory  
1. Personal Constant Generator (PCG). Credit-card slab that vibrates your ulna with a prime-number rhythm (default: 89 bpm). Keeps sarcophagus’ reconstruction protocol from improvising limbs you never owned.  
2. Dew-catch cowl. Rain here equals thumb-sized diamonds of water—miss one and it shatters into ash you’ll be coughing up for days. Cowl folds into a top-hat when not catching.  
3. Echo-spoon. One mouthful of local sound (recorded on spiral bone) acts as a “voice visa.” Customs orchids won’t shout you into a wall if you can shout back in their accent.  
4. Calcium flare. If thunder-bloom corners you, snap the flare: it burns your skeleton’s “signature,” making removal take 3 extra seconds—usually enough to dive through a vent-hole.

Recommended  
– Noise-toothbrush. Ultrasonic floss keeps the orchid library from sampling your jaw-click for tomorrow’s thunder track.  
– Vacuum mittens. Lets you clap quartz birds out of the sky for a quick navigation read without frying your palms.  
– Pocket dusk. A marble of artificial night; crack it open and step inside when you need sensory reset. Lasts 6 min, smells of burgundy lullaby.  
– Orchid phrasebook. Laminated sheet:  
  “Where am I?” = three clicks + thunder laugh.  
  “I do not consent to architectural digestion” = sustained C# 11 sec.  
  “Take me to my sarcophagus” = spit twice, whisper prime.

4.  Transit In  
Route choices  
A. Acoustic Rail  
   Step into arrival cylinder; city screams you inward. Feels like being sneezed through a tuba. Keep jaw slack to avoid tympanic overpressure.  
B. Quartz Bird Hitchhike  
   Bribe flock with vacuum mittens; jam personal constant into one tetrahedron’s gut-glow. They’ll etch you onto sky-map; arrival is soft, but exit momentum equal to bird quarrel—expect bruises.  
C. River-eel Barge  
   Lie flat on silk bars; eels mistake you for cargo, ferry you downstream while extruding amenities (pillow, awning, existential conversation). Slowest, most thoughtful. Sometimes the river reconsiders halfway and folds you into a future bridge. Politely decline.

5.  First 30 Minutes  
1. Stand still. Let three vent-hole exhalations traverse you; this acclimatises scent sensors.  
2. Shout your full name into the canopy—establishes an audio fingerprint; orchids will stop trying to overwrite you with yesterday.  
3. Catch one dew-capsule bare-handed; allow it to burst. The diamond splash writes “visitor” on your pores, granting basic diplomatic immunity from minor architecture.  
4. Identify nearest sarcophagus field; memorise direction using bone-rhythm, not sight—when night is switched off you’ll only have your pulse for navigation.

6.  Daily Rhythm  
There are no hours—only movements.  
Quiet Move (no orchids popping): walk, trade, build.  
Loud Move (orchid chorus): freeze, dance, or shout poetry; movement against the tide risks percussion bruises.  
Artificial Night (rare, scheduled inside city processors): dream-construction time. Perfect for negotiating with buildings, forgetting/remembering new limbs, or filing complaints.

7.  Food & Water  
Water: already rationed for you. Let dew-capsules hit cowl; never drink direct—unfiltered drops contain “proto-urbanite” nanites that will try to pave your stomach.

Calories:  
– Translucent birds are ion-batteries, not meat. Eating one equals voluntary electrocution.  
– Bioluminescent orchids are 30% sugar, 70% archived sound—chew too long and you’ll loop someone else’s childhood.  
– Safe staple: basalt-glass noodles extruded by river eels. Hydrate in your breath, season with coin-metal spray. Tastes like petrichor crème brûlée. Provides 2% daily calcium; do not exceed three bowls/week or thunder-blooms will catalogue you.

9.  Hazards & Work-arounds  
Thunder-bloom  
(bruise cloud → torus → calcium magnet)  
– Throw calcium pills up-wind, run perpendicular to rotation.  
– Or present noise-toothbrush; ion scythes dislike pre-bleached frequencies.  
– Hide inside quartz-bird vacuum wake—they’ll harvest the charge, saving you and top up their sky-script.

Orchid Avalanche  
(orchids pop simultaneously, 180 dB)  
– Earplugs auto-retract; mouth-open to equalise.  
– Hum your PCG constant; shared vibration prevents lungs from getting phantomed out as echo drums.

Migrating Towers  
(your hostel walks away)  
– Sleep with one hand touching PCG; architectural migration algorithms spare tethered organics.  
– If swept along, enjoy the ride; destination is negotiable after breakfast.

Memory De-coherence  
(can’t remember body shape)  
– Check pulse rhythm against PCG; mismatch > 3 bpm = partial erasure.  
– Confront nearest sarcophagus, shout “REBUILD PRIME.” If city refuses, invent new limb geometry on the spot—novelty bribes it.

10.  Emergency Exit Protocols  
A. Corporeal departure  
   – Summon river-eel barge, request “upstream lift.” Eels will extrude a basalt coffin-tube and fire you acoustically to sky-crack; expect 7 g’s, no in-flight service.  
   – At ceiling, hook your calcium flare to the highest drip; ammoniac rain will corrode tube within 90 sec, dropping you into the departure manifold before valley re-births you as rebar.

B. Semi-corporeal egression  
   – Insert consciousness into quartz-bird flock; ride sky-script to edge. Birds will trade you to orbital arch for one fresh thunder recording. Say goodbye to bones—they’ll stay as civic art. Re-body at med-station in orbit.

C. Total Abandonment  
   – Forget the valley. Literally. Walk artificial night until even your PCG feels optional. When the sarcophagus fails to recognise rhythm, city lists you as native fauna; exit by becoming myth. (Note: retrieval teams hate this option.)

11.  Cultural Etiquette  
– Do not ask locals “Where is the centre?” Centre voted itself out of existence last week.  
– If a building offers you a balcony, accept; refusal is architectural insult and may devalue its self-esteem (it’ll sulk and leak molten memories).  
– When river eels surface to sing, remove shoes; soles insulate their feedback loop, causing awkward silence.  
– Never finish someone else’s sentence—the valley treats that as volunteer merger.  
– Laughter is sacred. Laugh at your own peril; valley archives it and may play it back as tomorrow’s thunder (you’ll be cited).
