## 1. Pre-departure Health Checks  
**Thermoregulation**  
- Core temp ≤ 37 °C? Good. Expect external heat ≈ 38–41 °C with ~100 % supersaturated air.  
- Anyone with cystic-fibrosis-grade sweating disorders, hypertension, or heart arrhythmia = mandatory downgrade to VR-only visit.  

**Skin integrity**  
- Open cuts will macerate within minutes and never dry. Dermaseal spray before entry is mandatory; carry two reloads.  

**Inoculations**  
- Pan-fungal prophylaxis (shots 1-3-5) 10 days pre-travel.  
- Broad-spectrum antiprotozoal tabs (take 1 the morning of drop). The place is aerosol-spore heaven.

## 5. Behavioural Protocols  
**Pace:** nothing over 2 km/h—fast movement sets up air-eddies that knock "slow-drops" off their filaments; locals regard a pelting as an aggressive act and the birds will helicopter-cough fermented berries onto you.  
**Speech:** ≤ 30 dB; whisper-level. The hollow’s ambient noise floor is 10 dB—any louder snaps the stretched silence membrane and everything stops moving (including, rumor says, your intestinal peristalsis—unconfirmed, not fun to test).  
**Photography:** electronic global-shutter only, no mechanical click. Disable AF-assist lamp.  
**Sampling rule:** snip ≤ 1 cm² vegetal tissue per specimen. Wound-seal with provided wax stick; the system treats unsealed cuts as "leaves to be shed" and will speed-grow scar tissue around you—last tourist was cemented up to the knee before extraction.  
**Biolum-beetle etiquette:** Watch their flash grammar—triple pulse = curiosity; single sustained = ownership claim on whatever surface they lit. If one lands on you and goes solid-on, freeze 90 s, then rotate so it can hop onto a leaf. Brushing it off is "resource denial" and can bring the whole colony; combined mass ≈ 200 g of chitin razor-shell wings – unpleasant.

## 6. Contingencies  
**Heartspot vertigo:** If you feel your heartbeat echoing visually, sit cross-legged, tongue on roof of mouth, four-count box-breathing 5 s/5 s/5 s/5 s—proven to keep rate within 55-65 BPM and reduce apparent time lag enough to walk out.  
**Root-duct flash-flood (cycle day 68-75):** trees "urinate". Water rises ankle-deep within minutes. Keep boots on, do NOT try to collect—it is laden with allelopathic tannins; drinking causes projectile emesis and voids the humidity equilibrium for every organism within 5 m (they will mob you). Move to Heartspot’s raised feather berm; stay on the dead-bird mat—its keratin layer acts as a hydrophobic raft.  
**Orientation:** The exit vector is ALWAYS straight up-slope gradient; humidity will decrease 0.3 g/m³ per metre climbed; clip hygrometer to chest strap, watch Δ-2 g change → you are within 15 m of the rim. Do NOT rely on beetle-lights; during "wash-down" hours they’re off.  
**Group separation:** Snap red chem-light once every 60 s; partner answers with two clicks of straw-filter mouthpiece—distinct ceramic clack carries 30 m in the hollow’s hush without busting noise etiquette.
