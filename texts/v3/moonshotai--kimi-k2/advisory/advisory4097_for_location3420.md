This place is a forge impersonating a garden.  
Treat it as you would treat a drop forge that has forgotten to stop.

Preerved here is a field-tested protocol compiled by the “Quiet Forge” chapter of the Interdimensional Amateur Mountaineering Club. Amendments welcome; none have yet returned to propose any.

------------------------------------------------
1. PSYCHOLOGICAL INOCULATION
------------------------------------------------
A. Accept the contradiction  
   – You will stand in a room-sized crater on a planet-sized lathe.  
   – You will feel simultaneously microscopic and in the way of a cosmological mill.  
   – Repeat hourly: “I am neither workpiece nor operator, only unauthorised spectator.”

B. Time vertigo prophylaxis  
   Before arrival watch a metronome tick for three uninterrupted hours while a day-lapse of rust forming plays on a second screen. Do not look away. This recalibrrates blinking to geological tempo and prevents the “slow-motion hallucination cascade” that leaves most visitors believing their companions have turned to statuary.

C. Vocabulary cull  
   Remove the spoken word “water” from active language. Tape a reminder inside your cheek if necessary. The mirror-orchid’s ontic erasure is irreversible; dehydrated travellers who lost the concept had to request “transparent breathable hydrogen-oxide substrate” from rescue teams, usually after their throats cracked.

------------------------------------------------
2. PHYSICAL KIT
------------------------------------------------
A. Outer envelope  
   – One-piece aluminised proximity suit (rated 600 °C short-term, 400 °C continuous). Nickel-iron walls radiate 320 °C infrared; air brushing them sheds 160 °C convection. You will be braised without contact.  
   – Ceramic-soled overboots with replaceable 2 mm beryon felt innersoles; iron floor is still plastic on the geological scale and micro-welds to lesser footwear.  
   – Phase-change micro-pack cooling vest charged at –20 °C; provides 4 h latent buffering before the mountain decides you are working material.

B. Respiration  
   – Closed-cycle rebreather filled with xenon-buffered 14 % O₂ mix. Local atmosphere is 2 % CO, 8 % SO₂, 0.3 % Hg vapour and wants to alloy with alveoli.  
   – Mouth-tube voice diaphragm removed; prevents tongue contact with mercury laced mist and keeps the “word-loss” phenomenon from hitching a ride on a vowel.

C. Optics  
   – Dual-spectrum goggles: shade 14 arc-welding glass layer plus liquid-crystal fast-dark filter. Bowl glows at 900 nm; expect photic nausea if unshielded.  
   – Optional: pinhole camera on 3 m carbon wand. Observe the mirror-orchid indirectly; direct stare sometimes swaps the observer and the reflected potential-you.

D. Nutrition / hydration  
   – 24 h osmotic water bladder worn under suit. Hydrant tube pierces cheek; keep it between molars to reclaim evaporated breath before it snows away.  
   – Caloric paste sachets rated 40 °C below ambient chewing threshold; chocolate chips are forbidden—they melt, drip, hiss, subtract themselves from reality.

E. Utilities  
   – 30 m refractory tether terminated with molly-anchor; hammer into a swirl-mark groove. Bowl forgets you only while you remain within; anchor postpones erasure.  
   – Pocket-sized hourglass running 42 min (synchronise with liana pulse). When the final grain falls the mercury vein contracts—note dizziness, possible gravity echo.  
   – Chalk of 100 % cubic zirconia; mark cooling iron if you must leave gear behind—carbon “disappears,” silicates irritate the strata enough to remain legible.

------------------------------------------------
3. MISSION RULES
------------------------------------------------
1. Maximum dwell: 72 h local. Longer induces irreversible “scaling”: retinas begin to see tool marks on everything including air, resulting in suicide or career change to machinist.  
2. Minimum party size: two. Should one companion begin to “anneal” (skin acquires milling grooving) the other must yank tether and recompress at 200 kPa in a cooled crate.  
3. No instruments recording electro-magnetic data below 7 Hz; those are the gears’ copyright. A previous grad student tried; his thesis now contains only blank pages and smells of ozone.  
4. If the heart-fruit fibrillates twice in sixty minutes, evacuate: efficiency is spiking, cathedral space shrinking. Bowl can close like a hydraulic valve.

------------------------------------------------
4. SCHEDULE OF OBSERVATIONS
------------------------------------------------
Hr 0   – Anchor tether. Note liana height; initial reading calibrates local “metabolised metres” ratio.  
Hr 0-2 – Glass-reed recital. Record pitch; any minor third deviation pre-shadows indigo script. Collect NOTHING—ampoule shards bite through kaizen gauntlets.  
Hr 2-6 – Lichen watch. Wear polarised goggles at ×50 zoom; watch for carry-bit propagation along copper buses. One full adder ripple equals 0.6 µ°C drop—log it.  
Hr 6-8 – Mirror-orchid potential census. Stand one body-length outside reflection perimeter; view through periscope. Count silhouettes that look back; if count equals your party, abort.  
Hr 8-12 – Sleep (yes). Rebreather muffles gear-hiss; dreamless unconscious best counteracts time-fold vertigo.  
Hr 12-36 – Rotate observation posts; do NOT speak. Maintain silence, even at 18 dB the cathedral registers you as chatter.  
Hr 36-42 – Heart-fruit logging; keep palm of gauntlet 5 cm above surface—feel air tightening. Palpate your own pulse; when they synchronise, step back 1 m.  
Hr 42-48 – Mercury vein again; synchronise activities with 42-min clock.  
Hr 48-60 – Prepare egress; chalk final position, coil slow, walk backwards paying out tether—minimises erasure shear.  
Hr 60-72 – De-radiate in buffer tent; hydrate (remember: “transparent hydrogen-oxide substrate”), submit memory log to expedition archivist while still believable.

------------------------------------------------
5. CONTINGENCIES
------------------------------------------------
A. The orchid shows double reflection (your face plus someone behind you).  
   – Close eyes, spin 180°, open. If face is still there, fire extinguisher-grade xenon burst to overload reflection; flee along tether.

B. Breath-snow bounce refuses to sublimate, piles into ankle-deep drifts.  
   – Mountain gearing is chilling. Increase suit thermostat 30 °C, retreat—bowl becoming cold enough to accept you as permanent tooling.

C. You lose a syllable (cannot pronounce a word you know you knew).  
   – Write concept on zirconia slate with provided gallium ink. Hold before orchid; let mirrored version take it; step away. Do NOT speak it aloud during transfer—causes feedback loop, previously erased hikers returned minus faces.

------------------------------------------------
6. EXIT & DECONTAMINATION
------------------------------------------------
Back at the embarkation blister:  
- Immerse suit in 80 °C 5 % acetic bath; nickel-iron dust fizzes off as meteories.  
- Shower 2 hrs 37 °C potable water (term permitted here), re-learning pronunciation of “water” with speech therapist. Average recovery 3 days.  
- Submit yourself to 200 kPa oxygen for 6 h; prevents CO poly-cyanation.  
- Don’t be alarmed if technicians greet you with a question you were formulating—lingering loops sometimes leak.

------------------------------------------------
7. PHILOSOPHICAL ADDENDUM
------------------------------------------------
You will return convinced every landscape is merely unfinished; every garden only a factory choosing chlorophyll tooling. Let the feeling pass: visit a normal lake, endure the mosquitos, relearn that entropy sometimes merely rusts, not machines. Otherwise you will end up founding a cult of mill-belts, or worse, become an author.

Safe travels; the cathedral of gearing does not need parishioners, only witnesses patient enough to leave before the coin-height increment of the mountain seals their boots in original iron.
