Field Briefing: Visiting the Hollow Verdance  
(what they never put in the guild pamphlet)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
0.  First, decide if you really want this
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The Verdance is not spectacular; it is sub-audible.  
If you need awe, go watch a super-nova.  
If you need proof that the world can pause, keep reading.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  Paperwork & Permission
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The Quiet Keepers issue one brass token per lunar month.  
Apply in writing, on unbleached linen rag, with a quill you carved yourself.  
Ink must be walnut-based – iron gall scratches and they will hear it.  
No seal, no signature: bite your thumb and press.  
They will smell whether you mean silence.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2.  Medical: becoming quiet flesh
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A.  Heart  
    – Start diuretic tea (hawthorn, motherwort) three days before.  
    – Practise 4-7-8 breathing: four counts in, seven hold, eight out.  
    – Target resting pulse ≤ 48 bpm. If you cannot, the crawl-space will teach you.

B.  Ears  
    – Have wax plugs moulded; coat outer canal with silicone oil so jaw clicks don’t transmit.  
    – Optional: stapedius muscle botox (grey-market, 50 €). Lasts six weeks, removes self-voice by 12 dB.

C.  Gut  
    – 48 h liquid diet: oat milk, clarified broth, charcoal tablets.  
    – Final 12 h absolute fasting – peristalsis is thunder down there.

D.  Skin  
    – Trimmer, not razor; micro-stubble stops the plastic rustle of waxed coats.  
    – File nails round; varnish with clear shellac so edges won’t whisper.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3.  Clothing checklist – everything must pass the “drop-test”
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Drop the garment from 30 cm. If you hear it, burn it.

Layer 1 – Skin  
Frotté silk, seams outside, tags removed.

Layer 2 – Insulation  
Unwoven milkweed down pressed between two sheets of bacterian cellulose; no quilting, only spot-welded dots.

Layer 3 – Vapour  
Monolithic PTFE film, matte side out (gloss can squeak).

Layer 4 – Contact  
Oversuit of fine birch paper laminated to spider-silk felt; treated with lanolin against rot.  
Hood integrates the ear-plug lanyard so nothing dangles.

Footbags, not socks+shoes: two-piece felt shells with birch-tar sole, tensioned by a single silk drawstring – no buckles.

Gloves: kid skin, fingertips excised; you will need bare touch to locate the crawl-gate.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4.  Tools & souvenirs you may (quietly) carry
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- 1 palm-sized sheet of mica already warmed to body temp – to signal distress without words.  
- A 5 ml ceramic vial for one drop of anti-gravity water. It will collapse when exposed to dry air; keep it sealed inside your cheek until above ground, then spit into a humidity tent if you want the physics lab to see what you stole.  
- Writing kit: mushroom-paper, carbon stylus. No pens.  
- No light, no watches (ticking). Let the perpetual afternoon be your only clock.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5.  Route description (above → below)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Day 0 dusk: present token at the cooperage on Dripgut Lane.  
Monk gestures: index and middle fingers split, pointing down – the root-sign for “you may descend”.

They roll back a floorboard. The hole is 22 cm × 40 cm – exactly coffin-lid size. You enter head-first, exhaling. Two metres in, the shaft kinks vertical; gravity forgets you. Lower yourself on braided hemp so old it has petrified into wood; it will creak only once – forgive it.

At 4 m depth you meet the humidity inversion: a cold jelly that slithers into lungs. Let the diaphragm flutter – don’t cough.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.  Behavioural protocol inside
--------------------------------------------------------------------
a)  Posture  
    Lie supine, arms at 45°. Do not curl; fetal posture squeaks clothing.  
    Let plant mattress write Braille on your back – decode later, silently.

b)  Breathing etiquette  
    Match the chamber’s 6-second cycle: inhale 2 s, exhale 4 s. Tides of CO₂ sync with root-respiration; you become an anonymous node in the graph.

c)  Vision  
    Keep eyes half-lidded; full aperture causes reflex tears that patter when they land – an intolerable noise.

d)  Photography  
    No shutters, no drones. If you must record, bring a glass-plate camera pre-cocked; damp the mirror with silicone. Even so, the Keepers will confiscate plates on exit. Memory is the only legal film here.

e)  Conversation  
    Forbidden, but thoughts continue. The trick is to think in equations or topo-maps; avoid inner monologue – sub-vocal cords still twitch and the shrew will hear.

7.  Hazards nobody admits
--------------------------------------------------------------------
-  Hypothermic syncope: the 4°C air plus vasodilation from slowed heart can drop core temp to 34°C.  
   Counter: chemical hand-warmer pellets sealed in beeswax; crack them inside mittens if digits blanch.

-  Acoustic hallucinations: absolute quiet is +4 dB SPL (your blood). After 20 min you will “hear” choirs, chain-saws, childhood scoldings.  
   Counter: hum a single 40 Hz tone inside the nasal cavity – it masks oto-acoustic emissions.

-  Moss adhesion: spores glue to damp skin; within 36 h they sprout translucent fuzz that itches for months.  
   Counter: pre-treat exposed skin with grape-seed oil; post-visit scrub with 5% vinegar.

-  Chronophobia: perpetual afternoon erodes circadian markers. Some visitors re-emerge convinced only minutes passed; others scream that decades did.  
   Counter: swallow a knotted silk thread; untie one knot per “event” you witness – gives time a countable shape.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
8.  Exit ritual
--------------------------------------------------------------------
When the mica sheet warms (sign of distress) or when you feel the equilibrium-being brush your thoughts like wet silk, it is time. Crawl backwards; do not look over your shoulder – roots interpret it as predation.

On re-surfacing, the city noise will hit like gravel in a blender. Keep wax plugs for first hour; remove them gradually in stages: −20 dB every ten minutes. Sunlight will feel scalding; wear the smoked-lenses sewn into hood brim. Speak only after food: a single sip of honeyed milk vibrates larynx back into service.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
9.  Re-acclimatisation calendar
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Day 1–3: Whisper zone only.  
Day 4–7: Normal voice allowed indoors.  
Day 8–14: Resume shouting at sports events.  
Day 30: Heartbeat finally sounds acceptable again.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
10.  Final advice (listen between lines)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The Hollow Verdance is not sacred; it is an agreement.  
Your part of the contract is simple: be negligible.  
If you keep that promise, the place will keep its own – to remain exactly strange enough, forever.
