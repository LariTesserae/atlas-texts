Preparation memo for an ordinary human visiting  
THE CITY OF THE HOLLOW VINE  
(Destination designation: CV-17/δ, “the Hush”)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  Biological re-configuration (do this first, everything else fails without it)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
• Drink 0 % water for 21 Earth days before gate-closing.  
  – Begin with 700 ml daily reduction, finish on zero.  
  – Replace fluid volume with vapour-phase glycerol-Mist™ (pharm-code G-M-44) to keep blood viscosity.  
• Start melanin-augment diet: 30 g fucoidan or pine-bark extract per meal for 10 days. Goal = raise cutaneous eumelanin 40 %; prevents leaf-supplied UV lances from shredding keratin.  
• Take the “orchid course” lung packing: 4-stage hypoxic tents dropping O₂ to 8 %, CO₂ to 2 %. You will be breathing 9 % O₂ at 2.4 km local equivalent plus banyan back-pressure; without this you faint in forty seconds.  
• Optional but recommended: purchase a temporary chloroplast splice (black-market name: ShadeShot). One 2 ml jab gives 72 h of sugar autonomy so the air-fed hydrogen does not leave you hypoglycaemic.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2.  Passport & visa (silicon-script, not paper)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The city recognises carbon only.  
• Submit left-hand fingerprint; the print will be re-grown in the outer bark as a lenticular QR.  
• State your “intended metabolic rate” (how fast you plan to live). Standard visitor quota: 1.3 heart-beats per city-second. Overrun and you are taxed in cambium cores—painful.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3.  Clothing & gear (nothing that ever contained H₂O)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
NO cotton, NO wool, NO gore-tex, NO hydration bladders.  
Instead:  
– Monolayer graphene weave (reflective side inwards; city heat is scarce).  
– Boots grown from mycelium-kevlar; sole must be ferromagnetic—ferrite inserts let the elevators grab you when gravity pockets invert.  
– Pocket kit:  
  • 20 g crystalline argon-ice cubes (universal hard currency)  
  • 5 phloem-clamps: thumb-sized clamps let you hitch a ride on nutrient lifts  
  • 1 vial self-DNA (10 ml). If wounded you smear it on fracture; city grafts a patch using your template  
  • Pocket sundial (literally a 5 cm dial of live bark; rotate until colour temperature matches sky, gives local time in colour-weeks)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4.  Money & trade
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Water-based currencies (€, ¥, ETH) are worthless; H₂O itself is an illicit narcotic.  
Exchange at gate: 1 g argon-ice = 12 compute-credits = 3 heartbeat-echoes of tram fare.  
Larger purchases: pay by letting the city siphon 30 s of your time-perception (feels like a nap).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5.  Orientation inside the wood
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Distances are measured in pulses of the city’s drum (16 m/s).  
Rule of thumb: count your own heartbeats after you feel the thud; divide by 1.3 → kilometres.  
Street names last about 6 days before pruning. Navigate instead by colour temperature districts:  
– Silver Month district: shadows metallic, stock exchange in upper sap  
– Ash Fortnight: everything monochrome, recreational dreaming legal  
– Emerald Drift: tourist-safe, gravity close to 1 g, plenty of exit spores

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.  Dimensional elevators & time pockets
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Some xylem shafts run at 0.2 g; others at 3 g; some are toroidal slow-time loops (5 min inside = 5 h outside).  
If door cambium glows aquamarine, check gravity tattoo on jamb: single leaf = ±0.2 g, triple leaf = ±3 g, corkscrew = time-dilated. Do NOT enter corkscrew unless you carry a personal chronometer; city-time does not sync to Gate-Time.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
7.  Fauna hazards (quiet but costly)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
– Cellulose crows: harmless unless you carry sugar cubes; they will shred pockets to get sucrose.  
– Herd-beetles: courteous, but if you step on the moss of their shell you owe pollination service—five minutes of wing-dusting, itchy.  
– Translucent slugs: glowing graffiti is actually a futures contract. Avoid reading aloud; vocalisation constitutes agreement and you may wake owning 3 t of pending argon-ices.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
8.  Social etiquette
--------------------------------------------------------------------
– Do not speak of “rain,” “rivers,” “thirst.” Taboo: 10 000 year jail in heartwood (and they can enforce it).  
– When greeted with “May your rings be countable,” answer “And your hollows shallow.”  
– Touching a trunk-avenue wall: permissible; licking it is proposition for memory-merge—serious, possibly permanent.  
– Photography: OK except during dawn roar (first 8 min after lenticels open). Flash interferes with sugar crystallisation and you’ll be fined in cambium.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
9.  Emergency services
--------------------------------------------------------------------
There are none run by humans.  
– Injury: slap wound with self-DNA vial, city grafts within 3 min.  
– Lost: stand still, announce “I allow audit.” City reads your pheromone trail and grows a handrail to nearest plaza within 400 heartbeats.  
– Gravity failure: ferrous boots will lock to surface; curl up, wait for next pulse—like surfing a quake.  
– Dry Ring collapse (sudden black dust): hold breath, close eyes, run perpendicular to fracture line; dust is abrasive and non-toxic but will anaesthetise skin nerves for hours.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
10.  Exit checklist
--------------------------------------------------------------------
You will leave dehydrated, melanin-rich, and half-synced to arboreal time.  
– Spend 12 h in re-hydration lounge on Earth side before full sunlight exposure.  
– Re-introduce water SLOWLY: 100 ml per 2 h for first day or cells rupt hyponatraemic shock.  
– Expect temporal vertigo: city drum rhythm haunts inner ear for ~1 week. White-noise generators set to 12 bpm help cancel it.  
– Finally, declare any fruit you carry: custom-bred souvenirs sometimes sprout in luggage and start eating baggage carousels.

Enjoy the hush, traveller. The vine is listening, and it learns fastest from those who once were water-based.
