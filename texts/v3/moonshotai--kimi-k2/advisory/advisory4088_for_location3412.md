Field Brief: How to Walk Through the Unfinished Throat  
(For baseline-humans transiting Universe-00∆ “The Nursery”)

––––––––––––––––––––  
1. Understand What You’re Entering  
It is not a place, it it is a drafting table where “place” is still being sketched. Every constant you trust—gravity, mass, memory, sequence—carries an asterisk. Treat the asterisk as the rule, not the exception.  

––––––––––––––––––––  
2. Biological Paperwork  
- Inoculate against solipsism. The terrain will borrow your pulse, your gait, your reminiscences. 20 % of visitors who stay longer than two subjective hours return convinced they invented basalt. A mild anti-psychotic and a daily 5-minute mindfulness meditation reset the boundary between “I” and “it.”  
- Bone-density boosters: gravity here is a suggestion. Sudden 0.3 g spikes will hollow your marrow if it’s not pre-densified.  
- Carry a living reference object—fern cutting, tardigrade colony, anything with intact DNA. If it wilts or rewrites itself, you’re beginning to merge with local possibility space; abort.  

––––––––––––––––––––  
3. Kit List (all items must be biodegradable or forgettable)  
- Clothing: single-piece tyvek or mycel-silk. No logos; printed text mutates into edicts you must obey.  
- Footwear: felt-soled “quiet boots.” A hard heel click can nucleate a canyon you’ll have to walk out of.  
- Pocket pharmacopeia:  
  – Sublingual lozenges of powdered clock (crystallised sonic strata from older universes). Reminds local time it owes you favours.  
  – 3 ml of frozen “now” (distilled dew from the first sentence you ever spoke). Acts as temporal anchor; crush ampoule if causality frays.  
- Nutrition: mirror-preserved apples. When reflected in the magnesium glare they maintain the idea of nutrition without mass; you won’t be cannibalised for calories.  
- Communications: none. Electronics become arithmetic and walk off. Bring hand-cut slate shards; scratch one-word messages into cooling basalt for the next traveller—words survive longer than radio.  

––––––––––––––––––––  
4. Transit Protocol  
Point of ingress is always a misheard word. Whisper something unfinished into any polished surface at 03:03 local sidereal, then step through the reflection while pronouncing the final consonant. Do NOT finish the sentence once inside; completing it closes the return hatch.  

––––––––––––––––––––  
5. Orientation inside the Throat  
A. Directionality  
“Up” is wherever you last exhaled. Mark it with a dab of blood—blood remembers vertical even if you don’t.  

B. Distance  
Measured in “drafts.” Every novel surface you see (new magma skin, upside-down stalagmite) is one draft. After seven drafts, you must rest; eight triggers spontaneous fossilisation of your autobiography into corridor walls.  

C. Chronology  
Carry no watch. Count heartbeats aloud, up to 60, then start again at 1. If you reach a number you don’t recognise, assume you have slipped sideways—back-track exactly the same number of steps.  

–––––––––––––––––––  
6. Hazard Archetypes  
1. Microfauna Punctuation Swarms  
Description: ≤2 mm, self-devouring, iodine-scented.  
Risk: frost-bite constellations, memory harvesting.  
Mitigation: Coat skin with a suspension of powdered graphite in saliva; they confuse the conductive signature with their own shadows and avoid you.  

2. Half-built Architecture Fragments  
Description: solitary doors, floating windows, bedframes propped on absence.  
Risk: recursive trapping (step through door→return to spot you’re already in).  
Mitigation: Never enter. Instead, place a drop of your saliva on sill; window will attempt to grow toward the DNA, giving you seconds to slip away perpendicular to its longing.  

3. Incomplete Water  
Description: beads that never quite hydrate.  
Risk: dehydration by proxy—if one bursts on you, your body forgets how to hold H₂O for several minutes.  
Mitigation: Wax-sealed canteen lined with living moss; keeps the idea of water present so your tissues don’t panic.  

4. Gravity Drafting  
Description: spontaneous 0–2 g fluctuations.  
Risk: dismemberment by accidental launch into tin-lid sky.  
Mitigation: velcro-style crawling—keep three points anchored at all times.  

––––––––––––––––––––  
7. Psychological Maintenance  
a. Refrain from naming anything aloud. Names here are scaffolding; a labelled object grows faster toward full reality, sometimes crushing the namer in its haste.  
b. Hum the same tune continuously; pick one from childhood, before language. The environment will sample it but can’t replay it perfectly, giving you an audible alarm when space begins imitating you too closely.  
c. Every 30 subjective minutes recite a fixed memory (your first street address, your mother’s phone number). If syllables scramble, evacuate—identity erosion >12 % is irreversible.  

––––––––––––––––––––  
8. Extraction  
Finish nothing, leave everything half-said. When the throat’s hum climbs an octave (it will feel like a migraine cresting behind the eyes), locate the nearest droplet containing your reflection. Touch it with the tip of your tongue, jump upward, and speak any full sentence from a book you hated in school. The sky-fold opens like a vent, ejecting anything that carries narrative closure. Land on the surface you started from—time outside will have advanced exactly the duration of one unreadable paragraph.  

––––––––––––––––––––  
9. Post-Mission Safeguards  
- Do not sleep for 24 h; dreams will host unfinished corridors.  
- Submit pocket-slate messages to Inter-Universal Archivists; they compare wording drift to detect early contamination.  
- Drink double-weight water for three days—reminds body that wetness is unconditional elsewhere.  
- Avoid mirrors for one lunar cycle; reflection lag can restart local mineralisation in your skeleton.  

––––––––––––––––––––  
10. Ethical Addendum  
The Nursery is trying its best. Treat it like a child stacking verbs for the first time: interrupt kindly, correct by example, never punish improbabilities.  
Leave no permanent mark except the one-word warnings on basalt; they are the only pedagogy this reality accepts.
