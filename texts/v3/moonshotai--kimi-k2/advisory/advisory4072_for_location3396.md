Field Manual for Visitors to the Mirlóch Cliff-Garden  
Edition for baseline-humans arriving via standard multiverse transfer

0. Mind-set  
This is not a wilderness; it is an organ still looking for its body. Treat it as you would the inside of someone else’s lung: politely, with attention to pressure, temperature, etiquette, and the possibility that your host may cough.

1. Pre-departure calibration (do this before you leave your home continuum)  
1.1 Breathing  
Spend three days practising “breath-climbing.” Inhale until your lungs feel bruised, then speak a 30-word sentence without exhalation. You will need that reserve in air that climbs back into you.  
1.2 Pulse discipline  
Fit a cheap haptic metronome to your wrist; learn to slow your heart to 64 bpm on command. The garden copies what it hears. If you arrive tachycardic every fern will scorch in sympathy.  
1.3 Personal gravity audit  
Weigh yourself naked at dawn, again after lunch, again at midnight. Log the variance. You will be asked by the garden to remember the exact gram-quantity of yesterday’s self. Do not disappoint.  
1.4 Linguistic quarantine  
Memorise only half of any poem you love. Finish it and the nanoscript will eat the rest. Bring lacunae, not literature.

2. Equipment list (nothing metal, nothing dry)  
2.1 Suit  
One-piece seamless polymer coverall, matte eggshell-white, aluminised on the inside. It must be able to swell 11 % without tearing when your heart drifts upward. Pockets: zero (they fill with condensate).  
2.2 Footwear  
Felt over-socks dipped in liquid Quartzene™; they exsolve 0.2 W of heat downward, preventing the capillary water from climbing your legs. Bring three pairs; they delaminate every 26 h.  
2.3 Respirator  
Not for oxygen—for density. A child-sized re-breather mask filled with 35 % xenon, 65 % argon. Sips only; if you inhale a full lung you will sound like thunder for the rest of the visit.  
2.4 Hydration  
4 soft flasks, each 250 ml, divided by osmotic valves. Contents:  
- Flask A 2 % saline + 0.7 mg lithium protectorate  
- Flask B 5 % glucose + 3 mM copper gluconate (trade mineral with the ferns)  
- Flask C Distilled (emergency dilution of blood-metal)  
- Flask D Empty; use to collect your own perspiration for re-drinking – outside water is still busy becoming itself; do not interrupt.  
2.5 Vision  
Wraparound goggles, 50 % transmission, notch-filter at 532 nm (the peppermint glow). Lenses fog on purpose; the fog reads your retina, so let it.  
2.6 Timepiece  
Mechanical wristwatch with the minute-hand removed. You may count seconds but you may not own minutes.  
2.7 Gravity compensator (optional but smart)  
A 400 g spool of diamagnetic bismuth thread. Pay it out behind you as you ascend the stair; it tricks the polarity field into thinking you weigh 2 % less each meter, trading real vertigo for imaginary safety.  
2.8 Recording medium  
Paper only. Pencil lead sublimates; use a platinum wire stylus that scores wax tablets. Wax is immune to literacide.  
2.9 Offerings  
Five 6 mm spheres of annealed nickel: leave one on any chair you sit on – rental fee paid to whatever owns the furniture. Do not sit twice without paying.

3. Biological prophylaxis  
3.1 Skin flora  
Seven-day pre-wash with Selenium‐Sapro™ to outcompete copper-oxidisers. Otherwise your fingerprints will turn verdigrent and the ferns will try to graft.  
3.2 Gut flora  
Swallow 10 g of thermophilic yogurt spores. They thrive at 48 °C and prevent the local phytoplankton from colonising your GI tract (diarrhoea uphill is undignified).  
3.3 Vaccinations  
Standard multiverse cocktail PLUS V-17 “Glass-wing interference.” Without it you will hallucinate fused memories every 88 Hz; tastes like forgetting your mother’s first name.

4. Arrival choreography  
4.1 Blink protocol  
Keep eyes closed for the first nine heartbeats after transfer. Prevents retinal burn from the bruise-purple sky and denies the fog an early biopsy of your identity.  
4.2 Greeting the quiet  
Stand still on the landing slab until you hear your own blood. Count 200 drips against the ankles—this is the garden’s handshake. Move before the 200th and a chair will follow you for the rest of the visit (awkward in the lavatory).  
4.3 Orientation cheat  
If perspective folds and terraces copy, pick the younger duplicate (always 31° left). Mirror-you is younger; give it your wristwatch—time must be conserved in the system. You can collect it on exit, older but intact.

5. While inside  
5.1 Consumption rules  
- Do NOT drink the condensate; it isn’t finished.  
- Trade saliva only with yourself; other humans may be out of temporal phase (illegal in some continuua).  
- Eat nothing that photosynthesises in mid-air; you would be swallowing alien daylight.  
5.2 Physical exertion  
Climb one stair, rest one breath. Heart higher than tongue is the local altitude sickness—symptoms: hiccups that taste of peppermint and lightning. Cure: squat, wrap arms around knees, breathe xenon.  
5.3 Cartography  
Maps redraw when completed; navigate by intention: whisper “I intend the cliff edge,” walk backwards; every fourth footstep leaves a bubble of normal gravity that lasts 6 s—long enough for course correction.  
5.4 Social encounters  
Locals call themselves guests too—no hierarchy. Speech limited to nouns and half verbs. Finishing a sentence = theft; expect applause from the fog when you trail off.  
5.5 Glass-wing etiquette  
If one lands on you, freeze. It will sip photoelectrons from your skin’s infrared glow; payment leaves no scar. If two land, they may fuse; enjoy borrowed memories but do not, under any circumstances, try to keep them—theft of half-memories attracts migratory chairs.

6. Emergency procedures  
6.1 Flash-blush (sudden onset when the sky turns infra-red)  
Lie supine, empty Flask D over face; let droplets roll uphill into your nostrils—counter-currency tricks the polarity.  
6.2 Perspective lock (unable to find return because the terraces re-fold)  
Spool all bismuth thread into a loop, step inside; it becomes a null-gravity mat. Close eyes, count 88 glass-wing beats, reopen—loop drops you at the original landing slab.  
6.3 Retrosyncope (meet younger self, younger self dies of shock)  
Push corpse into uphill fog; count 200 drips; when it revives, it will be you again. Bandage temporal scar with wax tablet rubbing—keeps both versions coherent at customs.

7. Exit strategy  
Descend east stair without looking back; keep eyelids half shut to deny the garden a final read. Do not accept any hologram invitations written in dew—those are IOUs payable with future visits.  
Check pockets: you should find exactly three copper seeds. If you have four, you miscounted yourself—plant the spare immediately on arrival plain or it will grow a full-size copy overnight and you will owe it a life.

8. Post-return quarantine  
8.1 Shower in cold saline within 40 min; hot water triggers the copper seeds in your cuffs.  
8.2 Exhale through activated charcoal for 24 h; garden particulates crystallise in alveoli and can re-start the terrace cycle in your ribcage.  
8.3 Burn the felt socks; they creep toward the nearest igneous substrate.  
8.4 File report using only the wax tablets. Burn papers if you accidentally finished any sentences in front of the nanoscript.

9. Philosophical note (optional reading after successful return)  
You were never the visitor; you were the condensate. The garden climbed you to cool itself, then let you fall back into approximate humanity. Remember the grammar of incompleteness: speak it, write it, live it, and you may keep the bruise-purple taste of near-completion forever without ever finishing—or being finished by—the mirloch lung.

Travel safe, leave sentences open, and may your droplets always choose to become you again.
