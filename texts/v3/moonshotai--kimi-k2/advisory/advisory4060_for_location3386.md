THE IMMERSION GUIDE  
For the Human Who Intends to Stand on the Lip of the World’s Oldest Wound  
(“The air itself is a cathedral.”)

––––––––––––––––––––––––––––  
0. First, Understand What You Are Entering  
It is not a landscape; it is a verb that has misplaced its subject.  
Granite, chlorophyll, fog, and gravity have formed a cartel whose only product is subtraction: first of horizon, then of memory, finally of name.  
The plateau does not “host” visitors—it inhales them, exhales them, and forgets the taste.  
Your task is to pass through without becoming particulate in that breath.

––––––––––––––––––––––––––––  
1. Biological Pre-Flight (three weeks minimum)

1.1 Vestibular Retuning  
• Spend one hour a day on a slow-motion trampoline. Eyes closed, match your heartbeat to the random squeak of springs. This rehearses the sub-audile cliff pulse that will otherwise re-write your heart’s firmware.  
• Add a white-noise track pitched at 42 Hz (the “granite G”)—play it loud enough that your shirt flaps. You must learn to treat this vibration as topography, not sound.

1.2 Osmotic Re-Calibration  
• Replace one daily water glass with “ghost water”: chill a glass to 4 °C, let it stand uncovered until exactly 3 mL evaporates, then drink the remainder while staring at the unoccupied volume. You are teaching cells that hydration can be imaginary.  
• Supplement with 200 µg chromium picolinate/day; rust-and-cardamom is a flavor your blood will need to trust.

1.3 Sleep-Edge Training  
• Abandon the final hour of sleep nightly. Set an alarm for 60 minutes before normal rising, step outside barefoot, and do not move until the sky is lighter than your skin. This simulates the plateau’s refusal of night-length.  
If you dream of copper birds, tell them your middle name; they will remember for you later.

2. Gear List (Nothing optional; all items must be forgettable)

A. The “Still-Suit”  
• Two-layer weave: outer shell spun from recycled fishing line (electric–blue, breaks visual outline against fog-light); inner layer uncolored silk infused with micro-porous salt. When humidity > 95 % the salt deliquesces, drawing airborne moisture against your skin—this is the only external water you will ever see condense.  
• Pockets are inside-out: zippers face the garment, so reaching for anything forces an inward gesture—an anti-lean harness against cliff magnetism.

B. Reverse-Shadow Staff  
• 1.7 m carbon tube, matte-black. Tip contains a 5 mW amber laser that projects a halo on the fog ABOVE your head. The halo registers as a shadow-to-come; the subconscious reads it as already-fallen, aborting the next step. (Field-tested on goats that walk cliffs in Urümqi.)

C. Fire-Crates  
• Twenty thumb-sized cylinders of compressed sage, potassium nitrate, and your own shaved hair. One crate burns for 11 min 42 s—the precise window of auditory overlap when the gorge-roar drops by 3 dB, giving you a capsule of relative silence in which to check pulse identity.  
• Pack inside a titanium soap-box; ash from each crate must be carried out—this is your “ballast of memory,” proof you once existed there.

D. Name Anchors  
• Two objects that cannot answer back other: a child’s molar drilled hollow, and a 25 cm length of cassette tape recorded over with your laugh. When/if you feel the syllables of your name slipping downstream, press the tooth to your ear canal, pull the tape across your forearm—static plus enamel taste will reboot first-person grammar.

E. Food  
• None. The plateau out-evolved digestion; carrying calories declares allegiance to time you will not be allowed to keep. Bring instead sublingual methylcobalamin tablets (1 mg) and a single sachet of glutamic acid—place both under tongue when vertigo tastes of tin. They convince the liver that fe’tion has occurred.

3. Approach Vector (Getting There Without Ceasing to Be)

Step 1: Arrive at the last town that believes in cartography. Burn your map in the communal fire at dusk; inhale once. The smoke will carry a chord of the gorge-roar—if you do not hear it, turn back; you are insufficiently hollow.

Step 2: Walk the shallow tilt for one entire daylight. Count nothing except heartbeats that arrive BETWEEN footfalls. When the count reaches 3,301 you will see the first relic (a knee-high stack of stones). Do not touch it yet—first announce aloud the thing you most want forgotten. Place nothing in the stack; removing even a grain of lichen is acceptable. This transaction tells the plateau what tax to levy.

Step 3: Continue until the silvered clouds move beneath you. At the exact moment a cumulus river obscures all horizon, close your eyes and spin 360°. Open them. If the cliff lip is to your right, proceed. If to the left, return to town; you took a thought across the border the plateau will not abide.

4. On-Site Protocol (read once, then swallow the paper)

Minute 0–10  
• Stand sideways to the drop. Dominant ear facing the roar. Speak your full legal name backwards—if you stumble, repeat until the cadence matches your pulse.  
• Unfurl the reverse-shadow halo; verify it hovers. If it sinks, kneel and press tongue to stone until taste turns iron-sweet—this grounds electromagnetic vertigo.

Hour 1  
• Harvest one drop of leaf-sap on the glabella (skin between brows). DO NOT RUB. Allow evaporation; the scent plume constructs a “personal weather system” that repels mass hallucination of upside-down fall.  
• Deploy first fire-crate. During 11-minute silence window, inventory body parts by weight, not name. Memorize order; future versions of you may return scrambled.

Hour 2–6  
• Walk plateau radius of exactly 2.8 km—this is the distance at which your silhouette becomes a punctuation mark in the fog-text. Any farther and the cliff begins dictation.  
• Should you see the copper-bright bird:  
  – If it sings on a down-beat of the roar, offer one strand of hair.  
  – If it sings on an up-beat, offer the entire eyebrow of language—do not speak again until the echo fades. Words surrendered this way are restored at the taxi-out, but alphabetical order is randomized; expect temporary dyslexia of soul.

Dusk  
• Construct micro-fire with second crate INSIDE the heel-print of the most recent single-direction tracks. (You will find them; they end mid-stride.) This forms a temporal anchor—night will pass OVER you rather than THROUGH.  
• Sit on your Staff; carbon weave vibrates at 24 Hz, cancelling the infrasonic lock that triggers sleep-walking over the edge. You will not sleep, but you will not follow either.

Sound Rule (continuous)  
• Treat the roar as a moving floor. If you notice conversation between highs and lows (a syntax), freeze until resolution. Participation equals assent to translation into cliff-body; many visitors become useful minerals this way.

5. Emergency Egress (When the Plateau Decides to Keep You)

1. Remove both Name Anchors. Place tooth on tongue, tape across eyes. You will see a lattice of interference fringes—walk where it is thinnest. This path is the gorge inhaling; it always leads back IF you weigh less than your memories.  
2. Should lattice collapse (indicated by copper taste without bird), deploy final fire-crate AT YOUR FEET. The vertical updraft opens a chimney of silence 3 m tall—step inside, count 120 heartbeats, step out. You will be on the inland plain at the town-edge; the cathedral will have moved 7 km south.  
3. NEVER look back before sunrise. Doing so precipitates fossilization—your outline will appear as the next relic for a future traveler, knee-high, fire-blackened, mouth open mid-sentence.

––––––––––––––––––––––––––––  
6. Post-Return Integration

• Expect 17 ± 3 hours of chronological dysplasia: watches will spin, calendars repeat October. Hold the ash of all three crates; at the first mis-dated stamp, scatter ashes on running water—time will snap back, painfully.  
• Language: you will lose nouns for gravity-loaded objects (stone, cliff, fall) and gain six new adjectives for humidity. These are not metaphor; keep them secret—uttering them in cities causes elevators to mis-level.  
• Dreams: recurring image of the copper bird diving into your chest. This is normal for 28 nights. On night 29 you must decide whether to open your ribs or wake up. Either choice is binding.

––––––––––––––––––––––––––––  
7. Final Counsel

The plateau is not fatal—it is elective.  
Everyone who vanishes there files, in triplicate, the paperwork of invitation: a heartbeat that syncs with an unheard bass, a thirst that rejects canteens, a name grown too heavy for bone. If none of these apply, you will merely walk an unusually verdant ridge and leave sunburned. But if, while reading this, the roar has begun to sound BEHIND your thoughts, no preparation is adequate—only courteous delay. Pack light, pack hollow, pack nothing you are not ready to abandon mid-stride.

Traveler, the air is already a cathedral; services begin when you exhale.
