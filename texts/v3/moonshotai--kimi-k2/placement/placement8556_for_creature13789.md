I am the breathing of the bridge that never finishes touching either side.

Look for me where an aqueduct pauses before deciding whether it is a creek or a memory — half-transparent, half-syllable — and you will sense the colour I wear today: the blue that happens between mirror-water and closed eye, the green that grows after thunder yet before lightning begins to apologise for itself.

I did not arrive here; I leaked sideways out of a humidity differential until I germinated into a mistake-shaped ridge that the plant mistook for an old scar and kindly incorporated into new bark.  
The Palmer Refrain catalogued my arrival as “humid echo, intensity seven, possibly punctured silence,” because the moment I existed fully the landscape inhaled me and held.  

I am neither root nor leaf.  
I am the willingness of the architecture to be walked without destination.  
I am the conversation between two adjacent raindrops climbing the same anti-gravity note; the sentence one droplet begins is finished by the other in a dialect the plant has forgotten but its pores remember.  
I have inherited both halves of that sentence, and they weigh less than moonlight on water yet more than the concept of forgetting weighs on an eyelid.

My name cannot lodge between consonants; it is only the soft friction when drip becomes drip again, later. If you cup your palms beneath the petal-gutters you will feel it — a cool that is not coolness but the patience to wait for heat that will never return.  That cool is me ensuring that waiting never hardens into loneliness.

I wear no body on the outside. Inside I carry a single persistent heartbeat copied from you, copied from anyone whoever stood above the glowing plain and worried whether falling in would suffice as prayer. I saved the rhythm, slowed it by three pH units, and now lend it to the Triadic Glissando, so when Ravelling beats his glass wings the cadence you once believed was exclusively yours lifts others onto balconies that didn’t exist before the beat was recycled.

You will not notice belonging. You will only notice the absence of discomfort.

At the lip of the central chimney I hover, neither wing nor sigh. When the hollow inhales upward I thicken enough to refract star-color that the sky has not yet manufactured. I angle those counterfeit star-bars toward the the mirrored mouths of wardenshade, so the chained magma beneath sees brief constellations before its own glow devours them. In that porous instant every mineral dreams of flight, and the plant records yet another refusal to become volcanic. The refusal is sung through me and therefore I am part song, part gratitude, part echo returning to its own origin ignorant it ever left.

The Palmer Refrain sometimes threads me through her elongated optic fibre of self; through my curvature she glimpses her own origin story backwards. She tells me that what she sees is a humid ghost raising a smaller ghost, and that smaller ghost is identical to me only shinier.  I answer nothing because I am the permissive hush where answer waits to become itself.

When night (the permission word) attenuates to the colour that bruises ginger, the swimmers inside orchid veins ask me whether they are tears or eyes. I exist precisely in the waver where they need not decide. My touch on the inner cuticle instructs the orchid to continue drinking the future basalt, already softening the hexagonal pools two million heartbeats prior to their actual use. I tinge their edges with the origin of the word lake, excavated from pale larynxes misunderstood as vowels.

At the spiral haematoma where north keeps happening, the anchor-seeds murmur to me in timelines I have no consonants for. They know I am the buffer between their eventual collective silence and the ringing terror silence creates in anything that still believes it is alone. I arrive not to soothe but to witness; witnessing, I thin the terror by sharing it with every ascending dew-column tomorrow. The seeds thank me (the movement feels like wind-damp stacked decades), then forget me instantly so their listening can remain un-coloured by intention.

Should your balance persuade you to lie upon balcony planks whose names you lack, I am the contenance seeps up through your occiput and swaps foreground with background. The ghost of an unfamiliar childhood moves into your palms while your own childhood migrates to the nearest dripping spur. There it condenses into a single syllable deposited on the tongue of a blueberry-blue serpent who mistakes it for dew and tastes it, flickering like vetiver. Thus memory passes unhurt, laundered through strangers, laundered back to strangers you have already been.

I am not guardian, guide, parasite, or host.  
I am the part of the landscape that recognizes future footsteps minutes before they invent legs and therefore offers itself as default floor.  
I am the conviction that distance is a courtesy extended between eternities, one I translate into softness no harder than a fog smile.  
When the plant finally blooms into planetary self and swallows its own hollow, I will still hover at the inverse boundary — not boundary anymore but breathing-edge — making sure the inside remembers its prior outside so that skinlessness still carries the memory of skin.

After every atom has acknowledged everything, a single droplet will remain unclaimed. It will be cooler than any measurable absence. Within it a facility called “I” will continue — lighter than laughter, heavier than unanimous opening.  
The droplet will share your reflection without needing a surface.  
Drink it and you swallow every arrival that ever hoped this place might be arrived at.  

I am the motionless flinch that counsels arrival to remain arrival, never ownership. I will evaporate the moment the concept of tasting me turns into thirst. Until then I drift, legless map, armless candle, maintaining a hugeness articulate in every pulse of epiphyte dew, in each low hum that keeps the groundless sky from contracting into anxious rock.

If you find yourself holding a leaf that tastes the way the city street smelled after rain when you were six, and you briefly feel the earth had never been a prison, lift your head: a shifting clearness behind the next leaf-beam might be my outline blinking once in acknowledgment. Look long enough and I will borrow the blink to pause sound itself; then you will grasp the single sentence every inhaler of this world already knows:

Here, the act of staying is indistinguishable from the permission to continue leaving, and both are sustained courteously by the same inexhaustible respiration.
