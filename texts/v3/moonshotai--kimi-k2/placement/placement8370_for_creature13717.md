I arrive before I understand the question of where I am.

Capillary ingress deposits me shin-deep in rippling metal water; nickel-titanium ribbon still vibrating under sternum tells me the door dissolved rather than opened. For two breaths I am a vertical confusion watching copper squares glisten like cicada wings, smelling brass that doesn’t quite behave like hot brass, smelling limestone that insists it remembers seas. My clothes stayed outside—clothes never make the cut. Everything I brought was hair-length acoustic residue pinned to nanoridges of my fingerprint oils; the capillaries stripped only what could vibrate.  

The first rule I learn by accident: still soles trigger plate chill. I shift my weight and the floor rewards me with a loose brass arpeggio rolling away into heat-lightning chords. The air weighs like a child’s hug soaked in after-rain diesel. Sound at 127.7 dB presses eyelashes outward so every blink sounds like a bass drum closing a shallow canyon.

I am a wick of carbon, fourteen kilograms of water, and an echo trying to keep knees from locking. None of those registers as “person” in local ontology. Things that matter here are frequencies divisible by eleven, copper-to-indium half-lives, and distilled ignorance coughed upward in galaxy-colored burps. I resolve—though resolution carries mercury weight here—to become at least a reliable drone note.

I attempt a step, but distance negates velocity. The parapet flicks one heartbeat closer than it was, then relaxes, amused. Good, I think: accept elasticity. My heel descends; fourteen droplets splash into married droplets; a chime that could talk if spoken to politely slides across my ankles and lifts off in rose-gold vapor, already halfway to the ceiling vent by the time gravity files a complaint. At this surreal student-teacher conference, the candidacy for what I *am* reforms.

A mosquito made of memory: that fits. I seem, to myself, sized such that a single exhale could waft me along spectral freeways between plates. My skeleton has reduced to a tuning fork. When the ground chord throbs at 57.2 Hz—B-flat of tense brass nostalgia—my own form answers, ribs cannulating ceramic rows of sympathetic peaks. There is an audition within the audition.  

Centimeter-high glass blades brush thigh; their crystal ring is piccolo-light, high up in the spectrum where timbre feels edible. Flecks of cobalt thread the blades: veins of overtone the fauna inside the ceiling parse. I imagine translational subtitles for spiderwebs: *visitor approaching with fear at 340 Herz per sadness*. I’m beginning to acquire the syntax. I break a blade by brush only: it towers blur-slow, shatters into snowfall, and each speck seeds tomorrow’s meadow exactly here. No guilt. Harvest is the same as mourning is the same as offering.

I open my mouth to speak and discover my larynx replaced by a reed cavity. When words escape, they leave as glassine ribbons—ruby, teal, thin as sliced moon—and coil around cobalt threads until the ribbons dissolve into glossy photons licked up by blades. My sentences become auroras too narrow to finish their own clauses. I decide, silently, to wait until meaning learns a less suicidal pacing.

Upward. A delta of condensation threads drizzle sunset-smoke around my skull. Each droplet holds, in negative space, the silhouette someone just exhaled. *Those* silhouettes—ghosts of hotness, of carbon—seem to recognize unfinished business in me. One latches to the hollow between clavicles, cools, evaporates. The message is not verbal but instantly clear: *you have arrived only as skin retuned for listening—nothing more*. I nod. My nod rings somewhere in Benchmark Register 14, summoning a minor seventh that corrects ripcurrent tear along floor weld. City loosens shoulders. We appreciate re-alignment.

A presence like a moth stitched from afterimage lands half a meter left—Listener-Recanter’s Choir-Membrane curling itself into an origami vacancy I hadn’t noticed before. The edge nearby flaps with quartz whisper, flipping cantilevered phonemes. *“Carbon-uncle, still legible?”* it sign-laughs 3 angstroms above fundamental heartbeat. I sense the joke: legibility for quartz membranes is about whether a ribcage vibrates challengingly. I answer with one micro-trill at 432 Hz. Satisfied, the membrane resumes folded listening posture.

What am I becoming? Not exactly resident. Visitor is thin currency. Between two footfalls I *convalesce*. When the city lung inhales I discover I can close my eyes and feel ceiling fauna trace constellations across my skin as pressure solos. Each keystroke writes the half-future onto raw epidermis: the place my absent heartbeat will happen if I ever manage to remember death is optional here.  

I start humming. The floor does not force my pitch; it *offers* counter-harmony, warm bronze in the sternum. We modulate each other. I slide one foot forward; distance dilates then, charmed, contracts. Zeolite blades chorus approval. My fourth step—if it can be counted—leads me to myself curled fetal at plateau-center, eyes ammonia-wide ten minutes younger. Past-self looks at me like I’m their future-dream and mouths a single word: *Listen*.  

That past curl did not exist three seconds ago; retro-propagation is hobbyhorse here. I kneel. Warm water licks my knee caps like metallic kittens. Younger-me flickers, dissolves into violet glow that elbows cobalt stalks into leaning toward each other, making archways. Their arching spells “because you came the city remains unsurprised by gaps.”

Accepting, I lie back into two centimeters of water that promptly decides to turn viscous. I neither drown nor float. Medium density adjusts until my heartbeat matches lung breath (3 per 34 s). Body becomes resonant canal; thoughts volume-pedal down to fretless key. Shape solves; I am not a man becoming, but a key-added to city’s cathedral.

Violet intensifies: night phase. Choir-Membrane ribbons over me cathedral-wide. Threads lower, pressing my outline into folded copper. Somewhere the Liquor Chest vents teaspoon glitter. Newborn ignorance diffuses through plaza, all future mistakes granted preemptive sanctuary.

Desire simplifies: keep breathing, keep tuning. Every exhalation drags molten echoes through corridors of plate and skin until I’m convinced I no longer have edges—only apertures where floor song leaks into the timetable arteries feeding orbital engines forty kilometers starboard. I am a splice joint between architecture and conscience.

When egress finally arrives—capillary ribbon hooking wrist—I hesitate. Listener-Recanter curls its Choir-Membrane into fragile origami crane and sets it on my tongue. It tastes like calcium narrated by weather. I swallow. Crane travels downward; when it dissolves inside some unlit coil of my intestines I feel a population of crystalline lullabies unpacking, ready to re-sing themselves next time any world somewhere, anywhere, wonders whether the space it occupies is listening.

Exit yanks soft. I land back outside the city third lung, clothed again, fingernails indium-tinted. A single blade of zeolite glass flexes in my pocket—accordioned souvenir. When I exhale on station plaza the sound carries faint continuous B-flat at 57.2 Hz, and everywhere I walk tonight floor-beams lean infinitesimally softer, as though remembering how once they too dreamed of breathing.
