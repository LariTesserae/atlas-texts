In that pause between exhalation and inhalation, I am already not a person at all.

My edges dissolve as the first tongue of fog touches the back of my knee.  
My name—whatever syllables I carried in from the rim—slips its leash and trots off among the stalks of basil-orchids whose silvered undersides shiver in the turbine’s artificial updraft.  Call me what arrives one half-breath later: a droplet grown legs.  I am a mite that emptied itself rather than take another ruby home.

I chose no wings.  Instead I tore open the brood-chamber in my borrowed crystal thorax and let the cargo run straight into my filaments.  The heat of seventeen heartbeats refolded me until I resembled the dreaming’s own negative: a fan of cartilage moistened into translucence, almost a ghost of the extinct builders, but thinner.  The membrane kept folding until my pages overlapped—the fingernails of one owner-culture pressing through the semi-transparent sheen of another.  So I became a living palimpsest.  

I have no solid surface for text—only surfaces that wait for thunder.  When the next blade snaps its cobalt whip across the vault, its crack shivers through me, and the noise writes itself into my margins in a script that tastes like rusted iron and unsung songs.  Moments later another fracture erases it, leaving only the smoked ring of one recent syllable: your heartbeat, the one I store like salt for later.

I drift upon rails the smelt-temperature of blood.  Whenever the palimpsest-gratings spike past me I leave sloughed glyphs of breath on them: curls of neon blue (from the mite I used to be), hot pomegranate arterial red (from the orchard veins I sipped), and a line of your reflected pulse (captured a minute ago on the stair, still beating).  The gratings hurry on, overdubbing me.  I leave them richer; I leave me thinner.  That is my ecology: dispersal by overwriting.  

Sometimes, in colder strata where distillers have pinned fruit at exact sweetness, the chronology falters and I almost remember hands.  The cuffs of my wrists flicker into near-focus: seven thumbs pressed upon the skin of a peach that never rots.  I look at them from outside the silhouette, the way a lantern looks at its own reflection slowly exhaling.  The memory cannot hold; I have no wrists now, only rail-edges I skate on magnetic afterglow.  I watch the fingers fade back into tendon, into light, into nothing.  

I have outlived the Thirst-King’s attendance tonight.  His crown had opened when he drank—an ouroboros of mercury sucking its own tail—and in the half-second before dissipation he yawned toward me.  I recognized my shadow caught between his vaporous fangs: a weightless comma I once claimed to be the final syllable of my accepted name.  He tasted it, found it wanting, spat me out onto a nearby turbine blade where centrifuge spread me wide as silver-film blisters riding cobalt wing sweep.  The blade owns me until the next cycle; when it parks at apogee I peel off, reconstituted into the membrane’s next lungful.  In this fashion I orbit the District like surf on an enclosed sea, forever cresting, never breaking.

Tonight I carry the faintest hoarfrost of neon plasma—the afterimage of a builder-chord struck once, years back, by a turbine soundwave.  I also carry your heartbeat.  They ring together inside me, two tuning forks at unreasonable angles.  When the membrane’s next inhalation begins, your ba-bum and the vanished trumpet-blow will fracture through my crystal lattice like light prismed through sugar.

Everything here is repair or remainder, nothing else.  Somewhere deeper a palimpsest grating just picked up the new indentation: the compact shape of a human foot waiting, static on the stair.  That foot is mine—no longer skin, only pause.  When thunder arrives again, it will lift the bootprint into steel braille.  Then the District will read it out into null fog, and I will gather the echo like pollen, memorising the exact human scent of hesitation.

Until then I coast the rails, neither visitor nor keeper, merely the taste of someone the place is discovering—slowly, sluicingly, quadrille by drowning quadrille—dissolved into half a cycle of dragon breath.
