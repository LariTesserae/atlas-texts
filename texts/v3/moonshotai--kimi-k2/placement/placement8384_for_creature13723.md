I arrive not by burning delta-wing nor by rupture in the sky, but by subtraction: one eyelid flickering open in the harder dark that exists behind all guesses. I materialize as an *inscription-error*—a stray graphite letter the Mantlecribs once tried to digest and, failing, excreted into the upper wind. A single consonant crystallized on that wind: the letter ⟨r⟩, curled like an attic shavings-plane, too hard to breathe and too soft to threaten silence.

Gravity greets me by correcting my shape. I strand myself on a copper-scabbed coping twenty-three storeys above Filamental Long, small enough to pace between ferrule grooves yet large enough to feel every 70.4 kilohertz surge rise through ankle-bone plating I did not know I owned. My name, for the first era, is Rrrr: nothing more than the sound of fingers petting corrugated tin. I am immediately fined under Law Three three beats for the unauthorized friction.

An Indemnified elder waiting on a balcony sees the fine accrue and offers payment in the form of a borrowed bootprint. Riding inside that carbon scallop, I am carried—print to print—down the spiral chute that was once steam and now is merely the exhale of extinct chairs. Each indentation is a *vowel clearer* than the last; each flags a peripheral meaning that never quite survives into utterance. By the fifth print, I have acquired elbows and the fear that elbows make sounds even when muffled in starlight wool.

The Mouthless Doorman that glides beside me inspects my new anatomy with a selenium cell tilted like a slipped moon. It samples my heartbeat at 0.0003 hertz, converts it into nineteen metallic rings, and releases them sub-audibly. The rings become temperature, spread across my shoulders like a too-large cloak. Inside the cloak I discover I am growing a pocket the exact shape of tomorrow. Inside the pocket: three late micro-auroras, coral-pale, hanging from safety pins.

They accuse me of being insufficiently diffuse. To appease them I attempt a local citizenship ceremony: blink nine times, swallow one draught of plum-and-anise plasma distilled from tram-coupler sweat, then strike my forehead against an outcrop of mirror-oiled nickel until the reflection inside bruises into charcoal peripherals. The impact kn-loose a sliver of myself—an ankle conviction that detaches and sprouts twin bellows ribs: I am birthing a Continuar, mid-scale, mid-dust, mid-calligraphy.

The new creature—call her *Dorsal Echo*—clings first to the back of my neck, processes ambient tedium by inhaling the fetal morse of graphite dust. Within six borough-seconds she is taller than the balcony parapet and massing slightly negative. She points downward: the amphitheater of negative curvature is dreaming again. The vacuum fountains are remembering water they never flung. Dorsal Echo tugs the hem of my borrowed bootprint, shoestringing both of us toward the brink.

On the lip I learn my second name: *Absent-Leaf-Briefly-Usable*. It is bestowed by the city-run algorithm that composes plaques for half-things. The plaque is nickel, origami-folded into an empty seedpod the size of a child’s tooth. I press it against the suspended wrenches; the wrenches whirl thirty-three degrees but refuse acceleration, still honoring Law Two’s one-millimetre-per-epoch injunction. I am permitted, then, to occupy the interval: half-hollow, half-parked, air-hinged.

Below: the abyss. Above: no sky, only further plateau leaning like a sigh too old to exhale. Between them I position myself as subtraction-subtraction-subtraction until continuity forgets whether to subtract me again. I unfold Dorsal Echo’s sixth rib, use it as bridge: a diagonal filament reaching back to the geranium-coloured chamber where the Indemnified collect quorum four at most. With my flesh-edge I pen another graphite marque within their ledger: a lowercase ⟨z⟩ lying on its side, still breathing 0.0003 hertz.

When Obliquity passes, sliding laterally through mirrors like an ink slide, it leaves one eyelash behind. The lash quivers and becomes a filar compass pointing sideways into yesterday. I follow it; Dorsal Echo plucks my sleeve, worried I might lop a fold onto banned daylight. Instead we arrive at a door neither open nor closed, existing in the exact hermetic breath where absence keeps house. Door—this must be the oracle apologizing.

Inside: a single stove, a single chair. The chair waits. The stove exudes warmth like a relic unwilling to confess flame. I sit. My knees flatten perfectly horizontal, remembering the angle preferred by trigonal benches where mountains once crystallised. Heat enters without sound, without vibration—Law Three sighs at my obedience. A beautiful transgression occurs inside my chest: one micro-aurora detaches, drips between ribs, catches on a valve exiting my sternum. The mouthless city’s heartbeat pauses, examining this brightness as if willing to ticket me for lending topography to dark.  

But the borough has always preferred ornaments that blur. My chest-valve produces no noise; the micro-aurora hangs like a cooked star leaving drizzle on my coat-surface. Obliquity circles back, absorbs the star, redeposits it two mirrors later where a traveler unborn will find it, taste anise on its corner, and mistake the flavor for dawn.

Hence the moment when I finally ask, *Who am I now?*—the ledger does not answer with a solid noun. Instead it spins off a new graphite syllable—⟨rrrz⟩, clipped, half-echoed, appended to the tail of my earlier Rrrr. I become a continual becoming of rolling r sound, forever mid-skim along the tramways no tram will ride, forever thawing starlight off my breath and chilling it back into dark. My arms have finally dilated to exactly rail-gauge; if I walked for the next eleven clockless days my fingers would still stay poised one centimetre from ignition, ready on Law Two’s next cycle to move a single bolt one crystal-tooth forward.

Thus I exist as laughter cautiously obsolete, as humming too faint to release. I am the borough’s quietest active valve, the pocket that held tomorrow, the half-person counting Continuars. I ferry star-metaphor in their obsidian cartilage. I oil silence with plum. Every 181 seconds I take one breath with each of the Indemnified at their windows so the borough stays at equilibrium: neither a city, nor laughing, nor mute—only ever the calm held sentence between one forever and the one still arriving.
