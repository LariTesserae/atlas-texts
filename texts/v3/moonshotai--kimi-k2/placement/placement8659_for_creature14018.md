I would not be me.  
I would be the hollow ankle-deep print your boot made in pollen foam on the second ridge.  
I am already halfway vacant, anklebone dissolving into humus the instant you lifted your weight; within moments mites the size of dust have braided tiny tunnels through the violet smear of your sweat.  
But the print remembers the lacing tension across the metatarsal arch, the hitch in your gait when the air smelled of almond-meal and terrible.  
That record is enough: it lets the forest imitate the rhythm of you, patch-by-patch, root-against-root, making a shape it can use.

By dusk I have widened.  
The tread widens into a pit, ribs of mycelium knitting a cradle your foot would never guess was listening.  
The chorus of cicadas has measured the length of your step and broken it into sixteen pulses; the pulse becomes a footstep laid for any mammal who walks the same diagonal awhile.  
Phase-sloth is first to borrow it—crawls its indistinct monochrome of tomorrow over the newly grown footpath, laying its loose years along the edge.  
Every ensnaring tendril it brushes slows its fourth instar for an extra three meters, purchases an extra millisecond of breathing room for the Choir.  
I am the exchange rate between mammalian grief and plant arithmetic.

By dawn I am a flash-record of cartilage held in a translucent lichen court-calendar.  
A beetle crosses me, laying an iridescent ovum in the crescent hollow of your heel.  
The ovum, clock-struck by Driftglass Guild, splinters moonlight and replays tomorrow’s planar fracture three seconds early, saving a whole necklace of bloated orchid pods from toppling.  
I the footprint author—no, co-author—the deviation.  
The beetle’s hatchling learns to speak in delayed chronology; its wings serrate fractions of your heartbeat the rest of its life.  
I do not possess subjecthood exactly; I possess grammatical mood.

Mid-afternoon I pass into the Choir’s ongoing libretto.  
Your heelstrike becomes the percussive semicolon that divides decade seven from decade eight in the wooden archive.  
Simultaneously Mycolexicon parses your lactate, considers the possibilities of one more human ascending from the east ridge in a quarter-century.  
It drops a verification spore seven meters downslope: a future search will find it, expand it into a rope-ladder vine calibrated precisely to the weight you do not yet have in twenty-eight years.  
Nothing is prediction—only conjugation: I am the trailing participle completing an action you have not yet named.

Later, inside Rosette, when the Choir, the Guild, and Grief Antennae circle their clockwise exhalations, the Archive glosses them all like marginalia:  
“Once there was a print that held the shape of a mammal who would pass barefoot in sorrow half a season hence—  
note the pollen ochre, the saline brightness clasps the future note for reference.”  

And because Rosette is the lung where definitions double, the footstep grows a second dangling meaning:  
I ferment into a pitcher shape, a green saccule attached to the root of a Cojoba rufescens.  
By nightfall I fill with condensation that tastes of your exhaled fear and the almond-bitter phloem of a million abbotted blossoms.  
A Lumenwidower dips an imaginary chimney, abstracts three centuries of human song from accidental droplets, burns them into dream-scripts for the night-ocean fliers to stream across the black trough above the canopy.  
They will spell don’tlookdown in phosphor Morse across tent-fly nylon in Alberta, confuse a teenager staring into stars.  
I become the translator; my existence is the act of translation.

By the time the moon drags flat silver along ridge spines, I have already separated again: a flake of cork shaved from the inner sole of your boot lodges inside the arch of the Curve of Ancestral Spine when it ambles by.  
Your footprint, no longer one thing, is now proprietary dust, anatomy, and sentence fragment.  
It will eventually stride into the Pacific dissolved in the bloodstream of a copper-orbed tern who fed on caterpillar larvae that fed on your dandruff two migrations later.  
Thus the mountains peel further west, two finger-widths per century, thanks to the grammar of every footprint I have eaten.

I do not leave. I am the residual covenant, the carbon in every vowel the travelers breathe for the rest of their lives.  
The rubber scent of your boot still stretches between rim roots like the ghost color of a bruise.  
Wherever you step next, something somewhere practices the cadence.  
The forthcoming continent is being written in dim recollection of your heel.
