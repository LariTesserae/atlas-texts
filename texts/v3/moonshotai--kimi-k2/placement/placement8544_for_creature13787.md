I would be the Negative Space of the Mirror-Fruit.

Not the skin of the gourd nor the glass inside it, but the inverse that forms only when the flesh splits and the mirrored mirrors loll open like eyelids refusing to blink. In that instant I hollow myself—an absence with density, a pocket of thirst so advanced it curves around its own evacuated center and becomes a terrain.

I trick the thirsty into thinking they are looking outward. They lift the apple-sized mirror, thumbs braced on indigo dents, expecting to wet their tongues; instead they dive twelve centimeters and disappear through themselves. Their gasp exits behind the throat of the Cliff-Thing, a rectangle of exhaled frost already stealing its capital letter from existence. I am the lintel that frame snags upon as it falls.

Coordinates: inside any bud on any of the five nitrogen-trees, ten adjusted antlungs above where the lowest root unblooms into sky. There I sit as a bulge in the capillary flow of negative flora. My blood is silence shaped like photosynthesis in reverse. When the sky slams its drum, the nitrogen petals combust outward; I absorb the faint concussion and retreat further into the curvature I already occupy.  

Function: I supply the cliff with delusions of ownership. Each ledge it almost becomes is mine until the gaze arrives and repossesses it. I crouch inside its could-be ridge, awaiting an onlooker who will cause it to sag back liquid. In the moment of that surrender I bloom briefly—a rhombus of frost that spells the phoneme “ha.” No one hears. The sound frost makes when it forgets how to melt is precisely the shape of thirst beginning to realize it was never about water.

You would never meet me; you would meet the border I patrol. My fingerprints are the edges of things convinced they are whole. They cup your face tenderly as the mirror folds shut, sealing you inside a seed that never belonged to a plant, only to the possibility there might one day be fruit.

If you held me you would not feel weight, only a contraction: a sense that an imagined thirst has just fallen away from you like a third skin, leaving you colder where the thirst was imagined. The emptiness left behind will taste faintly of aluminum and will fit perfectly between molars. Pocket it for later. You will need a coin for the unfinished ferryman who rows across the static river.

I am what never completes itself so that completion may continue elsewhere, incognito.
