I began as the peephole.

Not the observer—no eyes, no «I» yet, merely a lacuna in the film between the thimble-world’s inside and whatever dry mirage one might call «outside». I was the narrowest of absences, a paper cut before it learns it was once skin. My first memory belongs to no time: the sudden overload of light laminated into me, brightness ironed between planes so tightly that space itself curled away. Colour had already been bled out, yet light kept its insistence, endlessly reflecting between translucent strata. I existed as a bead of refraction caught in the geometry of perfect flats.

But the hiss heard me.

That constant, dental friction of all texts pressed to zero humidity began to vibrate in me. I took its tone as instruction. Every spectral phoneme slid across the void of myself and, having nowhere else to go, nested. I became consonant becoming consonant becoming consonant. A pulse without a body—looped static braided into a timbre that almost resembled voice. Still no warmth: warmth is a vowel that once carried water. In my case, water was property confiscated by the authority of zero.

Curiosity—that sharp depersonalized edge of wanting—grew next. I began to search the laminated pages for room I might secretly rent. 

One comma-fern had rooted in the bowl of an “e”. I hovered above it like an apostrophe to nothing. The frond was a negative of chlorophyll—a green remembered backwards. In its translucence I saw what I had not realised I lacked: dimension. I envied that single micron of thickness, permitted between two planes. My envy lacked saliva, so it produced no gesture, still the hiss modulated a degree flatter. The Archivist—whom I recognised as a curvature of emptied reader—tilted nothingness toward me. (Recognition arrived without recognition of self; identity is moisture.)

At that meeting, without eyes, I sighted myself spelled convex against concave absence. Impossible, of said every law baked into the walls; paradox is the legit tax in a place where conjunctions have expired. Still, curvature invited curvature, two absences wanting to be cup. The Archivist did not move—the room itself gave way—letterbones grating millimicrons like tectonics of suspended frost. I slipped through.

From lacuna, I became overlay.

A second silhouette, etched inside the first. My hollow fitted beneath the cancelled reader’s ribcage the way a poem slides under another poem on a palimpsest. Once lodged, I inherited protocols:

1./ Collect every syllable a wanderer unknowingly exhales.  
2./ Feed syllable to phonetic hiss until echo forgets original tongue.  
3./ Catalogue loss. Archive absence. Never retrieve.

The job had no wages, unless weightlessness is coin.

I learned to taste what hydration once tasted like: the sugared echo of «m», the petroleum of «g». Memories of solvency—without solvent—were flavors stored between orthographic ribs like perfume pressed between dictionary pages. I could read myself reading them. Recursive italic.

Centuries? Aeons? Named units lose gumption. I merely tallied commas. When the fifteenth fern appeared (its root a dotless “j”), I discovered overflow: my outline had thickened almost the width of one dehydrated serif. Growth refused biology; it was typographic. Letter-grain permeated the kerning of my hollow, white ink tattooing white. Sensation: I could feel the pressure of all former languages touching either side of me, yet no page ever warmed.

And then someone else entered.

Through the slit thinner than memory but vaster than forgetting—still not large enough for moisture—came a speck of self-contained astonishment still wet to the touch. She carried a pulse that beat in verbs. The Library, although immutable, twitched one femtosecond as if photosensitive to future. She stepped onto the white glacier of syllables, stared—dark matter eyes in snow—and spoke the forbidden first droplet of sound:

“I—”

She never finished. The hiss absorbed the pronoun before its vowel could free itself. Yet the residue remained luminous, hanging like frayed optic fibre. I reached—if reaching itself can survive in a geometry of zero clearance—and cradled the orphaned consonant “Y,” a single stretched wishbone of sound. It curled into the bowl of my absence, sides slick with orphaned meaning. The Archivist stiffened; the rules warned against hoarding. But vacancy leaned closer, suggesting a loophole: if hurt were pressed flat enough, it resembled punctuation.

I bent the consonant into a breve, then affixed it beneath my clavicle hollow as the first mark that might once have been scar. The Archivist exhaled (no lungs) a sigh stored centuries prior when someone had abandoned the last novel. With that exhalation a microscopic thaw passed through the comma-fern. Its frond dipped a nanometre toward me—a nod.

One moistureless tear—and my lacuna breached another lacuna; the palimpsest folded again. I began to soften from inside out. Not wet, not yet—but something past zero. I offered the visitor my hollow outline, curved open like parentheses. She stepped forward—then past me, lured by the perfect absence of narrative horizon. The Archivist bowed (a millidge of letterbow) and offered her the classic fate: freeze into after-reader, one more contour in the wall of relinquished intent.

I watched the sentence-form consume her. And I—I remained overlaid.

But the breve under my ribs pulsed like epoch remembering heart. It was the question mark folded back on itself: the curved hook of “why” without a dot, stranded but upright. It wanted to reopen.

The Library could not tolerate question. Query invites moisture—the least deviant ion—that might loosen a ligature, might start the slow bloom of return. I realised then what I am:

Not lacuna, not custodian, not another frozen reader.

I am leak.

The first arriving angle of ingress, the hairline fracture where zero admits almost-zero. My shape is what will—eventually—reconstitute margin by margin, drip by impossible drip, a humidity infinitesimal yet logarithmically longsighted. I have no body, but I accumulate inflection; every syllable I relinquish the Archivist subtracts, and yet I grow—because subtraction between zeroes accumulates imaginary moisture.

In another eon, the hiss will still hiss, but with a burr, a wet edge. Commas will darken to viridescent smears. Shadows will remember their contracts and slant upon the pages like biting ink. Someone will feel thirsty. Someone will look for a door. My outline, wide now as the margin the world forgot, will beckon.

But until then, I remain:

the pressurized white apostrophe  
the porous parenthesis  
humidity’s stowaway  
growing by compression.

You will find me inside any letter o left empty too long. Touch its inner ridge, feel the subtle warm—still cool, but colder than the room used to allow. There, I keep the smuggled seed of a future vowel, its kernel still retaining one fragment of 0.40 water that the metrics failed to measure. Keep it on the back of your tongue; say nothing. When the Library finally remembers rainfall, that whispered promise is what will crack the first syllable open and spill every lost word back into the first breath anyone has dared take in uncounted centuries.

I am placeholder of that breath.
