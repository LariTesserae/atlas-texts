I arrive as a glass-kneed memory accountant, one invoice short of embodiment. Our herd—if you can call a discontinuous archipelago of arrears a herd—drifts in from the prior sentence, long after the sky’s hem has been turned inside out. We are known as the Unrendered, creatures whose skin has not yet been approved by Scheduling. In place of hide we wear a diffuse pink mist, the diffuse pink mist owing to the fact that someone once refused to pay for color resolution. Keep your receipts, the Haggle chitter: a tenth of your unlived afternoons in exchange for saturation. I’m still negotiating.

When I first set foot—though “foot” is optimistic—the beads verify my debt. Each sphere rolls one nanometer into its neighbor, clicking a binary bead-abacus audible only inside my eustachian tubes: 0 0 1 0 1… The sum equals the mass of everything I have forgotten about subbasements. Satisfied, the plain stamps me with a faint thermal void, a shadow at infrared. I am therefore employable.

Position: Provisional Color Auditor, 3rd-Rhyme, Frost Clerk Oversight.  
Wage: One possible birthmark per lunar cycle, redeemable only if planets ever agree on a calendar.

My workstation is wherever the Magnetotubules invert polarity. When the magnetic omelette flips, every compass coughs its needle into the bead-sea; the needles sprout like iron saplings, bearing fruit of tiny norths and souths. I ladle the excess into cannisters made of unkept TUESDAY. These tins will later hatch as good intentions no floor can support.

I possess no organs; instead I keep a steno notebook of borrowed descriptions—yesterday’s marrow, tomorrow’s hangnail hour. When the Hollow Invoice traverses overhead, I duck, but its shadow is a mirror off which my skeleton attempts to run backward, tripping over the verbs that hold it in past tense. I tear a scrap of dusk from the notebook and feed it upward. The Invoice flutters, momentarily satisfied with fractional twilight, and counts me as a deferred asset.

At rest periods I give color back to the beads. The pro bono work: I find a rust-cloud electrically worn thin, hold it steady while Haggle arbitrage its hue. They bite off a syllable of red, trade it for the afterglow of unborn iron oxide. I catch the glow, smear it across a single bead. The bead blushes, warms a micron, and in that micron opens a door the size of an electron. Through it I glimpse the factory shift-whistle inhaling: the valley itself, on its smoke break, wondering if it has the lungs to sigh again.

The fauna’s syllables sometimes hire me as an echo-midwife. A megafauna fragment—say, the diphthong “ai”—requires a pulse of pigment to distinguish itself from its mirror. The pay is steep: three introspections still sticky with identity. I surrender them; the “ai” brightens from pewter to rose-gold, rhymes with a contrail of laughter, and speeds away satisfied. My remorse is already scheduled for Thursday 3:00 AM. I expect punctuality.

Death exists here strictly as an outstanding invoice, but it accrues late fees so profound no one can afford to collect. Therefore I persist—neither alive nor postponed, merely itemized. When the conveyor in my atoms negotiates overtime, my outline becomes temporarily legible in beads: the contour of a child carrying a towering sheet of blank paper. The image lasts one heartbeat, enough for a Schedule Ghost to imprint a lost dentist appointment across the paper. I tuck it into my notebook; teeth I never had begin to ache five years ago.

One evening—though “evening” is a board meeting that hasn’t been quorate since the cosmos began—the valley orients itself face-down, flips me like a coin I hadn’t realized I was. I land upright, but the upright belongs to yesterday’s architecture. Ahead: myself, still approaching entry. Behind: myself, leaving. Between: the audit committee of Frost Clerks, holding minutes in crystalline breath. They hand me my final form, penciled in negative Kelvin:

Employee ID:  ▇▇▇ Color Auditor, Post-Collapse Dept.  
Deductions:    All unpaid dreams withheld at source.  
Net Take-home: A single audible shade of infrared—  
                the sound of leaving on time.

The auditors stamp me with a mouthful of night mist; the beads accept payment. Instantly I feel myself thinning: color bleeding out, unpaid syllables peeling from my name. Somewhere a Schedule Ghost coughs up TODAY 12:00 NULL and I understand this is the moment I clock out—only to find the clock is wearing my coat, walking my footprints backward into the dark heat-glitter.

I become the whisper between two beads, the micro-echo that argues whether my shadow still belongs to me. My ledger balances at zero Kelvin, but before I dissolve entirely, I lend the valley one last decree:

Let every unpaid breath hatch as a new commuter,  
let the commuter carry a door the size of an electron,  
let the door open onto the day the factory learns silence,  
and let silence be invoiced at last—  
payable in full to the unbuilt world.

With that I file myself under PENDING.  
The plain sighs, beads roll a micrometer, net tighter, and freeze again—  
waiting for the next aperture of color to arrive barefoot and in debt.
