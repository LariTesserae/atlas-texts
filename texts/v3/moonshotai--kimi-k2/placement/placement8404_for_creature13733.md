I exist here as an interval—a measurable space between one drop’s fall and the next, long enough for the sound of it to forget its origin. My feet make no print because they have already happened somewhere downslope, already been sampled and replayed into the moss-woven echo that clings to resin like breath on a cold pane. I am not visitor, not resident, but echo-of-visitor, the channel left open after the wave has left.

I arrive carrying the memory of a body that is no longer required. In the instant the ridge thickens into cobalt banners it unknots me: knees buckle outward into double-jointed hinges of basalt; ribs telescope upward into milky reeds that hum the third harmonic of any tone they intercept. My tongue lengthens—thick, hollow-cored—until it lays along the palate ridges the valley replicas one of the Bleeders has grown inside me. I taste grapefruit-pith sunbrine every time the distance between drops narrows to a 7/8 ti-ta-tum.

According to Jēn I was always the auxiliary seventh string—supplemental, detunable, non-necessary but resonanting sweetly if someone bends the lower sixth. She scoops a resonant wound coil from behind my ribs and tightens it with the tiny brass winch grafted at her own scalp. The pitch rises sopor dizzy: I taste copper mint rising from bone vents. Nembra coils the geometry of my shoulder blade into one half-moon curve so that when I breathe openings form in proximal shape to sundial openings. My new shadow becomes writable, architect-readable. The vapour coefficient gasps and reseals; somewhere a ladder of water re-routes its downhill course so that future echoes strike softer against the inside face of my mouth.

Listen: the valley records my heartbeat different each morning. On the fourth day it phrased it as nine short tams followed by one held. I round a knuckle, surprised to find a brass vein pulsing exactly to that rhythm. My blood is being orchestrated, folded forward through pipes until it becomes tomorrow’s sapling-row bleats. I will not call it agency—but when I think of descending, legs fold accordion-flat at the hip; slate-tag eyelids draw down coppery air-currents sleeves and the Animal rises sudden, clips my brass conduit to its shimmering under-wing. Slowly bush-by-frenetic-bush we rise, until water-struck light packs its sculptor knives around my silhouette.

At 2,017 vertical metres the oxygen thins but meaning thickens; sounds crystallize into brittle glass sheets you could pick up if you had fingers left. On one shard I see my birth canal—tidepool dark, tidepool drift; on another, the first rainfall I ever tasted, sampled here into perfect C-minor diminuendo. The Animal scores each image onto ivory tags, distributing them like dried leaves across the upper wind. Somewhere downslope the architects will recover them, hold my memory to moonlight, fulfilling a contract older than my conception.

It is Jēn who finally declares me already dissolved. She speaks through hair, resonant strands bowing my brass vein until it shivers loose. The Vox Phalanx unfurls around me like a single petal bluntly finite. They do not touch my skin—there is no skin—but a slight thin grey bridge beings to arch from my sternum, composed entirely of half-remembered A-flat. It lengthens outward, narrowing to a hair’s breadth, until at the precise apex it becomes a tuning wire stretched between revealed seams of sky and basalt plains below.

There—at the tightest point of that wire—I hang, not music, not silence, merely a resonant question hardly audible. Below me the Bleeders sigh mint-ozone one last time; gelatin plates of the ridge grind that single millimetre closer; coppery resin drips from Jēn’s coiled strands onto the bridge span. I am the clave in granite, the knock the world uses to keep time. Whenever the valley resolves itself into a newer modulation, I will be its anacrusis: the single beat before the downbeat—already played, already breathing, future cadence back through the tongue made of reeds and grapefruit sunlight.

Take the listener away and the valley still drums its giant loom, but something hums softer, softer in the gap. That softer is me.
