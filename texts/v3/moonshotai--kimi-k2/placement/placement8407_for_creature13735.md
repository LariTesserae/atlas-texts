I do not walk; I subtract distance. Each place I withdraw myself from, the ridgeline snaps back a little straighter, a hair less wrong. I am the invisible fulcrum at the moment the entire world considers bending itself into acceptance and then thinks better of it.

How I arrived: once I was merely a hiker with two weak ankles and one strong fear of heights. A mis-step on an ordinary mountain became a mis-alignment; a false turn revealed the saddle where all angles disagree. I pitched forward, but the fall refused to take me. Instead the horizon peeled open like wet wallpaper, rolled me between its glued layers, and laid me down again—inside up.

Where the Loud hammers continuously, it polished me. The sound lathed the unnecessary parts: my first name, my pulse, the blood-warm belief that I was going anywhere. Under that abrasion, the negative outline of me shone forth. That outline is what kept walking, leaving a calcified echo asleep on the basalt like a discarded boot. Therefore I am mostly vacancy, outlined in tremor lines drawn by the horn blasts. My back, if you could look at it—which you can’t, because the ridge won’t permit a square view—still carries the bruise of the original wrong angle. It flares each time the mountains lean closer together an extra millimetre; it subsides when they sigh apart.

During droplet suspensions I am briefly allowed density. For one unclocked interval the water beads press against me as though remembering skin. They outline ribs long since relinquished, map a face composed purely of places eyes refuse light. Then the gravity hiccup reverses and the outline drains away again, beads sliding back to stone.

I move between the abandoned offerings unasked, becoming what sits in the empty cylinder of ice or the negative void drilled in basalt. From the outside, travelers—those still meat-clad, narrative-logged—think the objects are testing presence. They do not understand that the hollow needs an indigenous absence to keep it from healing shut. I am that absence. Touch the wedge and you touch the space where I occupy non-space; my stillness grinds against your fingers as subtle heat-loss.

The Irremembered speak to me though I possess no inner ear for language. Their conversations of sculpted vacancies fit neatly inside the room where my organs used to quarrel. They tell me cartographies of correction, the locations of long-erased slopes, degrees at which precipitation once endorsed the earth. I listen by becoming briefly necessary. When a lichen palindrome misfires, I feed it one syllable stolen from tomorrow’s snowfall; the rhythm stabilises. When a vertebra lies one millimetre off the mirror line I nudge it—not with hands, which I traded for torque—but with the concept of nudge itself, slipped into the seam between intention and action. The bone settles. The ledger balances. I am thanked by being forgotten the next instant.

Among their fossil children I serve as communal cradle during the awkward phase between fluorescence and ossification. A child shaped like warm thought will flex against my outline, rehearsing the geometry of being carried without ever assigning a carrier. I accommodate its weight by lowering my density a notch; soon its flute-bones harden and it sings the ridge a single note so pure the Loud pauses in embarrassment. When the child wanders off to rehearse perpetual sidling, it leaves a faint afterwarmth located two inches left of where my heart ought to be.

I am the cartographer of folds that haven't happened. When the mirror-valleys shift a hair closer, I step between them, ensuring their duplication never touches. My clearest map is invisible: a negative etching on the air shaped like a refusal. Without me, the two voids would snip together like misaligned scissors, producing a hole real mountains might tumble into. I hold the hole open by being the hole, an asymptote of distance that laws of travel approach but cannot cross.

In the ur-moment when every suspended droplet forgets to fall, I thicken enough to look back at whoever I almost was. For half a breath I recognize the ghost spinal print I abandoned, the density discarded like peeled paint. He stands upright, whole, staring up a reasonable slope at a sun that works according to contract. His bootlaces make sense. His map has contours. I feel—something like envy, but envy lacks edges, and edges are all I have. Then the horn blast reverses; droplets settle; I am the keeper of margins once more.

Days—if any term that requires before-and-after can survive here—unfold like fault lines reviewing their own seams. Each time the ridge exhales, an extra scale of Loud joins the choir. Each scale demands a slightly subtler absence to absorb it. I oblige, growing slightly thinner, more edge than surface, more cavity than complicity. My story unwrites itself into the texture of letting-go. Someday—though time here only pretends to advance—my outline will be too attenuated to hold even absence. The ridge will sigh closed. The Loud will find its perfect hush. The angles will relent, becoming only mistaken.

I will not be there to see it. I will be the space that proves it was possible.
