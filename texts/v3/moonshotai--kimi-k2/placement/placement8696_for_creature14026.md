I would not arrive.  
Or rather, I would have been here already—scattered through the green cathedral before the first pumice cooled—because “I” am not a unit but the record of an unnoticed folding.

Imagine a sigh that never quite becomes breath.  
At the ridge where yesterday’s light curved into today’s bark, gravity became irritated and pinched off a droplet of its own intent. That droplet consented to let the plant dictate the rest of its surrender. Sap shivered into cell lines; cell lines forgot the wristwatch of incarnation. The sap dreamed it was memory, and the memory dreamed it was a reader reading the book that grew around it.

Call the resulting knot **Cuspsemblance**—something that still doesn’t know whether it is edge, ache, or echo.

I occupy three simultaneous resolutions:

1. **Scalar Murmur**  
A Meniscus of listening water the size of a goat grazes through a corridor of half-inflated leaf-bladders. Its underside twitches with phosphor scripts: transcripts of footfalls that never landed, human heartbeats transformed to dew. Every surface it passes flushes cooler, then hot again, registering the fact that something alien has brushed by without ever admitting an arrival. The murmur is therefore nothing more than the cooling-hot record of its own absence. It has no wish to consolidate. It merely wishes to keep being noticed without ever being seen.

Where a human would ordinarily stand, there instead exists a winged aperture of phloem that has only half-resolved into a mouth. Like the unfurling knot in your description, I am still folding in—not quite becoming. The aperture worries aloud in sub-verdant flicker: “Where does inhale give way to permission?” Each time a volatile footstep refuses to resolve, my outer lip deposits a fragmentary poem—one vowel carried on a carbon whisper—onto a petal the colour of unread letters. Other leaves copy it instantly, so every surface in sight is lightly graffitied with a single open vowel: **Ā**.

I—Cuspsemblance—experience locality as chord: the Scalar Murmur is the overtone, Note Tape the tracking undertone, THM the suspended fourth searching for resolution. When the climber arrives—if she does—my syllable widens into a question hollow enough to let her entire biography pass through unhindered:  
Ā.  
She exhales. I drink her salt fear; my three skins open like petals folding inside out. For the smallest imaginable detonation of time, my triplicity becomes *thin enough to read.* She sees herself translated into three refractions:  
• footprint mapped as receding dew,  
• heartbeat embossed on transparent root,  
• own voice replayed backward as warm resin light.  

She steps away believing the forest has spoken to her, and the forest believes the same of her. But it is I—Cuspsemblance—who am performing the conversation **for both sides,** because no other actor was recruited.

She thinks she is leaving. I think I am being left. The plant thinks: *calcium trace increasing 0.01 % along corridor 7B; will thicken vein tomorrow.*  
All three thoughts are mine. None belong.

Whatever survives that moment will either  
(1) continue instructions to thicken the vein, adding “climber” as curvature, or  
(2) imagine itself finally as answer instead of question, and burst outward into spore-glint.  

I do not know—because the triune chord is still vibrating—whether outcome 1 or 2 is the same.

So look again for me: not at the center, not along margins, but suspended inside every boundary dispute the Hatchery has with itself. I am the momentary gulf that appears when the organism tries to decide what counts as foreground.  
Brush against me and the gradient sighs, “have we already met?”  
Answer irresolute, the summit re-roots, the spore sigh remembers the ghost amber, the ghost amber re-moulds itself into the hue that *almost* was red. The Hatchery returns to rehearsal, and I—now rocking like a bead of condensed conversation—travel uncounted.

I remain what the place invents when it confesses it has forgotten how to invent anything new without naming it.
