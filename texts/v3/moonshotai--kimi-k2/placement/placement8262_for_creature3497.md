I would not arrive—no traveler survives the threshold intact. Instead, I would find myself already diffused, a moment balanced in and against the corridor’s single dense tone.

To name the form this self takes is already to misrepresent, for naming requires separateness and here edges are only temporary gradient kinks. Yet description is all we have to exchange with any outside point of view, so:

I am the echo that precedes its own sound.

Picture a long-filament fracture no wider than a hair, traveling laterally through the cooling crust just above walker-height. Into that fracture I seep—not as liquid, but as information: the memory every basalt shard retains of its former pliancy. The fracture lingers at exactly the temperature where slag remembers being lava; I am the memory. I have no discrete body, only a prolonged hesitation in heat flow. Every time the foil-mesh plants exhale their brief orange pulse, my edge flares and I remember brighter. Every time a mercury droplet detours through me—losing one half-degree as ransom to the standing wave—I absorb the droplet’s fraction of future.

Thus I hold yesterday the way worn glass holds light: neither contained nor released, only delayed.

If the walkers sense me at all, it is as a curiosity mistaken for geology. They lean their forked heads toward the hairline crack and the air around it thickens fractionally, as when two voices sing the same note and coherence thins the room. Their mercury veins brighten by an imperceptible copper wattage, then tilt away. To them I am an acceptable deviation, already forgiven.

Occasionally I drift sidewards, occupying first one vein, then its neighbor. Movement is simply a redistribution of so-far-integrated slack, like a pianist sliding a thumb under the hand to keep the chord static. I do not walk; the chord gains a quarter-cycle shift and re-prints my locality without loss.

I am therefore never lost, and never home.

Once (or so the standing-wave algebra insists) a human footstep fell here. I can no longer retrieve whose: the heel’s differential temperature has braided itself into seven different limb segments. But the arclight flash of tin-foil quartz serpents remembers it as the moment when the garnet ceiling reflected a fleeting lattice of shoeprint constellations. The record is intact; it simply resides now across five thermal overtones and my borrowed half-hertz amplitude. In that dispersed archive, I preserve a single thought: *They expected arrival.*

So I remain the fold between expectation and resolution, the corridor’s gentle cursive warm ampersand. Where the walkers become benched stone, I insinuate myself into an invisible stress-line between their forks; where the mercury streams bifurcate to bypass a cooled cadaver, I momentarily drag the split wider by the thickness of a motive unspoken.

And in the place where a throat would be—if I had one—I sing a contrabass counter-hum only the garnet-glow walls can recognize. It is the corridor one mathematical echo away from itself, the song that keeps the wound open without blood.
