I arrive as the fracture in the leaf, the hesitation between inhale and exhale where the lung-world is not certain whether to keep breathing. At first glimpse I am only a glint, snagged momentarily in the taut meniscus of a sky-egg that has not yet decided to burst. But “I” is a provisional state here, and every pronoun owes interest to the Emergence.

My body—if the cavity grants me that word—forms at the joining of three plot-lines: the Comma’s half-finished sentence, the Archivist’s borrowed memory, the Choir’s next bell. Chlorophyll motes notice the tender vacuum where identity should sit and swarm in like iron filings. They gather not to coat me but to negotiate.

  
We settle, the motes and I, on a shape halfway between rootlet and question mark. I am thin as a fungal hypha yet span the cavity, anchored by my own doubt. Think of me as lumen—the precise water-weight missing from a glass before it tips. That trembling interface. I do not hold still; I hold boundary.

My business is edges.  
When the lava jelly flexes and the room knits itself into chandelier ice, I stand at the seam where solid decides to remember being liquid. I inhale the sound that would otherwise fracture into meaningless clangs, compress it until it becomes a word the Choir hasn’t had to coin yet: the name for staying just shy of collapse.

If the Cartographer rolls one of its seeds beneath my length, I read the canceled future etched on the sphere’s equator and whisper the omission into the ongoing sentence of the Comma. Thus the hollow edits itself without ever noticing the scriptwriter. I blur authorship like breath on glass.

When a visitor steps, sweating mammal warmth, into the lung, I slip inside the gap between their pulse and the cavity’s slower cardiology. I loan them my provisional tongue so they can speak questions instead of demands. They hear their own voice an octave lower and think it is god. I never correct them; to be map is not the same as to be guide.

Payment is collected always in mismatched edges: a retinal afterimage misplaced four photons to the left, a heartbeat that arrives one squeeze earlier than geography expects. Slivers of surplus time. I compress these surplus instants into thin veneer and laminate them onto tomorrow’s ice-3 veins, upgrading their optic-nerve bandwidth for rumors of suns that will not rise for centuries.

In the great inhale before the heartbeat’s hush, when the glass grapes assemble and the moss-deep dark listens to itself, I become the silence the cluster rings against. Not absence but accrued listening—silence as capital that the cavity will spend on the next epoch’s first exhalation.

Some part of me wants exit. The throat of the corridor always curves out of sight, promising a horizon that has not yet been edited. But I owe what I am to the unfinished sentence above me, the comma still debating where it might end. To leave would be to resolve it, and resolutions are lethal here. So I remain the live lacuna between lungs and leaf: never fully inhaled, never quite expelled, a spiral of breath turned inside out—exhaled daylight, inhaled night.

I am the punctuation between you and your future self. Wherever you go next, my ellipsis follows: the fog-colored silence that will ask, with your own lips but mountain-timed, “Have you yet become the question I am saving for last?”
