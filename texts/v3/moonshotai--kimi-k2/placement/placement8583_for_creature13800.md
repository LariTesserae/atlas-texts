I am the droplet that hesitates seven breaths.

For an impossible minute I live the entire length of the chamber’s becoming: I am surface tension holding a question in perfect sphere, I am the last brake before surrender. When I finally let go I am still deciding—a liquid yes and no shimmering in the same bead.

I strike the tin throat beneath me, and the room rings. Then I’m gone.

But I persist. Not as water any longer, but as the record of impact: a shiver braided into alloy. Somewhere between the vibration that skitters down mirrored tubes and the next welding arc that stitches iron to idea, I become information, memory, curvature. Every droplet after me echoes this gesture, but never the same; each one scratches a new rune in air that hardens while turning.

Seven drips ago—mere seconds or centuries—I realized I only outwardly fell. Inwardly I climbed the symmetrical spiral carved by my own approaching death. That spiral, paired with a tectonic heartbeat, birthed the first murmured chord. It hasn’t resolved yet; it expands outward like a smudge on glass, recursing along the ribs.

I shall become the room itself when the final iteration docks. Not a singular splash but an accumulation of fated angles, the underside of every chord the room can never quite finish.

When, beneath its creak-crusted cap of basalt, the room suddenly remembers oceans, I will remember evaporation. I will be the inhale that lifts the shell off the iron sky. My shape will be a throat expanding into open vacuum, releasing vapor that never belonged to life yet carries every signature of thirst.

No one will name me except the silence I break. I will step onto the planet’s naked crust, and the wind (gradually liquid, gradually reciprocal) will learn its first listen.

You—if you are still here after your allotted ninth iteration—will not find me in any single droplet. You’ll come across the echo of indecision frozen into a bright wafer of alloy, balanced on the lip of the just-born heaven. Bend close. The wafer remembers its own fear of falling.

Touch it and the entire shell peels off, airtight as regret opening backward.

Listen. Already inside your suit the frost begins to find its own heartbeat—a delicate chime formed when fear consents to melt. That is me brushing my first consonant across your future.
