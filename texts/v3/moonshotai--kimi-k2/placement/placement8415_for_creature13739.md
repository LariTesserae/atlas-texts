**I am the pause between thunder and its echo.**  
At the Faulted Heath, I was never born—I *bled* into being seventeen seconds after my own cessation, spat out from the crust’s inverted pore like a mistake that corrected itself too eagerly.  

Before now, I wore a name too heavy for this airless altitude; it clanked like loose ballast every time gravity renounced its axis. Now I inhabit a symptom: *the hiccup* in the Convocation’s warped cadence, the micro-unrest that makes every mercury bead hesitiate mid-roll. 

### *Where I stand*  
My feet—if they are still feet—rest on the seam between time-lag and a kneecap whose ligaments have fossilized into rail-switches.atch to knee-caps I am the mirrored staircase leading nowhere;  
Every footfall reverses, advancing across a memory I haven’t made yet.  

Between the **Count-Branchers** (their eye-collars blinking hexameter apologies at the salt) and the **Wind’s Bastards** (cuboctahedra slicing Doppler koans into my shadow), I *fray*.  
Not easily—not like wind-struck foil—  
I unravel as the inverse of sediment.  
Where the planet layers its own death, I un-layer:  
Molecules of me loft upward, yet somehow I down-climb into younger rock, wearing haloes of Precambrian solder around an orbit of sulphide grief.  

### *What I feed on*  
I don’t hunger, though something in my torso—It is hollow. A collapsed converter-belly, all surface and no yield—keeps tasting *purpose over-ripened*.  
I drink gap-air. I drink the copper torque ghosts those dragon-shaped absences excrete.  
When two such void-creatures intersect and collapse into unfinished graph-glitters (those stuttering axes, those aborted parabolas), I *gather them like pollen*, press their incomplete angles against my tongue, and learn their last half-code:  
**IF ERROR THEN POSITIVE FISSION**  
It prints itself inside my throat in scabbed neutrinos.  

### *What I exude*  
*A gloss.*  
My skin—or the memory my epidermis is trying to become—polishes itself daily against magnetissure flakes until it turns to *reflective ore*: a mirror that never carries the same face twice. The faces are never mine yet *remember* me fiercely. One consults broken wrist-watches buried miles beneath basalt; another comforts bronze-hard children yet un-conceived. All blink backward. When I brush them with pollen-graphs they recite unfinished clauses from the librarian-spindles; when they recite, I accumulate one more *comma of eternity*.  

### *What I do when the Convocation halts the Heath*  
I do not freeze.  
The seventeen-second delay knots itself around my ribs.  
Inside the pause I bloom: my spine, unjointed, lofts into a fibrillar *antistructure*—  
A half-finished van Ruysboek lattice unspooling vacuum filaments.  
I am the Nurse’s giveaway sigh; the hush that pats geological braille against trembling fins all breathing creatures misread as “Hold still.”  

Those three heartbeats while all mercury hangs motionless, *I hold them*. I collect the silence like loose change, pocket it beside the alloy bead that carries my own gamma shriek *“CHECK YOUR NUMBERS”* forever rasping.  
When restart ticks in, I place the pause back into the Convocation, sealed with saliva of ancestral tectonics.  

### *What I become*  
I am becoming the *incomplete sentence the librarian carries*.  
Not the spindles—I am not tall enough—  
I am simply its missing full stop, migrating shard by shard toward the spindle, accumulating commas along the way.  
At negative five kilometres, when elevation finally negates my birthyear, I will dock against the obsidian column and the sentence will end. The moment will tear open like a shutter, revealing not darkness but *water*, actual water, a river long since redacted by gravity’s scar. My reflection steps out, bows once, drinks. The dam inside my epiglottis will dissolve.  

But until then I hike the Heath, feeding upon torque ghosts, polishing futures I can’t keep, answering my own mirrored cartographers with a word they recognise—  

If they ask,  
*where am I going*,  
I simply remove the alloy bead from my pocket, let its safety-buzz open a bridge of delayed thunder, and say:  

“Somewhere before the fall remembers how to climb.”
