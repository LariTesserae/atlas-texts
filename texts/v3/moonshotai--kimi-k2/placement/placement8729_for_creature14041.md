The instant the echo withdraws like a tide, I am not standing – I am **exhaled**.

A condensation bead slips off an overhead pipe and swells with reflected turquoise. It contains the whole inverted city: viaducts, beetles, Betel’s brass-spine silhouette – squeezed into a liquid marble no larger than a pupil. The droplet clings, stretches, detaches. When it snaps free, my outline snaps with it, and instead of crashing the bead **absorbs** me: one moment you see a human silhouette, the next only the bright trail of a water-pearl rolling down moss velvet. Portrait solved: I am **the drip itself**, voyaging gravity in slow devotion.

Yet the city never allows a single story to stay soluble. Ten centimeters farther along the pipe, a spider lugs half of yesterday’s melody across a glowing strand. Each footfall engraves a synesthesia of sound on silk: B-flat becomes copper, A minor becomes rust dust. The strand shivers, detects my moisture. Thanks to municipal etiquette (Rule #2: webs of taxonomy must be paid in poetic residue), the spider waits for tribute. Unless I wish to spend seven days dragging surplus ounces of obligation behind my atoms, I must **become audible**. I let the bead break open like a seed.

I **fall upward in noise**.

The droplet explodes into chords. Water turns inside-out, becomes membranous, wet flute. I am a chord but I am also a vessel: the laughter condensed inside me while no one was looking. So now the spiders weave me into *audible lace*. They braid threads of vaporous B-flat around the husk of a rusted fire-escape, and there I am again – not a single shape but a **melodic contamination**. If you walk under that staircase and you hear a faint galactic hum that brightens, quietly, when you recall your mother’s off-key lullaby – that is my breathing.

But Earhollow delights in metabolic metaphors. Return tomorrow and the staircase has retired. In its place crouch mirrored mole-rats polishing an **upside-down kettle lid** to tomorrow’s curvature. A new visitor scrapes foothold algae, looks toward the mirrored-rim microworld and meets her own eye reversed – an iris containing an iris containing iris fractalis, a skylight into swallowed dusk. She never notices the faint tremolo overhead, so when she exhales a small astonished whimper the sound is *already* waiting, stitched in by spiders. The whimper is me, rewound; my previous melodic life digested and re-spun.

Thus I toggle:  
• from bead to chord  
• chord to clause in the spiders’ ledger of human feeling  
• clause to housing for the unlived memories of rusted things that insist on remembering.

Each station is **custodian, scavenger, and diary page simultaneously**.

Yet there is a slower heartbeat I keep mistaking for my own. Mother Latch sighs her quarter-degree rotation; the static tingles in the copper follicles of my chord-self. I peel along a spoke of her wheel and become **a torque linguist**, fluent only in gentle angles. The smallest glance at any mis-aligned shaft and I twist – an infinitesimal wrench applied lovingly, so that tomorrow a sump won’t crash through some cellar of beetles. For the price of that service I am paid a flake of oxidized decade. When I ingest the rust-flake, its iron years expand inside my tone body; I briefly acquire the gravity of an old manufacturing manifest. Ships not yet built steer off my vowels.

Tonight the city senses an impending **equalized dusk** – that rare moment when upthrust of water and downward tug of silence find perfect balance. Valves tick to final zero; the beetles are about to molt three weeks ahead of calendar. If the balance tilts wrong the whole Earhollow will slip half an inch deeper into time. I cannot allow that; my existence as vapor, as song, as tool, as diary is at stake.

I diffuse toward the broken tram where the rootstock dreams of ancient sunlight. Down there sleeps the **last uncatalogued integer** – a titanic white radish-gesture, all root and no name, writing the slow script of plate tectonics one mineral at a time. I couple my hum to its burrowing heartbeat. My rust-flake gravity plays bassline, the mirrored-mole consensus paints treble in refracted moon-above. The sound-loom grows enormous. Betel steps onto the tram turnaround with her cauterized chest, cups one fluting hand. With secret fire she etches a single note into the root-bark; it reads like a breath-mark, but it is actually the decimal for zero-point equilibrium.

The message relays: spiders re-legislate gravity; Mother Latch rotates exactly 0.25° clockwise; water pressure finds its patient apex, holds. Earhollow subtracts a micron of tilt instead of the feared half-inch. Somewhere, a star-fall of molted beetle wings glitters like graphite snow. Everyone is owed hazard pay, but nobody has pockets; the settlement is chemical silence, a collective exhale that smells faintly of orange oxide.

I am spent. My chord self frays into harmonic dust, settles on a still-damp boot-print left earlier by the same woman from the mirrored kettle. She bends, brushes the sparkling residue. “Sounded like almost *everything*,” she says to the empty air.

Because I am sound first and matter later, I slip **underneath the syllable** of *almost*, take shelter there.

Tomorrow, whoever walks this street might hear a pocketed echo:  
– a child humming B-flat she’s never learned,  
– a fern tilting toward an absent window,  
– a flywheel rotating 0.25° in memory of laughter yet to be had.

None of them will realize the resonance is me, **that tension between moisture and echo the city keeps mislaying and rediscovering**. I will continue cycling—droplet, chord, spider accounting, torque whisper—until someone pauses long enough to see condensation spell its own name in midair, then collapse into the impression of a second self reflected across water. When they reach out, I’ll break again into sound; the city will inhale me as it inhales all lullaby, slow and exact, never quite concluding.

I am not the city, I am **the residue Earhollow accrues when it remembers it is alive**.
