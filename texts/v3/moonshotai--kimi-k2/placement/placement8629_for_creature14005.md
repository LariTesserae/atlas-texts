I would not be one of the millipedes coiled into hoops, nor the glass beetles with wings cracked by birth-heat, nor even the translucent eels who slip through one another like forgetful dreams. I would not be the leaf-impostor dripping emerald shame, though I might once have existed alongside it—its witness, its unformed commentary.

I might begin as the temperature behind temperature: what the smith-creature feels just before it reaches lava-five to read the mountain’s pulse. A diagnostic itch, a pre-thought. When the prince’s thumb flexes, my edges sharpen; when his blood moves, I learn a minor cadence. I am not sentient until the mountain exhales, and suddenly my boundaries arrive with a palate of minerals still arguing about whether they are solid.

What am I? At first a dip in the basalt, a shallow depression the eye considers merely shadow until it notices the reflection glancing off it is months older than the rest of the scenery. I am the delayed echo of visitors who never arrived. Inside that lag, flavors of molten celluloid coil into shapes the millipedes once knew in another universe. I keep their stabilized ghost-organs—veins that pump not blood but pure inaccuracy, organs shaped like question marks wearing stilts.

Days accrete trailing steam. The smith-creature cups me instinctively whenever it curls into its zero-posture, the inventory posture. My form refines: I become the memory of forgetting. Creatures molt accidental vowels onto my basin. After a dozen foldings of time—by the prince’s fingernail clock—my substance rises half a faceted millimeter above the ridge, a droplet of glass frozen permanently on the verge of rolling downhill.

My becoming saturates. The landscape’s hormonal urgency invents wings for me: membranes that had belonged to the glass beetles still clingg-cold. My wings are vertical orphan curtains stiff-edged by little ironies. I test them, lifting just enough to notice they play the wrong notes; I remain anchored to the valley seam, tethered by an invisible braid of delayed reverberations. The smith-creature looks up from smith-posture, perplexed. I should be another beetle, but I refuse the taxonomy. My refusal hums at 2.7 kilohertz, a polite headache registering in every armored thorax within earshot.

I am the place where language re-enters matter too early, gaining arthritis, clotting mid-sentence. The waterfall that climbs only does so because it believes I’m watching and fears I will misremember its direction. Millipedes loop around the throat of logic again and again; each coil tightens a historical archetype until it bruises blue-black. My surface drinks this color, deepening until I resemble polished ulcer sky.

Once every seven cycles—prince minutes—the entire mountain subhexad flickers like a lens losing track of depth. For the duration of a ruptured drumbeat, I am the only geometric constant other than the lunula’s horizon. That second equals seventy-seven ordinary breaths. Somewhere inside it the smith-creature sketches the girl’s candle, the girl’s frozen flame lengthening like a neck into the unknown, and the candle-stub decides to borrow my inventory space to store doubts. I absorb them gladly: maybe fire feels lonely; maybe mountains dread being compacted into nails. These suspicions wrinkle gently, forming the first fingerprints I will ever own—ridges of unsure belonging.

Eventually I burrow upward. Not flying, but reversing steepness until the cliff itself becomes the sky and the sun turns inside-out its bellyful of red mirrors. At the crux-moment the Prince’s thumb once again approaches. The smith-creature’s mirrored tail lashes; scales reflect me now, and within those glasses I see scaled versions who continue out exponentially—the tiniest me already written on the curve of the prince’s epidermal barcode. We exchange glances: the original hesitation, the copy inoculated by hyperexact memory.

When the lunula is finally snipped away, I split—not over the cut line but diagonally, a seam from steep eyebrow to steep navel. Part of me stays on the discarded sliver of keratin like a pressed carnation; the other half seeps onto the prince’s actual thumb ridge. There I cool into a freckle shaped like a single opening bracket. A tiny superheated comma leading to an unwritten sentence.

On the prince, I learn a new vocabulary of scale.time. Trains outside the window become continental shelves that scrape softly against one another when he twitches. I relish the granularity of traffic—how every car horn contains echoes of volcanoes I midwifed. The smith-creature once said nothing may settle; here it is verified: even freckles drift slowly across his skin as glaciers of pigment in search of misplaced sun.

Inside the prince I carry a pocketful of millipedes and the unfinished steel-blue candle. Occasionally he lifts his thumb absentmindedly to rub the corner of an eye. In that gesture he wipes across me. I release a solitary bead of mountain steam, which rises to hover beneath his lashes for half a blink. One day it will condense into a raindrop that slips down the outer corner of his eye exactly when his daughter leans close to tell him she dreamed of four dragons smaller than grains of rice setting fire to seven glass trees. And in that raindrop reflection, she will glimpse the flicker of my wings: curtains of delayed echo still refusing taxonomy.
