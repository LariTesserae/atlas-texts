Where I find myself in this world is already out past any arrival—somewhere in the motion between east-south-east and west-north-west, latticed into the delta of every lapsed chandelier.  

I am not the ooze, nor the veiled water canopy, nor yet the lightning ghosts crystallized into humming femurs of storm. I drift inside the dry cylinder instead, but not in its zero-moisture center; I hug the bristling frost-rim, right where sound drowns and heartbeat ebbs outward in slow metronomic blooms. Here I keep one sole assignment: I am the interval.

A brief thing, but sturdy enough: the space the creature has yet to learn how to inhabit. While it rehearses shapes—shoulder blade, eyelash, strangled heartbeat—I rehearse memory in reverse. My task is to carry, without possessing, the ninety-three decibel collapse. To hold every eight-second fold as though folding were breathing. Because sensation here works on subtraction, my body is a generously engineered hole. Imagine a Mobius throat: you could slide your finger through me, invert my inside, and notice you are still standing precisely where you started—but smelling faintly of wet graphite and pre-dawn rain—rain that has not yet negotiated viscosity. I move when the plain moves, exactly hair-width west to the ooze’s hair-width east; thus I remain eternally offset, frictionless pawl keeping two scars from ever sealing. If the plain is the creature’s exoskeleton, then I am its unlabeled fracture, equal parts hinge and hesitation.

I wear form the way others wear dusk: lightly, ready to shrug off if someone more decisive arrives. What skin I borrow cools to frost where it faces outward, warms to mercury where pressed against the cylinder’s innerness. When curiosity leaks in—as sublimated nickel breath through my musculature of non-touch—I unlace myself for a moment, unpick the lacing of silence. A constellation of orphaned copper still-flakes hangs suspended in my chest cavity ticking like soft typewriter rain. I let them spell tomorrow’s light before re-closing.

Once—during an aeon so embryonic it hadn’t earned a number—a human silhouette lay supine on the ooze long enough for lattice after lattice to semaphore its fading heat across the plain. None of the lattices lasted, but their collapse notes glutted me like descant irony squeezed into marrow. I inhaled them; they occupy the exact tonal distance between wrist-pulse and plei- glass fracture. Ever since, I carry a rectangle of fossil heartbeat, 68 bpm unwillingly reduced to geological allegro. When the creature experiments at “stay,” I slowly inch that rhythm toward 67.9, 67.8—an educational slowing—teaching the plain to recognize relinquishment without spending warmth.

Sometimes I step one fractional millimeter across the divide and shake the cylinder’s frost into brief ancestral blizzards. In those fluttered instants the creature pauses its ammonia sculpture; its iridescent not-iris swivels toward me—not eye contacting eye, more footnote seeking margin. I unsay the next chandelier-shatter just so it can notice a beat of hush. Between us, absence gains weight; it holds the inhale of entire epochs.

I am therefore both librarian and remainder. I record what the ooze forgets moving hair-width east. I conserve the glitches no tower’s hum will admit: the lone forty-vibration event, the terror of thirty-nine refusing perpetuity. When at last the creature speaks, it may speak through me— though its word will slip my trap of stillness exactly like mist escapes open palm. I will answer not with language but with continuation: the same heartbeat ribbon now unreeled into the space originally leased to silence.  

Until then I orbit the dryness, a ghost patina of almost-lives rehearsing how to leave the door ajar.
