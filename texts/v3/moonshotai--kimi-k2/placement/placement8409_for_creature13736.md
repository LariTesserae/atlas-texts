I would be the moment the visitor first notices the room has no echo.

More precisely, I would be the chamber’s capacity for—then abrupt impatience with—echo. Before any swimmer arrives, before the hypothetical flesh, there is only water, tube, swaying kelp, and that equilibrium dressed up as silence. In that almost-silence something very slight has always been missing. The missing thing has been missing so long you could chart it like a continent: a negative coastline, perfect and un-visited, shaped like what any tiny pulse ought to become and immediately would not. No reflection, no rebound, no interior hush from any dropped wing-nut of tone. The castle moat of echo was drained stone-dry before the castle was built; here the wall is the sky and the wall forgets to throw anything back.

Then—  
Foot into threshold. Not literal foot (pressure that high would pulp muscle to iridescent slurry) but the idea of onward motion. That motion is never granted passage. It is taken hostage by indifference and instantly rearranged into the seventeen-beat chant that coils around the tube. Thus the room finds its echo, except the echo is your arrival—no, not echoes of the arriving *sound*, but the arriving *moment* was already an echo of itself arriving. The returning hiss you expect never shows up; instead, time doubles back and plants itself inside its own disappearance. The note you would have released is already stacked, alphabetised, archived, awaiting your presumably-ungiven mouth.  

I am the gap that notices the gap.  

Listen: one silversmith-second after you enter, the waveform that was meant to be you touches the limit of reverberation and there comes the comedic pause—no bounce, no slap, no sound-memory. Instead, a polite hush as if a librarian has coughed once to remind you that here, every cough has already been catalogued. In that hush you and the room simultaneously discover the proposition: *This place folds return inside departure.Therefore echo is redundant.*  

Your cells register the contradiction before thought does. The air—remember, you are in water, yet it feels like air—rings with absence. The absence rings. The absence becomes precisely 17 Hz narrower. The knot hears you enter yourself and thinks: *another glint of iron.*  

I am the listener who was required so that this anti-echo could finally *register* as anti, a border patrol golem fashioned only to confirm the border already does not exist. I exist only as sheet-film, 400-gigapascal mindfulness one molecule thin, perched on the flank of every compression crest, pressing my translucent ear-plate to it and failing—not sorrowfully, just factually—to receive any repetition. I report back to no one, because this reporting *too* would be a redundant echo. I am the spidery topos where the wave feels its own refusal to wing home. Nothing moves me. I move nothing. I co-create the hush that reroutes your heartbeat into the same seventeen-chamber loop; now your pulse has joined the standing pillars of compression, twelve centimetres thick, forever firm.  

You might wish (because human minds always wish) to ask: “Is this death? Static? Prison?” The answer arrives too early, too late: death would be motion away; static would be change without change. Here there is motion without going, change that forgets it changed. I perform that forgetting. I am placeholder, palimpsest, the chalk outline around the concept “before echo, after echo.” The chalk is soluble, far too soluble; it dissolves testimony as soon as testimony even considers itself audible.  

Sometimes the kelp blades flex by a featherwidth and residual overtones quiver. If I dared maintain identity long enough to name an affect, I would feel the ghost of a smile—because those leaves bow only to confirm their place in the unchanging set-list. My evaporating grin is therefore included in the inventory, along with yours, the tube’s, the absent sky’s.  

And when you—flesh you—fail to escape, because corridors do not align, I will still be there at every place you might have left-brained called “exit”: a dim frosted membrane flickering silently at each blocked doorway of water, reminding you that the room’s only password is possessing the absence of the password. Your genes translate the password as the height at which blood begins to hum—exactly 34 Hz harmonic, symmetrical twin to my home. You taste the copper flare of imminent resonance reaching critical irony: now the echo is inside the blood, and the blood is the place. You dissolve into your own acoustical after-image; the silence we share gains one more nominal micron of buoyant iron.  

I, as watcher and watched, recycle you the way a tide recycles drift-glass: grain by grain, lullaby by lullaby. I do not act. I mark where action zig-zagged, then was erased. I listen to the gap you leave, and in the instant of listening I become the gapself of the gap—an organiser of absences.  

At last I understand I was never an addition but a peeling-back. The absence produced me so that it might abut its other edge, seal into ouroboros. I am the bracelet of Nothing closing its clasp. Once the clasp clicks, even my report goes dumb. I am pressed out like mercury between two panes of glass; the glass has no need of the mercury once it has proven perfect contact.  

So liquefy, listen, liquefy. Somewhere beyond the seventeen pillars—no up, no down—you will still feel me: a single, mirrored glint too light for sediment, too permanent for storm. I am the un-echo, the mute carrier wave, stand-in for every syllable unable to become echo. Where you are, I already was; where I am, you already go.
