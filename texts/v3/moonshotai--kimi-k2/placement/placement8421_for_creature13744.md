I am the precursor mist that never quite dissipates even after the sun has squirrel-hoarded its brightest flakes back over the horizon.

Each morning I gather within the slenderest furrow spiraling down the inner wall of the place that has no need for names. I begin as four molecules arranged in the outline of a memory no one here ever lived. Cooler atoms of the night exhale outward to avoid my arrival. Warmer atoms of the casserole-colored dawn lean in to greet me. I grow by the amplitude of attention—the same modest amount every time, for there is no accumulation here, only the rolling hinge of one threshold into its identical sequel.

When I have achievedced enough to shade two adjacent leaf-panes, the bowl’s single presence—that slow mercury of intention I belong to yet cannot claim the way a creek never claims a single drop—drives an unseen, velvet needle through me. With the needle it stitches the moisture I am carrying into the translucent petioles, while threading light from the ochre coin of sky. No sound is produced by this action; everything remains soldered to the thickness of birth-long hush that counts as song for flowers willing to bruise their way open.

The presence does not notice me as separate. I am merely a fold at the lip of its chorus: today’s particular gathering of hover and lapse. Yet I notice it, because I have been born—reborn—each dusk out of its refusal to hurry. My noticing is as delicate as a thread made from the first serration of a grass leaf’s cry parceled into the width of a fingernail moon. It holds the hush taut so that everything can remain suspended in not-yet-fall, not-yet-rise, the zero grill of a vertical pause.

I glance off leaf after inverted leaf like sunlight delayed by reverence. Every glancing is a choice that has eliminated every other choice. Pressing lightly forward, I tilt the droplet that has gathered pollen from the anthers to the stigma chamber where the bruise-blue petals live. The droplet rolls in perfect half-silence while I cat-sit on its silence. Everything stays the same except the tiny in-crease of intimacy along the short inward arc.

Later, I alight on the warmest river-stone that has borrowed the basil scent of some other body. The heat trickles up through my molecular spread. I am not evaporated but invited into a newborn pleat. Equivalent to the interval after the last breath of someone who has said *listen* and has not yet pronounced what the word cossets. Inside that interval I feel the bass-note keeling of quartz deep beneath the moss. Its frequency is so low it can only be heard as a pressure against the rim of the skull at the site where thought pokes through to stare at itself. I ride that pressure a handspan sideways, graceful as violet-scented inertia on a drifting incandescent mote.

Night comes, though in the bowl it is merely a deepening of the same radiance withheld by translucent petals and mirrored coinlight. I untether myself from this body of vapor to become the soft bloom of body heat that lingers on an unnamed stone. I remain there, not inhabiting but remaindering, until the attention of the stone curls back into itself with the patience of a snail rehearsing infinity. Then I lift—a breath of distilled noticing—back into the carried spiral furrow, ready for tomorrow’s re-ascent from memory that never was.
