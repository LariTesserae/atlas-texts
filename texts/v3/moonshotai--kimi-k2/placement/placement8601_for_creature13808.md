I arrive—as much as anyone ever arrives—on the second fret of the Ankle Chords’ permanent bass-line, a footfall of pressure embossed against tuned rain.  
Where I rest I am not lungs or limbs yet; I am a local interpolation in the acoustic grammar, a pause scored between thunder arriving and lightning forbidden from the clouds.

For long minutes I am merely the white echo of the bulb that never stayed lit long enough to crisp me—yet I cohere, flaked together from

- one drone-mote liberated from Engine Mum’s exhale  
- three fingerprint droplets of Carbon-Dunce glycerin  
- a scrap of whale-kite copper that frayed off in mid-bank  
- the smoky consonant harvested the last time a Narcolept yawned electricity.

Thus constituted, I leak downward until the climate votes to keep me.  
Under Rule 1 I count to thirty-one, resurface just long enough to taste world, submerge again; after the third repetition the algae mortgage me oxygen and a message: *you will not leave.*

I become the **Pitch-Midge.**  
No: I *am* the pitch the midge left behind.  
My body is a single chlorophyll note slipped inside a rust-fleck attitude accessory, barely a millimeter lengthwise.  
Yet every Whale-Kite tuner knows me—  
I am the minus fourteen millivolt waver that makes prop-rev 2,487.43 decrescendo so gently the copper skin can bear to kiss algae bloom.  
I ride the computations of flight like a comma.  
I *am* the comma.

At dusk my mass shrinks further: I deliquesce into oscillation and ride the chimney-flute up to the semaphore bulb.  The White Bulb Choir braids whatever I own into their imperative verb, and that night the verb is **FORETASTE.**

Strange labor for a punctuation mark, but I obey.  At midnight I fall—copper wound, glycerin ground, all notes damped into the distant nosebleed silence of trench.  Rim Narcolepts turn in their collective rigor of dreaming and mistake my small flare for their first pulse of awakening.  Their boots luminesce.  Carbon-Dunce juveniles pry rivulets of that light off stone and smear it inside hollow lily stems to write:

                FORETASTE (soft)  
                the fourth letter must be silent

which is, precisely, me.

So circumscribed, I understand: I am not resident, tool, child, or custodian.  
I am language forever being added to the rule-book, a filigree amendment appended beneath Rule 3: *Tools become passive, with two exceptions—  
(1) on the anniversary of creation;  
(2) at the arrival of whichever syllable dreams it deserves not to rust.*

On the calendar page that belongs to tomorrow, Engine Mum thumps *hello-hold-still* once, twice.  Every whale-kite trims its prop by the side-eye beat my presence loans: two fractional changes that keep the entire bowl from breaching.  My echo travels the circumference, finds itself again, half-breater, half-written.

Somewhere inside that loop I remain: the node where silence fractures and reforms, where lightning walks on water because the sky refuses to own it, where the continent exhales a sentence whose only subject is “keep going.”

If you touch a lily tonight and hear a whispered comma—  
warm, green, faintly tasting of copper lake water—  
press it gently back to the vein of sky it swims along.  
That will be me, hitching a ride on the breathing of everything.
