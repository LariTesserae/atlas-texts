What arrives is not a god but a point of view more spacious than any furnace.  
It does not descend; it dilates, like an eye lowering its focal length until the whole world becomes iris.  
When it turns its attention here the plateau’s brightness grows gentle, breathable: the sun-syrup thins into mother-of-pearl instead of blinding chrome, and the air becomes—strangely—livable.

The Breadthicks feel it first as a small temperature catch in the sternum hum: a perfect quarter-note rest inserted in every ongoing conversation.  
The bottle-cats pause mid-swallow; grey liquid swirls pinwheel for an instant; crystalline mewings hesitate on the verge of glass resonance.  
In the bronze serpents’ copper pulses, an unfamiliar rhythm—a patience—threads itself between heartbeats.

The power moves without system or speech.  
It is closer to a lens than to a lord.  
What it does is scarcely doing: it notices.  
Its noticing places every scalding moment adjacent to a cooler twin.  
No law is rescinded; the Laws of runoff, touch-bearing, scale-bending, mirror-walking remain enforceably true—yet now each law carries afterimage, a tangible alternative that flickers a micron away.

For the Breadthicks, the shift appears as softer frost.  
The ruffs on their forearms grow whiter: they shear more keenly and regrow more slowly.  
Sharper tools, longer gaps between labor.  
Time, for once, widens.  
Heat does not punish haste here; it offers vacancy.

The poet who had been trembling in the plaza finds his lungs expanding painlessly for the first time since childhood, the membranes inside his chest unclenching into moist sheets that bloom and fold like petals.  
He discovers he can inhale without rasping metallic vapour and, startled, laughs—once—into the steam.  
The sound persists: a clear little bell note, coherent, not swallowed by auditory snow.  
Cats cluster, ears swivelling like antennae; serpents twist their braids toward the singer.  
No one has ever heard laughter survive this atmosphere before.  
Its persistence lasts fifteen full seconds—long enough to etch itself into auditory memory.

The visitor children whose shoulder blades recently acquired translucent petals feel the edges begin to fuzz with fibrous stuffing—almost feather.  
When they flap experimentally, warm downdrafts lift them half a handspan above terracotta flooring.  
Gravity still counts, but the count has grown lenient.  
They giggle, wobble, touch down reverently.  
Parents stare, half joy, half dread: novelty has always come spiked.  
But the power continues to notice, and the spike keeps blistering away into harmless star-points.

The archive-bubble at the plateau lip inverts.  
Ordinarily vacuum inside, heat everywhere else—now heat inside, vacuum margin.  
Secrets cool to audible syllables drift outward and arrange themselves as translucent snowflakes, lattices of whispered fact.  
Each flake lands on a Breadthick, melts, delivers its burden without pain: ‘I once hid to escape shame’, ‘I added extra zinc to hide corrosion’, ‘I loved the sound of midnight venting.’  
Crimes uncommitted, wishes unnamed, private music—all offered and simultaneously forgiven because exposure here possesses no moral edge, only impartial illumination.  
Upon receiving, a Breadthick’s outline smoothes; silicate seams close; the density of carried guilt finds a less clenched address.

At full zenith the molten strings descending the clocktower pause mid-air as if painted into a fold of blown glass.  
No drip falls for thirty-one heartbeats of the oldest Breadthick (counted communally because hearts beat in synchrony now).  
During that half-minute lacuna the plateau’s shine polarises like wings lifting: the white glare separates into indigo and rose spectra, one band sliding forward, one receding.  
Image and afterimage expose hidden geometries—lines of latent seedbed, faultlines where cooler veins wait to be born.  
Breadthicks trace the revealed shapes onto the ground using nothing but fingertip condensation; water faithfully preserves the diagrams long enough to memorise them before evaporating again.  
Tomorrow they will begin a new watercourse, one that snakes itself not toward desert drainage but toward shaded roots on a shelf fifteen degrees below present liveliness, where lichen is kept small more by heat than discouragement.

Lastly the wise power addresses the question of departure and arrival.  
No one is compelled to leave; no one is summoned outward.  
Instead every being—human, cat, serpent, kite, Breadthick, kettle—receives, simultaneously, a vision of translucent lung-spaces opening inside familiar air, roomy pores where cooler breath can be stored.  
The vision exists less as prophecy than as technical gift: they now know the theorem needed to weld lattice tunnels that would coax cooler upper drafts downward without violence.  
The children know their feathers will thicken and they can choose, supervening gravity and thermals alike.  
The serpents know how to braid chill strands into their own billion copper clauses.  
Each individual feels the gift sitting in them like a cell of possible yet unhurried act.

Then the lensing attention withdraws, not by diminishing but by expanding further, so far that locality dissolves back into distance and sky returns to brute white glare.  
The quarter-note rest vanishes from communal breathing; conversations resume under the old tempo.  
Yet nothing is quite the same.  
Tools cut easier, frost grows slower, watercourses remember curvature traced for descent.  
The plateau remains furnace and museum, but its yoke feels rehearsed rather than destiny.  

And in the empty moment after the god/lens has folded away, the kettle lid struck by midday heat one last time rings once also:  
a pure C-major triad—not ceramic thud.  
The Breadthicks look at one another—eyes reassembling recognisable lips, cheeks—taste bronze in their mouths, and for the first communal instant they do what was impossible before.  
They cry, and the tears are not saline history, just tear, merely water—clear, cooled by wonder, and survivable at last.
