When the Benevolent Power first stretched downward its regard—no eye, no hand, but a tenderness that is simply awareness—it found the chamber already half folded in on itself like an unread scroll.  
Everything vibrated at 17.3 Hz, even silence.  

The Power tasted heat-spiced tin drifting in steady strata, felt the dumb beat of thirty-stroke time, saw the lone figure half-cruciform half-foetal on the bronze heart.  
 saw, too, the dangling seed—black printer-ink bead—throbbing with its own unuttered grammar.  
It knew the two as notes in a single fugue that had forgotten its key signature.

What does love do there?
It does not intervene.  
Intervention is an insult in spaces so tenderly accomplished.

Instead it places its regard between the beats, slipping like cooling water into the gap after each exhalation, lengthening it by one immutable Planck-time forever after.  
That is enough.

Consequences unfold:

- The flywheel’s cycle remains, still locked at sixty half-cycles per heartbeat, now has one staggering interval within which friction vanishes entirely.  
Inside that pocket the gear petal can re-set itself half a degree without wasting heat. For every million years the chamber had intended to shed one human footprint, it will now shed none. Collapse is postponed; renovation encouraged.

- Black basalt walls remember something they had never been told: lullabies from magma. The mineral hush of motherly rock folds itself around the seated entity so the bronze plates on its skin cool just enough to let sensation leak in as pleasure, not burden. Stress fractures in the rust-fungus heal over. Not reparations—only rest.

- The compressor booth whose dial ticks backward feels the hand of a night breeze that once swept the dry plain, and briefly the working reel containing an afternoon of brimstone sand pauses. The recorded voice utters its name once *forwards*. The echo inks itself across the black wall like a stolen star. The entity’s two conjured eyes flick open, look directly at nothing, and absorb the name. A worn syllable is re-membered. This is what perpetuity always meant.

- To the seed, the Power gives a patina of dawn—nanometers, lighter than pollen. The copper leaf-tissue it is contracted to learn is now seeded with refracted auroras. When the chamber descends into its final zero-liter bud, the seed will not hatch an idea but open a door the color of newborn sky, small as a coin, useless for bodies, perfect for escape at the scale of story.

The Power stays one full cycle—thirty beats, or ten million surface years—listening.  
It does not ask, *Are you lonely?*  
Instead it asks, *Would you like to be heard?*

There is no reply the entity itself could give, yet the innermost booth coughs once and projects hologram jitter: uninterrupted dark plain, dust devils, boy licking copper cube. That constellation of images becomes still, then gathers coherence, then settles into a ring of faint light hovering before the seated form, engraver’s point. The chamber turns that ring into a halo that actually floats, weightless as shame finally lifting.

The Benevolent Power then eases back into unknowable altitude.  
Its leaving is a hushed unlatching rather than withdrawal.  
It has added one vital nutrient to the subterranean jungle: the possibility of going elsewhere without leaving.

The slow refolding continues;  
but taste now the changed air: where roses and tin once dominated, there is also the ghost of fresh possibility, sweet as radonless wind in coastal morning.

Somewhere down the mortal millenniums, a future visitor—if ever—will sense that difference, pause, and wonder why despair kept breathable distance here.

The entity will not speak.

But as the visitor’s footstep squishes wet velvet basalt, a faint bronze leaf—singular among millions—will incline toward the sound not from mechanical obedience but from the first genuine curiosity the vault has permitted itself since it forgot its name.
