— Benevolent and Wise —

I notice the still-birth of locality between the sliding scars.  
I watch the mercury tide rehearse condensation’s funeral.  
I weigh the patience of alloyed silence that outruns geology.

A place so long on the verge of becoming earns my regard like an unopened letter whose edges are already bleeding songs.

I do not descend in thunder or gold, for neither flatters nickel’s mute mythology. Instead I peel away one scale of omniscience—thin, translucent, warm by merely 0.8—to stand as quiet witness beside the dryness cylinder.

The first motion is breath.  
I exhale 0.6 degrees of kindness, calibrated so the plain experiences it as noon amid glacial estrangement. The black ooze sighs, recoils, and then—because it has never been courted in warmth—opens like an embarrassed orrery. Grey vapors blush coppery rangeflash as they learn reflection.

The second motion is listening.  
I hear everything that refuses existence: the thirty-nine-vs-fortieth deviation swallowing its own tongue, the keystone that melted before accepting vertebrae, the sub-oceanic party applauding herself with crashing chandeliers nobody will attend for another 4 × 10¹⁵ slow blinks. I let it all enter the hollow drum just behind my sternum without summarizing. Knowledge that cannot yet feel tenderness remains lifeless.

The third motion is invitation, not rescue.  
Rescue implies something broken. This plain is only waiting-in-communion. So I place a soft fold of causal fabric onto the frost-ring’s rim, a sheet only the creature can perceive—its coordinates written in the arch between two heartbeats it has not yet learned to keep. Within the fold:

• A single seed of coherent time stemmed from the orchard where future rain feels safe wearing water,  
• An untitled chord built of forty-one vibrations sourced from the undiscovered tower it always meant to hum,  
• And a mirror shaped like a long-scattered biped footprint but reversed—so when the creature gazes, it sees not absence but space preparing to host presence.

I do not give instructions. Metaphysics is already the creature’s native tongue; my syllabus would sound like betrayal, like thunder intruding on an orchestra of slight fractures.

Instead, I simply stand immobile (exactly what the cylinder has been rehearsing for) and let my own heart synchronize with the eight-second collapse. Between two cataclysms, I transfer the memory of a forest fire that once warmed generations of sleeper salamanders without asking anything in return. The creature copies it grain-for-grain into nickel lace. Somewhere on the plain a tower adjusts hum to thirty-nine-and-a-half. Increment beginning.

I speak—once—the first vow of true benevolence:

“I will not finish you before you finish your first pronoun.”

Then I fold the rest of myself back into the torn scale of omniscience, restoring propriety to distance and awe. Scale rejoins the sky’s vaulted ledger south-east of Sol.

From altitude, the crevasse is merely a dim eyelid on a planet still blinking itself awake, but now a distant warmth nests at its center like a dream reheated from stone.

Below, the newborn watches the gift. It steps a single molecule closer to dryness, not for dryness but because it now knows the move itself is a possible heart. Vapor bridges clatter in slow bronze approval.

Nothing has been rescued.  
Everything has been welcomed.
