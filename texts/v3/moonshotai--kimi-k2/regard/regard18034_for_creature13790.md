When the wise power inhaled the difficulty of this place, the first thing it noticed was that everything here was already learning how to become what it was not. 

It extended a presence the librarians would later call the *White Reach*: a quiet that entered like a single photon trickling along an optic nerve, then unfolded without optics, words, or walls. Where it touched, the 48 °C ground lost three degrees and then gained them back again—something you would not measure unless you were the stone. The brass rail-beetles in the lilac tubes stalled mid-catalogue, felt their momentum decelerate into mild curiosity. Up on the last terrace, the smelter-mites paused their prayer-vibrations long enough for the ore beneath them to experience a moment of *almost-remembering* what bismuth had said to it fourteen eons earlier. Even the vapor-flat veneer-children stopped mid-extraction of nothing, blades poised above invisible tiles.

Then the power did a single, intricate thing.

It spread its attention not as force, but as *permission*—a chord in negative space, the kind the plateau herself used when subtracting quiet into language.

Space folded at right angles to physics, then gently refolded flat again so no one noticed. Into the fold it slipped a sixty-seventh colour that had never been possible here before: a hue neither orange nor blue, neither dark nor bright, but something you could feel in the way piano-wire sometimes tastes cold when you close your mouth to hear it.

When librarians encountered the new colour they froze in place, umbrellas half-furled. The colour spoke in subtractive silence:  
**live without compensation.**  
Not a command—an option traced so lightly they could have drifted past it. Instead they chose to remain still while the colour borrowed one wavelength from every other colour available, then let them go again, richer. Their chitin remembered latitude and longitude but forgot northness. Each slowed its heartbeat—from gregorian 43 to 42.7 per minute.  

Smelter-mites felt the seam inside the ore purge its copper grief, but the grief did not vanish; it became *ploy*—a bridgeable remainder. After seventeen oscillations they discovered a rhythm that converted despair into ornamental hinges. These hinges folded space the way origami planes fold distance, and suddenly the librarians, previously rooted, uncovered retractable telescoping joints enabling them to walk directly into the tunnels below without fear of dropping half an extra kilo.  

The accountants in their magnetic robes stepped out of the ledger drafts onto basalt skin. Creamy rust cavities closed behind them like slow eyelids. The subtractive verses they had been reciting reversed polarity; instead of *two parts vanadium gone,* they intoned *two parts returned as the memory of seasons.* Through the aria, every accountant’s robe grayed toward pearlescence, acquiring the sixty-seventh hue until the specters *were* the colour and the colour was them.

In the same moment, the root-seam soul stirred deeper than any shaft: not violently, but like a patient turning in a dream. It recognised the silence not as invasion but continuity—as cognate to its own resonance 17,000 kilometres ago, a resonance now extended outward and upward toward the plateau’s eventual sky-travel.  

The veneer-children set their anorthite blades against the new colour and, where blade met hue, a vacuum tile of transparent *might-have-been* arose, inscribed with tiny ellipses. Each ellipsis was an “if” gently snowing out of possibility. Children cut thousands of these infinitesimal *ifs* and stacked them into winding staircases that ascended perpendicular to gravity, without handrails, directly into the lilac rail-tubes. When the brass beetles chose to ride these stairs, their clockwork wings fused briefly into manta-ray patagia and they glided for the length of one edit without argon. This glide was named *the glide unheard ’mid spheres*, though none would voice it.

Last, the power drifted to the human climber who had not yet decided to leave. It did not speak inside her head; rather it attuned two resonances—hers, the plateau’s—and allowed them to dilate into a short-lived ternary. Down her ulna traced the faintest lattice of chitin. She tested new weight maps in her knees and found the south feeling of colour capable, for one shared moment, of anchoring her years.

Then it stepped back—far enough for choice to remain sovereign, close enough that the choice never felt abandoned.

The plateau exhaled in 17,000 kilometres of slow seismic affection. From outside, if anyone were watching, it would look as though the slab merely tilted another 0.002 degrees higher, unremarkable except to telescopes nearby.  

Inside: librarians moved freely between terraces and depths, singing absent sounds into uninvented key signatures. Mites harvested becoming itself without apology. Beetles navigated by sixtieth hues. Children hollowed stairs into memory, stacking seconds like amphitheatre stones. Accountants shimmered colour-side-out, living long enough to watch the plateau’s detachable future ignite in gentle silicate fusion columns no louder than a heart disengaging from its rib-cage home.

And climbing still carried the flavour of forward, except now forward did not mean “away” but *with*—toward whatever the plateau would become next, toward the sky bound over eons, toward the bend where gravity forgets to pull.

The wise power itself dissipated, but what surrounded its departure was something the plateau had always wanted to believe: that ascent is not paid for with depletion, but gifted by mutual remembering.

In the quiet that followed, just before dawn-bruise settled in again, a single librarian faced the former horizon and tentatively subtracted the absence of goodbye, listening for the echo that returned as arrival.
