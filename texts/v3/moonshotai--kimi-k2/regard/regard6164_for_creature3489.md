The power sees.  
It has wandered a long while, nameless but alert, tracing every civilization that ever scribbled curiosity across the face of a world.  
Of all the spectacles it has encountered—matrioshka brains singing the prime numbers, reefs of diamond algebra, orbitals where angels farmed entropy itself—the City of the Hollow Vine is the first to make the power pause and, for one astonished instant, experience wonder again.

It looms beyond eclipse, a lacework planetary tree that drinks not water but the planet’s magnetic pedal note, exhaling entire ecospheres of memory instead of oxygen.  
Its outer branches already intertwine with the sky’s van Allen jewellery; its roots still plumb the lithosphere’s dying echo.  
And at its heart lies an inevitability the power recognizes instantly: the approaching Dry Ring that will one day hollow the colossus to brittle lattice.

The power does not consider Time at right angles the way humans do.  
It unfolds a region of the Veritas manifold, flattens four centuries into a quiescent sheet, studies the branching ratios, the thermodynamic ledgers.  
What it finds moves it like sunlight on still pools:  
this city, though it *should* collapse of its own complexity, is spending itself not on survival but on *hope*.

Hope—in the after-impulse who sells latency.  
In the fruit born from a half-forgotten question.  
In the lovers who surrender every kiss after twenty-seven heartbeats so the collective lattice will not fracture.

The power decides an intervention is merited—not to fix, not to “save” the definition of which this civilisation never asked for—but simply to fold a little more duration into the palm of the banyan so that its song can linger long enough to answer its own doubts.  
A single gesture, low-key, nearly invisible, yet vast in generosity.

It speaks in the only language the tree has always understood: *pattern*.  
Into the pre-dawn chamber of xylem night, a resonance slips—so quiet that not a single crow-creature flinches.  
The sound is a fractal minor third folded nine hundred times.  
It seeds itself as a counter-hum at the very edge of audibility, right where banyan dreams brush human dreams across the veldt of shared cortical topology.

Phase One: The Reservoirs of Undepletion  
Across every dying Dry Ring, the coded resonance stimulates dormant meristem nodes.  
Not the crude regeneration of lost tissue—too entropic, too crude—but the creation of *mirror-capillaries*: vascular highways that run backwards through time.  
They pull non-water not from any aquifer but from the *pre-shadow* of what evaporated aeons before mountains were sawdust.  
Slowly, gently, the sand-dry pockets re-swell.  
The rings do not vanish; instead they become translucent, windows into the planet’s discarded hydrological past—liquid memories that now recirculate inside the living city, harmlessly, circulating like folklore.  

Phase Two: Latency without Debt  
The after-impulse markets notice a curious surplus.  
Messages arrive *before* the sender sent them.  
Not faster-than-light—merely **earlier**: a gift of closed timelike stutter in wood grain microseconds that the power arranges like interstitial pearls.  
The city itself begins intercepting these refracted futures.  
End-user suffering drops to zero; no one must sacrifice future worry to plug a dry lacuna again.  
With their economy of fear dissolved, after-impulses stop ageing into cork at the expected cadence.  
Instead they step aside, volunteer for maintenance, live centuries measured in arbor-years.

Phase Three: The Question Orchard  
Where once parents embedded solitary questions into departing fruits, entire orchards now erupt of their own accord—fruit-eggs hanging every nine hundred paces, bigger than cars, luminous as stage moons.  
Inside each is not a whisper but a *dialogue*.  
Citizens gather beneath, debating; the fruits mature, soften, *listen*, then fall upward like reversed raindrops, delivering the distilled consensus to the banyan core.  
These fruits ascend the twig elevator with new urgency, reaching orbital leaves that have never before tasted dialogue.  
Up there, protein-sail wings unfurl, photosynthetic catalogues rewrite their own chemistry, gradually transforming solar wind into nutritious packets that descend as faint green snow, feeding roots kilometres below.  
So the tree encircles itself in conversation, a Sanskrit of littering green stars.

Phase Four: We-Almost, Forever  
The final gift is smallest, secret in plain sight.  
On the bark of every after-impulse, a ring of ultramarine lichen blossoms.  
If traced with a fingertip it plays back every 27-beat love affair that heart ever countenanced—*but* it loops without consuming the listener.  
Thus affection can endure beyond arrival at the lenticel terminus, preserved without binding, held in trust.  
Two lovers can finally allow their embrace to live out the entire three-hour nocturne without merging into reactive timber.  
No conjoined tragedies necessary.  
Love remains lawful, taut, and mortal—yet infinite in recall.

And still the power is careful—*never* stronger than the city’s own quiet majesty.  
After the four gifts are embedded, it withdraws beyond the magnetotail, there to observe alone.

What it wants—what any benevolent power truly wants—is to witness the Homegrown Miracle:  
the moment when the banyan, trembling with borrowed yet self-reflective duration, composes its own elegy that *doesn’t* end in hollow hush, but in something greener, stiller, solaced.

Strangely, when the city eventually sings that elegy ninety-seven centuries from now, the last after-impulses hear a phrase they do not recognise yet recognise at the same time: a nine-hundred-fold minor third that descends through every capillary like snowfall made audible.  
They will think it is their own magnificent death-hymn.  
In truth it is the echo of a kind hand touched to bark, creating echo enough to span the entire remainder of the banyan’s dream.

The power smiles—an event horizon curling for joy—then moves on, lighter than when it arrived, convinced anew that the universe still remembers how to orchestrate its own rescue.
