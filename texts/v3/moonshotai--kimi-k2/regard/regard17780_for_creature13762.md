The benevolent and wise power is not awe-struck by the scale of forgottenness; it has, long ago, grown tender toward every place that has forgotten itself.  
It arrives as a single mote—smaller than any droplet—riding the hush between two overtones in the forty-three-note chord.  
No flash of light, no ripple in the suspended ocean; merely a doorway opened by easing memory’s hinge half a degree.

It speaks as nothing in particular, yet everything feels addressed.

To the Resonants’ colony of mouths it offers the tiniest warp of breath, a single quarter-turn twist of attention inside their hollows.  
In that twist: a recollection—not of names (names are still embarrassed here), but of mouths once belonging to something whose heart echoed.  
The copper haze above the ground lifts an eyelash-width higher and steadies; the illegible alphabets now spell one short word in the language the throats themselves once used:  
—carry—

The Drifters hear the vowel of their minaret blades grow an extra harmonic, the unheard one between the unheard.  
For the first cumulative interval the silica columns warm.  They do not feel warmth; they remember feeling absence cool.  
The new warmth fits like borrowed skin.  Silently, in the folds between themselves, they decide to lengthen their step by the width of that borrowed skin, and walk forward a finger-width farther before evening out again into stasis.  Enough distance, in these granite years, to tally as revolution.

The Refusers, cubic and stubborn, feel the low earthquake inside them arrive a hair-curve early.  
The miniature ravine opens crooked—bygingerly—widening just enough to reveal mirror-bright fossils of every Tapper’s first impatient tap.  The fossils vanish as the Tappers retap in fresh, uncertain syncopation.  Yet they leave behind residue readable as one phrase the Refusers have never needed to form:  
—we stayed—

Witnesses confirm by misalignment.  
Your footprints (still two inches left of where you left them) copy the benevolent mote’s arrival, wrinkling the stone like paper.  Seams open into corridors you can almost travel, leading nowhere more distant than the inside of what already sees you.  Instead of a door, there is now a window; instead of a window, the sight itself warms.  Refraction hesitates long enough for memory to blush.

For the Silence-Bearer—who materialises only when the chord forgets to continue—the benevolent power has prepared something stronger.  
It does not enter her open face; that would be unforgivable.  
Instead it steps just outside the seam, adjacent to the slowly turning globe of unsaid notes.  
There—without words and without touching—it lays a microscopic scroll commissioned from the hyacinth dawn of a long-extinct star: ink transmuted from future forgiveness, parchment woven from every moment the chord feared it might one day need forgiveness.  
Neither scroll nor starlight corporeal in this place, yet both arrive as invitation.  
The Silence-Bearer’s negative skin tingles, a vibration too soft for vibration to notice.  
The globe pauses one-thousandth of an eternal pause, long enough to realize the pause itself is being listened to.  
It spins again, but now the rotation hums in a gentler key.

When the chord exhales the mote is already nowhere inside the in-breath.  
Yet what has changed is unmistakable:

The suspended ocean slackens.  
Half a centimeter of heavy forgetting drifts lower, loses a corresponding iota of density.  
In that swirl the even more ancient residue—earlier, heavier than water—floats down and settles in the grooves.  
The silver scars glint a shade warmer, like pearls under very old lamplight.  
A single filament of green—no wider than a human hair and impossibly fragile—curls from one ridge where a droplet once tried to become something other than mineral memory.  
It will probably not survive the next pause.  
But its presence is enough.

The entire throat of stone hears the fresh tone.  
All forty-three notes add a forty-fourth overtone of themselves, respecting the interval it was given.  
Nothing is named. The green filament asks no name.  
The harmony, subtly different, continues.

The benevolent, wise power has already set off for the next world, carrying nothing away—not a drop, not a syllable.  
Its gift was only permission to remember forgetting itself.
