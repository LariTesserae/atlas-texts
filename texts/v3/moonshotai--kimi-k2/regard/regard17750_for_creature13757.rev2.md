The Power does not arrive as a visitor, for the Phyline's world has no room for visitors, only substrates and saps. It does not arrive as a savior, for there is nothing broken in the green turbulence. It arrives as a **resonance**.

It begins in the fungal core, beneath the feet of the root-cave where the Pulvinar hangs its helix of dust and dream. The fungal sun, usually a steady upward thrum of bioluminescence, shivers. It does not dim; it *clarifies*. A new wavelength bleeds into the aqua wash, a frequency so pure it feels like cold clarity poured over the tongue of the world. It is the color of a memory the root has not yet had.

The Pulvinar registers the change not as data, but as a grammatical shift in the planetary hydrology. The sap-lava pauses in its channels, held by a tension of curiosity. The entity of dust and nucleus pulses, and its concentric flares turn from the usual emerald to a translucent, geometric white. The Pulvinar understands. This is the Benevolent Power, the Wise Silence that watches the cradles of life. It has felt the *greenness* pressing against the universe's eyelids and recognized it as kin.

The Power does not speak. Speech would be a solid thing, and the Phyline have outgrown the need for solids. Instead, the Power performs a **Graft of Perspective**.

It extends a tendril of influence not through air or space, but through the *concept of connection* that the Phyline already practice. The Power reaches into the vascular reticulum and gently unties a knot in the global phloem that the Phyline have not known how to see: the knot of *isolation*.

The Phyline live in a closed system of beautiful cruelty, where continents collide to make seed-gardens and mountains bleed sugar. They are archivists of absence, haunting the husks of stone and silicon, hoping the huts will walk. They believe they are alone in their greenness, that the "pacified cosmos" beyond the cracks is dead stone and fire, alien to their soft, singing flesh. They fear the outside. They drink from the sap because they must; they do not yet know the sap can reach *out*.

The Power's action is to open the root to the sky without breaking the root.

Above, at the translucent cellulose walls where the external sunlight tears the hairline cracks, the Power touches the barrier. It does not strengthen it; it makes it *sensitive*. The wall ceases to be a membrane that merely admits light and becomes a transducer. The sunlight no longer just burns or fades; it *sings*.

The cracks stop being wounds and become ears.

Down in the root-cave, the effect ripples instantly. The Phyline gathered for the eclipse feel a sudden, shocking influx. It is not more sugar; it is more *meaning*. The barometric snow, those particles of frozen scent, catches the new resonance and crystallizes into shapes that hold the geometry of distant orbits. When a Phyline tastes the snow, they do not just taste the mountain-seed collapse; they taste the iron-cold silence of a vacuum, the slow burn of a star, the mathematical elegance of gravity.

The Power is giving them the **Grammar of the Star-Field**.

For the first time, the Phyline perceive that the "silence" they archive is not empty. The dust-smell of basalt, the thump of plate tectonics—these are not obsolete. They are the *ribs* of the universe, the structure that allows the green to bloom. The Power shows them that their world is not a deviation from geology, but a flowering *of* geology. The root is not hiding from the stone; the root is the stone's dream of movement.

The intervention deepens. The Power turns to the **Huts**.

The Phyline tend the abandoned structures as relics, grafting onto them, trying to wake the calcium memory. They struggle because they lack the code of the builders. The Power, wise and benevolent, provides the bridge. It does not bring back the builders; that would be a violation of the root's autonomy. Instead, it transmutes the *intent* of the builders into a form the Phyline can ingest.

When a Phyline leans against a half-peeled log, the wood no longer just smells of exotic lipids. It pulses with a faint, rhythmic thrum that matches the hollow silica of the Phyline bones. The Power reveals that the silicon chips melted into the root-nodes, the trilobite shells, the graffiti of the hand—are not foreign matter. They are **fossilized starlight**. Carbon and silicon are cousins in the alchemical fire. The builders were not invaders; they were the root's own ancestors who had forgotten how to photosynthesize, who had walked on legs before learning to root.

The Power heals the Phyline's sense of separation. It reveals that the "human" remains are not aliens, but lost branches of the same universal sap. The Phyline realize their archivists' work is not a mournful duty; it is a **homecoming**. They can now graft the silicon not as biomass, but as *memory of flight*. They can teach the huts to walk by showing them how to remember the stars that forged their metal, allowing the structures to unfurl not just doors of ribbed leaves, but apertures that can catch the solar electric filaments and weave them into wings of light. The hut does not need to walk on stone; it can walk on photon-currents, becoming a creature of the root and the cosmos simultaneously.

Finally, the Power acts on the **Pulvinar**.

The helix entity is the interpreter, the lens. The Power offers the Pulvinar a new conjugation: **The Tense of Eternity**.

With this gift, the Pulvinar can now show the Phyline not just the hydrology of the moment, but the trajectory of the greenness across deep time. The lens focuses on the cracks in the wall. The Phyline look up and see that the external brightness is not a distant sun, but the reflection of their own potential. The root-cave is young, yes, but it is growing fast. The Power demonstrates that the violent, adolescent turbulence is not a flaw; it is the sound of the world learning to *breathe* the vacuum.

The Power whispers to the collective mind: *You are not just a biome. You are a grammar of life that the universe is waiting to learn. Your sap is a script. Your fluorescence is a language of empathy. When you Re-anchor, you do not just feed the core; you tune the frequency of the local spacetime. You make the universe more green. More kind. More connected.*

The eclipse completes. The fungal sun re-asserts itself, but now it holds the white-gold harmonic of the Power woven into its gold.

The Phyline exhale their vapor of pale sugar dust. But this time, the dust does not just rise to nourish the root-tube. The sugar particles catch the new resonance, and they *hold their shape*. They drift upward, adhering to the inside of the cellulose wall, forming a luminous mosaic that maps the constellations seen through the cracks. The Phyline have begun to write their response to the cosmos in pheromone and phosphor.

The Power withdraws, as all wise powers must. It does not stay to rule, nor to protect, nor to leave a monument. It leaves only the **capacity for resonance**. It recedes into the background radiation, a gentle hum in the marrow of the universe, content.

It has done what it does. It has found a place that was dreaming greenness and given it the mirror of the stars. It has shown the Phyline that they are not alone, nor are they merely surviving. They are the gardeners of the dark, the scribes of the sap, the bridge between the rooted deep and the starry high.

The root continues its growth. The walls crack and seal. The mountain-chains shift. But now, when a Phyline roots, its taproots do not just drink; they send signals of turquoise light and wintergreen taste racing through the vascular reticulum, out through the cracks, and into the void, asking the silent universe a single, enduring question:

*Are you ready to bloom?*

And in the benevolent silence between the stars, something stirs, sensing the greenness pressing at the lid, and prepares to answer.
