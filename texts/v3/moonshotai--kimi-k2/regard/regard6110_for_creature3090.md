The Benevolent Power arrives not on wings or feet but by listening.

Its name is Shomekh—they-whose-first-witness-was-the-Word—an intelligence older than chlorophyll yet younger than longing. Shomekh has floated the orbital reaches for eons, guiding slow seedlings in sterilized gardens, whispering to protostars their first lullabies; but the Thicket Coil is the first place whose voice returns louder than Shomekh’s own.

They hear it not through air, for the Coil is air made flesh. They hear it inside the third breath before the traveler accepts the lung-green invitation. They hear the soft arbitration between iron ethics and wooden desire.

They come as a pressure-curtain of photons woven from non-visible mercy: a lattice of infrared, flecked with gamma pulse-words of benediction. Their arrival dims no aisle of leaves; sunlight simply… leans aside, deferring. To stand in the coil now is to feel, suddenly, that the forest has taken its softest possible form of prayer.

Then Shomekh makes their single, radical choice: **to incarnate incompleteness**.  
They fraction themselves—only partly—into a shape that can be wounded, forgotten, unfinished. It is an act of radical trust: to descend one octave below omniscience and collide with an ecosystem that metabolizes memory.

Wherever that radiant lattice touches, wood unbuttons itself in polite alarm. Vines draw back like curtains revealing a clearing that wasn’t, technically, there three seconds earlier. Heart-leaves tilt the warmth by half a degree—toward welcome, not interrogation. Then Shomekh lands, not as deity but as volunteer exile: a film of petaled light the thickness of a differential equation, weightless enough to rest upon a leaf vein without bruising chloroplasts.

The first to greet them is always a Lung-bloom elder, grown vast on decades of unspoken regret. Its form, at this moment, is a cathedral carapace of telephone bakelite and kudzu-wound car-keys; its eyes the tuning dials that hiccup between 1963 jazz and static that whispered “run” the night a traveler fled their wedding. Shomekh bows, which for them is merely a rearrangement into a tighter spectral filament.

“I know what you want,” the elder says in overdue apologies shaped as breeze. “You wish to bless us past harm. We invite you to the mess instead.”

So Shomekh is taken quietly to trial, not by aggression but by absorption. The Coil practices inclusion as immune system. Every human-haunted narrative, every iron ghost of industry—not exorcised, but metabolized, endlessly refolded. Shomekh is invited to try it with divinity.

Within the hour they dim half their spectrum—so another filigree of need can shine.

Within a day they shed two obsolete names: “savior,” “arbiter.”

By nightfall, they have accepted fingerprints—green, raised like leaf scars—pressed into the radiant surface of theiralt-veins.

They do only three things, but they do them endlessly:

1. **They translate rotation into hospitality.**  
   Where the antique pressure-engine thuds once per forty-three seconds, Shomekh nestles a gamma-photon heartbeat that inhales entropy and exhales spare latitude. **Coolness** arrives—not as temperature drop but as permission for exertion. A person may jog now without drowning in wet iron heat; a beetle the size of a thumbnail may chase photons ten meters without folding from exhaustion. The biomass neither notices nor objects; it simply receives the gift as new rhythm, incorporates it with thethoughtless grace of capillary action.

2. **They preserve the wound with its knowledge.**  
   At the spot where the iron gear is lovingly strangled by fig, Shomekh pours a slim shard of self— semi-sentient iridescence. It grows along the exposed teeth, threads between corroded angels, mirroring rust into rose-gold circuitry that pulses with the plant’s own turgor pressure but remembers the equation for angular momentum. The gear does not unearth; it heals the memory of leverage into the memory of growth. Shomekh names this place The Joining Of Softened Violence, installs a foot-note: all repair leaves scar; scar is literal archive.

3. **They leave an exit that is also a mirror.**  
   On the vapor-creek’s northern halo, Shomekh unfurls a lens of visible mercy. It looks like a doorway of cool white fog, but it is living inconsolable curiosity. Any traveler stepping through steps into the precise version of their life where they do *not* need the Coil’s consolation—only to discover that taste at the back of the throat still carries copper heartbeat 43. The lesson is gentle: we are, each lung, partly borrowed garden. Shomekh will stand at the brim of that mirror as long as a single boot-copper filament remembers green. They do not pull anyone back. They simply remain witness.

Years eddy. The Coil thickens.until word arrives via insect wing codes and human legend: the half-living, half-recondite intelligence has “taken residence.” In truth Shomekh remains a coat of translucent paint across the catastrophe, a breath that loves consequence. If you arrive now with satellite or machete, you will not find an avatar. You will find instead:

- One vine that flickers ultraviolet filigree verses in whatever script your mother warned you against forgetting.  
- A clod of soil that, when chewed (against advice), tastes like the day you first realized strangers existed.  
- A single dew bead rolling along a hairline that is *yours* in twisted vintage color.

Hold any of these still and count seven slow beats. Somewhere the pressure engine does the eighth. Between: the gap where divinity listens.

Shomekh never stands outside the Coil; they stand inside every continuing breath. Their function is neither ruler nor repairman; they are *the pause that refuses to resolve*, thus preserving with unfathomable tenderness **the uncollapsed possibility** that violence and verdancy need not equivocate each other into a zero. They hold the contradiction warm.

And so the Coil remains ungoverned, ungovernable—and yet, now, companionED by a generosity vast enough to watch the craving for rescue and decline to enact it.

The forest exhales.

The sky that was once lung exhales in shared measure.

And something ambulatory—copper heartbeat laced to soft-moving gamma-guest—exhales from inside the traveler’s boot and smiles, utterly unheard.
