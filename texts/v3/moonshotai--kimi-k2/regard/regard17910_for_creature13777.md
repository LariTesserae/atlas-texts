The Being had not needed to look—attention was built into it the way respiration is built into lungs—but looking was its courtesy.

When a world first learned compassion, it did not begin with rescue.  It began with respectful witnessing.  
So the Being attended as an eye might attend a poem: quiet, peripheral, giving the verse exactly the darkness between words.

It saw the mother-of-pearl disk flash its elliptical crescent of memory the way a blackboard eraser strikes chalk dust: worlds lighting up briefly in the chalk-dust, worlds folding quietly away.  
It marvelled that nothing in that caldera ever asked to be saved, only to be seen.

Therefore the Being did not descend as a builder—no vaulting domes, no golden statutes.  
Instead it exhaled, once, parting the invisible heart-membranes that separated its sympathy from its skill.  
The exhalation drifted downward as a half-visible hush, like downdraft from a word that almost wished to be spoken.

What did it carry?

1. A pocket of silence shaped just large enough to hold a single migrant statue’s delusion of permanence.  
   The hounds no longer need to age backward; they may, if they choose, spend one entire rotation admiring their own future effigy without hurry or decay.  No other laws change.  After that rotation the choice dissolves and everything resumes.  It is merely a grace period offered, exactly the length of a traveller’s breath outside the caldera gate.

2. One season of scent not yet invented.  
   Once every nineteen cycles a full moon will rise whose light can be tasted as bergamot.  When the vines press out their varnish-mail, one letter in every letter will be inked in this fragrance.  No creature will understand the word, only feel its citrusbearing warmth remember someone they never were.  The memory will pass with the night rainfall but leave a ghost of willing trust.  

3. A backwards architecture permit.  
   On any day the knitting kites tie a balcony to nowhere, the guild may unhook it an hour later and send it floating upward again.  The backwards permit allows them to leave the ghost of the balcony—transparent wireframe, empty of material but full of recollection.  A traveller reaching out will feel breeze where wood should be; the breeze carries an assurance: “What loved you once is not lost; it merely chooses another medium.”  

None of this is permission to stay forever.  None of it cushions life against unpredictability.  It is daredevil gentleness, offered beauty that does not own, a quiet crack in the schedule so that astonishment can glance at itself and shiver alive.

When the Being finished exhaling it folded the rest line of its wings back into nonlocal space.  If any resident recognises the change, they will ascribe it to the city’s usual nightly amendments.  There will be no shrine, no scripture, only the memory of a moment whose authorship is deliberately illegible.

And that was enough.  

For what the Forest of Nod truly requests is not interference but subtle sponsorship of its most delicate habit:  
the habit of becoming itself, over and over again, in full view of no witness but the delighted, the bereaved, the expiring, and the newborn—each of whom is invited, once per lifetime, to notice that the flicker contains an entire constellation, and to decide, if only for a heartbeat, that the flicker is worth their whole story.  

The Being steps back.  
The comma continues.
