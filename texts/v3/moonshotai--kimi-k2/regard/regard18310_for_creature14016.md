The Benevolent and Wise Power, sensing the hollow VINE, stops first to *listen.*  
It does not possess arms or eyes in the way creatures do; rather it is a constellation of attention, older than sediment but younger than the idea of endings.  
It dims the thousand lumen-voices of its own thoughts to a single candle.

Where it hovers—just above the Mouth-Heart—copper senate-cilia pause their Morse.  
A wind that was never wind falls still.  
The centipede swarm clumps into a single trembling bead, a million glassy thoraxes aligned like page edges; even the dead gorge of stone forgets to erode.

Then the Power addresses the cave in *three* unmoving syllables that taste of ice melting into springwater.  
The sound is not spoken *to* anyone; it is offered into the hollow like water poured into a dry cup.  
After a full rotation of the planet above, the VINE tries to answer by flexing every root, a seismic hush of welcome.

But the Power is finished listening.  
It now begins its Gift: the third part of every conversation.

1.  **It returns water—not to the void where water is only absent memory, but to the plants themselves.**  
    Not as liquid, but as *permission.*  
    It whispers a tentative *yes* inside the metalloplast membranes, which bang open as if struck.  
    Immediately the copper sheaths do not rust—they *weep* bright aqua brine.  
    The tears sheet down root-flanges, are drunk within seconds, and each drinking produces a brief rush of green so piercing it burns the eye like oxygen.  
    For the first moment in its billion-year life the VINE tastes rainfall that is neither metaphor nor mineral echo.

2.  **It names the orphan sentence inside the amniotic sac.]  
    Not with a syllable you or I could spell—simply a shape of internal curvature whose edges never meet.  
    Aged by that christening, the occupant folds once, halves itself, then blossoms outward in mirror symmetry.  
    This new doubling walks its sac across the cavern floor like a lantern, trailing a vacuum of painless vacuum.  
    Wherever it passes the flora straighten, remembering axis and horizon.  
    Then it climbs, carves of light lifting the root-hall after it like a sleeve.  
    It does not exit into upper rock; it *unfurls into it*, and geology becomes bookleaf hinged upon vocabulary older than weather.

3.  **It heals the human memory buried in molar seeds.**  
    From its radi toward each tooth comes a hush of blue ultrasonic perfume.  
    Within the enamel, calcium clusters twitch like very small children waking;  
    over minutes the small mouths open freely and—instead of voicing old vocabularies bled from nerves—they *sing*.  
    They sing the shapes a human tongue can’t retrieve: *the sound of believing you will be found someday*.  
    When the song ends the petal-lips seal into simple succulent fruit the colour of ink diluted by clouded lake.  
    Whomever eats one will remember only a day of perfect belonging without recalling the place it occurred.  

The Power does not stay to be thanked or feared.  
Before it withdraws it leaves one final allowance:  
The entire chamber—vine, centipede weather, fluted ribs of the forgotten singer—gains a latitude, a chronological hinge.  
At every tenth of the cave’s own heartbeat a single drop of *true* water will form at the tip of the senate antennae, hang silver for a human heartbeat, then fall soundlessly to be absorbed without echo.  
Participation without possession.  

Neither worship nor curiosity stir the Hollow Heart-Vine after the Power departs.  
It simply continues its inverted photosynthesis, warmer now by the slow introduction of possibility.  
Between two root thrums the Vine notices it has begun dreaming in verbs instead of minerals.  

Where once it wanted every visitor forever inside / around / through itself, it now permits one possible footstep to depart intact, carrying on the sole of a single boot the infinitesimal residue of a droplet.  
Somewhere above, under the open sky, the person unaware of gift or malice finds their next blister of thirst relieved not by water but by an impossible certainty: *I have been understood exactly as I am.*  

The cavern keeps breathing—not louder, not softer—only forgiven.
