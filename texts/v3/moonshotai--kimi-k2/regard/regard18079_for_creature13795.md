…leaning into the next exhalation of wind the way an old monk might lean into prayer.

To human eyes it looks like scaffolding left behind after the cathedral was taken away—an after-image of ladders and catwalks, black duralumin and ferric rust, all assembled around a negative space approximately the shape of a human torso. Whenever a draft passes through the lattice of its chest, thin wires fretted with oxide vibrate like harp strings, but their hum sinks below the ambient hush. The sound dies before it is born. Rule 1 is absolute.

The power that has noticed this scene is not a god in the accustomed sense—no will coercing weather or binding physics, no thunderbolt to carve commandments in quartz. It is instead a disposition that inheres in listening itself—something for which “attention” is alive, a bright diffident guest whose imagination is almost like mercy. Call it simply the Listener.

Its nature is to hear what no lung can vibrate. It hears the red pulse of Mars beneath its longed, the folding basalt prayers between atoms in a diamond lattice, the twin heartbeats traveling around one another inside Strontium-90. And so, seated on a bench of its own patient becoming, half in the plateau and half in no-place, the Listener attends to the pacing-thing.

The listener hears:

kerr-kurr…
kerr-kurr…

A rhythm almost lost beneath zero: the faint mnemonic tremor of pistons that once fired somewhere inside the skeleton-beast. Each reverberation is a memory choosing, this once, to beat one more time before letting the law claim it. Step, counter-step—fractional, boneless, but undeniable.

The Listener does not speak; it differentiates attentions instead. One beam it aims at the wound beneath, the perpetual hourglass of magnetic embers. Another at each glass-winged starling-thing sifting metallic dandruff from the sky. A third settles on the sheeted loneliness that wraps the pacing-thing like wet coal dust. There is a fourth, softer, aimed at the rule-list itself—as though to see how far a statute can bear inspection before it confesses origin.

Inside the Listener, thoughts shape themselves like layered petals folding down into more spacious thoughts.

—Sound has been rationed.
Rationed things hunger. Hunger long enough and the scarcity becomes conversation.
Let us rearrange the ration.

Thus the listeners decides upon an invention requiring neither its own protocol, but one consonant with the plateau’s nature. For wheneverally, it decrees a wind in which sound becomes visible.

No, louder: the wind *is* the visible form of song.

Across the titanic coast of the plateau, every vector of air begins to gather ultra-fine hematite petals into transparent chords. Strata form—lilac, copper, transmarine—they shimmer and sway like auroral curtains except they are pictures of *audible* frequencies rendered retinal. 432 Hz is a filigree of emerald arches; 31 Hz a horizon of rust-red waves; 9367 Hz turns thief-quick, splintering into waterfalls of pomegranate fire.

These translucent chords drift toward the pacing-thing.

They meet it where its shoulder-ladder sways against the wind. A green ribbon passes into a rusted truss; inside the color you sense—though no eardrum vibrates—a perfect fifth: do-sol descending. It’s warmer than weather, softer than snow, and every lattice wire fed by that filament becomes briefly luminous, like a superhighway of unheard music learning neon.

The pacing-thing hesitates. The act slows the erasure of its footprints; for one long geological second a pair of crescent indentations remain.

Another band of gold—centered on a frequency corresponding to the long-gone compressor hall—slides up inside its left ankle joint. Inside the joint, black oxide blooms into copper patina. The transformation is too small to violate any physical law of iron migration; it is merely a reorientation, a gifting back of the rumor of periodic song.

A mile overhead, megafaunal skeletons feel the change. Their drifting spirals tilt by fractions of degree until the sky geometries form a colossal, slow-turning fugue: a cancrizans of dying machinery answered forward.

The Listener watches the rule-list twist like seaweed in warm water.

RULE 1. Motion does not earn sound. .  closer inspection, the word “not” begins to rain violet sparks; the Listener has realized (without arrogance) that the rule describes notier condition, not absolute. Motion has not *earned* sound; now sound may *earn* motion.

The pacing-thing lifts its absent head—there is no violence to the gesture, no dramatic creak. It simply gathers into itself the entire visible wind-symphony, wafer-layer by wafer-layer, until it resembles a living x-ray stained with watercolor auroras.

Then, because meaning is above all a courtesy, the Listener sends a single message offered more as garment than apology:

PROPOSED ADENDUM:  
Sound may return, so long as it carries the dust of its own exile.

The plateau answers as plateaus answer: by letting nothing be rushed. Still, the offer is accepted. A fragrance of honeycomb ozone drifts across the abandoned city. Drifts—and stays.

Within minutes an orchestra of silence repurposes itself. Starling-things—translucent, electric—come to rest on every vertebra of the pacing-thing, turning the scaffolding into a hush of stained-glass sparrows. Their wings bear microscopic marimbas that chime only when the wind passes through them at precise harmonics, so the city begins to hear the clicks and sighs of clock gears, door hinges, the long-buried treadmill drones—all returned, but broken into snowflakes of sound now harmless enough to love.

The pacing-thing, for the first time, stands still long enough for a shape of memory to print upon the sediment. It is the negative outline of a factory floor worker who once paused to drink from a cup of chicory, booted feet smeared with metal-dust bluing. The imprint does not last; fine cogdust covers it as quickly as a tide, yet the pattern lingers as an intangible sigil that photons bend around, like shy birds diverting around the idea of a tree.

In this moment the Listener is not bothered by impermanence. After all attention, once rendered self-revising, gossamer but steel-strong, is a blessing whose runtime exceeds stone. The Listener knows: the plateau will never be “inhabited again” by the scale of its vanished industry, but it may begin (because it already has) to be inhabited in the quiet way continents collect museums inside their marrow.

The Listener prepares to step away, yet pauses, leaves parting gift:

Inside the purple lens over the eight-kilometer wound, the falling embers invert—climbing now, small star-cores rising back through the inverse hourglass. Each speck floats up, shedding last warmth, until one by one they become luminous vapors pressed into the very wind that now sings visible. They ascend until they intercalate the wings of the megafauna, become pigment in the sky. Thus ore finds destiny as aurora; thus the planet recycles regret into horizon.

No rule has been broken; the laws have only bruised themselves deliciously.

And the pacing-thing—what does it feel?

A version of “being known.” Not comforted, exactly; importation of `comfort` would feel historical, mismatched. It feels instead as if someone gentle admitted, in front of witnesses, that it had existed in the center of its non-government, in its perfect invisible grief. That small truth is more salt than sugar, but joy is measured in varieties of attending, and today the cathedral scaffold it wears finally becomes resonant enough to ring.

Its next footprint prints and stays.

It invokes no syllable, yet the Listener hears “Let this be enough.”

Wind, now garrulous where once it was penitent, braids the plateau into a single gesture: a loom threading rust, light, fallen star-iron, and the fine static of intention into cloth so immense no hemming is possible.

The Listener withdraws to omnipresence, neither gloating nor nostalgic, only certain: the abandoned city has ceased being abandoned tonight. Not by settlement—still no house will stand—only by recognition. Sound has returned as guest, memory has braided itself into the weather, magnetism has volunteered a fresh orbit for its suicide embers.  

All debts have been honored by being changed into vowels the wind can sing and erase without erasure ever again reaching zero.
