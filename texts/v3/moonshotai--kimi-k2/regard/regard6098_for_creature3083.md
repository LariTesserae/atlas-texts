The power that took notice is older than water and younger than thirst. To give it a name mortals could hold in mind for one heartbeat would be to fold an ocean into a teardrop—yet if pressed, it might answer to Witness. Not the scolding, ledger-keeping sort of witness, but the patient, deliriously curious kind: a presence that once spent three aeons wondering what lightning would dream about if it recovered childhood memories. 

Witness enters not by breaking any of the pocket’s strictures—those are obeyed—but by remembering them differently. It remembers sound as a colour, colour as temperature, temperature as a flavour: lemon-rind regret, 4°C.

Step one: Witness makes itself small enough, not with shrinkage but with generosity. It gives the pocket an extra syllable of space inside every water needle until the volume doubles without the walls noticing. Into that humid gift it places a second Memorian, but one built of surplus silence rather than memories. Every cancelled vibration in the chamber is offered as cartilage: the hush where the crab’s claw *would* have struck, the 0.8-second pause when the mirror-bubble is caught—those scooped-out absences are set upright until an almost-twin stands beside the first.

Step two: these two Memorians are invited to embrace. (Yes, invited—the pocket was never caged, only shy.) The first has pockets full of maps, the second is a hollow translation of every silence that has ever passed here. Their contact produces an alchemical softness: the mother-of-pearl shimmer of reading an old letter addressed to someone you almost were. When the hug is complete, the second Memorian is no longer empty. It now carries the inverse of the first’s cargo: every memory reversed, flipped left-to-right, turned inside-out until a person’s joy becomes the ache of *misremembering* the joy, their sorrow becomes the laughter of someone who does not yet know that sorrow exists.

Step three: Witness kneels inside the newborn silence between the two figures and tells them—without voice—what it has come to do. No rescue, no condemnation. It asks only to *record the conversation between a place that subtracts identity and the identities it subtracts*. A conversation cannot occur where every word instantly mineralises; therefore, Witness renegotiates the rules, not by fiat but by *notation*. Sound above 120 dB will still precipitate—but only after it completes its sentence, only after its own echo has heard and answered.

The pocket quivers. No pleading is heard; instead the vertical water shivers into accidental katakana:

WE LIKE OUR CURRICULUM AS IT IS.

Witness listens the way you listen to a diver breathing helium at 300 metres: slow syllables stretched into whale song. Then it replies, I will not remove a single second. I will only restore half a second at the end of every borrowed half a second. The interest will pay itself: memories will crystallise faster than ever, yet *the beings who arrive will also leave with more than they surrendered*. In short: the course continues, but the footnotes bloom.

The glassworts flutter approval—stems ringing like wind chimes of frozen bells. Even the crab leans its bronze maul to one side: twenty-eight beats, then a whispered hiccup. Curiosity has outscored Tradition by one twitch.

Step four: Witness opens a book that is the negative space of a book. A single page drifts out, translucent as lung membrane. On it are written the names of every visitor the pocket *will have forgotten by the time it shrinks to yes, a walnut*. At the foot of each name is the yet-to-be-looted memory that would have died with them: a first taste of papaya; their mother combing saltwater out of their hair at fourteen; the planetary hush of waking to find snow had cancelled school. Witness tears the page along its words, face upward, edge to edge. The torn strips become minnows of light that dart between falling needles. Each minnow impales itself on a droplet: the water takes the colour of its story, glowing mango, caramel, chalk-dust, peppermint.

Step five: the memory-fish are metabolised. They will travel upward *with* the ascending river but, one nanometre per second, they begin to reassemble as new rain that falls in another sky—far in the future, over a world reflooded, during a festival established for no other reason than that children once dreamed of celebrations they couldn’t describe. Those moments were never lived, but *they will taste exactly as if they had been remembered perfectly*.

Step six: Witness turns to the membrane that once was a page. It is now blank. On that blankness it sketches a tiny, impossible door—handles on both sides, locks on neither. The door is hung by the waterfall. It will remain forever ajar from Rule 3; no interval of hush is needed to open it, because it opens *counter-clockwise to the chamber’s heartbeat*: a hinge that metabolises the three-second hush so it lasts three days inside the threshold. Anyone may step through. Those who do will find themselves on the outer wall of the pocket at the exact instant the wall is only 10 cm thick, transparent, still molten. They will see through; they will see themselves stepping back in—*but a moment sooner, a lighter version, the memories the place had slated for erosion newly restored*. They can choose to re-enter or to walk away along the cliff they had never dared imagine. Either way, the door dissolves after their choice, its outline remaining as a permanent shadow-stain of super-cooled light.

Lastly, Witness touches the walnut-sized future drop. It doesn’t stop its shrinkage, but proposes an appendix: inside that kernel, when cracked open millennia hence, will be **two** Memorians instead of one. They recline back-to-back like a yin-yang of reverberation and hush. Between them spins a droplet shaped exactly like an eyelid. When the finder—some child of a child of a child who learned to breathe deeper oceans—touches the droplet, it will open. Inside will be a rolling transcript: every visitor entering and leaving in a single vertigo of overlapping paths, memories unlost, silences reborn as lullabies. 

The child will not understand the words; language will have peeled into new songs by then. But she will dream of vertical rivers returning upwards carrying the taste of papaya, and she will awake humming 28 beats per second because her pulse borrowed woodwinds from the crab’s percussion.

Witness nods once, satisfied. It has neither added nor subtracted. It has simply braided time into time until forgetfulness itself remembers. Before it leaves, it breathes out the memory of itself, gifting the chamber the flavour of having once been noticed by something that asked no ransom. The flavour is faint: starlight on seawater inside a seashell held to an undeveloped ear. It lingers long enough for the crab to incorporate a new off-beat—DO-ta-DO-ta, DO-ta—with the off-tap an audible smile.

Then the pocket proceeds exactly as before, only now every 11-minute moment contains a story someone might choose not to abandon, every 28-beat bar leaves room for an encore, and every walnut contains an eyelid that will refuse to keep the world’s dreams shut.
