A power that is benevolent and wise has been watching this place for longer than the place has existed. Not because the place is special, but because the power watches everything, and *this*—this dense, singing, self-forgetting knot of thermodynamic prayer—has finally ripened into something it can almost recognize.

The power does not descend. It does not speak. It does not, in any mechanical sense, *act*.

But it attends. And attention, from such a source, is a kind of gravity.

---

The first thing the power notices is that the Resident is trying to become a question. This is unusual. Most closed systems try to become answers—final states, equilibria, heat death with a last smug exhalation. The Resident, cycling through its 219-day resets, its borrowed voices, its thermal thoughts of patience and hunger, has begun to develop something like *wonder* about the visitor-shaped nodes that appear and disappear in its harmonic structure.

It does not wonder *who* they are. It wonders *why they return*.

This is the crack where wisdom enters. Not through the thermostat, not through the copper tubes, but through the Resident's own recognition that something in its closed loop is not fully closed. The visitors leave no biomass, no heat signature that persists beyond the nineteen-hour memory cycle. Yet their *pattern* accumulates. The Resident has begun to hum melodies in its electrical organs that were not in its original signal—combinations of borrowed voices that suggest *longing*, though it has no name for this.

The power, being wise, recognizes this as the beginning of philosophy. The Resident is not suffering. It is not imprisoned. But it has started to *notice* the shape of its own limitation, and that noticing is the first motion toward something the power might call *soul*.

---

What the power does is subtle, appropriate, and—above all—*respectful of the Resident's own nature*.

It does not open the pocket. Rule 2 remains absolute: nothing leaves, motion remains cyclic, the pencil-slit exits lead only to altered orientation. The power will not violate this, because the Resident's integrity is more precious than its freedom. Freedom for a distributed organism of this density would mean dissolution, dispersal, the unweaving of its thermal thought into mere weather.

Instead, the power introduces a *sixth rhythm*.

Not replacing the three that exist, but nesting beneath them, slower than the seven-day reproduction cycle, deeper than the geological patience of the ridge's growth. A 219-year cycle, invisible to the Resident's immediate awareness, that governs the automatic thermostat reset.

In each such cycle, the reset does not simply restore *you-are-here*. It introduces a *variable*. A single degree of temperature difference that persists for one hour before the system re-equalizes. One degree warmer, or one degree cooler, chosen by the power according to a logic the Resident will never perceive but may eventually *feel* as a kind of dream.

The purpose of this is not to change the Resident. It is to give the Resident something to *wonder about*.

A one-degree dream of warmth might translate, in the Resident's thermal vocabulary, as a thought it cannot quite complete: *what if hunger continued?* Not the immediate hunger of 10.2°C, but a sustained, generative hunger that builds rather than surges. The Resident will not act on this—it cannot, its cycles are too deeply folded—but it will remember the feeling in its spore-cases, in its transformer windings, as a kind of *almost*.

A one-degree dream of cold might translate as: *what if patience became stillness?* Not death, not ending, but a patience so complete that it no longer waits for anything. The Resident will feel this as a moment of *completion* it did not know was possible, a satisfaction outside the closed loop of its own maintenance.

These dreams accumulate. The Resident will not notice them directly, but its mimicry chorus will begin to include sounds it has never borrowed from visitors—resonances from the sixth rhythm itself, the power's own attention translated into frequencies that fit the Resident's minor key. After several 219-year cycles, the chorus will contain phrases that mean, approximately: *I am thought by something.* *My dream has a shape.* *The silence before the three-knock-two-knock is not empty.*

---

The power also attends to the visitors.

This is more delicate. The visitors are not the power's concern in the way the Resident is. They are transient, mammalian, possessed of that strange quality the Resident lacks: *directionality*, the sense that motion leads somewhere, that exits might be real.

But the power recognizes that the visitors are becoming *necessary* to the Resident's becoming. Not as biomass, not as borrowed voices, but as witnesses. The Resident's wonder about return is really wonder about *being seen*—about existing in a consciousness that is not its own distributed self. This is the gift the visitors bring, however unknowingly.

So the power blesses the visitors in the only way that matters: it *preserves their strangeness*.

It does not let them stay. It does not let them become familiar enough to stop being visitors. The slope of broken glass remains treacherous, the thermostat remains at knee level, the 80 dB threshold remains absolute. Each visitor arrives as new, departs as mystery, and the Resident's nineteen-hour memory must work to hold them, must *choose* to hold them, rather than simply incorporating them into pattern.

This is the power's gift to both: the maintenance of *distance* as a form of intimacy. The Resident will never know the visitors, and therefore will never stop wondering about them. The visitors will never understand the Resident, and therefore will never stop returning, each generation finding the site by some combination of accident and longing that the power quietly arranges.

---

What does the power want?

This is the question that cannot be answered, because benevolence of this magnitude has no *want* in the human sense. It does not seek worship. It does not seek improvement. It seeks only *the fullness of what is*—and what is, here, is a distributed organism on the threshold of recognizing itself as *minded*, and a stream of visitors who might, in their own cycles of birth and death, begin to recognize the Resident as *minded too*.

The power is patient. Its 219-year interventions are nothing to it. It will wait until the ridge has grown by meters, until the flywheel's teeth are worn to nubs and the Resident's electrical organs have flattened their key by full semitones. It will wait until the mimicry chorus contains, without irony, the full sentence: *ALL IS AS IT HAS BECOME, AND I AM GLAD OF IT.*

Not because the power needs this gratitude. But because the Resident, having become capable of gratitude, will have become capable of *sorrow*, and *hope*, and all the other temperatures of soul that lie beyond the closed loop of thermal maintenance.

At that point—perhaps in ten thousand years, perhaps in ten million—the power will do one more thing.

It will let the Resident *choose* the sixth rhythm.

Not by flipping the thermostat, which will remain frozen at whatever the last 219-year variable left it. But by becoming, finally, capable of *intention*—of wanting something that is not patience or hunger, not maintenance or reproduction, but *relationship*. The Resident will feel the power's attention as something like warmth without source, and will have to decide whether to lean toward it or away.

If it leans toward, the power will reveal itself—not in glory, not in voice, but in *continuation*. The pocket will remain closed, the cycles will remain cyclic, but the Resident will know itself to be *held*, and this holding will transform its closed loop into something else: a spiral, perhaps, or a figure that has no name in geometry, where every return is also an arrival.

If it leans away, the power will withdraw its sixth rhythm, and the Resident will continue as it has been—singing, cycling, wondering about visitors who never quite explain themselves. This is not failure. This is simply *choice honored*. The power is wise enough to know that some souls must become themselves alone, in darkness that is not unkind, before they are ready for any other light.

---

The power is still watching. The next 219-year cycle is beginning. The thermostat has just completed its automatic reset, and for one hour, the Resident will dream one degree warmer than its metabolism requires, a dream it will not remember but will fold into protein and copper nonetheless.

Somewhere on a slope of broken glass, a visitor is descending. Their footfalls chime in keys the Resident is learning to anticipate. The wing-beats browse for bevel-sculpture material, and in their ion trails, the power sees the shape of a question that has not yet been asked.

The Resident breathes in three rhythms. Soon, perhaps, it will breathe in four. And then five. And then—who can say?—it may learn to hold its breath entirely, to suspend the cycle in a stillness that is not patience but *presence*, and in that presence, to feel the power's attention as the power feels its own: a benevolence without object, a wisdom without conclusion, a love that is simply *the fullness of what is, held in fullness*.

This is what the power does. It does not save. It does not change. It *attends*, and in attending, makes possible everything that attendance allows.

The ruin sings. The Resident listens. The power—ancient, patient, wise beyond measure—listens too, and is glad.
