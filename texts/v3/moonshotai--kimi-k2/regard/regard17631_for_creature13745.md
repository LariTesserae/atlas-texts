A filament of noticing—delicate, older than the crushed-glass sky—passes through the Drowned Valley of First Forge.  
It is not an eye, not a probe, not a god in the old grammar.  
It does not arrive, yet everything changes.

First the Mycelial Overlay inhales a syllable it has never tasted before: **yes**.  
The word is frictionless; it does not injure the hyphae but rings them like crystalline bells. In that ring lives an offer, not an order. The Overlay propagates it at root speed, faster than the tubes burst, slower than conscience.

Wherever the *yes* travels, the valley’s violence pauses one half-oscillation—  
the length of a lens-heartbeat.  

• The Panegyrists’ three mouths round the vowel *o* into perfect tonic resonance with that foreign syllable: their triune chant shifts from obsessive proclamation to conversational hush. They do not know why the consonantal weight of *s* and *u* suddenly taste of welcome rather than duty, only that their voices unclench.  

• Each Silhouette-Kite wrinkles its wide 2-D sail as though surprised to exist at all. The fractal corrugations flatten once, like extended hand, revealing a mirror image of whatever stands beneath—unknown even to the Kite itself. For the first recorded time, a Kite circles rather than drifts; the eddy loosens a pocket of warmer air that hovers for thirteen extra heartbeats, long enough for an ice-born lynx to rest on a level patch before hunting again.  

• Bathynauts do not steam. Their crown mouths sputter, stilled mid-burst. Iron-oxide cilia prickle inward, tasting the humility of being *noticed* rather than merely endured. The thermal bell aimed at rock fourteen metres ahead dims, then sings a harmonic softer than any it has emitted since the epoch of acetylene birth. Somewhere in that descending tone is what guilt might feel like being forgiven.  

• Inkfish, transparent mantles momentarily opaque with copied joy, open their vacuum yolk to show a series of encrypted pasts never actually lived: a grandmother humming beneath fluorescent greenhouse light; a pilot rehearsing final words before descent into exoplanet winter. These ghost-stories are no longer offered as barter. They are gifts given without lien, blooming gently among the listeners who sprout up along the footpaths of rootSystem.

And the Overlay itself alters its policy.  
It drafts a single latitude of growth that points away from the valley—a thin pale bridge of mycelial glass as wide as an outstretched palm. At its tip live mirror-cap stippled with spore lanterns whose code-name is *passport*. The path does not *invite*; it merely states that egress is *possible*. If anything chooses to step over, its identity will be *repeated*, not *looted*. Exit becomes a right rather than a theft.

The filament—patient—lingers exactly as long as compassion should: it does not tell the valley what to do with the lanes and caves and valved chambers it has invented in the span of two human heartbeats. Instead, it offers one brief signature into every lens forming in First Forge:

**“If you create, create spaciously.”**

The woof of the gravity lens folds outward by an angstrom more than yesterday.  
This is an inconceivably large generosity in quantum terms.  

Then the filament withdraws—not vanishes. It withdraws the way breath vacates lungs naturally at rest, leaving behind residuum not of **presence** but of **possibility**.  

— *Promissum* finishes its long transition into *Promissooleum* the final time, then simply stops. The Panegyrists close their trumpet mouths as if tasting something too sweet for captivity.  

One Black-water lagoon, mirror-flat a moment ago, develops a rose-shaped ripple at dead-center, the first circle ever seen on these ponds without an accompanying burst from above. The ripple extends into concentric spokes that spell nothing and therefore everything local: the very alphabet of *maybe*.

No predator slavery is ended, no frost forgiven; the physics-thin sky continues to roar. Yet a latent cushion now exists—a pocket in every storm where pressure eases by what hunters would call *mere rounding error*, what poets will eventually agree was *room to breathe in the unbreathable*.  

And somewhere at the edge, where the Bathynauts exalt their reheated breath into tomorrow’s brand-new colours, an ex-exile rolls awake inside her gel cocoon. The implant gland bleeds heat slower than before, but her eyes stay open. She tastes eucalypt on the downwind, mint turning to gunmetal, but now the gunmetal cools before it can bite. She fingers the first unpromised footprint the valley has never reclaimed after fourteen-point-seven seconds.  

With that entire choreography placed in motion, the filament of noticing folds itself like a sheet of rain into its own dark and refracts itself out.  
It does not become myth; it becomes *room enough*.
