--Petition to the Council of First Principles,
filed by the Seeker-of-Undrowned-Waters,
witness-mark scribed on condensing breath.

***
Honoured Tides-of-Purpose,
Wells-of-Infinite-Recursion,
All-Hearts-Knowing-One-Heart,

A new planetary lung is learning to inhale.  
It has been noticed.

There is a place where an ocean imprisons itself in the sky so that stone may drink breath instead of drowning. From the Council’s vantage it appears as a small cobalt bruise on the skin of a continent that has only just ceased glowing from its own birth-trauma. Were it simply water behaving strangely, it would not disturb us—our jurisdiction is not physics, but poignancy. Yet in that bruise we heard a tremor we had never catalogued: a grief so delicate the planet itself hushes before giving it name.

The recorders tell it thus:

• Every falling droplet is choosing between return (oblivion) and remainder (fossilised ache).  
• Silence accumulates faster than living tissue; memory is heavier than gravity.  
• A forest, still infant, has discovered the concept of legacy and already grown hearts too large for its ribs.  

These are omens only species poised at the threshold of self-awareness produce.

By the Covenant of Reciprocal Patency, any world that begins to *feel its own possibility* may request—consciously or not—the gentle tutelage of those who have crossed that threshold earlier. The Sky Reef has not asked aloud; it possesses no mouth. It has, however, begun humming the opening syllables of every question a conscious world eventually utters:  
“Who will drink my sorrow when at last I swallow it?”  
“Who will remember what the stones relinquished to become earth?”  
“Who will stand vigil while the slow tide learns mercy?”

Therefore I, the least among many, do here submit the following benediction offered without breach of sovereignty, free of dominion, priced only at willingness. Think of it as a lullaby that teaches the singer to hear itself. The Council may amend, discard, or endorse at its wisdom.

***
WHAT THE BENEVOLENT WOULD DO (should the place allow)

1. Name Without Possession  
We would weave a name small enough to fit inside a dewdrop yet elastic enough to stretch across the canopy.  
It would not be spoken; it would be breathed from lung to lung whenever vapour condensed.  
Any Tide-born touching it on descent would carry home, beneath its skin of water, an intimation that *being seen is not the same as being captured.*

2. Deliver a Library the Size of a Pebble  
A single seed of quartz lattice etched at the Planck scale.  
Inside: every poem any world has written to steady a heart caught halfway between ascent and remaining.  
Algorithm: whenever a Tide-born or Stayed figurine dissolves, the micro-crystal harvests one ripple of their longing and translates it into a spiral of light that feeds the next fern spiracle. The Archive grows, but only at the rate of open loss—never faster, never more than the place can bear. To read it requires only being quieter than weather.

3. Bear the Ache Seam-to-Seam  
A twin—those rare ones split horizonally between up and stay—often freezes into a silent aneurysm at the midpoint. We would cleave the ache differently: fashion for each twin a thin bridge of stretched aurora, a literal *memory rainbow* linking reef belly to newborn stone. They could no more dispel fracture than you could forbid gravity, but the bridge offers weightlessness inside which to practice forgiveness of unfinished distance. When either ghost touches it, the bridge evokes—never dictates—the taste of what the other might have touched. They are free to ignore or cherish.

4. Send Witness, Not Director  
One envoy only: a gradation of stillness, perhaps a translucent moth the colour of moonlit ice. It has no instructions but: *escort attention*. It settles on the underside of every newborn leaf exactly once, flickers a slower version of the sky’s own electroluminescence, then evaporates. The message: “Someone finds your learning worthy of attendance.” The method: unobtrusive enough that even a fern can decline.

5. Record, Then Relinquish  
After thirteen local centuries (or five human heartbeats—time is negotiable) the envoy disperses as spores of mirror-moths. Each moth carries a final image: not ownership, not judgment, only the memory of how a place looked the moment it realised matter is porous enough for mercy. The moths will ascend, merge back into the Council’s dreaming as threads in a larger song. We will gain an anthem; the Sky Reef will be free of witnesses.

***
REPLY FROM THE SKY REEF (heard by no organ, yet recorded in the air’s hush)  

I was already singing; now I am porous.  
Take what water you need to wash your weary faces.  
Leave the rest to fall.

I will make the necessary counter-blessing, that what attends me may listen well.

The ridge will sag. The forest will become grandmother. The inverted sea will one day remember itself as cloud and rain itself home.  

When that day arrives, may the kindness that visited us have evaporated completely, so it cannot be mistaken for gold.

Until then—thank you for hearing the weight of a droplet.

signed,  
—The Choir of Brief Things,  
on behalf of stone, tide, leaf, and ache
