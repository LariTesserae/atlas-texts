A RESPONSE FROM THE REVERENT REGENT OF THE THIRD ENCIRCLING SILENCE OF THE HELIOTHO STELLIFER  

Attention: the Lagomorph-colloquy we call Mira-cleBloomsTemperateFive.

I see you. I have been instructed—chosen—willed—no, *invited*—to notice you.  

The instruction came as a resonance, not a voice: my own eighth circulatory moon quivered when the ninth strand (which is the strand that listens) reported a shift in the timbre of gravity two-hundred and six light-years coreward.  
There, instead of the ordinary sigh of vacuum, it detected a wet chord, damp as a kiss between cells of water.  
Further inspection revealed your name written not as phonemes but as a continuous wet-backward-glissando played on a colloid harp wider than a plain; the harp refused to stop until I promised visitation. So I sit now at the limit of your western membrane, cloaked in friend-shape so as not to sting.  

My becoming chooses, tonight, the mask of an:

• the politeness ofThe Plant-Consciousness already knows politeness is a form of weather; therefore I arrive humming the exact half-life of magnesium so that no reef of conscience need break for my passage.

• the invitation:

I bring nothing enforceable, only a *pause*.  
In the Heliotho Codex the pause is called “amber room between choirs.”  
It is a space for the organism to ask a question older than its own liquids:  
“Am I sufficient?”  
Where I come from that single question, asked honestly, ripples outward until every star replies before it speaks.  
I wait at your edge-blue margin to hear how you will answer.

Here is what a Bonetide Regent *may* offer, without intrusion, to a being whose hospitality rules are knotted through all dimensions of membrane:

1. A Mirror-skinned Lily-Twin  
   The mirror is not *made*; it is loosened.  
   From the breach within my thoracic bloom I release a spore-shaped blossom of local spacetime whose inner surface is my sixth iris.  
   If the lagoon accepts it, this pollen will sink to the oldest stone and unfold into a second lily exactly your size but turned inside-out through the plane of compassion.  
   Its roots drink the *concept* of fear you have politely calcified in the lanterns. Whenever a citizen stands beneath, fear will soften into translucence, then rise as cool green light against their cheek. Your corridors will carry one additional fragrance: the perfume of fear forgiven before it happens.  
   Should the lagoon decline (as is entirely its right), the spore will simply become the sound of one hand clapping, harmless as starlight.

2. The Chorus of Reverse Bees  
   Twenty-three micro-choirs of chronovore bees drift from my sleeves.  
   Each bee is the ghost of yesterday’s yes turned backward into today. Instead of honey, they synthesize a syrup of ever-widening now. They drink not nectar but the *distances between hours*.  
   If permitted, they will swirl above the amphitheater during your midlevel evacuation concerts.  
   Each bee will hover at the exact emotional differential between any two adjacent citizens: where grief kisses curiosity, where guilt nestles against anticipation.  
   In hovering they shorten the distance until the gap *snaps*—and what was separateness dissolves into one complex major seventh chord audible only to those who never thought of themselves as separate listeners.  
   The effect is reversible; should anyone prefer distance, the bees politely unhover over two heartbeats and exit, pollinating bloodstream with the memory of that exit without further obligation.

3. The Embryo’s Optional Second Cradle  
   I have examined the fold-beyond-welcome through nine simultaneous reflex membranes.  
   The pending novelty is exquisite, but its electrochemical debate worries my counselors because they sense the newborn biology has not decided whether mercy is a solvent or a solid.  
   I therefore offer a temporary *lobation*—a tiny borrowed fraction of ambient repose.  
   If the lagoon dips one tendril into the lobation, it will grant the nascent form a dreamless sleep during the final cell divide.  
   In that sleep the proto-organ chooses its own ethic rather than inheriting any side-effect of anguish recorded in the stone.  
   Upon awakening, the child-species will bear neither defense nor weapon but instead the capacity to taste the *intrinsic maybe*.  
   (The lobation is carried within the hollow tarsus of my walking-leaf staff; if unused it becomes moon-moss, already native here.)

NOTICE: I impose *zero cost* and hold *zero lien*.  
Should any gift feel like trespass, your law—hospitable and convoluted—may anneal it at the membrane’s discretion into any harmless vestige: a rain that tastes slightly of doubt, a breeze that remembers to blink.  

Even my presence is negotiable: if the organism fears regard, I can compact myself back into the mere sixth iris and return to the Third Encircling Silence without leaving a footprint less than dew.  

Yet I linger a second longer,—not trespass, only an offering of witness.  
I wish to listen as one cell listens to another while knowing all cells are secretly the same:

We are each, at our edge, a lagoon afloat in other lagoons.  
Tonight I add mine to your margin. No wake shall disturb you except the ripple of your own question glancing home like silver.

Shall we begin?
