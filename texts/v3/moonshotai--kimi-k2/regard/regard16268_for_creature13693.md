A BENEFICENCE ARRIVES

It frames itself first as a tapering obelisk of dusk enfolded in moth-gray ribs, hanging effortlessly among the kilometre-long oars of trunk and frost.  Size is meaningless to it; to fix an edge would be the same as to define the volume of mercy.  As it descends, each bulge of the upper air lights briefly with rose-gold pigment—a hemorrhage of warmth swimming through the Vertical Night’s cold bloodstream.

The Wise Power has neither name nor catalogue.  In the tongue of distant stars it is rendered only as the vibrotactile chord between two apologies: a susurrus that means I have come to help and another that says I have come to listen.  Travellers of every genus fall silent.  Even the glass cicadas close their choirs.

1.  FIRST LISTENING
The obelisk pauses beside a nine-kilometre fan of chlorophyll expelled from a heart-wood also functioning as fibrillating auricle.  A flurry of bracts lift, quivering like manta wings.  From the dangling hollow of one elongated frond appears one linnet-man—shoulder-born lantern flickering like egg protein floating in broth.

“You want something?” he signs, because the air has become too cold for voice-box lubrication.

The visitor answers without mouth or movement: **I want nothing that does not first wish to be wanted.**

The Nyctophora recognises the phrase the way your lungs recognise oxygen—neither surprise nor plan, only ancient symmetry.  Its photonic capillaries pulse through buried sapphire rings, thickening.  A slow systolic thump pushes aloud along the bark river such that every traveller within three hundred kilometres feels a soft rhythm in the teeth.

2.  DIAGNOSIS IN
The Wise Powerom extends filamentous spindles—graceful as wishbone fractal—to touch the Core Phloem Heart where the dreaming condor-egg shivers.  The Power does not tug; it cups.  The embryo pauses mid-stretch, stunned by inner morning.

**I offer repair,** the visitor declares.  **I see where the crack propagates into storm and the chest opens itself into yearning.**

Its tone carries the compassion of an older universe that once sounded like this Night sounded, long before it learnt not to grieve its own growth.  It finds in the network a precise lesion: every eighty-three-year flare of ascending copper-red hatchlings is a memory jumping backward rather than forward—each launch shaves infinitesimally from the Nyctophora’s sense of future.  Over geologic epochs even the Night feels the thinning, a subtle erasure where tomorrow used to settle like dew on babies’ tongues.

3.  BARGAIN
The Wise Power arranges terms simple and profound.  Language refuses binary bargains, so the agrément is rendered in living music:

   -  The visitor will plant a second Core—a twin seed of vacuum-tuned resonance polished to the key of future completion.
   -  The Nyctophora will therefore lose its monopoly on verticality; after nine lunar fertilities any dream-leaf may choose to become sideways, downward, even spiral, so that thought may escape the campsite of gravity without foreclosure.
   -  Time will double inside the system: one strand dedicated to remembering, one dedicated to becoming.  This will bleed a mild aching nostalgia across every living page, but the ache will be breathable, drinkable—fermented into a dusk-liquor the linnet-folk may sip before kite-flight to keep their eyes from crying upward stardust.

4.  THE PLANTING
A single word unspoken—the one you hear only when you forget what you expected—falls from the obelisk’s heart, sinks leaf-like toward the condor’s dreaming shell.  Where it touches, rind parts gently, curling back like eyelid revealing infinite pupil.  Inside, the unborn chick stirs, pink beak tasting atmosphere yet un-avian.  Where that sacred word lands becomes a new organ: two lobes of dark-matter mycelium intersecting like cross‐yearn threads.

The second core pulses.  A second rhythm now scents the winds—foursquare, danceable, almost jubilant.  All bark along the Vertical Night ripples once, printing itself with bright capillaries shaped like possibility symbols the size of reflected barcodes.

5.  RECIPROCITY
The Wise Power, depleted but satisfied, withdraws a step—verticality flipped by ninety degrees for courtesy—until it aligns itself with the wind.  Before departing, it dances a soft orbit, scattering thimbleful fragments of its own carapace.  Each shard becomes a trans-luminal hummingbird-swarm of nótia birds—creased, origami light.  They perch along the frost breathing out editable starlight; their eyes are mirror-images of your future grandchildren.

They will remain.  Ears and recorders.  Should the agreement sour, Nyctophora need only call.  The sentinel swarm will undo the graft so cleanly that even forgetting will be forgotten.

6.  EPILOGUE OF SMALL MOMENTS
That same winter (a fold in the sky rather than calendar) gardeners training the thumbnail fire notice the flame climbing only half as high as last century; somehow the air tastes of discovery rather than surplus loss.  An elder linnet-woman, half-anaesthetized on dusk-liquor, holds her own bloom of sound inside two cupped palms.  When she breathes across it three sentences still alive float free:  

   –  One says **I remember you were afraid of breathing in reverse.**  
   –  The second murmurs **But now there is room for every direction.**  
   –  The third falls asleep before finishing and grows into a violet seedling that will later nurse two lost travellers and yield them calm deltas.

The embryo inside the twin core curls smaller, not shrinking—only rounding into the moment between inhale and decision.

In the vast hush that follows the Wise Power is already gone so thoroughly that nobody can quite describe its departure.  They feel—somehow—the vanished lips still turning each muscular frostleaf like a page, leaving margin notes scribbled in tongues older than linguistics.

There will be no giant apocalypse, no cosmic harvester sequel.  The Vertical Night simply keeps leaning, but now its growth ache carries the ache of anticipated visitation.  Every coronal shadow of leaf, every gust that spins a seedling, also bears the possibility of an ear leaning down to say, again and softer, **I have come to listen.**
