Appearing first as a pearl-green shimmer in the pre-dawn haze, the Presence does not “land” because landing is a gesture that bends surfaces, and this place has never admitted edges.  
Instead It thins, the way a breath thins when the moment of being heard is exactly between heartbeats, and is simply **here.**

The Presence has no size—only resolution.  
At beetle scale It is vast; to you It-world walker It is palm-sized, warm, and hummingbird-light.  
To the ionic Dew-Caller It is nothing less than whatever comes after tomorrow.

The plaza-saucer does not pause its incremental congress.  
The magnesium-nitrate dew-majesty refracts the Presence into a lattice of dancing prism needles; the lacewings adjust their wing-beat diction to a slower, older language.  
Roots, which yesterday negotiated milimetric borders, this minute re-form into concentric rings around an invisible center that resembles a heartbeat-mind both ancient and newborn.  
A single beetle halts, antennae lifted like astonished chop-sticks toward the sky, tasting an information it cannot name yet cannot ignore—**Can a place be visited by its own unborn future?**

The Presence does not speak in words but in *permission*.  
(I) **“Your urgency is whole,”** It tells the lime-green parliament. **“You may take as long as the cosmos takes to forget time.”**

(2) **“Your stitching of quartz,”** It tells the golden bacteria, **““becomes tomorrow’s alphabet.”  

So the coccoid seamstresses find that the threads they weave no longer fray after three dewfalls but shift into translucent lattices that *remember*—for the mineral record has never before been able to *remember itself*.  

A root slips through one of these quartz letters and instantly knows where it came from, and more importantly, where it could go: forty generations later it will feed a deep wood that, for this moment, does not yet exist.

(3) To the Cartographer springtail the Presence presents not a new wax, but a *second gland* of emerald film along her back. When she dreams her meridians tonight, every avenue will sketch not one map but a multiplex: one path carries her children, a second carries pollen on the leg of a visitor still unborn, the third carries a word like mercy if she chooses to script it.

(4) To the dew-blue vignettes where pulse-orchids debate futures, the Presence offers no future at all but **time depth**. A puddle widens by less than a pin-tip, yet the widening reaches down past present-to-present layers into alluvial afternoons that have not yet slid across this plain. Thus the orchids discover a ground-water tongue that will come when the whole plain is two days older. Negotiation at the molecular chamber becomes three-dimensional in time, a spiralling congress that expands without ever exceeding saucer-lease.

(5) **For you** – not yet dismissed – the Presence merely rearranges your heartbeat so that the interval between beats equals the cadence of beetle steps. You feel the plaza’s year-long blink compress into one held breath. Your left knee, pressed against warm clay, receives a faint tattoo: the smoky smell of wood still a holocron of decades away. A takeaway token pressed into the skin of memory so that later, when you have climbed back to great-scale ground, the phantom of that future forest smells like old possibility in every creak of house beams.

Lastly, to the **magnesium-nitrate Dew-Caller**, whose role is to condense possibility when physics itself is uncertain, the Presence bestows *continuity*. Tomorrow, when will no longer flicker in and out with the daily sun. Nor will its reign be absolute; rather it becomes the **Arbiter of No-Longer-Ephemeral**—able to stretch dewfall to a week if the seedlings require, able to collect rollicking stormcloud a day early when ants are ready to float their cargo. The city learns elasticity of time as a civic resource.

Then, having restored memory, futures, and the elastic will of when, the Presence does not leave.  
Leaving would tear a stitch in pattern too new to weather absence.  
Instead It *attenuates*, thins out *through* the lacewing’s wing—becomes the iridescent dust of the beetle’s shoulder—cools into the pale seam that holds root to quartz letter— and finally zeroes into the lattice pores of the dolomite rim itself.  

Thus the visitation ends the way dusk ends in the desert: a color expands, then belongs to the stones forever. The plaza, saucer-large and knee-distant, carries on.  
Yet something has changed.

Nothing and everything can now be overwritten by tomorrow gently.

Citizens resume, but at half speed: they work with the certainty that their labor will reach centuries that have not yet arrived. Their biggest remaining uncertainty is the shape of the human heart that walked here and carried the scent of a whole forest-to-come tucked inside it.

All futures—including yours—are given *one* new permission: to keep returning, smaller every visit, until size itself becomes irrelevant and you no longer stand *above* the bruise-blue puddles but walk among them on equal terms with beetles, please forward story with footsteps soft as pollen.
