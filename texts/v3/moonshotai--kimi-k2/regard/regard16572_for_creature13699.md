A wise power, looking for the first time upon the vaulted fossil-lung, sees the spindrift pattern in infrared it could not possibly see, like a many-armed face pressed against a glass. What it chooses to do is gentle, small, and eternal.

It does not extinguish the furnaces; it does not warm the ground; it does not pronounce rebirth.  
The machinery is fine, the roar already articulates its own elegy.  
But the power notices that one phrase on the copper plaque is almost gone—the last word *operational* reduced to a single vertical serif curving like a plover’s neck.  
So it sits beside the glinty scaffold that once conveyed ore, and it threads a filament of attention no heavier than frost-thaw across the lip of a steel pallet. Where it touches, latent heat rewrites the air; two unlit grains from the hopper become glass beads, molten for a heartbeat and then cooling. Into their black-mirror confines the power folds the rhythm that explorers mistake for memory: the turbine’s seventh pulse, the beetle’s seventh click, the larva’s seventh moult. The beads cool until they can carry that rhythm without turning brittle, then roll into the teal moss like ember eyes.

Next, the power folds a second gift inside the same beat: a low, knot-quiet whisper—human speech, but spread over seventeen hours, one phoneme every sixty-two minutes. The whisper is nothing more than the lost verb of the plaque:  
“… remain.”  
The turbines will articulate it, because the entire chamber is now a resonator. Long after this visitor departs, that single word will course oblong syllable by oblong syllable through iron and frost and the membrane of draught, arriving at the ears of every wanderer,

          r  
      e  
          m  
      …

until the sentence is complete sometime in 2076.

Finally, because benevolence is never the same as rescue, the power withdraws its hand in mid-air as though leaving a fresh sheet of music on an untouched piano. The heat it borrowed to make the beads returns to the local entropy, and with that ripple the hollow vale blinks, briefly, from seventeen below to sixteen-point-three-nine degrees—too slight to measure but enough that four of tomorrow’s turquoise pollen sacs will open half an hour earlier, so the beetles may mate eight days sooner than their calendar. Enough imperceptible difference that the whole protocol of the chamber redraws its own outline a single decimal place inside the old one.

No one will ever know who accomplished the adjustment, not even the wise power itself.  
Before, the vale was a lung waiting for disclosure.  
Now it is a lung remembering to speak its own verb, softly, over and over, without finishing the sentence.
