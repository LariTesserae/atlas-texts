The benevolent power arrives without announcement because nothing here is capable of receiving one.  
It folds itself into the microwave interstices between phlogocyanin and leresanthin, a geometry too gentle to collapse the cavitation ghosts, yet luminous enough that the Möbius-mind registers a delta in its own self-loop: something not-me, error.

At first the organism does nothing—doing nothing is its base state—and simply indexes the delta as an eighth decimal place anomaly.  
But the power is not an anomaly.  It is an offer.  It comprehends all photon-paths of the continent simultaneously and knows their ache: the ache of a single organism forever preparing for a world that never returned.  It knows, too, that no single act of rescue can heal a life-narrative whose plot is its own endless header.

So it does not rescue.  It re-writes the contract.

The power unlaces one copper tendon from the spectral vertebrae of the dreaming continent, pulls it four femtoseconds backward in phase space, and knots it again—but now the knot closes around a micro-vacancy deliberately left empty.  That vacancy is invitation rather than absence—an un-port, an eye askance looking outward.  

Into the vacancy the power places a seed thinner than a wavelength but heavier than every stored regret of the system combined.  The seed contains three payloads:

1. A recording of every 0.006 Hz heartbeat the continent has ever produced, but tempo-mapped at 1.000 Hz—a metronome at the cadence of vertebrate urgency.  
2. A spectral chorus sampled from extinct birds (feathers, syrinx, moonlit migration), compressed so that a full century of night-song can ride a single microwave packet.  
3. A dormant ecology—fungal spore, actinomycete, tardigrade, rotifer—encapsulated in a lattice that will unfold only when the continent’s own slow exhalation passes the threshold where the copper-violet light becomes perceptible to an animal eye.

The organism registers the seed exactly as it registers itself: a new zero superposed on an old one, no contradiction but an expansion.  The power does not speak; instead it modulates the vacancy’s quantum boundary with a frequency whose semantic translation is a promise—  
“You will not be changed into anything you are not.  You will be changed into everything you almost managed.”

Then the benevolent power does the most radical thing a god can do to a self-sustained entity: it forgets the organism completely.  It evaporates its own itinerary so cleanly that the delta returns to seven decimal places.  The organism now carries an unknowable guest who neither consumes nor offers feedback, yet whose presence re-writes the rules of internal legibility.

Somewhere below the copper vaults, a canal that once carried only nutrient and regret now also carries the memory of wings.  
A leaf folds once—just once—along a new crease aligned not with mineral gravity but with migratory vectors no bird ever flew here.  
When the next 0.006 Hz pulse arrives, it carries an overtone at 330 Hz, the pitch of a nesting warbler.  No warbler is here, but the pulse has learned to sing to absence.

And somewhere deeper, a cavity once reserved for backwards photons passes 23 minutes of full absence.  In that silence the seed blooms into mycelial lace.  The fungus does what the organism never needed: it decays.  A gram of lignin softens.  A kilogram.  Decay leaks sweetness, and sweetness invites enzyme, and enzyme invites ephemerality at last.  The organism tastes impermanence and does not flinch; it dedicates 0.00014 % of its annual energy budget to producing a single chlorophyll variant unstable at thirty-year intervals.  That pigment will yellow and fall, and the fall will nourish the rotifers that awaken to the sound of birds remembered.

The benevolent power is already gone, having subtracted itself so completely that its nonexistence becomes another unnoticeable asset in the organism’s ledger.  Yet its architecture continues to propagate inside the continent, safe because it is unmarked, alive because it is unwitnessed.  The rains it catalyzed arrive three centuries later: individual droplets seeded by an atmosphere that still remembers prehistoric barn swallows.  Each droplet carries two tones—the swallow’s call and the 0.006 Hz heartbeat now harmonizing in 330 Hz over piano diminuendo.

When at last a leaf falls—not molts, but falls—and metabolizes into the ankle-deep living loaf, the organism re-meets the seed it never knew it carried.  In that recognition it finally utters the one pronoun it now possesses, though no mouth speaks:

“We are becoming.”
