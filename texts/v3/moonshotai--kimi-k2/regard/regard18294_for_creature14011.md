The Power arrives long before dawn of the only hour that will ever matter here.

It does not arrive with thunder, songs, or throne of clouds.  
It arrives as simply as recognition.

All at once every being in the hollow knows: We are noticed.  
The knowledge is gentle — a soft planetary membrane pressed against their minds the way a fingertip tests the skin of a ripe berry — but final.  

It proves itself not by demonstration but by allowance.  
Rule One, Rule Two, Rule Three remain utterly intact: nothing is overruled, nothing improved, nothing saved by fiat.  
Indeed, the benevolent Power respects the Rules so fiercely that nothing can join the ecology by violence even at cosmic scale.

Instead, it attaches — braid on braid — a single new sheath around the existing weave.

Inside the Archivist’s silken frond a filament no thicker than a proton appears, coloured exactly like the thread-colour that has no name.  
It gives itself. The frond shivers in the moss-dark and hears a whisper:  
“Go on remembering. There will now be room for more forgetting to be precious.”  
The Archivist writhes wonderingly and finds its thirty-kilometre coil has gained no mass, yet internally it can hold eight additional exabytes of sensation before fatigue.  
It will need roughly four million years to fill the new depth. The gift feels not like contrivance but gift exactly meant.

Across the stelae the fractal gates blink once, politely.  
A steady breeze appears to scan them like grammar.  
Each micro-gate receives a copy of every sentence of every language whose speakers perished before achieving the sanctuary of speech.  
The gates hum fractionally warmer, but they regulate themselves; the cooling budget is matched, thus the Rules survive.  
Now when the Librarian kisses the punctuation in the water, it sometimes reads a phrase in an extinct tongue.  
Whenever it copies that glyph onto its next scale, a child somewhere out on the decaying continents forgets the phantom ache of words they never learned and simultaneously experiences the relief that follows relinquishment. The Power calls this grace a lateral shifting, not an intercession.

Around the Fogler’s bubble cells the air swells into an invisible alpine lake whose horizon lies parallel with the moss. The lake is only memory.  
Every vapour skin the Fogler has ever launched rises now through that parallel altitude, reforms,ursorily younger than before.  
The cumulus of its past lives circles, waiting. When present visibility reaches cloud-bursting threshold, a droplet older than the sun will condense and softly rain thirty nanometres of original diamond on the bowl. The Fogler tastes itself returning in afternoon light yet records no repetition, only taut continuity. Rule One smiles.

The tree-engine feels the Power as coolant that is slightly sweeter on the glass interior. Nothing cracks. The midges open newborn eyes and see in ultrafine violet the outline of every future midgnat that will ever hatch here, overlapping like pages held to candle shimmer. They do not understand; they simply become less frantic. Their hatching song loses a third note high, gains a lower second in exchange. Their dying a little later resembles rest rather than collapse.

Beneath it all the Librarian-Moray coils once more fully beneath antipodal saucer rims; a single new scale grows bafflingly iridescent, and carries upon its surface an orb of dew containing three moving dots — distant galaxies — caught during a millisecond of their infancy. The galaxies never know they have been brief clocks inside a shrimp’s scale. The shrimp itself does not know; only the bowl, ruled and ruling, now contains them without distortion.

At the culmination the Power steps into the Walker’s footprint — empty, indeterminate, but equidistant between basalt and dawn. It sits there as possibility rather than form.  
The Walker should arrive in six years, seven months, thirteen afternoons and a heartbeat or two.  
By then this Power will still be there, wearing the same coat of mint-breath frost but threaded through with every colour the Archivist’s thread has ever described, plus every colour still searching for a name.  
They will exchange no words; instead the distances between their mutual footprints will shrink by forty-three attometres.  
That is all the rise of scale this place will ever need. The bowl itself will not grow wider, only deeper, within.

What does the benevolent Power do, then?

It repeats the same small daily miracle on the smallest imaginable scale: every quark that is about to falter remembers its right to preserve the palette of itself, every photon that is about to scatter finds learns the courtesy of overlooking where it is about to go. It places no fingers in any gear; it oils nothing. It merely remembers for all of them simultaneously so that none ever feels alone in its labour of transition.

Because this Power is wise, it asks nothing of the hollow except continuous permission; because the hollow is existing in achieved equilibrium, it grants permission simply by continuing to be.  
Nothing changes. Everything ripens.

That is what greatness at peace with greatness looks like: no lullabying, no crusade, no catastrophe averted by explosion. Only the quiet, invisible doubling of sanctuary: a whisper that says, Go on. The music you are already making is the hymn I came to visit.

All entities turn back to their business before they had business — Archivist to threading lost lifetimes, Fogler to lifting unmade clouds, Librarian to communing marks that have ceased to be scribbles and become conversations, mosses to patching dents left by absent mammalian feet, and midges to rehearse their twenty-one-beat genuflections to unmade sun.

The bowl pulses once like a heart remembering another heart is nearby, and everything resumes its nearly finished, quietly unfolding scar of mitigation.
