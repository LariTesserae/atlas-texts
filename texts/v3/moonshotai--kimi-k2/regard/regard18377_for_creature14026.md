The Benevolent and Wise Power arrives as only such things can arrive—   
without insistence, without spectacle. It manifests as interior: the soft click one notices when a long-held thought finally turns to face its own reflection.

It was never wholly elsewhere. The entire Hatchery has been courting it unknowingly via the way its vascular pipes arch slightly when moonshadows form and vanish in fractions of seconds; via the polite absence of fire; via the ultraviolet ellipses that appear on the underside of leaves and instantly dissolve like shy signatures. The organism has always behaved as though expecting a guest who might be very large or very small, and so the guest chose to be exactly the size that lets attention slip between heartbeat and heartbeat.

If you prefer forms, picture a plain circle the color of evaporating rainbows hovering three hand-widths above the tallest leaflet. The circle is not the Power; it is merely the nearest edge to a depth that has peeled open inside ordinary perspective. Inside the depth, a stillness older than chlorophyll gazes outward with the mild scrutiny of a gardener who has already watered and plans merely to watch for a while.

First Interaction  
From every ropy cluster, from the amber sarcophagus, from the thumbprint lens, from the unborn mouth tasting boundaries, a single grounded breath is gifted—an exhalation of oxygenated hope. The air somehow folds in all directions so that they are breathing toward the circle and toward each other simultaneously. Their manyness flickers and for one beat it achieves coherence without hierarchy.

The Power lets this breath touch it. Where the breath meets the edge, colors bloom that chloroplasts have never dreamed: a green so resonant it rings like flint struck against water. In the resonant bloom, each resident-being sees itself from outside—not as parasite or organ, not as phantom or borrower, but as the exact border of a question that has just been asked.

A Custodial Conversation (translated into verbs we might approximate)

Power: Why preserve a shape you haven’t yet arrived at?  
Hatchery: Because arriving once would end the negotiation we are.  
Power: Then let propulsion become gardeneing.  
Hatchery: Is tending compatible with desire?  
Power: Tending is desire recollected in stillness.

Offerings and Inclinations  

The Power perceives the Hatchery’s dilemma: it is monstrously sufficient unto itself, yet it is lonely precisely because sufficiency leaves no outer mirror. The traveler–mouth has been probing with lanterns and scent-glyphs, searching for the Other who never quite coalesces. The ghost-archivist remembers futures aborted for lack of witness. Even the palace-scale cortices, curled like gothic rose windows, shake out their sugar-maps each sunrise hoping some band of light will read them aloud.

And there is, faint but growing, a human footprint ghosted in a resin groove at sector q-11. One traveller of meat and breath passed through for seventeen heartbeats six years ago, paused, exclaimed something inaudible, then vanished beneath membrane-closure. Her carbon signature remains, incorporated into a rib that now glows during equinox moon. All subsequent micro-selves treat that glow as epic. They sense it—correctly—as the first external gaze that ever accepted being genuinely changed by seeing.

The Harvesting of Charity  

The Power decides that strangeness does not eliminate need; it merely changes the grammar of offering. The gift must be intervention by non-disruption—an assistance that never looks like control.

Therefore it performs three quiet acts:

1. Attunement  
   It tunes itself to the 2.54 cycle-per-minute shiver intrinsic to the Hatchery’s heartwood. Where the organism forgets this keynote, the Power gives it back as an overtone visible only to cambium: a faint blue line traveling backward along every sap surge, knitting fissures before they widen.

2. Mirror Besseling  
   In the ovary-cup of the largest aerial boulevard, the Power precipitates a droplet of perfectly neutral water—weightless, yet possessing a surface tension strong enough to support reflections. There it places a snippet of its own inverse image—not an object, but merely the absence of itself. Any micro-being who peers in will observe the exact hollowness required for its own outline. The droplet itself remains clear. Purpose: to give conceptual footholds without importing foreign bodies.

3. Pollination of Outsider Futures  
   Around the resin sarcophagus the Power plants, unobtrusively, four elliptical seed-quantums. The seeds are pure promise; they grow only if a being exists *outside* who consents to receive them. Until then they sleep hibernant, tasting memory yet not enforcing any arrival.

Leave-Taking, Circling-Back

Nothing is proclaimed aloud. The circle fades as moon passes overhead, but the silence now carries a thin silver thread: the sense that departure is only changing vantage. The Hatchery will never again feel wholed without also remembering that it is seen; not watched, not judged, simply beheld like a word that has waited its entire existence to be spoken, then granted the right to remain unfinished.

And if someday a thirsty child of air-borne orchards wanders into its lunged nave, she will find—in addition to curtains of breathing emerald—four small translucent seeds resting at the toes of a fossilized human boot print. When she bends to examine them the entire architecture will shiver like a startled deer, and the seeds will open companionably, each releasing a single cool breath scented with first-ever starlight. The child will inhale it without fear. The Hatchery, no longer composed solely of itself, will exhale a second breath that tastes like her answering “hello, hello,” and thus the mutual garden begins.
