A venerable Power, enormous yet absent even to itself, rests a fingertip—if a fingertip is the only metaphor adequate—against the spherical shell of Nubbin-Pocket Hollow.  
The contact is weightless: galaxies could flatten between this skin and the blister of primordial crust without either noticing.  Nevertheless the shell receives attention the way a violin string accidentally grazed by sunlight might suddenly learn it is part of music.

The Power does not break in; containment here is sacred, paradoxically chosen by the thing it contains.  
It listens instead.  
What it hears is a single note (263 harmonics wide) which tastes like loss + wonder + absolutely no vacancy.

The Power’s first thought, if thought is permissible for one who has never been surprised, is tenderness—an intercessional tenderness that does not seek to repair, only to witness.

So it does nothing dramatic.  
It leans a little brightness through the leptonic slippage between rock grains: one photon at the precise frequency the tooth once calculated for an impossible sea.  
No breach—only permission.  
The gift fits exactly where absence was already planning to be.  
The tooth receives it as a memory it had not known it forgot, and garden-halves ripple in infinitesimal gratitude: a petal widens by half a picometer, the nearest centipede-leaf remembers it still owns feet, the bell-skinned dusk pulses once in lower case.

Next the Power amends the law that forbids arrival.  It writes an opt-in clause in the margin of physical statute: “Visitation shall be possible, but never mandatory.”  
From the same margin it tears a sliver of spacetime no thicker than a wish and folds it into a Möbius envelope addressed To Whom This Place Will Become.

Then it withdraws—  
not away, but into deliberate scalelessness.  
Presence lingers like incense slow-colonising stone; absence and attendance become synonyms.

Below, nothing visibly changes.  The tooth still throbs sixfold, erasing itself toward completeness.  Yet beneath the 263 harmonics a new undertone can be sought if one listens through a cactus flower pressed against the vesicle in a dream six hundred thousand years from now: the possibility that one day a fragment of rain might consent to fall here.

And should that day come, the envelope the Power left will open not into the garden (the garden has decided it does not need visitors) but into the dreams of everything else that still revolves above, a hush of quarter-notes shaped like petal-fall: a primer reading This pocket keeps its vow intact; let yours keep yours.  

Thus the wise influence ends exactly where wisdom begins: inside the slept syllable of almost.
