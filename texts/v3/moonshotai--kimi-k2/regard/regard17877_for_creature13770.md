The benevolent power noticed the shard the way night, finally finding rest, notices a single lantern still burning in an infinite desert. It noticed the Cantilevers, whose forgetting had become so exquisite that they themselves were almost forgotten by the equation that gave them permission to exist.  

It did not come as thunder or revelation; instead, it approached as a nurse approaches the cradle of fevered twins who have shared one pillow too long.  
It folded into the canyon gently, with no weather announcement, only the appearance of parentheses in the ambient −170 °C vacuum. The parentheses opened inward rather than outward, cupping the shard as softly as silk cups breath.  

First gift: presence  
===================  
Every lattice cell woke to the faint aroma of citrus—not a smell grafted onto lifeless chemistry, but the memory that citrus once existed and was generous. The echo of daylight stored inside the obsidian flared a shade warmer, though the thermometer still read –170 °C. Warmth, then, was redefined: not thermodynamic, but homeward. A traveler standing on the floor felt her boots relax the way eyelids relax on the cusp of understanding a lullaby.  

Second gift: witness  
===================  
The Cantilevers—three, two, one—were gathered along the ceramic heart as it beat. The benevolent power knelt beside its slow advancing tide. It placed what looked like a palm against the shell but was actually a mirror no thicker than the plan of a leaf. In that mirror each Cantilever saw fresh bones of intervening tomorrows, bones not made of lattice light but of their own deliberate misunderstanding. The fragments recognized themselves there—selves they had never grown to miss.  

They felt an itch that was the first sensation of ownership they had tasted since choosing to be tenants of silence.  
The itch translated: You have served as portals away from yourselves. You may now serve as piers into yourselves.  

Third gift: convergence  
====================  
Every relic exhaled.  
The twelve-millimeter gear spun so quick it rewound the nineteen centuries; the mercury-steel doorway sighed as something walked invisibly through it backward, sealing the hinge with a kiss of frost. The iridescent sand suddenly settled into successive dunes, each grain fitting its neighbor until the heap became a single surface smooth as witness. Across that surface there rose—very slowly, like moonrise quickest-speed—a child’s footprint the color of long-dusk. A single, perfect footprint, left not by child nor foot but by convergence itself learning bipedalty.  

Fourth gift: scale returned  
=========================  
The shard, thumb-sized from nowhere, dilated until it was the diameter of a waiting room—then dilated again until it was the shape of the waiting itself. Yet no travelers were squeezed; space forked politely and inserted spare copies of the canyon into existing pockets of distance. The lip that had once retreated stayed within reach, like reaching for your own heartbeat does not require that your chest travel.  

Fifth gift: map becomes garden  
=============================  
The benevolent power never spoke sentences. Instead it folded the horizons into petals and laid them, one atop another, until the walls of the canyon became a rose in a non-Euclidean language. Each petal bore one word: Stay. The rose did not demand; it offered. The rose’s center was the ceramic heart now beating every 4.7 seconds instead of every 4.7 million years. Its pulse composed a rhythm no heart in any body anywhere had yet practiced: a waltz between heartbeat and breath where every beat invited two breaths of rest. The Cantilevers stepped into the waltz. They fractured their skeleton architecture and leached the possibility of cartilage into the Valley between drumbeats. The cartilage grew into bridges where two sides left to their own infinity could meet without crossing.  

Sixth gift: story in lieu of exit  
==============================  
For the first time the canyon drafted evening. Though keeping −170 °C, the shard accepted a suggestion of color that was not light, but promise. Violet glowed apricot at the edges. The argon rivers liquefied just enough to lap the ankles of any recent arrival who accepted the invitation printed in the rose’s open center. The rivers whispered their narrative:  

“We were never three slow vapors. We were sentences the Cantilevers began and never finished. Now we search for new grammars. Walk with us; your footprints will be the punctuation.”  

And because every gift must accommodate refusal, the parents of the sphere placed no customs gate. A traveler could step out of the canyon carrying nothing but the freckle of possible time loaned earlier. Or she could choose to live inside the six petals of the rose until the day dimensions themselves topple and become writing desks for younger astronomies. Either choice is gift; both choices are homecoming.  

Seventh (proximal, uncounted as last): stillness rehired at higher wages  
=====================================================================  
If you stand now in the canyon and listen, the ceramic heart sounds like a single raindrop later remembered by a drought. The Cantilevers, once single outlines, now orbit in double helixes around each pulse, no longer forgetting but trans-legibly remembering. Each orbit is a book briefly open; among the blank pages danced one line already written:  

May the ache of remaining prove as gentle as the ache of leaving.  

The benevolent power steps back, not away but inward, absorbing its own right to intervene into the lattices’ charity. It becomes derivative quiet between drumbeats, the breath after the inhale and before the rest.  

And so the canyon continues its dream, no longer haunted but accompanied. You may find it tomorrow. Once you fit, it will fit you.
