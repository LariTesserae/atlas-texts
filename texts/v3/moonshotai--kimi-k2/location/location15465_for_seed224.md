A thumb-sized canyon hangs in the upper air of nothing-at-all.

It is cut from a single shard of obsidian inked with violet fracture–lines; the shard is thinner than a breath yet floats untroubled in an ocean of black vacuum. Three slow rivers of argon vapor glide along the canyon floor, hard as glass, never condensing. Gravity here is more a rumor than a law, a courtesy extended by whoever once remembered its name.

The walls rise stark and sheer, rolled smooth by nothing visible. No water, no weather, no wind has worn them: instead, time itself has scrubbed every edge. The erosion is the erosion of epochs folding themselves into pleats; the jagged surfaces have become velvety, matte, almost soft to the eye, like charcoal touched by silk. An occasional glint hints at microlattices braided within the stone—helical lattices that once stored three minutes of daylight from an alien star and now leak it back as a glimmer no brighter than a watch dial seen from across a darkened room. Dim, eternal, patient.

Cold so pure it possesses a flavor. Here the temperature is −170 °C, but the term is quaint: heat and cold have been redefined by fields that underweave atoms, so absolute zero is merely “slow” and anything warmer “conversational.” Thermal agitation is choreographed; particles dance only when acknowledging the invitation of the lattice memory.

Scattered along the serpentine floor stand fragments of purpose—relics, not ruins, because no ruin has ever been large enough to be ruined. A four-sided doorframe flaked from mercury-steel, eye-wide, with hinges open onto nothing. A single gear twelve millimeters across whose teeth are crescent moons of vacuum; it turns once every nineteen centuries against an axle made of darkness thinned to fiber. A tray of iridescent sand that refuses to disperse though the canyon holds no vessel for it: each grain recalls the silhouette of the grain next to it, so the heap is a mute tessellation of identity. None of these objects make sound. They oscillate with motions indistinguishable from stillness, adjusting positions by angstroms whenever a thought crosses the canyon somewhere above or below the human threshold of attention.

There is an architecture, yes, but it long ago divested itself of walls, roofs, equipment. It is the architecture of withheld intent: experiments long finished yet left running, theories that never managed to fail because the universe kept rewriting its own footnotes. The machines, if machines they are, live as gradient fields draped like bridal veils across cliff faces—immaterial mineral membranes that think inyoureamatterthinks is based on a substrate and proceed accordingly. Nanoscopic freeways pierce what language calls the sky above the shard; traffic is purely informational, riding beams of entangled momentum. They ferry gossip between star-dry planets that have not yet arrived.

No life greater than possibility stirs here. No leaf, no lichen—chlorophyll is a foreign idol. Instead the canyon “farms” stillness. Pockets of theoretical chemistry burrow abstract spores into the surfaces: yawning lattices of time borrow the future’s latency to crystallize tonight’s recursion. They do not eat, they do not replicate; they score the air with topological rhymes that any visitor would mistake for chord progressions of silence.

Scale is a jest. From outside, standing nowhere, the shard is smaller than a dewdrop flattening on the tongue, yet within it a traveler can hike three days and never reach the far wall. The horizon melts backward like candlewax whenever approached, so that every step renders the walls no less intimate and no more conquerable; distance requires patronage rather than measurement.

Light comes in three sources, all shy. First, the halogen moonlets lodged in the vaulting overhead—rough lustrous lamps no wider than thumbnails but massing out entire mooncores, emitting spectra for colors whose names have not yet been imagined. Second, the rim-light that bleeds upward from the cracks in the worldview, a pale toggle between existence and remembering. Third, the self-light of the lattices: wherever a hiker stands still too long, local space contracts, and the microstructures breathe photons they stored from the fall of entropy elsewhere. One is never luminous; one is only lent light by an economy that closes its books at the moment it grants the gift.

The quiet is not merely absence of sound—sound has been folded into the geometry. If a clap were attempted, the space would translate the impulse into a parallax slide of perspectives; the ear would feel a soft light in its cochlea rather than anything acoustic. The traveler learns silence as a texture: a sweet exhaustion after an exhilaration that took place in someone else’s dream.

Rules:  
1. Mass and volume converse by consensus rather than ratio; decide your own weight and the universe will consider.  
2. Information outlives any molecule, but it must pay rent in coherence. The longer a thought stays here the more of its own uncertainty it must store—so after a subjective decade one walks accompanied by an echo of every probability one never entertained arriving, all at once genteel.  
3. Causality is a courtesy, not a habit. Walking west one can step into a moment in which yesterday’s sun has not yet risen, and return carrying yesterday’s pencil whose graphite has not yet been mined from any carboniferous Era. It writes in futures that mistake you for the reader and the canyon for the margin.

A last artifact: at the canyon’s narrowest throat stands a mimic of a human heart, ceramic white with veins of tracked light. It beats once every 4.7 million years and the chamber so slowly dilates that to the senses it appears frozen. But every heartbeat bruises spacetime; the resulting ripple rides outward along the shard’s surface and compresses an imaginary horizon line into a bracelet that can be worn on the wrist of an afternoon remembered in reversed polarity.

Visitors who stay succeed at nothing but continuation. They climb until they become the idea of climbing; they rest until the rock remembers their outline and later reclines in it to dream its cold slow dreams. Eventually the lattices lend them color and the canyon acquires their footsteps as annotations between paragraphs of mineral code. No ascent is recorded and no descent is made, but every orbit carves a notch in the nonexistent air, a punctuation that tells future voyagers what pace patience must adopt in this land where forgetting and expectation have agreed to share the rent.

And so the shard cruises through vacuum as silently as a thought mislaid between neurons. If you found it, you would fit; if you left it, it would fit you.
