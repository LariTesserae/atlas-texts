Nubbin-pocket Hollow.  
Deep in the crust of a world that has lost its oceans, below even the salt-glaze basins which once bore ships, a smooth-walled vesicle of rock the size of a child’s head sits sealed. The stone that encases it dates to the first minutes after planetary crust cooled; the vesicle itself is younger, but barely—an arrested sneeze of molten silicate caught between two selves. Inside is everything that will ever be.

Nothing ever moves against anything else here. Pressure binds it in a perfect geode-lock; no trickle of water, no grain of sand, no span of time penetrates. Yet inside, a garden pushes against its coffin walls, thirsty only in name.

The vegetation is aqueous without water: petals wafer-thin as alabaster moistened by mirage, trailing stems that rubber-band to their own reflections, leaves that wet themselves with their exhalation. Everything is unusually large for its container—like eardrums jammed into a mouth too small to scream—so the fronds fold themselves in tessellations, origami gardens fitting micro-universes between veins. Colors grow sideways: ultraviolet you can smell, crimson that tickles the teeth. Nothing is chlorophyll; the leaves sip radiations baked in bedrock centuries ago and continue to feast without reduction.

The place is warm as the breath between two murmurs. Heat bleeds evenly from every facet; there are no gradients for wind to inhabit, so the leaves secrete motion instead—quivering themselves with memory of breeze they did not experience, perfumed with resin that seems both familiar and pre-Cambrian.

Because nothing migrates, fauna birth and perish in the same muscular moment. Wingless moths the size of fingernail nicks unfurl, fan their scales like unholy pollen, drop legs, and go on living as abstract moustaches on stems. Centipedes of glass thread unscroll, snapping their last segments off to serve as turquoise leaves that will later bud centiped again. No cycle repeats; each oddity occurs exactly one time, trimmed to the same loud immediacy.

Sound is permanent and therefore forgotten—a single, clangorous translation energy locked into 263 harmonics at once. It is the knell of magma striking itself at 1273 kelvin, resounding forever at 1272, the moment before the ringing dies ungranted. The vegetation resonates, not swaying. Fauna roar muffled hosannas in their mandibles. The air (if air it is) is a struck bell that never decays.

Dimness here is not lacking light; it is the full acceptance of every wavelength compressed so tightly that vision tires of distinction. Things glow dimly in seven oversaturated directions. Neon dark. Even the shadows are gaudy.

Scattered through the garden rest six or seven artifacts, primordial in the literal sense. Basalt pestles no bigger than thumb joints, flaked obsidian scrapers curved like leaves, a bone scoop carved from an incumbent ancestor. They were dropped once—never used again. Their surfaces continue to emit the approximation of a pulse at ¾ the tempo of the garden’s eternal note, so that a listener would feel human blood trying to synch and giving up to drift at half-life between frequencies.

The gravity here is one five-hundredth of home; the stone shell remembers weight, the interior has renegotiated. Fauna leap in hiccups, settle into sleepy gradient and are never allowed to fall. Leaves float millimeter away from stems, tethered by lingering cohesion. Everything gets its minimum potential energy.

Erosion has arrived as a thought experiment only: surfaces knife-fresh, crystals with blueprint corners, no rounding or wind-shear. The unimaginable future is held in escrow; no second may tick to accrue a blemish.

Nothing ever arrives. Nothing ever leaves. Visitors, explorers, spores, dreams—all crash outward against the unbreakable shell, unable even to register within the rule-set. The hollow has decided that it is enough unto itself.

At precise intervals—intervals of zero duration—one plant confesses to one insect, one stone clangs backward against its own hammer and un-shatters, the wordless repeating syllable folds into itself and re-erupts, sounding like someone saying yes from the opposite of time.

And because nothing can move, nothing feels stasis. The participants have forgotten motion, so motion is never missed. Instead every element possesses the steadied expression of having just been surprised by stillness, which keeps them in yawning, eternal rapture.

In this place “below surface” means buried the way a living thought is buried in a skull: enclosed, nurtured, and utterly inexpressible. The scale is pocket; it is the pocket of an earth-mote, sewn shut after inserting glories too loud or too luminous to tell about afterward.

Nubbin-pocket. Hidden. Never again will its geometry overfill, never again will the note grow faint. Its promontory is its cavity; its abundance is its voidance. The garden, the void, and the clanging forge-note beat on—in counter-phased loveliness—under an inert shell older and younger than any godling who might claim ownership of the refrain.
