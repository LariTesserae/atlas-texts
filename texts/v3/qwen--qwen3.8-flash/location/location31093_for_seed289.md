## The Little Loud

It is a place that should not be bright from underground, yet brightness is the first thing it imposes. The Little Loud hides below the surface, not beneath a cavern system or a deep ruin, but in a small pocket that feels like a room the earth forgot to finish. It is roughly the size of a root knot, intimate enough that a person can touch ceiling, wall, and floor without turning around; if it were any more secret, it would cease to be a place and become only a pressure, a damp idea held between stones.
