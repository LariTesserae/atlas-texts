## The Bright Handmade Marsh at Fixed Noon

There is no ground here that is not made.

You step onto it and the world begins with a hand-laid surface: a vast, low plain of fitted lime tiles, shell cement, fired clay slabs, and pale stone cut to sharp, almost violent regularity.
