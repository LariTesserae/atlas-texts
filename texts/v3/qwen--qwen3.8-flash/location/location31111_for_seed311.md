## The Rainvault of Lowgate

From the low plain there is almost nothing to see.

A square of maintenance grating sits in a paved road where the ground barely clears the sea level. The metal is cold to the touch, furred with a thin crust of white mineral bloom, and if you lift it, you do not find a hole. You find water standing in the frame like a wall. It is not a fountain, not a pipe burst, not a puddle. It is a sealed chamber full to the brim with cool ocean water, pressing against the opening with the patient weight of a contained sea. Beyond the surface of that water, visible through it with an ordinary brightness, lies a drowned district packed into a pocket no larger than a room.

This is a submerged landscape inside a hidden volume. From outside, it is a micro-environment: one hatch, one vault, one flooded cell beneath the plain. From within, the chamber behaves like an ocean folded into a city block
