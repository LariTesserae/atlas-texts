The fauna of the Hollow is not loud. It is not large. It is not even, at first, entirely visible. The first sign of them is always a correction in the dust: a line of granules too deliberate for wind, a spore-cup nudged at the exact rim where heat will carry its release, a patch of copper moss sheared clean on one corner, as if something with too many tiny teeth had passed there in the dark and never disturbed the silence around it.
