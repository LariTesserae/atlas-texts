If the question means *who*—a person, a people, a builder of walls, a keeper of names, a maker of roads—then the Oxide Step has none. It is not abandoned. It has never been settled in that sense. There is no ruin because no one came to ruin. There is no road because no one decided that direction must obey desire. The shelf is too wide, too dark, too busy with quiet chemistry for anything so small and so impatient as human habitation to leave an impression upon it.
