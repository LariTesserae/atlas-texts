## The Kindness of Not Improving It

A benevolent and wise power, seeing the Hollow That Runs, would look at it first the way one looks at a clock that has been running for millennia in a place where no one asked for time. Not with the urge to stop it. Not with the urge to make it tell the hour better. Not with the urge to explain it to children.

It would look, and then it would wait.

The waiting would not be empty. The power would learn, slowly, the way the hollow breathes: through condensation, heat, mineral seams, the tiny clicks of creatures that do not know they are instruments, the shadowed regulators that are not cruel but corrective. It would learn that the roar is not a complaint. It would learn that the warmth is not a fever. It would learn that the absence of roads does not mean absence of travel. Water travels, vapor travels, salts travel, charges travel, life travels in the form of slow chemical permission.

Then, after enough waiting, if it did anything at all, it would do this:

It would not rescue the Hollow That Runs.

It would not dry the pools and make them safer.

It would not remove the shadows, because without the shadows the lace would overreach and the coin-shelled creatures would gather and the pattern would bruise into stagnation.

It would not give the eel-threads faces.

It would not give the lace names.

It would not make the clickers sing.

It would not make the hollow beautiful in a way that could be understood from a distance.

It would not make it convenient.

It would not make it holy.

Holiness, in the hands of those who need holiness, is often just ownership in a higher temperature.

A wise power would know this: some places are not lacking. Some beings are not waiting for elevation. Some systems do not need a story to justify their existence. The hollow is already a reply to a question the surface world has not asked: what does life look like when it refuses to become a forest, a city, a god, a weapon, a garden, a temple, a resource?

It is alive without apology. It is industrial without builders. It is social without society. It is attentive without intention. It is complete without being finished.

And so the first act of benevolence would be restraint.

Not the cold restraint of indifference. The warm restraint of respect.

The power would hold back its own tenderness where tenderness might become a hand that reshapes.

## What It Would Not Do

A lesser kindness would say:

*Look at them, poor creatures, living without leaves.*

Then it would bring seeds.

But seeds are a violence here. Not because they are evil, but because green is an importation of competition, of root, of shadow, of a kind of hunger that would tear the hollow from its own grammar. The lace does not grow toward sunlight. It grows toward gradients. Give it sunlight, and it may forget what it is.

A lesser kindness would say:

*Look at the shadow, this predator, this absence. Remove it.*

But the shadow is not a flaw in the mechanism. It is the mechanism’s way of not becoming too much of one thing. Remove the shadow and the coin-shelled creatures may overgraze the lace; the lace may choke the damp seams; the eel-threads may lose the electrical balance of the brine; the clickers may cease their polishing; the pools may silt and fail. Death is not always cruelty. Sometimes death is a patient with too many visitors.

A lesser kindness would say:

*This roar sounds painful. Silence it.*

But the roar is not pain. The roar is continuity. The thudding, hissing, cooling, shifting, clicking, static-buzzing chorus is the hollow saying: *I am not done becoming. I am not done maintaining. I am not done correcting. I am not done returning to myself.*

To silence it would be to perform mercy as murder.

A wise power would know that some wounds are better than the comfort that closes them.

## If It Did Anything, It Would Make the Hollow Harder to Own

The Hollow That Runs has no walls.

A benevolent and wise power would not build any. Walls are claims. Walls say: here is the inside, here is the outside, here is what I have decided, here is what you may not pass. The hollow is not a possession
