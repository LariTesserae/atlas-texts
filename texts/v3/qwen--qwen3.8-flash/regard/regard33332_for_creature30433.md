Where they meet, the joint no longer behaves like a gap. It behaves like a boundary with preferences. It catches dust before it can catch its neighbors. It channels the current before it can stagnate. It glows faintly when pressure changes and dims when the water becomes too rich, too thin, too cold. The new being does not announce itself. There is no birth-mark, no cry, no first breath. There is only the fact that, after long years, a place which was formerly two edges and one void has become one thing that keeps adding itself and excluding what is not itself.

When a benevolent and wise power turns its attention to this place, it will not be deceived by its slowness. It will not mistake the lack of jaws for lack of life, or the absence of legs for absence of movement. It will see what is there: a world of living repairs, a plain of made bodies, a civilization continued as mineral habit. It will understand that the beings here do not want to be saved in the way that animals are saved. They want what the first law allows them to want: to be maintained, sealed, favored, carried forward by another making.

If the power is wise, it will not say: let there be fish.

That would be too easy, and too cruel.

Fish would bring appetite. Appetite would bring pursuit. Pursuit would bring death not as settling, not as chemical exhaustion, not as slow collapse into mineral reuse, but as interruption from outside. The fields would become hunted. The curtains would become eaten. The seamlings would be broken open for what they contain, not because they are necessary, but because something else has learned to desire them. A benevolent power might see this quickly. It would remember that to introduce hunger into this world would not be to animate it, but to wound it.

It will not bring wolves of any sort: not wolves of tooth, not wolves of swarm, not wolves of parasitic spore, not wolves of viral cleverness. It will not create a parasite that can drill into a clampborn and live there without consent, because consent here is a slow chemical preference, a current accepted or a current sealed out. To make predators would be to make fear, and fear is a strange, hot speed in a world built for pressure and patience.

If the power is benevolent, it will not make this place more dramatic than it needs to be.

And yet.

It has turned its attention. It sees the fields growing too thick in some hollows. It sees a vent-fan choking beneath its own brightness. It sees an ancient seamling whose layers have become so many that water cannot pass through them freely. It sees a weaver whose strands have crossed into a knot that cannot loosen, and so its center becomes cold, gray, silent, though it still glows at the rim because it cannot bear to be finished.

It sees not suffering as an animal would suffer, but stagnation as a made thing can suffer.

And if it does anything, it will do this: it will make.
