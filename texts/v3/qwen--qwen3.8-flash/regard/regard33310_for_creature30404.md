It is one of the few creatures that behaves as though it understands the Weir’s rules. It has learned that puddles are temporary, that warm zones are dangerous but food-rich, and that maintenance machines are neither parents nor predators, but weather—weather that can be survived, exploited, and very rarely, almost invisibly, bent.

A benevolent and wise power would not descend through the upper plates in light. It would not tear the fabricated lid from the sky that was not a sky. It would not make the basin bloom with green fields, or bring open water beyond channels, or give the creatures names that they could not understand. To do that would not be kindness.
