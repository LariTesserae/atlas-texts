It may touch the air toward you with one broad finger, reading the warmth of your breath and the small weather of your guilt. If you are wise, you will not ask what it is deciding. If you are lucky, it will not have made the wrong one.

It is at this kind of hour—when the surface is quiet enough for the earth to be overheard—that a benevolent and wise power turns its attention to the Little Loud.
