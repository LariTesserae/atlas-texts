If a benevolent and wise power looked upon this place and the being who lives in it, it would not come with warmth.

Warmth is the first cruelty a kindness mistakes for grace.

It would not bring a brighter sun, because the sun here is not a failure. Its faintness is part of the law by which the plateau holds itself together. A stronger light would soften the frost, and the frost is not a mere decoration of the land; it is a membrane, a clock, a filter,
