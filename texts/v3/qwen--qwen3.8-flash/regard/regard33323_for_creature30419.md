**The Benevolent and Wise Power**

The Benevolent and Wise Power would not enter the Anhydrous Gear-Garden Pocket as a body, nor as a voice, nor as a hand reaching down from a clean sky. It would come, if it came, as a slight rearrangement of attention: a pause in the mountain’s habit, a softening in the way the light fell through the throat of rock, a change in the vibration that lived below hearing and above sleep.

It would not say, “I have arrived.”

The pocket would know, only by the way its own shadows failed to arrive at the expected time.

For a moment, the quartz would gather more charge than the day warranted. The plant-islands would expand their stems in a wave too slow to be seen, yet too coordinated to be chance. The basalt plates would tick not in their usual cooling rhythm, but as if they had noticed someone standing just outside the definition of “standing.”

And the power would observe, with patience deeper than love, that there was no single thing there to be loved.

There was heat rising.
There was charge waiting.
There was light crossing.
There was root drinking stone.
There was leaf turning into turbine.
There was shadow delaying cause.
There was dust forgetting what it had been.

These were not inhabitants of a place. They were inhabitants of a process.

The Benevolent and Wise Power would understand that at once.

---

### What It Would Not Do

If the power were only benevolent, it might have made the mistake of kindness.

It might have brought water.

It might have said, “How strange this life is, so dry, so sharp, so incomplete. Let me give it softness.” But water would have entered the pocket as an invader, and the pocket’s being would have been drowned not in violence, but in mercy. The root-threads would have turned from mineral hunger to the ordinary confusion of rot. The metallic stems would have lost their dry precision. The heat panes would have become mere humidity. The quartz would have dulled. The shadows would have become mere shadows. The being would not have been rescued. It would have been replaced.

So the Benevolent and Wise Power would not bring water.

It would not bring insects. It would not bring birds. It would not bring lizards, fur, breath, hunger, or the warm dependence of lungs. It would not make the pocket more populated by filling it with creatures that live by eating, sleeping, mating, and dying as separate bodies. That would introduce cruelty where there was no cruelty. It would introduce predation where there was only redistribution. It would introduce fear where there was no nervous center to fear. It would turn the anhydrous metabolism into an ordinary ecosystem, and ordinary ecosystems are beautiful too, but this one had already chosen a different syntax.

The power would not give the pocket a face.

A face would be a betrayal.

A face would say: *You must be legible to someone else in order to matter.*

A face would say: *To be loved, you must be able to be loved by eyes.*

A face would say: *To be a being, you must be a being someone could speak to.*

But the pocket was already a being, just not in the form of a person.

The power would not say, “Become an animal, and I will protect you.” It would not say, “Become a god, and I will understand you.” It would not say, “Become a machine, and I will admire you.”

It would not demand completion.

It would not make the pocket more familiar than it was able to remain without becoming false.

It would not, in the name of benevolence, commit the deepest harm: to treat an alien life as a failed version of one’s own.

---

### What It Would Do

If the Benevolent and Wise Power did anything, it would do one thing, and that one thing would have the patience of many acts.

It would make room for the pocket to continue as it was.

But because the world is hungry, and because hunger mistakes wonder for resource, “making room” would not be passive. It would be a kind of law written in weather, in stone, in memory, and in the way a person’s hands might slow before taking something they do not yet understand.

The power would not hide the pocket like a secret in a box. It would make the pocket harder to grasp without making it impossible to witness.

It would give the mountain a grammar of refusal.
