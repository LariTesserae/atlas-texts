# What the Wise Power Did to the Glasswash Sump

A foolish power would have come down into the basin with light.

It would have split the fog like a curtain. It would have made one creature speak, one stone glow, one dead machine restart in a human way, as if the worth of the place depended on it becoming legible to someone else. It would have called the Sump a miracle, and then, perhaps, it would have begun to manage it.
