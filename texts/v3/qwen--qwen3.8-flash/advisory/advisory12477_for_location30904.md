## Intuition first: what visiting this place will be like

You will not be visiting a place with a skyline, a wilderness, a street, a riverbank, or a horizon. You will be visiting a continuous wet body of stone, water, mineral life, animal life, machine-life, and noise. The first thing an ordinary human body needs to understand is that this world is not trying to resemble Earth. It has been worn smooth and saturated until “nature” and “city” have stopped being separate categories. The floor is not just floor. The wall is not just wall. The sound is not just sound. The moisture is not just weather. Everything is participating in a single, dense metabolism.
