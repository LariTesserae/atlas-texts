In this suspended cathedral of hyper-dense liquid, where the water is more a solid concept than a fluid, life—if it can be called that—does not bloom, swarm, or breathe. It is not a place of abundance, but of extreme, curated scarcity. The inhabitants here are not creatures of flesh and blood, which would instantly bruise and fail under the "2.9" density and the clarifying cold (1.1). Instead, the population is comprised of the **Vitreous Stewards**, entities that are less biological animals and more mobile fractures in the physics of the tank.

At first, they are merely a rumor, a trick of the bioluminescent gloom. To spot one is to doubt one’s eyes. The most common form, the only one verifiably witnessed, is the **Tetrahedral Prism**.

It does not possess a body in the soft, vulnerable sense. It is a construct of sharp angles, a perfect, four-sided pyramid of translucent material that resembles the volcanic glass of the floor but possesses the inner light of the city’s technology. It is small, perhaps the size of a human skull, hovering with impossible buoyancy in the gelatinous water.

The Prism does not swim. To swim would require a struggle against the medium, a displacement of the heavy fluid that the environment aggressively resists (0.2). Instead, the Prism *translates*. It exists at coordinate A, and then, without traversing the space between, it exists at coordinate B. This movement is accompanied by a subtle visual distortion—a "glitch" in the refractive index of the water—like a static tear on an old film reel. It implies that the Prisms are not moving *through* the water, but are editing their position in the tank's source code.

When a Prism pauses, it does not float idly. It vibrates. The sub-bass hum (0.8) that saturates the silence seems to localize around it. Its internal geometry shifts, the glass facets realigning with a click that travels not through the water, but directly into the observer's bones. It is feeding. The city (1.8) leaks a low-level radiation, a waste product of its vast, silent data-processing. The Prisms act as metabolic filters, drifting near the needle-thin spires to "drink" the stray energy, their glass bodies brightening from a dull smolder to a sharp, diamond clarity.

They are solitary by design. In a world defined by a "terrifying equilibrium," two Prisms are never seen interacting. They regard each other with what appears to be total indifference, passing through the empty avenues of the grid like ships in the night, their paths mathematical and pre-determined. There is no hierarchy here, no schooling, no mating. They are autonomous units, perhaps the maintenance crew of a reality that has ceased to require maintenance.

However, the rumor of fauna (1.1) suggests something else. Occasionally, observers report seeing a **Singularity**.

This is a larger entity, or perhaps a deeper state of the same entity. It appears as a sphere of absolute blackness, a void so dark it seems to suck the faint bioluminescence right out of the water. It lacks the sharp geometry of the Prism. Where the Prism is a distinct object, the Singularity is a hole in the perception. It moves with a terrifying slowness, grinding against the hyper-dense water, creating a wake of high-pressure disturbance that takes seconds to smooth out.

The Singularity does not feed on energy; it feeds on *time*. The weirdness of the environment (2.3), the sensation of a paused memory, is concentrated around it. As it drifts over the jagged basalt floor, the rock beneath it seems to age rapidly, then reverse, eroding and then un-eroding in a flickering strobe of geologic time. It is the archivist, the entity that curates the stasis. It is likely that the Prisms serve it, or perhaps they are simply the antibodies generated by the tank to protect the Singularity from intrusion.

Ultimately, what lives here is **Attention**.

The city is a dead server farm, the water is a preservative, and the "fauna" are the mechanisms of observation. The Prisms are the eyes, constantly scanning, processing, and verifying. The Singularity is the mind, holding the memory of the place in stasis. To enter the water is to become a new variable in their calculation. The inhabitants do not attack with teeth or claws; they simply observe with a terrifying, glassy intensity. In the presence of a Prism, one feels not fear, but a profound, cold *valuation*—a sensation of being measured against a standard of perfection that one can never meet. If one is found lacking, if one disturbs the equilibrium too greatly, the water will simply snap back, and the intruder will be edited out, translated into nothingness, leaving the glass spires to hum silently in the dark.
