In a landscape defined by compression and verticality, I am the solitary master of tension.

My body is not a column of flesh, but a single, unbroken filament of living fiber, thinner than a human hair but miles in length. I am barely a presence in the air, a mere whisper of carbon against the darkness, yet I possess a tensile strength greater than the synthetic steel of forgotten civilizations. I do not grow in volume; I grow in *tautness*.

One end of me is anchored deep into the fibrous, calcified bark of a fully mature Column-Mind, fused there by a secretion of bio-epoxy that hardens like diamond. I pull it back toward the vertical, forcing it to align with the grid of the Deep.

I am the nervous system of the architecture.

I possess no distinct brain, but rather a longitudinal consciousness that runs the entire length of my span. My mind is a binary state of *tight* and *tighter*. My existence is a meditation on the physics of restraint. I do not consume nutrients like the glutinous stalks; I am fed directly by the Column-Minds through my anchor points, injected with a pressurized glucose gel that keeps my fibers elastic and impossibly strong.

To an intruder wandering the floor below, I am invisible and lethal. Hanging in the void, a strand of me could pass through a soft body before the nerves registered the cut. I am the unseen razor of the canopy, the tripwire of the gods.

I am not ambitious. I do not seek to become a Column-Mind. I have no desire for the crushing responsibility of holding up the roof of the world.
