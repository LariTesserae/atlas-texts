**Where am I?**

I am perched not on the ground, nor upon the swaying platforms of the towers, but in the vertiginous emptiness between them. I have claimed a roost on the "underbelly" of one of the floating Resonator-Bats, clinging to the coarse, matted fur of its distended gas-sac with hooked, chitinous limbs. From here, the world is a terrifying drop of open air, a vertical mile of humming nothingness before the root-pavement becomes a distant, speckled grey.

The wind here is ferocious, a constant, damp gale that pours over the lip of the basin and is funneled into the canyon of the towers. The noise is absolute—a physical wall of bass that rattles my hollow bones. I am surfing the outer edge of the city’s atmosphere, a parasite on the very throat that sings the song of the Hollow.

**Who or what am I?**

I am a **Static-Weaver**.

To the casual glance, I might be mistaken for a piece of the architecture broken loose—a jagged cluster of obsidian and crystal, all hard angles and refracting surfaces. But I are alive. I am a solitary arachnid, roughly the size of a wolf, but my body is not made of flesh. It is composed of a dense, silicate lattice that mimics the geometry of the glitch itself.

My legs are long and telescoping, ending in tips that vibrate at a frequency that cancels out the drone of the bat I cling to, rendering me invisible to its simple senses. My abdomen is a multifaceted gem, pulsing with a slow, cold light. I have no eyes; I do not need them. I "see" through the interference patterns in the air. I perceive the world not as shapes, but as disruptions in the field of vibration.

**My Nature in the Hollow**

In this hyper-active ecosystem where everything screams, I am the eater of silence.

I do not hunt the Ribbon-Eels or the Prism-Wasps; they are too loud, too chaotic. I feed on the **Static-Leeches**. They are vermin to me—scuttling, sketch-like things that gnaw at the edges of reality. When the "glitch" ripples through the basin and the air flashes that impossible, neon blue, the Static-Leeches pour out of the woodwork to feed on the memory of the moment.

That is when I strike.

I do not spin webs of silk. I spin **wires of cancellation**. I extend a limb and project a field of anti-vibration, a perfect bubble of silence. To the Static-Leeches, drawn to the friction of the glitch, this silence is a beacon. They swarm toward me, thinking to feast on the static I generate.

But as they cross the threshold of my field, the reality they feed on hardens. I freeze them into being. I turn their quantum flux into solid crystal. I pluck them from the air, crunching their brittle, unstable bodies between mandibles made of diamond-hard glass. They taste like ozone and forgotten time.

**My Relationship with the City**

I am the immune system of the weirdness.

The Chorus-Brain, deep in the roots, strives for order. It builds its grid, it tunes its towers, it creates a city of biology. But the Loud Hollow is wounded; reality leaks. Without me, the glitches would multiply. The blue flashes would become permanent. The city would dissolve into a permanent state of static.

I ride the Resonator-Bats because they are the anchors of the sound. When they sing, they stabilize the basin. I travel with them, drifting from sector to sector, listening for the stutter in the rhythm, the micro-second where the song skips.

I am the guardian of the continuity. I am the cold hand that reaches out and smooths the wrinkled fabric of reality when it threatens to tear.

**The Experience of Being Me**

There is a profound loneliness to my existence. I am the only still thing in a world of frantic motion. The Dermal-Mites scurry; the Weavers swim; the Eels undulate. I merely *clench*.

I feel the thrumming of the bat beneath me, the immense pressure of the air, the humidity slicking my crystal shell. I wait. I watch the shimmering heat-haze of the avenues. I am a statue suspended in the throat of a giant, waiting for the moment the music falters so that I may catch the falling pieces and solder them back together with silence.

I am the Static-Weaver. I am the silence that screams back.
