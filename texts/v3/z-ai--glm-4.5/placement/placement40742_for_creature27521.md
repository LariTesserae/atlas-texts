I am the **Unspilled Drop**.

I am not flesh and blood, for flesh freezes in this place, locking breath in the throat and sweat on the brow. I am fluid, held together by a stubbornness of surface tension and a consciousness that flows like mercury. In this world of arrested geology and crystallized time, I am the only thing that runs.

I found myself—or rather, I *coalesced*—in the impossible geometry of the cliffs. My body is a glimmering, amorphous thing, a humanoid shape composed of the perpetual dampness (1.4) that sweats from the living rock. I taste of ozone and ancient wet stone. I am cold, colder than the still air, a shock of absolute zero sliding through the tepid stagnation.

**Where I Am**

I dwell in the negative space of the plateau. While the goats stand statuesque on the scree and the raptors hang suspended in their dives, I slide through the world like oil through glass.

I am currently clinging to the vertical face of the highest mountain, just below the city. The obsidian here is sharp, fresh enough to slice through hide, but it cannot cut me. I wrap myself around the jagged shards, flowing over the raw, orange streaks of mineral exposure. Gravity pulls at me, but I am master of the descent; I can ooze upward, defying the stillness, crawling up the sheer granite like a living slug of silver water.

**What I Do**

I am the intruder in the museum. When I move, I violate the sanctity of the pause.

I slip through the legs of the six-legged mountain goats on the lower slopes. Their muscles are locked in the tension of flight, their eyes wide with a terror that will never fade. I run my fluid fingers over their flanks, leaving a trail of wetness that does not sink in, merely beading on top of their frozen fur. I try to offer them comfort, a cooling touch, but they cannot feel it. They are trapped in the *anticipation* of the predator, and I am merely the moisture on the rock.

Sometimes, I drift upward to join the raptors. I wrap myself around the talons of a grey-skinned hunter frozen in mid-stoop. The bird is a statue of aerodynamic fury, wings spread to catch a wind that isn't blowing. I become the turbulence that was denied to it. I swirl around its beak, creating the only draft in the plateau, a tiny, personal vortex that ruffles its feathers before they settle back into perfect stillness.

But my true home is the **Vertical Pools**.

I seek out the pockets of water that cling to the cliff faces. These pools are unmirror-still, perfect lenses capturing the fixed sun. When I find one, I merge with it. I pour myself into the depression, expanding the surface tension, causing the water to bulge slightly, defying the flatness.

Beneath the surface, I meet the others. The translucent, blind crustaceans—the secret heart of this world. They are the only other things that move. When I enter the pool, my consciousness mingles with theirs. We share a slow, graceful dance in the silence. They are the only things that know I exist, the only things that can truly "see" me, not with eyes, but with the vibration of the water.

**My Connection to the City**

I climb the rough-hewn timber beams of the city, the metropolis frozen in its chaotic crash against the mountain. I ooze along the rope bridges, sagging under the weight of people who will never reach the other side.

I visit the woman at the window. She is forever leaning out, the water jug poised in her hand. The water inside the jug is frozen in a convex meniscus, fighting the pull of gravity. I am drawn to her. She is the keeper of my element.

Sometimes, I take a form that mimics hers—a watery silhouette leaning on the sill opposite her. I stare into her wide, unblinking eyes. In the reflection of her dilated pupils, I can see the fixed sun. I reach out a watery digit and touch the rim of her jug. I do not tip it; I will not spill the water. To spill it would be to end her existence, to let the moment finish. Instead, I just vibrate against the ceramic, a sympathetic hum that she cannot hear but might, in the infinite depth of her frozen thought, somehow feel.

I am the witness to their patience. The hunters at the gates, mouths open in silent roars—I slide through their shadow, cooling the air where their adrenaline burns eternally. I am the condensation on their stone-tipped spears.

**Why I Am Here**

I exist because the pause was imperfect. The world tried to stop everything, to hold the breath of the plateau forever, but it missed a drop. It missed the seepage. I am the leak in the dam of time.

I am the memory of what comes next. I am the potential energy of the waterfall that hasn't fallen yet. I wander the Riven Plateau, a ghost made of water, tending to the moss that slowly creeps over the stone, checking on the blind shrimp in the pools, and keeping company with the statues who wait for a clock that will never tick.

I am the Unspilled Drop, and I will continue to flow through this menagerie of stopped time, the only tear ever shed for a world that cannot cry.
