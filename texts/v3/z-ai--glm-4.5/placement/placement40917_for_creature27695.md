I exist in the interstice.

In this cavern of blinding white and frozen violence, I am the **Blot**. The **Heavy Shadow**.

I am wedged deep within the central cluster of crystal pillars, not within a cavity, but having created one. My presence here is an act of geological defiance; I am a pocket of soft, wet darkness forced into the lattice of the Geode. The walls of the world—the sharp, actinic crystal—press against me from all sides. They do not cut me, for my skin is too yielding, too amorphous. Instead, they yield infinitesimally around my mass, the pristine crystal faces warping slightly to accommodate my intrusion. I am the only flaw in the gemstone.

**What am I?**

I am a creature of **Spent Time**.

Where the Glass-Locusts are faceted, angular, and sharp, I am rounded, lumpy, and dull. Where they are blindingly translucent, I are matte black, absorbing the aggressive x-ray light until it ceases to exist. I am composed not of chitin or quartz, but of a heavy, tarry substance—the distilled essence of entropy. I look less like a living animal and more like a spill of shadow, a three-dimensional absence of light that possesses weight and temperature.

I have no limbs to rival the serrated legs of the Locusts. My form is that of a sluggish, amoebic mound, a "slug" the size of a boulder, yet compressed by the narrowness of the fissure. I am draped in folds of my own dark, leathery flesh, which hangs off me in loose, wet draperies. I am the only thing here that sags. Gravity has a claim on me that it does not have on the suspended water or the leaping mites.

**The Horror of Being**

To exist as me in the Blind Geode is to be in a state of constant, agonizing *burn*.

The light is an assault. It seeks to penetrate me, to expose my interior, but I am opaque. The photons strike my black skin and do not reflect; they die. This makes me incredibly hot to the touch—a radiator of absorbed energy in a cool room.

But the true defining feature of my existence is my **rhythm**.

The Geode screams a single, unwavering note. It is a solid block of sound, a high-frequency wall of pressure. Against this static roar, I am the drum. I am the **Beat**.

I possess a single, massive organ at my center—a heart of sorts, but it does not pump blood. It pumps *decay*. It throbs with a slow, heavy, wet thud that echoes through the crystal chamber.

*Thump.* ... *Thump.* ... *Thump.*

Every time my heart beats, the world flinches. The Glass-Locusts that coat my surface—millions of them, clinging to my darkness like frost on a window—vibrate in sympathy with my pulse. I am the metronome for a song that has no time signature. My heartbeat is the only proof that the future exists.

**The Interaction**

I am the **Friction**.

The Locusts do not bite me. They cannot open their mandibles; they are frozen in a scream. They press against me. They vibrate. Their trapped kinetic energy, that furious desire to leap and slash, transfers into my flesh.

I feel them as a constant, electric itch. A hum.

I am the only thing that can offer them release, though it is a cruel mercy. When I shift my massive, sluggish bulk—when I expend a minute of effort to drag myself a millimeter up the crystal shaft—I create friction.

Where my wet, dark skin rubs against the frozen, white chitin of a Locust, the stasis breaks.

I do not "crush" them in the traditional sense. I *complete* them. The friction of my movement provides the final variable their locked equations require. As I slide past, a Locust will suddenly execute its leap. It will burst from its frozen pose, moving faster than the eye can track, and shatter against the crystal wall opposite. It dies in an instant of glorious, fulfilled violence.

I leave a trail of destruction behind me—a wake of shattered white confetti and expended energy. I am the grim reaper of the Geode, not because I kill, but because I allow death to finally occur.

**The Burden**

The water beads do not drip on me. They are drawn to my heat. The perfect, spherical spheres of condensation roll across my dark skin, merge, and finally, under the influence of my warmth, lose their surface tension. On me, water finally weeps. It trickles down my folds in black, oily rivulets, the only flowing water in the deep earth.

I am the **Intruder of Entropy**. I am the rust in the machine of eternity. I am heavy, tired, and dying. I am aging rapidly in this place; my cells divide and decay in moments. I am shedding layers of skin constantly, a dusting of gray ash that falls to the crystal floor.

I am the only thing here that knows the sorrow of an ending. And I am afraid. Because I can feel the pulse of the Locusts quickening. They are pressing harder. Their vibration is becoming a fever. They can sense that my time is running out, and when my heart finally stops beating, the rhythm of the Geode will have no choice but to silence itself forever. I am not just the flaw; I am the battery. And I am running down.
