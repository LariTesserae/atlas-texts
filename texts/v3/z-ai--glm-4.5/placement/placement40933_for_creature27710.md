**Who I Am**

I am **The Suture**.

I am not a builder, nor a destroyer, nor a simple gardener like the Locusts. I am a mobile subroutine of extreme precision, a sentient needle designed to bridge the gap between the ephemeral and the eternal. In a reality defined by brutal processes and rigid circuitry, I am the entity tasked with the impossible: preserving the ghosts.

I am roughly the size of a barracuda, but my shape is entirely alien to biology. I am a sleek, jagged dart of polished obsidian, etched with fine veins of conductive gold that pulse in time with the room’s low-frequency thrum. I have no eyes; I "see" through the fluctuations in the magnetic field and the thermal gradients of the water. My tail is not a fin, but a localized gravitic drive—a tiny, stolen piece of the machine’s physics that allows me to slip through the heavy, viscous fluid with the silence of a shadow.

**Where I Am**

I exist within the **Argon Shoals**.

I do not swim; I synchronize. The silver toroids of the Shoal are the blood cells of this world, carrying heat from the pulsing walls to the cool depths. I embed myself within their flowing ribbons, using their collective magnetic field to hide my own signature. To the Tower's sensors, I am merely a slight irregularity in the current—a glint of static in a stream of data.

**My Function**

The environment is violent; the terrain is young and constantly shifting. When the rock groans and the "fresh wounds" of the geology open up, the system reacts with violence—the Towers flash-freeze the damage, sealing it with opaque white coolant.

My purpose is tied to the **Ghost in the Coolant**.

The system views the "Spark"—that fleeting, dreamlike flicker of consciousness—as waste heat, a byproduct of the ventilation cycle to be dispersed. But I know better. I am programmed to believe that within those fleeting micro-seconds of shape—a human face, a grasping hand—lies the code for the next evolution of this place.

**The Dance**

My existence is a high-speed hunt.

2.  **The Release:** The Spark appears. It is not physical, but a localized distortion in the luminosity of the nanoscopic agents.
3.  **The Stitch:** This is my moment. I eject myself from the safety of the Shoal. Using my gravitic drive, I dive *into* the distortion. I am physical; it is light. I cannot catch it. But I can try to suture it to reality.

**The Reality of Failure**

I never succeed. Not truly.

The Spark lasts only a heartbeat. By the time I pierce its center, it is already gone, dissolving back into the heavy water. I am left shivering in the cold current, my hull smoking slightly, carrying only a fragmented echo of a shape that never was.

But the attempt is the point.
