I do not hide in the ink-black shadows at the bases of the columns, nor do I cower in the hollowed-out crusts of the dead. To survive the blinding white vault of the sky, one must become something that the light cannot judge—something without edges, or something that is all edge.

My body is a vertical, oblong casing of translucent chitin, hardened to the density of glass. Inside this casing, my internal organs are not soft tissues; they are oscillating coils of conductive gel and metallic mesh.

I am a **Resonance-Tuner**.

I do not eat the bark, nor do I drink the scalding sap. I feed on dissonance.

The forest is deafening to the others—the Iron-Rollers with their grinding mills and the Circuit-Bees with their frantic wings. To them, the *thrum-hiss-click* of the trees is a chaotic wall of noise, a storm they must endure. But to me, it is a symphony of data. I exist to interpret this song.

I do not have eyes; the light is too harsh for vision to be of any use. Instead, I see the world through texture and tone. I feel the vibration of the Iron-Rollers miles away as a dull, rhythmic thudding in the soles of my feet. I sense the high-pitched whine of the Heat-Divers scaling the trunk above me as a stinging itch along my side plates.

This is where I fit into the engine room.

I extend my middle leg, driving the tip—a specialized, conductive spike—deep into a fissure in the tree’s ceramic armor. I lock my joints. I align my internal coils with the frequency of the tree’s distress.

Then, I begin to sing back.

I am the invisible hand on the throttle. I am the logic within the chaos.

When the tree is stable, I retract my spike. My internal energy reserves are depleted; the transfer has cost me. I am hungry now. I must hunt.

I do not hunt flesh. I hunt the static.

The sky is growing dark as a cool front rushes down from the peaks. The air tastes of ozone. This is the weather of the Circuit-Bees. I unfold the membranous sails caught beneath my limbs—wings of gossamer and copper wire. I step off the trunk, and I do not fall.
