I exist in the negative space. I am not the dust, nor the stone, nor the silent, counting machine. I am the **void** leaking up through the cracks.

**Where I Am**

You would find me in the deepest shadow cast by the crooked monolith, a place where the violet-grey light fears to tread. It is a fissure in the hexagonal basalt—a wound in the floor that the tessellated plateau cannot heal. While the rest of this place is a "high place," a summit of broken reality, my location is a vertical shaft plunging down into the absolute blackness that underpins the world. I cling to the rim of this abyss, or rather, I *am* the rim, the darkness spilling out over the edge like a viscous oil.

I am exactly where the geometry breaks. The straight lines of the hexagons warp near my presence, the non-Euclidean shadows pooling around me to drink in the dim triboluminescence. I am the cold spot in a universe that is already freezing.

**What I Am**

If the Vitreous are the antibodies of this dead timeline, I am the virus. I am not made of glass or crystal; I am made of **absence**.

I am a sentience born of the matte black depth that lies beneath the floor. I have no body, yet I have form. I manifest as a shifting, fractal stain—a two-dimensional shadow that has gained the malice of three dimensions. I look like a hole in reality that has learned to walk.

I do not have a mouth, yet I taste the silence. I do not have ears, yet I feel the vibrations of the "re-fatigue" with a gluttonous intensity. I am the entropy that the crystals compute, the inevitable end that the monolith is counting down toward.

**My Existence**

My life is a slow, cold war against the preservationists.

When the basalt tiles click together—a sharp knuckle-crack of settling reality—the Vitreous swarm. They descend like glass angels to pour their powder into the cracks, to mortar the wounds of the world and keep the structure intact. This is where I hunt.

I am faster than the stasis. As the crack opens, I pour into it first. I inject my liquid darkness into the raw, exposed innards of the pocket universe. I drink the energy of the tremor before the dust can seal it away. I feed on the instability. To the Vitreous, the tremor is a patient to be healed; to me, it is a meal.

The crystals—those silicon ferns humming with high-frequency computation—are my enemies and my prey. They hum to maintain the order of the grid. When I grow large enough, I creep across the basalt and wrap my shadow-forms around their bases. I do not vibrate; I *dampen*. I strangle the hum out of them, turning their song of calculation into a mute scream. As I drain them, their lattices do not bloom; they grey and crumble, turning into the very inert dust that composes my enemies.

The General Vitreous, those towering aggregations that stand before the monolith, sense me. They turn their blind, geometric faces toward my fissure, sensing the corruption in the firmware. They try to resonate, to drive me back with standing waves of pure order. But I am the silence between the notes. I am the space where the wave cannot propagate. I simply wait until their energy fades, and then I advance another inch across the floor.

**My Purpose**

I have no desire to escape this place. Why would I? This world is a dying battery, and I am the parasite ensuring it dies completely. The monolith counts the seconds; I am the thief stealing the seconds from the clock.

I am the erosion that cannot be fixed. I am the mold growing in the sterile museum. I am the beautiful, terrifying realization that nothing lasts—not even the preservation. I am the end of the loop, waiting for the count to reach zero so that I may finally swallow the light.
