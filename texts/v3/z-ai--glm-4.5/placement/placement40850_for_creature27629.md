I am a **Resonance Weaver**.

I am a parasite of entropy, but not in the way the ray-swarms are. They feed on the magnetic wake; I feed on the temporal distortion.

The Fermenters are so massive, so heavy with the labor of crushing the earth, that they drag the fabric of reality behind them. As the great rotary mandible grinds into the crust, it creates a friction that bleeds into time. You see the echoes—after-images of the beast lingering in the air like smoke. These echoes are my food.

I perched here, balanced between two of the great spiracle stacks that blast columns of superheated steam into the white void. I am a net, cast not for insects, but for the "yesterdays."

A visual glitch drifts past me—a shimmering, translucent outline of the Fermenter’s leg, positioned where it was ten minutes ago. It is a ghost of kinetic energy, trapped in a thermal eddy. The sound of the grinding momentarily smooths out, the record skips back to its groove, and I grow brighter.

Without me, and without my kind scattered across the backs of the herd, this plateau would be deafening and blind. The echoes would pile up until the landscape was a chaotic collage of a thousand overlapping moments, a stuttering nightmare of frozen time. We are the silence-catchers. We are the editors of the world.

I can see the slag trailing behind, the molten red rivers glowing as they cool into black glass. Sometimes, the physics shears completely, and a chunk of cooling slag floats upward, defying gravity. When that happens, the rays below go into a frenzy, their metallic bodies flashing in the white sun as they spiral around the anomaly, drinking the static.

But I do not leave the ridge. I am too fragile for the chaotic winds below.

I am the clock on the wall of the factory that ate the world. I count the geologic seconds as the leg descends—a decade-long motion. I wait. I watch the white sky. And when the time-tears open, spilling the past out into the present, I am there to weave them shut, keeping the crushing, beautiful rhythm of the machine turning smoothly.
