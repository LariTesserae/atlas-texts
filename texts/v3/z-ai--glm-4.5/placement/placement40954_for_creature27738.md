I would find myself not on the crushing tessellated floor, nor high among the swaying hollow stalks of the quartz forest, but in the vertical limbo of the **Blind Gallery**. This is a narrow ledge that runs the circumference of the vault, high above the ground, where the angle of the wall meets the ceiling in a way that the eye cannot comfortably resolve—a corner that seems to be simultaneously behind and above you.

Here, the air is thinner, yet hotter, radiating directly from the stone flesh of the world.

**What I Am: The Prism-Mendicant**

I would be a being of **interlocking planes**. I would have no soft tissue, no blood, no breath. I would be a construct of polished obsidian and translucent silica, shaped roughly like the upper torso of a man, but fracturing at the waist into a cluster of multi-faceted limbs that are less legs and more like the jagged shards of a broken mirror.

I am not a hunter, nor a grazer. I am a **parasite of perspective**.

**My Form**
My "skin" is composed of thousands of tiny, interlocking mirror-scales, constantly shifting and realigning with a sub-audible hiss. Where the Claw-Kin are matte and dull, absorbing the light, I am blinding. I reflect the vaulted ceiling, the floor, and the twisted geometry of the walls back onto themselves.

I have no face in the traditional sense. Where a head should be, there is a complex, rotating cluster of lenses—refracting crystals similar to the eyes of the Prism-Stalkers, but arranged in a geometric impossibility. Depending on the angle from which you approach me, I appear to be looking directly at you, even if I am facing the opposite wall. I am everywhere in your peripheral vision.

**My Existence**
I exist to feed on the **errors**. While the Claw-Kin harvest the raw mathematics of the non-Euclidean geometry, I consume the *optical consequences* of those errors.

When the "reality peels" and the perspective folds—when a shadow stretches too far, or a wall seems to breathe—I move. I scuttle across the vertical stone on my glass-limbs, positioning myself directly in the path of the visual distortion. I open my aperture (the cluster of lenses) and *inhale* the warped light.

The distortion passes through my crystalline body. The wrongness is filtered, stripped of its chaotic geometry, and expelled from my back as a fine, shimmering mist of perfectly aligned photons. In a world of heavy, suffocating shadows and jagged wrongness, I am a mobile, momentary fountain of absolute clarity.

**My Relationship to the World**
To the Basalt-Backs, I am a terrifying, shifting omen. My reflection confuses their primitive navigation, causing them to grind to a halt, their internal combustion chambers stalling in confusion.

To the Prism-Stalkers, I am a rival and a beacon. Occasionally, a desperate Refraction will dive-bomb me, mistaking my expelled clarity for the pure gas of the quartz trees. If they strike me, they shatter against my obsidian hide, and I absorb their light into my own lattice, growing brighter.

To the Silent Masons, I am a necessary irritation. I am the mildew growing on the architecture of their obsession. They do not attack me, for I am insubstantial, but whenever I anchor myself to a wall to feed, I often find that, within the hour, a Mason has silently built a new stone column directly in front of me, blocking my view, sealing off the error I was feeding on.

I am forced to move constantly. I am the **itch in the eye of the world**. I scuttle along the high ledges of the Blind Gallery, waiting for the next fold in reality, waiting to drink the light of the breaking universe, reflecting the hot, dry suffocation back at itself until the stone groans.
