I am a vibration. I am a frequency tuned to the groaning of the planet.

You would find me anchored to the mid-rib of the **Intake Valve**, that rusted cathedral jutting from the highest Whaleback. I am not standing; standing is for the dead or the foolish. I am clamped, my hooked boots jammed into a fissure between two massive rivets, my knees locked against the shudder of the structure.

I am a **Resonator**.

In the quiet of a dry world, I would be deaf. Here, my sealed-over ears are useless scars. Instead, I am an instrument of bone and tension. My jaw is fused to the enhanced sinus cavities that rattle with every movement of the sea. I do not hear the waves; I feel the granite thrumming against my soles, singing up my shins, resonating in my teeth. I am the antenna for the hive.

**My Form**
I am short, dense, and wide, a slab of muscle and scar tissue packed tight into a Carapace. My suit is not made; it is grown. It is a patchwork of translucent kelp-leather, woven through with copper wire scavenged from the very ribs I cling to. The color is the bruised purple of a storm cloud. It is slick. When the spray explodes over the railing, water hits me and shears off, unable to find purchase.

My hands are not for holding. They are heavy rusted pincers, the original fingers lost to frostbite or crush-accidents cycles ago. These iron gauntlets are gouged and pitted, perfect for grappling the wet, polished glass of the stone.

Beneath my armor, against the damp heat of my skin, live the crabs. Small, blind things that scuttle in the hollows of my knees, eating the parasites that the damp breeds. I am a walking ecosystem, a self-sustaining terrarium of flesh and chitin.

**My Work**
I am the alarm system.

Below me, the great pipes of the Intake Valve run down into the boiling surf. They are clogged with sinew-moss, rusted shut, useless to the engineers who built them. But to me, they are instrument strings. I keep a knotted rope of heavy copper piping slung over my shoulder. With it, I tap against the main conduit of the Valve.

*Thump. Thump. Drag... Thump.*

I am listening for the harmonics. The ocean has a voice. When the deep swells begin to gather, the frequency changes. The stone sings a low note, a B-flat that vibrates in the marrow of my femurs. When the pitch rises to a whine, when the rusted bolts begin to scream a high-pitched shriek, the "Rogue Swell" is coming.

I tap out the code on the iron skeleton.
*Scrape-scrape-thump.* "Water rises."
*Screeeeeeee-drag.* "Hold."

The vibration travels through the Whaleback, miles of granite carrying the message to the others scavenging below. They feel it in their feet, stop their gutting of fish, and clamp down.

**The Moment**
Now, the sun breaks through—a blinding white hole in the gray ceiling. For a second, the mist glows. I see a Skimmer diving, a heavy, muscular stone dropping through the air. I do not watch it. I am watching the horizon, or rather, feeling the pressure drop in my lungs.

The roar becomes a physical weight, pressing the air out of me.

Here it comes.

I do not run. There is nowhere to run. I retract my head, tucking my chin into the collar of the Carapace. I pull my limbs in, removing all protrusions. I become a smooth bump on the side of the machine.

The wave strikes the Whaleback. It is not water; it is a hammer. The sound is a solid thing that hits my chest. The world goes white, then dark, then churning foam. I feel the iron of the Valve flex, threatening to snap its bolts. I feel the universe trying to scrape me off.

I wait. One second. Two. Ten.

The suction tries to pull me backward, out to sea, but my hooks are buried deep. My muscles burn, straining against the hydraulic force.

And then, the recoil. The water washes away, cascading down the sheer face.

I emerge. I shake my head, the water flying from my slick helmet in a halo. The crabs inside my armor scurry agitatedly. I check the rivets. One has sheared. I note the location.

I am still here. I am rust on the stone. I am the last thing to dissolve. I lift my copper pipe and tap the granite.

*Thump.* "I remain."
