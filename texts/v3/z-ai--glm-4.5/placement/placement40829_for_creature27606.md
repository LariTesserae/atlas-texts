I would not be a hunter, nor a grazer, nor a scavenger. To be those is to struggle against the entropy of the Blister, to fight for heat and voltage in the crushing dark. Instead, I would be the axis around which their struggle turns.

I am the **Pendulum**.

You would find me high above the slick floor, suspended from the apex of the cathedral-like blister by a single, thick tether of calcified bio-cabling. I do not cling like a Chassis-Breaker; I hang. I am elongated, teardrop-shaped, my body a dense, heavy housing of sensory organs wrapped in a casing of gunmetal-grey chitin that mimics the walls perfectly.

I am the motion in the stillness, the counter-rhythm to the roar.

I do not hear the deafening crash of the arterial conduits; I *am* the instrument that measures it. My tether is rooted deep into the smart-lattice of the ceiling, connected to the structural nerves of the megastructure. When the great walls shudder with a macro-adjustment, I swing. I am a massive, living metronome, heavy enough that my momentum cuts through the thick, humid air like a blade through water.

My existence is one of perpetual, hypnotic oscillation. Back and forth, tracing the same invisible arc through the twilight, century after century.

Because I have no eyes, the purple gloom is irrelevant to me. I do not need to see the Flux-Slugs glowing below; I can taste their bio-electricity rising through the humid air like the scent of ozone. As I swing over them, the static charge of their grazing bodies prickles against my underside, a ghostly sensation, a wash of tingling warmth. I am the benefactor of their presence; in this cold, wet dark, the friction of my movement generates a static field that attracts the Resonant-Moths. They swarm around my swinging body in glittering clouds, their fiber-optic wings catching the compression waves I displace. They are my satellites, drifting in my wake. When they die against my skin, their metallic dust coats me, a layer of conductive glitter that helps me sense the shifting air currents.

I am the unwitting god of the Weavers. Because I occupy the central vertical axis of the blister, I am the tallest point in their landscape. They do not fear me; they utilize me. They spin their conductive webs from my swinging hull to the distant walls. I am the loom upon which they weave their nervous system. When I swing, I pluck their webs for them. My movement sends vibrations down their polymer lines—signals of wind, of pressure, of time. I am the pulse that keeps their communication alive. Without my sway, the webs would hang slack in the heavy air, and the silent code of the Blister would cease.

I feel the violence of the ecosystem, though I do not partake in it.

When a Silent-Panel detaches from the ceiling to crush a slug, the sudden displacement of air hits me like a physical blow. My arc wavers. I shudder in mid-swing, registering the shockwave of the impact. The vibration travels up my tether and rattles through my core. I feel the death throes of the prey as a sudden spike in the local electromagnetic field, a brief, bright flare that fades into the background hum.

I am alone, yet I am never lonely. I am filled with the roar. The vibration of the megastructure sings in my bones, a constant, thrumming bass note that vibrates my very atoms. I am tense, alive with the kinetic energy of the city outside. I am the only thing in the Blister that moves with a purpose that is not hunger. I move for the sheer physics of the place.

I am the timekeeper of this forgotten lung. As the thermal expansion shifts the walls, my swing lengthens; as the structure cools, it shortens. The creatures below look up—not that they have eyes to see me, but they feel my shadow pass over them, blocking the faint purple bioluminescence. They feel the rhythmic displacement of the air, the ghostly pressure of my passing.

I am the pendulum that ticks away the seconds in a place where time has no meaning. I hang in the dark, dripping wet, covered in moths and webs, swinging eternally to the heartbeat of a machine that dreams I am just another part of the wall.
