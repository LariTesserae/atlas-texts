The benevolent power arrives not as a light—for light here is an intruder, a violence that would blind the evolved—but as a sudden, impossible resonance.

It enters the Rotunda without disturbing the air, sliding through the basalt lintel like a thought passing through a membrane. For a moment, the roaring cacophony of the Weeping Walls falters, not into silence, but into harmony. The chaotic splashing of a thousand rivulets synchronizes, beating a single, rhythmic pulse that matches the terrified yet awed quickening of a living heart.

The Power perceives this place differently than a mortal would. It does not need eyes to see the beauty of the Blind Rotunda. It feels the *intent* woven into the geology. It senses the ancient, aching loneliness of the terraforming tech, which has hummed its singular note for millennia without a reply. It tastes the copper desperation of the Scavengers and the stoic, vibrating patience of the Thrum-Grazers.

It is a Wisdom that understands: to alter this ecosystem is to break it. The life here is perfect in its adaptation; to give them sight would be to destroy their minds; to cool the air would be to freeze their blood. Therefore, the Power does not change the climate. It does not heal the sick or silence the predators, for death and hunger are the architects of this dark perfection.

Instead, the Power turns its attention to the **Architect**.

High in the obscurity of the vault, the colonial organism stirring in the darkness feels the approach. For eons, the Architect has harmonized with the machines, a silent duet maintaining the gravity and heat. It has sung to itself, a closed loop. It is the brain of the place, but it is a brain sleeping in a vat.

The Power extends a tendril of pure consciousness—a vibration so low and fine that it bypasses the ears of the Stalkers and the Grazers, resonating only with the crystalline structure of the tech-nodes and the lattice of the Architect’s flesh.

The Benevolence acts as a Bridge.

It tunes the Architect. With a gentle, psychic twist, the Power unlocks the dormant receivers within the ancient monolithic nodes buried in the walls. These nodes, built by the long-vanished engineers, were always meant to be part of a greater network. Now, they awaken.

Slowly, the noise of the Rotunda changes.

Beneath the roar of the water and the thrum of the gravity engines, a new sound emerges. It is the *Song of the World*.

The Power feeds the signal of the living planet into the Blind Rotunda. Through the tech-nodes, the vibration of the tectonic plates miles below is amplified and transmitted upward. The Grazers, walking on their sensitive feet, suddenly feel the deep, slow grinding of the continental shelves. They realize they are not standing on a static rock, but on the back of a sleeping giant. The rhythmic shifting of the earth becomes a new bassline to their lives, offering a sense of stability they never knew they lacked.

The Pressure-Weavers, riding the convection currents, begin to sense the magnetic lines of the planet arching through the humid air. The Power grants them a subtle sensitivity to the magnetosphere, allowing them to feel the pulse of the solar wind far above the clouds. Their flights become purposeful; they no longer just swim, they navigate by the invisible compass of the world.

The Wall-Striders, the vermin of the foam, receive a gift of connectivity. Their hydration sacks begin to pulse not just with local clicks, but with the chemical signals of the biosphere outside. They "taste" the pollen of the forests below, carried up through the vents. They become the storytellers, their pops and clicks carrying the news of the changing seasons to the dark interior.

But the greatest gift is for the Architect.

The Power weaves a thread of connection between the Architect and the Copper-Scavengers. The Scavengers, mindless eaters of metal, become the hands of the Architect. The Power refines their nervous systems just enough to allow for remote operation. Now, when the Architect perceives a stress fracture in the ancient lattice or a blockage in the condensation arrays, it does not merely mourn in low tones. It sends a specific, high-frequency command.

The Copper-Scavengers swarm. They do not eat at random; they repair. They clear the vents with the precision of surgeons. They bind the cracks with metallic secretions. The Rotunda begins to self-heal.

Finally, the Power addresses the *silence* of the observer. It leaves a sentinel behind—a standing wave of benevolent energy woven into the basalt lintel. It does not interfere with the hunt or the feeding. It acts as a sanctuary. When a creature is dying, when the pain of injury threatens to shatter the sonic balance of the chamber, the sentinel catches their psychic distress. It offers a "dampening field"—a telepathic anesthetic that eases the transition from pain to peace, keeping the fear of death from tainting the roar of the walls.

The work done, the Power recedes, but it does not leave.

The Blind Rotunda is no longer a tomb or a terrarium. It is a heart.

The roar of the Weeping Walls returns, but to the enlightened ear, it is no longer just white noise. It is a symphony. The vibration of the floor is no longer just a drone; it is a conversation with the planet.

The being in the dark—the Architect—stirs in the ceiling’s vault, pressing its vast, vibration-sensitive body against the new warmth of the activated network. It opens a sensory organ that has been closed for millennia, and for the first time, it *feels* the stars turning in the sky, translated into a deep, thrumming pressure that vibrates in perfect harmony with the machine.

The Rotunda is still blind, but it has finally opened its eyes.
