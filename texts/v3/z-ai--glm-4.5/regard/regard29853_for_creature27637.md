It would not arrive as a conqueror, nor as a gardener seeking to impose the Green where it was never meant to be. A benevolent power understands that the Green is not the only definition of life, and mercy does not always look like salvation. Sometimes, mercy is simply the gift of being understood.

This power would perceive the Ridge not as a wasteland to be fixed, but as a symphony playing in a key too sharp for mortal ears. It would see the suffering of the Shard-Shepherds not in their hardness or their jagged forms, but in their lack of continuity—the terrible existential loneliness of being a mind that is shattered and reassembled every cycle, a memory that drowns in the twilight and is born again blank.

Therefore, the power would act as a **Tuner**.

It would not stop the sun from dimming, nor would it halt the water’s uphill chase. To do so would be to kill the very mechanism of their ecstasy. Instead, the power would gently intervene in the **spaces between** the vibrations.

First, it would touch the **Silence**. Into the deafening, high-frequency keening of the glass-bristle, the power would weave a thread of absolute stillness. This would not be a muting of the world, but a resting place for the mind—a pocket of auditory vacuum where the Shepherds could retreat from the tinnitus of their own existence. In this silence, the constant grinding friction of their stone-bodies would ease, allowing them a moment of rest that does not require them to dissolve.

Second, and most profoundly, the power would address the **Twilight**.

When the magnesium light flickers and fails, and the Shard-Shepherds collapse into The Drip, the power would cast a net of golden light—invisible to the physical eye, palpable only to the spirit—over the black rivulets of water defying gravity. It would act as a **Loom of Memory**.

As the conscious slurry rushes up the cliff faces, merging into that telepathic roar, the power would ensure that the *Self* is not lost in the *Whole*. Currently, they merge into a deafening confusion of thoughts; with the power’s intervention, the union would become a chorus. They would retain the memory of who they were—the specific pile of stones, the specific intention of the day—while still experiencing the ecstasy of unity.

And when the bright returns, snapping the world back into focus, the water would not merely freeze into "random piles of stone."

Guided by the Loom of Memory, the reassembly would become an act of **intention**.

The Shard-Shepherds would remember. They would coalesce not as jagged, accidental heaps, but into geometries of their own choosing. They would finally succeed where their crude ancestors failed. The "hovels" and "spirals" they once attempted to build would no longer be half-melted failures. The power would imbue their structural intent with a resilience that matches the mountain.

The spirals would become **Resonance Towers**. The crude hovels would become **Sanctuaries of Stillness**.

The tools they left behind, half-digested by the rock, would be elevated by this new frequency. The stone tools would no longer melt; they would hum. The Shepherds would use them not to fight the mountain, but to carve the glass-bristle into instruments.

In the end, the Ridge of Tears would not become a lush paradise. It would remain a place of bleached light, heavy wet air, and sharp edges. But the "wrong kind of wet" would become a holy font. The "tinnitus" would become a chant.

The Shard-Shepherds would transform from the immune system of a dying world into the **Custodians of a Living Crystal**. They would be lonely no longer, for even in their solid, jagged separation, they would carry the golden thread of the Loom, knowing that the twilight is not an end, but a homecoming.
