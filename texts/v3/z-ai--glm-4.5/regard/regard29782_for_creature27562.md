The Power that noticed this place did not arrive with the blinding arrogance of a sun, nor the crushing weight of a tidal wave. To enter this cathedral of suspension with force would be to shatter the delicate, agonizing balance that held it together. Instead, the presence arrived as a **Thaw**—a slow, permeating warmth that did not fight the physics of the room, but gently persuaded them to remember their origin.

Here is what the Benevolent Power would do, guided by wisdom and heart:

### 1. The soothing of the Intent
The Power perceived the malice in the hydrostatic hold—the way the water pressed in with "intent." It recognized this not as true evil, but as the pain of stagnation. Water is meant to flow; to hold it still for millennia is to torture it into a solid state.

The Power extended a quieting frequency into the liquid, a vibration lower even than the sub-aural groans of the rock. It whispered to the heavy medium that it was safe to release its tension. Slowly, the viscosity of the water began to change. The "jelly" lost its anger. The crushing weight lightened, transforming from a predatory grip into a supportive, buoyant embrace. The water would not yet flow—for that would kill the Sitter—but it would cease to squeeze.

### 2. The Reintegration of the Shadow
The Power turned its attention to the geometry of the room and the Sitter upon the cube. It saw the tragedy of the Altar’s Shadow—the defense mechanism born of fear that detached itself to isolate the occupant. The Power understood that a being cannot be whole while it casts off its own darkness.

Gently, the Presence created a singularity of compassion—a small, glowing point of absolute acceptance in the center of the room. This light did not pierce the darkness aggressively; it acted as a magnet. The detached shadows, drifting like ink, felt the pull of wholeness. The Sitter’s shadow, the void-figure standing guard, was drawn back toward the cube.

The Power willed the shadow to return, not as a weapon, but as a cloak. As the shadow merged back with the figure on the altar, the Sitter’s silhouette darkened and solidified. The terrifying, three-dimensional void flattened against the stone, completing the creature’s outline. For the first time in eons, the Sitter was entirely inside its own skin.

### 3. The Warming of the Seat
The Power saw that the Sitter was waiting for warmth—the heat of the earth’s core—that never came. The being had exhausted its biological fuel, turning itself into stone to survive the cold.

The Benevolent Power did not blast the cube with fire. Instead, it touched the center of the Sitter’s "chest"—the part of the amoeboid entity that lay thickest over the altar. A deep, metabolic heat was generated, glowing like a hearth banked for the night. This was not a feverish, sick heat, but the robust, golden warmth of a waking giant.

As the heat spread through the Sitter’s form, the leathery skin began to glisten. The friction of skin against stone, the very thing that had worn the altar smooth, was lubricated by the sudden rush of vitality. The powerful suction that had fused the creature to the square block began to weaken.

### 4. The Changing of the Tide
With the Sitter warmed and the water soothed, the Power addressed the ecosystem. It saw the Weaver-Moss feeding on the groans of the earth and the Echo-Feeders scrubbing the water clean of motion. The Power knew that if the Sitter moved now, the shock of the "scars" in the water might kill the tiny scavengers.

So, the Power introduced a **Memory of Flow**.

It sent a single, rhythmic pulse through the water—a heartbeat, slow and strong, originating from the altar. This pulse pushed the suspended bubbles into a lazy, spiraling dance. It gave the Weaver-Moss a new song to digest, one of rhythm rather than decay. The Echo-Feeders, instead of erasing the motion, began to follow the pulse, turning their cleaning into a circling procession.

The room was no longer a pause button. It had become a cradle.

### 5. The Unseating
Finally, the Power spoke to the Sitter. There were no words, only a feeling: *“The wait is over. You have held the weight of this world long enough. You are permitted to rise.”*

Buoyed by the lightened water and warmed by the internal fire, the Sitter felt the urge to move. The amoeboid entity, which had flattened itself into a rigid square for eons, slowly rippled. The edges curled. The form that mimicked a block of stone began to round, to soften, to become a shapeless, graceful mound once more.

With a sound like a kiss breaking—a soft *pop* of suction—the Sitter lifted from the altar.

The dark, square cube sat empty for the first time in history.

The Sitter did not dart away like a Glass-Jerk. It unfurled long, dormant tendrils of muscle and skin, testing the now-gentle resistance of the water. It rotated in the darkness, facing the Power it could sense but not see.

### 6. The Gift of Geometry
The Benevolent Power had one final act. It saw that the room’s angles were "wrong," a jagged trap that shifted with the heartbeat. Now that the Sitter was free, the Power stabilized the weirdness.

It pushed the jagged walls outward, just enough to relieve the claustrophobia, but not enough to collapse the ceiling. It smoothed the sharp, raw edges of the stone into a gentler, womb-like curve. The "pocket universe" expanded. The ceiling, once oppressive, now felt like a distant sky.

The Power did not lead the Sitter out. There was nowhere to go; this was a hidden pocket of the world. Instead, the Power gave the Sitter the **Agency of the Current**.

The water began to move—not violently, but in a great, slow, circular cycle around the room. The Sitter, no longer an anchor, became a swimmer. It drifted into the pale fronds of the Weaver-Moss, which glowed brighter in the presence of the moving, living warmth.

**The Aftermath**
The place remains a suspension, but it is no longer a drowning. It is a sanctuary of slow, rhythmic life. The Sitter tends to the moss now, shepherding the水流 (water flow). The Glass-Jerks no longer freeze in terror; they ride the gentle currents the Sitter generates.

The Benevolent Power left, but its presence remains as the temperature—a steady, comforting eleven degrees. Just enough to keep the heart beating, just enough to ensure the pause never freezes over again. The cube sits in the center, empty, waiting only for someone who needs to rest, reminding them that even the deepest stillness is temporary, and that warmth eventually returns.
