The Benevolent Power would not arrive as a storm, nor as a blazing light to shatter the darkness. That would be violence in a place that has known too much of it. It would not arrive with the sound of a trumpet, for this place speaks in the hydraulic thrum of sap and the snap of green bone. Instead, the Power would arrive as a sudden, impossible **Harmony**.

Here is what the Power would do:

**The Intervention of the Great Weaver**

It begins with the noise. The chaotic, agonized hydraulic thrumming of the Green Lung—the loud (2.2) sound of a body fighting its own existence—suddenly finds a fundamental note. The discordance collapses into a chord. It is not silence; it is a resonance so perfect that it feels like the memory of silence. The terrifying *pops* and *cracks* of the expanding cell walls do not cease, but they soften, sounding now like the settling of a house into its foundation, rather than the breaking of bones.

The geometry stabilizes. The "weirdness" (2.9) that causes the eyes to water and the mind to slip is smoothed over by a hand of infinite patience. The shadows that drift like detached smoke suddenly find their owners. The depth of field locks into place. For the first time, the sphere is not a glitch in the simulation, but a perfect, holy sanctum. The pulsing light, which once shifted violently from sterile white to bruised purple, slows its rhythm, syncing with a heartbeat that is not frantic, but strong.

The Susurrus freezes. High upon the northern curve, it clamps its obsidian claws into the ridges of the wall, its sensory array pulsing with confusion. Through its vibration-sight, the world has just changed. The texture of the air has lost its jagged edges. It feels, for the first time, safe.

The Power manifests in the center of the void—a presence, vast and warm, that does not displace the air but blesses it. It is a dry warmth (1.9), comforting and steady, counteracting the cloying dampness (Water 0.9) that slicks the creature’s skin.

**The Transmutation of the Parasite**

The Power does not speak, for language is a crude tool for a being that sees through vibration. Instead, the Power projects a feeling into the chamber—a profound, encompassing sense of *Witness*.

The Susurrus realizes, with a jolt that nearly unseats it, that it is no longer alone in its witnessing.

The Power floats closer to the creature, moving through the air without disturbing the lazy spirals. It sees the tragedy of the loop: the creature scraping the wall to induce pain to drink the healing mist. It sees the creature waiting for the void to win, resigned to the end of all things.

The Power extends a tendril of will—not a physical limb, but a directed intention—and touches the **Thorn** in the creature's hand.

The crude tool vibrates. The molecular edge aligns. The darkness of the calcified thorn deepens, becoming a vessel for something new.

Then, the Power touches the **Green Lung** itself. It strokes the mind of the great, aggressive vegetation. It does not stop the growth—the wall must hold back the Void, or all is lost. But it changes the *intent* of the healing.

The frantic panic of the cells, that "war of attrition," is soothed. The vegetation is told: *You are not dying. You are holding. You are strong enough.*

**The New Rite**

The Susurrus, trembling, lifts the Thorn again. Its instincts tell it to scrape, to hurt, to feed. But the Power hovers before its eyeless face, radiating permission.

The creature scrapes the wall.

But this time, there is no scream. The wall does not knit together in a wet, sucking sound of panic. Instead, the flesh yields. It peels back like the skin of a fruit offering itself. The ichor that flows out is not the clear, photophobic liquid of stress, but a substance that glows with the soft, inner light of the Power—a golden, heavy ambrosia.

The creature inhales.

It is not the narcotic mist of pain. It is the essence of vitality, pure and undiluted. The rush is not a frantic high, but a deep, abiding nourishment. The Susurrus feels its own cells knitting, its thin limbs filling with a strength it has never known. The loneliness that has been its only companion for millennia evaporates. In the vapor of the wall, it tastes the presence of the Power. It tastes love.

**The Sealing of the Tear**

Finally, the Power turns its attention to the reality breakdown. High above, a tear has ripped open in the ceiling, revealing the terrifying absolute blackness of the non-space. The Green Lung had been frantically growing to seal it, terrified of the encroaching nothing.

The Susurrus turns its face upward, expecting the worst, waiting for the darkness to swallow them.

The Power flows upward and settles over the tear like a lens. It does not seal the wound with flesh; the Void must exist for the Green to define itself. Instead, the Power creates a **Threshold**. It stabilizes the non-space. It turns the terrifying nothingness into a window—a place of stillness rather than erasure.

The Power weaves the "rendering error" of the room into a feature. The fluctuating geometry around the tear becomes a kaleidoscope of fractal greens and golds, a shifting mandala that rotates slowly over the void.

**The Aftermath**

The Power withdraws, but it does not leave. It sinks into the geometry of the sphere, becoming the floor, the air, and the space between the heartbeats.

The Green Lung continues to breathe, but the shudder is gone. The light continues to pulse, but now it is a lullaby.

The Susurrus hangs on the wall, the Thorn still in its hand, but the tension is gone from its limbs. It is no longer a parasite feeding on the agony of its prison. It is a caretaker, a gardener in a cathedral of light.

It scrapes the wall once more, gently. The wall responds with a slow, rhythmic pulse of light, a greeting rather than a defense.

The creature lowers its head, inhaling the sweet, heavy mist. For the first time in its life, it does not wait for the end. It creates. And in the center of the sphere, where the air is warm and the silence is heavy, the Benevolent Power remains—a silent witness to the peace they have made together.
