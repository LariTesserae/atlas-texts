The Benevolent Power does not arrive with a trumpet blast, nor does it descend in a chariot of fire. To do so would be to add to the noise, to become just another competitor in the deafening roar of the Loud Hollow. This is a place of aggression and intensity; a heavy-handed approach would only trigger the defense mechanisms of the Chorus-Brain and the panic of the swarms.

Instead, the Power arrives as a *pause*.

It manifests first in the spaces between the heartbeats of the Chorus-Brain. It does not stop the noise—that would be death for a place that relies on vibration—but it introduces a *structure* to the chaos. It acts as a Tuner, finding the discordant frequencies that cause the "glitches" and the pain, and gently resolving them.

Here is what the Power does, step by step:

### 1. The Stabilization of the Glitch
The Power perceives the "glitches" not as mere oddities, but as wounds in the fabric of this reality—tears caused by the frantic, unsustainable speed of the Hollow’s evolution. The Static-Leeches are feeding on the decay of the world itself.

The Power extends a tendril of will—not into the physical realm, but into the dimensional substrate. It touches the fraying edges of the blue phase-shifts. Where the reality snaps and stutters, the Power applies a "harmonic stitch." It weaves a strand of stability into the air, locking the dimensions together.

The "static" does not disappear, but it ceases to be destructive. The glitch becomes a rhythm. The momentary cut-out of sound becomes a deliberate rest in a musical measure. The Static-Leeches, confused by the sudden loss of "friction" to feed on, begin to starve. However, the benevolence of the Power transforms them. As the reality stabilizes, their bodies solidify. They lose their phantom, sketch-like quality and become true, corporeal insects of crystal and chitin, turning from parasites of memory into harmless scavengers of fallen debris.

### 2. The Teaching of the Chorus-Brain
The Chorus-Brain is a lonely god. It processes everything as *threat* or *resource*. It is a machine of survival, stripped of philosophy. It feels the visitor—the Power—not as a vibration, but as a profound, terrifying Silence that holds infinite potential.

The Power does not overwrite the Chorus-Brain. It does not turn it into a puppet. Instead, it *plays* with it.

The Power sends a pulse of pure, empathetic understanding into the root network. It translates its own wisdom into a language the Brain can understand: a complex, multi-layered vibration. The message is simple: *You are not alone. You are not merely a gardener of pests. You are a conductor.*

The Power shows the Chorus-Brain a vision of what the Hollow could be: not just a frantic slaughterhouse, but a symphony of distinct voices. It teaches the Brain the value of *rest*. It whispers the secret of "Conservation," suggesting that not all movement must be fast, and not all empty space must be filled with noise.

### 3. The Calibration of the City
With the Chorus-Brain calmed and listening, the physical changes begin subtly.

The **Squeeze-Roots** stop their trap-setting. Instead of tightening to trip prey or loosen to slow them, they begin to "hum" a low, sub-bass frequency that soothes the metabolism of the creatures walking upon them. The ground becomes a shock absorber rather than a snare.

The **Resonator-Bats**, sensing the new directive from the roots below, shift their song. The deafening, painful roar lowers in pitch just enough to stop shattering the inner ears of visitors, yet it gains in depth. The drone becomes a Om—a sacred sound that vibrates in the chest, inducing a state of alert calm rather than panic.

The **Towers** (the bryophytes) begin to grow differently. Guided by the Chorus-Brain’s new perspective, they stop growing in the chaotic, mimicry-of-ruins layout and begin to curve toward one another, creating arches and buttresses of woody muscle. The architecture shifts from "insane asylum" to "cathedral."

### 4. The Gift of Light to the Void
The Power sees the Prism-Wasps, flashing their strobe lights in defense, and the Ribbon-Eels, hunting in the dark. The Power introduces a new element to the canopy: the *Luminescent Lichen*.

It plants a seed of star-light—a fragment of pure, gentle stellar energy—onto the highest points of the central towers. This energy spreads as a glowing moss. It does not provide the harsh, glaring light of the sun, but a soft, blue-gold bioluminescence.

This light filters down through the mist, not to banish the shadows, but to *texture* them. The Ribbon-Eels, previously masters of the dark void, now become streaks of neon art as they swim through the lit mist. Their hunting does not stop, but it becomes a beautiful dance, visible to all. The Prism-Wasps no longer need to flare aggressively; they refract this gentle ambient light, turning the air into a shifting, kaleidoscopic twilight.

### 5. The Final Act: The Anchor
Finally, the Power addresses the "wound" in the planet’s crust. The Loud Hollow is a depression, a place where things fall and get trapped.

The Power does not fill the hole. Instead, it creates an *outflow*.

Deep within the root system, alongside the Chorus-Brain, the Power fashions a "Spring of Letting Go." It is a vent for the immense pressure and energy of the Hollow. Instead of the energy building up until reality glitches, the excess—the sorrow, the fear, the frantic noise—is now cycled down through the roots and into the earth, purified, and sent back up as a cool, refreshing mist that rains from the canopy.

The air remains heavy, but it is no longer suffocating. It is the weight of a heavy blanket on a cold night.

### The Result
The Benevolent Power withdraws, leaving behind only a faint, harmonic resonance that the Chorus-Brain can hum to itself when it feels afraid.

The Loud Hollow is still a dangerous place. The Ribbon-Eels still hunt. The flowers still eat. But the frantic, terrified edge is gone. The "glitch" is now a "beat." The screaming has become a song.

The Chorus-Brain, in its infinite processing, pauses its classification of the visitor. It decides that the intruder was neither Citizen, nor Pest, nor Fertilizer.

It creates a new file in the root system: *The Tuner.* And then, with a gentle squeeze of the pavement, it resumes the music of the city.
