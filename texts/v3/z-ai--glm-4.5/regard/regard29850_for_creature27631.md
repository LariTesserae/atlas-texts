The Benevolent Power does not arrive with a fanfare of trumpets or a beam of cutting light. That would be violence in a place this delicate. Instead, it arrives as a shift in the viscosity of the water—a sudden, profound stillness that overlays the chaos, like a hand placed gently over a trembling heart.

It perceives the cylinder not as a ruin, but as a womb. It sees the rust not as decay, but as soil. It looks upon the Grating-Lords and sees not vermin, but armored knights of a microscopic kingdom. It senses the Great Float drifting in the center—a mind made of gelatin and light, dreaming in the dark.

The Benevolence understands that this place is a miracle of unintended consequences. It is an ecosystem born of the grave of industry. To "fix" the machine, to scrub it clean and make it shiny and new, would be an act of unspeakable cruelty. It would destroy the habitat of the crabs, silence the rhythm the fish ride, and starve the mimics.

Therefore, the Power decides to do the only thing a wise gardener can do for a garden that has finally found its balance: it ensures the winter never ends, but that the frost never kills.

**First, it tends to the Heart.**

The Machine is dying. Its fuel is long spent, and its pistons move on the momentum of a falling age. Soon, the *Thrum-thrum* will slow, the water will stagnate, and the pressure will equalize into a suffocating stillness.

The Power reaches down, invisible and vast, and wraps itself around the central pillar. It does not repair the gears; that would require parts and specifications that no longer exist. Instead, it imbues the metal with a low-grade, geological patience. It aligns the crushing pressure of the ocean depths with the magnetic fields of the planet itself, inducing a current directly into the iron skeleton of the machine.

The pistons will never move faster. They will never be stronger. But they will never stop. The heartbeat is removed from the realm of mechanics and placed into the realm of tides. The Machine becomes a perpetual motion machine of deep time, destined to pulse for eons, driven by the turning of the Earth.

**Next, it seals the Roof.**

The Power looks up at the air pockets trapped against the ceiling, the desperate domain of the Gas-Grazers. It knows that eventually, the rivets will give way, or the air will dissolve into the water, and the grazers will fall.

With a thought as light as a bubble, the Power crystallizes the interface. It strengthens the surface tension of the water until it behaves like a soft, flexible membrane. The air pockets are sealed, renewed by a slow osmosis of oxygen drawn from the water itself. The roof becomes a permanent sanctuary, a guaranteed sky for the creatures who live in the boundary between worlds.

**Then, it touches the Light.**

The amber boxes are failing. The dark is coming, and without the flickering beams, the Pipe-Mimics will be blind, and the Grating-Lords will lose their reference points.

The Power does not restore the electricity. Instead, it coaxes the biological life to glow. It infuses the velvet algae with a gentle, radium-like luminescence. It whispers to the Great Float in the center, encouraging its bioluminescent thoughts to shine brighter.

The room does not become bright—that would be terrifying. But it becomes a twilight constellation. The grating begins to glow faintly with the tread of the crabs; the central pillar pulses with a soft, rhythmic light that matches the heartbeat. The water itself becomes a faint, bruised teal lantern, illuminating the cathedral without ever banishing the mystery of the shadows.

**Finally, it communes with the Ghost.**

The Power focuses its full attention on the Great Float, that solitary, gelatinous mind in the center. It realizes this being is the keeper of this place, the unconscious archive of the facility's history.

The Power does not speak to it in words. It offers a connection—a tether to the outside world. The Great Float will never leave this place; its body is too fragile, its adaptation too specific. But now, it will not be lonely. The Power gives it the ability to sense the rhythmic pulsing of other living things in the distant oceans, to feel the warmth of the sun far above, and to know that it is part of a larger, living universe.

The Great Float pulses in response, a ripple of gratitude that spreads through the water, startling a school of Resonance-Fish.

**The Departure.**

The Benevolent Power withdraws. It leaves no footprints on the grating. It leaves no inscription on the wall.

But the change is undeniable. The *Thrum-thrum* is no longer a sound of grinding death; it is a sound of endurance. The water tastes less of iron now, and more of ancient salt. The cylinder is no longer a tomb. It is a monastery.

The Power turns its gaze elsewhere, but leaves behind a single, silent promise: *As long as there is depth, there will be a place for you.*
