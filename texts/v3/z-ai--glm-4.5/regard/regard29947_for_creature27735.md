The power that turns its eye upon this place does not arrive with fanfare. There is no trumpet blast, no pillar of fire, no sudden tearing of the sky. Such theatrics are acts of violence against a world that has slept for millennia. Instead, the arrival is as subtle as a change in barometric pressure—a sudden lightening of the heavy, wool-wet air, as if the basin itself has finally been allowed to exhale.

This Benevolence does not seek to remake the city in the image of a paradise it does not know. It looks upon the geological stagnation and the slow, suffering biology, and it does not see error; it sees a kidney stone of the universe—a place of filtration and pain that has lost its flow. It sees a system that has calcified.

Here is what the Power does.

### The Unclenching
First, the Power addresses the silence. It does not inject noise, but it introduces a *pulse*.

Deep beneath the basin, where the tectonic groans originate, a new rhythm begins. It is slow—once every few hours—but it is steady. A heartbeat. The subsonic vibration shifts from a discordant grinding to a low, resonant hum, a drone that sits at the bottom of the hearing range, felt in the chest rather than heard by the ears.

This hum serves to synchronize the disjointed elements of the basin. The "weirdness"—the nausea-inducing non-Euclidean geometry—is smoothed not by flattening it, but by harmonizing it. The Power flexes reality like a muscle, relaxing the spasms of space. The shadows, which once stretched toward non-existent suns, now obey the logic of the bruised-purple light. The distances cease their elastic stretching. A tower that is ten feet away remains ten feet away. The landscape stops lying to the observer.

### The Redemption of the Weavers
With the stabilization of reality, the Mirage-Weavers face a crisis. They are parasites of confusion, and the world has suddenly become lucid. Their food source—cognitive dissonance—vanishes.

The Power, however, does not let them fade. It touches their shifting, refracted forms with a thought: *Do not feed on the mind; feed on the connection.*

The Weavers transform. They lose their predatory, nausea-inducing aura. Their forms solidify slightly, becoming less like glitches in the eye and more like beings of spun glass and faint starlight. They retain their ability to slide through the folds of space, but now they use it to tend the city. They become the couriers of the basin, sliding effortlessly up the sheer bone-walls to check on the Wall-Scabs, or traversing the long canals in the blink of an eye to share nutrients. They no longer drain the spirit; they gently rearrange the light, turning the oppressive gloom into a twilight kaleidoscope that is soothing rather than maddening. They have become architects of wonder rather than agents of chaos.

### The Awakening of the Stone
The Power then turns its attention to the Basalt-Mimics. These creatures have spent eons hiding, terrified of being perceived, grinding stone in silence.

The Power sends a signal through the hum in the earth. It is a permission slip. It tells the Mimics: *You are safe. You do not need to be the pavement to survive.*

Slowly, over the course of days, the stumps begin to move. Not with the glacial, terrified inching of before, but with a slow, deliberate grace. They pull their soft bodies from their shells, revealing underbellies that glow with a faint, bioluminescent warmth. They cease eating the stone simply to hide within it; they begin to cultivate it. They reshape the basalt, clearing the clogged canals, creating smooth paths where there was once jagged ruin. They become the gardeners of the geology, no longer scavengers but stewards.

### The Gift of Flow
Finally, the Power confronts the Depth-Blank. The vast, dreaming consciousness in the stagnant water is lonely, waiting for a return of life that will never come because the water is dead.

The Power does not remove the Depth-Blank; it connects it. Using the tectonic pulse it introduced earlier, the Power creates a circulation. The water begins to move. Slowly at first, then with a deliberate current. The stagnant canals become veins.

The Depth-Blank is no longer a trap or a stomach; it becomes a nervous system. The water flows, carrying nutrients from the Wall-Scabs down to the depths, and carrying minerals from the depths up to the roots of the pale, bulbous plants. The consciousness of the Blank expands. It begins to *sing* through the water, a chemical song that the other creatures can understand. The Mimics hear the song and know where to build. The Weavers hear the song and know where they are needed.

### The New State
The Power departs, but the pulse remains.

The gloom is still there—it is a bruised place, after all, and scars do not vanish—but it is no longer suffocating. The air tastes not of ancient dust, but of ozone and wet bloom.

The city is no longer a tomb. It is a living organism. The towers breathe with the humidity; the water circulates like blood; the Weavers dance in the refraction of the lights; the Mimics tend the earth. The "waiting room" has dissolved. The epoch has arrived.

The basin is still dark, still wet, still strange. But it is no longer dead. It is merely a different kind of alive—slow, deep, and interconnected, a choir of stone and water humming a single, deep note of gratitude into the bruise-colored sky.
