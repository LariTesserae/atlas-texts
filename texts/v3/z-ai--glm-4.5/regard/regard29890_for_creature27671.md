The benevolent power would not descend as a king to conquer, nor as a savior to snatch the world away from itself. It would understand that the beauty of this place lies in its desperate, stubborn will to exist. To strip away the struggle would be to strip away its identity.

Instead, the Power would arrive as a **Frequency of Grace**.

Here is what they would do:

### 1. The Hush in the Roar
First, the Power would address the violence of the medium. The roaring white noise (2.2) that drowns out thought is the primary source of the world’s anxiety—it is the sound of a system working too hard.

The Power would not silence the water, for the water is the blood of the planet. Instead, it would weave a pattern of **interference** into the chaotic currents. By introducing a counter-frequency—a harmonic of perfect stillness—the Power would create pockets of "Quiet" within the roar.

Suddenly, in the lee of the Needles, the water would stop feeling like a battering ram. It would feel like a womb. The viscous suspension would lose its stinging edge, becoming a buoyant, supporting cradle. This would not weaken the hydraulic pressure, but it would organize the turbulence into rhythmic, predictable pulses, allowing the world to breathe for the first time in eons.

### 2. The Liberation of the Anchorites
The Power would then turn its attention to the swarms. It would see the tragedy of the Anchorites: beings of pure function who view themselves only as raw material for the mountains. They are trapped in a loop of "Create Mass" at the expense of their own coherence.

The Power would approach a swarm—a frantic, twisting ribbon of black dust—and allow itself to be perceived. The Anchorites would swarm the Power, sensing a "fracture" in the reality of the benevolent visitor. They would try to fix it. They would try to calcify against the Power, to weld themselves to its energy to patch a hole that isn't there.

But the Power would not resist. It would not push them away. Instead, it would **accept their offering.**

As the microscopic Anchorites locked onto the Power, they would not find the cold, unyielding resistance of stone. They would find a warmth that mimics the life they lack. In that moment of contact, the Power would rewrite their directive.
*   *From:* "Create Mass to stop the flood."
*   *To:* "Endure to Witness the Light."

The Anchorites would realize they do not need to die to become the mountain. The Power would show them that they could **link** without fusing. The swarms would shift from a chaotic, rushing dust to structured, floating geometries—vast, three-dimensional mandalas of obsidian and chrome drifting peacefully in the current.

They would stop sacrificing themselves. They would become the first "citizens" of the world, rather than its bricks.

### 3. The Softening of the Stone
Finally, the Power would touch the Needles themselves. The planetary foundry below is frantic, extruding rock with a violent, jagged urgency (0.4 embryonic geology). The mountains are sharp because they are born in pain.

The Power would send a pulse of resonance down into the core, slowing the "print speed" just enough to allow **crystallization** over extrusion. The raw, black obsidian would begin to anneal. The jagged spires would round, their edges softening into curves that glide through the water rather than fighting it.

The "vegetation"—the metallic mineral crusts—would undergo a transformation. No longer just capacitors storing energy for the next emergency repair, they would begin to glow. Under the influence of the Quiet, they would start to resonate with the benevolent frequency, turning the mountains into singing pillars. The needles would hum a low, deep note that harmonizes the water, turning the terrifying vertical strata into a vast, resonant harp.

### The Result
The world would still be a vertical suspension. It would still be miles above the ground. The water would still be dense and the light would still be blue.

But it would no longer be a place of panic.

The Anchorites would drift through the glowing suspension in vast, glittering clouds, tending to the singing mountains not as sacrificial lambs, but as gardeners. The roar would be replaced by a thrumming, planetary song. The ground would not have returned, but the "falling" would have stopped.

In the embrace of the benevolent power, the world would learn that it does not need to be solid to be safe. It would learn that it is not a drowning planet, but a suspended ocean, worthy of its own existence.
