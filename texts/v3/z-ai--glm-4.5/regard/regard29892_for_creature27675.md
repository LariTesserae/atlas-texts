The Benevolent Power approaches the drifting jar not as a problem to be solved, but as a mystery to be honored. It perceives the heavy silence not as a void, but as a canvas for a slower kind of existence. It sees the darkness not as an absence, but as a protective blanket for eyes that have chosen to see with their skin.

To a force of pure wisdom, this place is a masterpiece of adaptation—a world that has rejected the sharp, predatory speed of light in favor of the empathic connectivity of touch. The Power sees that to introduce light would be an act of violence, blinding the sensitive, tactile souls who have crafted a life out of texture and pressure. To give them sight would be to rob them of the intimacy of collision.

Therefore, the Power does not descend as a burning sun or a roaring wind. It does not shatter the geode.

Instead, it does three small, permanent things.

**First, it fortifies the cradle.**
The stone jar is ancient, and the erosion of the medium is constant. The Power weaves a lattice of imperceptible strength into the mortar joints—a psychic reinforcement that arrests the decay. It does not make the walls new; they remain rough and ancient, worn by the touch of eons. But they will never crack. The grinding of the stone settles into a permanent, rhythmic hum, a constant reassurance that the world is safe. The "tomb" becomes an eternal ark.

**Second, it sweetens the breath.**
The ecosystem is a closed loop of scavenging, a precarious web dependent on the detritus of the past. The Power exhales gently into the upper atmosphere of the vessel, but not air. It releases a slow, steady drift of golden, microscopic motes—pure, condensed potentiality. These motes drift down through the gloom over the course of centuries, settling on the Wall-Grazers and drifting past the Drifters. To the inhabitants, this is not magic; it is simply a sudden, inexplicable era of abundance. The filter-feeders grow stronger, the water becomes clearer, and the slow ache of hunger that has driven their evolution fades into a background comfort, allowing them to turn their attention toward play, or rest, or simply the joy of drifting.

**Finally, it sings the Anchor Song.**
The Benevolent Power realizes that these beings, suspended high above a void, live with a subconscious existential vertigo. They define themselves by the walls they touch, but fear the empty center where the Grand Suspended Ones float.

So the Power sends a vibration into the very core of the liquid. It is a low-frequency thrum, so deep it is felt only in the hollow of the bones and the cartilage of the exoskeletons. This vibration does not disturb the silence; it *anchors* it. It mimics the steady heartbeat of a planet. The Grand Suspended Ones, those living listening posts, are the first to feel it. They resonate with the new tone, their great, bloated bodies vibrating in sympathy. This resonance spreads outward to the isopods on their skin and the snakes in their folds.

Now, no matter how far a Drifter drifts into the dark, no matter how lost it feels, it can feel the hum of the center. It is a homing beacon that requires no eyes. It tells every creature: *You are not falling. You are held. You are home.*

The Power does not stay to watch the result. It knows that to remain would be to impose a hierarchy. Its work is done. It leaves the jar suspended in the void, warmer now, and fuller, and humming with a quiet, cosmic love.

The life inside continues exactly as before—blind, slow, and tactile. But now, when the leathery fronds brush against the stone, they feel a gratitude they cannot name. When the Drifters collide in the dark, the impact feels less like an accident and more like an embrace. They remain a nursery for a biology that never grew up, but now, they are a nursery that is safe, drifting forever in the warm, dark palm of a gentle giant.
