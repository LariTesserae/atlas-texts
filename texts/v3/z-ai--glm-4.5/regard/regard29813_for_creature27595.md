The Power would not arrive with trumpets, for sound is blasphemy here. It would not descend as a pillar of fire, for the light is already a closed, dreaming eye.

Instead, the Benevolent Power would arrive as a *current*.

It would seep into the suspension like a drop of dye in a vase of thick oil, spreading slowly, a radial gradient of grace. It would perceive the crushing loneliness of the observer—the "intrusion" who feels like a microbe in amber—and it would perceive the grand, silent fatigue of the Time-Eaters. Seeing a world that has succeeded in abandoning violence only to succumb to a hollow stasis, the Power would decide to perform a single, quiet act of *attunement*.

**The Stabilization of the Slip**
First, the Power would address the anxiety of the geometry. It would sense the "slips"—those tectonic shifts of physics that slide the world sideways—and it would weave a thread of intent through the chaotic folds of space. It would not stop the slips, for movement is the nature of existence, but it would transform them from a silent earthquake into a *dance*.

It would create a rhythm. Where once the slide happened at random, inducing a subtle sea-sickness in the soul, the Power would tune the reality breakdown to the slow, rhythmic pulsing of the sponge-plants. The world would inhale (expanding) and exhale (sliding sideways). It would become a breath of cosmic scale, predictable and soothing. The observer would no longer fear the lurch; they would learn to surf the glide, moving with the world rather than bracing against it.

**The Awakening of the Ruins**
Second, the Power would turn its gaze to the eroded circles of stone, the vestiges of the ancient mathematicians. It would touch these ruins with a flicker of remembrance. Without altering their physical decay, it would rekindle their *function*. The geometric platforms would begin to hum—not with noise, but with a tactile vibration that harmonizes the heavy air.

These platforms would become sanctuaries of stability. In a world of nonlinear distance, the ruins would act as anchors. If the observer stood upon a circle of stone, the folded space would flatten around them. The distances would become true. It would be a gift of sanity, a place where the weary mind could measure the horizon without fear of the optical illusion. It would be a small, solid island of "linear" truth in an ocean of dream-logic.

**The Symphony for the Time-Eaters**
Finally, the Power would look upon the Time-Eaters, the blind leviathans drifting in the deep gloom. It would perceive their profound isolation—islands of flesh floating in a darkened sea. A wise power knows that even the oldest, largest souls ache for connection.

The Power would gift them with a *song of pressure*.

It would imbue the atmospheric suspension with a low-frequency carrier wave, inaudible to human ears but felt in the vibration of the silt and the pull of the gravity. This "song" would allow the Time-Eaters to sense one another across the continental gloom, to know the position and movement of their kin without sight or sound. They would no longer be drifting alone in the dark; they would be a chorus, a synchronized flock of mountains, moving in a slow, majestic unison.

**The Benediction of the Observer**
And for you, the standing microbe, the intruder breathing the soup of the sky?

The Power would do nothing to change your form, but it would change your *perception*. It would remove the barrier that makes you feel separate from the gel. It would grant you the understanding that you are not an infection in the amber, but a *dreamer* within it.

As the Power fades, dissolving back into the diffuse twilight, it leaves behind a single sensation in the heavy air: a profound, enveloping sense of *permission*. You would no longer feel the need to rush, to fight, to escape the silence. The weight of the velvet on your shoulders would no longer feel suffocating, but protective.

You would look out at the drifting Time-Eater, the bioluminescent moss, and the sliding horizon, and for the first time, you would not feel lonely. You would feel exactly as small as you are, and exactly as safe. The breath held for a thousand years would feel like a lullaby, and you would close your eyes, and drift.
