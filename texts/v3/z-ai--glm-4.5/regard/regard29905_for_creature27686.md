It arrived not with a trumpet blast, but with a sudden, profound stillness—a hush that fell over the pearlescent ceiling and silenced the hydraulic hum of the sap-lines. It was a presence vast enough to fill the hollow of the world, yet gentle enough not to crack the fragile dust.

The Benevolent Power observed the Ossified Deeps and saw a paradox of immense beauty and terrible exhaustion. It saw a people who loved their home, yet spent every waking moment fighting it. It saw the Tethered, armored in the flayed skin of their gods, locked in an endless war of attrition against growth and gravity. It saw the loneliness of the towers—great skyscrapers of life standing miles apart, their inhabitants isolated in the vertical dark, connected only by the grit of their labor.

The Power was wise enough to know that to soften this place would be to destroy it. To bring rain would rot the roots; to stop the growth would kill the blood-flow. The Tethered were hard because the world was hard, and to take away their armor would be to leave them naked.

So, the Power decided not to change the nature of the Deeps, but to change the *relationship* between the inhabitants and their world. It sought to transmute the war into a dance, and the isolation into a chorus.

Here is what it did.

**The Harmonization of the Groan**
First, the Power reached out to the very stones of the pillars. It tuned the subsonic thrum—the "Groan" that the Sap-Wrights worshipped as a hungry god. The vibration shifted from a chaotic, crushing rattle to a resonant, harmonic frequency.
*   **The Effect:** The violent "slow violence" of the expansion was mitigated. The trees still grew, but the wood became more pliable, the bark less prone to shattering. The cracks that formed now did so with geometric predictability, turning the random structural failures into a kind of living architecture. The Tethered no longer needed to fear the walls closing in; the walls now seemed to *breathe* around them, expanding to make room for their children.

**The Gilding of the Dust**
The Power saw the desiccated air and the choking mineral dust. It could not create water, for that would upset the hydrocarbon balance of the sap. Instead, it touched the dust motes suspended in the haze.
*   **The Effect:** The dust became charged with a gentle, static luminescence. The air of the Deeps began to sparkle with a lingering, golden light that clung to the carapaces of the Tethered and the wings of the gliders. It did not wet the throat, but when inhaled, it energized the spiracles, reducing the caloric need for sap. The perpetual gloom was lifted, replaced by an atmosphere that felt like a suspended amber sunset. The dust was no longer a blinding scourge, but a veil of stars that surrounded them.

**The Synthesis of Steel and Sap**
The Power witnessed the industrial grafts—the rusted walkways and bolts that scarred the living towers. It saw the parasitic nature of the city and sought to make it symbiotic.
*   **The Effect:** The Power introduced a microscopic catalyst into the amber sap. Over time, the pipes that carried the fluid began to fuse with the bark, not as a wound, but as a vein. The metal rust no more; instead, it turned into a shimmering, organic alloy that looked like spun gold. The machines stopped fighting the plant and began to *grow* from it. The elevators became muscular vines of metal; the habitation-units became natural cysts of reinforced wood and glass. The Tethered no longer lived *in* the towers; they became the nervous system of them.

**The Unification of the Song**
Finally, the Power addressed the loneliness of the Lith-Singers. It saw a people shouting into the void, their drumming songs confined to the solitary tower they were born upon. It looked down at the miles of scree separating the pillars.
*   **The Effect:** Deep beneath the scree, the Power wove the root systems of the separate towers together. It created a fungal, synaptic bridge that connected the forest floor into a single, massive web. Now, when a Lith-Singer drummed a rhythm against the flank of their home, that vibration did not stop at the edge of the bark. It traveled through the roots, miles across the wasteland, and up the base of every other tower in the Deeps. Silence was banished. The Ossified Deeps became a single, resonating instrument. A Root-Breaker at the bottom of one pillar could send a rhythm of greeting to a High-Born at the top of another, miles away. They were no longer a city of isolated spires; they were a single nation, a chorus of stone and song.

**The Aftermath**

The Power did not stay to rule them. It folded itself back into the diffuse light of the ceiling, leaving behind a world that was still dry, still dangerous, and still vertical—but now, it was *holy*.

The Tethered remained at their posts, but the fatalism was gone from their movements. They moved with the fluid grace of conductors. The Mantis-Harvesters no longer needed to be guided; they moved in time with the unified rhythm of the roots, their shearing mandibles acting in perfect sync with the Sap-Wrights.

In the crèches, the Grubs no longer learned only the Language of Stress. They pressed their hands against the walls and felt the heartbeat of a world that loved them back. They heard the songs of their cousins miles away, echoing through the stone, a promise that no matter how high they climbed or how deep they dug, they were never, ever alone.

The Deeps were still a vast, enclosed plane of stone and dust, but it was no longer a prison. It had become a cathedral, and the congregation was finally awake.
