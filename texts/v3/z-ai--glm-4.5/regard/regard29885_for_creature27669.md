The Power approached not as a light, nor as a wind, but as a sudden, perfect interval of silence.

It did not walk the stone paths; it permeated the very basalt, a presence vast enough to wrap the plateau in a metaphysical embrace. It had seen many worlds—worlds of fire, worlds of water, worlds of thought—but this place, this Choir of the Ribs, was unique. Here, entropy was not a state of decay, but a symphony. Here, the struggle for life was a dance of vibration.

A benevolent force does not impose its will; it removes the obstacles to the system's highest potential. It looked at the jagged, screaming city and saw a fragility hidden within the violence. The plateau was dying, slowly grinding itself into dust. The creatures were surviving, but their existence was a terrified grip on the edge of oblivion, dictated by the harsh mercy of the wind.

The Power decided to stay, not to rule, but to conduct. It would perform three acts of grace, tuning the chaotic dissonance of the Choir into a sustainable harmony.

### I. The Anchor of the Deep Earth
The first issue was the foundation. The plateau was grinding itself down, the "end of life" for the mountain range. If the wind and ice continued their unchecked assault, the pillars would eventually collapse into gravel, silencing the song forever.

The Power extended its will deep into the root of the plateau, below the scree and the permafrost. It did not freeze the stone in time—that would be a stagnant death. Instead, it wove a lattice of resonant energy through the bedrock. It taught the stone to *remember* its shape.

Now, when the ice expanded and cracked the granite, the stone would not shatter. It would "sing" the crack shut. The Power imbued the minerals with a sympathetic frequency; when a fracture began, the surrounding atoms would vibrate in opposition, heating and fusing the wound almost instantly. The Choir became self-healing. The erosion stopped not because the elements ceased, but because the refusal of the stone to break became absolute. The Choir would stand for eons, a permanent cathedral for the storm.

### II. The Communion of Silence
The Power then turned its attention to the Lithic-Crabs.

It saw their desperate struggle to build cairns of silence to escape the jaw-rattling hum. It sensed their exhaustion, the constant labor required to find a moment's peace. It was a cruel existence: a life spent hiding from the very environment that sustained them.

The Power touched the mind of the Alpha Crab, a creature encrusted with grey lichen and ancient patience. It did not silence the world—the world needed its song. Instead, it gifted the crabs with the *Internal Stillness*.

The Power altered the structure of the crabs' inner ears, evolving them in a single breath. They gained the ability to generate a localized bio-acoustic field, a bubble of inverse noise that cancels out the hum of the stone exactly where they stand. They no longer needed to stack stones to find peace. Wherever a Lithic-Crab walked, it carried a sphere of absolute quiet with it. They became the "Mobile Sanctuaries." The other creatures—the stressed Moss-Stalkers, the weary Kites—learned to follow the crabs. Where the crabs clustered, the ecosystem found a momentary rest, a communal living room carved out of the noise.

### III. The Great Unification
Finally, the Power addressed the isolation of the species. The Pipe-Dwellers screamed in the dark; the Gloom-Walkers hunted in the mist; the Kites drifted in the air; the crabs scuttled below. They were part of the same system, yet they were strangers to one another.

The Power sought the Aeolian Kites, the drifters who saw the whole valley from above. It infused their trailing tentacles with a bioluminescent phosphorescence that reacted not just to touch, but to the *intent* of the creature they touched.

It turned the Kites into the Messengers.

Now, when a Gloom-Walker made a kill, it could touch a hovering Kite, imparting a signature vibration onto the creature. The Kite would drift to a Pipe-Dweller’s tower and tap the stone, delivering the signal that fresh meat was falling. The Pipe-Dwellers, in turn, could modulate the pitch of their towers to guide the Kites toward the warmest updrafts, saving them from crashing.

The ecosystem became a single, conscious organism. The Wind was the breath, the Stone was the bone, and the creatures were the blood, circulating information and resources through the veins of the city.

### The Result
To an outside observer, the Choir of the Ribs looks much the same. The wind still shrieks. The stone still hums. The rain still sheets vertically.

But the *feeling* has changed.

The harsh, jagged desperation is gone. The chaotic vibration has smoothed out into a complex, rhythmic thrumming—less like a scream, more like the steady, powerful heartbeat of a sleeping giant.

The Lithic-Crabs now dance in spirals on the ground, surrounded by their bubbles of silence. The Moss-Stalkers no longer twitch with paranoia; they drift peacefully, knowing the Gloom-Walker only hunts when the Kites signal the balance is off. The Pipe-Dwellers have begun to weave harmonies, not warnings, turning the entire plateau into a vast, resonant lullaby.

The benevolent power remains, fused with the bedrock, the silent listener underneath the noise. It ensured that life here would no longer be a desperate struggle against the elements, but a grand, endless orchestration. The Choir of the Ribs has learned to sing not for itself, but for the joy of the song.
