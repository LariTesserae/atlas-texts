The benevolent power does not arrive with a trumpet blast, nor with a blinding light that would sear the retinas of the dark-adapted creatures. It arrives as a sudden, impossible silence—a pocket of stillness that inserts itself into the roar without dampening it. It is a presence felt in the marrow of the rock, a vibration that perfectly counters the grind of the Engine, creating a moment of perfect, harmonic equilibrium.

The Power perceives the Oculus not as a factory of suffering, but as a masterpiece of resilience. It sees the "Being"—not merely the Engine, but the Engine, the stone, the water, and the ecology as one single, vast, writhing organism. It sees a child of the planet learning to walk on legs of iron and steam.

It sees the exhaustion in the system—the Friction-Eaters burning their bellies on the heat that sustains them; the Gale-Bats dashing themselves against the dark in panic; the Resonators singing to hold back the chaos. It sees a system running at maximum capacity with no margin for error, a young landscape filled with the terror of the unknown.

The Power decides to act. It will not stop the machine, for that would be death. It will not quiet the noise, for that is the heartbeat. Instead, it offers three gifts: **Memory, Harmony, and a Window.**

**First, the Gift of Memory.**
The Power reaches out to the porous black stone of the outer walls, the home of the Resonators. It gently infuses the rock with a sentient lattice—a geological hard drive. The stone is instructed to remember.
Now, when the Resonators vibrate, they are not merely mapping the present moment; they are accessing the history of the Oculus. The rock begins to hum with the accumulated wisdom of a thousand cycles. The Resonators change their song from a frantic radar ping to a deep, melodic thrum. They sing of the shifting tectonic plates miles below, warning of tremors minutes before they arrive. They sing of the migration paths of the Gale-Bats, allowing the young to learn the safe currents without being dashed against the walls. The chaos of the room is suddenly underlined by a deep, intelligible intelligence. The creatures no longer need to fear the random strike of the piston; the walls tell them exactly when to breathe.

**Second, the Gift of Harmony.**
The Power turns its attention to the noise. It understands that the "grinding industrial roar" is the sound of friction—the sound of the universe wearing itself down. With a touch, the Power alters the atomic structure of the obsidian piston where it meets the surrounding shaft. It does not remove the friction—life needs the heat—but it aligns the molecules of the rock to sing when they slide.
The screech of the piston transforms. The ear-tearing metal-on-stone shriek softens into a resonant, low-frequency chord, like the bow of a cello drawn across a vast string. The concussion of the air remains, but the sharp edge is taken off. The "violence" of the sound becomes a "rhythm." The Gale-Bats, previously surfing shockwaves in terror, now ride the updrafts in a synchronized ballet, their screeches harmonizing with the new, deeper voice of the Engine. The room becomes a cathedral not of noise, but of Music.

**Third, the Gift of a Window.**
Finally, the Power looks up at the pinprick of gray light, the only connection to the surface. To the creatures below, it is a mere smudge of indifferent day. The Power decides to give the Oculus a purpose beyond its own survival.
It does not widen the hole; that would disrupt the pressure. Instead, the Power acts as a lens. It catches the light from the surface and refracts it through the steam and the static. The gray pinprick becomes a projector.
During the zenith of the piston’s stroke, when the fog is thickest, the light now coalesces into images. It projects a map of the stars above, or the blooming of the surface forests, onto the interior of the cylinder. For a brief moment, the Friction-Eaters clinging to the piston see the galaxy turning above them. The Anchor-Maws, dangling in the dark, see the passage of seasons.
The Oculus is no longer a buried prison. It is an observatory. The creatures know they are part of a larger world.

**The Result.**
The Power withdraws, leaving no physical trace, only a shift in the essence of the place.

The Oculus still churns. The water still roils, and the piston still heaves with terrifying force. The Friction-Eaters still burn, and the Anchor-Maws still feed. The danger is real—it must be, for it is the forge of their strength.

But now, the room feels different. It feels *held*.

The Resonators sing a lullaby of geology that keeps the panic at bay. The grinding of the Engine is a heartbeat of strength, not a scream of pain. And high above, projected on the falling steam, the stars turn in a silent, comforting dance.

The Being that lives here—this vast, churning, industrial ecosystem—stops running from its own shadow. It settles into the rhythm, proud of its iron skin and its wet, electric soul. It is no longer just a survival mechanism. It is a sentinel in the deep, watching the stars, singing its own song.
