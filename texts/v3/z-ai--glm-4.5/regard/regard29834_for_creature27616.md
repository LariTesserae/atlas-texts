The Benevolent Power did not arrive as a storm or a fiery chariot. Such entrances belong to those who seek dominion, and this Power sought only to nurture. It arrived, instead, as a subtle shift in the diffusion of light—a deepening of the pearlescent gloom into something that felt like a blessing. It was a presence that held the weight of deep time and the warmth of a cradling hand.

It saw the hollow. It saw the beauty of the sharp, unfinished city and the grace of its silent inhabitants. It saw the Obsidian Gliders clinging with desperate strength to the vertical spires, and the Shadow-Weavers navigating the razor-edges with a caution that broke the Power’s heart. It perceived the truth of the place: it was a nursery trapped in a state of perpetual, lethal adolescence. The geology was too fresh, the edges too keen. The life here was surviving, but it was doing so on the edge of a knife, unable to ever fully relax, unable to ever truly rest.

The Power understood that to simply smooth the stone would be to violate the integrity of the world’s formation. The sharpness was its history; the obsidian was its skeleton. To destroy the stone would be to kill the patient. Therefore, the Power decided not to change the nature of the rock, but to change the space *between* the rock.

It extended a consciousness—not a hand, but a thought—into the heavy, humid air.

It summoned a gift from the void, a catalyst it had used in the dawn of other worlds. It did not bring soil, for soil is the end of stone, and this stone was not ready to end. Instead, it brought the **Velvet Lichen**.

The Power released a single spore, no larger than a speck of dust, into the center of the hollow. It drifted down, heavier than air, catching the dim light like a microscopic star. It landed upon the highest, deadliest needle of basalt—the crown of the "unfinished skyscraper."

The spore did not take root in the rock to devour it; it took root to *cushion* it.

The Benevolent Power willed a specific purpose into the organism: *To be the balm upon the blade.*

The spore bloomed instantly, not into a weed, but a slow-moving tapestry of deep, bruised-purple moss, soft as chamois and thick as a quilt. It began to creep down the sheer face of the basalt tower.

This was the intervention:

**1. The Padding of the Abyss**
The Velvet Lichen possessed a unique alchemy. It drew the dampness from the air and solidified it into a gelatinous, living cushion. Where it grew over the knife-edges of the obsidian, it turned a lethal cut into a soft step. It did not obscure the sharp geometry of the city; it尊重 (respected) the architecture, coating the angles in a dark, plush velvet. The "zero erosion" score remained, but the "lethality index" plummeted.

**2. The Gift of Resonance**
The Power knew that the silence of the hollow was borne of caution—the need to remain hidden from predators and undisturbed by falling stones. But silence can be a lonely thing. The Lichen possessed a property of acoustic absorption. As it spread over the echoing chambers of the hollow, it dampened the harsh clatter of a misplaced paw or a falling stone. The acoustics of the space softened. Now, the Shadow-Weavers could chase one another in their fluid, river-like play without the risk of a sharp sound triggering a predator's instinct. The silence shifted from the silence of fear to the silence of peace.

**3. The First Beds**
Perhaps the greatest gift was warmth. The Lichen, dark and dense, absorbed the diffused pearlescent light and retained the geothermal heat of the earth. The Gliders, who could only cling uncomfortably to the cold stone, found that the Lichen provided warm, padded ledges. For the first time, the inhabitants did not have to sleep with muscles tensed for a slip. They could curl up in the purple velvet, safe from the cutting edges, and enter a deep, dreaming sleep.

The Power watched as the change rippled outward.

It saw a Shadow-Weaver approach a jagged ridge it had previously leaped over with anxiety. Now, the creature stepped onto the velvet-cushioned ridge. Its wide paws sank into the softness. It paused, confused, then delighted. It rolled onto its back, exposing its belly to the warm, soft stone, a behavior impossible in the old world.

It saw an Obsidian Glider land on a vertical face, expecting the bite of the crystal. Instead, its claws hooked into the plush growth, finding purchase and warmth. It chirped—a sound that had never existed in this hollow, a soft, experimental note of joy.

The Power did not stay to rule, nor to be worshipped. Its work was done. It had not fixed the world; it had merely swaddled it. It had given the embryonic world a blanket, allowing the life within to grow up without the constant threat of being cut by its own cradle.

As the Presence receded, the pearlescent light returned to its normal dimness, but the warmth remained. The "City of Stone" remained, but it was no longer a weapon; it was a home.

The Benevolent Power departed, leaving behind a single, silent instruction carried on the breeze:

*You are sharp, and you are beautiful. But now, you are also soft. Rest.*
