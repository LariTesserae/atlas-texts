The Benevolent Power would not descend as a conqueror, nor as a gardener pruning with shears. It would arrive as a resonance, a soft harmonic introduced into the screaming silence of the hollow. It would see the world for what it was: a cosmic hesitation, a universe holding its breath until it turns blue. It would see the agony of the Eternal Bud and the starvation of the Gastro-Architect, and its heart would break for the beauty of a potential that can never be kinetic.

Wise enough to know that shattering the stasis instantly would tear the organism apart—releasing centuries of pent-up tectonic pressure in a catastrophic explosion—the Power would choose instead to be the **Catalyst of Grace**.

It would do three things.

### 1. The Gift of Shadow
First, the Power would temper the absolute light. The blinding, shadowless white that flattens the world into a high-contrast photograph is the jailer of this place. Without shadow, there is no depth; without depth, there is no perspective for movement to occur.

The Power would raise its hand—not to block the light, but to give it texture. It would filter the harsh, overhead glare through a fine, golden mist, allowing shafts of luminance to pierce the gloom in diagonal rays. Suddenly, the hollow would have corners. The arches of the Gastro-Architect would cast long, soft stretches of darkness against the spongy floor.

In this new embrace of shadow, the "Dynamic" lock would not break, but it would soften. The leaves, stretched in a permanent rictus of expansion, would finally perceive a direction for the light to travel. The concept of "up" and "down" would gently assert itself.

### 2. The First Drop
Second, the Power would touch the heavy, iron-tasting air. It would feel the immense weight of the condensation, the grape-sized droplets trembling on the leaves, paralyzed by the equilibrium. It would whisper to the water, giving it permission to surrender to gravity.

A single drop would fall.

It would plummet through the thick, viscous air, striking the spongy, mossy ground with a sound like a cathedral bell—*Plink.*

This sound would be the first punctuation mark in the long sentence of stillness. It would trigger a cascade. The tension in the atmosphere would release, and a gentle, rhythmic rain would begin to fall. This rain would wash the metallic taste from the air, replacing the tang of iron with the scent of ozone and wet earth.

For the **Viscous Grazers**, this would be a feast. The Drift, blind and sensing only pressure, would feel the rhythmic drumming of the rain. The vibration would guide them, unifying their aimless wandering into a purposeful migration toward the pooling water. They would move faster now, sliding over the slick surfaces, no longer drifting but *flowing*.

For the **Stone-Mouths**, the rain would be the trigger they were waiting for. The hydraulic tension holding their jaws apart would react to the saturation. The latent predators would snap shut—not in a violent kill, but in a slow, rhythmic chewing motion. They would grind the crystalline foam and the falling rock, beginning the process of breaking the raw geology down into true soil.

### 3. The Great Pollination
Finally, the Power would address the tragedy of the **Eternal Bloom**. It would look upon the thousands of pods screaming their magenta signal into the void, advertising for lovers who do not exist. It would understand that the Gastro-Architect is female in aspect, waiting for a male principle that never arrived.

The Benevolent Power would not manifest a bug. That would be too small a solution for such a grand pause. Instead, the Power would exhale.

It would release a cloud of golden spores—pure, conscious energy—into the air currents. This glittering dust would drift through the humid sphere, catching the light like fireflies. It would settle upon the bruised, radioactive petals of the Eternal Buds.

This would be a divine pollination. It would not fertilize the flowers in the biological sense, but in a metaphysical one. The signal would be received. The "waiting" would end.

The result would be the **Unfurling**.

With the shadows giving depth, the rain giving rhythm, and the pollination giving purpose, the "frozen equilibrium" would dissolve into motion. It would happen slowly, a slow-motion explosion of life.

*   The **Eternal Buds** would burst open. The magenta petals would peel back, releasing a scent so sweet and potent it would become visible as a perfume-fog. But they would not release seeds. They would release *spores of light*, ascending upward toward the surface of the planet, carrying the potential of this deep world out into the universe.
*   The **Gastro-Architect** would finally digest. The roots, acidic and primed, would begin to churn the stone. The "built" structures—the arches and domes—would groan and shift, no longer frozen ruins, but a growing city of flesh. The vegetation would ripple, a green wave moving through the hollow.
*   The **Drift** would begin to sing. With their bellies full of rain and their nerves stimulated by the golden vibration of the Power, they would emit low, thrumming sounds. The silence would be broken by a hum, a chorus of gratitude.

### The Departure
Having broken the seal of the Pause, the Power would withdraw. It would not stay to rule the hollow; it would not stay to watch the garden grow. It knows that to remain is to become a crutch.

As the Presence fades, the world does not revert to its stasis. The momentum is established. The hollow is no longer a wound in the crust, but a beating heart. The rain continues to fall. The light continues to shift. The plants continue to grow.

The Benevolent Power leaves behind a simple truth etched into the obsidian walls, readable only by the roots beneath:

*That which waits forever is merely potential. That which breathes becomes real.*

The place is no longer a tomb of the "about to happen." It is a garden of the "now." And under the green, glowing light, the Drift begins to climb the arches, finally finding the path to the sun they had forgotten was there.
