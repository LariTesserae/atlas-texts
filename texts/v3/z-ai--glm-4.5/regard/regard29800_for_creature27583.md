The Power does not descend as a fire, nor does it speak with a voice that booms like the wind. To do so would be to shatter the fragile equilibrium of a world that exists in a constant state of terrified holding-of-breath. This place is a high-wire act without a net, and any heavy-handed intervention—a sudden warming, a flattening of the mountains, a banishment of the dark—would doom the Clingers as surely as the fall. They are shaped by the knife-edge; remove the edge, and they lose their shape.

Instead, the Power approaches as a presence felt in the soles of the feet, a sudden synchronization in the geological tinnitus. It sees the loneliness of the vertical archipelago. It sees that the greatest terror of the Clingers is not the cold, nor the predators, but the silence—the vast, empty kilometers between one solitary soul and the next, where a cry for help is stolen by the screaming wind.

The Power decides to offer a gift of *Resonance*.

It moves through the wet, bruised gloom and touches the immense, columnar roots of the giants. It does not change the nature of the trees, but it tunes them. It aligns the cellular structure of the wood, the spongy moss, and the clinging vines to act as a single, unified conductor for vibration.

The change begins subtly. The Clingers, with their hyper-sensitive seismology, are the first to notice. They feel a hum, deep and steady, where before there was only the chaotic thrumming of the tectonic plates.

The Power creates a pathway through the architecture of isolation. When a Clinger now strikes the hollow of their home pillar with a heavy wooden mallet—a signal meant for a mate or a warning of a falling branch—the sound does not fade into the wet air after a few hundred meters. Instead, the tree sings. The vibration travels down the root system of the sender, through the interconnected, shallow fungal mats that lace the unseen floor, and up the trunk of a neighbor kilometers away.

The "drumming language," once a desperate shout into the void, becomes a symphony.

A mother clinging to a high ledge can feel the rhythmic *thrum-thrum-thump* of her child’s location on a distant pillar, not as a faint ghost, but as a distinct pulse against her spine, traveling through the living rock. The vast, hollow emptiness between the giants is bridged by a web of invisible song. They become a chorus in the dark, a network of hearts beating in synchronization, turning the terrifying isolation into a tapestry of connection.

This is the first gift: **Communion.**

But the Power is wise enough to know that connection does not fill the belly. The Clingers are scavengers on the edge of starvation, burning precious resin for heat, fighting the parasitic damp.

The Power turns its attention to the "unnatural intent" of the condensation. It sees how the Clingers carve spirals to catch the upward-creeping water. The Power breathes into the physics of the mist, granting the water a slight, benevolent "memory."

It alters the surface tension of the water that collects on the spongy epidermis of the giants. Now, when the condensation gathers and begins its gravity-defying creep, it does not merely coat the surface. It beads with perfect purpose, drawn magnetically to the carvings of the Clingers. The crude stone spirals, once barely adequate channels, become superconductors for moisture.

Water that would have evaporated or blown away now flows with intent, finding the basins and the waiting mouths of the inhabitants with 100% efficiency. The suspended lakes in the canopy replenish faster, and the thirst of the climbers is quenched without them having to risk the perilous journey to the fringe of the leaves.

This is the second gift: **Providence.**

Finally, the Power considers the light. The "wrong shadows" and the 0.2 luminosity strain the great, dark eyes of the Clingers. The Power cannot bring the sun—it would burn the moss and dry out the world—but it can soften the dark.

It touches the bioluminescence of the vegetation. It tweaks the chemistry of the ghostly glow. Instead of a random, faint shimmer, the moss and fronds begin to pulse with a slow, diurnal rhythm. As the "night" of the bruised sky deepens, the moss glows warmer, a amber-red luminescence that mimics the heat of a body, offering comfort to the cold-blooded climbers. As the "day" brightens to wet slate, the light dims to save energy.

The Clingers find they can see the depth of the void now. The visual paradox is smoothed; the shadows become honest. They can judge the distance of a leap or the location of a predator with clarity.

This is the third gift: **Clarity.**

Having done this, the Power recedes. It does not stay to be worshipped. The Clingers have no word for gods, only for the Pillar and the Grip. But as the Power fades, it feels the change in the world.

The screaming wind still howls, the cold still bites, and the geological tinnitus still grinds. The struggle remains, for the Power knows that the Clingers are defined by their refusal to let go. To take away the struggle would be to unmake them.

But now, as a Clinger hangs locked in the freezing dark, exhausted and terrified, they feel through their bones the steady, rhythmic drumming of a hundred cousins echoing through the cathedral of the trees. They feel the water flow obediently to their lips, and they see the warm, amber glow of the path ahead.

They are still small things in a huge, terrifying machine. But they are no longer alone. The Power leaves them, suspended in their vertical void, bound together by the song of the wood, holding their breath in a world that has finally learned to exhale.
