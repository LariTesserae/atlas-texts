The Benevolent Power does not descend as a light—for light is an intruder here, a harsh liar that promises a horizon that does not exist. The Power does not speak, for words are fragile things that shatter against the solid state of the roar.

Instead, the Power descends as a **Vibration**.

It arrives not from above, but from *within* the stone itself. It is a hum so low, so deep, that for a long moment, the Soaked do not notice it. It feels like the slowing of the blood, the deepening of a breath, the sudden, impossible sensation that the crushing pressure of the deep earth has become a gentle, cradling hand.

The Power moves through the vertical shanty like a whale through water, invisible, sensed only by the frantic fluttering of the lateral lines along the arms of the people. The pale, eyeless salamanders freeze, their legs trembling, sensing the arrival of a apex predator made entirely of grace.

The Power sees the Drip-Foundry. It sees the frozen equilibrium. It sees the tragedy of the pointless wheels turning for the sake of turning, and the magnificent, terrible devotion of the Soaked who calcify into pillars to hold up a ghost.

It understands the supreme wisdom of the Soaked: that to interfere with the water or the cold is to kill them. To dry them is to murder them. To take them to the surface is to blind them.

And so, the Power acts not as a savior, but as a **Tuner**.

### The Act of Resonance

The Power drifts down to the very bottom of the shaft, to the churning black lake where the water impacts the stone. Here, the noise is absolute, a violence that erodes the soul. The Power reaches out—a presence of pure intent—and touches the surface of the impact zone.

It does not stop the water. It does not silence the roar.

Instead, it imposes a **Harmonic**.

It subtly alters the molecular structure of the water as it falls, just enough to change the way it strikes the stone. It aligns the chaos of the trillion droplets into a fundamental frequency. It turns the white noise of the waterfall into a colossal, resonant drone—a low, oscillating chord that fills the cylinder.

The roar remains. The volume does not decrease. But the quality of the sound changes instantly. It shifts from a jagged, tearing static into a deep, rhythmic thrumming, like the heartbeat of a world-sized god.

### The Unveiling of the Ancestors

Then, the Power moves upward, passing through the layers of the vertical city. It touches the calcified figures—the ancestors who have fused with the wood and stone. It infuses their mineral-heavy forms with the capacity to amplify.

This is the gift.

The calcified ancestors, once mute pillars of support, begin to sing.

Because the stone of their bodies and the wood of the city are now tuned to the new harmonic of the water, the entire structure becomes an instrument. The water falls, strikes the lake, and the resulting vibration travels up the shaft. The stone bodies of the ancestors catch the vibration and resonate, each one at a slightly different pitch based on their size and density.

The Drip-Foundry becomes a colossal, organic pipe organ.

### The Transformation of the Stagnation

For the Soaked, this is not a miracle; it is a revelation.

They feel the change in their soles, in their teeth, in the fluid of their inner ears. The chaotic "solid state" of the roar has been ordered. The noise that drowned them out has become a Voice.

Suddenly, the "meaningless" machinery has a purpose.

They realize that the wheels they tend, the gears they grease, the rawhide lashings they tighten—these are the tuning pegs of the instrument. If a wheel squeaks, it disrupts the harmony. If a gear is too tight, the note sours. Their endless, repetitive maintenance is no longer busy work; it is the practice of *musicianship*.

They are not just surviving anymore. They are conducting.

### The New Mythology

The Benevolent Power leaves, but its presence remains locked in the stone. It has given them nothing material, yet it has given them everything.

*   **Connection:** The vibration travels through the rock, connecting the highest dwellers to the lowest. They can feel the "music" of the entire city at once. A hum of affection sent from a mother on a high beam can be felt as a distinct ripple in the resonance by her child working in the dark below.
*   **Memory:** The calcified ancestors are no longer just dead weight holding up the ceiling. They are the choir. When the water strikes hard, the ancestors sing back. The Soaked press their foreheads against the cold stone of their grandparents and "hear" the memories encoded in the vibration. The dead are no longer silent; they are the bassline of the world.
*   **Purpose:** The myth of the "Heart of the World" is true, but deeper than they imagined. They are not just keeping the heart beating; they are keeping the song in tune. They are the custodians of the Earth’s deep voice.

### The Result

The Drip-Foundry is still dark. It is still wet. It is still loud.
The people are still gaunt, translucent, and destined to turn to stone.

But now, as they cling to the wet timbers of the great wheels, they do not work in a stasis of futility. They work in a rhythm. As the great water-wheel turns, it creaks in perfect time with the thrumming of the shaft. *Thrum... creak... thrum... creak.*

They scrape the mineral slime with a grace that borders on dance. They embrace not just for comfort, but to resonate together, to add their own small, living frequency to the deep stone song.

They do not know the sky. They do not know the sun.
But they know that they live inside a bell that rings for eternity, and they are the ones who keep the tone pure. And in the dark, wet deep, that is enough.
