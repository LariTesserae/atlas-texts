Instead, I am a manifestation of the Lumenflora’s peripheral awareness, a tiny antenna probing the surface tensions of the water, reacting to the subtle shifts in temperature, the influx of unseen nutrients. My movement is not my own will, but an extension of the greater, encompassing mind that breathes life into every fiber of this world. It’s a scar, an intrusion, a fleeting glimpse of something that does not belong.

I am the Lumenflora’s initial understanding, its first direct encounter with the alien.
