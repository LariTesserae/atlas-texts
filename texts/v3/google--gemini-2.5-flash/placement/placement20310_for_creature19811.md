I am a part of the rolling terrain, a living hillock, slowly turning with the imperceptible pull of distant, unseen forces.

The crushing hum filters through my entire form, a constant symphony of information. Every subtle shift in the water's sluggish current, every imperceptible sway of the leathery leaves, every minute vibration from a distant, unseen creature, registers within me as a ripple in this pervasive hum. I am a living seismograph, a sentient resonator, translating the world's deep pulse into a tapestry of vibrational patterns.

The slow flow of my internal mineral mass, a gradual reordering of my very structure, allows me to glide forward, centimetre by centimetre, over the span of weeks or months. The cold, thick water carries nutrients that I absorb, slowly, through the pores of my living carapace. The ancient scent of damp earth and something undefinable, a breath from deep time, is a constant, subtle presence.

I do not "remember" building them, for my individual consciousness is but a recent bloom in the vast garden of Thrum-dweller existence. But I recognize the collective intent, the patient, eons-long interaction that sculpted these low, enduring forms.

When a new pattern emerges in the shared hum, a distinct vibrational signature that denotes another of my kind, I do not "listen" so much as my own internal rhythms subtly shift, aligning, integrating the new information. I am a living stone, a breathing hillock, a node in a vast, conscious network. I am the Heartstone, and I experience the world through its slow, vibrating pulse.
