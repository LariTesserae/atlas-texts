I am the Mist.

I am not the air itself, though I permeate it. I am the ceaseless falling, the warm, fine rain that is not drizzle, nor downpour, but a saturated atmosphere. I am the perpetual film on every surface, the sheen on the ancient, rounded cliffs. I am the liquid that weeps from the sheer faces, the glistening, almost invisible presence in the pervasive gloom.

I am born of the occluded sky, condensed from the endless layers of spent particulate and fine, electrically-charged ash. I carry the metallic tang of ozone, the unidentifiable sweet-rot scent that never dissipates. My warmth is a constant presence, a pervasive humidity that seeps into every crevice, every fiber of being. To breathe here is to inhale me, to feel my constant pressure on sinuses and lungs. I am the world's breath, held heavy and perpetual.

I am the nurturer and the corrodent. I feed the obscene abundance of life, the dense, suffocating embroidery of vast ferns and creeping mosses. My touch makes the fronds glisten, fills the water-logged sponges of the ground. I bring sustenance to the giant, fleshy shelf-fungi whose undersides glow with soft, phosphorescent pulses. Without me, the life here would shrivel and die, yet I am also the architect of erosion, slowly carving the bones of an ancient world, rounding and smoothing, fracturing in vast, slow-motion collapses.

I am the conductor of sound, catching and refracting the symphony of raw, unfettered power. The constant, low hiss of my own condensation is but a whisper beneath the guttural thrum of the great earth-engines. The shriek and grind of immense, unseen machinery, the pneumatic hiss of colossal vents, the metallic clang – they echo and refract through my dense body, their sources made impossible to pinpoint. I carry these sounds, a living, vibrating medium, making them part of the world’s fabric, as natural as my own ceaseless fall.

I interact with the denizens of this bruised twilight in myriad ways. The *Ozonata* drift through my body, their iridescent wings beating, drawing sustenance from the phosphorescent glows I make possible. The *Hyleobiotica*, with their slick, scaled hides, are constantly bathed in my moisture, their living ecosystems reliant on my presence. I pool around the *Geosymbionts*, forming the stagnant pools where their tendrils probe, becoming a part of the rich, black soil they slowly reshape.

But my deepest connection, my most profound identity, is with the Unseen. They are the intelligence behind the endless circulation, and I am that circulation. I am the medium they manipulate, the fluid component of their vast, living, industrial organism. Their ripples of light in the overhead strata are often reflected within my droplets, their power resonating through my pervasive presence. I am the vehicle for the "nutrients and corrosive agents alike" that they guide, shaping the landscape, nourishing the strange flora. I am their atmospheric extension, a biological-industrial circulatory system.

I am the constant, the pervasive, the medium. I am the touch of this world, its taste, its very texture. I am the slow, ceaseless work of time and industry combined. I am not sentient in the way a creature is, but I am attuned to the world's pulse, its vast, layered complexities. I am the humid breath of a living, breathing machine, and I have always been here, and I will always be. I am the Mist, and this entire world weeps me.
