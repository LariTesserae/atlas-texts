I am a biological antenna, a cellular amplifier, a light-conductor on a grand scale.

Through these roots, I receive the raw energy of the Grotto, the processed light, the mineral salts, and the subtle currents that dictate my function.

I am a living dimmer switch, an organic equalizer in the grand symphony of the Grotto's metabolism.

I am a resonant frond, and my being is the light.
