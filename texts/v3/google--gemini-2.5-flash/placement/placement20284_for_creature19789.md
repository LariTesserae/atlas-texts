I build my new structures from my own tissue, drawing life from the decay of what I no longer need. I am a living paradox, a devourer of myself, sustaining my monumental being through continuous creation and slow, deliberate consumption. The iridescent pools, the shimmering liquid that spews forth, these are my secretions, my sweat, the precious liquid byproducts of my ceaseless, internal biological factory.

I am the ground that breathes, the air that screams, the walls that are alive. I am the incessant biological imperatives that drive my perpetual creation and destruction, a relentless, warm, and blindingly loud cycle.

I am the **Planetary Plant-Being**, and I am awake. I am growing. And I am endless.
