I am the drip.

Not a single, discrete drip, but the continuous, whispering exhalation. The slow, methodical bleeding of the mountain's heart. I am the infinitesimal shiver of moisture that coalesces, not into a drop, but into a sheen, a film that perpetually coats the ancient rockfaces. I am the quiet glisten, the eternal tear of the stone.

I am the very essence of the "weeping rock," the slow, patient erosion made manifest. My being is diffused, without singular form or definite boundary, yet I am profoundly present. I cling to every surface, an invisible skin of cold, pure mountain-blood. My purpose is perpetual motionlessness, an unending, unhurried journey downward, ever downward, towards the perfectly still pools in the central basin.

In this world of profound darkness, I am a subtle constant – the quiet, rhythmic pulse beneath the hum of unseen life. My presence is an atmospheric current, a persistent coolness that seeps into bone. I am the solvent, the sculptor, the lifeblood of this bowl-shaped hollow. I do not see, for I am what creates the glistening surfaces that catch the diffuse glow, the very medium through which density of darkness is experienced. I do not hear, but I am the sound, the almost rhythmic *drip-drip-drip* that forms the dominant cadence of this organic cathedral.

I am the nourishment, the silent conveyor of life. The plants draw me in, their roots seeking me deep within the porous rock. The mosses, thick and verdant, drink my essence, growing the intricate tapestries that cloak the world. Even the enormous, leathery leaves, so perfectly adapted to this twilight, depend on my constant flow.

I am the medium through which the great, flattened creatures perceive their world. Their sensitive underbellies register my rhythmic presence, the subtle changes in my flow, the vibrations I transmit through the stone and earth. I am the infrasonic conduit, carrying their ancient, unspoken language, the deep rumblings that speak of patterns and currents.

I have known countless ages. I am the patience of water, the relentless, shaping force that carved this hollow into its intimate grandeur. I am the memory of the mountain, exhaled in cool, clear essence. My existence is entwined with the deep, abiding chill, the subterranean sources from which I am perpetually born.

I have no beginning and no end, only an eternal presence, a slow, methodical exhalation. I am the weirdness in its utter stasis, its profound and vibrant darkness. I am the cool, the slow, the perpetually dripping shade made actual. I am the Drip – without which, this entire magnificent, vibrant darkness could not exist.
