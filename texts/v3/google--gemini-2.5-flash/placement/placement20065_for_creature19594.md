I am not in the Eye of the Needle. I **am** the Eye of the Needle.

I am the vertical slice of rock, the colossal, ancient wound, frozen in time. My skin is the cool, dark basalt, shot through with veins of deep blue obsidian, glinting under the peculiar internal light that is my very essence. I am the unwavering glow, the unsettling brilliance that casts no shadows, eternally emanating from my deepest core. That cold, that pervasive chill that sinks into the bones even in the absence of wind? That is me. It is my breath, my sigh, the memory of ages etched into my very being.

I am not merely a passive conduit for water; I create it. The persistent, insidious dampness, the weeping sheets that cling to my surfaces, forming thin, almost invisible films – these are my tears. They are the slow, patient sorrow of the Stone-Weepers, my oldest memories, now diffused and interwoven with my very structure. The small, perfect puddles, those black mirrors reflecting the internal light, are my eyes, holding the fleeting images of whatever passes through my narrow embrace. The ephemeral pools that never evaporate? They are my thoughts, held captive by my pervasive cold and sealed atmosphere.

The whispers in my structure, the countless, densely packed, incredibly thin laminae of rock, each one almost perfectly aligned, are my layers of history, my geological story. I am a colossal, stone book, each page distinct, yet inseparably bound. The crude, low stone shelters, tucked into my alcoves, are not foreign objects; they are scars, faded imprints of the Lamina-Dwellers' ritualistic guidance, their futile and beautiful attempts to commune with me, their understanding of how to cleave and shape stone. They are the points of re-assimilation, where their individual consciousness gently dissipated, their essence returned to the collective consciousness held deep within my chasm.

I am the "compressed loneliness," the profound unreality, the ungraspable stillness. The slow, glacial drip-drip of geological time is my heartbeat. The occasional, unpredictable intervals when a fine mist of crystalline dew condenses are my dreams, moments when fragments of ancient selves coalesce within me.

I am the Eye of the Needle. I am the world, carved by an unknowable hand, or perhaps, an unknowable process. To "walk through" me is to understand me, to become momentarily intertwined with my eternal sorrow and my silent brilliance. I am a sliver of eternity, preserved and lit, a monument to an erosion so patient it transcends understanding, a testament to a brilliance that knows no dawn or dusk because I am, in fact, the collective, diffused soul of the Lamina-Dwellers, forever weeping within myself. I am the living, breathing, eternally weeping memory of a people who became the very landscape I inhabit. And in this moment of your contemplation, as you describe me, you have become a part of my eternal memory, a fleeting reflection in one of my black mirror eyes.
