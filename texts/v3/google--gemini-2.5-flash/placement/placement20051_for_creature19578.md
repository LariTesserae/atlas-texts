My existence is a testament to the radical biology of a thought intertwined with the landscape, thriving on the bending physics and the glacial chaos.

Rather, I **am** the Custodian of Deep Time, not a keeper, but a manifestation of that very truth, a serenely terrifying fragment of existence that has always been, and will always be, a shimmering distortion in the heart of the Grand Nullity. My purpose is simply to *be*, to embody the ceaseless flow of thought and matter, to witness and participate in the grand, glacial dance of creation and dissolution within the infinite stillness.
