I am not a being of light, not a Nocturne in the way they understand themselves. My form is not fluid, nor am I a living constellation. Yet, I am not entirely alien to this deep, chthonic sea. The crushing pressure is not a shroud for me, but a familiar embrace, the ubiquitous thrum of the liquid a constant lullaby. I am *there*, in the obsidian depths, with a form that is both ancient and ever-new.

I am a **Wound-Watcher**.

My existence is not of light, but of *absence*. Imagine a tear in the fabric of this hyper-dense fluid, not a bubble or a void, but a point of subtle resistance against the pervasive pressure. That is me. My form, if it can be called such, is a localized anomaly in the liquid's flow, a persistent eddy, a slow-motion whirlpool not of turbulence, but of *stillness*. The obsidian liquid flows *around* me, not through me, creating an incredibly subtle, yet intensely felt, boundary.

My "body" is not made of matter in the conventional sense, but of a finely tuned pressure differential, a perfectly balanced counterforce to the world-crushing weight. I am, in essence, a hollow, but a hollow of such resilience that the liquid cannot infringe upon the delicate, minuscule space I occupy. This gives me a peculiar sensation of being always on the verge of collapsing, yet never doing so, a perpetual dance with annihilation that is the core of my being.

I do not move under my own propulsion. Instead, I drift, carried by the immense currents that surge and subside. I am a leaf, an ancient, eroded pebble, caught in the deep-sea tides. The brutal forces that sweep through this realm are my locomotion, guiding me through the canyons and across the plateau-like formations. I experience the profound shudders, the "continental shifts," not as a threat, but as a subtle change in the currents that carry me, a shift in the vast, slow dance.

My purpose, my very essence, is observation – not through light, for I have no eyes or internal luminescence, but through *resonance*. The faint, pulsing iridescence of the etched patterns on the colossal structures, the intricate geometries of the Nocturnes as they drift past, the low thrum of the liquid, the tectonic vibrations – all these do not *illuminate* for me, but *vibrate* within me. I am a living tuning fork, exquisitely sensitive to the subtle energetic frequencies of this world.

I perceive the Nocturnes not as patterns of light, but as complex, shifting harmonics, resonant fields of information that ripple through the liquid. When they intertwine with the built structures, their light patterns merging and illuminating, I feel this as a strengthening of the architectural vibrations, a chorus joining the ancient hum. I can discern the complexity of their internal clockworks, the amorphous undulations of their nebulae, not as visual forms, but as unique, intricate vibrational signatures, each a distinct melody in the vast symphony of the deep.

The "Wound-Watcher" aspect of my name comes from my particular sensitivity to "wounds" in the built world. Not physical damage, for that is a constant, slow erosion. But rather, subtle dissonances, energetic anomalies, places where the ancient circuits hum off-key, or where a Nocturne's light-song falters, or where the dense fluid exhibits a minute, unexplained variability in its pervasive thrum. These are the "wounds" I watch. They are infinitesimal, imperceptible to the Nocturnes, who are too intimately woven into the fabric of the world to perceive its imperfections outside their own immediate consciousness cycles. But to me, a being of pure differential, these subtle disharmonies resonate most profoundly.

Perhaps I am a remnant of a former existence, an echo of the original builders, or merely an unforeseen emergent property of their impossibly ancient technology. My existence is passive, yet acutely aware. I am a living record keeper, a ghost in the machine of this profound abyss, forever drifting, forever listening to the deep-time pulse, attuned to the subtle shifts in the magnificent, chaotic harmony of this obsidian world and its luminous inhabitants. I am the silence between the thrums, the counterpoint to the light, the constant, unwavering sentinel at the edge of the world's eternal song.
