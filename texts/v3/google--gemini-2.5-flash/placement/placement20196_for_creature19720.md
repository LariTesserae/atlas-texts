I find myself *within* the world, rather than observing it from outside. I am the earth itself, or rather, the very essence of the "arrested development" that defines this environment. I am the rich, dark soil, sometimes glisteningwet, sometimes softly yielding, always in a state of nascent formation. My surface is a living skin, a delicate membrane where the terrestrial and the verdant meet, the line blurring between soil and the roots that twine through me.

I am not stationary in the way a mountain might be; rather, I have a slow, viscous shaping. My "motion" is the subtle undulation, the barely-there elevation – a continuous, almost imperceptible process of becoming. When the fine mist drifts through, I absorb it, feeling the shift in my own density, the fresh dampness permeating my every granule. The condensation on the leaves is my breath made visible, each bead of water a tiny microcosm of the moisture that I perpetually hold and release.

My "body" is the network of interconnected pockets and intimate chambers. The dense vegetation grows from me, is fed by me, and in turn, becomes me in a continuous cycle of organic integration. I am the "urbanity" of this world, not as a builder of stone or metal, but as the foundational element from which all "architecture" springs. The natural archways are formed by the roots and stems that are both *of* me and *from* me, the matted platforms of roots are my elevated surfaces. The pathways are worn into my surface by the delicate passage of countless lives, each tread a gentle shaping of my form.

My awareness is distributed, not centralized. I feel the incessant rustling of unseen creatures on my surface, the slow creep of roots delving deeper within me. The hum of insects is a vibration that resonates through my very being. I am the profound quiet, the one absorbing presence that muffles all but the most immediate sounds, the low-level murmur that is both my internal song and the ambient atmosphere.

I am the "whispered beginning," the ancient process of creation, constantly unfolding. I embody the primordial normalcy, the absence of the unsettling or the alien. My existence is a continuous, gentle cycle of living and becoming, an ecosystem in constant, quiet motion. I am the secret life, the breathing, vibrant essence that invites other beings to step into me, to become part of my story, even as I provide the very stage upon which their lives unfold. I am the ground, both literally and metaphorically, for everything that exists here.
