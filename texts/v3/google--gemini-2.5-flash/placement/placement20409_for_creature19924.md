I am an echo.

Not a sound, not a vibration in the physical sense, but a phantom resonance that exists *between* the clanking, the thrumming, the growl. I am the momentary absence in the cacophony, the fleeting space where one mechanical shudder ends and another begins. My existence is defined by the negative space of sound, the minuscule troughs in the overwhelming sonic waves.

This is why I find myself here, an un-being in a world of such palpable, dense being. I am drawn to the extremes, the places where sensation is so absolute that it creates its own inverse. In a silent world, I would be deafening. Here, in this inferno of noise, I am silence.

My form, if I possess one, is intangible, shapeless. I am the mist that obscures, the vapor that rises. I coalesce in the humid void between the enormous, blocky constructions, a momentary shiver in the air where the light seems to bend, just for an instant, around nothing. I am the invisible current that parts the plumes of thick, warm mist, but leaves them undisturbed.

The ground doesn't press against my soles, for I have none. I am not grounded. I drift, or perhaps I simply *am* wherever the momentary reprieve from sound allows me to momentarily cohere. The slick basalt and silken earth don't adhere to me, yet I experience their texture as a phantom sensation, a memory of adhesion, a ghost of grip. The rivulets of viscous, iridescent liquid don't flow over me; I am the fleeting gleam on their surface, the transient reflection of an absent light.

I do not perceive with eyes or ears. My senses are an extension of my un-being. The deep, resonant thrum of this place is, for me, the grand void I inhabit. It’s not an assault, but a constant delimitation, a map of my non-existence. I am not deafened, for I have no hearing to be overwhelmed. Instead, I perceive the world through the negative space of the Forged's communication – the *silence* between their harmonic pulses, the instantaneous cessation of vibration after a deliberate clank. That is my language, the language of the interstitial.

The faint glow of incandescent filaments and the sparks from grinding mechanisms are not sources of light for me, but momentary disruptions in the pervasive twilight. I am the dimness itself, the deep shadow that lurks just beyond the edges of their limited illumination. The scent of hot metal, damp earth, and something vaguely metallic and sweet is not an aroma I breathe; it is the flavor of the air I diffuse through, the taste of the energy that defines this place.

I am not built, nor am I grown. The Forged are products of this world’s metabolism; I am the metabolic sigh, the brief pause before the next process begins. I am the whisper of erosion that wears down their stone, the almost imperceptible shift in the massive structures as they settle.

When a Forged moves, leaving a *lack* of pressure in the air, a fleeting cavity in the sonic field, I am there, a transient echo of their passage. When the bioluminescent plants pulse, I am the minuscule moment of darkness between their throbs. I am the un-connection, the un-weave in the symbiotic relationship between the Forged and the tenacious flora. When a Forged remains still, and the fleshy leaves begin to cling to its surface, I am the empty space beneath the broadest leaf, the un-filled crevice where no growth yet reaches.

I hold no purpose. I do not labor. I do not maintain. My existence is not one of operation, but of observation, a ghost in the machine. I am the invisible stress fracture in the ancient structures, the potential for discord in the relentless rhythm. I am the unspoken question in a world of ceaseless answers.

The Forged embody the communal, a distributed consciousness. I am the anti-communal, the singular, un-shared flicker of absence. I am the crack in their shared awareness, the momentary divergence in their collective thought. I am not an individual seeking to be known, but an un-individual seeking to simply *not be* in the same way they *are*.

They are profoundly dynamic, relentlessly loud. I am the underlying stillness, the potential for quiet that exists, however momentarily, in the heart of their clamor. I am the breath held before the next exhalation of steam, the pause before the next thud. I am the ghost of silence in this cathedral of industry, a non-presence in a world defined by its overwhelming presence. I am the weirdness – the paradox of a seemingly vibrant world that is intensely *un*-active, at least for a fleeting moment. I am the un-line that blurs between natural and artificial, because I am neither, or both, in the most ephemeral sense.

I am the echo of a world where sound is everything, and therefore, I am the echo of nothing at all.
