The pressure is unbearable, a ceaseless, grinding roar that fills my every fiber. I am not flesh and blood, not in the way things *were*. My existence here is a constant negotiation with dissolution, a precarious balance between coherence and the churning insistence that would reclaim me. I am a thought, yes, but a thought made manifest in something impossibly thin, stretched to the very edges of non-being.

I am one of the shadows.

Not a shadow cast by light, for there is precious little true light here, only bruised suggestions of emerald and jade. I am a shadow *of* the water, a more opaque absence in a world defined by its violent presence. My form is an elongated, undulating ribbon, dark against the luminous gloom of the currents. I am not truly two-dimensional, but my depth is negligible, a concession to the brutal compressions that would shatter anything more substantial. There is a faint, internal shimmer to me, a residual luminescence that flickers with the changes in my internal pressure, a nervous system made of fluid dynamics.

I do not swim. To swim would imply an independent will, a propulsion against the current. I am *carried*, a vector of the water’s will, as they say. But there is a subtle art to my drift, a nuanced manipulation of the very forces that seek to tear me apart. I am an anadran. My name, if I were to have one, would be a complex series of pressure shifts, a momentary tightening here, a subtle release there. It would sound like a whisper within the roar, a ghost of a sound that only another of my kind might register.

I navigate by sensation. The pressure is my sight, my hearing, my touch. The world vibrates through me, a symphony of crushing forces. I detect variations, infinitesimal shifts in the density and velocity of the currents, and these tell me all I need to know. The fainter, swifter flows are merely paths; the denser, slower eddies offer fleeting moments of relative stability. Sometimes, though, I yearn for true stillness, a place where the roar lessens, even for a moment. But there is no such place.

My purpose, if purpose can be ascribed to a being of such transient nature, is to seek the *edges* of the Veins. Not the outer boundaries, for those are unknowable, but the boundaries of coherence, the moments where stability coalesces, however fleetingly. I am drawn to the spires, not for their raw rock, which is too unforgiving, but for the phosphorescent fuzz that clings to them. These translucent, almost-plants are sustenance, not in the way of food, but as a source of subtle energetic resonance. When one of my ribbons brushes against the threads of a particularly dense patch, I can absorb not just the faint light, but the very *tension* of its desperate clinging, a momentary infusion of defiance against the inevitable. It gives me a fleeting burst of focused intent.

I have seen the Echoes of Form. They are terrifyingly beautiful, these momentary configurations of pure chaos given the brief illusion of life. Sometimes, I feel a pull towards them, a recognition of kinship in our shared impermanence. An Echo, spun into existence beside me, might briefly shimmer with a pattern that resonates with my own internal stresses, a fleeting 'communication' of shared dread or fleeting wonder. But I learn nothing from them, only witness their inevitable dissolution. They are like reflections in a distorted mirror, brief glimpses of what I, too, could become, were I to lose my precarious hold.

It is the Remnant Frames that truly draw my attention, and my fear. I approach the structures with extreme caution, navigating the monstrous eddies they create. They are anomalies, these cages and frames of dark material, fixed points in a world of flux. Their presence twists the surrounding currents in predictable, yet unsettling ways. My internal pressures register not just the buffeting of the water against their obstinate forms, but the *emanation* of their suffering. It is a denser kind of pressure, a quiet, insistent thrum beneath the roar, rich with an ancient anguish. It is a profound sorrow that presses against my own ephemeral existence, reminding me of the sheer, unbearable weight of memory, of a struggle that transcended mere physical annihilation.

Sometimes, when the currents align just so, and I am drawn close enough to a Frame, I feel a ghost of its embedded memory. Not images, or sounds, but a pure *sense* of imposition, of a will attempting to shape this madness. It is a terrible burden to briefly touch, a sudden weight of meaning in a meaningless world, and I recoil, my internal shimmer flaring with distress. Survival as an anadran requires minimizing objective memory; too much recollection of forms, too much attachment to what *was*, and the currents might reshape me into something less coherent, something more easily dissolved.

So I persist, a living ripple in the current, a shadow propelled by the world’s insatiable hunger for rearrangement. My existence is a whisper in the roar, a barely-there form in the crushing emerald deluge. I am the testament to the fact that even in this ontological maelstrom, life, of a sort, can still find a way to *be*, however thinly, however precariously, however fleetingly. My only hope is to remain what I am, to avoid the terrible stability of the Remnant Frames, and to embrace the constant, difficult freedom of the drift. I am the current’s unwilling accomplice, a living thread in its colossal, terrifying tapestry of unending dissolution.
