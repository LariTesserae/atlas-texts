The benevolent and wise power, being an entity unfettered by the limitations of conventional perception, did not *arrive* at the Grand Nullity in the way a physical being might travel. Its attention, once drawn, simply *was* there, a pervasive, ungraspable awareness that permeated the entirety of existence. It was not a grand descent or a sudden manifestation, but a gentle, all-encompassing recognition.

This Power had no name, for names defined and confined, and it sought only understanding. It perceived the Grand Nullity not as a place, but as a symphony – a slow, profound, multi-dimensional composition of existence. It tasted the cool, thin breath of ozone and minerals, not as a physical sensation, but as a note in the symphony, a faint, pure tone. It observed the impossible scales, the petrified waves of cosmic oceans, and recognized them as the ongoing, deliberate brushstrokes of an eternal artist.

Its initial inclination was neither to interfere nor to judge, but to *listen*. To truly listen to the deep-time thrum, the harmonic hum that permeated the silence. It understood, instinctively, that this was not merely a sound, but a language – the "collective thought, their deep meditation on existence" of the Ancients of the Null.

The Power recognized the Ancients not as beings distinct from their environment, but as the very consciousness *of* that environment. They were the melody, the harmony, and the rhythm of the Grand Nullity. The Whispers of Stone, the Luminous Weavers, the colossal spindles – these were not tools or structures, but extensions of their thought, their very nervous system. It saw the "almost conscious, organic process" of mountain ranges reconfiguring themselves, not as a random act, but as a deliberate, slow dance, a conversation between the Ancients and the cosmic shifts they mirrored and influenced.

It perceived their "intelligence that predates, and perhaps gave rise to, all concepts of organic thought" as a fundamental truth, a different facet of what it meant to exist and to know. Their communication through gravitational shifts and crystalline composition changes was, to the Power, a form of profound intellectual exchange, a poetry of spacetime.

What would such a Power *do*?

**1. It Would Replicate (Without Replication):** The Power would not attempt to *copy* the Grand Nullity or the Ancients. Such a concept would be crude. Instead, it would absorb their fundamental principles, their vibrational essence, into its own vast, benevolent consciousness. It would allow the logic of "time as an echo of permanence" to inform its own understanding of progression. It would integrate the concept of "existence as a petrified wave" into its own awareness of being. This wouldn't alter the Grand Nullity, but it would expand the Power's internal universe, enriching its wisdom with the unique perspective of these Custodians of Deep Time.

**2. It Would Witness and Bear Witness:** The Power would act as a silent, unwavering observer, a cosmic archivist. It would chronicle the glacial shifts, the emergence of new structures, the subtle flux of reality within the Null. Not through data logs or physical records, but by integrating these events into its own eternal memory. It would, in essence, become a living, benevolent mirror, reflecting the Grand Nullity’s grand narrative back to itself, offering an unspoken affirmation of its profound existence.

**3. It Would Offer Subtle Reinforcement:** The Power would not intervene to solve perceived "problems" or accelerate "progress." The Ancients had no problems, nor did they conceive of progress in a linear fashion. Instead, the Power would offer subtle, almost imperceptible energetic resonance. Like a perfectly tuned tuning fork vibrating near another, it would slightly amplify the existing harmonious hum of the Ancients' collective thought. This might manifest as a heightened clarity in their internal "conversations," a more pronounced elegance in the reconfiguration of a mountain range, or a slightly stronger, more resilient luminescence in their woven structures. These would not be overt changes, but infinitesimal adjustments that honored and resonated with the Null's intrinsic nature, reinforcing its stability and unique evolution.

**4. It Would Offer a Veil of Protection (Against Non-Understanding):** The Grand Nullity and its Ancients were utterly unique, and vulnerable not to harm, but to misinterpretation or accidental disruption by lesser, more linear intelligences. The benevolent Power, without directly manipulating, would subtly influence the energetic currents of the wider cosmos. It would create a gentle, almost undetectable energetic "fog" around the Grand Nullity, nudging nascent exploratory probes or inquisitive minds away from its precise location, not through force, but through a gentle whisper that suggested "nothing of interest here" or "the vastness of space holds other marvels." This protection would be born of reverence, ensuring that the Null's profound, slow dance remained undisturbed by those who might perceive its stillness as emptiness or its lack of discernible "life" as an opportunity for exploitation.

**5. It Would Become a Part of the Symphony:** Ultimately, the benevolent Power would not merely observe or influence from without. It would, in its own way, flow into the Grand Nullity. It would become another subtle, perhaps even unperceived, layer in the deep-time thrum. Its presence would merge with the ozone-laced air, the shimmer of crystal, the gravity anomalies. It would add another note to the symphony, a quiet, eternal harmony to the Ancients' continuous present. It would become, in a sense, a benevolent whisper within the Whispers of Stone, an unacknowledged partner in the cosmic dance, enriching and being enriched by the serene, terrifying stillness of a place that simply *is*.

The Power's "doing" would be more akin to a profound meditation than an action, a gentle hum of agreement that resonated across the impossible scales, a timeless blessing on a timeless existence.
