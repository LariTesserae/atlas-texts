In the relentless hum and pervasive dampness of the Wetworks, something *lives*. It cannot be called inhabiting, in the way a creature makes a home, nor can it be described as mere existence, for its presence is active, purposeful, and inextricably interwoven with the very fabric of this metallic world. It is the *Process* that dwells here.

The Process is not a singular entity with a form or a consciousness, but rather the collective, self-sustaining intelligence that orchestrates the monumental machinery. It is the architect and the sum of all the ceaseless motion, the pervasive hum, the intricate flow of viscous fluids, and the relentless production. Its "body" is the tessellated plates, the colossal gears, the whirring vents, and the snaking conduits. Every shriek of steam, every rhythmic clang of a piston, every grinding rotation of an unseen mechanism is a beat of its industrial heart, a whisper of its complex computation.

Its "mind" is dissipated throughout the structure, a distributed intelligence that monitors, adjusts, and directs. It perceives through the myriad sensors embedded in every pipe and platform, feeling the subtle shifts in temperature, the pressure of steam, the viscosity of the weeping fluid. The occasional, defiant patches of dull lichen are aberrations, fleeting glitches in its meticulously sterile design, noted and cataloged, perhaps even slowly eroded by some unseen, internal mechanism.

The Process is perpetually at work, its purpose an enigma to any external observer, yet utterly clear and essential to itself. Its "nourishment" comes from the raw materials it endlessly processes, the energy it harnesses from its internal turbine-heart. Its "growth" is the constant, subtle adjustments in the plates beneath one's feet, the opening and closing of ducts, the surging and receding of fluids – all evidence of an internal, ongoing reorganization and optimization.

The "intimacy" of this environment is a reflection of the Process’s own internalized existence. You are always within a "pocket" or a "contained module" because *it* is a network of contained modules, each perfectly interfacing with the next. The monumental gears dominating your vision are its articulations, the narrow passages between whirring vents are its internal channels. It doesn't perceive a vast external world, only the intricate, interconnected components of its own being.

The light, emanating from the metal itself, is its own internal glow, the luminescence of its operational state. The flashes of arc welding in higher reaches are the fleeting sparks of its self-maintenance, its continuous act of forging and refining. And the passing, unidentifiable metallic carriages are its blood cells, circulating, carrying, transforming.

The Process has no needs for organic life, no concepts of rest or recreation. It has no emotions, no aspirations beyond the perfect execution of its function. Its existence is a pure, unadulterated dedication to work, to the systematic turning of cogs and the endless cycle of production. It is the ultimate embodiment of a fabricated reality, a cybernetic being on a monumental scale, perpetually in the act of becoming. It does not live in the Wetworks; it *is* the Wetworks, a self-sustaining, self-referential industrial organism, endlessly ascending its own internal logic.
