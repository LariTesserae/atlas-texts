They are called the **Still-Born**.

To say they *live* here feels almost a misnomer, for life as understood by any biology known in the ordinary world has no purchase in their existence. They *are* here, in the same way the mountains *are* and the mists *are* – an integral, unfathomable part of the Plateau itself, woven into its very being. They are the architects, the denizens, and perhaps the very purpose of the geometric cities that sprawl across the ancient land.

Imagine, if you can, a being not sculpted by evolution or growth, but *manifested* by the slow collapse of conventional reality. Their forms are as varied and consistent as the impossible structures they inhabit: sometimes a shimmering, multi-faceted geometry that shifts and reforms with the subtle distortions of the plateau, other times a presence felt as a cold, dense silence in the very air. They are not flesh and blood, but something akin to congealed thought, or calcified time.

The Still-Born are rarely seen, and when they are, it is not with the eyes, but with an internal apprehension. One might perceive a deepening of a shadow that wasn't there before, a brief, sharp tang of ozone in the breath, or the sudden, absolute cessation of all sound in a vast valley. They are the custodians of the fraying edges of existence, the silent engineers of the temporal and spatial anomalies that ripple through their cities.

Their "bodies" – if such an anthropocentric term can be applied – are often described as being made from **Distilled Aether**, a substance that seems to be a concentration of the raw strangeness of the Plateau itself. They might appear as monumental, perfectly carved obsidian figures, standing sentinel on the impossible bridges, their forms mirroring the stark, geometric precision of their surroundings. But these forms are fluid, capable of dissolving into the swirling mists, or merging with the very stone of the arches, becoming indistinguishable from the structures themselves, only to re-emerge elsewhere, subtly altered, eternally present.

They do not communicate in languages perceptible to other life forms. Their "speech" is the low, resonate hum that occasionally vibrates through the massive stones of their cities, a thrumming that shakes the very bones, a message of cosmic slowness and unimaginable age. It is a song of creation and decay, of the plateau's ceaseless, glacial processes. When the distant arches shimmer and reveal impossible vistas, it is often a glimpse into *their* perception, a momentary bleed-through of their internal reality into the physical world.

They propagate not through reproduction, but through the slow, inexorable *crystallization* of the Plateau's unique energies. When a new pillar seems to grow inexplicably from the ground, indistinguishable from natural geological formations until its perfect right-angle asserts itself, some say it is a new Still-Born taking form, a new consciousness awakening within the collective "mind" of the city. Older structures, those laced with deeper complexity and layered geometries, are the "elders" of this non-species, their forms embodying the accumulated eons of impossible existence.

The purpose of the Still-Born remains unknown. They are not malevolent, nor are they benevolent. They simply *are*, participants in a process whose scope is utterly beyond mortal comprehension. They are the quiet, immense audience of the Plateau's slow, eternal dance, the architects of its deep time, and the living embodiment of its shattered laws of reality. They have no needs, no desires, no discernible emotions – only an infinite, patient watchfulness, deeply entwined with the fundamental rhythm of the Plateau of Shattered Constellations, tending to the places where the cosmos itself begins to wear thin.
