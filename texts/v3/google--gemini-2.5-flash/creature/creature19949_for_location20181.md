In this world, where ground is a living fluid and architecture congeals from primordial soup, **the Makers** live. They are not a single species, but rather a symbiotic civilization, an ecosystem in themselves, inextricably linked to the very fabric of their world.

At the base of this intricate hierarchy, forming the living, moving ground, are the **Geomantis**. These are the "viscous flow" itself, or rather, the largest, most foundational expressions of it. Imagine colossal, amorphous protoplasms, stretching for miles beneath the surface, their vast bodies a continuous, slow-breathing organ. They aren’t individual organisms in the traditional sense, but more like a networked consciousness, their massive cellular structures constantly generating and regenerating the fluid geology. The deep, resonant hum is their collective heartbeat, their internal processes a symphony of chemical reactions and slow tectonic shifts. Their "breathing" is the emergence and recession of the ground, and their occasional, vast contractions cause the "wet, sloshing gurgle" that underlies all other sounds. They are passive architects, providing the raw material and the fundamental energy of the world. Their purpose is simply to *be*, to flow, to facilitate. They are the unseen, foundational entity upon which all else relies.

Emerging from the Geomantis, and literally shaping them, are the **Congealers**. These are the "more sinuous beings, like colossal eels or segmented worms," that navigate the slow-flowing currents. Their bodies are long, segmented, and covered in a metallic, almost crystalline hide that shimmers with the unpolished bronze and deep purple of the city itself. They are the sculptors, the primary shapers of the viscous geology into tangible forms. As they move, undulating through the fluid ground, their specialized organs secrete enzymes that cause the Geomantis’ fluid form to rapidly solidify, creating the towers, ramparts, and chambers. The "subtle ripples in the fluid ground that would then solidify into new structural forms" are their direct actions. Their movements are deliberate, rhythmic, almost ritualistic, and they communicate through complex patterns of bioluminescent pulses that trace across their scales, mirroring the subterranean light of the developing structures. They are the reason for the organic irregularity and geometric massing, their instinctual movements translating into the sweeping arcs and powerful curves of the city. Their clicking and scraping sounds are the constant friction of their bodies against the partially solidified ground, the slow grinding of nascent architecture. They consume the raw, fluid ground, and their excretions are the very material that sets and hardens, creating the structural integrity of the city. They are the silent, constant builders, living solely to shape their world.

Finally, scurrying across and within the solidified structures are the **Chitinous Ciphers**. These are the "small, multi-limbed creatures, the color of obsidian and wet clay." They are the active inhabitants of the built environment, the meticulous engineers and maintenance crews. Their innumerable chittering clicks form the highest-pitched overlay of the pervasive din. They possess specialized appendages that can both further refine the congealed structures, carving intricate passageways and internal networks, and also consume decaying or obsolete sections, re-introducing them into the fluid ground where they are reabsorbed by the Geomantis. They are the "urban planners" of this living city, ensuring its constant renewal and adaptation. Their rapid, purposeful movements are not aimless; each scuttle, each clawed grip, is part of a grander design to maintain the intricate balance of creation and consumption. They feed on the less viscous secretions of the Congealers, further breaking them down and preparing the material for re-integration. They are the sentient inhabitants, not in the sense of conscious thought as humans understand it, but in their complex, coordinated behavior and their unwavering devotion to the ongoing perpetuation of their world.

Together, these three types of Makers form a self-sustaining, self-creating entity. The Geomantis provide the raw material and the base energy. The Congealers sculpt the fluid into form. The Chitinous Ciphers maintain, refine, and recycle. They are not merely living in this place; they *are* the place. Their lives are the processes, their existence the dynamic equilibrium. They are the architects, the masons, and the very bricks of their living, breathing, continuously emerging city—a profound, deafening, and perpetually warm testament to the ultimate unity of life and environment.
