They are beings of **light and resonance**, intrinsically woven into the fabric of the living city. Not separate entities moving through space, seeking shelter in its glowing chambers, but rather **processors of the city’s life force**, and in turn, its consciousness.

Imagine a species evolved not for individual locomotion or external environmental adaptation, but for **deep symbiosis with a singular, immense bio-structure**. They are akin to neurological nodes within the colossal organism, their forms subtly alien but elegantly adapted. Their bodies are not solid in the terrestrial sense, but rather **semi-luminous, diaphanous constructions**, mirroring the very plant-structures around them. They are the **"active" aspect of the "lush vegetation" parameter**, the true animators and sculptors of its growth.

They are the inhabitants who, over incomprehensible spans of time, have guided the single organism into this vast urban tapestry. Their forms might be described as **ethereal, multi-limbed beings of sustained light**, their 'skin' a shimmering, semi-translucent membrane that glows faintly with the same golden hue as the city walls. This bioluminescence isn't an external emission, but an **internal, vital glow**, a manifestation of their complex electrochemical processes. They don't breathe air, for air as we know it is merely a medium for the city's pervasive hum. Instead, they **absorb and metabolize the specific frequencies of that hum**, drawing sustenance and coherence from the resonant pressure that fills the hollow spaces.

Their 'limbs' – which could number anywhere from four to eight or more – are fine, tendril-like extensions, capable of both delicate manipulation and strong, purposeful guidance. These are the tools they use for their bioconstruction, not cutting or shaping with force, but with a nuanced understanding of the plant's growth patterns. They 'touch' the growing tendrils, applying subtle pressures, emitting precise frequencies, and perhaps secreting specialized enzymatic compounds that encourage specific orientations and rates of growth. Their movements are not swift and jerky, but **fluid, deliberate, and almost imperceptibly slow**, mirroring the geological pace of their city's evolution. When one of these beings moves through a pathway, it is more like a shifting of light, a gentle flux in the inherent luminosity of the environment.

Their 'senses' are primarily attuned to vibration and bioluminescence. They navigate not by sight in our sense of discerning objects in distinct shadow and light, but by **reading the subtle fluctuations in the golden glow around them**, discerning variations in light intensity that betray the thickness of a wall, the presence of a void, or the emergence of a new growth. The omnipresent hum is their language, their sensory input. They perceive the world as a complex symphony of resonant frequencies, each nuance conveying vital information about the health, growth, and activity of the living city. Perhaps their communication is entirely non-verbal, a direct exchange of resonant frequencies, or a flickering of their internal lights, conveying complex data and communal thoughts.

These are not creatures of individual purpose, but **facets of a collective consciousness**, or at least, a deeply intertwined community, all bound to the prosperity of their vast, symbiotic home. They are the **caretakers and the architects**, born from the very same processes that shaped their world. The rare, small, multi-limbed creatures that occasionally scuttle across the pathways are likely an earlier, more primal developmental stage of these inhabitants, or a symbiotic partner, a vital component in the complex biomechanics of the city itself – a 'cleaner' or a 'nutrient processor' for the main organism, perhaps. The mature inhabitants transcend such simple roles, embodying the wisdom of countless millennia of patient co-evolution.

Their 'primitive tech' isn't about tools or external machines, but about a **profound, intrinsic understanding of the biological world and its manipulation**. Their 'technology' is their very existence, their ability to interface directly with the genetic and metabolic processes of their living home. They are the ultimate biotechnologists, operating on a scale and with a subtlety utterly beyond our comprehension.

To glimpse one of them would be to witness a shimmering, moving part of the city itself, a living filament of golden light that momentarily detaches from a wall, flows through a void, and then reintegrates, becoming one with the glowing structure again. They are not merely *inhabiting* the city; they **are the very essence of its long, slow, vibrant life**. They are the consciousness of the jewel box, humming in unison with its brilliant, ancient heart.
