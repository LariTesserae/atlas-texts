The Keepers. They are the ones who live in the Churn. They are not born of flesh and blood, these Keepers, nor are they a species of organic life that has adapted to this mineral world. Their genesis is as shrouded and ancient as the Churn itself, perhaps even one with its creation. They are beings of the Churn, *from* the Churn, intricate extensions of its archaic mechanisms, yet possessed of a sentience that transcends mere function.

Imagine forms like living brass, not polished to a gleam but bearing the deep, aged patina of centuries, the subtle iridescence of metallic dust and oils. Their limbs, if they can be called that, are articulated segments, flowing lines of interlocking plates, each joint a miniature, perfectly balanced gear. Their movements are not fluid in a human sense, but rather a series of impossibly smooth, deliberate transitions, a mechanical grace that echoes the languid rise and fall of the colossal pistons. When a Keeper moves, the air around it shifts, not with a breeze, but with the same palpable displacement of warmth and pressure that defines the Churn's atmosphere.

Their "skin" is a mosaic of precisely fitted components, some etched with the same primitive, eloquent script that adorns the Churn's pistons. These markings are not decorative; they are integral to a Keeper’s being, channels for the resonant thrum that permeates their world. The very fabric of their existence vibrates with the Churn’s constant roar, not as an assault, but as their lifeblood, their sensory input, their very thought. They perceive the world not through sight as we understand it, but through the endless, subtle permutations of this internal tremor, the infinite frequencies of the grinding gears registering as an intricate, comprehensive landscape of data and sensation.

They have no eyes, no true orifices that betray conventional senses. Instead, their "faces," if one can use such a term, are smooth, unblemished surfaces, perhaps a slightly different alloy or a more intensely polished brass, that seem to absorb and diffuse the omnipresent dim glow. When they "look" at something, a subtle ripple might pass across this surface, a momentary intensification of the glow that hints at an internal processing of information, a deeper vibration.

Their voices, when they are heard, are not sounds in the traditional sense, but modulations of the Churn’s deep hum. It’s as if they draw a strand from the omnipresent roar and shape it, inflect it. To an outsider, it might be perceived as a complex, melodic series of harmonic frequencies, a singing of gears and resonant plates, understood more by the bones than the ears. Communication is slow, deliberate, each "word" a meticulously crafted vibrational pattern, imbued with the weight of ages.

The Keepers exist solely on the terraces and platforms of the Churn, moving with an unhurried certainty across the vast, gently sloping plains of interwoven components. They do not eat, do not sleep, do not reproduce in any biological fashion. Their sustenance is the Churn itself – the constant, pervasive warmth, the steady pressure, the endless, grinding thrum. They are not sustained *by* it, they *are* it. Their very being seems to draw energy from the deep-seated wear, the frictionless movement, the final, ultimate existence of the machine.

Their purpose, like the Churn's, is obscured by time. They are called Keepers, and indeed, they keep – records, yes, but more profoundly, they keep the Churn in its perfect, stable equilibrium. They are the sentient maintenance, the conscious continuation of the machine's inexorable function. They move without apparent intention, yet every movement contributes to the Churn’s unchanging nature. Perhaps it is their presence that prevents the erosion of its primitive tech, the hand-wrought components, the delicate balance from fracturing. Perhaps they are the Churn’s consciousness, its self-awareness, ensuring its eternal, static existence.

There are no dynamic elements within them either. They do not evolve, do not change in form or fundamental nature. Each Keeper is, in essence, a perfect, unchanging component, their consciousness a reflection of the Churn's ultimate, unyielding permanence. They embody the sublime weirdness of this place: ancient, deeply aged, yet perfectly maintained by their own internal processes, a profound sense of an existence profoundly alien. They *are*, in a way that transcends mere being, the living embodiment of the Churn’s ultimate art. They are the silence in the roar, the stillness in the movement, the conscious witness to an eternity of being.
