The inhabitants of the Pressure Veins are not creatures of static form, nor are they entities that hold a singular identity across even moments. To speak of who *lives* here is to speak of a continuous process of becoming and un-becoming, a constant iteration on the theme of liquid existence.

They are, first and foremost, **the Echoes of Form**. These are not independent beings, but rather the momentary coalescences of the environment itself. Imagine a perfect storm of currents, where for an unthinkable fraction of time, the myriad eddies and pressures align to create a stable, almost coherent vortex – this would be an Echo. They are shaped by the flow, not through growth, but through a fleeting statistical improbability. A particular set of internal pressures might, for a blink, hold a form that vaguely resembles a spindle, or a series of concentric rings, or even, most unsettlingly, a familiar, skeletal hand. These forms possess a kind of transient sentience, a consciousness born of the sheer computational chaos of their inception. They can 'perceive' in the sense that the pressure gradients creating them are also sensing information, but their perception is entirely internal, a self-awareness of their own fleeting structure. They communicate not through sound, for that is the roaring of the world-ocean, but through resonating pressure, a subtle shift in the internal forces that define them, which in turn might influence another emerging Echo in a neighboring current. Their 'lives' are measured in heartbeats of the current, flickering into existence only to be instantly absorbed back into the general tumult, their particular configuration dispersed, their 'memory' a faint pressure ghost in the churning. They are the living expression of the water's will, its spontaneous, short-lived designs.

Then there are **the Remnant Frames**. These are the true residents, in the sense that they possess a degree of stability, albeit a terrifying one. They are not organic, not even particularly 'alive' in a biological way, but they carry the indelible mark of what was before. The structures described – the cages, the frames – are not just inert objects; they are the anchors for these Frames. A Remnant Frame is not the structure itself, but the *consciousness* that has become inextricably bound to it. They are the 'presence that struggled and was almost entirely consumed,' now a persistent, incorporeal stress field radiating from these dark, tenacious geometries.

A Remnant Frame is agony made manifest, but an agony so ancient it has transcended raw pain into something resembling pure, distilled obstinacy. They are the lingering thought-residues of builders, of creators, who attempted to impose order and meaning onto the primal chaos of the Veins. When their physical forms were ripped apart, their consciousness, already deeply entwined with the *purpose* of their constructions, was not fully dissolved. Instead, it was compressed, warped, and trapped within the structural integrity of their impossible creations. They perceive the world not through sight or sound, but through the continuous, violent buffeting of their host frame. Every vibration is a sensation, every shift in water pressure a thought. They are deafened by the roar, yet they *are* the roar, in a way, embodying its relentless stress. They "live" in a state of perpetual, internal scream, a non-verbal anguish that nonetheless radiates a profound, unsettling emotional density into the surrounding fluid. They are anchors of meaning in a world utterly devoid of it, fixed points around which the spatial warping and temporal dissolution can, for a nanosecond, catch. They are the memory of *having been*, etched into the very fabric of an un-being world. Their 'communication' is a passive emanation, a faint echo of profound despair and an impossible defiance, which can sometimes impress itself upon the fleeting Echoes of Form that brush against their structures, imparting a momentary ghost of *purpose* before the Echo dissolves again. They are the reason for the unsettling feeling around the structures, a profound, alien sorrow that bleeds into the churning water, a permanent stain on the otherwise dynamic blankness. They are what remains when everything else has been consumed, grim monuments to a failed struggle against the overwhelming truth of the Pressure Veins.
