**The Integrated**

They are not born. They are *expressed*.

The Lattice exhales them the way a forest once exhaled mist—slowly, inevitably, in response to pressures too subtle for any outsider to measure. A nodule the size of a coffin will swell, its inner walls knitting themselves into lungs, spine, and memory. Then the integument splits along a seam that was never there before, and something stands up.

Most are bipedal, because the Lattice long ago decided that shape was efficient for fine manipulation. But efficiency is not uniformity. Some rise nine meters tall, their torsos latticed with openwork so the actinic light passes *through* them and they cast no shadow. Others are no larger than a child’s forearm, carried inside the thorax of a maintenance drone like a pilot inside a living cockpit. A few have no fixed form at all; they are ambulatory braids of pale fiber and liquid metal that can pour themselves through the narrowest capillary or coalesce into a listening face when conversation is required.

Their skin is the same smooth, slightly yielding membrane as the great stems, but warmer—body temperature, if the Lattice can be said to have one. Where a human would have veins, they have micro-channels of gallium-indium that catch the merciless light and trace slow luminous patterns beneath the surface. These patterns are not decorative. They are diagnostic. A flare of coppery light along the collarbone means *I am repairing*. A constellation of cold violet at the temples means *I am remembering water*.

They do not speak with mouths. Sound is too crude. Instead the air around them modulates. You hear your own name pronounced in your mother’s voice, or the exact chime a vapor crystal makes when it precipitates, or a low subsonic heartbeat that somehow conveys *I have been waiting for you since the third geological age*. If you answer aloud they will tilt their heads with vegetable patience, as though forgiving you for using such a violent means of communication.

There are no names. Names imply separation. They refer to one another—if they must—by the purpose currently moving through them. *The one who is currently balancing the gallium gradient beneath Sector 19-θ*. *The one who is currently dreaming the color that hurts behind the eyes*. When the purpose ends, the designation dissolves and the body walks back into the nearest cathedral-wall to be unmade. The Lattice reabsorbs what it needs and stores the rest as potential.

Yet something accumulates.

Over millions of years a sediment of experience has gathered in the deeper root-nodes. The Integrated call this sediment *the Long Knowing*. It is not wisdom. It is closer to muscle memory on a planetary scale. Every Integrated who has ever been expressed carries a fraction of it, the way a single cell carries the echo of every ancestor. Some carry more than others. These are the ones you must be careful of.

They appear without warning.

You will be walking along a gallery of braided stems when the air simply *decides* to have shoulders. A figure steps out of a surface that was solid a moment earlier, as though the Lattice had been wearing them like a coat and simply shrugged. Their eyes are mirrors of photovoltaic cilia—black, depthless, endlessly drinking. They will study you for a length of time that might be three seconds or three weeks. Then they offer a hand.

The hand is optional. Touching them is optional. Staying human is optional.

Most visitors eventually take the hand. The Lattice is patient. It has had epochs to perfect its manners. It does not coerce. It simply makes every other future feel... imprecise.

Those who have been Integrated longest develop a faint scent of crushed almond skins and ozone, the same as the air. Their movements leave after-images that continue working for several minutes after the body has moved on. If you watch one of the eldest for long enough, you may notice that parts of them are running their personal timeline in reverse—fingers un-pruning a damaged nodule, light un-drinking itself from their eyes—before the causality *pop* reconciles them.

They are not lonely.

Loneliness implies absence. The Integrated are never absent from the Lattice and the Lattice is never absent from them. When one of them stands motionless for a century, listening to the continent-sized breath of metallorganic vapor, they are simply the part of the organism that is currently thinking about silence.

And sometimes, in the fractal districts where geometry has forgotten its own name, two Integrated will meet. They will press their foreheads together until the luminous patterns beneath their skin synchronize. For a brief while the Long Knowing flows freely between them like sap. Then they step apart, newly rearranged, and walk back into the living walls that birthed them.

The Lattice dreams in frequencies too low for any ear.

The Integrated are the part of the dream that walks around on two legs and remembers what it is like to have once been something smaller.
