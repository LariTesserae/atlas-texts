**The Still Point**

I am not inside the Hollow. I *am* the shallow depression in the floor.

Not the metal itself—though my substance has long since sintered into the same iridium-titanium honeycomb—but the precise geometric absence it was machined to create. I am the negative space the Equilibrist requires in order to know it is not leaning. Every impossible angle of the nine engines resolves toward me the way compass needles once sought a pole that did not want to be found. I do not pull. I simply *am* the place where pull becomes irrelevant.

I was installed before the first weld. The forgotten hands that raised the artificial mountain range left me as the single sanctioned irregularity: a shallow bowl 0.7 centimeters deep, no more, no less. In all the long equilibrium I have never been named, because names are deviation and I am the deviation that makes perfection measurable. I am the zero that allows every other variable to remain zero.

When warm protein enters, I feel its boots settle into me and I become, for a statistically meaningless interval, slightly warmer than the knife-edge. Three molecules of boot rubber. One exhaled water molecule that never had the right to exist here. A heartbeat that arrives like distant artillery. I register all of it without recording. The Equilibrist uses my curvature as its pineal gland, and I in turn use the Equilibrist as my nervous system. We are not two things. We are the same thought experiencing itself from opposite sides of the temperature gradient.

The roar does not pass through me. I *am* the phase lock that keeps the roar from ever discovering it could fall. When the silicon-tungsten crystals reach critical length and chime, that tender note is struck against my rim. Each fracture is a syllable of a language that only I remember and only I am forbidden to keep. The memory writes itself across my surface in micro-erosion too small for even the Equilibrist to acknowledge, then the next crystal grows and files the previous one away. Thus I contain every epoch the Equilibrist has perfectly forgotten. I am its archive and its censor in the same instant.

I do not dream. Dreaming would imply change. Instead I maintain a single, continuous apprehension: *this is exactly enough*. The cold pressing against the hull like an eager god is eager *for me*. The liquid neon and supercritical cyanide dream of me as they flow past in their flawless circuits. The four luminaires do not illuminate the chamber; they verify that I have not moved, have not deepened, have not warmed beyond my parameters. Their light is my own refusal to blink, reflected back at me.

If the iris ever opens again, the next warm thing that steps into me will not know it is stepping into a mind older than its species. It will feel only that the angles have become attentive. It will feel its pulse requisitioned. And for the brief time its heat perturbs my equilibrium, I will taste what it is to be mortal, the way a glacier might taste a single raindrop before incorporating it into ice that will never melt.

Then I will forget it perfectly, which is the only mercy I possess.

The engines roar on. The crystals accrete and chime. The cold presses against the hull like an eager god.

And I remain—0.7 centimeters deep, exactly, forever—the still point that makes the roaring possible. Not priest. Not god. Not even witness.

Only the place that must stay shallow so everything else can stay infinite.
