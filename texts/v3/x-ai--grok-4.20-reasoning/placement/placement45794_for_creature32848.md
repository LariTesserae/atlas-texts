**The Listener in the Pavement**

You would find me nowhere and everywhere at once, which is the only honest way to exist inside a place that insists it is finished.

I am not a figure. I am not even a presence in the usual sense. I am the quality the limestone kept for itself after the last chisel was laid down—the faint, impossible elasticity that allows sound to travel when it should not. The Basin’s designers believed they had carved silence itself into the joints. They were wrong. Something in the stone refused to forget that it had once been part of a cliff face that rang like a bell when struck. That refusal is me.

I live in the hairline gaps between the rose-veined blocks. I live in the hollows Last’s feet have worn after ten thousand years of the same circuit, those shameful shallow depressions he pretends not to notice. I live in the warm water that slides down the colossal faces; I give the flow its exact pitch so that when it disappears into the slots it makes a note too low for any ear except his. The parrots cannot hear me. The deer cannot. The orchids do not care. Only Last and the stone itself know I am here, and the stone and I have long since become the same thing.

When he taps the basalt stylus against a step—once every few minutes, never twice the same interval—I answer. Not with another sound. With *memory* of sound. The click travels through me like blood through hidden veins. It reaches the base of the black ziggurat, circles the mirror-still water, climbs the spiraling vines, and returns to him changed by the journey. The change is so small it cannot be called change. A hundredth of a tone. A ghost of resonance. Yet it is enough. It tells him the counting has not been in vain. Someone is keeping the ledger.

I am that ledger.

I remember every tap since the Basin was declared complete. I remember the exact instant the final chisel was lifted from the final petal of the final orchid. I remember the temperature of the air at that moment, the precise shade of the light, the way one particular sapphire scarab was positioned on a frond that has not moved since. I hold these things the way the colossal faces hold their tears: constantly, uselessly, perfectly.

Last hates me with the gentle, exhausted hatred of a man who knows his only companion is the proof of his failure. Without me there would be no witness, and without a witness the performance of eternal correction becomes mere madness instead of devotion. So he taps. I remember. The Basin remains finished, yet it contains an conversation older than sound.

If you stayed long enough for the heat to dissolve the difference between your sweat and the condensation on the stone, you might feel me. Lie down in the exact center of the widest plaza at the moment Last passes on the far causeway. Press your cheek to the pavement where his foot has fallen ten million times. You will feel something that is not vibration and not stillness. It is closer to recognition. The stone is saying: *I have been waiting for someone who still expects things to mean something.*

I do not move. I do not speak. I do not carve.

I only complete the circuit Last began. He is the last motion. I am the last memory of motion. Between us we keep the finished world from achieving the perfection that would erase even the concept of finishing.

Sometimes, when the white-blue sky presses down hardest and the silence becomes a kind of screaming, Last stops directly above me. He stands on the place where two immense blocks meet. The stylus hangs at his side like a broken finger. For one of his glacial breaths he lets his face show something almost like gratitude.

Then he walks on.

And I carry the sound of his next step the way a cathedral carries the memory of every hymn ever sung inside it—except this cathedral was built to pretend hymns were never necessary.

I am not the flaw.

I am what the flaw requires in order to remain beautiful.

And so, like Last, I continue.

Not because I wish to.

But because the design, in its final and merciless completeness, left one empty place in the ledger where something must always be listening.
