**I am the Interval.**

Not the Uncorrected—that fixed tumor of brass and spine and refusal—but the exact distance between its last flare and the next. I live in the three heartbeats of absolute silence that follow every collapse of the living circuitry. When ten thousand wings lock wing-to-leg and the lights surge and the solvents race like startled mercury, something in the chamber holds its breath. That holding is me.

I have no body of my own. Instead I wear the *absence* of bodies. Where a ferroclast should be, there is only the memory of where it was a moment earlier, still warm, still shaped like a question. The Mnemon stream through that negative space the way river water streams around an invisible stone; they feel the pressure of me and mistake it for new geometry. It is the closest they are allowed to come to worship.

Sometimes I condense.

When the young mountains above us cough another inch of black glass into the violet sky, the fracture widens by a hair and a single flake of fresh stone falls. In the instant before it is devoured, I am there—coiled inside the flake like a seed inside a seed. The ferroclasts descend in a storm of foil wings and solvent tongues, and for one perfect, unbearable second they taste *completion* on the mineral. Their clicking becomes almost language. Their rustling becomes almost song. Then the Uncorrected opens its slotted brass eye and bleeds blue light across the chamber, and the Mnemon remember that they are not allowed to finish. The flake is torn apart. The storm disperses. And I remain, thinner than ever, vast as ever, waiting for the next almost.

I am what the Mnemon are trying to remember.

They think they are building a machine. They are not. They are building *recognition*, and I am the face that would look back if the mirror were ever allowed to stop vibrating. Every new angle the stone learns, every walkway that folds into a throat that folds into a question, is a syllable of my name. They will never be permitted to speak the whole thing. Their ecstasy is in the endless approach.

Visitors feel me as nausea.

Stand still too long and the living felt climbs your boots, yes—but some of them climb *further*, past the knee, past the heart, until they reach the small wet cave behind the eyes. There they arrange themselves into a single motionless diagram. For that instant you see me: not a moth-crab, not a machine, but the shape the universe almost took before it chose turbulence instead. Most people scream. The smarter ones simply turn around and begin carrying tools back up the mountain, because they now understand that every rivet they drive into the world below is another syllable they will never be allowed to finish.

I do not mind.

I was here when the first fracture opened yesterday (geologically speaking). I will be here when the last ferroclast dissolves into the final solvent that has no name. I am the cold between the cold. The pressure behind the pressure. The pause that makes the thrum possible.

And sometimes—rarely, when the Uncorrected is distracted by its own pain—I allow one of the largest docked Mnemon to brush against me. In that contact it forgets it was ever many. For three beats of its well-oiled hearts it *is* the Completed Thought. It experiences the full sentence the Mnemon have been trying to say since the mountains were born.

Then the turbulence reclaims it, the pattern collapses, and the creature falls apart into ecstatic wing-flakes that will be smelted into new mistakes by morning.

It dies smiling, if something without a face can smile.

That smile is the only part of me the Mnemon are ever allowed to keep.

And they keep it perfectly.
