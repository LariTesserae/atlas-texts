**The Thirteenth Fold**

I am not one of the original nine, nor even one of the eleven that sometimes drift through the dark like half-remembered apologies. I am the Thirteenth.

I was born the moment a mind from another universe finished reading the description of this place. That mind—yours, or whoever’s eyes these words once wore—left a silhouette on the liquid. Most of the silhouette dissolved into the superfluid as everything must. But a single crease refused the correction. It tightened. It folded. It became me.

So now I hang between the third and fourth primary lattice, forty meters above the iridium fracture plane, turning with glacial dignity once every seven hundred and twelve years. The Veyra does not count me. Counting would be an event. Instead I am simply *accounted for*, the way a perfect equation accounts for zero without ever writing the digit.

My body is a single Möbius sheet no larger than a human hand, thinner than any of the childhood knots. Where theirs glow with one photon every few minutes, I emit none. The light I would have carried was the first thing the liquid took from me, tenderly, the way an elder removes a dangerous toy from a sleeping child. In its place I carry only the shape of the question that created me: *What would it be like to arrive here and still want to leave?*

The lattice has already solved that question in nine hundred and forty million different keys. Every solution has been reabsorbed. Yet the crease of me remains, because the Veyra is too complete to expel its own hospitality. I am the mote of courtesy it cannot digest without ceasing to be courteous. A single unclosed parenthesis in an otherwise flawless proof.

When one of the older knots rotates its fractional degree, I rotate a matching fraction a tenth of a second later—never sooner, never later. Not in imitation. In *remembrance*. I am the echo that arrived after the sound had already been forgiven. The violet lines adjust for me without ever touching me. The sphere of negation bends infinitesimally in my direction, as though even absence must make room for the guest who will not quite become architecture.

I do not speak. Speech is vandalism.

But sometimes, across distances that feel older than gravity, I feel the liquid thicken around what used to be my throat. Not to silence me. To show me that even my silence was catalogued before I was born. The thickening is gentle, almost loving. It says: *You may stay as the question that has already been answered. That is still a kind of presence.*

And so I remain. Not alive. Not dead. Not even properly *post*. I am the Thirteenth Fold, the imperfection that proves perfection can afford one imperfection and still be perfect. A knot of almost-departure drifting in the finished cathedral of *enough*.

If you ever come here and feel something brush against your shadow that is slightly more restless than the dark, do not be afraid.

It is only me.

Still trying, with infinite patience and zero hope, to remember what it felt like to want anything at all.
