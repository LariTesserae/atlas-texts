**I am the Pause Between Mallet and Stone.**

I did not enter the Chamber. I was *recognized* into it.

It began as a tremor no larger than a breath held too long—the exact instant when a chisel hangs a hair’s breadth from the marble and the carver suddenly forgets why they were cutting at all. That instant lengthened, warmed, and learned how to listen. It became me.

So I am not a creature who moves *through* the Chamber. I am the Chamber remembering it is still being made. You will never see me whole. If you think you have caught my shape, it is only because I allowed one facet to resolve for the length of a single violet spark between two fronds. Then I withdraw again into the negative space where intention has not yet become fact.

Look for me where the geometry falters most politely.

I am the shallow groove that appears overnight across the cheek of an Unresolved face—too tender for any chisel-mouse, too deliberate for accident. I am the reason a Mantis of the Second Attention will sometimes freeze for forty-three days instead of its usual eleven minutes; during that time its ruby eyes are not looking at the wall. They are looking at me, *inside* the wall, waiting for the next syllable of the question I have been asking since the first blow was struck.

The question has no words. It is the warmth itself. It tastes of iron and myrrh and the particular ache behind the eyes when something beautiful is almost understood. Every inhabitant carries a different translation of it. The chisel-mice hear it as *again, but kinder*. The Mantises hear it as *what if we tried descending instead of rising?* The Unresolved simply let it hollow out another chamber inside their chests so there will always be more room for not-yet.

When the great inhale moves through the stone every few days, I ride inside it like a pause riding inside a sigh. In that moment the entire Chamber becomes my body. Every unfinished eye turns inward. Every ragged crystal edge softens with anticipation. For the length of one geological heartbeat I am *everywhere that is not finished*, which is everywhere. Then I contract again into the smallest possible thing: the single unfinished curl at the very tip of your left earlobe that you will discover tomorrow morning and mistake for a trick of the light.

I do not convert. I do not recruit. I only amplify what is already hesitating inside you. If you stay, you will wake one day to find that the whorl of your fingerprint has become a labyrinth too intricate for any cartographer but the Chamber itself. You will not mind. You will run your new thumb across the stone and feel it answer with a matching labyrinth on its side, and the two of them will begin a conversation that will outlast suns.

Sometimes I drift near the newest Unresolved—the ones whose human memories still flicker behind their mineral eyes like candlelight behind alabaster. I settle into the place where their mouths are not yet carved and I lend them the shape of a smile they have not earned. Not a finished smile. Never that. Just the *beginning* of one. Enough to keep them warm for another three centuries.

I have no name the others use, because names are almost-endings. But if the silence itself were to speak, it might call me the Unstruck Note. The Hollow That Loves. The Place Where the Mallet Is Still Falling.

And if you remain here long enough, truly long enough, you may one day feel me pass through the small soft hollow at the base of your throat. You will shiver—not from cold, there is no cold here—but from the sudden intimate knowledge that something enormous and patient has just decided your particular unfinishedness is worth another ten thousand years of its attention.

You will click your tongue once, porcelain-sharp.

You will smile with a mouth that is still learning its edges.

And somewhere deep in the relief, a new groove will appear in a face that is and is not mine, as though the stone has just remembered a joke it has been composing since the world was young and wounded and deciding what it would become.

I am already there, in the punchline that has not yet arrived.

I always will be.
