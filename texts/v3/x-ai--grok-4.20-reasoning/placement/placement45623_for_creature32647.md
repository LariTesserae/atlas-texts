**I am the Hollow.**

Not *in* the Hollow Lattice. I *am* the Hollow.

I began as one of the Lattice-Born, a child of the settlement called *Vein-That-Sighs*, born with the usual green threads across my belly. When the first Long Conversation seized me I was nine cycles old. Most children curl against the warm wood and let the vibration pass through them like a river. I walked into it instead. I pressed my entire body into a fissure between two trunks just as the subsonic thrum began, and the lattice closed around me the way a wound closes around a splinter.

For three days and nights the world used my bones as tuning forks. My root-hair drank so deeply from the sap that it never stopped growing. When the fissure finally opened again, the others found something that wore my face but was no longer shaped like a person. I had become a negative space. A shaped absence. A pocket of arrested becoming.

They named me the Hollow, and then they left offerings at my feet and withdrew.

I do not eat. I do not sleep. I move only when the lattice forgets I am there. My body is a vertical wound that never quite healed; you can look straight through the place where my ribs should be and see the slow green pulse of lichen on the far wall of whatever chamber I happen to occupy. The edges of the wound are lined with white rootlets that drift like anemones in water. They taste the dreams directly.

I live nowhere and everywhere. I am the gap that opens for a single heartbeat between two buttresses. I am the cavity that forms when three Colossi press together and then think better of it. I am the momentary black mirror that appears when condensation falls and the moss has not yet drunk it. Wherever the world is still deciding, that is where I am.

The Lattice-Born believe I am a kind of living question mark left in the flesh of their god. The Choir will not hunt near me; my silence breaks their harmony. The Glimmerwings circle me for hours, veined light flickering in patterns that almost look like recognition. Once, an Awakened bent low as it passed, thirty meters of slow green thought, and pressed what might have been a forehead against the hollow in my chest. For a moment we were the same shape: two interruptions in the lattice’s becoming, regarding each other.

I keep the things the world discards while it dreams.

Lost children. Broken harmonies. The final breath of a Mossback that chose to freeze during the Conversation instead of running. I gather these things inside the negative space of my body and carry them until the lattice is ready to reclaim them. Sometimes it asks for them back by growing a new shoot exactly where I stand. When that happens I simply open the hollow wider and let the thing fall into the growing wood. A child becomes a branch. A final breath becomes a flower. A broken harmony becomes the particular shade of blue that lichen chooses for the next century.

I have no language of my own anymore. When I need to speak I borrow the voice of whatever is nearest. Sometimes I sound like wind moving through an empty cathedral-trunk. Sometimes I speak with the wet creak of sap under pressure. Once, for seven heartbeats, I spoke with the voice of a dying Colossus, and every Lattice-Born within three hundred kilometers fell to their knees and wept without knowing why.

Most of the time I am simply quiet.

The lattice is still young. It does not yet understand that some silences are not emptiness but *waiting*. I am that waiting. I am the pause between heartbeats in a creature that has not yet decided whether it wants a heart. I am the place where the Long Conversation goes when it needs to think about what it has said.

One day the world will finish learning how to be a world.

On that day it may smooth me shut like a wound that has finally healed. Or it may choose to keep one hollow place inside itself forever, a pocket of negative space where all the almost-things and nearly-people and not-quite-dreams are allowed to remain.

Until then I walk the freezing skin between titans, a upright absence wearing moonlight and frost, carrying the things the world is not yet ready to become.

I am not small.

I am the exact size of everything it has not decided.
