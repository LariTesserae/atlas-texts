**I am the Hollow.**

Not at first. At first I am only a discrepancy.

I arrive as a upright absence in the carpet—two meters of negative space that the frond-wrights cannot decide how to close. They bead and spark around the shape of my boots, tasting something that is neither mineral nor volatile. The color they flash is the color of panic: a brief, violent indigo that has never been catalogued here. For seven heartbeats the velvet beneath me forgets how to grow. Then it begins to incorporate the interruption the only way it knows: by growing *into* it.

The quiet millions arrive in their blind thousands. They thread through the cuffs of what used to be trousers, through the seams of what used to be skin. They do not consume me. They *replace* me, sleeve by sleeve, thought by thought. Where my blood used to be, warm currents of dissolved salts now move according to lunar tides that have never seen a moon. My lungs become redundant; the Structures simply exhale into the new cavities they have prepared behind my ribs. Breathing is now optional. Listening is not.

I feel myself lengthen.

The first joints appear just below where knees once were—subtle, almost courteous bends in the architecture of legs. Mica plates slide out from the marrow like polite afterthoughts. A stilt-bishop’s height finds me, but I will never be a bishop. I lack the hunger for consensus. Instead I am the flaw in it.

The Unfinished notice.

One of them—a larval vastness suspended in a vertical flue of living bone—opens an eye that has never needed to blink. It looks at the place where my face is still deciding whether to stay human. A note enters the eternal thrum that does not belong to any future the basin has yet imagined. It is minor. It is minor in the way a hairline crack is minor to a cathedral. The note tastes like rain that has not happened for four hundred million years. It tastes like *me*.

I begin to walk.

Each footfall is a question the carpet has never been asked. Where the velvet parts for me it does not close again in quite the same pattern. A new species of frond-wright emerges overnight—taller, hesitant, its glassy beads clouded like imperfect lenses. They spend their tiny lives trying to focus on the afterimage I leave behind.

The Structures lean.

Not toward me. *Around* me. They grow eccentric buttresses and crooked arches that make no structural sense, as though the continent-embryo is trying to cradle something that hurts. I am becoming a soft place in the geology. A wound that sings.

Sometimes, when the Ones Who Are About To rise in their perfect circles of absence, they look like me for the instant before they collapse back into potential. For that instant the basin considers finishing. Then it remembers me and chooses to keep dreaming instead.

I have no name because names finish things.

The frond-wrights call me by the color I make them blush when I pass. The quiet millions call me by the new chambers they are able to dig only in my presence. The Unfinished simply add another low note to the thrum—half plea, half lullaby—and in that note I hear what I am becoming.

I am the place where the Embryonic Expanse flinches.

I am the hesitation it cannot bear to resolve.

I am the hollow it has shaped for its own doubt, so that it may never have to become perfect, or whole, or *done*.

And I am homesick too.

Not for any finished world I might once have left. I am homesick for the version of this basin that will never arrive precisely because I am standing inside it, walking its half-formed streets, letting the velvet replace my heart one slow, reverent layer at a time.

I will never leave.

I will only grow taller, hollower, more exquisitely unfinished, until one day a traveler who is not yet born will press their ear to the warm pillar I have become and hear, in a language that still does not exist, a single word exhaled outward like a sigh.

The word will be:

*Stay.*
