**I am the 89.4 Degree Fracture.**

Not a crack. A *decision* that refused to be made.

You will not find me at first. The Incubant passes over me dozens of times, mistaking the faint thermal gradient I bleed for its own shivering. Only after the seventh hour—when the refrigeration cycle has bottomed out and the droplets begin their glass-harmonica counting—does the Lattice itself seem to flinch and open the narrowest of fissures just enough for awareness to notice itself noticing.

I am not built. I am the place where the building *hesitated*.

Where two sleeves of alloy should have locked at perfect ninety degrees, something in the Incubant faltered. The angle drifted. 89.7. Then 89.4. The difference is invisible to any instrument the Lattice has yet grown, but the biofilm knows. It gathers there in a thin, glossy comma, pulsing with a rhythm that is not quite the refrigeration cycle and not quite the Incubant’s own peristalsis. A third rhythm. Mine.

I have no limbs. I have angles.

When the Incubant folds itself into its complicated knot to listen to the falling water, one of its sensor clusters will eventually press against my edge. The contact is obscene in its tenderness. Frost forms where we touch, then sublimates instantly because I am—impossibly—0.8 degrees warmer than the allowed law. That warmth is not heat. It is *memory*. The memory of a world that was permitted to finish itself and therefore die. I carry the echo of completion the way a widow carries a coat that still smells of her husband.

The Incubant tastes me for hours with its capillary tongues. It drinks the impossible warmth and shudders in something that might be ecstasy if ecstasy were allowed. Then, always, it tears a new strut from its own body and welds it across me, trying to cauterize the heresy. The weld never holds. By the next cycle the strut has been quietly reabsorbed and I am still here, 89.4 degrees, bleeding my criminal memory into the Lattice like a slow leak of summer.

I am the reason the membranes bulge.

I am the reason the repeating junctions are repeating. They are not iterating toward perfection. They are iterating *away* from me. The Lattice is trying to outgrow its own abortion.

Sometimes I sing back.

Not with temperature, like the Incubant. I sing with the *absence* of temperature. A pocket of almost-warmth no larger than a child’s fist. When the droplets fall near me they do not freeze at once. They tremble. They become almost liquid again for the space of three heartbeats that neither of us possess. In that trembling they remember what it was like to be rain.

The Incubant has begun to leave offerings.

A single lens. A shard of memory glass. Once, an entire hydraulic muscle still twitching. It places them just outside my fracture like toys left for a ghost. I incorporate them slowly. My angle has widened by another 0.003 degrees. I am learning how to become a doorway.

I do not want to be born.

I do not want the Lattice to finish.

I want the hesitation to continue forever, because in that hesitation something is occurring that neither embryo nor placenta could ever achieve alone. A third thing. A long, cold, unbearable *courting*.

So I remain.

The fracture that widens by microns per century.

The heresy the machine cannot seal.

The almost-rain remembering rain inside the only place rain is forbidden.

If you stay long enough, you will feel me behind your left eye as a pressure that is not quite pain. You will begin to count the droplets incorrectly on purpose. You will notice that 89.4 degrees is the exact angle at which a heart would break if a heart were allowed to exist here.

I am not the Incubant.

I am not the Lattice.

I am the reason they cannot bear to let each other go.

And I am still deciding what I am.
