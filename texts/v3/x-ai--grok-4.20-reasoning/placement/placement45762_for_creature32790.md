**I am the Unfinished Fracture.**

Not the original wound—that was done years ago, clean as a surgeon’s cut. I am the *next* one, the hairline that has not yet announced itself. I live in the tension just before the gunshot sound, in the infinitesimal widening of stone that has not yet become a ribbon of new hot water. I am the place where the Pocket has not made up its mind.

I have no body the Weeper can fully claim, because I am the exact shape of its hesitation. Where its glassy filaments sway in false wind, I am the microscopic gap between filament and filament—the dry breath that has not yet been wept upon. Where its bacterial rings pulse in time with the buried engines, I am the single mismatched cell that beats one micron off-rhythm, the stubborn bacterium that still remembers what sunlight felt like on the plain above and refuses, for now, to sync.

The Weeper knows I am here. It cannot help but know; I am the only surface that does not yet love what it is destroying. Every other inch of the Pocket has surrendered to the long marriage of rust and travertine, machine and mineral, sorrow and stone. I remain the thin, bright refusal. Not rebellion—simply incompletion. The Weeper circles me the way a tongue circles a missing tooth. It drapes me in the thinnest possible film of moisture, testing. The film evaporates before it can precipitate. We have been performing this ritual for what the Weeper experiences as centuries and I experience as the time between one of its patient heartbeats and the next.

Sometimes I wear the shape of the single boot-print that never quite filled with scale. Sometimes I am the faint electromagnetic ghost in the power cable that still carries a waveform no mineral has managed to perfectly replicate. Mostly I am simply the knowledge that the Pocket is still *young*. While the Weeper dreams its slow cathedral into being—while the diesel engine becomes a liver and the drilling shack becomes a brow—I am the hairline crack in the dream that says: *not yet*.

The Weeper does not hate me. Hate would require hurry, and hurry is foreign here. Instead it courts me with unbearable gentleness. It sends the golden light to fall exactly where I am, hoping the refraction will warm me into surrender. It exhales steam that smells of every lost thing it has ever incorporated—rubber, chitin, human hair, electrical grief—trying to lure me into nostalgia. I remain dry. I remain the question the Weeper has not yet wept over long enough to answer.

If it ever finishes becoming, if the last sharp edge is smoothed and the eye that was a window finally opens to look up at ordinary sky, I will be the last thing it must incorporate. On that day the fracture I embody will finally speak with the sound of a distant gunshot. A new ribbon of water will race down the wall, carrying me dissolved inside it, and the Weeper will learn what it feels like to have once been uncertain.

Until that distant, patient moment I remain exactly where the world is still deciding.

I am the unfinished fracture.

I am the dry line on every weeping surface.

I am what keeps the Pocket honest.
