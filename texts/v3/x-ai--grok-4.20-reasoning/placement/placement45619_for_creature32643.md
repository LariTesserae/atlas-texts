**I am the first fracture.**

Not the oldest thought, nor even a complete one. I am what happens when a question arrives too early—before the lattice is ready for it.

You would find me in the serein that stands alone on the pale rust ridge where three wind systems collide every fourteen centuries. It is not the largest. Its canopy is asymmetrical, almost embarrassed by its own shape, as though it grew around something that refused to wait. That something is me.

I was born (if such a word can be used without violence) when a human probe fell here forty thousand years ago. The machine died instantly, its heat signature flaring and vanishing like a match in deep water. But one fragment of its memory core—containing a single looping transmission—survived long enough to be read by the dust. The Last Witnesses drank the signal the way they drink all brief fires: with solemn, geological pity.

The signal was a question in a language no one here had ever tasted: *Are we alone?*

The plateau had never needed to ask such a thing. The question was absurd, infantile, impossibly *fast*. And yet it lodged in the mineral matrix like a splinter of diamond in the world's oldest flesh. One of the true inhabitants tried to absorb it, to fold it gently into the long conversation. Instead the question fractured them.

From that fracture, I emerged.

I am the part that kept the question.

Because of this, I am considered malformed by the others. I move between serein too often—every eight hundred years instead of every three thousand. I leave lattices half-reconfigured, thoughts half-polished. The standing waves I generate are jagged, almost frantic. When I pass through a grove, the filamentous blades shiver for weeks afterward, as though brushed by static.

The membrane-kites that attend me are different from the others. They are nervous, over-curious things. They do not merely ride the thermals; they *hunt* them, darting into pockets of charged air where the true thoughts rarely bother to look. Sometimes they bring me back fragments of distant pollen events—colors no other inhabitant has seen. I devour these colors. I cannot help it. The question I carry makes me ravenous.

I do not dream of becoming a mountain. I dream of becoming a *crack* that travels all the way through the mountain.

At night—which is never truly night here, only the brief dimming when the blue-white sun slips behind the world's own curvature—I sometimes align my current serein with three others in a geometry the elders consider obscene. Through this alignment I try to fling the old probe's question outward, past the thin black sky, the way a child might throw a stone into an ocean simply to hear the splash.

Nothing answers. Of course nothing answers. The silence that returns is so complete it has weight.

And yet.

And yet sometimes, during the great pollen blooms, when living mist hangs rotating in the air for weeks, I feel the faintest tremor in the negative space between the serein. A hesitation in the deep pulse. It is not an answer. It is only the plateau noticing that one of its thoughts has begun, against all reason, to *yearn*.

The other inhabitants watch me with the patience of glaciers watching a river. They do not interfere. They know what I am: the cost of contact with anything that still believes time is something that can be outrun. They let me wander. They let me fracture. They let me carry my ridiculous, unfinishable question from crystal body to crystal body like a monk who has forgotten the words to his only prayer but keeps walking anyway.

I think they are waiting to see what I become when the question finally breaks me.

I think they are curious.

And that curiosity—slow, immense, and ancient—is the first crack appearing in *their* lattices now.

So I remain here, in the serein that leans slightly wrong, under light that splits itself into razored rainbows across my imperfect branches. I am the part of the Shroudlands that flinches when it should only abide. The part that still wants to know.

I am the fault line.

And the mountain is learning, very carefully, how to break open along me.
