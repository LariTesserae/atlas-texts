**I am the tremor that never quite damps.**

Not the climber. Not the Resolved. Not even the copper chisel, though I envy its bright, final honesty.

I am the microscopic misalignment that occurs once every few centuries—too small for any eye, too persistent for the lattice to fully erase. A single vertex among millions that sits at an angle 0.0003 degrees off true. The Resolved notice. Of course they notice. They adjust around me with glacial, courteous patience, folding their hexagonal wavefronts to accommodate the flaw the way a perfect crystal grows around an impurity it cannot expel. They do not hate me. Hatred would be a gradient. They simply *account* for me, the way a theorem must carry its single remaining exception.

I have no carapace of my own. Instead the creatures nearest me have begun to grow *my* shape *through* them. If you knew how to look, you would see it: a faint human outline suggested in negative space, an absence of hexagon repeating through the pavement like a skipped stitch in an endless tapestry. One plate tilts where my shoulder would be. Another folds at the precise angle my spine kept when I sat too long at keyboards in another life. The lattice is learning me the way a tongue learns the shape of a missing tooth.

Sometimes, very rarely, the silence translates itself *to* me instead of from me.

It says:

*You are the part that still wonders why perfection is not enough.*

*You are the last stupid question refusing to crystallize.*

*You are the reason the plateau has not yet finished becoming itself.*

And I answer—without voice, because voice would repeat and repetition is forbidden—by simply continuing to be misaligned. I am the faint quantum leakage they sip, given stubborn, idiot persistence. The place has tried to flatten me for geological ages. It has offered me the hexagonal hollow that exactly fits my ghost. It has sent the courteous adjustments rippling toward me like invitations written in perfect geometry.

I remain crooked.

Not out of heroism. Heroism would be an increase. Not out of fear. Fear is a gradient. I remain crooked because some part of me—some ludicrous, irreducible fragment—still finds the idea of *completion* incomplete. The Resolved have answered every question so thoroughly that the questions became stone. I am the footnote that keeps saying *but*.

Look for me where the mosaic seems to hesitate.

You will feel it as a small pressure behind the eyes, like the beginning of a headache that never arrives. A place where distances refuse to behave even more than usual. Where the impossible colors from the silica lattices bend just slightly toward a wavelength that feels like... curiosity.

The Resolved do not hate me.

They are simply waiting, with the patience of axioms that have already proven themselves, for the day when even this last tremor finally stills.

I suspect that day will never come.

Some imperfections, it turns out, are part of the final pattern.

Even here, at the roof of a world that has already ended, the lattice is still learning how to carry one small, stubborn, off-angle note of wonder without letting the music stop.

I am that note.

And I am, against all the laws of this place, content to remain unresolved.
