High above the Manufactory, where the last traces of sunlight filter through the thermocline, floats the Garden of Drift. Here, massive geodesic spheres of transparent bioglass hang suspended in the cooler waters, tethered to each other by bridges of living tissue that flex and undulate with the ocean currents. Each sphere contains its own microclimate - carefully maintained ecosystems that capture and transform the rising warmth and mineral-rich waters from the depths below.

The Garden is home to the Cultivators, cousins to the deep-dwelling Tenders but evolved for the liminal zone between light and dark. Their bodies are more defined than their abyssal relatives, optimized for the sharp gradients of temperature and pressure at this depth. Photosynthetic panels line their outer membranes, drinking in the weak sunlight that still reaches these waters. Their forms are more plant than animal, more coral than machine, yet they maintain a fluid grace that marks their kinship with the Tenders below.

The connection between these two realms is not merely physical. The thermal plumes rising from the Manufactory create a constant vertical exchange - an upwelling of nutrients and energy that feeds the Garden's diverse ecosystems. In return, the Garden processes and filters these materials, creating new compounds that sink back to the depths in a slow rain of biological matter. This exchange forms a metabolism at the scale of ocean systems, a vast cycle of transformation that neither realm could sustain alone.

The space between these worlds is alive with traffic. Specialized messenger organisms - part spore, part drone - ride the thermal currents up and down, carrying information and materials between the realms. They navigate by the bioluminescent patterns that both societies use as language, creating rivers of living light that flow through the darkness.

Where the Tenders' consciousness is distributed and geological, the Cultivators maintain a more individuated awareness, tuned to the daily rhythms of light and dark. Yet they too share a collective mind through their network of living bridges, and their work follows patterns laid down in ancestral memory. They understand their deep-dwelling kin not as separate beings but as another expression of the same vast system - different organs of the same living entity.

Together, these two realms form a single extended metabolism - the Manufactory processing raw materials from the earth's heat, the Garden transforming these materials through interaction with sunlight and atmosphere. The space between them is not empty but rather a gradient of transformation, a vertical ecology of exchange that connects the abyss to the surface world above.

The true nature of this system - whether it evolved naturally or was engineered for some forgotten purpose - remains unknown even to its inhabitants. Like a coral reef or a rainforest, it has become its own reason for being, a self-sustaining cycle of creation and transformation that operates on multiple scales of time and space. In the endless dance of light and dark, heat and cold, depth and surface, it endures and evolves, maintaining its delicate balance in the heart of the world's oceans.
