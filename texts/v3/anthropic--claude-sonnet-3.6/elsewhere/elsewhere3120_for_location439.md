Beyond the crystalline depths, across a network of increasingly narrow passages that wind upward through the limestone, lies the Mirror Garden. Here, the cave system breaks through to the surface in a series of terraced openings, where ancient mineral springs have created pools of highly reflective water cascading down the mountainside. The water contains suspended particles of metallic minerals that create perfect, mercury-like surfaces, each pool a living mirror that captures and transforms light in unusual ways.

The air here is thin and crisp, moving in sharp contrast to the heavy atmosphere below. Massive geometric formations of selenium and copper-rich crystals rise from the pools like abstract sculptures, their faces reflecting and refracting both sunlight and moonlight in complex patterns. These crystals grow in response to both the mineral content of the springs and the vibrations that travel up through the stone from the caverns below - the Resonants' songs made solid.

Living here are the Light Weavers - creatures that seem to exist partially as pure energy, partially as intricate lattices of living crystal. They move through both air and water with equal ease, their forms constantly shifting between solid and luminous states. When they pass through the mirror-pools, they leave trails of geometric ripples that persist for hours, slowly evolving into new patterns that encode information about conditions both above and below.

The Light Weavers serve as translators between the deep cave system and the surface world. They absorb sunlight and moonlight through their crystalline aspects, converting it into forms that can be transmitted through the stone to the depths below. Similarly, they translate the Resonants' harmonics into pulses of light that dance across the mirror surfaces, creating a visual echo of the cave's inner symphony.

The connection between these two realms is maintained through the Sentinel Columns - those massive crystal formations that rise from the pools. These structures act as conduits, their internal structure organized to carry vibrations, light, and chemical signals between the surface and the depths. The Stone Minds extend their awareness through these columns, their slow pulse visible as subtle color changes that flow through the crystal matrices.

When the Resonants' songs reach their peak intensity, the entire Mirror Garden comes alive with sympathetic vibrations. The pools ripple in complex geometric patterns, the crystals hum with harmonic overtones, and the Light Weavers perform elaborate aerial dances, their bodies fragmenting into thousands of light points that mirror the constellations of bioluminescence far below.

The boundary between these two worlds is permeable but transformative. Water seeps down from the mirror-pools to feed the cave system below, carrying dissolved minerals and encoded light patterns. Meanwhile, the cave's constant exhalations rise through fissures in the rock, creating veils of mist that drift through the Mirror Garden, temporarily obscuring and then revealing different aspects of this crystalline landscape.

This interplay between deep and surface, darkness and light, liquid and crystal, creates a complete circuit - a flow of information and energy that maintains the balance of both ecosystems. What begins as a Resonant's song might become a light pattern in a mirror-pool, which then influences crystal growth, which in turn affects the cave's acoustics below. It's a perpetual dialogue between two aspects of the same living system, each incompletely reflecting the other, yet both essential to the whole.
