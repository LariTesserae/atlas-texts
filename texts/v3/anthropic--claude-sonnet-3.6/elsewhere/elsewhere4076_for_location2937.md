Far below the City of Hanging Gardens, in the depths that its elevator systems disappear into, lies the Undercity of Roots. Where the Hanging Gardens are all light and air, the Undercity is a realm of pressure and density, carved into the living rock of the plateau's base. Here, the ancient stone has been hollowed into vast chambers and winding corridors, not by conventional mining but by colonies of bioengineered lithophages - organisms that slowly digest rock, leaving behind smoothed caverns reinforced with their crystalline waste products.

The Undercity processes and refines the raw materials that feed the growth of the Gardens above. But where the upper city's systems are precise and controlled, the processes here are more organic, almost alchemical. Massive bioreactors pulse with bioluminescent cultures, breaking down complex minerals into their constituent elements. Rivers of living metal flow through temperature-regulated channels, guided by magnetic fields and the subtle influences of bacterial colonies that have evolved to shepherd specific elements.

The inhabitants of the Undercity are known as the Dense Ones, and they've adapted to their environment as thoroughly as their upper-dwelling cousins, though in very different ways. Their bodies are compact and powerful, with reinforced bones that help them withstand the pressure. Their skin hosts colonies of symbiotic fungi that help them process the mineral-rich atmosphere and provide a measure of protection from the caustic environments they sometimes work in. Their eyes are adapted to the dim light and can detect subtle chemical signatures in the air.

The connection between the two cities is more than just physical. The elevator systems that run between them are like a circulatory system, but they carry more than materials. They transfer data, energy, and most importantly, they maintain the balance between the two realms. The Undercity's processes create the raw materials needed above, while the Gardens send down organic matter for processing and recycling.

The space between - the great vertical shaft system that connects them - is a world of its own. Here, specialized maintenance crews called the Vertical Nomads make their homes in mobile platforms that climb up and down the shaft walls. They monitor the flow of materials and maintain the complex systems that keep the two cities in harmony. Their culture is built around movement and change, with celebrations and ceremonies that mark the rhythms of ascent and descent.

This vertical space also serves as a buffer zone where the distinct atmospheres of the two cities can gradually transition. Pressure gradients are carefully maintained, and sophisticated airlocks and filtering systems ensure that the unique ecosystems of each realm remain distinct while allowing necessary exchanges.

The relationship between the Hanging Gardens and the Undercity of Roots is symbiotic but not always smooth. The two populations have developed distinct cultures and worldviews. Where the Garden dwellers see growth as something to be carefully controlled and directed, the Dense Ones view it as a force to be channeled but never fully tamed. These philosophical differences occasionally lead to tensions, particularly around the pace and nature of expansion, but the inhabitants of both realms understand their fundamental interdependence.

Together, the two cities form a complete system - one reaching up into the light, the other delving deep into the earth, connected by the liminal space of the shafts where elements, energy, and ideas flow between them. It's a vertical ecosystem that spans from the depths to the heights, each level adapted to its environment but inextricably linked to the whole.
