What a captivating vision of Dewcatcher's Realm and its mysterious Drift Shepherds. Let me tell you about another place in this world, connected yet distinct - the Evaporation Gardens.

High above the galleries, where the ancient cliff face meets open sky, sprawls a terraced landscape of shallow pools and crystalline structures. This is where the processed water from below is released back into the world's water cycle, but the process is far from simple. The Evaporation Gardens are a shimmering expanse of engineered ecosystems, where water's transformation from liquid to vapor is slowed and made visible.

The terraces are lined with a material that resembles mother-of-pearl but is actually a living substrate - colonies of specialized organisms that grew from spores carried up from the galleries below. These organisms create microscopic patterns that catch and hold water molecules, releasing them in precise rhythms that create standing waves in the air itself. The waves are visible as shimming ribbons of refracted light, especially at dawn and dusk.

Here, the air-swimming creatures from below evolve into different forms. Some develop wings of near-perfect transparency, riding the thermal currents between the pools. Others become more plant-like, extending delicate tendrils into both water and air, their bodies serving as living bridges between states of matter.

The human workers here are known as Vapor Weavers. They wear suits of finely-woven mesh that change color in response to humidity levels, allowing them to "read" the air currents. Using long, flexible tools that resemble fishing rods crossed with conductor's batons, they guide the evaporation processes, ensuring that water returns to the atmosphere in forms that will eventually generate the specific types of precipitation needed by different regions.

The connection between the deep galleries and the Evaporation Gardens is maintained by a network of glass tubes that spiral up through the cliff face. Within these tubes, water flows not just up and down but in complex helical patterns. The Drift Shepherds can occasionally be seen within these tubes, their liquid bodies elongating to impossible lengths as they transit between depths and heights. Some say they carry messages between the realms, encoded in the geometric patterns of their forms.

Most fascinating is how the two spaces influence each other. The strange physics of water in the deep galleries finds echoes here in the gardens, where drops sometimes hang in the air for days, slowly rotating and refracting sunlight into spectra that don't appear anywhere else in nature. Meanwhile, the biological innovations of the surface - the color-changing mesh, the living substrate - are gradually migrating downward, leading to new hybrid forms in the galleries below.

During certain atmospheric conditions, usually just before major storms, the barrier between the two realms becomes particularly permeable. The Drift Shepherds rise in great numbers through the spiral tubes, their liquid bodies catching the sunlight and creating displays that draw visitors from distant cities. The Vapor Weavers work in concert with them, conducting intricate atmospheric choreographies that locals insist can influence weather patterns across entire regions.

The gardens serve as a kind of inverse mirror to the galleries - where one space collects and concentrates, the other disperses and transforms. Yet both are dedicated to the same essential mystery: the behavior of water at the boundaries of known physics, and the forms of life that emerge to shepherd it through its cycles of change.
