Between the Glass Garden below and the ancient city above lies the Resonance District - a neighborhood that has grown up around the geode's entrance, shaped by centuries of proximity to the chamber's otherworldly influence.

The buildings here are distinctive, constructed with acoustics in mind rather than just shelter. Their walls are thick stone, but hollowed in places to create resonating chambers. Windows are set at precise angles, many fitted with crystal panes that catch and amplify certain frequencies. The streets follow curved paths that create natural amphitheaters and whisper spots. Even the cobblestones are laid in patterns that subtly affect sound waves passing over them.

The district's inhabitants have adapted to life in perpetual sonic flux. Children grow up learning to distinguish dozens of subtle variations in the ever-present hum that rises from below. Local merchants advertise their wares through carefully pitched calls that harmonize with the background resonance. Street musicians cluster at nodes where the acoustics are particularly good, their music mingling with the Glass Garden's song in complex ways.

Many buildings sport crystal growths - not the living crystal-plants from below, but mineral formations that seem to sprout from mortar and stone. These growths appear most commonly around the homes of the Harmonists, who spend the most time in the Garden. Some say the crystals follow the music up from below, seeking to extend the Garden's influence. Others believe they're a sign of the Chorus's attention, though whether that's a blessing or a curse is debated.

The connection between above and below is more than just physical. The Garden's resonance affects dreams here, and children born in the district often show unusual musical aptitude. Time behaves strangely too - locals speak of moments when the past seems to leak through, carried on particular harmonics. Old songs linger in corners, and sometimes people swear they can hear echoes of conversations from decades ago.

Sound travels oddly between the two spaces. A whisper in the Garden might emerge as a clear note in a Resonance District cellar days later. Conversely, certain songs sung in the district's streets will ripple through the crystalline chamber below in unexpected ways, causing the crystal-flora to shift and change. The Harmonists study these connections, maintaining detailed records of acoustic phenomena that few outsiders can fully comprehend.

The district serves as a buffer zone, a place where the Garden's influence can gradually dissipate before reaching the wider city. But it's also a translation space - where the alien music of the Chorus begins to transform into something human ears can understand, and where human music begins its metamorphosis into something the Chorus can comprehend. The inhabitants of the Resonance District are intermediaries in this endless exchange, their lives conducted in the harmony between two very different kinds of song.
