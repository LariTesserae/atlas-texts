Far below the cathedral, at the terminus of countless hair-thin fissures that wind through miles of stone, lies the Pool of Echoes. Here, in absolute darkness, the chemical messages and thermal signals of the Choir are distilled into pure liquid meaning. The pool is small - perhaps ten feet across - but impossibly deep, its bottom lost in geological mysteries.

The liquid itself defies normal physics. It is dense yet volatile, maintaining a temperature just above freezing despite the surrounding rock's warmth. It has a quality of negative reflection - looking into it doesn't show what is, but rather what isn't, what could be, what was forgotten. The surface never truly stills, though no wind reaches here. Instead, it responds to the slow thoughts of the Choir above, forming patterns that might take decades to complete a single cycle.

Blind cave-dwelling creatures come here to drink. Over millennia, they've evolved transparent bodies similar to the Choir's, though they remain simple animals. When they drink from the pool, their bodies briefly illuminate with the same impossible colors seen in the cathedral above - brief, living echoes of the Choir's dreams.

The connection between these two places - the cathedral and the pool - exists in the near-infinite network of microscopic channels that connect them. Over eons, the chemical messages of the Choir have eaten these pathways through solid rock, creating a vertical nervous system miles long. Each droplet that falls into the pool carries fragments of the Choir's eternal meditation.

But the relationship flows both ways. The pool processes these messages, breaking them down and reconstituting them in new forms. The blind cave creatures that drink here become unwitting messengers, their modified body chemistry eventually finding its way back to the cathedral through the groundwater and rising moisture. They complete a cycle that might take centuries - the Choir dreams, the dreams dissolve, the dissolved dreams are transformed, and the transformation shapes new dreams.

In this way, the Pool of Echoes serves as both archive and editor of the Choir's thoughts. It is memory made liquid, meaning made manifest. The cold that preserves it and the darkness that cradles it are as much a part of its function as the heat and light are to the cathedral above. Together, they form a system of thought that operates on a scale barely comprehensible to human minds - not because it is too complex, but because it is too patient, too permanent, too perfectly content with the eternal now of its own existence.
