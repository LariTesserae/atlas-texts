Let me tell you about what lies beyond the factory walls, in stark contrast to the Nest-Queen's domain - the Salt Plain stretches out for kilometers, a crystalline desert where nothing truly dies.

Here, the ground crunches underfoot, an endless expanse of white and pale blue crystals that grow in geometric formations. What were once industrial waste pools have evaporated over decades, leaving behind minerals that continue to bloom like frozen flowers. The air is bone-dry and sharp with chemical tang, preserving anything that falls upon the salt.

In this harsh expanse lives the Lattice Walker - a being that exists as a moving pattern in the crystal structure itself. It propagates through the salt like a wave, leaving subtle reconfigurations in its wake. Where the Nest-Queen shepherds life, the Lattice Walker preserves death in perfect stasis. Birds that fall here are slowly encrusted, becoming delicate sculptures. Scattered machine parts from the factory's active days are transformed into glittering geological specimens.

The connection between these two realms is both physical and metaphysical. A narrow zone of transition exists where the factory's moisture meets the salt plain's aridity. Here, crystals climb the outer walls like ivy, while the Nest-Queen's moss attempts to creep outward. It's a constantly shifting boundary where preservation battles with transformation.

The Nest-Queen's birds rarely venture over the Salt Plain, but when they do, they cast shadows that the Lattice Walker captures in the crystal matrix, creating momentary photographs in the salt. In return, crystalline spores occasionally drift into the factory on dry winds, adding new mineral patterns to the Nest-Queen's ongoing experiments.

The two beings acknowledge each other as opposite but complementary forces - the Nest-Queen engineering new forms of life, while the Lattice Walker maintains perfect records of the past. They are like archivists working in different mediums, each preserving their own version of time's passage.

Between them, they hold the complete story of this place - one in living memory, the other in crystalline stasis. Their domains together form a kind of temporal ecosystem, where nothing is truly lost, only transformed or preserved in new ways.
