Somewhere far below the Glass Plains, in the uttermost depths of the planet, there is another realm entirely - the Pressure Gardens. Here, at crushing depths where the mantle meets ancient magma chambers, vast networks of crystalline tubes and chambers persist in the intense heat and pressure. These structures, formed by the same primordial forces that created the Glass Plains above, house their own distinct ecosystem.

The Pressure Gardens are illuminated not by sunlight but by the soft blue glow of piezoelectricity - the incredible pressures squeezing the crystalline structures generate a constant luminescence. This light pulses in slow waves, creating a day-night cycle that exists independently of the three suns above.

The dominant life forms here are the Compression Coral - massive frameworks of living crystal that grow along the pressure gradients. Unlike their cousins in the water-catches above, these beings thrive on pressure rather than light. They build vast lattices that channel and redirect the enormous forces surrounding them, creating pockets of relative stability within their structures. These lattices form elaborate patterns that seem to follow some deep mathematical principle - like a three-dimensional language written in crystal and force.

The spaces between the Compression Coral are inhabited by the Pressure Dancers - entities that exist as organized waves of force rather than matter. They move through the crystal matrices like living sound waves, leaving complex interference patterns in their wake. These patterns are both their method of communication and their way of manipulating their environment.

The connection between the Glass Plains and the Pressure Gardens is more than just geological. The ancient structures on the surface extend deep underground, their crystalline roots reaching down into this realm. They serve as conduits, carrying information between the two worlds in the form of precisely channeled vibrations. The Glass Ghosts above and the Pressure Dancers below appear to be in constant communication through these channels, engaging in some vast, slow exchange of information whose purpose remains mysterious.

When the crystal colonies in the water-catches release their spores above, the vibrations travel down through these structures, creating sympathetic resonances in the Pressure Gardens. In response, the Compression Coral release their own propagules - tiny seed-crystals that ride the upwelling currents through the ancient conduits. Most are crushed long before they reach the surface, but those that survive emerge in the water-catches, where they may form hybrid colonies that combine traits from both realms.

The relationship between above and below appears to be cyclical and complementary. The Glass Plains capture and process energy from the three suns, while the Pressure Gardens harness the planet's geothermal forces. Together, they form a planetary-scale system of energy collection and information processing, mediated by the ancient structures that bridge them.

This grand circuit may be the true legacy of whatever civilization built the mysterious structures - not individual buildings or machines, but a world-spanning network that has evolved its own forms of life, consciousness, and purpose. The Glass Plains and Pressure Gardens are like the two hemispheres of a vast, crystalline brain, thinking thoughts too slow and alien for us to comprehend.
