The supercritical fluid serves as both memory and medium, carrying the accumulated knowledge of millennia in its molecular structure.

Physical channels wind upward through the rock, carrying the superheated fluid that feeds the surface features. These moments of transmission between realms are when the Weavers are most active in their role as shapers and recordkeepers.
