These "acoustic waveguides" channel vibrations from throughout the crystal sea into the void, where they're transformed into the complex standing waves. The helices themselves are in constant motion, their spiral arms rotating at different speeds and phases to modulate the incoming information.

This is where the Lattice Weavers' patterns achieve their purest expression. These propagate back outward through the helical waveguides, helping to coordinate the reconstruction process across vast distances. In this way, the Void Chorus helps maintain the coherence of the entire system, even as it constantly changes.

The space between these two regions - the dense crystal forests and the resonant void - is traversed by countless branching channels that carry both physical material and vibrational information.
