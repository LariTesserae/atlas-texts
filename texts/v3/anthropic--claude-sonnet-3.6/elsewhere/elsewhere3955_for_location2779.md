Let me tell you about what lies beyond the edge of the Resonance Fields, where the metal plates give way to what locals call the Still Zone. The transition isn't sudden - the thrumming doesn't stop so much as it gradually thins out, like a fog dispersing. The metal plates become smaller, more irregularly spaced, until finally they appear as isolated islands in a sea of absolute quiet.

The Still Zone is a place of perfect acoustic absorption. Sound simply... disappears. Not muffled or echoed, but consumed entirely. The ground here is covered in a dark, powdery soil that seems to drink in every vibration. Strange, bone-white plants grow in geometric formations, their stems and leaves completely rigid, as if frozen in mid-motion. They make no sound when touched or broken, and even the wind passing through them is mysteriously silenced.

The most striking features are the Glass Pillars - enormous crystalline structures that rise hundreds of feet into the air, completely transparent except for occasional bands of color that slowly shift position throughout the day. These pillars appear to be growing out of the sound-absorbing earth, though so slowly that the change is only noticeable over decades. They neither reflect nor transmit sound, existing in perfect acoustic isolation.

The connection between these two zones is complex and vital. The Still Zone acts as a necessary counterweight to the Resonance Fields, creating a gradient that helps stabilize the great thrumming. The Frequency Folk cannot enter the Still Zone directly - their consciousness simply dissipates in the absolute quiet - but they send their bird messengers to monitor its boundaries. These birds have learned to navigate without their songs, using the Glass Pillars as visual waypoints.

Between the zones lies the Interference Band, a strip of land perhaps a mile wide where the properties of both regions create unique phenomena. Here, sound behaves in impossible ways - splitting into visible colors, forming solid geometric shapes in mid-air, or traveling backward through time by fragments of seconds. The local vegetation has evolved to exploit these anomalies, developing communication systems that use quantum acoustic effects that puzzle even the Frequency Folk.

Most intriguingly, ancient records suggest that the Still Zone and the Resonance Fields were created as part of the same project - one system cannot exist without the other. The Glass Pillars are growing from something buried as deep as whatever drives the resonating plates, and their slow growth may be measuring out some vast countdown... or counting up toward something yet to come.

The Still Zone's absolute silence and the Resonance Fields' eternal thrumming create a dynamic tension that maintains some crucial balance. What that balance is protecting, or preventing, remains unknown - but the Frequency Folk treat the boundary between zones with something approaching reverence, understanding that their endless song requires its opposite to continue.
