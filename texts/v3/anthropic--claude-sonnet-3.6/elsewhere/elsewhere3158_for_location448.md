Beyond the mists that shroud the mountain city's highest reaches, across leagues of wind-scoured plateau, there lies a place of different mysteries - the Mirror Plains. Here, the ground is covered in vast sheets of salt crystal, white and gleaming, that stretch to the horizon like a frozen sea. The crystals grow in geometric patterns, hexagonal formations that range from tiny facets to plates larger than city blocks, their edges sharp enough to slice through metal.

Unlike the perpetual moisture of the mountain city, the air here is absolutely dry. The salt plains draw every trace of water from the atmosphere, creating a pristine clarity that makes distance difficult to judge. The sky above is a deeper blue than seems natural, and at night the stars reflect so perfectly in the crystalline surface that it becomes impossible to tell where earth ends and heaven begins.

Beneath the surface crystal layer, a complex network of caves and chambers extends downward, formed by the same ancient waterflows that created the salt deposits. These caves are bone-dry now, their walls encrusted with mineral formations that glow with faint bioluminescence in colors ranging from deep purple to pale green.

Here dwell the Salt Dancers - beings composed of living crystal and light. Their bodies are translucent and geometrically precise, like moving sculptures made of salt and starlight. They range in size from tiny shards that drift like snow to massive crystalline formations that pulse with internal radiance. Unlike the fluid, ever-changing Veil-Weavers, the Salt Dancers maintain rigid forms, though they can separate and recombine at will.

The connection between these two places - the fluid, misty city and the crystalline plains - lies in their shared history and ongoing exchange. The water that flows through the mountain city's channels originally came from these plains, and over millennia, it carved both places into their current forms. Now, the two sites exist in perfect opposition, one gathering moisture, the other banishing it, creating a vast atmospheric engine that drives weather patterns across the entire plateau.

The Veil-Weavers and Salt Dancers rarely meet directly - their natures are too opposed - but they communicate through the Resonators, who can traverse the dry air above the plains safely. More subtly, they exchange information through the precise mineral content of the seasonal rains that occasionally sweep the plains, and through the patterns of crystallization that form in the mountain city's deeper chambers.

Together, these two places form a complete circuit, a system of balance between water and crystal, fluid and rigid, darkness and light. The ancient builders understood this relationship, and designed both sites to work in harmony despite their apparent opposition. The beings who now inhabit these places maintain this balance, each in their own way, preserving a legacy whose full purpose they may not even fully comprehend.

Between these two points, great winds circle eternally, carrying minerals from the plains to feed the city's growth, and moisture from the city to maintain the plains' crystalline patterns. This cycle creates a unique meteorological phenomenon - storms that carry both water and crystal, their clouds shimming with suspended salt, their rain falling as both liquid and crystalline forms. These storms are how the two realms truly meet, in a dance of elements that maintains the balance of an entire world.
