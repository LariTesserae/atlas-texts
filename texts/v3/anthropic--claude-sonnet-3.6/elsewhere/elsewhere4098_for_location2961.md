Across a great distance from the garden-temple, where the ocean floor drops away into midnight depths, there exists another realm of the Sea Weavers' world - the Abyss Gardens. Here, in the crushing dark, bioluminescent structures pulse and spiral in geometric patterns that mirror the kelp forests above, but with an altogether different character.

These deep gardens are cultivated from colonies of living light - strings of photophores, carefully bred deep-sea creatures, and mineral formations that glow with natural radioactivity. The structures here are more sparse than their surface counterparts, but no less intentional. Thin cables of specialized algae, evolved to withstand extreme pressure, create vast three-dimensional webs that harvest energy from thermal vents. These webs glow with a pale, ghostly light, marking out spaces as large as the garden-temple above, but with an ethereal, almost mathematical precision.

Here dwell the Deep Weavers, cousins to their surface kin but adapted to the profound pressures and eternal night of the abyssal zone. Their bodies are more translucent than their upper-water relatives, their eyes larger and more sensitive, their movements slower and more deliberate in the dense water. Their bioluminescent symbionts produce deeper, more intense light, and their communication relies more on pressure waves than visible signals.

The connection between these two realms is maintained by the Current Riders - specialized members of both communities who navigate the water columns between depth and shallows. They follow ancient paths marked by chains of permanent whirlpools, engineered over generations through the careful placement of thermal vents and living structures. These vertical highways carry nutrients, messages, and certain hardy species of fish between the two domains.

Messages travel between the realms encoded in the genetic patterns of specially cultivated creatures - microscopic organisms that can survive the journey between pressures. The Deep Weavers send up new strains of bioluminescent life, while the surface dwellers return with evolved variants of pressure-resistant plants.

Most fascinating is the daily exchange of dreams. The psychoactive chambers of the surface temple have their counterparts in the abyss, where Deep Weavers enter meditative states in pools of naturally occurring chemicals from the thermal vents. The shared visions experienced in these spaces somehow transcend the physical distance between the communities, creating a psychological bridge that maintains their cultural unity despite their physical separation.

These two realms form a single, vertically-oriented ecosystem - the surface gardens collecting and transforming sunlight, the deep gardens harvesting energy from the earth itself. Together they create a complete circuit, a flow of energy and information that sustains both communities through their patient, centuries-long work of cultivation and evolution.
