Far below the city of the Mist Tenders, at the mountain's base where the mists finally settle into clear air, there lies a vast salt flat that stretches to the horizon. The surface is not white but a pale, luminous blue, as if the sky has fallen and crystallized. This expanse is known as the Mirror Plains.

The salt forms in geometric patterns - perfect hexagons that range from inches to yards across, their edges raised slightly like the walls of shallow basins. After the rare rains, these basins hold thin sheets of water that reflect the sky with such precision that birds sometimes mistake them for open air and attempt to dive through. The water eventually evaporates, leaving fresh layers of crystal that contribute to the intricate stratification of the salt beds.

Here dwell the Salt Dancers - tall, spare people with skin that has a crystalline quality, refracting light in subtle ways that can make them seem almost invisible against the salt flats. They move with liquid grace, their bodies adapted to traveling across the treacherous surface where a misplaced step could shatter centuries of crystal growth.

The Salt Dancers make their homes in structures that emerge from and return to the salt with each season. During the dry months, they extract blocks of salt and arrange them in spiral patterns that echo, unknowingly, the gardens of the Mist Tenders above. Water is poured over these structures at twilight, dissolving them slightly so they fuse together overnight. When the rains come, the structures slowly dissolve, their minerals returning to the flats to form new crystals.

Between these two peoples - the Mist Tenders above and the Salt Dancers below - runs a network of deep channels through the mountain rock. These are the Descending Rivers, where the water from the Mist Tenders' carefully tended channels eventually flows, picking up minerals as it travels through the sacred resonance points. When this water reaches the salt flats, it carries unique properties: the ability to create unusually perfect crystal formations, and a subtle vibration that the Salt Dancers use in their most important rituals.

The Salt Dancers do not know the source of their blessed water, believing it comes directly from the mountain's heart. The Mist Tenders, in turn, do not know where their channels ultimately lead. Yet there is an unconscious symbiosis between the two peoples - the Mist Tenders' water management affects the Salt Dancers' crystal formations, while the salt flats act as a vast purification system that eventually returns water to the mountain through deep underground aquifers.

In rare instances, during the clearest days when the mountain mists briefly part, a Mist Tender standing at the highest plateau might catch a glimpse of blue light from the Mirror Plains. And sometimes, when the air is perfectly still at dawn, a Salt Dancer might hear the faintest echo of water moving through channels far above. But neither people has ever sought out the other, perhaps understanding at some deep level that their connection is meant to remain as subtle as the vibrations in their sacred stones.

The space between them - the long fall of the Descending Rivers through darkness - remains a mystery to both, a gap that preserves the delicate balance of their separate but intertwined existences. Like the resonance points in the city above and the crystal formations below, some harmonies work best when they're felt rather than seen, when the connection remains just below the threshold of conscious understanding.
