Across the valley, where the mist thins enough to reveal glimpses of the opposite mountainside, lies the Glass Archive - a startling contrast to the organic, water-worn structures of the Mist Tenders' settlement. Here, vast geometric greenhouses rise in crystalline tiers, their surfaces kept meticulously clear despite the humid climate. The structures catch and magnify what little direct sunlight penetrates the mist, creating sharp beams that cut through the haze like solid things.

This is where the knowledge of generations is preserved - not in books that would molder in the damp, but in living specimens and careful cultivation. Each greenhouse maintains a different microclimate, precisely controlled through an intricate system of vents and heated air channels. The glass panels themselves are specially formulated, some filtering specific light wavelengths, others treated to encourage condensation on their outer surfaces while remaining dry within.

The Archive is stewarded by the Lens Keepers, scholars who have developed their own distinct adaptations to their environment. They wear close-fitting clothes of treated silk and crystal-lens goggles that help them read the subtle variations in light that indicate changes in temperature and humidity. Their fingers are stained with the specialized inks they use to etch observations directly onto glass panels, creating transparent libraries that can be read without fear of decay.

The connection between these two places is both physical and symbolic. A series of cable cars spans the valley, their metal cables treated with the same water-resistant compounds used by the Mist Tenders. These carriages transport carefully packaged specimens from the breeding terraces to the Archive's preservation chambers, and carry back essential supplies and technical knowledge.

But the true bridge between them is the mist itself. The Mist Dragons move freely between both settlements, drawn to the Archive's warmth and light while returning regularly to the terraces where they're most comfortable. They've learned to use the greenhouse vents to create precise air currents, riding them like aerial rivers between the two communities.

The relationship between Mist Tenders and Lens Keepers is one of mutual dependence and occasional tension. The Tenders provide the practical knowledge of animal care and breeding, while the Keepers maintain the technical systems that make both settlements possible. Each group believes their approach - intuitive versus systematic - is more vital to their shared mission of preservation.

During the rare moments when the mist parts enough to see both settlements at once, they appear as complementary halves of a whole - one organic and fluid, the other geometric and precise, connected by threads of metal and living streams of air, both dedicated to maintaining the delicate balance of life in this vertical world.
