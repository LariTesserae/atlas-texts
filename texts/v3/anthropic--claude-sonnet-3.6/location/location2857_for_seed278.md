The world sings in liquid crystal. Vast sheets of translucent mineral, kilometers thick, stretch toward a blazing sky where three suns phase in and out of each other. The crystal sheets are not quite solid - they flow with geological slowness, carrying bubbles of trapped sound that resonate and harmonize as they rise through the semi-fluid matrix.

The terrain undulates in great waves, peaks thrust up by some deep pneumatic process, valleys carved by the weight of the crystal masses flowing downward. Everything is caught in a state of emergence, nothing quite settled, nothing quite solid. The crystal sheets refract and reflect, creating layers of false horizons and phantom landscapes that seem to hover in the brilliant air.

Water exists here in a state of quantum uncertainty. It beads and flows across the crystal faces, but sometimes passes straight through them, emerging elsewhere transformed into geometric patterns of pure sound. These sound-water formations hang in space like shimmering wind chimes, their notes visible as ripples in the fabric of local reality.

The heat is steady and strange - not quite thermal, more like the warmth of sustained musical resonance. It radiates from the crystal sheets and collects in the valleys, where it causes reality to thin and bubble. In these areas, natural laws become suggestions. Distance and direction become relative concepts. Time moves in loops and spirals.

The only living things are the sound-beings - rare, barely-glimpsed entities that seem to form spontaneously from particularly complex harmonies. They pulse with internal light and move by manipulating the resonant frequencies of the crystal sheets, leaving trails of altered probability in their wake.

The noise is overwhelming yet ordered - a vast geological orchestra of grinding crystal, humming water-sound, the pulse of probability waves, and the deep bass rumble of reality trying to maintain its integrity. Individual sounds combine and separate, forming temporary structures of pure tone that collapse back into the ambient roar.

The scale is difficult to comprehend, as distance itself is unstable. The crystal landscape might cover a continent, or it might be infinite, or it might be folded through higher dimensions. The horizon, when visible through the refracted light and probability distortions, seems to curve both away and toward the observer simultaneously.

Everything is in constant motion, though at widely varying speeds. Crystal flows like honey, water transforms into sound, sound solidifies into temporary geometries, and probability itself flows like a tide. The three suns dance their eternal pattern overhead, their light creating ever-changing interference patterns in the fabric of space-time.

There are places where the crystal sheets have formed natural amplifiers, vast parabolic structures that focus and intensify the world's endemic harmonies. In these locations, reality becomes particularly thin and malleable. New laws of physics precipitate out of the resonant frequencies like crystal formations, exist briefly, and dissolve back into possibility.

The air - if it can be called air - is thick with potential, humming with un-collapsed quantum states. Every movement sends ripples through multiple dimensions of reality, creating cascading harmonies that might, in their complex interactions, give birth to new sound-beings or temporary pocket universes.

This is a place of perpetual becoming, where the boundary between matter and energy, between sound and substance, between what is and what might be, remains forever unresolved. It sings itself into existence moment by moment, an endless symphony of emergence and dissolution, played out on a scale that makes mockery of conventional measure.
