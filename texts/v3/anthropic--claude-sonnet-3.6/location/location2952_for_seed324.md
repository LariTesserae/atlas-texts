Deep in liquid-filled chambers, the Nursery grows. Here, in these cathedral-sized spaces, new geometry emerges from carefully maintained solutions - a concentrated brine rich with dissolved minerals and microscopic crystalline seeds. The liquid itself has a thick, syrupy quality, moving in slow currents that carry an opalescent sheen.

The walls are not walls as we know them, but rather densely-packed columns of partially-formed crystal, each one humming at its own frequency as it grows. These vibrations combine into a constant deep chorus that reverberates through the viscous medium - sometimes a drone, sometimes rising to near-deafening crescendos as growth phases align.

Geometric shapes push out from the crystalline matrices - cubes birthing smaller cubes, spirals unfurling from hexagonal cores, fractal patterns emerging and complexifying in real-time. The forms appear to be guided by invisible templates, as if following some ancient architectural program embedded in the mineral structure itself.

In the dim light that filters through the liquid - sourced from bioluminescent cultures carefully maintained in upper chambers - the new growth glints and flashes. Occasional larger creatures, something like blind translucent eels, wind their way through the wider channels between growth zones, their bodies evolved or engineered to tend this strange mineral garden.

The temperature remains precisely controlled, warm enough to keep the growth-solutions active but not so hot as to destabilize the more delicate formations. Currents move in established patterns, carrying nutrients and growth catalysts where they're needed, sweeping away waste products through barely-visible channels in the crystal matrices.

This is not a natural space - every aspect is regulated, from the chemical composition of the liquid to the resonant frequencies that guide crystal formation. Yet there are no obvious machines or tools. The entire system seems to operate through carefully balanced natural processes, more like a coral reef than a factory, though clearly designed for a purpose.

Periodically, sections of mature crystal detach from their growth sites with a deep ringing sound that sets the whole chamber trembling. These completed segments drift upward through specially designed channels, making their way to whatever purpose or place they were grown for, while new growth immediately begins in the spaces left behind.

The whole chamber feels like both a birth canal and a song made solid - an architectural womb where the line between grown and built dissolves in the warm, humming dark.
