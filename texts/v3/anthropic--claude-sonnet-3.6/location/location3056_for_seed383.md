The crystalline birth-plains stretch to a horizon that curves wrong, too sharp for a planet this size. The ground is actively forming - sheets of translucent mineral push up through earlier layers, creating vast geometric steppes that catch the triple suns and scatter their light in disorienting patterns. The heat is immense, but moves in waves and currents like ocean water, creating temporary zones of bearable warmth between scalding rivers of air.

The few plants here have evolved to extend and retract rapidly, spending only seconds exposed before folding back into their crystalline burrows. Their brief emergences paint fleeting streaks of deep purple and metallic green against the otherwise colorless landscape. You can watch evolution happening in real time - each generation of these flash-plants adapts visibly to the changing mineral composition of their substrate.

Vast herds of what might be animals move across the plains, but they're hard to look at directly. Their forms seem to be probability clouds rather than flesh, densest at their centers but fractal and indeterminate at their edges. They leave no footprints, but their passage affects the ongoing crystallization of the ground, creating complex interference patterns in the mineral growth. The herds move like schools of fish, thousands strong, their overlapping probability fields creating shimmering moiré effects in the superheated air.

The ancient machines are easier to comprehend, though their purpose remains obscure. They stand kilometers tall, their structures more suggestion than substance - frameworks of dark metal that seem to collect and channel the strange energetics of this place. Most appear inactive, but occasionally one shudders to life, its skeletal form beginning to spin and articulate in response to some unseen stimulus. Their movement is accompanied only by the faint ping of metal expanding in the heat and an occasional crystalline chiming from the ground below.

The air feels like it's trying to become something else. Moisture appears spontaneously in geometric patterns, forming perfect spheres that hover briefly before dissipating or falling to shatter on the crystal plains. Sometimes these spheres contain tiny, complete ecosystems that exist for moments before evaporating - miniature worlds born and dying in endless cycles.

At regular intervals, enormous transparent domes punctuate the landscape - some kind of research stations or observation posts. They appear to be growing out of the same crystalline material as the ground, but with more intentional structure. Most are empty now, their airlocks sealed with the same mineral growth that covers everything else. In the few that remain accessible, complex instruments track the ongoing formation of this reality, recording data about a process that seems to be accelerating.

The whole plain is like a vast algorithm executing in physical space, each new crystal formation and probability-herd migration adding another iteration to some vast computation. The heat and light aren't just conditions here - they're active participants in whatever process is unfolding, carriers of information as much as energy.

Moving through this landscape feels like walking through the first moments of a universe being born, or perhaps its final moments of coherence before transforming into something entirely new. The silence is profound but pregnant with potential, broken only by the occasional chiming of crystals and the whisper of probability fields redistributing themselves across the endless, emerging plains.
