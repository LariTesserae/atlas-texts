Here at altitude, the city spreads in crystalline fractals across the plateau - not crystal as metaphor, but genuine angular growth in copper and silicon, branching and rebranching in precise mathematical ratios. The structures grow slowly upward through vapor deposition, atom by atom, like inverse snowflakes reaching toward the pale sun.

The beings who tend this place move in perfect geometric patterns through hollow channels within the crystal matrix. Their bodies are collections of metallic spheres joined by electromagnetic fields, rolling and reconfiguring as needed. They leave faint trails of inducted current in their wake, subtle patterns that other sphere-beings can read like literature or music.

The air is thin and cool, carrying an electric charge that makes one's skin (if one has skin) prickle. Occasional arcs of static discharge leap between crystal spires, accompanied by soft clicks and pops that echo through the latticed corridors. These discharges are part of the city's metabolism - power distribution through a living semiconductor nervous system.

Where crystal structures meet the underlying rock, they grip through roots of molecular machines that slowly transform the mineral substrate into more crystal city. The process releases trace amounts of noble gases that rise through the matrix in glowing threads, their soft illumination mixing with the diffuse sunlight that filters through the translucent upper layers.

The plateau itself is young, thrust up by recent tectonic activity, its edges still sharp and unstable. But the crystal city is slowly stabilizing it, extending delicate fibers deep into the rock like mycelium, analyzing and adjusting, preventing slides and settling.

In the gaps between major crystal formations, swarms of tiny machines move like schools of fish, maintaining and modifying the structures according to plans too vast for human minds to grasp. Their movements create a constant subliminal hum just at the edge of hearing, punctuated by occasional crystalline pings as new branches nucleate and begin to grow.

The scale is difficult to comprehend - the crystal city covers an area that might once have been a small nation, its fractal complexity maintaining the same precise patterns from the microscopic scale up to structures kilometers high. There is no empty space; where one branch ends, another begins, all of it suffused with purpose and activity.

Sometimes, in the deeper layers, ancient artifacts are found - bones, metal tools, fragments of simpler technologies. The crystal city simply grows around these, incorporating them into its structure like insects in amber. The sphere-beings study these remnants with what might be curiosity, though their thoughts are as alien as their forms.

At certain times, when the angle of sunlight is just right, the entire city seems to shimmer with internal light, its countless facets and channels creating a display that suggests consciousness on a vast scale. Perhaps it is speaking, or dreaming, or simply continuing its endless mathematical growth toward some incomprehensible perfection.
