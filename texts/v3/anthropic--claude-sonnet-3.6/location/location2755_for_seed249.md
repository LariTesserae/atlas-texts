The air shimmers with heat, but not like any desert you've known. This heat has weight, presence - it moves in vast, slow currents across the continental expanse. The currents are visible as subtle distortions, like looking through old glass, their boundaries marked by threads of condensation that form and disperse in endless patterns.

The ground is new here. Not young like volcanic islands, but new in a stranger way - plains of some crystalline material that seems to grow rather than form, advancing in geometric patterns that could be organic or mathematical or both. The crystal-plains are mostly flat but rise occasionally into sharp, geometric mesas that catch and split the heat-currents. Their surfaces are warm to the touch and seem to pulse very slowly, as if breathing.

Scattered across this vast crystalline landscape are pools of what looks like ordinary water but behaves like something else - remaining perfectly still despite the heat-currents, reflecting nothing, occasionally rising into temporary columns that twist upward before collapsing back with no sound. The pools appear and disappear over days or weeks, leaving behind patches of lush, dark vegetation that grows with unnatural speed and precision, forming perfect fractals of deep green and purple.

The only consistent sounds are the subtle hum of distant machinery and the whisper of the heat-currents. Massive industrial structures rise at seemingly random intervals - not ruins, but not quite buildings either. They appear to be some kind of environmental processors, their functions inscrutable but clearly active. Their surfaces shift and reconfigure slowly, like the crystal-plains but with more purpose. Pipes and conduits extend from them into the ground, possibly connecting to others thousands of miles away. Steam or vapor occasionally vents from their upper sections, adding to the complex atmospheric patterns above.

The few animals here are strange and purposeful - something like giant translucent millipedes that appear during certain atmospheric conditions to harvest the fractal vegetation, crystalline flying creatures that seem to feed on the heat-currents themselves, colonial organisms that might be plants or might be animals, forming and dissolving like living clouds at the intersections of thermal boundaries.

Everything moves here, but slowly - the crystal-plains extending, the processors reconfiguring, the pools migrating, the vegetation growing and being consumed. It's like watching plate tectonics sped up just enough to be visible, but with unfamiliar rules. The landscape feels like it's conducting some vast computational process, using heat and water and crystal as its medium, operating at a scale that makes its purpose impossible to grasp.

The heat-currents grow stronger as the day progresses, until they're substantial enough to lift small crystals into the air, creating sheets of glittering particles that dance in complex patterns before settling in new configurations. As they move, they catch the light in ways that seem to bend local spacetime, creating brief, impossible geometries that vanish when looked at directly.

It's an industrial process at planetary scale, perhaps, or an alien ecology, or mathematics made physical - or all of these at once, operating according to rules that seem just at the edge of comprehension.
