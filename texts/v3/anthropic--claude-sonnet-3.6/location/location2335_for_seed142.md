The city hangs in perpetual dawn, though there is no sun here - just an ambient pearl-white brightness that seems to emanate from the very air. The ancient plateau supporting it stretched flat eons ago, worn to near-perfect levelness by forces that might once have been rain and wind, though such mundane erosion seems impossible in this place now.

The buildings rise like crystal formations, their surfaces simultaneously liquid and solid - a transparent material that seems to constantly condense out of the moist air yet never quite finishes becoming matter. Water beads and flows across every surface, but the droplets move with deliberate purpose, as if each is a tiny machine. The structures themselves defy conventional geometry, their edges soft yet precise, merging and diverging in patterns that suggest an architecture conceived by mathematical principles from beyond human comprehension.

Between these fluid-solid towers, broad spaces stretch empty and still. The ground is a dark, glassy expanse broken by patches of impossibly green vegetation - not grass exactly, but something that shares grass's fundamental idea while expressing it through crystalline structures that chime softly when brushed. These plants never grow or die, locked in perfect stasis like everything else here.

The air is cool and water-heavy, thick with floating motes that might be fog or might be swarms of microscopic machines. They form shifting patterns that seem meaningful, like a language written in three dimensions, before dissolving again into formless haze. Occasionally these motes coalesce into larger forms - suggestions of animals that pad silently through the empty spaces or glide between buildings before dispersing back into the ambient cloud.

Despite its scale, the city is preternaturally quiet. The only consistent sound is a low harmonic hum at the edge of hearing, punctuated by the gentle tinking of water droplets completing their programmed paths and the crystalline chimes of the pseudo-vegetation. The hum seems to emanate from everywhere and nowhere, as if it's the sound of reality itself maintaining this impossible place.

There are no streets as such - the spaces between buildings seem to shift subtly when not directly observed, and movement through the city follows laws of topology rather than simple geometry. Patches of ground sometimes become transparent, revealing deeper layers of the plateau that contain strange machinery grown from the same liquid-crystal as the buildings above, pulsing with patterns of light that might be data or might be life, or might be something that renders that distinction meaningless.

Nothing here ever changes, yet nothing stays still. The water flows in perfect cycles, the mist-creatures form and dissolve in endlessly repeating patterns, the buildings remain eternal while seeming to flow like slow honey. It's a place that feels less built than grown or perhaps calculated - as if some vast intelligence worked out the final, perfect form of a city and then froze it at the exact moment of its solution, a moment that now stretches forever in all directions.

The plateau extends far beyond the city proper, but distance becomes an increasingly abstract concept near the edges of perception. The horizon, when it can be perceived at all, seems to curve in directions that human eyes were never meant to process. Scattered across the plain are other structures or entities that resist description - things that might be buildings or machines or living creatures or pure mathematics given form, their surfaces rippling with the same liquid-crystal substance that makes up everything else in this eternal moment.
