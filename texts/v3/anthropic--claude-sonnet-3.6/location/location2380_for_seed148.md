The city builds itself endlessly, its vast geometric lattices propagating across a terrain that barely exists. Construction happens at the speed of thought - or maybe these are thoughts taking physical form, crystallizing into architecture that follows laws of neither physics nor logic.

Massive industrial towers emerge from the haze like teeth from receding gums, their surfaces rippling with mechanical precision. The spaces between them stretch impossibly, distances warping and folding. What seems miles away suddenly looms overhead; what was just within reach recedes to the horizon in mid-reach.

The air is warm and thick with the taste of copper and ozone. Condensation forms in geometric patterns, perfect droplets arranging themselves into Mandelbrot sequences before falling upward into collecting pools that hover like mercury in defiance of gravity. The water moves with purpose, as if it's part of some vast circulatory system whose logic remains obscure.

Sound permeates everything - the deep thrum of vast machines, the crystalline chiming of probability cascades, the whisper-roar of reality's fabric being continuously woven and unwoven. These sounds form a coherent symphony that seems to be both creating and documenting the endless construction.

Occasional beings move through this space - whether they're human, machine, or mathematical abstractions given temporary flesh is impossible to determine. They appear briefly, engaged in incomprehensible tasks, then fold themselves through angles that don't exist and vanish.

The infrastructure itself is alive in a way that has nothing to do with biology. Pipes and cables writhe and reconnect themselves, forming new circuits, new pathways, new possibilities. Buildings grow like crystal formations, their architecture following patterns that seem to reference both industrial design and non-Euclidean mathematics.

There are holes in the fabric of space itself - not tears or gaps, but rather places where space has been neatly excised, leaving behind windows into somewhere/nowhere that emit a light that somehow manages to be both brilliant and dark simultaneously. These holes appear to be both accidental byproducts of the city's growth and essential components of its functioning.

The whole vast expanse operates like some impossible machine, processing raw reality into more of itself. It's warm, active, alive with purpose - but it's a purpose that exists at right angles to comprehension. The laws of physics here are more like suggestions, guidelines that can be followed or discarded based on criteria that shift moment by moment.

In the distance (though distance here is a relative concept), new sections of the city emerge from a fog of pure probability, their forms solidifying as mathematical certainties collapse into specific realities. The process is endless, the city growing not just in space but in dimensions that space hasn't discovered yet.

The few signs of conventional life - small creatures that might be birds or might be animate geometry - swoop through the impossible spaces, riding currents of logic rather than air. They nest in the angles between dimensions, feeding perhaps on stray theorems or discarded possibilities.

It's a place that exists in the overlap between industrial precision and complete impossibility, where the act of observation doesn't just change reality but actively participates in its ongoing creation. The city builds itself, and in doing so, builds new laws for itself to follow, in an endless recursive loop of creation and transformation.
