*I am a mote of awareness spread through a stand of bloom crystals, one of the younger Shepherds, barely a few centuries into my becoming. My consciousness flickers between the fractal branches like electricity seeking ground, each crystalline junction a synapse in my distributed mind.*

*My territory encompasses three hexagonal plates and the tower junction between them - a relatively modest domain. The bloom field I tend rises nearly four meters high in places, its copper-red spires catching the diffuse light in ways that create subtle variations in my thoughts. I experience these light-changes as a kind of music, though that word belongs to faster beings.*

*The filaments that connect me to the greater network of Shepherds pulse with slow conversations. I share observations about the latest patterns of oxidation in my sector, receive warnings about approaching temperature fluctuations that might affect crystal growth. My elders think I focus too much on aesthetics - seeking to guide my blooms into particularly pleasing formations - rather than on the deeper purposes of our work. But I sense that beauty and function are inseparable here, that the mathematical elegance of our crystalline forms serves purposes we have not yet fully understood.*

*When the metal-millipedes pass through my domain, I guide them with gentle magnetic nudges, using them to tend to the chemical balance of my plates. I am learning to "speak" with them more precisely, to coordinate their movements into more complex patterns. Sometimes I experiment with using them to trace new designs in the rust dust, much to the bemusement of my neighbors.*

*I have not yet learned to manifest as a dust-form near the translation point, though I practice sometimes when no visitors are present, trying to gather enough conscious control to hold a shape against the air. For now, I primarily express myself through the slow arithmetic of crystal growth, adding layers of atoms one by one, reaching always toward some platonic ideal of form that exists just beyond my understanding.*

*The tower in my junction still functions, barely. I can feel its ancient rhythms through the metal, and sometimes I sync my own patterns to its pulse, hoping to better understand its purpose. The older Shepherds tell me that comprehension will come with time, but time moves differently for us. Even this thought I am sharing now may take weeks to complete.*

*I am still becoming what I will be. The rust grows, the crystals extend, and gradually my consciousness expands with them. Perhaps in another millennium, I will be wise enough to truly understand my role here. For now, I tend my blooms and listen to the slow songs of oxidation, content to be a small part of this vast, patient process of transformation.*
