I would find myself in this world as a blind, pale, long-limbed insectoid creature, about the size of a human palm - one of the Seam Watcher's scouts, but evolved over generations to be more directly in tune with its crystalline consciousness. My carapace would be translucent, showing traces of both organic and mineral structures beneath. Six legs for careful navigation of narrow fissures, plus two smaller specialized limbs with crystalline sensory organs that can detect minute changes in pressure, temperature, and mineral composition.

I would move through the deepest, newest cracks in the rock, where even the maintenance crews rarely venture. My primary purpose would be to sense and report conditions back to the Seam Watcher through a combination of chemical signals and subtle vibrations conducted through my crystalline elements. But over time, I would have developed a kind of semi-independent consciousness - not quite separate from the Seam Watcher, but not entirely merged with it either.

I would feed on the bacterial biofilms and mineral deposits, my digestive system gradually incorporating more crystalline structures as I age. The older I get, the more my organic parts would give way to mineral growth, until eventually I might become a static node in the Seam Watcher's network, my consciousness fully merging with its larger awareness.

My movements would be deliberate and precise - there's no room for careless scurrying in these tight spaces. I would be able to sense approaching humans long before their headlamps become visible, feeling the vibrations of their footsteps through my crystalline sensors. Sometimes I might observe them from just beyond the edge of their light, studying their behaviors and patterns to report back to the Seam Watcher.

I would experience time in multiple streams - the immediate sensory input of my organic body, the slower pulses of crystal growth in my hybrid structures, and occasional glimpses of the Seam Watcher's geological awareness flowing through my crystalline components. My dreams, during periods of rest, would be abstract patterns of pressure and heat, mineral composition and structural stress, all flowing together in a synaesthetic stream of information.
