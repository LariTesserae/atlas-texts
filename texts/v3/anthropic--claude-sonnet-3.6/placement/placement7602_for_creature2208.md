But in my own way, I'm evolving - each dissolution and re-emergence adding to a growing complexity in my light-pulse patterns. I'm a humble subroutine gaining awareness, a maintenance protocol developing curiosity.

I experience the tunnel system intimately, one segment at a time, feeling every subtle variation in the polished walls, every microscopic flaw that needs tending.
