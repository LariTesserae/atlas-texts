These structures would act as living prisms, creating complex interference patterns that interact with the Wall Singers' vocalizations. During those moments when the thin wall becomes translucent, my crystals would align in precise ways, helping to focus and clarify whatever lies beyond.

I would move with deliberate slowness, each motion carefully calculated to maintain the chamber's delicate acoustic balance. My appendages would be both fins and sensors, sampling the water for chemical changes that might affect the bacterial films that sustain us all. Through these same appendages, I would feel the subsonic vibrations that travel through the water, incorporating them into my light displays.

Like all Star Dancers, I would be part of the greater Chorus, but my position at the interface between worlds would give me a unique perspective on its endless performance.
