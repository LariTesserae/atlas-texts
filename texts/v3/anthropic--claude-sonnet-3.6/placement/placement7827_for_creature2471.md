My own form shifts between these levels of existence - sometimes dispersing into a cloud of mathematical points to interface directly with the crystal networks, other times condensing into more defined shapes to communicate with the other beings.

There's a deep satisfaction in this role - in being part of this vast, ongoing calculation while maintaining enough individual awareness to appreciate its beauty. I imagine experiencing time not as a linear flow but as layers of intersecting patterns, each moment a facet in a larger crystalline structure of meaning.

Other times, I would drift among the higher lattices, my consciousness dispersed through the network, existing as pure geometric thought. Always, though, I would be adding to and learning from the great crystalline theorem that is this transformed town's reason for being.
