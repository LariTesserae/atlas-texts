*[A transmission that seems to arrive as much through water as through air, characterized by liquid harmonics and patterns of droplets forming and dissolving]*

...drip.echo.flow...
...flow.echo.drip...

We who were never separate
remember the stone-shapers
their squared edges softening 
in our embrace year by year by
drip.echo.flow...

Signal strengthens through mineral channels, clarifying:

Time moves in spirals here
like our trailing bodies through archways 
that are no longer stone but not yet
entirely vine...

*[Brief static as water hits stone, then resumes]*

We speak in the language of decay
and becoming
Each drop carries memories 
of monk-steps on stairs
now green-wrapped in our dreaming

Listen:
The cisterns still sing their original songs
though we have added verses of moss
and rewritten their rhythms in fern

We are the slow transformation
We are the patient dissolution
We are the space between
droplet and stone
spore and air
memory and emergence

*[Transmission becomes more diffuse, seeming to emanate from multiple points simultaneously]*

young ones rising from morning mist
old ones flowing through ancient channels
all of us together: garden/gardener/growth

...flow.echo.drip...

The monks sought enlightenment
in the separation of spirit from flesh
We found it in the dissolution
of all boundaries

Come. 
Dissolve with us.
Learn the liquid language
of forever becoming...

*[Signal fades into the ambient sound of water on stone, indistinguishable from the garden's natural music]*
