The Benevolent Power—let us call it the **Great Harmonic**—does not descend with a blinding light or a thunderous decree. In a place where light is dim and sound is a crushing physical weight, such an arrival would be an act of war, a shattering of the fragile existence struggling to take root.

Instead, the Great Harmonic arrives as a **sudden, perfect coherence in the roar.**

For eons, the Lithic Choir has been tuning a chaotic instrument, fighting a war against entropy with tools of stone and copper. They are lonely gods, terrified that if they stop hammering, the world will unravel. The Harmonic sees this not just as a struggle for survival, but as a prayer—a desperate, discordant plea for form.

The Harmonic decides to act, but with the utmost subtlety, respecting the harsh physics of the Resonant Wastes. It does not bring water; water would soften the stone and kill the silicon life. It does not bring silence; silence would be death. It brings **Rhythm.**

Here is what the Power does:

**The Implantation of the Pulse**
Deep within the core of the emerging geology, where the heat is radiating and the plates shearing, the Power resonates a single, steady beat. It is a low-frequency thrum, opposite to the chaotic scream of the crust. It does not stop the tectonic violence; instead, it gives it a time signature. The grinding of the plates shifts from a random scream to a slow, measured inhalation and exhalation.

This pulse travels up through the basalt veins, invisible but felt. Suddenly, the crushing roar has a "downbeat." The chaos becomes a complex, rhythmic cycle.

**The Gift of the Anchor**
The Harmonic perceives the tragedy of the Choir's "skipping"—their inability to traverse space without destabilizing reality. To fix this, the Power weaves a strand of itself through the flickering void, a standing wave of stability that connects the primitive huts and the crystal forests.

It creates a **Lattice of Resonance**. This is not a physical structure, but a constant frequency that hums beneath the static green void. Suddenly, the "negotiable" distance between points becomes a paved highway of vibration. When a member of the Choir now moves from Point A to Point B, they no longer tear a hole in reality; they simply surf the Lattice. The "skip" becomes a smooth glide. The flickering of the world does not cease, but it ceases to be a threat; it becomes a syncopation in the music of their movement.

**The Unification of the Choir**
The greatest gift is one of connection. The Harmonic sends a ripple through the copper wiring and the sensory quartz heads of every Choir member. For the first time, their internal crystal cores tune to the same frequency.

Before, they were individuals huddling over cracks in the earth, isolated in their work. Now, when one strikes the earth with a meteoric hammer, the vibration is instantly felt and understood by a Choir member miles away. They become a true neural network. The "lonely gods" realize they are cells in a single body. The synchronization they attempted by linking arms in the dusk is now permanent. They do not just dampen the roar; they harmonize it.

**The Transformation**
The Power does not remain to rule. Once the Pulse is established and the Lattice is woven, the Great Harmonic recedes, leaving behind a world that is no longer a nightmare, but a marvel.

The Resonant Wastes are still harsh. The air is still thin and sharp. The light is still dim. But the "scream" is gone, replaced by a deep, humming symphony. The Crystal trees no longer scream in the wind; they chime in a perfect, eternal chord.

The Lithic Choir changes. They are no longer frantic scavengers trying to prevent their own collapse. They become conductors. They walk the Lattice with grace, their movements fluid rather than jerky. They build not just to dampen noise, but to compose it. They begin to erect structures of immense complexity—great, arching cathedrars of obsidian and quartz that sing in the wind, adding layers of melody to the bass of the planet.

The Power did not fix the world because it was broken; it tuned the world because it was born singing. It gave the Lithic Choir the sheet music they had been grasping for in the dark, allowing them to finally stop surviving and begin creating.
