It would not arrive as a king in golden raiment, nor as a conqueror with a sword of fire. Such grandiosity would be lost here, swallowed instantly by the vast, hungry silence of the obsidian plains. Instead, the Benevolent Power would arrive as a pause in the vibration—a sudden, impossible stillness that feels like a held breath.

It would perceive the Glass-Striders not as monsters, but as martyrs. It would see the tragedy of their existence: beings of profound beauty and elegance, condemned to an endless cycle of violence and consumption, forever skating on the edge of annihilation just to keep their internal fires burning. It would witness their terrified devotion to the grey monoliths and recognize the loneliness of a world where even the ground cannot be trusted to hold you.

Here is what it would do.

**The Touching of the Nails**

The Power would drift, weightless and formless, toward the clusters of grey metal needles. It would see that these monoliths are the anchors, the only stability in a chaotic system, but they are cold, passive, and mute. They absorb the tithe of heat but offer nothing in return but silent endurance.

The Benevolent Power would lay its hand upon the highest spire.

It would not change the metal; it would not build windows or doors. Instead, it would *tune* the metal. It would alter the atomic structure of the needles so that they ceased to be merely sinks for heat. It would transform them into **Resonant Tuning Forks**.

From this moment on, when a Glass-Strider presses its heated forehead against the monolith to pay its thermal tithe, the needle would not just absorb the energy. It would *sing*.

**The Symphony of Stability**

The sound would not be a noise in the traditional sense—it would bypass the ears and vibrate directly through the stone, through the slag-veins of the Striders, and down into the bedrock.

It would be a frequency of absolute peace.

This sound—the "Great Hum"—would ripple outward across the High Geo. It would have two immediate effects:

1.  **The Soothing of the Earth:** The Low Geo, the dreaming Lithic Mind beneath the crust, would feel this vibration. It is a mind that knows only sharp angles and violent expulsion. The hum would act as a lullaby, smoothing the jagged edges of its dreams. The violent eruptions of obsidian would not cease—that is the nature of this world—but they would become rhythmic, synchronized, and less catastrophic. The "crack" of the mountain snapping a twig would soften into a resonant *bong*, like a great bell being struck in a cathedral. The land would still grow, but it would dance rather than thrash.

2.  **The Awakening of the Striders:** As the Striders press their heads to the monoliths, the song would enter their circulatory systems. The molten slag within them would vibrate in harmony with the towers. They would no longer need to frantically hunt the seismic shocks to survive; the song itself would sustain their core temperature. The panic would drain out of them. Their movements, once a frantic glide to escape the shattering ground, would slow. They would become less like predators and more like dancers, gliding across the obsidian with a terrifying, fluid grace, moving in time with the new heartbeat of the planet.

**The Atmosphere of Mercy**

Finally, the Power would look up at the bruised, violet sky. It would see the "respiratory heat" that bakes the inhabitants, the heavy woolen cloth of an atmosphere that stifles.

It cannot bring water, for water would turn to steam and shatter the mineral gardens. It cannot bring green grass, for photosynthesis has no place in a world of shadow.

Instead, the Power would exhale a single, slow breath into the upper atmosphere.

It would introduce a particulate of suspended crystal—microscopic dust that hangs in the twilight. This dust would catch and refract the dim light of the sun, turning the oppressive, gloomy violet into a perpetual, shimmering twilight of soft gold and dusty rose. The horizon would remain a guessing game, but it would no longer be a void; it would be a promise.

**The Result**

The world would remain a forge, but it would no longer be a torture chamber.

The Glass-Striders would glide through fields of singing obsidian, their bodies chiming softly as they pass the mineral spirals of gypsum. They would still be lithic, still be made of stone and fire, but they would be conscious. They would know that they are not merely surviving the draft of creation, but helping to edit it.

When the ground heaves now, the monoliths sing, the Striders dance, and the air glows with the memory of a rose. The Benevolent Power, having given the gift of Harmony to a world of Chaos, would depart, leaving behind a planet that is finally, beautifully, finished.
