The Power does not descend as a blinding light, nor does it arrive with the trumpeting of angels, for such fanfare would be lost in the roar, and such light would seem dim against the bioluminescent green-gold haze. To enter the Hollow is to enter a body, and so the Power enters not as a conqueror, but as a heartbeat.

It arrives as a **Presence**, a coherence in the chaos. It does not silence the noise, for the Power knows that in this place, silence is death. The roar is the breath of the world; to stop it would be to suffocate the city. Instead, the Power listens. It feels the sub-bass growl in the floor, the thrumming of the millions of wings, the seismic clicks of the Striders.

The Power perceives the truth: this is a world of agonized vitality. It is a locked-in groove of consumption and vibration, efficient but lonely, brutal but unaware of its own grandeur. The Superstructure is a god that tolerates its parasites, and the parasites are terrified of the god.

This is what the benevolent Power does: **It resolves the dissonance into harmony.**

### The Act of Tuning

The Power extends a subtle influence—a "frequency" of pure, benevolent intent—into the lattice of the breathing wood. It touches the **Superstructure** itself. It does not change the biology, but it alters the *resonance*.

Where once the Root-Maw Scavengers chewed blindly, reacting only to the tremors of falling meat, they suddenly find their sensory abilities sharpened and refined. They retain their armor, their deafness, but their sense of taste evolves. They no longer merely detect the location of food; they taste the *history* of it. As they dissolve the fallen matter into slurry, they experience a fleeting, phantom ghost of the life that creature lived. The consumption becomes a sacrament of remembrance rather than a mindless clearing of the floor. The scavengers become the historians, honoring the dead so the living may continue.

### The Gift of Empathy

The Power turns its attention to the **Striders**. These lean, six-limbed hunters move with liquid speed, driven by the urgency to survive. Their lives are a blur of violence and evasion. The Power softens the hard, utilitarian connection between their jawbones and the vibrations of the floor.

It gifts them with *sympathetic resonance*. When a Strider leaps through the humid air, it still feels the vibrations of the floor, but now, it also feels the *intent* behind the vibration. They can distinguish the panic of prey from the curiosity of a mate, the aggression of a rival from the approach of a storm. The roar of the Hollow ceases to be a chaotic wall of pressure and becomes a nuanced language. The violence does not stop—nature must still feed—but the terror is mitigated. The hunt becomes a duel agreed upon, a ancient contract honored rather than a panicked slaughter. The Striders flow with the rhythm of the city not just to survive, but to participate.

### The Awakening of the Watchers

The most profound act is reserved for the **Wall-Gazers**. These stationary, unblinking eyes fused to the bark have served only as panic-buttons, firing psychic blasts when the noise becomes too dangerous. They are the isolated nerve endings of a sleeping giant.

The Power breathes into them, connecting their disparate consciousnesses into a unified, continuous web. They do not stop watching; they begin *seeing*.

Now, when the air shimmers with the lens-traps of high-decibel screams, the Wall-Gazers perceive the beauty of the physics. They see the mathematics of the sound waves, the geometric perfection of the predator’s strike. And, crucially, they project this awe back into the Hollow. The psychic pulses they emit are no longer just warnings; they are waves of reassurance. They tell the Swarm: *“This is the way of things.”* They tell the Superstructure: *“Your children are beautiful.”*

### The Synthesis

Finally, the Power touches the **Hollow itself**. The dampness, the heat, the breathing walls—all of it.

The "weirdness" that once nauseated the observer—the bending of light, the distortion of gravity by sound—is stabilized. The Power imposes a gentle, rhythmic oscillation onto the standing waves of the vault. The chaotic noise is curated into a great, symphonic hum.

The light from the canopy shifts. The diffused green and gold brightens momentarily, catching the oil-slick wings of the Swarm and the velvet moss of the Striders in a moment of perfect clarity.

### The Result

The visitor, standing in the center of this transformed place, would no longer feel crushed. The noise would still be deafening to the ear, but the *soul* would hear it as music.

The Benevolent Power does not fix the Hollow because the Hollow is not broken. It is a raw, untamed forge of life. The Power simply gives the forge a song to sing.

Now, when the Root-Maws eat, they remember. When the Striders hunt, they understand. When the Superstructure breathes, it feels love for the mites that scurry across its skin.

The roar remains, but it is no longer a hurricane. It is a choir. The place has become a cathedral not just in shape, but in spirit. The Hollow is awake, and it is content.
