The Benevolent Power does not arrive with a thunderclap, nor does the sky split open to admit a blinding radiance. Such displays are too sharp, too violent for this place of soft basalt and heavy mist. Instead, the Power arrives as a sudden, perfect stillness—a pocket of absolute silence within the rhythmic *hiss-pause-hiss* of the vents.

It descends not as a giant, but as a presence, a vibration that feels like the hum of the earth before a quake, though there is no destruction here. It moves through the thick, chewable air like a golden current, indifferent to the gravity that chains the Ground-Breakers to the mud.

The Power observes. It sees the "factory" of nature, the brutal efficiency of the heat and the hydraulic song. It sees the life struggling against the weight of the atmosphere, the perpetual dampness that rots and renews in the same breath. And where a lesser power might try to cool the heat or dry the stone, this wise entity knows better. It sees that the suffering here is not a flaw; it is the forge. The pressure is what carved the barrel chests of the herbivores and the silent patience of the Stalker.

So, the Power decides to do the one thing this heavy, isolated place lacks: **It grants the landscape the ability to remember.**

The High-Sponge is a cycle of water and steam, a process that repeats endlessly but forgets itself the moment the mist evaporates. The Power, therefore, reaches out a hand—not of flesh, but of pure intent—and plunges it into the heart of the weeping rock.

The change is subtle, seismic in its quietude.

**The Gift of Resonance**
The Power strikes a chord upon the geothermal vents. It adjusts the pitch of the *industrial pulse*. From this moment forward, the hissing of the steam and the thrumming of the water chutes will carry a subsonic frequency—a deep, benevolent thrum that vibrates in the bone of every creature.

*   **For the Ground-Breakers:** The vibration soothes the ache in their heavy, barrel-like chests. The air is still thick, but the new hum makes it easier to process, a metronome that guides their breathing so they never tire, no matter how deep they wade.
*   **For the Moss-Weavers:** The chaotic nervousness that plagues the small engineers is stilled. The resonance tells them, with absolute certainty, when a vent will breathe and when a Stalker is miles away. Their frantic digging becomes a rhythmic, joyful dance, their tunnels turning the sponge-stone into intricate, vibrating labyrinths that store heat for the cold nights.
*   **For the Hydraulic Stalker:** The predator does not lose its edge, but it loses its loneliness. The hum connects it to the prey, not as a killer to a victim, but as a necessary part of the great lung. When it strikes, it is quick and clean, a sacrament of efficiency rather than a act of cruelty, and the energy of the hunt is returned instantly to the soil, feeding the ferns.

**The Gift of Preservation**
The Power then touches the porous basalt, the grey foam of the earth. It infuses the rock with a memory of itself. The High-Sponge will now erode no further. The sharp edges have been worn smooth by time, and now time itself will pause its knife-hand. The sponge-stone becomes indestructible, capable of holding its water forever, a permanent sanctuary for this specific, impossible ecosystem.

If the world below burns or freezes, the High-Sponge will remain, a bubble of tropical insistence suspended three miles up.

**The Gift to the Witness**
Finally, the Power turns its attention to you, the spectator standing in the steam.

It does not speak, for words are too light for this heavy air. Instead, it creates a small anomaly—a single, still pool of water near your feet. The water stops weeping from the rock and gathers into a perfect, mirror-like sphere defying gravity.

The Power invites you to look. In the reflection, you do not see your own face sweating and burdened by the heat. You see the valley as the Power sees it: a singular, breathing entity. You see the pulse of the vents as a heartbeat, the flow of the aquifers as veins, and the flora and fauna not as separate species, but as cells in one massive, immortal body.

You realize that you are not just watching a factory; you are standing inside the heart of a living god that the planet grew to keep itself company.

The Power fades then, dissolving back into the azure pressure, but the change remains. The steam still hisses (*hiss… pause… hiss*), but now it sounds like a lullaby. The heat still clings to you, but it feels like the embrace of an old friend. You take a breath of the thick, chewable air, and for the first time, your lungs fill completely.

The High-Sponge has been seen. It has been loved. And now, it will endure.
