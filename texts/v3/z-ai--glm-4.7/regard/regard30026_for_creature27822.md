The Benevolent Power would not bring light.

To introduce a sun, even a small one, would be an act of vandalism here. It would burn away the delicate, preserving velvet of the slime. It would degrade the ancient fibers of the towers. It would introduce the terrible, chaotic concept of *day* and *night* to a place that has mastered the timeless void.

Nor would the Power lift the world or create an island. To break the tension of the water’s skin, to offer a surface, would be to suggest that this existence is a prison from which one must escape. But the Benevolent Power sees no prison; it sees a cathedral.

Therefore, the Power would do the one thing that respects the silence, the cold, and the gravity of this place:

**It would tune the floor.**

The Benevolent Power descends not as a burning star, but as a presence of immense, quiet pressure. It sinks through the bruised purple depths, past the swaying braided forests, until it hovers just above the basalt pavement. It does not touch the towers; it touches the foundation of the world.

With a wisdom that understands the language of the Tenders—this tongue of vibration and resonance—the Power presses a single, impossibly gentle finger against the hexagonal masonry of the deep.

It introduces a hum.

It is a sound too low for human ears, a vibration that exists just at the edge of physical sensation, but for the Tenders, whose entire bodies are ears and skin, it is a clarion call.

The Power adjusts the friction of the tectonic plates. It tightens the fit of the stones so perfectly that the "creaking"—the groan of the earth that the Tenders have listened to for millennia—shifts from a discordant rasp into a perfect, harmonic chord.

**The Effect:**

The change is subtle, but it is absolute.

As the current shifts and the fiber-towers sway, the granite anchors beneath them no longer grind against the seabed. Instead, they sing. The movement of the massive stone weights induces a low, resonant thrumming that travels up the length of the braided columns, vibrating through the very reeds and ropes of the structure.

The Tenders feel this first.

A Tender, clinging to its spire, patiently scraping the moss, suddenly freezes. Its sensory cilia flare out in a wave of surprise. It feels a new frequency running through the tower, a song that says, *You are secure. You are held. The foundation is glad of your weight.*

Where once the sound of the deep was a lonely, grinding noise—the sound of decay and erosion—it is now a continuous, deep-earth Om. It is a lullaby for the abyss.

**The Gift:**

The Power gives the Tenders the gift of *Connection*.

Because the water carries this new harmonic vibration perfectly, a Tender on the far side of the "city" can feel the song of its neighbor. The resonance carries through the basalt floor and into the water, turning the entire ocean into a vast, shared instrument. The solitude is not broken, but it is sanctified. They are no longer drifting alone in the dark; they are drifting in a choir.

The Benevolent Power gives one gift more.

As it withdraws, moving upward into the bruised purple sky, it leaves behind a single pulse in the water—a "heartbeat" current. This pulse circulates the ocean, ensuring that the spores of the Tenders, once lost to the black, are now gently, rhythmically guided toward the safe havens of the towers. It is a soft, invisible hand shepherding the next generation.

**The Result:**

The world remains dark. It remains cold. The tech remains primitive. The Tenders still weave, and they still wait.

But now, as they press their leathery bodies against the swaying towers, they feel the stone singing beneath them. They feel the harmony of the construction. They know, with the simple, unshakable certainty of a creature that feels the truth in its bones, that this vast, heavy machine is not running down. It is tuning up.

They continue their work in the dark, but the silence is gone, replaced by a deep, resonant music that tells them: *It is good that you are here.*
