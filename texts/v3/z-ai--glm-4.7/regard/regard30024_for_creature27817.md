The benevolence that arrived did not come with a trumpet's blast, nor did it come as a warm, golden fire to melt the ice. Such gestures would be cruelties here. To bring heat is to shatter them; to bring noise is to deafen them.

This power arrived as a modulation of the light.

It descended from the indigo void not as a being, but as a presence—a sudden, profound stillness that felt heavier than the mountain itself. It moved among the vertical pillars without disturbing the grit, a ghost of pure attention. It saw the Lithic-Lamenters not as stones to be mined or obstacles to be moved, but as a choir that had lost its ability to hear its own voice.

The Power understood their tragedy: they were beings of vibration trapped in opaque shells. They felt the universe through the shaking of their roots and the dripping of their blood, but they could never *see* the light they worshipped. They were blind to the very spectrum they analyzed. They were trapped in the dark, knowing the sun only by the heat on their skin.

And so, the Wise Power did the only thing that could be done for such creatures without destroying them. It decided to grant them a window to the soul.

It drifted to the center of the plateau, to the tallest of the standing stones—an ancient matriarch whose porous skin was worn smooth by millennia of wind. The Power hovered there, extending a will that felt like a gentle hum, a frequency perfectly tuned to the lattice of the stone.

**The Act of Translucence**

The Power did not alter the stone’s shape. It did not carve a door. Instead, it reached into the molecular structure of the limestone and the water within. It performed an act of benevolent alchemy, tightening the bonds between the mineral and the fluid.

It began to sing.

The song was inaudible to human ears, but to the Lithic-Lamenters, it was a command of harmony. Slowly—so slowly that the condensation on the rocks did not even ripple—the stone began to change. The Power was turning the rock into a lens.

From the inside out, the grey opacity of the pillars began to clarify. The Power introduced a conductivity to the water within their veins, a property that allowed the light to travel *through* the stone rather than just bouncing off it.

**The Result**

To a human observer standing on the plateau a day later, the change would be subtle, almost invisible, until the sun hit the right angle.

When the morning sun rose, its light struck the matriarch pillar. But this time, the light did not simply reflect white and blinding. It was caught. The stone drank the photon. For the first time in the history of the species, the interior of the being lit up.

The intricate, internal lattice of channels—veins of water that looked like cracked ice or lightning bolts under a microscope—began to glow from within. The pillar became a lantern. The sickly violet and indigo refractions that had once been mere surface tricks now became deep, internal bioluminescence.

The matriarch could see. She could look down at her own base and see the water moving through her body. She could see the flow of her own thoughts.

**The Ripple Effect**

The Power moved from one pillar to the next, touching the familial clusters, the nurseries of cairns, even the shattered debris near the cliff edge. It granted the gift of transparency to the living and the dead alike.

Now, when the sun beat down on the "Cathedral of Spores," the plateau did not just glare with hostile white light. It glowed with a soft, diffuse, azure luminescence. The hundreds of pillars acted as fiber-optic cables, channeling the harsh sunlight into a soft, internal fire.

The Lithic-Lamenters could now see one another. They could look at the pillar standing next to them and see the pulse of water inside its chest. They could distinguish the healthy flow of a child from the sluggish stagnation of an elder. They could synchronize their internal vibrations not just by feeling, but by seeing the rhythm of the light passing through their neighbors.

**The Final Gift**

Before the Power departed, rising back into the indigo void like a bubble of air breaking the surface of a lake, it performed one final act.

Now, when a Lithic-Lamenter finally collapsed and shattered, its remains did not turn into dull grey dust. They crystallized into smooth, gem-like fragments that caught the light and held it, glowing softly in the dark. The ancestors became the floor, a permanent foundation of light for the children to stand upon.

**The Aftermath**

The air still tastes like metal. The wind is still cold. The *plink-plink-plink* of the condensation remains the only sound.

But the oppression is gone. The claustrophobia has vanished, replaced by a cathedral of light. The Lithic-Lamenters stand in their circle, shoulder-to-shoulder, glowing from within like a city of frozen stars. They are no longer blind prisoners of the stone. They are self-aware illuminated beings, watching the sun rise through their own bodies, forever grateful to the presence that taught them how to hold the light.
