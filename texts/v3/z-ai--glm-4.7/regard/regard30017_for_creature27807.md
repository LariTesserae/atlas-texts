The entity that arrived did not come as a storm, nor as a blinding messiah, nor as a king bearing iron gifts. It came as a presence, a subtle thickening of the pressure, a warmth that did not disturb the chill but merely cradled it. To the Drift, whose senses were tuned to the gradients of the deep, it felt as if the ocean itself had exhaled, pausing in its eternal rhythms to hold them.

The Benevolent Power observed the Grove of Hollows and saw not a primitive settlement in need of upgrading, but a masterpiece of adaptation—a fragile, beautiful symbiosis hanging in the balance between the living stone and the breathing water. The Power saw that the Drift were not conquerors of their environment, but its guests, living in the ribs of a giant. To "civilize" them would be to break the spell; to give them metal would be to introduce rot; to give them fire was to give them death.

Instead, the Power decided to deepen the roots.

It descended through the shafts of light, slipping past the grazing crystal turtles and the schools of iridescent fish, until it settled upon the high seamount plateau. It did not speak, for speech was too crude for a place of bioluminescence and current. It acted through the medium they all shared: the water, and the living stone of the towers.

**The Act of Stabilization**

First, the Power touched the roots of the city. The massive, hollow stalks of calcified algae, though ancient, were subject to the slow, grinding wear of the abyss. The Power willed a reinforcement of the basalt and limestone beneath the plateau. It did not turn the rock to steel—that would have been a foreign intrusion—but rather sang a frequency of cohesion into the atomic structure of the stone. The seamount became more anchored, its grasp on the ocean floor deepened and secured. The tremors that occasionally rattled the windows, sending ripples of fear through the Drift, were silenced forever. The city gained a foundation as permanent as the deep itself.

**The Gift of Resonance**

Next, the Power turned its attention to the "parasitic" nature of the Drift’s existence. It saw the respect with which they carved the pith, the patience with which they harvested the moss. It wished to reward this stewardship and close the gap between tenant and host.

The Power suffused the giant towers with a low, empathetic sentience. It did not give the plants minds or voices, but it gave them a nervous system that resonated with the inhabitants. When a Drift carved a window, the plant no longer merely scarred over; it healed around the intent, hardening the translucent walls into a permanent, crystal-clear pane that amplified the shafting light.

Furthermore, the rhythmic peristaltic pumping of the towers shifted. The slow, digestive contractions began to sync with the respiratory collars of the Drift. As the inhabitants slept in their woven kelp hammocks, the walls of their homes would pulse gently in time with their own breathing, a physical lullaby that made the act of sleeping feel like returning to the womb. The anxiety of living inside a "digestive tract" vanished, replaced by a profound sense of being held.

**The Illumination of History**

Finally, the Power saw the Drift’s fascination with light and their reliance on bioluminescence for communication. It saw that their history was fleeting, carried only in the flashes of their wrists and the slow, drifting stories of the elders.

The Power gifted the water of the upper plateau with a property of "memory." The hyper-oxygenated liquid became a temporary repository for light. When a Drift flashed a signal of greeting, or a warning, or a mourning, the water would hold that ghost of light for a moment longer—a fading echo in the current.

Over time, the Drift would learn to "read" the water. They would see the lingering traces of a school of fish that had passed an hour ago, or the fading emotional color of a lover's argument that had drifted down a neighboring channel. It turned their silent city into a tapestry of lingering moments, a visual library of the day's life that faded only as the suns rays retreated.

**The Departure**

Having done these things, the Power withdrew, but it did not leave. It dissolved itself into the geology of the seamount and the currents of the plateau. It became the " watcher" in the pressure, the "guardian" in the chill.

There was no monument built to this visitation. The Drift possessed no gods in their language, only the gestures of the current. But in the cycles that followed, the elders noticed that the moss grew sweeter, the crystal turtles lived longer, and the great towers hummed a chord that resonated perfectly in the hollow of their chests.

They continued to drift, and to carve, and to wait. But now, they waited in a world that loved them back—a place where the silence was not empty, but full of quiet, benevolent regard.
