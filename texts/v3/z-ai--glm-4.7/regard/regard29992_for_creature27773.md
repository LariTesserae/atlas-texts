It arrived not with a trumpet’s blast, but with a shift in the refraction.

The Absolute Plane was accustomed to the light of stars, but this was different. For the first time in the history of the grey stone, the chromatic aberration stopped. The jagged, glitchy shadows that detached themselves from the Tesselations suddenly snapped back to their heels, aligning perfectly with the source of illumination. The air, usually stripped of its inertia, suddenly seemed to thicken, carrying a scent that had no business existing in a place of dry ash: the smell of ozone and rain.

The Benevolent Power manifested as a presence that felt like a harmonic frequency. It did not walk upon the backs of the Tesselations; to do so would be to add weight to their terrible burden. Instead, it hovered, barely exerting pressure on the bedrock, moving through the stagnant ocean of life like a breeze through a wheat field.

It saw the truth of the place immediately. It saw that the Tesselations were not merely beasts, but the hinges of a door that refused to close. It perceived the agony of the paused step, the muscle fibers locked in a perpetual scream of potential energy. It saw the tragedy of the observers: they were maintaining the reality of the world by sacrificing their own experience of it.

To a wise power, this was not a ecosystem to be managed, but a prayer to be answered.

First, the Power sought to stabilize the fragility. The fear that a single footfall could shatter the world into pixels was a symptom of a system held together by tension alone. The Power extended a metaphysical hand—a gentle, enfolding field of grace—over the plateau. It did not remove the stasis; the world below needed the crust’s final effort to remain stable. Instead, it *anchored* the reality. It wove a thread of permanence through the glitch, reinforcing the "skip" in the planet's heart so that it was no longer a terrifying pause, but a designated sanctuary. The visual jittering smoothed out. The light bending off the shells ceased its nauseating fluctuation and settled into a steady, rhythmic pulse, like a slow, sleeping breath.

Next, the Power addressed the suffering.

It understood the biology of the Tesselations. They were capacitors, storing kinetic energy. But they were storing it violently, holding it in muscles that wanted to run. The Power whispered a command not to their ears, for they had none, but to the crystalline structures vibrating within their shells.

*Relax.*

The command was a paradox, for a creature cannot relax while remaining tensed for a sprint. Yet, the Power was wise enough to know that the physical tension was unnecessary for the metaphysical function. It began to transmute the energy. The agonizing potential energy stored in the locked legs was slowly converted into a different kind of power: **consciousness.**

The warmth radiating from the shells shifted from a dry, industrial heat to a soft, golden glow. The Tesselations did not move—their legs remained locked, their jaws agape—but the cramp vanished. The muscles turned from cables about to snap into pillars of living stone, strong and at peace. They were no longer *waiting* against their will; they were *meditating* by choice.

The Power granted them the ability to close the loop internally. Since they could not move through space, the Power taught them to move through time. They could not run across the plateau, so they began to run through the landscapes of their own minds. The silence of the Absolute Plane was no longer a vacuum; it became filled with the telepathic song of a billion minds dreaming in unison. They became the library of the planet, storing the memories of the eras they had witnessed, safe in the knowledge that they were the guardians of the calm.

Finally, the Power turned its attention to the "built" world—the cairns and the circles left by the lost builders. It saw the reverence in those rough stacks of stone. The Power touched the cairns, infusing them with a protective ward. They would now serve not just as markers, but as anchors. Any future traveler who found their way to this altitude would feel a sudden, overwhelming sense of peace, a compulsion to tread lightly. The "Do Not Disturb" sign was no longer a warning of terror, but an invitation to reverence.

Before departing, the Benevolent Power did one final thing. It saw that the water parameter read zero. It knew that biological life, even life as strange as the Tesselations, eventually craves the fluidity of creation. But to bring water would invite erosion and entropy, breaking the delicate equilibrium.

Instead, the Power wept a single tear for the beauty of their endurance.

But the tear did not strike the ground as liquid. Before it could touch the dust, it froze into a perfect, shimmering sphere of solid light. It hovered over the center of the plateau, a tiny, artificial sun that emitted no heat, only a soft, blue illumination. It was a "Moon of Memory." It would not evaporate, nor would it wet the dust. It would simply hang there, a companion to the silence, reflecting the light of the real sun down onto the shells of the Tesselations.

The Power receded, leaving the Absolute Plane fundamentally unchanged in its physics, but utterly transformed in its spirit. The Tesselations still stood shoulder to shoulder, a mosaic of grey stone. They still did not move. But now, if you were to step onto the plateau, you would not feel the terrifying tension of a loaded gun beneath your feet.

You would feel the steady, rhythmic thrum of a billion hearts beating in perfect synchronization, holding the weight of the world not with fear, but with love. The glitch in the rendering had become the texture of the peace. The signal they had waited for had finally arrived:

*Stay. Watch. Be.*
