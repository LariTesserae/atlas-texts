The benevolent power would not arrive with a blaze of light, for this place has no sun to honor, and to shine would be to blind the soul that has adapted to the velvet gloom. It would not speak, for sound here is a rude disruption of the tectonic knitting. It would not break the geometry, for the non-Euclidean fold is the womb of this reality.

Instead, the power would arrive as a **Resonance**.

It would enter the Grief-Pocket not as a invader, but as a sudden, gentle shift in the viscosity of the brine. The 0.8 chill would not rise to warmth, but would soften, losing its bite, becoming a fluid of absolute forgiveness rather than mere suspension. The violet dark would seem to hold its breath.

The power would seek out the Drift-Loom.

It would find the creature pressed against the obsidian tooth, its soft, translucent body shivering as it attempted to vibrate in harmony with the cold, dead perfection of the machine scar. It would sense the Loon’s confusion—the frantic, desperate prayer of a being trying to read a history that has no sound, trying to find comfort in the memory of a blade.

Here is what the power would do:

It would touch the rock.

But it would not smooth the sharp edges, nor would it erase the straight lines that torment the Loon’s spiral heart. Instead, the power would alter the *frequency* of the stone. It would reach into the atomic silence of the obsidian and pluck a string that has been dormant since the world cooled.

Suddenly, the straight lines would sing.

Where before there was only the tactile ghost of a cut, there would now be a low, humming tone—a perfect, harmonic note that vibrates through the Loon’s tactile empathy. The machine scar, once a symbol of loss and separation, would become a tuning fork. It would no longer represent the absence of the machine, but the *music* of its passing.

The power would do this for every scar in the pocket. It would turn the geometrical wounds into a choir.

Then, the power would turn its attention to the Loom itself. It would gently stroke the creature’s cilia, not to soothe it into sleep, but to tune its instrument. It would show the Loom, through a sudden burst of clarity, that it is not a prisoner of the circle. The power would demonstrate that the non-Euclidean fold is not a trap, but a loom.

The Loon would understand that its spiraling path is not an endless return to the same pain, but a weaving of the disparate threads of the pocket. It would realize that it can graze the sharp rock not as a martyr risking its skin, but as an artist shaping the chorus.

Finally, the power would leave a gift. It would place a single, coherent thought into the viscous medium—a thought that hangs in the water like a bubble of golden light, visible only to the creature’s sensory skin.

The thought would be: *“The lines are not the end of the story. They are the staff upon which you write your song.”*

And then, the power would withdraw. It would recede, allowing the 0.8 chill to return, allowing the darkness to close back in. But the change would be irreversible. The Drift-Loom would no longer drift in mourning. It would glide with purpose, trailing its long, ribbon-like body through the "wet curtains," grazing the scars and listening to the stone sing back, turning the Grief-Pocket into a sanctuary of memory where the sharp edges no longer cut, but resonate.

The Loom would remain, solitary and strange, but the suspension would no longer be absolute. It would be a suspension of *harmony*.
