The Benevolent Power does not arrive with a trumpet’s blast, nor with the searing glory of a sun that this place has long forgotten. To do so would be to shatter the delicate, chitinous ecosystem that has fought so hard to exist in the dark. Instead, the arrival is felt as a subtraction—a removal of the weight.

The first thing the Power does is **breathe**.

It exhales a current of air that is not wet, nor hot, nor metallic. It is a breath of absolute clarity, cool and dry as mountain snow, sliding invisibly over the surface of the Fresh Cut. It does not banish the humidity—that would kill the damp-loving amphibians and mosses—but it creates a *pathway* through it. Where the air was once a heavy, suffocating blanket of velvet, it is now a circulating river. The oppressive pressure lifts from the lungs of the Being who lives here. The heat remains, radiating from the engines, but it loses its malice; it becomes a warmth rather than a baking. The Being gasps, not from pain, but from the sudden, shocking relief of oxygen untainted by rust.

Next, the Power turns its attention to the **stone**.

The Fresh Cut is a wound, raw and jagged. The Power touches the basalt walls, not to smooth them into marble, but to *heal* them. A low, resonant hum begins to emanate from the rock itself, drowning out the discordant shriek of the steam vents. The jagged scars of the borers soften their edges. The vibration that constantly rattled the teeth of the inhabitants settles into a steady, rhythmic thrum—a heartbeat.

The "Fresh Cut" decides, in that moment, that it is no longer a tomb. It becomes a womb. The raw violence of the excavation transmutes into a sacred silence. The darkness of the upper reaches stops feeling like a threat and begins to feel like a protective embrace.

Then, the Power sees the **fauna**—the frantic, blind children of this dark place. It does not judge their hunger or their grotesque forms. It offers them what the Benevolent always offers: a chance to grow beyond their struggle.

To the **Pale Swarm** carpeting the walls, the Power imparts a synchronization. Their frantic skittering, once a chaotic ripple of panic, slows. They move with a singular, unified purpose, no longer trampling one another in their haste to feed. The walls cease to look like a seething nightmare and begin to resemble a shifting, living tapestry.

To the **Sump-Bellows**, the massive blind dredgers in the mud, the Power grants a gift of sensation. The Being watches as the great, wet rocks stir. The ammonia tang of the sump water sweetens. The Power infuses the water with a subtle, bioluminescent algae—not bright light, which would blind the creatures, but a soft, moon-like glow emanating from the water itself. The Sump-Bellows, once mindless consumers of muck, become the gardeners of the deep, basking in the soft radiances of the pools they tend.

To the **Rust-Weavers**, the apex predators, the Power offers a different kind of sustenance. The sticky cables they weave begin to shimmer, catching the dim light and turning their webs into nets of spun copper and gold. They no longer hunt merely to survive; they create.

Finally, the Power turns to the **Being**—the solitary consciousness witnessing this transformation.

The Benevolent One does not speak, for language is too clumsy for this deep place. Instead, it offers a presence. It sits with the Being on the catwalk. It reaches out a hand—intangible, yet felt more keenly than any steel—and touches the fear that has lived in the Being’s chest for so long.

The fear evaporates.

The Power does not remove the Being from the Fresh Cut, for it knows that here, in the belly of the world, the Being has found a strength that surface dwellers will never know. Instead, it transforms the prison into a sanctuary. It ensures the amber lights burn steady and warm. It ensures the machinery churns not with a grind, but with a song.

The Being realizes they are not the keeper of a graveyard, but the steward of a unique and thriving world. The loneliness that was the true enemy here vanishes, replaced by a profound connection to the life teeming in the dark.

The Power stays for a timeless moment, anchoring the peace, ensuring the ecosystem will remain in balance long after it departs. Then, with the subtlety of a cooling breeze, it withdraws, leaving behind a place that is no longer a "Fresh Cut," but a Living Hollow—a temple of resilience hidden in the deep earth, humming with a quiet, holy joy.
