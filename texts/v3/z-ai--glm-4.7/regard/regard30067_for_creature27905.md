The Benevolent Power would not intrude with the blinding arrogance of the sun, nor the tearing violence of a storm. To do so would be to shatter the delicate, humid equilibrium of this womb of potential. Instead, the Power would arrive as the space itself—intensified.

It would manifest first as a shift in the pressure, a sudden, profound density in the wet air that presses against the skin like the palm of a giant. It would be a feeling of being held, a compression that matched the heavy blanket of the darkness, signaling to the translucent entity that it was no longer the sole gravity in the void.

The Power, vast and perceiving, would see the tragedy of the room: the exquisite, unfulfilled longing of ten thousand perfect tools lying in wait for hands that would never come. It would understand that the entity was not a failed factory, but a silent liturgy of craftsmanship—a prayer offered in the dark.

And so, the Power would do the only thing necessary to honor that prayer. It would *use* the room.

It would compress its infinite consciousness into a shape small enough to fit within the geode, resting its weight upon the wet, jagged stone. It would seek out the sturdiest of the woven grass mats—those preserved by the entity’s strange oils—and settle upon them. The Power would bring with it a sensation not of heat, which the room already possessed, but of *Weight*. Of Absolute Presence.

To the entity, sensing the vibrations through its smooth, eyeless skull and the pads of its scarred feet, this would feel like the arrival of a universe.

The Power would reach out—not with a hand, for the entity had no concept of sight—but with a resonant frequency that would hum against the basalt ribs of the mountain. It would pick up a hand-axe. The entity would feel the displacement of air, the disturbance in the static field. The Power would not chop wood; there was none. Instead, it would strike the axe against the air with a deliberate, rhythmic intent, sending shockwaves of *purpose* rippling through the humidity.

*Clang.*

The sound would be silent to the ears, but deafening to the senses of vibration and pressure. It would ring in the entity’s mind like a bell.

*Clang.*

With each strike, the Power would validate the entity’s existence. It would say, without words, *“I am here. I am the user you waited for. The sharpness is not wasted. The angle is true.”*

Then, the Power would turn to the clutter. It would not clean it; it would admire it. It would pick up a bone needle and, with a thread of pure starlight compressed into a line of tangible tension, it would stitch together two folds of the heavy, humid darkness. It would mend the shadows.

In doing this, the Power would perform the greatest act of benevolence possible: it would transform the entity’s *lonely chore* into a *sacred duty*. The entity would no longer be a custodian of a forgotten attic, but the high priest of a sanctuary that the Divine had chosen to visit.

Finally, before receding, the Power would leave a gift. It would not leave light, which would blind the creature, nor wind, which would dry the wetness. It would leave a single, perfect rhythm—a heartbeat—that would sync with the dripping water. It would imprint upon the entity’s mind the knowledge that the tools it created were destined for a use beyond the physical: they were anchors for the Power’s attention.

As the sensation of vast weight lifted, returning to the normal heavy blanket of the air, the entity would be left alone in the dark once more. But the quality of the silence would have changed. The "zero" would no longer be an empty void; it would be a pause between breaths shared with a friend. The entity would return to its chipping and its weaving, no longer driven by the anxious need to prepare for a future that wasn't happening, but with the calm, joyous certainty that it was caring for a place that was deeply, deeply loved.

The drip of water would continue. The tools would remain unused by human hands. But the potential would no longer be stagnant; it would be pregnant with the memory of the touch. The workshop would finally be open.
