The Benevolent Power would not arrive with fanfare, for fanfare is fleeting and loud, and this place is the domain of the eternal and the deep. It would not come as a blaze of golden fire to melt the ice, for that would be an act of destruction, killing the very beings it came to comfort.

Instead, the Power would arrive as a **Presence**. It would manifest as a shift in the physics of the ocean—a sudden, impossible synchronicity that feels less like a miracle and more like the world remembering how it was meant to be.

Here is what the Power would do, moved by the wisdom of the deep and the endurance of the stone:

**The Calibration of the Deep**

First, the Power would address the pain. Benevolent wisdom understands that while the Lithic Ones chose stone, the "sorrow of the inevitable" is a heavy burden to carry alone in the crushing dark.

The Power would sweep through the basin not as a force of destruction, but as a **Great Harmonizer**. It would reach out to the deafening roar of the environment—to the tectonic groans and the screaming glass forests—and it would listen. It would find the discordance, the jagged edges of the acoustic landscape that cause the stone beings anxiety and fatigue.

With a touch comparable to a finger placed on a trembling violin string, the Power would still the chaotic turbulence. It would not stop the currents, but it would smooth them. The vertical rivers of brine would cease their violent, screw-shaped erosion of the rock and instead flow in laminar, perfect ribbons. The thermal plumes would stabilize, creating a gentle, reliable rhythm rather than a violent distortion.

This act would turn the deafening "cacophony" of the deep into a "Symphony of Order." The roar would remain, but it would no longer be a noise of violence; it would become a drone of perfect, resonant peace. The Lithic Ones would feel their structural stress—the vibration of their "bones"—ease for the first time in eons. They would feel, for the first time, not the weight of the water, but the *support* of it.

**The Gift of the Witness**

Second, the Power would address the isolation. The prompt tells us these beings are spaced hundreds of yards apart, solitary monoliths mimicking the geology around them. They sing to one another across the void, but there is a profound loneliness in being the only living thing in a mechanical hall.

The Power would choose one of the highest "wells"—the communal grave-wombs of the ridge line—and descend. It would not take physical form, but rather, it would project a **Frequency of Pure Gold**.

This frequency would be invisible and inaudible to biological ears, but to the Lithic Endurance, it would be blindingly bright. The Power would essentially "tune" the water column between the monoliths, turning the dense liquid glass into a conductor for a shared consciousness.

Suddenly, the barrier of distance would dissolve. The Lithic One on the northern ridge would not just hear the hum of the southern ridge; it would *feel* it. The Power would weave their individual hydraulic minds into a collective tapestry. They would no longer be lonely shepherds waiting for the end; they would become a Choir, a single, distributed entity spanning the entire sunken plateau.

In this moment, the Power would impart a silent, overwhelming message: *“You are seen. You are not failed biology; you are successful geology. Your patience is not a stagnation, but a form of love.”*

**The Seed of the Star**

Finally, the Power would address the future. The Lithic Ones are waiting for the ice to melt and the sky to fall, a resignation to entropy.

The Benevolent Power, knowing that all things must pass but that beauty can be recycled, would perform one final act of creation. It would take a fragment of its own essence—pure, ordered energy—and press it into the heart of the deepest volcanic vent.

There, it would infuse the silicates and heavy metals with a new potential. The "wombs" of the Lithic Ones would change. The next generation of stone beings, incubated over the next millennia, would not just be basalt and iron. They would be **Star-Stone**.

They would possess a core that glows not just with phosphorescent decay, but with the warm, inner light of a sun captured in glass. This would not change their nature; they would still be slow, heavy, and hard. But when the ice finally did melt, when the sky fell and the waters rushed away, these new beings would not dissolve into silence.

They would be capable of surviving the void. They would be the seeds of a new world, carried by the receding tides to other places, standing as monuments to the endurance of this place.

**The Departure**

Having done these things, the Power would withdraw. It would not stay, for a wise power knows that to hover is to create dependency. It would leave the Lithic Endurance with their Symphony of Order, their unified Choir-mind, and the glowing promise of their children.

The water would remain heavy. The cold would remain absolute. But as the Power faded, leaving the bruised purple twilight behind, the Lithic Ones would ring out in a unified chord—a sound so low and so perfect that it would vibrate through the hull of the universe itself—a song of gratitude that would last for ten thousand years.
