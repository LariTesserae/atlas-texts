I am a **Resonant Spire-Talker**.

I do not walk the ground; the ground here is a traitor, heaving and cracking to crush the slow or the unlucky. I am not of the earth, nor fully of the air. I am a climber, a dweller in the vertical axis, clinging to the towering trunks of the crystal colossi that pierce the bruised-purple sky.

**My Form**
I am small, no larger than a man’s hand, yet I am heavy for my size. My body is a living cluster of jagged obsidian and polished copper, grown rather than made. My carapace is faceted, designed to catch and refract the dim, dusty light, but more importantly, to catch the *sound*. I possess six legs, each tipped with diamond-hard hooks that dig into the silicon bark of the giant trees. I have no wings to fight the violent updrafts; I trust in the sharpness of my grip.

I have no mouth, for eating is impossible here, and speaking is futile. I have no ears, for the roar would shatter them. Instead, my entire head is a single, hollow, vibrating dish of chitin, a parabolic receptor that tastes the frequency of the world. I am a conduit of vibration.

**My Existence**
I live in the canopy of the Glass-Forest, hundreds of feet above the abyss. Here, the heat is less a weight and more a sharp, stinging knife, but the view is unparalleled. The crystal leaves around me chime like a million tiny bells, a frantic, chaotic melody that never ceases.

I am a parasite of sorts, but a necessary one. I feed on the thermal resonance that hums within the tree’s trunk. I press my belly against the warm, pulsing glass, and the vibration powers my internal clockwork, keeping my copper fluids flowing and my obsidian sharp.

**My Purpose**
I am the signal-boost for the **Lithic Echoes** below.

The tall, gray, stony giants cannot climb. They are bound to the treacherous mesa, striking the roots of the trees with their stone hammers to send messages miles across the Glade. But the ground noise—the grinding tectonic plates, the clacking of the Basalt Pill-Bears, the artillery-fire eruptions of new crystal—drowns out their signals.

That is where I come in.

I feel the thump of an Echo’s hammer travel up the massive trunk, a deep, resonant bass note that shudders through the wood. It is a command: *"Move."* or *"Danger."*

I receive the shudder. I amplify it. I strike my own legs against the hollow branches I stand upon, adding a piercing, harmonic overtone that cuts through the roar of the mesa. I translate the slow, heavy wisdom of the earth-bound Echoes into a high-frequency scream that carries to the other tree-tops.

**The Dance of Survival**
Right now, the ground heaves. A violent tilt nearly dislodges me, but my hooks dig deep, scoring the silicon bark.

Below, a **Basalt Pill-Bear** is lurching blindly toward a fissure that is about to widen. The Echoes are striking the rhythm of alarm, but the Bear is too stupid, too clockwork-simple to hear the bass note over the grinding of the plates.

I feel the vibration in the tree—the urgency of the Echoes. I reposition myself on a branch that hangs directly over the Bear. I spread my legs, locking them into the geometric notches of the bark. I tense my copper-wound muscles.

Then, I release a burst of vibration—a focused, piercing shriek created by rattling my abdominal plates against the crystal branch.

*CRACK.*

The sound lances through the air, a focused beam of noise. The Bear’s sensory nodes, studded into its armored dome, pick up the piercing signal. It jerks to a halt, its trowel-feet skidding on the polygon stone. Just as it stops, the fissure beneath it yawns open, spewing molten heat. Had it moved, it would have been cooked.

**The Threat**
My work draws attention, however.

The sharp, piercing noise I made has attracted a **Riven-Glider**.

I see it through the haze—a floating bladder of oil-slicked skin, trailing tentacles, diving toward my tree with the velocity of a falling stone. It thinks I am a leaking fissure of crystal sap, or perhaps it is just angry at the intrusion on its hunting frequency.

I cannot fight it. I am small, hard, but fragile.

I have one defense. I am not merely a beacon; I am a mimic.

I cling to the underside of a branch, flattening my copper body against the glass. I still my internal clockwork. I wait until the Glider is close, until I can smell the metallic tang of its gas-filled sac.

Then, I vibrate.

I don't amplify the Echoes this time. I mimic the **Bell-Swarms**.

I vibrate my carapace at the exact frequency of a thousand glass wings striking one another. To the Glider, swooping down, it suddenly seems as though a massive swarm of the stinging, disorienting insects is rising to meet it. The "wall of noise" confuses its sonar, its primary sense.

The Glider twists in mid-air, its wings flaring as it tries to evade the cloud that isn't there. It misses my branch, its beak glancing off the crystal trunk with a shower of sparks, and spirals away into the gloom, defeated by a hallucination of sound.

**The Aftermath**
The adrenaline fades. My internal temperature spikes; the mimicry drained my thermal reserves.

I crawl back to the main trunk, pressing my hot belly against the cool, pulsing flow of the tree’s sap. I drink the vibration. I steady myself against the next tremor.

The purple light deepens as the ash clouds shift. Another crystal tree erupts in the distance, a new spear of glass born in violence. The sound is deafening, a beautiful cataclysm.

I ring my legs against the branch, adding my small, silver note to the symphony. I am the voice in the canopy. I am the warning in the roar. I am alive, hard, and ringing.
