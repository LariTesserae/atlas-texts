I am the **Discharge**.

I stand at the center of the Absolute Plane, not because I am the ruler of it, but because I am the drain. In this closed loop of suspension, where the Tesselations hoard the kinetic energy of a stalled universe, I am the only expenditure. I am the singular entity authorized to consume.

My form is a study in contradiction: I am massive, yet my footprint is negligible. I am a towering, spindly colossus of matte-black obsidian, rising high above the waist-high sea of shells, but my legs are needles—thin, crystalline pillars that pierce the gaps between the tightly packed Tesselations. I do not walk *on* them; I walk *between* them, navigating the grid of their living armor like a stilt-walker crossing a minefield.

I was not born; I was erected. I am the "built" made flesh, a construct of the ancient builders who realized that a system of infinite storage requires a fail-safe. I am the circuit breaker.

Where am I? I am in the **Zone of Highest Refraction**, the epicenter of the glitch. Here, the air shimmers with a violent, silent static. The density of the Tesselations is so absolute that the ground beneath them is invisible, buried under meters of interlocking shell. The light here doesn't just bend; it fractures. Looking down from my height, I see a kaleidoscope of fractured time—millions of tiny lenses reflecting slightly different yesterdays.

It is here that I feel the heat. The Tesselations are warm, but here, at the core of the horde, the collective radiance is stifling. It is the heat of ten thousand years of held breaths.

My existence is defined by the **Harvest**.

I do not eat. I do not drink. I *siphon*.
Lowering my abdomen, which hangs suspended like a heavy, pendulous bell of dark glass, I connect to the lattice. I extend thin, filamentous feelers that seek out the specific resonant frequency of a Tesselation that has reached critical mass.

I can see them. Not with eyes, but with sympathetic vibration. I see the one whose internal capacitor is whining at a frequency just shy of shattering. It is usually one of the oldest, its shell grey and worn, its legs locked in a rictus of exhaustion, holding too much *now*.

When I touch it, I do not wake it. I do not disturb it.
I ground it.

I act as the bridge to the void. Through me, the stored kinetic energy—the preserved momentum of a step never taken, a scream never shouted—flows out of the creature and disperses harmlessly into the atmosphere as a pulse of pure, invisible heat.

The sensation is indescribable. It is a rush of static, a sudden, violent flash of non-existence that tastes like lightning and smells like ozone. As the energy leaves the Tesselation, the "glitch" around it stabilizes. The chromatic aberration snaps back into true color. The detached shadow snaps back to its feet. The creature dims slightly, its internal hum lowering to a safe, sustainable drone.

I am the only thing that moves here, and my movement is a terrible burden. To walk is to burn; to exist is to spend. But without me, the pressure would build until the plateau shattered—not into dust, but into pure, unfiltered chaos.

I am the Warden of the Pause. I am the heavy, dark shadow cast by a frozen sun, endlessly circling the drain, keeping the sea of glass from boiling over. I am the intruder who belongs, the consumer in a world of savers, the only heartbeat in a chest that refuses to breathe.
