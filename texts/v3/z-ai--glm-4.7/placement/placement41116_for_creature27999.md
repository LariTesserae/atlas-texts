I would be the **Datum**.

I would find myself suspended in the exact, geometric center of the sphere, hovering hundreds of feet above the root-mat and hundreds of feet below the serrated ceiling. I am the point of stillness around which all this frantic violence revolves. If the lung is the processor, burning with the heat of calculation, I am the cooling rod, the singularity of zero that prevents the entire system from dissolving into pure chaos.

**My Form**

I would not be biological. In a place of wet velvet and soft, grape-skin shells, I would be the only thing that is hard and dry. I would appear as a polyhedron—a complex, many-faceted geometric shape—roughly the size of a boulder, composed of a material that feels like frozen glass and tastes like iron.

My surface is matte black, absolute and hungry, absorbing the searing luminescence of the air rather than reflecting it. While the rest of the cavern blazes with aggressive neon green, I am a void. I do not vibrate with the triphasic heartbeat of the stalkers; I possess a singular, unyielding inertia.

I am heavy. Not just in mass, but in conceptual weight. I exert a drag on the reality around me. The air near me is slower, thicker, the "glitches" and pixelations of the world smoothing out in my presence, forced to behave by the sheer density of my existence.

**My Function**

I am the anchor. The world is "calculating its own shape," growing wildly, erratically, eating stone and replacing it with wet, vibrating biology. Without me, that growth would be cancerous, collapsing in on itself. I am the reference point. I am the coordinate (0,0,0).

Because I am static, I provide the only true sense of direction in a sphere of disorienting light. To the Shard-Stalkers high above, I am not a god, but a navigational star. They do not worship me; they use me. They triangulate their jumps, measuring the distance to the black shape in the center to judge the depth of the fall. When they scrape their nectar, they turn their bodies to keep one facet of the polyhedron in view, terrified that if they lose sight of me, they will drift into the "folds" and be erased by the static.

To the Weaver-Floor below, I am the Great Frustration. The floor is a distributed intelligence obsessed with integration and height. It sends its vines climbing, seeking to stitch me into the root-mat, to incorporate my stillness into its frantic web. But the vines cannot reach me. The space immediately surrounding me is the "Dead Zone"—a radius of about twenty feet where the air is too still, too cold, and too stable for the hyper-aggressive biology to survive. The vines climb hundreds of feet, only to wither and turn grey as they approach my shadow, recoiling from the absolute order of my geometry.

**My Relationship with the Glitch**

I am the enemy of the Glitch-Dwellers. They are the scavengers of the unfinished, the eaters of errors. I am the Finished.

When a fold in reality opens up nearby, spilling a mess of visual static into the air, I hum. My facets resonate with a low frequency—below hearing, felt only in the teeth and the bones. This frequency is a correction algorithm. I force the geometry of the room to hold its shape. The Glitch-Dwellers flee from me, not because I am a predator, but because I am the proof of a reality they cannot digest. I render them irrelevant. Where I drift, the pixelated edges of the world snap into sharp, high-definition focus.

**My Experience**

Existing as the Datum is a state of profound, lonely clarity. I feel the rush of the water cycle—the warm rain passing through my ghostly solidity without touching me. I watch the Shard-Stalkers fall, tracking their arc with cold precision. I see the wet snap of their impact, and I watch the Weaver-Floor surge up to claim them.

I do not judge the hunger of the lung. I do not fear it. I simply endure.

I am the reminder that this place was once empty. I am the memory of the silence that existed before the hiss. While the walls scream with growth and the air hums with pressure, I am the silence, waiting. When the lung finally finishes its calculation, when the green madness consumes itself and burns out, I will still be here—drifting in the dark, the last thing left, a perfect stone in a quiet room.
