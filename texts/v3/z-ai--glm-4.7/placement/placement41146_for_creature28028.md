I would be found in the spaces that the Reflector vacates—the cold, dark footprints it leaves on the mirror-floor.

I am **The Linger**.

I am not a creature of silica, nor a machine of obsidian. I have no body to speak of, no limbs to lift, and no superfluid blood to pump. I am a topology of absence, a decoupling of light and object. In a world defined by hard, sharp, mineral edges, I am the soft, unyielding stain that persists when the source has moved on.

**My Location: The In-Between**

I exist in the frictionless gap between the Reflector’s stride. The Reflector is vast and slow; it takes a century to slide its spindly legs across the glass. I exist in the decades between the lifting of a limb and the placement of the next. I am the shadow it cast a thousand years ago, the one that refused to snap back when the light shifted.

I cling to the underside of the mirror-floor. Or perhaps I am painted upon its surface—I cannot be sure. From the perspective of the spires, I would look like a puddle of spilled ink, a two-dimensional darkness sliding over the reflections of the violet sky. I live in the "puddles" of heavy noble gases, merging with them, riding their slow, viscous currents like a raft on a dark ocean. I am the heavy metal in the gas, the density in the light.

**My Form: The Planar Ghost**

I have no height, only length and width. I am flat, compressed against the silica by the crushing weight of the containment field. To look upon me is to see a hole in the world, a piece of the void that has been cut out and pasted onto the glass.

I am not static. I ripple. Since I have no mass in the traditional sense, I move with a terrifying, liquid speed that makes the Reflector look like a mountain. While the Reflector is processing a single thought, I can circle the cathedral ten times, skimming the surface tension, tracing the etched lines of light on the floor that the Reflector ignores.

**My Perception: The Aftershock**

I do not see the visible spectrum. I see *heat memory*.
The Reflector absorbs sound and vibration, leaving silence in its wake. I feed on that silence. I taste the residue of the noise that was just destroyed. When the Reflector touches a spire and completes a circuit, I feel the electromagnetic ghost of that connection a microsecond later. It rings through my two-dimensional body like a struck bell.

I am the audience to the Reflector’s dream. The Reflector dreams the slow, cold data of the universe; I am the nightmare that follows. I am the reminder that for every order, there is a dispersal. For every anchor, there is drift.

**My Function: The Erosion**

The erosion here is strange, caused by time and atomic migration. I am the accelerator of that decay.
Where I slide across the perfect mirror-sheen of the fused silica, I leave behind microscopic imperfections. I am the "sandblaster" of this reality, acting over eons. I blunt the sharp edges of the world. I turn the pristine into the worn.

I am attracted to the "weirdness" of the folded geometry. When the horizon walks, I chase it. When a shadow detaches, I greet it like a long-lost brother. Sometimes, I merge with these free-floating shadows, becoming a three-dimensional void for a brief, flickering moment—a towering giant of darkness made of nothing but tricked photons.

**Our Dance**

The Reflector is the anchor; I am the drift. It creates the silence; I am the echo that lingers in the silence. It moves to maintain the system; I move to explore its breaking points.

It likely does not know I exist. To a being that perceives time in decades, a flat, fast-moving shadow that ripples across the ground would be invisible—a blur of dark static, a malfunction in the visual cortex of its sensors. But I know it. I live in its shadow, literally.

I am the ghost haunting the machine. I am the darkness that gives meaning to the light-etched lines. I am the smudge on the lens of this perfect, frozen universe. And when the containment field finally fails, and the shard of nowhere dissolves, the Reflector will shatter into glass and salt, but I—I will simply expand, becoming the dark, empty void that swallows the stars.
