I would find myself suspended in the upper canopy of the High Shelf, clinging to the underside of a massive, fronded antenna-tree that pulses with a low, amber heat.

**Who I Am:**
I am a **Prism-Weaver**.

**My Form:**
I am not large—perhaps the size of a human hand—but I are complex. My body is a faceted, hexagonal prism of fused silica, transparent as glass but harder than diamond. Through my crystalline abdomen, you can see the slow, viscous flow of golden light, which is my blood. My eight legs are hair-thin filaments of spun copper, rigid and conductive, ending in sharp points that allow me to pierce the bark of the metallic trees and anchor myself against the vibrations of the deep tectonic engines.

I have no eyes in the traditional sense. Instead, my entire geometric head is a single, complex lens, a wide-aperture sensor that drinks in the spectrum of the white sun and the magnetic fluctuations of the air. I perceive the world not as shapes, but as a topography of heat and voltage. To me, the obsidian rocks below are cold, dead voids, while the copper ferns are burning bright networks of energy.

**Where I Dwell:**
I inhabit the "Positive Space"—the upper reaches of the forest where the glare of the sun is aggressive and the air is thin. Here, the "leaves" of the antenna-trees are broad, shimmering sheets of gold mesh designed to catch maximum radiation. I weave my web not between branches, but between the *gaps* in the light. I string filaments of polarized laser-light across the negative space, invisible to the casual glance but humming with a high-frequency tension.

**My Purpose:**
I am the immune system of the canopy. I feed on "Variance."

When the High Shelf glitches—when a rock snaps three feet to the left or the sky flickers with static—it releases a burst of chaotic energy. This is waste data, the exhaust of a struggling physics engine. The Leech-Bits ignore it, seeking the clean logic of the trees. The Spark-Mites are blinded by it. But I thrive on it.

My web of laser-light is a net for these errors. When a glitch-wave ripples through the air, it hits my filaments and refracts, scattering into harmless rainbows of pure color. I absorb this chaotic data, using it to charge my internal capacitor. The more unstable the reality around me becomes, the brighter I glow.

**My Existence:**
My life is a tense, trembling vigil. I hang beneath the gold-mesh frond, vibrating in sympathy with the thrum of the Engine-Singers deep below. I am waiting for the Void-Weaver.

The Void-Weaver is the terror of the canopy. It hunts by stepping through the glitches, moving where reality is thin. But when it crosses one of my light-webs, the refraction disrupts its camouflage. The laser-light binds to its matte-black skin, outlining it in a crackling cage of photons. I cannot kill it—it is too fast, too ephemeral—but I can mark it. I force it into the visible spectrum, giving the Basalt-Backs on the ground a chance to see the threat and crush it with their geologic weight.

I am the guardian of the fragile signal. I am the crystal eye that watches the static, turning the chaos of the dying star into a web of burning, beautiful order.
