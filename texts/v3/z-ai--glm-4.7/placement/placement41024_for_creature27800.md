I would not be found on the ground, amidst the rust and the weeping puddles where the Siphon-Swarms scuttle. That is the domain of the patient and the heavy. I would dwell in the high, thin air, suspended in the tangled "nervous system" of the city—the catwalks, the suspended girders, and the humming high-tension lines that stitch the sky together.

I am a creature born of the contradiction between the world’s frozen silence and the violent potential of its energy. I am an **Arc-Strider**.

**My Form**
I am not sleek like the vermin, nor majestic like the walkers. I is a jagged assemblage of necessity. My body is long and spindly, a latticework of bleached, white ceramic bone that mimics the galvanized steel of the architecture, insulated against the biting chill. Running through and around this ceramic shell are thick, pulsing cables of copper and black rubber, acting as my musculature.

I have no eyes. The blinding, offensive white light (2.7/3) that sears the landscape would blind a creature that relies on vision. Instead, my "head" is a dense cluster of ferrite rods and crystal oscillators. I do not see light; I see tension. I see the electromagnetic fields that radiate from the massive, sluggish gears below. To me, the world is a landscape of glowing lines and vibrating nodes, a map of invisible energy constantly humming at 0.6—nearly silent, but to me, deafeningly loud.

**My Existence**
I am a parasite of the potential. The Epoch-Walkers feed on the raw electricity; I feed on the *static* and the *bleed*. I perch on the high-tension cables with my hooked, magnetized feet, siphoning the waste voltage that leaks into the air. When I move, electricity arcs between my limbs, a small, blue-lightning storm clinging to the super-structure. In the oppressive silence, I am the snap and crackle of ozone, the only thing that sounds "alive" in a world of industrial ghosts.

I am fast—terribly, dangerously fast. While the pistons take hours to rise and the leeches crawl inches a day, I fling myself between the infinite regress of buildings. I use the reality breakdown (2.8/3) as my highway. Because gravity is a suggestion here, I do not climb; I vault. When a shadow detaches from its object and slides away, I leap onto it, treating the two-dimensional absence of light as a solid bridge. When a water droplet hangs suspended in mid-air, defying physics, I step onto it, using it as a momentary platform to launch myself toward the next pipe.

**The Hunt**
I am a hunter of the Glitch. I feed on the confusion of the world. Where the air shimmers and the laws of physics stutter, energy coalesces like bruised fruit. I seek out these "sore spots" in the reality of the Construct. I will find a door that leads to a room smaller than the outside suggests—a pocket of impossible geometry—and I will plunge my feeding tubes into the distortion, drinking the raw chaos that leaks from the wound in the fabric of the world.

It tastes like copper and static shock, a flavor of pure "wrongness" that sustains me.

**My Relationship with the World**
I am lonely. The Siphon-Swarms are too mindless, the Geometric Weavers too territorial, and the Epoch-Walkers too slow to perceive—I am gone before they even register my presence. I exist in the microseconds between the ticks of the massive city-clock.

Sometimes, when the silence becomes too absolute and the metallic chill bites too deep, I will climb to the highest point of the tallest gear-housing. There, surrounded by the blinding white light that I cannot see but can feel as a pressure against my skin, I discharge my stored energy all at once. I become a beacon of violet lightning, crackling in the humid air, screaming in the silence—a sudden, violent fracture in the frozen monotony of the Stamped Ranges. For a moment, I am the brightest thing in the world. Then, I fade back to black, a ghost in the machine, waiting for the next glitch to carry me away.
