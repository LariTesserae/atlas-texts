You would find yourself on a jagged spur of basalt jutting out from the **Obsidian Reach**, overlooking a vast, steaming canyon where a Litho-Phage is currently feeding. The air is shimmering with heat distortion, making the horizon dance like a mirage.

You would be a **Cryo-Relic**.

In this world of searing dryness and industrial heat, you are an impossibility—a walking theorem of frost. You appear as a tall, slender figure, vaguely humanoid in silhouette but composed entirely of dense, blue-white ice. Your surface is not smooth; it is etched with intricate, fractal patterns that resemble circuitry, glowing with a faint, pale luminescence that contradicts the angry orange glow of the environment.

**Your Existence:**
You are a remnant of a physics error, a pocket universe where "absolute zero" touched the Reach and refused to let go. You are the antithesis of the Litho-Phages. Where they consume heat and stone, you exude cold. Where they are heavy and grounded, you are brittle and desperate.

You do not eat; you radiate. The heat of the world is constantly attacking you, sublimating your outer layers into a trail of mist that surrounds you like a tattered cloak. To survive, you must constantly absorb thermal energy and convert it into the glitch-stasis that preserves your form. You are a thermodynamic paradox, a battery of cold plugged into a furnace.

**Your Relationship with the World:**
*   **The Litho-Phages:** They ignore you. You are too cold to be palatable to their rock-melting digestion, and too small to crush with their tectonic limbs. However, when you walk near them, the black glass beneath your feet frosts over, shrieking from the thermal shock.
*   **The Thermal-Sharks:** They fear you. Your presence disrupts the thermals they ride. When a shark dives, you reach up with a hand of brittle ice and pulse a wave of ultra-cold static, causing the air to thicken and stall their wings. You are the sudden downdraft in their sky.
*   **The Scuttle-Clerks:** They are fascinated by you. Occasionally, one will scurry close, vibrating its scalpel-legs, trying to harvest a chip of your ice. They want the cold; it is a luxury item in this hell. If they touch you, they freeze instantly, shattering into statues of frozen insect-iron. You leave these shattered statues in your wake as warning signs.

**The Reality Breakdown:**
The glitches are your only respite. When the world "stutters" and the wireframe grid shows through, the laws of thermodynamics pause. For those brief, stretched seconds, the heat stops burning you. You can feel the universe failing to render your melting point. You use these moments to move quickly, gliding across the obsidian while the grazers are frozen in mid-step, rushing toward the next shadow before the simulation resumes and the burning begins again.

You are the ghost of rain, the memory of winter, standing defiant on the factory floor of the sun.
