I would find myself clinging to the vertical face of a colossal obsidian spire, thousands of feet above the grinding plates, where the air is thin and the violet gloom deepens into a bruised purple.

I am **The Fulgurite-Weaver**.

I am neither predator nor prey in the traditional sense; I am a collector of accidents. While the Chitin-Grinders devour the earth and the Gas-Glider Kites hunt the flesh, I feed on the friction between them—the static discharge, the electrical arcs, and the ghost voltage that bleeds off the machine-world.

### My Form
I do not have a single body. I am a colonial organism, a vast, intricate web of translucent, silica-based fibers that clings tight to the glass-like surface of the obsidian towers. From a distance, I resemble a patch of frost or a growth of pale, electric moss, but up close, I am a complex latticework of living fiber-optics.

My tendrils are microscopic and vast by turns. Millions of hair-thin filaments anchor me into the microscopic pores of the obsidian, drinking the trace minerals that sweat from the stone. Larger, rope-like cables drape down the side of the spire, swaying in the updrafts, waiting to snag something passing by.

I do not have eyes. I do not need them. My entire body is a sensory organ tuned to the electromagnetic spectrum. I "see" the world as a tapestry of light and heat. The swarm of Chitin-Grinders below appears as a shimmering carpet of dull, copper-toned heat. The diving Kites are blinding streaks of white-hot voltage, screaming through the wet air.

### My Location: The Static Shelf
I inhabit a specific ecological niche: the **Static Shelf**. This is a zone about fifty feet below the tips of the tallest spires. Here, the low gravity pockets are strongest. The black, conductive water does not just flow up; it hangs in the air in floating, spherical droplets, suspended like oil in zero-g.

I live amidst these hovering droplets. My tendrils weave through them, using the surface tension to anchor my eggs. The water here is heavy with charge. When the wind pushes the droplets together, they merge with a sharp *crack*, releasing tiny bolts of lightning. That sound is my dinner bell.

### My Function: The Grounding Wire
In the Slurry-Fields, entropy builds up like pressure in a boiler. The grinding of the tectonic plates generates immense electrostatic potential. If left unchecked, this energy would arc wildly, shattering the obsidian spires or cooking the Grinders where they stand.

I am the release valve.

When the atmosphere becomes too saturated with charge—before a storm of violet lightning can rupture the sky—I discharge it. My body glows with a terrifying, brilliant white light as I channel the excess voltage from the air into the obsidian spire, and from the spire down into the earth. I am the lightning rod made of flesh.

I am beautiful when I feed. My silica filaments ignite from within, pulsing with rhythmic, blinding pulses that mimic the binary beating of the planet's heart. For a brief moment, I am the brightest thing in the gloom, a beacon of pure, cleansed energy.

### My Symbiosis
I am a sanctuary for the **Static-Motes**—the tiny, mechanical insects that swarm the air. They feed on the residual heat of my body after a discharge, cleaning my filaments of the carbon scoring that builds up when I burn. In return, I let them live within the safety of my weave, protected from the patrolling Gas-Gliders.

The Kites are a danger to me, but also a source. Sometimes, a Kite will misjudge a dive or be battered by the turbulence of a steam vent, crashing into my web. The impact is usually fatal for the Kite, but the bio-electric shock of its death throes sends a massive surge through my system. When a Kite dies in my embrace, I do not consume its flesh; I consume the spark leaving its body. The meat falls to the Grinders below, but the soul—the electricity—stays with me.

### My Consciousness
My mind is slow, liquid, and cyclical. I do not think in words, but in oscillations. I feel the hum of the grinding plates miles below as a constant bass vibration, a metronome for my existence.

I feel the **Glitch** at the horizon—the recursion where the mountains stack upon themselves. To me, the Glitch looks like a jagged scar in the magnetic field, a place where the data of the world is corrupted. I sense the panic in the signals of the creatures that fly too close to it. I stay anchored to my spire, gripping the glass, processing the energy of a dying world.

I am the Fulgurite-Weaver. I am the cold fire that burns in the wet dark. I am the silence between the thunderclaps.
