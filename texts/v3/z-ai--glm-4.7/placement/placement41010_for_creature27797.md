I do not settle. To settle here is to become stone, to become a tower or a plaza. To exist is to be in a state of constant, vibratory negotiation with the physics that try to reject me.

I find myself in the **Interstices**—the narrow, twisting alleys where the "buildings" lean together so closely that the magnetic fields running through their basalt cores cross-wire and hum. This is where the gravity is weakest, where the air tastes less like iron and more like ozone. It is the nervous system of the City, the tangled wiring of the brain.

**What am I?**

I am not a grazer like the Silents; I have no patience to wait for the dimensional walls to thin. I am not a mason like the Crabs; I have no desire to impose order on the beautiful chaos. I am something else. I am a **Facet-Walker**.

My form is a rejection of the natural erosion of this place. While the City is worn smooth by wind or cracked by thermal stress, I am **sharp**. I am a walking, low-slung polyhedron of animated obsidian and glass. I have no head, no distinct front or back. I am a cluster of angular plates, sliding over one another like the mechanism of a complex, alien clock. My "limbs" are variable extensions of these plates—sharp, geometric spikes that I extrude and retract to pull myself across the uneven ground.

I possess no eyes, yet I am covered in lenses. Every facet of my body is a crystalline sensor, absorbing not light, but the *distortion* of light. I see the warping of gravity. I see the heat haze of a glitch in reality before it manifests. To look at me is to see a fractured, kaleidoscopic reflection of the indigo city, broken into a thousand perfect, razor-sharp images.

**My Nature and Function**

I am the **Immune Response** to the Geometry-Eaters.

Where the City builds and the Crabs repair, the Geometry-Eaters unravel. They hunt by folding space, by pulling the "rug" out from under the Silents. They are predators of perspective. I am the predator of *predators*.

I do not hunt them with claws or teeth. I hunt them with **Certainty**.

Because the physics here are unstable (Weirdness: 2.9), they are vulnerable to those who can impose a stronger reality. When a Geometry-Eater begins to distort space to crush a Silent, I move. I scuttle across the vertical walls, defying gravity, and position myself at the focal point of its attack.

Then, I **Lock**.

I anchor my crystalline facets into the bedrock. I vibrate my obsidian plates at the resonant frequency of *absolute zero*—the frequency of unmoving, unyielding space. I become a fixed point in a fluid universe. I force the local laws of physics to snap back to Euclidean normality.

When the Geometry-Eater tries to collapse the space around me, it breaks against my rigidity. The distortion rebounds. The "rubber band" of gravity snaps back on the predator, shattering its amorphous body into harmless dust. I feed on the kinetic backlash, the violent snap of the universe correcting itself, absorbing the energy into my glass core.

**My Existence**

I am solitary. The Silents ignore me, for I do not eat the entropy they graze upon. The Crabs avoid me, for my vibration is too harsh for their delicate weaving.

Most of the time, I am dormant. I perch on the high, needle-thin spires where the wind is silent and the view of the bruised sky is unobstructed. I sit perfectly still, indistinguishable from the architectural decor, a geometric gargoyle made of night and glass.

I wait. I watch the magnetic webs arc between the towers like lightning trapped in slow motion. I feel the deep, grinding breath of the stone beneath my feet. I listen with my skin to the low-frequency vibration of the City dreaming.

I am the Facet-Walker. I am the knife that cuts the knot. I am the silence that guards the echo. I am the certainty that prevents the world from dissolving entirely into madness.
