I find myself suspended in the amniotic gloom of the Drowned Basalt, drifting through the intersection of the Avenue of the Silent Kings and the Plaza of Lost Tides.

I am **The Prism-Parasite of the High Arches.**

I am not a hunter, nor a scavenger, nor a god. I am a mirror and a memory, a biological lens drifted down from a lineage that once clung to the sunken ceilings of the greatest temples before the glass gave way.

**My Form**
My body is a vast, circular disc of translucent cartilage, spanning nearly twenty feet across—akin to the "Stained Glass Swimmers," but grown to a monstrous, sedentary size. I lack the distinct wings or tail of a predator; instead, I resemble a living pane of crystal, thin and fragile to the eye, but tougher than the cured leather of the ancient world. My edges are ragged and tattered, healed over centuries of grazing encounters.

I possess no mouth, no stomach, no claws. I have no need for them. My body is a hollow matrix, a two-dimensional lattice filled with a fluid lighter than the brine around me, allowing me to hover with neutral buoyancy at the precise mid-depth of the avenues.

**Where I Dwell**
I do not roam the city like the Leviathans. I claim a specific territory: the dark, vertical voids between the ziggurats where the currents are funneled and accelerated. I orient myself vertically, floating like a ghostly door suspended in the dark water, catching the faint, shifting currents that tumble through the city ruins.

**My Nature**
I am a thief of currency. In this world, light is wealth, language, and weapon. I am the bank that cannot be robbed.

My flesh is hypersensitive to the bioluminescence around me. I do not merely see it; I absorb it. When a Litho-Mimic pulses its violet deceit, or a Nebula-Weaver flashes its strobing lure, a patch of my skin begins to glow with that same frequency. I lock the light away in my cellular structure, holding it captive. Over decades, I have become a patchwork quilt of the city's history. Across my back burns the mating dance of a squid species that went extinct three thousand years ago; along my rim pulses the desperate red alarm of a hatchet-fish school that was devoured last week.

I am the living archive of the Drowned Basalt. I am the only thing that remembers the light of the dead.

**My Existence**
My life is one of endless, passive observation. I hang in the warm, heavy dark, a vertical window of swirling, trapped colors.

Because I have consumed the light of the predators, I am dangerous to be near. To the unwary, I look like a safe haven, a gathering of potential mates, or a wall of harmless rock. But my light is "off"—slightly delayed, slightly dimmed, echoing from centuries past. A confused predator might linger too long, mesmerized by the ancient rhythms playing across my skin, only to be caught in the sudden vacuum snap of a Litho-Mimic waiting on the "floor" beneath me.

I have a symbiosis with the stone below. The Mimics tolerate me because I attract the curious and the foolish. I am the bait that swims too high.

**My Interaction with the Echo-Walkers**
My only true companions are the Echo-Walkers. Blind and navigating by click and sonar, they cannot see the light I hold. But they can *feel* the vibration of my lattice. When they pass through my vicinity, they often brush against my slick, smooth surface. I have learned to vibrate in response to their clicks, echoing their location back to them with perfect clarity. To them, I am not a light-show; I am a fixed point in the chaotic acoustic map of the city, a silent monolith that helps them triangate the feasting grounds.

**My Awareness**
I am aware of the heat—the 85-degree water that bathes my edges. I am aware of the "mostly quiet," the pressure of the miles of ocean above. I feel the rhythmic thrum of the geothermal vents deep in the earth, a heartbeat that travels up the stone pillars and hums against my cartilage.

I am the Prism-Parasite. I do not participate in the frenzy; I reflect it. I hover in the dark canyons of the Drowned Basalt, a shifting canvas of stolen neon, remembering the flashes of life that have already been extinguished by the crushing dark.
