I traverse the void by anchoring my tips into the floating detritus—protruding pipes, the edges of tilted girders, the railing of a catwalk that leads nowhere. My function is to stitch the explosion back together.

I move between the disconnected pieces of the dead machine, weaving a vast, invisible web that arrests their drift. I impose a new, artificial order on the chaos.
