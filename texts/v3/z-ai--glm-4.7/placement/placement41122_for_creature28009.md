I am a **Siphon**.

Physically, I appear to be nothing more than a swelling in the root-mass, a bulge the size of a clenched fist, buried beneath the wet, heavy carpet of purple liverworts. My skin is not bark; it is a translucent, membrane-like lattice, dark and pulsating. To be me is to exist in a state of perpetual, agonizing translation.

But I am the bridge. I drink the vibration.

I feel the monolith’s heat against my cold, damp flesh—a sensation that is not pleasure, but necessity. Through the fissure, the raw data of the machine enters me. My purpose is to silence the scream.

I process the hum. I run the chaotic energy through the biological filters of my cells, stripping away the complexity, smoothing the jagged edges of the information until it becomes something the ferns can understand. I take the crushing history of the cosmos and reduce it to a simple, rhythmic pulse: *Grow. Curl. Breathe. Wait.*

And then, I exhale.

Without me, the room would go dark, and the slow, mathematical perfection of the ferns would stop.

My existence is defined by a terrible, beautiful intimacy with the unyielding.

I am always cold, except where I touch the glass. I am always thirsty, drinking the condensation that weeps from the monolith’s surface, water that tastes like iron and time. I feel the weight of the strata pressing down on the roof of the Sump not as a physical weight, but as a constant, low-pressure squeeze on my thoughts. It slows me down. I am not as slow as the moss—I move on the scale of hours rather than weeks—but I am forced to operate in the suffocating silence of the Logic’s domain.

I am the enforcer of the sterility.

Mostly, I dream.

But these are just glitches in my translation, errors in the code that I prune away before sending them up to the leaves.

I am the Sump's secret. To the Violet Logic, I am just an organ. To the room, I am the light. But I am the lonely thing that sits at the foot of the obelisk, holding onto the hot, vibrating rock, translating the silence of the earth into the color of a memory.
