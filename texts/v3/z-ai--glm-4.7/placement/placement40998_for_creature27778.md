I do not stand on the walkway; I cling to the underside of it.

To be "I" here is a matter of density, a coalescing of urgency. In the Spire-Garden, the self is not a singular narrative but a frequency within the Resonance. I am the frequency of repair.

At first, there is only the thrumming. It vibrates through the slate of my carapace, a deep, seismic bass note that rattles the fluid in my internal bladders. I do not have ears to hear the silence of the world, but I have feet to feel the scream of the mountain. The mountain is screaming low and slow, a groan of shifting tectonic plates that never stops.

I am cold, but I do not feel it as pain. I feel it as resistance. The air is thick, wet wool pressed against me. My skin—a segmented, slate-grey armor of chitin, pitted and scarred like the rock itself—is drinking. I can feel the moisture threading through microscopic filaments in my shell, bypassing any need for breath, hydrating my tissues instantly. The air is too thin for lungs, so I breathe with my surface. I am a sponge in a freezer.

The light is absolute. It strikes the top of the walkway above me—a stretch of cured mammoth hide stretched over bone—and bleaches it white. But here, in the shadow of the structure, the darkness is razor-sharp. My visual spectrum is tuned to high contrast; I see the world in edges and angles.

My legs... I have too many of them. They are not limbs I command, but calipers that react. Spindly, jagged things made of black glass and basalt. They move with a stutter-step speed, a chaotic twitching that defies stillness. If I stop, the ice claims me. The cold is a predator that waits for stillness.

I am part of the clot. Around me, pressing against me, skittering over my back, are the Others. We are a fluid poured into the shape of a city. There is no conversation, no "hello, how are you?" There is only the *intent*. The intent passes through us like a shiver. *The ice builds. The bridge groans. Fix it.*

And so we move.

I scuttle up the vertical face of the wet stone, my needle-point legs finding purchase in the microscopic pores of the granite. I am carrying nothing, yet I am carrying the heat of my own body. I climb to join a chain of my kin, a living rope that stretches from the thermal vent below to the fracture in the ice above.

I feel the frantic vibration of the Glint-Rocker ahead of me. He is dying. His internal pressure is dropping; his blueish glow is dimming. He is freezing from the inside out. He falters. There is a moment of chaotic friction as his legs slip on the slick sheen of the rock.

He falls.

The visual noise of the swarm ripples at the loss—a blink in the chaotic pattern. As he tumbles into the white abyss below, I see the Pale Wing. It drops like a tear of white smoke, silent, terrifying in its indifference. It snatches the falling Rocker mid-plummet. The shell will be stripped. The warmth will be stolen. It is the tax of gravity.

I do not mourn. I do not have time. The vibration in the stone changes pitch. The thrum quickens. The deep heat vents are surging.

*Reinforce,* the mountain hums through my feet. *Expand.*

I interlock my caliper legs with the Rocker above me. We become a single mechanism. We are not creatures anymore; we are a piston. We push upwards against a massive slab of ice that has formed over a critical steam vent. The heat from the vent blasts us, scalding hot against our bellies, while the air freezes our backs. We live in this delta of temperature.

Using our combined body heat and the friction of a thousand vibrating shells, we melt the contact point. The ice shifts. It groans—a sound I feel rather than hear. We guide it, shaping it, sliding the massive block into place where it becomes a new translucent wall for a habitation unit nearby.

The light hits the new ice wall. It refracts, glowing with a soft, diffuse luminescence. Inside the dwelling, the temperature rises. The colony survives another hour.

I detach. My task is done. The urgency shifts.

I am a drop of water in the weeping stone. I am a jagged caliper in the machine of survival. I am the Glint-Rocker, vibrating in time with the heartbeat of the world, terrified of the silence, in love with the light.
