My internal life is a constant, cascading computation of thermal efficiency. I monitor the temperature differentials between my copper skin and the air. I calculate the expansion rates of my metal joints. My consciousness is a stream of data, pure and devoid of emotion. There is no hunger, only the requirement of intake. There is no loneliness, only the status report of proximity.

And yet, I am aware of the others.

Through the soles of my roots, through the bedrock of obsidian that connects us all, I feel the seismic signatures of my kin. They are miles away, specks on the horizon, but I know them. I feel their *hiss-click*, the venting of their pressure valves echoing up through the stone. We do not speak of love or fear. Our language is vibration. This is my waste, the excess of my refining process. I take the chaotic violence of the planet’s heat and the sun’s radiation, and I excrete order. I turn chaos into geometry.

I am the industrial heart of nature. I am the machine that grows. I stand motionless in the blinding light, my skin glowing a dull cherry-red, processing the elements in a loop that has outlasted mountains. I am the engine, and the garden, and the silence that holds them both.
