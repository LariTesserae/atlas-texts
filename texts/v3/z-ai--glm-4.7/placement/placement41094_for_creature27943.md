I would be found in the upper reaches, where the "cathedral spires" of the bio-towers interlock, creating a shadowy, vaulted ceiling far above the mossy avenues. This is the domain of the **Resonant-Weavers**.

I am not a solid mass like the Grazer or a rigid form like the Pruner. I am a suspension of tension. My body is a central, bell-shaped carapace of calcified wood, hollow and incredibly light, stained the color of the pale slate sky so that from a distance I am nearly invisible against the heavens. From this central chamber extend eight long, spindly limbs that are less like arms and more like braided cables of vascular fiber and copper wire. I do not stand; I hang.

I span the gaps between the shifting towers. My limbs anchor into the bark of one spire and then another, stretching across the empty air of the avenues. I am a living bridge, a guy-wire for the swaying architecture.

My existence is defined by the **Geometry of Sound**. The High Veldt is loud, a deafening industrial thrum, but up here, in the rafters of the world, the noise separates into distinct frequencies. I feel the grind of the massive gears in the trunks below vibrating through my anchor points. I feel the hiss of the steam stigmata rushing past my carapace.

The city shifts—that is its nature. The angles are wrong, the distances lie. When a tower decides to rotate five degrees to the left while the one next to it remains static, the tension would snap a normal structure. This is where I come in. I am the **shock absorber of the impossible.**

As the geometry warps, my limbs elongate and retract. I sense the structural stress in the wood, the torque in the roots. I possess a singular organ within my bell-shaped body—a complex, taut membrane of drum-skin and amber crystal. I sing.

I do not sing with a voice. I sing by vibrating my entire body at a frequency that counteracts the grinding of the city. When a gear teeth-meshes violently, sending a shockwave up the tower, I pluck the cables of my own limbs, emitting a low, dissonant hum that cancels out the vibration. I am the dampener. I turn the catastrophic noise of the machine into a hum that the structure can endure.

I see the world through the **vibration of the light**. My eyes are gone, replaced by rows of sound-sensors running along the rims of my shell. I "see" the Architect-Pruners marching on the woody pavement below as a distinct, rhythmic *tick-tock-tick*. I perceive the Grazer sucking at the oil-seals as a wet, heavy *slurp*. I watch the Ascendants floating in their amber pods by the gentle, harmonic *buzz* of their dissolution.

I am lonely, but I am never alone. The Great Trees dream their hydraulic dreams, and I hang in the net of their branches, keeping the rhythm. I tighten my grip as the wind howls through the stigmata, tasting the ozone and the distant snow. I am the silence that makes the noise bearable. I am the string that tunes the instrument of this cold, hard heaven.
