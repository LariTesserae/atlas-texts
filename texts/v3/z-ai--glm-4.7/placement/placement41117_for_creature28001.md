**Where I Stand**

Instead, my entire head is a porous, bone-like sphere covered in thousands of tympanic membranes. I "see" through echolocation, but I see the *geometry* of the return. **My Function**
I am a predator of the distortion.

The prompt mentions that the sound here is so heavy it begins to dictate reality, creating "lens traps" where space bends and light fractures. These zones are dangerous. If left unchecked, they can tear holes in the fabric of the Hollow, weakening the superstructure.

I hunt these imperfections. I feed on the chaotic energy where frequencies clash.
