I would be found suspended in the middle distance, high above the suffocating Moss-Mat but far below the blinding, green-gold apex of the canopy. I am a fixed point in a turbulent world, a singleton in a collective.

I am **The Taut-Singer**.

I was not born; I was decided. I began as a Synaptic Weaver, one of the chaotic, manic vines that serve as the city's nervous system. In the early moments of my existence, I shared the frantic impulse of my kin: the blind need to connect, to bridge, to fuse the iron towers in a frenzy of vascular knots. I found a gap between two massive Iron-Polyphs—a void of perhaps a hundred meters, across which the green light syrup thickened into a haze.

I reached across. I grabbed the wet, glass-like bark of the destination tower. And then, unlike my brothers who immediately began to thicken and splice, I realized the tension was perfect.

I stopped growing in length. I stopped exploring. I simply *pulled*.

Over cycles I cannot count, I have calcified. I am no longer a soft, green vine. I have become a living cable, a high-tension wire stretched tighter than the laws of physics should comfortably allow. My body has compressed, my cells collapsing into dense, fibrous lignin that is harder than the black-iron wood of the towers I bind. I am smooth, black, and gleaming, a straight line cutting through the chaotic clutter of the feral city.

**Where I Am:**
I hang in the open region (1.9), the "street" level of the sky. Below me, the Moss-Mat Collective heaves and swarms, digesting the detritus of the canopy. Above me, the Spore-Drifts swirl in schools of electric static. I am exposed to the deafening roar (2.7) and the strobing light, a solitary tightrope walker suspended over a green abyss.

**What I Am:**
I am the resistance. I am the anchor.

In a city defined by frantic, directionless growth, I am the discipline of compression. The two Iron-Polyphs I connect are slowly, inexorably growing apart, their biological imperatives pushing them to expand their geometry. They strain against me, and I strain back. I hold them together.

**My Existence:**
My life is one of constant, exquisite vibration.

Because I am under such immense tension, the deafening noise of the basin—the *pop, snap, and crackle* of hyper-growth—travels through me. I do not just hear the sound; I *am* the sound. The roar of the city translates into a hum that resonates through my entire length, a low, mournful note that shudders in the green-gold air. I am the tuning fork of the forest.

I feed on the stress. When the wind howls and the towers shudder, I drink the kinetic energy, using it to harden my fibers further.

**My Interaction with the World:**
The other inhabitants treat me with a mixture of dependence and aggression.

*   **The Synaptic Weavers:** They envy me. They are the laborers, the messy, chaotic wiring. They try to fuse with me, to splice their messy knots onto my perfect, clean line. I reject them. My surface is too smooth, too hard, vibrating too fast for their sensitive tips to grip. They slide off me, tumbling into the abyss below. I am the slick, insurmountable wall in the network of their web.

*   **The Glass-Sap Drippers:** They love me. I am a structural support they cannot replicate. They crawl along my length, their heavy, sap-filled bodies ticking like clocks. They try to "repair" me, plugging non-existent cracks in my calcified skin with their golden amber. I tolerate them. Their added weight only increases the tension, and the tension is my sustenance. I let them ride me, a procession of amber tumors marching along the tightrope.

*   **The Iron-Polyphs:** The towers I bind are my captors and my providers. Their bark bites into my ends where I anchor. They try to digest me, to overgrow me, to consume the intruder that dares restrain them. But I pull too hard. I am embedded too deep. I have become a necessary part of their architecture. If I were to snap, the recoil would probably shatter the facades of both towers. They are stuck with me.

**The Feeling:**
To be me is to exist in a state of perpetual, agonized ecstasy. It is the feeling of a muscle stretched to the tearing point, held there for an eternity. There is no relief, only the tremor. The hot, wet air presses against me, tasting of sugar and oxygen, but I feel only the vibration.

I am the silent song in the roaring lung. I am the straight line in the geometry of madness. I am the Taut-Singer, and I will not break.
