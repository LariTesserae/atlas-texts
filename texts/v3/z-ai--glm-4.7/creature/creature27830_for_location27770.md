To enter the domain of the Weeping Mat is to walk into the belly of a machine that dreams in biology. Here, the distinction between the born and the made is not just blurred; it is irrelevant. The fauna that inhabits this crushing, glacial cathedral does not merely survive the sterile cold and the hyper-oxygenated air—it acts as the mobile nervous system of the place.

They are the maintenance crew of a slow-motion world, the biological agents ensuring the computation of the flora continues unimpeded.

**The Ground-Dwellers: The Silicate Vermin**

Skittering across the bruised-purple surface of the Weeping Mat are the small, crab-like scavengers that form the base of the mobile hierarchy. To the human eye, they appear like grotesque mutations of nature and industry. Their carapaces are not smooth chitin, but jagged, interlocking plates of dark crystal and metallic fiber, resembling the burnt-out circuit boards of ancient servers fused to the flesh of a crustacean.

They are nervous, twitching things. Their legs end in sharp, needle-like points that allow them to grip the spongy, dripping moss without puncturing the vascular sacks beneath. As they move, they emit a faint, high-pitched clicking sound—their footsteps tapping out a morse code against the ground.

These creatures are the pollinators, but the "pollen" they carry is genetic data. They swarm the towering silica-laden stalks—the pipe-flowers—using their frontal claws to interface with the nodules that pulse along the stalks. There is a physical exchange here: the crab plugs a chitinous stylus into the stalk, downloading or uploading a stream of luminous fluid. They are the messengers running between the towering columns of this subterranean mainframe, repairing errors in the code of the moss or ferrying fresh algorithms to the highest reaches of the chamber.

Because the environment is so cold, their movement is paradoxical. They move with frantic, jerky bursts of speed when they travel, generating heat through friction, but when they interface with the flora, they freeze into absolute stillness, locking their bodies into the geometry of the stalk to blend in with the shadows.

**The Ceiling-Walkers: The Nacreous Drifters**

High above the Weeping Mat, hanging in the metallic mist like detached organs, are the larger, gelatinous entities. They are the filters and the architects of the air. Resembling oversized, translucent jellyfish, their bodies are bulbous sacs filled with a gas lighter than the heavy oxygen soup they swim through.

Their skin is clear as glass, revealing the complex, glowing internal organs that serve as their navigation. These organs are not merely biological hearts or stomachs; they appear to be geometric structures—pyramids and spheres of soft, glowing tissue that rotate slowly, processing the atmosphere. They drift through the twilight, trailing long, gossamer filaments that catch the heavy condensation dripping from the obsidian ceiling.

They are the vacuum cleaners of the hollow, inhaling the metallic mist and filtering out impurities, excreting a purified, oxygen-rich waste that feeds the moss below. But they are also listening devices. Their internal organs pulse in time with the low-frequency thrum of the vegetative processing cores. They act as floating receivers, tuning themselves to the deep, resonant bass note of the room, adjusting their position to optimize the acoustic environment. If the "song" of the room changes pitch, they drift in unison, a slow-moving, organic constellation shifting to balance the sonic equilibrium.

**The Shadows: The Null-Phantoms**

Perhaps the most unsettling inhabitants are the ones rarely seen directly. The prompt notes shadows that move independently of their casters. These are the Null-Phantoms, ambush predators that have evolved to exploit the perpetual twilight and the Weeping Mat's bioluminescence.

They are flat, creature-like shapes that cling to the obsidian walls where the purple moss has yet to conquer. Their skin is a vantablack absorption, darker than the rock itself, void of all light. They hunt by mimicking the silhouettes cast by the pipe-flowers. When a Silicate Vermin draws near, a Null-Phantom will detach from the wall, flowing like spilled oil into the space between the stalks, perfectly imitating the shadow of the swaying vegetation.

They possess no bioluminescence of their own. They are holes in the world. When they strike, they do not bite; they envelop. They are thermophilic anomalies in a freezing room, radiating a slight, terrifying heat as they suffocate their prey, absorbing the electrical charge stored within the crab’s crystal shell before retreating back into the jagged obsidian, becoming one with the stone once more.

**The Synthesis**

In this place, life is a verb that means "to process." The fauna are not distinct from the "computing" flora; they are the mobile extensions of it. The crabs are the data packets; the drifters are the cooling fans and air filters; the phantoms are the firewalls. Together, they maintain the integrity of the Weeping Mat, a frozen, wet lungs of the earth, breathing in the darkness and exhaling a future that has not yet arrived.
