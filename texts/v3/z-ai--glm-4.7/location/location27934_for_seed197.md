There is no technology (0.3). No metal, no glass, no fire. The light source (1.9) is natural but diffused—filtered through layers of translucent leaves that glow with a mild bioluminescence, keeping the city in a perpetual, hazy twilight.

The predators eat the prey, the prey eats the moss, the moss drinks the condensation, and the roar of it all keeps the reality of the place vibrating on a frequency just at the edge of breaking.
