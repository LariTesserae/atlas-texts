This is not a place that is being destroyed by nature; it is a place that has become a hybrid organism. It is a greenhouse built by ghosts, powered by a dying engine, where the only motion left is the slow, loud heartbeat of the earth and the relentless, silent growth of the heat-drinking weeds. You stand in the center, sweating in the dark, listening to the roar, watching the steam rise, and you feel incredibly, startlingly alone.
