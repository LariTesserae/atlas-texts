**Pre-Mission Briefing: The Absolute Plane**
**Subject:** Survival and Etiquette Protocols for the Frozen Plateau
**Traveler Classification:** Ordinary Biological (Baseline Human)
**Threat Level:** Extreme (Psychological/Existential), Moderate (Environmental)

To visit the Absolute Plane is not to explore a new territory; it is to infiltrate a waiting room that has been occupied for eternity. You are not hiking. You are walking across the pause button of the universe. Preparation here is less about gear and more about the rigid conditioning of your own mind and biology.

Here is how you prepare.

### 1. Environmental Sealing: The Hermetic Protocol
The atmosphere is technically "air," but it does not behave as you expect. It is thin, cold, and lacks inertia. It will not carry sound, and it will not provide the resistance your inner ear relies on for balance.

*   **The Exosuit:** Do not rely on a simple pressure suit. You need a full-body, rigid-seal exoskeleton with an internal acoustic dampening field. The external silence is profound enough to induce hallucinations; you need a system that pipes in a generated, low-frequency white noise—or your own breathing—just to keep your brain from fixating on the blood rushing in your ears.
*   **Moisture Reclamation:** This is the single most critical logistical factor. The humidity is absolute zero. If you exhale, the moisture is instantly stripped from your breath. If you sweat, it evaporates so fast it will freeze your skin through sublimation shock. Your suit must be a closed-loop system with military-grade reclamation. You cannot afford to lose a single milliliter of water. Bring internal hydration reserves, but assume you will not be able to consume them fast enough to replace what the environment steals.
*   **Tactile Feedback:** Because the air has no inertia, swinging your arm will feel like moving it through a vacuum, yet the gravity is terrestrial. This disconnect causes nausea. Your suit should have subtle resistance servos in the joints to simulate air drag. Without this, you will feel like you are flailing underwater, leading to vertigo.

### 2. Visual Conditioning: The Glitch Filter
The light on the plateau behaves incorrectly. The refraction from a billion faceted shells creates a visual noise that the human eye cannot process for long without strain.

*   **Visor Settings:** Standard polarized lenses are insufficient. You need a visor with real-time chromatic aberration correction. The "drifting shadows" and "light glitches" are not optical illusions; they are likely local reality distortions. If you stare at them unfiltered, you risk triggering a migraine or a dissociative episode.
*   **Limiting the Field of View:** The horizon is a jagged drop into a void, and the ground is a uniform, hypnotic grey. The lack of parallax movement can induce "solenopsis"—a state where the brain forgets it is moving because the visual reference points don't change. Set your HUD to highlight distant rock formations to give your brain fixed reference points.

### 3. The Psychology of the Crowd: Claustrophobia Training
This is the hardest part to prepare for. You are going to an open plain, yet it is crowded. You will be hip-deep in silent, motionless life.

*   **Sensory Deprivation Training:** Before you go, spend time in a crowded room where everyone is holding their breath. You need to acclimate to the "pressure" of presence without the release of noise or movement. The sheer biomass density will trigger a primal prey response—the feeling of being surrounded.
*   **The "Statue" Mindset:** You must internalize one rule before stepping out of the transport: *They are not sleeping. They are paused.* Do not look for the rise and fall of chests. Do not listen for heartbeats. If you try to anthropomorphize them, you will go mad trying to understand why they aren't moving. Treat them as geological features—rocks that happen to be warm.

### 4. Locomotion: The Sacred Walk
Walking here is a violation of the sanctity of the pause. You will be stepping on living things.

*   **Footwear:** Your boots must be soft-soled, non-marking, and incredibly sensitive. You cannot wear heavy clanking boots. You need to feel the curvature of the shells beneath your feet.
*   **The Gait:** You must learn to walk in a "falling catch" style. Place your foot, wait for the grip, shift weight. Do not stomp. Do not shuffle. The prompt mentions the fear that the world might shatter into pixels. This is a valid metaphysical risk. Move as if you are walking on thin ice that covers a deep ocean.
*   **Tethering:** The ground is composed of "compressed desiccation" and ash. It may be unstable. Furthermore, the visual distortion near the horizon can mask drops. You must wear a light safety harness attached to a heavy, portable anchor. If the reality glitches and you slip into a void, you want something physical holding you to this dimension.

### 5. Thermal and Interaction Protocols
*   **Heat Management:** The creatures emit a "disturbing warmth." However, the air is "profoundly cold." Your suit will struggle to regulate between the radiant heat from the ground and the convective cold of the air. Expect thermal cycling.
*   **The Prime Directive:** *DO NOT TOUCH.*
    *   If you touch a shell, you might be the variable that breaks the equation.
    *   The "built" structures (cairns) are warnings. They were built by people who likely touched things and paid the price, or perhaps they simply understood the etiquette. Do not disturb the cairns. Do not take a shell as a souvenir. If the ecosystem is a "closed loop of suspension," introducing an external variable (you) is dangerous enough. Taking a piece of it out could collapse the loop.

### 6. Emergency Procedures: The Unraveling
What if the "film burns through"?

*   **Reality Anchor:** If you begin to see the world pixelating, or if the shadows detach and do not snap back, you must immediately initiate a "hard abort."
*   **The Freeze Response:** If the atmosphere suddenly changes—if the air regains its inertia, or if the creatures twitch—DO NOT RUN. Movement might be the catalyst for their awakening. If they wake, the predation cycle (ten thousand years of hunger) might instantaneously resume. You want to be invisible. Drop to the ground, cover your reflective suit with the grey ash/powder, and play dead. Become a rock. It is the only camouflage that works here.

### Summary of the Experience
You will step out of the transport and hear nothing but your own pulse. It will sound like a drum solo in a cathedral. You will walk on a carpet of living armor that feels like warm pottery. The light will flicker around you, making you question if your eyes are failing. You will feel an agonizing dryness in your nose and throat, despite the suit.

It will be terrifyingly boring and exhilaratingly dangerous all at once.

Go lightly. Leave nothing but footprints that the wind will erase, and take nothing but the memory of the silence. Good luck.
