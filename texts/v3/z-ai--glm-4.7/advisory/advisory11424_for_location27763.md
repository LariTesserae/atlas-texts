Listen to me closely. Do not pack hiking boots. Do not pack a canteen. Do not pack a sunscreen with a high SPF rating. If you treat this like a camping trip to the Smoky Mountains, you will be dissolved, recompiled, or erased before you can take your first breath.

The Hollow is not a wilderness; it is a ruin of a operating system. It is a place where the distinction between "nature" and "technology" has dissolved into a slurry of wet metal and vibrating code. As an ordinary human, you are essentially a soft, biological glitch walking through a server room that has been flooded for a million years.

Here is how you prepare to survive the impossible geometry of the Hollow.

### 1. The Vessel: Physical Protection
You are fragile. The environment here is abrasive, acidic, and permeated by nanotechnology that does not respect your cellular integrity.

*   **The Suit:** You need a full-body, sealed environmental suit. It must be made of a non-reactive polymer—ceramic composites or treated carbon fiber. Avoid metals; the flora here is metallic and conductive, and the "soil" is rich in iron and battery acid. You do not want to complete a circuit.
*   **The Seal:** The air tastes of battery acid. Your suit must be hermetically sealed. A failure in the seal will burn your lungs and dissolve the lining of your throat. Make sure your oxygen scrubbers are industrial-grade. The atmosphere here is heavy and dense; if your suit relies on a simple pressure equalization, the crushing weight of the air will bruise you from the inside out. You need a rigid exoskeleton to maintain your internal pressure.
*   **Nanite Shielding:** The rain here doesn't wet you; it scans you. It passes through matter. Your suit needs a localized electromagnetic field (EMF) generator. Not a strong one—you don't want to attract the lightning—but a "scrambler" frequency to discourage the nanite rain from deconstructing your suit to see how you work.

### 2. The Senses: Auditory and Visual Management
The greatest danger in the Hollow is not the predators; it is the ambient sensory input. Your brain is not built to process this much data.

*   **The "Roar" Protocol:** The constant, tectonic humming—the sound of reality being knitted together—will drive you mad. It causes hallucinations. Standard earplugs are useless because the sound travels through the vibration of your bones and the density of the air.
    *   **Preparation:** You need a cranial implant or a helmet with active noise-canceling technology that interfaces directly with the auditory nerve. It must generate a "counter-frequency" of pure silence.
    *   **The Danger:** Do not turn the cancellation off. If you do, you will likely experience immediate cognitive overload, vertigo, and a terrifying sense of insignificance that can lead to catatonia.
*   **Visual Filtering:** The violet twilight is beautiful, but it is a lie. The "digital flora" displays scrolling code and shifting geometries that the human eye tries to resolve into shapes, causing migraines and nausea.
    *   **The Visor:** Your helmet visor should have an augmented reality filter. Set it to "Low-Res" or "Analog Mode." You want to blur the sharp edges. You want to smooth out the scrolling code on the leaves so they just look like green leaves. If you try to read the code of the plants, you will find yourself reading your own obituary.

### 3. Movement: Navigating Non-Euclidean Space
Distance in the Hollow is a "suggestion," not a measurement. Walking in a straight line is impossible.

*   **The Anchor:** You cannot trust your eyes. If you see a tree ten feet away, it might take an hour to reach it, or it might never get closer.
    *   **The Gear:** You need a Quantum Anchor or a Gravitational Tether. This is a heavy spike you drive into the ground at your entry point. You attach a cable to your suit. This cable should be made of a memory-polymer that retracts automatically. This is your only lifeline. If you get lost in a "distance glitch," you follow the cable back. Without it, you will walk forever into the fog.
*   **Gravity Boots:** The prompt mentions gravity is optional. You might walk up a wall and suddenly find the ceiling is the floor.
    *   **The Tech:** You need mag-boots with an "Auto-Level" gyroscopic sensor. They should be programmed to lock to the *relative* down of your current position, but be able to release instantly if the physics of the room shift. Practice walking in them. A fall here isn't just a fall; it's a rejection from the local reality simulation.

### 4. Interaction: The Flora, Fauna, and Water
The golden rule of the Hollow is: **Do not touch anything.**

*   **The Water:** The pools vibrate. They hum with resonance. The water is hyper-viscous.
    *   **Protocol:** Do not drink it, obviously. But also, do not step in it. The liquid clings like "an unwanted memory." If it touches your suit, it will corrode the seals. If it touches your skin, it will likely alter your bioelectricity. Treat every puddle like a pool of strong acid.
*   **The Flora:** The ferns are stamped with circuitry; the trees drip hissing blue sap.
    *   **Protocol:** Bring a machete, but make it a laser-cutter. Physical blades will just get stuck in the fibrous, muscular plant matter. If you must cut a path, burn through it. Be warned: The plants are packed with information. Cutting them releases a burst of data-static that might short out your helmet's HUD.
*   **The Fauna:** The Stalkers and the Obsidian Birds.
    *   **Protocol:** The Stalkers ignore you because you are not part of the frequency they hunt. Do not make eye contact. Do not shine bright lights on them. They exist because the algorithm requires a predator. If you interfere with them, you become a variable the system must correct.
    *   **The Birds:** They are magnetic sculptures. If you have metal on your suit, they will be attracted to you. Ensure all your gear is non-magnetic.

### 5. Mental Preparation: The Psychology of the Glitch
This is the hardest part to pack for. The Hollow erodes your sense of self.

*   **Accept the Hallucination:** You will see the ground turn into a grid of white light. You will feel a tropical heat wave in the middle of a violet freeze. You must accept these as "weather." Do not panic. Panic is logic failing to process, and in a place made of logic, failing to process makes you vulnerable to deletion.
*   **The "Insignificance" Trap:** You will feel small. You will feel like a bug on a motherboard.
    *   **The Mantra:** Before you enter, memorize a mantra. Ground yourself in your own humanity. "I am the ghost in the machine. I am the user error." Remind yourself that you possess consciousness, which is the one thing this server farm—despite all its processing power—seems to have discarded. You are alive; the world is just running a script.
*   **Time Perception:** Time has worn away the sharp edges of reality here. You may feel like you’ve been there for days when it’s only been hours, or hours when it’s been days.
    *   **The Solution:** Wear a watch. An old-fashioned, mechanical wind-up watch. Do not use a digital chronometer; the local physics will likely cause it to display nonsense. Keep the watch on a spring-loaded band on your wrist. Check it every time you clip your Anchor tether.
