Traveler, you are preparing to step into the womb of the universe. This location is not a destination for the casual tourist; it is a planetary construction site, a place where the rules of stability have been temporarily suspended. If you value your continuity as a biological organism, you must respect the volatility of this geology.

Here is how you prepare for the High Geo, the Twilight Forge.

### 1. Respiratory and Thermal Physiology
The most immediate threat is not the violence of the earth, but the betrayal of the air.
*   **Oxygen Supplementation is Mandatory:** The altitude (Elevation 2.9) presents a severe hypoxic risk. Do not rely on your sea-level lungs. You must wear a pressurized delivery system or carry high-capacity supplemental oxygen. The air is "thin yet charged," which implies a lower partial pressure of breathable gas. Furthermore, the air tastes of ozone and pulverized dust; a high-grade particulate filter is non-negotiable to protect your alveoli from microscopic glass shards.
*   **Hydration Discipline:** You will not feel yourself sweating. The "dry, respiratory heat" creates a false sense of comfort. You will desiccate from the inside out. You must drink water on a strict schedule, regardless of thirst. Your suit should ideally include a humidifier for your oxygen intake to prevent your mucous membranes from cracking and bleeding in the arid atmosphere.
*   **Thermal Regulation:** The ambient temperature is "womb-warm," but the ground conducts heat upward. You are baking from the feet up. Wear boots with high thermal insulation soles (aerogel or ceramic weave) to prevent burns. Your body armor should be breathable but reflective; you need to vent your own body heat while shielding yourself from the ambient radiance of the earth.

### 2. Defensive Apparel (The "Obsidian Protocol")
The ground here is not merely sharp; it is predatory.
*   **Cut-Resistance:** Obsidian can be five times sharper than a surgical steel scalpel. If you slip and fall on standard trousers, you will sever arteries before you hit the ground. Your entire external layer—pants, jacket, gloves—must be constructed of cut-resistant fabrics (UHMWPE or woven ballistic synthetics). There should be no exposed skin.
*   **Eye Protection:** The "shattering" of the cooling rock sends shrapnel flying in a "rain of musical chimes." These are glass shards. A full-face polycarbonate shield is required. Do not rely on goggles alone; a stray shard high on the cheekbone could blind you.
*   **Sealed Joints:** The air is filled with "pulverized dust." Fine silicate dust will find its way into every seam, every joint, and every pore. Your suit must be hermetically sealed at the wrists, neck, and waist. Consider a positive-pressure system to keep dust out of your helmet.

### 3. Seismic Survival and Mobility
Walking here is not walking; it is negotiating an active bomb.
*   **The "Tremor" Gait:** Do not run. Do not stomp. You must walk with a rolling, distributing gait to minimize impact vibration. The ground is "restless," vibrating at a low frequency. Sudden sharp impacts (like jumping or running) could trigger a localized fracture beneath your feet.
*   **Tethering:** The ground heaves upward with terrifying speed (Dynamic 2.8). You could be standing on a plain one second and tossed twenty feet into the air the next. You must wear a heavy-duty climbing harness attached to a motorized winch. However, do not anchor to the *ground*—it moves. If possible, anchor your tethles to the **Metal Monoliths**. The description notes the ground splits *around* them, leaving them untouched. They are the only stable anchors in a chaotic sea.
*   **Fall Arrest:** The terrain is jagged and uneven. A fall here isn't just a tumble; it's a slide onto razor blades. Your knees and elbows must have hard-shell impact protection.

### 4. Sensory Navigation
*   **Active Sonar/Lidar:** Visibility is poor (0.4), with shadows that have no sharp edges. Depth perception will be nearly impossible in the "bruised" violet-gray twilight. Relying on your eyes to judge the distance of a basalt spire is a death sentence. Use a heads-up display (HUD) linked to Lidar or radar to paint the terrain in high-contrast wireframe. Trust the machine, not your eyes.
*   **Auditory Protection:** The silence (1.2) is misleading. When it breaks, it breaks with the sound of a "mountain snapping a dry twig." These reports are instantaneous and deafening. Wear active noise-canceling headphones that dampen sudden percussive spikes while amplifying the low-frequency hum of the earth's vibration. This will give you a few milliseconds of warning before a heave.

### 5. Psychological Preparation
This is the hardest part. This place induces existential claustrophobia.
*   **Embrace the Scale:** You are entering a "regional vastness" (2.2) where the density is sparse (1.0). You will feel incredibly small, like a mite on a shifting rug. Do not try to comprehend the whole landscape; focus on the ten feet immediately around you. Panorama views will only lead to vertigo.
*   **Accept the "Newness":** You are standing in a place with "no old things." This can be deeply unsettling to human psychology, which craves history and stability. You are in the "raw draft." You may feel a primal urge to flee the violence of creation. Acknowledge this fear, but do not let it drive you to run.
*   **The Silence of the Monoliths:** You will see the metal needles. Do not approach them expecting a welcome, and do not waste time trying to find a door. They are "nails driven through the fabric of the world." Treat them with reverence. If you must camp, camp near them for the stability they offer, but do not touch them. Their lack of weathering suggests they repel matter. If the earth moves around them, they might repel *you* too.

### Summary Checklist for the Forge:
1.  **Suit:** Full-body cut-resistant shell, ceramic insulated soles, hermetically sealed.
2.  **Head:** Full-face shield + HUD with Lidar/Thermal (to see heat signatures of rising magma). Active hearing protection.
3.  **Breath:** Pressurized O2 supply with humidifier and HEPA/Particulate filtration.
4.  **Movement:** Motorized tethering system. Anchor only to Grey Monoliths.
5.  **Mind:** Discipline your fear. You are a ghost in a geological foundry. Move softly, watch the wireframe, and breathe deeply of the tanked air.

Good luck. The world here is not finished; try not to get caught in the edits.
