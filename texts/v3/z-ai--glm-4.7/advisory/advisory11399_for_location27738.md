To the traveler preparing to enter the Hexagonal Crypt, understand this: you are not entering a habitat; you are entering a machine of light and stone that was built for a sensory spectrum you do not possess. This location is hostile to biology simply by existing. It is bright, loud, dry, and geometrically obsessive. If you treat this like a hike in a canyon, you will go blind, deaf, and mad before you have taken twenty steps.

Here is how you must prepare to survive the Cavern of Absolute White.

### 1. Ocular Fortification: Total Blackout
The single greatest threat here is not gravity or beasts, but the light. The text warns that "to look up is to go blind." This is not hyperbole. The actinic, saturated brilliance reflects off the pale gypsum floor and walls, creating an environment of 360-degree luminance. It will induce snow blindness (photokeratitis) in minutes.

*   **Welding-Grade Optics:** Standard sunglasses or even polarized glacier glasses will fail. You need tinted goggles rated for arc welding, ideally Shade 12 or higher. Your field of view will be narrow and dark, but you will retain your vision.
*   **The "No-Look" Protocol:** You must train yourself to keep your eyes strictly on the floor or your immediate feet. The instinct to look at the "ceiling" or the source of the light will be overpowering; it is a hypnotic pull. You must psychologically condition yourself to ignore the sky.
*   **Red Light Emergency Flashlight:** If your main goggles fail or are removed, do not open your eyes. Use a high-intensity red light flashlight to navigate, as red light is less likely to trigger the pain response, though in this saturation, even that may be useless.

### 2. Auditory Defense: The Anti-Vibration Rig
The sound here is not heard; it is felt. It is a low-frequency thrum (a "cello bow on a planet") that vibrates the fluid in your inner ear. This will cause immediate disorientation, nausea, and a terrifying sense of motion when you are standing still.

*   **Double-Plugged Hearing:** Earplugs alone will not stop the bone-conduction of the vibration. You need high-quality industrial over-ear ear defenders *worn over* heavy-duty foam earplugs. You are trying to create an acoustic seal so tight that your own heartbeat sounds like a jackhammer, because that is preferable to the drone of the room.
*   **Cinch Your Head:** The vibration may cause your teeth and skull to hum. Wear a tight-fitting skullcap or a buff. This helps dampen the resonance against the bone of the cranium.
*   **Anti-Nausea Medication:** Even with ear protection, the vibration will permeate your body. Take scopalamine patches or heavy-duty anti-emetics *before* you step through the portal. The conflict between your eyes (seeing a static room) and your inner ear (feeling motion/sound) will induce severe vertigo.

### 3. Dermatological and Respiratory Sealing
This place is a desiccator. It has a "parameter near zero" for water. It will suck the moisture out of your mucous membranes and skin with the "gentleness of sandpaper." The air tastes of chalk.

*   **Full-Body Impermeable Suit:** Do not expose skin. Wear a coverall that is non-porous, similar to a hazmat suit or a heavy-duty painter's Tyvek, but lined for insulation. The stone is cold (10°C), and the light is deceptively heating; you need to trap your body's moisture against your skin.
*   **Positive-Pressure Respirator with Humidification:** You cannot breathe the air directly. It will dry out your lungs and coat your alveoli in fine calcite dust. You need a respirator that not only filters particulates but also humidifies the air. Attach a hydration bladder to the respirator port. Every breath must be moist.
*   **Silicone Lubricants:** Apply medical-grade silicone oil or Vaseline to the inside of your nostrils, your lips, and your ear canals (over the plugs) to create a moisture barrier against the desiccating atmosphere.
*   **Eye Moisture:** Even with goggles, the dry air will leach tears. Use rewetting drops frequently.

### 4. Tactile and Footwear Considerations
The floor is a "labyrinth of relief carving." The text mentions "worn smooth at hip height" implying there is a texture gradient, but the floor is "built" and "hexagonal."

*   **Vibration-Dampening Boots:** You are standing on a giant, resonating speaker. Standard hiking boots will transmit the thrum directly into your shins and knees. Wear boots with thick, gel-injected soles. You want to walk on mattresses, not on stones.
*   **Knee and Elbow Pads:** You may stumble due to vertigo. The stone is harder than granite and unforgiving. If you fall, you will shatter bone.
*   **Gloves with Grip:** The stones are dry and likely dusty (despite the "no dust" note, the *potential* for powder from broken bricks exists). You need tactile feedback to feel the geometry, so wear mechanics' gloves with textured palms.

### 5. Psychological Anchoring
This is the most difficult preparation. The room is "infinite," "deceptive," and "finished." The patterns are so complex the eye cannot rest. This is a pattern-saturation nightmare. It is a place designed to induce awe and terror in equal measure.

*   **Tether System:** The scale is deceptive. If you walk twenty feet, you might feel you haven't moved, or you might suddenly be at the edge of a precipice. You must be physically tethered to a anchor point at the entrance, or to a companion. Do not rely on visual depth perception; the high contrast and lack of atmospheric perspective (haze) will destroy your ability to judge distance.
*   **The "Blind" Walk:** Because the visual texture is "too complex," looking around constantly will induce sensory overload and trance-like states. You may find yourself staring at a swirl of carving for hours, entranced by the geometry. Keep your vision limited. Blink often.
*   **Mental Mantra:** The silence (despite the roar) and the stillness are oppressive. The room feels like a "scream." You need a mental anchor—a song, a math problem, a memory—to remind you that you are biological and temporal, whereas this place is mineral and eternal.

### Summary of Gear Check:
*   **Eyes:** Shade 14 Welding Goggles (Sealed).
*   **Ears:** Silicone earplugs + Industrial Noise-Canceling Muffs.
*   **Face:** Full-face Respirator with integrated humidifier and hydration tube.
*   **Body:** Insulated, non-breathable coveralls (bright orange, ironically, so you can be seen against the beige).
*   **Feet:** Gel-cushion mountaineering boots.
*   **Stomach:** Transdermal anti-nausea patch.
*   **Safety:** 50-foot static climbing rope attached to your waist.

**Final Warning:**
Do not interact with the lithophytes (the crystal "plants"). They are silica clusters. They are likely sharp as glass. Do not attempt to break a brick. The "homogenous, bone-dry mineral heart" implies that breaking the structural integrity might cause a chain reaction of shattering in this friction-held universe. You are a visitor inside a watch. Do not touch the gears. Walk carefully, keep your head down, plug your ears, and pray the vibration doesn't stop—the silence might be louder than the roar.
