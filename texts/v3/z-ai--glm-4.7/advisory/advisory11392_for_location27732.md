**Subject:** Field Survival & Preparation Guide: The High Drought (Sector Designation: High-Density Silicate Forest)
**Clearance:** Class-C Interdimensional Traveler
**Severity:** Critical

Listen to me closely. If you are used to the lush, forgiving biospheres of Earth-Prime or the comfortable, climate-controlled domes of the Neo-Veridia sector, you need to flush those expectations right now. The High Drought is not a camping trip; it is a trek through the internal workings of a machine that forgot it was once a forest.

This place is alive, but it is alive in the way a server room is alive—humming, processing, and entirely indifferent to your comfort. If you go there thinking you can rough it with a canteen and a sturdy pair of boots, you will become a calcified statistic within the hour.

Here is your preparation protocol.

### I. Physiological Conditioning: The Internal War

**1. The Water Lockdown**
The most important number in this dossier is the water value: **0.1**.
You cannot drink the air. You cannot drink the ground. There is no dew. If you sweat, you are losing resources you cannot replace.
*   **Pre-Load:** 48 hours before departure, you must begin hyper-hydration combined with electrolyte loading. You need to turn your body into a camel.
*   **Moisture Reclamation:** A standard Nalgene bottle is a toy here. You require a closed-circuit suit with active sweat reclamation. Every exhale must pass through a condenser. You cannot afford to lose a single milliliter to the dry air. The humidity is so low it will pull water out of your eyes and skin. Expect your lips to crack and your mucous membranes to desiccate almost immediately. Pack medical-grade lubricants (saline-based) for your eyes, nose, and throat.
*   **Digestive Shutdown:** Once you arrive, switch to high-calorie, low-residue nutrient bars. You do not want to need to use the bathroom. Waste is water loss. In the High Drought, your body must become a closed loop.

**2. Auditory Calibration**
The silence rating is non-existent. The sound level is **2.5**—a deafening, harmonic drone caused by the wind moving through the hollow, silicate trees.
*   **Ear Protection:** Standard foam plugs won't cut it. You need active noise-canceling headphones with a custom-tuned dampener for low-frequency vibrations. The "roar" isn't just loud; it’s physical. It vibrates your teeth and rattles your ribs. Without protection, you will suffer from fatigue, vertigo, and eventual hearing loss within six hours.
*   **Mental Fortitude:** The sound never stops. There is no night, only a dimming of the gloom, and the wind does not sleep. You must meditate or practice dissociation techniques to tolerate the drone. If you are prone to migraines, do not go.

**3. Thermal Management**
The temperature is described as a "prickle-inducing cool."
*   **The Chill:** It is a dry, metallic cold. It seeps into joints. Dress in layers that trap air close to the body. Your outer layer should be windproof and shimmer-smooth to prevent snagging on the crystal bark.
*   **Static Charge:** The air is full of ozone and dry dust. Static electricity will be a constant enemy. Your gear must be heavily grounded. One spark from your hand could theoretically interact with the local "tech" (the biological processors), and at the very least, it will hurt like hell in the dry air.

### II. Gear & Equipment: Survival in the Server Farm

**1. The Exosuit or "Skin"**
Do not wear standard hiking fabrics. The ground is calcified root systems (harder than concrete) and the vegetation is silica-heavy (glass/ceramic).
*   **Footwear:** You need vibram-rammed soles with magnetic treads. The gravity fluctuates when the forest computes; you do not want to stumble. If you fall on the "glass" bark of a Pillar, you will slice your suit open.
*   **Suit Integrity:** The air is filled with nanoscopic spores carrying code. You must wear a hermetically sealed suit with a HEPA-grade bio-filter. If you breathe in a spore, you risk introducing foreign biological code into your system. We don't know what happens when a lung cell tries to execute plant-code, but you don't want to find out.

**2. Lighting Systems**
The light value is **1.0** (dim twilight).
*   **Illumination:** The forest generates its own neon blue and amber light. High-powered flashlights will likely blind you against the reflective surfaces or be absorbed by the smog.
*   **Recommendation:** Use low-lumen red or green light rigs to preserve your night vision and minimize attraction to the local fauna. The "darting things" (serrated manta-rays) are attracted to energy gradients. Don't be a beacon.

**3. Electronics & Navigation**
The Technology Level is **2.8**. This is a bio-digital supercomputer.
*   **Faraday Protection:** Keep all your tech—scanners, comms, GPS—inside shielded cases. The ambient radiation and biological data-transfer fields will fry unshielded circuits instantly. Your compass will spin uselessly.
*   **Navigation:** GPS is useless because the landmarks move. The trees realign every few hours.
    *   *Solution:* You must navigate by dead reckoning and inertial mapping. Drop passive physical markers (laser-etched stones) that won't be swept away. Watch the bioluminescent pulses; they seem to correlate with the "processing" cycles of the forest. If the veins of the trees pulse blue, you are safe. If they shift to red or strobe, take cover—a "realignment" (tectonic shift) is imminent.

### III. Environmental Hazards: The Dance of the Giants

**1. The Realignment**
The dynamic nature of the location is **1.9**. The floor moves. The trees rotate.
*   **The Rhythm:** You must sync your movement to the forest's cycle. Do not set up camp in a "plaza" or a clear between the roots. When the aperture closes, you will be crushed into calcified dust.
*   **Sleeping:** Never sleep on the ground. Sleep suspended. Use a hammock or a grav-anchor between two Pillars. If the ground shifts beneath you, you could fall into a fissure or be ground into the root-mat.

**2. The Fauna**
Weirdness is **2.5**. The animals look like machines.
*   **Ceramic Grazers:** The lumbering beasts with piston-legs create a rhythmic clacking. They are likely blind, sensing vibration. If you remain still and filter your sound output, they will ignore you. If you run, you trigger a predator-response algorithm.
*   **The Fliers:** The gliders ride updrafts. Keep your head on a swivel. They are serrated like knives. If one attacks, your suit needs ceramic plating; Kevlar won't stop a razor-sharp glider diving at terminal velocity.

**3. The "Bleeding" Trees**
Do not, under any circumstances, damage the vegetation.
*   **The Sap:** It isn't sap; it's conductive, gold-laced coolant. It smells of burnt sugar. If a tree is cut, the forest may identify you as a virus.
*   **Physics Anomalies:** The high density of biological computation creates gravity wells. If you feel suddenly heavy, or weightless, you are near a major processing node (a cluster of Pillars). Move away slowly. Rapid movement in a fluctuating gravity field can cause cardiac arrest.

### IV. Psychological Preparation

You are entering an urban environment that wasn't built for you. It feels like a city, but it has no doors, no writing you can read, and no temperature controls.
*   **The Scale:** You are an ant. The Pillars are hundreds of feet tall. This induces awe and agoraphobia simultaneously. The "cathedral" feeling can crush your ego.
*   **The Thirst:** The psychological toll of absolute dryness is severe. You will see mirages. You will crave water with a primal intensity that overrides logic. Pack a "hydration reserve"—a small ampoule of emergency water—not to drink, but to hold, to smell, to remind yourself that moisture exists. It keeps you sane.

**Final Briefing Note:**
The High Drought is a place of perfect, terrifying efficiency. It has no mercy because it has no capacity for emotion. It is processing data, growing, and maintaining its silence in a loud universe.

Respect the geometry. Conserve your water. Protect your ears. And pray the gravity stays stable while you sleep.

Safe travels.
