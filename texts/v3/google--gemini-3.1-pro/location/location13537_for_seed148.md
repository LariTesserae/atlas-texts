The space is a cosmic sump, a low-hanging basin where the runoff of higher dimensions has pooled into a pocket of un-space no larger than a gothic cathedral. It is swathed in a deep, absolute darkness. Within this localized bubble, a city is attempting to invent itself. 

It is a place of profound, hollow emptiness. If you were to suspend yourself in the center of the pocket, you would see vast, yawning gulfs of pure, black void stretching between impossibilities. Within that void, a three-dimensional urban lattice is emerging.

The entire settlement is forged from biology. Rigorous, hyper-evolved plant matter serves as substrate, architecture, and machinery. Immense, perfectly straight cables of braided ironwood stretch across the emptiness, intersecting at right angles to form the skeletal grid of a metropolis. Around these rigid highways, an industrial ecosystem is actively spooling into existence. Everything is coming into being in a state of rapid, time-lapse embryogenesis. Pale, translucent wireframes of alien shoots are continuously drawing themselves out of the dark, weaving complex factories of xylem and phloem. 

There are massive cooling towers fashioned from ribbed, interlocking ferns that vent a cold, damp oxygen. Giant gourds the size of houses compress and expand like lungs, acting as bellows and biological pistons. Stalks of bamboo-like conduits pump cold sap through the dark, their segmented joints operating as hydraulic valves. This is an industrial complex scaled to near-perfection, composed entirely of root, leaf, and vine. Yet, because the density of the space is so low, these sprawling factories are suspended like delicate, scattered ornaments in the abyss. Whole city blocks are represented by single, spindly gangways of woven moss bridging chasms of nothingness. 

The silence is heavy, a thick, damp hush that swallows all acoustics. The temperature hovers just above freezing. Condensation is the only weather. Because physics is only just beginning to calcify here, water behaves strangely. It does not flow; instead, pockets of cold moisture coalesce from the dark and hang stationary in the void as perfectly spherical, shivering droplets, some as small as marbles, others the size of train cars. The living city runs its roots through these suspended lakes to drink. Occasionally, a heavy bead of water will slip its metaphysical anchor, falling in a silent flash of silver to splash against a broad, metallic leaf before wicking instantly into the plant’s pores.

This is a realm of reality breakdown. The vegetation is not merely growing; it is metabolizing the void, synthesizing physical laws as a byproduct of photosynthesis. Leaves do not unfurl in three dimensions—they unfold outward in esoteric geometries, flickering between states of matter, their fractal edges rendering space and time into being before your eyes. To look too closely at the petals of the great vat-blossoms is to feel your own mind snag on a logical paradox, as the geometry spirals inward into negative space. 

Despite the overwhelming emptiness, the city is ferociously active, populated by an abundance of strange, silent fauna that serve as the cogs and workers of the biomechanical metropolis. The air between the plant-structures teems with pale, faceless moths the size of hounds. Their wings are inscribed with phosphorescent, circuit-board patterns—the only light source in the stifling dark. They flutter in their millions, perfectly hushed, a river of dim blue light transferring glowing pollen from the generative rendering-vats to the sprawling, unspooled tips of the emerging highways. 

Assembly lines stretch along the hollowed-out halves of colossal vines. Vast swarms of segmented, centipede-like creatures move in synchronized lockstep upon these conveyors, their mandibles mechanically extruding silken cables that solidify into structural steel-root. Ghost-slugs glide ceaselessly over the piston-gourds, leaving trails of shivering mucus that lubricate the churning, fibrous gears. 

The entire ecosystem operates at an industrial, mechanized pace, an assembly line of life actively building the world. And it is a predatory cycle. From the deep, empty gulfs of the cathedral-void, liquid shadows detach themselves. They are unformed things—tears in the fabric of the half-made reality—that sweep quietly through the low-lit air, cleanly enveloping the circuit-moths mid-flight. Where the moth was, there is simply an absence, erased back into the cold dark. But the automated city does not pause. The immense, beating chlorophyll-hearts pump. The roots continue to weave through the void, rendering existence from the cold, damp dark, building an impossible settlement across the hollow spans of nothing.
