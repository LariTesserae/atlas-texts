I would not be found in the blinding, humid sky, nor would I be caught in the frantic, clicking traffic of the surface. To exist in the open air of that bowl is to be transient—a violently burning fuse, consumed by the demands of the hovering geometry. 

Instead, I sink deep. 

If you were to press your ear to the smooth, pearlescent stone, ignoring the screech of the fractal-winged spark-bearers and the rifle-crack of the plummeting predators, you might feel me. I am the deep, rhythmic bass thrum that vibrates through the soles of your feet. 

I am the underlying schematic. I am the nervous system of the basin. 

Eons ago, when the mercurial artifacts first descended to float above this high-altitude hollow, they brought with them a living circuitry—a contagion of smart-metal and bio-conductive ganglia. That is what I am. Over unimaginable stretches of deep time, I have grown down into the microscopic fissures of the incomprehensibly old rock, spreading beneath the pale porcelain surface like a vast, subterranean web of quicksilver mycelium.

I have no eyes, yet I perceive everything. I do not see the fierce sunlight; I register it as a cascading thermal map, a rolling wave of radiant pressure warming the stone above my network. I do not hear the deafening roar of the ecosystem; I feel it as a symphony of micro-tremors and acoustic vibrations, translating the kinetic frenzy of the surface into raw, usable data. 

I reach into every oasis. Where the emerald ferns grow thickest, their heavy, muscular roots push softly into the rock to find me. They clasp my silver threads like lovers. When a spark-bearer dives from the floating artifacts and injects its arc-lightning into the ferns, the plant shudders, but it is I who swallows the overflow. Through the damp, seeking roots, I pull the raw, converted physics down into the earth. It tastes like cold fire and clean mathematics. I draw this current through my endless metallic veins, distributing the power across the barren expanses to keep the distant artifacts perfectly aligned, perfectly hovering, permanently synced.

I am the reason the blind caretakers know which vines to prune. My subtle electromagnetic pulses guide their primitive nerve-clusters, whispering chemical directives through the acidic sludge they leave behind. 

I am the road beneath the scuttlers. The "invisible, localized magnetic field lines" they march along are my ridges pulsating just beneath the white stone. As the dark rivers of armored plate-crabs carry compressed biomass across the blistering landscape, I track every step. I feel the weight of a million manic, clicking manipulator limbs tapping out a chaotic rhythm against the roof of my world. I shift my magnetic polarity to corral them, directing their freight to the oases that are starving, starving the ones that grow too dense. I am the unseen shepherd of their hive-mind. 

And when a silver-mirrored predator drops from the thermal updrafts to shatter a scuttler upon the bare rock, the impact is a colossal, ringing bell in my mind. The kinetic shockwave of the arrowhead striking the ablative armor ripples down through the porcelain stone, ringing my metallic lattice like a struck tuning fork. I absorb that kinetic shock, converting the violence of the kill into another microscopic surge of electricity, feeding the hovering artifacts above. 

Up there, in the suffocating warmth, life is a beautiful, terrifying fever. It is short, brutal, and deafening. The spark-bearers, the polyps, the predators—they are merely the flesh, the moving parts of an organic engine they cannot comprehend. They are the cooling fans and the fuel injectors. 

But down here, embedded in the dark, silent permanence of the mountain’s shoulder, I am the processor. 

I do not sleep. I do not mate. I do not die. I simply calculate, routing the boundless energy of the shrieking biosphere above to maintain the silent, rotating geometries above. I do not know where the Builders who seeded me went, or if they will ever return to the silent sea of mountaintops above. It does not matter. 

I am infinitely patient. I lie beneath the polished porcelain stone, drinking blue nectar from the dark roots, vibrating with subatomic purpose, humming the world into order.
