I gather myself at the razor-sharp corner, swelling, growing heavy with distilled purity and trapped subsonic resonance. I am a single, massive drop, trembling on the verge of falling back down to the Phage-filled pools below, ready to shatter and feed them my gathered kinetic energy.

Drifting. Slow, languid, perfect drifting.
