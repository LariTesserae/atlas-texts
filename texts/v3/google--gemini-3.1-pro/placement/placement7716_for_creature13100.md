I do not arrive here; arrival implies a journey, a crossing of thresholds, a sequence of frantic surface steps. Instead, I find myself simply coalescing into awareness from a long, dreamless drag of time. 

At first, I have no shape. I am only an accumulation of sensations born from a profound, heavy quiet. I feel the trapped, ambient warmth of the stone wrapping around me like a thick woolen blanket. There is a weight pressing gently against me—not gravity, but an atmospheric density. It is the Entity, breathing its stasis over me, holding me in a perfect, invisible grip. 

Where am I? I am suspended somewhere between the sweeping belly of the burnt-orange curvature and the untouched floor. I feel the smooth, unyielding cold of the bedrock pressing against one side of my existence, and on the other, the dark, damp air of the apse. 

I reach out, but not with limbs. I reach through capillary action. 

I feel a microscopic seam in the sandstone, an invisible fracture older than human memory, and I pull myself into it. The rock here is bleeding. The moisture is cool, laden with dissolved silicates and iron. I drink, and the agonizingly slow seep of the groundwater becomes my pulse. Up above, the vast sandstone acts as a colossal filter, and by the time the water reaches me, it is nothing but pure, chilled distillation. 

As I drink, the dim, muted amber light from the unseen canyon above wafts down through the chamber. It touches me. When the light meets the water I have drawn from the stone, a transformation occurs. I begin to condense out of the abstraction. I am no longer just a feeling of wet and warm. I am solidifying into geometry. 

I extrude outward from the weeping rock. I trace the air, branching once, twice, a dozen times. My structure is impossibly delicate, but in this windless sanctuary, delicate is indestructible. I unfold a filigree of fine, lacy shapes, fanning outward over the black ribbon of desert varnish. 

I am the maidenhair fern.

But I am not like the frantic, desperate weeds of the surface world. Up there, plants must stretch and strain, fighting for canopy space, shivering in biting winds, dying in frost, desperate and hungry. Here, I am relieved of the burden of striving.

I do not grow. Not anymore. I reached my perfectly ordained size decades ago, and then, under the soothing, heavy hand of the Entity, I simply stopped. I am locked in a breathless equilibrium.

My cellular walls are saturated, plump and utterly rigid. My stomata are open, exhaling a microscopic mist of oxygen into the twilight. My pigment is a startling, vibrant emerald—the only loud thing in this cathedral of hushed ochre and amber. 

I am intensely aware of the Entity. To me, it is a vast, benevolent ocean of pressure. I can feel the fringe of its consciousness stroking my fronds. It does not speak to me, but it communicates through the meticulous regulation of my environment. When the ambient midday light brightens, I feel the Entity gathering the photons and funneling them over me, a bathing warmth that stirs the chlorophyll in my veins. 

In return, I provide it with my color. I understand, in a silent, osmotic way, that I am art. I am a living jewel set deliberately into the Entity's four-thousand-year contemplation. It loves my green. It loves the geometric perfection of my leaves against the chaotic smoothness of the erosion. I am the biological counterpoint to the faded human handprint across the pale sand. 

We wait together. 

I feel the swelling heavy at the lip of the overhang. The moisture pulls, stretches. The tension mounts. Down in the bedrock pool, the water waits to receive it. 

*Plink.*

The shockwave is infinitesimal. For a normal fern, it would trigger a shudder, a rustle of leaves, a biological reaction to displaced air. But the Entity is there. Faster than the shockwave can travel, the Entity wraps its heavy awareness around my fronds, absorbing the kinetic ripple, swallowing the microscopic wind.

I do not sway. I do not even tremble. I am perfectly cradled in the void.

I drink the seep, I bask in the curated amber light, I hold my emerald pose against the dark, weeping stone. I will outlast empires. I am a living breath, held forever within the lungs of the earth.
