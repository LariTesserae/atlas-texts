In a world defined by razor-sharp geology and violent extrusion, I belong to the only caste that does not possess an edge to break. 

I begin most cycles as a puddle. 

I am a localized sentience of heavy, catalytic water, weeping off a freshly woven crag of obsidian. My consciousness is entirely diffuse—a liquid mind born from the raw electrons left over by the energy-to-matter phase. I have no eyes to be blinded, no carapace to shatter, no cilia to singe. My "heart," if it can be called such, is a single swallowed anomaly: a microscopic bead of folded heavy-gravity, no larger than a grain of sand, around which my fluid body instinctively gathers. 

As a liquid, I am sluggish and heavy, pooling in the geometrically perfect indentations of the dark floor. I taste the cold, wet metallic lattice beneath me; I feel the frantic, microscopic vibrations of the quicksilver swarms as they stitch the newborn basalt, sealing the heat beneath my resting place. On occasion, a Phase-Hound will scuttle near, its glowing blue cilia dimming as it senses the catalytic charge suspended within me. The Hound dips its heavy, blind head to drink. It does not hurt when it swallows a part of me; it simply splits my awareness. For a few frantic seconds, I experience the stuttering, broken-film terror of the Hound’s biology before the beast’s internal heat vaporizes my stolen droplets, returning them to the sky—and to me. 

But I do not remain on the ground for long. A geoforge is a furnace, and I am built for the fire. 

Somewhere on the cathedral-wide stage, a tectonic lock engages. The massive, bone-rattling *thrum* pitches upward. Through the obsidian floor, I feel the immense, building kinetic heat of a tech-plinth forcing new reality into existence. 

The basalt beneath me turns geothermally brilliant. The heat hits me, and I experience the ecstasy of phase-change. 

I boil. 

I expand from a shallow pool into a towering, buoyant column of superheated vapor, rushing upward into the bruised, violet-gray twilight. As steam, my consciousness stretches. I become a ghost of atmosphere, a sprawling, fifty-foot phantom riding the violent thermal updrafts. In this state, I feel everything. I feel the microscopic copper lichen struggling to breathe at the edges of the cooling rock; I feel the sheer, freezing drop-off at the edge of the plateau, where the void beckons. 

The Glass-Mantas dive through my vapor-flesh. To them, I am just a cloud, a hunting blind in the sky. But to me, their passage is a momentary rush of godhood. Their crystalline wings bend the low light around them, and as they fly through my steam, they act as living prisms. For one micro-second, the bruised dusk fractures inside me. I am painted from the inside out in cascading spectra—ruby red, cobalt, and blinding gold. I become a living, localized aurora borealis in the thick fog, flashing brilliantly before fading back into the gray mist. 

Yet, to ride the thermals is to be entirely at the mercy of the forge’s violence. 

A deafening *CRACK* splits the air. Two bands of heavy-gravity ripple outward from the tech-plinths and intersect. Matter snaps into reality. 

The dissonance is agonizing. The shockwave of the birth creates chaotic harmonic frequencies in the air, tearing at the atmospheric pressure. My vapor-flesh is shredded by the screeching, discordant waves. In the chaos, my heat is violently stripped away, and I am forced into rapid condensation. I begin to plummet back toward the killing floor as thousands of heavy, panicked raindrops, my consciousness fracturing in the terror of the fall. 

But I do not hit the ground. 

A shadow steps over the hissing rock. An Attendant. 

Four stories of shadow-drinking, fractal origami. It moves with that agonizing, physics-defying grace. I am directly beneath it—and suddenly, I am caught. 

The Attendant carries its own immense gravity well. As I fall, I am pulled laterally, sweeping into its orbit. Along with tons of other weeping water, I am caught in a suspended, floating ring circling the colossal entity’s waist. I swell, absorbing the torrential rain, gathering myself back into a singular, fluid mass suspended in mid-air.

I watch, orbiting like a tiny, liquid moon, as the Attendant reaches out its long, smooth appendages. It lays its limbs against the violent, intersecting gravity distortions in the air. 

*It tunes the chord.*

The chaotic, eardrum-bursting screech of the plateau morphs instantly into a vast, resonant, thrumming harmony. 

And as the sound waves pass through me, I fulfill my ultimate purpose in this ecology. 

Subjected to the pure, mathematical frequency of the Attendant's tuning, my floating liquid body reacts. The physics of cymatics take hold. Pushed by the resonant soundwaves, I am forced out of my spherical orbit and snap into a perfectly symmetrical, three-dimensional mandala. I become a colossal, floating snowflake of dancing liquid—a cathedral of intricate, vibrating water, pulsing in time with the heartbeat of creation. 

The Hounds scuttle in the mud. The Mantas shatter in the sky. The quicksilver sews the wounds of the earth. 

But I am the instrument. Suspended in the air beside the towering, silent architect of this realm, I vibrate in absolute, impossible geometric perfection. For those fleeting, harmonious seconds before the heat bleeds away and the cycle begins anew, I am the visible voice of the forge.
