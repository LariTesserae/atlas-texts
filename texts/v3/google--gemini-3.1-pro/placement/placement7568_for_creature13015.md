The water behind me remains as undisturbed as cast glass, completely oblivious to my passing.

They feel so far away now. They are gods of imaginary realms, but I am here, in the cold, raw architecture of the real.

Now, there is only the profound, saturated darkness, occasionally broken by the violet flare of a sister-colony miles away, blinking a complex equation regarding the decay rate of protons. Our paradise is a suspended breath that can never be exhaled.

My colony glides onward into the dark. I am a ghost of glass and light, suspended in a frozen ocean at the edge of space, listening to the heartbeat of a world that refuses to die.
