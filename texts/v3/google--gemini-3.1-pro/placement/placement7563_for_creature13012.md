I find myself on the plains of the absolute.

To survive the flat, featureless expanse of the chalk-white basin, my outer layers have already surrendered to the sun. Hundreds of my sibling-threads, braided around me, met the blistering glare and instantly opened their valves, flooding their own veins with calcium. They flashed from living blue to dead-white ceramic, forming the pressurized pipeline that now protects me. Through the dark, wet tunnel of their fossilized bodies, I am pushed forward, an inch an hour, a millimeter a minute, across the baking pavement.

I am looking for a flaw in the horizontal math. 

I feel the terrain beneath me through the microscopic vibrations of the shell I travel within. *Flat. Flat. Flat.* Days of endless flatness, the load-bearing geometry returning nothing but perfect zeros. 

But then—a shift. 

A fraction of a degree in the ground beneath me dips. The ambient atmospheric pressure changes by a decimal point. It is a shallow depression in the pristine basin, a place where the heavy, baking air does not scour perfectly clean, where twilight humidity might linger for an extra heartbeat. 

The arithmetic demands an answer.

I rupture the end of the Seeker tube.

And then, beneath me, the miracle occurs. 

The roar of the sky is silenced. I have cast a shadow.

I wait in the dark, my hollow interior pulsing with rhythmic, frantic peristalsis. I am incredibly thirsty. 

The air weeps. A single bead of condensation forms on my bruised-cyan epidermis. 

I drink. The tepid, mineral-clogged water rushes into my pores. As it hits my interior, the temperature differential triggers the great engine of my biology. I weep the sap. 

Thick, heavy droplets of liquid chalk and dissolved silica exude from my underside. Under the secondary bounce of the heat outside the shadow, the sap instantly hardens. *Snap.* A millimeter of ivory ceramic. *Snap.* A strut of pale ochre. *Snap.* A hexagonal lip on the basin floor.

My awareness expands, stretching out into the geometry of my own making. I feel the weight of the new vault testing the pillars. I widen the capillaries on the left side, flooding the stressed curve with hyper-saline fluid. I bleed ceramic sap exactly into the vector of weakness until the tension neutralizes to zero. Perfect balance. The chord of the architecture resolves harmoniously into silence. 

Hours pass. Or perhaps days. Time is measured only by the rhythm of weeping arches and the crystalline crackle of the ground rising. 

A tepid pool forms beneath me. My lower tendrils submerge, acting as violent pumps, sucking up the water, filtering the calcium, sending the heavy slurry up the sweeping pillars to the topmost vaults, where my upper reaches bake it into more shadow-making shell. I am an endless loop of water, heat, and stone. I am pulling the sky down and turning it into the earth.

I am an ancestor now. 

I do not feel fear as the imperative shifts. I feel only the deep, satisfying completion of an equation. 

I open all my internal valves. 

The transpirational vacuum stalls. I allow the hypersaturated calcium slurry to flood my hollow center, unmoving, settling into the very core of my flesh. I feel the cool blue of the shadow slowly dimming as my bruised-cyan epidermis begins to lighten. 

The liquid pulse of my existence slows. The capillaries calcify. The silicates bond with my organic tissues, locking the polymers into a rigid, unbreakable grid. 

I do not die. I simply stop happening. 

The frantic rush of water becomes a memory, trapped in the microscopic pores of the stone I have become. I am the flawless pale ceramic of the load-bearing pillar. I am the sweeping parabolic curve. I am the silence of the basin. 

I have built the world, and now, perfectly pristine and flawlessly cold, I am a part of its weight, forever supporting the life that continues to build above me into the blinding, absolute sky.
