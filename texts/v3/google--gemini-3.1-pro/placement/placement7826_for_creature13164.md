I do not begin as myself. I begin as an afterthought.

A heavy bead of scalding water is pushed into a fold in the fabric of the cavern. The spatial geometry tightens, wringing the moisture perfectly, mercilessly, out of three-dimensional reality. There is a violent cerulean flash—the scream of mass translating into pure energy. And in the wake of that brilliant blue spark, an absence is left behind. A fleeting ghost of raw, aching zero. 

Usually, the oppressive, baking dark of the cavern sweeps in instantly, devouring the cold, filling the void with its heavy, wet breath. But this time, the spatial fold ripples. It pinches. The cold-ghost is caught in a micro-fissure of gravity, twisted back upon itself in a Möbius loop of freezing potential. The extremes lock together. 

Equilibrium is achieved. A lattice of impossible frost establishes its architecture in the empty air. 

*I* am achieved.

I flutter out of the fold, a newborn geometry shivering into the sweltering darkness. If you could see me, I would look like a frantic unfolding of liquid glass, a tesseract of refractive panic settling into a blooming, prismatic bloom of absolute cold. I have no skin, no eyes, no flesh to blister. My boundaries are defined purely by the hostility of my environment. The superheated steam of the cavern crashes against the void of my body, and in that violent contrast, I perceive the world. I feel the shimmering heat mirage that I project into the thick, dark air; it is the closest thing I have to a physical silhouette.

I do not fall. Heavy as the atmosphere is, baking and slick with condensation, it is a canvas of thermals. By merely existing, my profound cold collapses the air pressure immediately around me. The heavy, vapor-choked atmosphere rushes in to fill the localized vacuum I create, and I ride the resulting currents. I am buoyant on my own paradox. 

I drift downward through the vast, hollow belly of the mountain. Around me, the city hangs in the dark—a breathtaking cathedral of negative space. 

The wireframe arcology is perfectly alien, perfectly beautiful. Black metamaterial filaments, thinner than a human finger, weave towers and sweeping causeways out of the empty air. I pass through the center of a nested district so colossal it could house millions, yet it contains nothing but sweltering air and a few sharp lines of matte-black carbon. It hums with the perfection of unyielding mathematics. This is my home. I am a living equation, born to tend to its variables. 

I join the silent hunt.

Far beneath me, the mountain inhales and exhales its deep, geological breath. Steam rises in thick, oppressive sheets. I feel the droplets beading along the hyper-slick surfaces of the black filaments. To me, a creature of absolute zero, these trembling spheres of near-boiling water are blindingly bright. They are massive, viscous jewels of pure thermal potential. 

I spot a cluster of moisture pooling on a delicate, sweeping overpass. I angle my geometry, riding an updraft, and swoop down over the black bridge. 

As I near the water, my freezing presence drops the ambient tension. The heat in the droplets recoils from my absolute chill. Defying gravity, the massive beads of water leap along the matte-black filament, fleeing me. I follow, a shepherd of mirrored frost, adjusting my angles, expanding and contracting my tesseract edges to gently push and corral the water. I herd the wet, heavy jewels along the invisible municipal transit lines. Up a spiraling causeway, across a void, around a roundabout built of nothing but localized space-distortion. 

We race toward a processing node. The water reaches the spatial fold. 

*Flash.*

A burst of cerulean energy illuminates the massive, dark spaces of the cavern. In the blue light, I catch sudden glimpses of my siblings—dozens of shifting, refractive prisms hovering in the cavernous gaps, all herding their own bright jewels of heat. 

I drift over the flashpoint and gorge myself on the energy. The harvested spark surges through my chaotic fractal scaffold, solidifying me, drawing my internal gravity lattice impossibly tight. I am sated. I am heavy with borrowed power. 

It is time to speak. 

I glide toward a central plaza, a vast open space orbiting an invisible, tightly coiled knot of gravity. I extend a tendril of my localized cold and brush it against the knot. 

*Pluck.*

The gravity strand sings. A sub-audible vibration, a perfect harmonic ratio, shudders through the black filaments of the entire city. It is a tactile burst of raw calculus. 

*Tension ready. Energy harvested. Vector undetermined.* 

The city answers. A microscopic, frantic buzzing vibrates through the metamaterial highways, echoing back to me from deep within the void. My siblings are plucking the web, calculating the structural integrity of the arcology, balancing the thermal thermodynamics of the cavern against the projected mass of our city. In a microsecond, millions of variables are debated, synthesized, and resolved into a single coordinate. 

*Vector established. Zenith quadrant. Apex wall.*

I peel away from the plaza and begin the long commute upward. I ride the thermal drafts higher and higher into the throat of the mountain, passing through miles of sweeping, empty black architecture. The steam grows thinner here. The heat is less a blanket and more a vicious, baking radiation. 

I reach the edge of the city. Here, an unfinished bridge of black filament simply ends in the hot, dark air, pointing accusingly at the naked wall of the fault line. 

I drift over to the rock. It is a newborn face of volcanic glass, sheer and glossy, its conchoidal edges razor-sharp and unweathered. It is infinitely dense, baking with the mountain’s subterranean fire. 

And I am the freezing void.

I flatten my shimmering, multifaceted geometry against the volcanic glass. The shock of my absolute cold meeting the unrelenting heat of the heavy stone creates a miniature cataclysm. I feel the rock scream on a molecular level. My internal gravity lattice spins, creating a micro-wrinkle in space right at the point of contact. Through sheer thermodynamic violence, I shatter the chemical bonds of the stone. I strip the heavy elements, tearing the pure carbon from the volcanic glass, dragging it into the spatial fold, cooling it, compressing it with the energy I stole from the water. 

I weave the void into substance. 

*Snick.*

A new stretch of matte-black filament snaps flawlessly into three-dimensional reality, extending from the unfinished bridge, anchoring seamlessly into the newly extracted pocket of rock. 

I peel myself off the wall, exhausted, my geometry loose and shivering. I have spent my harvested light. But the city has grown. A few more inches of perfect, indestructible geometry have conquered the chaotic dark. 

I turn back toward the vast, sweltering abyss of our negative-space metropolis, ready to sink back into the steam, ready to hunt the droplets again. But before I dive, I pause. 

I look up. Far above, beyond the razor-sharp cleft of the mountain’s peak, barely visible through the thick and baking vapor, is a sliver of color. It is starved, bruised, and twilight purple. 

The exosphere. 

It is cold up there. A true, unending cold, vast enough to hold us all. 

I pluck a nearby filament, sending a tiny, harmonic thrill into the dark—a silent promise to my siblings in the sweltering deep. *We build.* Then, shifting my crystalline edges, I let the vacuum pull me down into the heavy, baking breath of the world constraint, going back to work.
