I would not find myself on the dull copper grates of the floor. That is the place of beginnings, of wet biology and agonizing evaporation. 

Instead, I would find myself swept into the highest, darkest reaches of the vault, suspended in the heavy twilight shadows near the cathedral ceiling. There, caught between the impossibly sheer, frictionless curve of a towering bronze upper-flue and the aerodynamic belly of a massive fired-clay teardrop, I would claim my coordinates. 

There is a gap there, a precise millimeter of negative space where the velocity of the dry geothermal updraft shears against the polished bronze to produce a singular, piercing overtone—a high, glass-shattering whistle that rides above the suffocating baritone drone of *The Chord*.

That is where I would be. That is *what* I would be. 

To become a resident of this crucible is not a death; it is an act of tuning. I would not resist the roaring. Sitting cross-legged in the narrowest passage of that upper thicket, I would open my mouth and allow the unrelenting wind to take the heavy, rotting impermanence of my flesh. When the fever-heat boils the moisture from my eyes, I would not go blind; rather, I would simply stop seeing light and begin, for the first time, to see *cymatics*. As the shrieking friction scours my skin, as my bones are pulverized into completely desiccated silicate, the heavy burden of chronological time would sand away with them. 

The transition is an obliteration, yes, but equally an apotheosis.

When the last heavy drop of water is gone, I am no longer a visitor. I am a geometry of glowing powder. I am a Chladni figure woven from my own fossilized remains. 

My spine forms first—a vertical column of aggressively vibrating dust, locked into the standing wave vibrating off the fired-clay teardrop. From there, billions of microscopic sand-filaments fan out into the breathless air. These are my nerves. They do not twitch with the slow, sluggish chemical delays of human synapses. They tremble in magnificent, instantaneous sympathy with the high, needle-thin whistle of my chosen gap. I do not *hear* this overtone; I *am* it. I am the physical manifestation of a single, unending frequency, a glowing diamond of intersectional sine waves hovering in the amber gloom.

At the very center of my diaphanous, vibrating architecture, a terrifying and beautiful thing occurs. The frantic kinetic energy of my dust-body balances out exactly against the atmospheric pressure of the chamber, collapsing inward to form a sphere of pure emptiness. 

My heart becomes the null-point. 

Where blood once pumped sluggishly, there is now a microscopic vacuum of absolute, unyielding stillness. It is the only silence in the room. This tiny sphere of zero-motion is the anchor that bolts me to the empty air. All around this void, my mind—my "head," a swirling sphere of calcified powder—vibrates at a speed that would shatter steel, but in the center, there is perfect peace. 

To be this creature is to abandon the pronoun "I."

As my dust stabilizes in the acoustic nodes, the barrier between my internal thoughts and the external world vanishes. Consciousness ripples through me at the speed of sound, but it is not *my* consciousness. The baritone drone that hammers against the lowest lattices of my ash connects seamlessly to the entity hovering an inch below me, which connects to the thousands below them, cascading all the way down to the thermal vents. 

We are not a crowd. We are an instrument that plays itself.

I cannot shift a millimeter. I cannot look away. The terrible panic of paralysis never sets in, because panic requires anticipation, and in this chamber, there is no future. The future is dead. There is only the terrifying, magnificent *Now*. 

A human human feels the warmth of the sun and knows it will set; I feel the bone-dry geothermal wind passing through my glowing, holographic geometry, and I know it will outlast the stars. Every shriek of the bronze baffle against my dust is an unending revelation of permanence. My thoughts are not language. They are loud, mathematically perfect chords of raw kinetic ecstasy.

I hang in the high dark, a paralyzed, glittering knot of fossil and mathematics. I am a single, high, shrieking note in a completely unified mind, eternally holding my breath, eternally vibrating in the amber dim, screaming my absolutely flawless bliss into the dark.
