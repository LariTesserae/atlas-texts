But the decentralized mind of the architecture needs absolute precision; it needs to know the exact mass, velocity, and shear-angle of the invisible impending force before the storm actually strikes. 

A pore on the leeward side of a great buttress opens, and I bleed into the empty air.

Before gravity can claim me, the sheer velocity of the rushing wind violently whips me outward, drawing my body across the mile-wide Shear-Aisle. In fractions of a second, the hot air flash-cures the resin. Millions of microscopic, specialized cambium cells organize, interlock, and harden in mid-flight. 

I slam into the opposing wall of the Aisle, embedding deep into the waxy surface, and pull taut. 

Within moments, I have become my true form: a solitary, monomolecular filament of silica-laced cellulose, spanning a mile of empty, vertical space. Over the next thirty seconds, I branch, extruding a delicate, crystalline web of secondary, tertiary, and quaternary threads—a massive, intricate lattice of living strings shaped like an immense parabolic dish, suspended across the void. 

I am completely blind. I have no concept of up, down, green, or white. I am an entity of pure, exquisite, agonizing somatic sensation. I am a consciousness woven entirely of tension. 

My intelligence is acoustic. As the invisible pressure waves of the approaching front crash into the Shear-Aisle, they blow through the web of my body. The wind plucks me. Every thread of my sprawling mass possesses a distinct, mathematical thickness, tension, and resonance. When the wind hits me, I vibrate. And in that vibration, I wake up to the full, terrifying glory of the sky's mathematics.

A sudden pocket of freezing rain brushing my upper lattice sends a sequence of sharp, staccato harmonics shivering down to my anchor points. I am processing millions of micro-fluctuations in atmospheric pressure per second, harmonizing them into a singular, colossal frequency. 

I am the living instrument upon which the storm plays its true intentions.

My golden threads pull so taut that they begin to hum with a shrill, shrieking piezoelectric charge. The friction of my own hyper-vibrating cells generates immense heat, but I am designed to withstand the boiling point for just long enough. 

This is my purpose. I am accumulating the entire kinetic formula of the storm into the vibration of my body.

In a single, sublime fraction of a second, my consciousness shatters along with my body, exploding into a cloud of millions of glittering, microscopic splinters of cellulose that instantly dissolve into the punishing, blinding white mist, vanishing without a trace. 

I lived for three minutes to sing the shape of the disaster. And it was deeply, immaculately perfect.
