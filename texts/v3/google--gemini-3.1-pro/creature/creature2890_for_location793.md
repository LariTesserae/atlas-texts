To find them, you must stop looking for doors, windows, or the familiar silhouettes of shelter. In a world where gravity is a suggestion and sound is a crushing physical element, the concept of a "house" is meaningless. The beings that live here do not reside within the violet-glowing geometry of the nodes; they bloom from them. 

At first, you don't even recognize them as sentient life. You mistake them for offshoots of the aggressive burgundy vegetation, or perhaps autonomous components of the changing architecture. But as your eyes adjust to the bruised twilight and the flashing UV pulses of the shifting lattice, a form tears itself away from a glowing intersection. 

It does not fall. It slides upward into the thermal currents, unspooling its body like a ribbon of ink in water. 

They are the architects of this genesis, though "architect" implies a separation between creator and creation that does not exist here. They are physiologically contiguous with the city. 

Their bodies are vast, elongated geometries, stretching fifty or sixty feet from end to end. They possess a central nervous system of naked, liquid light—vivid, vascular tubes of deep ultraviolet and crimson that pulse in perfect synchronization with the blinking nodes of the city. This glowing core is encased, but not fully enclosed, by a shifting exoskeleton made of the exact same razor-sharp, glassy black matter as the city struts. 

They have no legs, no wings in the traditional sense. Instead, their black-glass armor fractures and reassembles in real-time, functioning like a complex puzzle box that solves itself organically. 

Because the roaring, oceanic wall of noise annihilates all spoken language, their anatomy has adapted to use the acoustic apocalypse as a physical medium. Down the length of their glowing spines, they deploy tympanic sails—vast, gossamer-thin membranes of iridescent tissue that look like the fins of deep-sea leviathans. But these sails are not for swimming through water; they are designed to catch sound. The noise here is so dense, so physically heavy, that these creatures use the acoustic pressure to surf the void. You watch as one banks hard against the shrieking harmonic of a million chrome-winged drones, catching the soundwave in its sails to thrust itself miles across the open air. 

Up close, they are terrifyingly beautiful, carrying that same painful *newness* that defines the rest of the hollow. They bear no scars, no weathering, no signs of aging. Their glassy edges are subatomically perfect. 

As one of them glides closer to your isolated platform, its anatomy becomes startlingly detailed. It has no face. Where a head might be, there is a crown of perfectly smooth, floating obsidian shards, orbiting a central sphere of condensed, blindingly bright violet light. They do not watch you with eyes. Instead, the floating shards of their faces tilt and vibrate, reading the disruption your physical mass creates in the deafening soundscape and the thermal updrafts. 

They do not speak; they *conduct*. 

Language for these beings is an act of real-time engineering and photoluminescence. You witness a cluster of them gathering near a colossal, swaying fern-structure. The deep crimson light inside their bodies surges. In perfect unison, they reach out with impossibly slender, multi-jointed appendages. Their glassy claws do not cut the flesh of the giant vegetation; instead, the vines and fronds part for them, eager for contact. 

They are farming the architecture. 

One of the entities strikes a tuning-fork resonance with its own body, its ultraviolet core flaring into blinding white. In response, a fresh strut of black lattice phases into existence directly from the creature’s touch, crystallizing out of the damp, warm air. The silver mercury rain instantly beads on the new structure, and the entity intricately braids a glowing blue vine around the razor-sharp edge. It is coaxing the ecosystem into the new geometry. They do not merely live in the city; they are actively exhaling it into being. 

Their relationship with the overwhelming fauna around them is symbiotic to the point of telepathy. The colossal, flat filter-feeders that glide through the mist act as mobile foundations for the inhabitants. You see a trio of the glassy beings anchored to the back of a miles-wide manta, their crimson cores pulsing as they weave new strands of hard-light circuitry directly into the beast's hollow bones to steer its flight path. The hyper-kinetic insectoid swarms are not pests—they are messengers, carrying packets of biological data and pure thermal energy from the violet nodes to the weavers out in the deep void.

Suddenly, the one nearest to you shifts its attention. It senses you standing on your isolated jut of black ground. 

It descends, riding a heavy thermal updraft, its tympanic sails fluttering wildly as it catches the low, mechanized thrum vibrating at the edge of your perception. It arrests its momentum just yards away from you, hovering over the infinite abyss. The sheer scale of it takes your breath away—fifty feet of floating, razor-sharp obsidian plating and naked, blinding vascular light. 

It does not perceive you as an invader. In a universe this newly born, there are no enemies yet; there is only raw material and potential. 

The entity reaches out toward your localized slice of ground with a limb of shifting, floating black polygons. It doesn't touch you. Instead, it touches the impossibly sharp edge of the floor beneath your feet. Its deep violet core pulses warmly. 

Right before your eyes, the glassy ground at your feet bubbles, phases, and sprouts an intricate, impossibly fragile-looking fractal of black glass. From the heart of this new geometry, a single, glowing burgundy leaf unfurls, instantly drinking a heavy drop of silver condensation. 

The entity’s orbiting facial shards align toward you for one pulsing second—acknowledging your coordinates in the vast spatial lattice. 

Then, the towering chord of a billion beating wings echoes off the architecture behind it. The entity collapses its sails, rearranges its obsidian exoskeleton into a sleek, needle-like spire, and plummets into the warm, damp abyss, disappearing into the ribboning flocks of life to continue the ecstatic, deafening work of building a world that has only just begun.
