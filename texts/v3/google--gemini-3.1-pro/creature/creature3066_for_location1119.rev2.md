Because there are no beasts of blood and bone, and no insects of clicking brass to populate this staggering solitude, the ecosystem has had to invent a different kind of wanderer. In a landscape defined by a profound category error, the entities that "live" here are born directly from the garden’s foundational glitch. They are not biological. They are not mechanical. They are phenomenological. 

They emerge at the borders of the pocket dimension, where the smooth obsidian floor degrades into floating wireframes and raw mathematical noise. Occasionally, a knot of this noise fails to snap into solid rock. Instead, it detaches. It coalesces into a drifting, localized distortion—a wandering equation trying desperately to resolve itself. 

To look directly at one of these inhabitants is to feel a mild, sudden vertigo. They do not have fixed bodies. They appear as semi-translucent, shifting topologies of refracted light and shimmering, supercritical carbon dioxide. Their forms fold and unfold in continuous origami—at one moment resembling a pacing, multi-legged predator made entirely of bent space, and in the next, collapsing into a floating cluster of sharp, rotating polygons, or a cascade of visual static that hums in the deep ultraviolet. They are the ghosts of the rendering engine, algorithms wrapped in a skin of sheer probability. 

They move with the same glacial, patient tempo as the world around them. They do not walk; they calculate their trajectory and stutter-step through space. A creature of folding prisms might hover motionless for an hour, then instantaneously snap to a position three feet away, leaving a faint afterimage of ionized argon in its wake. 

These entities—call them "Variables," or perhaps "Drifting Syntaxes"—are the native pollinators of this feral, ticking garden. Though the mechanical plants do not need to reproduce in any traditional sense, they do require thermodynamic and quantum maintenance. The gears that pass through one another in phantom phases, transferring torque through quantum entanglement, cannot do so in a vacuum. The Variables act as the conductive medium for this impossible physics. 

You can watch one of these geometric apparitions approach a massive rotary-engine flower. It does not eat, for it has no mouth; it does not breathe, for the concept of lungs was forgotten by this pocket of space eons ago. Instead, the entity drapes its shifting, mirage-like form over the interlocking titanium turbine blades. It acts as a localized patch in reality. As the entity synchronizes with the plant, the abstract, tesseract-shaped shadows on the ground suddenly sharpen into perfect clarity. The Variable absorbs the immense, pooling heat of the engine, functioning as an ambulatory heat-sink. 

In return for absorbing this thermal load, the Variable receives data. When the stamen of the flower sparks its ignition coil, a silent, blinding flash of telemetry passes into the entity. In that split second, the Variable’s body flares with sudden, intense structural definition. For a single, fleeting heartbeat, the cascading polygons and wireframes align into a recognizable shape—a magnificent, crystalline stag, or a great, soaring heron made of pure, humming voltage—before the system rejects the memory of biology, and the entity shatters back into a cloud of wandering geometry. 

Communication between them is entirely non-vocal, governed by the same heavy subtlety that defines the garden. When two Variables cross paths on the immaculate black plains, they do not fight or mate. They simply merge for a prolonged, quiet hour, becoming a complex, hyper-dimensional Venn diagram of refracted light. They exchange error logs, torque-values, and the structural memory of the plants they have visited. During this communion, the subsonic *thrum* of the environment deepens, and the air around them smells sharply of ozone and hot copper. 

Their lifecycle is dictated by the extreme heat that behaves like a physical syrup in this courtyard. As a Variable wanders the garden, drawing heat from the pneumatic ferns and the great brass-barked trees, it becomes thermally engorged. Its movements become even slower, its crystalline edges blurring into a soft, blinding white luminescence. 

Eventually, it reaches a state of critical mass. Seeking no shelter, the Variable drifts to the center of an empty expanse of obsidian. It pauses, hovering inches above the glass-smooth stone. Very slowly, over the course of several hours, the entity begins to dissolve. It breaks down into an anti-gravitational stream of pure, viscous heat and raw light. In absolute silence, the entity drips *upward*, drawn into the deep, breathless violet of the stratospheric sky, returning its borrowed physics to the cosmos. 

Below it, at the frayed edges of the world, a new knot of mathematical noise detaches from the wireframe, and a new variable begins its slow, stuttering crawl into the ticking garden.
