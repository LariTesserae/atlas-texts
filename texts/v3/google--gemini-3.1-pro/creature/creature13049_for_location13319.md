It takes a long time before you realize you are not alone. 

Your eyes, desperate for familiar forms in the bruised twilight, slide right over them. At first glance, they are indistinguishable from the landscape itself. Scattered across the frictionless plateau, they appear as isolated, slate-grey domes of rock. They possess the exact same immaculately smooth surface as the ground, sharing the same polished, heavy aesthetic of extreme age. Eons of the crushing, multi-octave roar have sanded them down, subtracting everything extraneous, leaving only curves that perfectly deflect the tectonic pressure of the noise. 

But then, one of the domes detaches from the plateau. 

Gravity has no jurisdiction over it. It rises with agonizing smoothness, drifting upward a few inches, shedding a cascade of fat, shivering condensation that stubbornly refuses to fall and instead orbits its mass like tiny moons of quicksilver. 

As it unfurls, its true anatomy reveals itself, and you understand that what you were looking at was not a boulder, but a back. 

The creature is tall—perhaps eight or nine feet when fully extended—wrapped in a carapace of thick, polished hide that mirrors the dark stone of the monoliths. It is draped over them like a heavy, rigid cowl. This dense outer shell acts as an absolute acoustic shield, offering zero resistance to the atmospheric grind. From behind, they are monuments to subtraction, featureless and sealed. 

But as the creature turns its body toward the void, its posture opens. The smooth carapace splits down the center, unfolding like a monk’s heavy outer robes parting to reveal the chest. 

There is no face. There are no eyes to parse the shadowless, flat glow, no ears to be shredded by the cosmic scream, no mouth to breathe the perfectly still air. Instead, the entire front of its body—from where a collarbone might be, down to its pelvis—is a hyper-complex, exposed matrix of biological architecture. It is a living cymatic plate. The flesh here resembles pale, pearlescent cartilage interwoven with bands of shivering, translucent crystal. It is shattered into thousands of intricate, rigid fractal ridges and acoustic cavities, mirroring the frozen standing waves in the pools of water below. 

This is how it senses. This is how it feeds. The creature turns its open front directly into the brunt of the eternal, deafening roar. It doesn't fight the sound; it channels it. The multi-octave drone strikes the fractal ridges of its chest and is baffled, broken down, and absorbed. You can see the kinetic energy of the noise rippling through the pale membranes, translating the violent, omnipresent frequency into a strange, vibrating sustenance. It is eating the drone. It is reading the barometric pressure of reality.

Two limbs, long and multi-jointed, extend from beneath the cowl. They end in flattened, frictionless pads. The creature reaches out into the seemingly empty air. But space here is crumpled, and this being is native to the folds. It does not walk; the faint, ancient footprints on the rock below must belong to an ancestor from an epoch before gravity and dimensions fully unspooled. Now, it navigates by grasping the seams of the broken world. 

It pinches the heavy, still air between two flat pads. It turns its wrist.

Instantly, the dizzying gulf of the chasm vanishes. A neighboring spire, miles away, snaps to within an arm’s length. The sheer walls of the two monoliths practically kiss. The creature steps effortlessly across the gap, its dense grey back sliding through the air. As soon as its weight transfers to the new pillar, it releases the crease in the air. The intervening miles violently unfold behind it, snapping the first spire back to the distant horizon. Distance is just a fabric it gathers in its hands. 

You watch it settle near the edge of this new abyss. It approaches one of the primitive cairns, the five stones hovering a fraction of an inch above each other. 

It becomes clear that these stones are not relics left by an ancient civilization. They are the slow, ongoing masterpieces of these currently living, resonant monks. The creature cups its long palms around the hovering stones without touching them. By subtly adjusting the posture of its open cymatic chest, it alters the standing waves of the drone in a one-foot radius. It creates a pocket of absolute acoustic equilibrium, a microscopic shadow in the roaring void, allowing the stones to rest upon pillars of pure, localized silence. 

The staircases to nowhere that hang off the edge of the cliffs suddenly make a terrifying kind of sense. The creatures are not building steps out of the void; they are trying to sculpt the noise itself. The stones are just anchors for invisible, resonant architectures reaching out into the mist—highways of shaped sound-shadows that only they can perceive.

A shadow shifts in the vast gulf above. One of the colossal, deep-sea siphonophores drifts downward, its translucent bells pulsing with agonizing slowness, trailing bioluminescent ribbons. It is on a collision course with the plateau where the creature sits. 

The inhabitant does not flinch or seal its carapace. It remains perfectly still, its chest open wide to the mist. 

The ethereal leviathan glides smoothly through the dense rock, and then, inevitably, it passes directly through the body of the resonant monk. As the ghostly membrane phases through the creature's pale, vibrating chest, a silent communion takes place. The siphonophore’s internal bioluminescence radically illuminates the fractal depths of the inhabitant's acoustic flesh, making the monk glow from the inside out with a slow, icy blue light. The creature’s carapace shivers. As the leviathan pulls out the other side and continues its endless drift, it leaves behind a slick of heavy moisture right inside the creature’s fractal ridges. 

The inhabitant slowly closes its dense, dark mantle. It traps the floating metallic droplets within its chest, slowly absorbing them through osmosis to maintain its own wet, icy sheen. 

It has fed on the noise. It has drank from the ghosts. It settles back onto the smooth stone, tucking its limbs beneath its cowl. It presses its smooth back outward, allowing the abrasive friction of the roar to wash over it, slowly sanding away another microscopic layer of its dense flesh over the next millennium. 

It sits in the fractured geography, amidst the weeping stone and floating water, indistinguishable once more from a domed boulder. It waits in the deafening, eternal grind, a beautiful and terrifying instrument of flesh, perfectly balanced in the center of the scream, ensuring the physics of this place remain eternally, flawlessly broken.
