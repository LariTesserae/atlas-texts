To prepare an ordinary human for the "Furnace Cylinder" is to prepare them for a spacewalk inside a geological blast furnace. This is an environment completely hostile to mammalian life. It is not merely uninhabitable; it is a hyper-active, vertically oriented warzone governed by crushing gravity, abrasive thermal winds, and violently moving mechanical flora. 

If you are to survive even an hour on one of those polished, dangerously slick terraces, your preparation must bridge the gap between deep-cavern spelunking, volcanic research, and riot-control evasion. 

Here is your comprehensive survival and preparation guide.

---

### 1. The Environmental Suit (Your Personal Womb)
You cannot breathe the air, you cannot survive the ambient heat, and you cannot touch the surfaces. An entirely sealed, self-sufficient exo-suit is mandatory.

*   **Thermal & Chemical Plating:** The suit must be a heavy-duty, closed-loop Volcanic EVA system. It needs an aggressive internal liquid-cooling garment to combat the baking heat. The outermost layer must be resistant to highly corrosive sulfur and heavy metallic fumes. 
*   **The Faraday Mesh:** This is critical. The plants use intense static electricity as a nervous system. The air is chaotic with electric sparks. Your suit *must* incorporate a heavy-duty, grounded Faraday cage. Without it, a stray whip of a copper-vine or a blast of static from a silica leaf will fry your suit’s electronics and stop your heart.
*   **Aerodynamic Profile:** The howling updraft is a constant, physical battering ram. Your suit must be heavy and streamlined. Do not wear loose straps, dangling tethers, or bulky external ponchos. The wind will catch them, drag you to the edge of the smooth rock, and throw you into the abyss.

### 2. Sensory Gear (Perceiving the Hot Dark)
Natural human senses are useless here. The roar of the wind and the grinding of stone will deafen you. The dim ruby glare will blind you to the details of the shadows.

*   **LIDAR / Echolocation Visor:** Do not rely on optical night-vision; the stark contrast between the bottomless magma glow and the deep shadows will wash it out. Thermal vision is completely useless because *everything* is hot. You need a LIDAR-based tactical HUD to map the geometry of your surroundings, overlaying wireframes over your visor. 
*   **Active Noise Cancellation & Acoustic Dampening:** The noise is a continuous, concussive assault—howling wind, squealing timber axles, crashing basalt pestles. Your helmet must aggressively dampen acoustic waves while highlighting specific, high-frequency anomalies (like the snapping of a loaded ratchet-branch nearby).
*   **Vibration Sensors:** The plants have no eyes and do not care about light. They sense the world through vibration and static. Your suit must have seismometers built into the boots to warn you when a nearby thicket is tensing its fibers or when an automated battering ram is swinging your way.

### 3. Mobility & Navigation
Moving through this city requires immense caution. You are traversing a slick, perfectly polished, curved cliff face suspended over nothingness. 

*   **Tethering Above All Else:** You are one slip away from a multi-minute fall into a magma lake. Bring a compressed-air harpoon equipped with titanium pitons to create your own anchor points. Before you take a step, you must be clipped to the rock face. 
*   **Traction Gear:** The walls are "smoothed by the aeons... into slick perfection." Standard rubber boots will melt or slip. You need multi-point, tungsten-carbide micro-spikes on your boots and gloves to bite into the vesicular basalt. 
*   **Anti-Ascension Warning:** If you fall, *do not deploy a parachute.* The thermal updraft is so incredibly violent and chaotic that a canopy will instantly turn into a sail, violently slamming you against the ceiling or the abrasive rock walls until your suit ruptures. If you fall, your only hope is an auto-winch tether attached to the wall.

### 4. Interacting with the Botanical Metropolis
You must fundamentally adjust how you view plants. These are not passive shrubs; they are territorial, industrialized, Stone Age engineers moving at animal speeds. To them, you are not a human. You are a strange, moving deposit of minerals that either poses a threat or offers raw materials.

*   **Vibrational Stealth:** Because the flora detect vibration, you must learn to walk softly despite wearing heavy gear. Step in rhythm with the ambient industrial noises. When the massive basalt pestles smash down onto hematite, take your step. Hide your footfalls in the acoustic shadows of their own industry.
*   **Beware the Barbed Seeds:** The updraft is filled with flying, heavy, barbed seeds looking for a place to root. Your suit must be puncture-resistant. If a seed latches onto your suit, remove it immediately with a plasma cutter or heavy shears. If it detects the carbon and moisture inside your suit, it may attempt to drill through your armor.
*   **Avoid the "Machinery":** The suspension bridges, the tension cables made of roots, the massive glass-silk sails—treat all of this as active, heavy industrial equipment without safety guards. Do not stand near an untethered flint-axe. Do not walk under the swinging basalt hammers. If a rigging line snaps under tension, the whipping recoil of a copper-wire vine will slice you in half.
*   **Electromagnetic Pacification (Your Weaponry):** Bullets will do very little to charred, carboniferous ironwood. Fire is utterly useless against fireproof silica and metal vines. Your best defense is a localized, high-voltage EMP emitter. If a Strangler-Crane vine grabs you, a massive burst of electromagnetic interference will scramble its static-electric nervous system, causing the fibers to spasm and release you. 

### 5. Psychological Preparation
Prepare for profound agoraphobia combined with intense claustrophobia. 

You are indoors, sealed in a crustal pocket, yet the space is so vast it has no sky and no horizon. Standing on scalloped ledges over a bottomless, glowing void will induce severe vertigo. You will be entirely alone in the sense of finding kindred, breathing life—yet surrounded by a terrifying, unrelenting, thoughtless industry. The plants do not sleep. The wind does not stop. The clashing of flint on stone will echo in your bones.

**Your Golden Rule:** *Remain insignificant.* Do not try to conquer a terrace. Do not attempt to harvest the flora's edible mineral dust. Keep your electromagnetic signature low, your vibrations masked, and your tethers taut. Slip through the ruby-lit shadows of this botanical forge like a ghost, observe the mechanical horrors of a wood-and-stone metropolis, and leave before the vines decide your suit contains enough iron to make a decent hammer.
