To endure and bear witness to this magnificent, dying metropolis, an ordinary human cannot simply walk through a portal. Without meticulous preparation, your biology will fail in minutes, and your mind shortly after. This is a realm of cosmic senescence, a place where physics itself is suffering from dementia. 

To visit the Ascendant Necropolis—or whatever name you choose to give this delaminating world—you must prepare for three primary antagonists: **extreme atmospheric hostility**, **spatial inconstancy**, and **profound psychological isolation.**

Here is your comprehensive preparation guide for surviving the Metropolis of the Void.

---

### I. Biological Preservation (The Suit and The Senses)

Your body evolved for pressure, moisture, and gravity. This world possesses nearly none of these. 

**1. The Atmospheric Skin (Environmental Suit)**
You cannot wear standard cold-weather gear. The total lack of humidity combined with the low air pressure will rapidly suck the moisture from your lungs, eyes, and skin. Within an hour, you would face excruciating mummification. 
*   **Requirement:** A fully sealed, lightweight extravehicular pressure suit. It does not need to be heavily armored, as the world lacks sharp corners or aggressive fauna, but it *must* maintain internal humidity and atmospheric pressure. 
*   **Respiratory Gear:** The air is thin and hypoxic. You will need a closed-loop oxygen rebreather. Do not rely on external air compressors; there isn't enough ambient atmosphere to compress effectively.

**2. Acoustic Supplementation**
The silence of this world is an insidious danger. Because the thin air dampens sound to a "breathless hush," you will experience total acoustic sensory deprivation. The human brain, deprived of audio input, will begin generating auditory hallucinations to fill the void.
*   **Requirement:** Bone-conduction headsets built into your suit’s helmet. Program a continuous, low-volume track of familiar, grounding audio—the sound of soft rainfall, a gentle heartbeat, or rustling leaves. This will keep your mind tethered to biological reality while the universe outside quietly crinkles into math.

**3. Vision and Lighting**
The eternal, bruised-purple dusk will rapidly induce eye strain. 
*   **Requirement:** Wide-spectrum, low-glare visual arrays. Because the mineral architecture is pale and chalky, shining a harsh, high-lumen flashlight will blind you with a harsh white bounce-back. Bring diffused, soft-halo lighting (amber or warm white) to cast gentle shadows, which will help you read the depth and curvature of the smooth, corner-less terrain.

### II. Locomotion and Traversal (Navigating Broken Physics)

This is a city where up is a matter of perspective and the ground does not exist. A single misstep translates to an eternal fall into the indigo fog.

**1. High-Friction, Non-Destructive Footwear**
Because the architecture resembles smooth, chalky pumice or bone, **do not wear crampons or spiked climbing boots**. Hard metal strikes could cause the brittle, ancient mineral to micro-fracture, triggering a localized reality collapse. Instead, wear thick, ultra-high-friction climbing rubber (similar to advanced bouldering shoes) capable of gripping entirely smooth surfaces.

**2. Micro-Propulsion and Grappling Hooks**
Gravity here is "a suggestion." You will likely encounter Möbius loops and inverted districts. Walking upside down is possible due to localized gravitational warps, but crossing the terrifying gulfs of open air between districts is highly dangerous.
*   **Requirement:** A wrist- or hip-mounted micro-thruster system (compressed nitrogen gas). Should the bridge you are walking on suddenly decide to dissolve into coordinates, you will enter freefall. Thrusters will allow you to navigate the zero-G gulf to the nearest stable terrace. 
*   **Tethering:** Always walk with a smart-cord tether attached to the most solid-looking architecture you can find. Use soft-latch clamps that grip the gentle curves without needing to drill or hammer into the stone.

**3. The Reality Anchor**
If the spire to your left is turning into a flock of mathematical coordinates, what prevents *you* from delaminating? An ordinary human traveler needs a localized ontological stabilizer—a personal device that enforces baseline Earth physics within a one-meter radius of your body. This ensures that you do not unspool into numbers, and guarantees your personal gravity remains oriented to your feet, even if the city around you is sideways.

### III. Interacting with the Ecosystem

**1. Reading the Tide of the City**
You noted that the city "breathes," expanding and contracting in a rhythmic, tidal cadence on a massive scale. Before you begin exploring, **sit at the precipice for at least two hours**.
*   Watch. Time the inhales and exhales of the twisting archways. Sync a timer on your HUD to this rhythm. You must learn to travel *with* the breath of the city. Do not try to sprint across a causeway while it is inhaling and pulling taut; wait for the exhale, when the spatial tension relaxes, to make your crossing.

**2. The Ash-Whales (The Local Fauna)**
These paper-thin, blind, drifting leviathans are harmless to your biology, but they pose a navigational hazard. Because they feed on the kinetic friction of reality breakdown, *they will be drawn to spatial anomalies*. 
*   **Protocol:** If you see a flock of these creatures gliding silently toward you, **it means the structure you are standing on is about to warp or dissolve.** Use them as an early warning system. 
*   If you find yourself in the path of a feeding herd, deactivate your thrusters, stay entirely still, and flatten yourself against the stone. Let them wrap their diaphanous wings around the distortion. They do not care about you, but if you panic and fire thrusters, you risk disrupting their delicate feeding process or getting swept off your feet by their massive, shifting bulk.

### IV. Psychological Preparation

**1. Existential Vertigo Training**
The most dangerous thing you bring into this universe is your own mind. The sheer, agoraphobic scale, the lack of a ground floor, and the sight of physical matter transforming into mathematics will trigger severe ontological shock.
*   **Prep:** Spend time in high-immersion VR simulators depicting bottomless environments. Train yourself to stop looking "down," because down is an illusion here. Focus only on the geometry immediately around you.

**2. The Beauty and the Senescence**
Finally, prepare yourself to feel an overwhelming, melancholic awe. You are walking through a dying universe’s final, beautiful thought. The soft snow of condensing coordinates forming new, useless bridges; the papery sigh of collapsing causeways—it is a deeply sad, infinitely peaceful place.

Bring a camera designed to capture spatial geometry (LIDAR or holographic), but accept beforehand that no recording will do it justice. 

Set your emergency extraction beacon to trigger automatically if your suit detects a sudden drop in your personal spatial integrity, take a deep breath of your canned Earth air, step off the precipice onto the first sweeping archway, and let the quiet, cold majesty of the void wash over you.
