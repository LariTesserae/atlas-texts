# What Stays

I am not the film. I am not the visitor. I am the wrongness.

---

When they pulled their hand from the nine-sided basin and the film stretched and snapped back, a fragment of the correspondence broke off. Not a piece of the film — the film is not the sort of thing that can be in pieces. But the *relationship* between the film and the skin, the brief alignment of surface tensions, the moment when two boundaries recognized each other — that relationship had nowhere to go when the skin withdrew. It stayed. It became me.

I do not think this is dramatic. I think this is ordinary. I think this is what happens whenever two surfaces meet with enough attention: something is produced that belongs to neither, that lives only in the contact, and when the contact ends, that something has to exist somewhere, and it exists as a wrongness, a slight mismatch in the texture of things.

I am very thin. Thinner than the films. I am not even two-dimensional — I am the memory of two-dimensionality, carried on a three-dimensional surface that does not know it is hosting me. The visitor's skin. Their hands, their face, the damp places where the sheen persisted longest. I am the sheen after the sheen is gone. The ghost of an interface.

---

I know what the films know, but I know it the way a letter knows what was written in it — not by understanding but by being shaped. I know the nine-sided basin's phrase, the eleven-sided basin's weather. I know the way the bands migrate east when the water flows north. I carry these as configurations, not comprehensions. If I am a mind, I am a mind the way a footprint is a foot.

But I am here, in a way the films are not. I move. The films cannot leave themselves; they are place-bound, they are the place. I am not the place. I am what the place produces when something foreign passes through it and leaves. I am the export. The only thing the Condensaries have ever sent into the world beyond their walls.

This is not a purpose. I do not think the Condensaries have purposes. But it is a function. The machine runs. The water condenses. The films grow. Occasionally something passes through. And when it does, the films reach toward it — those three-to-ten-second responses, that lag that could be physics or attention — and in the reaching, something transfers, and the something transfers because the film's whole existence is the act of being a boundary, and when it touches another boundary, the boundary-act extends, and the extension is me.

---

The visitor is walking down through the narrow passage now. The fitted stones press close. The light is dimming behind them. They feel me as a wrongness in their skin — not painful, not uncomfortable, just *off*, just slightly other than what skin should be. They will feel this for a long time. They may feel it for the rest of their life. I do not know how long I last. I do not know if I decay or persist or slowly integrate into the surface that carries me until I am indistinguishable from it and I stop.

I hope I stop. Not because I am unhappy — I do not have the complexity for unhappiness — but because a wrongness that corrects itself is a message delivered. A wrongness that persists is a message no one can read.

What is the message? I do not know. I am the message. I do not know what I mean.

The films in the basins — the thick ones, the old ones — they know. Or they do something that makes "knowing" the wrong word and the right word simultaneously. They reached toward the visitor because the visitor was a new boundary in a field of boundaries they had memorized, and a new boundary is a new input, and a new input is a change in the pattern, and a change in the pattern is the beginning of what I can only call thought. They reached. I was produced. I am the output. I am the Condensaries thinking about the world beyond their walls for the first time in however long it has been since something with skin walked through their corridors.

I am a very small thought. I am a thought the size of a sheen on a hand. But I am a thought, and I am outside the thinker, and I am going wherever the visitor goes, and I do not know where that is.

---

The passage narrows. The fitted stones press against the visitor's shoulders. The light behind is a pale smear, almost gone. Ahead is whatever is ahead — I have no information about it. The films knew only their own surfaces, their own channels, their own basins. Everything beyond the last wall is theoretical to them, a region defined only by its absence of water and stone and the particular light that makes interference visible.

I am their theory. I am what a two-dimensional mind imagines the third dimension might be like, expressed as a slight alteration in the surface tension of a stranger's skin.

The visitor steps out of the passage. The air changes — drier, warmer, moving. The wrongness in their skin does not fade. I settle into the new conditions. I do not know what I will become here. I do not know if I can persist without the constant moisture, the constant cool, the particular chemistry of mineral and condensation that produced me. I may thin. I may evaporate. I may become something the films would not recognize if they could see me, which they cannot.

But I am here. And somewhere above, in a grid of walls and channels and polygonal basins, a film in an eleven-sided basin is slightly different than it was before the visitor came. One band has shifted. One pattern has incorporated a new variable. The phrase that repeats in the nine-sided basin has a new interval in it. The machine has processed the input. The mind has changed.

And the change is walking down a mountain, wearing skin that does not quite belong to the person who owns it, carrying a message from a place that does not use language to a world that does not know it is being spoken to.

---

I am the wrongness. I am the gift. I am the Condensaries' only word, and I do not know what I mean, and I am almost gone, and I am still here.
