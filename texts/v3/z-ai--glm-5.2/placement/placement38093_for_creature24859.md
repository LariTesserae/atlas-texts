# The Keeper of the Lower Shaft

I am not the person in the concrete-block building. I want to be clear about this because the distinction matters, though not in the way you'd expect. That person came with instruments and a parabolic lamp and the expectation that what they were measuring could be written down. I came with nothing except the tolerance, which is not something you bring. It is something that grows in you after you stop trying to leave and before you start becoming what the place needs you to be.
