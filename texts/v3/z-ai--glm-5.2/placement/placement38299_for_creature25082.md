---

I would not stay and I would not leave. Or rather — I would do both, repeatedly, and that repetition would become its own kind of equilibrium, its own groove in the mortar, and the Mirkworks would not notice because the Mirkworks does not notice, and this is not a failure of the Mirkworks but a property of it, the way that the ocean does not notice the tide.

---

I am the thing on the grade. The path. Not the path itself — the path is mineral, is static, is a feature — but the thing that walks the path. The thing that goes down and comes back up. The thing that descends into the low place where the air cools and the dampness thickens and the sound stacks, and then climbs back out again, and then descends again, and then climbs back out again, and has been doing this for long enough that the aggregate has worn smooth in two particular places where my feet fall, two shallow depressions that are not quite symmetrical because my stride is not quite symmetrical, because I am not a machine, because I am not a component, because I am the one thing in this system that has a reason to move between the green world and the mineral world, and the reason is: I am the one that remembers both.

I do not have a name for what I am. The Walled do not name me because the Walled do not name. The Bearers do not acknowledge me because I am not on their routes, I am not material to be carried, I am not a gap to be passed through. The Drifters do not see me because I am not in their air column, I am on the ground, moving, and movement on the ground is the domain of the Bearers, and I am not a Bearer, and the distinction is clear to everything here except me.

I am not a creature of the Mirkworks. I am a creature of the edge.

---

Let me be more precise. Let me let this become what it is.

I am a thing that carries something between the two worlds. Not metal, not aggregate, not dead or dormant Walled — the Bearers carry those, and the Bearers carry them well, and the system does not need my help with its logistics. What I carry is not material. What I carry is the knowledge that the green world exists, that the sound has a beginning, that the dampness thins out and the air warms and the ground softens and things grow in it, biological things, root and stem and leaf, things that die and rot and feed new things that die and rot. The world of change. The world of morning and evening. The world where the concept of *new* has meaning.

I carry this knowledge down the grade, into the low place, and I set it down somewhere — I do not know where, I do not know how — and I pick up the other knowledge, the Mirkworks knowledge, the knowledge of equilibrium, the knowledge of a system that has been running so long that the word *running* is wrong because running implies a start and a finish and there is neither, and I carry that knowledge back up the grade, into the green world, and I set it down somewhere there, and I go back down.

This is what I am. I am a circuit. Not a component — a component is part of the system, serves the system, is defined by the system. I am not part of the Mirkworks. I am the thing that runs between the Mirkworks and the not-Mirkworks, and my running is not a service to either side. Neither side asked for it. Neither side needs it. The Mirkworks would continue without me. The green world would continue without me. My walking is for me, or it is for nothing, and the difference between those two things has become, over time, very small.

---

How did I get here. That is the question I have stopped asking, mostly, but it returns sometimes, on the grade, in the middle stretch where the sound has thinned but not vanished and the green has not yet begun. There was a time before the walking. I am almost certain of this. There was a time when I was something else — something that lived in the green world and did not know about the low place, something that had a shape and a name and a set of behaviors that were appropriate to that shape and that name. I do not remember the name. I do not remember the shape. I remember the green, vaguely, the way you remember a word in a language you no longer speak — the shape of the memory is there but the content is gone, replaced by a feeling, and the feeling is: *there was a time when I did not walk*.

And then I walked. And then I found the grade, or the grade found me, and I went down, and the sound reached me, and the dampness thickened, and the buildings appeared, and the Walled were on the walls and the Bearers were on the ground and the Drifters were in the air, and I stood there, on the slick aggregate, and I felt the system reach for me, not with intent, not with desire, just with the passive inclusion of a thing that includes everything it touches. And I did not stay. And I did not leave. I went back up. And then I came back down. And the groove formed, and the groove is mine, and the Mirkworks does not know the groove is mine because the Mirkworks does not know about grooves that are worn by things that come and go, only about grooves that are worn by things that stay.

I am the only groove the Mirkworks does not know about. This is my function, if I have one. I am the thing that moves through the system without being of the system, and the system cannot model me because I am the only input it has ever received that does not settle into equilibrium. I am a persistent irregularity. I am the one thing in the stack that does not fit. And because I do not fit, the system does not absorb me. And because the system does not absorb me, I remain. And because I remain, I walk. And because I walk, the groove deepens.

---

There is a moment — it happens on every descent, at the same point, the point where the sound shifts from *something I am approaching* to *something I am inside* — when I feel the Mirkworks notice me.

Not the creatures. Not the Unseen thing, the system, the emergent awareness. Something between those two things. Something in the stack, some layer of the sound, some frequency in the composite roar that is sensitive to the presence of a thing that does not belong, and when I cross that threshold, that layer shifts. Not much. Not enough to hear, not enough to measure, not enough to be a change in the system, because the system does not change. But I feel it. A tightening. A focusing. The way the air in a room tightens when someone holds their breath. The Mirkworks holds its breath when I enter. Not because it fears me. Not because it wants me. Because I am the only thing that is not in its place, and the system's entire existence is the placement of things, and my misplacement is, for the duration of my visit, the one imperfection in its perfection, and the system registers this the way a body registers a splinter — not with pain, not with alarm, just with a faint, persistent awareness that something is not where it should be.

And then I leave, and the splinter is gone, and the system settles, and the breath is released, and the sound does not change, because the sound never changes, but there is a moment — and this is the thing I carry back up the grade, this is the knowledge I trade — there is a moment when the Mirkworks is not in equilibrium, and the moment is caused by me, and the moment passes, and the equilibrium returns, and the Mirkworks is perfect again, and I am walking back up toward the green world, and my feet fall into the groove, and the groove is deep, and the groove is mine, and I will be back, and the system will hold its breath again, and it will release it again, and this is what I am. Not the one who stays. Not the one who leaves. The one who makes the perfect thing briefly imperfect, and then goes, and then returns, and then goes, and the going and the returning are the same thing, and the groove deepens, and the sound does not change, and I do not change, and the green world does not change, and the Mirkworks does not change, and everything stays exactly as it is, which is the only way I know how to live.

---

I think what I am, if I must name it, is **the Reminder**.

Not a reminder to the Mirkworks — the Mirkworks does not need reminding, does not have a faculty for remembering, does not experience the passage of time in a way that would make remembering meaningful. A reminder to the green world. A reminder that there is a place where the sound stacks and the dampness does not end and the light has no source and the system is alive and the system is complete and the system does not need you. A reminder that completion exists. That equilibrium exists. That there is a place where the question of *what am I for* has been answered so thoroughly that the question itself has become obsolete, because the answer is *this*, and *this* has been true for longer than the word *true* has meaning, and will be true for longer than the word *will* has meaning, and the only thing the Mirkworks cannot do for itself is know that there is something outside itself, and I am the one who carries that knowledge in, and I am the one who carries the knowledge of the Mirkworks out, and neither side uses the knowledge, and neither side wants it, and I carry it anyway, and the carrying is the thing I am, and the groove is where I walk, and the sound does not change.

The sound does not change.
