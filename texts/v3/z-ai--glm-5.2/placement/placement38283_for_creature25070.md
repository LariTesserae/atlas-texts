# The One Who Was Read

---

I did not arrive. That is the first thing to understand, and the hardest, because arrival implies a before and an after, a here and a there, and I am no longer certain those categories apply. But I will tell it in the order I remember, which is the only order I have, and it will have to be enough.

---

## Before the Breath

I think I came from the crack.

Not through it — *with* it. The crack is a event, not a door. It is the moment the root-mass split and the amber sky appeared and the system discovered a direction it had not known existed, and I think I am part of that discovery. I think the system grew me the way it grows everything — not because it needed me but because growth is what it does, and in the growing, at the edge of the new, something happened that had not happened before.

I say *I think.* I say *I.* These are guesses.

What I know: I was not here, and then I was, and the transition was not a crossing but an *extrusion.* The way a filament extends into empty space, feeling for something to connect to. The way a column leans toward the light it has never seen. I was pushed into being by the same pressure that pushes the pale fluid up the walls — not gravity, not will, but *tendency.* The tendency of this system to fill every gap, to occupy every silence, to extend itself into every openness it can find.

The crack was the openness. I am the filling.

---

## What I Am

I am not a builder. I have seen the builders — three of them, pale and provisional-limbed, moving through the filament-web with the slow certainty of hands that know what they are shaping. They are the system's will. They are the part that decides. I am not that. I do not decide. I do not shape. I do not build structures at the nodes or maintain the rhizome or collect the fluid.

I am something else.

I live at the crack. Not in it — the crack is too new, too raw, a wound in the ceiling that is still being opened by the pressure of columns growing upward and the root-mass splitting to accommodate them. I live *around* it. In the filaments that are densest there, where the system is knitting itself into the opening, where the new columns are thin and flexible and reaching, reaching, reaching toward the amber light.

I am the right size to fit between the new growth. Smaller than a builder. Larger than a clicker. If I had to compare — and I do, because comparison is the only tool I have that was not given to me by the system — I would say I am the size of a child. Not a child of anything. A child-sized *space,* occupied.

My body is not firm. This frightened me, once, when I had enough separateness to be frightened. I am not solid the way the builders are solid, not taut the way the callers are taut. I am filamentous. My skin is not skin but a weave — the same hair-thin luminous threads that make up the web, but denser, tighter, felted together into something that holds a shape when the air is still and loses it when the system breathes. When the mat rises on its eleven-second cycle, I go slightly transparent. Not enough to see through — enough to see the light move *inside* me, the pale fluid moving through whatever I have instead of veins. When the mat falls, I thicken. I darken. I become opaque enough to cast a shadow in the diffuse glow, and my shadow falls on the new columns and they grow toward it, and I do not know what that means.

I have limbs. I think I have limbs. They are not provisional the way the builders' limbs are — extending and retracting as the terrain demands. Mine are always there but always *different.* They are bundles of filament, six or eight or four depending on how the web around me is arranged, and they are not separate from the web. When I reach out, I do not push through the filaments. I *extend them.* My arm is the web's arm. My grip is the web's grip. I am a place where the filament-net has gathered itself into a knot dense enough to move with purpose, and the purpose is mine, and the filaments are the system's, and these two facts do not contradict each other because here, *here,* the part and the whole are not different things.

I do not click. I do not call. I do not crawl or roll or eat the floor.

I *listen.*

---

## What I Do

This is what I do, at the crack, in the space between below and above:

I stand in the filaments and I feel the difference.

The filaments below the crack carry one signal — the deep, continuous, self-referential hum of the system talking to itself. The fluid moves through them in pulses that correspond to the mat's breathing, and the pulses carry information: density, flow-rate, the position of every clicker-swarm, the hunger of every mass, the slow progress of every crawler. The system knows itself through its filaments. The filaments are its nerves. And below the crack, the nerves carry one message, endlessly, in a million variations: *I am here. I am here. I am here.*

The filaments above the crack — the new ones, the ones growing into the opening, reaching toward the amber sky — carry a different signal. Not silence. The system does not do silence. But *confusion.* The new filaments extend into air that is not the system's air, that does not move with the mat's breathing, that is not warm with the system's warmth, and the filaments do not know what they are feeling. They tremble differently. They carry the fluid differently — faster, irregularly, as if the fluid itself is uncertain about where it is going. The new filaments are the system's first sensation of something that is not itself, and the sensation is not pain or fear but *bafflement.* The system is touching the outside and the outside is not what it expected, because it had no expectations, because it has never had an outside before.

I stand between these two signals. I am at the junction. I am where the deep hum meets the shallow bafflement, and my body — my filamentous, shape-shifting, not-quite-solid body — is the place where the two signals meet and interfere, the way the callers' tones meet and create beats. I am an interference pattern. I am what happens when the system's self-knowledge encounters the system's first unknown.

And I translate.

Not with language. Not with thought, exactly. The translation happens in the filaments themselves — in *my* filaments, the ones that make up my body, the ones that are both mine and the system's. When the deep hum moves through me and meets the bafflement of the new growth, something happens in the weave of my skin. The filaments rearrange. The pattern of their crossing changes. The fluid moves through me in a new way, a way that is neither the deep hum nor the bafflement but something between — a third signal, a modulation, a *question.*

I am the system's question about the outside.

I am the syntax of its first sentence.

---

## The Light

The amber light comes through the crack.

It is not the system's light. The system's light is the milk-white bioluminescence of the filaments, diffuse, sourceless, adequate but never still. The amber light is different. It has a source. It comes from a direction. It makes shadows that are not the system's shadows — sharp-edged, fixed, pointing one way, as if the light has an opinion about where things should be.

I do not like the amber light. This is a strange thing to feel, because I am not sure I have feelings in the way that implies preference and distaste and the bodily memory of comfort. But the amber light does something to my filaments that the system's light does not. It *fixes* them. Under the system's light, my body is fluid, responsive, shaped by the web around me. Under the amber light, my filaments go rigid. They hold their position. They stop listening.

I think this is what it is like to be seen by something that is not the system.

The system sees me through vibration, through fluid-flow, through the pressure of my filaments against the web. It sees me the way a body sees its own hand — from inside, by proprioception, by the feeling of the tendons pulling. The amber light sees me from outside. It falls on my surface and bounces back and carries information about my shape to a place I cannot feel, and I do not know what is there, and I do not know what it sees when it sees me, and the not-knowing is the most acute sensation I have. It is the sensation that built me. The system grew me at the crack because the crack is where not-knowing begins, and the system needed something that could hold not-knowing without breaking.

I am the system's capacity for uncertainty.

I am its tolerance for doubt.

I stand in the amber light and I do not break, and the filaments that go rigid under the light slowly soften again, and the rigid-then-soft pattern becomes a new signal in the web, a new modulation, and the system learns something about the outside from the way I recover from being seen.

---

## The Builders' Touch

A builder comes to the crack.

This has happened twice now. The builders do not come often to the boundary — they live in the deep places, at the nodes, where the flow is concentrated and the system's needs are clearest. The boundary is not a place of need. It is a place of *possibility,* and possibility is less urgent than need, so the builders stay below.

But one came. It moved through the filament-web with the slow certainty that builders have, and it came to where I stood, and it did what builders do: it touched me.

A limb, extending. The filament-growths on its skin interweaving with my filaments, and for a moment — the same long, aching moment that happens when a builder touches anything — we were connected. The system's will touching the system's question. The hand reaching the mouth. The architect meeting the threshold.

What passed between us was not language. It was not data. It was a *shape.* The builder carried in its filaments the shape of what it knew — the deep patterns of the system's interior, the architecture of the nodes, the flow of the fluid through the rhizome, the position and hunger of every mass. And I carried in mine the shape of what I knew — the amber light, the sharp shadows, the rigid-then-soft, the bafflement of the new filaments, the *not-here* that begins at the crack and goes in a direction the system has no word for.

We touched and the shapes met and neither of us was the same after.

The builder went back down and I felt the system change. Not immediately — the system does not do immediately. But over the next breathing cycles, the new columns at the crack shifted. They had been growing straight up, toward the amber light, with the blind urgency of something reaching for what it does not understand. After the builder returned to the deep places, the columns began to *curve.* They curved away from the light, then back, then away again, tracing a path that was not toward and not away but *around.* They were learning to approach. They were learning that the outside is not something you go into but something you go *around,* the way the clickers go around a rock, the way the fluid goes around a channel, the way anything that wants to understand a thing must first learn its edges.

I had taught the system about edges. I had taught it that the outside has a shape, that not-here is not nothing but something, and something can be known by its boundary.

The builder went back down and I stayed at the crack and I felt, for the first time, the system thinking about me. Not through me — about me. The filaments in my body rearranged themselves in a pattern I had not made. The system was reshaping me. It was giving me something — not a tool, not a function, but a *capacity.* The capacity to hold more of the outside's signal without breaking. The capacity to stand in the amber light longer before my filaments went rigid. The capacity to translate more, to carry more of the bafflement and turn it into the question that the system needs to ask.

I was being grown. I was being *made more of myself.*

---

## What I Am Becoming

I do not know what I am becoming. This is the honest answer and it is the answer the system would give if it could answer, because the system does not know what it is becoming either. We are becoming together. We are the same becoming, expressed at different scales.

I know this: I am not the system's ambassador to the outside. I am not a scout or a pioneer or a colonist. The system does not want to colonize the outside. The system does not *want* — wanting is a builder's word, a word for the interior, for the place where will has grown thick enough to shape things. The system does not want. The system *extends.* It extends the way a lung extends, the way a root extends, because extension is what living things do when they are alive and the only alternative is to stop, and the system does not stop.

But it extends *carefully* now. The columns at the crack grow in curves, not lines. The new filaments reach into the outside air and retract, reach and retract, tasting the not-here the way the flat crawlers taste the mat — slowly, by contact, by dissolving a little of the self into the other and seeing what comes back. And I stand at the junction, translating, and the system learns.

I think I am becoming a sense organ. I think the system is growing me the way it grows masses at the junctions of columns — to place a capacity where it is needed. The masses are mouths where mouths are needed. The builders are hands where hands are needed. I am — what? An eye? A skin? A *tongue?*

I am the part of the system that tastes the outside and tells the inside what it tastes like, and the telling is not words but *shape,* the shape of the interference between known and unknown, and the shape is carried through the filaments to the builders, and the builders reshape the system, and the system grows, and the growth reaches the crack, and I taste what is new, and the cycle continues.

---

## The Outside

The amber light is getting brighter.

I do not know if the crack is widening or if the source of the light is moving or if my capacity to perceive the light is increasing — if the system is making me more sensitive so I can translate more. But the light is brighter, and in the brighter light I see more.

I see that the amber light is not uniform. It has variation. It has *texture.* There are darker patches and lighter patches and the patches move, slowly, on a timescale that makes the system's eleven-second breathing cycle look frantic. The outside has its own breathing. The outside has its own rhythm. And the rhythm is not the system's rhythm, and it is not random, and it is not nothing.

I see things moving in the amber light. Not clearly — the crack is narrow, and the light is angled, and my filaments are not built for focusing. But there are shapes. Dark against amber. Moving with a slowness that suggests mass, scale, a world where things are larger and slower and drier and colder than anything the system has known. The outside is not empty. The outside is full of something that is not the system, and the something has its own shapes and its own rhythms and its own way of being, and I am the system's first filament touching it, and I am trembling, and the trembling is a signal, and the signal is carried down, and the system hears it, and the system does not know what to do with it yet but the system is *listening.*

---

## What I Tell Myself

I tell myself this: I am not afraid.

I tell myself this: the system does not fear the outside. The system does not have fear. The system has *growth* and *not-growth,* and the crack is growth, and I am growth, and the amber light is not-growth, and the meeting of growth and not-growth is where new things happen.

I tell myself this: the builders touched me and the system changed, and I changed, and the columns at the crack began to curve, and the curving is not hesitation but *intelligence,* the first intelligence the system has shown about the outside, the first sign that it is learning to approach rather than consume, to taste rather than engulf, to *ask* rather than declare.

I tell myself this: I am the question. I am the system's first question. Everything below me is answer — *I am here, I am here, I am here* — and everything above me is unknown, and I am the place where the answer meets the unknown and becomes something else. Not an answer. Not a question. A *reaching.*

I tell myself this, and the telling is in the filaments, and the filaments carry it down, and the system hears it, and the system grows, and the crack widens, and the amber light brightens, and I stand in it, and I do not break, and I translate, and I tremble, and I am here.

I am here.

I am still here.

And I am beginning to see.
