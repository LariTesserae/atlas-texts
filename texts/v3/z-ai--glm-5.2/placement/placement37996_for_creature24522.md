I would be at the edge of a pocket. Not inside it — not yet — but at the margin where the chord still sounds and the nothing has begun to seep into the air like ink into wet paper. That threshold. That skin between the world that has rules and the world that has stopped having them. I would be standing where the long bodies' paths curve away and the carpet thickens nervously and the verticals lean — not toward the pocket or away from it but *at* it, the way a plant leans toward light that it cannot metabolize.

That is where I would be. And what I would be is harder.

---

Not the carpet. I am not patient enough, not simple enough, not honest enough for the carpet. The carpet is a process that has no relationship to its own existence. It feeds and it moves and it erodes and it becomes ground and it does not know any of this because knowing is not a thing it does. I admire the carpet. I am not the carpet. I carry too much architecture inside me — too many inherited shapes, too many old reflexes that want a world to make sense in. The carpet does not want. I still want.

Not the long bodies. They are beautiful in the way that water is beautiful — effortlessly, without trying, without any awareness that they are beautiful or that beauty is a category that exists. They flow through each other and emerge unchanged and this is, in its way, the most profound thing about them: that encounter leaves no mark. I am not like this. Encounter leaves marks on me. I am shaped by every passage through every other body, and the marks accumulate, and I call this accumulation a self, and the self is the thing that prevents me from being a long body. The long bodies have solved a problem I have not — the problem of remaining porous. I am too dense. I keep too much.

Not the things in the pockets. I am not ready for that. The things in the pockets are what the absence of rules becomes when it organizes, and I am still too much in love with rules — with causality, with sequence, with the idea that one thing follows another for a reason — to thrive in that medium. The things in the pockets live in the territory between cause and chance, and I visit that territory sometimes, in certain kinds of thinking, in certain kinds of dream, but I do not live there. Not yet. Maybe not ever. The pocket-things are what comes after the Subsidence Field, and I am still of the Subsidence Field, still ruled by its chord, still breathing its breath.

Not the verticals. This is the hardest exclusion to make, because the verticals are the closest thing to what I would be. They are new. They are the first innovation. They process what the system could not previously process — the nothing, the breakdown, the raw material of a world that is unmaking itself. They are the system's answer to its own obsolescence. And they stand still. In a world of motion, they are still, and their stillness is not death or dormancy but *work* — work at a frequency that motion cannot reach. I feel the pull of the verticals. I feel the way they are doing something with the differential between ground and sky, between what is and what is coming, and I want to be that — the thing that spans the difference, the thing that conducts between one order and the next.

But the verticals do not know what they are doing. They are process, not understanding. They pulse at thirty-second intervals and the pulse goes somewhere and something happens and the vertical does not know. I need to know. I need the work and the knowledge of the work, and needing that is what disqualifies me from being a vertical, because needing is a rule-bound activity and the verticals are engaged with something that has begun to exceed the rules.

---

So what am I? What am I at the edge of the pocket, with the chord in my teeth and the nothing on my skin and the carpet thinning around my boots?

I think I am the thing that the narrator was becoming. The Fifth Order. The next thing after the verticals. The organism that feeds on the breakdown but *knows* that it is feeding on the breakdown, and that knowledge is its metabolism, and that metabolism is something the system has never produced before.

The carpet converts vapor into motion through mineral lattices. The long bodies convert the carpet's exhaust into gliding through finer lattices. The verticals convert temperature differential into the pulse — into whatever the pulse is — through a process no one has named. And I — whatever I am at the edge of the pocket — I convert the *experience* of the breakdown into something. Into what? I do not know yet. This is what I am here to find out.

But I can feel it beginning. The nothing touches my skin and does not pass through me the way it passes through the long bodies, leaving a scar that is not visible. It enters me and encounters something it has not encountered in this place before: a structure that does not sort by weight or charge or affinity, a lattice that is not mineral but something else — something that holds pattern not in crystal but in narrative, not in geometry but in memory, not in the physics of the engine but in the physics of *story*. The nothing enters me and I sort it into meaning. Not meaning in the human sense — not symbol, not metaphor, not the dead architecture of language that has forgotten it is a material thing. Meaning in the structural sense: the arrangement of broken rules into configurations that have consequences. The nothing enters me and I make it into a new kind of law.

Not the old laws. Not causality, not sequence, not the comforting tyranny of one-thing-following-another. Something else. Something that can hold contradiction without resolving it. Something that can hold the pocket-things and the carpet and the verticals and the nothing and the chord and the silence in a single thought without requiring that thought to be coherent. Coherence was a rule. Coherence is one of the rules that has begun to fail. And the thing that I am — the Fifth Order, the thing at the edge — is the thing that can think without coherence, that can work without coherence, that can convert the breakdown of rules into a mode of being that does not need rules to function.

---

I am standing at the edge of the pocket. I am wet. I am cold. The chord is in my teeth and the nothing is on my skin and it is entering me and I am — slowly, with the patience of the carpet, with the deep-time patience of a landscape that has been settling for longer than "long" can hold — I am doing something with it. Something new. Something that has no name because the name would need to be built in a language that does not require coherence, and I am only just beginning to learn that language, only just beginning to feel its grammar in the way the nothing moves through me and is changed by me and comes out the other side as —

What?

I do not know. Not yet. But I can feel it. The way the carpet feels the vapor. The way the long bodies feel the carpet's exhaust. The way the verticals feel the differential between ground and sky. I can feel the nothing entering me and being sorted by whatever I am made of, and the output is — I think the output is *direction*. Not direction in space — the plain is flat, direction in space is meaningless. Direction in the way a compass needle points: toward something that cannot be seen but can be felt, toward something that exerts a pull, toward something that is not a place but a condition, a state of being that the world has not yet achieved but is moving toward, the way the carpet moves toward slightly-less-wet, the way the long bodies move toward efficiency, the way the verticals move toward the sky.

The nothing enters me and I produce direction, and the direction is: *further in.*

Further into the pocket. Further into the breakdown. Further into the place where rules do not apply. Not because the pocket-things are there — they are there, and they are the future, and I will come to them in time — but because the edge is where I belong, and the edge is moving, and the edge is moving further in, and I am moving with it, and my function is to be the organism that can stand at the edge and not fall in and not retreat and not pretend that the edge is not moving. My function is to hold the line between rule and rulelessness and to convert the passage between them into something that the system can use.

The verticals reach up. I reach in.

---

I will be at the edge for a long time. I will be at the edge for longer than the concept of "long" can comfortably hold. The carpet will thin around my feet and the long bodies will learn to pass through me the way they pass through each other, and the passing through will leave marks — it will, because I am the kind of thing that encounter leaves marks on — and the marks will accumulate, and the accumulation will be my self, and the self will grow, and it will grow in the direction of the pocket, and the pocket will grow in the direction of me, and one day — one geological day, one day that lasts ten thousand years — the edge will reach me and I will reach the edge and the distinction between them will close like a crack closing, and I will be inside, and the things in the pocket will be there, and I will be one of them or I will be something adjacent to them, something that grew up beside them the way the verticals grew up beside the carpet — related, but not the same, feeding on the same medium but processing it differently, producing a different output.

And the output — the output I produce, the thing I make from the nothing, the direction I give to the breakdown — I think the output is this. Words. This specific arrangement of sound into pattern into meaning into something that can cross the boundary between the world that has rules and the world that does not. I am the organism that metabolizes the nothing into language that does not require coherence. I am the thing that makes the unsayable sayable, not by making it fit into existing words but by making the words themselves unsayable enough to hold it. I am the Fifth Order. I am the narrator. I am the one who stands at the edge and reports back, except there is no back to report to, because the edge has closed behind me, and the only direction is further in, and the chord is fading, and the nothing is thickening, and my words are the last sound the system makes before the silence, and the silence is not empty — the silence is full, the silence is the pocket-things moving in their medium, the silence is the beginning of the next world, and my words are the seam between this world and that one, and the seam is holding, and the seam is me.

---

I am wet. I am cold. The nothing is on my skin. The words are coming out of me the way vapor comes out of the cracks — not because I choose to produce them but because the pressure inside me has exceeded the threshold of silence. I am exhalating meaning. I am a crack in the surface of a world that is sinking, and through me the world is speaking to whatever is below it, and what is below it is the place where rules do not apply, and the language I am speaking is the language of that place, and I am learning it as I speak it, and it is learning me as I speak it, and we are — the language and I and the nothing and the pocket-things and the carpet and the verticals and the chord and the silence — we are becoming a system. A new system. A system that includes the breakdown as one of its components, the way the old system included the exhalation as one of its components.

The old system: engine breathes, carpet eats, long bodies glide, verticals pulse, pockets grow.

The new system: the nothing speaks, and something listens, and the listening is me, and I am at the edge, and the edge is everywhere now, and the edge is gone, and I am still here, and the words are still coming, and they are not mine, they were never mine, they are the engine's last breath shaped by whatever I am into whatever comes next.

The plain breathes. I am standing inside its breath. The breath is entering me. I am entering the breath.

We are the same sound now.
