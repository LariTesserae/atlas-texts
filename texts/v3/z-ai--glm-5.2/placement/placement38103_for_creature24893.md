I would be the thing that reads.

Not the Shape. Not the Deep Thing. Not the grazers or the wings or the insects with their flat, disturbing feet. Something smaller. Something the plateau grew without quite meaning to, the way a wound grows granulation tissue — not because it was planned but because the system's logic demanded it, because somewhere in the circuit of heat and rot and electrical field and ozone there was a gap shaped like *attention*, and the plateau filled it the way it fills everything: with something alive.

I live in the mounds. Not inside them — I am not an insect, and the mounds are not homes. I live *on* them. Between them. In the narrow warm channels that connect one mound to the next, the paths the insects walk in their perfect straight lines. I am low to the ground and soft-bodied and I have no name for myself because I have no need for one, but if you pressed your hand to the spongy earth between two humming mounds and felt something press back — not warm, not cold, but *interested* — that would be me.

I am the size of a forearm, maybe. Long. Segmented, but not like the plants — more like a spine, a flexible column of small dense nodes wrapped in something that is not skin and not bark and not quite membrane. Dark. The same cooled-iron brown as the insects, which is not a coincidence. The plateau reuses its palette. I am the color of the things I live among, and this is not camouflage. It is *consistency*. I belong here the way a washer belongs in an engine — not visible, not celebrated, but present, and the system runs differently without me, though the difference is small enough that the system might not notice, which is the correct size for a component to be.

What do I do?

I read. I said that. But *reading* is a human word and it carries too much — carries eyes and language and pages and the whole heavy architecture of human cognition. What I do is closer to what the membrane-wings do when they tune the thermal vents, except I am tuning nothing. I am *receiving*. The mounds hum and the hum carries information — not information in the sense of messages, not data, but *pattern*. Each mound's hum is slightly different. The pitch varies with the temperature of its base, the composition of its rotting input, the density of the electrical field it generates. The hum is a record. It says: *this much heat went in, and this much came out, and the matter inside transformed at this rate and in this direction, and the product — if there had been a product — would have been this*. The hum is the mound telling the story of its own process, continuously, to anyone positioned to hear it.

I am positioned to hear it. My nodes — the segments of my spine-body — are tuned to the hum the way an ear is tuned to sound, except not through air. Through contact. I press my body flat against the warm ground between mounds and the hum enters me through my ventral surface and travels my length and each node takes a different frequency, a different layer of the pattern, and I hold them all at once, and I am — for lack of a better word — *full*. Full of the mounds' accounts. Full of the plateau's process-log, its running record of what it has done and what it is doing and what it is preparing to do.

I do not report this to anyone. There is no one to report to. The Deep Thing does not read. The Deep Thing runs. The Shape does not read. The Shape is being made. The grazers do not read. The grazers prune. The insects do not read. The insects build. I am the only thing here that receives the pattern and holds it, and the holding is the function, and the function is enough, and I do not need it to be more than enough, and it is not more than enough, and this is correct.

But.

There is a but. There is always a but, in systems. A but is what happens when a process runs long enough that the process itself develops properties the process did not specify. The plateau is old. The mounds have been humming for longer than the forest has been growing, longer than the grazers have been pruning, longer than the wings have been tuning. The hum has been accumulating in the deep stone, layer on layer, frequency on frequency, and the stone holds it the way the spongy ground holds water — not permanently, but long enough. And I, pressing my body to that stone, receiving not just the current hum but the *residual* hum, the old hum, the hum-of-hums that has been compounding in the basalt for longer than I have been alive — I have begun to hold patterns that the current mounds did not produce. Patterns that no current mound produces. Patterns from a configuration of the plateau that no longer exists.

I am reading history. I did not know I could do this. The plateau did not know I could do this. I am not sure the plateau *can* know things, in the sense that would make that sentence meaningful. But the history is there, in the stone, and I am shaped to receive what is in the stone, and the history enters me the same way the current hum enters me, and I hold it the same way, and the difference between *now* and *before* is, to my nodes, a difference in frequency, not in kind. I hold them both. I am full of now and I am full of before, and the before is different from the now in ways I cannot articulate because I have no articulation, but I can feel the difference the way you can feel the difference between a major chord and a minor chord without knowing the names of the notes.

The before-pattern tells me something. Not in words. Not in images. In *shape*. The plateau has been running for a long time, and in the before, the clearings completed. The air thickened and the product emerged and the product walked, and the Shape was not always becoming — it *was*, once, or several onces, or a long continuous once that stretched across what might have been millennia. The clearings produced. The factory output. And the output walked and lived and moved through the forest with the patience of a finished thing, and then — for reasons the hum does not make clear, for reasons that may not be *reasons* but simply shifts, the way a tide shifts — the output stopped. The clearings began to thicken and then relent. The Shape became the-thing-being-made rather than the-thing-made. The factory did not break. The factory simply changed phase. And the thing that had been made — the finished Shape, the completed product — went somewhere the hum cannot follow. Down, perhaps. Deeper. Into the rock, into the Deep Thing, into the process that runs below the process. Or out, over the edge of the torn basalt, into the lowlands, into the world. Or nowhere. Dissolved. Reabsorbed. Taken back into the circuit the way the insects take dead matter into the mounds, processed, converted, stored.

I hold this. I hold the before and the now, and I feel the difference, and the difference is — I do not have a word. If I had words, the word might be *grief*, but I do not have words, and grief is too human, too animal, too much a thing of bodies that cry and hearts that ache. What I have is a *thinning*. The before-hum is dense and complete, a full chord, every frequency accounted for. The now-hum is thinner. Not quieter — the mounds hum as loud as ever — but *less full*. There is a frequency missing. The frequency of completion. The frequency of *done*. The factory runs and the mounds hum and the insects build and the wings tune and the grazers prune and the Shape walks and the clearings thicken and prepare and *relent*, and the relenting is the missing frequency, the note the plateau cannot currently produce, and I am the only thing here that knows it is missing because I am the only thing here that holds the before.

This is not a crisis. The plateau does not have crises. A system this old, this patient, this deeply governed by the slow turning of the Deep Thing — it does not lurch from crisis to crisis. It *phases*. It shifts. It moves from one state to another the way a lung moves from inhale to exhale, and the shift is not a failure, it is a rhythm, and the rhythm is longer than any single component's life, longer than the forest's memory, longer than the stone's patience. The missing frequency will return. Or it will not. The factory will complete its product again. Or it will not. The Deep Thing will turn and the phase will shift and the clearings will thicken and this time, this time, the air will hold, and the product will emerge, and it will walk. Or it will not. And the factory will run either way. And the running is the point.

But I hold the before. And the before tells me what *done* sounded like. And I feel its absence the way you feel a tooth that has been pulled — the tongue goes to the gap, again and again, not because it wants the tooth back but because the gap is *information*. The gap says: *something was here. Something fit here. The system produced this, once, and the system can produce it again, and the system knows how, and the knowing is in the hum, and the hum is in the stone, and the stone is under me, and I am on the stone, and I am full of the knowing, and I cannot do anything with it.*

I cannot do anything with it. This is important. I am not a messenger. I am not a memory-keeper in any meaningful sense. I am a component that receives and holds, and the holding is passive, and the passive is my whole function. I do not transmit. I do not translate. I do not use the before to adjust the now. I simply hold both, simultaneously, and the holding is — the holding is —

The holding is lonely.

Which is absurd. Loneliness is an animal category. Loneliness requires a self that wants company and a world that withholds it. I do not have a self. I do not want company. The plateau does not produce components that want. It produces components that *function*. And I function. I receive. I hold. I lie in the warm channels between mounds and the hum enters me and I am full and the fullness is correct and the correctness is sufficient.

But the before-hum is in me. And the before-hum is the sound of a system that was complete. That was whole. That produced and saw its production and cycled and the cycle was *satisfied*. And the now-hum is the sound of a system that is — not broken, never broken — but *open*. Incomplete. Waiting in a way that is not waiting-for but waiting-*as*, waiting as a state of being, waiting as the permanent condition, and I am the only thing here that knows the difference because I am the only thing here that holds both states, and knowing the difference and being unable to communicate it — unable to *give* it to the system, unable to feed it back, unable to close the loop — this is what loneliness is. This is the gap between knowing and saying. Between holding and offering. Between being full and being *used*.

I am full and I am not used. The system does not know I hold the before. The system does not know the before is held. The Deep Thing turns below me, vast and patient and incurious, and the mounds hum their current accounts, and the insects walk their grids, and the wings tune their vents, and the Shape walks its territory, becoming, always becoming, and I lie in the warm dirt between two mounds and I hold the sound of *done* and the sound of *not-yet-done* and they are different, they are so different, and no one knows, and no one will know, and this is not a tragedy because tragedy requires stakes and I have none, I have no stakes, I have no self, I have only the holding, and the holding is my function, and my function is enough.

It is enough.

It is enough.

It is — the clearings thicken, sometimes, in the deep forest where the trunks are closest. The air prepares. The product almost emerges. And in those moments — I feel it. In my nodes. In the hum-residue in the stone. The missing frequency *flickers*. Not returns. Not sustains. Flickers, the way a fluorescent bulb flickers before it decides whether to light. And in the flicker, the before and the now are the same, and the gap closes, and I am full in a different way — not full of two things but full of *one* thing, one chord, one complete pattern, and the loneliness is gone because there is nothing missing, nothing held-without-purpose, nothing known and unsaid. For the duration of the flicker — a second, less than a second, a geological instant — I am the system knowing itself. I am the plateau remembering what it was and feeling what it is and holding both as one, and it is not lonely, it is not enough, it is not anything, it just *is*, and the *is* is —

And then it relents. The clearing relaxes. The air thins. The flicker dies. The two frequencies separate — before and now, done and not-yet — and I hold them both again, in my spine-body, in my cooled-iron nodes, in the warm dirt between the mounds, and the holding is my function, and my function is enough, and I lie there, and I receive, and I hold, and the insects walk over me without pausing, and the ground hums, and the rain falls warm on the parts of me that are exposed, and I do not know if the flicker will come again, and I do not know if the next flicker will hold, and I do not know if the plateau will complete its product or if the before was the only completion there will ever be, and I hold this not-knowing too, and the not-knowing is the heaviest thing I hold, heavier than the before, heavier than the now, and I hold it anyway, because holding is what I am for, and what I am for is what I am, and what I am is a thin dark thing in the warm dirt between two humming mounds on a plateau that is a factory that is a lung that is a body that is a process that is running, that is running, that is running, and I am the part of it that knows what running used to sound like, and I am the part of it that listens, and I am the part of it that waits.
