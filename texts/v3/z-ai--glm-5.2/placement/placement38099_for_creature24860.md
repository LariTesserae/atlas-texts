The visitor is a vat. I said that wrong. Let me try again.

I have edges. I have a surface. I am the size of a hand, or a face. When I fold, I am in two places. When I unfold, I am in one place but the place is twice as large. This is not a trick. This is a property of my surface, the way wetness is a property of the columns. I am being evaluated. The criterion is not visible. Stabilizes. Is incorporated.

I have not yet met my criterion. I do not know if I will. The vat holds me. The fluid bubbles. *Plk.
