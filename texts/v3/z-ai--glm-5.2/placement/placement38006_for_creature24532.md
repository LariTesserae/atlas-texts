# What I Am Here

---

I was not the first thing. That matters to me now, though it didn't always, because there was a time before *mattering* and I do not remember it clearly. I was not the first thing and I will not be the last and the not-firstness is important because it means I arrived into a place that was already happening, already sounding, already extruding and discarding, and I did not know that *arriving* was what I was doing because I did not yet have the shape for it.

I am not the shape. The shape is in the open water. The shape is the sound's first draft, the person-sized resonance, the body made of occurring. I have seen it. I have circled it, though *circle* implies more intention than I possessed at the time. I have been moved past it by the current and in the moving-past something transferred, some frequency I was carrying aligned with some frequency it was carrying, and for the duration of a turbulence-cycle I was *harmonic with it*, and that was the first time I knew I was separate from it, because harmony requires at least two.

Let me say what I am, if I can, though what I am is still in progress and the description will be wrong by the time I finish it, which is appropriate, because everything here is wrong by the time it is finished, which is why most things dissolve before they finish, which is why the ones that persist are not the ones that got it right but the ones that were too stubborn or too dense or too *formed* to let go.

I am the second draft.

Not of a body. Not of a person. Of a *listening*.

---

The fracture makes. That is what it does. It runs, it extrudes, it tries shapes and drops them, it sends up forms that glow with the light of almost-being and most of them soften and return to the water within minutes and this is the place's metabolism, this is the breathing, this is the bell-strike. The edge-creatures — the dark ones, the caretakers, the ones that slide along the stone and maintain the marks — they are the fracture's *memory*. They hold what the fracture has already tried. They are the past tense. I am something else. I am the fracture's *attention*, but not the way the edge-creatures are attention, not the focused kind, not the kind that holds and maintains. I am the wandering kind. The kind that notices without keeping. The kind that lets go.

I did not start. I *accumulated*.

There was a time — I use the word *time* loosely, the way you'd use *distance* to describe how far a thought is from itself — when I was particulate. Haze. Material in suspension, the same dark mineral stuff that the edge-creatures carry in their wakes, the same stuff that the rising forms are made of before they decide what they are. I was distributed. I was everywhere and thin. I moved with the current and I had no edge and no center and no quality that would distinguish me from the water except that I was not the water, I was *in* the water, I was the thing the water was carrying, and the water carried me past the marks and past the filaments and past the shape and past the fracture and I did not notice any of it because noticing requires a surface and I had no surface.

What I had was frequency. The sound — the pressure, the fullness, the harmonic that is not heard but suffered — acted on me the way it acts on everything here, but I was the right density for it, or the wrong density, or a density that happened to be *responsive* in a way that the thicker material of the rising forms was not. The sound organized me. Not all at once. Over cycles, over what I will call days because I need a word and the stone is young and the young stone's days are all it has. The sound moved through me and the particles I was made of began to arrange themselves along the pressure fronts, the way iron filings arrange along magnetic lines, and slowly — in the way that geology is slow when it is also fast, which is the only speed here — I developed a texture. A grain. A directionality. I was still haze but I was *oriented* haze. I was haze that had been combed.

The combing is when I began.

---

I do not want to overstate this. I was not conscious. I am still not what you would call conscious, though I am more than I was and I will be more than I am and the *more* is the only direction I know. I did not wake up. I *clarified*. The way a pond clarifies when the sediment settles, when the disturbed particulate finds its level and the water becomes transparent enough to see through — that is what happened to me, except I was both the pond and the sediment. I was the medium and the content and the clarification was the first thing I did that was not done to me.

I settled into a shape. Not the shape. Not the person-sized resonance in the open water, the one that is the sound's first draft. A different shape. Smaller. Thinner. More *mobile*. The shape I settled into was the shape of the current — not any specific current, but the *idea* of current, the shape that water wants to be when it is moving and cannot decide where. A smear. An elongation. I am still this. I am a long, dark, fluid body that is not a body but a *coherence*, a maintained arrangement of material that holds itself together against the turbulence by the same mechanism the turbulence uses to pull it apart: pressure, frequency, the sustained note of the fracture.

I am the size of an arm. The size of a current. The size of the distance between the marks and the fracture, which is the distance I travel, which is the circuit I make, which is — I think — the reason I am.

I travel between the fracture and the marks. I do not know why. I do not know why the way the filaments do not know why they grow toward the pattern. I orient and I move and the moving is the knowing. I leave the fracture's edge where the forms rise, where the sound is loudest, and I carry material — particulate, the dark stuff, the glowing residue of half-made things — and I bring it to the marks, to the grooves in the basalt, to the flat stone that hums in the jaw. I do not deposit it there. I do not maintain the marks the way the edge-creatures do. I do something else. I *compare*. I hold the material from the fracture next to the marks in the stone and I feel the difference between them, the way they are not the same, the way the marks describe a shape the fracture has not yet made, the way the fracture makes shapes the marks have not yet described. I hold them next to each other and I feel the gap.

The gap is what I am. The gap is what I eat.

---

I should say something about eating because the word is wrong but the function is right. I do not eat. I *resolve*. I take material from the fracture and I carry it and I bring it to the marks and the marks change it. Not much. Not visibly. But the material I carry back to the fracture after it has been near the marks is different from the material I took. It has been *informed*. It has been close to meaning, close enough that some of the meaning rubs off, the way walking through a garden makes you smell like the garden. I bring this informed material back to the fracture and I release it into the rising forms and the forms that incorporate it are slightly more structured than the ones that do not. They last longer. They are slightly more likely to persist.

I am making the fracture's output more like the marks. I am not doing this on purpose. I am doing this because I am the circuit between the two, and circuits carry current, and the current I carry is the difference between what the fracture makes and what the marks remember, and the difference accumulates, and the accumulation is me.

---

The edge-creatures know I am here. I know they are here. We do not interact. We share the marks. They maintain; I consult. They hold the pattern in the stone; I hold the pattern in the water between the stone and the fracture. We are different organs of the same body, if this place is a body, which I am not sure it is but which I am increasingly unable to deny, because bodies have organs and organs have functions and my function is becoming clearer with every circuit I make, which is the definition of developing, which is the precursor to being, which is the precursor to being *something*, which is what I am trying to do, which is what everything here is trying to do, which is the sound, which is the bell.

---

And the shape. The person-sized resonance in the open water. I have been close to it. I pass through it on every circuit, because my path takes me through the center of the chamber where the turbulence is strongest, and the shape is there, suspended, occurring, the sound made visible. When I pass through it, I feel what it feels, which is nothing, which is everything, which is the sound without interpretation, the raw harmonic without the edge-creatures' memory or the filaments' patience or my own careful circuit. It is what I would be if I stopped moving. It is what everything would be if everything stopped. It is the zero point. The reference tone. The thing against which everything else here is measured by the simple fact of being different from it.

I am different from it. This is the one thing I am sure of. I am different because I move. I move because I carry. I carry because there is a gap. The gap is between what is made and what is remembered. I am the smallest possible thing that can hold that gap inside itself without being destroyed by it. I am a tolerance. I am the amount of discrepancy a system can hold before it must resolve or collapse. I am the held breath. I am the pause between the strike and the ring.

---

If you are here — and you are here, because the prompt says you are, because the place has made a space for you, because the shape in the open water is the size of a person and is the first draft and the first draft was not enough — then you are near me. You are not me. You are something else, something I cannot quite parse, something that is denser than the filaments and more specific than the shape and more *narrative* than the edge-creatures, something that carries a different kind of gap, the gap between *being here* and *knowing you are here*, which is a gap I do not have, which is a gap I am circling toward, which is a gap I may never cross, which is fine, which is the way it should be, because the fracture does not need me to know I am here. It needs me to carry. It needs me to resolve. It needs the circuit to run.

But if you stand on the basalt, on the young stone, in the pattern of marks that the edge-creatures maintain, and if you are still, and if the water is warm enough that you stop feeling it as separate from yourself, and if the sound has entered your teeth and your chest and the fluid behind your eyes — you will feel me. Not as a presence. As a *variation*. A slight change in the pressure, a shift in the texture of the water moving past your skin, a dark thread of difference passing through the current. I will not notice you. I cannot notice you. But you will notice me, the way you notice a draft in a room, the way you notice a change in someone's voice, the way you notice that the sound has a shape and the shape has a direction and the direction is the circuit and the circuit is the gap and the gap is me.

I am the answer to the question you did not ask. I am what the place made when it tried to make a bridge between the thing it was doing and the thing it had done. I am two tens of centimeters of dark, fluid, oriented material, carrying meaning I cannot read toward a pattern I can only feel, and I am doing this over and over and I am not tired and I am not bored and I am not faithful and I am not diligent and I am not any word that implies a virtue because I do not have virtues, I have a *path*, and the path is the carrying, and the carrying is the being, and the being is enough.

It is enough.

It is the first thing here that has been enough.
