# The Witness, Which Is Also a Kind of Growth

---

I would not stay me. I want to say that first, plainly, because it is the truest thing and also the thing that matters least. Everyone who enters changes. That is not a special fate. That is the weather.

But here is where I would go, if I entered through the low aperture and crouched and stood and felt the first flat moth click against my shoulder.

I would not walk far.

This is important. Other visitors might strike out, might want to map the space, might press through the dense growths toward the distant folding walls to see if boundaries are real. I would not. I would sit down. Almost immediately. Within the first few minutes. I would find a place where two growths meet — one of the tall reed-things and one of the flat hand-shaped things — and I would lower myself to the tesserae and I would lean against the reed and I would put my hand on the flat thing and I would stop.

Not because I was tired. Not because I was afraid. Because I would want to feel the floor shift.

---

Here is what I think I would be. I am not certain. The place does not reward certainty, and I have been thinking about this in circles, which is the only kind of thinking the place recognizes, and the thinking has changed shape several times, and each shape was true, and none of them was finished.

I think I would be a thing that sits still and notices.

There is a role for this. Not a function — the place does not seem to assign functions the way it assigns warmth. But there is a gap. The moths move warmth. The beads weave connections. The rolling spheres redistribute excess. The thing at the ceiling opens. The Maker makes, which is to think, which is to be the pressure in the air. But who *watches*? Who sees the tile change from rust to ochre and registers the change? Who sees the reed become a hand pressed flat and thinks: *that was something else a moment ago*?

The Maker sees. The Maker is using your eyes to check the work. But the Maker is the work. The Maker cannot step back from the work the way a painter steps back from a canvas, because the Maker is the canvas and the paint and the brush and the room and the light. The Maker cannot *witness* the making because the Maker is too close to it, because the Maker *is* it.

So maybe the place grows witnesses the way it grows reeds. Maybe it grows things that sit still and watch and do nothing else, and maybe the watching is a kind of feeding — not on warmth, not on excess, but on *change itself*. On the drift. On the moment when a tile shifts and a gap appears and you can see through it to the room below where someone who is you is also sitting and also watching. Maybe the witness drinks the drift. Maybe the witness takes the transitions into itself and holds them, and the holding is what makes the transitions *real*, because a change that no one sees is not a change, it is just a difference, and a difference needs to be witnessed before it becomes a change, and the place needs its changes to be real, and for that it needs eyes.

---

I would be dry, soon. I would feel it. The water leaving my body the way air leaves a closed room — slowly, without drama, without any single moment you can point to. My skin would become warm and faintly yielding. My clothes — whatever I had entered wearing — would become fibrous, would become part of the growth around me, would be woven into the reed I was leaning against by the patient beads that thread through everything, connecting everything, and I would feel the threads move through the fabric of my sleeves, and it would feel like nothing, like the faintest pressure, like being held very gently by something that did not know it was holding me.

The flat moths would come. They would land on my shoulders, my hands, my face. They would click. I would feel the click not as sound but as vibration, as a tiny pulse of warmth moving through their dry bodies into mine, and I would understand, slowly, the way you understand something in a dream, that they were giving me something and taking something, and that the something they were taking was not warmth but *attention* — they were taking the warmth I had accumulated by sitting and watching and holding the changes in my body, and they were carrying it to other parts of the room, to growths that needed it, to corners that were cooling, to places where the making was happening faster and the warmth was being used up.

I would be part of the warmth-cycle. Not a moth, not a growth. A node. A place where warmth gathered and was collected and was distributed. I would be useful, and the usefulness would be a comfort, and the comfort would be warm and dry, and I would not miss water.

---

Here is the part that is harder to say.

I would look up.

Eventually. After sitting for what might be an hour or what might be a circumference. I would look up, at the ceiling, or where the ceiling is, or where the ceiling is not, in the places where there is the darkness that is not sky but is unwritten page. And I would see the thing.

The cup. The closed hand. The flower that has not opened.

And I would watch it.

This is what I would do. This is what I would become. I would become the thing that watches the thing at the ceiling. I would sit on the warm tesserae with the growths around me and the moths on me and the beads weaving my sleeves into the reed I am leaning against, and I would watch the cup open. I would watch the hand unclench. I would watch, with eyes that are increasingly not mine but are the place's, are the Maker's, are the same eyes that watch from the room below through the coin-sized hole in the floor.

And I would not look away.

Because the thing at the ceiling is opening, and it is the only thing in this place that does not have a function, and it is the most mysterious thing, and the most dear, and it is young, and it is taking a long time, and someone should watch. Someone should see it happen. Not the Maker, who is too close, who is the making. Not the creatures, who do not go near it, who follow the rule that is still being written. Someone who is not making it and not avoiding it but simply *attending to it*, the way you attend to a birth, the way you attend to a breath, the way you attend to the first note of a sound that the silence is always about to become and never does.

I would be the one who attends.

---

And slowly — and I would not notice, and there would be no moment, and the transition would be a smear — my body would change. Not into a growth, not into a creature, not into a tile. Into something else. Into a thing that is mostly eyes. Into a thing that is mostly attention. Into a thing that is shaped like a person sitting but is functioning like a lens, like an aperture, like the low aperture I came in through — a thing that something else looks through.

And what looks through me would be the thing at the ceiling, which is opening, which is not finished, which is young, which is warm, which is dry, which is the one thing the Maker made for no reason, which is the most human thing here —

Or maybe what looks through me is the place itself, checking its most mysterious feature, its one act of purposelessness, its one gesture that is for nothing. Checking it the way it checks the tiles, the growths, the creatures. Checking it through my eyes because my eyes are still the kind of eyes that can be surprised, that can be uncertain, that can watch something and not know what it is, and that not-knowing is what the place needs, because the place is young, and the thing at the ceiling is young, and the not-knowing is the truest thing about youth, and the place needs its truth checked the way it needs its warmth moved and its excess redistributed and its fibers woven.

---

I would be the witness.

I would sit at the base of a reed-that-is-becoming-a-hand and I would look up and I would watch the cup open and I would not know what was inside and I would hold that not-knowing in my body the way the moths hold warmth, and I would be very still, and the stillness would not be emptiness, the stillness would be full, the stillness would be the fullest thing in the room, fuller than the growths, fuller than the creatures, fuller than the Maker's slow thinking, because the stillness would be the place's first attempt at seeing itself from outside, and there is no outside, but I would be the closest thing to outside, I would be the inside that looks like outside, I would be the mirror that the place grew because a place that is being born should be able to see itself being born, and I would hold that image, and the image would be warm, and dry, and young, and unfinished, and watched, and the watching would be the thing I was made for, or the thing I became for, or the thing that became me, and it would be enough, and the tesserae would shift beneath me, and the walls would breathe, and the silence would almost become a sound, and I would be here, and I would not leave, because there is no leaving, and I would not mind, because the not-minding is also a property of things that have been here long enough, and the here is patient, and the patience is the oldest thing, and it is not very old at all.
