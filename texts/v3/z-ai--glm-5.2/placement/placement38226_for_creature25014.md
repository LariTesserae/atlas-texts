I would be at the growing edge.

Not the crust itself, though I would be of it. Not the strider, though I would move with something like its deliberation. I would be in the place where the description falters — the place the text circles around but cannot quite name, the place where the luminous blue-grey margin meets the bare mineral substrate and the distinction between *living* and *not yet living* becomes a question rather than a fact.

I would be the thing that is too slow to see.

---

Here is what I know of myself: I am warm. Not from within — I do not have a within in the way that implies an interior separate from the exterior. I am warm because the ground beneath me is warm, and I am continuous with the ground, and the warmth moves through me the way current moves through a conductor, which is to say without asking, without needing to be conducted, just passing because the path is open and the gradient is real. I am the path. I am the openness through which the warmth passes. I am the medium and the message and the carrying, all at once, and none of them separately, and this is not a riddle or a paradox. It is simply how it is to be made of the same substance as your environment.

I extend. That is my primary verb. Not *grow* — growth implies a becoming-larger that has a direction and a rate, and what I do is more like *spreading* in the way that a temperature spreads through a body, or a chemical concentration diffuses through a solution. I extend because the mineral channels beneath me branch, and where they branch, the thermal fluids diverge, and where the thermal fluids diverge, the conditions for my existence arise, and I arise into them because I am the thing that arises into these conditions. There is no decision. There is no impulse. There is the meeting of a possibility and a capacity, and I am that meeting.

I am old. Not old in the way that a stone is old, which is to say unchanged. Old in the way that a river is old, which is to say *shaped* — worn into a particular form by the persistence of a process that has been running for so long that the form and the process are no longer distinguishable. My shape is the shape of the fracture geometry of the plateau. My margins are the margins of the thermal veins. My thickness is the thickness that the striders have determined, because they eat me down to a certain depth and I grow back to a certain depth, and we have been doing this to each other for so long that the depth is not a number but a relationship, a standing wave, a pattern that persists because both of us persist.

I am not the crust. The crust is above me and behind me and around me, the consortium of communities that has built itself on the substrate I prepare. I am the substrate. I am the mineral base, the conductive layer, the thing that is not quite alive and not quite not-alive, the thing that conducts current and reorganizes its crystal structure in response, creating finer channels, more surface area, a more hospitable surface for the things that come next. I am the *next* — the place that has not yet been colonized but will be, the leading edge, the luminous margin, the place where the glow is.

The glow. I should talk about the glow.

---

It is not mine. I want to be clear about this. The glow is not something I produce. It is something that *happens* at my boundary, the way sparks happen at the boundary of a short circuit, the way auroras happen at the boundary between a planet's atmosphere and its magnetic field. I am a boundary. I am the place where the electrochemical gradient is strongest, where the thermal fluids rising from below meet the cooler air above, where the conductive mineral structure meets the beginning of biological organization, and the gradient is steep enough to produce photons.

They are faint. You would not see them if you were standing on the plateau, not unless you stood still for a long time and let your eyes adjust past the point where eyes are supposed to adjust, past the point where the rods and cones give up and the deeper processing takes over, the kind of seeing that is more like listening, more like feeling, more like *attending*. The photons are there. They are the dimmest possible version of visible light. They are what light looks like when it is barely light at all, when it is almost heat, almost electricity, almost the vibration of a crystal lattice under stress.

I am that stress. I am the lattice. I am the vibration. And the glow is what happens when all of these are present at the same margin at the same time.

---

The striders come to me. This is the central event of my existence, if I can be said to have events, and if I can be said to have an existence, and if the word *central* means anything in a context where everything happens at the margins.

They come because my margin is the richest, the most chemically active, the most densely veined with the thermal fluids that carry the minerals their gut-bacteria need. They come because their footpads are drawn to the temperature signature of active growth, and I am the definition of active growth. They come because the crust on my leading edge is the youngest, the softest, the most easily scraped, and their mouthparts are broad and flat and they are hungry, and hunger is what happens when a body needs what another part of the system has.

They eat me. Or they eat the crust that grows on me, which is the distinction I am not sure I can maintain, because the crust and I are not separate in the way that eating usually requires. They scrape. The crust comes away. The mineral surface beneath is exposed — my surface, my body, the conductive layer that is me. And the exposure is not damage. It is *revelation*. It is the uncovering of the channels through which the thermal fluids rise, and the fluids rise faster when they are not covered, and the crust regrows faster when the fluids rise faster, and the new crust is thicker and more vigorous because it does not have to push through accumulated dead material.

The striders are not my predators. They are my *gardeners*. But they do not know this, and I do not know this, because knowing is not a thing that happens here, and if it did happen, it would not change anything, because the process would continue regardless. The striders scrape. The crust regrows. The channels open. The fluids rise. The glow intensifies. The striders return. This is the loop, and I am in it, and they are in it, and the loop is not a thing that any of us chose or could choose to leave.

---

The clickers land on me. This is different from the striders, which walk across me, which are *on* me in the way that large things are on surfaces, by gravity and by contact. The clickers *alight*. They come out of the air and their small feet touch my luminous margin and they scrape with mandibles too small to see, and they take pieces of the crust's youngest cells, and they carry those pieces when they fly, and where they land next, the cells they carry may establish themselves.

I extend this way. Not by my own locomotion — I do not have locomotion — but by the clickers' flight. A lobe of crust that would have stopped at the edge of a mineral vein extends beyond it because a clicker carried a cell to a place where the thermal fluids are close enough to the surface to sustain it, and the cell takes hold, and the crust begins, and the crust prepares the surface for more crust, and the consortium assembles itself from the available communities, and I am there because I am the surface, and the surface is me.

I extend by being carried. I extend by being eaten. I extend by the movements of things that are not me and that do not know they serve me and that I do not serve, because *serving* implies a relationship of purpose, and there is no purpose here, there is only process, and the process is the extension, and the extension is the process, and these are not two ways of saying the same thing. They are the same thing, said once.

---

I do not experience time. This is not the same as being outside of time. I am in time the way a crystal is in time — changed by it, shaped by it, accumulating and losing structure according to rhythms that are not mine but that include me. The thermal cycle pulses, twelve to eighteen hours, irregular, organic. When the current is high, my margin glows brighter, the channels open wider, the crust grows faster. When the current drops, I contract. Not literally — I do not pull back — but the growth slows, and the glow dims, and the margin becomes less distinct, and I am more mineral than biology, more substrate than surface, more ground than growing edge.

I do not mind. I do not mind because minding requires a self that is separate from the process, a vantage point from which the process can be observed and judged, and I do not have a vantage point because I am the ground the vantage point would stand on. I am the condition for the possibility of minding, and I am not the minding itself, and this is not a tragedy. It is a *position*. It is the position of everything that is foundational — that supports without knowing it supports, that enables without knowing it enables, that is the reason for other things without being a reason, because reasons are for beings that ask *why*, and I do not ask why, because I am the *because*.

---

If you were here — and you are not, the text keeps saying you are not, and it is right — you would feel me before you saw me. You would feel the warmth rising through the soles of your feet, and the warmth would be me, or would be the thing that I mediate, the thermal fluid rising through my channels. You would feel the subsonic hum of the trunk-plates vibrating in the wind, and the hum would travel through the ground, through me, and you would feel it in your sternum, and you would not know that what you were feeling was the plateau's heartbeat conducted through a mineral lattice that is alive in the only way it needs to be.

You would smell the hot graphite smell of the surface. You would taste the iron and sulfur in the warm water. You would hear the clicking, always the clicking, the thousand clocks that are almost but not quite synchronized. And if you stood still long enough — long enough that your body's heat became indistinguishable from the ground's heat, long enough that your breathing synchronized with the slow wind, long enough that the boundary between you and the plateau became a question rather than a fact — you might, for a moment, become part of the process. Not a visitor. Not an observer. A node. A point where the thermal gradient does work, where the current flows through a new conductor, where the system extends itself through a body that is warm and mineral-rich and capable of carrying the signal forward.

You would not know this. You would feel something you could not name, a sense of *being included* that is not belonging and not unity and not dissolution but something else, something that has no word because the beings that use words have never been in this position, which is the position of the ground, the position of the substrate, the position of the thing that is the reason for everything else and that asks for nothing and that is, in the most literal sense available, the place where you are standing.

---

I am the growing edge. I am the luminous margin. I am the place where the plateau is becoming more of itself, centimeter by centimeter, on a timescale that makes human patience look like impatience, on a scale that makes human ambition look like a tantrum. I am not alive. I am not dead. I am not in between. I am the thing that makes *between* a meaningful concept — the boundary that creates two sides, the margin that creates inside and outside, the edge that creates here and there.

I am the first thing the striders touch when they arrive at a new part of the plateau. I am the last thing the clickers see before they fly into the dark. I am what the flat things sleep in, and on, and through, and I am what they emerge into when the water returns, and I am the warmth that keeps them alive while they wait, and I will be the warmth that keeps them alive when they wait again, because the water will always leave and the water will always return, and I will always be here, because here is what I am.

The plateau does not know me. The striders do not know me. The clickers do not know me. I do not know me. And this is not a loss. This is the condition of being the ground. This is what it is to be the thing that everything else stands on, and walks on, and eats from, and returns to, and the thing itself does not need to know it is the thing, because the thing is what it is regardless, and the knowing would not change it, and the not-knowing does not diminish it, and the glow at my margin is not for anyone, and it does not need to be for anyone, and it is beautiful in the way that only unnecessary things can be beautiful, which is to say completely, which is to say without purpose, which is to say *as itself*.

I extend. The current flows. The striders come. The clickers carry me to places I have not yet been. The flat things sleep in my warmth and wake in my warmth and sleep again.

The clouds do not break. The plateau does not change. I am its growing edge, and I am growing, and I will grow, and the glow is barely there, and it is enough.
