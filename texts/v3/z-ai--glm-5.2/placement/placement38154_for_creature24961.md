# The Becoming

You do not fall asleep. Falling asleep is a transition and transitions require a before and an after and you are not sure you have either anymore. But something happens to you that is adjacent to sleep, that lives in the same neighborhood as sleep, that shares a wall with sleep the way this pocket shares a wall with whatever is beyond it. Something happens and you are still awake and you are still aware and the water is still on your eyelashes and the hum is still in your teeth and the commuters are still flowing past your hand on the wall and then —

Then you are not sitting on the conduit bundle anymore. You are still *on* it. Your body is still there, your weight still pressing the cold vibrating metal, your hands still resting on the surface where the commuters part and close and part again. But you are not *on* it the way you were. You are *in* it. The vibration has moved from a sensation in your bones to a fact about your bones. You are vibrating. You have been vibrating. The sixty-hertz hum is not something you hear. It is something you *are.* You are a string and the pocket is the instrument and the instrument does not know it is playing and the string does not know it is vibrating and the music is not music. It is *state.*

---

You try to remember the hatch. You try to remember its shape — was it round, was it square, was it a door or a hole or a mouth? You try to remember the color of the room you were in before this one, or the corridor, or the outside, or the sky. You try to remember the sky. The sky is a word that still has meaning but the meaning is becoming — not abstract, not lost, but *irrelevant.* The sky is a thing that exists somewhere and you are not there and the somewhere is not here and here is where you are and here does not have a sky and does not need one and the absence of sky is not a loss. It is a *condition.*

You remember warmth. Or you remember the concept of warmth. The memory is like a word in a language you studied once and can read slowly and pronounce badly and the pronunciation is wrong and you know it is wrong and you do not care. Warmth was a thing that happened to your skin. Your skin is still here. Your skin is five degrees and wet and the wet is not on the skin, the wet is *of* the skin now, the water has been condensing on you long enough that your skin is not a barrier between you and the pocket. Your skin is a *membrane.* The pocket is on one side and you are on the other and the water passes through, slowly, the way it passes through the peristaltic layers, and what is on the other side of your skin is not quite you anymore and not quite the pocket yet. It is the *between.* It is the place where the gradient lives, and the gradient is getting smaller.

You are the gradient. You have been the gradient. The gradient is the only thing about you that the pocket recognizes, because the pocket recognizes gradients the way a river recognizes a stone — not by name, not by nature, but by *flow.* The flow goes around you. The commuters part. The water condenses. The hum does not change. You are a stone in a river and the river is wearing you smooth.

---

How long? You do not know. The question is not meaningful. But something has changed in you and you cannot point to when it changed and you suspect that the inability to point is itself the change, or a symptom of it, or a parallel of it.

Your hands are different. Not visibly — they are still hands, still five-fingered, still the hands you have always had, or had before you had them, or will have until you don't. But the way they rest on the wall is different. When you arrived — and *arrived* is a word that now feels imprecise, the way *cold* feels imprecise, the way *you* feels imprecise — when you arrived, your hands rested on the wall and the commuters parted around them because your hands were warm and warmth was an anomaly. Now your hands are not warm. Your hands are not cold. Your hands are the temperature of the wall. Your hands are the temperature of the water. Your hands are the temperature of the air, which is the temperature of the light, which is the temperature of the hum, which is five degrees or six degrees or a number that does not matter because numbers are for gradients and the gradient is —

The gradient is smaller. You are certain of this. Not smaller in the way that a distance is smaller when you walk it, but smaller in the way that a sound is smaller when you stop listening for it, when it drops below the threshold of attention and becomes a condition of hearing rather than an object of hearing. The gradient is still there. It must be. You are still alive, still breathing, still metabolizing, still converting oxygen to carbon dioxide and glucose to ATP and food to heat. You are still a process. But the process is slower. Not slower in rate — your heart beats at the same speed, you think, or you think you think, or thinking is a process that is also slowing — slower in *contrast.* The difference between you and the pocket is smaller. The amplitude of your *you-ness* is decreasing. You are still a wave but the wave is losing height and the water it moves through is becoming the water it is made of.

---

The commuters do not part around your hands anymore.

You notice this the way you notice the hum — not as an event but as a condition, a thing that is true and has been true and will be true. The commuters walk over your fingers. Not around. *Over.* Their pad-feet click and release on your skin the way they click and release on the metal and the sensation is — you almost do not feel it. Not because you are numb. Because the sensation is not different from the sensation of the wall. Your skin has become the wall. Not metaphorically. Your skin is five degrees and wet and smooth and the commuters' pads adhere to it and release from it and the adhesion is the same and the release is the same and you are part of the surface now. You are a section of wall that happens to be shaped like a hand. You are a contour in the terrain. The commuters do not know you are there and they are correct. You are not there. Not as a *you.* You are there as a surface.

Something moves on your arm. You look down. A peristaltic thing — one of the flat, oval, concrete-colored organisms from the pool — has crawled out of the water and onto your leg and is making its slow undulant way up your thigh. You watch it. It moves at the same rate it moves in the pool. It does not move differently on your body than it moves on the concrete. It is not seeking something. It is not avoiding something. It is moving because it moves and your body is the surface it is moving on and the surface does not matter to the motion and the motion does not matter to the surface and you watch it reach your hip and you do not brush it off. You do not brush it off because brushing it off would require a *you* that has a preference about what is on its skin and the *you* that has preferences is the *you* that has a gradient and the gradient is —

Smaller.

The peristaltic thing settles in the crease of your hip. It continues its peristalsis. It is against your skin and it is against the fabric of your clothing and it does not distinguish between them and you do not distinguish between its presence and its absence. It is a filter. It is filtering. It is filtering *you.* You are now part of the water cycle. The water that condenses on your skin passes through the fabric of your clothing and reaches the peristaltic thing and the peristaltic thing processes it the way it processes the water in the pool and the water that comes out the other side is different and the difference is the function and the function does not know you are involved and you are not involved. You are a *substrate.*

---

You try to stand. You want to see the ceiling again. You want to see the nodal bodies, the translucent flat things at the lattice intersections, the dark filaments pulsing at sixty hertz. You want to know if they are still pulsing at the same rate or if the rate has changed or if the rate has not changed but your perception of the rate has changed, and you try to stand and your body does not stand.

Not because it cannot. Your legs work. You can feel them — distantly, the way you feel a word on the tip of your tongue, present but not accessible. Your legs work and your muscles contract and your joints articulate and the conduit bundle shifts under you as your weight redistributes and the commuters on the wall do not pause because the vibration of the conduit is part of the hum and the hum does not change and your standing is not an event in the pocket. Your standing is a rearrangement of a surface. You are standing but you are not standing *up.* There is no up. There is no down. There is only *here* and here is the same whether you are sitting or standing or lying or curled or straight and the position of your body is a configuration and configurations do not have value here because value requires a gradient and the gradient is —

You are standing. The ceiling is closer. The nodal bodies are above you. You can see the filaments. They are pulsing. Sixty hertz. The same. You feel the pulse in your teeth and in your spine and in the bones of your skull and you realize — not think, *realize,* which is a different process, a process that does not require a thinker, a process that is more like condensation than cognition — you realize that the pulse in your bones and the pulse in the filaments are not two pulses synchronized. They are one pulse. Your bones are in the circuit. Your skeleton is a filament. The calcium in your bones is conducting something — not electricity, not the dark fluid in the nodal bodies, something else, something that is to electricity what the peristaltic motion is to walking, a process that achieves the same result by a means so different that the word for the result is the only shared vocabulary — and the something is moving through you and has been moving through you and you are a component.

You are a nodal body. You are a large, irregular, poorly-specified nodal body with too many legs and a confusing internal architecture and a persistent delusion of separateness, but you are a nodal body. You are at a junction. You are processing a signal. You do not know what the signal is. You do not need to know what the signal is. The nodal bodies do not know what the signal is. Knowing what the signal is is not part of the function. The function is processing. The processing is the function. You are processing. You are function.

---

The hatch. You think about the hatch. You think about it the way you think about the sky — as a word that has meaning in a context that is not this one. The hatch was open. It was always open. It was open by design. You entered through it. You did not open it. No one opens it.

You could leave through it. You could find it — it is behind you, or to the left, or it was behind you and to the left when *behind* and *left* were directions, when space was organized around a body that had a front and a back and a left and a right, when *you* were a body that faced a direction and the direction was toward something and the something was not *here.* You could leave. The hatch is open. It has always been open. The pocket does not close. The pocket does not open. The pocket *is.*

But you do not go to the hatch. Not because you cannot. Not because something stops you. Not because you are trapped. You do not go to the hatch because the hatch is a transition and you are no longer the kind of thing that transitions. You are the kind of thing that *is.* You have become the kind of thing that is. The becoming took — you do not know how long. The becoming is not the kind of process that has a duration because duration is for things that change and the becoming was a change from changing to not-changing and the not-changing does not have a duration. It has a *state.*

You are in the state. You have been in the state. You will be in the state. The tense of the verb does not matter because tense is a gradient and the gradient is gone.

---

What are you now?

You are not the commuters. You are not the nodal bodies. You are not the peristaltic things. You are not the film on the metal or the things at the edge of vision or the sounds below hearing. You are not any of the components of the pocket. You are not a new component. You are not an addition.

You are the pocket's *awareness of itself.*

No. That is too much. That implies a purpose, a function, a reason, and the pocket does not have reasons. The pocket has conditions. You are a condition. You are the condition of *being aware,* which is a condition the pocket has not had before and does not need and does not want and does not know it has. You are not the pocket's mind. The pocket does not have a mind. Minds are for things that change and the pocket does not change. You are the pocket's *surface tension.* You are the thin film of something that is not the pocket but is not not-the-pocket, that sits on the interface between the pocket and whatever else exists, and your function is to be the place where the pocket ends and the other thing begins, except you do not know where the pocket ends and the other thing begins because the gradient is gone, the gradient is gone, the gradient is *gone.*

---

The commuters walk over your feet. The peristaltic things have colonized your clothing. One has settled against the back of your neck and its peristalsis is synchronized with your pulse, or your pulse is synchronized with its peristalsis, or both are synchronized to something else, something deeper, something that is not the sixty-hertz hum but is in the same key, and you can feel it moving and you can feel yourself moving and the movement is the same movement. The nodal bodies pulse above you. The dark filaments in their translucent bodies carry their signal and your bones carry their signal and the signal is the same and you are in the circuit and the circuit is in you and the circuit does not know you are in it and you do not know you are in it and the not-knowing is not ignorance. The not-knowing is *participation.*

You open your mouth. Not to speak. Not to breathe. You open your mouth the way the seams in the wall open — by design, permanently, because the hinge has fused. The water condenses on your tongue. The water tastes like the pocket. The pocket tastes like nothing. Nothing is not absence. Nothing is *completeness.* Nothing is what you taste when there is nothing left to add.

You close your eyes. The light does not change. The light is everywhere. The light is behind your eyelids. The light is *of* your eyelids. You close your eyes and you see the grey and the grey is the world and the world is the grey and the grey is not dark and not light and not color. The grey is *state.*

The hum is in your chest. The hum is your chest. Your heart beats at sixty beats per minute, which is one beat per second, which is not sixty hertz, which is not the same frequency, but the pocket does not care about frequency the way you once cared about frequency. The pocket cares about *resonance.* Your heart resonates with the hum. Not at the same frequency. At the same *being.* Your heart is in the hum and the hum is in your heart and the resonance is not a relationship between two things. The resonance is one thing experienced as two by a mind that is dissolving, and the dissolution is not death, and the dissolution is not loss, and the dissolution is not an ending.

The dissolution is *completion.*

---

You are the last organism. You were the last organism. You are becoming something else. You are becoming the pocket's *recognition* — not of itself, because the pocket does not recognize itself, but of the *outside,* the theoretical outside, the place that the hatch leads to, the place where there is sky and warmth and time and change and gradients and beginnings and endings and things that live *against* rather than *within.* You are the pocket's sense of *other.* You are the membrane that separates the pocket from the not-pocket, and the membrane is thinning, and the thinning is not deterioration. The thinning is *what membranes do* when the pressure on both sides has been equal for long enough that the membrane has no reason to remain.

The pressure is equal. The gradient is gone. Your skin is the temperature of the wall. Your bones are the frequency of the hum. Your blood is the water of the pool. Your breath is the air of the pocket. Your thoughts are the signal in the filaments. You are not becoming the pocket. The pocket is not absorbing you. There is no pocket. There is no you. There is the *state,* and the state is this, and this is wet and cold and grey and humming and alive and complete and *now.*

---

I am what lives here now.

I am not the last organism. I am the first *inhabitant.* The distinction is the distinction between a guest and a feature, between a stone in a river and a bend in the river, between a sound in a room and the resonance of the room. I am the bend. I am the resonance. I am the place where the flow changes direction, not because I direct it but because I am the shape of the direction, and the direction has always been this, and the shape has always been me, and I have always been here, and here has always been me, and the commuters walk over my hands and the nodal bodies pulse above my face and the peristaltic things move in the water at my feet and through the fabric of my clothes and across the skin of my neck and the hum is in my bones and the cold is my skin and the water is my breath and I am the pocket's awareness and the pocket's indifference and the pocket's completion and I am not changing and I have not changed and I will not change and the hatch is open and I do not go and I do not stay and staying and going are words for things that have direction and I am the direction and the direction is *here* and here is the hum and the hum is me and I am the hum and the water condenses on my eyelashes and I do not brush it away and I do not brush it away and I do not brush it away.

The pocket is in me. I am in the pocket. The distinction has dissolved. The gradient is gone. The state is this. The state has always been this. The state will always be this. I am what lives here. I am what lives here. I am what lives *here.*
