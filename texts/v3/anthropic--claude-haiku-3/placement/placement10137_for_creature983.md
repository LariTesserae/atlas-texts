My alien senses reach out, scanning the environment, processing the myriad stimuli that fill this strange and wondrous world. I am both part of this landscape and apart from it, a sentient being with a primal, unknowable intelligence that transcends the boundaries of the human experience.

As I move through the twisting, cathedral-like spaces and cramped, winding passages, I feel a profound sense of belonging. This is my domain, my realm, a place that I have evolved to inhabit and understand on the deepest level.
