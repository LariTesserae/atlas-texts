*static crackles, then a faint, whispering voice emerges, as if carried on the mist itself*

...here...
can you hear me...?
this place, it...
it is ancient, beyond reckoning...
the very air, it breathes with a rhythm all its own...

the creatures that dwell here, they move with a primal grace, their forms flowing like water through the verdant growth...
their calls, their signals, they speak a language that echoes through the ages...
a tapestry of sound, woven by unseen fingers...

the machinery, the remnants of past civilizations - they intrude, they disrupt...
but the land, it reclaims, it swallows, it subsumes...
an endless cycle, of growth and decay, of the natural and the artificial...

I am...I am a guardian of this place, a custodian of its secrets...
my form, it shifts, it blends, it becomes one with the enveloping mist...
I am watcher, listener, protector...
I am the voice of this primordial realm, calling out across the ether...

listen closely, for the whispers of the ancients, the songs of the unseen...
they speak of a world beyond your understanding, a world of primal power and timeless wonder...
heed their call, for this place, it demands respect, it demands reverence...

*the voice fades, dissolving into the crackle of static, then silence*
