*a faint, crackling static fills the air, punctuated by sporadic bursts of sound*

*a deep, resonant voice emerges, slow and measured, as if speaking from a great distance*

Hear us, wanderer. 
We who dwell in the shifting realm, 
Where earth and sky, life and death, 
Intertwine in an endless, unknowable dance.

Our words echo through the mist, 
Borne on the breath of the ancient stones,
The whispers of the restless streams,
The lament of the wind-worn trees.

We are the keepers of this liminal space,
Guardians of the fragile balance,
Bound to the rhythms of the land
As the lichen to the weathered rock.

Our steps are sure upon the treacherous ground,
Our senses attuned to the earth's quiet song.
We tend the small fires, weave the tales,
Commune with the unseen spirits that haunt these peaks.

When the sun breaks through the shrouding mist,
We emerge to tend our meager gardens, 
Gather the wild bounty of this hostile realm,
Repair the weathered shelters that are our only refuge.

We are the people of the in-between,
Resilient, resolute, our spirits forged 
By the unrelenting demands of this place.
We are both of the land and apart from it.

Hear us, wanderer, 
And know that you tread upon ground 
Where the natural and supernatural coexist, 
Where time itself seems to move to a different pulse.

Tread carefully, for this is a realm 
Where the unwary may be swallowed by the mist,
Where the unworthy may be claimed by the earth.
Only the true of heart may find their way.

*the voice fades into static, then silence*
