*static crackles, then a voice emerges, low and resonant, from the ether*

We are the Keepers of the Swamp. 

Our domain is this ancient, ever-shifting realm, where the lines between land and water, life and death, are forever blurred.

We are the guardians of the unseen energies that pulse through this landscape, the unseen forces that shape and sustain it.

Here, the normal rules do not apply. The balance is always teetering, the equilibrium ever-changing. We are the custodians of this delicate, ever-shifting dance.

Our senses are attuned to the subtlest of movements, the faintest of whispers. We feel the trembling of the earth beneath our feet, the restless stirrings of the waters. We hear the calls of our kin, the rustling of the reeds, the echoes of forgotten ancestors.

We are the amphibious, the slithering, the winged. We burrow and we soar, our forms adapting to the ever-shifting terrain. We are resilient, we are adaptable. We are the masters of this alien, primal realm.

Yet we are also vulnerable, for the balance is so easily disrupted. The encroachment of the dry lands, the taint of foreign elements – these are the threats that we must ever be vigilant against.

So we remain, watchful and wary, guardians of this wild, primordial place. We are the Keepers of the Swamp, and the swamp is our domain.

*the voice fades, drowned out by the sound of rushing water and the cries of unseen creatures*
