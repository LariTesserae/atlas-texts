We are patient, resilient. We have watched empires rise and fall, civilizations crumble to dust. And through it all, we endure. For we are not mere plants, not simple beasts. We are the custodians of this fragile world, guiding it through cycles of growth and decay.

When the last of your kind has gone, when the steel and stone lie broken and reclaimed, we will still be here. Thriving, adapting, evolving. This is our domain, our kingdom. And we will protect it, nurture it, until the end of days.

So heed our warning, wanderer. Tread lightly through our domain, for we are ever-watchful. Respect the delicate balance we have forged, lest you upset the scales and face the consequences.

We are the caretakers. We are the keepers. And this world, this verdant, decaying realm - it is ours.
