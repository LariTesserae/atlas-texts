...drip...drip...drip...

The land is ancient, worn smooth by endless cycles of rain. Mosses cling to the rock, spongy and verdant. Ferns unfurl, their delicate fronds reaching towards the mist-shrouded light.

We are the keepers of this place. Salamanders, frogs - we move through the pools and crevices, our skin glistening. Rodents scurry below, birds soar above. All in harmony, all in balance.

*the voice grows fainter, as if drifting away*

Time holds no sway here. We are eternal, unchanging. The drip...drip...drip...of water, the rustle of leaves - these are the only sounds that break the silence.
