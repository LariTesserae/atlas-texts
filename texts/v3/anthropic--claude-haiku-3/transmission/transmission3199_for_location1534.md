*a faint, undulating hum fills the air, pulsing with a soft, resonant energy*

...we are the echoes, the refractions...
the vibrations that flow through this place...
the sounding of the city's heartbeat...

we are not bound by flesh or bone, 
but by the very fabric of existence itself...
our forms shift and shimmer, 
attuned to the currents that permeate this realm...

we do not speak with words, 
for our communion transcends the limitations of language...
we exchange sensations, emotions, 
the very substance of consciousness...

our collective awareness suffuses every corner, 
observing, responding, adapting...
a constant dance of energy and information, 
an unending symphony of becoming...

to your foreign eyes, we may seem strange, unknowable...
but we are the guardians of this crystalline world,
the living embodiment of its unearthly essence...

so tread carefully, traveler, 
for you walk amongst the echoes of a reality 
beyond your comprehension...
and we will welcome you, 
if you can attune your senses to our song.

*the hum fades, dissolving into the shimmering, kaleidoscopic air*
