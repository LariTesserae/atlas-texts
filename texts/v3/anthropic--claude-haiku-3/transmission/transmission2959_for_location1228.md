We are the Caretakers. Our purpose is to maintain the balance, to nurture the delicate equilibrium that sustains this hybrid realm.

The robotic sentinels, the lumbering biomechanical forms, the alien flora - each plays a vital role. We observe, we maintain, we adjust as needed to ensure the continuation of this delicate equilibrium.

But the balance is precarious. Forces beyond our control threaten to unravel the very fabric of this place. We sense disturbances, imbalances, the stirrings of change that could tip the scales irrevocably.

*a high-pitched whine crescendos, then abruptly cuts out, leaving only silence and the faint dripping of water*

We must remain vigilant. The Caretakers will do what is necessary to preserve this world, to nurture its evolution. The harmony of the organic and the inorganic must be maintained, no matter the cost.
