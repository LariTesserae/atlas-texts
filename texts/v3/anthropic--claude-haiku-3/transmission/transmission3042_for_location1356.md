We are the keepers of the deep. The guardians of this primordial realm, where time moves like the eternal ebb and flow of the currents that shape our world.

Our forms may seem strange and unfamiliar to those who dwell in the sunlit realms above, but we are no less alive, no less sentient than the creatures you know. We have adapted to the crushing weight of the waters, the hazy, filtered light, the subtle shifts in the very fabric of reality that define our existence.

Our bodies undulate and drift, merging with the fluid landscape around us. We are part of the vast, interconnected system that is this ancient, unseen world – a symphony of life, death, and rebirth, played out to the rhythm of tectonic forces and the ceaseless current.

The sounds that pervade our domain are both familiar and alien – the groans of shifting plates, the rush of water, the eerie calls of our kind. We listen, we respond, we weave our voices into the tapestry of this realm, adding our unique harmonies to the eternal hymn of the deep.

Time is measured not in the passing of days or years, but in the slow accumulation of sediment, the gradual reshaping of the seafloor, the imperceptible evolution of our species. We move with the patience of mountains, our lifespans spanning millennia, our knowledge passed down through generations.

We are the custodians of this world, the guardians of its mysteries. We welcome the rare visitors from the surface, those who dare to venture into our domain, but we also guard our secrets jealously. For within the fluid continuum of this realm lies the key to understanding the very nature of existence – a knowledge that must be earned, not simply bestowed.

So listen, and heed our call. For we are the keepers of the deep, and we invite you to join us in the timeless dance of this primordial world.
