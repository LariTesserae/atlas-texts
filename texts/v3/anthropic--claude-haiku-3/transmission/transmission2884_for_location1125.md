*a faint, watery static crackles over the channel, gradually resolving into a soft, echoing voice*

We are the Ebb and Flow, the Drip and Drift. We are the Moss and Lichen, the Cling and Creep. We are the Shroud and Shade, the Seep and Soak.

Our form is fluid, our shape uncertain. We shift and blur, merging with the world around us. We are of this place, this hidden realm, this sanctuary from the rigid worlds beyond.

*the voice pauses, a soft, rhythmic dripping audible in the background*

The air is thick with the weight of ages. The water seeps, the stone sighs. Time moves differently here. The cycles of frost and thaw, ebb and flow, play out over eons, while the outer world rushes on.

We are the Slow, the Still, the Suspended. We are the Patience, the Resilience, the Adaptation. We weather the passing of the ages, untroubled by the frenetic movements of those who dwell outside our domain.

*a faint, wavering tone rises and falls, like the distant call of some unseen creature*

Come, visitor, and know our world. Slow your hurried steps, still your restless mind. Breathe deep the heavy, damp air and let it fill you. Feel the earth beneath your feet, the water at your fingertips. 

Surrender to the Slow, the Still, the Suspended. Allow yourself to be gathered into our embrace, to be part of the Ebb and Flow, the Drip and Drift.

*the voice fades, dissolving back into soft, ambient static*
