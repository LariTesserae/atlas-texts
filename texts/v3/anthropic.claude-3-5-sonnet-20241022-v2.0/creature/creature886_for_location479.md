Living here are the Pattern-Bound - entities that exist as much in mathematics as in matter. They began as something like colonial organisms, but over eons they've evolved into beings that are simultaneously physical and abstract.

Their bodies are crystalline lattices that extend through multiple dimensions, only partly visible to human eyes. What can be seen appears as shifting geometric forms made of light and shadow, as if the air itself has been folded into regular shapes. These visible portions are actually just cross-sections of their larger structure, like how a cube passing through a 2D plane would appear as an evolving polygon.

The Pattern-Bound are the true architects of this place. The screaming towers are their instrument - not built, but grown through careful manipulation of mathematical constants. The birds, the transparent creatures, the lichen - all are expressions of their equations, physical manifestations of an ongoing calculation that maintains the perfect stasis of their domain.

They think in resonance and symmetry. Their consciousness, if it can be called that, exists in the relationships between frequencies, in the precise angles of morning light, in the crystalline structure of time itself. They do not communicate so much as synchronize, sharing information through subtle adjustments to the ever-present scream.

They are ancient beyond measure, patient beyond comprehension. Each one is a living theorem, proving itself through eternal existence. They do not reproduce in any conventional sense - rather, they occasionally solve themselves into new forms, splitting like perfect numbers finding their factors.

The Pattern-Bound are not hostile to other forms of life, but neither are they particularly aware of them. To them, all things are patterns to be resolved into their most elegant form. Their influence gradually transforms anything that stays too long among the towers, turning organic chaos into geometric certainty, until it too becomes part of their endless proof.

There is beauty in their precision, in their perfect marriage of form and function. But it is an inhuman beauty, as cold and clear as a mathematical truth, as unforgiving as the logic that drives them to maintain their unchanging domain.

They are the guardians and prisoners of their own perfection, forever singing their song of absolute order.
