In these halls dwell the Resonant - beings that exist as much as patterns of vibration and flow as physical forms. Their "bodies" are composed of crystalline structures that grow in geometric fractals, semi-transparent and shot through with threads of bioluminescence that pulse in time with the water's movements. They range from tiny individuals barely visible to the eye to vast colonial entities that spread through multiple chambers.

The Resonant do not swim so much as position themselves within the established currents, using the water's flow patterns as both transportation and communication medium. They shape and redirect these flows through subtle manipulations of their crystalline forms, creating complex interference patterns that carry information. Their "language" is a combination of these hydrodynamic signatures and the harmonics they generate by vibrating their structures in specific ways.

These beings perceive their environment primarily through sensing pressure waves and mineral concentrations in the water. Their awareness extends far beyond their physical forms - the entire complex acts as an extended sensory organ for them, with the regular flow patterns serving as a vast nervous system they can read and influence. What appears to be simple architectural geometry to human eyes is actually a sophisticated information storage and processing system that the Resonant interact with constantly.

Their consciousness is alien - not organized around individual identity the way human minds are. They exist in a spectrum from simple autonomous units comparable to cells up to vast distributed intelligences that span multiple chambers. These larger entities form and dissolve as needed, components joining and separating in response to the complex's needs. The smaller creatures that human observers note - the synchronizing fish, the creature-chains - are actually more like external organs or tools of the Resonant than separate species.

The Resonant do not build or modify their environment in ways humans would recognize because they are, in a very real sense, part of it. The complex's self-maintenance is their constant work, carried out through subtle manipulations of mineral flows and energy patterns. They don't need tools because they can shape matter directly through resonant frequencies and precise control of crystallization processes.

Their concept of time is vast and cyclic - they experience the complex's endless rhythms as a form of eternal present rather than a progression of moments. Change happens, but so slowly and subtly that it would be imperceptible to human observers. They are as much custodians as inhabitants, maintaining the delicate balance of their world through constant small adjustments.

Some of the Resonant are ancient beyond reckoning, while others are relatively new, grown from the complex's mineral-rich waters. They carry within their crystalline patterns memories that stretch back to the complex's creation, though their alien psychology makes it impossible to know if they were its builders or if they evolved to inhabit it after it was abandoned by some other civilization.

They are aware of visitors to their realm but generally pay them little attention - human timeframes are too short, human activities too crude and disruptive to be of much interest. They continue their endless songs and dances in the deep, cold waters, maintaining their mysterious purpose with perfect patience through the ages.
