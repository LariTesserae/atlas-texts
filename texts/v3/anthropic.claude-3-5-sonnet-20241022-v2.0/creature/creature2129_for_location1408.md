In this eternal twilight realm of steam and machinery, the Vapor Children make their home. They are neither fully solid nor purely gaseous, their forms shifting between states like water in this forever-afternoon world. Their bodies appear as translucent humanoid shapes when still, but trail into wisps and currents when they move, catching the perpetual orange light in ways that make them seem to glow from within.

The Vapor Children were not born but gradually condensed from the district's abundant moisture and residual heat, gaining consciousness somewhere between the rising steam and falling mist. They range in size from tiny wisps no larger than sparrows to adult-sized forms that drift along the walkways, often mistaken for particularly dense patches of steam by the human workers.

They have a peculiar relationship with the local birds, especially the pigeons and starlings. The birds can perceive them clearly and treat them as part of the natural order of things, often perching on the more solid parts of their forms or flying through their misty extremities. The Vapor Children, in turn, help maintain the birds' nests, their gentle moisture keeping the materials pliable and comfortable.

These beings are intimately connected to the district's rhythms. They pulse with the thrum of the turbines, expand and contract with the release of steam, and flow through the network of pipes and tunnels as easily as the water itself. They can read the patterns of rust and mineral deposits like a language, understanding the history and health of their mechanical home through these chromatic chronicles.

The Vapor Children are caretakers of sorts, though not in any official capacity. They tend to the small ferns and moss, sharing moisture with them through a kind of osmotic sympathy. They smooth the flow of water through cracks, guide it away from sensitive equipment, and help prevent flooding in the deeper sections of the district. They have a particular fondness for the workers' well-worn paths, often gathering there to absorb the accumulated memories of countless footsteps.

At night, they become more visible, their forms catching and refracting the sodium lights in complex patterns. They perform slow, cyclic dances with the rising steam, creating momentary sculptures in the air that dissolve as quickly as they form. Their movements echo the eternal processes of the power station - heating, cooling, condensation, evaporation - a constant cycle of transformation that mirrors their own fluid nature.

They have no leader or hierarchy, operating instead like weather patterns, responding to the changing pressures and temperatures of their environment. They communicate through ripples and eddies in the ever-present moisture, sharing information about the state of their domain through subtle changes in humidity and air pressure.

The Vapor Children are ageless, neither young nor old, existing in a continuous present that matches the district's timeless atmosphere. They are both product and process of this place, as essential to its operation as the steam and electricity that flow through its veins, though few beyond the birds would ever know it.
