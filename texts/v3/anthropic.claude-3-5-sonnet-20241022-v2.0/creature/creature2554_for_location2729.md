Here live the Resonant Folk - a people shaped by generations of dwelling in perpetual song. Their bodies and culture have evolved in profound harmony with their sonic environment.

Physically, they are slight but sturdy, with enlarged ear structures adapted to process and filter the constant frequencies. Their inner ear bones have unusual density and articulation, allowing them to parse the subtle harmonic languages of their home. Their skin is pale but faintly translucent, like cave fish, with a network of sensitive nerve endings that can detect vibration patterns. Many have developed a subtle swaying gait that moves with, rather than against, the dominant rhythms of their environment.

Their social structure revolves around acoustic lineages - families who maintain and tune specific regions of the vessel network, passing down jealously guarded knowledge of resonant frequencies and harmonic relationships. Children are born into their acoustic inheritance, their earliest memories shaped by the specific tones of their family's territory. They learn their craft through touch and body memory, developing an intuitive understanding of vibration that transcends conscious thought.

The acoustic priests form a separate caste, marked by distinctive modifications - ritual scarification that changes the resonance of their skull cavities, and ossification patterns in their bones that develop through long exposure to the deepest frequencies. They enter their calling young, and over decades their bodies become living instruments, attuned to harmonics that others cannot perceive.

The general population practices various crafts beyond ceramics - they weave sound-dampening textiles from cave-grown fibers, create specialized tools for vessel maintenance, and cultivate the strange fungi and plants that thrive in the sonic environment. Their sign language has evolved into an intricate art form, incorporating elements of dance and gesture that complement the ever-present music of the vessels.

They mark time not by sun or moon, but by subtle shifts in the resonant patterns - daily cycles caused by pressure changes, seasonal variations in the flow of underground waters, and longer cycles that may correspond to deeper geological rhythms. Their histories are encoded in rhythm and harmony rather than written words, passed down through precisely calibrated vessels that sing the stories of their making.

Some are born with rare sensitivities - the ability to "taste" particular frequencies, or see sound-induced patterns in the air. These individuals often become innovators in vessel design or explorers of the deeper chambers. There are whispered stories of those who became too attuned, who began to perceive layers of reality in the harmonics that gradually drew them away from ordinary existence.

The Resonant Folk move through life in a state of constant awareness of their sonic environment, their every action considered for its acoustic impact. They dream in frequencies, and it's said that even when they venture to the surface (which few do), they carry the song of the vessels in their bones.

In the deepest parts of their city dwell the most changed among them - those who have evolved or transformed to exist in the spaces where sound becomes substance. These beings may no longer be entirely human, their forms blurred by generations of exposure to the fundamental frequencies of the earth itself. They are rarely seen, but their influence is felt in the harmonic patterns that rise up from below, shaping the music of all the chambers above.
