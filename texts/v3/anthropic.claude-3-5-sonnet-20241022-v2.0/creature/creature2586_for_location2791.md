The Lithokine (as they call themselves in the slow percussion of their wall-speech) are beings of living crystal and translucent tissue, their internal structures visible as complex geometric patterns that shift with their movements. They stand roughly the height of a human but with much more elongated proportions - their limbs, of which they have six, spiral out from a central core in a way that mimics the architecture they tend. These limbs can both telescope and spiral, allowing them to navigate their city's complex geometries with fluid grace.

Their bodies contain networks of specialized organs that process and store the dense, nutrient-rich water they inhabit. These organs glow with subtle bioluminescence, creating internal light shows that serve as a secondary form of communication among their kind. The patterns of their thoughts are literally visible within them, shifting colors and intensities that ripple through their crystalline flesh.

The Lithokine exist in a state of semi-symbiosis with various other species that share their city. Most numerous are the Filter-Kin, colonies of delicate, tentacled creatures that process nutrients from the water and share them with their hosts through a complex chemical exchange. The Lithokine in turn protect and cultivate these creatures, carefully positioning them throughout the city's structures to maximize their effectiveness.

Their consciousness operates on multiple time scales simultaneously. While they can respond to immediate stimuli when necessary, their primary mode of thought unfolds over weeks or months. A single conversation between two Lithokine might span a season, with each response carefully considered and refined before being transmitted through their percussion language. They experience time as a thick medium to be shaped rather than a linear flow to be followed.

Young Lithokine begin as small, seed-like crystals that gradually accumulate organic matter, growing over decades into their adult forms. During this time, they are tended by the entire community, positioned in special nursery chambers where the currents and mineral content of the water are carefully maintained. It takes nearly a century for a Lithokine to reach full maturity, but they can live for millennia if undisturbed.

They have a profound connection to their environment that goes beyond mere adaptation. The crystalline forests they cultivate in the upper reaches of their city are actually extensions of their own consciousness - vast, shared neural networks that allow them to process information and store memories on a community level. Through these structures, individual Lithokine can access the accumulated knowledge of their entire civilization, though interpreting this knowledge requires years of practice.

Their technology, if it can be called that, focuses on the manipulation of growth patterns in both organic and crystalline materials. They don't build so much as cultivate, guiding the development of their city through subtle adjustments that play out over generations. Their tools are living things, bred and grown for specific purposes, often incorporating elements of their own crystalline biology.

The Lithokine have no concept of waste or destruction - everything in their society is part of a continuous cycle of transformation. When a Lithokine reaches the end of their extremely long life, their crystalline components are carefully integrated into the city's structure, while their organic elements return to the nutrient cycle that sustains their world. In this way, each generation literally builds upon the physical legacy of those who came before.

They are profoundly peaceful beings, their very nature making conflict or violence almost impossible to conceive. Their greatest challenges come from maintaining the delicate balance of their ecosystem and preserving the accumulated knowledge of their civilization through the vast spans of time they inhabit. To a Lithokine, patience is not a virtue but a fundamental aspect of existence - as essential as the water they inhabit or the light they transform into thought.
