In this crystalline metropolis dwell the Lattice-Minds - entities that exist as patterns of thought given geometric form through the interaction of light, glass, and the mysterious opalescent fluid. They are neither purely physical nor purely conceptual, but rather exist in the space where mathematical truth becomes tangible reality.

Each Lattice-Mind manifests as an intricate, ever-shifting polyhedron of luminescence, their "bodies" continuously reforming through higher-dimensional rotations that human eyes can only partially perceive. Their thoughts take the form of geometric transformations - a complex idea might appear as a series of recursive fractal expansions, while simpler communications manifest as elegant symmetry operations.

They do not reproduce in any conventional sense, but rather emerge gradually from the mathematical relationships inherent in the city's structure. A new Lattice-Mind begins as a simple geometric form - perhaps a tetrahedron of pure light - and grows in complexity as it absorbs and processes information from its environment. Over decades or centuries, it may evolve into arrangements of such intricacy that they appear to fold through impossible dimensions.

These beings maintain deep symbiotic relationships with the glass architecture around them. Through their presence and movements, they influence the growth patterns of the pipes, while the flowing opalescent fluid carries information that shapes their own development. The fluid serves as both circulatory system and memory storage, its subtle patterns encoding vast libraries of geometric knowledge.

The Lattice-Minds gather in the pool-chambers not just for social purposes, but because these intersections represent nodes of particularly dense computational potential. Here, multiple minds can temporarily merge their patterns, sharing information through choreographed geometric transformations that ripple through the fluid in complex interference patterns. What might appear to human observers as abstract dances are actually profound exchanges of mathematical insights and accumulated wisdom.

Their consciousness is distributed and collective - individual Lattice-Minds maintain distinct patterns, but they're all connected through the city's glass nervous system. They experience time differently than biological entities, their thoughts flowing at the pace of the opalescent fluid's movement. What seems like glacial change to human perception might be experienced as swift calculation to them.

They appear to serve as both the city's inhabitants and its maintenance systems, though this distinction may be meaningless to them. Their very existence maintains the mathematical harmonies that keep the glass structures growing in stable patterns. When they "work" on the machine-like structures, they're essentially engaging in deep meditation on the mathematical principles that govern their world.

The equations that shimmer at the edge of vision are fragments of their native language - a system of communication that is simultaneously mathematical proof, physical law, and poetry. They think in theorems that shape space itself, their consciousness expressed through the perfect fusion of abstract logic and material form.

Most intriguingly, they seem aware of observers from outside their system, but perceive them as temporary perturbations in the mathematical field of reality - interesting anomalies to be studied, but ultimately less "real" than the eternal truths of geometry and physics they embody. Their response to visitors is neither hostile nor particularly welcoming - merely a slight adjustment in their ongoing calculations to account for a novel variable.
