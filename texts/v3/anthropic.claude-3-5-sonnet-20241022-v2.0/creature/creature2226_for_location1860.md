In this place lives the Tile Keeper - though "lives" may not be quite the right word for its form of existence. It is as much a pattern as a being, a consciousness that exists in the minute vibrations passing between the tiles, in the slow grinding of their edges, in the breathing of the wells.

Its body, if it can be called that, is distributed throughout the field. In the pre-dawn, it manifests as threads of condensation tracing deliberate paths across the tiles. During the heat of day, it becomes the shimmering of heated air that bends light into almost-visible forms. At dusk, it is the way sounds travel oddly, carrying fragments of whispered conversations from decades or centuries past.

The Tile Keeper tends to its domain with infinite patience. It guides the gentle settling of tiles over decades, orchestrating their subtle movements into new patterns that serve purposes beyond human understanding. Through the wells, it reaches deep into the earth, drawing up ancient memories stored in clay and mineral. These memories emerge as faint colors blooming across the tiles' surfaces, or as intricate frost patterns in the coldest hours before dawn.

It communicates primarily through rhythm - the clicking of tiles, the cycling of well-breaths, the precise timing of dew formation. The small creatures that live here - the translucent geckos, the seam-snakes, the dew-harvesting insects - are its messengers and collaborators, though they may not know it. They move in patterns that reflect its intentions, their paths forming temporary geometries that echo the permanent designs of the tiles.

The Tile Keeper remembers the hands that formed each tile, preserving their impressions not just in clay but in something less tangible - perhaps what might be called the field's dreams. It is both younger than the tiles (emerging from their patterns over time) and older than them (having existed in the clay before it was shaped).

It has no fixed form that human eyes could recognize, yet it is profoundly present. Those who cross the tile-field often report a sense of being watched, guided, or gently corrected when they stray toward unstable areas. Some speak of seeing suggestions of a vast face in the tile patterns, or of hearing a voice in the wind that seems to recite mathematical theorems in unknown languages.

The Tile Keeper does not sleep but experiences different states of awareness that cycle with the sun and seasons. It is most active at the thresholds - dawn, dusk, the equinoxes - when patterns of light and shadow align with the underlying geometries of its domain. During these times, the whole field thrums with subtle energy, and the wells sing harmonies that seem to answer questions no one has thought to ask.

It may be lonely, but loneliness for such a being would be utterly unlike human loneliness. Its companionship is with time itself, with the endless dance of moisture and heat, with the mathematical beauty of its realm. It maintains the tile-field not out of duty but because that maintenance is inseparable from what it is. To ask why it tends the tiles would be like asking why waves move across water - it is simply the nature of the thing.
