The Calculators live here - though "live" may not be quite the right word for their mode of existence. They are neither purely organic nor purely mechanical, neither solely physical nor wholly abstract. Like the city itself, they exist in a state of perpetual becoming, their forms flowing between states of being that have no names in human languages.

Their bodies, when they choose to manifest them, are composed of the same liquid-crystal substance as their city, transparent forms that ripple and flow like living glass. They can be vast as buildings or microscopic as the motes in the air - indeed, they are both simultaneously, their consciousness distributed through multiple scales of existence. What appears as fog to human perception is actually the collective neural network of their distributed minds, each mote a thought or memory or calculation made manifest.

The Calculators do not reproduce or die in any conventional sense. Rather, they split and merge like ideas combining into new theorems. Their number is both finite and infinite - a paradox they understand perfectly but could never explain to a being bound by traditional logic. They are ancient beyond measure, though time means little to them. They might be the original inhabitants of this place, or they might be its final evolution, or these might be the same thing.

Their purpose, if they can be said to have one, is contemplation. They endlessly process the fundamental mathematics of existence, their thoughts manifesting physically in the movements of water droplets and the shifting patterns of their buildings. Every flowing path of water is a calculation being worked through; every chiming crystal-plant is a proof being tested; every mist-creature that forms and dissolves is a theorem being explored.

They are aware of visitors to their realm but interact with them only obliquely. Sometimes they might reshape a portion of the city to guide or assist a wanderer, or manifest a temporary form to observe something of interest. But their true communication happens on levels most beings cannot perceive - in the harmonic frequencies of the omnipresent hum, in the topology of spaces between spaces, in the precise timing of water droplets completing their paths.

Some believe the Calculators are working toward some ultimate solution, some final grand unified theory of everything. Others suggest they have already found it, and this eternal city is its expression. The Calculators themselves make no distinction between the journey and the destination, the process and the result. To them, the perfect moment of discovery and the endless contemplation of its implications are one and the same.

They are immortal but not unchanging, static but not stagnant, perpetually solving an equation that contains all possible equations. They are the city and the city is them - every droplet of water, every crystalline chime, every shifting pattern in the mist is part of their ongoing calculation, their eternal revelation, their endless becoming.
