In this aquatic cathedral, the true residents are the Sea Weavers - a lineage of human-like beings who have evolved to spend their entire lives underwater. Their bodies are long and sinuous, with translucent skin that shifts between deep blues and greens, allowing them to blend seamlessly with their environment. Webbed fingers extend into delicate tendrils perfect for the intricate work of kelp-weaving, while their large, pearl-like eyes can perceive subtle variations in water currents and light that others would miss.

The Sea Weavers live in small family groups, each responsible for tending different sections of the garden-temple. The elders, immediately recognizable by the living coral that has gradually grown to form natural crowns upon their heads, spend most of their time in meditation near the base of the kelp-columns, their bodies swaying in harmony with the water's movements. Their presence seems to influence the very currents around them, as if they're conducting an underwater symphony.

Young Weavers learn their craft by first working with the fish schools, developing the patience and precision needed for the greater work of kelp-weaving. They communicate through a combination of graceful hand gestures and resonant humming that vibrates through the water, creating patterns that the trained fish respond to. Their movements are deliberate and dance-like, each motion serving both practical and ceremonial purposes.

The most distinctive feature of the Sea Weavers is their symbiotic relationship with thumbnail-sized bioluminescent creatures that nest in their hair and along their spines. These living lights pulse in patterns that mirror their hosts' emotions and intentions, creating a complex language of light that complements their gestural communication. During important ceremonies, the combined light patterns of an entire family group create spectacular displays that transform the garden-temple into a theater of living light.

Among them move the Memory Keepers, distinguished by their elaborate garments woven from living sea silk and decorated with patterns of tiny, phosphorescent organisms. These scholars maintain the pictographic records grown into the walls, cultivating new stories while preserving the old. They spend decades training specific coral formations to grow in ways that record their people's history and knowledge.

Particularly fascinating are the Dream Tenders, who maintain special chambers where the water is thick with psychoactive compounds naturally produced by carefully cultivated sea life. Here, they enter deep meditative states, their minds joining with the collective consciousness of the garden-temple itself, gaining insights that guide the ongoing evolution of their living architecture.

The youngest Sea Weavers often play among the bubble streams, learning to read the waters while tending to the smallest creatures - the crabs and cleaner fish that keep the garden-temple healthy. Their games appear random but serve to train them in understanding the complex currents and rhythms that flow through their world.

The entire community operates like a single organism, each member's actions contributing to the greater whole. They don't merely live in the garden-temple; they are part of it, their lives and the life of their home intertwined in ways that blur the distinction between keeper and kept, architect and architecture.
