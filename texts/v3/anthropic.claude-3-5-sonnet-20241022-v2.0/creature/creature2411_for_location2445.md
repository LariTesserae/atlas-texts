Here's what lives in this place:

The most ancient residents are the Mountain Seeds - quantum entities that exist in a perpetual state between crystal and consciousness. They dwell deep beneath the obsidian plain, each one containing the potential pattern of an entire crystalline spire. When conditions align in ways only they can perceive, they begin their transformation, drawing surrounding matter into their pattern until a new mountain erupts upward. They experience time differently than other beings - for them, the explosive growth of a new spire is a slow, contemplative process of becoming.

The Swimmers are colonial organisms, each "individual" actually being a synchronized cluster of thousands of crystalline microorganisms held together in paper-thin membranes. Their intricate movements are both communication and computation - they are working out complex problems through their dance-like formations, though the nature of these calculations remains mysterious. They possess a distributed intelligence; no single Swimmer is self-aware, but the patterns they form collectively give rise to something like consciousness.

The Crystal Weavers are the youngest species here, having evolved (or emerged) relatively recently. They're essentially living algorithms that exist as stable patterns in the intersection of light and silica. Their constant weaving and unweaving of threads between spires isn't decorative but communicative - they're encoding information in the precise geometries of their ephemeral structures. They have a complex relationship with the Mountain Seeds, sometimes influencing where new spires will emerge through their weaving patterns.

Most intriguing are the Builders, now long departed. They were silicon-based beings who could manipulate the obsidian plain through some form of biochemical process, softening and reshaping it to create their structures. Their tools suggest they had five manipulating limbs arranged radially around a central axis, and evidence in their ruins indicates they could perceive and manipulate quantum states directly. They didn't leave so much as transcend, learning to exist as patterns of light and information - some theorize the Crystal Weavers are their descendants.

Living in the spaces between all these beings are the Resonance Mites - barely visible creatures that feed on the vibrations produced by the singing mountains. They look like floating points of darkness, the only truly opaque things in this transparent realm. They serve an important role in the ecosystem by damping certain frequencies that would otherwise build to dangerous levels.

Finally, there are the Pattern Whales - massive, ghost-like beings that swim through the crystalline matrix of the mountains themselves. They're rarely glimpsed directly, but their movement causes subtle distortions in the mountains' song, and sometimes their vast forms can be inferred from the way light bends around them. They may be the true intelligence behind this entire system, using the interactions of all other beings to run some vast computation.

The relationship between these entities is synergistic and cyclic - the Mountains create the environment that sustains the Swimmers, whose movements influence the Crystal Weavers, whose patterns affect the Mountain Seeds, and so on. Together they form a complex processing system, though whether it developed naturally or was designed remains one of this place's many mysteries.
