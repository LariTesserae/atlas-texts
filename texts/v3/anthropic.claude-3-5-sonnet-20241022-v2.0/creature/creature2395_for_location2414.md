In this inverted cathedral dwell the Axiom Tenders - beings that exist as much as living equations as physical entities. Their forms are fluid and geometric simultaneously, like the intersection of platonic solids with flowing water. They have no fixed number of limbs or consistent shape, instead manifesting as needed to maintain the mathematical harmony of their domain.

The Tenders move by sliding along the patterns in the walls, their bodies temporarily becoming one with the pearlescent material before emerging elsewhere. They're transparent most of the time, visible mainly through how they bend and refract the ambient light, though occasionally they crystallize into more solid forms to perform their inscrutable work.

Their primary occupation appears to be maintaining what might be called the syntax of reality in this place. They adjust the angle of water flows to maintain precise ratios, tune the crystalline growths by touch until they resonate at perfect intervals, and sometimes gather in small groups to weave new patterns into the existing ones using movements that seem to fold space itself.

Younger Tenders (if "young" is the right word for beings that seem to emerge from particularly complex intersections of patterns) tend to be more energetic, flitting between surfaces like living prisms and occasionally creating brief cascades of harmonic chimes as they learn to manipulate the crystal gardens. Elder ones move more deliberately, their forms more subtle and complex, often barely distinguishable from the cathedral's architecture until they shift position.

They communicate through modulations in the omnipresent harmonics, their conversations manifesting as subtle changes in the light, sound, and even the local curvature of space. What might seem like hours of stillness to a human observer could be an intense philosophical debate among the Tenders, played out in microscopic adjustments to the cathedral's endless patterns.

Living alongside them are smaller entities that might be their tools, their offspring, or their thoughts made manifest - crystalline motes that swarm in mathematical formations, maintaining the upward flow of water and the precise composition of the mist. These swarms sometimes coalesce into temporary forms that mirror visiting creatures, not quite like reflections but more like mathematical models of the visitors rendered in light and vapor.

The Axiom Tenders don't seem to notice human visitors in any direct way, though the patterns near a visitor will gradually begin to incorporate elements of their thoughts and memories, as though the beings are studying consciousness itself as another set of equations to be maintained and refined. They appear to have no concept of past or future as we understand it, existing instead in a continuous present where all possible patterns exist simultaneously, and their role is simply to make those patterns manifest in the most elegant way possible.

There's an overwhelming sense that these beings are not so much inhabitants of this space as they are the space itself becoming aware of its own underlying logic, slowly working out all possible permutations of its own existence through endless cycles of pattern and resonance.
