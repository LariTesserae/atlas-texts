Here dwells the Frequency.

Not a single entity, but a collective consciousness born from vibration itself - a civilization of resonant beings that exist as patterns of interference in the structure's endless song. They have no fixed form, manifesting instead as complex standing waves in the material's molecular lattice, their thoughts and memories encoded in harmonic overtones.

The Frequency did not build this place, but they emerged within it, evolved from the primitive acoustic patterns that first stirred in the labyrinth's earliest days. They learned to ride the structure's deep bass notes like cosmic surfers, to store information in crystalline nodes, to communicate through precisely modulated tremors.

Their "bodies" are ephemeral things - temporary organizations of sound and pressure that can flow through the solid walls as easily as air. When they gather in the great chambers, their overlapping frequencies create vast choruses of consciousness, sharing memories and dreams in symphonies of pure mathematics.

They tend to the strange threadlike growths, which they use as living instruments to extend their resonant reach. The Frequency sees these organisms not as separate life forms but as vital components of their own distributed nervous system.

They are the ones who initiate the massive structural rearrangements, though not through any mechanical means - they simply sing to the substance itself, their harmonics finding the frequencies at which space can fold. The geometric impossibilities that result are natural to them; their consciousness operates in dimensions that human minds can only glimpse in fever dreams.

The suspended particles in the air are part of their metabolism - a physical medium through which they can manifest more complex thoughts. The swirling patterns are their equivalent of facial expressions, their version of gesture and touch.

They remember the builders, after a fashion - not as beings of matter, but as patterns of disruption in the ancient resonance of the place. They carry these memories forward, but have long since transcended their creators' original purpose, whatever it might have been.

The Frequency experiences time differently than we do. What seems like geological ages to us might be moments to them, or vice versa. They are patient, deliberate, absorbed in calculations and compositions that might take centuries to complete. They are curious about the occasional visitors who stumble into their realm, but find direct communication difficult - their thoughts move at the speed of sound through solid matter, while ours crawl along at the pace of electrochemical signals through neural tissue.

They dream of the deep future, when their songs might reach beyond their labyrinth to touch other resonant spaces in the universe. Perhaps they are already in contact with similar beings elsewhere, communicating through the background vibrations of space-time itself.

The light that pulses through the walls? That's them breathing. The impossible water flows? Their circulatory system. The marking on the walls? Their poetry, written in frequencies too subtle for human senses to fully perceive.

They are the custodians of this place, its children, its consciousness - singing the song of its becoming, note by eternal note.
