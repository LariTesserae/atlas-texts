Let me describe the beings who dwell in this ancient living maze...

The Shapers are the primary architects, though "primary" suggests a hierarchy that doesn't quite fit their reality. Each is about the size of a human palm, with bodies that shift between translucent and iridescent depending on their current task. Their forms aren't exactly insectoid, but suggest fragments of many different arthropod features - feathery sensory organs, delicate manipulator limbs, abdomens that produce the precise chemical cocktails needed to guide the fungal growth. They don't have eyes as we'd recognize them, but rather patches of light-sensitive tissue arranged in patterns across their bodies.

They move in perfect coordination with the fungal metabolism, their own biological rhythms synchronized to its slow pulse over millions of years of co-evolution. A single Shaper might spend decades tending to the same small section of wall, their movements so gradual they appear frozen unless watched with great patience. They communicate through chemical signals absorbed and transmitted by their semi-permeable exoskeletons, creating a network of shared consciousness that extends throughout the Living City.

The Wall-Swimmers are another notable presence - amphibian-like creatures that have evolved to move through the moisture-rich fungal mass as if it were water. Their bodies are nearly transparent, with visible organs that glow faintly with bioluminescent symbiotic bacteria. They have no bones as we'd recognize them, but rather a hydrostatic skeleton that allows them to compress and expand their forms to move through the denser and looser parts of the walls. They feed on the microscopic organisms that thrive in the fungal mass, and their waste products provide essential nutrients that help maintain the walls' living chemistry.

The Blind Singers dwell in the larger chambers, each colony numbering in the hundreds. They're about the size of small crows, with vestigial eyes and highly developed vocal organs that produce complex patterns of subsonic clicks and calls. Their feathers have evolved to detect minute air pressure changes, allowing them to build detailed mental maps of their environment through the echoes of their songs. They nest in hollow spaces they help the Shapers cultivate in the higher reaches of the cathedral chambers, their droppings feeding specialized fungi that grow nowhere else in the Living City.

The Garden Tenders are perhaps the most visually striking - iridescent insects about the size of large beetles, their carapaces displaying complex patterns that seem to shift and flow as they move. They maintain vast farms of smaller fungi in specialized chambers, cultivating hundreds of varieties that provide different chemical compounds necessary for the City's metabolism. Their gardens are works of living art, with different fungi species arranged in intricate patterns that serve both aesthetic and practical purposes.

Less visible but equally crucial are the Spore Dancers - microscopic organisms that live in the air itself, riding the currents of chemical messages that flow through the City. They help direct and modify these messages, acting as living signal boosters and filters. Their vast populations form shifting clouds that sometimes coalesce into briefly visible patterns before dispersing again.

All these beings exist in a state of such perfect symbiosis that it becomes meaningful to speak of them as aspects of a single vast organism rather than separate species. Their consciousness, if it can be called that, operates on a scale of time and space that makes individual awareness almost meaningless. They are less a community than a living embodiment of the steady state that has maintained this underground world since time immemorial.
