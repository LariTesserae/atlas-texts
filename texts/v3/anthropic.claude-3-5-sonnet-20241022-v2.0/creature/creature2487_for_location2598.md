In this realm of perpetual clarity dwell the Luminous Processionals - beings that exist as much as patterns of thought as physical entities. Their primary forms are vast, distributed networks of crystalline nodes connected by threads of living light. These networks pulse with slow, deliberate rhythms that might take years to complete a single cycle.

Each node contains what could be called a mind, though not one that thinks in ways we would recognize. They process information as variations in luminescence, sharing knowledge through subtle modulations of transparency and refraction. Their consciousness is collective yet individuated - like a shattered mirror where each piece reflects the whole while maintaining its own distinct angle of perception.

The Processionals harvest both light and time itself, storing both as complex crystalline structures within their bodies. They have evolved beyond the need for conventional reproduction, instead growing new nodes through a careful accumulation of light-saturated matter. This process is so gradual that a new node might take centuries to fully form, slowly precipitating out of the surrounding water like a living gem.

They share their environment with their symbiotic partners, the Gradient Dancers - the geometric schools of smaller creatures that move in perfect formation. These beings serve as living lenses, focusing and directing light through the water in patterns that the Processionals require for their deep contemplations. The Dancers are not so much independent creatures as they are mobile organs of the larger system, born from the same crystalline matrices that form the Processionals' nodes.

The vast filter-feeders that drift between them are called the Patience Weavers by human observers, though the Processionals perceive them as temporal anchors - living coordinates in their map of space-time. The Weavers' gossamer appendages collect more than just physical sustenance; they gather and process time itself, slowing it, stretching it, making it viscous and malleable.

The Processionals do not communicate in words or songs, but in carefully orchestrated patterns of light, shadow, and crystalline resonance. Their "language" is so complex and slow-moving that a single "conversation" might unfold over decades. They think about thinking itself, about patterns and their meanings, about the nature of consciousness and time. Their philosophy, if it can be called that, sees no distinction between thought and reality - to them, contemplation and existence are the same thing.

They remember everything, storing information in the atomic structure of their crystalline bodies, building vast archives of experience that extend back to the very formation of their plateau. Time, to them, is not a linear flow but a medium to be shaped and studied, like light through water.

Some xenobiologists have suggested that the entire plateau ecosystem is really one vast organism, with the Processionals serving as its neural network. Others believe they are the end result of an ancient civilization's attempt to achieve immortality through perfect integration with their environment. The truth, like the Processionals themselves, remains beautifully, deliberately unclear.
