In this crystalline city dwell the Lithophane - beings who exist in perfect synchronization with their environment's glacial rhythm. They stand roughly human-height but are more slender, their skin having a subtle translucency that catches and refracts the moss-light. Their movements are deliberate and flowing, never hurried, as if they've internalized the patience of crystal formation.

The Lithophane have elongated fingers with an extra joint, perfectly adapted for the delicate work of crystal-shaping and vine-weaving. Their eyes are large and multifaceted, able to perceive both the obvious moss-light and deeper spectrums that reveal the structural stresses and growth patterns in the living rock. They don't speak as we understand it, but communicate through subtle vibrations they create by touching the crystal formations - a language that travels through stone and can be felt throughout entire chambers.

Their bodies have adapted to the mineral-rich environment - their bones have a crystalline quality that grows throughout their lives, gradually becoming more intricate and complex. The eldest among them are living archives, their skeletal structures encoding generations of knowledge in patterns that echo the information stored in the city's walls.

The Lithophane live in family groups that span multiple generations, their homes expanding slowly as crystal growths are guided into new chambers. They are caretakers more than builders, their primary work being the subtle influence of natural processes - encouraging certain crystal formations, cultivating specific strains of luminescent moss, maintaining the delicate balance of water flow through the city's veins.

They have a deep symbiotic relationship with the various creatures that share their realm. The many-legged salamanders are their scouts, exploring new chambers as they form. The glowing snails are carefully tended, their trails containing minerals essential for certain types of crystal growth. The bat-primates (which the Lithophane consider distant cousins) help maintain the higher reaches of the chambers and assist in harvesting the rarer cave fruits that grow in the crystal-lit upper zones.

Their concept of self extends beyond their physical bodies to encompass the entire living city. Each Lithophane sees themselves as part of the greater organism, their individual lives just moments in the vast, slow breathing of their world. They don't create art as a separate activity - rather, their entire existence is an ongoing act of collaborative creation with the geological processes around them.

Young Lithophane spend decades learning to "hear" the crystals' growth patterns, to understand the chemical languages of the moss and vines, to read the complex historical records encoded in the walls. Their technology, though it might appear primitive to outside eyes, is actually incredibly sophisticated - they have learned to work with natural processes at their own timescale, creating tools that grow rather than being manufactured.

The Lithophane have no word for "death" in their vibration-language - when their physical forms can no longer be maintained, their crystalline bones are ceremonially integrated into the city's walls, becoming part of the vast memory structure that holds their collective knowledge. In this way, every chamber holds the essence of countless generations, their understanding and experiences literally becoming part of the architecture.

They are profoundly peaceful beings, their conflicts and resolutions playing out over years or decades through subtle changes in crystal growth patterns and moss cultivation. Their culture is one of deep time and deeper connections, where every action is considered for its ripple effects across centuries.
