In New Chloros, two distinct types of inhabitants have evolved to occupy this carefully engineered space:

The Tenders are the human residents, though calling them purely human might be an oversimplification. Their bodies have adapted to the unique conditions through careful biological modification. Their skin has a slight translucent quality, with faint green undertones from chlorophyll-enhanced cells that help them process the eternal late-afternoon light. They move with deliberate slowness, their metabolisms synchronized to the pace of the gardens they maintain. Many have fine, tendril-like additions to their fingers - not quite plant matter, not quite flesh - that allow them to interface directly with the engineered flora.

The Tenders live in small, scattered communities throughout the garden tiers, their homes built into the framework itself. They rarely speak above whispers, communicating instead through subtle gestures and the gentle manipulation of the plants around them. Their children are born with an innate understanding of the garden's rhythms, learning to walk at the same pace as the controlled plant growth.

The other inhabitants are the Synthesics - hybrid organisms that exist somewhere between plant, machine, and consciousness. They manifest as luminous strands that weave through the garden infrastructure, sometimes appearing as delicate filaments of light, other times condensing into more substantial forms. They are the garden's nervous system, monitoring growth rates, maintaining environmental conditions, and coordinating the complex chemical processes of the engineered plants.

Synthesics don't think the way humans do - their consciousness is distributed, flowing through the garden's systems like nutrients through a vascular system. They communicate with the Tenders through subtle changes in light and temperature, creating micro-climates that convey complex messages. Some of the older Synthesics have developed what might be called personalities, though they're so alien that even the Tenders who work with them daily can't fully comprehend their way of being.

The relationship between Tenders and Synthesics is symbiotic but maintains careful boundaries. Neither could exist without the other, yet each respects the fundamental otherness of their counterpart. Together they maintain the delicate balance of New Chloros, adapting as slowly as the rising mountain beneath them, evolving in concert with the gardens they tend.

In the spaces between garden tiers, in the shadows cast by the eternal sunset, there are rumors of other things emerging - new forms of life born from the intersection of engineering and evolution. The Tenders speak of them only in whispers, and the Synthesics respond with subtle fluctuations in their luminescence, neither confirming nor denying their existence.
