Let me describe the Crystallids, as I'll call them, though they surely have their own name for themselves - one expressed in frequencies too low for human ears to detect.

These beings exist in a state of continuous transformation between crystal and living tissue. Their bodies are composed of translucent segments that can either lock rigid or flow with surprising fluidity, allowing them to navigate both the wide chambers and the narrowest passages of their domain. Each segment contains intricate lattices of living crystal that process light and vibration into sustenance and information.

The smallest Crystallids - the workers - are about the length of a human hand. Their six-sided bodies echo the hexagonal tunnels they maintain, with each segment capable of rotating independently. They move in perfect synchronization with their neighbors, their crystalline edges never quite touching but maintaining precise spacing through some form of electromagnetic sensing. When they work, they secrete mineral compounds that both dissolve and rebuild the tunnel walls, guided by the deep harmonic frequencies that pervade the hive-maze.

The defender caste is more substantial, ranging from the size of large dogs to small cars. Their segments are thicker, with faceted protrusions that can either reflect light in dazzling displays or focus it into intense beams. They move more independently than the workers, patrolling in irregular patterns that nevertheless form part of some larger choreography.

Most ethereal are the Resonators - the floating beings that drift through the larger chambers. Their bodies are arranged in complex geometric patterns that seem to shift and reconfigure constantly. They appear to act as living communication nodes, gathering the subtle vibrations and light patterns from throughout their section of the maze and transforming them into the low frequencies that coordinate hive activities.

The queens are vast, ancient beings, their crystalline bodies grown into permanent fusion with their chamber walls. Each segment of their impossibly long forms is a self-contained ecosystem, hosting specialized symbiotic organisms that process minerals and maintain the complex biochemistry of the hive. They grow continuously, though so slowly that it might take centuries to add a single new segment.

What appears at first to be random movement reveals itself, over time, to be an intricate dance of information and resource exchange. The Crystallids communicate through a combination of light patterns, vibrations, and electromagnetic fields, creating a constant flow of data that shapes their behavior on both individual and collective levels. They don't think as we do - their consciousness, if we can call it that, seems distributed throughout the entire maze-hive, emerging from the interactions of countless individuals over vast spans of time.

Their relationship with their environment is so intimate that it becomes difficult to say where the Crystallids end and their habitat begins. The crystalline plants are tended not as separate organisms but as extensions of the hive itself. The geometric patterns that cover the walls serve simultaneously as historical record, scientific data, and cultural expression - a living library written in light and crystal.

These beings process time differently than we do - what seems like frenetic activity to them might appear nearly motionless to human eyes. They are creatures of deep time, their lives and purposes playing out over millennia, each individual a brief sparkle in patterns that have been flowing through these tunnels since before our species learned to walk upright.
