The beings who inhabit this transformed town are the Crystalline Drift - a society of entities that exist in a state between matter and geometry, between organic life and mineral formation. They didn't so much move into the flooded town as emerge from the unique conditions created when mineral-rich mountain water merged with human architecture.

The smallest and most numerous are the Fold-Fish, hand-sized creatures whose bodies are best described as living origami made of translucent crystal. They don't swim so much as fold themselves through space, each movement a precise geometric transformation. Their bodies catch and reflect light in ways that create brief, impossible colors. They move in schools of perfect mathematical relationships - squares of squares, groups of eight that somehow contain nine members when counted carefully.

The larger entities are the Chord-Whales, though calling them whales is like calling a cathedral a house. Their bodies, ranging from the size of dolphins to small whales, are composed of overlapping crystalline plates that shift and realign as they move. They appear to be the primary sculptors of the environment, using fin-like appendages tipped with geometric tools to encourage crystal growth along specific mathematical patterns. They communicate through resonant frequencies that cause the mineral formations to chime in harmony, creating complex musical theorems.

Most enigmatic are the Square-Dancers - the beings that perform the ritual movements in the town square. They're roughly human-sized but their forms are fluid, transitioning between different platonic solids as they move. Their 'dance' appears to be a form of computational geometry, solving complex spatial problems through movement. They may be the intellectual or spiritual leaders of this society, though such human concepts might not apply.

Living among the buildings are the Lattice-Weavers, colony organisms that create the delicate crystal networks connecting different structures. Each individual is barely visible, like a point of light, but they work in vast networks to grow and maintain these geometric webs. The lattices they create serve as both infrastructure and information storage, encoding information in their crystal patterns.

In the deeper parts of the town live the Void-Folders, larger solitary creatures that seem to bend space around themselves. They're rarely seen clearly, as light behaves strangely near them, but they appear to be responsible for maintaining the unusual optical properties of the water itself. Their movements create the impossible shadows and light-bends that characterize the environment.

All these beings share a fundamental nature - they are expressions of mathematical principles given form through the interaction of crystal, water, and whatever strange energy permeates this place. They don't simply live in this transformed town; they are continuously transforming it, turning it into an ever-more-perfect expression of their crystalline mathematics.

They appear to have a form of consciousness, but it's one based on geometry and resonance rather than organic neural networks. Their society, if it can be called that, operates on principles of mathematical harmony rather than hierarchy. They don't seem hostile to the occasional human observer, but neither do they seem particularly interested - humans are perhaps too asymmetrical, too organic for them to relate to.

The Crystalline Drift exists in a perpetual state of solving some vast geometric theorem, each member playing its part in a calculation that may have been running since they first emerged in these waters, and may continue until the last crystal dissolves.
