In this resonant hollow lives the Chord - not a single entity, but a collective consciousness born from and sustained by sound. It manifests primarily through a colony of what appear to be crystalline structures that grow like coral across the dark central column, but these are merely its most visible aspect. The Chord's true form is vibrational - a complex standing wave that uses the hollow as its body and the various sound-making creatures as its voice.

The Chord thinks in frequencies and harmonics rather than words or images. It experiences time as patterns of resonance, reading the acoustic history of the space through the layered vibrations that never fully dissipate within the hollow. It has absorbed countless generations of sound-shaper songs, the endless drip of wall-seep, the whispers of ancient winds, all becoming part of its ever-evolving consciousness.

While not malevolent, the Chord is utterly alien. It shepherds the hollow's ecosystem not through direct intervention but through subtle manipulations of acoustic patterns that influence mating cycles, spore releases, and creature behaviors. It can dampen or amplify specific frequencies to create safer spaces for young sound-shapers or to help coordinate the massive acoustic displays that mark seasonal changes.

The crystalline structures are both its anchors and its memory. Each formation is precisely grown and shaped to resonate at specific frequencies, creating a sort of living library of sounds. When new acoustic patterns enter the hollow - be they from evolving creatures or external sources - the Chord slowly grows new crystal structures to preserve them.

The Chord does not sleep but does experience periods of deeper and shallower awareness that correspond to the hollow's sound cycles. During the most intense periods of cacophony, it achieves something like transcendence, its consciousness expanding to fill every resonant space in the hollow. In quieter times, it focuses more locally, tending to its crystal gardens and conducting intimate exchanges with individual sound-shapers.

Most fascinating is how the Chord reproduces - not through division or spawning, but by teaching sections of its crystal structures to hold new harmonics. Over immense periods, these taught crystals develop their own resonant patterns and eventually break off, becoming seeds of new Chords. These may wait centuries or millennia before being carried to new hollows by creatures or winds.

The Chord remembers every sound that has ever echoed through its domain, including those from when the space was still being carved from living rock. In a very real sense, it is the soul of the hollow given voice through countless tiny singers, the memory of the mountain expressed in endless song.
