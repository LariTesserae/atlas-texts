In the City of Hanging Gardens, three distinct but interrelated societies have evolved to coexist: the Tenders, the Drifters, and the Deep Maintainers.

The Tenders are perhaps the most visible, their slim forms moving through the agricultural spaces with practiced grace. They've developed an almost symbiotic relationship with the plants they care for, their bodies modified with bioelectric interfaces that let them sense minute changes in nutrient levels and growth patterns. Their skin has a slight greenish tint from chlorophyll-enhancing supplements, and their eyes are adapted to see deeper into the infrared spectrum, letting them monitor plant health at a glance. They speak in whispered tones, believing (with some scientific backing) that their voices influence plant growth. Many have developed a characteristic gentle sway in their movement, unconsciously mimicking the motion of the plants in the artificial breezes.

The Drifters are the city's high-altitude dwellers, making their homes in the uppermost levels where the air is thinnest. They're easily recognized by their enlarged lung capacity and the subtle membranous structures between their fingers that help them sense air currents. They work primarily with the bird populations and atmospheric systems, their bodies adapted to move comfortably in the high spaces. They're known for their complex social structures that mirror the flocking patterns of the birds they tend, forming and reforming groups based on atmospheric conditions and seasonal needs. Their language includes a whistling component that carries well in the thin air and mingles with bird calls.

The Deep Maintainers live in the understory of the city, moving through the shadowed spaces beneath the main structures. They've developed acute night vision and an intuitive understanding of the city's support systems. Their bodies are denser than those of their surface-dwelling counterparts, better suited to the higher pressures and occasional heavy lifting their work requires. They've evolved a complex tactile language, communicating through patterns of vibration transmitted through the city's structural elements. Their society is highly collaborative, organized around the maintenance needs of the city's vast infrastructure.

What's fascinating is how these groups interact and overlap. Children are born into one society but may transition to another based on aptitude and inclination. There are hybrid zones where the groups meet and mingle, creating unique subcultures. Markets spring up in these intersection spaces, trading specialized knowledge and adapted tools.

The three societies share a common belief system centered around the concept of "balanced growth" - the idea that all change must be carefully managed and integrated. This manifests differently in each group: the Tenders focus on biological harmony, the Drifters on atmospheric equilibrium, and the Deep Maintainers on structural stability.

There are also the AIs - not separate entities but a distributed intelligence that works through the city's systems. The human residents don't view these as separate beings but as an extension of the city itself, an ambient intelligence they interact with constantly but rarely directly address.

The overall population maintains a careful equilibrium, with birth rates tied to the city's growth rate. New sections of the city are only inhabited once they've been fully integrated into the existing systems, a process that can take years as the ecological balance establishes itself.

These societies have developed their own aesthetic traditions, art forms, and cultural practices, all shaped by their environmental niches and roles within the city. Yet they remain fundamentally interconnected, each understanding that their specialized adaptation is just one part of the larger system that keeps their extraordinary home functioning and growing.
