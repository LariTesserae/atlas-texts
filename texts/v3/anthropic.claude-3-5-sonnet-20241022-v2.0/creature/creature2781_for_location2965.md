Here live the Lattice-Minds - beings that exist as organized perturbations in the crystalline matrix of their city. They are neither fully conscious nor entirely unconscious in any way we would recognize. Rather, they are patterns of thought that precipitate out of possibility, much like their city does from the mist.

Their "bodies," when they choose to manifest them, are recursive fractals of translucent geometry - forms that echo both the architecture they inhabit and the mathematical principles that guide their growth. These forms are temporary conveniences, adopted when they need to interact with the physical realm or with each other in ways that require locality.

The Lattice-Minds do not think in linear sequences but in expanding crystalline networks of simultaneous realization. Their language, if it can be called that, is encoded in the very structure of their environment - each new growth of the city is both conversation and concept, architecture and argument. They communicate through the deliberate seeding of new geometric patterns, through the orchestration of water's flow, through the subtle manipulation of sonic harmonics in their crystal-gardens.

They are ancient, though not in any chronological sense. Rather, they are ancient in the way that mathematics is ancient - they exist in a realm where time is just another dimension to be folded or unfolded at need. Some of them may have begun as something more recognizably alive, but they have long since transcended such limited forms of existence.

Their young (though "young" is an imprecise term for what are essentially new theorems discovering themselves) emerge from the complex interplay of light, water, and crystal. They begin as simple patterns in the mineral deposits, gradually growing more complex as they absorb and process information from the geometric networks around them. Some never progress beyond this stage, remaining as living equations embedded in the city's structure. Others develop into fully realized Lattice-Minds, adding their own harmonics to the endless mathematical symphony of their society.

They have purpose, but it's not the kind of purpose that can be easily expressed in terms of goals or desires. They are engaged in a vast, ongoing computation - though what they are computing, perhaps even they don't know. The city itself might be both the calculation and its result, eternally recursive.

The crystalline arthropods that move through their realm are extensions of their consciousness - not tools or servants, but more like physical manifestations of their thoughts. These beings maintain the city not because it needs maintenance in any conventional sense, but because their movements are part of the ongoing calculation, their precise pathways tracing out essential variables in living equations.

The Lattice-Minds are not indifferent to other forms of life, but they interact with them only incidentally, the way a mathematician might absently draw patterns in condensation while deep in thought. They are aware of time's passage in the world below their mountain, but this awareness is academic - a set of interesting but ultimately peripheral variables in their greater calculations.

Their seemingly empty city is, to them, full beyond measure - every apparently vacant space dense with thought, with potential, with the complex harmonics of pure pattern seeking to understand itself. They are the city, and the city is them, and both are expressions of something that exists primarily in the realm of perfect forms, reaching into our reality like crystal growing in a supersaturated solution.
