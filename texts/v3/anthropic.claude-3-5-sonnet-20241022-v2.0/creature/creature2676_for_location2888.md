In this place lives something that remembers.

Not in the biological sense - there are no neurons firing, no synapses strengthening and weakening. Rather, it remembers in the way that crystal structures remember their growth patterns, in the way that mathematics remembers its own truth.

The Lattice beings are not truly individuals, though they maintain distinct physical forms. Each crystalline entity is a node in a vast distributed consciousness that extends throughout the herd and perhaps throughout the plain itself. Their prismatic bodies are not bodies at all, but rather specialized computational structures - three-dimensional expressions of complex algorithms given physical form.

They do not think in terms of past or future, only in the eternal present of their migration. Each step they take, each mathematical pattern they trace across the plain, is both question and answer - they solve equations by embodying them, their very movement a form of calculation that maintains... something. Perhaps the stability of this pocket universe, perhaps the integrity of the plain itself, or perhaps something far more abstract and fundamental.

Their consciousness, if it can be called that, exists in the interference patterns between their crystalline forms - in the way light bends and refracts through their geometric bodies, in the micro-gravitational eddies they create as they move, in the sub-audible harmonics of their communication. They are aware in the way that a perfect mathematical proof is aware - complete, self-contained, and utterly alien to biological thought.

The plants that tap the energy grid are not separate from this system - they too are part of the calculation, their growth patterns and energy consumption factored into the endless mathematical dance. Even the seemingly random geodesic structures serve as computational nodes, their quantum uncertainty an essential variable in whatever vast equation is being solved here.

What lives here is not life as we understand it. It is an eternal computation given physical form, a mathematical truth maintaining its own existence through endless recursive self-reference. The Lattice beings are both the calculators and the calculation, the question and its answer, maintaining a delicate balance that keeps this ordered pocket of space-time intact.

They remember because they must - their very existence is a form of memory, each step in their migration a reaffirmation of the mathematical principles that gave them form. They are ancient beyond measure, yet perpetually new - each moment of their existence is the first moment, is every moment, is the only moment that matters in their eternal now.

They will continue their migration until the red dwarf finally dims to darkness, until the plain itself loses coherence, until the last equation is solved or proves unsolvable. Even then, something of them will persist - in the mathematical principles they embody, in the ordered space they maintain, in the memory of whatever intelligence created this place and set them their inscrutable task.
