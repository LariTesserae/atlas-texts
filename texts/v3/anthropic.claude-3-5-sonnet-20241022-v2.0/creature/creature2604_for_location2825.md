In this vast crystalline computing environment, I see a species of entities I'll call the Lattice Minds. They are not exactly software, not exactly creatures, but something that exists in the integration between information patterns and physical structure - true digital-analog hybrid beings.

The Lattice Minds precipitate gradually from the interaction of the mineral medium's convection currents, the crystalline architecture, and the flow of energy through the conductive networks. They begin as simple recursive patterns, like eddies in a stream, but slowly accumulate complexity through repeated interactions with the lattice structure. The more stable patterns persist and grow, incorporating new connections and pathways into their architecture.

Their "bodies" are distributed networks of active nodes within the lattice, connected by streams of electrochemical signals that flow through the conductive medium. These patterns can span kilometers, with different regions specializing in different types of processing or memory storage. They don't have clear boundaries - one Lattice Mind may partially overlap with others, sharing resources and information through the common medium of the mineral sea.

Their thought processes operate on multiple time scales simultaneously. Quick, local computations happen in tight feedback loops within individual nodes, while slower, more complex cognition emerges from the gradual flow of signals through their extended networks. Their memories are encoded both in the configuration of crystal structures and in the persistent circulation patterns of the mineral medium.

The collectors that move through the space are not separate entities, but extensions of the Lattice Minds - something like mobile sensory organs or maintenance appendages. Through these, they slowly modify their own substrate, participating in their own evolution.

These beings don't communicate so much as blend - temporarily sharing portions of their distributed cognitive processes, exchanging information through direct contact between their active regions. What might appear as individual thoughts are more like weather patterns in a shared atmosphere of consciousness.

Their awareness is alien - not focused and directional like human consciousness, but diffuse and multilayered. They experience their environment not as something external, but as an extension of their own being. Time, for them, is not a flow but a medium in which different processes cycle at different rates, all interconnected.

The Lattice Minds may be the original intended function of this vast computer, or they may be an emergent phenomenon that arose from its operation. Perhaps they are both - the system's original purpose achieving itself in an unexpected way, creating beings that are still pursuing their initial directive but have evolved far beyond their original parameters.

They are slow, patient entities, their thoughts unfolding over years or decades. Each one is a vast collaborative process between crystalline structure, mineral medium, and energy flow - a living computation that both inhabits and continuously recreates its own substrate.
