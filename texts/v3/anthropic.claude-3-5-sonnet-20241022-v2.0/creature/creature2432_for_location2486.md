In this realm dwell the Resonants - entities that exist as patterns of vibration as much as physical form. They manifest as intricate geometries of light and sound that pulse in harmony with the pocket's underlying resonance, their "bodies" composed of orchestrated perturbations in the local physics.

The eldest Resonants are vast, diffuse beings that extend throughout the space, their consciousness distributed across multiple platforms. They move like slow thoughts given form, their shifting patterns creating subtle interference waves in the mist. These ancient ones rarely take distinct shape, preferring to exist as harmonious disturbances in the pocket's natural rhythms.

Younger Resonants appear more defined, often assuming crystalline forms that echo the vegetation. They flow between states - sometimes solid enough to interact with the physical structures, other times dispersing into pure frequency patterns that ripple through the air. Their movements create musical interactions with the environment, adding new layers to the pocket's constant symphony.

The smallest and most numerous are quicksilver beings that dart between the platforms, their forms suggesting abstract geometries in constant motion. They might be the pocket's equivalent of insects or birds, though they seem to serve a purpose in maintaining the space's intricate patterns. They gather around the luminescent veins, their movements synchronizing with the fluid's pulses in complex dances.

All Resonants communicate through modulated frequencies that manifest as overlapping patterns of light, sound, and gravitational fluctuation. Their "language" is inseparable from the pocket's own rhythms - each conversation becomes part of the environment's ongoing symphony, adding new motifs to ancient themes.

They appear to maintain the ancient structures, though not in any conventional sense. Rather, their very existence helps sustain the pocket's delicate balance, their harmonics reinforcing the space's fundamental patterns. They are both curators and exhibits, maintainers and maintained.

Most intriguing are the rare hybrid forms that seem to emerge when Resonants interact intensely with the crystal vegetation. These temporary beings blur the line between life and vibration even further, creating stunning displays of coordinated movement and light that suggest something like ritual or reproduction.

The Resonants show clear signs of intelligence and purpose, but their consciousness likely bears little resemblance to human thought. They are as much expressions of the pocket's physics as independent entities, their awareness distributed through frequency and pattern rather than contained in discrete minds.

They acknowledge visitors to their realm, though their attempts at communication can be overwhelming - a symphony of coordinated lights and sounds that encode meaning in ways that might take years to begin to understand. They seem neither hostile nor particularly welcoming, simply continuing their eternal patterns while incorporating new elements into their ongoing mathematical dance.
