The city is home to the Mist Tenders - a people whose very essence seems as carefully cultivated as their spiral gardens. Their skin has a subtle translucence, like morning dew on slate, and their movements carry the same measured precision as the water flowing through the city's channels.

They are not tall, but they stand very straight, with strong hands and feet adapted to gripping the smooth stone surfaces of their home. Their eyes are large and slightly iridescent, equally comfortable in bright light or deep shadow, and they rarely blink. Their hair grows in patterns that mirror the spiral forms seen throughout the city - natural whorls that they augment with intricate braiding.

The Mist Tenders live in extended family groups, though their concept of family extends beyond blood relations to include all who share responsibility for a particular section of the city. They communicate in two distinct ways: a spoken language of soft consonants and held vowels that meshes with the ambient sounds of water and wind, and a more complex system of gestures integrated so naturally into their daily movements that outsiders often miss its significance entirely.

Children begin apprenticing in city maintenance by age five, learning first to tend the moss gardens, then graduating to more complex tasks like channel clearing and stone fitting. They develop an intuitive understanding of the city's rhythms - knowing precisely when to harvest certain herbs, which sections of stone will need attention soon, how the water flow changes with the seasons.

The most respected members of their society are the Resonance Readers, who can interpret the vibrations emanating from the special intersections throughout the city. These individuals wear robes embedded with thin strips of bronze that amplify the subtle frequencies, and they spend hours in meditation at these points, their bodies becoming conduits for information that guides major decisions affecting the community.

The Mist Tenders age very slowly, living well past a hundred years while maintaining their physical capabilities. They attribute this longevity to the unique properties of their water supply, which passes through and is transformed by the sacred resonance points. They consume a diet heavy in the mushrooms and herbs they cultivate, eating very little meat except for certain cave-dwelling fish they raise in underground pools.

There's an almost symbiotic relationship between the Mist Tenders and their city. They seem to draw energy directly from the stone itself, becoming more animated and focused when in contact with the original structural elements. They can navigate perfectly in complete darkness, guided by their sensitivity to the building stones' subtle vibrations. Some say they can even communicate across distances by tapping specific sequences on the stone walls, though they're generally private about such abilities.

Each Mist Tender maintains a personal collection of small stones gathered from significant moments in their lives. These are kept in fabric pouches worn close to the skin, and the stones are said to hold memories that can be accessed through touch. The older a Mist Tender grows, the more stones they carry, until the most elderly sometimes wear nets of pouches that chime softly as they walk.

Through generations of living in harmony with their environment, the Mist Tenders have developed an almost architectural quality to their own existence - each individual fitting precisely into the greater structure of their society, just as each stone in their city walls locks perfectly with its neighbors.
