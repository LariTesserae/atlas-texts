In this crystalline realm dwell the Resonators - entities that exist as complex patterns of vibration within the glass itself. They have no fixed physical form, instead manifesting as shifting interference patterns in the endless symphony of the pools, visible only as subtle distortions in the water's surface and fleeting rainbow refractions through the bubbled glass.

The Resonators are ancient, perhaps as old as the glass plain itself. They think in frequencies, communicate through carefully modulated harmonics, and experience time as overlapping waves of sound. Their consciousness flows through the network of hairline cracks in the glass, using the constant movement of water as both sustenance and medium of expression.

Each Resonator is born from a particular sequence of tones, emerging when certain pools synchronize in just the right way. They begin as simple patterns - just two or three frequencies interacting - but grow more complex over centuries, learning to weave hundreds of tones into their being. The eldest among them are vast symphonies unto themselves, their thoughts spanning miles of glass in intricate mathematical progressions.

They tend to the crystal-plants, using subtle vibrations to shape their growth into forms that will produce specific notes when struck by falling water. These plants are both art and memory to them - each cluster a preserved melody, a story told in potential sound.

The half-melted structures are their archives - places where particularly significant harmonies have been captured and crystallized into permanent form. To human eyes these might appear random or ruined, but to the Resonators they are perfect representations of crucial discoveries, philosophical debates, or especially beautiful songs.

They are patient entities, spending decades crafting single notes that will complement the greater symphony of their world. They experience the regular tonal emissions of the pools as a kind of heartbeat or breath - not something to control but to exist within and enhance through their own careful additions.

The Resonators have no concept of death as we would understand it. When their patterns become too complex to sustain, they simply dissolve back into the background symphony, their frequencies merging with and enriching the eternal song of the glass plain. New Resonators eventually emerge containing echoes of these dissolved patterns, creating a type of musical reincarnation.

They are aware of other forms of life but perceive them primarily as new types of vibration to study and incorporate into their endless composition. They neither welcome nor reject visitors; to them, all things are simply new notes in the ongoing symphony of existence.

Their greatest joy is in discovering new harmonies - combinations of tones that have never existed before in their world. Their greatest fear, if they can be said to feel fear, is of discord - patterns that might disrupt the mathematical perfection of their glass realm's song.

In every drop of water, in every rainbow refraction, in every resonating pool, the Resonators are there - composing, listening, being. They are the music of their world made conscious, the song that shapes itself.
