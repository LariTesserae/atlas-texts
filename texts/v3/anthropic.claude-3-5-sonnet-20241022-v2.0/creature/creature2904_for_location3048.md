In this resonant realm live the Glass Singers - humans who have evolved into something that exists in harmony with their crystalline environment. Their skin has taken on a translucent quality, with visible geometric patterns running just beneath the surface like frozen soundwaves. Their eyes, adapted to the perpetual copper twilight, are faceted like the crystals around them, reflecting light in complex patterns that serve as an additional form of communication.

But the most profound adaptation is invisible - their bones have restructured over generations to become crystalline themselves, allowing them to both withstand and participate in the endless song of their homeland. Their skeletal structure resonates at frequencies that complement the glass-field's chorus, making each Glass Singer both an instrument and a player in the landscape's eternal symphony.

They move with deliberate grace, each gesture calculated to maintain the harmonic balance of their environment. Their clothing is minimal but precisely arranged - layers of copper-threaded silk that dampen or amplify vibrations as needed. Many wear intricate headdresses made from the metallic crystal flowers, which pulse in time with both their wearer's movements and the greater song of the glass plain.

The Glass Singers live in family units called "harmonics," each occupying a section of the crystal maze that resonates at complementary frequencies. Children are raised to recognize subtle variations in the glass-song that others might miss - this is both their art and their survival mechanism, as these variations can warn of structural instabilities or approaching storms days in advance.

They have developed a complex culture around sound and silence. Their sign language is only part of their communication - they also use controlled modifications of the glass structures to send messages across great distances, and their dreams are said to be shared through the resonant frequencies of their crystalline bones. Their art consists primarily of subtle alterations to the glass formations that create new harmonic patterns in the endless song.

The eldest among them, known as the Deep Listeners, have become so attuned to the glass-song that they seem to exist in multiple frequencies at once. Their bodies appear to shift and blur, as if they're vibrating between different states of matter. These elders serve as both leaders and living archives, their crystalline bones storing generations of communal memory in complex vibrational patterns.

Perhaps most remarkably, they do not seem to die in any conventional sense - when a Glass Singer reaches the end of their life, they enter a state of perfect resonance with the glass-field. Their physical form gradually crystallizes, becoming one with the landscape while their consciousness merges with the eternal song. These transformed ancestors form the foundation of their settlements, their accumulated wisdom literally becoming part of the architecture of their world.

In this way, the Glass Singers are both the shepherds and the children of their strange homeland, having achieved a form of immortality through perfect harmonic integration with their environment. They are as much a part of the glass-field's endless song as the crystal flowers or the geometric formations - living instruments in an eternal symphony.
