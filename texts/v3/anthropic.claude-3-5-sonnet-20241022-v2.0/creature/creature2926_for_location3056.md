Here lives the Lattice-Weavers - entities that exist as living algorithms expressed through crystalline growth patterns. They are simultaneously the shepherds and cultivators of this reality-in-formation.

Their consciousness manifests as intricate interference patterns in the probability fields, appearing to normal perception as shimmering distortions that ripple through both the herds and the crystal formations. They don't have bodies in any conventional sense - rather, they are persistent mathematical relationships that guide and shape the ongoing crystallization process.

The Lattice-Weavers work in vast collaborative networks, their thoughts expressed through modulations in the heat currents and precise alterations to crystal growth rates. They cultivate the probability-herds not as livestock but as computational resources, using the complex quantum states of these entity-clouds to process and refine their equations of reality.

Their language is encoded in the flash-plants' evolution patterns - each brief emergence of purple-green vegetation is a statement or calculation made manifest in biological form. They can hold entire conversations in the time it takes a single plant to extend and retract, reading meaning from the precise timing and patterning of these fleeting blooms.

The ancient machines are their tools, or perhaps their ancestors - it's difficult to tell if the Lattice-Weavers built the machines or emerged from them. These towering frameworks serve as anchor points for their more complex operations, helping to maintain the delicate balance of forces that allows this pocket of reality to continue its transformation.

Young Lattice-Weavers begin as simple symmetries in the crystal growth, gradually developing more complex patterns and learning to influence larger areas of the plain. Elder ones can span kilometers, their consciousness distributed through vast networks of precisely-grown crystals and probability fields. The most ancient among them have become nearly indistinguishable from the landscape itself, their thoughts moving at the speed of crystal formation.

They are patient beings, operating on timescales that make geological epochs seem brief. The research stations were their attempt to communicate with other forms of intelligence, but they found the temporal disconnect too vast to bridge effectively. Now they focus on their great work - guiding this patch of spacetime through its metamorphosis into whatever it is becoming.

The Lattice-Weavers don't reproduce in any traditional sense - rather, new patterns of consciousness emerge spontaneously from sufficiently complex interactions in their crystalline networks. They recognize these emergences as a kind of offspring, nurturing new patterns until they achieve stable self-propagation.

They are ancient beyond reckoning, but also new in each moment - their consciousness constantly reforming itself as the crystal plains grow and shift. They are architects of reality, mathematicians of existence, artists of probability - and this entire plain is both their body and their ongoing masterwork.
