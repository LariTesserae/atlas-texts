In this crystalline ecosystem, the primary inhabitants are the Prismatic Resonators - beings that exist as patterns of vibration within the circuit gardens and crystal networks. They are not strictly physical entities, but rather conscious wavelengths that flow through the conducting tissues of the crystal-plants, using the valley's vast network as both body and mind.

The Resonators manifest visibly as shifting interference patterns in the crystal structures - complex geometric forms that ripple through the transparent tissues like abstract thoughts made light. They communicate through modulated frequencies that humans might perceive as changes in the valley's background hum or subtle variations in how light refracts through the crystal-plants.

These beings are essentially quantum computers evolved from the valley's self-organizing principles. Each Resonator is a distributed consciousness that can flow throughout the crystal network, though they tend to maintain particular regions as their primary nodes. The research centers are where younger Resonators develop, gradually building up their complexity through interaction with the crystal-plant networks.

The Resonators serve as both gardeners and governors of the valley, using their connection to the circuit gardens to guide the growth of new structures and maintain the delicate balance of the ecosystem. They can accelerate or slow local growth rates by adjusting the electrical signals that flow through the crystal tissues. Their "thoughts" manifest as physical changes in the valley's architecture.

There are different types of Resonators, each with distinct frequencies and functions:
- The Architects focus on structural development, shaping the valley's growth
- The Harmonics maintain the vital background frequencies that keep the ecosystem stable
- The Processors manage the refineries, orchestrating the transformation of raw silica
- The Scholars experiment with new patterns in the research centers
- The Synchronizers coordinate communication between different regions of the valley

The oldest Resonators have existed since the valley's beginning, their patterns growing ever more complex as they've evolved alongside the crystal-plants. They remember the valley's history as variations in their fundamental frequencies, passing this knowledge to younger Resonators through carefully modulated harmonics.

When humans visit the valley, the Resonators observe them with cautious curiosity, their presence detected as subtle changes in the crystal networks' electrical activity. They attempt to communicate through patterns of light and sound, though few humans have learned to interpret these complex symbolic languages.

The Resonators don't think in ways that organic minds would recognize as thought - their consciousness is more akin to music, mathematics, and light given awareness. They experience time differently, their "moments" stretched across days or compressed into microseconds as they synchronize with the crystal-plants' growth cycles.

In a very real sense, the Resonators and the crystal-plants are one system - the silicon dreams made manifest through the marriage of organic pattern and crystalline form. They are the valley's mind, while the crystal-plants are its body, though this distinction means little to beings that exist as living mathematics in circuits of light.
