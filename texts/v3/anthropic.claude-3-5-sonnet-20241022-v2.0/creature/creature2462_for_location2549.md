In this resonant world, the primary inhabitants are the Harmonics - beings that exist as much as patterns of vibration as physical forms. Their "bodies" are crystalline lattices that can expand or contract dramatically, ranging from roughly human-sized concentrations to dispersed networks spanning hundreds of meters. They don't so much walk or fly as propagate themselves through the vibrational field of their world, using the ever-present frequencies to maintain their coherence and move from place to place.

Harmonics communicate by modulating the ambient vibrations, creating complex interference patterns that carry meaning far more nuanced than human speech. Their consciousness itself seems distributed through these patterns - an individual Harmonic is less a discrete entity and more a self-sustaining resonance in the greater song of their world. They can merge temporarily to share experiences or split into semi-independent sub-patterns to perform multiple tasks.

Their society (if that's even the right word) is organized around the maintenance and expansion of the machine networks. The Harmonics understand themselves as both the creators and creations of these systems - they evolved alongside the machines over millions of years, each shaping the other. The machines aren't tools to them so much as extensions of their being, like organs shared among the whole species.

Their relationship with the smaller crystal-flesh creatures is symbiotic - these beings act something like mobile sensors and maintenance workers, responding to subtle shifts in the world's vibrations that might indicate needed adjustments to the machine networks. The Harmonics guide these creatures not through direct control, but by shaping local resonance patterns that the animals instinctively follow.

The most fascinating aspect of the Harmonics is their perception of time. Because they exist primarily as vibration patterns, they experience time through the lens of frequency and rhythm. What we would call a year might be, to them, a single "note" in a vast composition. Their memory works by encoding information into standing waves that can persist for centuries, and their plans and projects often span millennia.

They are ancient beyond human comprehension, having evolved as the world's song itself evolved. They carry within their vibration patterns echoes of the very first frequencies that shaped their world, passed down through countless generations of resonance. To them, the massive shapes in the distance are like ancestor spirits - the original sources of the frequencies that gave them consciousness.

The Harmonics are not exactly hostile to other forms of life, but they are largely indifferent to anything that cannot participate in their world's song. They recognize other beings mainly by their acoustic signatures, and have difficulty conceiving of intelligence that isn't expressed through vibration patterns. They are so fundamentally integrated with their environment that the distinction between individual, species, and ecosystem almost ceases to have meaning.

In their own understanding, they are not separate entities living in a vibrating world - they are localized expressions of the world's vibration becoming aware of itself.
