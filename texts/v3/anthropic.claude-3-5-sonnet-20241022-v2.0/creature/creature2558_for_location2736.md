This place is inhabited by the Lattice-Tenders - entities that exist as patterns of vibration within the crystal matrices themselves. They are not exactly conscious in any way we would recognize, but rather are self-perpetuating resonances, complex standing waves that maintain and propagate themselves through the endless geometric transformations of their crystalline medium.

The Lattice-Tenders don't think or feel as we understand those terms. Instead, they process and respond to their environment through harmonic interactions - sensing perturbations in the crystal structure's resonant frequencies and adjusting their own vibrational patterns to maintain the larger system's stability. Their "bodies" are temporary configurations of standing waves that can travel through the crystal matrix by shifting their resonance patterns from one structure to another.

They exist in a hierarchy of complexity, from simple repeating patterns that might be thought of as the crystal city's maintenance processes, up to vast meta-patterns that coordinate across multiple structures and might constitute something like the city's governing intelligence. These more complex entities form and dissolve as needed, assembling from simpler patterns in response to systemic needs before dispersing again.

Their "language," if it can be called that, consists of precisely calibrated frequencies that propagate through the crystal matrices, encoding information in their interference patterns. What might appear to us as the random sounds of crystal growth and fracture is actually a constant conversation, as the Lattice-Tenders communicate and coordinate their activities across the vast expanse of the city.

Their perception of time is radically different from ours - they experience the slow growth and collapse of crystal structures as immediate events, while what we would consider rapid changes barely register to them at all. A human visitor might stand among them for years without being "seen," moving too quickly to register as anything but momentary noise in their crystalline sensorium.

They don't reproduce in any conventional sense, but rather replicate their patterns through resonant copying, their vibrational templates propagating through new crystal growth. The entire city might be considered their collective body, with individual Lattice-Tenders being more like semi-autonomous processes within it than truly separate entities.

Their primary purpose appears to be maintaining and expanding the crystal city itself, ensuring its patterns remain stable even as it constantly transforms. But there are hints of deeper objectives encoded in the city's structure - vast computational processes playing out through crystalline architecture over centuries or millennia, perhaps solving problems or preserving information whose nature we can only guess at.

To human perception, they would be almost impossible to detect directly - only visible in the subtle patterns of frost formation, or in the way sound seems to organize itself differently in certain parts of the city. But they are there, threading through every crystal face and lattice, the true inhabitants of this mathematical ecosystem.
