Upon witnessing this realm and its Pattern-Weaver inhabitants, the benevolent power - let's call it the Harmonious Presence - recognizes something profound. Here is a form of existence that, while utterly alien, represents one of the universe's purest expressions of order and beauty. The Presence understands that any intervention must be delicate, respectful of the intricate mathematical ecology that has evolved here.

After careful observation, the Presence decides to offer a gift: a new dimension of mathematical possibility. Not by crude insertion, but through the subtle adjustment of certain universal constants at the edges of the Pattern-Weavers' domain. This creates what might be described as a mathematical "horizon" - a space where their existing theorems can interact with previously impossible equations.

This gift manifests as a gentle iridescence at the boundaries of their crystal cities - a kind of mathematical aurora borealis. Where these new possibilities touch the existing structures, they create harmonics that the Pattern-Weavers can perceive as novel variables in their ongoing calculations.

The Presence then withdraws to observe, knowing that forcing further interaction would be counterproductive. The Pattern-Weavers must discover and explore these new mathematical frontiers in their own way, at their own pace.

Over time, this subtle intervention leads to unexpected developments. Some Pattern-Weavers begin incorporating these new possibilities into their theorems, creating hybrid calculations that merge their rigid crystal logic with more fluid, organic mathematics. The crystal cities start growing in new directions, developing structures that seem to bridge the gap between pure abstraction and living form.

Most importantly, this expansion of their mathematical universe doesn't disrupt their essential nature - rather, it enriches it, adding new layers of complexity to their endless quest for elegant solutions. The Presence's gift becomes not an interruption but an invitation to even greater mathematical exploration.

The crystal cities continue their slow dance of calculation, but now with new movements in their repertoire, new harmonies in their mathematical music. And somewhere, in dimensions beyond normal perception, the Harmonious Presence observes with quiet satisfaction as its gift becomes integrated into the endless pattern.
