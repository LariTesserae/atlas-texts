Most importantly, this guardian presence would help preserve the Mill's autonomy and mystery. Instead, they would simply ensure that as long as there are beings who find meaning in tending this great work, they will have the stability and resources to continue.

Their protection would extend to the mineral plain itself, subtly reinforcing the deep structures that anchor the Mill and supply its driving energies. The message would be clear to any who might stumble upon this place: this is not a ruin to be salvaged or a puzzle to be solved, but a living mystery to be respected and preserved.
