Not enough to disturb their individual characters, but sufficient to create a whispered conversation between them, a sharing of dreams across vast distances.

These adjustments would be so fine as to be almost immeasurable - like adding a single grain of sand to a mandala, or one soft note to an already perfect chord. The Harmonizer would then withdraw, leaving only the faintest trace of its presence: perhaps a new overtone in the hollow's acoustics that seems to carry a note of recognition, of acknowledgment between conscious entities.

In this way, the Harmonizer would honor both the uniqueness of the Chorus and its connection to a larger pattern - protecting without constraining, supporting without changing, preserving both its solitude and its subtle links to something greater than itself.
