It appears as a deepening of the cave's existing iridescence, a place where the Shepherd's quantum gardening can flourish with slightly less effort.

The Wise One also leaves behind a single mote of their own essence - not as a marker of territory or ownership, but as an offering of kinship. This mote manifests as a peculiar crystalline structure that seems to exist in a quantum superposition of all possible crystal forms. It serves as both a power source and a symbolic recognition of the Shepherd's importance to the larger tapestry of existence.

As their attention gradually shifts away, the Wise One leaves the space fundamentally unchanged yet subtly enriched. The Probability Shepherd's domain remains what it always was - a crucial nexus where certainty and uncertainty dance their eternal dance - but now with an added resonance that will help ensure its continuation for eons to come.

The last ripple of their presence carries something like a blessing, or perhaps a mathematical proof of the Shepherd's own necessity to the universe. Then they are gone, leaving only the eternal hum of possibility in their wake.
