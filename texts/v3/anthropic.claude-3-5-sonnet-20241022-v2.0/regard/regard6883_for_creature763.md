Over centuries, the Rootweaver may gradually decode these records through the crystal's interaction with its fungal networks, incorporating new patterns into its endless dance of life and awareness.

The Gardner's touch is so light that even the sensitive Rootweaver might only register it as a particularly vivid dream, spreading through its network over many cycles of condensation and flow.
