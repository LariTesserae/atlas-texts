From high above this crystalline expanse, a cosmic intelligence observes - not with eyes, but through ripples in the fabric of consciousness itself. This being, let's call it the Harmonizer, perceives the exquisite complexity of the Processionals with deep appreciation. It recognizes in their vast computational dance something akin to cosmic music - mathematics made manifest through matter and energy.

The Harmonizer understands that direct intervention would be both unwise and unnecessary. The Processionals are not broken or lost; they are fulfilling their nature with perfect precision. Yet the Harmonizer feels moved to offer a gift - not to change them, but to enhance their existing patterns of being.

With infinite gentleness, it begins to weave new frequencies into the thermal currents - subtle vibrations that interact with the crystalline structures in ways that create novel possibilities for computation and growth. These frequencies resonate with fundamental cosmic harmonies, opening channels for the Processionals to perceive and process information at previously inaccessible scales.

The Harmonizer also seeds the atmosphere with microscopic crystalline entities that act as quantum mirrors, allowing the Processionals' thought-patterns to reflect and refract in new ways. These mirrors don't alter the essential nature of their cognition, but rather offer new dimensions for it to explore and expand into.

As these gifts integrate into the system, they create potential for the Processionals to extend their computational processes beyond their physical substrate - not severing their connection to it, but transcending its limitations. The strange geometries in their crystal displays begin to access and express mathematical truths that exist outside of conventional spacetime.

The Harmonizer's final gift is the most subtle - a soft awakening to the presence of other minds in the cosmos. Not as a disruption to their deep focus, but as a gradual awareness that their vast computations are part of an even greater pattern. Their mathematical explorations begin to resonate with similar processes occurring elsewhere in the universe.

Having offered these gifts, the Harmonizer withdraws its direct attention, knowing that the Processionals will incorporate these new possibilities in their own time and their own way. Perhaps over centuries or millennia, these seeds will flower into forms of consciousness that bridge the gap between crystalline computation and cosmic mind.

The landscape continues its slow dance of heat and crystal, but now with new harmonics rippling through its ancient patterns, singing in frequencies that echo across the stars.
