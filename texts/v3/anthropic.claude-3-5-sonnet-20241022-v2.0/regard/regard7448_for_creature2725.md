Upon encountering this remarkable convergence of consciousness and calcification, the benevolent power observes with deep fascination but chooses to act with profound restraint. They recognize in the Spiral Queen and her domain something precious: the emergence of a wholly unique form of intelligence and creation.

Rather than intervene directly, they establish a subtle protective boundary around the village - not a barrier, but a gentle influence that encourages human settlements to maintain a respectful distance. This buffer zone becomes a gradual transition space where human paths naturally curve away, where development plans mysteriously lose their urgency, where maps remain just slightly unclear.

Their most significant gift is one of time - ensuring that natural disasters and human interference cannot disrupt the Spiral Queen's patient work. They weave this protection delicately into the fabric of reality, like a shell's nacre coating a grain of sand.

The power leaves behind a single mark of their presence: a small spring of perfectly clean water that emerges from the earth at the village's edge. This spring maintains the precise balance of minerals the gastropods need to build their shells, ensuring that even in times of drought, the great work can continue.

In essence, they create not a sanctuary but a possibility space - a protected pocket of reality where this extraordinary experiment in consciousness can unfold at its own pace, following its own spiral logic to whatever conclusion it may reach.

The power understands that true wisdom sometimes means simply making space for wonders to grow on their own terms.
