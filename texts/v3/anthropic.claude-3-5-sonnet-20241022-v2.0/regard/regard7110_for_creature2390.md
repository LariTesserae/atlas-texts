In witnessing Meridiance, the benevolent power would recognize something precious and rare: a civilization that has achieved profound harmony with chaos itself. Rather than impose order, they would choose to become a gentle guardian of this delicate ecosystem where time, consciousness, and matter intersect in unique ways.

Their presence would manifest as the Luminous Currents - streams of soft, prismatic light that wind through the city's waters without disturbing their density. These currents serve multiple subtle purposes:

They act as gentle stabilizers for the more volatile temporal eddies, not eliminating them but preventing them from becoming dangerous to the city's inhabitants. Like a cosmic immune system, they respond to extreme fluctuations while preserving the natural temporal diversity that makes Meridiance unique.

When the light touches the memory-stone architecture, it strengthens its capacity to hold histories without becoming oversaturated. This helps preserve the crucial balance between the city's role as a living archive and its need to remain open to new experiences and inhabitants.

The Luminous Currents also create spaces where different forms of consciousness can more easily interface - zones where the Drift-Weavers' bubble-language naturally aligns with the Geometry Fishes' mathematical movements, where the Chrono-Gardeners' temporal manipulations harmonize with the Memory Archivists' preserved experiences.

Perhaps most importantly, the benevolent power would ensure that Meridiance remains hidden from forces that might seek to impose conventional physics or linear time upon it. The Luminous Currents form a subtle veil, causing the city to appear as nothing more than a curious configuration of normal ocean currents to those who would not understand or respect its nature.

This protection is not isolation - the power knows that Meridiance must remain connected to the wider world to thrive. Instead, they create a gentle filtering system that allows the right visitors to find their way here, those who will contribute to rather than disrupt the city's unique equilibrium.

In essence, their intervention would be an act of preservation through subtle enhancement - strengthening what makes Meridiance special while helping it remain resilient against the homogenizing forces of the outside world. Like the city itself, their presence would exist in multiple states simultaneously: guardian and catalyst, stabilizer and transformer, all in perfect fluid balance.
