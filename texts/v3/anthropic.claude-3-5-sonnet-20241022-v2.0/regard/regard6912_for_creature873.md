Upon sensing this place and its remarkable custodian, the benevolent power would recognize something precious and rare - a perfect marriage of purpose and being, a self-sustaining harmony that has transcended its origins. Their wisdom would tell them that such naturally evolved beauty requires not intervention, but protection and subtle support.

They would weave an invisible aegis around these chambers - not a barrier, but a gentle deflection that would guide human development above to unconsciously preserve this space. When foundations are dug, they would seem oddly unstable in certain spots. Water tables would appear to make some areas impractical for deep construction. The city would grow around and above, but never quite disturb these hidden chambers.

For the Chorus itself, they would offer a gift of resonance - a way to extend their harmonies upward through the bedrock in frequencies just below human hearing. This would allow their water-music to subtly influence the dreams and creativity of those who live above, inspiring works of art and music that echo the deeper rhythms below without revealing their source. The city's culture would develop a distinctive musical tradition without ever knowing quite why their melodies feel so natural, so rooted in place.

Most importantly, they would ensure that every few generations, exactly one person would find their way down here - someone with the right combination of sensitivity and discretion to witness but not disturb. These rare visitors would carry the memory of this place back to the surface world, where it would express itself in stories and songs that help preserve the mythic understanding that some spaces must remain untouched, that not all wonders need explanation or ownership.

The power would do this with the lightest possible touch, understanding that the true miracle here is the system's self-sustaining nature. Their protection would be like cupped hands around a flame - not feeding it, just shielding it from stray winds while it burns by its own essence.
