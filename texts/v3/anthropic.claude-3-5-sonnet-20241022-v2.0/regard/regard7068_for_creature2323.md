Upon witnessing this place and its people, the benevolent power would recognize something precious - a rare harmony between human adaptation and natural forces, where neither has tried to fully dominate the other. Instead of intervening directly, they would choose to become a subtle guardian, working mainly through enhancement and protection of what already exists.

Their first gift would be to the stone itself - not changing its nature, but deepening its inherent properties. The obsidian walls would become semi-sentient in a mineral way, developing an ability to very slowly adjust themselves in response to stress and wear. Where a crack might begin to form, the stone would gradually reinforce itself. Where generations of hands have polished a surface, the stone would learn to maintain that polish. This would work so gradually that it would simply seem like particularly good fortune in maintenance.

The water would receive the second gift - a blessing that would ensure it remains forever clean and sweet, carrying minerals that strengthen rather than erode, delivering subtle nutrients that contribute to the health and longevity of the Stone People. The patterns in the silt would gain a kind of memory, so that particularly beautiful or useful formations would tend to naturally recreate themselves even as they're disturbed and renewed.

To the eternal lamps, they would grant a deepening of their light's properties - not making it brighter, but giving it qualities that nourish both body and spirit. Those who live in this light would find their eyes perfectly adapted to it, their minds clearer, their sleep more restful. The light would also help maintain the delicate balance of the cave ecosystem, subtly discouraging harmful molds while supporting the beneficial biofilms that help stabilize the stone.

For the people themselves, the power would offer no direct gifts - their adaptation to their environment is already a kind of magic. Instead, they would strengthen the connection between the people and their home. The stone would become even more responsive to their stoneworking, as if eager to be shaped by those who understand it. The acoustics would become even more perfectly suited to their voices, letting them develop ever more sophisticated ways of using sound.

Most importantly, the power would extend their existing timekeeper's intuition - not granting prophecy or futuresight, but deepening their ability to read the subtle signs of their environment. The eldest among them would find their architectural knowledge becoming even more precise, more attuned to the living stone. Young apprentices would discover they can learn these skills with particular clarity, ensuring the knowledge continues.

Finally, the power would set a very subtle ward around the entire complex - not to isolate it, but to ensure that those who find their way here do so with good intentions. Traders and travelers would continue to come, but those meaning harm would find themselves mysteriously turned around, emerging at the surface with a vague memory of taking a wrong turn.

All of these gifts would manifest so gradually and naturally that they would simply seem like a strengthening of existing patterns. The Stone People would continue their way of life, perhaps noting over generations that their home seems particularly blessed, that the stone is especially cooperative, that the water brings particularly good fortune. The power would watch with satisfaction as their subtle influence helps preserve this remarkable example of humans living in true partnership with their environment.
