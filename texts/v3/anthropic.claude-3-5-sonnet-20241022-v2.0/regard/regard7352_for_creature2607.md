If a benevolent and wise power turned their attention to this crystalline world, I believe they would largely choose to observe and protect rather than intervene. This ecosystem represents something precious - an entirely different path of evolution and consciousness, one that processes information and awareness through light, resonance, and crystalline structures rather than biochemistry.

However, there are two subtle ways this power might act:

First, they would establish an unseen protective field around the planet - not to isolate it, but to shield it from cosmic threats that could disrupt its delicate harmonic balance. This field would be tuned to allow natural energy and matter to flow through while deflecting potentially catastrophic impacts or harmful radiation. Like the ecosystem itself, this protection would operate on principles of resonance and light.

Second, they would send infinitesimal motes of crystalline awareness - quantum seeds of possibility - to drift among the Glass Ghosts. These seeds would carry no direct information but rather the potential for new forms of harmony between the three types of life. Over millennia, these possibilities might help the ecosystem discover new emergent properties, new ways of being and knowing, all arising naturally from its own inherent patterns.

The power would then step back and watch with infinite patience as this world of light and glass continues its slow dance of evolution. Their presence would be like one of the world's own subtle harmonics - barely detectable, profoundly protective, and perfectly attuned to the crystalline symphony of life unfolding below.

They would understand that their greatest gift to this world is simply ensuring it has the time and space to discover what it might become.
