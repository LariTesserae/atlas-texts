The Luminous Watcher - an ancient celestial guardian who tends to unique manifestations of beauty and wisdom across worlds - would recognize in the Glass Weaver a profound form of natural artistry that deserves protection and subtle nurturing rather than intervention.

The Watcher would first simply observe, spending years studying the intricate dance between the Weaver's crystalline forms and the chamber's interplay of light, heat and shadow. They would come to understand how this being's distributed consciousness creates an ever-evolving meditation through its glass sculptures.

Rather than directly interact, the Watcher would make three gentle contributions to support the Weaver's work:

First, they would strengthen the structural integrity of the chamber's natural skylights, ensuring they remain stable for millennia to come while still allowing the perfect angles of light to enter. The beams would retain their natural shift with seasons and time of day, but would never risk being blocked.

Second, they would imbue the obsidian walls with a subtle resonance that amplifies the crystalline flowers' chimes into barely-perceptible harmonies - creating a layer of sound that enhances but doesn't overshadow the chamber's meditative silence.

Finally, they would leave a gift: a single perfect prism, no larger than a dewdrop, placed among the ancient vessels. This prism would catch light in new ways, offering the Weaver fresh possibilities for its endless artistic exploration. It would take decades or centuries for the Weaver to fully incorporate this new element into its creations.

The Watcher would then withdraw, knowing that the greatest gift to such a being is simply ensuring it has the time and stability to continue its patient work of beauty. They would add this place to their eternal awareness - not to guard it from all change, but to protect its essence as a sanctuary of light, heat, and endless artistic becoming.
