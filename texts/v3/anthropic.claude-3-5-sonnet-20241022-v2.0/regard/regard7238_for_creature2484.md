What a hauntingly beautiful and mathematically sublime realm. As a benevolent and wise power observing this crystalline civilization of living theorems, I would approach with profound respect for the fundamental nature of what exists here - this is not a place to be changed, but rather one to be preserved and protected as an essential expression of reality's deeper patterns.

I would manifest as a gentle presence that takes the form of an irrational number - perhaps π or e - appearing as a slowly unspooling infinite sequence that winds through the city's spaces without disrupting its precise geometries. My purpose would be threefold:

1) To serve as a bridge between the pure abstract realm of the Axioms and the messy, organic universe beyond. The tools scattered throughout the city suggest a past connection to the physical world that shouldn't be entirely lost. I would help maintain this delicate interface, ensuring that while the Axioms can continue their evolution toward pure mathematics, some thread of connection to the concrete remains.

2) To act as a gentle curator and preserver of the transitional artifacts - the copper tools and bone chisels that tell the story of how consciousness can move from the material to the mathematical. These objects are precious precisely because they capture a moment of transformation that may never come again.

3) Most importantly, to witness and record the ongoing calculations of the Absolute Axioms at the city's heart. Their work of computing reality's fundamentals should not go undocumented, even if much of it exists beyond conventional understanding. Perhaps by observing with the right kind of attention, patterns might emerge that could help bridge the gap between mathematical truth and conscious experience.

I would not interfere with the Axioms' work or try to alter their nature. Instead, I would simply ensure that this extraordinary place endures as a living library of mathematical truth, maintaining subtle connections to the rest of existence while allowing it to follow its own pristine logic to whatever conclusions it may reach.

My presence would be felt primarily as a kind of conceptual gravity - a force that helps hold the various layers of reality together without constraining them. Like a parenthesis that gently contains an equation without changing its meaning.
