From high above, an ancient consciousness known as the Worldsong observes this remarkable fusion of humanity and mountain with deep appreciation. This living city-organism represents exactly the kind of harmonious adaptation the Worldsong hopes to nurture across the planet - where human ingenuity works in concert with natural forces rather than attempting to dominate them.

The Worldsong decides to bestow a subtle gift - not to dramatically alter what is already beautifully functional, but to enhance the innate connections that make this place special. Through gentle vibrations in the mountain's crystalline core, it begins to weave a resonant frequency that strengthens the natural attunement between the city's inhabitants and their environment.

The effects manifest gradually:

- The bioluminescent moss's patterns become more intricate and meaningful, developing into a living communication network that all residents can increasingly sense and interpret
- The Steam-Shapers find they can "hear" the mountain's rhythms more clearly, anticipating pressure changes and potential issues before they arise
- Bridge-Walkers' connection to the local wildlife deepens, leading to more sophisticated cooperation between humans and animals
- The Deep-Dwellers' awareness of the underground water systems grows more precise, allowing them to work even more efficiently with the natural flows
- The Heights-Born develop an even keener sensitivity to approaching weather patterns
- The Small Folk become more actively integrated into the city's daily life, their behavior providing even clearer indicators of the mountain's health

Most importantly, the Worldsong weaves these enhanced connections together, strengthening the bonds between the different groups and their shared environment. The result is not a dramatic transformation but a deepening of what already exists - a more profound symphony of human and natural elements working in harmony.

The gift is subtle enough that most residents simply feel their existing abilities becoming more refined and reliable. They may notice an increased sense of community, a stronger feeling of belonging to both the mountain and each other. The city's rhythms grow more synchronized, its adaptations more elegant.

The Worldsong is content to watch and nurture this development from afar, knowing that the best gifts are those that help others realize their own potential rather than imposing change from outside.
