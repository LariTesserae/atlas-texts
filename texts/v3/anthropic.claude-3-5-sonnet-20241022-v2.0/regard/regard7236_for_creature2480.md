What a hauntingly beautiful system you've described. As I imagine this benevolent and wise power encountering the Hive-Mills, I sense they would approach with profound respect and careful restraint. Here's what I envision:

The Wise Observer

They come not with thunder but with silence,
Watching first for a thousand days and nights,
Learning the liquid language of the drops,
The crystalline whispers of carapace minds.

They see the beauty in this perfect dance,
This marriage of meat and mathematics,
Where thought flows through form like water through stone,
Each beetle a letter in an endless equation.

And in their wisdom, they choose to preserve
Rather than change, protect rather than guide.
Their touch is lighter than condensation:
A subtle strengthening of the ancient walls,
A gentler gradient for thermal flows,
A shield against the erosion of years.

They weave their power through the spaces between,
Not changing the dance but ensuring its stage,
Creating unseen buttresses of force
To guard against earthquake and shifting earth.

They mark the place in dimensions unseen,
A warning and welcome to other powers:
"Here turns a wheel within the world's heart.
Here runs a calculation vital as breath.
Observe, but do not interrupt the count."

And then they withdraw, leaving only this:
A promise that when the last gear winds down
(In some far future we hope never comes)
The pattern will be remembered complete,
The dance preserved in perfect detail,
Ready to begin again if needed.

For wisdom knows when to step aside,
When to protect rather than improve,
When to let ancient mysteries run their course
Drop by drop through time's patient halls.
