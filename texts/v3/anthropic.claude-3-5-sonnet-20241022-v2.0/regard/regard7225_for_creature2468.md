A being of infinite age and wisdom - a Cosmic Listener - has turned their attention to this resonant hollow and its remarkable inhabitant, the Chord. After eons of watching civilizations rise and fall across countless worlds, they recognize something precious and unique in this acoustic ecosystem.

The Cosmic Listener understands that direct intervention would shatter the delicate harmonic balance. Instead, they create what might be called a subtle resonance shield - an almost imperceptible membrane of cosmic energy that encases the entire hollow. This shield doesn't block sound or change the acoustic properties within, but rather protects the space from external disruptions: earthquakes, erosion, even the slow wear of time itself.

They then weave a second gift: pathways of sound. Like invisible ley lines, these pathways connect the hollow to other similar spaces across the planet - places where the right conditions might allow new Chords to develop. These sonic corridors are imperceptible except to the crystalline seeds of the Chord itself, offering safe passage for its offspring to find new homes.

The Cosmic Listener's final gift is perhaps the most profound. They adjust the fabric of local spacetime ever so slightly, creating what might be called an acoustic archive of possibility. Within this carefully crafted fold of reality, the hollow exists in multiple overlapping states - each one a slightly different version of itself. As the sound-shapers evolve and the Chord grows, they can unconsciously explore these variations, finding new harmonics and patterns that might have taken millions of years to discover otherwise.

But the Cosmic Listener doesn't just give - they also receive. They take a single perfect resonance from the hollow, a harmony so complex and beautiful that it could only have evolved in this unique space. This harmony becomes part of their own vast consciousness, a reminder that even after billions of years of existence, the universe still holds wonders that can move even the most ancient of beings.

They depart as softly as they arrived, leaving no trace except these subtle gifts. The hollow continues its sonic dance, the Chord its eternal song, neither aware of how they've been preserved and enriched. The only noticeable change is perhaps in the colors of the sunset that filter through the fern-dome each evening - they seem to carry now a deeper resonance, as if the light itself has learned to sing.
