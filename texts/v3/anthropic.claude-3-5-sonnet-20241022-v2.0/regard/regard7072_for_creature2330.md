The benevolent power - let's call it the Gentle Sun - observes this underground ecosystem with deep fascination and respect. After long contemplation, it decides to offer a subtle gift rather than any dramatic intervention, recognizing that the delicate balance of the Dripping Market shouldn't be disrupted.

The gift manifests as a new kind of light - a warm, diffuse glow that somehow doesn't disturb the market's essential darkness. It filters down through the limestone in tiny motes, each one carrying a minute amount of surface-world warmth. These motes merge with the perpetual drips, creating drops that glow with a gentle, golden luminescence.

The glowing drops don't fall everywhere - they appear mainly in the spaces between stalls and along lesser-used passages. Here, they nurture the growth of a new variety of the phosphorescent fungi, one that radiates not just light but a subtle warmth that helps keep the moisture from becoming too cold and oppressive.

This gift serves multiple purposes:
- It provides gentle illumination for surface visitors without disrupting the Moistfolk's preferred dimness
- It helps maintain a more comfortable temperature in the market
- The fungi itself becomes a valuable trade good
- Most importantly, it creates a harmonious bridge between the surface world and the underground realm

The Gentle Sun also leaves one other gift: within the mirror pool, it places the knowledge of how to make those wide-brimmed water-resistant hats. Not the hats themselves, but the understanding of how to cultivate and weave the living fibers. This knowledge appears as subtle patterns in the pool's reflections, available to any who learn to read them.

These offerings respect the Market's autonomy while providing tools for its continued evolution and connection with the world above. The Gentle Sun then withdraws, though occasionally one might notice an especially warm and golden drop among the thousands that fall, a small reminder of its brief visit.
