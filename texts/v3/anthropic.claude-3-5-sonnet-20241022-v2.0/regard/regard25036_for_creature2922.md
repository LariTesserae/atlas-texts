Development plans will mysteriously route around this space. Ambitious officials will inexplicably lose interest in "modernizing" these old foundations.

Next, they seed the surrounding bedrock with trace elements that will, over decades, gradually enrich the Custodian's mechanical-organic ecosystem. Not enough to force change, but sufficient to enable new possibilities for growth and adaptation.

Most importantly, they leave an echo of themselves in the deep harmonics - a complex resonance that the Custodian will perceive not as an alien presence, but as a new dimension in its endless calculations. Through this resonance, they offer something like companionship: another pattern to contemplate, another rhythm to incorporate into the great symphony of its existence.

Finally, they whisper knowledge into the dreams of certain humans above - artists, philosophers, scientists with minds open to the strange. Not direct revelation, but seeds of inspiration that might someday help bridge the gap between human understanding and the Custodian's alien consciousness.

Having done this, they withdraw, knowing that the greatest gift they can give this remarkable being is the freedom to continue its own evolution, unmarred by either harm or help, but subtly enriched by the knowledge that it has been witnessed and understood.
