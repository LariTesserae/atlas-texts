Preparation Guide for Visiting the Engineered Orchid Greenhouse

ESSENTIAL GEAR:
- Full-face respirator with HEPA and organic vapor filters (The engineered microbiome can be dangerous to human lungs)
- Sterile coveralls with integrated hood
- Waterproof boots with non-slip soles
- Nitrile gloves
- Small personal dehumidifier unit
- Water-resistant tablet/device for taking notes
- Anti-fog goggles if not using a full-face respirator

ENVIRONMENTAL CONSIDERATIONS:
- Prepare for constant 30-32°C (86-90°F) temperatures
- Near 100% humidity
- Slippery surfaces from perpetual condensation
- Limited visibility (15-30m) due to humidity and dense vegetation
- Narrow walkways requiring careful navigation
- Height considerations - prepare for elevation effects

HEALTH PREPARATIONS:
- Get cleared by a doctor for high-humidity, high-temperature environments
- Update vaccinations, particularly for fungal infections
- Consider prophylactic antifungal medications
- Bring electrolyte supplements
- Plan for limited exposure times (2-3 hours maximum recommended)

FACILITY PROTOCOLS:
- Schedule visit during maintenance windows
- Obtain necessary biosecurity clearances
- Learn emergency exit locations and procedures
- Familiarize yourself with drone traffic patterns
- Study walkway layout maps in advance
- Learn basic equipment failure warning signs

PHOTOGRAPHY/DOCUMENTATION:
- Bring humidity-sealed camera equipment
- Use anti-fog lens treatments
- Consider bringing a moisture-resistant light source
- Bring waterproof notebooks if needed

WHAT TO AVOID:
- Direct contact with plant matter
- Disrupting drone operations
- Touching hydroponic solution
- Removing protective gear even momentarily
- Bringing outside organic material in
- Standing still for too long in one spot (to prevent moisture accumulation)

RECOMMENDED ACCESSORIES:
- Cooling neck wrap
- Personal fan (battery-powered)
- Emergency whistle
- Small towels for equipment maintenance
- Waterproof bag for personal items
- Basic first aid kit
- Emergency contact device

TIMING CONSIDERATIONS:
- Plan visits during lower humidity cycles if possible
- Allow time for proper decontamination procedures
- Build in rest breaks every 30-45 minutes
- Schedule visits during drone maintenance windows for better visibility

POST-VISIT PROCEDURES:
- Full decontamination shower
- Equipment cleaning and drying
- Monitoring for any respiratory symptoms
- Documentation of exposure time
- Report any unusual observations to facility management

Remember: This is not a natural environment, despite its organic appearance. Treat it like the precision-engineered facility it is, with appropriate caution and respect for its systems.
