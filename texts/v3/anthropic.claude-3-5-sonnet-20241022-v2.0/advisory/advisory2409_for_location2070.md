Here's how I recommend preparing for a visit to the Mirror Market:

Essential Preparations:
- Wear soft-soled shoes with excellent grip for wet stone surfaces
- Bring layered clothing including a base thermal layer - temps are consistently cool
- Pack a high-quality headlamp with red light option plus backup batteries
- Bring an empty water bottle - you'll want to taste the different "flavors"
- Wear or bring dark, muted colors - bright clothing can disturb the subtle lighting
- Learn basic hand gestures used by crystal-farmers (seek a guide's help with this)

Critical Behaviors:
- Move slowly and deliberately at all times
- Speak only in whispers when necessary
- Never touch crystals without explicit permission
- Stay on marked paths between gardens
- Avoid sudden movements or loud sounds
- Be prepared for time disorientation - bring a watch but don't rely on it heavily

Recommended Equipment:
- Small notebook and pencil for observations (ink can run in the humidity)
- Waterproof bag for electronics
- Moisture-wicking underlayers
- Light felt overgarment (can be provided by guides)
- Non-flash camera (if permitted by guides)
- Small cloth for wiping condensation from lenses

Health Considerations:
- If claustrophobic, practice breathing exercises beforehand
- Bring any medications needed for 2x your planned stay
- Consider mask if sensitive to mineral dust
- Stay hydrated but be selective about which water sources you sample
- Rest frequently - the constant humidity can be taxing

Mental Preparation:
- Practice meditation or mindfulness to adjust to the slow pace
- Read about basic crystallography and cave formations
- Learn to observe subtle environmental changes
- Be prepared for altered time perception
- Study basic speleology (cave science)

Cultural Notes:
- Bring small gifts for guides (locally appropriate options can be researched)
- Learn proper greeting gestures
- Respect silence as default mode of communication
- Accept that some areas/knowledge will be off-limits
- Be prepared to move at the crystal-farmers' pace

Duration Planning:
- Allow 3x longer than you think you need
- Plan flexible departure times
- Include adjustment periods before and after
- Consider multiple visits if possible

What Not to Bring:
- Strong perfumes or scents
- Bright or reflective materials
- Sound-making devices
- Metal jewelry (can affect crystal growth)
- Food (unless specifically approved)

Remember that the Mirror Market operates on its own timescale and cultural logic. Success as a visitor depends more on adaptability and respect than on specific preparations. The most important preparation is mental: cultivating patience, observation skills, and comfort with silence.
