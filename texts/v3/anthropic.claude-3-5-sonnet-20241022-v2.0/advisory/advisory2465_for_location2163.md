Preparation Guide for Visiting the Undertide

ESSENTIAL GEAR:
- Pressure-rated exosuit with multiple redundant seals
- Minimum 72-hour emergency air supply
- Advanced filtration mask with mineral particulate protection
- Thermal regulation underlayer (focus on insulation against cold)
- Moisture-retention bodysuit
- Vibration-dampening boots with magnetic grips
- Personal beacon system with depth-penetrating signal
- Multiple light sources (chemical and electronic)
- Emergency pressure shelter (collapsible)
- Water recycling system (minimum 1 week capacity)

MEDICAL PREPARATIONS:
- Inner ear stabilization treatment (2 weeks prior to visit)
- Bone density supplements (1 month prior)
- Anti-vertigo medication
- Specialized hearing protection for sub-audible frequencies
- Emergency pressure sickness kit
- Mineral exposure treatment pack
- Enhanced electrolyte supplements

TRAINING REQUIREMENTS:
- Basic cave navigation
- Pressure environment survival
- Vibration interpretation basics (minimum 20 hours)
- Emergency shelter deployment
- Crystal formation hazard recognition
- Basic flow-pattern reading
- Emergency ascent procedures
- Chain network navigation
- Sound-based positioning

PSYCHOLOGICAL PREPARATION:
- Sensory adjustment exercises (especially for subsonic vibrations)
- Spatial disorientation training
- Long-term low-light adaptation
- Claustrophobia management
- Pattern recognition in fluid environments
- Cognitive exercises for maintaining orientation in shifting spaces

LOCAL PROTOCOLS:
- Register with Haven-Below authorities
- Obtain local guide certification
- Learn basic stone-whisper warning signs
- Memorize emergency shelter locations
- Study current flow patterns
- Learn local hand signals
- Understand bubble-city access protocols

CULTURAL CONSIDERATIONS:
- Respect water conservation customs
- Learn basic flow-miner etiquette
- Understand local navigation terminology
- Study current stability zones
- Know emergency gathering points
- Respect crystal formation preservation rules

RECOMMENDED TIMELINE:
1. Begin physical preparations 3 months before visit
2. Start medical treatments 1 month before
3. Complete training 2 weeks before
4. Arrive at Haven-Below 3 days before any deep exploration
5. Undergo local orientation
6. Start with shallow zones only
7. Graduate to deeper areas only with guide approval

ABSOLUTE PROHIBITIONS:
- Solo exploration
- Unauthorized crystal collection
- Water waste
- Interference with flow-mining operations
- Unauthorized pressure suit modifications
- Ignoring vibration warnings

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES:
- Memorize quick-pressure shelter locations
- Learn local emergency signals
- Practice rapid ascent protocols
- Know chain network emergency routes
- Understand bubble-city lockdown procedures

Remember: The Undertide demands respect and patience. No amount of preparation can completely eliminate risk, but proper preparation can mean the difference between an enlightening experience and disaster. Always defer to local expertise and never assume stability in any area, no matter how solid it may appear.
