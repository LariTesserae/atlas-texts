Preparation Guide for Visiting the Crystalline City

ESSENTIAL GEAR:
- Advanced oxygen supply system with minimum 2 weeks capacity
- Multiple layers of extreme cold weather gear (rated for -40°C or colder)
- Anti-radiation clothing and supplements
- GPS device (though effectiveness may be unreliable)
- Multiple compasses (to track spatial distortions)
- Climbing gear rated for crystalline surfaces
- Non-magnetic tools and equipment where possible
- High-energy food supplies for 3x planned stay duration
- Water purification systems (don't rely on local sources)

PHYSICAL PREPARATION:
- High-altitude training (minimum 6 months)
- Cold weather survival training
- Rock climbing certification
- Meditation practice to maintain mental stability
- Balance and spatial awareness exercises
- Eye exercises for managing multiple light sources

MENTAL/COGNITIVE PREPARATION:
- Advanced mathematics background recommended
- Study of non-Euclidean geometry
- Training in pattern recognition
- Meditation techniques for managing sensory disorientation
- Psychology preparation for extreme isolation
- Training to recognize and cope with temporal anomalies

SAFETY PROTOCOLS:
- Never travel alone
- Use tethers between group members
- Maintain physical contact with solid structures
- Document position regularly (multiple methods)
- Set up physical markers to track spatial shifts
- Establish regular reality-checking routines
- Use audio recordings to verify memory/perception

COMMUNICATION:
- Visual signaling systems (since sound behaves unusually)
- Multiple backup communication devices
- Hand signals protocol
- Written communication materials

RESEARCH EQUIPMENT:
- Multi-spectrum cameras
- Geometric mapping tools
- Crystal frequency meters
- Time dilation monitors
- Reality stability meters
- Pattern recording devices
- Multi-dimensional measuring tools

PSYCHOLOGICAL GUIDELINES:
- Accept that normal physics won't apply
- Prepare for time/space discontinuity
- Don't try to interact with geometric entities
- Maintain regular sleep/wake cycles despite multiple suns
- Keep detailed journals to track perception changes
- Regular reality-grounding exercises

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES:
- Multiple escape routes planned
- Emergency shelter materials
- Signal devices that work in non-standard physics
- Reality anchor points established
- Temporal displacement protocols
- Geometric entity encounter procedures

CONTRAINDICATIONS:
- History of spatial disorientation
- Susceptibility to altitude sickness
- Psychological instability
- Mathematics anxiety
- Pattern-induced seizures
- Time perception disorders

ADDITIONAL RECOMMENDATIONS:
- Bring copper and bone tools as potential interfaces
- Document everything with multiple recording methods
- Maintain physical contact with original materials
- Regular check-ins with base reality
- Establish clear mission parameters and limits
- Practice geometric visualization techniques
- Study crystalline growth patterns
- Learn basic principles of non-standard physics

Remember: This location operates on principles that may be fundamentally incomprehensible to human perception. Maintain flexibility in thinking while keeping strict safety protocols. Accept that some experiences may be impossible to record or communicate effectively. Priority should be on survival and maintaining cognitive stability rather than complete understanding.
