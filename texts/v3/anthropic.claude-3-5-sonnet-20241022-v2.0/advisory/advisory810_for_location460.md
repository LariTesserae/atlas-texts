- Minimum 6 months of virtual reality training in non-Euclidean navigation
- Inner ear enhancement surgery to handle spatial distortions

- Quantum uncertainty tolerance building exercises
- Meditation training for maintaining mental stability in fluid reality conditions

Mental Preparation:
- Accept that normal concepts of distance, time, and causality won't apply
- Prepare for possible temporary disconnection from conventional reality
- Study advanced mathematics and quantum mechanics to better comprehend your surroundings
- Practice remaining calm when fundamental constants shift
- Develop mental techniques for maintaining your sense of self when boundaries blur

Safety Protocols:
- Never spend more than 15 minutes (external time) in the pocket universe
- Maintain constant communication with the observation team
- Keep your reality anchor active at all times
- Don't attempt to interact with or collect samples of proto-matter
- Have an emergency quantum teleport preset to your entry point

What Not to Bring:
- Any conventional electronic devices
- Standard measuring instruments
- Traditional timepieces
- Regular matter samples
- Liquid or gas-based equipment

Special Considerations:
- Your perception of time will be unreliable - set external automated extraction protocols
- Be prepared for your consciousness to experience unusual expansions or contractions
- Accept that some of what you experience may be fundamentally incomprehensible
- Understand that recording devices may fail in unpredictable ways
- Remember that what looks solid may not be, and vice versa

Recovery Protocol:
- Plan for at least 48 hours in a quantum decompression chamber after visit
- Schedule several reality reintegration therapy sessions
- Expect temporary sensory readjustment issues
- Allow time for your temporal perception to re-normalize
- Keep detailed journals of your experience while memories are fresh
