- Sample collection vials (for scientific documentation)

- Learn basic communication methods of the local inhabitants

- Practice non-verbal communication skills

Biological/Environmental Preparation:
- Get vaccinated against fungal infections
- Build tolerance to constant rhythmic sounds (the moth wings may be psychologically taxing)
- Practice moving in low-light conditions
- Train in basic cave navigation
- Learn to recognize signs of geological instability
- Study the behavior patterns of the cave moths to predict swarm events

Mental Preparation:

Research Priorities:
- Document interaction patterns between species
- Map acoustic properties of different chambers

Safety Protocols:
- Never explore alone
- Maintain constant communication with base camp
- Establish safe zones for tremor events
- Create evacuation plans for moth swarms
- Set up quarantine procedures for fungal exposure
- Monitor psychological state for effects of constant rhythm exposure

Time Management:
- Plan visits around predicted tremor cycles
- Allow time for eyes to adjust to bioluminescent lighting
- Schedule rest periods in quieter chambers
- Build in observation time for learning cave moth patterns
