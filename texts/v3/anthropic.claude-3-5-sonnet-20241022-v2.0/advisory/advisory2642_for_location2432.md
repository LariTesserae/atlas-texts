Preparation Guide for Visiting New Thessalonica

Essential Gear:
- Moisture-wicking, quick-dry clothing (at least 3 layers)
- Waterproof boots with non-slip soles
- Multiple pairs of moisture-resistant socks
- Dehumidifying storage containers for electronics
- Anti-fog goggles or glasses
- Waterproof bags for all belongings
- Personal dehumidifier for sleeping quarters
- Anti-fungal powder/cream
- Moisture-absorbing undergarments
- Waterproof notepad and writing implements

Medical Considerations:
- Get vaccinated against common mold-related illnesses
- Bring anti-fungal medications
- Pack antihistamines for possible reactions to unique spores
- Consider bringing supplements for Vitamin D deficiency
- Include medication for possible motion sickness (the constant movement of sea life can be disorienting)
- Bring eye drops for the artificial atmosphere

Psychological Preparation:
- Practice meditation or grounding techniques to manage the psychological impact of constant surveillance by sea creatures
- Prepare for potential claustrophobia
- Develop strategies for maintaining regular sleep patterns in unchanging light
- Consider bringing light therapy equipment
- Be ready for the emotional impact of perpetual twilight

Technical Requirements:
- Acquire necessary permits from the city's administration
- Study pressure adjustment protocols
- Learn basic emergency procedures for dome breach scenarios
- Familiarize yourself with oxygen recycling systems
- Understand basic fungal management protocols

Cultural Considerations:
- Learn appropriate behavior near the central temple/machine
- Study local customs regarding water conservation
- Understand protocols for interaction with maintenance systems
- Learn local gestures and customs that may have evolved in this unique environment

Communication:
- Bring waterproof communication devices
- Learn local emergency signals
- Understand how to use internal communication systems
- Have backup methods of contact with the outside world

Acclimatization Period:
- Plan for 3-4 days of adjustment to the constant humidity
- Gradually adapt to the artificial lighting schedule
- Learn to orient yourself in the circular city layout
- Practice using local transportation systems

Survival Skills:
- Learn basic pressure door operation
- Understand emergency oxygen mask usage
- Know locations of pressure shelters
- Study evacuation routes and procedures
- Learn to read basic system monitoring displays

Documentation:
- Waterproof camera equipment
- Humidity-resistant journal materials
- Backup storage devices
- Protective cases for all documentation equipment

Specialized Equipment:
- Personal humidity monitor
- Pressure gauge
- Emergency oxygen supply
- Basic tool kit for dealing with corroded mechanisms
- UV protection (for possible exposure to artificial lighting)

Mental Preparation Exercises:
- Practice focusing despite constant movement in peripheral vision
- Develop techniques for maintaining time awareness without natural light cues
- Learn to distinguish between normal machinery sounds and warning signals
- Build tolerance for constant background humidity

Remember:
- Always carry emergency water purification tablets
- Keep at least one backup set of dry clothes
- Maintain regular contact with surface authorities
- Document any unusual creature behavior
- Respect local customs regarding the central temple/machine
- Never attempt to communicate with the creatures outside the dome
- Always follow local guidance regarding pressure protocols

The key to a successful visit is accepting that you're entering an environment that humans weren't designed for but have adapted to inhabit. Patience, preparation, and respect for local systems and customs will be essential.
