Preparation Guide for Visiting the Obsidian Plateau City

ESSENTIAL GEAR:

1. Respiratory Protection
- High-quality dust mask or respirator rated for fine mineral particles
- Multiple filters (obsidian dust will clog them quickly)
- Emergency backup mask

2. Protective Clothing
- Heat-resistant, breathable fabrics
- Thick-soled boots with good grip (obsidian is extremely slippery)
- Full skin coverage to protect from sharp edges and dust
- Goggles with side protection (essential for dust protection)
- Heavy-duty gloves

3. Navigation & Communication
- Local guide (mandatory - do not attempt solo exploration)
- Learning basics of the knot-language system before arrival
- Personal marker ropes and ability to tie basic warning patterns
- Sound-amplifying device (for areas where sound dies quickly)
- Backup communication method (sound-based communication unreliable)

HEALTH CONSIDERATIONS:

1. Physical Preparation
- Inner ear conditioning (for constant vibrations)
- Dental check-up (vibrations can aggravate dental issues)
- Strong stomach (constant movement can cause motion sickness)

2. Medical Supplies
- First aid kit specialized for cuts and abrasions
- Anti-motion sickness medication
- Pain relievers for headaches (vibration-induced)
- Electrolyte supplements (hot, dry environment)
- Plenty of water purification methods

SAFETY PROTOCOLS:

1. Movement Guidelines
- Never touch growing crystal formations
- Stay on marked safe paths
- Learn basic warning signs for imminent geological activity
- Keep minimum 50-foot distance from active Lithophage areas
- Travel in groups of at least three

2. Emergency Procedures
- Memorize locations of nearest human settlements
- Learn recognition patterns for different Lithophage species
- Know emergency shelter locations
- Understand quick-evacuation routes

CULTURAL CONSIDERATIONS:

1. Local Customs
- Learn basic knot-language greetings
- Understand local taboos regarding crystal formations
- Respect community warning systems
- Never attempt to harvest or collect obsidian without permission

2. Timing
- Plan visits during periods of lower geological activity (consult locals)
- Allow time for acclimatization to constant vibrations
- Schedule activities around known crystal growth patterns

ADDITIONAL RECOMMENDATIONS:

1. Documentation
- Camera with dust protection
- Impact-resistant equipment cases
- Vibration-dampening mounts for any sensitive equipment

2. Comfort Items
- Noise-canceling devices for sleep
- Anti-vibration sleeping pad
- Electrolyte-rich snacks
- Cooling neck wraps

3. Research
- Study basic geology of obsidian
- Learn about known Lithophage species
- Understand local weather patterns
- Familiarize yourself with warning signs of geological events

PROHIBITED ITEMS:

- Heavy machinery (can trigger unwanted geological activity)
- Metal tools (can conduct harmful vibrations)
- Glass containers (risk of breakage)
- Traditional camping equipment (mostly unsuitable)

Remember: This is not a destination for inexperienced travelers. The combination of geological hazards, unusual fauna, and challenging environmental conditions requires thorough preparation and respect for local knowledge. Always defer to resident humans' expertise about their environment.
