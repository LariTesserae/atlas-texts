Preparation Guide for Visiting the Resonant Labyrinth

ESSENTIAL GEAR:
- Highly durable protective suit with:
  * Self-contained breathing apparatus (minimum 24-hour supply)
  * Fine particle filtration system
  * Moisture-proof sealing
  * Impact absorption capabilities
  * Sound dampening features
- Multiple redundant lighting sources
  * LED headlamp
  * Chemical light sticks
  * Battery-powered floodlight
- Recording equipment:
  * Seismometer
  * Audio recorder with low-frequency capability
  * High-speed camera
- Navigation tools:
  * 3D mapping device
  * Motion sensors
  * Marking system (luminescent paint or electronic beacons)
  * Climbing gear rated for unusual angles

PHYSICAL PREPARATION:
- Vestibular adaptation training
- Experience with non-Euclidean spaces
- Strong core strength and flexibility
- Practice moving in zero/variable gravity conditions
- Inner ear conditioning for unusual sound environments

SAFETY PROTOCOLS:
1. Never travel alone - minimum team of three
2. Maintain physical connection to team via tether system
3. Establish base camp at entry point with minimum 72-hour supplies
4. Regular radio check-ins on encrypted frequency
5. Emergency teleport beacon if available
6. Define clear abort criteria

PSYCHOLOGICAL PREPARATION:
- Meditation practice for maintaining mental stability
- Training in pattern recognition without fixation
- Exposure therapy to non-standard geometries
- Development of strong reality anchoring techniques
- Practice in maintaining orientation during spatial distortion

RESEARCH PREPARATION:
- Study of non-Euclidean geometry
- Basic mycology and xenobiology
- Acoustic physics
- Material science
- Ancient writing systems
- Fluid dynamics

CONTINGENCY PLANNING:
1. Structural shift response protocol:
   - Immediate bracing position
   - Wait for movement to cease
   - Re-establish team contact
   - Verify position relative to base
2. Water anomaly protocol:
   - Avoid direct contact
   - Document flow patterns
   - Monitor suit integrity
3. Growth encounter protocol:
   - Maintain distance
   - Record but do not touch
   - Watch for spore release

RECOMMENDED VISIT DURATION:
- Maximum 4 hours per expedition
- Minimum 12-hour rest between visits
- Total exposure limit of 20 hours per month

DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS:
- Continuous video recording
- Regular environmental readings
- Sample collection (if safe)
- Detailed personal logs
- Sketch mapping
- Sound recordings

POST-VISIT PROCEDURES:
1. Full decontamination
2. Medical check-up
3. Psychological evaluation
4. Equipment sterilization
5. Data backup and analysis
6. Team debriefing

SPECIAL NOTES:
- Be prepared for time dilation effects
- Maintain awareness of psychological state
- Watch for pattern-induced hypnosis
- Stay alert for architectural shifts
- Keep track of water movement patterns
- Monitor resonance effects on equipment
- Preserve energy - environment is physically and mentally taxing

The key to surviving this environment is maintaining a balance between scientific curiosity and cautious respect. The space demands both physical and mental flexibility while requiring strict adherence to safety protocols. Remember: this is not a place for heroes or lone explorers - success comes from careful preparation, team coordination, and the wisdom to know when to retreat.
