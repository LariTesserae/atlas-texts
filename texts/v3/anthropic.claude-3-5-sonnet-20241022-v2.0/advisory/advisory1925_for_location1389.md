Preparation Guide for Visiting the Living Cathedral

ESSENTIAL GEAR:
- Waterproof diving suit with thermal regulation (the approach will be underwater)
- Standard scuba gear for the approach
- Quick-dry clothing for inside (the humidity is constant)
- Non-slip footwear (the living surfaces can be slick)
- Waterproof bags for any electronics/equipment
- Small towels (you will get wet, even inside)
- Anti-fog goggles or glasses (the humidity will affect normal eyewear)

PHYSICAL PREPARATION:
- Basic swim certification required
- Comfort with enclosed spaces
- Good balance (surfaces are organic and sometimes shift slightly)
- Ability to climb stairs/terraces
- No severe sensory sensitivities (constant vibration/humming)

MEDICAL CONSIDERATIONS:
- Check for plant matter allergies
- Bring anti-nausea medication (the combination of movement, vibration, and shifting light patterns can cause disorientation)
- Hydration supplements (the warm, humid environment can be dehydrating)
- Basic first aid kit with waterproof bandages
- Consider ear protection if sensitive to constant low-frequency sounds

DOCUMENTATION/PERMISSIONS:
- Standard multiverse travel documentation
- Specific biosecurity clearance (this is an engineered organism)
- Photography permits (if planning to document)
- Visitor liability waiver
- Medical clearance for underwater approach

TIMING AND DURATION:
- Plan visits during daylight hours (the bioluminescent displays are less visible but overall visibility is better)
- Allow 30 minutes for approach and entry protocols
- Recommended visit duration: 2-3 hours maximum (the environment can be physically taxing)
- Schedule rest breaks (the constant vibration can be fatiguing)

BEHAVIORAL GUIDELINES:
- Do not touch the living surfaces unnecessarily
- Maintain distance from maintenance robots
- Stay on designated paths/terraces
- No sampling or collection of material
- Respect marked restricted areas
- Follow all decontamination protocols before and after visit

CULTURAL CONTEXT:
- Research basic principles of bioarchitecture
- Familiarize yourself with cathedral terminology
- Understanding of basic marine biology helpful
- Knowledge of local customs regarding living structures

ADDITIONAL RECOMMENDATIONS:
- Bring a small cushion (seating areas are organic surfaces)
- Consider motion sickness prevention
- Carry emergency signal device
- Bring water in sealed container
- Small snacks in waterproof packaging
- Camera in underwater housing
- Audio recording equipment (the cathedral's sounds are unique)

EXIT STRATEGY:
- Know emergency exit locations
- Carry backup air supply
- Be familiar with decompression protocols
- Keep communication device accessible
- Know distress signals

Remember: This is both a building and a living organism. Treat it with appropriate respect and care. The environment inside is precisely maintained - any disruption could affect the cathedral's biological systems. When in doubt, err on the side of minimal impact.
