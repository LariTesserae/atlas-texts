Preparation Guidelines for Visiting the Aviary of Hours

ESSENTIAL GEAR:
- Warm, layered clothing (the chamber is consistently cool)
- Non-slip, waterproof boots (for the wet floor)
- Multiple light sources (preferably non-electric backup options)
- Basic climbing gear for tower ascent
- Water-resistant notebook and multiple writing implements
- Time-keeping devices: both mechanical (wind-up watch) and digital
- Empty vessels for water collection (glass or ceramic preferred over metal)
- Basic first aid kit
- Protein-rich snacks and water (plan for longer-than-intended stays)

PSYCHOLOGICAL PREPARATION:
- Meditate regularly for at least two weeks before visiting
- Practice maintaining mental anchors (personal memories, mathematical constants)
- Study basic temporal philosophy to build cognitive resilience
- Establish a strong sense of personal chronology
- Learn grounding techniques for spatial/temporal disorientation

SAFETY PROTOCOLS:
- Never visit alone; maintain minimum group size of three
- Establish time limits for chamber exposure (recommend max 2 hours initially)
- Use buddy system for arch traversal
- Maintain physical contact with at least one solid surface at all times
- Set regular check-in intervals with base camp below
- Agree on clear evacuation triggers and signals beforehand

RESEARCH PREPARATION:
- Study known languages found in bowl inscriptions
- Learn basic ornithology for comparative observation
- Bring equipment for measuring acoustic frequencies
- Practice quick-sketch techniques for documenting shifting patterns
- Research local folklore about the tower

CONTRAINDICATIONS:
- Avoid visiting if prone to vertigo or spatial disorientation
- Screen for history of temporal lobe sensitivity
- Not recommended for those with recent psychological trauma
- Caution advised for those with synesthesia or similar perceptual variations

DOCUMENTATION GUIDELINES:
- Record personal temporal experience at regular intervals
- Document any linguistic anomalies in speech patterns
- Photograph/sketch wall patterns at timed intervals
- Note any physical or cognitive changes after arch traversal
- Monitor and record any unusual sensory experiences

POST-VISIT PROTOCOL:
- Schedule immediate debrief with team
- Plan for 24-hour observation period
- Document any linguistic anomalies for at least one week
- Maintain regular sleep schedule to re-establish temporal rhythm
- Consider counseling if temporal displacement symptoms persist

CULTURAL CONSIDERATIONS:
- Respect local traditions regarding the tower
- Leave offerings as locally customary
- Avoid disturbing existing copper bowls
- Maintain silence during apparent mist-bird song cycles

SPECIAL NOTES:
- Bring no timepieces you're emotionally attached to; temporal anomalies may affect their function
- Consider wearing an eye patch to maintain normal depth perception when viewing the pool
- Bring string or rope to maintain physical connection to entry point
- Consider ear protection that still allows perception of ultra-low frequencies
- Prepare for the possibility that your visit duration may be experienced differently by those outside the chamber

This preparation guide is necessarily incomplete due to the location's anomalous nature. Adjust protocols based on the most recent visitor reports and local conditions.
