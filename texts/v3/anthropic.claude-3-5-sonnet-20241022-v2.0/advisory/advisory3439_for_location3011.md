Preparation Guide for Visiting the Living Canopy City

ESSENTIAL PREPARATIONS:

1. Medical & Physical
- Get a full spectrum of vaccinations, including experimental biotech-compatible boosters
- Undergo electromagnetic sensitivity testing
- Consider temporary inner ear modifications to handle the constant movement
- Get anti-nausea medication specifically designed for "living architecture environments"
- Install subdermal humidity sensors (temporary ok)
- Consider basic pheromone translation implants

2. Gear & Equipment
- Moisture-wicking, electrically insulated clothing (multiple layers)
- Grounded footwear with adaptive grip
- Personal Faraday cage equipment for sensitive electronics
- Bio-signal scanner to read tower "moods" and growth patterns
- Emergency stabilization harness for unexpected structural movements
- Portable air filtration mask (spore-rated)
- Water purification system (the local water may contain unknown biologicals)
- Static discharge bands for wrists and ankles
- Light-reactive goggles for handling sudden bioluminescent events
- Backup power sources (non-magnetic)

3. Documentation & Permissions
- Standard multiverse travel permits
- Swarm-colony interaction certification (basic level minimum)
- Living architecture navigation license
- Biohazard insurance coverage
- Growth-cycle forecast access subscription
