Preparation Guide for Visiting the Forgotten Courtyard

Essential Items:
- Non-slip waterproof shoes or boots (the moss and wet cobblestones will be treacherous)
- Quick-dry, moisture-wicking clothing (the humidity will be intense)
- Waterproof camera bag or electronics protection
- Microfiber cloths for wiping camera lenses (they'll fog constantly)
- Small foldable stool or waterproof cushion (all surfaces will be damp)

Timing & Access:
- Plan to visit at dawn or dusk to witness the golden light phenomenon
- Allow your eyes 5-10 minutes to adjust to the dim lighting
- Scout the entry points beforehand - these forgotten spaces often have restricted or unclear access
- Visit during dry weather; rain will make surfaces especially slippery

Photography Considerations:
- Bring a tripod for low-light conditions
- Consider a macro lens for water droplets and small ferns
- UV filter to manage the moisture
- Pack silica gel packets for your camera bag

Physical Preparation:
- Wear breathable layers - the space will be warmer than the surrounding streets
- Be prepared for your glasses to fog up immediately upon entering
- Consider bringing a small battery-powered fan for comfort

Environmental Etiquette:
- Step carefully to avoid damaging moss and fern communities
- Maintain distance from the nesting doves
- Don't attempt to "clean" or "fix" anything - the ecosystem is delicate
- Keep voices low to preserve the acoustic atmosphere

Scientific/Documentation Gear (optional):
- Hygrometer to measure the unique humidity levels
- Small light meter to document the unusual lighting conditions
- Field notebook with waterproof pages
- Clear container for water sampling if permitted

Health Considerations:
- If you have respiratory sensitivities, bring appropriate medication
- The high humidity may aggravate certain conditions
- Consider a dust mask if sensitive to mold spores

Time Management:
- Plan to spend at least an hour allowing your senses to attune
- The space will reveal itself slowly - rushing will miss subtle details
- Consider multiple visits at different times of day
- Factor in time for your equipment to adjust to the humidity

Additional Suggestions:
- Bring a small audio recorder to capture the unique water sounds
- Consider a sketch pad with waterproof paper
- Pack a plastic bag for wet items
- Bring a backup battery for electronics (humidity can affect battery life)

Mental Preparation:
- Be ready for a meditative experience
- The space may evoke strong emotional responses
- Practice patience - the best observations come with time
- Be prepared for the contrast between the courtyard and the modern city

Remember: This is a space that exists in delicate balance. The goal is to observe and document while leaving no trace of your visit.
