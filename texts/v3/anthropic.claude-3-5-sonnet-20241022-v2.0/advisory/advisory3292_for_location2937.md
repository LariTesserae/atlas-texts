For visiting the City of Hanging Gardens, here's a comprehensive preparation guide:

Essential Physical Preparations:
- Undergo altitude acclimation training for at least 2-3 weeks prior to visit
- Get a full medical checkup, especially focusing on cardiovascular health
- Obtain prescription altitude sickness medication
- Consider getting supplemental oxygen supplies for emergencies
- Purchase UV-protective clothing and eyewear (the thin atmosphere offers less protection from solar radiation)
- Invest in comfortable, non-slip footwear suitable for transparent walkways
- Bring layers of clothing - the temperature regulation means it's never extreme, but variations exist between zones

Documentation and Permissions:
- Apply for a visitor's permit well in advance
- Obtain necessary vaccinations and health certificates
- Register your biometrics for city access systems
- Download and familiarize yourself with the city's navigation app
- Get insurance that covers high-altitude environments and advanced medical facilities

Technical Equipment:
- Acquire a personal air quality monitor
- Bring a reliable comm device compatible with the city's network
- Get a backup power bank (the city has abundant power, but better safe than sorry)
- Consider a personal humidity monitor if you're sensitive to moisture levels
- Bring a high-quality camera with polarizing filters (for dealing with all the reflected light)

Cultural and Practical Preparation:
- Learn basic protocols for using transit pods and transparent walkways
- Study the city's zone system and daily rhythm patterns
- Familiarize yourself with emergency procedures and locations of medical facilities
- Learn the basic hand signals used by maintenance crews
- Understand the rules regarding interaction with the engineered ecosystems
- Study the schedule of light/shade patterns in different city sectors

Health Considerations:
- Start a hydration routine before arrival and maintain it strictly
- Bring moisturizer and eye drops (the filtered air can be quite dry)
- Consider bringing electrolyte supplements
- Pack any personal medications in extra quantities
- Get used to checking air quality readings regularly

Social and Cultural Notes:
- The city operates on its own time system based on light cycles - learn it
- Respect the various ecological zones and their specific rules
- Be prepared for the unique social norms that develop in a highly engineered environment
- Learn the basic etiquette regarding personal space in transit pods and walkways

What to Avoid:
- Don't attempt to collect specimens from the gardens without permission
- Avoid interfering with maintenance drones or robotic systems
- Don't try to access restricted construction zones
- Never attempt to override environmental controls
- Don't use flash photography near sensitive plant species or animals

Special Considerations:
- Be prepared for the psychological impact of constant height and transparency
- Understand that weather patterns at this altitude can change quickly
- Know that some areas operate on artificial day/night cycles
- Be ready for the unique acoustic environment created by wind and architecture

Recommended Experience Level:
- Prior experience with high-altitude environments
- Comfort with advanced technological systems
- Basic understanding of controlled ecosystems
- Good sense of spatial awareness
- Ability to follow complex safety protocols

Duration of Visit:
- Plan for at least a week to properly acclimate
- Allow time to adjust to the city's rhythms
- Build in extra days for unexpected closures or system maintenance

The key to visiting the City of Hanging Gardens is understanding that you're entering a carefully balanced system. Your presence as a visitor needs to work within this system rather than disturb it. Preparation should focus on both physical adaptation to the environment and understanding how to move through this unique space without disrupting its delicate equilibrium.
