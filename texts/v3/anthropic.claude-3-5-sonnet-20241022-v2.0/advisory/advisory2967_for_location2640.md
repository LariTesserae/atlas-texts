Preparation Guide for Visiting the Crystal Garden Moon

ESSENTIAL GEAR:
- Advanced vacuum-rated spacesuit with at least 24 hours of life support
- Multiple backup oxygen systems
- Radiation shielding (both conventional and quantum)
- Magnetic boots for stability in low gravity
- Emergency beacon and quantum-encrypted communications array
- Portable airlock system for ship-to-surface transfers

SPECIALIZED EQUIPMENT:
- Multi-spectral sensors to monitor crystal activity
- Quantum field detector to avoid areas of intense probability fluctuation
- High-resolution holographic recording devices
- Ground-penetrating radar for mapping root networks
- EMP shielding for all electronic equipment (the crystal processing activity can interfere with standard systems)
- Portable force field generator to protect against sudden crystal growth or reconfiguration

SAFETY PROTOCOLS:
1. Maintain minimum 10-meter distance from all crystal structures
2. Never touch the black spherical nodes - their quantum fields could disrupt suit systems
3. Watch for signs of crystal movement/growth and evacuate area immediately if detected
4. Monitor suit quantum shielding levels constantly
5. Stay within line of sight of your ship/base at all times
6. Travel in pairs minimum, preferably groups of three

PHYSICAL PREPARATION:
- Low gravity training (1/6 Earth standard)
- Vacuum environment certification
- Extended EVA experience required
- Mental conditioning for extended exposure to non-Euclidean geometries

MENTAL/PSYCHOLOGICAL PREPARATION:
- Meditation training to maintain focus amid the alien thought patterns emanating from the crystal networks
- Acceptance that human minds cannot fully comprehend the processes being observed
- Preparation for severe spatial disorientation near the cathedral structures
- Recognition that time may feel subjectively different near major processing nodes

RESEARCH PREPARATION:
- Study of quantum computation theory
- Familiarization with known crystal-based life forms
- Background in xenoarchaeology
- Understanding of non-biological information processing systems

CONTINGENCY PLANNING:
- Multiple escape routes mapped at all times
- Emergency quantum field dampeners in case of dangerous fluctuations
- Backup transport vehicle kept ready
- Protocol for handling unexpected crystal growth or structure reformation
- Plan for sudden changes in local space-time geometry

DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS:
- Full sensor suite recordings
- Regular status reports to orbital station
- Detailed mapping of any changes in crystal configurations
- Documentation of all quantum field fluctuations
- Analysis of any patterns in crystal light emissions

TIME MANAGEMENT:
- Limit initial visits to 4 hours maximum
- Schedule regular breaks to process the alien environment
- Allow time for equipment recalibration between visits
- Plan observations during different orbital phases to observe varying energy patterns

Remember: This is not a biological garden - it's an ancient computational system that merely resembles one. Treat it with the caution appropriate for advanced alien technology that has been running autonomously for eons. The beauty of the crystal formations can be hypnotic, but never forget you're walking through what amounts to a planet-sized quantum computer of unknown purpose.
