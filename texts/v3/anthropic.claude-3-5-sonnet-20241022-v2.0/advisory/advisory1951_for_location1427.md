Preparation Guide for Visiting the Bio-Industrial Growth Factory

ESSENTIAL GEAR:
- Waterproof everything. Multiple layers recommended:
  * Base layer: Moisture-wicking synthetics
  * Mid layer: Sealed neoprene or similar
  * Outer layer: Heavy-duty waterproof coating with electromagnetic shielding
- Sealed boots with excellent grip and chemical resistance
- Full face respirator with both particulate and organic vapor filters
- Backup filtration system
- Sealed goggles with anti-fog coating
- Heavy-duty nitrile gloves
- Portable air quality monitor
- Multi-spectrum light source
- Emergency beacon

ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS:
- Constant moisture exposure
- Unknown biological/chemical agents in water droplets
- Unstable flooring
- Electromagnetic fields
- Potential biohazards
- Low visibility
- Disorienting acoustics

NAVIGATION & SAFETY:
- Never go alone - minimum 3-person team recommended
- Use physical tether system - floor instability makes electronic tracking unreliable
- Mark entry point clearly - structure may shift during visit
- Maintain constant communication with surface team
- Establish "safe zones" near standard power ports - these areas tend to be more stable
- Avoid contact with walls and any moving structures
- Watch for mechanical maintenance "insects" - they may view visitors as foreign bodies to be processed

HEALTH PRECAUTIONS:
- Up-to-date vaccinations including experimental biotech variants if available
- Recent physical exam focusing on immune system strength
- No open wounds or skin conditions
- Prophylactic broad-spectrum antibiotics advised
- Mental preparation for sensory disruption
- Consider anti-nausea medication - environment can trigger motion sickness

SCIENTIFIC EQUIPMENT:
- Multi-parameter environmental sensor array
- Sample collection containers (sealed, electromagnetically shielded)
- Non-contact temperature sensors
- Electromagnetic field detector
- High-speed camera with low-light capability
- Audio recorder with infrasonic capability
- Portable spectrometer
- Chemical reaction test kit

TIMING & DURATION:
- Limit initial visits to 2 hours maximum
- Plan visits during periods of maximum natural light
- Allow 24-hour decontamination period after exit
- Schedule follow-up medical screening 48 hours post-visit

EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS:
- Carry chemical cauterizing agent for wall breaches
- Emergency oxygen supply
- Quick-deploy flotation device (for unstable floor situations)
- Signal flares (non-incendiary type)
- Cutting tool for emergency exit
- First aid kit including neutralizing agents for unknown biologics

PSYCHOLOGICAL PREPARATION:
- Review footage/images of similar environments
- Practice sensory adaptation exercises
- Establish clear abort criteria
- Prepare for significant cognitive dissonance
- Plan regular psychological debriefings

DOCUMENTATION:
- Maintain detailed log of all unusual sensations or reactions
- Record any patterns in factory's growth or movement
- Document all interactions with mechanical maintenance entities
- Note any changes in standard equipment functionality
- Track personal biometrics throughout visit

Remember: This environment challenges our basic assumptions about the boundaries between biological and mechanical systems. Maintain extreme caution and resist the urge to interact with or sample anything without proper protocols in place. The factory's purpose and products are unknown - treat everything as potentially hazardous until proven otherwise.
