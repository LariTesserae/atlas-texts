Preparation Guidelines for Visiting the Sealed Industrial Dome

ESSENTIAL GEAR:
- Respirator with fine particulate filters (rated for metallic dust)
- Heat-resistant clothing with cooling system
- Electromagnetic field-shielded equipment containers
- Non-magnetic tools and accessories
- Anti-static boots with excellent grip
- Protective goggles with low-light enhancement
- Multiple light sources (preferably LED)
- Water supply (minimum 3L) - the environment is extremely dehydrating
- Basic first aid kit with burn treatment supplies

ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS TO CONSIDER:
- Strong electromagnetic fields can interfere with electronic devices
- Risk of overheating in the constant warm environment
- Metallic dust accumulation in lungs and equipment
- Poor visibility conditions
- Risk of disorientation in the complex layout
- Possible toxic emissions from the metallic-organic hybrid structures
- Slipping hazards near coolant pools
- Risk of electromagnetic interference with medical implants

RECOMMENDED PREPARATIONS:
1. Medical screening before visit:
   - Check for metal implants
   - Verify heart condition
   - Test for electromagnetic sensitivity

2. Equipment Testing:
   - All gear should be tested in strong magnetic fields
   - Calibrate navigation equipment for indoor use
   - Verify all seals on protective gear

3. Physical Conditioning:
   - Heat acclimation training
   - Practice moving in confined spaces
   - Balance training for narrow walkways

NAVIGATION PROTOCOLS:
- Use physical markers (non-magnetic)
- Maintain constant communication with outside
- Map your route before entering
- Always identify multiple exit paths
- Stay away from active machinery edges

TIMING CONSIDERATIONS:
- Limit initial visits to 2-3 hours maximum
- Allow time for gear decontamination after visit
- Plan visits during facility's lowest activity periods (if identifiable)

SPECIAL NOTES:
- Bring no magnetic storage devices
- Avoid wearing any metal jewelry or accessories
- Consider buddy system mandatory
- Document any changes in the "unchanging" patterns
- Maintain distance from liquid metal coolant pools
- Do not touch or sample the metallic-organic hybrids without proper containment equipment

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES:
1. Carry emergency oxygen supply
2. Know location of emergency exits
3. Have backup communication method
4. Bring chemical light sticks as backup illumination
5. Carry electromagnetic field detector

RESEARCH PREPARATION:
- Study facility blueprints if available
- Learn about similar industrial complexes
- Understand basic electromagnetic field safety
- Review automated system behaviors
- Learn to recognize warning signs of system malfunctions

PSYCHOLOGICAL PREPARATION:
- Be prepared for sensory disruption from machine noise
- Accept limited visibility conditions
- Understand spatial disorientation risks
- Prepare for feelings of isolation
- Practice calm breathing in confined spaces

POST-VISIT PROCEDURES:
1. Full equipment decontamination
2. Medical check-up
3. Document all observations
4. Monitor for delayed reactions to environment
5. Clean all gear of metallic dust

Remember: This environment, while seemingly stable, is inherently hazardous. No unnecessary risks should be taken, and all safety protocols must be strictly followed. The combination of electromagnetic fields, heat, and industrial processes creates a unique set of challenges that requires thorough preparation and constant vigilance.
