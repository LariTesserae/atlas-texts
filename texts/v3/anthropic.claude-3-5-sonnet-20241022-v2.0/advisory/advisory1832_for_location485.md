- Keep your intended destination in peripheral vision rather than direct sight

2. Interaction:
- Avoid direct contact with crystalline vegetation
- Don't attempt to interpret the patterns/writing all at once - parse gradually
- If caught in a phase shift, remain mentally flexible but physically still
- Never try to force logical consistency onto what you observe

3. Safety:
- Check TPS coordinates every 15 minutes
- Rotate reality anchors regularly
- If philosophical cold becomes overwhelming, activate cognitive warming protocols
- Maintain regular communication with base reality (if possible)
- Keep detailed logs of subjective time versus measured time

1. Accept that normal cause-and-effect relationships may not apply
2. Maintain flexible definitions of "here" and "now"
3. Don't try to resolve paradoxes - observe and document them instead
4. Keep a physical token from home reality to maintain psychological grounding
5. Practice dealing with spatial-temporal discontinuity

- Activate emergency reality shelter
- Engage full cognitive protection

- Document each iteration
- Look for subtle variations that might indicate an exit point

RESEARCH PROTOCOLS:

1. Document everything, but expect recordings to be unreliable
2. Use multiple observation methods simultaneously
3. Accept that some observations may be fundamentally incompatible
4. Maintain theoretical flexibility in analysis

2. Schedule reality readjustment therapy
3. Expect temporary difficulties with normal space-time navigation
