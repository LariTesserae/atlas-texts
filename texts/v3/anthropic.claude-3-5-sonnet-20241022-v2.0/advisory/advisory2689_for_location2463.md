Preparation Guide for Visiting the Suspended Aquarium-World

- Anti-vertigo medication (the impossible physics and fluid dynamics can trigger severe disorientation)
- Polarized goggles with adjustable UV/IR filters to manage the unusual light spectrum
- Noise-canceling headphones with fine frequency control (to manage the sub-audible humming if it becomes overwhelming)
- A personal temporal anchor device (to maintain your connection to normal spacetime)
- Recording equipment specifically designed for non-standard electromagnetic frequencies
- A quantum stabilization harness (to prevent molecular destabilization from proximity to altered physics)

PHYSICAL PREPARATION:
- Practice meditation or other mindfulness techniques (2-3 weeks minimum)
- Undergo vestibular adaptation training
- Complete at least 20 hours in a sensory deprivation tank
- Take courses in non-Euclidean geometry comprehension
- Develop resistance to synesthesia effects through controlled exposure therapy

MENTAL PREPARATION:
- Accept that your senses will report contradictory information
- Understand that time perception will be unreliable
- Prepare for possible reality disconnection syndrome
- Study theoretical physics focusing on closed systems and quantum mechanics
- Practice maintaining mental equilibrium while processing impossible events

SAFETY PROTOCOLS:
- Never observe the sphere for more than 30 minutes without a 10-minute reset period
- Maintain at least 2 meters distance from the sphere's boundary
- Keep your temporal anchor active at all times
- Have a reality anchor person present (someone who stays completely outside the sphere's influence)
- Establish clear emergency extraction protocols

OBSERVATION TECHNIQUES:
- Use peripheral vision when possible to reduce direct exposure to reality-bending effects
- Rotate your observation position every 5 minutes to prevent perceptual lock
- Take frequent breaks to recalibrate your senses to normal physics
- Document experiences immediately, as memories may become fluid
- Use multiple recording methods to capture different aspects of the phenomenon

HEALTH MONITORING:
- Wear biometric sensors to track physiological responses
- Monitor cognitive function with regular reality-check exercises
- Watch for signs of temporal displacement sickness
- Track any synesthesia effects or sensory bleed-through
- Regular checks for quantum entanglement contamination

CONTRA-INDICATIONS:
- History of seizures or neurological disorders
- Recent psychedelic experiences
- Unstable vestibular conditions
- Quantum sensitivity syndrome
- Reality anchor instability

RECOVERY PROTOCOL:
- Minimum 24-hour decompression period after exposure
- Gradual reintroduction to normal physics
- Sensory realignment therapy
- Temporal recalibration exercises
- Reality consolidation meditation

DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS:
- Maintain detailed logs of all sensory experiences
- Record any temporal anomalies
- Document any synesthesia effects
- Track pattern changes in crystal formations
- Monitor personal cognitive shifts

Remember: This is not just a physical journey but a profound challenge to your understanding of reality. Maintaining psychological stability is as important as physical safety. Always approach with respect for the unknown and be prepared to accept that some aspects of the experience may remain permanently inexplicable.
