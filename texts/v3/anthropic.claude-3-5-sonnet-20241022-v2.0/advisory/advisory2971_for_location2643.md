Preparation Guide for Visiting the Hanging Garden-City

Essential Equipment:
- Moisture-resistant clothing and gear (quick-dry, anti-fungal treated)
- Non-slip, soft-soled climbing shoes or boots
- Harness and safety equipment for vertical movement
- Waterproof bags for electronics and sensitive items
- Portable dehumidifier for sleeping quarters
- UV-resistant goggles (the constant bioluminescence may affect vision)
- Breathing mask with spore filters
- Silent communication devices (standard phones may disturb inhabitants)
- Bio-neutral soap and sanitizers (to avoid disrupting the ecosystem)

Health Preparations:
- Get vaccinated against common fungal infections
- Undergo allergy testing for various fungal spores
- Build up balance and climbing strength
- Practice moving silently and deliberately
- Acclimate to low-light conditions beforehand
- Learn basic sign language or non-verbal communication

Cultural Considerations:
- Study movement meditation practices
- Learn basic mycology and fungal cultivation
- Practice maintaining silence for extended periods
- Understand subsonic and pheromone-based communication customs
- Research moth/bird-like beings' social protocols
- Learn to read bioluminescent patterns

Environmental Adaptation:
- Spend time in humidity chambers to acclimate
- Practice three-dimensional navigation
- Learn to read water flow patterns
- Train in vertical climbing and traversing
- Develop night vision adaptation
- Learn to distinguish toxic from safe fungi

Behavioral Guidelines:
- Move slowly and deliberately at all times
- Avoid sudden movements or loud noises
- Never touch living surfaces without permission
- Respect the natural growth patterns
- Follow local guides' lead on interaction with environment
- Maintain cleanliness to prevent contamination

Special Considerations:
- Bring pure water supplies (local water may contain unknown compounds)
- Pack lightweight, minimal equipment
- Include backup lighting that mimics local bioluminescence
- Consider bringing gifts appropriate for the inhabitants
- Plan extended adjustment period upon arrival
- Arrange for local guide familiar with vertical navigation

Safety Measures:
- Multiple backup climbing systems
- Emergency extraction plan
- Waterproof distress beacons
- Anti-fungal medical supplies
- Location tracking devices
- Water filtration system

Mental Preparation:
- Practice sensory adjustment exercises
- Develop comfort with perpetual twilight
- Build tolerance for constant moisture
- Learn to navigate by bioluminescent landmarks
- Prepare for altered sense of gravity
- Practice patience and slow movement

Time Management:
- Plan for slower travel times due to careful movement
- Allow adjustment period for circadian rhythm
- Schedule regular rest periods for physical/mental recovery
- Build in extra time for vertical navigation
- Account for potential communication delays

This location requires a unique combination of physical preparation, environmental adaptation, and cultural sensitivity. Visitors should expect to move at the pace of the city itself - slow, deliberate, and mindful. The experience demands patience, respect for the living architecture, and a willingness to adapt to an entirely different way of existing in space.
