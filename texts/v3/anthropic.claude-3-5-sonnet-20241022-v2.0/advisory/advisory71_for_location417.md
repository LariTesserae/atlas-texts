Preparation Guide for Visiting the Self-Computing City

ESSENTIAL PREPARATION (Begin 2-3 months before visit)

Physical Conditioning:
- Altitude training is crucial - the city exists at varying elevations with thin air
- Practice walking on irregular surfaces and steep inclines
- Develop excellent balance through yoga or similar practices
- Build cardiovascular endurance for navigating vertical spaces

Mental Conditioning:
- Daily meditation to strengthen mental flexibility
- Study basic topology and fractal mathematics
- Practice maintaining focus while experiencing spatial/sensory distortion
- Learn to recognize and accept paradoxical phenomena without resistance

EQUIPMENT & GEAR

Clothing:
- Moisture-wicking, quick-dry layers
- Sturdy boots with excellent grip
- Waterproof outer layer that can handle upward-flowing water
- Clothing should be loose-fitting to accommodate rapid temperature changes
- Avoid synthetic materials that might react oddly with the city's chemistry

Navigation & Communication:
- Analog compass (digital devices may malfunction)
- Physical maps drawn on specially treated paper (streets will update themselves)
- Manual recording devices (mechanical pencils, waterproof notebooks)
- Multiple redundant timepieces
- Non-electronic emergency signaling devices

Protection:
- Filtered breathing mask (for areas with unknown atmospheric conditions)
- UV-protective goggles with side shields
- Noise-canceling earplugs (adjustable for the city's acoustic anomalies)
- Grounding bracelet or similar device to manage quantum effects
- Anti-fungal treatments for skin and clothing

Supplies:
- Water purification systems (city water may have unusual properties)
- High-energy, shelf-stable food (local food sources may be incompatible)
- Basic first aid kit plus supplements for altitude sickness
- Multiple light sources (chemical lights preferred over electronic)
- Rope and climbing gear for vertical navigation

BEHAVIORAL PROTOCOLS

Social Interaction:
- Learn basic corvid body language and respect their hierarchies
- Avoid direct contact with the mycelial network
- Maintain respectful distance from arthropod archivists
- Practice non-verbal communication methods
- Accept that some conversations will be incomprehensible

Navigation Rules:
- Never assume a path will remain the same twice
- Mark your route with temporary, removable markers
- Stay within sight of at least two known reference points
- Avoid trying to map the city (it will only confuse you more)
- Trust your intuition about which areas feel "permitted"

Safety Guidelines:
- Don't attempt to analyze or interfere with the self-assembly processes
- Avoid making permanent changes to any structures
- Keep distance from factory outputs and industrial processes
- Never attempt to decode the mathematical messages
- If you encounter a cosmic phenomenon, don't interact directly

Mental Health Maintenance:
- Schedule regular "reality anchor" sessions
- Maintain a dream journal to process experiences
- Accept that not everything will make sense
- Practice letting go of the need to understand everything
- Plan regular breaks in more normalized spaces

ADDITIONAL CONSIDERATIONS

- Arrange for an experienced guide familiar with the city's patterns
- Set up regular check-ins with contacts outside the city
- Have an extraction plan that doesn't rely on specific locations
- Maintain flexibility in your schedule and expectations
- Consider bringing offerings for the various non-human residents
- Learn to recognize signs of cognitive strain or reality fatigue
- Establish personal rituals to maintain your sense of self

Remember: The city is not hostile, but it operates on principles far beyond human comprehension. Your goal is not to understand it completely, but to observe and experience it while maintaining your own integrity. Approach with humble curiosity rather than analytical ambition.

This preparation guide should be considered a starting point rather than a comprehensive manual. The city's nature means that no preparation can be complete, but these guidelines should help maintain your safety and sanity during your visit.
