Preparation Guide for Visiting Dome 9

Essential Equipment:
- Humidity-adaptive clothing with moisture-wicking properties
- Anti-fungal treatments for all clothing and equipment
- Breathable but protective footwear (the living surfaces require careful interaction)
- Personal dehumidifier unit for sleeping quarters
- UV-protective eyewear (despite the filtered light, extended exposure requires protection)
- Chemical sensor array to detect and interpret the dome's pheromone communications
- Portable air filtration mask with adjustable settings

Medical Preparations:
- Full spectrum of anti-fungal medications
- Immune system evaluation and possible temporary boosters
- Allergy screening for common bioengineered compounds
- Pheromone sensitivity assessment
- Vaccination against engineered microorganisms
- Inner ear adjustment supplements (the constant subtle movements can cause disorientation)

Mental Preparation:
- Meditation training to handle the constant subtle movements in peripheral vision
- Temporal adjustment exercises (the lack of day/night cycles is psychologically challenging)
- Biomimetic awareness training to understand and respect the living architecture
- Sensory integration practice for processing the chemical communication layer

Cultural/Behavioral Guidelines:
- Learn the proper protocols for touching and interacting with living surfaces
- Understand movement patterns that won't disturb the arthropod maintenance crews
- Practice the local etiquette regarding personal space in confined environments
- Study basic biological system indicators to recognize signs of dome stress

Acclimatization Period:
- Plan for 3-4 days of adjustment to the perpetual twilight
- Gradually increase exposure to the pheromone-rich atmosphere
- Start with short excursions from adaptation chambers
- Use circadian rhythm supplements during initial adjustment

Documentation Requirements:
- Biological contamination clearance
- Pheromone sensitivity certification
- Environmental impact assessment
- Living system interaction training completion certificate

Restricted Items:
- No strong artificial fragrances
- No untreated organic materials from outside
- No sharp objects that could damage living surfaces
- No unauthorized biological samples or specimens

Emergency Preparations:
- Know locations of pressure-neutral zones
- Carry personal air supply
- Have quick-deploy isolation pod available
- Maintain communication device compatible with the dome's biological interfaces

Recommended Additional Items:
- Biometric monitoring device calibrated for dome conditions
- Moisture-sealed storage containers
- Light therapy lamp for private quarters
- Chemical signal translator device
- Micro-arthropod repellent (approved types only)
- Bio-compatible waste management supplies

Duration Considerations:
- Limit initial visits to 2 weeks maximum
- Plan regular breaks in neutral environments
- Schedule psychological evaluations for extended stays
- Monitor biological integration markers

Remember that Dome 9 is not just a location but a living entity. Your presence affects its balance, and proper preparation is crucial for both your safety and the dome's stability. Approach with respect, patience, and awareness of your role as a temporary participant in this carefully maintained ecosystem.
