Preparation Guide for Visiting the Cistern-City

Essential Equipment:
- Waterproof everything. Multiple layers of waterproofing on all gear
- Moisture-wicking, quick-dry clothing (multiple changes)
- Waterproof boots with excellent grip for slick surfaces
- Lightweight emergency flotation device
- Waterproof headlamp with backup batteries
- Timing device that doesn't rely on standard physics (mechanical watch may behave erratically)
- Rope with non-slip coating (minimum 50ft)
- Basic climbing gear (carabiners, harness)
- Waterproof notebook and writing implements
- Anti-nausea medication
- Noise-canceling earplugs (adjustable/removable type)

Physical Preparation:
- Practice walking on slippery surfaces
- Build inner ear/balance conditioning
- Swimming proficiency required
- Strength training focusing on core stability
- Meditation practice to maintain mental equilibrium
- Exposure therapy to irregular sounds/vibrations

Mental Preparation:
- Accept that causality will not work normally
- Study basic non-Euclidean geometry
- Practice maintaining focus during sensory disorientation
- Develop awareness of breathing patterns
- Learn to trust physical sensations over visual input

Safety Protocols:
- Never travel alone
- Establish communication methods that don't rely on sound
- Use guide ropes in areas near deep pools
- Learn local residents' warning signals
- Map safe zones and rest areas
- Develop emergency exit strategy accounting for time distortions

Cultural Considerations:
- Learn basic local gesture language
- Study the breathing cycle timing
- Respect residential areas
- Bring tradeable goods (consult locals for preferences)
- Practice moving in rhythm with the chamber's expansion/contraction

Health Precautions:
- Get vaccinated against fungal infections
- Bring antibiotics/antifungals
- Monitor for signs of temporal sickness
- Stay hydrated but don't drink local water
- Watch for signs of psychological strain
- Regular rest periods in stable areas

Navigation Tips:
- Use physical markers rather than visual landmarks
- Learn to read water patterns
- Develop awareness of gravity shifts
- Time movements with chamber breathing
- Watch local residents' movement patterns

Absolute Don'ts:
- Don't ignore the breathing rhythm
- Don't attempt to record sound
- Don't touch the wall growth directly
- Don't enter deep pools
- Don't resist temporal anomalies
- Don't panic if causality breaks down

Special Considerations:
- Plan extra time for everything
- Accept that plans may need to change based on local physics
- Maintain physical contact with solid surfaces when possible
- Rest frequently to reorient yourself
- Keep expectations flexible
- Document experiences immediately

Remember: This environment requires constant adaptation and awareness. Success depends on working with rather than against its unique properties. When in doubt, observe and mimic local residents' behaviors.
