Preparation Guide for Visiting Dewcatcher's Realm

Essential Gear:
- Waterproof clothing and boots with excellent grip (the environment is constantly humid and surfaces are slick)
- Multiple layers of quick-drying, breathable fabrics (temperatures vary between galleries)
- Protective goggles with anti-fog coating (to prevent moisture accumulation and protect from air-swimming creatures)
- Water-resistant pack with waterproof compartments for electronics and documents
- Personal lighting device (though bioluminescence provides ambient light, you'll want backup)
- Moisture-wicking gloves with grip enhancement
- Breathing mask with fine filters (for areas with high microbial density)

Documentation and Permits:
- Standard multiverse transit papers
- Hydrology Priest-issued visitor permit
- Gallery access credentials (different levels for different depths)
- Environmental impact waiver
- Biological contamination insurance

Medical Preparations:
- Up-to-date vaccinations including specialized treatments for water-borne pathogens
- Anti-fungal medications (both preventive and reactive)
- Electrolyte supplements (the humid environment can affect mineral balance)
- Inner ear stabilizers (the unusual physics can cause vertigo)
- Antihistamines (for potential reactions to airborne organisms)

Training Requirements:
- Basic safety orientation (minimum 4 hours)
- Environmental hazard recognition course
- Emergency procedures for gravity anomalies
- Basic navigation in three-dimensional space
- Recognition of warning symbols and signage
- Proper use of elevated walkways and safety harnesses

Cultural Considerations:
- Learn basic hand signals used by condensation farmers
- Understand taboos regarding interference with certain water formations
- Respect boundaries marked by Hydrology Priests
- Follow local customs regarding interaction with air-swimming creatures
- Observe silence protocols in sensitive harvesting areas

Safety Protocols:
- Never travel alone in the galleries
- Maintain constant contact with your assigned guide
- Keep personal flotation device accessible
- Avoid touching bioluminescent surfaces directly
- Stay on marked pathways
- Keep minimum safe distance from harvest-frames
- Don't attempt to collect water samples without permission

Communication:
- Waterproof communication device tuned to local frequencies
- Emergency beacon
- Gallery map with current position tracking
- Signal mirror (useful in cases of equipment failure)

Additional Considerations:
- Allow time for physical adjustment to the unusual environment
- Bring calming aids (the constant motion and sounds can be overwhelming)
- Pack emergency rations in waterproof containers
- Carry a personal dehumidifier for rest periods
- Include backup copies of all documentation
- Consider bringing anti-vertigo medication
- Pack spare sets of clothing in waterproof bags

Time Management:
- Plan for slower movement than usual due to environmental conditions
- Include rest periods for atmospheric adjustment
- Allow extra time for security and safety checkpoints
- Factor in decontamination procedures at entry and exit points

Mental Preparation:
- Study basic principles of non-standard water physics
- Familiarize yourself with common air-swimming species
- Practice meditation or centering techniques
- Prepare for the psychological impact of altered gravity
- Accept that some phenomena will defy conventional explanation

Remember: Dewcatcher's Realm operates on its own logic and timeline. Flexibility and patience are as important as physical preparation. The environment demands respect and careful observation. When in doubt, defer to local expertise and never assume standard physics apply.
