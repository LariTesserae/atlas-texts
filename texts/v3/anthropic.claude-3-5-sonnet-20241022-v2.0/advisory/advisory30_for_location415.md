Preparation Guide for Visiting the Glass-Creature Plains

ESSENTIAL GEAR:
- Thermally regulated hazmat suit with crystalline-resistant coating
- Self-contained breathing apparatus (minimum 12-hour capacity)
- Magnetic boots with broad surface area to prevent sinking
- Multiple backup power sources (the environment may interfere with electronics)
- Geiger counter and phase-state detector
- High-durability recording equipment in waterproof cases

PHYSICAL PREPARATION:
- Undergo vestibular adaptation training (minimum 2 weeks)
- Practice maintaining balance on unstable surfaces
- Build cold resistance through gradual exposure
- Learn meditation techniques to manage sensory overload

SAFETY PROTOCOLS:
- Never make direct contact with the creature-mass or melt-pools
- Maintain constant movement to prevent being enveloped
- Stay at least 10 meters from any synchronization events
- Exit immediately if suit integrity falls below 95%
- Travel in pairs minimum, maintain visual contact
- Set up emergency beacons every 500 meters

MENTAL PREPARATION:
- Study non-Euclidean geometry basics
- Practice distinguishing real vs. reflected horizons
- Learn to recognize signs of cognitive disruption from the harmonic frequencies
- Develop awareness of proprioceptive hallucinations
- Accept that normal physics rules may not apply

RESEARCH PREPARATION:
- Review all available footage of mass-movement patterns
- Study the blackout zone migration charts
- Learn to interpret bioacoustic signatures
- Familiarize yourself with known creature-mass behaviors

SCHEDULING:
- Plan visits during periods of minimal synchronization activity
- Avoid times when multiple blackout zones converge
- Limit initial exposure to 4 hours maximum
- Allow 48 hours for physical/mental recovery between visits

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES:
- Carry crystalline dissolvent spray
- Know symptoms of harmonic-induced psychosis
- Have signal flares that function in reality-distorted conditions
- Maintain constant communication with base camp
- Keep emergency teleport beacon accessible

DOCUMENTATION:
- Bring multiple backup recording devices
- Use crystalline-proof sample containers
- Take readings from multiple instruments simultaneously
- Document personal psychological states hourly
- Maintain detailed environmental logs

PROHIBITED ITEMS:
- Glass or crystal equipment (may resonate dangerously)
- Standard liquid containers (contents may be altered)
- Metallic jewelry or accessories
- Non-shielded electronic devices
- Temporal measurement devices (will malfunction)

POST-VISIT PROTOCOL:
- Undergo full decontamination
- Submit to psychological evaluation
- Document any lingering sensory effects
- Quarantine all samples and equipment
- Review recordings for reality distortions

Remember: This environment challenges basic assumptions about the separation between living and non-living matter. Maintain scientific objectivity but be prepared to question fundamental physical laws. Your perception of reality may be permanently altered by extended exposure.
