- Emergency ascent system

3. Specialized Gear
- Heat-resistant sampling tools
- Thermal imaging equipment

1. Deep Diving Certification
- Technical diving qualification
- Hot-water diving experience
- Decompression protocol training

2. Physical Conditioning
- Heat acclimation exercises
- Pressure tolerance training

- Schedule around bioluminescent coral's peak activity periods
- Allow for slow, careful approach and exit
