For visiting the Maintenance Spire chamber, I recommend the following preparations:

- Heat-resistant, moisture-wicking clothing (temperatures likely 30-40°C with high humidity)
- Sturdy boots with excellent grip (surfaces will be slick with condensation)

- Hard hat with attached headlamp
- Several backup light sources (the steam may limit visibility)
- Insulated gloves (hot surfaces, possible electrical hazards)
- Basic tool kit for emergencies
- Water-resistant pack for equipment
- Recording devices in waterproof cases

- Avoid direct contact with the metallic growths (potentially biohazardous)
- Watch for falling condensation (may be caustic)
- Stay on marked walkways/catwalks
- Bring a partner and maintain visual/audio contact
- Establish clear exit routes before exploring

DOCUMENTATION:
- Bring multiple cameras (different wavelengths if possible)
- Audio recording equipment (for mechanical patterns)
- Thermal imaging device
- Sample containers (if permitted)
- Notebook with water-resistant paper

NAVIGATION:

RESEARCH:

CONTINGENCIES:

- Schedule regular gear checks
