Preparation Guide for Visiting the Carbon Garden

ESSENTIAL GEAR:
- Advanced environmental suit with:
  * Multi-layer electromagnetic shielding
  * Self-contained air supply (minimum 24 hours)
  * Thermal regulation system
  * Static discharge protection
  * Reinforced insulation against electrical currents
- Backup power systems (isolated from main suit systems)
- Emergency beacon (EM-shielded)
- Specialized boots with non-conductive soles
- Faraday cage backpack for sensitive equipment

MONITORING EQUIPMENT:
- Multi-spectrum electromagnetic field detector
- Resonance frequency analyzer
- Static charge monitor
- Atmospheric composition sensor
- Ground current detector
- Structural integrity scanner for detecting safe paths

SAFETY PROTOCOLS:
1. Never travel alone - maintain minimum group size of three
2. Keep at least 50 meters between team members to prevent cascade failures
3. Avoid areas where field strength exceeds 2.5 tesla
4. Monitor standing wave patterns - retreat if harmonics approach material resonance frequencies
5. Regular suit integrity checks every 15 minutes
6. Maintain constant communication with base camp
7. No visits during peak electrical activity periods

HEALTH CONSIDERATIONS:
- Full medical screening for electromagnetic sensitivity
- Inner ear assessment (due to constant low-frequency exposure)
- Psychological evaluation for isolation/darkness adaptation
- Supplemental vitamin D recommended for extended visits
- Neural monitoring for effects of sustained exposure to EM fields

NAVIGATION:
- Standard GPS won't function - rely on ground-based triangulation
- Mark safe paths with non-conductive markers
- Map resonance zones and avoid crossing pattern boundaries
- Keep track of major discharge cycles to predict dangerous areas

RESEARCH GUIDELINES:
- No metal equipment unless specifically shielded
- Use carbon-fiber or ceramic tools when possible
- Maintain distance from primary filament structures
- Document pattern changes and electrical activity cycles
- Sample collection only with approved containers

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES:
1. Activate emergency shield if electrical activity spikes
2. Move to designated safe zones if suit integrity compromised
3. Use backup air supply if primary systems fail
4. Deploy emergency shelter if caught in major discharge event
5. Activate rescue beacon only as last resort (may attract charges)

PSYCHOLOGICAL PREPARATION:
- Meditation training recommended
- Dark adaptation exercises
- Exposure therapy to constant low-frequency noise
- Preparation for sensory monotony
- Training for maintaining focus in low-light conditions

TIME LIMITATIONS:
- Maximum recommended visit: 6 hours
- Mandatory rest period between visits: 12 hours
- Total exposure limit per week: 24 hours
- Extended stays require specialized preparation

Remember: The Carbon Garden operates on principles we don't fully understand. Respect the environment's patterns and cycles. Don't attempt to interact with or modify the filament structures. This is purely an observational expedition environment.
