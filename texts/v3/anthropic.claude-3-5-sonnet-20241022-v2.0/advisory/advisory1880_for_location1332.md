Preparation Guide for Visiting the Industrial Aviary Ecosystem

Essential Gear:
- Waterproof boots (mid-calf height minimum) - the floor is perpetually wet
- Breathable waterproof jacket with hood - protection from constant drips and bird activity
- N95 mask or respirator - for protection from fungal spores and old industrial residue
- Camera with low-light capabilities and weather sealing
- Sturdy gloves - surfaces may be sharp or degraded
- Small LED headlamp - for darker corners and backup lighting
- Moisture-resistant bag for electronics
- Change of dry clothes stored in waterproof bag
- Anti-fungal foot powder
- Hand sanitizer

Health Preparations:
- Updated tetanus shot
- Basic respiratory health check (due to fungal presence)
- Review any history of mold allergies
- Consider prophylactic antihistamines if sensitive to damp environments

Timing & Duration:
- Plan visit for 2-3 hours maximum due to moisture exposure
- Arrive 2 hours before dusk to witness both regular activity and the evening bird display
- Check weather forecast - heavy rain can make the space dangerous
- Consider seasonal bird activity patterns

Safety Considerations:
- Never go alone - buddy system essential
- Test floor stability before each step - concrete may be degraded
- Maintain distance from bird nests to avoid defensive behavior
- Watch for falling debris from ceiling/rafters
- Avoid touching fungi or disturbing established ecosystems
- Keep track of entry/exit points - space can be disorienting

Documentation Equipment (Optional):
- Sound recording equipment (waterproofed)
- Thermometer/humidity meter
- Small specimens containers for approved scientific collection
- Field notebook with waterproof pages

Behavioral Guidelines:
- Move slowly and deliberately to avoid startling birds
- Speak in low voices
- No flash photography
- Avoid touching or disturbing moss patterns
- Give wide berth to mammal nests/colonies
- No food/drinks inside (attracts wildlife)

Research Preparation:
- Study basic bird behavior patterns
- Learn to identify toxic vs. safe fungi
- Understand building architecture basics
- Review local wildlife regulations
- Learn emergency exit protocols for abandoned structures

Post-Visit Protocol:
- Thoroughly clean all equipment
- Dry all clothing completely
- Check for any hitchhiking spores/organisms
- Document observations promptly
- Monitor for any respiratory symptoms for 48 hours

Special Notes:
- Respect the space as an emerging ecosystem
- Consider bringing a naturalist or industrial archaeologist
- Plan multiple visits across seasons if possible
- Be prepared for strong emotional/psychological impact
- Accept that some areas may be inaccessible for safety
- Consider the site's historical significance while visiting

This preparation guide emphasizes both practical safety and mindful interaction with a unique transitional environment. The goal is to observe and document while minimizing impact on the delicate balance between industrial decay and ecological emergence.
