- Descend gradually with multiple acclimatization stops
- Maintain constant contact with surface team
- Monitor reality consistency readings
- Establish baseline measurements before entering the anomalous zone

- Never remain in one spot for more than 5 minutes
- Maintain physical movement to prevent phase-coupling
- Regular reality checks using standardized protocols
- Constant monitoring of suit integrity and phase state
- Document everything but avoid direct observation of transitions

3. Emergency Procedures
- Immediate activation of phase stabilization field if experiencing reality blur
- Use of quantum tether for emergency extraction
- Implementation of cognitive anchor protocols if experiencing identity dissolution
- Emergency ascent only as absolute last resort

CONTRAINDICATIONS:

- Quantum state susceptibility

- Fluid dynamics phobia

- Subsequent visits: Up to 2 hours with proper acclimation

POST-VISIT PROCEDURES:

- Cognitive baseline testing
- Phase state normalization therapy

ADDITIONAL NOTES:
- Bring no metal objects that could be affected by phase transitions
- Avoid making assumptions about causality while in the space
- Document but do not attempt to interact with the quasi-biological entities
- Be prepared for temporary loss of normal physical constants
- Accept that some observations may be fundamentally unrecordable
