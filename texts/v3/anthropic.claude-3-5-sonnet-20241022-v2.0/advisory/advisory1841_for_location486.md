For visiting this subterranean maintenance level, here's how a multiverse traveler should prepare:

Essential Gear:
- Waterproof boots (at least 8" high) with excellent grip for wet surfaces
- Moisture-wicking base layer clothing
- Lightweight, breathable long sleeves and pants (protection from pipes/rats)
- Small battery-powered fan or personal cooling device
- Headlamp with backup batteries (essential)
- Secondary light source (flashlight)
- Water-resistant backpack
- Portable water filter (for emergencies)
- Basic tools (multi-tool, adjustable wrench)
- Digital compass or mapping device (easy to get disoriented)

Health & Safety:
- Up-to-date tetanus shot
- Respiratory mask (N95 minimum) for mold/spores
- Anti-fungal cream
- Basic first aid kit
- Hand sanitizer
- Emergency antibiotics (consult doctor)
- Electrolyte packets (hot environment)
- Water (1 liter minimum)

Navigation & Documentation:
- Detailed infrastructure maps if available
- Permission documents/access credentials
- Communication device that works underground
- Camera with good low-light capability
- Notebook in waterproof case
- Markers for tracking return route

Special Considerations:
- Inform someone topside of your location/duration
- Plan for 2-3 hour maximum stays (heat stress)
- Learn basic maintenance robot protocols
- Study rat behavior patterns
- Bring small food offerings for rats (may help prevent aggression)
- Consider bringing a local guide

Mental Preparation:
- Practice breathing exercises (humid environment)
- Study grid patterns and navigation
- Prepare for sensory adjustment period
- Accept constant moisture/dripping
- Understand time distortion effects
- Practice low-light movement

Behavioral Guidelines:
- Move slowly and deliberately
- Respect rat territories
- Don't interfere with robots
- Avoid touching fungi
- Keep distance from steam vents
- Stay within lit areas when possible
- Monitor personal hydration
- Take regular breaks in drier areas

Remember: This environment is not designed for human comfort. The combination of heat, humidity, and confined space can be physically and psychologically challenging. Plan shorter visits initially to acclimatize.
