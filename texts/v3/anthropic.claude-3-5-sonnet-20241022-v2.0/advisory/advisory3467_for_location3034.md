Preparation Guide for Visiting the Industrial Gardens

Essential Equipment:
- Thermal regulation suit rated for near-freezing temperatures (0-4°C)
- Moisture-wicking base layers
- Anti-fog goggles or smart lenses with environmental data display
- Non-slip, sanitized footwear (to prevent contamination of the ecosystem)
- Personal air quality monitor
- Electromagnetic interference (EMI) shielded devices
- Water-resistant data tablet for documentation
- Emergency beacon (in case of separation from guide)

Medical Preparations:
- Up-to-date immunizations
- Antihistamines (the engineered pollen may trigger reactions)
- Cold-weather medications
- Humidity adaptation period recommended (2-3 days pre-visit)
- Medical clearance for those with respiratory sensitivities

Documentation Requirements:
- Biosecurity clearance
- Non-interference agreement
- Temporal synchronization certificate (to align with facility's artificial day/night cycles)
- Insurance coverage for engineered environment exposure

Physical Conditioning:
- Acclimatization to cold environments
- Balance training for narrow walkways
- Core strength for extended periods of standing
- Breathing exercises for high-humidity environments

Sensory Preparation:
- Practice in low-light conditions
- Adaptation to constant artificial illumination
- Familiarization with robot movement patterns
- Training to recognize emergency signals in near-silent environments

Behavioral Guidelines:
- Maintain prescribed distance from maintenance robots
- No sudden movements near pollinator creatures
- Follow designated paths only
- No touching of plants or equipment
- Strict adherence to airlock protocols
- Respect artificial night cycle zones

Special Considerations:
- Bring no organic materials from outside
- Accept temporal disorientation from perpetual light
- Prepare for limited verbal communication (sound-sensitive environment)
- Understanding of basic biosystem indicators
- Knowledge of emergency exit protocols

Recommended Study:
- Basic botany of engineered plants
- Artificial ecosystem dynamics
- Robotic maintenance patterns
- Bioluminescent organism behaviors
- Frost pattern navigation basics

Duration Guidelines:
- Maximum visit time: 4 hours per session
- Mandatory rest periods between visits
- Progressive exposure recommended for first-time visitors

Post-Visit Protocol:
- Full decontamination procedure
- 24-hour health monitoring
- Report any unusual reactions
- Submit observation data to facility
- Gradual return to normal day/night cycle

Remember:
- This is not a natural environment - treat it as a complex machine
- Maintain constant awareness of your position relative to exits
- Watch for frost pattern changes indicating system adjustments
- Stay with your guide at all times
- Report any unusual sensory experiences immediately

The industrial gardens require respect for both their biological and technological components. Successful visits depend on thorough preparation and strict adherence to protocols. Despite their beauty, these are working environments optimized for production, not human comfort.
