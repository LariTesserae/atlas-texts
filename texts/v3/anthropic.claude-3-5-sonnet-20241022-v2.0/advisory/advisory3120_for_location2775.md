Preparation Guide for Visiting the Living Cathedral

Essential Equipment:
- Waterproof, breathable clothing (the humidity is constant)
- Non-slip shoes with good grip (moss surfaces can be slick)
- Waterproof backpack
- Multiple moisture-wicking layers
- Backup set of dry clothes in sealed bags
- Headlamp with red light option (to observe without disrupting bioluminescence)
- Water filtration system (despite the spring, bring your own water initially)
- Anti-fungal powder/spray for skin and equipment
- Small towels for wiping condensation

Environmental Protection:
- Respiratory mask with HEPA filter (spores may be present)
- Disposable gloves
- Protective eyewear (for potential spores/debris)
- Antimicrobial clothing treatment before entry

Scientific/Documentation Equipment:
- Light meter (to measure bioluminescence patterns)
- Humidity/temperature sensors
- Non-invasive sampling tools if permitted
- Camera with low-light capabilities
- Notebook with waterproof pages

Health Considerations:
- Recent physical exam (especially respiratory system)
- Updated vaccinations
- Antihistamines (in case of allergic reactions)
- Basic first aid kit with fungal infection treatments
- Immune system check (those with compromised immunity should avoid visiting)

Cultural/Spiritual Preparations:
- Research ancient protocols for sacred spaces
- Practice silent movement and observation
- Meditation practice (to sync with the space's breathing rhythm)
- Journal for recording personal experiences
- Understanding of basic mycological ecosystems

Time Management:
- Plan for gradual acclimation period (minimum 30 minutes)
- Allow several hours for full experience
- Include time for sitting quietly to observe patterns
- Consider multiple visits if possible (different times/cycles)

Special Considerations:
- Avoid touching unknown organisms
- Maintain respectful distance from resident creatures
- No flash photography
- Minimize sound/disruption
- Follow established pathways on firmer moss sections
- Be prepared for potential disorientation (environment is very different from surface world)

Exit Protocol:
- Clean/decontaminate equipment before leaving
- Change into clean clothes
- Document any unusual reactions/experiences
- Monitor health for several days after visit

Mental Preparation:
- Accept that this is a living system that may react to your presence
- Be prepared for unusual sensory experiences
- Understand that time perception may alter in this environment
- Practice patience and careful observation
- Be ready to exit if overwhelmed

Ecological Etiquette:
- Nothing removed from site
- Nothing left behind
- Minimal impact movement
- Respect for all life forms encountered
- Understanding that you are a guest in an ancient ecosystem

This preparation guide assumes you have necessary permissions and access protocols. Given the unique nature of this environment, consider seeking guidance from experienced visitors or local experts before your first visit.
