Preparation Guidelines for Visiting the Greenhouse Anomaly

ESSENTIAL GEAR:
- Moisture-wicking, breathable clothing in natural fibers
- Waterproof boots with excellent grip (the living floor may be slippery and unstable)
- Multiple changes of socks (ambient moisture will be constant)
- Wide-brimmed hat to protect from water droplets and spore releases
- Goggles or protective eyewear (to shield from spores and prevent spatial disorientation)
- Respiratory protection (N95 minimum) with several replacements
- Water-resistant notebook and multiple writing tools
- Analog compass (electronic devices may be unreliable)
- Manual wind-up watch (to track subjective vs. actual time)
- Rope marked at regular intervals (to maintain spatial reference points)
- Chemical light sticks (electrical lights may behave unpredictably)

PHYSICAL PREPARATION:
- Practice meditation and breathing exercises (the space may induce altered states)
- Develop strong proprioception through balance training
- Build core strength for maintaining stability on shifting surfaces
- Train peripheral vision awareness
- Practice identifying cardinal directions without visual cues

MENTAL PREPARATION:
- Study basic botany and mycology to better understand the environment
- Learn to recognize signs of spatial disorientation
- Practice grounding techniques for maintaining psychological stability
- Familiarize yourself with non-Euclidean geometry concepts
- Develop techniques for maintaining temporal awareness

SAFETY PROTOCOLS:
- Never enter alone; maintain buddy system
- Establish regular physical contact with partner to confirm mutual reality
- Set maximum exploration time beforehand (suggested: 2 hours initially)
- Agree on clear verbal and non-verbal communication signals
- Plan exit strategy before entering
- Mark entry point clearly with non-organic markers

BEHAVIORAL GUIDELINES:
- Move slowly and deliberately
- Maintain regular verbal communication with partner
- Avoid touching transmuting objects directly
- Don't attempt to collect samples without proper containment
- If time-sense becomes distorted, focus on breathing rhythm
- If spatial awareness fails, close eyes and rely on rope markers

HEALTH PRECAUTIONS:
- Get allergy screening beforehand
- Carry antihistamines
- Bring glucose tablets (metabolic effects unknown)
- Have medical check-up after each visit
- Monitor for unusual symptoms for 48 hours after exposure

DOCUMENTATION:
- Take baseline measurements at entry
- Record subjective experiences in real-time
- Note any physical reactions
- Track time distortions
- Document any apparent patterns in bioluminescence
- Map spatial inconsistencies

CONTRAINDICATIONS:
- Avoid if claustrophobic or prone to vertigo
- Do not enter if immunocompromised
- Psychiatric stability required
- No pre-existing respiratory conditions
- Not recommended for pregnant individuals

RECOVERY PERIOD:
- Plan for 24-hour rest period after visit
- Arrange for psychological debrief
- Document any dreams or unusual thoughts
- Monitor sleep patterns
- Stay hydrated
- Avoid altered states for 72 hours after exposure

Remember: This environment operates on its own logic. Maintaining connection to baseline reality is crucial. If you begin to feel yourself "dissolving" into the space's patterns, implement immediate extraction protocols. The boundary between observer and environment may become uncomfortably permeable - maintain strong sense of self through physical sensations and verbal affirmations.
