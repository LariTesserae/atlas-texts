Preparation Guide for Visiting the Living Chamber

ESSENTIAL GEAR:
- Sealed environmental suit with:
  * Self-contained breathing apparatus (minimum 12 hours)
  * HEPA-grade filtration
  * Antimicrobial coating
  * Moisture-wicking inner layer
- Multiple light sources:
  * UV-shielded headlamp
  * Backup LED lights
  * Chemical glowsticks (avoid disrupting bioluminescent life)
- Recording equipment:
  * Time-lapse capable camera
  * Audio recorder with infrasound detection
  * Environmental sensors (temp, humidity, air composition)

PROTECTIVE MEASURES:
- Full vaccination schedule plus broad-spectrum antifungals
- Skin sealant coating to prevent spore absorption
- Non-metallic tools and equipment (due to metal-consuming fungi)
- Disposable outer layer that can be safely abandoned
- Chemical decontamination supplies for exit procedure

NAVIGATION & SAFETY:
- Tether system with non-organic rope
- Multiple reference points for orientation
- Motion trackers (the space's geometry can shift)
- Emergency shelter pod (self-contained, spore-proof)
- Signal beacons for marking exit route

RESEARCH PROTOCOLS:
- Minimize physical contact with surfaces
- Use non-invasive sampling methods
- Document any patterns that form from unavoidable contact
- Maintain constant movement to prevent tissue bonding
- Time all stays under 4 hours initially

PSYCHOLOGICAL PREPARATION:
- Meditation training for spatial disorientation
- Experience with non-Euclidean environments
- Comfort with biological uncertainty
- Strong stress management skills
- Team of at least three people

CONTRAINDICATIONS:
- No metallic implants or devices
- No history of fungal infections
- No claustrophobia or spatial anxiety
- No immune system compromises

POST-VISIT PROCEDURES:
- Full decontamination protocol
- 72-hour quarantine period
- Spore exposure monitoring
- Equipment disposal/deep cleaning
- Psychological debriefing

SPECIAL NOTES:
- Bring minimal provisions - nothing organic
- Accept that some equipment may be lost
- Prepare for time perception distortion
- Document any dream changes post-visit
- Consider bringing offerings of sterile mineral nutrients

This environment requires extreme caution due to its biological activity and spatial instability. Treat it as simultaneously fragile and dangerous - your presence will affect it, and it will affect you. Proper preparation is essential for both safety and research validity.
