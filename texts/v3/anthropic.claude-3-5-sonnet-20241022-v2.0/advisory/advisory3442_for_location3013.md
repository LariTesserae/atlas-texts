Preparation Guide for Tunnel Exploration

ESSENTIAL GEAR:
- Analog equipment only - no complex electronics
- Wind-up flashlight and chemical light sticks as backups
- Mechanical analog watch (pocket watch preferred)
- Analog compass (though be prepared for unreliability)
- Traditional paper maps and waterproof notebook
- Glass or ceramic containers (avoid metal)
- Natural fiber clothing (cotton, wool) - synthetic materials may react unpredictably
- Rubber-soled boots and thick gloves (electrically insulating)
- Disposable coveralls to protect clothing from the wall condensation
- Respirator with biological and chemical filters
- Goggles with side protection
- Several bandanas or cloths for emergency face coverage

PHYSICAL PREPARATION:
- Practice crawling and crouching for extended periods
- Build tolerance for humid, warm environments
- Learn meditation techniques to maintain calm in spatially distorting environments
- Practice operating equipment by touch/muscle memory
- Get vaccinated for standard pathogens (though likely ineffective against hybrid organisms)

MENTAL PREPARATION:
- Study basic electrical theory to understand charge phenomena
- Learn to identify common fungi and insects as baseline references
- Practice maintaining focus during sensory disorientation
- Develop techniques for reality-grounding (counting, touching physical objects)
- Accept that normal physics may not apply
- Prepare for potential time dilation effects

SAFETY PROTOCOLS:
- Never explore alone
- Use buddy system with rope connections
- Establish time limits and strict return triggers
- Define clear communication signals
- Avoid touching any surfaces unnecessarily
- Do not attempt to collect specimens
- If swarms approach, remain still
- If time distortion occurs, focus on physical anchors
- When in doubt, retreat

CONTRAINDICATIONS:
- Those with claustrophobia
- Anyone with electronic medical devices
- People sensitive to electromagnetic fields
- Those prone to spatial disorientation
- Individuals with compromised immune systems

DOCUMENTATION:
- Bring mechanical camera (no electronics)
- Use graphite pencils for notes (ink may react)
- Make regular marking points with chalk
- Record observations verbally to companion

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES:
- If lost in spatial distortion, stop moving
- If electronic discharge occurs, insulate yourself
- If swarms become aggressive, use chemical lights to disorient
- If time dilation intensifies, focus on breathing rhythm
- If wall liquid contacts skin, immediately clean with sterile water
- Always maintain awareness of exit route

RESEARCH FIRST:
- Study historical infrastructure maps
- Interview maintenance workers
- Research local power grid history
- Consult urban exploration groups
- Review any similar anomaly reports

AFTER-VISIT PROTOCOL:
- Quarantine exposed equipment
- Document all temporal inconsistencies
- Monitor for unusual symptoms
- Dispose of contaminated clothing
- Record detailed observations while fresh

Remember: This location represents a profound breakdown of normal physical laws. The key to survival is maintaining flexibility in your expectations while holding firmly to basic safety protocols. Accept that some experiences may be impossible to fully document or explain.
