I would be just inside the rim, where the last covered passage opens onto black stone. From there I could see the pale terraces below the ridge, though I would rarely go far enough out to lose contact with the growth.

At first I think I would be a watcher. I would flatten myself against the warm rock and stay until the light changed, difficult to distinguish from a loose skin. But the young would come through the passage behind me, and the opening would not be wide enough for us all. I would have to move.

I am one of the hand-length inhabitants, with a small notch in one membrane. It makes my descents turn slightly, so I have learned to push away at an angle and land where I mean to. When the air is especially warm, I can cross from the rim growth to a trunk near the central chamber without touching anything between.

I return to a hollow midway up that trunk. Its entrance has grown smooth around the passage of bodies, and just inside it narrows sharply. There are three young there, not all mine. I touch each one with a pad when I arrive. Sometimes one is asleep far enough back that I have to reach between the others.

On a night when a new frond opens near the rim, I go to feed and find the passage crowded. Two adults are coming up, and behind them a young one has stopped where the tube bends. I press flat. The adults pass over me, their pads briefly dimpling my back, but the young one stays where it is.

I turn and touch the lining beside it. The plant is firm there, though beyond the bend the wall gives a little under my weight. I go through first. After a while the young one follows, pausing at the place where the passage widens, then climbing past me toward the fresh red threads.

Later I return by way of the central chamber. A fallen skin catches on my hind limb and travels with me for several steps before dropping into the dust. Above, the sky is still visible between the trunks. At the far wall, another creature is climbing toward the same hollow. We reach it together, and one of us waits while the other enters.
