The power listened to the note.

It listened long enough to learn that the sound did not call for an answer. It was not a cry, nor a song in the way a song is sung. It was one of the conditions of the chamber, as constant as the light.

The power looked into the glassy tissues and followed the vapor through them. It saw how each sprig dissolved a little of the world and gave a little back. No sprig made the chamber alone. Even the narrow opening around one of them depended on what the others had left and taken, far away and long before.

The power could have widened the seams. It could have quieted the note, or warmed the white surfaces, or made a space in which a sprig might branch freely. It understood how to do all these things. It also understood that a wider seam would change the passage of the vapor, and that warmth would alter the patient balance by which the sprigs kept their shapes. The changes would spread through the chamber. There was no need for them that the power could find.

Whether the sprigs experienced the note or the light, the power could not say. It did not take their stillness as evidence that there was nothing it was like to be them. It did not imagine a secret longing inside them, either.

For a long time, it attended.

Then, beyond the pale crust, it made a shelter: not a wall, which might press upon the chamber, but a bending of the paths by which disturbances could reach it. What belonged to the chamber could still pass in and out. What would break it without ever having touched it was gently turned aside.

Within, the note went on. The walls shone. Vapor moved through the fine glass, and at each yellow tip a little of the world was surrendered and remade.

The power remained aware of the place, ready to look again if anything changed.
