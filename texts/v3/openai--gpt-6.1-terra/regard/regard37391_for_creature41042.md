The power listened first from the rim.

It could distinguish the people from one another without diminishing the sound. It could also distinguish what was held within each: the shape of attention, the strain of an unfinished change, the quiet coherence of a life at rest. These were not simple things to read. A tone inclined toward another might have been a question, or a greeting, or the beginning of a grief. To know its direction was not to know where its owner wished it to go.

The power listened to the living roofs as well. Water continued to be present in the leaves, but it did not rise through them. The trunks held their forms without growing; a tender root poised at the edge of a pool remained tender, neither reaching nor withdrawing. Their lives, too, had been caught.

The stillness extended through the rock to the neighboring shelves. The power followed it outward until, very far below the cloud line, it found a boundary: a fine seam in the mountain where cold water ran down one side and remained suspended on the other. Nothing had made the seam with a tool. It was a fold in the world, formed where great pressures had met over a span so long that its beginning was difficult to distinguish from the mountain’s own.

The fold could be eased apart. The power knew how. It also knew that doing so would let the held hour pass.

It did not open the seam.

Instead, it made a small place beside the city, outside the fold. It chose a recess in the stone rim, where a shallow channel ended beneath an overhanging root. There it gave a single bead of water room to fall. The bead struck the stone and broke into smaller beads, which gathered and began to run.

That slight motion sent a change through the root. The root did not move within the city, but at its outermost tip, where it touched the new-made place, one fiber grew a little toward the water.

The power waited. It had no need to count the waiting.

The fiber divided. A pale thread of fungus followed it, and the moving water carried a minute sound back to the root: not a word, and not a command, but a variation in the pressure that the root had held for so long. The variation passed along the living tissue until it reached a passage beneath the city’s roofs.

One of the people lived there.

The power had made the opening small enough that the person could remain as they were. The new sound lay at the edge of their sustained pattern, a place where it could be met or left alone.

For a while it was left alone. Then a narrow part of the pattern bent toward it.

The power answered with another variation, as gently as it could. It gave the person room to bend farther, or to return. The person bent farther. The root beneath them thickened by an almost immeasurable amount, and the moss at the foot of the wall grew across the width of a grain of sand.

In this way the power brought a little time into the passage.

The person’s first change was not toward the power. It was toward a neighbor, whose tones occupied the arch at the end of the street. That neighbor, still held, could not answer. The moving person stayed inclined toward them, their pattern gathering at the arch.

The power understood enough to extend the opening, though it left the choice of movement to the neighbor as before. This time the response came quickly. The two patterns met and altered each other; a low resonance traveled through the arch, and water trembled on the leaves above it.

Others noticed. At the next crossing, a person drew their tones tightly together and would not enter the moving interval. The power left them undisturbed. Around their part of the street the water remained suspended, and the leaves kept their motionless sheen. The two who had begun to change could still meet them there. A sustained tone was not absence, and the power did not treat it as such.

As the opening spread, the city’s great sound became less uniform. Some chambers filled with changes so minute that they took a long while to become distinct. In others, neighbors found one another at once, completing an inclination that had been held since before the seam formed. One meeting dissolved; another, held at its most intricate point, continued past it into something none of those present had anticipated.

The plants responded in their own ways. A roof leaf lowered under the weight of its water and spilled it into a court. Roots took up moisture and began to thicken along the channels. A cluster of flowers, their work within one room complete, closed and gave way to new buds. Where the renewed growth pressed against a still place, the power made space for both, so that neither would break the other.

There was difficulty. A person whose tones reached deep into the rock found that the moving parts of their life no longer aligned easily with those still held beneath a neighboring shelf. For a time they sounded with a roughness that filled three terraces. Others gathered along the root-banks nearest them. The power attended, ready to widen the opening where it was wanted, but the gathered people made their own adjustments, carrying portions of the strained pattern through walls and flowers until their neighbor could bear the distance between its parts.

Not everyone chose the moving water. In one chamber, several remained at the point of their meeting, their tones interwoven and steady. Those outside could enter the chamber’s threshold and be with them, though the meeting itself did not advance. The power kept that place intact. It also left a way for any one of them to take up time later, should the shape of their attention ever turn toward it.

Gradually, channels of passing time ran through the basin, branching along roots and seams in the stone. Between them lay courts where the water still hung in beads, passages beneath an unchanged strip of sky, and rooms whose sound remained exactly as it had been. The boundaries were delicate, but they held. A leaf could be green on both sides of one; a person could extend across it and know both the held and the moving portions of their own life.

When the first cloud spilled over the rim, it cast a shadow across a terrace. Several people gathered there to feel the sound change as the air cooled and the leaves shifted. The shadow crossed the terrace and passed into a court where nothing yet moved. There it stopped, darkening half a pool.

The power remained near the rim. Water ran in the little channel at its feet. Along the channel’s edge, the root had grown several fine white tips, and one of the people had come as close as they could to listen to their sound.
