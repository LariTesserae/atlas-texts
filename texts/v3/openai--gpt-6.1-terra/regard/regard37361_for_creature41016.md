The power comes first to the dry ground above the chamber. It does not break through. It feels the rind from rootward to tipward, and waits through many crackings, until it can tell the sound of growth from the sound of a fold held under too much strain.

There is one such fold. It tightens each time the deep note passes through, and does not loosen afterward. The power lays a little moisture along its outer edge. The plant takes this in slowly. When the next note comes, the fold gives, and the note passes on.

The mite is hidden then. The power waits for it too.

At last a thread grows across the chamber, and the mite comes out upon it. Partway across, the thread begins to withdraw. The mite turns and reaches the wall before the thread has shortened by half. It disappears into a seam.

The power does not make the threads last longer. The plant may need to draw them back, and the mite has learned to watch them. But where the thread touched the wall, the power leaves a slight roughness, a place the mite can catch hold if it arrives in haste. The plant grows around it and keeps it.

The grain of stone remains buried. The mite searches near it once, passing over the place without stopping. Later, at the base of another fold, the power brings a little grit close enough to the surface that the plant’s growth lifts three grains into the chamber. They settle among the knots on the floor.

One is too large for the mite to move. Another slips between two knots and cannot be reached. The third the mite finds and rolls ahead of itself. It presses the grain into a seam, feeds there, and leaves the grain beside the opening. By the next time it emerges, a new leaf has partly covered it.

The power watches the leaf rise and does not turn it aside. There are still the other grains, and more beyond the rind, if they are needed.

In time a bead gathers on the ceiling. In it is a view of the chamber from outside: the pale green body folded into the dark, its new edges fine and bright. The power looks at that view until the plant absorbs the bead.

Then it withdraws from the ground, leaving no passage behind it. The chamber continues to grow. A shadow stretches across the floor, and a leaf begins, just afterward, to lift into it.
