The power listened at the wall.

It could have stopped the pumps. It could have lifted the chamber whole from the made ground and set it among the roots of a forest, with rain falling into it and birds calling overhead. But the water came through that ground, and the plants drew their scant nourishment from what the water carried. The pumps did work beyond the wall. Stopping them would change things far beyond the little room.

The power stayed a while, attending to what was already there.

Under the ferns, the flatworm pressed against the concrete and glided over a grain of black sediment. Its body flexed around a root. Something smaller than itself moved nearby, and the worm changed direction.

The power did not know whether the continuous trembling troubled it. The worm had never known stillness, and the power, for all its wisdom, would not invent an answer on its behalf.

So it made one small place quieter.

A patch of concrete beneath the thickest roots received the vibration less readily than the floor around it. The water passed over it as before. A loose root could still brush the worm there; the movements of its prey could still reach it. But the great roar came softened through the floor.

For some time the worm remained elsewhere. It found food beneath a browning frond, crossed a narrow strip of bare concrete, and returned to the border. Eventually it passed over the quieter patch. It lingered there, then moved on. Over the following days it came back often, though it continued to hunt among the other roots.

The power watched the water too, tracing it back through brick and slag. Far above the chamber, in a service duct, a pipe had begun to fail. A dark bead formed at a seam and hung there. What the pipe carried had no place among the ferns.

The power drew a maintenance worker’s attention to the bead. She put down her lamp, touched the seam with a gloved finger and found the leak. Before the end of the day the pipe had been isolated. It was repaired two mornings later.

In the chamber, nothing marked the repair. Water entered through the pinholes and passed through the grate. A fly settled on the wired glass. Beneath the ferns, the worm found a morsel caught against a root and began to feed.

The power remained long enough to see that the water stayed clear. Then it left the quiet patch where it was, beneath the sheltering fronds, and went to attend to other things.
