The power came to the basin without parting its water. It found the seam, and the chamber beyond, and the lives packed so closely together there.

For a long time it did nothing. Not because the chamber was perfect: the grazing animals sometimes went hungry, and some of the small ones were eaten before they reached the ring. In the coldest months a frond could stiffen around a leg and hold it fast. Yet the same cold slowed the creature’s need for food and kept the growth from filling every passage.

The power followed the water out through the seam. Above it, in the floor of the basin, a slab of stone had begun to settle. Another winter would press it down upon the crack. The seam would close, slowly enough that the growth would use much of what remained in the chamber before dying.

There were other cracks nearby, but none led into the chamber. Opening one from within would break the walls and bring a rush of silt. The power considered the slab instead.

It found a thin fault beneath its far edge and let a little water enter. There the water froze, though the season above was warm. The ice widened the fault by less than the width of a filament. The slab shifted and came to rest on a buried ridge of stone.

The next click reached both walls of the chamber. Much later, the packed vegetation drew tight.

At the ceiling, the visitor backed out from under the ring. One grain had slipped away from its neighbors. The creature felt along the gap, pressed against the grain, and moved it a small distance. Then it grazed there, where the disturbed covering had exposed a fresh film.

The slab would settle again in time, but not for many seasons. The seam remained narrow. Cold water entered it; traces of the basin’s winter followed. When the growth stiffened, one of the smaller animals was held between two fronds. Its threads moved against the pale surface, then grew still.

The power eased a little water between the fronds, enough to separate them. The animal worked free and disappeared among the cups.

Afterward the water passed through the seam as before. Silt continued to gather in the corner, and the rock slowly acquired its skin. The power remained near the basin, not holding the chamber unchanged, but attending to the stone that sheltered it and the water on which it depended.
