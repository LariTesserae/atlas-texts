The power came without a shadow. Nothing in the basin fell silent. Air continued through the chambers, and water pressed through the seams, and a creature at the edge of a pool lifted one limb and set it down again.

For a long time the power attended to the place. It followed water into passages too narrow for mist, where films grew upon the stone. It felt the hum through the pads of the pale animals and learned how a change in its pitch could draw one of them across a ridge. It watched the little cases open when a droplet reached them, and watched them close as the stone dried.

Beneath an overhang, a cluster of translucent beads lay in a seam. The water there had begun to recede. By the time the next mist came, the seam would be dry.

Most of the beads were already empty. Within three of them, tiny forms shifted, too small yet to leave.

There was water in the rock a short distance above. It passed through a fine crack and emerged on the other side of the ridge, where it fed a broad film and a pool inhabited by a pale creature. The power traced it back through the stone, then farther still, until it found a place where the passage widened and water moved slowly along its floor.

There it lifted a grain of mineral from the water and set it against the wall of the passage. Another settled beside it, and another. Over the following days, the water laid down more. A small lip formed, scarcely higher than the grit around it. Part of the flow gathered against it and entered a hairline crack leading toward the sheltered seam. Most continued to the pool beyond the ridge.

On the sixth day a bead of water appeared above the cluster. It grew heavy and slipped down the stone. By then one of the three small forms had stopped moving. Water reached the other two, and remained around them.

The lip held through the dry interval. When the deeper flow increased again, it loosened and went away, grain by grain. Water passed as it had before. The broad film on the far side of the ridge continued to grow, and the creature in the pool made clean arcs through it with its brushes.

One of the two young came out of its bead. It fed in the seam and remained there through many changes in the sound beneath it. The other emerged later, during a wet spell, and was carried out into the basin by a sudden thread of water. The power followed it to a shallow hollow, where it found moisture and a little film.

The young creature stayed there as the water thinned. When the hollow dried, it did not move again.

The power remained for a while beside it. Then it turned its attention back through the stone, to the living passages, and to the other small creature still feeding beneath the overhang.

The hum filled the basin. In the lee of the ridge, a drop struck a pool, and after it another.
