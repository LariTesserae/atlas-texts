The power came without crossing the sky. There was no change in the light, and the heat went on gathering in the hollow.

It attended first to the compression beneath the floor. It felt the grains passing through the plants, the collars widening, the young rock accepting what the roots returned to it. It found that the shadow of a blade could be present before the blade itself, and that this was not a wound or an error. The hollow was making itself in more than one order.

The power waited through many compressions. It had no need to count them, but counting let it learn the pace of the place. It learned, too, that each plant used the shared machinery a little differently: one retained a grain for a long time in its outermost blade; another sent its grains down almost as soon as they formed. Their separateness was part of the work.

At length the power noticed a narrow seam beneath the smallest plant. Gas passed along it to the roots. As the rock grew, the seam would sometimes close; usually another opened before the plant had drawn the last nourishment from it. In one of the hollow’s possible futures, no other seam opened in time.

The power did not remove that future. It could see others in which the plant died and left space for something the hollow had not yet made, and it could not say that those futures were lesser. But it saw a way to give the plant another chance within the one narrow passage it had noticed.

While the pressure below was easing, the power loosened a mineral grain resting at the seam’s edge. The grain rolled into the opening and remained there. If the seam closed around it, a fine channel might be left on either side.

Nothing else changed. The plant went on passing grains through its chambers. Its youngest blade cast a shadow, then did not, then did again.

The power stayed until the next compression. The toothed edges turned inward; a leaf clicked softly. When the pressure released, the blades opened. The grain remained in the seam.

Afterward the power kept the hollow in its knowledge. It would not need to watch every moment. If something came that could destroy the place without knowing what it was, the power would be able to notice. For now there was afternoon light, warmth under the pale floor, and room for the mountain to continue.
