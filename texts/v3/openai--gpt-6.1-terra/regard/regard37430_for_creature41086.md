The power noticed the street at the moment a pressure door opened. It noticed the force passing through the roof and the torn edge of the mat afterward. It noticed, too, the repair: charged particles finding longer paths around the breaks, cells at the edges dividing slowly into the exposed places.

It could have stopped the door.

The water held behind it would then have found another way. Some would have risen through the foundations above the harbor. Some would have remained in the pipes, growing warmer and losing the substances carried away by each release. The mat might have been spared the next shock and killed by the stillness that followed.

The power waited through several reversals. It followed the water into the city and back. It learned which flows fed the green filaments, which sustained the layers in the pipes, and which reached the roof with the mineral differences the mat could use. It found that the pressure door opened more violently than it once had: a fitting deep in the rock had narrowed as mineral accumulated against it.

In a short while, by the measure of the city above, the door would open hard enough to lift the roof flake on which most of the mat grew. The flake would strike the staircase and break. A few threads might remain attached to the roof; they might not.

The power entered the fitting where the mineral had collected. It loosened the deposit a grain at a time. The released grains traveled onward in the current. Most settled in a broad chamber where they would do no harm; some passed into the street and lodged among the paving stones.

When the door next opened, the chord was lower. The roof flake stayed in place. The mat’s edges lifted and settled again with little damage.

The change in pressure moved through the machinery. A vane turned, a shutter narrowed, and water passed through the opposite windows a little more quickly. Several green filaments came loose from the balcony. One caught on a cable and held there, its divided tip extending into the light.

The power remained to see whether the adjustment would last. Mineral began to gather again inside the fitting, though more slowly than before. The power altered its inner surface so that the current could carry most of the grains onward. It left enough roughness for the thin-cased organisms living there to find purchase.

Over many cycles the mat spread beyond the roof flake. Some of it reached the fastening where the second current passed, and grew densely along the boundary between the waters. At one reversal a corner of the flake shifted despite the gentler flow. Threads stretched and broke. The living edge closed around the tear.

The power had made no promise that the flake would stay forever, or that the street would remain unchanged. It watched as the mat recovered, then turned its attention to the water leaving the street, following it toward the foundations of the city. Behind it, the door opened and closed; the vanes turned; the dark growth continued across the roof.
