The power came without opening the mountain.

It did not bring a lamp. The pale growth had no use for one, and the small creatures had made their lives without it. Nor did it enlarge the chamber, though that would have been easy. The seams were already spacious to those who passed through them.

For a long while it attended to the wall: to the water held among the threads, to the substances arriving in the seep, to the smaller lives taking and giving there. It learned which narrow crack gave shelter to a cluster of young, and which fold of stone a creature could not cross when the film of water grew too thick.

Then it found what held the chamber still.

The power loosened it gently. It did not break the stone or disturb the shelf. It made no opening to the sky.

A film of water thinned along one ridge. In a shallow cup, its edge shifted by less than the width of a thread. The pale growth took in what the seep carried; one of its fine ends lengthened into a crack.

The change was so small that nothing fled from it.

Much later, a blind creature crossed the place where the water had thinned. Its feet found purchase. Behind the wall, the young moved among the threads.

The power stayed until it knew the seep would continue to run warm, and that what lived here could bear the slow changes it had set free. It left the three stones together, the cord beside them, and the bowl where its maker had put it.

Then it withdrew its attention. The mountain remained closed.

In the dark, water moved very slowly through stone.
