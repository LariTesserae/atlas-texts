The power came from a place where things happened one after another, and at first it listened for a change in the great noise.

There was none. The blue charge did not travel along the filaments. A silver fish did not turn its eye. The great animals held their fans open, and the notes of their calls filled the water around them, each in its proper place.

The power knew how to set a sea in motion. It could have sent currents among the terraces and carried the sand-grain creatures through the valves. The fish would have fled the hunters, and the hunters would have followed; the fans would have folded and opened again.

It did not do this.

It drew close to one of the great animals. Its legs entered the plateau through collars of interwoven tissue, and the broad plates of its body sheltered a multitude of smaller lives. The power attended to it as carefully as it could, seeking to understand what being alive was like for a creature whose call filled the water without ever beginning or ending.

It found no plea to be released, and no assurance that release would be a gift. It found a life unlike its own, and knew that its understanding had limits.

So the power made a place for its attention in the sea. It was not a chamber or a shelter. The water remained clear through it, and the animal’s lowest tones occupied it as fully as before. Nothing was taken from the animal, and nothing was required of it. The power held itself open to whatever it could know of that life, however long such knowing might require, or whatever knowing might mean here.

Beyond the plateau, where time could carry dangers toward it, the power kept watch. Should some other force come intending to break the terraces or set the sea running, it would meet that force before it reached the living ground.

Among the fronds, the fish hung in their crescents. The machinery filled the water with its immense roar. The power listened to the great animal and, through the animal, to the plateau and the innumerable lives within it.

The noise did not diminish. It continued to fill the bright water, and there was room in the power’s attention for all of it.
