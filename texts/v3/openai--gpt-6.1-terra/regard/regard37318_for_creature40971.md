The power came to the plateau without parting the water. For a long while it did nothing that could be seen.

It followed a warm current through the forest and waited where the current divided. It watched the pale undersides of leaves turn toward it and away. It entered a stretched distance beside a root and found, far within it, a small animal feeding on the film that covered the root’s surface. The animal’s limbs moved so slowly that the film had time to grow between one touch and the next.

Beside a stone ring, the broad, flat inhabitant lay with one edge folded beneath it. Sediment had settled over its back. Among the grains were several of the tiny cups that grew on the stones, and these opened and closed as the water passed. The power considered lifting the folded edge, then waited. After a time the animal lifted it for itself. Water flowed underneath, bearing a loose veil of spores, and the animal settled farther across the ring.

The power went on through the forest.

A grazer was feeding along a band of leaves newly turned by the current. Farther ahead, a great braided trunk had come apart near the bed. Its stems had spread over the undergrowth, and a slow rain of torn tissue was falling from them. The small transparent creatures that lived in the trunk were moving among the loose strands, finding places to shelter.

The grazer continued toward the fallen stems. It entered among them and fed for a while, but when it turned, the strands tightened around its body. It drew itself back and tried another way. Each movement brought more fibres against its skin. The seam of its mouth opened and closed without finding a leaf.

The power touched the fallen trunk. It could have drawn every strand clear at once. Instead it held the outer stems apart while the smaller creatures withdrew into their folds. Then it eased one strand at a time away from the grazer. The animal remained coiled where it was until a pale leaf brushed against its side. Its mouth opened along the leaf’s margin, and it began to feed.

The power kept a way open until the grazer had passed through. Behind it, the stems came together again, loosely enough for water to move between them. In the years that followed, they took root there, making a low arch crowded with fans and translucent veils. Grazers passed beneath it, leaving shining lines along the leaves.

The power stayed near the plateau. Sometimes, when a heavy fall of sediment covered the cups on a stone, a current arrived and lifted it away. Once, a young grazer followed an old trail into a fold of distance from which the trail did not emerge. A line of pale leaves turned ahead of it, leading back to water where other grazers fed.

Much continued without help. Leaves grew across the impossible gaps. The silver belly passed above the crowns, and small creatures rose and settled in its wake. The brighter clearing remained bare, though spores entered it with every current.

From time to time the power rested beside the broad inhabitant at the stone ring. Water passed beneath them both. The animal gathered what it needed, and the power watched the forest lift and fold across the plateau.
