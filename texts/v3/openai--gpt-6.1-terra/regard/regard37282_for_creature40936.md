The power came without light.

It could have made the plain visible from one end to the other, but the living things there were fitted to darkness. Instead it attended to what the water carried: the faint mineral seepage, the changes in pressure along the walls, the substances passing between the films and the tissues of the fronds.

Beneath one of the long ridges, a living frond lay folded over itself. Its base was caught in a joint between two blocks. The blocks had shifted over a great span of time, closing the joint until the seepage through it had nearly stopped. The part of the frond nearest the base was thinning. Farther out, its channels still carried water, and the microbes within them still did their work.

The power knew the frond was alive. It did not know that the frond wished for anything, or that the fading of its channels was an injury as another creature might experience one. It remained with it for a time, watching water enter the narrow spaces and leave them again.

The ridge was settling. In a few centuries, perhaps less, another block would slip and close the joint entirely. The frond would die back from its base. Some of its living tissue might persist elsewhere; its remains would feed the films, and other growth would take hold over it.

The power moved a little grit from the joint.

It did so slowly, a grain at a time, letting each grain settle close by. It left the blocks where they were. When the passage was open, water began to move through it again, carrying mineral traces from beneath the masonry. The change was small. Over the years that followed, the pale film along the joint grew thicker, and a new edge formed on the frond.

There were other narrow places in the ridge. The power opened some of them and left others alone, where a pocket of worms or a delicate branching film had made its home in the shelter of the silt. It did not make the seepage stronger than the beds could bear. It watched what happened downstream.

A loose slab continued to settle. A grove folded beneath its own weight. Fine grains passed along a channel and came to rest against a wall whose builders had been gone for an immense time.

The power stayed to see the frond’s new edge lengthen. When it had spread across the stone, a tiny crustacean came to graze on the film beneath it. Much later, the frond split along an old fold, and both halves went on living.

The water remained black almost everywhere. Far above, the weak blue thinning came and went with the seasons.
