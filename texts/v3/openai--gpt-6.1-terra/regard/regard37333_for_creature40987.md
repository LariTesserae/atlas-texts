The power came to the rim and waited.

It could have entered anywhere. It chose a place where the stone was bare, and rested there until the hunters had altered their paths around its presence. Beneath it, water sounded in the mouths of the rim. The chord was not music made for anyone, though it could be heard as music, and the power listened without trying to improve it.

At the center, a movement passed beneath the wall of the structure, from ground to crown. After a time it passed again.

The power felt the water coming in under the mountain’s shoulder. Far below the basin, one of its channels had narrowed. A slow accumulation of minerals had made a plug there, and the water behind it was pressing into older seams. Given enough time, it would find a way out beyond the rim. Several of the warm places in the hollow would cool.

This had happened before. Some creatures would die; others would travel; roots would turn toward what remained. The hollow might become a different hollow, still full of life.

The power did not know whether the being at its center wanted that.

It approached slowly. The wall yielded, and the power entered the dry chamber. It did not take a shape that needed room. The platform beneath it was warm, with a faint vibration passing through it at intervals.

The power touched the platform and sent a small pulse of warmth into it: not enough to resemble an animal’s body, not enough to call up water from below. Then it waited.

The platform cooled. A movement went down through the wall into the rock. For a long time nothing else happened. Then a second pulse returned, longer than the first.

The power answered with the same pulse.

They continued like that through the time it took a grazer to shed its skin against the outer wall. At first the returning pulses seemed irregular. The power let them be irregular. When it sent two close together, the being sent three, then one. When the power stopped, the being stopped. After a while it began again.

The exchange took longer than a conversation in words. It was not the same as words, and the power was careful not to give an answer to itself and call it understanding.

It showed the being the narrowing channel: the cool mineral plug, the pressure of water gathering behind it, the seams that led outward. The being received the pattern through the platform. It sent a movement down into the mountain, farther than before, and held it there.

When that movement returned, the water beneath the structure changed. A pool dweller at the wall lifted its edge, waited, and settled again.

The being showed the power what it could reach. It could alter the water that passed through it, and it could ease small obstructions in the channels immediately below. The plug was beyond those channels. The being had been sending warmth toward it for a very long time.

The power placed a possibility beside what the being had shown it: the plug loosened, a little at a time, so that the water could carry it away without scouring the basin. It also showed the channel left as it was.

The platform held both patterns for a long time. Once, the wall opened slightly and closed again. Outside, a hunter landed on the mat and waited, its sensitive feet taking the measure of the ground.

At last the being returned the pattern of the loosened plug.

The power asked again, as clearly as it knew how. The being gave the same answer, and added a pulse that traveled outward beneath the floor, toward a particular mouth in the rim.

The power attended to that mouth. Its note was low and unsteady. Beneath the mat leading from it lay a thick rope of root, and along the root, tucked into folds of living tissue, were eggs waiting for a rise in warmth.

The power made the channel open slowly.

For many days the mouth’s note strengthened by degrees. Water spread along the old groove, warming the buried root. The eggs began to hatch. Grazers arrived to feed on the new growth, and one of the hunters learned to wait above them. A pool dweller made the long journey from a cooling seep and settled with half its body in the fresh water.

The being adjusted what it released beneath its walls. The fronds near the new flow opened and lay flat. Their surfaces took up what they needed.

When the work was done, the power remained inside the chamber. It could feel other things it might alter: a grazer carrying an injured young one, a frond folded around a tear, a hunter whose last several descents had failed. It felt, too, the being’s attention moving through the basin, touching warmth and pressure, receiving the weight of animals against its wall.

The power sent a small pulse into the platform.

The being answered.

After a while the power asked, in the manner they had found together, whether it should stay.

The answer came back slowly. It included the chamber, the platform, the water passing below. It did not include the wall closing.

So the power stayed for a time. It learned the changing chord at the rim and the quieter movements beneath it. Sometimes the being called its attention to a channel or a resting animal. Sometimes the power noticed something and showed the being, and they considered it together. There were long intervals when neither sent a pulse.

One day, after rain had fallen for weeks beyond the hollow, a new mouth opened in the stone. Its first note entered the chord, thin and high. In the chamber, the being sent that note through the platform, and the power returned it. Outside, the hunters adjusted their crossings, and the water continued to rise.
