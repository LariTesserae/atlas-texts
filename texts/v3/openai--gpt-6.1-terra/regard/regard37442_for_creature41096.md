The power attends first to the water.

It follows the seepage beneath the basin and the line that carries water to the pump. Beyond the wall, the pumping station serves people who do not know about the slot in the concrete. The power does not stop the machine. Its pounding passes through the basin, and the films continue to grow through it.

Beneath one tilted plate, a passage has become almost closed. Water still enters, but less of it leaves, and the space held open by the water is shrinking. The power eases the plate upward by an amount too small for an eye to see. A little water passes beneath it. A few cells come loose and are carried into another crack.

The power leaves the other plates where they are. Some will seal over the life beneath them. New undersides will be exposed; deposits will gather there in turn. The basin is not held at one fortunate moment.

Near the slot, a screw has worked loose inside the old switch box. In time, water would reach the exposed connection. The power lets a droplet fall onto the box just as a maintenance worker comes around the building. She sees the wet streak, opens the cover, and finds the fault.

While she is there, she notices the warm basin behind the pipes. She tests the water, then calls someone who knows the station’s drainage. Together they mark the place on a plan, so that anyone clearing the pipes will know where the seepage goes. The worker replaces the cover and leaves the mineral crust undisturbed.

The pump continues. On the rail, another droplet falls, leaving a ring that overlaps the others. Beneath the loosened plate, water moves through a passage wider than it was that morning.
