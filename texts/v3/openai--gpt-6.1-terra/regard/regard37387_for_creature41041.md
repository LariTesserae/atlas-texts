The power came first as attention. Nothing in the chamber changed.

It followed the water backward through the cracks in the vault, and forward through the narrow passages below the basin. It found swimmers in the dark beyond the chamber, and other pale fringes on stone the creature of the ribs had never reached. It found the places where the water cooled and slowed, and where it rose again through beds of gravel, carrying the chamber’s fine dust into other lives.

It attended to the disk. The disk had once answered a pattern of pressures that no longer existed. Now its bubbles followed the brightest water and struck wherever that water led them. It could not choose another pattern, and it could not stop.

The doorway was older still. On its far side there was a place the water could not reach. The power looked through, then left the doorway as it had found it.

Above the chamber, a sheet of limestone was pulling away from the vault. Its fall would not destroy the basin. It would cover three of the inhabited columns and send a great pressure through the passages beneath them. The water would find new routes eventually, but several of the quiet hollows would be packed with silt first, and much of what lived there would be unable to escape.

The fall was still many seasons away. There was time to make room for the water without tearing open a passage all at once.

The power loosened a seam in the rock above the high ledge. It did this grain by grain, leaving the wiry plants and their insects undisturbed. A little water began to gather behind the seam. When enough had collected, it pressed through and ran down the wall in a thread.

The rust-red growth along its path lay flat. Below it, a fringe emerged from an old opening and tested the wet stone. It withdrew when the flow increased, then returned farther to one side.

For a long time the new thread carried very little. The power watched where it went. It widened the seam only when the passages below had taken the previous increase. In a hollow beyond the chamber, where the current began to scour too hard for the young fringes living there, it left a fallen stone in place to divide the flow.

At last the day came when the limestone sheet fell. It struck the basin with a sound that drove every exposed part of the creature into the rock. Spray reached the vault and rained back for a long while. One column was buried to its cup; another lost a piece from its side. Several swimmers were caught beneath the fall.

But the pressure went partly into the new seam. The hollows filled and cleared. By the next brightening, water was running through them again.

The creature did not emerge all at once. A fringe appeared under a shelf, closed, and disappeared. Later, strands came out at the bases of the unburied columns. One explored the fresh white face of the fall, found water moving through a narrow space beneath it, and stayed.

The power attended for a while longer. It could have uncovered the buried cup, but water had already begun to collect in a hollow on top of the fallen sheet. When the next surge came, a branch reached it. The branch swelled there, and in time its small rounded bodies washed into the basin.

The new seam continued to change. Some of the growth beneath it died; other patches spread into the damp stone nearby. The disk sent a bubble toward it, and a report sounded behind the wall. The flow shifted. Fringes closed and opened elsewhere.

The power did not correct this. It remained until the water had found several ways through, then let its attention travel farther along the passages, where other things were living in the dark.
