The power did not begin by quieting the sound. It listened from within a seam, where the ringing was sharpest, and found that the sound passed through the animals as it passed through the water and stone. Whether they could hear it, the power could not tell.

It made no disturbance there at first. It learned the shape of each crowded place: how the weight of a jointed animal rested on six smaller backs; how a white hair supported three bodies and a bead of moisture; how the pebble wall leaned against the soft side of a nodule. It attended to the creature with its mouth against the leaf, and to the one facing the wall. Neither gave a sign that could be understood.

Then, beyond the wall, the power made a little more room.

The pebbles did not move, and the space they enclosed remained the same. But the mineral on their outer side drew gently back, opening a seam wide enough for the six-legged animal to turn. The new surface shone like the old. A thin film of water gathered on it, and the ringing continued across it without a break.

For a while nothing happened.

Then the animal lifted one foot from the stone and set it down again. It stood for a long time before lifting another. At last it turned, slowly, and walked along the outside of the wall until it reached the new seam. There it stopped.

The power waited. The animal touched the damp mineral with its mouth.

After that, the power made room in other places, carefully, leaving the stones in contact where contact held water, and leaving the water where mouths rested against it. Beneath the arched back of a jointed creature, it eased a nodule upward by less than the width of a hair. A little water ran into the opened space and came to rest against a blunt crystal.

The creature lowered its back. The smaller animals standing on it shifted as it did. One lost its footing and slid down the smooth side, coming to rest in the gloss below. It remained there, then climbed onto a leaf.

The leaf bent.

Across the chamber, the motion took hold unevenly. A leg came down. A mouth closed. The soft creature wrapped around a bead drew its head away, leaving a faint dimple that slowly rounded out again. Along one wet rim, the six-legged animals parted as another came through. Some stayed where they were, while others moved into the spaces between them.

The power continued to open the seams, slowly enough for water to find new hollows and for the fronds to spread their thick leaves. It left the clay fragment where it lay, with the dark animals in its marks. They began to move too, though most remained within the curves.

The sound was still very loud. The power made a place farther into the mineral where it was softer, and joined that place to the chamber by a narrow passage. It put water there and a surface on which the fronds might grow. Some of the animals entered; others passed the opening without turning. The power left it open.

In time, the animal beside the pebble wall returned to it. It stood facing the stones once more, with room now on every side. After a while, it climbed over.

The enclosed space was scarcely larger than a seed. The animal fitted inside. It stood there on the damp stone while others moved along the seam beyond, and the pebbles held their places around it.
