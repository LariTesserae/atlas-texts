The power attends first to the ringing.

It follows the sound through the water and into the stone, where the highest note runs along the young folds. It feels the same note taken up by the creature’s fringe, and the fine seam beginning there. When the seam breaks, the note continues. The creature gathers itself and tries again.

The power could still the water. It could close the hollow’s mouth against the sea. But the creature feeds on what the current brings, and the small silver animal knows the crack by the entrance. The lines rising to the ceiling need the ringing. How much of the hollow’s life depends on the violence of the water is not yet clear.

So the power waits through many reversals.

It watches the stones. Two have begun to grow into the floor, but the third shifts a little with every surge. Once, when the water draws sharply outward, that stone lifts at its base. The slab above slips to one side and settles there. The creature remains in its cup, its filaments drawn tight.

The power reaches into the soft floor beneath the loose stone. It raises a fold just enough to meet the stone’s lower edge. On the next inward surge the stone presses into the fold, and the floor holds it. The power leaves the fold soft, so that it can continue to grow in its own time.

The shelter still trembles. It does not move from its place.

After a while the creature extends several filaments and touches the new fold. It holds them there through one reversal, then another. A thin ridge rises around their tips. The creature releases it and returns to the cup.

The power remains nearby while the light changes. Other dark threads withdraw into the hollow ridges when it attends to them closely, so it gives them room. It follows a broken seam as it tumbles out through the mouth and down the mountain’s steep flank; long after it has left the ringing behind, it thickens a little. The power keeps watch until the seam comes to rest among a scatter of pale fragments. It cannot tell whether anything inside is alive.

Back in the hollow, the creature climbs. It spreads itself along a line that has reached the ceiling, and particles gather among its filaments. The silver animal comes out and feeds beside it. When the current turns, the animal slips away and the creature folds down into its cup.

The power watches the fourth stone through the next surge. It has stayed where it slipped, leaving a wider opening on one side of the shelter. The creature comes up through that opening and turns its fringe toward the roof.

A new line begins. It reaches almost to the canopy before the water takes it. Then another rises, finer than the first, and holds.
