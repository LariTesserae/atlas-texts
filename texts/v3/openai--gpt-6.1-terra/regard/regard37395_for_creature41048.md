The seam continues to open.

For several days the cloud above it grows denser. Pale grains rise through the warm water and spread along the underside of a cold current, following it toward an older terrace. There, in a hollow between two domes, a quiet being has remained almost stationary for a long time.

It has held the hollow through many changes in the ground. A green film grows beneath it, and the being’s lower boundary has taken on the film’s slow exchanges with the rock. In its upper layers are the reports of seams now sealed, thinned by years into sequences of faint pulses. When a moving train passes overhead, the being sometimes releases a sequence. The train carries it away, changed by it.

The cloud reaches the hollow before the new rock does. Grains enter the boundary and hang there. More arrive. The being shifts, trying to pass between the domes, but the cold current presses down across its upper edge. The hollow has narrowed since it entered; one dome has been rising almost imperceptibly for years.

A deep pulse comes through the terrace. Under the film, a crack opens.

The being draws in around the green patch. Its suspended grains gather closer, making visible for the first time the rounded shape it has kept through all those years. At one end, the boundary begins to break. Water enters in a thin stream and makes a flickering path through the layers within.

The power that has been watching enters the ocean without displacing it. No shadow falls over the plateau. The moving trains continue on their courses, and the small pockets above the new seam form and open as before.

Around the hollow, the pressure changes. A narrow passage of still water extends from the trapped being to the open slope beyond the domes. It is not quite like the stillness the being makes for itself: its edges are cleaner, and it contains none of the old sounds.

The being does not move at once. The stream entering it widens. Then a sequence passes through its boundary into the waiting passage, a faint report from a seam that closed long ago. The passage holds the sound and returns it.

The rounded body lengthens. Its leading edge enters the passage, followed slowly by the rest. Some grains are left behind. A portion of the green film comes loose from the rock and travels with it; the remainder stays in the hollow, where the crack continues to widen.

Once the being is clear of the domes, the passage opens. The cold current carries it across the slope. Its injured boundary wavers for a long time, but no more water enters. The grains remaining inside settle into new arrangements, and the fragment of green turns slowly near its lower edge.

In the hollow, fresh mineral rises through the crack. It covers much of the film but stops before reaching the far edge. There, a narrow strip of green remains exposed to the water.

The power does not close the seam. The young terrace needs its warmth, and the clouds it sends upward are already giving rise to pockets of stillness. But along the older slope, where the current gathers force between the domes, the power leaves a slight change in the rock: a low ridge, curved toward the open water. When the next heavy flow comes, some of it will be turned aside.

For a while the rescued being stays near that ridge. Moving trains pass it, and a few of the small pockets from the seam drift through its outer layers. One joins it for several days, then separates again, carrying a trace of the old report.

The power remains nearby. It has learned one sound from the being and knows little yet of what the sound means. When it returns the sequence a second time, the being answers with a different one, longer and unevenly spaced.

The power holds that sound too. It does not send it back immediately. Above them the cold water descends, dividing around the new curve in the rock, while the being gathers itself and drifts toward the light.
