The power came without a voice at first, and listened.

The calls did not form a message. The ticking in the leaves was not a clock, nor were the answering throats asking to be delivered. The power listened long enough to hear a winged creature call from the same stem many times, and to hear another answer from somewhere it could not see.

It looked beneath the leaves, where the clear grazers moved in their rows. It watched one reach a clean track left by another and turn aside, finding more to eat. It watched a fish take one from the water. The fish remained a while beneath the leaf, nearly still, then went on its way.

The power could have made the plain brighter, or raised dry hills above it, or opened a river through the moss. It could have silenced the amphibians and filled their channels with creatures that would never take a life. But it did not know that any of these would be a kindness to those already there. It stayed and learned the paths beneath the roots, and which leaves sheltered eggs, and where the smallest animals went when a heavy foot came down.

One day—or what would have been a day elsewhere—a broad-winged creature fell between two stems. Its wing had caught in a strand of root. It pulled and settled, pulled and settled, each movement drawing the strand tighter.

The power made itself small enough to reach the place. It loosened the root without touching the wing, and held a leaf nearby. The creature climbed onto it on trembling legs. For a time it remained there, its damp wings folded close. Then it climbed higher, where the fronds overlapped, and disappeared among them.

The power did not follow. It left the leaf where it was.

Afterward it stayed beneath the canopy. A grazer crossed the back of its hand, feeding as it went; the power held still until it had passed. Nearby, an amphibian lifted its head and the grazer on a neighboring leaf vanished into its mouth. Droplets struck the water and spread in rings.

The power watched the rings reach the moss. It had room in its care for the fish, the amphibian, the grazers, and the winged creature calling now from somewhere in the green. When another voice answered, it listened for that one too.
