The power came without a shadow. The little shells stayed open, and the swimmers went on scraping.

For a long while it attended to the basin. It followed water from the higher shelves into the split pipe and out again, through the spray and the trough and the collapsed wall. It watched the dark hunters take swimmers and the swimmers graze upon the living films. It waited through many periods of light and dimness, until it could distinguish the changes that returned from those that did not.

The shaft had worn thin inside its bearing. Soon, in the slow measure of the basin, it would seize. The pipe would fall; its spray would cease. Water would still reach the trough pool, but the face of the frame would dry, and the shell animals clustered there would close until they could no longer open.

A many-legged creature was crossing that face when the power first noticed the wear. It had come down from a seam in the rock, following a thread of water over the iron. Beneath it were three young, so small that their feet could scarcely be seen. They paused where the thread broke into droplets. One of the young began to drift away, then caught a rough edge and drew itself back.

The power could have lifted them all to another shelf. It could have stopped the shaft and made a new channel with a touch. But there were animals in the seam above them, and others in the pool below, and the hunter beneath the collapsed wall had young of its own.

So the power worked slowly.

It gathered dissolved mineral from the water and laid it inside the worn bearing, a little at a time, as the water itself might have done. It eased the shaft back into its seat. At the largest split in the pipe, where the iron had begun to tear, it made a narrow seam of stone. The seam held the edges together while leaving room for water to escape.

The shaft knocked more softly after that. Droplets continued to jump from the pipe. One break sent its spray slightly farther down the frame than before, and over the following days a few shell animals settled there. The old cluster remained wet.

The many-legged creature and its young crossed the broken thread when the next droplet reached them. They went down behind a crust of mineral, where the power could no longer see them without moving the crust. It left the crust where it was.

At dusk a swimmer brushed an animal in a crevice. Light appeared, and another point answered it along the shelf. In the brief gleam a dark hunter folded over a swimmer, and several others scattered into the trough pool.

The power stayed. It watched the repaired bearing as water passed through it, and the spray as it fell. When a film began to grow across one of the pipe’s openings, it waited to see whether the opening would close. It did not. Water found a way through the film, and the animals on the iron remained wet.
