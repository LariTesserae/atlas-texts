The power came without crossing the plateau. It attended first to the stone, then to the slow commerce of leaf and root. It listened long enough to distinguish a charge moving through one blade from a charge moving through another, and longer still to notice how a grove altered its work when a crystal fell.

It found no wound in the place. The sharp peaks needed no softening; the heaps beneath the trunks were not stores laid by against a failure. What the groves did was intricate, and it belonged to them.

At the edge of one stand, the power answered a faint charge in a leaf with a fainter one of its own.

The leaf continued its work. After a time, the power answered another charge. This time the pattern passing through the crown changed. The power fell silent, and the former pattern returned.

It waited.

A crystal formed at a leaf tip and dropped onto a frond. Another followed. Then a charge passed through the crown unlike those that had passed before. The power answered it. The grove sent the charge again, with a small alteration, and the power answered that as well.
