The power does not arrive as a shadow. The workers have enough shadows to attend to, and one laid suddenly across their feet would be an interruption.

It comes first as a pause in the light.

The silver front is crossing the basin when a narrow band of leaves remains unturned. The front passes it, returns through itself, and the leaves hold their position still. In that band, the distances do not change. The unfinished wall stays beneath the worker’s hand.

The worker waits until the rest of the plain has settled. Then it taps the wall twice.

A response comes from the leaf plates nearby: three soft clicks, spaced far enough apart that they cannot be mistaken for the sound of a press.

The worker rises with some difficulty. It taps again.

The plates answer. For a long time the exchange is halting. The power can feel the powder moving within the worker, can distinguish every change it has undergone, but this is not the same as knowing what the worker means by a particular sequence of clicks. The worker moves closer so that it can hear the replies. Once, it corrects a rhythm by tapping it out on the power’s unturned leaves.

At last the power asks whether it may help with the slow circulation in the worker’s lower chambers.

The worker rests one hand on the unfinished wall. It asks whether the powder would be changed.

Some of it might need to be cleared, the power answers. The passages could be widened. The worker’s powder would remain its own.

The worker is quiet. Then it asks the power to show it how.

They make their way toward the sorting column where the worker sleeps. The journey takes most of the remaining bright hours. The power keeps the distances steady when the next front passes, but otherwise leaves the plain as it is. At a seam where a grazer’s returning shadow lies across the path, the worker stops and waits. After a while the grazer comes into view, tests the next plate and moves on.

At the column, two other workers listen to the proposed treatment. They bring a shallow tray and instruments for opening fine channels. Together they clear the chambers where powder has settled, saving what they remove. The power guides their hands when the passages become too narrow to see. It opens several connections that have closed with age and leaves others alone.

Afterward the worker stands, walks to the doorway, and returns. Its steps are easier, though not as quick as those of the younger workers. It touches the tray where its old powder has been gathered.

They place the tray near the warm outer foliage. The worker will decide later where the powder should go.

In the following bright hours, the power visits the unfinished district with the workers. They have questions for it about the shifting rooms. One room has moved beyond its frame three times; another has developed a shadow of a doorway so narrow that none of them could pass through it.

The power studies the arches and the light above them. It could give each shadow a fixed position, but the arches are still growing, and the workers are not certain what rooms they will want when the roofs are ready. Instead, at their request, it alters a few of the frames. A worker can now set a frame to hold a shadow steady while a foundation forms, then release it when the room is ready to change again.

They try the first altered frame around the narrow doorway. When they loosen it, the shadow widens a little. The workers add powder along the new edge and leave the old ridge where it has already taken root. It makes a low ledge beside the entrance.

The shell-bearing animals begin using the warmth beneath the frame. One carries powder into the ledge, and leaves with dark, curled tips grow there. The workers trim them back from the doorway and let the rest remain.

Before the power goes, it asks whether there is anything else.

The older worker leads it to the first low wall. It can reach the wall easily now. On the far side, the shadow of another room has appeared, though nothing has yet been built to cast it.

The worker asks the power to wait while it fetches a frame.

They set the frame together. The power holds it level until the worker has pressed its feet into the floor, then lets go. The worker pours in a handful of powder. Both remain beside it for a while, watching the dark outline gather its first pale edge.

When the next silver front crosses the basin, the room moves slightly within its frame. The worker adjusts one corner, and the power, seeing that its help is no longer needed, leaves the light to move across the leaves as before.
