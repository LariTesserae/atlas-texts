The power first noticed the hollow because of the pumping station’s complaint. The sound traveled farther than the water did, through the rock and into the ground above it. There were many such sounds in the world, made by engines that had outlasted their makers. This one varied. It complained, and then, from another part of the hollow, something answered.

The power listened through several turns of the seasons. It learned the difference between a railing’s loose tremor and the tension passed deliberately along a wet edge. It learned, too, how much it did not understand. A stack that seemed still might be sustaining an argument too slow for it to hear as an argument. A sheet that appeared to have withdrawn might be listening through the floor.

When the power came closer, it did not stop the engines. Their noise was troublesome, but it carried news.

It chose a drop above the reservoir. Holding the drop a little longer than the draft could account for, it let the fine films beneath the lid touch it, then let it fall.

The disturbance was noticed. For a time, the films reached and withdrew, testing the draft and one another. The power repeated the gesture only when another drop had gathered. After many attempts it managed a pattern simple enough to be distinguished from weather, and waited for an answer.

The answer came from several places at once. Some films gave back the pattern. Others broke it. One held so still that the drop fell without touching it.

The power understood this much: it had entered a meeting place without being asked. It withdrew from the gap and waited elsewhere.

News of the strange drops passed through the hollow, altered at each exchange. It reached the stacks under the galleries, the sheets fitted around levers, and the solitary colony near the pumping station. Some thought the draft had changed. Some thought another colony had found a new way to deceive them. Young colonies sent questions so quickly that their elders could not finish answering.

The power returned at long intervals. It answered what it could and admitted, through mistakes and corrections, what it could not. It learned to ask before holding a drop. Several colonies refused it; several more wanted to know whether it could feel the east gallery’s railing, which had begun to hum differently. The solitary sheet sent no answer, though it remained in its place beside the engine.

At last, through the drops, a request took shape. It concerned a door in the upper workshop, swollen tight in its frame. There were sheets on the other side, still living at the last exchange, but the lever fitted to the latch had slipped. The colonies had been trying to open it for longer than the power had been listening.

The power could have removed the wall. Instead, it went to the door and felt for the pressure the sheets had been attempting. It lifted the latch, eased the wood past the stone threshold, and left the door ajar.

A damp edge emerged. Then another. They pressed against the waiting sheets outside, and the power could not follow all that passed between them.

Later, it helped mend the latch. It did this under direction, moving a pin when asked, holding it still while the sheets tested the lever. They rejected its first repair because the catch was too smooth: the mechanism no longer held the door open at the width they preferred. The second was accepted.

There were other requests. A drain needed clearing without disturbing the roots that had grown around its grille. A stack wanted a way to send news across a channel without joining the stack on the opposite bank. The power laid a narrow strip of fiber between two damp stones; both stacks tested it, and one asked for it to be removed. It was removed.

The engine’s bearing troubled several of the oldest colonies. They had felt its wear for years. When the power offered to make the engine silent, their answer took a long time to assemble, and it was no. They wanted the bearing sound again, not the thud abolished. So the power lifted the flywheel just enough for them to feed oil where the bearing had worn. The complaint changed, becoming lower and more even. Across the hollow, sheets held themselves still to hear it.

The power could also have raised the dead colonies from their broken films, perhaps, or made something that answered exactly as they once had. It did not offer this. It had heard the living disagree over a repeated pattern in a new sheet, and had felt how carefully they made room for that disagreement.

At the reservoir, the power kept no place for itself. It came when called and, when the meeting was finished, released the drop it was holding. The draft caught it for a few seconds before it fell.

The clock remained without numbers. The blank sheets remained among the inhabited ones. The solitary colony continued to receive news through the floor, including news of the repaired bearing, which it acknowledged with a single slow change of tension.

Above the roofs, the leaves accumulated moisture. The power could have lightened them, but they had carried such weight before, and no one had asked. One by one, their drops fell onto the metal and passed into the familiar noise of the hollow.
