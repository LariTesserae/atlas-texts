The power first became aware of the bowl through its noise.

From a great distance it sounded like a single note. Coming nearer, the power heard the millions of voices within it, and the changes that passed among them: the turning of a crown, the tightening of a swarm, the little feet finding hold. It entered the bowl without quieting the wind.

For a long time it watched a single grazer at the edge of the platform. The creature settled, took a grain between its dark mouthparts, and rose again. The grain was too large. It carried the thing into the air, turning clumsily, until the wind pulled it free. The grazer followed it down and caught it once more. On the third attempt the grain broke.

The power could have made all the grains small enough. It watched instead.

The grazer had come from a fissure beside the oldest root. There were eggs in that fissure now, and several young climbing toward the open air. One slipped as the stone shifted beneath it. It fell past the others, caught a fine thread growing from the root, and hung there with its sails closed.

The power attended to the thread. It was alive, and would eventually draw back into the root. Before then, the young creature found its footing and continued upward.

The power stayed to see it reach the wind.

As days passed, it came to know the blue line in the arch: how it gathered the crowns, how the gathered crowns pressed the animals together, and how the animals used those moments. It looked into the shifting joints and found a creature sheltering there, a many-limbed thing whose body had grown around a moving strut. Each time the strut turned, the creature moved with it. It had made its home there so long ago that neither could now move freely without the other.

A crack had opened in the arch above them. The exposed joints were adjusting to it, but slowly, and the next movement would close the creature’s chamber before it could withdraw.

The power held the damaged edges apart for the length of that movement. The strut passed. The creature drew its limbs into another opening, and the joints continued their rearrangement around the crack.

When the power released the stone, the arch shivered. A blue line ran through it. The nearest crowns leaned together, and among the animals caught between them, some fed and some found mates. Then the fronds sprang open.

The power remained until the joints had settled into their new pattern. After that, it went away, leaving the wind to lift the glitter from the crowns and carry it over the rim.
