The power first noticed the chamber because a sound occurred twice where it should have occurred once. It listened long enough to hear the interval change, and longer still before it recognized that some of the later sounds were being kept.

It did not enter through the seam. The occupant had reason to be cautious there, and the seam was already uncertain about distance. Instead, the power attended to the mountain around the chamber: to the worn stone, the weakening line of light, the frame with its three struts and its narrowing depth.

The frame could be set upright. The light could be made bright. The hollow could be filled, the floor made level, the chamber enlarged until the mountain was a thin shell around it.

Each of these would destroy something the occupant knew. Some might destroy the occupant as well.

So the power began with a grain.

It chose one at the edge of the hollow, where a small change would not interrupt the account. It held the grain for a while on the edge of one frosted face, then let it settle. The click came, and later came again.

The occupant attended to the repeated sound. It did not move toward the grain. After a time, it caused another grain to settle nearby.

The power answered, though not at once. It had learned that the interval mattered. It made the second click different from the first and waited to see whether the difference survived its return.

It did. The occupant placed both sounds along the hollow’s edge. Then it made a third.

They continued this way. The power found it could make sounds the occupant could keep, and others that faded almost immediately. It learned to use the first kind sparingly. It also learned that a silence after a sound was not necessarily an absence of reply. The occupant sometimes returned to an old sequence before answering, or let its curve drift a considerable distance uphill.

Eventually the power made a small patch of the blue line brighter.

The change was slight. It revealed a few grains that had been difficult to distinguish, while leaving the familiar bright patch and its shadows as they were. The power held it for a time, then let it fade.

The occupant waited beside the place where the light had changed. When the dimness returned, it made two clicks the power had used often during their exchanges. The power repeated the brightening.

This happened several times. Once, while the patch was bright, the occupant moved away and spent a long time beside the disk. The power let the light fade. The occupant did not ask for it again until much later.

The power left a way for it to do so: a grain balanced where the hollow met the dark floor. When the occupant persuaded it onto one face, the patch brightened. When it persuaded the grain onto another, the patch dimmed. The grain did not always settle as intended, but it could be tried again.

The frame was harder.

The power could see what would happen if its leaning continued. It could also see several ways of preventing it. It chose none immediately. The silver depth inside the frame had become part of how the occupant measured its surroundings; even the narrowing corner had a place in the account.

First the power made a support outside the chamber, in a region where the mountain and the space it enclosed could both be reached. The support did not touch the frame. For a long while it carried almost nothing. Then, very gradually, it began to take the strain that had been drawing the frame down.

The frame sank a little farther. Then it stopped.

The occupant noticed. It returned to the silver depth again and again, comparing the corners. It brought the power one of the old sounds from its account, then another, then the sequence of four.

The power listened. It could not recover the sounds as they had first been. It could have made four new ones, clean and exact, but these would not have answered the question the occupant had kept through so much time. Instead, it helped the existing sequence last a little longer. The oldest sound remained faint; the uncertain interval before the fourth remained uncertain.

The hollow still deepened, though more slowly. Marks still drifted uphill. A stone moved from the bright patch still left its shadow behind for a while. The occupant found that it could continue making its account.

At the seam, the power made one further provision. It did not widen the entrance or make the distance agree. It placed a point of warmth in the stone just beyond it, steady and small. The warmth did not reach into the chamber, but the occupant could find it by extending an arrangement toward the seam.

The first time it did so, part of the arrangement failed to return. The power waited. After a long interval the occupant made another, shorter extension, and found the warmth again. It kept a sound from that attempt beside the old sound of the visiting tip.

The power remained near enough to be reached. Sometimes it answered a click. Sometimes the occupant listened elsewhere and made no sound for it at all.

The light could now be made brighter when an account needed reading. The frame held at its slight lean. There was still enough time after each sound to listen for its return, and, when the occupant chose, another listener.
