One season the brighter weather lasted longer than usual. The clouds passed beneath the plateau without rising to its rim, and the condensation screens hung dry through the nights. In the hollows, the upper folds of moss kept their color, but underneath them the stored water thinned. The trees closed their leaves.

A bed near the western causeway was connected by pale runners to three other beds, one of them inside a house. The house had a screen above its cistern, but the reeds had fallen years before, and the cistern held only a little water in its deepest seam. A runner had entered that seam and thickened there. It could go no farther.

The power found the bed in all four places. It knew how long the runners had taken to cross the stone, and how much of the bed had died back during earlier dry seasons. Some of it would live through this one. Some would not.

The power could have brought rain. Instead it raised the fallen screen.

The old reeds were brittle. It drew the roots of a nearby tree apart just enough to free a length of soft metal caught between them, and used this to bind the screen to its frame. The clear growth in the weave had dried to a nearly weightless film. Most of it remained attached.

That night there was still no rain. A little moisture gathered on the screen, less than a cupful, and ran into the cistern. The next night there was more. The runner in the seam took it up. Over the following days, water moved slowly through the bed, and the folds inside the house began to lift from the floor.

The power remained until the weather changed. It watched a pale tip emerge from beneath the lintel and enter a joint in the courtyard paving. By morning the courtyard had become a stair, and the tip lay exposed on the upper step. It dried there. Another grew from the same runner, found a damp edge beneath the door, and continued downward.

When the clouds returned, rain fell thinly for several days. The repaired screen grew heavy with clear cords. Water crossed the cistern’s apron and entered two old channels, one leading toward the western bed and the other to a stretch of bare ground. Before the season ended, a green film appeared along the second channel.

The power left the screen standing. It did not mend the door, which had fallen flat across the threshold; the runner was using it.
