The power’s attention entered the basin with the rain.

It did not descend through the fern crowns or gather into a shape on the stone. It attended first to the water, following it from the broken wall into the seams, and from there into the dark passages beneath the grove.

In one of the quieter channels, a seamling was feeding. Its lobes opened into the current. Fine particles settled against them, and a small creature struck one lobe and was held. The seamling drew both slowly to its mouth.

Farther up, near the wall, eggs hung from their clear stalks. Water moved around them, bringing a little warmth from the surface. Several had hatched recently. The young grazed close to the stone, where the current was weakest.

The power could feel a change coming there. A flake had slipped between two roots and lodged in the mouth of the channel. Each rain brought grit against its upper edge. Before long the opening would narrow, and the next heavy fall of water might drive the flake down across it.

There were other passages beneath the wall. Some of the young might reach them if the water slowed. Others would not.

The power waited through an afternoon and a night, while a snail crossed the rock above and a cup filled and spilled. By morning more grit had gathered behind the flake.

Then, in the pause between two drops from an overhanging root, the flake shifted. It rose just enough to turn on its edge and settle into a wider crack beside the channel. Water passed over the scattered grit and carried it down into the larger seams.

The eggs remained on their stalks. One opened three nights later. The newly hatched seamling held to the stone as the water moved past it, and after a time began to graze.

Above, the grove went on filling and spilling. A lizard took a seamling that came out too early one morning; rain washed its tracks from the shelf. Roots continued to grow through the cracks, and the stone continued to change around them.

The power stayed long enough to feel the cleared water reach the deep channels. Then its attention moved on.
