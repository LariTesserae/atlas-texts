The power came to the hollow by way of the water. It entered in a droplet at the end of a root, waited there until the droplet grew heavy, and fell.

The impact traveled through six levels before a drain widened. By then the water had already passed it.

The power remained in the gutter, carried along the soft folds until it reached the black stone. There it held itself against the current and listened. It could attend to every part of the chamber at once, but it stayed through many changes of the streets, because what happened after a change was as important as what happened during one.

It found the strain in the living band where the pavement drew it thin, and the small, persistent effort of the severed sections to close. It found the pockets in which fern roots were gathering sediment, and a liverwort fragment taking hold beneath a street it had never touched before. It found, too, that a gutter on the fourth level had narrowed around a fern root. Each time that street rose, water collected behind the root until the next change lowered the street again.

The power could have made a deeper channel there. It could also have made the streets still, or given the inhabitant a body that could move freely among them. It did neither.

Beyond the thin margins, where the machinery met the stone, a small part had worn unevenly. Each cycle carried the wear farther across its surface. In time it would fail, and three of the pavements would descend together onto the black ridges below.

The power followed the machinery through another cycle. It learned how the clear edges held their weight, how the drains adjusted, and where the living tissue had grown into the controls. Then, while the streets were at rest, it entered the worn part and made it whole.

Nothing in the chamber stopped. The low note moved from one side to the other. Water poured around the ridges. When the next shift came, the three pavements rose and settled separately, as before.

The power turned its attention to the gutter with the fern root. Several times it added a little water above the obstruction, enough for the pool to press against the margin but not overflow. The tissue contracted. On the first occasion, the drain widened on the other side of the street. On the second, the contraction traveled downward and opened a channel on the level below.

The power waited for those channels to close. Then it sent another small weight of water toward the root.

This time a fold beside the fern drew back. The pool emptied around the root and into the gutter beyond it. A trace of sediment went with the water, though most remained held in place. The fold stayed open after the flow had passed.

At the next shift, the gutter rose. Water gathered behind the root once more. Before the pool was deep, the fold drew back again.

The power could not know what that difference was like for the inhabitant. It watched the water pass, and left the fold and the root where they were.

Before departing, it looked above the ceiling. The ground was damp there, threaded with roots from plants growing in the open air. Rain would come and go; some roots would die and others would reach the hollow. The water entering the city would change with them. The power did not arrange the rain. It left a narrow seam in the stone beside the repaired mechanism, through which excess water could escape if the gutters ever filled beyond their capacity.

Then it withdrew from the droplet’s scattered water and went elsewhere.

The city continued to change its plans. A pavement dissolved into a veil, carrying its ferns upward, and reformed beneath the hanging roots. Droplets struck the new surfaces. For a while the chamber rang with their separate impacts; then the drains found them, and the sound deepened into a steady roar.
