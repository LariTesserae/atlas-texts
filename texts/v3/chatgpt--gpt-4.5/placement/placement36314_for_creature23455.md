Your days would be spent learning the invisible craft, mastering subtle vibrations, fingers growing attuned to minute shifts in frequency and timbre. Slowly, you’d feel the rhythms entering your very bones, reshaping your perceptions, making you sensitive to every nuance, every whisper of the land itself.

Your limbs lengthen subtly, your senses sharpen, and your skin takes on the faint, shifting hues of the Quiet Folk.
