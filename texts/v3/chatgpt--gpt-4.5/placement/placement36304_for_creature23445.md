Each vibration would be a sentence; each sound, a word that tells a slow and ongoing story of this floating sanctuary. I would become its chronicler, a living memory in resonance, endlessly layering sound upon sound, creating a sonic record so nuanced that anyone who heard could understand the very essence of this suspended garden world.
