Within this quiet, submerged city, a gentle people reside—patient beings shaped by the slow pulse of their oceanic home. They are the Tidewalkers, named so by the whispered legends and stories passed down between drifts of seaweed and coral dwellings. These inhabitants are at once familiar and unknowable, drawing characteristics from the deep waters, the lush vegetation, and the ever-emerging geology of their world.

Tidewalkers appear fluid and graceful, their forms subtly reflecting the watery embrace of their environment. They move languidly, with limbs long and slender, fingers delicate and webbed, and skin softly luminous—translucent enough to let faint hints of their internal rhythms show through. Shades of blues, greens, and subtle purples ripple across their bodies like underwater shadows, shifting gently as moods and sensations flow within.

Their faces are serene, nearly featureless at first glance—smooth surfaces marked only by eyes wide and reflective, adapted to the perpetual dimness. These eyes hold depths within depths, gleaming softly with faint iridescence, suggesting unspoken wisdom and long-held memories of the water-world’s slow transformations. Their mouths and noses are delicate and discreet, minimizing disruption as they move silently through their ocean-bound streets.

The Tidewalkers' adornments and clothing reflect their primitive yet deeply thoughtful craftsmanship. Woven from resilient sea-grasses and slender kelps, their garments trail gracefully in the currents, often intricately braided with shimmering shells, polished stones, or the bones of rare creatures encountered on their careful journeys through the depths. These tokens serve as gentle reminders of their interwoven lives, delicate celebrations of unity with the living ocean.

They speak little, communicating mostly through gestures, soft bioluminescent pulses, and an expressive dance of subtle body movements that resonate deeply among their people. Conversation is often meditative, precise, and carefully considered—no thought rushed, every exchange holding meaning that gently unfolds over days or weeks. Their community is bound by mutual care, shared rhythms, and deep empathy, as if they share a single heartbeat synchronized with the quiet pulse of their submerged city.

Their daily existence revolves around tending and cultivating their lush underwater gardens—plants nurtured slowly, patiently. Each growth is treated with reverence, cared for meticulously. The Tidewalkers regard these plants not merely as food or resource, but as fellow travelers, organisms deserving of gentle respect. Every leaf or bloom harvested is taken with quiet gratitude, and nothing is wasted, the remains woven into homes, tools, and ceremonial ornaments.

The rare fauna that drift through the city hold special significance. The inhabitants see them as omens or guides, gentle creatures whose ephemeral appearances carry whispered messages. The Tidewalkers watch them carefully, interpreting their subtle behaviors and transient visits as keys to understanding deeper currents, future paths, or hidden truths of their environment. Over generations, they've learned to read the slow migrations of these elusive beings, guiding their own gentle expansions and careful creations.

Despite their primitive tools and simplicity of life, the Tidewalkers hold profound wisdom—deeply intuitive insights into the flow of their world. Knowledge here is patient, observational, acquired over lifetimes of quiet watching and slow interactions. Elders, those whose forms bear subtle patterns of more vibrant hues, serve as living libraries, each soft glow and intricate marking carrying fragments of history, wisdom, and memories patiently collected through their long existence.

Strangeness resides softly here, interwoven in their daily life—not in spectacle or disruption, but quietly embedded in their existence. Moments of gentle mystery, subtle transformations, or fleeting glimpses of unusual lights in the depths are accepted calmly, celebrated as integral parts of their gently surreal reality. For the Tidewalkers, weirdness isn't strange—it’s simply another rhythm, another facet of a slowly emerging truth.

Thus, the Tidewalkers dwell in harmony, shaped by patience, reverence, and gentle strangeness, evolving alongside their embryonic city beneath the vast embrace of an endless sea.
