In this submerged vastness, the world exists in an eternal, roaring twilight, suspended in perpetual cold. The scale defies perception: a near-boundless watery abyss beneath skies rendered indistinct by drifting veils of vapor, gently lit by diffuse, greyish daylight filtering from above. No distinct sun pierces the heavy mantle of cloud and mist, yet the illumination remains constant, flat, and unchanging, lending a timeless, unsettling stability to the landscape.

This realm is young, a recent upheaval from whatever cataclysm birthed its deep, icy seas. The planet-scale ocean covers sharp, jagged ridges of submerged mountains, newly formed and scarcely smoothed by the currents. Spires of volcanic glass and bare mineral peaks jut sharply from the seafloor, glinting faintly, untouched by organic life. The slopes, edges, and underwater cliffs are harshly angular, as erosion has barely had time to wear them down.

Despite the near absence of plant life—no algae drape these stones, no coral colonizes the depths—the waters teem with abundant, yet utterly alien, fauna. Transparent creatures drift quietly in the currents, intricate gelatinous beings glowing faintly from within. Swarms of fragile, glassy organisms rise and fall, their movements synchronized, weaving intricate dances in the darkness. Their biology is unrecognizable, their physiology defying simple classification; some pulse with faint electric energy, others propel themselves silently with fluid jets, all suspended effortlessly in the chilled abyss.

Scattered throughout this expanse are signs of an industrial past or perhaps a present whose logic evades easy understanding. At wide intervals, enigmatic structures rise from the seabed, slender towers of metallic struts, silently vibrating with internal machinery. Occasionally, the dim glow of electric arcs sparks briefly within their skeletal frameworks, hinting at ongoing operations or automated maintenance. They resemble extraction platforms or monitoring outposts, but their exact purpose remains ambiguous, their creators long absent or concealed.

Sound dominates this vast landscape, an overwhelming, ceaseless roar. This deafening ambience is driven by powerful, hidden engines beneath the ocean floor, vast submerged turbines cycling endlessly, unseen generators whose hum vibrates through the waters, creating powerful infrasonic waves. These immense vibrations resonate through every surface, reverberating between the submerged peaks and valleys, enveloping all life in a permanent, bone-deep drone. The denizens seem indifferent or adapted, silently moving through the perpetual thunder.

Despite this roaring, nothing here truly changes. A frozen equilibrium dominates. Currents flow, but never alter course. The enormous machines rumble on tirelessly, unaffected by time. This stark constancy feels eternal, suggesting a place engineered deliberately into unchanging permanence—a colossal mechanism preserved in unending motion.

This submerged expanse remains inscrutable, boundless yet empty, industrial yet lifeless, bustling with unseen forces yet frozen in immobility—a paradox suspended in endless waters beneath indifferent skies.
