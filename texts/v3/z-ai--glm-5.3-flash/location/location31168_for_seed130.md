# The Weeping Works

Sound arrives before sight, and never entirely leaves. It is a roar with grain in it — a thousand small pressures braided into one long pressure — and it lives in the floor as much as in the air. Stand still anywhere down here and the vibration climbs your shins, settles in your teeth, and turns the water on every surface into fine trembling ridges. Silence is extinct here. The quietest place is only a place where the roar has a different shape.
