It hears the mountain first, the way you did, the way everyone must. Its days are not our days; call the time it spends listening three days, or three ages, or three of whatever unit attention is kept in where it comes from. It has drawn near to many places, and it has one habit it has never broken: it arrives the way each place already is. In loud places it arrives as thunder. In places that count, it arrives as arithmetic. Here — where every hello is a hush, where silence is a substance that pools in the lee of things — it arrives as attention. And on this summit, attention and weather were never distinguishable to begin with.

So its arrival goes unnoticed, which is its first courtesy and its first decision. In air that keeps whatever it is given, an announcement is permanent. It knows this. It chooses the hush.

Then it does what any careful visitor does: it takes the census properly. Not by bodies. It walks the plateau in units of pause, cairn to cairn, and counts what can hold still, and for how long, and what each thing holds still *for*. The stone, finished, holding one note longer than anything in the world has held anything. The storm, a player that will not end, migrating uphill generation by generation, its dead organs legible in the sun. The green, pooled in every shadow the loud thing casts. The glass-skinned amphibian, its whole biography ten meters long, holding the fundamental through its belly. The midges, a species whose address is on the calendar. The bird on its one circuit. The scatter of upright stones, each an intention, and intentions weathering more slowly than lichen.

And it finds the open questions. Who cut the stair. Who set the fresh stone below the south stair. Whether the far drip-line has a tender of its own. Whether the stone in the deep hollow is still falling. It considers each one the way a keeper considers a well — and it does not draw water from any of them. This is the reasoning, and it is the center of everything the power does here: these questions are not gaps in the census. They are what the quiet is *for*. A lesser power would close them, one by one, out of kindness, and the kindness would be a drought. Green is not a place here; it is an acoustic condition. Neither is mystery a lack; it is a condition. Close a question on this summit and you still the very thing that pools.

So the power makes a study of not-doing, and the not-doings are worth listing, because each one is a mercy with reasons attached.

It does not still the storm. It considered this longest, because the storm kills — a garden dies in a season, roots and all, when the wind reorganizes and the lee moves. But it saw the arithmetic the keepers live inside without ever writing down: the gardens are the storm's shadow. Still the lamp and the shadow goes. There is no version of this summit where the roar is gone and the green stays, and the power does not deal in versions. Instead, when a garden dies, it arrives in time to be present. It sits the death out in the open, under the plainest light on the summit, attending, the way you would sit with a thing that cannot be held. And afterward — this is unmeasured, and it is so — when the storm moves on and the green returns to the abandoned lee, it returns a season sooner than the numbers would predict. Not a miracle. Attendance. On this summit, attendance is a hydrology. Something pools where it pools.

It does not mend the note. This is its strangest gift, so it must be told slowly. The mountain has kept the pitch and mislaid the blow; the fundamental rings in the hollows under the plateau with no audible beginning, and the mountain, which keeps every address the weather has ever had, has never once volunteered its own. The power is, among other things, a keeper of what is lost. It goes down — or in, or back; its directions are its own — and it finds the blow. The young tall range. The impact no ear attended. The first instant of the ring. And it holds that memory, entire, and says nothing. It does not sow it into any hollow — the deep hollow must never be spent, and no hollow is large enough anyway. It simply carries it, the way the stone tool left point-up carries *I will return*: as a held thing. Which means the mountain, without ever learning it, is now remembered — its own first moment kept, in secret, by someone who will not tell. Everything on this summit lives on things being held. Now one more thing is held, and nothing is changed, and that is the whole point.

It does not answer the bird. The bird cries the flutes' note an octave up; below, the fundamental; between them, the whole storm accompanying. It is a duet, and both claims about who taught whom come out the same size, and the power knows about duets. It knows the one thing a duet cannot survive is a third part. It listens from the edge of the amphitheater, once, all the way through, and does not applaud. Applause would spend the silence. It adds its listening to the silence instead, which here is the same as adding nothing, which here is the same as adding everything.

It does not speed the water up. One drop at a time is the only speed water travels here, and the basins have accepted that, and the power does not overrule what has been accepted.

What it *does* is one thing, and it is small, and it is done at the local speed.

It climbs the stair. The stair does not ask names — it never has — and the treads are dished like worn spoons, which is the shape of an absence held longer than names last, and the power's feet fit the dishes the way everyone's feet do, because the dishes were made by arriving, and it is arriving. At the cairn below the south stair it sets a fresh stone, point-up. No lichen. *I will return.*

And now there are two unlichened stones on that cairn, and no one in the valley admits to either climb, and this is correct. The power has been many things in many places — author, judge, rescuer, weather — and it has learned the hard way what titles cost. Here it wished to be counted the way the mountain counts: as one more thing that can hold still, and for how long, and for what. So it registered itself the only way this summit keeps a register — one intention among the scatter, indistinguishable, unproven, somewhere above the cloud line. If you widen the census now, the honest total is one more than it was. No one can verify this. That is how everything true is counted here.

And some nights — deep in the held breath, when the flutes fall out one by one and the torn cap drifts open over ordinary stars — there is a presence standing out in the open beside the drip-lines, its own and the other one, the two of them ticking at each other across the dark. The quietest music it has ever attended is the amphibian's heartbeat, audible now, and it attends. It listens to two drip-lines and one heart and the memory of a blow it will not tell, and it raises what serves it for a palm against the dark.

The palm means hello. The palm means quiet. And in air that keeps whatever it is given, it has always meant a third thing as well: *I am still here.* The power holds the gesture for the length of the pause — twenty minutes, sometimes an hour — which is, by the keepers' only definition, to hold a note longer than you meant to. Whether it meant to is one of the questions the quiet is for.

The keepers would say there was one meaning, and that the confusion belongs to places where mountain, storm, and song arrive separately. They would be right. By the time the roar comes back and the cap seals and the flutes take up their arrays again, the mountain has kept one more address the way it keeps them all — without ever volunteering it — and the storm has played through a stranger it did not notice, and nothing has changed except the one thing nothing here can measure: the total of what is held.

Which went up by one. Which was the whole of what the power came to do.
