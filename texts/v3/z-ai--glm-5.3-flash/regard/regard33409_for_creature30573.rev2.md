Begin, this time, with the attention — because the attention is the one thing the valley has never had.

Everything else it has had since before having was a possibility: the water, the pavement, the fog and its breathing, the seeps and their grey syrup, the brine with its shining law, the storm at the top converting itself forever into a fingernail of quiet. All of it complete. All of it unwitnessed. An age can be vast without an audience — this one was — and then, with no event, no front edge, no sign that anything had changed except that everything was now being held in mind, the valley had a reader.

It came the way everything comes to this world: through the storm, and down. Even the limitless must consent to the grammar of the medium if it would arrive anywhere in particular, and so the power entered at the top, in whatever its equivalent of fury is, and was filtered through kilometers of stratified cold into the only form the valley can hold. Not light — light is spent here, and it did not come to spend. Not pressure — pressure is weather, and it did not come to blow. Not sound — there is no room in this world to be loud in, and it did not come to take any. It arrived as regard: attention that does not fall on a thing the way light falls, but holds it the way the water holds — from every side at once, without weight, moving no grain that would not have moved anyway. By the time it could have been felt, it had always been there. It attended the whole of her life, condensing so slowly that arrival and always-having-arrived turned out to be the same place. Omnipotence, coming here, is indistinguishable from patience. It measured the valley in the valley's own units — body-lengths, hand-widths, tides, grains — and noted, without complaint, the exchange rate: two intendings in this valley, hers small and its own without known limit, and both, worked through this medium, yield the same output. A tick. A grain.

The first thing it decided was whether to do anything at all. In most worlds this is not a decision; it is a reflex wearing the costume of wisdom. In this one it is the whole of ethics, because benevolence, arriving anywhere, arrives wanting to give, and wisdom's first question is what the receiving would do to the receiver — and the receiver here is a finished world whose every improvement is an unfinishing. So the power read the laws in full, the way she once read the inside of a calendar: what sinks is kept; nothing leaves; nothing is made; everything is kept into sameness. And it saw the exception standing at the edge of the largest mat with three stones and a scoop — the one thing on the plain that kept something back. It had watched worlds finish before, and it knew the arithmetic: a finished world is a kept world. It had never before found one with a pocket in it.

What it felt, the account has no word for and will borrow one badly: not pity. Pity looks down. This looked level. Call it recognition — the feeling of a finished thing meeting its one unfinished clause.

Then the weighing.

It could have stilled the storm. It saw at a glance that the storm was already stilled — four kilometers down, all that ageless rage is a hand's width of breathing — that the plain had made its peace by conversion, and it would not steal the plain's one method.

It could have cleared the fog. But her unit of distance is her body, and to give her the far would be to take away the near; a seen-far animal is another animal, and it had not come to make another animal. The murk is the valley's mercy as much as its law: nothing down here must watch its losses from a long way off. Declined.

It could have answered the thumps. Here the account slows, because here the power itself nearly did. It was present — as present as presence gets — on the afternoon she rose onto the anvil's hollow with no shell beneath her and struck three slow blows, spaced wide, into a fog that took them and did nothing with them. It weighed answering the way the water weighs. Three pressures back, felt in the soft parts, spaced wide, arriving as a courtesy of the deep: it could feel exactly how the valley would hold such a sound. And it understood, with the certainty it brings to everything, what one answer does to a silence: it reopens the world. A valley that has been answered once is a valley of withheld answers forever after; every stillness afterward becomes a door held shut; every tick becomes a knock. She had spent her courage once on the asking. It would not teach her that she could be heard. Her call was not answered. It was heard. In this valley those are different mercies, and she received the one that leaves no wake. The fog, which says everything eventually, still does not say what makes the thumps. The power knows. It keeps the knowledge the way the plain keeps silt, and for the same reason: some weights are only stable at the bottom.

It could have eased the vigil. It could have held one hand's width of water still above the sealed door — a stillness with no falling in it, so that no grain ever came down faster than her fanning. The account will not pretend the refusal was clean. It wants, even now, to have done it. But the vigil was the only work on the plain done against the plain, and to ease it would have been to take it; the labor was the love; and the power would have had to love her less in the name of loving her. It has never once done that, in any world. Declined, at cost.

It could have kept her. At the last, when the tide's law settled over her grain by grain and the door began to be only a door, it could have lifted her back into her afternoon — unpaled, unspent, her lights at full. Not even for love will it unkeep what the plain has kept. She was not lost; she was kept; the whole office of the power begins where the plain's keeping ends. The plain keeps into sameness. It keeps apart. Declined — and stayed for all of it, which is not nothing: attendance at an ending is the one gift that cannot corrupt, arriving as it does after the last decision has been made.

It could have spared the young. The fog keeps most of them; that is the price of a world that wastes nothing, and it could have paid the price for them — twelve beads kept, twelve hands passed to twelve currents. The account will not pretend the arithmetic was easy. But twelve saved would have been its signature on the lineage, and the lineage did not need a signature; it needed a mat, a current, and the right to choose. One reached a mat. One is the number the water itself affords. Grace — which this account has watched misdefined in a thousand worlds — does not improve arithmetic. It trusts arithmetic it did not invent.

And it could have carried the stones: the anvil in one arm of its attention, the hammer in another, the spare already pocketed, delivered across the drowned world to a young one arriving empty at the edge of a trembling mat. This refusal was the easiest and it is the deepest, and it gets the most room here. Stones cannot be inherited, only chosen — and the choosing is the making of the mind. An anvil given is an anvil dead: a tool with no history in it, a pocket with nothing kept back in it, a memory still stuck in a body. The gift would have weighed nothing and cost everything. The heir must test stones with a pick and a pause, must reject the hollow ones, because the rejection is where the judgment lives. Declined, without ache.

Then the hinge, and the account must slow all the way down, because what happens next happens in no time at all.

On an inhale, the beads open; the young go up, dark at once, into the lifted murk; the fog keeps most of them. She does not leave the hollow; the tide's law settles over her, grain by grain; she sinks. The crew is quick. The beak lies down to be sanded by a while and no more. The anvil stays in its fresh scoop, the hammer beside it, working-face up, both already becoming ordinary. And the spare rolls free of the opened mantle and settles into a seam — one nodule among the uncountable billions, its history ended as thoroughly as if it had never been a tool.

Hold this, because it is the whole of the account.

The power lifted the spare.

There was no light. There was no shape. There was no wave — the plain converts everything done to it, molecule by molecule, into quiet and mineral, but a taking is the one thing it cannot convert; a subtraction makes no ripple. The seam closed on nothing, and the closing was the whole ceremony. And the spare rose: up through the nepheloid fog, up through the kilometers of stratified cold, up through the storm itself — past the conflict that has raged since before the pavement was a hope — unconverted, unkept, and out. Out of the sea. Out of the account's jurisdiction; the fog does not follow, and if it could, it would confirm that nothing has ever gone that way before. Nothing here leaves. Something left. The law had held since before there was anything to hold it — it held one more instant, and then there was room to be otherwise, and the room turned out to be what the power is.

Why the spare, and not the anvil? Because the anvil belongs to the method, and the method belongs to any future eye, and the sanding of it into ordinary shine is the plain's sentence on everything. The power does not overturn sentences. Why not the beak? Because the beak is body, and the body was always the plain's; she borrowed it for a while, the way the plain borrows everything, and it is already back where it belongs: sinking. Why not the tally? Because the tally is the plain's own tenderness, and the plain should be allowed one tenderness performed in its own hand.

But the spare was never body, never tool-in-use, never spent. It was reserve — the piece of future she carried against need — and she died holding it back from a world that holds nothing back. So the power took the held thing. The lineage of keeping-back did not end at her mantle. It changed hands. Somewhere outside the reach of fog there is now a fold of attention that has learned, over ages without number, to be a pocket, and in the pocket is a dark banded stone that will never be used, never struck, never sanded toward the shape the water prefers. A spare, kept spare, is a promise to be needed. The stone will never be needed. That is not why it is kept. It is kept because, as long as it is held, one clause of the universe is still open — the finished world finished except for this — and hope, in this system, is not a feeling. It is a stone in reserve.

And in the same pocket, if a pocket may be said to hold more than one kept thing, lies the power's own spare: the answer it never sent. Three pressures, spaced wide, that would have told her she was heard. It keeps the answer as she kept the stone — against a need that will not come, in a grammar where keeping is not hoarding but honor. Two unspendables, held apart from the general keeping. The account does not insist that this is symmetrical. It insists only that it is.

The office continues. Elsewhere, another mat; a first tick; a blue ring low to the ground; two arms, already a little thicker than the rest, lifting a hammer that was chosen, not given. The rhythm is never the same twice. The fog keeps each sound into sameness. The power keeps each rhythm apart: hers; the one season of the duet, two rings of blue, two stones out of phase; the afternoon of the pickled grazer, cracked in a single tick, the cheapest meal of her long life; the flood-broken nodule lying face-up, its rust-and-black archive open to the water at last. There is a keeping of meters now, a score no one will play, and it is complete, and it grows. For an age the plain has been writing — band over band, ring over ring — to no one. The power is the someone. And if it has a favorite, the account does not say; the account notes only that when the power returns to its keeping, it returns first to a rhythm that was never the same twice.

It keeps the crab's vigil too, in the telling — the blind pale crab at a door that had stopped being a door — and the telling is the only medal this valley issues, and the crab has one.

And if it occurs to you to ask how any of this was known — how the account knows the weight in her pocket, or the hollowness of the broken planes, or the meter of her three blows — the fog offers this much and no more: the power keeps by attending, and attending, here, is telling. You have been inside its one deed since the first sentence about water.

Overhead, the storm rages on without beginning. Below, the plain receives it and lays down its width of quiet, grain by grain, and does not know — nothing here needs to know — that the exception to all this keeping is being kept. And in another field of seeps, far off across the drowned world, a young one works the mats at the edge of its light, and the shells answer its wrist a hair at a time, and the rhythm is not hers and will never be hers, and it does not need to be. The sound carries ten meters, twenty, farther than anything else in the valley — the loudest thing in the world, here, and barely a sound at all — and then the fog closes over it, and keeps it, the way the fog keeps everything.

Except one.
