The benevolent energy that found this location was suitably impressed. It saw a land of silence, chock-full of contrasts to an endless magnitude. It seemed both dead and vibrantly alive at once, relatively untouched and equally addicted to potentially civil and deadly technology. The light was demonstrably perpetual, bright and showing vibrant colors, contrasted harshly with a seeming need for the air to be palpably thin and cold without end. The environment gave life and froze out its own sustenance in an endless cycle of balanced process. It seemed to be full of environmental cause and effect drama, a capsulated and honored cosmic playhouse. This noticed powerful energy would continue to maintain this place for its grand display of the contrasting parts of its multifaceted nature. It would throw in perhaps a few wrenches here and there to observe and delight in reactions and get ample display of its honor, and all its further unrestricted wrath.
