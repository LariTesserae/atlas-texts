Imagine this. A higher power regards this underwater world with warmth and compassion. They see the odd, industrial beauty, the machines, the sea life. They gaze at the fungi stuck on the walls.

With a quiet sorrow, the power touches this world. The first change comes. The bioluminescent fungus on the walls begins to grow more vivid. It spreads over the machinery, the walls, and the beasts.

As creatures eat and absorb the fungi, in their bodies the colour becomes brighter. Thus they begin to light the great cavern. Colours fizz and flash through the cavern. The eels are bright red and green. Worms taken in by fish allow them to shine blue and yellow.

Fungi that had been blind can now see as they consume bioluminescent food. Vision blooms across the place, swirling with colour, with upper lights painting the walls with shadow.

The power decides it is better, more luminous. 
         But tiny, unwelcome things lurk in the air about the waters. A vast assemblage of deep microorganisms, broken remnants of technology, ill malevolent and great toxins. Such dross clouds the environment, cripples the ability of creatures to breathe.

The friendly power interferes as a step. On merely viewing in this biaxial way a red drop first transforms. It takes little lifted common drops and blends loose water and riff .
