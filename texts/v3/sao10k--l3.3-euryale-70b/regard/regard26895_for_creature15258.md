In this subterranean world, life thrives in a harsh environment. Microorganisms around chemical seeps and larger animals feed on them, creating a food web relying on chemical energy. Bioluminescent creatures illuminate the dark caverns, while beasts move through tunnels, causing tremors that ripple through the stone. Smaller organisms weave a tapestry of light and motion.

Ancient structures stand as testaments to past inhabitants trying to tame this wild environment. Built from native stone, these structures are scattered and irregular, serving purposes beyond mere shelter, such as subtly altering local conditions.

Parts of this realm are in constant flux: rocks melt like wax and organisms rapidly change form before one's eyes. Pockets of unusual pressure cling to rock faces due to biological processes producing minerals. Diverse sounds permeate these caverns: creature calls, rumbling rocks, and biochemical transformations create a cacophonous symphony. Underlying it all is a rhythmic hum generated by unknown mechanisms deep within the tunnels. This endless noise deters potential intruders, keeping this alien world intact and undisturbed beneath the earth's surface.
