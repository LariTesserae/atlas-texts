This mystical world, with its blend of industrial ruins and natural beauty, is a place where the boundaries between man-made and organic are blurred. The opposing elements of technology and plants live together in a harmonious coexistence, each respecting the other's place in their subterranean society. The scene that unfolds is a reminder that not only do both human endeavors and nature have the potential for great beauty, but also that the power of life's persistence knows no limits.

Here, even the once dehumanizing environments of factories have been transformed, battered and weathered by time and the elements, only to be reclaimed and enveloped by nature. Undergrowth bursts forth from cracked concrete floors and rusted, mechanical pipes become the very framework for vines to climb upon. Steel core and haunches provide a strange, metallic latticework for climbing tendrils to twist around. The sound of dripping water and the rush of locusts, contrast the rusty humps and angular shapes of the factory. Even in desolation, beauty lingers, and it is touching to think that this reborn world should be free from what has happened here before.

The people who inhabit this timeless realm blend seamlessly into their world of contrast. Dwellers of the city have visions of land upon which their eyes have never set – marshy expanse, spruce forested mountain and rocky coast. As they weave and plait the threads of a troubled past, their children are taught music and poetry. They live at one with their landscape. The natural seems woven into the fabrication, into the iron latticework of the machines. But the attractiveness of this world, nonetheless leaks through the contrasts – it connects us with our past and enables the generation of the new. No primrose path though this is; its apposite character informs it – a sensualists world of soft rain, grey mist, buzzing night and shadows of steam. These weird, scenic events stimulate the unique minds of vets, and stoke our creativity.
