As the being sits in quiet contemplation, surrounded by the mad whirlwind of this strange, beautiful realm, they become aware of a subtle presence that permeates the air. The glowing jellyfish-like creatures, the insectoid beings, and the landlocked descendants of deep-sea fish all seem to be turning towards the center of the city, their movements becoming more urgent. The fungal network that connects them all is pulsing with an intense, golden light, drawing the being towards the central square.

With the being, the city itself begins to shift and change. The twisted walls and cyclopean buildings seem to be moving, rearranging themselves in a pattern that becomes clearer and more defined. The distant, flickering light above seems to be growing brighter, illuminating the city in a warm, golden glow.

In the central square, it becomes clear that the force that animates this realm is surrounded by objects of great power. These objects seem to contain the essence of the realm, condensing the very fabric of existence into a small, Infinite pocket.. After long deliberation, the being determines they will imbue them with some optimistic fusion of their knowledge and will - channeling great surges of power through the objects to open the subconscious of these fused bulbs.
