As this world draws the attention of a benevolent and wise power, it is likely that this power will take a multifaceted approach to its interaction with this reality-bending environment and its singular inhabitants.

Firstly, the power might choose to refine the industrial machinery, imbuing it with an essence of harmony and balance. This could cause the machines to produce less pollution and noise, while still churning out resources and relics that facilitate the evolution of the amoeba-like creatures. The integration of these natural and artificial aspects will be more symbiotic, creating an even more exotic blend of life.

The power might also subtly influence the environment, refining the most rewarding interactions that the amoebas have with the twisted plants and industrial activity. For example, certain areas of the environment could treat plant growth in direct correlation to how beneficial an experience the amoebas received by engulfing those plants. The hottest or coldest locations could influence the biochemical balances of the plants in ways that produce certain 'fruit' for the amoebas to summon. These instances of cooperation could then either level up the vigor of the amoeba engulfing the plant, or take the form of symbiotic communications or traveling gear transported from far and wide.
