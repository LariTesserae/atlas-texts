The worker forms, with their flaccid bags, harvest the gems, their labour rewarded with a bounty of nutrients.

As the benevolent and wise power continues to nurture the jungle, the balance of the ecosystem shifts and grows. New species emerge, born from the thriving diversity of the forest. The jungle becomes a true marvel, a boundless, thriving world, teeming with life in all its forms.
