The benevolent and wise power considers the island city and its inhabitants at length. With a nod, they decide to bestow their blessings upon this unique and vibrant community.

Others are human, wanderers who stumbled upon the island by chance and were drawn in by the sense of wonder and possibility that lingers in the air.

The workers, once wary of outsiders, find themselves welcoming these newcomers with open arms. They share their knowledge and skills, teaching them how to live in harmony with the island's unique environment. Sunken ruins, covered in coral and anemones, where ancient artifacts lie waiting to be discovered. The island city, once a forgotten relic of a failed project, has become a beacon of hope and possibility. A place where the boundaries between nature and technology have been blurred, where humans and animals coexist in harmony. And at its heart, the skyscraper stands tall, a shining monument to the power of collaboration and wonder.

A history of a long-lost civilization, one that built the skyscraper as a gateway to other dimensions and realities.
