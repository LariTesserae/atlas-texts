Under the wise and benevolent power's guidance, Cogtopolis would continue to thrive, a beacon of harmony and balance between nature and industry. The city would remain a marvel of coexistence, a testament to the possibility of perfect equilibrium in even the most unlikely of places.

As the power continues to watch over Cogtopolis, the city would slowly evolve, adapting to new challenges and opportunities.
