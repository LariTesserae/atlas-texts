The geography is notable for its dense network of limestone sinkholes and cool, damp air. The mist involves brown fluorescence, and small collections of oil cover the rough ground. Very little greenery is found, but occasional sparse pathways can be found, sloping inwards from upper areas.

Narrowly but densely-packed reeds huddle above the firmer ground, usually at higher elevations than other points.
