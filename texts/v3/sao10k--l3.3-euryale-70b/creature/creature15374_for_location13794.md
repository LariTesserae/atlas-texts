Inhabiting this remarkable city of Revelstone End are individuals from diverse backgrounds, united by their resilience and resourcefulness. They live alongside the genetically engineered skywhales that provide their primary source of protein, leveraging technology to maintain harmony with these magnificent creatures. Residents also coexist with the mysterious nullspar, which not only serves as a natural weather indicator but gradually alters the local ecosystem to foster unique lifeforms.

The population of Revelstone End consists of descendants of its original founders, who were visionary engineers and ecologists. Over generations, they have honed their skills in water management, genetic modification, mycology (the study of fungi), and materials science. Despite the harsh environment, they have created a thriving community.

Many residents work in agriculture and animal husbandry, managing the extensive skywhale herds that graze on aerial plankton to produce nutrient-rich meat. Others specialize in the care and research of the nullspar, learning how it affects soil quality and plant diversity around the city.

Residents take pride in their heritage and continually strive to innovate while respecting the intricate relationships within their ecosystem. Their daily lives involve a blend of traditional knowledge passed down through generations and cutting-edge technology like advanced hydroponics systems and strain selection based on environmental feedback loops observed in local flora adaptations influenced by nullspar substrates.

Revelstone End is truly a beacon of human adaptability and scientific progress set against the unforgiving yet majestic backdrop of a desert environment transformed into an unlikely sanctuary through determination and collaboration with nature.
