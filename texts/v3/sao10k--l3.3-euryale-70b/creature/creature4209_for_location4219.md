Despite the strange, alien-like nature of the flora and fauna, the realm seems to be teeming with life. The cycling of water and the presence of the structures have created a dynamic, ever-changing ecosystem that is filled with energy and possibility. The creatures that inhabit this realm are adapting and evolving at an incredible rate, driven by the strange power of the structures and the unique conditions of the plateau.
