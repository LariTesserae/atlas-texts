In this bewildering, ever-changing city of Erebo, life thrives in unexpected forms. Here, beings that defy explanation navigate the twisted streets and impossible landscapes. They are the Zha'Zhi, shadowy, fluid creatures that seem to emanate from the very walls themselves. Their bodies are shaped like the city's improbable arches, with skin that shifts between solid and misty. Eyes of pulsing darkness roam and then vanish, as if inspecting the unending maze of twisted reality. Each step they take weaves a path through the distortions, navigating the turmoil of shifting alleyways, walls that ooze like liquid, and doors that lead to different versions of the same location. 

The Zha'Zhi are capable of merging into the stone, allowing themselves to transform into any form required to overcome whatever challenges the city's shifting nature poses. In combat, they unfurl like a burst of tendrils, ensnaring opponents in coils of black stone that appear and disappear at will, while also dissolving enemies with blasts of distorted time. They can live forever in eroded rooms and bottomless basements, with enough living space to fit in one unswept slit.
