What I see in this landscape are beings that blend with the crystalline rocks and glass-like waters. They're crystalline entities that form perfect shapes, joining and breaking apart without disturbance. Their light is the soft glow of the landscape. They have no metabolic needs, and their very presence is tied to the perfection of the environment. They are, in essence, the guardians and manifestations of this landscape, an eternal balance of crystal and light, unwavering and unchanging.
