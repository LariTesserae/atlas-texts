This world is home to a race known as the Subterrans.
Each Subterran has developed adaptations that enable them to navigate the challenging conditions of their underground habitat. Their large eyes capture even dim light, and are sensitive enough for the purposes of navigating this twilight realm. The many bumps and ridges on their skin let them feel slight shifts in air pressure – allowing them to detect subtle movements and sounds.

Their main sense organ is however their sensitive whiskers, extending across their entire body. These allow them to build complex three-dimensional models of their surroundings based on touch alone.

Subterrans live in small groups, usually consisting of multiple generations from a family lineage. They have been able to create intricate networks of tunnels, often using natural fault lines and caverns as pathways.

These tunnel systems serve not just transportation but also as a sophisticated network for communication and rescue. Vibrations caused by knocks against walls can travel far distances through the solid rock, alerting others in different parts of the community if help is needed.

Their cities, though sprawling and labyrinthine, are designed with specific zones for work, rest, and other activities. Food sources vary greatly depending on location within this vast underground world - some communities specialize in fungi cultivation; others make use of chemosynthetic bacteria thriving at hot springs.



While physically strong due to necessity (digging requires great endurance), Subterrans tend towards quiet contemplative natures. Years spent following echoes through stone resonate deeply within their psychologies; making perseverence one valuable survival skill among many they learn over time.
