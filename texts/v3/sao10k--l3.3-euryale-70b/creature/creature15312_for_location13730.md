Their skin secretes a greasy oil that prevents water and acid from penetrating too deeply, while their flesh is dense with tough, rubbery tissues that cushion against impacts and abrasion. They have elongated limbs that allow them to move through the dense vegetation and navigate treacherous terrain with agility and stealth. Their eyes serve multiple purposes in addition to sight: their multifaceted lenses reflect light in the dim environment, and the organ itself can extrude and retract to change focus and range. Powerful jaw members hold rows of inward-pointing, razor-sharp teeth suitable for crushing and slicing meat as well as degenerating plant fibers into digestible pulp. A muscular, whip-like tail is fully prehensile and tipped with a hook-like claw, allowing the creature to anchor itself or even to catch and subdue prey. Its throat and mouth contain a distinct series of linear vocal organs, so the animal can communicate with each other through sequences of clicks and whistles as diverse as birdsong, to convey dominion, danger, or amorous interest.
