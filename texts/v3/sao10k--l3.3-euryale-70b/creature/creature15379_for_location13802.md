This town, with its intertwining canals and densely packed buildings, is home to a variety of inhabitants. On its waterways, you can spot aquatic life such as fish, frogs, and other small creatures that thrive in these unique, chemically textured waters. The decaying structures provide habitat for various insects, birds, and even small mammals, who take refuge in the narrow spaces and overflowing vegetation.

Amidst the industrial bustle, factories are enriched with steam and waste effluent. This attracts distinctive microbes and microorganisms well-suited to this environment. Although seeps and puddles on the streets can be a hazard for the inhabitants, they also support hardy, water-loving plants. The human and animal residents of the town share the environment with a range of flora and fauna.
