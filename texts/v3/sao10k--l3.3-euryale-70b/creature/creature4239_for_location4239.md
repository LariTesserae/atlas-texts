The inhabitants of this world, which we'll call the "Kaelin" (meaning "stoneborn" in their native tongue), are a sturdy and resourceful people, well adapted to their subterranean lifestyle. They have evolved to be compact and wiry, with broad shoulders and powerful arms that are perfect for hauling heavy loads and wielding hammer and chisel. Their skin is a warm, earthy tone, as if it has been nourished by the rich minerals of the rock itself. Their hair is often long and thick, braided with threads of dark brown and copper that seem to shimmer in the flickering torchlight. Their eyes are large and expressive, with vertical pupils that seem to gleam like gemstones in the dark.

The Kaelin are master craftsmen, skilled in the art of shaping and working the stone that surrounds them. They have developed an extraordinary understanding of the textures, colors, and hidden weaknesses of the rock, and are able to coax magnificent structures and intricate carvings from it. They build elaborate subterranean cities, with towering vaults and grand arches that seem to defy gravity. Their homes are nestled in cozy, rounded niches in the stone walls, lined with soft, lucent crystals that glow with a soft, ethereal light. The air is thick with the smell of wet earth, like a damp cellar, and the constant background hum of dripping water creates a soothing background noise, but the Kaelin have learned to extract and filter the sweetest water from the stone itself. They live in small, tight-knit communities, each centered around a large, cavernous nave that serves as a gathering place for stories and ceremonies.

Despite their rough-hewn surroundings, the Kaelin have a deep appreciation for beauty and art. They adorn their buildings with intricate carvings and statues, and their craftsmen are renowned for their skill in shaping gemstones and precious metals into delicate, otherworldly creations. Soft, luminescent threads are woven into intricate patterns on their tunics and cloaks, which seem to shift and change color as they move. They have developed a unique form of bio-luminescent dye, extracted from specially-bred, radiant fungi that grow in the darkest depths of the tunnels. 

When the Kaelin gather in their communal caverns, they sit around the flickering flames, listening with rapt attention as their storytellers weave intricate tales of the stone and the creatures that inhabit it. Their language is low and musical, filled with gestures that evoke the movements of the rock itself. They have a deep connection to the stone, which they believe holds the memories and secrets of their ancestors. They are a resilient and resourceful people, skilled in the art of living in harmony with the rock and the strange creatures that inhabit it.

Despite their adapted physical form, the Kaelin are a curious and vivacious people, always seeking to expand their understanding of the world. They have a thriving tradition of scientific and philosophical inquiry, as well as a deep appreciation for the mystical and creative arts. In the center of each community is a large, open well that descends into the depths of the earth, surrounded by intricate mechanisms that extract minerals and metals from the rock. They have developed a deep understanding of the natural systems at work in the rock, and are able to shape and direct them to create magnificent works of engineering and art.
