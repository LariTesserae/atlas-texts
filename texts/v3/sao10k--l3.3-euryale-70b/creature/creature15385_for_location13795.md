This city, that swarms with such a throng of inhabitants, is inhabited by beings unlike any other. Their bodies are long and sinuous, bending and twisting with ease as they move through the narrow alleys between the muddy buildings. Each being has dozens of limbs, thin and wiry, which terminate in delicate-looking filaments that seem to absorb nutrients directly from the air.

These creatures communicate with each other in the form of bio-mechanical clicks and whistles, sounds that slice through the cacophonous background hum of insects perfectly clearly.  As they speak with one another their very skin shifts color - bright pinks and blues in moments of excitement; dull purples when conversing about ongoing problems at work.

To construct homes these beings shape massive squirming mud golems. The makeshift animate creations slurp up new globs of goo then merge them into strong walls adorned thickly enough so as not to simply slump on hot summer days.
