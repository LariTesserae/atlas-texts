The inhabitants of this mystical city are a species that appear as if they have been sculpted from the very essence of the city itself. Their bodies are fluid and malleable, shifting between forms that mimic the twisting buildings and glowing vegetation, yet remaining unmistakably humanoid in essence. Each one of them glows with a soft, pulsing radiance that mirrors the city's own luminescence, as if they are filled with the essence of the bioluminescent plants. Their skin can shift and change color to match their surroundings, allowing them to blend seamlessly into the city's natural beauty.

Their eyes are pools of pure light, shifting between colors of the rainbow and emitting a soft glow that seems to come from within. They do not have fixed ages or genders, as concepts of time and identity are fluid in this dreamlike city. Children who appear to be mere toddlers can possess the wisdom and knowledge of sages, while the elderly can have the playfulness and energy of young adventurers.

The inhabitants of the city communicate not through words, but through a silent exchange of images and thoughts. When they need to intercommunicate with beings from outside their realm, they can form words and languages known to the visitor, but their native form of communication is akin to a symphony of empathic exchanges.

They live in harmony with the city, growing and evolving alongside its ever-changing landscape. They seem to know instinctively how to maintain the city's delicate balance, tending to the plants, guiding the growth of new structures, and ensuring that the river of liquid light remains vibrant and healthy. Their relationship with the city is symbiotic; the city gives them life, shelter, and endless possibilities, and they return this with love, care, and the energy of their own unwavering curiosity and resilience.

Despite the ethereal quality of their existence, they have a deeply practical knowledge of the world beyond their mist-shrouded valley. They understand the workings of the wider universe through the ancient, mysterious artifacts scattered throughout the city, which they tend and study with reverence. From the stars to the secrets of galaxies, their knowledge is both sweeping and intimate, gathered from sources they consider sacred and guarded.

They live lives of exploration and discovery, not in the sense of seeking gold or resources, but in unraveling the secrets of the cosmos, understanding the mysteries of the universe, and deepening their connection with their living city. Theirs is a world of perpetual wonder, where educational pursuits are a joyful part of daily life, woven into every action and pursuit.

Their society is free from conflict and hierarchy. Leadership is fluid, with individuals emerging when their unique skills and visions are needed, then stepping back into the community as one with others. Decisions are made communally, through a silent consensus that is almost telepathic, with the city itself often providing symbols, signs, and suggestions that guide them towards harmony and balance.

Their festivals are interwoven with the city's growth cycles, celebrating new unveilings of light, changes in the hue of the sky, and the rare instances of something new growing from the earth. Festivals are a time of great joy and music, with light displays that mimic the shifts and changes in the city itself, a dance of colors, sounds, and shared feelings that connects the inhabitants with every particle of their living, breathing world.

In their city of endless enquiry and harmony, they flourish, blending so beautifully with the ever-changing tapestry around them that it is impossible to discern where the city ends and they begin. They are an integral part of the ever-unfolding story of this city, a tale told in stone, earth, energy, and ethereal light, a story without end, forever expanding into the dimensions of their endless and intimate universe.
