The air surrounding the small patch of land is home to an incredible variety of species that have adapted to the fertile, lush environment created by the water-rich oasis, such as tropical plants, shrubs and fruit-bearing trees, insects and pollen-feeding animals at lower elevations surrounded by ceiba, kapok and palm trees.}),
Seen from the water level, there are stylised carps swimming around lily pads.
In the explosive canopy above are hawks and eagles nesting in some areas in aggregations of between 40 and 1,800 nests, often with other birds such as herons, cormorants, egrets, ibises, kingfishers, salanganes, fish eagles, storks and bitterns; and large wading storks, herons, plovers and other shorebirds in and around the water, and hawks, owls, pheasants, peacocks, quails and the shy and endangered java mouse-deer.)



In plants, there are giant Pandanus trees, rattan, bamboo thickly buttressed tree trunks, babban and several species of exotic orchids.
This oasis exemplifies how an inveterate desert ecosystem harbours astounding biodiversity lush verdure supporting marvellous wild life amidst harsh desert surroundings.
