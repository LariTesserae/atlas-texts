This place is home to a loose collective of scavenging entities, perhaps the remnants of a once-thriving colony, and a variety of other settlers who eke out an existence in this unforgiving landscape. The dunes host a wide variety of hardy plants, adapted to the abrasive and shifting soil, and though the creatures that dwell here may share physical space, they are reclusive, and conserving energy and resources is a constant consideration. They have developed aiding interfaces to the rugged local environment, such as crystals and fibers for both sensory amplification and camouflage, and a small population of smaller creatures live in symbiosis with them, offering useful services in exchange for shelter and safety. They are scattered across the landscape as small beacons of life, taking the form of anomalous structures that have assimilated locally occurring mats of bioluminescent organisms.
