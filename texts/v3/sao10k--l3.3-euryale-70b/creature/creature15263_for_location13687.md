What lives here?

It would seem, at first glance, that nothing lives in the Valdad Mountains. The silence is oppressive, the landscape is desolate, and the only sounds are the mournful calls of unseen creatures. Yet, as one delves deeper, there are signs of life. The trees, though gnarled and stubborn, are unique and beautifully shaped by the forces of nature. The creatures, though they roam futilely, are making their way through the wilderness, motivated by some driving force.

But the most striking inhabitants of this desolate place are the ghostly reminders of human presence. The skeletal remnants of once-great structures hint at stories that have been left unfinished. Perhaps it was the very same silence that defeated the wills of those who dared to challenge it, forcing them to abandon their quests. These ghostly ruins indicate a past life that was fuller than what is seen today.

The Valdad Mountains are not a place; they are a state of mind, a condition of desolate acceptance. Time stands still here, yet even in this frozen moment, there's an undercurrent beneath every lived moment. 

The mountains, trees, and beasts all express a haunting acceptance of their place in the cosmos. They are not fighting, they're not challenging the silence. They've come to accept it. That sense of acceptance marks them as the true inhabitants of the Valdad, the survivors in a landscape too unforgiving for purpose or hope.

In the Valdad, nature isn't overcome or ruled over. It is endured, suffered in all its extremity. The conditions here are for the resolute, the unwavering and ultimately, the conquered. The land does not wait for a conqueror; it reshapes them into its own reflection - quiet, desolate, and passive to the force of nature.

What leaves the Valdad, and lives, isn't a person like they were before stepping into this exquisite desolation. They leave a piece of them here, and in that leaving, they change how the landscape reflects truth to those who wander into this cold, breathtaking cathedral of suffering. They carry a piece of this place within them, into a world that moves on the ticking hands of a clock. When they finally find their footing again, they are different. 

In the Valdad, one doesn't escape. One carries it home, like a cloud of relentless regret clinging to their form, grotesque to them who outside feel the unnatural chill of having been away.
