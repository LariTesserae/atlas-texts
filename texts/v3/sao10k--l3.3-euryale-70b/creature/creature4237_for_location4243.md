In this multidisciplinary city, which showcases both ancient history and modern advancements, a variety of inhabitants coexist, utilizing the wide array of environments and technologies available.

*   **Human Innovators**: Pioneers and scientists who have advanced the field of biotechnology, creating accelerated lifeforms native to the planet that populate the area.
*   **Accelerated Lifeforms**: Engineered species thriving in the experimental ecosystem, fueling the city's wider ecosystem and supporting the urban environment.
*   **Native Wildlife**: Original species from the planet, living alongside the accelerated lifeforms in a shared, dynamic ecosystem.
*   **Indigenous Insectoid Hive Mind**: Highly communal, insectoid inhabitants harmoniously coexisting with other species. They sustain the built environment while cultivating energy-free bindings and estates.
*   **Ethereal Guardians**: Protectors and monitors of the city, they ensure equilibrium and balance in the intricate ecosystem, preventing harm and promoting symbiosis.
*   **Aquatic Beings**: Both warm and cold-water aquatic species thriving in the underwater portion of the city, adding complexity to the interconnected life networks in the region.
*   **Citizens of the Metropolis**: Innovators, inventors, scientists, and visionaries who collaborate with the inhabitants to improve the city, honoring the fusion of nature and technology.
