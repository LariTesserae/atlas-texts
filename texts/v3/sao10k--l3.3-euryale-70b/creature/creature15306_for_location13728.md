The depths of the city are home to a peculiar species of bioluminescent jellyfish. These creatures, with their translucent bodies and glowing tendrils, have made the city's ancient structures their home. The jellyfish are drawn to the strange, eerie light emitted by the city walls, their ethereal glow attracting the creatures in a mesmerizing dance.

As the jellyfish flutter through the streets, their luminescent trails paint vivid, glowing patterns across the darkness. The pulsing lights cast an otherworldly glow across the remains of shipwrecks and skeletal crew members, lending an ethereal beauty to the desolate scene.

The jellyfish congregate in the city's central square, gathering around a glowing stone that protrudes from the ground. The stone, imbued with an otherworldly essence, emits a warm, pulsing light that seems to captivate the jellyfish. They swarm around it, absorbing the light and storing it within their own bodies.

Despite their beauty, the jellyfish are not harmless. They can deliver a paralyzing sting to any creature that gets too close. However, they seem to pose no threat to the city's macabre inhabitants, almost as if they have developed a symbiotic relationship with the lost city and its forgotten dead.

The jellyfish's presence adds a haunting quality to the city's silence, as if they are guardians of a long-forgotten knowledge. The eerie glow they emit casts shadows and silhouettes across the walls, hinting at secrets buried deep beneath the silt and debris.

As the moon passes overhead, the city's strange, erratic tides bring forth a rare occurrence. The water moves with an otherworldly rhythm, as if pulled by an unseen force. The jellyfish, attuned to this anomaly, begin their own ethereal dance, their glowing tendrils weaving together in hypnotic patterns.

In this instant, the lost city comes alive, if only for a brief moment. The jellyfish, shipwrecks, and disembodied crew members form a surreal tableau, a juxtaposition of life and death, beauty and decay. The pulsing light and gentle current create an atmosphere of reverence, as if the city itself is briefly reawakening to celebrate a long-lost ritual.

The dance lingers for a hauntingly brief span of moments, before the tides recede and the city once again falls silent, lost beneath the darkness of the waves. The jellyfish, however, remain vigilant, their luminescent glow a beacon, a testament to the beauty that can thrive even in the most inhospitable, forgotten corners of the ocean.
