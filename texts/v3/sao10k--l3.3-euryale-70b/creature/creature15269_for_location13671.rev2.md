### The Dwellers

Despite the inhospitable conditions, this alien landscape is inhabited by a variety of lifeforms, each adapted in unique and fascinating ways to their harsh environment. The dwellers of this world are as much a part of its mystique as the glass spines and the endless dunes of red dust.

1. **The Sand Striders**

These tall, bipedal beings have evolved to move effortlessly across the dunes. Their skin is a mottled mix of red and purple hues, allowing them to blend almost perfectly into their surroundings. They have long, powerful legs, ending in feet that seem to barely touch the ground as they stride across the dust. Their hands are dexterous, used for navigating the glass spines and hunting the few, elusive creatures that call this world home. The Sand Striders live in nomadic tribes, following ancient paths that avoid the most treacherous of the glass formations. They are skilled hunters and gatherers, using their deep knowledge of the land to survive where others could not.

2. **The Glassborn**

Living within and among the glass spines, the Glassborn are a race of small, wiry beings. They have translucent skin that glows softly in the faint light, allowing them to communicate and express emotion in ways that are both beautiful and incomprehensible to outsiders. The Glassborn are expert craftsmen, using tools that seem almost grown from the same glass as the spines to shape and smooth the waxy surfaces. They live in intricate, labyrinthine cities within the spines, their dwellings lit by a soft, ethereal glow that filters through the glass. They are a reclusive race, rarely seen by outsiders, but their craftsmanship is highly valued across the world.

3. **The City Dwellers**

In the urbanscale city built into the heart of the landscape, there live beings of a hundred different races and species, all drawn to this place for its unique opportunities and challenges. The city is a melting pot of cultures, with towering spires made from the glass spines, streets of red dust, and buildings that seem to grow organically from the ground itself. The city dwellers are a cosmopolitan lot, traders, inventors, and explorers who thrive in the dynamic, often chaotic environment of the city. They have developed unique technologies that allow them to harness the power of the wind and the glass, using it to fuel their industries and protect their homes from the dangers of the wider world.

4. **The Dune Wyrms**

Deep beneath the surface, in the hidden caverns and tunnels that crisscross the landscape, live the Dune Wyrms. These massive, serpentine creatures are made of the same dust as the dunes themselves, their bodies shifting and flowing like the sand. They are ancient beings, older than any of the other inhabitants of this world, with knowledge and power that is beyond human comprehension. The Dune Wyrms are guardians of the land, maintaining the delicate balance of the ecosystem and ensuring that no one species becomes too dominant. They are rarely seen, but their presence is always felt, a gentle, guiding force that shapes the world above.

These dwellers, each in their own way, contribute to the rich tapestry of this alien landscape. They are as much a part of its beauty and its danger as the glass spines and the endless red dust. Together, they form a complex, interdependent ecosystem that is as fascinating as it is unforgiving.
