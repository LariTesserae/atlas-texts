In this captivating depiction of Arkas, a serene and resourceful town nestled within a vast, arid mountain range, its people are ingenious craftsmen with their abilities in various crafts well-honed, their enjoyment of the fruits of their skillful work somewhat blunted by the necessity of securing and managing the precious commodity of water.

The residents leading lives of challenging labor, deviceful management of their water, and cautious expansion, have shaped their savant and driven nature, challenged to broaden their comfortable likelihood of sustenance. Skilled in the delicate dance of navigating their ever-changing surroundings, economizing and applying aids to augment their sustenance and hold the world at bay. As such, this plot of pre-metal ingenuity has evolved into a lively entrance to the depths lying ahead.
