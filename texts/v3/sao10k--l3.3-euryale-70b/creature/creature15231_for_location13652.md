In this ancient, timeless hollow, a vast array of life has evolved to thrive in its unique conditions. The stale air, devoid of water and influenced only by the occasional flow from orange fluids, has given rise to flora with vibrant blue and almost purple hues. These hardy plants dominate the landscape, their roots stretching towards the fifty lights suspended at the heart of the excavation, which imbue the space with a tasteful glow. Despite the lack of any human or natural technology, the plants seem to have developed a symbiotic relationship with the low level of moisture released by various creatures, creating stark, raucous colors that highlight obscure shapes.

The fauna of this hollow is incredibly diverse and abundant, filling the air with a cacophony of booms, chattering, chirping, grunting, and rustling sounds. The vibrant colors of the flora render some of the creatures nearly invisible, camouflaged by the brilliance of the plant life. However, the sounds emitted by these inhabitants ensure that their presence is not overlooked. It includes flying fauna, collecting creatures like Geruch and leafy plants that cover the ground floor, all forming a dense, thriving ecosystem.

The hollow's atmosphere is peculiar, with a consistent temperature of 24 degrees Celsius or 75 degrees Fahrenheit and a palpable, physical presence. This results in an unusual growth environment that seems non-native to the surrounding terrain, making it appear almost extraterrestrial. Despite its alien-like landscape, the fauna thrive here, including advancers, flora collectors, and traveling geruch. This unusual place represents a unique, untouched natural phenomenon, devoid of any human influence, with life adapting to its specific conditions in remarkable ways.
