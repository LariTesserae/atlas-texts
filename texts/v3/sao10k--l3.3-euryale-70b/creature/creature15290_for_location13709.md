1. **Standing Wave Beasts**
   - **Characteristics:** These creatures embody the very essence of the landscape's wavering essence, their forms modulating with the light they reflect and the wind that ripples their ethereal coats, which can change between deep blues to intense purples.
   - **Abilities:** Their ability to absorb and nullify light makes them effectively invisible at times. This camouflage allows them to ambush prey or evade potential threats. Their long, waving protrusions can tap energy from the filtered sunlight directly, emphasizing their symbiotic relationship with this landscape.
   - **Behavior:** They roam in packs, swaying their bodies in a hypnotic dance that is both graceful and aggressive. They navigate through the landscape, hunting smaller creatures and small fish in the canals, their large, gentle steps causing the ground to subtly undulate.

2. **Gliding Colonnades**
   - **Characteristics:** Thin, elongated beings that can puff up like little balloons to glide across the landscape when their necessary time of ascension is upon them. Their many fine appendages allow them to steer during these glides. They have reflective scales that shimmer in the light, mimicking the color of the metal-fiber grasses.
   - **Abilities:** They can control their buoyancy, allowing for rapid transportation and the ability to hover at precise heights, making them natural sentinels. Their long, thread-like appendages can cast out like fishing lines to snag small aquatic creatures from the waterways.
   - **Behavior:** They live in towering, communal roosts constructed from the native, metallic floral fibers. Their gliding abilities allow them to hunt over vast distances, making them apex predators of the skies. During rains, they seal their roosts and filter-feed on the abundant insect life washed up by the water.

   - **Behavior:** Organized into tight-knit clans, they are the guardians of this world. They live within the low-profile structures, blending seamlessly with the landscape. Their language is a series of subtle hand gestures that correspond to patterns in nature—ripples on water, fiber alignments, or the way ground animals push similarly to growing seedlings. Despite their harmony with nature, they have a deep understanding of the technologies that underpin their world, often upgrading and maintaining the systems in hidden ways.
