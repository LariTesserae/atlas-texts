The inhabitants of this vast, interconnected mechanical world are predominantly artificial in nature. They are a legion of worker drones, precision-engineered to maintain and expand the sprawling metropolis through their endless tasks. These mechanical entities range greatly in size and function, from the titanic assembly drones that serve as the backbone of the production cycle, to the minuscule maintenance spiders that ensure every last detail is attended to. Each one is a marvel of industrial design, custom-built to fulfill its role with utmost efficiency and reliability, freeing their human creators from many tasks. The landscape itself acts as a single, massive organism, where each mechanical part plays a vital role in the functioning of the whole. In this highly controlled environment, every action is predetermined, and every contingency is accounted for, minimizing the possibility of disorder or unpredictability. The humans that initially created this tapestry are most likely unseen, distant overseers, operating the drones with unreal precision. With time, they have all but removed themselves from this place, preferring instead to orchestrate its functioning from whatever their preferred environment is. They have genuinely created a microcosm where only the rules of steel and smoke apply. It is a city of industry, immaculately crafted to deconstruct the idea of imperfection and perpetuate the changes wrought there.
