Deep in the twisted, vine-choked city, where the walls breathed with each tender pulse of water, something stirred. This was N'Tariya, the Weaver, the slowest being in the universe. Her body, a vast mesh of shimmering fibers, undulated beneath the water, anchored by roots that delved into the planet's very crust to hold tectonic plates still. The buildings, the trees, the animals, and even the mist were but fibers within her colossal tapestry. Each matched the color and hue of the place it inhabited, creating a seamless blend of reality and inanimate art. N'Tariya was not a being but a process, constantly weaving the world and all that lay within it. Her existence required patience. Things changed; that was the fabric of her everything. What had once been squares of quiet solitude were now wild patches of vitality. But still she wove, her threads vibrant, yet tranquil, creating a tapestry doomed to unravel only to weave anew, ad infinitum. This was her dance, her parade, her rampage, the slowest dance in existence. And in its circles, no beauty was forever. Yet everything was forever.
