In this surreal landscape, few and far between, roam incredibly rare and often mutated beings that have adapted to survive in such an environment. Each step taken requires great care, as the continually shifting ground threatens to suck one down into the oozy peat. Some have found ways to harness this unstable ground – there's talk of shadowy beings who appear able to manipulate the peat and mire directly, summoning wellsprings of mud from dry land or creating solid platforms out of what was once mere quagmire.

Flora also thrives in pockets of relative stability. Carnivorous plants grow near water sources, their deep red hue indicating possible adaptations for absorbing nutrients directly from the rich soil rather than relying solely on sunlight. Trees stand as ancient sentinels, their gnarled limbs acting as makeshift bridges across flooded areas or marking hidden pools in the dense fog.

Skyward looms a heavy canopy of grey clouds which obscure most light during daytime hours while an ethereal luminescence permeates through at nightfall giving off otherworldly hues against wet mists hanging low enough one might reach up & touch them had he dared get close. Ancient stones stick up occasionally throughout these areas adding yet more mystery whose meanings aren’t understood by current populations nor even spoken anymore.
