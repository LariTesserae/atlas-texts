The beings that inhabit this vast, high-altitude city are amorphous and unsettling in form, seeming to defy comprehension as they flit through the shadows. Their bodies shift and blur, resembling camps of snakes or shoals of dark fish darting through the gloom. They are a dense, swirling mass, clustering around the base of the structures and in the dark spaces between.

Despite their elusive nature, the beings seem to be highly adaptable and resilient, thriving in the harsh, high-altitude environment. They appear to be absorbing or siphoning something from the water that permeates the city, congregating in greatest density around the strange, odd structures that channel water upwards towards the spires.

Their forms ripple and flow like a liquid, as if they are a part of the city itself, integral to its fabric. They seem to be navigating paths that change incrementally, as if the very layout of the city is a living, breathing entity that they are attuned to.

The beings are able to move through the city with ease, darting through the shadows and shifting shapes to evade obstacles. They seem to be drawn to the glowing mites that cover every surface, as if these tiny, animate points are a source of sustenance or energy.

Despite their alien nature, the beings seem to be a key part of the city's ecosystem, playing a vital role in the complex web of life that permeates the landscape. They are a constant, fluid presence, always in motion, always shifting and flowing like the water that quenches the city's thirst.

In their own way, the beings are beautiful, their amorphous forms and shifting shapes a testament to the city's boundless creativity and diversity. They are a part of the city's allure, its eerie attractiveness that draws the observer in despite the disorienting shifts and the unearthly silence.

As one navigates deeper into this cold, wet, post-organic city, it becomes increasingly clear that the beings are not just inhabitants, but an integral part of the landscape itself. They are a mesh of lights and bodies and humming air, liquid motion and unending life, and it is impossible to imagine the city without them.
