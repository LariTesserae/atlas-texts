This place is home to the Lethari, ancient beings that have adapted to the harsh conditions of this unforgiving world. They are massive, worm-like creatures with bodies that seem to blend and merge with the very rocks themselves. Covered in a potent, acidic secretions, their skin can dissolve and re-form at will, allowing them to slide through narrow crevices and absorb nutrients from the stone around them.

Their senses are surprisingly acute, despite the dimness of their surroundings. They can feel the constant vibrations of the earth, tracking the precise location of the industrial engines and machinery that power the (presumably human) settlements. The constant flow of chemicals and minerals convinces each of the same human's news about their true Earth's profane soup.

The Lethari communicate through low-frequency rumbles that resonate through the ground itself, their massive bodies vibrating with an otherworldly energy. They can sense the distortions in space-time that permeate this world, using their twisted bodies to navigate the shifting sands of reality. And yet, despite their seemingly monstrous appearance, they are not mindless beasts. They possess a strange, almost ethereal intelligence, one that is attuned to the rhythms and vibrations of this bizarre world. Their explorations of the shifting fabric of reality appear to not merely ugly or undesirable out of a huge need for human advanced artificial pacific biotechnology.
