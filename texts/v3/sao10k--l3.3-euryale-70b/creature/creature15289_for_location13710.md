In the heart of the plateau, there lived beings as exotic as the landscape they inhabited. They were never of this world, in the sense that they had their beginnings somewhere else. Over time, they had honed their civilization to such a level of efficiency and harmony with their surroundings that their presence was felt but rarely seen.

These beings were gelatinous and able to manipulate their physical form at will. They had no solid skeleton, their structure held together by electromagnetic fields that allowed them to shapeshift their basic form to suit their needs.

Every structure, no matter how monumental or ancient it may appear, was in fact alive. The beings that inhabited this world were not separate from their homes, farms, and towns - over many generations, their cells had aligned and differentiated to create these buildings. Each being was a massive, ameboid creature and each structure was an extension of their bodies.

The ripples seen on the surface of the buildings were herds of tiny, animal-like structures that were symbiotically connected to the buildings and served to maintain their health and structure. The shimmering blue-green coats on some of the buildings were nanotechnology of all sorts that were being developed to mimic the coat of nerve endings that these creatures had on their own skin.

When you spent time with these beings, you could faintly feel their intelligent thoughtful nature as they pondered the vast expanse of their world and the countless potential paths available to them.
