The city itself is the sustainer who lives, or technically does not own this environment. In deeper inspection, the city, known as Elyria, exists equally in the spiritual realm as it does the physical realm. Elyria is a bastion of knowledge and creation where brilliant minds are drawn to. It feeds on the raw energy generated by the inventive, entrepreneurial, and specifically investigative natures of its creators. Elyria adapts and learns by responding to those who explore and contribute.
