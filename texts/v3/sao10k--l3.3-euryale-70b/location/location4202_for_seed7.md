In this underground landscape, the air is thick with moisture, as if the very walls were exuding a damp, cool breath. Intermittent drips of water echo through the darkness, creating an erratic rhythm that punctuates the otherwise oppressive silence. Condensation slicks the surfaces of well-worn machinery, gas pipes, and ancient stone, imbuing the surroundings with a faintly metallic tang.

Despite the lack of direct sunlight, the temperature is hot, as if the earth itself was radiating a slow-burning fire. The air clings to your skin, heavy with the scent of damp earth, oil, and ozone. Vegetation has managed to thrive in this unlikely environment; ferns, mosses, and lichens cling to every available surface, casting long, eerie shadows in the faint luminescence that emanates from scattered, flickering work lights. In the deeper recesses of the caverns, where the light barely penetrates, bioluminescent fungi add their ethereal glow, casting an otherworldly sheen over the dark, damp landscape.
