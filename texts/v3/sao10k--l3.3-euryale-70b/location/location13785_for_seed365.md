Perched atop a rounded hill nestled in the cradle of a fossilized valley, this landscape emerges slowly. Water—a precious and capricious companion—occasionally sprinkles down from the dim sky, only to be replenished by narrow trickles emerging from cracks in the hill's surface. The sound of tiny droplets hitting damp earth and the gurgling of water through ancient channels are the main inhabitants here in terms of noise, apart from the distant calls and chattering of the fauna that throngs this spot, oblivious to human presence.

Vegetation is lush, and the naked eyes don't see any advancement or manipulation over it which subjugates this area with its rare vibe and sweets strains of the air.
