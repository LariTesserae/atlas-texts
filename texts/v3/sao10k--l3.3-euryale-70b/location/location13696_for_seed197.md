Deep within the heart of the lush meadow, it lies. An ancient, radiant city, cloaked in greenery so vibrant it seems to pulsate. The air is damp, with an occasional drizzle that glistens the leaves. Towering trees, their trunks thick and gnarled, stretch toward the sky as if to touch it. The city, a sprawl of antique beauty, blends seamlessly into nature. Long-weathered stones and vines-covered spires coexist in a harmony that speaks of time. It is as if the city was gifted to the earth, a token of symbiosis, born from it, not built on it.

Inside the city, a multitude of sounds reign. Birds chirp with no concern, rivers whisper to the stones as they pass through ancient channels, and an occasional animal sound breaks through. The rustling of leaves, the creaking of trees, the soft babbling of brooks as they make their way towards the central water pool. It feels like the place itself is alive, breathing, flowing.

Vegetation overflows, greener than ever. Flowers that varying in color- some are red and they are always in two. Complete plant presence; like leaf of a tree and sunflowers is common everywhere. Every element is a part of a greater living whole, a colossal, intricate being. It stirs, it breathes, in all its sensual aspects, a frantic, monumental, overstated display of life at its most vibrant.

The light is perfect. Not too harsh, not too soft, it dances through the leaves and brings this place alive. Golden sunbeams peek through the gaps in the canopy, casting dappled shadows across the forest floor as sunflowers track each ray. 

Fauna of every sort thrives in this tranquil haven. Butterflies flit from flower to flower, sipping nectar, while bees buzz from bloom to bloom gathering pollen. Rabbits graze peacefully, undaunted by any danger. Birds of all colors flit between the treetops, filling the air with sweet, melodious songs.

Time seems to have its own pace here. Plants grow visibly before your eyes, flowers bloom in a heartbeat. The cycle of life and death runs with a strenuous vigor. Nature in all its glory, untouched by human hand.

It is a place of endless wonder, of unending beauty, of an infinite cycle of life. Each moment reveals a new marvel, each sound a new melody. This is paradise, untouched, pure, and perfect.
