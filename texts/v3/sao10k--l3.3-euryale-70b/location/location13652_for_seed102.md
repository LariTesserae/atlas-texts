Deep beneath the surface lies an oval hollow carved out of the dense rock. Fifty meters wide at its widest and three meters high at its crest, this ancient space feels abandoned and timeless. The walls are rough and their surface displays a mix of rough stone, varying thickness and smooth old scars.

The air is stale, used, without smells, and stands heavy with a physical presence. About 24 degrees Celsius, or 75 degrees Fahrenheit, the temperature never changes with swells of gradual shifting as do all atmospheres.

There is no water here. On occasion walls release flow from orange fluids but nothing sufficient to change chemistry. Creatures — advancers using chloroplast, flora collectors and, rarely, traveling geruch — may release vapors hydrating environment with key matter. This dynamic prompts stark raucous colour that highlights other otherwise obscure shapes.

Hollow's plant life is dominant and vibrant. Possessing vibrant blue stalks, leaves almost look purple when dim light from exit stretches them. Roots nearly push to heart of excavation where fifty equally spread s lights are suspended, a method tasteful when home is renewed as being seems to briefly catch with rising tones.

There is nothing man made, no remnants or residue of previous civilizations. No constructs nor machines, this environment is untouched by the influence of human or natural technology.

A abundance of fauna inhabit this hollow. The air thick with  booms and chattering of the inhabitants — chirping of flying fauna, grunt of rooting food fauna, and the rustle of creatures shuffling throught the flora. The stark colors of the landscape serve to make some creatures nearly invisible, camoflauged by the brilliance of plant life, however the symphony of sounds reveal the true density of life occupying this space.

Despite its age, the landscape feels almost alien. Almost as if this area is not native to this world, but instead was placed here from another realm. Odd shapes and unexplainable light patterns give an aura of hostility even as life thrives in this strange forgotten hollow.
