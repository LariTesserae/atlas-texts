In the heart of the dense, jade underbrush, overshadowed by enormous mountains, lies a set of refined, climate-controlled factories. The undergrowth is kept trimmed back with rakes and smothering membranes. Air pumps draw oxygen from the surface, now a barren expanse. Carpet-like mosses develop along edges, eventually getting crushed flat by use. Noise from the factories is heavy, creaking low tones.

Early days were harder before the big storm partially flooded the hollow. Now drive systems are sealed, venting from stables to prevent flooding.

Once-productive agricultural regions above became mountains of sterile salt through over-farming. Rare animals like chemical-regenerating coated roaches and acid-digesting birds on five legs were pressured to the bonderos once fullvine regions died.

Earth proper moves as planets — clearer from a gondola car as dirt pulls the water out with it. Water from truth river under the bonderos manipulates activity by feeding cool-down flows of metal and priority resources.
