Below the surface, the water is deep, riddled with crevices and ravines, each carved with precision. No vegetation mars this cold perfection; only rocks, as polished as figurines, form landscapes both meta and micro. Engines thrum noiselessly, their outputs muted like a field recording with the pulse edited out. The light, softened, falls like cigarette smoke as far as each eye can see. Shapes cluster, join, break in perfect, stable indifference. There are no changes, aberrations, or metabolic lonelinesses, and any creature trying to live here will get the gull shot.
