In the midst of this landscape, there lies a city unlike any other. It is situated on the banks of a winding river that snakes through the valley, the water level fluctuating wildly depending on the time of day and week. The river itself cuts through a landscape of twisted stone and exposed geology, like a teenager's botched experiment with clay. Occasional rock faces are exposed, water-worn, and razor-sharp, alongside expanses of twisted, eroded pines that writhe and twist, curled atop rocks like knotted fingers.
