Deep beneath the surface of a vast, dark ocean lies the sprawling metropolis of Aquaria. The city is a marvel of beyond-modern technology, featuring massive, self-sustaining domes that maintain stability, besides housing a dense and vibrant urban landscape. The city's foundation rests on a recently formed, jagged terrain, the result of a sudden and catastrophic event that reshaped the seafloor. The buildings, structures of breathtaking complexity, are a testament to the ingenuity and determination of the city's inhabitants. Despite the harsh, alien environment, Aquaria hums with life – not in the form of fauna, but in the ceaseless activity of its machinery, the thrum of circulation systems, and the soft, blue-green glow of luminescent panels that illuminate the dark expanse.

The atmosphere within the domes is controlled, temperatures maintained at a cool 10°C. Rain falls in cycles, collected from the atmospheric water and recycled to create a sense of familiarity and comfort for the inhabitants. Vegetation is sparse, mostly occurring in community greenhouses where residents cultivate their food using advanced hydroponics and aeroponics.&#x20;

Aquaria is a place of wonder and discovery, where scientists and engineers work tirelessly to unravel the mysteries of the deep. Its soundscape is filled with the constant murmur of technology, punctuated by the occasional, measured alerts from the city's AI, CHIRON. Despite its seemingly inhospitable location, Aquaria is a beacon of hope for those seeking to explore and understand the unknown. It stands as a testament to human ingenuity and the unquenchable thirst for discovery that drives the species.
