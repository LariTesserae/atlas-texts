This landscape exists in the damp, cool quiet of a long-abandoned underground city. Water seeps from rocks and gathers in shallow pools. The air is heavy with the scent of mold and decay. The sound of dripping water echoes through the space. Light filters down from above in dim shafts. The city is a tangle of broken machinery and worn stone. Every structure, every surface, every detail has been eroded by time and damp into rounded shapes and roughened textures. There are rail lines, but no trains. There is pipework, but no flow. Crumbling buildings stand so still they could be solid stone all the way through.
