A gloomy cavern emerges, a thousand meters below the surface. Infrequent dripping echoes through the space as seep water condenses on rough, granular walls. The air is stale and warm, with a faint moldy smell. A few sparse fungal growths flank areas where moisture is more pronounced.

The high density of the rock gives the cavern a heavy, oppressive feeling. However, the darkness amplifies every sound, making the faint drips sound like loud splashes. A few ancient roots from long-dead trees hang like skeletal fingers through cracks in the ceiling.

No signs of fauna aside from distant scurrying footsteps that are quickly lost in the overwhelming silence. Stillness characterizes this place, where the only changes are the gradual growth of the fungi and the slow seepage of water.

Some spots show indications of human- or tool-facilitated modifications - partially carved walls or roughly chiseled stone pedestals. However, these appear ancient and worn, reflecting a long-abandoned presence. The rest is raw geology, rough and untouched.
