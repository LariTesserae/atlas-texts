The world operates on slow, gradual cycles. The shift from day to night on the surface barely affects the inhabitants and plants in this underground universe. They have evolved to thrive under conditions that remain relatively constant year-round. Change is gradual - over long durations. The overall ecosystem requires patience and stability, ensuring that any fluctuation in one part of the web of life doesn't have devastating effects elsewhere.
