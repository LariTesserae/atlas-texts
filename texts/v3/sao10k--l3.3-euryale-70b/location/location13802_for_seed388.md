We find ourselves in a compact, canal-woven town — its buildings crowded together on 'islands' between narrow, dark waterways. Worn, humid brick facades crowd over the canals, which run with a drizzle of oily water. Water is everywhere, regularly seeping from stone and masonry into the canals, and sometimes the town's lower paths are softly awash in shallows of warm, chemically-textured, quiet, black water.

The town is installed into the low, defaced hills of an extensively strip-mined area. From any one part of town you can see the step-like contours of spoil tips left behind by the mining industry.

The density of settlement has made this an industrial place, home to factories, power generation, and dense networks of electrical distribution. While the town is well-lit within the scale of a single building, the light expertly stays within its own area. Because of this, the place feels shrouded in darkness when you look out at anything large-scale.

Small spots of lush vegetation grow in out of gutter-traps, underleaks, and small places in walls and pavement where soil has accumulated for long enough to give them a hold.

Several different sounds are in audible contention at all times, building up into the generally loud soundscape.
