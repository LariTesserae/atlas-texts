This landscape is high-altitude — nearly alpine — and densely vegetated with giant ferns, Horsetails, palm trees, and broadleaf species. The air is thick with the sounds of insects, birds, and larger animals. The heat and density of these life-forms have created a misty air that swallows and reveals great jungle trunks in layers like sketches of themselves. Despite the cramped space near the base of the trees, there are many small airborne animals and insects that flutter through the shed fronds of the giant palms.
