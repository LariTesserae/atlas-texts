Nestled in the heart of a vast, arid mountain range lies the town of Arkas, its elevation making it a haven from the sweltering heat below. The temperature hangs at a cool, crisp level, creating a perfect climate for the residents who call this place home. Built on a plateau, Arkas is a marvel of pre-metal era engineering, with stone walls, intricately carved, that serve as both protection and boundary.

Vegetation, though sparse, comes in the form of hardy species that have adapted to the arid conditions, finding pockets of damp soil and shielded spots to flourish in. Clouds that drift through the mountain range occasionally bless the town with sparse rain showers, replenishing the wellspring at the town's center and moistening the soil just enough for the hardy flora to cling to life.

Despite its high elevation, echoes of larger landscapes beyond Arkas can be imagined: towering cliffs, carved by time and weather, loom in the distance, their peaks hiding in the clouds. The ground beneath is worn smooth, a testament to the ages of weather and human tread.

The town itself is dense with history and activity. Lanes, paved in stone, wander through the interconnected buildings of rough-hewn rock. Roofs are flat and covered in patchwork quilts of dried mud and pebbles, creating a mosaic effect. Wellsprings, homes, shops, and shared spaces all exist in a tightly knit fabric, showing how much value is placed on each container of precious water.

The tech is that of early tool users, with metal still a wonder on the brink of invention. Wood and stone tools are on full display in the hands of craftsmen expertly fashioning items of every need.

Despite the town's bustling activity, fauna is surprisingly rare, as if the town exists in a delicate balance against the harsher conditions surrounding it.

Though quiet, the sounds within Arkas can be enriching - hammer striking, chatter, the occasional call from atop the watchtowers guarding the town, emphasizing its strong organizational base and communal spirit. Arkas, both stable and vibrant, has created an entirely standardized life, devoid of any corrosive inclinations towards advancement past its sturdy, steady state.
