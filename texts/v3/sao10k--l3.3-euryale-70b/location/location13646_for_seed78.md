\*\*The High Algal Steppes\*\*: A frigid, windswept expanse of icy tundra punctuated by bursts of vibrant, hardy vegetation. Weather patterns are unpredictable and harsh, with temperatures fluctuating wildly.

The landscape stretches as far as the eye can see, a seemingly endless, rolling plain of ice and algal growth that appears almost three-dimensional in its complexity. Here and there, skeletal remains of long-abandoned woodframe buildings jut upward, remains of humanity's earlier, more optimistic attempts to settle this unforgiving environment.

Fauna abounds: massive, lumbering cryo-beasts; giant, iridescent arachnid creatures; and swarms of bioluminescent, plankton-sized scavengers all call this unforgiving, beautiful place home. Sound is constant — the wind whipping through ice caverns, the staccato beat of cryo-beast footsteps, and the cacophonous, tinnitus-like hum of arachnid vocalizations all blend to create a rich, ever-changing audio landscape.

Despite the appearance of desolation, all around evidence of recent water presence can be seen: slick, glassy ice atop bedrock, flash-flood channels chewed into the terrain over what look to have been only days, occasional small sapphire springing from where the ice yields.

These elements are grafted onto a planet that, seen from orbit, shows Venus-dense cloud cover; surface observations are almost impossible via normal means.
