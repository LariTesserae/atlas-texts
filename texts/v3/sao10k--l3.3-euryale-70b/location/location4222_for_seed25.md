The air is cool and damp, heavy with the scent of something akin to wet stone, yet it carries the faintest hint of unknown minerals - powdery and metallic. It's as if this place has never seen the warmth of the sun, locked in a cool shadow of some sort. Intermittently, a light rain falls in non-patterned droplets, more like an errant thought the world forgot about, tracing the metallic tang through the air. The water doesn't run; it simply pools in seamless depressions on the stone underfoot before slowly being absorbed, leaving no hint of its passing.
