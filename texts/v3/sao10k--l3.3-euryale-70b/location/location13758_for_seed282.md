This hidden pocket universe doesn't pull punches – the first thing that strikes you is the discordant concoction of cold, humidity, and steel. You find yourself in a massive underground industrial factory that appears to have been abandoned for decades. The air inside is damp enough to render the metal infrastructure rusted and heavily corroded in most places; rickety catwalks and conveyors stretch across the void like metallic vines.

Vegetation is virtually non-existent in this forsaken place – only a few diseased-looking yellow weeds can be seen sprouting from cracked concrete or the outer crust of harder machinery that hasn't budged with the slow telluric bulges elsewhere. Despite this, the dim glow knocks off scraps of old equipment and creates bare passageways fire-engine red from huge, plywood-red static illumination fasades clinging to sagging rust.
