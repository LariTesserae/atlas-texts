This is a desolate, vast expanse of ice stretching as far as the eye can see, with massive icebergs floating in the distance. The Antarctic winds howl across the landscape at incredible velocities, causing the ice to vibrate and creating an otherworldly, constant drone. The sky is a deep, foreboding grey and the air is bitterly cold. Dotting the landscape are abandoned research stations and equipment, remnants of a long-forgotten expedition to study the unique phenomena of this place. Wildlife is scarce but present, mostly in the form of hardy penguins and seals that seem to thrive in this unforgiving environment.
