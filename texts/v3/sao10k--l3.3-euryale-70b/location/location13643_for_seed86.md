In this place, the damp air clings to the ground, a result of intermittent rivulets of water that cross the area. Some of these ephemeral rivers are "dry" much of the time, but they flood briefly after rains.

The terrain is a systems system of ridges and articuli carved by outflow driven by the water. The arms end at broad floodplains which are, in turn, casualties of even more extensive flows exiting the region.

The area is a major crossing point for herding animals, explained by southwestern animals in fall and winter and northeastern ones in spring and summer.

Both grazing and travel push out all but sparse vegetation.
