In the dimly lit, largely submerged environment, a small, packed city thrives. The city's single feature fills its area with different levels of construction, forming layers that give depth and diversity to the saturated streets. Interconnected buildings and arches tower over narrow, winding roads. The structure is largely industrial in nature, driven by machinery and electrified systems. These produce gentle, omnipresent hums that join the sounds of waves above and the lapping of water against the walls, creating a unique city orchestra. Occasionally, a figure walks by, breaking the silence with the soft splash of footsteps. They move almost aimlessly, with no clear purpose, giving the impression of a slow, gradual place.

Vegetation is mostly confined to small, artificial gardens, where lush, hardy plants provide a splash of color against the industrial backdrop. However, the density of the city allows little room for anything but these sparse gardens, making the city feel in-balance with its environment without feeling overly natural.

A theme observed in the use of available light: dark strips of buildings consist of rarely used apartments while every bit of electric additions and light concentrators can be seen in and around the marketplaces.
