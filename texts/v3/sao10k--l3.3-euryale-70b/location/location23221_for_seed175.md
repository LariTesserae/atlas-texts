In this landscape, there's an otherworldly sense, a feeling of being in a dream or a place where time doesn't quite move the same way as everywhere else. The sky is a pale, washed-out blue, and there's a misty haze right above the ground. This haze is made of fine water droplets suspended in the air. It's like a thick fog, but it stays low and moves slowly, curling snake-like over the ground.

This place is far from everywhere else. You have to climb steep hills and make your way over rocky crossings. The elevation here is high, even though the peaks around it are even higher. The air is warm and humid, with a hint of the earthy smell from the wet rock.

The ground has soft clothes strewn over it. The clothes are sodden and soggy. Some of the clothes have torn open and let bright intestines flow out. When the clothes move, their soft ooze floods outward.
