In this realm, the terrain stretches out as a vast, arid expanse of salt-encrusted dunes that have reached an equilibrium, their shapes unchanging over time. The cool air clings close to the ground, heavy with the weight of time, and there is an eerie absence of water, not a drop in sight.&#x20;

Sparse vegetation thrives in spots, with stunted trees standing like sentinels, their bark thick and rust-red.&#x20;

Towering mountain ranges pierce the sky, some still climbing high into the clouds, their peaks capped in snow, while others have been worn down to nothing more than vast plateaus or scattered boulders.

In some areas, structures dot the landscape, ancient adobe buildings or simple abodes, with nothing to suggest a larger settlement but rather the occasional dwelling or the remains of what used to be more grand.&#x20;

Tools lie scattered about, forgotten or left behind, from agricultural implements to what could be early-day medical tools. Human and animal remains pepper the landscape, leaving the air heavy with the weight of what once lived.

The sunlight is bright and the colour of sandy-blond hair, each beam illuminating a new highlight of the eroding landscape slowly being weathered away. In the thin air, the animals are persistently, unwaveringly, stuck in time - each one independently not moving as if time never had any effect upon them.

A kind of quietness settles and is now a seeming fact of nature as nothing occurs in the world we find ourselves within.
