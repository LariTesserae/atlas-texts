The landscape is a sprawling, high-altitude ecosystem, cloaked in an ever-present mist. The terrain is hilly, with gentle slopes and broad vistas. Water trickles through this landscape in narrow, ephemeral streams that appear and disappear as you move. The ground beneath your feet is damp and spongy.

The landscape is comprised almost entirely of living, botanical mass. Towering tree-like organisms provide structural elements, with broad trunks, extensive root systems, and verdant canopies. Vines and epiphytes cling to these structures, adding layers of greenery. Everything is covered in a soft, granular soil that supports a dizzying variety of micro-organisms.

The temperature is cold, with frequent frosts and a biting wind. But even so, the trees and vines thrive, pumping moisture from the ground to the air, generating heat and life. This ecosystem has been around for a long time, but its formation is not describable as 'settled.'

The landscape is full of movement, with whole sections of terrain updating their topography, plants squirming through the soil, pursuing opportunities.

The plants here are completely symbiotic. Whole organisms circulate nutrients and water through precisely managed tubes and channels. Individual plants do not actually compete or fight for resources; they all benefit as they change and distribute

There's something electric or industrious in the air, like the glow of a lantern, the hum of machinery or recent tracks from cool wheels.

It is rare to find sightings of non-botanical life in this ecosystem — no birds chirping, no squirrels playing, just the soft stirring of plant life.

This tightly-woven mass of plant life creates unexpected tunnel-vision paths, curves and pure visual chaos.

As you wander through this landscape, you are surrounded by a riot of colors and textures. Every step reveals a new pattern and arrangement. Despite its alien feel, this is a very familiar, almost archetypal landscape. The plants could be particular species; instead, they are woven elements — functional, emotional, terrific, etc.
