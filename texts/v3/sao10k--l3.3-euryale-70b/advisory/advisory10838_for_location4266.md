Preparing to visit this underground city would require careful planning considering its unique environment. Here's a comprehensive preparation guide:

1. **Clothing and Gear**:
   - Lightweight, durable, and breathable clothing suitable for high heat and variable humidity.
   - Comfortable, sturdy boots for walking and navigating possibly uneven terrain.
   - A bottle or hydration pack to ensure adequate hydration.
   - Snorkeling or scuba gear if significant water areas are anticipated.
   - Self-sustaining lighting sources; headlamps or portable lights to complement the available illumination.
   - Mining or caving helmets for safety in low-ceilinged areas.

2. **Environmental Adaptation**:
   - Acclimatization to high temperatures; hydrate well and consider altitude sickness medication if applicable.
   - Imagery training for navigating in low light and cramped spaces.
   - Psychological preparation for the sounds of machinery and wildlife, as well as confined spaces.

3. **Tech and Safety Equipment**:
   - Correspondence device or walkie-talkie for group communication.
   - Emergency supply kit containing water purification tablets, food, first aid supplies, matches/candle, and other survival tools.
   - Oxygen supply or portable mask if the atmosphere is dense or toxic.
   - Multi-tool or pocket knife for various tasks.

4. **Educational and Outdoor Skills**:
   - Study of basic first aid and emergency response procedures.
   - Environment-specific survival techniques; navigating in the dark and using available light sources efficiently.
   - Swimming or water survival skills if needed.

5. **Legal and Ethical Considerations**:
   - Obtain any required permits or permissions for travel and exploration.
   - Familiarize yourself with local laws, taboos, and environmental regulations.
   -Understand local culture and customs to minimize social conflicts.

6. **Fitness and Health**:
   - Ensure you are physically fit for prolonged periods of walking and possibly climbing.
   - Address any respiratory or claustrophobia issues with medication or therapy.
   - Keep up with vaccinations and general health checks.

7. **Navigational Aids**:
   - City map or guidebook if available.
   - Compass or GPS device (though functionality may vary underground).

8. **Communication and Diplomacy**:
   - Learn basic communication phrases if entering foreign territories.
   - Show respect and caution when interacting with the local wildlife and inhabitants.
