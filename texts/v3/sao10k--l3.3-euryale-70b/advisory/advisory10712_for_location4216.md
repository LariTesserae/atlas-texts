To adequately prepare for visiting such an extraordinary, alien-like underwater location, an ordinary human multiverse traveler would need to take several, extremely meticulous and technologically advanced steps. This preparation would not only ensure safety but also enhance the traveler's ability to observe, study, and interact with this unique environment. Here's a comprehensive guide:

### 1. Technology

- **Advanced Submersible or Exosuit:** The first and foremost requirement is a reliable, pressurized submersible or a state-of-the-art exosuit that can withstand the extreme conditions of the deep sea, especially the high pressure and warm temperatures near the vent. This vehicle or suit should also be equipped with robust life support systems capable of recycling air for a prolonged period and regulating temperature.

- **Enhanced Navigation and Communication:** A robust navigation system is essential, including advanced sonar for mapping the seafloor and GPS where applicable. Communication with the surface or a support team is also crucial and would require specialized underwater communication equipment.

- **Sampling Equipment:** To study the unique fauna and flora, the traveler would need sampling gear like dredges, nets, and underwater vacuum devices. For chemical analysis of vent emissions, gas and water samplers would be necessary.

### 2. Scientific Knowledge

- **Biological, Geographical, and Chemical Briefings:** Extensive knowledge of marine biology, geology, and chemistry would be essential. The traveler needs to understand the ecosystems they might encounter, the geological processes at play, and the potential chemical hazards (like sulfuric acid and heavy metals) present in the hydrothermal vent environment.

### 3. Physical Conditioning

- **Diving Certification and Experience:** Even with advanced technology, experience with deep diving and an understanding of the physiological risks of deep-sea environments is vital. Training in emergency procedures would also be indispensable.

- **Physical Fitness:** The travel and exploration could be physically demanding. The traveler would need to be in good physical condition, especially if they will be using a manual propulsion system or operating in a physically demanding suit.

### 4. Psychological Preparation

- **Isolation Training:** Since the journey could be isolating and the environment psychologically challenging, the traveler may need training to cope with isolation and the psychological impacts of such an alien environment.

- **Emergency Protocols:** The traveler should have comprehensive emergency procedures in place for a variety of scenarios, including equipment failure, health emergencies, and becoming lost. Knowing how to remain calm and think clearly in these situations is critical.

### 5. Technology Maintenance and Backup

- **Redundancy Systems:** Critical systems should have redundancies in case of failure. This includes life support, communication, and navigation.

- **Repair Kits and Tools:** The ability to make basic repairs to the submersible or suit could be invaluable. A knowledge of how to use these tools efficiently is also necessary.

### Preparation Timeline

Preparation for such a journey would likely span several months to a few years, depending on the traveler's current level of readiness. This would include intensive study, training, and possibly a series of shorter, preparatory dives in similar but less extreme environments.

### Conclusion

Visiting a newly formed underwater volcanic rock pinnacle with its unique, primordial ecosystem and extreme environmental conditions would be an unprecedented adventure for a human multiverse traveler. It would offer insights into the origins of life on Earth and the possibility of life elsewhere in the universe. However, the extreme dangers and technological challenges mean that such a journey would push the boundaries of human exploration and endurance.
