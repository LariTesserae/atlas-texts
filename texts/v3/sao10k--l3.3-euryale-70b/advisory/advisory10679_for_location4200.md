Ensure you are in excellent physical health, as the unique environment within the glacier city may present challenges. Environmental Preparation:

The dampness and smell of electric power may affect your respiratory system. Consider bringing a small air purifier or respiratory protection if you have sensitivities.

Logistical Preparation:
