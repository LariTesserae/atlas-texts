As I immerse myself in this mystical world, I find myself taking the form of an euMiNA, one of the giant, slow-moving beasts that roam the misty landscape. My body while seemingly cumbersome, is well-suited to traversing this world. My long snout allows me to filter feed on the rich plant life that blankets everything. My thick, armored hide protects me from accidental collisions and my slow metabolism means I can go for long periods without sustenance.

I am at once a part of this world and a remover of its obstruction. As I make my way through the misty expanse, I leave behind trails of broken and crushed plant growth which immediately become fodder for smaller, faster creatures to feast upon. As I pass by the ruins of the Helix, I feel a sense of indifference - a curiosity as to what purpose the smooth curvatures and ridges once served - a reminder to myself that my existence will have the same end. Despite the initial impression of stillness, the landscape teems with life. Towering, slow-moving euMiNA roam the misty expanse, their footsteps causing faint tremors in the ground. Smaller creatures dart between the roots of the ancient tree-forms, their calls echoing softly through the mist.
