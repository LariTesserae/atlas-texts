The Sugar Studies continent is depicted as a harsh industrial landscape where a shallow layer of bitterly cold water covers the entire surface. Distributed generation equipment from a bygone era is scattered throughout, with some parts submerged and emitting steam due to their high heat settings. The atmosphere is filled with harmful sulfur waste, a dangerous surface environment that could be calamitous if lightning were to occur.

Despite the extreme conditions, signs of past agricultural attempts are visible. Crops such as paramelon were cultivated but ultimately unsuccessful, and makeshift shelters intermingle with industrial waste. Traveling through this environment is dangerous due to the remnants of technology and altered landscapes.

Life may still exist in this environment, with aquatic organisms contributing to a relentless struggle to survive. Some life forms may have mutated to blend into their rusting metallic surroundings.

Effective travel and exploration in this region would require waterproof robots, resilient to the harsh elements and built to maintain what remains of civilization. They would navigate unseen, adhering to a refined lifecycle.
