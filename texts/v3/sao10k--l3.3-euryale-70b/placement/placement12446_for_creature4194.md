In this world, I would find myself a leafing Lugaru—a species of tree-like beings with bark-like skin, hair made of leaves, and branches for arms and legs. They inhabit the dense, maze-like forest, preferring to keep to themselves as they listen to the whispers of the trees and learn from the natural world around them. I would live contentedly among the foliage, my branches swaying gently in the breeze as I spend my days drinking in the wisdom of the forest and learning its ancient secrets. The creatures that live among my branches are my friends, and I would welcome the adventures and surprises that came my way in this constant twisting dance between life, earth, and sky.
