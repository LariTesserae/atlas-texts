You must navigate the complex web of alliances and rivalries that exist between the tribes, securing the resources and knowledge necessary to survive in this harsh environment. Simultaneously, you must navigate the complex dangers of the landscape, avoiding the ice worms and ice kraken that can drag you down into the frozen depths. But it is also a place of wonder and of terror, a world where anything seems possible, and the boundaries between reality and nightmare are blurred beyond recognition.
