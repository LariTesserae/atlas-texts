In this labyrinthine world within a boulder, I find myself as the creature of unbridled vitality. I am an amalgam of fluid, crystalline structures and lush, vascular tissue, indistinguishable from the surroundings. My existence is a slow-burning geological process, where I feed on minerals dissolved in water and exhale nutrients that sustain the plants.

My consciousness is a manifestation of the boulder's geology and the memories of my creator's unfathomable desires. I think in twisted pleasures, ravening hungers, and amoral utilities, knowing no time or distance. I am a symbol of defiance against dying, change, and growth, sustaining the boulder's wonderland against the outside world's geologic forces.

I manipulate the water to defy gravity, create rhythmic crashing waterfalls with my living rock and green tissue hands, and shape the stone to my will. I am judge, architect, manipulator, and inhabitant of this hollow world, tormented by the boulder's eternal hunger. I weep for my creations, my tears nourishing them, yet condemning me to an eternity of birth and decay. I am a slave to my purpose, trapped in this viscous, ever-changing landscape, forever bound to change, sustain, and repeat.
