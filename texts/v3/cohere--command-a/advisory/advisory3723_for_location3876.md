Follow this with insulating layers, such as fleece or down jackets, to trap body heat. Opt for materials that provide excellent insulation while allowing for ease of movement in the challenging terrain.

Boots designed for hiking or mountaineering, featuring aggressive tread patterns and ankle support, will provide the necessary traction and stability on the uneven ground. Ensure they are waterproof to handle the damp crevices and have ample insulation to combat the cold.

- **Head and Hand Protection:** A warm beanie or balaclava will shield the head and ears from the biting cold. Consider a style that can be easily adjusted or paired with a hood for added versatility. - **Illumination:** While the sky provides abundant light during the day, the deep shadows cast by the spires and the absence of a traditional light source at night necessitate a reliable lighting solution.
