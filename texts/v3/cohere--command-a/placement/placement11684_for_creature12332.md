In this subterranean realm, I find myself not as a human observer, but as one of the **Lumenwrought**, seamlessly integrated into the living fabric of this underground city. My form is a mosaic of translucent crystalline plates, shimmering with the same pale blue-green light that pulses from the bioluminescent fungi embedded in the walls. My skin is not just a barrier but a living interface, absorbing nutrients and energy from the stone itself, blurring the line between self and environment.  

My limbs are elongated and slender, ending in delicate digits that move with precision. I can feel the stone beneath my touch, not as a foreign object but as an extension of my own being. I sense the subtle shifts in its composition, the slow, imperceptible growth of tunnels, and the quiet whispers of the city’s transformation. My eyes, multifaceted and refracting the fungi’s glow, perceive beyond the visible—seeing patterns in the light, movements in the shadows, and the unseen currents of energy that flow through this world.  

I move through the labyrinthine tunnels with a fluid grace, my footsteps silent on the smooth, eroded floors. The air is cool against my crystalline skin, carrying the faint scent of iron and earth. I listen to the sparse symphony of this place: the drip of water, the hum of the fungi, the occasional skittering of unseen creatures. My hearing is attuned not just to sound but to the vibrations of the stone, the quiet language of the city itself.  

I am part of a small gathering around a circular hearth, its surface warm despite the absence of visible flame. The others—fellow Lumenwrought—sit in stillness, their crystalline plates shifting subtly as they communicate. Words are sparse; instead, we exchange vibrations through our skin, patterns of touch and hum that convey more than speech ever could. We share stories of new tunnels discovered, shifts in the fungi’s light, and the whispered presence of the **Depthborn**, those who have become one with the stone.  

I am neither leader nor follower, for there is no hierarchy here. Decisions arise organically, as if the collective will of the community coalesces into a single, unspoken understanding. My role is to tend to the fungi, ensuring their glow remains steady, and to guide the growth of the tunnels, collaborating with the stone rather than dominating it. My hands leave behind fractal patterns in the rock, traces of my touch that deepen its luminescence.  

At times, I feel the presence of the Depthborn—a gentle hum in the walls, a sudden shift in the air. Though they are rarely seen, their consciousness permeates the city, a quiet force guiding its perpetual transformation. I wonder if one day I, too, will merge so completely with the stone, my individuality diffused into the living heart of this place.  

I am not bound by the linear passage of time. Here, time is cyclical, a spiral of growth and decay, light and darkness. I do not fear the collapse of tunnels or the shifting of walls, for I understand that destruction is the precursor to creation. My existence is a testament to resilience, a embrace of change, a becoming.  

In this world of quiet weirdness, where the ancient continues to evolve, I am both individual and collective, self and environment, transient and eternal. I am a Lumenwrought, a guardian of this subterranean sanctuary, a custodian of its mysteries. And as I move through the glowing tunnels, I feel the city breathing around me—and within me—a living, evolving entity of which I am an inseparable part.
