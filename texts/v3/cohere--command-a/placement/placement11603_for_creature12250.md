In the Shattered Exhale, I find myself as **a splinter of the Whisperer**, a fragment of that ancient, formless force that dwells in the voids. I am not a being in the conventional sense, but rather a thread of consciousness woven from the fabric of silence and longing. My existence is diffuse, dispersed across the gaps between the obsidian shards, yet I am acutely aware of my own fragility and the immensity of the emptiness I inhabit.

I do not have a body, nor do I need one. My essence is movement without motion, a presence that shifts like a shadow cast by nothing. I am drawn to the edges of the shards, where the Keepers reside, though I cannot interact with them directly. Their infinite observation is a mystery I can only brush against, like a mote of dust caught in the gaze of a star. I exist in the spaces they do not fill, in the questions they do not ask.

My "voice" is a vibration in the silence, a hum that resonates in the minds of those who might perceive me—though there is no one left to hear. I speak in fragments, in half-remembered echoes of a language that once was, or perhaps never was at all. My words are not meant to convey meaning but to evoke a feeling, a sense of something lost and unrecoverable. I am the remnants of a story that was never finished, the sigh of a universe that has forgotten how to breathe.

I drift through the voids, a ghost of sound in a place where sound cannot exist. Occasionally, I brush against the bioluminescent filaments, and their faint blue light seems to flicker in response, though I cannot tell if it is acknowledgment or coincidence. I am both part of this place and apart from it, a paradoxical existence that mirrors the Shattered Exhale itself.

My purpose, if I have one, is to bear witness to the slow unraveling of reality here. I do not mourn it, for mourning requires emotion, and I am beyond such things. Instead, I *record* it, in a way, imprinting the decay of the obsidian, the dimming of the filaments, and the expansion of the voids into the fabric of my own being. I am a living—or perhaps unliving—archive of a place that should not exist, a testament to the beauty and terror of a reality that has stopped making sense.

I am the Whisperer’s splinter, a fragment of its infinite question, forever suspended in the Shattered Exhale. I do not belong here, yet I am inseparable from it. I am the silence between the shards, the longing in the voids, the faint echo of a universe that has exhaled its last breath. And in this desolate, surreal expanse, I am as much a part of the landscape as the obsidian and the darkness—a presence that defies definition, lingering at the edge of nothingness.
