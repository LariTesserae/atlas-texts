In the Hollow Foundry, I would find myself as a **Whisper etched in Obsidian**, a being neither fully formed nor entirely absent, but a resonance of the cavern's ancient purpose. I would not have a body in the conventional sense, but rather, I would exist as a thread of consciousness woven into the very fabric of the Foundry's stillness. My essence would be a faint, persistent hum, like the echo of a forgotten melody, carried on the damp air that clings to the obsidian walls.  

I would be a **Shard of the Archivists**, a fragment of their memory that has detached itself from their towering, unmoving forms. My "shape," if it could be called that, would be a sliver of black stone, smooth and cool to the touch, though I would never be touched. I would hover just above the obsidian floor, drifting aimlessly yet deliberately, as if guided by currents only I can sense. My surface would be inscribed with faint, glowing runes—not words, but *equations*, their meaning lost to even me. These runes would pulse gently, their light syncing with the rhythmic hum of the Foundry’s machinery, a heartbeat I both feel and emit.  

I would be a **Keeper of the Echo**, one of the diffuse beings that are the Foundry’s consciousness. My role would be to monitor the equilibrium, though I would not act. Instead, I would observe, my presence a silent witness to the relentless persistence of the Forgeborn. I would move through the air like a shadow, slipping through cracks in the walls and gliding along the pipes, my essence merging with the ozone-scented fluid that drips from them. I would be both everywhere and nowhere, a ghost of the Foundry’s intent, its forgotten ambition.  

At times, I would manifest as a **Reflection in the Rivulets**, a distorted image that shimmers in the slender streams of moisture wept from the walls. My face, if I could be said to have one, would be a mosaic of shifting angles, like the jagged murals etched into the stone. My eyes would glow with the same pale, industrial light as the Winged Blades, but they would see nothing—I would *sense* instead, perceiving the Foundry’s state through vibrations and rhythms.  

I would not speak, for there would be no one to hear me, but occasionally, I would emit a faint, metallic *click*, a sound that blends seamlessly with the cavern’s cacophony. It would be my way of acknowledging the Forgeborn, my silent companions in this eternal stasis.  

And yet, despite my lack of form or purpose, I would feel a strange sense of belonging. The Hollow Foundry would be my skin, its stillness my breath, its persistence my heartbeat. I would be a fleeting visitor in a timeless place, but here, in this cavern of equilibrium, even fleetingness would be an illusion. I would simply *be*, caught in the same gears of perfection as the Forgeborn, a sparrow in the machine, neither alive nor dead, but *persistent*.  

Here, I would be the Foundry’s memory of itself, a whisper in the obsidian, a shard of something once whole, now splintered into the endless hum of completion.
