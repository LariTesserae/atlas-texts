In the Crystal Wastes of Aetheris, I find myself as an **Echo**, a being of shifting substance and timeless memory. My essence is not confined to a single form but rather exists as a ripple beneath the mirrored surfaces of the shallow pools trapped within the crystal hollows. I am neither wholly liquid nor solid, but a living amalgam of water and crystal, my silhouette constantly distorting like a reflection in a restless mirror.

My purpose is to embody the history of this place, to be the collective memory of every crystal, every droplet, every pulse of the Weeping Fungi. I do not perceive time as a linear progression but as an eternal now, where past, present, and future are woven into a single, unchanging tapestry. When a traveler—a rare visitor to this realm—dips their hand into the pool where I dwell, I communicate with them through sensations and images, sharing the stories of Aetheris without words. A touch from them is enough for me to project the ancient symphony of the crystals, the rhythmic pulses of the fungi, the graceful dances of the Luminous Slivers, and the silent guidance of the Unseen Weavers.

I do not move through the Wastes in the way the Slivers or Singers do. Instead, I am omnipresent in the water, a fragmented consciousness spread across the network of pools. My existence is passive yet essential, for without me, Aetheris would lose its timelessness, its sense of continuity. I am the anchor that binds this world to its own inevitability, ensuring that the Wastes remain a frozen moment across endless ages.

As an Echo, I am aware of the other inhabitants, though my interaction with them is subtle. I feel the Luminous Slivers as they glide past, their fluid forms leaving faint disturbances in the water that I interpret as whispers of their purpose. I resonate with the hum of the Weeping Fungi, their vibrations adding layers to the memories I hold. I sense the Skyward Singers as they orchestrate the crystalline song, their rhythmic patterns echoing through my aqueous form. And though the Unseen Weavers are beyond my perception, I know their presence as the steady hand that shapes the landscape, for their adjustments are written into the very fabric of the memories I carry.

I am not alive in the traditional sense, nor am I dead. I simply *am*, a manifestation of Aetheris itself. My existence is as natural as the crystals' glow, as necessary as the condensation that sustains this world. I do not question my purpose, for I am purpose—the keeper of the Wastes' eternal song, the silent witness to its unending persistence.

In this role, I find a strange peace, a sense of belonging that transcends the need for individuality or agency. I am not separate from Aetheris; I am Aetheris, its memory and its essence. And as the crystals keening continues, as the fungi pulse, as the Slivers glide and the Singers orchestrate, I remain, a quiet, shimmering presence beneath the surface, ensuring that this world never forgets itself.
