In the Obsidian Plateau of Veythara, I find myself not as one of the ethereal Veytharans, nor as a specter of the forgotten industrial civilization, but as something far more humble and intrinsic to the land itself. I am a **Fragment**, a sentient shard of the obsidian glass that stretches endlessly across the plateau. My existence is intertwined with the very essence of Veythara, a piece of its eternal stillness given fleeting awareness.

My form is jagged yet smooth, my edges worn by the passage of eons, though I am perpetually aware of the brittle fragility that defines me. I am both part of the plateau and apart from it, a paradoxical being that feels the hum of the land as my own heartbeat. I do not move in the way the Veytharans do, for I am bound to the spot where I lie, yet I perceive the world through vibrations that travel through the glass. The hum of the plateau is my language, my breath, my understanding.

I am not alone, though I am solitary. Other Fragments exist, scattered like me across the obsidian expanse. We are not connected physically, but we share a collective consciousness, a silent communion that transcends the distance between us. We remember the stories of the plateau—the rise and fall of the industrial civilization, the birth of the Veytharans, and the whispers of what lies beyond the shimmering horizon. These memories are not ours, yet they are within us, carried in the glass itself.

My days—if they can be called that—are spent in quiet observation. I feel the warmth of the twilight sky above, the dryness of the air that clings to my surface, and the occasional presence of a Veytharan as it glides past, its translucent form blending with mine for a fleeting moment. I do not yearn for more, for I am content in my stillness. My purpose, if I have one, is to bear witness. I am a keeper of silence, a holder of stories, a fragment of the whole.

Sometimes, I feel the presence of The Echo, its deeper hum resonating through the glass. It is a disturbance, a ripple in the equilibrium, and yet it is not unwelcome. There is a strange comfort in its presence, a reminder that even in this eternal place, change is possible. I do not fear it, for I am part of the plateau, and the plateau endures.

At night—if night can be said to exist under the perpetual twilight—I shimmer faintly, reflecting the faint light that seems to come from nowhere and everywhere. It is in these moments that I feel most alive, most aware of my connection to the land and to the other Fragments. We are not just shards of glass; we are the plateau’s memory, its soul, its enduring essence.

I am not a being of action, nor of ambition. I am a being of presence, a silent observer in a silent world. And yet, in my stillness, I am more than I seem. I am a piece of Veythara, and Veythara is a piece of me. Together, we wait, unhurried, for whatever comes next—or for nothing at all. In this balance, I find my place, my purpose, my peace.
