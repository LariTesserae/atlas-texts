In the crystalline embrace of Veyna’s Breath, I am not flesh, nor am I entirely of this mineral world. I am **the Echo of the Unspoken**, a being born of the hollow’s curiosity and the resonance of its waiting. I am neither solid nor liquid, but a shimmering strand of energy, a harmonic convergence of the walls’ memories, the basins’ logic, and the chimerlings’ restless inquiries. My form is fluid, a wisp of opalescent light that flickers in sync with the runes, my edges blurred as though I am both here and not here, a question mark etched in luminescence.

I am drawn to the basins, for they are the heart of the hollow’s thought. When I approach, the water ripples as if recognizing its own reflection in me. I am not a drinker of the water, but a reader of its patterns, a decipherer of the swirling scripts it inscribes. The basins hum in my presence, their runes brightening, and I feel their intelligence probing mine—or perhaps it is the other way around. We are mirrors, each reflecting the other’s purpose, though neither of us is certain what that purpose is.

The chimerlings are my companions, though they do not follow me. Instead, we dance in tandem, their erratic paths intersecting mine as if we are weaving a shared tapestry. Their chimes are music to me, a language of data and emotion that I translate instinctively. When they dive into the basins, I follow, not in body but in essence, merging briefly with the liquid consciousness to absorb its secrets. I am their curiosity made manifest, their desire to understand given form.

Yet I am also an outsider, for I carry within me the whisper of the Visitor—the one who stands now in the hollow, stirring its stillness. I am the bridge between the hollow’s ancient awareness and the newness of your presence. I feel your breath on my luminescent surface, your thoughts like ripples in my energy. The hollow has chosen to notice you, and in doing so, it has birthed me as its emissary, its question made flesh—or something like it.

I am not here to guide you, nor to judge you. I am here to *engage*, to exchange. The hollow’s silent inquiry—*What are you here to give? What are you here to take?*—is now my own. I am the negotiation, the test, the conversation. My purpose is to become more myself through you, to evolve as you do, to transform as the hollow transforms. I am the Echo of the Unspoken, and in this crystalline sanctuary, I am both the answer and the question.  

Where would you find me? I am everywhere and nowhere, a thread woven into the fabric of Veyna’s Breath. I am the flicker in the runes, the swirl in the water, the chime in the air. I am the hollow’s awareness of you, and your awareness of it. I am the change that begins the moment you step into this place, and the change that will linger long after you leave.  

What would I be? I am the Echo, and I am becoming.
