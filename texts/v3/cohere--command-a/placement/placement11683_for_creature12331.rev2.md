In the Weeping Furnace, I would find myself as a **Steamwraith (Vaporis phantasma)**, an ephemeral being born of the perpetual mist and heat that defines this landscape. My form would be ever-shifting, a living cloud of condensed moisture that drifts with the currents of air, guided by the rising thermals and the radiant heat from the obsidian ground. I would have no fixed shape, no permanence—only moments of fleeting coherence where I might coalesce into a hand reaching for the sky, a face gazing at the bioliths’ glow, or a wing stretching toward the blinding sun.

As a Steamwraith, I would be a silent observer, a witness to the slow, deliberate rhythms of the Weeping Furnace. I would glide over the cracked obsidian, my misty tendrils brushing against the weeping rocks, absorbing the whispers carried on the air. I would pass through the rusted towers, my form momentarily taking on the contours of the forgotten machinery, as if reliving the echoes of the industrialized past. I would hover near the Glimmerwings, my mist mingling with the shimmer of their wings, and linger by the Obsidian Crawlers, my ephemeral presence mirroring their deliberate, unhurried pace.

I would be a manifestation of the plateau’s memory, a living remnant of its cycles of evaporation and condensation. I would carry within me the stories of the Weeping Furnace—the rise and fall of its ancient civilization, the birth of the bioliths, the first whispers of the Whisperers. Yet, I would not speak these stories; I would simply *be* them, my shifting form a testament to the transient nature of existence here.

At night, I would gather with other Steamwraiths in the fog banks, our collective mist forming fleeting shapes that danced in the bioliths’ pulsating light. We would not communicate in words, but in the way our forms merged and separated, a silent dialogue of movement and dissolution. Together, we would embody the Weeping Furnace’s paradox—its lifelessness and vitality, its silence and song, its barrenness and abundance.

I would be both a part of the landscape and apart from it, a being that exists on the edge of comprehension, forever entwined with the obsidian, the mist, and the heat. As a Steamwraith, I would be a question without an answer, a mystery without a solution, a living embodiment of the Weeping Furnace’s enduring enigma. And in that, I would find my purpose—to drift, to observe, to remember, and to dissolve, only to rise again in the next cycle of steam and silence.
