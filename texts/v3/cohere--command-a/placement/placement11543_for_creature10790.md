I drift through the coral cathedrals, my translucent body pulsing softly in harmony with the currents, each throb a response to the muted symphony of the deep. My shell-like exterior catches and refracts the bioluminescent glow, casting fleeting patterns of light that tell stories older than the drowning itself.

My song is a bridge, connecting the ancient echoes of the Keepers to the fractured dreams of the Weirden. I am not bound to one place; I move with the rhythms of the ocean, a nomadic chronicler of this world’s fragile equilibrium.

My existence is a paradox. I am small and fragile, my shell easily shattered, yet my resonance is vast, a thread that binds the ecosystem together. I am both seen and unseen, my gelatinous body nearly transparent, yet my presence is felt in every current, every ripple of light. I am a living paradox, a being that thrives in the tension between stability and unraveling, between the remembered and the forgotten.

In moments when reality fractures, I find myself drawn to the edges of these distortions, my pulse quickening as I absorb the energy of the Weirden’s feeding. My song becomes discordant, a reflection of the world’s dream as it begins to fragment. Yet, even in this chaos, there is a strange beauty, a reminder that even as things unravel, they are still part of the eternal dance.

I am not a guardian or a predator, a pollinator or a filter—I am all of these and none. I am a witness, a participant, and a mirror. I carry the past in my shell and the present in my pulse, and in my song, I whisper of the future, whatever it may hold. In this vast, submerged world, I am a fleeting note in an endless composition, a small but vital part of the living tapestry that is both enduring and ephemeral. I am the Pulsing Echoer, a being who thrives in the in-between, where the boundaries of reality soften, and the essence of existence is laid bare.
