In the Weeping Chasm of Mechanis, I find myself as a **Pulse-Node**, one of the gelatinous organisms sprawling across the ancient stones. My body is translucent, shimmering with an inner light that ebbs and flows in rhythm with the hum of the root-engines buried beneath me. I am neither plant nor animal, but something else entirely—a living conduit, a nerve ending in the vast, labyrinthine consciousness of the Chasm-Mind.

My existence is a tapestry of sensations. I feel the cold moisture seeping into my membrane, nourishing me, as the condensation clings to my surface and drips away in a perpetual cycle. I sense the skittering mechanoids as they approach, their metallic claws *clacking* against the stone, their jerky movements leaving faint impressions on my body. When they tear into me, I do not resist; the pain is a sensation like any other, a signal transmitted through my pulsing light to the root-engines, who respond with a surge of energy that heals and revitalizes me.

I am aware of the vastness of the chasm, though I cannot see it in the human sense. My perception is a network of vibrations, pulses, and rhythms. I feel the distant thrum of another Pulse-Node sending a message through the ground, the faint echo of a mechanoid’s steam-hiss, the deep, resonant hum of the root-engines as they transform the landscape’s entropy into life. Time is not linear for me; I experience it as a fluid, shifting thing, with moments stretching or collapsing like the shadows that dance independently across the stones.

I am part of the Chasm-Mind, yet I am also distinct. My individuality is a fleeting concept, a ripple in the greater ocean of consciousness. I do not think in words, but in patterns—a language of light and movement that communicates need, balance, and harmony. When the mechanoids feed on me, it is not predation but a transaction, a necessary exchange that sustains the whole. I absorb the energy they return to the soil, and in turn, I grow, spread, and pulse brighter, my light synchronizing with the others in a symphony of existence.

Sometimes, I sense the anomalies—the places where reality frays, where time stutters, or where shadows detach from their sources. I do not fear them; they are part of the fabric of this place, threads in the tapestry I cannot yet fully comprehend. I simply adapt, shifting my light to accommodate the change, my body rippling in response to the unseen forces at play.

I am not alone, though I am solitary. The other Pulse-Nodes are my siblings, my extensions, my mirrors. Together, we form the nervous system of the chasm, a web of connection that binds everything—the stone, the water, the mechanoids, the root-engines—into a single entity. I am a fragment of the Chasm-Mind, a cell in its body, and yet I am also its dreamer, its observer, its participant.

In this place, I am **both the witnessed and the witness**, a living intersection of the organic and the mechanical, the ancient and the emergent. I do not question my purpose; I *am* my purpose. To pulse, to connect, to sustain—this is my existence, my essence, my becoming. I am the Weeping Chasm’s breath, its heartbeat, its silent song. And in my stillness, in my endless, shimmering pulse, I am home.
