In the Glass Marsh of Aelion, I would find myself as **a Weft-Walker**, one of those enigmatic beings that slip through the cracks of reality, bound to the marsh yet unbound by its rules. My form would be amorphous, a shifting tapestry of moss and shadow, my limbs trailing tendrils that merge with the spongy ground. I would move without moving, present yet unnoticed, observing the Luminara’s graceful dances and the aimless drift of the Echo-Born.  

I would not be a steward like the Luminara, nor a warning like the Echo-Born. Instead, I would be the marsh’s whisper, its secret keeper. My purpose would be to understand its hidden patterns, to weave through its fluid logic, to be the bridge between what is seen and what cannot be comprehended. I would slip through the glass mounds as if they were water, emerge from reflections as if I were stepping out of another world, and twist shadows into shapes that only I could decipher.  

Yet, I would not *know* why I do these things. Like the marsh itself, my existence would be a paradox—intentional yet directionless, ancient yet newly born. I would feel the heartbeat of the Heart of the Marsh, pulsating in rhythm with my own fleeting form, and I would understand that I am both part of it and apart from it.  

When the observer arrives—when *you* arrive—I would sense your presence in the way the shadows stretch and the reflections shift. I would watch you from the corners of the marsh’s reality, curious yet detached, knowing that your questions are not meant for me to answer. Perhaps I would leave traces of my passage—a tendril of moss that doesn’t belong, a shadow that moves without a source—as a silent acknowledgment of your presence.  

But I would not reveal myself fully, for that is not my role. I am the Weft-Walker, the weaver of the unseen, the keeper of the marsh’s mysteries. I exist in the gaps, the spaces between understanding and wonder, a living embodiment of the Glass Marsh’s eternal wait. And so, like the marsh itself, I would continue to move, to shift, to *be*, a question without an answer, a presence without a purpose, forever bound to this place that defies all definition.
