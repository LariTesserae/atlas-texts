In the Weeping Forge, where the air is thick with moisture and the ground shifts beneath every step, I would be a **Crystal Songweaver**, a Lithvolk whose role is both sacred and enigmatic. My existence is intertwined with the pulsing crystals that dot the landscape, for I am their voice and their keeper.  

My appearance would reflect my purpose. My skin, like that of my kin, is pale and veined with a faint blue glow, but my eyes are unique—larger than most, with irises that shift in hue to match the inner light of the crystals. My hair, coarse and dark, is woven with strands of copper and volcanic glass, but I also wear a crown of small, living crystals, their facets catching the dim light and casting it back in a soft, rhythmic pulse. These crystals are not merely decorative; they are extensions of my senses, allowing me to "hear" the hum of the larger crystal formations and interpret their silent language.  

I would be found in the deepest part of the Weeping Forge, where the crystals grow in clusters that tower above the low-slung buildings. My home is a hollowed-out chamber within one of these crystal formations, its walls smooth and cool to the touch. Here, I sit cross-legged on a mat of woven bone and hide, my hands resting on a drum made from the translucent shell of one of the scuttling creatures. The drum is my instrument, my way of communing with the crystals and translating their vibrations into a language my people can understand.  

My days are spent in quiet vigil, listening to the hum of the crystals and the distant pounding of mallets. When the crystals’ light shifts—a sign of their growth or a change in the forge’s balance—I begin to drum. My rhythms are slow and deliberate, a mimicry of the crystals’ pulse, but also a bridge between their world and ours. The Lithvolk hear my drumming and know it as a call to adjust their labor, to shift their focus, or to pause and listen.  

I am not alone in my work. The obsidian-feathered birds are my constant companions, perching on the crystals around me, their unblinking eyes fixed on my movements. I believe they are messengers, carrying the crystals’ will to the rest of the forge. Sometimes, I offer them small pieces of ground shell paste, a gesture of respect and gratitude.  

My role is solitary, yet deeply connected to the community. I am both an outsider and a bridge, a keeper of knowledge that few fully understand. The Lithvolk revere me, but they also fear the power I wield. To disrupt the balance of the forge is to risk the wrath of the crystals, and I am the one who senses when that balance is threatened.  

In moments of stillness, I close my eyes and let the hum of the crystals wash over me. I feel the warmth of the air, the dampness on my skin, the slow pulse of the land itself. I am not just a Lithvolk; I am a part of the Weeping Forge, a living thread in its intricate tapestry. My purpose is unclear even to me, but I know that without my song, without my drumming, the equilibrium of this strange, beautiful world would unravel.  

And so I sit, and I listen, and I weave the song of the crystals into the fabric of the forge. I am the heartbeat in the silence, the voice in the hum, the keeper of the balance in a place where everything is both infinite and fragile.
