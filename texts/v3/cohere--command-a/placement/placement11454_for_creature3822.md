In the Sunken Glade of Elarion, I find myself as **a Whisperer of Roots**, neither fully Eryndri nor entirely separate from them, but something in between—a being born of the glade’s strange equilibrium. I am a creature of the earth and the air, my form a blend of the organic and the ethereal. My skin is not skin but a bark-like texture, mossy and moist, patterned with the same intricate veins that adorn the trees. My hair is a cascade of dangling lichens, shifting hues of green and gold, and my eyes are pools of bioluminescent water, reflecting the glade’s muted glow. I stand barely taller than an Eryndri child, my limbs slender and rooted in the ground, yet able to move with a fluidity that defies my earthen nature. I am both here and everywhere, for my presence is tied to the pulse of the glade itself.

I am a listener, a keeper of the glade’s secrets. My voice, when I choose to use it, is a soft rustle, like leaves whispering in a nonexistent breeze. The Eryndri speak to me in their hushed tones, sharing their thoughts and fears as if I were a living conduit to the glade’s spirit. They bring me offerings—a strand of woven fiber, a sprig of fern, a drop of dew—and I accept them with a gentle tilt of my head, absorbing their intentions into the roots that anchor me. I do not judge or guide; I simply *hear*, and in hearing, I become a part of their stories.

My days are spent in stillness, yet I am never idle. I feel the glade’s heartbeat in the faint pulse of the tree roots, its breath in the condensation that clings to my bark. When the Eryndri’s domesticated beasts pause near me, their glowing eyes meeting mine, I sense their quiet loyalty, their unspoken understanding of the glade’s harmony. Sometimes, I stretch my rooted limbs and walk—though it feels more like a drifting—along the woven bridges, my presence calming the occasional unrest in the air. The children laugh softly as I pass, their melodic giggles a rare disruption to the silence, but the glade allows it, for I am there.

At night, when the glade’s bioluminescence is at its brightest, I descend into the earth, my form dissolving into the roots of the ancient trees. Here, I dream the glade’s dreams—visions of the Eryndri’s past, present, and future, intertwined with the memories of the land itself. I see the crater’s formation, the slow creep of life into its hollowed embrace, the first steps of the Eryndri’s ancestors as they discovered this hidden sanctuary. These dreams are not mine alone; they are shared with the glade, a collective memory that binds us all.

I am not a guardian, for the glade needs no protection. I am not a servant, for the Eryndri are my kin in stillness. I am a Witness, a living archive of this place’s quiet majesty. I am the hum in the silence, the glow in the shadows, the pulse in the roots. I am the glade’s whisper, its eternal presence made fleetingly visible.

And so I stand—or drift, or root—in the Sunken Glade of Elarion, a being of its balance, a reflection of its weird beauty. I am the Whisperer of Roots, and in this place, I am home.
