In the Subterranean Foundry of Arathi, the inhabitants are as much a product of their environment as the cavern itself. They are the **Forgeborn**, a people shaped by the relentless fusion of earth and industry, their lives intertwined with the machinery and the molten veins of the underworld.  

At first glance, the Forgeborn appear human, but their features are etched with the marks of their existence. Their skin bears a subtle sheen, as though it has absorbed the metallic dust and heat of the foundry, giving it a coppery or iron-gray hue. Their eyes, adapted to the dim, orange glow, reflect the light of the furnaces like polished ore, with irises that range from deep amber to molten silver. Their hair, if they have any, is coarse and often interwoven with strands of soot or fine metallic filaments, a living testament to the air they breathe.  

The Forgeborn are not born in the traditional sense. They emerge from the very heart of the foundry, their bodies coalescing from the elemental forces of fire, earth, and labor. Some believe they are the children of the cavern itself, born from the heat of the smelting pits and the sweat of their ancestors. Others whisper that they are the spirits of fallen workers, reborn in the forge’s fiery embrace. Regardless of their origin, they are bound to this place, their existence as inextricable from the foundry as the iron beams bracing the walls.  

They move with a purpose, their bodies lean and muscular from years of physical labor. Their hands are calloused, yet dexterous, capable of shaping raw metal into intricate forms. They wear clothing pieced together from leather, cloth, and scavenged metal, often reinforced with patches of steel or chainmail to protect against the hazards of their work. Tools are extensions of their bodies—hammers, tongs, and wrenches hang from belts or are strapped to their forearms, ready at a moment’s notice.  

The Forgeborn do not sleep as others do. Instead, they enter a state of **torpor**, their bodies slowing to match the geological pace of the cavern while their minds remain alert, dreaming of designs and innovations. This rest is brief, for the foundry demands constant attention. They work in shifts that seem endless, their days and nights blurred by the perpetual glow of the furnaces.  

Despite the harshness of their environment, the Forgeborn are not without culture. Their language is a blend of guttural sounds and rhythmic clangs, mimicking the machinery they worship. They tell stories of the foundry’s creation, of the first spark that ignited the smelting pits, and of the **Great Forger**, a mythical figure said to have carved the cavern from the earth with a hammer of pure fire. Their art is functional yet beautiful—intricately carved tools, statues of gears and pistons, and murals etched into the limestone walls, depicting scenes of labor and creation.  

The Forgeborn have no leaders in the traditional sense. Instead, they are guided by the **Overseers**, individuals chosen for their skill and ingenuity. The Overseers are marked by their attire—long, soot-stained coats adorned with gears and cogs—and their ability to communicate with the machinery itself. It is said they can hear the whispers of the foundry, understanding its needs and desires as if it were a living being.  

Children, rare among the Forgeborn, are treasures. They are taught the ways of the foundry from a young age, their small hands shaping metal under the watchful eyes of their elders. These children are the future, the promise that the foundry will endure, even as the world above forgets its existence.  

The Forgeborn are not free, yet they do not yearn for freedom. They are bound to the foundry, but it is a bond of choice, of purpose. They are the guardians of this hidden metropolis, the keepers of its fire, and the artisans of its steel. In their hands, the earth is transformed, and in their hearts, the foundry lives.  

And so they toil, day after day, year after year, their lives a symphony of metal and sweat, of heat and shadow. They are the Forgeborn of Arathi, the soul of the Subterranean Foundry, and the pulse that keeps its heart beating.
