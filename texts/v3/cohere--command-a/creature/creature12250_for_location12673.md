In this desolate, surreal expanse known as the Shattered Exhale, nothing lives in the conventional sense. Yet, existence here is not entirely absent—it is *other*, a presence that defies comprehension and lingers at the edges of perception. If one were to describe what inhabits this place, it would be something akin to the remnants of consciousness, the echoes of existence itself, rather than beings or organisms. Here is what dwells within:

### **The Shards’ Keepers**  
The obsidian shards, suspended in their fractured dance, are not merely inert. Within their dark, reflective surfaces resides something that might be called *the Keepers*. They are not alive, nor are they dead; they are the memories of form, the ghosts of structure. The Keepers are the shards’ consciousness, a collective awareness that persists despite the absence of motion or change. They do not act, they do not speak, but they *observe*. Their perception is infinite and instantaneous, drinking in the stillness, the voids, the filaments, as if savoring the slow unraveling of reality. They are the witnesses to the Shattered Exhale’s eternal moment, guardians of a place that has no need for guardianship.

### **The Voids’ Whispers**  
Between the shards, in the endless voids, there is a presence that is not a presence—a *Whisperer*. It is not a being but a force, a residue of language that has outlived its speakers. The Whisperer is the silence made tangible, the pressure that fills the emptiness. It is not heard with ears but felt in the mind, a faint, insistent murmur that suggests meaning without ever delivering it.
