It would be a spark of curiosity, nudging them to explore the uncharted territories of their own potential.

The power would **celebrate the otherness of this world**, refusing to impose external norms or expectations. Instead, they would **mirror the Symbionts' humility**, offering a silent acknowledgment of their grandeur without interference. The power would act as a midwife to the Symbionts' evolution, present but unobtrusive.

Finally, the power would compose a **hymn of potential**, a cosmic melody that resonates with the Symbionts' own hum of existence.
