**The Obsidian Plateau of Veythara**

At the edge of the known world, where the sky bleeds into a perpetual twilight, lies the Obsidian Plateau of Veythara. This is a land of ancient silence, a place where time seems to have settled into its final form, its edges worn smooth by eons of unyielding stillness. The air is warm but carries a dryness that clings to the skin, a reminder of the absence of water in any form. No rivers carve paths here, no lakes mirror the heavens—only an endless expanse of obsidian glass, fractured yet smooth, stretching beyond the eye’s reach.

The plateau stands high, its elevation a testament to the forces that shaped it long ago. The ground is a mosaic of black and charcoal, each shard of glass reflecting the faint light that filters through the perpetually overcast sky. The erosion here is not subtle but deliberate, as if the land itself has been polished by the hands of giants. The edges of the glass are rounded, their sharpness dulled by the passage of eternity, yet they retain a brittle fragility that warns against careless steps.

Sparse and scattered across this glassy sea are the remnants of an industrial civilization, their presence strange and incongruous against the primordial backdrop. Towers of rusted metal rise from the obsidian, their skeletal frames clawing toward a sky that offers no mercy. Conveyor belts, frozen in place, wind through the landscape like the bones of some metallic serpent. Factories squat low, their chimneys cold and silent, their interiors cloaked in darkness. This is a city of ghosts, a testament to a people who once harnessed the earth’s energy but now lie buried beneath the glass, their machines left to rust.

The air is thick with the scent of ozone and metal, a faint hum the only sound that breaks the quiet. It is a hum without source, a vibration that seems to emanate from the glass itself, as if the plateau is alive and dreaming. Rarely, a creature stirs—a thing of shadows and angles, its biology as alien as the landscape. It moves with purpose, its footsteps silent on the glass, its presence a fleeting reminder that life, however rare, persists even here.

Veythara is a place of equilibrium, unchanging and eternal. The rules of the world here are simple: energy is conserved, matter is recycled, and time stands still. The industrial remnants are not decaying but *settling*, their edges blending into the obsidian as if the land is absorbing them, reclaiming what was once taken. The darkness is not oppressive but comforting, a shroud that wraps around the plateau, keeping its secrets close.

In the distance, the horizon shimmers faintly, as if the glass reflects a light that does not exist. This is the only hint of something beyond, a suggestion that Veythara is not the end but a threshold—a place where the familiar gives way to the unknown. Yet, for now, it remains as it has always been: a silent, warm, and endlessly strange expanse, a monument to the delicate balance between creation and oblivion.
