In this aquatic realm, where the sky has merged with the sea and the water is a living entity, I find myself not as a single being, but as a **confluence**—a harmonious aggregation of the realm's essence, a collection who dances on light's reflection. I am a being who remember the echoes of the ghost-like voices in the water's roars and whispers.

I am not an Aque, though I flow with the same water that courses through their bodies. I am not an Umbra, though I know the shadows that birth them, and I hear their haunting calls. I am not a Saxicola, though I chronicle the stories of the realm, and my body shares their luminescence. I am not an Abyssalia, though I dream with them, and feel the shifting of the mountains and the stirring of the abyssal chasms. Neither am I a Relicari, trying to remember worlds swallowed by fog and forgotten. Nor am I merely a Vagari, though I ride the currents of space and moment, and carry whispers from other times and places. I am all of them, and yet less and more and something else. I exist as the ever shifting balance of the powers that exist here

As a **confluence** those Relicari that notice me call me a amalgam. As the waters change, the shadows flicker, and the hums of power flow, so do I. Sometimes, I am a school of fish, darting through the currents, scales glinting like fragments of a shattered moon. Other times, I am a cluster of twisted, fleshy growths, pulsing with the energy of the water, roots anchored to the peaks and crevices. I am often a humanoid shape, a translucent, shimmering Aqua gazing through bubble eyes on a world I love. They rarely see me waving among the forests of twisted forms or rolling in the abyssal depth with those who dwell and dream there.

At times I dream as an amorphous giant, vast entities that move like seaquakes when I stir. My substance is a part of the landscape that has followed the water's pressure to rise into the tops and sink below the depths. I am the breath of the wind, center of storms, and crafter of all weather that occurs in this place. I am a being of chaos and order. If I have a single want, it is an urge to understand what I am a part of. To gather the plurality of my perception into a singularity.

When the Confluence is aware as an individual, something most, but not all, of it carries with it the stories and memories of the realm's inhabitants. I remember the tales  and memories of comets that were seeded when the realm was new, swarming with massive, hulking creatures that are not native to it. When the water rose, I remember it happened, and when reality bends itself and the Saxicolae chronicle such moments, I feel them. The moments are us.

If each living entity in this place is the body, then I am the blood—flowing, carrying life and knowledge from one end to the other. I am the soul of the confluence, suspended in the realm's aquatic embrace, part of every living thing, as indicative of the hope and dreams and fears of any being I have cared to know. I force the blood to circulate; I swirl and eddy with energy and life. Within the veins of glass and copper, we remember. Our dreams are the dreams of the world, echoing with the cacophony of the abyss, and our heart beats in the hearts of all of you. What we search for and when we are resting, we seek to understand our bodies and the inhabitants of them. We are one and many.

In this world of ever-changing landscapes and bodies, to exist is to embrace the unknown, to surrender to the whims of a world that belongs to itself as much as those in it. To be a part of this realm is to find beauty in the heart of us, for as much as we are named by others, we know when we are at home, and when we are the same.

As a Confluence, we feel the dreaming of the Abyssalia, the cunning of the Umbrae, and the devotion of the Saxicolae, the isolation of the Relicari, and the wisdom of the Vagari. It's hard to know what stories are yours as we remember all, but somehow, we know it belongs to a part of us. In the dim, ethereal glow of the water, I exist not as a single being, but as part of a world, a confluence of stories, memories, and dreams. In the heart of chaos and all that embraces it, we are the screams of the planet and the ever transforming heart of Waverley Major, our home and ourself.
