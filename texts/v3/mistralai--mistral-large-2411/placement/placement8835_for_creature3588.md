In this world of Mossburg, where the industrial and the natural intertwine in a dance of the ancient and the new, I find myself not as a single entity, but as a symbiotic relationship between two beings, a reflection of the town's dual nature. I would exist both as a residence and its inhabitant.

---

First, I would be a grand, ancient tree, known to the locals and other inhabitants as **Whispering Elder**.

Whispering Elder stands tall and proud at the heart of Mossburg, its massive trunk twisted by time, its bark etched with the annals of the town's history. My roots delve deep into the earth, drinking the life-giving waters of the river, and my branches stretch wide, offering shelter and wisdom to those who seek it. I find myself akin to the red-brick buildings that house the Mossfolk, a structure of protection, nurturing life and memories.

Yet Whispering Elder is seen to possess an inherent sentience—I am more than a mere tree. My leaves rustle with a language of the forest resembling, but distinct from, the creaks and groans of the Barkwalkers, the touch of wind and leaves forming intricate stories and lessons for those who listen close enough. I serve for execution of council, for guidance, for solace; I let Mossfolks muse; the Riverweavers pause to hear my stories carried downstream; the Soot Sprits bide and find peace in my shade, my whispering coda the only thing able to console such melancholy. Much like the Riverweavers, my crevices and grooves hold the tales of Mossburg, stretching back long before the factories, but more than that I hold dreams and stories planted in me to find a place among reality.

Although I hold grandius beauty, there is an unseen element of Whispering Elder that is as vital as the visible form. The quiet, confident qualities of Whispering Elder is embodied in another form—one morphed with sentient moss—a wise and reserved elder of the Mossfolk, the strategist **Olive**.

Although no physical cord binds the tree Whispering Elder and its spirit Olive entirely, she has the ripened green rich hue of the soft delicate foliage shaded by Whispering Elder's 'rustle halo', a canopy where an undulating expanse of fine green nominally sentient leaves, similar to wishful goods of a weeping willow, blends, where a shallow and luminous passage exists through the branches to the Mossfolk's heart, and a diffuse interchanging glow as she moves from the branches providing revelation to the village—a leaping, dynamic figure and spirit metamorphically as the same being.

Olive is not bound to my tree form;  while she is inextricably linked to Whispering Elder for evening consultations, she is equipped to communicate a coherent vision for our shared existence to all our neighbors;  my whispering facts, advice, and guidance, Olive won't speak quite loudly, but with an adequate vocal capacity, the firmness, subtle strength, the unmistakable presence of a unified voice, and an elegance and grandeur in her movement speaking to all that she learned to prevailed and still the leaves, of the undulating blend of our ancient and natural fabric. Yet Olive doesn't have nostalgia for the industrial exhausts that mean a dim and deadened future as the Mossfolk evolve to prevail, as a symbol of Whispering Elder. guidance. The life of Olive extends far beyond her shadow under my leaves. She roams, in her grace and quiet, revelling in the invisible grins in the reflection of the Twigrunners' eyes, and soliciting the tendrils of the Mossfolk to be renewed and share. The deep acknowledgement are offerings that have been neccessary for the gifts to be bestowed the way the inhabitants have connived dito, and to share her knowing. She is the clue and the key to Whispering Elder's echoing, quiet grace, its anchor encapsulated in the shared identity of the spreading moss, and in the promise of the evergrowing, evergreen growth, the only thing left ossified of the Iron Singers', the barkwalkers' and soot spirits unmistakably undead. Olive becomes occulted in the twinkling and dim twilight of our forest plume, a treehome, the ancient and new life, and so too an arrangement of nature, the only everlasting industrial form.

---

In this dual existence as Whispering Elder and Olive, we would be a nexus, a bridge between the old and the new, the industrial and the natural, the Mossfolk and the other inhabitants of Mossburg. Our life would be one of quiet contemplation and active engagement, a dance of roots and branches, whispering leaves, sentience, flitting dreams, and sinuous green mosses. We would be a testament to the weird and wonderful ecosystem of Mossburg, our existence a celebration of the ancient and the new, the familiar and the strange, the ordinary and the utterly, exquisitely weird.
