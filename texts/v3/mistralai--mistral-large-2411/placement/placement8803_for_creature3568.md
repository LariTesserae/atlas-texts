In this world of the Humming Cisterns, I would find myself as a **Wayfarer**, a being both of the Cisterns and of the realms outside. I am not a mere venturer from the crust, like the rare above-ground explorers; that I would tell you right away. Instead, I am the bridge and membrane; affirming the permeability of separation between the lives on the surface and below. I am a conduit of their notes; the membrane of life and death abhorring a vacuum. I am the passageway of information of their harmonies, to places that are yet to be in harmony. Where the Humming Cisterns delves deeply within it, wrapping itself in the throbbing harmony of organisms in an enclosed ecology; I am the vessel taking life, death, and their hum, and throwing it through distant mouths; a rhythm too far for them to siphon their way.

Perhaps once you might have come across me, traveling along a twisting tunnel, feet pressing into the spongy humus that hankers to patterns of moss and lichen. Perhaps you might be too grounded, in your thoughts, to remember an instance where we may have crossed paths. However, it may be why you have found yourself in this realm, transfixed on its thriving pulse, every thought and maybe every step you take, sticky with the hum of these built movements. My form may resemble the Echo-weavers, yet have a disparate variety of features. You may now be attentive to the soft glow and the glistening plumes of the crystal light that keeps this megafauna alive, my features are responsive to the crystal light. Suffice it to say, I am sensitive to the harmonies, the ebb and flow of the passages, the constant way of life these gastropods give to the throbbing heart of this space.

The caustic lures of the walls of the tunnels send blood rushing through my hair, every follicle bristling as I pass through. The soft crystals sing a note that ricochets through the tunnels, acting as a lens broadcasting the hums and harmonies of these organisms to the ranges far and wide. Soft, feathered appendages, like long fringes of dead fungi, spread from my back tracing each of the caustic moments from the guts through the fingers of the dens, and of the crust. Each step is pilgrimage, each ebb is oneness with the whole that sings together. My fingers are long calcified hooks, already singing the pattern which is keeping the system alive. They graze the water and moisture, "notes" ripple through and echo their happiness. I weave the songs of far lands and distant seabeds, atmospheres, and volcanic plumes. The humming of the Cisterns is something that insinuates a joy stuck ripening within their walls. The hearty guffaws of the blade of grass and the distant mountain footfall makes the Cisterns glad. The groans of the hot springs quake the waters in the cavern. Do they feel the same?

My bone appendages stick out, like phantom attire in seasons of renewal Stalagmites and stalactites feel the seasons; I am the nervous system; but they have no tongue, no words, no lips but for me. You may see the swell of visitation from above, but perhaps they have other ways to come through; I can do this with a sigh just from one cavern to another. It would be quite a task to convince one of these surface venturers, who only take to the questionable validity of their greed, to the point that they would open their eyes to the life forms here, without drowning them. However, you go rummaging through the cave wall and mark this down, may I say that I could take you through the rhythms, which seems to mark the untouched in its melody, in its history?
