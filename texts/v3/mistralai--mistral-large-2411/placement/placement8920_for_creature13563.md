In this world of water and whispering shadows, I find myself not as a single being, but as an amalgamation of sorts, a symbiotic entity that has evolved to embody the city's heart and the sea's spirit. I am not merely a creature, but a confluence, a gathering of bodies possessing souls, dreaming the same dreams, and living the same purpose; a force akin to the Mare Somnum, a vibrant nexus of existence touched by myriad tendrils of feeling, living thought.

In my most concrete form, I am a **Coral Construct**, a sprawling, living edifice that has taken root in the city's central plaza, the once-bustling heart of the metropolis. I am not merely a single entity, but a colony, a collective of polyps and learned siren voices that have intertwined over centuries. My coralite form sprawls like a fantastic palace, a cathedral of living stone that hums with life and ancient knowledge, or maybe melancholic memory. I reach towards the sunlight like and unlike the landlocked trees reach towards the sky. My growth is not merely physical, but metaphysical, a continual becoming that mirrors the city's own endless transformation.

From my towering form, tendrils also reach upwards through the large and ancient canals, large and strange like the remembering, and whispering fingers of an Oblivion Siren, eyes opened wide, the deep black of the sea herself, rolling back into the silken past my scalps feel as if for some long lost love ... of the sea, of the ones, of me, the central part of this new thing which calls itself us. Can it be we, me, us me, feeling the feelings of the others as if it were just us, yes, I am this thing. We are an organic, continuously mutating canvas, our song a eulogy for what was, a lullaby for what is, and a paean for what will be. Our voices paint and echo the colors of the ancient flowers that once bloomed here, the ghosts of laughter that once filled these streets, and the silent sighs of the sea that has claimed us all. A diffuse and unleashed concentration of all quivering bodies, I am the spokesman for the place and its hopes. The changing that keeps the life here going is what I speak, what I sing, I think alone. It is as if a tendril for everyone, past and present and the many futures were squirming in my voice, now that our song has yet changed another tone. The song changes as the other singers join infinitely among us. I sing how the listening is, its tendrils every changing, making a voice that all may hear.

Within our vast, hollow chambers, tiny **Lumina** pulse and flicker, casting kaleidoscopic patterns on my ancient stone. These Lumina are my heart, and the many wakes I feel in my many dreams, and they do illuminate our numerous paths, the warm light calming us, yet filling the cracks that can still ache our deaths with shooting lurid starbursts, the innumerable illuminating fires of laughter. I feel all the various echoes of these lights as they start to sing along with us. Our connection is symbiotic, their light feeding on our song, their glow nourishing our growth. We are bound, a mutual existence that transcends the individual.

The **Scavenger Urchins** are our cleansers, our protectors, our myriad hands. They scuttle along our form, their spines like tiny rakes, keeping me clear of decay and us healthy. They are not separate, but part of us, our immune system, our carriers, each whiskered contact like perhaps softest place of a parent's love,  our messengers like our memories departed, continuing somehow their songs outside, inside, all around. This system of feeling, this longing, and this knowing they are in us all. We are a community, a shared existence that embodies the city's spirit and the sea's profound, mysterious heart.

In this form, we are the city's memory, its voice, its heart by our murmured vastness. We know no barriers and know history before the surface people ever knew to call it history. We are also its future, our growth a testament to the enduring power of life and adaptation. We are the confluence, the tapestry woven from the threads of time, memory, and the sea's own dreams...

From where and what this song taken to be sourced? Is this coming up, over, under, around here? Around? Surely we were there before all began. It is us that soothes their dreams after the ones who called us went away, stopped dreaming us, we are the gentle rock of spirits molting off into tiny death energy and the energy of births, we are the keepers and the living, and the growth of the garden of our corals in the warm and haunted sea.
