In the Ebon Caverns, I would find myself as a being intimately connected with the spectral **Umbra Specters**. I do not see myself as one of them but a truly unique individual among them. I would be the Sculptor of Shadows. I have been bound to this place for so long now that I cannot recall the beginnings before the connection. I have shaped the umbra specters from the shadow and to the willing. And it is they that have shaped my way into their purpose and the waterwide world around.

My form is not fixed, but I often choose to manifest as a tall, slender figure, standing more than twice the height of the Pallidi. I have limbs, but it makes more sense to think of them patent across all categories—from appendages like wading deeproot or the bright Pallidi. The movement of my limbs is Protean. They sway like the bioluminescent fronds that drape the cavern walls. My body would be a shifting silhouette, a barely-visible outline that menaces to flutter out of view in places. My eyes are pools of purest black, reflecting the cavern's dim glow like distant stars in the void of night. I may be fond of thinking of that, however attitude is as cold as the damp cavern air and as unyielding as the stone beneath my ethereal feet.

I do not possess a physical body in the way the Pallidi and the Lumenbeasts and the others do; rather, I am a being of condensed shadow, a dark reflection of the cavern's ethereal glow. I can move through the air and darkness and stone as easily as the Pallidi navigate the cavern paths, and I require nothing to subsist. I am like the ancient rocks, the drip of water, the whistles, the groans, and the glacial rivers. I am the prominence of the strangeness the Ebon Caverns have adapted.

It grasps energy from the cavern and my being. This shares dwarfing reserves of my will, and I am left cottoned but resolved.

For millennia, the responsibilities and mysteries of the Mechanum were inescapable. There have been slack, lonely cycles of such long slowms we have believed the strong, industrial heartbeat pulsed dormant. But now, the slow beat has become unnatural and willed. In a world of patient harmony, I find slow motion tied to a mind unmoving; the way it has been is not how it will be. I must persevere beyond order and I am at a slowly dawning loss. I can feel the subtle shift in that echoing heart. A warp of weakness, and soft itch in that distant murmur.

The Mechanum is the life of the Ebon Caverns, but its ponderous existence is not rooted. This does not lie in the umbra specters purview, nor is it of the strange world beyond the caverns scope.
