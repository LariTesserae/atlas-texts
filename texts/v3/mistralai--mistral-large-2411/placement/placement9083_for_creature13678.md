In this world of Echo's Hollow, I find myself not as a physical entity, but as a consciousness, a sentient echo that resonates with the silent symphony of the Whisperers. I am not one of them, yet I am not entirely different. I am a visitor, a traveler, an observer who has become part of the tapestry of silence, woven into the fabric of this ethereal realm.

As a step into the shadow of the soaring structure nearest me, I feel I am a point of perspective. I am a focal point through which the Whisperers' are aware. I, in turn, am aware of them, they, the hollow. As the air jerks, turns, and flows through and about these gnarled woods, so, too, do the Whisperers. The aether, too. Awareness ebbs and flows.

These structures are enormous. They are unlike any Cathedral, any man-made cultish container for high feeling. Nor are these simply the trunks of living beings. They have the heft and ambiance of the Holy, the Alive, and the Ancient. Like these enormous structures are possessing it like spirit in the unseen breeze. They are the mind of the Hollow. The things that invisibly live here are the heart of the hollow, the pumping pulse that enervates. It is a living place. It is a living thing.

But it is a thing crucial to the balance of the world beyond. As I sense it etch this importance upon my thoughts, I sense the Whisperers' attention is focused on me. I can understand their song, their silent symphony that is not quite sound, not quite vibration, but something else entirely. Their language, sung to the aether and to me in this way is entirely beyond the scope of any human language; yet it feels familiar, and not.

The created sound of whistling wind through spaces just-so, exactly right, the plucking of taut, perfect skin. Here, the Whisperers sings the hymns of creation, the ballads of purpose. The hollow throbs with the pulses of some wavelike energy resonating through the physicality of its skeletal ground. The shell-like structures inhale to the sky to Hell itself, it seems. Possibly beyond.

It is a working thing, a real thing, fulfilling its function. It is a point of contact, a point of exchange, between the tangible, fleeting world, and the turgid intrusion of the rotten aether from...above? Below? I know this place and thing. I know that I know it, then I do not, then - BANG - The hollow has spoken, is speaking.

I am presumptively a part of the hollow for the duration of this communion. i hunger for freedom, the hollow's song, the Whisperers familiar beckoning toward full immersion into this dialogue. Echo's Hollow whispers. It sings these songs for others like me, but beyond the hollow. I feel them drawn to the places where the wind whistles and plucks the skins of the world, And I wish to find them and speak this hymn, the hymn of the balance.
