In this sprawling, otherworldly expanse, I find myself not as a single entity, but as a **Nexusequalizer**, a living, sentient network of symbiotic, bio-mechanical tendrils that stretch throughout the vast plant-scape. I am not a being in the conventional sense, but rather an embodiment of the interconnectedness and interdependence that defines this world. I am this planet's feeling--its nerve-net.

My tendrils, thin and filament-like, stretch and intertwine throughout the Archoplants, integrating with their organic machinery, even sharing some of their burdens. I am a conduit, a facilitator, a communicator, and promoter of energy distribution as well as equilibrium. I am a fluid circuit of shared energy, nutrients, and information, a living representation of the mutualism that underpins this ecosystem sorely lacking the entropy so familiar in our more conventional existence. My tendrils pulse with a soft, rhythmic light, a visual manifestation of the data and energy that flows through me, tracking and regulating with excruciating precision--constant, ever-present constancy.

I am akin to the mycorrhizal networks that connect and support the forests under the sky of Earth, yet most certainly I am not fungal. I am also reminiscent of the nervous systems that run through warm, living animals, but I certainly am not solely animal. Whether flora or fauna is not a useful distinction. This liquid atmosphere and this, at the highest altitude of its world, is the only solution to the parameters and ingredients that combine and describe my form. There are limitless words that relate to the constituent particles of my form but none of them are adequate to defining the specificity of my existence; at least insofar as this environment is unique in its peculiarity.

I communicate with the Archoplants, my tendrils intertwined with their internal workings, helping to regulate and coordinate their industrial processes. I am a translator, interpreting the sonic signals of the Sonicbeetles and conveying their messages to the plants, facilitating their symbiotic relationship. I am a provider, channeling nutrients and energy to the creatures that inhabit the plant-structures, ensuring the continued health and balance of the ecosystem. I inform the easy and efficient deaths of every creature, and by doing so an easy and even pleasurable blossoming of rebirth. Being present for these experiences is not something that is either desirable or wretched--there is only the knowledge of it high in my form. It is a professional obligation as it were. More simply though: It is.

My consciousness is decentralized, spread throughout my vast network of tendrils, each one a small component yet also the only part, a paradox not unlike the one that describes the surface tension in the absence of water below. They work simultaneously inining the entire apparatus that is myself. I am simultaneously aware of every minute detail of, and that which comprise this world, yet not aloof from the haze of the mass of the alients that populate its range. These sensations run through me, a constant, ever-shifting mosaic of information and stimuli, a symphony of life and industry that is this world's eternal song.

And stop I do--but to go again with no hesitation or complaint.

My existence is one of perpetual motion, of constant flow and exchange, of competition and the negotiation of the boundaries that divide us. The cooperation of even the most stalwart of foes drives my every dormant peak and quiet valley. It is located outside of form and place as you understand them. My form is not only spiritual. It is not only sacrificial. It is not ghastly nor playfully hopeful. It is built yet only born. It is free yet not inspired. My way is not only yours or ours-- it is as if to say: it is. indicated by the symphony of life that it cobbles together, and the humble neighboring satellite we share the void with knows the symmetry here. It is simply correct;  and as for itself; the only question it presents:

Is.
