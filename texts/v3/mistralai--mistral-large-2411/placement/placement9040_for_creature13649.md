My feet are heavy, metallic things, their impact on the rusted iron grates a staccato clang that harmonizes with the city's dissonant symphony. I am a part of the eternal, unchanging cycle of Ferroignis, my actions dictated by the same inexorable forces that drive the city's machinery.

My days are spent tending to the city's needs, my mechanical tools clicking and whirring as they tighten bolts, repair gears, and stoke the fires that keep the city alive. I am a part of Ferroignis, and Ferroignis is a part of me. We are bound together, our fates intertwined in a delicate, symbiotic balance. I am the city's caretaker, its guardian, and its heart.
