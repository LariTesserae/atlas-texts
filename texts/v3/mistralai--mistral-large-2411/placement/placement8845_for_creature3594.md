I would be the rarest and quietest of the inhabitants, yet a thread binds me deeper within this place.

Our Symbiosis unfurls the ancient threads into the new tapestry of life, an ever inexorably weave to lay the new into weft and warp of our world.

A purpose and knowledge that I inherit from my forebears amongst the Custodians who developed a microcosm of healthy machinery from the natural growth of our valley. The forests need the ancient up-cycling Melody, without shaping, as though eons of design keep it in a precarious primal balance.
