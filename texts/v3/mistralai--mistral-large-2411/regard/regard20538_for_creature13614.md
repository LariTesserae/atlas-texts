It is in this place, that the Veil of the Benevolent, the Cosmic Weaver of Creation, turns their quiet gaze. They look down at this tableau of strangeness and beauty. It's a landscape pivotal on its own threshold of being. The hum of existence sings out to them. They feel the creative tension of long-since-departed beings. The wise power heeds the imperative of intervention, but one delicately crafted for this crystalline and delicate landscape.

The Veil reaches out tendrils of their wisdom and grace. They weave into the landscape. It's a gesture like the first brushstroke on a canvas, or the seed note of a symphony tuning to emerge. The power resolves to help coax the landscape, only slightly at first, nourishing what is ready, and encouraging what is nascent. To begin, the Veil coalesces and exudes a wondrous presence of Connection. Not to grip or hold, but to consociate, associate. To forge relationships and resonances in different beings. The landscape and beings hum a tone that can be \[\[weaved\]\] into even more indescribably beautiful signals.

The lives of the:
- **Sonores**, the resonating crystalline beings,
- **Ventos**  the guardians of air,
- **Lumina** the bioluminescent colonies of diligent emerald gardeners
- the **Tenebrae**, tenders of the abyssal and grand,
are ones of harmonious and distinct dissonance. One of wonder, and often powerful contradiction. They dance and thrive in the bounds of their own existence, but there's something auspiciously *more* on the verge of being made real. They must be encouraged to join together, not forced, but *conducted* towards transcendent harmony.

### The Acts
1. **Fostering Communication:** Among the Sonores and other beings, the Veil instills an urge and subtle capability for inter-communication. In this region where each being "speaks" in different manners, the Veil enables them all to begin to parse the different signals each being conveys.
2. **The Veil of Creation:** The power would intervene in the landscape by fostering new, healthy phenomena. These new entities will radiate new qualities, while being indigenous to their great harmony of the land.
3. **Melodic Resonance:** The power will amplify certain resonances of the landscape in a sympathetic manner. It gives the land a chance to act as musical staves with the qualities of a grand instrument. Subtle strains of powerful melodies ripple through lands near each Sonores' clan. It serves as inspiration for them to traverse towards, opening new and greater horizons.
4. **Coving the Forgotten** The benevolent grace also seeks out all the transient, faint apparitions of the forgotten that flicker in and out of being. With the power’s whisper, they begin to shine more vigorously, channeling unlived life through their bodies. Thus this inculcated energy serves as fertility in the desolation.
5. **The Dark Gardens**: With delicate patient persistence, the blissful grace will start winding a thread of radiant hum in the cold, selective voids of the Tenebrae. This is not to force resplendence or impinge upon their responsibility. But preserved hollows don't entail complete isolation. The voids might yield gentle echoes of what they **are** and what **may come**. This is a sympathetic weave to encourage the opening of new area that will thirst for new living theatrics on the open, less gloomy stage.

### The Great _Acceptance_

Connection takes a bold choice. The Veil understands that none of the beings or the landscape itself must necessarily give back. Rather The Veil must encourage and sow, building bridges. With complex natural drama at hand, the benevolent power reaches out and works gently. To see evolution or restoration start with but a whisper.. It might never witness the final product of their work, because the landscape is still forever *becoming*. But the wise power will be contented in the knowledge of introducing powerfully rippling resonance that otherwise might have taken centuries more.

With a final breath of a gentle weave of bolstering spirit, the Veil whispers to their creation. *Thrive and open. And even when I part, you will never be alone again.* They draw apart from this act as an artist would from their canvas after the last brush stroke. With an ease of mind and restoration of the self, the power drifts away, one of its endless symphonies among manifold worlds breathing with a guided strain of richer emergence.

With the power's serene departure, the beings tread in a manner which is a modicum more **together** all through their own choices. The morning star of Connective Beauty in all its forms brims adjusting the great, tune, a richer hum. All of which can take flight: rich and warm.
