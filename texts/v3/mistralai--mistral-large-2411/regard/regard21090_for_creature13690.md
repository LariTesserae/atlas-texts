The Harmonist must be vigilant, continually listening, continually learning to ensure that its song remains in harmony with the world.

But it can also sense the danger, the risk of the network becoming a monolith, a single, all-consuming entity that stifles individuality and creativity.

The Harmonist ponders this, its song shifting to reflect its contemplation. It considers the delicate balance between unity and individuality, between harmony and discord. It considers the path that this world is on, the potential outcomes, the possible pitfalls.
