But Aeris does not stop at the borders of the highlands. It knows that the threat to this ethereal realm comes from the world beyond, from the forces of industry and progress that are encroaching on the silent, mist-laden hills. And so, it turns its attention outward, its voice carried on the wind, its messages borne on the rain, seeking to influence the world beyond, to guide it towards a more harmonious coexistence with the magic and wonder of the highlands.

And as Aeris works its magic, both within the highlands and beyond, it begins to see the fruits of its labor.
