In the pulsating heart of a world that has known nothing but time and water, there exists a vast, high expanse of submerged territory, a place of ancient slumber and warm, silken currents that whisper secrets to those who can listen. This is not an ocean as we know it, but a realm where the very air is liquid, a dense and endless exhalation from the planet's core,creating a scale and physics of its own. Clothing is not required here, bodies are caressed by the viscous fluid and every being resonates just inside of actual stillness.

The landscape, if it can be called such, is a vertical ecosystem that has settled into its final, primordial form. Great pillars of eroded stone and colossal, gnarled vegetation loom in the dim light under this atmospheric-water, rising from an unseen abyss below in endless time-transfixed patience. These titans are the silhouettes of a world that has long since forgotten the touch of the sky, their forms twisted and sculpted by the incessant,slow caress of the ubiquitous fluid.  The watery atmo-sphere is filled with sprawling,lush fronds akin to ferns or kelps that sway hypnotically, their colors muted and ghostly in the low light. They cling to the sides of the stone pillars and the colossal trees known as numenma, and to the rests of long forgotten colonizers that the erosion has tumbled into the troughs beneath.

There is a quiet here, the kind that calls attention to the absence the of neighing and roaring of surface beasts but a bare sound of life can just be heard if one has the inclination to listen. The sounds are the faintest whispers of flowing currents and the soft murmurs of distant, sparse fauna communicating in clicks and trills through the dense liquid. These creatures— rarified orbs and luminescent pulsora that navigate implicitly, their tender skin glinting in the dimness— are few and far between, yet they cast eerie, dancing lights across and through the perpetual twilight of their impossibly dense home. You do not easily distinguish between large spaces and crowds.

The upper reaches of this dense and hypoxic fluid are occupied by a few scattered remnants of a long-forgotten civilization. Half swallowed stone shapes, hinting at a complex and perhaps ancient past where intelligent life thrived and where it interacted with and perhaps hybridized with the numenma beneath the fluid. The few recognizable built structures are near-touching with each other, and partially dissolved within the social communions of the ecology.

Beneath the golden-grey luminescent mist, reality bends substantially and sometimes seems to breathe, forms emerging and retreating from the shadows with a nonchalant rhythm. Here, the rules of the world are fluid, literally and metaphorically. Time seems to ebb and flow with the currents creating bizarre cycles borne pumped by the erotic fibrillations of the tube-like, numenma roots that seem to reach from the surface and into the hidden abyss. Some roots end blindly, opening their belly to reveal deep claw marks,revealing that something huge lives therein and that survival here requires compliance to a logic that shifts and eludes our own. Life here needs to negotiate the peculiarities of existence, the untrustworthy geometry, and the watery air's alien chemistry.

There is a sense of isolation and solitude in this boundless world, an abysmal loneliness that permeates the silence the way the heat permeates the living fluid. It is inescapable, a characteristic of its own and yet, through this solitude, a greater vitality pulses, one of activity and adaptation. And so, in this warm, dense, aquatic realm, where the last vestiges of a strange civilization are slowly swallowed by the erosion of its own rules, it nonetheless hums with the energy of deep time and it exhales a foreboding that the strange contradiction will shatter the spectacular beauty of the greatest lonliness that exists in an immensity of its own scale. This is a place of strange beauty and haunting desolation, of vastness and intimacy, where the ancient and the active coexist in a delicate, pulsating dance bound by laws unfamiliar to the waking world.
