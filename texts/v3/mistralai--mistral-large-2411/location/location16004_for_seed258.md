In the awakening embrace of a primordial dawn, a vast, elevated terrace unfurls like a new leaf, floating in a sky painted with hues of gold and cerulean. This is the Nascent Highland, a place of eternal beginning, where the world is forever locked in the moment of its own creation. The air is thick and damp, a warm and misty blanket that envelops all, as if the very atmosphere is eager to nurture the burgeoning life that abounds. Water is everywhere, bubbling up from the spongy ground, dripping from the leaves that cover every surface, pooling in clear, shallow expanses that mirror the endless sky. It patters softly, ceaselessly, a constant gentle rain that feels more like a caress than precipitation.

The vegetation is not mere decoration or afterthought, but the very heart and substance of this realm. Plants have taken over every conceivable surface, piled atop one another in a jubilant celebration of life. They are not so much growing as continually coming into being, a never-ending genesis. Delicate ferns unfurl their fronds before your eyes, while vast, twisted trees spiral towards the sky, their bark smooth and new. There are no old leaves here, no decay, only an eternal spring. Mosses like velvet carpet the ground, punctuated by clusters of mushrooms that seem to bloom like flowers. Every shade of green is here, painting a landscape that is both familiar and utterly alien.

Yet, for all its exuberance, this is a landscape with room to breathe. Plants cluster in loose, sprawling groups, separated by wide, open spaces where the soft, moist breeze flows unimpeded. The sky is vast and open, an eternal dawn that never quite breaks into full day, casting a warm, diffused light over everything. The sense of space is almost disorienting, as if the air itself is a tangible presence, a silent observer of this eternal bloom.

Stretching out in all directions, the landscape is a mosaic of loosely connected platforms, each a floating island of life suspended in the endless sky. They are not mere hunks of rock and soil, but intricate constructions, woven from plant matter by unseen hands. Vines as thick as tree trunks crisscross beneath, supporting the structures, while above, flowers the size of houses open their petals to the sky. This is not a place of mere nature, but of design, of intent, though what force wields such power is unknown.

The sounds of life fill the air, a symphony of growth and vitality. The patter of water is the constant backdrop, accompanied by the rustle of leaves, the creak of growing wood, the snap of unfurling fronds. Yet, despite the cacophony, there is an eerie absence. The sounds of fauna are rare, barely more than an occasional chirp or distant call, a fleeting reminder of the life that should be present, but is not.

This is a world of strange rules, where time seems frozen in the moment of creation, where plants dominate every surface, every nook and cranny. It is a place of eternal dawn, of endless growth, of life forever suspended in the moment of its inception. It is the Nascent Highland, a realm of timeless beginning, a testament to the raw, unyielding power of life.
