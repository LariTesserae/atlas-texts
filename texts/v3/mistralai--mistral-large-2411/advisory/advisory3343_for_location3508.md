2. **Respiratory Aid**:
   - **Oxygen Supplement**: Given the thin air, bring a portable oxygen supplement to ensure you have enough oxygen, especially for extended stays or physical exertion.

3. **Hydration and Nutrition**:

2. **Lighting**:
   - **Flashlight and Headlamp**: Although the environment is in perpetual dusk, a flashlight or headlamp can be useful for navigating fissures or examining details.

1. **First Aid Kit**:
