### 1. **Physical Preparation**

#### **Clothing and Gear:**
- **Breathable, Lightweight Clothing:** Given the hot and humid conditions, wear breathable, quick-drying fabrics like synthetics or lightweight cotton. Avoid heavy materials that can become waterlogged.

#### **Equipment:**
- **Headlamp and Spare Batteries:** The dim, flickering industrial lights might not be reliable. A headlamp will provide constant, hands-free illumination.
- **Helmet:** Protect your head from falling water droplets or loose rocks. A climbing helmet is ideal.
- **Hydration Pack:** Stay hydrated in the hot environment. A hydration pack allows for easy access to water without the need to stop and retrieve a bottle.
