**Preparation Guide for the Ordinary Human Multiverse Traveler**

**Physical Preparation:**

1. **Diving Training:** Given the submerged nature of this realm, it's crucial to have basic scuba diving training. You should be comfortable with open water diving and have experience in wreck diving, as you'll be exploring submerged structures.

2. **Fitness:** Ensure you're physically fit. Swimming in a dense saline solution might be more taxing than in normal water. Regular swimming and cardiovascular exercises before your trip will help.

3. **Vaccinations:** Although there's no mention of wildlife, it's always a good idea to be up-to-date with your vaccinations, especially tetanus, as you'll be exploring old structures.

4. **Hydration:** Prepare for your body to be more dehydrated than usual due to the saline environment. Start increasing your water intake a few days before your trip and maintain this during your stay.

**Equipment:**

1. **Diving Gear:** Bring your own reliable diving equipment. This should include a wetsuit (a 3mm should be sufficient given the warm water), mask, fins, snorkel, buoyancy compensator device (BCD), regulator, and dive computer.

2. **Underwater Flashlight:** Although the realm is bright, a flashlight will help you explore darker corners of the structures and signal to any fellow travelers.

3. **Gloves:** To protect your hands from sharp edges and potential hazards in the submerged structures.

4. **Underwater Camera or GoPro:** To document your journey in this unique environment.

5. **Non-electronic Navigational Tools:** Given the potential for unusual electromagnetic activity due to the bioluminescence, pack a compass and underwater maps, if available.

6. **First Aid Kit:** For treating minor injuries. Make sure it's in a waterproof container.

7. **Rehydration Salts or Tablets:** To replenish your electrolytes after dives.

**Mental Preparation:**

1. **Expect Sensory Deprivation:** The lack of wildlife and the constant murmur of water can feel isolating. Prepare for this by practicing mindfulness and getting comfortable with silence.

2. **Cultural Sensitivity:** Remember, this was once a human habitation. Treat the structures with respect, like you would any historical site.

3. **Familiarize Yourself with the Environment:** Try to understand the rhythm and flow of the water currents. Watch videos of wreck dives and underwater ruins to get an idea of what to expect.

**Safety Precautions:**

1. **Never Dive Alone:** Always have a buddy with you. If possible, have a local guide or someone experienced with this specific realm.

2. **Gradual Ascents:** To avoid decompression sickness, make sure to ascend gradually and do safety stops.

3. **Stay Hydrated:** Drink plenty of water before and after each dive.

4. **Avoid Touching the Bioluminescent Water:** Until you understand more about its properties, it's best to avoid direct contact with bare skin.

5. **Respect the Structures:** Avoid touching or disturbing the structures and murals. They might be stable, but they're also ancient.

**Communication:**

1. **Learn Basic Sign Language:** Since verbal communication will be difficult, learn basic sign language or underwater signals to communicate with your dive buddy.

**Documentation:**

1. **Keep a Journal:** Document your experiences, feelings, and any odd occurrences. This could help future travelers or even yourself if you decide to return.

2. **Sketch or Photograph Murals:** The murals could tell a story about the people who once lived here. Documenting them could provide valuable insights.

**Post-Visit:**

1. **Monitor Your Health:** After your visit, keep an eye on your health. If you feel unwell or notice anything unusual, seek medical help immediately.

2. **Share Your Experience:** Contribute your knowledge to multiverse travel forums or platforms to help future travelers.

**Final Note:**
This realm, while eerie and alien, is also a testament to human resilience and artistry. Treat it with respect and wonder. Enjoy your adventure!
