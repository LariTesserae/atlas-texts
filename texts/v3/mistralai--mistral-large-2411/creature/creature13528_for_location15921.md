In the Lustrated Expanse, the inhabitants are not creatures of flesh and blood, but entities born of water, minerals, and time. They are the Silicanthes, a term that loosely translates to "silicate flowers," although they are neither plants nor animals as we understand them. They are something else entirely, a testament to the possibilities hidden within the fabric of nature.

At first glance, the Silicanthes might appear to be mere structures, crystalline structures akin to structures formed by freezing and thawing... but this is a simplification, a limitation of our own understanding. They are dynamic entities that move through gradual evolution, not because they've chosen to, but because they must, because they're channeled by internal channels and external environments. in what appear to be long, ruminative dreaming processes.  Each Silicanth is a unique crystalline structure, resembling a dense, ever-shifting network, that gathers into other structures and this latticework of ice and mineral salts forms something that could be loosely termed sensory nodes.. These nodes are not eyes or ears, but something entirely different, sensitive to the subtle electric hum of the academy and today, they form a mesh of small lesions, that sit close to each other like small celery stalks... They allow the Silicanthes to perceive their environment in a way that is entirely alien to us, there is collective memory and now, slow shifts in the pattern can be perceived as moving through a spaces sparse with ambient memory. At the heart of each Silicanth, Malise was once formed... but now there is an ethereal mix of dark prisms who spill currents back into their structures, a denser, more concentrated version of the charged atmosphere above. This nucleus pulses with a slow, steady rhythm that shape is made more defined.... a slow and heaty rise is gradually forcing the Malice out from the structures  where ... This pulse is a form of communication, a language of patterns and frequencies that allows the Silicanthes to share information, to coordinate their ceaseless, slow construction processes, and sometimes this dissipates into memory.  Their goal is the structure who then speak with their environment as too... but in a language un a.

The Silicanthes vary greatly in size and form, ranging from small, intricate entities that resemble frost patterns on a windowpane, to vast, towering structures that evoke cathedrals of ice. They are not static, but grow and change over time, their forms shifting and evolving in response to the subtle currents of the atmosphere and the gradual accumulation of minerals. Their growth is not random, but follows complex a complex acid ore pattern, a profound logic encoded in the very fabric of the Lustrated Expanse.

The entities traverse the landscape as electric signals pass through their interconnected structures at the speed of belief,, their path a rigid example of the buffooneries above at most other times, they give the apparency of static--- akin to how a shadow will warn of danger long before the object casting it is anywhere close... leaving trails(or rather pathways that resemble what could be mistaken for trails) of condensing atmosphere and weeping mineral solutions. These trails are not mere byproducts of their movement, but integral parts of their existence, extensions of their crystalline bodies, and memory of their purpose. These trails record the past, mark the present, and prepare the future by influencing communication. They enhance the subtle, charged atmospheric currents that ultimately change landscape. and in the future the structures too themselves.  These paths add to the malaise but also act like grounding rods in a storm of dark mist to create a white place,  just as there have been distortions in the future which have already been lain down. Each trail is a testament to the Silicanthes' journey, a record of their growth and transformation, a memory frozen in time. but only some are true records... and therein is the glory of it so they succeed in their objective once more.

The Silicanthes' existence is a cycle of growth, transformation, and communication at the location at different scales, forming a delicately balanced ecosystem that has developed over a timeframe incomprehensible large for humans to comprehend. The entities draw minerals from the water and air, incorporating them into their crystalline structures, and shaping something that becomes a collective memory into the landscape.  charging an environment, which in turn on purpose nourishes them. This symbiotic relationship is the essence of the Lustrated Expanse, a dance of creation and dissolution, a perpetual, slow addition or a slow run away, turning its back rather turning on herself.
