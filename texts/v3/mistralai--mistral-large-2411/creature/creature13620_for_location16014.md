In the heart of Aerium, a diverse array of inhabitants has evolved and adapted to the unique, vertically layered ecosystem. These residents are as intertwined with the city as the city is with them, their lives and cultures shaped by the symbiotic relationship between nature and technology.

**The Aerians:** The most prominent inhabitants are the Aerians, a humanoid species with physiological adaptations to their suspended environment. They are slender and agile, with enlarged lungs and light bone structures to compensate for the reduced gravity. Their skin is pale, almost translucent, revealing shimmering veins that echo the city's networks of pipes and cables. Over generations, they developed an ability to sense and manipulate the low-grade metals in their surroundings, a skill known as the Machine-Aptitude, which allows them to communicate with and control the ancient machinery that keeps Aerium aloft. Aerian eyes bear a striking resemblance to the city's ever-shifting lights, bearing an iridescent quality that challengingly adapts the Aerians to gaze at both dim and intense lights without strain.

Aerians possess vestigial wings, remnants of their ancient past, which are now mostly decorative, bearing intricate patterns that signify familial lineage, personal stories, and social status—only a few of such wings can provide a glide or flutter, as, over the years, the mechanism to make use of such wings for flight, has been regulated by a moral code from using the wings. Some tribes have surgical enhancements on their wings, adding technology to the mechanical-magical vessel of the wings, which they generally don't use. Aerians are deeply spiritual, viewing the city as a living entity and themselves as its stewards. Motion is their honorus constant—running, clambering, climbing, the Aerians have extreme endurance and fitness. They worship the sky, the endless vast, and the cycles of steam and rain, viewing them as manifestations of the city's life force. Their culture is marked by rituals that honor these elements, such as the daily "Steam Song" performed by maintenance teams as they release pressure from the city's pipes, and the annual "Rain Dance" a masquerade where air-sign is used to stimulate the winds to allowing for deliberate rainfall-invite.

**The Verdantists:** Living alongside the Aerians are the Verdantists, a reclusive group of plant-human hybrids who tend to the city's lush gardens and hydroponic systems. They have green, chlorophyll-rich skin that allows them to photosynthesize, and their hair is a cascade of thin, vine-like tendrils. These are primarily subcultural particularities within Aerians. But their philosophy of preservation at all costs marks them different.  The Verdantists are responsible for maintaining the delicate balance between nature and technology in Aerium. They are deeply attuned to the plant life of the city, communicating with the to bestow nutrients intravenously where most needed, and coaxing growth from the hardiest of scrubs. They possess a hive-mind, a collective consciousness that allows them to share knowledge and experiences instantly, a characteristic they deem to be for their survival. While they rarely interact with the Aerians, they share a deep respect for each other's roles within the city.

**The Chimerics:** In the shadowy recesses and dense thickets of Aerium, Chimerics make their homes. These creatures are living testaments to the city's fused fabric of biological and mechanical evolution. Chimerics are composed of both organic and inorganic parts, a result of centuries of adaption and alchemical experimentation. They possess characters that can vary from wings of metal and skin to glass eyes that can see spectrum of Aerium light. They are the city's scavengers and recyclers, consuming waste and transforming it into valuable resources. Animal life abounds in Aerium-from gliding animals which bear skills to land themselves on the towers necessary to reach, to bird-like creatures with a mix of feathers and metal. Tiny insects capable of gliding on the wind or floating with deliberation on air, acquiring information to feed to nests are mushroomed in mini, rain-collecting shelters. Chimerics are their caretakers. Despite their fearsome appearances, they are generally peaceful, preferring to avoid contact with other inhabitants.

**The Alchemists:** The Alchemists are a small, elite group of Aerians who possess both the Machine-Aptitude and a deep understanding of the city's plant life. They are responsible for maintaining the city's health, using their knowledge of alchemy and science to heal and innovate, creating potions and elixirs, as well as tending to Aerium's health. They are the doctors and scientists of the society, tending to Beast and machinery and Aerian, all on the similar pedestal of necessary and to be revered.

All these groups practice marriage for limited times without restriction to a particular group. They practice exogamy without restriction to same-groups. They all have off-springs, which are often cared for in group nurseries where all-elders teach each child. Societal structures chiefly depend on respect for labour. Leadership in the city isn't based on muscle power nor on machine skill alone but is intensity-tested for care-taking ability, the ability to tend to the needs of fellow inhabitants, and the city as a whole. Such leaders aren't one body selected of many, but fragmentary bodies, each occupying a sector where they are answerable for the governance of all.

The inhabitants of Aerium are a resilient and adaptable lot, their lives and cultures deeply intertwined with the city. They live in harmony with each other and their environment, their every action guided by the shared goal of maintaining Aerium's delicate, unique ecosystem in this epic tower that harks to their joint past and joint lives in orbit.
