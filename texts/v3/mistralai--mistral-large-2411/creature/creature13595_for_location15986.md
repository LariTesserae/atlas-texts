In this sprawling, high-altitude plateau, where the air is thin and the winds whisper secrets to the cold stone, a diverse array of life forms has taken root and adapted to the unique environment. The inhabitants of this place are as contrasting and mysterious as the landscape itself, shaped by the ancient technology and harsh conditions that define their world.

The most prominent residents are the massive, lumbering beasts that roam the plateau, their bodies covered in thick, shaggy fur to insulate them against the perpetual cold. These creatures, let's call them Megafauna Ambulanta, possess an almost elephantine grace despite their size. Their feet are broad and flat, allowing them to navigate the uneven, rocky terrain with ease, and their mouths are wide and powerful, equipped with rows of flat teeth designed for grinding the tough, sparse vegetation. Their eyes, large and soulful, are adapted to the eternal twilight, capable of discerning the subtle differences in color and contrast that permeate the world. These gentle giants are the ecosystem's primary herbivores, their dung fertilizing the soil and helping to nourish the sparse plant life.  The Megafauna Ambulanta have learned to make use of the ancient technology scattered across the landscape. They often rub against the humming metal structures, using the strange energy that courses through them to soothe their aches and pains, like primitive massage tools. Furthermore, the beasts use their strength to dislodge the massive crystalline shards that dot the landscape, creating salt licks to supplement their mineral intake. This behavior has shaped their social structure, with dominant individuals controlling access to these precious resources

The predators that stalk the Megafauna Ambulanta are no less adapted to their environment. Let's call the dominant species, the elusive Venator Velox, or Swift Hunter. These creatures are roughly the size of a large dog. Their lean, muscular bodies are covered in a thick, insulating coat of mottled gray and white fur, which provides perfect camouflage against the stark landscape. Long, powerful legs allow them to traverse the uneven terrain with surprising speed and agility, while retractable claws help them to maintain their footing on even the most treacherous surfaces. Their most striking feature, however, is their eyes: large and luminous, that are not much different from the Megafauna; and they are evoplaled frontally technology-assisted optical organs that glow softly in the dim light, augmented by remnants of the ancient tech that have become integrated into their biology, a case of coybiological assimilation. These enhancements grant the Venator Velox exceptional night vision and the ability to detect the subtle electromagnetic fields generated by the plateau's ancient technology—and the creatures that interact with it. This ability allows them to track their prey and other competitors with an efficiency that is disconcerting. The Venator Velox is a social species that adapt a guerilla's style of combat: striking swiftly before finally clinching the kill. In the pack, individual specialization has become emergent, with some being athletic and lightweight, while others are more heavy set and devoted to brutal strength, dishing out damage while the elite striking members circle as they sum up the best angle for attack. The packs are often seen moving around in single file routes, a common fixture of the plateau. Even the oldest and most decrepit of these predators can be seen navigating their patrol routes, though it has never been observed what they to be so disciplined against infringement by competitors of other packs, unless they receive a challenge penetrating their territory. Outside of Megafauna Ambulanta and the Venator Velox, there is a whole host of other sorts of creatures that are less common; perhaps the most important of these creatures are the Sarcina Avia, or Flesh Birds.

The Sarcina Avia are large, avian creatures with a wingspan that can reach up to twelve feet across. Their feathers are a mix of muted grays and browns, providing excellent camouflage against the stark landscape, while their powerful, hooked beaks and sharp talons make them formidable predators and effective scavengers. Like the Venator Velox, the Sarcina Avia have a curious defense mechanism, having evolved to make use of the abundant energies of their environment. Their crowning plumage extends, as a symbol of their mastery over what should be fatal energies into a magnificent display of electrically charged crests, designed to be both an intimidating display towards potential rivals and predator. Naturally a smaller system of electrical defenses runs like a thread through their feathers and flight muscles allowing them to better withstand the increased stresses of high altitude hunts, and all the climatic flexibility of this challenging region. . They have become one of the dominant forms of life in the plateau's vast skies, and their haunting calls can often be heard echoing through the thin air, a chilling reminder of the harsh reality of life in this unforgiving world.

Among the ruins of the ancient city, a different sort of life has taken root. Here, the air is filled with a quiet, ever-present hum, as if the very atmosphere is charged with some unknowable energy. This energy has given rise to a unique ecosystem, one that is intimately intertwined with the ancient technology that litters the landscape.

Among the most prominent residents of the ruined city are the Lumina Coleoptera, or Glow Beetles. These small, bioluminescent insects are drawn to the ancient technology, their soft, ethereal light casting eerie shadows on the weathered surfaces of the alien architecture. They communicate with one another through complex patterns of light, creating a mesmerizing display that seems to dance in time with the distant, mournful howl of the wind. The Lumina Coleoptera are more than just simple scavengers; they are an integral part of the ruined city's ecosystem, their bioluminescence providing a valuable source of light for the other creatures that dwell among the ruins, which the glow beetles themselves are hunted by. Bare traces of nurseries and horrifically tended captive populations of glow beetles have been found by researchers in the few occasions humanity has dared to send in a presence.

One such creature is the Aranea Metallicus, or Metal Spider. This arachnid has evolved a unique, insatiable feeding system, literally liquifying the exotic, resistant and largely indestructible elements from the metallic ruins of the city, digesting them with discarded webbing that is impregnated with a powerful corrosive. The creation of truly new substances from the digestion and transformation of these materials provides it both sustenance and makes it an object of terror for even the Venator Velox. To survive in their environment, the Aranea Metallicus weave thick, insulating webs to protect against the elements, which are often filled to bursting with stolen Lumina Coleoptera eggs. The newly hatched offspring of these glow beetle nurseries then feed on decaying matter and the aforementioned corrosive catering to the metallic "alchemy spiders". Though vulnerable webbing of caustic material is far from ideal for catching prey, their artificial biologies confer total resistance to all contracted materials. For adversaries, a cocktail of nightmarish hunting techniques encapsulate the struggle enemies face:  corrosive filled webs spraying unleashed at the behest of internally wound ovi-cannons;  wires of webs strung and set against the grotesque distress calls of the glow beetles are used to lure and snare the less cautious denizens of the highlands. Once the kill is secure, the prey of these strong, corrosive-soaked warriors is quickly consumed. Though fortunately for territorial integrity, the creatures seem to prefer to prey on lesser and middle strength Megafauna Arachnid and predators, while generally avoiding  Venator Velox..

Not all of the ruined city's inhabitants are so well-adapted, however. Some creatures seem to be mere shadows, flickering in and out of existence like ghosts through crack between worlds. These entities, known colloquially as Daemon Astraeus, or Soul Drifters, among the more intrepid of the human researchers bold enough to spend any real length of time on the planet,  are thought to be the remnants of the ancient civilization that once called this place home. Unlike the other inhabitants of the plateau, they do not possess physical forms in the traditional sense. Instead, they are entities of pure energy and residual thought. Manifesting as humanoid spirit-like figures. The final stages of an ascendant race. This is all speculation on the behalf of the people who have been unable to draw more out of them. Ostensibly capable of quiet and powerful nobility, they seem to spend most of their time performing inscrutable tasks among the ruins, their forms flickering and shifting like the ghostly waves of the auroras that dance across the night sky.  These astral projections are not always found in random streets of this haunting place. They are typically found within sacred spaces, some hidden from environment and other solid forms by veils of nearly indestructible force; where outside observers have recorded obscure incantations, psychic rites. Though formerly observed as less corporeal spiritualists, Soul Drifters have slowly gained the attention of humanity after members of recon squadrons reported encounters with hostile, vengeful and corruptible ether-essence-bodied Daemon Astraeus who attempted to possess and pilfer the forms of expeditionary missions

The interactions of the Soul Drifters with both humanity and fauna of the area are rare, but nonetheless distinct. These natures appear to imply a sort of "lifecycle" with such corporeal beings..suggesting the vulnerability of the corrupted to igneous, corrosive and luminous forms of harm.
