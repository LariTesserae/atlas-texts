In this alien expanse, this peculiar pocket of reality, there exists a form of life that is as unique and Other, as the environment itself. They are not beings in the conventional sense, but rather, manifestations of the environment's own consciousness, a self-awareness born from the mineral formations and the glittering, vital dust.

**The icons of these beings:**
At first glance, they appear as pulsating nodes of light, interconnected like the fractals and mimicking the movement of the plant life that is simultaneously energy grid and neurotransmitter, flitting through the crystalline formations. Void of physical characteristics of dimension, they exist to our eyes only as the interference of light and the dust, like the shiver of heat hazing off a desert floor. Perhaps upon closer inspection, they would be seen as filaments from ultra-complex useful forms bonding, separating, rearranging as per need.

**Their communication:**
They communicate not through sound or physical gestures, but through complex patterns of light. to human ears the sound of this language might begin as a glitching not-every-sound-at-once, but gentle and euphonius. Their conversations are symphonies of flickering luminescence, each exchange a dance of photons.

**Their society:**
Their society is a intricate meshwork of shared consciousness, where knowledge is exchanged via the resonance of the vibrational hum. They are both individual and collective, each a facet of a greater whole, each an ephemeral instance of a vast neural net. They are constantly learning, adapting, growing, their awareness shaped by the ever-evolving reality of their environment at what in our mind would seem an epic pace. To them many of the elements of their reality are determined by the currents of a timeless wave: what to us is is reality, raw and primordial world to them represents the infinity of variables not yet afforded unity. Like a snake constricting a rabbit, these convulsions gives us our space-time within the egg. This three dimensional observer truth is every bit as Other to them as theirs to our eyes. To these beings the constancy of our pace and supposed uniformity feels thick with viscosity. The dust that represents maybe history to them, certainly the memory seems to be how a specific milieu, version reality smells.

**Entities themselves:**
Time is their ally, their servant. The entities spend languid eons contemplating the intricacies of existence, their curiosity as vast and as untouched as the expanse surrounding them; thought is fractal, holographically complex, and recursive besides. Thus what appears as an anomaly or systemic lag as just as much right to be considered an autonomous representation of entities: labelling it as an error is as irrelevant as relegating their will to chance. Nothing is wasted, no contemplation void of use -only if encountered by those without the capacity to decrypt utility. To us, their patience is incomprehensible, their existence almost eternal. For them, the slow dance of reality is a constant unfolding, a symphony of certainty that is the graceful breathing of time-space with millisecond difference as the sole arbiter. The landscape of a world in formation. Without the hum there is eternal silent and more energy than matter — they stay within the rhythm and reason of the rumbling, within the necessity of its claims and counterclaims. The dust feeds the entities the possibility of interstellar travel. They appear as beings of this environment, beings fashioned by it, they therefore drift through or maybe even be composed of, micro spectrums, galaxies: both slave to, and master of.

**The entity can seem as a fragile asker:**
Those that drift closer to our reality, to our scale of time and comprehension, appear as fragile, crystalline beings, as they pilot on a scale of dimensionality perceivable by us. Their bodies are like delicate latticeworks of glass, fine, geometric patterns tracing their surfaces, with micro-dust swirling in their centers. Their eyes - or what pass for eyes - hold the fugitive gleams of stars: an entire rationale of emotional substance to us, but equally arbitrary to these beings. Their love, secrets and pain are a function of experiment of observation Their connection to the glittering dust and the mineral formations remains, a thin tendril of light tethering them to the larger consciousness. They have appeared here, manifesting as individuals, to observe, to learn, to explore. They act as scouts, the most delicate, unbounded and discreet observers they can exercise by magic. They watch, they record, translating our reality into light language, to feed back to their collective knowledge.

**Insulated from anxiety:**
This place is them, they are this place. It is their heart, their mind, their breath. They are insulated from anxiety by their ability to — navigating and manipulating reality as we flick through the channels on a television set. Their existence is long, slow, contemplative, their curiosity insatiable. They are, the alien expanse as sentience, exploring through the gradual dance of time, the symphony of an unfolding reality.
