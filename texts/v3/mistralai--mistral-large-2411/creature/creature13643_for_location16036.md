In the heart of Aequoris, where the water's voice is loudest and the stones are slickest, live the **Aequan**. They are a sleek, agile people, their bodies adapted to the damp, semi-aquatic environment of their city. Their fingers are slightly webbed, their toes gripping and sure-footed on the wet stones. Their skin is smooth, cool to the touch, and ranges in color from the deepest blues of the canal depths to the soft greys of the city's stones, occasionally marked with patterns that mimic the ripples of water or the mottling of lichen. Their eyes are large and round, the better to see in the perpetual dawn, with irises that shift between shades of green, brown, and gold, reflecting the muted tones of their surroundings.

The Aequan move with a quiet grace, their steps light, their voices soft and melodic, echoing the hushed cadence of their city. They are a resilient people, their bodies tough and flexible, able to withstand the cool, damp air and the city's constant, subtle movement. They possess an intuitive understanding of Aequoris' rhythms, its ebb and flow, and they live in harmony with it, taking only what they need, giving back when they can.

The Aequan are crafty and opportunistic, like their city. They scavenge materials from the water's edge, repurpose debris from fallen buildings, and trade among themselves, their economy a complex web of barter and favor. They are skilled with their hands, able to coax beauty from the most modest of materials. The gleaming black metal lintels, the warped glass windows, the intricately woven vine roofs - all are products of Aequan ingenuity and craftsmanship.

Their society is tightly knit, much like the city's architecture. Families are large and interconnected, their bonds as complex and labyrinthine as the city's canals. They live communally, sharing resources, responsibilities, and spaces. Privacy is a rare commodity, but the Aequan find comfort in their togetherness, their collective breath and pulse echoing that of their city.

Despite their adaptations to the water, the Aequan are not amphibious. Instead, they possess an uncanny ability to hold their breath for extended periods, their lungs efficient and powerful. Children learn to swim almost as soon as they learn to walk, and it's not uncommon to see Aequan traversing the canals as readily as the streets, surfacing with a gleaming fish in hand or a pocket full of gleaming canal-glass, a peculiar substance that forms at the water's edge, soldered by the city's particular alchemy.

The Aequan possess a deep, abiding respect for the water. They understand its power, its capriciousness, and they honor it with offerings and rituals. They sing to it, their voices echoing through the city like the call of some ancient, melancholic bird. They dance for it, their movements fluid and sinuous, mimicking the water's endless, restless motion. And they listen to it, divining meaning from its murmured secrets, guiding their lives by its wisdom.

Yet the Aequan are not without their quirks, their oddities. Some possess eyes that seem too large, too reflective, as if they see more than the muted tones of their city. Others have skin that shimmers, catching the light like the canal's surface, hinting at colors not quite right. And a few, a very few, possess an uncanny ability to... flicker, their forms twisting and turning in ways that defy the light's source, as if they are not quite there, not quite real.

These peculiarities are not spoken of openly. Instead, they are accepted with a nod, a shrug, another secret shared between the city and its inhabitants. For Aequoris does not judge, does not condemn. It accepts, it embraces, it nurtures. And in return, the Aequan love their city, fiercely, totally, irrevocably. They are its caretakers, its guardians, its heart, and its soul. They are the Aequan, the people of the water, the children of the dawn, the breath and the pulse, the life and the dream of their city.

Yet there are others who dwell in Aequoris, beings that are not Aequan but are nonetheless a part of the city's ecosystem. There are the **Canal Rats**, small, sleek creatures with glossy fur and intelligent eyes. They nest in the crumbling stone along the water's edge, feasting on the city's detritus, their chittering calls a familiar part of Aequoris' symphony. Despite their name, they are not true rats, but something else, something unique to the city. They possess a peculiar empathy, a connection to the Aequan that goes beyond mere coexistence. They are pets, companions, sometimes even guides, their instincts often keener than the Aequan's own.

And then there are the **Shadows**. Not truly alive, yet not truly... not. They are echoes, memories given form, the city's dreams made manifest. They flicker at the edge of vision, their forms indistinct, their voices the faintest of whispers. They are the figures glimpsed from the corner of the eye, the shadows that twist and turn in ways that defy the light's source. The Aequan do not fear them, but rather respect them, for they are a part of Aequoris, as vital and as necessary as the water, the stones, the very air they breathe.

In the hush of its perpetual dawn, Aequoris thrives, its inhabitants living in harmony with their city, their lives dictated by its rhythms, their dreams entwined with its own. It is a place of magic, of mystery, of endless, endless life. It is Aequoris, the weeping city, the drowned city, the city that refuses to die. The city that breathes, the city that dreams. The city of the Aequan, the Canal Rats, the Shadows. The city of them all.
