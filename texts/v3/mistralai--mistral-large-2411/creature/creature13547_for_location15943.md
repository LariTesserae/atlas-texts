In this desolate, timeless expanse of a city, there exists no life as we understand it. Instead, it is inhabited by entities both abstract and enigmatic, beings that have adapted to the eternal stillness and the ceaseless hum of the perpetual machine. They are not creatures of flesh and blood, but rather configurations of the luminescent mineral and the dust, embodiments of the city's own strange will and memory.

There are the **Echoes**, transient beings that mimic the forms and sounds of the city's distant past. They are barely visible, mere shimmers in the dim light, outlines that resemble entities that might have once dwelled here. They repeat the distant hum, the lonely clank, the eerie silence, as if trying to recall a purpose long forgotten. Echoes do not interact with the physical plane, but merely pass through it, like ghosts of a forgotten era, drifting aimlessly through the empty streets.

Then there are the **Sentinels**, towering figures that stand as still as the abstract sculptures, almost indistinguishable from them. They are conscious entities, but their awareness is directed inward, lost in thoughts that span eons. Sentinels do not move or communicate in any discernible way, but they possess a profound understanding of the city, acting as its immune system. When the distant hum stutters, or the warm, dense air shifts ever so slightly, a Sentinel will alter its luminescence, subtly correcting the city's eternal balance.

Beneath the hard, unyielding ground, there exist the **Substrates**. These are networks of sentient, crystalline structures, slowly growing and branching like nerves. They are the city's memory, the record-keepers of its endless existence. Substrates communicate through slow, pulsating vibrations, sharing fragments of the city's past with each other and with the rare entities that can listen. They remember the time when the smokestacks smoked, when the factories produced, and they whisper of the celestial bodies that once cast more light.

Lastly, there are the **Motiles**, the only entities that exhibit motion with purpose. Resembling complex mechanisms more than living beings, Motiles are the size of small insects, but their structures are intricate, with wheels, pistons, and gears made of the same strange mineral. They move along the glass-like formations, their actions seemingly random, but collectively, they perform the city's maintenance—repairing crumbling buildings, cleaning the untouched dust from the Sentinels, and even tending to the Substrates, helping them grow.

All these entities coexist in a symbiotic relationship with the city and the eternal machine. They do not experience life or death as we know it, but rather, they simply are, and will always be, just like the city itself. They are not born, but emerge from the city's own need and memory. They do not die, but rather, they become dormant, their forms blending back into the city's structure until they are needed again. They are the city, and the city is them, bound together in an eternal, quiet, ancient weirdness, out of time, out of mind.
