In this peculiar, forgotten realm, where time and nature seem to hold no sway, there exist beings that are as much a part of the landscape as the mineral tiles and the crystalline 'plants'. They are not creatures as we understand them, for they are not born, do not grow, and do not die. Instead, they are eternal, unchanging, much like the world they inhabit.

These beings, let's call them the Tilarians, are both vast and minute, their forms sprawling across the tiles like complex, geometric tattoos. They are not physical in the conventional sense, but are more akin to patterns of energy, pulsating with the same resonant hum that fills the air. Their bodies, if we can call them that, are intricate webs of light and sound, interwoven with the quartz veins that pulse beneath the surface of the tiles. The Tilarians' forms shift subtly, their borders expanding and contracting rhythmically, as if they are breathing. Yet, this is not respiration as we know it, but rather a constant, rhythmic oscillation of their energy patterns.

Each Tilarian is unique, their forms differing greatly from one another. Some are compact, their energy densely packed into complex mandalas, while others are sprawling, their forms stretching out in delicate, lacy patterns, reminiscent of frost on a windowpane. Their colors vary too, ranging from the palest silver to the deepest gold, with every metallic hue in between. Despite their variations, there is a harmony to their forms, a sense of balance and symmetry that is echoed in the intricate mosaic of the tiles beneath them.

The silvery pools, defying any known physics, are integral to the Tilarians' existence. These pools are, in fact, not separate entities, but extensions of the Tilarians themselves. They are their senses, their means of perceiving and interacting with their environment. The pools' fractal patterns shift and change in response to the hum of the air, the resonance of the tiles, the presence of other Tilarians. This information is fed back to the Tilarians, allowing them to 'see' and 'hear' their world in a way that is utterly alien to any creature from our reality.

The Tilarians do not communicate in the conventional sense. There is no language, no exchange of words or signals. Instead, they resonate with each other, their energy patterns pulsating in complex, synchronized rhythms that convey more than words ever could. It's a constant, silent conversation, a harmony of existence that fills the air with a symphony of hums.

Despite their alien nature, the Tilarians are not without emotion or personality. Their resonance patterns convey a sense of calm, of contentment, of curiosity. They take pleasure in the harmonies of their world, in the rhythmic pulsing of their energy patterns, in the complex dance of their silvery pools. They are beings of pure energy, of pure resonance, living in a state of constant, harmonious existence.

Yet, there's a sadness to them too, a sense of longing. They are aware of the vast, empty expanse beyond their hidden realm, of the worlds and realities that exist outside their own. But they are trapped, their energy patterns interwoven with the tiles, their existence tied to the resonance of their world. They cannot leave, cannot experience the universe beyond their own. So they resonate with their longing, their desire for the unknown, their curiosity about the unseen. It's a haunting melody, a symphony of yearning that underscores the hum of their existence, a testament to their eternal, unchanging lives in this forgotten, secret place.
