In the Humming Cisterns, life is not merely present, but vocal, constantly echoing its existence through the labyrinthine chambers. The inhabitants of this realm are a symphony of diverse forms, each contributing a unique verse to the eternal song that resonates through the moist, warm air.

The most pervasive of these residents are the **gastropods**, their numbers beyond count, wriggling over the slick, condensed-packed humus. They are not mere snails, but ancient, adapted creatures, their shells twisted into intricate spirals that echo the convoluted paths of the Cisterns themselves. Their colors blend with the earthy tones of the walls, ranging from deep rust to pale ochre, giving them a camouflage that is both protective and aesthetically harmonious. Their constant, gentle scraping is the bass note of the Cisterns' symphony, a steady, soothing rhythm that underscores all other sounds. They are the custodians of the realm, their mucous trails leaving paths of nourishment for the mosses and lichens, while their waste contributes to the rich humus. Their slow, methodical existence is a testament to the eternal cycle of life and decay that governs the Humming Cisterns. The gastropods converge a long living system bound by the very patterns of water that lead to their survival. The keeping of trinkets, perhaps little offerings to appeasing greater spirits, so a symbiotic conversation may continue; the organic architecture behind these denizens reveals the full communications of the Cisterns; they seem to pass along their messages at a slow, slow rate that the Cisterns' suffering ceases. The connection every being in this beast is very symbiotic. It seems as each is a root or organ of a singular megafauna left to decompose and reform anew.

The chambers are dotted with multitudinous **invertebrates**, each a marvel of adaptation. There are centipedes, their legs elongated and feathery, skimming the surface of the shallow pools with a grace that belies their frightening appearance. There are spiders, pale as the stone, their webs glistening with moisture, trapping the condensation and funneling it into the cracks and crevices where the roots of the mushrooms await. They seem like busy workers attending to their functions in a grand workshop, equal parts organic and inorganic. The numerous movements of the insects and the gastropods convinced the groaning of the walls, the heavy burps of the megafauna they compose, twisted into movement as these roots and limbs clear every path they may be compelled to. These trails seem Ceaseless. The signs of their movements allude to an almost dictated rhythmic bustling. Then there are larvae with glowing tails, pale blinks toward the dome crystals; these all reveal the Cisterns' truth and its upcoming byproducts. The larvae seem to contribute musically, lights wavelengths flickering in music, answers to the echoes; which are really questions.

The trails of the fish every day slowly eat away at the architecture, repurposing the entire pattern of their slowly evolving flow. They are both predators and prey, feeding on the smaller invertebrates and, in turn, becoming meals for the **bats**.

The **bats** of the Humming Cisterns are unlike any found on the surface. Their forelimbs are incredibly elongated, ending in hooked claws that allow them to crawl along the ceilings with an eerie, fluid grace. Their fur is almost luminescent in its paleness, their eyes mere vestiges, replaced by sophisticated ears that can detect the minutest of sounds. The pink crystals forming the ceiling glow, holding births of stalagmites, and some of the more disturbing entities seem to pugnaciously oppose the development of the fruit of the Cisterns these creatures thread and grow, with the bats calling out to the crystal passages revealing previous rooms currently overgrown, these creatures crawl over complex echoes.
