They carry goods and cargo, their forms adorned with mosses and other growths that thrive in the city's unique ecosystem. There are also the **Geometers**, herds of floating beings that resemble complex geometric shapes, their forms shifting and changing as they drift through the rail-sheets, seemingly driven by an unseen purpose.

They seem to serve a purpose, their roots delving into cracks and crevices, creating hanging biomes that contribute to the city's ecosystem. They are cared for by the **Botanists**, humans who have adapted to the city's oddness, their eyes reflecting the bright lights of the city, their skin marked with the same iridescent patterns found on the Lumenplants. They move with a quiet grace, their steps light and sure as they tend to the city's unique vegetation.

Their movements are slow and deliberate, their forms towering over the other inhabitants. They communicate in a language of hums and vibrations, their thoughts intertwined with the city's underlying heartbeat. They are the city's builders, their constant labor contributing to its relentless expansion.

There are also the **Shifters**, beings who embody the city's palpable sense of weirdness. Their forms are mutable, changing when you're not looking, their shapes fluid and ever-shifting. They move through the city like ghosts, their steps silent, their forms ephemeral. They are the city's dreamers, their thoughts shaping the city's reality, contributing to its constant state of flux.

Finally, there are the **Chronists**, beings who embody the city's unique relationship with time. Their forms are marked with symbols that seem to drip like the intermittent rain, pooling in places and evaporating in others. They move with a sense of deliberate urgency, their steps echoing the city's own rhythms and tides of interstitial progress. They are the city's historians, their thoughts intertwined with its past, present, and future, guiding its relentless, ever-rising growth.
