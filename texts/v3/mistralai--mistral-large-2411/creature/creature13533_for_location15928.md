In the heart of the Chasm of Echoing Shadows, a dense, misty symphony of life thrums in the near perpetual darkness. This is not a place where the warmth of sun-kissed creatures dwells, but rather a sanctuary for beings adapted to the cool, echoing cathedral of stone, sustained by the ancient harmony of the ever-flowing elements.

Though the chasm is awash with myriad forms of life, they are not the beasts and birds of the sunlit world. Instead, they are creatures forged from the depths of biological novelty; shadows given flesh and sentience. Let us begin by examining the faint, eery luminescence cast by the lichen tapestries which adorn the walls of the chasm. This radiation, faint as it is, supports a multitude of creatures. Let us meet three of them:

First, there is the blind, albino *skitter-shrimp.* These translucent, scuttling creatures click and clang ornately across the stony chasm surfaces. Their antennae bristle with extraordinarily sensitive hairs that perceive the world through the littlest vibrations and chemical traces. From time to time, the shrimp crawl forth to consume and farm the luminous lichen, propagating its spreading glow. Their eyes, though vestigial and sightless, gleam like tiny mother-of-pearl moons in the gloom, hinting at the developement of some deep dreaming organ that allows for some sort of nonconventional perception; a self-awareness of sorts one might deem "crude consciousness". Their tap-tap-tapping across the chasm walls serves as a constant communication medium, and their antennae drums form ripples in the strange, quasi-viscous air, forming temporary and extraordinary patterns, a sort of tactile poetry only they and a few mosses can fathom.

Above, droning in the mist, there are creatures called *whisper-flies*. They are similar in appearance to the glow-flies of the world above, but their bodies are much larger, and their insides are starkly bioluminescent. In tandem, their myriad fleeting bodies create an otherworldly light show against the walls of lichen, as though stars waltz with the thunderous clouds of some distant cosmos. These luminescent bugs are the sole pollinators of the dimly lit, alien flora that calls the Chasm home. A colony of whisper-flies can light up a entire craggy ledge with an intensity reminiscent of dappled moonlight. Their collective hum is both ethereal and eerie, creating echoing melodies with bizarre, somnolent harmonies. The flies, though slightly inquisitive towards other fauna should a large organism find itself in the chasm, are largely docile, the spectral green-blue glow of their pillowy abdomens casting eerily evocative trails that wisp through the mist like the ghosts of ancient serpents.

Finally, there is the *bladder-beast.* A soft-bodied, gelatinous, floating sentience, reminiscent of a transparent jellyfish, its entire body‑called an umbrella‑laced with darker channels, within which the lifeblood of the organism can be seen pulsating aglow, bared to the open world. Moving through the chasm with a pulsating, rhythmic dance, feeding on the microscopic plankton drifting in the mists, tracing invisible currents with their terminal tendrils, these quivering bulbs drift and hover through the mists pressuring the density to leave clouds swirling in their wake. Their only visible organs are internal squirming crimson bladders, their use unknown, wrapped in tangles of blacklights which cast spinning orbs that resemble the dim glow of two moons in perpetual eclipse. The bladder-beast is a peerless, malevolent predator, a half-sentient tidal work of wobbly, diaphanous flesh, drifting, shivering, its sea of bifurcated bacterially derived flagella rivaling sorcerous terrors of the abyss. Its "rhapsody" is a sound unlike anything else in the chasm, echoing ghastly, clutching backbeats of stuttering microtrash-scratching, writhing, sighing, scouring, snapping, clapping, gnashing, peeling, samadhi.

Thousands and thousands of other species can be observed thriving in the darkness of the chasm. Set as it is in the mountain, hidden away and isolated from the rest of the world, this scene serves well as a persevered, primitive example of its own, a revelation of life sculpted by forces not prevalent today, how spirits wrought in a remote silence of ancient dreams might have danced in a world forgotten, or one yet to be found.
