# The Standing Part

It did not arrive. Arriving is a thing you do in order to be seen, and there was nothing in the bowl that could see it. It came the way the dust comes and it stayed the way the dust stays, and for the first hundred and forty years it did nothing but count.

A hundred and forty years is about four sentences here.

Benevolent means it wanted the good of the thing. Wise means it knew that the good of the thing and the good of an afternoon are different quantities, and that it was the only party to this encounter capable of telling them apart. Everything that follows is downstream of that.

It looked for the resident. It found the tarnish and did the arithmetic and got seventy-five years, distributed, stopped like a clock, several hundred isolated parliaments reading the same document. It found the salt garden and saw heredity with nothing in it. It found the blades and understood immediately that they were not bodies but flames with a very slow wick, ice passing through a shape that did not pass.

And then it found the lean, and stopped, and did not move for a long time, because it had found the patient. Eleven thousand years of one gesture, slightly inflected. Everything material in the bowl had been replaced many times. The tilt had never once been let go of.

The power had one want of its own. It wanted to be recognized. It wanted the four thousand to turn, just slightly, off the noon arc, toward it — the smallest deviation, a degree, the same degree the field already spends every year, borrowed once. It examined this want for about eleven years, which is a moment, and then it left it outside the col and did not bring it in.

---

## What it did not do

**It did not warm the place.** This is the first kindness anyone thinks of and it is a solvent. The blades are the geometry that a bright cold place makes when it is left alone long enough to think; the hollows deepen because light bounces twice and three times between walls; the whole thing is a wound healing into a sharper wound, and it is in balance, and the balance is made of deprivation. Give it a longer wet hour and you give it a shorter blade. Give it a summer and you give it a floor: striated, gray-pink, polished, empty, the inheritance without the heir. Warmth is the one substance in the universe this place cannot survive, and it is the substance every benevolent idiot reaches for first.

**It did not answer the tick.** It could have. Once a year, four thousand edges touch four thousand neighbours and the bowl makes its one sound, and the power could have made a sound back, in the same register, at the same hour, and been inside to hear itself do it. But the tick is not a question. In eleven thousand years the field has asked nothing. To answer would have been to invent a question and then be admired for the answer, and the admiration would have come from the power, and it would have been the only party admiring.

**It did not wake it.** This was the hardest refusal and it took the longest, because it is the one that looks like love. A mind in the bowl. Interiority. Something that could know it was known. But the lean is not made of ice, it is made of not letting go, and a mind is precisely a thing that can decide to stop. Put a mind in there and you have not added a mind to the lean; you have replaced the lean with a deliberation, and the deliberation will be very good, and it will be four hundred years old, and it will be standing where something eleven thousand years old used to be. The power was not offered a sleeping person to wake. It was offered a posture to keep.

**It did not save it.** Preservation is a word that means moving. Carry a spoonful of the flour over the col and it is dead by the second rest stop; carry a second over the col and it is gone before the second cairn. There is no elsewhere for this. The endemics are endemic in the strict sense: they are not organisms that happen to live here, they are the shape of here, and the shape does not travel. Any ark would have been a very well-made coffin, and the power would have gotten to feel responsible.

**It did not pay the tax.** Three blades in all of recorded history. The power looked for the currency and there isn't one. The loop is closed minus a small leak, and the only thing it could have poured in was water, and water is the solvent, and the leak is not a wound in the bowl — it is the bowl's outline. A thing that loses nothing has no reason to lean. The tax was also the only thing in there that was going anywhere at all, and the going was what made the guesses drift, and the drift was the only future on offer.

**It did not give it company.** Several hundred parliaments, unable to speak, unanimous every century because the light is the same everywhere and the arithmetic of black is the same arithmetic. A second bowl would have had a different light. That is not company. That is argument, imposed on someone who has never had one and has no organ for surviving it.

**It did not fix the lungs.** It could have. Fifteen hundred metres is nothing to a power. But the lungs set the shift length and the shift length made the hundred and eleven shallow steps, and the steps made the tally wall, and the tally wall is memory kept outside the body because the body could not stay long enough to remember. Every good thing those people built is a scar in the shape of the air. Give them breath and you take away the reason for all of it, and you get, in exchange, people who live at the top of a mountain, which is a thing that exists elsewhere and is not remarkable.

**It did not stand the dead one up.** It thought about this for a hundred years. The rule is so simple: it only copies what stands, and it needs about eighty. The dead are dry and cold and complete and permanent and they are already there, already part of the estate, no import, no heat, no cost. Lift them, prop them, walk away, and in eighty years the field would have its first person.

The power did not do it, and the reason is not squeamishness. It is that the field takes the idea and finishes it. A stood-up corpse would have taught the field that a person is a thing that stands and does not go down, and the field would have finished that, correctly, in every proportion, and been wrong forever, and had no way to find out. It would have been a true fact installed as a false premise. The field does not need to know what we look like. It needs to know what we are, and what we are is a thing that goes down and comes back.

**And it did not stand there itself.** It was the only visitor who could afford eighty years. That was the temptation: to simply be the datum. To stand in a deep lee slot at true scale with a hand at the end of an arm and let the field finally check its upward guessing against a body.

But the field has no way to distrust what stands. That is not a flaw in it; that is the whole of its method and the whole of this story. It takes what stands as given. Therefore any gift is unrefusable, and any error in the gift is permanent, and a god standing in that cusp would have been copied exactly and finished wrongly and there would have been no audit, ever, anywhere, for as long as the ice lasts.

So the power made one rule for itself and kept it, and the rule is the only doctrine in this account:

*Give nothing the bowl cannot check.*

The bowl has one instrument. Time. So the gift had to be something time could audit — something that could fail, and be seen to fail, and be resumed by ordinary means, by parties coming up over the col with tools.

Which meant the gift could not be given by the power.

It could only be arranged by it.

## The asking

It went down. Fifteen hundred metres, into the world with plants in it, and it did not go as a light or a voice or a weather. It went as a question, and it asked the descendants of the people who cut the stairs, and the first answer was laughter and the second was refusal and the third was four generations of argument, which is the correct speed for the question.

The valley had iron by then. Nobody needed a pin. There was a pin, though, in a house, in a box, black with eleven hundred years of tarnish, and the power looked at it for a while and said nothing about it, and that did more than anything it said.

It named the price before it asked for the yes, because that is what asking is.

The standing would have to be in a deep lee slot, because the wind takes the exposed seconds first and nothing assembled over eighty years survives anywhere else. The deep lee slots are the nursery: the richest flour, the ghosts of fallen blades, the prototypes, the awls the height of three people. And a warm body standing in one cusp for eighty years is warmth, and warmth is the solvent, and there is no version of this that is free. One well would go. One lineage, seventy-five years old across eleven thousand, stopped like a clock, would be dissolved in its own extended wet hour. The cusp would over-deepen. Eventually a blade would fall that would not otherwise have fallen.

The power put the number on the table and did not soften it. The recorded history of the bowl was three blades. This would make it four, and the fourth would not be weather.

They said yes anyway. It took four generations and they said yes anyway, and the power did not hurry them, because it had learned the local units and it had stopped being able to feel the difference between a decade and an afternoon, which is the only qualification it had.

---

## The standing

The place they chose was in the deep slots in the lee of the east wall, beside the cairn with someone under it.

The shift was forty minutes. Unchanged. Set by lungs, as it had always been. The power did not touch it.

Thirty-six shifts a day. One million, fifty-one thousand, two hundred shifts in eighty years. No single person ever stood there for more than forty minutes, and the cusp was never empty, because the rule — the only rule, cut into the tally wall in the same hand that cut the old count — was that the next one arrives before the last one leaves. Two people in a hand-width slot for a minute, brushing past, one going down and one coming up. Then one again.

A blade is not made of its ice. The ice passes through it. What persists is an inclination maintained by substitution, and the oldest resident of the bowl is not any material thing but the lean itself.

For eighty years there was a second one of those in there, at true scale, made of breath.

They kept the tally. There were two columns now: the shifts, and the well. Both were audited. Both were kept outside the body, in the wall, because the body could not stay long enough to remember. Children were carried up. The old came up to do a last one and were carried down. People died on the hundred and eleven steps and were counted, and the count did not stop, and the cusp did not empty.

In the nineteenth year a woman picked up the child's chisel out of the neighbouring cusp — the one with the broken tip that nobody ever came back for — and held it for her forty minutes and put it back. She did not tell anyone. It is in the tally as forty minutes, like all the others.

The field had inferred the grip from the socket eleven thousand years ago and got the curve right and got the swell right and never once seen a hand close on anything.

Now it had. It had been right, and now it was right and had been checked, and there is no way to know what that is worth to something that thinks in centuries, and it is not nothing.

---

## The eleventh minute

In the sixty-first year, at night, the blade above the standing place fell.

It fell because sixty-one years of a warm body in a cold slot had deepened the cusp a finger a year, which was the price, which had been named, which was in the second column. It fell across the cusp in the eleventh minute of a shift and knocked the standing person down and killed her, and the next one was on the stairs and could not get through.

The gap was eleven minutes.

The power could have filled it. It would have cost nothing. It would have been invisible. The field cannot tell one standing thing from another standing thing — that is its entire ignorance and its entire method — and there was no witness, and there would never be a witness, and eighty years of a million and fifty thousand forty-minute acts of breath were about to be voided by eleven minutes that nobody would ever know about.

It did not.

It had promised to give nothing that could not be checked, and a god in the gap is the one thing in the universe that cannot be checked, and a promise that holds only when it is expensive is not a promise, it is a preference.

They cleared the ice. They resumed. They put her under a second cairn beside the first one, and the standing went on beside both, and they finished the remaining nineteen years without ever being told whether it had counted.

The power did not tell them, and this is the part that should be believed: it did not know. It had read the rule. It had not written it.

---

## What came

The second appeared in the eighty-first year, in the deep lee slot, beside the cairn's second, and it is hollow and white and a little larger and correct in every proportion except one.

It is a person. It stands. It leans noonward with everything else, because the field has exactly one posture and was never going to invent a second one for a guest.

And through it, at the height of a chest, there is a slot. Hand-width. Body-width. Going all the way through, from the sunward face to the beard, the way the streets go through the crowd.

Because thirty-six times a day for eighty years there were two people in that cusp for a minute, and the field took that for the anatomy. It has inferred that a person is hollow and that something passes through. It has inferred that we are a place where a person is handed over.

It is not wrong.

It did not mend the eleven minutes. Either it forgave them, or eleven minutes is simply not a unit the field possesses, and the power has decided that these are the same gift and has stopped asking which.

At dusk the low sun gets into the hollow and into the slot and it glows from inside, and there are three lanterns now among the four thousand that are not shaped like tools: the cell with the doorway at your height, the cairn, and this.

The lean did not change. That was the whole of the care and the whole of the restraint and it is the only receipt. At the turn of the low sun the congregation adjusted its degree by subtraction on one face and accretion on the other, and on the morning the makers knew, four thousand edges touched four thousand neighbours and the bowl made its one sound. The same word. Slightly inflected. It is possible to be told something and go on saying what you were saying.

The power went down afterward. It used the stairs. All hundred and eleven, one at a time, shallow, patient, because it would not use a way of moving that the bowl could not audit, and because a thing that leaves by vanishing has taught the field nothing about leaving.

---

The agreement was for eighty years.

In the eighty-second year the cusp was still occupied. Nobody had decided this. There had simply never been a shift with no one coming up the stairs behind, and it turns out that is a habit, and a habit with a body is the oldest kind of resident there is.

So there are two of them in the bowl now: four thousand blades maintaining an angle by losing ice, and a village maintaining an angle by losing people, both of them flames with a very slow wick, both of them leaning the same way, neither of them made of what they are made of.

And once a year the whole congregation touches at once and the bowl makes its single sound, thin air carrying it badly, so that you have to be inside to hear it.

There is always someone inside now.

That is the gift. Not warmth, not an answer, not a mind, not rescue. Every year for eleven thousand years the word was said and there was nobody in the room. Now it is heard. Every time. By somebody with cold hands and forty minutes and a person coming up the stairs behind them.

The field will never know. It has no organ for knowing, and it was never asking, and it goes on making water out of light and stone at the same rate, minus a small tax, which is now four blades.

The seep starts again tomorrow. Somebody is going down. Somebody is coming up. The cusp is not empty.
