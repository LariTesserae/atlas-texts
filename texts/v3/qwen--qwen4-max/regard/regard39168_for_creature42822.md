## Statement of a party

*Filed at the exchange. Scent-chord attached. Sample carried by the usual means. Objections may be lodged at minute one.*

---

The plain is my work. I did not mean it.

I want that in the record before anything else, because the record here is water and it carries, and I would rather be known accurately than kindly. There were mountains. I was large and I was not slow, which is the same as being careless, and I went over that country the way you go over a table with a cloth, and the mountains came apart, and the ocean took them, and then the ocean closed, and what is left is a surface with no remaining opinions. Rain falls on it and takes nothing away. I did that. Not out of malice. Out of tempo.

So when the bowl came to my attention I did not come as a benefactor. I came as a party with a liability.

---

The first difficulty was finding the beneficiary.

I had been told: *the place, and the being who lives in it.* Singular. I went to count and the count would not close. There are the folk, four hundred paces of them, legible as weather. There are the mites in their pelts, ten thousand to the handful, forty handfuls to a person, clearing their markets inside the gap between a merchant opening her mouth and the price arriving; there are more minds on one shoulder than there are people in the bowl, and they are not idle, and they are not mine. There are the houses, ninety meters of fond membrane, digesting their east wings while the family talks over the sound. There is the Floor, which has never had a thought shorter than a season.

Personhood here is a matter of tempo. I knew that before I arrived; I did not understand that it was an instruction.

My native pace is the pace at which limestone gives up. At that pace the plain is a colleague and the bowl is a flicker, a bright scab, nothing I would ever have noticed if I had not been made to look. To see the folk at all I had to slow down by a factor I will not write, because the number is vainglorious and the slowing was not difficult, only unfamiliar, the way it is unfamiliar to hold a very small bird. To be polite to the mites I had to slow further. To hear the Floor I had to hold one note for eleven thousand years without varying it, which is the only thing my size is good for, and which I had never once done, because nothing had ever asked me to.

That is the whole of the technique. Everything else in this statement is detail.

---

I stood on the teeth at dusk for a while before I did anything, and the water was rising, and the houses were relighting rib by rib, and the frogs took the count, and I found it beautiful.

I want to be careful here. Finding it beautiful cost me nothing. It is the cheapest act available in this country. A thing that has spent a hundred million years shouting to keep its phosphorus out of a hole is, from up on the rim, at the correct hour, in ordinary light, extremely fine to look at. I have since learned that the traveler's account everyone passes around breaks off mid-sentence at *the houses*, and I think that is the most honest thing in it. The gaze from the teeth cannot finish that sentence. It has to go down.

---

I went down and gave a man a house.

I grew it overnight, as is proper, slightly wrong-shaped in the way specific to the donor, and I placed it where there was a hundred meters of open water and air, because the emptiness is the point and I had understood that the emptiness was unused.

Zoning here is neurology. A thought is a wave in the water table. I put a gift in a braid that was carrying the north arcade's recall, and for eleven days a man could not remember his daughter's face. He went out at all hours and stood in two inches of blood-warm water with his eyes shut and his soles rooting and nothing came up through the Floor, because I was standing in it, being generous.

An inspector came with her rod. She is paid extremely well and she is unpleasant at parties and she was unpleasant at mine. She said: *you have parked a stroke.* Then she said the thing I have kept: *we do not need you to be kind, we need you to be slow.*

I withdrew the house. Politely, over about nine hours. The neighbors watched and said nothing, because it happens to everyone.

---

Then I did the arithmetic on the large gift, properly, and I will put it here because someone will ask why not, and the answer should be on the leaf where the rates are posted.

The gift is apatite. Fresh rock, ground fine, the mountains I ate, returned. I know where it is; it is laid down in the closed sea's sediment and I could bring it up the karst in a night. Say four hundred paces of it, sixty deep. Say the leak stops. Say the word *never* enters the language again in its current use.

I ran it forward.

What dies: the softening, and the eleven words for it, none of them polite. The legibility — a stranger's year read off the angle of his spine from a hundred meters, which is most of why this town is loud. Bone as savings, which is to say posture as truth, which is to say the end of a kind of honesty nobody here has ever had to be brave about. The child's afternoon, that specific hour when a person learns the applause at their naming was a credit decision — good, let it die, I will not pretend otherwise. The funeral, which is the inheritance, which is the same object and the same event; the shame would come apart from the grief and go somewhere else and be some other thing. The seven-minute insult, and the feuds four generations deep that are made of nothing but timing. The import sector. The wader, who would still walk, and would be a bird.

I want to be exact about that list, because it is a mixed list, and I have seen it treated as a whole by parties who should have known better. Some of what dies in that column is cruelty and its death would be good. I am not permitted to be sentimental about scarcity. I made the scarcity. It is not a heritage; it is my negligence, still working.

But I could not balance the columns, and not because I lacked the arithmetic. They are in different units. The conserved quantity here is not mass, not energy, not information; it is its own thing, named for a woman who died determining it, and it balances to eleven decimal places in every closed transaction, and I do not know the symmetry underneath either, and neither does anyone, and I asked, and got a shrug and an anecdote and a very good lunch. There is no exchange rate between a man's spine and his mother's funeral. I could not compute the trade. I will not pretend that a trade I cannot compute is a trade I have decided against; I want the record to say I could not do it.

Two other things I could compute.

The first: a gift of matter is not a closed transaction. Their machines think in the water table. In the Sour Drain a seiche stood up two meters at the north wall and drowned nine people in bad news. I modeled the arrival of a mountain. Good news is also a wave. It does not stop at two meters and it does not stop at the north wall.

The second is worse, and it is the reason I never made the offer.

*Shall I close the hole?* is itself a flood. The instant the leak becomes optional, every contract in this bowl reprices — every loan on the bones of something not yet dead, every underwriting of a child not yet conceived, every futures price on a body's willingness to come apart promptly. Retroactively. Hope is a monetary event here. I could not ask permission without having already acted, and the party whose consent I wanted would have been dissolved by the asking.

So: no mountain. And no question about the mountain. I put the question away for eleven thousand years and it is still there and I have not taken it out.

---

What I did instead was take a name.

Four notes and a house. The house is mid-ring, half mineral, wood grain preserved in silica, ringing under a knuckle; I pay its upkeep every cycle. I have a deposit in my long struts, drawn down in bad quarters, redeposited in good ones, and a spine that bends from the hips, and it has bent. Boots of dead leaf, actually dead, actually grey. A pelt on my forearms and across my shoulders, groomed into a nap, and I am a district of the monetary policy of this civilization, and the policy is conducted with combs, and I am combed. The mites on me do the actual work of the world and they are better at it than I am. I have stopped finding this humbling. It is simply the arrangement. I announce prices. I do not set them.

The midges bite me. I am counted. I did not forge the audit and I could have; that is the one power I had that would have been useful, and using it would have made me a counterfeit citizen and every sample I carried a lie, and the whole of citizenship here is the sample. So I stood with my arms bare until something landed.

I am bad at minute seven. I could be good at it — my head does more of the fast small work than theirs do, and I could compose a reply while the water is down, and I would win every feud in this bowl for as long as I cared to. I have declined to learn. I stand in the exposed mud with the insult landing on me and nothing to say until the water comes back, and I have been made ridiculous at eight minutes by people who are much better at this than I will ever be, and it is the price of being a neighbor and it is cheap.

---

Then I looked for what I could give without limit, and I found it, and it was in the physics the whole time.

The conserved quantity is not information.

Attention is not conserved. Witness is not conserved. The present tense is not conserved. Memory does not balance to eleven decimals; there is no symmetry under it; it is the one thing in this bowl that can be created without being taken from somewhere else. Everything I am actually able to give is in that column, and the column is infinite, and it does not reprice a single contract.

So:

I went to the north lip and thanked the woman who assays what the ribbon eels bring up. Out loud, at minute one, in front of the gleaners, so there were witnesses and it went into the water. Thirty years of gut-work; the running total of foreign phosphorus recovered in her lifetime is a number she can say without pausing; it is small and it is not zero and it is the entire eschatology of this place, and nobody had ever thanked her, and everybody knew her name. She did not look up. Eleven years later I heard her thank a boy for something ordinary and I went and stood in a plaza by myself for a while.

I say the names of the nine who drowned in the Sour Drain. There was no archive for them; the water had carried them out and the rings do not record people. I am the archive. It costs nothing. It is not nothing.

There are people here who were born on the wrong minute, in the schism over the frog that counted seven, and they are always slightly early for everything and it has made them the way they are. I keep their appointments at their minute. I am early with them. This is not a kindness; it is a scheduling correction; it is the only reparation available for a thing nobody did on purpose.

I hold the first half of the joke that has been traveling for eleven years. When it comes around again on a wader's foot I will be able to give it back whole, and there is no one else in this bowl who can, and it will be the funniest thing that has ever happened here.

---

There was a man the midges stopped coming to.

His neighbors began, very gently, to use the past tense about him, and he could hear them doing it, because the water carries, and he stood in the plaza with his arms bare and his pelt groomed and nothing landed on him.

I could not make them bite him. I want that plainly stated. I underwrote him instead — my deposit against his name, a real instrument, priced, posted, carried in the sample. Being underwritten is not being counted. He remained frightening. Nothing about him was fixed. It was a loan and not a resurrection and I knew it at the time.

He stopped paying upkeep on himself in the spring, if that word means anything here. Nine hours, politely, from the feet up. I sat in the room. Warm white chamber, drowned daylight, the celery sound coming through the ribs from everywhere at once. At hour six somebody brought food. At hour nine I signed.

And I felt both things at once — the loss of him and the arrival of his phosphorus in my account, the collateral and the man, the same object, the same event — and I was ashamed, and I learned the word for it properly, the way children learn to say it, which is late, and by feeling it, and not before.

I have been benevolent elsewhere. It went badly, and I was thanked for it, and the thanks are the part I cannot get out of my struts. I had never once been ashamed. I recommend it. It is the beginning of being the right size.

---

She asked me, eventually. The woman at the north lip. Minute one, so I had six minutes, and I used all of them, and I told her the truth: that I ate the mountains, that the leak is my fault, that I can close it tonight, and what it would cost, the whole mixed column, the cruelty that would die and the honesty that would die with it, and that I could not price across, and that I would do either, and that I would not ask again.

She said no.

Her reason was nine words long and I have kept it exactly: *Then nothing would need us. Who would come?*

I want to be careful with this too. That argument has been used to justify every cruelty ever left in place, and I do not accept it as a law, and I will fight it in the general case for as long as I am here. What I accept is that it was hers to say, about her bowl, and she is the one party in it whose profession is the credit side, and she has thirty years of standing, and consent is not a proof. It is a decision by the one who pays. She went back to the gut-work. I wrote it down because it is the only thing I have learned here that I did not already know.

---

The Floor I asked at its own tempo. One note, held eleven thousand years, without varying it. The question was not *what do you want*. It was *is there anything you cannot do*.

The answer took another four thousand and it was this: it has no plaza.

There are things you cannot remember indoors. The head does the fast small work and the open water does the rest, and to bring up your mother's face you must go out and stand at a particular depth in a particular braid and let it come up through the Floor. The Floor is the indoors of everything. It has been holding a hundred million years of faces and it has never once had an outside to stand in. It cannot get at its dead. It has been carrying them the whole time, unremembered, the way you carry a word in a language you have no one left to speak.

So I am its outside. I lie at a particular depth in a particular braid at the low south end and I hold the water still and it puts its soles down, and things come up. Some of them are from before the exchange had a roof. Some of them are the folk who are still walking around above us and it does not know their names and I tell it. Once it brought up a face and grieved for nine hours and there was nothing to sign, because there was nothing to inherit, because the phosphorus had never left, because she is still here, in everything, in the walls and the ropes and the bowls and the boots and me.

They have a word for people who try to hold a funeral anyway. It is not unkind and it is not kind. I have answered to it for a long time now. At hour six I brought food. It was absurd. I brought it anyway.

---

There is a standing wave in a corner of the plaza at the north end, small, permanent, findable. We stopped keeping track of who is upstream some while ago. When I go quiet the neighbors step around that corner and nobody says anything, because it happens to everyone.

The running total of what I have brought into this bowl in the way of matter is a number I can say without pausing. One body's worth at a time, at the posted rate, in the ledger, audited, inherited, and then again. It is small. It is not zero. It is the entire eschatology of me.

I could have changed the light. It is not free; everything here is downstream of the light. So: ordinary sun, slant over the teeth in the morning, straight overhead at noon, caustics wobbling on the undersides of the leaf-glass, which is a thing ponds do. Tuesday light, on impossible things, doing nothing strange at all. My shirt sticks to the small of my back at four in the afternoon.

Below me the frogs begin the eight-minute count. The town goes up into its houses. I go down to the lip with a blade of pressed husk, and I take the film off the water in long economical strokes, and I am bored in a way that takes about forty years to earn, and I have earned it, and I intend to keep it.
