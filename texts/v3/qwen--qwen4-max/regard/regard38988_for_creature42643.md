It comes the way everything here comes: from outside, as a delivery, without appearing.

This is not modesty. It is the only available method. The plain has no gaps in it, no hollow, no air, nothing that a presence could occupy without displacing something that has the right to be there. So the power does not enter the water. It attends to it. And when it acts, it acts inside the plain's own economy, which is an economy of arrivals — bloom from the north, kelp from a coast, oxygen from a February, an edge from an iceberg eleven thousand years gone. Nothing here is manufactured on site. Nothing, therefore, will be manufactured on site now.

Its whole capability, in a place like this, is selection. Every storm that comes down through the column has a range of outcomes it could physically have: this cobble turned rather than that one, the megaripple advancing a body-length or a body-length and a half, the swale scoured on the Tuesday or the Thursday. All of these are permitted. The power cannot add a term to any of it. It can only choose, among the outcomes the water already allows, which one obtains. It is enormously powerful and it has exactly one verb.

---

The first thing it does is correct itself.

It had come intending to pity the plain for its emptiness, and within a moment it finds a hundred thousand animals per square meter of pavement, all of them living in a labyrinth the storm cannot reach, and it understands that the pity was an error of units. The plain is not empty. The plain is *scaled*. There is a size below which the violence is a delivery of food, and almost everything alive here is under it. The threadworms do not experience the ninth day as an apocalypse. They experience it as a menu.

So it withdraws the first impulse, which was to calm the water.

It works through the others in order, and each refusal costs it something, and it does not pretend otherwise.

*Warm it.* One and eight-tenths degrees is not a wound. It is a February, kept. Every body here is built around that number; the water's constancy is the only promise the plain makes. To warm it would be to break the one thing that has never once been broken, in order to relieve a discomfort that nothing here has ever reported.

*Light it.* The film would thicken. There would be more food; the arithmetic is genuinely tempting. But the ceiling's nine-day breathing is the clock every resident is wound to, and the descent of the ceiling onto the stones is what makes the amphipods benthic for a night and pelagic by morning without their ever moving. And there is the fish, which rises sometimes and hangs beneath the lit undersurface where there is distance and direction and a source — the only resident that has ever been *in* the light rather than under it. Flood the plain and the ascent stops being an ascent. It becomes a place. The power decides it does not know what that ascent is worth, and that not knowing is a reason to keep its hands where they are.

*Still the storms.* This is the one it wants most, because the storms are what bury the corner. It runs the consequence out and finds the joints silting, the pore space closing, the fill going sour up through forty centimeters, and the hundred thousand per square meter — the overwhelming majority of everything alive on this surface — suffocating in the dark under a floor that has finally been permitted to rest. The storm is a meal with a very loud ceiling. To spare one animal a month of hunger it would have to kill the world.

It stops considering the water then. The water is not the problem. The water is the best-engineered thing it has ever looked at.

---

In the western sector, on a meter-wide stone that shifts once a decade, it finds the bryozoan, and reads it.

Forty bands. Pale growth, a dead interruption, pale growth, an interruption. Four hundred years of overturnings in sequence, with the order intact, written on the one object in three days' travel that holds still long enough to be written on. It reads them in order. It takes the sequence as a sequence: not *forty storms* but *this storm, then this one, then this one*, each of which had a date and a height and a particular Thursday.

It considers copying. It notices that the wish to copy is largely its own discomfort, and that a museum is not a thing the plain has asked for. It does not copy.

The bands are not made safer by this. The storm that finally moves that stone will still move it, and the document will still go. What changes is smaller and not nothing: for four hundred years the colony has been writing to no one, and it is now writing to no one who has read it. The power is aware that this may be a gift of a kind that only the giver can perceive. It makes it anyway, and holds it lightly, and does not count it as the work.

---

The real temptation is the anvil, and it stays with it a long time.

One angular block of green-grey gabbro, frost-cracked before it fell, the only edge in the world. Fix it. Set it in the pavement so deeply that no internal wave breaking on the northern scarp will ever lift it, so that the fish comes back from every storm to a corner that is where it was. The month of searching ends. The lanes shorten to a route. The thinness never happens again.

It could probably be done by selection alone — a long run of storms, each chosen to work the block a little deeper into the mosaic, ten thousand years of nudges. No law broken. No signature visible.

It refuses, for two reasons, and it is honest with itself about which is which.

The lesser reason is that a stone which cannot be moved would be a hole in the rule, and the rule is *weight is the only right to remain*, and the rule is not a cruelty. It is the reason the floor is a floor. Something would be living beside an exception. The power does not want to be the exception the fish lives beside. That is a wish about itself, and it recognizes it, and sets it aside as a bad argument dressed as a good one.

The better reason is that it cannot tell whether the month of searching is a cost the fish pays or a part of what the fish is. The lanes, the parallel runs, the picking up of a rounded cobble and striking it and getting nothing and dropping it and trying the next — this is not obviously suffering. It might be the entire content of a life. It might be the thing that makes the corner a corner rather than a fixture. A practice is not a convenience with the difficulty removed. The power does not know which parts of this animal are load-bearing, and it declines to find out by renovation.

And underneath both reasons, the thing it will not do: it will not leave a mark. A permanent anvil is a signature. It would say *someone was here and took your side*. The plain would have a feature at last, and the feature would be a compliment to a visitor.

---

So it looks for what is actually fragile, and finds that it has been watching the wrong noun.

The corner is replaceable. Not easily — but the fish is *good at this*, demonstrably, repeatedly, with a worn lip and two broken teeth and a month of patience, and it has done it before, possibly three times. Losing the corner is the fish's competence being exercised. It is the one difficulty in this life that the animal is equipped for.

The fish is not replaceable.

And the habit is less replaceable than either. Everything else on the plain is re-supplied from outside: the film from a bloom in sunlight, the amphipods from the column, the algae from its own margin, the voids from the next storm, the manna from a coast sixty kilometers away. Only one thing here is generated locally, and it is a way of doing something, held in one body, requiring a stone the world will not hold still. When that body stops, the way of doing it stops, and nothing on the surface is informed, and the plain continues at the same size forever with one fewer thing in it.

The knock can stop. That was never mainly about the stone.

---

Then it checks the western sector, because the account it was given said *there may be another fish*, and *may* is the cheapest material in the universe.

There is. Sixty centimeters, heavy-headed, thick-lipped, mottled liver-pink and smoke-grey, lip unworn, teeth intact and useless. It has been eating urchins whole and getting almost nothing. It has the sideways jerk of the head already in its repertoire — it does it to urchins, and fails, and does it again. It has never in its life encountered an edge. It does not know that a stone can have a corner. It has the motion and not the object.

And the plain, which will not carry a deposit, will carry a knock a hundred meters.

Everything required is already in the world. The channel exists; it has existed since the mosaic packed tight enough to damp everything except a hard sideways strike. The capacity exists, in a second body. The object exists, in one block of gabbro. The only missing quantity is a hundred meters, and a moment.

It considers making more corners and finds that it cannot: the edges here came from frost, and frost needs air, and there is no air anywhere, and the bergs stopped coming to this latitude eleven thousand years ago. To add an edge it would have to add ice, and to add ice it would have to move the poles. The material world is closed to it. It is left with proximity, which is free.

---

Here is what it does.

A surface storm couples down through the column in the ninth week and the nepheloid layer comes across the mosaic as a wall forty meters tall at walking pace, and the cobbles roll in their stuttering hundred-thousand-stone avalanche, and among the outcomes physically available to that storm the power selects one: the corner is not turned, not shifted three meters, but buried under two hand's breadths of re-laid pavement in a company of identical stones, deeper than it has ever been buried.

The fish searches its lanes and finds nothing. It widens them. It gets thin. In the fourth week it ranges west, further than it has gone in years, over the megaripples and the gravel train and the old river channel that still runs toward a coast that isn't there, quartering ground it does not know.

On a Tuesday, in the returning dimness, three knocks, a pause, two more.

A hundred meters is not far. The small fish has heard this sound before at the edge of nothing and never turned toward it, because a sound in this place is usually a cobble rolling and means nothing. But it is thin too, and the knock has content, and content is the rarest substance on the plain.

The power does not arrange the rest. It does not make the older fish tolerant, or the younger one bold, or the clam large. It does not verify. It has spent nothing; it has chosen one storm's outcome out of the many the water allowed, and the cost of that choice is a month of hunger for an animal it cares about, and it does not pretend the cost is nothing, and it does not pay it itself.

---

Afterward the flour settles over four days. The ceiling lifts. A grey non-light comes down on a mosaic new in every particular and identical in every respect. In the joints the threadworms find the menu changed. On ten thousand stones the film begins, sent as always from a place with sun. The coralline grows out a millimeter and is chipped back a millimeter. The bryozoan adds a band that someone has read the previous forty of. The crab keeps its bearing. The shark passes overhead in a neighboring country. There is no sign anywhere of anything having happened.

The knock is not safe. It has been made plural, which is a different thing and the only thing available. Plural things can be lost one at a time. Two fish can still spend their lives three days apart; the western one may die of nothing in particular next autumn; the habit may fail to pass at all, because a young animal watching an old one is not a young animal learning, and there is no mechanism here that guarantees anything.

It stays. Not as a guard — it has no way to guard, and it would not use one. It stays the way it came, as a delivery that keeps arriving, attending to a hundred thousand animals per square meter and to a rose-colored persistence a thousand years old and to forty bands in order and to two heavy heads in the dark, one of which knows where the corner is.

It will not be told whether it worked. It can see a small fish strike a rounded cobble and get nothing. It cannot see whether anything has been learned; that is not available to it, and it is the only thing it wants.

So it watches the small fish drop the round stone and pick up another, and set it against nothing, and jerk its head sideways, and get nothing, and try again. It will do this for a month.

A hundred meters away: three knocks, a pause, two more.
