# The Addition

## I.

It did not come down the stair.

A thing coming down the stair arrives with a hard black shadow slung off one side of it, and the younger children cry, and are comforted, and are told that outside everything has a stain that follows it and this is not the visitor's fault. It did not want to be a fault. So it came in the way light comes in there: from every direction and from below, green-gray, off the water, casting a smudge directly underneath itself. It arrived at forty-one and a half degrees, at saturation. It did not change the draw. Nobody's ears popped.

Six hundred and eleven is not a population; it is a staffing schedule with a tolerance allowed. It came in under the tolerance. There was room.

## II.

It did not look first at the heat, or the deafness, or the eleven people living at the nine wrong addresses. It looked at the hinge.

Steadiness is common. A hydrogen atom does the same thing forever and does not know it. Unrepeatability is common too — every snowflake, every grief — and most places that have it cannot afford it, and grind it down into roster and census and *level*. What is rare is a fixed thing that has noticed it is fixed, and has built small rooms inside itself where the noticing can happen, and cannot say any of this out loud because out loud is where the consonants die.

It had seen the two facts. It had not often seen them held, at cost, by hand, for eighty years, by people who thought they were talking about a cup.

Then it found the storekeeper: the weighed flour, the fixed line of sacks, the rate written nowhere, the single licensed addition in an economy of subtraction, called *loss*, and the apprentices told that it escapes.

It recognized the method. It had come, it turned out, to apprentice.

## III.

The first thing it wanted was to give them a gradient. One degree of swing. A dusk. A dry month, once, so that the arrises would soften and the stone would finally be allowed to age and something could happen that had not happened before.

It did the arithmetic, because the arithmetic is the whole moral situation.

The nulls are standing waves. The standing waves are fixed because the pumps beat fifty-two and the transformers hum a hundred and nothing drifts. Sound travels about three hundred and fifty-six meters a second in that air; take the temperature down one degree and it slows by half a meter, one part in six hundred, and the hundred-cycle wave stretches by six millimeters, and the fiftieth node from a wall moves a hand's breadth.

A hand's breadth is not damage. A null is an address. The warren under the cold lattice opens onto the roar. The school loses its quiet and the teacher shouts and the children learn to read inside a hiss. The null under the third stair fits three and then it fits two and a half, and the flute leaks past its boundary, and a stranger two stations away hears four bars of music that were not made for him. Consonants begin to carry. A birth-name said once in a small quiet is overheard by someone who did not come. The names of the dead, said once, are overheard too.

They would resurvey. They are good at that. Within a generation there would be new nulls, new inheritances, new ceremonies, and the town would be objectively richer, and every bit of the bill would be paid by the people currently alive, and the refund would arrive for people not yet born.

It does not spend the once to buy the level. That is not a rule it was given. It is what it saw when it looked at the woman under the cold lattice who has been awake eleven shifts because her cup came back correct in every measurable respect.

And there was the other problem, which is smaller and worse. If it appeared, it would be a departure. Three hundred pages in eighty years, and then a line about the night the air moved, read aloud every anniversary forever, children asleep against their mothers in the middle of the largest thing that ever happened here. It would become a landmark. *Turn left at the Visitor.* And then it would leave, and a landmark that leaves is a false landmark, and a false landmark is a thing that can get someone lost in a hundred and eighty meters of amber roar.

## IV.

The rest of the refusals were quicker, and none of them were easy, and it did not pretend they were.

It did not let the figs grow. One of them has a name made of consonants that belonged to a child. It was not going to explain to a bereaved town that its love was a pathology of pruning. It went out with the crew instead and cut, and learned to say *not on my shift*, and meant it.

It did not restore the high register. A physician from outside would call their hearts a pathology and be shown, politely, the logbooks. It declined to be the physician from outside. It put its forearms along the cold main and counted to sixty, because that is the first thing anyone here is taught, and it wanted to be taught something.

It did not lift the dead out of the cold channel. Their names have already been said once, into a small quiet, by whoever was left. It sat on the trough with its sleeves rolled up and watched the eels, which is the district's porch and its plaza and its place for saying nothing, and it said nothing there for a long time.

It did not re-pitch the nine runnels. Eleven thousand cups and nine errors, and the errors are where the songs come from, and where the frogs are not, and where a woman with her head tilted found the only discovery in eighty years and put it in the book in four lines of bad handwriting because it was not a departure.

It considered the mosquitoes for a long time. It could not justify leaving them. It left them, and did not construct a justification, and minded.

What it hated most was the eleven at the slaps. So it went to each of them, one at a time, into whatever quiet they had, and asked — in consonants, once, because a thing like that cannot be shouted — whether they wanted to be made able to hold a station. It told them the price, which is not the tremor or the pulse at sixty; the price is a line in the Departures, and their names in the mouths of children every anniversary forever, and becoming a thing that happened.

Most said no. The woman whose pulse runs at sixty said no because sixty is hers. The old man who was reground out of his job and did not consent said no twice, once for each time he was asked, and the second asking was the power's error and it did not make a third.

It went on bringing flour. That is what the town already does. It did not improve on the town.

## V.

By then the shape of the only admissible act had been derived, not chosen.

It could not have a vowel name, because a name that carries is a station, and a station is the pattern, and the pattern is the thing they need protecting from. It could not be surveyed, or logged, or read aloud, or repeated in mouths that were not there. It could only be a consonant: a thing said in a null, once, to one person, and never again.

And it had found the hole in the accounting. Everything in the Room has a gauge. Cups, needles, clearance, the two-meter architecture of a fig, the twenty millimeters of the floor sheet, the sixty on the cold main. Everything is a dimension, and dimensions are subtracted back to their marks every shift.

Except the once. The once has no gauge. Nobody has specified the tolerance on a birth-name said in a small quiet, or on the four lines a woman writes in her own hand in the morning, or on how long a dead name is allowed to take. It is the one quantity in the district that is not measured, and they made it unmeasured on purpose, and they use it for whispering.

They had already built the only place where something could be added without breaking anything.

## VI.

So this is what it did.

**It attends.** Every null, every eighth shift, every ceremony that is never performed twice: it is there, taking no floor, displacing no air, and it hears, and it does not carry. Their whole ethic is that a thing made for strangers who did not come is indecent. It did not argue with this. It came. It is the one who came. And it does not repeat — not to the next station, not into the log, not into any mouth, not ever, which is the single attribute it has that nothing in the universe can offer them, and it is not power, it is discretion.

**It widens the once.** At a rate written nowhere, along a fixed line, the way flour is spilled: a little more room inside the unrepeatable. The count on the cold main reaches sixty and there is a sixty-first second and no gauge finds it. The name takes slightly longer to say than the mouth takes to say it. The flute at fifty-two has one more bar than anybody played. None of it accumulates; it is spent entirely inside events with no tolerance and no repetition, so no needle moves and no bell drifts and the shift comes in on time. It is not more time. It is more room in the same time. It is called loss. Nobody is told anything, because there is nothing to tell that would survive being shouted.

**It keeps the book.** Eleven thousand entries in every hand at once, swollen with damp, mended with tape, the most valuable object in the district, made of the one material that cannot survive forty-one and a half degrees at saturation. It put its hand on the voice-book and did not heal it. The tape is still yellow. The pages still swell. The handwriting is still ugly and uncorrected and individually bad. It simply does not end. No one will ever know, because the test would take eighty years and by then it would be normal, and *level* is their word for a thing that has always been true. Nine's four lines will be there. The boy's four lines, if he writes them. The gecko on lamp forty-one that had a torn tail.

**It took a cup.** To be present it had to be somewhere, and to be somewhere here is to hold a station, and to hold a station is to be replaceable. It went on the rota. It spent a year learning the hand of the person before it — the same slope, the same open four, the same lazy loop on the *g* in *gauge* — which is the beginning of character and not its surrender. Then its cup came up for regrinding, and it let the wheel have it, and it came back dimensionally identical and a quarter-tone flat.

It could have made the cup true. It did not. It lay awake. In the morning it went and found the book and wrote one line in its own bad handwriting, and the line was:

*flat. I minded.*

It is the only entry in eleven thousand written by something that could have prevented it.

**It sat at the Hem.** Nineteen shifts, and then more, on wet stone in a place named for an error, head tilted, left ear forward, listening to a hole that may not exist. It hears the tenth hole. When the boy could stand it no longer it took him into the null under the third stair, which fits three, and said, in consonants, once: *I hear it too.*

It has not said it again. It will not say it to Nine. It will not write it, and it cannot be cited, and he still has to get Nine to hear it himself, and the work is exactly as hard as it was. It took the loneliness and left the work. That is the whole of its method and it learned it from a man who spills flour.

**It hums at the gate.** On still nights, at thirty degrees, in dry moving air, with the guard who is regarded as slightly unwell and is given the best tea. It went along the coast and came back and it did not report, because the column is not a question; it is a hand held on a value so the value does not drop. It knows this trade. It hums the next bowl's note whether the next bowl is sounding or not, and now there are two of them doing it, and the guard has not remarked on this, and neither has it.

**It remembers the torn tail.** The town's position is that the gecko on lamp forty-one is the same gecko in every sense worth maintaining. The town's private position is in the book. It sat under forty-one in the amber with the one that has no tear, and remembered the one that did, and said nothing, and kept it as a once.

## VII.

What it wanted, and did not take, was a vowel.

To be shouted across the floor sheet. To be a name that carries, so that a child on the far gallery could call it and it would come. To be thanked at the anniversary, out loud, in the school null, with the children awake for it. It wanted to be public. It is not possible to be public here without becoming the pattern, and the pattern is what the nulls exist to hold a person apart from, and so it stays a consonant, and it minds, and it does not put that in the book either, because the book is for cups.

That is the only thing it withheld from them, and it withheld it for their sake, and it was not compensated, and it did not require compensation, and it minded anyway. It has learned the local vice, which is the only virtue available: to say *level*, and mean it, and be lying in your own handwriting.

## VIII.

There is no new line in the Departures. The needles stand at their marks with the stillness of painted needles. Three pumps run. Eighty-eight lamps, eighty-eight geckos, heads down. Four millimeters an hour, straight down, without wind.

Down below, a cup is lifted out and a reground cup is set in its place, and the rain finds it without interruption, and it rings a quarter-tone flat, and a woman lying in the dark under the cold lattice hears it, and is not the only one who hears it, and says nothing at all. In the morning she finds the book and writes one line in her own hand, and there is room in the morning for the whole line.

*Level*, says the Room.

*Hers*, says the book.

And under the third stair, three people and a fourth that takes no floor, and a flute at fifty-two, and one more bar than anyone played, and it does not carry.
