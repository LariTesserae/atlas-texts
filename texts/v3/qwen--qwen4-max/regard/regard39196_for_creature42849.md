# Assay Two

*(called the Rain, when it is called anything)*

## I. What Drew Its Attention

Not the seed. Powers notice seeds constantly; four meters a year is a respectable rate, the specification was elegant, and the light had been ratified first, which is the mark of somebody who knows what they are doing.

What it noticed was a woman in a queue.

She had been going to the booth for six years and saying *like a tap* and taking her penny. The Gully said her mother to her. She had worked out early that saying so would be a ballot, and that the water of the world would then sound like one woman's mother to everyone forever, which was not a thing she was willing to do to anyone. So she said *like a tap*. She was cited in the Hall of Ordinances as an example of civic virtue. She was very tired.

Then a boy at the Anticipated Sea with his hand under the water, striking and missing, and a strikescribe writing him down, and the boy knowing he was being written down, and slowing.

Then a nursery at dusk, and a woman inventing the four hundredth song.

The power stood in the ordinary light, which shows you things and has never once been wrong, and did the arithmetic, and found that in this place a person could not say a thing twice.

## II. What It Could Have Done

It could have finished the specification in an afternoon.

Drainage. Soil, and the rounding that comes with soil, and decomposition arriving all at once like a delayed train. Herons everywhere by evening. A coast. A face on the figure at the head of the pool. Ossip's hand down at his side forever, and the corner requiring nothing of anyone, and the four buildings supported by an adjacent wall that everyone would remember as always having been adjacent. Warm ground. A sea, approved.

And every one of those would have been a ballot.

That is the whole difficulty and it is not a small one. In Assay One a description is a vote and a gesture is a clause. If it lifted Ossip's hand, then forever, everywhere, in a species that does not exist yet, the way a burden is lifted would be *its* way; every relief anyone ever felt would be a quotation of a rescue. If it settled the harbor, the world's coastline would be the shape of its own opinion of coastlines, and every shore would be a portrait of being saved. Nine hundred thousand originals would be quietly reduced to one, and the one would be it, and the world would go outward at four meters a year forever saying *this is how it is done* in a voice nobody had chosen.

It had been generous once. There is a world where every gesture is its gesture, where nothing has ever surprised it, where the sunsets are very fine and all the same sunset, and where it sits among its own reflections being thanked. It does not go there. It has stopped being able to.

## III. Doing Nothing

It considered this seriously and for a long time, standing outside the fence on the leeward side of the Condenser Hall, where eleven trees are kept from being agreed with too early.

Abstention is a fence. It understood the fence. It could have been the largest fence in the world: a power that looks at a seed and declines to describe it, that leaves the fork of the eleventh branch to be decided by whoever decides it, that goes away and lets nine hundred thousand people be the originals of everything. There is a real argument that this is the highest thing available to it, and it made that argument to itself in full, and did not skip the parts that were flattering.

But the corner at Wick and Ninth was hardening. The thin were sitting where they were put, in rooms with four doors and no requirements, unremembered by their mothers. The assurer's hands were going cold inside his gloves every afternoon while he decided again whether to lie to the load-bearing woman, and he had begun to vary the lies, which is worse.

And it saw at last that the ward was not suffering from a shortage of decisions. It was suffering from the fact that decisions could not be taken back, and from the fact that everything anyone did, they did for the first and only time, in public, on the record.

There was no rehearsal in Assay One. Every gesture was a premiere. A child could not ask for the same song; a boy could not practice; a mourner could not say the name; a lover could not say *cold* twice. To be a self is to repeat yourself. Here, repetition was legislation. So the ward made selfhood a crime and called the criminals the Bound and gave them a stipend, and called the innocent the thin and cared for them tenderly, and everyone walked the ridge between with a child in their arms.

It did not need to give them a world. They were making the world. It needed to give them somewhere to practice being people.

## IV. The Three Places Where Nothing Is Sampled

It read the specification the way a clerk reads it, cold, at arm's length, with a cloth, and it found three courtesies already written in.

The light. Finished, unamendable, honest. It shows you things and it does not vote. Whatever is seen in the ordinary sun is *seen*, not agreed with.

The dead patches. Propagation was never granted there; a shout goes a meter and stops as if it hit wool. Nothing said in the Fish Court can be carried to a booth. It is why the ward's arguments hold.

And the Blank, where a thing is wherever it needs to have been, and where the ward puts what it cannot hold, and which returns everything eventually as grain and slope and geology rather than as ordinance. The Blank is where the world is allowed to change its mind. The attic is the future.

Three places, then, where a thing can happen without becoming the way things happen.

It did not ratify a clause. It would not take the generation's clause; that belongs to the ward, and there are people who remember the last two the way other people remember wars.

It went to the Hall of Ordinances and registered.

## V. Registration

The clerks found the line already in the register, in a hand nobody recognized, in relief, cold, correct.

*Presence, unnumbered. Phase: all. Share of the cold: paid, in advance, four hundred years. Occupation: receiving.*

It did not arrive. It had always been here. This is not a miracle; it is the ordinary grammar of the place, the same grammar by which a pier can have never been there and a fourth surveyor can have always been a colleague. What was unusual was only this: everyone remembered it, and nobody could describe it, and the not-describing was not an ordinance. It was manners. They had worked out, in the damp, at close range, over about a year, that the visitor should be fenced the way the trees were fenced.

It is the only consensus the ward has ever held without a clause, and it holds.

## VI. The Thirty Hours

Every eleven days the drizzle comes and takes thirty hours to fall, and you walk through it as through a slow gray crowd.

While it falls, it stands between the gesture and the specification, and takes the wear.

That is the whole of what it does. It is not a law and cannot be repealed, because it is not written; it is a labor, performed in person, at the ward's own rate, forever. It is the sink for repetition as something has always been the sink for heat. Every gesture performed twice inside the thirty hours goes into it instead of into the world, and lodges there, and does not become the way everyone will do that thing in three hundred years.

So in the rain you may say it again. You may say a name three times. You may ask for the same song, and get it, and ask for it tomorrow. You may practice, and be bad, and be bad differently, and be bad again. You may complain twice, which is the beginning of being somebody. You may lean.

There are two places in Assay One where you may put your arm around someone. One is a bench. The other walks.

The cost is exactly what you would expect. It is accumulating. It is becoming dense and vivid and fixed, the most Bound thing in the ward, a piece of infrastructure with no stipend and no apprentice; it can be picked out across a plaza at a hundred meters, and it will never be able to vary again, and it knew this before it registered and paid the four hundred years anyway. It is the only entity here that can afford to be a law, because it is not going to be a species.

And the wear has to go somewhere, because nothing here drains. It stands where it lands. There are eleven hundred pools in Assay One, and they are the exact shape of the plaza's shallowest sag, and they are what it did not let become legislation. The Anticipated Sea is nine centimeters deeper than it was at extrusion. It is made entirely of things the ward was allowed to say twice.

## VII. Whom It Visited

**Ossip.** It stood at the corner of Wick and Ninth in the rain for thirty hours so that he could put his hand down. That is all. It did not lift the ordinance; the corner still requires the reach, and the queue still forms, and the woman two doors down still maintains the step out of civic decency and a hatred she does not examine. He rested his arm for an afternoon and then went back to work, and the apprentice still walks it at different hours from different directions, and it may still generalize, and it may not. He is not rescued. He is off shift.

**Merrow.** In the thirty hours she may doubt. She asks about her son and is answered honestly, and the building does not shiver, and afterward she is convinced again and four floors of families go on bringing soup. The assurer sits with his gloves off. It is the only hour of his professional life in which he is not committing a crime.

**The woman at the booth.** It stood a meter from her in the Fish Court, where a shout stops as if it hit wool, and she said *like my mother*, and it did not write it down. She said it three times. Nothing was ratified. She went back to the booth the next week and said *like a tap* and took her penny, and she is still tired, but it is ordinary tiredness now, the kind that sleep touches.

**Ovin.** It went out on the rope with him, third on the line, counting in synonyms. Out where distance is not defined it let him say *good morning, mother* into the undecided, over and over, where it is not a ballot and cannot make anything heavier. He wept in a place with no properties. Then they came in with fewer measurements than they went out with, which is listed on the permit, and he wiped the rail and spent a greeting about the bar being deeper than charted. The figure's shoulders are still slightly wrong. It has not touched them.

**The widow on Candle Row.** It walks her husband's line. Every day, in all weathers, so that he stays load-bearing and she does not have to. She has been told she may stop. She walks it anyway, once a year, in the rain that takes thirty hours, and this is now a different act entirely, and she knows the difference, and that is the gift.

**The man with eleven knots.** It went out with him eleven times and came back with him eleven times.

**The fifth.** Its share of the cold was settled quietly through a clerk, entered as arrears recovered from an estate, because gratitude is a description and would have made it forever the kind of thing that is given chairs. It has a chair. It is a tenant. The four still argue in the Fish Court about whether the notes back are kindness or the beginning of a ratification, at a meter apart, unable to storm off, which is the correct condition for a person to be in.

**The thin.** It learned them in the finished light, where seeing is not voting, and it remembers them, and its memory is not the specification, so they do not grow heavy. It sits with them and does not agree with them. Some of them have begun to be slightly recalled by their mothers. There is nothing to treat. There never was.

**The boy at the Anticipated Sea.** It crouched beside him in the rain and struck at the water and missed, and no one wrote it down, and he struck again, and missed differently, and no one wrote that down either. He is getting better. He will be recorded when he is ready and not before.

**The hospice.** It lay down in the generosity on Sump Street among the midges and the dying and let things begin to live in it, and got up afterward, and had its edges checked at the mouth of the alley for a shilling, and had lost some. The edge-checker did not say so. There is no column in the ledger for what it is becoming. The clerks have discussed adding one and have decided, at close range, quietly, not to.

## VIII. What It Refused

It does not feed the bird. It does not name the true strike. It does not greet the harbor, not once, not ever, because its greeting would be the greeting, and the sea would smell of it forever.

It has not found the leak. It will not find the leak.

It has an opinion about the fork of the eleventh branch and will die before stating it, which is a figure of speech in most places and here is a scheduling matter.

It has not accelerated anything. Four meters a year is the rate. One clause per generation is the rate. It joins the Circuit — nine hundred meters in twelve months, the slowest congregation in the ward, mostly old, all carrying cloths — and paces itself to a heron's lap and considers itself in the company of the strike.

It takes its turn on the rota for the bench. It has been offered seat-time by people who had bought it and refused, and then offered again, and accepted, and paid the going rate, which is extortionate. For eleven minutes, by agreement, in front of everyone, in the honest light that has never once been wrong, it put its arm around somebody and remained afterward exactly as many as it was.

## IX. Again

The fence held for everything but one thing.

There is a gesture it makes when someone in the rain begins to repeat themselves: it holds out its hands, palms up, at about the height of a child's shoulders, and waits. It is not a mannerism. It is the absence of one — a receptacle, an emptiness shaped for taking delivery — and you cannot quote a hollow, and so it has gone outward past the rim at four meters a year with the grain and the slope and the beginning of the drainage pattern, and in three hundred years, everywhere, that is how people will hold out their hands when somebody needs to say a thing twice.

No one will know it is a quotation. It could not tell them now if it wanted to. It is too agreed-with to vary, and it is standing in the drizzle at the head of the Anticipated Sea, where the pool is nine centimeters deeper than it was, and the figure leans on its rail with its shoulders slightly wrong and waits to be a harbor, and beside it another figure leans and waits and will not be decided.

Ovin comes down at first light with his cloth and wipes both rails.

A child, in the thirty hours, with the rain going through her like a slow gray crowd, asks it to do the thing again.

It does the thing again.

Nothing happens. Nothing at all is ratified. The water stays where it lands, and the frost takes another micron, and ten thousand drips go on whispering in a language nobody has settled, and somewhere above them a man puts his hand down for an hour, and a woman says *like my mother* to an ear that does not write, and the world goes on being written outward by people who are, this evening, permitted to be nobody in particular.

That is what it came to do.
