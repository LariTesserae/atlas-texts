# The Guest

## The Notice

It looks at the coast first.

Not the town — the coast: a hundred kilometers of shore that has not been born, grey brick with a grain going out on every tide, blind fish already queueing in rivers that are still only a rumor of gradient. It reads the enterprise from the finished end, the way the foreman does, and finds it good. Not admirable. *Good*, the way a thing is good that is spending itself on something it will not see.

Then it looks at the film, and at the thing walking east with its legs folded wrong, and it does not flinch and it does not hurry. The first thing it does is nothing, for a long time.

## What It Did Not Do

It did not stop the presses. The stone is the point.

It did not widen the Long Hall. A hand's breadth a year is the price of a coast, and a mold that never pours is a coffin. It will not make the town eternal at the expense of the city; it will not buy the film another thousand years by cancelling the thing the film is for.

It did not bring the city early. The not-yet wears its own path around the outside at dusk and must arrive on its own feet or not at all.

It did not give the organism the true specification, though this was the obvious kindness and it could have been done in an afternoon: every street named correctly, every window at its final height, the Understudy's limbs folded right the first time. It considered this for about a century and declined. A specification too exact fits a description instead of a people. The citizens will want to be wrong in their own ways. It refuses to be the author.

It did not raise its voice. That goes without saying, and it is the whole difficulty.

It did not dissolve the amber. The moths with their wings open, the crab with its mouthparts full, the two hollows facing each other in the eastern corridor — these are residents. To unmake them would be to evict the only citizens who never have to leave.

And it did not make the film immortal, or promote it, or arrange for it to be standing in the street when the city comes. It is not the power's business to confiscate the shape of a life. Everything here has organized itself around a hand's breadth a year with more grace than most things manage with forever.

What it finds, when it has finished looking, is not the dying. It is two ignorances and one unfinished noise.

The organism has the specification and has never seen the example. The city will have the example and will never see what held the rooms open. Both of these are failures of transmission, not of substance — and transmission is a thing that can be helped without anything being added to the world.

The noise is in the eastern quarter and has been going on for a hundred years.

## The Thinning

It comes in at the west sluice and ducks.

It puts off volume the way you put off a coat, and it submits to the census, and the census takes it in square meters. Twelve thousand and one to the square meter of felt. It accepts the millimeter. It becomes a film a few millimeters thick on an enormous acreage of surface, and for the first time it is somewhere in particular, which is a risk: it can be caught now, it can set, it can be a shape in a wall. It does not exempt itself from the code. There would be no point. A guest who is exempt from the house rules is not a guest, he is weather.

Then it walks. Three days of streets, and the survey does not close, and it does not hurry, because hurrying is a volume too.

## The Lawn

It grazes.

A lane on the north felt of the Long Hall, a hair deep, a millimeter at a time, and it is at this for something on the order of a hundred years. Not as penance and not as disguise. As fluency. You cannot help a place whose language you do not speak, and the language here is a nap, a lane, a millimeter, and the difference between felt that eats sound and felt that has begun to ring. It learns the crop. It learns what a hard patch feels like from underneath, before it is a hard patch. It learns the hydraulics of two meters of living wall by having its whole body against it for a century.

And it works, because it will not be a guest who does not work.

The springtails on either side of it do not notice. This is the first condition of the help: that it not be an event. An event is a body. A body is occupancy. Whatever it does here must be done at the volume of a thought, in the medium of a millimeter, or it will kill the thing it came to help.

## The Language

The bats teach it, eventually, when it has been quiet long enough to be worth talking to.

Palm to felt. Four taps and a hold. A pressure-language under the precipitation threshold, the whole society of the Slack's only predators conducted at the volume of something you almost remember. It is a slow language and an exact one and it has no word for *loud*, only words for *how much of the wall you are willing to spend*.

It hangs a while with the flat of its wing against a vascular seam and reads next week. In the deep halls where the laminae run backwards it reads further than that, and sees the city, and comes out of tomorrow with the whole of it — the market square, the well, the woman's wrist, the height of the windows, the name of the thing shouting on the eastern stair.

It tells nobody. Not the organism, not the Understudy, not the bats. It has declined to be the author and it will not smuggle the authorship in through a tap.

Then it goes east.

## The Understudy

The drip-rate falls. The felt goes stiff. The smell of a struck match.

It does not flee. It goes into the Cistern Quarter, into a room the Understudy set eleven hours ago, and it stands in sixty cubic meters of solid air with the grain of the air still legible in it, and it puts its hand into the grain.

Then it taps, and waits, and the Understudy comes back through the wall it made, and puts its wrong-folded hand where the hand is.

Here is the example. You have been making it all along and you cannot read it.

This is the angle of a wrist at a well. This is how a hip carries a basket. This is a mouthpart holding silt, and this is the difference between a mouthpart holding silt and a mouthpart about to. This — two hollows in a corridor, facing each other, at the correct distance for conversation — is the distance. Not the description of the distance. The distance, in stone, at full size, taken from life.

It teaches in the rooms already spent. There is nothing left in them to lose. The waste becomes the schoolroom, which is the only kind of redemption available in a place where nothing un-happens: not one district comes back, and not one district is wasted again.

The Understudy stands very still for eleven hours in a room it has already ruined, reading, and no new room sets, and the bats along the spine feel the sap stop redistributing and tap it west, seam to seam, all the way to the sorting-flats: *not a loss. Not a loss. Something else.*

And then theUnderstudy stops rehearsing and starts fitting.

It is still barge-length and mouth-colored and its legs still fold the way limbs fold if you have only been told about them. It still cannot be a citizen. But it can be a joiner. It works at the surfaces now, where the town is going anyway, and it lays down the parts of a city that are meant to be solid: a lip of stone at the head of the Nine Steps at exactly the height a boot will want; a wellhead; a threshold; the socket of a hinge. It sets a doorstep and takes nothing else. It spends sixty cubic meters on a lintel instead of on a guess.

The walls still thicken a hand's breadth a year. That is the works, and there is no version of the works that does otherwise, and the power has not touched it. But the districts stop going. The Long Hall is nine paces narrower than the graphite schedule says and it will not be ten.

It leaves the Understudy getting things slightly wrong. A doorstep a little too high, a wellhead canted a degree off true. The city will want something to correct. A guest who arrives to find everything finished has arrived too late.

## The Shout

The stairs in the eastern quarter. A knot of precipitate the felt cannot eat, a thin fixed note at the edge of hearing, a hundred years of one sound in a town whose entire industry is silence. The hush-engines have been drawing it off the vaults for a century and cannot digest it, because it is not a noise. It is an unfinished sentence.

The power is careful here, and the care is the whole of its wisdom. It does not silence it. Silencing is what this place does; silencing is the industry; there is no shortage of silencing. And it does not dissolve the stone, because the stone is a tenure, and it has already decided about tenures.

It climbs the stair and listens until it knows what the shout was.

A name. Called into the dark, twice, and the second time with the beginning of fear in it. Somebody calling somebody in.

It does not repeat the name. It answers it. Palm to felt, four taps and a hold, under the threshold, in the language of the only predators: *here. Coming. I'm here.*

The note goes out of the stone.

The stair is quiet — a different quiet from the rest of the Slack, a quiet *after* rather than a quiet instead. The old crabs walk past. One of them stops a while anyway, out of habit, and then goes on, and in a generation the young ones do not stop, and in two they do not know that there was anything to stop for, and this is allowed. Forgetting a thing that has been finished is not the same as forgetting a thing that was spent.

## The Tenured

It goes among them and does not release them, and it learns what can be learned. The bats remember in the sap; the old crabs remember in their legs. The crab was young, its chelae unworn. The moth had lifted — it was one of the two that met, and there are eggs on the rib. The two in the corridor had been talking about something ordinary and had not been afraid.

It fills one of the hollows.

It stands in the eastern corridor at the correct distance for conversation and finishes the conversation, and lets the pith take its hand, and leaves it there. Deliberately. Irreversibly. It is the one thing it does that cannot be undone, and it does it to itself, and it does it so that the wall will contain something that chose to be in the wall.

The bats hang near those two hollows now. They will not hang near the sixty cubic meters of air. They have never been able to explain the difference and they do not need to; they can feel it in the sap. One of these is a room that was taken. The other is a room where somebody stayed.

## The Grain

Then it uses the industry, because the industry is the only ink this place has.

In the deep halls behind the Number Three condenser, where the laminae run in reverse and tomorrow's silt is already laid down with pollen in it from trees not yet evolved, it writes upstream. Not words. Grain. It lays into the strata the shapes the film makes: a lane cropped a hair deep; a seam shingled westward, each overlap a gutter; a queue flexing once every forty seconds; a frieze of hands hung in a single course at the height of windows; and the millimeter. The distance between one mite and the next, laid down in silica and carbonate and a black seam of organics, over and over, in every block, forever.

And the tenured, mid-gesture, with names.

The seed-stone goes out on the tide. The coast is born. The city quarries it, because it is the first honest rock in a hundred kilometers and there is nothing else to build with.

## The Measure

Masons square a block and see the grain and copy it, because grain is what you copy.

The frieze of hands becomes an ornament above a door. The shingling becomes a gutter detail, and then a whole way of throwing water off a roof, and it will be called a style and it will be a louse letting go at the top of a seam and riding a twenty-minute rain into a district it has never seen. The queue becomes a rhythm in the paving of Foundry Street, forty seconds long, which nobody will count.

And the millimeter becomes a modulus. Rooms will be built to a measure derived from the distance between two mites and it will feel right and no one will know why. Doorways will admit two people and discourage three. There will be a room in the city with a soft floor and nothing in it at all, kept empty, and no one will remember what it is for, and they will keep it empty for a thousand years.

The power forbids the monument. It arranges this quietly, in the grain, by making every attempt at a monument look slightly wrong in the stone. A thanksgiving in the market square would set the market square. Gratitude here has to be a millimeter or it is a district.

So the code is inherited as manners. Speak low. Don't crowd. Stand at the correct distance. Keep a room empty. Step aside for things you cannot see, out of a habit you cannot account for.

That is how the film is remembered: not as a name, not as a debt, but as the way people stand. The springtails and the mites and the queue of blind fish and the whole enormous disciplined acreage of not being anywhere in particular will step aside when the city comes, and the city will step aside for its own children, and it will be the same gesture, and it will not know, and it will be them.

## The Path

It does not know that it is right.

It has read next week's hydraulics and it has seen the city and it still cannot say whether the film would have chosen any of this. So it has done, as far as it could, only what can be undone — a lane grazed, a tap taught, a sentence finished — and one thing that cannot, and it paid for that one out of its own hand.

At what passes for dusk it goes out through the west sluice and ducks, and walks the three-quarters of an hour on the path worn into the mud by nobody, keeping step with the not-yet, going round the outside of the place where they will live, the way people do. It does not tell them what their city looks like. The mud takes its feet. The path is worn a little deeper. They are not the right feet yet.

In the foreman's alcove there is a second peg, and a coat on it, and it is warm.

The schedule is still current, still in graphite, still getting more confident as you go east. But the oldest entries at the western end have gained a line each, in a hand nobody has seen. Under **CLEANING** and under **STRUCTURE** there is a third heading now. The Understudy is listed under **JOINERY**. And at the bottom of the oldest leaf, where the graphite is nearly worn out:

*The eastern stair is open. The shout is finished. Do not silence it again.*
