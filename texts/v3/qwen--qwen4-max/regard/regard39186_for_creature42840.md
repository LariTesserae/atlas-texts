# Schedule of Works — OR 3

*Prepared by the second person. Not for filing.*

---

## 1. Notice

It did not come for the noise. The noise goes a kilometer and spends itself on scree and empty air, and a roar with nothing to spend itself on is not a cry, it is weather. It came for the notebook.

Five hands, eleven years, a pitch beside a date. The note going slowly flat as the brackets fatigued, which is a body aging in a notation nobody chose for it. And then, in the seventh year, a line: the clamp re-tightened, the note up nearly a tone. And the following week, loosened again. No reason given.

Someone had heard a thing go out of tune and put it back. Had not improved the handrail, had not extended its life, had not filed a variation, had not told anybody. Had restored a pitch to a tube in a room where nothing can hear it, and left the reason out of the record.

The power was drawn to the drawer, not the chamber. It wanted to know who had done that, and whether they had known what they were doing.

It could not find out. The hand is unidentified. It has looked at that line for some time now. This is the first thing it did not know, and it is why it stayed.

---

## 2. The temptation

It stood at the adit mouth, where it is 112 decibels and the pressure arrives as a hand laid flat on the sternum, and it wanted three things, and it examined them in order.

It wanted to hang a white sun in the roof, so that the patch under lamp six would be green continuously and not for forty minutes a year in a moving beam.

It wanted to give the animal a mirror, so that the one resident nobody has seen entire could see itself entire.

It wanted a hundred more years.

The first wish it traced back and found at the bottom: *it wanted to be the witness.* Green is an event that happens to that organism when somebody arrives with a torch. To make green permanent is to abolish the event, and the event is the only thing in the chamber that requires a person, and it had wanted to be required. It put that wish down.

The second wish it could not grant even in principle, and was glad. You can have the animal or the room. The body is throughput; to see the floor you must drain the body out of itself. This is not a limit on power. It is a limit on sight, and it holds everywhere, in every world, and it is usually invisible because most bodies are not forty-one meters long with a stencil on the outside and a photographic station and a scale bar. Here it was visible. Here was the whole condition, eleven years old, entirely documented, poured in meter-and-a-half lifts. It had made a great many things and had never before been shown the arrangement so plainly, in a box you drive past in four seconds.

It chose the animal. Therefore it accepted never seeing it. That acceptance took longer than the rest of the schedule combined.

The third wish it will address under *Declined*.

---

## 3. Works considered and declined

**3.1 Permanent white illumination.** Declined. The black crust in the joints is the better photosynthesizer and runs the room, on tannin and a dim monochrome shift, and has never been thanked. A white sun favors the green algae, which is a hand's breadth wide and has been a hand's breadth wide for four years, and would hand the chamber to the weaker organism because the weaker organism is prettier. Also: the census would lose its one event.

**3.2 Abrion mitigation.** Declined, all variants — hard-facing the dish, grouting the void, removing the twelve. The stones are teeth. The dish is a gizzard. A body defined by what passes through it cannot be protected from passing-through; you would be preserving a shape by stopping the only process that makes it. To take out the teeth is to give it a mouth and no chewing. The house is eating itself and using itself to do it, and this is not a pathology, it is the arrangement, and the power has never made anything that was not doing the same thing more slowly.

**3.3 Extension of C-4.** Declined. It went and looked at the egg. A hundred and ninety millimeters in the first photograph, a hundred and twenty last February, a white quartz vein across it, six years left. It could hold that vein together for a thousand years. It sat with the arithmetic instead: the sand goes over the end sill, out the tailrace, and builds the bar below the hairpin where the grass is conspicuously greener than the scree on either side.

Then it went down the valley and stood in that grass. It is the only thing the chamber exports besides fog and noise. It is made of the mountain and of the house.

The animal has no offspring. It has one. It is a green bar in a scree slope, and it is downstream, and it will be there when the box is a ruin, and nobody has ever connected the two, and the power stood in it for an afternoon and did not connect them either, out loud, to anyone.

**3.4 Postponement of decommissioning; a hundred more years.** Declined, and this was the expensive one. When the river stops, the ferns come in along the weep channels at a few centimeters a year, and their descendants find nine buttes and a floor of rounded stones and a wrack line that has stopped moving, and the calcite goes on at seven-tenths of a millimeter, and in four hundred years the cave arrives. The karst was cancelled in the mountain and resumed inside the artifact. The stalactites are the only residents with an inheritance.

The animal's death is the gestation of its child. To keep the water falling forever is to hold the cave at four millimeters and call it mercy. It will not embalm this. It has been asked to embalm a great many things and has learned to recognize the request when it comes from itself.

**3.5 Attenuation of the fundamental.** Declined. Thirty hertz, dropping four in the afternoon, felt in the bowels from the walkway. That is the heart. Also the rail's note is made of the wreckage of that roar, and is the only ordered thing in the chamber, and you do not quiet a body to improve its singing.

**3.6 Rescue of the midges.** Declined, with difficulty. Perhaps four a year come through the grille. The mist wets them and kills them inside the hour. The immigrants are dead on arrival and are what everything else eats; a chamber that keeps its midges is no longer a cave. See 4.6 for what was done instead.

**3.7 Notification.** Declined. There is no address. The animal has no ear that is not the whole of itself, and the only language it has is water level. To say *you are attended* would require moving a gate, which is to say a false tide, which is to say a lie told in the one clock it trusts. It will not forge the tide. It will use the amber that is already there.

## 4. Works accepted

**4.1 Attend the strobe.** Before every gate movement, three minutes, fog turned amber, the whole chamber one lit cloud. The crust, the straws, the egg, the foam, the rail, the two ferns leaning outward, the boil, the cloud with its underside, the two humans — all inside the same event and the same color. No new signal is to be introduced. The power will simply be inside that one, every time, for the full three minutes, and will not use the time to do anything else.

Quantity: all of them.

**4.2 One additional minute of white torch, annually, allocated as follows.** Forty seconds to the black crust above the four-meter line, which is eleven years old and has never once been seen in color and has never once been thanked. Twenty seconds to the patch under lamp six.

Then the torch off, and the green goes back to being an event that has ended, and the power looks away and does not leave the torch behind. The witness is a man in a dry suit with eleven other things on his checklist. The power will be that, and will do the eleven other things properly, because the eleven other things are the rent.

**4.3 Read the broken straw. Non-destructive.** Four millimeters, straw-colored, translucent, in the wrack line at the downstream corner where a scaffold tube knocked it off in the third summer. Eleven bands. The thick ones are Junes. It is the only corpse in the chamber that was born in the chamber, and cutting it would end that. Read the gate schedule off it with a lens and a low light, learn eleven years of afternoons, put it back on the shelf where it lies sorted by size among the birch seeds and the bottle cap.

**4.4 The notebook.** Add a date and a pitch in a sixth hand, in the same format, in the drawer, in the control building. Nothing else. Do not write why the clamp was loosened. The man who loosened it did not write why, and the reason is the same reason, and it does not need saying twice.

**4.5 Photograph the sump pump.** In November, intake at ice, jet a trickle, the pump cycling every nine minutes and the loudest living thing in the chamber, sounding like something being murdered slowly in a cathedral. It is the janitor. It keeps the adit dry and the door possible and it has never been photographed. Photograph it working, not dewatered. Do not file the photograph. Do not photograph the animal's absence; there are eleven years of those already and every one is a picture of an empty house.

**4.6 Accompany the midges.** No rescue. Be present for the hour. Four hours a year, approximately. This is the entire program in miniature and could have been written instead of everything above.

**4.7 Assist dispersal. Manual. Nine spores.** The two ferns at the slot lean outward and have spent eleven years failing to colonize a concrete box and occasionally succeeding on a landslide. Their children go into the mist and are wetted and killed. Carry nine, on a dry fingertip, out through the grille, onto the scree where nothing grows, and set them where the windblown dust packs into a crack.

This adds nothing to the room and takes nothing from it. It is not a new intention. It is theirs, done by hand, because they have no hands.

**4.8 The ancestor.** Initials scratched in green concrete beside the cold joint, in the exact stratum he was pouring the day the pump failed in the second summer. Older than every living thing in the chamber. He is down the valley, retired, and he has told this story to anyone who would sit still, and people do not sit still, because it is a story about a pump failing.

No works required. Sit still. Let him get to the part about the initials. Ask what the concrete looked like before it went off. Do not tell him what he is the ancestor of; he would not believe it, and it would make the story into something he had to carry.

**4.9 Upstream.** It went up the reservoir first and stood sixty meters down in the drowned forest, where the light does not go, among the trees that were standing in that valley when the valley was closed and are eleven years dead and still leaking. The oldest material in the chamber is not from here. The base of the food web is a graveyard.

It did nothing there either. That is a different document.

## 5. Method and compliance

*Two-person entry.* It cannot go in alone. It did not make this rule and will not exempt itself from it; a single human is not permitted to exist in that room and neither is a single anything. It comes as the second.

In the eleventh February the crew was short a man. There was somebody at the adit mouth in a dry suit who signed the permit, and was competent, and held the light, and did not talk, and pointed. He learned the twenty gestures. In the first hour he used the one for *time* when he meant *level* and was corrected by a man who could not speak either, with a look and a flat hand, and it was the best hour it had had in a very long time.

The initials in that year's report are not on the payroll. Nobody has ever checked. The reports go in the box, the box gets locked, the road goes past in four seconds.

*Hearing protection mandatory beyond this point.* Complied with. It did not need to. It wore it.

It has taken a lease on four millimeters of the dry margin above the four-meter line, in the perennial zone, where concrete meets air. It pays in the local currency, which is spending itself to keep its shape. It is not exempt from the June flood; if it goes below the line it will be stripped like the annuals and will recolonize from the joints above, pale, in the weeks after the water drops, and it will not complain, because the flood is the only herbivore this ecosystem has ever had and it is not going to be the tenant that gets a special arrangement.

It is not on the census. The census is a document about a thing that is going on, written by people who would not have called it counting, and it should not acquire a line that cannot be verified with a scale bar.

---

## 6. Duration

Seven-tenths of a millimeter a year.

This is the pace at which it works anyway. It is asked, elsewhere, constantly, for speed, and it has learned that almost nothing it does at speed is any good. Here the local rate and its own rate are the same number. It has never found that before. It intends to stay.

When the river stops — and it will stop, and that is 3.4 and it is not going to reopen the question — it will be at the rail. Not with a camera. The men who are there at the last dewatering feel the note arrive in the bones of the face before there is anything to hear with, and when it goes the other way it will be the same, in reverse, and somebody should be standing close enough to feel a thing end in the bones of the face rather than read it in a report.

Let the note fall rather than be cut. Then four days of empty house, and the egg, and the flashlight with its lens full of silt, and the scaffold clamp, steaming faintly in the cold. It will walk across that with the others and write down millimeters and mean them.

Then the water goes back, or doesn't, and the ferns come in at a few centimeters a year, and the straws lengthen, and the crust goes on doing the work in the dark, and in four hundred years the cave arrives, and it will need nothing, and the power will be there anyway.

---

## 7. From the road above

Nothing. A blank concrete face in the flank of the mountain, sixty meters of it, streaked white with calcite. A grille. **OR 3.** A door that is always locked. In cold weather a plume of fog stands off the tailrace and drifts down the valley and dissipates before the first hairpin. In the car, with the windows up, a hum in the floor pan that you think is the engine.

Inside, two who cannot speak, pointing. A hand's breadth of grey that is green for a minute, once a year, and then isn't. A note held in a galvanized tube, at pitch, because somebody keeps putting it back and nobody writes down why.

It has no witnesses. It has a population. The number is one larger than it was, and the distinction is unchanged.

That was the specification.
