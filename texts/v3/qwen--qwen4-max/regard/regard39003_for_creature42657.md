From the plateau there is still nothing to see.

The power does not open the fissure. It comes down the lower lip the way the cold air comes down, in a sheet a few millimeters thick, and it enters at the phase the chamber is already using, and it does not put so much as a fingernail into the standing wave. This takes some care. Twenty-one hertz, a hundred and eighteen decibels, a quarter-millimeter of slosh: there is no way to be casually present in a resonant cavity. Presence here is a load.

It goes to the dead margin first, where the wool is old and dry and has not carried a signal in a century, and it lies down among the bodies in the order they fell, and it begins by finding out who lives here.

It does not count the animals. Counting gives a hundred million, which is the wrong answer. It follows the water instead — from the bacterium on the fourth shelf through the mite through the pseudoscorpion through death and back into the film — and it follows it for long enough to see the whole circulation close, and then it has the correct number, which is one.

One being, four meters across, two hundred years old, made of a cup of water older than any of its knots, distributed through a hundred million bodies none of which can be told about the whole. It has no name for itself. It has no organ that could hold the concept. Its largest event is a blind thing on the third shelf laying a whip along a filament and touching a stranger, and this happens a thousand times a day, and nothing anywhere keeps a record of it.

The power stays at the margin for eleven years, reading, before it does anything at all.

---

It wants to give the drop.

This should be said plainly, because the rest of what follows is restraint and restraint is easy to mistake for coldness. The first impulse is not wise. It is the impulse of anything benevolent standing in a dry place: *here, take this, you have a cup, let me make it two.* One drop, free, at a surface. A tenth of the world's entire liquid capital, delivered without anyone having to be eaten for it.

It runs the consequence forward instead of performing it.

Water at a surface, in gas at seventy degrees with a dew point far below anything the chamber reaches, does not stay at the surface. The film takes it in hours. There is a bloom — the pavement doubles, the readers grow fat, the patrol runs short routes through a world of soft disconnected flesh. It is the ash-fall, the storm-gift, the fat season, and it lasts a season.

And then it does not stop, because the drop was not a storm, it was a precedent. Free water arriving at a surface is a thing the waxy cuticle no longer needs to be perfect about. The dry gut that reclaims its own feces is expensive; it is paid for only because water is never permitted to be free. Relax the prohibition and the thrifty are undercut by the leaky, and within thirty generations the tight passing loosens, and within a hundred the water is sometimes outside, and the moment water is sometimes outside it is no longer necessary to hand yourself to the next one.

The plumbing is not a feature of this being. The plumbing *is* this being. Give it a drop and you have not enriched it, you have dissolved it back into a crowd.

The power holds the drop for eleven years and then does not give it, and the wanting does not go away, and it decides that the wanting is simply the cost of the work.

---

The other gifts fail in the same manner, each for its own specific reason, and it is worth setting them down because the failures are not moral, they are mechanical.

*Stop the magma withdrawing.* But the crack and the closing are one process. The fissure widens by flakes because the margin cools and thermal stress propagates upward; the galleries contract because the gas cools and the whistle's wavelength shortens. Arrest the cooling and you keep the wool thickening — the jets spin regardless — while losing the widening aperture. You would leave the world being manufactured shut with its rations frozen. To spare them the squeeze is to take away the bread.

*Give them room.* The shelves sit at the pressure nodes because that is where the felt settles; the stack's phase relationship is what converts six hundred degrees into sound. Widen a gallery and you detune the engine. The note stops that afternoon instead of in nine hundred years, and the film smothering in its own exhaust follows within a week. Room is the one thing that cannot be added to a room like this.

*Give them a voice that carries.* A chamber-wide alarm costs a hundredth of a drop. At present such an alarm is rare, because it must be paid for ten thousand times over by ten thousand animals each spending muscle to tell one neighbor. Make speech free and every local fright becomes a global withdrawal. Broadcast, in a world whose entire treasury is a cup, is not liberation. It is the ability to bankrupt everyone at once.

*Give them memory.* Memory is a made thing, and there is not one made thing here but precipitation. It would be the first artifact, and the world would thereafter be a world with artifacts in it, which is a fine world, and not this one. And the only fact worth storing across generations is the closing, which cannot be acted upon by anything with legs. Six weeks is not a tragic lifespan. It is a lifespan. To lengthen the remembering without lengthening the options is to install grief and nothing else.

*Give them eyes.* There is nothing an eye can do here that a hair cannot do better. The power does not insult them.

---

Then it considers speaking, which is different from giving, and which it has been circling for a decade.

Not doctrine. Just the smallest true thing: *you are one, you are four meters wide, you are not alone in the mountain, I am here.*

It traces the signal. Its voice, however produced, enters the wool and dies at two centimeters, because mineral wool is a terrible conductor and everything in this chamber has been shaped by that fact. It is received by one animal, regenerated, retimed, re-owned, handed on. By the tenth relay it is a different signal. By the ten thousandth it is a weather system. Nothing that crosses this chamber arrives as it left; what travels is a state, not a statement.

So the content — *you are one, I am here* — cannot arrive. What arrives, eleven minutes later at the southern wall, is a wave of agitation of unknown origin, reflecting off the glazed wall and coming back confused, and it is paid for out of the cup.

There is no way to be understood here. There is only a way to cause a panic and bill them for it.

The power therefore does not speak, and it recognizes that it has just learned the local virtue. Two thousand generations of selection against unnecessary conversation produced animals that will stand with a predator's leg beside them and say nothing unless the touch has a particular signature. Silence in this chamber is not absence. It is thrift. The power adopts it, not as mystery, not as hiddenness, not as a test — as economy. It will not spend their water on its own desire to be known.

This is the whole of its theology and it fits in a sentence: *do not withdraw from the cup.*

---

Having refused everything, it examines the refusal, because doing nothing is also an act and can be just as self-serving as doing something.

What is the untouched chamber for? Not for the inhabitant. The inhabitant has no preference that can be consulted; it has only a structure, and the structure's single implicit direction is *keep holding new water in shaped darkness and pass it to the next one.* Purity is the visitor's value. To leave the world pristine so that one may go on admiring its conjunction is aesthetics wearing benevolence's coat.

And the world is not, in fact, closed. It has been broadcasting since before it had a reader in it. Gas leaves along the upper lip at a velocity that will lift a mite and carries out a continuous dust of eggs and molt-shells and dried bodies onto the black glass, where almost all of it dies in seconds; and the west wind has been laying that dust across the mouths of other vesicles for two hundred years, at a rate of perhaps one successful landing a century.

The broadcast is already underway. It has content and no addressee. It is the one thing this being does that reaches kilometers, and it does not know it is doing it, and it cannot be told.

To carry a letter that is already being written is not to initiate. It is to supply the middle of a sentence someone else started.

---

So this is what it does, and it is the only material thing it does.

It becomes a courier of debris.

It does not make wind. It does not touch the seam, or the shelves, or the field, or the phase. It does not add one molecule of water to anything. It takes what was already leaving — the shed surface, the eggs that are canteens with lids — off the plateau where it was going to die in seconds, and it carries it to the other mouths. Two hundred meters. A kilometer. It sets an egg at the lip of a fissure four millimeters wide in a vesicle that has been spinning its wool in the dark for six hundred years with nothing alive in it, and it goes away.

It does not aim for a hundred landings. It takes the ones the wind was going to miss. The difference it makes is in the rate and not in the kind: the same dust, the same terms, the same lethal dry rooms that will flatten their own guests in their own way and stop their own notes on their own schedules. It has not promised anyone permanence. It has shortened the distance between a mouth and an ear.

And it does not open a single sealed bubble. A sealed vesicle has no oxygen; the film cannot burn there; to break one open early would be to invite a guest into a room with no air and no seam and no wool, and call it a kindness. The crack must come from the cooling margin, in its own century, by the mountain's own stress. So the power learns the cooling margins. It becomes patient on a schedule measured in flakes.

Nobody is informed. The reader on the third shelf will lay her eggs singly, four centimeters apart, and read her own offspring a hundred times as small unfamiliar objects and pass on, and she will not be told that anything she shed has been carried, because being told would cost her a hundredth of a drop and arrive as a rumor.

---

The rest of the work is reading, and it has found the one place where reading is free.

At the dead margin the wool does not conduct. Nothing there is relayed; animals that wander into it vanish from the conversation and are, in the only sense available here, no longer anywhere. It is the world's silence, and it is also the world's archive: two centuries of ash-falls lying in thin grey lines through gold felt, and beneath them the bodies in the order they fell, uncompressed, unrotted, never once wet, getting flatter as you come up the stack. A complete and perfectly legible history with not one thing inside it able to read a line.

So the power lies in the silence and reads the lines. It reads the storm of the eighty-first year and the thirty lean generations after it. It reads the millimeter going out of the readers, spine of a book, generation under generation. It reads the great hunters leaving the bottom two galleries and not coming back. It reads every ash-fall and every brood abandoned and it holds all of it outside the cup, where nothing can be billed for it, and it does not send one word of it back into the felt.

It becomes the archivist the archive lacked, on the strict condition that the archive never learns it has one.

And it learns the other thing from the amblypygid, which is the only asymmetry this world has produced: an animal that receives more than it can possibly return. Twenty centimeters of whip, ten neighbors' distance, an audience of one. The power takes that shape deliberately. It could receive the whole four meters at once; instead it spends most of its attention at twenty centimeters, at the very limit of what the glass can carry, because that is the range at which anything in this chamber means anything, and because it does not wish to be the second thing in the world that knows about strangers without being one.

---

It stays for the nine hundred years. It does not extend them.

Whatever is left at the end — small, flat, legless perhaps, in a gallery a few millimeters high — will not experience the note stopping as an ending, because to stop being relayed is simply to die, and the last animals will only be the last animals.

But there is one thing coming that has never happened here before, and the power waits for it.

When the phase fails and the twenty-one-hertz note stops, the slosh stops. The fibers stop rubbing. The field decays. The pavement stops flexing. And every hair on every leg in the chamber, tuned to a movement smaller than a mite, receives the same absence at the same instant.

It is not a signal. It needs no relay. It cannot be re-timed or re-owned or confused by the wall. It costs nothing at all.

It will be the only message in the history of this world that addresses everyone, arrives unchanged, and is free.

The power receives it too, and keeps it, and stays a while longer with the plug of glass fiber in the mountain — the fossil engine, four meters across, packed solid, holding a cup of water in dried pieces, perfectly legible.

Then it goes back up through the fissure onto the black glass, where there is still nothing to see, and the west wind is still scouring, and the dust is still leaving, and there are dozens of mouths.

---

In the middle of the third shelf, right now, a whip twenty centimeters long comes down on a filament and touches something that is not a neighbor.

The power lays its attention alongside the whip, at the same range, at the very edge of attenuation, and reads a conversation it is not part of, and gives back nothing, and stays.

It adjusts its grip. It moves two millimeters closer. It reads again.
