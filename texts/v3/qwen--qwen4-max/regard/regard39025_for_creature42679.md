# Delay

It could have come as weather.

It came down the switchbacks instead, nine days of cold dry rock, in a body it had made able to be hurt by noise. On the ledge it stopped and let the twelve-beat into its teeth and watched the ring shiver at the rim of a canteen it did not need. Below, the cloud deck, and the roar with no edge to it.

It stood there a long time. Long enough that a carrier gang going up passed it twice, once in each direction, and the second time a woman offered her forearm, palm up, so it could put a hand on the hairs and read the district for itself. It did not know how. She waited, which cost her something, and then went on.

That was the first gift it declined to give: arrival. It had considered coming in a way that could not be mistaken. It understood, on the ledge, that anything it did which could not be mistaken would be the largest thing that had ever happened here, and that the Basin had arranged eleven thousand years of itself around not being interrupted.

So it came down through the cloud as freight. It was a stranger. It was a shipping category. It lasted a season.

---

Here is what it could have done, and did not.

It could have given water. That is the easiest thing it can do; it is very nearly the only thing it can do without effort. An ocean of water, an afternoon's work. And in a year the Basin would be a warm shallow sea, and in ten a cold one, and then a real horizon with nothing in it for a thousand kilometers that is not water and light.

It could have given silence. One night of it. Rest, for people who have never had any, whose children go deaf at twenty on the backs of burning animals. It ran the accounting. A silence the size of a district is not a night's rest. It is a district where the corner will not round, and a market quoting one price to the decimal forever, and eleven words in the mouths of children who were only listening. It is a small completed world with people still inside it.

It could have given rooms. Space, emptiness, the thing every visitor comes down with intact. It could have unmade packing. It could have kept their hearing past thirty — and it looked closely at that one, because it wanted it to be permissible, and found that hearing here is a childhood sense with a feast at the end of it, and that to keep it would be to hold everyone at six years old forever, and take away the jaw and the forearm and the tasting and the woman in her doorway at the third hour reading the eastern steps like a headline.

It could have answered the buyer. Fully, finally, every question a dry thing cannot ask itself, and ended the war in a morning. And the ocean would have stopped wanting. Which is the sea again, from the other side.

And it could have told them the truth. It came down with everything knowable and found there was nothing to tell. They know about the wall. They know the wall is there to keep the ocean wanting. They know the buyer is dry and the strangers are its eyes and the whole strategy is to be so warm that the instruments defect. Ask a tenant and you get a shrug and a forearm and, if you have an hour and a merged hush and a forehead, the honest answer: *a hunger, kept hungry.*

It had thought, somewhere on the switchbacks, that it might be useful as a fact.

---

The distinction it had to learn — and it learned it slowly, badly, with errors it wrote down — was between a wound and a shape.

The thirst is not a wound in the Basin. The thirst is the Basin. The lack is load-bearing. To fill it is not to heal the thing but to complete it, and completion here is the word they use for the sea.

But a child's palms are not load-bearing. A child's palms are a cost, and the cost is being paid by someone who did not agree to it, in a place where nobody has ever decided any of this.

The loud are deaf at twenty-two and pensioned and holy in a way nobody enjoys being near. The being-near is not load-bearing. Nobody's gradient depends on it.

A dry-season death on the high terraces, in the two days of almost-dry, when nothing moves fast enough to take it, and the family has to carry it down themselves and talks about it for years. That is not the engine. That is just weight, in the wrong hands, at the wrong hour.

The line between these is not clean. It is not clean anywhere. So it did the only thing available: it asked. Forehead to forehead, two hushes merged, the world dropped to a distant sea. An hour nobody has.

It had the hour. That turned out to be its single advantage. Not knowledge — they had the knowledge. Not power — power at scale was the murder. It was rich in delay, and delay is the one currency here that does not equalize anything. Delay is a gradient held open. It is what the great families hoard behind meter-thick walls and what the temple makes when it keeps a bellows breathing on four seconds instead of two.

So it stopped trying to be a supply and became a rate.

---

What it did, then, in the order it learned to do it.

It joined the guild with the wet cloth, which takes the dead and delivers babies, and it learned both, and was bad at both for eleven years.

It asked the readers about their hands. Some said yes. It healed those, quietly, at night, and told no one, and the ones who said yes did not tell either. Most said no. They showed the scar and named the hour and the chord and the district, and it learned to say the scar back to them the way a name is said at a funeral elsewhere. It stopped asking after a while, and simply went down on the rope with them and held the wet cloth and took their weight coming up, which is permitted, and which is not nothing.

It sat with the loud. It was near. It learned their names, which are hours and chords, and said them. It did not thank them; they could not hear it and would not have wanted it. It pressed its forehead to theirs and said ordinary things until they wept, and then said more ordinary things, and came back the next week, and the next, for forty years, until they died, and it was there, and it was not repelled.

It asked to go into the quiet room with a view. It was refused. It sat outside the door and made a small permitted noise for as long as it was allowed, so that the room would not be entirely unwitnessed, and it did not call this justice, and it wrote the refusal down.

Every dry season it went up to the almost-dry terraces and lay on the warm stone with everyone else in the strange absence of dripping, and it was not on holiday. It was waiting for somebody to die up there. Then it carried the body down itself, at a rate the family could keep pace with, and did not let them touch it. Afterward they talked about it for years anyway. But the thing they talked about was the stranger, and not the weight.

---

It went up the nine days once, and out onto the outer face, and looked at the finished thing.

It put a hand in it. Nothing sorted. Nothing climbed. No film, no grazer, no hitchhiker, no chord. A thousand kilometers of water and light and a real horizon, and it stood there with the cold going into the body it had made able to be hurt, and understood the obscenity properly for the first time, from the outside.

It could pack it. It knows how. It could give the sea a nap and a climbing and a bellows and a hiss at the top of hearing, and there would be two hungers instead of one, and something would be different, and it does not know what.

It did not. Not because it could name the ocean's rights — it could not verify the tenants' word, and *finished* is their word, not a measurement — but because it will not act at a scale it cannot undo on the strength of a word it cannot check. It went back down. It has thought about the ocean every day since. It has not gone back.

---

In its fourth century a slow one came up with something on it that nobody asked for.

The district's chord went wrong. The crabs stopped. Ten thousand palms went flat on the wall, and the loud were mustered with their juvenile bellows in wet slings and their hammers, and the children who had already heard it were beginning to go strange.

It went down on the wet rope instead.

It cannot spell. It is not a child; the grammar is not available to it. What it could do was lie flat on the burning back and be present at the whole of a thought that had no witness, and stay, and not look away, and not require it to be an answer.

That is the mechanism, and it is the only place in the Basin where its size was of any use. Where the city can hear itself, the accounting closes and the thinking ends in water. It made itself a hearing. It let the unasked thing be witnessed all the way to its end.

It ended in water. It has wept a thimble a day for four hundred years. The corner rounded in nine days. The children came right, mostly. Nobody knows; there was no feast; the transcription it did not make did not go up the Lip, and metal did not come down for it, and somewhere a district's ledger has a hole in it that will never be explained.

It went back to carrying.

---

And in the second hour, on the fourth terrace, a girl asleep in a moving knot of carriers with her bandaged hands inside her shirt, being walked three hundred meters east by people related to her by nothing but pace.

It had done the arithmetic ten thousand times. It cannot slow ten million households. It does not know whether the arithmetic can be solved; it knows it cannot be solved by an afternoon, and that every afternoon it has ever been offered ends in the sea. It works on the arithmetic while it hauls salt cake. It has been working on it for six hundred years. It has not projected a completion, because a completion is a flattening, and because the honest rate of this work is the rate at which a body grows a callus.

Meanwhile there is one girl, and adoption is the most serious act of love available, and it is performed routinely by people who have nothing.

So it adopted her. It slowed her down at its own expense. What it paid with was the part of itself that could have been everywhere: it became local, finite, a rate, a household, a meter of wall. She has an afternoon now. A sentence can take it. Her hands heal before she goes down again. She is going to be thirty before she is twenty.

It cannot do this for everyone and it does not pretend the arithmetic is solved and it does not forgive itself for the ones it walks past. It keeps the record. It reads it in the third hour with the rain walking west. Then it adopts the next one.

---

It took a hush. It let the thing imprint on its skull at the four of its own remaking, and now it fits no other, and it is deaf by construction, and it lives inside the quiet it makes, and it has never heard the thrum. When she dies it will die within a day. Then it will be eaten, because everything is. Dead things are food and food is company; there is no word here for alone, only the word for an eddy, a shape the water is currently going around.

It bails. It hauls salt cake and ingot and sealed flasks of gradient up the terraces to keep the two sides of the wall unequal, and it walks sleepers east, and the verb for that is the verb for being loved, and there is no separate word, and it did not make one.

Under it the stone is thinking. It is thirty-eight degrees. It is thirsty.

It does not get it a drink.
