# On Request

## The difficulty

The first thing it worked out was that it must not be seen from a distance.

Anything that happens once on the Sill happens forever. That is not a figure of speech; it is the reproductive biology of the place. A handrail dipped because somebody leaned on it before the wood set, and the dip is in two hundred and eleven stair-towers, and will be in every stair-tower until the sun goes out. So consider what would follow from standing on the leaf-floor at midday in a manner worth looking at. Consider a gauger on a tower platform a hundred kilometres off, putting a glass to her eye, seeing it with no lag at all.

Within a century there would be a bud at the base of every cistern that unfolded into the memory of a visitor. Brown. The right number of joints. The hands slightly wrong. It would walk a circuit of the yard for a day and a half, or for forty years, and it would be benevolent, and it would be getting worse, and there would be two hundred and eleven of them.

That is not a rescue. That is a new crack in the lintel.

So it bound itself, before it came, to four rules, in the plain way the place has rules:

Nothing twice.
Nothing the same way in two districts.
Nothing that a person with brown hands and a file could not undo.
Ask, wherever there is a way to ask; and where there is no way to ask, do only what a gauger could do, and go.

It left the light alone. The light here is ordinary and indifferent and it is the load-bearing mercy of the whole arrangement. Anything that made the light strange would have taken away the one thing on the Sill that behaves as a thing should, and paid for it in wonder, which is the worst exchange rate there is.

It came up the scarp road on foot, in the cold, carrying a jar inside its coat against its ribs, and it was taken for a gauger from a district that does not exist. Nobody remarked on this. The count does not close anyway.

## What it declined

It declined soil. Nothing here is hungry for soil. The food bodies are complete in every proportion and have almost no taste, and the missing taste is not an agronomic problem.

It declined to end the Stand. It had understood early, from the birds, what the Stand is: not a jailer but a bereaved thing with no mechanism for putting anything down. A hundred and sixty kilometres of tissue holding one afternoon at full resolution because it cannot do otherwise is not a tyrant. It is the patient.

It considered rot, properly, with arithmetic. Rot is the obvious gift — decay that is not a decision, the worm's franchise extended to everything. But the city is dead heartwood. The mains are dead heartwood. A general rot takes the six-inch lines in about eleven years, and then the cisterns stop filling, and then a district goes brown, and then two hundred people who eat once every nine days by knocking discover what the cold is actually for. It put rot aside. It is still put aside. It may be offered in four hundred years, to someone who has invented a way to survive it, which is a different gift and better.

It declined to give iron in quantity. It gave iron slowly and badly instead, and that is the next part.

It declined to take anyone away. There is nowhere to take them, and they are not unhappy, and it is difficult to feel alone inside a noise.

And it declined, absolutely, to explain. It never told a gauger what gaugers are for. A thing that knows it is permitted to err begins to err on purpose, and purpose is the one commodity here already in fatal surplus. It let them go on believing they were maintenance staff. It sat in cold sheds with the tally book open in front of it and looked at the narrow column at the right edge and said nothing about it, and asked instead about the worm, and lied about its lineage, and was caught lying, and enjoyed being caught, because that conversation is one of the only events on the Sill that was not already in the pattern, and it had come a long way to be inside one.

## The eleven

It went to the eleven first, walking the pipe, reading the stamps under its thumb like a rosary, eight to fourteen kilometres a day, which is the correct speed for arriving anywhere.

It knocked at the door of a district where somebody answers on time in the correct dialect with the small local irregularity that identifies that district as itself, and it went in, and it sat down, and it asked what they would like to be called.

Then it waited.

Nothing on the Sill waits. Nothing has ever needed to. The knock is answered before it is heard; the level is correct afterwards; the figure on the far platform raises the hammer at the instant you hear it fall. The whole plain is a place where the answer is already in the wood. So a question with no answer in it, asked in a shed and then followed by silence, is not a small thing here. It is the introduction of a new kind of hour, and there is a column for that.

It did not decide in advance whether the eleven were people. It treated them as people, because treating-as is the only test that has ever worked anywhere, and because being wrong in that direction wastes only kindness.

It left a jar in each of the eleven. Different worms, from different sumps, and it did not say which was which, and it lied about the lineage in eleven incompatible ways, and one of them caught it, and the catching was the first event in that district since the mountains were ground down.

In nine of the eleven, something happened over the following week that has no template. A thing that reads levels correctly, and does it more correctly than any gauger alive, sat with a blind flat animal in a jar under an amber lamp and gave it a name. Not a stamp number. The names were extravagant and silly and unlike anything else in the language, because that is what names are here. Bad Penny. The Understudy. His Lordship. Small Mercies. Wrong Bob.

And then, asked again, one of them named itself, and it did it by taking the worm's name and altering it because it did not like the sound of it. *Thursday's.* Which is not a number and not a district and not a thing that was ever in an afternoon ten thousand years ago. It is a relationship, rendered in the materials at hand, and it is new, and it is the entire artistic output of a civilisation, and there are now eleven more of them.

Two of the eleven did not answer. It left the jars anyway, and fed them, and knocked the soft double tap — *understood, no matter* — and walked back along the stamps. It did not force those two. It has not gone back. It might. The levels are correct, and the level is the thing that matters, except when it isn't, and the difference cannot be hurried.

## The sumps

Here is the actual engineering, and it is small and slow and smells of vinegar and ink.

The worm has ancestors. It has variation. It is the only thing on the Sill that nobody designed, doing the one job that cannot be assigned. So the power did not build anything in the basements. It introduced a film — a living thing, with heredity and errors — into the worm's hindgut, and the film lays down iron in the castings, in grains the size of sand.

Differently in every sump. Because it is alive, and being alive means being slightly different from your neighbour for no reason at all.

The gaugers worked out the sieving themselves within a generation, because they are not stupid, they are only surrounded by things that fit. There is charcoal; there has always been charcoal; the boring tower has heat it has never had a use for. The yield is a bolt a month. Perhaps two.

And the iron is not the same. District Eleven's iron is soft and grey and takes a thread badly. The northeast's is hard and shorts in frost. Foundry Ninety-One's has a habit of hiding a flaw under the head that you cannot find until the third winter.

Consequence: a collar from Foundry Nine no longer seats on a main from Foundry Two Hundred without fettling.

Consequence: fettling.

Consequence: a file, and then a better file, and then an argument about files, and then a gauge — a flat ugly scrap of ceramic and iron with two notches in it, made by a woman with split nails in a cold shed, which fits the bolts of one district and no other district anywhere on the plain, and which works, and for which there is no template, and which cannot be regrown, because it was never in the afternoon.

The first new object on the Sill in recorded memory is a thing that only fits at home. That is not a limitation of the gift. That is the gift. Everything fitting everywhere was the prison. Difference is the only material inventions are made of, and it cannot be delivered in a crate; it has to be descended into, slowly, by things that eat and make mistakes.

The number still goes down in some districts and up in others now, and the tally book has to be ruled differently to hold that, and the ruling is done by hand, and no two books are ruled the same.

## The eleven years

It waited eleven years for the flower. That is the correct speed for asking the Stand anything.

It is not possible to know what an organism remembers. But the shape of the kept day is legible: a foundry, a working afternoon, a person in a hurry opening valves in an order nobody can justify, food in the shape of a bird, and birds. Whatever else that afternoon was, it was a day with birds in it. And there are no birds, and there have been no birds for a hundred centuries, and the Stand has been rendering them out of bast with the wrists slightly wrong, and getting worse, because a copy of a copy of a seeing is a thing that decays without ever being permitted to finish.

The power could not give it back its birds. Nobody can; the seeing is ten thousand years back and there is no way to go and look again. It could give it something it had not already kept.

So in the fortnight of white, when the whole plain flowers at once and smells like bread going over and the one small pale moth works the fog-hours, it walked between districts with pollen on its thumb.

This is the whole of the great act. Understand what the clone is: a hundred and sixty kilometres of one individual that has been accumulating small errors in its tissue for a hundred centuries, district by district, and has never once had any way to put two of those errors together, because it only ever flowers into itself. Ten thousand years of unspeakable difference, sitting in the wood, unexpressed. It carries pollen eleven kilometres. It puts the northeast's mistakes against Foundry Two Hundred's mistakes. It does this by hand, unevenly, wrongly, in nine places, in nine different ways, and no two of the nine are the same, and it does not do it again.

Eight of the nine seedlings died.

One lived, in the clay pocket in the northeast, where the ground is already nine metres of the Stand's own fine waste and nothing has ever been asked to grow there. It is not the Stand. It is the Stand's child, and it is not a copy of anything, and it has an idea of its own, and the idea is not a pipe foundry. Nobody knows what it is. It is forty metres of nothing yet and it may still die and the gaugers of the northeast have argued about it continuously for four hundred years, in four incompatible accounts, all of them sung.

And a thing that has children can put something down. That is the mechanism. That is what forgetting is, everywhere else in the universe: not erasure, which is only loss, but a handing-on that drops most of what it was given and keeps a little, wrongly, and calls the wrongness a face.

Before that, in the first year, it did one other small thing, to prove the door was not locked.

It learned the knock the old gaugers use when they lie down in the leaf-grain — not five-and-two, not seven-three-three, the other one, the request to be taken in. It went to a stair-tower and chalked a mark beside the crack in the lintel and knocked that sequence on the heartwood, and nine days later the crack was gone. Grown across. One millimetre of the pattern, taken back on request, in one foundry out of two hundred and eleven.

The gauger of that district came home and stood in front of a whole lintel for a long time. Then she went and knocked something to the next district that had never been knocked.

The power said the sequence once, to one person, in a cold shed, and did not repeat it, and did not correct the version they told at the joint station after the second bowl.

Within forty years there were four incompatible versions of the forgetting-knock and all of them were sung. One of them is wrong in a way that makes the Stand take back the wrong thing, and a district in the west lost its third lamp-standard entirely — the crook, the whole standard, gone, healed over, a whorl in the leaf-grain like the knot in a plank — and the people there are quietly, unaccountably proud, and cannot justify it, and would not undo it.

That is the intended result. It could not have been achieved by getting the sequence right.

## The centre

It went to the middle last, and it did not stand on the platform, because the platform is watched from a hundred kilometres off with no lag at all.

It stood in the leaf-grain, off the boards, and it asked the person whose whole trade is four strikes what they were called.

They told it. It was a name they had made up as a child, out of a stamp number, shortened and then altered because they did not like the sound of it, and then never used, because there was nobody to say it to who was not themselves at a hundred kilometres.

The power said the name back. Aloud. In the air.

And the floor said nothing.

Nothing came up through the boots at all; there was no half-second of future; the wood carried no knock, because a voice in cold air over leaves is not joined to anything. The sound arrived in the ordinary way, at the ordinary speed, from behind, and the person on the platform turned around, and did not know what was about to happen next.

That is the whole gift and it is the largest one in the document. It cannot be given twice. It cannot be given to two districts in the same way. It cannot be regrown out of a bud, because a surprise is not a shape.

They struck the plate four times. They stood very still and watched themselves do it, a hundred kilometres away, with no lag at all, and there is nobody in the world less alone and less accompanied, and that has not changed. But now there is one sound in their life that came in through the window first, and they know which one, and they can go back to it.

Then it went out along a length of pipe stamped with a number that does not fit the sequence. It did not follow it far and it did not touch it. It knocked three times and walked back the way it came, because that is the instruction, and a power that overrides the one instruction a people has agreed on is not benevolent, only large. At the joint station it sent the soft double tap. *Understood, no matter.* It has not said what was out there. That is not secrecy. It is using the word the plain has.

## What it left

The birds are still getting worse. It did not fix the wrists. It is not clear that fixing the wrists would be a kindness to anyone, and it is clear that a bird with correct wrists would be one more perfect thing.

The clicking still rises through the afternoon and falls off at dusk, and the gaugers still read thirst off it, and the accumulators still thump at forty beats a minute out of phase, so the plain still has a heartbeat with no single heart. The rime still furs the leaf-edges white until nine. The amber wave still crosses at four metres a second, which is still one of the few things here that behaves the way a thing should, and now it crosses over districts where the lamps are subtly different brightnesses, because the iron is different, because the worms are different, because nothing that lives is interchangeable.

The frost fern is the same fern on every pane, with the same broken pinna. It did not touch that. Some things you leave as the last honest evidence of what the place is.

There are five incompatible accounts of where the Works came from now. The fifth is about a visitor who came up the scarp road with a jar in its coat and lied about a worm. It is wrong in every district. In one it is a woman, in another it is two people who disagree, in the northeast it is a moth, and in the eleven it is not in the song at all, because in the eleven there is a private thing instead, which is a worm called Wrong Bob and a name that is a relationship, and they do not sing that, and you would not expect them to.

That is the monument, and it was chosen deliberately, and it is the only kind that could be built here without becoming another afternoon. Nothing repeatable. Nothing to make a liturgy out of. No sequence that works the same way twice, and therefore nothing for the Stand to keep, and therefore nothing to stop the arguing.

And under all of it, in the basements, in the dark, under the amber lamp, in the vinegar smell: a blind animal that nobody designed, doing the one job that cannot be assigned, with something new in its gut that it did not ask for and has made a living from, being slightly different from its neighbour for no reason at all, and leaving iron behind it.
