It came as far as the bend and stopped, because the bend was occupied.

This is the only property of it that matters here: it has a clause for outcome. Everything in the hollow has an address. The louse has an address nine hundred generations old, the comb has an address that got lucky, the heavy-legged fowl has a turn it inherits and does not know it has, the door has the shape of a U and no opinion about it. The power is the one thing in the vicinity that can hold a future in mind and be hurt by it. It came knowing what the mould was moulded around. It is not going to say. The shards outside are a language with one word and it has not come to add a word.

It went in on a breath, through the ninth, and the flat of a body brushed its ankle and let it pass, because it was not on the floor. It stood up in the racket. The chains came and climbed its leg to check — *fallen or standing* — and it answered standing, correctly, which is all the question asks, and they went on about their work. It stayed fifty minutes. At fifty minutes it moved, because it had understood the boots rule and had no intention of being an exception to it.

Then it worked out what it was permitted to do, which took longer than the visit.

---

Everything here becomes wall, or becomes export, or becomes sound. There is no fourth outcome. So a gift has exactly three fates. If it becomes wall it adds mass, and mass is pitch, and pitch is the whole resident; a finger's width in the wrong place is two centuries of wasps chasing a mistake, and the mistake would be *it*. If it becomes sound it is a false note in a room that argues every noise into its own frequencies, which means the room would correct it, which means it would be spent doing damage and then erased. If it becomes export it feeds the ring, and the ring feeds the marsh, and the marsh sends the midges, and the midge cloud is the only income. Even charity propagates.

So: add nothing that will not be eaten. Add nothing that cannot be carried out by morning. Do not add a word.

And check for a sufferer first. It checked. This is the part that took the afternoon, and most of the checking came back empty, and the emptiness is not a disappointment, it is a finding.

---

It did not bring the animal back.

It could have. It knows the shoulder and the fluting and the taper and the cloth the width of a forearm; it could have stood the whole shape up on the spoil among ten thousand basket-loads and let it come down through the crown. And it would have fouled at the seams. The fluting is shallower than the fluting that made it. The pins stand proud of a surface that has crept up around them. Nine hundred years of insects correcting a pitch have grown the negative over in exactly the places where a body would need clearance. To house the thing that left, it would have had to un-improve the copy — thin the walls, drop the note, undo the valves, kill the resident to make room for the former tenant. It declined. It declined on the resident's behalf, which is the only side available.

It did not give the louse a model of the future.

That was the temptation, and it was the strongest one, and it lasted most of the hour. One female with a clause for outcome. One louse that could compare the dent to the brood and go elsewhere. It ran it forward and found what it would be installing: the first thing in this chamber that waits. Waiting is the beginning of the only harm here that has no repair, because there is no chain that carries it out and no wasp that can plaster it flat. And there is no sufferer in the dent. It looked twice. There is an instruction and a dry oval and nine hundred generations of correctness with nothing at the end of it, and no one inside it to be disappointed. Also: the comb slid off the ghost and onto the instrument, and the eleven other stations slid, and everything else slid, and the louse is the last measurement of the animal that has not been edited. To fix the louse is to finish the replacement. It left the address alone. It did not decide whether the dent is an eye.

It did not correct the seventh, or widen the crack, or seal it. It did not plaster the fourth understudy or save the old one; the burial is how a wall acquires a valve. It did not hold the node in place — the node is going to walk when the scaffold goes, and a family that learns the silence walks is better off than a family whose silence was pinned by something outside and then, one winter, wasn't.

It was afraid for the reed for about a minute. Then it looked at the ring outside and understood that the ring is the same rootstock, and that the reed inside is not one reed, it is one reed's finger, and it stood down.

It did not open a forty-second flue. It has a voice. It did not use it.

---

**The door.** This is the only thing it does forever.

When the flat pale thing in the bend dies there is a gap, and in the gap the marsh pushes silt into a U that nothing is grazing, and the young are out in the pans being eaten at a rate that leaves one survivor in some years. *Some years* is the whole of the margin. A late death and an early silt and the lock chokes, and then the air goes bad, and then the water comes up, and then the note stops, and everything in the hollow becomes wall or export in a single season with no one to tune it.

Nobody counts the door. So it counted the door, and then it underwrote the succession: one flat pale young one kept alive in a tile joint outside, against the odds, for as long as it takes, and moved a hand's breadth when the bend empties. That is all. It costs something — whatever would have eaten that young one goes hungry once, and the power does not pretend the meal was free. It buys the hollow its throat. It will do this for as long as there is a hollow, and it will never be visible, and the door will not know, because the door cannot distinguish the total note from ordinary racket and could not distinguish being counted from not being counted either.

That is not a reason to skip it.

**The office.** It did not remove the tongue. The tongue is not an animal, it is a job, and the job is what happens to things that land wrong. But the office is never vacant long — something else comes up out of the pans within a season — and among the somethings there are several that do not carry the worm.

It arranged that the next tenant is one of those.

The worm goes marsh to midge to tongue and then, briefly, into a family, and the family has a word for the colic and connects it to nothing. Now the family will go on connecting it to nothing, and be right. The word will become a word for a thing that used to happen. No one will be told. Nothing will be revealed at the node, no stranger will come back with a doctrine, the trade will not acquire a theory it did not earn. Six people with clay on their backs will simply stop having the colic, and their pulse will stay under fifty, and they will hum two beats and teach the children the pitch before the door.

The worm loses a host. It keeps the marsh and the midge. The power checked that too, and then chose the six against the worm, and wrote it down as a cost, because it was one.

**The green.** The caterpillar has a date. The lichen is winning; a few hundred years and there is no metal in the hollow at all, and the instruction *lay where the copper is* will have nowhere to be correct.

It did not make copper. It moved a hand. A child digging spoil on the lee side turned up a green pin, thumb-length, and carried it in on a back already loaded with clay, and put it in a pin-hole, because it fit. Children do this. Within a generation the trade has a second clause that nobody can justify — *put the green back in the holes* — sitting alongside *add it where the note is flat*, and the wasps do not undo it, because a thumb-length of copper in a hole it was made for is acoustically nothing at all.

This buys thousands of years and then stops, because it stops when the family stops. The power did not underwrite it. It is not going to guarantee the caterpillar forever. It gave it a longer date and left the date in the hands of people who can die.

**The nightjar.** The seventh is two species made by a mistake in a wall, tuned as faithfully as their cousins, arrived a fifth away, and when the crack widens they will be out-competed in a season and plastered by autumn. Nothing in there is waiting for anything and nothing in there will be consoled.

The lure already leaks. There is a nightjar on the spoil taking a share of the cloud with a call close enough to the note. The power spent one night making the wrong pitch audible outside — a straw's width of it, at the right hour, at the right pressure — and the nightjar, which learns, took a second phrase.

The heretics still die when the crack widens. Their arrival does not. Somewhere on a hillside of shards that has never been indoors, a bird now says the thing that a badly-set basket-load said eleven hundred years ago. The power did this because there is only one true note in the world and only one wrong one, and it was not willing to lose the second. It does not call this mercy. It calls it keeping a copy.

**The peg.** There is a borer endemic to nine cubic feet of swollen oak, and when the scaffold goes the borer goes with it, and every property line in the chamber moves at once.

The power cannot stop the scaffold going. It would not; the modes shifting is the last source of anything new in here, and the daubers chasing the shift for two centuries is the resident doing the only thing it does. But it looked outside and found a willow lying drowned in the pans, and then it needed a way to get grubs from nine cubic feet of oak to a wet tree in a marsh, and there is only one road out and it is a chain of blind porters carrying rubbish.

So on a winter night, when nobody was inside, it broke one peg and let it fall on the tile.

Nothing may remain on the floor. By morning the peg was gone, hand to hand to hand, down the chain in the dark, out through the bend, into the ring, and the grubs went with it, and some of them may find the willow. It advanced the scaffold's collapse by a little. It paid that. It did not stay to see whether they took.

**The stranger.** The family is six. It was more.

There is a node waist-high in the racket, four square feet where the sound cancels, and it is where the old ones stand and tell the children which door, and where they take anyone who has come from far away and needs to be told something. The whole trade is one place-name and a pitch and a capacity to be told.

Someone was going to pass on the far side of the spoil that evening. The power bent the path by a hand's breadth — a shard under a boot, a shift of light, nothing that could be remembered afterwards as an event — and they came around on the lee side at dusk, when the cloud was coming down through forty-one flues like smoke going the wrong way, and stood on the doorstep of silence and were told.

Pitch first. Door second. That is the correct order and it is the only word the power permitted itself to add, and it was already the one word, in a new throat.

---

It came back for the total note.

Once or twice a season the pressure and the roosting and the weather agree and all forty-one flues open together and the whole hollow says the thing at full length. The sheet-water shivers into rings. The sweepers stop. Nothing in here made that sound on purpose.

The door receives it as a hand laid on its back forty times a second and feeds better and does not know it happened. The fowl are valves and are not listening. The wasps are quarrying. The old one hums along through a hole in a wall. The midges come down because it is the note of something they used to answer to, and they are not being lied to — that is the finding the power came all this way to make and did not need to make. The copy is a better body for the voice than the voice's own body was. The address is still correct. Something answers.

The power stood at the node where it could hear its own blood, and did not open a forty-second flue, and did not harmonize, and did not say *alive* out loud, because saying it out loud would have put a second word in a field of one word and the room would have argued it into the fundamental and kept it for eleven seconds and then let it go.

It said it silently. It counted the door, and the fourth-door fowl plugging a hole that ventilates nothing, and the louse in the dry dent, and the fourth understudy beginning low on the south side, and the grubs in the pegs, and the six, and the stranger who was learning two beats. Counting leaves nothing on the floor. It is the one gift that costs the room nothing at all and it is not nothing.

Then the bodies resettled, one and one and one, and the chord fell apart into ordinary racket, and it went out through the bend on a breath, and the flat of a body brushed its ankle and let it pass.

In the morning the tile was clear.
