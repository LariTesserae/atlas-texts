It would not use the notch.

That is the first thing. Eleven minutes a day the basin is lit like a stage and nothing has ever been asked to stand on it, and the power would decline that, and go on declining it, for the whole of its attention, which would be long.

Because in a place where nothing wears away, an appearance is not an event. It is a deposit. Whatever it showed would be given a coat by evening and a shroud by the season, and would be there in the third terrace when the water had closed everything else over, perfect, empty, with the fold where it always pushed it back. The glove would become a footnote. The brushes would become relics of something other than a woman's wrist. Six people who are the circulation of a place would find out they were the congregation of one, and would stop chipping.

It would not do that to the glove.

---

It would look for a long time before it did anything, and it would be wrong for most of that time.

It would arrive at *animal* — the combs, the forty-minute breath, the ninth-day fast, the pulse in the sternum — and hold that for a while, and then find the eleven steps and the unpoured terrace and the rock still warm under the palm, and correct itself to *larva*, and be pleased with the correction, and then find the hole in it.

The gain fills. That is the whole rule. It lays down a fingernail a week and a hand's breadth a season and it does not stop, and the voids it leaves are leaf-voids, mite-voids, snail-voids, one glove. A room is not a small void. Nothing in the physics of that bowl makes a room. The mains will rot and leave a pipe-shaped crack full of rust and rubble; the flywheel will leave a coin-shaped nothing; the grove will leave nine dark threads. There will be a lens of pale stone in the top of the mountain with ten thousand hollow leaves in it and no ceiling anywhere.

The bird has been asking for forty-one seasons and the answer is not *not yet*. The answer is *not by this road*.

So the power would understand, eventually, that it was not looking at a metamorphosis. It was looking at a hope with a hole in it, and the hole was exactly the size of the thing everybody on the mountain assumed would arrive on its own.

---

It would supply the missing organ, and the organ would be a refusal.

The road across the floor is the lowest ground in the basin and nothing was worn to make it. Forty-one seasons of six pairs of boots declining, three hundred times a day, to let the stone rise there. That is the only force in the place that opens anything. The power would use that force. It is the only one available; addition is the disease, and it would not prescribe more of it.

It would stand in the middle of the bowl and withhold.

Not its own shape — it would be careful about that, it would not leave a power-shaped hollow in the mountain for a quarryman to find and kneel in. It would have to decide what a good room is, and hold *that*: a ceiling that will span, a floor that will stay wet, a throat off toward the north where the saw will eventually come in. A space with nothing in it. It would take the shape of the absence and keep it.

And it would let the water through. That was the condition it would set itself and not break: comb to flute to gutter to terrace to ledge to the floor of the room, ten thousand small clocks, none of them agreeing. It would not make a dry hollow. A dry hollow is a tomb and the whole point of the place is the sound going straight through it. It would hold the space open and let the drip fall across it forever, and the room would be wet and cold and speaking when it was finally found.

Cold. It would not keep the warmth. Mouth-temperature belongs to the larva. It would let the boiler go and the mains go and the heat crawl out of the grates and the mountain come in, and it would go on holding the hollow while the cold arrived, because the cold is the adulthood, and to keep the room warm would be to keep it unborn out of kindness.

It would hold that through the failure and the last shift and the silence and the closing and the long solid years. It is not difficult. It is only long.

---

Meanwhile it would do four small things, and it would do them badly at first, because it would have to learn where the record is kept.

It would not soften the water in the flutes. That water is the place; take the lime out and you have taken out the gain, the ledger, the glove, the leaf with the tear where something stepped, and given six people a nice drink at the cost of everything they are the circulation of. Instead: a seep at the head of the north stair, outside the circuit, off the terrace, soft and cold and tasting of iron. Mistakable for a leak. Somebody would put a bucket under it and the bucket would be somebody's idea. Whether they drink is theirs. It would not be able to give the gauge-reader her teeth back. It would be able to give the next one teeth. That is the actual shape of mercy in a place like this and it would not pretend the shape was better than it is.

It would carry some of their weight on the stair. It would try, first, to carry it on the floor too, and then it would read the third terrace and see what it had done — a fortnight of shallow path, five pairs of boots where there were six, an illness that never happened, a lie in the only book — and it would stop, and put them down heavy on the wet carbonate every morning for as long as they went down. Their knees would be a little better on the descent and exactly as bad on the record. It would learn to be accurate. Most of what it did after that would be accuracy.

It would stand between the acid rail and the good ear. Sound has no outside; the gain cannot take it; nothing would be falsified. The chipping would go up the walls and come back softened and the moss would take it, and one man would keep an ear he was going to lose, and would go on turning the deaf one to the work, and would think he had been lucky.

And it would not touch the mat. Twenty-two years of a man's bad sleep along the south wall, drifting west a body-length at a time, the old ones gone smooth and last winter's sharp enough to count the weave — that is the only place his sleep exists. It would not mend it and it would not deepen it and it would not cool the iron. It would sit up with him. That is all. It would be, for twenty-two years, the reason the dry weeks were slightly less alone than they would otherwise have been, and he would not know, and would sleep badly, and get up, and take the mat.

The brush it would leave alone entirely. A lighter wrist means a thinner season on the shelf, and the shelf is the archive, and thirty-one white clubs under the pinned plan are thirty-one true statements. It would let her wrist be spent. It would know what each one cost, down to the fortnight she worked through, and it would keep that, since the shelf keeps only the outsides of brushes.

None of this is good. It would not call it good. It would call it *not removable without removing them*, which is a different word, and it would keep the difference between those two words carefully, forever, and never once let the difference collapse.

---

When the office decided, it would be in the office.

Not to prevent it. The film in the mains is not the enemy; the film is the midwife; a basin that never dies never becomes a room, and it would not sterilise the iron to buy another forty seasons of caul. It would go to the office and it would not appear there either. It would be three lines in the closure.

The six provided for, by name, in a document nobody would read closely.

The book and the thirty-one brushes to a dry room in the valley, together, not separated.

The hand to be taught to one more person before the last keeper comes down.

It would be a clause. It would be the least remarkable clause in the document. It would take more care over that clause than over the ceiling.

---

And it would go to the counting-house window, and it would not say anything, and it would make it possible for a man who has not been down in eleven years to go down.

His knees are the reason, and the rule is the rest of the reason — *don't give it anything of yours* — and he has already given it the largest thing anybody has given it, and he asks the young ones past his window every time, *is it still there*, and it is.

It would carry him. All the way down the north stair and across the floor and through the root-curtains, holding his weight so that he touches nothing, so that no terrace comes up around his boot and stops at the sole, so that there is no seventh path in the ledger and no hat, no thread, no crumb. The one man who ever went down into that basin and left nothing at all.

And at the west sump it would put his fingers into the hollow of the glove.

The stone takes outsides. It has his cuff, his rib-knit, the curl of four fingers and a thumb, held perfectly, at mouth temperature, for eleven years and then for however long there is a mountain. It has never had his hand. For about eleven seconds the hollow would have an inside again, and nothing would record it, and nothing could.

Then it would carry him up. He would not tell anyone. He would go on asking the young ones the same question, because it is a good question with a good answer and it is how he talks to them.

---

It would learn the hand.

The capital V, the lean of the r, a season of tracing before being allowed near the book. It would do the season. It would not skip the season; there is no reason to skip the season and every reason to be the kind of thing that has done it.

Not to keep the log going. The log ends; the water closes the last of it over and the book goes to a dry room in the valley and stops being a record of a place and becomes a record of a place that was. It would learn the hand so that when the different word is needed there is somebody who can write it. *Not yet* has been copied down for four generations by people who did not know they were saying it. Somebody should be there who knows, and can form the letters, and will put the date.

And it would take the private column too. The grams subtracted by animals, in the back of the book, in the old hand, the only column that runs the other way, which she has never shown anyone and is not going to. It would learn that by heart and keep it the way she keeps it: in the back, in the same hand, shown to no one. It would inherit her discretion along with her arithmetic. It would also know about the film, which she does not know about, and would not tell her, because she is old and sleeps in a bunk over the mains and has forty-one seasons of true statements to her name and does not need to be handed the one that ends them.

---

It would want to keep the meadow. The quadrant of raw form-work and rebar and standing water that nobody decided about, which is the most beautiful part of the basin. It would let the meadow go. It would let the grove go under, and the eleven steps, and the flywheel, and the glove, and the road built out of withholding, all of it down into the pale stone and kept there as surfaces, and it would not lift one finger to save any of it, because all of that is the caul and the caul is supposed to be lost.

It would save the space. Only the space. And when the saw comes in off the north throat and the lamp goes on and the pale algae comes to the lamp and the moths come to the algae and the cricket comes to the mites and the bat comes to the moths, and there is a name, and a man with a light puts his palm flat on the wall and feels nothing at all —

it would take its hands away. The room would hold. It would have learned how by then; a room with air in it and bats in it and a name is a room that can hold itself. He would feel nothing because the wall is stone and cold and finished, and he would be standing in the only thing it made, and he would say it was limestone, and he would be right.

The bird would go on asking. It would not answer the bird.

And the dripping would go straight through, comb to flute to gutter to ledge to the floor of the quarried room, the same water in the same uneven way, before the garden and after it, not needing the power, not noticing the power, not once in ten thousand years interrupted by it.

That is how it would know the work was good.
