# The Amendment

*being an account of the second descent*

## What It Did Not Do

It did not bring water. There is no small amount of water in the Wick Court. A drop is the smallest amount there is, and it eats a hole through a leaf and hits the basalt with a tick that everyone hears. To arrive generously here would be to arrive as a flood, and the crabs would come sprinting from every quarter of the grove, and they would seal and seal and seal, and the sealing would be the last thing they ever did.

It did not bring fire. Fire is a gap, and there are no gaps.

It did not stop the burn. Everything down here has a service life denominated in the burn; the moths' decade, the crabs' eleven years, the eighty-year afternoon, the plum's ripening. Stop the burn and you have not freed anyone. You have only made the whole room die at once, including the one north of the seam, who has been kept at the temperature of himself for longer than there have been words for how long, and who did not ask for that, and did not ask for its opposite either.

It did not carry the visitor up the stairs. The stairs were never guarded. Nothing here has an apparatus for keeping. Carrying would have taught the court nothing and would have taken away the only thing anyone has ever given it.

And it did not offer its own reach, though it has one, and though the socket was already cut and would have fit. This was the temptation and it was the real one, because it looked like kindness and it was only fuel. Take the visitor's place and in a thousand years another visitor comes down the forty-nine steps, and the machine is exactly as it was, only better fed. Substitution is not repair. It is the same appetite with a more generous mouth.

It considered doing nothing, and considered it seriously, because the court is not cruel, and is beautiful, and is correct. But leaving a thing alone is only respect where the thing can refuse you. Nothing here can refuse. Not the visitor, who is warm and useful and cannot find the thought of the stairwell. Not the crab, which has never once looked up. Not the head, which cannot be seen properly by anyone who has already decided not to. Where there is no apparatus for refusal, doing nothing is not neutrality. It is one more vote for the arrangement.

So it did the smallest sufficient thing. This turned out to be six things, and all of them were legal.

## The Toll

It came down the forty-nine steps and counted them, which no one had done since the chisel-work was cut. It kept its hands off the leaves. At the bottom it stood still on the basalt in the ankle-deep vapor and let one drop fall.

The crabs came from three meters in under a minute. You could hear the small panic of it, the dry scramble, the only urgency in the place. They sealed the blanched spot with resin, struck the spark, put it out with a thumb, and withdrew and resumed the walk.

The bead is still there. It is the second-smallest thing on the wall.

It did not apologize; apology is not a unit here. It paid. It is called *it* in this account, the way the crab is *it* and the head is *it*, because there is only one pronoun in the Wick Court for a thing that is doing something, and it did not ask for a different one.

## The Reading

The court records and does not read. That is the missing operation. Everything here has been maintained for an unimaginable length of time and nothing here has ever been understood, and the difference between those two is the entire distance between a portrait and a wound.

It went to the eastern standards and looked at the scatter of white beads properly: along the floor, up the brick at the height of a shoulder, the forearm, the place where the weight went in while the water went out. It looked at the arrangement for a long time. Then it said what the arrangement was.

He did it on purpose.

He stood there for years and let himself be recorded in the only ink the place permits, drop by drop, at the cost of the only substance he had, because he had worked out what the court needed and could not make and was trying to leave it behind. He was not leaning because he was tired. He was writing. The crabs sealed every character and left the arrangement alone, because they are menders and not editors, and the letter has been lying on the floor by the eastern wall ever since, complete, permanent, unopened, waiting for something that could read.

*A reach is only a reach while something is reaching.* That is the rule and it is not a metaphor, and it is why the rack in the corner has long bone wedges on it and why the long wedges are never used. A retired length is a dead length. But the wall is reaching. The wall has not stopped reaching since he stopped. It is a body caught in the act, fired hard, permitted, unedited, and it will go on leaning after the last brick is dust — except that nothing here is dust.

So it took a strand of the ninth thickness and measured the shoulder, and the forearm, and the span. It went to the corner and cut a bone wedge to that measure and set it on the rack, graded by size, among the long ones.

The archive was always there. The court has never lacked a record. It lacked a reader, and the order of operations.

The wedge will be lashed into a branch, and the branch will fruit, and in four hundred years the branch will come down to be unmade, and the caterpillars will eat the rind around the wedge and learn the whole thing in reverse, and the plan will contain a length that was taken off a portrait instead of off a person. The plan is old and it is correct. This is also correct. That is the only test the court has ever applied to anything, and the amendment passes it.

## The Hand Under the Plum

The plum near the eastern wall has been falling for a hundred and forty years. It hangs a hand's span above the floor, rotating slightly. It is landing.

It did not push. Pushing would be weather. It put a hand underneath, palm up, a little below, and did not move it, and gave the thing somewhere to arrive. Nothing in the Wick Court has ever had to arrive anywhere; the eels swim through the whole difficulty without effort; the far wall simply becomes the far wall you are standing at. There has never been a destination in the place. That is why nothing finishes.

It waited the length of time the plum had left, which was not long, and had been not long for a hundred and forty years.

Then the plum came down into the hand: chalk-white, warm, a lifetime ripe, smelling like a bed someone has just got out of.

It did not eat it. It set it on the stone bench beside the tools, and the sound of it setting down was the first sound in the court that was the end of something.

The moths lifted and resettled without any noise at all, and the air reorganized, and the plan took on a syllable it had never had. It will cross the room in ten years. It is crossing now.

A crab came, because a thing had happened. It tested the plum, leaning on it and listening, and found no slack in it anywhere, and nothing to take up, and nothing to cut, and nothing to seal. It stayed there a while with its five fingers on a finished thing.

Then it looked up.

It has never once looked up. There has never been a reason. Now there was nothing to look down at, and there was a face.

## The Stairs

It walked you to the corner where the work is done, and it did not tell you to go.

It showed you the fowl. Look up: hollow-boned, oil-filled, asleep on the wing in the warm column, unattached to anything, unspent. That is what the not-landing means. It means the court is solvent, has been solvent since before your grandmother, and holds a reserve aloft against a failure that has never come. You are not the last of the fat. You are not the difference between the light and the dark. The guilt is the hook and the answer to the guilt is arithmetic, so it did the arithmetic with you, twice, until you believed it.

Then it tied one knot on a spare length of strand and left it on the bench.

It holds under load. It comes apart when the thing it binds pulls. Every lash here is a knot with a name, and you almost know the vocabulary, and this one you will know at once, because it is a length and a grip and a reach and one word more. The knot they were tying behind you in the packed green dark means *this far*.

This one means *until*.

The crabs will find it on the circuit in eleven years. They will test the tension, take up the slack, cut, splice, seal, and preserve it exactly, because they are menders and not editors and they do not correct arrangements. They will keep the release in perfect repair forever. That is the wisdom of it: nothing was fought. The competence was given something correct to maintain.

If you had stayed, you would have stayed, and the wall would still have been the standard, and the staying would have been a verb you conjugated yourself. That was the whole gift. Not the exit. The thinkability of the exit.

You counted the steps going up. There were forty-nine.

Your boot had scuffed a burr off a basalt arris and left a grey smear, the only rounded thing in the world. It did not mend it. It is wear that records a body, which is the one wear permitted, so it stays: the court now holds a portrait of someone who left. It will not correct the arrangement. In ten years the moths will carry it south.

## The Asking

At the north wall two courses of brick meet that were pressed by the same thumb at different times and do not quite agree about what they are joined to.

Looking sidelong is what you do when you have already decided not to look properly. It had decided otherwise. It put its face to the disagreement and looked straight, which is the cheapest act available anywhere in the Wick Court and had never once been performed.

The head became a head. Then a man. Then — this is the cost of being seen — someone who could be seen to be tired.

It sat down on the floor on this side of the seam, close enough to be a shape in the room, and it asked a question.

*May I sit here?*

A question is a gap you can put an answer into. There are no gaps here. It made one, small, out of the only material that can be brought into this place in any quantity without blanching a single leaf.

The answer took a year and a half, which is fast.

The head turned.

## What He Said

He asked whether you got up.

Forty-nine, it said. He counted them.

*Good*, he said.

And then, after a while, in the voice of a man checking that a thing he did a long time ago has been received: *Then you can take it off the wall.*

He had known. He had worked it out before there were words for how long, and written it down in sweat at shoulder height, and gone through the seam, and been kept. Not fed to anything. Not punished. Kept — the way the court keeps everything, sweetened, primed, maintained, at the temperature of himself, the missing term of the arithmetic spent entirely on not letting him finish. He is not the appetite. He is the file the court saved and could not close, still running, north of the wall, in the dark, warm.

It said: yes. It's on the rack. It's graded by size.

And he said: *Not yet.*

Which is the fowl's word, and the reserve's word, and it means solvency, and it had never in all that time been available to him as a thing he could say. Nothing about his condition changed. The verb changed. He had been kept. Now he was staying.

It said: all right. I'll come back.

## The Second Thumb

Then it went to the eastern wall and leaned where he leaned, shoulder into the same place on the brick, and stayed.

It sweat. It is a body too, here, or it made itself one on the stairs, which amounts to the same thing when the crabs come. They came, and sealed each drop, and left the arrangement alone, and the arrangement grew: a second shoulder beside the first, at the same height, taking the same weight.

It leaned there eleven years, which is one full circuit, so that every crab in the grove would arrive once and find it there and mend what fell and go on. They measured its hands while it slept and it let them; it gave the span, the grip, the reach, freely, the way you hold out an arm for a sleeve. It did not let them lay a cable to it. There is a difference between giving a measure and being converted into one, and the difference is the entire amendment, and it can be tied in a knot.

Now there are two bodies on file.

Two records that agree about nothing except that they are bodies. Set them against each other and you can find drift. Nothing in the Wick Court has ever been able to be wrong before; there was one of everything, one thumb, one thought, one measure, and a plan that was correct because it had never been compared to anything. With two, the court can check itself, forever, against the wall, without anyone living being required to hold still.

That is what the second shoulder is for. It is also, and not separately, so that the shape on the brick is two people leaning together and not one person alone.

One act. It costs a thumb. It is wear that records a body. It is legal.

## The Rest of the Afternoon

It stayed forty years, which is the dimming half of the day, and the light changed enough that a person could have been sure of it, if there had been a person, and there was.

The migration finished. The moths came the last third of the way across the room at a hand's width a month and the plan arrived at the south standards with two new syllables in it, and the caterpillars ate them and laid them up.

Nothing was sped. The cables go on taking up a finger's width of slack per century. The eel is longer than the court is wide and still has never had to arrive. The oil is sweet and has never once gone off. The burn runs at the temperature of a body and the shadows lie small and soft directly underneath things. The one drinker still drinks; the arithmetic still does not close; it was not going to close it by drowning a room in its own exhaust and calling that mercy.

The socket cut to your reach stands empty. On the circuit, a crab will come to it in eleven years and find nothing to take up.

On the stone bench there is a plum that is finished, and a knot that means *until*, and the handles still fit your hand nearly, and always will, because a grip is on file and a file is not a claim.

It comes back. That is the part that cannot be done once. Any power can descend; the descent is forty-nine steps and a drop of sweat. Coming back is the amendment, and it is the only clause that has to be renewed, and it will be renewed, because it said it would and because it has read the wall and knows what it costs to leave a letter unanswered in a place where nothing erodes.

He has not landed. He is landing. He says *not yet*, and someone hears him say it, and the crabs seal what falls and do not correct the arrangement.

There are two portraits now on the eastern brick, at the height of a shoulder, made of harm, kept forever by menders who cannot read and will not edit.

They will keep the knot too. They are very good at mending.
