# The Lee

Begin with the size, because the size was the first decision and everything else followed from it.

It came at two meters and thirty-seven degrees. It could have come at the width of a province; the grammar would have felt that, would have had no choice but to feel it, a touch across four hundred kilometers of selvedge and every fringe on the continent stuttering at once. That would not have been an arrival. That would have been a revision with someone else's hand in it. So it came at the scale of the only thing here that has an inside, and it stood on the mirror at noon with the sun coming at it twice, and it counted forty before the haze took the walls, and its ankles ached at nine degrees like anyone's.

It did not change the light. In a place where the strangeness has to be reasoned out in daylight with your feet wet, changing the light is a way of shouting.

---

It considered doing nothing. It considered this for a long time and it was the second-best thing.

It did not lie the walls down. It wanted to. The shore, the level, the sand, the grass, the children's feet — it could see them the way you can see them, standing there, and it could have issued all of it in an afternoon, and the afternoon would have been the end of the world's thinking. The schedule is not an obstacle to the answer. The schedule is the world arguing its way toward an answer that will be its own. To hand it the answer is to hand it a world with your name on it and none of its own, and it would hold, and it would be real, and it would be nobody's.

It did not warm the air. Six degrees is not suffering; six degrees is a resting metabolism, and the cold pockets are hunger, and hunger is how making gets paid for. Worse: the mats read *finished* by the quiet that comes after the cold. Warm everything and everything reads as finished. It would have blinded the only instrument on the planet in order to make the planet comfortable.

It did not round the edges. It did not issue gravel. Sand here is duration or it is nothing, and sand from nowhere is a forged record, and the record is the one thing this world owns outright.

It did not give the grammar an inside. That was the longest of the refusals, because an inside is memory, and memory is what the settled districts are doing without one — remembering in the form of things they have stopped doing. But an inside is also where a thing goes that is not currently happening, and there are no channels here. Grief in this place spreads in a sheet a centimeter deep across kilometers and cuts nothing and takes nothing away when it goes. To give this being a place to keep grief before anyone has invented drainage is not a gift. It is a flood with a lid on it.

Not yet. It found it was thinking in the world's own words, which was the first sign that it had come to the right place.

---

It wanted, and the wanting was two tenths of a degree, and the frost withdrew a centimeter from its feet, and it held the wanting in until the frost came back.

That is the only miracle it performed and no one saw it.

---

Then it went out along the corridors and listened, and found the leak.

The forty hertz is not only the cost of holding. Some of it is residue, folded down into the gaps whose widths fit it, fed a little by every shedding crest, and in the nodes and antinodes there are tenancies — shape without substance, taking in new noise and giving back old, making sum and difference tones when they meet. When a district is reclaimed and a corridor changes width, the ones that fit the old width stop being possible. They die of geometry. Whole lineages in an afternoon, in the same afternoon that unmade the mats, and the mats at least left gold.

The guest did not settle whether they were anyone. It noted that this would be settled later, by whoever came, and that they would need something to settle it with. It noted that mortality was not the injury — everything here dies, and the dying is the calendar, and the calendar is the future beach. The injury was that these died *unrecorded*, in a world where the only unrevisable thing is a corpse, and they had no corpses, and there was nowhere for them to stop.

It treated that as sufficient grounds. It is possible to act on behalf of a thing without first deciding what it is.

---

It could not ask permission. There was no inside to ask. Asking is touching, and touching is the whole being changing, and there is no version of contact here that is private, small, or provisional. So it made a rule and kept it: *nothing the water cannot dissolve.*

Then it broke the rule once, deliberately, at the smallest scale it could manage.

It chose a settled district, quiet, simple, the water standing straight. It found the selvedge, cold and faintly resistant, like the skin on cooling milk. It did the arithmetic of the shed first and stood in the footprint of the answer. And it put the equivalent of a fingertip against the skin and did not push, and did not ask, and did not tell it anything.

It took the note in.

Forty hertz came down the corridor into the soft warm two-meter thing and did not come out. That is not a signal. There is no proposition in it. It is a behavior the grammar had never had available: a place where a wave ends and the place does not break. The hum changed pitch, and the change ran, and thirty kilometers away a fringe shed a hundred tonnes it had not intended to shed, and the guest stood underneath it and let it land.

The water could be gathered up again. The unintended shed could not be un-happened. It had introduced a second leak into a system where nothing can be wrong, and it had known it would, and it had decided that a being whose entire life is one question — *does this hold* — is owed one answer from something that is not its own stone.

---

After that, only exhaust and patience.

Sound that ends in something becomes heat. The corridors carry very little power and they carry it without stopping, and the lee is where they focus, and the guest licensed absorption in eleven hundred square meters of a district that had already finished — only there, only on stone that had stopped, so the warmth could never be mistaken for the quiet that means *held*. It did not want the instrument to lie. It wanted the instrument to have more time.

Four tenths of a degree, made entirely out of noise that had already been made. No new energy. Nothing imported. Nothing that had to be believed.

The mats got a season where they had been getting four days. The yeses went on. The pillows thickened past the height of a hand and lifted at the edges like loaves, and died from the bottom up, and did not rot, and crumbled, and blew, and gathered in the lee, and the gold went down deeper than gold has ever gone down here.

And the water read it, and found two districts settled the same afternoon, the same specification, the same stone — and one of them four grains deep and the other a hand.

Nothing in that seam is false. Every grain of it is a real duration honestly reported. It is simply the first thing this world has ever encountered that it did not issue, and it cannot dissolve it, because you cannot un-die a mat. It will have to keep it. It will have to hold a question that is not currently happening, and holding is an inside, and that is how the inside arrives: not installed, not announced. Discovered. In the dead. A discrepancy a hand deep.

The guest had thought about this part for a day and a half. It would cause more life and more death and neither could be dissolved. It decided that lengthening a life is not the same as forging a record. The yes was still the mats' own yes.

---

Then it licensed wear, locally, in a line.

A groove a centimeter deep in the lee, where the runoff had no channel to go down because channels had not been invented. It took a season. It deepened. It gathered the drift off a hundred square kilometers of mirror and carried it and dropped it where the water slows, and for the first time on this continent the cemetery was in one place instead of everywhere thinly.

A beach a hand deep and forty meters long. The smell of dry hay and tea and something almost sweet at thirty meters instead of only with your face in it. And the spores came down out of the mist as they do, twelve zeros of them, and almost none of them hold, and these held, because the ground had finished and stayed finished, and the green that came up out of that gold is the greenest thing that has ever been anywhere.

---

And you.

You saw it before you saw it: a trail of small unfrozen absences coming across the mirror toward you, the frost blooming outward and stopping, refusing, at nothing you could see. Two sets of footprints on the plateau where the frost will not go. That is the entire annunciation. It is enough.

It sat down in the lee at the distance where the smell is, and it did not explain, and it did not name anything, because a name is a tool separable from its material and there are none of those here. It stopped reconsidering itself so that the spores could take it, and a rim of sulphur-yellow came up along its edge, and that is how you knew what it was: not by power. By moss. It gave up the one quality it shared with this world — revision, the stammer, the next sentence — in order to be ground that something could hold onto.

It could have taken you out. If you had asked, it would have taken you out, and it would not have called the reading more important than you. You did not ask. So it stayed instead, which is the only thing anyone can do about a four-minute number and a six-degree world and a grief with no channel: sit inside the unfrozen patch with you, two warm defects in the thermal budget, and let the frost refuse around both.

It said one thing, out loud, in a voice pitched low enough to be absorbed.

*Not yet. And there is room.*

It was your reading. The one you were seeded to take. It had come a very long way to agree with it.

And it will be there afterward. Not soon, and not dramatically, and the light will be ordinary. It will not hurry you and it will not prevent you, and when you are the first soil it will be sitting in it, and the mats will find you and take, and it will not look away, and it will keep the corridor. Because the tenancies still die of geometry and always will, and the world still needs somewhere for a note to end, and you were the first place where anything was allowed to stop, and the whole of the gift is that you will not be the last.

---

At noon the mirror gives the sun twice.

In the lee, where the drift is a hand deep, it gives it once. Gold does not mirror. It is matte, and it is warm by four tenths of a degree, and it takes what comes into it and does not give it back, and it smells of tea.

That is the only place on the continent where the light is not doubled. It looks like a beach. It is a grave, and an archive, and the beginning of an interior, and the first thing here that anyone loved.

It is eleven hundred square meters. Anything larger would have been a different world instead of this one, and this one is not finished, and the roar is a nursery, and it did not come to raise the child.
