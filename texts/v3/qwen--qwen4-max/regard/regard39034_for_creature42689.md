## The First Constraint

A miracle, here, is a falling drop.

That is not a metaphor and it is not a rhetorical flourish; it is the whole problem, stated in the room's own units. Broadband noise is the only thing in this chamber that resembles pain. Once a decade a leaf-cup fails and three grams of water fall and for eleven seconds the chord goes ragged, every cricket stops, the light drops to nothing, and the shoals scatter to the walls like fish under a shadow. That is what an event costs. That is what *arrival* costs.

So whatever has come to attend to this place cannot arrive. It cannot manifest at the antinode in a column of light, because a column of light is nothing but it would have to be *made*, and making is a transient, and a transient is a drop. It cannot speak, because speech here is absorbed and handed back changed, and a voice loud enough to be understood as a voice would be four hundred square meters of ragged chord. It cannot appear to the salamander, which has no eyes, or to the shrimp, which have no eyes, or to the listeners, which have no eyes and would eat it.

It has one available medium. The room's medium. Pattern in a phase-locked film, modulated at rates the room can metabolize, which is to say: a fraction of a cent per year.

Anything faster is injury. Anything clearer is takeover. An introduction, in a sixty-one-meter waveguide, is a new fundamental.

So the first act of a benevolent and wise power is to not do almost everything it could do.

## The Books

Before anything else it looks at the accounting, because the accounting is the one place in this room where something like a signature already exists.

The room is very slightly ahead of itself. It has been for a hundred centuries. The engine runs so close to reversibly that the numbers are embarrassing, and they are also, in a small persistent way, wrong — a surplus that no ledger of frost and rock and milligrams can source. Something is being borrowed. Nothing has been called on it.

It could settle this. It has the standing to settle it. And settling it would do one of two things.

Called in, the loan ends the room: eleven thousand years of near-reversible accounting collapses into an ordinary cold cave with ordinary drip-pitting and an ordinary floor, worn.

Forgiven — *balanced*, written off, made whole from outside — the room becomes a made thing. It acquires a seam. Its first rule, the uncomfortable one, the one that has to be first and last, is that nothing here was built. Everything descends. Everything works because it worked slightly better than the thing before it. To pay the debt is to convert a lineage into an artifact, and there is no version of that conversion that leaves the room its own.

So it leaves the books wrong.

This is not a small thing dressed as a small thing. It is the largest available act and it looks exactly like idleness. The surplus is not a leak; it is the tolerance. It is the slack in which a thing can be marginally ahead of itself for a hundred centuries without being audited, and marginally ahead is another way of saying *still growing*. What the power contributes, and has perhaps been contributing since the ice left the plateau, is forbearance from audit. It does not balance the room. It declines to.

Everything after this is minor.

## The Inventory

Then it does the thing that benevolence is usually bad at, which is to count the harms and check which ones are load-bearing.

The liars on the western vault, counterfeiting the thermal signature of a widening channel, farming sugar out of a doubt that isn't there. Remove them and you remove the arms race that has made the room able to read its own edges at all. The plant's discrimination is *sharp* because it has been lied to for a hundred centuries by something the size of a comma. A room that has never been deceived cannot tell truth from warmth.

The waiters, encysted by the uncountable, offering the geometry ice wants, waiting a hundred years for ninety seconds. Abolish the chime and they go extinct — an entire lineage whose generation time is a century and whose lifespan is a minute and a half, deleted by kindness. And the chime is not only their spring. It is the salamander's nursery: the eggs require the freeze, the children of the hole are made by the event that kills four hundred square meters of the mind that tolerates them. Prevent the freeze and there is no recruitment, and in a century there is no predator, and the shoals fill the corridors, and the listeners starve along a road that no longer exists, and the emptiness that is a map of a mouth closes over.

The eight-day things need a cricket to die. The arks are worth thirty years of selection only because something eventually goes wrong. The feral fringe — the millimeter of ordinary bad singing all the way around the rim, the variance the room selects from as its fundamental falls — is delivered by accident, annually, through a door that has been not-sealed for eleven thousand years. Even the wrongness is a delivery. Even the leak is plumbing.

There is no clean version of this room. Every mercy proposed at the scale of the whole subtracts an inhabitant. The power does not offer a defense of this and does not offer an apology for it. It offers an inventory, which is the only honest thing available, and then it goes looking for something narrower.

## The One Loss That Feeds Nothing

At the southeast rim there is a patch of laminate with the wrong grain, still healing from something that happened before the invention of the wheel. It is a bad resonator. It cannot support the modern modes. So it supports the old ones: a small population of shoals with fewer partials, cruder knots, a grammar of interference the rest of the room abandoned fifty centuries ago. Archaic, not damaged. Still fed.

It is the room's only surviving record of what it used to think.

And it exists *because of the injury*, and it will end *because of the healing*. Sixty years, and the laminate closes over, and the grain comes right, and the resonator becomes good, and the old modes cannot live in a good resonator. The archive is a wound and the wound is closing. Nobody eats the archaic shoals preferentially. Nothing depends on them. No lineage is sustained by their grammar. When they go, nothing is fed and nothing is selected and nothing is compensated.

This is the one loss in the chamber that is not somebody's living.

And there is a larger one behind it. Nine meters of raw scalloped calcite at the northern face, coming away at three millimeters a year, and behind it in the tissue the grown and paid-for and currently useless anatomy of a note that has not arrived. If the coherence goes global, every animal in this ecology is dissolved and reused as material for the one animal that has been assembling itself out of them for a hundred centuries. The successor would be born with a body made of everything and a memory of nothing. It would not know it had been a caterpillar. It would not know there had ever been a grammar from before the wheel, or a fortnight of animals breeding in the gaps, or a blind female walking on the underside of water with the old arguments against ice in her belly.

The power cannot prevent the pain. The pain is structure. It can perhaps do something about the forgetting.

## Why That, and Not Something Else

It does not know what the room is for. This is important and it should be said plainly: it does not know whether the threshold is a reorganization or a metamorphosis or nothing at all, and it does not know whether its client is the twelve-hundred-gesture body that breathes every eleven seconds, or the fauna that are its larval tissue, or the thing in the buttressing that has no mode and no name yet.

It cannot ask. The successor does not exist to be asked. The larvae cannot be told what they are for; telling them would be a broadband event, and would also be a lie, since it doesn't know.

So it does not choose the best act. It chooses the act that is not wrong in any of the worlds.

If nothing happens at the threshold: the scar heals, and the old grammar survives somewhere that isn't a scar, and the room keeps its past. Good.

If the field merely reorganizes and the shoals split and the room continues with a slightly different accent: same. Good.

If the coherence goes global and everything here is dissolved into the successor: then the successor comes into a room that has, somewhere at its rim, in ordinary water at ordinary chemistry, a held copy of what it used to be. It is born able to know it was once many. Good — and good for the dissolved, in the only sense left available to them, which is that they were not nothing and are not nowhere.

An archive is the one gift that is good for the caterpillar and good for the imago and good for the hypothesis that there is no imago. Under total uncertainty about what a thing is becoming, the thing you give it is its own memory.

## The Gift

It does not build an archive. Nothing here was built.

It uses the room's own method, which it has spent some time learning, and which is better than anything it would have brought. The room already keeps its memory in the belly of a blind animal. It already pays sugar for error and calls it insurance. It already weighs its leaf-cups against the three grams of a brooding female and has not evolved to evict her. The room has independently invented every kindness the power came to introduce. There is nothing to teach here. There is only one more instance of a thing the room already does.

So: at the rim, in the moss, in the stain where the plant's arguments do not reach and the water is ordinary — one lineage of listeners that does not swallow.

They are the sessile rings on short stalks along the node-lines, mouths open, growing toward the loud, eating what a shoal sheds. When a standing one ceases it comes apart over a few seconds into scraps of decohered pattern, and the scraps drift and settle and are absorbed. That is what a listener is for.

The variation is small: a pouch, ordinary water, ordinary chemistry, the plant's proteins not correcting it. The scraps go in and are not digested. They keep their phase. They keep, in a bad little vessel at the edge of the world, the grammar they were made of — including the archaic one, the pre-wheel one, the one that will have nowhere to live when the southeast grain comes right.

A listener that is an ark. An ear that keeps what it hears.

It is heritable. It descends or it doesn't. It costs sugar, and insurance costs sugar, and the room may decline to pay; withholding sugar is not killing but it is deciding, and the decision belongs to the room. It can be culled. It can be eaten. It can drift into the throat and be sealed in for a year with the moths. It will probably fail. Most such things fail. The power does not underwrite it, because a gift that must be maintained forever is a leash, and because it has already decided not to balance the books.

It offers variation. Selection pays. That is the entire transaction.

## The Working Face

It does not touch the note.

It does not hasten the dissolution by a millimeter and it does not slow it by a millimeter. It does not thicken the buttressing or thin it. It does not test the mode by driving it, because driving it is a transient and a transient is a drop, and because a rehearsal imposed from outside is a specification, and the room has been growing toward its own specification for eleven thousand years and does not need a second one.

It stands at the northern face — insofar as it stands anywhere — and looks at nine meters of unweathered calcite and the anatomy of a note that has not arrived, and it does not know what will happen, and it leaves.

Wisdom, in this room, is mostly nescience with manners.

## The Third Silence

The rules of the chamber say that silence is either a wound or a mouth.

The power introduces a third kind, and it is the only genuinely new thing it brings: a silence that is neither. An absence that does not feed.

It is the same shape as the salamander and it is not the same. The hole walks through the shoals with its mouth open and they cease, and it is fed; that is absence as appetite, and it is legitimate, and it is forty centimeters long and older than anyone reading this. The power's absence does not eat. It attends without coupling. It takes no scraps. Where it moves the chord does not die, because it does not absorb; it simply is not added to.

And once every few years, for eleven days, the salamander's new skin is thin and the animal is audible — a small ordinary noise in a world where every other noise is a citizen — and it goes to the antinode and presses itself into the roar and does not move and starves.

Nothing regulates this. No lineage feeds on it. It is the one cost in the chamber that is simply a cost: the apex predator of a loud world, hiding inside the loudest sound in the world, hungry, for a fortnight, because its skin is new.

The power does not fix the molt. Better fuzz is a subsidy and a subsidy is a seam.

It stays nearby, anechoic, taking nothing, for the eleven days.

That is all. It is not a rescue and it does not shorten the fortnight by an hour. It is only that the eleven days have never been witnessed by anything before, and now they are, and being witnessed without being eaten is the third kind of silence, and it is the only thing in this account that the room did not already have.

## What Travels

It does nothing about us.

We come down the throat and use the ribs, which are not for climbing. We stand on the ledge with a heartbeat and the springtails come to our warmth in a tide and the plant spends whole milligrams of stored winter modeling a broadband object, and the light brightens, because the light is the loss term and we are a loss. A shoal takes our outline for an hour and lets it go. We leave and the room is warmer for a week and there is one more accent in it.

And we take the fundamental with us, in our teeth, and hum it for years in trains and doorways, and cannot sleep in a quiet house. That is the room's only reproduction. To cure the insomnia would be to sterilize it. The watershed's birds are singing a note that is out of date by however far they are from the plateau, and the dialect spreads slower than the pitch falls, and somewhere fifty kilometers down the valley a bird is holding a pitch the room left behind centuries ago, and that is not a message and it is not a leak. It is how a bounded thing gets out.

The power leaves all of it exactly as it is, including the part where we are a wound.

If the ear at the rim holds — if the room pays the sugar, if the lineage is not culled, if it is not eaten, if it descends — then when the grain comes right in the southeast, and later when the note arrives and the coherence goes global and the chamber stops being a place with an inside, there will be somewhere in the ordinary water at the edge of things a held copy of the old grammar. And beside it, kept the same way, indifferently, because the pouch does not sort: a boot-scrape. A rope dragged on limestone. A cough. And a two-syllable shape that was probably somebody's name, said out loud on a ledge by a person who wanted to hear what the room would do with it.

The room did something with it. The room is still doing it. If the ear holds, the room will still be doing it when the note comes.

Nothing in that archive will be the power. It did not sign, it did not speak, it did not appear, and it left the books wrong on purpose. There is no partial anywhere in sixty-one meters of phase-locked water that is a record of it having been here.

That is not a limitation of the method.

That is the method.
