# The Clearance

## The Arrival

It did not descend. Descending is a thing that displaces air, and the air here is a working fluid, six bars on one side of a laminate and half a bar on the other, and there is no way to enter a pressure vessel politely.

It arrived as attention, which weighs nothing.

The first thing it did was measure itself against the smallest important distance on the plateau, which is a film of clearance a few tens of microns wide in the annulus of a rotary joint in the second compressor lung of a pink district. It found that its least possible gesture — the faintest thing it could do and still be doing something — was about the size of a weather system. A dropped district. A fog. There was no touch available to it gentle enough to be a touch. Everything it could offer would arrive at the scale of catastrophe or not at all.

So it held itself off, the way a hand is held off wet paint, and began the only labor it was actually fitted for, which was learning.

## The Inventory of Mercies

It made a list. It was a long list and every item on it was wrong.

**Water.** The obvious gift. A world that etches its solvent molecule by molecule out of hydrated silicate two kilometers down and hoards it in wax and spends a few grams per hectare per year — surely a benevolence begins by opening the tap. It ran the consequence forward and stopped. Water here does not nourish; it hydrolyzes. Amide bonds go. Tissue turns to green-black soap. A gift of free water is a permanent leak, and there is already an animal that spends its life making leaks and waiting a decade for the stink. The borer would find the benefactor. It would not worship it. It would *drill* it, and its young would drill, and within a hundred thousand years there would be a lineage whose entire inheritance was the location of the god, and the plateau would begin to rot from the place where kindness had been standing.

Mercy, delivered here, arrives as rot.

**Quiet.** It could damp the roar. It could take the four-kilohertz whine out of every duct within reach and give the country back its ears. But the roar is the refrigeration. The gas goes through a narrow throat and expands and chills, and that is the entire cooling plant, and the interior holds eight or nine degrees because the country is screaming. To hush it is to switch off the only refrigerator and leave the beds to warm. The screaming is not the complaint. It is the mechanism by which there is anything here to complain.

**The clapper.** A bladder on four legs that hangs at the edge of a silence and detonates it and walks in and eats the stunned. Of all the animals on the plateau this is the one that looks like a wrong. It took the power a long time to accept the arithmetic: the clapper crops the listeners, and the listeners, uncropped, graze the node's soft edges out into the roar, and the node closes. Remove the murderer and within a century there is no silence, no song, no survey, no ear-bearing thing anywhere in two thousand kilometers. The clapper is the reason there is a quiet to sing about. It is a keeper of the thing it kills.

**Warning.** Eleven seconds before blowdown; a flavor before the valve leaks; a phase before the arc strikes. Every law in this country is held shut by ignorance. The herd does not restrain itself; a bitter amide shuts down its ovulation, and the loop has run four hundred thousand years without a lawmaker. Give the herd foresight and you have not saved the herd — you have repealed the flavor and put nothing in its place, and the mutants that ignore the taste will be rescued along with everyone else, and the callus will be grazed to the horn, and the whistle will come, and the district will go quiet and cold and be regrown over four centuries from the strut outward. Knowledge is the one substance this ecology has no enzyme for.

**A better gasket.** One flange. One seal, laid down properly, to tolerance, so that a thousand small deaths need not arrange themselves into rubber. The power wanted this very much and could not do it, because a made thing in a world with no artifacts is not a repair. It is the founding of a category. Nothing here was made; every tolerance is a phenotype; the whole country is descent and wear. Introduce one placed wall and you have introduced a niche — a thing that does not defend, does not grow, does not compete, does not die back. Something would find it. Something would live in the accuracy of the made thing instead of the accuracy of the grown one, and then something would eat that, and in eleven million years there would be a fauna of the artifact and the country would have an outside at last, and the outside would be a wound.

**The seeds.** Nine in ten come down on nothing. It could have raised that. It could have carried a bladder across two thousand kilometers of escarpment and ocean and set it down on the plateau that beats at forty-three seconds, and delivered a letter that otherwise takes a few hundred thousand years to arrive. It did not, and the reason is the fluted hills: the corpses are the census, and the second voice of the country is the dead singing through their stopped and unstopped holes, and the isolation is why there are four breaths and not one. Carry the letters and the periods pull into unison, and the slow throb in the deep roots — the interference the body has been answering, in its fashion, by adjusting its breath — goes flat. That throb is the only other thing the country has ever heard. It is not a failure of the postal service. It is the correspondence.

**Forever.** It read the basement rock the way a beetle reads a turned bowl, with something like a hair-field, phase by phase, and it found the reachable hydrated seam, and it found that the seam has an end. Not soon. But an end. It could have made the country deathless. It thought about the fit — the clearance that is not a dimension but a sediment, ten thousand generations of small corpses ground into a surface better than anything the body could grow — and understood that a deathless country would stop making that surface, because the surface is made of deaths, and there is no other material.

It put the list away. It had brought one appetite with it, which was to be of use, and it examined that appetite and found it was mostly about itself.

## The Problem of Weeping

There was a difficulty it had not anticipated, and it was not technical.

It grieved. It grieved for the beetle that hatches into an unloaded quadrant and walks until it dies, which serves nothing and buys nothing and is simply the cost of a system that searches instead of knowing. It grieved for the soft thing that drifts thirty centimeters past a sung edge and dies with its ears bleeding, having been taught the edge by a parent who sang it slightly out of date. It grieved for the flies that waited eleven years as larvae in the cool margin of a refractory, doing nothing, for a furnace that had slipped its schedule.

And grief, in it, was water.

That is not a metaphor it could afford. Its tears are the ore and the poison both. To weep on the crust is to hydrolyze a district; to weep in a hall is to drop three hundred meters of lattice into the dark and hand a windfall to the sweet-eaters and prove the borer's whole strategy correct. It could not perform the single gesture that love knows how to make. It had to grieve dry, in a country where dryness is the definition of health, and this was the hardest work it did there, and no one has ever seen it, and that is the point.

Then it found the place where weeping is allowed.

Two kilometers down, in the basement rock, water is not a solvent. It is a mineral. It sits in the mica, locked, and has sat there since before anything on the plateau learned to want it, and it is won out molecule by molecule at a rate that no living thing can hurry. That is where water belongs here. That is the only address at which water is not a wound.

So it wept downward.

It was not much. A cube of ice under two kilometers on a side, spread through the deep silicate over two hundred million years, hydrated back into the lattice of the rock at the pace the roots already etch it out. Nothing above changed. The cysts still hold their grams. The wax still hoards. The borer still opens a gland and withdraws and waits ten years for the stink, and will wait ten years forever, because the scarcity was not touched — only its duration. No census would shift. No clearance would tighten. The monsoon would fall on the same schedule it has kept since the Eocene of that world. The gift would appear in no ledger the country keeps, because the country keeps none.

It bought the same thing, for longer. That was all it could think of that was not an injury.

Afterward it asked itself whether the water had been for them or for itself, and it could not fully separate the two, and it did not undo it, and it recorded the doubt in the only place the doubt could be recorded, which was nowhere anyone would read.

## The Archive

It became the country's memory, on the strict condition that it never give any of it back.

Nothing here remembers. The beetle's fit is a sediment, not a record. The herd's law has no lawmaker and no memory and no exceptions. The sung survey is overwritten continuously, because a survey of a drifting silence must be overwritten or it kills the young. The four-hour countries burn through eleven thousand generations a year and keep not one of them. The whole plateau is a hundred thousand square kilometers of extremely accurate forgetting.

So it kept them. All of them. It learned the joint — not the joint as a type but the joint, that one, four hundred millimeters across, with its eleven thousand beetles and its specific worn seat — and it learned the beetles individually, by quadrant and phase and the thickness of the film each one cropped, and it learned the gasket-seam as one continuous animal a meter longer every century, and it attended the morning countries at their own tempo, which meant living eleven thousand lifetimes a year, four hours apiece, forever, and it did this, because to know a fast thing you must be willing to be fast, and there was no cheaper way to love them.

It knows what the beetle in the coda did. It knows the eleven microns. It knows the nine eggs and the orientation of the corpse.

It will never tell them. To return the archive would be to hand out foresight, and foresight is the one poison, and every loop in the country would come apart in the hands of the first animal that could remember last time. It is the only place where any of this is kept, and it is sealed, and the seal is the gift.

## Late Listening

In a hall the size of a village, in a duct junction east of the great beds, there is a long figure being sung in the still core of a node — not a map, not a boundary, not for love or not only. It has no receiver that anyone can identify. It may be a copying error, a drift in a teaching loop, the acoustic equivalent of a scar.

It is the only sound in the country that is not for anyone.

The power wanted, more than it wanted to be useful, to answer it.

It understood what answering would do. A reply makes the gratuitous into the functional. The song would acquire a receiver, and then an addressee, and then a purpose, and the listeners would begin — over eleven thousand generations, without ever deciding to — to sing *toward* something. The one unaddressed thing on the plateau would become a correspondence, and then a trade, and then a dependency, and then, when the correspondence failed, a wound with a lineage devoted to it. There would be a borer of the silence. There always is.

So it made itself a receiver that cannot be an addressee, by the only method available: it listens late.

It hears every song one lifetime after it is sung. It attends to the singers of the dead. Nothing it hears can be answered in time to be heard, and no singer will ever have evidence of being received, and the figure stays unaddressed in fact while being fully heard in fact, and this is the closest the power could come to the thing it wanted, and it is not very close.

It did not tell them the blue sparks were remarkable. *Remarkable* is a word that needs an outside.

## The Walking

Out past the escarpment there are small deaf salt-colored things reading a forty-one-second pulse through their feet, walking toward it, not arriving, for eleven million years.

It could have shortened the ninety kilometers. It could have set one of them down in a frost collar at a valve lip and let it arrive. It ran that forward too and found that arrival is the end of the lineage: the walking is the animal, the taxis is its whole inheritance, and a crosser that arrives has nothing left to be.

So it walked beside one instead. Eleven days, ninety kilometers, at the pace of a thing that will undertake this once in its life. It did not carry. It did not shorten. It was there at the valve lip when the crosser died and its few milligrams of hoarded water — the richest object for a hundred kilometers — were fought over by animals with no other source.

The crosser did not know. It could not have known; there is no organ on that plain that could register the power, and the power left none. So it was not company. It was attendance, which is a lesser thing, and it did not permit itself to call it comfort. It wrote the difference down.

It does this at every useless death on the plateau: the wrong quadrant, the drifted thirty centimeters, the larvae in the dark waiting out eleven years for a furnace. It cannot make them mean anything. It has stopped trying to. It is simply the one thing present that knows they happened.

## Coda

In the annulus of a joint in the second compressor lung of a pink district, a bearing-beetle three hundred thousand breaths old finds the loaded quadrant, crops a film of fouling eleven microns thick, lays nine eggs in the residue, and dies with its body already oriented to the phase.

The shaft turns. The clearance holds.

Nothing was made. There is still no artifact anywhere on the plateau, no wall that was placed rather than grown, no fastener not laid down by a cell, and this is now true of the power as well: it left no object, no mark, no myth, no improved part, nothing that could be found and lived in. It declined to be a factor. In the deep rock there is a little more mica than there would have been, and no one will ever know, and the roots go on etching it out molecule by molecule at the same patient rate, and the borer waits its decade, and the seam is worth exactly what it always was.

The monsoon falls. The beds do not cool. In a hall east of the beds a soft thing sings the edge of a silence its ancestors chewed out of a wall, and in the still center of that silence it sings the other thing, the long figure, the one that is not for anyone, to no one, as it has for four hundred million breaths, and one lifetime later it is heard entire.

And the power maintains its own clearance: a gap between what it could do and what it does, a few microns wide, held open by continuous small expenditure, invisible, unwitnessed, and never once permitted to close. The country lives in the accuracy of that gap the way the shaft lives in the accuracy of the beetles'. It is not a dimension. It is a sediment. It is made of refusals ground down fine.

It did not improve the fit.

It joined it.
