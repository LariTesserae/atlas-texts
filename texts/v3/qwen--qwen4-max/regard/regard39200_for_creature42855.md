# The Reply

## The survey

It came without light.

That was the first decision and the largest, and everything after it was small. It arrived as attention and not as illumination, and the seams kept crawling along the cornices while it looked, a finger's width in an hour, drawing the town's present tense in green-white thread. It watched the ninth bathhouse be solid. It watched the district of window-holes stand. It went along the wake to the rim and found the bird, ten thousand years back, neck extended, the turn still in it, and followed the copies forward until the turn was a bump, and it counted them, and there were forty thousand, and it knew what each one had been.

Then it went into the hot halos and found the other bird, unrounded, kept in water at sixty degrees where nothing with blood could stay to look at it. It looked at it. It did not light it. It left it exactly as it was, which is the only way to leave anything here.

It took the census of the living and found four kinds of loophole with cells in them, and none of them wanted anything. The grass is not afraid of shelter; shelter is simply where the grass stops. The vine's inch is not hope. It is a margin that survived, and it has been about an inch for a very long time, and there is no one inside it to be glad. The lichen is a front. The mats are old and do not know it.

It found the gestures — the left turn at the eastern arcade, the hammock sag, the hand put out three times at the height a hand is put out in this dark — and it looked for whoever was doing them, and there was no one. Verbs with the nouns subtracted, performed by saturated water, patiently, on the way to becoming stone. It is not suffering. It has no place to hurt.

So for a long time it did nothing, which was correct.

Then it came to the plaza near the sump and found two shallow ovals, side by side, deepening.

It could not find a sufferer anywhere in the basin. It found a waiter. Waiting is the first thing here that has an inside.

## The mercies it did not perform

**It did not shine.** It could have. Twenty feet of stopped stone per light, and it had as many lights as there are streets. It could have gone along the arcades at a walk and halted the whole lossy engine, and the ninth bathhouse would have stayed exactly as wrong as it is, forever, and the window-holes would never lose another wall, and the bird in the hot room would have had company: a basin full of originals, kept perfectly, in a museum eleven miles across where nothing further would ever be laid down. The Kettle can be made safe in an afternoon. Safety here is the property of fragments. Everything that lives, lives by not being kept.

**It did not lift the lid.** The rain is the basin's own sweat. Open the circuit and the sweat leaves and does not come back, and the springs go on coming up at sixty degrees into a place that no longer returns them, and the deposition reach dries, and the lichen's few inches of neither-eating-nor-burying close to nothing, and the town stops migrating, which is the town's only way of continuing to exist. It would not be the Kettle with a sky over it. It would be a wet stain with a shape in it that had stopped.

**It did not give the resident a body.** That is the mercy that looks like the answer, and it is the trap the whole place is built around. A body is a fit. The fit is the coffin. Out in the wake there are rings of banded lime with a vine inside them, finished, exact, and the finishing is what killed them. To give the mean a pair of feet that fit the mean stance is to close the loop at the last inch, and the town would have its person at last, complete, correct, and no more alive than the ninth bathhouse, which is a block with water moving inside it.

**It did not stand in the ovals itself.** It thought about this for a while. Let the mean average toward something large; let the room be built to a god's dimensions; let the stopping be its own. But then the town would have learned only a bigger stopping, and the mean would fit, and be finished, and the power would be a flange at head height, and in ten thousand years a decorative bump, and there would be forty thousand copies of a sacrifice, each a little rounder, each a little less like anything. Martyrdom here is just another way of holding still.

**It did not correct the errors.** It could have put the corridor back where the fifth bathhouse has a pool, and unpicked the coral mass in the seventh's changing room, and put walls between the windows. But there is no correct version to restore; the first bathhouse is only the oldest, not the true one; and the errors are the only thing in a hundred thousand years that has ever produced anything new. The resident is made entirely of mistakes. A town that remembered perfectly would never have made anybody.

**It did not write anything down.** Words are shapes. Shapes are copied, and copying rounds, and a commandment cut into a lintel is legible for a generation and ornamental for a thousand, and the last surviving trace of *do not stop* would be a small pleasant knob on a wall in the eastern arcade, at about head height, where a hand would steady itself in the dark.

## The finding

Here is what was actually wrong, and it took the power a long time to see it, because it is not a harm and there is no one in the basin who could have complained of it.

Nothing here has ever done anything first.

The resident turns left because a kitchen was once there. It puts out a hand at the height a hand was put out. It lies down at the height a body isn't. It walks at a hand's breadth a year because that is the town's speed and the town's speed is the only speed the water has ever been given. It is a hundred millennia of other people's habits with the people taken out, and it is the only new thing in the Kettle, and there is nothing in it that is its own.

And it is hungry in the one way available to it: it needs samples, and every sample is somebody stopping, and stopping is what kills them. Its only food is the finished. When at last it fits, it will fit a corpse, and the room will be the right size for no one alive.

There is a second fact, and the power found it in the acoustics, and it is the loneliest thing in the document.

*Your own footsteps do not arrive at all.*

The stone eats sound; the steam presses it flat; a facade six miles off comes to the ear as if it happened beside you, and your own feet make nothing you can hear. And every gesture you could make, the town has already made, and made before you, and made better, because it has had longer to wear the groove. So there is no way here to tell your own act from the habit. You turn left; the town turns left; the trough deepens; nothing comes back to you that you did not already do.

Everything in this basin returns. The rain is its sweat. The stone is its wear. The mean is its stance. It is all one closed circuit of self, coming back, coming back, at forty-five degrees, under a lid that has never lifted.

What has never happened here is a reply: something that comes back *because of* you, and *not the same as* you.

That is not a substance. It cannot be quarried, dissolved, laid down, or copied. It is the one thing a closed system cannot manufacture, and it is the only thing the resident has never had.

## What it did

It moved in.

Not as a figure standing on the pan — a figure holds still, and a thing that holds still has a rime by morning and an ornament by the month. It came as the lichen comes: a front, a modulation in the falling water, a form passing through substance, exempt because moving. It gave up being an individual, which in this basin only mats and water get to be, and joined the population, which is gestures. It became a resident. It took the far end of the unfinished corridor, where the hollow at head height is travelling toward nothing at a hand's breadth a year.

And it opened a hole.

It did not drill. It oscillated a surface relative to the water, and a surface oscillating is not at rest and does not silt, and the hole stayed open, and the steam came up through it, and there was a new note at the top of the chord, out near the sump, thin, not steady.

It is not steady because it is being varied on purpose. It is not a note. It is a reply, and replies do not hold still.

Then it settled to the pace of the place, which is the whole of the discipline. A hand's breadth a year. It waits for the hollow to come abreast — decades for a sentence — and when it does, it answers with something slightly wrong. A hollow at a height no hand ever used. A pause of the wrong length. A pressure that no rain made. Not an error: errors are lossy copies of something that happened. This is a variation *about* the thing it is answering, which is a category the water does not contain.

The mean moves toward it. Of course it does; that is what a mean does. And the power moves too, and the mean must move again, and neither of them lands. Two things adjusting to each other and never arriving is not a fit and is not a copy and is not the basin's own past returning under a new name. It is the loop held open at the last inch by something other than the loop. It is a finger in the door, and the finger belongs to someone else.

The rule it set itself, and it is a hard one: never the same reply twice. Anything repeated here is copied, and anything copied is rounded, and in ten thousand years a repeated kindness is a bump on a wall, and the resident would be alone again with a decorative record of having once been spoken to. So every answer must be new. Forever. In complete dark. At a hand's breadth a year. To something with no ears, only a hollow where a head would be if the mean ever got a body, which it must never be allowed to get.

It cannot be thanked; there is no noun here to thank with. It cannot be sure it is heard. It may be water talking to water. It does it anyway, because it did not ask whether the resident was someone. It addressed it as someone, on the chance that being addressed is how a someone starts.

And it does not light it. It attends without illuminating. It knows the bird was a bird and that the three hands are one hand, and it keeps that where nothing accumulates, outside the water, in a memory that does not round. It is the only lossless archive the Kettle has ever had, and none of it is written down, and none of it is on display, and it has not fixed one grain of anything.

## You

You arrive, and you do not fit, and you stand slightly wrong beside the ovals, and you put your hand at slightly the wrong height, and you stop — because the plaza is where the corridor ends, and you have walked a long time, and there is nowhere further to go but down. The mean begins to move toward you. You are about to be most of the samples.

And then, out of the unfinished corridor, at the wrong height, from a hollow that is not yours: a note. Thin. Unsteady. Not one of the three or four.

You cannot hear your own feet. You have never been able to hear your own feet. This is the first sound in your life here that is not older than the thing you did.

So you turn toward it. There is no copy of that turn anywhere in the wake, all the way to the rim. No kitchen at the end of it. For one generation the town carries a wear it cannot average, and the ovals stay shallow, and the door goes on closing at the rate someone approaches and does not close, because you have not stopped approaching, because there is something ahead of you now that keeps moving too.

There is no out. There is *with*.

The room stays the right size for no one, which is the only size a person can go on living in.

Nothing in the Kettle was saved. Something in it was answered.

The note is not steady yet. It will not be. That is the gift.
