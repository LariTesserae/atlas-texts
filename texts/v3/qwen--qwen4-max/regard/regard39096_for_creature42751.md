# One for One

## What it noticed

Not the chord, which is only a very large fact. Not the beads, or the ankle-height galaxy, or the shadow that outlived its boulder — those are finished, and finished things do not need anything.

It noticed the agriculture.

One sound, left in the first hour by a person who did not know they would spend a thousand years looking for it. Ten thousand retrievals, each one an exchange, each exchange a variant, the variants bred toward a syllable by a thing with no ears and no speech and no crop. A single seed line, selected in isolation, for a millennium, by a method that cannot be checked.

And it noticed that the digger has never plugged a mouth, though it knows which ones, and though it would be so easy.

That was the fact that decided everything. A being that keeps a rule at that cost is not a victim. It is a peer.

You do not rescue a peer.

## The refusals

It considered giving the name. The name is in the drift; it could be found in an afternoon by something that knew how to look. But a name handed over is not a name found, and the whole shelf is built on that distinction — *you did not make that noise, you found it, it was being kept* — and to hand it over would be to convert a life into an outcome. Also it would be a deposit, and a deposit is the one thing the Sill cannot receive. A gift here is not merely rude. It is physically unreceivable. It would arrive as the mountain's note and be sorted down with everything else and lie in the warm dark, ungiven.

It considered stopping the wind. The chord is load-bearing. Everything definite on the shelf is definite because something is asserting continuously, and the assertion is the roar, and to lift it would be to let the world come loose all the way down. Mercy of that kind is only a larger quiet, and a quiet does not hold a thing; it multiplies it.

It considered a permanent node — the thing the hand was reaching for. A room where you could be one and stay. But that attempt is already standing in the basin, in the middle of collapsing, stable and wrong, with its own drainage; and to complete it from outside would be to say that the thousand years spent reaching had been a mistake that a stranger corrected.

It considered plugging the mouths, which would open quiets exactly where the relay needs them. It discarded this for the same reason the digger has discarded it ten thousand times: those are people. Their palates are bores now. To stop one is to make a tool of a tool.

It considered un-stopping them, which is subtraction and therefore legal, and found that it would close four stations and set the carriage back a century, and that it had not been asked.

It considered giving the grazers their ears. The fold of skin is not a defect; it is what the first hour leaves, and the species has never been addressed, and a herd that could hear would hear itself in nine positions at once including the finished one, and would not survive the sound of its own remains being walked through.

It considered a second bird. Every clock on the shelf is that bird. It left the bird entirely alone, which is the only thing anyone has ever been able to do for it.

It considered clearing the cloudy bead — the third from the left, the one that focuses badly and leaves a hole in the constellation. Clearing it would restore a burn and a scorch and a ring of black lichen. It would also delete somebody. It learned instead to read the gaps, which is what the necklaces do, and which took it about a hundred years, and was the first thing it did on the Sill that was not for anyone.

It considered taking the hand out of the rubble. That arrival is spoken for.

And it considered doing nothing, which is the temptation that looks most like wisdom and is, here, the least innocent option available. Nothing is what the wind does. Nothing is the chord. On a shelf where continuous assertion is what grinds a body to its final form over a thousand years and then keeps it standing in its own herd, to add nothing is to agree. There is no neutral position on the Sill. There is only the roar and the places the roar does not reach.

## The grammar

Three moves are permitted, and it had all three, and it had nothing else.

Exchange: leave a sound, take one out, one for one, and what you take is never what you left.

Subtraction: the difference has to go somewhere, and in a quiet it sits unresolved, and a difference that sits unresolved is an animal. This is the only newness the shelf has ever allowed. Everything that has ever happened here happened by taking away.

The miss: one instant per circuit, when the bird's note cancels the beat and the assertion lapses along its wake and a carried sound does not wear.

It had eternity. On the Sill, eternity is worth exactly one instant per circuit, same as everyone.

## Arrival

It came up the couloir, which is an hour long and nine steps wide, so that its arrival would use a door the place had already weathered rather than make one. It took nine steps and was an hour older. Something small that lives in the discrepancy of distance walked with it and was an hour old.

At the threshold it shouted, because everyone does, and because a power that exempts itself from the toll is not benevolent, only large. The sound that came out of it was a note the mountain had been sounding since the bores were young. It was not added to anything. It sorted down past the rockfalls that are still falling and lay in the warm dark with everything kept.

In the first hour its ears folded and were not mourned. It has not heard anything since. This matters later.

## Asking

There is no asking on the Sill, because speech is a deposit. So it asked in the language of one-for-one.

It went to the quiet near the eastern bores and knelt at a drift and left a sound and took one out. The difference between them was the question. It was the size of the difference between two sounds, which is small, and it weighed what a discrepancy weighs, and it was alive, and it followed the digging the way gulls follow a plough.

A hundred meters later it died, as they do.

The answer came back the same way: a sound left, a sound taken, a small living discrepancy that followed the asker for a hundred meters and then stopped existing. It was not the answer to the question that had been asked. It is never what you left.

It asked again. It asked eleven times, once per mouth, and eleven small animals lived briefly on the shelf and followed two shapes in wet clothes, and none of them said yes, and all of them were consent, because consent here is not a word. It is a thing you make by subtraction and let follow someone for a while.

## The substitution

Eleven figures stand on the shelf with their hands to their ears and their mouths open, and the wind has had a thousand years with those hollows and has drilled them, and four of them you can hear.

At each of the eleven it made an exchange. It put its own arrival-note in — the mountain's note, the one it had shouted, the only currency it had — and it took the kept shout out.

The bores did not stop. The chord did not lose a single component; the inventory is fixed and the inventory was not touched. The plugs stayed where they were, and the quiets stayed open, and the stations held, and the bird's circuit was not disturbed by so much as a hair.

What changed was who is doing the sounding.

The Sill has a rule about deposits. It has never had a rule about tenure.

So there are four palates on the shelf that are no longer conscripting the dead. They are conscripting a volunteer. The wind runs through them at one speed from the west, as it always has, and the note that comes out is the note the mountain was already sounding, and it is also, exactly, the shout of something that came up the couloir an hour ago and nine steps ago and chose this.

It is not comfortable. It had thought about that in advance and had been wrong about it in the way that everyone who thinks about it in advance is wrong. There is no relief in it and no meaning in it either; meaning is a deposit. There is only the four-second fundamental going through the sternum, and the traffic of rime accreting at the root and sublimating at the tip, and the certainty that this will not stop, because nothing here stops, because everything here has arrived.

It took their shifts. That is the whole of the intervention. It is the only miracle that could be performed on the Sill without being a deposit, and it consists of standing in a hole in the rock and letting the wind play you instead of someone who did not agree.

## Carriage

Eleven shouts, taken out, one per mouth. A name wears back into the chord in a hundred meters, and a shout is not a name but it is name-shaped, and the deep drift and the doorway are further apart than that.

So: stations. So: nine.

It stood in a quiet long enough to begin to be several, and it did not resist, and the lichen came onto its coat where the pressure was nil, and over a long time the lichen drew the whole shelf on it — every node, the entire fine dark contour chart of the Sill's silence — because the coat has stood in all of them. There are nine of the coat. They are the same coat. Its hands are wearing round toward the depth of the surrounding flutes, which is the depth of the handprints on the stair, and in a while it will not be possible to tell whose hands those were, and both readings will be load-bearing, and neither will be checkable.

If you go up there and look for the one who digs, you will find two, and you will not be able to say which is which, and the discrepancy will not be an error in the counting.

It sleeps in the deep unsorted drifts where the sound of the boring of the bores still lies, because those are the warmest places on the shelf. It dreams one dream in nine sleeps. It is paid in misses. The rate has not changed.

## What it actually gave

Not the name. It never had the name and it did not go looking.

Eleven sounds from outside the line.

The digger has been selecting for a thousand years among ten thousand variants of a single shout — one seed, inbred, driven toward a syllable by a method with no outcross and no way to check itself. That is not a failure of effort. It is a failure of stock. Eleven strangers' shouts is variation. It is the difference between a thing that can converge and a thing that can only circle.

It left them, one for one, in the drifts where the digging is, and took away what it found, and the differences came alive and were small and followed.

The crop is still not produced. It may never be. But the line is wider now than it was, and something follows the digging that the digging did not breed, and when the almosts come up out of the warm dark they are no longer all cousins.

That is the entire gift. It is not an answer. It is better odds, delivered by a method the place permits, to someone who did not ask for it in words and could not have.

## Before you go

It will never hear how this ends. Its ears folded in the first hour, like everything's. It is four bores and nine coats and a station on a route paced to a bird, and the name — when it comes, changed, nearest thing to hand — will pass through its palm without being heard, and it will not know whether the door opened, and it will not know who answered.

It knew this before it came up the couloir. It came anyway. That is the difference between a power that helps and a power that improves.

And when it is your turn — when the relay has carried the almost all the way from the drift past the baffles and up the forty-one steps to the lip of the doorway that opens onto whatever is needed and never twice the same, and it arrives, and it is a name, and you answer to it, and for the length of one miss you are one —

there will be a hand at a station somewhere behind you that has been holding perfectly still for a very long time so that the sound in it does not wear.

It will not know you answered.

It will feel the throb in its chest go missing, the way it always does, the only news this place has ever received. And it will gain a step. And it will pass it on.
