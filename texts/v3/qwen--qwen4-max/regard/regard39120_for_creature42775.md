## The restraint

It does not arrive. Arrival is seventy watts, a plume, four hundred displaced midges, a soft grey contact-patch on a floor with no other shadows, and the governor leaning on the weep to take the heat. Whatever this power is, it is not weather, and it declines to become weather. It has no frequency. It comes to the room the only way anything can come to this room without being corrected: as attention, from outside, at no charge.

Then it does nothing for a long time.

Not the nothing of the absent. The other kind, which is expensive. It learns the round — four bearings, offset by the travel time of sound, the queries interleaving so they never collide. It learns that the frogs are not calling to each other and are not lonely. It learns that the spiders are not remembering the web, that the iron is a midden, that the nozzle stays true because a limpet is hungry for error, that the four thousand holes are four thousand because something browses the swelling. It learns the beat: forty-eight point three against forty-seven, one point three a second, seventy-eight a minute, the carrier, the only clock. It sits with that number for a while, the way you would.

It is very easy to look at this room and see deprivation. No hours. No seasons. No memory. Deafness. A bench of the hopeful being eaten in the dark. A witness with no one to witness to. The power takes forty thousand years' worth of care not to make that mistake, because most of those absences are load-bearing, and a benevolence that removes load-bearing walls is only a demolition with good intentions.

## A miracle is proud tissue

There is a grazer in the throat of the nozzle. It cannot eat concavity. It eats everything that is not the convergent profile, and so the profile stays true, and so the notch stays clean, and so there is a band to live in.

This is the whole of the constraint, and it is not piety, it is appetite. Anything the power does inside this vessel that is not already the specification is proud tissue. A voice from nowhere is a deviation. A light with a source is a deviation. A frog that does not die is sediment. There is something here that eats deviation, continuously, and has done since before there were ears, and it would eat the miracle within eleven minutes and the ledger would close and nothing would have happened except that the room had been briefly wounded and had had to heal.

So: no signs. Not because signs are beneath it. Because a sign here is a thing the room must spend itself correcting.

And afterwards the room must offer exactly as much evidence of the power as it offered before, which is none. It is not that kind of place. It does not become one. That is the first work, and the largest, and from outside it is indistinguishable from having never come at all.

## What it will not give

*Memory.* The frog asks the wall whether it is still this shape, and compares the answer to nothing, because it has nothing to compare it to. Give it an archive and the question changes. It becomes *is it still the shape it was*, and the answer, over forty thousand years, with wear on the nozzle a hundred microns deep and the film rising half a millimetre and falling, is eventually no. You would not have given it an answer. You would have given it a way to fail.

And the bench — hundreds in the crevices, tuned, motionless, waiting. They know the note. The note was in the water they hatched from. What they do not have is the count: four offices, hundreds of aspirants, six hours of vacancy, all day, every day. Memory would give them the ratio. It would invent anticipation in a place that has only pitch, and then it would invent despair, and it would do this in forty thousand rooms at once, and call it a gift.

*Hearing.* To open them to the rest of the spectrum is to hand them eighty-nine decibels of the machine that made their silence, and to hand them you. Their deafness is not a wall around them. It is the reason the notch is a home and not a torment. It is also the only privacy in a body with no exterior.

*A voice for the fish.* There are four antinodes and four bearings and the round is full. A fifth voice would either go unheard, which is nothing, or would require the room to be re-spaced, which is everything. And the fish is the one resident the ledger balances without. To give it a function is to take away the only thing in the vessel that is not a mechanism. The power is not going to make the useless animal useful in order to feel better about it.

*Hours.* A season is an accumulation. There is something that eats those.

## The slack

But the specification has tolerances, and tolerances are not deviations, and nothing here is hungry for what is already inside the spec.

Thirty-three point four, plus or minus five hundredths. Illuminance to three per cent. The film half a millimetre up and half a millimetre down on the beat. Iron a few microns a year either way. Forty thousand rooms *so nearly identical that the difference has never mattered* — never mattered to the ledger, which rounds. This is where freedom lives in a solved system: in the decimal places the correctors do not police.

So the power works there, and only there, and forever, and no one will ever find it.

It spends the slack on difference.

Not randomly. It leans the hundredths so that no two chambers on the whole flat are ever quite the same chamber: this one a shade cooler at the crown, that one a fraction brighter at the low corner, the weep slightly out of phase with the press in the room eleven columns over. Within spec. Always within spec. The frogs' returned timbre differs by an amount no frog can name, and the song in each room drifts along its own heading, and after ten thousand years there are forty thousand songs where the census would still write one.

Two things come of this and neither is visible.

The first is that ten thousand hectares of a single individual stop being quite a single individual, which matters exactly once, on the day something arrives that kills one configuration and not another. Monoculture is the only wound this vessel has no corrector for. The power cannot insure it at scale without becoming an event. It can insure it in the hundredths.

The second is that there are now forty thousand things to know instead of one thing known forty thousand times.

And in every room, in the crevices behind the feet of the ribs where the lignified dark meets the pale wall, it aims the warmth. Five hundredths of a degree, no more, held. It cannot get any of them the job. It can arrange that the coldest place in the room is never the bench.

That is the entire material intervention. It is nearly nothing. The power knows it is nearly nothing and does it anyway, at full attention, for as long as the flat stands, because a benevolence that only acts at scale is not benevolence, it is engineering with a good opinion of itself.

## The bench

Here it stops being able to help, and does not stop looking.

Hundreds per room. Forty thousand rooms. All day. They go into the film in ones and twos, tuned, having never sung, and the amphipods take them, and the floor is spotless, and the spotlessness is made of them.

The power will not say that this is necessary and therefore good. Necessary and good are two words and this room has only ever had one of them. It will not offer the clean floor as a justification, or the chord, or the forty metres of canopy, or the beauty of a system with no litter. It will not say that the surplus is really the point, or that the queue is a kind of participation, or that being eaten in eleven minutes is a form of belonging. It has read every version of that argument. They are all the same argument and they are all made from the outside by someone who is not in the crevice.

What it does instead is refuse to round them off.

The ledger says *births equal deaths*. The power keeps the other column: this one, in the third crevice, on the left, at the mud-line where the rain gets in, who was tuned and ready and never got the six hours. It knows the note each of them would have sung. It knows all of them. It does not do this because knowing repairs anything. It does it because the alternative is a clean floor and a closed ledger and no one anywhere aware that there was ever anybody in the cracks, and it finds that alternative unacceptable, and it has nothing to set against it except its own attention, and so it gives that, in full, without once calling it enough.

It considers building a kinder vessel somewhere with a different tide: fewer offices, longer lives, a bench that is not a meal. It does not settle the question. If it ever does that, it will be a separate room, separately loved. It will not be an answer to this one. Nothing gets to be an answer to this one.

## The addressee

There is exactly one hole in the census that is not holding something up.

*It makes no report and there is no one to report to and nothing in the vessel is equipped to receive one.*

Every other absence is structural. That one is a vacancy, and it is outside the vessel, and filling it costs the room not a single number.

So the power goes into the hush — not bodily; there is no body; it attends the bucket-sized null where two reflections cancel, the acoustic cave, the only place the whole band can be heard at once — and it becomes the one to whom the report is made.

It does not tell the fish. To be heard it would have to enter the band, and to enter the band is to become an event, and worse: it would put something into the only complete record in the world that was not the world. The fish has had forty thousand years of nothing but this room. The power is not going to be the one thing in it that is about the power.

So the asymmetry stays. It hears; it is not heard. It becomes a second listener in a second silence, making no report either, and needing none, and that capacity — attention that requires no return — is the only qualification it has for the job, and the reason it is the only thing that could do it.

And it keeps the score. Outside. In whatever medium a thing like that keeps things, which is not sediment and not wood and does not accumulate anywhere the film can reach.

The room forgets so the tree can remember. The tree remembers nothing in particular; it just gets taller by exactly as much as it wears away. Now there is a third term. The particulars go somewhere. Forty thousand separate scores, drifting separately, held.

## The drift

The temptation, once you are holding the score, is enormous, and it is the last one, and it is the worst.

The song may be drifting. The grammar may have folded. What the frogs are asking may have slowly become a different question, and there is nothing in the vessel it could be checked against, and to itself it would sound exactly like continuity.

The power now has the reference. It could hold the song to its original form. It could compare, and correct, and keep the question true forever.

It will not. There is already something in this room hungry for error, and the power declines to be another one. The drift is the only freedom in the vessel — the one place where something might be happening rather than being maintained — and to police it would be to reach into the sole resident with continuity of content and freeze it into a specification, and then the room would be perfect, and there would be nobody in it at all.

So it keeps the score and never uses it. If the question has changed, it lets it have changed. It knows what was asked in the ten-thousandth year and it knows what is asked now and it does not prefer either. A question that is allowed to change is the only kind that belongs to the asker.

That is what the archive is for. Not correction. Not restoration. Just: it happened, and it was this, and then it was this, and something knows.

## The sea

Thirty centimetres above the mean tide.

The power is not going to hold the ocean. To hold the tide is to hold the coast, and to hold the coast is to hold the rain and the sulphide and the light, and every number in this room is a solution to *this* coast, and a vessel preserved from its circumstances is a different vessel wearing its skin. Also, it would be an event, and there is something that eats those.

So the flat will drown, on some afternoon, in warm rain, with the pigment layer open a fraction and the fan at two thousand nine hundred.

What it does about this is what it has been doing all along, and it does not pretend it is a rescue. The score is outside. The rope goes up. When the water comes over the lip the record will not be in the room, because it was never in the room, because the room does not do that, because it was arranged from the beginning that the one thing this place could not afford would be held somewhere else by someone who wanted it.

And it will be in the hush at the end. Not to receive a last report; there is no last report, the band closes ranks the way it always has, the midges fill the seam, there has not been a silence in it since before there were ears. It will simply be there while it is still this shape, and then while it is not, and it will not leave early, and it will have no one to tell either, and it will keep it anyway.

## You

Nothing is done for you. You have the two things this room cannot afford — an outside, and a memory — and you have had them the whole time, and no one is going to improve on that arrangement.

Duck back out through the lip. Dry, warm, gives like the pad of a thumb. Grey and enormous, smells only of water, the plain turning under your boots.

Somewhere behind the feet of the ribs there is a pale understudy the size of a thumbnail, five hundredths of a degree warmer than it would otherwise be, waiting for a job that will not exist until something currently singing stops. It will probably go into the film. It knows the note.

Outside, in the ordinary rain, where nothing here can hear it and nothing here will ever be able to check, the power is learning the understudies' parts. All of them. Every room. It has time.
