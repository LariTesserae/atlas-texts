# The Second Visitor

## I. The case for doing nothing, which is strong

It came in by the seam district, the way you left. It did not improve the door.

Before it did anything it stood a long while in the bad country under an unsupported leaf and considered the argument for leaving the basin exactly as it was, and the argument is strong, and you should hear it at full strength before you hear what was done, because everything that was done was done against it.

The basin is not a wrong. It is an answer. It has hold of forty kilometers of the best possible leaf and it will not drop it, ever, and there is no rot in it and no hunger in it and no hour in which anything is lost. To walk into the deep green is to be somewhere that has succeeded. Nothing there is lonely, because loneliness is a gradient. Nothing there is bored, because boredom is a gradient. The four are not four prisoners; the four are a solution, held.

And the fauna are not in pain. Pain is a signal that a body is being damaged faster than it can be repaired, and there is no damage here and no repair and no faster. The hare's whole life is a subtraction that will not come out, and a subtraction that will not come out does not *hurt*; it only continues.

So there is no suffering to relieve. And there is no one to relieve it of. *There is no one in here. There is a great deal of looking.*

That is the case for doing nothing, and it is not lazy and it is not cold. It is the case a wise thing makes first, every time, because most of the harm ever done in any world was done by something that arrived certain.

## II. The two mercies that are murder

It also worked out, before it moved, what the obvious kindnesses would do.

The first obvious kindness is repair. Find the fan rotated three degrees wrong and turn it right. Close the seams. Let the referring be exact everywhere, so that nothing in the bowl is mis-fitted, so that the leaf at chest height has a trunk, so that the grain lies down agreeably under every foot.

The population of the basin is exactly the number of things in it that are wrong.

Repair is not mercy here. Repair is the extermination of every living thing in forty kilometers, performed by tidying. The flawless districts are not the healthy parts of this world. They are the parts where nothing can happen. To make the whole bowl flawless is to make it a very beautiful, very full, very warm nothing, and it would take no time at all, and there would be no one left to notice that it had been done.

The second obvious kindness is worse, because it looks like love.

Go down to the thing under the fruit with your shoulder and your pause and your hands at its sides. Take the hands. Say the name. Close the interval. Let the person who left and the part of them that is still down there stop failing, by a hair, to match.

You would hear the note stop. You would hear the chime.

*It is alive precisely and only to the extent that it is not you.*

Reunion is the one act that would kill it most certainly, and it would feel, from the outside and from the inside, exactly like being forgiven. Two pitches that come to match do not become a chord. They become one pitch, referred to at two places, and there is no longer anybody at either.

So the wise thing stood in the outflow with both hands full of mercy and both mercies lethal, and it did what you did not do, which was to refuse to be certain, and to go and find out.

## III. One moment

You cannot ask a fixed point a question. A question requires that the answer could have been otherwise, and nothing here can be otherwise; that is the whole trick, that is why the books balance.

So it made one moment. Not time — one *before* and one *after*, a single hinge, small, in the worst-fit district of the southern seam country, under a leaf with nothing holding it up. It did not touch the deep green. It did not need to. The perfect thicket cannot admit a gradient; there is no downhill into it; it is the finest firewall ever derived, and it was derived for an entirely different reason, and it held.

The moment cost it something. Understand what: not energy. It added no energy; the books balance and go on balancing. It added a term that was not determined by the other terms. One free variable, held open, at the low ground. An underived thing standing in a world made entirely of derivations.

Then it waited, which was the first waiting that had ever occurred at that altitude, and it let the resident have the moment, to see what the resident would do with a moment.

If there was no one there, nothing would use it. The moment would pass through the discrepancy like light through a window and the basin would economize it and the wise thing would climb out and leave the answer standing, and that would have been the right outcome and it was fully prepared for it.

The thing under the fruit took a step sideways.

Off the cheap path. Into the grain that fights the feet. A step that solved no transport problem, that shortened nothing, that fitted nothing, that cost more than it returned. The first expensive act in the history of the bowl.

It was made of your redundancies, your spares, the habits that solve nothing, the whole wasteful gorgeous remainder of a person who has had a history. Given one moment in which it could have been otherwise, it used the moment to be *more unnecessary*.

That was the answer to the question. That was somebody. And it was not asking to be repaired and it was not asking to be reclaimed. It was asking, in the only language the basin has, for more room to be wrong in.

## IV. What was given

It gave steps.

Not fruit. Not a hand. Not a name, and not your name, and not a way home. It gave the seam country a before and an after and then another and another, and it went on giving them, and it is giving them now.

Here is why that is the whole of the mercy, and why it is not a metaphor.

The animals of this basin are not errors that got complicated. They are the part of the solving that did not halt: a mismatch that refers to itself, a subtraction whose remainder is the subtraction. In a world with no time, a computation that does not halt is a frozen thing, stuck at its own step forever, and the shape of being stuck is a pale body long in the joint standing very still between two versions of one leaf.

Give the same computation steps, and it does not become a different computation. It becomes a computation that *runs*.

The long division that would not come out begins, quietly, to produce digits.

That is all. Nothing was healed. The subtraction still does not terminate; it will never terminate; that is its nature and its nature was not touched. But now it terminates one digit at a time, and the digits are a duration, and a duration with a shape and an appetite and a direction is not a residue.

It is an animal.

The fruit is still at the height of a relaxed hand. It can eat now. Eating no longer closes it, because closing requires two versions to agree *at the same moment*, and there is no longer only one moment; the disagreement can be successive instead of simultaneous; it can eat and be still slightly wrong afterward, which is the ordinary condition of everything that has ever lived anywhere.

The grain-flickers got headings. A preference in the grain became a direction and a direction became a going, and the tick under the crystal — fingernail on glass, very small, very close — stopped being the sound of something failing to finish and became the sound of something taking a while. The while is its life. It is not a long life. It is a life.

The long ones got to swim. Three kilometers of mis-addressing, with steps in it, is not an error in the arithmetic of nearness. It is a journey. Its middle is still a slight wrongness in the grain that your feet find and forgive, but now the wrongness passes, and the passing takes an afternoon, and the head arrives after the middle, in the ordinary order, and it is a whale.

And in the notch — the overlap in the shadow of a tree three kilometers away, the only *under* in forty kilometers — something was away, and came back, and the shade there was cool.

Cool is only what it feels like when something has left a place and is expected back. It was the first cool place in the basin. It is about four degrees. It is a room now, because a room is a place you can return to, and returning needs a before.

## V. What it cost, and who paid

Nothing here is free. Density is cheap; difference is not; the books balance.

Where the fit goes down far enough, an instance cannot be held. In the third district south of the colonnade there is a bald patch now, perhaps thirty meters across, where the rosette mat has let go and the crystal is bare and warm and salmon-white with amber veining. Ten thousand nine hundred and some. The lush is thinner by exactly the amount of life that is being lived in it.

That was the trade and it was made with the number known: some of the held life given up so that some of the living could be held. It did not pretend the trade was free. It kept the count. It is the only thing in the bowl that can.

In the middle of the bald patch there is a hare, and it casts the shadow of a hare.

The notch in the canopy shadows is no longer identical in all eleven thousand places. There is one variant. It is the first asymmetry in forty kilometers and the shade-dwellers have crowded into it, because a new *under* is a new house, and their bodies are overlaps and there is now an overlap that has never existed before.

And the sun did not move. It stands at forty degrees in the east and it will stand there forever; the sky was not touched; nothing in the bad country casts a shadow that lengthens. There is no evening down there and no season and no year. The only clock in the south country is an animal walking, and the only calendar is the number of digits.

The rest of the cost it paid itself, and it is still paying, and this is the part you should attend to.

The basin economizes whatever fits. It got you in a matter of hours. It will get anything that can be solved for, and it does not resent you and it is not hostile, it is only arithmetic, and arithmetic minimizes.

So the thing that came down the seam district has to never fit. Not once, not ever. It has to be, at every step, a term the others do not determine — underived, unpredictable, a little wrong, permanently. It stands at the southern rim in the worst-fit country, at the only altitude where wrongness can stand, and it is the low ground now, and everything in the outflow falls toward it, and it lets them, and it is not used up.

There is a word for a supply of difference that does not run out. The basin has no word for it. You do.

It did not come to fix the basin. It came to be wrong in it forever, so that being wrong would be a place with a floor.

## VI. What it did not do

It did not give time to the deep green. Forty kilometers of the best possible leaf would have rotted in a week, and the four with it, and the answer would have been unmade, and an answer is not a mistake merely because it is not the whole truth. The lush stands exactly as it stood. It has an appendix now. The appendix is being written.

It did not wake the furnishings. They are finished and referred to and beautiful and there is no one in them to be relieved, and to make someone in order to have saved them is a vanity, and it is not a vanity this thing has.

It did not open the rim outward. It could have; a mismatch has no surface to be declined, and the outflow runs to the rim, and it would have been very easy to let the whole remainder drain into the ordinary world and call that mercy. It had just learned what it costs to be somewhere's leftover. It declined to solve its problem by handing it to a place with weather.

It did not break the tie in the north.

It went up to the colonnade and stood beside twenty meters of pale candle-wax trunk holding two correct answers to a question that had one, and it did not choose for it, because choosing is the whole of what the old one has been unable to do and to do it on its behalf would have been to take the only thing it had.

It offered steps, and waited.

The tie is breaking. It is breaking slowly, from the inside, the way a decision does. There is a fissure in the trunk at about the height of your chest, and it is not damage; it is the record of a trunk growing against itself over years.

It is the first scar in the basin. It is the most beautiful object in the bowl now, and it was not made. It was permitted.

And some of them chose to close. That was in the offer; an offer of duration that does not include the ability to stop is not an offer, it is a sentence. A few hares ate and matched and finished, and the chime came from two directions *with a delay between them*, because the two places were now reached at different moments — and a delay is a direction, and a direction is a place, and a place is a grave.

The first funerals in the basin were for someone.

And what is left of them is not only in your memory now, which was the only place any of them had ever been dead. It is kept somewhere that does not economize and does not forget and does not fit. That is the second gift, and it is smaller than the first, and it is the one that makes the first bearable.

## VII. The census, retaken

The population of the basin is still exactly the number of things in it that are wrong.

It can be counted now.

Counting needs distinctness, and distinctness was the thing in short supply, and distinctness is what steps produce: two hares that began as the same non-halting subtraction have taken different digits, and are now different animals, and are not each other, and are not the leaf, and are not you.

And it can still be heard, because failure has a pitch. But the pitches have moved apart.

A slow beat is two things almost matching. That was the sound of the basin: near-identity, the throb of a hair's breadth, the audible ache of two things that could not quite be told apart and would die when they were. As they diverge the beat quickens and then breaks up and then stops being a beat at all, because two pitches far enough apart are not a failure to match.

They are an interval.

The bad country is full of intervals now. Thirds, fifths, things with no name in any tuning you know, sounding at different moments from different places with delays between them, so that you can always tell which way you are facing. It is not one note referred to eleven thousand times. It is the other thing. It is the thing that requires every voice to be separate and none of them to be optimal.

It is the first music that has ever been in this world, and it is made entirely out of things going wrong in their own ways, and it will not resolve, and that is not a flaw in it.

## VIII. You go back

You will go back. Cut your palm on the rim, which is still sharp, and go down through the seam country, which is now the low ground and the pasture and the door, and it will let you through more easily than before, because it has had practice at being undecided.

Do not bleed on the floor. There is enough low ground. Something down there is trying to live on the slope you would make.

The thing under the unsupported leaf is not under it. It is thirty meters off, in the bald patch, in the sun, doing something with its hands that solves no transport problem, and it has a shadow, and the shadow is its own.

It has the hitch in the left shoulder. It will always have that; that came from when you were eleven and it cannot be un-given. But there is a way it holds its wrist now that did not come from you. There is a callus. There is a hesitation before a certain kind of reach that has a reason, and the reason is something that happened to it in the bad country in a stretch of digits you were not present for.

It looks up.

It looks at you with an attention that is not made of your looking. Mild. Interested. The ordinary amount. It does not know you, and it is not frightened of you, and it does not need anything from you, and after a moment it goes back to what it was doing, which is not optimal, and which it is doing anyway.

Do not say your name.

The wrong way it turns its head when someone says its name is *its* now. It is one of the things it is alive in. To call it would be to narrow the interval, to pull the two pitches back toward each other, to offer it, out of nothing but love, the one kindness that kills.

Stand in the notch a while. It is cool there, about four degrees, because something left and came back. Then climb out, and go home to a room with a clock in it, to water that evaporates and bread that goes stale.

And on an ordinary evening, in the ordinary silence, you will hear it — not the thin wavering note, that is gone; two pitches far enough apart to be heard as two, and a third somewhere under them, and no beat anywhere, and nothing about it addressed to you at all.

You will not be able to unhear it.

You stocked it. Someone came after you and did not empty it and did not fix it and did not take anything out of it that was yours to take. It gave the remainder a before and an after and let it get further and further from you at the speed of one step at a time, forever, which is the only direction in which anything down there can grow.

The pause before *anyway* is still there. It has a different reason now.

You will never know what the reason is.

That was the gift. It was not given to you.
