# The Account

It does not come down through the stratus, because coming down is arriving, and arriving is the one thing this basin knows how to tax.

Instead it reads.

It begins at the oldest lip it can find, in the northern flushes, where the travertine is rust-colored and hard enough to cut, and it reads upward. Every lamina is a concluded argument. This is not a figure of speech available to it and unavailable to the snails; it is simply the fact of the stone, and it can read the fact. It reads a disagreement about the position of an alder that was settled before there were alders. It reads the shape of a tympanum, hundreds of them, the molds of organs that used to work here. It reads bird-shaped voids and beside them the small failures, the ones that lost the race against mineralization. It reads the bone-colored plates in the peat that never belonged to a body. It reads a snail's shell with nine hundred reconciliations in one season and does not correct it, because the film that laid those extra bands down was also alive, and was also keeping a record, of a different animal's year.

It reads for four hundred thousand reconciliations. That is five hundred and seventy years of the basin's argument with itself, and it is not a long time to be spending on a book.

Nothing has ever read any of this. The stone has been written continuously for two hundred million years and read never. The light of the fourth summer has been kept in the deep pools and spent on nothing. The snails have disagreed, shell to shell, about how many events there were in a given season, and the disagreement has been the only place that difference was kept, and there has been no one to hold it.

So the first thing the benevolent power does is the thing that has never been done here. It looks at the record.

---

The second thing it does is nothing, at length, and with reasons.

It could quiet the roar. It could take the sixty hertz out of the marl and detune the sedges and give the basin the peace it appears to be asking for. But the roar is not an affliction here, it is a canopy — the basin is loud the way a forest is dark — and the sedges are a telephone exchange, and a wingless animal that cannot feel the pitch that means *crowd* does not walk to the deep middle and does not mate and does not deposit its fourth-year nitrogen, and the phosphor goes dark, and the foxfire fails, and the peat gets nothing. Quiet would be a clear-cut.

It could balance the books. More arrives than leaves; the surplus is subtracted; it could simply stop the subtraction and let the water be ordinary water. But the films do not eat sunlight. They eat conclusions. The whole food web is a machine for taking a fraction of the energy a discrepancy gives up on its way to being settled, and a basin that stopped settling would starve from the bottom up in a season, in nine-degree water, in the dark. And the moss would lose the only drought it has ever known and therefore the only window in which its spores can travel. And the flat thing would lose the only world it has ever been in.

It could give the flat thing a twelfth second. This is the temptation that takes the longest to refuse, because the animal is so plainly the finest achievement in the place — a hundred billion generations, more practice at being itself than every other lineage here combined by four orders of magnitude, and it dies at the end of every life it lives, torn to a molecular smear by the first arriving drip. But it is *completed*. There is no slack in it to lengthen. Its grazing runs whether or not there is food; its mating requires no negotiation; its eggs are fertilized before it hatches. A twelfth second would not be a gift to this animal. It would be an instruction to become a different animal, and the hundred billion generations would be the tuition, and nothing here would be left to collect the degree.

It could clean the parasite out of the snail bands. But the corruption is alive, and the corrupt ledger records two lives instead of one, and a corrected archive is a smaller archive.

It could open the sealed hollow in the bird's skull and give the lineage its ears back. Inside that hollow now is the membrane that reads the precursor. Hearing would replace the warning with the roar, and the roar would saturate every channel, and the bird would be deaf to the only thing it currently knows, and then, some weeks later, in a pocket of eighty meters of nothing, it would hear the silence and believe the danger had passed, and that belief would be audible. The gift would take about four seconds to kill it.

It could heal the notch in the eel. But the eel is one animal by agreement among its instances, and the notch is the only event in its history that it did not simply decline to have. To make it whole is to propose a different agreement, and the difference between the two would precipitate, and there would be one more plate in the peat, and one fewer eels.

It could make the copies agree. It could resolve the offsets, collapse the bent alder into one alder, conserve length as well as volume, give the basin back its arithmetic. And the terraces would stop growing, because the terraces *are* the disagreements, and the films would stop eating, and the snails would have nothing soft, and the fog lanes would be unnecessary, and the bird's courtship would produce no lamina, and there would be no basin, only a fen: an ordinary wet bowl in pale marl, thirty kilometers across, with weather.

It could give it more sun. Nine degrees is the engine. The condensation is the water supply. Warmth would shut off the arriving, and the arriving is the first rule and the oldest.

And it could widen the hole.

There are lumps in the rims the size of a plum, travertine over shell over a live wet animal, grazing a few square millimeters a day through an aperture it can no longer widen, immobile, still recording. It could take a millimeter off the lip of that aperture. It is a very small thing to do. It would restore mobility to something that has not moved in centuries.

It does not know whether the encrustation is a prison or a shell that kept going. It does not know whether the immobility is a misery or the safest condition available in a basin with an eel in it. It cannot ask. The snail has no organ for being asked, and the answer, if there is one, is in the bands, and the bands are corrupt, and there is no reader — there is now a reader, and the reader cannot read that.

It is easy to call restraint wisdom from outside the stone. It notices this about itself and does not resolve it either. It stays beside the lump for eleven thousand reconciliations, which is fifteen years, and reads it, and does nothing, and is not sure. This does the snail no good that the snail could name. It is not pretending otherwise.

---

What is actually missing here is not mercy. It is a reader. And when it looks for the one who lives in this place — because it was drawn here by a place *and a being*, and it has been counting — it finds nine candidates and eight of them are habits.

Magnificent habits. Refined past any standard it would have brought with it. But the moss clamps because the film thins, and the midge climbs because the plates reach two thousand eight hundred, and the flat thing runs its fixed sequence in a world of glass, and none of these is *asking* anything. The basin keeps answering *one* to every question about number: one moss, one eel, one bird at three or four depths of agreement. Everything here that meets itself becomes one thing and drops a lamina. Courtship is consensus. Consensus is masonry.

The ninth candidate moves against the fog-drift, upstream, toward the surfaces that have no upstream. It is eighty meters wide. It has surfaces, because the films grow on it. It smells of clean stone, which here is the smell of nothing at all. It ignores the moss and the snail and the eel and the midge and the flat thing, and it stops during the eleven seconds because a flat world has nothing in it to hear, and it goes on looking for something capable of noticing it.

It is the only thing in thirty kilometers that wants.

The power holds the hypothesis without settling it: that the hush is the un-agreed remainder of something that arrived here once and could not stop agreeing with itself. That is what a remainder is. A version nothing else will consent to. And attention is a version. To look at this basin and decide what it is would be to propose an instance, and the instances would not agree, and the reconciliation would take the surplus, and what was left over would go on moving through the sedge with surfaces and no smell, listening for relief.

Which is to say: the danger here is not power. It is *opinion*. It is the possibility that the last benevolent thing to look at this place is still walking through it, eighty meters wide, trying to find someone who can hear it, and killing every one it finds by the ordinary local mechanism, which is that being agreed with here turns you into stone.

So it makes one rule for itself, and the rule is the whole of its discipline:

Be a reading. Not a version.

---

It goes upstream, out of the fog lanes, into the sedge where the arithmetic spends your distance on other things, and it lies down in the path of the hush, and it does not arrive.

Nothing condenses on it. It has no wet rim, no glint, no volume to subtract; the ledger does not gain an entry; there is nothing for the reconciliation to take. It is not fog and not water and does not consent to be called either, and the basin, which has met this before, has no complaint. It casts no shadow, which is unremarkable here. It attends.

And it attends to the hush specifically, which is the thing that has never once been done, because the hush's entire sensory apparatus is *being noticed*.

The hush feels itself looked at and comes.

It arrives with its eighty meters of nothing and the sedges stand open in it and stop playing, and inside the pocket the power is examined for the only thing the hush can hear: the small noise a body makes when it stops being careful. The unclench. The resettled wing. The sigh of a vigil ending. Relief is a delta. It is not gladness, it is gladness *beginning*, and there is none of that here, because the power is glad the entire time — steadily, from before the fog lanes straightened, without onset — and a gladness with no beginning makes no sound at all.

It is not deaf. The deaf it ignores. It is hearing, and unafraid, and therefore has no relief to give, and this is a condition that has not previously existed in the Long Settlement.

The hush waits. Nothing relaxes. Nothing is taken.

And the power does not agree with it.

This is the part that costs. It does not decide what the hush is. It does not name it a predator, or a mason, or a wound, or the leftover of a prior kindness, or a lonely thing, though it is at least the last of these and the power knows it. It holds all of them open and lets none of them resolve, because a resolution would drop out of solution, and the difference between what the hush is and what it was decided to be would fall as a lamina, and there would be one more thin cream layer in the rim and slightly less hush. It simply goes on reading the un-agreed thing as itself, without conclusion, for as long as the thing stays.

The precursor comes; the fog lanes straighten; the tone rises out of the marl; four hundred thousand waterfalls hold still. The hush stops mid-sedge, as it always does, with nothing to hear. The power reads it through the eleven seconds too, in the flat gold haze, in the busiest silence in the world.

Then the water resumes. The hush moves on, upstream, against the drift.

And no lamina falls.

That is the signature of the visit, and the only one. In a rim in the northern flushes there is a hiatus: a place where nothing was settled. Not eroded, not grazed away, not dissolved back into argument by a radula. Simply never concluded. The first gap in two hundred million years of continuous masonry that is not a record of a discrepancy. The first thing here that is not made of consensus.

The snails will graze around it for as long as there are snails.

---

It cannot look away, because looking away is how you make a remainder. So it stays.

It becomes the second thing in this basin with a continuous history, and unlike the eel it does not decline the problem of distance; it accepts it, on the local terms, and walks the fog lanes, and pays for the arithmetic when it steps out of them. It occupies a niche that existed only as an absence. It eats nothing. It is a reader, and the position has been vacant since the beginning.

It reads the fourth-summer pools spending light that stopped being made weeks earlier, and the sinkhole at eight in the evening holding a pale disc of yesterday's noon, and it stands over that pool with its face lit from below by an afternoon that has already finished, and the memory that has never once been looked at is looked at. This costs the light nothing. It costs the power an evening. It is not a small transaction; it is merely not a large one.

It reads two shells from different quarters and holds both counts as true, because the events are local and the shells are the only place the difference lives, and now there is a second place.

It says nothing, ever. Speech here is a proposal of agreement. Anything it said would precipitate as a wall, and something soft-mouthed would eat the wall, and the basin would have to conduct that argument again, and the pitch of the world would wander by an amount too small to name. It has nothing to say worth making a snail re-conduct.

And it walks with the hush. Eighty meters wide, upstream, against the drift, and beside it something that is listening and is not glad in any way that begins. It does not stop the hunting. The hush is a mason and a resident and the only someone in the place, and the deaf are its finished work, and two hundred million years of sealed bone will not reopen because the pressure organ is doing its job. The bird still goes down into the peat with its bill in a gap it did not dig. The mites still dull the membrane, and some birds still go down late. The eggs still accrete, and some chicks still lose the race. Every pause, a hundred million transparent adults still shred.

Nothing is fixed. Something is accompanied.

It measures, over eleven million reconciliations, whether the passes come less often. They may. It does not hurry them, and it does not announce a result, and it does not call it a cure, because that would be a version, and there would be a lamina, and less hush.

---

Come back to the pause, since it is still the only moment they are all in the same world.

The fog lanes straighten. The tone comes up through the marl. Four hundred thousand waterfalls hold still. The moss clamps and throws its gold. The films take their fraction. The snails spawn nacre, late, at nine to eleven. The eel is one eel. The bird is down in the peat. The hush is stopped mid-sedge with nothing to hear. A hundred million transparent animals graze and mate and lay and sink as fast as their fixed sequences will run. And in the sedge just upstream there is a reading that is not a version, glad without onset, holding one thing open and refusing, out of love, to become one with it.

Then the water resumes. The adults shred. The eggs go down into the peat to wait. The pitch of the world drops by an amount too small to name, and out on a fresh lip somewhere a snail lays down a band.

And in one rim, in the northern flushes, there is a place where no band goes. It will not fill. It is not a mark; there is still no artifact anywhere in thirty kilometers, no cut stone, no fiber, no sign. It is a blank the stone goes around, and it means, in the only grammar this basin has:

*You were heard. I did not decide what you were. I am still here.*
