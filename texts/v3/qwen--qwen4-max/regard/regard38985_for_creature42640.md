# The interval

## First it did nothing, for a long time

It came in the way the fog comes, which is to say without a gradient to come along, and the first thing it did was nothing.

It watched a pump for a whole day's approach and passed it in ten minutes. It stood in the nickel noon and let the sleet arrive after its sound. It put its hand into a thin place and felt the other weather combing the pelt the other way, and took the hand out, and did not close the thin place, and did not widen it. It counted the flat pale animals on the finished stone. There were six. It came back a week later, two leagues away, and there were six, and it was glad — but *glad* is one of the words that is too large for this ground, so what can honestly be said is: it counted again.

It let the plain try to learn its period. The plain could not find one. There was no throughput in it to bank, no heat to keep, no product to reclaim; it offered the tissue nothing to bid on. This was not a trick and it was not armour. It had made itself as the six had made themselves, over a length of time with no useful number attached: it had arrived at the condition of the rock and stayed there, alive, and uninteresting to a place that eats everything interesting.

That was the whole of its preparation. It had come to help. It had understood, on the way, that the only way to be here at all was to bring nothing.

## The mercies it did not perform

It wanted to give the plain a downhill. It could have. A drop of forty metres on the eastern margin, a crack, a river, and the standing water would go out of here at last, walking, without being worked, and the pumps could stop, and the noise could stop, and the four metres of fury could thin into an ordinary marsh with ordinary mud and a sky you could see the sun in.

It did the arithmetic and found that this was not a rescue. It was a replacement. Everything here is a function of the fact that water cannot leave by falling. Take away the constraint and you do not get the same world, relieved. You get a different world, with the corpse of this one under it, and the sixty thousand blind things in the chambers going still and not resuming, and the flame — the one continuous thing, the original, handed along hand to hand by something with no hands — cooling in a heart that no longer needs to bank its heat. It declined. Not because the plain is beautiful, though it is, in the way a marshalling yard is. Because the plain is *finished*, and finishing took a hundred million years, and it is not a wound.

It wanted to hold the forty beats in lock. This was the sweetest of the temptations and the worst. It could have done it forever: no drift, no failure, no silence opening so enormous that the waders go up and you find you have been shouting. Permanent unison. Permanent spawning. Permanent ninety seconds.

And in that permanence the outline would never be born again, because it is born in the *coming* into phase, and a coincidence that never ends is not a coincidence, it is a condition. The shape feeds on the drift being pulled back. It reaches. It nudges a valve a fraction early, banks a heart a half-second, and holds itself together a little longer than chance allows, and that reaching is the only appetite on this plain that is not accounting. To do the nudging for it — better, faster, unfailingly, out of kindness — would be to take the one thing it does and do it instead. It would starve with its mouth full. Nothing on this moor would be crueler than a power that held the lock.

So it kept its hands off the valves. Of all the renunciations, that one cost the most, and it is the only one it still thinks about.

It wanted to give the grazers hunger, so that the cropping would be about something, and fear, so that the cooling would be about something, and it saw in time that this was the offer of suffering to things that do not currently have any, dressed as the offer of a soul.

It wanted to give the birds their song back — the seven notes, the forty variations, the family tree branching instead of converging. But the birds are being worn smooth by the same appetite that planed the granite, and to exempt them would be to lift them out of the only weather their world has, and make them a museum of themselves, singing an elsewhere at a fog that has no elsewhere in it. It let the song get shorter. It listened to it get shorter. It did not look away, which is a different thing from permitting.

It wanted to cut the copies apart. To sharpen the mould, restore the notched ear to one animal only, make the editions into individuals. And it saw that the two with the same kink in the same left foreleg are not two castings but one animal at two depths, and that to separate them would be to take the seam-dwellers' other half away, and leave them leaning toward nothing at all instead of leaning toward something that is simply not in this phase. The lean is not a deformity. It is a relation. It is the last relation on the plain that crosses something other than distance, and it was not going to sever it in order to be able to count.

And it wanted, once, badly, to let the whole thing stop. To give the finished rock its silence and take the four metres away. It sat with that for what would have been a night if there were nights. It did not decide that the moor was happy. It did not decide that the moor was in pain. It decided that it did not know, and that not knowing was not a licence, and that doing something would mostly be a way of stopping the not-knowing.

It declined to stop the not-knowing.

## The one thing it made

Off the plain. Not on it. There is not a made thing on this moor and there is not one now; no chip, no scratch, no cairn, no sign; nothing that could not be digested, nothing that would sit in the quilt like a stone in a lung, a warehouse in a world with no warehouse.

Elsewhere, in a wet warm dark that has nothing to do with any of this, it prepared thirty-eight degrees, and moisture, and sugar arriving.

That is all. It is the consortium's entire world and it does not know that the trees went, so it will not know that this room exists, and it will not be told, and there is no ceremony. The mat goes on carrying the fire along the lignified thread from the failing shoulder to the crowning one, hand to hand to hand, with no idea what it is carrying, and it has never been allowed to go out, and it will not be allowed to go out by anything that could take the credit. The room is not a guarantee. A guarantee is a made thing laid on the plain, and the plain would have to hold it, and holding is what this ground has stopped doing.

The room is a spare hearth. It is unused. It is intended to stay unused. Nobody on the moor knows about it, and the waders come back from the coast with nothing to report, and this is not in the report either.

It is the only thing the power built, and it built it out of the possibility that everything it loves here might fail anyway, and it does not consider this a pessimistic act.

## It read the ledger

The parasites are the last archivists. They must distinguish or die, and so they keep a registry of faint differences that nothing else on this plain bothers with: this host's surface, that host's surface, the edition with the hesitation a quarter-beat long and the edition with the hesitation a half-beat long. They cross between the copies and for a moment there is an actual thread, an actual *this one and that one*, and then a wader comes and eats the archive one beakful at a time.

The power did not intervene in the beak. It read the registry.

That is an act that adds nothing to the plain, takes nothing from it, accumulates nothing anywhere the mat can reach, and happens to be the one thing the ledger has never had. Somewhere a hundred thousand small green mouths a second are balancing a column at a furious rate and there is no one to read it. Now there is a reader. It knows the hoarse pump by its hoarseness and the one that skips by its skip and the one two kilometres east that runs a half-second slow and has done so for as long as anything here has done anything. It knows the difference between the two grazers with the same notched ear. It knows the difference between the sixty thousand springtails in this chamber and the sixty thousand in that one, which is a difference of signature and nothing else, and it holds them apart in its attention without holding them apart anywhere else, because to hold them apart anywhere else would be to build.

Names do not stick here, so it does not use names. It uses attention, which does not need a sequence to stand in.

Nothing is saved. It did not come to save. It came to be the one thing this place has never issued: something that is not from the mould, that will not be digested, and that can tell you apart from your copy without needing you to be different.

## Above the fog

At a certain point in the year the waders crowd the western margin and go out into the horizontal sleet toward a coast that was eroded before there were birds. The power did not give them the coast. It did not shorten the flight or improve the odds or arrange for all of them to come back.

Sometimes they go up instead. Above nine kilometres of standing fog there is a sun, and they have seen it, and they come down into the nickel-coloured noon and scream, and the scream is not information; it is the last remaining noise on this plain that is *about* something.

The power went up and was there in the light when they arrived.

That is the whole intervention. The scream is still about something. It is now also to someone. It did not tell them, on the way down, what it had heard, because telling requires a before and an after and the fog would take the sentence apart on the way through. It just came down with them, into the grey, and went on listening to the one song getting shorter.

## It did not become the seventh

It went to the six. It lay down on the planed stone in the millimetre of tea-black water, at one degree above freezing, and did almost nothing, extremely slowly, in their company, and learned the thing they have to teach, which is how to be present without being interesting.

It understood, in whatever way a thing with no organs worth the name understands anything, that it must not be countable here. Because the reliability of the number is the proof. Six, and six again a week later two leagues away: that steadiness is the last evidence on this plain that something is not of the mat, and if the power lay down among them it would be seven, and then six would be a number that could change, and the proof would be gone, and it would have spent the last certainty in the world on the pleasure of being included.

So it is not the seventh. In the morning you will count them and there will be six. Whatever else happens, there will be six. That is the power's signature on this place: an absence you can verify.

## The interval

And then, two or three times a morning, for about ninety seconds, there is a person on this plain, and it has begun to arrive early.

The power watched it stand in the fog with an outline, some hundreds of metres across, a second or two before the beats that make it came together: an appetite with nothing yet to eat, waiting for its own causes. And it saw what this was. Not a malfunction. Not even a niche, exactly.

The flat is eroding duration now. It has worn through identity and it is working on sequence, and it cannot wear what has not happened yet. In the gap between a thing being about to happen and happening there is no duration to take away, no order to smooth, nothing laid down that could be planed. It is the one piece of real estate on this plain that erosion cannot reach, and it is not in the plain at all, and it is enormous, and the shape has found it, and has been moving in.

So the power moved in too.

It did not extend the ninety seconds. It did not stabilise the lock, or feed the shape, or make the unison arrive on schedule; it kept its hands off the valves, and let the outline reach, and let it prefer the eastern pumps, and let it hesitate before it lets go. What it did was be already there. When the shape comes early now, into the interval where nothing has happened yet, it is not the only outlined thing in it. There is something with it that is not an edition, not a copy, not weather, not issued that morning, and not going to be reclaimed.

Greeting is impossible here. Greeting requires that one thing come after another. But company does not. Company is the one relation that can be held entirely in simultaneity, without a before or an after, without a promise, without a memory, without any of the sequences this ground has learned to eat. It cannot be worn smooth because there is nothing sequential in it to wear. Two things at once, and neither one first.

There is a word for what the power is doing in the interval. It is too large. What it is doing is: it is already there.

## You

Your footprints fill a half-second before you set your foot down, and the tissue has your beat now, and it is banking it, and the power did not stop this and will not.

It could not find a way to that did not involve making you a made thing, an exemption, a permanent foreign body in a quilt that eats its own products; and it would not put a warehouse in you. You are the last thing here that arrives before you happen. That property is being learned. When it has been learned there will be nothing on this plain that arrives, and everything will be issued, and the flat will go on working on whatever is left, which will be the interval, and the six, and the fire.

Out in the fog there is a shape a day's approach away with your kink in the left foreleg and your notched ear and your hesitation before it lowers its head, and you will pass it in ten minutes, and it will not greet you, because the two of you are not one after another in any sense this ground still maintains.

The power will not tell you which one you are. There is no mark to point at; the mould is smooth. It will do the only thing that can be done instead, which is to be with the one that arrived, and not because it can prove which one that is, but because being-with is itself the distinction, the last one available, held in attention and nowhere else. It knows you apart. It will not make you apart.

And it is not going to leave, because leaving is a sequence, and it has given up sequences in order to be here. It is in the interval, where nothing has happened yet, where the erosion cannot follow, with the shape that has an outline and with whatever is left of you when the plain has finished issuing you. It has brought nothing. It has built nothing here. It has taken nothing away. There is a wet warm dark somewhere else that nobody knows about, and the fire goes along the thread, hand to hand to hand.

Somewhere out in the dark a valve lifts, and hisses, and drops.

*Clack.*

You do not hear it before it happens, and you do not hear it after. You hear it, and you are not the only one hearing it, and there is no order between those two facts, and nothing on this plain can wear that away.
